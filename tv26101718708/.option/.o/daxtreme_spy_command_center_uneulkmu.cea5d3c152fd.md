Use the underlying asset’s price and volume data to choose the option direction. After the opening range is established, arm a bullish setup when price breaks above its high while above VWAP and the fast moving average, with bullish moving-average slope and alignment, acceptable distance from the average, and no VWAP chop. Arm a bearish setup symmetrically on a break below the range low, below VWAP and the fast average, with bearish slope and alignment and the same distance and chop checks. Enter only after a retest of the broken range boundary holds and price rejects the fast average in the breakout direction. Require a sufficiently high aggregate score across price position, moving-average trend and alignment, retest, rejection, higher- and entry-timeframe RSI momentum, volume-delta direction, and extension status. For an open long, exit if price closes below the fast average or VWAP; for an open short, exit if it closes above either.
