Track confirmed swing highs and lows on the traded instrument and a correlated external instrument. When corresponding swings are within a synchronization window, signal short if either instrument makes a higher swing high while the other makes a lower swing high, or if the traded instrument makes a higher high after the external instrument has already made a higher high and then fallen below that swing. Signal long under the mirrored low conditions: either instrument makes a lower swing low while the other makes a higher swing low, or the traded instrument makes a lower low after the external instrument has already made a lower low and then risen above that swing. Emit each signal only once per traded-instrument swing. No exit condition is defined.
