Use the underlying instrument’s price and session data to decide direction. During the configured entry window, go long when price is above VWAP, the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, and the close is above both EMAs and either the prior-day high or premarket high. Require a bullish candle that either retests or touches one of those highs and closes above it, or touches the fast EMA and closes back above it. Reverse these conditions for shorts using VWAP, EMA ordering, prior-day or premarket lows, and a bearish close. Limit entries to the configured daily trade cap and enter only when flat. On a new session high while long, or new session low while short, take a partial profit. Exit the remaining position on a close across the fast EMA or, for a level-based entry, back across its entry level. Exit all remaining size if price touches the slow EMA, and close any open position in the end-of-day flatten window.
