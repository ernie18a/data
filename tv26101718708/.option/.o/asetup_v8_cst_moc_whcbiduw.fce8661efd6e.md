Use the traded instrument’s price, volume, and time of day. Score bullish and bearish alignment across moving-average order, price relative to rising or falling VWAP, higher-timeframe trend, trend-zone RSI, directionally aligned and strengthening MACD histogram, and elevated, expanding volume. Require a matching candlestick or price-action pattern: engulfing candle, long-wick reversal, decisive swing breakout or breakdown, strong reversal at a nearby support or resistance level, or a confirmed high-volume sweep and reclaim or rejection of a recent extreme. For regular entries, require at least four of six score conditions, matching pattern, and agreement from at least two of moving-average alignment, price versus VWAP, and higher-timeframe trend; the highest tier requires all six. Suppress regular entries during blocked intraday periods and within the end-of-day window, and enforce a per-direction cooldown. In the end-of-day window, allow a matching-pattern entry with at least four score conditions, trend agreement, qualified volume, cooldown, and room before the prior-day extreme in the trade direction; suppress it if a regular entry fires. For an open long or short, exit at the full profit target, protective stop, time limit, or when the opposing trend alignment and pattern signal a reversal. After the first profit target, move the stop to entry.
