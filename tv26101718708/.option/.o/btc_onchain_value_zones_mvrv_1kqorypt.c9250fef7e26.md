On each daily close, calculate Bitcoin’s valuation ratio from price and realized price, or market capitalization and realized capitalization. When it crosses below the cost-basis level, signal a long; crossing below a lower capitulation level is a stronger long signal. Crossing back above cost basis confirms recovery. When it crosses above an upper valuation level, signal selling or exiting longs; crossing above a higher euphoria level is a stronger exit signal. No short-entry rule is defined.
