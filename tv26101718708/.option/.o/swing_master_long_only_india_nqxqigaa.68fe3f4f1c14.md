Long-only. Calculate each instrument’s percentage distance from a moving average and subtract the benchmark index’s percentage distance from the same type and length of moving average. While this relative-strength difference is above its threshold, enter long when any of these occurs: price breaks above the swing high formed during the preceding bearish trend phase while the trend is bullish; price crosses above its high-based moving average while the trend is bullish and that trigger is rearmed; or the fast price moving average crosses above the slow one. If configured, require a bullish trend for every entry. Exit an open long when the relative-strength difference falls below the threshold. No short decision is defined.
