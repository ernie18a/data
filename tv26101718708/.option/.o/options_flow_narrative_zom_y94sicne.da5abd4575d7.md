Go long when the instrument’s smoothed buying-volume proxy exceeds its selling-volume proxy, the VIX is below its own short-term average, and the instrument crosses upward through a volatility-adjusted lower band below VWAP. Go short when the selling-volume proxy exceeds the buying-volume proxy, the VIX is above its short-term average, and the instrument crosses downward through a volatility-adjusted upper band above VWAP. No exit rule is defined.
