At each confirmed swing high on the traded instrument, compare it with the previous confirmed swing high, provided the two swings are within the configured bar-separation limit. For each comparison instrument, use its highest high in a small window around each swing. If the traded instrument makes a higher high while any comparison instrument makes a lower high, or the traded instrument makes a lower high while any comparison instrument makes a higher high, signal short. At each confirmed swing low, make the analogous comparison using each instrument’s lowest low: if the traded instrument makes a lower low while any comparison instrument makes a higher low, or makes a higher low while any comparison instrument makes a lower low, signal long. Confirm signals when the new swing is recognized; no exit condition is defined.
