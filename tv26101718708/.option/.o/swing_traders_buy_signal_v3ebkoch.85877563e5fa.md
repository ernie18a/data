Go long when the instrument’s technical-factor score reaches the buy threshold, its price is above its short- and medium-term averages, and its relative performance versus a broad-market benchmark is positive. Classify as a stronger long when the score reaches the higher threshold, price and moving averages form a bullish hierarchy, relative performance is positive, and momentum is bullish. The score counts bullish conditions across price versus moving averages, moving-average alignment, momentum, benchmark-relative performance, stochastic momentum, and above-average volume. No short or exit decision is defined.
