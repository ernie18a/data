Use advancing and declining volume for the target market group, sampled at the chosen resolution. Compute net advancing volume as advancing volume minus declining volume, then measure its change from the prior sample as a percentage of current combined advancing and declining volume. Go long when this measure exceeds the upper threshold; go short when it falls below the lower threshold. Otherwise retain the current position. An optional reverse setting swaps long and short signals. The strategy has no separate exit-to-flat signal.
