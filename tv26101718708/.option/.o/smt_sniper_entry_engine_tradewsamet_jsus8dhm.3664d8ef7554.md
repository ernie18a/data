On each completed bar, compare the traded market with a selected positively related market. A bullish candidate occurs when exactly one market sweeps below its own prior rolling low and the sweeping market closes back above that low; a bearish candidate mirrors this at prior rolling highs. Accept a candidate only when the traded market’s candle direction, rejection close, volatility-adjusted sweep depth and range expansion pass the active preset’s quality requirements, and the pair’s recent return correlation, external-range location, divergence separation and combined confluence score pass that preset’s context requirements. Respect separate directional cooldowns. An accepted candidate arms one pending setup and stores its extreme, trigger and volatility reference. During the confirmation window, accumulate evidence from directional displacement, a close beyond the candidate candle’s opposite extreme, and imbalance or micro-structure confirmation. The least selective preset requires displacement and the candle break; the intermediate preset also requires either imbalance or micro-structure confirmation; the strictest requires both. Emit a long signal only when the bullish setup completes, and a short signal only when the bearish setup completes, on a completed bar, before expiry, without invalidation, and when the final-signal cooldown permits. Invalidate if price closes beyond the stored setup extreme by its volatility-based buffer; expire setups whose required evidence does not arrive in time. The trade-management logic separately exits an active position when its instrument’s price reaches its volatility-based stop or fixed risk-multiple target, giving the stop priority if both are touched on one bar.
