Long-only, one position at a time. Enter long when the close is above VWAP and a longer-term simple moving average, a shorter-term exponential moving average is above a slower one, RSI is above its midpoint, volume exceeds its recent average by a multiple, the close breaks above the prior lookback-period highs, and the candle closes bullish above the previous bar’s high. While long, do not exit until a minimum holding period has passed and all of these are true: the close is below VWAP and the longer-term average, the shorter-term exponential average is below the slower one, the current close and previous close are each below the preceding bar’s low, and RSI is below a weak-momentum threshold. No short entries.
