On the traded instrument, compare its newest confirmed swing high or low with an older swing of the same kind. At both swing times, compare the corresponding high or low on a correlated external instrument. If the traded instrument makes a higher high while the external instrument does not, signal short; if it makes a lower low while the external instrument does not, signal long. Ignore the divergence if an intervening swing has already exceeded the relevant traded-instrument pivot boundary. End the active signal if either instrument breaks the older pivot boundary, or if either closes beyond the intervening swing in the reversal direction.
