Use the main instrument and up to two related instruments. Track their rolling correlations and relative performance, plus the main instrument’s drawdown from its recent high and volatility relative to its usual level. Feed these conditions into an online-learned model that compares hedge, diversify, and hold actions; train it to favor hedging when the main instrument subsequently falls, diversification when related assets decouple and outperform, and holding when the main instrument holds up. Recommend a hedge (for example, short a positively correlated related instrument) when the model selects hedge, its smoothed hedge confidence exceeds its decision threshold, and the main instrument is below its recent high. Flag diversification when the model selects diversify and average correlation is low. Otherwise hold. This logic gives no direct long entry or exit signal.
