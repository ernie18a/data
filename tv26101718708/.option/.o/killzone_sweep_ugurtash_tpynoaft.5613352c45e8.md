Track prior-day and completed-session highs and lows as liquidity levels. A breach that closes back inside the level within a reclaim window creates a reversal setup: a swept low points long; a swept high points short. Optionally require a correlated instrument to diverge over the same lookback: for a long, the traded instrument makes a new low while the peer does not; for a short, it makes a new high while the peer does not. Optionally wait for a close beyond the reclaim bar’s opposite extreme, or for a retracement into the resulting fair-value gap, before entry. Apply any enabled session, depth, direction, weekday, higher-timeframe bias, range-location, session-position, repeat-raid, adaptive-performance, and one-open-trade gates. Exit at the stop beyond the raid extreme or at the configured target; optional management can move the stop to breakeven, take a partial, or trail it. The correlated-instrument divergence gate is off by default.
