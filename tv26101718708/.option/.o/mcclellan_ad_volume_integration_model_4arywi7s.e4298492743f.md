For the traded instrument, calculate a market-breadth series as advancing issues minus declining issues, multiplied by advancing volume minus declining volume; if either volume series is unavailable, use a neutral multiplier. Subtract a longer-term EMA of this series from a shorter-term EMA. Enter long when the result was below a configurable threshold on the prior bar and is above it now, provided no position is open. Close the long after a configurable number of bars from entry. No short signal.
