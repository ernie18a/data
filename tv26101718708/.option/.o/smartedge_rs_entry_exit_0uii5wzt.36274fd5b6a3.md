Compare the traded instrument’s price strength with a benchmark by smoothing their price ratio; classify relative strength as rising or falling over a confirmation window. Use moving averages, RSI, and an optional volume filter. Go long when the fast moving average crosses above the slow one while relative strength is rising, RSI is between the oversold and overbought thresholds, and volume passes the filter. Also enter long on a pullback recovery: in an uptrend, price moves back above the fast average after being below it, relative strength is rising, RSI is below a moderate upper threshold, and volume passes. Exit long when the fast average crosses below the slow one, or when relative strength is falling while RSI is overbought. Go short when the fast average crosses below the slow one while relative strength is falling, RSI is below the complement of the oversold threshold, and volume passes. Cover a short when the fast average crosses above the slow one or relative strength is rising.
