Compare the instrument’s performance against a market benchmark over a lookback period. Relative outperformance favors the advancing-stage classification and a long signal when the instrument transitions from basing to advancing; relative underperformance favors the declining-stage classification and a short signal when it transitions from topping to declining. Use broader-market conditions to adjust profit-taking while in an advancing stage: market deterioration or climax conditions lower the exhaustion threshold for trimming or exiting the long, and any exhaustion signal during severe market danger triggers a strong exit signal. A transition from declining to basing signals covering shorts.
