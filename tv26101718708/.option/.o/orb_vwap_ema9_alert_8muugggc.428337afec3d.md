During the trading window, allow at most one long and one short signal per day. Build an opening range from the opening session. A long requires a fresh close above that range, followed within a limited number of bars by a bullish pullback-and-reclaim of the range high, VWAP, or short EMA. Require price and EMA above VWAP, both slopes rising, strong relative and expanding volume, a bullish candle closing above the prior bar’s high, and the traded instrument above VWAP. Also require SPY above its VWAP; for technology symbols, require QQQ above its VWAP as well. A short mirrors these conditions: fresh close below the opening range, bearish pullback-and-rejection of its low, VWAP, or EMA; price and EMA below VWAP, both slopes falling, strong relative and expanding volume, a bearish candle closing below the prior bar’s low, and SPY below VWAP (plus QQQ for technology symbols). Invalidate a setup if price crosses the EMA against its direction before signaling. Mark entry at the signal close and derive stop and profit levels from ATR; no exit decision rules are specified.
