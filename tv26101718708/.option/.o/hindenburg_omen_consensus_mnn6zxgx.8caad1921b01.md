For each enabled market universe, open a signal window when both new 52-week highs and new 52-week lows exceed their respective threshold shares of that universe’s issues, and its benchmark is at or above its level from the trend lookback period earlier. Keep the window open for the configured duration. When overlapping open windows meet the configured consensus minimum and the NYSE McClellan Oscillator—an EMA of normalized advancing-minus-declining issues at the shorter smoothing period minus its EMA at the longer smoothing period—is negative, exit long positions or enter short positions in the traded instrument. Otherwise, this indicator gives no trading direction.
