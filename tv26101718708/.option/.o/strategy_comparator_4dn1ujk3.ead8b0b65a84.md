Across configured assets and timeframes, backtest each candidate strategy and rank asset–strategy pairs by the selected performance metric averaged across timeframes. Use the highest-ranked strategy (or the manually selected one) to determine positions on the chart instrument. Its rules use price and volume data: hold long or short continuously; take Bollinger or Keltner band reversals or breakouts; follow moving-average or MACD direction; trade momentum or channel breaks; use RSI, random-walk, stochastic, or aggregate technical-rating signals; trade outside bars or consecutive moves; follow Supertrend or Parabolic SAR direction; or trade cumulative wick imbalance. Enter or reverse when the selected rule signals long or short, and go flat when that rule signals an exit or the configured side restriction closes the position. Configurable parameters determine lookbacks, thresholds, and rule variants.
