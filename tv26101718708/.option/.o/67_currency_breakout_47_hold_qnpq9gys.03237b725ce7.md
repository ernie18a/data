During each defined trading session, compare each of the seven major currency pairs involving a given currency with that pair’s high and low from the previous completed session. Count a pair as confirming that currency’s strength when its close breaks above the prior high if the currency is the pair’s base currency, or below the prior low if it is the quote currency; reverse those directions to count weakness. Go long the currency when at least six pairs confirm strength and at least four remain closed beyond their breakout levels in the corresponding direction. Go short when at least six confirm weakness and at least four remain beyond their levels in that direction. Emit at most one signal per currency per session. No exit rule is specified.
