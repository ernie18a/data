For gold, score bullish evidence when the dollar index is below its trend average, when it makes a higher swing high while gold makes a higher swing low, or when it breaks below recent support with an unusually large range while gold holds above its recent low. Score bearish evidence for the inverse conditions: the dollar index above its trend average, a lower dollar-index swing low paired with a lower gold swing high, or a strong dollar-index breakout above resistance while gold stays below its recent high. Divergence evidence requires the paired swings to occur close together in time; apply the configured trend and momentum filters, suppress repeated divergence signals for a cooldown period, and keep confirmed divergence evidence active briefly. Go long when bullish evidence outweighs bearish evidence, and short when bearish evidence outweighs bullish evidence; classify a bias as strong when at least two signals agree and none oppose. When the scores tie, take no directional action. No separate exit condition is defined.
