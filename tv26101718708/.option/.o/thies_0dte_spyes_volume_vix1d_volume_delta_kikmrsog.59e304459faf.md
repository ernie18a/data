Use SPY and ES volume, their signed-volume direction, and short-term implied volatility as context for the traded instrument. Go long when combined volume is above its recent average, buying pressure is strengthening and aligned with price, and implied volatility is stable or falling. Go short when volume is above average, selling pressure is strengthening and aligned with price, and implied volatility is rising. Exit when pressure reverses against the position or price breaks the combined-volume average. Stand aside when volume is below average or pressure is weak or flat; a pressure reversal against price on rising volume may signal a reversal trade. This is discretionary guidance, not a complete signal generator.
