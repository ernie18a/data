Compare confirmed swing highs and lows on the traded instrument with those on a related reference instrument. A bearish signal occurs when the traded instrument sweeps a prior swing high but the reference instrument fails to make a corresponding higher high; a bullish signal occurs when the traded instrument sweeps a prior swing low but the reference instrument fails to make a corresponding lower low. Optionally require the confirming candles on both instruments to point in the signal direction. No exit rule is defined.
