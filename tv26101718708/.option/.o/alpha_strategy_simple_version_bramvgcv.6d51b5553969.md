Using rolling data for the traded instrument and the S&P 500 benchmark, estimate the instrument’s beta from their returns, then calculate its alpha as its cumulative return minus beta times the benchmark’s cumulative return. Smooth this alpha and a Sharpe-style measure of the instrument’s price change relative to its price volatility. Enter long when the instrument closes above its open, smoothed Sharpe-style measure exceeds 1, and smoothed alpha is positive. Exit the long when either measure falls below its threshold: Sharpe-style measure below 1 or alpha below zero. No short entry.
