Compare the traded instrument with SPY: form their price ratio, subtract its rolling average, subtract the rolling average of that deviation, then divide by the rolling standard deviation of the deviation. Go long when this normalized relative-price measure is below a lower entry boundary and the instrument’s one-bar return is at or below a configured minimum. Go short when it is above an upper entry boundary and the one-bar return is at or above a configured minimum. Exit a long when the measure rises above its long-exit boundary, unless a short-entry condition is active; exit a short when it falls below its short-exit boundary, unless a long-entry condition is active.
