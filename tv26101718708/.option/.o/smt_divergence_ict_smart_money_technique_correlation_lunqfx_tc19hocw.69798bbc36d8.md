At each confirmed swing high, compare the traded instrument’s move from its previous swing high with the correlated instrument’s move over the same interval. If one makes a higher high and the other does not, and the correlated instrument’s move is large enough to be meaningful, signal short. At each confirmed swing low, if one makes a lower low and the other does not under the same conditions, signal long. For inversely correlated pairs, compare the correlated instrument’s lows at the traded instrument’s highs and its highs at the traded instrument’s lows. Require the absolute rolling correlation between the instruments to meet a minimum strength threshold; otherwise suppress signals. No exit logic is defined.
