For the traded instrument, mark a bullish liquidity sweep when price falls below the prior rolling swing low and closes back above it; mark a bearish sweep when price rises above the prior rolling swing high and closes back below it. Keep each sweep eligible for a short, fixed recent-bar window. Enter long when an eligible bullish sweep coincides with either unusually high volume relative to its recent history or a candle with a small body relative to its full range, external stablecoin dominance is below its moving average, and aggregated open interest across the monitored venues is at or below its moving average. Enter short on the mirrored bearish sweep and volume or candle condition when stablecoin dominance is above its moving average and aggregated open interest is above its moving average. Process each sweep cluster at most once. Open a signal when flat; add in the same direction only if already holding that direction and price has moved adversely beyond the position’s average entry. Do not open an opposite position from an existing position. If regime filtering is enabled, an opposing trend regime reduces entry size but does not block or change the signal. Exit a long after reaching the profit threshold if price closes below its fast moving average, or if price closes beyond the adverse loss threshold, a ratcheted stop is hit, the catastrophe stop is hit, or the maximum-profit limit is reached. Apply the mirrored conditions to shorts. The ratchet raises a long stop, or lowers a short stop, after successive favorable price milestones; if prior losses are being recovered, move the stop to the recovery price when reached.
