Use a broad-market volatility index as a fear filter for long-only staged recovery entries in the traded instrument. Start a sequence after a substantial pullback from a recent high, recent oversold momentum, and improving momentum with price reclaiming short-term strength. Require acceptable higher-timeframe conditions, no active crash-like selloff, and elevated volatility stress or cooling fear; exceptionally high fear may relax the higher-timeframe filter when local recovery is visible. After the first entry, allow a second long entry only if price falls below the first entry into a deeper zone within a time window, then confirms a rebound or rejection, with stricter volatility stress and recovery-quality checks. After that, allow a final long entry only after a further decline below the staged basket average within its time window, followed by stronger recovery evidence and a volatility index that was recently extremely elevated and has since cooled. Reset the sequence when its stages expire or the final stage has aged out. The indicator defines no short or exit signal.
