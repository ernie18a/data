At each confirmed swing low, compare it with the prior confirmed swing low within the configured spacing window. Signal long when price makes a lower low, the difference between a short-period and longer-period simple moving average of price makes a higher low, and—when breadth confirmation is enabled—the external market-breadth index makes a higher low. If the zero-side filter is enabled, both oscillator readings must be below zero. At each confirmed swing high, signal short when price makes a higher high, the oscillator makes a lower high, and—when enabled—the external breadth index makes a lower high; both oscillator readings must be above zero when the zero-side filter is enabled. No exit rule is defined.
