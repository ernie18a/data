Use external NQ, ES, and YM trend readings plus VIX as a market-wide bias filter. For each, assess price against an EMA envelope and a long-term EMA; also assess the three indices against VWAP. Give the envelope the greatest weight, the long-term EMA a smaller weight, and VWAP the least; invert VIX’s contribution so falling VIX favors long bias and rising VIX favors short bias. Aggregate the readings into a composite score: a high score indicates strong long bias, a moderately high score long bias, a middle score neutral, a moderately low score short bias, and a low score strong short bias. Use this only to confirm or caution on a separate signal for the charted instrument; it defines no standalone entry or exit.
