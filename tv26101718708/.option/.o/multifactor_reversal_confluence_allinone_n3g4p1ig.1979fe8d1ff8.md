When configured to borrow volume from an external instrument, use that volume with the chart instrument’s price data to detect directional exhaustion and climax. Signal a long reversal after downside exhaustion and a bullish volume climax, or a short reversal after upside exhaustion and a bearish volume climax, when enough reversal detectors agree and a matching liquidity sweep is followed by a displacement break; an earlier configured lifecycle stage may signal before that confirmation. Suppress reversal signals when the mean-reversion regime is weak or volatility shocks indicate an intensifying cascade. An optional external shelf may strengthen conviction, and an optional benchmark may adjust conviction; neither independently establishes direction.
