Use the selected Commitments of Traders category’s net positions and their stochastic position within a rolling range as the external market filter. In reversal mode, go long when the COT index is in its lower extreme and the momentum oscillator crosses upward out of its oversold zone; go short when the index is in its upper extreme and the oscillator crosses downward out of its overbought zone. In trend mode, go long on the upward oversold crossover when the COT index is above its midpoint, and short on the downward overbought crossover when the index is below its midpoint. If the COT filter is disabled, take the corresponding oscillator crossovers without the COT condition. Exit a long on the downward overbought crossover and a short on the upward oversold crossover; optional profit and stop levels also close positions.
