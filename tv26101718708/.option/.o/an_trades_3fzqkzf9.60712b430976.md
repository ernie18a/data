On a confirmed bar, signal long when price is touching a bullish inverted fair-value-gap zone or a bullish higher-timeframe fair-value-gap zone, and either a recent low sweep has occurred, price is at the current session low, or the higher-timeframe bullish zone is being touched. Require a bullish delivery shift by default: price closes above the open of the latest bearish delivery candle. If enabled, also require bullish divergence from a correlated instrument, where the traded instrument makes a lower low and the correlated instrument does not. Block the signal if the nearest opposing liquidity is closer than the same-direction liquidity, an unusually large lower wick is present, or price has closed back below the swept low. Reverse these conditions for short signals: bearish zone, recent high sweep or session high or higher-timeframe bearish-zone touch, bearish delivery shift, optional bearish divergence, and corresponding opposing-liquidity, upper-wick, and swept-high invalidation blocks. The system defines entries only; it has no exit signal.
