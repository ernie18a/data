Exit or avoid long positions when either the short-term rate of change in the high-yield-bond-to-Treasury ratio signals a sharp decline or the short-term-to-mid-term volatility index ratio signals panic. Keep the market locked out until price closes above its trend average while the volatility ratio is calm, or a maximum lockout period expires. A milder credit decline or elevated volatility without panic signals caution and reduced exposure. No short-entry rule is defined.
