Transform each bar’s open, high, low, and close into oscillator values using the selected oscillator. Build volatility-scaled trailing bands from those oscillator values and maintain a trend direction: switch bullish when the oscillator high (or close, if wick checks are disabled) rises above the prior upper band; switch bearish when the oscillator low (or close) falls below the prior lower band. Enter or hold long while bullish, and close the long when bearish. The strategy is restricted to long entries, so bearish signals do not establish short positions. Apply the configured date window to entries; exits are not date-window restricted.
