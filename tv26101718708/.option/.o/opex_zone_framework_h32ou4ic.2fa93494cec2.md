While the weekly options levels are valid, use the instrument’s daily close to set its stance: below the put floor, go short toward deep support; from the put floor up to but below the gamma flip, stand aside; from the gamma flip up to but below the pin, favor buying dips with a stop below the gamma flip; at or above the pin, favor longs toward the three call resistance levels. Above the highest call resistance, treat the market as extended with no edge. Reassess on daily closes and stop using the levels after expiry.
