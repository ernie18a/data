On the traded instrument, select one moving-average pair: fast EMA versus medium EMA, fast EMA versus slow HMA, or medium EMA versus slow HMA. Enter long when the selected faster average crosses above the other and the instrument's close is above its higher-timeframe slow HMA. Enter short when it crosses below and the close is below that higher-timeframe average. Manage an open position with configured stop-loss and take-profit levels, optionally moving the stop to breakeven, taking a partial profit, or trailing it by ATR after the configured reward thresholds. The multi-symbol screener and alignment score are informational and do not affect trades.
