On a middle timeframe, combine directional votes from the traded instrument’s momentum, price-versus-fair-value stretch, and signed volume flow with votes from an implied-volatility trend (rising volatility votes short) and correlated leader markets (adjust for correlation direction). Weight each active vote by its historically measured forward edge, then take the sign of the weighted balance. Long or short only when the balance clears the conviction threshold, enough independent axes agree, and the direction is permitted by the net higher-timeframe votes; realized volatility and trend efficiency reduce conviction. Otherwise stand aside. No separate exit rule is defined.
