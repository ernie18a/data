Use the selected external sector or index’s price data to determine its trend. With RSI enabled, mark it bullish when RSI is above its upper threshold and bearish when RSI is below its lower threshold; between the thresholds, retain the previous trend. If an EMA filter is also enabled, require the fast EMA to be above the slow EMA for bullishness and below it for bearishness; otherwise retain the previous trend. With only the EMA filter enabled, mark bullish when the fast EMA is above the slow EMA and bearish otherwise. When the selected external trend is bullish, enter long on the traded instrument if flat. When it is bearish, exit the long. Never enter short.
