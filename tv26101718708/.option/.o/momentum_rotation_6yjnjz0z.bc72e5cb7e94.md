At the first trading day of each month, compare the recent multi-month returns of SPY, QQQ, and IWM, adding a fixed bonus to each asset above its long-term moving average and subtracting the same bonus from each below it. If the highest-scoring asset is above its moving average, close any existing position and go long that asset; resolve ties in the order SPY, QQQ, then IWM. If no asset qualifies, close all positions and stay in cash.
