Use a macro sentiment series built from Bitcoin supply relative to the combined market capitalization of DAI, USDT, and USDC, adjusted by Bitcoin’s price. Apply an RSI to this series, smooth it with a weighted moving average, and compare it with a second, slower weighted moving average. Enter long on an upward crossover while the smoothed RSI is in the oversold zone; enter short on a downward crossover while it is in the overbought zone. Exit a long on a downward crossover in the overbought zone, and exit a short on an upward crossover in the oversold zone. These signal-based exits apply only when stop-loss and take-profit orders are both disabled; otherwise, positions exit through the configured stop-loss or take-profit.
