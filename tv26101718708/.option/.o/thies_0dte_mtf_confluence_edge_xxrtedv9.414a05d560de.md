For each of four configured timeframes, compare SPY and ES: calculate each instrument’s volume relative to its own volume average, and measure signed pressure from relative volume weighted by where the close falls within the bar’s range, smoothed over time. Score the timeframe bullish when their average relative volume exceeds its configured activity threshold, their combined pressure is positive, and both close above their opens; score it bearish when activity exceeds the threshold, combined pressure is negative, and both close below their opens; otherwise score it neutral. Outside the configured regular trading session, set all timeframe scores to neutral. Sum the scores. A sustained total at or above the configured bullish threshold signals long; a sustained total at or below its negative signals short. The total must remain beyond the threshold for the configured number of chart bars, and the signal triggers when that sustained condition first becomes true. No exit rule is specified. VIX1D is displayed as volatility context and does not change the signals.
