During the permitted daily trading window, open short positions in an out-of-the-money call and put referenced to the underlying price. If the underlying reaches the call strike, cover the short call and open a larger long call; if it reaches the put strike, cover the short put and open a larger long put. For each repair position, track the simulated option value and close it if that value falls sufficiently from its peak. Close all positions when the strategy reaches its loss limit, or after reaching its profit target if profits subsequently retrace; also close at the daily cutoff. After a qualifying portfolio exit, wait through a cooldown before starting a new paired short cycle.
