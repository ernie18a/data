Build a smoothed composite of standardized macro-proxy movements: domestic and global equities, credit quality, short-term rates, yield-curve slope, the dollar, volatility, and Treasury-bill-versus-equity performance. Reverse the signs of credit quality, short-term rates, the dollar, volatility, and Treasury-bill-versus-equity measures so higher composite values indicate easier, risk-on conditions; combine the components with normalized equal or inverse-volatility weights, optionally limiting outliers. For the traded instrument, enter long when the composite crosses above its long trigger while rising and, if enabled, the instrument is above its moving average; also enter or maintain long when the composite exceeds its strong-easing floor. Enter short when the composite crosses below its short trigger while falling and, if enabled, the instrument is below its moving average. Exit a long when the composite crosses below its long exit level or a short signal occurs. Exit a short when the composite crosses above its short exit level, a long signal occurs, or the composite falls below its stress guard.
