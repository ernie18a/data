On each weekly bar after the configured start date, enter long when commercial net positioning increased for the traded pair’s base currency and decreased for its quote currency, the traded instrument’s EMA is rising, and each enabled auxiliary instrument’s EMA is rising (or falling when that auxiliary is set to inverse). Enter short when those positioning and EMA directions are reversed. COT, traded-instrument EMA, and each auxiliary confirmation can be disabled independently. Place a long stop at the current week’s low and a short stop at its high. Close open positions once their unrealized profit is positive.
