On each chart timeframe, average the open, high, low, and close of BTC candles from multiple exchanges. Derive a price source from the square root of the mean of half the squared averaged OHLC values. Compare it with bands around its moving average, whose width is a moving average of the averaged candle range. In long-only mode, enter long when the source crosses above the upper band and the averaged BTC candle closes above its open; exit long when the source crosses below the lower band. In two-sided mode, enter long on an upper-band crossover and short on a lower-band crossunder; an opposite entry reverses the position.
