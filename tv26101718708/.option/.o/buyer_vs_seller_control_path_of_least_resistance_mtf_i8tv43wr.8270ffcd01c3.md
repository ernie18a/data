For each selected timeframe, estimate signed buyer control by combining order-flow imbalance, cumulative flow, price versus session value and its slope, moving-average structure, and volume-weighted buying pressure; reduce its influence when strong pressure produces little price progress, and scale it by how well flow explains price movement. Rank the absolute control against its own recent history for strength. Fuse the selected timeframe controls, weighted by strength, into a consensus direction; reduce conviction when timeframe readings are correlated, when the chart is choppy, or during unusually turbulent conditions. The path is up or down by the consensus sign, and neutral when adjusted conviction is too weak. Enter long when the chart-timeframe control is positive and strong enough and newly aligns with an up path; enter short on the corresponding negative alignment with a down path. Also allow a bullish reversal when price falls while control rises, a bottom-exhaustion event occurs, and control momentum turns upward; allow a bearish reversal on rising price with falling control, top exhaustion, and downward-turning control momentum. Treat a neutral path as stand aside; no separate position-specific exit rule is defined. If configured to use a matching external futures instrument, derive the control and signal inputs from that instrument; benchmark relative strength and session measures affect signal grading, not direction.
