During the configured date window, when both entry and stop levels are set and the traded instrument has no open position, compute the ratio of the designated long asset’s close to the designated short asset’s close on the selected timeframe. If that ratio crosses the entry threshold in either direction, go long the designated long asset or short the designated short asset, according to which instrument is being traded. For an open long position, exit at the configured take-profit or stop-loss level. The source does not define an exit for short positions.
