Calculate three moving averages of the average of open, high, low, and close from a selected reference series: the traded instrument’s Heikin-Ashi candles, or, by default, the S&P 500; Nasdaq or the traded instrument’s own candles may be used instead. Enter long when the fastest average is above the middle average and the middle is above the slowest. Enter short when the fastest is below the middle and the middle is below the slowest. Close a long when the short ordering occurs; close a short when the long ordering occurs. Long and short entries can each be disabled independently.
