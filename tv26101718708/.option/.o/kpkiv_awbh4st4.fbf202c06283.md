For a call, calculate intrinsic value as the greater of zero and underlying spot high minus strike; for a put, use the greater of zero and strike minus underlying spot low. Add successive fixed increments to form ascending levels. When the traded instrument’s close crosses above one of the first six levels, go long and set the next level as the target and the prior candle’s low as the stop. Exit when the traded instrument’s high reaches the target or its close falls to or below the stop. There is no short signal.
