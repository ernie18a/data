Enter long when the traded instrument’s EMA is rising, its swing-breakout trend is bullish, ADX exceeds its threshold, and (if footprint filtering is enabled) the selected BTC or ETH footprint-delta average is positive. Enter short under the mirrored conditions with falling EMA, bearish swing-breakout trend, and negative benchmark delta. Missing footprint data does not block entry. If daily and current-timeframe trends agree, allow the entry; if daily trend is unknown, also allow it. When they oppose, block entry only while price lies in the middle band between the daily swing’s 38.2% and 61.8% retracement levels. For an allowed countertrend entry in the shallow retracement zone, set the first partial-profit target at the 38.2% boundary; otherwise use staged ATR-based profit targets. Exit on a configured percentage loss, or after price reaches a configured profit threshold and then returns to entry. Close the position when the swing-breakout trend reverses.
