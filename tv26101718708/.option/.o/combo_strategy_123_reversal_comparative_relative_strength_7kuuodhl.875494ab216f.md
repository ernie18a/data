Track the smoothed ratio of the traded instrument’s closing price to a benchmark’s closing price. Set the relative-strength state long when the ratio rises above an upper threshold, short when it falls below a lower threshold, and neutralize an existing state when the ratio crosses a middle threshold against it. Separately, set a reversal state long after two consecutive higher closes when the fast stochastic is below the slow stochastic and above its midpoint; set it short after two consecutive lower closes when the fast stochastic is above the slow stochastic and below its midpoint. Carry each state forward until its next signal. Signal long only when both states are long, short only when both are short, and otherwise neutral.
