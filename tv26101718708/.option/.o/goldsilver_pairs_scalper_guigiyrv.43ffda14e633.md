On synchronized Gold and Silver data, estimate their rolling return correlation and hedge beta from relative return volatility. Permit new trades only when correlation is sufficiently strong and beta is within a plausible positive range. Form a relative-value score by combining the standardized Silver return residual (Silver movement minus beta-adjusted Gold movement) with the inverse standardized Gold-to-Silver price ratio, so positive scores favor Gold and negative scores favor Silver. When the score reaches an extreme and turns back toward its mean, enter long Gold/short Silver for a positive extreme, or long Silver/short Gold for a negative extreme, using the estimated beta for hedge sizing. Close both legs when the score reverts near its mean, moves further against the entry by a volatility-scaled stop, correlation or beta becomes invalid, a maximum holding period expires, or leveraged losses exhaust allocated equity. After an exit, wait briefly before allowing another entry; halt trading after liquidation.
