When trading is enabled, calculate the same-timeframe price spread between the asset’s Coinbase USD market and Binance USDT market. Generate signals using the highest-priority enabled rule: (1) cross the fast and slow weighted moving averages of the spread; otherwise (2) cross the raw spread through a threshold; otherwise (3) cross a smoothed fast average of the spread through that threshold. An upward crossover enters long; a downward cross enters short when shorting is allowed, or closes the position in long-only mode. Apply any enabled date-window restriction to entries and long-only closes.
