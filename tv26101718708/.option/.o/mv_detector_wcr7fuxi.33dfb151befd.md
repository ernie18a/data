Go long on the traded stock when all of these hold: its price is above its 50-, 150-, and 200-day averages; those averages are ordered 50-day above 150-day above 200-day; the 200-day average is rising; the price is at least 75% of its 52-week high and at least 30% above its 52-week low; its ratio to the Nifty index is above its 50-day average and higher than it was 20 trading days earlier; its recent 20-day high-low range is less than 55% of its 60-day range; its ATR as a percentage of price is below its 50-day average; its price is no more than 15% above its 50-day average; it closes above the prior 20-day high; and its volume exceeds 1.5 times its 50-day average. The index comparison is the only decision condition using data outside the traded stock. No short or exit decision is specified.
