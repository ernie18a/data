For the traded stock, evaluate daily data. Require the configured uptrend conditions and quality filters to pass: the stock’s relative-strength rating, calculated against a broad-market benchmark from weighted multi-period returns, must meet its configured threshold; relative volume and any configured average-volume and dollar-volume floors must pass; and its sector ETF must be up on the day when that filter is enabled. When a qualifying EMA undercut-and-reclaim or near-EMA tap occurs, relaxed relative-strength and relative-volume thresholds may apply and the sector check is waived, while configured liquidity floors remain. A trigger is either a recent low below a monitored daily or weekly EMA followed by a close back above it (with a bullish reclaim candle if required), a near-EMA tap that holds above it, or a qualifying bullish pullback to a daily EMA. Mark long-ready when trend, effective quality, and an enabled trigger all pass; mark watch when trend and effective quality pass without a trigger; otherwise mark not ready. Candlestick patterns and setup scores are informational. No short or exit decisions are defined.
