In the rebalancing strategy, track the broad-market ETF’s drawdown from its all-time high. After it reaches a deep-drawdown threshold and later recovers by a specified fraction of that threshold, mark a market bottom and skip scheduled portfolio rebalances for the rest of that calendar year. Resume rebalancing the following year. At a rebalance, allocate more to the leveraged ETF if it has separately crossed its own drawdown trigger; otherwise use the base allocation. The other selectable strategies use only the traded instrument’s data for their entry or cash-deployment decisions.
