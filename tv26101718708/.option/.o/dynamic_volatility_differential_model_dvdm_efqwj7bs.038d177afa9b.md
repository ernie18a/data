For the traded instrument, compute realized volatility from its price returns and compare it with an external implied volatility measure; define the spread as implied volatility minus realized volatility. Enter long when the spread exceeds its positive dynamic threshold, and enter short when it falls below the corresponding negative threshold. Set each threshold to the rolling standard deviation of the spread multiplied by a chosen factor. Exit a long when the spread crosses upward through zero; exit a short when it crosses downward through zero.
