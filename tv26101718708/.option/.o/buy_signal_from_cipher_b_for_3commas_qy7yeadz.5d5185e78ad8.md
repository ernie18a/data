On a confirmed bar, enter long only when flat, the WaveTrend main line crosses above its signal line while the signal line is oversold, the traded instrument’s close is above its hourly simple moving average (or the prior close was above its prior hourly average), and BTC’s five-minute price is above its simple moving average (or its prior price was above its prior average). Set a percentage-based stop-loss and take-profit from the entry price; exit when either is reached. There are no short entries. An optional rule closes an open position when the traded instrument’s close is below its hourly average (or its prior close was below its prior average); this rule is disabled by default.
