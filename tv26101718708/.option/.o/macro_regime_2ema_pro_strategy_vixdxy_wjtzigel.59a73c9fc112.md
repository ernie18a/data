On each confirmed bar, classify each selected external macro series on the configured main and/or confirmation timeframes. A series votes risk-on when its value is below its moving average and the average is falling; it votes risk-off when its value is above its moving average and the average is rising; otherwise it is neutral. Combine the selected series by majority: allow longs when risk-on votes reach at least half the selected series and exceed risk-off votes; allow shorts symmetrically. Any conflicting-timeframe warning or tied votes block trades unless warning trades are enabled. Enter long when the traded instrument closes above both trend moving averages and the macro gate allows longs; enter short when it closes below both and the macro gate allows shorts. Depending on the configured entry mode, trigger when that full condition first becomes true or on each qualifying flat opportunity. While holding a position, close it if the opposite macro gate becomes allowed when opposite-regime exits are enabled. Initial and trailing stops, optional break-even and partial profit targets, and optional time or trend-filter exits also manage positions.
