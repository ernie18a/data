For the traded asset and a global liquidity composite, calculate each enabled momentum oscillator (rate of change, commodity channel index, relative strength index, money flow index, and/or Chande momentum oscillator) using each series’ own lookback and time lag. For each enabled oscillator, cast a vote for long when the traded asset’s lagged value exceeds the liquidity composite’s; otherwise vote short. Go long when the total votes are positive, short when negative, and take no action when they sum to zero or no oscillators are enabled.
