Classify the traded instrument, total crypto market capitalization, and Bitcoin dominance with the same reversal-and-structure signal process. Treat a confirmed short-class signal on a series as its bullish macro signal, and a confirmed long-class signal as its bearish macro signal. A local long or short signal is eligible only when it passes the enabled local filters. Enter long on an eligible local long; also enter long when bullish signals on both external series occur within the recent alignment window, even if the local long is absent. Enter short on an eligible local short; also enter short when bearish signals on both external series occur within that window, even if the local short is absent. For an eligible local long, classify the macro combination as premium long when both external signals are bullish, or altcoin-rally long when total market capitalization is bullish and Bitcoin dominance is bearish. For an eligible local short, classify it as crash short when both external signals are bearish, or safe-haven short when total market capitalization is bearish and Bitcoin dominance is bullish. On a new signal, do nothing if already in that direction; otherwise close any opposite position before entering. No separate flat-position exit signal is defined.
