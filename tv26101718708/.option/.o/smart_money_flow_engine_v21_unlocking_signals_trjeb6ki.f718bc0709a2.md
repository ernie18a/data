Enter long when the external volume feed confirms healthy higher-timeframe volume and the chart’s higher-timeframe trend is bullish; if reference-index confirmation is enabled, that index must also be bullish. The chart must additionally have bullish moving-average and VWAP alignment, bullish RSI within its allowed range, sufficient bullish structure/flow score, positive flow imbalance, and adequate trend strength. Require a bullish RSI threshold cross, bullish structure break, or bullish liquidity sweep as the trigger. Enter short using the mirrored bearish conditions, with the external volume feed confirming healthy volume and any enabled reference index bearish; require a bearish RSI threshold cross, bearish structure break, or bearish liquidity sweep. Only enter while flat, after the configured cooldown, within the daily signal cap, and, when enabled, during the allowed session; validate at bar close when required. The external volume feed is the configured volume symbol, or the detected futures feed for supported index symbols, and supplies both the volume-spike component of the flow score and the higher-timeframe volume gate. While long, exit on weak trend strength, a downward VWAP cross, bearish divergence, bearish liquidity sweep, dry external-feed volume, or an insufficient bullish score. While short, exit on weak trend strength, an upward VWAP cross, bullish divergence, bullish liquidity sweep, dry external-feed volume, or an insufficient bearish score. Confirm exits at bar close when required.
