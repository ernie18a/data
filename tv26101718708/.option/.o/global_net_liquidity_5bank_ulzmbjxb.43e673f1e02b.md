Use global net liquidity, calculated as the combined assets of the Fed, ECB, BoJ, PBoC, and BoE minus the US Treasury General Account and Fed reverse repos. If liquidity is rising and within 8% of its rolling cycle peak, favor long BTC; altcoin exposure is also acceptable. If liquidity is flat and remains well below its peak, favor BTC over altcoins and avoid treating a price rally alone as confirmation. If liquidity is falling, reduce risk and favor cash. This is a regime and allocation guide, not an explicit short or exit signal.
