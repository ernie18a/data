Build a smoothed directional score from the traded instrument’s price change and volume-derived buying/selling pressure. Enter long when the score crosses upward through a positive threshold, price is above a volatility-adjusted long-term trend band, and daily reference-market data confirms either rising price with rising open interest or falling price with falling open interest. Enter short when the score crosses downward through a negative threshold, price is below the lower trend band, and daily reference-market price falls while open interest rises. While long, exit if the score turns sufficiently negative, price crosses below the lower band, or unusually high reference-market open interest coincides with a negative score. While short, exit if the score turns sufficiently positive, price crosses above the upper band, or unusually high reference-market open interest coincides with a positive score.
