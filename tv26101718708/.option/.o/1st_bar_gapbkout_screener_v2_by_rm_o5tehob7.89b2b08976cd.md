For each watched instrument, detect whether its first daily candle gaps up (its low is above the previous candle’s high) or down (its high is below the previous candle’s low). Continue tracking it only if the second candle is fully inside the first candle’s range. On the third and fourth candles, report an upward breakout when the close is above the first candle’s high, a downward breakout when the close is below its low, or continued containment when the candle remains fully inside that range.
