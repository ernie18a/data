On each bar, calculate a smoothed risk score from weekly altcoin-to-Bitcoin data: combine normalized MACD, RSI, and price deviation from a long-term average, then adjust it using available market sentiment, macroeconomic PMI, and social-risk data. Repeatedly buy the altcoin when the score is at or below the configured buy levels, increasing the buy amount cumulatively as deeper levels are reached. While holding a long position, sell configured portions when the score reaches successive sell levels, requiring each earlier sell stage to have completed before the next. Any new buy resets the sell-stage progression. Otherwise, take no action; the strategy does not open short positions.
