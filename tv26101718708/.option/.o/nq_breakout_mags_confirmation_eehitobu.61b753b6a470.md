At the start of each trading day, build breakout levels from the traded instrument’s prior-day and prior-week highs and lows and recent hourly swing highs and lows, merging nearby levels and ignoring distant ones. Set directional bias from hourly structure: rising swing highs and lows favor longs; falling highs and lows favor shorts; mixed structure allows either. Optionally use a related external instrument as confirmation: derive its bias from hourly swings and track the direction of its latest confirmed break above or below its own prior-day or hourly swing levels. A long breakout is eligible only when the traded instrument’s bias permits longs and any enabled external bias or breakout filters agree; apply the corresponding inverse conditions to shorts. During the configured trading window, when no position is open and the daily trade limit has not been reached, enter long when a bar closes (or, if configured, trades) across a marked level upward by the configured breakout threshold; enter short on the corresponding downward crossing. Mark each triggered level as used, including breakouts rejected by filters. Set the stop beyond the breakout bar, subject to the configured minimum and maximum risk rules; skip the trade if the maximum-risk rule requires it. Set the profit target at the configured risk multiple. Exit at the stop or target; if both are touched on the same bar, count the result as a stop.
