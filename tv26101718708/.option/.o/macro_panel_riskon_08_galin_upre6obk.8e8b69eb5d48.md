Each day, score eight external market conditions: a broad-market ETF is above its short- and intermediate-term averages; a growth ETF is above its short-term average; a small-cap ETF is above its intermediate-term average; an equal-weight market ETF outperforms the broad-market ETF over the past month; a volatility index is below its calm threshold; a high-yield credit ETF is above its intermediate-term average; and the broad-market ETF has a positive one-month return. Give one point for each condition. Use higher scores to permit more long exposure and lower scores to reduce exposure, with no new longs at the lowest scores. Override the score and prohibit new entries whenever the broad-market ETF falls below its short-term average. The framework specifies no short or exit signal.
