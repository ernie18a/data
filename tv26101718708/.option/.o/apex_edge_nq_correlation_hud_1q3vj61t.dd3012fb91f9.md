For the traded instrument and each of seven configurable related equities, determine bullish or bearish confluence by majority vote across short- and higher-timeframe Hull trend, short- and higher-timeframe RSI relative to its midpoint, and price relative to a rolling range midpoint. Count an equity as aligned only when its rolling price correlation with the traded instrument meets a configurable threshold and its confluence matches the traded instrument’s bias. Signal long when the traded instrument is bullish, at least a configurable number of equities are aligned bullish, and the volatility index has bearish confluence. Signal short under the inverse conditions: bearish traded-instrument confluence, enough aligned bearish equities, and bullish volatility-index confluence. No exit condition is specified.
