Go long when the market benchmark’s short-term average is above its medium-term average and its momentum is oversold, while the traded instrument is in an upward pullback: its close is below its short-term average, above its medium-term average and long-term average, and its short-term average is above its medium-term average. Go short when the benchmark’s short-term average is below its medium-term average and its momentum is overbought, while the traded instrument is in the inverse downward pullback: its close is above its short-term average, below its medium-term and long-term averages, and its short-term average is below its medium-term average. No exit rule is defined.
