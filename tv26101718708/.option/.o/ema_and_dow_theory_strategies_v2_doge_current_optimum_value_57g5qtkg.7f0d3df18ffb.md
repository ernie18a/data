Track the traded instrument’s fast and slow EMAs, the external OTHERS.D index’s fast and slow EMAs, and swing structure on the traded instrument. Set the swing trend bullish when price closes above the latest confirmed swing high, and bearish when it closes below the latest confirmed swing low; retain the current swing trend otherwise. Enter long when the swing trend is bullish, the instrument’s fast EMA is above its slow EMA (or equal while the index’s fast EMA is below its slow EMA), RSI is below its overbought limit, and re-entry restrictions allow it. Enter short when the swing trend is bearish, the instrument’s fast EMA is below its slow EMA, RSI is above its oversold limit, and re-entry restrictions allow it. Close an opposing position when the swing trend reverses or when the entry signal calls for the opposite side. While a position is open, take partial profits at successive favorable price targets based on volatility; also scale out when the instrument’s trend color weakens while the position is profitable. Close on the configured loss threshold when the opposing entry signal is absent. Hold any remaining position until the swing trend reverses.
