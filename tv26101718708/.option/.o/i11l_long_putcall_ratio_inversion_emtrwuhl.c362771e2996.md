Use the daily put/call ratio, smooth it, and compare it with its rolling mean plus and minus a multiple of its rolling standard deviation. Enter long when the smoothed ratio crosses down through the upper band. There is no short-entry rule. Exit a long if the traded instrument’s low falls below its entry price by the stop-loss fraction, or its high rises above its entry price by the profit-target fraction. If short positions exist, close them when the smoothed ratio is above its rolling mean; the strategy has no rule that opens them.
