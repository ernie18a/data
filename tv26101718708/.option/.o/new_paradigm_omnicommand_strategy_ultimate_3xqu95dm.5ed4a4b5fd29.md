Use the relevant volatility index for the traded asset to estimate a daily move from that asset’s daily open, creating upper and lower range walls. Enter long when price crosses above the chosen moving average, volume is unusually high, the trading window is open, trend strength is sufficient, price is above the hourly 50-period EMA, momentum is not overextended, and price remains clear of the upper wall. Enter short on the mirrored conditions: cross below the moving average, price below the hourly EMA, momentum permits further downside, and price remains clear of the lower wall. Exit positions at the configured profit target or stop; flatten all positions outside the trading window when the time filter is enabled.
