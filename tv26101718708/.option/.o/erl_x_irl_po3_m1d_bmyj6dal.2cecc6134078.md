Build the higher-timeframe dealing range on the traded instrument. Long setup: price wicks below the range low, reclaims it, and then closes back above the latest internal swing high with sufficient displacement; at least one correlated peer must hold its corresponding sellside level. Short setup: mirror these conditions at the range high and latest internal swing low, with at least one peer holding its corresponding buyside level. Invalidate a setup if price remains beyond the raided range edge, reaches the opposite range edge before the structure shift, or closes back through the sweep extreme after confirmation. If peers reach the opposite range edge while the traded instrument has not, treat the instrument as the laggard; a subsequent fair value gap in the setup direction confirms catch-up. The indicator provides setup context rather than an entry signal.
