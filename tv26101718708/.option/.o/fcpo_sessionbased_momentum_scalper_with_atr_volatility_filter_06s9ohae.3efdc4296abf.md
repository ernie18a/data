During configured trading sessions on enabled weekdays, allow new trades only when outside configured exclusion windows, below the daily trade limit, not in a loss-pause period, and within the monthly profit and loss limits. Require recent true-range volatility to lie within configured bounds and the current candle to have a sufficiently large body relative to both its full range and a minimum size. Enter long on a qualifying bullish candle or short on a qualifying bearish candle, subject to the enabled direction setting. If enabled, also require a long close above the descending line through recent pivot highs or a short close below the ascending line through recent pivot lows; when a valid line is unavailable, do not block the trade. Set a profit target and stop loss from the entry price. Exit at either level, after the configured maximum holding time, or at the configured end-of-day exit time. Track closed-trade results: pause trading for a day after two consecutive losses, and block new entries when the monthly profit or loss limit is reached until the next month. The moving average is display context only and does not affect decisions.
