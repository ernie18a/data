For an enabled instrument with valid externally supplied gamma-zone boundaries, evaluate 2- and 5-minute closes. Go long when price crosses back above the lower boundary after closing below it, subject to optional positive market-breadth confirmation and optional volume-exhaustion confirmation; or when price breaks above the upper boundary after having closed below the lower boundary within a recent lookback, subject to optional positive breadth and expanding volume. Go short when price crosses down through the upper boundary after closing above it, or breaks below the lower boundary after having closed above the upper boundary within a recent lookback; require negative breadth confirmation when enabled and expanding volume when enabled. Separately, a 2-minute close crossing above an externally supplied long-entry level signals a long entry, and crossing below an externally supplied short-entry level signals a short entry. Preliminary alerts mark rising or falling price entering the zone after a recent visit beyond the corresponding boundary, with breadth and volume confirmation; they are setups rather than entries. No exit decision is defined.
