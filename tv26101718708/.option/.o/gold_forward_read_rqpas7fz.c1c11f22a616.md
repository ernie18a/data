For gold, infer each external market’s direction of influence from its recent correlation with gold: dollar and yields default to inverse, while oil counts only when a meaningful link is established. Standardize their short-term moves and map them into gold-bullish or gold-bearish pushes. If dollar and yield both move strongly but point in opposite directions, make no directional decision. Combine aligned active pushes, including oil only when linked, and compare the result with gold’s own short-term move. Go long when the bullish push exceeds gold’s move by a meaningful amount, and short when the bearish push exceeds gold’s move by a meaningful amount; suppress either signal if the active push is opposed by a meaningful 5-minute or 15-minute push, or if recent dollar-gold correlation has turned positive.
