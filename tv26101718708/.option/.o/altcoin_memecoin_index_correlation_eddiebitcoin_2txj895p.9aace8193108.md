For the traded asset, compare a fast EMA with a slow EMA; for the selected broad altcoin or meme sector, compare the same type of EMAs on that sector’s market-cap share of total crypto market capitalization. Enter or reverse long when both fast EMAs are above their slow EMAs; enter or reverse short when both are below. If the sector filter is disabled, use only the asset’s EMA comparison. Close an open position when its loss or gain crosses the configured stop-loss or take-profit threshold. A stop-loss close is withheld when the opposite-direction entry condition is already confirmed; otherwise, block re-entry in that same direction until its trend alignment improves. When a profitable position loses full asset-and-sector bullish alignment (long) or bearish alignment (short), close a configured portion if the opposite entry condition is absent. Only open trades within the enabled date window and while equity exceeds the minimum capital floor.
