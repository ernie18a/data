Enter long on a confirmed buy signal when the trend and timing scores meet their buy criteria and enough bullish confirmation layers align. Confirmation includes a bullish higher-timeframe trend and relative strength versus a benchmark: the instrument’s benchmark-relative price is above a rising average, with momentum above its floor. Block repeat entries while a trade is active and respect the signal cooldown. A warning is issued during a trade if price loses its trend average, becomes overextended, shows distribution or a trap, or momentum fades. Exit during a trade if price falls below its stop, loses the base trend average while momentum is negative, or weakens below the momentum floor while under its trend average. The rules generate long signals only; they do not generate short entries.
