For three correlated instruments, track each instrument’s high and low within successive six-hour quarters anchored at 18:00 New York time. After a prior quarter is complete, compare each current quarter’s running extremes with that instrument’s prior-quarter extremes. On a confirmed bar, if at least one but not all three instruments have exceeded their own prior-quarter highs, signal short on the traded instrument. If at least one but not all three have fallen below their own prior-quarter lows, signal long. Emit each direction at most once per quarter. No exit rule is specified.
