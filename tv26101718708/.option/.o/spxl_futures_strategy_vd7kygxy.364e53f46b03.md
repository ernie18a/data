At the end of the session, assess the external equity-index futures for the traded instrument. In the default mode, calculate each of the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow, and Russell 2000 futures ratings by averaging bullish/bearish votes from price versus multiple moving averages and an Ichimoku trend test on the selected multi-hour timeframe. Lower each index’s sell threshold when the S&P futures’ daily RSI and money-flow readings are both overbought. Sell when any index rating is at or below its threshold, or when all of these volatility/breadth conditions hold: front VIX futures’ roll yield is low, the trend in the share of stocks above their long-term average is falling, either VVIX or VXX is high within its Bollinger range, and SKEW is not at an extreme. Otherwise, buy only when every index passes its threshold test: its current and prior ratings are above threshold, or its current rating is nonnegative. In the alternate trend mode, sell if any of the four futures is below its long-term exponential average; buy if all four are above. A sell signal opens a short position when shorting is enabled; otherwise it closes positions. A buy signal opens a long position.
