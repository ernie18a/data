For the traded instrument, open long when its volatility-normalized fast-versus-slow weighted-price oscillator crosses upward through its lower one-standard-deviation band, provided the higher-timeframe price structure is bullish (price above the faster average and that average above the slower average), RSI is not overbought, MACD momentum is not weakening, money flow is not materially negative, volume is sufficient, and the halving-cycle reading is not strongly adverse. Also require stablecoin dominance to be falling or not rising, dominance to favor the traded instrument (BTC dominance rising for BTC, falling for other assets), and CME futures basis not to indicate crowded leveraged longs. Open short on the mirrored oscillator cross downward through the upper band, with bearish higher-timeframe structure, RSI not oversold, weakening MACD momentum, nonpositive money flow, sufficient volume, a nonstrongly bullish halving reading, stablecoin dominance rising or not falling, dominance not favoring the instrument, and no stablecoin-dominance dry-powder condition. Close a long when the oscillator crosses below zero, or when RSI crosses down out of overbought territory while higher-timeframe structure is bearish, or when a short entry triggers. Close a short on the mirrored zero cross, or when RSI crosses up out of oversold territory while higher-timeframe structure is bullish, or when a long entry triggers. The engine also gives directional forecast guidance when its composite projection is consistently above or below current price at near and farther horizons and its external-market consensus confidence is adequate; this guidance is not an entry signal.
