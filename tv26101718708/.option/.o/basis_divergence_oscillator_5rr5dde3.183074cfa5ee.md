Use the traded instrument’s price and its cash/spot reference to calculate the price-minus-reference basis, then standardize that basis over a rolling window. On confirmed price swing highs, signal short for regular bearish divergence when price makes a higher high but the standardized basis makes a sufficiently lower high; signal short for hidden bearish divergence when price makes a lower high but the basis makes a sufficiently higher high. On confirmed swing lows, signal long for regular bullish divergence when price makes a lower low but the basis makes a sufficiently higher low; signal long for hidden bullish divergence when price makes a higher low but the basis makes a sufficiently lower low. Compare only successive pivots within the configured maximum separation; require a minimum basis-oscillator difference scaled by its variability, and for regular divergences require the bearish pivot to be overbought or the bullish pivot oversold when that filter is enabled. Also signal short when the basis oscillator confirms a local peak in its upper extreme zone, and long when it confirms a local trough in its lower extreme zone. Signals become available when their pivots are confirmed. The framework defines no exit rule.
