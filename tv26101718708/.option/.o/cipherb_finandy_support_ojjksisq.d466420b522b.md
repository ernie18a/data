When the global market filter is enabled, use the BTC perpetual market as a regime gate for trades on the chart instrument. Permit a long entry only when the instrument’s own long setup and other enabled entry filters qualify, the instrument is above its local trend average, and BTC’s current or previous completed bar is above its corresponding long-term simple moving average. Permit a short entry only when the instrument’s own short setup and other enabled filters qualify, the instrument is below its local trend average, and BTC’s current or previous completed bar is below that moving average. Entries require no open position and a confirmed bar. The BTC regime does not independently trigger entries or exits.
