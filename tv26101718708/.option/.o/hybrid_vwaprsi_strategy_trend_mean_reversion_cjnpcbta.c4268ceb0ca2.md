Use a broad-market benchmark’s daily trend to filter trades in the traded instrument. Go long when the benchmark is above its long-term moving average and the instrument is below its session-anchored VWAP, has an oversold momentum reading, and shows elevated volatility. Go short when the benchmark is at or below its moving average and the instrument is above VWAP, has an overbought momentum reading, and shows elevated volatility. Exit either position with a trailing stop whose distance is based on the traded instrument’s volatility.
