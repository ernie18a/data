On confirmed bars, generate a directional setup from a liquidity sweep that rejects a prior swing level, an inverted fair value gap, or a live order block, with candle direction aligned and displacement required when enabled. Sweep entries also require price not to have moved too far from the relevant swing. A bullish SMT occurs when the instrument makes a lower pivot low while at least one comparison instrument fails to make a lower low; bearish SMT is the reverse at pivot highs. Count directional confluences: a confirmed setup, recent same-direction SMT, recent same-direction sweep, matching market-structure shift or trend, overlap with a matching gap or order block, same-direction consecutive-close confirmation, location in discount for longs or premium for shorts, and close on the matching side of the New York open. Emit a lower-grade long or short when the count reaches its configured minimum but stays below the higher-grade threshold; require a confirmed setup, chop filter, and directional cooldown. Emit a higher-grade signal when the count reaches its higher threshold and also passes its session gate, chop filter, optional volume filter, directional premium/discount rule when blocking is enabled, and cooldown. The chop filter compares short-term volatility with its longer baseline; the optional volume filter compares current volume with its average. Risk levels are plotted from the signal close using volatility-scaled stop distance and reward multiples; the display is invalidated when the stop or first target is touched, but no separate exit signal is generated.
