For each enabled timeframe no shorter than the chart timeframe, evaluate only after a candle closes. On a chart showing one of the three correlated instruments, compare the three instruments’ candle directions: signal long when exactly one closes up and the other two close down; signal short when exactly one closes down and the other two close up. Ignore the signal if any candle is unchanged. Suppress either signal when the traded instrument is in an accumulation zone, defined by a recent window containing enough bars whose volume is elevated relative to its moving average and whose high-low range is compressed relative to ATR. No exit rule is specified.
