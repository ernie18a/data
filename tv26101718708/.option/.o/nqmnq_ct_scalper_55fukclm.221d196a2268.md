Trade the instrument counter to its short-term trend only when price is sufficiently stretched from its moving-average and VWAP means and a weighted exhaustion score clears a context-adjusted threshold. For a long, require a directional downtrend; for a short, an uptrend. Also require adequate volume and volatility, sufficient ADX, a directional confidence score, and no strong higher-timeframe trend against the reversal. Block the fade when opposing hidden RSI divergence is active. Build confidence and exhaustion from mean distance, RSI/MFI extremes, Bollinger and VWAP-band extremes, volume climax or drying up, wick or candle rejection, fading or flipping squeeze momentum, cumulative-delta divergence or reversal, order-flow imbalance or absorption, liquidity-sweep rejection, swing-structure reversal, NQ-versus-ES or NQ-versus-SPY divergence, market breadth, and higher-timeframe momentum fading. Weight and required evidence vary with session, trend regime, ADX, persistence or mean-reversion measures, and higher-timeframe alignment. Enter through one of six confirmation paths: general exhaustion, price/delta divergence, rejected liquidity sweep, aggressive opposing order flow, absorption, or a retest of a previously formed high-volume zone. Each path must satisfy its own directional confirmation count, adjusted upward when momentum still strongly opposes the fade and in selected sessions; take at most one entry per direction per bar, and require the projected reversion target to offer adequate room. Close an open trade when its target or stop is reached on a later bar. The additional pattern-based exits are effectively disabled because their required score exceeds the maximum possible score.
