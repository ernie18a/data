Use the underlying’s price, volume, volatility, VWAP, and higher-timeframe trend to generate directional entries. Go long on an upside break of the opening range or open-anchored volatility band, or during the late-day window when the first-session move was bullish and volatile, or the day’s return is strongly positive; apply corresponding downside rules for shorts. Require the relevant session window, elevated relative volume where specified, VWAP confirmation for momentum entries, and a minimum signal-quality grade. Block early trades that oppose a significant opening gap. Limit entries with a cooldown and stop new entries after the cutoff. Exit at the target, stop, holding-time limit, or hard end-of-day cutoff; after the first target, move the stop to entry.
