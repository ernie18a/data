On each bar, score six directional signals: MACD and its signal line are bullish/bearish only when both are above/below zero; compare the moving average of closes with the moving average of opens; compare current close with its lookback close; compare close with a long-term moving average; compare OBV with its moving average; and compare the instrument’s price ratio to an external benchmark with that ratio’s moving average. Omit the benchmark vote when disabled or when the instrument is Bitcoin. Sum the votes. While flat, enter long when the sum meets the configured positive threshold for the required consecutive bars, realized volatility is within its configured cap, and price extension above its long-term average is within the buy limit. While long, exit when the sum meets the corresponding negative threshold for the required consecutive bars, volatility is within the cap, and price extension is above the configured sell floor. Do not open short positions or repeat entries while already long. Classify an eligible entry as full size when its score exceeds the entry threshold by the configured margin and extension is below the full-size limit; otherwise classify it as half size.
