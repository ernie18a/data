Track the traded instrument and correlated instruments over recurring structural cycles. A bullish signal occurs when cycle lows diverge: the traded instrument makes a lower low while a comparator makes a higher low, or the traded instrument makes a higher low while a comparator makes a lower low. Use either close-based cycle lows versus the preceding cycle or wick lows versus eligible prior cycles, according to the selected signal mode. A bearish signal occurs when cycle highs diverge in either direction between the traded instrument and the primary comparator, using the corresponding close-based or wick-based comparison. When the premium/discount filter is enabled, allow bullish signals only when both compared traded-instrument lows are below the active true open, and bearish signals only when both compared highs are above it. Treat a bullish signal as invalidated if the price series that established its lower-low side breaks below that signal low; treat a bearish signal as invalidated if the series that established its higher-high side breaks above that signal high.
