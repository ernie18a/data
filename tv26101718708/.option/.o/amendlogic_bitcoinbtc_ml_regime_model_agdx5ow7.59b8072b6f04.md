Estimate the probability of an upward move from normalized short-, medium-, and long-term returns, RSI, distance from trend averages, volume and volatility, plus DXY and inflation-protected Treasury fund trends and their interaction with momentum. Update a logistic model walk-forward using subsequent price direction as the label. Use its probability only after sufficient training and when recent directional accuracy is adequate; otherwise use a fallback bullish/bearish score based on the traded asset’s higher-timeframe trend, dollar trend, and Treasury fund trend, with the asset trend weighted more heavily. Optionally blend in a short-term momentum-and-volume score. Block new entries during abnormal return, volume, or volatility conditions. Enter long when the final upward probability crosses a bullish threshold and the higher-timeframe trend agrees if that filter is enabled; enter short when it crosses a bearish threshold with the trend agreeing. Exit a long when probability falls below its exit threshold, and exit a short when it rises above its exit threshold. Apply a minimum holding period to position changes; retain the position otherwise.
