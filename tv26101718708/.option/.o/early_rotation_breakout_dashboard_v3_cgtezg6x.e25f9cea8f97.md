For each stock, compare its recent performance with its industry ETF, and compare the industry with its sector ETF and the sector with the broad-market benchmark. Treat the broader trend as supportive when both benchmark comparisons are positive, or as building when both are improving and neither is materially negative. Favor a long when the stock’s relative strength versus its industry is positive or improving and price breaks above a recent high; stronger breakout confirmation also requires supportive broader trends, positive relative strength, and elevated volume. An emerging long setup occurs when price is near a recent high, relative strength is improving, price is above its trend average, and the broader trend is supportive. Maintain a long trend after a qualifying major breakout; consider a later breakout a renewed long opportunity when a higher, compressed base has formed. Exit the trend when price falls materially below the original major breakout level and below its trend average. The indicator defines no short decision.
