For the traded instrument, calculate a price-momentum oscillator as the difference between a fast and slow weighted moving average, normalized by average true range, and its rolling standard deviation. Go long when the oscillator crosses upward through its lower one-standard-deviation band, provided the higher-timeframe trend is bullish (price above the medium average and that average above the long average), RSI is below its overbought threshold, the MACD histogram is rising or unchanged, money flow is not materially negative, volume is sufficiently near or above its recent average, and the time-based Bitcoin halving-cycle bias is not strongly bearish. Go short when the oscillator crosses downward through its upper one-standard-deviation band, provided the higher-timeframe trend is bearish (price below the medium average and that average below the long average), RSI is above its oversold threshold, the MACD histogram is falling or unchanged, money flow is not materially positive, volume is sufficiently near or above its recent average, and the halving-cycle bias is not strongly bullish. Exit a long when the oscillator crosses below zero; exit a short when it crosses above zero. The additional RSI-based exit clauses are internally contradictory and therefore cannot trigger. Forecasted oscillator crossings and confidence or urgency labels do not alter these entry and exit conditions.
