For the traded stock, compare its weighted multi-period price performance with the equal-weighted average performance of the DAX, MDAX, SDAX, and TecDAX indices. Classify relative strength as strong when it exceeds a positive threshold, positive when above zero but below that threshold, improving when below zero but its smoothed value is rising, and weak otherwise. Count high-volume down days over a recent lookback; the volume filter passes when the count is within its allowed maximum. Mark the setup as favorable when relative strength is strong and the volume filter passes with room below its maximum; mark it watchlist-quality when the filter passes and strength is positive, improving, or strong exactly at the maximum; otherwise mark it unfavorable. These are screening states, not explicit long, short, or exit decisions.
