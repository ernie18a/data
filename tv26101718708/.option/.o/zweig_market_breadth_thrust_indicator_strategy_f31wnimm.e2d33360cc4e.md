For the traded instrument, calculate a smoothed market breadth ratio from the selected exchange’s daily advancing and declining issues: advancing issues divided by the sum of advancing and declining issues. For a combined market, average the corresponding counts across the included exchanges before calculating the ratio. Enter long when the ratio crosses above a lower threshold. While long and the instrument is above its average entry price, partially exit when the ratio crosses down below an upper threshold; close the full position when it crosses down below the lower threshold.
