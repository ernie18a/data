When usable open-interest data for the instrument is available, use its change and rate of change; otherwise use the instrument’s volume as a participation proxy if enabled. Combine normalized volatility expansion, range expansion, candle-body expansion, participation change and acceleration, and price distance from a slower trend baseline into a cascade-pressure score. Require that score to exceed its configured threshold and persist over the configured confirmation window. If price shows strong downside displacement below its fast and slow baselines, or a failed upward move, while sufficiently below the slow baseline, classify downside liquidation risk and take a short bias. If price shows strong upside displacement above both baselines, or a failed downward move, while sufficiently above the slow baseline, classify upside liquidation risk and take a long bias. If pressure exceeds the threshold without either directional condition, classify two-way risk and take no directional position. When pressure is below its threshold but a configured cooling measure indicates easing after recent elevated pressure, classify risk as cooling; otherwise remain neutral. The indicator defines no entry, exit, or position-management rule, so cooling or neutral alone does not instruct an exit.
