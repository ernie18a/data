On the traded instrument, when flat and outside the post-trade cooldown, enter after a confirmed bar if the external volatility index is above a minimum level and either its rise from the previous confirmed daily close exceeds a threshold or its recent rate of change exceeds a threshold. Optionally require the short-dated volatility index to exceed the longer-dated one; optionally restrict entries to specified sessions. Enter long, or short if reverse-signal mode is enabled. Optionally, when the volatility index has fallen sufficiently below its previous confirmed daily close, enter short (long in reverse-signal mode), provided the main spike condition is absent and the same flat, cooldown, and session requirements hold. For spike trades, reduce the position when the volatility rise retreats below an initial threshold, then exit the remainder when it retreats to a tighter reversion threshold; also exit at a fixed percentage stop or profit target, or after a maximum holding period. For volatility-crush trades, exit after a maximum holding period or when the main spike condition returns. Reverse-signal mode reverses entry direction and keeps stop and target levels direction-aware.
