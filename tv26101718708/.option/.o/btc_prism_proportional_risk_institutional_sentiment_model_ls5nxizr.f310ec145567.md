For the traded asset, combine normalized measures of its price trend and momentum with external on-chain activity and holder behavior, stablecoin buying power, and spot ETF demand into an aggregate risk score and smooth it. Treat a cross below the smoothed score while the aggregate is elevated, or a downturn in the smoothed score while the aggregate is extremely elevated, as an exit signal. Keep the defensive state active until the aggregate cools below a lower threshold. Repeatedly elevated readings within a recent window also flag exhaustion. No long or short entry rule is specified.
