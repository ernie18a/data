Compute Hull moving averages using hourly prior-bar OHLC4 for DXY, XAUAUD, and BTCUSD, all with the same configurable length. Also compute the traded instrument’s daily OHLC4 percentage change from the prior day. Enter long when DXY’s fast Hull is below its slow Hull, XAUAUD’s is above its slow Hull, BTCUSD’s is above its slow Hull, the daily change exceeds a positive threshold, and no position is open. Enter short when those three Hull comparisons are respectively above, below, and below, the daily change is below the positive threshold, and no position is open. Close a long when DXY’s fast Hull rises above its slow Hull and the daily change is below the threshold. Close a short when DXY’s fast Hull falls below its slow Hull and the daily change is above the threshold. A Hull average is a weighted moving average of twice the half-length weighted moving average minus the full-length weighted moving average, smoothed by a weighted moving average over the square root of the length.
