For the traded instrument, calculate a macro reference from the average of the lookback price ratios for oil and gold and the inverse dollar-index ratio, scaled by the instrument’s own price from the same lookback. Go long when the instrument crosses above this reference or shows buying absorption (estimated buying volume exceeds its average-volume threshold and the close is above the candle midpoint), provided price is above its trend average and money flow is above its bullish threshold. Go short when it crosses below the reference or shows selling absorption (estimated selling volume exceeds its average-volume threshold and the close is below the candle midpoint), provided price is below its trend average and money flow is below its bearish threshold. No exit rule is specified.
