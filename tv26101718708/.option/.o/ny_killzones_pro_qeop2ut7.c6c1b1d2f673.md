Compare the traded instrument’s highs and lows with one or two comparison instruments over the same session ranges or intraday cycle ranges. Signal short when either instrument makes a higher high beyond the prior range’s high while another does not; signal long when either makes a lower low beyond the prior range’s low while another does not. For cycle comparisons, use the nearest earlier completed range from the same day whose corresponding liquidity level has not already been swept. Ignore the signal if bullish and bearish divergences occur together. Confirm session signals when the session ends and cycle signals when the cycle ends; a signal may be shown while its range is still forming. Remove a confirmed signal if price on the traded instrument closes back through its triggering extreme or the signal expires. No separate position exit rule is defined.
