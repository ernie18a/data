Choose the benchmark ETF for the traded instrument’s sector, falling back to a broad-market ETF if no sector mapping is available. Go long when the instrument’s fast EMA crosses above its slow EMA and its RSI is above the neutral midpoint, provided the benchmark is above its own EMA. Go short when the fast EMA crosses below the slow EMA and RSI is below the neutral midpoint, provided the benchmark is below its own EMA. No exit rule is defined.
