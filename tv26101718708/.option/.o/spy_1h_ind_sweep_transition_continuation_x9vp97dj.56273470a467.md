On confirmed bar closes, enter at most one position while flat and outside the post-exit cooldown; prefer long over short when both qualify. Long and short setups are mirror images. A long may qualify through any of three routes:

1. Reversal: Price sweeps below a prior major or tactical daily low, or the lower expected-move band, then closes back above that level. Within a short confirmation window, require the weighted confluence score to meet its trend-aligned threshold (higher when countertrend). Confluence includes higher-timeframe trend alignment, a break of confirmed or internal structure, reclaim of the daily open or intraday VWAP, strong relative volume, directional displacement, expanding realized volatility, and an extreme VIX move. Only a closed-bar setup qualifies.
2. Transition: Price crosses back above the daily fast trend average after being below it. While that reclaim remains active, require a non-strongly-falling average, no established opposing higher-timeframe trend, a recent bullish structure break, limited extension from the average, and sufficient confluence from regime, recent structure, displacement, relative volume, and expanding volatility. Qualification permits entry during a brief subsequent execution window.
3. Continuation: In a bullish regime, price breaks above a recent range high while above a rising daily fast average. Require a structure break, limited extension, and sufficient score from regime, swing structure, displacement, relative volume, expanding volatility, and the VIX condition.

For shorts, reverse the price, level, trend, reclaim, structure, and extension directions. A bearish transition requires a non-strongly-rising average and no established opposing bullish higher-timeframe trend. VIX extreme/compression confluence applies in the direction specified by the corresponding setup logic.

If multiple routes qualify in the same direction, prioritize reversal, then transition, then continuation. Do not enter the opposite direction on the same bar as a selected long. Reject an entry unless its route-specific structural stop is on the protective side of entry and its risk is positive and within the daily-volatility risk cap. Reversal stops sit beyond the sweep extreme; continuation stops sit beyond the broken range, blended with the relevant swing; transition stops also account for the daily fast average. Long and short entries use the corresponding inverse stop logic.

While in a position, exit if price reaches the initial stop. Also exit on a confirmed opposing swing break beyond the protected structure, when enabled. After the minimum holding period, exit if an activated trailing stop is hit; the trail follows favorable price extremes by a volatility distance and may be tightened to confirmed swing structure. If enabled, a risk-multiple profit target is another exit. Activate the runner after favorable movement reaches the configured risk multiple.
