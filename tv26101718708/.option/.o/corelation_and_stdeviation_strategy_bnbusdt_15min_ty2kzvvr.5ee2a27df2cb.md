Long-only; no short entries. Build a composite score by summing contributions from price, trend, momentum, volatility, and volume indicators, including an external market advance/decline series. For each indicator, multiply its rolling standard deviation by its rolling correlation with a smoothed traded price and, except for the trend-stop measures, by the rolling standard deviation of its deviation from its short moving average. From the composite, derive its price-correlation product and its volatility weighted by that correlation, then apply the same correlation-and-volatility calculation to these derived series. Also calculate the composite’s two-bar change and its rate of change, the corresponding changes and rates for the correlation product, and the first difference of the change in volatility of the volatility-weighted series. A setup requires the smoothed traded price to be falling. Define six setups: (1) the derived volatility of the volatility-weighted series crosses below zero; (2) the next-level derived volatility crosses zero in either direction; (3) the rates of change of the composite, correlation product, and volatility-weighted series all cross zero; (4) their two-bar changes all cross zero; (5) the correlation product, volatility-weighted series, and composite two-bar change all cross zero; or (6) the composite two-bar change crosses each of the correlation product’s two-bar change and the volatility-weighted series’ two-bar change, and those latter two changes cross each other. Within the enabled trading window, open a long from flat on setup 1, 2, or 3 only when the configured entry-price source is below the detected rolling swing low. While long, add positions on setups 1–4 when the smoothed price is below the average entry price and the open-trade count is within the earlier portion of its allowed range; in the later portion, add only on setup 6 when the smoothed price is below average entry price. Close the long when a separate Supertrend turns bearish, or while bearish when any setup occurs, provided the profit target line is below the smoothed price and both a prior rolling swing high and a swing low are available. An optional partial exit is also triggered when a short moving average exceeds the lowest recorded entry price by the configured intermediate profit margin, intermediate selling is enabled, both swing points are available, and average entry price is above the smoothed price.
