Use PVSRA candle classifications calculated from ETH/USDT volume and OHLC as external signals, alongside the traded instrument’s EMAs and price. Go long on a confirmed green PVSRA candle opening above the intermediate EMA when a red PVSRA candle previously opened and closed below that EMA and at least one red PVSRA candle closed below it. The entry is allowed whether the close is below or above the long-term EMA. Set the stop at the recent rolling low of ETH/USDT and the target at the entry close plus the distance from that close to the stop, scaled by the chosen risk/reward setting. Close the long if, on the bar after an ETH/USDT blue or green PVSRA candle opened above both EMAs, a red PVSRA candle opens and closes above the long-term EMA, provided at least one green PVSRA candle closed above that EMA. Also close on the bar after entry if the PVSRA candle is red or violet, or on the bar after the sell setup if it is green. The strategy has no short entry.
