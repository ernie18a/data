Also require the SPX stale-high filter to pass: after an extended period without a new all-time high, SPX must be sufficiently above its 200-day EMA. Enter only while flat and within the configured start-date range. Never enter short.

Track SPX’s all-time high. If SPX falls far enough below it, arm a recovery hold; while holding a position, block normal exits until SPX recovers the relevant pre-drop high. For a losing position, exit when QQQ closes below both the entry-time QQQ close and its 21-day EMA, while SPX closes below its 7-day average. For a winning position, exit if either SPX shows a bearish short-versus-long average condition and closes below its short average while QQQ is below its 21-day EMA, or QQQ’s low falls below the entry-time QQQ price by the configured loss allowance and below its 50-day average. Independently exit if the traded instrument falls by the configured hard-stop percentage from its average entry price, or account equity falls by the configured maximum percentage from its peak during the trade; these risk exits override the recovery hold.
