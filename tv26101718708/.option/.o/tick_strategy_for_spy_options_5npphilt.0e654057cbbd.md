On confirmed bars during the configured trading window, enter long when the market-wide TICK index’s Hull moving average crosses above its smoothed line, its short-term exponential average is positive, the traded instrument’s RSI average is above its midpoint while RSI remains below its upper bound, volatility index data exceeds its configured threshold, and the instrument closes above its exponential moving average. A recent qualifying long signal can also trigger an entry while flat if price is above that moving average. Enter short on the mirrored conditions: TICK Hull average crosses below its smoothed line, its short-term exponential average is negative, RSI average is below its midpoint while RSI remains above its lower bound, volatility exceeds the threshold, and price is below its exponential moving average. Close longs on a bearish TICK/RSI trend change or bearish price-versus-TICK divergence; close shorts on the bullish equivalents. Apply a protective stop for longs below the prior bar’s low and for shorts above the prior bar’s high, with distance adjusted by a fraction of daily average true range. Close all positions near the end of the trading day.
