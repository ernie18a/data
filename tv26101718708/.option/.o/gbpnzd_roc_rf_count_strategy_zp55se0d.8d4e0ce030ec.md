For each of six currency pairs (including the traded pair), determine whether its rate of change is positive or negative; assign a positive vote for rising momentum and a negative vote for falling momentum, reversing the vote for EUR/GBP. Sum the votes, smooth the total with a short moving average and then a second smoother. Go long when this smoothed score crosses above zero; close the short and go long. Go short when it crosses below zero; close the long and go short. Optional configured profit, loss, and trailing stops can exit either position.
