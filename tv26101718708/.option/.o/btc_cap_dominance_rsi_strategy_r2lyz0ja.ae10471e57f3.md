On each completed bar, use external crypto market data to calculate Bitcoin market capitalization as total crypto market capitalization minus crypto market capitalization excluding Bitcoin, and Bitcoin dominance as Bitcoin capitalization divided by total capitalization. Calculate RSI for Bitcoin capitalization and dominance over the same lookback; when the dominance combination is enabled, average those two RSI values, otherwise use the capitalization RSI alone. Enter long when this signal crosses above a configurable bull threshold, and enter short when it crosses below a configurable bear threshold. An opposite entry reverses the position; no separate exit condition is specified.
