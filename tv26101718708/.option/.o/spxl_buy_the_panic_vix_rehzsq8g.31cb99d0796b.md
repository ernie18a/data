Trade the target instrument using external VIX data and the target’s own price indicators. Track whether the position is flat, in its first, second, or third entry stage, has sold half and retained a core, or has reloaded. After a cooldown, when VIX has recently reached at least the panic level and has fallen sufficiently from its recent peak, open the first position if flat. Under the same conditions, add at the next stage while below the maximum stage, provided the position is not beyond its drawdown limit. Require VIX to have recently reached the higher extreme-panic level for the final add. From the retained-core state, reload after the same panic-and-fall conditions when sufficient cash is available; do not reload again until a new eligible state is reached. Sell half the tracked position only once per euphoria episode, when VIX has remained below its euphoria threshold for the confirmation period and the target’s RSI is overbought, its price is sufficiently above its long-term moving average, and its close is above that average. After the sale, retain the remaining core and allow a later reload. Reset to flat only when the position is fully closed.
