On the traded instrument, form a short setup when price is near the upper edge of its recent price channel, the candle body is small relative to volatility, trading volume meets its relative-volume filter, and momentum is overbought on the entry, intermediate, and daily timeframes. Form a long setup using the lower channel edge and oversold momentum on all three timeframes. Require an allowed trading session and exclude the opening period when that filter is enabled. Confirm direction with the external market benchmark: its entry-timeframe momentum must be above its midpoint for a short setup or below its midpoint for a long setup; when strict benchmark alignment is enabled, its entry and intermediate timeframe momentum must also be overbought for shorts or oversold for longs. Enter short on the bar after a valid short setup if the traded instrument closes below the previous bar’s low; enter long on the bar after a valid long setup if it closes above the previous bar’s high. The indicator defines no exit rule.
