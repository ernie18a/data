For the traded instrument, form support and resistance zones by clustering confirmed pivot highs and lows within an ATR-based width, retaining zones with sufficient pivot and price-touch strength and removing zones broken by a close. A long setup occurs when price wicks into a support zone while closing above it, then the next bar closes back above that zone’s upper edge. A short setup occurs when price wicks into a resistance zone while closing below it, then the next bar closes back below that zone’s lower edge. Confirm a long only when the US Dollar Index closes below its moving average on both the daily and four-hour timeframes; confirm a short only when it closes above its moving average on both. Missing index data prevents confirmation. No exit decision is defined.
