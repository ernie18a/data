Compute a directional strength score from directional movement, adjusted by price path efficiency and acceleration. Build an adaptive envelope around a smoothed price midpoint, sizing its width from a blend of ATR and a high true-range percentile, with width adjusted by the strength score. Enter long when price closes above the upper envelope, the score is above its entry threshold and rising, positive directional movement leads negative, and—when the intermarket filter is enabled—the dollar index is below its trend EMA. Enter short when price closes below the lower envelope, the score is above its threshold and falling, negative directional movement leads positive, and—when enabled—the dollar index is above its trend EMA. Place a stop using the wider protective distance implied by the envelope and a weekly ATR risk distance; set the target at a chosen multiple of that initial risk. Optionally trail the stop using the envelope and weekly ATR. An opposite entry reverses the position.
