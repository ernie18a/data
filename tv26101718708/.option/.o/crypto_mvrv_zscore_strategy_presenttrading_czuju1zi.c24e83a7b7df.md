Use external Bitcoin market capitalization and realized capitalization data to calculate a valuation spread: compute the market-capitalization deviation from realized capitalization, normalized by the standard deviation of market capitalization; subtract the market-cap-to-realized-cap ratio; then normalize this spread by subtracting its rolling mean and dividing by its rolling standard deviation. Smooth the resulting score with a short moving average. Enter long when the score crosses above the long-entry threshold and exit long when it crosses below the short-entry threshold. Enter short when it crosses below the short-entry threshold and exit short when it crosses above the long-entry threshold. Allow long trades, short trades, or both according to the selected trade direction.
