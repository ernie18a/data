For a traded asset, compare its volatility-adjusted momentum power and volatility-disorder level with the benchmark. Go long when the asset is in the efficient regime (positive power, below-normal volatility disorder), has greater power than the benchmark, and the benchmark is stable (negative power and below-normal volatility disorder). Exit a long if the asset shifts from efficient to chaotic (nonpositive power with elevated volatility disorder), or remains explosive (positive power with elevated volatility disorder) for an extended period; tighten stops or take profits in those conditions. No short-entry rule is specified.
