At each session open, build an opening range from the initial session bars for the traded instrument and each enabled, available external instrument. Freeze each range after the opening period. A confirmed close beyond the traded instrument’s upper or lower range boundary arms a long or short setup. Each external instrument votes when it has a confirmed close beyond its own frozen range boundary in the matching direction; invert the vote for instruments configured to move oppositely. Once the armed direction has votes from at least the required number of live external instruments, enter that direction. If no external instruments are live, allow the traded instrument’s range break alone to trigger. If enabled, require relative volume to meet its threshold; skip this filter when volume data is unavailable. Allow at most one entry per session. Exit or mark the position invalid when a confirmed close crosses the opposite boundary of the traded instrument’s opening range; do not reverse into a new trade that session.
