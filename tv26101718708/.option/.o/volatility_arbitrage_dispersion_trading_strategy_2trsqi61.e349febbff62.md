Build a weighted basket of returns from configured peer instruments, excluding the traded instrument. Estimate basket-implied correlation from basket and component volatilities. Permit signals only when implied correlation is above a minimum, its z-score is not above a spike limit, the traded instrument’s short-term realized volatility is not too high relative to its long-term volatility, and—if enabled—implied volatility exceeds realized volatility by a required premium. Hedge the traded instrument’s returns against the basket using a rolling beta; cumulate the hedged residual and fit a mean-reverting autoregressive process. Trade only when its estimated half-life is within an acceptable range and the model is valid. Standard direction: go long when the residual score crosses back above the negative entry threshold, or is below it without hook confirmation; go short when it crosses back below the positive entry threshold, or is above it without hook confirmation. Reject long signals beyond the negative statistical stop and short signals beyond the positive statistical stop. An optional reverse setting swaps long and short directions. Enter only when flat and outside the post-exit cooldown. Set a protective stop using a multiple of the traded instrument’s ATR; optionally set a profit target at the model’s fair-value price, falling back to a risk/reward target when fair value is unavailable or lies against the position, or use a risk/reward target directly. Also close when the score reaches the configured fair-value exit threshold or statistical stop, or when the holding period reaches a limit based on estimated half-life and a cap. Statistical exits use the unreversed signal direction.
