For the target instrument, calculate its correlation with a basket of external equity ETFs. For each ETF, assign an up or down trend from whether its normalized adaptive moving-average oscillator is above or below zero, multiply that direction by its correlation with the target, and average the results. Go long when the aggregate implied direction is above a positive noise threshold; go short when it is below the corresponding negative threshold; otherwise remain neutral or exit. The aggregate may be normalized once or twice before applying the same threshold rule. An optional faster signal uses a Hull moving average of the selected aggregate: a cross above zero indicates long, and a cross below zero indicates short.
