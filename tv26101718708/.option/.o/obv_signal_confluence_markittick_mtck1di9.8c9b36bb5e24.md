On a confirmed bar, calculate cumulative volume flow by adding volume when price rises and subtracting it when price falls, then compare it with a moving average of that flow. Go long when the flow crosses above its average and go short when it crosses below. If enabled, require the higher-timeframe flow to agree with the trade direction, directional strength to meet its minimum, and current volume to meet its relative-volume threshold. An opposite signal closes the existing position and opens the new direction. Use volatility-based stop and risk-multiple target levels for trade management; target hits are reported as they occur.
