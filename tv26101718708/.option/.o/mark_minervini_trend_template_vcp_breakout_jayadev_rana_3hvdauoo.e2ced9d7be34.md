Long-only. Permit an entry only when the trend template passes its configured minimum score: price is above the medium- and long-term averages; the medium average is above the long average; the long average is rising; the short average is above both longer averages; price is above the short average; price is sufficiently above its trailing-year low and near its trailing-year high; and the instrument’s return relative to a selected market benchmark over a lookback period ranks above a configured threshold against its own trailing-year history. Also require a contraction base whose nested high-low ranges tighten successively, whose smallest range is below its limit, and whose recent volume is not expanding. Enter long on a close above the base’s prior high, within the permitted extension, with volume at or above its configured average-volume multiple. Set a stop at the base low subject to a maximum permitted loss and a profit target above entry. Exit the long if the stop or target is touched, or if price closes below the short average when that exit rule is enabled. Never enter short.
