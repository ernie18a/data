Open a position only when a trend, breakout, Donchian breakout, or mean-reversion setup triggers and the required directional filters and weighted confluence threshold are met. External context adds directional votes: a broad-market index above its daily long-term average favors longs; being below it gives no short vote. Low or falling volatility-index readings favor longs, while fearful or sharply rising readings favor shorts; extreme fear may block longs when that option is enabled. Longs and shorts also require their respective trend, ribbon, order-flow, structure, and channel filters. Close positions using instrument-price-based stops and profit exits.
