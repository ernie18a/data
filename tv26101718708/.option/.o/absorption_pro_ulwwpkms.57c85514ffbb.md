When a correlated instrument is supplied, compare its confirmed swing highs and lows with the traded instrument’s: a new higher high in the traded instrument paired with a lower high in the correlated instrument adds bearish weight; a new lower low paired with a higher low adds bullish weight. Combine that weight with the strategy’s other indicator and market-level scores. Enter or add long when the combined score crosses the long threshold, or short when it crosses the short threshold; select thresholds according to whether price is above or below its long-term moving average. Close existing positions when the opposite entry signal occurs. Attach stop-loss and take-profit exits to each entry.
