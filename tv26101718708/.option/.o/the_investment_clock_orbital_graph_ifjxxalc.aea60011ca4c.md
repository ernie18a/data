Compare year-over-year GDP growth and CPI inflation with their respective sustainable levels. If both are above, favor commodities, cyclical value, industrials, and energy; avoid information technology and basic materials. If growth is below and inflation above, favor cash, defensive value, utilities, pharmaceuticals, and consumer staples; avoid energy. If growth is above and inflation below, favor stocks, cyclical growth, telecoms, information technology, and basic materials; avoid consumer discretionary. If both are below, favor bonds, defensive growth, financials, and consumer discretionary; avoid pharmaceuticals and consumer staples. These are asset-class allocation suggestions, not explicit long, short, or exit signals for a specific traded instrument.
