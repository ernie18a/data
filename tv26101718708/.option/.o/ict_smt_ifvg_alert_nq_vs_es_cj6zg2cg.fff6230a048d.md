For the traded index, compare its price with the other index (NQ versus ES). Signal long when either index makes a new low below its prior swing low while the other stays above its own prior swing low, and the traded index confirms a bullish inverse fair value gap: price previously formed a sufficiently large bearish gap, then re-entered it and closed at or above its lower boundary. Signal short when either index makes a new high above its prior swing high while the other stays below its own prior swing high, and the traded index confirms a bearish inverse fair value gap: price previously formed a sufficiently large bullish gap, then re-entered it and closed at or below its upper boundary. Swing comparisons use prior bars, excluding the current bar; gaps are identified from separated highs and lows across bars. If the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled, require the same directional divergence on five-minute data as well. The logic provides no exit signal.
