Calculate the daily share of advancing NYSE stocks among advancing and declining stocks, and the daily share of advancing volume among advancing and declining volume. Average these two shares, or use only the stock share if volume is excluded, then smooth the result with a simple moving average. Enter long on the traded instrument when the smoothed breadth measure crosses above a low threshold. While long, trail a stop upward using the greater of its prior level and the instrument’s current close minus a volatility multiple of its average true range; exit if the instrument’s low reaches the stop or the holding period expires. No short signal.
