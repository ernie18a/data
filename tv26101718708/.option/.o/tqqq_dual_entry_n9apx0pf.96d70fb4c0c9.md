Use the external benchmark’s price, volume, and moving averages to trade the target instrument long only. When flat, enter at the next bar’s open after either entry condition first becomes true: (1) the benchmark has stayed above its entry moving average for the required consecutive bars and, if enabled, is at least the required percentage above it; or (2) its close is above a short EMA, volume exceeds a volume-average threshold, and that EMA is rising over the required lookback. Each entry condition triggers only when its full condition changes from false to true. If both trigger together, use the moving-average entry. For a moving-average entry, schedule an exit for the next open when the benchmark has stayed below its exit moving average for the required consecutive bars and, if enabled, is at least the required percentage below it. For an EMA entry, schedule an exit for the next open when the benchmark crosses below the short EMA; if it ever closes above the entry moving average during the trade, permanently switch to the moving-average exit rule. Independently, exit intrabar if the target instrument’s low reaches the configured loss level below its entry price, using the bar open if it gaps past that level, otherwise the loss level. Execute scheduled entries and exits at the following bar’s open.
