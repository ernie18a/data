Calculate fast and slow triple exponential moving averages (TEMA) on the traded chart timeframe and on a user-selected higher timeframe, using only completed higher-timeframe bars. Enter long when the chart-timeframe fast TEMA is above its slow TEMA and the higher-timeframe fast TEMA is above its slow TEMA, within the configured trading date window. Exit long when the chart-timeframe fast TEMA falls below its slow TEMA, within that window. Enter short when both timeframes’ fast TEMA values are below their slow TEMA values, within the window. Exit short when the chart-timeframe fast TEMA rises above its slow TEMA; this exit is not restricted to the date window. Apply the configured long-only, short-only, or both-directions order setting.
