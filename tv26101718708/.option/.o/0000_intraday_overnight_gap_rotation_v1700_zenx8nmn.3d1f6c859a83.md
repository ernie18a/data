On each trading day, evaluate the enabled instruments in the configured universe and, if a calendar exclusion is active, hold cash. Otherwise apply the selected mode: (1) at the open, rank instruments by the overnight return from the previous close to today’s open and buy the strongest qualifying gaps; (2) at the open, rank by that gap in ascending order and buy the most negative qualifying gaps, seeking a rebound; or (3) at the close, rank by the prior session’s open-to-close return and buy the strongest qualifying instruments. In each mode, require the ranking signal to meet its configured minimum-move filter, select up to the configured number of highest-ranked qualifying instruments, and allocate equally. Exit open-entry positions at that day’s close; exit close-entry positions at the next open. If no instrument qualifies, hold cash. The system takes long positions only; it does not short.
