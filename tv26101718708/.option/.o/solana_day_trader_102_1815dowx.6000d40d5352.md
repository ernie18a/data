On each enabled 15-minute, 30-minute, and 1-hour timeframe, consider a long when a confirmed downside-volatility washout is followed by short-term recovery and the weighted bullish confluence reaches the base threshold. Confluence rewards bullish fast-versus-slow EMA alignment and adaptive-average posture near the washout, rising volume balance, positive signed-volume pressure near the washout, sufficiently strong trend quality, and unusually high recent volatility; a recent bullish fair-value gap may contribute if enabled. Higher confluence raises the tier; low trend strength raises it to the highest tier. A recent close below the lower Bollinger Band can demote higher tiers, an optional trend-quality dead zone can suppress a signal, and an optional BTC close above its EMA is required for the highest tier. By default, only 15-minute signals create entries. The system is long-only. While a position is active, a lower-Bollinger-Band close, volatility-exhaustion reversal, or price-range exhaustion can initiate an exit warning; two distinct exit triggers within a short window, nearby overhead liquidity, or an enabled sharp trend-quality collapse triggers a full exit. After a full exit, allow a new long if entry conditions re-arm during the re-entry window.
