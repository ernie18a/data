Build a global liquidity series from central-bank balance sheets, subtracting the US Treasury account and reverse-repo balances, and adding broad money measures, with non-US amounts converted to USD. Optionally reduce the series according to Treasury-bond volatility. Smooth the series, then compare fast and slow EMAs. Enter long when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA; enter short when it crosses below. A crossing in the opposite direction reverses the position.
