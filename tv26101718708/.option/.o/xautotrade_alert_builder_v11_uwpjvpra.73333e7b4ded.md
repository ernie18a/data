On each bar, independently evaluate the configured long and short signal rules by comparing each side’s selected pair of data sources. A rule can trigger when the first source crosses above or below the second, or is greater or less than it; enable or disable either side independently. Enter long when the enabled long rule triggers and enter short when the enabled short rule triggers. For signal-based exits, a short entry signal closes an open long, and a long entry signal closes an open short. Alternatively, exit each position at its configured stop-loss or profit-target distance from entry, measured in ticks or as a percentage, or delegate exits to the selected external ATM strategy.
