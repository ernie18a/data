Compare the traded instrument with a separate base market using either their volatility-scaled deviations from moving averages or their RSI values; subtract the base market’s measure from the traded instrument’s measure. In trend mode, go long when this relative measure crosses above a positive threshold and short when it crosses below the corresponding negative threshold, optionally requiring a trending regime. In mean-reversion mode, Fisher-transform the relative measure; go long at an extreme positive reading and short at an extreme negative reading, optionally requiring a mean-reverting regime. Enter only when price is between the prior-bar Donchian channel boundaries and the instrument is flat. Exit longs when price reaches or falls below the lower trailing Donchian boundary, and shorts when price reaches or rises above the upper boundary. Also exit at the fixed volatility-based stop; an optional volatility-based profit target and optional daily or per-trade loss or gain limits can close positions.
