During the New York session, use a daily volatility index value (prefer the short-term index when available) to derive a daily implied-move fraction by dividing its percentage value by the square root of the number of trading days in a year. Anchor a symmetric price channel at the instrument’s session open using that fraction. After an initial observation window, go long if its closing price is above the session open or short if below; take at most one entry per session. Set a volatility-scaled stop and profit target from the entry price. Exit a long when price closes at or above the channel’s upper boundary, or a short when it closes at or below the lower boundary. Close any remaining position a configured interval before the session ends.
