For the traded instrument, allow a signal only when all enabled filters pass: its RSI average is rising for a long or falling for a short; its trend is down for a long or up for a short; its Bollinger Band width has been wide recently; its price average is falling for a long or rising for a short; the VIX RSI is not too low for a long or too high for a short; and the signal occurs on a weekday. The RSI, trend, volatility, price slope, VIX RSI, and weekday filters are independently optional. Then signal long if the VIX MACD histogram is below zero and rising (signal 1), or above zero and rising (signal 2). Signal short if the histogram is above zero and falling (signal 1), or below zero and falling (signal 2). No exit condition is defined.
