On the traded instrument, a bullish reversal signal occurs when a confirmed swing high exceeds the previous swing high while the trend is bearish; a bearish reversal signal occurs when a confirmed swing low falls below the previous swing low while the trend is bullish. When strict confirmation is enabled, show the bullish signal only if the confirming instrument’s trend is bullish and, if volume confirmation is enabled, bullish-bar volume exceeds bearish-bar volume. Apply the inverse checks for a bearish signal. These signals do not define an exit rule.
