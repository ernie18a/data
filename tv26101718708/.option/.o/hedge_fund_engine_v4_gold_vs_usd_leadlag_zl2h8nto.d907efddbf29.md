For gold, compare its log returns with the USD index’s log returns over a rolling window and require their correlation to be sufficiently negative. Measure the USD index’s cumulative return over a shorter lag window and combine it with gold’s cumulative return over that same lag to form a dislocation; standardize that dislocation against its rolling mean and standard deviation. Go long gold when the standardized dislocation is unusually low, the USD index has fallen over the lag window, and gold’s ATR relative to its price indicates sufficient volatility. Go short gold when the standardized dislocation is unusually high, the USD index has risen over the lag window, and the same volatility condition holds. No exit logic is defined.
