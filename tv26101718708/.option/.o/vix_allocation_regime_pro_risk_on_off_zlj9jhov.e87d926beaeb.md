Use the external VIX level and its moving average to set a portfolio regime, checking panic first: if VIX is above the panic threshold, allocate mainly to cash with smaller allocations to long-term Treasuries and gold; otherwise, if VIX is above the risk-off threshold and its moving average, shift toward Treasuries, gold, and cash; otherwise, if VIX is below the risk-on threshold and its moving average, favor equities, including technology and semiconductors, with some cash; in all other cases, use a balanced mix of equities, gold, Treasuries, and cash. Rebalance when the regime changes.
