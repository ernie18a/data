At the start of Monday’s 15:15 India time candle, compare the underlying instrument’s opening price with the current month’s first candle open and the current week’s first candle open. If the monthly open is above the weekly open and the weekly open is above the candle open, signal long puts (bearish). If the monthly open is below the weekly open and the weekly open is below the candle open, signal long calls (bullish). Otherwise, make no trade; equal levels do not qualify.
