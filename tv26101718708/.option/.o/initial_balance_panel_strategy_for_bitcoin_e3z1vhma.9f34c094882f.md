Across a configurable basket of crypto pairs, define each pair as bullish when its close is above the initial-balance high for each of the Asia, London, and New York sessions; define it as bearish when its close is below all three session lows. Enter long when the bullish-pair count meets a configurable quorum and the traded instrument’s ATR-based volatility stop is in an uptrend. Enter short when the bearish-pair count meets its configurable quorum and that stop is in a downtrend. Optional filters can require the traded pair itself to be bullish or bearish, or require it to be neutral. Close a long when the volatility-stop trend flips down, and close a short when it flips up.
