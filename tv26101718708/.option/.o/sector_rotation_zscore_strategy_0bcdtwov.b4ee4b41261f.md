Compare two external instruments by taking the rolling z-score of the log of their price ratio, optionally smoothing it. Enter long on an upward cross above a configurable negative threshold and exit long on a downward cross below a configurable positive threshold. Optionally enter short when the score crosses upward from below a configurable extreme-low threshold; exit short when it crosses upward above the long-entry threshold. Gate entries on the traded instrument’s trend strength and, optionally, a bullish pivot-based price bias for longs or bearish bias for shorts; allow each bias and score cross to match within a configurable window. Optional exits include a fall in trend strength, a pivot-bias flip against the position, or a score exit cross latched until the pivot bias confirms. Apply optional percentage take-profit and stop-loss levels, and close positions outside an enabled date range.
