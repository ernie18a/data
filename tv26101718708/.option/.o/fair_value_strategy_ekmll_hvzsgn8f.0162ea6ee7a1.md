Using daily net liquidity (central bank assets minus treasury cash balance minus reverse-repo balance), derive fair value for a selected market index, then calculate the selected index’s deviation from that fair value. When net liquidity is positive and the deviation crosses above the overbought level, signal a short; when it crosses below the oversold level, signal a long. In long/short mode, take the opposing position at each signal. In short-only mode, enter short on the overbought signal and exit on the oversold signal; in long-only mode, enter long on the oversold signal and exit on the overbought signal. If inverse trading is enabled, swap long and short actions. Apply the configured start-date gate to entries and close any open position on the configured close date.
