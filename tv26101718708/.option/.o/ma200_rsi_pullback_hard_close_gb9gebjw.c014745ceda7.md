Go long on the traded instrument when its price is above its long-term moving average and its momentum indicator is oversold, provided the broad-market ETF is above its own long-term moving average, the volatility index is below a high-volatility threshold, and the post-exit cooldown has elapsed. While long, exit if momentum becomes overbought, price falls below its long-term average, price reaches the stop set below entry using market volatility, or the broad-market ETF falls below its long-term average. After any exit, wait through a cooldown before allowing another entry. Never open short positions.
