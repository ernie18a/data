For a broad US equity index, enter long when all are true: market volatility is elevated; the policy rate is no higher than its earlier level over a chosen lookback (unless manually overridden); broker margin-account balances have declined from the prior quarter; the strongest-performing sector among broad-market sectors and semiconductors has outperformed the broad market over a chosen lookback by a chosen margin; and a majority of available leading-theme bellwethers have reported earnings at or above estimates (no available earnings data passes neutrally). A manually forced pass or fail may override the policy-rate and earnings checks. Also require the high-yield credit spread to be below its ceiling when the credit safeguard is enabled; require year-over-year inflation to be below its ceiling only when the inflation safeguard is enabled. Missing safeguard data passes. Otherwise remain flat. No short or exit rule is specified.
