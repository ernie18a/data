On a confirmed bar, treat a move as a candidate only when its range is large relative to recent ATR and its body is decisive. Confirm it with the corresponding futures contract’s volume at or above its recent average times a filter; use that futures data only to validate the move, while defining zones from the traded instrument’s prices. For an upward candidate, find the most recent bearish candle within the lookback and mark its high-to-low range as a bullish zone; for a downward candidate, find the most recent bullish candle and mark its range as a bearish zone. A bullish zone signals long bias on a retest; exit if a confirmed close falls below its lower edge. A bearish zone signals short bias on a retest; exit if a confirmed close rises above its upper edge. Remove invalidated zones.
