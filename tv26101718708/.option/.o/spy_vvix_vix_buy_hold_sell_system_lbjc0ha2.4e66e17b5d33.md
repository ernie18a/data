For the traded broad-market ETF, go long when its price is above its trend average and in a rising range-filter trend, implied volatility-of-volatility is below its caution threshold and falling, and implied volatility is not rising. Exit or reduce the position if volatility-of-volatility reaches its panic threshold, both volatility measures are rising, volatility-of-volatility is at least at its danger threshold and rising, or the ETF is below its trend average and in a falling range-filter trend. Otherwise, hold when the ETF remains above its trend average and in a rising range-filter trend without panic risk. No short entry is specified.
