For the traded index, define bullish trend as price above its trend average and rising range filter, and bearish trend as price below its trend average and falling range filter. Using external volatility-index data, define VVIX and VIX as rising when each is above its own average and prior value; VVIX is falling when below its average and prior value. Enter or maintain long when the index is bullish, the range filter is rising, VVIX is below its caution level and falling, and VIX is not rising. Exit or defend when VVIX is at its panic level or VVIX and VIX are both rising; also exit when VVIX is at its danger level and rising, or when the index is bearish with a falling range filter. Otherwise, hold when the index is bullish with a rising range filter and panic risk is absent. Apply buy before sell before hold; when none applies, retain the previous state.
