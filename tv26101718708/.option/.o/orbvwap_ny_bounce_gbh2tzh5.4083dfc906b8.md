After the opening range is complete, an upside close beyond its high sets a long bias for the session; a downside close below its low sets a short bias. During the permitted entry window, place a limit at the selected session-anchored VWAP: long only while price is above it, short only while price is below it. Require the correlated market instrument(s) to agree with the direction, based on their combined position relative to their VWAP and opening-range references; apply any enabled calendar, short-side, range-size, and positioning gates. Allow no more than the configured number of trades per session. Exit using the configured stop and target rules, any enabled break-even or trailing-stop rules, or the forced session-end close. Reset the range, breakout direction, and trade allowance each session.
