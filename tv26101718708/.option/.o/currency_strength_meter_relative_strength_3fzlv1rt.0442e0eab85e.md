For each of the major currencies, estimate strength from its behavior across the available currency pairs: for each pair, locate the latest close within its recent high–low range, optionally smooth that position with greater weight on more recent ranges, and convert the position into an ordered strength score. Give the base currency that score and the quote currency its complementary score, then average each currency’s scores across its pairs. For the traded instrument, go long when its base currency is at or above the configurable strong threshold and its quote currency is at or below the configurable weak threshold; go short when the base is weak and the quote is strong. No exit rule is defined.
