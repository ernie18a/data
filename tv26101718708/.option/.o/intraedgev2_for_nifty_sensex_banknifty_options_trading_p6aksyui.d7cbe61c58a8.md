After the opening range is established, treat a confirmed close beyond its high or low as a candidate long or short, respectively. Allow the candidate only when the directional trend score agrees: combine VWAP behavior, swing structure, the chart’s moving-average slope and position, higher-timeframe moving-average position, and structural divergence versus the correlated index; require a clear majority of directional votes. On options charts, use the underlying instrument’s VWAP and price for directional assessment. Return WAIT when the opening range is unset, the market is choppy, the daily range is largely exhausted, the late-session entry cutoff applies, or a gap trap, opposing gap magnet, launchpad restriction, or exhausted opening momentum blocks the candidate. A launchpad after an upward gap blocks shorts; one after a downward gap blocks longs. No explicit position exit rule is implemented.
