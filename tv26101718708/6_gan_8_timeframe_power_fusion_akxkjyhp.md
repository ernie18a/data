## Description

6 GAN 8 TIMEFRAME POWER FUSION

6 GAN  8 Timeframe Power Fusion is a multi-layer market analysis engine designed to combine trend structure, market participation, momentum, volatility, and price flow into a single decision framework.

The purpose of this indicator is not to generate signals from a single formula or a single oscillator. Instead, it attempts to evaluate the market through multiple independent perspectives and only produces high-confidence signals when those perspectives begin to align.

Multi-Timeframe Analysis Engine

One of the core components of the system is the 8 Timeframe Synchronization Model.

The indicator continuously analyzes:

1 Minute
5 Minute
15 Minute
30 Minute
1 Hour
4 Hour
Daily
Weekly

Rather than relying on one chart timeframe, the system evaluates directional agreement across all monitored periods.

When multiple timeframes begin moving in the same direction, market structure becomes stronger and directional confidence increases.

The TF score displayed in the panel represents how many timeframes currently support the active direction.

Example:

TF 3/8 = Weak alignment
TF 5/8 = Moderate alignment
TF 7/8 = Strong alignment
TF 8/8 = Maximum synchronization

The highest-quality trade opportunities often emerge when higher and lower timeframes begin moving together.

6 GAN Trend Structure Layer

The second core component is the 6 GAN Trend Engine.

Instead of using a single trend line, the system analyzes multiple GAN-inspired trend structures simultaneously.

Each GAN layer evaluates price behavior from a different perspective and trend duration.

The purpose is to measure whether trend strength exists across short-term, medium-term, and long-term structures.

When most GAN layers agree, trend reliability increases significantly.

Example:

GAN 2/6 = Weak trend structure
GAN 4/6 = Developing trend
GAN 5/6 = Strong trend
GAN 6/6 = Full trend agreement

A signal supported by both Timeframe Synchronization and GAN Synchronization generally carries more weight than a signal supported by only one of them.

Volume Fusion Layer

The Volume Fusion Engine combines:

Delta
CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta)
OBV (On Balance Volume)

These components help evaluate participation behind price movement.

Price can move without conviction, but sustainable trends are usually supported by volume.

The FLOW section of the panel provides insight into whether buying pressure or selling pressure is strengthening.

Special attention should be given to:

RES↑ (Resistance Break)
SUP↓ (Support Break)

These events often indicate significant shifts in market participation.

RSI Momentum Layer

The RSI component is not used as a standalone overbought/oversold tool.

Instead, it acts as a momentum confirmation layer.

Strong trends tend to maintain strong RSI conditions for extended periods.

The indicator uses RSI to measure whether market momentum supports the direction suggested by the GAN and Timeframe engines.

ATR Volatility Layer

ATR is used to evaluate market activity and volatility expansion.

Two important states are monitored:

COMP (Compression)

Low volatility environment.

Markets often consolidate and store energy during compression phases.

EXP (Expansion)

Volatility expansion.

This frequently occurs after compression and can lead to directional movements or breakout opportunities.

Compression zones should not automatically be considered entry zones. Instead, they should be viewed as areas where the market may be preparing for its next significant move.

Understanding the Power Score

The Power Score combines all major layers:

8 Timeframe Engine
6 GAN Engine
Volume Fusion
RSI Momentum
ATR Volatility

The score is designed to provide a quick overview of overall market strength.

General interpretation:

Below 35 = Bearish pressure
35–65 = Neutral zone
Above 65 = Bullish pressure
Above 80 = Strong directional environment

The score should not be interpreted as a prediction. It is a measurement of current market conditions.

Best Use Cases

The indicator performs best when multiple layers align simultaneously.

A stronger bullish scenario may include:

TF 7/8 or higher
GAN 5/6 or higher
FLOW showing buying pressure
ATR in expansion mode
Power Score above 65

A stronger bearish scenario may include:

TF 7/8 or higher in the opposite direction
GAN 5/6 or higher bearish alignment
FLOW showing selling pressure
ATR expansion
Power Score below 35

The highest-quality opportunities generally occur when Timeframes, GAN structures, volume flow, and volatility expansion all point in the same direction.

This indicator should be viewed as a market analysis framework rather than a standalone signal generator.

Its primary objective is to help traders understand when multiple layers of market information begin to align and when the probability of directional continuation may be increasing.

//@version=6
indicator("8 GAN × 8 TF POWER FUSION", shorttitle="8GAN 8TF FUSION", overlay=true, max_labels_count=500, max_bars_back=500)

//━━━━━━━━ INPUTS
useFill   = input.bool(true, "Sine Envelope")
ampMul    = input.float(0.5, "Sine Amp Çarpan", minval=0.1, maxval=2.0, step=0.1)

emaLen      = input.int(34, "EMA Len", minval=1)
atrLen      = input.int(14, "ATR Len", minval=1)
triSizeAtr  = input.float(1.6, "Triangle Size ATR", step=0.1)

mergeLen  = input.int(5, "Mum Birleştirme", minval=1)
lenTrend  = input.int(20, "Volume Trend Uzunluğu", minval=1)
smoothLen = input.int(10, "Volume Smooth", minval=1)
srLen     = input.int(25, "Fusion Destek Direnç", minval=2)

scoreBuyLevel  = input.int(65, "BUY Score Level", minval=50, maxval=100)
scoreSellLevel = input.int(35, "SELL Score Level", minval=0, maxval=50)

showSine   = input.bool(true, "Show Sines")
showPanel  = input.bool(true, "Panel")
showBarCol = input.bool(true, "Mum Renk")

//━━━━━━━━ COLORS
bullCol = color.rgb(0, 230, 160)
bearCol = color.rgb(255, 70, 110)
neutCol = color.rgb(140, 145, 155)

//━━━━━━━━ 8 TIMEFRAME
c1   = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "1", close)
c5   = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "5", close)
c15  = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "15", close)
c30  = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "30", close)
c60  = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "60", close)
c240 = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "240", close)
cD   = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "D", close)
cW   = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, "W", close)

//━━━━━━━━ SINE 8TF
p1 = input.int(10, "Periyot 1m", minval=5)
p2 = input.int(20, "Periyot 5m", minval=5)
p3 = input.int(60, "Periyot 15m", minval=5)
p4 = input.int(120, "Periyot 30m", minval=5)
p5 = input.int(240, "Periyot 1H", minval=5)
p6 = input.int(480, "Periyot 4H", minval=5)
p7 = input.int(1440, "Periyot 1D", minval=5)
p8 = input.int(2880, "Periyot 1W", minval=5)

amp = ta.atr(14) * ampMul

s1 = c1 + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p1)
s2 = c5 + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p2 + math.pi / 8)
s3 = c15 + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p3 + 2 * math.pi / 8)
s4 = c30 + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p4 + 3 * math.pi / 8)
s5 = c60 + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p5 + 4 * math.pi / 8)
s6 = c240 + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p6 + 5 * math.pi / 8)
s7 = cD + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p7 + 6 * math.pi / 8)
s8 = cW + amp * math.sin(2 * math.pi * bar_index / p8 + 7 * math.pi / 8)

d1 = s1 > s1[1]
d2 = s2 > s2[1]
d3 = s3 > s3[1]
d4 = s4 > s4[1]
d5 = s5 > s5[1]
d6 = s6 > s6[1]
d7 = s7 > s7[1]
d8 = s8 > s8[1]

upCount = (d1 ? 1 : 0) + (d2 ? 1 : 0) + (d3 ? 1 : 0) + (d4 ? 1 : 0) + (d5 ? 1 : 0) + (d6 ? 1 : 0) + (d7 ? 1 : 0) + (d8 ? 1 : 0)
downCount = 8 - upCount

composite = (s1 + s2 + s3 + s4 + s5 + s6 + s7 + s8) / 8
compSlope = composite > composite[1]

//━━━━━━━━ 8 GAN LAYER
gan1 = ta.linreg(close, 8, 0)
gan2 = ta.linreg(close, 13, 0)
gan3 = ta.linreg(close, 21, 0)
gan4 = ta.linreg(close, 34, 0)
gan5 = ta.linreg(close, 55, 0)
gan6 = ta.linreg(close, 89, 0)
gan7 = ta.linreg(close, 144, 0)
gan8 = ta.linreg(close, 233, 0)

g1 = gan1 > gan1[1]
g2 = gan2 > gan2[1]
g3 = gan3 > gan3[1]
g4 = gan4 > gan4[1]
g5 = gan5 > gan5[1]
g6 = gan6 > gan6[1]
g7 = gan7 > gan7[1]
g8 = gan8 > gan8[1]

ganUp = (g1 ? 1 : 0) + (g2 ? 1 : 0) + (g3 ? 1 : 0) + (g4 ? 1 : 0) + (g5 ? 1 : 0) + (g6 ? 1 : 0) + (g7 ? 1 : 0) + (g8 ? 1 : 0)
ganDown = 8 - ganUp

ganScore = ganUp / 8.0 * 100.0
ganBull = ganUp >= 6
ganBear = ganDown >= 6

//━━━━━━━━ SINE VISUAL
plot(showSine ? s1 : na, "S1-1m", color.new(#2962FF, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s2 : na, "S2-5m", color.new(#00BCD4, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s3 : na, "S3-15m", color.new(#009688, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s4 : na, "S4-30m", color.new(#8BC34A, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s5 : na, "S5-1H", color.new(#FFEB3B, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s6 : na, "S6-4H", color.new(#FF9800, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s7 : na, "S7-1D", color.new(#F44336, 85), 1)
plot(showSine ? s8 : na, "S8-1W", color.new(#9C27B0, 85), 1)

sMax = math.max(s1, math.max(s2, math.max(s3, math.max(s4, math.max(s5, math.max(s6, math.max(s7, s8)))))))
sMin = math.min(s1, math.min(s2, math.min(s3, math.min(s4, math.min(s5, math.min(s6, math.min(s7, s8)))))))

pUpper = plot(sMax, "Upper", color.new(color.white, 100))
pLower = plot(sMin, "Lower", color.new(color.white, 100))

fillColor =
     upCount >= 6 and ganBull ? color.new(color.green, 82) :
     downCount >= 6 and ganBear ? color.new(color.red, 82) :
     color.new(color.gray, 90)

fill(pUpper, pLower, useFill ? fillColor : na, title="Sine Cloud")

lineColor =
     upCount >= 6 and ganBull ? color.lime :
     downCount >= 6 and ganBear ? color.red :
     color.gray

plot(composite, "COMPOSITE", color.new(lineColor, 0), 3)

//━━━━━━━━ VOLUME FUSION LAYER
mOpen = open[mergeLen - 1]
mClose = close

float mVol = 0.0

for i = 0 to mergeLen - 1
    mVol += volume[i]

delta =
     mClose > mOpen ? mVol :
     mClose < mOpen ? -mVol :
     0.0

deltaEma = ta.ema(delta, smoothLen)

cvd = ta.cum(delta)
cvdTrend = ta.ema(cvd, lenTrend)

obvStep =
     mClose > mClose[1] ? mVol :
     mClose < mClose[1] ? -mVol :
     0.0

obv = ta.cum(obvStep)
obvTrend = ta.ema(obv, lenTrend)

norm(src) =>
    hh = ta.highest(src, 100)
    ll = ta.lowest(src, 100)
    hh == ll ? 0.5 : (src - ll) / (hh - ll)

deltaNorm = norm(deltaEma)
cvdNorm = norm(cvdTrend)
obvNorm = norm(obvTrend)

volFusion = (deltaNorm + cvdNorm + obvNorm) / 3.0
volFusionEma = ta.ema(volFusion, smoothLen)

volRes = ta.highest(volFusion, srLen)
volSup = ta.lowest(volFusion, srLen)

volBullBreak = ta.crossover(volFusion, volRes[1])
volBearBreak = ta.crossunder(volFusion, volSup[1])

volBull = volFusion > volFusionEma
volBear = volFusion < volFusionEma

//━━━━━━━━ POWER LAYERS
emaLine = ta.ema(close, emaLen)
atrVal  = ta.atr(atrLen)
rsiVal  = ta.rsi(close, 14)
atrAvg  = ta.sma(atrVal, 20)

trendBull = close > emaLine
trendBear = close < emaLine

sineScore = upCount / 8.0 * 100.0

trendScore =
     trendBull ? 100.0 :
     trendBear ? 0.0 :
     50.0

rsiScore =
     rsiVal >= 60 ? 100.0 :
     rsiVal <= 40 ? 0.0 :
     50.0

volumeScore =
     volBullBreak ? 100.0 :
     volBearBreak ? 0.0 :
     volBull ? 75.0 :
     volBear ? 25.0 :
     50.0

atrCompression = atrVal < atrAvg
atrExpansion = atrVal > atrAvg

atrScore =
     atrExpansion and close > open ? 100.0 :
     atrExpansion and close < open ? 0.0 :
     atrCompression ? 50.0 :
     50.0

powerScore = math.round((sineScore * 0.25) + (ganScore * 0.25) + (trendScore * 0.18) + (volumeScore * 0.20) + (rsiScore * 0.07) + (atrScore * 0.05))

powerBull = powerScore >= scoreBuyLevel
powerBear = powerScore <= scoreSellLevel

powerLong =
     powerBull and
     compSlope and
     trendBull and
     ganBull and
     not volBearBreak

powerShort =
     powerBear and
     not compSlope and
     trendBear and
     ganBear and
     not volBullBreak

//━━━━━━━━ STATE
var int lastSig = 0

newBuy = powerLong and lastSig != 1
newSell = powerShort and lastSig != 2

if newBuy
    lastSig := 1

if newSell
    lastSig := 2

barCol =
     showBarCol ?
     lastSig == 1 ? color.new(bullCol, 55) :
     lastSig == 2 ? color.new(bearCol, 55) :
     color.new(neutCol, 85) :
     na

barcolor(barCol)

//━━━━━━━━ SIGNAL LABELS
if newBuy
    label.new(bar_index, low - atrVal * triSizeAtr, "", xloc=xloc.bar_index, style=label.style_triangleup, color=color.new(bullCol, 75), textcolor=color.new(bullCol, 100), size=size.huge)
    label.new(bar_index, low - atrVal * triSizeAtr, "BUY\n" + str.tostring(powerScore), xloc=xloc.bar_index, style=label.style_triangleup, color=color.new(bullCol, 10), textcolor=color.black, size=size.large)

if newSell
    label.new(bar_index, high + atrVal * triSizeAtr, "", xloc=xloc.bar_index, style=label.style_triangledown, color=color.new(bearCol, 75), textcolor=color.new(bearCol, 100), size=size.huge)
    label.new(bar_index, high + atrVal * triSizeAtr, "SELL\n" + str.tostring(powerScore), xloc=xloc.bar_index, style=label.style_triangledown, color=color.new(bearCol, 10), textcolor=color.white, size=size.large)

//━━━━━━━━ COMPACT RIGHT PANEL
if barstate.islast and showPanel
    var table pan = table.new(position.top_right, 2, 9, bgcolor=color.new(#0A0A0A, 10), frame_width=2, frame_color=#333333, border_width=1)

    table.cell(pan, 0, 0, "POWER", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 0, str.tostring(powerScore) + "%", text_color=powerBull ? bullCol : powerBear ? bearCol : neutCol, text_size=size.large)

    table.cell(pan, 0, 1, "TF", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 1, str.tostring(upCount) + "/8 " + (compSlope ? "↑" : "↓"), text_color=upCount >= 6 ? bullCol : downCount >= 6 ? bearCol : neutCol)

    table.cell(pan, 0, 2, "GAN", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 2, str.tostring(ganUp) + "/8 " + (ganBull ? "↑" : ganBear ? "↓" : "→"), text_color=ganBull ? bullCol : ganBear ? bearCol : neutCol)

    table.cell(pan, 0, 3, "TREND", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 3, trendBull ? "BULL" : "BEAR", text_color=trendBull ? bullCol : bearCol)

    table.cell(pan, 0, 4, "RSI", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 4, str.tostring(rsiVal, "#"), text_color=rsiVal > 55 ? bullCol : rsiVal < 45 ? bearCol : neutCol)

    flowTxt =
         volBullBreak ? "RES↑" :
         volBearBreak ? "SUP↓" :
         volBull ? "UP" :
         volBear ? "DOWN" :
         "MID"

    flowCol =
         volBullBreak ? bullCol :
         volBearBreak ? bearCol :
         volBull ? bullCol :
         volBear ? bearCol :
         neutCol

    table.cell(pan, 0, 5, "FLOW", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 5, flowTxt, text_color=flowCol)

    atrTxt =
         atrExpansion ? "EXP" :
         atrCompression ? "COMP" :
         "MID"

    table.cell(pan, 0, 6, "ATR", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 6, atrTxt, text_color=atrExpansion ? bullCol : color.yellow)

    sigTxt =
         newBuy ? "BUY" :
         newSell ? "SELL" :
         "HOLD"

    sigCol =
         newBuy ? bullCol :
         newSell ? bearCol :
         neutCol

    table.cell(pan, 0, 7, "SIGNAL", text_color=#888888)
    table.cell(pan, 1, 7, sigTxt, text_color=sigCol, text_size=size.large)

    table.cell(pan, 0, 8, "Ω", text_color=#888888)
