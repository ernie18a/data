Calculation: Active session CVD = on chart bars, separately accumulate volume * (2 * (close - low) / (high - low) - 1) when high != low, otherwise 0, during each configured Asia, London, and New York UTC-hour session; reset the accumulators at each UTC day change and select Asia, else London, else New York, else no value.
Long: a newly confirmed pivot low of Active session CVD, using the same pivot lookback parameter for bars to its left and right, and the preceding confirmed pivot low are in the same session AND ((the newer pivot-bar low is lower than the preceding pivot-bar low AND the newer CVD pivot value is higher than the preceding CVD pivot value) OR (the newer pivot-bar low is higher than the preceding pivot-bar low AND the newer CVD pivot value is lower than the preceding CVD pivot value))
Short: a newly confirmed pivot high of Active session CVD, using the same pivot lookback parameter for bars to its left and right, and the preceding confirmed pivot high are in the same session AND ((the newer pivot-bar high is higher than the preceding pivot-bar high AND the newer CVD pivot value is lower than the preceding CVD pivot value) OR (the newer pivot-bar high is lower than the preceding pivot-bar high AND the newer CVD pivot value is higher than the preceding CVD pivot value))
