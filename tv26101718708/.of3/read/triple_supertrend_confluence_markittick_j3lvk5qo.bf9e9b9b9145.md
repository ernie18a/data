Calculation: Filtered source = hl2 when filtering is disabled; otherwise the selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA(WMA(hl2, lookback), lookback), VWMA(VWMA(VWMA(hl2, lookback), lookback), lookback), HMA, SMA(hl2, lookback) + (hl2 - hl2[lookback]) / lookback * half the lookback, or Kalman filter of hl2.
Calculation: Core multiplier = base multiplier when adaptive scaling is disabled or ATR PercentRank is unavailable; otherwise minimum multiplier + (maximum multiplier - minimum multiplier) * normalized PercentRank(ATR(core ATR lookback), rank lookback).
Calculation: Core trend = Supertrend(filtered source, core multiplier, core ATR lookback).
Calculation: Fast trend = Supertrend(filtered source, fast multiplier, fast ATR lookback); its ATR lookback and multiplier are shorter and smaller than the core settings.
Calculation: Slow trend = Supertrend(filtered source, slow multiplier, slow ATR lookback); its ATR lookback and multiplier are longer and larger than the core settings.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = Supertrend(hl2, base multiplier, core ATR lookback) on T1, using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = ATR(core ATR lookback) on the entry bar * stop ATR multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, core trend direction changes from down to up AND (consensus is disabled OR at least two of the core, fast, and slow trend directions agree) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback) >= ADX threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend direction is up) AND (volume filter is disabled OR current volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR current volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (cooldown is disabled OR no up-to-down core trend flip within the cooldown bars before this bar had its preceding core trend flip within the cooldown bars).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, core trend direction changes from up to down AND (consensus is disabled OR at least two of the core, fast, and slow trend directions agree) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback) >= ADX threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend direction is down) AND (volume filter is disabled OR current volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR current volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (cooldown is disabled OR no down-to-up core trend flip within the cooldown bars before this bar had its preceding core trend flip within the cooldown bars).
Exit: long, low <= entry price - initial risk distance OR high >= entry price + initial risk distance * TP1 risk:reward multiple OR high >= entry price + initial risk distance * TP2 risk:reward multiple OR high >= entry price + initial risk distance * TP3 risk:reward multiple.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + initial risk distance OR low <= entry price - initial risk distance * TP1 risk:reward multiple OR low <= entry price - initial risk distance * TP2 risk:reward multiple OR low <= entry price - initial risk distance * TP3 risk:reward multiple.
