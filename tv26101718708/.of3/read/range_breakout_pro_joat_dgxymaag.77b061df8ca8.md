Calculation: reference range = high/low of the last completed bar of selected prior-period T1 (shorter) or T2 (longer), sampled on chart bars, or maximum high/minimum low of chart bars in the configured initial-balance or custom session window after it closes; the signal-count and end-of-period boundary is T2 for the longer prior-period basis and T1 otherwise
Calculation: range midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2
Calculation: range size = range high - range low
Calculation: range ADR = SMA(high - low, ADR lookback, T1, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars and captured when the reference range forms
Calculation: trend EMA = EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T3, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: entry price = close of the confirming chart bar
Calculation: body points = top body points if abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= top body threshold, else next body points if >= middle body threshold, else lower body points if >= bottom body threshold, else lowest body points; use the zero-range body fallback when high = low; the three thresholds are descending and the four point parameters are separate
Calculation: candle-range points = top range points if (high - low) / ATR(ATR lookback) >= top range threshold, else next range points if >= middle range threshold, else lower range points if >= bottom range threshold, else lowest range points; use the nonpositive-ATR range fallback when ATR <= 0; the three thresholds are descending and the four point parameters are separate
Calculation: volume points = top volume points if volume ratio >= top volume threshold, else next volume points if >= middle volume threshold, else lower volume points if >= bottom volume threshold, else lowest volume points; volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) on chart bars, using the volume-ratio fallback if volume or its average is unavailable or the average <= 0; the three thresholds are descending and the four point parameters are separate
Calculation: momentum points = both-aligned points if close - close[shorter momentum lookback] and close - close[longer momentum lookback] are both strictly positive for long or both strictly negative for short; one-aligned points if exactly one change aligns; neither-aligned points otherwise; the shorter lookback is shorter than the longer lookback
Calculation: penetration points = top penetration points if penetration / ATR(ATR lookback) >= top penetration threshold, else next penetration points if >= middle penetration threshold, else lower penetration points if >= bottom penetration threshold, else lowest penetration points; penetration = (close - range high) for long or (range low - close) for short, divided by ATR when positive and using the nonpositive-ATR penetration fallback otherwise; the three thresholds are descending and the four point parameters are separate
Calculation: RSI points = RSI-alignment points when RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above the long RSI threshold for long or below the short RSI threshold for short; otherwise zero points
Calculation: trend-alignment points = trend points when trend filtering is disabled, the trend EMA is unavailable, or close is above the trend EMA for long or below it for short; otherwise zero points
Calculation: first-entry points = first-entry bonus when no earlier entry has been accepted in the current range period; otherwise zero points
Calculation: ADR-range points = ADR-range bonus when range ADR is valid and range size / range ADR is within the inclusive ADR-score lower and upper thresholds; otherwise zero points
Calculation: breakout score(side) = clamp(round to the score increment of base score + body points + candle-range points + volume points + momentum points + penetration points + RSI points + trend-alignment points + first-entry points + ADR-range points, score floor, score ceiling)
Calculation: raw stop = entry price minus ATR(ATR lookback) times ATR stop distance for a long and plus it for a short in ATR mode; range midpoint minus ATR times stop buffer for a long and plus it for a short in midpoint mode; range low minus ATR times stop buffer for a long and range high plus it for a short in structure mode
Calculation: risk distance = min(max(abs(entry price - raw stop), ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum risk multiple), ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum risk multiple)
Calculation: stop level = entry price minus risk distance for a long or plus risk distance for a short
Calculation: target 3 level = entry price plus risk distance times the target-3 R-multiple for a long or minus it for a short
Long: reference range ready AND ((direct-break mode AND close > range high AND close[previous chart bar] <= range high AND close - range high >= ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer) OR (retest mode AND, while no setup is armed, arm an upward setup when close > range high and close[previous chart bar] <= range high; mark it retested when low <= its fixed crossed level + ATR(ATR lookback) * retest tolerance, including on its arming bar, and keep it retested until cleared; cancel it when close < its fixed crossed level - ATR(ATR lookback) * retest tolerance; the setup is satisfied when retested and close > its fixed crossed level; clear it at the range-period boundary, on cancellation, or on accepted entry)) AND (trend filter disabled OR trend EMA unavailable OR close > trend EMA) AND (ADR filter disabled OR range ADR valid AND range size * 100 / range ADR >= minimum ADR percentage AND <= maximum ADR percentage) AND breakout score(long) >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR breakout score(long) >= strong threshold) AND accepted entries in the current range period < maximum signals per period AND no position is open AND no position closed on this chart bar AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available
Short: reference range ready AND ((direct-break mode AND close < range low AND close[previous chart bar] >= range low AND range low - close >= ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer) OR (retest mode AND, while no setup is armed, arm a downward setup when close < range low and close[previous chart bar] >= range low; mark it retested when high >= its fixed crossed level - ATR(ATR lookback) * retest tolerance, including on its arming bar, and keep it retested until cleared; cancel it when close > its fixed crossed level + ATR(ATR lookback) * retest tolerance; the setup is satisfied when retested and close < its fixed crossed level; clear it at the range-period boundary, on cancellation, or on accepted entry)) AND (trend filter disabled OR trend EMA unavailable OR close < trend EMA) AND (ADR filter disabled OR range ADR valid AND range size * 100 / range ADR >= minimum ADR percentage AND <= maximum ADR percentage) AND breakout score(short) >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR breakout score(short) >= strong threshold) AND accepted entries in the current range period < maximum signals per period AND no position is open AND no position closed on this chart bar AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop level OR high >= target 3 level OR the current range-period boundary is reached
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop level OR low <= target 3 level OR the current range-period boundary is reached
