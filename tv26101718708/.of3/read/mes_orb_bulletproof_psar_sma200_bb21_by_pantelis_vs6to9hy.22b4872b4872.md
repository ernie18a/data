Calculation: Opening-range high = highest(high) over chart bars from the first in-session bar until the opening-range duration elapses
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest(low) over chart bars from the first in-session bar until the opening-range duration elapses
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening-range high - opening-range low
Calculation: ATR = ATR(lookback, T1), requested on chart bars with lookahead off; use the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR * stop-distance multiple
Long: long trading enabled AND within the configured session AND opening range complete AND no position open AND no earlier long entry this session AND opening-range size >= minimum range fraction * ATR AND acceptance-lookback consecutive chart-bar closes > opening-range high AND (PSAR entry filter disabled OR close > PSAR(start, increment, maximum))
Short: short trading enabled AND within the configured session AND opening range complete AND no position open AND no earlier short entry this session AND opening-range size >= minimum range fraction * ATR AND acceptance-lookback consecutive chart-bar closes < opening-range low AND (PSAR entry filter disabled OR close < PSAR(start, increment, maximum))
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - risk distance OR (core allocation active AND high >= entry price + TP1 R multiple * risk distance) OR (runner allocation active AND PSAR trailing enabled AND PSAR(start, increment, maximum) < close AND low <= max(entry price - risk distance, PSAR(start, increment, maximum))) OR (runner allocation active AND high >= entry price + opening-range size * TP2 range multiple) OR (flat-at-session-end enabled AND current bar is outside the configured session AND previous chart bar was inside it)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + risk distance OR (core allocation active AND low <= entry price - TP1 R multiple * risk distance) OR (runner allocation active AND PSAR trailing enabled AND PSAR(start, increment, maximum) > close AND high >= min(entry price + risk distance, PSAR(start, increment, maximum))) OR (runner allocation active AND low <= entry price - opening-range size * TP2 range multiple) OR (flat-at-session-end enabled AND current bar is outside the configured session AND previous chart bar was inside it)
