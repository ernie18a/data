Calculation: Opening range high = maximum of high (or max(open, close) when the body source is selected) from session open through opening-range end; reset each session
Calculation: Opening range low = minimum of low (or min(open, close) when the body source is selected) from session open through opening-range end; reset each session
Calculation: Opening range size = opening range high - opening range low
Long: On an intraday chart bar at or after opening-range end, with the opening range locked, price crosses above the opening range high using the selected test (close > opening range high and close[1] <= opening range high, or high > opening range high and high[1] <= opening range high), and (volume filter disabled or volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier), and (one-signal-per-side disabled or no earlier long signal this session)
Short: On an intraday chart bar at or after opening-range end, with the opening range locked, price crosses below the opening range low using the selected test (close < opening range low and close[1] >= opening range low, or low < opening range low and low[1] >= opening range low), and (volume filter disabled or volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier), and (one-signal-per-side disabled or no earlier short signal this session)
Exit: long, crosses(close, opening range high + opening range size * first target multiplier) OR crosses(close, opening range high + opening range size * second target multiplier) OR crosses(close, opening range high + opening range size * third target multiplier)
Exit: short, crosses(close, opening range low - opening range size * first target multiplier) OR crosses(close, opening range low - opening range size * second target multiplier) OR crosses(close, opening range low - opening range size * third target multiplier)
