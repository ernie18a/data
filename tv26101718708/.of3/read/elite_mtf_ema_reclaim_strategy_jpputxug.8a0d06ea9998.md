Calculation: Curvature(x) = x - 2 * x[1] + x[2]
Calculation: Spread percent(a, b) = ABS(a - b) / close
Calculation: Chop-free = NOT chop kill enabled OR (Spread percent(EMA(close, slow), EMA(close, slowest)) >= min spread AND ADX(ADX lookback) >= min ADX AND NATR(ATR lookback) >= min ATR percent AND NOT (EMA(close, slow) crosses EMA(close, slowest) in either direction on any of the last cross lookback bars, counting the current bar))
Calculation: Long setup = (NOT T1 alignment enabled OR EMA(close, slow, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, slowest, T1, last completed bar)) AND (NOT T2 alignment enabled OR EMA(close, slow, T2, last completed bar) > EMA(close, slowest, T2, last completed bar)) AND (NOT strict stack enabled OR (EMA(close, fastest) > EMA(close, fast) AND EMA(close, fast) > EMA(close, slow))) AND EMA(close, slow) > EMA(close, slowest) AND EMA(close, slow) > EMA(close, slow)[1] AND EMA(close, slowest) > EMA(close, slowest)[1] AND Curvature(EMA(close, slow)) > min curvature AND Curvature(EMA(close, slowest)) > min curvature AND Chop-free
Calculation: Short setup = (NOT T1 alignment enabled OR EMA(close, slow, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, slowest, T1, last completed bar)) AND (NOT T2 alignment enabled OR EMA(close, slow, T2, last completed bar) < EMA(close, slowest, T2, last completed bar)) AND (NOT strict stack enabled OR (EMA(close, fastest) < EMA(close, fast) AND EMA(close, fast) < EMA(close, slow))) AND EMA(close, slow) < EMA(close, slowest) AND EMA(close, slow) < EMA(close, slow)[1] AND EMA(close, slowest) < EMA(close, slowest)[1] AND Curvature(EMA(close, slow)) < -min curvature AND Curvature(EMA(close, slowest)) < -min curvature AND Chop-free
Calculation: Long pullback event = Long setup AND low <= EMA(close, pullback lookback) AND close > EMA(close, slowest)
Calculation: Short pullback event = Short setup AND high >= EMA(close, pullback lookback) AND close < EMA(close, slowest)
Calculation: Long reclaim event = Long setup AND close > EMA(close, reclaim lookback) AND close[1] <= EMA(close, reclaim lookback)
Calculation: Short reclaim event = Short setup AND close < EMA(close, reclaim lookback) AND close[1] >= EMA(close, reclaim lookback)
Calculation: Long armed = there is a bar s within the last retest window bars, counting the current bar, such that Long reclaim event is true on s AND no Long entry occurs on any bar from s up to the bar before the current bar AND (reclaim without pullback allowed OR there is a bar p on or before s such that Long pullback event is true on p AND no Long entry occurs on any bar from p up to s)
Calculation: Short armed = there is a bar s within the last retest window bars, counting the current bar, such that Short reclaim event is true on s AND no Short entry occurs on any bar from s up to the bar before the current bar AND (reclaim without pullback allowed OR there is a bar p on or before s such that Short pullback event is true on p AND no Short entry occurs on any bar from p up to s)
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar * ATR stop multiplier
Long: Long setup AND ((retest required AND Long armed AND low <= EMA(close, retest lookback) AND close > EMA(close, retest lookback)) OR (NOT retest required AND ((NOT next-bar reclaim confirmation enabled AND Long reclaim event) OR (next-bar reclaim confirmation enabled AND Long reclaim event[1] AND close > EMA(close, reclaim lookback)))))
Short: Short setup AND ((retest required AND Short armed AND high >= EMA(close, retest lookback) AND close < EMA(close, retest lookback)) OR (NOT retest required AND ((NOT next-bar reclaim confirmation enabled AND Short reclaim event) OR (next-bar reclaim confirmation enabled AND Short reclaim event[1] AND close < EMA(close, reclaim lookback)))))
Exit: long, ATR risk enabled AND (low <= entry price - Entry risk OR high >= entry price + Entry risk * RR target OR (breakeven enabled AND close >= entry price + Entry risk AND low <= entry price) OR (trailing enabled AND close >= entry price + Entry risk AND low <= close - ATR(ATR lookback) * trail multiplier))
Exit: short, ATR risk enabled AND (high >= entry price + Entry risk OR low <= entry price - Entry risk * RR target OR (breakeven enabled AND close <= entry price - Entry risk AND high >= entry price) OR (trailing enabled AND close <= entry price - Entry risk AND high >= close + ATR(ATR lookback) * trail multiplier))
