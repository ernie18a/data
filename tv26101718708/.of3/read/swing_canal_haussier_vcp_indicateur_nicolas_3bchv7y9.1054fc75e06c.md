Calculation: market trend = current market-reference close > SMA(market-reference close, market moving-average lookback) on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: channel = use the latest two confirmed pivot lows and latest two confirmed pivot highs, each using the pivot-sensitivity parameter for bars on both sides; extrapolate a lower line from the lows and an upper line from the highs, with each line's slope equal to the price difference divided by the bar distance between its pivots. The channel is valid when both lines exist, both slopes are positive, the absolute slope difference divided by the absolute lower-line slope is <= slope-tolerance parameter / 100, and the lower line < the upper line.
Calculation: near lower channel = valid channel AND (abs(close - lower channel line) / close * 100 <= maximum-distance-percent parameter OR abs(close - lower channel line) <= ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) * ATR-distance-multiplier parameter).
Calculation: RSI condition = RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI minimum parameter AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI maximum parameter.
Calculation: weak volume = SMA(volume, short-volume lookback) < SMA(volume, long-volume lookback).
Calculation: decreasing volume = volume < volume[1] OR SMA(volume, short-volume lookback) < SMA(volume, short-volume lookback)[1].
Calculation: ATR contraction = ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) < SMA(ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars), ATR smoothing lookback) AND ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) < ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars)[1].
Calculation: range contraction = SMA(high - low, short-range lookback) < SMA(high - low, short-range lookback * range-lookback multiplier) AND high - low < (high - low)[1].
Calculation: relative-strength condition = relative-strength ratio > SMA(relative-strength ratio, relative-strength average lookback) AND SMA(relative-strength ratio, relative-strength average lookback) > SMA(relative-strength ratio, relative-strength average lookback)[relative-strength trend lag], where the ratio is current chart-timeframe close / current chart-timeframe relative-strength-reference close.
Calculation: setup score = min((market trend ? market-trend weight : 0) + (valid channel ? channel weight : 0) + (near lower channel ? lower-channel weight : 0) + (RSI condition ? RSI weight : 0) + (weak volume ? weak-volume weight : 0) + (decreasing volume ? decreasing-volume weight : 0) + (ATR contraction ? ATR-contraction weight : 0) + (range contraction ? range-contraction weight : 0) + (relative-strength condition ? relative-strength weight : 0), score-cap parameter).
Long: setup score >= score-threshold parameter AND market trend AND valid channel AND near lower channel AND RSI condition AND weak volume AND decreasing volume AND ATR contraction AND relative-strength condition AND close > open AND close > high[1] AND (earnings filter disabled OR earnings date - current datetime > minimum-days-before-earnings parameter OR earnings date < current datetime).
