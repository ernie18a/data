Calculation: Confirmed pivot levels = PivotHigh(high, left strength, right strength) and PivotLow(low, left strength, right strength), calculated on T1 bars and optionally T2 bars, then sampled onto chart bars after right-strength confirmation; use the same left and right strengths on T1 and T2.
Calculation: Shelf bounds start at each pivot price ± half the merge distance; merge same-side pivots within the merge distance when the minimum chart-bar spacing is met, expand bounds to contain merged pivots, and increment touches. Merge distance = min(ATR(lookback, timeframe of pivot, pivot bar) × shelf tolerance, max(tick-distance threshold, minimum tick)); shelf midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2.
Calculation: Shelf strength = touch score + timeframe-overlap score + relative-pivot-volume score + flipped-level score; rank = shelf strength + proximity score. When major-level prioritization is enabled, select up to the configured count per side by rank; otherwise select by nearest distance. Keep zones on the appropriate side of close and deduplicate zones within one minimum tick.
Calculation: Synthetic delta = volume × (2 × (high > low ? (close − low) / (high − low) : 0.5) − 1).
Calculation: Synthetic delta ratio = volume > 0 ? synthetic delta / volume : 0.
Calculation: Long stop = selected stop source if below entry price, otherwise min(low, entry price − minimum tick), then subtract stop buffer; the source is either lowest(low[1], stop lookback) with latest confirmed chart pivot low as fallback, or latest confirmed chart pivot low with low as fallback, according to the selected stop method.
Calculation: Short stop = selected stop source if above entry price, otherwise max(high, entry price + minimum tick), then add stop buffer; the source is either highest(high[1], stop lookback) with latest confirmed chart pivot high as fallback, or latest confirmed chart pivot high with high as fallback, according to the selected stop method.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × risk/reward parameter.
Calculation: Short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × risk/reward parameter.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, structure strength, structure strength) is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high OR confirmed chart PivotLow(low, structure strength, structure strength) is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, structure strength, structure strength) is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high OR confirmed chart PivotLow(low, structure strength, structure strength) is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close crosses above the latest confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, BOS strength, BOS strength) plus breakout buffer; a new tap of the nearest active support zone (current high/low overlaps its midpoint ± tap distance and the preceding bar did not) or a support-level sweep was armed within the maximum bars since tap; the BOS bar is in either configured trading session; and the same-direction cooldown has elapsed. A support sweep is low below and close above the last completed T3 bar low, last completed T4 bar low when weekly levels are enabled, or a completed configured Asia/London session low; each level arms only on its first sweep after forming. Key-level sweeps are used only when enabled, require a confirmed bar in a trading session, and override a same-bar shelf tap. Require close > EMA(HTF close, bias lookback, T1 current/forming bar sampled on chart bars) only when the bias filter is enabled. When delta confirmation is enabled, require synthetic delta ratio ≥ delta threshold, and, when volume confirmation is enabled, volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiple with a valid positive average, and, when cumulative-delta confirmation is enabled, SUM(synthetic delta, min(max(chart bars since latest arm, 1), cumulative-delta cap)) > 0. When displacement confirmation is enabled, require high − low ≥ ATR(lookback) × range parameter, abs(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ body parameter (ratio is zero for zero range), and, when directional close is enabled, close > open.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close crosses below the latest confirmed chart PivotLow(low, BOS strength, BOS strength) minus breakout buffer; a new tap of the nearest active resistance zone (current high/low overlaps its midpoint ± tap distance and the preceding bar did not) or a resistance-level sweep was armed within the maximum bars since tap; the BOS bar is in either configured trading session; and the same-direction cooldown has elapsed. A resistance sweep is high above and close below the last completed T3 bar high, last completed T4 bar high when weekly levels are enabled, or a completed configured Asia/London session high; each level arms only on its first sweep after forming. Key-level sweeps are used only when enabled, require a confirmed bar in a trading session, and override a same-bar shelf tap. Require close < EMA(HTF close, bias lookback, T1 current/forming bar sampled on chart bars) only when the bias filter is enabled. When delta confirmation is enabled, require synthetic delta ratio ≤ −delta threshold, and, when volume confirmation is enabled, volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiple with a valid positive average, and, when cumulative-delta confirmation is enabled, SUM(synthetic delta, min(max(chart bars since latest arm, 1), cumulative-delta cap)) < 0. When displacement confirmation is enabled, require high − low ≥ ATR(lookback) × range parameter, abs(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ body parameter (ratio is zero for zero range), and, when directional close is enabled, close < open.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
