Calculation: Inner VWAP deviation bands = anchored VWAP(price source, anchor period) ± VWAP standard deviation(price source, anchor period) × inner multiplier
Calculation: Outer VWAP deviation bands = anchored VWAP(price source, anchor period) ± VWAP standard deviation(price source, anchor period) × outer multiplier
Long: When VWAP is valid and the anchor period has not changed, after no lower excursion is active, start one when close crosses below the inner lower band; select the outer lower band if close is already below it, or upgrade to it if a later close is below it; go long when close crosses above the selected band, then clear the excursion. Clear any excursion when the anchor period changes.
Short: When VWAP is valid and the anchor period has not changed, after no upper excursion is active, start one when close crosses above the inner upper band; select the outer upper band if close is already above it, or upgrade to it if a later close is above it; go short when close crosses below the selected band, then clear the excursion. Clear any excursion when the anchor period changes.
Exit: Long, high reaches or exceeds the VWAP fixed at the long signal, on a later bar no more than the expiry parameter bars after that signal
Exit: Short, low reaches or falls below the VWAP fixed at the short signal, on a later bar no more than the expiry parameter bars after that signal
