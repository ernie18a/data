Calculation: Working-stripe state = on each confirmed chart-bar close, set bullish if either the pair model has fast MA > slow MA, absolute MA separation >= ATR × its separation parameter, and slow MA > its value at the separate slope lookback ago, or the single-MA model has close > its MA; set bearish on the reversed comparisons; otherwise retain the prior state, initially neutral. Use separate working-stripe MA source, method, fast and slow lookbacks, ATR lookback, separation parameter, slope lookback, and single-MA lookback. Calculate on chart bars or T1 bars; when T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed T1 bar sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Higher-stripe state = on each confirmed chart-bar close, set bullish if either the pair model has fast MA > slow MA, absolute MA separation >= ATR × its separate separation parameter, and slow MA > its value at its separate slope lookback ago, or the single-MA model has close > its MA; set bearish on the reversed comparisons; otherwise retain the prior state, initially neutral. Use separate higher-stripe MA source, method, fast and slow lookbacks, ATR lookback, separation parameter, slope lookback, and single-MA lookback. Calculate on chart bars or T2 bars; when T2 is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed T2 bar sampled on chart bars. Auto single-MA models use EMA(close, single-MA lookback); pair models use EMA(close, separate fast and slow lookbacks).
Long: Working-stripe state is bullish.
Short: Working-stripe state is bearish.
Long: Higher-stripe state is bullish.
Short: Higher-stripe state is bearish.
Long: At a confirmed chart-bar close, both stripe states are bullish and they were not both bullish on the preceding chart bar.
Short: At a confirmed chart-bar close, both stripe states are bearish and they were not both bearish on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Signed extension = (close − selected moving average(reference source, reference method, reference lookback)) / ATR(context lookback) on chart bars, or zero when that ATR is zero.
Calculation: Extension rank = PercentRank(abs(signed extension), history lookback).
Calculation: Activity rank = PercentRank(ATR(context lookback, T3), history lookback), using the current or forming T3 bar and sampling T3 values on chart bars with lookahead off; T3 is the activity timeframe selected from the chart interval.
Calculation: Trend separation = absolute fast-to-slow MA separation divided by the corresponding ATR. Use the higher-stripe pair and ATR, except when the higher stripe uses a single-MA model, in which case use the working-stripe EMA pair and ATR.
Calculation: Bullish body fraction above EMA = zero when open = close; otherwise clamp((max(open, close) − EMA(close, entry lookback)) / abs(close − open), 0, 1).
Calculation: Bearish body fraction below EMA = zero when open = close; otherwise clamp((EMA(close, entry lookback) − min(open, close)) / abs(close − open), 0, 1).
Calculation: Long setup is armed when the working-stripe state changes to bullish while the higher-stripe state is bullish and no bullish higher-stripe cooldown is pending; it remains armed through its setup bar and clears after alignment is lost or a qualifying first long signal. A cooldown starts when the higher-stripe state changes from bearish to bullish, and completes after the working-stripe state is bullish, then bearish, then bullish while the higher-stripe state remains bullish; the initial bullish working state counts if already present at the higher-stripe change. Initially unarmed.
Calculation: Short setup is armed when the working-stripe state changes to bearish while the higher-stripe state is bearish and no bearish higher-stripe cooldown is pending; it remains armed through its setup bar and clears after alignment is lost or a qualifying first short signal. A cooldown starts when the higher-stripe state changes from bullish to bearish, and completes after the working-stripe state is bearish, then bullish, then bearish while the higher-stripe state remains bearish; the initial bearish working state counts if already present at the higher-stripe change. Initially unarmed.
Calculation: Long retest is ready after bullish alignment, when no long setup is armed, and high >= EMA(close, entry lookback) + ATR(retest lookback) × retest-distance parameter. Clear it on alignment loss, a new long setup, a qualifying first long signal, or a fresh EMA touch with a qualifying bullish candle and passing long filters; otherwise retain its prior state. Initially not ready.
Calculation: Short retest is ready after bearish alignment, when no short setup is armed, and low <= EMA(close, entry lookback) − ATR(retest lookback) × retest-distance parameter. Clear it on alignment loss, a new short setup, a qualifying first short signal, or a fresh EMA touch with a qualifying bearish candle and passing short filters; otherwise retain its prior state. Initially not ready.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, both stripe states are bullish AND (a long setup is armed for this bar OR a long retest is ready and low <= EMA(close, entry lookback)) AND close > open AND bullish body fraction >= signal-sensitivity parameter AND (signed extension <= 0 OR extension rank is unavailable OR extension rank < maximum extension-rank parameter) AND (activity rank is unavailable OR activity rank >= minimum activity-rank parameter) AND trend separation >= the ATR ratio mapped from the minimum strength level.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, both stripe states are bearish AND (a short setup is armed for this bar OR a short retest is ready and high >= EMA(close, entry lookback)) AND close < open AND bearish body fraction >= signal-sensitivity parameter AND (signed extension >= 0 OR extension rank is unavailable OR extension rank < maximum extension-rank parameter) AND (activity rank is unavailable OR activity rank >= minimum activity-rank parameter) AND trend separation >= the ATR ratio mapped from the minimum strength level.
