Calculation: RSI tier = 5 if RSI(selected RSI source, RSI lookback) is below the severe oversold threshold; else 4 if below the oversold threshold; else 1 if above the severe overbought threshold; else 2 if above the overbought threshold; else 3. The four thresholds are separate parameters ordered from lowest to highest.
Calculation: EMA stack tier = 1 for a fully descending stack of six EMAs; else 2 when the shortest-lookback EMA is above the next EMA and the next EMA is below the third; else 3 when the first three are descending in lookback order and the third is below the fourth; else 4 when the first four are descending in lookback order but the full six-EMA stack is not; else 5 for a fully ascending stack; else 3. The six separate EMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: MACD tier = 1 if the MACD line is below zero, its histogram is below zero, and the histogram is falling over the short trend lookback; else 2 if the line is below zero and the histogram is at least zero or rising over the short trend lookback; else 3 if the MACD line crosses above its signal line while at most zero or crosses below it while at least zero; else 5 if the line and histogram are above zero and both rise over their respective long and short lookbacks; else 4 if the line and histogram are above zero and the line is above its signal line or the histogram is rising over the short trend lookback; else 3. The MACD line is the selected line moving average of close at the fast lookback minus that at the slow lookback; the signal line is the selected signal moving average of the MACD line. Fast and slow lookbacks, signal lookback, line and signal MA types, and the long MACD lookback are separate parameters; fast is shorter than slow. The short trend lookback is shared with the Bollinger tier.
Calculation: ADX/DI tier = 5 if ADX is above the strong ADX boundary and +DI > -DI; else 4 if ADX is at least the middle ADX boundary and at most the strong boundary and +DI > -DI; else 3 if ADX is at least the weak boundary and below the middle boundary and the absolute difference between +DI and -DI is at most the DI equality tolerance; else 2 if ADX is at least the weak boundary and below the middle boundary and -DI > +DI; else 1 if ADX is below the weak boundary and -DI > +DI; else 3. Calculate ADX and directional indicators with the separate DI lookback and ADX smoothing parameters. The three ADX boundaries are separate parameters ordered weak to middle to strong.
Calculation: Ichimoku tier = 1 if the conversion line is below the base line, close is below the cloud, and close is below close at the displacement lookback; else 2 if the conversion line is below the base line and close is in the cloud or is not below close at the displacement lookback; else 3 if the conversion and base lines cross while close is in the cloud; else 5 if the conversion line is above the base line, close is above the cloud and above close at the displacement lookback, and leading span A is above leading span B with their difference greater than its prior-bar value; else 4 if the conversion line is above the base line and close is above the cloud and above close at the displacement lookback; else 3. The conversion and base lines are midpoints of the highest high and lowest low over their respective lookbacks; leading span A is their midpoint, and leading span B is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback. Cloud bounds use both leading spans at the displacement lookback. Conversion, base, leading-span-B lookbacks, and displacement are separate parameters.
Calculation: Bollinger tier = 1 if the selected source is below the lower band, or is at or below it while the lower band or basis is falling over the short trend lookback; else 5 if the source is above the upper band, or is at or above it while the upper band or basis is rising over that lookback; else 2 if the source is at or above the lower band and below the basis; else 4 if it is above the basis and at or below the upper band; else 3. The basis is the selected moving average of the selected source over the Bollinger lookback; the bands are the basis plus or minus the standard deviation of that source times the deviation multiplier. The source, lookback, MA type, and multiplier are separate parameters.
Calculation: OBV tier = 1 if OBV is below its selected moving average and falling over the OBV short-rise lookback; else 2 if it is below that average and rising over the short-rise lookback or at least the average multiplied by one minus the near-average tolerance; else 3 if its absolute distance from the average is at most the absolute average times the near-average tolerance when the absolute average is positive, or at most the tolerance otherwise; else 4 if OBV is above its average and rising over the short-rise lookback; else 5 if it is above its average, rising over the long-rise lookback, and its change exceeds the spike multiplier times the SMA of absolute OBV change over the spike-average lookback; else 3. OBV is cumulative volume added when close rises, subtracted when close falls, and unchanged when close is unchanged. The OBV moving-average type and lookback, short-rise, long-rise, and fall lookbacks, near-average tolerance, spike multiplier, and spike-average lookback are separate parameters; the long-rise lookback exceeds the short-rise lookback.
Calculation: Composite = the seven tiers' mean, linearly mapped from the tier scale 1–5 to 0–100 and clamped to that range.
Calculation: Support-zone bounds = pivot low minus ATR on the pivot-confirmation bar times the zone-height parameter, and pivot low plus that amount times the inside-edge fraction parameter.
Calculation: Resistance-zone bounds = pivot high minus ATR on the pivot-confirmation bar times the zone-height parameter times the inside-edge fraction parameter, and pivot high plus ATR on the pivot-confirmation bar times the zone-height parameter.
