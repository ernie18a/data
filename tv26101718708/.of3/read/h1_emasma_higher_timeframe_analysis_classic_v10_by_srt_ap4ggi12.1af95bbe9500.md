Calculation: Configured moving averages = the enabled subset of four independently parameterized EMA or SMA(close, lookback) values, ordered by increasing lookback.
Calculation: MA compression = any pair of configured moving averages whose absolute difference is less than ATR(spacing lookback) multiplied by the spacing threshold.
Calculation: H1 moving-average bias = neutral if fewer than four moving averages are enabled or MA compression is true; otherwise strongly bullish if the fastest > second-fastest > second-slowest > slowest and close > fastest; bullish if second-slowest > slowest and second-fastest > second-slowest and close > second-slowest; strongly bearish if the fastest < second-fastest < second-slowest < slowest and close < fastest; bearish if second-slowest < slowest and second-fastest < second-slowest and close < second-slowest; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Ladder bias = compare the five rolling-lowest(low, lookback) levels at five distinct, descending lookbacks and the five rolling-highest(high, lookback) levels at those same lookbacks; add one up-point for each adjacent pair where the longer-lookback low is lower than the shorter-lookback low, add one down-point where the longer-lookback high is higher than the shorter-lookback high, and classify their difference as strongly bullish/bearish at or beyond the ladder strong-score threshold, bullish/bearish for a positive/negative difference below that threshold, or neutral at zero.
Calculation: H1 composite bias = neutral when MA compression is true; otherwise add the H1 moving-average bias score (strong bullish/bearish = +2/-2, bullish/bearish = +1/-1, neutral = 0) to the Ladder bias score (same scoring); classify the total as strongly bullish/bearish at or beyond the H1 composite strong-score threshold, bullish/bearish for a positive/negative total below that threshold, or neutral at zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend(T) = on chart bars, compare current chart close with EMA(shorter HTF lookback), EMA(longer HTF lookback), and SMA(longest HTF lookback) from T; bullish if close > EMA(shorter) > EMA(longer) > SMA(longest), bearish if all inequalities are reversed, otherwise neutral. Use the same three lookbacks for T1 and T2. With lookahead off and gaps filled, use the last completed T bar historically and the still-forming T bar in realtime.
Calculation: Combined higher-timeframe bias = combine Higher-timeframe trend(T1) and Higher-timeframe trend(T2): strongly bullish/bearish when both are bullish/bearish, bullish/bearish when one is bullish/bearish and the other neutral, and neutral when they conflict or are both neutral.
Calculation: Qualified bullish engulfing = on a confirmed chart bar, the previous bar closes above its open, its real body strictly contains the real body of the bar before it, its range is positive and at least ATR(previous bar, engulfing ATR lookback) multiplied by the engulfing range threshold, that ATR is positive, and its real-body-to-range fraction meets the engulfing minimum-body fraction. Use the global ATR lookback and range threshold unless the engulfing override is enabled, then use the separate engulfing parameters.
Calculation: Qualified bearish engulfing = on a confirmed chart bar, the previous bar closes below its open, its real body strictly contains the real body of the bar before it, its range is positive and at least ATR(previous bar, engulfing ATR lookback) multiplied by the engulfing range threshold, that ATR is positive, and its real-body-to-range fraction meets the engulfing minimum-body fraction. Use the global ATR lookback and range threshold unless the engulfing override is enabled, then use the separate engulfing parameters.
Calculation: Qualified bullish long-tail bar = on a confirmed chart bar, the previous bar has positive range at least ATR(previous bar, LTB ATR lookback) multiplied by the LTB range threshold, that ATR is positive, its lower-wick-to-range fraction meets the LTB tail threshold, its body bottom is at least the LTB body-location fraction up from its low, it closes above its open, and the LTB body filter is disabled or its real-body-to-range fraction meets the LTB minimum-body fraction. Use the global ATR lookback and range threshold unless the LTB override is enabled, then use the separate LTB parameters.
Calculation: Qualified bearish long-tail bar = on a confirmed chart bar, the previous bar has positive range at least ATR(previous bar, LTB ATR lookback) multiplied by the LTB range threshold, that ATR is positive, its upper-wick-to-range fraction meets the LTB tail threshold, its body top is at most the LTB body-location fraction down from its high, it closes below its open, and the LTB body filter is disabled or its real-body-to-range fraction meets the LTB minimum-body fraction. Use the global ATR lookback and range threshold unless the LTB override is enabled, then use the separate LTB parameters.
