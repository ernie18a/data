Calculation: FVG strength(gap, ATR) = the strength-off score when strength rating is disabled; otherwise the zero-ATR score if ATR <= 0, or the score for the highest of four separately parameterized descending gap/ATR thresholds met (gap/ATR >= threshold), falling to the lowest-band score if none is met; use separately parameterized descending band scores and clamp enabled-rating scores between zero and the maximum strength score.
Calculation: Bull FVG(T) = on T bars using completed T bars, replace the stored zone with bottom=high[3], top=low[1], formation time=time[1], and strength=FVG strength(low[1]-high[3], ATR(T, ATR lookback, last completed T bar)) when low[1] > high[3] and the gap is at least ATR(T, ATR lookback, last completed T bar) × ATR gap multiplier when ATR filtering is enabled, or at least minimum gap size otherwise; then clear it if close[1] < bottom, or otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Bear FVG(T) = on T bars using completed T bars, replace the stored zone with bottom=high[1], top=low[3], formation time=time[1], and strength=FVG strength(low[3]-high[1], ATR(T, ATR lookback, last completed T bar)) when high[1] < low[3] and the gap is at least ATR(T, ATR lookback, last completed T bar) × ATR gap multiplier when ATR filtering is enabled, or at least minimum gap size otherwise; then clear it if close[1] > top, or otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Merged FVG(side) = on chart bars, use returned active same-side FVGs from T1, T2, and T3, where T1 is the lowest anchor timeframe and T2 and T3 are separate higher-timeframe parameters; seed with T1 if present, then consider T2 and T3 in order, seeding with the first available if none is seeded or otherwise adding a candidate only when its midpoint is within ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed T1 bar) × confluence proximity multiplier of the current merged midpoint; on each seed or addition update bounds to their union, constituent strength to the maximum, count by one, and formation time to the latest constituent time; merged strength is min(maximum constituent strength + max(count - 1, zero) × extra-timeframe strength bonus, maximum strength score.
Calculation: Stored bounds(merged FVG) = merged bounds when zone normalization is disabled; otherwise preserve the merged midpoint and set height to ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed T1 bar) × ATR zone-height multiplier for the ATR method, or close(T1, last completed T1 bar) × zone-height percentage / 100 for the percentage method.
Calculation: Mitigated(zone, side) = using low and high from the last completed T1 bar on chart bars, in touch mode low <= top for bullish zones or high >= bottom for bearish zones; in full-fill mode low <= bottom for bullish zones or high >= top for bearish zones; in midpoint mode low <= midpoint for bullish zones or high >= midpoint for bearish zones.
Calculation: Retained zones(side) = previously created same-side zones with stored bounds; on chart bars remove mitigated zones when showing mitigated zones is disabled, otherwise retain them; after storing a new zone, discard oldest zones as needed to keep at most the maximum-zones-per-side parameter.
Long: On confirmed chart bars, bullish FVG display is enabled AND merged bullish FVG count >= minimum timeframe confluence AND merged strength >= minimum strength AND no prior bullish FVG signal exists or merged formation time > its formation time AND no retained bullish zone has an absolute midpoint distance from the merged midpoint < ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed T1 bar) × duplicate-zone distance multiplier.
Short: On confirmed chart bars, bearish FVG display is enabled AND merged bearish FVG count >= minimum timeframe confluence AND merged strength >= minimum strength AND no prior bearish FVG signal exists or merged formation time > its formation time AND no retained bearish zone has an absolute midpoint distance from the merged midpoint < ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed T1 bar) × duplicate-zone distance multiplier.
