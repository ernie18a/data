Calculation: BB expansion = upper Bollinger Band(shorter lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, T1, last completed T1 bar) > upper Bollinger Band(longer lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, T2, last completed T2 bar) AND lower Bollinger Band(shorter lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, T1, last completed T1 bar) < lower Bollinger Band(longer lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, T2, last completed T2 bar)
Calculation: EMA uptrend = EMA(close, trend lookback, T3, last completed T3 bar) >= EMA(EMA(close, trend lookback, T3), smoothing lookback, T3, last completed T3 bar)
Long: (BB expansion OR BB expansion[expansion validity lookback chart bars ago]) AND open of last completed chart bar < Bollinger Band basis(shorter lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) AND close of last completed chart bar > Bollinger Band basis(shorter lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) AND EMA uptrend
Short: (BB expansion OR BB expansion[expansion validity lookback chart bars ago]) AND open of last completed chart bar > Bollinger Band basis(shorter lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) AND close of last completed chart bar < Bollinger Band basis(shorter lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) AND NOT EMA uptrend
Calculation: Long ATR stop = round(close of first in-position chart bar − ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) × ATR stop multiple, rounding precision), retained until flat
Calculation: Short ATR stop = close of first in-position chart bar + ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) × ATR stop multiple, retained until flat
Calculation: Long ATR target = close of first in-position chart bar + ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) × ATR stop multiple × reward ratio, retained until flat
Calculation: Short ATR target = close of first in-position chart bar − ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) × ATR stop multiple × reward ratio, retained until flat
Exit: Long, ATR risk mode AND (low of last completed chart bar <= Long ATR stop OR high of last completed chart bar >= Long ATR target) OR bracket risk mode AND (low of last completed chart bar <= entry price − stop-loss tick parameter × minimum tick size OR high of last completed chart bar >= entry price + take-profit tick parameter × minimum tick size)
Exit: Short, ATR risk mode AND (high of last completed chart bar >= Short ATR stop OR low of last completed chart bar <= Short ATR target) OR bracket risk mode AND (high of last completed chart bar >= entry price + stop-loss tick parameter × minimum tick size OR low of last completed chart bar <= entry price − take-profit tick parameter × minimum tick size)
