Calculation: Fair value basis = selected smoother(selected OHLC-derived source, lookback); anchored VWAP resets at T1 and uses chart bars
Calculation: Upper threshold band = basis + (basis × median stored upper price-to-basis ratio − basis) × threshold width; lower threshold band = basis − (basis − basis × median stored lower price-to-basis ratio) × threshold width
Calculation: +1 deviation band = basis + (basis × median stored upper pivot price-to-basis ratio − basis) × deviation width; +2 and +3 add the same spread successively; −1 deviation band = basis − (basis − basis × median stored lower pivot price-to-basis ratio) × deviation width; −2 and −3 subtract the same spread successively
Calculation: Trend state starts neutral; on each bar it becomes down if, in Cross mode, the selected downtrend source is below the lower threshold band or, in Direction mode, the basis is below its prior value; otherwise it becomes up if, in Cross mode, the selected uptrend source is above the upper threshold band or, in Direction mode, the basis is above its prior value; otherwise it retains its prior state
Long: Trend state is up OR low crosses below any negative deviation band OR any enabled configured cross uses Below and its source crosses below its selected deviation band
Short: Trend state is down OR high crosses above any positive deviation band OR any enabled configured cross uses Above and its source crosses above its selected deviation band
