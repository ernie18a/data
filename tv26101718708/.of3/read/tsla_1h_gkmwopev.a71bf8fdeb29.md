Calculation: Channel index = (HLC3 - EMA(HLC3, channel smoothing lookback)) / (channel normalization parameter * EMA(abs(HLC3 - EMA(HLC3, channel smoothing lookback)), channel smoothing lookback))
Calculation: Composite oscillator = EMA(arithmetic mean(RSI(close, RSI lookback), Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback)), shorter oscillator lookback) - EMA(arithmetic mean(RSI(close, RSI lookback), Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback)), longer oscillator lookback) + channel weight * EMA(Channel index, channel lookback)
Calculation: Smoothed flow score = EMA(MFI(HLC3, MFI lookback) - flow center, flow smoothing lookback) * flow multiplier
Long: Flat AND Composite oscillator > 0 AND Smoothed flow score > 0 AND Composite oscillator > Composite oscillator[1] AND Smoothed flow score > Smoothed flow score[1] AND close > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) AND (Composite oscillator[1] <= 0 OR Smoothed flow score[1] <= 0 OR Composite oscillator[1] <= Composite oscillator[2] OR Smoothed flow score[1] <= Smoothed flow score[2] OR close[1] <= EMA(close, longer trend lookback) OR close[1] <= EMA(close, shorter trend lookback))
