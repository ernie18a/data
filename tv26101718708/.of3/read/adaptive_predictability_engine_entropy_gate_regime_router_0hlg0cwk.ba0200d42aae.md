Calculation: Order-flow input = on confirmed chart bars, either sum over completed T1 bars contained in each chart bar of sign(close − close[1]) × volume, using T2 when automatic selection cannot provide T1; or, when lower-timeframe data is disabled or unavailable, configured volume source × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), or zero when high = low
Calculation: Predictability score = entropy weight × (1 − minimum of PermutationEntropy(configured price source, pattern order, delay τ, entropy window), PermutationEntropy(configured price source, pattern order, delay 2τ, entropy window), and PermutationEntropy(configured price source, pattern order, delay 4τ, entropy window)) + (1 − entropy weight) × clip(2 × abs(Hurst(configured price source, Hurst lookback, lags {ℓ, 2ℓ, 4ℓ, 8ℓ, 16ℓ}) − 0.5), 0, 1); use Hurst 0.5 when unavailable
Calculation: Predictability gate = confirmed chart bar AND pattern count ≥ min(entropy window, warmup minimum) AND (gate disabled OR (entropy weight × PercentRank(1 − minimum permutation entropy, gate lookback) + (1 − entropy weight) × PercentRank(Hurst structure, gate lookback) ≥ adaptive percentile threshold AND Predictability score ≥ absolute floor))
Calculation: Shift state = shift guard enabled AND shift score > shift threshold, where shift score = (abs(SMA(price change, shorter shift window) − SMA(price change, longer shift window)) + abs(StDev(price change, shorter shift window) − StDev(price change, longer shift window))) / StDev(price change, longer shift window), or zero when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Expert votes = sign(EMA(configured price source, shorter lookback) − EMA(configured price source, longer lookback)); sign(configured price source − session VWAP(hlc3)); sign(EMA(Order-flow input, order-flow smoothing)); RSI fade (+1 below its lower threshold, −1 above its upper threshold, otherwise 0); band fade (+1 below the negative band z-score threshold, −1 above the positive band z-score threshold, otherwise 0), with z-score = (configured price source − SMA(configured price source, band lookback)) / StDev(configured price source, band lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero; and range fade (+1 below the lower range-position threshold, −1 above the upper range-position threshold, otherwise 0), with range position = (configured price source − lowest low over range lookback) / (highest high over range lookback − lowest low over range lookback), or 0.5 when the denominator is zero. Add each enabled external score as clip(score / external scale, −1, 1); missing votes are zero
Calculation: Expert weights = initialize equally across the six internal experts and enabled external experts; when Hedge weighting is enabled, Predictability gate is open, and Shift state is false, update each weight from its previous value × exp(−learning rate × loss), where loss is 0.5 for a zero previous vote, 0 when its sign matches the current price-change sign, and 1 otherwise; normalize, then shrink toward equal weights by the fixed-share fraction. Retain weights when learning is frozen; use equal weights when Hedge weighting is disabled
Calculation: Committee score = zero when Predictability gate is closed; otherwise compute the normalized sum of expert weight × expert vote × affinity divided by the sum of expert weight × affinity, using affinity 1 when routing is disabled, trend affinity clip((Hurst − 0.5) / 0.5, 0, 1) for trend experts, reversion affinity clip((0.5 − Hurst) / 0.5, 0, 1) for reversion experts, and the greater affinity for auto external experts; use Hurst 0.5 when unavailable and score zero when the denominator is zero. Multiply the score by the shift conviction multiplier when Shift state is true
Long: confirmed chart bar AND Committee score ≥ positive entry threshold
Short: confirmed chart bar AND Committee score ≤ −positive entry threshold
Exit: long, confirmed chart bar AND Committee score ≤ 0 AND Committee score > −positive entry threshold
Exit: short, confirmed chart bar AND Committee score ≥ 0 AND Committee score < positive entry threshold
