Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero otherwise
Long: The latest cross of EMA(close, shorter lookback) and EMA(close, longer lookback) was upward and no long signal has occurred since it, AND (low <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) OR low <= EMA(close, longer lookback)), AND ADX(DI lookback, same smoothing lookback) > threshold, AND low < lowest(low[1], divergence lookback), AND CVD > lowest(CVD[1], divergence lookback), AND close > open
Short: The latest cross of EMA(close, shorter lookback) and EMA(close, longer lookback) was downward and no short signal has occurred since it, AND (high >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) OR high >= EMA(close, longer lookback)), AND ADX(DI lookback, same smoothing lookback) > threshold, AND high > highest(high[1], divergence lookback), AND CVD < highest(CVD[1], divergence lookback), AND close < open
Exit: long, low <= the lowest low over the stop lookback ending on the long signal bar OR high >= the long signal bar close + (long signal bar close - that lowest low) * reward-risk ratio
Exit: short, high >= the highest high over the stop lookback ending on the short signal bar OR low <= the short signal bar close - (that highest high - short signal bar close) * reward-risk ratio
