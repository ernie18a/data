Calculation: Bullish C2 = low < low[1] AND close > low[1]
Calculation: Bearish C2 = high > high[1] AND close < high[1]
Calculation: Bullish C3 = low[1] < low[2] AND close[1] <= low[2] AND low >= low[1] AND close > low[2]
Calculation: Bearish C3 = high[1] > high[2] AND close[1] >= high[2] AND high <= high[1] AND close < high[2]
Calculation: Bar bias = neutral if (low < low[1] AND high > high[1] AND close >= low[1] AND close <= high[1]) OR (high <= high[1] AND low >= low[1]); otherwise bullish if Bullish C2; otherwise bearish if Bearish C2; otherwise bullish if Bullish C3; otherwise bearish if Bearish C3; otherwise bullish if close > close[1]; otherwise bearish if close < close[1]; otherwise neutral. Evaluate per bar of its timeframe; calculate T1 and T2 on their own bars, sample the last completed bar, and carry its value to chart bars. T1 is longer than T2, and T2 is longer than the chart timeframe.
Long: Bar bias of the last completed T1 bar is bullish
Short: Bar bias of the last completed T1 bar is bearish
Long: Bar bias of the last completed T2 bar is bullish
Short: Bar bias of the last completed T2 bar is bearish
Long: Bar bias of the preceding completed chart bar is bullish, or Bar bias two chart bars back is bullish when the current chart bar is C3 and the alternate C3 reference setting is selected
Short: Bar bias of the preceding completed chart bar is bearish, or Bar bias two chart bars back is bearish when the current chart bar is C3 and the alternate C3 reference setting is selected
