Calculation: Projected line price(y1, y2, x1, x2, chart bar time) = y1 + ((y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1)) * (chart bar time - x1)
Long: On chart bars using the current forming T1 bar and prior completed T1 bar, chart high > Projected line price(high(T1, 1 bar ago), high(T1, current forming bar), time(T1, 1 bar ago), time(T1, current forming bar), chart bar time) OR chart high > Projected line price(low(T1, 1 bar ago), high(T1, current forming bar), time(T1, 1 bar ago), time(T1, current forming bar), chart bar time)
Short: On chart bars using the current forming T1 bar and prior completed T1 bar, chart low < Projected line price(low(T1, 1 bar ago), low(T1, current forming bar), time(T1, 1 bar ago), time(T1, current forming bar), chart bar time) OR chart low < Projected line price(high(T1, 1 bar ago), low(T1, current forming bar), time(T1, 1 bar ago), time(T1, current forming bar), chart bar time)
