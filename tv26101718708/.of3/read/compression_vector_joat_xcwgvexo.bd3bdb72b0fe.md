Calculation: Compression zone = each run of consecutive confirmed chart bars, starting after max(long ATR lookback × warm-up multiplier, minimum warm-up bars), where ATR(short ATR lookback) / ATR(long ATR lookback) < ATR compression ratio AND Bollinger Band Width(close, band lookback, band deviation multiplier) < SMA(Bollinger Band Width(close, band lookback, band deviation multiplier), band lookback) × BB squeeze ratio AND ATR(short ATR lookback) < SMA(ATR(short ATR lookback), band lookback) × ATR squeeze ratio; zone high and low are the highest high and lowest low in the run; retire an untriggered zone after a bar older than its age limit fails to trigger.
Long: close > compression zone high AND current bar index > zone start bar index AND zone is untriggered and active AND abs(close - open) > ATR(short ATR lookback) × minimum impulse-body multiple AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI cutoff) AND current chart bar is confirmed
Short: close < compression zone low AND current bar index > zone start bar index AND zone is untriggered and active AND abs(close - open) > ATR(short ATR lookback) × minimum impulse-body multiple AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI floor) AND current chart bar is confirmed
