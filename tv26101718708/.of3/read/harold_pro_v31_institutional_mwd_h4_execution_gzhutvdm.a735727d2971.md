Calculation: T1 close series = T1 close sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically; forming T1 bar in real time)
Calculation: T2 close, high, and low series = corresponding T2 values sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T2 bar historically; forming T2 bar in real time)
Calculation: T3 open, high, low, and close series = corresponding T3 values sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T3 bar historically; forming T3 bar in real time)
Calculation: Directional Fibonacci levels = highest T2 high series[1] and lowest T2 low series[1] over the same chart-bar swing lookback; when T2 close series > SMA(T2 close series, T2 trend lookback) on chart bars, set origin to swing low and target to swing high; when below, set origin to swing high and target to swing low; otherwise retain anchors; for each retracement ratio, level = target - (target - origin) × ratio; extension = target + (target - origin) × (extension ratio - 1); zone bounds are the minimum and maximum of the levels for the zone's two ratios
Calculation: Active bullish order block = when T3 close series > highest(T3 high series[1], BOS lookback) over chart bars, replace the zone with the T3 low-to-high range of the nearest preceding chart bar within the separate scan depth where T3 close series < T3 open series; retain the zone if no qualifying bar is found; clear it when T3 close series < its midpoint
Calculation: Active bearish order block = when T3 close series < lowest(T3 low series[1], BOS lookback) over chart bars, replace the zone with the T3 low-to-high range of the nearest preceding chart bar within the separate scan depth where T3 close series > T3 open series; retain the zone if no qualifying bar is found; clear it when T3 close series > its midpoint
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of (close - SMA(close, Keltner lookback)) over the Keltner lookback with offset zero, computed on chart bars; Keltner and Bollinger lookbacks are equal
Long: Chart timeframe equals the execution timeframe AND T1 close series > SMA(T1 close series, T1 trend lookback) on chart bars AND T2 close series > SMA(T2 close series, T2 trend lookback) on chart bars AND T1 trend lookback is shorter than T2 trend lookback AND close is within the Fibonacci zone bounds, inclusive, AND (strict premium/discount filter is disabled OR close < the midpoint Fibonacci level) AND active bullish order block low <= close <= active bullish order block high AND lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger deviation multiplier) < lower Keltner Channel(high, low, close, Keltner lookback, Keltner ATR multiplier) AND upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger deviation multiplier) > upper Keltner Channel(high, low, close, Keltner lookback, Keltner ATR multiplier) AND squeeze momentum > 0 AND squeeze momentum > its value on the prior chart bar AND (killzone filter is disabled OR hour(datetime, New York) is within either configured killzone window)
Short: Chart timeframe equals the execution timeframe AND T1 close series < SMA(T1 close series, T1 trend lookback) on chart bars AND T2 close series < SMA(T2 close series, T2 trend lookback) on chart bars AND T1 trend lookback is shorter than T2 trend lookback AND close is within the Fibonacci zone bounds, inclusive, AND (strict premium/discount filter is disabled OR close > the midpoint Fibonacci level) AND active bearish order block low <= close <= active bearish order block high AND lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger deviation multiplier) < lower Keltner Channel(high, low, close, Keltner lookback, Keltner ATR multiplier) AND upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger deviation multiplier) > upper Keltner Channel(high, low, close, Keltner lookback, Keltner ATR multiplier) AND squeeze momentum < 0 AND squeeze momentum < its value on the prior chart bar AND (killzone filter is disabled OR hour(datetime, New York) is within either configured killzone window)
Exit: long, hybrid-exit parameter enabled AND chart timeframe equals the execution timeframe AND (squeeze momentum < its value on the prior chart bar AND prior-bar squeeze momentum > 0 OR close >= Fibonacci extension level OR an active bearish order block exists AND close >= its low)
Exit: short, hybrid-exit parameter enabled AND chart timeframe equals the execution timeframe AND (squeeze momentum > its value on the prior chart bar AND prior-bar squeeze momentum < 0 OR close <= Fibonacci extension level OR an active bullish order block exists AND close <= its high)
