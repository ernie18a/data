Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), or 1 when the SMA is unavailable or not positive
Calculation: Body participation = 100 × abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Close location = clamp(100 × (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), 0, 100)
Calculation: Range in ATRs = max(high - low, minimum price increment) / max(ATR(ATR lookback) or true range when ATR is unavailable, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Shock quality score(side) = clamp(clamp(relative volume / shock relative-volume threshold × 100, 0, volume-score cap) × volume-score weight + clamp(body participation / minimum body-participation threshold × 100, 0, body-score cap) × body-score weight + clamp(range in ATRs / minimum range-in-ATR threshold × 100, 0, range-score cap) × range-score weight + (close location for bullish side or 100 - close location for bearish side) × location-score weight, 0, 100)
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, close > open AND close location >= bullish close-location threshold AND relative volume >= shock relative-volume threshold AND body participation >= minimum body-participation threshold AND range in ATRs >= minimum range-in-ATR threshold AND shock quality score(bullish) >= shock quality threshold
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, close < open AND close location <= bearish close-location threshold AND relative volume >= shock relative-volume threshold AND body participation >= minimum body-participation threshold AND range in ATRs >= minimum range-in-ATR threshold AND shock quality score(bearish) >= shock quality threshold
Long: on a confirmed chart bar later than the most recent qualifying bullish shock and within the follow-through window, close > that shock's high + max(ATR(ATR lookback) or true range when ATR is unavailable, minimum price increment) × follow-through distance in ATRs AND relative volume >= minimum follow relative volume AND no earlier real-flow continuation was confirmed for that shock
Short: on a confirmed chart bar later than the most recent qualifying bearish shock and within the follow-through window, close < that shock's low - max(ATR(ATR lookback) or true range when ATR is unavailable, minimum price increment) × follow-through distance in ATRs AND relative volume >= minimum follow relative volume AND no earlier real-flow continuation was confirmed for that shock
Short: on a confirmed chart bar later than the most recent qualifying bullish shock and within the follow-through window, close < that shock's low + (that shock's high - that shock's low) × absorption threshold AND close < open AND no earlier absorption event occurred for that shock
Long: on a confirmed chart bar later than the most recent qualifying bearish shock and within the follow-through window, close > that shock's high - (that shock's high - that shock's low) × absorption threshold AND close > open AND no earlier absorption event occurred for that shock
Long: in detailed event-label mode, on a confirmed chart bar later than the most recent qualifying bullish shock and within the follow-through window, close > that shock's high AND relative volume <= thin-move relative-volume threshold AND no earlier real-flow continuation or thin-move event occurred for that shock
Short: in detailed event-label mode, on a confirmed chart bar later than the most recent qualifying bearish shock and within the follow-through window, close < that shock's low AND relative volume <= thin-move relative-volume threshold AND no earlier real-flow continuation or thin-move event occurred for that shock
