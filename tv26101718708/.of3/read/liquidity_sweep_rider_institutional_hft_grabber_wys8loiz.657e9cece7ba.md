Calculation: cooldown = cooldown multiplier × max(swing left lookback + swing right lookback, minimum cooldown)
Long: long sweep entries enabled AND low < latest confirmed PivotLow(low, swing left lookback, swing right lookback) AND close > latest confirmed PivotLow(low, swing left lookback, swing right lookback) AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND (no earlier long trigger OR chart bars since previous long trigger > cooldown)
Short: short sweep entries enabled AND high > latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing left lookback, swing right lookback) AND close < latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing left lookback, swing right lookback) AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND (no earlier short trigger OR chart bars since previous short trigger > cooldown)
Exit: long, ATR exits enabled AND (low <= entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier × take-profit risk/reward ratio)
Exit: short, ATR exits enabled AND (high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier OR low <= entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier × take-profit risk/reward ratio)
