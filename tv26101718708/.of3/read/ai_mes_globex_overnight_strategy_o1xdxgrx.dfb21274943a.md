Calculation: Range-build window = chart-bar ET HHMM >= range-build start time and < range-build end time
Calculation: Globex window = chart-bar ET HHMM >= range-build end time AND (chart-bar ET HHMM < Globex end time OR chart-bar ET HHMM >= Globex start time)
Calculation: Active session = range-build window OR Globex window
Calculation: Session range high = highest chart-bar high during each range-build window, locked at its end until the next range build
Calculation: Session range low = lowest chart-bar low during each range-build window, locked at its end until the next range build
Calculation: Range size = session range high - session range low
Calculation: Range midpoint = (session range high + session range low) / 2
Calculation: Valid range = range size >= minimum range-size parameter AND range size <= maximum range-size parameter
Calculation: T1 EMA = EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback) on T1 bars, returned on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in real time)
Calculation: Long breakout = (valid range AND close crosses above session range high + breakout buffer AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long-breakout RSI threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * breakout volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart close > T1 EMA)) OR (chart close crosses above the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot lookback on each side) AND chart close > T1 EMA AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long-breakout RSI threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * breakout volume multiplier))
Calculation: Short breakout = (valid range AND close crosses below session range low - breakout buffer AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short-breakout RSI threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * breakout volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart close < T1 EMA)) OR (chart close crosses below the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, pivot lookback on each side) AND chart close < T1 EMA AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short-breakout RSI threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * breakout volume multiplier))
Calculation: Long fade = valid range AND low <= session range low + fade buffer AND close > session range low AND close[1] <= session range low AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * fade volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart close > T1 EMA)
Calculation: Short fade = valid range AND high >= session range high - fade buffer AND close < session range high AND close[1] >= session range high AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > overbought threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * fade volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart close < T1 EMA)
Calculation: Long stop = at the signal bar, if a latest confirmed Pivot Low exists, min(signal close - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier, latest confirmed Pivot Low - stop buffer); otherwise signal close - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier
Calculation: Short stop = at the signal bar, if a latest confirmed Pivot High exists, max(signal close + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier, latest confirmed Pivot High + stop buffer); otherwise signal close + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier
Calculation: Long target = at the signal bar, range midpoint if Long fade AND NOT Long breakout; otherwise signal close + (signal close - Long stop) * reward-to-risk parameter
Calculation: Short target = at the signal bar, range midpoint if Short fade AND NOT Short breakout; otherwise signal close - (Short stop - signal close) * reward-to-risk parameter
Long: Globex window AND current-session entry count < per-session trade limit AND position is flat AND (news blackout filter disabled OR chart-bar ET HHMM is outside [blackout start time, blackout end time)) AND (Long breakout OR Long fade)
Short: Globex window AND current-session entry count < per-session trade limit AND position is flat AND (news blackout filter disabled OR chart-bar ET HHMM is outside [blackout start time, blackout end time)) AND (Short breakout OR Short fade)
Long: Long fade AND Globex window AND NOT (current-session entry count < per-session trade limit AND position is flat AND (news blackout filter disabled OR chart-bar ET HHMM is outside [blackout start time, blackout end time)))
Short: Short fade AND Globex window AND NOT (current-session entry count < per-session trade limit AND position is flat AND (news blackout filter disabled OR chart-bar ET HHMM is outside [blackout start time, blackout end time)))
Exit: long, low <= Long stop OR high >= Long target OR (current chart bar is outside Active session AND the previous chart bar was inside Active session)
Exit: short, high >= Short stop OR low <= Short target OR (current chart bar is outside Active session AND the previous chart bar was inside Active session)
