Calculation: Support and resistance zones = on each chart bar, use confirmed pivot lows and highs, respectively, with common left and right pivot lookbacks; retain the latest pivot-memory pivots per side, requiring positive pivot price and positive ATR. For each unassigned pivot seed, group unassigned pivots within ATR times cluster tolerance of its price, requiring minimum spacing between every pair; keep groups with at least minimum touches. A pivot’s weight is (baseline weight + shared weight factor × (normalized volume + normalized wick)) / (1 + pivot age / weight-decay lookback), where normalized volume is safe pivot-bar volume divided by SMA(volume, volume lookback) when that average is positive, otherwise 1; safe volume is pivot-bar volume when positive, otherwise 1; normalized wick is safe pivot-bar wick divided by ATR when positive, otherwise 0; safe wick is the upper wick for pivot highs and lower wick for pivot lows, or 0 if invalid; pivot age is at least one bar. Zone center is the pivot-price weighted mean, bounds are center plus or minus ATR times zone half-width, and strength is the sum of pivot weights; retain the strongest zone-count clusters per side. Refresh zones each bar, keep surviving prior active zones whose centers are within ATR times cluster tolerance of a refreshed cluster, drop other unmatched active zones, and add refreshed zones except those near an already broken zone of the same side within ATR times cluster tolerance times the broken-zone exclusion factor. A close beyond an active resistance top or support bottom marks that zone broken and archives it; a zone dropped by refresh is not broken. Deduplicate the broken-zone archive by same-side centers within ATR times cluster tolerance times the archive-merge factor and cap its size at the archive limit. A zone is renderable when active, has at least minimum touches, and has positive ordered bounds.
Calculation: Successful-test cooldown = support-bounce and resistance-rejection signals share recorded zone centers and bars; a test passes when no recorded center is within ATR times cluster tolerance with fewer than cooldown bars elapsed. Record each passed test, retain only the latest cooldown-memory records, and evaluate and record support bounces before resistance rejections. This cooldown applies only to successful tests.
Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold AND ((a resistance zone active before this bar has close > its top AND close[1] <= its top) OR (a renderable active support zone has low <= its top AND close >= its bottom AND close >= open AND successful-test cooldown passes) OR (a previously close-broken resistance zone has 0 < bars since break <= retest window AND low <= its top AND close >= its bottom AND close >= open) OR (a renderable active support zone has low <= its top AND close > its bottom))
