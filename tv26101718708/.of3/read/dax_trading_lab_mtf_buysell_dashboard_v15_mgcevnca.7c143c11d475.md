Calculation: MACD A = MACD(close, fast lookback A, slow lookback A, signal lookback A)
Calculation: MACD B = MACD(close, fast lookback B, slow lookback B, signal lookback B)
Calculation: Estimated volume delta = if high = low then 0, otherwise volume * ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low)
Long: MACD A line > MACD A signal line on chart bars OR MACD B line > MACD B signal line on chart bars OR MACD A line > MACD A signal line on T1, T2, T3, or T4, sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (current timeframe bar in real time; last completed timeframe bar historically) OR MACD B line > MACD B signal line on T1, T2, T3, or T4, sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (current timeframe bar in real time; last completed timeframe bar historically)
