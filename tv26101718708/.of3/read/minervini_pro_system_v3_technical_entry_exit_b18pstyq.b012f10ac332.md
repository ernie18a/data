Calculation: Average volume = SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)
Calculation: Pivot high = highest(high[1], breakout lookback)
Calculation: Eligible setup = close > SMA(close, short trend lookback) AND close > SMA(close, medium trend lookback) AND close > SMA(close, long trend lookback) AND SMA(close, short trend lookback) > SMA(close, medium trend lookback) AND SMA(close, short trend lookback) > SMA(close, long trend lookback) AND SMA(close, medium trend lookback) > SMA(close, long trend lookback) AND SMA(close, long trend lookback) > SMA(close, long trend lookback)[slope lag] AND close >= highest(high, long-range lookback) * high-proximity parameter AND close >= lowest(low, same long-range lookback) * low-recovery parameter AND (close / market index close) > SMA(close / market index close, relative-strength lookback) AND (close / market index close) > (close / market index close)[relative-strength lag] AND highest(high, short range lookback) - lowest(low, short range lookback) < (highest(high, longer range lookback) - lowest(low, longer range lookback)) * range-contraction parameter AND ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 < SMA(ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100, ATR-percent average lookback) AND abs(close - close[1]) / close * 100 < SMA(abs(close - close[1]) / close * 100, close-tightness average lookback) AND (close - SMA(close, short trend lookback)) / SMA(close, short trend lookback) * 100 <= maximum-extension parameter AND (close - SMA(close, short trend lookback)) / SMA(close, short trend lookback) * 100 > minimum-extension parameter AND (market filter disabled OR (market index close > SMA(market index close, short market lookback) AND market index close > SMA(market index close, long market lookback) AND SMA(market index close, short market lookback) > SMA(market index close, long market lookback))) AND (weekly filter disabled OR (T1 close > SMA(close, short weekly lookback, T1, current/forming bar) AND SMA(close, short weekly lookback, T1, current/forming bar) > SMA(close, long weekly lookback, T1, current/forming bar))); evaluated on chart bars using current/forming T1 values
Calculation: Breakout condition = Eligible setup AND close > Pivot high AND close > open AND volume > Average volume * breakout-volume multiplier AND close > low + (high - low) * strong-close fraction
Long: Eligible setup AND close > highest(high[1], early-trigger lookback) AND volume > Average volume * early-volume multiplier AND SMA(volume, short dry-up lookback)[1] < SMA(volume, longer dry-up lookback)[1]
Long: Breakout condition
Long: Eligible setup AND bars since Breakout condition <= retest-window parameter AND low <= Pivot high * (1 + retest-tolerance parameter / 100) AND close >= Pivot high * (1 - retest-tolerance parameter / 100) AND close > SMA(close, short trend lookback) AND close > open
Calculation: Active pivot = Pivot high captured at entry
Calculation: Active stop = At entry, the greater of lowest(low, initial-stop lookback) and lowest(low, trailing-stop lookback); on each later bar, the greater of the previous active stop and lowest(low, trailing-stop lookback)
Exit: long, close < Active stop OR (close < Active pivot AND close < SMA(close, short trend lookback)) OR (close < EMA(close, short exit lookback) AND close < close[1]) OR (close < open AND volume > Average volume * distribution-volume multiplier AND close < close[1])
