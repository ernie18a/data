Calculation: McGinley ribbon = five McGinley Dynamic recurrences of the selected price source, with lookbacks in fixed Fibonacci proportions from shortest to longest around a central base lookback; each line updates as prior line + (source − prior line) / max(smoothing × its lookback × speed factor, positive denominator floor), initialized to the source. The shared speed factor is the clamped power of source / max(prior central line, positive denominator floor), using separate exponent and lower and upper bound parameters.
Calculation: Ribbon metrics = count the adjacent McGinley-line pairs ordered in the same direction, using the larger count for up or down; ribbon spread = (maximum line − minimum line) / ATR; normalized middle-line slope = (middle line − middle line[slope lookback]) / (ATR × slope lookback). Direction is up or down when all lines are fully stacked in that direction; otherwise it is the sign of the normalized slope, with zero treated as up.
Calculation: Official Regime = on chart bars using the current, possibly forming bar, initialize raw and official states to Ranging and update only when ready: sufficient history for the longest ribbon lookback, volatility percentile lookback, and ADX readiness span, with valid ADX and percentile. ADX is ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback); volatility percentile is PercentRank(ATR(ATR lookback) / abs(selected price source), volatility lookback). Using the prior raw state, trend ADX threshold is reduced by the ADX hysteresis margin when that state is Up or Down; required alignment is relaxed to the alignment retention threshold in that case, otherwise all adjacent pairs must be stacked; required spread is minimum spread × spread retention factor in that case, otherwise minimum spread. Volatility threshold is reduced by the volatility hysteresis margin when the prior raw state is Volatile. Raw state is Volatile if ready, alignment fails the applicable alignment test, and volatility percentile meets the applicable volatility threshold. Otherwise it is Up or Down if ready, ADX, alignment, and spread meet their applicable trend tests, and either the prior raw state is already a trend or official-state flips in the flip-count lookback are below the flip-count threshold; choose direction from Ribbon metrics. Otherwise raw state is Ranging. A Volatile raw state becomes official immediately; other raw states become official only after the same raw state persists for the hold duration. Count changes in official state for the flip-count lookback.
Calculation: Trend Quality = round(100 × the weighted average of four clipped components, multiplied by a clipped volatility component). The components are: ADX normalized from an ADX quality floor (trend ADX threshold minus an ADX quality offset) to an ADX quality ceiling (trend ADX threshold × ADX quality span); fraction of adjacent ribbon pairs stacked in the prevailing direction; ribbon spread divided by spread reference; and abs(normalized middle-line slope) divided by slope reference. Use separate weights for the four components and divide their weighted sum by the sum of weights. The volatility component is one below the volatility percentile threshold; at or above it, clip (volatility extension threshold − volatility percentile) / (volatility extension threshold − volatility percentile threshold).
Long: Official Regime is ready and changes to Up from a state other than Up.
Short: Official Regime is ready and changes to Down from a state other than Down.
Long: Official Regime is Up AND Trend Quality ≥ minimum quality AND (higher-timeframe requirement is disabled OR higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR (strict mode and Official Regime(T1, evaluated on T1 bars using the last completed T1 bar) is Up) OR (non-strict mode and that higher-timeframe regime is Up or Ranging)).
Short: Official Regime is Down AND Trend Quality ≥ minimum quality AND (higher-timeframe requirement is disabled OR higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR (strict mode and Official Regime(T1, evaluated on T1 bars using the last completed T1 bar) is Down) OR (non-strict mode and that higher-timeframe regime is Down or Ranging)).
