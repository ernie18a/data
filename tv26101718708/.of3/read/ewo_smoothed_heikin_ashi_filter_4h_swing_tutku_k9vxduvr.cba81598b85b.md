Calculation: EWO = 100 * (MA(source close, fast lookback, MA type) / MA(source close, slow lookback, same MA type) - 1), calculated on chart bars using the selected source timeframe (T1 when higher-timeframe use is enabled, otherwise the chart timeframe); use the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time
Calculation: SHA = Heikin-Ashi of source OHLC after applying MA(first smoothing lookback, first MA type) separately to each OHLC field, optionally followed by MA(second smoothing lookback, second MA type) separately on each resulting Heikin-Ashi OHLC field; first and second smoothing have separate lookbacks and types, and ALMA uses shared offset and sigma parameters; calculated on chart bars using the selected source timeframe (T1 when higher-timeframe use is enabled, otherwise the chart timeframe); use the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time
Long: EWO crosses above MA(EWO, signal lookback, same MA type) AND SHA close > SHA open for at least the streak lookback consecutive chart bars AND, when bar-close confirmation is enabled, the chart bar is confirmed
Short: EWO crosses below MA(EWO, signal lookback, same MA type) AND SHA close < SHA open for at least the streak lookback consecutive chart bars AND, when bar-close confirmation is enabled, the chart bar is confirmed
