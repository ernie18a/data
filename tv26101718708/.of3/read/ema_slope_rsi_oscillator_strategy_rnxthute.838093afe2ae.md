Calculation: NTZ boundary = NTZ threshold when the NTZ filter is enabled, otherwise zero
Calculation: Short MA = selected moving average(MA source, short MA type, short MA lookback); Long MA = selected moving average(MA source, long MA type, long MA lookback), with the long lookback longer than the short lookback
Calculation: MA percent spread = 100 * (Long MA - Short MA) / Short MA
Calculation: Normalized slope = (100 / pi) * arctan((Long MA - Long MA[slope smoothing lookback]) / max(standard deviation of that difference over slope-scale lookback, positive floor))
Calculation: Slope change = current normalized slope - normalized slope on the prior completed chart bar
Calculation: Normalized acceleration = 0 when the highest raw acceleration over the acceleration-normalization lookback is zero; otherwise min(50 * raw acceleration / highest raw acceleration over that lookback, 50), where raw acceleration = 2 * slope smoothing lookback * abs(slope change)
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = SMA(RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback), RSI smoothing)
Calculation: Centered RSI = Smoothed RSI - RSI midpoint
Calculation: Slope-RSI gap = abs(normalized slope - centered RSI)
Calculation: Bullish price-RSI pivot divergence = a simultaneous pivot low of low and Smoothed RSI, using left and right spans equal to pivot-span parameter minus divergence-sensitivity parameter, where the price pivot is below and the RSI pivot above at least one corresponding prior pivot among the latest max-pivots
Calculation: Bearish price-RSI pivot divergence = a simultaneous pivot high of high and Smoothed RSI, using left and right spans equal to pivot-span parameter minus divergence-sensitivity parameter, where the price pivot is above and the RSI pivot below at least one corresponding prior pivot among the latest max-pivots
Calculation: Bullish slope-RSI divergence = Normalized acceleration >= minimum acceleration AND Slope change < 0 AND normalized slope < -NTZ boundary AND Centered RSI > Centered RSI[lookback] AND Centered RSI > Centered RSI[twice the lookback] AND (gap threshold is zero OR Slope-RSI gap >= gap threshold)
Calculation: Bearish slope-RSI divergence = Normalized acceleration >= minimum acceleration AND Slope change > 0 AND normalized slope > NTZ boundary AND Centered RSI < Centered RSI[lookback] AND Centered RSI < Centered RSI[twice the lookback] AND (gap threshold is zero OR Slope-RSI gap >= gap threshold)
Calculation: Long oscillator signal = oscillator entries enabled AND a pending arm from a Centered RSI cross above normalized slope while the NTZ filter is disabled or abs(normalized slope) > NTZ boundary, and (past-NTZ cross filter disabled OR NOT (Centered RSI > NTZ boundary AND normalized slope > NTZ boundary)), within the maximum crossing period, with Centered RSI and normalized slope both rising; a later cross-up replaces or clears the arm, a later valid cross-down cancels it, blocked cross-downs and crosses inside the NTZ do not cancel it, and the arm expires after the maximum crossing period
Calculation: Short oscillator signal = oscillator entries enabled AND a pending arm from a Centered RSI cross below normalized slope while the NTZ filter is disabled or abs(normalized slope) > NTZ boundary, and (past-NTZ cross filter disabled OR NOT (Centered RSI < -NTZ boundary AND normalized slope < -NTZ boundary)), within the maximum crossing period, with Centered RSI and normalized slope both falling; a later cross-down replaces or clears the arm, a later valid cross-up cancels it, blocked cross-ups and crosses inside the NTZ do not cancel it, and the arm expires after the maximum crossing period
Calculation: Bearish RSI-slope cross condition = RSI-slope cross exit enabled AND the latest RSI cross below normalized slope occurred within the maximum crossing period and has not been superseded by a cross above, AND Centered RSI < Centered RSI[1] AND normalized slope < normalized slope[1] AND close < open AND (delta mode disabled OR current chart bar confirmed)
Calculation: Bullish RSI-slope cross condition = RSI-slope cross exit enabled AND the latest RSI cross above normalized slope occurred within the maximum crossing period and has not been superseded by a cross below, AND Centered RSI > Centered RSI[1] AND normalized slope > normalized slope[1] AND close > open AND (delta mode disabled OR current chart bar confirmed)
Calculation: Long ATR stop(category) = close on entry bar - entry ATR factor * ATR(entry ATR lookback) at entry; afterward, after the category grace period, max(previous stop, high - category ATR factor * ATR(trailing ATR lookback)); standard, divergence, and oscillator categories have separate trailing ATR factors and grace periods
Calculation: Short ATR stop(category) = close on entry bar + entry ATR factor * ATR(entry ATR lookback) at entry; afterward, after the category grace period, min(previous stop, low + category ATR factor * ATR(trailing ATR lookback)); standard, divergence, and oscillator categories have separate trailing ATR factors and grace periods
