Calculation: Midline = 0 for MACD, VMACD, and CCI; 50 otherwise
Calculation: Oversold threshold = 2 * Midline - overbought parameter
Calculation: Range force = if Highest(close, oscillator lookback) > Highest(close, oscillator lookback)[1] then Highest(close, oscillator lookback) - Lowest(close, oscillator lookback) else if Lowest(close, oscillator lookback) < Lowest(close, oscillator lookback)[1] then -(Highest(close, oscillator lookback) - Lowest(close, oscillator lookback)) else close - close[1]
Calculation: DRM = EMA(if RMA(abs(Range force), oscillator lookback) != 0 then 50 + 50 * RMA(Range force, oscillator lookback) / RMA(abs(Range force), oscillator lookback) else 50, oscillator smoothing)
Calculation: VMACD = (EMA(volume * close, fast lookback) / EMA(volume, fast lookback) - EMA(volume * close, slow lookback) / EMA(volume, slow lookback)) / (EMA(volume * close, slow lookback) / EMA(volume, slow lookback))
Calculation: Oscillator = PPO(close, fast lookback, slow lookback) for MACD; VMACD for VMACD; EMA(RSI(close, oscillator lookback), oscillator smoothing) for RSI; EMA(MFI(close, oscillator lookback), oscillator smoothing) for MFI; EMA(CCI(hlc3, oscillator lookback), oscillator smoothing) for CCI; DRM for DRM; SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, oscillator lookback), oscillator smoothing) for Stochastic; SMA(Stochastic(RSI(close, oscillator lookback), RSI(close, oscillator lookback), RSI(close, oscillator lookback), oscillator lookback), oscillator smoothing) for Stoch RSI
Calculation: Basis = EMA(Oscillator, MACD signal lookback) for MACD or VMACD; EMA(Oscillator, basis lookback) for RSI, MFI, CCI, or DRM; SMA(Oscillator, basis lookback) for Stochastic or Stoch RSI
Calculation: Trend line = VWAP(close) when VWAP trend mode is selected; EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) otherwise
Long: MACD or VMACD selected AND hlc3 > Trend line AND Oscillator crosses above Basis AND (Oscillator < 0 OR Oscillator[1] < 0)
Short: MACD or VMACD selected AND hlc3 < Trend line AND Oscillator crosses below Basis AND (Oscillator > 0 OR Oscillator[1] > 0)
Long: RSI, MFI, CCI, DRM, Stochastic, or Stoch RSI selected AND trend mode is not Off AND (trend mode is Range OR (trend mode is 200 EMA or VWAP AND hl2 > Trend line AND close > Trend line)) AND at least Fast cooldown chart bars since the prior Fast Long oscillator signal, if any AND ((Oscillator crosses above Basis AND Oscillator < Oversold threshold) OR (Oscillator crosses above Oversold threshold - extreme offset))
Long: RSI, MFI, CCI, DRM, Stochastic, or Stoch RSI selected AND trend mode is not Off AND (trend mode is Range OR (trend mode is 200 EMA or VWAP AND hl2 > Trend line AND close > Trend line)) AND at least Slow cooldown chart bars since the prior Fast or Slow Long oscillator signal, if any AND Oscillator crosses above Oversold threshold AND no Fast Long oscillator signal on this bar
Short: RSI, MFI, CCI, DRM, Stochastic, or Stoch RSI selected AND trend mode is not Off AND (trend mode is Range OR (trend mode is 200 EMA or VWAP AND hl2 < Trend line AND close < Trend line)) AND at least Fast cooldown chart bars since the prior Fast Short oscillator signal, if any AND ((Oscillator crosses below Basis AND Oscillator > overbought parameter) OR (Oscillator crosses below overbought parameter + extreme offset))
Short: RSI, MFI, CCI, DRM, Stochastic, or Stoch RSI selected AND trend mode is not Off AND (trend mode is Range OR (trend mode is 200 EMA or VWAP AND hl2 < Trend line AND close < Trend line)) AND at least Slow cooldown chart bars since the prior Fast or Slow Short oscillator signal, if any AND Oscillator crosses below overbought parameter AND no Fast Short oscillator signal on this bar
Long: Alternate Colors enabled AND Oscillator > Midline
Short: Alternate Colors enabled AND Oscillator <= Midline
Long: Fill Lines enabled AND Oscillator > Basis
Short: Fill Lines enabled AND Oscillator <= Basis
Long: Divergence enabled AND PivotLow(Oscillator, pivot-left lookback, pivot-right lookback) is confirmed AND Oscillator[pivot-right lookback] > the prior confirmed oscillator pivot-low value AND low[pivot-right lookback] < low at the prior confirmed oscillator pivot low
Short: Divergence enabled AND PivotHigh(Oscillator, pivot-left lookback, pivot-right lookback) is confirmed AND Oscillator[pivot-right lookback] < the prior confirmed oscillator pivot-high value AND high[pivot-right lookback] > high at the prior confirmed oscillator pivot high
