Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick); body ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum tick); upper wick = high - max(close, open); lower wick = min(close, open) - low; volume ratio = volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), denominator floor); volume trend ratio = SMA(volume, shorter volume lookback) / max(SMA(volume, longer volume lookback), denominator floor); compression ratio = ATR(shorter ATR lookback) / max(ATR(longer ATR lookback), denominator floor); ATR expansion ratio = ATR(event ATR lookback) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback)[expansion lookback], denominator floor).
Calculation: Event directions = volatility breakout direction + structure break direction + volume shock direction + absorption reversal direction + regime shift direction. Volatility breakout direction is +1 when TrueRange > ATR(event ATR lookback) * breakout ATR multiplier and close location > upper close-location threshold and volume is not required or volume ratio > volume-confirmation threshold; it is -1 under the same range and volume conditions when close location < lower close-location threshold; otherwise 0. Structure break direction is +1 when close > Highest(high, structure lookback)[1], (close - Highest(high, structure lookback)[1]) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback), minimum tick) > structure-distance threshold, and volume is confirmed; it is -1 when close < Lowest(low, structure lookback)[1], (Lowest(low, structure lookback)[1] - close) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback), minimum tick) > structure-distance threshold, and volume is confirmed; otherwise 0. Volume shock direction is +1 when volume ratio > volume-shock threshold, (TrueRange > ATR(event ATR lookback) * shock-range threshold or body ratio > shock-body threshold), and close > open; it is -1 under those shock conditions when close < open; otherwise 0. Absorption reversal direction is +1 when volume ratio > volume-shock threshold * absorption-volume fraction, body ratio < absorption-body threshold, either wick > range * absorption-wick fraction, and lower wick > upper wick; it is -1 under those conditions when lower wick <= upper wick; otherwise 0. Regime shift direction is +1 when either (ATR expansion ratio > expansion threshold and compression ratio[compression offset] < compression threshold) or (ADX - ADX[ADX change lookback] > ADX-change threshold and TrueRange > ATR(event ATR lookback) * regime-range threshold), and close > close[regime direction lookback]; it is -1 under those regime-shift conditions when close <= close[regime direction lookback]; otherwise 0. Volume is confirmed when volume confirmation is not required or volume ratio > volume-confirmation threshold.
Calculation: Selected event = the volatility breakout, structure break, volume shock, absorption reversal, or regime shift condition selected by the event-type parameter, or, for composite selection, at least the event-count threshold of those five conditions. Event direction is the sum of all five event directions, even when only one event type is selected. Directional event mark is eligible only on a confirmed bar, after available history exceeds the pre-event lookback plus projection horizon plus warm-up bars, and when no prior event occurred within the minimum event gap. Its direction is event direction when nonzero; otherwise it is +1 if close > close[pre-event lookback], -1 if close < close[pre-event lookback], and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Fingerprint distance between an anchor bar and a historical bar = sqrt(sum of feature-weight parameters times squared differences between corresponding features, divided by the sum of those weights) plus regime-mismatch penalty. The features, in order, are tanh((close - close[pre-event lookback]) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback) * sqrt(pre-event lookback), minimum tick)); tanh((LinReg(close, pre-event lookback, 0) - LinReg(close, pre-event lookback, 1)) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback), minimum tick) * slope scale); tanh(compression ratio - compression center); tanh(TrueRange / max(ATR(event ATR lookback), minimum tick) - range center); close location * location scale - location center; body ratio * body scale - body center; tanh((upper wick - lower wick) / max(high - low, minimum tick) * wick scale); tanh((Highest(high, structure lookback) - close) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback), minimum tick) * upper-structure scale); tanh((close - Lowest(low, structure lookback)) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback), minimum tick) * lower-structure scale); tanh(structure-break distance); tanh(volume ratio - volume center); tanh(volume trend ratio - volume-trend center); 2 * PercentRank(ATR(event ATR lookback), ATR-rank lookback) / 100 - rank center; tanh(ATR expansion ratio - expansion center); tanh(ADX / ADX scale - ADX center); and, for each shape sample k, tanh((close[round(pre-event lookback * (shape sample count - k) / shape sample count)] - close[pre-event lookback]) / max(ATR(event ATR lookback) * sqrt(pre-event lookback), minimum tick)). Each feature has its corresponding weight parameter; shape samples share a weight. Regime is volume shock when volume ratio > shock-regime threshold and TrueRange > ATR(event ATR lookback) * shock-regime range fraction; otherwise volatility expansion when ATR expansion ratio > regime expansion threshold or compression ratio > regime expansion compression threshold; otherwise compression when compression ratio < regime compression threshold; otherwise uptrend when ADX > trend threshold, DI+ > DI-, and LinReg slope > 0; otherwise downtrend when ADX > trend threshold, DI- > DI+, and LinReg slope < 0; otherwise range. Penalty is zero for matching regimes, regime-penalty strength times the trend mismatch fraction when both regimes are trends, and regime-penalty strength otherwise.
Calculation: Analog up fraction = the fraction of selected analogs whose projected close at the projection horizon is above the anchor. For event analogs, eligible historical events are older than the latest stored event, within the memory lookback, and at least the projection horizon plus minimum event gap before the anchor. Optional strict direction matching excludes direction mismatches when anchor direction is nonzero; optional strict type matching excludes type mismatches. Event match score is min(match-score cap, match-score scale * exp(-(fingerprint distance + direction-mismatch penalty + type-mismatch penalty)) * (1 + recency boost * (1 - min(age / memory lookback, 1)))). Keep scores meeting the minimum match threshold; if fewer than the minimum analog count remain and closest-match fallback is enabled, use all eligible events. Select up to the maximum analog count by descending score. If no event analogs are selected and bar-window fallback is enabled, search historical bars at least the projection horizon plus minimum event gap before the anchor and within the memory lookback; score them with fingerprint distance and recency boost using age divided by maximum eligible age, and include bars meeting the minimum match threshold or all bars when closest-match fallback is enabled. Select up to the maximum analog count by descending score. For each selected candidate and each forward step j, projected close = anchor close + direction-adjustment * (historical close j bars after the candidate - candidate close) * clamp(anchor ATR / candidate ATR, projection-scale lower bound, projection-scale upper bound) * path-move scale. Direction-adjustment is -1 only when opposite-direction mirroring is enabled, anchor direction is nonzero, and candidate direction is its opposite; otherwise it is +1. Event candidate direction is its stored event direction; bar candidate direction compares its close with its close pre-event lookback bars earlier. The fraction is unavailable when no analog is selected. For latest-event anchoring, use the latest stored event close, ATR, direction, type, and fingerprint when one exists; otherwise use current close, ATR, direction, and fingerprint. Current-bar anchoring always uses those current values. Event type for stored events is the first active type in this precedence: structure break, volatility breakout, volume shock, absorption reversal, regime shift.
Calculation: Displayed bias direction = when analog up fraction is available and analog-driven bias is enabled, up if analog up fraction >= upper bias-fraction threshold, down if it <= lower bias-fraction threshold, and neutral otherwise; otherwise use anchor direction, which is the latest stored event direction under latest-event anchoring when one exists, or the comparison of close with close[pre-event lookback] in all other cases.
Long: directional event mark is eligible and its direction is positive OR displayed bias direction is up.
Short: directional event mark is eligible and its direction is negative OR displayed bias direction is down.
