Calculation: ORB high = high of the opening-range chart bar
Calculation: ORB low = low of the opening-range chart bar
Calculation: ORB size percentage = (ORB high - ORB low) / ORB low
Long: After the opening-range period and within the entry session, close crosses above ORB high AND ORB size percentage <= maximum ORB size percentage AND day of week is not Tuesday
Short: After the opening-range period and within the entry session, close crosses below ORB low AND ORB size percentage <= maximum ORB size percentage
