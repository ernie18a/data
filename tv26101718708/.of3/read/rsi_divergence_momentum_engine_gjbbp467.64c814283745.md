Calculation: RSI = RSI(close, RSI length)
Calculation: RSI signal = MA(RSI, signal MA type, signal MA length)
Calculation: RSI pivot low = pivot low(RSI, pivot left bars, pivot right bars), with its RSI value, low price, and bar index at bar current - pivot right bars
Calculation: RSI pivot high = pivot high(RSI, pivot left bars, pivot right bars), with its RSI value, high price, and bar index at bar current - pivot right bars
Calculation: previous RSI pivot low = most recent RSI pivot low confirmed before the current RSI pivot low, with its RSI value, low price, and bar index
Calculation: previous RSI pivot high = most recent RSI pivot high confirmed before the current RSI pivot high, with its RSI value, high price, and bar index
Calculation: pivot distance low = bar index of RSI pivot low - bar index of previous RSI pivot low
Calculation: pivot distance high = bar index of RSI pivot high - bar index of previous RSI pivot high
Calculation: bull RSI gap = RSI of RSI pivot low - RSI of previous RSI pivot low
Calculation: bear RSI gap = RSI of previous RSI pivot high - RSI of RSI pivot high
Calculation: ATR = ATR(high, low, close, ATR length)
Calculation: ADX = ADX(high, low, close, ADX length, ADX smoothing)
Calculation: DI plus = DI+(high, low, close, ADX length)
Calculation: DI minus = DI-(high, low, close, ADX length)
Calculation: bull direction misalignment = direction penalty enabled AND DI minus > DI plus AND ADX > ADX[1]
Calculation: bear direction misalignment = direction penalty enabled AND DI plus > DI minus AND ADX > ADX[1]
Calculation: volume average = SMA(volume, volume average length)
Calculation: trend EMA = EMA(close, trend EMA length)
Calculation: price EMA = EMA(close, price EMA length)
Calculation: VWAP = VWAP((high + low + close) / 3, volume)
Calculation: bull structure score = number of true items among: (EMA score disabled OR (close >= price EMA AND price EMA >= price EMA[1])), (VWAP score disabled OR close >= VWAP), (swing score disabled OR low > lowest(low[1], structure lookback) OR low > low[1]), (reversal score disabled OR (close > open AND close >= (high + low) / 2))
Calculation: bear structure score = number of true items among: (EMA score disabled OR (close <= price EMA AND price EMA <= price EMA[1])), (VWAP score disabled OR close <= VWAP), (swing score disabled OR high < highest(high[1], structure lookback) OR high < high[1]), (reversal score disabled OR (close < open AND close <= (high + low) / 2))
Calculation: bull grade = 3 if ADX >= strong grade ADX level AND bull structure score >= strong grade structure score AND bull RSI gap >= strong grade divergence gap AND RSI of RSI pivot low <= strong grade bull RSI max AND NOT bull direction misalignment; else 2 if ADX >= normal grade ADX level AND bull structure score >= normal grade structure score AND NOT bull direction misalignment; else 1
Calculation: bear grade = 3 if ADX >= strong grade ADX level AND bear structure score >= strong grade structure score AND bear RSI gap >= strong grade divergence gap AND RSI of RSI pivot high >= strong grade bear RSI min AND NOT bear direction misalignment; else 2 if ADX >= normal grade ADX level AND bear structure score >= normal grade structure score AND NOT bear direction misalignment; else 1
Calculation: bars since bull gap = barssince(open > close[1] + ATR * gap ATR multiplier)
Calculation: bars since bear gap = barssince(open < close[1] - ATR * gap ATR multiplier)
Calculation: bear trend lock bars = barssince(RSI > trend lock bear level)
Calculation: bull trend lock bars = barssince(RSI < trend lock bull level)
