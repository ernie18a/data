Calculation: Similarity score = average of RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1 current/forming bar), Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback, T1 current/forming bar), and MFI(close, MFI lookback, T1 current/forming bar), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Directional outcome counts = (bullish wins, bearish wins) among samples i from prediction horizon + 1 through historical lookback chart bars ago for which abs(current Similarity score − Similarity score[i]) ≤ similarity threshold; each sample uses close[i] ± ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1 current/forming bar)[i] × target ATR multiple as targets and scans the next prediction horizon chart bars, counting the sample at the first bar where only the upper or lower target is reached; if both targets are reached on a bar, continue scanning, and do not count samples with no single-target hit
Long: bullish count in Directional outcome counts > bearish count in Directional outcome counts
Short: bearish count in Directional outcome counts > bullish count in Directional outcome counts
