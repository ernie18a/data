Calculation: Order-flow buy pressure = 100 × sum(buy-volume estimates over the pressure lookback) / sum(volume over the pressure lookback), where each buy-volume estimate is volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume when high = low; use neutral pressure when total volume is zero.
Calculation: Mean-reversion z-score = (close − SMA(close, mean-reversion lookback)) / standard deviation(close, mean-reversion lookback), or zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Zone bounds = pivot ± half the zone width when normalized; otherwise, for a pivot low use [pivot, pivot + width] and for a pivot high use [pivot − width, pivot]. Width is ATR(zone ATR lookback) × zone ATR multiplier or confirmation-bar close × zone percentage.
Calculation: Pivot conviction(side) = 10 × the weighted mean of seven factors, using separate weights: RSI extreme (long: clamp((oversold reference − pivot RSI) / oversold reference, 0, 1); short: clamp((pivot RSI − overbought reference) / (100 − overbought reference), 0, 1)); same-side pivot divergence (long: when the pivot low is below the prior pivot low and its RSI is higher, clamp the RSI increase / divergence reference; short: when the pivot high is above the prior pivot high and its RSI is lower, clamp the RSI decrease / divergence reference; otherwise zero); rejection wick fraction divided by its reference; relative volume excess over SMA(volume, volume lookback), divided by the volume-climax excess reference; directional z-score stretch divided by its reference; directional order-flow pressure beyond neutral divided by its reference; and pivot-wick pierce beyond the corresponding Bollinger Band divided by band-pierce reference × ATR. Evaluate factors on the pivot candle; clamp each to [0, 1].
Calculation: Live zone strength = the weighted mean of birth conviction / 10, capped retest count / retest normalization, capped defended-reclaim count / defend normalization, capped mean recorded zone-wick distance / ATR, and capped mean recorded zone volume / volume baseline, multiplied by the idle-time decay factor and clamped to [0, 10]. The decay factor is 1 when disabled; otherwise it is max(1 − (1 − decay floor) × min(idle bars / decay length, 1), decay floor).
Calculation: Reclaim score = 10 × the weighted mean of capped sweep depth / depth reference, capped reclaim-speed factor (reclaim window minus elapsed bars plus one, divided by reclaim window), reclaim-bar rejection-wick fraction, capped volume surge above baseline / surge reference, zone birth conviction / 10, and capped reclaim displacement / displacement reference; clamp to [0, 10].
Long: A confirmed pivot low's bullish pivot conviction ≥ minimum conviction AND bullish zones are enabled AND the pivot is within the configured recent-build window AND (overlap suppression is disabled OR no active, unswept zone overlaps it by at least the overlap threshold, measured against the smaller zone height).
Short: A confirmed pivot high's bearish pivot conviction ≥ minimum conviction AND bearish zones are enabled AND the pivot is within the configured recent-build window AND (overlap suppression is disabled OR no active, unswept zone overlaps it by at least the overlap threshold, measured against the smaller zone height).
Long: An active bullish zone is within the configured close-to-midpoint proximity AND (strong-only visibility is disabled OR its live zone strength meets the visibility threshold OR the zone is being swept).
Short: An active bearish zone is within the configured close-to-midpoint proximity AND (strong-only visibility is disabled OR its live zone strength meets the visibility threshold OR the zone is being swept).
Long: The first unswept touch of an active bullish zone has live zone strength ≥ the strong-grade threshold AND low ≤ zone top AND low ≥ zone bottom AND close > zone bottom AND mean-reversion z-score ≤ the negative stretch reference.
Short: The first unswept touch of an active bearish zone has live zone strength ≥ the strong-grade threshold AND high ≥ zone bottom AND high ≤ zone top AND close < zone top AND mean-reversion z-score ≥ the stretch reference.
Long: A bullish zone is in the swept state after low < zone bottom − sweep-depth threshold × ATR, and close > its configured reclaim confirmation level (zone bottom, midpoint, or top), with no accepted break having ended the swept state; if strong-reclaim-only is enabled, reclaim score ≥ its threshold.
Short: A bearish zone is in the swept state after high > zone top + sweep-depth threshold × ATR, and close < its configured reclaim confirmation level (zone top, midpoint, or bottom), with no accepted break having ended the swept state; if strong-reclaim-only is enabled, reclaim score ≥ its threshold.
Exit: long, a swept bullish zone accepts below its bottom: close < zone bottom − decisive-break threshold × ATR OR elapsed bars since sweep ≥ reclaim window AND close < zone bottom.
Exit: short, a swept bearish zone accepts above its top: close > zone top + decisive-break threshold × ATR OR elapsed bars since sweep ≥ reclaim window AND close > zone top.
Short: A swept bullish zone meets the accepted-break condition for a bearish-zone flip AND breaker flipping is enabled.
Long: A swept bearish zone meets the accepted-break condition for a bullish-zone flip AND breaker flipping is enabled.
