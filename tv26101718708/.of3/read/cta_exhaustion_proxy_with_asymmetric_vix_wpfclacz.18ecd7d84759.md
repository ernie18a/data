Calculation: Trend score = on each chart bar, sum three values: +1 when the selected price source is above its selected-type (SMA, EMA, or HMA) moving average at the fast, medium, and slow lookbacks, respectively, and −1 otherwise; the lookbacks increase from fast to medium to slow.
Calculation: Extreme run length = number of consecutive chart bars on which the trend score is at its maximum or minimum; increment on each such bar and reset to zero otherwise.
Calculation: Score velocity = current trend score minus the trend score at the velocity lookback.
Calculation: Volatility reading = the close of the still-forming T1 volatility-index bar, mapped onto chart bars, falling back to the close of the still-forming T1 volatility-futures bar if the index close is unavailable.
Long: (trend score on the previous chart bar is minimum AND extreme run length on the previous chart bar >= the longer extreme-duration parameter AND close crosses above the selected-type moving average of the selected price source at the fast lookback AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility reading > the long volatility threshold)) OR (trend score was minimum within the previous recent-extreme-window chart bars AND extreme run length on the previous chart bar >= the shorter extreme-duration parameter AND extreme run length on the previous chart bar < the longer extreme-duration parameter AND score velocity >= the activation threshold AND close crosses above the selected-type moving average of the selected price source at the fast lookback AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR close > open AND high > low AND (close − open) / (high − low) >= the minimum reversal-body fraction) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility reading > the long volatility threshold)) OR (trend score on the previous chart bar <= −the activation threshold AND score velocity >= the activation threshold AND close crosses above the selected-type moving average of the selected price source at the fast lookback AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility reading > the long volatility threshold)).
Short: (trend score on the previous chart bar is maximum AND extreme run length on the previous chart bar >= the longer extreme-duration parameter AND close crosses below the selected-type moving average of the selected price source at the fast lookback AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility reading < the short volatility threshold)) OR (trend score was maximum within the previous recent-extreme-window chart bars AND extreme run length on the previous chart bar >= the shorter extreme-duration parameter AND extreme run length on the previous chart bar < the longer extreme-duration parameter AND score velocity <= −the activation threshold AND close crosses below the selected-type moving average of the selected price source at the fast lookback AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR close < open AND high > low AND (open − close) / (high − low) >= the minimum reversal-body fraction) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility reading < the short volatility threshold)) OR (trend score on the previous chart bar >= the activation threshold AND score velocity <= −the activation threshold AND close crosses below the selected-type moving average of the selected price source at the fast lookback AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility reading < the short volatility threshold)).
