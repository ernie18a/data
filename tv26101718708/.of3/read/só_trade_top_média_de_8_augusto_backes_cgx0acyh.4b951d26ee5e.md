Calculation: M = SMA or EMA(close, lookback, T1, last completed bar), selected by the MA-type parameter and read on chart bars
Long: ((close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close >= open[1] AND open <= close[1]) OR (2*abs(close-open) < min(close,open)-low AND high-max(close,open) < abs(close-open) AND close > open) OR (low < low[1] AND low < low[2] AND close > close[1] AND close > open)) AND close > M AND (rare-signal parameter disabled OR open > M)
Exit: long, ((close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close <= open[1] AND open >= close[1]) OR (2*abs(close-open) < high-max(close,open) AND min(close,open)-low < abs(close-open) AND close < open) OR (high > high[1] AND high > high[2] AND close < close[1] AND close < open)) AND close < M AND (rare-signal parameter disabled OR open < M)
