Calculation: Midpoint(T, last completed T bar carried to chart bars) = (high of last completed T bar + low of last completed T bar) / 2; T1 is longer than T2, which is longer than T3
Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot low = most recent confirmed chart-bar PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback)
Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot high = most recent confirmed chart-bar PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback)
Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe condition = chart close > Midpoint(T2, last completed bar) OR chart close > Midpoint(T3, last completed bar)
Calculation: Bearish higher-timeframe condition = chart close < Midpoint(T2, last completed bar) OR chart close < Midpoint(T3, last completed bar)
Calculation: New York session condition = New York time of the current chart bar is >= morning start parameter and < morning end parameter, OR >= afternoon start parameter and < afternoon end parameter
Calculation: Bullish approach condition = New York session condition AND bullish higher-timeframe condition AND close > Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) AND (abs(close - Midpoint(T1, last completed bar)) / Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) < proximity threshold OR latest confirmed pivot low exists AND abs(close - latest confirmed pivot low) / latest confirmed pivot low < proximity threshold)
Calculation: Bearish approach condition = New York session condition AND bearish higher-timeframe condition AND close <= Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) AND (abs(close - Midpoint(T1, last completed bar)) / Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) < proximity threshold OR latest confirmed pivot high exists AND abs(close - latest confirmed pivot high) / latest confirmed pivot high < proximity threshold)
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current chart bar low > high at the gap lookback chart bars ago
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current chart bar high < low at the gap lookback chart bars ago
Long: close > Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) OR bullish fair value gap OR (bullish approach condition AND bullish approach condition was false on the prior chart bar AND current chart-bar index minus the index of the most recent bullish warning, initialized to the initial warning index parameter, is greater than the shared warning cooldown parameter) OR (New York session condition AND bullish higher-timeframe condition AND close > Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) AND (low at the retest lookback chart bars ago <= Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) OR latest confirmed pivot low exists AND low at the retest lookback chart bars ago <= latest confirmed pivot low AND close > latest confirmed pivot low OR bullish fair value gap AND close > open))
Short: close <= Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) OR bearish fair value gap OR (bearish approach condition AND bearish approach condition was false on the prior chart bar AND current chart-bar index minus the index of the most recent bearish warning, initialized to the initial warning index parameter, is greater than the shared warning cooldown parameter) OR (New York session condition AND bearish higher-timeframe condition AND close <= Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) AND (high at the retest lookback chart bars ago >= Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) AND close < Midpoint(T1, last completed bar) OR latest confirmed pivot high exists AND high at the retest lookback chart bars ago >= latest confirmed pivot high AND close < latest confirmed pivot high OR bearish fair value gap AND close < open))
