Calculation: Chart supply/demand zones = On each chart bar, detect pivot highs and lows using the same left and right swing lookback. For a pivot high, require pivot high − lowest low over the swing lookback ≥ departure ATR multiplier × current chart ATR; for a pivot low, require highest high over the swing lookback − pivot low ≥ that amount. Center each zone on the pivot price with half-height equal to zone-height ATR multiplier × current chart ATR. Skip a zone if an already stored same-side center is within duplicate-distance ATR multiplier × current chart ATR. Retain zones in birth order, evicting the oldest when the per-side cap is reached. Mark a zone dead when close breaks beyond its edge by the break ATR buffer or its age from the pivot bar exceeds the optional expiry age. If break-flipping is enabled, create an opposite-side zone at the broken zone's center. Dead and expired zones remain stored and eligible for signal calculations and duplicate checks until evicted.
Calculation: Zone retests and strength = A chart-bar range touches a zone when low ≤ zone top and high ≥ zone bottom; update its last-touch bar. Within the retest window after a touch, count a retest if price moves beyond the opposite edge by the bounce ATR distance, or, when wick confirmation is enabled, the touch bar's wick penetrates its edge by the wick ATR distance and that bar closes outside the edge. After a counted retest, clear the last touch. A zone is confirmed when its retest count reaches the confirmation-count threshold. Zone strength is the capped retest count plus the capped post-touch move divided by current chart ATR (touch close minus subsequent low for supply; subsequent high minus touch close for demand), plus an age-tier score, plus an unbroken-zone score; dead zones receive no unbroken-zone score. A missing zone has strength zero.
Calculation: Signal-zone metrics = If confirmed-only signals are enabled and any stored zone on either side is confirmed, use only confirmed zones on both sides; otherwise use all stored zones. For each side, distance is the minimum distance from close to any zone interval (zero inside), and penetration is the maximum of zero and the overlap depth between the current bar range and any zone. Select each side's nearest zone by minimum absolute distance from close to zone center. Calculate all these metrics over the selected zones, including dead zones.
Calculation: Resolved side = If only supply is near, resolve supply; if only demand is near, resolve demand. If both are near, greater penetration selects that side; on a penetration tie, enabled chart EMA trend selects supply when the shorter-lookback EMA(close) is below the longer-lookback EMA(close), or demand when it is above; otherwise smaller zone distance selects the side, with ties selecting supply. For this calculation, near means distance ≤ proximity ATR multiplier × chart ATR. Override that result when the closer of the two center-nearest zones is touched from below (close ≤ bottom and high ≥ bottom, without also touching from above), selecting supply, or touched from above (close ≥ top and low ≤ top, without also touching from below), selecting demand. For this override, compare distance to zone intervals and break ties toward supply.
Calculation: Doji = On chart bars, range = high − low, body = absolute(close − open), upper wick = high − max(open, close), and lower wick = min(open, close) − low. Require positive range, body ≤ doji-body fraction × range, and range ≥ minimum-range ATR multiplier × chart ATR. When strict-doji mode is enabled, also require each wick ÷ range ≥ minimum-wick fraction.
Calculation: Quality results = Use the center-nearest demand zone for long checks and the center-nearest supply zone for short checks; with no such zone, wick and band checks fail and the fresh-touch check passes. Long checks are: low penetrates the demand bottom by effective wick ATR threshold × chart ATR and close is above the bottom; close ≤ bottom + effective edge-band fraction × zone height; no prior touch or elapsed bars since touch ≥ effective freshness threshold; and highest high over the approach lookback − current low ≥ effective approach ATR threshold × chart ATR. Short checks are: high penetrates the supply top by effective wick ATR threshold × chart ATR and close is below the top; close ≥ top − effective edge-band fraction × zone height; no prior touch or elapsed bars since touch ≥ effective freshness threshold; and current high − lowest low over the approach lookback ≥ effective approach ATR threshold × chart ATR. In soft mode, each side passes with at least the soft required count of its four checks. In strict mode, it passes with at least max(strict required count, soft required count) checks and either its wick or band check, or a strict rescue: that side's zone is closer by interval distance than the opposite side's zone, its center-nearest zone strength reaches the strict-rescue threshold, and its wick or band check passes. Off mode passes. When strict auto-tightening is enabled, derive the effective thresholds from their separate base thresholds and strict multipliers: cap wick and approach tightening by their respective cap multipliers; clamp the edge-band threshold to its lower and upper bounds and its relative floor; cap the added freshness bars by its maximum addition.
Calculation: T1 confluence = Calculate the T1 ATR using the chart ATR lookback and the T1 bars currently mapped to chart bars, including the still-forming T1 bar in realtime. Calculate T1 fast and slow EMAs on T1 bars using separate shorter and longer lookbacks, sampled onto chart bars from the T1 bar currently mapped, including the still-forming bar in realtime. Calculate T1 pivot highs and lows with equal left/right T1 swing lookback on T1 bars, sampled onto chart bars from the T1 bar currently mapped. On each pivot, retain the latest supply or demand band centered on that pivot's T1 high or low, with half-height equal to the T1 zone ATR multiplier × T1 ATR. T1 trend is up when fast EMA > slow EMA and down when fast EMA < slow EMA. T1 demand or supply proximity means the current chart-bar range intersects the respective latest band.
Long: Doji AND current chart bar is within proximity ATR multiplier × chart ATR of at least one signal-zone side AND demand penetration > 0 AND, if previous-candle side selection is enabled, the previous chart bar is bearish; otherwise resolved side is demand and close ≥ (open + close) ÷ 2. If one-bar confirmation is disabled, require this long base condition on the current bar; if enabled, require it on the previous chart bar and current close > previous close + confirmation ATR multiplier × current chart ATR. Require the long quality result. Require the long candidate to be at least the cooldown lookback after the preceding long candidate that passed quality and side exclusivity before T1 and strength gates. If the T1 trend gate is enabled, require T1 trend up; if T1 demand-proximity gate is enabled, require T1 demand proximity. In soft quality mode, require either nearest-zone strength to reach the soft strength threshold; in strict mode, require either nearest-zone strength to reach the strict strength threshold. When long and short candidates both pass on the same bar, retain long only if resolved side is demand.
Short: Doji AND current chart bar is within proximity ATR multiplier × chart ATR of at least one signal-zone side AND supply penetration > 0 AND, if previous-candle side selection is enabled, the previous chart bar is bullish; otherwise resolved side is supply and close ≤ (open + close) ÷ 2. If one-bar confirmation is disabled, require this short base condition on the current bar; if enabled, require it on the previous chart bar and current close < previous close − confirmation ATR multiplier × current chart ATR. Require the short quality result. Require the short candidate to be at least the cooldown lookback after the preceding short candidate that passed quality and side exclusivity before T1 and strength gates. If the T1 trend gate is enabled, require T1 trend down; if T1 supply-proximity gate is enabled, require T1 supply proximity. In soft quality mode, require either nearest-zone strength to reach the soft strength threshold; in strict mode, require either nearest-zone strength to reach the strict strength threshold. When long and short candidates both pass on the same bar, retain short only if resolved side is supply.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ target price; stop price = low of the signal bar when one-bar confirmation is disabled, otherwise low of the preceding doji bar, minus stop-buffer parameter × minimum tick; target price = entry price + target risk/reward multiplier × max(entry price − stop price, minimum tick).
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ target price; stop price = high of the signal bar when one-bar confirmation is disabled, otherwise high of the preceding doji bar, plus stop-buffer parameter × minimum tick; target price = entry price − target risk/reward multiplier × max(stop price − entry price, minimum tick).
