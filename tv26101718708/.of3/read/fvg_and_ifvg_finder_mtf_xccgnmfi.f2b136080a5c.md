Long: low[1] > high[3] on chart bars, or on bars of any enabled higher timeframe T1–T5 using that timeframe’s completed bars [1] and [3], with the resulting values sampled on chart bars; if wait-for-close is enabled, the detection bar must be confirmed. OR, on a chart bar, an active bearish FVG from the chart timeframe or T1–T5 is filled at its top: close >= top in Close mode or high >= top in H/L mode; the top is low[3] at formation.
Short: high[1] < low[3] on chart bars, or on bars of any enabled higher timeframe T1–T5 using that timeframe’s completed bars [1] and [3], with the resulting values sampled on chart bars; if wait-for-close is enabled, the detection bar must be confirmed. OR, on a chart bar, an active bullish FVG from the chart timeframe or T1–T5 is filled at its bottom: close <= bottom in Close mode or low <= bottom in H/L mode; the bottom is high[3] at formation.
