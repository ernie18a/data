Calculation: selected cumulative flow = cumulative sum of sign(change(close)) * volume for OBV, or cumulative sum of nz(footprint delta * adjustment) for CVD; adjustment is 1 + row influence when delta and footprint bias agree, max(0, 1 - row influence) when they conflict, and 1 otherwise; footprint bias is the sum of the close’s direction relative to the POC midpoint and value-area bounds, plus the POC buy/sell imbalance direction
Calculation: transformed flow price = EMA(if normalized flow deviation > 0 then high + normalized flow deviation else low + normalized flow deviation, source smoothing lookback), where normalized flow deviation = (selected cumulative flow - SMA(selected cumulative flow, volume smoothing lookback)) / standard deviation(selected cumulative flow - SMA(selected cumulative flow, volume smoothing lookback), volatility lookback) * standard deviation(high - low, volatility lookback)
Calculation: flow MACD = selected moving average(EMA(transformed flow price, source smoothing lookback), short lookback) - adaptive EMA(close, long lookback, prior adaptive confidence, adaptation strength), where adaptive EMA uses effective length max(1, long lookback * (1 + adaptation strength * (1 - 2 * min(1, abs(prior adaptive confidence) / confidence normalization))))
Calculation: directional channel = recursive threshold filter of the linear regression projection of flow MACD over the slope lookback; the threshold is the cumulative mean absolute difference between the projection and the prior channel value; the channel value follows the projection when it exceeds the prior value by more than the threshold or falls below it by more than the threshold, and otherwise holds; channel direction is the sign of its change, carried forward when unchanged
Calculation: adaptive confidence = 100 * weighted mean of bullish votes - 50, with votes 1, 0, or 0.5 for positive, negative, or zero directions of flow MACD, directional channel, adjusted footprint delta, and footprint bias; each weight is the SMA over the adaptive lookback of prior-bar prediction hits against close direction, scored 1 for a match, 0 for a mismatch, and 0.5 if either direction is zero; use neutral confidence when total weight is zero
Calculation: upper pivot tracker = projected flow MACD at each new rolling high over the pivot lookback, carried forward otherwise; lower pivot tracker = projected flow MACD at each new rolling low over the pivot lookback, carried forward otherwise
Long: channel direction is positive
Short: channel direction is not positive
Long: channel direction changes from nonpositive to positive
Short: channel direction changes from nonnegative to negative
Long: adaptive confidence is greater than or equal to zero
Short: adaptive confidence is less than zero
Long: lower pivot tracker = lower pivot tracker[2] AND lower pivot tracker[2] != lower pivot tracker[3]
Short: upper pivot tracker = upper pivot tracker[2] AND upper pivot tracker[2] != upper pivot tracker[3]
