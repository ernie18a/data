Short: on a completed T1 bar, high > highest(high, sweep-lookback parameter, T1)[1] AND close < highest(high, sweep-lookback parameter, T1)[1] AND the prior T1 bar did not satisfy the same high-sweep condition.
Long: enter at the next chart bar's open if flat and the prior confirmed chart bar was the first later bar after a completed T1 crossover or crossunder of SMA(close, longer lookback, T1) and anchored VWAP(hlc3, volume, configured anchor-time parameter, T1), excluding the VWAP reset bar; the trigger uses chart-bar OHLC and current T1 SMA and anchored VWAP(hlc3, volume, TX-cross bar, T1) values, which can be still forming if the chart bar closes before the T1 bar; the selected breakout mode requires either close > both lines or low > both lines; a short stop-out permits one opposite trigger for the same cross if no newer cross replaced it; a new cross reanchors the trigger VWAP and replaces the setup, and an anchor-time reset clears an armed setup; discard the entry if not flat at the next open.
Short: enter at the next chart bar's open if flat and the prior confirmed chart bar was the first later bar after a completed T1 crossover or crossunder of SMA(close, longer lookback, T1) and anchored VWAP(hlc3, volume, configured anchor-time parameter, T1), excluding the VWAP reset bar; the trigger uses chart-bar OHLC and current T1 SMA and anchored VWAP(hlc3, volume, TX-cross bar, T1) values, which can be still forming if the chart bar closes before the T1 bar; the selected breakout mode requires either close < both lines or high < both lines; a long stop-out permits one opposite trigger for the same cross if no newer cross replaced it; a new cross reanchors the trigger VWAP and replaces the setup, and an anchor-time reset clears an armed setup; discard the entry if not flat at the next open.
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after entry, low <= setup-arm chart bar low OR high >= entry price + target-distance parameter; if both levels are touched on one bar, the stop applies.
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after entry, high >= setup-arm chart bar high OR low <= entry price - target-distance parameter; if both levels are touched on one bar, the stop applies.
