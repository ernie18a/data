Long: Position is flat AND entries today < daily trade limit AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND a chart-bar close crossed above VWAP within the breakout lookback AND the lowest low since that cross through the current bar <= current VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND (rejection filter is disabled OR min(open, close) - low > abs(close - open) * wick/body ratio) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume factor) AND (distance filter is disabled OR abs(close - VWAP) > distance factor * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter is disabled OR close > VWAP(T1, still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars))
Short: Shorts are enabled AND position is flat AND entries today < daily trade limit AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND a chart-bar close crossed below VWAP within the breakout lookback AND the highest high since that cross through the current bar >= current VWAP AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND (rejection filter is disabled OR high - max(open, close) > abs(close - open) * wick/body ratio) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume factor) AND (distance filter is disabled OR abs(close - VWAP) > distance factor * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter is disabled OR close < VWAP(T1, still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars))
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) OR high >= entry price + target multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) OR low <= entry price - target multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
