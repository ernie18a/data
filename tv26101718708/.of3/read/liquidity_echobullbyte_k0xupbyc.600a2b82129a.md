Calculation: Calibration wick/body ratio = SMA(upper wick + lower wick, calibration lookback) / SMA(abs(close - open), calibration lookback), or the fallback ratio when average body is zero; upper wick = high - max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) - low
Calculation: Calibration volume variation = standard deviation(volume, calibration lookback) / SMA(volume, calibration lookback), or its fallback when average volume is zero
Calculation: Volume-average lookback = long volume lookback when calibration volume variation exceeds its cutoff, otherwise short volume lookback
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback), or its fallback when average volume is zero
Calculation: Raw vacuum threshold = clamp(vacuum base + calibration volume variation * vacuum slope, vacuum floor, vacuum ceiling)
Calculation: Raw wick threshold = clamp(calibration wick/body ratio * wick factor, wick floor, wick ceiling)
Calculation: Raw pressure threshold = clamp(pressure base + (1 - calibration wick/body ratio / pressure scale) * pressure slope, pressure floor, pressure ceiling)
Calculation: Effective vacuum threshold = when auto-calibration is on, min(raw vacuum threshold * loose-bias factor, vacuum cap); otherwise manual vacuum threshold
Calculation: Effective wick threshold = when auto-calibration is on, max(raw wick threshold * tight-bias factor, wick floor); otherwise manual wick threshold
Calculation: Effective pressure threshold = when auto-calibration is on, max(raw pressure threshold * tight-bias factor, pressure floor); otherwise manual pressure threshold
Calculation: Grind path is enabled when its setting is on or calibration wick/body ratio is below the automatic grind cutoff
Calculation: Close-location delta = close location within the bar's high-low range, normalized from negative one at the low to positive one at the high, using minimum price increment when the range is zero
Calculation: Pressure gradient = (current close-location delta - close-location delta at the gradient span bars ago) / gradient span
Calculation: T1 range ratio = current T1 high-low range divided by the mean T1 range over the compression-average lookback; evaluated on chart bars from T1 values, using the T1 bar still forming live and preceding completed T1 bars
Calculation: Register a T1 compression zone from the current T1 high and low when T1 range ratio is below the compression threshold and the zone overlaps no live zone; expire it when its chart-bar age exceeds zone lifetime; count a touch when high >= zone high - ATR * zone-touch margin and low < zone high, or low <= zone low + ATR * zone-touch margin and high > zone low
Calculation: Bull level = low of the nearest live compression zone below close whose touch count meets the minimum; when T1 zones are disabled, use the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, left span, right span)
Calculation: Bear level = high of the nearest live compression zone above close whose touch count meets the minimum; when T1 zones are disabled, use the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, left span, right span)
Long: at chart-bar close, (volume is valid and volume ratio has stayed below the effective vacuum threshold for the required consecutive bars and low < bull level and close >= bull level - ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum body distance and lower wick >= ATR(ATR lookback) * effective wick threshold and pressure gradient > effective pressure threshold) OR (grind path is enabled and a low within the prior grind lookback bars < bull level - ATR(ATR lookback) * grind excursion margin and close > bull level and close < bull level + ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum grind return distance and close > open and pressure gradient > effective pressure threshold)
Short: at chart-bar close, (volume is valid and volume ratio has stayed below the effective vacuum threshold for the required consecutive bars and high > bear level and close <= bear level + ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum body distance and upper wick >= ATR(ATR lookback) * effective wick threshold and pressure gradient < -effective pressure threshold) OR (grind path is enabled and a high within the prior grind lookback bars > bear level + ATR(ATR lookback) * grind excursion margin and close < bear level and close > bear level - ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum grind return distance and close < open and pressure gradient < -effective pressure threshold)
Long: at chart-bar close, no active position and a bullish sweep was recorded within the sweep window and calibration lookback has elapsed and ATR and EMA are available and close > open and abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum commitment body and volume is valid and volume ratio >= minimum commitment volume ratio and (zone-boundary close filter is disabled or close > sweep level) and (EMA filter is disabled or close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) and (two-bar confirmation is disabled or close[1] > open[1] and close[1] > sweep level) and (signal spacing is disabled or chart bars since the last signal >= minimum signal spacing)
Short: at chart-bar close, no active position and a bearish sweep was recorded within the sweep window and calibration lookback has elapsed and ATR and EMA are available and close < open and abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum commitment body and volume is valid and volume ratio >= minimum commitment volume ratio and (zone-boundary close filter is disabled or close < sweep level) and (EMA filter is disabled or close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) and (two-bar confirmation is disabled or close[1] < open[1] and close[1] < sweep level) and (signal spacing is disabled or chart bars since the last signal >= minimum signal spacing)
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry price - ATR on entry bar * stop distance; initial short stop = entry price + ATR on entry bar * stop distance
Calculation: Active long stop = the greatest of the initial long stop, entry price when breakeven is enabled and first target is hit, and the ratcheting trailing stop when enabled and started; trailing stop = max(previous trailing stop or current stop, lowest low over the trailing lookback - current ATR * trailing offset)
Calculation: Active short stop = the least of the initial short stop, entry price when breakeven is enabled and first target is hit, and the ratcheting trailing stop when enabled and started; trailing stop = min(previous trailing stop or current stop, highest high over the trailing lookback + current ATR * trailing offset)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + ATR on entry bar * first target distance
Exit: short, low <= entry price - ATR on entry bar * first target distance
Exit: long, high >= entry price + ATR on entry bar * second target distance or low <= active long stop or (first target is not hit and chart bars since entry > target timeout)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - ATR on entry bar * second target distance or high >= active short stop or (first target is not hit and chart bars since entry > target timeout)
