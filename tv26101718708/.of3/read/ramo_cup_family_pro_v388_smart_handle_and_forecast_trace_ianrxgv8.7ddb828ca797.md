Calculation: RiskOK(E, St, s) = s * (E - St) > 0 AND 100 * abs(E - St) / abs(E) <= MaxRiskPct AND abs(E - St) / ATR(AtrLen) <= MaxRiskATR
Calculation: BowlExtreme(L, s) = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over ages E to E + max(12, L - 2E) - 1, where age = bars back from the current bar and E = max(4, round(EdgePct * L / 100))
Calculation: Neckline(L, s) = highest high (lowest low if s = -1) over ages B + A to min(L - 1, B + M), where B = age of the BowlExtreme(L, s) bar, A = max(5, round(ArmMinPct * L / 100)), M = max(A + 4, round(ArmMaxPct * L / 100))
Calculation: RightRim(L, s) = highest high (lowest low if s = -1) over ages max(0, B - M) to B - A
Calculation: Depth(L, s) = abs(Neckline(L, s) - BowlExtreme(L, s))
Calculation: Completion(L, s) = clamp(100 * s * (RightRim(L, s) - BowlExtreme(L, s)) / Depth(L, s), 0, 100)
Calculation: RimDiff(L, s) = 100 * minimum over ages max(0, B - M) to B - A of abs(high (low if s = -1) - Neckline(L, s)) / Depth(L, s)
Calculation: CurveFit(L, s) = 100 * share of SampleCount sample ages a = round(k * (L - 1) / (SampleCount - 1)), k = 0 to SampleCount - 1, whose close lies within CurveTol * Depth(L, s) of BowlExtreme(L, s) + s * Depth(L, s) * n^CurvePower, where n = min(1, abs(a - B) / span) and span = max(1, L - 1 - B) if a >= B, else max(1, B)
Calculation: BottomWidth(L, s) = 100 * share of the same sample ages with s * (close - BowlExtreme(L, s)) <= BottomDepthFrac * Depth(L, s)
Calculation: ArmConsensus(L, s) = s * (LeftArm - CenterArm) >= ArmSlopePct * Depth(L, s) AND s * (RightArm - CenterArm) >= ArmSlopePct * Depth(L, s) AND min(s * (LeftArm - CenterArm), s * (RightArm - CenterArm)) >= CenterLiftPct(s) * Depth(L, s), where LeftArm, CenterArm and RightArm = SMA(close, ArmSeg) at ages min(L - ArmSeg - 1, round(f * L)) for f = LeftArmFrac, CenterArmFrac, RightArmFrac; ArmSeg = max(3, round(ArmSegPct * L / 100)); CenterLiftPct(s) = InvCenterLiftPct if s = -1, else ArmSlopePct
Calculation: Momentum(L, s) = s * (close - EMA(close, MomLen)) >= -MomATR * ATR(AtrLen), where MomLen = max(4, min(12, ArmSeg))
Calculation: Valid(L, s) = if StrictMode: 100 * Depth(L, s) / abs(Neckline(L, s)) in [MinDepthPct, MaxDepthPct] AND Completion >= MinCompletion AND CurrentRecovery >= MinCurrentRecovery AND Momentum AND 100 * B / (L - 1) in [MinBottomPos, MaxBottomPos] AND ArmConsensus AND BottomWidth >= max(MinBottomWidth, StrictBottomWidth) AND CurveFit >= max(MinCurveFit, StrictCurveFit) AND RimOK; otherwise the same depth, completion, CurrentRecovery, Momentum and ArmConsensus tests AND (CurveFit >= MinCurveFit OR BottomWidth >= MinBottomWidth OR RimOK) AND PassCount >= PassMin; where CurrentRecovery = 100 * s * (close - BowlExtreme(L, s)) / Depth(L, s), RimOK = Completion < CompletePct OR RimDiff <= min(MaxRimDiff, RimMatchTol), and PassCount = number of true tests among depth, bottom position, completion, bottom width, curve fit, rim, the three arm tests, current recovery and momentum
Calculation: Completed(L, s) = Valid(L, s) AND Completion(L, s) >= CompletePct AND RimDiff(L, s) <= min(MaxRimDiff, RimMatchTol)
Calculation: Breakout(L, s) = Valid(L, s) AND s * (close - Neckline(L, s)) > abs(Neckline(L, s)) * BreakBufPct / 100
Calculation: FreshBreak(L, s) = Breakout(L, s) AND NOT Breakout(L, s)[1]
Calculation: Retest(L, s) = Valid(L, s) AND 1 <= bars since FreshBreak(L, s) <= RetestBars AND s * (AdvPrice - Neckline(L, s)) <= tol AND s * (close - Neckline(L, s)) >= -tol, where AdvPrice = low if s = +1, else high, and tol = abs(Neckline(L, s)) * RetestTolPct / 100
Calculation: HandleExt(L, s) = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over HandleLen bars, where HandleLen = max(5, min(45, round(HandleBarsPct * L / 100)))
Calculation: Handle(L, s) = Completed(L, s) AND HandleMinPct <= 100 * max(0, s * (Neckline(L, s) - HandleExt(L, s))) / Depth(L, s) <= min(HandleMaxPct, HandleUpperPct) AND s * (FarExt - Neckline(L, s)) <= HandleContainFrac * Depth(L, s), where FarExt = highest high (lowest low if s = -1) over HandleLen bars
Calculation: Target(L, s) = Neckline(L, s) + s * TargetFactor * Depth(L, s)
Calculation: HLCand(L, s) = a right-arm swing point of side s (pivot low if s = +1, pivot high if s = -1, confirmed by SwingLeft and SwingRight bars, strictly between BowlExtreme(L, s) and Neckline(L, s), age <= max(6, round(SwingAgePct * L / 100))) where Swing = its price, Entry = Swing + s * EntryBufATR * ATR(AtrLen), Stop = Swing - s * StopATR * ATR(AtrLen), abs(close - Entry) <= EntryNearATR * ATR(AtrLen), and RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s)
Calculation: TrendCand(L, s) = NOT Breakout(L, s) AND abs(close - TrendBase) <= TrendRetestATR * ATR(AtrLen), where Support = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over SupportLen = max(6, min(40, round(SupportPct * L / 100))) bars, TrendBase = Support + TrendBaseFrac * (Neckline(L, s) - Support), Entry = TrendBase, Stop = Support - s * StopATR * ATR(AtrLen), and RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s)
Calculation: HandleCand(L, s) = Handle(L, s) AND abs(close - Entry) <= EntryNearATR * ATR(AtrLen), where Entry = HandleExt(L, s) + s * EntryBufATR * ATR(AtrLen), Stop = HandleExt(L, s) - s * HandleStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), and RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s)
Calculation: SweepCand(L, s) = s * (AdvPrice - Ref) < 0 AND s * (close - Ref) > 0 AND s * (close - open) > 0 AND abs(close - Entry) <= SweepNearFactor * EntryNearATR * ATR(AtrLen), where Ref = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over the SweepLen bars before the current bar, Entry = Ref + s * EntryBufATR * ATR(AtrLen), Stop = AdvPrice - s * SweepStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), and RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s)
Calculation: BreakCand(L, s) = FreshBreak(L, s) AND abs(close - Neckline(L, s)) <= EntryNearATR * ATR(AtrLen), where Entry = close, Stop = Neckline(L, s) - s * RetestStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), and RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s)
Calculation: RetestCand(L, s) = Retest(L, s) AND RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s), where Entry = Neckline(L, s), Stop = FastSupport - s * RetestStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), and FastSupport = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over FastSupLen = max(3, min(12, round(FastSupPct * L / 100))) bars
Calculation: SafeTrig(L, s) = (Completed(L, s) OR Breakout(L, s)) AND s * (close - Entry) >= 0 AND RiskOK(Entry, Stop, s), where Entry = Neckline(L, s) + s * SafeBufATR * ATR(AtrLen), and Stop = whichever of Neckline(L, s) - s * SafeStopATR * ATR(AtrLen) and (lowest low, or highest high if s = -1, over SafeLen bars) - s * SafeStop2ATR * ATR(AtrLen) lies farther from Entry
Calculation: AEValid(s) = the two most recent confirmed pivots of side s (pivot lows if s = +1, pivot highs if s = -1; span AEPivotLeft and AEPivotRight) are AEMinGap to AEMaxGap bars apart, their prices differ by at most AELevelTolPct %, both are narrow (width <= AdamMaxWidth) or both wide (width >= EveMinWidth), the newest pivot is no older than AEMaxPivotAgePct % of the gap and no more extreme price has printed since it, AEHeight = abs(AENeck - AEExt) >= AEMinHeightATR * ATR(AtrLen), AECompletion(s) >= AEMinCompletion and >= AENeckApproachPct, and s * (AENeck + s * AEHeight * TargetFactor - close) > TargetAheadATR * ATR(AtrLen); where AENeck = highest high (lowest low if s = -1) between the two pivots, AEExt = the more extreme of the two pivot prices, NewestPivot = newest pivot price, AECompletion(s) = clamp(100 * s * (close - AEExt) / AEHeight, 0, 100)
Calculation: AEScore(s) = clamp(0.25 * LevelSimilarity + 0.20 * 88 + 0.15 * 84 + 0.25 * AECompletion(s) + 0.15 * Freshness + 3 * AEFreshBreak + 4 * AERetest, 0, 99), where LevelSimilarity = clamp(100 - 100 * level difference % / AELevelTolPct, 0, 100), Freshness = clamp(100 - 35 * newest pivot age / max(AEPivotRight + 1, round(gap * AEMaxPivotAgePct / 100)), 55, 100)
Calculation: AEPick(s) = AEScore(s) if AEValid(s) AND (s = +1 OR BearCtx) AND AEScore(s) >= max(ScoreThreshold, AEStrongScore) AND AECompletion(s) >= AEMinCompletion AND (CupBest(s) < ScoreThreshold OR AEScore(s) >= CupBest(s) - 4 OR AEScore(s) >= CupBest(s) + AEVsCupMargin), else 0; BearCtx = close <= EMA(close, FastLen) AND EMA(close, FastLen) < EMA(close, FastLen)[SlopeLag]
Calculation: AETrig(s) = AEValid(s) AND ((AECompletion(s) >= AEEntryCompletion AND s * (close - AENeck) <= abs(AENeck) * BreakBufPct / 100 AND abs(close - NewestPivot) <= EntryNearATR * ATR(AtrLen) AND RiskOK(NewestPivot, NewestPivot - s * StopATR * ATR(AtrLen), s)) OR (1 <= bars since the AE neckline break <= RetestBars AND s * (AdvPrice - AENeck) <= abs(AENeck) * RetestTolPct / 100 AND s * (close - AENeck) >= -abs(AENeck) * RetestTolPct / 100 AND RiskOK(AENeck, AdvExt6 - s * RetestStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), s))), where AdvExt6 = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over 6 bars
Calculation: SmartBreak(s) = a handle of side s has formed on a Completed structure (100 * HandleDepth / Depth in [HandleMinPct, HandleMaxPct], handle extreme within HandleUpperPct of Depth from Neckline, age <= HandleMaxBars, recovery from the extreme >= HandleRecoverPct) and price then first closed beyond HandlePivot = highest high (lowest low if s = -1) over HandlePivotLen bars before that bar, and the handle stays in this break state until reset (too deep, too old, or HandleStopHit); BreakLevel = HandlePivot at the break; HandleEntry = BreakLevel + s * HandleEntryATR * ATR(AtrLen); HandleStop = HandleTip - s * HandleStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), where HandleTip = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) since the handle formed
Calculation: SmartEarly(s) = SmartBreak(s) AND s * (close - HandleEntry) <= EntryNearATR * ATR(AtrLen) AND RiskOK(HandleEntry, HandleStop, s)
Calculation: SmartRetest(s) = SmartBreak(s) AND 1 <= bars since the break AND s * (AdvPrice - BreakLevel) <= tol AND s * (close - BreakLevel) >= -tol AND RiskOK(BreakLevel, HandleStop, s), where tol = abs(BreakLevel) * HandleRetestTolPct / 100
Calculation: HandleStopHit(s) = SmartBreak(s) AND s * (AdvPrice - HandleTip) < -HandleInvalATR * ATR(AtrLen)
Calculation: RelaxedOK(L, s) = s * (Neckline(L, s) - BowlExtreme(L, s)) > 0 AND 100 * Depth(L, s) / abs(Neckline(L, s)) in [RelaxDepthLo * MinDepthPct, RelaxDepthHi * MaxDepthPct] AND Completion(L, s) >= max(RelaxMinCompletion, MinCompletion - RelaxCompSlack) AND s * (close - Neckline(L, s)) >= -RelaxArmFrac * Depth(L, s) AND s * (Target(L, s) - close) > RelaxTargetATR * ATR(AtrLen) AND trend context of side s, where for s = +1 close >= EMA(close, FastLen) OR EMA(close, FastLen) > EMA(close, FastLen)[SlopeLag], and for s = -1 close <= EMA(close, FastLen) AND EMA(close, FastLen) < EMA(close, FastLen)[SlopeLag]
Calculation: RelaxedTrig(s) = NOT StrictMode AND RelaxedOK(LStar(s), s) AND s * (close - Neckline(LStar(s), s)) <= PreBreakATR * ATR(AtrLen) AND RiskOK(close, AdvExt8 - s * RelaxStopATR * ATR(AtrLen), s), where AdvExt8 = lowest low (highest high if s = -1) over 8 bars
Calculation: StrongTrend(s) = s * (close - EMA(close, FastLen)) > 0 AND s * (EMA(close, FastLen) - EMA(close, FastLen)[SlopeLag]) >= 0 AND s * (close - EMA(close, SlowLen)) > 0
Calculation: CupScore(L, s) = clamp(0.16 * CenterScore + 0.22 * Completion + 0.14 * RimScore + 0.16 * WidthScore + 0.32 * CurveScore + 3 * Completed + 3 * Handle + 3 * FreshBreak + 5 * Retest + 2 * (FreshBreak AND volume > VolMult * SMA(volume, VolLen)) + ActionBonus - 28 * Late, 0, 99) if Valid(L, s) AND s * (Target(L, s) - close) > TargetAheadATR * ATR(AtrLen), else 0; where CenterScore = clamp(100 - 2 * abs(100 * B / (L - 1) - 50), 0, 100), RimScore = 60 if Completion < CompletePct, else clamp(100 - 100 * RimDiff / MaxRimDiff, 0, 100), WidthScore = clamp(100 * BottomWidth / max(2 * MinBottomWidth, 7), 0, 100), CurveScore = clamp(72 * CurveFit / max(MinCurveFit, 1), 0, 100), ActionBonus = 9 if RetestCand, else 8.5 if SweepCand, else 8 if HandleCand, else 7.5 if TrendCand, else 7 if HLCand, else 4 if BreakCand, else 1 if Breakout AND s * (close - Neckline) < 0.40 * abs(Target - Neckline), else 0, and Late = Breakout AND NOT Retest AND NOT (HLCand OR TrendCand OR HandleCand OR SweepCand OR BreakCand) AND (s * (close - Neckline) >= 0.72 * abs(Target - Neckline) OR abs(close - Neckline) >= 3 * ATR(AtrLen))
Calculation: Pick(L, s) = CupScore(L, s) + RankStep * (k - 1) + MacroPref * L / Lmax, where k = index of L in the scale set and Lmax = its largest lookback, if CupScore(L, s) - ContextPenalty * (ContextSide = -s AND L <= SmallL) >= ScoreThreshold AND (s = +1 OR BearCtx), else 0
Calculation: CupBest(s) = max over L of Pick(L, s)
Calculation: LStar(s) = the L that attains CupBest(s), or the largest L with RelaxedOK(L, s) when CupBest(s) = 0
Calculation: BestPick(s) = max(CupBest(s), AEPick(s)); if that is 0 and StrictMode is off, ScoreThreshold when RelaxedOK(L, s) holds for some L
Calculation: ContextSide = LocalSide on the timeframe T among T_D, T_12H, T_6H, T_4H (last completed bars) with the largest LocalScore, if that LocalScore >= ContextStrong and ContextFilterOn, else 0
Calculation: LocalScore = max(BestPick(+1), BestPick(-1)) on the chart timeframe, with LocalSide = +1 if BestPick(+1) >= BestPick(-1), else -1
Calculation: ExitStruct(s) = s * (close - Extreme(s)) < -InvalATR * ATR(AtrLen) OR s * (close - Target(s)) >= 0 OR (StrongTrend(-s) AND s * (close - Neckline(s)) < -ConflictATR * ATR(AtrLen)), where Neckline(s), Extreme(s), Target(s) = those of the structure of side s that attains BestPick(s) (the cup at LStar(s), or the AE pair when AEPick(s) wins)
Calculation: LongState = (BestPick(+1) > 0 AND BestPick(+1) >= BestPick(-1)) OR (LongState[1] AND NOT ExitStruct(+1))
Calculation: ShortState = BestPick(-1) > BestPick(+1) OR (ShortState[1] AND NOT ExitStruct(-1))
Calculation: EntryTrig(s) = SafeTrig(LStar(s), s) OR RetestCand(LStar(s), s) OR HLCand(LStar(s), s) OR TrendCand(LStar(s), s) OR HandleCand(LStar(s), s) OR SweepCand(LStar(s), s) OR BreakCand(LStar(s), s) OR AETrig(s) OR SmartEarly(s) OR SmartRetest(s) OR (NOT StrictMode AND RelaxedTrig(s))
Long: LongState
Short: ShortState
Long: LongState AND EntryTrig(+1)
Short: ShortState AND EntryTrig(-1)
Exit: long, ExitStruct(+1) OR HandleStopHit(+1) OR low <= stop level of the long entry in force
Exit: short, ExitStruct(-1) OR HandleStopHit(-1) OR high >= stop level of the short entry in force
Long: for T* = the scanned timeframe other than the chart timeframe with the largest LocalScore, LocalSide on T* = +1 AND LocalScore on T* >= ScoreThreshold AND LocalScore on T* > LocalScore on the chart timeframe + 0.5 AND the T* bowl is not the same physical bowl as the chart structure
Short: for T* = the scanned timeframe other than the chart timeframe with the largest LocalScore, LocalSide on T* = -1 AND LocalScore on T* >= ScoreThreshold AND LocalScore on T* > LocalScore on the chart timeframe + 0.5 AND the T* bowl is not the same physical bowl as the chart structure
Long: chart timeframe is T_D AND BridgeOn AND LocalScore on the chart timeframe < ScoreThreshold AND on T_12H the selected structure has LocalSide = +1, normal-cup type, LocalScore >= BridgeMinScore, Completion >= BridgeMinCompletion, lookback >= BridgeMinScale, and Neckline differs from BowlExtreme
