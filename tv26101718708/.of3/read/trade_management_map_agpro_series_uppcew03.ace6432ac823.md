Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), current true range, instrument minimum tick)
Calculation: Slow EMA slope = EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) - EMA(close, longer EMA lookback)[slope lookback], using the current EMA when the prior EMA is unavailable
Calculation: Long trend = EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND Slow EMA slope >= -Safe ATR × trend-slope tolerance
Calculation: Short trend = EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND Slow EMA slope <= Safe ATR × trend-slope tolerance
Calculation: Auto management side = long when Long trend and not Short trend; short when Short trend and not Long trend; otherwise long when close >= EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), short otherwise
Calculation: Relative volume passes = volume is unavailable or nonpositive, or its SMA(volume, volume lookback) is unavailable or nonpositive, or volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × relative-volume threshold
Calculation: Map invalidation reference at map start = for a long map, min(lowest(low, invalidation lookback), anchor close - Safe ATR at map start × fallback-risk multiplier × invalidation offset fraction); for a short map, max(highest(high, invalidation lookback), anchor close + Safe ATR at map start × fallback-risk multiplier × invalidation offset fraction)
Long: On a confirmed bar, more than the setup-lookback number of chart bars have elapsed AND close > highest(high, setup lookback)[1] AND (management side is Long Context OR management side is Auto and Auto management side is long) AND (activation confirmation is Close Only OR it requires trend and Long trend OR it requires trend and volume and Long trend and Relative volume passes) AND (no map has started OR the latest map was invalidated by a confirmed close below its long invalidation reference or above its short invalidation reference OR bars since the latest map start >= map cooldown)
Short: On a confirmed bar, more than the setup-lookback number of chart bars have elapsed AND close < lowest(low, setup lookback)[1] AND (management side is Short Context OR management side is Auto and Auto management side is short) AND (activation confirmation is Close Only OR it requires trend and Short trend OR it requires trend and volume and Short trend and Relative volume passes) AND (no map has started OR the latest map was invalidated by a confirmed close below its long invalidation reference or above its short invalidation reference OR bars since the latest map start >= map cooldown)
