Calculation: Reversal probability(side) = 50% if no factors are enabled; otherwise round(100 × matching enabled factor count / enabled factor count). For long, enabled factors match when RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI oversold level; volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier and close > open; close < (highest(high, zone lookback) + lowest(low, zone lookback)) / 2; min(close, open) − low >= (high − low) × wick ratio; or close > EMA(close, EMA lookback). For short, use respectively RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI overbought level; volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier and close < open; close > the same midpoint; high − max(close, open) >= (high − low) × wick ratio; or close < EMA(close, EMA lookback). Each factor has its own enable setting; enabled factors are equally weighted.
Long: An unexpired bullish setup exists from a trigger candle whose close < low[n] for every n from 1 through lookback − 1, no subsequent close has fallen below the trigger candle's low, and the first close above its high occurs within confirmation bars after the trigger; signal on that confirming bar if non-repainting mode is off, or on the following bar if it is on; and probability display is off or Reversal probability(long) >= minimum probability.
Short: An unexpired bearish setup exists from a trigger candle whose close > high[n] for every n from 1 through lookback − 1, no subsequent close has risen above the trigger candle's high, and the first close below its low occurs within confirmation bars after the trigger; signal on that confirming bar if non-repainting mode is off, or on the following bar if it is on; and probability display is off or Reversal probability(short) >= minimum probability.
