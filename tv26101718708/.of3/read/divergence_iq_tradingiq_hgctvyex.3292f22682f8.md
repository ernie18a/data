Calculation: normalized indicator = source in raw mode; (source - rolling minimum(source, normalization window)) / (rolling maximum(source, normalization window) - rolling minimum(source, normalization window)) in rolling min-max mode; (source - rolling mean(source, normalization window)) / rolling standard deviation(source, normalization window), or 0 when standard deviation is 0, in rolling z-score mode; robust scaling constant * (source - rolling median(source, normalization window)) / rolling median of the absolute deviations from that median, or 0 when median absolute deviation is 0, in robust scaling mode; rolling windows end on the current chart bar
Calculation: divergence high = close when closing-price mode is enabled, otherwise high
Calculation: divergence low = close when closing-price mode is enabled, otherwise low
Calculation: immediate high reference = latest confirmed pivot high of the normalized indicator and its paired divergence high, advanced on each later chart bar that sets a higher normalized indicator value
Calculation: immediate low reference = latest confirmed pivot low of the normalized indicator and its paired divergence low, advanced on each later chart bar that sets a lower normalized indicator value
Long: on confirmed chart bars, (pivot mode AND a new pivot low of the normalized indicator is confirmed using the left lookback and right lookahead AND the latest two confirmed pivot lows have a lower divergence low and a higher normalized indicator value) OR (immediate mode AND at least two pivot lows are stored AND normalized indicator[1] < immediate low reference AND divergence low[1] < its paired divergence low AND normalized indicator[1] < normalized indicator[2] AND normalized indicator[1] < normalized indicator AND the reference has not already signaled) OR (pivot mode AND a new pivot low of the normalized indicator is confirmed using the left lookback and right lookahead AND the latest two confirmed pivot lows have a higher divergence low and a lower normalized indicator value) OR (immediate mode AND at least two pivot lows are stored AND normalized indicator[1] > immediate low reference AND divergence low[1] > its paired divergence low AND normalized indicator[1] < normalized indicator[2] AND normalized indicator[1] < normalized indicator AND the reference has not already signaled)
Short: on confirmed chart bars, (pivot mode AND a new pivot high of the normalized indicator is confirmed using the left lookback and right lookahead AND the latest two confirmed pivot highs have a higher divergence high and a lower normalized indicator value) OR (immediate mode AND at least two pivot highs are stored AND normalized indicator[1] < immediate high reference AND divergence high[1] > its paired divergence high AND normalized indicator[1] > normalized indicator[2] AND normalized indicator[1] > normalized indicator AND the reference has not already signaled) OR (pivot mode AND a new pivot high of the normalized indicator is confirmed using the left lookback and right lookahead AND the latest two confirmed pivot highs have a lower divergence high and a higher normalized indicator value) OR (immediate mode AND at least two pivot highs are stored AND normalized indicator[1] > immediate high reference AND divergence high[1] < its paired divergence high AND normalized indicator[1] > normalized indicator[2] AND normalized indicator[1] > normalized indicator AND the reference has not already signaled)
Calculation: long stop = round to minimum tick of signal-bar close - signal-bar ATR(lookback) * stop-loss parameter in ATR mode; round to minimum tick of signal-bar close * (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100) in percentage mode
Calculation: long target = round to minimum tick of signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR(lookback) * profit-target parameter in ATR mode; round to minimum tick of signal-bar close * (1 + profit-target percentage / 100) in percentage mode
Calculation: short stop = round to minimum tick of signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR(lookback) * stop-loss parameter in ATR mode; round to minimum tick of signal-bar close * (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) in percentage mode
Calculation: short target = round to minimum tick of signal-bar close - signal-bar ATR(lookback) * profit-target parameter in ATR mode; round to minimum tick of signal-bar close * (1 - profit-target percentage / 100) in percentage mode
Exit: long, after entry, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, after entry, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
