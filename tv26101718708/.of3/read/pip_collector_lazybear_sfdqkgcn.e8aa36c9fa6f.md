Long: On chart bars, the selected data field equals or crosses its EMA with the center lookback, OR that EMA is between low and high, AND the still-forming T1, T2, and T3 bars each have the selected data field above its EMA with the same center lookback; each timeframe’s values are mapped onto chart bars.
