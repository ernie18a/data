Calculation: EMA envelope score on T1 bars = strong-envelope weight if low > EMA(high, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming bar); normal-envelope weight if high > EMA(high, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming bar) AND low > EMA(low, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming bar); negative strong-envelope weight if high < EMA(low, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming bar); negative normal-envelope weight if high < EMA(high, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming bar) AND low < EMA(low, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming bar); otherwise zero, checking conditions in this order
Calculation: Composite score = sum of the scores for three direct feeds and the negated score for the inverted feed; each feed score is its EMA envelope score plus long-EMA weight when close > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, still-forming bar), otherwise negative long-EMA weight; each direct feed also adds VWAP weight when close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3, T1, still-forming bar), otherwise negative VWAP weight
Calculation: Normalized bias = clamp((composite score + score offset) / score range × bias percentage scale, lower percentage bound, upper percentage bound)
Long: normalized bias >= long-bias threshold
Short: normalized bias < short-bias threshold
