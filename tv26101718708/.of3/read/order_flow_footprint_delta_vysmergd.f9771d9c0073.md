Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low; otherwise 0
Calculation: Signed volume delta proxy = volume when close > open, -volume when close < open, otherwise 0
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = close > open AND body ratio >= imbalance body threshold AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * impulse volume multiplier
Calculation: Bearish imbalance = close < open AND body ratio >= imbalance body threshold AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * impulse volume multiplier
Calculation: Bullish absorption = volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * absorption volume multiplier AND body ratio <= absorption body threshold AND close >= open
Calculation: Bearish absorption = volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * absorption volume multiplier AND body ratio <= absorption body threshold AND close <= open
Calculation: Upper sweep level = max(highest(high, OF2 sweep lookback)[1], high of the last completed T2 bar), calculated on chart bars
Calculation: Lower sweep level = min(lowest(low, OF2 sweep lookback)[1], low of the last completed T2 bar), calculated on chart bars
