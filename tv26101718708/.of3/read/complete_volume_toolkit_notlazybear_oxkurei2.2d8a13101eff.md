Calculation: Estimated volume delta = sum over the T1 bars within each chart bar of volume * (2 * close - high - low) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0; when intrabar analysis is disabled, use the chart bar; include the T1 bar still forming.
Calculation: Volume cluster score = the selected KDE density, histogram-bin occupancy, or their mean, calculated from chart-bar volume (sum of T1 volume within each chart bar when intrabar analysis is enabled); KDE density = mean(exp(-0.5 * ((current volume - each volume in the cluster lookback) / (bandwidth * (maximum volume - minimum volume)))^2); histogram-bin occupancy = count of lookback volumes in the current volume's equal-width bin across the minimum-to-maximum range divided by the cluster lookback; either density is 1 when the range is 0.
