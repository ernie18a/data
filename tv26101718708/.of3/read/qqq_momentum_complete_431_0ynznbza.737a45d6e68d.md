Calculation: Microstructure state = evaluated on T1 bars using confirmed wick pivots with the pivot confirmation lookback; reset to neutral and clear swing and setup state when the gap from the prior bar close exceeds the reset-gap limit, and ignore pivots until more than twice the pivot lookback bars after reset; in neutral, become Rising on higher latest and previous swing highs and lows or a close above the latest swing high plus the tick buffer, otherwise become Falling on lower latest and previous swing highs and lows or a close below the latest swing low minus the buffer, preferring Rising if both conditions hold; Falling becomes Reclaim Up on an upward close break with a latest swing low and guard=min(latest swing low, current low), while Rising becomes Reclaim Down on a downward close break with a latest swing high and guard=max(latest swing high, current high); in Reclaim Up or Reclaim Down, a close beyond the guard against the direction or expiry after the reclaim timeout changes state to the opposite direction's Falling or Rising state, otherwise the first later directional pivot sets a trigger, a later opposite pivot sets a retest only if it holds beyond the guard plus or minus the buffer, a later wick sweep cancels the retest, and a close beyond the trigger plus or minus the buffer changes state to Recovery or Decline and sets the guard to the retest; in Recovery or Decline, a close beyond the guard against the direction changes state to the opposite direction's Rising or Falling state, otherwise raise the Recovery guard to each later swing low or lower the Decline guard to each later swing high; carry state otherwise
