Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) if available, true range otherwise, minimum tick size)
Long: Long Expansion mode is selected OR Auto mode is selected AND ((close-confirmation is disabled OR bar is confirmed) AND close > highest(high[1], prior-range lookback) + Safe ATR * break-buffer multiple OR NOT ((close-confirmation is disabled OR bar is confirmed) AND close < lowest(low[1], prior-range lookback) - Safe ATR * break-buffer multiple) AND close >= EMA(close, slow lookback))
Short: Short Expansion mode is selected OR Auto mode is selected AND NOT ((close-confirmation is disabled OR bar is confirmed) AND close > highest(high[1], prior-range lookback) + Safe ATR * break-buffer multiple) AND ((close-confirmation is disabled OR bar is confirmed) AND close < lowest(low[1], prior-range lookback) - Safe ATR * break-buffer multiple OR close < EMA(close, slow lookback))
