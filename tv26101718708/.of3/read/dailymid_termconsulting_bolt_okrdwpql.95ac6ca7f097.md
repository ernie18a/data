Calculation: SMA difference = SMA(close, shorter difference lookback) - SMA(close, longer difference lookback)
Calculation: Two-bar volume-weighted price-change mean = mean((close[1] - close) * volume / force scale parameter, (close[2] - close[1]) * volume[1] / force scale parameter)
Calculation: Two-bar scaled deviation = RMS(close - SMA(close, shortest exit lookback), close[1] - SMA(close, shortest exit lookback)[1]) / deviation scale parameter
Long: SMA(low, low lookback)[1] < SMA(low, low lookback) AND SMA(high, high lookback)[1] < SMA(high, high lookback) AND SMA difference is positive AND close > SMA(close, confirmation lookback) AND Two-bar volume-weighted price-change mean < entry force threshold AND close > SMA(close, long-term filter lookback) AND SMA difference[1] < SMA difference; confirmation lookback is shorter than both difference lookbacks, and long-term filter lookback is longer than both difference lookbacks
Short: SMA(low, low lookback)[1] > SMA(low, low lookback) AND SMA(high, high lookback)[1] > SMA(high, high lookback) AND SMA difference is negative AND close < SMA(close, confirmation lookback) AND Two-bar volume-weighted price-change mean > entry force threshold AND SMA difference[1] > SMA difference; confirmation lookback is shorter than both difference lookbacks
Exit: Long, Two-bar volume-weighted price-change mean > long exit force threshold AND close < SMA(close, longer exit lookback)
Exit: Short, Two-bar volume-weighted price-change mean < short exit force threshold AND close > SMA(close, longer exit lookback) AND SMA(close, shorter exit lookback) > Two-bar scaled deviation; shortest exit lookback is shorter than shorter exit lookback, which is shorter than longer exit lookback
