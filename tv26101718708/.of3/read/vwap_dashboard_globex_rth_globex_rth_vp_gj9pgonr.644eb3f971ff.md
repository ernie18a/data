Calculation: SVP VAH/VAL = for each profile period, initialize a fixed range on its first bar at (high+low)/2 ± range factor × max(high−low, tick factor × minimum tick); divide the range into row-count equal rows, allocate each bar's volume to overlapped rows in proportion to overlap/max(high−low, minimum tick), choose the highest-volume row, then expand the value area from that row by adding the adjacent row with greater volume (choose the upper row on ties) until included volume reaches the value-area percentage of total; VAH and VAL are the upper and lower edges of the included rows
Long: close >= VWAP(OHLC4, volume, since UTC Globex reset) OR close > Globex SVP VAH OR (during the configured New York RTH session AND close >= VWAP(OHLC4, volume, since RTH session open)) OR (during the configured New York RTH session AND close > RTH SVP VAH)
Short: close < VWAP(OHLC4, volume, since UTC Globex reset) OR close < Globex SVP VAL OR (during the configured New York RTH session AND close < VWAP(OHLC4, volume, since RTH session open)) OR (during the configured New York RTH session AND close < RTH SVP VAL)
