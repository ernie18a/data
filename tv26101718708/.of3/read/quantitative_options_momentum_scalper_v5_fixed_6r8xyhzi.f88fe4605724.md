Calculation: Long trailing stop = on long entry, (if wick anchor is enabled then min(low, low[1]) else low) - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier; on each later bar while long, max(previous long trailing stop, (if wick anchor is enabled then low[1] else close) - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = on short entry, (if wick anchor is enabled then max(high, high[1]) else high) + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier; on each later bar while short, min(previous short trailing stop, (if wick anchor is enabled then high[1] else close) + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier)
Long: position is flat or short AND close > session VWAP(hlc3, volume) AND EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND (momentum filter disabled OR (trigger mode is crossover AND StochRSI %K(close, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, %K smoothing) crosses above StochRSI %D(close, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, %K smoothing, %D smoothing) AND StochRSI %K < overbought threshold) OR (trigger mode is directional state AND StochRSI %K > StochRSI %D AND StochRSI %K < overbought threshold)) AND (volatility filter disabled OR NOT (Bollinger lower(close, BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) > Keltner lower(close, KC lookback, ATR(ATR lookback), KC ATR multiplier) AND Bollinger upper(close, BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) < Keltner upper(close, KC lookback, ATR(ATR lookback), KC ATR multiplier))) AND (RVOL filter disabled OR volume <= 0 OR volume >= minimum RVOL * SMA(volume, RVOL lookback))
Short: position is flat or long AND close < session VWAP(hlc3, volume) AND EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) < EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND (momentum filter disabled OR (trigger mode is crossover AND StochRSI %K(close, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, %K smoothing) crosses below StochRSI %D(close, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, %K smoothing, %D smoothing) AND StochRSI %K > oversold threshold) OR (trigger mode is directional state AND StochRSI %K < StochRSI %D AND StochRSI %K > oversold threshold)) AND (volatility filter disabled OR NOT (Bollinger lower(close, BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) > Keltner lower(close, KC lookback, ATR(ATR lookback), KC ATR multiplier) AND Bollinger upper(close, BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) < Keltner upper(close, KC lookback, ATR(ATR lookback), KC ATR multiplier))) AND (RVOL filter disabled OR volume <= 0 OR volume >= minimum RVOL * SMA(volume, RVOL lookback))
Exit: long, close < long trailing stop
Exit: short, close > short trailing stop
