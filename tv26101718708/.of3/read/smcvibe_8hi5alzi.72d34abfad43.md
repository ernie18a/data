Calculation: BiasScore(T) = clamp(total bullish score weight minus total bearish score weight, between negative score cap and score cap), calculated on T bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T bar historically, still-forming T bar live); each bullish component adds one score weight when true: on the bar before current, lower wick (min(open, close) − low) exceeds body-wick ratio × abs(open − close), exceeds minimum wick-range ratio × (high − low), and upper wick (high − max(open, close)) is below maximum opposite-wick-range ratio × (high − low); the bar before current is bullish and the bar two bars earlier bearish, with the former close at least the latter open and the former open at most the latter close; or EMA(close, EMA lookback, T) > EMA(close shifted back one T bar, EMA lookback, T). Each bearish component subtracts one score weight when true: the mirrored upper-wick rejection; the bar before current is bearish and the bar two bars earlier bullish, with the former close at most the latter open and the former open at least the latter close; or EMA(close, EMA lookback, T) < EMA(close shifted back one T bar, EMA lookback, T).
Calculation: NetBias = short weight × BiasScore(T1) + short weight × BiasScore(T2) + middle weight × BiasScore(T3) + long weight × BiasScore(T4), where T1 < T2 < T3 < T4.
Long: NetBias > 0
Short: NetBias < 0
