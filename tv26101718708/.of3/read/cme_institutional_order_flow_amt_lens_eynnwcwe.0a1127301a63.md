Calculation: Bar range = max(high - low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Bar delta = native footprint delta, with unclassified live-bar volume estimated from close location, when footprint data is active; otherwise volume × (2 × (close - low) / bar range - 1)
Calculation: Live relative volume = volume / relative-volume baseline / elapsed-bar fraction; baseline = SMA(volume, lookback), except on chart timeframe T1 use the mean of prior confirmed bars at the same clock hour once the minimum sample count is reached; elapsed-bar fraction is one on confirmed bars and the clipped elapsed-time fraction on developing realtime bars
Calculation: Bullish delta divergence = (low < low[n1] OR low < low[n2]) AND bar delta > 0 AND (close > open OR close > low + reclaim fraction × bar range)
Calculation: Bearish delta divergence = (high > high[n1] OR high > high[n2]) AND bar delta < 0 AND (close < open OR close < high - reclaim fraction × bar range)
Calculation: Absorption effort = live relative volume >= effort-scaled relative-volume threshold OR abs(bar delta) / (SMA(abs(bar delta), lookback) if positive, otherwise delta floor) >= delta-intensity threshold
Calculation: Standard bullish absorption = outside the cash-open blackout AND lower wick / bar range >= chart-duration-selected wick threshold AND close >= low + reclaim fraction × bar range AND absorption effort AND (bar delta < 0 OR rejected bottom-row sell imbalance OR abs(bar delta) / volume >= delta-volume threshold)
Calculation: Standard bearish absorption = outside the cash-open blackout AND upper wick / bar range >= chart-duration-selected wick threshold AND close <= high - reclaim fraction × bar range AND absorption effort AND (bar delta > 0 OR rejected top-row buy imbalance OR abs(bar delta) / volume >= delta-volume threshold)
Calculation: Bullish footprint stack = at least stack-depth consecutive upper-row buy volumes each >= imbalance ratio × the adjacent lower-row sell volume, with both volumes positive and buy volume >= minimum volume
Calculation: Bearish footprint stack = at least stack-depth consecutive lower-row sell volumes each >= imbalance ratio × the adjacent upper-row buy volume, with both volumes positive and sell volume >= minimum volume
Calculation: Rejected bottom-row sell imbalance = a sell imbalance in the lowest row fraction AND (lower wick / bar range >= rejection-wick threshold OR close > open); rejected top-row buy imbalance = a buy imbalance in the highest row fraction AND (upper wick / bar range >= rejection-wick threshold OR close < open)
Calculation: Bullish absorption evidence = when native footprint data is active, bullish footprint stack OR trapped shorts (footprint POC location <= lower POC-location limit AND close > footprint POC AND (bar delta > 0 OR live relative volume >= trap-volume threshold)) OR rejected bottom-row sell imbalance OR standard bullish absorption OR (bullish delta divergence AND live relative volume >= footprint-divergence threshold); otherwise, standard bullish absorption OR (bullish delta divergence AND live relative volume >= proxy-divergence threshold)
Calculation: Bearish absorption evidence = when native footprint data is active, bearish footprint stack OR trapped longs (footprint POC location >= upper POC-location limit AND close < footprint POC AND (bar delta < 0 OR live relative volume >= trap-volume threshold)) OR rejected top-row buy imbalance OR standard bearish absorption OR (bearish delta divergence AND live relative volume >= footprint-divergence threshold); otherwise, standard bearish absorption OR (bearish delta divergence AND live relative volume >= proxy-divergence threshold)
Calculation: Bullish absorption mark = bullish absorption evidence AND NOT bearish drive AND (NOT bearish absorption evidence OR close >= open OR bar delta >= 0)
Calculation: Bearish absorption mark = bearish absorption evidence AND NOT bullish drive AND NOT bullish absorption mark
Calculation: Bullish drive = live relative volume >= drive-volume threshold × effort-scaled volume multiplier AND abs(close - open) / bar range >= effort-scaled body threshold AND close > open AND (bar delta > 0 OR bar delta / volume >= drive-delta threshold)
Calculation: Bearish drive = live relative volume >= drive-volume threshold × effort-scaled volume multiplier AND abs(close - open) / bar range >= effort-scaled body threshold AND close < open AND (bar delta < 0 OR bar delta / volume <= -drive-delta threshold)
Calculation: Bullish shelf = on a confirmed intraday bar, use the first applicable source: native bullish footprint stack; otherwise standard bullish absorption with live relative volume >= shelf-volume threshold; otherwise bullish drive with live relative volume >= drive-shelf threshold. Its zone is bounded by the footprint stack expanded to include the bar low and minimum shelf span, or by low and min(high, low + max(minimum shelf span, lower wick × shelf-wick fraction)) for absorption, or by low and min(open, low + max(minimum shelf span, (open - low) × drive-origin fraction)) for a drive. Minimum shelf span = max(tick-size floor, ATR(lookback) × ATR-span fraction, stack-depth × tick-size floor)
Calculation: Bearish shelf = on a confirmed intraday bar, use the first applicable source: native bearish footprint stack; otherwise standard bearish absorption with live relative volume >= shelf-volume threshold; otherwise bearish drive with live relative volume >= drive-shelf threshold. Its zone is bounded by the footprint stack expanded to include the bar high and minimum shelf span, or by max(low, high - max(minimum shelf span, upper wick × shelf-wick fraction)) and high for absorption, or by max(open, high - max(minimum shelf span, (high - open) × drive-origin fraction)) and high for a drive. Minimum shelf span = max(tick-size floor, ATR(lookback) × ATR-span fraction, stack-depth × tick-size floor)
Calculation: Active shelves are same-side created shelves merged when their zones overlap within the join tolerance; a bullish shelf expires when close falls below its lower edge minus the ATR stop fraction, or low falls below its lower edge minus one footprint row span after the shelf-age delay; a bearish shelf expires when close rises above its upper edge plus the ATR stop fraction, or high rises above its upper edge plus one footprint row span after the shelf-age delay; evict the oldest shelf when the capacity is exceeded
Calculation: Demand-shelf retest = close is within retest tolerance of the nearest active bullish shelf whose lower edge <= close + retest tolerance; supply-shelf retest = close is within retest tolerance of the nearest active bearish shelf whose upper edge >= close - retest tolerance; retest tolerance = max(minimum tick size × tolerance ticks, price tick step × tolerance ticks)
Long: bullish absorption mark OR bullish drive OR bullish delta divergence OR bullish shelf created OR demand-shelf retest
Short: bearish absorption mark OR bearish drive OR bearish delta divergence OR bearish shelf created OR supply-shelf retest
