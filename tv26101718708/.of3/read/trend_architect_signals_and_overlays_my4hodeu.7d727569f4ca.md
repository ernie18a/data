Calculation: Fast ribbon = ALMA(close, ribbon lookback, fast offset, shared sigma)
Calculation: Slow ribbon = ALMA(close, same ribbon lookback, slow offset, same sigma)
Calculation: Body/range ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, epsilon)
Calculation: PRISM regression = endpoint of the least-squares polynomial fit of close over the PRISM lookback, with degree d and x scaled from 0 at the oldest bar to 1 at the newest
Calculation: PRISM rail direction(source, factor, period) = direction of recursive bands source ± factor × SMA(true range, period); upper band is min(current upper band, prior upper band) unless prior source > prior upper band, when it resets to current upper band; lower band is max(current lower band, prior lower band) unless prior source < prior lower band, when it resets to current lower band; direction switches from bearish to bullish when source > upper band and from bullish to bearish when source < lower band
Long: Fast ribbon crosses above Slow ribbon
Short: Fast ribbon crosses below Slow ribbon
Long: PRISM enabled AND (both PRISM rail directions of PRISM regression are bullish now and were not both bullish on the previous bar AND Fast ribbon is not below Slow ribbon with the bearish gap widening AND the most recent PRISM raw transition was not bullish AND (bar quality is disabled OR (close >= open AND Body/range ratio >= the bar-quality threshold)) OR a pending bullish raw transition held because Fast ribbon was below Slow ribbon with the bearish gap widening is within the structure hold window and Fast ribbon crosses above Slow ribbon OR a pending bullish raw transition held for bar quality, after Fast ribbon was not below Slow ribbon with the bearish gap widening and the most recent raw transition was not bullish, is within the bar-quality hold window and (close >= open AND Body/range ratio >= the bar-quality threshold)); structure holds are enabled by the structure-lock setting, use the shorter structure hold window, and are canceled by an opposite raw transition; bar-quality holds use the longer bar-quality hold window and are canceled by an opposite raw transition; both rail periods use separate parameters, with one shorter than the other, and their factors are separate parameters
