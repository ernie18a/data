Calculation: Selected price = the configured OHLC-derived price from chart bars or T1/T2 bars, sampled on chart bars; T1/T2 use lookahead off (last completed bars historically, still-forming bars in real time).
Calculation: Keltner Channel(source, basis lookback, true-range lookback, multiplier) = EMA(source, basis lookback) ± multiplier × EMA(chart-bar true range, true-range lookback); long and short channels use separate parameters.
Calculation: MLMA(source, trend source, window, forecast, noise, band multiplier, MA type) uses MA(source, window, MA type) plus the forecast-weighted sum of recency-weighted source residuals and a correction proportional to source[1] − output[1] (zero when output[1] is unavailable). Forecast weights are the final-horizon row of Kstarᵀ × pseudoinverse(Ktrain), where Ktrain is the distance-weighted Gaussian RBF kernel matrix plus noise² times the identity, and Kstar uses the same kernel over the forecast horizon. Bands are output ± MA(abs(source − output), window, MA type) × band multiplier × clamp(ATR(volatility lookback) / MA(ATR(volatility lookback), volatility-average lookback, MA type), lower volatility bound, upper volatility bound). Long and short MLMA calculations use separate parameters and sources; all calculations run on chart bars using sampled source values.
Calculation: Long and short MLMA trend state starts bearish, changes to bullish when its trend source is above its upper band and its output rises, changes to bearish when its trend source is below its lower band and its output falls, and otherwise retains its prior state.
Calculation: KEMAD filtered price uses the first state of the configured-order scalar Kalman filter: each state initializes from source and initial covariance; each chart bar predicts the estimate from its prior value, adds process noise to prior covariance, calculates gain as predicted covariance divided by predicted covariance plus measurement noise, then updates estimate and covariance with the source measurement.
Calculation: KEMAD bands = EMA(KEMAD filtered price, smoothing lookback) ± ATR(ATR lookback) multiplied by separate upper and lower multipliers; KEMAD state starts neutral, becomes bullish above the upper band, becomes bearish below the lower band, and otherwise retains its prior state.
Long: crosses above the long channel level selected by the entry mode (upper band for breakout, basis/output for pullback, lower band for mean reversion) AND latest nonzero Pivot Point SuperTrend trend is bullish AND (Keltner selected OR (MLMA selected AND long MLMA trend is bullish)) AND (WMA confirmation disabled OR long entry price > WMA(selected WMA source, lookback)) AND (ADX momentum filter disabled OR (ADX(chart-bar high, low, close, DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > threshold AND (DI check disabled OR DI+ > DI−))) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(configured RSI source, lookback) < long overbought threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR selected volatility measure / MA(selected volatility measure, lookback, MA type) < threshold; volatility measure is chart-bar ATR or standard deviation of selected source) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR chart close < upper Bollinger Band(selected source, lookback, MA type, deviation)) AND (volume filter disabled OR MA(chart volume, smoothing lookback, MA type) / MA(chart volume, average lookback, same MA type) > threshold) AND (KEMAD filter disabled OR KEMAD state is bullish) AND long entries enabled AND account equity > 0 AND position is flat or short AND datetime is within the configured backtest interval.
Short: crosses below the short channel level selected by the entry mode (lower band for breakout, basis/output for pullback, upper band for mean reversion) AND latest nonzero Pivot Point SuperTrend trend is bearish AND (Keltner selected OR (MLMA selected AND short MLMA trend is bearish)) AND (WMA confirmation disabled OR short entry price < WMA(selected WMA source, lookback)) AND (ADX momentum filter disabled OR (ADX(chart-bar high, low, close, DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > threshold AND (DI check disabled OR DI− > DI+))) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(configured RSI source, lookback) > short oversold threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR selected volatility measure / MA(selected volatility measure, lookback, MA type) < threshold; volatility measure is chart-bar ATR or standard deviation of selected source) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR chart close > lower Bollinger Band(selected source, lookback, MA type, deviation)) AND (volume filter disabled OR MA(chart volume, smoothing lookback, MA type) / MA(chart volume, average lookback, same MA type) > threshold) AND (KEMAD filter disabled OR KEMAD state is bearish) AND short entries enabled AND account equity > 0 AND position is flat or long AND datetime is within the configured backtest interval.
Long: MLMA is selected AND long MLMA trend changes.
Short: MLMA is selected AND short MLMA trend changes.
Long: Pivot Point SuperTrend trend is bullish on consecutive chart bars.
Short: Pivot Point SuperTrend trend is bearish on consecutive chart bars.
Long: KEMAD filter and visuals are enabled AND KEMAD state is bullish.
Short: KEMAD filter and visuals are enabled AND KEMAD state is bearish.
Exit: Long, OBV(selected OBV source, chart volume) crosses below its selected moving average AND (ADX exit confirmation disabled OR exit DI+ < exit DI−) OR Pivot Point SuperTrend trend changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Short, OBV(selected OBV source, chart volume) crosses above its selected moving average AND (ADX exit confirmation disabled OR exit DI+ > exit DI−) OR Pivot Point SuperTrend trend changes from bearish to bullish.
Exit: Long, within the configured backtest interval AND chart low ≤ entry price − entry price × stop-loss fraction OR chart high ≥ entry price + entry price × stop-loss fraction × risk-reward ratio.
Exit: Short, within the configured backtest interval AND chart high ≥ entry price + entry price × stop-loss fraction OR chart low ≤ entry price − entry price × stop-loss fraction × risk-reward ratio.
Exit: Long, within the configured backtest interval AND trailing stop has activated after a favorable rise by activation fraction × current close from entry price AND chart low ≤ highest high since activation − max(current close − selected long channel lower band, 0).
Exit: Short, within the configured backtest interval AND trailing stop has activated after a favorable fall by activation fraction × current close from entry price AND chart high ≥ lowest low since activation + max(selected short channel upper band − current close, 0).
