Calculation: Anchored VWAP A = cumulative(((high + low + close) / 3) × volume) / cumulative(volume) on chart bars since anchor A; reset at the selected session or calendar boundary, or at each confirmed strict swing high or low pivot (pivot lookback P; pivot strictly exceeds or falls below all 2P neighboring highs or lows), backfilling swing sums from pivot through confirmation; simultaneous high and low count as one reset.
Calculation: Anchored sigma A = sqrt(max(cumulative(((high + low + close) / 3)^2 × volume) / cumulative(volume) − Anchored VWAP A^2, 0)) on chart bars since anchor A.
Calculation: Bands A = Anchored VWAP A ± band multiplier × adaptive factor × Anchored sigma A; adaptive factor is 1 + KER weight × (1 − KER(close, KER lookback)) when adaptive bands are enabled, otherwise 1.
Calculation: KER regime = trending when KER(close, KER lookback) is above its rolling median over regime lookback chart bars; ranging when at or below; inactive when either value is unavailable.
Calculation: VWAP side occupancy = above when at least occupancy threshold of the preceding occupancy lookback chart bars have close above Anchored VWAP A; below when at least that many do not; otherwise neutral. It is neutral until occupancy lookback bars have elapsed since anchor A; missing VWAP values count as not above.
Long: KER regime is ranging AND close[1] < lower band A[1] AND close >= lower band A AND close <= upper band A.
Short: KER regime is ranging AND close[1] > upper band A[1] AND close <= upper band A AND close >= lower band A.
Long: KER regime is trending AND VWAP side occupancy is above AND, after low <= Anchored VWAP A, close > Anchored VWAP A on the touch bar or within the hold parameter bars after the latest touch; overlapping touches extend the deadline, occupancy ceasing to be above or an anchor reset cancels the pending setup, and each setup signals once.
Short: KER regime is trending AND VWAP side occupancy is below AND, after high >= Anchored VWAP A, close < Anchored VWAP A on the touch bar or within the hold parameter bars after the latest touch; overlapping touches extend the deadline, occupancy ceasing to be below or an anchor reset cancels the pending setup, and each setup signals once.
