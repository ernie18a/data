Calculation: Anchor range = high(T1, last completed bar) - low(T1, last completed bar), mapped onto chart bars
Calculation: Body ratio = if high > low then 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low) else 0
Long: (Mode 1 enabled AND crosses above PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier AND Body ratio >= minimum body ratio) OR (Mode 2 enabled AND crosses above PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars) OR (Mode 3 enabled AND close is above PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars for the hold-count-th consecutive chart bar); the mode trigger occurs the signal-delay number of chart bars before this confirmed chart bar; same-direction blocking is disabled or the previous signal direction was not long; at least the same-direction wait period has elapsed since chart start or the previous long signal
Short: (Mode 1 enabled AND crosses below PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier AND Body ratio >= minimum body ratio) OR (Mode 2 enabled AND crosses below PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars) OR (Mode 3 enabled AND close is below PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars for the hold-count-th consecutive chart bar); the mode trigger occurs the signal-delay number of chart bars before this confirmed chart bar; same-direction blocking is disabled or the previous signal direction was not short; at least the same-direction wait period has elapsed since chart start or the previous short signal
Exit: long, crosses above PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars + first extension factor * Anchor range AND the first-target alert has not fired since the latest long signal
Exit: long, crosses above PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars + second extension factor * Anchor range
Exit: short, crosses below PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars - first extension factor * Anchor range AND the first-target alert has not fired since the latest short signal
Exit: short, crosses below PivotPoint(high, low, close) from the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars - second extension factor * Anchor range
