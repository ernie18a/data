Calculation: HTF bias = on chart bars, compare the close and EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars; use the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time; bias is bullish when the close is above the EMA, bearish when below, and neutral when equal
Calculation: EMA trend state = bullish when EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), bearish when EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback), and neutral otherwise; fast lookback < middle lookback < slow lookback
Calculation: Relative body expansion = abs(close - open) > SMA(abs(close - open), body average lookback) * body expansion multiple
Calculation: Normalized displacement = abs(close - EMA(close, value EMA lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: Long score = (EMA trend state is bullish ? major score weight : 0) + (close < EMA(close, value EMA lookback) ? minor score weight : 0) + (close > high of the previous chart bar AND Relative body expansion ? minor score weight : 0) + (HTF bias is bullish ? major score weight : 0)
Calculation: Short score = (EMA trend state is bearish ? major score weight : 0) + (close > EMA(close, value EMA lookback) ? minor score weight : 0) + (close < low of the previous chart bar AND Relative body expansion ? minor score weight : 0) + (HTF bias is bearish ? major score weight : 0)
Calculation: ATR stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) * (LONG stop ATR multiple in LONG mode, FAST stop ATR multiple in FAST or HYBRID mode)
Long: (((FAST mode AND Long score >= FAST score threshold) OR (LONG mode AND Long score >= LONG score threshold) OR (HYBRID mode AND Long score >= HYBRID score threshold)) AND target multiple >= minimum reward-to-risk AND Normalized displacement > minimum displacement AND (mode is not LONG OR HTF bias is bullish) AND (mode is LONG OR Efficiency Ratio(close, efficiency lookback) >= chop threshold)) OR (the most recently closed position was a losing long AND HTF bias is bullish)
Short: (((FAST mode AND Short score >= FAST score threshold) OR (LONG mode AND Short score >= LONG score threshold) OR (HYBRID mode AND Short score >= HYBRID score threshold)) AND target multiple >= minimum reward-to-risk AND Normalized displacement > minimum displacement AND (mode is not LONG OR HTF bias is bearish) AND (mode is LONG OR Efficiency Ratio(close, efficiency lookback) >= chop threshold)) OR (the most recently closed position was a losing short AND HTF bias is bearish)
Exit: Long, ((LONG mode AND EMA trend state is bearish) OR (FAST mode AND chart bars since entry >= FAST minimum hold bars) OR HYBRID mode) AND (low <= close - ATR stop distance OR high >= close + ATR stop distance * target multiple)
Exit: Short, ((LONG mode AND EMA trend state is bullish) OR (FAST mode AND chart bars since entry >= FAST minimum hold bars) OR HYBRID mode) AND (high >= close + ATR stop distance OR low <= close - ATR stop distance * target multiple)
