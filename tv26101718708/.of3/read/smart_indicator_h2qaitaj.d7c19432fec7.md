Calculation: Bearish structure flip = bearish engulfing AND short structure
Calculation: Long and short sweep counts = increment the long count on a bullish liquidity sweep and reset it on a bearish liquidity sweep; increment the short count on a bearish liquidity sweep and reset it on a bullish liquidity sweep; also reset the long count on a new rolling swing high and the short count on a new rolling swing low when the respective count is positive; process bullish sweep before bearish sweep
Calculation: Waiting long BOS = set on a bullish liquidity sweep with the prior highest high over the BOS lookback as its level; remain set until close exceeds that level, then clear
Calculation: Waiting short BOS = set on a bearish liquidity sweep with the prior lowest low over the BOS lookback as its level; remain set until close falls below that level, then clear
Calculation: Long setup stop = on each bullish liquidity sweep, store prior liquidity low - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiplier; retain until a bearish liquidity sweep or long invalidation
Calculation: Short setup stop = on each bearish liquidity sweep, store prior liquidity high + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiplier; retain until a bullish liquidity sweep or short invalidation
