Calculation: Bar delta = if high equals low then zero, otherwise volume * (close - open) / (high - low)
Calculation: BSL zones = on chart bars, create a zone when PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) is confirmed; its top is high at the pivot bar and its bottom is max(open, close) there, extended downward to top minus ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot bar times minimum zone-thickness multiplier if the initial height is smaller; when overlap filtering is enabled, reject a new zone that intersects an unswept BSL zone unless its top is higher, in which case remove the intersecting zone; retain the newest zones up to the maximum zones per type, removing the oldest
Calculation: SSL zones = on chart bars, create a zone when PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) is confirmed; its bottom is low at the pivot bar and its top is min(open, close) there, extended upward to bottom plus ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot bar times minimum zone-thickness multiplier if the initial height is smaller; when overlap filtering is enabled, reject a new zone that intersects an unswept SSL zone unless its bottom is lower, in which case remove the intersecting zone; retain the newest zones up to the maximum zones per type, removing the oldest
Calculation: Zone quadrant deltas = divide each zone from bottom to top into the quadrant count of equal parts; from the zone's creation bar through and including its first sweep bar, add each chart bar's bar delta times the positive overlap length between that bar's high-low range and each quadrant, divided by the bar's high-low range, or zero when the range is zero
Calculation: Outer-delta share = absolute delta of the top quadrant for a BSL zone or bottom quadrant for an SSL zone divided by the sum of the absolute deltas of all zone quadrants plus delta epsilon
Calculation: Bar-delta ratio = absolute bar delta divided by volume plus delta epsilon
