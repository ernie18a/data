Calculation: Opening-range VAH and VAL = For chart bars whose open datetime is within the opening-range interval, set range high to the maximum high and range low to the minimum low; set bin height to max(minimum price increment, round((range high - range low) / (row-count * minimum price increment)) * minimum price increment), and bin count to min(maximum-bin-count, max(1, ceil((range high - range low) / bin height))); distribute each bar's volume among bins in proportion to its high-low overlap, or wholly to the bin containing its low when high <= low; choose the lowest-price bin with greatest volume as POC; starting at POC, repeatedly choose the adjacent bin with greater volume, breaking ties toward the nearer bin and then the upper bin, and include it only if accumulated volume plus its volume does not exceed total volume times the value-area percentage; stop at the first bin that would exceed that amount; VAL is the lower edge of the lowest included bin and VAH the upper edge of the highest included bin.
Long: If the first chart bar after the opening-range interval opened below VAL, enter at the close of the first subsequent chart bar that closes above VAL and below VAH; OR if it opened strictly between VAL and VAH, enter at the close of the first subsequent chart bar that closes above VAH; the chart-bar open time in the session timezone must be at or before the entry-cutoff time.
Short: If the first chart bar after the opening-range interval opened above VAH, enter at the close of the first subsequent chart bar that closes above VAL and below VAH; OR if it opened strictly between VAL and VAH, enter at the close of the first subsequent chart bar that closes below VAL; the chart-bar open time in the session timezone must be at or before the entry-cutoff time.
Calculation: Long target = VAH for value reversion after an opening below VAL; entry price + (entry price - VAL) for value breakout after an opening strictly within the value area.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price - (VAH - entry price) for value reversion after an opening below VAL; VAL for value breakout after an opening strictly within the value area.
Calculation: Short target = VAL for value reversion after an opening above VAH; entry price - (VAH - entry price) for value breakout after an opening strictly within the value area.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price + (entry price - VAL) for value reversion after an opening above VAH; VAH for value breakout after an opening strictly within the value area.
Exit: long, (high >= long target AND (low > long stop OR conservative-fill parameter is disabled)) OR (low <= long stop AND (high < long target OR conservative-fill parameter is enabled)) OR (chart-bar open time in the session timezone >= time-close AND high < long target AND low > long stop)
Exit: short, (low <= short target AND (high < short stop OR conservative-fill parameter is disabled)) OR (high >= short stop AND (low > short target OR conservative-fill parameter is enabled)) OR (chart-bar open time in the session timezone >= time-close AND low > short target AND high < short stop)
