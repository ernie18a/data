Calculation: RSI value = optionally EMA-smoothed RSI(close, RSI lookback), using the configured RSI smoothing lookback when smoothing is enabled
Calculation: RSI score = 0 except when RSI is oversold, overbought, or between the corresponding threshold and midline; in the bullish region, use RSI score weight times the configured oversold/rising, oversold/not-rising, ordinary-rising, or ordinary/not-rising factor, respectively; in the bearish region, use the negative weight times the corresponding overbought/falling, overbought/not-falling, ordinary/falling, or ordinary/not-falling factor; when bullish divergence holds (low < lowest(low, divergence lookback)[1] AND RSI > lowest(RSI, divergence lookback)[1] AND RSI < midline), multiply the positive score by the configured divergence factor and cap it at the weight; when bearish divergence holds (high > highest(high, divergence lookback)[1] AND RSI < highest(RSI, divergence lookback)[1] AND RSI > midline), multiply the negative score by the configured divergence factor and floor it at the negative weight
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback).histogram, with fast lookback shorter than slow lookback
Calculation: MACD score = positive MACD score weight when EMA(close, impulse EMA lookback) is rising AND MACD histogram is rising; otherwise negative weight when that EMA and histogram are both falling; otherwise positive weight times the configured partial factor when histogram is positive AND that EMA is rising; otherwise negative weight times the partial factor when histogram is negative AND that EMA is falling; otherwise positive weight times the configured momentum-shift factor when histogram is negative, rising, and greater than its value two bars ago; otherwise negative weight times that factor when histogram is positive, falling, and less than its value two bars ago; otherwise zero
Calculation: VIDYA score = positive VIDYA score weight when VIDYA(close, VIDYA lookback, CMO lookback) is rising AND close is above VIDYA, multiplied by the configured non-accelerating factor unless its slope is greater than its prior-bar slope; otherwise negative weight when VIDYA is falling AND close is below VIDYA, multiplied by the configured non-accelerating factor unless its slope is less than its prior-bar slope; otherwise positive weight times the configured partial factor when VIDYA is rising AND close is below it; otherwise negative weight times that factor when VIDYA is falling AND close is above it; otherwise zero
Calculation: Volume score = zero when volume is below the configured low-volume fraction of its longer-lookback SMA; otherwise, when volume exceeds its volume-lookback SMA and OBV is above its volume-lookback SMA and rising, use positive volume score weight if buying pressure exceeds its neutral level and volume exceeds its SMA times the configured spike multiplier, the weight times the configured non-spike factor if buying pressure exceeds neutral but there is no spike, or the weight times the configured neutral-pressure factor otherwise; when volume exceeds its SMA and OBV is below its SMA and falling, apply the corresponding negative weight and selling-pressure factors; otherwise zero. OBV is On-Balance Volume; buying pressure = (close - low) / (high - low), selling pressure = (high - close) / (high - low), and both equal the configured neutral pressure when high = low
Calculation: Depth score = positive depth score weight for a bullish candle body (close > open AND abs(close - open) > (high - low) times the configured body fraction), multiplied by the configured loose-spread factor unless (high - low) / ATR(ATR lookback) is below the configured tight-spread threshold; if the lower wick exceeds the configured wick fraction of the bar range, multiply by the configured lower-wick factor and cap at the weight. Use the corresponding negative weight, loose-spread factor, and upper-wick factor for a bearish body. Otherwise use the positive partial weight when only the lower wick exceeds the wick fraction, the negative partial weight when only the upper wick does, or zero. A zero bar range has no wick rejection; when ATR is not positive, use the configured spread-ratio fallback
Calculation: Sector score = zero when current or lookback sector ETF or benchmark close is unavailable; otherwise calculate sector relative strength as EMA(sector ETF percentage change over sector lookback minus benchmark percentage change over sector lookback, sector smoothing lookback). Use positive sector score weight above the configured strength threshold, negative weight below its negative, or relative strength times weight times the configured neutral scaling factor between thresholds; multiply by effective sector weight and cap at plus or minus the sector score weight. Effective sector weight applies the configured non-US reduction when applicable; use zero for an unmapped sector when neutral-unmapped is enabled. Sector ETF and benchmark closes use current chart-bar values sampled on chart bars with lookahead off
Calculation: Normalized score = clamp((RSI score + MACD score + VIDYA score + Volume score + Depth score + Sector score) / sum of the six component score weights times the configured normalization scale, between the configured lower and upper score caps)
Long: Normalized score >= long-entry score threshold AND (volume confirmation is not required OR (volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND NOT volume below the configured low-volume fraction of its longer-lookback SMA)) AND NOT volume below that low-volume threshold AND (core alignment is not required OR (RSI score > 0 AND MACD score > 0 AND VIDYA score > 0)) AND (confirmed-bar filter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Short: Normalized score <= short-entry score threshold AND (volume confirmation is not required OR (volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND NOT volume below the configured low-volume fraction of its longer-lookback SMA)) AND NOT volume below that low-volume threshold AND (core alignment is not required OR (RSI score < 0 AND MACD score < 0 AND VIDYA score < 0)) AND (confirmed-bar filter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Long: NOT long-entry condition AND NOT short-entry condition AND Normalized score > weak-bull label threshold
Short: NOT long-entry condition AND NOT short-entry condition AND Normalized score < weak-bear label threshold
Exit: long, Normalized score < long-exit score threshold AND (confirmed-bar filter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Exit: short, Normalized score > short-exit score threshold AND (confirmed-bar filter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
