Calculation: HAWeightedNetVolume = volume * (Heikin-Ashi close - Heikin-Ashi open) / (Heikin-Ashi high - Heikin-Ashi low) * ((Heikin-Ashi high + Heikin-Ashi low + Heikin-Ashi open + Heikin-Ashi close) / 4)
Long: SMA(close, shortest SMA lookback) > EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, second-shortest EMA lookback) < EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND SMA(close, middle SMA lookback) < EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND SMA(close, longest SMA lookback) < EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, longest EMA lookback) < EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, third-shortest EMA lookback) + band multiplier * ATR(third-shortest EMA lookback) < EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND LinearRegression(HAWeightedNetVolume, regression lookback, offset = minus the same regression lookback) > net-volume threshold
Short: SMA(close, shortest SMA lookback) < EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, second-shortest EMA lookback) > EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND SMA(close, middle SMA lookback) > EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND SMA(close, longest SMA lookback) > EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, longest EMA lookback) > EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, third-shortest EMA lookback) - band multiplier * ATR(third-shortest EMA lookback) > EMA(close, shortest EMA lookback) AND LinearRegression(HAWeightedNetVolume, regression lookback, offset = minus the same regression lookback) < -net-volume threshold
