Calculation: volatility ratio = ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback)
Calculation: market regime = bullish trend when ADX(DMI lookback, ADX smoothing) > trend threshold and close > SMA(close, trend lookback); bearish trend when ADX exceeds the threshold and close < the SMA; otherwise high-volatility range when volatility ratio > high-volatility threshold; otherwise low-volatility range
Calculation: swing range = latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) minus latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), retaining each latest pivot until replaced
Calculation: body ratio = absolute value of close minus open divided by high minus low, using the zero-range body-ratio fallback when high equals low
Calculation: Fibonacci trail level and direction = when swing high > swing low, select the trail fraction from the trail-mode setting; automatic mode uses the aggressive fraction in a bullish trend, the balanced fraction in a high-volatility range, and the conservative fraction otherwise; volatility adjustment = clamp(1 / max(volatility ratio, volatility-ratio floor), adjustment bounds); bullish level = clamp(swing low + swing range * selected fraction * volatility adjustment, swing low, swing high); bearish level = clamp(swing high - swing range * selected fraction * volatility adjustment, swing low, swing high); initialize level to bullish level or close if unavailable, and initialize direction as bullish when close > SMA(close, trend lookback), otherwise bearish; on confirmed chart bars with both levels available, ratchet a bullish level upward to max(previous level, bullish level) and flip bearish only when close < level, body ratio >= body-ratio threshold, and level - close > ATR(ATR lookback) * penetration multiple, resetting the level to the bearish level; ratchet a bearish level downward to min(previous level, bearish level) and flip bullish only when close > level, body ratio >= body-ratio threshold, and close - level > ATR(ATR lookback) * penetration multiple, resetting the level to the bullish level
Calculation: confidence grade = grade the confidence scale times the weighted sum of: structure factor (when pivots exist, clamp(swing range / ATR when ATR > 0, otherwise structural fallback, times structure scaling, to score bounds), multiplied by the direction-specific structure factor); regime factor (trend-alignment factor when trend and trail directions match, high-volatility-range factor in a high-volatility range, otherwise other-regime factor); ADX factor (clamp(ADX / ADX normalization, score bounds)); volume factor (clamp(volume participation / volume normalization, score bounds), where volume participation is volume / max(EMA(volume, volume lookback), volume denominator floor) when volume > 0 and the volume-participation fallback otherwise); volatility factor (volatility-favor fallback outside the volatility bounds, otherwise clamp(maximum factor - absolute value of volatility ratio minus preferred volatility ratio divided by volatility scale, to score bounds)); and proximity factor (when SMA, trail level, and positive ATR exist, clamp(maximum factor - absolute value of trail level minus SMA(close, trend lookback) divided by ATR times proximity scale, to score bounds; otherwise proximity fallback). Use separate weights for structure, regime, ADX, volume, volatility, and proximity; assign A+, A, B, or C when the score meets its corresponding grade threshold, otherwise D
Calculation: trade risk levels = on each confirmed long or short signal, store its close as entry price; stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on the signal bar times the stop multiplier selected by trail mode; second-target distance = stop distance times the second-target multiplier selected by trail mode
Long: HMA(close, shorter ribbon lookback) > HMA(close, longer ribbon lookback)
Short: HMA(close, shorter ribbon lookback) <= HMA(close, longer ribbon lookback)
Long: market regime is bullish trend
Short: market regime is bearish trend
Long: Fibonacci trail direction is bullish
Short: Fibonacci trail direction is bearish
Long: Fibonacci trail direction changes from bearish to bullish on a confirmed chart bar AND (confidence filter is disabled OR confidence grade >= minimum confidence grade)
Short: Fibonacci trail direction changes from bullish to bearish on a confirmed chart bar AND (confidence filter is disabled OR confidence grade >= minimum confidence grade)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + second-target distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance
Exit: short, low <= entry price - second-target distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance
