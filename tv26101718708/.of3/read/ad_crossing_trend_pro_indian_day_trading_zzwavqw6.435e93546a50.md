Calculation: A/D flow = rolling sum over the rolling lookback of either EMA of ((2 × selected close − selected low − selected high) / (selected high − selected low)) × selected volume over the smoothing lookback, or that unsmoothed expression; use zero when selected high equals selected low, and divide by the rolling lookback when normalization is enabled
Long: EMA(A/D flow, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(A/D flow, longer lookback) AND the absolute difference between those EMAs is greater than the SMA of that absolute difference over the strength lookback multiplied by the strength multiplier
Short: EMA(A/D flow, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(A/D flow, longer lookback) AND the absolute difference between those EMAs is greater than the SMA of that absolute difference over the strength lookback multiplied by the strength multiplier
