Calculation: cumulative deviation(k) = first-step deviation * sum(step multiplier^j for j = 0 through k - 1)
Calculation: post-target peak = first close at or above average entry price * (1 + take-profit parameter); thereafter, the maximum of its prior value and close on each bar with close at or above that target
Long: At chart-bar close, when position is flat and there are no open trades, RSI(close, lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (current T1 bar in real time; completed T1 values historically) crosses below the oversold level between successive chart bars AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback), both calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off (current T2 bar in real time; completed T2 values historically) AND (date filter disabled OR chart-bar open datetime is within the configured inclusive date range)
Long: At chart-bar close, in a long position with a remaining configured safety order, close <= base entry price * (1 - cumulative deviation(k) / 100), where k is the next safety-order index, AND (date filter disabled OR chart-bar open datetime is within the configured inclusive date range)
Exit: Long, close <= average entry price * (1 - stop-loss parameter / 100) OR (maximum-hold exit enabled AND elapsed time since the base-entry chart-bar open datetime >= maximum-hold duration) OR (post-target peak is defined AND close <= post-target peak * (1 - trailing-deviation parameter / 100))
