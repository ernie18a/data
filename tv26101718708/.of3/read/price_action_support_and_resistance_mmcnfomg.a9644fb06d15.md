Calculation: S/R zone = ATR(ATR-lookback parameter) × zone-width parameter
Calculation: Support and resistance levels and touch counts = on each chart bar, confirm pivot lows and highs using pivot-strength parameter bars on both sides. For each confirmed pivot, compare its price with the existing same-side level using the S/R zone on the pivot bar; if the distance is at most that zone, update the level to (old level × old touch count + pivot price) / (old touch count + 1) and increment the count, otherwise set the level to the pivot price, the count to one, and the last-touch bar to the pivot bar. Then, if the current bar overlaps that level's current zone (low ≤ level + zone AND high ≥ level − zone) and at least the touch-separation parameter bars have passed since the last-touch bar, increment the count and record the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND min(open, close) − low ≥ max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment) × rejection-wick/body parameter AND min(open, close) − low > high − max(open, close)
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND prior bar close < prior bar open AND open ≤ prior bar close AND close ≥ prior bar open
Calculation: Breakout-retest setup = on a chart bar with resistance touches ≥ minimum-touch parameter, close crosses above resistance level + S/R zone × zone-fraction parameter; save that resistance level and breakout bar. On a later bar within the retest-bars parameter, a retest occurs when low ≤ saved level + current S/R zone AND high ≥ saved level − current S/R zone AND close ≥ saved level AND (bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing). Keep the setup if these retest conditions do not occur; clear it when they occur, even if the signal cooldown prevents a Long.
Calculation: Resistance warning = while a Long position is open, resistance touches ≥ minimum-touch parameter AND ((high ≥ resistance level − S/R zone AND low ≤ resistance level + S/R zone AND close < open AND high − max(open, close) ≥ max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment) × rejection-wick/body parameter AND high − max(open, close) > min(open, close) − low) OR (high ≥ resistance level − S/R zone AND low ≤ resistance level + S/R zone AND close < open AND prior bar close > prior bar open AND open ≥ prior bar close AND close ≤ prior bar open) OR (high > resistance level + S/R zone × zone-fraction parameter AND close < resistance level). Count a warning only if at least the signal-cooldown parameter bars have passed since the prior counted warning; this timestamp persists across positions. The first counted warning during a Long activates rolling protection from the following bar.
Long: Flat AND no re-entry is armed AND (no prior Long signal OR at least the signal-cooldown parameter chart bars since the prior Long signal) AND ((support touches ≥ minimum-touch parameter AND low ≤ support level + S/R zone AND high ≥ support level − S/R zone AND ((low < support level − S/R zone × zone-fraction parameter AND close > support level) OR bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing)) OR breakout-retest setup)
Long: Flat AND a prior Long exit armed re-entry AND the current bar is after that exit and within the re-entry-bars parameter AND re-entry has not occurred since that exit AND close > (saved setup level + ATR(ATR-lookback parameter) × re-entry-buffer parameter) AND prior bar close ≤ that same current threshold AND (bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing OR (close > open AND close > prior bar close)); save the setup level from the prior Long (its breakout level if it was a breakout-retest setup, otherwise its support level) at that Long and retain it through exits and re-entries
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry but before resistance-warning protection activates, close < lowest(low[1], exit-lookback parameter) sampled on the entry bar OR, on a bar after resistance-warning protection activates, close < lowest(low[1], exit-lookback parameter) on the current bar
