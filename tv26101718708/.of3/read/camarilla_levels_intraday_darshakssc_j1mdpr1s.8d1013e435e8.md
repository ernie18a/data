Calculation: Prior completed T1 range = high − low of the last completed T1 bar, evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: Reversal offset = prior completed T1 range × shared range multiplier / reversal divisor, evaluated on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: Breakout offset = prior completed T1 range × shared range multiplier / breakout divisor, evaluated on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar
Long: close crosses above last completed T1 close + breakout offset OR close crosses below last completed T1 close − reversal offset
Short: close crosses above last completed T1 close + reversal offset OR close crosses below last completed T1 close − breakout offset
