Calculation: Source = close on chart bars, or close calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically; current forming T1 bar in real time)
Calculation: Fan gap = abs(EMA(Source, fast lookback) - EMA(Source, next-longer lookback)) / EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) * 100
Long: EMA(Source, fast lookback) > EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) > EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) > EMA(Source, longest lookback) AND Fan gap >= fan gap threshold
Short: EMA(Source, fast lookback) < EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) < EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) < EMA(Source, longest lookback) AND Fan gap >= fan gap threshold
Long: EMA(Source, fast lookback) crosses above EMA(Source, next-longer lookback)
Short: EMA(Source, fast lookback) crosses below EMA(Source, next-longer lookback)
Long: EMA(Source, fast lookback) > EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) > EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) > EMA(Source, longest lookback) AND Fan gap >= fan gap threshold AND low <= EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) * (1 + retest tolerance / 100) AND low >= EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) * (1 - retest tolerance / 100) AND close > EMA(Source, next-longer lookback)
Short: EMA(Source, fast lookback) < EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) < EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) AND EMA(Source, third-longer lookback) < EMA(Source, longest lookback) AND Fan gap >= fan gap threshold AND high >= EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) * (1 - retest tolerance / 100) AND high <= EMA(Source, next-longer lookback) * (1 + retest tolerance / 100) AND close < EMA(Source, next-longer lookback)
