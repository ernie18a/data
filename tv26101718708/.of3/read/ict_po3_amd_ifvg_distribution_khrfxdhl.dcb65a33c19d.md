Calculation: Manipulation phase = chart bars from the first bar whose opening time is at least the accumulation-duration parameter after each T1 period start until the first distribution breakout or next T1 period start.
Long: During the manipulation phase, close crosses above the stored top (low[2] on its creation bar) of an unmitigated bearish FVG formed when high < low[2] AND close[1] < low[2], with no earlier chart bar after its creation having high > its stored top.
Short: During the manipulation phase, close crosses below the stored bottom (high[2] on its creation bar) of an unmitigated bullish FVG formed when low > high[2] AND close[1] > high[2], with no earlier chart bar after its creation having low < its stored bottom.
Long: During the manipulation phase, once a long IFVG signal has occurred in the current T1 period, close crosses above the maximum chart-bar high recorded from that T1 period start through the first manipulation-phase bar.
Short: During the manipulation phase, once a short IFVG signal has occurred in the current T1 period, close crosses below the minimum chart-bar low recorded from that T1 period start through the first manipulation-phase bar.
