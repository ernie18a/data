Calculation: CISD level = for a high sweep, the minimum of min(open, close) on the chart bar that set the sweep high and consecutive earlier bullish chart bars, stopping at the first non-bullish bar or the lookback; for a low sweep, the maximum of max(open, close) on the chart bar that set the sweep low and consecutive earlier chart bars with close <= open, stopping at the first bullish bar or the lookback
Long: (T1 low < prior completed T1 low AND T1 close > prior completed T1 low AND bias filter permits bullish AND (NOT (T1 high > prior completed T1 high AND T1 close < prior completed T1 high AND bias filter permits bearish) OR T1 close > T1 open)) OR (in fast sweep mode, within the current T1 period, chart price has traded below the prior completed T1 low and a chart bar closes above it AND bias filter permits bullish)
Short: (T1 high > prior completed T1 high AND T1 close < prior completed T1 high AND bias filter permits bearish AND (NOT (T1 low < prior completed T1 low AND T1 close > prior completed T1 low AND bias filter permits bullish) OR T1 close <= T1 open)) OR (in fast sweep mode, within the current T1 period, chart price has traded above the prior completed T1 high and a chart bar closes below it AND bias filter permits bearish)
Long: in confirmation mode with CISD enabled, a prior T1 model has a low sweep permitted by the bias filter AND a chart bar closes above its CISD level
Short: in confirmation mode with CISD enabled, a prior T1 model has a high sweep permitted by the bias filter AND a chart bar closes below its CISD level
Exit: Long, an active bullish T1 model exists AND (chart low < that model's T1 low OR chart high >= that model's T1 high)
Exit: Short, an active bearish T1 model exists AND (chart high > that model's T1 high OR chart low <= that model's T1 low)

Short: bearish FVG marking is enabled AND high < low[2] on the current forming T2 bar, calculated on T2 bars and surfaced on chart bars
