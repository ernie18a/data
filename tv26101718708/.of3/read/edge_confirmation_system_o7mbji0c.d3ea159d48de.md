Calculation: normalized momentum = map the percent difference 100 × (EMA(close, momentum fast lookback) - EMA(close, momentum slow lookback)) / EMA(close, momentum slow lookback) from its rolling lowest to highest over the oscillator range lookback linearly to [-1, 1]; use zero when the rolling highest equals the rolling lowest
Long: close > EMA(close, longest trend lookback) AND EMA(close, shortest trend lookback) > EMA(close, middle trend lookback) AND normalized momentum > 0 AND normalized momentum > EMA(normalized momentum, momentum smoothing lookback) AND normalized momentum < overbought threshold AND (volume requirement disabled OR (volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) × volume surge multiplier AND close > open)) AND the most recent signal is absent or short
