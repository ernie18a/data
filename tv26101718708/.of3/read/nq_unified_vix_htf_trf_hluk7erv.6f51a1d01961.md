Calculation: Entry price = close of the entry bar
Calculation: Initial risk = ATR(lookback) × stop-distance multiplier on the entry bar
Calculation: Upper extension = maximum of (high of the last completed T2 bar − low of the last completed T2 bar) × extension multiplier + low of the last completed T2 bar and (opening-range high − opening-range low) × extension multiplier + opening-range low after the opening range completes; before completion, use only the T2 value. T2 values are calculated on T2 bars and carried to chart bars.
Calculation: Lower extension = minimum of (high of the last completed T2 bar − low of the last completed T2 bar) × (1 − extension multiplier) + low of the last completed T2 bar and (opening-range high − opening-range low) × (1 − extension multiplier) + opening-range low after the opening range completes; before completion, use only the T2 value. T2 values are calculated on T2 bars and carried to chart bars.
Long: crosses above Twin Range Filter(close, fast lookback, fast multiplier, slow lookback, slow multiplier) AND chart bar is confirmed AND traded-series close > Twin Range Filter(traded-series close, fast lookback, fast multiplier, slow lookback, slow multiplier) on the last completed T1 bar, calculated on T1 bars and carried to chart bars AND secondary-series close <= Twin Range Filter(secondary-series close, fast lookback, fast multiplier, slow lookback, slow multiplier) on the T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and carried to chart bars AND chart-bar time in New York is at or after session start and before session cutoff
Short: crosses below Twin Range Filter(close, fast lookback, fast multiplier, slow lookback, slow multiplier) AND chart bar is confirmed AND secondary-series close > Twin Range Filter(secondary-series close, fast lookback, fast multiplier, slow lookback, slow multiplier) on the T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and carried to chart bars AND chart-bar time in New York is at or after session start and before session cutoff
Exit: Long, (low <= entry price AND a close at or above the upper extension has occurred since entry) OR (low <= entry price − initial risk AND no close at or above the upper extension has occurred since entry) OR high >= entry price + initial risk × reward-risk ratio OR New York time >= session cutoff OR calendar day changes
Exit: Short, (high >= entry price AND a close at or below the lower extension has occurred since entry) OR (high >= entry price + initial risk AND no close at or below the lower extension has occurred since entry) OR low <= entry price − initial risk × reward-risk ratio OR secondary-series close <= Twin Range Filter(secondary-series close, fast lookback, fast multiplier, slow lookback, slow multiplier) on the T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and carried to chart bars OR New York time >= session cutoff OR calendar day changes
