Calculation: Opening-range high = the maximum high of chart bars during the selected opening interval beginning at the regular-session open; lock it when the interval ends and clear it at regular-session end.
Calculation: Opening-range low = the minimum low of chart bars during the selected opening interval beginning at the regular-session open; lock it when the interval ends and clear it at regular-session end.
Calculation: Body fraction = high > low ? abs(close - open) / (high - low) : 0.
Calculation: Pivot highs = the most recent pivot-retention-count confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) values on chart bars.
Calculation: Pivot lows = the most recent pivot-retention-count confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) values on chart bars.
Calculation: Nearest resistance = the minimum value above close among pivot highs, the previous completed T1 bar's high, and the previous completed T1 bar's close; sample those T1 values on chart bars.
Calculation: Nearest support = the maximum value below close among pivot lows, the previous completed T1 bar's low, and the previous completed T1 bar's close; sample those T1 values on chart bars.
Long: At chart-bar close, the opening range is locked, the bar is in the regular session and (session filter disabled or the bar is in the signal window), close > opening-range high, open > opening-range high, no long signal has fired since session reset or the most recent close below opening-range high, (body filter disabled or Body fraction >= minimum body fraction), (volume filter disabled or volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) on chart bars), and (support/resistance filter disabled or Nearest resistance is absent or Nearest resistance - close >= target distance).
Short: At chart-bar close, the opening range is locked, the bar is in the regular session and (session filter disabled or the bar is in the signal window), close < opening-range low, open < opening-range low, no short signal has fired since session reset or the most recent close above opening-range low, (body filter disabled or Body fraction >= minimum body fraction), (volume filter disabled or volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) on chart bars), and (support/resistance filter disabled or Nearest support is absent or close - Nearest support >= target distance).
