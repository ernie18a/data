Calculation: Session Open = open when session filtering is enabled and the chart bar enters the configured T2 session; otherwise the prior chart-bar value (initially open)
Long: (within the configured T2 session or session filtering is disabled) AND flat AND (high crosses above Session Open + percentage-range multiplier × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar, sampled on chart bars) OR Session Open differs from its prior chart-bar value AND high > Session Open + percentage-range multiplier × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar, sampled on chart bars))
Short: (within the configured T2 session or session filtering is disabled) AND flat AND (low crosses below Session Open − percentage-range multiplier × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar, sampled on chart bars) OR Session Open differs from its prior chart-bar value AND low < Session Open − percentage-range multiplier × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar, sampled on chart bars))
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit tick distance OR low <= entry price − stop-loss tick distance OR (trailing stop enabled AND high >= entry price + trailing activation tick distance AND low <= highest high since trailing activation − trailing offset tick distance) OR (end-of-session close enabled AND session filtering enabled AND outside the configured T2 session)
Exit: short, low <= entry price − take-profit tick distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss tick distance OR (trailing stop enabled AND low <= entry price − trailing activation tick distance AND high >= lowest low since trailing activation + trailing offset tick distance) OR (end-of-session close enabled AND session filtering enabled AND outside the configured T2 session)
