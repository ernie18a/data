Calculation: Adaptive ATR multiplier = base multiplier × (adaptive multiplier disabled ? neutral factor : ATR percentile rank(ATR(ATR lookback), volatility-rank lookback) below low threshold ? low-volatility factor : above high threshold ? high-volatility factor : neutral factor); use a parameterized fallback rank when the percentile is unavailable.
Calculation: Raw trail bands = EMA(close, trail EMA lookback) ± ATR(ATR lookback) × adaptive ATR multiplier.
Calculation: Trail direction = initialized neutral; after chart-bar index reaches max(ATR lookback, trail EMA lookback, R-squared lookback) plus warm-up offset, set to long when close is above the previous upper trail band, otherwise set to short when close is below the previous lower trail band, otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Trail bands = when ratcheting is enabled and direction is short, upper band is raw upper band on a direction change and otherwise the lower of raw upper band and previous upper band; when ratcheting is enabled and direction is long, lower band is raw lower band on a direction change and otherwise the higher of raw lower band and previous lower band; all other bands equal their raw bands.
Calculation: HTF bias = compare close and EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) from the last completed bar of higher timeframe T1; evaluate on chart bars using those T1 values.
Calculation: Regime score = regime ADX weight × min(ADX(ADX lookback) / ADX saturation level, unit maximum) × score scale + regime choppiness weight × clamp(score scale − Choppiness Index(high, low, choppiness lookback), zero, score scale) + regime R-squared weight × correlation(close, bar index, R-squared lookback)^2 × score scale; regime component weights sum to one.
Calculation: RSI momentum score(side) = for long, full, moderate, or marginal score at or above separately parameterized strong, moderate, or marginal RSI thresholds, otherwise zero; for short, the same score levels at or below separately parameterized strong, moderate, or marginal thresholds, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Quality weights = custom component weights when custom override is enabled; otherwise use the base weight vector selected by volume mode, add separately parameterized signed context adjustments per component, clamp each weight at zero, and normalize their sum to the quality score scale (leave all weights zero if their sum is zero). Opening context adds to HTF and subtracts from RSI and breakout; expiry context adds to HTF and regime and subtracts from RSI and breakout; high ATR percentile adds to regime and subtracts from RSI; choppy regime adds to RSI and subtracts from breakout; trending regime adds to breakout and subtracts from RSI. Opening and expiry adjustments apply only when their respective switches are enabled.
Calculation: Signal quality(side) = HTF weight × HTF score + volume weight × volume score + RSI weight × RSI momentum score(side) + regime weight × regime score / score scale + breakout weight × breakout strength(side). HTF score is full for alignment, zero for opposition, and a parameterized partial score for neutral or unavailable bias, including when the HTF filter is disabled. Volume is available only when volume mode enables it, current volume is positive, and SMA(volume, volume lookback) is positive; volume score is full when its filter is disabled or volume is unavailable, otherwise full when volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume threshold and zero otherwise. Breakout strength is min(abs(close − previous upper trail band) / ATR, breakout cap) / breakout cap for long, or min(abs(previous lower trail band − close) / ATR, breakout cap) / breakout cap for short; it is zero when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Contexts for quality weights = opening context when the chart is intraday, opening adjustment is enabled, and IST bar time is from session start through before opening-window end; expiry context when expiry adjustment is enabled, the weekday is in the expiry-day set, and IST bar time is from expiry-window start through before session end; high-volatility context when ATR percentile rank exceeds its separate weight threshold; choppy context when regime score is below the choppy threshold; trending context when regime score is at or above the trending threshold.
Calculation: Risk levels(side) = ATR on the entry chart bar × selected stop multiple for initial risk distance; TP1 and TP3 are entry price plus that distance times their respective selected multiples for long, and minus it for short. The selected stop and target multiples come from the configured risk preset or separate custom parameters. When break-even is enabled, the active stop becomes entry price after TP1 is first touched on an eligible confirmed chart bar without touching the then-active stop; this applies starting on the following bar.
Long: trail direction changes from short to long AND warm-up is complete AND chart bar is confirmed AND (session gate is disabled OR chart is a non-intraday calendar chart OR IST bar time is from session start through before session end) AND signal quality(long) >= minimum quality AND (choppy-signal skip is disabled OR regime score >= choppy threshold).
Short: trail direction changes from long to short AND warm-up is complete AND chart bar is confirmed AND (session gate is disabled OR chart is a non-intraday calendar chart OR IST bar time is from session start through before session end) AND signal quality(short) >= minimum quality AND (choppy-signal skip is disabled OR regime score >= choppy threshold).
Exit: long, chart bar is confirmed AND bars since entry >= minimum hold bars AND (low <= active stop OR high >= TP3).
Exit: short, chart bar is confirmed AND bars since entry >= minimum hold bars AND (high >= active stop OR low <= TP3).
