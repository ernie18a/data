Calculation: Choppy = 1 - Choppiness Index(chop lookback) / 100 < chop persistence threshold AND ADX(ADX length) < chop ADX threshold
Calculation: HTFUp = EMA(close, fast EMA length, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, mid EMA length, T1, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, RSI length, T1, last completed bar) > RSI midline
Calculation: HTFDown = EMA(close, fast EMA length, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, mid EMA length, T1, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, RSI length, T1, last completed bar) < RSI midline
Calculation: DemandZones = zones created on each bar where PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length) exists and volume[pivot length] > SMA(volume, volume SMA length)[pivot length] * zone volume multiple, with bottom = PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length) - ATR(ATR length)[pivot length] * zone ATR multiple * zone inner factor and top = PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length) + ATR(ATR length)[pivot length] * zone ATR multiple; only the newest max zones are kept, and a zone is removed when close < zone bottom - ATR(ATR length) * zone invalidation ATR multiple
Calculation: SupplyZones = zones created on each bar where PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length) exists and volume[pivot length] > SMA(volume, volume SMA length)[pivot length] * zone volume multiple, with top = PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length) + ATR(ATR length)[pivot length] * zone ATR multiple * zone inner factor and bottom = PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length) - ATR(ATR length)[pivot length] * zone ATR multiple; only the newest max zones are kept, and a zone is removed when close > zone top + ATR(ATR length) * zone invalidation ATR multiple
Calculation: DemandProximity = some DemandZones zone has close >= zone bottom - ATR(ATR length) * zone distance ATR multiple AND close <= zone top + ATR(ATR length) * zone distance ATR multiple
Calculation: SupplyProximity = some SupplyZones zone has close >= zone bottom - ATR(ATR length) * zone distance ATR multiple AND close <= zone top + ATR(ATR length) * zone distance ATR multiple
Calculation: PointOfControl = mid-price of the highest-volume bucket among POC bucket count equal-width buckets spanning Lowest(low, POC lookback) to Highest(high, POC lookback), where each of the last POC lookback bars' volume is split across the buckets its high-low range overlaps in proportion to the overlap
Calculation: ValueAreaHigh = upper edge of the value area of the PointOfControl volume profile, grown from the PointOfControl bucket
Calculation: ValueAreaLow = lower edge of the value area of the PointOfControl volume profile, grown from the PointOfControl bucket
Calculation: POCProximity = |close - PointOfControl| < ATR(ATR length) * POC band ATR multiple
Calculation: WithinValueArea = close >= ValueAreaLow AND close <= ValueAreaHigh
Calculation: ZoneBounceLong = DemandProximity AND close > open AND EMA(close, fast EMA length) > EMA(close, fast EMA length)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI(close, RSI length)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI length) > zone bounce RSI lower bound AND RSI(close, RSI length) < zone bounce RSI upper bound AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) * zone bounce volume multiple AND (HTFUp OR (ADX(ADX length) > zone bounce ADX threshold AND close > close[2])) AND (close > PointOfControl OR |close - PointOfControl| > ATR(ATR length) * zone bounce POC distance ATR multiple)
Calculation: EmaCrossLong = EMA(close, fast EMA length) crosses above EMA(close, mid EMA length) AND close > EMA(close, slow EMA length) AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI midline AND RSI(close, RSI length) < EMA cross long RSI upper bound AND SMA(close, SMA length) > SMA(close, SMA length)[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) * EMA cross volume multiple AND HTFUp AND ADX(ADX length) > EMA cross ADX threshold AND NOT (close < PointOfControl AND POCProximity) AND NOT (close < ValueAreaHigh AND ValueAreaHigh - close < ATR(ATR length) * VAH proximity ATR multiple)
Calculation: Ema200RetestLong = EMA(close, slow EMA length) > EMA(close, slow EMA length)[5] AND |low - EMA(close, slow EMA length)| < ATR(ATR length) * EMA200 touch ATR multiple AND close > EMA(close, slow EMA length) AND close > open AND RSI(close, RSI length) > EMA200 RSI lower bound AND HTFUp AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) AND (close > PointOfControl OR |close - PointOfControl| > ATR(ATR length) * EMA200 POC distance ATR multiple)
Calculation: SupportBounceRaw = DemandProximity AND (close > open OR (MIN(open, close) - low) > |close - open| * support wick multiple) AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI(close, RSI length)[2] AND RSI(close, RSI length) > support RSI lower bound AND RSI(close, RSI length) < support RSI upper bound AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) AND NOT SupplyProximity AND (close > close[1] OR low[1] < low[2]) AND ADX(ADX length) < support ADX ceiling
Calculation: MomentumLong = EMA(close, fast EMA length) - EMA(close, fast EMA length)[3] > 0 AND EMA(close, fast EMA length) - EMA(close, fast EMA length)[3] > EMA(close, fast EMA length)[1] - EMA(close, fast EMA length)[4] AND close > EMA(close, fast EMA length) AND close > close[2] AND close > open AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI midline AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI(close, RSI length)[2] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) * momentum long volume multiple AND NOT SupplyProximity AND ADX(ADX length) > momentum long ADX threshold AND (DemandProximity OR close > EMA(close, slow EMA length)) AND (close > PointOfControl OR |close - PointOfControl| > ATR(ATR length) * momentum long POC distance ATR multiple)
Calculation: ZoneRejectShort = SupplyProximity AND close < open AND EMA(close, fast EMA length) < EMA(close, fast EMA length)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI length) crosses below EMA(RSI(close, RSI length), RSI signal length) AND RSI(close, RSI length) < zone reject RSI upper bound AND RSI(close, RSI length) > zone reject RSI lower bound AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) * zone reject volume multiple AND HTFDown AND close < EMA(close, slow EMA length) AND (close < PointOfControl OR POCProximity OR |close - ValueAreaHigh| < ATR(ATR length) * VAH proximity ATR multiple)
Calculation: EmaCrossShort = EMA(close, fast EMA length) crosses below EMA(close, mid EMA length) AND close < EMA(close, slow EMA length) AND RSI(close, RSI length) < RSI midline AND RSI(close, RSI length) > EMA cross short RSI lower bound AND SMA(close, SMA length) < SMA(close, SMA length)[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) * EMA cross volume multiple AND HTFDown AND ADX(ADX length) > EMA cross ADX threshold AND NOT (close > PointOfControl AND POCProximity)
Calculation: MomentumShort = EMA(close, fast EMA length) - EMA(close, fast EMA length)[3] < 0 AND EMA(close, fast EMA length) - EMA(close, fast EMA length)[3] < EMA(close, mid EMA length) - EMA(close, mid EMA length)[3] AND close < EMA(close, fast EMA length) AND close < EMA(close, mid EMA length) AND close < open AND RSI(close, RSI length) < RSI midline AND RSI(close, RSI length) < RSI(close, RSI length)[2] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume SMA length) * momentum short volume multiple AND NOT DemandProximity AND ADX(ADX length) > momentum short ADX threshold AND (SupplyProximity OR close < EMA(close, slow EMA length)) AND (close < PointOfControl OR POCProximity)
Calculation: LongStop = if DemandProximity then bottom of the newest DemandZones zone meeting the DemandProximity test - ATR(ATR length) * stop buffer ATR multiple, otherwise Lowest(low, 2 * pivot length) - ATR(ATR length) * stop buffer ATR multiple
Calculation: ShortStop = if SupplyProximity then top of the newest SupplyZones zone meeting the SupplyProximity test + ATR(ATR length) * stop buffer ATR multiple, otherwise Highest(high, 2 * pivot length) + ATR(ATR length) * stop buffer ATR multiple
Calculation: LongRisk = close - LongStop
Calculation: ShortRisk = ShortStop - close
Calculation: LongTarget = close + risk-reward ratio * LongRisk
Calculation: ShortTarget = close - risk-reward ratio * ShortRisk
Calculation: LongTargetBlock = (close < PointOfControl AND PointOfControl < LongTarget AND PointOfControl - close < POC block risk fraction * LongRisk) OR (close < ValueAreaHigh AND ValueAreaHigh < LongTarget AND ValueAreaHigh - close < POC block risk fraction * LongRisk)
Calculation: ShortTargetBlock = (close > PointOfControl AND PointOfControl > ShortTarget AND close - PointOfControl < POC block risk fraction * ShortRisk) OR (close > ValueAreaLow AND ValueAreaLow > ShortTarget AND close - ValueAreaLow < POC block risk fraction * ShortRisk)
Calculation: LongRaw = (ZoneBounceLong OR EmaCrossLong OR Ema200RetestLong OR (SupportBounceRaw AND NOT (some SupplyZones zone has bottom > close AND bottom - close < ATR(ATR length) * supply-above ATR multiple) AND NOT (close < PointOfControl AND PointOfControl - close < ATR(ATR length) * POC resistance ATR multiple)) OR MomentumLong) AND NOT Choppy AND LongRisk > ATR(ATR length) * min risk ATR multiple AND LongRisk < ATR(ATR length) * max risk ATR multiple AND bars since last Long > cooldown bars AND NOT LongTargetBlock
Calculation: ShortRaw = (ZoneRejectShort OR EmaCrossShort OR MomentumShort) AND NOT Choppy AND ShortRisk > ATR(ATR length) * min risk ATR multiple AND ShortRisk < ATR(ATR length) * max risk ATR multiple AND bars since last Short > cooldown bars AND NOT ShortTargetBlock

Long: Ema200RetestLong AND NOT Choppy
Long: SupportBounceRaw AND NOT Choppy
Long: MomentumLong AND NOT Choppy
Long: LongRaw AND NOT LongRaw[1]
Long: WithinValueArea[1] AND close > ValueAreaHigh
Short: ZoneRejectShort AND NOT Choppy
Short: EmaCrossShort AND NOT Choppy

Short: ShortRaw AND NOT ShortRaw[1]
Short: WithinValueArea[1] AND close < ValueAreaLow
