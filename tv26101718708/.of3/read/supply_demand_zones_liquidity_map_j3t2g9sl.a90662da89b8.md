Calculation: demand zone = the range from the lowest low to the highest high of the consecutive chart bars immediately before a bullish impulse bar; each base bar has high − low < base-range parameter × ATR(shared lookback), and the base count is between the minimum and maximum base-count parameters; the impulse bar has close > open, high − low > impulse-range parameter × the same ATR, and abs(close − open)/(high − low) > body-dominance parameter; retain at most the newest max-zones parameter per side, expire zones after the zone-age parameter, mark a zone broken when close < its bottom, and cease to consider it fresh after a touch (low <= its top) more than one chart bar after creation.
Calculation: supply zone = the range from the lowest low to the highest high of the consecutive chart bars immediately before a bearish impulse bar; each base bar has high − low < base-range parameter × ATR(shared lookback), and the base count is between the minimum and maximum base-count parameters; the impulse bar has close < open, high − low > impulse-range parameter × the same ATR, and abs(close − open)/(high − low) > body-dominance parameter; retain at most the newest max-zones parameter per side, expire zones after the zone-age parameter, mark a zone broken when close > its top, and cease to consider it fresh after a touch (high >= its bottom) more than one chart bar after creation.
Calculation: equal-lows pool = a level shared by at least two chart-bar lows within an equal-level-tolerance parameter.
Calculation: equal-highs pool = a level shared by at least two chart-bar highs within an equal-level-tolerance parameter.
Long: a retained, unbroken demand zone is more than one chart bar old AND low <= its top AND close >= its bottom.
Long: a fresh demand zone exists AND low <= its top AND close >= its bottom AND ((prior-day-extreme filter is enabled AND low < the low of the last completed T1 bar, using T1 values on chart bars, AND close > that low) OR (low < an equal-lows-pool level AND close > that level)).
