Long: close > SMA(close, trend lookback) AND low <= Trend-dependent range level * long-entry level multiplier AND close > open AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier AND (H1 trend filter disabled OR T1 close (bar still forming on T1 bars, carried to chart bars) > SMA(close, trend lookback, calculated on T1 bars, carried to chart bars))
