Long: current chart bar is confirmed AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above the long trigger level AND long quality meets the configured minimum quality AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long exhaustion threshold AND abs(SMA(close, short lookback) - EMA(close, longer EMA lookback)) >= chop ATR multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback) AND (close <= SMA(close, short lookback) OR close - SMA(close, short lookback) < extension ATR multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback)) AND (session filter is disabled OR current chart bar is within the configured session and timezone) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND long side is armed (initially armed and rearmed when RSI crosses below the midline) AND no prior long signal or at least the cooldown number of chart bars have elapsed since the prior long signal
Short: current chart bar is confirmed AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below the short trigger level AND short quality meets the configured minimum quality AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short exhaustion threshold AND abs(SMA(close, short lookback) - EMA(close, longer EMA lookback)) >= chop ATR multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback) AND (close >= SMA(close, short lookback) OR SMA(close, short lookback) - close < extension ATR multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback)) AND (session filter is disabled OR current chart bar is within the configured session and timezone) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND short side is armed (initially armed and rearmed when RSI crosses above the midline) AND no prior short signal or at least the cooldown number of chart bars have elapsed since the prior short signal
