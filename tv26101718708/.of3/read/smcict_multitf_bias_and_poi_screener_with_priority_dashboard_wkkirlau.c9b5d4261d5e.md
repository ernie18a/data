Calculation: Structure(T) = On T bars, track the latest and prior confirmed pivot highs and lows using the shared structure pivot lookback on both sides; bullish when latest high and low exist and (close > latest high OR both latest and prior highs and lows exist, latest high > prior high, latest low > prior low, and close >= latest low); bearish when latest high and low exist, close <= latest high, and (close < latest low OR both latest and prior highs and lows exist, latest high < prior high, and latest low < prior low); otherwise neutral. T1, T2, and T3 are the anchor, primary, and local timeframes. Calculate on T bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off: historical bars use the latest completed T bar, realtime bars use the forming T bar.
Calculation: Timeframe bias(T) = EMA direction or Structure(T), according to the selected bias-combination mode: EMA only; structure only; same non-neutral direction from both or neutral; or EMA direction unless non-neutral structure opposes it, in which case neutral. EMA direction is bullish when EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), and bearish when fast < slow; if the shared slow-EMA slope filter is enabled, also require slow EMA minus slow EMA at the slope lookback ago to be > ATR(bias ATR lookback) * minimum slope/ATR ratio for bullish, or < the negative of that for bearish. T1 uses the anchor EMA lookbacks; T2 and T3 share the primary/local EMA lookbacks. The slope-filter setting, slope lookback, minimum slope/ATR ratio, and structure pivot lookback are shared across timeframes. Calculate on T bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off: historical bars use the latest completed T bar, realtime bars use the forming T bar.
Calculation: Selected bias = Timeframe bias(T1) when anchor-only mode is selected; Timeframe bias(T2) when primary-only or primary-with-anchor-filter mode is selected; bullish only when T1 and T2 are both bullish, bearish only when both are bearish, otherwise neutral, when aligned-anchor-and-primary mode is selected.
Calculation: FVG(T) = the most recent qualifying bullish and bearish three-candle gap on T bars. Bullish when current low > the first candle high, with zone from that high to current low; bearish when current high < the first candle low, with zone from current high to that low. If middle-candle close confirmation is enabled, require its close > first candle high for bullish or < first candle low for bearish. If the automatic body threshold is enabled, require the middle candle's signed body percentage to exceed the average absolute body percentage over the body-average lookback times the body-threshold multiplier for bullish, or its negative to exceed that threshold for bearish. Require gap size >= ATR(FVG ATR lookback) * shared minimum gap/ATR ratio. A zone remains valid until its age from the first candle exceeds its timeframe-specific maximum age, or a later T bar closes beyond its far boundary or wicks beyond it according to the shared invalidation setting. The middle-close setting, automatic-threshold setting, body-average lookback, body-threshold multiplier, FVG ATR lookback, minimum gap/ATR ratio, and invalidation setting are shared across T1, T2, and T3; maximum age is separate for each. Calculate on T bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off: historical bars use the latest completed T bar, realtime bars use the forming T bar.
Calculation: Order Block(T4) = the latest bullish and bearish block formed on T4 bars when close breaks above the prior OB range high or below the prior OB range low, respectively. The range uses highest(high, OB range lookback) and lowest(low, OB range lookback), each taken at the prior bar; its lookback spans the OB pivot lookback on both sides plus its center candle. A bullish block uses the parsed range of the latest bearish candle; a bearish block uses the parsed range of the latest bullish candle. Parse a candle's high and low in reverse when its range >= the high-volatility multiplier * ATR(high-volatility lookback). Require block size > ATR(OB size lookback) * minimum OB size/ATR ratio. A bullish block remains valid until a later close falls below its lower boundary; a bearish block remains valid until a later close rises above its upper boundary; either expires when age from its source candle exceeds the OB maximum age. The OB pivot lookback, high-volatility multiplier and lookback, OB size lookback, minimum OB size/ATR ratio, and maximum age are separate from the structure and FVG parameters. T4 is the context OB timeframe, selected as T2 or the custom OB timeframe by the timeframe-sync setting. Calculate on T4 bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off: historical bars use the latest completed T4 bar, realtime bars use the forming T4 bar.
Long: Selected bias is bullish OR Structure(T1) is bullish OR Structure(T2) is bullish OR Structure(T3) is bullish OR FVG tracking is enabled AND (anchor context permits FVG(T1) OR FVG(T2) OR FVG(T3)) contains a valid bullish gap OR OB tracking is enabled AND Order Block(T4) contains a valid bullish block.
Short: Selected bias is bearish OR Structure(T1) is bearish OR Structure(T2) is bearish OR Structure(T3) is bearish OR FVG tracking is enabled AND (anchor context permits FVG(T1) OR FVG(T2) OR FVG(T3)) contains a valid bearish gap OR OB tracking is enabled AND Order Block(T4) contains a valid bearish block.
