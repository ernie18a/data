Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close[efficiency lookback]) / sum(abs(close − close[1]), efficiency lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Regime on each requested timeframe = trending when ADX(adx lookback) > trend ADX threshold AND efficiency ratio > trend efficiency threshold; otherwise volatile when ATR > SMA(ATR, ATR-average lookback) × volatility threshold; otherwise ranging. A trending regime is up when close ≥ EMA(close, longer trend lookback), else down.
Calculation: Trend-quality score = clamp((efficiency ratio − trend efficiency threshold) / (1 − trend efficiency threshold), unit range) × (1 when close ≥ EMA(close, longer trend lookback), else −1).
Calculation: Trend score = clamp(weighted sum of sign(EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) − EMA(close, longer trend lookback)), clamp((close − EMA(close, longer trend lookback)) / (ATR × distance scale), unit range), and clamp((LinReg(close, regression lookback, current offset) − LinReg(close, regression lookback, prior offset)) / ATR × slope sensitivity, unit range) × Correlation(close, bar index, regression lookback)^2, to the unit range).
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(weighted sum of centered RSI(close, RSI lookback) and clamp((RSI − RSI[RSI slope lag]) / RSI slope scale, unit range), to the unit range).
Calculation: Structure score = clamp(weighted sum of (close − lowest(low, structure lookback)) / (highest(high, structure lookback) − lowest(low, structure lookback)) mapped to the unit range, and a swing-break value that is positive above the latest confirmed pivot high, negative below the latest confirmed pivot low, and zero otherwise, to the unit range).
Calculation: Flow score = clamp(weighted sum of centered MFI(hlc3, flow lookback) and EMA(((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), flow lookback), to the unit range); use zero for the bar delta when high = low.
Calculation: Mean-reversion score = clamp(negative z-score of close against SMA(close, mean-reversion lookback) and stdev(close, mean-reversion lookback), divided by the mean-reversion scale, to the unit range); use zero when standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Volatility score = percentile rank of ATR over the volatility-rank lookback, normalized to the unit interval.
Calculation: Factor accuracy multiplier, when enabled, is one until the minimum sample count is met; thereafter clamp one plus weighting sensitivity times the difference between the factor's follow-through hit rate and the baseline hit rate weighted by that factor's logged up/down counts, between its minimum and maximum weights. Log a factor on confirmed chart bars when its absolute score meets the logging threshold; after the follow-through horizon, a hit requires close to have moved from the logged close in the logged direction by the configured ATR multiple, using ATR at resolution.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = clamp(the regime-selected weighted average of trend, trend-quality, momentum, structure, flow, and mean-reversion scores, each multiplied by its factor accuracy multiplier, multiplied by one minus volatility damping times the positive part of volatility score, to the unit range). Use separate factor weights for trending, ranging, and volatile regimes. Calculate on chart bars using the current T1–T7 bar in real time and the most recently completed T1–T7 bar historically; T1–T7 are ordered from shorter to longer timeframes.
Calculation: Value position, when enabled = valid external value/auction position clamped to the unit range, or otherwise (close − session VWAP(hlc3)) / (stdev(close, value lookback) × value scale), clamped to the unit range; use zero if standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Options/positioning score, when enabled = valid external options/positioning bias normalized by its scale and clamped to the unit range, or otherwise the clamped weighted sum of normalized implied-volatility rank, normalized z-score of implied-volatility index close minus EMA(implied-volatility index close, term lookback) over the normalization lookback on T8, and normalized z-score of (futures close − spot close) / spot close over the normalization lookback. Use implied-volatility-index bars from the current T8 bar in real time and the most recently completed T8 bar historically; use chart bars for futures and spot basis.
Calculation: Intermarket score, when enabled = valid external intermarket signal normalized by its scale and clamped to the unit range, or otherwise the leader's return over the leader lookback, scaled and clamped to the unit range and multiplied by −1 when the leader is inverse, then gated to zero unless abs(Correlation(close, leader close, correlation lookback)) meets the minimum correlation.
Calculation: Volatility-risk-premium direction = clamp((implied-volatility-index close − stdev(log(close / close[1]), realized-volatility lookback) × annualization scale) / realized volatility, to the unit range).
Calculation: Context direction = normalized weighted average of available value position, options/positioning score, intermarket score, and volatility-risk-premium direction; use zero when none are available.
Calculation: Confidence = clamp(one minus the configured penalties for high VPIN, high order-flow self-excitation, high implied-volatility percentile rank, active change-point, and high VPIN together with low price-impact percentile rank, to the configured confidence bounds). VPIN uses signed sub-timeframe volume, falling back to signed chart-bar volume; price impact is the percentile rank of abs(Correlation(close change, signed volume, impact lookback)) × stdev(close change, impact lookback) / stdev(signed volume, impact lookback).
Calculation: Change-point state = two-sided CUSUM of the z-score of log(close / close[1]) against its baseline lookback, with configured slack and detection threshold; reset both sums and record the chart bar when either threshold fires. It is active while chart bars since the last fire are fewer than the hold parameter. Its direction is up when the high sum ≥ abs(low sum), otherwise down.
Calculation: Scalp composite = weighted sum of T1–T4 timeframe biases; intraday composite = weighted sum of T2–T6 timeframe biases; positional composite = weighted sum of T4–T7 timeframe biases. When context data are available, blend scalp and intraday composites with context direction using the context-weight parameter, and blend the positional composite using its configured positional context weight; otherwise use each raw composite.
