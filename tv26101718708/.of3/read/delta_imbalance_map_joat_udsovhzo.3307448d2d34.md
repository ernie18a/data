Calculation: Chart ATR = ATR(lookback) on chart bars, using high − low when unavailable.
Calculation: Volume rank = PercentRank(volume, lookback) on chart bars, treating missing volume as zero and using 50 when unavailable.
Calculation: Buyer and seller shares = on each chart bar, sum T1 intrabar volume with close > open for buyers and close < open for sellers, split flat intrabar volume equally, and divide each sum by total volume as a percentage; if total volume is zero, both shares are 50%; if no T1 intrabars are available, assign chart-bar volume wholly to buyers when close >= open and wholly to sellers otherwise; use the prior completed chart bar's shares.
Calculation: Gap size = low − high[2] for a bullish gap; low[2] − high for a bearish gap.
Calculation: Directional tilt = buyer share − seller share for a bullish gap; seller share − buyer share for a bearish gap.
Calculation: Zone score = min(score cap, gap size / max(Chart ATR × minimum gap ATR, minimum price increment) × gap-size weight + max(directional tilt, 0) × tilt weight + abs(close[1] − open[1]) / max(Chart ATR, minimum price increment) × candle-body weight + Volume rank × volume-rank weight).
Calculation: Zone acceptance(candidate bottom, candidate top, candidate score) = scan existing zones newest to oldest; overlapping means max(candidate bottom, prior bottom) < min(candidate top, prior top); for each overlapping zone, remove it and continue if it is inactive or candidate score > prior score, otherwise reject; accept if the scan ends. Use zone status at the start of the current bar; after the candidate scan on each chart bar, mark a zone inactive if bars since creation > age limit or, on a bar after creation, the selected mitigation test holds (bullish zone: low < top in Touch mode or low <= bottom in Full Fill mode; bearish zone: high > bottom in Touch mode or high >= top in Full Fill mode).
Long: confirmed chart bar AND low > high[2] AND Gap size >= Chart ATR × minimum gap ATR AND abs(Directional tilt) >= minimum tilt AND Volume rank >= minimum volume rank AND Zone acceptance(high[2], low, Zone score).
Short: confirmed chart bar AND high < low[2] AND Gap size >= Chart ATR × minimum gap ATR AND abs(Directional tilt) >= minimum tilt AND Volume rank >= minimum volume rank AND Zone acceptance(high, low[2], Zone score).
