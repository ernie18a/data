Calculation: Quote age(series) = reference datetime (current datetime on realtime chart bars; chart-bar datetime historically) minus the series’ T1 bar datetime, in minutes.
Calculation: Smoothed yield change(series) = (EMA(close, smoothing lookback, T1) - EMA(close, smoothing lookback, T1)[comparison lookback]) * 100; calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars, using the latest T1 value available (still forming in realtime, completed historically); the offset counts T1 bars.
Calculation: Smoothed percent change(series) = (EMA(close, smoothing lookback, T1) / EMA(close, smoothing lookback, T1)[comparison lookback] - 1) * 100; calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars, using the latest T1 value available (still forming in realtime, completed historically); the offset counts T1 bars.
Calculation: Yield direction(series) = +1 if the series close, smoothed yield change, and T1 datetime exist, quote age is between zero and maximum quote age, and smoothed yield change <= -yield minimum move; -1 if they exist, quote age is in that range, and smoothed yield change >= yield minimum move; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Market direction(series, threshold) = +1 if the series close, smoothed percent change, and T1 datetime exist, quote age is between zero and maximum quote age, and smoothed percent change <= -threshold; -1 if they exist, quote age is in that range, and smoothed percent change >= threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Alignment score = 2 * Yield direction(10-year yield) + (if enabled, Market direction(volatility index, volatility-index threshold), otherwise 0) + (if enabled, Market direction(dollar index, dollar-index threshold), otherwise 0) + (if crude oil is enabled, Market direction(crude oil, crude-oil threshold) for an inverse relationship or its negative for a direct relationship, otherwise 0).
Long: Yield direction(10-year yield) = +1 AND alignment score >= minimum alignment score AND (2-year confirmation is disabled OR Yield direction(2-year yield) = +1) AND (chart EMA filter is disabled OR chart close > EMA(chart close, chart EMA lookback)).
Short: Yield direction(10-year yield) = -1 AND alignment score <= -minimum alignment score AND (2-year confirmation is disabled OR Yield direction(2-year yield) = -1) AND (chart EMA filter is disabled OR chart close < EMA(chart close, chart EMA lookback)).
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, the long condition holds AND the previous chart bar did not satisfy the long condition (a missing prior value counts as false).
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, the short condition holds AND the previous chart bar did not satisfy the short condition (a missing prior value counts as false).
