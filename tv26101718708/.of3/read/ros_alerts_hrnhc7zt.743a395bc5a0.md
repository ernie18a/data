Calculation: Bullish OB zone = on each confirmed pivot high using pivot lookback L on each side, replace the zone with the body of the nearest preceding bearish candle within OB search-span multiplier × L chart bars whose body size ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum OB-size multiplier; retain the prior zone if none qualifies, then clear it if close < zone bottom − ATR(ATR lookback) × invalidation-buffer multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish OB zone = on each confirmed pivot low using pivot lookback L on each side, replace the zone with the body of the nearest preceding bullish candle within OB search-span multiplier × L chart bars whose body size ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum OB-size multiplier; retain the prior zone if none qualifies, then clear it if close > zone top + ATR(ATR lookback) × invalidation-buffer multiplier.
Long: low ≤ bullish OB zone top AND high ≥ bullish OB zone bottom AND close > open AND close > VWAP(HLC3, T1 anchor, calculated on chart bars through the current bar) AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) ≥ ADX threshold AND (within session S1 in Europe/London OR within session S2 in America/New_York) AND no earlier long signal since the most recent bar outside the bullish OB zone.
Short: high ≥ bearish OB zone bottom AND low ≤ bearish OB zone top AND close < open AND close < VWAP(HLC3, T1 anchor, calculated on chart bars through the current bar) AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) ≥ ADX threshold AND (within session S1 in Europe/London OR within session S2 in America/New_York) AND no earlier short signal since the most recent bar outside the bearish OB zone.
