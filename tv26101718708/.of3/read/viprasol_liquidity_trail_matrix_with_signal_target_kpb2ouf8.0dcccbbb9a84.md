Calculation: Trailing trend and bands = initialize bullish; for each Band k from 1 through 4, use multiplier base multiplier × (1 + (k − 1) × band spacing) and ATR(trail ATR lookback) on chart bars; bullish candidate = source − ATR × multiplier and bearish candidate = source + ATR × multiplier; while bullish, each band is max(current bullish candidate, prior band), and while bearish, each band is min(current bearish candidate, prior band); bullish flips bearish when source < the prior selected flip band, and bearish flips bullish when source > the prior selected flip band, resetting all bands to current candidates for the new trend. Warm-up completes when chart bars elapsed >= max(warm-up multiplier × trail ATR lookback, minimum warm-up bars).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare close of T1's last completed bar with EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) on T1 bars at T1's last completed bar; carry that value to chart bars; bullish if close > EMA, bearish otherwise, neutral while unavailable.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI regime = bullish when RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) is inclusively within bull RSI bounds and regime source > SMA(regime source, regime MA lookback) for regime confirmation bars consecutively; bearish when RSI is inclusively within bear RSI bounds and regime source < that SMA for the same consecutive bars; neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Retest state = in a bullish trend, a bar touches the bands when low <= Band 1, with depth set to the deepest band whose level is >= low; in a bearish trend, a bar touches when high >= Band 1, with depth set to the deepest band whose level is <= high. Retain the greatest touched depth and a pending window for the retest-window length, refreshing the window on each touch; clear pending state on a trend flip, expiry, or retest signal.
Calculation: Long retest score = sum of depth points, long candle points, trend-age points, volume points, bullish-bias points, and bull-regime points when RSI regime scoring is enabled; when enabled, round the sum × score scale / score ceiling, otherwise use the sum. Depth points map depths 1, 2, 3, and 4 to separate depth-point parameters. Long candle ratio = (close − low) / (high − low), or the midpoint when high = low; ratio > upper candle threshold gives upper candle points, else ratio > middle candle threshold gives middle points, else lower points. Trend-age points use bars since the current trend began: age inclusively between age bounds gives in-range points, below the lower bound gives young-trend points, otherwise old-trend points. If cumulative volume is positive, volume points are surge points when volume > prior SMA(volume, volume lookback) × surge ratio, above-average points when volume > that SMA, otherwise low-volume points; use current SMA if prior SMA is unavailable. If cumulative volume is zero, use no-volume points. Bullish HTF bias gives bullish-bias points, bearish bias gives zero, and unavailable bias gives neutral-bias points. When regime scoring is enabled, confirmed bull regime gives aligned-regime points, neutral gives neutral-regime points, and confirmed bear gives zero.
Calculation: Short retest score = sum of depth points, short candle points, trend-age points, volume points, bearish-bias points, and bear-regime points when RSI regime scoring is enabled; when enabled, round the sum × score scale / score ceiling, otherwise use the sum. Use the same depth, age, volume, and point parameters as the long score. Short candle ratio = (high − close) / (high − low), or the midpoint when high = low, using the same candle thresholds and points. Bearish HTF bias gives bullish-bias points, bullish bias gives zero, and unavailable bias gives neutral-bias points. When regime scoring is enabled, confirmed bear regime gives aligned-regime points, neutral gives neutral-regime points, and confirmed bull gives zero.
Calculation: Entry price = signal-bar close. Initial stop for a long = entry − ATR(risk ATR lookback) × stop multiplier in ATR mode, otherwise min(signal-bar low − ATR × wick buffer, entry − ATR × minimum stop distance); initial stop for a short = entry + ATR × stop multiplier in ATR mode, otherwise max(signal-bar high + ATR × wick buffer, entry + ATR × minimum stop distance). Risk = entry − initial stop for a long, or initial stop − entry for a short. Targets 1, 2, and 3 = entry plus risk × their separate target multipliers for a long, or entry minus risk × those multipliers for a short. If break-even is enabled, after target 1 is first touched on a confirmed post-entry bar without the stop also touched, set the active stop to entry for subsequent bars; otherwise it remains the initial stop.

Short: current chart bar changes trend from bullish to bearish AND bar is confirmed AND warm-up is complete.

Short: bearish trend AND within the pending retest window after a band touch AND close < Band 1 AND close < open AND bar is confirmed AND warm-up is complete AND bars since either direction's prior retest signal >= retest cooldown AND short retest score >= minimum retest score AND (RSI regime filter disabled OR confirmed bear regime AND (ADX chop filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, ADX smoothing equal to ADX lookback) >= ADX minimum)).
Exit: long, confirmed chart bar after the entry bar AND (low <= active stop at bar start OR high >= target 3 OR timeout enabled AND bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade).
Exit: short, confirmed chart bar after the entry bar AND (high >= active stop at bar start OR low <= target 3 OR timeout enabled AND bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade).
