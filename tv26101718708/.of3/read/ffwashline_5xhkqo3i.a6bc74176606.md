Calculation: Upper-tail ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low; lower-tail ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Wash-line score = score scale × (tail weight × clamp((stored tail ratio - minimum spike-tail ratio) / max(full-tail ratio - minimum spike-tail ratio, tail denominator floor), 0, 1) + obviousness weight × stored pivot obviousness + cluster weight × cluster score + departure weight × clamp(maximum excursion / max(full-departure ATR × initial ATR, minimum price increment), 0, 1) + retest weight × retest score + room weight × clamp(farthest positive distance to a retained opposing pivot / risk / minimum room multiple, 0, 1)) / max(sum of score weights, weight-sum floor) × (1 - (1 - end-of-life freshness floor) × clamp(line age / line lifetime, 0, 1)) × breach multiplier^breach count. Risk = max(abs(level - initial stop), minimum price increment), where initial stop is spike + initial ATR × stop buffer for bearish lines and spike - initial ATR × stop buffer for bullish lines. Pivot obviousness = touch-count weight × clamp(pivot touches / touch-count saturation, 0, 1) + age weight × (1 - clamp(pivot age / maximum swing age, 0, 1)) + newest-pivot weight × (1 if newest in its pool, else 0). Cluster score is the full-cluster score when cluster count >= full-cluster count, the middle-cluster score when count = middle-cluster count, otherwise the base-cluster score. Retest score is the full-retest score when touches >= full-retest count, the single-retest score when touches = single-retest count, otherwise the no-retest score. All weights, score levels, counts, floors, freshness, breach multiplier, and room multiple are separate parameters.
Calculation: Confirmed-bar wash-line state = On confirmed chart bars only, set pivot lookback L to the base pivot length, or when timeframe adaptation is enabled to max(pivot floor, round(base pivot length × (seconds(T1) / seconds(chart timeframe))^scaling exponent)); calculate ATR(ATR lookback), PivotHigh(high, L, L), and PivotLow(low, L, L), adding newly confirmed pivots before scanning. Keep at most the swing-pool cap per side. For each pivot, remove it if its age exceeds maximum swing age, or if close is above a high pivot or below a low pivot. Otherwise set excursion to high - level for high pivots or level - low for low pivots: if excursion > ATR × minimum pierce ATR and the matching tail ratio >= minimum spike-tail ratio, consume the pivot as a poke and count it; otherwise, if excursion >= -ATR × pivot-touch tolerance, increment its pivot touches. For each side with at least one qualifying poke, emit a poke event and create one line: bearish for high pivots, bullish for low pivots; use the greatest qualifying high level or lowest qualifying low level, current high or low as spike, current ATR as initial ATR, and store the selected pivot's obviousness, the qualifying count as cluster count, and the matching tail ratio. Drop the oldest line if the active-line cap is exceeded. A line cannot update or trigger on its creation bar. On later confirmed chart bars, for each live line calculate its stop from spike and current ATR × stop buffer; increment breaches when close is beyond the line level. Retire it if the selected stop mode is breached (high > stop for bearish or low < stop for bullish in wick mode; close > stop for bearish or close < stop for bullish otherwise), else if breaches >= maximum breach count, else if age > line lifetime. For each remaining line, count a touch only if it was already departed before this bar, high >= level - ATR × touch tolerance for bearish or low <= level + ATR × touch tolerance for bullish, and close is not beyond the level; store the touch bar. Update maximum excursion to max(previous maximum, level - low for bearish or high - level for bullish), and mark departed once it reaches minimum-departure ATR × initial ATR. Score each remaining line with Wash-line score using its current state and retained opposing pivots. A selected pivot's obviousness uses its pivot touches, age at the poke, and whether it was newest in its pool immediately before scanning.
Calculation: Selected active wash line = Among live lines, select the line maximizing score × clamp(1 - abs(close - level) / (max(ATR, minimum price increment) × proximity window), 0, 1); break ties by smaller abs(close - level). Its reported signed score is its direction (+ bullish, - bearish) times that effective score.
Long: A bullish poke event OR the selected active wash line is bullish OR a bullish trigger event. A bullish trigger requires a live bullish line after its creation bar and at least one enabled condition: inside candle (high <= high[pattern lookback] AND low >= low[pattern lookback]) with a touch now; lower-tail ratio >= rejection-tail ratio with a touch now; or outside candle (high > high[pattern lookback] AND low < low[pattern lookback]) and close > open, with a touch now or within the recent-touch interval. It also requires no prior trigger on that line or elapsed bars since its prior trigger >= trigger cooldown, retouches >= minimum trigger touches, and score >= minimum trigger score.
Short: A bearish poke event OR the selected active wash line is bearish OR a bearish trigger event. A bearish trigger requires a live bearish line after its creation bar and at least one enabled condition: inside candle (high <= high[pattern lookback] AND low >= low[pattern lookback]) with a touch now; upper-tail ratio >= rejection-tail ratio with a touch now; or outside candle (high > high[pattern lookback] AND low < low[pattern lookback]) and close < open, with a touch now or within the recent-touch interval. It also requires no prior trigger on that line or elapsed bars since its prior trigger >= trigger cooldown, retouches >= minimum trigger touches, and score >= minimum trigger score.
