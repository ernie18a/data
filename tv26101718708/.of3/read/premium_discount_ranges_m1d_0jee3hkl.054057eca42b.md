Calculation: SwingHigh(T1, N, turn) = high of the newest closed T1 candle c that has N closed T1 candles on each side of it, where high(c) is above the high of each of those 2N candles, and if turn is on, either c is bearish and the older neighbour is bullish, or c is bullish and the newer neighbour is bearish
Calculation: SwingLow(T1, N, turn) = low of the newest closed T1 candle c that has N closed T1 candles on each side of it, where low(c) is below the low of each of those 2N candles, and if turn is on, either c is bullish and the older neighbour is bearish, or c is bearish and the newer neighbour is bullish
Calculation: Range(T1, N, turn) = held high, held low and direction, computed on closed T1 candles; chart bars take its value as of the last closed T1 candle. It is first set, once SwingHigh and SwingLow both exist with SwingHigh above SwingLow, to high = SwingHigh, low = SwingLow, and direction bullish if SwingLow is newer than SwingHigh, otherwise bearish. Afterwards, for each closed T1 candle, in order: if the high is unsettled and the candle's high is above it, the high moves to the candle's high; else if the low is unsettled and the candle's low is below it, the low moves to the candle's low; else if close is above high, rebuild up: low = lowest low since the high was last set, including this candle, high = the candle's high, direction bullish; else if close is below low, rebuild down: high = highest high since the low was last set, including this candle, low = the candle's low, direction bearish. The edge a rebuild sets is unsettled until N consecutive closed candles have failed to move it. Otherwise nothing changes.
Calculation: Equilibrium(T1, N, turn, live) = (high + low) / 2 of Range(T1, N, turn), read on chart bars; if live is on and an edge of Range is unsettled, that edge is replaced by the greater (for the high) or lesser (for the low) of itself and the highest chart-bar high or lowest chart-bar low since it was last set
Long: T1 is longer than the chart timeframe AND direction of Range(T1, N, turn) is bullish AND close is below Equilibrium(T1, N, turn, live)
Short: T1 is longer than the chart timeframe AND direction of Range(T1, N, turn) is bearish AND close is above Equilibrium(T1, N, turn, live)
