Long: For any of four separately parameterized pivot lengths, a later bar within the confirmation-window parameter after the latest confirmed pivot low (equal left and right lookbacks) has low < that pivot low, no new unbreached pivot high or low is breached through the confirming bar, close > that pivot low, and close > open; OR close > the breach-bar high of a still-unbroken sell-sweep zone created from a confirmed sell sweep while zone creation is enabled, with no close from breach through confirmation above that high and no existing sell zone within the duplicate-distance parameter measured in minimum ticks, on a bar after zone creation.
Short: For any of four separately parameterized pivot lengths, a later bar within the confirmation-window parameter after the latest confirmed pivot high (equal left and right lookbacks) has high > that pivot high, no new unbreached pivot high or low is breached through the confirming bar, close < that pivot high, and close < open; OR close < the breach-bar low of a still-unbroken buy-sweep zone created from a confirmed buy sweep while zone creation is enabled, with no close from breach through confirmation below that low and no existing buy zone within the duplicate-distance parameter measured in minimum ticks, on a bar after zone creation.
Calculation: risk distance = ATR(ATR lookback) × stop ATR multiplier when ATR stops are enabled, otherwise entry price × stop percentage / 100
Calculation: selected target distance = risk distance × the separate TP risk-reward parameter for TP3 if enabled, otherwise TP2 if enabled, otherwise TP1 if enabled
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= entry price - risk distance OR a take-profit level is enabled and high >= entry price + selected target distance
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + risk distance OR a take-profit level is enabled and low <= entry price - selected target distance
