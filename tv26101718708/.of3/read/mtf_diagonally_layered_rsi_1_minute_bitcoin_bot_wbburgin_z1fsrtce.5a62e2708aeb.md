Calculation: Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T) = RSI(close,L) on T bars, using the last completed T bar in security mode, or 100 - 100 / (1 + RMA(max(close - close[N],0),L*N) / RMA(max(close[N] - close,0),L*N)) on chart bars in manual mode, where N is the number of chart bars per T; use separate higher timeframes T1 and T2, with T1 shorter than T2
Long: ((RSI(close,L) crosses above oversold AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T1) < oversold AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T2) < oversold) OR (Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T1) crosses above oversold AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T2) > oversold AND RSI(close,L) > oversold) OR (Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T2) crosses above oversold AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T1) > oversold AND RSI(close,L) > oversold)) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback))
Short: short trading enabled AND ((RSI(close,L) crosses below overbought AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T1) > overbought AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T2) > overbought) OR (Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T1) crosses below overbought AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T2) < overbought AND RSI(close,L) < overbought) OR (Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T2) crosses below overbought AND Higher-timeframe RSI(L,T1) < overbought AND RSI(close,L) < overbought)) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback))
Exit: long, RSI(close,L) crosses below overbought OR stop loss enabled AND close crosses below (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100) × close of the most recent bar satisfying the long-entry condition
Exit: short, RSI(close,L) crosses above oversold OR stop loss enabled AND close crosses above (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) × close of the most recent bar satisfying the short-entry condition and EMA filter
