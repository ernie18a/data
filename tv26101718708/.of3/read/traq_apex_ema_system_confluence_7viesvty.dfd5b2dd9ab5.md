Calculation: LongRibbonAligned = EMA(source, ribbon length 1) > EMA(source, ribbon length 2) AND EMA(source, ribbon length 2) > EMA(source, ribbon length 3) AND EMA(source, ribbon length 3) > EMA(source, ribbon length 4) AND EMA(source, ribbon length 4) > EMA(source, ribbon length 5)
Calculation: ShortRibbonAligned = EMA(source, ribbon length 1) < EMA(source, ribbon length 2) AND EMA(source, ribbon length 2) < EMA(source, ribbon length 3) AND EMA(source, ribbon length 3) < EMA(source, ribbon length 4) AND EMA(source, ribbon length 4) < EMA(source, ribbon length 5)
Calculation: BarsSinceLongPullback = bars since the last bar on which open > EMA(source, ribbon length 1) AND low <= EMA(source, ribbon length 3); a bar on which close < EMA(source, ribbon length 5) OR NOT LongRibbonAligned makes it unbounded, even if that bar also meets the pullback test
Calculation: BarsSinceShortPullback = bars since the last bar on which open < EMA(source, ribbon length 1) AND high >= EMA(source, ribbon length 3); a bar on which close > EMA(source, ribbon length 5) OR NOT ShortRibbonAligned makes it unbounded, even if that bar also meets the pullback test
Calculation: TrendingRegime = ABS(EMA(source, ribbon length 1) - EMA(source, ribbon length 8)) >= ATR(atr length) * min ribbon spread multiple
Calculation: HigherTFState(T) = 1 if EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar) > EMA(source, MTF slow length, T, last completed bar) AND EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar) > EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar)[slope lookback] AND ABS(EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar) - EMA(source, MTF slow length, T, last completed bar)) >= ATR(atr length, T, last completed bar) * min HTF spread multiple; -1 if EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar) < EMA(source, MTF slow length, T, last completed bar) AND EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar) < EMA(source, MTF fast length, T, last completed bar)[slope lookback] AND the same spread condition holds; otherwise 0
Calculation: BullStructureBreak = close crosses above the last confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND close - that PivotHigh >= min BOS points
Calculation: BearStructureBreak = close crosses below the last confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND that PivotLow - close >= min BOS points
Calculation: RecentBullBreak = BullStructureBreak on a bar within the last signal expiry bars, inclusive, with no BearStructureBreak since it
Calculation: RecentBearBreak = BearStructureBreak on a bar within the last signal expiry bars, inclusive, with no BullStructureBreak since it
Calculation: RecentInternalLowSweep = low <= the last confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) on a bar within the last signal expiry bars, inclusive, with no PivotLow confirmed after that bar
Calculation: RecentInternalHighSweep = high >= the last confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) on a bar within the last signal expiry bars, inclusive, with no PivotHigh confirmed after that bar
Calculation: BullGapRetest = for some bull gap among the max tracked FVGs most recent unmitigated bull gaps created on an earlier bar, the current bar has low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom AND close > gap midpoint; a bull gap is created by a bar with low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= min FVG size, has top = low, bottom = high[2], midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2, and is mitigated on any bar with low <= midpoint
Calculation: BearGapRetest = for some bear gap among the max tracked FVGs most recent unmitigated bear gaps created on an earlier bar, the current bar has high >= gap bottom AND low <= gap top AND close < gap midpoint; a bear gap is created by a bar with high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= min FVG size, has top = low[2], bottom = high, midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2, and is mitigated on any bar with high >= midpoint
Calculation: InNYSession = datetime is within the NY session window
Calculation: RecentExternalLowSweep = a chart bar within the last signal expiry bars, inclusive, that is in the current T3 period and on which low crosses below the last completed T3 bar low, or that is in the current T4 period and on which low crosses below the last completed T4 bar low
Calculation: RecentExternalHighSweep = a chart bar within the last signal expiry bars, inclusive, that is in the current T3 period and on which high crosses above the last completed T3 bar high, or that is in the current T4 period and on which high crosses above the last completed T4 bar high
Calculation: LongConfluenceScore = count of the following that hold: RecentBullBreak; RecentInternalLowSweep; BullGapRetest; InNYSession; close > VWAP(hlc3) AND VWAP(hlc3) >= VWAP(hlc3)[VWAP slope lookback]; RecentExternalLowSweep
Calculation: ShortConfluenceScore = count of the following that hold: RecentBearBreak; RecentInternalHighSweep; BearGapRetest; InNYSession; close < VWAP(hlc3) AND VWAP(hlc3) <= VWAP(hlc3)[VWAP slope lookback]; RecentExternalHighSweep
Long: BarsSinceLongPullback <= pullback lookback AND close > EMA(source, ribbon length 1) AND close > open AND ABS(close - open) >= ATR(atr length) * min body multiple AND LongRibbonAligned AND close > EMA(source, bias length) AND EMA(source, bias length) - EMA(source, bias length)[slope lookback] >= -close * flat threshold / 100 AND TrendingRegime AND HigherTFState(T1) != -1 AND HigherTFState(T2) != -1 AND RecentBullBreak AND RecentInternalLowSweep AND LongConfluenceScore >= min score
Short: BarsSinceShortPullback <= pullback lookback AND close < EMA(source, ribbon length 1) AND close < open AND ABS(close - open) >= ATR(atr length) * min body multiple AND ShortRibbonAligned AND close < EMA(source, bias length) AND EMA(source, bias length) - EMA(source, bias length)[slope lookback] <= close * flat threshold / 100 AND TrendingRegime AND HigherTFState(T1) != 1 AND HigherTFState(T2) != 1 AND RecentBearBreak AND RecentInternalHighSweep AND ShortConfluenceScore >= min score
