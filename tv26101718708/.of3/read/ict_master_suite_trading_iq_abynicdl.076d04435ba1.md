Long: On a confirmed chart bar ending a T1 bar, close crosses above the latest confirmed ZigZag swing high from T1 while structure direction is Up (BoS) or Down (MSS).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar ending a T1 bar, close crosses below the latest confirmed ZigZag swing low from T1 while structure direction is Down (BoS) or Up (MSS).
Calculation: Session range = highest(high) and lowest(low) during the configured session.
Long: A bullish liquidity sweep has occurred and has not been traded; while flat, choose the bullish FVG formed after the sweep whose upper edge is nearest the configured fraction of the range from the sweep low to the highest high since the sweep, provided close is above that edge and the edge is at or above the sweep low; enter at that edge.
Short: A bearish liquidity sweep has occurred and has not been traded; while flat, choose the bearish FVG formed after the sweep whose lower edge is nearest the configured fraction of the range from the sweep high to the lowest low since the sweep, provided close is below that edge and the edge is at or below the sweep high; enter at that edge.
Long: While flat, the setup swing low is below both adjacent swing points and close is at or above the following swing high; a bullish FVG formed after the setup overlaps a bullish breaker block formed after the setup, close is at or above the FVG upper edge, and the breaker block lower edge is at or above the setup swing low; enter at the lower of the FVG upper edge and breaker block upper edge.
Short: While flat, the setup swing high is above both adjacent swing points and close is at or below the following swing low; a bearish FVG formed after the setup overlaps a bearish breaker block formed after the setup, close is at or below the FVG lower edge, and the breaker block lower edge is at or below the setup swing high; enter at the higher of the FVG lower edge and breaker block lower edge.
Long: After the configured session ends, its low has been raided and not previously traded; while flat, a bullish breaker block formed after the raid has an upper edge above the session low and close is above that edge; choose the qualifying block with the highest upper edge and enter at that edge.
Short: After the configured session ends, its high has been raided and not previously traded; while flat, a bearish breaker block formed after the raid has a lower edge below the session high and close is below that edge; choose the qualifying block with the lowest lower edge and enter at that edge.
Long: In bullish structure, price retraces to the configured fraction of the bullish swing range from the swing high after the bullish structure shift.
Short: In bearish structure, price retraces to the configured fraction of the bearish swing range from the swing low after the bearish structure shift.
Long: During the configured session, a bullish FVG forms while the bias from the last completed T1 bar is bullish.
Short: During the configured session, a bearish FVG forms while the bias from the last completed T1 bar is bearish.
Exit: Long, low <= sweep low OR high >= highest high since the bullish sweep.
Exit: Short, high >= sweep high OR low <= lowest low since the bearish sweep.
Exit: Long, low <= selected Unicorn stop (setup swing low or the lower of the FVG and breaker block lower edges, less the configured tick offset) OR high >= selected Unicorn target (nearest swing high above entry or configured Fibonacci extension).
Exit: Short, high >= selected Unicorn stop (setup swing high or the higher of the FVG and breaker block upper edges, plus the configured tick offset) OR low <= selected Unicorn target (nearest swing low below entry or configured Fibonacci extension).
Exit: Long, low <= selected liquidity-raid stop (raided session low, bullish breaker block lower edge, or raided session low + (session high - raided session low) × long target Fibonacci parameter) OR high >= selected target (swing high above entry or session high - (session high - raided session low) × long target Fibonacci parameter).
Exit: Short, high >= selected liquidity-raid stop (raided session high, bearish breaker block upper edge, or raided session high + (session low - raided session high) × short target Fibonacci parameter) OR low <= selected target (swing low below entry or session low - (session low - raided session high) × short target Fibonacci parameter).
Exit: Long, low <= configured OTE stop below the swing low OR high >= configured OTE target (swing high or selected Fibonacci extension).
Exit: Short, high >= configured OTE stop above the swing high OR low <= configured OTE target (swing low or selected Fibonacci extension).
Exit: Long, low <= selected Silver Bullet stop (nearest swing low or low of the last completed T1 bar) OR high >= selected target (nearest swing high above entry or high of the last completed T1 bar).
Exit: Short, high >= selected Silver Bullet stop (nearest swing high or high of the last completed T1 bar) OR low <= selected target (nearest swing low below entry or low of the last completed T1 bar).
