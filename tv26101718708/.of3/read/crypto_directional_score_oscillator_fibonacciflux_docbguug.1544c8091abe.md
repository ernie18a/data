Calculation: Trend = clamp((EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) − EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) / (trend ATR multiplier × max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick)), trend lower clip, trend upper clip) on chart bars
Calculation: Momentum = clamp(log(close / close[momentum horizon]) / max(StDev(log(close / close[1]), normalization lookback) × sqrt(momentum horizon), momentum denominator floor), momentum lower clip, momentum upper clip) / momentum divisor; use zero for a log return when either close is nonpositive
Calculation: Flow = clamp(clamp((close-location multiplier × close − high − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick), location lower clip, location upper clip) × min(volume / SMA(volume, normalization lookback), relative-volume cap) / flow divisor, flow lower clip, flow upper clip)
Calculation: Efficiency = clamp(abs(close − close[efficiency lookback]) / (SMA(abs(change(close)), efficiency lookback) × efficiency lookback), efficiency lower clip, efficiency upper clip); use zero when the denominator is unavailable or nonpositive
Calculation: MeanReversion = clamp((EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) − close) / (mean-reversion ATR multiplier × max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick)), mean-reversion lower clip, mean-reversion upper clip)
Calculation: LogVolatility = clamp(log(ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-mean lookback)), log-volatility lower clip, log-volatility upper clip)
Calculation: FixedScore = logistic(clamp(fixed scale × (Efficiency × (trend coefficient × Trend + momentum coefficient × Momentum) + (1 − Efficiency) × mean-reversion coefficient × MeanReversion + flow coefficient × Flow), fixed logit lower clip, fixed logit upper clip))
Calculation: RegimePosteriors = on each confirmed chart bar with Bayesian-ready data, normalize prior state probabilities multiplied by the product of Student-t likelihoods for Trend, Momentum, Efficiency, Flow, and LogVolatility in each of Bull, Bear, Range, and Stress; each likelihood uses its state- and feature-specific center and scale and the degrees-of-freedom parameter; prior probabilities equal the state-transition matrix multiplied by the previous confirmed-bar posteriors, initialized with equal state probabilities; the transition matrix uses the persistence parameter and its other transition parameters
Calculation: ExpertLogits = (trend expert scale × Trend, momentum expert scale × Momentum, mean-reversion expert scale × MeanReversion, flow expert scale × Flow); expert probabilities are logistic(clamp(each expert logit, expert-probability lower clip, expert-probability upper clip))
Calculation: AdaptiveWeights = for each expert, use its matured predictions sampled on confirmed chart bars at the timestamp cadence set by the adaptation horizon; score each prediction after that horizon with binary log loss against whether current close > close[adaptation horizon], retain up to the adaptation sample limit, and when the minimum matured sample count is met set its raw weight to max(minimum expert weight, exp(−greediness × (its mean loss − the lowest expert mean loss))); normalize the raw weights; otherwise apply no adaptive weighting
Calculation: BayesianLogit = stress conviction × (directional-state coefficient × (Bull posterior − Bear posterior) + sum of each expert logit × its normalized gate); each regime gate is the posterior-weighted sum of its state-specific gate parameters; in adaptive mode after AdaptiveWeights is ready, multiply each regime gate by its corresponding adaptive weight and renormalize the gates
Calculation: UpScore = FixedScore when model mode selects the fixed branch; otherwise logistic(clamp(BayesianLogit, Bayesian logit lower clip, Bayesian logit upper clip))
Calculation: DownScore = 1 − UpScore
Calculation: ActiveReady = volume SMA(volume, normalization lookback) is positive and available, relative volume is available, EMA(close, slow EMA lookback), StDev(log(close / close[1]), normalization lookback), close[efficiency lookback], and Flow are available; for Bayesian mode also require LogVolatility to be available
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, ActiveReady AND UpScore crosses above the signal threshold
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, ActiveReady AND DownScore crosses above the signal threshold
