Calculation: Pivots(side, X) = pivots of that side known by now on X, where now is the current bar of X (for T1, the last completed T1 bar, so chart bars read the last completed T1 bar); a support pivot at bar p is the lowest low over bars p - pivot left strength through p + pivot right strength, a resistance pivot is the highest high over the same window, and a pivot is known from bar p + pivot right strength on; keep only pivots with now - p <= scan lookback, at most min(scan lookback, pivot cap) of the most recent.
Calculation: LinePrice(line, b) = p1 + (p2 - p1) * (C(b) - C(a1)) / (C(a2) - C(a1)), where line has older anchor (a1, p1) and newer anchor (a2, p2), and C(x) is the bar number of x on the chart or the open time of x on T1.
Calculation: Touches(line, endBar) = (count, last, avgError, avgReject) over pivots q of the line's side with a1 <= q < endBar, oldest first; q is a touch if |price(q) - LinePrice(q)| <= max(touch tolerance * ATR(ATR length) at q, touch tick floor * tick) and q - last >= max(spacing floor, pivot left strength + pivot right strength), where last is the bar of the last counted touch, initially a1 minus that spacing; count = number of touches; last = bar of the last touch; avgError = mean over touches other than a1 and a2 of min(|price(q) - LinePrice(q)| / tolerance at q, 1), or 1 if none; avgReject = mean over those touches of clamp((close(q) - LinePrice(q)) / (high(q) - low(q)), 0, 1) for support or clamp((LinePrice(q) - close(q)) / (high(q) - low(q)), 0, 1) for resistance, or 0 if none.
Calculation: BodyCuts(line) = number of bars b with a1 < b < a2 whose body edge, min(open, close) for support or max(open, close) for resistance, is more than body tolerance * ATR(ATR length) at b beyond LinePrice(b), below it for support and above it for resistance.
Calculation: BreakBar(line) = earliest bar b with a2 < b <= now whose break basis price is more than tol(b) below LinePrice(b) for support or more than tol(b) above LinePrice(b) for resistance, where tol(b) = max(break buffer * ATR(ATR length) at b, minimum break ticks * tick) and break basis price is the close, the average of open and close, or the body edge on the wrong side (min(open, close) for support, max(open, close) for resistance), as selected by the break basis parameter; none if no such bar.
Calculation: Reclaimed(line) = some bar b with BreakBar(line) < b <= now whose break basis price is more than tol(b) above LinePrice(b) for support or more than tol(b) below LinePrice(b) for resistance, with tol(b) as in BreakBar.
Calculation: Valid(line) = BreakBar(line) is none if the break handling parameter selects invalidation on confirmed break, otherwise not Reclaimed(line).
Calculation: Candidates(side, X) = the lines (a1 older, a2 newer) from Pivots(side, X) such that span = a2 - a1 in bar numbers is between min line length and scan lookback; slope = (p2 - p1) / span is positive for support and negative for resistance; slope metric = |slope| / ATR(ATR length) at a2 if slope mode is ATR-relative, else |slope|, is between min slope and max slope; BodyCuts(line) <= allowed body cuts; Valid(line); and Touches(line, endBar).count >= minimum touches, where endBar = BreakBar(line) if defined, else now + 1.
Calculation: Score(line) = touch weight * T + recency weight * (1 - min((now - L) / scan lookback, 1)) + span weight * min(span / scan lookback, 1), where T and L are the count and last-touch bar from Touches(line, endBar) with the same endBar as in Candidates.
Calculation: Primary(side, X) = the line in Candidates(side, X) with the highest Score; ties go to the line enumerated first (newer anchor from the most recent backward, then older anchor from the nearest backward); none if Candidates is empty.
Calculation: Strength(line) = 100 * (w_count * min(max(T - 2, 0) / full-credit touches, 1) + w_precision * (1 - avgError) + w_path * (1 - BodyCuts(line) / (allowed body cuts + 1)) + w_recency * (1 - min((now - L) / max(recency fraction * scan lookback, 1), 1)) + w_rejection * avgReject + w_span * min(span / max(span maturity multiple * min line length, 1), 1)) * (broken multiplier if BreakBar(line) is defined, else 1), where T, L, avgError, avgReject come from Touches(line, endBar) as in Score.
Calculation: Anchors(side, X) = the older and newer anchors of Primary(side, X) (bar numbers on the chart, open times on T1), or none if Primary(side, X) is none.
Long: Primary(support, chart) exists AND show active lines enabled
Long: show active lines enabled AND show second lines enabled AND Candidates(support, chart) has at least two members
Long: T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(support, T1) exists
Long: Primary(support, chart) exists AND (repeat mode OR Anchors(support, chart) != Anchors(support, chart)[1])
Long: T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(support, T1) exists AND (repeat mode OR Anchors(support, T1) != Anchors(support, T1)[1])
Long: (Primary(support, chart) exists AND Strength(Primary(support, chart)) >= MAX(strong threshold, weak threshold + 1)) OR (T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(support, T1) exists AND Strength(Primary(support, T1)) >= MAX(strong threshold, weak threshold + 1))
Long: (Primary(support, chart) exists OR (T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(support, T1) exists)) AND (repeat mode OR any of Anchors(support, chart), Anchors(resistance, chart), Anchors(support, T1), Anchors(resistance, T1) differs from its value on the previous chart bar)
Short: Primary(resistance, chart) exists AND show active lines enabled
Short: show active lines enabled AND show second lines enabled AND Candidates(resistance, chart) has at least two members
Short: T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(resistance, T1) exists
Short: Primary(resistance, chart) exists AND (repeat mode OR Anchors(resistance, chart) != Anchors(resistance, chart)[1])
Short: T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(resistance, T1) exists AND (repeat mode OR Anchors(resistance, T1) != Anchors(resistance, T1)[1])
Short: (Primary(resistance, chart) exists AND Strength(Primary(resistance, chart)) >= MAX(strong threshold, weak threshold + 1)) OR (T1 lines enabled AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND Primary(resistance, T1) exists AND Strength(Primary(resistance, T1)) >= MAX(strong threshold, weak threshold + 1))
