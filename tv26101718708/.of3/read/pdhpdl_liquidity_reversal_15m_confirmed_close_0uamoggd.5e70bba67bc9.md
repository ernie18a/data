Calculation: Prior session high = maximum high of chart bars in the most recently completed configured session
Calculation: Prior session low = minimum low of chart bars in the most recently completed configured session
Calculation: Current T1 high = maximum high of chart bars since the current T1 period began
Calculation: Current T1 low = minimum low of chart bars since the current T1 period began
Calculation: Stop buffer = buffer ticks * minimum tick
Calculation: Long stop = previous long stop, initialized at entry to Current T1 low - Stop buffer; when trailing is enabled, a confirmed PivotLow(low, left lookback, right lookback) is available, and high >= entry price + initial risk * activation multiple, update it to the greater of the previous long stop and PivotLow(low, left lookback, right lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR buffer
Calculation: Short stop = previous short stop, initialized at entry to Current T1 high + Stop buffer; when trailing is enabled, a confirmed PivotHigh(high, left lookback, right lookback) is available, and low <= entry price - initial risk * activation multiple, update it to the lesser of the previous short stop and PivotHigh(high, left lookback, right lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR buffer
Long: On a confirmed chart bar close, a low has been below Prior session low since the current T1 period began or the most recent long signal, and close > Prior session low and close[1] <= Prior session low, and (rejection-close filter disabled or close > (high + low) / 2), and (session filter disabled or the bar is within the configured session), and (one-long-per-T1 filter disabled or no long signal has occurred in the current T1 period), and no short position is open, and close - (Current T1 low - Stop buffer) > minimum tick
Short: On a confirmed chart bar close, a high has been above Prior session high since the current T1 period began or the most recent short signal, and close < Prior session high and close[1] >= Prior session high, and (rejection-close filter disabled or close < (high + low) / 2), and (session filter disabled or the bar is within the configured session), and (one-short-per-T1 filter disabled or no short signal has occurred in the current T1 period), and no long position is open, and (Current T1 high + Stop buffer) - close > minimum tick
Exit: Long, low <= Long stop
Exit: Short, high >= Short stop
