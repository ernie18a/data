Calculation: Pivot zones = selected Classic, Fibonacci, Woodie, Camarilla, or DeMark Pivot Points from high, low, and close of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars; each P, R1–R4, and S1–S4 zone spans its pivot level ± zone width.
Calculation: Zone width = selected percentage of chart close, selected multiple × ATR(zone length, T1, last completed bar), or selected fixed price distance; refresh with the zones.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = zone width × selected breakout fraction.
Calculation: Zone bias = on chart bars, classify close into its gap between adjacent zones using the breakout buffer; gaps outside R4 and S4 are neutral, and a new T1 period resets the bias. For an interior gap, let U and L be its upper and lower zones. In order: if previous close is within U extended by the buffer and close ≤ U top + buffer, bias is bearish if U is enabled, otherwise neutral; else if previous close is within L extended by the buffer and close ≥ L bottom − buffer, bias is bullish if L is enabled, otherwise neutral; else if previous close > U top + buffer and close > U bottom − buffer, bias is bullish if U is enabled, otherwise neutral; else if previous close < L bottom − buffer and close < L top + buffer, bias is bearish regardless of whether L is enabled; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Zone proximity = open is within either zone bordering the current gap, or open > a bordering zone top and close < that zone bottom; at an outer gap, use the nearest outer zone.
Calculation: Engulfing range is valid = abs(close − open) is between separately selected minimum and maximum bounds, each set as a percentage of close, a multiple of ATR(engulf length, chart bars), or a fixed price distance.
Calculation: Pin-bar direction(open, close, high, low) = none if high = low, body/range exceeds the selected maximum, longer-wick/range is below the selected minimum, or longer-wick/body is below its selected minimum; otherwise bullish if lower wick > upper wick, bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Bullish sweep-engulf = low[1] < lowest(low, sweep lookback)[2] AND pin-bar direction(open[1], close[1], high[1], low[1]) is bullish AND close > open AND close > high[1] AND engulfing range is valid.
Calculation: Bearish sweep-engulf = high[1] > highest(high, sweep lookback)[2] AND pin-bar direction(open[1], close[1], high[1], low[1]) is bearish AND close < open AND close < low[1] AND engulfing range is valid.
Long: bar closes AND zone bias is bullish AND zone proximity AND ((close > open AND (close[1] < open[1] OR close[2] < open[2]) AND (body-only mode: open ≤ close[1] + gap allowance × minimum tick size AND close > (open[1] if close[1] < open[1], otherwise high[1]); full-range mode: low < low[1] AND high > high[1]) AND engulfing range is valid) OR bullish sweep-engulf) AND (lower-timeframe EMA disabled OR close > EMA(lower source, lower length)[lower offset] OR bullish sweep-engulf) AND (higher-timeframe EMA disabled OR close > EMA(higher source, higher length, T2, forming bar) sampled on chart bars) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI length) ≤ upper RSI limit) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR Bollinger Bandwidth(BB length, multiplier) ≥ minimum bandwidth) AND (no-trade filter disabled OR chart timeframe > T3 OR datetime is outside the configured no-trade window) AND primary entries since the latest chart session start or enabled NY/London reset are below the trade limit.
Short: bar closes AND zone bias is bearish AND zone proximity AND ((close < open AND (close[1] > open[1] OR close[2] > open[2]) AND (body-only mode: open ≥ close[1] − gap allowance × minimum tick size AND close < (open[1] if close[1] > open[1], otherwise low[1]); full-range mode: low < low[1] AND high > high[1]) AND engulfing range is valid) OR bearish sweep-engulf) AND (lower-timeframe EMA disabled OR close < EMA(lower source, lower length)[lower offset] OR bearish sweep-engulf) AND (higher-timeframe EMA disabled OR close < EMA(higher source, higher length, T2, forming bar) sampled on chart bars) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI length) ≥ lower RSI limit) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR Bollinger Bandwidth(BB length, multiplier) ≥ minimum bandwidth) AND (no-trade filter disabled OR chart timeframe > T3 OR datetime is outside the configured no-trade window) AND primary entries since the latest chart session start or enabled NY/London reset are below the trade limit.
Calculation: Long stop = selected entry-bar low − stop buffer, lowest low through the entry bar over the long swing lookback − stop buffer, active zone lower boundary − stop buffer, entry price − fixed distance, or entry price − ATR(stop length, chart bars) × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop = selected entry-bar high + stop buffer, highest high through the entry bar over the separate short swing lookback + stop buffer, active zone upper boundary + stop buffer, entry price + fixed distance, or entry price + ATR(stop length, chart bars) × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × the applicable main target ratio; first-entry and additional-entry ratios are separate parameters.
Calculation: Short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × the applicable main target ratio; first-entry and additional-entry ratios are separate parameters.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
Exit: long, partial-TP enabled AND high ≥ entry price + (entry price − long stop) × TP1 ratio AND applicable main target ratio > TP1 ratio.
Exit: short, partial-TP enabled AND low ≤ entry price − (short stop − entry price) × TP1 ratio AND applicable main target ratio > TP1 ratio.
Exit: long, breakeven enabled AND applicable main target ratio > TP1 ratio AND high has reached entry price + (entry price − long stop) × TP1 ratio AND low ≤ entry price.
Exit: short, breakeven enabled AND applicable main target ratio > TP1 ratio AND low has reached entry price − (short stop − entry price) × TP1 ratio AND high ≥ entry price.
Exit: long, chart timeframe ≤ T3 AND day-end closing enabled AND this is the configured day-end close bar, OR chart timeframe ≤ T4 AND week-end closing enabled AND this is the configured Friday close bar.
Exit: short, chart timeframe ≤ T3 AND day-end closing enabled AND this is the configured day-end close bar, OR chart timeframe ≤ T4 AND week-end closing enabled AND this is the configured Friday close bar.
