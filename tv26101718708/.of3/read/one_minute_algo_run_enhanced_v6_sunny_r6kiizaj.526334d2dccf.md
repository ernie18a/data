Calculation: Opening range = first T4 bar high/low or highest high/lowest low over the configured opening window, according to the selected method; calculate on completed T4 bars, carry values to chart bars, and lock when the window is complete. Width = high − low; midpoint = (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Long targets = opening-range high + opening-range width × separate long first- and second-target multipliers.
Calculation: Short targets = opening-range low − opening-range width × separate short first- and second-target multipliers.
Calculation: Breakout body fraction = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick), on chart bars.
Calculation: Current-day POC = lowest-price bin with maximum accumulated volume; bins span the current-day low to high, and each T3 bar’s volume is distributed across price bins in proportion to high-low overlap (zero-range bars assign volume to their containing bin). Update the developing profile on chart bars from T3 bars.
Calculation: Adaptive quality thresholds = (base ADX minimum + penalty, min(body cap, base body minimum + penalty / body scale)); penalty is zero unless adaptive filtering is enabled and the completed-trade sample reaches its minimum, then the larger penalty applies below the lower win-rate threshold, the smaller penalty below the higher threshold, otherwise zero. Win rate is the successful fraction of the recent configured sample; reaching the second target or breakeven after the first target is successful, and stopping before the first target is unsuccessful.
Calculation: Live directional score = the sum of votes for EMA trend on T1, EMA trend on T2, close versus current-session VWAP, close versus opening-range midpoint, +DI versus −DI, and close versus opening-range boundaries. Each vote is positive, negative, or zero for bullish, bearish, or tied; midpoint vote is zero until the range locks. Compute on chart bars, using EMA values from the last completed T1 and T2 bars and VWAP from the current/forming T4 bar.
Long: At range lock, EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND EMA(close, fast lookback, T2, last completed bar) > EMA(close, slow lookback, T2, last completed bar) AND (opening VWAP confirmation disabled OR last completed T4 close at lock >= VWAP at that completed T4 bar); freeze the bias for the trading day.
Short: At range lock, EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND EMA(close, fast lookback, T2, last completed bar) < EMA(close, slow lookback, T2, last completed bar) AND (opening VWAP confirmation disabled OR last completed T4 close at lock <= VWAP at that completed T4 bar); freeze the bias for the trading day.
Long: Live directional score >= score threshold AND NOT (range locked AND (opening-range width < minimum width OR (chop filter enabled AND ADX(DMI lookback, smoothing) < effective ADX minimum) OR (POC block enabled AND current-day POC exists AND abs(close − current-day POC) <= ATR(lookback) × POC radius)).
Short: Live directional score <= −score threshold AND NOT (range locked AND (opening-range width < minimum width OR (chop filter enabled AND ADX(DMI lookback, smoothing) < effective ADX minimum) OR (POC block enabled AND current-day POC exists AND abs(close − current-day POC) <= ATR(lookback) × POC radius)).
Long: Range locked AND NOT choppy AND live directional score >= score threshold AND the previous chart bar’s score was below the score threshold.
Short: Range locked AND NOT choppy AND live directional score <= −score threshold AND the previous chart bar’s score was above −score threshold.
Long: Range locked AND chart-bar time is within the configured trade window AND close crosses above opening-range high AND opening-range width >= minimum width AND (chop filter disabled OR ADX(DMI lookback, smoothing) >= effective ADX minimum) AND (POC block disabled OR current-day POC unavailable OR abs(close − current-day POC) > ATR(lookback) × POC radius) AND (chop filter disabled OR breakout body fraction >= effective body minimum) AND (opening-bias requirement disabled OR frozen opening bias is long) AND (re-entry enabled OR no earlier long signal this trading day).
Short: Range locked AND chart-bar time is within the configured trade window AND close crosses below opening-range low AND opening-range width >= minimum width AND (chop filter disabled OR ADX(DMI lookback, smoothing) >= effective ADX minimum) AND (POC block disabled OR current-day POC unavailable OR abs(close − current-day POC) > ATR(lookback) × POC radius) AND (chop filter disabled OR breakout body fraction >= effective body minimum) AND (opening-bias requirement disabled OR frozen opening bias is short) AND (re-entry enabled OR no earlier short signal this trading day).
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= long second target OR (long first target has been reached since entry AND low <= entry price) OR (long first target has not been reached since entry, high < long first target, AND low <= opening-range midpoint).
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= short second target OR (short first target has been reached since entry AND high >= entry price) OR (short first target has not been reached since entry, low > short first target, AND high >= opening-range midpoint).
