Calculation: Channel band width = EMA(ATR(channel ATR lookback), channel smoothing lookback) × band spacing
Calculation: Channel extension = (close − EMA(close, channel lookback)) / channel band width
Calculation: Long histogram dip fraction = (highest(MACD histogram, long lookback) − lowest(MACD histogram, short lookback)) / highest(MACD histogram, long lookback) when the highest value is positive; otherwise 0
Calculation: Short histogram dip fraction = (highest(MACD histogram, short lookback) − lowest(MACD histogram, long lookback)) / absolute value of lowest(MACD histogram, long lookback) when the lowest value is negative; otherwise 0
Calculation: Initial risk distance = stop multiple × ATR(exit lookback)[1] on the chart bar before entry
Calculation: Long stop initializes to entry price − initial risk distance; when high reaches entry price + initial risk distance × break-even arm multiple, raise it to at least entry price; once armed, if trailing is enabled, update it each chart bar to the greater of its current value and highest(high, trail lookback) − trail multiple × ATR(exit lookback)
Calculation: Short stop initializes to entry price + initial risk distance; when low reaches entry price − initial risk distance × break-even arm multiple, lower it to at most entry price; once armed, if trailing is enabled, update it each chart bar to the lesser of its current value and lowest(low, trail lookback) + trail multiple × ATR(exit lookback)
Long: long direction enabled AND bars since the last entry exceed the cooldown parameter AND (trend-cross entry enabled AND MACD crosses below its signal line AND close < EMA(close, channel lookback) AND EMA(close, channel lookback) < EMA(close, channel lookback)[channel slope lookback] AND ADX(DMI lookback) >= minimum ADX AND DI− > DI+ AND (higher-timeframe agreement disabled OR MACD histogram on T1 bars, using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars, is below 0) AND Channel extension > −maximum extension AND (pullback-only restriction disabled OR MACD line > 0) OR pullback entry enabled AND close > EMA(close, channel lookback) AND EMA(close, channel lookback) > EMA(close, channel lookback)[channel slope lookback] AND ADX(DMI lookback) >= minimum ADX AND DI+ > DI− AND (higher-timeframe agreement disabled OR MACD histogram on T1 bars, using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars, is above 0) AND Channel extension < maximum extension AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > MACD histogram[1] AND MACD histogram[1] <= MACD histogram[2] AND Long histogram dip fraction >= pullback-depth parameter AND close > open OR reversal entry enabled AND low <= EMA(close, channel lookback) − channel band width × reversal band level on the current or previous reversal-tag lookback bars AND MACD histogram > MACD histogram[1] AND MACD histogram[1] <= MACD histogram[2] AND close > open AND close > close[1])
Short: short direction enabled AND bars since the last entry exceed the cooldown parameter AND NOT (the Long condition) AND (trend-cross entry enabled AND MACD crosses below its signal line AND close < EMA(close, channel lookback) AND EMA(close, channel lookback) < EMA(close, channel lookback)[channel slope lookback] AND ADX(DMI lookback) >= minimum ADX AND DI− > DI+ AND (higher-timeframe agreement disabled OR MACD histogram on T1 bars, using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars, is below 0) AND Channel extension > −maximum extension AND (pullback-only restriction disabled OR MACD line < 0) OR pullback entry enabled AND close < EMA(close, channel lookback) AND EMA(close, channel lookback) < EMA(close, channel lookback)[channel slope lookback] AND ADX(DMI lookback) >= minimum ADX AND DI− > DI+ AND (higher-timeframe agreement disabled OR MACD histogram on T1 bars, using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars, is below 0) AND Channel extension > −maximum extension AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < MACD histogram[1] AND MACD histogram[1] >= MACD histogram[2] AND Short histogram dip fraction >= pullback-depth parameter AND close < open OR reversal entry enabled AND high >= EMA(close, channel lookback) + channel band width × reversal band level on the current or previous reversal-tag lookback bars AND MACD histogram < MACD histogram[1] AND MACD histogram[1] >= MACD histogram[2] AND close < open AND close < close[1])
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + initial risk distance × target multiple OR opposite-MACD-cross exit enabled AND elapsed chart bars since entry >= minimum hold bars AND MACD crosses below its signal line OR time stop enabled AND elapsed chart bars since entry >= time-stop bars
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price − initial risk distance × target multiple OR opposite-MACD-cross exit enabled AND elapsed chart bars since entry >= minimum hold bars AND MACD crosses above its signal line OR time stop enabled AND elapsed chart bars since entry >= time-stop bars
