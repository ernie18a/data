Calculation: primary-timeframe price = price source from T1's current bar, including the still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: long-term price = price source from T2's current bar, including the still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: smoothed historical volatility = SMA(STDEV(ROC(primary-timeframe price, return lookback) / 100, volatility lookback), volatility smoothing), calculated on chart bars
Calculation: holding duration = number of chart bars since entry
Long: no open position AND smoothed historical volatility >= low-volatility threshold AND SMA(primary-timeframe price, shorter moving-average lookback) > SMA(primary-timeframe price, longer moving-average lookback) AND SMA(primary-timeframe price, shorter moving-average lookback) > SMA(long-term price, longer moving-average lookback) AND (high-volatility skip disabled OR smoothed historical volatility <= high-volatility threshold) AND (Bollinger-extreme skip disabled OR primary-timeframe price is between the lower and upper Bollinger Bands(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier))
Long: no open position AND (high-volatility skip disabled OR smoothed historical volatility <= high-volatility threshold) AND (low-volatility skip disabled OR smoothed historical volatility >= low-volatility threshold) AND (Bollinger-extreme skip disabled OR primary-timeframe price is between the lower and upper Bollinger Bands(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier)) AND Z-score(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period) < lower z-score threshold
Short: no open position AND (high-volatility skip disabled OR smoothed historical volatility <= high-volatility threshold) AND (low-volatility skip disabled OR smoothed historical volatility >= low-volatility threshold) AND (Bollinger-extreme skip disabled OR primary-timeframe price is between the lower and upper Bollinger Bands(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier)) AND Z-score(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period) > upper z-score threshold
Exit: long, primary-timeframe price >= upper Bollinger Band(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier) OR primary-timeframe price < primary-timeframe price - trailing-stop multiplier * ATR(chart-timeframe bars, ATR lookback) OR primary-timeframe price >= middle Bollinger Band(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier) OR holding duration >= maximum holding bars
Exit: short, primary-timeframe price <= lower Bollinger Band(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier) OR primary-timeframe price > primary-timeframe price + trailing-stop multiplier * ATR(chart-timeframe bars, ATR lookback) OR primary-timeframe price <= middle Bollinger Band(primary-timeframe price, Bollinger period, band multiplier) OR holding duration >= maximum holding bars
