Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA(close, short lookback); medium EMA = EMA(close, medium lookback); long EMA = EMA(close, long lookback), with short < medium < long; long SMA = SMA(close, separate long lookback).
Calculation: Long structure condition = (short EMA filter disabled OR close > fast EMA) AND (medium EMA filter disabled OR (close > medium EMA AND fast EMA > medium EMA)) AND (long EMA filter disabled OR (close > long EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA)) AND (long SMA filter disabled OR close > long SMA).
Calculation: Short structure condition = (short EMA filter disabled OR close < fast EMA) AND (medium EMA filter disabled OR (close < medium EMA AND fast EMA < medium EMA)) AND (long EMA filter disabled OR (close < long EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA)) AND (long SMA filter disabled OR close < long SMA).
Calculation: VWAP rising = VWAP(hlc3) > VWAP(hlc3) at the slope lookback; VWAP falling = VWAP(hlc3) < VWAP(hlc3) at the slope lookback.
Calculation: Long VWAP condition = the selected VWAP mode uses either (close > VWAP(hlc3) AND VWAP rising), (close > VWAP(hlc3) AND low <= VWAP(hlc3) + ATR(ATR lookback) * VWAP retest tolerance), or (close > VWAP(hlc3)); the retest branch also requires VWAP rising.
Calculation: Short VWAP condition = the selected VWAP mode uses either (close < VWAP(hlc3) AND VWAP falling), (close < VWAP(hlc3) AND high >= VWAP(hlc3) - ATR(ATR lookback) * VWAP retest tolerance), or (close < VWAP(hlc3)); the retest branch also requires VWAP falling.
Calculation: Long money-flow condition = MFI(hlc3, MFI lookback) > bullish MFI threshold AND MFI is rising AND (CMF filter disabled OR CMF(volume, high, low, close, CMF lookback) > 0), with MFI enabled.
Calculation: Short money-flow condition = MFI(hlc3, MFI lookback) < bearish MFI threshold AND MFI is falling AND (CMF filter disabled OR CMF(volume, high, low, close, CMF lookback) < 0), with MFI enabled.
Calculation: Long RSI condition = RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bullish RSI threshold AND its change from the prior chart bar >= minimum RSI change AND RSI < overbought threshold, with RSI enabled.
Calculation: Short RSI condition = RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bearish RSI threshold AND its change from the prior chart bar <= negative minimum RSI change AND RSI > oversold threshold, with RSI enabled.
Calculation: Long MACD condition = MACD is enabled AND (the selected confirmation mode uses either (MACD line crosses above signal line OR (MACD line > signal line AND a bullish cross occurred within the freshness lookback)), (MACD histogram > 0 AND rising), their OR, or their AND) OR (MACD histogram rises across each of the two most recent chart-bar changes).
Calculation: Short MACD condition = MACD is enabled AND (the selected confirmation mode uses either (MACD line crosses below signal line OR (MACD line < signal line AND a bearish cross occurred within the freshness lookback)), (MACD histogram < 0 AND falling), their OR, or their AND) OR (MACD histogram falls across each of the two most recent chart-bar changes).
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high = low; candle close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or the zero-range fallback when high = low; strong body means body percentage >= minimum body percentage.
Calculation: Bullish candle condition = candle filter disabled OR (strong body AND candle close position >= bullish close-position threshold); bearish candle condition = candle filter disabled OR (strong body AND candle close position <= bearish close-position threshold).
Calculation: ATR condition = (close = 0 ? 0 : 100 * ATR(ATR lookback) / close) >= minimum ATR percentage; volume condition = volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier; breakout volume condition = volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * separate breakout volume multiplier.
Calculation: MACD flat = abs(MACD histogram) < nz(SMA(abs(MACD histogram), flatness lookback)) * flatness multiplier; price is stuck near averages when abs(close - medium EMA) < ATR * stuck-distance multiplier AND abs(close - VWAP(hlc3)) < ATR * stuck-distance multiplier; EMA compression = abs(fast EMA - medium EMA) <= ATR * compression multiplier.
Calculation: Chop condition = (price is stuck near averages AND MACD flat) OR (EMA compression filter enabled AND EMA compression AND MACD flat) OR NOT ATR condition.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot resistance and support levels are the most recent retained pivot highs and lows, confirmed using separate left and right lookbacks; nearest resistance is the closest retained level above close and nearest support is the closest retained level below close. Proximity = ATR * S/R proximity multiplier.
Calculation: Support bounce = nearest support exists AND abs(close - nearest support) <= proximity AND low <= nearest support + proximity * bounce fraction AND close > open AND close > nearest support; resistance rejection = nearest resistance exists AND abs(close - nearest resistance) <= proximity AND high >= nearest resistance - proximity * bounce fraction AND close < open AND close < nearest resistance.
Calculation: A resistance break is recorded when nearest resistance exists AND close > nearest resistance AND the prior chart-bar close <= nearest resistance; a support break is recorded when nearest support exists AND close < nearest support AND the prior chart-bar close >= nearest support. Store the broken level and chart-bar index until another break; initially each is undefined.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = a stored resistance break is within the confirmation lookback AND close > the stored resistance + ATR * breakout buffer AND breakout volume condition AND bullish candle condition; bearish breakdown = a stored support break is within the confirmation lookback AND close < the stored support - ATR * breakout buffer AND breakout volume condition AND bearish candle condition.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = nearest support exists AND low < nearest support AND close > nearest support AND breakout volume condition AND bullish candle condition AND candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage; bearish liquidity sweep = nearest resistance exists AND high > nearest resistance AND close < nearest resistance AND breakout volume condition AND bearish candle condition AND candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage.
Calculation: Reclaimed resistance = a stored resistance break exists AND close > that resistance AND low <= that resistance * (1 + reclaim tolerance) AND close > open; lost support = a stored support break exists AND close < that support AND high >= that support * (1 - reclaim tolerance) AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish S/R context = support bounce OR reclaimed resistance OR bullish breakout; bearish S/R context = resistance rejection OR lost support OR bearish breakdown.
Calculation: T1 close, EMA, VWAP, RSI, and MACD histogram are requested on chart bars with lookahead off; historical chart bars use the last completed T1 bar and real-time chart bars use the forming T1 bar. The EMA, RSI, and MACD lengths are separate T1 parameters. Long T1 bias = T1 disabled OR ((T1 EMA filter disabled OR T1 close > T1 EMA) AND (T1 VWAP filter disabled OR T1 close > T1 VWAP)); short T1 bias is the corresponding less-than condition.
Calculation: Long T1 momentum = T1 MACD histogram > its value on the prior chart bar AND T1 RSI > its momentum threshold; short T1 momentum = T1 MACD histogram < its value on the prior chart bar AND T1 RSI < its momentum threshold.

Calculation: Long gate condition = (long ready OR early long ready) AND no prior long or short signal exists OR the chart-bar distance from the prior signal is >= cooldown lookback; AND long score > short score AND long score - short score >= minimum score gap AND (rising-score requirement disabled OR long score > its prior chart-bar value, using zero if unavailable) AND (trend reset disabled OR no long signal has occurred since a chart-bar close below the fast EMA). Short gate condition is symmetric, with short score > long score and reset by a close above the fast EMA.
Calculation: Primary long readiness = selected signal mode uses either (long structure condition AND (VWAP filter disabled OR long VWAP condition) AND (MACD disabled OR long MACD condition) AND (RSI disabled OR long RSI condition) AND long gate trigger), or (long gate trigger AND (MACD disabled OR long MACD condition) AND (long structure condition OR (breakout-against-structure enabled AND bullish breakout)); long gate trigger = support bounce OR reclaimed resistance OR VWAP retest hold OR (fast EMA crosses above medium EMA AND long structure condition) OR bullish breakout OR bullish liquidity sweep OR (close > fast EMA AND abs(close - fast EMA) <= ATR * gate-distance multiplier AND long VWAP condition).
