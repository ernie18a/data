Calculation: Session high(session) = highest(high) across chart bars in that session
Calculation: Session low(session) = lowest(low) across chart bars in that session
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = (low < Session low(Asia) AND close > Session low(Asia)) OR (low < Session low(London) AND close > Session low(London)) OR (low < Session low(New York) AND close > Session low(New York)) OR (low < low of the last completed T1 bar sampled on each chart bar AND close > low of the last completed T1 bar sampled on each chart bar) OR (low < low of the last completed T2 bar sampled on each chart bar AND close > low of the last completed T2 bar sampled on each chart bar)
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = (high > Session high(Asia) AND close < Session high(Asia)) OR (high > Session high(London) AND close < Session high(London)) OR (high > Session high(New York) AND close < Session high(New York)) OR (high > high of the last completed T1 bar sampled on each chart bar AND close < high of the last completed T1 bar sampled on each chart bar) OR (high > high of the last completed T2 bar sampled on each chart bar AND close < high of the last completed T2 bar sampled on each chart bar)
Long: Bullish liquidity sweep AND Bullish liquidity sweep on the previous chart bar AND close > previous chart bar high AND low > high[gap lookback] AND close > open AND close - open > ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement multiplier AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))
Short: Bearish liquidity sweep AND Bearish liquidity sweep on the previous chart bar AND close < previous chart bar low AND high < low[gap lookback] AND close < open AND open - close > ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement multiplier AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))
