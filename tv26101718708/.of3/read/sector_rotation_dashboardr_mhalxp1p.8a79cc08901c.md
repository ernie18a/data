Calculation: Percent return = 100 * (close / close[lookback] - 1), or zero when close[lookback] is unavailable
Calculation: Sector relative strength = SMA(sector close / broad-market close, shorter relative-strength lookback) / SMA(sector close / broad-market close, longer relative-strength lookback) - 1, or zero when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Sector volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or 1 when the average volume is zero
Calculation: Trend state = bullish when close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) and EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback); bearish when close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) and EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback); otherwise neutral
Calculation: Price-volume score = 1 when volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) > high-volume threshold and high > low and (close - low) / (high - low) > upper close-strength threshold and close > open; -1 when volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) > high-volume threshold and high > low and (close - low) / (high - low) < lower close-strength threshold and close < open; otherwise 0
Calculation: Sector score = clamp(relative-strength contribution + CMF contribution + volume contribution + trend state * trend weight + price-volume score * price-volume weight, lower score bound, upper score bound); relative-strength contribution is strong positive when sector relative strength > positive strong relative-strength threshold, moderate positive when it > positive mild relative-strength threshold, strong negative when it < negative strong relative-strength threshold, moderate negative when it < negative mild relative-strength threshold, otherwise neutral; positive strong threshold > positive mild threshold and negative strong threshold < negative mild threshold; CMF contribution follows the same piecewise thresholds and contributions using CMF(high, low, close, volume, CMF lookback); volume contribution is positive when sector volume ratio > high-volume-ratio threshold, negative when it < low-volume-ratio threshold, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Futures divergence average = (percent return(S&P 500 futures close, short performance lookback) - percent return(broad-market close, short performance lookback) + percent return(Nasdaq-100 futures close, short performance lookback) - percent return(technology-sector close, short performance lookback)) / 2
