Calculation: Pre-London range high = maximum chart-bar high during the configured ET pre-London window, frozen at London session start
Calculation: Pre-London range low = minimum chart-bar low during the configured ET pre-London window, frozen at London session start
Calculation: Pre-London range size = pre-London range high - pre-London range low
Calculation: Long stop = at the long-entry signal, min(latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) - pivot stop buffer, signal-bar close - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × stop-floor multiple); undefined if there is no confirmed pivot low
Calculation: Long target = at the long-entry signal, signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - long stop) × reward-to-risk ratio; undefined if the long stop is undefined
Calculation: Short stop = at the short-entry signal, max(latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) + pivot stop buffer, signal-bar close + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × stop-floor multiple); undefined if there is no confirmed pivot high
Calculation: Short target = at the short-entry signal, signal-bar close - (short stop - signal-bar close) × reward-to-risk ratio; undefined if the short stop is undefined
Long: inside the configured ET London session AND position is flat AND entries already made in the current London session < session trade limit AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= long RSI minimum AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (HTF bias filter disabled OR chart-bar close > EMA(close, HTF lookback) on T1's current bar, updating while T1 is forming in realtime and exposed at T1 close historically with lookahead off) AND ((pre-London range size >= minimum range size AND close crosses above pre-London range high) OR close crosses above latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback))
Short: inside the configured ET London session AND position is flat AND entries already made in the current London session < session trade limit AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= short RSI maximum AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (HTF bias filter disabled OR chart-bar close < EMA(close, HTF lookback) on T1's current bar, updating while T1 is forming in realtime and exposed at T1 close historically with lookahead off) AND ((pre-London range size >= minimum range size AND close crosses below pre-London range low) OR close crosses below latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback))
Exit: Long, (long stop and target are defined AND (low <= long stop OR high >= long target)) OR (current chart bar is outside both configured ET pre-London and London windows AND the previous chart bar was inside either window)
Exit: Short, (short stop and target are defined AND (high >= short stop OR low <= short target)) OR (current chart bar is outside both configured ET pre-London and London windows AND the previous chart bar was inside either window)
