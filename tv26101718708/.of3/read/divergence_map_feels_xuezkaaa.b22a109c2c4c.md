Calculation: Trend-adjusted delta = cumulative sum from zero of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero otherwise, minus EMA of that cumulative sum (delta smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Divergence strength = min(100, max(0, 100 × absolute difference between trend-adjusted delta at the new swing and at the previous same-type swing / (recent trend-adjusted delta range if positive, otherwise 1))), where recent range is HIGHEST(trend-adjusted delta, strength lookback) − LOWEST(trend-adjusted delta, strength lookback) on the swing confirmation bar
Short: On a confirmed bar that confirms a swing high using the swing lookback on each side, the new swing high > the previous confirmed swing high AND trend-adjusted delta at the new swing < trend-adjusted delta at the previous confirmed swing high AND divergence strength >= minimum strength
Long: On a confirmed bar that confirms a swing low using the swing lookback on each side, the new swing low < the previous confirmed swing low AND trend-adjusted delta at the new swing > trend-adjusted delta at the previous confirmed swing low AND divergence strength >= minimum strength
