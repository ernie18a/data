Calculation: Signal ALMA = ALMA(close, fast signal lookback, ALMA offset parameter, ALMA sigma parameter, T1 last completed bar), carried onto chart bars; T1 is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Signal fast regression = Linear Regression(close, shorter signal regression lookback, regression offset parameter, T1 last completed bar), carried onto chart bars.
Calculation: Signal slow regression = Linear Regression(close, longer signal regression lookback, regression offset parameter, T1 last completed bar), carried onto chart bars.
Calculation: Trend line = DEMA(close, trend lookback, T2 last completed bar), carried onto chart bars; T2 is higher than T1.
Calculation: Chart EMA = EMA(close, chart EMA lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Fast entry SMA = SMA(close, shorter entry lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Slow entry SMA = SMA(close, longer entry lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Signal Z-score = ZScore(Signal ALMA - SMA(Signal ALMA, normalization lookback on chart bars), normalization lookback on chart bars); its input is the T1 series carried onto chart bars.
Calculation: DirectionalCrossEvent(fast, slow, direction, minimum net change, minimum rate) = after fast enters and remains above slow for up or below slow for down, normalized favorable chart-close change since that transition is at least the minimum net change and that change divided by chart bars since the transition is at least the minimum rate; both thresholds must be positive. Normalize change as favorable chart-close change divided by minimum tick size, tick scalar, and the price normalizer when it exceeds the normalizer selection threshold, otherwise by the transition close; multiply by the percent conversion factor.
Calculation: Bullish alignment = Chart EMA > Signal ALMA AND Signal ALMA > Signal fast regression.
Calculation: Bearish alignment = Chart EMA < Signal ALMA AND Signal ALMA < Signal fast regression.
Calculation: Both signal lines above trend = Signal ALMA > Trend line AND Signal fast regression > Trend line.
Calculation: Both signal lines below trend = Signal ALMA < Trend line AND Signal fast regression < Trend line.
Calculation: Full rising fan = Bullish alignment AND Signal fast regression > Signal slow regression.
Calculation: Full falling fan = Bearish alignment AND Signal fast regression < Signal slow regression.
Calculation: Partial rising trend = Chart EMA > Signal ALMA AND Signal ALMA > Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression > Signal slow regression.
Calculation: Partial falling trend = Chart EMA < Signal ALMA AND Signal ALMA < Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression < Signal slow regression.
Calculation: Durable rising trend = Partial rising trend AND Signal slow regression > Trend line.
Calculation: Durable falling trend = Partial falling trend AND Signal slow regression < Trend line.
Calculation: Trend Stochastic K = Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback, T2 last completed bar), carried onto chart bars.
Calculation: Trend Stochastic D = SMA(Trend Stochastic K, first smoothing lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Trend Stochastic slow D = SMA(Trend Stochastic D, second smoothing lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Chart Stochastic K = Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Chart Stochastic D = SMA(Chart Stochastic K, first smoothing lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Chart Stochastic slow D = SMA(Chart Stochastic D, second smoothing lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Rising stochastic event = (Trend Stochastic D > first overbought threshold AND Full rising fan) OR (Trend Stochastic slow D > second overbought threshold AND Durable rising trend) OR (Trend Stochastic D > third overbought threshold AND Partial rising trend AND a stored rise-event close exists AND chart close > the latest chart close while the rise AL event held).
Calculation: Falling stochastic event = (Trend Stochastic D < first oversold threshold AND Full falling fan) OR (Trend Stochastic slow D < second oversold threshold AND Durable falling trend) OR (Trend Stochastic D < third oversold threshold AND Partial falling trend AND a stored fall-event close exists AND chart close < the latest chart close while the fall AL event held).
Calculation: Minimum profit reached(side) = open position profit >= minimum profit × absolute position size AND signed chart-close gain from entry price for side >= minimum profit.
Calculation: Long pullback active = latched during a long position after Chart Stochastic slow D < long pullback threshold AND (fall AL event OR Falling stochastic event); reset at entry or add-on.
Calculation: Short pullback active = latched during a short position after Chart Stochastic slow D > short pullback threshold AND (rise AL event OR Rising stochastic event); reset at entry or add-on.
Calculation: Last position level = chart close at the most recent initial entry or add-on.
Calculation: Impulse wave changed since add-on = the relation between Signal fast regression and Signal slow regression has changed since the previous add-on.
Calculation: Hemi-transit = latched for a nascent long after chart close rises above Trend line, or for a nascent short after chart close falls below Trend line; reset at entry.
Long: Fast entry SMA crosses above Slow entry SMA.
Short: Fast entry SMA crosses below Slow entry SMA.
Long: DirectionalCrossEvent(Signal ALMA, Signal fast regression, up, rise AL net-change parameter, rise AL rate parameter).
Short: DirectionalCrossEvent(Signal ALMA, Signal fast regression, down, fall AL net-change parameter, fall AL rate parameter).
Long: DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, up, rise EMA net-change parameter, rise EMA rate parameter).
Short: DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, down, fall EMA net-change parameter, fall EMA rate parameter).
Long: Rising stochastic event.
Short: Falling stochastic event.
Long: position is flat AND trading mode permits long entries AND trading mode is not No Trade AND Fast entry SMA crosses above Slow entry SMA AND ((trading mode is not Range AND chart close > Trend line) OR (long nascent entry enabled AND Signal ALMA > Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression > Signal slow regression AND Signal slow regression < Trend line)).
Short: position is flat AND trading mode permits short entries AND trading mode is not No Trade AND Fast entry SMA crosses below Slow entry SMA AND ((trading mode is not Range AND chart close < Trend line) OR (short nascent entry enabled AND Signal ALMA < Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression < Signal slow regression AND Signal slow regression > Trend line)).
Long: position side is long AND pyramiding is enabled with capacity remaining AND ((Fast entry SMA crosses above Slow entry SMA AND chart close > Trend line AND Both signal lines above trend AND chart close > Last position level AND Long pullback active) OR (chart close < Last position level AND Last position level - chart close > pyramid minimum span AND chart close > entry price AND (fall AL event OR fall EMA event OR Falling stochastic event)) OR (chart close < Last position level AND Last position level - chart close > pyramid minimum span AND chart close < entry price AND fall AL event AND Falling stochastic event AND long accumulation enabled AND Impulse wave changed since add-on)).
Short: position side is short AND pyramiding is enabled with capacity remaining AND ((Fast entry SMA crosses below Slow entry SMA AND chart close < Trend line AND Both signal lines below trend AND chart close < Last position level AND Short pullback active) OR (chart close > Last position level AND chart close - Last position level > pyramid minimum span AND chart close < entry price AND (rise AL event OR rise EMA event OR Rising stochastic event)) OR (chart close > Last position level AND chart close - Last position level > pyramid minimum span AND chart close > entry price AND rise AL event AND Rising stochastic event AND short accumulation enabled AND Impulse wave changed since add-on)).
Exit: long, stop offset > 0 AND entry-bar low - stop offset > 0 AND low <= entry-bar low - stop offset.
Exit: short, stop offset > 0 AND entry-bar high + stop offset > 0 AND high >= entry-bar high + stop offset.
Exit: long, Minimum profit reached(long) AND ((long natural exit enabled AND Signal ALMA < Signal fast regression) OR (long signal exit enabled AND Signal ALMA > Signal fast regression AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Signal ALMA, Signal fast regression, up, rise AL net-change parameter, rise AL rate parameter) AND positive Z-score threshold has been exceeded during this position) OR (long price-event exit enabled AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, up, rise EMA net-change parameter, rise EMA rate parameter) AND Bullish alignment AND Both signal lines above trend) OR (long stochastic exit enabled AND Rising stochastic event)).
Exit: short, Minimum profit reached(short) AND ((short natural exit enabled AND Signal ALMA > Signal fast regression) OR (short signal exit enabled AND Signal ALMA < Signal fast regression AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Signal ALMA, Signal fast regression, down, fall AL net-change parameter, fall AL rate parameter) AND negative Z-score threshold has been exceeded during this position) OR (short price-event exit enabled AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, down, fall EMA net-change parameter, fall EMA rate parameter) AND Bearish alignment AND Both signal lines below trend) OR (short stochastic exit enabled AND Falling stochastic event)).
Exit: long, flip-flop mode selected AND chart close < entry price AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, down, fall EMA net-change parameter, fall EMA rate parameter) AND Bearish alignment AND Signal ALMA < Trend line AND Signal fast regression < Trend line AND Signal slow regression < Trend line AND ((position opened as nascent AND Hemi-transit) OR (position opened as nascent AND Signal ALMA < Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression < Signal slow regression AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Signal ALMA, Signal fast regression, down, fall AL net-change parameter, fall AL rate parameter) AND long accumulation disabled) OR (position opened above Trend line AND position not opened as nascent AND exclusive long mode disabled)).
Exit: short, flip-flop mode selected AND chart close > entry price AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, up, rise EMA net-change parameter, rise EMA rate parameter) AND Bullish alignment AND Signal ALMA > Trend line AND Signal fast regression > Trend line AND Signal slow regression > Trend line AND ((position opened as nascent AND Hemi-transit) OR (position opened as nascent AND Signal ALMA > Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression > Signal slow regression AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Signal ALMA, Signal fast regression, up, rise AL net-change parameter, rise AL rate parameter) AND short accumulation disabled) OR (position opened below Trend line AND position not opened as nascent AND exclusive short mode disabled)).
Exit: long, position bale-out enabled AND chart close > Trend line AND Rising stochastic event AND Signal ALMA > Signal slow regression AND Signal fast regression > Signal slow regression AND Signal slow regression > Trend line AND open position profit > 2 × minimum profit × absolute position size.
Exit: short, position bale-out enabled AND chart close < Trend line AND DirectionalCrossEvent(Chart EMA, Signal ALMA, down, fall EMA net-change parameter, fall EMA rate parameter) AND Bearish alignment AND Both signal lines below trend AND Signal slow regression < Trend line AND open position profit > 2 × minimum profit × absolute position size.
