Calculation: OTE upper and lower bounds on chart bars = from the last completed T1 bar, with range = high − low: for long bias, upper = high − range × OTE start and lower = high − range × OTE end; for short bias, lower = low + range × OTE start and upper = low + range × OTE end. Bias is forced long/short or, in auto mode, long when that bar’s close >= open and short otherwise.
Calculation: T1 equilibrium = (high + low) / 2 of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish FVG confluence = strict-overlap mode AND gap top >= OTE lower AND gap bottom <= OTE upper, OR favored-half mode AND (gap top + gap bottom) / 2 < T1 equilibrium.
Calculation: Bearish FVG confluence = strict-overlap mode AND gap top >= OTE lower AND gap bottom <= OTE upper, OR favored-half mode AND (gap top + gap bottom) / 2 > T1 equilibrium.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, (low > high[2] AND (displacement filter disabled OR close[1] > high[2]) AND high[2] > 0 AND (low − high[2]) / high[2] × 100 >= minimum gap size AND (show-only-confluent disabled OR bullish FVG confluence)) OR (low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom for an active bullish FVG that was confluent when formed and has not previously been tapped; active means no earlier bar since formation filled it under the selected mode: wick low <= gap bottom, close close < gap bottom, or average low <= (gap top + gap bottom) / 2).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, (high < low[2] AND (displacement filter disabled OR close[1] < low[2]) AND high > 0 AND (low[2] − high) / high × 100 >= minimum gap size AND (show-only-confluent disabled OR bearish FVG confluence)) OR (low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom for an active bearish FVG that was confluent when formed and has not previously been tapped; active means no earlier bar since formation filled it under the selected mode: wick high >= gap top, close close > gap top, or average high >= (gap top + gap bottom) / 2).
