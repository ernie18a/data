Calculation: reference high/low = (Highest(high, reference interval), Lowest(low, reference interval)) for each market over the last completed T1 or T2 bar, or the completed T1-length RTH opening range; T1 is shorter than T2. Test T1/T2 levels during the following equal-length interval and opening-range levels through RTH close.
Calculation: relative lag = 100 × abs((instrument running high/low since test-window start ÷ its corresponding reference high/low) − (sister-market running high/low since test-window start ÷ its corresponding reference high/low)), when both reference levels are positive.
Long: On confirmed chart bars during RTH with sister-market data available, for an enabled reference test window, one market's running low is below its reference low and the other's is not, relative lag ≥ minimum-lag parameter, and elapsed time since the first breach > grace-period parameter; OR an unmatched low breach remains at test-window end with relative lag ≥ minimum-lag parameter; OR a previously qualified, still-open high SMT's lagging market first trades above its reference high before test-window end.
Short: On confirmed chart bars during RTH with sister-market data available, for an enabled reference test window, one market's running high is above its reference high and the other's is not, relative lag ≥ minimum-lag parameter, and elapsed time since the first breach > grace-period parameter; OR an unmatched high breach remains at test-window end with relative lag ≥ minimum-lag parameter; OR a previously qualified, still-open low SMT's lagging market first trades below its reference low before test-window end.
