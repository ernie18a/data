Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative(volume × hlc3) / cumulative(volume), reset on each configured T1 boundary change or at the first chart bar at or after the configured anchor timestamp.
Calculation: Anchored population deviation = sqrt(cumulative(volume × (hlc3 − Anchored VWAP)^2) / cumulative(volume)) since the same anchor reset.
Calculation: Fibonacci level(ratio) = Anchored VWAP ± deviation multiplier × Anchored population deviation × selected ratio.
Calculation: Reaction edges(core) = core ± max(minimum tick, ATR(zone lookback on chart bars) × zone ATR factor or tick half-width × minimum tick).
Calculation: T3(x, lookback, factor) = apply three times x → (1 + factor) × EMA(x, lookback) − factor × EMA(EMA(x, lookback), lookback), feeding each result into the next.
Calculation: Cloud MA(source, type, lookback, T3 factor, T) = the selected EMA, SMA, WMA, HMA, RMA, or T3 of source, or EMA of Heikin-Ashi close for HEMA; calculate on T bars and map to chart bars using the last completed T bar when T is higher, the last available intrabar when T is lower, or the current bar when equal.
Calculation: Session VWAP(source, session window, timezone) = volume-weighted average over bars inside the configured session, reset at each session start and retaining its last value outside the session.
Calculation: Trail distance = max(minimum tick, ATR(trail lookback on standard chart bars) × trail ATR factor or tick distance × minimum tick); long stop starts at round-to-tick(entry price − distance) and ratchets on each later confirmed non-exit bar to max(prior stop, standard close − distance); short stop starts at round-to-tick(entry price + distance) and ratchets to min(prior stop, standard close + distance).
Long: Cloud MA(cloud source, fast type, fast lookback, fast T3 factor, T2) > Cloud MA(cloud source, slow type, slow lookback, slow T3 factor, T3).
Short: Cloud MA(cloud source, fast type, fast lookback, fast T3 factor, T2) < Cloud MA(cloud source, slow type, slow lookback, slow T3 factor, T3).
Long: Gann HiLo(high lookback, low lookback, T4) direction is bullish, calculated on T4 bars and mapped to chart bars using the last completed T4 bar when T4 is higher, the last available intrabar when T4 is lower, or the current bar when equal.
Short: Gann HiLo(high lookback, low lookback, T4) direction is bearish, calculated on T4 bars and mapped to chart bars using the last completed T4 bar when T4 is higher, the last available intrabar when T4 is lower, or the current bar when equal.
Long: BREAK signals enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND close > an enabled anchored VWAP center or selected upper/lower Fibonacci level AND previous close <= that same level's prior value AND no anchor reset; Fib levels require positive Anchored population deviation; AND (cloud filter disabled OR fast Cloud MA > slow Cloud MA AND close > both) AND (Gann filter disabled OR Gann HiLo(T4) direction bullish AND close > its level) AND (session filter disabled OR no sessions selected OR configured rule passes: All requires every selected session VWAP available and below close, Any requires at least one available selected session VWAP below close) AND (trailing disabled OR standard close and trail distance ready) AND (trailing disabled OR no trail exit on this bar) AND (trailing disabled OR trail gate disabled OR no active managed trail) AND no simultaneous qualifying short candidate.
Short: BREAK signals enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND close < an enabled anchored VWAP center or selected upper/lower Fibonacci level AND previous close >= that same level's prior value AND no anchor reset; Fib levels require positive Anchored population deviation; AND (cloud filter disabled OR fast Cloud MA < slow Cloud MA AND close < both) AND (Gann filter disabled OR Gann HiLo(T4) direction bearish AND close < its level) AND (session filter disabled OR no sessions selected OR configured rule passes: All requires every selected session VWAP available and above close, Any requires at least one available selected session VWAP above close) AND (trailing disabled OR standard close and trail distance ready) AND (trailing disabled OR no trail exit on this bar) AND (trailing disabled OR trail gate disabled OR no active managed trail) AND no simultaneous qualifying long candidate.
Long: Reaction signals enabled AND a valid upper or lower reaction core and its edges are available AND no anchor reset AND (confirmed chart bar OR reaction confirmation disabled and trailing disabled) AND first selected reaction or more than the configured cooldown bars since the previous selected reaction; the selected event across both cores is, in priority order, a break-and-reclaim, rejection, hold, or acceptance, with the upper core winning equal-priority ties; a long event is either an enabled reclaim on a later bar within its window after close crossed below that core's bottom edge, with close > core and previous close <= prior core; an enabled rejection where low <= top AND high >= bottom AND previous close > prior top AND close > core AND close > open; an enabled hold-up where low <= top AND high >= bottom AND the selected mode is Conservative with previous close > prior top and close >= core, Balanced with previous close > prior top and close >= bottom, or Aggressive with previous close >= prior core and close >= bottom, and hold-down is false; or enabled acceptance after a fresh upward edge break, with consecutive closes above the top edge reaching the configured count including the break bar; AND (trailing disabled OR standard close and trail distance ready) AND (trailing disabled OR no trail exit on this bar) AND (trailing disabled OR trail gate disabled OR no active managed trail) AND no simultaneous qualifying short candidate. Reclaim and acceptance consume that core's armed state; acceptance resets when a close fails to stay beyond its edge; armed state clears at anchor resets or whenever reaction evaluation is disabled, unavailable, stopped, or trail-locked.
Short: Reaction signals enabled AND a valid upper or lower reaction core and its edges are available AND no anchor reset AND (confirmed chart bar OR reaction confirmation disabled and trailing disabled) AND first selected reaction or more than the configured cooldown bars since the previous selected reaction; the selected event across both cores is, in priority order, a break-and-reclaim, rejection, hold, or acceptance, with the upper core winning equal-priority ties; a short event is either an enabled reclaim on a later bar within its window after close crossed above that core's top edge, with close < core and previous close >= prior core; an enabled rejection where low <= top AND high >= bottom AND previous close < prior bottom AND close < core AND close < open; an enabled hold-down where low <= top AND high >= bottom AND the selected mode is Conservative with previous close < prior bottom and close <= core, Balanced with previous close < prior bottom and close <= top, or Aggressive with previous close <= prior core and close <= top, and hold-up is false; or enabled acceptance after a fresh downward edge break, with consecutive closes below the bottom edge reaching the configured count including the break bar; AND (trailing disabled OR standard close and trail distance ready) AND (trailing disabled OR no trail exit on this bar) AND (trailing disabled OR trail gate disabled OR no active managed trail) AND no simultaneous qualifying long candidate. Reclaim and acceptance consume that core's armed state; acceptance resets when a close fails to stay beyond its edge; armed state clears at anchor resets or whenever reaction evaluation is disabled, unavailable, stopped, or trail-locked.
Exit: long, trailing enabled AND an active long trail AND a later confirmed standard chart bar close < the prior long stop.
Exit: short, trailing enabled AND an active short trail AND a later confirmed standard chart bar close > the prior short stop.
