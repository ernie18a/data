Calculation: current-date entry count = number of prior long and short entries since the current date began
Calculation: total entry count = number of prior long and short entries since strategy initialization
Long: On chart bars, close crosses above (low of the T2 bar still forming + long retracement parameter × (high of the T2 bar still forming − low of the T2 bar still forming)) AND Hurst exponent(close, Hurst lookback parameter) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the T1 bar still forming > Hurst threshold parameter AND current-date entry count < current-date entry limit parameter AND total entry count < total entry limit parameter
Short: On chart bars, close crosses below (low of the T2 bar still forming + short retracement parameter × (high of the T2 bar still forming − low of the T2 bar still forming)) AND Hurst exponent(close, Hurst lookback parameter) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the T1 bar still forming < Hurst threshold parameter AND current-date entry count < current-date entry limit parameter AND total entry count < total entry limit parameter
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − risk percentage parameter) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + reward-to-risk parameter × risk percentage parameter)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + risk percentage parameter) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − reward-to-risk parameter × risk percentage parameter)
