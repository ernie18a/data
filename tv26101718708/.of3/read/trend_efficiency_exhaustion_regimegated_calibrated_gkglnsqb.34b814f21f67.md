Calculation: Adaptive fast and slow Efficiency Ratio lookbacks = when adaptive horizons are enabled, bounded round(Ehlers Homodyne Discriminator(hl2, cycle minimum, cycle maximum) × separate fast and slow cycle fractions); otherwise use separate fixed fast and slow lookbacks, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Current leg = up after a confirmed pivot low and down after a confirmed pivot high, using pivots with the configured half-width; current leg displacement = source minus the low anchor for an up leg, or the high anchor minus source for a down leg
Calculation: Leg displacement percentile = fraction of the most recent leg-memory completed positive leg displacements that are less than or equal to current leg displacement, available after the minimum number of completed legs
Calculation: Divergence quality at an up-leg extreme = slope weight × min(1, positive ER-fast drop between successive pivot highs divided by slope scale) + span weight × min(1, bars between those highs divided by leg memory) + volume weight when volume at the newer high is lower than at the prior high; use zero for the slope term unless the newer high exceeds the prior high and ER-fast has dropped
Calculation: Divergence quality at a down-leg extreme = slope weight × min(1, positive ER-fast drop between successive pivot lows divided by slope scale) + span weight × min(1, bars between those lows divided by leg memory) + volume weight when volume at the newer low is lower than at the prior low; use zero for the slope term unless the newer low is lower than the prior low and ER-fast has dropped
Calculation: OU half-life = -ln(2) / ln(1 + regression slope), where regression slope = correlation(change in source, lagged source, OU lookback) × stdev(change in source, OU lookback) / stdev(lagged source, OU lookback); defined only when the slope is negative and 1 plus the slope is positive
Calculation: Internal regime = internal ER/ADX/variance-ratio blend classified as strong trend, trend, neutral, reversion, or chaos
