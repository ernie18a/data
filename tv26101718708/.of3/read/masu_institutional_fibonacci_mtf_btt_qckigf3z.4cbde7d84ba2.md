Calculation: Latest qualifying Fibonacci swing points = retain the latest high equal to the highest high over the Fibonacci swing lookback including the current bar, provided high minus the lowest low over that window is at least the minimum swing ATR multiple times ATR; and retain the latest low equal to the lowest low over that window, provided the highest high minus low is at least the same ATR multiple.
