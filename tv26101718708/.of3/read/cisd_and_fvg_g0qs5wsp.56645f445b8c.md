Calculation: Latest confirmed swing high = last non-na pivot high(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback)
Calculation: Latest confirmed swing low = last non-na pivot low(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback)
Calculation: Bullish structure break = latest confirmed swing high exists AND close > latest confirmed swing high AND close[1] <= latest confirmed swing high
Calculation: Bearish structure break = latest confirmed swing low exists AND close < latest confirmed swing low AND close[1] >= latest confirmed swing low
Calculation: Structure direction = neutral initially; on each chart bar, retain the prior direction unless the BOS/CHOCH toggle is enabled and no prior accepted label exists or the current bar index minus the prior accepted label bar exceeds the label-spacing parameter; when that gate passes, assign bullish for a bullish structure break, then bearish for a bearish structure break, with bearish taking precedence if both occur
Calculation: Strong body = abs(close - open) > (high - low) * body-strength threshold
Calculation: Bullish reversal(T) = on chart bars, request.security(T, close) > request.security(T, open) AND request.security(T, close)[1] < request.security(T, open)[1]; use the current requested bar as sampled on chart bars, which can still be forming in real time, and [1] as the prior chart bar's returned value
Calculation: Bearish reversal(T) = on chart bars, request.security(T, close) < request.security(T, open) AND request.security(T, close)[1] > request.security(T, open)[1]; use the current requested bar as sampled on chart bars, which can still be forming in real time, and [1] as the prior chart bar's returned value
Calculation: Latest bullish IFVG top = the formation-bar high[2] of the most recently mitigated bullish FVG, formed with low > high[2] and low - high[2] > minimum gap size while the FVG toggle is enabled, and later mitigated when low <= its formation-bar high[2]
Calculation: Latest bearish IFVG bottom = the formation-bar low[2] of the most recently mitigated bearish FVG, formed with high < low[2] and low[2] - high > minimum gap size while the FVG toggle is enabled, and later mitigated when high >= its formation-bar low[2]
Long: chart timeframe equals T1, T2, or T3, ordered shortest to longest, AND low < lowest(low, sweep lookback) AND bullish structure break AND structure direction after current-bar updates is bearish AND a latest bullish IFVG exists AND low <= its top AND close > open AND strong body AND (bullish reversal(T1) OR bullish reversal(T2) OR bullish reversal(T3))
Short: chart timeframe equals T1, T2, or T3, ordered shortest to longest, AND high > highest(high, sweep lookback) AND bearish structure break AND structure direction after current-bar updates is bullish AND a latest bearish IFVG exists AND high >= its bottom AND close < open AND strong body AND (bearish reversal(T1) OR bearish reversal(T2) OR bearish reversal(T3))
