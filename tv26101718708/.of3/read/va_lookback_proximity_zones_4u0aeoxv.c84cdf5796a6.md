Calculation: LookbackWindow = the chart bars from N bars back through the current bar, where N = bars back parameter in Bars Back mode and N = lookback span / chart bar duration in Auto Lookback mode; in Fixed Range mode, the chart bars whose time is between start time and end time inclusive; in Anchor mode, the chart bars whose time is at or after start time, through the current bar
Calculation: ValueArea = (high, low) of a market profile over LookbackWindow, where each chart bar contributes the high and low of the last completed T1 bar; the profile range runs from the lowest low to the highest high of the contributions and is split into equal levels, the number of levels being the tpo levels parameter; a level's count is the number of contributions whose high-low range overlaps the level inclusively; starting from the level with the highest count (lowest level on ties), repeatedly add the adjacent level above if its count is at least the count of the level below, otherwise the level below, using only the remaining side when one is exhausted, until the summed count of included levels reaches value area percent of the total count; high = upper edge of the highest included level, low = lower edge of the lowest included level
Long: close >= ValueArea low AND (close - ValueArea low) / (ValueArea high - ValueArea low) * 100 <= long max percent
Short: close <= ValueArea high AND (close - ValueArea low) / (ValueArea high - ValueArea low) * 100 >= short min percent
