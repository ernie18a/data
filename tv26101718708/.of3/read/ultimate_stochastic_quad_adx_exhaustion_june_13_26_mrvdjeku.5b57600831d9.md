Calculation: Stochastic thresholds = lower and upper thresholds selected together by a threshold-mode parameter
Calculation: Main stochastic = one of four Stochastic %D(close, high, low, separately parameterized %K lookback, corresponding separately parameterized %D smoothing) calculations selected by a main-source parameter
Calculation: Continuation stochastic = one of the same four Stochastic %D calculations selected by an independent continuation-source parameter
Calculation: ADX = ADX(ADX length, same ADX length)
Calculation: ATR = ATR(ATR lookback)
Long: main stochastic crosses above the stochastic-midline parameter after visiting the lower stochastic threshold AND (ADX-exhaustion filter disabled OR (ADX exceeded its threshold while the main stochastic was at or below the lower threshold or at or above the upper threshold AND ADX < ADX[1])) AND (candle-color filter disabled OR close > open) AND (price-pivot filter disabled OR low > lowest(low, price-pivot lookback)[1]) AND (volume-climax filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (minimum-volatility filter disabled OR ATR > SMA(ATR, ATR-baseline lookback) * ATR multiplier)
Short: main stochastic crosses below the stochastic-midline parameter after visiting the upper stochastic threshold AND (ADX-exhaustion filter disabled OR (ADX exceeded its threshold while the main stochastic was at or below the lower threshold or at or above the upper threshold AND ADX < ADX[1])) AND (candle-color filter disabled OR close < open) AND (price-pivot filter disabled OR high < highest(high, price-pivot lookback)[1]) AND (volume-climax filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (minimum-volatility filter disabled OR ATR > SMA(ATR, ATR-baseline lookback) * ATR multiplier)
Long: continuation stochastic crosses above the stochastic-midline parameter while the most recent master signal is long
Short: continuation stochastic crosses below the stochastic-midline parameter while the most recent master signal is short
