Calculation: VPOC = selected source of the completed lower-timeframe T1 bar with the greatest volume among T1 bars within the current chart bar; use the first bar in a tie
Calculation: TPOC = midpoint of the equal-width lot spanning the current chart bar's low-to-high range that contains the greatest number of completed T1 selected-source values within that chart bar; use the first lot in a tie
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, (((close > low[1] AND VPOC < low[1]) OR (close < low[1] AND VPOC < close AND abs(close - VPOC) >= half the chart-bar range)) AND VPOC < chart-bar midpoint) OR (((close > low[1] AND TPOC < low[1]) OR (close < low[1] AND TPOC < close AND abs(close - TPOC) >= half the chart-bar range)) AND TPOC < chart-bar midpoint) OR (close > high[1] AND TPOC < chart-bar midpoint AND VPOC > chart-bar midpoint AND current chart-bar range > range-expansion parameter times previous chart-bar range AND abs(TPOC - VPOC) >= half the chart-bar range)
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, (((close < high[1] AND VPOC > high[1]) OR (close > high[1] AND VPOC > close AND abs(close - VPOC) >= half the chart-bar range)) AND VPOC > chart-bar midpoint) OR (((close < high[1] AND TPOC > high[1]) OR (close > high[1] AND TPOC > close AND abs(close - TPOC) >= half the chart-bar range)) AND TPOC > chart-bar midpoint) OR (close < low[1] AND VPOC < chart-bar midpoint AND TPOC > chart-bar midpoint AND current chart-bar range > range-expansion parameter times previous chart-bar range AND abs(TPOC - VPOC) >= half the chart-bar range)
