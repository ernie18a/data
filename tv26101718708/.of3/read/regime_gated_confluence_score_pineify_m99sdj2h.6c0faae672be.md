Calculation: Trend factor = clip(spread ratio * spread blend weight + normalized fast-EMA slope * slope blend weight, shared factor bounds), where spread ratio = (EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) - EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback), and normalized fast-EMA slope = (EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) - EMA(close, fast EMA lookback)[slope lookback]) / (ATR(ATR lookback) * sqrt(slope lookback)).
Calculation: Momentum factor = clip((RSI(close, momentum lookback) - RSI center) / RSI scale, shared factor bounds).
Calculation: Volume factor = clip(EMA(close-location * clipped relative volume / relative-volume cap, volume smoothing lookback), shared factor bounds), where close-location = clip((2 * close - high - low) / (high - low), shared factor bounds) when bar range exceeds the minimum price increment and zero otherwise; relative volume = clip(volume / SMA(valid volume or zero, volume baseline lookback), zero, relative-volume cap); valid volume is reported volume greater than zero; volume is available when the baseline is positive and the SMA of valid-volume indicators over the volume baseline lookback meets the volume-coverage threshold; use zero volume factor when unavailable.
Calculation: Regime = VOLATILE when ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), regime evidence lookback) meets the shock entry ratio, or when the prior regime was VOLATILE and this ratio meets the shock hold ratio; otherwise TREND when absolute spread ratio meets the trend entry strength and Kaufman Efficiency Ratio(close, regime evidence lookback) meets the trend entry efficiency, or when the prior regime was TREND and both measures meet their respective hold thresholds; otherwise RANGE when both measures are below their respective hold thresholds; otherwise TRANSITION. Start in TRANSITION and set the regime to TRANSITION when score readiness fails.
Calculation: Raw score = the sum of the normalized weighted trend, momentum, and available volume contributions, where each contribution is its factor times its separate regime-specific weight divided by the sum of available weights; omit the volume term and weight when volume is unavailable, and negate the momentum factor only in RANGE. Use separate trend, momentum, and volume weights for each regime.
Calculation: Agreement = clip(abs(raw score) / the sum of the absolute normalized weighted contributions, agreement bounds), or zero when that sum is at or below the agreement epsilon.
Calculation: Score = score scale * raw score * (agreement floor + agreement span * agreement) * volatility gate, where volatility gate is clip(shock entry ratio / (ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), regime evidence lookback)), volatility gate bounds) in VOLATILE and one otherwise. Score is unavailable unless ATR and its baseline exceed the minimum price increment, trend and momentum factors, Kaufman Efficiency Ratio, and volatility ratio are valid, slow EMA lookback exceeds fast EMA lookback, trend hold thresholds do not exceed their corresponding entry thresholds, and shock hold ratio does not exceed shock entry ratio.
Long: score crosses above the direction threshold on a confirmed bar.
Short: score crosses below the negative direction threshold on a confirmed bar.
