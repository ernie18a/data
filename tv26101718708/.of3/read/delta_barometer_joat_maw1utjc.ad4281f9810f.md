Calculation: Signed volume = volume when close >= open, otherwise -volume
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of signed volume from the first available chart bar
Calculation: ATR regime ratio = ATR(base ATR length) / SMA(ATR(base ATR length), regime lookback)
Calculation: Selected ATR regime = low when ATR regime ratio < low threshold, otherwise high when ATR regime ratio > high threshold, otherwise medium; low, high, and medium arming tests are respectively ATR regime ratio < low threshold, ATR regime ratio > high threshold, and neither
Calculation: Relative volume component = clamp(max(volume / EMA(volume, volume-average lookback) - 1, 0) / volume-rate scale, 0, component cap) when the EMA is positive, otherwise 0
Calculation: Trend-strength score = score scale × (TSS RSI weight × clamp(abs(RSI(close, RSI period) - RSI(close, RSI period)[TSS slope lookback]) / RSI-slope scale, 0, component cap) + TSS volume weight × relative volume component + TSS ATR weight × clamp(max(ATR(base ATR length) / ATR(shorter TSS ATR lookback) - 1, 0) / ATR-expansion scale, 0, component cap)); the TSS ATR lookback is shorter than the base ATR length, and the ATR component is 0 when the shorter ATR is not positive
Calculation: Pressure score = pressure score scale × (pressure delta weight × abs(signed volume / volume) when volume is positive, otherwise 0, plus pressure volume weight × clamp(max(volume / EMA(volume, pressure volume-average lookback) - 1, 0) / pressure volume-rate scale, 0, component cap) when that EMA is positive, otherwise 0, plus pressure body weight × clamp(abs(close - open) / (high - low), 0, component cap) when high > low, otherwise 0)
Calculation: ATR trail = band ATR(base ATR length) × the low, medium, or high ATR multiplier selected by the ATR regime; initialize direction up and trail at close - band; while the prior direction is up, trail is max(close - band, prior trail) and direction flips down with trail set to close + band when close < trail; while the prior direction is down, trail is min(close + band, prior trail) and direction flips up with trail set to close - band when close > trail
Calculation: Bullish ATR-engine signal = Engine A enabled AND Engine A arming setting admits the current regime AND ATR trail direction flips from down to up AND trend-strength score >= minimum TSS score
Calculation: Bearish ATR-engine signal = Engine A enabled AND Engine A arming setting admits the current regime AND ATR trail direction flips from up to down AND trend-strength score >= minimum TSS score
Calculation: Bullish delta-engine signal = Engine B enabled AND Engine B arming setting admits the current regime AND cumulative delta crosses above SMA(cumulative delta, delta moving-average lookback) AND pressure score >= minimum pressure score AND RSI(close, RSI period) < RSI overbought gate
Calculation: Bearish delta-engine signal = Engine B enabled AND Engine B arming setting admits the current regime AND cumulative delta crosses below SMA(cumulative delta, delta moving-average lookback) AND pressure score >= minimum pressure score AND RSI(close, RSI period) > RSI oversold gate
Calculation: Hybrid bullish condition = the hybrid mode requires both bullish engine signals; or prioritizes Engine A and accepts a bullish ATR-engine signal or a bullish delta-engine signal when the ATR engine is not bearish; or prioritizes Engine B and accepts a bullish delta-engine signal or a bullish ATR-engine signal when the delta engine is not bearish; or accepts either bullish engine signal
Calculation: Hybrid bearish condition = the hybrid mode requires both bearish engine signals; or prioritizes Engine A and accepts a bearish ATR-engine signal or a bearish delta-engine signal when the ATR engine is not bullish; or prioritizes Engine B and accepts a bearish delta-engine signal or a bearish ATR-engine signal when the delta engine is not bullish; or accepts either bearish engine signal
Long: hybrid bullish condition AND current bar is confirmed AND no prior confirmed signal exists or at least the cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the prior confirmed signal
Short: hybrid bearish condition AND current bar is confirmed AND no prior confirmed signal exists or at least the cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the prior confirmed signal
Long: ATR trail direction is up
Short: ATR trail direction is down
Long: cumulative delta > SMA(cumulative delta, delta moving-average lookback)
Short: cumulative delta <= SMA(cumulative delta, delta moving-average lookback)
Long: trend-strength score > bullish color threshold OR pressure score > bullish color threshold
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + ATR(base ATR length) at entry × target ATR multiple OR low <= min(ATR trail at entry, entry price - ATR(base ATR length) at entry × minimum stop ATR distance)
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - ATR(base ATR length) at entry × target ATR multiple OR high >= max(ATR trail at entry, entry price + ATR(base ATR length) at entry × minimum stop ATR distance)
