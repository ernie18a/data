Calculation: Anchored VWAP = sum(volume * hlc3) / sum(volume) over chart bars since each T1 boundary; use hlc3 if summed volume is zero
Calculation: Anchored VWAP deviation = sqrt(variance) when variance is positive, otherwise 0, where variance = sum(volume * hlc3^2) / sum(volume) - Anchored VWAP^2 over chart bars since each T1 boundary
Calculation: T2 VWAP = sum(volume * hlc3) / sum(volume) over chart bars since each T2 boundary; use hlc3 if summed volume is zero
Calculation: Smoothed CCI = EMA(CCI(hl2, CCI lookback), CCI smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Chikou touch = barssince(current close lies within the high-low range shifted back by the shift parameter and expanded by tick tolerance times minimum tick; when segment mode is enabled, use the segment from close[1] to close instead of the current close) <= touch lookback; compute on T1 bars using the still-forming T1 bar and use that result on chart bars when lower-timeframe mode is enabled and T1 is shorter than the chart timeframe, otherwise compute on chart bars
Long: Smoothed CCI crosses above -CCI crossback level AND ((wide-zone mode is enabled AND close < Anchored VWAP - inner-band multiplier * Anchored VWAP deviation) OR (wide-zone mode is disabled AND low >= Anchored VWAP - (outer-band multiplier + zone buffer) * Anchored VWAP deviation AND low <= Anchored VWAP - (signal-band multiplier - zone buffer) * Anchored VWAP deviation)) AND (weekly VWAP bias filter is disabled OR close > T2 VWAP) AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (reversal-candle filter is disabled OR close > open) AND (CCI extreme-visit filter is disabled OR lowest(Smoothed CCI, extreme lookback) < -CCI extreme level) AND (Chikou filter is disabled OR Chikou touch)
Short: Smoothed CCI crosses below CCI crossback level AND ((wide-zone mode is enabled AND close > Anchored VWAP + inner-band multiplier * Anchored VWAP deviation) OR (wide-zone mode is disabled AND high >= Anchored VWAP + (signal-band multiplier - zone buffer) * Anchored VWAP deviation AND high <= Anchored VWAP + (outer-band multiplier + zone buffer) * Anchored VWAP deviation)) AND (weekly VWAP bias filter is disabled OR close < T2 VWAP) AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (reversal-candle filter is disabled OR close < open) AND (CCI extreme-visit filter is disabled OR highest(Smoothed CCI, extreme lookback) > CCI extreme level) AND (Chikou filter is disabled OR Chikou touch)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop-loss multiplier
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop-loss multiplier
