Calculation: Previous T1 high/low = high/low of the last completed T1 bar; previous T1 midpoint = their average; T1 open = open of the current forming T1 bar.
Calculation: T2 close, high, low, and EMA = close, high, low, and EMA(close, EMA lookback, T2, forming bar), sampled on chart bars from the current forming T2 bar; T2 midpoint = (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish T2 bias = T2 close > T2 midpoint AND T2 close > T2 EMA; bearish T2 bias = T2 close < T2 midpoint AND T2 close < T2 EMA; otherwise T2 bias is neutral.
Calculation: ATR = ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback). Sweep buffer = ATR * sweep-buffer multiplier.
Calculation: Daily range = high - low of the current forming T1 bar, sampled on chart bars; ADR = SMA(daily range, ADR lookback); ADR high/low = T1 open +/- ADR; near ADR high/low = close >= ADR high - ATR * ADR proximity multiplier / close <= ADR low + ATR * ADR proximity multiplier.
Calculation: Strong volume = volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * strong-volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND high - low > ATR * displacement multiplier AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) > displacement body-fraction threshold AND (close - low) / (high - low) > bullish close-strength threshold; bearish displacement = close < open AND high - low > ATR * displacement multiplier AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) > displacement body-fraction threshold AND (high - close) / (high - low) > bearish close-strength threshold.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = lower-wick fraction > wick threshold AND body fraction < absorption body-fraction threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * strong-volume multiplier AND high - low < SMA(high - low, aggression lookback) * small-range multiplier AND close > low + (high - low) * absorption close-location threshold; bearish absorption is the corresponding upper-wick condition with close < high - (high - low) * absorption close-location threshold.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = (high > previous T1 high + sweep buffer AND close < previous T1 high) OR (high > locked Asia-session high + sweep buffer AND close < locked Asia-session high); bearish sweep = (low < previous T1 low - sweep buffer AND close > previous T1 low) OR (low < locked Asia-session low - sweep buffer AND close > locked Asia-session low).
Calculation: Kill zone = UTC hour is within the configured duration after the London, New York, or Asia open hour.
Calculation: Bullish market-structure shift = a latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing length, swing length) exists and close > it; bearish market-structure shift = a latest confirmed PivotLow(low, swing length, swing length) exists and close < it.
Calculation: Bullish aggression = rounded and clamped SMA of bullish aggression points over the aggression smoothing lookback; award the configured bullish-candle, above-average-body, above-average-range, strong bullish close, bullish-displacement, and strong-volume weights when each condition holds. Bearish aggression uses the corresponding bearish conditions and weights. The body and range averages are SMA(body, aggression lookback) and SMA(high - low, aggression lookback).
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion = rounded and clamped SMA over the exhaustion smoothing lookback of points for rising(close, run lookback), upper-wick fraction > wick threshold, close below the bar midpoint, falling volume ratio for the configured comparison bars while close is rising, and close > previous T1 high. Bearish exhaustion uses falling(close, run lookback), lower-wick fraction > wick threshold, close above the bar midpoint, falling volume ratio while close is falling, and close < previous T1 low. Each condition contributes its configured weight.
Calculation: Bullish reversal score = rounded and clamped sum of configured weights for bullish sweep, lower-wick fraction > wick threshold, close > open, bearish exhaustion > reversal-exhaustion threshold, T2 bias not bearish, bullish market-structure shift, and bullish absorption. Bearish reversal score uses bearish sweep, upper-wick fraction, close < open, bullish exhaustion, T2 bias not bullish, bearish market-structure shift, and bearish absorption.
Calculation: Bullish continuation score = rounded and clamped sum of configured weights for bullish aggression > continuation-aggression threshold, close > previous T1 midpoint, strong volume, bullish T2 bias, no bearish sweep, close > previous T1 high and previous T1 midpoint, not near ADR high, and bullish displacement. Bearish continuation score uses the corresponding bearish conditions and low-side levels.
Calculation: Bullish context score = bullish aggression * aggression weight + bullish reversal score * reversal weight + bullish continuation score * continuation weight; bearish context score uses the corresponding bearish values and weights.
Calculation: Bullish setup score = sum of configured weights for bullish sweep, kill zone, strong volume, bullish aggression > bearish aggression, bearish exhaustion > setup-exhaustion threshold, close < previous T1 midpoint, bullish market-structure shift, bullish displacement, bullish absorption, compression, not near ADR high, and liquidity above. Compression = highest(high, compression lookback) - lowest(low, compression lookback) < SMA(high - low, compression lookback) * compression lookback * compression multiplier. Liquidity above = abs(high - high[1]) <= ATR * equal-level tolerance multiplier OR high[1] >= previous T1 high - ATR * equal-level tolerance multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish setup score = sum of configured weights for bearish sweep, kill zone, strong volume, bearish aggression > bullish aggression, bullish exhaustion > setup-exhaustion threshold, close > previous T1 midpoint, bearish market-structure shift, bearish displacement, bearish absorption, compression, not near ADR low, and liquidity below. Liquidity below = abs(low - low[1]) <= ATR * equal-level tolerance multiplier OR low[1] <= previous T1 low + ATR * equal-level tolerance multiplier.
Long: bullish sweep is labeled when high > previous T1 low - sweep buffer and close > previous T1 low OR low < locked Asia-session low - sweep buffer and close > locked Asia-session low.
Short: bearish sweep is labeled when high > previous T1 high + sweep buffer and close < previous T1 high OR high > locked Asia-session high + sweep buffer and close < locked Asia-session high.
Long: bullish market-structure shift.
Short: bearish market-structure shift.
Long: bullish T2 bias.
Short: bearish T2 bias.
Long: close > previous T1 high AND close > previous T1 midpoint.
Short: close < previous T1 low AND close < previous T1 midpoint.
Long: bullish context score > bearish context score + context margin AND neither bullish nor bearish entry is active.
Short: bearish context score > bullish context score + context margin AND neither bullish nor bearish entry is active.
Long: an eligible bullish sweep is followed within the confirmation window by close > previous T1 low AND close > open, then by (bullish displacement OR bullish absorption) AND bullish close strength > confirmation threshold; if the A-only filter is enabled, bullish setup score >= A-grade cutoff.
Short: an eligible bearish sweep is followed within the confirmation window by close < previous T1 high AND close < open, then by (bearish displacement OR bearish absorption) AND bearish close strength > confirmation threshold; if the A-only filter is enabled, bearish setup score >= A-grade cutoff.
