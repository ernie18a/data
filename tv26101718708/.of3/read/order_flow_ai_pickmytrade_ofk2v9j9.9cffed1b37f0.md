Calculation: On each confirmed chart bar, sum volume from completed T1 candles into buying when close > open, selling when close < open, otherwise by comparing close with the previous T1 close; if equal, inherit the latest resolved direction, or classify as neutral if none exists. T1 is strictly lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Usable volume requires a standard time-based chart, T1 below the chart timeframe, at least one T1 candle, no more than the intrabar cap, valid HLC3 and nonnegative volume for every T1 candle, and positive total classified volume; otherwise the bar is unusable.
Calculation: Net pressure share = (buying volume − selling volume) / (buying volume + selling volume + neutral volume).
Calculation: Relative activity = current classified volume / the mean classified volume of the preceding L_activity usable chart bars; require a complete valid baseline.
Calculation: Prior ATR = ATR(L_ATR) on the chart timeframe, taken from the preceding completed chart bar. Pressure response = sign(net pressure share) × (close − open) / prior ATR.
Calculation: Flow mean = sum of T1 HLC3 × volume across the L_flow latest chart bars / their summed volume. Rolling pressure = (summed buying volume − summed selling volume) / summed classified volume across those same bars. Both require at least the configured valid-window fraction of usable bars and positive summed volume; calculations use completed T1 candles within confirmed chart bars.
Calculation: Event feature vector, frozen at event close when high > low and the flow mean and rolling pressure are ready: absolute net pressure share; relative activity normalized as activity/(activity + activity scale); clipped and normalized pressure response; pressure-aligned wick fraction; pressure-aligned close location; clipped and normalized pressure-side distance from flow mean in prior ATR units; and normalized pressure-aligned rolling pressure. For buying, aligned wick is the upper wick and close location is (close − low)/(high − low); for selling, aligned wick is the lower wick and close location is (high − close)/(high − low).
Calculation: A training case is continuation if, from the chart bar after its event, the pressure-side barrier at event close ± barrier distance × frozen prior ATR is touched before the opposite barrier within the outcome deadline. A same-bar double touch, invalid outcome prices, or no touch by the deadline is discarded. Train only on resolved cases, retaining the latest library limit; score an event only from cases resolved before that event.
Calculation: Mean log-distance = mean of log(1 + absolute feature difference) across the event feature vector. The classifier uses the exact K nearest resolved cases, with newer cases winning distance ties; score only if the library has at least max(warm-up count, K) cases, both outcomes, and every selected case is within the distance ceiling. Weight each neighbor by 1/max(distance, distance floor); effective count = (sum of weights)^2 / sum of squared weights; neighbor vote = weighted continuation fraction; smoothed base rate = (continuations + smoothing amount)/(library size + 2 × smoothing amount); continuation vote = base rate + (neighbor vote − base rate) × effective count/(effective count + shrinkage). Freeze the vote at event close; no valid score is available when these requirements fail.
Calculation: Initial risk R = round up to tick(max(minimum risk ticks, prior ATR × stop scale)), frozen at the signal close. The reference entry is the next chart bar open. Long target = round up to tick(entry + R × target multiple); short target = round down to tick(entry − R × target multiple).
Calculation: Long active stop starts at entry − R and can only rise; short active stop starts at entry + R and can only fall. Once favorable excursion reaches the break-even activation in R units, the break-even reference is entry plus the configured buffer for long positions (rounded down to tick), or entry minus it for short positions (rounded up). Once favorable excursion reaches the trail activation, the trailing reference is the favorable high minus the configured trail distance × R for longs (rounded down), or the favorable low plus that distance for shorts (rounded up). Enabled references tighten the stop after checking current-bar boundaries and become active on the next bar.

Space all four event types by at least the shared event spacing.
Short: At a spaced pressure event, net pressure share < 0 and (pressure response ≥ DRIVE threshold or pressure response ≤ resisted ceiling), with usable volume, relative activity ≥ raw activity threshold, absolute net pressure share ≥ raw imbalance threshold, and prior ATR > 0. Space all four event types by at least the shared event spacing.
Long: At a spaced DRIVE or RESISTED event with a valid classifier vote, either buying pressure has continuation vote ≥ vote threshold or selling pressure has continuation vote ≤ 1 − vote threshold.
Short: At a spaced DRIVE or RESISTED event with a valid classifier vote, either selling pressure has continuation vote ≥ vote threshold or buying pressure has continuation vote ≤ 1 − vote threshold.

Short: With managed plans enabled, a spaced selling DRIVE meets both raw and entry imbalance and activity thresholds, flow mean and rolling pressure are ready, rolling pressure ≤ −entry pressure threshold, close < flow mean, flow mean < its value L_slope chart bars ago, absolute(close − flow mean)/prior ATR ≤ maximum mean distance, and the optional entry classifier gate is off or enabled with this event's valid continuation vote ≥ vote threshold; additionally either (a) this side is enabled, chart OHLC is valid, datetime is at or after the plan start, the plan is flat with no pending entry and no exit this bar, the cooldown is complete, and the side is armed, initially or after the configured consecutive usable flat bars without a pending entry or same-side unspaced raw DRIVE; or (b) a long plan is held, chart OHLC is valid, and opposite-DRIVE exit is enabled.
Exit: long, valid chart OHLC and (low ≤ active long stop from the start of the bar or high ≥ long target, or flow exit is enabled and at least the configured consecutive usable closes have close < flow mean and rolling pressure ≤ −entry pressure threshold, or the inclusive holding-bar count from the next-open fill reaches the maximum hold). Also exit a pending long plan if the next chart bar has invalid OHLC.
Exit: short, valid chart OHLC and (high ≥ active short stop from the start of the bar or low ≤ short target, or flow exit is enabled and at least the configured consecutive usable closes have close > flow mean and rolling pressure ≥ entry pressure threshold, or the inclusive holding-bar count from the next-open fill reaches the maximum hold). Also exit a pending short plan if the next chart bar has invalid OHLC.
