Calculation: Tolerance = tolerance multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR length) if tolerance mode is ATR, else tolerance percent * close / 100
Calculation: EqhLevels = levels of live equal-high zones, where a zone's level is the highest price among its pivot highs; first, each bar, waiting pivot highs are dropped when high exceeds their price by more than Tolerance or their pivot bar is more than max gap + swing length bars earlier, and waiting pivot highs beyond the newest waiting cap are dropped; then each confirmed pivot high p = PivotHigh(high, swing length, swing length), confirmed on the bar swing length after its pivot bar, is handled as follows: if a live zone is within Tolerance of p, p joins the nearest such zone when its pivot bar is at least min gap bars after that zone's last pivot bar, and is dropped otherwise; if no live zone is within Tolerance of p, p forms a zone with all waiting pivot highs within Tolerance of p whose pivot bars are between min gap and max gap bars earlier, when any exist; otherwise p waits; a zone is live from the bar after its latest pivot is confirmed until the first bar whose high exceeds its level, or until max zone age bars after its last pivot bar (if max zone age > 0), or until it is removed as the zone farthest from close when live zones exceed max zones per side
Calculation: EqlLevels = EqhLevels mirrored: low for high, PivotLow for PivotHigh, lowest for highest, falls below for exceeds
Short: high > L AND close <= L, for some L in EqhLevels
Long: low < L AND close >= L, for some L in EqlLevels
Long: close > L, for some L in EqhLevels
Short: close < L, for some L in EqlLevels
