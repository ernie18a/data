Long: SuperTrend(chart factor, chart ATR lookback) changes from bearish to bullish on chart bars AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback) >= ADX minimum threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR SuperTrend(T1 factor, T1 ATR lookback, on T1 bars) is bullish on the current T1 bar, which may still be forming, sampled on chart bars with lookahead off)
Short: SuperTrend(chart factor, chart ATR lookback) changes from bullish to bearish on chart bars AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback) >= ADX minimum threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR SuperTrend(T1 factor, T1 ATR lookback, on T1 bars) is bearish on the current T1 bar, which may still be forming, sampled on chart bars with lookahead off)
Exit: long, (stop-loss enabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100)) OR (take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage / 100))
Exit: short, (stop-loss enabled AND high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100)) OR (take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage / 100))
