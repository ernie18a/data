Calculation: Trailing stop = on long entry, low - ATR(lookback) × initial-stop multiplier; while long, max(previous trailing stop, low - ATR(lookback) × trailing multiplier); on short entry, high + ATR(lookback) × initial-stop multiplier; while short, min(previous trailing stop, high + ATR(lookback) × trailing multiplier); reset while flat
Long: confirmed chart bar AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) > threshold AND close crosses above the upper Keltner Channel(close, lookback, multiplier) AND flat AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured cash-session window) AND daily loss halt inactive AND (consecutive-loss breaker disabled OR consecutive losing trades < maximum consecutive-loss parameter)
Short: confirmed chart bar AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) > threshold AND close crosses below the lower Keltner Channel(close, lookback, multiplier) AND flat AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured cash-session window) AND daily loss halt inactive AND (consecutive-loss breaker disabled OR consecutive losing trades < maximum consecutive-loss parameter)
Exit: Long, confirmed chart bar AND trailing stop exists AND low <= trailing stop OR session filter enabled AND bar time is within the configured end-of-day window OR daily loss halt active OR consecutive-loss breaker enabled AND consecutive losing trades >= maximum consecutive-loss parameter
Exit: Short, confirmed chart bar AND trailing stop exists AND high >= trailing stop OR session filter enabled AND bar time is within the configured end-of-day window OR daily loss halt active OR consecutive-loss breaker enabled AND consecutive losing trades >= maximum consecutive-loss parameter
