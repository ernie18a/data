Calculation: Normalized breadth = (advances − declines) / (advances + declines + unchanged) from synchronized T1 observations on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar; use zero for missing unchanged values and no value when advances or declines are missing, timestamps differ, or the total is not positive.
Calculation: Cumulative breadth = cumulative sum of normalized breadth from the anchor-year parameter, reset on entering that year and incremented on chart bars with a valid T1 observation.
Calculation: Confirmed breadth regime = when breadth data is fresh and valid and the number of observations since the anchor year exceeds the trend EMA lookback, classify cumulative breadth minus EMA(cumulative breadth, trend EMA lookback) as impulse when it is at or above zero and cumulative breadth is above its previous rising-lookback observations, fading when it is at or above zero otherwise, and weak when below zero; change the regime only after the same classification holds for the regime confirmation count of consecutive valid observations on confirmed chart bars.
Calculation: Bearish breadth divergence = on a confirmed chart bar with fresh, regime-ready breadth, a newly confirmed chart-bar pivot high (left-pivot lookback, right-pivot confirmation lookback) is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high and cumulative breadth at the current pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot, while the confirmed breadth regime is not impulse.
Long: Confirmed breadth regime is impulse.
Long: Thrust signals are enabled AND the chart bar is confirmed AND a valid T1 breadth observation is fresh AND the number of observations since the anchor year exceeds the breadth baseline lookback AND cumulative breadth minus EMA(cumulative breadth, trend EMA lookback) is at or above zero AND cumulative breadth is above its previous rising-lookback observations AND Z-score(SMA(normalized breadth, impulse smoothing lookback), breadth baseline lookback) exceeds the impulse Z threshold AND close is above EMA(close, price filter lookback) AND there is no prior kickoff or more than the kickoff cooldown in chart bars has elapsed since the prior kickoff.
Short: Confirmed breadth regime is weak.
Short: Bearish breadth divergence.
Short: The chart bar is confirmed AND a valid T1 breadth observation is fresh AND the number of observations since the anchor year exceeds the breadth baseline lookback AND confirmed breadth regime is weak AND Z-score(cumulative breadth − EMA(cumulative breadth, trend EMA lookback), breadth baseline lookback) is below the negative trend Z threshold AND bearish breadth divergence occurred within the recent-divergence lookback in chart bars AND this condition was false on the previous chart bar.
