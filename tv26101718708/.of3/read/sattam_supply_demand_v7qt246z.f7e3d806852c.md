Calculation: displacement factor(sensitivity) = max(minimum factor, base factor - sensitivity * factor step)
Calculation: supply trigger(pivot length, displacement window, sensitivity, ATR length) = computed on T1 bars, chart bars take the value of the last completed T1 bar; true at bar j if the most recent PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length) with pivot candle at bar k, confirmed at bar k + pivot length, satisfies 0 <= j - (k + pivot length) <= displacement window AND close(j) < low(k) - ATR(ATR length, k) * displacement factor(sensitivity) AND no bar i from k + pivot length to j - 1 has close(i) < low(k) - ATR(ATR length, k) * displacement factor(sensitivity)
Calculation: demand trigger(pivot length, displacement window, sensitivity, ATR length) = computed on T1 bars, chart bars take the value of the last completed T1 bar; true at bar j if the most recent PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length) with pivot candle at bar k, confirmed at bar k + pivot length, satisfies 0 <= j - (k + pivot length) <= displacement window AND close(j) > high(k) + ATR(ATR length, k) * displacement factor(sensitivity) AND no bar i from k + pivot length to j - 1 has close(i) > high(k) + ATR(ATR length, k) * displacement factor(sensitivity)
Long: new T1 bar opens AND demand trigger(pivot length, displacement window, sensitivity, ATR length)[1] = true
Short: new T1 bar opens AND supply trigger(pivot length, displacement window, sensitivity, ATR length)[1] = true
