Calculation: Long stop price = if stop-loss method is ATR, entry price - ATR(ATR lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) * stop-loss multiplier; otherwise, lowest(low, swing lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) if below entry price, else entry price - ATR(ATR lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) * fallback ATR multiplier
Calculation: Short stop price = if stop-loss method is ATR, entry price + ATR(ATR lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) * stop-loss multiplier; otherwise, highest(high, swing lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) if above entry price, else entry price + ATR(ATR lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) * fallback ATR multiplier
Calculation: Long take-profit price = if take-profit method is ATR, entry price + ATR(ATR lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) * take-profit multiplier; otherwise, entry price + max(abs(entry price - Long stop price), minimum tick) * risk-reward ratio
Calculation: Short take-profit price = if take-profit method is ATR, entry price - ATR(ATR lookback, chart timeframe, entry bar) * take-profit multiplier; otherwise, entry price - max(abs(entry price - Short stop price), minimum tick) * risk-reward ratio
Long: ((MTF filter disabled AND Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) direction on the chart timeframe changes from bearish to bullish) OR (MTF filter enabled AND all enabled T1–T6 Supertrend directions are bullish now and were not all bullish on the previous chart bar; each direction is sampled on chart bars from the latest available Tn bar, developing in real time and last completed historically)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= overbought threshold)
Short: ((MTF filter disabled AND Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) direction on the chart timeframe changes from bullish to bearish) OR (MTF filter enabled AND all enabled T1–T6 Supertrend directions are bearish now and were not all bearish on the previous chart bar; each direction is sampled on chart bars from the latest available Tn bar, developing in real time and last completed historically)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= oversold threshold)
Exit: long, high >= Long take-profit price OR low <= Long stop price OR (reversal exit enabled AND Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) direction on the chart timeframe changes from bullish to bearish)
Exit: short, low <= Short take-profit price OR high >= Short stop price OR (reversal exit enabled AND Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) direction on the chart timeframe changes from bearish to bullish)
