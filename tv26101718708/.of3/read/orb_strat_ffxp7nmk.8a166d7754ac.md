Calculation: opening-range high = maximum high of chart bars from session start through the opening-range duration, for the current local day
Calculation: opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars from session start through the opening-range duration, for the current local day
Calculation: risk distance = signal-bar close × risk-fraction parameter
Long: close > VWAP(hlc3)
Short: close <= VWAP(hlc3)
Long: within the trade window AND the current day's opening range is set AND no position is open AND entries today are below the daily trade-limit parameter AND (if close confirmation is enabled, close > opening-range high AND close[1] <= opening-range high; otherwise, close crosses above opening-range high) AND (if the ADX filter is enabled, ADX(ADX lookback) > ADX-threshold parameter AND, if rising-only is enabled, ADX(ADX lookback) > its prior-bar value) AND (if the volume filter is enabled, volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (if the long-term trend filter is enabled, close > EMA(close, longer trend lookback)) AND (if the short-term trend filter is enabled, close > EMA(close, shorter trend lookback)) AND (if the VWAP filter is enabled, close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND (if the RSI filter is enabled, RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI ceiling parameter)
Short: within the trade window AND the current day's opening range is set AND no position is open AND entries today are below the daily trade-limit parameter AND (if close confirmation is enabled, close < opening-range low AND close[1] >= opening-range low; otherwise, close crosses below opening-range low) AND (if the ADX filter is enabled, ADX(ADX lookback) > ADX-threshold parameter AND, if rising-only is enabled, ADX(ADX lookback) > its prior-bar value) AND (if the volume filter is enabled, volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (if the long-term trend filter is enabled, close < EMA(close, longer trend lookback)) AND (if the short-term trend filter is enabled, close < EMA(close, shorter trend lookback)) AND (if the VWAP filter is enabled, close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND (if the RSI filter is enabled, RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI floor parameter)
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close − risk distance OR high >= signal-bar close + risk distance × reward/risk parameter OR (if trailing is enabled, high reaches signal-bar close + risk distance × trailing activation parameter and low <= highest high since activation − ATR(ATR lookback) × trailing-distance parameter) OR during the session-exit interval
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + risk distance OR low <= signal-bar close − risk distance × reward/risk parameter OR (if trailing is enabled, low reaches signal-bar close − risk distance × trailing activation parameter and high >= lowest low since activation + ATR(ATR lookback) × trailing-distance parameter) OR during the session-exit interval
