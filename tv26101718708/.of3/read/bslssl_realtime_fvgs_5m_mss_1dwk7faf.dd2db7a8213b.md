Calculation: Active liquidity band = cluster at least the minimum cluster count of same-side pivots strictly within ATR(liquidity ATR lookback) × clustering margin / margin scale of a new pivot, scanning the retained alternating ZigZag pivots newest to oldest and stopping at the first same-side pivot beyond that distance; the ZigZag uses PivotHigh(liquidity left lookback, liquidity right lookback) and PivotLow(liquidity left lookback, liquidity right lookback), replacing its latest same-side pivot with a more extreme one. Initial bounds are the cluster midpoint ± that distance, and the oldest matching pivot is the band anchor. A buyside band activates when high exceeds its upper bound and spans from the anchor to the lesser of high and anchor + buyside margin × ATR(liquidity ATR lookback); a sellside band activates when low is below its lower bound and spans from the greater of low and anchor − sellside margin × ATR(liquidity ATR lookback) to the anchor. While each bar stays strictly within the anchor ± its side margin × ATR(liquidity ATR lookback), the band expands to that bar's extreme; otherwise it expires.
Long: low > high[gap lookback] AND low − high[gap lookback] > ATR(FVG volatility lookback) × FVG volatility multiple AND the interval from high[gap lookback] to low does not overlap an active liquidity band
Short: high < low[gap lookback] AND low[gap lookback] − high > ATR(FVG volatility lookback) × FVG volatility multiple AND the interval from high to low[gap lookback] does not overlap an active liquidity band
Long: After a PivotHigh(MSS left lookback, MSS right lookback) value is reported on T1 bars, chart-bar close > its latest value; the pivot is calculated using the T1 bar still forming and sampled on chart bars
Short: After a PivotLow(MSS left lookback, MSS right lookback) value is reported on T1 bars, chart-bar close < its latest value; the pivot is calculated using the T1 bar still forming and sampled on chart bars
