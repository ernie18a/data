Calculation: Fast SMA = SMA(close, fast SMA lookback)
Calculation: Medium SMA = SMA(close, medium SMA lookback)
Calculation: Slow SMA = SMA(close, slow SMA lookback)
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA(StochasticRSI(close, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback), K smoothing lookback), with the unsmoothed value set to 50 when the RSI range is zero
Calculation: Stochastic RSI D = SMA(Stochastic RSI K, D smoothing lookback)
Calculation: MACD line = MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback).line
Calculation: MACD signal = MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback).signal
Calculation: Divergence RSI = RSI(close, divergence RSI lookback)
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = The latest two paired confirmed pivot lows, where PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) and PivotLow(Divergence RSI, the same left and right pivot lookbacks) confirm on the same chart bar, have a lower price and higher RSI at the latest pivot, whose center bar is within the divergence scan lookback
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = The latest two paired confirmed pivot highs, where PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) and PivotHigh(Divergence RSI, the same left and right pivot lookbacks) confirm on the same chart bar, have a higher price and lower RSI at the latest pivot, whose center bar is within the divergence scan lookback
Calculation: Current-day closed P&L = Sum of realized trade P&L since the current day began
Long: Fast SMA > Medium SMA AND Stochastic RSI K crosses above Stochastic RSI D AND min(Stochastic RSI K, Stochastic RSI D) <= oversold threshold
Long: Fast SMA > Medium SMA AND Fast SMA > Slow SMA AND Medium SMA > Slow SMA AND MACD line crosses above MACD signal AND MACD line < 0 AND MACD signal < 0 AND Divergence RSI > divergence floating level AND Bullish RSI divergence
Long: Chart bar is confirmed AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is within the configured session) AND (daily profit limit is disabled OR Current-day closed P&L < daily profit target) AND (daily loss limit is disabled OR Current-day closed P&L > -daily loss limit) AND position is flat or short AND ((Fast SMA > Medium SMA AND Stochastic RSI K crosses above Stochastic RSI D AND min(Stochastic RSI K, Stochastic RSI D) <= oversold threshold) OR (Fast SMA > Medium SMA AND Fast SMA > Slow SMA AND Medium SMA > Slow SMA AND MACD line crosses above MACD signal AND MACD line < 0 AND MACD signal < 0 AND Divergence RSI > divergence floating level AND Bullish RSI divergence)
Short: Fast SMA < Medium SMA AND Stochastic RSI K crosses below Stochastic RSI D AND max(Stochastic RSI K, Stochastic RSI D) >= overbought threshold
Short: Fast SMA < Medium SMA AND Fast SMA < Slow SMA AND Medium SMA < Slow SMA AND MACD line crosses below MACD signal AND MACD line > 0 AND MACD signal > 0 AND Divergence RSI < divergence floating level AND Bearish RSI divergence
Short: Chart bar is confirmed AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is within the configured session) AND (daily profit limit is disabled OR Current-day closed P&L < daily profit target) AND (daily loss limit is disabled OR Current-day closed P&L > -daily loss limit) AND position is flat or long AND ((Fast SMA < Medium SMA AND Stochastic RSI K crosses below Stochastic RSI D AND max(Stochastic RSI K, Stochastic RSI D) >= overbought threshold) OR (Fast SMA < Medium SMA AND Fast SMA < Slow SMA AND Medium SMA < Slow SMA AND MACD line crosses below MACD signal AND MACD line > 0 AND MACD signal > 0 AND Divergence RSI < divergence floating level AND Bearish RSI divergence)
Exit: long, low <= close of the chart bar that generated the entry minus stop-loss points times minimum tick OR high >= close of the chart bar that generated the entry plus take-profit points times minimum tick
Exit: short, high >= close of the chart bar that generated the entry plus stop-loss points times minimum tick OR low <= close of the chart bar that generated the entry minus take-profit points times minimum tick
