Calculation: prior kill-zone low = lowest(low of chart bars during the immediately preceding completed kill-zone occurrence)
Calculation: high sweep depth = (high - prior kill-zone high) / ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: low sweep depth = (prior kill-zone low - low) / ATR(ATR lookback)
