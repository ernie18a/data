Calculation: Smoothed HA(pre-smoothing lookback, post-smoothing lookback) = EMA each of open, high, low, and close with the pre-smoothing lookback; form Heikin-Ashi OHLC from those values (HA close is their mean, HA open is the prior HA open and close mean, seeded with the pre-smoothed open and close mean, and HA high/low are the maximum/minimum of pre-smoothed high/low and HA open/close); EMA each HA OHLC with the post-smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Smoothed HA direction = bullish when Smoothed HA close >= Smoothed HA open, otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Smoothed HA body = abs(Smoothed HA close - Smoothed HA open).
Calculation: Protected swing levels = track the latest confirmed pivot high and low of either raw high/low or Smoothed HA high/low, as selected, using the structure pivot lookback on both sides; while structure direction is bullish, update protected low to each newly confirmed pivot low and use the latest confirmed pivot low if unset; while bearish, update protected high to each newly confirmed pivot high and use the latest confirmed pivot high if unset; on a direction flip, set the newly relevant protected level to the latest confirmed pivot of its type and clear the other level.
Calculation: Active direction = on chart bars, use Smoothed HA direction when structure lock is disabled; when enabled, initialize from Smoothed HA direction, retain bullish direction until a bearish structure break and bearish direction until a bullish structure break. A bearish break is close < protected low - buffer × ATR(ATR lookback) in close-confirmation mode, or low < that level in wick-confirmation mode; a bullish break is close > protected high + buffer × ATR(ATR lookback) in close-confirmation mode, or high > that level in wick-confirmation mode.
Calculation: MTF direction count(side) = count of enabled T1–T6 whose Smoothed HA direction on bars of that timeframe equals side; each timeframe uses the shared pre- and post-smoothing lookbacks and its separately selected confirmed mode (last completed bar) or live mode (currently forming bar).
Calculation: Extra HTF direction = on bars of the separately selected T7, use either Smoothed HA direction or EMA mode, where EMA mode is bullish if close > EMA(close, separate EMA lookback) and EMA >= its prior value, bearish if close < EMA and EMA <= its prior value, and otherwise neutral; use the last completed T7 bar in confirmed mode or the currently forming T7 bar in live mode.
Long: Active direction changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Active direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: Active direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (no MTFs are enabled OR MTF direction count(bullish) >= min(required MTF agreement, number enabled)) AND (extra HTF filter is disabled OR Extra HTF direction is bullish) AND (entry mode is reversal OR EMA(Smoothed HA close, separate slow-baseline lookback) > its prior chart-bar value) AND (impulse filter is disabled OR Smoothed HA body > its prior chart-bar value).
Short: Active direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (no MTFs are enabled OR MTF direction count(bearish) >= min(required MTF agreement, number enabled)) AND (extra HTF filter is disabled OR Extra HTF direction is bearish) AND (entry mode is reversal OR EMA(Smoothed HA close, separate slow-baseline lookback) < its prior chart-bar value) AND (impulse filter is disabled OR Smoothed HA body > its prior chart-bar value).
