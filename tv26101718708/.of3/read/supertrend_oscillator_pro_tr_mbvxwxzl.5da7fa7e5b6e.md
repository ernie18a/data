Calculation: oscillator = in percentage mode, clamp(MA(100 × (close − Supertrend(hl2, ATR lookback, factor)) / max(factor × ATR(ATR lookback), denominator floor), smoothing lookback, selected MA type), −oscillator cap, oscillator cap); in ratio mode, linearly map MA((close − Supertrend(hl2, ATR lookback, factor)) / max(factor × ATR(ATR lookback), denominator floor), smoothing lookback, selected MA type) from its rolling minimum to maximum over the ratio normalization lookback onto [−ratio half-range, ratio half-range], or use zero when that rolling range is zero
Calculation: upper oscillator level = upper threshold in percentage mode; upper threshold × ratio half-range / 100 in ratio mode
Calculation: lower oscillator level = lower threshold in percentage mode; lower threshold × ratio half-range / 100 in ratio mode
Calculation: reversal oscillator threshold = reversal percentage threshold in percentage mode; reversal percentage threshold × ratio half-range / 100 in ratio mode
Long: (crossover(oscillator, lower oscillator level) OR crossover(oscillator, upper oscillator level)) AND last accepted level-cross direction is not long OR crossover(oscillator, oscillator[1]) AND oscillator < −reversal oscillator threshold AND more than the reversal spacing parameter bars since the previous reversal signal of either direction
Short: (crossunder(oscillator, lower oscillator level) OR crossunder(oscillator, upper oscillator level)) AND last accepted level-cross direction is not short OR crossunder(oscillator, oscillator[1]) AND oscillator > reversal oscillator threshold AND more than the reversal spacing parameter bars since the previous reversal signal of either direction
