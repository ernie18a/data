Long: On chart bars using each enabled T1–T6 current/forming bar and completed prior bars, mark a long setup when any enabled family qualifies: (the prior bar is inside the bar before it and the bar before that closed at or below its open, for an enabled inside reversal; or the prior bar is inside and the bar before that closed above its open, for an enabled inside continuation; either family may require a hammer on the prior bar); (the prior bar broke only the low of the bar before it and the current bar is inside or breaks the prior high, for an enabled 2-2 reversal, optionally requiring a hammer and/or a prior bar that broke the older low and closed above it); (the prior bar broke only the high of the bar before it and the current bar is inside or breaks the prior high, for an enabled 2-2 continuation, optionally requiring a hammer); (the prior bar is a hammer and meets either the reversal context—prior bar broke only the older low, or the older bar closed down and the prior bar did not break only the older high, or the prior bar broke neither side and the older bar did not close up—or the continuation context—prior bar broke only the older high, or the older bar closed up and the prior bar did not break only the older low); (the prior bar broke both sides of the older bar and the current bar is inside or breaks the prior high, for an enabled 3-2 expansion, optionally requiring a hammer); (the current bar breaks both sides of the prior bar and closes above its open, for an enabled outside-bar expansion); or (the current bar breaks only the prior low and qualifies as a bullish Failed 2 under the selected detection method, for an enabled Failed 2 signal). Each family’s separate FTFC filter, when enabled, requires all monitored timeframe bars to close above their opens. If the lead-direction filter is enabled, a lower-timeframe signal must align with the highest enabled higher-timeframe signal in force; a counter-direction confirmed Failed 2 is suppressed.

Each family’s separate FTFC filter, when enabled, requires all monitored timeframe bars to close below their opens. If the lead-direction filter is enabled, a lower-timeframe signal must align with the highest enabled higher-timeframe signal in force; a counter-direction confirmed Failed 2 is suppressed.
Long: On chart bars, mark bullish Strat candle classifications when the chart bar breaks only the prior bar’s high, or breaks both sides and closes above its open, or breaks only the prior low and qualifies as a bullish Failed 2 under the selected detection method.
Short: On chart bars, mark bearish Strat candle classifications when the chart bar breaks only the prior bar’s low, or breaks both sides and closes at or below its open, or breaks only the prior high and qualifies as a bearish Failed 2 under the selected detection method.
Long: On chart bars using each enabled T1–T6 current/forming bar, mark bullish Full Timeframe Continuity when every monitored timeframe bar closes above its open; for FTFC candle coloring, compare the chart bar’s close with each monitored timeframe’s open.
Short: On chart bars using each enabled T1–T6 current/forming bar, mark bearish Full Timeframe Continuity when every monitored timeframe bar closes below its open; for FTFC candle coloring, compare the chart bar’s close with each monitored timeframe’s open.
Calculation: Exhaustion target = the nearest prior confirmed pivot high above all more recent highs, or the nearest prior confirmed pivot low below all more recent lows; search back by a parameterized lookback, excluding the forming bar and requiring the pivot to exceed the intervening highs or lows.
Exit: long, after a long signal is in force and stops are enabled, the current bar’s low touches or falls below the active stop; the stop is the current bar’s low for the current-bar reference or for a Failed 2, otherwise the prior completed bar’s low, and moves to the trigger high when the configured magnitude or exhaustion break-even condition is met; or the current bar’s high reaches the enabled magnitude target at the bar two periods ago’s high or the enabled exhaustion target.
Exit: short, after a short signal is in force and stops are enabled, the current bar’s high touches or exceeds the active stop; the stop is the current bar’s high for the current-bar reference or for a Failed 2, otherwise the prior completed bar’s high, and moves to the trigger low when the configured magnitude or exhaustion break-even condition is met; or the current bar’s low reaches the enabled magnitude target at the bar two periods ago’s low or the enabled exhaustion target.
