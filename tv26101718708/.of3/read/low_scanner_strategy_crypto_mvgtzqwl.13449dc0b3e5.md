Calculation: chart-scaled lookback(P) = P × 7 / chart timeframe multiplier on intraday chart bars; 7 on other chart bars
Calculation: volume RSI signal = −EMA(RSI(CUM(change(chart close sampled on the first chart bar of each T1 interval and carried forward) × chart-bar volume), RSI lookback)[1] − WMA(RSI(CUM(change(chart close sampled on the first chart bar of each T1 interval and carried forward) × chart-bar volume), RSI lookback), chart-scaled lookback(P1))[1], chart-scaled lookback(P2))
Calculation: custom weighted trend = WMA(3 × WMA(close, trend lookback / 6) − WMA(close, trend lookback / 4) − WMA(close, trend lookback / 2), trend lookback / 2)
Long: volume RSI signal crosses above its centerline OR linear regression of chart close with regression lookback and regression offset crosses above the custom weighted trend calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars, using the T2 value at the shift-parameter offset (completed when the offset is positive; still forming when it is zero)
