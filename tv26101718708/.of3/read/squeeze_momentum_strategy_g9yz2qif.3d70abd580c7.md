Calculation: EVWMA = nz(close[1], 0) × (floating volume − volume) / floating volume + volume × close / floating volume, where floating volume is cumulative volume or rolling sum of volume over the EVWMA lookback, as selected by the volume mode
Long: Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > 0 AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > its prior-bar value (missing prior value treated as zero)
Short: Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < 0 AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < its prior-bar value (missing prior value treated as zero)
Long: long trades enabled AND datetime is within the configured entry window AND (VWMA filter disabled OR EVWMA > 0) AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > 0 AND current Bollinger lower band(close, BB lookback, shared channel multiplier) < current Keltner lower band(SMA center, SMA of selected true-range or high-low range, KC lookback, shared channel multiplier) AND current Bollinger upper band(close, BB lookback, shared channel multiplier) > current Keltner upper band(SMA center, SMA of selected true-range or high-low range, KC lookback, shared channel multiplier) AND prior-bar Bollinger lower band > prior-bar Keltner lower band AND prior-bar Bollinger upper band < prior-bar Keltner upper band
Short: short trades enabled AND datetime is within the configured entry window AND (VWMA filter disabled OR EVWMA < 0) AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < 0 AND current Bollinger lower band(close, BB lookback, shared channel multiplier) < current Keltner lower band(SMA center, SMA of selected true-range or high-low range, KC lookback, shared channel multiplier) AND current Bollinger upper band(close, BB lookback, shared channel multiplier) > current Keltner upper band(SMA center, SMA of selected true-range or high-low range, KC lookback, shared channel multiplier) AND prior-bar Bollinger lower band > prior-bar Keltner lower band AND prior-bar Bollinger upper band < prior-bar Keltner upper band
Exit: long, NOT (Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > 0 AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > its prior-bar value, missing prior value treated as zero) AND NOT (Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < 0 AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < its prior-bar value, missing prior value treated as zero)
Exit: short, NOT (Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > 0 AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) > its prior-bar value, missing prior value treated as zero) AND NOT (Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < 0 AND Squeeze Momentum(close, high, low, KC lookback) < its prior-bar value, missing prior value treated as zero)
