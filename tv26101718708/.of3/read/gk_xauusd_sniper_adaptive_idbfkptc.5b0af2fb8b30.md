Calculation: Bullish trend = close > ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) > ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND ZLEMA(close, main lookback) - ZLEMA(close, main lookback)[trend slope lookback] > ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum slope in ATRs
Calculation: Bearish trend = close < ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) < ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND ZLEMA(close, main lookback) - ZLEMA(close, main lookback)[trend slope lookback] < -ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum slope in ATRs
Calculation: Bullish momentum = ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) > ZLEMA(close, fast lookback)[1] AND close > close[1]
Calculation: Bearish momentum = ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) < ZLEMA(close, fast lookback)[1] AND close < close[1]
Calculation: Bullish candle = close > open AND close >= high - (high - low) * candle edge fraction
Calculation: Bearish candle = close < open AND close <= low + (high - low) * candle edge fraction
Calculation: Strong body = abs(close - open) > ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum body in ATRs
Calculation: Bullish separation = ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) > ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND abs(ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) - ZLEMA(close, main lookback)) > ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum separation in ATRs
Calculation: Bearish separation = ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) < ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND abs(ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) - ZLEMA(close, main lookback)) > ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum separation in ATRs
Calculation: Bullish expansion = close > high[1] AND bullish candle AND strong body
Calculation: Bearish expansion = close < low[1] AND bearish candle AND strong body
Calculation: Long pullback and reclaim = a pullback touch occurred on the current or one of the prior pullback window bars, where a touch is low <= ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) OR low <= ZLEMA(close, main lookback) OR close <= ZLEMA(close, fast lookback); AND crosses above(close, ZLEMA(close, fast lookback)) OR crosses above(ZLEMA(close, fast lookback), ZLEMA(close, main lookback))
Calculation: Short pullback and rejection = a pullback touch occurred on the current or one of the prior pullback window bars, where a touch is high >= ZLEMA(close, fast lookback) OR high >= ZLEMA(close, main lookback) OR close >= ZLEMA(close, fast lookback); AND crosses below(close, ZLEMA(close, fast lookback)) OR crosses below(ZLEMA(close, fast lookback), ZLEMA(close, main lookback))
Calculation: Acceptable range = highest(high, range lookback) - lowest(low, range lookback) > ATR(ATR lookback) * anti-chop threshold in ATRs
Calculation: Within stretch = abs(close - ZLEMA(close, main lookback)) <= ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum stretch in ATRs
Calculation: Long score = count of true conditions among: bullish trend; bullish separation; close > EMA(close, bias lookback); bullish expansion; high > highest(high, structure lookback)[1]; long pullback and reclaim; acceptable range; within stretch; bullish momentum
Calculation: Short score = count of true conditions among: bearish trend; bearish separation; close < EMA(close, bias lookback); bearish expansion; low < lowest(low, structure lookback)[1]; short pullback and rejection; acceptable range; within stretch; bearish momentum
Calculation: Long raw setup = (bullish trend AND bullish separation AND bullish expansion AND close > EMA(close, bias lookback) AND bullish momentum AND acceptable range) OR (bullish trend AND long pullback and reclaim AND bullish candle AND strong body AND within stretch AND bullish momentum AND acceptable range) OR long score >= score threshold
Calculation: Short raw setup = (bearish trend AND bearish separation AND bearish expansion AND close < EMA(close, bias lookback) AND bearish momentum AND acceptable range) OR (bearish trend AND short pullback and rejection AND bearish candle AND strong body AND within stretch AND bearish momentum AND acceptable range) OR short score >= score threshold
Calculation: Trend side = starts neutral; on each chart bar, if neutral set to long when bullish trend holds, or to short when bearish trend holds; on a confirmed chart bar, switch to long when bullish trend and bullish momentum hold while the side is not long, or switch to short when bearish trend and bearish momentum hold while the side is not short; otherwise retain the side
Calculation: Setup consumed = starts false; reset to false when a confirmed chart bar changes trend side, then set to true when a confirmed chart bar has a long raw setup while trend side is long or bullish trend holds, or a short raw setup while trend side is short or bearish trend holds; otherwise retain its value
Long: on an unconfirmed chart bar, long raw setup AND (trend side is long OR bullish trend) AND NOT setup consumed AND close > ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND bullish candle
Short: on an unconfirmed chart bar, short raw setup AND (trend side is short OR bearish trend) AND NOT setup consumed AND close < ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND bearish candle
Long: on a completed chart bar, long raw setup AND (trend side is long OR bullish trend) AND NOT setup consumed AND close > ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND bullish candle
Short: on a completed chart bar, short raw setup AND (trend side is short OR bearish trend) AND NOT setup consumed AND close < ZLEMA(close, main lookback) AND bearish candle
Exit: long, after at least one chart bar since the latest long entry and before a target has already been signaled for that entry, high >= entry price + target distance
Exit: short, after at least one chart bar since the latest short entry and before a target has already been signaled for that entry, low <= entry price - target distance
