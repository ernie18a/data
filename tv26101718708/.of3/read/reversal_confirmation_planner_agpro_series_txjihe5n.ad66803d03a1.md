Calculation: Process bar = live-updating confirmation mode OR current bar is closed.
Calculation: Safe ATR = max(nz(ATR(ATR lookback), true range), minimum tick).
Calculation: Bar range = max(high - low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Close location = clamp((close - low) / bar range, 0, 1).
Calculation: Lower wick ratio = max(min(open, close) - low, 0) / bar range.
Calculation: Upper wick ratio = max(high - max(open, close), 0) / bar range.
Calculation: Body efficiency = abs(close - open) / bar range.
Calculation: Bull momentum score = clamp(score midpoint + ((EMA(close, short EMA lookback) - nz(EMA(close, short EMA lookback)[momentum lookback], EMA(close, short EMA lookback))) / safe ATR) * momentum score weight + (close > EMA(close, short EMA lookback) ? above-EMA bonus : below-EMA penalty), 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bear momentum score = clamp(score midpoint - ((EMA(close, short EMA lookback) - nz(EMA(close, short EMA lookback)[momentum lookback], EMA(close, short EMA lookback))) / safe ATR) * momentum score weight + (close < EMA(close, short EMA lookback) ? above-EMA bonus : below-EMA penalty), 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume baseline > 0 ? nz(volume, 0) / volume baseline : 1, where volume baseline = nz(SMA(nz(volume, 0), volume lookback), nz(volume, 0)).
Calculation: Volume score = clamp((volume ratio - weak volume ratio) / volume ratio span * volume normalization weight + volume score base + (volume ratio - nz(volume ratio[volume slope lookback], volume ratio)) * volume slope weight, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Sweep ideal depth = the ideal sweep depth parameter selected by sensitivity.
Calculation: Rejection wick threshold = conservative ? minimum rejection wick ratio + conservative wick increment : aggressive ? max(minimum rejection wick ratio - aggressive wick decrement, aggressive wick floor) : minimum rejection wick ratio.
Calculation: Close-location threshold = the close-location parameter selected by sensitivity.
Calculation: Sweep score(depth) = if sweep ideal depth equals minimum sweep depth ATR then 0 else clamp((depth - minimum sweep depth ATR) / (sweep ideal depth - minimum sweep depth ATR) * score maximum, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bull sweep = a confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) exists AND low < last confirmed swing low - safe ATR * minimum sweep depth ATR AND close > last confirmed swing low AND close location >= close-location threshold.
Calculation: Bear sweep = a confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) exists AND high > last confirmed swing high + safe ATR * minimum sweep depth ATR AND close < last confirmed swing high AND close location <= 1 - close-location threshold.
Calculation: Bull rejection = rejection-only reactions are allowed AND (close < close[momentum lookback] OR close < EMA(close, long context EMA lookback)) AND lower wick ratio >= rejection wick threshold AND close location >= close-location threshold AND close > open.
Calculation: Bear rejection = rejection-only reactions are allowed AND (close > close[momentum lookback] OR close > EMA(close, long context EMA lookback)) AND upper wick ratio >= rejection wick threshold AND close location <= 1 - close-location threshold AND close < open.
Calculation: Bull sweep score = clamp(Sweep score((last confirmed swing low - low) / safe ATR) * sweep penetration weight + close location * sweep close-location weight, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bear sweep score = clamp(Sweep score((high - last confirmed swing high) / safe ATR) * sweep penetration weight + (1 - close location) * sweep close-location weight, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bull rejection score = clamp(lower wick ratio * rejection wick weight + close location * rejection close-location weight + (close > open ? body efficiency * rejection body weight : 0), 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bear rejection score = clamp(upper wick ratio * rejection wick weight + (1 - close location) * rejection close-location weight + (close < open ? body efficiency * rejection body weight : 0), 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bull reaction score = clamp(max(bull sweep ? bull sweep score : 0, bull rejection ? bull rejection score : 0) * reaction evidence weight + clamp((EMA(close, long context EMA lookback) - low) / safe ATR / extension divisor * score maximum, 0, score maximum) * extension weight + volume score * reaction volume weight, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Bear reaction score = clamp(max(bear sweep ? bear sweep score : 0, bear rejection ? bear rejection score : 0) * reaction evidence weight + clamp((high - EMA(close, long context EMA lookback)) / safe ATR / extension divisor * score maximum, 0, score maximum) * extension weight + volume score * reaction volume weight, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: For a bullish plan, reaction reference = bull sweep ? last confirmed swing low : low + lower wick ratio * bar range * rejection reference fraction; confirmation line = reaction bar high + safe ATR * confirmation buffer ATR; invalidation edge = reaction bar low - safe ATR * invalidation buffer ATR; target = confirmation line + max(abs(confirmation line - invalidation edge), minimum tick) * target room multiple.
Calculation: For a bearish plan, reaction reference = bear sweep ? last confirmed swing high : high - upper wick ratio * bar range * rejection reference fraction; confirmation line = reaction bar low - safe ATR * confirmation buffer ATR; invalidation edge = reaction bar high + safe ATR * invalidation buffer ATR; target = confirmation line - max(abs(confirmation line - invalidation edge), minimum tick) * target room multiple.
Calculation: For an active plan, confirmation progress = clamp((bullish plan ? close - reaction reference : reaction reference - close) / max(abs(confirmation line - reaction reference), minimum tick) * score maximum, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: For an active plan, room R = active room / max(abs(confirmation line - invalidation edge), minimum tick), where measured room = abs(target - close), structural room = bullish plan ? last confirmed swing high - close : close - last confirmed swing low, and active room = structural room > 0 ? min(structural room, measured room) : measured room.
Calculation: Room score = clamp((room R - room score baseline) / max(preferred room-to-risk ratio - room score baseline, minimum room-score span) * score maximum, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Confirmation score = clamp(stored reaction score * reaction confirmation weight + confirmation progress * progress weight + (bullish plan ? bull momentum score : bear momentum score) * momentum confirmation weight + volume score * volume confirmation weight + room score * room confirmation weight, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: A candidate starts a plan only on a process bar when its reaction score meets the minimum reaction score, it wins side selection (bull wins ties), and no plan is active, the active plan has failed or expired, or its score exceeds the active plan's reaction score by the opposite-side replacement margin; for an unconfirmed same-side plan, replacement also qualifies when the score exceeds its reaction score by the same-side replacement margin and more than one bar has elapsed since its reaction.
Calculation: A started plan stores its side, reaction bar, reaction score, reaction reference, confirmation line, invalidation edge, and target; it fails when a process bar closes beyond its invalidation edge, expires when unconfirmed and more than the confirmation window has elapsed since its reaction, confirms on a process bar when close crosses its confirmation line and confirmation score meets the confirmation threshold, and becomes terminal when failed, expired, or its confirmed target is touched; reset it after the terminal hold period.
Long: Bullish candidate starts a plan.
Short: Bearish candidate starts a plan.
Long: Active bullish plan AND process bar AND not previously confirmed AND not failed AND not expired AND close > confirmation line AND confirmation score >= confirmation threshold.
Short: Active bearish plan AND process bar AND not previously confirmed AND not failed AND not expired AND close < confirmation line AND confirmation score >= confirmation threshold.
Exit: long, Active bullish plan AND process bar AND not previously failed AND close < invalidation edge.
Exit: short, Active bearish plan AND process bar AND not previously failed AND close > invalidation edge.
