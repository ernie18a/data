Calculation: Camarilla levels = standard Camarilla R3/R4/S3/S4 from the configured prior RTH or ETH high, low, and close; R6 = close × high / low; S6 = 2 × close − R6. In Auto source mode, use ETH high/low when the prior post-market or current premarket high/low extends beyond the prior RTH high/low by more than the tolerance × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar; otherwise use RTH high/low. Auto close follows the selected source unless overridden.
Long: On a confirmed intraday chart bar with high > low, when the RTH-only filter applies the bar is in RTH; low < Lowest(low, sweep lookback)[1]; body low ≥ high − body-zone fraction × (high − low); high − body high ≤ max(opposite-wick fraction × (high − low), minimum tick); body low − low ≥ max(sweep-wick ATR fraction × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar, sweep-wick tick floor × minimum tick); when the inside-body filter is enabled, body low ≥ low[1] AND body high ≤ high[1]; in hard-filter mode, the prior candle is not bearish with body > max(prior-body ATR fraction × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar, prior-body tick floor × minimum tick); and either level mode is Tag Only or an enabled long level lies within the sweep-wick span expanded by max(level-proximity ATR fraction × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar, level-proximity tick floor × minimum tick). Long levels are the configured premarket low (during premarket it must be unchanged for the maturity lookback), previous RTH low, previous RTH close, current still-forming T2 low accumulated on chart bars (unchanged for the maturity lookback), optional prior completed overnight low, enabled Camarilla S3/S4/S6, and any active custom level.
Short: On a confirmed intraday chart bar with high > low, when the RTH-only filter applies the bar is in RTH; high > Highest(high, sweep lookback)[1]; body high ≤ low + body-zone fraction × (high − low); body low − low ≤ max(opposite-wick fraction × (high − low), minimum tick); high − body high ≥ max(sweep-wick ATR fraction × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar, sweep-wick tick floor × minimum tick); when the inside-body filter is enabled, body low ≥ low[1] AND body high ≤ high[1]; in hard-filter mode, the prior candle is not bullish with body > max(prior-body ATR fraction × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar, prior-body tick floor × minimum tick); and either level mode is Tag Only or an enabled short level lies within the sweep-wick span expanded by max(level-proximity ATR fraction × ATR(lookback) computed on T1 bars from the last completed T1 bar, level-proximity tick floor × minimum tick). Short levels are the configured premarket high (during premarket it must be unchanged for the maturity lookback), previous RTH high, previous RTH close, current still-forming T2 high accumulated on chart bars (unchanged for the maturity lookback), optional prior completed overnight high, enabled Camarilla R3/R4/R6, and any active custom level.
