Calculation: Estimated delta = sum over each T1 intrabar in each chart bar, including the still-forming T1 intrabar, across Binance and the selected Bybit, OKX, and Bitget perpetual feeds, of volume × (2 × buy ratio − 1), where buy ratio = 0.5 if high = low, otherwise 0.5 + 0.5 × (close − low) / (high − low) when close >= open, else 0.5 × (close − low) / (high − low)
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of estimated delta on chart bars, reset at each T2 session boundary; the current T2 session is still forming
Calculation: Bull percentile = 0 if no prior completed T2 session CVD highs are stored; otherwise 100 × count of stored highs <= the current T2 session-to-date CVD high / count of stored highs, evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: Bear percentile = 0 if no prior completed T2 session CVD lows are stored; otherwise 100 × count of stored lows >= the current T2 session-to-date CVD low / count of stored lows, evaluated on chart bars
Long: Lowest(close, swing length) < Lowest(close, swing length)[swing length] AND Lowest(CVD, swing length) > Lowest(CVD, swing length)[swing length] AND bull percentile >= divergence percentile threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > SMA(close, trend lookback)) AND the first three conditions were false on the prior chart bar
Short: Highest(close, swing length) > Highest(close, swing length)[swing length] AND Highest(CVD, swing length) < Highest(CVD, swing length)[swing length] AND bear percentile >= divergence percentile threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < SMA(close, trend lookback)) AND the first three conditions were false on the prior chart bar
Long: close < close[1] AND CVD > CVD[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, absorption volume lookback) × absorption volume multiplier AND bull percentile >= divergence percentile threshold AND these four conditions were false on the prior chart bar
Short: close > close[1] AND CVD < CVD[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, absorption volume lookback) × absorption volume multiplier AND bear percentile >= divergence percentile threshold AND these four conditions were false on the prior chart bar
