Calculation: Sweep score(d, l) = score-scale parameter × (wick-weight × min(max(w, 0) / ATR(ATR lookback), 1) + reclaim-weight × min(max(r, 0) / ATR(ATR lookback), 1) + close-position-weight × cp + volume-weight × vol + T1-bias-weight × bias), when high > low, ATR > 0, and l exists; otherwise 0, where for d=long, w=min(open, close)−low, r=close−l, cp=(close−low)/(high−low), and bias is neutral-factor parameter when the T1 filter is off, otherwise 1 when close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) and 0 otherwise; for d=short, w=high−max(open, close), r=l−close, cp=1−(close−low)/(high−low), and bias is neutral-factor parameter when the T1 filter is off, otherwise 1 when close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) and 0 otherwise; vol is neutral-factor parameter when the volume filter is off or volume or its SMA is unavailable or nonpositive, otherwise clamp((volume/SMA(volume, volume lookback)−1)/(volume-spike multiple−1), 0, 1); T1 values are computed on T1 bars using the last completed bar and read on chart bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with bar_index ≥ max(2 × swing lookback, warm-up floor), a retained, unconsumed confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) formed within the maximum sweep age has low < level and close > level; consume it as swept, using the newest matching level l, and mark long if Sweep score(long, l) ≥ minimum score; consume an eligible level without a signal if close < level.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with bar_index ≥ max(2 × swing lookback, warm-up floor), a retained, unconsumed confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) formed within the maximum sweep age has high > level and close < level; consume it as swept, using the newest matching level l, and mark short if Sweep score(short, l) ≥ minimum score; consume an eligible level without a signal if close > level.
Long: While flat and ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, enter at close on a confirmed bar when a long signal fires and no short signal fires on that bar; with confirmation disabled, a long signal fires on a qualifying long sweep, and with confirmation enabled, it fires while the most recent qualifying sweep is long and within the confirmation window when close > the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, structure lookback, structure lookback); each new qualifying sweep replaces the pending sweep, a bearish sweep takes precedence if both directions qualify on one bar, and a pending sweep expires after the confirmation window.
Short: While flat and ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, enter at close on a confirmed bar when a short signal fires and no long signal fires on that bar; with confirmation disabled, a short signal fires on a qualifying short sweep, and with confirmation enabled, it fires while the most recent qualifying sweep is short and within the confirmation window when close < the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, structure lookback, structure lookback); each new qualifying sweep replaces the pending sweep, a bearish sweep takes precedence if both directions qualify on one bar, and a pending sweep expires after the confirmation window.
Calculation: Long initial stop = if abs(entry price − (sweep-bar low − ATR on entry bar × stop-buffer parameter)) < ATR on entry bar × minimum-stop-distance parameter, then entry price − ATR on entry bar × minimum-stop-distance parameter, else sweep-bar low − ATR on entry bar × stop-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Short initial stop = if abs(entry price − (sweep-bar high + ATR on entry bar × stop-buffer parameter)) < ATR on entry bar × minimum-stop-distance parameter, then entry price + ATR on entry bar × minimum-stop-distance parameter, else sweep-bar high + ATR on entry bar × stop-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Long risk distance = max(abs(entry price − (sweep-bar low − ATR on entry bar × stop-buffer parameter)), ATR on entry bar × minimum-stop-distance parameter).
Calculation: Short risk distance = max(abs(entry price − (sweep-bar high + ATR on entry bar × stop-buffer parameter)), ATR on entry bar × minimum-stop-distance parameter).
Calculation: Long TP1 = entry price + long risk distance × TP1 multiple.
Calculation: Short TP1 = entry price − short risk distance × TP1 multiple.
Calculation: Long TP3 = entry price + long risk distance × TP3 multiple.
Calculation: Short TP3 = entry price − short risk distance × TP3 multiple.
Exit: long, while long position is open, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, low ≤ entry price if break-even is enabled and TP1 was touched before this bar, otherwise low ≤ long initial stop, OR high ≥ Long TP3.
Exit: short, while short position is open, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, high ≥ entry price if break-even is enabled and TP1 was touched before this bar, otherwise high ≥ short initial stop, OR low ≤ Short TP3.
