Calculation: composite = EMA(weighted mean of eight factors, smoothing lookback), where the factors are optionally clamped to ±winsor limit: Z-score(log(equity close 1 / equity close 1[return lookback]), z-score lookback), Z-score(log(equity close 2 / equity close 2[return lookback]), z-score lookback), -Z-score(credit close 1 / credit close 2, z-score lookback), -Z-score(short yield, z-score lookback), Z-score(long yield - short yield, z-score lookback), -Z-score(dollar-index close, z-score lookback), -Z-score(volatility-index close, z-score lookback), and -Z-score(bill-fund close / equity close 1, z-score lookback); a Z-score is (value - SMA(value, z-score lookback)) / stdev(value, z-score lookback), with zero or missing results set to zero; the weighting mode selects equal weights or inverse-volatility weights of 1 / stdev(factor, weight lookback)^volatility exponent, with zero weight when that standard deviation is zero, and weights are normalized to sum to one; each input close is mapped from T1 with lookahead off and gaps carried forward, using the last completed T1 close historically and the forming T1 close in realtime, while return lags, Z-scores, factor standard deviations, and EMA are calculated on chart bars.
Long: (composite crosses above long-entry threshold AND composite - composite[slope lookback] > 0 AND (price filter disabled OR close > SMA(close, price-filter lookback))) OR composite >= always-long floor
Short: composite crosses below short-entry threshold AND composite - composite[slope lookback] < 0 AND (price filter disabled OR close < SMA(close, price-filter lookback))
Exit: Long, composite crosses below long-exit threshold
Exit: Short, composite crosses above short-exit threshold OR composite < short-exit guard
