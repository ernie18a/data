Calculation: Candle fingerprint = [abs(close−open)/max(high−low, minimum tick), (high−max(open,close))/max(high−low, minimum tick), (min(open,close)−low)/max(high−low, minimum tick), (min(open,close)−low)/max(high−low, minimum tick), min(volume/SMA(volume, volume-average lookback), relative-volume cap)/relative-volume cap when SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)>0, otherwise relative-volume fallback; optionally append min((high−low)/safe ATR, candle-range cap)/candle-range cap, (clamp((close−EMA(close, trend lookback))/safe ATR, −trend-distance cap, trend-distance cap)+trend-distance cap)/(2×trend-distance cap), and candle context encoded by separate inside, normal, and outside scores. Safe ATR is ATR(ATR lookback) when positive, otherwise minimum tick. Inside means high≤high[1] AND low≥low[1]; outside means high>high[1] AND low<low[1]; otherwise normal. The first five dimensions are always used; each optional dimension is included according to its setting. Use the current chart bar for the current fingerprint and each candidate's chart bar for its historical fingerprint.
Calculation: Historical outcome = up when up>down, otherwise −down, where up=(high on the bar forward-outcome lookback bars after the candidate−candidate close)/max(ATR(ATR lookback) at the candidate, minimum tick), and down=(candidate close−low on that same bar)/max(ATR(ATR lookback) at the candidate, minimum tick).
Calculation: Best historical match = the eligible candidate with the highest score, ties going to the most recent candidate. Eligible candidates are historical chart bars within the history lookback, at least forward-outcome lookback plus two bars ago, whose absolute historical outcome is at least the minimum outcome move. Candidate score = 100×(1−min(Euclidean distance between current and candidate candle fingerprints, √active dimension count)/√active dimension count).
Long: best historical match score≥minimum match score AND best historical match outcome>0 AND current bar index−last signal bar index≥signal cooldown, with the last signal bar index initialized to zero.
Short: best historical match score≥minimum match score AND best historical match outcome≤0 AND current bar index−last signal bar index≥signal cooldown, with the last signal bar index initialized to zero.
