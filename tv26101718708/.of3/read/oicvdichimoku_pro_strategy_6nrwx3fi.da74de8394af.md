Calculation: CVD = EMA(volume × (close > open ? 1 : -1), CVD smoothing lookback)
Long: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND (change in open interest over one bar > OI threshold OR CVD > 0) AND (Ichimoku filter disabled OR close > Ichimoku Cloud top(tenkan lookback, kijun lookback, Senkou B lookback), sampled displacement bars ago)
Short: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND (change in open interest over one bar < −OI threshold OR CVD < 0) AND (Ichimoku filter disabled OR close < Ichimoku Cloud bottom(tenkan lookback, kijun lookback, Senkou B lookback), sampled displacement bars ago)
Exit: long, low ≤ the stop level set at the most recent entry signal (signal-bar close − ATR(lookback at signal) × stop multiplier when ATR stops are selected, otherwise signal-bar close − signal-bar close × stop percentage) OR high ≥ signal-bar close + ATR(lookback at signal) × first target multiplier OR high ≥ signal-bar close + ATR(lookback at signal) × second target multiplier
Exit: short, high ≥ the stop level set at the most recent entry signal (signal-bar close + ATR(lookback at signal) × stop multiplier when ATR stops are selected, otherwise signal-bar close + signal-bar close × stop percentage) OR low ≤ signal-bar close − ATR(lookback at signal) × first target multiplier OR low ≤ signal-bar close − ATR(lookback at signal) × second target multiplier
