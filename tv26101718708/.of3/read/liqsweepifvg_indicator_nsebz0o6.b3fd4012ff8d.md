Calculation: Live session levels = the high and low of each enabled NY, London, or Asia session, recorded at session end while session-level tracking is enabled; retain only the configured live depth and history depth for each session and level type; consume a level on its first later touch, even if the touch does not exceed the raid buffer.
Calculation: Equal-high/low levels = pair a swing high where highestbars(high, 3) = -1 with a later high[1] within the configured tolerance, after more than the configured minimum bars; a high[1] above the swing high plus tolerance breaks that swing. Mirror for swing lows using lowestbars(low, 3) = -1, low[1], and a break below the swing low minus tolerance. Sweep an equal-high level when high[1] > the higher pair price plus tolerance; sweep an equal-low level when low[1] < the lower pair price minus tolerance.
Calculation: Reversal arms = a first-touch session-high raid with high >= level + raid buffer, when that session is enabled for arming, or an enabled equal-high sweep arms short; a first-touch session-low raid with low <= level - raid buffer, when enabled for arming, or an enabled equal-low sweep arms long. Arms expire after the configured trigger window; a qualifying trigger consumes all live arms of its direction.
Calculation: Live T1 FVG = a bullish three-bar gap with low > high[2] or bearish three-bar gap with high < low[2], formed on completed T1 bars and meeting the configured minimum gap size; remove it when a chart-bar close passes its far edge. A chart bar interacts with it when low <= gap top and high >= gap bottom.
Long: a live long arm AND ((the selected trigger is return through level AND close > the raided level of a live long arm) OR (the selected trigger is iFVG inversion AND a live bearish chart-bar FVG, high < low[2] with low[2] - high at least the configured minimum gap size, formed no earlier than the configured lookback before the newest live long arm, AND close > low[2])) AND (the higher-timeframe FVG filter is disabled OR a live long arm interacted with a live T1 FVG at some chart bar from arming through the trigger).
Short: a live short arm AND ((the selected trigger is return through level AND close < the raided level of a live short arm) OR (the selected trigger is iFVG inversion AND a live bullish chart-bar FVG, low > high[2] with low - high[2] at least the configured minimum gap size, formed no earlier than the configured lookback before the newest live short arm, AND close < high[2])) AND (the higher-timeframe FVG filter is disabled OR a live short arm interacted with a live T1 FVG at some chart bar from arming through the trigger).
