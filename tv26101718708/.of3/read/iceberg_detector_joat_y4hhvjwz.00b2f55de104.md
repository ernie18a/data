Calculation: Upper wick = high - max(open, close)
Calculation: Lower wick = min(open, close) - low
Calculation: Absorption volume = SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) >= volume multiplier
Calculation: Resistance wick candidate = upper wick > max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) * wick-to-body ratio AND high >= highest(high, pivot lookback) - (highest(high, pivot lookback) - lowest(low, pivot lookback)) * 0.25
Calculation: Support wick candidate = lower wick > max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) * wick-to-body ratio AND low <= lowest(low, pivot lookback) + (highest(high, pivot lookback) - lowest(low, pivot lookback)) * 0.25
Calculation: Resistance test = confirmed bar AND absorption volume AND resistance wick candidate AND (NOT support wick candidate OR upper wick >= lower wick)
Calculation: Support test = confirmed bar AND absorption volume AND support wick candidate AND (NOT resistance wick candidate OR lower wick > upper wick)
Calculation: New resistance iceberg level = arithmetic mean of the high values of matching resistance tests in the test-window lookback, where each high is within ATR(ATR length) * cluster-tolerance multiplier of the current high
Calculation: New support iceberg level = arithmetic mean of the low values of matching support tests in the test-window lookback, where each low is within ATR(ATR length) * cluster-tolerance multiplier of the current low
Long: Support test AND (an active support iceberg level is within ATR(ATR length) * cluster-tolerance multiplier of the current low OR (no active support iceberg level is within that tolerance AND at least minimum test count matching support tests occur in the test-window lookback AND the fraction of those tests with close >= open is >= wick-rejection percentage)) OR (confirmed bar AND an active resistance iceberg exists AND close >= its level + ATR(ATR length) * break-distance multiplier)
Short: Resistance test AND (an active resistance iceberg level is within ATR(ATR length) * cluster-tolerance multiplier of the current high OR (no active resistance iceberg level is within that tolerance AND at least minimum test count matching resistance tests occur in the test-window lookback AND the fraction of those tests with close <= open is >= wick-rejection percentage)) OR (confirmed bar AND an active support iceberg exists AND close <= its level - ATR(ATR length) * break-distance multiplier)
