Calculation: Uptrend = EMA(close, fast trend lookback) > EMA(close, slow trend lookback) AND EMA(close, extension mean lookback) > its value slope lookback bars ago
Calculation: Downtrend = EMA(close, fast trend lookback) < EMA(close, slow trend lookback) AND EMA(close, extension mean lookback) < its value slope lookback bars ago
Calculation: Upward extension = if ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, the average of (close − EMA(close, extension mean lookback)) / ATR and (close − session VWAP) / ATR; use close when session VWAP is unavailable; otherwise 0
Calculation: Downward extension = if ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, the average of (EMA(close, extension mean lookback) − close) / ATR and (session VWAP − close) / ATR; use close when session VWAP is unavailable; otherwise 0
Calculation: Upper-wick rejection = high − max(open, close) >= abs(close − open) × wick-to-body parameter AND high − max(open, close) >= min(open, close) − low
Calculation: Lower-wick rejection = min(open, close) − low >= abs(close − open) × wick-to-body parameter AND min(open, close) − low >= high − max(open, close)
Calculation: Confirmed bearish RSI divergence = at a newly confirmed pivot high, pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND RSI at the pivot < RSI at the previous confirmed pivot high
Calculation: Confirmed bullish RSI divergence = at a newly confirmed pivot low, pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low AND RSI at the pivot > RSI at the previous confirmed pivot low
Calculation: Confirmed top factors = at the confirmed pivot high, RSI >= upper RSI threshold; confirmed bearish RSI divergence; volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume-spike multiplier; high >= upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation multiplier); upward extension >= extension threshold; StochRSI(RSI(close, RSI lookback), stochastic-RSI lookback, smoothing) >= upper StochRSI threshold; upper-wick rejection; close < open AND previous close >= previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close; RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) >= upper RSI threshold sampled on chart bars at the pivot; or high > highest(high, sweep lookback) over the window ending one bar before the pivot AND pivot close < pivot high
Calculation: Confirmed bottom factors = at the confirmed pivot low, RSI <= lower RSI threshold; confirmed bullish RSI divergence; volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume-spike multiplier; low <= lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation multiplier); downward extension >= extension threshold; StochRSI(RSI(close, RSI lookback), stochastic-RSI lookback, smoothing) <= lower StochRSI threshold; lower-wick rejection; close > open AND previous close <= previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close; RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) <= lower RSI threshold sampled on chart bars at the pivot; or low < lowest(low, sweep lookback) over the window ending one bar before the pivot AND pivot close > pivot low
Calculation: Confirmed top confidence = 0 if the denominator is zero, otherwise clamp 100 × (sum of the weights of enabled confirmed top factors that pass + trend adjustment − hidden-divergence penalty if a confirmed pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed pivot high and its RSI is higher) / (sum of weights for all enabled confirmed factors + trend weight) to 0–100; trend adjustment is trend weight × 1 when Kaufman efficiency ratio(close, efficiency lookback) >= strong-trend threshold, otherwise trend weight × weak-trend weight fraction, added in a downtrend and subtracted in an uptrend, or zero in neither
Calculation: Confirmed bottom confidence = 0 if the denominator is zero, otherwise clamp 100 × (sum of the weights of enabled confirmed bottom factors that pass + trend adjustment − hidden-divergence penalty if a confirmed pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed pivot low and its RSI is lower) / (sum of weights for all enabled confirmed factors + trend weight) to 0–100; trend adjustment is trend weight × 1 when Kaufman efficiency ratio(close, efficiency lookback) >= strong-trend threshold, otherwise trend weight × weak-trend weight fraction, added in an uptrend and subtracted in a downtrend, or zero in neither
Calculation: Confirmed top vote count = number of enabled confirmed top factors that pass at the confirmed pivot
Calculation: Confirmed bottom vote count = number of enabled confirmed bottom factors that pass at the confirmed pivot
Calculation: Early top confidence = 0 if no early-factor weights are enabled, otherwise min(100, 100 × sum of weights of enabled passing early top factors / sum of weights of enabled early factors); evaluate the current live bar, using RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Early bottom confidence = 0 if no early-factor weights are enabled, otherwise min(100, 100 × sum of weights of enabled passing early bottom factors / sum of weights of enabled early factors); evaluate the current live bar, using RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Early top factors = on the current live bar, RSI >= upper RSI threshold; volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume-spike multiplier; high >= upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation multiplier); upward extension >= extension threshold; StochRSI >= upper StochRSI threshold; upper-wick rejection; T1 RSI >= upper RSI threshold; or high > highest(high, sweep lookback) over the window ending one bar before the current bar AND close < high
Calculation: Early bottom factors = on the current live bar, RSI <= lower RSI threshold; volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume-spike multiplier; low <= lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation multiplier); downward extension >= extension threshold; StochRSI <= lower StochRSI threshold; lower-wick rejection; T1 RSI <= lower RSI threshold; or low < lowest(low, sweep lookback) over the window ending one bar before the current bar AND close > low
Calculation: Active tracked reversal = with follow-through enabled, select the confirmed top when only the top confirms or both confirm and top confidence >= bottom confidence; otherwise select the confirmed bottom when only the bottom confirms or bottom confidence > top confidence
Calculation: Short target = at a selected confirmed top, use the highest price below entry among EMA(close, extension mean lookback), session VWAP (or close if unavailable), and the latest confirmed pivot low; if none is below entry, use entry − fallback reward/risk multiple × (pivot high + hard-invalidation ATR multiple × ATR at confirmation − entry)
Calculation: Long target = at a selected confirmed bottom, use the lowest price above entry among EMA(close, extension mean lookback), session VWAP (or close if unavailable), and the latest confirmed pivot high; if none is above entry, use entry + fallback reward/risk multiple × (entry − (pivot low − hard-invalidation ATR multiple × ATR at confirmation))
Long: Early-warning enabled AND Early bottom confidence >= early-warning threshold AND Early bottom confidence on the previous chart bar < early-warning threshold
Short: Early-warning enabled AND Early top confidence >= early-warning threshold AND Early top confidence on the previous chart bar < early-warning threshold
Long: Early bottom confidence >= early-warning threshold AND Early top confidence < early-warning threshold
Short: Early top confidence >= early-warning threshold
Long: confirmed bullish RSI divergence
Short: confirmed bearish RSI divergence
Long: a pivot low confirmed after the right-confirmation lookback AND confirmed bottom confidence >= minimum confidence AND confirmed bottom vote count >= minimum votes AND no prior confirmed bottom signal or at least cooldown bars since it
Short: a pivot high confirmed after the right-confirmation lookback AND confirmed top confidence >= minimum confidence AND confirmed top vote count >= minimum votes AND no prior confirmed top signal or at least cooldown bars since it
Long: Uptrend OR (NOT Uptrend AND NOT Downtrend)
Short: Downtrend OR (NOT Uptrend AND NOT Downtrend)
Exit: long, follow-through enabled AND the active tracked reversal is a bottom AND (close < pivot low − hard-invalidation ATR multiple × ATR at confirmation OR highest high since confirmation >= Long target)
Exit: short, follow-through enabled AND the active tracked reversal is a top AND (close > pivot high + hard-invalidation ATR multiple × ATR at confirmation OR lowest low since confirmation <= Short target)
