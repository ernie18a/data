Calculation: Price = selected price source or selected alternative source, optionally smoothed by SWMA; alternatives are (open + close + 3 × (high + low)) / 8, close + high + low − 2 × open, (close + 5 × (high + low) − 7 × open) / 4, (open + close + 5 × (high + low)) / 12, high when close > open otherwise low, or high when OHLC4 > Heikin-Ashi open otherwise low; Heikin-Ashi open starts at (open + close) / 2 and thereafter is the prior Heikin-Ashi open plus prior OHLC4, averaged.
Calculation: Adaptive lookback = integer part of the prior lookback multiplied by the contraction factor when at least one enabled length filter passes and all enabled length filters pass, otherwise multiplied by the expansion factor; clamp between that line’s separate minimum and maximum lookbacks; initialize at their average; contraction and expansion factors are the smoothing input and its complement to two. Conversion, base, span, and Chikou lookbacks update independently. Their enabled filters are their separately selected volume oscillator above the volume threshold, fast ATR above slow ATR, and bullish Chikou trend.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = selected OBV, TFS, Klinger-style EMA spread, cumulative-volume EMA spread, or VZO. TFS is the lookback mean of volume on up candles, negative volume on down candles, and zero on dojis. OBV is cumulative signed volume by close change. Klinger-style spread is the fast EMA minus the slow EMA of volume multiplied by positive scale when close rises and negative scale otherwise. Cumulative-volume spread is the fast EMA minus the slow EMA of the selected OBV, candle-pattern cumulative delta, or PVT base. VZO is the EMA of volume signed by close change divided by the EMA of volume, scaled to percent. Each oscillator selection and its applicable lookbacks are separate inputs.
Calculation: Conversion line = the average of Donchian midpoints of high and low over the adaptive conversion lookback and over its integer-divided reactive lookback. Base line = the same calculation with the separate adaptive base lookback. A Donchian midpoint is the average of the highest high and lowest low over the lookback.
Calculation: Leading span A = the average of the conversion and base lines; leading span B = the Donchian midpoint over the adaptive span lookback. Current cloud top and bottom = maximum and minimum of each span at the cloud offset minus one bar ago. Effective Chikou offset is the adaptive Chikou lookback or the selected fixed offset.
Calculation: Chikou trend = bullish when price > highest(high, Chikou lookback)[Chikou lookback], bearish when price < lowest(low, Chikou lookback)[Chikou lookback], otherwise neutral. Chikou past price = price[effective Chikou offset]; prior Chikou past price = price[effective Chikou offset minus one].
Calculation: Trend strength = EMA of DX; DX = 100 × abs(plus DI − minus DI) / (plus DI + minus DI), where plus and minus DI are their EMA-smoothed directional movements divided by EMA-smoothed true range and multiplied by 100; zero denominators give zero. Kumo thickness = abs(cloud top − cloud bottom) / max(abs(cloud top), abs(cloud bottom), minimum price increment) × 100.
Calculation: Signal warm-up is complete when the chart bar index reaches the maximum of the effective maximum conversion, base, span, and Chikou lookbacks and the effective cloud offset.

Calculation: Panel score(position, trend) = 1 when trend is bullish and position is above cloud, 2 when bullish and position overlaps cloud, 3 when bullish and below cloud; −1 when bearish and below cloud, −2 when bearish and overlapping cloud, −3 when bearish and above cloud. Panel positions are above, below, or overlapping the current cloud. Panel module scores are: TK range position with conversion > base as trend; base-line position with close > base as trend; close above the high at the panel offset or below the low at that offset, scored against close’s cloud position; close versus cloud as +1, −1, or 0; and cloud position scored with leading span A > leading span B as trend. Panel consensus is the maximum score if every module score is positive, the minimum score if every module score is negative, otherwise zero. Panel calculations use their separately selected panel lengths.
Long: Any displayed panel module score > 0 OR panel consensus > 0.
Short: Any displayed panel module score < 0 OR panel consensus < 0.

Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low over the swing lookback on both sides, confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Long: When swing detection and swing markers are enabled, a confirmed swing low occurs.
Short: When swing detection and swing markers are enabled, a confirmed swing high occurs.
Calculation: Wave completion uses the latest alternating confirmed swing points, a minimum duration, and the configured enabled patterns, in priority order N, W, P, Y, V, I. N is bullish when A < B, A plus the tolerance fraction of abs(A−B) < C < B minus that fraction, and D > B plus that fraction; bearish uses the reverse inequalities. W requires A, C, and E within the tolerance fraction of the first leg and B and D within that fraction, with matching swing directions; B > A is bearish, otherwise bullish. P requires the C−D range to be within configured narrowing bounds relative to A−B and C and D within the A−B range; direction is D’s swing direction. Y requires the C−D range to exceed the configured expansion bound and C and D each outside the A−B range; direction is D’s swing direction. V requires C within the tolerance fraction of A; a swing-low C is bullish, otherwise bearish. I is a single leg; direction is up when B > A, otherwise down. Only the first matching enabled pattern is completed for the latest endpoint.
Long: When swing and wave detection are enabled, a bullish wave completes.
Short: When swing and wave detection are enabled, a bearish wave completes.
Calculation: Completed N-wave targets = V: B + (B − C); E: B + (B − A); N: C + (B − A); NT: C + (C − A); 2E: B + twice (B − A); 3E: B + three times (B − A).
Long: After a bullish N-wave completes, high reaches any of its V, N, E, NT, 2E, or 3E targets for the first time.
Short: After a bearish N-wave completes, low reaches any of its V, N, E, NT, 2E, or 3E targets for the first time.
