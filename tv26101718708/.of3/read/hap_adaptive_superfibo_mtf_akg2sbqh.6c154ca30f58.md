Calculation: Adaptive multiplier = manual multiplier when adaptive mode is off; otherwise clamp(base multiplier / volatility ratio × ADX factor, minimum multiplier, maximum multiplier), where volatility ratio = ATR(short lookback) / ATR(long lookback) when both are positive, else 1; ADX factor = low-ADX factor when ADX(DMI length, ADX smoothing) is below the low threshold, high-ADX factor when it is above the high threshold, else 1.
Calculation: Selected trend = SuperTrend(ATR length, adaptive or manual multiplier) on chart bars when no other timeframe is selected, otherwise computed on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (the T1 bar still forming in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically).
Calculation: Dashboard trend on each of T2, T3, T4, and T5 = SuperTrend direction using the same ATR length and multiplier, computed on that timeframe's bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (the timeframe bar still forming in realtime and the last completed bar historically).
Calculation: Average body = SMA(abs(close - open), body lookback), replacing zero with 1.
Calculation: Active bullish OB zones = on chart bars, when OBs are enabled and close crosses above the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) from a prior close at or below it, search i = 1 through min(age of latest confirmed Pivot Low, OB scanback); choose the nearest bearish candle satisfying each enabled filter: low[i-2] - high[i] > average body × minimum FVG factor (gap is 0 when i < 2), and close[i-1] - open[i-1] >= average body × displacement factor; if none qualifies, choose the nearest bearish candle whose low equals the minimum low in the search. Accept the zone if each enabled filter passes: minimum search low is below the previous confirmed Pivot Low or no such pivot exists (sweep), and zone midpoint is below the midpoint of the latest confirmed Pivot High and Pivot Low (premium-discount). The zone is high[i] to low[i]; remove it when close is below its bottom, low is below its bottom minus zone size × wick tolerance / 100, or its touch count reaches the maximum. A touch is low at or below the selected midpoint or upper edge after prior low was above that level.
Calculation: Active bearish OB zones = on chart bars, when OBs are enabled and close crosses below the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) from a prior close at or above it, search i = 1 through min(age of latest confirmed Pivot High, OB scanback); choose the nearest bullish candle satisfying each enabled filter: low[i] - high[i-2] > average body × minimum FVG factor (gap is 0 when i < 2), and open[i-1] - close[i-1] >= average body × displacement factor; if none qualifies, choose the nearest bullish candle whose high equals the maximum high in the search. Accept the zone if each enabled filter passes: maximum search high is above the previous confirmed Pivot High or no such pivot exists (sweep), and zone midpoint is above the midpoint of the latest confirmed Pivot High and Pivot Low (premium-discount). The zone is high[i] to low[i]; remove it when close is above its top, high is above its top plus zone size × wick tolerance / 100, or its touch count reaches the maximum. A touch is high at or above the selected midpoint or lower edge after prior high was below that level.
Calculation: MTF score = +1 for each bullish Dashboard trend on T2, T3, T4, and T5, and -1 for each bearish trend.
Long: Selected trend is bullish
Short: Selected trend is bearish
Long: Selected trend changes from bearish to bullish
Short: Selected trend changes from bullish to bearish
Long: Dashboard trend on T2 is bullish OR Dashboard trend on T3 is bullish OR Dashboard trend on T4 is bullish OR Dashboard trend on T5 is bullish
Short: Dashboard trend on T2 is bearish OR Dashboard trend on T3 is bearish OR Dashboard trend on T4 is bearish OR Dashboard trend on T5 is bearish
Long: MTF score > 0
Short: MTF score < 0
Long: MTF score is maximum AND its previous chart-bar value was below maximum
Short: MTF score is minimum AND its previous chart-bar value was above minimum
Long: An active bullish OB is touched when low is at or below its selected mitigation level AND prior low was above that level AND the chart bar is after the OB source candle
Short: An active bearish OB is touched when high is at or above its selected mitigation level AND prior high was below that level AND the chart bar is after the OB source candle
