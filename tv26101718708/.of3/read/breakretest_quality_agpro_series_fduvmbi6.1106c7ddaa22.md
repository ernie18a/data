Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), true range when ATR is unavailable, instrument minimum tick)
Calculation: Retest quality score = sum of the individually rounded weighted reclaim, rejection, depth, speed, volatility, and volume factors; clamp each normalized factor to the unit interval. Reclaim factor = side-adjusted close distance from the broken level / (safe ATR at break × reclaim ATR scale). Rejection factor = (side-specific wick / max(bar range, instrument minimum tick) + directional-candle bonus + body / max(bar range, instrument minimum tick) × body fraction weight) / rejection normalization. Depth factor = 1 − max(side-specific overshoot, 0) / (safe ATR at break × depth ATR scale). Speed factor = 1 − (bars since break − first eligible bar) / max(maximum retest bars − first eligible bar, minimum positive divisor). Volatility factor = 1 − max(bar range − safe ATR at break, 0) / (safe ATR at break × excess-range ATR scale). Volume factor = volume weight when volume confirmation is disabled; otherwise, volume weight × (volume / max(SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), volume baseline floor)) / relative-volume scale. Each factor has its own weight; the reclaim ATR scale, body fraction weight, directional-candle bonus, rejection normalization, depth ATR scale, excess-range ATR scale, minimum positive divisor, volume baseline floor, and relative-volume scale are separate parameters. Side-specific wick is the lower wick for long and upper wick for short; the directional-candle bonus applies when close is above open for long or below open for short. Side-specific overshoot is max(broken level − low, 0) for long or max(high − broken level, 0) for short.
