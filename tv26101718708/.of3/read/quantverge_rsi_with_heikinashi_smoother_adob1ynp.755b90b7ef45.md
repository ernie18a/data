Calculation: HA RSI close = mean(RSI(open, RSI lookback), RSI(high, RSI lookback), RSI(low, RSI lookback), RSI(close, RSI lookback)) on chart bars
Calculation: HA RSI open = mean(prior HA RSI open, prior HA RSI close) on chart bars; initialize to mean(RSI(open, RSI lookback), RSI(close, RSI lookback))
Calculation: HTF HA RSI close = mean(RSI(T1 open, same RSI lookback), RSI(T1 high, same RSI lookback), RSI(T1 low, same RSI lookback), RSI(T1 close, same RSI lookback)) on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars with lookahead off: the last completed T1 bar historically until its final chart bar, and the still-forming T1 bar in realtime
Long: HA RSI close crosses above HA RSI open AND HA RSI open <= RSI(low, RSI lookback) AND (strict mode disabled OR HA RSI close[1] < oversold level) AND (HTF filter disabled OR HTF HA RSI close > midline) AND (price action filter disabled OR close > open) AND at least cooldown bars have elapsed since the preceding signal in either direction
Short: HA RSI close crosses below HA RSI open AND HA RSI open >= RSI(high, RSI lookback) AND (strict mode disabled OR HA RSI close[1] > overbought level) AND (HTF filter disabled OR HTF HA RSI close < midline) AND (price action filter disabled OR close < open) AND at least cooldown bars have elapsed since the preceding signal in either direction
