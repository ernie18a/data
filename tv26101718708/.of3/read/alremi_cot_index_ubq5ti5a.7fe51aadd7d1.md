Calculation: net positions = long position data − short position data
Calculation: COT index = 100 × (net positions − lowest net positions over the lookback period) / (highest net positions over the lookback period − lowest net positions over the lookback period)
Long: commercial COT index ≥ upper threshold OR noncommercial COT index ≥ upper threshold OR nonreportable COT index ≥ upper threshold
Short: commercial COT index ≤ lower threshold OR noncommercial COT index ≤ lower threshold OR nonreportable COT index ≤ lower threshold
