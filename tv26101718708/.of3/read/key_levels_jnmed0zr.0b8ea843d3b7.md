Calculation: Battle Line = on chart bars, use the basis parameter to choose either the midpoint of max(current trading day’s premarket high, high[T1, last completed bar], close[T1, last completed bar]) and min(current trading day’s premarket low, low[T1, last completed bar], close[T1, last completed bar]), or the mean of those five levels; premarket high and low are the maximum high and minimum low of bars before the regular-session parameter
Long: crosses above Battle Line using close or high, as selected by the confirmation parameter, AND the chart bar is within the regular-session parameter AND Battle Line is available
Short: crosses below Battle Line using close or low, as selected by the confirmation parameter, AND the chart bar is within the regular-session parameter AND Battle Line is available
