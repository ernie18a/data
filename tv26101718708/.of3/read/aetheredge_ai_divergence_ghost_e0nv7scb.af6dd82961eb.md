Calculation: neural oscillator = weighted sum of z-score(RSI(close, RSI lookback), normalization lookback), z-score(MACD line(close, fast, slow, signal), normalization lookback), z-score(Stochastic(high, low, close, stochastic lookback), normalization lookback), z-score(MFI(hlc3, MFI lookback), normalization lookback), and z-score(CCI(close, CCI lookback), normalization lookback); z-score(x, n) = (x - SMA(x, n)) / stdev(x, n), or 0 when stdev is 0; each weight is max(0, correlation(z-scored indicator value lagged by correlation lag, (close - close[return lookback]) / close[return lookback], correlation lookback)), treating missing correlations as 0, normalized by their sum when adaptive weighting is enabled and the sum is positive, otherwise all weights are equal.
Calculation: divergence confidence = confidence blend weight * min(1, abs(latest price pivot - previous price pivot) / max(previous price pivot, price floor) * price scale + abs(latest oscillator pivot - previous oscillator pivot) * oscillator scale) + (1 - confidence blend weight) * proportion of normalized RSI, MACD line, stochastic, MFI, and CCI values that move in the divergence direction between the previous and latest price-pivot bars.
Long: on a chart bar confirming a price pivot low, the latest two price pivot lows are separated by at least the minimum pivot gap and at most the maximum pivot gap, and ((latest price pivot low < previous price pivot low AND the latest of the two most recent confirmed neural-oscillator pivot lows is greater than the previous one) OR (latest price pivot low > previous price pivot low AND the latest of the two most recent confirmed neural-oscillator pivot lows is less than the previous one)), and divergence confidence is at least the minimum confidence, and no prior bullish signal exists or the current bar index minus the prior bullish signal bar index is greater than the bullish cooldown.
Short: on a chart bar confirming a price pivot high, the latest two price pivot highs are separated by at least the minimum pivot gap and at most the maximum pivot gap, and ((latest price pivot high > previous price pivot high AND the latest of the two most recent confirmed neural-oscillator pivot highs is less than the previous one) OR (latest price pivot high < previous price pivot high AND the latest of the two most recent confirmed neural-oscillator pivot highs is greater than the previous one)), and divergence confidence is at least the minimum confidence, and no prior bearish signal exists or the current bar index minus the prior bearish signal bar index is greater than the bearish cooldown.
