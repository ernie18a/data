Calculation: opening-range high = maximum high of chart bars in the configured opening window, starting at the first chart bar of T1 or the configured session; freeze when the window ends
Calculation: opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars in the configured opening window, starting at the first chart bar of T1 or the configured session; freeze when the window ends
Calculation: setup strength(side) = clamp(round_to_increment(base points + body points + range points + volume points + momentum points + penetration points + RSI bonus + HTF bonus + opening-bias bonus, score increment), score floor, score ceiling), where body points use abs(close-open)/(high-low), or body fallback when high=low, with separate descending cutoffs and points for each tier; range points use (high-low)/ATR(ATR lookback), or zero when ATR is not positive, with separate descending cutoffs and points for each tier; volume points use volume/SMA(volume, volume lookback), or volume-ratio fallback when the SMA is not positive, with separate descending cutoffs and points for each tier; momentum points use close-close[short momentum lookback] and close-close[long momentum lookback], awarding separate points when both, either, or neither is aligned with side; penetration points use (close-opening-range high)/ATR for long or (opening-range low-close)/ATR for short, or zero when ATR is not positive, with separate descending cutoffs and points for each tier; RSI bonus applies when RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above the long RSI threshold for long or below the short RSI threshold for short; HTF bonus applies when the HTF filter is disabled or close is aligned with EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, last completed bar); opening-bias bonus applies when close is above the opening-window first bar open for long or below it for short
Calculation: risk distance = min(max(abs(entry price - raw stop), minimum ATR risk multiple * ATR(ATR lookback)), maximum ATR risk multiple * ATR(ATR lookback)), where raw stop is entry price minus/plus the ATR stop multiple * ATR for long/short in ATR mode, the opposite opening-range edge in opposite-edge mode, or the opening-range midpoint in midpoint mode
Long: opening range formed AND inside the selected trade window AND no open position AND (one-entry-per-T1-period cap disabled OR no earlier entry in this T1 period) AND (close > opening-range high in close-trigger mode OR high > opening-range high in wick-trigger mode) AND NOT (close < opening-range low in close-trigger mode OR low < opening-range low in wick-trigger mode) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range size is between the minimum and maximum configured percentages of SMA(high-low, ADR lookback, T1, last completed bar at opening-window start)) AND setup strength(long) >= minimum strength AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available
Short: opening range formed AND inside the selected trade window AND no open position AND (one-entry-per-T1-period cap disabled OR no earlier entry in this T1 period) AND (close < opening-range low in close-trigger mode OR low < opening-range low in wick-trigger mode) AND NOT (close > opening-range high in close-trigger mode OR high > opening-range high in wick-trigger mode) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range size is between the minimum and maximum configured percentages of SMA(high-low, ADR lookback, T1, last completed bar at opening-window start)) AND setup strength(short) >= minimum strength AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= entry price - risk distance, or entry price if the first target was reached before this bar and break-even-after-first-target is enabled
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + risk distance, or entry price if the first target was reached before this bar and break-even-after-first-target is enabled
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high reaches entry price + risk distance * first target R multiple for the first time and the current stop is not hit on that bar
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low reaches entry price - risk distance * first target R multiple for the first time and the current stop is not hit on that bar
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high reaches entry price + risk distance * second target R multiple for the first time and the current stop is not hit on that bar
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low reaches entry price - risk distance * second target R multiple for the first time and the current stop is not hit on that bar
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high reaches entry price + risk distance * third target R multiple and the current stop is not hit on that bar
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low reaches entry price - risk distance * third target R multiple and the current stop is not hit on that bar
Exit: long, after the entry bar, on the first bar of the next T1 period in auto mode or the first bar outside the configured trade window in custom mode, unless the current stop or third target is hit on that bar
Exit: short, after the entry bar, on the first bar of the next T1 period in auto mode or the first bar outside the configured trade window in custom mode, unless the current stop or third target is hit on that bar
