Calculation: channel = volume-weighted polynomial regression of selected source against normalized bar age from selected anchor, using weight one when volume is absent or nonpositive; center is the current fitted value and residual sigma is the square root of weighted residual sum of squares divided by total weight. Anchor is the rolling-window start, selected swing-low pivot, selected swing-high pivot, or the more recent swing pivot; fall back to the rolling-window start when a pivot is unavailable. Inner and outer bands are center plus or minus residual sigma times their separate multipliers.
Calculation: multi-factor extreme counts = z-score each active factor over the normalization lookback, using zero when its standard deviation is zero, then count scores at or above the positive extreme threshold and at or below its negative. Factors are source minus session VWAP; z-scored selected-range magnitude signed by source change over its lookback (zero sign treated as positive); efficiency ratio (net source change divided by summed absolute one-bar changes); source minus reference-instrument close; signed clipped Parabolic SAR extension divided by ATR; selected RSI or Stoch RSI; source minus its linear regression divided by ATR; KST; and cumulative volume times close-location value minus its EMA, where close-location value is zero for a zero-range bar. Use only selected and available factors; VWAP and order flow require volume, and basis requires a valid nonzero reference close. The required count is the selected manual threshold or the timeframe-adaptive threshold for the chart timeframe, capped at the active-factor count; no consensus qualifies when no factors are active.
Calculation: reversion score = max(0, -Correlation(close minus SMA(close, mean lookback) on the prior bar, current close minus prior-bar close, correlation lookback)).
