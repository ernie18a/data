Long: An unbroken resistance trendline is crossed upward (close > current trendline price AND close[1] <= prior trendline price) AND confirmation passes (confirmation mode selects close-only OR selects volume confirmation and (SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) <= 0 OR volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) >= volume threshold) OR selects impulse confirmation and abs(close - current trendline price) / ATR(ATR lookback) >= impulse threshold) OR a prior upward resistance trendline break has not yet been retested, bars since break are between the retest minimum and maximum, abs(close - current trendline price) <= ATR(ATR lookback) * retest tolerance, and close > current trendline price.
