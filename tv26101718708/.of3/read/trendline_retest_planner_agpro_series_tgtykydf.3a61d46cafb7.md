Calculation: safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR length), true range, minimum price increment)
Calculation: active retest line = on each chart bar, form support through the latest two confirmed pivot lows and resistance through the latest two confirmed pivot highs, using pivot length for both pivot sides; each line is valid while its newer anchor is no older than maximum trendline age. In manual mode select the specified side. In auto mode, a support candidate has close >= support line - safe ATR × invalidation distance and a resistance candidate has close <= resistance line + safe ATR × invalidation distance; select support if both are candidates and its absolute close-to-line distance is no greater than resistance's, or if resistance is not a candidate and support exists; otherwise select resistance. The selected line is active only if it exists; project it through the current chart bar.
Calculation: high-normalized score(x, weak, strong) = clamp((x - weak) / (strong - weak) × score scale, score floor, score ceiling)
Calculation: low-normalized score(x, good, bad) = clamp((bad - x) / (bad - good) × score scale, score floor, score ceiling)
Calculation: peak score(x, low, ideal, high) = high-normalized score(x, low, ideal) if x <= ideal, otherwise low-normalized score(x, ideal, high)
Calculation: retest score = min(raw score, invalidation cap if invalidated, otherwise distance cap if neither near the line nor touched recently), where raw score is age weight × peak score(active line age, age lower bound, min(maximum trendline age × age ideal factor, age ideal cap), maximum trendline age) + touch weight × high-normalized score(min(touch count, required touches + two anchor pivots), two anchor pivots, required touches) + depth weight × (if touched recently, peak score(probe depth in ATR, zero, ideal probe depth, maximum probe depth), otherwise low-normalized score(absolute close-to-line distance in ATR, zero, activation distance × sensitivity multiplier)) + close-response weight × high-normalized score(defended-side close response in ATR, negative pocket width, close-response threshold) + volume weight × (if volume support is enabled, high-normalized score(volume / SMA(volume, volume baseline length), weak relative volume, strong relative volume) or one when the baseline is not positive; otherwise neutral volume score). Touch count is two anchor pivots plus each bar after the newer anchor in the inclusive touch-count lookback whose high-low range intersects the ATR-scaled pocket and whose close is within the ATR-scaled invalidation distance on the defended side. Probe depth is max((line - low) / safe ATR, zero) for support or max((high - line) / safe ATR, zero) for resistance. Defended-side close response is (close - line) / safe ATR for support or (line - close) / safe ATR for resistance. Near means absolute close-to-line distance <= safe ATR × activation distance × sensitivity multiplier. Touched recently means a pocket touch occurred within the response window since the active line or side last changed. Pocket touch means high >= line - safe ATR × pocket width × sensitivity multiplier and low <= line + safe ATR × pocket width × sensitivity multiplier. Invalidation means close < line - safe ATR × invalidation distance for support or close > line + safe ATR × invalidation distance for resistance. The distance cap applies only when not near and not touched recently; the invalidation cap overrides it.
Calculation: retest states = READY when an active line was touched recently, retest score >= READY threshold, close is on the defended side of the line, and the line is not invalidated; MONITOR when touched recently, retest score >= MONITOR threshold, not READY, and not invalidated; failing when touched recently, not invalidated, and either close is not on the defended side or retest score < MONITOR threshold; follow-through when touched recently, retest score >= MONITOR threshold, not invalidated, and close >= line + safe ATR × follow-through distance for support or close <= line - safe ATR × follow-through distance for resistance.
Calculation: new event(state) = (live updating selected or current chart bar confirmed) AND state AND NOT state[1]
Long: active resistance line AND (new event(pocket touch) OR new event(READY) OR new event(MONITOR) OR new event(follow-through) OR new event(failing) OR new event(invalidation))
Short: active support line AND (new event(pocket touch) OR new event(READY) OR new event(MONITOR) OR new event(follow-through) OR new event(failing) OR new event(invalidation))
