Calculation: Initial Balance high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range session, fixed when that session ends
Calculation: Previous T1 high/low = highest high and lowest low of the previous completed T1 period
Calculation: Previous session high/low = highest high and lowest low of the previous configured trading session
Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured premarket session
Calculation: Overnight low = lowest low during the configured overnight session
Calculation: Initial Balance size in ticks = round((Initial Balance high - Initial Balance low) / instrument tick size)
Calculation: Long stop = the latest confirmed pivot low after the latest confirmed pivot high above Initial Balance high since Initial Balance completion, or the lowest low from retest through entry if no such pivot low exists, offset below the reference by the configured point or percentage amount
Calculation: Short stop = the latest confirmed pivot high after the latest confirmed pivot low below Initial Balance low since Initial Balance completion, or the highest high from retest through entry if no such pivot high exists, offset above the reference by the configured point or percentage amount
Long: Initial Balance complete AND a chart-bar high has exceeded Initial Balance high AND on a later chart bar low <= Initial Balance high and close < SMA(configured source, trigger lookback) AND on a later chart bar close > Initial Balance high and SMA(configured source, trigger lookback) AND long entries enabled AND no earlier entry this session AND current time before the configured entry cutoff and flat time in the configured session timezone AND day of week not configured as skipped AND (IB-size filter disabled OR Initial Balance size in ticks <= configured maximum) AND (breach invalidation disabled OR the current breakout has not been canceled by a low < Initial Balance low) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected SMA/EMA/WMA/VWMA(close, its lookback, T5) is available and chart close is above it; calculate the MA on T5 bars and sample it on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming T5 bar in real time and the prior completed T5 bar historically until its final chart bar) AND for each enabled location filter with an available level, chart close is above that level: previous T1 high, previous T1 low, previous session high, previous session low, premarket high, premarket low, overnight low, SMA(close, its independent lookback, T2), SMA(close, its independent lookback, T3), or the shorter-lookback or longer-lookback SMA(close, respective independent lookback, T1, T2, T3, or T4); calculate each MTF SMA on its timeframe bars and sample it on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming timeframe bar in real time and the prior completed timeframe bar historically until its final chart bar
Short: Initial Balance complete AND a chart-bar low has fallen below Initial Balance low AND on a later chart bar high >= Initial Balance low and close > SMA(configured source, trigger lookback) AND on a later chart bar close < Initial Balance low and SMA(configured source, trigger lookback) AND short entries enabled AND no earlier entry this session AND current time before the configured entry cutoff and flat time in the configured session timezone AND day of week not configured as skipped AND (IB-size filter disabled OR Initial Balance size in ticks <= configured maximum) AND (breach invalidation disabled OR the current breakout has not been canceled by a high > Initial Balance high) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR selected SMA/EMA/WMA/VWMA(close, its lookback, T5) is available and chart close is below it; calculate the MA on T5 bars and sample it on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming T5 bar in real time and the prior completed T5 bar historically until its final chart bar) AND for each enabled location filter with an available level, chart close is below that level: previous T1 high, previous T1 low, previous session high, previous session low, premarket high, premarket low, overnight low, SMA(close, its independent lookback, T2), SMA(close, its independent lookback, T3), or the shorter-lookback or longer-lookback SMA(close, respective independent lookback, T1, T2, T3, or T4); calculate each MTF SMA on its timeframe bars and sample it on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming timeframe bar in real time and the prior completed timeframe bar historically until its final chart bar
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + an enabled target's configured R-multiple * (entry price - long stop)
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price - an enabled target's configured R-multiple * (short stop - entry price)
Exit: Long, highest high since entry >= entry price + configured break-even trigger R-multiple * (entry price - long stop) AND low <= entry price
Exit: Short, lowest low since entry <= entry price - configured break-even trigger R-multiple * (short stop - entry price) AND high >= entry price
Exit: Long, current time >= configured flat time in the configured session timezone
Exit: Short, current time >= configured flat time in the configured session timezone
