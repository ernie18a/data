Calculation: relative-strength ratio = chart close divided by the valid benchmark close on chart bars; if the current benchmark close is unavailable or nonpositive, use its previous valid close, or chart close if none exists; use 1 if the resulting denominator is nonpositive.
Calculation: support buffer = support ATR multiplier × (ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1 bars, current forming bar), or, if unavailable, max(ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, chart bars, current bar), chart close × ATR fallback fraction)); map the T1 value to chart bars.
Calculation: opening-range high and low = benchmark high and low of the first T4 bar of each session; use on chart bars after that T4 bar completes.
Calculation: entry trigger = trigger-bar high rounded up to the instrument tick.
Calculation: initial stop = trigger-bar low rounded down to the instrument tick.
Calculation: setup risk distance = max(entry trigger − initial stop, minimum setup risk distance); after fill, risk distance = max(actual fill price − initial stop, minimum fill risk distance).
Calculation: eligible MA resistance = the lowest selected available chart EMA or SMA whose value is above entry trigger or above chart close.
Calculation: structural resistance = the short-lookback T1 highest high if above entry trigger; otherwise the long-lookback T1 highest high if above entry trigger; otherwise none; use the all-time high if the long-lookback high is unavailable.
Calculation: nearest resistance = the lower of eligible MA resistance and structural resistance when both exist, or the one that exists.
Calculation: first target = nearest-tick-rounded entry reference + first-target R multiple × target risk; use entry trigger and entry trigger − initial stop before fill, then actual fill price and max(actual fill price − initial stop, minimum fill risk distance) after fill.
Calculation: second target = nearest-tick-rounded max(target anchor, entry reference + minimum runner R multiple × target risk); target anchor selects the short-lookback T1 highest high above entry reference, else the long-lookback T1 highest high above entry reference, else entry reference + fallback R multiple × target risk; before fill, cap the anchor at eligible MA resistance when enabled and available; after fill, recalculate without that cap.
Calculation: runner stop = the greater of actual fill price and the highest tick-floored value, on chart bars strictly after the first-target bar, of the selected chart EMA or last-completed T1 EMA.

Long: relative-strength ratio > Highest(relative-strength ratio on prior chart bars, RS lookback) AND chart high > Highest(chart high on prior chart bars, RS lookback).
Long: relative-strength ratio >= its previous chart-bar value.
Short: relative-strength ratio < its previous chart-bar value.
Long: chart close >= EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar) AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar) >= EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar).
Short: chart close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar) OR EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar).
Long: benchmark close on T4 bars, current forming bar, > opening-range high after the first T4 bar completes.
Short: benchmark close on T4 bars, current forming bar, < opening-range low after the first T4 bar completes.
Long: benchmark close on T2 bars, current forming bar, > its previous T2 close.
Short: benchmark close on T2 bars, current forming bar, <= its previous T2 close.
Long: (benchmark close on T4 bars > opening-range high OR established session spread AND range position >= upper range-position threshold) AND (benchmark close on T4 bars >= benchmark VWAP calculated on T3 bars, current forming bar, OR that VWAP is unavailable); established session spread means T4 session high − session low >= max(benchmark close on T1 bars, current forming bar × minimum spread fraction, instrument tick); range position = clamp((benchmark T4 close − T4 session low) / max(T4 session high − T4 session low, minimum day-range floor) × 100, 0, 100).
Long: on the configured entry chart, during the regular session and not its final bar, while flat with no setup pending and not on the bar immediately after an exit; EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar) >= EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar) × (1 − trend tolerance); and at least one of: (a) chart low <= the shorter T1 EMA + upper support-band multiplier × support buffer AND (chart low >= the shorter T1 EMA − support buffer OR chart close >= the shorter T1 EMA − support buffer); (b) chart low <= the longer T1 EMA + upper support-band multiplier × support buffer AND ((chart low >= the longer T1 EMA − support buffer AND (chart low < the shorter T1 EMA OR shorter T1 EMA <= longer T1 EMA)) OR (chart low < the longer T1 EMA − support buffer AND chart close >= the longer T1 EMA − support buffer)); (c) chart close is between min(shorter T1 EMA, longer T1 EMA) − support buffer and max(shorter T1 EMA, longer T1 EMA) + support buffer; (d) session low < previous completed T1 low AND chart close >= that low AND abs(chart close − that low) <= reclaim proximity multiplier × support buffer; AND chart close > chart open; AND, if the volume-dry-up filter is enabled, either current T1 close >= current T1 open or current T1 volume <= SMA(volume, dry-up lookback, T1 bars, current forming bar) × dry-up volume multiplier; AND chart close is not below any selected chart EMA or SMA; AND, if the minimum reward-risk filter is enabled, nearest resistance is absent or nearest resistance − entry trigger >= minimum reward-risk multiple × setup risk distance; AND, if the setup-count filter is enabled, setup count is below its cap, counting since the latest new short-lookback T1 high or eligible longer-T1-EMA retest reset (a retest after the configured away interval or a session-start gap beyond the support buffer, at most once per session).
Exit: long, first target is reached for the first time during the position (chart high >= first target).
Exit: long, chart high >= second target OR (first target has not previously been reached and chart high < first target AND chart low <= initial stop) OR (first target was reached previously or chart high >= first target, AND ((bar is after fill and chart open <= stop held at bar start) OR (chart close < stop after target and runner-stop updates AND trailing checks are allowed, including the configured opening-bar skip)) OR end-of-session expiration is enabled and this is the final session bar.
