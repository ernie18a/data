Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick size)
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), or one if that average is unavailable or nonpositive
Calculation: Prior close = close[1], or current close if unavailable; prior impulse high/low = highest(high, impulse lookback)[1] and lowest(low, impulse lookback)[1], or current high/low if unavailable
Calculation: Engine is warm when bar index exceeds max(slowest EMA lookback, impulse lookback, relative-volume lookback, ATR lookback) + max(slope lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) + warm-up offset
Calculation: Mid-EMA slope in ATR = (EMA(close, middle EMA lookback) - EMA(close, middle EMA lookback)[slope lookback]) / safe ATR
Calculation: Confirmed bullish trend = engine is warm AND EMA(close, shortest lookback) > EMA(close, intermediate lookback) > EMA(close, longest lookback) AND close > EMA(close, intermediate lookback) AND mid-EMA slope in ATR >= minimum EMA slope AND ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) >= minimum ADX AND +DI >= -DI
Calculation: Confirmed bearish trend = engine is warm AND EMA(close, shortest lookback) < EMA(close, intermediate lookback) < EMA(close, longest lookback) AND close < EMA(close, intermediate lookback) AND mid-EMA slope in ATR <= -minimum EMA slope AND ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) >= minimum ADX AND -DI >= +DI
Calculation: Provisional bullish trend = NOT confirmed bullish trend AND EMA(close, shortest lookback) > EMA(close, longest lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longest lookback) AND mid-EMA slope in ATR > -minimum EMA slope AND +DI >= -DI × bullish-watch DI factor
Calculation: Provisional bearish trend = NOT confirmed bearish trend AND EMA(close, shortest lookback) < EMA(close, longest lookback) AND close < EMA(close, longest lookback) AND mid-EMA slope in ATR < minimum EMA slope AND -DI >= +DI × bearish-watch DI factor
Calculation: Effective preferred pullback RVOL = min(preferred pullback RVOL parameter, weak dry-up RVOL parameter - RVOL separation parameter); effective weak dry-up RVOL = max(weak dry-up RVOL parameter, effective preferred pullback RVOL + RVOL separation parameter); start RVOL ceiling = max(start RVOL parameter, effective preferred pullback RVOL)
Calculation: Pullback pocket state = while inactive, start a bullish pocket when confirmed or provisional bullish trend, low <= EMA(close, shortest lookback) + EMA-lane ATR tolerance × safe ATR OR low <= EMA(close, intermediate lookback) + EMA-lane ATR tolerance × safe ATR, close > EMA(close, longest lookback), relative volume <= start RVOL ceiling, and close <= max(open, prior close); start a bearish pocket when confirmed or provisional bearish trend, high >= EMA(close, shortest lookback) - EMA-lane ATR tolerance × safe ATR OR high >= EMA(close, intermediate lookback) - EMA-lane ATR tolerance × safe ATR, close < EMA(close, longest lookback), relative volume <= start RVOL ceiling, and close >= min(open, prior close); initialize its bar count to one, top/bottom to the starting high/low, anchor high/low to max(prior impulse high, starting high)/min(prior impulse low, starting low), and RVOL sum to starting relative volume; on each later active bar increment the count and RVOL sum and extend the top/bottom to the bar's extremes; clear the pocket after a trigger, or when depth exceeds the hard depth limit, close crosses its invalidation level, close crosses the longest-lookback EMA against the pocket, or count exceeds maximum pullback bars, with a trigger taking precedence over invalidation
Calculation: Average pullback RVOL = pullback RVOL sum / pullback bar count
Calculation: Pullback depth = for a bullish pocket, (anchor high - pocket bottom) / max(anchor high - anchor low, minimum tick size) × percentage scale; for a bearish pocket, (pocket top - anchor low) / max(anchor high - anchor low, minimum tick size) × percentage scale
Calculation: Bullish trigger level = pocket top + trigger ATR buffer × safe ATR; bearish trigger level = pocket bottom - trigger ATR buffer × safe ATR
Calculation: Bullish invalidation level = pocket bottom - invalidation ATR buffer × safe ATR; bearish invalidation level = pocket top + invalidation ATR buffer × safe ATR
Calculation: Pullback invalidation = depth exceeds hard depth limit OR bullish close < bullish invalidation level OR bearish close > bearish invalidation level OR bullish close < EMA(close, longest lookback) OR bearish close > EMA(close, longest lookback) OR pullback bar count > maximum pullback bars
Calculation: Dry-up score = maximum score at or below effective preferred pullback RVOL, zero at or above effective weak dry-up RVOL, and linear interpolation between them
Calculation: Depth score = maximum score at or below preferred depth limit, zero at or above hard depth limit, and linear interpolation between them
Calculation: Duration score = subminimum duration factor × pullback bar count / minimum pullback bars below the minimum; maximum score from the minimum through maximum pullback bars; zero above the maximum
Calculation: Trend slope score = clamp(abs(mid-EMA slope in ATR) / max(minimum EMA slope × slope-score factor, slope-score floor) × score scale, zero, score scale); ADX score = clamp((ADX - minimum ADX) / max(minimum ADX, ADX-score floor) × score scale, zero, score scale)
Calculation: Trend score = zero without a confirmed or provisional trend; otherwise trend slope score × slope-score weight + ADX score × ADX-score weight + confirmed-trend bonus or provisional-trend bonus
Calculation: Trigger pressure score = clamp(score scale - max(distance from close to the pocket-side trigger level, zero) / safe ATR × trigger-pressure factor, zero, score scale)
Calculation: Trigger quality score = trend score × trend-quality weight + dry-up score × dry-up-quality weight + depth score × depth-quality weight + duration score × duration-quality weight + trigger pressure score × trigger-pressure-quality weight
Long: active bullish pocket AND pullback bar count >= minimum pullback bars AND dry-up score >= minimum dry-up score AND pullback depth <= trigger depth limit AND trigger quality score >= minimum trigger quality AND relative volume >= average pullback RVOL × recovery RVOL ratio AND close > bullish trigger level AND close > open AND (close-confirmation parameter disabled OR bar confirmed)
Short: active bearish pocket AND pullback bar count >= minimum pullback bars AND dry-up score >= minimum dry-up score AND pullback depth <= trigger depth limit AND trigger quality score >= minimum trigger quality AND relative volume >= average pullback RVOL × recovery RVOL ratio AND close < bearish trigger level AND close < open AND (close-confirmation parameter disabled OR bar confirmed)
Long: active bullish pocket AND pullback bar count >= minimum pullback bars AND dry-up score >= minimum dry-up score AND pullback depth <= hard depth limit AND trigger quality score >= minimum watch quality AND no trigger or invalidation on this bar AND no watch mark yet in this pocket AND same-side watch cooldown elapsed
