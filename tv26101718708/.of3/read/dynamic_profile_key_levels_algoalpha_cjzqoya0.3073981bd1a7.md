Calculation: Volume-profile levels = Volume Profile(high, low, volume, lookback mode and limit, dynamic pivot period, resolution, value-area percentage, node-smoothing radius); node zones use the zone-radius parameter.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)[1], when volume is available and the prior SMA is greater than zero.
Long: On a confirmed bar after level creation, an active enabled volume-profile peak, trough, or ranked high/low-volume row (or an enabled fixed POC, VAH, or VAL when signal scope includes all levels) closes above its top after its remembered nonzero side was below its bottom (break), OR a recorded touch (high >= bottom AND low <= top) captured an above side and the first close outside afterward is above the top, without a break or retest on that bar (rejection), OR within the retest-window parameter after a prior upward break, the bar touches the level/zone (high >= bottom AND low <= top) and closes above the top without a break on that bar (first successful retest); the level has not been stopped by the stop-on-break setting or expired at the level-expiry parameter, and minimum relative volume is zero or relative volume is defined and >= that parameter, and at least the signal-cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the last accepted signal on that level.
Short: On a confirmed bar after level creation, an active enabled volume-profile peak, trough, or ranked high/low-volume row (or an enabled fixed POC, VAH, or VAL when signal scope includes all levels) closes below its bottom after its remembered nonzero side was above its top (break), OR a recorded touch (high >= bottom AND low <= top) captured a below side and the first close outside afterward is below the bottom, without a break or retest on that bar (rejection), OR within the retest-window parameter after a prior downward break, the bar touches the level/zone (high >= bottom AND low <= top) and closes below the bottom without a break on that bar (first successful retest); the level has not been stopped by the stop-on-break setting or expired at the level-expiry parameter, and minimum relative volume is zero or relative volume is defined and >= that parameter, and at least the signal-cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the last accepted signal on that level.
