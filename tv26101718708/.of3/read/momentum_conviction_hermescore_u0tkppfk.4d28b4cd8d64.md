The shorter EMA and MACD lookbacks are less than their respective longer lookbacks. Each check has its own enable parameter.
Long: on confirmed chart-bar close, EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback) > 0 AND Conviction score(long) >= minimum score
