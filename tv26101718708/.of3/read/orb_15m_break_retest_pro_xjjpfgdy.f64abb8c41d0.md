Calculation: opening-range high = highest high of chart bars in the configured opening-range window
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening-range window
Calculation: opening-range close position = (close of the last opening-range bar - opening-range low) / (opening-range high - opening-range low), using 1 as the denominator when opening-range high equals opening-range low
Long: while flat with no pending setup, close > opening-range high + acceptance distance AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND (T1 EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1 current still-forming bar), sampled on chart bars) AND (opening-range close filter disabled OR opening-range close position >= bearish cutoff) AND entry gates are satisfied; within the retest-bar limit from that breakout, low <= breakout opening-range high + retest tolerance AND close > breakout opening-range high AND (re-entry invalidation disabled OR no intervening close < breakout opening-range high)
Short: while flat with no pending setup, close < opening-range low - acceptance distance AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND (T1 EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1 current still-forming bar), sampled on chart bars) AND (opening-range close filter disabled OR opening-range close position <= bullish cutoff) AND entry gates are satisfied; within the retest-bar limit from that breakout, high >= breakout opening-range low - retest tolerance AND close < breakout opening-range low AND (re-entry invalidation disabled OR no intervening close > breakout opening-range low)
Long: flat AND a valid long breakout setup is pending within its retest-bar limit and has not been invalidated AND low <= breakout opening-range high + retest tolerance AND close > breakout opening-range high AND entry gates are satisfied now AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND (T1 EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1 current still-forming bar), sampled on chart bars) AND (opening-range close filter disabled OR opening-range close position >= bearish cutoff)
Short: flat AND a valid short breakout setup is pending within its retest-bar limit and has not been invalidated AND high >= breakout opening-range low - retest tolerance AND close < breakout opening-range low AND entry gates are satisfied now AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND (T1 EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1 current still-forming bar), sampled on chart bars) AND (opening-range close filter disabled OR opening-range close position <= bullish cutoff)
Exit: Long, low <= (if break-even is enabled and current high >= entry + break-even trigger ticks, entry + break-even offset ticks; otherwise entry - chart-timeframe-selected stop-distance ticks) OR (trailing disabled OR take-profit-with-trailing enabled) AND high >= entry + chart-timeframe-selected target-distance ticks OR trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND low <= highest high since activation - trailing offset
Exit: Short, high >= (if break-even is enabled and current low <= entry - break-even trigger ticks, entry - break-even offset ticks; otherwise entry + chart-timeframe-selected stop-distance ticks) OR (trailing disabled OR take-profit-with-trailing enabled) AND low <= entry - chart-timeframe-selected target-distance ticks OR trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND high >= lowest low since activation + trailing offset
Entry gates: opening range ready AND current chart bar is inside an enabled configured trade session in the configured time zone AND (opening-range width filter disabled OR minimum width ticks <= (opening-range high - opening-range low) / tick size <= maximum width ticks) AND trades today < daily trade limit AND (one-trade-per-session restriction disabled OR no trade has occurred this session) AND (news filter disabled OR current time is outside every configured news-time blackout interval)
