Calculation: Trend ratios = (close[1] / EMA(close, chart-trend base lookback × max(1, seconds per day ÷ chart-bar duration))[1] − 1) × 100, and the same formula for each auxiliary close series using a shared, shorter market base lookback; EMAs run on chart bars, and all ratios use the prior completed chart bar.
Calculation: Feature vector = clipped Z-scores of the one-bar, medium-lookback, and long-lookback log returns; centered and scaled RSI; Z-score of (close − EMA(close, feature base lookback × max(1, seconds per day ÷ chart-bar duration))) / ATR; volume; ATR / close; both auxiliary-series trend ratios; and the chart trend ratio. Also include clipped interactions of the one-bar return Z-score with volume Z-score, medium-lookback return Z-score with ATR / close Z-score, and the first auxiliary-series Z-score with medium-lookback return Z-score, each divided by a shared interaction scale. Use a shared normalization lookback and feature clipping bound; the return lookbacks are ordered short, medium, long.
Calculation: Model probability = sigmoid(clamp(bias + sum(weight × corresponding feature), ±score cap)). Initialize bias and weights to zero. On each confirmed chart bar, train on the configured number of samples, using feature vectors from the prediction horizon plus each sample offset bars ago and labels indicating whether the later close exceeds the earlier close. For each sample, subtract learning rate × error from the bias and subtract learning rate × (error × feature + regularization × weight) from each weight, where error is predicted probability minus label. Increment the training count once per chart bar; make the model probability available only after the minimum training count.
Calculation: Reliable model = model probability when the training count meets its minimum and the moving average over the reliability lookback of resolved hits meets the minimum hit rate; a resolved hit compares whether the model probability at the prediction horizon ago exceeded 0.5 with whether the current close exceeds the close at that horizon ago. Otherwise use the fallback probability: neutral probability plus fallback scale times the mean of chart-trend direction twice, auxiliary-series A direction, and auxiliary-series B direction, where chart-trend direction is positive when its ratio is above zero, auxiliary A direction is positive when its ratio is below zero, and auxiliary B direction is positive when its ratio is above zero.
Calculation: Final probability = reliable model probability, optionally blended with a sensing probability using the sensing weight; sensing probability is sigmoid of sensing scale times the mean of the clipped one-bar return Z-score and its interaction with volume Z-score divided by the interaction scale.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, position is not long, bars since the last position change meet the minimum hold, final probability is at least the long-entry threshold, absolute one-bar return Z-score is below the anomaly threshold, volume Z-score is below the anomaly threshold, ATR / close Z-score is below the anomaly threshold, and the trend filter is disabled or the chart trend ratio is above zero.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, position is not short, bars since the last position change meet the minimum hold, final probability is at most the short-entry threshold, absolute one-bar return Z-score is below the anomaly threshold, volume Z-score is below the anomaly threshold, ATR / close Z-score is below the anomaly threshold, and the trend filter is disabled or the chart trend ratio is at most zero.
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after the minimum hold, final probability is below the long-exit threshold and the short-entry conditions do not hold.
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after the minimum hold, final probability is above the short-exit threshold and the long-entry conditions do not hold.
