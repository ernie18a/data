Long: long side is enabled AND close crosses above highest(high, chart bars in the session-opening interval) AND the chart bar is confirmed
Short: short side is enabled AND close crosses below lowest(low, chart bars in the session-opening interval) AND the chart bar is confirmed
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar at entry) * ATR multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar at entry) * ATR multiplier OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage parameter) OR high >= entry price * (1 + profit percentage parameter) OR chart bar is confirmed at the intraday exit-time parameter
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar at entry) * ATR multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar at entry) * ATR multiplier OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage parameter) OR low <= entry price * (1 - profit percentage parameter) OR chart bar is confirmed at the intraday exit-time parameter
