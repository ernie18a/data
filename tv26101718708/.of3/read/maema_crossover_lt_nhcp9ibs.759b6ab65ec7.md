Calculation: Long moving average = SMA(HLC3, longer lookback, T1, still-forming T1 value sampled on chart bars with lookahead on)
Calculation: Short moving average = EMA(HLC3, shorter lookback, T1, still-forming T1 value sampled on chart bars with lookahead on)
Calculation: Relative MA gap = absolute value of (long moving average − short moving average) divided by long moving average
Calculation: Entry price = OHLC4 on the entry bar
Calculation: ATR amount = ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) × shared ATR multiple
Long: long entries enabled AND ((long moving average on the previous chart bar > short moving average on the previous chart bar AND short moving average crosses above long moving average) OR (long moving average on the previous chart bar <= short moving average on the previous chart bar AND relative MA gap > gap threshold AND close crosses above short moving average))
Short: short entries enabled AND ((long moving average on the previous chart bar < short moving average on the previous chart bar AND short moving average crosses below long moving average) OR (long moving average on the previous chart bar >= short moving average on the previous chart bar AND relative MA gap > gap threshold AND close crosses below short moving average))
Exit: Long, (dynamic-zone stop enabled AND OHLC4 < max(nearest-rank percentile(close, shared zone lookback, upper percentile parameter, chart bars) at entry, long moving average at entry)) OR (dynamic-zone stop disabled AND OHLC4 < short moving average at entry − stop ATR fraction × ATR amount at entry) OR (High > max(entry price × (1 + long profit percentage / 100), long moving average at entry + ATR amount at entry)); stop and target levels are set at entry and held while the position is open
Exit: Short, (dynamic-zone stop enabled AND OHLC4 > min(nearest-rank percentile(close, shared zone lookback, 100 minus lower percentile parameter, chart bars) at entry, long moving average at entry)) OR (dynamic-zone stop disabled AND OHLC4 > short moving average at entry + stop ATR fraction × ATR amount at entry) OR (Low < min(entry price × (1 − short profit percentage / 100), long moving average at entry − ATR amount at entry)); stop and target levels are set at entry and held while the position is open
