Calculation: relative strength ratio = chart close / benchmark close on chart bars
Long: on chart bars, current/forming T1 close > SMA(T1 close, T1 SMA lookback) AND current/forming T1 SMA > its value on the previous completed T1 bar AND current/forming T1 RSI(T1 close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND benchmark close exists and is > 0 AND relative strength ratio > SMA(relative strength ratio, relative strength lookback) AND ROC(relative strength ratio, relative strength lookback) > 0 AND chart high > highest(chart high[1], swing lookback) AND chart low > lowest(chart low[1], swing lookback); T1 values are calculated on T1 bars and used on chart bars
