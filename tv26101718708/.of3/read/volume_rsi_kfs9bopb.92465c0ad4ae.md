Calculation: Volume Power = selected smoothing (identity, SMA, EMA, RMA, HMA, or WMA; smoothing lookback) of RMA(clamp(scaled volume strength, lower scale cap, upper scale cap), rolling RMA lookback), where raw volume strength = (high > low ? (close − open) / (high − low) : 0) × ((volume > 0 ? volume^(1 − volume boost) : 0) × volume factor + (1 − volume factor)); scaling lookback = scaling-days lookback × bars per day; bars per day = max(minimum bar count, round(session seconds per day / chart-bar seconds)) when chart-bar seconds is positive, otherwise fallback bars per day; scaled volume strength = scale multiplier × raw volume strength / RMA(abs(raw volume strength), scaling lookback) when that RMA is positive, otherwise 0.
Long: On confirmed chart-bar close, RSI(close, RSI lookback) is defined AND (Volume Power ≥ long threshold OR (Volume Power < long threshold AND Volume Power > short threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) ≥ RSI midline))
Short: On confirmed chart-bar close, RSI(close, RSI lookback) is defined AND (Volume Power ≤ short threshold OR (Volume Power < long threshold AND Volume Power > short threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midline))
