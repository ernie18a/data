Calculation: Adaptive A-drop threshold = if adaptive mode is enabled and the qualifying-drop history has at least its minimum sample count, clamp(the upper median of the latest history-window qualifying drops × the adaptive factor, the adaptive floor, the adaptive ceiling); otherwise use the selected preset/custom minimum A-drop percentage. A qualifying drop is the percentage decline from a confirmed pivot high to its following confirmed pivot low, positive and below the maximum history-drop percentage.
Calculation: Qualifying ABC = after cooldown, the latest five alternating confirmed pivots are low P1, high P2, low P3, high P4, low P5; consecutive same-type pivots are collapsed to the more extreme pivot. (P2 − P3) / P2 meets the adaptive A-drop threshold; (P4 − P3) / (P2 − P3) is at most the selected preset/custom maximum B-retracement percentage; P4 < P2; and no pivot high among the preceding pivot records selected by the impulse-lookback parameter is >= P2. If deep C is disallowed, P5 >= P3 × (1 − the allowed C-penetration fraction).
Calculation: Profile POC = the center of the lowest-price row with the greatest accumulated volume, using the selected number of equal-price rows spanning the highest high to lowest low in the profile interval. Allocate each bar's volume uniformly across its high-low range in proportion to overlap with each row; allocate a zero-range bar's volume to its price row. The interval runs from P2 in full-range mode or P3 in B-C mode through P5 for immediate construction or the confirmation bar for deferred construction.
Calculation: Risk distance = Profile POC − P5 when positive; otherwise P5 × the fallback-risk fraction.
Calculation: Target 1 = Profile POC + risk distance × target 1 multiple.
Calculation: Target 2 = Profile POC + risk distance × target 2 multiple.
Calculation: Target 3 = Profile POC + risk distance × target 3 multiple.
Calculation: Active ABC profile = a profile for a Qualifying ABC, constructed immediately when deferred construction is disabled, or after the first pending confirmation when enabled: a later confirmed pivot low above P5, close > P4 + ATR(ATR lookback) × breakout distance with volume / SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback) × 100 >= breakout volume threshold, or pending age >= time cap. Discard a pending pattern if close < P5 − ATR(ATR lookback) × invalidation distance, checking invalidation before confirmation. The profile remains active until replaced by a new Qualifying ABC or until no simulated position remains open and high >= Target 3 or low <= P5.
Long: Active ABC profile AND this structure's weak signal has not fired AND close > Profile POC for at least the weak consecutive-bar parameter, counted after profile construction and reset by any close <= Profile POC.
Long: Active ABC profile AND this structure's medium signal has not fired AND close > Profile POC for at least the medium consecutive-bar parameter, counted after profile construction and reset by any close <= Profile POC.
Long: Active ABC profile AND this structure's strong signal has not fired AND close > Profile POC for at least the weak consecutive-bar parameter, counted after profile construction and reset by any close <= Profile POC AND volume / SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback) × 100 >= strong-volume threshold.
Exit: long, low <= P5.
Exit: long, a new Qualifying ABC is detected.
Exit: long, high >= Target 3 under exit policy A or C.
Exit: long, high >= Target 3 under exit policy B after the target scale-out logic has reduced the remaining position.
Exit: long, low <= highest high since entry × trail basis − ATR(ATR lookback) × trailing multiple under exit policy A AND Target 1 has been reached.
Exit: long, low <= highest high since entry × trail basis − ATR(ATR lookback) × trailing multiple under exit policy B after the target scale-out logic has reduced the remaining position.
Exit: long, low <= entry price under exit policy C AND Target 1 has been reached.
