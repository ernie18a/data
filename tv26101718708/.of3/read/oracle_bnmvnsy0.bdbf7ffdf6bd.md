Calculation: projected price(offset) = fit a weighted least-squares polynomial to the latest closes, with x increasing from oldest to newest and each weight equal to weight-base + ((x−1)/lookback) × weight-strength; choose the lookback with the greatest R² from the candidate-count parameter of evenly spaced integer lookbacks between adaptive-minimum and historical lookback, breaking ties toward the shorter lookback; R² is 1 − sum of squared fit errors divided by the sum of squared deviations from the mean, clipped to its valid range; evaluate the fit at lookback + offset; if curve damping is enabled, blend that value toward the fitted endpoint tangent extrapolated by offset, using curve-strength × offset / projection-horizon; if volume adjustment is enabled, multiply it by 1 + bias × offset / projection-horizon × volume-impact, where bias = (volume of bars with close > open divided by total volume over volume-lookback − volume-midpoint) × volume-scale; if EMA filtering is enabled, shrink its displacement from close by counter-trend reduction when it opposes the trend, defined as close above EMA(close, trend-lookback) or down otherwise; if RSI filtering is enabled, shrink its displacement from close by RSI-softening reduction when RSI(close, RSI-lookback) is above overbought and the forecast exceeds close, or below oversold and the forecast is below close; if support/resistance filtering is enabled and both the current confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot-lookback, pivot-lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot-lookback, pivot-lookback) exist, replace a forecast above resistance while close is below it with resistance × resistance-cap multiplier, or a forecast below support while close is above it with support × support-cap multiplier; if confidence scaling is enabled, shrink its displacement from close by R².
Long: projected price at an offset after the first projected point >= projected price at the preceding offset
Short: projected price at an offset after the first projected point < projected price at the preceding offset
