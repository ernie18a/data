Calculation: Liquidity levels = confirmed chart-bar PivotHigh(high, major pivot lookback, major pivot lookback), PivotLow(low, major pivot lookback, major pivot lookback), and corresponding minor pivots; major lookback is longer than minor lookback, and the selected swing categories determine which pivots are used. Deduplicate unswept same-side levels within one instrument minimum tick; retain levels only within the level-age window and storage capacity. When equal-level detection is enabled, same-side unswept levels within the selected ATR-multiple or close-based percentage tolerance form equal-level clusters.
Calculation: Sweep strength = the capped weighted average, expressed as a percentage, of six capped components: directional wick fraction (bullish: (close-low)/max(high-low, instrument minimum tick); bearish: (high-close)/max(high-low, instrument minimum tick)); directional nonnegative body fraction (bullish: max(close-open,0)/max(high-low, instrument minimum tick); bearish: max(open-close,0)/max(high-low, instrument minimum tick)); min(penetration/ATR(ATR lookback), 100%) when ATR is positive, otherwise zero; min(volume/(SMA(volume, volume lookback)*volume normalization parameter), 100%) when the SMA is positive, otherwise zero; min(abs(close-EMA(close, fast EMA lookback))/(ATR(ATR lookback)*EMA normalization parameter), 100%) when ATR is positive, otherwise zero; and min(equal-cluster touch count/touch-count normalization parameter, 100%). Use separate wick, ATR, volume, body, EMA, and equal-cluster weights selected by the score-profile parameter; divide the weighted sum by the sum of weights, and return zero if the weight sum is zero.
Calculation: Bullish sweep signal = on a confirmed chart bar, an unswept sell-side liquidity level has low < level price < close; pass each enabled candle-filter-preset gate: bullish wick rejection (close-low)/(high-low) at least the preset-dependent wick threshold, bullish body rejection max(close-open,0)/(high-low) at least the body threshold, volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)*the preset-dependent volume multiplier, high-low >= ATR(ATR lookback)*the range multiplier with positive ATR, and level price-low >= ATR(ATR lookback)*the penetration multiplier. If the trend filter is enabled, require fast EMA > slow EMA when it requires trend alignment, or fast EMA < slow EMA when it requires the opposing trend. If the T1 bias filter is enabled, require the T1 close > EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1). Evaluate this bias on chart bars using the lookahead-off T1 values, including the current T1 bar while it is forming in real time. If minimum-score filtering is enabled, require Sweep strength >= minimum-score parameter. Consume the level once the non-score gates pass, even if minimum-score filtering suppresses the signal.
Calculation: Bearish sweep signal = on a confirmed chart bar, an unswept buy-side liquidity level has low < level price < high and close < level price; pass each enabled candle-filter-preset gate: bearish wick rejection (high-close)/(high-low) at least the preset-dependent wick threshold, bearish body rejection max(open-close,0)/(high-low) at least the body threshold, volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)*the preset-dependent volume multiplier, high-low >= ATR(ATR lookback)*the range multiplier with positive ATR, and high-level price >= ATR(ATR lookback)*the penetration multiplier. If the trend filter is enabled, require fast EMA < slow EMA when it requires trend alignment, or fast EMA > slow EMA when it requires the opposing trend. If the T1 bias filter is enabled, require the T1 close < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1). Evaluate this bias on chart bars using the lookahead-off T1 values, including the current T1 bar while it is forming in real time. If minimum-score filtering is enabled, require Sweep strength >= minimum-score parameter. Consume the level once the non-score gates pass, even if minimum-score filtering suppresses the signal.
