Long: position side is flat AND (session filter is disabled OR bar datetime is within the configured entry session in Asia/Taipei) AND low < the most recent confirmed PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) AND close > that pivot low AND close > open AND volume > volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= buying-pressure threshold
Short: position side is flat AND (session filter is disabled OR bar datetime is within the configured entry session in Asia/Taipei) AND high > the most recent confirmed PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) AND close < that pivot high AND close < open AND volume > volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (high − close) / (high − low) >= selling-pressure threshold
Calculation: Long protective stop = long signal bar low minus one minimum tick at entry; thereafter, the maximum of its prior value, entry price after close first reaches entry price + initial long risk × trail activation multiple, close − ATR(lookback) × ATR trail multiple after trailing activates when ATR trailing is enabled, and close − ATR(lookback) when cap mode is tighten and volume > cap volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (high − max(open, close)) >= wick/body multiple × abs(close − open) AND (close − entry price) / initial long risk >= minimum profit R; initial long risk = entry price − initial stop
Calculation: Short protective stop = short signal bar high plus one minimum tick at entry; thereafter, the minimum of its prior value, entry price after close first reaches entry price − initial short risk × trail activation multiple, close + ATR(lookback) × ATR trail multiple after trailing activates when ATR trailing is enabled, and close + ATR(lookback) when cap mode is tighten and volume > cap volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (min(open, close) − low) >= wick/body multiple × abs(close − open) AND (entry price − close) / initial short risk >= minimum profit R; initial short risk = initial stop − entry price
Exit: long, low <= Long protective stop OR (time stop is enabled AND bars since entry >= time-stop lookback AND close < entry price) OR (cap mode is immediate close AND volume > cap volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (high − max(open, close)) >= wick/body multiple × abs(close − open) AND (close − entry price) / initial long risk >= minimum profit R)
Exit: short, high >= Short protective stop OR (time stop is enabled AND bars since entry >= time-stop lookback AND close > entry price) OR (cap mode is immediate close AND volume > cap volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (min(open, close) − low) >= wick/body multiple × abs(close − open) AND (entry price − close) / initial short risk >= minimum profit R)
