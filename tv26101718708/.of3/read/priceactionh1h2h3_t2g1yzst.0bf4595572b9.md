Calculation: Bull trend = EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, medium lookback) AND EMA(close, medium lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback), with short < medium < long lookbacks
Calculation: Bull surge = Bull trend AND ((close > open AND high - low >= surge ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback)[previous bar]) OR (close > open AND close[previous bar] > open[previous bar] AND max(high, high[previous bar]) - min(low, low[previous bar]) >= surge ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback)[two bars ago]))
Calculation: Pullback bar = close < close[previous bar] OR low < low[previous bar]
Calculation: Tight bearish channel = bearish closes in the channel lookback >= channel lookback - 1 AND highest(high, channel lookback) - lowest(low, channel lookback) <= channel ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback) AND close < close[channel lookback]
Calculation: Pullback breakout event = On confirmed chart bars, when flat, Bull surge starts a setup; initialize surge high and low from the current bar if the one-bar surge condition is true, otherwise from the current and previous bars. While tracking the surge, update surge high to each higher high. The first Pullback bar starts the pullback phase, sets reference high to high, Fib level to surge high - Fib abandonment percentage / 100 * (surge high - surge low), and lowest pullback close to close. In the pullback phase, lower the lowest pullback close on each lower close and update reference high on each Pullback bar. If close < Fib level, clear the setup. Otherwise, this event occurs when high > reference high AND reference high > lowest pullback close - stop ATR buffer * ATR(ATR lookback). On a tight-channel event, set reference high to current high and remain in the pullback phase; on an unfiltered event, enter long position management. Clear an active setup or position when Bull trend fails; reset the entry H count on a Fib cancellation or trailing-stop exit.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = On an unfiltered Pullback breakout event, initialize the stop to lowest pullback close - stop ATR buffer * ATR(ATR lookback) and begin a follow-through check. On the next confirmed bar, if close < open, increment the entry H count and return to the existing pullback phase with reference high = current high when the count is at most the H-count limit; otherwise clear the setup. If close >= open, end the follow-through check and manage the stop on that bar. Thereafter, if a recorded candidate exists and high > its recorded high, raise the stop to its recorded low if that low > the current stop, then clear the candidate. If low < low[previous bar], record the current low and high as the candidate. A stop breach is close < the current stop.
Calculation: Bull-trend H marker event = During Bull trend, initialize the running high to the first high and the count to zero. A high above the running high updates it and resets the count to zero and pullback-ready state to false. Otherwise a Pullback bar sets pullback-ready state. When pullback-ready, not a Pullback bar, high > high[previous bar], and count < H-count limit, increment the count and emit an event. Reset the running high, count, and pullback-ready state when Bull trend fails.

Long: Pullback breakout event AND NOT Tight bearish channel
Long: Pullback breakout event AND Tight bearish channel
Exit: long, close < Long trailing stop
