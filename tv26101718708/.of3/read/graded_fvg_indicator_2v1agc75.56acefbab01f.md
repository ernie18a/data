Calculation: linear ramp(x, lower bound, upper bound) = 1 if the bounds are equal and x is at least the bound; 0 if they are equal and x is below it; otherwise clamp (x − lower bound) / (upper bound − lower bound) to 0–1
Calculation: pre-gap ATR = ATR(ATR lookback)[2], falling back to current ATR when unavailable
Calculation: gap ATR = for a bullish gap, (low − high[2]) / pre-gap ATR; for a bearish gap, (low[2] − high) / pre-gap ATR
Calculation: FVG displacement score = weighted sum of ramp(body ATR, body-ATR lower, body-ATR upper), ramp(body share, body-share lower, body-share upper), ramp(close location toward gap direction, close-location lower, close-location upper), and ramp(leg ATR, leg-ATR lower, leg-ATR upper); body ATR = max((close[1] − open[1]) × gap direction, 0) / pre-gap ATR; body share = directional body / (high[1] − low[1]); close location = (close[1] − low[1]) / candle range for bullish gaps and (high[1] − close[1]) / candle range for bearish gaps; leg ATR = max((close − open[2]) × gap direction, 0) / pre-gap ATR; use zero when a denominator is nonpositive; gap direction is +1 for bullish and −1 for bearish; each ramp has its own weight parameter
Calculation: FVG structure score = if a swing or range break occurred, min(1, swing-break weight when a swing break occurred + range-break weight when a range break occurred + squeeze weight × squeeze score); otherwise continuation score if the latest swing-break direction as of candle 1 matches the gap direction, else zero. A swing break is a candle 2 or 3 close through the latest intact Pivot High or Pivot Low, using swing strength on each side; a range break is candle 3 closing beyond the structure-lookback highest high or lowest low ending at candle 1. Squeeze score = 1 − ramp(compression-range ATR, compression lower, compression upper), where compression-range ATR is the highest high minus lowest low over max(minimum compression lookback, floor(structure lookback / compression divisor)) bars ending at candle 1, divided by pre-gap ATR
Calculation: FVG volume score = ramp(volume[1] / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)[2], relative-volume lower, relative-volume upper) when volume mode uses volume and both values are positive; otherwise volume is unavailable
Calculation: FVG size score = zero below the gap-noise threshold; ramp(gap ATR, gap-noise threshold, ideal-gap lower) below ideal-gap lower; one through ideal-gap upper; otherwise max(minimum large-gap credit, 1 − large-gap decay × ramp(gap ATR, ideal-gap upper, ideal-gap upper × decay-extension multiple))
Calculation: FVG context score = freshness weight × (1 − ramp(traffic share, traffic lower, traffic upper)) + range-edge weight × min(1, abs(gap-midpoint position − range midpoint) × edge scale); traffic share is the fraction of available context-lookback bars preceding candle 1 whose high-low ranges overlap the gap; gap-midpoint position is its midpoint within the context-lookback high-low range ending at candle 1, or the range midpoint if that range has no height
Calculation: FVG core score = (displacement weight × FVG displacement score + structure weight × FVG structure score + size weight × FVG size score + context weight × FVG context score) / (displacement weight + structure weight + size weight + context weight)
Calculation: FVG score = weighted sum of FVG displacement score, FVG structure score, FVG size score, and FVG context score plus volume weight × FVG volume score × ramp(FVG core score, zero, full-volume core threshold), when volume is available; otherwise 100 × FVG core score. The weights are separate parameters
Calculation: FVG tier = tier from FVG score / 10 against high-grade and medium-grade thresholds, with the medium threshold capped at the high threshold; if both 10 × FVG displacement score is below the displacement gate and gap ATR is below the gap-size gate, tier is LOW; if either fails, cap tier at MEDIUM
Calculation: active zone = a displayed zone created on an earlier confirmed chart bar and not retired or removed by its per-side oldest-first zone cap; a fresh zone has had no wick penetration. A close beyond the far edge always retires a zone; the selected mitigation rule may also retire it on a full wick fill or a close beyond the midpoint. A qualifying new same-direction non-inversion FVG merges with an active non-inversion FVG when overlap divided by the smaller zone height reaches the merge threshold; use the union of the ranges, the higher score and tier, and reset the merged zone to fresh. Support and resistance have separate zone caps; an IFVG can flip only once
Calculation: IFVG break-body ATR = max((close − open) × break direction, 0) / ATR(ATR lookback), where break direction is +1 for an upward close through a bearish FVG and −1 for a downward close through a bullish FVG
Calculation: IFVG score = original zone score × (grade-floor factor + break-strength factor × ramp(IFVG break-body ATR, break-body lower, break-body upper))
Calculation: IFVG tier = the lower of the original zone tier and the tier from IFVG score / 10 against the high-grade and medium-grade thresholds, without reapplying the FVG displacement or gap-size gates
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, low > high[2] AND FVG tier ≥ minimum displayed tier
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, high < low[2] AND FVG tier ≥ minimum displayed tier
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, an active fresh bullish FVG has low < its top
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, an active fresh bearish FVG has high > its bottom
Long: IFVG flipping is enabled AND an active bearish FVG closes above its top AND the selected break requirement passes (any close-through, IFVG break-body ATR ≥ strong-body minimum, or a bullish FVG forms on the breaking bar) AND IFVG tier ≥ minimum displayed tier
Short: IFVG flipping is enabled AND an active bullish FVG closes below its bottom AND the selected break requirement passes (any close-through, IFVG break-body ATR ≥ strong-body minimum, or a bearish FVG forms on the breaking bar) AND IFVG tier ≥ minimum displayed tier
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, an active fresh support IFVG has low < its top
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, an active fresh resistance IFVG has high > its bottom
