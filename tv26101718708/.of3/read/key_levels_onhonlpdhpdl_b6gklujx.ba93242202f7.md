Calculation: Previous day high = highest(high of chart bars in the preceding day)
Calculation: Previous day low = lowest(low of chart bars in the preceding day)
Calculation: Overnight high = highest(high of chart bars in the overnight session)
Calculation: Overnight low = lowest(low of chart bars in the overnight session)
Long: close crosses above previous day high OR close crosses above overnight high
Short: close crosses below previous day low OR close crosses below overnight low
