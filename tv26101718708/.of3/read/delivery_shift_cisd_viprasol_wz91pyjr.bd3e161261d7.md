Calculation: bearish displacement ratio = for each pending origin from a bullish candle preceded by a bearish candle, (highest close from the origin through the current bar, limited to the maximum leg length, minus the origin open) / (the open of the oldest consecutive bearish candle immediately before the origin, found within the maximum leg length, minus the origin open)
Calculation: bullish displacement ratio = for each pending origin from a bearish candle preceded by a bullish candle, (the origin open minus the lowest close from the origin through the current bar, limited to the maximum leg length) / (the origin open minus the open of the oldest consecutive bullish candle immediately before the origin, found within the maximum leg length)
Calculation: bearish CISD = on a confirmed chart bar, a pending bearish origin is crossed when close < origin open; if its bearish displacement ratio > displacement threshold, it fires and clears pending origins, otherwise discard it and continue scanning older crossed origins, stopping at the first uncrossed origin or first passing ratio; scan while the counter is at most the candidate scan limit
Calculation: bullish CISD = on a confirmed chart bar, a pending bullish origin is crossed when close > origin open; if its bullish displacement ratio > displacement threshold, it fires and clears pending origins, otherwise discard it and continue scanning older crossed origins, stopping at the first uncrossed origin or first passing ratio; scan while the counter is at most the candidate scan limit
Calculation: CISD direction = bullish if bullish CISD fires, otherwise bearish if bearish CISD fires, otherwise none
Calculation: bearish liquidity sweep = a Pivot High(high, swing period, swing period) level younger than the expiry period bars is wicked when high >= its level on a confirmed chart bar; within the liquidity lookback after a wicked high, close < the most recently wicked high level
Calculation: bullish liquidity sweep = a Pivot Low(low, swing period, swing period) level younger than the expiry period bars is wicked when low <= its level on a confirmed chart bar; within the liquidity lookback after a wicked low, close > the most recently wicked low level
Calculation: strong displacement = the direction's displacement ratio >= displacement threshold + (ratio ceiling - displacement threshold) * midpoint fraction
Calculation: confluence score = count of the true checks for strong displacement, same-direction liquidity sweep, HTF EMA alignment, and volume > SMA(volume, volume average length) * volume multiplier; HTF EMA alignment is bullish when chart close > EMA(close, HTF EMA length, T1, bar still forming) and bearish when chart close < that EMA, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: delivery state = direction of the most recent CISD, or neutral before the first CISD
