Calculation: MA 1 = the selected EMA, SMA, WMA, or RMA of source 1 over lookback 1; calculate on chart bars unless timeframe T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, in which case sample T1 on chart bars using its last completed bar when confirmed-HTF mode is enabled or its still-forming bar otherwise.
Calculation: MA 2 = the selected EMA, SMA, WMA, or RMA of source 2 over lookback 2; calculate on chart bars unless timeframe T2 is higher than the chart timeframe, in which case sample T2 on chart bars using its last completed bar when confirmed-HTF mode is enabled or its still-forming bar otherwise.
Calculation: MA 3 = the selected EMA, SMA, WMA, or RMA of source 3 over lookback 3; calculate on chart bars unless timeframe T3 is higher than the chart timeframe, in which case sample T3 on chart bars using its last completed bar when confirmed-HTF mode is enabled or its still-forming bar otherwise.
Calculation: compared pair = MA 1 and MA 2 when exactly those two are shown; MA 2 and MA 3 when exactly those two are shown; MA 1 and MA 3 when exactly those two are shown; when all three are shown, MA 2 and MA 3 if lookback 1 is no greater than both other lookbacks, otherwise MA 1 and MA 3 if lookback 2 is no greater than both other lookbacks, otherwise MA 1 and MA 2; otherwise none.
Calculation: structure state = bullish when, on a confirmed chart bar, the shorter-lookback MA in the compared pair crosses above the longer-lookback MA; bearish when it crosses below; otherwise retain the previous state, initially neutral. For equal lookbacks, treat the lower-numbered MA as shorter.
Long: structure state is bullish.
Short: structure state is bearish.
