Calculation: On each chart bar, use the still-forming T1 candle and all T2 bars returned so far within it. For each T2 bar, estimated buy fraction = neutral-share parameter when high = low; otherwise clamp(neutral-share parameter + buy-share scale parameter × [close-position weight parameter × (2 × clamp((close − low) / (high − low), 0, 1) − 1) + body weight parameter × clamp((close − open) / (high − low), −1, 1) + wick weight parameter × (lower wick − upper wick) / (high − low)], buy-fraction floor parameter, buy-fraction ceiling parameter), where lower wick = max(min(open, close) − low, 0) and upper wick = max(high − max(open, close), 0).
Calculation: On each chart bar, using the still-forming T1 candle’s high-to-low range and all T2 bars returned so far within it, estimated buy and sell volume per price cell = sum the T2 bars’ estimated buy and sell volumes. For each T2 bar with positive volume, buy volume = volume × estimated buy fraction and sell volume = volume − buy volume. If high = low, assign both to the cell containing close. Otherwise, distribute each side across cells touched by the bar, in proportion to its cell weight divided by the sum of that side’s weights: cell weight = overlap with the T2 bar’s range / that range × [1 + row concentration multiplier parameter × (1 − min(abs(cell midpoint − side anchor) / range, 1)) ^ price-cell concentration parameter]. Clamp the buy anchor, a weighted average of close, high, and open using separate buy-anchor weights, to the T2 bar’s low and high; clamp the sell anchor, a weighted average of close, low, and open using separate sell-anchor weights, likewise. Divide the T1 range into the price-cell count parameter; if its range is zero, pad each side by half the minimum price increment × the price-cell count parameter.
Calculation: Price-cell delta = estimated buy volume per price cell − estimated sell volume per price cell.
Calculation: On each chart bar, using the still-forming T1 candle and all T2 bars returned so far within it, overall estimated T1 delta percent = 100 × (sum of estimated buy volume per price cell − sum of estimated sell volume per price cell) / (sum of both sides’ estimated volume), when total estimated volume is positive.
Calculation: On each chart bar, using the still-forming T1 candle and all T2 bars returned so far within it, battle score per equal-price segment = delta weight parameter × (segment buy volume − segment sell volume) / segment total volume + win-consistency weight parameter × (buyer win evidence − seller win evidence) / total win evidence; use zero for either ratio when its denominator is zero. Segment buy and sell volumes are the price-cell volumes apportioned by overlap with each segment. For each positive-volume T2 bar, win evidence = abs(2 × estimated buy fraction − 1), apportioned among the equal-price segments it touches in proportion to overlap with its range; if high = low, assign it to the segment containing close. Divide the T1 range into the battle-segment count parameter.
Calculation: Normalized battle-segment strength = abs(battle score) × sqrt(segment total volume / maximum segment total volume), when maximum segment total volume is positive.
Long: current still-forming T1 close >= current still-forming T1 open OR overall estimated T1 delta percent > 0 OR any price-cell delta > 0 OR any battle-segment score > 0 AND that segment’s total volume > 0 AND its normalized strength > minimum battle strength parameter.
Short: current still-forming T1 close < current still-forming T1 open OR overall estimated T1 delta percent < 0 OR any price-cell delta < 0 OR any battle-segment score < 0 AND that segment’s total volume > 0 AND its normalized strength > minimum battle strength parameter.
