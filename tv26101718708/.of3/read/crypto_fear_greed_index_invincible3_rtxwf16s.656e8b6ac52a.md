Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(neutral score + (ROC(close, momentum lookback) − SMA(ROC(close, momentum lookback), ranking lookback)) / StDev(ROC(close, momentum lookback), ranking lookback) × momentum scale, lower score bound, upper score bound); use zero for the standardized value when the deviation is zero.
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp(upper score bound − percentile rank(ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) / close × ATR percent scale, ranking lookback), lower score bound, upper score bound) when ROC(close, volatility direction lookback) < zero; otherwise clamp(neutral score + percentile rank(ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) / close × ATR percent scale, ranking lookback) / volatility divisor, lower score bound, upper score bound).
Calculation: RSI score = clamp(RSI(close, RSI lookback), lower score bound, upper score bound).
Calculation: Volume pressure score = clamp(SMA(volume when close > close[1], volume lookback) / SMA(volume when close > close[1] or close < close[1], volume lookback) × upper score bound, lower score bound, upper score bound); use neutral score when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Range position score = clamp((close − lowest(low, range lookback)) / (highest(high, range lookback) − lowest(low, range lookback)) × upper score bound, lower score bound, upper score bound); use neutral score when the highest high equals the lowest low.
Calculation: Crypto breadth score = upper score bound × the mean of the available bullish indicators for Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and BNB, where each indicator is one when that asset's chart-timeframe close is above its EMA(asset close, breadth lookback) and zero otherwise; use neutral score when none are available.
Calculation: Stablecoin dominance score = clamp(neutral score − change(USDT dominance, dominance lookback) × stablecoin sensitivity, lower score bound, upper score bound); use neutral score when the change is unavailable.
Calculation: Crypto market score = the mean of the available scores for total crypto market capitalization and crypto market capitalization excluding Bitcoin, where each score is clamp(neutral score + (market capitalization − EMA(market capitalization, market lookback)) / EMA(market capitalization, market lookback) × market deviation scale, lower score bound, upper score bound); use neutral score when neither is available.
Calculation: BTC dominance context score = strong positive dominance score when ROC(total crypto market capitalization, dominance lookback) > zero and change(BTC dominance, dominance lookback) < zero; positive dominance score when that ROC > zero and the change is at least zero; strong negative dominance score when that ROC < zero and the change > zero; negative dominance score when that ROC < zero and the change is at most zero; otherwise neutral score, including when either input is unavailable.
Calculation: Sentiment index = clamp(selected SMA, EMA, RMA, or WMA of the weighted mean of the nine scores—momentum, volatility, RSI, volume pressure, range position, crypto breadth, stablecoin dominance, crypto market, and BTC dominance context—using a separate weight for each score and replacing unavailable scores with the neutral score; use the neutral score when the sum of weights is zero, then apply the smoothing lookback. These calculations run on chart bars; external series use chart-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Market regime = bullish when close > EMA(close, asset regime lookback) and crypto market score >= neutral score; bearish when close < EMA(close, asset regime lookback) and crypto market score < neutral score; mixed otherwise.
Calculation: Momentum fading = sentiment index > crowding threshold AND ROC(close, fading short ROC lookback) < ROC(close, fading long ROC lookback).
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low in low and sentiment index, using the divergence pivot lookback on each side, is lower in price and higher in sentiment than a retained earlier pivot low by more than the minimum sentiment rise; pivot distance is between max(pivot lookback × gap multiplier, minimum pivot gap) and maximum divergence gap; when the zone filter is enabled, both sentiment pivot values are at or below the bullish divergence ceiling; every intervening low and sentiment value is at or above its straight-line interpolation between the pivots. Retain the most recent pivot lows up to the pivot memory limit.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high in high and sentiment index, using the divergence pivot lookback on each side, is higher in price and lower in sentiment than a retained earlier pivot high by more than the minimum sentiment fall; pivot distance is between max(pivot lookback × gap multiplier, minimum pivot gap) and maximum divergence gap; when the zone filter is enabled, both sentiment pivot values are at or above the bearish divergence floor; every intervening high and sentiment value is at or below its straight-line interpolation between the pivots. Retain the most recent pivot highs up to the pivot memory limit.
