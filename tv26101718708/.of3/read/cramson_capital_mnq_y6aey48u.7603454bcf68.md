Calculation: OpeningRangeHigh = maximum high of chart bars whose open datetime is within the session’s parameterized opening-range window.
Calculation: OpeningRangeLow = minimum low of chart bars whose open datetime is within the session’s parameterized opening-range window.
Calculation: OpeningRangeVAH/VAL = opening-range volume-profile value-area bounds: divide the opening-range price span into tick-aligned rows using parameterized initial row count and row-count bounds; distribute each opening-range bar’s volume across rows in proportion to each row’s overlap with its high-low span, assigning zero-range bars’ volume to their price row; choose the greatest-volume row as POC, breaking ties toward the lower row; expand from the POC toward the adjacent row with greater volume (ties go to the row closer to POC, then the upper row), including a row only while cumulative volume remains at or below the parameterized value-area percentage of total volume; VAH and VAL are the upper and lower edges of the included rows.
Long: For a valid opening range (OpeningRangeHigh > OpeningRangeLow), on the first setup of the session, after the opening-range window ends, a close > OpeningRangeHigh, followed on a later chart bar by a close < OpeningRangeVAH AND > OpeningRangeVAL, followed on a later chart bar by a close > OpeningRangeVAH; each close must occur by the parameterized entry-cutoff time, and no intervening close may be < OpeningRangeLow; signal long at the final close.
Short: For a valid opening range (OpeningRangeHigh > OpeningRangeLow), on the first setup of the session, after the opening-range window ends, a close < OpeningRangeLow, followed on a later chart bar by a close < OpeningRangeVAH AND > OpeningRangeVAL, followed on a later chart bar by a close < OpeningRangeVAL; each close must occur by the parameterized entry-cutoff time, and no intervening close may be > OpeningRangeHigh; signal short at the final close.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= OpeningRangeLow OR high >= entry price + reward-risk parameter × (entry price − OpeningRangeLow) OR bar open datetime >= parameterized time-close time.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= OpeningRangeHigh OR low <= entry price − reward-risk parameter × (OpeningRangeHigh − entry price) OR bar open datetime >= parameterized time-close time.
