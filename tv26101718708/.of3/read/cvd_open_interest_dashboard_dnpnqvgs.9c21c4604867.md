Calculation: open-interest change = current chart bar's open-interest close minus the prior chart bar's open-interest close
Calculation: CVD direction on chart bars = 1 when signed volume delta > total volume × CVD threshold, -1 when signed volume delta < -total volume × CVD threshold, otherwise 0; signed volume delta = sum(volume when close > open, -volume when close < open, 0 otherwise) over T1 bars within each chart bar, including any T1 bar still forming; total volume = sum(volume) over those bars
Long: (abs(open-interest change) > SMA(abs(open-interest change), anomaly smoothing period) × anomaly multiplier AND open-interest change > 0 AND CVD direction = 1) OR (SMA(close, macro smoothing period) > SMA(close, macro smoothing period)[1] AND SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period) > SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period)[1])
Short: (abs(open-interest change) > SMA(abs(open-interest change), anomaly smoothing period) × anomaly multiplier AND open-interest change > 0 AND CVD direction = -1) OR (SMA(close, macro smoothing period) < SMA(close, macro smoothing period)[1] AND SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period) > SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period)[1])
Exit: long, (abs(open-interest change) > SMA(abs(open-interest change), anomaly smoothing period) × anomaly multiplier AND open-interest change < 0 AND CVD direction = -1) OR (SMA(close, macro smoothing period) < SMA(close, macro smoothing period)[1] AND SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period) < SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period)[1])
Exit: short, (abs(open-interest change) > SMA(abs(open-interest change), anomaly smoothing period) × anomaly multiplier AND open-interest change < 0 AND CVD direction = 1) OR (SMA(close, macro smoothing period) > SMA(close, macro smoothing period)[1] AND SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period) < SMA(open interest, macro smoothing period)[1])
Calculation: Candle-volume direction = 1 when signed volume delta > P1 × total volume, -1 when signed volume delta < -P1 × total volume, otherwise 0; signed volume delta is the sum of volume for candles closing above their open minus volume for candles closing below their open, using chart candles in normal mode or T1 candles in high-precision mode.
Calculation: Open-interest anomaly = |open interest - prior-bar open interest| > SMA(|open interest - prior-bar open interest|, P2) × P3
Long: (SMA(close, P4) > SMA(close, P4)[1] AND SMA(open interest, P4) > SMA(open interest, P4)[1]) OR (open-interest anomaly AND open interest - prior-bar open interest > 0 AND candle-volume direction = 1)
Short: (SMA(close, P4) < SMA(close, P4)[1] AND SMA(open interest, P4) > SMA(open interest, P4)[1]) OR (open-interest anomaly AND open interest - prior-bar open interest > 0 AND candle-volume direction = -1)
Exit: short, (SMA(close, P4) > SMA(close, P4)[1] AND SMA(open interest, P4) < SMA(open interest, P4)[1]) OR (open-interest anomaly AND open interest - prior-bar open interest < 0 AND candle-volume direction = -1)
