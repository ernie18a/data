Calculation: Normalized(x, lookback) = if Highest(x, lookback) != Lowest(x, lookback), 2 * (x - Lowest(x, lookback)) / (Highest(x, lookback) - Lowest(x, lookback)) - 1, else 0
Calculation: Credit ratio = if close of credit series 2 != 0, close of credit series 1 / close of credit series 2, else undefined
Calculation: Risk-on score = (Normalized(close of risk-on series 1, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-on series 2, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-on series 3, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-on series 4, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-on series 5, normalization lookback) + Normalized(Credit ratio, normalization lookback)) / 6
Calculation: Risk-off score = (Normalized(close of risk-off series 1, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-off series 2, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-off series 3, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of risk-off series 4, normalization lookback)) / 4
Calculation: Macro score = (Normalized(close of housing series, normalization lookback) + Normalized(close of building-permit series, normalization lookback) + Normalized(if close of wage series at the shorter ROC lookback ago != 0, ROC(close of wage series, shorter ROC lookback), else 0, normalization lookback) + Normalized(if close of wage series at the longer ROC lookback ago != 0, ROC(close of wage series, longer ROC lookback), else 0, normalization lookback)) / 4
Calculation: Moving-average alignment score = 1 if close of anchor series > EMA(close of anchor series, EMA lookback) AND close of anchor series > SMA(close of anchor series, short SMA lookback) AND close of anchor series > SMA(close of anchor series, middle SMA lookback) AND close of anchor series > SMA(close of anchor series, long SMA lookback) AND SMA(close of anchor series, short SMA lookback) > SMA(close of anchor series, middle SMA lookback) > SMA(close of anchor series, long SMA lookback); -1 if all corresponding comparisons are <; otherwise 0; EMA lookback is shorter than the short SMA lookback, and the short, middle, and long SMA lookbacks are ordered from shorter to longer
Calculation: Technical score = ((RSI(close of anchor series, RSI lookback) - 50) / 50 + Normalized(Cumulative volume delta(close of anchor series, volume of anchor series, positive volume when close rises from the prior bar, negative volume when it falls, zero when unchanged), normalization lookback) + Moving-average alignment score) / 3
Calculation: Regime score = (Risk-on score + Macro score + Technical score - Risk-off score) / 4
Long: Regime score >= upper risk-on threshold
Short: Regime score <= lower risk-off threshold
