Calculation: Active swing high(T) = the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) on T bars, cleared after a bullish BOS; T1, T2, and T3 are ordered from shorter to longer duration. On chart bars, use the current forming T bar in real time and the last completed T bar historically (lookahead off).
Calculation: Active swing low(T) = the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) on T bars, cleared after a bearish BOS. On chart bars, use the current forming T bar in real time and the last completed T bar historically (lookahead off).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe BOS overlap(T) = a T BOS level is within one minimum price tick of any non-null bullish or bearish BOS level from a longer T timeframe on the same chart bar; this is false when T has no longer timeframe.
Long: With the T module enabled, the T bullish BOS flag sampled on chart bars changes from false to true, T-bar close > Active swing high(T), T-bar close[1] <= Active swing high(T), the displacement filter is disabled OR T-bar ATR(ATR lookback) is undefined OR T-bar close - open >= ATR(ATR lookback) * body/ATR ratio, volume validation is disabled OR T-bar SMA(volume, volume lookback) is undefined OR T-bar volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier, and NOT Higher-timeframe BOS overlap(T).
Short: With the T module enabled, the T bearish BOS flag sampled on chart bars changes from false to true, T-bar close < Active swing low(T), T-bar close[1] >= Active swing low(T), the displacement filter is disabled OR T-bar ATR(ATR lookback) is undefined OR T-bar open - close >= ATR(ATR lookback) * body/ATR ratio, volume validation is disabled OR T-bar SMA(volume, volume lookback) is undefined OR T-bar volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier, and NOT Higher-timeframe BOS overlap(T).
Long: With the T module enabled, on a chart bar low < Active swing low(T), close >= Active swing low(T), and NOT (low[1] < Active swing low(T) AND close[1] >= Active swing low(T)).
Short: With the T module enabled, on a chart bar high > Active swing high(T), close <= Active swing high(T), and NOT (high[1] > Active swing high(T) AND close[1] <= Active swing high(T)).
Long: High-volume coloring is enabled, chart SMA(volume, volume lookback) is defined, chart volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier, and close >= open.
Short: High-volume coloring is enabled, chart SMA(volume, volume lookback) is defined, chart volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier, and close < open.
