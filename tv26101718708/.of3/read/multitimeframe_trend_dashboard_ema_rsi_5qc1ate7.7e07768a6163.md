Calculation: available timeframe(T) = T is not below the chart timeframe and its EMA, RSI, and close values are available
Calculation: timeframe score(T) = (EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) ? 1 : EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) ? -1 : 0) + (close(T) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) ? 1 : close(T) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) ? -1 : 0) + (RSI(close, RSI lookback, T) > 50 ? 1 : RSI(close, RSI lookback, T) < 50 ? -1 : 0); evaluate on T bars using the last completed T bar when closed-only is selected or the still-forming T bar otherwise, and carry values to chart bars
Calculation: bullish alignment = at least one enabled available T1–T5 and every enabled available timeframe has score(T) >= score threshold
Calculation: bearish alignment = at least one enabled available T1–T5 and every enabled available timeframe has score(T) <= -score threshold
Long: On chart bars, at least one available T1–T5 has EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) OR close(T) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, T) > 50 OR timeframe score(T) > 0
Short: On chart bars, at least one available T1–T5 has EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) OR close(T) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, T) < 50 OR timeframe score(T) < 0
Exit: long or short, confirmed chart bar AND neither current bullish alignment nor current bearish alignment AND previous chart bar bullish alignment or bearish alignment
