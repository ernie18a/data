Calculation: AverageRange(T) = arithmetic mean of high − low over the visible T candles; include the forming candle on T1 and only completed candles on T2 and T3
Calculation: TrueSwingLow = a candle’s low is below both adjacent lows and a down-to-up candle-color turn straddles it; TrueSwingHigh = a candle’s high is above both adjacent highs and an up-to-down turn straddles it, with up meaning close > open and down meaning close < open
Calculation: ReversalThreshold = the configured point-distance parameter in points mode; otherwise, the high − low of the previous completed T4 bar multiplied by the range-fraction parameter, falling back to the point-distance parameter if that bar is unavailable
Calculation: FVGZoneBounds = the outer wick-gap edges, optionally extended at either seam adjoining the middle candle to the adjacent candle’s body edge when the bodies have a directional gap but their wicks touch or overlap
Calculation: ZoneState = a bullish zone fills when a T candle closes below its bottom, and a bearish zone fills when it closes above its top; when inversion is enabled, a filled zone reverses direction until a later close through the original far edge spends it, otherwise the first fill spends it. T1 state uses T1 snapshots including the forming candle on chart bars; T2/T3 state advances on confirmed chart bars
Calculation: NormalizedEMASlopeAngle = signed round(arctan(abs(EMA(close, EMA lookback) − EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1]) × normalization factor × lowest(low, range lookback) / (HLC3 × (highest(high, range lookback) − lowest(low, range lookback)))) in degrees), positive when the EMA rises and negative when it falls; use chart bars
Long: On T1, evaluated on chart bars using the T1 snapshot including its forming candle, or on T2/T3 at each layer close using completed candles, the first later candle whose low falls below an untaken PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and closes above it, with no intervening low below that pivot
Short: On T1, evaluated on chart bars using the T1 snapshot including its forming candle, or on T2/T3 at each layer close using completed candles, the first later candle whose high rises above an untaken PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and closes below it, with no intervening high above that pivot
Long: On aligned T1 chart and correlated-market candles, the chart makes a sellside sweep and the peer’s low on the matching chart-swing candle is at or below neither the peer’s current low nor below that matching low; invalidate if a later chart T1 low falls below the sweep candle’s low
Short: On aligned T1 chart and correlated-market candles, the chart makes a buyside sweep and the peer’s high on the matching chart-swing candle is at or above neither the peer’s current high nor above that matching high; invalidate if a later chart T1 high rises above the sweep candle’s high
Long: On T1, evaluated on chart bars using the T1 snapshot including its forming candle, or on T2/T3 at each layer close using completed candles, the first eligible candle after at least one intervening candle closes above a TrueSwingHigh; on T1 only, when the raid-first filter is enabled, an earlier T1 candle must wick above an untaken pivot high, and when the displacement filter is enabled the break candle’s range must meet its configured multiple of AverageRange(T1) or form a fresh upward wick gap
Short: On T1, evaluated on chart bars using the T1 snapshot including its forming candle, or on T2/T3 at each layer close using completed candles, the first eligible candle after at least one intervening candle closes below a TrueSwingLow; on T1 only, when the raid-first filter is enabled, an earlier T1 candle must wick below an untaken pivot low, and when the displacement filter is enabled the break candle’s range must meet its configured multiple of AverageRange(T1) or form a fresh downward wick gap
Long: On each T layer’s own candle close using completed candles, a candle wicks below a prior TrueSwingLow and closes back above it, then on that candle or within the configured T1-duration-equivalent window closes at least ReversalThreshold above the lowest low since the raid; any intervening close below the raided swing low cancels the reversal
Short: On each T layer’s own candle close using completed candles, a candle wicks above a prior TrueSwingHigh and closes back below it, then on that candle or within the configured T1-duration-equivalent window closes at least ReversalThreshold below the highest high since the raid; any intervening close above the raided swing high cancels the reversal
Long: On T1, scanned on chart bars using only completed T1 candles, or on T2/T3 at each layer close using completed candles, the newest low exceeds the oldest high by at least the configured gap-height multiple of AverageRange(T), and the middle candle is bullish with a body at least the configured displacement multiple of AverageRange(T); a live bullish gap is long, and an inverted bearish gap is long
Short: On T1, scanned on chart bars using only completed T1 candles, or on T2/T3 at each layer close using completed candles, the newest high is below the oldest low by at least the configured gap-height multiple of AverageRange(T), and the middle candle is bearish with a body at least the configured displacement multiple of AverageRange(T); a live bearish gap is short, and an inverted bullish gap is short
Long: On completed T1 candles scanned on chart bars, an upward run of same-direction candles has each newer open above the preceding close, no join exceeds the configured inter-candle time-gap threshold, and the span from the earliest close to the latest open meets the configured minimum-height multiple of AverageRange(T1); a live upward block is long, and an inverted downward block is long
Short: On completed T1 candles scanned on chart bars, a downward run of same-direction candles has each newer open below the preceding close, no join exceeds the configured inter-candle time-gap threshold, and the span from the earliest close to the latest open meets the configured minimum-height multiple of AverageRange(T1); a live downward block is short, and an inverted upward block is short
Long: The forming T1 candle’s close is at or above its open
Short: The forming T1 candle’s close is below its open
Long: On chart bars, NormalizedEMASlopeAngle exceeds the configured angle threshold
Short: On chart bars, NormalizedEMASlopeAngle is below the negative configured angle threshold
