Calculation: normalized source index = 100 * (source - lowest(source, source-range lookback)) / (highest(source, source-range lookback) - lowest(source, source-range lookback)), or 50 when the highest and lowest are equal
Calculation: effective bias = selected manual bias, or, in automatic mode, bullish when normalized source index is at least the bullish threshold, bearish when it is at most the bearish threshold, and neutral otherwise
Long: ((trigger mode is band exit AND Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) crosses above the oversold threshold) OR (trigger mode is EMA cross in zone AND Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) crosses above EMA(Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback), EMA lookback) AND Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) is below the midline)) AND (EMA confirmation is disabled OR Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) is at least EMA(Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback), EMA lookback)) AND (regime filter is disabled OR EMA(Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback), EMA lookback) is above the midline) AND (bias mode is off OR effective bias is bullish)
Short: ((trigger mode is band exit AND Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) crosses below the overbought threshold) OR (trigger mode is EMA cross in zone AND Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) crosses below EMA(Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback), EMA lookback) AND Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) is above the midline)) AND (EMA confirmation is disabled OR Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback) is at most EMA(Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback), EMA lookback)) AND (regime filter is disabled OR EMA(Williams %R(high, low, close, %R lookback), EMA lookback) is below the midline) AND (bias mode is off OR effective bias is bearish)
Long: divergence markers are enabled AND, when a Williams %R pivot low is confirmed using the pivot-left and pivot-right lookbacks, its value is above the previous confirmed Williams %R pivot low AND the price low at the current pivot is below the price low at the previous pivot
Short: divergence markers are enabled AND, when a Williams %R pivot high is confirmed using the pivot-left and pivot-right lookbacks, its value is below the previous confirmed Williams %R pivot high AND the price high at the current pivot is above the price high at the previous pivot
