Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low from the session anchor through the configured opening-range duration, using at least one chart bar; freeze both when the duration ends.
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2; opening-range width = opening-range high - opening-range low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe close and EMA = close and EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback) from the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars; use only when the chart timeframe is below T1.
Calculation: Trend score = weighted average of EMA alignment, directional ADX strength, higher-timeframe direction, and fast-EMA slope, using their configured score weights. EMA alignment is maximum score when EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), minimum when reversed, and midpoint otherwise. Directional ADX strength is midpoint score plus or minus min(maximum score, ADX × directional-strength scale) / 2 according to whether +DI >= -DI. Higher-timeframe direction is maximum score when higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA, minimum when below, and midpoint otherwise. Fast-EMA slope score is clamp(midpoint score + (EMA(close, fast lookback) - EMA(close, fast lookback)[slope lag]) / ATR(ATR lookback) × slope scale).
Calculation: Momentum score = weighted average of RSI score, MACD-histogram score, rate-of-change score, and candle-body score. RSI score = clamp(midpoint score + (RSI(close, RSI lookback) - midpoint score) × RSI scale + (RSI(close, RSI lookback) - RSI(close, RSI lookback)[RSI momentum lag]) × RSI acceleration scale). Histogram score = clamp(midpoint score + MACD histogram / ATR(ATR lookback) × histogram scale + histogram-expansion adjustment); expansion means abs(MACD histogram) > abs(MACD histogram[prior bar]), and the adjustment is positive for a positive histogram and negative otherwise. Rate-of-change score = clamp(midpoint score + (close - close[rate-of-change lookback]) / ATR(ATR lookback) × rate-of-change scale). Candle-body score = clamp(midpoint score + (close - open) / ATR(ATR lookback) × body scale).
Calculation: Long/short volume scores = midpoint score without usable volume. Otherwise, relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback); volume base = weighted average of clamp((relative volume - low-volume reference) × relative-volume scale), volume percentile rank, and a volume-spike score. Long volume score = clamp(volume-base weight × volume base + OBV weight × (OBV > EMA(OBV, OBV lookback) ? supportive score : opposing score)); short volume score uses OBV < EMA(OBV, OBV lookback).
Calculation: Volatility score = weighted average of clamp((ATR / SMA(ATR, volatility lookback) - ATR-ratio reference) × ATR expansion scale), clamp((Bollinger Band Width / SMA(Bollinger Band Width, bandwidth lookback) - bandwidth reference) × bandwidth scale), and clamp(((high - low) / SMA(high - low, bar-range lookback) - range reference) × range scale); use midpoint score when a required average is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Long/short structure scores = weighted averages of VWAP position, room to the nearest resistance/support, swing position, and gap context. VWAP score is supportive when close is above VWAP for long or below VWAP for short, opposing otherwise, and midpoint without usable volume. Resistance is the nearest value above close among prior-session high, latest confirmed pivot high, and pre-anchor-session high; support is the nearest value below close among prior-session low, latest confirmed pivot low, and pre-anchor-session low. Room score uses the configured ATR-distance bands, with the highest room score when no level exists. Swing score is supportive when close is above the latest confirmed pivot high for long or below the latest confirmed pivot low for short, opposing otherwise. Gap context compares session open with prior-session close in ATR units and also checks whether session open is beyond prior-session high/low.
Calculation: Long/short breakout-quality scores = weighted average of distance beyond the opening-range edge, candle-body ratio, consecutive closes beyond that edge, elapsed time from range lock, and retest status. Distance score is determined by the configured ATR-distance bands; candle-body ratio is abs(close - open) / (high - low); consecutive-close score increases with the consecutive-close count; elapsed-time score decreases across its configured minute bands; retest score is supportive after at least one qualifying retest and neutral otherwise. Use midpoint quality score when close is not beyond the relevant edge.
Calculation: Long/short liquidity scores = clamp(midpoint score + sweep adjustment + equal-level adjustment - opposite-sweep adjustment). A low sweep is low < opening-range low and close >= opening-range low; a high sweep is high > opening-range high and close <= opening-range high. Equal highs/lows compare the rolling highest high/lowest low with prior-session high/low within the configured ATR tolerance.
Calculation: Market regime = Strong Trend when ADX >= strong-trend threshold and ATR / SMA(ATR, volatility lookback) >= strong-volatility threshold; otherwise Trending when ADX >= trend threshold; otherwise Choppy when Choppiness Index >= choppiness threshold; otherwise High Volatility when ATR ratio >= high-volatility threshold; otherwise Low Volatility when ATR ratio <= low-volatility threshold; otherwise Weak Trend when ADX >= weak-trend threshold; otherwise Range.
Calculation: Effective confidence threshold = clamp(base confidence threshold + risk-mode adjustment + regime adjustment - breakout-day adjustment). Regime adjustment is selected by market regime; breakout day means opening-range width is below the configured fraction of its average over the configured number of prior sessions. Risk mode also sets the maximum allowed veto count: the aggressive setting allows more vetoes than normal, and conservative allows none.
Calculation: Long/short confidence = clamp(adaptive weighted average of trend, momentum, directional volume, volatility, directional structure, directional breakout quality, and directional liquidity scores). Short confidence uses maximum score minus trend and momentum scores. In Strong Trend or Trending regimes increase trend weight and reduce breakout-quality and liquidity weights by their configured adjustments; in Range or Choppy regimes reduce trend weight and increase breakout-quality and liquidity weights by their configured adjustments; divide by the resulting weight sum.
Calculation: Long veto count = count of: upper-wick fraction exceeds its threshold; MACD histogram has declined for the configured consecutive comparisons; close is more than the configured ATR extension above the fast EMA; or nearest resistance is closer than the configured ATR distance. Short veto count mirrors this using lower wick, rising MACD histogram, close below fast EMA, and nearby support.
Calculation: Long/short retest count = count one retest episode after a first close beyond the respective opening-range edge when a later bar closes beyond that edge and reaches the configured ATR tolerance around it; do not count another episode until price leaves the tolerance band.
Calculation: Long entry price = opening-range high in touch-trigger mode when high exceeds it, otherwise close. Short entry price = opening-range low in touch-trigger mode when low is below it, otherwise close.
Calculation: Long/short initial stop = selected stop basis: entry price minus/plus ATR times the stop multiple; opening-range midpoint; opposite opening-range edge; or entry-bar low/high. For sweep reversals use the sweep low/high. Clamp the stop to at least the configured minimum distance, the greater of an ATR fraction and minimum price increment, on the adverse side of entry.
Calculation: Long/short risk unit = entry price minus long stop / short stop minus entry price. Fixed-R targets are entry price plus/minus one risk unit for target one and configured risk multiples for targets two and three. Opening-range projection targets are the more favorable of the configured opening-range-width projection and configured risk-unit projection for each target. Dynamic-EMA mode has no fixed targets.
Calculation: KNN vote = winning fraction among the configured number of nearest resolved candidate episodes, ranked by the sum across six features of log(1 + absolute feature difference). Features are direction-oriented normalized trend, momentum, volume, volatility, structure, and breakout quality scores. Retain up to the configured history limit; resolve each episode after entry by checking its one-risk-unit target and stop on later bars, with the stop taking priority when both are touched on one bar; discard episodes touching neither within the configured outcome window. Gate only when mode is Gate and library size reaches the greatest of the configured gate minimum, warm-up minimum, and neighbor count.
Long: opening range is locked AND the bar is confirmed AND ((close > opening-range high in close-confirmed mode) OR (high > opening-range high in touch mode) OR (a low sweep occurred within the configured false-breakout window AND close > opening-range midpoint)) AND close-confirmation mode is not conservative or (consecutive closes above opening-range high >= configured confirmation count OR long retest count >= one) AND long retest count >= configured required retests AND long confidence >= effective confidence threshold AND long veto count <= risk-mode maximum AND no enabled ATR, volume, trend, VWAP, or wide-opening-range filter blocks the long AND elapsed time from session open <= configured late-entry limit AND outside the configured blackout AND news kill switch is off AND session signal count is below its limit AND long has not already signaled since the last eligible re-arm AND first-break-direction restriction allows long AND (KNN gate is inactive OR KNN vote >= configured minimum). Require usable volume for the volume filter; apply the VWAP filter only when usable volume exists. Wide-range filter blocks when width exceeds its configured multiple of average prior-session opening-range width or its configured ATR multiple.
Short: opening range is locked AND the bar is confirmed AND ((close < opening-range low in close-confirmed mode) OR (low < opening-range low in touch mode) OR (a high sweep occurred within the configured false-breakout window AND close < opening-range midpoint)) AND close-confirmation mode is not conservative or (consecutive closes below opening-range low >= configured confirmation count OR short retest count >= one) AND short retest count >= configured required retests AND short confidence >= effective confidence threshold AND short veto count <= risk-mode maximum AND no enabled ATR, volume, trend, VWAP, or wide-opening-range filter blocks the short AND elapsed time from session open <= configured late-entry limit AND outside the configured blackout AND news kill switch is off AND session signal count is below its limit AND short has not already signaled since the last eligible re-arm AND first-break-direction restriction allows short AND (KNN gate is inactive OR KNN vote >= configured minimum). Require usable volume for the volume filter; apply the VWAP filter only when usable volume exists. Wide-range filter blocks when width exceeds its configured multiple of average prior-session opening-range width or its configured ATR multiple.
Long: first confirmed close above the locked opening-range high in the session.
Short: first confirmed close below the locked opening-range low in the session.
Long: after the first close above the locked opening-range high, a later bar closes above it and low <= opening-range high + configured ATR tolerance, starting a new retest episode.
Short: after the first close below the locked opening-range low, a later bar closes below it and high >= opening-range low - configured ATR tolerance, starting a new retest episode.
Long: opening range is locked AND long has not been used AND late-entry limit has not passed AND long confidence >= effective confidence threshold minus the configured arming margin.
Short: opening range is locked AND short has not been used AND late-entry limit has not passed AND short confidence >= effective confidence threshold minus the configured arming margin.
Long: long is armed AND ATR > zero AND close <= opening-range high AND opening-range high - close <= configured ATR proximity AND opening-range high - prior close > prior ATR × configured proximity.
Short: short is armed AND ATR > zero AND close >= opening-range low AND close - opening-range low <= configured ATR proximity AND prior close - opening-range low > prior ATR × configured proximity.
Long: trend score >= configured bullish-trend threshold.
Short: trend score <= configured bearish-trend threshold.
Long: EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses above EMA(close, middle lookback) on a confirmed bar.
Short: EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses below EMA(close, middle lookback) on a confirmed bar.
Long: opening range is locked AND long confidence >= short confidence AND long confidence - short confidence > configured directional-bias margin.
Short: opening range is locked AND short confidence > long confidence AND short confidence - long confidence > configured directional-bias margin.
Exit: Long, long stop is touched after the entry bar, or on the touch-mode entry bar when the entry-bar stop check applies; use the stop moved to entry after target one when adaptive stop is enabled. If stop and target are touched on the same bar, apply the configured same-bar priority.
Exit: Short, short stop is touched after the entry bar, or on the touch-mode entry bar when the entry-bar stop check applies; use the stop moved to entry after target one when adaptive stop is enabled. If stop and target are touched on the same bar, apply the configured same-bar priority.
Exit: Long, high reaches target one, target two after target one, or target three after target two under a fixed-R or opening-range-projection plan; target three closes the plan. If stop and target are touched on the same bar, apply the configured same-bar priority.
Exit: Short, low reaches target one, target two after target one, or target three after target two under a fixed-R or opening-range-projection plan; target three closes the plan. If stop and target are touched on the same bar, apply the configured same-bar priority.
Exit: Long, under dynamic-EMA mode, close < EMA(close, fast lookback) when that EMA > entry price + configured ATR margin; after target one, close < EMA when that EMA > target-one price + configured ATR margin; after target two, close < EMA when that EMA > target-two price + configured ATR margin. The target-three condition closes the plan.
Exit: Short, under dynamic-EMA mode, close > EMA(close, fast lookback) when that EMA < entry price - configured ATR margin; after target one, close > EMA when that EMA < target-one price - configured ATR margin; after target two, close > EMA when that EMA < target-two price - configured ATR margin. The target-three condition closes the plan.
Exit: Long, a confirmed close below opening-range high within the configured false-breakout window after a long breakout signal and before target one, when the prior close was at or above opening-range high or the touch-mode entry bar closed inside the range.
Exit: Short, a confirmed close above opening-range low within the configured false-breakout window after a short breakout signal and before target one, when the prior close was at or below opening-range low or the touch-mode entry bar closed inside the range.
Exit: Long, a new session begins while a long plan is active.
Exit: Short, a new session begins while a short plan is active.
