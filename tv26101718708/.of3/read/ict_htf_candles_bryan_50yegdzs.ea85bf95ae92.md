Short: SMT display enabled AND, on chart bars taking values from an enabled, valid HTF set’s own timeframe parameter (T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, T6, or T7), at least one of the three reference instruments is enabled for SMT on both bars and has valid, nonmissing highs and lows on the forming bar and immediately preceding completed bar, AND (high of the forming bar > high of the preceding completed bar) on the chart instrument differs from the same comparison on that reference instrument.
