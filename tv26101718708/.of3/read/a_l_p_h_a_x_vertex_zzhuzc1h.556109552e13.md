Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands(close, BB lookback, BB standard-deviation multiplier) are strictly inside Keltner Channels(close, KC lookback, KC ATR multiplier).
Calculation: Coil length = consecutive completed chart bars ending at the current bar on which Squeeze is true, or zero if Squeeze is false.
Calculation: Valid release = Squeeze[1] AND NOT Squeeze AND Coil length[1] >= minimum coil length.
Calculation: Session gate = session filter disabled OR the current bar's UTC hour is within an enabled selected session window.
Calculation: Cooldown = no prior non-conflicted long or short signal OR the number of completed chart bars since that signal is at least the cooldown parameter.
Calculation: Long candidate = Valid release AND close >= Keltner basis(close, KC lookback) AND Session gate AND (HTF filter disabled OR (on chart bars, using lookahead-off T1 values—forming T1 bar in realtime and last available completed T1 bar historically, T1 close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1) AND that EMA > its value slope-lookback T1 bars ago)) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX(DMI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) >= ADX floor) AND (DI gate disabled OR +DI(DMI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) > -DI(DMI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback)) AND the count of these true conditions is at least the minimum confluence: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bullish RSI level AND (RSI hook disabled OR crossover(RSI, bullish RSI level) OR (RSI > bullish RSI level AND RSI > RSI[1] AND RSI[1] > RSI[2])); MACD confirmation disabled OR (MACD histogram with separate short, long, and signal lookbacks, short < long, is > 0 OR greater than its prior value); volume confirmation disabled OR volume or its moving average is unavailable OR SMA(volume, volume lookback) = 0 OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier; AND Cooldown.
Calculation: Short candidate = Valid release AND close <= Keltner basis(close, KC lookback) AND Session gate AND (HTF filter disabled OR (on chart bars, using lookahead-off T1 values—forming T1 bar in realtime and last available completed T1 bar historically, T1 close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1) AND that EMA < its value slope-lookback T1 bars ago)) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX(DMI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) >= ADX floor) AND (DI gate disabled OR -DI(DMI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) > +DI(DMI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback)) AND the count of these true conditions is at least the minimum confluence: RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bearish RSI level AND (RSI hook disabled OR crossunder(RSI, bearish RSI level) OR (RSI < bearish RSI level AND RSI < RSI[1] AND RSI[1] < RSI[2])); MACD confirmation disabled OR (MACD histogram with separate short, long, and signal lookbacks, short < long, is < 0 OR less than its prior value); volume confirmation disabled OR volume or its moving average is unavailable OR SMA(volume, volume lookback) = 0 OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier; AND Cooldown.
Long: Long candidate AND NOT Short candidate
Short: Short candidate AND NOT Long candidate
