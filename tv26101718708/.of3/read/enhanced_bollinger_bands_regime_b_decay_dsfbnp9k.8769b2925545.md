Calculation: basis = selected SMA, EMA, HMA, KAMA(fast parameter, slow parameter), or VWMA of the basis source over the basis lookback
Calculation: upper deviation = standard deviation(basis source, basis lookback) for symmetric bands; otherwise max(√2 × √(Σ(max(basis source[i] − basis[i], 0)²) / basis lookback), 0.5 × standard deviation(basis source, basis lookback))
Calculation: lower deviation = standard deviation(basis source, basis lookback) for symmetric bands; otherwise max(√2 × √(Σ(max(basis[i] − basis source[i], 0)²) / basis lookback), 0.5 × standard deviation(basis source, basis lookback))
Calculation: upper band = basis + band multiplier × upper deviation
Calculation: lower band = basis − band multiplier × lower deviation
Calculation: %B = (close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band), or 0.5 when the band width is zero
Calculation: squeeze = selected TTM test (lower band > basis − Keltner multiplier × SMA(selected true range or high − low, Keltner lookback) AND upper band < basis + Keltner multiplier × SMA(selected true range or high − low, Keltner lookback)), bandwidth-percentile test (percent rank of (upper band − lower band) / basis × 100 over the bandwidth lookback < squeeze percentile, using bandwidth 0 when basis is 0), or both tests ANDed
Calculation: active upper walk = not squeeze AND close is above the upper band for at least the walk length consecutive chart bars; active lower walk = not squeeze AND close is below the lower band for at least the walk length consecutive chart bars; each consecutive-bar count resets when its close condition fails
Calculation: walk-extreme state = on entering an active walk, initialize its extreme to the current close and %B and its decay count to 0; while active, on each new higher close in an upper walk or new lower close in a lower walk, increment that side's count if %B is lower than its prior extreme's %B by more than the minimum decay magnitude (upper) or higher by more than that magnitude (lower), otherwise reset its count to 0, then store the new close and %B; reset tracking when the walk becomes inactive
Short: active upper walk AND current close > prior upper-walk extreme close AND current %B < prior upper-walk extreme %B − minimum decay magnitude AND updated upper-walk decay count ≥ minimum consecutive decays
Long: active lower walk AND current close < prior lower-walk extreme close AND current %B > prior lower-walk extreme %B + minimum decay magnitude AND updated lower-walk decay count ≥ minimum consecutive decays
