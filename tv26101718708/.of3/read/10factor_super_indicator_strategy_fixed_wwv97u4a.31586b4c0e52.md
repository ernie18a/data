Calculation: ATR percentage = if close != 0 then 100 × ATR(close, ATR lookback) / close, else 0
Calculation: composite score = if the sum of the separate EMA, Supertrend, MACD, RSI, ADX, VWAP, Bollinger, Stochastic RSI, OBV, and ATR weights is greater than 0, then 100 × the sum of each factor’s weight multiplied by its vote, divided by that total weight, else 0; each vote is +1 when its bullish condition holds, −1 when its bearish condition holds, and 0 otherwise; the ATR vote is +1 when ATR percentage > minimum ATR percentage and 0 otherwise; EMA conditions are EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) for bullish, and both inequalities reversed for bearish; Supertrend conditions are bullish and bearish direction, respectively, using separate ATR lookback and multiplier parameters; MACD conditions are MACD line > signal line and MACD line < signal line, respectively, using fast, slow, and signal lookbacks, with fast shorter than slow; RSI conditions are RSI(close, RSI lookback) > upper RSI threshold and RSI(close, RSI lookback) < lower RSI threshold, respectively; ADX conditions are ADX > ADX minimum AND +DI > −DI, and ADX > ADX minimum AND −DI > +DI, respectively, from DMI(high, low, close, DMI lookback, ADX smoothing); VWAP conditions are close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) and close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3), respectively; Bollinger conditions are close > basis AND close < upper band, and close < basis AND close > lower band, respectively, using separate lookback and deviation multiplier parameters; Stochastic RSI conditions are K > D AND K > lower stochastic bound AND K < upper stochastic bound, and K < D AND K > lower stochastic bound AND K < upper stochastic bound, respectively, using separate lookback, K smoothing, and D smoothing parameters; OBV conditions are OBV(close, volume) > EMA(OBV(close, volume), OBV smoothing lookback) and OBV(close, volume) < EMA(OBV(close, volume), OBV smoothing lookback), respectively
Long: composite score ≥ long score threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND Supertrend direction is bullish AND ADX > ADX minimum AND ATR percentage > minimum ATR percentage
Short: composite score ≤ short score threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND Supertrend direction is bearish AND ADX > ADX minimum AND ATR percentage > minimum ATR percentage
Exit: long, composite score < exit score threshold OR low ≤ entry price − ATR(close, ATR lookback) × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + ATR(close, ATR lookback) × take-profit multiplier
Exit: short, composite score > −exit score threshold OR high ≥ entry price + ATR(close, ATR lookback) × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − ATR(close, ATR lookback) × take-profit multiplier
