Calculation: Band state(a, b) = per-pair state set to +1 when a − b > k × ATR(lookback, chart bars), set to −1 when a − b < −k × ATR(lookback, chart bars), and otherwise retains its prior value; initialize at 0.
Calculation: Normalized MA rate(M, n) = 100 × (M − M[n]) / (n × M), using chart bars and defined only when M is nonzero and both values exist.
Long: For each of five independently configured MAs M (SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, HMA, VWMA, TEMA, KAMA, or JMA of a selected close, open, high, low, hl2, hlc3, or ohlc4, with its own lookback and timeframe T), on chart bars using the last completed T bar historically and the still-forming T bar live: close crosses above M when M is visible; OR a selected fast MA crosses above a selected slow MA in any enabled pair slot; OR the selected fast MA crosses above the selected slow MA when the Golden Cross marking is enabled; OR, on the last chart bar with the alignment row enabled and at least two displayed or included MAs, their values form a strictly descending sequence when ordered by lookback × timeframe duration; OR, when the rate column is enabled, any displayed or included M has Normalized MA rate(M, n) greater than the flat threshold. For each crossing condition, use the ordinary cross when the noise band is disabled; when enabled, use Band state(a, b) changing from −1 to +1.
Short: Using the same five MAs and chart-bar sampling: close crosses below M when M is visible; OR a selected fast MA crosses below a selected slow MA in any enabled pair slot; OR the selected fast MA crosses below the selected slow MA when the Golden Cross marking is enabled; OR, on the last chart bar with the alignment row enabled and at least two displayed or included MAs, their values form a strictly ascending sequence when ordered by lookback × timeframe duration; OR, when the rate column is enabled, any displayed or included M has Normalized MA rate(M, n) less than the negative flat threshold. For each crossing condition, use the ordinary cross when the noise band is disabled; when enabled, use Band state(a, b) changing from +1 to −1.
