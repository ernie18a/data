Calculation: Smoothed stochastic RSI sum(source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing) = z + SMA(z, K smoothing) + SMA(SMA(z, K smoothing), D smoothing), where z = Stochastic(RSI(source, RSI lookback), RSI(source, RSI lookback), RSI(source, RSI lookback), stochastic lookback).
Calculation: Mean completed-HTF smoothed stochastic RSI = the arithmetic mean of Smoothed stochastic RSI sum(close, shared RSI, stochastic, K, and D parameters) on the last completed bars of each independently specified higher timeframe T1, T2, T3, and T4, sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Recent confirmed minimum and maximum = On chart bars, when neither extreme watcher is active, arm a low watcher if the mean is at or below the lower watch threshold; otherwise arm a high watcher if it is at or above the upper watch threshold. A low watcher triggers when the mean reaches or exceeds the reversal midpoint; a high watcher triggers when it reaches or falls below that midpoint. A trigger clears both watchers and starts its tracker at the current mean, with count zero; process the trigger bar immediately, incrementing its count because it sets no new extreme. The high-watcher trigger starts a falling tracker: each strict new minimum resets its count, and each other bar increments it; when the count reaches the confirmation-bars parameter, emit a confirmed minimum and stop that tracker. The low-watcher trigger starts a rising tracker: each strict new maximum resets its count, and each other bar increments it; when the count reaches the confirmation-bars parameter, emit a confirmed maximum and stop that tracker. Watchers and trackers operate independently. A recent confirmed minimum or maximum is an event in the inclusive rolling window of max(1, round(T5 duration / chart-bar duration)) chart bars.
Calculation: Long EMA pullback retest event = On chart bars, a crossover of EMA(close, short EMA lookback) above EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) or EMA(close, long EMA lookback) starts or restarts the long process, recording the short EMA as the base and peak. While rising, update the peak to the highest short EMA. After the reversal-count parameter consecutive strict declines, lock the peak. Once peak minus base is positive and peak minus current short EMA is at least that height times the pullback-depth parameter, enter the retest phase. Set the retest level to peak minus that height times (1 minus the early-retest parameter). On the first confirmed chart bar when the short EMA is at or above this level, finish the process and emit an event only if EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) is above EMA(close, long EMA lookback). Apply the phase checks in order so pullback qualification and retest can occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Short EMA pullback retest event = On chart bars, a crossunder of EMA(close, short EMA lookback) below EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) or EMA(close, long EMA lookback) starts or restarts the short process, recording the short EMA as the base and trough. While falling, update the trough to the lowest short EMA. After the reversal-count parameter consecutive strict increases, lock the trough. Once base minus trough is positive and current short EMA minus trough is at least that height times the pullback-depth parameter, enter the retest phase. Set the retest level to trough plus that height times (1 minus the early-retest parameter). On the first confirmed chart bar when the short EMA is at or below this level, finish the process and emit an event only if EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) is below EMA(close, long EMA lookback). Apply the phase checks in order so pullback qualification and retest can occur on the same bar.
Long: Long EMA pullback retest event AND NOT a recent confirmed minimum
Long: Long EMA pullback retest event AND a recent confirmed minimum
Short: Short EMA pullback retest event AND NOT a recent confirmed maximum
Short: Short EMA pullback retest event AND a recent confirmed maximum
