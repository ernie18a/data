Calculation: Fourier coefficient(source field, frequency, lookback) = (1/lookback when frequency = 0 or frequency = lookback/2, otherwise 2/lookback) × sum(source[i] × cos(2π × frequency × i/lookback)) for i = 0 to lookback − 1
Calculation: Trend MA(length, frequency) = optionally apply LinearRegression(value, regression lookback, regression offset) to either the selected shared MA of close + Fourier coefficient(configured source field, frequency, Fourier lookback), using length, or that adjusted close when MA smoothing is disabled; the selected MA is EMA, SMA, ALMA, FRAMA, RMA, SWMA, VWMA, WMA, or LinearRegression, with separate ALMA, FRAMA, and inner LinearRegression parameters
Long: Trend MA(shorter lookback, short-frequency parameter) > Trend MA(medium lookback, medium-frequency parameter) AND close > Trend MA(longer lookback, long-frequency parameter)
Short: Trend MA(shorter lookback, short-frequency parameter) < Trend MA(medium lookback, medium-frequency parameter) AND close < Trend MA(longer lookback, long-frequency parameter)
Exit: long, take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance in ticks OR stop-loss enabled AND low <= entry price − stop-loss distance in ticks OR trailing stop and offset enabled AND high >= entry price + trailing activation distance in ticks AND low <= highest high since activation − trailing offset in ticks
Exit: short, take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price − take-profit distance in ticks OR stop-loss enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance in ticks OR trailing stop and offset enabled AND low <= entry price − trailing activation distance in ticks AND high >= lowest low since activation + trailing offset in ticks
