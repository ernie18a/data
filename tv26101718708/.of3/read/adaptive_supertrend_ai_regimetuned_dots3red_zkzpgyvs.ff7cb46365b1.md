Calculation: Smoothed market state = mode of current and prior raw market states over the state-smoothing lookback on chart bars, resolving ties in this order: volatile, trending, ranging, uncertain; each raw state is volatile if ATR(ATR-ratio lookback) / max(SMA(ATR(ATR-ratio lookback), ATR-baseline lookback), denominator floor) > volatility threshold, otherwise trending if the mean of min(ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) / ADX normalization divisor, normalization cap) and normalization cap - min((Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) - choppiness offset) / choppiness scale, normalization cap) > trend threshold, otherwise ranging if that mean < range threshold, otherwise uncertain.
Calculation: Adaptive band multiplier = target multiplier on initialization; thereafter previous adaptive band multiplier + (target multiplier - previous adaptive band multiplier) / multiplier-transition length, where the target multiplier is the separate trending, ranging, volatile, or uncertain multiplier selected by the smoothed market state.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, the classic SuperTrend(hl2, ATR lookback, adaptive band multiplier), initialized up, has its prior direction down and close > its current upper band.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the classic SuperTrend(hl2, ATR lookback, adaptive band multiplier), initialized up, has its prior direction up and close < its current lower band.
