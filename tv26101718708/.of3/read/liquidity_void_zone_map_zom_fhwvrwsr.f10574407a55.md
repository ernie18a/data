Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), volume floor parameter)
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment parameter)
Calculation: Bullish void zone = on a chart bar with at least two prior bars where low > high[2], low - high[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * gap multiple, close > open, abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * body multiple, close location > bullish location threshold, and volume ratio >= volume threshold, create a zone with top = low and bottom = max(high[2], low - ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum zone height multiple); retain only the newest maximum zone count total zones, expire when age exceeds maximum zone age in chart bars, and mark broken when close < bottom
Calculation: Bearish void zone = on a chart bar with at least two prior bars where high < low[2], low[2] - high >= ATR(ATR lookback) * gap multiple, close < open, abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * body multiple, close location < bearish location threshold, and volume ratio >= volume threshold, create a zone with top = min(low[2], high + ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum zone height multiple) and bottom = high; retain only the newest maximum zone count total zones, expire when age exceeds maximum zone age in chart bars, and mark broken when close > top
Long: On a later chart bar, any retained, unbroken bullish void zone is touched (high >= bottom AND low <= top), and close > top AND close > open AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bullish RSI threshold
Short: On a later chart bar, any retained, unbroken bearish void zone is touched (high >= bottom AND low <= top), and close < bottom AND close < open AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bearish RSI threshold
