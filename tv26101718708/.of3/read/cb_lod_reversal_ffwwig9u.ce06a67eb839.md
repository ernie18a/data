Calculation: completed average volume = SMA(volume, volume lookback, T2), evaluated on T2 bars at the last completed T2 bar and carried to T1 bars
Long: On a completed eligible T1 bar (all T1 bars when the session-only parameter is off, otherwise regular-session T1 bars), close > the close of the most recent earlier eligible T1 bar in the same T2 day whose low < every earlier eligible T1 low that day (at least one), no eligible T1 bar between that setup bar and the current bar has closed above the setup close, and completed average volume >= minimum average volume
Short: On a completed eligible T1 bar immediately following an eligible T1 bar in the same T2 day whose high > every earlier eligible T1 high that day (at least one), close < the close of that new-high bar, and completed average volume >= minimum average volume
