Calculation: signed streak = consecutive rising closes counted positively, consecutive falling closes counted negatively, and zero when the close is unchanged; reset to one in the new direction when direction reverses
Calculation: volume-weighted median price = the close at which cumulative volume first reaches half the total volume after sorting the collected closes by price
Calculation: score = clamp(score scale × HMA(momentum weight × (close − volume-weighted median price) / max(EMA(high − low, range lookback), minimum price increment) + oscillator weight × (mean(RSI(close, longer RSI lookback), RSI(signed streak, shorter RSI lookback), PercentRank(ROC(close, ROC lookback), rank lookback)) − oscillator center) / oscillator scale + volume weight × clamp(ZScore(volume, volume lookback) / volume scale, negative unit bound, positive unit bound), score smoothing lookback), negative score bound, positive score bound)
Calculation: eligible high pivot pair = successive confirmed PivotHigh(score, equal pivot-side lookbacks) values separated by no more than the divergence lookback
Calculation: eligible low pivot pair = successive confirmed PivotLow(score, equal pivot-side lookbacks) values separated by no more than the divergence lookback
Calculation: regular bullish divergence = eligible low pivot pair AND current score pivot low > prior score pivot low AND low at current pivot < low at prior pivot
Calculation: hidden bullish divergence = eligible low pivot pair AND current score pivot low < prior score pivot low AND low at current pivot > low at prior pivot
Calculation: regular bearish divergence = eligible high pivot pair AND current score pivot high < prior score pivot high AND high at current pivot > high at prior pivot
Calculation: hidden bearish divergence = eligible high pivot pair AND current score pivot high > prior score pivot high AND high at current pivot < high at prior pivot
