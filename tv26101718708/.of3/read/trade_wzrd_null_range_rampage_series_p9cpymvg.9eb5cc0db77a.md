Calculation: Volume center = the midpoint of the first equal-price bin whose cumulative allocated volume reaches half the total volume, using profile bin count bins over the range from highest(high, range lookback) to lowest(low, range lookback); allocate each bar's volume across bins in proportion to overlap with its low-to-high range; if total allocated volume is zero, use the range midpoint.
Calculation: Volume sigma = the square root of the volume-weighted mean squared difference between bin midpoints and their volume-weighted mean, using the same bins and allocations as Volume center; if total allocated volume is zero, use one quarter of the range width.
Calculation: Volume-center position = (Volume center - lowest(low, range lookback)) / (highest(high, range lookback) - lowest(low, range lookback)) * 100.
Calculation: Upper wall = Volume center + Volume sigma * outer-wall sigma multiplier.
Calculation: Lower wall = Volume center - Volume sigma * outer-wall sigma multiplier.
Calculation: Upper outside count = consecutive chart bars ending on the current bar with high above Upper wall, reset to zero when the channel is invalid or high is not above Upper wall.
Calculation: Lower outside count = consecutive chart bars ending on the current bar with low below Lower wall, reset to zero when the channel is invalid or low is not below Lower wall.
Calculation: Normalized kinetic fuel = normalize(volume * abs(close - close[1])) between its rolling lowest and highest values over the normalization lookback; use zero when the raw value is missing or the rolling highest equals the rolling lowest.
Calculation: Long stop = at the entry signal, place the stop below either the signal-bar low or Lower wall, as selected by the stop model, by ATR(ATR lookback) * stop buffer; if this is not below the signal close, use signal close - ATR(ATR lookback).
Calculation: Short stop = at the entry signal, place the stop above either the signal-bar high or Upper wall, as selected by the stop model, by ATR(ATR lookback) * stop buffer; if this is not above the signal close, use signal close + ATR(ATR lookback).
Calculation: Long target = at the entry signal, use Volume center when the selected target is Volume center and its distance above the signal close exceeds ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum target distance; otherwise use the midpoint between Lower wall and Volume center when the selected target is half-channel and its distance above the signal close exceeds that minimum; otherwise use signal close + (signal close - Long stop) * reward-to-risk multiple.
Calculation: Short target = at the entry signal, use Volume center when the selected target is Volume center and its distance below the signal close exceeds ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum target distance; otherwise use the midpoint between Upper wall and Volume center when the selected target is half-channel and its distance below the signal close exceeds that minimum; otherwise use signal close - (Short stop - signal close) * reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close with a valid channel, the signal model permits either a discount trap (close > Lower wall AND ((low < Lower wall AND close[1] > Lower wall AND Lower outside count[1] <= trap window) OR (close[1] < Lower wall AND Lower outside count[1] <= trap window))) or an upward Volume center reclaim (close > Volume center AND close[1] <= Volume center[1] AND close - Volume center > ATR(ATR lookback) * reclaim-distance parameter); volume is missing, its average is missing, or the volume gate is off, or volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume gate; no earlier signal exists or more than the cooldown bars have elapsed since the last signal; any raw long trap has extension depth (Lower wall - low) / ATR(ATR lookback) <= maximum extension and its same-side depth-bucket history passes the probability filter; any raw reclaim on either side has its same-side, balance-alignment-bucket history pass the probability filter; and, when balance alignment is required, Volume-center position < long balance cutoff. A history bucket passes when it has fewer than minimum samples or no rate, or its rounded win percentage from completed trades is at least minimum win probability. Trap depth buckets use four ranges separated by three extension thresholds; reclaim buckets distinguish same-side aligned and counter-aligned cases using the balance midpoint.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close with a valid channel, the signal model permits either a premium trap (close < Upper wall AND ((high > Upper wall AND close[1] < Upper wall AND Upper outside count[1] <= trap window) OR (close[1] > Upper wall AND Upper outside count[1] <= trap window))) or a downward Volume center reclaim (close < Volume center AND close[1] >= Volume center[1] AND Volume center - close > ATR(ATR lookback) * reclaim-distance parameter); volume is missing, its average is missing, or the volume gate is off, or volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume gate; no earlier signal exists or more than the cooldown bars have elapsed since the last signal; any raw short trap has extension depth (high - Upper wall) / ATR(ATR lookback) <= maximum extension and its same-side depth-bucket history passes the probability filter; any raw reclaim on either side has its same-side, balance-alignment-bucket history pass the probability filter; and, when balance alignment is required, Volume-center position > short balance cutoff. A history bucket passes when it has fewer than minimum samples or no rate, or its rounded win percentage from completed trades is at least minimum win probability. Trap depth buckets use four ranges separated by three extension thresholds; reclaim buckets distinguish same-side aligned and counter-aligned cases using the balance midpoint. The long entry condition is false.
Exit: Long, on a chart bar after the entry bar, low <= Long stop OR (high >= Long target AND low > Long stop).
Exit: Short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, high >= Short stop OR (low <= Short target AND high < Short stop).
