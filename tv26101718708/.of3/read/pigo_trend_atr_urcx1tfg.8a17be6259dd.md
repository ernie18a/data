Calculation: ImpulseFilter(T) = d != SMA(d, impulse SMA lookback) AND d[1] != SMA(d, impulse SMA lookback)[1], where m = ZLEMA((high + low + close) / 3, impulse lookback), h = SMMA(high, impulse lookback), l = SMMA(low, impulse lookback), and d = m - h if m > h, m - l if m < l, otherwise 0; calculate on T bars and sample chart bars from the still-forming T bar.
Calculation: Direction(T) = up when (flat filter disabled OR ImpulseFilter(T)) AND ((single-EMA mode AND EMA(close, EMA1 lookback) > EMA(close, EMA1 lookback)[1]) OR (two-EMA mode AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback))); down when the same filter holds and the corresponding comparison is <; neutral otherwise. Calculate on T bars and sample chart bars from the still-forming T bar; [1] means the previous T bar.
Long: Direction(T) = up on a displayed timeframe
Short: Direction(T) = down on a displayed timeframe
Long: Direction(T1) = up AND Direction(T2) = up AND Direction(T3) = up AND Direction(T4) = up AND Direction(T4)[1] != up AND high(T4) - low(T4) <= exhaustion fraction * ATR(ATR lookback, T4); T1 > T2 > T3 > T4, and T4 is the entry/chart timeframe. Higher-timeframe values are sampled on T4 chart bars from still-forming bars.
Short: Direction(T1) = down AND Direction(T2) = down AND Direction(T3) = down AND Direction(T4) = down AND Direction(T4)[1] != down AND high(T4) - low(T4) <= exhaustion fraction * ATR(ATR lookback, T4); T1 > T2 > T3 > T4, and T4 is the entry/chart timeframe. Higher-timeframe values are sampled on T4 chart bars from still-forming bars.
Exit: Long, low <= entry_price - stop ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T4 at entry) OR high >= entry_price + minimum target ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T3 at entry), with T3 ATR sampled on T4 chart bars from the still-forming T3 bar at entry.
Exit: Short, high >= entry_price + stop ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T4 at entry) OR low <= entry_price - minimum target ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T3 at entry), with T3 ATR sampled on T4 chart bars from the still-forming T3 bar at entry.
