Calculation: opening-box high = highest high during the configured opening session
Calculation: opening-box low = lowest low during the configured opening session
Calculation: opening-box height = opening-box high - opening-box low
Calculation: breakout buffer = ATR(ATR lookback) * breakout-buffer parameter
Calculation: retest tolerance = ATR(ATR lookback) * retest-tolerance parameter
Calculation: breakout relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, RVOL lookback), or 1 when that SMA is not positive
Calculation: entry-box high = opening-box high captured when the entry order is submitted
Calculation: entry-box low = opening-box low captured when the entry order is submitted
Calculation: entry-box height = opening-box height captured when the entry order is submitted
Calculation: long initial stop = entry-box high - stop-distance parameter * entry-box height
Calculation: short initial stop = entry-box low + stop-distance parameter * entry-box height
Calculation: initial risk = absolute(entry price - position-side initial stop)
Long: completed opening box AND close crosses above opening-box high + breakout buffer AND no earlier qualified breakout in this box AND (regime filter disabled OR (close > EMA(close, regime lookback) on T1 bars using the last completed T1 value on chart bars AND that EMA value > its value on the preceding completed T1 bar)) AND (RVOL filter disabled OR breakout relative volume >= minimum relative volume)
Short: completed opening box AND close crosses below opening-box low - breakout buffer AND no earlier qualified breakout in this box AND (regime filter disabled OR (close < EMA(close, regime lookback) on T1 bars using the last completed T1 value on chart bars AND that EMA value < its value on the preceding completed T1 bar)) AND (RVOL filter disabled OR breakout relative volume >= minimum relative volume)
Long: (entry mode selects direct breakout AND no entry already taken in this box AND a qualified long breakout) OR (entry mode selects retest confirmation AND no entry already taken in this box AND a qualified long breakout was followed by a first retest of the upper boundary within the retest window AND current low <= opening-box high + retest tolerance AND ((outside-close confirmation is required AND close > opening-box high) OR (otherwise AND close > open AND close >= opening-box high - retest tolerance)))
Short: (entry mode selects direct breakout AND no entry already taken in this box AND a qualified short breakout) OR (entry mode selects retest confirmation AND no entry already taken in this box AND a qualified short breakout was followed by a first retest of the lower boundary within the retest window AND current high >= opening-box low - retest tolerance AND ((outside-close confirmation is required AND close < opening-box low) OR (otherwise AND close < open AND close <= opening-box low + retest tolerance)))
Exit: Long, low <= long initial stop OR (breakeven adjustment enabled AND initial risk > 0 AND highest high since entry >= entry price + breakeven-trigger multiple * initial risk AND low <= max(long initial stop, entry price)) OR (measured-box target selected AND high >= entry-box high + measured-target multiple * entry-box height) OR (fixed-risk target selected AND high >= entry price + fixed-risk target multiple * initial risk) OR (next-opening-box exit selected AND a new configured opening session begins)
Exit: Short, high >= short initial stop OR (breakeven adjustment enabled AND initial risk > 0 AND lowest low since entry <= entry price - breakeven-trigger multiple * initial risk AND high >= min(short initial stop, entry price)) OR (measured-box target selected AND low <= entry-box low - measured-target multiple * entry-box height) OR (fixed-risk target selected AND low <= entry price - fixed-risk target multiple * initial risk) OR (next-opening-box exit selected AND a new configured opening session begins)
