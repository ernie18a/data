Calculation: Middle band = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA of the selected price source over the MA lookback, calculated on the selected candle series at the chart timeframe or T1; on T1 bars, use the bar still forming and map values to chart bars. The selected series is regular, Heikin Ashi, Renko with an ATR lookback, Line Break with a reversal-count parameter, Kagi with a reversal-amount parameter, or Point & Figure with high/low source, traditional sizing, a box-size parameter, and a reversal-count parameter.
Calculation: Upper Bollinger band = middle band + selected deviation × upper multiplier; lower Bollinger band = middle band − selected deviation × lower multiplier. The deviation is ATR with its separate lookback or standard deviation of the selected price source over the MA lookback, calculated on the same selected candle series and timeframe and mapped to chart bars.
Calculation: Bollinger Band Width = (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band) / middle band.
Calculation: Percent B = (selected candle series close at the selected timeframe − lower Bollinger band) / (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band), using selected-timeframe values mapped to chart bars.
Long: datetime is within the configured inclusive start and finish timestamps AND the selected Long Open criteria meet the configured requirement (any, all, or at least the separate minimum count) AND the selected Long Close criteria do not meet their configured requirement; each criterion can be assigned to neither, Long Open, Long Close, or both: chart close crosses above or below the upper, middle, or lower Bollinger band; chart close is above or below the upper, middle, or lower Bollinger band; Bollinger Band Width crosses above or below, is above, or is below either of two separate width thresholds; Percent B crosses above either of two separate Percent B thresholds or crosses below either threshold. Band comparisons use chart-bar close and band values mapped to chart bars.
Exit: Long, datetime is outside the configured inclusive start and finish timestamps OR the selected Long Close criteria meet the configured requirement (any, all, or at least the separate minimum count).
Exit: Long, high reaches entry price + (close on the latest bar that issued a long-entry order × take-profit percent / 100) OR low reaches entry price − (close on the latest bar that issued a long-entry order × stop-loss percent / 100).
