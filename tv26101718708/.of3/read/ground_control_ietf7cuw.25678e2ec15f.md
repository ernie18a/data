Calculation: selected main moving average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or HMA(close, main-MA lookback)
Calculation: selected fast moving average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or HMA(close, separate fast-MA lookback)
Calculation: percent below selected main moving average = (selected main moving average - close) / selected main moving average * 100
Calculation: percent above selected main moving average = (close - selected main moving average) / selected main moving average * 100
Long: Long trading is enabled AND the cool-off filter permits entry (cool-off is disabled, no exit has occurred, the most recent exit was not a net loss, or the configured chart-bar count since the most recent exit has elapsed) AND at least one entry test is enabled AND the enabled entry tests are combined by AND when all-required is selected or by OR otherwise: RSI(close, RSI lookback) is below or above its separate entry threshold as selected; SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-average lookback) is below or above its separate entry threshold as selected; MFI(hlc3, MFI lookback) is below or above its separate entry threshold as selected; MACD entry test is selected MACD line crossing above signal line, MACD histogram below its entry threshold, or MACD line below its entry threshold; close is below or above the selected main moving average; percent below or above that average is at least its separate entry percentage threshold; selected fast moving average crosses above or below the selected main moving average; or Supertrend(ATR lookback, ATR multiplier) is in the selected entry direction or changes into that direction, according to the selected trend or signal mode
Short: Short trading is enabled AND the cool-off filter permits entry (cool-off is disabled, no exit has occurred, the most recent exit was not a net loss, or the configured chart-bar count since the most recent exit has elapsed) AND at least one entry test is enabled AND the enabled entry tests are combined by AND when all-required is selected or by OR otherwise; use the corresponding long entry tests unchanged when short inversion is disabled, and when enabled invert them as follows: for each RSI, RSI average, or MFI test, a selected below test becomes greater than 100 minus its separate entry threshold and a selected above test becomes less than 100 minus its threshold; MACD crossover becomes crossunder, histogram-below becomes histogram greater than negative its entry threshold, and line-below becomes line greater than negative its entry threshold; below/above moving-average tests reverse, and each percentage test uses the opposite-side percentage calculation and the opposite-side entry percentage threshold; the selected moving-average cross direction reverses; and the selected Supertrend direction reverses, including the direction of a signal change
Exit: Long, any enabled stop-loss or take-profit is reached (low is at or below entry price minus the dollar stop amount or entry price times one minus the stop percentage; high is at or above entry price plus the dollar target amount or entry price times one plus the target percentage) OR indicator exit tests trigger while take-profit override is not enabled with take profit: at least one exit test is enabled and the enabled tests are combined by AND when all-required is selected or by OR otherwise; RSI(close, RSI lookback), SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-average lookback), and MFI(hlc3, MFI lookback) are each tested below or above their separate exit thresholds as selected; MACD exit test is selected MACD line crossing below signal line, MACD histogram above its exit threshold, or MACD line above its exit threshold; selected fast moving average crosses in the selected exit direction; Supertrend is in the selected exit direction or changes into that direction according to the selected trend or signal mode; close is below or above the selected main moving average; or percent below or above that average is at least its separate exit percentage threshold
Exit: Short, any enabled stop-loss or take-profit is reached (high is at or above entry price plus the dollar stop amount or entry price times one plus the stop percentage; low is at or below entry price minus the dollar target amount or entry price times one minus the target percentage) OR indicator exit tests trigger while take-profit override is not enabled with take profit: at least one exit test is enabled and the enabled tests are combined by AND when all-required is selected or by OR otherwise; use the corresponding long exit tests unchanged when short inversion is disabled, and when enabled invert RSI, RSI-average, and MFI thresholds and comparisons around 100, invert MACD crossover to cross above and histogram-above or line-above to below negative the separate exit threshold, reverse the selected moving-average cross direction and Supertrend direction, and reverse above/below moving-average tests; for the below-percentage exit test use percent above the average and the above-percentage exit threshold, and for the above-percentage exit test use percent below the average and the below-percentage exit threshold
