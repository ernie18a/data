Calculation: Accumulation bounds = Highest(high, accumulation lookback) and Lowest(low, accumulation lookback) when their difference ≤ ATR(ATR lookback) × maximum accumulation range multiplier; while a bar stays within the bounds expanded by ATR(ATR lookback) × sweep buffer multiplier, extend the bounds to new highs and lows.
Calculation: Candle body fraction = 0 when high = low; otherwise abs(close − open) / (high − low).
Calculation: HTF bias(T) = 1 when close > SMA(close, HTF lookback) on T, −1 when close < SMA(close, HTF lookback) on T, and 0 otherwise; calculate on T bars using the current bar, which may still be forming, and map values to chart bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, after a qualifying accumulation and a recorded low sweep (the sweep bar's low < recorded lower bound − ATR(ATR lookback) × sweep buffer multiplier; an upper-bound breach takes priority if both bounds are breached on that bar), close > recorded upper bound AND (displacement requirement is off OR candle body fraction ≥ minimum displacement body fraction) AND base score points + (HTF bonus points if HTF bias(T1) = 1 AND HTF bias(T2) = 1, otherwise 0) ≥ minimum score.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, after a qualifying accumulation and a recorded high sweep (the sweep bar's high > recorded upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) × sweep buffer multiplier), close < recorded lower bound AND (displacement requirement is off OR candle body fraction ≥ minimum displacement body fraction) AND base score points + (HTF bonus points if HTF bias(T1) = −1 AND HTF bias(T2) = −1, otherwise 0) ≥ minimum score.
