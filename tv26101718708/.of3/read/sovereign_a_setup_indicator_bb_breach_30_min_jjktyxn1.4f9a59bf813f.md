Calculation: Bull trend alignment = On chart bars, SMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > SMA(close, longer trend lookback) on each of four distinct requested timeframes T1, T2, T3, and T4; calculate each SMA on that timeframe’s bars using its current bar, still forming in realtime, with lookahead off, then map the values to chart bars.
Calculation: Bear trend alignment = On chart bars, SMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < SMA(close, longer trend lookback) on each of four distinct requested timeframes T1, T2, T3, and T4; calculate each SMA on that timeframe’s bars using its current bar, still forming in realtime, with lookahead off, then map the values to chart bars.
Calculation: Bull EMA stack = EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, medium lookback) AND EMA(close, medium lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback).
Calculation: Bear EMA stack = EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, medium lookback) AND EMA(close, medium lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback).
Calculation: Bull setup phase = An idle setup becomes waiting for BOS when Bull trend alignment AND Bull EMA stack AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback) AND low < lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation parameter); store the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot sensitivity, pivot sensitivity) as its target and the current low as its breach low. While waiting, update the breach low to the lowest low; if a target exists and close > the stored target, change to armed and initialize impulse high to high. On each armed chart bar, update impulse high to the highest high since BOS; prep level = impulse high - (impulse high - breach low) * prep retracement parameter; entry-zone level = impulse high - (impulse high - breach low) * entry-zone retracement parameter. Reset after that bar’s armed marks if low <= entry-zone level + the applicable pip buffer, Bull EMA stack fails, Bull trend alignment fails, or close < breach low; allow one prep mark per setup.
Calculation: Bear setup phase = An idle setup becomes waiting for BOS when Bear trend alignment AND Bear EMA stack AND close < EMA(close, fast lookback) AND high > upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation parameter); store the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot sensitivity, pivot sensitivity) as its target and the current high as its breach high. While waiting, update the breach high to the highest high; if a target exists and close < the stored target, change to armed and initialize impulse low to low. On each armed chart bar, update impulse low to the lowest low since BOS; prep level = impulse low + (breach high - impulse low) * prep retracement parameter; entry-zone level = impulse low + (breach high - impulse low) * entry-zone retracement parameter. Reset after that bar’s armed marks if high >= entry-zone level - the applicable pip buffer, Bear EMA stack fails, Bear trend alignment fails, or close > breach high; allow one prep mark per setup.
Long: A bull setup is waiting for BOS AND close > its stored PivotHigh target.
Short: A bear setup is waiting for BOS AND close < its stored PivotLow target.
Long: A bull setup is armed.
Short: A bear setup is armed.
Long: A bull setup is armed AND its prep mark has not fired AND low <= its prep level.
Short: A bear setup is armed AND its prep mark has not fired AND high >= its prep level.
