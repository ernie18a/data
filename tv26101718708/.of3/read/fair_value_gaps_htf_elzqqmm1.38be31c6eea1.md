Calculation: Bullish FVG on T1/T2/T3 = zone [high[2], low] when the current timeframe bar has low > high[2]; values are sampled on chart bars and may use a still-forming timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bearish FVG on T1/T2/T3 = zone [high, low[2]] when the current timeframe bar has high < low[2]; values are sampled on chart bars and may use a still-forming timeframe bar.
Calculation: An FVG zone is unmitigated until the selected mitigation mode is met on a chart bar: Tap = bullish low <= zone top or bearish high >= zone bottom; Close inside = zone bottom <= close <= zone top; Full fill = bullish close < zone bottom or bearish close > zone top.
Long: On a chart bar, for any enabled T1/T2/T3, a bullish FVG is newly observed when its middle-bar time is nonmissing and was missing or differed on the previous chart-bar sample.
Short: On a chart bar, for any enabled T1/T2/T3, a bearish FVG is newly observed when its middle-bar time is nonmissing and was missing or differed on the previous chart-bar sample.
Long: On a chart bar, bullish-zone overlap was false on the previous chart bar and is true now: low <= zone top and high >= zone bottom for any unmitigated stored bullish FVG from an enabled T1/T2/T3 with its bullish display switch enabled. Store a new zone only when its gap height is at least the minimum-size percentage of ATR(ATR lookback) on that timeframe's current bar (unless the minimum is zero; use 1 when ATR is missing or <= 0); retain at most 3 times the per-timeframe FVG history limit, counting both directions.
Short: On a chart bar, bearish-zone overlap was false on the previous chart bar and is true now: high >= zone bottom and low <= zone top for any unmitigated stored bearish FVG from an enabled T1/T2/T3 with its bearish display switch enabled. Store a new zone only when its gap height is at least the minimum-size percentage of ATR(ATR lookback) on that timeframe's current bar (unless the minimum is zero; use 1 when ATR is missing or <= 0); retain at most 3 times the per-timeframe FVG history limit, counting both directions.
