Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above its latest confirmed pivot low (left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long-zone threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) on T1 bars, still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars > higher-timeframe threshold
Short: RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below its latest confirmed pivot high (left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short-zone threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) on T1 bars, still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars < higher-timeframe threshold
Exit: long, low <= close * (1 - stop-distance parameter) OR high >= close * (1 + target-distance parameter) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= upper Bollinger Band(RSI(close, RSI lookback), band lookback, band deviation multiplier) * long exit factor
Exit: short, high >= close * (1 + stop-distance parameter) OR low <= close * (1 - target-distance parameter) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= lower Bollinger Band(RSI(close, RSI lookback), band lookback, band deviation multiplier) * short exit factor
