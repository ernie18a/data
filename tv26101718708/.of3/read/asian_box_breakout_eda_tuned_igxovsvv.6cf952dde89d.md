Calculation: Session high = highest high from the first chart bar of the configured trading session through the current chart bar
Calculation: Session low = lowest low from the first chart bar of the configured trading session through the current chart bar
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar; sampled on chart bars) × initial-stop multiple
Calculation: Long peak high = highest high from long entry through the current chart bar
Calculation: Short trough low = lowest low from short entry through the current chart bar
Calculation: Long stop = if (Long peak high − entry price) / Stop distance ≥ trail activation multiple, then max(entry price − Stop distance, Long peak high − trail gap multiple × Stop distance), else entry price − Stop distance
Calculation: Short stop = if (entry price − Short trough low) / Stop distance ≥ trail activation multiple, then min(entry price + Stop distance, Short trough low + trail gap multiple × Stop distance), else entry price + Stop distance
Long: On the first chart bar of the current day passing the shared setup gate (position is flat; bar is within the configured trading session; Monday is not excluded when Monday skipping is enabled; session high and low are available; ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar; sampled on chart bars) > 0; (Session high − Session low) / ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar; sampled on chart bars) ≥ minimum width-to-ATR ratio), the trade-direction setting permits long and the trend filter is disabled or the close of the still-forming T1 bar > EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1 bars, still-forming bar; sampled on chart bars); submit a stop at that bar's session high, and enter long when a later bar's high ≥ that stop
Short: On the first chart bar of the current day passing the shared setup gate (position is flat; bar is within the configured trading session; Monday is not excluded when Monday skipping is enabled; session high and low are available; ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar; sampled on chart bars) > 0; (Session high − Session low) / ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar; sampled on chart bars) ≥ minimum width-to-ATR ratio), the trade-direction setting permits short and the trend filter is disabled or the close of the still-forming T1 bar < EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1 bars, still-forming bar; sampled on chart bars); submit a stop at that bar's session low, and enter short when a later bar's low ≤ that stop
Exit: Long, low ≤ Long stop OR end-of-day closing is enabled and the current chart bar is outside the configured trading session while the previous chart bar was inside it
Exit: Short, high ≥ Short stop OR end-of-day closing is enabled and the current chart bar is outside the configured trading session while the previous chart bar was inside it
