Calculation: Basis = selected EMA, SMA, HMA, WMA, VWMA, or RMA of the chosen source over the basis lookback.
Calculation: Band boundaries = Basis plus or minus the band-width parameter multiplied by ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback).
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR when positive, otherwise the instrument minimum price increment.
Calculation: Safe basis = Basis when available, otherwise close.
Calculation: Upward burst magnitude = (high − upper band) / Safe ATR when high > upper band and the closing-burst requirement is disabled or close > upper band; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Downward burst magnitude = (lower band − low) / Safe ATR when low < lower band and the closing-burst requirement is disabled or close < lower band; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Upward burst stack = min(maximum stack height, 1 + floor(upward burst magnitude / ATR per stack step)); downward burst stack is the same calculation using downward burst magnitude.
Calculation: Unit clamp(x) = min(max(x, 0), 1).
Calculation: Fade score(direction, peak magnitude, peak stack, peak RSI, peak rejection share, entry close) = score maximum × the weighted mean of: unit-clamped peak magnitude / heat-saturation parameter; peak stack / maximum stack height; direction-aware RSI exhaustion (RSI midpoint minus peak RSI for long, peak RSI minus RSI midpoint for short) / RSI exhaustion scale; peak rejection share / rejection normalization; (direction × change(Basis, trend lookback) / Safe ATR + trend offset) / trend scale; 1 minus normalized PercentRank(ATR, volatility lookback); and abs(entry close − Safe basis) / (max(band-width parameter, score band-width floor) × Safe ATR × reversion-room factor). Use separate parameters for the seven component weights; return 0 if their sum is 0.
Calculation: Fade star tier = 4 at or above the four-star score cutoff, 3 at or above the three-star cutoff, 2 at or above the two-star cutoff, 1 at or above the one-star cutoff, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Risk distance = min(max(abs(entry price − raw stop), ATR at entry × minimum-risk parameter), ATR at entry × maximum-risk parameter). Raw stop is entry price minus/plus ATR at entry × stop-distance parameter for the ATR stop mode; Lowest(low, swing lookback)/Highest(high, swing lookback) minus/plus ATR at entry × stop-buffer parameter for the swing stop mode; or the lower/higher of the entry-bar and previous-bar lows/highs minus/plus ATR at entry × stop-buffer parameter for the burst-extreme mode. Use minus for long and plus for short.
Calculation: Stop price = entry price minus risk distance for long, plus risk distance for short; calculate at entry and retain for the position.
Calculation: TP3 price = Safe basis at entry plus/minus half of the band width × Safe ATR when mean-reversion mode is selected and the basis is beyond entry in the position direction; otherwise entry price plus/minus risk distance × TP3-R parameter. Use plus for long and minus for short.
Long: A downward burst qualifies when the previous bar's downward burst magnitude is positive, at least the magnitude two bars ago, and greater than the current magnitude, with current-bar close confirmation when enabled; its previous-bar stack is at least the minimum stack parameter and its previous-bar magnitude is at least the minimum burst-magnitude parameter; the long side is enabled; the rejection-wick requirement is disabled or the previous bar's lower-wick share of its range is at least the minimum rejection parameter; and the RSI gate is disabled or RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the previous bar is at or below the oversold parameter. If both directions qualify, select long only when its peak magnitude is greater than the upward peak magnitude. Require Fade star tier at least the minimum-stars parameter, ATR available, no open position, no position resolution on this bar, and elapsed chart bars since the previous accepted signal at least the signal-spacing parameter.
Short: An upward burst qualifies when the previous bar's upward burst magnitude is positive, at least the magnitude two bars ago, and greater than the current magnitude, with current-bar close confirmation when enabled; its previous-bar stack is at least the minimum stack parameter and its previous-bar magnitude is at least the minimum burst-magnitude parameter; the short side is enabled; the rejection-wick requirement is disabled or the previous bar's upper-wick share of its range is at least the minimum rejection parameter; and the RSI gate is disabled or RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the previous bar is at or above the overbought parameter. If both directions qualify, select short when its peak magnitude is at least the downward peak magnitude. Require Fade star tier at least the minimum-stars parameter, ATR available, no open position, no position resolution on this bar, and elapsed chart bars since the previous accepted signal at least the signal-spacing parameter.
Long: The previous bar's downward burst magnitude is positive, at least the magnitude two bars ago, and greater than the current magnitude, with current-bar close confirmation when enabled; its previous-bar stack is at least the maximum stack height.
Short: The previous bar's upward burst magnitude is positive, at least the magnitude two bars ago, and greater than the current magnitude, with current-bar close confirmation when enabled; its previous-bar stack is at least the maximum stack height.
Long: On chart bars, downward burst magnitude > 0 and upward burst magnitude = 0, or both magnitudes = 0 and close <= Safe basis.
Short: On chart bars, upward burst magnitude > 0, or both magnitudes = 0 and close > Safe basis.
Long: On chart bars, the last completed T1 bar has downward burst magnitude > 0 and upward burst magnitude = 0.
Short: On chart bars, the last completed T1 bar has upward burst magnitude > 0.
Long: On chart bars, the last completed T2 bar has downward burst magnitude > 0 and upward burst magnitude = 0.
Short: On chart bars, the last completed T2 bar has upward burst magnitude > 0.
Exit: long, low <= Stop price or high >= TP3 price or elapsed chart bars since entry >= maximum-bars-in-trade parameter.
Exit: short, high >= Stop price or low <= TP3 price or elapsed chart bars since entry >= maximum-bars-in-trade parameter.
