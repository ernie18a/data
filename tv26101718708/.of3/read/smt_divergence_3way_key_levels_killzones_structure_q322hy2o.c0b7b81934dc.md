Calculation: SMTCorrelation = correlation threshold is disabled OR the maximum PearsonCorrelation of close and each selected correlated market's close over the correlation lookback meets the threshold, treating missing correlations as zero
Calculation: RunOpen(direction) = open of the first candle in a consecutive run of bearish candles for bearish direction or bullish candles for bullish direction
Calculation: StructureBias(pivot length, break mode, ATR body threshold) = on its evaluation bars, start neutral; set bullish and consume the latest confirmed pivot high when close in close mode or high in wick mode exceeds it and the body filter passes; then set bearish and consume the latest confirmed pivot low when close in close mode or low in wick mode falls below it and the body filter passes; otherwise retain the bias
Long: On chart bars, an enabled internal or external confirmed pivot low and the preceding confirmed pivot low disagree with at least one selected correlated market on whether a lower low was made at those corresponding bars, or with every selected market when confirmation requires both; comparison feeds are active, SMTCorrelation passes, the pivot bar passes the optional session filter, and bullish low signals are enabled
Short: On chart bars, an enabled internal or external confirmed pivot high and the preceding confirmed pivot high disagree with at least one selected correlated market on whether a higher high was made at those corresponding bars, or with every selected market when confirmation requires both; comparison feeds are active, SMTCorrelation passes, the pivot bar passes the optional session filter, and bearish high signals are enabled
Long: While enabled internal or external SMT is forming, the running low since the prior confirmed pivot low and that pivot low disagree with at least one selected correlated market on whether a lower low was made at the corresponding bars, or with every selected market when confirmation requires both; comparison feeds and forming signals are enabled, and bullish low signals are enabled
Short: While enabled internal or external SMT is forming, the running high since the prior confirmed pivot high and that pivot high disagree with at least one selected correlated market on whether a higher high was made at the corresponding bars, or with every selected market when confirmation requires both; comparison feeds and forming signals are enabled, and bearish high signals are enabled
Long: On chart bars, for any enabled T1, T2, or T3 level, the traded instrument's low reaches or falls below that timeframe's last completed bar low while at least one selected correlated market's low stays above its own corresponding level, or every selected market does so when confirmation requires both; this is the first sweep of that level in its period, comparison feeds are active, SMTCorrelation passes, and bullish low signals are enabled
Short: On chart bars, for any enabled T1, T2, or T3 level, the traded instrument's high reaches or exceeds that timeframe's last completed bar high while at least one selected correlated market's high stays below its own corresponding level, or every selected market does so when confirmation requires both; this is the first sweep of that level in its period, comparison feeds are active, SMTCorrelation passes, and bearish high signals are enabled
Long: After either configured session ends, the traded instrument's low reaches or falls below that session's low while at least one selected correlated market's low stays above its own low for that session, or every selected market does so when confirmation requires both; this is the first sweep of that session low, comparison feeds are active, SMTCorrelation passes, and bullish low signals are enabled
Short: After either configured session ends, the traded instrument's high reaches or exceeds that session's high while at least one selected correlated market's high stays below its own high for that session, or every selected market does so when confirmation requires both; this is the first sweep of that session high, comparison feeds are active, SMTCorrelation passes, and bearish high signals are enabled
