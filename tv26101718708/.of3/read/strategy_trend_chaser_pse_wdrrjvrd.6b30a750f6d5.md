Calculation: entry ATR = floor(ATR(lookback) × 10^decimal precision) ÷ 10^decimal precision on each flat bar or entry signal, held otherwise
Calculation: long stop level = let base = entry price if max(close, close[1]) > entry price + entry ATR × loss multiple, otherwise entry price - entry ATR × loss multiple; let trail = max(close - 2 × entry ATR × trailing multiple, previous long stop level or 0); if base < trail, max(trail, entry price), otherwise base
Long: position is flat or short AND datetime is within the start-date and end-date parameters AND MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) crosses above 0 AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > 0 AND (MACD line(close, trend fast lookback, trend slow lookback) - MACD line(close, trend fast lookback, trend slow lookback)[1]) × sensitivity > upper Bollinger Band(close, channel lookback, standard deviation multiplier) - lower Bollinger Band(close, channel lookback, standard deviation multiplier)
Exit: long, low <= long stop level
Exit: short, high >= entry price + entry ATR × loss multiple OR MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) crosses above 0 AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > 0
