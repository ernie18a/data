Calculation: Structure bias = bullish when the latest pivot high and latest pivot low are each above their preceding counterparts; bearish when each is below; otherwise neutral. Calculate pivot highs and lows with the same left and right swing lookback on the selected structure timeframe (chart timeframe or T1), then sample them onto chart bars using the last completed T1 bar historically and the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar in real time. When swing-break invalidation is enabled, reset bullish bias to neutral if close < min(latest pivot high, latest pivot low), and bearish bias to neutral if close > max(latest pivot high, latest pivot low).
Calculation: Golden box = between the maximum and minimum of two levels. Let pivot high and pivot low be the latest values, range = abs(pivot high - pivot low), and upward leg mean the latest pivot high occurred after the latest pivot low. In Fibonacci mode, the levels are pivot high - range * lower retracement level and pivot high - range * upper retracement level for an upward leg, or pivot low + range * lower retracement level and pivot low + range * upper retracement level otherwise. In dynamic ATR mode, center = the level at the midpoint of the lower and upper retracement levels using the same leg direction; the bounds are center ± ATR(length) * width multiplier. ATR uses chart bars.
Calculation: Signed-volume sum = sum over the delta lookback of volume when close > open, -volume when close < open, and 0 when close = open.
Long: close is within the Golden box, inclusive, AND Structure bias is bullish AND (HTF alignment is not required OR EMA(close, shorter lookback, T2 bars, sampled onto chart bars using the last completed T2 bar historically and the current, possibly still-forming T2 bar in real time) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T2 bars, sampled the same way)) AND (volume alignment is not required OR (Signed-volume sum > 0 AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * spike multiplier)) AND (a confirmation candle is not required OR (close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close >= open[1] AND open <= close[1]) OR (high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= wick-ratio parameter AND close > open)).
Short: close is within the Golden box, inclusive, AND Structure bias is bearish AND (HTF alignment is not required OR EMA(close, shorter lookback, T2 bars, sampled onto chart bars using the last completed T2 bar historically and the current, possibly still-forming T2 bar in real time) <= EMA(close, longer lookback, T2 bars, sampled the same way)) AND (volume alignment is not required OR (Signed-volume sum < 0 AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * spike multiplier)) AND (a confirmation candle is not required OR (close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close <= open[1] AND open >= close[1]) OR (high > low AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= wick-ratio parameter AND close < open)).
