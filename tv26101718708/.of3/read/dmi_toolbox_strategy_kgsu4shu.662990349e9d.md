Calculation: +DI = RMA(100 × RMA(+DM, DI lookback) / RMA(true range, DI lookback), +DI smoothing lookback), where +DM is the high change when positive and greater than the downward low change, otherwise 0.
Calculation: -DI = 100 × RMA(-DM, DI lookback) / RMA(true range, DI lookback), where -DM is the downward low change when positive and greater than the high change, otherwise 0.
Calculation: ADX = 100 × EMA(abs(+DI − -DI) / (+DI + -DI, or 1 when their sum is 0), ADX lookback).
Calculation: upward-DI state = true when +DI crosses above SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback), remains true until +DI crosses below it, and is otherwise false.
Long: (upward-DI state AND chart bars since its latest activation, counting the crossover bar, <= maximum-age parameter AND close > SMA(close, long price lookback) AND close > SMA(close, shorter filter lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, shorter filter lookback) AND (crossover(+DI, +DI threshold) OR crossover(SMA(ADX, ADX smoothing lookback), SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback)))) OR +DI > -DI OR crossover(+DI, SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback)) OR crossover(ADX, +DI) OR crossover(ADX, SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback)) OR crossover(ADX, SMA(ADX, ADX smoothing lookback)) OR (crossunder(ADX, SMA(ADX, ADX smoothing lookback)) AND SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback) > +DI) OR upward-DI state(T1, still-forming T1 bar, evaluated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR upward-DI state(T2, still-forming T2 bar, evaluated on T2 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR upward-DI state(T3, still-forming T3 bar, evaluated on T3 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR upward-DI state(T4, still-forming T4 bar, evaluated on T4 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR upward-DI state(T5, still-forming T5 bar, evaluated on T5 bars and mapped to chart bars); long price lookback > shorter filter lookback.
Short: +DI < -DI OR crossunder(+DI, SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback)) OR (crossunder(ADX, SMA(ADX, ADX smoothing lookback)) AND +DI > SMA(-DI, -DI smoothing lookback)) OR NOT upward-DI state(T1, still-forming T1 bar, evaluated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR NOT upward-DI state(T2, still-forming T2 bar, evaluated on T2 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR NOT upward-DI state(T3, still-forming T3 bar, evaluated on T3 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR NOT upward-DI state(T4, still-forming T4 bar, evaluated on T4 bars and mapped to chart bars) OR NOT upward-DI state(T5, still-forming T5 bar, evaluated on T5 bars and mapped to chart bars).
Exit: long, crossunder(ADX, SMA(ADX, ADX smoothing lookback)) AND ADX > minimum ADX parameter OR close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage).
