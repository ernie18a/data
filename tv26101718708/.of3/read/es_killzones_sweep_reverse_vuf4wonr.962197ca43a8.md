Calculation: Swing low = low[swing length] when it is below Lowest(low, swing length), the swing high condition is false, and the swing direction changes to low
Calculation: Bullish order-block bottom = lowest selected low after its swing high through the bar before its upside break; top = selected high of that bar, using wick or candle body as configured
Calculation: Bearish order-block top = highest selected high after its swing low through the bar before its downside break; bottom = selected low of that bar, using wick or candle body as configured
