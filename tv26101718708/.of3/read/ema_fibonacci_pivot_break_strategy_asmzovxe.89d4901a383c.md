Calculation: Long stop = if candle-stop mode, signal-bar low - stop-buffer ticks * tick size; otherwise signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier
Calculation: Short stop = if candle-stop mode, signal-bar high + stop-buffer ticks * tick size; otherwise signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - Long stop) * reward/risk parameter
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar close - (Short stop - signal-bar close) * reward/risk parameter
Long: position is flat AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the allowed session) AND close > open AND open <= EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND open <= Pivot Points(pivot-formula parameter, long-level parameter, high(T1, last completed bar), low(T1, last completed bar), close(T1, last completed bar)) held on chart bars AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND close > Pivot Points(pivot-formula parameter, long-level parameter, high(T1, last completed bar), low(T1, last completed bar), close(T1, last completed bar)) held on chart bars AND Long stop is defined AND close > Long stop
Short: position is flat AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the allowed session) AND close < open AND open >= EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND open >= Pivot Points(pivot-formula parameter, short-level parameter, high(T1, last completed bar), low(T1, last completed bar), close(T1, last completed bar)) held on chart bars AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND close < Pivot Points(pivot-formula parameter, short-level parameter, high(T1, last completed bar), low(T1, last completed bar), close(T1, last completed bar)) held on chart bars AND Short stop is defined AND Short stop > close
Exit: long, low <= Long stop OR high >= Long target
Exit: short, high >= Short stop OR low <= Short target
