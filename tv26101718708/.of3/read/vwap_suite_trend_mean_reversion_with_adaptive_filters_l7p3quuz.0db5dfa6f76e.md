Calculation: VWAP bands = VWAP ± sqrt(max(sum(volume * typical price²) / sum(volume) - VWAP², 0)) * the respective Band 1, Band 2, or Band 3 multiplier; VWAP is sum(volume * typical price) / sum(volume), or typical price when summed volume is zero, and the sums restart at each VWAP reset
Long: Trend Following mode selected AND flat AND close crosses above the level selected by the trend trigger (VWAP, upper band 1, or upper band 2) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume threshold) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 > minimum ATR percent) AND (bandwidth filter disabled OR (upper band 1 - lower band 1) / close * 100 > minimum bandwidth percent) AND (slope filter disabled OR (VWAP - VWAP[slope lookback]) / VWAP[slope lookback] * 100 > minimum slope percent) AND (secondary indicator disabled OR (RSI selected AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > trend long RSI threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close > EMA(close, regime lookback)))
Short: Trend Following mode selected AND flat AND close crosses below the level selected by the trend trigger (VWAP, lower band 1, or lower band 2) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume threshold) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 > minimum ATR percent) AND (bandwidth filter disabled OR (upper band 1 - lower band 1) / close * 100 > minimum bandwidth percent) AND (slope filter disabled OR (VWAP - VWAP[slope lookback]) / VWAP[slope lookback] * 100 < -minimum slope percent) AND (secondary indicator disabled OR (RSI selected AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < trend short RSI threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close < EMA(close, regime lookback)))
Long: Mean Reversion mode selected AND flat AND (the selected trigger is Outside Band and close < selected lower band, or Band Cross and close crosses below selected lower band, or Band Reclaim and low < selected lower band AND close > selected lower band; selected lower band is band 1, 2, or 3) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume threshold) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 > minimum ATR percent) AND (bandwidth filter disabled OR (upper band 1 - lower band 1) / close * 100 > minimum bandwidth percent) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback) < maximum ADX) AND abs((close - VWAP) / ATR(ATR lookback)) <= maximum VWAP deviation in ATR AND (secondary indicator disabled OR (RSI selected AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above the mean-reversion oversold threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close > EMA(close, regime lookback)))
Short: Mean Reversion mode selected AND flat AND (the selected trigger is Outside Band and close > selected upper band, or Band Cross and close crosses above selected upper band, or Band Reclaim and high > selected upper band AND close < selected upper band; selected upper band is band 1, 2, or 3) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume threshold) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 > minimum ATR percent) AND (bandwidth filter disabled OR (upper band 1 - lower band 1) / close * 100 > minimum bandwidth percent) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback) < maximum ADX) AND abs((close - VWAP) / ATR(ATR lookback)) <= maximum VWAP deviation in ATR AND (secondary indicator disabled OR (RSI selected AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below the mean-reversion overbought threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close < EMA(close, regime lookback)))
Exit: Long, low <= the initial stop (signal-bar close - signal-bar ATR * stop multiple when ATR stop is selected; otherwise signal-bar close - abs(signal-bar close - lower band 1 at signal in Trend Following mode or lower band 3 at signal in Mean Reversion mode)), raised to at least entry price when break-even is enabled and the highest high since entry >= entry price + signal-bar ATR * break-even threshold, OR high >= the take-profit target (VWAP when VWAP Target is selected or Mean Reversion mode is selected, otherwise upper band 2; target captured at entry when fixed take-profit is enabled, current target otherwise)
Exit: Short, high >= the initial stop (signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR * stop multiple when ATR stop is selected; otherwise signal-bar close + abs(upper band 1 at signal in Trend Following mode or upper band 3 at signal in Mean Reversion mode - signal-bar close)), lowered to at most entry price when break-even is enabled and the lowest low since entry <= entry price - signal-bar ATR * break-even threshold, OR low <= the take-profit target (VWAP when VWAP Target is selected or Mean Reversion mode is selected, otherwise lower band 2; target captured at entry when fixed take-profit is enabled, current target otherwise)
