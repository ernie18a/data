Calculation: Opening range = highest chart-bar high and lowest chart-bar low during the first T1 after the ET session-open time parameter
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening-range high − opening-range low
Calculation: Long ATR trail = highest value since breakeven was first secured in the position of close − ATR(lookback, chart bars) × trail-multiplier parameter
Calculation: Short ATR trail = lowest value since breakeven was first secured in the position of close + ATR(lookback, chart bars) × trail-multiplier parameter
Long: Opening range is built AND before the ET session-end time parameter AND no position is open AND no long entry has occurred that trading day AND an upward breakout is active, activated after the previous confirmation-bars-parameter consecutive chart-bar closes above the opening-range high and reset by a later close below it, AND (low <= opening-range high + retest-buffer parameter OR (fallback is enabled AND low > opening-range high + retest-buffer parameter AND chart bars since breakout activation >= fallback-wait parameter))
Short: Opening range is built AND before the ET session-end time parameter AND no position is open AND no short entry has occurred that trading day AND a downward breakout is active, activated after the previous confirmation-bars-parameter consecutive chart-bar closes below the opening-range low and reset by a later close above it, AND (high >= opening-range low − retest-buffer parameter OR (fallback is enabled AND high < opening-range low − retest-buffer parameter AND chart bars since breakout activation >= fallback-wait parameter))
Exit: Long, (no close since entry has reached entry price + breakeven-trigger parameter AND low <= opening-range low) OR (a close since entry has reached entry price + breakeven-trigger parameter AND low <= max(entry price, Long ATR trail)) OR (opening-range size < range-mode-threshold parameter AND high >= opening-range high + opening-range size × reward-risk parameter) OR at/after the ET session-end time parameter
Exit: Short, (no close since entry has reached entry price − breakeven-trigger parameter AND high >= opening-range high) OR (a close since entry has reached entry price − breakeven-trigger parameter AND high >= min(entry price, Short ATR trail)) OR (opening-range size < range-mode-threshold parameter AND low <= opening-range low − opening-range size × reward-risk parameter) OR at/after the ET session-end time parameter
