Calculation: Midnight price = open of the bar whose opening time is midnight in New York, reset each day
Calculation: Midnight cross count = after the midnight bar, count the first close above or below midnight price as one and each later change between above and below; ignore closes equal to midnight price
Long: midnight cross count = required-cross parameter AND (entry delay disabled OR chart bars since the count reached the required-cross parameter >= entry-delay parameter) AND no trade taken today AND position is flat AND close > midnight price
Short: midnight cross count = required-cross parameter AND (entry delay disabled OR chart bars since the count reached the required-cross parameter >= entry-delay parameter) AND no trade taken today AND position is flat AND close < midnight price
Exit: Long, New York bar opening time = configured exit time OR close < midnight price OR take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit ticks × minimum tick size OR breakeven enabled AND high >= entry price + breakeven trigger ticks × minimum tick size AND low <= entry price + breakeven offset ticks × minimum tick size
Exit: Short, New York bar opening time = configured exit time OR close > midnight price OR take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit ticks × minimum tick size OR breakeven enabled AND low <= entry price - breakeven trigger ticks × minimum tick size AND high >= entry price - breakeven offset ticks × minimum tick size
