Calculation: Session VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(max(Σ(volume × HLC3²)/Σvolume − (Σ(volume × HLC3)/Σvolume)², 0)), using chart bars since the current session reset; zero when Σvolume = 0
Calculation: VWAP z-score = (close − session VWAP) / session VWAP standard deviation; zero when standard deviation = 0
Calculation: Delta = sum(volume of T1 bars within the current chart bar where close > open) − sum(volume where close < open); when T1 data is unavailable, use a positive value if chart close > open, a negative value if chart close < open, or zero otherwise
Calculation: Momentum score = the weighted mean of body, wick, slope, volume, and EMA-distance scores using separate weights, clamped to the score floor and ceiling; zero when the weights sum to zero. Body score = min((HA body / SMA(HA body, body lookback ending on the previous chart bar)) / body ratio for ceiling × score ceiling, score ceiling), or score floor when the average is zero. Wick score = max(score ceiling × (1 − opposing HA wick / HA body), score floor), using the lower wick for bullish HA, upper wick for bearish HA, and mean wick for doji; score floor when HA body is zero. Slope score = min(slope sensitivity × abs((EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) − its value slope lookback chart bars ago) / slope lookback / ATR(ATR lookback)) × score ceiling, score ceiling), or score floor when ATR is zero. Volume score = min((volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback)) / volume ratio for ceiling × score ceiling, score ceiling), using a separate fallback volume ratio when average volume is zero. Let z = (HA close − EMA(HA close, EMA lookback)) / STDEV(HA close, standard-deviation lookback), or zero when standard deviation is zero; EMA-distance score linearly interpolates between four separately parameterized (abs(z), score) knots, then declines linearly from the last knot by a separate decay parameter per abs(z) unit, bounded by the score floor and ceiling.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, (bullish HA AND HA close > EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND momentum score ≥ bias threshold) OR (momentum score > strong threshold AND bullish HA AND HA lower wick ≤ HA body × wick tolerance percentage AND HA body > SMA(HA body, body lookback ending on the previous chart bar) AND HA close > EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (delta filter disabled OR Delta > 0))
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, (bearish HA AND HA close < EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND momentum score ≥ bias threshold) OR (momentum score > strong threshold AND bearish HA AND HA upper wick ≤ HA body × wick tolerance percentage AND HA body > SMA(HA body, body lookback ending on the previous chart bar) AND HA close < EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND (delta filter disabled OR Delta < 0))
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, the strong-long condition holds, the previous chart bar did not meet the strong-long condition, and the long gate was unlocked at the start of the bar. The gate starts unlocked, locks after a fresh strong-long signal, and resets after a confirmed bar with low ≤ EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) only when that bar does not meet the strong-long condition; a reset is usable from the next chart bar.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the strong-short condition holds, the previous chart bar did not meet the strong-short condition, and the short gate was unlocked at the start of the bar. The gate starts unlocked, locks after a fresh strong-short signal, and resets after a confirmed bar with high ≥ EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) only when that bar does not meet the strong-short condition; a reset is usable from the next chart bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, session bar count ≥ minimum session bars AND close > 0 AND session VWAP standard deviation / close ≥ minimum SD percentage of price AND momentum score < MR maximum AND (fade filter disabled OR momentum score < its value on the previous chart bar) AND VWAP z-score ≤ −MR SD threshold AND HA body < SMA(HA body, body lookback ending on the previous chart bar) AND previous chart bar was bearish HA AND current real high ≥ max(previous HA open, previous HA close) AND current real low ≤ min(previous HA open, previous HA close)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, session bar count ≥ minimum session bars AND close > 0 AND session VWAP standard deviation / close ≥ minimum SD percentage of price AND momentum score < MR maximum AND (fade filter disabled OR momentum score < its value on the previous chart bar) AND VWAP z-score ≥ MR SD threshold AND HA body < SMA(HA body, body lookback ending on the previous chart bar) AND previous chart bar was bullish HA AND current real high ≥ max(previous HA open, previous HA close) AND current real low ≤ min(previous HA open, previous HA close)
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar, the previous chart bar met the strong-long condition and the current chart bar meets neither the strong-long nor the long-bias condition
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar, the previous chart bar met the strong-short condition and the current chart bar meets neither the strong-short nor the short-bias condition
Calculation: Long stop = next chart bar open after a fresh strong-long signal − stop-distance parameter × (signal bar HA high − signal bar HA low)
Calculation: Short stop = next chart bar open after a fresh strong-short signal + stop-distance parameter × (signal bar HA high − signal bar HA low)
Exit: long, stop tracking enabled, an active long stop exists, and a confirmed chart bar closes below the active long stop
Exit: short, stop tracking enabled, an active short stop exists, and a confirmed chart bar closes above the active short stop
