Calculation: Accepted swing anchor = a confirmed pivot high or low on a confirmed chart bar, using separate left- and right-pivot-lookback parameters; accept a high if no prior confirmed low exists or its (pivot high − prior confirmed low) / ATR(ATR-lookback) at the pivot bar meets the minimum-swing-ATR parameter, and accept a low symmetrically; accept a candidate if ATR at the pivot is nonpositive; if both qualify, select the greater prominence, favoring the high on a tie; retain selected highs and lows for subsequent opposing-pivot tests.
Calculation: Swing AVWAP = volume-weighted mean of the source parameter over chart bars from the latest accepted pivot through the current bar, including pivot-to-confirmation bars; use max(volume, 0) as weights and valid source samples only.
Calculation: Swing deviation = square root of the volume-weighted population variance of the same source samples and weights over the same anchor-to-current interval.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with an accepted anchor and positive cumulative weight, and no new anchor on this bar, close crosses above Swing AVWAP OR close crosses above Swing AVWAP + Swing deviation × outer-deviation-multiplier.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with an accepted anchor and positive cumulative weight, and no new anchor on this bar, close crosses below Swing AVWAP OR close crosses below Swing AVWAP − Swing deviation × outer-deviation-multiplier.
