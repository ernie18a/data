Calculation: bars in position = chart bars elapsed since entry, incrementing each bar while a position is open and resetting when flat
Long: (session filter disabled OR current chart bar is within the session parameter) AND EMA(close, shorter fast lookback) > EMA(close, longer slow lookback) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, separate trend-filter lookback)) AND low <= EMA(close, shorter fast lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter fast lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above the long RSI trigger AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier
Short: (session filter disabled OR current chart bar is within the session parameter) AND EMA(close, shorter fast lookback) < EMA(close, longer slow lookback) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, separate trend-filter lookback)) AND high >= EMA(close, shorter fast lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter fast lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below the short RSI trigger AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * target multiplier OR (trailing multiplier > 0 AND low <= highest(high, bars in position) - ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier) OR (time-stop length > 0 AND bars in position >= time-stop length) OR (session filter enabled AND current chart bar is outside the session parameter)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * target multiplier OR (trailing multiplier > 0 AND high >= lowest(low, bars in position) + ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier) OR (time-stop length > 0 AND bars in position >= time-stop length) OR (session filter enabled AND current chart bar is outside the session parameter)
