Calculation: DCA drop(n) = base DCA drop + (n - 1) × linear increment in linear mode; base DCA drop × geometric multiplier^(n - 1) in geometric mode
Calculation: Lateral = BBW(close, Bollinger lookback, deviation multiplier) < BBW threshold AND ADX(ADX lookback) < ADX threshold when both are required; otherwise either comparison
Calculation: VWM = EMA(ROC(close, momentum lookback) × volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), volume floor), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Cycle low = minimum(low) since the most recent fill, reset after each fill
Calculation: Bars since cycle low = consecutive bars since Cycle low last decreased, reset to zero when it decreases or after a fill
Long: flat with no active DCA cycle AND close > 0 AND close <= Highest(high, swing lookback) × (1 - initial drop percentage / 100) AND Lateral
Long: flat after an exit and before another entry AND trend re-entry enabled AND close > 0 AND Supertrend direction < 0 AND ADX(ADX lookback) >= trend ADX minimum AND +DI(ADX lookback) > -DI(ADX lookback) AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND close > close[slope lookback] AND NOT Lateral
Long: position is long AND pending DCA level n is within the maximum DCA levels AND Lateral AND ((rebound confirmation disabled AND close <= initial entry price × (1 - DCA drop(n) / 100)) OR (rebound confirmation enabled AND the level has been armed since the last fill by Cycle low <= initial entry price × (1 - DCA drop(n) / 100) AND Cycle low <= most recent fill price × (1 - minimum gap percentage / 100) AND Bars since cycle low >= confirmation bars AND close >= Cycle low × (1 + rebound percentage / 100) AND (green-candle requirement disabled OR close > open)))
Exit: long, close > average entry price × (1 + minimum profit percentage / 100) AND (VWM has declined on each of the last decay bars AND VWM[decay bars] > impulse threshold OR (Supertrend safety exit enabled AND Supertrend direction crosses above 0))
Exit: long, filled DCA count >= rescue threshold AND price has closed at or above the then-current average entry price × (1 + breakeven arm percentage / 100) while the filled DCA count was at least the rescue threshold AND open profit <= 0
