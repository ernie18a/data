Calculation: Oscillator = if ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) > 0, percentage scale × (WMA(close, shorter lookback) − WMA(close, longer lookback)) / ATR; otherwise WMA(close, shorter lookback) − WMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: Halving-cycle value = cos(2π × max(current wall-clock datetime − cycle start, 0) / cycle duration − phase offset)
Calculation: Dominant cycle = retain an initial cycle length; after confirmed pivot highs using the pivot lookback, when the latest pivot is younger than the maximum pivot age and the third latest pivot index is positive, set the cycle length to the average of the gaps between the latest three pivot highs, clamped between the cycle lower and upper bounds
Calculation: Forecast oscillator(t) = A × sin(phase + forecast phase shift + 2πt / D) + A × medium-wave weight × sin((phase + forecast phase shift) / medium-cycle multiplier + 2πt / (D × medium-cycle multiplier)) + A × halving weight × Halving-cycle value + A × macro weight × M + A × t × (USDT dominance fell over its activity lookback ? USDT-fall weight × abs(USDT dominance momentum) : USDT dominance rose over its activity lookback ? −USDT-rise weight × abs(USDT dominance momentum) : 0) + A × (BTC dominance bias ? BTC-bullish weight : −BTC-bearish weight) × abs(BTC dominance momentum) + A × futures-basis weight × (valid CME Bitcoin futures basis in contango and not overleveraged ? basis : in backwardation ? −abs(basis) : 0), where D is Dominant cycle, phase is 2π × bars since latest pivot high / D, A is standard deviation of Oscillator over integer(max(D, cycle lower bound)), M is +1 when S&P 500 close is above its EMA and US Dollar Index close is at or below its EMA, −1 when US Dollar Index close is above its EMA and S&P 500 close is at or below its EMA, otherwise 0; USDT and BTC dominance momentums are percentage changes over their respective momentum lookbacks when their values exceed their respective validity thresholds, otherwise 0; BTC dominance bias is BTC dominance above its EMA when the traded instrument’s base currency is Bitcoin, otherwise BTC dominance below its EMA; futures basis is the percentage difference between CME Bitcoin futures close and traded instrument close when the futures close is valid, otherwise 0
Long: crossover(Oscillator, −standard deviation of Oscillator over deviation lookback × deviation multiplier) AND T1 close > EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < upper RSI bound AND MACD histogram(fast lookback, slower lookback, signal lookback) >= its prior value AND CMF(CMF lookback) > lower money-flow threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume threshold multiplier AND Halving-cycle value > long cycle threshold; T1 values are calculated on T1 bars and taken on chart bars from the T1 bar still forming, without lookahead
Short: crossunder(Oscillator, standard deviation of Oscillator over deviation lookback × deviation multiplier) AND T1 close < EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) < EMA(close, longer lookback, T1) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > lower RSI bound AND MACD histogram(fast lookback, slower lookback, signal lookback) <= its prior value AND CMF(CMF lookback) < upper money-flow threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume threshold multiplier AND Halving-cycle value < short cycle threshold; T1 values are calculated on T1 bars and taken on chart bars from the T1 bar still forming, without lookahead
Long: Forecast oscillator crosses from negative to nonnegative between consecutive forecast samples separated by the forecast sampling interval, at an offset from that interval through the forecast horizon less one interval
Short: Forecast oscillator crosses from positive to nonpositive between consecutive forecast samples separated by the forecast sampling interval, at an offset from that interval through the forecast horizon less one interval
Exit: long, crossunder(Oscillator, zero)
Exit: short, crossover(Oscillator, zero)
