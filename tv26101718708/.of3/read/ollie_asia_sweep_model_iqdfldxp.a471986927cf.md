Calculation: SessionRange = highest high minus lowest low of chart bars in the current configured Asia session, accumulated through the current bar
Calculation: BodyWickRatio = sum(abs(close - open), LookbackN) / sum((high - max(open, close)) + (min(open, close) - low), LookbackN), using chart bars; if the summed wick is zero, the result is NA
Calculation: MovePercent = 100 * (close - lowest low since the first below-session-low sweep) / SessionRange for a long setup, or 100 * (highest high since the first above-session-high sweep - close) / SessionRange for a short setup
Calculation: ElapsedMinutes = minutes between the current chart bar's open time and the first sweep bar's open time
Long: During the configured trade window, low < the current day's Asia-session low and SessionRange > 0, no earlier bar between the Asia session and trade window breached either Asia boundary, and no earlier bar in the trade window that day swept either boundary; OR, after a first below-session-low sweep, during the trade window, close > open, close[1] > open[1], low > high[2], and no earlier post-sweep bar with that same two-candle gap pattern had ElapsedMinutes <= MaxDelay, MovePercent >= MinDisplacementPct, and BodyWickRatio >= MinBodyWickRatio
Short: During the configured trade window, high > the current day's Asia-session high and SessionRange > 0, no earlier bar between the Asia session and trade window breached either Asia boundary, and no earlier bar in the trade window that day swept either boundary; OR, after a first above-session-high sweep, during the trade window, close < open, close[1] < open[1], high < low[2], and no earlier post-sweep bar with that same two-candle gap pattern had ElapsedMinutes <= MaxDelay, MovePercent >= MinDisplacementPct, and BodyWickRatio >= MinBodyWickRatio
