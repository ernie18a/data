Calculation: TriggerUp = (method M1 AND (close if close-confirm, else high) > Highest(high, swing lookback)[1]) OR (method M2 AND low < low[1] AND ((close-confirm AND close > close[1] AND close > open) OR (NOT close-confirm AND high > high[1]))) OR (method M3 AND high > high[1] AND low < low[1] AND (NOT close-confirm OR close > open))
Calculation: TriggerDn = (method M1 AND (close if close-confirm, else low) < Lowest(low, swing lookback)[1]) OR (method M2 AND high > high[1] AND ((close-confirm AND close < close[1] AND close < open) OR (NOT close-confirm AND low < low[1]))) OR (method M3 AND high > high[1] AND low < low[1] AND (NOT close-confirm OR close < open))
Calculation: PlanetHour = Chaldean planet ruling the planetary hour containing the bar time, where each planetary day starts at sunrise at the location parameter and ends at the next sunrise, has 12 equal hours from sunrise to sunset and 12 equal hours from sunset to the next sunrise, its first hour is ruled by the planet of the UTC weekday of its starting date, and each later hour is ruled by the next planet in the cycle Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon
Calculation: ScoreUp = (ATR on AND (high - low) >= ATR(atr lookback) * atr multiplier) + (volume on AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) + (RSI on AND RSI(close, rsi lookback) <= RSI oversold) + trend on + (hour ruler on AND PlanetHour is Venus or Jupiter) + (day ruler on AND UTC weekday is Thursday or Friday)
Calculation: ScoreDn = (ATR on AND (high - low) >= ATR(atr lookback) * atr multiplier) + (volume on AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) + (RSI on AND RSI(close, rsi lookback) >= RSI overbought) + trend on + (hour ruler on AND PlanetHour is Mars or Saturn) + (day ruler on AND UTC weekday is Tuesday or Saturday)
Calculation: EffMin = min(min confluence, ATR on + volume on + RSI on + trend on + hour ruler on + day ruler on)
Long: TriggerUp AND ScoreUp >= EffMin AND position side is not long AND bars since last entry >= min bars between signals
Short: TriggerDn AND ScoreDn >= EffMin AND position side is not short AND bars since last entry >= min bars between signals AND NOT (TriggerUp AND ScoreUp >= EffMin AND position side is not long)
Exit: long, (low <= entry price - stop multiple * ATR(atr lookback) at entry bar) OR (high >= entry price + target multiple * ATR(atr lookback) at entry bar) OR TriggerDn
Exit: short, (high >= entry price + stop multiple * ATR(atr lookback) at entry bar) OR (low <= entry price - target multiple * ATR(atr lookback) at entry bar) OR TriggerUp
