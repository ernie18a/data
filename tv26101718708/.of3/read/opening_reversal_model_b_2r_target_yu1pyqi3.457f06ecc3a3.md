Calculation: RTH bar = a bar inside the configured RTH session in the configured timezone.
Calculation: RTH bar count = count of RTH bars since the current RTH session began, starting at one.
Calculation: Prior RTH levels = the preceding RTH session's highest high, lowest low, and last close, carried through the current RTH session.
Calculation: Prior non-RTH high/low = the highest high and lowest low from bars after the preceding RTH session ended and before the current RTH session began, carried through the current RTH session.
Calculation: Current RTH open = the open of the first bar of the current RTH session.
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = the highest high and lowest low of the first opening-range-bar-count RTH bars; eligible only when RTH bar count exceeds that parameter.
Calculation: RTH order count = entry orders submitted during the current RTH session; reset at its start and incremented when an entry order is submitted.
Calculation: Short failure score(level) = count of these true conditions, when level is available: prior bar close > prior bar open AND prior bar close < level; prior bar high ≥ level AND current close < level; current close < current open AND current range > 0 AND abs(current close - current open) / current range ≥ failure-body-fraction parameter; current low < prior bar low; current high < prior bar high; high two bars ago is within the level zone AND close two bars ago < level AND current high is within the level zone AND current close < level. The level zone is level ± level-zone parameter.
Calculation: Long failure score(level) = count of these true conditions, when level is available: prior bar close < prior bar open AND prior bar close > level; prior bar low ≤ level AND current close > level; current close > current open AND current range > 0 AND abs(current close - current open) / current range ≥ failure-body-fraction parameter; current high > prior bar high; current low > prior bar low; low two bars ago is within the level zone AND close two bars ago > level AND current low ≤ level + level-zone parameter AND current close > level. The level zone is level ± level-zone parameter.
Calculation: Short signal-bar score(level) = count of these true conditions: close < (high + low) / 2; abs(close - open) > 0 AND high - max(open, close) ≥ abs(close - open) × tail-to-body parameter; close < prior bar low; level is available AND prior bar high ≥ level - level-zone parameter; high - low ≥ SMA(high - low, average-range lookback parameter) × minimum signal-range fraction parameter.
Calculation: Long signal-bar score(level) = count of these true conditions: close > (high + low) / 2; abs(close - open) > 0 AND min(open, close) - low ≥ abs(close - open) × tail-to-body parameter; close > prior bar high; level is available AND prior bar low ≤ level + level-zone parameter; high - low ≥ SMA(high - low, average-range lookback parameter) × minimum signal-range fraction parameter.
Calculation: Short signal = the bar is in the configured RTH session and RTH bar count is within the signal-bar-count parameter; and at least one available level among prior RTH high, prior non-RTH high, current RTH open, prior RTH close, and eligible opening-range high has current high within its level zone, current close < that level, short failure score(level) ≥ minimum failure-score parameter, and short signal-bar score(level) ≥ minimum signal-score parameter; and (high + tick-size parameter) - (low - tick-size parameter) is > 0 and ≤ maximum-risk parameter.
Calculation: Long signal = the bar is in the configured RTH session and RTH bar count is within the signal-bar-count parameter; and at least one available level among prior RTH low, prior non-RTH low, current RTH open, prior RTH close, and eligible opening-range low has current low within its level zone, current close > that level, long failure score(level) ≥ minimum failure-score parameter, and long signal-bar score(level) ≥ minimum signal-score parameter; and (high + tick-size parameter) - (low - tick-size parameter) is > 0 and ≤ maximum-risk parameter.
Calculation: Short follow-through = while waiting after a short signal, including on its signal bar, close < signal-bar low OR (close < open AND high - low > 0 AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) ≥ follow-through body-fraction parameter). The follow-through check occurs before timeout; the wait counter starts on the signal bar and a follow-through can qualify through the bar that first exceeds the follow-through-window parameter.
Calculation: Long follow-through = while waiting after a long signal, including on its signal bar, close > signal-bar high OR (close > open AND high - low > 0 AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) ≥ follow-through body-fraction parameter). The follow-through check occurs before timeout; the wait counter starts on the signal bar and a follow-through can qualify through the bar that first exceeds the follow-through-window parameter.
Calculation: Short reversal high = max(signal-bar high, bar immediately before the follow-through bar's high); short follow-through low = follow-through bar low.
Calculation: Long reversal low = min(signal-bar low, bar immediately before the follow-through bar's low); long follow-through high = follow-through bar high.
Calculation: Short pullback = during the active short pullback wait, including on the follow-through bar, (close > open OR close > (high + low) / 2) AND short reversal high - short follow-through low > 0 AND (high - short follow-through low) / (short reversal high - short follow-through low) ≤ maximum pullback-depth parameter. The pullback check occurs before timeout and can qualify through the bar that first exceeds the pullback-window parameter.
Calculation: Long pullback = during the active long pullback wait, including on the follow-through bar, (close < open OR close < (high + low) / 2) AND long follow-through high - long reversal low > 0 AND (long follow-through high - low) / (long follow-through high - long reversal low) ≤ maximum pullback-depth parameter. The pullback check occurs before timeout and can qualify through the bar that first exceeds the pullback-window parameter.
Calculation: Short entry price = qualifying pullback bar low - tick-size parameter; short stop price = short reversal high + tick-size parameter; short risk = short stop price - short entry price; short target = short entry price - short risk × reward/risk parameter.
Calculation: Long entry price = qualifying pullback bar high + tick-size parameter; long stop price = long reversal low - tick-size parameter; long risk = long entry price - long stop price; long target = long entry price + long risk × reward/risk parameter.
Short: while flat and idle, with RTH order count below the daily-order-limit parameter, a short signal occurs, then short follow-through confirms, then short pullback qualifies, and short risk is > 0 and ≤ maximum-risk parameter; submit a short stop-entry at the short entry price, filled when a later bar's low ≤ that price.
Long: while flat and idle, with RTH order count below the daily-order-limit parameter, a long signal occurs and no short signal occurs on that signal bar, then long follow-through confirms, then long pullback qualifies, and long risk is > 0 and ≤ maximum-risk parameter; submit a long stop-entry at the long entry price, filled when a later bar's high ≥ that price.
Exit: long, low ≤ short? no. low ≤ long stop price OR high ≥ long target OR the configured RTH session ends.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop price OR low ≤ short target OR the configured RTH session ends.
