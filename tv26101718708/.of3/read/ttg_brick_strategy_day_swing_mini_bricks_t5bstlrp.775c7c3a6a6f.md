Calculation: Bull/bear bricks = For a source range with high H and low L where H > L, set height = H − L; stack contiguous bull zones of that height above H and bear zones of that height below L, adding a zone when a confirmed chart bar's open and close both lie within it, up to the maximum-bricks-per-side parameter; each zone's midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2.
Calculation: Session ranges = Accumulate the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in each New York after-hours/premarket session and regular session, updating during the session and resetting at its start; add after-hours/premarket-range continuation bricks during regular-session bars and regular-session-range continuation bricks during after-hours bars.
Calculation: T1/T2 ranges = Use the high and low of the last completed bar of T1 (the shorter timeframe) and T2 (the longer timeframe), read on chart bars; add continuation bricks on confirmed chart bars and reset them at the start of each new T1 or T2 period.
Long: Alerts enabled AND (close-only confirmation disabled OR chart bar confirmed) AND the close crosses the top, midpoint, or bottom of any bull brick from an enabled source.
Short: Alerts enabled AND (close-only confirmation disabled OR chart bar confirmed) AND the close crosses the top, midpoint, or bottom of any bear brick from an enabled source.
