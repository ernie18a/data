Calculation: Position direction = +1 for long and -1 for short
Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), minimum price increment)
Calculation: Pullback Depth ATR = (Highest(high, pullback lookback) - low) / Safe ATR for long; (high - Lowest(low, pullback lookback)) / Safe ATR for short
Calculation: Ideal Pullback Depth = ideal pullback depth ATR × sensitivity multiplier; Deep Pullback Depth = max(deep pullback risk ATR × sensitivity multiplier, Ideal Pullback Depth + deep-depth offset); Minimum Useful Depth = max(minimum depth floor, Ideal Pullback Depth × minimum-depth fraction)
Calculation: Risk Pocket = the interval between Base EMA + Position direction × Safe ATR × pocket-width parameter and Slow EMA - Position direction × Safe ATR × pocket-width parameter; Risk Edge = Slow EMA - Position direction × Safe ATR × invalidation-buffer parameter
Calculation: Pullback Near Pocket = low <= pocket top + Safe ATR × near-pocket buffer for long, or high >= pocket bottom - Safe ATR × near-pocket buffer for short; Pullback Touches Pocket = high >= pocket bottom AND low <= pocket top
Calculation: Target Room ATR = max(Position direction × (Target Guide - close) / Safe ATR, zero), where Target Guide is Highest(high, target lookback)[1] for long or Lowest(low, target lookback)[1] for short, replaced by close + Position direction × Safe ATR × clean-room parameter when the reference is missing or leaves less than the minimum usable room
Calculation: Slope Score = clamp((Position direction × (Base EMA - Base EMA[slope lookback]) / Safe ATR - weak-slope threshold) / (strong-slope threshold - weak-slope threshold) × score scale, score floor, score ceiling); Trend Score = clamp(score scale × trend-stack weight + Slope Score × trend-slope weight - sensitivity adjustment, score floor, score ceiling)
Calculation: Depth Score = clamp((Pullback Depth ATR - Minimum Useful Depth) / (Ideal Pullback Depth - Minimum Useful Depth) × score scale, score floor, score ceiling) when depth <= Ideal Pullback Depth; otherwise clamp((Deep Pullback Depth - Pullback Depth ATR) / (Deep Pullback Depth - Ideal Pullback Depth) × score scale, score floor, score ceiling)
Calculation: Defense Response Score = close-location score × close-location weight + wick-response score × wick-response weight + close-back score × close-back weight when price touches or nears the pocket, otherwise the default defense score. For long, close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) and wick response = (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment); for short, close location = (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) and wick response = (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, minimum price increment). Normalize these scores with their configured weak and strong thresholds. Close-back score is its configured strong, inside-pocket, or outside-pocket score when close is respectively beyond Base EMA in the trend direction, inside the pocket, or otherwise.
Calculation: Volume Score = normalized low-good score of volume / SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback) when pullback depth is at least Minimum Useful Depth and price nears the pocket, or the candle closes against the trend relative to open or Fast EMA; otherwise the default volume score. Use the configured neutral ratio if the volume baseline is nonpositive.
Calculation: Target Room Score = clamp((Target Room ATR - weak-room threshold) / (clean-room parameter - weak-room threshold) × score scale, score floor, score ceiling); Continuation Score = clamp(Trend Score × trend-score weight + Depth Score × depth-score weight + Volume Score × volume-score weight + Defense Response Score × defense-score weight + Target Room Score × room-score weight, score floor, score ceiling)
Calculation: Review Ready = bullish or bearish EMA stack AND confirmation allowed AND Pullback Touches Pocket AND no latched invalidation AND Pullback Depth ATR <= Deep Pullback Depth × risk-watch depth fraction AND Continuation Score >= max(ready-score parameter, watch-score parameter + score increment) AND Defense Response Score >= sensitivity-specific minimum defense response AND Target Room ATR >= max(clean-room parameter × minimum ready-room fraction, follow-through move ATR parameter)
Calculation: Review Ready Start = Review Ready AND (Review Ready was false on the prior bar OR the trend direction differs from the prior bar)
Calculation: Invalidation is latched by a confirmed close below the long Risk Edge or above the short Risk Edge, using the prior bar's active-side Risk Edge if the prior context ended; it remains latched until a confirmed close beyond the far edge of the same-side pocket or the trend side changes. Invalidation Start = a current or prior-context confirmed breach when no same-side invalidation is already latched. Confirmation is allowed intrabar in live-update mode and only on a confirmed bar otherwise.
Calculation: Follow-Through = while a single episode started by Review Ready Start is pending, a confirmed bar reaches its frozen favorable target (qualification close + direction × qualification Safe ATR × follow-through move ATR parameter) before reaching its frozen Risk Edge, within the follow-through proof window; if both levels are reached on one bar, the Risk Edge takes precedence.
Long: Bullish EMA stack AND Review Ready Start OR a confirmed long Follow-Through
Short: Bearish EMA stack AND Review Ready Start OR a confirmed short Follow-Through
Exit: long, Invalidation Start for the long side
Exit: short, Invalidation Start for the short side
