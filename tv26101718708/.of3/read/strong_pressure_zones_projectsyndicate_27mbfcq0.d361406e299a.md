Calculation: Pressure volume = sum of enabled external venue volume series on each chart bar when positive, otherwise traded-instrument volume
Calculation: Pressure weight = Pressure volume × (high − low) × pressure-weight scale
Calculation: Historical magnitude = power of the clipped ratio of pivot-bar Pressure weight above the rolling low to the rolling high-minus-low over the magnitude lookback and magnitude contrast; cap the rolling high, replace an equal rolling low with the rolling high times the equal-range factor, and use the neutral-rank fallback if the denominator is not positive
Calculation: Pool band = a band centered on the pivot price, with height from either ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × ATR-band parameter or close × price-band percentage / 100, as selected
Calculation: Active pressure pools = create support pools at confirmed pivot lows and resistance pools at confirmed pivot highs, using separate primary left and right lookbacks and an optional additional symmetric pivot lookback; allow only enabled sides and pivots within the configured history window, unless that window is disabled; use the pivot-bar Pressure weight and require Historical magnitude ≥ minimum magnitude; when overlap merging is enabled, suppress creation if the new band overlaps an unswept pool by at least the configured overlap fraction of the smaller band. Set the pool age from its pivot bar and remove it when age ≥ maximum age. For an unswept support pool, count a touch when low ≤ band top AND low ≥ band bottom AND close > band bottom; otherwise enter swept state when low < band bottom − sweep-depth parameter × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback). For an unswept resistance pool, count a touch when high ≥ band bottom AND high ≤ band top AND close < band top; otherwise enter swept state when high > band top + sweep-depth parameter × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback). A support reclaim closes above its configured bottom, midpoint, or top level; a resistance reclaim closes below its configured top, midpoint, or bottom level. Reclaim takes priority over acceptance, resets swept state, increments defended reclaims, and updates the last-interaction bar regardless of alert or RSI settings. While swept, accept a support break when close < band bottom − break-acceptance parameter × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) OR elapsed bars since sweep ≥ reclaim-window parameter AND close < band bottom; accept a resistance break symmetrically above band top. On acceptance, remove the pool if breaker flipping is disabled; otherwise reverse its side, reset touch and rejection-wick counts, and update its last-interaction bar. An unaccepted swept pool remains active. Accumulate Pressure volume over the pool lifetime, counting its confirmation bar twice; initialize last interaction at the pivot bar and update it on touches, reclaims, and flips.
Calculation: Pool strength = clip to configured score bounds 10 × [magnitude-weight parameter × live-pool magnitude rank + (1 − magnitude-weight parameter) × defense rank × idle-decay factor]. Live-pool magnitude rank is the power, by magnitude contrast, of the clipped pool Pressure weight rank between the lowest and highest live-pool weights, using the neutral-rank fallback when equal. Defense rank is the weighted mean, divided by the sum of its four separate weights, of clipped touch count / touch-normalization parameter, mean pool Pressure volume / SMA(Pressure volume, volume-baseline lookback), mean rejection wick / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), and defended reclaims / sweep-normalization parameter; use zero when the weight sum is not positive. Rejection wick is band top − low for support touches and high − band bottom for resistance touches. Idle-decay factor is one when decay is disabled; otherwise it is the greater of the decay-floor parameter and 1 − (1 − decay-floor parameter) × the lesser of idle bars / decay-duration parameter and one.
