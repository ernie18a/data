Calculation: Channel midline = selected EMA or SMA(source, midline lookback), or Kijun(high, low, Kijun lookback).
Calculation: Inner and outer Keltner bands = channel midline ± corresponding band multiplier × selected EMA or SMA(true range, range lookback).
Calculation: %B = (close − (channel midline − deviation multiplier × StDev(source, deviation lookback))) / (2 × deviation multiplier × StDev(source, deviation lookback)).
Calculation: WaveTrend thresholds = selected fixed levels or lower and upper tail percentiles of the WaveTrend signal line over its history, bounded away from zero by the minimum threshold.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = SMA(abs(close − close[lookback]) / sum(abs(close − close[1]), lookback), smoothing lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: WaveTrend confirmation(side) = pass when disabled; in extreme mode, the lowest or highest WaveTrend signal line over the confirmation lookback reaches the corresponding threshold; in cross mode, the WaveTrend fast line crosses above or below its signal line within that lookback; flexible mode accepts either; Auto uses crosses in a trend and extremes in a range, with regime determined by the selected efficiency-ratio or ADX threshold.
Calculation: Bullish WaveTrend divergence = on a WaveTrend bullish cross, the current swing-window price low is below and later than the prior such price low, within the maximum pivot distance, while the current WaveTrend signal-line low is higher than its prior value and at or below the negative divergence threshold; update the prior values at each bullish cross.
Calculation: Bearish WaveTrend divergence = on a WaveTrend bearish cross, the current swing-window price high is above and later than the prior such price high, within the maximum pivot distance, while the current WaveTrend signal-line high is lower than its prior value and at or above the divergence threshold; update the prior values at each bearish cross.
Calculation: RSMK = EMA(log(relative close / relative close[lookback]), smoothing lookback), where relative close is close divided by benchmark close sampled on chart bars, or close divided by 1 in absolute mode.
Calculation: LRETF confirmation(side, family) = the selected trend test, turn test, both, or either points in the side's direction; the trend test compares RSMK with its SMA, its zero line, or both, and the turn test compares RSMK with its value at the turn lookback.
Calculation: LRETF active(family) = LRETF is enabled and its selected mode is not off, and either adaptive selection is disabled or the confirmed pool has at least the minimum result count and its best mean R across the two targets exceeds the all-signal pool's best mean R by the required margin.
Calculation: AutoShow(type) = auto hiding is off, or the type has fewer than the minimum resolved first-target results, or its higher mean R across the two targets exceeds the minimum mean-R threshold; mean R credits target-first results with reward/risk, stop-first results including same-bar ties with a one-risk-unit loss, and excludes neither-hit-before-timeout results. Reversal types use signal-close entry, a stop beyond the excursion extreme by ATR × buffer, a favorable midline first target if available, and the inner band second target; pullback types use signal-close entry, a stop beyond the pullback-window extreme by ATR × buffer, a favorable inner-band first target if available, and the outer band second target; a missing first target uses the second target. Types are separated by recent divergence for reversals and recent opposite divergence warnings for pullbacks.
Calculation: Trend strength = abs(EMA(close, long lookback) − EMA(close, long lookback)[slope lookback]) / ATR(lookback).
Calculation: Breakout stop = entry price − position side × max(abs(entry price − selected breakout-bar stop), minimum-risk ATR multiple × ATR(previous breakout bar, lookback)); the selected stop is the breakout bar's low for a long or high for a short in wick mode, or its open in open mode.
Long: %B crosses above its lower threshold AND the below-threshold excursion did not touch the lower outer Keltner band AND (trend filter is disabled OR EMA(close, long lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback)[slope lookback]) AND WaveTrend confirmation(long) AND (divergence is not required OR a bullish divergence occurred within the validity lookback) AND same-side reversal cooldown has elapsed AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the bar is confirmed) AND (LRETF active(reversal) is false OR LRETF confirmation(long, reversal)) AND AutoShow(the corresponding reversal type).
Short: %B crosses below its upper threshold AND the above-threshold excursion did not touch the upper outer Keltner band AND (trend filter is disabled OR EMA(close, long lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback)[slope lookback]) AND WaveTrend confirmation(short) AND (divergence is not required OR a bearish divergence occurred within the validity lookback) AND same-side reversal cooldown has elapsed AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the bar is confirmed) AND (LRETF active(reversal) is false OR LRETF confirmation(short, reversal)) AND AutoShow(the corresponding reversal type).
Long: A bullish WaveTrend divergence occurs while divergence marking is enabled.
Short: A bearish WaveTrend divergence occurs while divergence marking is enabled.
Long: Pullbacks are enabled AND EMA(close, long lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback)[slope lookback] AND the pullback-window low reaches the selected zone (channel midline or lower inner Keltner band) AND (low <= channel midline OR close[1] >= channel midline[1]) AND close > channel midline AND close > open AND (WaveTrend direction confirmation is disabled OR WaveTrend fast line > its signal line) AND (minimum trend strength is zero OR trend strength meets it) AND opposite-divergence mode is off or warn, or no bearish divergence occurred within the veto lookback AND no qualifying long reversal occurs on this bar AND same-side pullback cooldown has elapsed AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the bar is confirmed) AND (LRETF active(pullback) is false OR LRETF confirmation(long, pullback)) AND AutoShow(the corresponding warned or unwarned pullback type).
Short: Pullbacks are enabled AND EMA(close, long lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback)[slope lookback] AND the pullback-window high reaches the selected zone (channel midline or upper inner Keltner band) AND (high >= channel midline OR close[1] <= channel midline[1]) AND close < channel midline AND close < open AND (WaveTrend direction confirmation is disabled OR WaveTrend fast line < its signal line) AND (minimum trend strength is zero OR trend strength meets it) AND opposite-divergence mode is off or warn, or no bullish divergence occurred within the veto lookback AND no qualifying short reversal occurs on this bar AND same-side pullback cooldown has elapsed AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the bar is confirmed) AND (LRETF active(pullback) is false OR LRETF confirmation(short, pullback)) AND AutoShow(the corresponding warned or unwarned pullback type).
Long: No breakout position is open AND the minimum gap since the previous breakout entry has elapsed AND the confirmed close crosses above EMA(close, band lookback) + band multiplier × ATR(lookback) AND the following bar opens above the prior bar's upper band AND the selected breakout-bar stop is below that open AND the breakout direction setting permits long.
Short: No breakout position is open AND the minimum gap since the previous breakout entry has elapsed AND the confirmed close crosses below EMA(close, band lookback) − band multiplier × ATR(lookback) AND the following bar opens below the prior bar's lower band AND the selected breakout-bar stop is above that open AND the breakout direction setting permits short.
Exit: long, low <= breakout stop OR (bar confirmed AND close < EMA(close, band lookback)) OR bars since breakout entry >= maximum bars in trade.
Exit: short, high >= breakout stop OR (bar confirmed AND close > EMA(close, band lookback)) OR bars since breakout entry >= maximum bars in trade.
