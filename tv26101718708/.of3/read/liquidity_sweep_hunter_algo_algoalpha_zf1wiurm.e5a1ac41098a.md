Calculation: Swept-band stats = at the sweep bar, count distinct clusters of overlapping heatmap cells around retained liquidity levels touched by high (upper) or low (lower); cells are visible if drawn within the cell-width parameter, and their height is MAX(HIGHEST(high, range lookback) - LOWEST(low, range lookback), minimum tick × price resolution) / price resolution. The reclaim level is the lowest swept upper level or highest swept lower level. Average band strength is the mean of each cluster’s maximum normalized level strength; level strength starts at zero, adds capped volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) on confirmed bars closing down for upper levels or up for lower levels, multiplied by separate weights for the nearest levels, and is capped at the strength cap; normalize against the maximum strength among the nearest-level-count active upper and lower levels when the cells are drawn, clamped to zero through one.
Long: A confirmed wick-based sweep touches at least the minimum band count of visible lower bands, the strength filter is disabled or their average normalized strength meets the minimum strength parameter, and within the reclaim-window parameter bars from the sweep the close is above the highest swept lower level.
Short: A confirmed wick-based sweep touches at least the minimum band count of visible upper bands, the strength filter is disabled or their average normalized strength meets the minimum strength parameter, and within the reclaim-window parameter bars from the sweep the close is below the lowest swept upper level.
Exit: long, low <= the lowest low over the stop-lookback parameter bars before entry OR high >= entry price + (entry price - that lowest low) × reward-to-risk parameter OR bars since entry > expiry parameter.
Exit: short, high >= the highest high over the stop-lookback parameter bars before entry OR low <= entry price - (that highest high - entry price) × reward-to-risk parameter OR bars since entry > expiry parameter.
