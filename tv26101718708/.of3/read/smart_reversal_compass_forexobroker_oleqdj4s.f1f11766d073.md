Calculation: bullish RSI divergence = latest confirmed pivot low, using pivot-length bars on each side, is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low, and RSI(close, RSI length) at the latest pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot; the latest pivot is within divergence-lookback bars.
Calculation: bearish RSI divergence = latest confirmed pivot high, using pivot-length bars on each side, is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high, and RSI(close, RSI length) at the latest pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot; the latest pivot is within divergence-lookback bars.
Calculation: volume climax = volume >= SMA(volume, volume-SMA length) * volume-climax multiplier.
Calculation: bullish confluence count = (bullish RSI divergence ? 1 : 0) + (volume climax ? 1 : 0) + (((high > low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= pin-wick ratio AND close > open) OR (close > open AND close > high[1] AND open < low[1])) ? 1 : 0) + ((a confirmed pivot low exists AND abs(low - latest confirmed pivot low) <= ATR(ATR length) * S/R tolerance) ? 1 : 0).
Calculation: bearish confluence count = (bearish RSI divergence ? 1 : 0) + (volume climax ? 1 : 0) + (((high > low AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= pin-wick ratio AND close < open) OR (close < open AND close < low[1] AND open > high[1])) ? 1 : 0) + ((a confirmed pivot high exists AND abs(high - latest confirmed pivot high) <= ATR(ATR length) * S/R tolerance) ? 1 : 0).
Long: bullish RSI divergence OR (bullish confluence count >= minimum confluences AND bullish confluence count > bearish confluence count)
Short: bearish RSI divergence OR (bearish confluence count >= minimum confluences AND bearish confluence count > bullish confluence count)
