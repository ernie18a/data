Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum high of chart bars timestamped in New York from 09:30 inclusive to 09:30 + duration exclusive, when time(T2, weekday 09:30–16:00 session) is not na; reset at each new T1 bar and make available on the first chart bar after the interval.
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars timestamped in New York from 09:30 inclusive to 09:30 + duration exclusive, when time(T2, weekday 09:30–16:00 session) is not na; reset at each new T1 bar and make available on the first chart bar after the interval.
Calculation: Long retest low = minimum low from the first chart bar closing above the opening-range high through the first later chart bar closing below that high and at or above the opening-range low.
Calculation: Short retest high = maximum high from the first chart bar closing below the opening-range low through the first later chart bar closing above that low and at or below the opening-range high.
Long: With the opening range set and a long breakout setup active, close < opening-range high AND close >= opening-range low; clear the pending setup at each new T1 bar or on a close below the opening-range low.
Long: With the opening range set, a close above its high starts a long breakout setup; after a retest, enter long at the close of a later bar with close > opening-range high, provided no close below opening-range low has canceled the setup; then clear the setup.
Short: With the opening range set and a short breakout setup active, close > opening-range low AND close <= opening-range high; clear the pending setup at each new T1 bar or on a close above the opening-range high.
Short: With the opening range set, a close below its low starts a short breakout setup; after a retest, enter short at the close of a later bar with close < opening-range low, provided no close above opening-range high has canceled the setup; then clear the setup.
Exit: Long, initialize the stop at the long retest low; activate trailing when close >= entry + risk multiple × (entry − long retest low), then on each bar raise the stop to SMA(close, lookback) if it is higher; exit when close < current stop and clear the active stop.
Exit: Short, initialize the stop at the short retest high; activate trailing when close <= entry − risk multiple × (short retest high − entry), then on each bar lower the stop to SMA(close, lookback) if it is lower; exit when close > current stop and clear the active stop.
