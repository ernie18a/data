Calculation: Direction(T) = sign(Stochastic %K(close, high, low, lookback, K-smoothing) − Stochastic %D(close, high, low, lookback, K-smoothing, D-smoothing)); calculate on T bars and sample on each chart bar from either T’s last completed bar or still-forming bar, according to data mode; T1 through T4 are ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Agreement run = consecutive chart-bar count where all four timeframe directions share the same nonzero sign; when day reset is enabled, the first chart bar of each calendar day is not an agreement bar.
Long: T1, T2, T3, and T4 directions are positive AND the completed-run history contains at least the minimum sample count capped by its history capacity AND ((continuation mode is enabled AND the agreement run exceeds the linear-interpolated continuation percentile of retained completed run lengths AND this state was false on the previous chart bar) OR (exhaustion mode is enabled AND the agreement run exceeds the linear-interpolated percentile at the greater of the exhaustion and continuation percentile parameters AND T1 Stochastic %K is at or above its upper extreme threshold or at or below its lower extreme threshold AND this state was false on the previous chart bar) OR the agreement run exceeds every retained completed run length); continuation and exhaustion events are restricted to confirmed chart bars when the signal-on-close gate is enabled.
Short: T1, T2, T3, and T4 directions are negative AND the completed-run history contains at least the minimum sample count capped by its history capacity AND ((continuation mode is enabled AND the agreement run exceeds the linear-interpolated continuation percentile of retained completed run lengths AND this state was false on the previous chart bar) OR (exhaustion mode is enabled AND the agreement run exceeds the linear-interpolated percentile at the greater of the exhaustion and continuation percentile parameters AND T1 Stochastic %K is at or above its upper extreme threshold or at or below its lower extreme threshold AND this state was false on the previous chart bar) OR the agreement run exceeds every retained completed run length); continuation and exhaustion events are restricted to confirmed chart bars when the signal-on-close gate is enabled.
