Calculation: Normalized magnitude(ratio) = w1 when ratio > c1, w2 when c2 < ratio <= c1, w3 when c3 < ratio <= c2, w4 when c4 < ratio <= c3, w5 when c5 < ratio <= c4, w6 when c6 < ratio <= c5, w7 when c7 < ratio <= c6, and w8 otherwise; c1 through c7 are descending cutoff parameters and w1 through w8 are corresponding weight parameters.
Calculation: SuperTrend direction = a trailing-band direction on smoothed Stochastic RSI x, where x is SMA of 100*(R - lowest(R, stochastic lookback))/(highest(R, stochastic lookback) - lowest(R, stochastic lookback)), with R = RSI of the T1 close mapped onto chart bars using either the last completed or still-forming T1 bar according to the close-wait setting; RSI, stochastic, smoothing, and bands are calculated on chart bars. Raw upper and lower bands are x plus and minus the SuperTrend offset. The upper band is the raw upper band if it is below the prior upper band or prior x is above the prior upper band, and otherwise is the prior upper band. The lower band is the raw lower band if it is above the prior lower band or prior x is below the prior lower band, and otherwise is the prior lower band. From the second chart bar onward, when the prior trendline equals the prior upper band, direction is bullish if x exceeds the upper band and bearish otherwise; when they differ, direction is bearish if x is below the lower band and bullish otherwise. Initialize direction bearish on the second chart bar; the trendline is the lower band when bullish and upper band when bearish.
Calculation: Bar flow = ((close-location measure + close-open spread measure + normalized spread measure + range-location measure + range-scaled close-change measure + normalized close-change measure) / 6) * volume strength, on chart bars. The close-location measure is 2*(close-low)/(high-low)*100-100, or 0 when high equals low. The close-open spread measure is (close-open)/(high-low)*100, or 0 when high equals low. The normalized spread measure is sign(close-open)*100*Normalized magnitude(abs(close-open)/SMA(abs(close-open), EVEREX normalization lookback)). Let range = highest(high, range lookback)-lowest(low, range lookback). The range-location measure is 2*(close-lowest(low, range lookback))/range*100-100, or 0 when range is zero. The range-scaled close-change measure is (close-close[1])/range*100, or 0 when range is zero. The normalized close-change measure is sign(close-close[1])*100*Normalized magnitude(abs(close-close[1])/SMA(abs(close-close[1]), EVEREX normalization lookback)). Volume strength is 1 when volume is missing, otherwise Normalized magnitude(volume/SMA(volume, EVEREX normalization lookback)).
Calculation: EVEREX oscillator = WMA(2*(100-100/(1+WMA(max(Bar flow, 0), EVEREX flow length)/WMA(-min(Bar flow, 0), EVEREX flow length)))-100, EVEREX oscillator smoothing), calculated on chart bars.
Long: EVEREX oscillator turns from non-rising to rising AND (SuperTrend direction flips bullish while smoothed Stochastic RSI is below the midpoint threshold OR SuperTrend direction flipped bullish within the prior confirmation-window chart bars AND smoothed Stochastic RSI is rising).
Short: EVEREX oscillator turns from rising to non-rising AND (SuperTrend direction flips bearish while smoothed Stochastic RSI is above the midpoint threshold OR SuperTrend direction flipped bearish within the prior confirmation-window chart bars AND smoothed Stochastic RSI is falling).
