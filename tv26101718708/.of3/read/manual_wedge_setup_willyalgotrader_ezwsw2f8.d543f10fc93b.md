Calculation: Wedge = sort the distinct selected anchor-count bars chronologically and require them all closed; assign each bar’s High or Low to an upper or lower boundary, with at least two contacts per boundary; fit each boundary through its assigned contacts; retain assignments whose boundaries have positive width at the first and last anchors, converge by more than the minimum-convergence-ticks parameter, satisfy the strict-same-direction parameter when enabled, and satisfy the alternating-contact parameter when enabled; selected range = highest selected High − lowest selected Low; scale = max(selected range, minimum-tick × range-floor-ticks parameter); penetration = max(0, each selected High − upper boundary, lower boundary − each selected Low); tolerance = max(scale × tolerance-percentage parameter / 100, minimum-tick × minimum-wick-tolerance-ticks parameter); require penetration ≤ tolerance and choose the valid assignment with least penetration, breaking ties by lowest integer bitmask with chronological anchors as ascending bits and upper assignment as 1.
Calculation: Initial stop = round to minimum tick of lowest assigned lower Low − stop-offset-ticks parameter × minimum tick for a falling wedge, or highest assigned upper High + stop-offset-ticks parameter × minimum tick for a rising wedge; undefined for other formations.
Calculation: Targets = H = absolute difference between the earliest assigned upper High and earliest assigned lower Low; entry = breakout close; TP3 = round to minimum tick of breakout boundary + trade direction × H × near-target-ratio parameter; TP1 and TP2 = round to minimum tick of entry + (TP3 − entry) × their respective path-percentage parameters / 100.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until the candle after the first confirmed TP1 touch; from that candle onward use entry price when the break-even-after-TP1 parameter is enabled.
Long: Valid Wedge and upper-boundary slope < 0 and lower-boundary slope < 0.
Short: Valid Wedge and upper-boundary slope > 0 and lower-boundary slope > 0.
Long: The first confirmed close outside either boundary after the last selected anchor and before the apex is above the upper boundary, and the initial stop is below entry, and entry < TP1 < TP2 < TP3.
Short: The first confirmed close outside either boundary after the last selected anchor and before the apex is below the lower boundary, and the initial stop is above entry, and entry > TP1 > TP2 > TP3.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed candle after entry, High ≥ TP1 for its first touch or High ≥ TP2 for its first touch or High ≥ TP3.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed candle after entry, Low ≤ TP1 for its first touch or Low ≤ TP2 for its first touch or Low ≤ TP3.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed candle after entry, Low ≤ active stop and High < TP3 and neither TP1 nor TP2 is touched for the first time on that candle.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed candle after entry, High ≥ active stop and Low > TP3 and neither TP1 nor TP2 is touched for the first time on that candle.
