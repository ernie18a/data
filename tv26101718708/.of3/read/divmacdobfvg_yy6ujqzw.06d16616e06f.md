Calculation: MACD histogram = EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback) − SMA(EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback), signal lookback)
Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = [high[2], low]; bearish FVG zone = [high, low[2]].
Calculation: Bullish OB zone = [low, high] of the lowest-low bar from the latest confirmed pivot high through its first close above it; scan at most the maximum-scan parameter. Bearish OB zone = [low, high] of the highest-high bar from the latest confirmed pivot low through its first close below it; scan at most the maximum-scan parameter.
Calculation: A bullish FVG or OB zone remains active until a chart-bar close is below its lower boundary; a bearish FVG or OB zone remains active until a chart-bar close is above its upper boundary.
Calculation: Long stop price = selected bullish OB lower boundary or bullish divergence pivot low − ATR(lookback) × stop-margin parameter. Short stop price = selected bearish OB upper boundary or bearish divergence pivot high + ATR(lookback) × stop-margin parameter.
Long: A confirmed histogram pivot low, compared with the previous confirmed histogram pivot low, is within the minimum and maximum pivot-distance parameters AND ((the current pivot-bar low is lower than the previous pivot-bar low AND the current histogram pivot is higher than the previous histogram pivot) OR (the current pivot-bar low is higher than the previous pivot-bar low AND the current histogram pivot is lower than the previous histogram pivot)) AND (the zero-side filter is disabled OR the current histogram pivot is below zero). Use separate left- and right-lookback parameters; compare lows at the histogram pivot bars.
Short: A confirmed histogram pivot high, compared with the previous confirmed histogram pivot high, is within the minimum and maximum pivot-distance parameters AND ((the current pivot-bar high is higher than the previous pivot-bar high AND the current histogram pivot is lower than the previous histogram pivot) OR (the current pivot-bar high is lower than the previous pivot-bar high AND the current histogram pivot is higher than the previous histogram pivot)) AND (the zero-side filter is disabled OR the current histogram pivot is above zero). Use separate left- and right-lookback parameters; compare highs at the histogram pivot bars.
Long: histogram[1] < histogram[2] AND histogram[1] < 0 AND histogram > histogram[1] AND the latest T1 histogram available within the current chart bar > its value on the previous chart bar. T1 is below the chart timeframe; its current bar may still be forming in real time, and historical chart bars use the last intrabar value.
Short: histogram[1] > histogram[2] AND histogram[1] > 0 AND histogram < histogram[1] AND the latest T1 histogram available within the current chart bar < its value on the previous chart bar. T1 is below the chart timeframe; its current bar may still be forming in real time, and historical chart bars use the last intrabar value.
Long: low > high[2] AND close[1] > high[2] AND low − high[2] > ATR(lookback) × minimum-gap-size parameter.
Short: high < low[2] AND close[1] < low[2] AND low[2] − high > ATR(lookback) × minimum-gap-size parameter.
Long: the current chart-bar close is the first close above the latest confirmed pivot high(high, OB swing lookback, OB swing lookback), before a newer pivot high is confirmed.
Short: the current chart-bar close is the first close below the latest confirmed pivot low(low, OB swing lookback, OB swing lookback), before a newer pivot low is confirmed.
Long: a bullish divergence or histogram-low marker has previously occurred AND an active bullish zone exists AND (close is within the zone OR the distance from close to its nearest boundary is at most the zone-proximity parameter) AND histogram > histogram[1] AND histogram ≤ 0.
Long: a bullish divergence or histogram-low marker has previously occurred AND an active bullish zone exists AND low ≤ its upper boundary (limit buy at that boundary).
Short: a bearish divergence or histogram-high marker has previously occurred AND an active bearish zone exists AND (close is within the zone OR the distance from close to its nearest boundary is at most the zone-proximity parameter) AND histogram < histogram[1] AND histogram > 0.
Short: a bearish divergence or histogram-high marker has previously occurred AND an active bearish zone exists AND high ≥ its lower boundary (limit sell at that boundary).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop price OR high ≥ the lower boundary of the nearest active bearish zone above close OR (histogram > 0 AND histogram < histogram[1] AND histogram[1] > 0 AND histogram[1] > histogram[2]) OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − long stop price) × risk/reward parameter.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop price OR low ≤ the upper boundary of the nearest active bullish zone below close OR (histogram ≤ 0 AND histogram > histogram[1] AND histogram[1] ≤ 0 AND histogram[1] < histogram[2]) OR low ≤ entry price − (short stop price − entry price) × risk/reward parameter.
