Calculation: opening-range range = highest(high during the chart bars in the UTC opening-range interval of the current weekday) - lowest(low during the chart bars in the UTC opening-range interval of the current weekday)
Long: weekday AND bar UTC time is within the entry window AND opening-range range <= ATR(ATR lookback) × opening-range ATR cap AND ATR(ATR lookback) <= SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), regime lookback) × ATR regime cap AND no earlier long signal occurred in the current session AND close > highest(high during the chart bars in the UTC opening-range interval of the current weekday) AND close[1] <= highest(high during the chart bars in the UTC opening-range interval of the current weekday) AND close > open AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume threshold
Exit: long, low <= close on the long entry-signal bar - opening-range range × stop-distance parameter OR high >= close on the long entry-signal bar + opening-range range × target-distance parameter OR weekday AND bar UTC time equals the session-close time
