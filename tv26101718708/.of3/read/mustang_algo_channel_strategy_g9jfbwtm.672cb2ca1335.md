Calculation: sentiment ratio = supply parameter / ((DAI market cap close + USDT market cap close + USDC market cap close) / BTC price close), using source closes on the chart timeframe
Calculation: sentiment oscillator = WMA(RSI(sentiment ratio, RSI lookback), oscillator smoothing lookback), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars with lookahead off (current forming T1 bar in realtime; completed T1 bar historically)
Calculation: sentiment median = WMA(sentiment oscillator, median lookback), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars with lookahead off (current forming T1 bar in realtime; completed T1 bar historically)
Long: long direction enabled AND datetime is within the inclusive start-date and end-date parameters AND sentiment oscillator crosses above sentiment median AND sentiment oscillator < lower threshold
Short: short direction enabled AND datetime is within the inclusive start-date and end-date parameters AND sentiment oscillator crosses below sentiment median AND sentiment oscillator > upper threshold
Exit: Long, stop loss enabled AND ((SL/TP mode is percentage AND low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100)) OR (SL/TP mode is ticks AND low <= entry price − stop-loss ticks × minimum tick size) OR (SL/TP mode is pips AND low <= entry price − stop-loss pips × 10 × minimum tick size)) OR take profit enabled AND ((SL/TP mode is percentage AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage / 100)) OR (SL/TP mode is ticks AND high >= entry price + take-profit ticks × minimum tick size) OR (SL/TP mode is pips AND high >= entry price + take-profit pips × 10 × minimum tick size)) OR stop loss disabled AND take profit disabled AND long-only direction AND datetime is within the inclusive start-date and end-date parameters AND sentiment oscillator crosses below sentiment median AND sentiment oscillator > upper threshold
