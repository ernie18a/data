Calculation: PDH = high of the last completed T1 bar, held on chart bars until the next T1 bar completes
Calculation: PDL = low of the last completed T1 bar, held on chart bars until the next T1 bar completes
Calculation: Offset range = SMA(high - low, P_heightLookback, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: PDH sweep level = PDH + Offset range × P_sweepOffset; PDH breakout level = PDH + Offset range × P_breakOffset; PDL sweep level = PDL - Offset range × P_sweepOffset; PDL breakout level = PDL - Offset range × P_breakOffset
Calculation: Golden Pocket zone = prior T1 range between PDL and PDH at the midpoint of P_fibLow and P_fibHigh, centered in a band of thickness Offset range × P_zoneHeight
Calculation: LevelStrength(side) = round(clamp(weighted sum of volume credit, range credit, rejection credit, round-distance credit, and isolation credit divided by the sum of their separate weights, 0, 1) × P_scoreScale, to P_scorePrecision); volume credit = min(max(volume ratio, 0) / max(P_volumeFullCredit, P_volumeFloor), 1); range credit = min(max(range ratio, 0) / max(P_rangeFullCredit, P_rangeFloor), 1); rejection credit = min(max(rejection fraction, 0) / max(P_rejectionFullCredit, P_rejectionFloor), 1); round-distance credit = min(distance from level to nearest multiple of round step divided by round step × P_roundDistanceFraction, 1); isolation credit = 1 - min(same-side confluence count / P_confluenceSaturation, 1); volume ratio = last completed T1 volume / SMA(volume, P_baselineLookback, T1, last completed bar); range ratio = last completed T1 high-low range / SMA(high-low, P_baselineLookback, T1, last completed bar); high rejection fraction = (PDH - last completed T1 close) / (PDH - PDL); low rejection fraction = (last completed T1 close - PDL) / (PDH - PDL); confluence count = count of preceding completed T1 highs for the high side or lows for the low side, up to P_confluenceLookback, within SMA(high-low, P_baselineLookback, T1, last completed bar) × P_confluenceTolerance of the level; round step = P_roundStep when positive, otherwise 10^(floor(log10(max(last completed T1 close, P_priceFloor))) - P_roundDigits)
Calculation: PDH strength = LevelStrength(high) using PDH and preceding T1 highs
Calculation: PDL strength = LevelStrength(low) using PDL and preceding T1 lows
Calculation: Breakout volume baseline = SMA(volume, P_expansionLookback, chart bars preceding the current bar)
Long: on chart bars, PDL strength >= P_fadeStrengthMinimum AND low <= PDL sweep level AND close > PDL
Short: on chart bars, PDH strength >= P_fadeStrengthMinimum AND high >= PDH sweep level AND close < PDH
Long: on chart bars, (PDH strength >= P_weakTierFloor AND PDH strength < P_strongTierFloor OR last completed T1 volume < SMA(volume, P_baselineLookback, T1, last completed bar)) AND ((close crosses above PDH breakout level AND volume > Breakout volume baseline) OR (after an earlier chart-bar close above PDH breakout level, low <= PDH AND close > PDH))
Short: on chart bars, (PDL strength >= P_weakTierFloor AND PDL strength < P_strongTierFloor OR last completed T1 volume < SMA(volume, P_baselineLookback, T1, last completed bar)) AND ((close crosses below PDL breakout level AND volume > Breakout volume baseline) OR (after an earlier chart-bar close below PDL breakout level, high >= PDL AND close < PDL))
Exit: long, (entry price <= PDH AND (high >= Golden Pocket lower edge AND low <= Golden Pocket upper edge OR high >= PDH OR low <= PDL breakout level)) OR (entry price > PDH AND low <= Golden Pocket lower edge)
Exit: short, (entry price >= PDL AND (high >= Golden Pocket lower edge AND low <= Golden Pocket upper edge OR low <= PDL OR high >= PDH breakout level)) OR (entry price < PDL AND high >= Golden Pocket upper edge)
