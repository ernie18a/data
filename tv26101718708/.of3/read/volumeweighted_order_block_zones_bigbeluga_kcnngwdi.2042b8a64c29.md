Calculation: Volume strength(v, s) = strength-base parameter − clamp(int(alpha-base parameter − volume-ratio multiplier parameter × (s > 0 ? v / s : fallback-ratio parameter)), minimum-alpha parameter, maximum-alpha parameter).
Calculation: Active bullish OB = a zone [low[swing lookback], high[swing lookback]] from a confirmed pivot low whose pivot-bar Volume strength(volume[swing lookback], SMA(volume, volume-SMA lookback)[swing lookback]) meets the minimum-strength parameter, with level low[swing lookback] + ATR(ATR lookback, current chart bar) × displacement parameter. The zone is created if, at pivot confirmation, a bar in offsets 0 through swing lookback−1 has close or high at or above the level and no low from the most recent such bar through confirmation is below the pivot low; otherwise it is created when a confirmed close crosses above the level. A newer qualifying pivot replaces the pending trigger. Remove the zone when its low is breached, when a newer bullish zone overlaps it, or when it is the oldest zone removed because total active zones exceed the maximum-active-zones parameter.
Calculation: Active bearish OB = a zone [low[swing lookback], high[swing lookback]] from a confirmed pivot high whose pivot-bar Volume strength(volume[swing lookback], SMA(volume, volume-SMA lookback)[swing lookback]) meets the minimum-strength parameter, with level high[swing lookback] − ATR(ATR lookback, current chart bar) × displacement parameter. The zone is created if, at pivot confirmation, a bar in offsets 0 through swing lookback−1 has close or low at or below the level and no high from the most recent such bar through confirmation is above the pivot high; otherwise it is created when a confirmed close crosses below the level. A newer qualifying pivot replaces the pending trigger. Remove the zone when its high is breached, when a newer bearish zone overlaps it, or when it is the oldest zone removed because total active zones exceed the maximum-active-zones parameter.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an active bullish OB exists with current low at or above its bottom, AND ((low[1] ≤ its top AND low > its top) OR (close[1] ≤ its top AND close > its top)), AND more than the retest-cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the prior long retest signal (initial prior-signal bar index 0).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an active bearish OB exists with current high at or below its top, AND ((high[1] ≥ its bottom AND high < its bottom) OR (close[1] ≥ its bottom AND close < its bottom)), AND more than the retest-cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the prior short retest signal (initial prior-signal bar index 0).
