Calculation: Shared filter = volume >= minimum volume AND RVOL(volume, RVOL lookback) >= RVOL threshold AND high - low >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum range multiple AND close >= minimum price
Calculation: Body percentage = ABS(close - open) / (high - low) * 100
Calculation: Gap state = On chart bars, at the first bar after each T1 period changes, calculate (open - previous chart bar close) / previous chart bar close * 100; if its absolute value meets the minimum gap percentage, activate a gap, clear its partial-fill flag, and set its direction up when open > previous chart bar close or down otherwise, with gap top and bottom equal to the higher and lower of those prices; otherwise deactivate the gap. On each chart bar while active, calculate fill percentage as (gap top - low) / (gap top - gap bottom) * 100 for an up gap or (high - gap bottom) / (gap top - gap bottom) * 100 for a down gap; set the partial-fill flag when it reaches the partial-fill percentage and deactivate the gap when it reaches the full-fill cutoff.
Long: Confirmed bar close AND Shared filter AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND (high[2] > low[2] AND high[1] <= high[2] AND low[1] >= low[2] AND close > high[1] OR close > Highest(high, ATH lookback)[1] AND close[1] <= Highest(high, ATH lookback)[1] OR Gap state is active and down and partially filled AND close > open AND close > gap bottom OR high > high[1] AND low < low[1] AND Body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND close > open AND close >= high[1])
Short: Confirmed bar close AND Shared filter AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND (high[2] > low[2] AND high[1] <= high[2] AND low[1] >= low[2] AND close < low[1] OR Gap state is active and up AND close < open AND high < gap top AND low > gap bottom OR high > high[1] AND low < low[1] AND Body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND close < open AND close <= low[1])
