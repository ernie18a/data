Calculation: momentum = EMA(ROC(close, momentum lookback), momentum smoothing lookback), optionally multiplied by min(volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), volume-ratio cap) when the volume average is positive, or by one otherwise
Calculation: EMA 1 = EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1), sampled on chart bars from the requested bar with lookahead off (last completed on historical bars, still forming in real time); T1 is shorter than T2
Calculation: EMA 2 = EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T2), sampled on chart bars from the requested bar with lookahead off (last completed on historical bars, still forming in real time)
Calculation: valid positive momentum peak = confirmed pivot high of momentum using separate left and right lookbacks, greater than minimum peak strength and at least minimum peak distance after the preceding valid positive peak
Calculation: valid negative momentum trough = confirmed pivot low of momentum using separate left and right lookbacks, less than negative minimum peak strength and at least minimum peak distance after the preceding valid negative trough
Calculation: efficiency ratio = abs(close - close[n]) / sum(abs(close[i] - close[i + 1]), i from 0 to n - 1), or zero when the denominator is zero
Long: momentum > 0 AND momentum crosses above the latest valid positive momentum peak stored on the previous bar AND close > the high of that peak's price bar AND EMA 1 > EMA 2 AND (momentum expansion filter disabled OR 100 * (momentum - peak) / abs(peak) >= its minimum) AND (breakout displacement filter disabled OR (close - peak price high) / ATR(breakout ATR lookback) >= its minimum) AND (efficiency filter disabled OR efficiency ratio >= its minimum) AND bars since the previous primary signal >= cooldown AND, if prior signals exist, bars since both the previous long and previous short primary signals >= signal spacing AND no tracked trade is active
Short: momentum < 0 AND momentum crosses below the latest valid negative momentum trough stored on the previous bar AND close < the low of that trough's price bar AND EMA 1 < EMA 2 AND (momentum expansion filter disabled OR 100 * (trough - momentum) / abs(trough) >= its minimum) AND (breakout displacement filter disabled OR (trough price low - close) / ATR(breakout ATR lookback) >= its minimum) AND (efficiency filter disabled OR efficiency ratio >= its minimum) AND bars since the previous primary signal >= cooldown AND, if a prior short signal exists, bars since it >= signal spacing AND no tracked trade is active AND no long primary signal occurs on this bar
Long: a new valid negative momentum trough is confirmed, its price low is below the previous valid negative trough's price low, and its momentum value is above the previous valid negative trough's momentum value
Short: a new valid positive momentum peak is confirmed, its price high is above the previous valid positive peak's price high, and its momentum value is below the previous valid positive peak's momentum value
Long: momentum exceeds the latest valid positive momentum peak by either a percentage of that peak's absolute value or an absolute momentum distance, according to the threshold mode, AND no bullish sweep is active; reset an active bullish sweep when momentum crosses below its swept level in Cross Back mode or closes below it in Close Back mode
Short: momentum falls below the latest valid negative momentum trough by either a percentage of that trough's absolute value or an absolute momentum distance, according to the threshold mode, AND no bearish sweep is active; reset an active bearish sweep when momentum crosses above its swept level in Cross Back mode or closes above it in Close Back mode
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= EMA 2 on the entry bar OR high >= entry price + (entry price - EMA 2 on the entry bar) * TP3 reward/risk multiple
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= EMA 2 on the entry bar OR low <= entry price - (EMA 2 on the entry bar - entry price) * TP3 reward/risk multiple
