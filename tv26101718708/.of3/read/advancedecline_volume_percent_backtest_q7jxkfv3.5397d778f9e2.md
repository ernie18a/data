Calculation: Volume-change percentage = ((A - D) - nz((A - D)[1], 0)) * 100 / (A + D), where A and D are advancing-volume and declining-volume closes on T1's still-forming bar, sampled on each chart bar, and [1] is the previous chart bar's sampled value
Calculation: Threshold state = if volume-change percentage > upper threshold then long, else if volume-change percentage < lower threshold then short, else previous threshold state (neutral initially)
Long: (trade-reverse parameter is off AND threshold state is long) OR (trade-reverse parameter is on AND threshold state is short)
Short: (trade-reverse parameter is off AND threshold state is short) OR (trade-reverse parameter is on AND threshold state is long)
Calculation: normalized advancing-declining volume change = percentage change in (advancing volume at T1 − declining volume at T1), using the previous value of that difference or zero if unavailable, divided by the current sum of advancing and declining volume at T1
Long: normal direction AND (normalized advancing-declining volume change > upper threshold OR normalized advancing-declining volume change ≥ lower threshold AND normalized advancing-declining volume change ≤ upper threshold AND previous unreversed signal is long) OR reversed direction AND (normalized advancing-declining volume change < lower threshold OR normalized advancing-declining volume change ≥ lower threshold AND normalized advancing-declining volume change ≤ upper threshold AND previous unreversed signal is short)
Short: normal direction AND (normalized advancing-declining volume change < lower threshold OR normalized advancing-declining volume change ≥ lower threshold AND normalized advancing-declining volume change ≤ upper threshold AND previous unreversed signal is short) OR reversed direction AND (normalized advancing-declining volume change > upper threshold OR normalized advancing-declining volume change ≥ lower threshold AND normalized advancing-declining volume change ≤ upper threshold AND previous unreversed signal is long)
