Long: Combine the enabled conditions assigned long using the configured long AND/OR connector; each of up to six conditions compares its selected indicator with its selected operator (>, <, >=, <=, =, !=, crosses above, or crosses below) against either a tunable scalar or another selected indicator. Indicators are ATR(high, low, close, lookback), either of two independently parameterized EMA/SMA/RMA/WMA(source, lookback) moving averages sharing a source, CCI(source, lookback), RSI(source, lookback), BBW(SMA(source, lookback), lookback, multiplier), ADX(DI lookback, smoothing), MFI(source, lookback), or momentum(source, lookback).
