Calculation: MarketProfileVA(B, valueAreaPct, levelCount) = [VAH, VAL] of bar set B: split the price range from the lowest low to the highest high of B into levelCount equal rows; each bar adds one count to each row from the row holding its low through the row holding its high, clipped to the row range; start from the row with the most counts (first if tied) and repeatedly add whichever row just above or just below the included rows has more counts (above if tied) until the included counts reach valueAreaPct percent of all counts; VAH is the upper edge of the highest included row, VAL the lower edge of the lowest
Calculation: BalanceBars(T) = on T bars, the bars from the start bar through the current bar, or through the breakout bar while OutOfBalance(T)[1] is true; the start bar is the first bar of T's data, moved to the first of the last min(bars since breakout bar, maxWindowBars) bars when OutOfBalance(T) turns false by the spread test
Calculation: OutOfBalance(T) = on T bars: if OutOfBalance(T)[1] is false, true when close > BalanceHigh(T) or close < BalanceLow(T), else false; if OutOfBalance(T)[1] is true, false when BalanceLow(T) at the breakout bar <= close <= BalanceHigh(T) at the breakout bar; otherwise false when min(bars since breakout bar, maxWindowBars) >= minWindowBars and minSpreadPct <= 100 * (VAH - VAL) / (highest high - lowest low) <= maxSpreadPct over the last min(bars since breakout bar, maxWindowBars) bars, where [VAH, VAL] = MarketProfileVA of those bars; otherwise true; breakout bar = the latest bar where OutOfBalance(T) turned true
Calculation: BalanceHigh(T) = VAH of MarketProfileVA(BalanceBars(T), valueAreaPct, levelCount) + ATR(atrPeriod) of T bars * atrScale
Calculation: BalanceLow(T) = VAL of MarketProfileVA(BalanceBars(T), valueAreaPct, levelCount) - ATR(atrPeriod) of T bars * atrScale
Long: OutOfBalance(T1, last completed bar)[1] AND close > BalanceHigh(T1, last completed bar)[1]
Long: OutOfBalance(T2, last completed bar)[1] AND close > BalanceHigh(T2, last completed bar)[1]
Long: OutOfBalance(T3, last completed bar)[1] AND close > BalanceHigh(T3, last completed bar)[1]
Short: OutOfBalance(T1, last completed bar)[1] AND close < BalanceLow(T1, last completed bar)[1]
Short: OutOfBalance(T2, last completed bar)[1] AND close < BalanceLow(T2, last completed bar)[1]
Short: OutOfBalance(T3, last completed bar)[1] AND close < BalanceLow(T3, last completed bar)[1]
