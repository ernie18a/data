Calculation: Chart EMAs = EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), EMA(close, middle EMA lookback), EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Chart RSI = RSI(close, RSI lookback), evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Chart MACD outputs = MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: T1 EMAs = EMA(close, the same shorter, middle, and longer EMA lookbacks, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off; historically the completed T1 values update on the final chart bar of each T1 interval, while realtime values use the still-forming T1 bar.
Calculation: Chart bullish EMA structure = chart shorter EMA > chart middle EMA AND chart middle EMA > chart longer EMA
Calculation: Chart bearish EMA structure = chart shorter EMA < chart middle EMA AND chart middle EMA < chart longer EMA
Calculation: T1 bullish EMA structure = T1 shorter EMA > T1 middle EMA AND T1 middle EMA > T1 longer EMA
Calculation: T1 bearish EMA structure = T1 shorter EMA < T1 middle EMA AND T1 middle EMA < T1 longer EMA
Calculation: Long score = EMA-structure score weight if Chart bullish EMA structure, plus the shared confirmation score weight for each true condition: Chart RSI > RSI midline; Chart MACD line > Chart MACD signal line; Chart MACD histogram > its value on the previous chart bar; T1 bullish EMA structure. Each false condition contributes zero.
Calculation: Short score = EMA-structure score weight if Chart bearish EMA structure, plus the same shared confirmation score weight for each true condition: Chart RSI < RSI midline; Chart MACD line < Chart MACD signal line; Chart MACD histogram < its value on the previous chart bar; T1 bearish EMA structure. Each false condition contributes zero.
Calculation: Long entry condition = Long score >= minimum score AND (T1 filter disabled OR T1 bullish EMA structure) AND ((Chart bullish EMA structure AND low <= chart shorter EMA AND close > chart shorter EMA AND Chart RSI > Chart RSI[1] AND Chart RSI > long pullback RSI threshold AND Chart MACD line > Chart MACD signal line) OR Chart MACD line crosses above Chart MACD signal line)
Calculation: Short entry condition = Short score >= minimum score AND (T1 filter disabled OR T1 bearish EMA structure) AND ((Chart bearish EMA structure AND high >= chart shorter EMA AND close < chart shorter EMA AND Chart RSI < Chart RSI[1] AND Chart RSI < short pullback RSI threshold AND Chart MACD line < Chart MACD signal line) OR Chart MACD line crosses below Chart MACD signal line)
Long: Chart bullish EMA structure OR (chart shorter EMA > chart middle EMA AND chart middle EMA < chart longer EMA) OR (Long entry condition AND NOT Long entry condition[1]) OR chart shorter EMA crosses above chart middle EMA OR chart middle EMA crosses above chart longer EMA
Short: Chart bearish EMA structure OR (chart shorter EMA < chart middle EMA AND chart middle EMA > chart longer EMA) OR (Short entry condition AND NOT Short entry condition[1]) OR chart shorter EMA crosses below chart middle EMA OR chart middle EMA crosses below chart longer EMA
