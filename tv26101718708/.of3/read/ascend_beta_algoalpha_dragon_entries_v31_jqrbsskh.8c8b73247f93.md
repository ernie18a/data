Calculation: Active range boxes = boxes produced by the pivot-based range detector using the range-detection lookback and pivot confirmation width of half that lookback.
Calculation: Context box bounds = the most recently broken box bounds when available; otherwise the first active box bounds.
Calculation: Session window = current time is within the configured entry session, or outside-session entries are enabled.
Calculation: Elapsed session time = current local clock time minus the configured reference clock time.
Calculation: Session open = first open on the current trading date; session high and low = running highest high and lowest low since that date began.
Calculation: Pulse direction = on the first in-window bar at or after the pulse duration, long if close >= session open, otherwise short; retain until the next trading date.
Calculation: Prior structure high/low = highest high and lowest low over the MSS lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Flip structure high/low = highest high and lowest low over the flip-break lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Impulse high/low = highest high and lowest low over the impulse lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Base high/low = highest high and lowest low over the base-bar lookback, excluding the current bar; base range = base high - base low.
Calculation: Active direction = pulse direction when initialized, then the side of any qualifying A reversal, B range break, or late flip in script evaluation order; retain until changed or the next trading date.
Calculation: A long event = session window AND no A event yet today AND elapsed session time is between the opening-range duration and A deadline AND pulse direction is short AND close > prior structure high + entry buffer.
Calculation: A short event = session window AND no A event yet today AND elapsed session time is between the opening-range duration and A deadline AND pulse direction is long AND close < prior structure low - entry buffer.
Calculation: B long event = session window AND no A or B event yet today AND pulse direction is long AND elapsed session time is between the opening-range duration and B deadline AND close >= session high + entry buffer AND close >= open.
Calculation: B short event = session window AND no A or B event yet today AND pulse direction is short AND elapsed session time is between the opening-range duration and B deadline AND close <= session low - entry buffer AND close <= open.
Calculation: Leg high/low = running highest high and lowest low since direction initialization, an A/B event, a late flip, or a qualifying new continuation extreme.
Calculation: Flip cooldown = no prior A/B/late-flip event today OR bars since that event >= flip-cooldown lookback.
Calculation: Recent low extreme = the lowest low over twice the recent-extreme lookback occurred within the recent-extreme lookback; recent high extreme is the corresponding highest-high condition.
Calculation: Late long flip = session high - session low >= flip-move threshold AND active direction is short AND flip cooldown AND recent low extreme AND close > flip structure high + entry buffer AND close >= open AND elapsed session time >= flip-minimum time AND close - leg low >= minimum leg move.
Calculation: Late short flip = session high - session low >= flip-move threshold AND active direction is long AND flip cooldown AND recent high extreme AND close < flip structure low - entry buffer AND close <= open AND elapsed session time >= flip-minimum time AND leg high - close >= minimum leg move.
Calculation: Switch side and bar = side and current bar of the latest A event, B event, or late flip; fresh switch = current bar is later than that bar and within the flip-retest lookback.
Calculation: Continuation used = reset false on date change, A/B event, late flip, or qualifying new extreme; set true after a C or X marker and false after an E marker. A qualifying new extreme is a high or low beyond the prior continuation extreme by the continuation-reset distance in the active direction.
Calculation: Strong candle(side) = optional same-side candle body AND local impulse >= primary-impulse threshold AND local adverse wick <= maximum-adverse-wick threshold; local impulse is close - lowest low over the MSS lookback for long, or highest high over it - close for short; local adverse wick is close - low for long, or high - close for short.
Calculation: Directional box context(side) = box-context requirement is disabled OR the latest box break matches side and is within the box-context lookback OR close is inside an active box.
Calculation: Box-edge gate(side) = edge-touch requirement is disabled OR an active-box edge is touched within the edge buffer OR a recent matching box break is retested within the edge buffer.
Calculation: No-chase gate(side) = no-chase requirement is disabled OR context-box bounds are absent or invalid OR long close <= context-box lower bound + box height * premium-fraction parameter OR short close >= context-box lower bound + box height * discount-fraction parameter.
Calculation: Family gate(family,tier) = in Beta mode, X and E require their respective enable switches; in Alpha Strict mode, only primary-tier C and D are allowed.
Calculation: Entry location gate(side,family,tier) = directional box context(side) AND box-edge gate(side) AND no-chase gate(side) AND family gate(family,tier).
Calculation: Marker slot(side) = entry markers enabled AND session window AND daily marker count below its limit AND minimum spacing from any prior marker AND minimum same-side spacing from the prior marker.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low > high two bars ago; bearish gap = current high < low two bars ago. Their levels are the midpoint between the gap edges.
Calculation: Armed bullish gap = most recent bullish gap formed in the session window while active direction is long and elapsed session time is between the opening-range duration and gap-arm deadline; armed bearish gap is the corresponding short condition.
Calculation: PD equilibrium, premium, and discount = context-box lower bound plus box height times their respective fraction parameters.
Calculation: PD long location = recent bullish box break within the PD-validity lookback AND close <= PD premium, OR inside-active-box entries are enabled AND close is inside an active box AND close <= PD equilibrium.
Calculation: PD short location = recent bearish box break within the PD-validity lookback AND close >= PD discount, OR inside-active-box entries are enabled AND close is inside an active box AND close >= PD equilibrium.
Calculation: PD long trigger = close > highest high over the PD-trigger lookback excluding the current bar AND (reclaim is disabled OR low <= PD equilibrium); PD short trigger = close < lowest low over that lookback excluding the current bar AND (reclaim is disabled OR high >= PD equilibrium).
Calculation: Base breakdown = active direction is short AND impulse high - low >= minimum trend impulse AND base range <= maximum base range AND close < base low - entry buffer; base breakout is the corresponding long condition using high - impulse low and close > base high + entry buffer.
Calculation: Pullback rejection short = active direction is short AND impulse high - low >= minimum trend impulse AND high - impulse low is within the pullback range AND high >= prior high AND close <= high - max(minimum rejection distance, local adverse wick * adverse-wick fraction); pullback rejection long is the corresponding long condition using impulse high - low, low <= prior low, and close >= low + max(minimum rejection distance, local adverse wick * adverse-wick fraction).
Calculation: Raw bullish box break = close > the upper boundary of an active range box; raw bearish box break = close < its lower boundary.
