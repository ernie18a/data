Calculation: IBD-style score = w1 × (mapped T1 close / mapped T1 close n1 chart bars ago) + w2 × (mapped T1 close / (mapped T1 close n2 chart bars ago × scale2)) + w3 × (mapped T1 close / (mapped T1 close n3 chart bars ago × scale3)) + w4 × (mapped T1 close / (mapped T1 close n4 chart bars ago × scale4)); request T1 close onto chart bars with no lookahead, using the still-forming T1 bar in real time and confirmed T1 values historically; lag terms count chart bars.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = max(current chart-bar high × (1 − trailing-stop percentage), previous bar’s long trailing stop while long; otherwise zero).
Calculation: Short trailing stop = min(current chart-bar low × (1 + trailing-stop percentage), previous bar’s short trailing stop while short; otherwise the inactive ceiling).
Long: ((normal direction mapping with long entries enabled AND RSI(close, RSI length) was at or below the oversold-level parameter within the recent-signal lookback AND SMA(MA source, MA lookback) crosses above VWAP) OR (reversed direction mapping AND RSI(close, RSI length) was at or above the overbought-level parameter within the recent-signal lookback AND SMA(MA source, MA lookback) crosses below VWAP)) AND each enabled trend-template filter passes: close > SMA(close, medium-term lookback) AND close > SMA(close, long-term lookback); SMA(close, medium-term lookback) > SMA(close, long-term lookback); SMA(close, long-term lookback) > its value slope-lookback chart bars ago; SMA(close, short-term lookback) > SMA(close, medium-term lookback) AND SMA(close, short-term lookback) > SMA(close, long-term lookback); close > SMA(close, short-term lookback); (close / lowest(low, low-lookback) − 1) × 100 >= low-rise threshold; (1 − close / highest(high, high-lookback)) × 100 <= high-distance threshold. Enabling the full-template filter requires all seven conditions above AND IBD-style score >= IBD threshold; enabling the separate IBD filter requires IBD-style score >= IBD threshold. AND chart-bar datetime is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive date range AND position is flat or fast-exit is enabled.
Short: ((normal direction mapping with both sides enabled AND RSI(close, RSI length) was at or above the overbought-level parameter within the recent-signal lookback AND SMA(MA source, MA lookback) crosses below VWAP) OR (reversed direction mapping AND RSI(close, RSI length) was at or below the oversold-level parameter within the recent-signal lookback AND SMA(MA source, MA lookback) crosses above VWAP)) AND chart-bar datetime is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive date range AND position is flat or fast-exit is enabled; the short-only selection produces no short entry.
Exit: long, (trailing stop is enabled AND low <= long trailing stop) OR (trailing stop is disabled AND fixed stop is enabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − fixed-stop percentage)) OR (take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)).
Exit: short, (trailing stop is enabled AND high >= short trailing stop) OR (trailing stop is disabled AND fixed stop is enabled AND high >= entry price × (1 + fixed-stop percentage)) OR (take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage)).
