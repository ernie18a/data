Calculation: Chart FVG/VI zones = on chart bars, bullish FVG when low[1] > high[3], bearish FVG when high[1] < low[3], bullish VI when min(open[1], close[1]) > max(open[2], close[2]), and bearish VI when max(open[1], close[1]) < min(open[2], close[2]); require each gap to be at least ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap ratio unless ATR is unavailable; zone midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2; a bullish zone becomes an IFVG after a later close below its bottom, and a bearish zone after a later close above its top.
Calculation: Structure(T) = on T bars, retain pivot highs and lows confirmed using pivot lookback on both sides, replacing each retained swing only when its distance from the prior retained swing is at least ATR(ATR lookback, T) * swing separation factor; set direction bullish when close > retained high and prior direction is not bullish, or bearish when close < retained low and prior direction is not bearish; mark CHoCH only when the new direction opposes the prior direction, and clear the breached swing. Sample each T result on chart bars from the current forming T bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Level context = previous completed T1 high/low and T2 high/low; T1 ATR = ATR(ATR lookback, current forming T1 bar), sampled on chart bars with lookahead off; tolerance = T1 ATR * level proximity factor; near T1 high/low when absolute close distance <= tolerance, or high >= T1 high and close <= T1 high + tolerance, or low <= T1 low and close >= T1 low - tolerance, respectively; near T2 high/low when absolute close distance <= tolerance * T2 proximity factor, respectively; recent T1-high/low sweep when the prior completed T2 high is > T1 high and its close <= T1 high, or the prior completed T2 low is < T1 low and its close >= T1 low, respectively, and the sweep occurred within sweep lookback chart bars.
Calculation: MSS bearish = bearish CHoCH on T3 or T4 AND (recent T1-high sweep OR near T1 high OR near T2 high); MSS bullish = bullish CHoCH on T3 or T4 AND (recent T1-low sweep OR near T1 low OR near T2 low). IFVG near above/below = the nearest IFVG midpoint strictly above/below close is within T1 ATR * IFVG proximity factor.
Calculation: Bias direction = neutral unless previous completed T1 high and low exist and T1 ATR > 0; otherwise select the first matching direction in this order: bearish MSS with IFVG near above; bullish MSS with IFVG near below; bearish MSS; bullish MSS; bearish CHoCH on T3 or T4; bullish CHoCH on T3 or T4; bearish Structure(T3–T6) count >= alignment count; bullish Structure(T3–T6) count >= alignment count; bearish Structure(T3–T6) count > bullish count; bullish Structure(T3–T6) count > bearish count; otherwise neutral. T3–T6 are the four structure frames ordered longest to shortest.
Long: bullish chart FVG/VI zone detected OR Bias direction = bullish
Short: bearish chart FVG/VI zone detected OR Bias direction = bearish
