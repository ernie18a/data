Calculation: Bullish sweep = current chart-bar low is below the lowest low of the sweep lookback across prior completed chart bars and its close is above that low, OR its low is below a previous completed T1 or T2 low and its close is above that low
Calculation: Bearish sweep = current chart-bar high is above the highest high of the sweep lookback across prior completed chart bars and its close is below that high, OR its high is above a previous completed T1 or T2 high and its close is below that high
Calculation: Sweep direction = side of the latest sweep within the setup-context lookback; each new sweep clears the opposite side, a bearish sweep takes precedence when both occur on the same chart bar, and direction is none when no sweep remains recent
Calculation: Bullish displacement = the latest bullish candle with body percentage at least the minimum displacement body percentage and body size at least the minimum displacement size, recorded after a bullish sweep while one remains recent; cleared by a bearish sweep and counts while its age is within the setup-context lookback
Calculation: Bearish displacement = the latest bearish candle with body percentage at least the minimum displacement body percentage and body size at least the minimum displacement size, recorded after a bearish sweep while one remains recent; cleared by a bullish sweep and counts while its age is within the setup-context lookback
Calculation: Bullish FVG = latest chart-bar gap formed after a bullish sweep while one remains recent, with current low above high from the FVG gap offset bars ago and zone bounds at that high and current low; cleared by a bearish sweep and valid while its age is within the FVG-validity lookback and a bullish sweep remains recent
Calculation: Bearish FVG = latest chart-bar gap formed after a bearish sweep while one remains recent, with current high below low from the FVG gap offset bars ago and zone bounds at current high and that low; cleared by a bullish sweep and valid while its age is within the FVG-validity lookback and a bearish sweep remains recent
Calculation: Bullish OTE = close is between H - (OTE high retracement × (H - L)) and H - (OTE low retracement × (H - L)), where H and L are the highest high and lowest low over the Fibonacci-leg lookback including the current chart bar, H > L, and close < open
Calculation: Bearish OTE = close is between L + (OTE low retracement × (H - L)) and L + (OTE high retracement × (H - L)), where H and L are the highest high and lowest low over the Fibonacci-leg lookback including the current chart bar, H > L, and close > open
Calculation: Bullish FVG pullback = a valid bullish FVG exists and the current chart-bar range overlaps its zone OR the prior chart-bar close is below its lower edge and the current close is at or above that edge
Calculation: Bearish FVG pullback = a valid bearish FVG exists and the current chart-bar range overlaps its zone OR the prior chart-bar close is above its upper edge and the current close is at or below that edge
Calculation: Bullish inversion = on chart bars, the latest available T3 close is above the T3 high sampled on the previous chart bar and above the latest available T3 open; T3 values use no lookahead and may be from a still-forming T3 bar in real time
Calculation: Bearish inversion = on chart bars, the latest available T3 close is below the T3 low sampled on the previous chart bar and below the latest available T3 open; T3 values use no lookahead and may be from a still-forming T3 bar in real time
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of eight 1-or-0 tests on the current chart bar: its opening time is within any of the four New York session windows; its range overlaps the level-proximity buffer around any previous completed T1 or T2 high or low; a sweep direction is active; displacement matches the sweep direction; a valid FVG matches the sweep direction; OTE matches the sweep direction; FVG pullback matches the sweep direction; inversion matches the sweep direction
Long: sweep direction is bullish AND confluence score is at least the minimum score AND the chart bar is confirmed
Short: sweep direction is bearish AND confluence score is at least the minimum score AND the chart bar is confirmed
