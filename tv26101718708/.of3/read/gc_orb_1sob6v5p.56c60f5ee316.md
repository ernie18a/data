Calculation: Dealing range = [floor(floor(close) / dealing-range-size parameter) × dealing-range-size parameter, lower bound + dealing-range-size parameter]; range = upper bound − lower bound.
Calculation: Near-GB(tolerance) = abs(close − level) / dealing-range < tolerance / 100 for any level = dealing-range lower bound + dealing-range × configured GB percentage / 100.
Calculation: Calendar state = partition-open event on the scheduled month-specific partition day, or on Monday when that day is one or two calendar days earlier, excluding weekends; calendar GBT active on the scheduled month-specific GBT day, on Friday one or two days before it, or on Monday one or two days after it; chart bars since the partition-open event; and the month-specific partition number and calendar days.
Calculation: Gold clue = first qualifying bullish fair value gap midpoint, else first qualifying bearish fair value gap midpoint, else first qualifying wick midpoint, scanning prior chart bars from nearest to farthest; gap sizes are low[n−2]−high[n] for bullish and abs(low[n]−high[n−2]) for bearish, and wick sizes are high[n]−max(open[n],close[n]) or min(open[n],close[n])−low[n]; each size must be between the month-specific partition number × (1−clue-size-tolerance) and that number × (1+clue-size-tolerance), with upper wick checked before lower wick; search is capped by the clue-search cap; each midpoint is the mean of the two gap edges or the wick edge and body edge.
Calculation: Clue hit = a gold clue exists, its price is nonzero, abs(close − clue price) / clue price < clue-proximity parameter, and calendar GBT is active or bars since partition open equals either configured clue-hit offset; clue hit remains latched until the next partition-open event.
Calculation: IND stage-ready = a clue hit has occurred since the latest partition-open event OR (a gold clue exists AND bars since partition open > max(calendar GBT day − partition-open day, 0) AND calendar GBT is inactive) OR bars since partition open equals either configured stage offset.
Calculation: Stop distance, captured on the entry bar = fixed-point stop parameter, or dealing-range × DR stop-percent parameter / 100, or ATR(stop lookback) × ATR stop multiplier, according to the selected stop method.
Long: Detection mode is IND AND hour(NY time)+minute(NY time) is within the canonical-time-sum tolerance of a configured canonical GBT sum AND Near-GB(IND tolerance) AND NY time is within the IND session AND IND stage-ready AND close > open OR detection mode is DR AND hour(NY time)+minute(NY time) equals a configured 3-multiple GBT sum AND Near-GB(DR tolerance) AND NY time is within the DR session AND Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) is bullish for at least the configured consecutive-bar minimum AND (close − dealing-range lower bound) / dealing-range × 100 < discount boundary.
Short: Detection mode is IND AND hour(NY time)+minute(NY time) is within the canonical-time-sum tolerance of a configured canonical GBT sum AND Near-GB(IND tolerance) AND NY time is within the IND session AND IND stage-ready AND close <= open OR detection mode is DR AND hour(NY time)+minute(NY time) equals a configured 3-multiple GBT sum AND Near-GB(DR tolerance) AND NY time is within the DR session AND Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) is bearish for at least the configured consecutive-bar minimum AND (close − dealing-range lower bound) / dealing-range × 100 > premium boundary.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance × reward:risk parameter.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price − stop distance × reward:risk parameter.
