Calculation: ATR bands = selected price-source EMA basis ± selected ATR × ATR multiplier; the basis is the fast EMA in single-EMA mode or the mean of fast and longer-lookback EMAs in dual-EMA mode; ATR is true ATR or SMA(true range, ATR lookback), as selected.
Calculation: trailing tracker = lower ATR band initially; thereafter, when direction is long, max(current lower ATR band, previous tracker if previous direction was long, otherwise current lower ATR band); when direction is short, min(current upper ATR band, previous tracker if previous direction was short, otherwise current upper ATR band).
Calculation: tracker direction = long initially; thereafter long if selected price source > previous upper ATR band, short if selected price source < previous lower ATR band, otherwise previous direction; calculate on T1 bars using the current bar, including while it is forming, and map values to chart bars; if the selected T1 is below the chart timeframe, use the chart timeframe; when the filter is enabled, calculate the same direction on T2 bars using the current bar, including while it is forming, and map values to chart bars, using T1 if selected T2 is below T1.
Calculation: relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, baseline lookback), or zero when the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: trend-volume delta = 100 × (cumulative up-bar volume minus cumulative down-bar volume) / their sum since the most recent direction change; up bars contribute volume, unchanged bars contribute half volume to each side, down bars contribute volume to the down side, and delta is zero when the sum is zero.
Calculation: ATR distance = abs(selected price source − trailing tracker) / ATR when ATR is positive and the tracker exists, otherwise zero.
Calculation: trend strength = min(ATR distance, distance cap) / distance cap × distance weight + min(abs(trend-volume delta), flow cap) / flow cap × flow weight; multiply by the misaligned-flow factor when flow is not confirmed, then cap at the strength cap.
Calculation: trade-window score = min(relative volume, RVOL cap) / RVOL cap × score cap for instruments classified as open around the clock; otherwise, cap session score plus min(relative volume, RVOL cap) / RVOL cap × volume weight at the score cap. Session score is the overlap score during London–New York overlap or New York cash open; otherwise the London-open score during London open or New York power hour; otherwise the New York pre-open score, New York mid-session score, or default-session score, in that order.
Long: tracker direction = long.
Short: tracker direction = short.
Long: tracker direction changes from short to long AND a new T1 bar is detected AND the T1 bar is not stale under the closed-market age limit AND (the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR T2 tracker direction = long) AND (the window gate is disabled OR trade-window display is disabled OR trade-window score ≥ good-window threshold) AND (the action gate is disabled OR the long setup condition holds) AND long-flip alerts are enabled.
Short: tracker direction changes from long to short AND a new T1 bar is detected AND the T1 bar is not stale under the closed-market age limit AND (the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR T2 tracker direction = short) AND (the window gate is disabled OR trade-window display is disabled OR trade-window score ≥ good-window threshold) AND (the action gate is disabled OR the short setup condition holds) AND short-flip alerts are enabled.
Long: the most recent tracker direction change was to long AND bars since that change ≤ fresh-signal lookback AND the T1 bar is not stale under the closed-market age limit AND relative volume ≥ hot-volume threshold AND (trade-window display is disabled OR trade-window score ≥ good-window threshold) AND ATR distance ≤ maximum setup distance AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) ≤ long RSI limit.
Short: the most recent tracker direction change was to short AND bars since that change ≤ fresh-signal lookback AND the T1 bar is not stale under the closed-market age limit AND relative volume ≥ hot-volume threshold AND (trade-window display is disabled OR trade-window score ≥ good-window threshold) AND ATR distance ≤ maximum setup distance AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) ≥ short RSI limit.
Long: tracker direction = long AND trend-volume delta ≥ zero.
Short: tracker direction = short AND trend-volume delta ≤ zero.
Long: T2 tracker direction = long when the higher-timeframe filter is enabled.
Short: T2 tracker direction = short when the higher-timeframe filter is enabled.
Long: tracker direction = long AND trend strength ≥ hot-strength threshold.
Short: tracker direction = short AND trend strength ≥ hot-strength threshold.
