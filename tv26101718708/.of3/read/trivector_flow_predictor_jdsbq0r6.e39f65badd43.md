Calculation: Source price = selected source, either HLC3 or close.
Calculation: Bar range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); body ratio = abs(close - open) / bar range; bullish close position = (close - low) / bar range; bearish close position = (high - close) / bar range.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = Kaufman efficiency ratio(source price, efficiency lookback); fractal energy = 1 - efficiency ratio.
Calculation: Volatility unit = max(ATR(volatility lookback), volatility estimator weight × SMA(Parkinson volatility, volatility lookback), volatility estimator weight × SMA(Garman-Klass volatility, volatility lookback)) × market-profile volatility factor.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = clamp(volatility unit / SMA(volatility unit, volatility baseline lookback), lower volatility ratio bound, upper volatility ratio bound).
Calculation: Fused core = EMA(weighted sum of ZLEMA(source price, core lookback), DEMA(source price, core lookback), HMA(source price, core lookback), KAMA(source price, efficiency lookback), and two-pole low-pass(source price, core lookback), core smoothing lookback); slow core = EMA(fused core, long core lookback, where the long lookback is twice the core lookback).
Calculation: Pressure = EMA((fused core - fused core[1]) / volatility unit, pressure smoothing lookback); phase = EMA((close - fused core) / volatility unit, phase smoothing lookback); phase velocity = phase - phase[1]; extension = abs(phase).
Calculation: Directional movement = DMI(ADX lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback); ADX rising = ADX rising over its rising lookback.
Calculation: Rail distance factor = input rail distance × ADX adjustment (high-ADX factor above its threshold, low-ADX factor below its threshold, otherwise neutral factor) × efficiency adjustment (high-efficiency factor above its threshold, low-efficiency factor below its threshold, otherwise neutral factor); bullish rail = fused core - volatility unit × rail distance factor; bearish rail = fused core + volatility unit × rail distance factor.
Calculation: Pulse and pulse rail start bullish; when the prior pulse is bullish, a close below the prior rail changes pulse to bearish and rail to the bearish rail, otherwise pulse stays bullish and rail is the greater of the bullish rail and prior rail; when the prior pulse is bearish, a close above the prior rail changes pulse to bullish and rail to the bullish rail, otherwise pulse stays bearish and rail is the lesser of the bearish rail and prior rail. The initial prior rail is the bullish rail.
Calculation: Bullish rail flip = pulse changed from bearish to bullish; bearish rail flip = pulse changed from bullish to bearish.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or neutral ratio when the average is zero; body volatility = abs(close - open) / volatility unit; bullish candle = close > open AND bullish close position >= minimum close position AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio; bearish candle = close < open AND bearish close position >= minimum close position AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio.
Calculation: Delta normalization = clamp(EMA(volume × (close - open) / bar range, volume lookback) / SMA(volume, volume lookback), negative delta cap, positive delta cap), or zero when the volume average is zero.
Calculation: Noise floor passes when the noise filter is disabled OR volatility unit >= SMA(bar range, noise lookback) × noise factor OR body volatility >= minimum displacement × noise displacement factor.
Calculation: Confirmed bar passes when close confirmation is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed.
Calculation: Compression range high = prior bar's highest(high, ignition range lookback); compression range low = prior bar's lowest(low, ignition range lookback); compression ratio = (range high - range low) / volatility unit; compressed = compression ratio <= maximum compression width OR efficiency ratio < compression efficiency threshold; compressed previously = the same condition on the prior bar.
Calculation: Sweep high = prior bar's highest(high, sweep lookback); sweep low = prior bar's lowest(low, sweep lookback).
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND body volatility >= minimum displacement AND bullish close position >= displacement close-position threshold; bearish displacement = close < open AND body volatility >= minimum displacement AND bearish close position >= displacement close-position threshold.
Calculation: Bullish FVG event = FVG signal enabled AND low > high[2] AND body volatility >= minimum displacement × FVG displacement factor AND close > open; bearish FVG event = FVG signal enabled AND high < low[2] AND body volatility >= minimum displacement × FVG displacement factor AND close < open.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high and swing low = pivot high and pivot low using the structure pivot lookback on both sides; retain the latest confirmed levels and the prior levels used for current-bar break checks.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = structure signal enabled AND a prior confirmed swing high exists AND close > prior swing high + volatility unit × breakout buffer AND close[1] <= prior swing high; bearish structure break = structure signal enabled AND a prior confirmed swing low exists AND close < prior swing low - volatility unit × breakout buffer AND close[1] >= prior swing low.
Calculation: On a bullish structure break, retain the broken swing high and break bar; on a bearish structure break, retain the broken swing low and break bar. Bullish retest = retest signal enabled AND a retained bullish break is within retest memory AND low <= broken high + volatility unit × retest tolerance AND close > broken high AND close > open. Bearish retest = retest signal enabled AND a retained bearish break is within retest memory AND high >= broken low - volatility unit × retest tolerance AND close < broken low AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish pulse event = lowest(phase, setup lookback) < negative pullback phase AND phase crosses above zero AND close > fused core AND phase velocity > zero; bearish pulse event = highest(phase, setup lookback) > pullback phase AND phase crosses below zero AND close < fused core AND phase velocity < zero.
Calculation: Bullish ignition event = close > compression range high + volatility unit × breakout buffer AND close[1] <= compression range high AND compressed previously AND bullish displacement AND phase velocity > zero; bearish ignition event = close < compression range low - volatility unit × breakout buffer AND close[1] >= compression range low AND compressed previously AND bearish displacement AND phase velocity < zero.
Calculation: Bullish sweep event = low < sweep low - volatility unit × sweep buffer AND close > sweep low AND bullish candle AND phase velocity > zero; bearish sweep event = high > sweep high + volatility unit × sweep buffer AND close < sweep high AND bearish candle AND phase velocity < zero.
Calculation: Bullish flip event = rail-flip signal enabled AND bullish rail flip AND close > fused core AND phase velocity > zero AND bullish close position >= rail-flip close-position threshold; bearish flip event = rail-flip signal enabled AND bearish rail flip AND close < fused core AND phase velocity < zero AND bearish close position >= rail-flip close-position threshold.
Calculation: Bullish structure event = bullish structure break AND close > fused core AND phase velocity > zero; bearish structure event = bearish structure break AND close < fused core AND phase velocity < zero; bullish FVG event confirmation = bullish FVG event AND close > fused core AND phase velocity > zero; bearish FVG event confirmation = bearish FVG event AND close < fused core AND phase velocity < zero; bullish retest event = bullish retest AND close > fused core AND phase velocity >= zero; bearish retest event = bearish retest AND close < fused core AND phase velocity <= zero.
Calculation: Apply the late-entry filter to pulse, ignition, sweep, structure, FVG, and retest events using their respective maximum-extension parameters; apply its separate rail-flip maximum-extension parameter to flip events. When the late-entry filter is disabled, pass these events without an extension restriction.
Calculation: Raw bullish event = any late-filtered bullish pulse, ignition, sweep, flip, structure, FVG, or retest event; raw bearish event = any corresponding bearish event.
Calculation: For T1 and T2, calculate close and EMA(close, MTF EMA lookback) on bars of that timeframe, using its still-forming bar in real time, and sample those values on chart bars. Bullish MTF agreement counts closes above their respective EMAs; bearish MTF agreement counts closes below them.
Calculation: EMA bias points are full EMA points when close is above EMA(close, EMA bias lookback), partial EMA points when not above it but above EMA(close, fast EMA lookback), and zero otherwise; short EMA points use the corresponding below comparisons.
Calculation: Long core points = full core points when fused core > slow core AND pressure > zero, partial core points when pressure > zero, and zero otherwise; short core points use fused core < slow core and pressure < zero.
Calculation: Long rail points = rail points when pulse is bullish, otherwise zero; short rail points = rail points when pulse is bearish, otherwise zero. Long DI points = DI points when DI+ > DI-, otherwise zero; short DI points = DI points when DI- > DI+, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Long MTF points = bullish MTF agreement × MTF points per agreement when the MTF stack is enabled, otherwise neutral MTF points; short MTF points use bearish MTF agreement and the same parameters.
Calculation: Long event points select the first matching event in this order: ignition, structure, FVG, retest, pulse, sweep, flip; short event points use the same priority. Each event type has its own points parameter.
Calculation: Long phase points = full phase points when phase > zero AND phase velocity > zero, partial phase points when phase velocity > zero, otherwise zero; short phase points use phase < zero AND phase velocity < zero, then phase velocity < zero, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Long candle points use the higher candle-quality points when bullish close position >= the higher close-position threshold, the lower points when >= the lower threshold, otherwise zero; short candle points apply the same tiers to bearish close position.
Calculation: Long displacement points = full displacement points on bullish displacement, partial displacement points when body volatility >= the reduced displacement threshold AND close > open, otherwise zero; short displacement points use bearish displacement and close < open.
Calculation: Long delta points use the higher points when delta normalization exceeds the higher positive threshold, the lower points when it exceeds the lower positive threshold, otherwise zero; short delta points use the corresponding negative thresholds.
Calculation: ADX points = clamp((ADX - ADX score floor) / ADX score range × maximum ADX points, zero, maximum ADX points); ADX rising points are awarded when ADX is rising. Efficiency points = clamp(efficiency ratio × efficiency score weight, zero, maximum efficiency points); fractal points = clamp((1 - fractal energy) × fractal score weight, zero, maximum fractal points).
Calculation: Volume points are full volume points when the volume filter is disabled or volume ratio >= minimum volume ratio, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Extension points use the highest, middle, or lowest extension points when extension is within the respective near, middle, or far extension threshold, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Expansion state = volatility ratio > expansion threshold AND ADX rising; expansion points are awarded in that state. Chop state = efficiency ratio < minimum efficiency OR (ADX < chop ADX threshold AND fractal energy > chop fractal threshold); impulse state = ADX >= impulse threshold AND efficiency ratio >= impulse efficiency threshold AND NOT compressed; reversal risk = extension > reversal extension threshold AND fractal energy > reversal fractal threshold; compression state = compressed AND volatility ratio < compression volatility threshold.
Calculation: Regime penalty = chop penalty when chop state is true, else fractal penalty when fractal energy > high-fractal threshold, else zero.
Calculation: Long score and short score are each the clamped sum of their EMA, core, rail, DI, MTF, event, phase, candle, displacement, delta, ADX, ADX-rising, efficiency, fractal, volume, and extension points, plus expansion points, minus regime penalty.
Calculation: Base long score and base short score are each the clamped sum of their EMA, core, rail, DI, MTF, phase, candle, delta, ADX, efficiency, fractal, volume, and extension points, minus regime penalty.
Calculation: Dynamic minimum score = clamp(effective minimum score + chop score adjustment when chop state is true - impulse score adjustment when impulse state is true + compression score adjustment when compression state is true, dynamic score floor, dynamic score ceiling).
Calculation: Bias becomes bullish when base long score >= base short score + score dominance threshold; otherwise it becomes bearish when base short score >= base long score + score dominance threshold; otherwise it retains its prior value, initially neutral.
Calculation: A hard EMA filter passes long when disabled or close > EMA(close, EMA bias lookback), and passes short when disabled or close < that EMA. A VWAP filter passes long when disabled or close > VWAP(hlc3), and passes short when disabled or close < VWAP(hlc3); when VWAP is unavailable, use the EMA bias value.
Calculation: MTF filter passes long when the stack is disabled or bullish MTF agreement >= required MTF agreement; it passes short when disabled or bearish MTF agreement >= required MTF agreement.
Calculation: Volume filter passes when disabled or volume ratio >= minimum volume ratio. Candle filter passes long when disabled, bullish candle, or a bullish ignition, flip, structure, FVG, or retest event; it passes short under the corresponding bearish conditions.
Calculation: Efficiency filter passes when efficiency ratio >= minimum efficiency OR either raw directional event exists. Prediction filter passes long when disabled OR (prediction confidence >= minimum prediction confidence AND prediction side is not bearish) OR bullish sweep event; it passes short when disabled OR (prediction confidence >= minimum prediction confidence AND prediction side is not bullish) OR bearish sweep event.
Calculation: Base long filter = confirmed bar AND long EMA filter AND long VWAP filter AND long MTF filter AND volume filter AND long candle filter AND efficiency filter AND long prediction filter AND noise floor; base short filter uses the corresponding short filters.
Calculation: Long regression value = linear regression(source price, regression lookback, current offset); prior regression value uses the prior offset; regression slope = their difference; regression quality = clamp(1 - stdev(source price - regression value, regression lookback) / stdev(source price, regression lookback), zero, one), or zero when the denominator is zero; regression vector = clamp(regression slope / volatility unit × regression scaling factor, negative one, one).
Calculation: Score dominance vector = clamp((long score - short score) / score scale, negative one, one); phase vector = clamp(phase velocity × phase-velocity weight + phase × phase weight, negative one, one); pressure vector = clamp(pressure × pressure weight, negative one, one); DI vector = clamp((DI+ - DI-) / DI scale, negative one, one).
Calculation: Rail vector = positive rail-vector value for bullish pulse and negative rail-vector value for bearish pulse; MTF vector = clamp((bullish MTF agreement - bearish MTF agreement) / MTF agreement scale × MTF vector weight, negative MTF vector cap, positive MTF vector cap) when the stack is enabled, otherwise zero; delta vector = clamp(delta normalization × delta vector weight, negative delta vector cap, positive delta vector cap); regression-quality vector = regression vector × (base regression-quality weight + regression quality × regression-quality weight).
Calculation: Flow vector = clamp(weighted sum of score dominance, phase, pressure, DI, regression-quality, rail, MTF, and delta vectors, negative one, one), using a separate flow weight for each vector.
Calculation: Prediction regime multiplier depends on impulse, compression, and chop states; reversal multiplier depends on reversal risk; quality multiplier = base quality multiplier + regression quality × quality weight; horizon scale = square root of prediction horizon / base horizon; predicted price = close + volatility unit × flow vector × horizon scale × prediction strength × regime multiplier × reversal multiplier × quality multiplier.
Calculation: Prediction side is bullish when predicted price > close, bearish when predicted price < close, and neutral otherwise. Prediction confidence = clamp(abs(flow vector) × confidence scale + regression quality × regression confidence weight + impulse confidence adjustment when impulse state is true - chop confidence adjustment when chop state is true, zero, one hundred).
Calculation: Major long candidate = cooldown since the last major signal AND absolute close movement since its close >= volatility unit × minimum move since signal AND (same-direction signals allowed OR last major side was not long) AND base long filter AND raw bullish event AND long score >= dynamic minimum score AND long score >= short score + score dominance threshold. Major short candidate uses corresponding short conditions.
Calculation: A major long signal occurs when the major long candidate is true AND (the major short candidate is false OR long score > short score); a major short signal occurs when the major short candidate is true AND (the major long candidate is false OR short score > long score). Store the signal bar, close, and side after either signal; require the configured minimum bars between major signals.
Calculation: TP1 reached during a long position when any high since entry >= long TP1; TP1 reached during a short position when any low since entry <= short TP1. Initial long stop = min(pulse rail, entry price - volatility unit × stop-distance parameter); initial short stop = max(pulse rail, entry price + volatility unit × stop-distance parameter). Initial risk is max(abs(entry price - initial stop), volatility unit × stop-distance parameter); TP1 and TP2 are entry price plus risk × their respective reward multiple for longs and minus risk × the respective multiple for shorts.
Calculation: Active long stop starts at the initial long stop; when trailing invalidation is enabled, update it to max(previous stop, min(pulse rail, close - volatility unit × trailing buffer)); when break-even-after-TP1 is enabled and TP1 has been reached, update it to max(stop, entry price). Active short stop starts at the initial short stop; when trailing is enabled, update it to min(previous stop, max(pulse rail, close + volatility unit × trailing buffer)); when break-even-after-TP1 is enabled and TP1 has been reached, update it to min(stop, entry price).
Calculation: Long confidence loss = long position AND confidence exit enabled AND long score < confidence exit threshold AND short score > long score; short confidence loss = short position AND confidence exit enabled AND short score < confidence exit threshold AND long score > short score.
Calculation: Long stop hit = long position AND close <= active long stop; short stop hit = short position AND close >= active short stop. Long TP2 hit = long position AND close >= long TP2; short TP2 hit = short position AND close <= short TP2.
Long: major long signal AND position side is flat or short.
Short: major short signal AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND (long stop hit OR long confidence loss OR (TP2 exit enabled AND long TP2 hit)).
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND (short stop hit OR short confidence loss OR (TP2 exit enabled AND short TP2 hit)).
Long: bullish sweep event AND (late-entry filter disabled OR extension <= maximum sweep extension).
Short: bearish sweep event AND (late-entry filter disabled OR extension <= maximum sweep extension).
Long: prior bias is bullish AND base long score < base short score + score dominance threshold AND base short score < base long score + score dominance threshold.
Short: prior bias is bearish AND base short score < base long score + score dominance threshold AND base long score < base short score + score dominance threshold.
Calculation: Bull exhaustion = bullish bias AND extension > exhaustion extension threshold AND RSI(source price, exhaustion lookback) > overbought threshold AND bullish close position < exhaustion close-position threshold; bear exhaustion = bearish bias AND extension > exhaustion extension threshold AND RSI(source price, exhaustion lookback) < oversold threshold AND bearish close position < exhaustion close-position threshold.
Short: bull exhaustion.
Long: bear exhaustion.

When enabled, initialize it from current prediction side and refresh it on an entry or exit signal, a non-neutral direction flip meeting the confidence threshold, a confidence change meeting its threshold, or a confirmed bar reaching the refresh interval or forecast horizon; displayed prediction side is the stored side between refreshes.
