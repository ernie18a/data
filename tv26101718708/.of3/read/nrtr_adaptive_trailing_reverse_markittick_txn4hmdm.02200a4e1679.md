Calculation: Adaptive source = close, SMA(close, adaptive lookback), EMA(close, adaptive lookback), RMA(close, adaptive lookback), WMA(WMA(close, adaptive lookback), adaptive lookback), VWMA(VWMA(VWMA(close, adaptive lookback), adaptive lookback), adaptive lookback), HMA(close, adaptive lookback), SMA(close, adaptive lookback) + (close - close[adaptive lookback]) / adaptive lookback × (adaptive lookback / 2), or a recursive Kalman estimate of close with process noise 1 / adaptive lookback, measurement noise 1, initial estimate close, and initial error 1, selected by the adaptive filter method
Calculation: NRTR = on chart bars, offset is adaptive source[1] × percent offset / 100 in percent mode or ATR(ATR lookback)[1] × ATR multiple in ATR mode; initialize trend up, extreme to adaptive source, and NRTR to adaptive source minus offset; in an uptrend, extreme = max(previous extreme, adaptive source[1]) and NRTR = max(previous NRTR, extreme − offset), flipping down when adaptive source[1] < NRTR and resetting extreme to adaptive source[1] and NRTR to extreme + offset; in a downtrend, extreme = min(previous extreme, adaptive source[1]) and NRTR = min(previous NRTR, extreme + offset), flipping up when adaptive source[1] > NRTR and resetting extreme to adaptive source[1] and NRTR to extreme − offset
Calculation: Stop loss = NRTR level on the signal bar
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, NRTR trend flips from down to up AND (ADX(ADX lookback, same lookback for DI and ADX smoothing)[1] >= ADX threshold when the ADX filter is enabled) AND (no long stop loss has been hit during the cooldown lookback when the cooldown guard is enabled)
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, NRTR trend flips from up to down AND (ADX(ADX lookback, same lookback for DI and ADX smoothing)[1] >= ADX threshold when the ADX filter is enabled) AND (no short stop loss has been hit during the cooldown lookback when the cooldown guard is enabled)
Exit: long, low <= stop loss OR high >= entry price + abs(entry price - stop loss) × TP3 multiple
Exit: short, high >= stop loss OR low <= entry price - abs(entry price - stop loss) × TP3 multiple
