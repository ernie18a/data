Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR(fast regime lookback) / ATR(slow baseline lookback) when the slow ATR is nonzero; otherwise 1.
Calculation: Entry offset = zero when the dynamic offset is disabled; otherwise the selected low, normal, or high volatility offset converted from pipettes to price, using the ratio at the entry signal bar (high when the ratio meets or exceeds the high threshold; otherwise low when the ratio is at or below the low threshold; otherwise normal).
Calculation: Active swing lookback = high volatility swing lookback when the ratio meets or exceeds the high threshold; otherwise normal/low volatility swing lookback, using the ratio on the core setup bar.
Calculation: Long setup stop = lowest low over the active swing lookback on the most recent qualifying long core setup bar.
Calculation: Short setup stop = highest high over the active swing lookback on the most recent qualifying short core setup bar.
Calculation: Long initial risk = entry signal bar close + entry offset − long setup stop.
Calculation: Short initial risk = short setup stop − (entry signal bar close − entry offset).
Calculation: Active reward target multiple = high volatility target when the ratio meets or exceeds the high threshold; otherwise low volatility target when the ratio is at or below the low threshold; otherwise normal target, using the ratio on the entry signal bar.
Long: No open position AND (lag confirmation disabled AND the current bar satisfies the long core setup conditions below OR lag confirmation enabled AND the most recent qualifying long core setup bar is within the lag window, no later short core setup has occurred, no out-of-session bar has occurred since it, and the position has remained flat) AND STC(close, STC length, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, smoothing factor) > STC(close, STC length, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, smoothing factor)[1] AND long initial risk >= minimum stop distance; long core setup conditions: close crosses above UT Bot trailing stop(close, UT sensitivity, UT ATR lookback) AND STC(...) <= lower long-zone threshold AND (session filter disabled OR setup bar is within the configured session) AND (range floor filter disabled OR high − low >= ATR(range baseline lookback) × minimum range percentage) AND (range ceiling filter disabled OR high − low <= ATR(range baseline lookback) × maximum range percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR EMA(volume, shorter volume lookback) > EMA(volume, longer volume lookback) AND EMA(volume, longer volume lookback) != 0) AND (wick filter disabled OR high = low OR upper wick as a percentage of high − low <= maximum counter-wick percentage) AND (velocity filter disabled OR EMA(close, velocity baseline lookback) − EMA(close, velocity baseline lookback)[velocity evaluation window] >= −maximum allowed slope) AND (session filter disabled OR entry signal bar is within the configured session); when lag confirmation is disabled, use the current bar's long core setup conditions.
Short: No open position AND (lag confirmation disabled AND the current bar satisfies the short core setup conditions below OR lag confirmation enabled AND the most recent qualifying short core setup bar is within the lag window, no later long core setup has occurred, no out-of-session bar has occurred since it, and the position has remained flat) AND STC(close, STC length, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, smoothing factor) < STC(close, STC length, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, smoothing factor)[1] AND short initial risk >= minimum stop distance; short core setup conditions: close crosses below UT Bot trailing stop(close, UT sensitivity, UT ATR lookback) AND STC(...) >= upper short-zone threshold AND (session filter disabled OR setup bar is within the configured session) AND (range floor filter disabled OR high − low >= ATR(range baseline lookback) × minimum range percentage) AND (range ceiling filter disabled OR high − low <= ATR(range baseline lookback) × maximum range percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR EMA(volume, shorter volume lookback) > EMA(volume, longer volume lookback) AND EMA(volume, longer volume lookback) != 0) AND (wick filter disabled OR high = low OR lower wick as a percentage of high − low <= maximum counter-wick percentage) AND (velocity filter disabled OR EMA(close, velocity baseline lookback) − EMA(close, velocity baseline lookback)[velocity evaluation window] <= maximum allowed slope) AND (session filter disabled OR entry signal bar is within the configured session); when lag confirmation is disabled, use the current bar's short core setup conditions.
Exit: Long, low <= (entry price if breakeven is enabled and the highest high since entry >= entry price + long initial risk × breakeven milestone; otherwise long setup stop) OR high >= entry signal bar close + entry offset + long initial risk × active reward target multiple OR (partial take profit is enabled AND no partial reduction has occurred AND, on a bar after entry, high >= entry signal bar close + entry offset + long initial risk × partial target milestone).
Exit: Short, high >= (entry price if breakeven is enabled and the lowest low since entry <= entry price − short initial risk × breakeven milestone; otherwise short setup stop) OR low <= entry signal bar close − entry offset − short initial risk × active reward target multiple OR (partial take profit is enabled AND no partial reduction has occurred AND, on a bar after entry, low <= entry signal bar close − entry offset − short initial risk × partial target milestone).
