Calculation: RS rating = round(clamp(base + score multiplier × (recent weight × recent relative return + older weight × older relative return) + momentum boost, score floor, score ceiling)); recent horizon = round(RS lookback × recent fraction), shorter than the RS lookback; each relative return is the stock return minus the RS benchmark return, multiplied by the percent scale; recent returns use close / close[recent horizon] − 1, and older returns use close[recent horizon] / close[RS lookback] − 1; momentum boost is +boost size, −boost size, or zero according as the stock-minus-benchmark return over the shorter boost lookback is greater than, less than, or equal to that over the longer boost lookback. Stock and benchmark closes are T1 closes sampled onto chart bars, using the developing T1 bar in realtime; offsets are measured in chart bars, and the RS lookback, though specified in days, is applied as chart bars. The rating is unavailable when required close values are unavailable.
Calculation: RS trend delta = current RS rating − RS rating at the shorter trend lookback ago, measured in chart bars.
Long: (current T1 close − last completed T1 close) / last completed T1 close > zero-change threshold, with both closes available and the prior close nonzero OR (current broad-market reference T1 close − last completed broad-market reference T1 close) / last completed broad-market reference T1 close > zero-change threshold, with both closes available and the prior close nonzero OR round(new-highs T1 close / (new-highs T1 close + new-lows T1 close) × percent scale) ≥ breadth upper threshold, with both closes available and their sum positive OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) ≥ RSI upper threshold OR RS rating ≥ RS upper threshold OR RS trend delta > strong trend threshold OR (RS trend delta > upward trend threshold AND RS rating at the longer trend lookback ago is available). T1 closes use the developing T1 bar in realtime; the RSI runs on chart bars and uses the current chart bar.
Short: (current T1 close − last completed T1 close) / last completed T1 close < zero-change threshold, with both closes available and the prior close nonzero OR (current broad-market reference T1 close − last completed broad-market reference T1 close) / last completed broad-market reference T1 close < zero-change threshold, with both closes available and the prior close nonzero OR round(new-highs T1 close / (new-highs T1 close + new-lows T1 close) × percent scale) ≤ breadth lower threshold, with both closes available and their sum positive OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) ≤ RSI lower threshold OR RS rating ≤ RS lower threshold OR RS trend delta < negative strong trend threshold OR (RS trend delta < negative downward trend threshold AND RS rating at the longer trend lookback ago is available). T1 closes use the developing T1 bar in realtime; the RSI runs on chart bars and uses the current chart bar.
