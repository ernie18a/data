Calculation: Channel width = current close times the configured width percentage, or ATR(channel lookback, T1 current bar sampled on chart bars) times the configured ATR multiplier, or a fixed price distance, according to the selected mode
Calculation: Channel center = EMA(source, lookback, T1, configured offset; sampled on chart bars with lookahead off; current-bar offset uses the still-forming T1 bar and a prior-bar offset uses a completed T1 bar)
Calculation: Upper channel = channel center + channel width / two
Calculation: Lower channel = channel center - channel width / two
Calculation: Minimum engulf body = current close times the configured percentage, or ATR(lookback, T3 chart bars) times the configured multiplier, or a fixed price distance, according to the selected mode
Calculation: Maximum engulf body = current close times its separately configured percentage, or ATR(lookback, T3 chart bars) times its separately configured multiplier, or a separately configured fixed price distance, according to the selected mode
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle closes above its open; prior candle is bearish or is bullish with body no larger than minimum engulf body times the configured prior-body fraction; current body is between the minimum and maximum engulf bodies; and, in body-only mode, current open is no higher than prior close plus the configured gap allowance in price ticks and current close is above prior open if the prior candle is bearish, otherwise prior high; in full-range mode, current low is below prior low and current high is above prior high
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle closes below its open; prior candle is bullish or is bearish with body no larger than minimum engulf body times the configured prior-body fraction; current body is between the minimum and maximum engulf bodies; and, in body-only mode, current open is no lower than prior close minus the configured gap allowance in price ticks and current close is below prior open if the prior candle is bullish, otherwise prior low; in full-range mode, current low is below prior low and current high is above prior high
Calculation: Pin bar = candle range is at least minimum engulf body times the configured pin-range fraction; body divided by range is at most the configured maximum; longer wick divided by range is at least the configured minimum; and longer wick divided by body is at least the configured ratio; bullish when lower wick is longer, bearish otherwise
Calculation: Bullish pin-and-engulf setup = the prior candle is a bullish pin bar; its low is below the lowest low over the configured sweep lookback window ending before it; current candle closes above its open and above prior high; and current body is between minimum engulf body times the configured pin-range fraction and maximum engulf body
Calculation: Bearish pin-and-engulf setup = the prior candle is a bearish pin bar; its high is above the highest high over the configured sweep lookback window ending before it; current candle closes below its open and below prior low; and current body is between minimum engulf body times the configured pin-range fraction and maximum engulf body
Calculation: Channel bias = bullish when (prior close > upper channel AND current close > upper channel) changes from false to true; bearish when (prior close < lower channel AND current close < lower channel) changes from false to true; otherwise retain the prior bias, initially neutral
Calculation: Squeeze value = linear regression of close minus average(average(highest high, lowest low over the KC lookback), SMA(close, KC lookback)), using the KC lookback and current endpoint
Calculation: Session trade count = primary positions opened since the latest session reset; reset when a chart bar crosses a NY, Asia, or London session start if T3 is at or below T4, otherwise reset at the chart session's first bar
Calculation: Timed close = day-end closing enabled AND T3 at or below T4 AND a chart bar spans the configured NY end time shifted earlier by the chart-bar duration and configured end-time adjustment; OR week-end closing enabled AND T3 at or below T5 AND the NY date is Friday AND a chart bar spans that shifted end time
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry-bar low minus price-tick size times the configured buffer, or lowest low over the configured swing lookback minus buffer, or lower channel minus buffer, or entry price minus configured fixed distance, according to the selected stop mode
Calculation: Initial short stop = entry-bar high plus price-tick size times the configured buffer, or highest high over the separately configured swing lookback plus buffer, or upper channel plus buffer, or entry price plus configured fixed distance, according to the selected stop mode
Calculation: Main target for each entry = entry price plus (entry price minus its initial long stop) times that entry's main target multiple for longs; entry price minus (its initial short stop minus entry price) times that entry's separately configured main target multiple for shorts
Calculation: First target for each entry = entry price plus (entry price minus its initial long stop) times the configured first-target multiple for longs; entry price minus (its initial short stop minus entry price) times the configured first-target multiple for shorts
Calculation: Active long stop = initial long stop until break-even is enabled and the high reaches first target while that entry's main target multiple exceeds the first-target multiple; then entry price
Calculation: Active short stop = initial short stop until break-even is enabled and the low reaches first target while that entry's main target multiple exceeds the first-target multiple; then entry price
Long: chart timeframe equals T3 AND T1 is at least T3 AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter is disabled OR T2 is at least T3) AND channel bias is bullish AND open is within the channel boundaries inclusive AND (bullish engulfing OR bullish pin-and-engulf setup) AND session trade count is below its configured maximum AND not the timed-close bar AND (Friday trading is enabled OR the NY date is not Friday) AND (no-trade window is disabled OR T3 is above T4 OR the NY chart-bar time is outside the configured window whose endpoints are shifted earlier by the configured chart-bar offset) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, lookback) is available and at or below its configured overbought threshold) AND (lower-timeframe EMA filter is disabled OR close is above EMA(source, lookback, T3 chart bars, configured offset) OR bullish pin-and-engulf setup) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter is disabled OR close is above EMA(source, lookback, T2, configured offset; sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using the still-forming T2 bar at current-bar offset and a completed T2 bar at a prior-bar offset)) AND (squeeze filter is disabled OR squeeze value is at or below its configured sign threshold) AND (Bollinger filter is disabled OR Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation multiplier) is at least its configured width threshold)
Short: chart timeframe equals T3 AND T1 is at least T3 AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter is disabled OR T2 is at least T3) AND channel bias is bearish AND open is within the channel boundaries inclusive AND (bearish engulfing OR bearish pin-and-engulf setup) AND session trade count is below its configured maximum AND not the timed-close bar AND (Friday trading is enabled OR the NY date is not Friday) AND (no-trade window is disabled OR T3 is above T4 OR the NY chart-bar time is outside the configured window whose endpoints are shifted earlier by the configured chart-bar offset) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, lookback) is available and at or above its configured oversold threshold) AND (lower-timeframe EMA filter is disabled OR close is below EMA(source, lookback, T3 chart bars, configured offset) OR bearish pin-and-engulf setup) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter is disabled OR close is below EMA(source, lookback, T2, configured offset; sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using the still-forming T2 bar at current-bar offset and a completed T2 bar at a prior-bar offset)) AND (squeeze filter is disabled OR squeeze value is at or above its configured sign threshold) AND (Bollinger filter is disabled OR Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation multiplier) is at least its configured width threshold)
Exit: long, low is at or below the active long stop OR high is at or above the main target OR (partial take profit is enabled AND the entry's main target multiple exceeds the first-target multiple AND high is at or above the first target) OR timed close
Exit: short, high is at or above the active short stop OR low is at or below the main target OR (partial take profit is enabled AND the entry's main target multiple exceeds the first-target multiple AND low is at or below the first target) OR timed close
