Calculation: Accumulation range = highest high and lowest low over the accumulation lookback, stored when accumulation begins and widened to include a bar that breaks both range boundaries or fails the sweep wick or volume test.
Calculation: Structure-break level = for long, the selected upper boundary, midpoint, or lower boundary of the stored range; for short, the selected lower boundary, midpoint, or upper boundary, according to the structure-break mode.
Long: After a stored accumulation range, low < range low − manipulation ATR multiple × selected ATR, high ≤ range high + manipulation ATR multiple × selected ATR, (close − low) / max(high − low, range floor) ≥ recovery-wick ratio, and volume confirmation is off or spike-bar volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback); within the structure-break timeout after that sweep, close > structure-break level; at least one bar after that break, a bullish FVG forms with low > high[2] and low − high[2] ≥ minimum-gap ATR multiple × selected ATR, with displacement requirement off or (close > open and abs(close − open) / max(high − low, range floor) ≥ displacement ratio) or (close[1] > open[1] and abs(close[1] − open[1]) / max(high[1] − low[1], range floor) ≥ displacement ratio); selected ATR is ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars or ATR(ATR lookback, T2, current T2 bar) sampled on chart bars, with the T2 bar still forming; use the latest qualifying bullish gap zone, replacing it before a signal only when a qualifying bullish gap is wider than the active zone; and either a qualifying zone is created or replaced on this bar, or the first qualifying entry occurs at least one and no more than the retest-wait parameter bars after the active zone was created, with retest mode selected and close[1] ≥ zone bottom and low ≤ zone top and close ≥ zone bottom, or break mode selected and the zone has been touched (low ≤ zone top) and close > zone top; trend filter is off or current T1 close > EMA(close, short lookback, T1, current T1 bar) and EMA(close, intermediate lookback, T1, current T1 bar) and EMA(close, long lookback, T1, current T1 bar), sampled on chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar; VWAP filter is off or close > VWAP(hlc3) and zone bottom > VWAP(hlc3).
Short: After a stored accumulation range, high > range high + manipulation ATR multiple × selected ATR, low ≥ range low − manipulation ATR multiple × selected ATR, (high − close) / max(high − low, range floor) ≥ recovery-wick ratio, and volume confirmation is off or spike-bar volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback); within the structure-break timeout after that sweep, close < structure-break level; at least one bar after that break, a bearish FVG forms with high < low[2] and low[2] − high ≥ minimum-gap ATR multiple × selected ATR, with displacement requirement off or (close < open and abs(close − open) / max(high − low, range floor) ≥ displacement ratio) or (close[1] < open[1] and abs(close[1] − open[1]) / max(high[1] − low[1], range floor) ≥ displacement ratio); selected ATR is ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars or ATR(ATR lookback, T2, current T2 bar) sampled on chart bars, with the T2 bar still forming; use the latest qualifying bearish gap zone, replacing it before a signal only when a qualifying bearish gap is wider than the active zone; and either a qualifying zone is created or replaced on this bar, or the first qualifying entry occurs at least one and no more than the retest-wait parameter bars after the active zone was created, with retest mode selected and close[1] ≤ zone top and high ≥ zone bottom and close ≤ zone top, or break mode selected and the zone has been touched (high ≥ zone bottom) and close < zone bottom; trend filter is off or current T1 close < EMA(close, short lookback, T1, current T1 bar) and EMA(close, intermediate lookback, T1, current T1 bar) and EMA(close, long lookback, T1, current T1 bar), sampled on chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar; VWAP filter is off or close < VWAP(hlc3) and zone top < VWAP(hlc3).
