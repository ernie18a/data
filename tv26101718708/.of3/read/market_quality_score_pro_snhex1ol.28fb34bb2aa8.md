Calculation: Relative-strength score = on chart bars, calculate from current T1 close and benchmark close values (the T1 bar forms in real time; historical T1 values are confirmed): percent-rank over the long lookback of relative return from the recent-skip lag to the long lag, relative return over the medium lookback, relative return over the shorter lookback, current close / Highest(T1 close, long lookback), and the percentage change in the relative-strength line over its slope lookback; relative return is instrument return minus benchmark return when a benchmark applies, otherwise instrument return, and the relative-strength line is close / benchmark close when a benchmark applies, otherwise close; combine available ranks by their separately configured weights, renormalized over available ranks, then apply EMA with the relative-strength smoothing length for indices only
Calculation: Earnings-quality score = for equities, the available weighted mean of clamp01((clamp(EPS year-over-year growth, EPS lower cap, EPS upper cap) - EPS lower cap) / (EPS upper cap - EPS lower cap)), clamp01((clamp(revenue year-over-year growth, revenue lower cap, revenue upper cap) - revenue lower cap) / (revenue upper cap - revenue lower cap)), and clamp01(clamp(ROE, ROE lower cap, ROE upper cap) / full-score ROE); use quarterly revenue growth when available, otherwise trailing-twelve-month revenue growth; use trailing-twelve-month ROE when available, otherwise fiscal-year ROE, otherwise trailing-twelve-month net income / latest quarterly equity when equity is positive; combine available metrics by their separately configured weights, renormalized over available metrics
Calculation: Drawdown state = on chart bars using current T1 close values, drawdown = (close / Highest(T1 close, drawdown lookback) - 1) as a percentage; ATR percentage = ATR(T1, ATR lookback) / T1 close as a percentage, with ATR calculated on T1 bars using the current T1 bar; drawdown threshold = min(maximum drawdown, negative ATR multiplier times ATR percentage); drawdown passes when drawdown >= drawdown threshold
Calculation: Trend-quality score = on chart bars using current T1 close values, combine available scores by their separately configured weights, renormalized over available scores: clamp01((percentage difference of close from SMA(T1 close, long-term trend lookback) - lower SMA-distance bound) / SMA-distance score range), clamp01((percentage difference of SMA(T1 close, shorter trend lookback) from SMA(T1 close, long-term trend lookback) - lower average-spread bound) / average-spread score range), and clamp01((drawdown - drawdown threshold) / max(abs(drawdown threshold), denominator floor)); the moving averages and drawdown lookbacks run on chart bars taking T1 values
Calculation: Full history = on chart bars using T1 close values, if the minimum-history requirement is at least the full-history threshold require history at the full-history and long-term trend lookbacks; otherwise, if it is at least the long-term trend threshold require history at the long-term trend lookback; otherwise, if it is at least the medium-history threshold require history at the medium-history lookback; otherwise require history at the short-history lookback. Minimum history = require history at the medium-history and short-history lookbacks when the minimum-history requirement is at least the long-term trend threshold, at the short-history lookback when it is at least the medium-history threshold, and otherwise no history lookback
Calculation: Model coverage = relative-strength coverage weighted with trend coverage and, for equities, earnings coverage; relative-strength coverage is the fraction of relative-strength components available, trend coverage is the fraction of trend components available, and earnings coverage is the fraction of fundamental metrics available; use separately configured coverage weights for equities and other asset classes, counting unavailable equity earnings coverage as zero. Confidence = clamp01(the weighted mean of model coverage and history quality, using the configured confidence weights); history quality is the configured full-history, limited-history, or insufficient-history score according to Full history and Minimum history
Calculation: Total score = clamp01(weighted mean of available relative-strength, earnings-quality, and trend-quality scores using their separately configured weights; relative strength is available only when its score exists, its coverage meets the minimum usable relative-strength coverage, and any applicable benchmark is usable; earnings quality is available only for equities when its score exists; trend quality is available when trend coverage is positive; omit unavailable components and renormalize the weights; earnings weight is zero outside equities); smooth relative strength with EMA using the relative-strength smoothing length and total score with EMA using the total-score smoothing length for indices only
Calculation: Safety filter passes = for equities, average(T1 close * T1 volume, turnover lookback) >= minimum turnover AND close > SMA(T1 close, long-term trend lookback) AND drawdown passes; for ETFs, close > SMA(T1 close, long-term trend lookback) AND drawdown passes AND (volume unavailable OR average turnover >= minimum turnover); for bonds, indices, and other asset classes, close > SMA(T1 close, long-term trend lookback) AND drawdown passes; turnover average runs on chart bars taking T1 values
Calculation: Good entry gate = Full history AND model coverage >= minimum buy coverage AND confidence >= minimum buy coverage AND (benchmark not required OR strict benchmark is disabled OR benchmark is available) AND (safety filter disabled OR Safety filter passes)
Calculation: Strong entry gate = Good entry gate AND relative-strength score is usable AND relative-strength score >= strong relative-strength threshold AND trend coverage is positive AND trend-quality score >= strong trend threshold AND confidence >= max(minimum buy coverage, strong confidence threshold) AND model coverage >= max(minimum buy coverage, strong coverage threshold) AND drawdown >= max(drawdown threshold, strong drawdown floor) AND (not equities OR (earnings-quality score is available AND earnings-quality score >= strong earnings threshold))
Calculation: Good confirmation = Good entry gate AND Total score >= buy threshold on the current and previous chart bars
Calculation: Strong confirmation = Strong entry gate AND Total score >= strong-buy threshold on the current and previous chart bars AND Total score > Total score[score-rise lag] AND SMA(Total score - Total score[score-momentum lag], score-momentum smoothing length) > 0 AND current T1 close >= Highest(T1 close, price-high lookback)[1]; score offsets and smoothing run on chart bars
Long: (Strong confirmation AND NOT Strong confirmation on the previous chart bar) OR (Good confirmation AND NOT Good confirmation on the previous chart bar AND NOT Strong confirmation)
Short: Total score crosses below weak-score threshold
Exit: long, Total score crosses below buy threshold AND Minimum history AND model coverage >= minimum watch coverage
