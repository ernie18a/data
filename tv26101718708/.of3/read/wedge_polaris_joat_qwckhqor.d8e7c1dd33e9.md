Calculation: upper projected boundary = max(the active pattern’s two boundary lines projected to the current bar)
Calculation: lower projected boundary = min(the active pattern’s two boundary lines projected to the current bar)
Calculation: lower body-percentile price = min(open, close) + lower body-fraction parameter × abs(close − open)
Calculation: upper body-percentile price = min(open, close) + upper body-fraction parameter × abs(close − open)
Calculation: breakout channel width = max(upper projected boundary − lower projected boundary, instrument minimum tick size), measured at breakout
Calculation: target 3 level = entry price plus for long or minus for short (breakout channel width × target-extension parameter × clamp(pattern duration / max(mean duration in the rolling breakout-history window including this breakout, duration-average floor), duration-factor floor, duration-factor ceiling) + abs(clamp((ATR(ATR lookback) − SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback)) / max(STDEV(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback), ATR Z-score denominator floor), ATR Z-score lower bound, ATR Z-score upper bound)) × ATR(ATR lookback)); calculated at breakout and held constant
Calculation: stop level = entry price minus for long or plus for short (breakout channel width × stop-width parameter); calculated at breakout and held constant
Long: On a confirmed chart bar in an active zigzag-pivot converging pattern, close > upper projected boundary AND (Z-score(abs(close − open), body-length lookback) >= strong-body Z threshold AND (lower body-percentile price > upper projected boundary OR upper body-percentile price < lower projected boundary) OR consecutive chart bars with closes outside the projected channel since the last in-channel close >= breakout-confirmation count)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar in an active zigzag-pivot converging pattern, close < lower projected boundary AND (Z-score(abs(close − open), body-length lookback) >= strong-body Z threshold AND (lower body-percentile price > upper projected boundary OR upper body-percentile price < lower projected boundary) OR consecutive chart bars with closes outside the projected channel since the last in-channel close >= breakout-confirmation count)
Long: On a confirmed chart bar in an active zigzag-pivot converging pattern, close > upper projected boundary AND Z-score(abs(close − open), body-length lookback) >= strong-body Z threshold AND (lower body-percentile price > upper projected boundary OR upper body-percentile price < lower projected boundary)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar in an active zigzag-pivot converging pattern, close < lower projected boundary AND Z-score(abs(close − open), body-length lookback) >= strong-body Z threshold AND (lower body-percentile price > upper projected boundary OR upper body-percentile price < lower projected boundary)
Exit: long, high >= target 3 level AND high[1] < target 3 level OR low <= stop level AND low[1] > stop level
Exit: short, low <= target 3 level AND low[1] > target 3 level OR high >= stop level AND high[1] < stop level
