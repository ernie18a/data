Calculation: Setup phase = use each completed chart bar as C1 while idle; test only the next completed chart bar as C2; if neither sweep setup occurs, reset C1 to that bar; after a valid C2, test only the next chart bar as C3; if C3 does not meet the breakout, enabled-filter, session, and flat-position conditions, reset C1 to C3; after an eligible C3, begin a new setup on the next bar.
Long: C2 is the completed chart bar immediately after C1 AND C2 low < C1 low AND C2 high <= C1 high AND C2 close >= C1 low AND C2 close <= C1 high; mark the long setup pending through the following C3 bar.
Short: C2 is the completed chart bar immediately after C1 AND C2 high > C1 high AND C2 low >= C1 low AND C2 close >= C1 low AND C2 close <= C1 high; mark the short setup pending through the following C3 bar.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the previous candle's low-to-high zone, created when order-block detection is enabled, the previous candle closes below its open, the current close exceeds the previous high, and either displacement filtering is disabled or the current close minus open exceeds ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the displacement multiplier; detection may use forming bars when configured, otherwise completed bars; remove the zone when close is below its low if close mitigation is selected, otherwise when low is below its low; retain only the newest configured count per side.
Calculation: Bearish order block = the previous candle's low-to-high zone, created when order-block detection is enabled, the previous candle closes above its open, the current close is below the previous low, and either displacement filtering is disabled or the current open minus close exceeds ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the displacement multiplier; detection may use forming bars when configured, otherwise completed bars; remove the zone when close is above its high if close mitigation is selected, otherwise when high is above its high; retain only the newest configured count per side.
Long: C3 is the chart bar immediately after a valid bullish C2 AND, while C3 is forming, C3 high >= C1 high + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the breakout-buffer multiplier AND the position was flat at C3's start AND (MA filter disabled OR (EMA selected AND C3 close > EMA(EMA source, EMA lookback)) OR (SMA selected AND C3 close > SMA(SMA source, SMA lookback))) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) is defined and >= ADX threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR C3 datetime HHMM is within the configured inclusive session bounds, wrapping midnight when the start is later than the end) AND long signals are enabled.
Short: C3 is the chart bar immediately after a valid bearish C2 AND, while C3 is forming, C3 low <= C1 low - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the breakout-buffer multiplier AND the position was flat at C3's start AND (MA filter disabled OR (EMA selected AND C3 close < EMA(EMA source, EMA lookback)) OR (SMA selected AND C3 close < SMA(SMA source, SMA lookback))) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) is defined and >= ADX threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR C3 datetime HHMM is within the configured inclusive session bounds, wrapping midnight when the start is later than the end) AND short signals are enabled.
Long: The C3 long condition holds AND order-block detection is enabled AND C1 or C2 range overlaps an active bullish order-block zone expanded above and below by ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the order-block proximity multiplier.
Short: The C3 short condition holds AND order-block detection is enabled AND C1 or C2 range overlaps an active bearish order-block zone expanded above and below by ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the order-block proximity multiplier.
Exit: long, on chart bars using the ordered available T1 bars within each chart bar, including a still-forming T1 bar when available; from the first T1 bar whose high reaches the entry price (C1 high), exit on the first T1 bar whose low <= C1 low - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the stop-distance multiplier OR whose high >= entry price + abs(entry price - stop) times the reward-risk ratio; include the entry-touch bar in the check and give the stop priority if both levels are hit in one T1 bar; if no T1 bars are available, check chart-bar highs and lows starting after the entry chart bar, with the same stop priority.
Exit: short, on chart bars using the ordered available T1 bars within each chart bar, including a still-forming T1 bar when available; from the first T1 bar whose low reaches the entry price (C1 low), exit on the first T1 bar whose high >= C1 high + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) times the stop-distance multiplier OR whose low <= entry price - abs(stop - entry price) times the reward-risk ratio; include the entry-touch bar in the check and give the stop priority if both levels are hit in one T1 bar; if no T1 bars are available, check chart-bar highs and lows starting after the entry chart bar, with the same stop priority.
