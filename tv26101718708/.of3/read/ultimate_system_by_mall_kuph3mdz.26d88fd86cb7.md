Calculation: Score(T) = sum of eight directional votes, each multiplied by the vote unit: candle direction is +1 when close(T) > open(T), −1 when close(T) < open(T), otherwise 0; close-range position is +1 when (close(T)−low(T))/(high(T)−low(T)) is at least the upper position cutoff, −1 when at most the lower position cutoff, otherwise 0, using 0.5 when high(T)=low(T); EMA stack is +1 when EMA(close(T), short lookback) > EMA(close(T), middle lookback) > EMA(close(T), long lookback), −1 for the reverse stack, otherwise 0; RSI is +1 above its upper zone cutoff, −1 below its lower zone cutoff, otherwise 0; MACD histogram(close(T), fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) is +1 above zero, −1 below zero, otherwise 0; volume confirmation is +1 when close(T)>open(T) and volume(T)>SMA(volume(T), volume lookback)×volume multiplier, −1 when close(T)<open(T) and that volume condition holds, otherwise 0; Stochastic(close(T), high(T), low(T), lookback) is +1 above its upper cutoff, −1 below its lower cutoff, otherwise 0; close structure is +1 when close(T)>close(T, nearer lag)>close(T, farther lag), −1 for the reverse, otherwise 0. The shorter EMA lookback is less than the middle, which is less than the longer; the nearer lag is less than the farther lag. Indicators are calculated on T bars; decisions run on chart bars using T's bar still forming.
Calculation: NormalizedScore(T) = round(100 × (Score(T) + eight vote units) / sixteen vote units), for each of T1, T2, T3, and T4.
Calculation: TotalScore = round(NormalizedScore(T1)×W1 + NormalizedScore(T2)×W2 + NormalizedScore(T3)×W3 + NormalizedScore(T4)×W4), where the separate timeframe weights increase from W1 through W4.
Calculation: BiasPoints = bias vote unit × the sum of these eight votes, each 1 when its condition is true and 0 otherwise: T5 close > T5 open; T5 close > EMA(T5 close, middle EMA lookback); T4 close > T4 open; T4 close > EMA(T4 close, middle EMA lookback); T4 close > EMA(T4 close, long EMA lookback); T4 close > EMA(T4 close, bias-long EMA lookback); EMA(T4 close, middle EMA lookback) > EMA(T4 close, long EMA lookback); and Recovery, where Recovery is T4 close > EMA(T4 close, middle EMA lookback) and (T4 close − Lowest(T4 low, recovery-range lookback on chart bars)) / Max(Highest(T4 high, recovery-range lookback on chart bars) − Lowest(T4 low, recovery-range lookback on chart bars), range floor) > recovery cutoff. The bias-long EMA lookback is longer than the long EMA lookback. These decisions run on chart bars using T4 and T5 bars still forming.
Calculation: ShortBan = Recovery AND T5 close > EMA(T5 close, middle EMA lookback) AND BiasPoints >= short-ban cutoff. The T4 low and high series in Recovery are sampled on chart bars from T4 bars still forming.
Long: NormalizedScore(T1) >= bullish display cutoff OR NormalizedScore(T2) >= bullish display cutoff OR NormalizedScore(T3) >= bullish display cutoff OR NormalizedScore(T4) >= bullish display cutoff OR TotalScore >= bullish display cutoff OR BiasPoints >= bullish bias-display cutoff.
Short: NormalizedScore(T1) <= bearish display cutoff OR NormalizedScore(T2) <= bearish display cutoff OR NormalizedScore(T3) <= bearish display cutoff OR NormalizedScore(T4) <= bearish display cutoff OR TotalScore <= bearish display cutoff OR BiasPoints <= bearish bias-display cutoff.

Short: NormalizedScore(T1), NormalizedScore(T2), and NormalizedScore(T3) are each <= bearish alignment cutoff AND they were not all <= that cutoff on the previous chart bar AND NormalizedScore(T4) > bearish alignment cutoff AND chart bars since the previous short signal >= signal cooldown AND NOT ShortBan.
