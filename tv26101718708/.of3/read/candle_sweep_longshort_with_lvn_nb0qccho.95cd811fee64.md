Calculation: Candle range = high - low
Calculation: Body percentage = candle range > 0 ? abs(close - open) / candle range * 100 : 0
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / volume[1]
Calculation: Consecutive red candles before the current bar = count of immediately preceding bars with close < open
Calculation: Consecutive green candles before the current bar = count of immediately preceding bars with close > open
Calculation: LVN validation(side) = at least one zone with nonzero high or low has high >= zone low AND low <= zone high AND (side is long AND close > (zone high + zone low) / 2 OR side is short AND close < (zone high + zone low) / 2)
Long: low < lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) AND close > lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) AND close > open AND volume ratio >= volume-ratio threshold AND (candle range > 0 ? (close - low) / candle range * 100 : 50) >= close-position threshold AND (LVN filter disabled OR LVN validation(long)) AND (range filter disabled OR candle range >= candle range[1] * (1 + minimum range increase percentage / 100)) AND body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND (RSI filter disabled OR (long overbought check disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long overbought threshold) AND (long below-RSI-MA check disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-MA lookback))) AND consecutive red candles before the current bar < long red-candle invalidation count
Short: high > highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < open AND volume ratio >= volume-ratio threshold AND (candle range > 0 ? (high - close) / candle range * 100 : 50) >= close-position threshold AND (LVN filter disabled OR LVN validation(short)) AND (range filter disabled OR candle range >= candle range[1] * (1 + minimum range increase percentage / 100)) AND body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND (RSI filter disabled OR (short below-oversold check disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short oversold threshold) AND (short above-RSI-MA check disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-MA lookback))) AND consecutive green candles before the current bar < short green-candle invalidation count
