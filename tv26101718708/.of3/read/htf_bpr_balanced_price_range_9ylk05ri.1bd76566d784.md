Calculation: Bearish BPR = on T1 bars, for a current bearish FVG (high < low[2] AND close[1] < low[2]), the interval [max(high, high[i+2]), min(low[2], low[i])] for the nearest i from 2 to less than the maximum-distance parameter with a bullish FVG (low[i] > high[i+2] AND close[i+1] > high[i+2]) and low[2] > high[i+2] AND high < low[i]; the current T1 bar may be forming
Calculation: Active BPR = a created BPR remains active until chart time minus the older FVG's first T1 bar time exceeds the maximum-age parameter times chart-bar duration, or a confirmed chart bar has the selected T1 close below the bullish BPR's lower boundary or above the bearish BPR's upper boundary; selected T1 close is the last completed bar when HTF-close confirmation is enabled, otherwise the forming bar
