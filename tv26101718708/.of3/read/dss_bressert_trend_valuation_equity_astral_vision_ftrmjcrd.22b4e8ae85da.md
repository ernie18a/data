Calculation: DSS = DSS Bressert(close, high, low, period, EMA length), using close, high, and low from the still-forming T1 bar sampled on chart bars
Long: (Indicator view AND Trend mode AND (the latest applied crossover of DSS and EMA(DSS, trigger length) is upward OR no applied crossover has occurred; regime starts bullish; apply crossovers on the current chart bar or one chart bar later when No Repaint is enabled)) OR (Equity view AND (the latest crossover as of the previous chart bar is upward OR none has occurred; regime starts bullish)) OR (Indicator view AND Extremes mode AND DSS >= overbought threshold)
Short: (Indicator view AND Trend mode AND the latest applied crossover of DSS and EMA(DSS, trigger length) is downward; apply crossovers on the current chart bar or one chart bar later when No Repaint is enabled) OR (Equity view AND the latest crossover as of the previous chart bar is downward) OR (Indicator view AND Extremes mode AND DSS <= oversold threshold)
