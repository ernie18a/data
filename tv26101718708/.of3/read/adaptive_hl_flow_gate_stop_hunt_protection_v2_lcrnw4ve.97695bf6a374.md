Calculation: Hybrid volatility = ATR(ATR lookback) × (1 − volatility blend weight) + Parkinson volatility(high, low, ATR lookback) × volatility blend weight
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close[efficiency lookback]) / sum(abs(close − close[1]), efficiency lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Adaptive trail = use the base multiplier × hybrid volatility × volatility factor × efficiency factor × ADX factor around the selected source; volatility factor is clamp(hybrid volatility / SMA(hybrid volatility, volatility baseline lookback), minimum volatility factor, maximum volatility factor); efficiency factor is clamp(efficiency intercept − efficiency ratio × efficiency slope, minimum efficiency factor, maximum efficiency factor); ADX factor is the low-ADX multiplier below the minimum ADX, the high-ADX multiplier above the high-ADX threshold, and the neutral multiplier otherwise. The upper candidate is source − adaptive multiplier × hybrid volatility and the lower candidate is source + adaptive multiplier × hybrid volatility. The upper trail is max(upper candidate, prior upper trail) when prior close exceeds prior upper trail, otherwise the upper candidate; the lower trail is min(lower candidate, prior lower trail) when prior close is below prior lower trail, otherwise the lower candidate. Start bullish; switch bearish when close falls below prior upper trail and bullish when close rises above prior lower trail; otherwise retain direction. Use the lower trail when bullish and upper trail when bearish; round to instrument tick size when tick rounding is enabled.
Calculation: Flow gates = apply the selected EMA, RMA, SMA, WMA, or HMA to highest(high, structure lookback) and lowest(low, structure lookback); gate midpoint is their average, gate range is max(high basis − low basis, hybrid volatility × minimum range multiplier), upper gate is midpoint + gate range × gate tightness, and lower gate is midpoint − gate range × gate tightness; round gates to instrument tick size when tick rounding is enabled
Calculation: Gate direction = bullish when close > upper gate, bearish when close < lower gate, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Candle fractions = body size, upper wick, and lower wick divided by high − low; when range is zero, body fraction is one and wick fractions are zero
Calculation: Oscillator direction = use MFI(hlc3, momentum lookback) when volume is valid, otherwise RSI(close, momentum lookback); bullish above the oscillator midpoint plus deadband, bearish below the midpoint minus deadband, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Pressure direction = EMA of signed candle close position × volume weight; signed position is twice (close − low) / (high − low) minus one, or zero when range is zero; volume weight is clamp(volume / average volume, minimum volume weight, maximum volume weight) when volume is valid, otherwise one. Direction is bullish above the pressure threshold, bearish below its negative, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Volume pulse = valid volume exceeds SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume pulse multiplier; pulse direction is bullish when enabled and close ≥ open, bearish when enabled and close < open, otherwise neutral. Range expansion = high − low exceeds SMA(high − low, sweep lookback) × range expansion multiplier. Compression expansion = when enabled, prior hybrid volatility / SMA(hybrid volatility, compression lookback) is below the compression threshold, current ratio exceeds the expansion ratio threshold, and current range exceeds average range
Calculation: Stop-hunt events and risk = prior high/low are highest(high[1]) and lowest(low[1]) over the sweep lookback. Sweep volume passes when volume confirmation is disabled, volume is invalid, or volume exceeds average volume × sweep volume multiplier. A buy-side sweep is a range expansion with high > prior high, close < prior high, and upper wick fraction ≥ minimum wick fraction; a sell-side sweep mirrors this below prior low with lower wick fraction. Either may also qualify as a fake wick when its wick and price rejection conditions hold, without range expansion. Sweep strength is clamp(wick fraction × wick strength weight + range expansion bonus + sweep-volume bonus + volume-pulse bonus, zero, maximum strength). Require strength ≥ the risk block score plus the cluster add-on when cluster filtering is enabled and a qualifying sweep or fake wick occurred within the cluster lookback, capped at maximum strength. Store the latest qualifying sweep high or low, its prior structure level, strength, and age. A sweep is active through the block duration; active risk is stored strength × clamp(one − age / block duration, zero, one), otherwise zero. A buy-side sweep is reclaimed when close exceeds its stored high; a sell-side sweep is reclaimed when close falls below its stored low
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = sign of EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback) minus its prior value, calculated on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off; historical chart bars use the last completed T1 bar and realtime chart bars can use the forming T1 bar
Calculation: Middle-timeframe direction = sign of EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback) minus its prior value, calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off; historical chart bars use the last completed T2 bar and realtime chart bars can use the forming T2 bar
Calculation: Flow score = clamp(trail direction × trail weight + gate direction × gate weight + oscillator direction × oscillator weight + ADX direction × ADX weight + pressure direction × pressure weight + volume-pulse direction × volume-pulse weight + sweep bias × sweep weight + liquidity-memory bias × liquidity-memory weight + compression-expansion bias × expansion weight + enabled T1 direction × T1 weight + enabled T2 direction × T2 weight + session-VWAP direction × VWAP weight, minimum score, maximum score). ADX direction is the DMI direction when ADX ≥ minimum ADX, otherwise neutral. Sweep bias is bullish for a sell-side sweep, bearish for a buy-side sweep, otherwise neutral. Liquidity-memory bias points toward the side with greater active risk. Compression-expansion bias follows candle direction when compression expansion is active. VWAP direction applies only when enabled, intraday, and session VWAP is available
Calculation: Long and short firewalls = block both sides when the chop filter is enabled and (efficiency ratio < minimum efficiency ratio or ADX < minimum ADX), candle body quality is enabled and body fraction < minimum body fraction while either wick fraction ≥ minimum wick fraction, stretch filtering is enabled and abs(close − adaptive trail) / hybrid volatility > maximum entry stretch, or gap filtering is enabled and abs(open − close[1]) > hybrid volatility × gap-risk multiplier. Also block longs when stop-hunt protection is enabled and active buy-side risk ≥ risk block score without a buy-side reclaim, or when HTF conflict blocking is enabled and enabled T1 or T2 direction is bearish. Block shorts symmetrically for active sell-side risk and bullish T1 or T2 direction
Calculation: Flow state = on signal-ready chart bars, candidate state is bullish when flow score ≥ flow threshold and the long firewall is clear; otherwise bearish when flow score ≤ negative flow threshold and the short firewall is clear; otherwise neutral when neutral reset is enabled and abs(flow score) ≤ neutral threshold; otherwise retain prior state. Commit a changed candidate when it is neutral or the minimum bars between flips have elapsed; otherwise retain prior state. Hold prior state when not signal-ready. Update the last-flip bar on each committed change
Calculation: Signal-ready = realtime confirmation mode or the current chart bar is confirmed
Calculation: Screener score = clamp(screened instrument gate direction × screener gate weight + direction of close versus the selected moving average of close × screener average weight + RSI direction × screener RSI weight, minimum score, maximum score). Calculate on each screened instrument’s selected T3 bars and sample onto chart bars with lookahead off; historical chart bars use the last completed T3 bar and realtime chart bars can use the forming T3 bar. Screener RSI direction is bullish above the oscillator midpoint plus momentum deadband and bearish below the midpoint minus deadband
Long: flow state changes to bullish from any other state
Short: flow state changes to bearish from any other state
Long: signal-ready AND flow state is bullish AND bars since the last flow-state change ≥ retest delay AND low ≤ upper gate AND close > upper gate AND close > adaptive trail AND oscillator direction ≥ neutral AND no active buy-side sweep block
Short: signal-ready AND flow state is bearish AND bars since the last flow-state change ≥ retest delay AND high ≥ lower gate AND close < lower gate AND close < adaptive trail AND oscillator direction ≤ neutral AND no active sell-side sweep block
Long: flow state is bullish AND close > adaptive trail AND close > upper gate AND chart bar index is a multiple of continuation spacing AND no active buy-side sweep block
Short: flow state is bearish AND close < adaptive trail AND close < lower gate AND chart bar index is a multiple of continuation spacing AND no active sell-side sweep block
Long: active sell-side sweep AND not reclaimed AND low ≤ its stored structure level AND close > that level
Short: active buy-side sweep AND not reclaimed AND high ≥ its stored structure level AND close < that level
Long: active buy-side sweep AND close > its stored sweep high AND the buy-side reclaim condition was false on the prior bar
Short: active sell-side sweep AND close < its stored sweep low AND the sell-side reclaim condition was false on the prior bar
Long: for a screened instrument, screener score ≥ flow threshold
Short: for a screened instrument, screener score ≤ negative flow threshold
