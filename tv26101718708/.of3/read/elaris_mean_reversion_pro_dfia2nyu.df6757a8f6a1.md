Calculation: Mean = selected moving average(source, mean lookback, mean type: SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, VWMA, or HMA)
Calculation: Upper band = if band mode is Z-Score, Mean + standard deviation(source, deviation lookback) × Z multiplier; if ATR, Mean + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier; if Hybrid, Mean + (standard deviation(source, deviation lookback) × Z multiplier + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier) / 2
Calculation: Lower band = if band mode is Z-Score, Mean − standard deviation(source, deviation lookback) × Z multiplier; if ATR, Mean − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier; if Hybrid, Mean − (standard deviation(source, deviation lookback) × Z multiplier + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier) / 2
Calculation: Lower extreme touched = lowest(low, extreme-touch lookback) <= Lower band
Calculation: Upper extreme touched = highest(high, extreme-touch lookback) >= Upper band
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND (min(open, close) − low) / abs(close − open) >= wick ratio AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) × 100 <= maximum opposite-wick percentage
Calculation: Bearish rejection = close < open AND (high − max(open, close)) / abs(close − open) >= wick ratio AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) × 100 <= maximum opposite-wick percentage
