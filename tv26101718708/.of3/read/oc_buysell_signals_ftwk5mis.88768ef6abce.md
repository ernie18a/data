Calculation: candle body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: long stop level = entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiple in ATR stop mode; signal-bar low - ATR(ATR lookback) * candle stop buffer in candle-wick stop mode; signal-bar open - ATR(ATR lookback) * candle stop buffer in candle-body stop mode; undefined in no-stop mode; ATR is sampled on the signal bar and locked for the trade
Calculation: short stop level = entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiple in ATR stop mode; signal-bar high + ATR(ATR lookback) * candle stop buffer in candle-wick stop mode; signal-bar open + ATR(ATR lookback) * candle stop buffer in candle-body stop mode; undefined in no-stop mode; ATR is sampled on the signal bar and locked for the trade
Calculation: long target level = entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * target ATR multiple in ATR-target mode, or entry price + abs(entry price - long stop level) * target stop multiple in stop-multiple mode with a stop active; undefined in opposite-signal mode
Calculation: short target level = entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * target ATR multiple in ATR-target mode, or entry price - abs(entry price - short stop level) * target stop multiple in stop-multiple mode with a stop active; undefined in opposite-signal mode
Long: close > open AND close > max(open[1], close[1]) AND candle body >= SMA(candle body, body lookback) * body multiplier AND (volume multiplier <= 0 OR volume >= SMA(volume, body lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (alternation not required OR previous signal was short) AND NOT(no-overlap enabled AND risk exits active AND a position is open AND (both stop and target are active OR previous signal was long)) AND (bar confirmed OR intrabar signaling enabled)
Short: close < open AND close < min(open[1], close[1]) AND candle body >= SMA(candle body, body lookback) * body multiplier AND (volume multiplier <= 0 OR volume >= SMA(volume, body lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (alternation not required OR previous signal was long) AND NOT(no-overlap enabled AND risk exits active AND a position is open AND (both stop and target are active OR previous signal was short)) AND (bar confirmed OR intrabar signaling enabled)
Exit: long, risk exits enabled AND ((stop active AND (close-trigger mode AND close <= long stop level OR wick-trigger mode AND low <= long stop level)) OR (target active AND (close-trigger mode AND close >= long target level OR wick-trigger mode AND high >= long target level)))
Exit: short, risk exits enabled AND ((stop active AND (close-trigger mode AND close >= short stop level OR wick-trigger mode AND high >= short stop level)) OR (target active AND (close-trigger mode AND close <= short target level OR wick-trigger mode AND low <= short target level)))
