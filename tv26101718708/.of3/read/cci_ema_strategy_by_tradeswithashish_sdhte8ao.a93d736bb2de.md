Calculation: Long trailing stop = running maximum since the long entry signal of max(EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), EMA(EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), longer EMA lookback))
Calculation: Short trailing stop = running minimum since the short entry signal of min(EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), EMA(EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), longer EMA lookback))
Long: flat AND confirmed bar AND close > EMA(EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), longer EMA lookback) AND close > open AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND CCI(hlc3, longer CCI lookback) > 0 AND CCI(hlc3, shorter CCI lookback) > positive CCI threshold AND close > VWAP AND (close - low) / (high - low) > long close-location threshold AND volume > volume[1]
Short: flat AND confirmed bar AND close < EMA(EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), longer EMA lookback) AND close < open AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND CCI(hlc3, longer CCI lookback) < 0 AND CCI(hlc3, shorter CCI lookback) < -positive CCI threshold AND close < VWAP AND (close - low) / (high - low) < short close-location threshold AND volume > volume[1]
Exit: long, after a bar in the position satisfies CCI(hlc3, longer CCI lookback) < 0 OR close < EMA(EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), longer EMA lookback) OR close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), low <= Long trailing stop OR high >= entry-signal-bar OHLC4 × (1 + target return percentage / 100)
Exit: short, after a bar in the position satisfies CCI(hlc3, longer CCI lookback) > 0 OR close > EMA(EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), longer EMA lookback) OR close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), high >= Short trailing stop OR low <= entry-signal-bar OHLC4 × (1 - target return percentage / 100)
