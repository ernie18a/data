Calculation: HTF trend(T) = bullish when EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, medium lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback), bearish when EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, medium lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback), otherwise neutral; calculated on T bars using the current, still-forming T bar and projected onto chart bars
Long: EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, medium lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND at least vote-threshold of HTF trend(T1), HTF trend(T2), HTF trend(T3), HTF trend(T4) are bullish AND ((ATR(lookback) > SMA(ATR(lookback), same lookback) AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[1] > EMA(close, short lookback)) OR (close > high[1] AND high - low > ATR(lookback))) AND close <= EMA(close, short lookback) * (1 + late-entry buffer / 100)
Short: EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, medium lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback) AND at least vote-threshold of HTF trend(T1), HTF trend(T2), HTF trend(T3), HTF trend(T4) are bearish AND ((ATR(lookback) > SMA(ATR(lookback), same lookback) AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[1] < EMA(close, short lookback)) OR (close < low[1] AND high - low > ATR(lookback))) AND close >= EMA(close, short lookback) * (1 - late-entry buffer / 100)
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar low * (1 - stop buffer / 100) OR high >= signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - signal-bar low * (1 - stop buffer / 100)) * risk/reward
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar high * (1 + stop buffer / 100) OR low <= signal-bar close - (signal-bar high * (1 + stop buffer / 100) - signal-bar close) * risk/reward
