Calculation: ORB high and low = maximum high and minimum low, respectively, of chart bars from the ORB start-time parameter through the ORB duration parameter, reset each New York trading day
Calculation: ORB range = ORB high - ORB low
Calculation: armed setup = reset to none each New York trading day and after entry; while flat, not yet traded that day, and the daily loss limit is not hit, arm the first matching setup by bar order, resolving same-bar matches in this priority: long sweep if low < last completed T1 low and close > last completed T1 low (level = last completed T1 low); short sweep if high > last completed T1 high and close < last completed T1 high (level = last completed T1 high); long ORB if close > ORB high and ORB range is between the ORB minimum and maximum range parameters (level = ORB high); short ORB if close < ORB low and ORB range is between those parameters (level = ORB low); retain the setup and level until invalidated: long ORB when close < ORB low, short ORB when close > ORB high, long sweep when close < last completed T1 low - ATR(lookback parameter) × sweep invalidation multiple, or short sweep when close > last completed T1 high + ATR(lookback parameter) × sweep invalidation multiple; an invalidated setup can be replaced starting on the next bar
Calculation: daily loss limit hit = strategy equity - strategy equity at the start of the current New York trading day <= -daily loss parameter
Long: New York time >= ORB start-time parameter + ORB duration parameter AND New York time < exit cutoff-time parameter AND flat AND not yet traded that New York day AND daily loss limit is not hit AND ((armed setup is long ORB AND low <= ORB high + ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple AND close >= ORB high - ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple AND close > ORB low) OR (armed setup is long sweep AND low <= last completed T1 low + ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple AND close >= last completed T1 low - ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple))
Short: New York time >= ORB start-time parameter + ORB duration parameter AND New York time < exit cutoff-time parameter AND flat AND not yet traded that New York day AND daily loss limit is not hit AND ((armed setup is short ORB AND high >= ORB low - ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple AND close <= ORB low + ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple AND close < ORB high) OR (armed setup is short sweep AND high >= last completed T1 high - ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple AND close <= last completed T1 high + ATR(lookback parameter) × retest buffer multiple))
Exit: long, (ORB entry AND (low <= entry-signal close - ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × stop multiple OR high >= entry-signal close + ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × ORB target multiple)) OR (sweep entry AND (low <= entry-signal close - ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × stop multiple OR high >= entry-signal close + ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × sweep target multiple)) OR New York time >= exit cutoff-time parameter OR daily loss limit is hit
Exit: short, (ORB entry AND (high >= entry-signal close + ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × stop multiple OR low <= entry-signal close - ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × ORB target multiple)) OR (sweep entry AND (high >= entry-signal close + ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × stop multiple OR low <= entry-signal close - ATR(lookback parameter) on entry bar × sweep target multiple)) OR New York time >= exit cutoff-time parameter OR daily loss limit is hit
