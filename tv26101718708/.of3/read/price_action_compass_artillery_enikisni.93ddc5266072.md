Calculation: Uptrend = EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, shorter lookback)[slope lookback] AND close > EMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: Downtrend = EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, shorter lookback)[slope lookback] AND close < EMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: Range high = highest(high, range lookback)
Calculation: Range low = lowest(low, range lookback)
Calculation: Range mode = NOT Uptrend AND NOT Downtrend AND Range high - Range low <= ATR(lookback) * range-height cap
Calculation: Bullish trigger = high > low AND high - low <= ATR(lookback) * maximum-bar-size multiple AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body-to-range ratio AND close > open AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= bullish close-strength threshold AND high > high[1]
Calculation: Bearish trigger = high > low AND high - low <= ATR(lookback) * maximum-bar-size multiple AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body-to-range ratio AND close < open AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= bearish close-strength threshold AND low < low[1]
Calculation: Uptrend pullback entry = in an uptrend, low <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) arms a pullback; a bullish trigger while armed, including on the same bar, increments the uptrend entry count and clears the arm; when not in an uptrend, reset the count and clear the arm
Calculation: Downtrend pullback entry = in a downtrend, high >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) arms a pullback; a bearish trigger while armed, including on the same bar, increments the downtrend entry count and clears the arm; when not in a downtrend, reset the count and clear the arm
Long: long direction enabled AND current day's combined signal count is below its limit or the limit is disabled AND ((Range mode AND close <= Range low + (Range high - Range low) * range-edge fraction AND Bullish trigger) OR (Uptrend AND Uptrend pullback entry occurs AND (second-entry filter disabled OR uptrend entry count >= 2)))
Short: short direction enabled AND current day's combined signal count is below its limit or the limit is disabled AND ((Range mode AND close >= Range high - (Range high - Range low) * range-edge fraction AND Bearish trigger) OR (Downtrend AND Downtrend pullback entry occurs AND (second-entry filter disabled OR downtrend entry count >= 2)))
