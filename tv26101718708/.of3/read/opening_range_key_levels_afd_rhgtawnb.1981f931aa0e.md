Calculation: RangeLow(D) = lowest low of chart bars in the first D of the regular session, defined only after that window ends
Calculation: RangeBreak(D) = UP at the first regular-session bar after the D window ends with close > RangeHigh(D) if that bar is outside killzone window; DN at the first regular-session bar after the D window ends with close < RangeLow(D) if that bar is outside killzone window; the most recent of these gives the state; NONE before either
