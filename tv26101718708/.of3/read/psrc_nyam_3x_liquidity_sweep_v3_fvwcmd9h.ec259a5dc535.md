Calculation: Structure(T, swing-pivot lookback) = retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, each formed with equal left and right lookbacks; an upward break is a close crossing above the retained pivot high and a downward break is a close crossing below the retained pivot low; trend starts at 0 and becomes +1 or -1 on the respective break; CHoCH is an upward break when prior trend <= 0 or a downward break when prior trend >= 0. Calculate on T bars and sample on chart bars with non-lookahead behavior: the current T bar in realtime and the last completed T bar historically.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = when bias agreement is required, +1 if trends on T1 and T2 are both +1, -1 if both are -1, otherwise 0; when agreement is not required, +1 if either trend is +1, otherwise -1 if either is -1, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Equal-high/low levels on chart bars = when a new swing-pivot high/low is within equal-pivot tolerance × ATR(equal-pivot ATR lookback) of the preceding pivot of the same kind, set the level to the higher high or lower low respectively; retain each level until swept.
Calculation: Buy-side sweep count = number of enabled Asian-session low, London-session low, prior completed T1 low, and equal-low pools swept since the T1 boundary; sell-side sweep count uses the corresponding highs. A low is swept when chart low < level and chart close > level; a high is swept when chart high > level and chart close < level. Count each pool at most once per T1 boundary. Session levels are the configured sessions' chart-bar highs/lows; prior T1 levels use the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Setup state = allow at most one setup; start a long or short setup when its arm condition occurs while idle. Save the long leg from the latest T4 swing low to current T4 high, or the short leg from current T4 low to the latest T4 swing high. Long invalidation is leg low - ATR(protection lookback) × invalidation buffer; short invalidation is leg high + ATR(protection lookback) × invalidation buffer. For range = leg high - leg low, long premium zone is [leg high - range × premium maximum, leg high - range × premium minimum] and standard zone is [leg high - range × premium minimum, leg high - range × standard minimum]; short premium zone is [leg low + range × premium minimum, leg low + range × premium maximum] and standard zone is [leg low + range × standard minimum, leg low + range × premium minimum]. A chart bar overlaps a zone when its high is at least the zone bottom and its low is at most the zone top. Keep each zone-tag flag once set; update the most recent tap bar on every overlap with either zone. After evaluating entries, clear the setup on an entry, long close < long invalidation, short close > short invalidation, NY AM session end, or—when session timeout is disabled—arm age exceeding the maximum-wait parameter.
