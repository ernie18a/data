Long: On a chart bar within the configured lookback of the latest chart bar, an enabled slot T1–T5 has low > high[2] on the current bar if its timeframe equals the chart timeframe, or on its last completed bar if its timeframe is higher; sample higher-timeframe values on chart bars and mark each gap once.
