Calculation: Up-cross count of short EMA = SUM(1 when close crosses above EMA(close, short EMA lookback), cross-count lookback chart bars)
Calculation: Up-cross count of middle EMA = SUM(1 when close crosses above EMA(close, middle EMA lookback), cross-count lookback chart bars)
Calculation: Up-cross count of long EMA = SUM(1 when close crosses above EMA(close, long EMA lookback), cross-count lookback chart bars)
Calculation: Down-cross count of short EMA = SUM(1 when close crosses below EMA(close, short EMA lookback), cross-count lookback chart bars)
Calculation: Down-cross count of middle EMA = SUM(1 when close crosses below EMA(close, middle EMA lookback), cross-count lookback chart bars)
Calculation: Down-cross count of long EMA = SUM(1 when close crosses below EMA(close, long EMA lookback), cross-count lookback chart bars)
Calculation: Long stop = close on long signal − stop multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars at signal); hold fixed while long
Calculation: Long target = close on long signal + target multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars at signal); hold fixed while long
Calculation: Short stop = close on short signal + stop multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars at signal); hold fixed while short
Calculation: Short target = close on short signal − target multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback, T1 bar still forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars at signal); hold fixed while short
Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bull RSI lower bound AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bull RSI upper bound AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > MACD signal line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > bullish MACD threshold AND ((up-cross count of short EMA < up-cross count of middle EMA AND up-cross count of short EMA < up-cross count of long EMA AND up-cross count of short EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses above EMA(close, short EMA lookback)) OR (up-cross count of middle EMA < up-cross count of short EMA AND up-cross count of middle EMA < up-cross count of long EMA AND up-cross count of middle EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses above EMA(close, middle EMA lookback)) OR (up-cross count of long EMA < up-cross count of short EMA AND up-cross count of long EMA < up-cross count of middle EMA AND up-cross count of long EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses above EMA(close, long EMA lookback)))
Short: RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bear RSI upper bound AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bear RSI lower bound AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) < MACD signal line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) < bearish MACD threshold AND ((down-cross count of short EMA < down-cross count of middle EMA AND down-cross count of short EMA < down-cross count of long EMA AND down-cross count of short EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses below EMA(close, short EMA lookback)) OR (down-cross count of middle EMA < down-cross count of short EMA AND down-cross count of middle EMA < up-cross count of long EMA AND down-cross count of middle EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses below EMA(close, middle EMA lookback)) OR (down-cross count of long EMA < down-cross count of short EMA AND down-cross count of long EMA < down-cross count of middle EMA AND down-cross count of long EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses below EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND NOT (down-cross count of middle EMA < down-cross count of short EMA AND down-cross count of middle EMA < up-cross count of long EMA AND down-cross count of middle EMA < cross-count limit AND close crosses below EMA(close, middle EMA lookback))))
Exit: Long, low <= Long stop OR high >= Long target
Exit: Short, high >= Short stop OR low <= Short target
