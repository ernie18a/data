Calculation: filter close = close(T1) mapped onto chart bars with lookahead off; use the current, still-forming T1 bar in realtime and the last available completed T1 value on historical chart bars until T1 closes
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, trading direction is Long or Both AND SuperTrend(high, low, close, longer lookback, multiplier A) is bullish AND SuperTrend(high, low, close, shorter lookback, multiplier B) is bullish AND (VIX filtering is disabled OR the selected long VIX rule passes: Below Mean + SD means filter close < SMA(filter close, lookback) - standard deviation(filter close, same lookback) * band multiplier; Below Mean means filter close < SMA(filter close, lookback); Falling VIX means filter close < EMA(filter close, trend lookback); Above Mean means filter close > SMA(filter close, lookback); Rising VIX means filter close > EMA(filter close, trend lookback); Any always passes). These SMA, standard deviation, and EMA calculations run on chart bars using filter close from T1.
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, trading direction is Short or Both AND SuperTrend(high, low, close, longer lookback, multiplier A) is bearish AND SuperTrend(high, low, close, shorter lookback, multiplier B) is bearish AND (VIX filtering is disabled OR the selected short VIX rule passes: Above Mean + SD means filter close > SMA(filter close, lookback) + standard deviation(filter close, same lookback) * band multiplier; Above Mean means filter close > SMA(filter close, lookback); Rising VIX means filter close > EMA(filter close, trend lookback); Any always passes). These SMA, standard deviation, and EMA calculations run on chart bars using filter close from T1.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed chart bar, SuperTrend(high, low, close, longer lookback, multiplier A) is bearish OR SuperTrend(high, low, close, shorter lookback, multiplier B) is bearish
Exit: Short, on a confirmed chart bar, SuperTrend(high, low, close, longer lookback, multiplier A) is bullish OR SuperTrend(high, low, close, shorter lookback, multiplier B) is bullish
Exit: Long, standard exits are enabled AND four-step take profit is disabled AND (high >= entry price * (1 + standard take-profit percentage / 100) OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100) OR (trailing stop is enabled AND highest high since entry >= entry price * (1 + trailing-stop percentage / 100) AND low <= highest high since trailing-stop activation - entry price * trailing-stop percentage / 100))
Exit: Short, standard exits are enabled AND four-step take profit is disabled AND (low <= entry price * (1 - standard take-profit percentage / 100) OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) OR (trailing stop is enabled AND lowest low since entry <= entry price * (1 - trailing-stop percentage / 100) AND high >= lowest low since trailing-stop activation + entry price * trailing-stop percentage / 100))
Exit: Long, four-step take profit is enabled AND (low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100) OR (ATR-based targets are selected AND (high >= entry price + ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 1 OR high >= entry price + ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 2 OR high >= entry price + ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 3 OR high >= entry price + ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 4)) OR (percentage-based targets are selected AND (high >= entry price * (1 + target percentage 1 / 100) OR high >= entry price * (1 + target percentage 2 / 100) OR high >= entry price * (1 + target percentage 3 / 100) OR high >= entry price * (1 + target percentage 4 / 100)))
Exit: Short, four-step take profit is enabled AND (high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) OR (ATR-based targets are selected AND (low <= entry price - ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 1 OR low <= entry price - ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 2 OR low <= entry price - ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 3 OR low <= entry price - ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier 4)) OR (percentage-based targets are selected AND (low <= entry price * (1 - target percentage 1 / 100) OR low <= entry price * (1 - target percentage 2 / 100) OR low <= entry price * (1 - target percentage 3 / 100) OR low <= entry price * (1 - target percentage 4 / 100)))
