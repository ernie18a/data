Long: MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) crosses below its signal line AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) AND volume > volume[1] AND RSI(close, lookback) crosses below the overbought threshold AND close < close[price lookback] AND more than the signal cooldown period has elapsed since the previous Long signal.
Short: MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) crosses above its signal line AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) AND volume > volume[1] AND RSI(close, lookback) crosses above the oversold threshold AND close > close[price lookback] AND more than the signal cooldown period has elapsed since the previous Short signal.
