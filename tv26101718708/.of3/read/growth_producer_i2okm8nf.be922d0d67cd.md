Long: (RSI(VWAP(close), long lookback) crosses above long oversold level OR MFI(hlc3, lookback) crosses below lower level) AND EMA(close, lookback) > SMA(close, same lookback) AND (position is flat OR (position is long AND RVI(close, lookback, smoothing lookback) > benchmark))
Short: (RSI(VWAP(close), short lookback) crosses below short overbought level OR MFI(hlc3, same lookback) crosses above upper level) AND EMA(close, lookback) < SMA(close, same lookback) AND (position is flat OR (position is short AND RVI(close, lookback, smoothing lookback) > benchmark))
Exit: long, RSI(VWAP(close), long lookback) crosses below long overbought level OR high >= average entry price * (1 + long take-profit 1) OR high >= average entry price * (1 + long take-profit 2) OR low <= average entry price * (1 - long stop-loss)
Exit: short, RSI(VWAP(close), short lookback) crosses above short oversold level OR low <= average entry price * (1 - short take-profit 1) OR low <= average entry price * (1 - short take-profit 2) OR high >= average entry price * (1 + short stop-loss)
