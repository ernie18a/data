Calculation: Crystallization score = round to nearest integer of the sum of: clamp(correlation(bar-to-bar close change, its one-bar lag, correlation lookback) × correlation weight + correlation offset, score floor, correlation score cap); clamp((ADX from DMI(ADX/DMI length, same ADX smoothing length) − ADX baseline) × ADX weight + change in that ADX over the ADX slope interval × ADX slope weight, score floor, ADX score cap); and clamp((standard-deviation ratio pivot − standard deviation of bar-to-bar close change over the return standard-deviation lookback / SMA of that standard deviation over the baseline lookback, using the standard-deviation ratio fallback when the SMA is not positive) × standard-deviation weight, score floor, standard-deviation score cap).
Long: Crystallization score >= signal threshold AND Crystallization score[1] < signal threshold AND DI+ from DMI(ADX/DMI length, same ADX smoothing length) > DI− from that DMI AND close > EMA(close, regime EMA lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND at least the cooldown interval in chart bars has elapsed since the chart's first bar or the previous signal AND the chart bar is complete.
Short: Crystallization score >= signal threshold AND Crystallization score[1] < signal threshold AND DI− from DMI(ADX/DMI length, same ADX smoothing length) > DI+ from that DMI AND close < EMA(close, regime EMA lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND at least the cooldown interval in chart bars has elapsed since the chart's first bar or the previous signal AND the chart bar is complete.
