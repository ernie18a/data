Calculation: Prior T1 range = prior completed T1 high minus prior completed T1 low.
Calculation: OTE bounds = for a bullish sweep, upper = prior completed T1 high minus prior T1 range times the shallow retracement fraction and lower = prior completed T1 high minus prior T1 range times the deeper OTE fraction; for a bearish sweep, lower = prior completed T1 low plus prior T1 range times the shallow retracement fraction and upper = prior completed T1 low plus prior T1 range times the deeper OTE fraction, where the deeper fraction exceeds the shallow fraction; simulated long entry price = bullish upper bound and simulated short entry price = bearish lower bound.
Calculation: Candidate T1 swing highs/lows = Pivot High(high)/Pivot Low(low) at the configured recent offsets, using completed T1 bars and requiring each pivot to be strictly above/below its immediately adjacent T1 bars.
Calculation: NY DR high/low = highest high/lowest low during the New York build window, using T2 bars when the chart timeframe is shorter than T2 and chart bars otherwise; lock both at the build window close.
Calculation: Long stop = prior completed T1 low when the prior-candle stop method is selected, otherwise bullish OTE lower bound minus prior T1 range times the stop-buffer fraction.
Calculation: Short stop = prior completed T1 high when the prior-candle stop method is selected, otherwise bearish OTE upper bound plus prior T1 range times the stop-buffer fraction.
Calculation: Long target = prior completed T1 high in prior-extreme mode; in next-swing mode, the nearest candidate T1 swing high above the sweep bar's chart high, or prior completed T1 high if none; in fixed reward/risk mode, simulated long entry price plus (simulated long entry price minus long stop) times the reward/risk multiplier.
Calculation: Short target = prior completed T1 low in prior-extreme mode; in next-swing mode, the nearest candidate T1 swing low below the sweep bar's chart low, or prior completed T1 low if none; in fixed reward/risk mode, simulated short entry price minus (short stop minus simulated short entry price) times the reward/risk multiplier.
Long: After the NY DR build window closes, while NY DR tracking is enabled and during its extension window, the chart close is strictly above the locked NY DR high for the configured number of consecutive chart bars, and no NY DR direction has yet been confirmed that day.
Short: After the NY DR build window closes, while NY DR tracking is enabled and during its extension window, the chart close is strictly below the locked NY DR low for the configured number of consecutive chart bars, and no NY DR direction has yet been confirmed that day.
Long: On chart bars taking values from the current forming bar and prior completed bar of each of T3 through T9, current high is greater than prior high AND (current low is at least prior low OR (current low is below prior low AND current close is at least current open)).
Short: On chart bars taking values from the current forming bar and prior completed bar of each of T3 through T9, current low is below prior low AND (current high is at most prior high OR (current high is above prior high AND current close is below current open)).
Long: On chart bars, a bullish sweep is locked within the configured sweep window from the start of a T1 candle (automatic mode uses the configured fraction of T1 duration; otherwise use the configured sweep-window duration): chart high exceeds the prior completed T1 high AND chart low is at least the prior completed T1 low; no signal has fired for that T1 candle; the first subsequent chart bar satisfying chart low at or below bullish OTE upper bound AND chart close at or above bullish OTE lower bound is within the configured New York session start hour (inclusive) and end hour (exclusive); if the range filter is enabled, prior T1 range does not exceed its limit; if the swing filter is enabled, the sweep bar did not cross a candidate T1 swing high strictly between the prior T1 high and its chart high; if the VWAP filter is enabled, chart close is above VWAP(hlc3); if the DR filter is enabled and a confirmed DR bias is active after the configured time gate, that bias is bullish; and if the time gate and pre-gate block are enabled, the chart bar is at or after the gate.
Short: On chart bars, a bearish sweep is locked within the configured sweep window from the start of a T1 candle (automatic mode uses the configured fraction of T1 duration; otherwise use the configured sweep-window duration): chart low is below the prior completed T1 low AND chart high is at most the prior completed T1 high; no signal has fired for that T1 candle; the first subsequent chart bar satisfying chart high at or above bearish OTE lower bound AND chart close at or below bearish OTE upper bound is within the configured New York session start hour (inclusive) and end hour (exclusive); if the range filter is enabled, prior T1 range does not exceed its limit; if the swing filter is enabled, the sweep bar did not cross a candidate T1 swing low strictly between its chart low and the prior T1 low; if the VWAP filter is enabled, chart close is below VWAP(hlc3); if the DR filter is enabled and a confirmed DR bias is active after the configured time gate, that bias is bearish; and if the time gate and pre-gate block are enabled, the chart bar is at or after the gate.
Long: A bullish sweep is locked within its configured T1 sweep window, no signal has fired for that T1 candle, the first chart bar satisfying chart low at or below bullish OTE upper bound AND chart close at or above bullish OTE lower bound is within the configured New York session, and at least one enabled filter fails: prior T1 range exceeds its limit; the sweep bar crosses a candidate T1 swing high strictly between the prior T1 high and its chart high; chart close is at or below VWAP(hlc3); an active confirmed DR bias is bearish; or the enabled pre-gate block is before its time gate.
Short: A bearish sweep is locked within its configured T1 sweep window, no signal has fired for that T1 candle, the first chart bar satisfying chart high at or above bearish OTE lower bound AND chart close at or below bearish OTE upper bound is within the configured New York session, and at least one enabled filter fails: prior T1 range exceeds its limit; the sweep bar crosses a candidate T1 swing low strictly between its chart low and the prior T1 low; chart close is at or above VWAP(hlc3); an active confirmed DR bias is bullish; or the enabled pre-gate block is before its time gate.
Exit: long, on chart bars after the entry bar, chart high is at or above long target OR (chart low is at or below long stop AND chart high is below long target).
Exit: short, on chart bars after the entry bar, chart low is at or below short target OR (chart high is at or above short stop AND chart low is above short target).
