Calculation: Flow volume = selected volume series, or borrowed volume when configured and the selected series has never been positive; treat missing values as zero.
Calculation: Signed volume delta = on chart bars, when lower-timeframe estimation is enabled and T1 intrabars are available, sum T1 volume × (2 × clamp((T1 close − T1 low) / (T1 high − T1 low), 0, 1) − 1), using 0.5 for zero-range intrabars; otherwise use flow volume × (2 × NormalCDF(clamp((close − close[1]) / StDev(close − close[1], fallback lookback), with z = 0 when the standard deviation is zero) − 1). Aggregate available T1 intrabars within each chart bar, including a still-forming intrabar when available.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = cumulative sum(signed volume delta) on chart bars.
Calculation: Detrended CVD = cumulative delta − EMA(cumulative delta, CVD normalization lookback).
Calculation: Normalized CVD = robust z-score of detrended CVD over the CVD normalization lookback, using median and scaled median absolute deviation, or detrended CVD / StDev(detrended CVD, CVD normalization lookback), using zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Flow oscillator = select normalized CVD, RSI(detrended CVD, CVD-RSI lookback), or MFI(hlc3, flow volume, MFI lookback); when oscillator smoothing is enabled, smooth with EMA or UltimateSmoother using the oscillator smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Oscillator overbought and oversold levels = positive and negative normalized-CVD overbought magnitude, or the selected CVD-RSI or MFI overbought and oversold levels.
Calculation: Oscillator extreme levels = positive and negative normalized-CVD extreme magnitude, or the selected CVD-RSI or MFI extreme levels.
Calculation: Minimum oscillator pivot separation = divergence magnitude parameter × StDev(flow oscillator, oscillator-gap lookback).
Calculation: Agreement score = sign(normalized CVD) + sign(CVD-RSI − 50) + sign(MFI − 50).
Calculation: VSA volume = chart volume, or, when time-of-day adjustment is enabled, chart volume divided by the EMA estimate for the same clock-time slot, updated with current volume and using the session-memory and slot-size parameters.
Calculation: VSA close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high equals low.
