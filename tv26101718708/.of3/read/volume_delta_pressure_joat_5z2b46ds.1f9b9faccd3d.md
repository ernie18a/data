Calculation: Intrabar delta = sum of completed T1 intrabar volume for intrabars with close > open minus sum for intrabars with close < open, aggregated on each chart bar; doji volume is split equally between buying and selling.
Calculation: Proxy delta = volume × (2 × clamp(close-position fraction × close-vs-body weight + candle-direction fraction × (1 − close-vs-body weight), 0, 1) − 1), where close-position fraction is (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 if high = low; candle-direction fraction is 1 if close > open, 0 if close < open, or 0.5 if close = open.
Calculation: Bar delta = Intrabar delta when an intrabar method is selected and T1 intrabar volume is positive; otherwise Proxy delta.
Calculation: Pressure = (EMA(bar delta, delta-smoothing lookback) − SMA(EMA(bar delta, delta-smoothing lookback), pressure-normalization lookback)) / stdev(EMA(bar delta, delta-smoothing lookback), pressure-normalization lookback), or 100 × bar delta / classified volume, according to normalization mode; use 0 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low of low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low and CVD(bar delta, reset-anchor parameter) at the new pivot is above CVD at the preceding pivot; pivots use the divergence-pivot lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Bearish CVD divergence = a newly confirmed pivot high of high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high and CVD(bar delta, reset-anchor parameter) at the new pivot is below CVD at the preceding pivot; pivots use the divergence-pivot lookback on both sides.
Long: Bullish CVD divergence AND divergence labels enabled AND positive volume observed on this or a prior chart bar
Short: Bearish CVD divergence AND divergence labels enabled AND positive volume observed on this or a prior chart bar
Long: confirmed chart bar AND positive volume observed on this or a prior chart bar AND Pressure crosses above zero AND abs(bar delta) ≥ SMA(abs(bar delta), delta-expansion lookback) × expansion multiplier AND SMA(abs(bar delta), delta-expansion lookback) > 0 AND (price-confirmation filter disabled OR close > close[1]) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish CVD divergence occurred within the divergence-recency lookback) AND bars since prior long signal ≥ same-side cooldown parameter
Short: confirmed chart bar AND positive volume observed on this or a prior chart bar AND Pressure crosses below zero AND abs(bar delta) ≥ SMA(abs(bar delta), delta-expansion lookback) × expansion multiplier AND SMA(abs(bar delta), delta-expansion lookback) > 0 AND (price-confirmation filter disabled OR close < close[1]) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish CVD divergence occurred within the divergence-recency lookback) AND bars since prior short signal ≥ same-side cooldown parameter
