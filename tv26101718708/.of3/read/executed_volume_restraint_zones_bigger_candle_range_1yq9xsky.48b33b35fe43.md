Calculation: BodyFraction = high > low ? abs(close - open) / (high - low) : 0
Calculation: VolumeProfilePOC = midpoint of the lowest-price bin with the greatest total allocated volume across the profile lookback of chart bars; divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over those bars into the bin count, flooring the range at the traded instrument's minimum price increment; for each bin, sum volume[n] * max(min(high[n], bin high) - max(low[n], bin low), 0) / max(high[n] - low[n], minimum price increment) over the same bars
Long: bar datetime >= current time - zone lookback duration AND volume > minimum actual volume AND volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) >= relative volume multiplier AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum wick fraction AND (BodyFraction <= maximum body fraction OR (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= strong wick fraction)
Short: bar datetime >= current time - zone lookback duration AND volume > minimum actual volume AND volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) >= relative volume multiplier AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum wick fraction AND (BodyFraction <= maximum body fraction OR (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= strong wick fraction)
Long: more than profile lookback chart bars exist AND close <= VolumeProfilePOC(profile lookback, bin count)
Short: more than profile lookback chart bars exist AND close > VolumeProfilePOC(profile lookback, bin count)
