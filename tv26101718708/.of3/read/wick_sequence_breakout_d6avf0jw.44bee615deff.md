Calculation: upper wick share = if high > low then 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low), else 0
Calculation: lower wick share = if high > low then 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low), else 0
Calculation: bearish wick candle = upper wick share ≥ bearish minimum upper-wick share AND lower wick share ≤ bearish maximum lower-wick share AND (formation ATR filter disabled OR high − low ≥ ATR(formation ATR lookback) × formation ATR multiple)
Calculation: bullish wick candle = lower wick share ≥ bullish minimum lower-wick share AND upper wick share ≤ bullish maximum upper-wick share AND (formation ATR filter disabled OR high − low ≥ ATR(formation ATR lookback) × formation ATR multiple)
Calculation: bearish pattern = count(bearish wick candles in the current and preceding window-size chart bars) ≥ minimum wick-candle count AND count(bullish wick candles in those bars) < minimum wick-candle count
Calculation: bullish pattern = count(bullish wick candles in the current and preceding window-size chart bars) ≥ minimum wick-candle count AND count(bearish wick candles in those bars) < minimum wick-candle count
Calculation: active formation box = when no box is active, create a box if either pattern is true now and was false on the preceding chart bar; set its top to highest(high, window size) and bottom to lowest(low, window size) over those bars; keep it active until its first breakout, whether or not that breakout signals
Calculation: box buy volume = sum over overlapping formation bars and subsequent overlapping chart bars before the first breakout of volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick size), where overlap means high > box bottom AND low < box top
Calculation: box sell volume = sum over overlapping formation bars and subsequent overlapping chart bars before the first breakout of volume × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick size), where overlap means high > box bottom AND low < box top
Long: bullish signals enabled AND active formation box AND (close > box top in close-breakout mode, otherwise high > box top) AND box buy volume ≥ box sell volume AND (breakout size filter disabled OR high − low ≥ SMA(high − low, average-range lookback)[1] × breakout size multiple)
Short: bearish signals enabled AND active formation box AND (close < box bottom in close-breakout mode, otherwise low < box bottom) AND box sell volume > box buy volume AND (breakout size filter disabled OR high − low ≥ SMA(high − low, average-range lookback)[1] × breakout size multiple)
Calculation: stop distance = ATR(stop ATR lookback) × stop ATR multiple when ATR-based stops are selected, otherwise entry price × stop percentage / 100
Exit: long, target/stop tracking enabled for the signal AND current bar is after its signal bar AND (low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR (a take-profit level is enabled AND high ≥ entry price + stop distance × the reward multiple for TP3 if enabled, otherwise TP2 if enabled, otherwise TP1))
Exit: short, target/stop tracking enabled for the signal AND current bar is after its signal bar AND (high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR (a take-profit level is enabled AND low ≤ entry price − stop distance × the reward multiple for TP3 if enabled, otherwise TP2 if enabled, otherwise TP1))
