Calculation: Indecision score = for each chart bar, update separate Doji and Spinning Top scores as (prior score + number of matches among bars 1 through lookback) / lookback, with each score initialized at zero; Doji matches when abs(close[n] - open[n]) / (high[n] - low[n]) < doji threshold; Spinning Top matches when that ratio < spinning top threshold AND >= doji threshold; the score is the average of the two updated scores.
Calculation: Fear score = for each chart bar, update separate Shooting Star, Hanging Man, and Bearish Engulfing scores as (prior score + number of matches among bars 1 through lookback) / lookback, with each score initialized at zero; on bar n, let body = abs(close[n] - open[n]), upper wick = high[n] - max(open[n], close[n]), and lower wick = min(open[n], close[n]) - low[n]; Shooting Star matches when upper wick / body > shooting star threshold AND lower wick < body; Hanging Man matches when lower wick / body > hanging man threshold AND upper wick < body; Bearish Engulfing matches when close[n] < open[n+1] AND open[n] > close[n+1] AND body / abs(close[n+1] - open[n+1]) > engulfing threshold; the score is the average of the three updated scores.
Calculation: Greed score = for each chart bar, update separate Marubozu, Hammer, Bullish Engulfing, and Three White Soldiers scores as (prior score + number of matches among bars 1 through lookback) / lookback, with each score initialized at zero; on bar n, let body = abs(close[n] - open[n]), upper wick = high[n] - max(open[n], close[n]), and lower wick = min(open[n], close[n]) - low[n]; Marubozu matches when body / (high[n] - low[n]) > marubozu threshold; Hammer matches when lower wick / body > hammer threshold AND upper wick < body; Bullish Engulfing matches when close[n] > open[n+1] AND open[n] < close[n+1] AND body / abs(close[n+1] - open[n+1]) > engulfing threshold; Three White Soldiers matches when close[n] > open[n] AND close[n+1] > open[n+1] AND close[n+2] > open[n+2] AND close[n] > close[n+1] AND close[n+1] > close[n+2]; the score is the average of the four updated scores.
Calculation: Candle Emotion Index = (Indecision score + Fear score + Greed score) / 3
Long: Candle Emotion Index crosses above the long entry threshold AND position is flat AND at least the cooldown period in chart bars has elapsed since the most recent entry or exit
Short: Candle Emotion Index crosses above the short entry threshold AND position is flat AND at least the cooldown period in chart bars has elapsed since the most recent entry or exit
Exit: Long, close > open AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * volume multiplier AND close > entry price
Exit: Long, current long-position holding count >= maximum holding period AND close >= entry price
Exit: Long, current long-position holding count >= loss holding period AND close < entry price * (1 - loss threshold)
Exit: Short, close < open AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * volume multiplier AND close < entry price
Exit: Short, current short-position holding count >= maximum holding period AND close <= entry price
Exit: Short, current short-position holding count >= loss holding period AND close > entry price * (1 + loss threshold)
