Calculation: acceptance line = impulse low + hold fraction × (impulse high − impulse low) for a bullish impulse; impulse high − hold fraction × (impulse high − impulse low) for a bearish impulse
Calculation: required extension = impulse high + minimum extension ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, previous completed chart bar at the impulse) for a bullish impulse; impulse low − minimum extension ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, previous completed chart bar at the impulse) for a bearish impulse
Calculation: follow-through efficiency = max(0, close − impulse close) ÷ cumulative absolute close-to-close path since the impulse close × 100 for a bullish impulse; max(0, impulse close − close) ÷ cumulative absolute close-to-close path since the impulse close × 100 for a bearish impulse; 0 when the path is 0
Long: At a confirmed chart-bar close, when no impulse observation was active at the start of the bar, after any prior observation has ended by acceptance, a close beyond the acceptance line against its direction, or reaching the observation-window bar count: high > low AND ATR(ATR lookback, previous completed chart bar) > 0 AND high − low ≥ minimum range ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, previous completed chart bar) AND abs(close − open) ÷ (high − low) × 100 ≥ minimum body percentage AND close > open AND close ≥ high − extreme-close fraction × (high − low) AND (volume filter disabled OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > 0 AND volume ≥ minimum relative-volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume-average lookback))
Short: At a confirmed chart-bar close, when no impulse observation was active at the start of the bar, after any prior observation has ended by acceptance, a close beyond the acceptance line against its direction, or reaching the observation-window bar count: high > low AND ATR(ATR lookback, previous completed chart bar) > 0 AND high − low ≥ minimum range ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, previous completed chart bar) AND abs(close − open) ÷ (high − low) × 100 ≥ minimum body percentage AND close < open AND close ≤ low + extreme-close fraction × (high − low) AND (volume filter disabled OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > 0 AND volume ≥ minimum relative-volume multiple × SMA(volume, volume-average lookback))
Long: At a confirmed chart-bar close during a bullish impulse observation, the required number of consecutive post-impulse closes through the current close are strictly above the acceptance line AND high ≥ required extension AND follow-through efficiency ≥ minimum efficiency percentage
Short: At a confirmed chart-bar close during a bearish impulse observation, the required number of consecutive post-impulse closes through the current close are strictly below the acceptance line AND low ≤ required extension AND follow-through efficiency ≥ minimum efficiency percentage
Exit: long, At a confirmed chart-bar close during a bullish impulse observation, close < acceptance line
Exit: short, At a confirmed chart-bar close during a bearish impulse observation, close > acceptance line
