Calculation: Supply zone = body range [min(open, close), max(open, close)] of the nearest preceding T2 bar within the order-block lookback whose close > open OR abs(close - open) < doji body fraction × ATR(ATR lookback, T2); these calculations run on T2 bars and are sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T2 bar historically, forming T2 bar in realtime)
