Calculation: Upward magnet break = primary magnet touch count ≥ minimum touches AND close > current primary magnet + current ATR(ATR lookback) × break margin AND close[1] ≤ current primary magnet + current ATR(ATR lookback) × break margin AND volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier
Calculation: Downward magnet break = primary magnet touch count ≥ minimum touches AND close < current primary magnet − current ATR(ATR lookback) × break margin AND close[1] ≥ current primary magnet − current ATR(ATR lookback) × break margin AND volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier
Calculation: Magnet score = round(min(min(primary magnet touch count × touch-score weight, touch-score cap) + min(max((volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) − 1) × volume-score weight, 0), volume-score cap) + (break-score bonus if an upward or downward magnet break occurs, otherwise 0), total-score cap))
