Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVGs = on each new T1 bar, use the last completed T1 bar and the T1 bar three bars ago: bullish when low[1] > high[3], with bounds [high[3], low[1]]; bearish when high[1] < low[3], with bounds [high[1], low[3]]. T1 is the chart timeframe multiplied by its configured factor and snapped to a standard resolution. On chart bars, a bullish gap becomes invalid when close < its lower bound, a bearish gap when close > its upper bound; a tap occurs when high >= the gap’s lower bound and low <= its upper bound. Retain only the configured maximum number of these gaps.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = on chart bars, confirmed pivot highs/lows using the pivot-strength parameter on each side; when equal-level detection is enabled and a pivot is within ATR(ATR lookback) × equal-level-tolerance parameter of the previous same-side pivot, create an equal-high/low level, otherwise create a swing level if enabled. Also include the previous-period high/low from the last completed T2 bar when enabled (T2 is the previous-day source timeframe), and the high/low of each configured session window after it ends when enabled. Retain only the configured maximum number of levels.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = a high level is swept when high > level and close <= level; a low level is swept when low < level and close >= level. A close above a high level or below a low level breaks it; swept or broken levels are no longer active for clear-path checks.
Calculation: V-shape context(direction, zone) = a still-valid matching-direction higher-timeframe FVG was tapped within the HTF-tap window of chart bars, and the zone overlaps that FVG expanded by ATR(ATR lookback) × HTF-proximity parameter; OR, when sweep context is enabled, a matching-side liquidity level was swept within the sweep window of chart bars. For a long, matching-side means a low level; for a short, a high level.
Calculation: Reclaimed fraction(direction, displacement open, displacement close, inversion close) = 1 when abs(displacement open − displacement close) = 0; for a long, (inversion close − displacement close) / (displacement open − displacement close); for a short, (displacement close − inversion close) / (displacement close − displacement open).
Calculation: BPR(new FVG, inverted FVG) = overlap with positive size between their price bounds, where overlap size is min(upper bounds) − max(lower bounds) and is at least ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-overlap parameter. A new bullish FVG forms when low > high[2]; a new bearish FVG forms when high < low[2]. Pair it with the most recent unused opposite-direction FVG whose close crossed beyond its far bound within the FVG-age lookback; each qualifying inverted FVG is used once. A qualifying pair rejected by the context filter while non-V BPRs are hidden still consumes its inverted FVG.
Long: bullish BPR forms AND (V-shape gate is disabled OR non-V BPRs are shown OR V-shape context(bullish, overlap zone)).
Short: bearish BPR forms AND (V-shape gate is disabled OR non-V BPRs are shown OR V-shape context(bearish, overlap zone)).
Long: on a confirmed chart-bar close, an untriggered bearish FVG crosses above its upper bound (close > upper bound and close[1] <= upper bound) within the FVG-age lookback from its displacement candle AND the prior chart candle is bearish (close[1] < open[1]) AND Reclaimed fraction(long, that FVG’s displacement open, that FVG’s displacement close, close) >= strong-close parameter AND V-shape context(bullish, that FVG zone) AND no unswept and unbroken liquidity low is below the current low within ATR(ATR lookback) × path-danger-zone parameter.
Short: on a confirmed chart-bar close, an untriggered bullish FVG crosses below its lower bound (close < lower bound and close[1] >= lower bound) within the FVG-age lookback from its displacement candle AND the prior chart candle is bullish (close[1] > open[1]) AND Reclaimed fraction(short, that FVG’s displacement open, that FVG’s displacement close, close) >= strong-close parameter AND V-shape context(bearish, that FVG zone) AND no unswept and unbroken liquidity high is above the current high within ATR(ATR lookback) × path-danger-zone parameter.
