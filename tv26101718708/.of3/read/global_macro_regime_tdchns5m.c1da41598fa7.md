Calculation: For each enabled, data-ready market, calculate a trend score of +1 when the selected moving average of close at the shorter lookback minus the selected moving average at the longer lookback exceeds the margin parameter times ATR at the longer lookback, -1 when it is below the negative of that threshold, and 0 otherwise; calculate a volatility score of +1 when close is at or above Volatility Stop(close, selected volatility measure, stop lookback, stop factor) and -1 otherwise, where the measure is ATR, standard deviation of close, or the average absolute deviation of close from its SMA at the stop lookback; the market score is the trend score, volatility score, or their sum according to the selected method, provided the required calculations are ready. The moving average type is shared across both lengths. A positive score is bullish and a negative score bearish; a zero score contributes nothing when neutral regimes are enabled and is bullish when they are disabled. Calculate these scores on each market's T1 bars using the current T1 bar, which may still be forming live, and pass the values to chart bars.
Calculation: For each regime, sum contributions from enabled, data-ready markets with nonzero directional signals: bullish or bearish signals from global equity indexes, the high-beta/low-volatility ratio, the cyclical/defensive ratio, industrial metal prices, the silver/gold ratio, copper prices, bitcoin prices, and Australian, euro, and British pound exchange rates contribute respectively to Goldilocks/Reflation or Inflation/Deflation; signals from equity volatility, bond volatility, implied correlation, the US dollar index, and US credit spreads contribute respectively to Inflation/Deflation or Goldilocks/Reflation; signals from Brent crude, agricultural commodity prices, German, UK, and Japanese 10-year yields, US 2-year and 10-year yields, and the US 10-year breakeven rate contribute respectively to Reflation/Inflation or Goldilocks/Deflation. Divide each regime's contribution sum by the sum of all four regimes' contributions and multiply by 100, then apply SMA over the smoothing lookback on chart bars using the T1 values; a zero smoothing input uses a lookback of one.
Calculation: Dominant regime = the regime with the greatest smoothed share when all shares are available; retain the prior regime while it remains tied for greatest, otherwise resolve ties in the order Goldilocks, Reflation, Inflation, Deflation. Retain the prior regime when shares are unavailable.
Calculation: Allocated asset = the data-ready asset selected for the dominant regime on the preceding chart bar, requiring its selected open or close price to be available on the current and preceding chart bars. If no regime asset is assigned, use the chart instrument for Goldilocks and Reflation and cash for Inflation and Deflation; if any regime asset is assigned, use each assigned asset and cash for unassigned regimes.
Long: allocated asset is present and the preceding chart bar's allocated asset is absent or different.
Exit: long, allocated asset is absent or differs from the preceding chart bar's allocated asset.
