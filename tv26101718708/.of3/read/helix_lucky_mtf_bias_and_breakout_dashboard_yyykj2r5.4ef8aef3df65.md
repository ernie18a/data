Calculation: AdaptiveSupertrendDirection uses ATR(period) or SMA(true range, period), according to the ATR mode. Let volume weight = volume / SMA(volume, period), or 1 when that SMA is zero; multiplier = base multiplier * (correlation offset + abs(correlation(close, bar index, period)) * correlation scale) * sqrt(max(volume floor, volume weight)); raw up and down bands are source - ATR * multiplier and source + ATR * multiplier. Each band persists by taking the maximum of the raw up band and prior up band when close is above the prior up band, or the minimum of the raw down band and prior down band when close is below the prior down band; otherwise use its raw band.
