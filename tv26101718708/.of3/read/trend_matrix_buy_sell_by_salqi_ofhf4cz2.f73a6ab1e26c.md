Calculation: bullish score = count of chart closes above each EMA with short, intermediate, and long lookbacks, calculated on the current T1, T2, or T3 bar and mapped to chart bars; use the same lookbacks on each timeframe, with short < intermediate < long
Calculation: bearish score = count of chart closes at or below those same EMA values
Calculation: upward breakout = chart close >= the last completed T5 close mapped to chart bars plus the configured breakout percentage of that close
Calculation: downward breakout = chart close <= the last completed T5 close mapped to chart bars minus the configured breakout percentage of that close
Long: in trend-change mode AND bullish score >= bullish score threshold AND bullish score on the previous chart bar < bullish score threshold AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR chart close > VWAP(hlc3) calculated on the current T4 bar and mapped to chart bars) AND (close confirmation disabled OR current chart bar is confirmed) AND (strict alternation disabled OR most recent signal is not long)
Long: in strong-trend-only mode AND bullish score >= strong-trend score threshold AND bullish score on the previous chart bar < strong-trend score threshold AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR chart close > VWAP(hlc3) calculated on the current T4 bar and mapped to chart bars) AND (close confirmation disabled OR current chart bar is confirmed) AND (strict alternation disabled OR most recent signal is not long)
Long: in breakout-confirmed mode AND bullish score >= bullish score threshold AND upward breakout AND NOT (bullish score on the previous chart bar >= bullish score threshold AND upward breakout on the previous chart bar) AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR chart close > VWAP(hlc3) calculated on the current T4 bar and mapped to chart bars) AND (close confirmation disabled OR current chart bar is confirmed) AND (strict alternation disabled OR most recent signal is not long)
