Calculation: Stop price(side) = using the close of the opening bar as entry price, select by stop-loss mode: fixed percentage gives entry price * (1 - stop percentage) for long or entry price * (1 + stop percentage) for short; swing gives lowest(low, swing lookback) for long or highest(high, swing lookback) for short on the opening bar; ATR gives entry price minus ATR(stop lookback) * stop multiplier for long or plus it for short, with ATR measured on the opening bar
Calculation: Take-profit target(side) = using the close of the opening bar as entry price, select by take-profit mode: fixed percentage gives entry price * (1 + target percentage) for long or entry price * (1 - target percentage) for short; risk-reward gives entry price + (entry price - Stop price(side)) * risk-reward multiple; ATR gives entry price plus ATR(target lookback) * target multiplier for long or minus it for short, with ATR measured on the opening bar
Long: EMA(close, short lookback) crosses above EMA(close, long lookback) AND DPO(close, cycle lookback, centering mode) > 0 AND DPO(close, cycle lookback, centering mode)[1] > 0 AND DPO(close, cycle lookback, centering mode)[2] > 0 AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA filter source > EMA(EMA filter source, EMA filter lookback)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ((ADX variant is ORIGINAL AND ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold) OR (ADX variant is MASANAKAMURA AND ADX(DI lookback, DI lookback) > ADX threshold))) AND long trading enabled AND position side is not long
Short: EMA(close, short lookback) crosses below EMA(close, long lookback) AND DPO(close, cycle lookback, centering mode) < 0 AND DPO(close, cycle lookback, centering mode)[1] < 0 AND DPO(close, cycle lookback, centering mode)[2] < 0 AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA filter source < EMA(EMA filter source, EMA filter lookback)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ((ADX variant is ORIGINAL AND ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold) OR (ADX variant is MASANAKAMURA AND ADX(DI lookback, DI lookback) > ADX threshold))) AND short trading enabled AND position side is not short
Exit: long, (stop loss enabled AND close <= Stop price(long)) OR (take profit enabled AND (((take-profit mode is fixed percentage OR take-profit mode is risk-reward OR take-profit mode is ATR) AND close >= Take-profit target(long)) OR (take-profit mode is multiple fixed percentages AND (high >= entry price + round(entry price * first target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment OR high >= entry price + round(entry price * second target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment OR high >= entry price + round(entry price * third target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment OR high >= entry price + round(entry price * fourth target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment))))
Exit: short, (stop loss enabled AND close >= Stop price(short)) OR (take profit enabled AND (((take-profit mode is fixed percentage OR take-profit mode is risk-reward OR take-profit mode is ATR) AND close <= Take-profit target(short)) OR (take-profit mode is multiple fixed percentages AND (low <= entry price - round(entry price * first target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment OR low <= entry price - round(entry price * second target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment OR low <= entry price - round(entry price * third target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment OR low <= entry price - round(entry price * fourth target percentage / 100 / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment))))
