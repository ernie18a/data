Calculation: Live resistance = the most recently confirmed PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback); on a confirmed bar with ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, test the prior level first and end it if close > level + minimum-close-through parameter × ATR, then replace it with a newly confirmed pivot high if one exists.
Calculation: Live support = the most recently confirmed PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback); on a confirmed bar with ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, test the prior level first and end it if close < level - minimum-close-through parameter × ATR, then replace it with a newly confirmed pivot low if one exists.
Calculation: Break score(direction, level) = one point for each passed test: participation (volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) >= relative-volume threshold when that SMA is defined and > 0, otherwise high - low >= no-volume-range parameter × ATR); conviction (abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= body/range parameter, using zero when high = low, and close > open for up or close < open for down); displacement ((close - level) / ATR for up or (level - close) / ATR for down >= close-beyond-level parameter). Scores of one, two, and three correspond to tiers C, B, and A.
Calculation: Flip tolerance(level) = max(ATR on the level's pivot-confirmation bar × zone-width parameter, minimum tick size).
Calculation: Armed flip level = a live level whose break meets the minimum-tier parameter, created only if the retest or trap signal is enabled at the break; monitor it on confirmed bars from the next bar through the retest-window parameter bars inclusive, then remove it at the first retest or trap price condition or at the window end.
Long: On a confirmed bar, a live resistance exists, ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, close > resistance + minimum-close-through parameter × ATR, and the tier corresponding to Break score(up, resistance) is at least the minimum-tier parameter.
Short: On a confirmed bar, a live support exists, ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, close < support - minimum-close-through parameter × ATR, and the tier corresponding to Break score(down, support) is at least the minimum-tier parameter.
Long: On a confirmed bar, the retest signal is enabled, an armed resistance-origin flip level exists, low <= level + Flip tolerance(level), and close > level.
Short: On a confirmed bar, the retest signal is enabled, an armed support-origin flip level exists, high >= level - Flip tolerance(level), and close < level.
Long: On a confirmed bar, the trap signal is enabled, an armed support-origin flip level exists, and close > level + Flip tolerance(level).
Short: On a confirmed bar, the trap signal is enabled, an armed resistance-origin flip level exists, and close < level - Flip tolerance(level).
Long: On a confirmed bar, the sweep signal is enabled, a live support exists, ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, low < support, close > support, and low[1] >= support.
Short: On a confirmed bar, the sweep signal is enabled, a live resistance exists, ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, high > resistance, close < resistance, and high[1] <= resistance.
