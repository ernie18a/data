Calculation: EMA slope = EMA(close, shorter lookback) - EMA(close, shorter lookback)[slope lookback]
Calculation: Candle body percentage = if high = low then 0 else abs(close - open) / (high - low) * 100
Calculation: Daily equity change (%) = (current equity - day-start equity) / day-start equity * 100
Calculation: Entry window = bar open time is within the configured morning interval or afternoon interval ending at the last-entry time, inclusive, in the configured timezone
Calculation: Session-end bar = bar open time is before the configured session-end time and bar close time is at or after it, in the configured timezone
Long: A bearish reference bar is followed by bullish bars, each with low above the reference low and close above the preceding close
Short: A bullish reference bar is followed by bearish bars, each with high below the reference high and close below the preceding close
Long: A bearish candle is followed by a bullish candle
Short: A bullish candle is followed by a bearish candle
Long: A long setup is armed by the most recent bearish candle during the entry window while flat and while the daily loss guard is disabled or daily equity change is above the negative daily loss limit; it remains valid only within the setup lifetime, while EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) and close >= pullback low - invalidation buffer; a later confirmed bullish candle has body percentage >= minimum body percentage, and EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) and (slope filter disabled or EMA slope > 0), and (previous-bar-break filter disabled or close > high[1]), and (EMA-reclaim filter disabled or close > EMA(close, shorter lookback)), and (VWAP filter disabled or close > VWAP(hlc3)); long trading is enabled, the position is flat, the bar is confirmed, the entry window is open, the daily loss guard is disabled or daily equity change is above the negative daily loss limit, entries today are below the daily maximum, and bars since the previous entry exceed the cooldown or there was no previous entry
Short: A short setup is armed by the most recent bullish candle during the entry window while flat and while the daily loss guard is disabled or daily equity change is above the negative daily loss limit; it remains valid only within the setup lifetime, while EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) and close <= pullback high + invalidation buffer; a later confirmed bearish candle has body percentage >= minimum body percentage, and EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) and (slope filter disabled or EMA slope < 0), and (previous-bar-break filter disabled or close < low[1]), and (EMA-reclaim filter disabled or close < EMA(close, shorter lookback)), and (VWAP filter disabled or close < VWAP(hlc3)); short trading is enabled, the position is flat, the bar is confirmed, the entry window is open, the daily loss guard is disabled or daily equity change is above the negative daily loss limit, entries today are below the daily maximum, and bars since the previous entry exceed the cooldown or there was no previous entry
Exit: Long, low <= the active stop price OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR Session-end bar
Exit: Short, high >= the active stop price OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR Session-end bar
Exit: Long, after break-even is enabled and high reaches entry price + break-even trigger, low <= max(entry price - stop-loss distance, entry price + break-even offset)
Exit: Short, after break-even is enabled and low reaches entry price - break-even trigger, high >= min(entry price + stop-loss distance, entry price - break-even offset)
