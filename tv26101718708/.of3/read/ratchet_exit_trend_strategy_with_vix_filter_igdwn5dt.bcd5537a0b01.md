Calculation: VIX close = T1 close sampled on chart bars while the T1 bar is forming
Calculation: Long ratchet stop = while flat, reset stage and high-water mark; while long, update high-water mark to the highest high since entry, advance stage from initial to pinned when (close − entry price) / entry price × 100 ≥ first profit trigger and to trailing when it ≥ second profit trigger (stage never decreases); stage stop = entry price × (1 − initial stop distance / 100) in initial stage, entry price × (1 − pinned stop distance / 100) in pinned stage, or high-water mark × (1 − trailing distance / 100) in trailing stage; active stop = stage stop on the first long bar, otherwise max(previous active stop, stage stop)
Long: (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close < VIX threshold) AND (SMA(close, short lookback) crosses above SMA(close, trend lookback) OR (close > SMA(close, trend lookback) AND close[1] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[1] AND close[2] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[2] AND close[3] < SMA(close, trend lookback)[3]) OR (close > SMA(close, medium lookback) AND close[1] > SMA(close, medium lookback)[1] AND close[2] > SMA(close, medium lookback)[2] AND close[3] < SMA(close, medium lookback)[3]) OR (close > SMA(close, long lookback) AND close[1] > SMA(close, long lookback)[1] AND close[2] > SMA(close, long lookback)[2] AND close[3] < SMA(close, long lookback)[3]))
Exit: long, low ≤ Long ratchet stop
Exit: long, VIX filter enabled AND VIX close > VIX threshold
Exit: long, SMA(close, short lookback) crosses below SMA(close, trend lookback)
