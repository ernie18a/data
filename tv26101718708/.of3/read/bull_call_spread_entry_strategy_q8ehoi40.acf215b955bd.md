Calculation: Market change = 100 × (close(T1, current forming bar) − close(T1, previous completed bar)) / close(T1, previous completed bar), evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: Bull setup score = 0 if RSI(close, RSI lookback) > upper RSI threshold; otherwise the sum of 1 for each true condition: lower Bollinger Band < lower Keltner Channel AND upper Bollinger Band > upper Keltner Channel; ADX(high, low, close, DMI lookback, DMI smoothing) > its prior-bar value; RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its prior-bar value; −DI(high, low, close, DMI lookback, DMI smoothing) < its prior-bar value; RSI(close, RSI lookback) < lower RSI threshold; close < lower Keltner Channel; close < Bollinger basis AND close > lower Bollinger Band; Lowest(low, bounce lookback) <= lower Bollinger Band
Calculation: Bear setup score = 0 if RSI(close, RSI lookback) < lower RSI threshold; otherwise the negative sum of 1 for each true condition: lower Bollinger Band < lower Keltner Channel AND upper Bollinger Band > upper Keltner Channel; ADX(high, low, close, DMI lookback, DMI smoothing) < its prior-bar value; RSI(close, RSI lookback) < its prior-bar value; +DI(high, low, close, DMI lookback, DMI smoothing) > its prior-bar value; RSI(close, RSI lookback) > upper RSI threshold; close > upper Keltner Channel; close > Bollinger basis AND close < upper Bollinger Band; Highest(high, bounce lookback) >= upper Bollinger Band
Long: Bull setup score > 0
Short: Bear setup score < 0
Long: close < Bollinger basis AND Bull setup score >= entry strength threshold AND (market filter disabled OR (Market change < too-hot threshold AND Market change > too-cold threshold)) AND chart-bar datetime >= start datetime AND chart-bar datetime < end datetime AND position is flat or short; enter with a limit at the signal-bar close, filled only at a price <= that close during the next chart bar, otherwise cancel
Exit: Long, chart bars since entering long >= holding-bar parameter AND weekday is Thursday
