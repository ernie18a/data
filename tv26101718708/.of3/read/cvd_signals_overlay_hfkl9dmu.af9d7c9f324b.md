Calculation: CVD = on chart bars, reset to the selected bar's delta at each boundary chosen by the reset mode parameter (session/T1 share a boundary, or T2 or T3), otherwise add the selected bar's delta to the prior CVD value; initialize CVD at zero. The delta method parameter selects volume if close >= open and -volume otherwise; volume * (2*close - high - low) / (high - low) when high > low and 0 otherwise; or volume if close > close[1], -volume if close < close[1], and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: CVD MA = the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or VWMA of CVD over the MA lookback parameter on chart bars; for EMA, initialize to CVD on the first bar or at each selected CVD reset boundary, then use alpha * CVD + (1 - alpha) * prior CVD MA, where alpha = 2 / (MA lookback parameter + 1).
Long: CVD crosses above CVD MA AND (no prior CVD cross below CVD MA OR at least the signal spacing parameter bars have elapsed since the most recent CVD cross below CVD MA)
Short: CVD crosses below CVD MA AND (no prior CVD cross above CVD MA OR at least the signal spacing parameter bars have elapsed since the most recent CVD cross above CVD MA)
