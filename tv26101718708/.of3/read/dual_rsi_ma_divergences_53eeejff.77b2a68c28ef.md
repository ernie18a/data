Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence(RSI series, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) = RSI series has a confirmed pivot high, its value at that pivot is less than its value at the previous confirmed pivot high, and high at that pivot is greater than high at the previous confirmed pivot high
Long: Bullish RSI divergence(RSI(close, longer lookback), left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) OR Bullish RSI divergence(RSI(close, shorter lookback), left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback)
