Calculation: Leverage evidence = Positive level(max(ln(max((Response evidence + vacuum epsilon level) / (Effort evidence + vacuum epsilon level), ratio floor)), 0), ln(max(vacuum ratio reference, 1 + reference floor)), vacuum leverage curve)
Calculation: Bull-vacuum evidence = Weighted geometric evidence(Leverage evidence / 100, Response evidence / 100, Path-efficiency evidence / 100, bullish directional evidence; vacuum leverage, response, efficiency, and direction weights); bear-vacuum evidence uses bearish directional evidence and the same weights
Calculation: Active candidate = highest of bull-drive, bear-drive, bull-absorption, bear-absorption, and churn evidence, with ties resolved in that order; separation = highest evidence − second-highest evidence
Calculation: Confirmed regime = a state machine initialized to Transition and updated only on confirmed chart bars. In Transition, enter the active candidate when its evidence is at least the active-entry threshold and its separation is at least the active-entry separation; otherwise enter Balance when Balance evidence is at least the balance-entry threshold and the highest active evidence is below the active-entry threshold; otherwise remain Transition. In Balance, enter an active candidate only after the same qualifying candidate persists for the balance-escape confirmation bars; otherwise remain Balance while Balance evidence is at least the balance-hold threshold, and move to Transition when it is lower. In any active regime, a different candidate can switch the regime when its evidence meets the active-entry threshold, its separation meets the active-entry separation, and its evidence exceeds the current regime's evidence by the active-switch margin for the active-switch confirmation bars. If no switch commits, Bull Drive loses ownership after Response Field is at or below zero for the semantic-field guard bars, and Bear Drive after Response Field is at or above zero for those bars; on that guard exit, enter Balance only when Balance evidence meets the active-to-balance threshold and highest active evidence is below the active-entry threshold, otherwise enter Transition. Otherwise, an active regime holds while its evidence meets the active-hold threshold. When it is lower, count consecutive weak bars and, separately, consecutive bars where Balance evidence meets the active-to-balance threshold and highest active evidence is below the active-entry threshold; after the active-exit confirmation bars, enter Balance if the latter count meets the active-to-balance confirmation bars, otherwise enter Transition.
