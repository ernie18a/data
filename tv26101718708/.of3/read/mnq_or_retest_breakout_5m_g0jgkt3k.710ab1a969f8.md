Calculation: Lower opening-range level = lowest low in the configured opening-range build window of the current NY or Asia session
Calculation: Opening-range width in ticks = round((upper opening-range level - lower opening-range level) / minimum tick size)
Calculation: Bullish gap zone = [high[2], low] of the latest T1 bar in the current session where low > high[2]
Calculation: Bearish gap zone = [high, low[2]] of the latest T1 bar in the current session where high < low[2]
Calculation: Upward breakout event = a bullish T1 bar during the active NY or Asia trade window, after its opening-range build window, with an available opening range, manual skip disabled, opening-range width in ticks below its maximum, session trade allowance available, no active upward breakout, and close > upper opening-range level + minimum breakout distance in ticks × minimum tick size
