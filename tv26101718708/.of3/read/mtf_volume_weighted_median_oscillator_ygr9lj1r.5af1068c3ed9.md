Calculation: range base = max(EMA(high - low, range lookback), minimum tick), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically
Calculation: main line = (volume-weighted median - EMA(volume-weighted median, length × multiplier parameter)) / range base, optionally WMA-smoothed over the smoothing lookback when smoothing is enabled; calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically
Calculation: histogram = (close - volume-weighted median) / range base, optionally WMA-smoothed over the smoothing lookback when smoothing is enabled; calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically
