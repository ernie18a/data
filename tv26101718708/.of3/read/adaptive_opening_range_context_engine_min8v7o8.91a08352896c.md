Calculation: opening-range high = highest(high) during the opening-range session when the range-source parameter selects high/low; otherwise highest(max(open, close)) during that session
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest(low) during the opening-range session when the range-source parameter selects high/low; otherwise lowest(min(open, close)) during that session
Calculation: opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2
Calculation: opening-range size = opening-range high - opening-range low
Long: on a confirmed supported intraday chart bar after the opening-range lock bar and during the display session, no directional attempt is active AND close > opening-range high; OR an upper attempt is active and not yet accepted AND close > opening-range high AND consecutive closes above the high during the attempt meet the acceptance-bars parameter; OR an upper attempt is active and not yet retested AND close = opening-range high AND low <= opening-range high + max(ATR(lookback), minimum tick size) * retest-tolerance parameter; OR an upper retest has occurred and expansion has not yet occurred AND close > opening-range high + opening-range size * extension parameter
Short: on a confirmed supported intraday chart bar after the opening-range lock bar and during the display session, no directional attempt is active AND close < opening-range low; OR a lower attempt is active and not yet accepted AND close < opening-range low AND consecutive closes below the low during the attempt meet the acceptance-bars parameter; OR a lower attempt is active and not yet retested AND close = opening-range low AND high >= opening-range low - max(ATR(lookback), minimum tick size) * retest-tolerance parameter; OR a lower retest has occurred and expansion has not yet occurred AND close < opening-range low - opening-range size * extension parameter
Exit: long, on a confirmed supported intraday chart bar after the opening-range lock bar and during the display session, an upper attempt is active AND close < opening-range midpoint
Exit: short, on a confirmed supported intraday chart bar after the opening-range lock bar and during the display session, a lower attempt is active AND close > opening-range midpoint
