Calculation: Setup support = the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) while setup stage is 0; hold when a reversal starts and clear on setup invalidation or long entry.
Calculation: Reversal high = the high of the reversal bar; hold until setup invalidation or long entry.
Calculation: Setup stage = 0 with no active setup; set to 1 when low <= setup support × (1 + support-zone tolerance / 100), close > open, and close > high[1]; set to 2 when stage is 1 and a confirmed PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) > setup support; set to 3 when stage is 2 and close > reversal high; evaluate the long condition before resetting to 0 when close < setup support while stage > 0 or on long entry.
Calculation: Signal ATR = ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) on the long-signal bar, retained for the position.
Calculation: Trade support = setup support retained for the position when available.
Long: position is flat AND setup stage = 3 AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, mid lookback) AND ((close > EMA(close, mid lookback) AND EMA(close, mid lookback) > EMA(close, mid lookback)[slope lookback]) OR (close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, mid lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback) AND EMA(close, trend lookback) > EMA(close, trend lookback)[slope lookback])) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(close, reset at each date boundary)) AND (market filter disabled OR selected market-series close > EMA(selected market-series close, external filter lookback, chart timeframe)) AND (sector filter disabled OR selected sector-series close > EMA(selected sector-series close, external filter lookback, chart timeframe)); fast, mid, slow, and trend EMA lookbacks are progressively longer.
Exit: Long, low <= MIN(MAX(if structure stop enabled and trade support exists then trade support − stop buffer × signal ATR else entry price − maximum-risk ATR × signal ATR, entry price − maximum-risk ATR × signal ATR), price one minimum increment below entry price)
Exit: Long, EMA exit enabled AND close < EMA(close, mid lookback)
Exit: Long, support-break exit enabled AND trade support exists AND close < trade support
Exit: Long, take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price + target ATR multiple × signal ATR
Exit: Long, maximum holding period set AND chart bars since entry >= maximum holding period in chart bars
