Calculation: Opening balance = highest(high) and lowest(low) of completed chart bars within the selected opening window from regular-session start; require minimum accumulation bars and high > low.
Calculation: Adaptive window lengths = candidate bars = max(minimum accumulation bars, ceil(candidate duration / chart-bar duration)); containment bars = min(candidate bars - 1, max(1, ceil(containment duration / chart-bar duration))); seed bars = candidate bars - containment bars.
Calculation: Adaptive cutoff = nearest-rank percentile at the selected range percentile of the latest baseline sample count eligible candidate-window ranges; candidate-window range = highest(high, candidate bars) - lowest(low, candidate bars); require a full baseline before scanning.
Calculation: Adaptive balance = highest(high) and lowest(low) over the seed bars preceding the latest containment bars, when those containment bars stay within the seed range, the candidate-window range is at most the adaptive cutoff, and seed high > seed low; scan completed regular-session bars before the last regular-session bar, and after a prior cycle only after a close outside its balance.
Calculation: Calendar balances = for each separate pair (T1,C1), (T2,C2), (T3,C3), and (T4,C4), highest(high) and lowest(low) of the first minimum accumulation bar count completed T bars in each C calendar container; process subsequent bars on T bars and read the result on chart bars using the last completed T bar.
