Calculation: ZoneHalfWidth(level) = configured tick width in minimum tick units or configured percentage of level, according to zone mode
Calculation: ZoneTouches(high, low, level) = high >= level - ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND low <= level + ZoneHalfWidth(level)
Calculation: ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) = (close - low) / (high - low), or the midpoint when high = low
Calculation: LongBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) = ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) >= upper close-position threshold OR (ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) >= midpoint threshold AND close > open); upper and lower thresholds are equidistant from the midpoint
Calculation: ShortBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) = ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) <= lower close-position threshold OR (ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) <= midpoint threshold AND close < open)
Calculation: RetestDistance(level) = ZoneHalfWidth(level) times the configured excursion multiplier
Calculation: DevelopingPOC = close of the T2 bar with greatest volume in the current T1 period, updating to the latest T2 close on volume ties; computed on T2 bars and mapped to chart bars
Calculation: DevelopingDelta = sum within the current T1 period of T2 volume when T2 close > T2 open, minus T2 volume when T2 close < T2 open; doji T2 bars contribute zero; computed on T2 bars and mapped to chart bars
Calculation: ExcursionFromAbove(level, distance, lookback) = any low in the configured number of chart bars before the arrival and confirmation bars <= level - distance
Calculation: ExcursionFromBelow(level, distance, lookback) = any high in the configured number of chart bars before the arrival and confirmation bars >= level + distance
Calculation: AcceptedAbove(level, lookback) = any close in the configured number of chart bars before the arrival and confirmation bars > level
Calculation: AcceptedBelow(level, lookback) = any close in the configured number of chart bars before the arrival and confirmation bars < level
Calculation: MergedOHLC = open of the arrival chart bar, maximum high of the arrival and confirmation bars, minimum low of the arrival and confirmation bars, and close of the confirmation bar
Calculation: LongArrivalValid(level) = level is configured Long AND close[2] > level - ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND the arrival bar overlaps the zone AND (current-bar zone touch is not required OR the confirmation bar overlaps the zone) AND arrival close > arrival open AND (arrival wick dominance is not required OR arrival lower wick > arrival upper wick)
Calculation: ShortArrivalValid(level) = level is configured Short AND close[2] < level + ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND the arrival bar overlaps the zone AND (current-bar zone touch is not required OR the confirmation bar overlaps the zone) AND arrival close < arrival open AND (arrival wick dominance is not required OR arrival upper wick > arrival lower wick)
Calculation: LongReactionApproachOK(level) = reaction approach-side filter disabled OR ExcursionFromAbove(level, RetestDistance(level), configured reaction approach lookback)
Calculation: ShortReactionApproachOK(level) = reaction approach-side filter disabled OR ExcursionFromBelow(level, RetestDistance(level), configured reaction approach lookback)
Calculation: LongOptionalBiasScore = number of true conditions among: session filter disabled OR all-day enabled OR within an enabled session; T1 direction filter disabled OR current T1 close > current T1 open; delta filter disabled OR DevelopingDelta > 0 OR neutral delta allowed AND absolute delta percentage <= configured neutral threshold; POC filter disabled OR chart close >= DevelopingPOC + configured price buffer. Delta percentage is DevelopingDelta divided by current T1 volume times one hundred, or zero when T1 volume is zero. T1 values are requested on chart bars from the current developing T1 bar.
Calculation: ShortOptionalBiasScore = number of true conditions among: session filter disabled OR all-day enabled OR within an enabled session; T1 direction filter disabled OR current T1 close < current T1 open; delta filter disabled OR DevelopingDelta < 0 OR neutral delta allowed AND absolute delta percentage <= configured neutral threshold; POC filter disabled OR chart close <= DevelopingPOC - configured price buffer. Delta percentage is DevelopingDelta divided by current T1 volume times one hundred, or zero when T1 volume is zero. T1 values are requested on chart bars from the current developing T1 bar.
Long: Any configured, enabled Long level is unlocked and satisfies (LongArrivalValid(level) AND LongReactionApproachOK(level) AND confirmation high >= arrival high AND LongBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close > level) AND ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) >= midpoint threshold AND LongOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score) OR (breakout enabled AND LongReactionApproachOK(level) AND close[2] > level - ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND arrival bar overlaps the zone AND NOT LongArrivalValid(level) AND confirmation high > arrival high AND LongBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close > level) AND ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) >= midpoint threshold AND LongOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score AND current volume > configured breakout volume multiplier times the selected baseline, which is either arrival-bar volume or prior-bar SMA of chart volume) OR (merged setup enabled AND close[2] > level - ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND MergedOHLC range overlaps the zone AND (merged-body requirement disabled OR LongBodyDirectionOK(MergedOHLC)) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close > level) AND ClosePosition(MergedOHLC) >= midpoint threshold AND LongOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score) OR (merged setup enabled AND close[2] > level - ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND MergedOHLC range overlaps the zone AND (merged-body requirement disabled OR LongBodyDirectionOK(MergedOHLC)) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close > level) AND LongOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score); OR any configured, enabled Short level is unlocked and satisfies (breakout-retest setup enabled AND its BRL has not been consumed since price last reached level - RetestDistance(level) or the reset-cycle boundary AND ExcursionFromBelow(level, RetestDistance(level), configured retest lookback) AND AcceptedAbove(level, configured retest lookback) AND (retest touch is not required OR confirmation or arrival bar overlaps the zone) AND LongBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) AND ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) >= midpoint threshold AND close > level AND LongOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score). At least one qualifying setup must also pass the global long-signal spacing gate: no prior long signal OR chart bars since the prior long signal > configured spacing OR (current low < prior long-signal candle low AND current close > prior long-signal candle close).
Short: Any configured, enabled Short level is unlocked and satisfies (ShortArrivalValid(level) AND ShortReactionApproachOK(level) AND confirmation low <= arrival low AND ShortBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close < level) AND ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) <= midpoint threshold AND ShortOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score) OR (breakout enabled AND ShortReactionApproachOK(level) AND close[2] < level + ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND arrival bar overlaps the zone AND NOT ShortArrivalValid(level) AND confirmation low < arrival low AND ShortBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close < level) AND ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) <= midpoint threshold AND ShortOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score AND current volume > configured breakout volume multiplier times the selected baseline, which is either arrival-bar volume or prior-bar SMA of chart volume) OR (merged setup enabled AND close[2] < level + ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND MergedOHLC range overlaps the zone AND (merged-body requirement disabled OR ShortBodyDirectionOK(MergedOHLC)) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close < level) AND ClosePosition(MergedOHLC) <= midpoint threshold AND ShortOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score) OR (merged setup enabled AND close[2] < level + ZoneHalfWidth(level) AND MergedOHLC range overlaps the zone AND (merged-body requirement disabled OR ShortBodyDirectionOK(MergedOHLC)) AND (confirm-close-beyond-level filter disabled OR close < level) AND ShortOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score); OR any configured, enabled Long level is unlocked and satisfies (breakout-retest setup enabled AND its BRS has not been consumed since price last reached level + RetestDistance(level) or the reset-cycle boundary AND ExcursionFromAbove(level, RetestDistance(level), configured retest lookback) AND AcceptedBelow(level, configured retest lookback) AND (retest touch is not required OR confirmation or arrival bar overlaps the zone) AND ShortBodyDirectionOK(open, high, low, close) AND ClosePosition(open, high, low, close) <= midpoint threshold AND close < level AND ShortOptionalBiasScore >= configured minimum optional score). At least one qualifying setup must also pass the global short-signal spacing gate: no prior short signal OR chart bars since the prior short signal > configured spacing OR (current high > prior short-signal candle high AND current close < prior short-signal candle close).
Calculation: When level locking is enabled, a signal locks its selected level; a locked level becomes available when current high >= level + RetestDistance(level) OR current low <= level - RetestDistance(level), and locks and retest-consumption flags reset at each reset-cycle boundary.
Long: In real time, any configured, enabled Long level's bar overlaps its zone and its level-alert cooldown has elapsed.
Short: In real time, any configured, enabled Short level's bar overlaps its zone and its level-alert cooldown has elapsed.
