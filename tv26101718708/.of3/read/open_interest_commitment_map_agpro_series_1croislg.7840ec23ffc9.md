Calculation: OI percentage change = 100 × (participation / nz(participation[OI change lookback], participation) − 1), where participation is open interest when available or, when volume proxy fallback is enabled, EMA(volume, max(round(OI baseline lookback / proxy-length divisor), minimum proxy lookback))
Calculation: OI z-score = (OI percentage change − nz(EMA(OI percentage change, OI baseline lookback), OI percentage change)) / max(nz(STDEV(OI percentage change, OI normalization lookback), 0), positive deviation floor)
Calculation: ATR-normalized price move = (close − nz(close[price-move lookback], close)) / max(nz(ATR(ATR lookback), true range), minimum price increment)
Calculation: active persistence = the maximum, over the persistence window, of the percentage of bars satisfying each of: available participation AND OI z-score above the expansion threshold AND OI percentage change positive AND ATR-normalized price move above the directional threshold AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) above EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close above EMA(close, longer trend lookback); available participation AND OI z-score above the expansion threshold AND OI percentage change positive AND ATR-normalized price move below the negative directional threshold AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) below EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close below EMA(close, longer trend lookback); available participation AND OI z-score above the expansion threshold AND OI percentage change positive AND absolute ATR-normalized price move at most the absorption-move threshold; available participation AND OI z-score below the negative unwind threshold AND OI percentage change negative AND absolute ATR-normalized price move above the unwind-move threshold
Calculation: commitment score = clamp(weight for OI z-score × clamp(abs(nz(OI z-score, 0)) / OI z-score scale × 100, 0, 100) + weight for OI change × clamp(abs(nz(OI percentage change, 0)) / OI change scale × 100, 0, 100) + weight for price movement × clamp(abs(ATR-normalized price move) / price-move scale × 100, 0, 100) + weight for persistence × clamp(active persistence, 0, 100) + weight for volume × nz(PERCENTRANK(volume, OI normalization lookback), median percentile fallback), 0, 100)
Long: available participation AND OI percentage change positive AND OI z-score above the expansion threshold AND ATR-normalized price move above the directional threshold AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND commitment score >= minimum commitment score AND the same condition was false on the prior bar
