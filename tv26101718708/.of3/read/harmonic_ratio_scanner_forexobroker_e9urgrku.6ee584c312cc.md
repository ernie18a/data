Calculation: Confirmed swing sequence = prepend each confirmed pivot high of high and pivot low of low on chart bars, using the swing-lookback parameter on both sides; prepend the high before the low if both confirm on one bar, and retain at most the pivot-storage-capacity parameter of swings.
Calculation: Harmonic ratio = abs(newest swing price - preceding swing price) / abs(preceding swing price - second-preceding swing price).
Long: The first qualifying triplet among up to the triplet-scan-depth parameter of newest-first stored swing triplets has abs(preceding swing price - second-preceding swing price) > ATR(current chart bar, ATR-lookback parameter) * minimum-swing-multiple parameter, abs(newest swing price - preceding swing price) > 0, and abs(harmonic ratio - an enabled target-ratio parameter) < the ratio-tolerance parameter converted from percent to a fraction; its newest swing bar differs from that of the previously detected ratio, its newest swing is a pivot low, and its preceding swing is a pivot high.
Short: The first qualifying triplet among up to the triplet-scan-depth parameter of newest-first stored swing triplets has abs(preceding swing price - second-preceding swing price) > ATR(current chart bar, ATR-lookback parameter) * minimum-swing-multiple parameter, abs(newest swing price - preceding swing price) > 0, and abs(harmonic ratio - an enabled target-ratio parameter) < the ratio-tolerance parameter converted from percent to a fraction; its newest swing bar differs from that of the previously detected ratio, and NOT (its newest swing is a pivot low and its preceding swing is a pivot high).
Long: The long ratio condition holds AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) AND chart bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown parameter AND the current chart bar is confirmed.
Short: The short ratio condition holds AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) AND chart bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown parameter AND the current chart bar is confirmed.
