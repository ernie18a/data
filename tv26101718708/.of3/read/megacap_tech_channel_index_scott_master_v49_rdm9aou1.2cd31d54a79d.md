Calculation: Smoothed RSI = SMA(RSI(close, auto-tuning enabled and chart interval at or below the intraday cutoff parameter ? short RSI lookback : long RSI lookback), RSI smoothing lookback); when auto-tuning is disabled, use the long RSI lookback
Calculation: Oversold threshold = auto-tuning disabled ? fallback oversold parameter : chart-bar count reaches long calibration lookback ? interpolated lower percentile of smoothed RSI over the long calibration lookback : chart-bar count reaches warm-up lookback ? interpolated warm-start oversold percentile of smoothed RSI over the warm-up lookback : fallback oversold parameter
Calculation: Overbought threshold = auto-tuning disabled ? fallback overbought parameter : chart-bar count reaches long calibration lookback ? interpolated upper percentile of smoothed RSI over the long calibration lookback : chart-bar count reaches warm-up lookback ? interpolated warm-start overbought percentile of smoothed RSI over the warm-up lookback : fallback overbought parameter
Calculation: Long reset eligibility = initially true; becomes true when close <= EMA(close, basis lookback) * (1 + reset offset parameter); becomes false after a long entry
Calculation: Long hard stop = close on the long signal bar - ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) * ATR stop multiplier when ATR stop is enabled; otherwise close on the long signal bar * (1 - fixed stop percentage)
Calculation: Long trail stop = on the first bar in the long position, close - ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) * trail multiplier; thereafter, the greater of the prior long trail stop and close - ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) * trail multiplier; when close > entry price * (1 + trail activation percentage), also take the greater of that result and entry price + ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) * trail adjustment parameter
Long: position is flat AND low <= EMA(close, basis lookback) - ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) * lower channel width parameter AND close > open AND smoothed RSI <= oversold threshold AND long reset eligibility AND close < EMA(close, basis lookback) * (1 + chase limit parameter) AND sector index close (T1, last completed bar) > EMA(sector index close, index lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND volatility index close (T1, last completed bar) <= volatility block parameter AND (earnings guard disabled OR outside earnings blackout) AND during the configured entry session AND on a confirmed chart bar
Exit: Long, low <= max(long hard stop, long trail stop)
Exit: Long, (smoothed RSI >= overbought threshold OR close >= EMA(close, basis lookback) + ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) * exhaustion width parameter) AND held for at least the minimum hold parameter number of chart bars AND during the configured entry session AND on a confirmed chart bar
Calculation: chart bars per trading day = round(P6 / chart interval in minutes)
Calculation: smoothed RSI = SMA(RSI(close, P3 if P1 is enabled and the chart interval is intraday and no greater than P2 minutes, otherwise P4), P5)
Calculation: adaptive oversold threshold = if P1 is enabled and at least round(chart bars per trading day × P7) chart bars are available, the P8th linearly interpolated percentile of smoothed RSI over that many bars; otherwise, if P1 is enabled and at least round(chart bars per trading day × P9) chart bars are available, the P10th linearly interpolated percentile of smoothed RSI over that many bars; otherwise P11
Calculation: earnings blackout = P18 and a prior reported earnings event exists and ((current time is before the latest reported earnings time + P19 days and that time is no more than P20 days ahead) or (current time is at or after the latest reported earnings time + P19 days, no more than P20 days after it, and the latest EPS surprise is unavailable or not strictly between P21% and P22%)); EPS surprise = 100 × (reported EPS − estimated EPS) / abs(estimated EPS) when both exist and estimated EPS is nonzero, otherwise unavailable
