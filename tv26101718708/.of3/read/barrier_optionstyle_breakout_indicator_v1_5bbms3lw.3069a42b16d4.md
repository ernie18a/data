Calculation: Anchor price = EMA(close, anchor lookback), VWAP((high + low + close) / 3), or close, by anchor mode
Calculation: Expected move = anchor price × selected volatility × sqrt(horizon bars / bars per year), where selected volatility is annualized standard deviation of log returns, annualized ATR as a fraction of close, or a weighted combination of those measures
Calculation: Upper barrier = manual upper price, anchor price + expected move × sigma, or highest(high[1], range lookback), by barrier mode
Calculation: Lower barrier = manual lower price, anchor price − expected move × sigma, or lowest(low[1], range lookback), by barrier mode
Calculation: Breakout buffer = ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, close × percentage / 100, or minimum price increment × tick count, by breakout buffer mode
Calculation: Retest tolerance = ATR(ATR lookback) × retest ATR multiplier, close × retest percentage / 100, or minimum price increment × retest tick count, by retest tolerance mode
Calculation: Bullish breach = high >= upper barrier in touch mode; close > upper barrier in close mode; close > upper barrier + breakout buffer in buffered-close mode
Calculation: Bearish breach = low <= lower barrier in touch mode; close < lower barrier in close mode; close < lower barrier − breakout buffer in buffered-close mode
Calculation: Breakout quality = volatility filter disabled or valid PercentRank(selected volatility, volatility lookback) >= minimum volatility percentile; AND volume filter disabled or (SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0 and volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) >= minimum volume ratio)
Calculation: Breakout age = current chart-bar count − stored breakout chart-bar count
Calculation: Breakout state = neutral initially; each bar compute new-break conditions using the side at bar start; a quality-confirmed bullish breach with prior side not bullish stores bullish side, upper barrier, and current bar, then a quality-confirmed bearish breach with prior side not bearish stores bearish side, lower barrier, and current bar; each update clears retest and failure flags; retest and failure conditions set their flags, which remain set until reset; reset side, barrier, and flags when age exceeds the longer of the retest and failure windows and either flag is set
Long: (bullish breach AND breakout quality AND prior active side is not bullish) OR (bullish breach AND NOT breakout quality) OR (active side is bullish AND failure flag is false) OR (retest enabled AND active side is bullish AND breakout age > 0 AND breakout age <= retest window AND low <= active barrier + retest tolerance AND close >= active barrier)
Short: (bearish breach AND breakout quality AND prior active side is not bearish) OR (bearish breach AND NOT breakout quality) OR (active side is bearish AND failure flag is false) OR (retest enabled AND active side is bearish AND breakout age > 0 AND breakout age <= retest window AND high >= active barrier − retest tolerance AND close <= active barrier)
Exit: long, active side is bullish AND breakout age > 0 AND breakout age <= failure window AND (close < active barrier when failure requires a close back inside, otherwise low < active barrier)
Exit: short, active side is bearish AND breakout age > 0 AND breakout age <= failure window AND (close > active barrier when failure requires a close back inside, otherwise high > active barrier)
