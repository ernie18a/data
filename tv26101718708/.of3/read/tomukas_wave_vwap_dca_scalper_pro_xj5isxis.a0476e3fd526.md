Calculation: Smoothed range filter = with R = EMA(EMA(abs(close - close[1]), range lookback), 2 × range lookback − 1) × range sensitivity and P = previous filter or close when unavailable, close > P ? max(P, close − R) : min(P, close + R)
Calculation: Recent volume node = HLC3 of the most recent chart bar where volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Calculation: Near-node condition = abs(close − recent volume node) / close < node-proximity threshold
Long: flat AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar is inside configured session in configured timezone) AND close > EMA(close, long trend lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, long trend lookback) AND close > smoothed range filter AND WaveTrend(HLC3, shorter lookback, longer lookback) crosses above SMA(WaveTrend(HLC3, shorter lookback, longer lookback), signal lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(HLC3)) AND (momentum filter disabled OR MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback) > MACD signal(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) AND close > EMA(close, momentum lookback)) AND (volume-zone filter disabled OR recent volume node is absent OR close > recent volume node OR near-node condition) AND close > high[1]
Short: flat AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar is inside configured session in configured timezone) AND close < EMA(close, long trend lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < EMA(close, long trend lookback) AND close < smoothed range filter AND WaveTrend(HLC3, shorter lookback, longer lookback) crosses below SMA(WaveTrend(HLC3, shorter lookback, longer lookback), signal lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(HLC3)) AND (momentum filter disabled OR MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback) < MACD signal(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) AND close < EMA(close, momentum lookback)) AND (volume-zone filter disabled OR recent volume node is absent OR close < recent volume node OR near-node condition) AND close < low[1]
Long: long position AND number of prior same-side add entries since initial entry = n AND close ≤ initial entry close × (1 − (n + 1) × add interval percentage), where n ranges from zero to one less than the maximum number of adds
Short: short position AND number of prior same-side add entries since initial entry = n AND close ≥ initial entry close × (1 + (n + 1) × add interval percentage), where n ranges from zero to one less than the maximum number of adds
Exit: long, close ≥ position average entry price × (1 + basket take-profit percentage) OR close ≤ position average entry price × (1 − basket drawdown-stop percentage)
Exit: short, close ≤ position average entry price × (1 − basket take-profit percentage) OR close ≥ position average entry price × (1 + basket drawdown-stop percentage)
