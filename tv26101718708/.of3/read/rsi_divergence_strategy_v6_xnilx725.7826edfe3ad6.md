Short: An enabled regular or hidden divergence between the latest two confirmed RSI pivot highs, using their associated high prices, with pivot-bar distance between the minimum and maximum pivot-separation parameters, divergence strength filter passed, latest pivot RSI >= short RSI threshold, and no previous bearish drawing or latest pivot bar at least the drawing-separation parameter after the previous bearish drawing; regular means latest price higher and RSI lower, hidden means latest price lower and RSI higher
Long: In RSI-only mode, RSI(source, lookback) <= long RSI threshold; or in divergence-entry mode, RSI(source, lookback) <= long RSI threshold and either the delay parameter is immediate and an enabled bullish divergence between the latest two confirmed RSI pivot lows passes the pivot-separation and divergence-strength filters, or the delay parameter is positive and bars since that qualifying bullish divergence >= the delay parameter; if the short entry predicate also holds, require RSI(source, lookback) < 50
Short: In RSI-only mode, RSI(source, lookback) >= short RSI threshold; or in divergence-entry mode, RSI(source, lookback) >= short RSI threshold and either the delay parameter is immediate and an enabled bearish divergence between the latest two confirmed RSI pivot highs passes the pivot-separation and divergence-strength filters, or the delay parameter is positive and bars since that qualifying bearish divergence >= the delay parameter; if the long entry predicate also holds, require RSI(source, lookback) > 50
