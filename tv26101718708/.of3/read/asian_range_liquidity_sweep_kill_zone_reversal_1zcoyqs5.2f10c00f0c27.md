Calculation: Asian range high and low = highest high and lowest low during the Asian-range session; midpoint = (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: The three POI scanners use timeframe parameters T1, T2, and T3, with T1 shorter than T2 and T2 shorter than T3; the structure-bias scanner uses separate timeframe parameter T4. Each runs on chart bars using the selected timeframe's current values with lookahead off; if its selected timeframe is below the chart timeframe, it uses the chart timeframe. Current values may come from a still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Chart-bar bearish FVG = the low at the configured chart-FVG lag exceeds the current high, with gap size at least the minimum ATR-multiple parameter; bullish FVG = the high at that lag is below the current low by at least that size. Scanner bearish FVG = the low at the older scanner-FVG lag exceeds the high at the newer scanner-FVG lag; bullish FVG = the older-lag high is below the newer-lag low. Use ATR with the ATR-lookback parameter.
Calculation: Active HTF supply and demand POIs = enabled bearish and bullish FVGs and order blocks from T1, T2, and T3. A bearish order block is a bullish candle at the configured origin lag followed by a close below its low; a bullish order block is a bearish candle there followed by a close above its high. The zone is that candle's high-to-low. A close through a demand zone's lower boundary flips its order block to a bearish breaker or its FVG to a bearish inverse FVG when that type is enabled; otherwise it retires. A close through a supply zone's upper boundary flips it to a bullish breaker or bullish inverse FVG when enabled; otherwise it retires. Retire zones whose midpoint is farther from close than the maximum ATR-distance parameter, and discard the oldest zones above the zone-cap parameter. Add zones only when POI display is enabled; test a price against a zone when it is between its boundaries.
Calculation: Downward structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed Pivot Low; upward structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed Pivot High. Chart-bar pivots use the swing-pivot-strength parameter on both sides; T4 bias pivots use a separate bias-pivot-lookback parameter on both sides.
Calculation: T4 structure bias = bearish after a T4 close crosses below its latest confirmed Pivot Low, bullish after a T4 close crosses above its latest confirmed Pivot High, and otherwise retain the prior bias, initially neutral.
Calculation: Bearish CISD = a bearish candle closes below the open of the first candle in the latest bullish run, crossing below that level; bullish CISD = a bullish candle closes above the open of the first candle in the latest bearish run, crossing above that level. The CISD level is that run's first-candle open.
Calculation: Bearish hidden order block = the highest-high bullish candle between the latest two bearish chart FVGs when their separation is within the hidden-OB-lookback parameter; its zone is from the candle body's lower edge to its high. Bullish hidden order block = the lowest-low non-bullish candle between the latest two bullish chart FVGs within that lookback; its zone is from its low to the body's upper edge. A tap means the bar's high and low overlap the zone.
Calculation: HTF bearish or bullish POI tap = the tested price lies inside an active supply or demand POI, respectively. A high side is protected when the enabled early-POI filter finds a bearish POI at a bar high during Asia and downward structure breaks during Asia meet the required-count parameter, or when the enabled strong-high filter qualifies; define strong high as a swing-high sweep and close back below it followed by a downward structure break within the strong-sweep window, with the swept high in the configured outer fraction of the then-current Asian range. Define protected low symmetrically using bullish POIs, upward breaks, a swept swing low, and the opposite outer fraction.
Calculation: A qualifying short sweep occurs once per Asian range when, during the enabled London entry window (including the optional early-start window), high exceeds the Asian range high and close returns below it, the high is not protected, and the optional T4 bias permits shorts (bias is bearish or neutral). If POI is required, the high must be inside an active supply POI. A qualifying long sweep is the symmetric Asian-low sweep, with the low unprotected, bias bullish or neutral, and any required demand POI. Disable London sweeps on dates in the configured news-date list when the news filter is enabled. The swept extreme and its bar update to each new extreme until the Trade 1 entry fires.
Calculation: Sweep validation = after a qualifying sweep, a same-direction chart FVG created on or after the latest sweep-extreme bar overlaps a bar; validation can also be satisfied at trigger time by a tap of the latest post-sweep same-direction FVG or a tap of a same-direction hidden order block.
Calculation: MSS confirmation = a same-direction structure break has armed the setup and the latest post-sweep same-direction FVG is tapped on a later bar. CISD confirmation = after a same-direction CISD, a later bar retraces to its level. FVG confirmation = the latest post-sweep same-direction FVG is tapped on a later bar. Hidden-OB confirmation = a same-direction hidden order block is tapped. The selected confirmation-model parameter chooses one of these or accepts any in automatic mode.
Calculation: Trade 1 entry price = the selected FVG price when a FVG is tapped, otherwise the hidden-order-block entry edge when tapped, otherwise the CISD level when retapped, otherwise the prior close. FVG price is the configured proximal edge, midpoint, or distal edge. Trade 1 stop reference is selected by the stop-mode parameter: the protective high or low from the entry zone's reference bar through the trigger bar, the latest sweep extreme, the sweep candle's high or low, or the trigger candle's high or low. If the protective-zone reference is unavailable, use the trigger candle. Trade 1 stop = short reference plus stop-buffer ATRs, or long reference minus stop-buffer ATRs. Reward/risk = distance from entry to the opposite Asian range boundary divided by distance from entry to stop.
Calculation: OTE impulse origin = the latest short-sweep high or long-sweep low; its endpoint starts at the close-through-range bar's low for shorts or high for longs and updates to each new impulse extreme until Trade 2 fires. OTE level = origin + (endpoint - origin) × the configured OTE fraction. Trade 2 stop = the configured stop fraction level plus stop-buffer ATRs for shorts or minus them for longs. Trade 2 target = the most recently calculated inducement target, retained until recalculated; when unavailable, endpoint + (endpoint - origin) × the configured fallback-extension fraction. An inducement target is the lowest low below the Asian low for shorts or highest high above the Asian high for longs over the configured lookback.

Exit: short, low is at or below the Asian range low OR (break-even is active and the favorable move has reached the configured R threshold and high is at or above entry) OR (break-even is not active and high is at or above the Trade 1 stop).
Exit: long, high is at or above the Asian range high OR (break-even is active and the favorable move has reached the configured R threshold and low is at or below entry) OR (break-even is not active and low is at or below the Trade 1 stop).

Exit: long, high is at or above the Trade 2 target OR low is at or below the Trade 2 stop.
