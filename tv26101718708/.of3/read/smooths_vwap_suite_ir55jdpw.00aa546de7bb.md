Calculation: T1 VWAP = VWAP(source parameter, T1 anchor period, accumulated on chart bars from the start of the current forming T1 period through the current bar)
Calculation: T2 VWAP = VWAP(source parameter, T2 anchor period, accumulated on chart bars from the start of the current forming T2 period through the current bar), where T2 is longer than T1
Calculation: T1 volume-weighted standard deviation = sqrt(max(sum(source parameter^2 * volume) / sum(volume) - T1 VWAP^2, 0)), accumulated on chart bars from the start of the current forming T1 period through the current bar
Calculation: First T1 VWAP deviation bands = T1 VWAP ± first SD multiplier * T1 volume-weighted standard deviation
Calculation: Second T1 VWAP deviation bands = T1 VWAP ± second SD multiplier * T1 volume-weighted standard deviation
Long: ((high > T1 VWAP AND low < T1 VWAP AND close > T1 VWAP AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) > EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] AND close > T2 VWAP) OR (((low <= lower first T1 VWAP deviation band AND close > lower first T1 VWAP deviation band) OR (low <= lower second T1 VWAP deviation band AND close > lower second T1 VWAP deviation band)) AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) > EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] AND close > T1 VWAP AND close > open)) AND current chart bar index - (bar index of the most recent accepted long or short signal, or zero if none) > signal cooldown parameter
Short: ((high > T1 VWAP AND low < T1 VWAP AND close < T1 VWAP AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) < EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] AND close < T2 VWAP) OR (((high >= upper first T1 VWAP deviation band AND close < upper first T1 VWAP deviation band) OR (high >= upper second T1 VWAP deviation band AND close < upper second T1 VWAP deviation band)) AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) < EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] AND close < T1 VWAP AND close < open)) AND current chart bar index - (bar index of the most recent accepted long or short signal, or zero if none) > signal cooldown parameter
