Calculation: trend VWMA base signals = on a confirmed close cross above VWMA(close, trend VWMA lookback), record the close and set a pending long setup, clearing any pending short setup; on a confirmed close cross below it, record the close and set a pending short setup, clearing any pending long setup; each same-direction close cross updates its recorded close; a pending long setup signals when VWMA crosses above its recorded close and the previous trend VWMA base signal was short or none; a pending short setup signals when VWMA crosses below its recorded close and the previous trend VWMA base signal was long; clear a setup on its signal; the first eligible signal is long
Calculation: mean-reversion HMA base signals = on a confirmed close cross above HMA(close, reversion HMA lookback), record the close and set a pending long setup, clearing any pending short setup; on a confirmed close cross below it, record the close and set a pending short setup, clearing any pending long setup; each same-direction close cross updates its recorded close; a pending long setup signals when HMA crosses above its recorded close and the previous mean-reversion HMA base signal was short or none; a pending short setup signals when HMA crosses below its recorded close and the previous mean-reversion HMA base signal was long; clear a setup on its signal; the first eligible signal is long
Calculation: trend-line touch register = initially false; on a new position (flat to non-flat or a side reversal), reset false; otherwise set true if the bar high and low straddle the trend EMA, either trend EMA band (trend EMA * (1 + band percentage) or trend EMA * (1 - band percentage)), or the close is strictly between the bands; otherwise retain its prior value; reset false on either trend VWMA base signal
Long: trend VWMA long base signal AND close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) AND the prior chart bar's trend-line touch register is true AND long enabled AND current bar time is at or after the configured start time
Long: mean-reversion HMA long base signal AND close < EMA(close, mean EMA lookback) * lower multiplier AND long enabled AND current bar time is at or after the configured start time
Short: trend VWMA short base signal AND close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) AND the prior chart bar's trend-line touch register is true AND short enabled AND current bar time is at or after the configured start time
Short: mean-reversion HMA short base signal AND close > EMA(close, mean EMA lookback) * upper multiplier AND short enabled AND current bar time is at or after the configured start time
Exit: long, position opened by trend-following entry AND (trend VWMA short base signal OR (mean-reversion HMA short base signal AND close > EMA(close, mean EMA lookback) * upper multiplier)) AND no short entry condition is true
Exit: long, position opened by mean-reversion entry AND (mean-reversion HMA short base signal OR (trend VWMA short base signal AND close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) AND the prior chart bar's trend-line touch register is true)) AND no short entry condition is true
Exit: short, position opened by trend-following entry AND (trend VWMA long base signal OR (mean-reversion HMA long base signal AND close < EMA(close, mean EMA lookback) * lower multiplier)) AND no long entry condition is true
Exit: short, position opened by mean-reversion entry AND (mean-reversion HMA long base signal OR (trend VWMA long base signal AND close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) AND the prior chart bar's trend-line touch register is true)) AND no long entry condition is true
