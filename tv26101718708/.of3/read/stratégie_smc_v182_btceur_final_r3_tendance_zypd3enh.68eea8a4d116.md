Calculation: Latest swing high/low = initialize to highest(high)/lowest(low) over the initial swing lookback on chart bars; update to high[swing length]/low[swing length] when bars since high/low equals highest(high)/lowest(low) over 2 * swing length + 1 chart bars equals swing length
Calculation: Swing midpoint = (latest swing high + latest swing low) / 2
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = lowest(low, liquidity lookback) < low - close * liquidity tolerance / 100 for bullish setups; highest(high, liquidity lookback) > high + close * liquidity tolerance / 100 for bearish setups
Calculation: Bullish OB setup = close[1] < open[1] AND high[1] < low[3] AND close > latest swing low AND bullish liquidity sweep AND close < swing midpoint
Calculation: Bearish OB setup = close[1] > open[1] AND low[1] > high[3] AND close < latest swing high AND bearish liquidity sweep AND close > swing midpoint
Calculation: Active OB at touch check = high[1]/low[1] and direction from a bullish or bearish OB setup when no OB exists or position is flat; otherwise retain the existing OB; clear after the touch check if bullish close < OB low or bearish close > OB high
Long: position is flat AND active bullish OB exists at touch check AND low <= active OB high + mitigation buffer factor * close AND close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, current/forming bar) sampled on chart bars AND min(max(risk amount / max(abs(close - active OB low), minimum stop distance), minimum lot), maximum lot) >= minimum lot
Short: position is flat AND active bearish OB exists at touch check AND high >= active OB low - mitigation buffer factor * close AND close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, current/forming bar) sampled on chart bars AND min(max(risk amount / max(abs(close - active OB high), minimum stop distance), minimum lot), maximum lot) >= minimum lot
Exit: Long, low <= bullish OB low saved at the entry signal OR high >= entry-signal bar close + reward multiple * max(abs(entry-signal bar close - bullish OB low saved at the entry signal), minimum stop distance)
Exit: Short, high >= bearish OB high saved at the entry signal OR low <= entry-signal bar close - reward multiple * max(abs(entry-signal bar close - bearish OB high saved at the entry signal), minimum stop distance)
