Calculation: Triangle geometry = on chart bars, project lines through the two latest confirmed pivot highs and the two latest confirmed pivot lows (each pivot uses the pivot lookback on both sides); calculate the current and prior line values, each line’s slope, the widths at the earlier first pivot and at the current bar, the current bar’s age from that earlier pivot, the percentage width reduction, and the intersection bar rounded to the nearest bar and bars until that intersection.
Calculation: Valid triangle = both pivot pairs exist and each pair is chronological AND age is between the minimum and maximum pattern ages AND starting width and current width are positive AND starting width divided by ATR is at least the minimum starting width in ATR AND current width divided by ATR is at most the maximum current width in ATR AND width reduction percentage is at least the minimum compression percentage AND upper-line slope is less than lower-line slope AND current upper boundary is above current lower boundary AND bars until the rounded intersection is at least the minimum past-apex allowance and at most the maximum forward-apex bars.
Calculation: Breakout quality score(side) = round(compression score + apex score + boundary-distance score + candle-body score + close-location score + volume score), where compression score = clamp((width reduction percentage - minimum compression percentage) / max(compression-score normalization percentage - minimum compression percentage, compression denominator floor), 0, 1) × compression points; apex score = (clamp(1 - abs(bars until intersection - apex target bars) / max(maximum forward-apex bars, apex-window denominator floor), 0, 1) × apex-window weight + clamp(1 - current width in ATR / max(maximum current width in ATR, apex-width denominator floor), 0, 1) × apex-width weight) × apex points; boundary-distance score = clamp(boundary distance / max(ATR × impulse ATR scale, minimum price increment), 0, 1) × impulse points; candle-body score = clamp(abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), 0, 1) × body points; close-location score = clamp((long: close - low; short: high - close) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), 0, 1) × close-location points; volume score when volume participation is enabled = clamp((volume / volume average - 1) / max(volume full-score ratio - 1, volume-ratio denominator floor), 0, 1) × volume points, with volume average = SMA(volume, volume lookback) and volume / volume average treated as 1 when the average is not positive; otherwise volume score is the volume-disabled score.
Calculation: Active breakout boundary = linearly project the broken upper or lower triangle boundary from the most recent shown breakout’s two corresponding pivot anchors to the current bar; active age is bars since that breakout.
Long: (Valid triangle AND close > current upper boundary + ATR × minimum break distance in ATR AND close[1] <= prior upper boundary AND Breakout quality score(long) >= minimum displayed breakout score AND (no earlier shown breakout OR the sum of the four pivot-anchor bar indices differs from that of the previous shown breakout OR bars since the previous shown breakout > visual cooldown bars)) OR (the most recent shown breakout is bullish AND active age is between the retest minimum and maximum bars AND low is between the active breakout boundary - ATR × retest tolerance in ATR and the active breakout boundary + ATR × retest tolerance in ATR AND close > active breakout boundary).
Short: (Valid triangle AND close < current lower boundary - ATR × minimum break distance in ATR AND close[1] >= prior lower boundary AND Breakout quality score(short) >= minimum displayed breakout score AND (no earlier shown breakout OR the sum of the four pivot-anchor bar indices differs from that of the previous shown breakout OR bars since the previous shown breakout > visual cooldown bars)) OR (the most recent shown breakout is bearish AND active age is between the retest minimum and maximum bars AND high is between the active breakout boundary - ATR × retest tolerance in ATR and the active breakout boundary + ATR × retest tolerance in ATR AND close < active breakout boundary).
