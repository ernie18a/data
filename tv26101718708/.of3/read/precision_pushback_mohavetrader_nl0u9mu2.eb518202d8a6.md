Calculation: Fast smoothed %R = EMA(Williams %R(short lookback), fast smoothing length), with raw %R = -50 when its range is zero
Calculation: Slow smoothed %R = EMA(Williams %R(long lookback), slow smoothing length), with raw %R = -50 when its range is zero; long lookback exceeds short lookback
Calculation: Bullish rail event = the end of a consecutive chart-bar run where fast and slow smoothed %R are both above -pressure threshold; its low and high are the run’s lowest low and highest high, and its low becomes the lower rail
Calculation: Bearish rail event = the end of a consecutive chart-bar run where fast and slow smoothed %R are both below -100 plus pressure threshold; its high and low are the run’s highest high and lowest low, and its high becomes the upper rail
Calculation: Body midpoint = (high + low) / 2 + (high != low ? abs(close - open) / (high - low) × (close - (high + low) / 2) : 0)
Calculation: Fast adaptive rail = prior fast rail + the body-midpoint difference divided by max(fast base length / (1 + abs(body midpoint - prior fast rail) / ATR(fast ATR lookback)), 1), with the step capped at fast step-cap multiplier × ATR(fast ATR lookback) when that cap is positive; initialize to body midpoint
Calculation: Fast rail direction = up when fast rail rises more than ATR(fast ATR lookback) × deadzone reference × deadzone scaling factor, down when it falls more than that amount, otherwise its prior direction; initialize up
Calculation: Structure adaptive rail = prior structure rail + the body-midpoint difference divided by max(structure base length / (1 + abs(body midpoint - prior structure rail) / ATR(fast ATR lookback)), 1), with the step capped at structure step-cap multiplier × ATR(fast ATR lookback) when that cap is positive; initialize to body midpoint
Calculation: Structure direction = up when body midpoint crosses above structure rail + ATR(structure ATR lookback) × channel half-width; down when it crosses below structure rail - ATR(structure ATR lookback) × channel half-width; otherwise its prior direction; initialize down
Calculation: Bullish campaign ceiling = highest high of bullish rail events completed after the bullish campaign begins, excluding its initiating event; unset until such an event completes and reset on each bullish campaign flip
Calculation: Defended level = the initiating event’s rail at an event-based campaign start; while bullish, update to each completed bullish-event low; while bearish, update to each completed bearish-event high; set to the bullish campaign ceiling on a fast-rail bearish flip; clear on a rail-assisted bullish flip or a no-ceiling, both-rails-down bearish flip
Calculation: Bullish-event reference low = first completed bullish-event low after a campaign flip, then update only when a lower event low occurs; reset on each campaign flip
Calculation: Bearish-event reference high = first completed bearish-event high after a campaign flip, then update only when a higher event high occurs; reset on each campaign flip
Long: no campaign and a bullish rail event completes, or bearish campaign and defended level and lower rail exist and close > defended level and close > lower rail, or bearish campaign and fast rail direction is up and structure direction is up
Short: no campaign and a bearish rail event completes and no bullish rail event completes, or bullish campaign and defended level and upper rail exist and close < defended level and close < upper rail, or bullish campaign and bullish campaign ceiling is set and fast rail direction is down, or bullish campaign and bullish campaign ceiling is unset and fast and structure rail directions are down
Long: a bullish rail event completes and its low is greater than or equal to the existing bullish-event reference low, or a bearish rail event completes and its high is greater than the existing bearish-event reference high
Short: a bullish rail event completes and its low is below the existing bullish-event reference low, or a bearish rail event completes and its high is less than or equal to the existing bearish-event reference high
Long: bullish campaign and RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= bullish buyer-control threshold, or bearish campaign and RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bearish buyer-control threshold
Short: bullish campaign and RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bullish seller-control threshold, or bearish campaign and RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= bearish seller-control threshold
