Calculation: Directional score = the sum of five components, each using its own weight: DMI/ADX contributes +weight when ADX(DMI length, smoothing length) > ADX trend minimum and +DI > -DI, −weight when ADX > the minimum and -DI > +DI, otherwise zero; Supertrend contributes +weight when bullish and −weight when bearish; EMA contributes +weight when close > EMA(close, EMA length) and −weight when close < it; MACD contributes +weight when its line > its signal line and zero, and −weight when its line < its signal line and zero; Ichimoku contributes +weight when close is above current unshifted Span A and Span B and conversion > base, and −weight when close is below both spans and conversion < base, otherwise zero. Ichimoku conversion, base, and Span B are midpoints of highest high and lowest low over their respective lookbacks; Span A is the mean of conversion and base.
Calculation: Volatility and volume score = the sum of five components, each using its own weight: ATR contributes +weight when ATR(ATR length) > SMA(ATR(ATR length), ATR-average length) × upper ATR ratio, and −weight when it is below that average × lower ATR ratio; VWAP contributes +weight when close > VWAP(hlc3) and −weight when close < it; volume contributes +weight when volume > SMA(volume, volume-average length) × upper volume ratio and −weight when it is below that average × lower volume ratio; OBV contributes +weight when OBV > EMA(OBV, OBV-EMA length) and −weight when OBV < it; CMF contributes +weight when CMF(CMF length) > its positive threshold and −weight when CMF is below the corresponding negative threshold. Each component contributes zero otherwise.
Calculation: Directional long average = the sum of max(directional score, zero) over the current and preceding score-window chart bars, divided by the score-window length.
Calculation: Directional short average = the sum of max(−directional score, zero) over the current and preceding score-window chart bars, divided by the score-window length.
Calculation: Volatility and volume long average = the sum of max(volatility and volume score, zero) over the current and preceding score-window chart bars, divided by the score-window length.
Calculation: Volatility and volume short average = the sum of max(−volatility and volume score, zero) over the current and preceding score-window chart bars, divided by the score-window length.
Calculation: Long score = directional long average + volatility and volume long average.
Calculation: Short score = directional short average + volatility and volume short average.
Calculation: Session eligible = session filter disabled OR current chart bar time falls within either configured session window.
Calculation: Cooldown eligible = no closed trade yet OR chart bars since the latest closed trade ≥ cooldown length.
Calculation: Selected stop lookback = longer stop lookback when the impulse filter is enabled and abs(close[1] − open[1]) > ATR(ATR length) × impulse-body multiple; otherwise shorter stop lookback. The longer stop lookback exceeds the shorter stop lookback.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest(low, selected stop lookback) − ATR(ATR length) × stop ATR multiple, calculated at the entry signal and fixed for that trade.
Calculation: Short stop = highest(high, selected stop lookback) + ATR(ATR length) × stop ATR multiple, calculated at the entry signal and fixed for that trade.
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close + (signal-bar close − long stop) × reward/risk parameter, fixed for that trade.
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar close − (short stop − signal-bar close) × reward/risk parameter, fixed for that trade.
Long: (long score ≥ entry score minimum AND session eligible AND open-trade count < open-trade limit AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR close[1] > open[1]) AND cooldown eligible AND close − long stop > zero AND close − long stop ≤ ATR(ATR length) × maximum stop-distance multiple) OR (long score ≥ entry score minimum AND session eligible AND (open-trade count ≥ open-trade limit OR NOT cooldown eligible) AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR close[1] > open[1])) OR Supertrend is bullish OR long score ≥ entry score minimum OR directional long average ≥ panel color threshold OR volatility and volume long average ≥ panel color threshold.
Short: (short score ≥ entry score minimum AND session eligible AND open-trade count < open-trade limit AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR close[1] < open[1]) AND cooldown eligible AND short stop − close > zero AND short stop − close ≤ ATR(ATR length) × maximum stop-distance multiple) OR (short score ≥ entry score minimum AND session eligible AND (open-trade count ≥ open-trade limit OR NOT cooldown eligible) AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR close[1] < open[1])) OR Supertrend is bearish OR short score ≥ entry score minimum OR directional short average ≥ panel color threshold OR volatility and volume short average ≥ panel color threshold.
Exit: long, short score ≥ entry score minimum OR low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: short, long score ≥ entry score minimum OR high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
