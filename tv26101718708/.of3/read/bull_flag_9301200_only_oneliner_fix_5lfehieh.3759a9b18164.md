Calculation: Pullback retracement (%) = (impulse high − pullback low) / (impulse high − impulse low) × 100
Long: close > open AND high − low >= impulse ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) AND volume >= impulse volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Long: During the configured session, on a bar that processes an active setup initialized by an in-session bar satisfying the impulse condition (including a setup initialized on this bar), high > high[1] AND volume >= breakout volume multiplier × SMA(volume, the same volume lookback). Pullback low is the minimum low across processed in-session bars since the impulse; the bar count starts at one on the impulse and advances on each such bar. A new in-session impulse resets the setup. It becomes inactive when pullback retracement > maximum pullback percent OR bar count > maximum pullback bars; the breakout check still applies on the bar that deactivates it. A breakout deactivates the setup.
Exit: long, low <= pullback low on the entry bar OR high >= entry-bar close + reward/risk parameter × (entry-bar close − pullback low on the entry bar)
