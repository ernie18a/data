Calculation: High Good Score(x, weak, strong) = clamp(score minimum + (x - weak) / max(strong - weak, normalization span floor) * (score maximum - score minimum), score minimum, score maximum)
Calculation: Peak Score(x, low, ideal, high) = High Good Score(x, low, ideal) when x <= ideal; otherwise clamp(score minimum + (high - x) / max(high - ideal, normalization span floor) * (score maximum - score minimum), score minimum, score maximum)
Calculation: Acceptance Score = clamp(close-distance weight * High Good Score(reclaim close distance in ATRs, distance baseline, max(break-distance ATR parameter, reclaim-distance ATR parameter + distance extension)) + retest-depth weight * Peak Score(retest depth in ATRs, depth baseline, acceptance-pocket ATR parameter * ideal-depth fraction, acceptance-pocket ATR parameter * high-depth fraction) + volume weight * (neutral-volume score when volume is unavailable, otherwise High Good Score(volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), weak-volume ratio, strong-volume ratio)) + trend weight * trend score + wick weight * clamp(reclaim-wick fraction * wick multiplier + close-location fraction * location multiplier + body-share fraction * body multiplier, score minimum, score maximum), score minimum, score maximum); the weights sum to one. Reclaim close distance in ATRs is the side-adjusted distance from close to the reclaimed pivot divided by ATR. Retest depth in ATRs is max(side-adjusted penetration beyond the pivot, zero) divided by ATR. Trend score is the full-alignment score when close and the shorter-lookback EMA align with the longer-lookback EMA, the partial score when close is on the side of the longer-lookback EMA but the EMAs do not align, and the weak score otherwise. For a long, reclaim wick is the lower wick and close location is (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick); for a short, reclaim wick is the upper wick and close location is (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum tick). Body share is abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Risk Edge = for a long, min(reclaim-bar low, reclaimed pivot level - reclaim-bar ATR * risk-edge buffer ATR parameter); for a short, max(reclaim-bar high, reclaimed pivot level + reclaim-bar ATR * risk-edge buffer ATR parameter)
Calculation: Target = reclaimed pivot level + side direction * max(abs(reclaimed pivot level - risk edge), reclaim-bar ATR * minimum-risk-distance ATR parameter) * target R multiple
Long: bullish side enabled AND (close crosses above the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, left pivot strength, right pivot strength) + ATR(ATR lookback) * break-distance ATR parameter OR a bullish breakout watch remains armed after that cross, within the retest window and before a close below the pivot high - ATR(ATR lookback) * acceptance-pocket ATR parameter OR, while that watch is active after its breakout bar, low <= pivot high - ATR(ATR lookback) * level-loss ATR parameter AND close >= pivot high + ATR(ATR lookback) * reclaim-distance ATR parameter OR an accepted bullish reclaim plan is active). A bullish plan is accepted when that later-bar retest condition occurs and Acceptance Score >= watch threshold. When bar-close confirmation is enabled, require a confirmed bar.
Short: bearish side enabled AND (close crosses below the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, left pivot strength, right pivot strength) - ATR(ATR lookback) * break-distance ATR parameter OR a bearish breakout watch remains armed after that cross, within the retest window and before a close above the pivot low + ATR(ATR lookback) * acceptance-pocket ATR parameter OR, while that watch is active after its breakout bar, high >= pivot low + ATR(ATR lookback) * level-loss ATR parameter AND close <= pivot low - ATR(ATR lookback) * reclaim-distance ATR parameter OR an accepted bearish reclaim plan is active). A bearish plan is accepted when that later-bar retest condition occurs and Acceptance Score >= watch threshold. When bar-close confirmation is enabled, require a confirmed bar.
Exit: long, an unresolved accepted bullish reclaim plan is within its expiry window AND (confirmed close < Risk Edge OR confirmed close >= Target)
Exit: short, an unresolved accepted bearish reclaim plan is within its expiry window AND (confirmed close > Risk Edge OR confirmed close <= Target)
