Calculation: Body fraction = high > low ? abs(close - open) / (high - low) : zero
Calculation: Compression bounds and stall count = while idle, after a candle with close > open or close < open and high - low > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × impulse-strength parameter, wait for the first candle with high - low < ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × compression-tightness parameter; set bounds to its high and low, and count each small-range candle plus an initial-bar bonus parameter; update bounds with each later small-range candle; the compression is eligible when stall count >= minimum-stall-bars parameter; reset after a close beyond either bound with body fraction >= minimum-body-fraction parameter
Calculation: Trailing distance = trailing-stop parameter × trailing-distance multiplier parameter
Long: flat position AND the previous bar closed above the high of an eligible compression AND body fraction[1] >= minimum-body-fraction parameter AND close > open AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback parameter) AND close > VWAP(hlc3)
Short: flat position AND the previous bar closed below the low of an eligible compression AND body fraction[1] >= minimum-body-fraction parameter AND close < open AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback parameter) AND close < VWAP(hlc3)
Exit: long, (stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance parameter) OR high >= entry price + first profit-target parameter OR high >= entry price + second profit-target parameter OR (highest high since entry >= entry price + trailing distance AND low <= highest high since entry - trailing distance)
Exit: short, (stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance parameter) OR low <= entry price - first profit-target parameter OR low <= entry price - second profit-target parameter OR (lowest low since entry <= entry price - trailing distance AND high >= lowest low since entry + trailing distance)
