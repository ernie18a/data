Calculation: BestMA(criterion) = candidate moving average (SMA, EMA, or KAMA of close at each candidate length) with the highest backtest value of criterion, where criterion is long-only profit of closes crossing it, short-only profit, combined profit, or support/resistance hold rate; re-selected when T2 changes
Calculation: FibLevel(p) = FibLow + p * (FibHigh - FibLow), with FibHigh and FibLow the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low of fib_pivot_len
Calculation: NearestSupport = most recently added pivot low of sr_pivot_len, where a pivot within merge_frac * ATR(atr_len) of a kept support level is not added; NearestResistance = same on pivot highs
Calculation: Levels = (price, score) pairs within level_range * ATR(atr_len) of close from: last confirmed HTF pivot high and low (htf_pivot_len); HTF EMA(htf_ema_short) and HTF EMA(htf_ema_long); each BestMA criterion; FibLevel(0), FibLevel(0.382), FibLevel(0.5), FibLevel(0.618), FibLevel(0.786), FibLevel(1); CloudTop; CloudBottom; EMA(ema_short); EMA(ema_long); KAMA(kama_len); VWAP; each trend line with its channel and channel midline; pivot highs or lows of sr_pivot_len with at least min_touch touches and positive score. Each source has a fixed score parameter. Touches = bars with price within tl_tol * ATR of the level, spaced at least chan_gap bars apart. Pivot score = touches - violations (closes beyond the level by chan_break_atr * ATR) + volume term (+w_vol_hi if chart volume at the pivot bar exceeds vol_hi * SMA(real volume, vol_len), -w_vol_lo if below vol_lo * that SMA; real volume = volume of the volume-source instrument, or chart volume if unset). Levels below close form SupportLevels; levels above close form ResistanceLevels. Same-side levels within merge_frac * ATR merge (near and far edges kept, scores summed); a support and a resistance within merge_frac * ATR merge into the stronger one
Calculation: SupportRank(k, x) = far edge of the k-th highest-score support level below x; ResistanceRank(k, x) = far edge of the k-th highest-score resistance level above x (k = 1 to 3)
Calculation: TopSupport(k) and TopResistance(k), k = 1 to 3 = k-th highest-score SupportLevels below close and ResistanceLevels above close within sr_max_dist * ATR(atr_len) of close, excluding earlier picks; near edge = highest support price or lowest resistance price; far edge = lowest support price or highest resistance price
Calculation: RRPoints(RR) = 0 if RR undefined; min(rr_cap_lo, max(rr_floor_lo, rr_slope_lo * (RR - 1))) if RR <= rr_split; else min(rr_cap_hi, rr_base_hi + rr_slope_hi * (RR - rr_split))
Calculation: LongTarget = TopResistance(1).near - tp_buf * ATR(atr_len), replaced by TopResistance(2).near - tp_buf * ATR(atr_len) when LongTarget - close < rr_near * ATR(atr_len) and TopResistance(2) exists
Calculation: LongStop = TopSupport(1).far - sl_inst * ATR(atr_len)
Calculation: LongRR = (LongTarget - close) / (close - LongStop) when close > LongStop
Calculation: ShortTarget = TopSupport(1).near + tp_buf * ATR(atr_len), replaced by TopSupport(2).near + tp_buf * ATR(atr_len) when close - ShortTarget < rr_near * ATR(atr_len) and TopSupport(2) exists
Calculation: ShortStop = TopResistance(1).far + sl_inst * ATR(atr_len)
Calculation: ShortRR = (close - ShortTarget) / (ShortStop - close) when ShortStop > close
Calculation: LongLimit = min(ideal, TopSupport(1).near + entry_buf * ATR(atr_len)) with ideal = LongStop + (LongTarget - LongStop) / (min_rr + 1), defined when LongTarget > LongStop; second term omitted when TopSupport(1) undefined
Calculation: ShortLimit = max(ideal, TopResistance(1).near - entry_buf * ATR(atr_len)) with ideal = ShortStop - (ShortStop - ShortTarget) / (min_rr + 1), defined when ShortStop > ShortTarget; second term omitted when TopResistance(1) undefined
Calculation: TrendMult and SRMult = (trend_hi, sr_hi) if ADX(adx_len) >= adx_hi; (trend_mid, sr_mid) if ADX >= adx_mid; else (trend_lo, sr_lo); TrendMult multiplies trend points, SRMult multiplies S/R points
Calculation: PD = RSI(close, rsi_len) pivot low of div_pivot_len confirmed on this bar AND low at that pivot bar < low at previous RSI pivot low AND RSI at that pivot > RSI at previous RSI pivot low; ND = RSI pivot high confirmed AND high at that pivot bar > high at previous RSI pivot high AND RSI at that pivot < RSI at previous RSI pivot high
Calculation: PDRun and NDRun = consecutive PD and ND events in the current run; a run is archived into PDAvgRun or NDAvgRun (mean of archived run lengths) and reset when the opposite event occurs or RSI(close, rsi_len) crosses rsi_mid
Calculation: DivBonus(run, avgRun) = 0 if run = 0 or avgRun = 0; else bonus_hi if run / avgRun >= ratio_hi, bonus_mid if >= ratio_mid, bonus_lo if >= ratio_lo, else 0
Calculation: SupportBounce = low <= NearestSupport + bounce_band * ATR(atr_len) AND close > NearestSupport; ResistanceBounce = high >= NearestResistance - bounce_band * ATR(atr_len) AND close < NearestResistance
Calculation: BelowSupport = close < NearestSupport - below_band * ATR(atr_len); AboveResistance = close > NearestResistance + below_band * ATR(atr_len)
Calculation: BounceLong = bounce_w * SupportBounce + pattern_w * (SupportBounce AND (BullRejection OR BullEngulfing)) + rr_bounce_w * (SupportBounce AND LongRR >= rr_bounce) + DivBonus(PDRun, PDAvgRun); BullRejection = lower wick > wick_mult * body AND close >= open; BullEngulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous open AND open <= previous close; lower wick = min(close, open) - low; body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: BounceShort = bounce_w * ResistanceBounce + pattern_w * (ResistanceBounce AND (BearRejection OR BearEngulfing)) + rr_bounce_w * (ResistanceBounce AND ShortRR >= rr_bounce) + DivBonus(NDRun, NDAvgRun); BearRejection = upper wick > wick_mult * body AND close <= open; BearEngulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous open AND open >= previous close; upper wick = high - max(close, open)
Calculation: CloudTop / CloudBottom = max / min of Ichimoku Senkou A and Senkou B (tenkan_len, kijun_len, senkou_len) as computed kijun_len bars earlier
Calculation: HTFCloudTop / HTFCloudBottom = max / min of Ichimoku Senkou A and Senkou B (same lengths) on HTF bars, last completed bar, without lag shift
Calculation: BollingerUpper / BollingerLower = SMA(close, band_len) +/- band_mult * STDEV(close, band_len)
Calculation: MACDLine / MACDSignal = MACD line (macd_fast, macd_slow) and its signal line (macd_signal) of close
Calculation: LongTrendPts = min(cap_trend, w_ma * (close > BestMA(long)) + w_ema * (EMA(close, ema_short) > EMA(close, ema_long)) + w_kama * (KAMA(close, kama_len) > KAMA(close, kama_len)[1]) + w_macd * (MACDLine > MACDSignal) + w_cloud * (close > CloudTop))
Calculation: ShortTrendPts = min(cap_trend, w_ma * (close < BestMA(short)) + w_ema * (EMA(close, ema_short) < EMA(close, ema_long)) + w_kama * (KAMA(close, kama_len) < KAMA(close, kama_len)[1]) + w_macd * (MACDLine < MACDSignal) + w_cloud * (close < CloudBottom))
Calculation: MainUpTL = value at this bar of the best-scoring uptrend line through two pivot lows of main_pivot_len; candidate score = touches * w_touch - violations * w_viol + bars_since_second_pivot * w_age; candidate valid when violations <= max_viol AND close >= line - tl_tol * ATR(atr_len); violation = close below line; touch = low within tl_tol * ATR(atr_len) of line, spaced chan_gap bars apart; MainUpTL undefined once that line is broken
Calculation: MainDownTL = mirror of MainUpTL on pivot highs of main_pivot_len (violation = close above line; touch = high within tl_tol * ATR(atr_len) of line)
Calculation: UpTLBreak = an uptrend line of any layer, not yet broken, breaks on this bar, where it breaks when two consecutive closes are below line - chan_break_atr * ATR(atr_len) or a low is below line - big_break_atr * ATR(atr_len); DnTLBreak = mirror for downtrend lines (closes above line + chan_break_atr * ATR, or high above line + big_break_atr * ATR)
Calculation: AtChannelSupport = a channel-validated uptrend line has low within tl_tol * ATR(atr_len) of the line, or a channel-validated downtrend line has low within tl_tol * ATR(atr_len) of its channel line; AtChannelResistance = a channel-validated downtrend line has high within tl_tol * ATR(atr_len) of the line, or a channel-validated uptrend line has high within tl_tol * ATR(atr_len) of its channel line; channel-validated = unbroken line with a parallel channel line, offset to the maximum excursion past the line, with at least chan_min_touch touches of that parallel line within chan_touch * ATR(atr_len), spaced chan_gap bars apart
Calculation: Squeeze = 2 * band_mult * STDEV(close, band_len) < squeeze_thr * ATR(atr_len); SqueezeBreakUp = Squeeze[1] AND NOT Squeeze AND close > SMA(close, band_len); SqueezeBreakDown = Squeeze[1] AND NOT Squeeze AND close < SMA(close, band_len)
Calculation: LongSRPts = clamp(0, cap_sr, w_htf_room * (HTFResDist undefined OR HTFResDist > htf_warn) + w_fib * (FibLevel(0.618) defined AND close > FibLevel(0.382)) + w_cloud2 * (close > CloudTop) + w_near * (close - NearestSupport < near_sr * ATR(atr_len)) + w_tl * (close > MainUpTL) + w_chan * AtChannelSupport + w_tl_break * DnTLBreak + w_tl_break_pd * (DnTLBreak AND PD) - w_chan_opp * AtChannelResistance + w_squeeze * SqueezeBreakUp - w_below * BelowSupport)
Calculation: ShortSRPts = clamp(0, cap_sr, w_htf_room * (HTFSupDist undefined OR HTFSupDist > htf_warn) + w_fib * (FibLevel(0.382) defined AND close < FibLevel(0.618)) + w_cloud2 * (close < CloudBottom) + w_near * (NearestResistance - close < near_sr * ATR(atr_len)) + w_tl * (close < MainDownTL) + w_chan * AtChannelResistance + w_tl_break * UpTLBreak + w_tl_break_pd * (UpTLBreak AND ND) - w_chan_opp * AtChannelSupport + w_squeeze * SqueezeBreakDown - w_above * AboveResistance)
Calculation: LongMomPts = min(cap_mom, w_rsi * (RSI(close, rsi_len) < rsi_ob) + w_pd * PD)
Calculation: ShortMomPts = min(cap_mom, w_rsi * (RSI(close, rsi_len) > rsi_os) + w_pd * ND)
Calculation: HTF = T1 if chart timeframe is shorter than T1, else T3
Calculation: HTFResDist = (nearest level above close among last confirmed HTF pivot high (htf_pivot_len), EMA(close, htf_ema_short, HTF, last completed bar), EMA(close, htf_ema_long, HTF, last completed bar)) minus close, as percent of close; undefined if none above close
Calculation: HTFSupDist = (close minus nearest level below close among last confirmed HTF pivot low (htf_pivot_len), EMA(close, htf_ema_short, HTF, last completed bar), EMA(close, htf_ema_long, HTF, last completed bar)), as percent of close; undefined if none below close
Calculation: HTFResBlock = HTFResDist defined AND HTFResDist <= htf_warn; HTFSupBlock = HTFSupDist defined AND HTFSupDist <= htf_warn
Calculation: LongHTFPts = min(cap_htf, w_htf_tr * (EMA(close, htf_ema_short, HTF, last completed bar) > EMA(close, htf_ema_long, HTF, last completed bar)) + w_htf_rsi * (RSI(close, htf_rsi_len, HTF, last completed bar) < htf_rsi_ob) + w_htf_cloud * (close > HTFCloudTop) + w_vol_strong * VolStrong - w_vol_weak * VolWeak); VolStrong = HTF last completed bar volume > vol_hi * SMA(volume, vol_len) on HTF; VolWeak = HTF last completed bar volume < vol_lo * SMA(volume, vol_len) on HTF
Calculation: ShortHTFPts = min(cap_htf, w_htf_tr * (EMA(close, htf_ema_short, HTF, last completed bar) < EMA(close, htf_ema_long, HTF, last completed bar)) + w_htf_rsi * (RSI(close, htf_rsi_len, HTF, last completed bar) > htf_rsi_os) + w_htf_cloud * (close < HTFCloudBottom) + w_vol_strong * VolStrong - w_vol_weak * VolWeak)
Calculation: LongScore = clamp(0, 100, TrendMult * LongTrendPts + SRMult * LongSRPts + LongMomPts + LongHTFPts + RRPoints(LongRR)); then if resistance levels above close within gate_dist * ATR(atr_len) include at least one strong source (HTF, Fibonacci, or pivot), LongScore = max(0, LongScore - sum of scores of those strong levels) in soft-block mode, or 0 in hard-block mode
Calculation: ShortScore = clamp(0, 100, TrendMult * ShortTrendPts + SRMult * ShortSRPts + ShortMomPts + ShortHTFPts + RRPoints(ShortRR)); then if support levels below close within gate_dist * ATR(atr_len) include at least one strong source (HTF, Fibonacci, or pivot), ShortScore = max(0, ShortScore - sum of scores of those strong levels) in soft-block mode, or 0 in hard-block mode
Calculation: ScoreDiff = LongScore - ShortScore
Calculation: TrailLongInst(anchor, rejection) = instant stop of a long trail anchored at anchor: Ref = SupportRank(1, anchor), or anchor - trail_init * ATR(atr_len) if undefined; Stop = Ref - sl_inst * ATR(atr_len); Risk = anchor - Stop; on each later bar, if close - anchor > Risk, Ref moves to the first k in 1..3 with SupportRank(k, close) > Ref and close - (SupportRank(k, close) - sl_inst * ATR(atr_len)) > Risk; if rejection is true and high >= TopResistance(1).near - rej_band * ATR(atr_len) and close < TopResistance(1).near, Ref moves to the highest TopSupport(k).far (k = 1 to 3) below close and above Ref; Tr2 = running max of (high - tr2_mult * ATR(atr_len)); Stop = running max of (Ref - sl_inst * ATR(atr_len), Tr2 - sl_inst * ATR(atr_len))
Calculation: TrailLongBar(anchor, rejection) = TrailLongInst(anchor, rejection) with sl_close in place of sl_inst in the stop terms
Calculation: TrailShortInst(anchor, rejection) = mirror of TrailLongInst: Ref = ResistanceRank(1, anchor), or anchor + trail_init * ATR(atr_len); Stop = Ref + sl_inst * ATR(atr_len); Risk = Stop - anchor; Ref moves to the first k with ResistanceRank(k, close) < Ref and Stop_k - close > Risk; rejection when low <= TopSupport(1).near + rej_band * ATR(atr_len) and close > TopSupport(1).near moves Ref to the lowest TopResistance(k).far above close and below Ref; Tr2 = running min of (low + tr2_mult * ATR(atr_len)); Stop = running min of (Ref + sl_inst * ATR(atr_len), Tr2 + sl_inst * ATR(atr_len))
Calculation: TrailShortBar(anchor, rejection) = TrailShortInst(anchor, rejection) with sl_close in place of sl_inst in the stop terms
