Calculation: Structure bias = on confirmed bars, close above the latest unbroken PivotHigh(high, swing length, swing length) plus an optional ATR(ATR length) × structure-break displacement sets bullish bias; close below the latest unbroken PivotLow(low, swing length, swing length) minus the same optional buffer sets bearish bias; each pivot level triggers once until replaced, and bias persists until the opposite break.
Calculation: Order-block candidate = at a confirmed swing high, select the nearest bullish candle among the pivot bar and the preceding source-search lookback bars; at a confirmed swing low, select the nearest bearish candle in that range. Reject it unless its high-low range is at least minimum order-block size × ATR(ATR length) at the source bar and, when the volume filter is enabled, its volume is at least the volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback) at the source bar. Use the source candle's open-close body or high-low range as zone boundaries. Create candidates only below the per-direction candidate capacity; candidate age starts at the source bar.
Calculation: Breaker quality score = round the weighted mean of six scores clipped to 0–100: structure-break displacement divided by its ATR cap; source-candle range divided by its ATR and impulse-score cap; 100 × (1 − abs(retest penetration ratio − ideal penetration ratio) / max(ideal penetration ratio, 1 − ideal penetration ratio)); 100 × confirmation rejection-wick fraction of candle range; 100 × (1 − min(bars from retest entry / retest window, 1)); and distance from break close to zone midpoint divided by break-bar ATR and distance-score cap. Use separate parameters for the six weights, the ideal penetration ratio, and the three score caps; normalize by the sum of weights, or use the unweighted mean if that sum is zero.
Calculation: Retained breaker zone = a confirmed breaker that passes the optional quality threshold, remains within the per-direction active-breaker cap (dropping the oldest beyond the cap), and has not exceeded the maximum age; it is active until invalidated and touched thereafter.
Long: structure bias is bullish.
Short: structure bias is bearish.
Long: a bullish-source order-block candidate at a confirmed swing high receives an unbroken bullish structure break before candidate expiry; within the retest window after the break, low reaches or falls below the zone top; before the retest window after entry expires, maximum penetration (zone top − lowest low since entry) / zone height meets minimum retest penetration and, before any close below zone bottom − ATR(ATR length) × confirmation displacement, close exceeds zone top + that displacement; the quality filter is disabled or breaker quality score meets its minimum.
Short: a bearish-source order-block candidate at a confirmed swing low receives an unbroken bearish structure break before candidate expiry; within the retest window after the break, high reaches or exceeds the zone bottom; before the retest window after entry expires, maximum penetration (highest high since entry − zone bottom) / zone height meets minimum retest penetration and, before any close above zone top + ATR(ATR length) × confirmation displacement, close falls below zone bottom − that displacement; the quality filter is disabled or breaker quality score meets its minimum.
Long: price first overlaps an active bullish breaker zone (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom).
Short: price first overlaps an active bearish breaker zone (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom).
Exit: long, close falls below a retained active or touched bullish breaker zone's bottom − ATR(ATR length) × invalidation displacement.
Exit: short, close rises above a retained active or touched bearish breaker zone's top + ATR(ATR length) × invalidation displacement.
