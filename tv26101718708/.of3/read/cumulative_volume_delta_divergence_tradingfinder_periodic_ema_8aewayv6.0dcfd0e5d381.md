Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = rolling sum or EMA over the CVD lookback of estimated volume delta, where estimated volume delta = volume * (2 * close - high - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low; volume is current-symbol volume or, in aggregate volume mode, the sum of current and configured source volumes.
Long: The latest and preceding confirmed pivot lows, each using the pivot lookback bars on each side and whose confirmation bar satisfies close[pivot lookback] > EMA(close, EMA lookback), both have cumulative volume delta[pivot lookback] < 0; latest pivot low < preceding pivot low; latest cumulative volume delta[pivot lookback] > preceding cumulative volume delta[pivot lookback]; current bar is fewer than the maximum pivot span bars after the latest pivot; the pivots are fewer than the maximum pivot span bars apart; preceding pivot low - latest pivot low > minimum price difference.
Short: The latest and preceding confirmed pivot highs, each using the pivot lookback bars on each side and whose confirmation bar satisfies close[pivot lookback] < EMA(close, EMA lookback), both have cumulative volume delta[pivot lookback] > 0; latest pivot high > preceding pivot high; latest cumulative volume delta[pivot lookback] < preceding cumulative volume delta[pivot lookback]; current bar is fewer than the maximum pivot span bars after the latest pivot; the pivots are fewer than the maximum pivot span bars apart; latest pivot high - preceding pivot high > minimum price difference.
Calculation: volume-delta histogram = rolling sum or exponential moving average over a histogram period, selected by an accumulation mode, of selected volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), with zero delta when high = low; selected volume is chart-symbol volume or, in external-volume mode, chart-symbol volume plus volume of the selected market symbols
