Calculation: Stop distance = ATR(period) × multiplier for ATR or STDEV stop mode; otherwise close × shared fixed-percent/reward-risk parameter / 100
Calculation: Target distance = ATR(the same period) × the same multiplier for ATR or STDEV take-profit mode; otherwise close × the shared fixed-percent/reward-risk parameter / 100
Calculation: Long stop level = for static volatility mode, selected price source − stop distance on entry; for trailing mode, the greater of selected price source − stop distance and the prior long stop level; for fast trail mode, initialize to selected price source − stop distance and, when high exceeds prior high, raise it to the greater of its current level plus the change in ohlc4 and its current level; for volatility mode, initialize to selected price source − stop distance and thereafter use the greater of the highest selected price source since entry minus stop distance and the prior long stop level; for chandelier mode, initialize to high − stop distance and thereafter use the greater of the highest high since entry minus stop distance and the prior long stop level; for activator mode, initialize to selected price source − stop distance and thereafter add sign(close − the prior level, or prior close if missing) × entry stop distance / stop multiplier / stop period × bars since entry to the prior level; for moving-average mode, initialize to selected price source − stop distance and thereafter add sign(prior close − prior stop level) × the absolute change in the selected moving average of close to the prior level; for SAR mode, use the Parabolic SAR recurrence with separate initial acceleration, increment, and maximum parameters, resetting to the lesser of prior low and the extreme on long entry, then clamp to the lesser of its level and low two bars ago
Calculation: Short stop level = for static volatility mode, selected price source + stop distance on entry; for trailing mode, the lesser of selected price source + stop distance and the prior short stop level; for fast trail mode, initialize to selected price source + stop distance and, when low is below prior low, lower it to the lesser of its current level plus the change in ohlc4 and its current level; for volatility mode, initialize to selected price source + stop distance and thereafter use the lesser of the lowest selected price source since entry plus stop distance and the prior short stop level; for chandelier mode, initialize to low + stop distance and thereafter use the lesser of the lowest low since entry plus stop distance and the prior short stop level; for activator mode, initialize to selected price source + stop distance and thereafter add sign(close − the prior level, or prior close if missing) × entry stop distance / stop multiplier / stop period × bars since entry to the prior level; for moving-average mode, initialize to selected price source + stop distance and thereafter add sign(prior close − prior stop level) × the absolute change in the selected moving average of close to the prior level; for SAR mode, use the Parabolic SAR recurrence with separate initial acceleration, increment, and maximum parameters, resetting to the greater of prior high and the extreme on short entry, then clamp to the greater of its level and high two bars ago
Calculation: Long target level = entry price + target distance in volatility take-profit mode, or entry price + (entry price − long stop level) × reward-risk parameter in risk/reward mode
Calculation: Short target level = entry price − target distance in volatility take-profit mode, or entry price − (short stop level − entry price) × reward-risk parameter in risk/reward mode
Exit: long, low is at or below the long stop level when a stop is enabled OR high is at or above the long target level when take-profit is enabled
Exit: short, high is at or above the short stop level when a stop is enabled OR low is at or below the short target level when take-profit is enabled
