Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, previous-volume baseline lookback)[1], calculated on chart bars and separately on T1 bars when evaluating T1 pivots.
Calculation: Active support/resistance levels = confirmed chart pivot highs and lows using separate resistance and support left-lookback parameters and a shared right-confirmation parameter; chart reaction is the confirming close’s distance from the pivot divided by ATR at the pivot. When enabled and T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, calculate T1 pivots, source close, ATR, relative volume, and pivot time on T1 bars and sample only last completed T1 values on chart bars; validate T1 levels against the current completed chart bar. Create resistance only when pivot price minus source close >= source ATR * minimum reaction, and support only when source close minus pivot price >= source ATR * minimum reaction; also require the level not to have been crossed, using close or wick according to the configured break mode, and require pivot relative volume >= the volume threshold when creation-volume filtering is enabled. Keep the pivot price fixed, with scale equal to source ATR and buffer equal to zero, tick count * minimum tick, or detection ATR * buffer factor according to configured buffer mode. Merge same-side levels within max(minimum tick, current chart ATR * minimum spacing), retaining the existing price, scale, and buffer; count chart evidence only for pivots separated by more than the confirmation parameter and T1 evidence only for distinct pivot times. Track best normalized close reaction, highest qualifying pivot relative volume, source types, visits, and one rejection per visit; visits require the configured outside-bar separation. Retire the oldest level at the active-level limit. A level remains active until broken or until the configured bar-limit expiry applies.
Calculation: Level grade = grade base + (normalized close reaction >= higher reaction threshold ? higher reaction points : normalized close reaction >= lower reaction threshold ? lower reaction points : no reaction points) + min(evidence bonus cap, positive part(separated evidence count - evidence offset)).
Calculation: Level score = clamp(score base + min(reaction score cap, best normalized close reaction * reaction score weight) + min(evidence score cap, positive part(separated evidence count - evidence score offset) * evidence score weight) + source bonus for chart and T1 confluence + min(volume score cap, positive part(highest pivot relative volume - volume score offset) * volume score weight) when pivot relative volume exists + min(rejection score cap, rejection count * rejection score weight) - min(test penalty cap, positive part(visit count - test penalty offset) * test penalty weight) - min(age penalty cap, bars since creation / age scale), score floor, score ceiling).
Calculation: Displayed levels = up to separate support and resistance counts of qualifying active levels on the matching side of the prior chart close, spaced by current chart ATR * minimum spacing; calculate selection from the prior close, rank by configured creation-recency, score, or normalized distance priority, and select successively. Normalized distance is absolute distance from the reference close divided by max(current chart ATR, minimum tick); recency and score priorities use distance as a secondary rank, while distance priority favors higher evidence as a tie-breaker.
Calculation: Eligible event level = active level with grade >= minimum grade and score >= minimum score, and either event scope is all qualifying levels or the level is among the displayed levels selected from the prior chart close.
Long: On a completed chart bar, an eligible resistance level breaks when the configured break price (close or high) > level price + fixed level buffer, and transition-volume filtering is disabled or current chart relative volume >= its threshold.
Short: On a completed chart bar, an eligible support level breaks when the configured break price (close or low) < level price - fixed level buffer, and transition-volume filtering is disabled or current chart relative volume >= its threshold.
Long: When retest alerts are enabled, an eligible resistance break creates a pending retest at that level with tolerance max(minimum tick, level ATR scale * retest tolerance); on a later chart bar within the retest window, before invalidation by close < level price - tolerance, low is between level price - tolerance and level price + tolerance, close > level price, close > open, and transition-volume filtering is disabled or current chart relative volume >= its threshold.
Short: When retest alerts are enabled, an eligible support break creates a pending retest at that level with tolerance max(minimum tick, level ATR scale * retest tolerance); on a later chart bar within the retest window, before invalidation by close > level price + tolerance, high is between level price - tolerance and level price + tolerance, close < level price, close < open, and transition-volume filtering is disabled or current chart relative volume >= its threshold.
Long: On a completed chart bar, an eligible active support level that has not broken or expired rejects when high >= level price - contact tolerance and low <= level price + contact tolerance, low >= level price - contact tolerance, close > level price + contact tolerance, and close > open; rejection alerts are enabled, the level has not already rejected in this visit, its rejection cooldown has elapsed, and reaction-volume filtering is disabled or current chart relative volume >= its threshold. Contact tolerance = max(minimum tick, level ATR scale * contact tolerance factor).
Short: On a completed chart bar, an eligible active resistance level that has not broken or expired rejects when high >= level price - contact tolerance and low <= level price + contact tolerance, high <= level price + contact tolerance, close < level price - contact tolerance, and close < open; rejection alerts are enabled, the level has not already rejected in this visit, its rejection cooldown has elapsed, and reaction-volume filtering is disabled or current chart relative volume >= its threshold. Contact tolerance = max(minimum tick, level ATR scale * contact tolerance factor).
