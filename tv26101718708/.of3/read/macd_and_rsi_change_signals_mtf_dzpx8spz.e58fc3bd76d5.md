Calculation: low RSI change = change(RSI(log(low), price RSI lookback))
Calculation: fast-slow RSI change = change(RSI(MA(source price, shorter lookback, source MA type) - MA(source price, longer lookback, source MA type), oscillator RSI lookback))
Calculation: smoothed RSI change = change(RSI(MA(fast-slow RSI change, smoothing lookback, signal MA type), oscillator RSI lookback))
Calculation: midpoint = average(fast-slow RSI change, smoothed RSI change)
