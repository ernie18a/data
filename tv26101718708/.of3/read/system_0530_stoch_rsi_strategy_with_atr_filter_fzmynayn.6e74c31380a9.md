Calculation: StochRSI %K = SMA(Stoch(RSI(close, RSI lookback), stochastic lookback), K smoothing); StochRSI %D = SMA(%K, D smoothing); calculate on chart bars and T1 bars with the same parameters, and return T1 values to chart bars using lookahead off (the current T1 bar may still be forming).
Long: While flat, start or restart a long wait when chart %K[1] crosses above chart %D[1] AND chart %K[1] <= the long trigger threshold AND (cooldown is disabled OR chart bars since the previous long entry signal >= the cooldown); enter if, within the wait window counting the trigger bar, T1 current-bar %K > T1 current-bar %D AND T1 current-bar %K < the long confirmation threshold; an eligible short trigger replaces the wait, and the wait counter advances on chart bars without a trigger condition.
Short: While flat, start or restart a short wait when chart %K[1] crosses below chart %D[1] AND chart %K[1] >= the short trigger threshold AND (cooldown is disabled OR chart bars since the previous short entry signal >= the cooldown); enter if, within the wait window counting the trigger bar, T1 current-bar %K < T1 current-bar %D AND T1 current-bar %K > the short confirmation threshold; an eligible long trigger replaces the wait, and the wait counter advances on chart bars without a trigger condition.
Exit: long, close < low of the long entry signal bar OR chart current-bar %K > the long extreme threshold OR T1 current-bar %K > the long extreme threshold OR a chart-bar %K crossunder of %D is pending since entry or the most recent take-profit and T1 current-bar %K < T1 %K on the last completed T1 bar.
Exit: short, close > high of the short entry signal bar OR chart current-bar %K < the short extreme threshold OR T1 current-bar %K < the short extreme threshold OR a chart-bar %K crossover of %D is pending since entry or the most recent take-profit and T1 current-bar %K > T1 %K on the last completed T1 bar.
