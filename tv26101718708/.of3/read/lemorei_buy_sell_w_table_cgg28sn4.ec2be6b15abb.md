Calculation: Opening window = the configured initial count of chart bars in the RTH session, with RTH membership read from the current T1 session timestamp mapped onto chart bars
Calculation: RSI strength = RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its selected moving average over the RSI-MA lookback AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its value one bar ago AND (opening-window RSI-slope relaxation enabled in the opening window OR the RSI moving average > its value one bar ago) AND (RSI cap disabled OR RSI < cap)
Calculation: Regime permission = regime switch disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) >= trend threshold OR (ignition enabled AND ADX < trend threshold AND ADX is strictly rising over the ignition lookback AND (ignition VWAP filter disabled OR close >= VWAP(hlc3))) OR (opening-window regime bypass enabled AND opening window)
Calculation: Green-candle confirmation = opening-window green-candle bypass enabled in the opening window OR green-candle confirmation is disabled for the applicable mode OR close > open; the applicable mode uses trend confirmation when ADX >= trend threshold or neither trend nor ignition mode applies, and ignition confirmation otherwise
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), defined only when SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0
Calculation: Entry cooldown = no unreset long entry OR chart bars since the last long entry >= entry cooldown
Calculation: Long stop level = low minus ATR(ATR lookback) times initial stop offset at the first long entry; on each additional long entry take the maximum of the prior stop and that entry-bar value; on each held bar when trailing is enabled also take the maximum with EMA(close, fast lookback) minus ATR(ATR lookback) times trailing offset
Long: MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses above its signal line AND RSI strength AND Regime permission AND close >= VWAP(hlc3) AND Green-candle confirmation AND Entry cooldown
Long: MACD line > signal line AND RSI strength AND close crosses above EMA(close, fast lookback) AND (pullback-dip requirement disabled OR a close was below EMA(close, fast lookback) within the pullback lookback) AND Regime permission AND close >= VWAP(hlc3) AND Green-candle confirmation AND Entry cooldown
Long: opening momentum entry enabled AND opening window AND close >= VWAP(hlc3) AND (ORB filter disabled OR (high-break mode AND high > the highest high of the opening-range bars) OR (close-break mode AND close > the highest high of the opening-range bars)) AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback) AND (EMA alignment requirement disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback)) AND MACD line > signal line AND (opening histogram-rise requirement disabled OR MACD histogram > its value one bar ago) AND RSI strength AND Relative volume >= opening relative-volume threshold AND (gap filter disabled OR previous close <= 0 OR absolute percentage gap from the first RTH bar open to the previous chart-bar close <= gap threshold) AND Regime permission AND Green-candle confirmation AND Entry cooldown
Long: burst entry enabled AND close >= VWAP(hlc3) AND Relative volume >= burst volume threshold AND high - low >= ATR(ATR lookback) times burst range factor AND (breakout requirement disabled OR close > the highest high over the prior breakout lookback bars, excluding the current bar) AND (close-near-high requirement disabled OR (close - low) / (high - low) >= close-near-high threshold) AND (EMA alignment requirement disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback)) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its value one bar ago AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its selected moving average AND (RSI cap disabled OR RSI < cap) AND MACD line > its value one bar ago AND MACD histogram > its value one bar ago AND (burst regime bypass enabled OR Regime permission) AND Green-candle confirmation AND Entry cooldown
Long: buy-more reminder enabled AND position side is long AND Regime permission AND close >= VWAP(hlc3) AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback) AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI strength AND (histogram-flip requirement disabled OR MACD histogram >= 0 AND MACD histogram > its value one bar ago) AND (ADX-rise requirement disabled OR ADX > its value one bar ago) AND chart bars since last long entry >= minimum bars after entry AND (no prior buy-more reminder OR chart bars since last reminder >= reminder cooldown) AND no regular long-entry signal on this bar
Exit: long, low <= Long stop level when wick trigger is selected OR close <= Long stop level when close trigger is selected
Exit: long, position P/L <= configured maximum-loss threshold
Exit: long, take-profit-on-turn-down enabled AND position P/L > 0 AND EMA(close, fast lookback) < its value one bar ago AND close < EMA(close, fast lookback)
Exit: long, MACD exit enabled AND MACD line crosses below its signal line AND (profit-only restriction disabled OR position P/L > 0)
