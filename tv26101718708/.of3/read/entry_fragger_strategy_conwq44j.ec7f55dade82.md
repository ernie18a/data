Short: On a completed chart bar, the previous bar's PVSRA color is green and the current bar's PVSRA color is red or violet.
Exit: long, on a completed chart bar, the previous bar's PVSRA color is green and the current bar's PVSRA color is red or violet, open > EMA(close, lookback), close > EMA(close, lookback) + standard deviation(close, same lookback) / scaling parameter, at least one earlier green PVSRA bar had open and close above EMA(close, lookback), and the count of green PVSRA bars with open and close above EMA(close, lookback) since the previous filtered sell signal (or start) >= the short threshold.
