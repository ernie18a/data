Calculation: Directional Convexity Index = 100 × sqrt((DX(+DI(DCX lookback), −DI(DCX lookback)) / 100) × ((1 − convexity weight) × clamp(abs(close − close[DCX lookback]) / max(sum(abs(change(close)), DCX lookback), ε), efficiency floor, 1) + convexity weight × (1 + (RMA(max(change(change(close)), 0), DCX lookback) − RMA(max(−change(change(close)), 0), DCX lookback)) / max(RMA(max(change(change(close)), 0), DCX lookback) + RMA(max(−change(change(close)), 0), DCX lookback), ε)) / 2); calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the current forming T1 bar onto chart bars.
Calculation: Adaptive Volatility Envelope upper/lower = selected EMA, HMA, or ALMA of (high + low + close) / 3 over the AVE lookback ± adaptive multiplier × blended volatility; blended volatility = w × linear-interpolated percentile(TR, AVE lookback, TR percentile parameter) + (1 − w) × ATR(AVE lookback); w = clamp((stdev(TR, AVE lookback) / max(EMA(TR, AVE lookback), ε)) / volatility-of-volatility reference, 0, 1); adaptive multiplier = base multiplier × (1 + adaptation parameter × (2 × clamp(Directional Convexity Index / 100, 0, 1) − 1)); ALMA offset and sigma are separate parameters; calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the current forming T1 bar onto chart bars.
Long: chart close > Adaptive Volatility Envelope upper AND Directional Convexity Index > entry threshold AND rising(Directional Convexity Index, rise lookback) on chart bars AND +DI(DCX lookback) > −DI(DCX lookback) from the current forming T1 bar AND (intermarket filter disabled OR filter-series close from the current forming T2 bar < chart-bar EMA of filter-series close sampled from T2, over the filter lookback)
Short: chart close < Adaptive Volatility Envelope lower AND Directional Convexity Index > entry threshold AND falling(Directional Convexity Index, rise lookback) on chart bars AND −DI(DCX lookback) > +DI(DCX lookback) from the current forming T1 bar AND (intermarket filter disabled OR filter-series close from the current forming T2 bar > chart-bar EMA of filter-series close sampled from T2, over the filter lookback)
Exit: Long, chart low ≤ min(Adaptive Volatility Envelope lower at entry, entry price − stop multiple × ATR(weekly ATR lookback, current forming T3 bar sampled at entry)) OR chart high ≥ entry price + target R multiple × abs(entry price − that stop)
Exit: Short, chart high ≥ max(Adaptive Volatility Envelope upper at entry, entry price + stop multiple × ATR(weekly ATR lookback, current forming T3 bar sampled at entry)) OR chart low ≤ entry price − target R multiple × abs(entry price − that stop)
Exit: Long, trailing enabled AND chart low ≤ max(Adaptive Volatility Envelope lower, entry price − ATR(weekly ATR lookback, current forming T3 bar sampled on chart bars))
Exit: Short, trailing enabled AND chart high ≥ min(Adaptive Volatility Envelope upper, entry price + ATR(weekly ATR lookback, current forming T3 bar sampled on chart bars))
