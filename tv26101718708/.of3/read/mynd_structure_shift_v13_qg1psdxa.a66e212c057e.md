Calculation: Bar range = high - low
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / bar range, or 0.5 when bar range is zero
Calculation: Structure bias = the direction of the latest close crossover of a confirmed swing level established before that bar's pivot update; +1 for a cross above the swing high, -1 for a cross below the swing low, initially 0
Calculation: HTF bias = UP when T1 close > EMA(T1 close, EMA lookback, T1 bar still forming) and (slope filter off or the mapped T1 EMA on this chart bar > its value on the previous chart bar) and (ADX floor off or ADX(T1, DI length, ADX smoothing, T1 bar still forming) >= ADX threshold); DOWN uses the inverse comparisons; otherwise FLAT. These T1 values are sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Bull sweep = low < most recent confirmed swing low - minimum pierce ATR multiple * ATR(shared ATR lookback) AND close > that swing low AND (HTF proximity filter off OR abs(low - prior completed T1 low) <= level tolerance ATR multiple * ATR(shared ATR lookback))
Calculation: Bear sweep = high > most recent confirmed swing high + minimum pierce ATR multiple * ATR(shared ATR lookback) AND close < that swing high AND (HTF proximity filter off OR abs(high - prior completed T1 high) <= level tolerance ATR multiple * ATR(shared ATR lookback))
Calculation: Bull displacement = bar range >= displacement ATR multiple * ATR(shared ATR lookback) AND close position >= 1 - close-position fraction AND close > open
Calculation: Bear displacement = bar range >= displacement ATR multiple * ATR(shared ATR lookback) AND close position <= close-position fraction AND close < open
Calculation: Quality score(direction) = 25 * (min(sweep pierce depth in ATR / (3 * max(minimum pierce ATR multiple, 0.01)), 1) + min((bar range / ATR(shared ATR lookback)) / (1.5 * displacement ATR multiple), 1) + (SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0 ? min((volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback)) / (1.5 * volume multiple), 1) : 0.5) + HTF alignment score), where HTF alignment score is 1 when HTF bias agrees with direction, 0 when it opposes, and 0.5 when flat
Long: a confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is higher than the preceding confirmed swing low
Short: a confirmed swing high is lower than the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low
Long: close crosses above the most recent confirmed swing high established before the current bar's pivot update
Short: close crosses below the most recent confirmed swing low established before the current bar's pivot update
Long: Bull sweep
Short: Bear sweep
Long: a Bull sweep is pending and, on the sweep bar or within its confirmation window (with one extension if grace is enabled), Bull displacement AND volume >= volume multiple * SMA(volume, volume lookback); a qualifying displacement confirms before invalidation is checked, while a nonconfirming close below the sweep bar's low invalidates the setup; also, if enabled, HTF bias is not DOWN, structure bias is +1 OR bars since the last structure break >= staleness lookback, and Quality score(long) >= minimum score; if cooldown is enabled, no prior long signal occurred within the cooldown lookback
Short: a Bear sweep is pending and, on the sweep bar or within its confirmation window (with one extension if grace is enabled), Bear displacement AND volume >= volume multiple * SMA(volume, volume lookback); a qualifying displacement confirms before invalidation is checked, while a nonconfirming close above the sweep bar's high invalidates the setup; also, if enabled, HTF bias is not UP, structure bias is -1 OR bars since the last structure break >= staleness lookback, and Quality score(short) >= minimum score; if cooldown is enabled, no prior short signal occurred within the cooldown lookback
