Calculation: MA(x, lookback) = ALMA(x, lookback, offset, sigma), HMA(x, lookback), SMA(x, lookback), or VWMA(x, lookback) for the corresponding selected type; otherwise EMA(x, lookback). The selected type and lookback are shared across calls.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum(source × weight) / cumulative sum(weight), accumulated on chart bars and reset at the selected T1 period change or configured Asian, London, or New York session start in New York time; weight = volume when present and positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Anchored standard deviation = sqrt(max(0, cumulative sum(source² × weight) / cumulative sum(weight) − Anchored VWAP²)), accumulated since the same reset.
Calculation: Upper band = Anchored VWAP + band multiplier × anchored standard deviation; lower band = Anchored VWAP − band multiplier × anchored standard deviation.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) below the preceding confirmed pivot low, with RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the new pivot above RSI at the preceding pivot; when the zone restriction is enabled, either pivot RSI must be at or below the oversold threshold.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) above the preceding confirmed pivot high, with RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the new pivot below RSI at the preceding pivot; when the zone restriction is enabled, either pivot RSI must be at or above the overbought threshold.
Calculation: VWAP normalized slope = (Anchored VWAP − Anchored VWAP[slope lookback]) / ATR(ATR lookback) when ATR > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Normalized close-to-VWAP gap = (close − Anchored VWAP) / ATR(ATR lookback) when ATR > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: VWAP trend warm-up = chart bars since the selected VWAP reset >= max(color warm-up, slope lookback).
Long: On chart bars, MA(Heikin-Ashi close, MA lookback) > MA(Heikin-Ashi open, MA lookback).
Short: On chart bars, MA(Heikin-Ashi close, MA lookback) <= MA(Heikin-Ashi open, MA lookback).
Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= oversold threshold and RSI(close, RSI lookback) < overbought threshold.
Short: RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= overbought threshold.
Long: Bullish RSI divergence occurred within the divergence recency lookback.
Short: Bearish RSI divergence occurred within the divergence recency lookback.
Long: VWAP trend color is enabled AND VWAP trend warm-up is met AND ((price-to-VWAP mode AND normalized close-to-VWAP gap > price buffer) OR (slope mode AND VWAP normalized slope > slope dead zone) OR (combined mode AND normalized close-to-VWAP gap > price buffer AND VWAP normalized slope > slope dead zone)).
Short: VWAP trend color is enabled AND VWAP trend warm-up is met AND ((price-to-VWAP mode AND normalized close-to-VWAP gap < −price buffer) OR (slope mode AND VWAP normalized slope < −slope dead zone) OR (combined mode AND normalized close-to-VWAP gap < −price buffer AND VWAP normalized slope < −slope dead zone)).
Long: On chart bars, MA(Heikin-Ashi close, MA lookback) crosses above MA(Heikin-Ashi open, MA lookback) AND a lower-band touch under the selected touch mode occurred within min(band recency lookback, chart bars since VWAP reset) chart bars AND chart bars since VWAP reset >= minimum wait AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= oversold threshold within the RSI recency lookback chart bars) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish RSI divergence occurred within the divergence recency lookback chart bars) AND (T2 trend filter disabled OR MA(Heikin-Ashi close sampled from T2) > MA(Heikin-Ashi open sampled from T2), with T2 values using lookahead off and the MA evaluated on chart bars) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum ATR) AND (close-only setting disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed). A lower-band touch means low <= lower band in wick-touch mode, close <= lower band in close-beyond mode, or low <= lower band AND close > lower band in wick-through-close-back mode.
Short: On chart bars, MA(Heikin-Ashi close, MA lookback) crosses below MA(Heikin-Ashi open, MA lookback) AND an upper-band touch under the selected touch mode occurred within min(band recency lookback, chart bars since VWAP reset) chart bars AND chart bars since VWAP reset >= minimum wait AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= overbought threshold within the RSI recency lookback chart bars) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish RSI divergence occurred within the divergence recency lookback chart bars) AND (T2 trend filter disabled OR MA(Heikin-Ashi close sampled from T2) < MA(Heikin-Ashi open sampled from T2), with T2 values using lookahead off and the MA evaluated on chart bars) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum ATR) AND (close-only setting disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed). An upper-band touch means high >= upper band in wick-touch mode, close >= upper band in close-beyond mode, or high >= upper band AND close < upper band in wick-through-close-back mode.
