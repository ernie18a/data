Calculation: Close location = (regular-session T1 close - low of the still-forming regular-session T1 bar) / (high - low) of that bar, expressed as a percentage; when high equals low, use the neutral-location parameter. Calculate on T1 bars and sample on chart bars.
Calculation: Range ATR = (high - low of the still-forming regular-session T1 bar) / ATR(T1 lookback, calculated on T1 bars through the still-forming bar and sampled on chart bars), expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Relative strength = ROC(regular-session close, RS lookback, T1 still-forming bar sampled on chart bars) - ROC(benchmark close, same lookback and T1 bar state).
Calculation: Relative volume = cumulative regular-session volume through the current T4 slot / mean prior-session cumulative volume at that same T4 slot, using min(history-lookback parameter, history-cap parameter) sessions; calculate on T4 bars, including the forming bar, and sample on chart bars. It is unavailable outside the live regular session or when no prior-slot average exists.
Calculation: EMA and VWAP comparisons = above20 iff regular-session T1 close > EMA(close, short EMA lookback, calculated on T1 bars through the still-forming bar and sampled on chart bars); above50 and above200 use their respective EMA lookbacks; above T2 VWAP and above T3 VWAP iff T1 close >= VWAP(hlc3, anchored to T2 or T3 respectively, calculated on T1 bars through the still-forming bar and sampled on chart bars); fully bullish iff T1 close > EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200; fully bearish iff T1 close < EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200.
Calculation: Day breakdown = T1 close < low of the last completed T1 bar OR (T1 close < close of the last completed T1 bar AND close location < breakdown-location parameter).
Calculation: Day weakening = NOT day breakdown AND T1 close < T1 open AND close location < weakening-location parameter.
Calculation: Healthy pullback = NOT day breakdown AND T1 close >= close of the last completed T1 bar AND close location >= healthy-location parameter.
Calculation: Strong day = T1 close > close of the last completed T1 bar AND T1 close > T1 open AND close location >= strong-location parameter.
Calculation: Above PDH = T1 close > (high of the last completed T1 bar when the regular session is live, otherwise high of the current T1 bar); Above PDL uses the corresponding lows; above prior close = T1 close >= close of the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Stretch danger = (Range ATR >= danger-range parameter AND close location < danger-range-location parameter) OR close location < danger-location parameter OR (T1 close - high of the current T1 bar) / ATR(T1 lookback, calculated on T1 bars through the still-forming bar and sampled on chart bars) <= -HOD-distance parameter.
Calculation: Scalp long context = NOT (day breakdown OR (T1 close < T1 open AND close location < scalp-location parameter) OR (NOT above200 AND relative strength < zero-RS parameter) OR stretch danger) AND above200 AND relative strength >= zero-RS parameter AND (strong day OR healthy pullback OR (T1 close > T1 open AND close location >= scalp-location parameter)) AND (NOT regular session live OR relative volume unavailable OR relative volume >= scalp-volume parameter).
Calculation: EOD score = piecewise(strong day: EOD-strong-day points; healthy pullback: EOD-healthy-day points; day weakening: EOD-weakening points; day breakdown: EOD-breakdown points; otherwise: EOD-mixed-day points) + piecewise(fully bullish: EOD-fully-bullish points; above20 AND above50 AND above200: EOD-three-EMA points; above50 AND above200: EOD-two-EMA points; above200: EOD-one-EMA points; otherwise: EOD-no-EMA points) + piecewise(relative strength >= RS-high parameter: EOD-RS-high points; >= RS-strong parameter: EOD-RS-strong points; >= zero-RS parameter: EOD-RS-positive points; >= RS-weak parameter: EOD-RS-neutral points; otherwise: EOD-RS-negative points) + piecewise(above T2 VWAP AND above T3 VWAP: EOD-both-VWAP points; above either: EOD-one-VWAP points; otherwise: EOD-no-VWAP points) + piecewise(above PDH: EOD-PDH points; above prior close: EOD-prior-close points; above PDL: EOD-PDL points; otherwise: EOD-no-level points) + piecewise(Range ATR <= EOD-low-range parameter: EOD-low-range points; <= EOD-mid-range parameter: EOD-mid-range points; <= EOD-high-range parameter: EOD-high-range points; otherwise: EOD-extreme-range points) + piecewise(NOT regular session live OR relative volume unavailable: EOD-volume-unavailable points; strong day OR healthy pullback: piecewise(relative volume >= RVOL-high parameter: EOD-bull-volume-high points; >= RVOL-confirm parameter: EOD-bull-volume-confirm points; >= RVOL-normal parameter: EOD-bull-volume-normal points; >= RVOL-minimum parameter: EOD-bull-volume-minimum points; otherwise: EOD-bull-volume-low points); day breakdown OR day weakening: piecewise(relative volume >= RVOL-confirm parameter: EOD-bear-volume-high points; >= RVOL-normal parameter: EOD-bear-volume-normal points; otherwise: EOD-bear-volume-low points); otherwise: piecewise(relative volume >= RVOL-confirm parameter: EOD-other-volume-high points; >= RVOL-minimum parameter: EOD-other-volume-normal points; otherwise: EOD-other-volume-low points)).
Calculation: After-hours score = piecewise(strong day: after-strong-day points; healthy pullback: after-healthy-day points; day weakening: after-weakening points; day breakdown: after-breakdown points; otherwise: after-mixed-day points) + piecewise(fully bullish: after-fully-bullish points; above20 AND above50 AND above200: after-three-EMA points; above50 AND above200: after-two-EMA points; above200: after-one-EMA points; otherwise: after-no-EMA points) + piecewise(relative strength >= RS-high parameter: after-RS-high points; >= RS-strong parameter: after-RS-strong points; >= zero-RS parameter: after-RS-positive points; >= RS-weak parameter: after-RS-neutral points; otherwise: after-RS-negative points) + piecewise(above T2 VWAP AND above T3 VWAP: after-both-VWAP points; above either: after-one-VWAP points; otherwise: after-no-VWAP points) + piecewise(close location >= after-high-location parameter: after-high-location points; >= after-upper-location parameter: after-upper-location points; >= after-mid-location parameter: after-mid-location points; >= after-low-location parameter: after-low-location points; otherwise: after-bottom-location points) + piecewise(above PDH: after-PDH points; above prior close: after-prior-close points; above PDL: after-PDL points; otherwise: after-no-level points) + piecewise(NOT regular session live OR relative volume unavailable: after-volume-unavailable points; strong day OR healthy pullback: piecewise(relative volume >= RVOL-high parameter: after-bull-volume-high points; >= RVOL-confirm parameter: after-bull-volume-confirm points; >= RVOL-minimum parameter: after-bull-volume-minimum points; otherwise: after-bull-volume-low points); day breakdown OR day weakening: piecewise(relative volume >= RVOL-confirm parameter: after-bear-volume-high points; otherwise: after-bear-volume-low points); otherwise: after-other-volume points).
