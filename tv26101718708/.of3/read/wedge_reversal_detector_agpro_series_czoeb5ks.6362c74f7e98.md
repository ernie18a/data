Calculation: Strict wedge = on a bar with a newly confirmed pivot, form pairs from the latest stored-pivot-count confirmed pivot highs and lows, using the same pivot length on both sides; require the two pivot intervals to overlap, the span from the earliest first pivot to the latest second pivot to meet the minimum span, and the latest second pivot to be within the maximum candidate age. Interpolate each rail through its pivot pair; require initial width at span start >= ATR(lookback) × minimum initial-width ATR, final width at span end > minimum price increment, final width / initial width <= maximum final-width ratio, absolute change in the rails' midpoint >= ATR(lookback) × minimum travel ATR, and at least the minimum extra-touch count of other stored pivots within ATR(lookback) × touch tolerance ATR of their corresponding rail during the span. A rising wedge has both rail slopes positive and the lower slope greater than the upper slope; a falling wedge has both slopes negative and the upper slope less than the lower slope. Select the qualifying strict wedge with highest maturity, breaking ties by latest end bar. Activate it only when close is within the rails plus or minus ATR(lookback) × containment tolerance, its pivots differ from the active wedge, no break episode is active, and the rearm cooldown since the last resolution has elapsed.
Calculation: Maturity = clamp(clamp((1 − final width / initial width) × convergence scale, 0, score cap) × convergence weight + clamp(span / minimum span × span scale, 0, score cap) × span weight, 0, score cap)
Long: active strict falling wedge is unbroken and uninvalidated AND bar is later than its end bar AND confirmed bar AND break cooldown since the previous break has elapsed AND (close > upper rail + ATR(lookback) × break buffer when close-break is required, otherwise high > upper rail + ATR(lookback) × break buffer)
Short: active strict rising wedge is unbroken and uninvalidated AND bar is later than its end bar AND confirmed bar AND break cooldown since the previous break has elapsed AND (close < lower rail − ATR(lookback) × break buffer when close-break is required, otherwise low < lower rail − ATR(lookback) × break buffer)
Exit: long, after an upward break, confirmed close < the lower rail value at the break bar − ATR(lookback at break) × invalidation buffer
Exit: short, after a downward break, confirmed close > the upper rail value at the break bar + ATR(lookback at break) × invalidation buffer
Exit: long, while the upward-break target episode is active, high >= target center − target half-width before the projection window expires; target center = break close + initial wedge width × target distance multiplier, and target half-width = ATR(lookback at break) × target half-width ATR
Exit: short, while the downward-break target episode is active, low <= target center + target half-width before the projection window expires; target center = break close − initial wedge width × target distance multiplier, and target half-width = ATR(lookback at break) × target half-width ATR
