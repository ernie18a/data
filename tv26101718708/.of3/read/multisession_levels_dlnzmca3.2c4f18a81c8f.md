Long: (T1 levels enabled AND, on chart bars, close crosses above the high of the previous completed T1 bar) OR (T2 levels enabled AND, on chart bars, close crosses above the high of the previous completed T2 bar) OR (T3 levels enabled AND, on chart bars, close crosses above the high of the previous completed T3 bar)
Short: (T1 levels enabled AND, on chart bars, close crosses below the low of the previous completed T1 bar) OR (T2 levels enabled AND, on chart bars, close crosses below the low of the previous completed T2 bar) OR (T3 levels enabled AND, on chart bars, close crosses below the low of the previous completed T3 bar)
