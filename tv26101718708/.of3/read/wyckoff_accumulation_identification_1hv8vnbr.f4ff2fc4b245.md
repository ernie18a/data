Calculation: Spring test = low < SC low AND close >= SC low * spring reclaim factor AND close <= AR high * spring upper factor AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum range) > spring wick fraction
Calculation: SOS breakout = close > AR high AND volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * volume spike multiple * SOS volume factor AND close > open
Calculation: LPS test = bars since SOS >= LPS wait bars AND low <= AR high * LPS upper band factor AND low >= AR high * LPS lower band factor AND volume < SMA(volume, volume average lookback) AND low > SC low AND close >= open
Calculation: Accumulation phase = On each completed chart bar, carry forward one of these phases and update it after checking that bar’s signals: idle, post-SC, trading range, post-Spring, post-SOS, post-LPS, or markup. From idle, enter post-SC when low <= lowest(low, lookback)[1] AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * volume spike multiple AND priorBars = min(current chart bar index, prior trend sample cap) is >= minimum prior history bars AND close[priorBars] >= low * (1 + prior downtrend minimum percent / 100). In post-SC, if low < SC low * SC replacement factor AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * volume spike multiple * SC replacement volume factor, replace SC low and restart the AR candidate; otherwise, from the AR candidate start delay onward, track the highest high. Enter trading range when (AR candidate high - SC low) / SC low * 100 >= AR minimum bounce percent AND (AR candidate high - close) / AR candidate high * 100 >= AR minimum retracement percent AND bars since SC >= minimum AR age. After these checks, return to idle if bars since SC > post-SC maximum duration. In trading range, first return to idle if bars since SC > lookback; otherwise enter post-Spring on Spring test, or post-SOS on SOS breakout, or return to idle if close < SC low * range breakdown factor. In post-Spring, return to idle if bars since SC > lookback; otherwise enter post-SOS on SOS breakout. In post-SOS, evaluate LPS test and enter post-LPS if true; then enter markup if close > AR high * markup factor. In post-LPS, enter markup if close > AR high * markup factor. In markup, update the peak high before each check and return to idle if close < peak high * (1 - phase reset drawdown percent / 100). A return to idle clears the stored SC, AR, Spring, SOS, LPS, and peak values.
