Long: Long entries are enabled AND the chart bar is in the trading session AND position size is zero AND no entry has triggered on the current date AND the Asian-session high and low are available AND chart-bar high >= highest chart-bar high during the Asian session
Short: Short entries are enabled AND the chart bar is in the trading session AND position size is zero AND no entry has triggered on the current date AND the Asian-session high and low are available AND chart-bar low <= lowest chart-bar low during the Asian session
Exit: Long, chart-bar low <= entry price - ATR(lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the last completed T1 bar, multiplied by a parameter OR the chart bar is outside the trading session and the previous chart bar was inside it
Exit: Short, chart-bar high >= entry price + ATR(lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the last completed T1 bar, multiplied by a parameter OR the chart bar is outside the trading session and the previous chart bar was inside it
