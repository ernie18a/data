Calculation: M = ALMA(close, MA lookback, offset, sigma) OR EMA(close, MA lookback) OR VWMA(close, MA lookback) OR HMA(close, MA lookback) OR EMA(close, MA lookback) × (1 + TSI(close, short smoothing, long smoothing))
Calculation: S = M − M[lookback]
Calculation: Volatility multiplier = ATR(smart ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(smart ATR lookback), historical ATR lookback), or 1 if either ATR value is not positive
Calculation: Stop percentage = ATR(auto ATR lookback) × stop ATR multiplier / close when automatic ATR calculation is selected; otherwise fixed stop percentage
Calculation: Trail start percentage = ATR(auto ATR lookback) × start multiplier / close when automatic ATR calculation is selected; otherwise trail start percentage × volatility multiplier when adaptive ATR trailing is selected; otherwise fixed trail start percentage
Calculation: Trail follow percentage = ATR(auto ATR lookback) × follow multiplier / close when automatic ATR calculation is selected; otherwise trail follow percentage × volatility multiplier when adaptive ATR trailing is selected; otherwise fixed trail follow percentage
Calculation: Long stop = entry price × (1 − stop percentage) on entry; thereafter the greater of the previous long stop and highest high since entry × (1 − current stop percentage)
Calculation: Short stop = entry price × (1 + stop percentage) on entry; thereafter the lesser of the previous short stop and lowest low since entry × (1 + current stop percentage)
Calculation: Trailing offset = max(one tick, round(entry price × trail follow percentage / tick size) × tick size)
Long: S > 0
Short: S ≤ 0
Long: position side is flat or long AND cooldown since the most recent condition-based exit has passed AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, same smoothing lookback) ≥ threshold AND (ADX rising filter disabled OR ADX > ADX[1])) AND ((slope-entry mode selected AND S crosses above 0) OR (price-cross entry mode selected AND close crosses above M) OR (mid-trend re-entry enabled AND (slope-entry mode or price-cross entry mode selected) AND S > 0 AND S > S on the bar before the most recent condition-based exit AND S > S[1] AND (slope-acceleration filter disabled OR S has risen for at least the acceleration lookback consecutive bars)))
Short: position side is flat or short AND cooldown since the most recent condition-based exit has passed AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, same smoothing lookback) ≥ threshold AND (ADX rising filter disabled OR ADX > ADX[1])) AND ((slope-entry mode selected AND S crosses below 0) OR (price-cross entry mode selected AND close crosses below M) OR (mid-trend re-entry enabled AND (slope-entry mode or price-cross entry mode selected) AND S < 0 AND S < S on the bar before the most recent condition-based exit AND S < S[1] AND (slope-acceleration filter disabled OR S has fallen for at least the acceleration lookback consecutive bars)))
Exit: Long, reverse MA exit enabled AND close crosses below M OR VWMA exit enabled AND close crosses below VWMA(close, VWMA exit lookback) OR slope-deceleration exit enabled AND S has fallen while remaining above 0 for at least the deceleration lookback consecutive bars OR zero-cross exit enabled AND S crosses below 0 OR stop loss enabled AND low ≤ long stop OR take profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR trailing stop enabled AND high has reached entry price × (1 + trail start percentage) AND low ≤ highest high since trailing activation − trailing offset
Exit: Short, reverse MA exit enabled AND close crosses above M OR VWMA exit enabled AND close crosses above VWMA(close, VWMA exit lookback) OR slope-deceleration exit enabled AND S has risen while remaining below 0 for at least the deceleration lookback consecutive bars OR zero-cross exit enabled AND S crosses above 0 OR stop loss enabled AND high ≥ short stop OR take profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR trailing stop enabled AND low has reached entry price × (1 − trail start percentage) AND high ≥ lowest low since trailing activation + trailing offset
