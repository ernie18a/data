Calculation: Raw direction = on each chart bar, 1 if chart close > selected upper level, -1 if chart close < selected lower level, otherwise the previous raw direction (initially 0). Use high, low, and close from the T1 bar still forming. The upper-level parameter selects one of: T1 close + (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ first-level divisor parameter); T1 close + (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ second-level divisor parameter); T1 close + (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ third-level divisor parameter); T1 close + (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ fourth-level divisor parameter); (T1 high ÷ T1 low) × T1 close. The lower-level parameter selects one of: T1 close - (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ first-level divisor parameter); T1 close - (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ second-level divisor parameter); T1 close - (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ third-level divisor parameter); T1 close - (T1 high - T1 low) × (shared factor parameter ÷ fourth-level divisor parameter); T1 close - ((T1 high ÷ T1 low) × T1 close - T1 close).
Long: (reverse parameter is off AND raw direction = 1) OR (reverse parameter is on AND raw direction = -1)
Short: (reverse parameter is off AND raw direction = -1) OR (reverse parameter is on AND raw direction = 1)
