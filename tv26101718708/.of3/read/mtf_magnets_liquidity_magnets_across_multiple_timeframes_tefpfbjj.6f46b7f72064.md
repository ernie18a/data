Calculation: MA score = mean of the directional scores for SMA(close, each of six shared ascending MA lookbacks), EMA(close, each of those lookbacks), VWMA(close, its lookback), HMA(close, its lookback), and the Ichimoku action; each MA score is a positive score unit when the average is below close, a negative score unit when above close, and zero when equal or undefined; Ichimoku conversion and base are midpoints of Highest(high, conversion lookback)/Lowest(low, conversion lookback) and Highest(high, base lookback)/Lowest(low, base lookback), respectively, Span A is their mean, Span B is the midpoint of Highest(high, longer span lookback)/Lowest(low, longer span lookback), and the cloud spans are shifted by the cloud displacement lookback; its action is positive when close exceeds both shifted spans and conversion exceeds base, negative when close is below both and conversion is below base, otherwise zero; conversion, base, and span lookbacks are increasingly longer.
Calculation: Oscillator score = mean of these directional scores: RSI(close, RSI lookback) is positive below its lower band while rising from the prior bar, negative above its upper band while falling, otherwise zero; smoothed Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback) has k = SMA(raw stochastic, smoothing lookback) and d = SMA(k, smoothing lookback), positive when k and d are below the lower band and k>d, negative when both exceed the upper band and k<d, otherwise zero; CCI(close, CCI lookback) is positive below the negative CCI band while rising, negative above the positive CCI band while falling, otherwise zero; DMI(DMI lookback, ADX smoothing) is positive when ADX exceeds its minimum, +DI>−DI, and ADX rises, negative when ADX exceeds its minimum, −DI>+DI, and ADX rises, otherwise zero; Awesome Oscillator(hl2, shorter lookback, longer lookback) is positive above zero while rising, negative below zero while falling, otherwise zero; Momentum(close, momentum lookback) is positive when rising from the prior bar and negative when falling; MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) is positive when its line exceeds its signal line and negative when below; StochRSI(RSI(close, RSI lookback), stochastic lookback), smoothed by the same k and d smoothing lookbacks, uses the same bands and crossover comparisons as the smoothed Stochastic; Williams %R(high, low, close, lookback) is positive below its lower band while rising and negative above its upper band while falling; Bull Bear Power(high, low, EMA(close, its lookback)) is positive above zero while rising and negative below zero while falling; Ultimate Oscillator(high, low, close, shorter/medium/longer lookbacks and corresponding weights) is positive above its upper band and negative below its lower band; every unlisted case scores zero.
Calculation: Technical rating = mean of MA score and Oscillator score. Compute on chart bars for the chart rating and on T1–T4 bars for their ratings; use the last completed T1–T4 bar for signal votes, mapped onto chart bars, and the still-forming T1–T4 bar for table ratings, mapped onto chart bars.
Calculation: Active magnet levels on each timeframe's bars = remove each upper level when high reaches or exceeds it and each lower level when low reaches or falls below it; append confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) and PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) levels, retaining at most the stop-level cap per side by dropping the oldest; when volume exceeds SMA(volume, volume lookback) times the volume multiple, append below-close levels close × (1 − reciprocal of each of three leverage parameters + maintenance margin) and above-close levels close × (1 + reciprocal of each leverage parameter − maintenance margin), retaining at most the liquidation-level cap per side by dropping the oldest; pivot and previous-day levels use the stop-level weight, liquidation levels use the liquidation-level weight; track current Tday high/low and previous completed Tday high/low, and include the previous high only while current Tday high remains below it and it is above close, and the previous low only while current Tday low remains above it and it is below close, when previous-day levels are enabled.
Calculation: Magnet(side, timeframe bar) = from active levels on the requested side of close, choose the candidate level within the maximum-distance parameter whose cluster has greatest pull; its cluster contains side levels within candidate price × cluster-width parameter, strength is the sum of their weights, center is their weight-averaged price, and pull is strength divided by the greater of center-to-close distance as a percentage and the pull-floor parameter; return the center, strength, and pull. Upper and lower magnets use levels above and below close, respectively. Compute on the timeframe's bars; for signal votes use the last completed T1–T4 bar mapped to chart bars, and for table arrows use the still-forming T1–T4 bar mapped to chart bars.
Calculation: Technical-only agreement = signed sum of technical votes from enabled chart/T1–T4 timeframes divided by the maximum possible vote sum, expressed as a percentage; each timeframe contributes one vote opportunity, with a positive vote when its Technical rating is at least the technical signal threshold, a negative vote when it is at most the negative threshold, and zero otherwise; the chart uses its current confirmed bar and T1–T4 use their last completed bars. In all-timeframe technical mode, use this vote for every enabled timeframe regardless of component-selection flags.
Calculation: Combined agreement = signed sum of selected Pull, Strength, and Technical votes from enabled chart/T1–T4 timeframes divided by the maximum possible vote sum, expressed as a percentage; each selected component contributes one vote opportunity, and an enabled timeframe contributes only selected components. Pull votes positive when upper pull exists and exceeds lower pull, negative when lower pull exists and exceeds upper pull, otherwise zero; Strength votes by the same comparison of upper and lower strengths; Technical votes positive at or above the technical signal threshold, negative at or below its negative, otherwise zero. Chart values use the current confirmed chart bar and T1–T4 values use their last completed bars, mapped onto chart bars; if there are no vote opportunities, agreement is undefined.
Long: At a confirmed chart-bar close, position side is not long AND ((signal source is chart technicals AND chart Technical rating is at least the technical signal threshold) OR (signal source is all-timeframe technicals AND Technical-only agreement is at least the agreement threshold) OR (signal source is combined all-timeframe Pull, Strength, and Technicals AND Combined agreement is at least the agreement threshold)).
Short: At a confirmed chart-bar close, position side is not short AND ((signal source is chart technicals AND chart Technical rating is at most the negative technical signal threshold) OR (signal source is all-timeframe technicals AND Technical-only agreement is at most the negative agreement threshold) OR (signal source is combined all-timeframe Pull, Strength, and Technicals AND Combined agreement is at most the negative agreement threshold)).
Long: On any enabled table row for chart/T1–T4, Technical rating on its current bar is above the rating-direction threshold; chart uses its current chart bar and T1–T4 use their still-forming bars mapped onto chart bars.
Short: On any enabled table row for chart/T1–T4, Technical rating on its current bar is below the negative rating-direction threshold; chart uses its current chart bar and T1–T4 use their still-forming bars mapped onto chart bars.
Long: On any enabled table row for chart/T1–T4, an upper magnet exists and either no lower magnet exists or upper pull is at least lower pull; use current chart-bar magnets or still-forming T1–T4 magnets mapped onto chart bars.
Short: On any enabled table row for chart/T1–T4, a lower magnet exists and either no upper magnet exists or lower pull exceeds upper pull; use current chart-bar magnets or still-forming T1–T4 magnets mapped onto chart bars.
Long: On any enabled table row for chart/T1–T4, an upper magnet exists and either no lower magnet exists or upper strength is at least lower strength; use current chart-bar magnets or still-forming T1–T4 magnets mapped onto chart bars.
Short: On any enabled table row for chart/T1–T4, a lower magnet exists and either no upper magnet exists or lower strength exceeds upper strength; use current chart-bar magnets or still-forming T1–T4 magnets mapped onto chart bars.
