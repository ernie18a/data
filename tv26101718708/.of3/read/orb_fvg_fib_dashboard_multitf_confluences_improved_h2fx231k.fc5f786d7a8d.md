Calculation: ORB high = highest high from T1 bars sampled on chart bars during the ORB session parameter in America/New_York, including the still-forming T1 bar
Calculation: ORB low = lowest low from T1 bars sampled on chart bars during the ORB session parameter in America/New_York, including the still-forming T1 bar
Calculation: Running breakout high = highest high on chart bars closing above ORB high since the range state was reset
Calculation: Running breakout low = lowest low on chart bars closing below ORB low since the range state was reset
Long: A bullish FVG (low > high[FVG-offset parameter])
Short: A bearish FVG (high < low[FVG-offset parameter])
Long: close > (ORB high + ORB low) / 2
Short: close <= (ORB high + ORB low) / 2
Long: datetime is within the start-date and end-date parameters AND close > ORB high AND (flat position AND the ORB session has ended OR short position) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) * volume-multiplier parameter OR volume > prior chart-bar volume) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI-lookback parameter) > RSI-threshold parameter) AND (FVG filter disabled OR low > high[FVG-offset parameter]) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram(close, fast-lookback parameter, slow-lookback parameter, signal-lookback parameter) > 0) AND (T2 EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, T2-EMA-length parameter, T2, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars) AND (EMA-cross filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter EMA-lookback parameter) > EMA(close, longer EMA-lookback parameter)) AND (internal-FVG filter disabled OR a bullish FVG with high[FVG-offset parameter] between ORB low and ORB high has occurred since the range state was reset) AND (external-FVG filter disabled OR a bullish FVG with low > ORB high has occurred since the range state was reset) AND each enabled Fibonacci retracement ratio parameter has been reached since the range state was reset, on a bar closing above ORB high with low <= Running breakout high - (Running breakout high - ORB high) * ratio or on a bar closing below ORB low with high >= Running breakout low + (ORB low - Running breakout low) * ratio
Short: datetime is within the start-date and end-date parameters AND close < ORB low AND (flat position AND the ORB session has ended OR long position) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) * volume-multiplier parameter OR volume > prior chart-bar volume) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI-lookback parameter) < 100 - RSI-threshold parameter) AND (FVG filter disabled OR high < low[FVG-offset parameter]) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram(close, fast-lookback parameter, slow-lookback parameter, signal-lookback parameter) < 0) AND (T2 EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, T2-EMA-length parameter, T2, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars) AND (EMA-cross filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter EMA-lookback parameter) < EMA(close, longer EMA-lookback parameter)) AND (internal-FVG filter disabled OR a bearish FVG with low[FVG-offset parameter] between ORB low and ORB high has occurred since the range state was reset) AND (external-FVG filter disabled OR a bearish FVG with high < ORB low has occurred since the range state was reset) AND each enabled Fibonacci retracement ratio parameter has been reached since the range state was reset, on a bar closing above ORB high with low <= Running breakout high - (Running breakout high - ORB high) * ratio or on a bar closing below ORB low with high >= Running breakout low + (ORB low - Running breakout low) * ratio
Exit: Long, EMA(close, shorter EMA-lookback parameter) crosses below EMA(close, longer EMA-lookback parameter) on a chart bar later than the latest long entry or flip
Exit: Short, EMA(close, shorter EMA-lookback parameter) crosses above EMA(close, longer EMA-lookback parameter) on a chart bar later than the latest short entry or flip
