Calculation: opening range high = maximum high of chart bars in the configured ET range-building window, locked when the window ends
Calculation: opening range low = minimum low of chart bars in the configured ET range-building window, locked when the window ends
Calculation: opening range size = opening range high - opening range low
Calculation: opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2
Calculation: long stop = min(opening range low - stop buffer, signal-bar close - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier), fixed at entry
Calculation: short stop = max(opening range high + stop buffer, signal-bar close + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier), fixed at entry
Calculation: long target = opening range midpoint if the long fade condition is true; otherwise signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - long stop) * reward-risk parameter, fixed at entry
Calculation: short target = opening range midpoint if the short fade condition is true; otherwise signal-bar close - (short stop - signal-bar close) * reward-risk parameter, fixed at entry

Short: during the configured post-range session AND session trade count below its limit AND position flat AND opening range size >= minimum AND opening range size <= maximum AND close crosses below opening range low - breakout buffer AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short breakout RSI threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * breakout volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart-bar close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1 bars), calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off: current forming T1 bar in realtime, last completed T1 bar historically)
Long: during the configured post-range session AND session trade count below its limit AND position flat AND opening range size >= minimum AND opening range size <= maximum AND low <= opening range low + fade entry buffer AND close > opening range low AND close[1] <= opening range low AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * fade volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart-bar close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1 bars), calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off: current forming T1 bar in realtime, last completed T1 bar historically)
Short: during the configured post-range session AND session trade count below its limit AND position flat AND opening range size >= minimum AND opening range size <= maximum AND high >= opening range high - fade entry buffer AND close < opening range high AND close[1] >= opening range high AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > overbought threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * fade volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart-bar close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1 bars), calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off: current forming T1 bar in realtime, last completed T1 bar historically)
Long: during the configured post-range session AND (position not flat OR session trade count has reached its limit) AND opening range size >= minimum AND opening range size <= maximum AND low <= opening range low + fade entry buffer AND close > opening range low AND close[1] <= opening range low AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * fade volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart-bar close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1 bars), calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off: current forming T1 bar in realtime, last completed T1 bar historically)
Short: during the configured post-range session AND (position not flat OR session trade count has reached its limit) AND opening range size >= minimum AND opening range size <= maximum AND high >= opening range high - fade entry buffer AND close < opening range high AND close[1] >= opening range high AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > overbought threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * fade volume multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR chart-bar close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1 bars), calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off: current forming T1 bar in realtime, last completed T1 bar historically)
