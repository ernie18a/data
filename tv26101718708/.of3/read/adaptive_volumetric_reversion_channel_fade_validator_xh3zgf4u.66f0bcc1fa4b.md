Calculation: Tactical mean = volume-weighted polynomial regression of the price source versus elapsed chart bars from the selected confirmed swing-pivot anchor or rolling anchor, using the selected degree, volume weights (or 1 when volume is missing or nonpositive), and the configured history cap; use the rolling anchor if no pivot is available.
Calculation: Tactical deviation = square root of the volume-weighted residual sum of squares divided by total weight for the same anchored regression.
Calculation: Macro mean = simple moving average for degree 0, linear regression for degree 1, or volume-weighted quadratic regression for degree 2 over the macro lookback; macro slope = current minus prior linear-regression value for degree 1 and 0 otherwise; macro deviation = standard deviation of the price source over the macro lookback.
Calculation: Density ratio at price p = volume in the live histogram bin for (p − macro mean) / macro deviation divided by the maximum bin volume; bin historical chart-bar volumes by (price source − current macro fit evaluated at that bar position) / macro deviation over the live lookback, capped by the macro lookback, using the configured bin count and sigma range.
Calculation: Favored density node = thin when adaptive polarity is off or until sufficient outcome samples exist; otherwise thin or thick, whichever has the greater forward target-hit rate.
Calculation: Recent high and low sweeps = a high sweep when current high exceeds the equal-high level and close falls below it, or exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high and closes below it with close < open; a low sweep mirrors this using equal lows or the latest confirmed pivot low and close > open. Equal levels are the latest two confirmed pivots within the configured ATR-scaled tolerance; a sweep is recent for the configured sweep-recency lookback.
Calculation: Upper and lower ride-risk scores = rounded score scale times the sum of six separately parameterized weighted components: capped macro-slope magnitude in the fade-opposing direction; capped centerline-spread magnitude in the fade-opposing direction when the absolute spread is not narrowing; 1 when closes have walked beyond the corresponding outer band for the configured streak; capped ROC magnitude in the fade-opposing direction divided by its configured volatility scale; 1 when the correlation-based OU half-life of close minus tactical mean is unavailable or exceeds the follow-through horizon; and the fraction of configured higher-timeframe Supertrend directions opposing the fade. Compute each Supertrend on T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, and T6 using the configured factor and ATR length, on chart bars carrying each timeframe's last completed bar. Set both scores to 0 when ride-risk calculation is disabled.
Long: bullish divergence enabled AND a newly confirmed pivot low in the configured low source is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low while the selected RSI or Stoch RSI value at the pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot, with the pivot low at or below the selected lower band (or no band requirement).
Short: bearish divergence enabled AND a newly confirmed pivot high in the configured high source is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high while the selected RSI or Stoch RSI value at the pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot, with the pivot high at or above the selected upper band (or no band requirement).
Long: low ≤ tactical mean − tactical deviation × outer-band multiple AND at least one of (close > lower outer band; bullish divergence confirmed within the divergence-recency lookback; recent low sweep; density role is extra tell AND lower-band density is favored) AND the configured minimum bars have elapsed since either side's previous raw fade AND ((selected regime gate passes AND (density role is not hard gate OR lower-band density is favored) AND (band-walk gate is off OR lower band is not being walked) AND (ride-risk gate is off OR ride-risk calculation is disabled OR lower ride-risk < risk threshold)) OR NOT (selected regime gate passes AND (density role is not hard gate OR lower-band density is favored)). The regime gate passes according to its selected mode: off; variance-ratio only when VarianceRatio(log(close / close[1]), k, sample lookback) ≤ 1 − neutral band; reversion only when max(0, −Correlation(close[1] − SMA(close, reversion-mean lookback)[1], close − close[1], correlation lookback)) ≥ reversion threshold; both; or either. The lower band is being walked when consecutive closes are below it for the configured walk streak. Lower-band density is favored when its ratio is ≤ the thin threshold if thin is favored, or ≥ the thick threshold if thick is favored.
Short: high ≥ tactical mean + tactical deviation × outer-band multiple AND at least one of (close < upper outer band; bearish divergence confirmed within the divergence-recency lookback; recent high sweep; density role is extra tell AND upper-band density is favored) AND the configured minimum bars have elapsed since either side's previous raw fade AND ((selected regime gate passes AND (density role is not hard gate OR upper-band density is favored) AND (band-walk gate is off OR upper band is not being walked) AND (ride-risk gate is off OR ride-risk calculation is disabled OR upper ride-risk < risk threshold)) OR NOT (selected regime gate passes AND (density role is not hard gate OR upper-band density is favored)). The upper band is being walked when consecutive closes are above it for the configured walk streak. Upper-band density is favored when its ratio is ≤ the thin threshold if thin is favored, or ≥ the thick threshold if thick is favored.
