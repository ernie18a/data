Calculation: Bullish structure = latest confirmed swing low > previous confirmed swing low AND latest confirmed swing high > previous confirmed swing high; bearish structure = both comparisons are lower.
Calculation: Bullish CHoCH = bearish structure AND close > latest confirmed swing high; bearish CHoCH = bullish structure AND close < latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bullish SMT = instrument makes a lower swing low while correlated-instrument swing lows do not make a lower low; bearish SMT = instrument makes a higher swing high while correlated-instrument swing highs do not make a higher high. Use the same pivot lookback for both instruments.
Calculation: Bullish footprint-delta divergence = instrument makes a lower pivot low AND cumulative footprint delta is higher than its value at the previous delta lookback; bearish footprint-delta divergence = instrument makes a higher pivot high AND cumulative footprint delta is lower than its value at the previous delta lookback. Apply separate long and short footprint-volume filters.
Calculation: Regular bullish RSI divergence = price makes a lower pivot low AND RSI makes a higher pivot low; regular bearish RSI divergence = price makes a higher pivot high AND RSI makes a lower pivot high. Hidden bullish RSI divergence = price makes a higher pivot low AND RSI makes a lower pivot low; hidden bearish RSI divergence = price makes a lower pivot high AND RSI makes a higher pivot high.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, lookback). ATR = ATR(lookback). The main-flow liquidity gate requires relative volume and ATR to exceed their separate minimum parameters; the countertrend liquidity gate uses separate minimum parameters.
Calculation: Directional regime = Efficiency Ratio(close, lookback) exceeds its adaptive threshold; upward or downward regime additionally requires close above or below close[lookback]. Consolidation and choppy regimes use the complementary efficiency-ratio and ATR comparisons.
Calculation: The higher-timeframe calculations run on chart bars using last completed bars from T1, T2, T3, T4, and T5, where T1<T2<T3<T4<T5. Use separate fast and slow EMA lookbacks, with fast shorter than slow. The daily-bias calculation uses last completed T5 bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish gate = fast EMA > slow EMA on T2 and T3 AND, on T4, fast EMA > slow EMA OR the absolute EMA separation divided by close is below its parameter. Bearish gate reverses these comparisons, using fast EMA < slow EMA on T2 and T3 and fast EMA < slow EMA OR small separation on T4.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe confidence = weighted sum of T1, T2, T3, T4, and T5 votes, plus a divergence adjustment when T1 opposes both T2 and T3. Each vote compares close with the slow EMA, fast EMA with slow EMA, and close with the midpoint of the recent high-low range; T4 and T5 use their EMA-separation measures. Use separate weights for each timeframe.
Calculation: Flow confidence is a separate weighted score for each setup and side. It combines directional-score, support/resistance, volatility, candle, liquidity, timeframe, regime, agreement, and setup-specific structure factors; apply the setup-specific confidence threshold.
Calculation: Countertrend confidence is a separate long and short score combining exhaustion z-score, distance from EMA/VWAP, band extension, flow reversal, structure reversal, regime, context, and factor agreement, less velocity and opposing hidden-divergence penalties. Its dynamic confidence floor is an EWMA mean plus EWMA standard deviation, calculated separately for each side and setup.
Calculation: Bullish volume-zone rejection = price reaches or crosses the latest high-volume zone low and closes above it, with a bullish engulfing candle or hammer, sufficient relative volume, a lower wick above its ATR floor, and a close in the upper portion of the bar; in a trending regime, higher-timeframe confidence must be positive. Bearish rejection reverses the conditions at the latest zone high.
Calculation: Bullish volume-zone hold = upward regime, ADX above its adaptive floor, low <= latest volume-zone midpoint, close above that midpoint and EMA, and a bullish engulfing candle or bullish body above its average-body threshold; relative volume must exceed its threshold. Bearish hold reverses the conditions.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = footprint total volume exceeds average volume times its adaptive threshold AND bar range is below average range times its adaptive threshold AND body/range is below its adaptive threshold AND close > open. Bearish absorption uses close < open.
Calculation: Bullish aggressor signal = footprint buy volume / sell volume exceeds its adaptive ratio threshold AND footprint total volume exceeds average volume times its adaptive threshold. Bearish aggressor signal uses sell volume / buy volume.
Calculation: Capitulation down = a bearish bar within the context-dependent recent window has relative volume above the bearish climax threshold and body at least the ATR-scaled body threshold. Capitulation up uses a bullish bar and a separate, lower volume threshold.
Calculation: A large directional candle has the matching candle direction and body size at least ATR times the marker-body parameter.
