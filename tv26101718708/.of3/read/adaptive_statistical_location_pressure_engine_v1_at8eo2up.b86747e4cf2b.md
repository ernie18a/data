Calculation: Completed-day regime = percentile rank of the mean range percentage of the last completed T1 bars among prior completed-day means; classify by the configured low, normal, high, and extreme rank bands. Compute on chart bars at each trading-day boundary.
Calculation: Matched session samples = valid completed sessions for the active configured session, with at least two bars, positive open, and range within the data guard; match weekday, completed-day regime, and previous completed-session state when enabled, then back off by dropping previous state, regime, weekday, session identity, and finally all conditioning until the minimum distribution count is met.
Calculation: Session range percentile = percentile rank, counting values less than or equal, of 100 × (session high − session low) / session open in the matched session range samples.
Calculation: Session location percentile = percentile rank of 100 × abs(session close − session open) / session open in the matched upper-excursion samples when close > open, or matched lower-excursion samples when close < open.
Calculation: Efficiency percentile = percentile rank of abs(session close − session open) / (session high − session low) in matched completed-session efficiencies; use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Directed-close percentile = percentile rank of (1 + direction × (2 × (session close − session low) / (session high − session low) − 1)) / 2 in matched completed-session directional-close values, where direction is positive above the session open and negative below; use 0.5 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Block progress percentile = percentile rank of signed block price progress divided by max(session-range increase since the prior block, denominator-floor × session open × matched median session range percentage / 100, minimum price increment), among prior completed blocks for the same session and elapsed-age bucket. Use the last completed non-overlapping block on chart bars; require unchanged nonzero direction across the block.
Calculation: Weakening pressure = available block progress percentile at or below the lower-quartile threshold, or negative signed block progress, AND current session efficiency below prior-block efficiency AND positive retreat from the session extreme; strengthening pressure = block progress percentile at or above the median threshold AND efficiency and directed-close percentiles at or above the high-rank threshold AND positive signed progress AND a new session extreme. Hold pressure between completed blocks and reset it when direction differs from the prior block.
Calculation: Directional expansion = nonzero session direction AND session range percentile at or above the stretch threshold AND efficiency and directed-close percentiles at or above the high-rank threshold. Track whether directional expansion has occurred in each direction during the active session.
Calculation: Session state = reversion in progress if a prior exhaustion candidate exists and close has crossed the session open against its direction; otherwise exhaustion candidate if that direction previously expanded, location percentile is at or above the extreme threshold, range percentile is at or above the stretch threshold, and pressure is weakening; otherwise strong expansion if directional expansion and pressure is strengthening; otherwise directional expansion if its conditions hold; otherwise two-sided volatility if range percentile is at or above the stretch threshold and efficiency percentile is at or below the lower-quartile threshold; otherwise statistical extreme if location percentile is at or above the extreme threshold; otherwise compressed if range percentile is below the central threshold; otherwise normal. Unavailable session statistics are warming.
Calculation: Newly admitted event = on the active focus session, a valid new expansion, extreme-location entry, or weakening-pressure event after prior expansion in that direction, with weakening, extreme, then expansion priority; optionally admit a still-valid state at a completed-block checkpoint. Admit at most one event per family and direction per session, only with no other pending event, sufficient displacement from the session open relative to the matched median range, and before session end.
Calculation: Event forecast probabilities = historical outcome frequencies keyed by event direction, elapsed-age bucket, family, location, and pressure; back off from session-plus-location-plus-pressure to location-plus-pressure, location, family, then pooled family while preserving direction and age, until the minimum outcome support is reached. Apply configured prior shrinkage when its complement has sufficient support; use an optional regime, weekday, or previous-state feature only when its paired walk-forward Brier-gain lower bound is positive and both sample requirements are met.
Calculation: Event barriers = event price plus event direction × continuation-distance multiple × matched median range in price, and event price minus event direction × the lesser of the toward-open-distance multiple × matched median range in price and distance to the session open. Resolve through the earlier of the configured horizon or session end: an opening price beyond a barrier wins first; otherwise the first single barrier touched wins, both barriers touched in one bar is ambiguous, and no touch is timeout. Gaps censor the event.
Long: active focus session is in directional expansion or strong expansion and closes above its open.
Short: active focus session is in directional expansion or strong expansion and closes below its open.
Long: active focus session newly enters strong expansion and closes above its open.
Short: active focus session newly enters strong expansion and closes below its open.
Long: active focus session crosses into the upper extreme-or-tail location tier from a prior bar that was not already in that tier and closes above its open.
Short: active focus session crosses into the lower extreme-or-tail location tier from a prior bar that was not already in that tier and closes below its open.
Short: active focus session newly becomes an exhaustion candidate above its open.
Long: active focus session newly becomes an exhaustion candidate below its open.
Long: active focus session is in reversion in progress and closes above its open.
Short: active focus session is in reversion in progress and closes below its open.
Long: a newly admitted event has sufficient forecast support AND ((event direction is positive AND continuation probability reaches the probability threshold) OR (event direction is negative AND toward-open probability reaches the probability threshold)).
Short: a newly admitted event has sufficient forecast support AND ((event direction is negative AND continuation probability reaches the probability threshold) OR (event direction is positive AND toward-open probability reaches the probability threshold)).
Long: a pending event resolves continuation first with positive event direction, or resolves toward-open first with negative event direction.
Short: a pending event resolves continuation first with negative event direction, or resolves toward-open first with positive event direction.
