Calculation: Long stop distance = abs(lowest(low, stop lookback)[1] - close - stop buffer multiplier / round-level parameter)
Calculation: Short stop distance = abs(highest(high, stop lookback)[1] - close + stop buffer multiplier / round-level parameter)
Long: SMA(HLC3, SMA lookback, chart bars) crosses its value shifted by the SMA offset parameter in either direction AND open is below both values AND close is above both values AND the high and low accumulated on chart bars during the current, still-forming T1 period are both above SMA(HLC3, the same SMA lookback, T1 bars, still-forming bar) and its value shifted by the same offset parameter on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars AND the start-time limiter is disabled or datetime is after the configured start datetime
Short: SMA(HLC3, SMA lookback, chart bars) crosses its value shifted by the SMA offset parameter in either direction AND open is above both values AND close is below both values AND the high and low accumulated on chart bars during the current, still-forming T1 period are both below SMA(HLC3, the same SMA lookback, T1 bars, still-forming bar) and its value shifted by the same offset parameter on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars AND the start-time limiter is disabled or datetime is after the configured start datetime
Exit: long, low reaches entry price minus Long stop distance OR high reaches entry price plus target multiple times Long stop distance
Exit: short, high reaches entry price plus Short stop distance OR low reaches entry price minus target multiple times Short stop distance
