Calculation: Volume ratio = SMA(volume, shorter volume lookback, T1, last completed bar) / SMA(volume, longer volume lookback, T1, last completed bar), when the denominator is positive
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) / close(T1, last completed bar) * 100, when close is positive
Calculation: Percent below highest high = (highest(high, high lookback, T1, last completed bar) - close(T1, last completed bar)) / highest(high, high lookback, T1, last completed bar) * 100, when the highest high is positive
Calculation: Setup score = the number of true conditions among: close > SMA(close, shorter trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND that SMA > SMA(close, medium trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND that SMA > SMA(close, longer trend lookback, T1, last completed bar); RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) is between the RSI minimum and maximum; volume ratio >= volume surge threshold; ATR percentage is between the ATR percentage minimum and maximum; percent below highest high is between the minimum and maximum below-high distances
Long: On chart bars, using values from the last completed T1 and T2 bars, setup score >= minimum score AND close(T1) > 0 AND ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) > 0 AND (market-trend filter disabled OR benchmark close(T2) > SMA(benchmark close, market SMA lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility-index close(T2) < volatility threshold)
