Calculation: Fast moving average = EMA(close, shorter moving-average lookback) or SMA(close, shorter moving-average lookback), selected by moving-average-type parameter
Calculation: Slow moving average = SMA(close, longer moving-average lookback)
Calculation: Volume expansion percent = (volume - SMA(volume, volume lookback)) / SMA(volume, volume lookback) × 100 when the SMA is nonzero, otherwise zero
Calculation: Close quality = (close - low) / (high - low) when high differs from low, otherwise zero
Calculation: Benchmark-relative strength = (close / close[relative-strength lookback] - 1) - (benchmark close / benchmark close[relative-strength lookback] - 1)
Calculation: Institutional score = separate tunable point values for fast average above slow average, slow average rising over its slope lookback, close above fast average, volume expansion meeting its threshold, close quality meeting its threshold, ATR above its average, ATR below its average, OBV above its average, ADL above its average, benchmark-relative strength above its very-strong threshold or above zero, benchmark close above its shorter-lookback SMA while that SMA is above its longer-lookback SMA, and close quality meeting its threshold again
Calculation: Fast-average percent slope = (fast moving average - fast moving average[slope lookback]) / fast moving average[slope lookback] × 100
Long: OBV > SMA(OBV, OBV lookback) AND ADL > SMA(ADL, ADL lookback) AND benchmark-relative strength > 0 AND ATR(ATR lookback) < SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) AND institutional score >= watch-score threshold parameter AND (confirmed-bar-only parameter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Long: Flat at the initial stage AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND SMA(close, longer moving-average lookback) > SMA(close, longer moving-average lookback)[slow-average slope lookback] AND close > fast moving average for at least the confirmation-bars parameter consecutive chart bars AND (breakout requirement parameter is disabled OR close > highest(close[1], breakout lookback) OR close > highest(close[1], near-high lookback) × near-high fraction parameter) AND volume expansion percent >= volume-expansion threshold parameter AND benchmark-relative strength > 0 AND benchmark close > SMA(benchmark close, shorter benchmark lookback) > SMA(benchmark close, longer benchmark lookback) AND institutional score >= entry-score threshold parameter AND at least the cooldown-bars parameter chart bars have elapsed since the prior exit AND (confirmed-bar-only parameter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Long: After the initial long entry and before the confirmation add, ((close - average long entry price) / average long entry price) × 100 >= confirmation-profit parameter AND close > fast moving average AND benchmark-relative strength > 0 AND (confirmed-bar-only parameter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Long: After the confirmation add and before the expansion add, ((close - average long entry price) / average long entry price) × 100 >= expansion-profit parameter AND close > fast moving average AND (confirmed-bar-only parameter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Short: Volume expansion percent >= volume-expansion threshold parameter AND close <= close[1] AND benchmark close > SMA(benchmark close, shorter benchmark lookback) > SMA(benchmark close, longer benchmark lookback)
Exit: long, close < fast moving average × (1 - stop-percent parameter / 100) AND (confirmed-bar-only parameter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
Exit: long, close < fast moving average for at least the confirmation-bars parameter consecutive chart bars AND volume expansion percent met its threshold on at least one bar in the current consecutive below-average streak AND (confirmed-bar-only parameter is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed)
