Calculation: Chart VWAP = if sum(volume, chart VWAP lookback) > 0 then sum(((high + low + close) / 3) * volume, chart VWAP lookback) / sum(volume, chart VWAP lookback) else (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Chart VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(sum(volume * (((high + low + close) / 3) - Chart VWAP)^2, chart VWAP lookback) / max(sum(volume, chart VWAP lookback), volume denominator floor)
Calculation: Inner lower band = Chart VWAP - Chart VWAP standard deviation * inner band multiplier
Calculation: Inner upper band = Chart VWAP + Chart VWAP standard deviation * inner band multiplier
Calculation: Outer lower band = Chart VWAP - Chart VWAP standard deviation * outer band multiplier
Calculation: Outer upper band = Chart VWAP + Chart VWAP standard deviation * outer band multiplier
Calculation: Smoothed VWAP pressure = EMA(if Chart VWAP standard deviation > 0 then (((high + low + close) / 3) - Chart VWAP) / Chart VWAP standard deviation else 0, pressure EMA lookback)
Calculation: Chart VWAP velocity = EMA(ROC(Chart VWAP, chart velocity ROC lookback) / max(Chart VWAP standard deviation, minimum tick size), chart velocity EMA lookback)
Calculation: Turbulence index = if SMA(StDev(Chart VWAP velocity, turbulence lookback), turbulence average lookback) > 0 then StDev(Chart VWAP velocity, turbulence lookback) / SMA(StDev(Chart VWAP velocity, turbulence lookback), turbulence average lookback) else turbulence fallback
Calculation: VWAP velocity on T = EMA(ROC(VWAP on T, higher-timeframe velocity ROC lookback) / max(VWAP standard deviation on T, minimum tick size), higher-timeframe velocity EMA lookback), computed on T bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off using the current T bar while it forms
Calculation: VWAP on T = if sum(volume, higher-timeframe VWAP lookback) > 0 then sum(((high + low + close) / 3) * volume, higher-timeframe VWAP lookback) / sum(volume, higher-timeframe VWAP lookback) else (high + low + close) / 3, computed on T bars
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation on T = sqrt(sum(volume * (((high + low + close) / 3) - VWAP on T)^2, higher-timeframe VWAP lookback) / max(sum(volume, higher-timeframe VWAP lookback), volume denominator floor)), computed on T bars
Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = (min(open, close) - low > wick-to-body ratio * abs(close - open) AND min(open, close) - low > wick-to-range ratio * (high - low)) OR (close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close > open[1] AND open <= close[1]) OR either bullish pattern condition on the prior bar
Calculation: Bearish reversal pattern = (high - max(open, close) > wick-to-body ratio * abs(close - open) AND high - max(open, close) > wick-to-range ratio * (high - low)) OR (close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close < open[1] AND open >= close[1]) OR either bearish pattern condition on the prior bar
Long: signals enabled AND crosses above(hlc3, inner lower band) AND smoothed VWAP pressure < pressure threshold below zero AND turbulence index < laminar turbulence threshold AND abs(smoothed VWAP pressure) <= breakout threshold AND at least two of (chart VWAP velocity > chart bullish velocity threshold, VWAP velocity on T1 > T1 bullish velocity threshold, VWAP velocity on T2 > T2 bullish velocity threshold) AND (reversal filter disabled OR bullish reversal pattern) AND no prior long or short signal within the signal cooldown lookback
Short: signals enabled AND crosses below(hlc3, inner upper band) AND smoothed VWAP pressure > pressure threshold above zero AND turbulence index < laminar turbulence threshold AND abs(smoothed VWAP pressure) <= breakout threshold AND at least two of (chart VWAP velocity <= chart bullish velocity threshold, VWAP velocity on T1 <= T1 bullish velocity threshold, VWAP velocity on T2 <= T2 bullish velocity threshold) AND (reversal filter disabled OR bearish reversal pattern) AND no prior long or short signal within the signal cooldown lookback
Exit: long, low <= outer lower band OR high >= outer upper band OR turbulence index > turbulent threshold OR abs(smoothed VWAP pressure) > breakout threshold
Exit: short, high >= outer upper band OR low <= outer lower band OR turbulence index > turbulent threshold OR abs(smoothed VWAP pressure) > breakout threshold
