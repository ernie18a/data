Calculation: Neighbor score = on each bar, append a record (long feature, short feature, sign(previous close - close)), then scan all stored records in insertion order and append a record’s label when sqrt((current long feature - record long feature)^2 + (current short feature - record short feature)^2) exceeds the maximum distance so far in that scan; retain the latest floor(sqrt(neighbor-count parameter)) appended labels across scans and sum them. Each feature is the selected RSI(close, its lookback), CCI(close, its lookback), ROC(close, its lookback), or 99 * (volume - lowest(volume, its lookback)) / (highest(volume, its lookback) - lowest(volume, its lookback)); when all features are selected, use their arithmetic mean. The long-feature lookback is longer than the short-feature lookback.
Long: Neighbor score > 0 AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR(shorter ATR lookback) > ATR(longer ATR lookback)) AND NOT (Neighbor score[1] > 0 AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR(shorter ATR lookback)[1] > ATR(longer ATR lookback)[1]))
Short: Neighbor score < 0 AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR(shorter ATR lookback) > ATR(longer ATR lookback)) AND NOT (Neighbor score[1] < 0 AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR(shorter ATR lookback)[1] > ATR(longer ATR lookback)[1]))
Exit: Long, Neighbor score = 0 OR (volatility filter enabled AND ATR(shorter ATR lookback) <= ATR(longer ATR lookback))
Exit: Short, Neighbor score = 0 OR (volatility filter enabled AND ATR(shorter ATR lookback) <= ATR(longer ATR lookback))
