Calculation: Displacement = abs(close - open) > ATR(ATR lookback) * the execution-mode displacement multiplier; strong displacement = abs(close - open) > ATR(ATR lookback) * the execution-mode displacement multiplier * the strong-displacement factor
Calculation: Directional close-location average = SMA(if high > low then ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low) else 0, delta lookback)
Calculation: Range position = if highest(high, range lookback) > lowest(low, range lookback) then (close - lowest(low, range lookback)) / (highest(high, range lookback) - lowest(low, range lookback)) else midpoint; discount is below the lower range boundary and premium is above the upper range boundary
Calculation: HTF bias = sum the signs of EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, current still-forming bar) versus EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, current still-forming bar), and likewise for T2 and T3; these EMAs run on their T bars and their values are taken on chart bars; when the chart interval is at least T4, use T5, T6, and T7 instead; classify bullish at or above the positive bias threshold, bearish at or below its negative, otherwise neutral
Calculation: HTF alignment = the HTF bias is neutral or agrees with the direction, or the HTF bias filter is disabled
Calculation: HTF1 candle direction = close > open for bullish or close < open for bearish on the current still-forming T1 bar, taken on chart bars; use T5 instead when the chart interval is at least T4
Calculation: Qualifying fair value gap = bullish when low > high[2] or bearish when high < low[2]; require gap height at least ATR(ATR lookback) times the minimum gap multiple except in all-gap mode, and in strict mode also require displacement
Calculation: Active HTF order block = on the selected HTF bar, including its still-forming bar, body size exceeds HTF ATR times the HTF displacement threshold and the candle direction agrees while the prior HTF candle is opposite; use the prior HTF candle high and low as the zone, cap its height using chart ATR and the HTF zone-height cap, invalidate bullish zones when chart close is below the zone and bearish zones when chart close is above it, and expire them by the HTF age limit; use T8 unless chart interval is at least T4, when T5 is used, or the chart interval is at least T8, when T9 is used; HTF calculations run on their source bars and values are taken on chart bars
Calculation: Order block score at creation = the lesser of the score cap and base points plus the applicable directional weights for a supporting valid sweep, a qualifying same-direction gap, matching market trend, enabled and aligned HTF bias, volume above SMA(volume, volume lookback) times the volume multiple, a directionally favorable range position, strong displacement, enabled matching HTF1 candle direction, close inside an active same-direction HTF order block, and directional close-location average above the positive threshold for bullish or below the negative threshold for bearish; store the score with the order block; on reclaim add the reclaim score boost, capped at the score cap
Calculation: Bullish order block = on a displacement bar with close > open, create a zone from the high and low of the bearish candle at the impulse lookback plus its offset; cap the zone height at ATR(ATR lookback) times the OB height limit, calculate and store its score at creation, and create it only when order block creation is enabled and the OB count is below its limit
Calculation: Bearish order block = on a displacement bar with close < open, create a zone from the high and low of the bullish candle at the impulse lookback plus its offset; cap the zone height at ATR(ATR lookback) times the OB height limit, calculate and store its score at creation, and create it only when order block creation is enabled and the OB count is below its limit
Calculation: Active chart-timeframe order block = a bullish zone remains active until close is below its lower boundary, a bearish zone until close is above its upper boundary; it expires beyond the OB age limit, is discarded when its midpoint is farther from close than ATR(ATR lookback) times the maximum OB distance, and cannot trigger again after entry; an invalidated, untriggered, unreclaimed zone may reactivate once within the reclaim window when close passes beyond its far boundary
Calculation: Entry session passes = chart interval at least T4, or session filter disabled, or a selected session is active in New York local time: London 02:00-10:00, New York 08:00-16:00, or Asian 19:00-02:00
