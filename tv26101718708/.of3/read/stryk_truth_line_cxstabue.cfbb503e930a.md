Calculation: Chart-bar delta = footprint buy volume − sell volume when true footprint delta is selected and available; otherwise sum T2-bar volume signed positive when close > open or when close = open and close > prior close, negative when close < open or when close = open and close < prior close, and zero otherwise; if no T2 bars are available, use chart-bar volume signed by close versus open, with zero for a doji.
Calculation: T1 delta = sum of chart-bar deltas in a T1 candle; the live value includes the forming T1 candle, and the completed value is fixed at its close.
Calculation: Score(x, history) = percentile rank of x in history, or 100 × (x − lowest(history)) / (highest(history) − lowest(history)) in range mode; use 50 when the range is flat or the selected session history has fewer than the minimum scoring sample count.
Calculation: Participation = max(Score(EMA(volume, volume-smoothing lookback), selected history), Score(volume, selected history)) when spike-aware scoring is enabled, otherwise Score(EMA(volume, volume-smoothing lookback), selected history); selected history is the rolling participation lookback or prior bars from the same session; in rolling range mode, raw volume uses percentile rank.
Calculation: T1 average absolute delta = mean of absolute T1 deltas among the last delta-lookback completed T1 candles with the current delta source, when the minimum candle-average sample count is available.
Calculation: N-candle block = (sum of T1 deltas, last T1 close − first T1 open) over the delta-lookback candles; the live block contains the forming T1 candle and the preceding delta-lookback-minus-one completed candles; prior blocks contain completed T1 candles.
Calculation: Average absolute block delta = mean absolute delta of matching-source completed blocks over the block-average lookback; when fewer than the minimum full block history but at least one matching block is available, use T1 average absolute delta × square root of the delta lookback.
Calculation: Verdict(blocks, average absolute block delta) = ABSORPTION UP if the newest block delta ≤ −absorption multiple × average absolute block delta and its price change ≥ −flat tolerance × square root of the delta lookback; ABSORPTION DOWN if its delta ≥ absorption multiple × average absolute block delta and its price change ≤ flat tolerance × square root of the delta lookback; swap these absorption labels if absorption inversion is enabled; otherwise BUYERS if every block delta > meaningful-delta multiple × average absolute block delta and summed block price change > flat tolerance × square root of the delta lookback; otherwise SELLERS if every block delta < −meaningful-delta multiple × average absolute block delta and summed block price change < −flat tolerance × square root of the delta lookback; otherwise CONSOLIDATE if every block’s absolute delta ≤ small-delta multiple × average absolute block delta; otherwise MIXED.
Calculation: Fuel = max(fuel floor, bell(participation, fuel peak, bell width) × delta agreement × optional climax factor); bell = 1 at or above its peak and a Gaussian bell below it; delta agreement is 1 unless enabled for true footprint delta with a positive chart-bar average absolute delta, then it is the agreement multiplier when delta agrees with close − open (or close = open), and the disagreement penalty otherwise; optional climax factor above the climax floor is max(minimum climax factor, 1 − climax decay rate × (participation − climax floor)).
Calculation: Paid = fuel ≥ paid threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive line = previous line + (close − previous line) × (minimum follow speed + (maximum follow speed − minimum follow speed) × fuel^follow exponent) + lag compensation × fuel × (close − prior close), clipped to close if it overshoots; initialize at the first close.
Calculation: Confirmed live verdict = Verdict of the latest memory-block-count blocks, including the live block; confirmed T1-close verdict = Verdict of the latest memory-block-count completed blocks, updated at T1 close.
Calculation: Selected regime = live verdict after it holds for the sticky-bar parameter when live-sticky mode is selected, otherwise the confirmed T1-close verdict; LONG regime means BUYERS or ABSORPTION UP, and SHORT regime means SELLERS or ABSORPTION DOWN.
Calculation: Confirmed up flip = while flip state is down, close crosses from at or below the prior adaptive line to above the adaptive line by the hysteresis parameter × ATR, fuel is paid, and chart-bar delta > 0; confirm immediately if instant-conviction mode is selected and absolute chart-bar delta ≥ conviction multiple × its chart-bar average absolute delta and participation is MID, otherwise confirm after the hold-bar parameter when price remains above the line, or at the next T1 close if the prior chart close remains above the line, according to the selected confirmation mode.
Calculation: Confirmed down flip = while flip state is up, close crosses from at or above the prior adaptive line to below the adaptive line by the hysteresis parameter × ATR, fuel is paid, and chart-bar delta < 0; confirm immediately if instant-conviction mode is selected and absolute chart-bar delta ≥ conviction multiple × its chart-bar average absolute delta and participation is MID, otherwise confirm after the hold-bar parameter when price remains below the line, or at the next T1 close if the prior chart close remains below the line, according to the selected confirmation mode.

Short: live verdict = SELLERS or ABSORPTION DOWN.
Short: confirmed T1-close verdict = SELLERS or ABSORPTION DOWN.
