Calculation: active S/R levels = store each confirmed close pivot in the first empty slot, up to the active-level capacity; a pivot is close[PivotLength] when it equals the highest or lowest close over a window of twice PivotLength plus one bars, and is rejected if any active level has either a same-side wick-through (open and close on one side, high or low piercing the level) at PivotLength or PivotLength plus one bars ago, or a same-side near rejection at PivotLength or PivotLength minus one bars ago where the wick is within the proximity tolerance; proximity tolerance percentage = ATR(ATR lookback) / close × 100 / tolerance divisor; increment a level’s break count when open and close straddle it, then remove it when its break count reaches MaxBreaks or abs(level − close) / close × 100 reaches proximity tolerance percentage × distance multiplier
Calculation: S/R test direction = reset to zero each bar, then scan active levels in storage order and retain the last matching result: −1 if open < level AND high > level − instrument minimum tick size × ticks-per-sensitivity × test sensitivity AND close < level; +1 if open > level AND low < level + instrument minimum tick size × ticks-per-sensitivity × test sensitivity AND close > level
Calculation: S/R break direction = reset to zero each bar, then scan active levels in storage order and retain the last matching result: +1 if open < level AND close > level; −1 if open > level AND close < level
Calculation: TradingView technical rating on T1’s still-forming bar, mapped to chart bars = average of the available moving-average score and oscillator score; moving-average score is the mean of available votes (+1 when close is above the average, −1 when below, zero when equal) for six SMAs with separate increasing lookbacks, six EMAs with separate increasing lookbacks, one HMA, one VWMA, and an Ichimoku vote (+1 when Span A > Span B AND close > Span A AND close < Base Line AND prior close < Conversion Line AND close > Conversion Line; −1 for the reverse conditions); oscillator score is the mean of available +1/−1/zero votes: RSI is +1 below its lower threshold and rising, −1 above its upper threshold and falling; Stochastic is +1 when K and D are below the lower threshold, K > D, and prior K < prior D, −1 when both are above the upper threshold, K < D, and prior K > prior D; CCI is +1 below its lower threshold and rising, −1 above its upper threshold and falling; DMI is +1 when ADX exceeds its minimum and +DI crosses above −DI, −1 when ADX exceeds its minimum and +DI crosses below −DI; Awesome Oscillator is +1 on a cross above zero or when current and prior values are positive, current > prior, and the value two bars ago > prior, −1 for the reverse; Momentum is +1 when current Momentum > prior, −1 when current Momentum < prior; MACD is +1 when its line > signal line, −1 when its line < signal line; Stochastic RSI is +1 when close < trend EMA, K and D are below the lower threshold, K > D, and prior K < prior D, −1 when close > trend EMA, K and D are above the upper threshold, K < D, and prior K > prior D; Williams %R is +1 below its lower threshold and rising, −1 above its upper threshold and falling; Elder Ray is +1 when close > trend EMA, Bear Power < 0 and rising, −1 when close < trend EMA, Bull Power > 0 and falling; Ultimate Oscillator is +1 above its upper threshold and −1 below its lower threshold; use separate lookback parameters for each indicator, fast and slow lookbacks for AO and MACD with fast shorter than slow, and three increasing lookbacks for Ichimoku and Ultimate Oscillator
Calculation: maximum safety-order deviation = sum of price deviation × safety-order step scale^(index − 1) for each index from the first safety order through the maximum safety-order count
Calculation: base-order reference price = close from the selected start-signal calculation (chart-bar close for S/R or immediate start; T1 close for RSI or technical-rating mode)
Calculation: take-profit level = (average position entry price when the selected basis is total position volume, otherwise base-order reference price) × (1 + position-side sign × take-profit percentage), where position-side sign is +1 for long and −1 for short
Calculation: stop-loss level = base-order reference price × (1 − position-side sign × stop-loss percentage)
Calculation: trailing take-profit state = inactive while flat and reset when flat; after take-profit level is reached, trailing is active and the extremum is updated to max(current high, prior extremum) for long or min(current low, prior extremum) for short, with extremum initialized to zero; trailing stop = extremum × (1 − position-side sign × trailing take-profit percentage)

Short: S/R test direction = −1

Short: S/R break direction = −1
