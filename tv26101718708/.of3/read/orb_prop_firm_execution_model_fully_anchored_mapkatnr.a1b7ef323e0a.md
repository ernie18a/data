Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum of high on chart bars opening from the session open in America/New_York until the opening-range duration has elapsed; reset each day
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum of low on chart bars opening from the session open in America/New_York until the opening-range duration has elapsed; reset each day
Long: current bar time >= session open + opening-range duration AND opening-range high is defined AND crosses above(close, opening-range high) AND close > VWAP(HLC3, volume, reset each day) AND close > SMA(close, lookback) AND SMA(close, lookback) > SMA(close, lookback)[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * volume multiplier
