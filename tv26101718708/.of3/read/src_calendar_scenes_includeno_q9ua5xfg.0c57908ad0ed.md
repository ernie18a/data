Calculation: Band basis = selectable moving average(close, band lookback); smoothed upper and lower bands = EMA(band basis ± ATR(ATR lookback) × volatility multiplier × (1 + (50 − abs(Stochastic %K − 50)) / 100), band smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Regression centerline = selected regression method over band basis: logarithmic fits log(max(band basis, minimum price increment)) and exponentiates the result; linear fits EMA(band basis, linear-source smoothing lookback); adaptive fits log(max(HMA(band basis, adaptive-source lookback), minimum price increment)) and HMA-smooths the fitted slope; leading fits band basis over regression lookback minus leading offset and scales the fitted slope by the leading factor. Regression channel boundaries at the current and regression-lookback endpoints are the centerline ± StDev(band basis, regression lookback); normalized regression slope = (centerline now − centerline slope lookback bars ago) / ATR(ATR lookback).
Calculation: Bullish heat ratio = EMA(SUM(close > band basis ? 1 : 0, heat lookback), heat smoothing lookback) / the sum of bullish and bearish heat sums; bearish heat ratio uses close < band basis. Use 1 for a zero or undefined denominator and 0 for an undefined numerator.
Calculation: Volume Sentiment Breakout bullish and bearish contexts = the corresponding active Volume Sentiment Breakout Channels state or a breakout within the freshness lookback; a disabled VSB filter makes both contexts true. An active bullish state ends when close crosses below its SMA(low since bullish breakout) guide; an active bearish state ends when close crosses above its SMA(high since bearish breakout) guide.
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR averaging lookback) × ATR expansion threshold.
Calculation: No-chase-high warning = NOT (active bullish VSB state AND close ≥ bullish VSB guide) AND (close > upper regression boundary OR close > smoothed upper band + ATR(ATR lookback) × extension distance) AND bullish heat ratio ≥ heat-dominance threshold AND (Stochastic %K is above its hot threshold and falling or crosses below %D, OR high > smoothed upper band and close < smoothed upper band) AND NOT (Stochastic %K rising AND close > previous close AND close is within the upper close-location fraction of high). No-bottom-fish warning mirrors this at the lower regression boundary and band with bearish heat, a rising cold %K or lower rejection, and a falling bearish close.
Long: VSB bullish context AND normalized regression slope > flat-trend threshold AND close is within the regression channel AND (low ≤ smoothed lower band + ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance OR close ≤ band basis + ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance OR Stochastic %K < bullish pullback threshold) AND (Stochastic %K crosses above %D OR (%K > %D AND %K rising)) AND bullish heat ratio ≥ calendar minimum heat ratio AND NOT ((close > upper regression boundary OR close > smoothed upper band) AND normalized regression slope > flat-trend threshold AND bullish heat ratio ≥ extreme-trend threshold AND (ATR expansion OR %K > fast-bullish stochastic threshold)).
Short: VSB bearish context AND normalized regression slope < −flat-trend threshold AND close is within the regression channel AND (high ≥ smoothed upper band − ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance OR close ≥ band basis − ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance OR Stochastic %K > bearish rebound threshold) AND (Stochastic %K crosses below %D OR (%K < %D AND %K falling)) AND bearish heat ratio ≥ calendar minimum heat ratio AND NOT ((close < lower regression boundary OR close < smoothed lower band) AND normalized regression slope < −flat-trend threshold AND bearish heat ratio ≥ extreme-trend threshold AND (ATR expansion OR %K < fast-bearish stochastic threshold)).
Long: (close > upper regression boundary OR close > smoothed upper band) AND normalized regression slope > flat-trend threshold AND bullish heat ratio ≥ extreme-trend threshold AND (ATR expansion OR Stochastic %K > fast-bullish stochastic threshold).
Short: (close < lower regression boundary OR close < smoothed lower band) AND normalized regression slope < −flat-trend threshold AND bearish heat ratio ≥ extreme-trend threshold AND (ATR expansion OR Stochastic %K < fast-bearish stochastic threshold).
Long: (close ≥ smoothed upper band − ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance OR high ≥ smoothed upper band OR close > upper regression boundary) AND normalized regression slope > flat-trend threshold AND bullish heat ratio ≥ extreme-trend threshold AND Stochastic %K > bullish fade-trap threshold.
Short: (close ≤ smoothed lower band + ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance OR low ≤ smoothed lower band OR close < lower regression boundary) AND normalized regression slope < −flat-trend threshold AND bearish heat ratio ≥ extreme-trend threshold AND Stochastic %K < bearish fade-trap threshold.
Long: normalized regression slope ≥ vertical minimum slope AND VSB bullish context AND bullish heat ratio ≥ heat-dominance threshold AND close > band basis AND Stochastic %K < maximum bullish-vertical threshold AND NOT no-chase-high warning AND (bullish VSB breakout AND strong bullish close AND %K > %D OR close crosses above smoothed upper band AND %K > %D AND strong bullish close OR low ≤ band basis + ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance AND close > band basis + ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance AND %K > %D AND %K rising AND strong bullish close OR active bullish VSB state AND low ≤ bullish VSB guide + ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance AND close > bullish VSB guide AND strong bullish close). A strong bullish close has close > open and close within the strong-close fraction of the high.
Short: normalized regression slope ≤ −vertical minimum slope AND VSB bearish context AND bearish heat ratio ≥ heat-dominance threshold AND close < band basis AND Stochastic %K > 100 − maximum bullish-vertical threshold AND NOT no-bottom-fish warning AND (bearish VSB breakout AND strong bearish close AND %K < %D OR close crosses below smoothed lower band AND %K < %D AND strong bearish close OR high ≥ band basis − ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance AND close < band basis − ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance AND %K < %D AND %K falling AND strong bearish close OR active bearish VSB state AND high ≥ bearish VSB guide − ATR(ATR lookback) × near-band distance AND close < bearish VSB guide AND strong bearish close). A strong bearish close has close < open and close within the strong-close fraction of the low.
