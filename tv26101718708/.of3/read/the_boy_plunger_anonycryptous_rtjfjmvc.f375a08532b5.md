Calculation: Nearest T1 pivot level = the closest by absolute distance from close among the four latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and four latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) on T1; calculate pivots on T1 bars and sample them on chart bars from T1 with lookahead off, including the still-forming T1 bar.
Calculation: Pullback eligibility = a bullish or bearish breakout setup occurs when close is respectively above or below the nearest T1 pivot level, the candle direction matches, the level is within the watching ATR distance, and volume spikes; on that bar or within the pullback window, the bullish setup qualifies when close <= fast ribbon MA + ATR * pullback buffer and close >= slow ribbon MA - ATR * pullback buffer, or the bearish setup qualifies when close >= fast ribbon MA - ATR * pullback buffer and close <= slow ribbon MA + ATR * pullback buffer.
Calculation: Active FVG zones = a bullish zone forms when low > high[2], low - high[2] > ATR with the distinct longer FVG lookback * minimum gap multiplier, and close[1] > high[2]; its initial bounds are high[2] to low and it narrows as price fills it until fully filled. A bearish zone forms when high < low[2], low[2] - high > ATR with the distinct longer FVG lookback * minimum gap multiplier, and close[1] < low[2]; its initial bounds are high to low[2] and it narrows as price fills it until fully filled.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a long, entry price minus ATR at entry * stop multiplier in ATR mode, lowest(low, swing lookback) in swing mode, or the lower of those stops in hybrid mode; for a short, entry price plus ATR at entry * stop multiplier in ATR mode, highest(high, swing lookback) in swing mode, or the higher of those stops in hybrid mode.
Calculation: Target k = entry price + position side * abs(entry price - initial stop) * the separate R-multiple parameter for target k.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop when trailing is disabled or before target 1; entry price after target 1 and before target 2; after target 2, for a long the greater of the prior trailing stop and fast ribbon MA - ATR * trail buffer, and for a short the lesser of the prior trailing stop and fast ribbon MA + ATR * trail buffer.
