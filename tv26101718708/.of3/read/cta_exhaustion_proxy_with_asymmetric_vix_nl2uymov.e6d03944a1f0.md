Calculation: direction sum = (selected source > MA(selected type, selected source, shorter lookback) ? 1 : -1) + (selected source > MA(selected type, selected source, intermediate lookback) ? 1 : -1) + (selected source > MA(selected type, selected source, longer lookback) ? 1 : -1)
Calculation: extreme-run count = consecutive chart bars ending on the current bar with direction sum = -3 or +3; reset to 0 otherwise
Calculation: velocity = direction sum - direction sum[velocity lookback]
Calculation: upward reclaim = close > MA(selected type, selected source, shorter lookback) AND prior-bar close <= prior-bar MA(selected type, selected source, shorter lookback)
Calculation: downward reclaim = close < MA(selected type, selected source, shorter lookback) AND prior-bar close >= prior-bar MA(selected type, selected source, shorter lookback)
Calculation: body fraction = high > low ? abs(close - open) / (high - low) : 0
Calculation: VIX reading = VIX close(T1, still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) when available; otherwise fallback volatility-futures close(T1, still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars)
Long: (VIX filter disabled OR VIX reading > long VIX threshold) AND upward reclaim AND ((direction sum on prior bar = -3 AND extreme-run count on prior bar >= slow extreme-run threshold) OR ((direction sum on prior bar = -3 OR direction sum[velocity lookback] = -3) AND extreme-run count on prior bar >= sharp extreme-run threshold AND extreme-run count on prior bar < slow extreme-run threshold AND velocity >= velocity threshold AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close > open AND high > low AND body fraction >= body-fraction threshold))) OR (direction sum on prior bar <= -non-extreme score threshold AND velocity >= velocity threshold))
