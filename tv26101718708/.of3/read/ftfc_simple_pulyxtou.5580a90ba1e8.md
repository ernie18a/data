Calculation: continuity score = sum over open(T1*), open(T2), open(T3), and open(T4) of +1 when chart close >= that open and -1 otherwise; each open is from the current still-forming bar of its timeframe, sampled on chart bars, and T1* is the chart timeframe when its duration is at least T1, otherwise T1.
Calculation: price strength (%) = abs((close - open(T2)) / open(T2)) * 100.
Calculation: open spread (%) = (max(open(T2), open(T3), open(T4)) - min(open(T2), open(T3), open(T4))) / min(open(T2), open(T3), open(T4)) * 100.
Long: continuity score >= long-entry threshold AND price strength (%) >= minimum-strength parameter AND open spread (%) >= minimum-spread parameter for the debounce parameter consecutive chart bars AND cooldown counter is zero (decrement it toward zero each chart bar and set it to the cooldown-length parameter after a losing exit to flat) AND (position is flat OR position is short and continuity score >= short-exit threshold for the debounce parameter consecutive chart bars).
Short: shorts are enabled AND continuity score <= short-entry threshold AND (monthly-red gate is disabled OR close < open(T4)) AND price strength (%) >= minimum-strength parameter AND open spread (%) >= minimum-spread parameter for the debounce parameter consecutive chart bars AND cooldown counter is zero (decrement it toward zero each chart bar and set it to the cooldown-length parameter after a losing exit to flat) AND (position is flat OR position is long and continuity score <= long-exit threshold for the debounce parameter consecutive chart bars) AND NOT (position is flat AND the long-entry condition qualifies).
Exit: long, continuity score <= long-exit threshold for the debounce parameter consecutive chart bars AND no short entry is triggered.
Exit: short, continuity score >= short-exit threshold for the debounce parameter consecutive chart bars AND no long entry is triggered.
