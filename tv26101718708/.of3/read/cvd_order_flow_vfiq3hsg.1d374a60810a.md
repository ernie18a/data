Calculation: Delta = sum of volume over all T1 bars within each chart bar, adding volume when close > open and subtracting volume when close < open; calculate on chart bars using completed T1 bars
Calculation: CVD = running sum of Delta on chart bars, reset at each exchange session open when session accumulation is selected and otherwise never reset
Calculation: Volume trust = SMA(min(full coverage, count of T1 bars within each chart bar / expected T1 bars per chart bar) × volume cap, trust lookback), where volume cap is zero before positive chart volume is observed, the tick-volume cap for tick-volume data, and the regular-volume cap otherwise
Calculation: Signal grade = 0 below the lower trust threshold, 1 from the lower to below the middle threshold, 2 from the middle to below the upper threshold, and 3 at or above the upper threshold
Long: Signal grade >= minimum grade AND bullish signals enabled AND ((current confirmed pivot low of the selected low or close source, using left and right pivot lookbacks, is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low, and EMA(CVD, divergence-smoothing lookback) at the current pivot is higher than at the previous pivot, OR hidden divergences are enabled AND the current pivot low is higher than the previous pivot low AND EMA(CVD, divergence-smoothing lookback) at the current pivot is lower than at the previous pivot) AND the current pivot is between the minimum and maximum bar distances from the previous pivot low AND (no preceding pivot high exists OR the preceding pivot high minus the current pivot low is at least the swing ATR multiplier × ATR(absorption period) on the confirmation bar) AND the absolute CVD change between same-side pivots is at least the minimum CVD-move multiplier × SMA(abs(Delta), absorption period) on the confirmation bar AND (the volume average at the current pivot is <= 0 OR the current pivot volume is at least the minimum pivot-volume multiplier × its volume average) OR absorption is enabled AND the previous chart bar has Delta < 0 with abs(Delta) >= effort multiplier × SMA(abs(Delta), absorption period) and SMA(abs(Delta), absorption period) > 0, its high-low range <= range threshold × ATR(absorption period) and ATR(absorption period) > 0, AND the current confirmed chart bar has close > open)
