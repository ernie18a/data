Calculation: T1 structure bias = Long when the latest two PivotHigh(high, pivot-strength parameter, same pivot-strength parameter, T1, last completed T1 bar) values and latest two corresponding PivotLow(low, same parameters, T1, last completed T1 bar) values are both rising; Short when both pairs are non-rising; Neutral otherwise
Calculation: Entry price = close in close mode; otherwise, for a long, max(open, level + breakout-distance parameter), and for a short, min(open, level - breakout-distance parameter)
Calculation: Adjusted risk = max(minimum-stop-distance parameter, raw risk after limiting it to the maximum-stop-distance parameter in cap mode); raw risk is entry price minus breakout-bar low for a long, or breakout-bar high minus entry price for a short; skip mode rejects raw risk above the maximum-stop-distance parameter
Long: On chart bars, an unused marked NQ level is crossed above level + breakout-distance parameter (close > threshold and prior close <= threshold in close mode, or high > threshold and prior high <= threshold in wick mode), and the level is marked, the configured New York trading window is open, no position is open, the daily trade count is below its configured maximum, and the bar is confirmed in close mode; the configured NQ direction permits long (in automatic mode, stored T1 structure bias is not Short), each enabled confirmation filter agrees (stored confirmation-symbol T1 structure bias is not Short and/or its latest breakout since levels were marked is upward, with a breakout defined as its chart-bar close crossing above a marked confirmation level × (1 + confirmation-breakout-percent parameter / 100)), and the configured stop rule does not skip the risk
Short: On chart bars, an unused marked NQ level is crossed below level - breakout-distance parameter (close < threshold and prior close >= threshold in close mode, or low < threshold and prior low >= threshold in wick mode), and the level is marked, the configured New York trading window is open, no position is open, the daily trade count is below its configured maximum, and the bar is confirmed in close mode; the configured NQ direction permits short (in automatic mode, stored T1 structure bias is not Long), each enabled confirmation filter agrees (stored confirmation-symbol T1 structure bias is not Long and/or its latest breakout since levels were marked is downward, with a breakout defined as its chart-bar close crossing below a marked confirmation level × (1 - confirmation-breakout-percent parameter / 100)), and the configured stop rule does not skip the risk
Exit: long, low <= entry price - adjusted risk OR high >= entry price + adjusted risk × reward-to-risk parameter
Exit: short, high >= entry price + adjusted risk OR low <= entry price - adjusted risk × reward-to-risk parameter
