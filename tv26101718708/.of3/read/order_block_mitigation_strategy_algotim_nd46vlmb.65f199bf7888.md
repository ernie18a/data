Calculation: Break(side) = the first bar, at or after the latest PivotHigh(high, swingPeriod, swingPeriod) for long or PivotLow(low, swingPeriod, swingPeriod) for short, whose close is beyond that pivot price by bosBufferAtr * ATR(atrPeriod), above for long and below for short
Calculation: Origin(side) = the most recent bar, from the bar of the pivot broken by Break(side) through originWindow bars after it (capped at the current bar), whose close is below its open for long or above its open for short
Calculation: Zone(side) = on a bar where Break(side) occurs and Origin(side) exists with (high - low) of Origin(side) >= impulseMinAtr * ATR(atrPeriod) at Origin(side)'s bar, the zone with top = high of Origin(side), bottom = low of Origin(side), disp = abs(close - broken pivot price) / ATR(atrPeriod), impulse = (high - low) of Origin(side) / ATR at Origin(side)'s bar, birthAtr = ATR(atrPeriod), dist = abs(close - Midpoint(zone)) / ATR(atrPeriod)
Calculation: LiveZones(side) = list of Zone(side) values, updated each bar: append the zone created on this bar, if any; remove the oldest while more than maxZones are listed; remove each zone with Mitigation(Z, side) >= invalidationPct
Calculation: Mitigation(Z, side) = for long, max(0, (Z.top - lowest low since and including Z's creation bar) / (Z.top - Z.bottom) * 100); for short, max(0, (highest high since and including Z's creation bar - Z.bottom) / (Z.top - Z.bottom) * 100)
Calculation: Midpoint(Z) = (Z.top + Z.bottom) / 2
Calculation: Trigger(side) = the newest Z in LiveZones(side) such that the bar's low (long) or high (short) lies within Z, Z has produced no decision of this side on an earlier bar, Mitigation(Z, side) <= entryCapPct, and (NOT midFilter OR close >= Midpoint(Z) for long, close <= Midpoint(Z) for short); none if no such Z exists
Calculation: WickTest(side) = for long, min(open, close) - low >= wickFraction * (high - low); for short, high - max(open, close) >= wickFraction * (high - low)
Calculation: CloseTest(side) = for long, close - low >= closeFraction * (high - low); for short, high - close >= closeFraction * (high - low)
Calculation: EngulfTest(side) = for long, close > open AND close > open[1] AND open <= close[1]; for short, close < open AND close < open[1] AND open >= close[1]
Calculation: Rejection(side) = the test or tests selected by rejectionPattern from WickTest(side), EngulfTest(side), CloseTest(side), and WickTest(side) AND CloseTest(side)
Calculation: Score(Z, side) = round(mean of five terms, each limited to 0 through 100: 100 * Z.disp / dispCap; 100 * Z.impulse / impulseCap; 100 - Mitigation(Z, side); 100 * (ATR(atrPeriod) / Z.birthAtr - 1); 100 * Z.dist / distCap)
Long: entriesEnabled AND Trigger(long) exists AND Rejection(long) AND (NOT expansionFilter OR high - low >= expansionMult * ATR(atrPeriod)) AND (NOT volumeFilter OR volume >= volumeMult * SMA(volume, volumeAvgPeriod))
Long: entriesEnabled AND Trigger(long) exists AND Rejection(long) AND (NOT expansionFilter OR high - low >= expansionMult * ATR(atrPeriod)) AND (NOT volumeFilter OR volume >= volumeMult * SMA(volume, volumeAvgPeriod)) AND Score(Trigger(long), long) >= premiumThreshold
Short: entriesEnabled AND Trigger(short) exists AND Rejection(short) AND (NOT expansionFilter OR high - low >= expansionMult * ATR(atrPeriod)) AND (NOT volumeFilter OR volume >= volumeMult * SMA(volume, volumeAvgPeriod))
Short: entriesEnabled AND Trigger(short) exists AND Rejection(short) AND (NOT expansionFilter OR high - low >= expansionMult * ATR(atrPeriod)) AND (NOT volumeFilter OR volume >= volumeMult * SMA(volume, volumeAvgPeriod)) AND Score(Trigger(short), short) >= premiumThreshold
