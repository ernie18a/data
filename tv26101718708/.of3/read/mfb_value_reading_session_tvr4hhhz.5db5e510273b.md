Calculation: Previous-session volume profile = on chart bars, assign each T1 bar's volume from the last completed session to the row containing its close, across that session's chart-bar high-to-low range, using a row-resolution parameter; POC is the center of the highest-volume row (lowest row wins ties); starting at POC, expand the value area by adding the adjacent row with greater volume (upper row wins ties) until its volume reaches the target-fraction parameter of total session volume; VAH and VAL are the value area's upper and lower bounds
Calculation: VA MID = (VAH(previous-session volume profile) + VAL(previous-session volume profile)) / 2
Long: during the configured current session, current session open > VAH(previous-session volume profile) AND POC(previous-session volume profile) > VA MID(previous-session volume profile)
Short: during the configured current session, current session open < VAL(previous-session volume profile) AND POC(previous-session volume profile) < VA MID(previous-session volume profile)
