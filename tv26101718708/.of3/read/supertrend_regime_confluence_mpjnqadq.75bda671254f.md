Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR(ATR length) / SMA(ATR(ATR length), regime lookback), or 1 if that SMA is not positive
Calculation: Volatility regime = volatile if ATR ratio > the higher regime threshold, ranging if ATR ratio < the lower regime threshold, and trending otherwise
Calculation: Adjusted SuperTrend multiplier = clamp(base multiplier × (1 + (ATR ratio − 1) × volatility adjustment) in the volatile regime, base multiplier × ranging adjustment in the ranging regime, or base multiplier otherwise, bounded by base multiplier × lower multiplier bound and base multiplier × upper multiplier bound
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR(ATR length), or the fallback value if ATR is unavailable
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or the fallback ratio if that SMA is not positive
Calculation: Volume points = highest volume points if volume ratio ≥ the highest volume ratio threshold, else middle volume points if volume ratio ≥ the middle volume ratio threshold, else lower volume points if volume ratio ≥ the baseline volume ratio threshold, else lowest volume points; the ratio thresholds and point values descend in the stated order
Calculation: Band displacement points(direction) = highest displacement points if directional displacement / Safe ATR ≥ the highest displacement threshold, else middle displacement points if it is ≥ the middle threshold, else lower displacement points if it is ≥ the lower threshold, else positive displacement points if directional displacement > 0, else zero points; directional displacement is close − SuperTrend band for bullish direction and SuperTrend band − close for bearish direction; thresholds and point values descend in the stated order
Calculation: EMA alignment points(direction) = highest alignment points if direction agrees with close versus EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) and absolute distance from that EMA / Safe ATR exceeds the far-distance threshold, else aligned points if direction agrees, else near-distance points if that normalized distance is below the near-distance threshold, else unaligned points; the far-distance threshold exceeds the near-distance threshold
Calculation: Regime points = trending points in the trending regime, volatile points in the volatile regime, or ranging points in the ranging regime; trending points exceed volatile points, which exceed ranging points
Calculation: Prior band-distance points = highest distance points if abs(close[1] − SuperTrend band[1]) / Safe ATR ≥ the highest distance threshold, else middle distance points if it is ≥ the middle threshold, else lower distance points if it is ≥ the lower threshold, else lowest distance points; thresholds and point values descend in the stated order, and points are zero if the prior band is unavailable
Calculation: Signal score(direction) = min(round(Volume points + Band displacement points(direction) + EMA alignment points(direction) + Regime points + Prior band-distance points), score cap)
Calculation: Stop distance at entry = ATR(ATR length) × ATR stop multiplier in ATR stop mode, entry price × stop percentage / 100 in percent stop mode, or abs(entry price − SuperTrend band) in SuperTrend stop mode
Calculation: Take-profit distance at entry = stop distance at entry × reward-to-risk parameter in risk:reward mode, or entry price × take-profit percentage / 100 in percent mode
Long: SuperTrend(source, ATR length, adjusted SuperTrend multiplier) flips bullish
Short: SuperTrend(source, ATR length, adjusted SuperTrend multiplier) flips bearish
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, SuperTrend(source, ATR length, adjusted SuperTrend multiplier) flips bullish AND datetime is within the configured start and end times AND more than the cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the last entered position (initial last-entry bar index is zero) AND long trading is enabled AND Signal score(bullish) ≥ minimum score AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback)) AND (ranging filter is disabled OR volatility regime is not ranging) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, SuperTrend(source, ATR length, adjusted SuperTrend multiplier) flips bearish AND datetime is within the configured start and end times AND more than the cooldown parameter bars have elapsed since the last entered position (initial last-entry bar index is zero) AND short trading is enabled AND Signal score(bearish) ≥ minimum score AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback)) AND (ranging filter is disabled OR volatility regime is not ranging) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − stop distance at entry OR (take-profit mode is risk:reward or percent AND high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance at entry) OR (trailing stop is enabled AND high reaches entry price + stop distance at entry after entry AND low ≤ highest high since trailing activation − ATR at entry × trailing offset multiplier) OR (stop mode is SuperTrend AND SuperTrend(source, ATR length, adjusted SuperTrend multiplier) flips bearish)
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + stop distance at entry OR (take-profit mode is risk:reward or percent AND low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance at entry) OR (trailing stop is enabled AND low reaches entry price − stop distance at entry after entry AND high ≥ lowest low since trailing activation + ATR at entry × trailing offset multiplier) OR (stop mode is SuperTrend AND SuperTrend(source, ATR length, adjusted SuperTrend multiplier) flips bullish)
