Calculation: Smoothed momentum = EMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), momentum-smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Velocity = Smoothed momentum - Smoothed momentum[1]
Calculation: Smoothed velocity = EMA(Velocity, velocity-smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Acceleration = EMA(Smoothed velocity - Smoothed velocity[1], acceleration-smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Curvature z-score = (Acceleration - SMA(Acceleration, z-score lookback)) / STDEV(Acceleration, z-score lookback), or 0 when STDEV is 0
Calculation: Higher-timeframe momentum = EMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), momentum-smoothing lookback) on T1 bars, using the last completed T1 bar's value on chart bars
Long: (confirmation disabled AND Curvature z-score crosses above ignition threshold AND Velocity > 0 AND Smoothed momentum > oversold level AND Smoothed momentum < overbought level AND (HTF filter disabled OR Higher-timeframe momentum < overbought level)) OR (confirmation enabled AND an unconfirmed pending setup opened within the confirmation window by those same ignition-long conditions AND Velocity > 0)
Short: (confirmation disabled AND Curvature z-score crosses below negative ignition threshold AND Velocity < 0 AND Smoothed momentum > oversold level AND Smoothed momentum < overbought level AND (HTF filter disabled OR Higher-timeframe momentum > oversold level)) OR (confirmation enabled AND an unconfirmed pending setup opened within the confirmation window by those same ignition-short conditions AND Velocity < 0)
Short: (confirmation disabled AND Curvature z-score crosses below negative exhaustion threshold AND Smoothed momentum >= overbought level AND Rising(Smoothed momentum, prior-trend lookback)[1] AND (HTF filter disabled OR Higher-timeframe momentum >= overbought level) AND (volume-climax filter disabled OR volume >= volume-climax multiple * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback))) OR (confirmation enabled AND an unconfirmed pending setup opened within the confirmation window by those same exhaustion-top conditions AND Velocity < 0)
Long: (confirmation disabled AND Curvature z-score crosses above exhaustion threshold AND Smoothed momentum <= oversold level AND Falling(Smoothed momentum, prior-trend lookback)[1] AND (HTF filter disabled OR Higher-timeframe momentum <= oversold level) AND (volume-climax filter disabled OR volume >= volume-climax multiple * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback))) OR (confirmation enabled AND an unconfirmed pending setup opened within the confirmation window by those same exhaustion-bottom conditions AND Velocity > 0)
