Calculation: expected move = open(T1, current bar, forming in real time) × (close(T1, selected volatility-proxy bars, last completed bar historically and forming bar in real time) / 100) × sqrt(1 / annualization day-count parameter), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: higher-timeframe trend = EMA(close, higher-timeframe lookback, T2 bars), computed on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars using the last completed T2 bar historically and the forming T2 bar in real time
Calculation: stop distance = if ATR stop is enabled, min(ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, maximum stop distance); otherwise maximum stop distance
Long: close crosses above the selected SMA or EMA of chart close over the trend lookback AND volume > SMA(volume, average-volume lookback) × volume-spike multiplier AND (time filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(chart bars, ADX lookback) > ADX threshold) AND close > higher-timeframe trend AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI ceiling AND open(T1, current bar) + expected move - close > wall-clearance parameter
Short: close crosses below the selected SMA or EMA of chart close over the trend lookback AND volume > SMA(volume, average-volume lookback) × volume-spike multiplier AND (time filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(chart bars, ADX lookback) > ADX threshold) AND close < higher-timeframe trend AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI floor AND close - (open(T1, current bar) - expected move) > wall-clearance parameter
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance OR (time filter enabled AND datetime is outside the configured session)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance OR (time filter enabled AND datetime is outside the configured session)
