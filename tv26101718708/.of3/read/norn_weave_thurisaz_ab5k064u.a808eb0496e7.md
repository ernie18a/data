Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR(high, low, close, auto-calibration ATR lookback) / close × 100
Calculation: ADX threshold = if auto calibration is enabled, nearest-rank percentile of ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) over the calibration lookback at the calibration percentile, using the fallback threshold if unavailable; otherwise the manual ADX threshold
Calculation: ATR factor = if auto calibration is enabled, base ATR factor + clamp(1 − ratio, lower adjustment bound, upper adjustment bound) × ATR adjustment, where ratio = ATR percentage / SMA(ATR percentage, ATR-average lookback) when that average is available and positive, otherwise 1; otherwise the manual ATR factor
Calculation: Stop-loss threshold = if auto calibration is enabled, clamp(−SMA(ATR percentage, stop-loss smoothing lookback) × stop-loss multiplier, minimum stop-loss bound, maximum stop-loss bound), using the fallback stop-loss threshold if the SMA is unavailable; otherwise the manual stop-loss percentage
Calculation: Chart Dow trend = persistent state initialized neutral; update the latest swing high to high[lookback] when it equals the highest high in the centered window of left and right lookback bars, and the latest swing low to low[lookback] when it equals the lowest low in that window; set trend up when close exceeds the latest swing high, down when close is below the latest swing low, and otherwise retain the prior trend
Calculation: T1 Dow trend = persistent state initialized neutral and evaluated on chart bars using last completed T1 values; update the latest swing high and low from the latest nonmissing T1 pivot high and low, using equal left and right pivot lengths and pivot outputs shifted by one T1 bar; set trend up when the last completed T1 close exceeds the latest swing high, down when it is below the latest swing low, and otherwise retain the prior trend
Calculation: T1 Fibonacci levels = using the latest T1 swing high and low, range = swing high − swing low; long 0.382 and 0.618 levels = swing low + range × 0.382 and swing low + range × 0.618; short 0.382 and 0.618 levels = swing high − range × 0.382 and swing high − range × 0.618
Long: position is flat or short AND EMA(close, swing lookback × EMA scale ratio) > its prior chart-bar value AND chart Dow trend is up AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold AND (footprint-delta filter is disabled OR its selected external footprint-delta SMA is unavailable OR that SMA > 0) AND (T1 Dow trend is up or neutral OR (T1 Dow trend is down AND (either long Fibonacci level is unavailable OR close < long 0.382 level OR close > long 0.618 level)))
Short: position is flat or long AND EMA(close, swing lookback × EMA scale ratio) < its prior chart-bar value AND chart Dow trend is down AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold AND (footprint-delta filter is disabled OR its selected external footprint-delta SMA is unavailable OR that SMA < 0) AND (T1 Dow trend is down or neutral OR (T1 Dow trend is up AND (either short Fibonacci level is unavailable OR close > short 0.382 level OR close < short 0.618 level)))
Exit: long, close-based return from entry price < stop-loss threshold
Exit: short, close-based return from entry price < stop-loss threshold
Exit: long, break-even stop is enabled AND close-based return from entry price has reached the break-even trigger since entry AND low <= entry price
Exit: short, break-even stop is enabled AND close-based return from entry price has reached the break-even trigger since entry AND high >= entry price
Exit: long, chart Dow trend is down
Exit: short, chart Dow trend is up
Exit: long, high > (long countertrend entry with entry close < long 0.382 level ? long 0.382 level : entry-bar close + ATR(high, low, close, take-profit ATR lookback) × ATR factor × TP1 multiple)
Exit: short, low < (short countertrend entry with entry close > short 0.382 level ? short 0.382 level : entry-bar close − ATR(high, low, close, take-profit ATR lookback) × ATR factor × TP1 multiple)
Exit: long, high > entry-bar close + ATR(high, low, close, take-profit ATR lookback) × ATR factor × TP2 multiple
Exit: short, low < entry-bar close − ATR(high, low, close, take-profit ATR lookback) × ATR factor × TP2 multiple
Exit: long, high > entry-bar close + ATR(high, low, close, take-profit ATR lookback) × ATR factor × TP3 multiple
Exit: short, low < entry-bar close − ATR(high, low, close, take-profit ATR lookback) × ATR factor × TP3 multiple
