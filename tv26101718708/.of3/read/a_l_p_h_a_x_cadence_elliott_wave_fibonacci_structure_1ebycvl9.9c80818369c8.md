Calculation: Swings = newest-first list of (price, type) entries, updated on each bar for each newly confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing pivot length, swing pivot length) (type High) or PivotLow(low, swing pivot length, swing pivot length) (type Low): if the list is empty, prepend the pivot; else if the pivot type equals Swings[0].type, ignore the pivot; else if abs(pivot price - Swings[0].price) < ATR(ATR length) * minimum leg multiple, replace Swings[0] with the pivot; else prepend the pivot
Calculation: Market structure = 0 initially, then +1 on a bar where the upside break price > High level, -1 on a bar where the downside break price < Low level, otherwise unchanged; upside break price = close (or high if close-only switch is off); downside break price = close (or low if close-only switch is off); High level = price of the most recent PivotHigh(high, swing pivot length, swing pivot length), reset to the upside break price after each upside break; Low level = price of the most recent PivotLow(low, swing pivot length, swing pivot length), reset to the downside break price after each downside break
Calculation: HTF bull = EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: HTF bear = EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: Bull bias = (Market structure = 1) OR (Market structure = 0 AND HTF bull)
Calculation: Bear bias = (Market structure = -1) OR (Market structure = 0 AND HTF bear)
Calculation: Not extreme chop = chop filter off OR Choppiness Index(chop length) < chop maximum
Calculation: Bullish trigger candle = (close > open AND (close - low) / (high - low) > trigger range fraction) OR (close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close >= open[1])
Calculation: Bearish trigger candle = (close < open AND (high - close) / (high - low) > trigger range fraction) OR (close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close <= open[1])
Calculation: Bull W2 = types of Swings[0..2] are Low, High, Low AND Swings[1].price > Swings[2].price AND NOT (types of Swings[0..4] are Low, High, Low, High, Low)
Calculation: Bull W4 = types of Swings[0..4] are Low, High, Low, High, Low AND Swings[1].price > Swings[3].price AND Swings[3].price > Swings[4].price
Calculation: Bear W2 = types of Swings[0..2] are High, Low, High AND Swings[1].price < Swings[2].price AND NOT (types of Swings[0..4] are High, Low, High, Low, High)
Calculation: Bear W4 = types of Swings[0..4] are High, Low, High, Low, High AND Swings[1].price < Swings[3].price AND Swings[3].price < Swings[4].price
Calculation: Bull leg = Swings[1].price - Swings[2].price when Bull W2; Swings[3].price - Swings[4].price when Bull W4
Calculation: Bear leg = Swings[2].price - Swings[1].price when Bear W2; Swings[4].price - Swings[3].price when Bear W4
Calculation: Zone entry long = Low between Fibonacci retracement(Swings[2].price, Swings[1].price, shallower zone fraction) and Fibonacci retracement(Swings[2].price, Swings[1].price, deeper zone fraction), AND close >= Fibonacci retracement(Swings[2].price, Swings[1].price, deeper zone fraction)
Calculation: Zone entry short = High between Fibonacci retracement(Swings[2].price, Swings[1].price, shallower zone fraction) and Fibonacci retracement(Swings[2].price, Swings[1].price, deeper zone fraction), AND close <= Fibonacci retracement(Swings[2].price, Swings[1].price, deeper zone fraction)
Calculation: Layer count(side, structure term, zone term, trigger term) = number of true terms among: structure term, zone term, trigger term, HTF term, volume term, Not extreme chop, RSI-zone term, EMA term, RSI-momentum term, plus 1; HTF term = (HTF bias filter off OR HTF bull) for long, (HTF bias filter off OR HTF bear) for short; volume term = (volume filter off OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume length) * volume multiple); RSI-zone term = (RSI filter off OR RSI(close, RSI length) >= RSI long minimum) for long, (RSI filter off OR RSI(close, RSI length) <= RSI short maximum) for short; EMA term = (EMA filter off OR close > EMA(close, chart EMA length)) for long, (EMA filter off OR close < EMA(close, chart EMA length)) for short; RSI-momentum term = (RSI filter off OR RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI(close, RSI length)[1]) for long, (RSI filter off OR RSI(close, RSI length) < RSI(close, RSI length)[1]) for short

Short: Not extreme chop AND (hard HTF alignment off OR HTF bear) AND Bear W2 AND Bear bias AND Bear leg >= ATR(ATR length) * minimum leg multiple AND Swings[0].price < Swings[2].price AND Zone entry short AND Bearish trigger candle AND Layer count(short, Bear bias, Zone entry short, Bearish trigger candle) >= minimum layers
Short: Not extreme chop AND (hard HTF alignment off OR HTF bear) AND Bear W4 AND Market structure = -1 AND Bear leg >= ATR(ATR length) * minimum leg multiple AND Zone entry short AND Bearish trigger candle AND Layer count(short, Market structure = -1, Zone entry short, Bearish trigger candle) >= minimum layers
Short: Not extreme chop AND (hard HTF alignment off OR HTF bear) AND (Bear W2 OR Bear W4) AND Bear bias AND Bear leg >= ATR(ATR length) * minimum leg multiple AND close crosses below Swings[1].price AND Layer count(short, Bear bias, true, true) >= minimum layers
