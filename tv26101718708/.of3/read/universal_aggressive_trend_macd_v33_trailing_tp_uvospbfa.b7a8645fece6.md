Calculation: close-based open profit (%) = if long, 100 * (close - entry price) / entry price; if short, 100 * (entry price - close) / entry price
Calculation: peak close-based open profit (%) = maximum close-based open profit (%) over confirmed closes since entry
Long: long entries allowed AND position is flat AND (no previous entry OR chart bars since previous entry >= cooldown bars) AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND (EMA slope filter disabled OR EMA(close, trend lookback) > EMA(close, trend lookback)[slope lookback]) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND ((MACD arming disabled OR (MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crossed above its signal line within the arming-validity lookback bars AND MACD line > signal line)) OR (continuation entries enabled AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 AND close > highest(high, continuation lookback)[1]))
Short: short entries allowed AND position is flat AND (no previous entry OR chart bars since previous entry >= cooldown bars) AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND (EMA slope filter disabled OR EMA(close, trend lookback) < EMA(close, trend lookback)[slope lookback]) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND ((MACD arming disabled OR (MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crossed below its signal line within the arming-validity lookback bars AND MACD line < signal line)) OR (continuation entries enabled AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 AND close < lowest(low, continuation lookback)[1]))
Exit: Long, low <= entry-signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry signal bar * stop multiplier
Exit: Short, high >= entry-signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry signal bar * stop multiplier
Exit: Long, trailing take-profit enabled AND peak close-based open profit (%) >= minimum peak profit (%) AND current close-based open profit (%) <= (trailing mode is relative AND peak close-based open profit (%) * (1 - giveback (%) / 100) OR trailing mode is percentage points AND peak close-based open profit (%) - giveback (percentage points))
Exit: Short, trailing take-profit enabled AND peak close-based open profit (%) >= minimum peak profit (%) AND current close-based open profit (%) <= (trailing mode is relative AND peak close-based open profit (%) * (1 - giveback (%) / 100) OR trailing mode is percentage points AND peak close-based open profit (%) - giveback (percentage points))
Exit: Long, opposite-MACD exit enabled AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses below its signal line
Exit: Short, opposite-MACD exit enabled AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses above its signal line
