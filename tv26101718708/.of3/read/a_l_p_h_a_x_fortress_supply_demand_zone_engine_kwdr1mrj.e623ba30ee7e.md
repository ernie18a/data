Calculation: Qualifying departure = (high − low) > ATR(ATR lookback) × departure-range threshold AND abs(close − open) > (high − low) × departure-body fraction AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume threshold).
Calculation: Base range = include the candle immediately before the departure; extend backward through consecutive candles, up to the maximum-base-candles parameter, while abs(close − open) < base-body fraction × ATR(departure candle) AND each candle is not a qualifying departure; stop at the first failure.
Calculation: Chart demand zone = formed on a confirmed chart bar when the previous bar is a qualifying bullish departure; bottom = base low and top = max(base high, departure open). Chart supply zone = formed on a confirmed chart bar when the previous bar is a qualifying bearish departure; top = base high and bottom = min(base low, departure open).
Calculation: T1 demand zone = formed on bars of T1 when the previous T1 bar is a qualifying bullish departure; bottom = low of the candle immediately before the departure and top = max(high of that candle, departure open). T1 supply zone = formed on bars of T1 when the previous T1 bar is a qualifying bearish departure; top = high of the candle immediately before the departure and bottom = min(low of that candle, departure open). Chart bars consume lookahead-off T1 values: the last completed T1 bar historically and the current forming T1 bar in real time.
Calculation: Active chart zones = retain the newest zones up to the active-zones-per-side parameter; remove supply when close > top in body-close mode or high > top in wick-touch mode, and remove demand when close < bottom in body-close mode or low < bottom in wick-touch mode. Mark a zone tested after a standard rejection signal, used after any signal, and swept after a sweep signal. Keep only the latest same-side T1 zone; maintain and invalidate it on chart bars using the same mitigation conditions.
Calculation: Zone grade = A+ when score ≥ A+-grade cutoff, A when score ≥ A-grade cutoff, B when score ≥ B-grade cutoff, otherwise C. Score = min((departure range / ATR at departure) × move-score weight, move-score cap) + (volume confirmation enabled ? min((departure volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) × volume-score weight, volume-score cap) : volume-off points) + VWAP-confluence points when enabled and session VWAP is within the zone boundaries expanded by ATR at formation × VWAP tolerance + HTF-overlap points when enabled and an active same-side T1 zone overlaps + premium/discount points when supply forms above or demand below the dealing equilibrium + base points. Use a volume ratio of one when the volume average is zero.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA(estimated bullish volume, volume-delta lookback) − EMA(estimated bearish volume, volume-delta lookback), where estimated bullish volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), estimated bearish volume = volume − estimated bullish volume, and when high = low the two estimates divide volume equally.
Calculation: Dealing equilibrium = (highest(high, dealing-range lookback) + lowest(low, dealing-range lookback)) / 2.
Calculation: Common entry gate = signal toggle enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND current bar index − last signal bar ≥ cooldown-bars parameter AND (trend filter disabled OR long: close > EMA(close, trend lookback), short: close < EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback) ≥ minimum ADX) AND (volume-delta filter disabled OR long: volume delta > 0, short: volume delta < 0) AND (premium/discount filter disabled OR long: close < dealing equilibrium, short: close > dealing equilibrium).
Calculation: Zone eligibility = an active zone with grade at least the minimum-grade parameter AND (one-signal-per-zone disabled OR zone unused).
Calculation: Target level = signal-bar close + ATR(signal bar, ATR lookback) × target-distance parameter for long signals, or signal-bar close − ATR(signal bar, ATR lookback) × target-distance parameter for short signals.
Long: Common entry gate with long filters AND Zone eligibility for a demand zone AND (fresh-zone filter disabled OR zone not tested) AND low ≤ zone top AND low ≥ zone bottom − ATR(ATR lookback) × touch tolerance AND close > open AND close > zone bottom AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ minimum rejection-wick fraction.
Short: Common entry gate with short filters AND Zone eligibility for a supply zone AND (fresh-zone filter disabled OR zone not tested) AND high ≥ zone bottom AND high ≤ zone top + ATR(ATR lookback) × touch tolerance AND close < open AND close < zone top AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ minimum rejection-wick fraction.
Long: Common entry gate with long filters AND sweep signals enabled AND Zone eligibility for a demand zone AND zone not previously swept AND low < zone bottom − ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum sweep depth AND close > zone bottom.
Short: Common entry gate with short filters AND sweep signals enabled AND Zone eligibility for a supply zone AND zone not previously swept AND high > zone top + ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum sweep depth AND close < zone top.
Exit: long, high ≥ long target level OR close < associated demand-zone bottom in body-close mode OR low < associated demand-zone bottom in wick-touch mode.
Exit: short, low ≤ short target level OR close > associated supply-zone top in body-close mode OR high > associated supply-zone top in wick-touch mode.
