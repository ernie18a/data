Calculation: Source move (%) = (to-source / from-source − 1) × 100 when the from-source is nonzero; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Qualifying dump = absolute source move > anomaly threshold AND close < open AND (absolute source move ≤ dump cap OR cap disabled) AND (daily low-break filter disabled OR low < lowest(low, daily low-break lookback, T1, prior completed T1 bar)); calculate the rolling low on T1 bars and sample it on chart bars.
Calculation: Reclaim setup = starts on a confirmed chart bar with a qualifying dump when cooldown is inactive and position is flat or long with pyramid capacity; store that bar’s high and low, and while reclaim is pending replace them with the high and low of each confirmed bar making a lower low whose absolute open-to-close change is within the per-bar dump cap.
Calculation: Green setup low = lowest low since the qualifying dump while the green-close setup is pending.
Calculation: Flat range (%) = (highest(high, flat lookback) − lowest(low, flat lookback)) / lowest(low, flat lookback) × 100 when the lowest low is positive; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Entry filter passes = close is not below the highest high over the separate resistance lookback on the prior completed T1 bar within the configured resistance proximity AND (trend filter disabled OR change in the configured trend series’ close over its trend lookback > 0); calculate the high on T1 bars and sample it on chart bars; calculate the change on T2 bars and sample it on chart bars using the current T2 bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Cooldown inactive = no trade-close cooldown is active, or current datetime is at or after the last trade-close datetime plus the configured cooldown duration.
Calculation: Position profit (%) = (close − average entry price) / average entry price × 100.
Calculation: Peak profit (%) = highest position-profit percentage on confirmed chart bars since the most recent entry or add fill.
