Calculation: bullish CISD level = when a chart bar sets the current chart-period low below the preceding chart-period low, use its open if its close is at or below its open and the preceding bar's close is at or above its open, unless both bars close at their opens; otherwise scan i from 2 through lookback, stopping if low[i] < low, and at the first close[i] >= open[i] with close[i-1] < open[i-1], use open[i-1], raised to the highest open[j] among down bars from j=i-1 through 0; if the level is below the current open, use the current high when close > open, otherwise the current open
Calculation: bearish CISD level = when a chart bar sets the current chart-period high above the preceding chart-period high, use its open if its close is at or above its open and the preceding bar's close is at or below its open, unless both bars close at their opens; otherwise scan i from 2 through lookback, stopping if high[i] > high, and at the first close[i] <= open[i] with close[i-1] > open[i-1], use open[i-1], lowered to the lowest open[j] among up bars from j=i-1 through 0; if the level is above the current open, use the current low when close < open, otherwise the current open
