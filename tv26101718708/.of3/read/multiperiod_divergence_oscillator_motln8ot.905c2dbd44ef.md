Calculation: BullHiddenDivergence = hidden divergence enabled AND PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length) AND low at current PivotLow > low at previous PivotLow AND UO at current PivotLow < UO at previous PivotLow AND bars between previous and current PivotLow <= max bars between pivots
