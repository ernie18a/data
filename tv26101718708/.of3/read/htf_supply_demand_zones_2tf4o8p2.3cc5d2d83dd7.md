Calculation: Demand zones = On each completed T1 bar where close > open, use the nearest preceding bearish completed T1 bar within the shared maximum-lookback parameter; its configured body or full high-low range sets the zone boundaries, if the zone width exceeds one minimum tick. Retain only the newest zones up to the shared zone-count parameter. A demand zone is broken when close < its bottom if the shared close-break parameter is enabled, otherwise when low < its bottom.
Calculation: Supply zones = On each completed T1 bar where close < open, use the nearest preceding bullish completed T1 bar within the shared maximum-lookback parameter; its configured body or full high-low range sets the zone boundaries, if the zone width exceeds one minimum tick. Retain only the newest zones up to the shared zone-count parameter. A supply zone is broken when close > its top if the shared close-break parameter is enabled, otherwise when high > its top.
Long: On a chart bar, low <= the top of any retained unbroken demand zone AND high >= that zone's bottom
Short: On a chart bar, high >= the bottom of any retained unbroken supply zone AND low <= that zone's top
