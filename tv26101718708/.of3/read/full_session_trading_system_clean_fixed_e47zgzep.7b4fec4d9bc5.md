Calculation: HTF EMA pair = (EMA(close, shorter lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars using the current T1 bar while it is forming in real time and the latest completed T1 bar historically, EMA(close, longer lookback) with the same sampling)
Calculation: Asia session range high/low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the current Asia session
Calculation: stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar × stop multiplier when ATR stops are enabled, otherwise fixed stop offset
Long: (UTC bar-open time is in the London or New York session AND the number of true conditions in {EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND (HTF filter disabled OR first HTF EMA in the HTF EMA pair > second HTF EMA), low < lowest(low, lookback) on the previous chart bar, RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its midpoint, close < EMA(close, shorter lookback), candle confirmation disabled OR close > open} >= minimum trend conditions) OR (UTC bar-open time is in the Asia session AND close <= Asia session range low + ATR(ATR lookback) × range-proximity multiplier AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold)
Short: (UTC bar-open time is in the London or New York session AND the number of true conditions in {EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND (HTF filter disabled OR first HTF EMA in the HTF EMA pair < second HTF EMA), high > highest(high, lookback) on the previous chart bar, RSI(close, RSI lookback) < its midpoint, close > EMA(close, shorter lookback), candle confirmation disabled OR close < open} >= minimum trend conditions) OR (UTC bar-open time is in the Asia session AND close >= Asia session range high - ATR(ATR lookback) × range-proximity multiplier AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > overbought threshold)
Exit: long, low <= entry-signal bar close - stop distance OR high >= entry-signal bar close + stop distance × reward/risk parameter
Exit: short, high >= entry-signal bar close + stop distance OR low <= entry-signal bar close - stop distance × reward/risk parameter
