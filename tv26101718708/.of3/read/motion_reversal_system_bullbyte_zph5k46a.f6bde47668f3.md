Calculation: Adaptive directional gate = max(base directional threshold, mean(abs(signed Kaufman Efficiency Ratio), shared baseline lookback) + adaptive ER multiplier * stdev(abs(signed Kaufman Efficiency Ratio), shared baseline lookback)); update on chart bars including the current bar.
Calculation: Commitment at bar offset i = candle sign * abs(close[i]-open[i]) / max(high[i]-low[i], minimum price increment) * min(volume[i] / max(SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)[i], volume-average floor), volume-ratio cap) / volume-ratio scale when volume measurement is selected; use volume measurement for Volume mode when volume is positive, and for Auto mode only on non-index instruments with positive volume. Otherwise use wick measurement: candle sign * wick commitment scale * abs(close[i]-(high[i]+low[i])/2) / max(high[i]-low[i], minimum price increment). Candle sign is positive when close[i] >= open[i], negative otherwise; Wick mode always uses wick measurement.
Calculation: Trend commitment deviation(D) = (mean of D * Commitment(i) over the prior trend-statistics lookback bars where D * Commitment(i) > 0 - D * Commitment(0)) / sample standard deviation of those prior values; deviation is zero when their standard deviation is zero. D is positive for a bull trend and negative for a bear trend.
Calculation: Failure count(D) counts these current-bar conditions: D * (close-close[1]) < 0; trend-side wick / max(abs(close-open), minimum price increment) >= wick-rejection threshold, where the trend-side wick is high-max(open,close) for D positive and min(open,close)-low for D negative; Trend commitment deviation(D) >= commitment-anomaly threshold; and max(high-low, minimum price increment) >= range-expansion threshold * ATR(ATR lookback).
Calculation: Failure score(D), when the selected signal mode's count gate is met, = abs(close-close[1])/ATR * (1 + min(trend-side wick/body, wick-strength cap)/wick-strength scale) * (1 + min(max(Trend commitment deviation(D),0), anomaly-strength cap)/anomaly-strength scale) * (1 + min(max(high-low, minimum price increment)/ATR, range-strength cap)/range-strength scale), where body = max(abs(close-open), minimum price increment).
Calculation: Pressure meter(D) during a confirmed trend = velocity strength * (1 + wick strength) * (1 + anomaly strength) * (1 + range strength), where velocity strength is abs(close-close[1])/ATR, halved when D * (close-close[1]) >= 0, and the other strengths are the corresponding normalized terms in Failure score(D); it is zero without a confirmed trend.
Calculation: Adaptive pressure baseline = rolling mean of the pressure meter over the shared baseline lookback + pressure-baseline multiplier * its rolling standard deviation, calculated on chart bars including the current bar.
Calculation: Target scale at entry = 1 + min(Failure score(D) / max(adaptive pressure baseline, positive denominator floor), target-score cap) * target nudge.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR at entry * stop-distance parameter; target distance = ATR at entry * target-2-distance parameter * Target scale at entry.
Long: On a confirmed closed chart bar, while flat and after the configured cooldown since the prior closure, with history available for the directional, trend-statistics, and shared baseline lookbacks: signed Kaufman Efficiency Ratio(close, directional lookback) < -Adaptive directional gate AND abs(close-close[directional lookback])/ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum move size in ATR AND Failure count(-1) meets the selected mode (all four conditions, at least three, or the velocity condition and at least one other). Entry price is the signal-bar close.
Short: On a confirmed closed chart bar, while flat and after the configured cooldown since the prior closure, with history available for the directional, trend-statistics, and shared baseline lookbacks: signed Kaufman Efficiency Ratio(close, directional lookback) > Adaptive directional gate AND abs(close-close[directional lookback])/ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum move size in ATR AND Failure count(+1) meets the selected mode (all four conditions, at least three, or the velocity condition and at least one other). Entry price is the signal-bar close.
Exit: Long, on a later confirmed closed chart bar, high >= entry price + target distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance.
Exit: Short, on a later confirmed closed chart bar, low <= entry price - target distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance.
