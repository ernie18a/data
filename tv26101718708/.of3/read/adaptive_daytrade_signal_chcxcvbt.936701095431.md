Calculation: Pullback zone = EMA(close, shorter lookback) * pullback percentage / 100
Calculation: Long stop = on entry, using entry-bar values, if swing stop is enabled, min(lowest(low, swing lookback), entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiplier); otherwise, entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiplier; hold until the next entry
Calculation: Short stop = on entry, using entry-bar values, if swing stop is enabled, max(highest(high, swing lookback), entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiplier); otherwise, entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiplier; hold until the next entry
Long: ADX(lookback) >= trend threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, longer lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)[trend-slope lookback] AND close > EMA(close, longer lookback)
Short: ADX(lookback) >= trend threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, longer lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)[trend-slope lookback] AND close < EMA(close, longer lookback)
Long: ((ADX(lookback) >= trend threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, longer lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)[trend-slope lookback] AND close > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND low >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) - pullback zone AND low <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) + pullback zone AND close > open AND (VWAP alignment disabled OR close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the session parameter in exchange time)) OR (ADX(lookback) < trend threshold AND close <= lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger multiplier) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= oversold threshold AND close > open AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the session parameter in exchange time))) AND (most recent signal side is short or neutral)
Short: ((ADX(lookback) >= trend threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, longer lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)[trend-slope lookback] AND close < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND high >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) - pullback zone AND high <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) + pullback zone AND close < open AND (VWAP alignment disabled OR close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the session parameter in exchange time)) OR (ADX(lookback) < trend threshold AND close >= upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger multiplier) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= overbought threshold AND close < open AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the session parameter in exchange time))) AND (most recent signal side is long or neutral)
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + (entry price - long stop) * TP2 multiple
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price - (short stop - entry price) * TP2 multiple
