Calculation: active leg = under the selected anchor policy, Swing starts a bearish leg at each accepted confirmed swing high and a bullish leg at each accepted confirmed swing low; accept alternating pivots only when their separation is at least the minimum-swing parameter times ATR(swing ATR lookback), and replace a same-type pivot only with a more extreme one. Structure break starts a bullish leg when close > the last accepted swing high and the leg is not already bullish, anchored at the lowest low from that swing high through the current chart bar; start a bearish leg on the inverse break, anchored at the highest high. Fast starts a bullish leg at the prior low when a new high follows a low, and a bearish leg at the prior high when a new low follows a high; a new high or low is a chart-bar extreme over the fast lookback, and if both occur, choose the high when close ≥ open, otherwise the low. Manual starts one bullish leg on the first chart bar at or after the selected anchor datetime. Swing pivots use separate left and right lookback parameters; ATR amplitude is evaluated on the confirmation bar. All leg calculations use chart bars.
Calculation: anchored VWAP = sum(selected price source × effective volume weight from the active leg anchor through the current chart bar) / sum(effective volume weights over those bars); effective volume weight is volume, optionally capped at a configured multiple of median volume over its lookback, and multiplied by exp(−ln(2) × age in bars / half-life) when half-life weighting is selected; otherwise there is no age decay.
Calculation: active-leg weighted standard deviation = sqrt(max(sum(effective volume weight × selected price source²) / sum(effective volume weight) − anchored VWAP², 0)).
Calculation: retest tolerance = active-leg weighted standard deviation × touch-tolerance parameter when the deviation is positive; otherwise ATR(swing ATR lookback) × fallback ATR-tolerance parameter.
Calculation: risk distance = ATR(risk ATR lookback on the entry bar) × selected stop multiplier; the risk ATR lookback is separate from the swing ATR lookback, and the selected stop and three target multipliers come from the selected risk preset or separate custom parameters.
Long: an accepted confirmed swing high is above the previous accepted swing high by more than equality-tolerance parameter × ATR(swing ATR lookback), or an accepted confirmed swing low is above the previous accepted swing low by more than that tolerance.
Short: an accepted confirmed swing high is below the previous accepted swing high by more than equality-tolerance parameter × ATR(swing ATR lookback), or an accepted confirmed swing low is below the previous accepted swing low by more than that tolerance.
Long: active leg direction > 0.
Short: active leg direction < 0.
Long: a bullish leg is re-anchored on a confirmed chart bar after the warm-up threshold, the greater of the sum of the pivot left and right lookbacks and the warm-up lookback.
Short: a bearish leg is re-anchored on a confirmed chart bar after the warm-up threshold, the greater of the sum of the pivot left and right lookbacks and the warm-up lookback.
Long: close crosses above anchored VWAP, the current bar does not re-anchor the leg, and the confirmed chart bar is after the warm-up threshold.
Short: close crosses below anchored VWAP, the current bar does not re-anchor the leg, and the confirmed chart bar is after the warm-up threshold.
Long: on a confirmed chart bar after the warm-up threshold, position is flat, risk distance > 0, active leg direction > 0, low ≤ anchored VWAP + retest tolerance, high ≥ anchored VWAP − retest tolerance, and the immediately preceding uninterrupted run has reached the minimum-away-bars parameter, with each bar's low > its anchored VWAP + its retest tolerance.
Short: on a confirmed chart bar after the warm-up threshold, position is flat, risk distance > 0, active leg direction < 0, low ≤ anchored VWAP + retest tolerance, high ≥ anchored VWAP − retest tolerance, and the immediately preceding uninterrupted run has reached the minimum-away-bars parameter, with each bar's high < its anchored VWAP − its retest tolerance.
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, low ≤ entry price if break-even is enabled and TP1 was reached on an earlier bar, otherwise low ≤ entry price − risk distance; OR high ≥ entry price + risk distance × TP3 multiplier.
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, high ≥ entry price if break-even is enabled and TP1 was reached on an earlier bar, otherwise high ≥ entry price + risk distance; OR low ≤ entry price − risk distance × TP3 multiplier.
