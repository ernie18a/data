Calculation: Price range = max(high - low, tick size)
Calculation: ATR floor = max(ATR(ATR lookback), tick size)
Calculation: Effective volume = volume if available and positive, otherwise the zero-volume replacement parameter
Calculation: Relative volume = effective volume / SMA(effective volume, volume-baseline lookback) if that average is positive, otherwise the invalid-average replacement parameter
Calculation: Close location = clamp((close - low) / price range, 0, 1); body ratio = abs(close - open) / price range; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low
Calculation: Volume center = VWMA(hlc3, structure lookback) when defined, otherwise EMA(hlc3, structure lookback)
Calculation: Deviation = max(STDEV(hlc3, structure lookback) when defined, otherwise ATR floor, ATR floor * deviation-floor factor)
Calculation: Safe ratio(numerator, denominator) = numerator / denominator when abs(denominator) exceeds the ratio epsilon, otherwise zero
Calculation: Signed volume = effective volume * ((close location - 0.5) * 2)
Calculation: Participation = clamp(Safe ratio(EMA(signed volume, participation lookback), EMA(effective volume, participation lookback)), -1, 1)
Calculation: Center slope = clamp(Safe ratio(volume center - volume center[1], ATR floor), -1, 1); displacement = clamp(Safe ratio(close - volume center, deviation), -1, 1)
Calculation: Direction = participation * participation weight + center slope * slope weight + displacement * displacement weight
Calculation: Control center = volume center + deviation * participation * control adjustment factor; control half-width = max(deviation * control-width factor, ATR floor * minimum control-width factor * profile scale); control upper/lower = control center +/- control half-width
Calculation: Outer upper/lower = volume center +/- deviation * outer-width factor * profile scale
Calculation: Value half-width = max(deviation * value-width factor, ATR floor * minimum value-width factor * profile scale); value upper/lower = volume center +/- value half-width
Calculation: Session control center, upper, lower and outer upper/lower = the corresponding current control and outer levels on the first confirmed chart bar at initialization or each T1 boundary; hold those values through the T1 interval
Calculation: Raw bullish ribbon = direction >= ribbon direction threshold AND Safe ratio(EMA(hlc3, ribbon-basis lookback) - EMA(hlc3, ribbon-basis lookback)[1], ATR floor) >= -ribbon slope tolerance AND close >= volume center; raw bearish ribbon = direction <= -ribbon direction threshold AND the same slope <= ribbon slope tolerance AND close <= volume center
Calculation: Bull ribbon = current bar confirmed AND raw bullish ribbon now AND raw bullish ribbon one bar ago; bear ribbon = current bar confirmed AND raw bearish ribbon now AND raw bearish ribbon one bar ago
Calculation: Trend line = EMA(EMA(WMA(close, weighted-close lookback), trend smoothing lookback), the same trend smoothing lookback); trend slope = Safe ratio(trend line - trend line[1], ATR floor)
Calculation: Bull trend evidence = close > trend line + ATR floor * trend-price buffer AND trend slope >= trend-slope threshold; bear trend evidence = close < trend line - ATR floor * trend-price buffer AND trend slope <= -trend-slope threshold
Calculation: Trend state = initialize bullish; on confirmed chart bars switch to the opposite state only after its trend evidence occurs for the trend confirmation count of consecutive bars; reset the candidate count when evidence is neutral or matches the current state
Calculation: Fast ribbon = EMA(EMA(hlc3, fast ribbon lookback), ribbon smoothing lookback); slow ribbon = EMA(EMA(hlc3, slow ribbon lookback), the same ribbon smoothing lookback), with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Momentum = EMA(close, short momentum lookback) - EMA(close, long momentum lookback), with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: Bull votes = count of close > open, close > close[1], momentum > momentum[1], fast ribbon > slow ribbon, bull ribbon, bullish trend state, lower wick > body, and direction > 0; bear votes = count of close < open, close < close[1], momentum < momentum[1], fast ribbon < slow ribbon, bear ribbon, bearish trend state, upper wick > body, and direction < 0
Calculation: Bull sweep = confirmed AND low < lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) AND close > lowest(low[1], sweep lookback); bear sweep = confirmed AND high > highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < highest(high[1], sweep lookback)
Calculation: Bull break = confirmed AND close > highest(high[1], break lookback) AND close > open; bear break = confirmed AND close < lowest(low[1], break lookback) AND close < open, with the break lookback shorter than the sweep lookback
Calculation: Participation passes = relative volume >= high-profile volume threshold when profile scale >= profile-scale cutoff, otherwise relative volume >= standard volume threshold
Calculation: Compressed = value half-width < ATR floor * compression factor
Calculation: Bull acceptance = confirmed AND close > value upper AND close > session control upper AND direction >= acceptance direction threshold AND body ratio >= acceptance body threshold AND close location >= bullish close-location threshold; bear acceptance = confirmed AND close < value lower AND close < session control lower AND direction <= -acceptance direction threshold AND body ratio >= acceptance body threshold AND close location <= bearish close-location threshold
Calculation: Bull reclaim = confirmed AND low <= session control lower AND close > session control upper AND direction >= reclaim direction threshold AND lower wick > upper wick AND body ratio >= reclaim body threshold; bear reclaim = confirmed AND high >= session control upper AND close < session control lower AND direction <= -reclaim direction threshold AND upper wick > lower wick AND body ratio >= reclaim body threshold
Calculation: Bull liquidity condition = bull sweep AND bull break AND participation passes AND direction >= liquidity direction threshold AND close > session control center; bear liquidity condition = bear sweep AND bear break AND participation passes AND direction <= -liquidity direction threshold AND close < session control center
Calculation: Long rejection = high >= session outer upper AND close < session outer upper; short rejection = low <= session outer lower AND close > session outer lower
Calculation: Structural long = (bull acceptance OR bull reclaim OR bull liquidity condition) AND NOT long rejection AND bull votes >= strong vote threshold; structural short = (bear acceptance OR bear reclaim OR bear liquidity condition) AND NOT short rejection AND bear votes >= strong vote threshold
Calculation: Raw turn long = confirmed AND Safe ratio(highest(high, turn lookback) - lowest(low, turn lookback), ATR floor) >= turn-range threshold AND bars since lowest low in the turn lookback is between the turn-age minimum and maximum inclusive AND Safe ratio(close - lowest(low, turn lookback), ATR floor) >= turn-rise threshold AND bull votes >= turn vote threshold AND (bull break OR bull sweep) AND NOT compressed; raw turn short = confirmed AND the same range condition AND bars since highest high in the turn lookback is within the same age limits AND Safe ratio(highest(high, turn lookback) - close, ATR floor) >= turn-drop threshold AND bear votes >= turn vote threshold AND (bear break OR bear sweep) AND NOT compressed
Calculation: Turn long = on each confirmed chart bar increment the consecutive count when raw turn long is true and raw turn short is false, otherwise reset it to zero; true only when the resulting count equals the turn confirmation count. Turn short uses the converse conditions and its own count
Calculation: Ribbon long = bull ribbon AND NOT bull ribbon[1] AND bullish trend state AND direction >= ribbon-entry direction threshold AND close > volume center; ribbon short = bear ribbon AND NOT bear ribbon[1] AND bearish trend state AND direction <= -ribbon-entry direction threshold AND close < volume center
Calculation: Trend long = trend state changes from bearish to bullish AND bull ribbon AND direction >= trend-entry direction threshold; trend short = trend state changes from bullish to bearish AND bear ribbon AND direction <= -trend-entry direction threshold
Calculation: Integrated long = confirmed AND close > open AND close > trend line AND close > volume center AND fast ribbon > slow ribbon AND momentum > momentum[1] AND bull votes >= strong vote threshold AND NOT compressed; integrated short = confirmed AND close < open AND close < trend line AND close < volume center AND fast ribbon < slow ribbon AND momentum < momentum[1] AND bear votes >= strong vote threshold AND NOT compressed
Calculation: Long request = confirmed AND close > open AND (structural long OR turn long OR ribbon long OR trend long OR integrated long); short request = confirmed AND close < open AND (structural short OR turn short OR ribbon short OR trend short OR integrated short)
Calculation: Signal state = initialize neutral; after each confirmed chart bar set to long when a long request occurs while the prior state is neutral or short, set to short when a short request occurs while the prior state is neutral or long, and otherwise hold
Long: Long request AND signal state entering this bar is neutral or short
Short: Short request AND signal state entering this bar is neutral or long
