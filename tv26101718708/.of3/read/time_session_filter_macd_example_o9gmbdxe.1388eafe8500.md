Calculation: Smoothed T1 MACD signal line = SMA(MACD signal line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, selected MACD moving-average type, T1 data mapped to chart bars: last completed T1 bar historically, forming T1 bar in realtime), round(T1 duration in minutes / chart timeframe duration in minutes))
Calculation: Smoothed T2 trend moving average = SMA(selected trend moving average(close, trend lookback, selected trend moving-average type, T2 data mapped to chart bars: last completed T2 bar historically, forming T2 bar in realtime), round(T2 duration in minutes / chart timeframe duration in minutes))
Long: Smoothed T1 MACD line crosses above Smoothed T1 MACD signal line AND Smoothed T1 MACD line < 0 AND Smoothed T2 trend moving average > its value one chart bar ago AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar open and close are both within the configured session on selected weekdays)
Short: Smoothed T1 MACD line crosses below Smoothed T1 MACD signal line AND Smoothed T1 MACD line > 0 AND Smoothed T2 trend moving average < its value one chart bar ago AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar open and close are both within the configured session on selected weekdays)
Exit: long, Smoothed T1 MACD line crosses below Smoothed T1 MACD signal line OR session-end closing is enabled AND session filter is enabled AND (chart-bar open or close is outside the configured session on selected weekdays)
Exit: short, Smoothed T1 MACD line crosses above Smoothed T1 MACD signal line OR session-end closing is enabled AND session filter is enabled AND (chart-bar open or close is outside the configured session on selected weekdays)
