Calculation: Bar range = max(high - low, minimum tick)
Calculation: ATR denominator = max(coalesce(ATR(ATR lookback), TrueRange), minimum tick)
Calculation: Upper wick ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / Bar range
Calculation: Lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / Bar range
Calculation: Short reclaim ATR = (selected pivot high - close) / ATR denominator
Calculation: Long reclaim ATR = (close - selected pivot low) / ATR denominator
Calculation: SFP score(wick ratio, reclaim ATR, volume passes, swing age) = round(min(SFP score cap, max(0, min(wick score cap, (wick ratio - wick score base) / wick score span * wick score weight)) + max(0, min(reclaim score cap, reclaim ATR * reclaim score weight)) + (volume passes ? volume score weight : 0) + min(age score cap, swing age / age score span * age score weight)))
Long: On bar close, select the highest active confirmed Pivot Low(low, pivot length, pivot length) within swing lookback bars that has not been used by a prior qualifying SFP, among those with low <= pivot low and close > pivot low; lower wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND long reclaim ATR >= minimum reclaim ATR AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND SFP score(lower wick ratio, long reclaim ATR, volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier, bars since selected pivot bar) >= minimum SFP score
Short: On bar close, select the lowest active confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot length, pivot length) within swing lookback bars that has not been used by a prior qualifying SFP, among those with high >= pivot high and close < pivot high; upper wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND short reclaim ATR >= minimum reclaim ATR AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND SFP score(upper wick ratio, short reclaim ATR, volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier, bars since selected pivot bar) >= minimum SFP score
