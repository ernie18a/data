Calculation: Spread ratio = (high - low) / SMA(high - low, lookback), or zero when SMA(high - low, lookback) is not positive
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or one half when high equals low
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low
Calculation: Climax side = buying climax if close location >= close-location threshold; otherwise selling climax if close location <= 1 - close-location threshold; otherwise buying climax if close >= open, else selling climax. The close-location threshold is selected by the common sensitivity setting
Calculation: Normalized EMA slope = (EMA(close, trend lookback) - EMA(close, trend lookback)[slope lag]) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum price increment)
Calculation: Climax trend points = aligned-trend points when (climax side is buying climax AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND normalized EMA slope > trend-slope threshold) OR (climax side is selling climax AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND normalized EMA slope < -trend-slope threshold); opposing-trend points when (climax side is buying climax AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND normalized EMA slope < -trend-slope threshold) OR (climax side is selling climax AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND normalized EMA slope > trend-slope threshold); neutral-trend points otherwise
Calculation: Climax score = round(clamp(clamp(relative volume / (relative-volume threshold × relative-volume score divisor) × relative-volume score cap, score floor, relative-volume score cap) + clamp(spread ratio / (spread-ratio threshold × spread score divisor) × spread score cap, score floor, spread score cap) + clamp((climax side is buying climax ? close location : 1 - close location) × close-location score cap, score floor, close-location score cap) + clamp(body ratio × body score cap, score floor, body score cap) + Climax trend points, score floor, base-score ceiling). Relative volume is volume / SMA(volume, lookback), or zero when that SMA is not positive
Calculation: Qualifying climax = confirmed bar AND (volume Z-score >= its threshold OR relative volume >= its threshold) AND spread ratio >= its threshold AND (close location >= its threshold OR close location <= 1 - its threshold OR body ratio >= body-fraction threshold) AND Climax score >= minimum event score AND (no previous qualifying climax OR bars since the previous qualifying climax >= cooldown period AND > tracking window). Volume Z-score is (volume - SMA(volume, lookback)) / standard deviation(volume, lookback), or zero when the standard deviation is zero. The volume Z-score, relative-volume, spread-ratio, and close-location thresholds use the common sensitivity setting
Long: Qualifying climax AND climax side is selling climax
Short: Qualifying climax AND climax side is buying climax
