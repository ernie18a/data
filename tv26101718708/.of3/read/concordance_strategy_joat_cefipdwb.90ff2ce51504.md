Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume baseline lookback) when that SMA is positive, otherwise the configured fallback.
Calculation: Local bullish alignment = EMA(close, fast baseline lookback) > EMA(close, slow baseline lookback) AND EMA(close, slow baseline lookback) > EMA(close, anchor baseline lookback).
Calculation: Local bearish alignment = EMA(close, fast baseline lookback) < EMA(close, slow baseline lookback) AND EMA(close, slow baseline lookback) < EMA(close, anchor baseline lookback).
Calculation: T1 bullish alignment = the same three EMA relationships as local bullish alignment, calculated on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: T1 bearish alignment = the same three EMA relationships as local bearish alignment, calculated on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (2 * StDev(close, compression lookback) * band deviation) / EMA(close, compression lookback) when the EMA is nonzero, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bollinger/Keltner squeeze = Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band AND Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band, where each band uses EMA(close, compression lookback) as its basis and the Keltner offset is EMA(TrueRange, Keltner lookback) * Keltner multiplier.
Calculation: Bollinger width expansion = SMA(Bollinger width, compression lookback) > 0 AND Bollinger width > SMA(Bollinger width, compression lookback) * width expansion factor.
Calculation: Directional trend score = alignment weight for each bullish local or T1 alignment and its negative for each bearish alignment, plus slope weight for each fast or slow EMA above its value slope lookback bars ago and its negative when below, plus DI weight when ADX > ADX gate and DI+ > DI− and its negative when DI− > DI+, plus expansion weight when not in a squeeze and width is expanding, positive if close > EMA(close, anchor baseline lookback) and negative otherwise; DMI uses the ADX lookback for both DI and ADX smoothing.
Calculation: Bullish regime = directional trend score >= regime score threshold AND ADX > ADX gate * regime ADX factor.
Calculation: Bearish regime = directional trend score <= negative regime score threshold AND ADX > ADX gate * regime ADX factor.
Calculation: Soft bullish state = directional trend score > 0 AND local bullish alignment AND T1 fast EMA >= T1 slow EMA, using last completed T1 values on chart bars.
Calculation: Soft bearish state = directional trend score < 0 AND local bearish alignment AND T1 fast EMA <= T1 slow EMA, using last completed T1 values on chart bars.
Calculation: Confirmed structure pivots = the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, structure lookback, structure lookback) and PivotLow(low, structure lookback, structure lookback).
Calculation: Up displacement = close > open AND abs(close − open) > SMA(abs(close − open), body lookback) * displacement factor.
Calculation: Down displacement = close < open AND abs(close − open) > SMA(abs(close − open), body lookback) * displacement factor.
Calculation: Recent buy-side sweep = a confirmed bar swept below the last completed T2 low by more than ATR(ATR lookback) * sweep buffer and closed above that low, or, when weekly sweeps are enabled, did the same below the last completed T3 low; the sweep remains active through the sweep confirmation window and expires afterward.
Calculation: Recent sell-side sweep = a confirmed bar swept above the last completed T2 high by more than ATR(ATR lookback) * sweep buffer and closed below that high, or, when weekly sweeps are enabled, did the same above the last completed T3 high; the sweep remains active through the sweep confirmation window and expires afterward.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity stack = recent buy-side sweep AND close > latest confirmed structure pivot high AND previous close <= that pivot high AND (displacement is not required OR up displacement).
Calculation: Bearish liquidity stack = recent sell-side sweep AND close < latest confirmed structure pivot low AND previous close >= that pivot low AND (displacement is not required OR down displacement).
Calculation: Local Fibonacci anchors = initialize high and low to highest(high, local Fibonacci lookback) and lowest(low, local Fibonacci lookback), respectively, then update each to the newest confirmed PivotHigh or PivotLow using the separate Fibonacci pivot lookback; retain each anchor's bar index.
Calculation: T1 Fibonacci high and low = highest(high, T1, last completed bar, Fibonacci lookback) and lowest(low, T1, last completed bar, Fibonacci lookback), read on chart bars.
Calculation: Local downswing = the local low anchor bar index is later than the local high anchor bar index. T1 downswing = the lowestbars offset for low on T1 completed bars is less than the highestbars offset for high on T1 completed bars, read on chart bars.
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement level = for a downswing, low anchor + (high anchor − low anchor) * ratio; otherwise, high anchor − (high anchor − low anchor) * ratio.
Calculation: Fibonacci confluence count = count of the six configured Fibonacci ratios whose local retracement level differs from its corresponding T1 retracement level by no more than close * confluence tolerance / 100.
Calculation: Long OTE = local downswing is false AND close is between the local retracement levels at the fourth and sixth of the six configured ratios, inclusive.
Calculation: Short OTE = local downswing AND close is between the local retracement levels at the fourth and sixth of the six configured ratios, inclusive.
Calculation: HTF direction concordance = HTF agreement is disabled OR local downswing equals T1 downswing.
Calculation: Pressure composite = pressure weight * pressure Z-score + momentum weight * ((RSI(close, momentum lookback) − RSI midpoint) / RSI bias scale) + delta weight * delta Z-score. Pressure Z-score is (cumulative flow − SMA(cumulative flow, pressure normalization lookback)) / StDev(cumulative flow, pressure normalization lookback), or 0 when the deviation is not positive; cumulative flow sums ((2 * close − low − high) / (high − low)) * volume * max(relative volume, volume weighting floor), using 0 for the fraction when high = low. Delta Z-score standardizes volume * (close − open) over the delta normalization lookback, or is 0 when its standard deviation is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish pressure = pressure composite > 0 AND pressure composite > its previous chart-bar value.
Calculation: Bearish pressure = pressure composite < 0 AND pressure composite < its previous chart-bar value.
Calculation: Adaptive regression bands = on each chart bar, fit ordinary least-squares regression to close over each length from the minimum regression window through the maximum in step increments, with x ordered from oldest to newest; among fits with positive regression and variance denominators and R-squared >= the fit threshold, select the fit with greatest R-squared. Set center = intercept + slope * selected length, residual deviation = sqrt(sum of squared residuals / max(selected length − 2, 1)), and upper/lower bands = center ± residual deviation * band multiplier; bands are unavailable if no fit qualifies.
Calculation: Bullish sigma reentry = close > adaptive lower band AND previous close <= previous chart-bar adaptive lower band AND selected regression slope > 0.
Calculation: Bearish sigma reentry = close < adaptive upper band AND previous close >= previous chart-bar adaptive upper band AND selected regression slope < 0.
Calculation: Bullish ledger participation = volume / highest(volume, local Fibonacci lookback) > ledger intensity threshold AND delta Z-score > 0 AND relative volume >= relative-volume gate; use 0 for the ratio when the highest volume is not positive.
Calculation: Bearish ledger participation = volume / highest(volume, local Fibonacci lookback) > ledger intensity threshold AND delta Z-score < 0 AND relative volume >= relative-volume gate; use 0 for the ratio when the highest volume is not positive.
Calculation: Long score = strong evidence weight for bullish regime + strong evidence weight for bullish liquidity stack + unit evidence weight when OTE is disabled or Long OTE + unit evidence weight when HTF direction is concordant + full confluence weight when confluence count >= full confluence count threshold, otherwise partial confluence weight when count >= partial confluence count threshold + unit evidence weight for each of bullish pressure, bullish sigma reentry, bullish ledger participation, and relative volume >= relative-volume gate.
Calculation: Short score = strong evidence weight for bearish regime + strong evidence weight for bearish liquidity stack + unit evidence weight when OTE is disabled or Short OTE + unit evidence weight when HTF direction is concordant + full confluence weight when confluence count >= full confluence count threshold, otherwise partial confluence weight when count >= partial confluence count threshold + unit evidence weight for each of bearish pressure, bearish sigma reentry, bearish ledger participation, and relative volume >= relative-volume gate.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND session passes (non-intraday bars pass; intraday bars pass during an enabled configured London or New York interval, or when both are disabled, and pass the configured closing interval only when close avoidance is disabled or the bar is outside that interval) AND ((long score >= main entry score threshold AND bullish regime AND HTF direction concordant) OR (long score >= max(main entry score threshold − soft gate offset, soft gate floor) AND soft bullish state AND bullish pressure AND bullish sigma reentry AND (confluence count >= partial confluence count threshold OR bullish ledger participation))).
Short: confirmed chart bar AND session passes (non-intraday bars pass; intraday bars pass during an enabled configured London or New York interval, or when both are disabled, and pass the configured closing interval only when close avoidance is disabled or the bar is outside that interval) AND ((short score >= main entry score threshold AND bearish regime AND HTF direction concordant) OR (short score >= max(main entry score threshold − soft gate offset, soft gate floor) AND soft bearish state AND bearish pressure AND bearish sigma reentry AND (confluence count >= partial confluence count threshold OR bearish ledger participation))).
Calculation: Long initial risk at entry = signal-bar close − (min(low, prior completed T2 low or low if unavailable, prior completed T3 low or low if unavailable, latest confirmed structure pivot low or low if unavailable) − ATR(ATR lookback) * structure buffer − ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiple); retain this value for the position.
Calculation: Short initial risk at entry = (max(high, prior completed T2 high or high if unavailable, prior completed T3 high or high if unavailable, latest confirmed structure pivot high or high if unavailable) + ATR(ATR lookback) * structure buffer + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiple) − signal-bar close; retain this value for the position.
Calculation: Long managed stop = max(average entry price − long initial risk, average entry price when close >= average entry price + long initial risk * break-even trigger else average entry price − long initial risk, close − ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing ATR multiple).
Calculation: Short managed stop = min(average entry price + short initial risk, average entry price when close <= average entry price − short initial risk * break-even trigger else average entry price + short initial risk, close + ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing ATR multiple).
Exit: long, low <= long managed stop OR high >= average entry price + long initial risk * first target multiple OR high >= average entry price + long initial risk * second target multiple OR (directional trend score <= 0 AND pressure composite < 0) OR (time exit is enabled AND chart bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade).
Exit: short, high >= short managed stop OR low <= average entry price − short initial risk * first target multiple OR low <= average entry price − short initial risk * second target multiple OR (directional trend score >= 0 AND pressure composite > 0) OR (time exit is enabled AND chart bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade).
