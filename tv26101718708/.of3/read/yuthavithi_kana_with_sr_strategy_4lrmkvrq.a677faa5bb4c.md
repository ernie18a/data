Calculation: moving average = SMA(price source, shorter lookback)
Calculation: relative position = (close - low(T1, current T1 bar, still forming in real time, sampled on chart bars)[T1-selection setting chart bars ago]) / (high(T1, current T1 bar, still forming in real time, sampled on chart bars)[T1-selection setting chart bars ago] - low(T1, current T1 bar, still forming in real time, sampled on chart bars)[T1-selection setting chart bars ago])
Long: moving average > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND Heikin-Ashi open of the prior completed chart bar < Heikin-Ashi close of the prior completed chart bar AND moving average > moving average[previous chart bar] AND close < moving average + ATR(ATR lookback) × smallest band multiplier AND MACD histogram(close, shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, signal lookback) > zero AND (support/resistance filter disabled OR relative position > upper relative-position threshold)
Short: moving average < EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND Heikin-Ashi open of the prior completed chart bar > Heikin-Ashi close of the prior completed chart bar AND moving average < moving average[previous chart bar] AND close > moving average - ATR(ATR lookback) × smallest band multiplier AND MACD histogram(close, shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, signal lookback) < zero AND (support/resistance filter disabled OR relative position < lower relative-position threshold)
Exit: long, close > moving average + ATR(ATR lookback) × largest band multiplier OR close < moving average - ATR(ATR lookback) × middle band multiplier OR high >= entry price + (close of the latest bar satisfying the long-entry condition × take-profit percentage / 100)
Exit: short, close < moving average - ATR(ATR lookback) × largest band multiplier OR close > moving average + ATR(ATR lookback) × middle band multiplier OR low <= entry price - (close of the latest bar satisfying the short-entry condition × take-profit percentage / 100)
