Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, minus volume when close < open; doji volume contributes nothing
Calculation: Trend alignment = mean of +1 when T1 current close > EMA(close, long lookback, T1 current bar) otherwise −1; +1 when T2 current MACD line > MACD signal line otherwise −1; and +1 when chart close > EMA(close, short lookback) otherwise −1. T1 and T2 values are taken on chart bars from the current higher-timeframe bar, including its still-forming value in real time, using lookahead off
Calculation: Flash move = abs(close − close[lookback]) / close[lookback]
Calculation: Signal strength = trend weight × trend alignment + momentum score + volume score + weekend score, multiplied by the Saturday multiplier on Saturday or the Sunday multiplier on Sunday; momentum score is the positive momentum weight when RSI(close, RSI lookback) is below the oversold threshold and rising, the negative momentum weight when it is above the overbought threshold and falling, and zero otherwise; volume score adds the volume-confirmation weight when volume exceeds SMA(volume, short volume lookback) × surge factor and SMA(volume, long volume lookback) × minimum-volume factor, and adds the divergence weight when close is below close[divergence lookback] and cumulative volume delta is above its value that many bars ago, or subtracts it when the reverse comparisons hold; weekend score is zero on weekdays and the weekend bonus on weekends, multiplied by the weekend-hours bonus during the configured weekend hours
Calculation: Signal confidence = abs(signal strength) / confidence scale
Calculation: Final leverage = rounded-to-step clamp of minimum leverage and maximum leverage around minimum leverage + (maximum leverage − minimum leverage) × ((volatility safety + signal confidence) / 2) × instrument risk profile; volatility safety is the low-volatility score when ATR(ATR lookback) / close is below the low-volatility threshold, the medium-volatility score when below the high-volatility threshold, and the high-volatility score otherwise
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) × base stop multiple / (1 + (final leverage − reference leverage) / leverage adjustment scale)
Calculation: Target distance = ATR(ATR lookback) × base target multiple × (1 + signal confidence × target-confidence factor)
Long: signal strength > minimum signal strength AND trend alignment > 0 AND (weekend-only filter disabled OR Saturday and bar hour ≥ Saturday start hour OR Sunday and bar hour ≤ Sunday end hour) AND daily realized P&L / equity > −daily loss limit AND weekend realized P&L / equity > −weekend loss limit AND consecutive losing trades < loss streak limit AND available capital > minimum capital per trade + safety buffer AND position is flat AND flash move ≤ flash threshold AND equity ≥ initial equity × emergency-stop fraction
Short: signal strength < −minimum signal strength AND trend alignment < 0 AND (weekend-only filter disabled OR Saturday and bar hour ≥ Saturday start hour OR Sunday and bar hour ≤ Sunday end hour) AND daily realized P&L / equity > −daily loss limit AND weekend realized P&L / equity > −weekend loss limit AND consecutive losing trades < loss streak limit AND available capital > minimum capital per trade + safety buffer AND position is flat AND flash move ≤ flash threshold AND equity ≥ initial equity × emergency-stop fraction
Exit: long, low ≤ close[1] − stop distance[1] OR high ≥ close[1] + target distance[1] OR trailing is enabled and the long trailing stop is triggered OR abs((close − entry price) / entry price) × 100 exceeds the liquidation distance OR flash move > flash threshold OR equity < initial equity × emergency-stop fraction OR bar day is Monday and bar hour is the weekend-close hour
Exit: short, high ≥ close[1] + stop distance[1] OR low ≤ close[1] − target distance[1] OR trailing is enabled and the short trailing stop is triggered OR abs((entry price − close) / entry price) × 100 exceeds the liquidation distance OR flash move > flash threshold OR equity < initial equity × emergency-stop fraction OR bar day is Monday and bar hour is the weekend-close hour
Calculation: Liquidation distance = liquidation factor / final leverage × liquidation percentage scale
Calculation: Long trailing stop activates after price moves favorably by the trailing activation percentage and trails the highest high since entry by the trailing distance percentage of the order close
Calculation: Short trailing stop activates after price moves favorably by the trailing activation percentage and trails the lowest low since entry by the trailing distance percentage of the order close
