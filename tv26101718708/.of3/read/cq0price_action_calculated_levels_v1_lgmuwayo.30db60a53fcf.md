Calculation: zigzag pivots = pivots on chart bars, where a pivot high is a bar whose high is the highest of the last zigzag period bars including itself, a pivot low is a bar whose low is the lowest of those bars, a pivot of the opposite type to the newest pivot is added as a new pivot, and a pivot of the same type as the newest pivot replaces it only if more extreme
Calculation: leg start = price of the third-newest zigzag pivot as of the previous bar
Calculation: leg end = price of the second-newest zigzag pivot as of the previous bar
Calculation: fibonacci level(ratio) = round(leg end + (leg start - leg end) * ratio)
Calculation: prior high = highest high of bars after the leg end pivot and before the current bar, or minus infinity if none
Calculation: prior low = lowest low of bars after the leg end pivot and before the current bar, or plus infinity if none
