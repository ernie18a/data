Long: close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band (close, Bollinger lookback, standard-deviation multiplier) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > the selected EMA, SMA, RMA, or WMA of close over the trend lookback) AND (volatility filter is disabled OR standard deviation(close, volatility lookback) > SMA(standard deviation(close, volatility lookback), volatility-smoothing lookback)) AND time is within the configured start and end times AND (ROC filter is disabled OR ((close - close(T1, last completed bar)) / close(T1, last completed bar)) × 100 > ROC threshold) AND the direction setting permits long trades
Short: close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band (close, Bollinger lookback, standard-deviation multiplier) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < the selected EMA, SMA, RMA, or WMA of close over the trend lookback) AND (volatility filter is disabled OR standard deviation(close, volatility lookback) > SMA(standard deviation(close, volatility lookback), volatility-smoothing lookback)) AND time is within the configured start and end times AND (ROC filter is disabled OR ((close - close(T1, last completed bar)) / close(T1, last completed bar)) × 100 > ROC threshold) AND the direction setting permits short trades
Exit: long, close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band (close, Bollinger lookback, standard-deviation multiplier) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − long stop-loss percentage / 100) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + long profit-target percentage / 100) OR a long trailing stop is hit, activated when price reaches entry price plus the current close × long trailing-activation percentage / 100 and trailing the highest high since activation by the current close × long trailing-offset percentage / 100
Exit: short, close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band (close, Bollinger lookback, standard-deviation multiplier) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + short stop-loss percentage / 100) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − short profit-target percentage / 100) OR a short trailing stop is hit, activated when price reaches entry price minus the current close × short trailing-activation percentage / 100 and trailing the lowest low since activation by the current close × short trailing-offset percentage / 100
