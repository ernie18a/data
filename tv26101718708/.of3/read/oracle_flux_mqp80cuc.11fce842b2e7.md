Calculation: Heikin-Ashi values = standard Heikin-Ashi open, high, low, close; HA source = their average of high, low, and close; HA run = signed count of consecutive bullish or bearish HA candles, reset to zero on a doji; HA body ratio = absolute HA body divided by HA range; bullish or bearish wick rejection = the corresponding wick is no greater than the HA range times its threshold.
Calculation: Flux Wave outputs = use HA source; form a price channel from EMA(source, channel length) and EMA(abs(source − channel EMA), channel length), and a VWAP channel from VWMA(source, channel length), falling back to SMA(source, channel length) when unavailable, and EMA(abs(source − channel VWMA), channel length); normalize each displacement by its channel deviation times an adaptive factor based on ATR(source, ATR length)/max(source, minimum price increment); form money-flow input from ((close − open)/(high − low)) × volume × min(sqrt(volume/SMA(volume, channel length)), volume-ratio cap), using zero for a zero-range candle and a volume ratio of one when its average is zero; normalize this input by its EMA absolute deviation and the adaptive factor; the three engines are EMA of the respective normalized inputs over the average length, with the money-flow input scaled; acceleration is EMA of the change in normalized price-channel displacement over max(minimum smoothing length, signal smoothing); primary is the bounded weighted mean of the three engines plus acceleration times an acceleration weight; signal is SMA(primary, signal smoothing).
Calculation: Internal VWAP oscillator = use HA source; calculate VWMA(source, length), falling back to SMA(source, length) when unavailable; normalize source displacement from it by its EMA absolute deviation and an adaptive factor based on ATR(source, ATR length)/max(source, minimum price increment); apply two successive EMAs, scale, add Flux Wave acceleration times an acceleration weight, and clamp to a symmetric bound.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low of the Flux Wave signal is below the prior confirmed price low while its signal value is above the prior pivot signal, the pivot separation is within the divergence lookback, and the signal pivot meets the oversold qualification; regular bearish divergence mirrors this at pivot highs using the overbought qualification. Hidden bullish divergence = within the divergence lookback, price makes a higher pivot low and the signal a lower pivot low, with the configured hidden-bull qualification; hidden bearish divergence = price makes a lower pivot high and signal a higher pivot high, with the configured hidden-bear qualification. Pivots use separate left and right lookbacks; regular bullish divergence remains active for its persistence period.
Calculation: Current and previous session VWAP, current and previous week-anchored VWAP, and five-session-anchored VWAP = cumulative sum(source × volume)/cumulative volume from the respective anchor, using chart bars; nearest VWAP = the enabled anchor value closest to close; price above or below nearest VWAP compares close with that value. A bullish VWAP test touches within the tolerance of nearest VWAP and closes above it; a bearish VWAP test touches within tolerance and closes below it. A current-session VWAP alignment compares close with current-session VWAP; previous-session alignment compares close with previous-session VWAP.
Calculation: Market structure levels = latest confirmed pivot high and low, each using the same left and right pivot lookback; bullish or bearish structure break = close above the pivot high or below the pivot low; bullish or bearish liquidity sweep = price crosses beyond the corresponding pivot and closes back across it. Trend = HA close above or below EMA(HA close, trend lookback). Opposite-body hold = within the hold lookback, close crosses the HA open of the most recent opposite-color HA candle and the current HA run meets its directional minimum.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = retracements of the qualifying swing legs formed from confirmed market-structure pivots and the intervening high and low over the swing-leg lookback, with the code's fallback leg from the last opposite pivot when a level is unavailable; bullish-leg levels are high − ratio × (high − low), and bearish-leg levels are low + ratio × (high − low). A level rejection requires the bar range to intersect the level within the price-relative tolerance and close on the rejecting side. Major bullish rejection is a rejection at a designated deep level of a bearish leg or at either designated major level of a bullish support leg; major bearish rejection is a rejection at a designated resistance level. Deep bullish rejection is a rejection at either designated deep support level. Fib key rejection also includes the additional designated support or resistance level used by the score.
Calculation: Money-flow value = SMA(((close − open)/(high − low)) × volume, body lookback)/SMA(volume, volume lookback) × scale, using zero for a zero-range candle and zero when average volume is zero; inflow means this value is nonnegative and outflow means it is negative. Bullish or bearish money-flow shift means the sign changes from the opposite sign within the preceding two bars; strong shift additionally requires the value and Flux Wave money-flow engine to move farther in that direction while the prior engine is on the opposite side of zero.
Calculation: Flux Wave directional trigger = a primary/signal bullish or bearish cross in the corresponding oversold or overbought zone, or within the zone lookback after entering that zone, or a qualifying divergence; alternatively, within the configured recent-cross window, the primary has crossed in that direction, is on the corresponding side of the signal, is moving in that direction, and the signal is inside its directional zone limit. Momentum confirmation also accepts the directional trigger or qualifying divergence; with a recent-cross window, it additionally accepts a recent cross with primary on the corresponding side of signal and moving in that direction. Wave rollover = at least two of the three Flux Wave engines point in the direction, primary is on the corresponding side of signal and moving in that direction, and a cross occurs, the directional engine count increases from the prior bar, or primary extends its move over the prior two bars.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish fib-wave-money-flow reversal = a major directional fib rejection, directional wave rollover, directional money-flow shift, and the corresponding exhaustion/divergence qualification. Bullish exhaustion/divergence qualification is a regular bullish divergence at an oversold or extreme-oversold signal, an active regular bullish divergence, or an oversold signal; bearish qualification is an extreme-oversold-qualified regular bearish divergence, regular bearish divergence, hidden bearish divergence, or overbought signal. Bullish reversal confluence = deep bullish fib rejection, active bullish divergence, bullish wave rollover, bullish money-flow shift, and major bullish fib rejection.
Calculation: Oracle directional scores = sum of oscillator, VWAP, HA, and structure pillar scores plus the applicable fib and reversal bonuses. Each pillar score adds its configured point weights for these tests: oscillator, directional primary/signal relation, agreement of all three engines, cross or divergence, not being in the opposite extreme zone, and directional movement; VWAP, price on the directional side of nearest VWAP, directional VWAP test, current-session alignment, and previous-session alignment; HA, candle direction, run minimum, body minimum, wick rejection, and strong candle; structure, trend direction, structure break, liquidity sweep, and money-flow sign. Oscillator fib bonus adds the configured rejection points when a fib key rejection occurs, plus rollover and reversal points when applicable. Structure fib bonus adds its configured points for bearish money-flow shift with bearish fib key rejection, or for bullish deep rejection with bullish money-flow shift or inflow, or bullish money-flow shift with bullish fib key rejection. Bullish reversal-confluence bonus adds its configured points for the designated deepest rejection, current or recent bullish divergence, wave rollover, and money-flow shift. Directional pillar counts count pillars meeting the pillar minimum; required counts also require the oscillator and HA pillars to meet that minimum.
Calculation: Strong-standard long setup = signal engine enabled AND HA bullish trend gate AND Flux Wave momentum confirmation AND bullish Flux Wave directional trigger AND (VWAP confluence disabled OR price above nearest VWAP OR bullish VWAP test) AND (trend filter disabled OR bullish trend OR bullish fib-wave-money-flow reversal with bullish liquidity sweep or wave rollover) AND bullish VWAP key alignment OR bullish reversal exception (reversal with bullish VWAP test or bullish liquidity sweep) AND all required strong pillars, including oscillator and HA, meet their minimums AND at least two Flux Wave engines are bullish with all engines bullish or a valid bullish reversal with wave rollover AND money-flow filter passes AND Flux Wave agreement filter passes AND signal is in the bullish zone and not overbought AND strong chop filter passes AND elite strong-long filter passes AND Flux Sync filter passes AND bullish Oracle score meets the strong threshold adjusted by the chop score bonus AND the standard regime gate passes. Strong pillars require every pillar to meet its minimum, and the required pillar count; elite strong-long requires either the valid bullish reversal with at least two bullish engines and bullish money-flow shift, or all engines bullish with VWAP and structure pillars meeting their minimums. Flux Wave agreement requires all engines bullish or a valid bullish reversal with at least two bullish engines and wave rollover. The strong chop filter requires ADX at or above its strong threshold, no Bollinger-width squeeze, HA body at least its minimum, and primary/signal spread at least half its configured minimum spread. Flux Sync accepts its sync condition or a score at least the strong threshold plus its extra margin with all three engines bullish, strong chop, and bullish wave rollover.
Calculation: Strong-standard short setup = the exact directional mirror of the strong-standard long setup: bearish HA gate, Flux Wave trigger and momentum, bearish VWAP and trend conditions, bearish VWAP key alignment or bearish reversal exception, bearish pillar and engine requirements, outflow, bearish zone, elite strong-short filter, bearish Flux Sync, bearish Oracle threshold, and the same strong chop and standard regime gates. Elite strong-short requires either a valid bearish reversal with at least two bearish engines and bearish money-flow shift, or all engines bearish with VWAP and structure pillars meeting their minimums.
Calculation: Strong reversal long setup = signal engine enabled AND bullish fib-wave-money-flow reversal AND bullish HA trend gate AND bullish money-flow shift AND Flux Wave agreement filter passes AND strong chop filter passes AND all required strong pillars meet their minimums with at least three pillars meeting their minimum AND bullish Oracle score meets the strong threshold minus its reversal margin, subject to its reversal regime gate, AND active bullish divergence AND bullish Flux Sync. Strong reversal short setup mirrors this, requiring bearish reversal, HA gate, money-flow shift, Flux Wave agreement, chop, pillars and score, bearish Flux Sync, and either extreme-qualified or regular bearish divergence or overbought signal.
Calculation: Swing reversal arm = at a major low/high, within the confirmation window, either a divergence or extreme-oversold/overbought exhaustion with bullish/bearish fib rejection, liquidity sweep, or VWAP test context; the extreme is within an ATR tolerance of the major lookback extreme. Divergence may be a regular divergence still recent within the confirmation window or a leg divergence: the leg extreme reaches the configured fraction of the oversold/overbought level and the recent wave extreme diverges from it by at least the configured gap. Invalidate the arm if price exceeds its armed extreme by the ATR invalidation buffer or the confirmation window expires. Re-arming is blocked until price moves the configured ATR distance from the prior signal's locked extreme, unless a new more extreme low/high occurs. Strong swing long = signal engine enabled AND an active low arm AND primary is bullish with at least two bullish engines, a bullish cross or rising primary, money inflow or improving money-flow value, and close above the prior-bar highest close or high over the structure-break lookback. Strong swing short mirrors this at a high arm and requires close below the prior-bar lowest close or low; block it during an up-thrust when close is above a rising trend EMA, ADX meets its threshold, and positive DI exceeds negative DI.
Calculation: Final strong long or short signal = corresponding strong-standard, strong-reversal, or strong-swing setup; signal cooldown has elapsed, except a swing signal may bypass cooldown when the last signal was not on the same side; and the opposite-signal guard does not block it. The guard blocks a long after a short signal within its guard period while close remains at or below that strong-short bar's high; it blocks a short after a long signal while close remains at or above that strong-long bar's low. The shorter reversal cooldown applies to a bullish reversal following a short signal. Cooldown state updates on any long or short signal.
Calculation: Normal long setup = signal engine enabled AND bullish HA trend gate AND bullish Flux Wave momentum confirmation AND primary above signal AND bullish VWAP confluence gate AND bullish trend filter gate AND money-flow filter passes AND normal chop gate passes AND at least three pillars meet the normal pillar minimum, including the HA pillar, AND bullish Oracle score is at least the normal threshold and below the strong threshold AND bullish zone filter passes AND no strong long setup. Normal short setup mirrors this with bearish conditions. Normal signals require the signal cooldown.

Calculation: Position risk distance = ATR(exit lookback) × stop-loss ATR multiple at entry. Initial long stop = entry price − risk distance; initial short stop = entry price + risk distance. At unrealized profit of at least one risk unit, ratchet the stop to breakeven; at two risk units, ratchet it to entry plus half a risk unit for a long or entry minus half a risk unit for a short. Once unrealized profit reaches the trailing activation threshold, update enabled long ATR trail to the greatest prior value of high − ATR × trail multiple, and enabled long swing trail to the greatest prior value of lowest low over the swing lookback − ATR × buffer; long stop is the greatest applicable stop. For shorts, use the least prior value of low + ATR × trail multiple and highest high over the swing lookback + ATR × buffer; short stop is the least applicable stop.
Exit: long, low <= current long stop AND (previous long stop unavailable OR previous low > previous long stop)
Exit: short, high >= current short stop AND (previous short stop unavailable OR previous high < previous short stop)
Exit: long, minimum bars in position have elapsed AND HA reversal is bearish with the configured opposite-candle run and body minimum AND (HA exits are enabled AND (trailing has activated OR unrealized profit in risk units meets the HA exit minimum))
Exit: short, minimum bars in position have elapsed AND HA reversal is bullish with the configured opposite-candle run and body minimum AND (HA exits are enabled AND (trailing has activated OR unrealized profit in risk units meets the HA exit minimum))
