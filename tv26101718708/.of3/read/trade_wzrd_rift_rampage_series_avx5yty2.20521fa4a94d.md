Calculation: Profile resets on each T2 period boundary or confirmed alternating chart-bar pivot reversal, using the same pivot lookback on both sides; at each reset, row height is fixed ticks-per-row times instrument tick size, or ATR(profile-row lookback) divided by rows-per-ATR in auto mode, with a one-tick minimum; row base is floor(reset-bar open / row height) × row height. At chart-bar close, distribute each completed T1 bar's volume equally across the rows from its low row through its high row, up to the profile row-cap; levels use the developing profile through that chart bar.
Calculation: Developing POC is the midpoint of the lowest-price row with maximum volume. Starting at POC, expand the value area by adding the higher-volume adjacent row until its volume reaches the configured percentage of total profile volume; ties choose the higher-price row. VAL and VAH are the lower and upper value-area edges. POV is the midpoint of the lowest-volume row inside the value area excluding POC; ties choose the lower-price row. These levels are unavailable when profile volume is zero or no qualifying POV row exists.
Calculation: Bar delta = sum over completed T1 bars in the current chart bar of T1 volume × sign(T1 close − previous T1 close).
Calculation: CVD = cumulative bar delta on chart bars, reset to zero on a profile-reset bar before adding that bar's delta.
Calculation: Bar delta percentage = 100 × bar delta / chart-bar volume when volume is positive, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Signal node = POV when a selected void-break setup is true, otherwise POC when a selected bounce setup is true, otherwise VAL for a long sweep or VAH for a short sweep; later setup checks override earlier ones when multiple setups are true.
Calculation: Long stop, computed and stored at entry = nearest-tick-rounded signal-bar low − ATR(risk/filter lookback) × stop multiple in ATR-stop mode; otherwise nearest-tick-rounded min(signal-bar low, signal node) − ATR(risk/filter lookback) × node buffer.
Calculation: Short stop, computed and stored at entry = nearest-tick-rounded signal-bar high + ATR(risk/filter lookback) × stop multiple in ATR-stop mode; otherwise nearest-tick-rounded max(signal-bar high, signal node) + ATR(risk/filter lookback) × node buffer.
Calculation: Long target, computed and stored at entry = nearest-tick-rounded nearest developing POC, VAH, or POV above entry when next-node mode is selected and that node is more than the next-node distance parameter × ATR(risk/filter lookback) above entry; otherwise nearest-tick-rounded entry + (entry − long stop) × reward-risk parameter. Entry is the signal-bar close.
Calculation: Short target, computed and stored at entry = nearest-tick-rounded nearest developing POC, VAL, or POV below entry when next-node mode is selected and that node is more than the next-node distance parameter × ATR(risk/filter lookback) below entry; otherwise nearest-tick-rounded entry − (short stop − entry) × reward-risk parameter. Entry is the signal-bar close.
Long: Long direction enabled AND not a profile-reset bar AND ((sweep model selected AND low < VAL AND close > VAL AND close > open AND (rejection-wick filter disabled OR high > low AND 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ wick threshold)) OR (bounce model selected AND low ≤ POC AND close > POC AND close > open AND low > VAL AND (rejection-wick filter disabled OR high > low AND 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ wick threshold)) OR (void model selected AND close > POV AND previous close ≤ current POV AND close > open)) AND (CVD-divergence filter disabled OR low < lowest(low over prior chart bars, divergence lookback) AND CVD > lowest(CVD over prior chart bars, divergence lookback)) AND (bar-delta confirmation disabled OR bar delta > 0) AND (ATR momentum guard disabled OR high − low ≤ ATR(risk/filter lookback) × range multiplier) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is inside the configured session) AND no prior long signal within the cooldown bars has a signal node less than ATR(risk/filter lookback) away.
Short: Short direction enabled AND not a profile-reset bar AND ((sweep model selected AND high > VAH AND close < VAH AND close < open AND (rejection-wick filter disabled OR high > low AND 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ wick threshold)) OR (bounce model selected AND high ≥ POC AND close < POC AND close < open AND high < VAH AND (rejection-wick filter disabled OR high > low AND 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ wick threshold)) OR (void model selected AND close < POV AND previous close ≥ current POV AND close < open)) AND (CVD-divergence filter disabled OR high > highest(high over prior chart bars, divergence lookback) AND CVD < highest(CVD over prior chart bars, divergence lookback)) AND (bar-delta confirmation disabled OR bar delta < 0) AND (ATR momentum guard disabled OR high − low ≤ ATR(risk/filter lookback) × range multiplier) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is inside the configured session) AND no prior short signal within the cooldown bars has a signal node less than ATR(risk/filter lookback) away.
Long: bar delta percentage ≥ whale delta threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier.
Short: bar delta percentage ≤ −whale delta threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ stored long stop.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low > stored long stop AND high ≥ stored long target.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ stored short stop.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high < stored short stop AND low ≤ stored short target.
