Calculation: stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on entry bar × stop ATR multiple if ATR risk mode is enabled; otherwise fixed stop points × minimum tick size
Calculation: first target distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on entry bar × first-target ATR multiple if ATR risk mode is enabled; otherwise fixed first-target points × minimum tick size
Calculation: second target distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on entry bar × second-target ATR multiple if ATR risk mode is enabled; otherwise fixed second-target points × minimum tick size
Calculation: support/resistance buffer = ATR(ATR lookback) on the current chart bar × support/resistance buffer multiple
Calculation: relative volatility = 100 × ATR(volatility ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(volatility ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) when that SMA is positive; otherwise 100
Long: no open position AND long signals enabled AND ((long trigger mode selects crossover and long signal source crosses above long reference source) OR (long trigger mode selects crossunder and long signal source crosses below long reference source) OR (long trigger mode selects above-level and long signal source crosses above long level) OR (long trigger mode selects below-level and long signal source crosses below long level) OR (long trigger mode selects non-zero event and long signal source is nonzero while its previous value is zero)) AND (baseline filter disabled OR close > selected moving average(baseline source, baseline lookback)) AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the inclusive start and end dates) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) >= ADX threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR relative volatility is between the minimum and maximum volatility bounds) AND (support/resistance filter disabled OR (T1 levels disabled OR abs(close - last-completed T1 high carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer AND abs(close - last-completed T1 low carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer) AND (T2 levels disabled OR abs(close - last-completed T2 high carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer AND abs(close - last-completed T2 low carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer))
Short: no open position AND short signals enabled AND ((short trigger mode selects crossover and short signal source crosses above short reference source) OR (short trigger mode selects crossunder and short signal source crosses below short reference source) OR (short trigger mode selects above-level and short signal source crosses above short level) OR (short trigger mode selects below-level and short signal source crosses below short level) OR (short trigger mode selects non-zero event and short signal source is nonzero while its previous value is zero)) AND (baseline filter disabled OR close < selected moving average(baseline source, baseline lookback)) AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the inclusive start and end dates) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) >= ADX threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR relative volatility is between the minimum and maximum volatility bounds) AND (support/resistance filter disabled OR (T1 levels disabled OR abs(close - last-completed T1 high carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer AND abs(close - last-completed T1 low carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer) AND (T2 levels disabled OR abs(close - last-completed T2 high carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer AND abs(close - last-completed T2 low carried onto chart bars) > support/resistance buffer))
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance AND high < entry price + first target distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance AND low > entry price - first target distance
Exit: long, second target enabled AND high >= entry price + second target distance AND (low > entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + first target distance)
Exit: short, second target enabled AND low <= entry price - second target distance AND (high < entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - first target distance)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + first target distance AND (second target disabled OR high < entry price + second target distance)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - first target distance AND (second target disabled OR low > entry price - second target distance)
