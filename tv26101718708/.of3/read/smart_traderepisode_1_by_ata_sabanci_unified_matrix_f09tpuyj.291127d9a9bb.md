Calculation: Chart-bar buy and sell volume = in precise mode, absolute T1 up-volume and down-volume aggregated onto each chart bar, including the forming T1 bar; otherwise, if high = low, half the chart-bar volume each, else buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and sell volume = volume × (high − close) / (high − low)
Calculation: Effective event lookback = the minimum of the chart-bar lookback, the event-buffer capacity minus one, and the available buffered bars
Calculation: Ranked events = within the effective event lookback ending on the current chart bar, the highest-buy event has the greatest buy volume, the highest-sell event the greatest sell volume, the lowest-positive-buy event the smallest positive buy volume, and the lowest-positive-sell event the smallest positive sell volume; ties select the most recent bar; buyer-event price is that bar's high and seller-event price is its low
Calculation: Highest-buy event power and delta = starting buy volume is buy volume at the highest-buy event; consumed volume is sell volume summed from the bars after that event through the current bar; remaining power percentage = 100 × (starting buy volume − consumed volume) / starting buy volume; cumulative delta = buy volume minus sell volume summed over those same bars
Calculation: Depletion events = for the highest-buy event, the earliest post-event bar at which cumulative sell volume reaches starting buy volume × depletion threshold / 100 or starting buy volume × (depletion threshold + dominance increment) / 100; for the highest-sell event, use cumulative buy volume and starting sell volume in the same formulas
Calculation: Nearest event = the event with the smallest bars-ago value among highest-buy, highest-sell, lowest-positive-buy, lowest-positive-sell, highest-buy depletion at either threshold, and highest-sell depletion at either threshold; ties prioritize them in that order; highest-sell and lowest-positive-sell events and highest-buy depletion events are bearish, while highest-buy and lowest-positive-buy events and highest-sell depletion events are bullish
Calculation: Post-event momentum = among up to the post-event lookback of bars after the nearest event, count closes above and below the prior bar's close; bias is bullish if the first count is greater, bearish if the second is greater, mixed if equal and nonzero, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Recent directional event = nearest-event age is within the recent-event window AND ((nearest event is bearish OR bullish) AND post-event momentum is bullish or bearish)
Calculation: Trend = when the channel is enabled and its slopes are available, split the chart-bar lookback into the segment count, with the final segment taking any remainder; select each segment's most recent highest high and lowest low, fit separate linear regressions against bar index, and classify as uptrend if both slopes exceed ATR(ATR lookback) × slope factor, downtrend if both are below its negative, otherwise ranging; when disabled or unavailable, ranging
Long: highest-buy event starting volume > 0 AND (nearest event is bearish AND nearest-event age is within the recent-event window AND post-event momentum is bullish AND bullish post-event count ≥ reversal-count threshold OR nearest event is bullish AND nearest-event age is within the recent-event window AND post-event momentum is bullish OR NOT recent directional event AND (trend is uptrend AND cumulative delta > 0 OR trend is downtrend AND cumulative delta > 0 AND remaining power percentage ≥ long-power minimum AND post-event momentum is bullish OR trend is ranging AND cumulative delta > 0 AND post-event momentum is bullish)) AND (highest-buy event is absent OR close > its high OR highest-sell event is absent OR close > its low)
Short: highest-buy event starting volume > 0 AND (nearest event is bearish AND nearest-event age is within the recent-event window AND post-event momentum is bearish OR nearest event is bullish AND nearest-event age is within the recent-event window AND post-event momentum is bearish AND bearish post-event count ≥ reversal-count threshold OR NOT recent directional event AND (trend is uptrend AND remaining power percentage < opponent-dominance threshold AND cumulative delta < 0 AND post-event momentum is bearish OR trend is downtrend AND cumulative delta < 0 OR trend is ranging AND cumulative delta < 0 AND post-event momentum is bearish)) AND (highest-buy event exists AND close ≤ its high OR highest-sell event exists AND close ≤ its low)
Calculation: Long protective levels = at entry, seller-event low − signal-bar close × stop buffer and buyer-event high − signal-bar close × target buffer; place both orders only if both event lines exist, the stop is below signal-bar close, and the target is above it
Calculation: Short protective levels = at entry, buyer-event high + signal-bar close × stop buffer and seller-event low + signal-bar close × target buffer; place both orders only if both event lines exist, the stop is above signal-bar close, and the target is below it
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop level established at entry
Exit: long, high ≥ long target level established at entry
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop level established at entry
Exit: short, low ≤ short target level established at entry
