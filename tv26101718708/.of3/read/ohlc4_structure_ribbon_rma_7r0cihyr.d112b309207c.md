Calculation: six-RMA structural score = sign(guarded normalized spread) × a bounded piecewise-linear magnitude map with separately parameterized raw-strength and score anchors; the spread is the negative lag-span times the regression slope of the six RMA(OHLC4, L1…L6) values on lag coordinates L1−1…L6−1, where L1<L2<L3<L4<L5<L6<L7<L8 are separate increasing lookbacks; normalize the spread by ATR with a separate lookback. For the guard, body=abs(spread), rawAbs=abs(spread/ATR), peak=max(peakDecay×peak[1],body), peakPct=100×body/peak, and order=100×abs(RMA(OHLC4,L1)−RMA(OHLC4,L6))/sum of the five adjacent absolute RMA differences. Same-side means the nonzero sign of spread/ATR equals its prior sign; ATR drag means same-side, rawAbs<rawAbs[1], body>=body[1], and ATR>ATR[1]. Eligibility requires same-side, peakPct>=minimum peak percentage, order>=minimum order, and rawAbs>=minimum raw strength. Trigger requires eligibility and ATR drag. Trigger blend is the lesser of maximum blend and the product of clamped peak and order confidence times (base drag weight + drag weight×clamped drag magnitude/full-drag percentage), where each confidence is its percentage above its minimum divided by the remaining range to 100%. Release and reset the guard floor and strength when not same-side, not eligible, or body< body[1]. On trigger, floor=max(rawAbs, (prior floor if present, otherwise prior rawAbs if present, otherwise rawAbs)×floor decay) and strength=max(trigger blend, prior strength×strength decay). Otherwise, while prior floor exists and prior strength is positive, floor=max(rawAbs, prior floor×floor decay) and strength=prior strength×strength decay; otherwise clear both. Guarded normalized magnitude=rawAbs+strength×max(max(rawAbs,floor if present)−rawAbs,0).
Calculation: regime state = a persistent classification from the six-RMA stack and chart structural score. The bullish stack requires each RMA from L1 through L6 to be strictly above the next; the bearish stack requires each to be strictly below the next. Bullish or bearish extreme entry requires the matching full stack and score beyond its separate extreme-entry rail; that state persists only with the same full stack and score beyond its extreme-release rail. Exceptional entry and persistence use separate exceptional-entry and exceptional-release rails and the matching full stack. The accepted bullish full-stack state requires the bullish stack, no active bullish or bearish extreme/exceptional state, and either continuation of an active bullish stack, bullish lineage with score at or above the same-side re-entry rail, or non-bullish lineage with score at or above the opposite/initial transfer rail. The bearish state is symmetric with score at or below the negative rails. When neither accepted direction applies, retain bullish lineage as bullish resistance or bearish lineage as bearish support. Accepted direction and severity update lineage; otherwise retain lineage, and clear active direction when its prior full stack is lost. Use separate parameters for the re-entry, transfer, extreme entry/release, and exceptional entry/release rails.
