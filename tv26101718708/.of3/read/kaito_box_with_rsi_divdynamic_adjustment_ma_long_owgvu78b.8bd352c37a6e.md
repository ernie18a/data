Calculation: Selected MA(source, lookback) = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of source over lookback, evaluated on chart bars; for trend conditions, source is the T1 close mapped onto chart bars by the timeframe request (last completed T1 bar historically, still-forming T1 bar in real time); VWMA uses chart-bar volume.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = low < Lowest(low[1], left lookback) AND low <= Lowest(low, right lookback) AND RSI(low, RSI lookback) > Lowest(RSI(low, RSI lookback)[1], left lookback) AND RSI(low, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = low > Highest(low[1], left lookback) AND low >= Highest(low, right lookback) AND RSI(low, RSI lookback) < Highest(RSI(low, RSI lookback)[1], left lookback) AND RSI(low, RSI lookback) > overbought threshold.
Calculation: Uptrend = (Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback A) > Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback) AND Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback B) > Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback) AND Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback C) > Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback) AND T1 close > Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback)) OR (high > Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback A) AND high > Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback B) AND high > Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback C) AND high > Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback)); each short lookback is shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: Downtrend = (Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback A) < Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback) AND Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback B) < Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback) AND Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback C) < Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback) AND T1 close < Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback)) OR (low < Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback A) AND low < Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback B) AND low < Selected MA(T1 close, short lookback C) AND low < Selected MA(T1 close, long lookback)); each short lookback is shorter than the long lookback.
Long: Downtrend AND close <= Lowest(low, box lookback) AND Bullish RSI divergence
Long: Downtrend AND close <= Lowest(low, box lookback) AND Bullish RSI divergence AND close < average entry price
Exit: long, Uptrend AND close >= Highest(high, box lookback) AND Bearish RSI divergence
Exit: long, Uptrend AND close >= Highest(high, box lookback) AND Bearish RSI divergence AND close > average entry price
