Calculation: In-session = chart bar time is within the core-session parameter in New York time OR within the extended-session parameter AND ATR(session volatility lookback) > SMA(ATR(session volatility lookback), session volatility averaging lookback) × session volatility multiplier
Calculation: Impulsive non-doji candle = high − low > ATR(impulse lookback) × impulse multiplier AND abs(close − open) > (high − low) × minimum body fraction
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = a confirmed swing low, lower than its adjacent lows, is below the preceding confirmed swing low and is within the sweep recency parameter
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = a confirmed swing high, higher than its adjacent highs, is above the preceding confirmed swing high and is within the sweep recency parameter
Calculation: Liquidity-pattern flag = count of high[k] > high[k+1] over k from one bar ago through the bar before the lookback boundary >= count threshold OR count of low[k] < low[k+1] over the same range >= count threshold
Calculation: Comparison-series confirmation = comparison-series close on the still-forming chart-timeframe bar is within the proximity parameter of its last completed T1 bar high or low, OR the comparison filter is disabled
Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe zone = latest stored bullish fair value gap zone, updated from T1 if it reports a gap, else T2 if it reports a gap, else T3 if it reports a gap, otherwise retained; each gap is computed on bars of its timeframe from the high before the intervening bar and the current low, and values are taken on chart bars from still-forming T1, T2, and T3 bars, where T1 is longer than T2 and T2 is longer than T3
Calculation: Bearish higher-timeframe zone = latest stored bearish fair value gap zone, updated from T1 if it reports a gap, else T2 if it reports a gap, else T3 if it reports a gap, otherwise retained; each gap is computed on bars of its timeframe from the low before the intervening bar and the current high, and values are taken on chart bars from still-forming T1, T2, and T3 bars, where T1 is longer than T2 and T2 is longer than T3
Calculation: Long score = sum of the parameterized zone, sweep, session, comparison-series, and liquidity-pattern weights for satisfied conditions: close within the bullish higher-timeframe zone, bullish liquidity sweep, in-session, comparison-series confirmation, and liquidity-pattern flag
Calculation: Short score = sum of the same respective parameterized weights for satisfied conditions: close within the bearish higher-timeframe zone, bearish liquidity sweep, in-session, comparison-series confirmation, and liquidity-pattern flag
Calculation: Grade(score) = S when score meets the S threshold, otherwise A when it meets the A threshold, otherwise B when it meets the B threshold, otherwise C
Long: a bearish fair value gap formed within the inversion-age parameter has close above its upper boundary AND the current bar is an impulsive non-doji candle AND a bullish liquidity sweep AND in-session AND the long signal has not fired since its last reset (a chart-bar high above all earlier chart-bar highs or close outside both stored higher-timeframe zones) AND Grade(Long score) meets the selected minimum grade
Short: a bullish fair value gap formed within the inversion-age parameter has close below its lower boundary AND the current bar is an impulsive non-doji candle AND a bearish liquidity sweep AND in-session AND the short signal has not fired since its last reset (a chart-bar low below all earlier chart-bar lows or close outside both stored higher-timeframe zones) AND Grade(Short score) meets the selected minimum grade
