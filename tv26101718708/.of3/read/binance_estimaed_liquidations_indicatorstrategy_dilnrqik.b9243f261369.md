Calculation: volume spread = volume of the second selected market − volume of the first selected market
Long: (MA mode enabled AND WMA(volume spread, short lookback) crosses above WMA(volume spread, long lookback)) OR (MA mode disabled AND difference mode enabled AND volume spread crosses above threshold) OR (MA mode disabled AND difference mode disabled AND smoothing mode enabled AND SuperSmoother(WMA(volume spread, short lookback), scaled short lookback) crosses above threshold) OR (MA mode disabled AND difference mode disabled AND smoothing mode disabled AND WMA(volume spread, short lookback) crosses above threshold), AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured date range) AND close > WMA(close, trend lookback)
Short: (MA mode enabled AND WMA(volume spread, short lookback) crosses below WMA(volume spread, long lookback)) OR (MA mode disabled AND difference mode enabled AND volume spread crosses below threshold) OR (MA mode disabled AND difference mode disabled AND smoothing mode enabled AND SuperSmoother(WMA(volume spread, short lookback), scaled short lookback) crosses below threshold) OR (MA mode disabled AND difference mode disabled AND smoothing mode disabled AND WMA(volume spread, short lookback) crosses below threshold), AND long-only mode disabled AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured date range)
Exit: long, high >= entry price × (1 + limit-distance parameter) OR low <= entry price × (1 − stop-distance parameter)
Exit: short, low <= entry price × (1 + limit-distance parameter) OR high >= entry price × (1 − stop-distance parameter)
