Calculation: Pivot lookback = manual pivot lookback, or rounded target structure duration divided by chart-bar duration, clamped to separate auto minimum and maximum parameters when auto-scaling is selected.
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) when positive; otherwise close times an ATR fallback ratio.
Calculation: Break range = max(high − low, minimum tick); body fraction = abs(close − open) / break range.
Calculation: Resistance boundary = rightmost segment of the upper convex hull of the latest pivot-window confirmed PivotHigh(selected high, pivot lookback) points; extend it to the current bar. It is valid with at least two distinct points and a negative slope whose absolute value divided by safe ATR is between the minimum and maximum slope parameters.
Calculation: Support boundary = rightmost segment of the lower convex hull of the latest pivot-window confirmed PivotLow(selected low, pivot lookback) points; extend it to the current bar. It is valid with at least two distinct points and a positive slope divided by safe ATR between the minimum and maximum slope parameters.
Calculation: Resistance channel width = maximum positive distance below the resistance boundary among the latest stored confirmed pivot lows at or after its start.
Calculation: Support channel width = maximum positive distance above the support boundary among the latest stored confirmed pivot highs at or after its start.
Calculation: Volume tier = high when PercentRank(volume, volume lookback) reaches the upper cutoff; otherwise low when it reaches or falls below the lower cutoff; otherwise medium. The tier is eligible when its corresponding tier switch is enabled.
Calculation: External agreement = true when the external gate is disabled, no directional confirmation series is wired, or the most recent nonzero external directional confirmation within the agreement window matches the break direction.
Calculation: Selected VWAP bias = sign of the external bias series when external bias is selected; otherwise sign(close − selected internal VWAP). Internal VWAP is session VWAP, rolling sum(hlc3 × volume) / sum(volume), or order-flow-swing-anchored VWAP. For the order-flow anchor, return = change(close), z = return / stdev(return, delta normalization lookback) when that deviation is positive, else zero; delta = volume × z / sqrt(1 + z²); cumulative delta resets its VWAP anchor on a confirmed PivotHigh or PivotLow of cumulative delta. Calculate internal bias on selected timeframe T8 and sample it on chart bars with lookahead off: the T8 bar is forming live and the latest available completed T8 bar historically.
Calculation: Resistance break condition = confirmed close above a valid resistance boundary plus break buffer × safe ATR, with break range at least displacement × safe ATR, body fraction at least its minimum parameter, and (close − low) / break range at least the anti-wick parameter; signal only once until the boundary endpoints change.
Calculation: Support break condition = confirmed close below a valid support boundary minus break buffer × safe ATR, with break range at least displacement × safe ATR, body fraction at least its minimum parameter, and (high − close) / break range at least the anti-wick parameter; signal only once until the boundary endpoints change.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the selected stop reference (broken resistance or entry-bar low) and entry price minus minimum stop distance × safe ATR.
Calculation: Short stop = the higher of the selected stop reference (broken support or entry-bar high) and entry price plus minimum stop distance × safe ATR.
Calculation: Target distance = the lower of the selected channel width (resistance width for long, support width for short) or fallback target ATR distance when width is unavailable, and maximum target ATR distance; when ATR targeting is selected, use fallback target ATR distance before applying the cap.
Long: Resistance break condition.
Short: Support break condition.
Long: MTF trend strip enabled AND, for each Tn in T1–T7, LinearRegression(close, shared trend lookback, current offset, Tn) > LinearRegression(close, shared trend lookback, prior Tn-bar offset, Tn); calculate on Tn bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off (forming Tn bar live, latest available completed Tn bar historically).
Short: MTF trend strip enabled AND, for each Tn in T1–T7, LinearRegression(close, shared trend lookback, current offset, Tn) < LinearRegression(close, shared trend lookback, prior Tn-bar offset, Tn); calculate on Tn bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off (forming Tn bar live, latest available completed Tn bar historically).
Long: VWAP alignment enabled AND selected VWAP bias is positive.
Short: VWAP alignment enabled AND selected VWAP bias is negative.
Long: Entries enabled AND resistance break condition AND volume tier eligible AND external agreement AND (VWAP agreement is not required OR selected VWAP bias is positive) AND (immediate-entry mode OR retest-entry mode with no confirmed close below the current resistance since the break, elapsed bars within the arm window, a post-break low at or below resistance plus touch tolerance × safe ATR, and a confirmed close above resistance).
Short: Entries enabled AND support break condition AND volume tier eligible AND external agreement AND (VWAP agreement is not required OR selected VWAP bias is negative) AND (immediate-entry mode OR retest-entry mode with no confirmed close above the current support since the break, elapsed bars within the arm window, a post-break high at or above support minus touch tolerance × safe ATR, and a confirmed close below support).
Exit: long, low at or below long stop OR high at or above entry price plus target distance; stop takes precedence if both barriers are reached on one bar.
Exit: short, high at or above short stop OR low at or below entry price minus target distance; stop takes precedence if both barriers are reached on one bar.
