Calculation: Transformed OHLC = for each X in open, high, low, and close, MA(Bollinger %B(X, band lookback, band deviation), smoothing lookback, smoothing MA type) when smoothing is enabled, otherwise Bollinger %B(X, band lookback, band deviation).
Calculation: Range offset = MA(True Range(transformed high, transformed low, transformed close), ATR lookback, ATR MA type) × ATR multiplier.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = transformed close − range offset, raised to max(transformed close − range offset, previous long trailing stop) when (previous transformed low if wick mode is enabled, otherwise previous transformed close) > previous long trailing stop.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = transformed close + range offset, lowered to min(transformed close + range offset, previous short trailing stop) when (previous transformed high if wick mode is enabled, otherwise previous transformed close) < previous short trailing stop.
Calculation: Trend direction = +1 initially; thereafter the previous direction, changed to +1 when the previous direction is −1 and (current transformed high if wick mode is enabled, otherwise current transformed close) > previous short trailing stop, or changed to −1 when the previous direction is +1 and (current transformed low if wick mode is enabled, otherwise current transformed close) < previous long trailing stop.
Calculation: Annual high filter passes = ((H is missing OR H[P chart bars ago] is missing OR H > H[P chart bars ago]) AND (H is missing OR H[2P chart bars ago] is missing OR H > H[2P chart bars ago])) OR (H is missing ? true : H[P chart bars ago] is missing ? close > H : H[2P chart bars ago] is missing ? close > max(H, H[P chart bars ago]) : close > max(H, min(H[P chart bars ago], H[2P chart bars ago]))) OR annual filtering is disabled; H is the high of the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars, and H[P] and H[2P] are that mapped series P and 2P chart bars ago.
Calculation: Annual low filter passes = ((L is missing OR L[P chart bars ago] is missing OR L < L[P chart bars ago]) AND (L is missing OR L[2P chart bars ago] is missing OR L < L[2P chart bars ago])) OR (L is missing ? true : L[P chart bars ago] is missing ? close < L : L[2P chart bars ago] is missing ? close < min(L, L[P chart bars ago]) : close < min(L, max(L[P chart bars ago], L[2P chart bars ago]))) OR annual filtering is disabled; L is the low of the last completed T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars, and L[P] and L[2P] are that mapped series P and 2P chart bars ago.
Long: Trend direction = +1
Short: Trend direction = −1
Long: previous-close coloring is enabled AND transformed close > previous transformed close OR previous-close coloring is disabled AND transformed close > transformed open
Short: previous-close coloring is enabled AND transformed close < previous transformed close OR previous-close coloring is disabled AND transformed close < transformed open
Long: Lowest(Trend direction, signal-delay lookback) = +1 AND Annual high filter passes AND (new-high/low filtering is disabled OR Highest(high, short high/low lookback) = Highest(high, long high/low lookback)) AND bar datetime ≥ January 1 of the current calendar year minus the backtest-year lookback AND the trade-direction setting permits long
Short: Highest(Trend direction, signal-delay lookback) = −1 AND Annual low filter passes AND (new-high/low filtering is disabled OR Lowest(low, short high/low lookback) = Lowest(low, long high/low lookback)) AND bar datetime ≥ January 1 of the current calendar year minus the backtest-year lookback AND the trade-direction setting permits short
Exit: long, Highest(Trend direction, signal-delay lookback) = −1
Exit: short, Lowest(Trend direction, signal-delay lookback) = +1
