Calculation: volume-weighted standard deviation(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars) = sqrt(max(sum(volume * source^2 over the rolling time window and minimum recent bar count) / sum(volume over the same bars) - RVWAP(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars)^2, 0))
Long: long entries enabled AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the date range, inclusive) AND confirmed chart bar AND close crosses above RVWAP(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars) - volume-weighted standard deviation(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars) * band multiplier
Short: short entries enabled AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the date range, inclusive) AND confirmed chart bar AND close crosses below RVWAP(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars) + volume-weighted standard deviation(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars) * band multiplier
Exit: Long, confirmed chart bar AND high crosses above RVWAP(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars)
Exit: Short, confirmed chart bar AND low crosses below RVWAP(source, rolling time window, minimum recent bar count, chart bars)
