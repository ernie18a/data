Calculation: Bullish displacement = abs(close - open) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick size) >= body-to-ATR threshold AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum tick size) >= body-to-range threshold AND close > open; bearish displacement uses close < open.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = latest confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) > prior confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, swing lookback).
Calculation: Bearish BOS = latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) < prior confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback).
Calculation: Swing range midpoint = (max(latest confirmed swing high, latest confirmed swing low) + min(latest confirmed swing high, latest confirmed swing low)) / 2, when both exist.
Calculation: Relative volatility = ATR(ATR lookback) / max(ATR(volatility regime lookback), minimum tick size).
Calculation: FVG probability = clamp(logistic(intercept + Σ(weight[k] × logit(clamp(factor[k], factor-score floor, factor-score ceiling)))), probability floor, maximum score), where logit(p) = ln(p / (1 - p)); factor 1 scores gap size divided by ATR in four tiers using three separate cutoffs and four separate scores; factor 2 scores alignment of the gap side with the fast/slow EMA trend; factor 3 scores alignment with close beyond the buffered HTF EMA; factor 4 scores volume on the prior bar divided by max(prior SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), one volume unit), in three tiers using two cutoffs and three scores; factor 5 scores bullish gap midpoint below the swing range midpoint or bearish gap midpoint above it, with a separate neutral score when no swing range exists; factor 6 scores alignment with bullish or bearish BOS; factor 7 scores inside versus outside a configured killzone; factor 8 scores normal, expanded, or compressed relative volatility. Each factor weight, score, cutoff, intercept, clipping bound, and output bound is a separate parameter. logistic(x) = 1 / (1 + exp(-x)).
Calculation: Shared signal context = relative volume, volume / max(SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), one volume unit), >= minimum relative volume AND (killzone requirement disabled OR current bar is inside a configured killzone) AND (time filter disabled OR current bar is outside the configured open, close, and enabled lunch blackout periods in the configured timezone) AND Relative volatility >= compression threshold.
Calculation: Bullish order-block retest = low is <= top and >= bottom of a stored bullish order block AND close > bottom; a bullish order block is the range of the nearest prior bearish candle within the order-block lookback preceding a bullish displacement, and the retest is within the order-block age limit.
Calculation: Bearish order-block retest = high is >= bottom and <= top of a stored bearish order block AND close < top; a bearish order block is the range of the nearest prior bullish candle within the order-block lookback preceding a bearish displacement, and the retest is within the order-block age limit.
Calculation: Cooldown = at least the cooldown parameter number of bars since the latest same-side displayed signal label; when A+ only is enabled, only trend signals with at least two raw triggers, expanded relative volatility, and at least two same-direction displacement bars among the current and preceding two bars produce labels that update cooldown.

Short: high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= minimum FVG-size ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) AND (displacement filter disabled OR bearish displacement[1]) AND the gap does not overlap a tracked FVG or IFVG.
