Calculation: Volume spike = current volume is strictly greater than the volume of each of the previous N comparison bars; the mode selects either the previous N chart bars or the previous N non-doji bars with the same direction (close > open or close < open). In same-direction mode, no spike qualifies until N such prior bars exist.
Calculation: CISD level = the open of the oldest bar in the contiguous run of bars with close <= open after a bullish reclaim, or close > open after a bearish reclaim, beginning at the nearest matching bar found by scanning backward from the bar before the reclaim within the scan-window parameter; if none is found, use the preceding bar's open.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, the reclaim-signal setting permits signals AND the configured bias permits bullish signals AND close > open AND low <= Volume Profile VAL(high, low, close, volume, prior completed T1 interval built from chart bars, row-count parameter, value-area-percentage parameter) AND Volume spike.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the reclaim-signal setting permits signals AND the configured bias permits bearish signals AND close < open AND high >= Volume Profile VAH(high, low, close, volume, prior completed T1 interval built from chart bars, row-count parameter, value-area-percentage parameter) AND Volume spike.
Long: CISD tracking is enabled AND a prior bullish reclaim has an unconfirmed tracker retained by the profile-history setting AND current chart-bar close > that tracker's CISD level.
Short: CISD tracking is enabled AND a prior bearish reclaim has an unconfirmed tracker retained by the profile-history setting AND current chart-bar close < that tracker's CISD level.
