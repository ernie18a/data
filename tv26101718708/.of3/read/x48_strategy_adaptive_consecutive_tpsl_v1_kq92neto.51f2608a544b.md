Calculation: UpStreak = count of consecutive bars ending at the current bar where close > close[1], or 0 if close <= close[1]
Calculation: DownStreak = count of consecutive bars ending at the current bar where close < close[1], or 0 if close >= close[1]
Calculation: ATRTrail(lookback, multiplier) = with p = prior ATRTrail value or 0 and d = multiplier × ATR(lookback), max(p, close − d) if close > p and close[1] > p; min(p, close + d) if close < p and close[1] < p; close − d if close > p; otherwise close + d
Long: UpStreak >= up-count parameter AND close > MA(close, long MA type, longer lookback) AND MA(close, fast MA type, shorter lookback) > MA(close, long MA type, longer lookback)
Short: DownStreak >= down-count parameter AND close < MA(close, long MA type, longer lookback) AND MA(close, fast MA type, shorter lookback) < MA(close, long MA type, longer lookback)
Exit: Long, (DownStreak >= down-stop-count parameter AND close > MA(close, long MA type, longer lookback) AND close[2] < MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback) AND close[1] < MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback) AND close < MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback)) OR MA(close, fast MA type, shorter lookback) crosses below MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback) OR ATRTrail(fast ATR lookback, fast ATR multiplier) crosses below ATRTrail(slow ATR lookback, slow ATR multiplier) where fast ATR lookback is shorter than slow ATR lookback OR close < highest(high, breakout lookback) × (1 − callback percentage / 100) OR (long take-profit percentage > 0 AND high >= entry price × (1 + long take-profit percentage / 100)) OR (stop-loss percentage > 0 AND low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100))
Exit: Short, (UpStreak >= up-count parameter AND close < MA(close, long MA type, longer lookback) AND close[2] > MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback) AND close[1] > MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback) AND close > MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback)) OR MA(close, fast MA type, shorter lookback) crosses above MA(close, middle MA type, intermediate lookback) OR ATRTrail(fast ATR lookback, fast ATR multiplier) crosses above ATRTrail(slow ATR lookback, slow ATR multiplier) where fast ATR lookback is shorter than slow ATR lookback OR close > lowest(low, breakout lookback) × (1 + callback percentage / 100) OR (short take-profit percentage > 0 AND low <= entry price × (1 − short take-profit percentage / 100)) OR (stop-loss percentage > 0 AND high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100))
