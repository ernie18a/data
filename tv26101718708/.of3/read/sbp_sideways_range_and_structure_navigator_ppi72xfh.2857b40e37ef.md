Calculation: Clamped ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum price tick).
Calculation: Candle measures = body = abs(close − open); range = max(high − low, minimum price tick); body fraction = body / range; body low = min(open, close); body high = max(open, close); safe body = max(body, minimum price tick).
Calculation: Directional evidence = let +DI, −DI, and ADX come from DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback); let balance = (+DI − (−DI)) / (+DI + (−DI)) when the denominator is positive, else zero; let fast = EMA(close, fast EMA lookback), slow = EMA(close, slow EMA lookback), and center = EMA(hlc3, slow EMA lookback); then balance × DI weight + (fast > slow ? EMA alignment weight : fast < slow ? −EMA alignment weight : zero) + clip(EMA(change(close), momentum lookback) / Clamped ATR / momentum normalization, ± evidence clamp limit) × shared component weight + clip((close − center) / Clamped ATR / price displacement normalization, ± evidence clamp limit) × shared component weight.
Calculation: Acceptance = EMA(EMA(hlc3, slow EMA lookback) + Directional evidence × Clamped ATR × acceptance offset, acceptance smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Profile levels = on each confirmed chart bar, use chart bars since the current calendar day began, capped at the maximum profile lookback; divide the lowest low to highest high into profile row count equal bins; allocate each bar's volume, treating missing volume as zero, to each overlapping bin as volume × overlap size / max(high − low, minimum price tick); if the profile span and total allocated volume are positive, take the first bin with maximum volume as the peak and its midpoint as control; independently expand from the peak toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until included volume reaches total volume × the core volume fraction or outer volume fraction, choosing the lower bin on ties, to obtain core and outer lower and upper edges; set channel center to the average of the core edges, channel half width to max(core width / channel divisor, minimum price tick × minimum tick multiple), premium to center + half width × channel offset, and discount to center − half width × channel offset; otherwise levels are undefined. Use the preceding chart bar's calculated levels on the current bar, undefined on the first bar after a calendar day boundary.
Calculation: Cloud measurements = cloud fast = EMA(hlc3, cloud fast lookback); cloud slow = EMA(hlc3, cloud slow lookback); progress = close − close[progress lookback]; normalized slope = (cloud fast − cloud fast[slope lookback]) / Clamped ATR; sideways range = highest(high, sideways range lookback) − lowest(low, sideways range lookback); path = sum(abs(change(close)), efficiency interval lookback); efficiency = abs(close − close[efficiency interval lookback]) / path when path > minimum price tick, else zero path efficiency fallback; separation = abs(cloud fast − cloud slow); bullish outside percent = max(body high − max(body low, Acceptance + Clamped ATR × outside offset), zero) / safe body × 100; bearish outside percent = max(min(body high, Acceptance − Clamped ATR × outside offset) − body low, zero) / safe body × 100.
Calculation: Cloud direction state = initialize neutral and update only on confirmed chart bars; sideways means sideways range ≤ Clamped ATR × sideways range limit AND efficiency ≤ efficiency limit AND separation ≤ Clamped ATR × EMA separation limit AND abs(cloud fast − cloud fast[sideways slope lookback]) ≤ Clamped ATR × sideways slope limit. Clear a bullish state when the latest routed signal is short OR a sharp bearish reversal occurs OR sideways; clear a bearish state symmetrically. A sharp bullish reversal means close > open, body ≥ Clamped ATR × reversal body size, body fraction ≥ reversal body fraction, close > cloud fast, and close > the previous chart bar's high; a sharp bearish reversal is the symmetric condition using close < open and close < the previous chart bar's low. From neutral, set bullish when the latest routed signal is long, not sideways, close > cloud fast, cloud fast ≥ cloud slow, (normalized slope ≥ minimum directional slope OR progress > zero), and (bullish outside percent ≥ primary outside threshold OR bullish outside percent on both the current and previous chart bars ≥ secondary outside threshold); otherwise set bearish under the symmetric conditions using the bearish outside percent. Retain the state otherwise; check the bullish start before the bearish start.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND close > open AND body fraction ≥ signal body fraction AND body ≥ Clamped ATR × signal body ATR fraction AND Directional evidence ≥ direction evidence threshold AND +DI from DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > −DI from DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) AND ADX from DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) ≥ minimum ADX AND close > Acceptance AND close > prior profile control AND ((prior outer lower is defined AND low < prior outer lower AND close > prior outer lower) OR (prior discount is defined AND low ≤ prior discount AND low ≤ prior profile control AND close > prior discount AND close > prior profile control) OR (body low > Acceptance AND Acceptance > its value on the previous chart bar) OR (prior core upper is defined AND close on the previous chart bar ≤ prior core upper AND close > prior core upper + Clamped ATR × continuation buffer AND close > prior profile control)) AND (no routed signal yet OR latest routed signal is short and at least the signal gap bars have elapsed since it).
