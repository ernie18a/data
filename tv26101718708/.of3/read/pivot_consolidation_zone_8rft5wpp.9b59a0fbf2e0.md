Calculation: Breakout margin = breakout ATR multiplier × ATR(zone ATR lookback)
Calculation: Active zone = the earliest-starting qualifying pair of a confirmed pivot high and confirmed pivot low, each using the same pivot lookback on both sides; pivot high > pivot low; each pivot has at least one retest whose bar range overlaps the range from its pivot price to the opposite extreme of its pivot bar; retain the high until a close > pivot high + breakout margin and the low until a close < pivot low − breakout margin; the current bar is no more than the maximum pair age after the earlier pivot; every close from that pivot through the previous bar is within [pivot low − breakout margin, pivot high + breakout margin]; and, when the ATR height cap is enabled, pivot high − pivot low ≤ maximum height ATR multiplier × ATR(zone ATR lookback). Zone top and bottom are the paired pivot prices. Extend the zone left from the earlier pivot by up to the zone left-edge lookback, stopping at the first close outside the same expanded range.
Calculation: Pre-breakout buy volume = sum over chart bars from the active zone's left edge through the bar before the breakout, omitting bars with high ≤ low, of volume × included range / (high − low) × (close − low) / (high − low), where included range is high − low when outer wicks are included, otherwise max(0, min(high, zone top) − max(low, zone bottom)).
Calculation: Pre-breakout sell volume = sum over chart bars from the active zone's left edge through the bar before the breakout, omitting bars with high ≤ low, of volume × included range / (high − low) × (high − close) / (high − low), where included range is high − low when outer wicks are included, otherwise max(0, min(high, zone top) − max(low, zone bottom)).
Long: breakout signals enabled AND an active zone AND close > zone top + breakout margin AND (volume confirmation not required OR pre-breakout buy volume ≥ pre-breakout sell volume)
Short: breakout signals enabled AND an active zone AND close < zone bottom − breakout margin AND (volume confirmation not required OR pre-breakout sell volume > pre-breakout buy volume)
