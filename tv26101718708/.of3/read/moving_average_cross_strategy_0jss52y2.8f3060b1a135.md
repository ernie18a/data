Long: EMA(open, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(open, longer lookback) OR Williams %R(close, lookback) crosses below oversold threshold
Short: EMA(open, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(open, longer lookback) OR Williams %R(close, lookback) crosses above overbought threshold
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + profit-target distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - profit-target distance
