Long: selected close (regular or Heikin-Ashi close on the chart timeframe) > selected close[1] AND selected close[1] > selected close[2] AND selected close[2] < selected close[3] AND datetime is within the configured date range AND time is within the configured entry session AND the weekday is enabled and is Monday through Friday AND long entries are enabled
Short: selected close (regular or Heikin-Ashi close on the chart timeframe) < selected close[1] AND selected close[1] < selected close[2] AND selected close[2] > selected close[3] AND datetime is within the configured date range AND time is within the configured entry session AND the weekday is enabled and is Monday through Friday AND short entries are enabled
Exit: long, low <= entry price - close * stop-loss parameter OR high >= entry price + close * take-profit parameter OR time is outside the configured exit session
Exit: short, high >= entry price + close * stop-loss parameter OR low <= entry price - close * take-profit parameter OR time is outside the configured exit session
