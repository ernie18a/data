Calculation: Pseudo-random value(range, seed) = (random multiplier × prior value modulo (modulus + seed)) modulo range, using modulus when seed is missing and the initial value when prior value is missing
Calculation: Entry stop = max(0, long ? (ATR mode ? low[maximum trade length] − ATR(ATR lookback)[maximum trade length] × entry ATR multiple : percentage mode ? low[maximum trade length] × (1 − entry stop percentage/100) : low[maximum trade length] − fixed entry stop amount) : (ATR mode ? high[maximum trade length] + ATR(ATR lookback)[maximum trade length] × entry ATR multiple : percentage mode ? high[maximum trade length] × (1 + entry stop percentage/100) : high[maximum trade length] + fixed entry stop amount))
Calculation: Entry fee = 0 when fee mode is none, entry price × entry fee percentage/100 when fee mode is percentage, or fixed entry fee when fee mode is fixed value
Calculation: Entry risk percentage = (abs(entry price − entry stop) + entry fee/100) / entry price
Calculation: In-trade stop candidate = ATR mode ? (long ? SMA(min(open, close), price-basis lookback)[1] − ATR(ATR lookback) × in-trade ATR multiple : SMA(max(open, close), price-basis lookback)[1] + ATR(ATR lookback) × in-trade ATR multiple) : X-trailing mode ? (long ? highest high since entry × (1 − entry risk percentage × trailing X multiple) : lowest low since entry × (1 + entry risk percentage × trailing X multiple)) : (long ? highest high since entry × (1 − trailing percentage/100) : lowest low since entry × (1 + trailing percentage/100))
Calculation: Active stop = entry stop on the entry bar; thereafter max(0, long ? max(previous active stop, in-trade stop candidate) : min(previous active stop, in-trade stop candidate))
Long: (all-long entry mode = enabled OR (moving-average entry mode = enabled AND rising(nz(SMA(close, entry MA lookback))[maximum trade length], entry MA change lookback)) OR (random entry mode = enabled AND round(pseudo-random value(entry random frequency, seed)) = first category) OR (ADX entry mode = enabled AND DI+(DI lookback)[maximum trade length] crosses above DI−(DI lookback)[maximum trade length] AND SMA(DX(DI+(DI lookback), DI−(DI lookback)), ADX smoothing)[maximum trade length] > ADX threshold)) AND (date filter = disabled OR datetime[maximum trade length] is within date range) AND (bar-direction filter = disabled OR close[maximum trade length] > open[maximum trade length]) AND (rising-volume filter = disabled OR rising(volume[maximum trade length], volume rise lookback)) AND (MA filter = disabled OR rising(nz(SMA(close, filter MA lookback))[maximum trade length], filter MA change lookback)) AND (external-indicator filter = disabled OR nz(external indicator signal[maximum trade length]) = 1) AND (random filter = disabled OR round(pseudo-random value(filter random frequency, seed)) = first category) AND (shorts-only filter = disabled)
Short: (all-short entry mode = enabled OR (moving-average entry mode = enabled AND falling(nz(SMA(close, entry MA lookback))[maximum trade length], entry MA change lookback)) OR (random entry mode = enabled AND round(pseudo-random value(entry random frequency, seed)) = second category) OR (ADX entry mode = enabled AND DI−(DI lookback)[maximum trade length] crosses above DI+(DI lookback)[maximum trade length] AND SMA(DX(DI+(DI lookback), DI−(DI lookback)), ADX smoothing)[maximum trade length] > ADX threshold)) AND (date filter = disabled OR datetime[maximum trade length] is within date range) AND (bar-direction filter = disabled OR close[maximum trade length] < open[maximum trade length]) AND (rising-volume filter = disabled OR rising(volume[maximum trade length], volume rise lookback)) AND (MA filter = disabled OR falling(nz(SMA(close, filter MA lookback))[maximum trade length], filter MA change lookback)) AND (external-indicator filter = disabled OR nz(external indicator signal[maximum trade length]) = 0) AND (random filter = disabled OR round(pseudo-random value(filter random frequency, seed)) = first category) AND (longs-only filter = disabled)
Exit: long, after the entry bar and close < previous active stop
Exit: short, after the entry bar and close > previous active stop
