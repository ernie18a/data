Calculation: Aggregate volume = sum of current-bar volume across the volume sources on the chart timeframe
Long: The configured entry direction permits long AND ((reverse trades is disabled AND close crosses above EMA(close, fast price EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, slow price EMA lookback)) OR (reverse trades is enabled AND close crosses below EMA(close, fast price EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, slow price EMA lookback)))
Short: The configured entry direction permits short AND ((reverse trades is disabled AND close crosses below EMA(close, fast price EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, slow price EMA lookback)) OR (reverse trades is enabled AND close crosses above EMA(close, fast price EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, slow price EMA lookback)))
Exit: long, aggregate volume > volume multiplier × (SMA(aggregate volume, fast volume SMA lookback) if fast volume exit is selected, otherwise SMA(aggregate volume, slow volume SMA lookback)) OR close crosses below EMA(close, slow price EMA lookback)
Exit: short, aggregate volume > volume multiplier × (SMA(aggregate volume, fast volume SMA lookback) if fast volume exit is selected, otherwise SMA(aggregate volume, slow volume SMA lookback)) OR close crosses above EMA(close, slow price EMA lookback)
