Calculation: support/resistance zones = clusters of same-type confirmed pivot highs/lows using equal left/right pivot lookback; cluster pivots within ATR(ATR lookback) × merge-distance parameter, score by volume and reaction plus a base bonus, decay scores by age, and discard zones below the viability-score threshold or beyond the maximum-age threshold
Calculation: zone proximity factor = max(minimum proximity factor, 1 − min(1, abs(zone midpoint − close) / (ATR(ATR lookback) × proximity scale)))
Calculation: bull zone weight = sum(zone score × zone proximity factor) for each unbroken support zone below close whose score meets the minimum display-score threshold
Calculation: bear zone weight = sum(zone score × zone proximity factor) for each unbroken resistance zone above close whose score meets the minimum display-score threshold
Long: bull zone weight > bear zone weight × trend-imbalance parameter
Short: bear zone weight > bull zone weight × trend-imbalance parameter
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, close > the top of an unbroken resistance zone and close[1] <= that zone top, with (volume-break filter disabled OR (volume >= SMA(volume[1], volume lookback) × volume-floor multiplier AND (volume cap disabled OR volume <= SMA(volume[1], volume lookback) × volume-cap multiplier))) and (momentum filter disabled OR high − low >= ATR(ATR lookback) × momentum multiplier)
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, close < the bottom of an unbroken support zone and close[1] >= that zone bottom, with (volume-break filter disabled OR (volume >= SMA(volume[1], volume lookback) × volume-floor multiplier AND (volume cap disabled OR volume <= SMA(volume[1], volume lookback) × volume-cap multiplier))) and (momentum filter disabled OR high − low >= ATR(ATR lookback) × momentum multiplier)
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, the most recent break was upward and occurred between the retest minimum and maximum bars ago, and low <= the broken resistance-zone top, close > that top, and close − low >= ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-retest-reaction; if the mitigation filter is enabled, the zone has not already been retested
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, the most recent break was downward and occurred between the retest minimum and maximum bars ago, and high >= the broken support-zone bottom, close < that bottom, and high − close >= ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-retest-reaction; if the mitigation filter is enabled, the zone has not already been retested
Calculation: initial stop = for long, zone-aware stops enabled ? max(entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier, broken support-zone bottom − ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-buffer parameter) : entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier; for short, zone-aware stops enabled ? min(entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier, broken resistance-zone top + ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-buffer parameter) : entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier
Calculation: effective stop = initial stop until TP1 first touches; if break-even trailing is enabled, use entry price starting on the following bar
Calculation: risk distance = abs(entry price − initial stop)
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry-bar hold, low <= effective stop OR (low > effective stop AND high first touches an unreached target at entry price + risk distance × its TP1, TP2, or TP3 multiplier)
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry-bar hold, high >= effective stop OR (high < effective stop AND low first touches an unreached target at entry price − risk distance × its TP1, TP2, or TP3 multiplier)
