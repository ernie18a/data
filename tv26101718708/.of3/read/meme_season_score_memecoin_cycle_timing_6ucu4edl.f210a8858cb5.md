Calculation: Meme-season score = EMA of the weighted average of six chart-bar factors, using one ROC lookback for each ROC and one normalization lookback for each percentile rank: the percentile rank of ROC of meme dominance (meme-sector market cap divided by total market cap, times 100); the percentile rank of ROC of meme-sector market cap; 100 minus the percentile rank of ROC of BTC dominance; the percentile rank of ROC of other-altcoin dominance; 100 minus the percentile rank of ROC of stablecoin market cap; and the percentile rank of ROC of ROC of meme share (meme-sector market cap divided by total market cap). Normalize the six separate factor weights by their sum, or use 50 when their sum is zero; use a separate score-smoothing lookback for the EMA. Source values are closes sampled from T1 with lookahead off: last completed T1 bars historically and the still-forming T1 bar in realtime. All calculations run on chart bars using those sampled values.
Long: Meme-season score crosses above the season-activation threshold OR crosses below the winter threshold
