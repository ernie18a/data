Calculation: CRT-A range = high and low of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: CRT-B range = high and low of the last completed T2 bar at the configured anchor, sampled on chart bars; aggregate chart bars when the configured anchor is custom
Calculation: CRT-C range = high and low accumulated from chart bars during the configured CRT session, available after the session closes
Calculation: CRT-D range = high and low of the last completed T3 bar, accumulated from chart bars and sampled on chart bars
Calculation: ORB range = high and low accumulated from chart bars during the configured opening interval, available after the interval closes
Calculation: Bearish-run open = open of the first bearish chart bar in the current consecutive bearish run
Calculation: Bullish-run open = open of the first bullish chart bar in the current consecutive bullish run
Calculation: Bullish regime = in ADX mode, ADX at or above its threshold and EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) on the last completed T4 bar, sampled on chart bars; in ribbon mode, EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) on chart bars; otherwise false
Calculation: Bearish regime = in ADX mode, ADX at or above its threshold and EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) on the last completed T4 bar, sampled on chart bars; in ribbon mode, EMA(close, shorter lookback) <= EMA(close, longer lookback) on chart bars; otherwise false
Calculation: Entry gate = confirmed chart bar on a weekday within the configured entry window and outside the configured news window; no open position or trade closing on this bar; daily trade and loss counts below their configured limits; chart-bar range no greater than the spike multiplier times ATR(previous chart bar); and, when enabled, ATR(chart bar) no greater than the volatility multiplier times SMA(ATR, configured averaging lookback)
Calculation: CRT midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2
Calculation: CRT range size = range high - range low
Calculation: CRT stop distance = max(abs(entry price - sweep extreme) + sweep buffer, minimum stop distance)
Calculation: ORB stop distance = max(abs(entry price - ORB midpoint), minimum stop distance)
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price - position side × selected stop distance
Calculation: CRT midpoint R = position side × (CRT midpoint - entry price) / CRT stop distance
Calculation: CRT opposite-edge R = position side × (opposite range edge - entry price) / CRT stop distance
Calculation: CRT TP1 = opposite range edge when CRT midpoint R is below the configured minimum TP1-R and the configured action uses the range edge; otherwise CRT midpoint
Calculation: CRT TP2 = opposite range edge plus position side × shifted extension × CRT range size when TP1 uses the range edge; otherwise opposite range edge; shifted extension is the configured TP3 extension when positive, otherwise the fallback extension
Calculation: CRT TP3 = opposite range edge plus position side × twice the shifted extension × CRT range size when TP1 uses the range edge; otherwise opposite range edge plus position side × configured TP3 extension × CRT range size
Calculation: ORB TP1, TP2, TP3 = entry price + position side × ORB stop distance × each target's separate configured R multiple
Calculation: Current stop = initial stop; after TP1, move to entry when breakeven-after-TP1 is enabled; after TP2, move to TP1 when stop-to-TP1 is enabled, otherwise move to entry when breakeven movement is enabled; with an ATR runner after TP2, ratchet long stop to max(current stop, close - trail multiple × ATR(chart, configured lookback)) and short stop to min(current stop, close + trail multiple × ATR(chart, configured lookback))
Long: Entry gate AND an enabled CRT-A, CRT-B, CRT-C, or CRT-D range is selected by regime permission and priority CRT-A > CRT-B > CRT-C > CRT-D > ORB AND its low has been swept with the deepest sweep no farther below the range low than the configured sweep fraction of range size AND its configured confirmation holds: close > max(range low, bearish-run open captured at the latest lower sweep) AND close > open; OR close > range low AND close > open; OR, after the reference range changes and the preceding chart close was inside it following a low sweep, current close is above range low and below midpoint with no lower sweep than the saved wick AND CRT midpoint R > 0 AND CRT opposite-edge R >= configured minimum AND, if the minimum TP1-R action is to skip, CRT midpoint R >= configured minimum TP1-R AND CRT stop distance <= maximum stop distance AND, if the daily midpoint filter is enabled, close <= midpoint of CRT-A range AND regime is not bearish
Short: Entry gate AND an enabled CRT-A, CRT-B, CRT-C, or CRT-D range is selected by regime permission and priority CRT-A > CRT-B > CRT-C > CRT-D > ORB AND its high has been swept with the highest sweep no farther above the range high than the configured sweep fraction of range size AND its configured confirmation holds: close < min(range high, bullish-run open captured at the latest higher sweep) AND close < open; OR close < range high AND close < open; OR, after the reference range changes and the preceding chart close was inside it following a high sweep, current close is below range high and above midpoint with no higher sweep than the saved wick AND CRT midpoint R > 0 AND CRT opposite-edge R >= configured minimum AND, if the minimum TP1-R action is to skip, CRT midpoint R >= configured minimum TP1-R AND CRT stop distance <= maximum stop distance AND, if the daily midpoint filter is enabled, close >= midpoint of CRT-A range AND regime is not bullish
Long: Entry gate AND the first eligible chart close above ORB range high AND no regime-permitted CRT signal is selected ahead of ORB AND (regime filter disabled OR bullish regime) AND ORB stop distance <= maximum stop distance AND, if the daily midpoint filter is enabled, close <= midpoint of CRT-A range
Short: Entry gate AND the first eligible chart close below ORB range low AND no regime-permitted CRT signal is selected ahead of ORB AND (regime filter disabled OR bearish regime) AND ORB stop distance <= maximum stop distance AND, if the daily midpoint filter is enabled, close >= midpoint of CRT-A range
Exit: long, after the entry chart bar, low <= current stop
Exit: short, after the entry chart bar, high >= current stop
Exit: long, after the entry chart bar, current stop is not hit AND fixed-target runner is enabled AND high >= the selected model's TP3
Exit: short, after the entry chart bar, current stop is not hit AND fixed-target runner is enabled AND low <= the selected model's TP3
Exit: long, after the entry chart bar, current stop and fixed TP3 are not hit AND configured flat time or trading-day change occurs; exit at close
Exit: short, after the entry chart bar, current stop and fixed TP3 are not hit AND configured flat time or trading-day change occurs; exit at close
Exit: long, after the entry chart bar, current stop and fixed TP3 are not hit AND flat time or trading-day change has not occurred AND TP1 has not previously been reached AND high >= the selected model's TP1
Exit: short, after the entry chart bar, current stop and fixed TP3 are not hit AND flat time or trading-day change has not occurred AND TP1 has not previously been reached AND low <= the selected model's TP1
Exit: long, after the entry chart bar, current stop and fixed TP3 are not hit AND flat time or trading-day change has not occurred AND TP1 has been reached but TP2 has not AND high >= the selected model's TP2
Exit: short, after the entry chart bar, current stop and fixed TP3 are not hit AND flat time or trading-day change has not occurred AND TP1 has been reached but TP2 has not AND low <= the selected model's TP2
