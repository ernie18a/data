Calculation: Full body = abs(close - open) >= body fraction * (high - low)
Calculation: Bullish candle = (Full body AND close > open) OR (lower wick > small-wick fraction * (high - low) AND upper wick < wick-cap fraction * (high - low)) OR (lower wick > large-wick fraction * (high - low))
Calculation: Bearish candle = (Full body AND close < open) OR (upper wick > small-wick fraction * (high - low) AND lower wick < wick-cap fraction * (high - low)) OR (upper wick > large-wick fraction * (high - low))
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > volume[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)
Calculation: Strong volume spike = volume > strong-volume multiplier * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)
Calculation: Lower-wick ATR excursion = open - ATR excursion multiplier * ATR(ATR excursion lookback) > low
Calculation: SFP(left lookback, right lookback) = Bullish candidate when low < the most recent confirmed PivotLow(low, left lookback, right lookback), open and close are above that pivot low, and low < lowest(low, right lookback)[1]; bearish candidate when high > the most recent confirmed PivotHigh(high, left lookback, right lookback), open and close are below that pivot high, and high > highest(high, right lookback)[1]. Candidate direction carries forward, with a current bullish candidate taking precedence over a current bearish candidate. The bullish invalidation flag carries forward as 1 when close < the latest bullish-candidate low or that low > lowest(close, close-invalidation lookback)[1]; it resets to 0 on a bullish candidate when neither condition holds, and otherwise carries its prior value. The bearish invalidation flag is symmetric using close > the latest bearish-candidate high or that high < highest(close, close-invalidation lookback)[1]. A bullish reversal is a current bullish candidate with bullish candidate direction and an initialized bullish invalidation flag other than 1; a bearish reversal is symmetric. A bullish pre-trigger requires no current bearish candidate, bars since the latest bullish candidate <= SFP confirmation window, bullish candidate direction, bullish invalidation flag other than 1, close > the latest bullish-candidate high, and close >= (1 + SFP displacement fraction) * high[1]. A bearish pre-trigger is symmetric, requiring close < the latest bearish-candidate low and close <= (1 - SFP displacement fraction) * low[1]. Signal state carries forward the latest bullish pre-trigger as bullish, bearish pre-trigger as bearish, or either current candidate as neutral, with bullish pre-trigger taking precedence; a bullish trigger is a bullish pre-trigger when prior signal state was not bullish, and a bearish trigger is a bearish pre-trigger when prior signal state was not bearish.
Calculation: Turtle breakout direction state = carry forward the latest breakout direction from close crossing above the prior high extreme or below the prior low extreme, with close rising for bullish breakouts and falling for bearish breakouts; current-bar priority is longer-lookback bullish, longer-lookback bearish, shorter-lookback bullish, shorter-lookback bearish.
Calculation: Turtle long-marker state = carry forward the latest of a bullish shorter-lookback structural breakout or bearish longer-lookback structural breakout, with the bullish event taking precedence when both occur on one bar.
Calculation: Turtle short-marker state = carry forward the latest of a bearish shorter-lookback structural breakout or bullish longer-lookback structural breakout, with the bearish event taking precedence when both occur on one bar.
Calculation: Long VWATR = SMA(TrueRange * (volume / SMA(volume, long ATR lookback) when that average is positive, otherwise the zero-average volume weight), long ATR lookback)
Calculation: Short VWATR = SMA(TrueRange * (volume / SMA(volume, short ATR lookback) when that average is positive, otherwise the zero-average volume weight), short ATR lookback)
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close - close[short ATR lookback]) / sum(abs(close - close[1]), short ATR lookback) when the denominator is positive, otherwise the zero-denominator efficiency fallback
Calculation: Regime scale = regime base + regime slope * Efficiency ratio
Calculation: Long trailing stop = selected mode: LinReg(close, LinReg lookback, offset 1) - long stop multiplier * ATR(long ATR lookback); or a ratcheted stop from LinReg(close, LinReg lookback, offset 1) - long stop multiplier * Regime scale * Long VWATR, resetting to that raw stop when the prior stop is missing or close[1] <= prior stop[1], otherwise taking the maximum of the raw and prior stops; or a ratcheted high-based stop, resetting to high - reset-bar multiplier * Long VWATR when the prior stop is missing or close[1] <= prior stop[1], otherwise taking the maximum of the prior stop and high - long stop multiplier * Long VWATR; or LinReg(close, LinReg lookback, offset 1) - long stop multiplier * Long VWATR.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = selected mode: LinReg(close, LinReg lookback, offset 1) + short stop multiplier * ATR(short ATR lookback); or a ratcheted stop from LinReg(close, LinReg lookback, offset 1) + short stop multiplier * Regime scale * Short VWATR, resetting to that raw stop when the prior stop is missing or close[1] >= prior stop[1], otherwise taking the minimum of the raw and prior stops; or a ratcheted low-based stop, resetting to low + reset-bar multiplier * Short VWATR when the prior stop is missing or close[1] >= prior stop[1], otherwise taking the minimum of the prior stop and low + short stop multiplier * Short VWATR; or LinReg(close, LinReg lookback, offset 1) + short stop multiplier * Short VWATR.
Calculation: Open P&L fraction = open P&L divided by the absolute product of the first open-trade entry price and total open position size
