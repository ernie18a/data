Calculation: momentum adjustment = ((close − close[score lookback]) / (close[score lookback] when nonzero, otherwise a small positive fallback)) × (1 + RMA(max(close − close[1], 0), score lookback) / RMA(max(close[1] − close, 0), score lookback)); use 1 for the RMA ratio when its denominator is zero
Calculation: pattern score = average of the decayed body ratios over the score lookback × (1 − normalized Shannon entropy of the three-dimensional histogram of decayed body, weighted upper-wick, and weighted lower-wick ratios) × (momentum weight + momentum adjustment × momentum scale) × trend multiplier; body ratio = (close − open) / (high − low), upper-wick ratio = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) × upper-wick weight, and lower-wick ratio = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) × lower-wick weight; on each bar, shift prior ratios and multiply them by the decay factor before appending the current ratios; bin each ratio using floor((ratio − lower ratio bound) / (ratio range width / bin count × (1 + ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback)))), clamped to the available bins; normalize histogram entropy by log(bin count cubed); trend multiplier is the trend boost when RMA(max(high − high[1], 0), score lookback) / ATR(ATR lookback) differs from RMA(max(low[1] − low, 0), score lookback) / ATR(ATR lookback), and the neutral multiplier otherwise
Calculation: trailing stop = after price moves favorably from entry by ATR(ATR lookback at entry) × trailing activation multiplier × point value × minimum tick, follow the highest high since activation for a long or lowest low since activation for a short at a distance of entry price × trail-offset percentage × minimum tick / 100
Long: at least score lookback bars precede the current bar AND pattern score > long threshold base / threshold adjustment factor AND abs(pattern score) > minimum score strength AND stdev(close, volatility lookback) / SMA(close, volatility lookback) × percent scale < volatility threshold limit AND high − low > max(ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum price-move multiplier, minimum tick × minimum-tick multiplier) AND abs(momentum adjustment) > minimum momentum strength AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × minimum volume multiplier
Short: at least score lookback bars precede the current bar AND pattern score < short threshold base / threshold adjustment factor AND abs(pattern score) > minimum score strength AND stdev(close, volatility lookback) / SMA(close, volatility lookback) × percent scale < volatility threshold limit AND high − low > max(ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum price-move multiplier, minimum tick × minimum-tick multiplier) AND abs(momentum adjustment) > minimum momentum strength AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × minimum volume multiplier
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100) − ATR(ATR lookback at entry) × stop ATR multiplier OR low <= active trailing stop OR bars held > maximum hold bars
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) + ATR(ATR lookback at entry) × stop ATR multiplier OR high >= active trailing stop OR bars held > maximum hold bars
