Calculation: Rolling Z(x, lookback) = (x − SMA(x, lookback)) / standard deviation(x, lookback).
Calculation: OU slope b = Cov(price source[i], price source[i−1]) / Var(price source[i]) over the OU lookback, for i from one through the lookback; OU intercept a = mean(price source[i−1]) − b × mean(price source[i]).
Calculation: OU mean = a / (1 − b); OU equilibrium sigma = standard deviation of price source[i−1] − (a + b × price source[i]), divided by sqrt(max(OU lookback − degrees-of-freedom adjustment, minimum denominator)) / sqrt(1 − b²); OU half-life = ln(2) / −ln(b) when zero < b < one; OU Z = (price source − OU mean) / OU equilibrium sigma.
Calculation: Session gap = session guards enabled AND current bar interval > gap multiplier × rolling median bar interval; bars since gap reset to zero at a session gap and otherwise increment. A gap is in the OU window if any gap occurred within the OU lookback.
Calculation: Statistical-core direction = zero unless enabled, OU half-life is defined and within its maximum, Hurst(price source, OU lookback) is below its ceiling, enabled ADF t-statistic(price source, OU lookback) is below its cutoff, enabled VarianceRatio(price source, OU lookback, middle q) is below its ceiling, and no enabled session gap invalidates the OU window; then it is long when OU Z < −entry Z threshold, short when OU Z > entry Z threshold, and otherwise zero.
Calculation: Statistical-core strength = zero when statistical-core direction is zero or OU Z is undefined; otherwise min(abs(OU Z) / separate strength Z scale, strength cap).
Calculation: Annualized realized volatility = standard deviation of log returns of the price source over the realized-volatility lookback × sqrt(trading days per year × session minutes per day / chart-bar minutes) × percent scale; volatility risk premium = volatility-index close − annualized realized volatility.
Calculation: Volatility regime = classify volatility-index close below the low cutoff, between the low and high cutoffs, or above the high cutoff; within each band classify ADX as trending when above its cutoff, and in the high band classify as spike when absolute volatility-index rate of change over its lookback exceeds its cutoff. Regime multiplier uses a separate parameter for each resulting state and a default parameter when the regime is unavailable.
Calculation: Basis Kalman beta resets to its initial slope and covariance at a session gap; on each chart bar with nonzero spot and futures log returns, update covariance with process variance, compute gain as covariance × spot return / (spot return² × covariance + observation variance), update beta by gain × (futures return − beta × spot return), then update covariance by (one − gain × spot return) × covariance. Basis residual = futures close − spot close × exp(risk-free rate × time to expiry) − Kalman beta × (spot close − spot close[1]); basis Z = Rolling Z(basis residual, basis lookback).
Calculation: Basis direction = zero unless enabled and basis Z is defined; then short when basis Z > basis threshold, long when basis Z < −basis threshold, otherwise zero. Basis strength = min(abs(basis Z) / basis threshold, strength cap) when enabled and defined, otherwise zero.
Calculation: ATM implied volatility = (ATM call close + ATM put close) / (straddle scale × close × sqrt(days to expiry / year scale)) when enabled and defined. Options skew Z = Rolling Z(OTM put close / OTM call close, skew lookback). Skew direction is long when skew Z > skew threshold, short when skew Z < −skew threshold, and zero otherwise; skew strength = min(abs(skew Z) / skew threshold, strength cap), or zero when disabled or undefined.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP signal uses Rolling Z(close − VWAP(hlc3), VWAP-deviation lookback): short above its positive threshold, long below its negative threshold, otherwise zero; its strength is min(abs(Z) / threshold, strength cap). Session-anchored VWAP and its volume-weighted deviation standard deviation accumulate hlc3 and nonnegative volume from each session gap; its Z is (close − anchored VWAP) / anchored deviation standard deviation, with the same direction rule and a separate threshold and strength.
Calculation: Microstructure direction and strength combine the rolling and anchored VWAP signals: when anchored VWAP is available, use their common direction and weighted strengths if directions agree; use the sole nonzero direction and its strength multiplied by its corresponding weight if only one is nonzero; use zero if both are nonzero and disagree. Without anchored VWAP, use the rolling VWAP signal and strength. Multiply strength by the volume-spike factor when volume exceeds its moving average times the spike threshold; by the order-flow factor when direction is long and order-flow imbalance is negative or direction is short and imbalance is positive; and by the VPIN penalty when VPIN Z exceeds its cutoff. Order-flow imbalance = rolling sum of estimated buy volume minus sell volume / rolling volume sum. Estimate buy fraction with the logistic of price change divided by its rolling standard deviation times a coefficient; VPIN = rolling sum of absolute buy-sell volume imbalance / rolling volume sum, and VPIN Z is its rolling Z-score.
Calculation: GEX at each configured strike offset = (call open interest − put open interest) × Black-Scholes gamma(close, strike, time to expiry, volatility) × volume weight; volume weight is one when volume weighting is disabled or unavailable, otherwise clamp current call-plus-put volume / its moving average to configured bounds. Volatility is ATM implied volatility when defined, else volatility-index close, else a fallback parameter. Choose the strike with greatest absolute GEX, breaking ties by preference for ATM then nearer offsets. GEX direction is short for a call-heavy wall above close, long for a put-heavy wall below close, and zero otherwise; strength = min(abs(close − wall) / ATR(ATR lookback), strength cap), or zero when GEX is disabled or unavailable.
Calculation: PCR direction = long when enabled PCR > its high threshold and PCR change > zero; short when PCR < its low threshold and PCR change > zero; otherwise zero. PCR strength is the applicable threshold excess divided by a strength scale, capped at its maximum. L6 direction and strength use PCR when PCR is enabled, otherwise GEX; L6 weight uses its separate PCR weight when PCR is enabled, otherwise its GEX weight.
Calculation: Constituent residual Z = Rolling Z(stock log return − beta × index log return, residual lookback), where beta = covariance(stock returns, index returns) / variance(index returns) over that lookback and index return is the price-source log return. Count residual Z scores above the positive residual threshold and below its negative. Residual-dispersion direction is long when the positive count reaches the agreement threshold and the negative count does not, short when the negative count reaches it and the positive count does not, otherwise zero. Strength = min((max count − agreement threshold + one) × strength increment + base strength, strength cap) when enabled and direction is nonzero, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Correlation multiplier uses the average rolling correlation of price-source returns with configured constituent returns when its rolling Z-score is defined: use the high-correlation multiplier above the high cutoff, the low-correlation multiplier below the low cutoff, otherwise one; use one when disabled or undefined.
Calculation: Cross-asset stress = sum, for available currency-cross, dollar/risk-off, and commodity feeds, of abs(log return) / rolling standard deviation(log return, stress lookback). When enabled, chart-bar duration is within its configured bounds, and at least the minimum number of feeds are available, use the high-stress multiplier above the stress cutoff, the low-stress multiplier below the low-stress cutoff, otherwise one; otherwise use one.
Calculation: Expiry multiplier = one except on the configured expiry weekday after the configured hour when expiry mode is enabled and raw-score direction and pin direction are nonzero; pin direction points from close toward the nearest strike-step increment, and the multiplier is the aligned or opposed expiry parameter according to whether those directions agree.
Calculation: Raw composite = the sum of each enabled layer direction × strength × its separate weight for statistical core, basis, skew, microstructure, L6, and residual dispersion. Composite multiplier = clamp(volatility-regime multiplier × correlation multiplier × VRP multiplier × cross-asset multiplier × expiry multiplier, minimum multiplier, maximum multiplier); VRP multiplier uses its high or low parameter above or below its respective threshold, otherwise one. Composite = raw composite × composite multiplier; dominant direction is its sign.
Calculation: Candidate horizon in weighted-composite mode is the longest qualifying horizon: session when abs(composite) reaches its session threshold, OU half-life is undefined or exceeds its session minimum, and enabled long-q variance ratio is below its ceiling; else swing when the corresponding swing conditions pass; else scalp when abs(composite) reaches its scalp threshold and enabled short-q variance ratio is below its ceiling; else zero. In confluence-count mode, count nonzero layer directions matching dominant direction among statistical core, basis, skew, microstructure, L6, and residual dispersion; if count reaches minimum confluence, assign session at or above the high count cutoff, swing at or above the middle count cutoff, otherwise scalp; else zero. In strict mode, assign session only when dominant direction is nonzero and each enabled statistical-core, basis, skew, and microstructure direction equals dominant direction or zero; otherwise zero.
Calculation: TCA passes when disabled or when abs(close − OU mean) / close × basis-point scale exceeds round-trip cost × the separate candidate-horizon cost multiplier × required-edge multiple; undefined OU mean fails when TCA is enabled.
Calculation: Final signal = sign(composite) when candidate horizon is nonzero, TCA passes, time filters pass, and the enabled high-trend block does not classify the regime as spike; otherwise zero. Time filters pass when enabled open and close exclusion intervals and the configured lunch-hour exclusion do not apply, and the session-start exclusion does not apply. Session-start exclusion uses the configured bar skip or the rounded warmup-minutes / chart-bar-minutes, with a minimum of one bar, when automatic scaling is enabled.
