Calculation: SRSI sum(source) = SMA(StochRSI(source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback), K smoothing) + SMA(SMA(StochRSI(source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback), K smoothing), D smoothing); RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing, and D smoothing are separate parameters.
Calculation: SRSI channel = mean(SRSI sum(close, T1), SRSI sum(close, T2), SRSI sum(close, T3), SRSI sum(close, T4)) / 2; T1, T2, T3, and T4 are separate timeframe parameters ordered shortest to longest. Compute each SRSI sum on its timeframe bars and use its current, unoffset value on chart bars; the requested bar can still be forming in realtime.
Calculation: Confirmed channel minimum and maximum = A low watch starts when SRSI channel <= low threshold while neither watch is active; otherwise, a high watch starts when SRSI channel >= high threshold while neither is active. A low watch triggers when channel >= midpoint; a high watch triggers when channel <= midpoint; a trigger clears its watch. After a high-watch trigger, track the channel minimum; a new low resets the non-new-low count, otherwise increment it, including on the trigger bar. Output a one-bar confirmed minimum when the count reaches the confirmation-bars parameter, then stop tracking. After a low-watch trigger, apply the symmetric process to track and confirm the channel maximum.
Calculation: Recent confirmed channel minimum = a confirmed channel minimum occurred within the recent-confirmation lookback on chart bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Recent confirmed channel maximum = a confirmed channel maximum occurred within the recent-confirmation lookback on chart bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Long EMA retest signal = On chart bars, use fast, mid, and slow EMAs of close with separate lookbacks ordered fast < mid < slow. At each fast EMA cross above either mid or slow EMA, replace any existing setup and initialize the impulse low and peak to the fast EMA. While the setup rises, update the peak when the fast EMA rises. Lock the peak after the turn-count parameter consecutive declines. Then wait until peak minus fast EMA >= (peak minus impulse low) * pullback-depth parameter. After that, on the first touch where fast EMA >= peak - (peak - impulse low) * (1 - early-retest parameter), output a signal only if mid EMA > slow EMA; consume the setup on that touch even if the trend condition fails.
Calculation: Short EMA retest signal = On chart bars, use fast, mid, and slow EMAs of close with separate lookbacks ordered fast < mid < slow. At each fast EMA cross below either mid or slow EMA, replace any existing setup and initialize the impulse high and bottom to the fast EMA. While the setup falls, update the bottom when the fast EMA falls. Lock the bottom after the turn-count parameter consecutive rises. Then wait until fast EMA minus bottom >= (impulse high minus bottom) * pullback-depth parameter. After that, on the first touch where fast EMA <= bottom + (impulse high - bottom) * (1 - early-retest parameter), output a signal only if mid EMA < slow EMA; consume the setup on that touch even if the trend condition fails.
Long: Long EMA retest signal = true AND Recent confirmed channel minimum = false
