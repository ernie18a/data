Calculation: EMA source = previous chart-bar close on realtime bars; current chart-bar close otherwise
Calculation: RSI input = T1 close from the last completed T1 bar on realtime bars; current T1 bar's close otherwise, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Bullish and bearish candle counts = counts of bars with close > open and close < open, respectively, in the preceding pullback lookback bars
Long: Williams Fractal low confirmed, with its pivot at the fractal-period bars ago
Short: Williams Fractal high confirmed, with its pivot at the fractal-period bars ago
Long: datetime >= start-time parameter AND datetime <= end-time parameter AND Williams Fractal low confirmed with its pivot at the fractal-period bars ago AND EMA(EMA source, shorter lookback) >= EMA(EMA source, longer lookback) AND (if the EMA-band parameter selects the fractal bar: close[fractal period] < EMA(EMA source, shorter lookback)[fractal period] AND close[fractal period] > EMA(EMA source, longer lookback)[fractal period]; otherwise: EMA source < EMA(EMA source, shorter lookback) AND EMA source > EMA(EMA source, longer lookback)) AND (if the EMA-gap filter is enabled: absolute difference between the two EMAs >= EMA-source price times the gap-percent parameter) AND (if the RSI filter is enabled: RSI(RSI input, RSI lookback) >= long RSI minimum AND RSI(RSI input, RSI lookback) <= long RSI maximum AND, if RSI direction confirmation is enabled, RSI is rising over the direction lookback) AND (if the pullback filter is enabled: bearish candle count < bullish candle count) AND position is flat
Short: datetime >= start-time parameter AND datetime <= end-time parameter AND Williams Fractal high confirmed with its pivot at the fractal-period bars ago AND EMA(EMA source, shorter lookback) < EMA(EMA source, longer lookback) AND (if the EMA-band parameter selects the fractal bar: close[fractal period] > EMA(EMA source, shorter lookback)[fractal period] AND close[fractal period] < EMA(EMA source, longer lookback)[fractal period]; otherwise: EMA source > EMA(EMA source, shorter lookback) AND EMA source < EMA(EMA source, longer lookback)) AND (if the EMA-gap filter is enabled: absolute difference between the two EMAs >= EMA-source price times the gap-percent parameter) AND (if the RSI filter is enabled: RSI(RSI input, RSI lookback) >= short RSI minimum AND RSI(RSI input, RSI lookback) <= short RSI maximum AND, if RSI direction confirmation is enabled, RSI is falling over the direction lookback) AND (if the pullback filter is enabled: bullish candle count < bearish candle count) AND position is flat
Exit: long, bars since the most recent flat bar >= exit-delay parameter AND (low <= EMA(EMA source, longer lookback) at the entry signal OR high >= entry-signal close + (entry-signal close - EMA(EMA source, longer lookback) at the entry signal) times the long target multiple)
Exit: short, bars since the most recent flat bar >= exit-delay parameter AND (high >= EMA(EMA source, longer lookback) at the entry signal OR low <= entry-signal close - (EMA(EMA source, longer lookback) at the entry signal - entry-signal close) times the short target multiple)
