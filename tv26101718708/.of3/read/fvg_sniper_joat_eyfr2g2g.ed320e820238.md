Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) when available and > 0, otherwise minimum tick, calculated on chart bars
Calculation: Middle-candle body ratio = abs(close[1] - open[1]) / max(high[1] - low[1], minimum tick), calculated on chart bars
Calculation: FVG grade = min(maximum grade, maximum grade × (min(gap height / (Safe ATR × gap-size normalization), size-score cap) × gap-size weight + min(middle-candle range / (Safe ATR × displacement normalization), displacement-score cap) × displacement weight + (current volume > 0 AND SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0 ? min(volume[1] / SMA(volume, volume lookback), relative-volume cap) / relative-volume cap : fallback relative-volume score) × relative-volume weight)), assigned when the gap forms
Calculation: HTF bias = 0 when the filter is disabled or the T1 request is unavailable; otherwise 1 when T1 close > EMA(T1 close, HTF EMA lookback) and -1 otherwise, calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically; T1 bar still forming in real time)
Calculation: Risk = clamp(abs(entry - raw stop), ATR at entry × minimum risk ATR, ATR at entry × maximum risk ATR), where raw stop is entry minus ATR at entry × ATR stop distance for a long in ATR mode, entry plus that distance for a short in ATR mode, the triggering gap's lower boundary minus ATR at entry × gap stop buffer for a long in gap-protected mode, or its upper boundary plus that buffer for a short
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, IFVG source enabled AND bearish FVG detection enabled AND a tracked bearish FVG formed on a confirmed chart bar with high < low[2], (low[2] - high) >= Safe ATR × minimum gap ATR, and middle-candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio, with age from one chart bar through gap lifetime plus one chart bar, closes above its upper boundary
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, IFVG source enabled AND bearish FVG detection enabled AND a tracked bearish FVG formed on a confirmed chart bar with high < low[2], (low[2] - high) >= Safe ATR × minimum gap ATR, and middle-candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio, with age from one chart bar through gap lifetime plus one chart bar, closes above its upper boundary AND FVG grade >= minimum signal grade AND HTF bias >= 0
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, rejection source enabled AND bullish FVG detection enabled AND a tracked bullish FVG formed on a confirmed chart bar with low > high[2], (low - high[2]) >= Safe ATR × minimum gap ATR, and middle-candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio, with age from one chart bar through gap lifetime plus one chart bar, is not mitigated, inverted, or previously reacted, current low <= its upper boundary AND current low >= its lower boundary AND close > its lower boundary AND (directional rejection close not required OR close > open) AND FVG grade >= minimum signal grade AND HTF bias >= 0
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, IFVG source enabled AND bullish FVG detection enabled AND a tracked bullish FVG formed on a confirmed chart bar with low > high[2], (low - high[2]) >= Safe ATR × minimum gap ATR, and middle-candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio, with age from one chart bar through gap lifetime plus one chart bar, closes below its lower boundary
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, IFVG source enabled AND bullish FVG detection enabled AND a tracked bullish FVG formed on a confirmed chart bar with low > high[2], (low - high[2]) >= Safe ATR × minimum gap ATR, and middle-candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio, with age from one chart bar through gap lifetime plus one chart bar, closes below its lower boundary AND FVG grade >= minimum signal grade AND HTF bias <= 0
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, rejection source enabled AND bearish FVG detection enabled AND a tracked bearish FVG formed on a confirmed chart bar with high < low[2], (low[2] - high) >= Safe ATR × minimum gap ATR, and middle-candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio, with age from one chart bar through gap lifetime plus one chart bar, is not mitigated, inverted, or previously reacted, current high >= its lower boundary AND current high <= its upper boundary AND close < its upper boundary AND (directional rejection close not required OR close < open) AND FVG grade >= minimum signal grade AND HTF bias <= 0
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after entry, low <= entry - Risk OR high >= entry + Risk × TP3 R multiple OR chart bars since entry >= maximum trade duration
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after entry, high >= entry + Risk OR low <= entry - Risk × TP3 R multiple OR chart bars since entry >= maximum trade duration
