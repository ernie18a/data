Calculation: ATR stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar × stop multiplier
Long: MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback) crosses above its signal line AND position side = flat AND (no prior position close OR bars since prior position close >= cooldown lookback)
Short: close > EMA(close, shorter context lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer context lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter trigger lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer trigger lookback) AND MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback) crosses below its signal line AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR SMA(close, shorter higher-timeframe lookback) on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically; forming T1 bar in realtime) < SMA(close, longer higher-timeframe lookback) on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically; forming T1 bar in realtime)) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bearish threshold AND short trades enabled AND position side = flat AND (no prior position close OR bars since prior position close >= cooldown lookback)
Exit: long, signal-exit mode enabled AND SMA(close, shorter trend-filter lookback) crosses below SMA(close, longer trend-filter lookback)
Exit: short, signal-exit mode enabled AND SMA(close, shorter trend-filter lookback) crosses above SMA(close, longer trend-filter lookback)
Exit: long, signal-exit mode disabled AND (low <= entry-signal close − ATR stop distance OR high >= entry-signal close + ATR stop distance × take-profit multiplier)
Exit: short, signal-exit mode disabled AND (high >= entry-signal close + ATR stop distance OR low <= entry-signal close − ATR stop distance × take-profit multiplier)
