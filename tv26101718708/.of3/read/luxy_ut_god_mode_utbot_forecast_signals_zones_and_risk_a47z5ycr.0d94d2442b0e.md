Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR(shared volatility and structure lookback) / SMA(ATR(shared volatility and structure lookback), volatility-average lookback), or neutral when the average is zero.
Calculation: UT trailing stop = a ratcheting stop on close using distance ATR(trailing lookback) × effective sensitivity × asset-class multiplier × volatility-mode multiplier × clamped volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) × (1 + (1 − Kaufman Efficiency Ratio(close, ER lookback)) × chop strength when smart chop is enabled); the volume clamp has separate floor and cap parameters. The volatility-mode multiplier is neutral in fixed mode and otherwise uses separate low-volatility and high-volatility thresholds and multipliers for dynamic and aggressive modes. Classic mode uses only ATR × sensitivity. Initialize the stop at close; when close and its prior value are both above the prior stop, set it to the greater of the prior stop and close minus distance; when both are below, set it to the lesser of the prior stop and close plus distance; otherwise set it to close minus distance when close is above the prior stop, or close plus distance when below.
Calculation: Minimum-move threshold = the greater of the minimum percent-change parameter and the lesser of (ATR(trailing lookback) / close as a percentage) and the percent cap, multiplied by the ATR-percent weight.
Calculation: Active resistance and support zones = separately cluster confirmed pivot highs and lows, using a pivot-strength lookback on each side and tolerance ATR(shared volatility and structure lookback) × zone-width multiplier. Merge each pivot into the last same-side zone within tolerance by averaging its center with the pivot; otherwise create a zone. Keep up to the zone-capacity parameter per side, evicting the zone farthest from close when full, and discard zones farther from close than close × the stale-distance percentage. A price is in a zone when its distance from any zone center is at most the tolerance.
Calculation: Buffered base stop for each position side = the selected stop method applied to entry price: ATR distance uses ATR(stop lookback) × its multiplier; percentage distance uses entry × stop percentage; tick distance uses tick count × minimum tick; swing distance uses the lowest low or highest high over the swing lookback when protective, otherwise ATR(stop lookback) × its swing fallback multiplier. Scaled ATR and smart-adaptive methods use separate ATR-percent bands and multipliers, with distance floored by entry × minimum-stop percentage. Safer uses the farthest protective level among ATR, percentage, and swing stops, with its own swing fallback multiplier. If no stop is available, use ATR(stop lookback) × fallback multiplier. Then apply the ATR buffer using ATR(trailing lookback) × buffer multiplier. Final stop adds extra ticks × minimum tick on the protective side; if it is on the wrong side of entry, replace it with entry plus or minus ATR(trailing lookback) × wrong-side floor multiplier, the ATR buffer, and extra ticks.
Calculation: Enabled target levels = entry price plus or minus the absolute distance from entry to buffered base stop, multiplied by each enabled target’s separate R-multiple parameter. The extra-tick buffer is excluded from this distance.
Long: close > UT trailing stop.
Short: close < UT trailing stop.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, crossover(close, UT trailing stop), or, when delayed confirmation is enabled, crossover(close, UT trailing stop) from the configured delay interval ago and close above the trailing stop on each intervening bar; close > UT trailing stop; the preceding confirmation-lookback minus one bars each closed above their respective trailing stops; and, when enabled, ADX(ADX lookback) ≥ ADX threshold, bars since last signal ≥ cooldown (or cooldown is zero), volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume threshold, Volatility ratio ≥ high-volatility threshold, RSI(close, entry RSI lookback) < overbought threshold, close > HMA(close, Hull lookback), SuperTrend is bullish, EMA(shorter lookback, T1) ≥ EMA(longer lookback, T1), close is outside every resistance zone, low > UT trailing stop, close > lowest(close, swing lookback) × (1 + swing margin), and percent change from close[1] > Minimum-move threshold. Evaluate the T1 EMA filter on chart bars using lookahead-off T1 values: the T1 bar still forming in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically. The preceding signal must not be long, and the cooldown must have passed.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, crossunder(close, UT trailing stop), or, when delayed confirmation is enabled, crossunder(close, UT trailing stop) from the configured delay interval ago and close below the trailing stop on each intervening bar; close < UT trailing stop; the preceding confirmation-lookback minus one bars each closed below their respective trailing stops; and, when enabled, ADX(ADX lookback) ≥ ADX threshold, bars since last signal ≥ cooldown (or cooldown is zero), volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume threshold, Volatility ratio ≥ high-volatility threshold, RSI(close, entry RSI lookback) > oversold threshold, close < HMA(close, Hull lookback), SuperTrend is bearish, EMA(shorter lookback, T1) ≤ EMA(longer lookback, T1), close is outside every support zone, high < UT trailing stop, close < highest(close, swing lookback) × (1 − swing margin), and percent change from close[1] > Minimum-move threshold. Evaluate the T1 EMA filter on chart bars using lookahead-off T1 values: the T1 bar still forming in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically. The preceding signal must not be short, and the cooldown must have passed.
Long: When divergence labels are enabled, a confirmed price pivot low and RSI pivot low occur at the same pivot; the price pivot is lower than the preceding paired price pivot low and the RSI pivot is higher than the preceding paired RSI pivot low. Use RSI(close, divergence RSI lookback) and the divergence pivot-strength lookback.
Short: When divergence labels are enabled, a confirmed price pivot high and RSI pivot high occur at the same pivot; the price pivot is higher than the preceding paired price pivot high and the RSI pivot is lower than the preceding paired RSI pivot high. Use RSI(close, divergence RSI lookback) and the divergence pivot-strength lookback.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ final stop + stop-touch tolerance, or high ≥ the farthest enabled target.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ final stop − stop-touch tolerance, or low ≤ the farthest enabled target.
