Calculation: Trend filters pass = (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR (T1 EMA(close, middle lookback) is available and (T1's last completed close > EMA(close, shorter lookback, on T1 bars at the last completed T1 bar) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, on T1 bars at the last completed T1 bar) > EMA(close, middle lookback, on T1 bars at the last completed T1 bar)) OR (T1 EMA(close, middle lookback) unavailable AND pass-if-history-insufficient is enabled))) AND (current-timeframe filter disabled OR (close > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND EMA(close, longer lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)[slope lookback])).
Long: EMA alignment AND EMA alignment[1] AND close > highest(high, breakout lookback)[1] AND Trend filters pass AND this is the first breakout signal in the current uninterrupted EMA alignment period.
