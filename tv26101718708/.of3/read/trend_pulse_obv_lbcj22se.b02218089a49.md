Calculation: Trend Pulse midline = cascade EMA(selected price source, period, pole count, zero-lag transform); upper band = midline + cascade EMA(true range, period, pole count, zero-lag transform) × band multiplier, where the transform replaces x with x + (x − x[floor((period − 1)/(2 × pole count))]) when enabled, cascade EMA uses length max(1, floor(2/α)) cascaded pole count times, α = −β + sqrt(β² + 2β), and β = (1 − cos(2π / period)) / (sqrt(2)^(2 / pole count) − 1)
Long: flat AND within selected date range AND close crosses above Trend Pulse upper band AND (regime filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, regime lookback)) AND (OBV filter disabled OR OBV > EMA(OBV, OBV EMA lookback))
Exit: Long, low ≤ signal-bar close − ATR(ATR lookback)[1 at signal bar] × stop multiplier (fixed at entry) OR within selected date range AND ((midline exit enabled AND close < Trend Pulse midline) OR (regime exit enabled AND regime filter enabled AND close < EMA(close, regime lookback)))
