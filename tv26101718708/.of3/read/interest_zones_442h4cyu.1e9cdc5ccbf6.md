Calculation: Active support/resistance levels(T) = On chart bars, expire levels older than the maximum age in chart bars; then add confirmed T-bar pivot highs/lows only when the pivot high minus max(high[pivot length - 1], high[pivot length + 1]) or min(low[pivot length - 1], low[pivot length + 1]) minus the pivot low, respectively, is at least minimum strength * ATR(ATR lookback) on T bars; skip a pivot when an active level of the same type is within merge threshold * ATR(ATR lookback) of its price, and evict the oldest same-type level when adding at the active-level cap. Keep enabled level types from enabled T1-T4 separately. Pivot and ATR calculations run on T bars; their values are sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T bar historically, forming T bar in real time). Deactivate a level after its break condition is met.
Long: On a chart bar, for any active resistance level from enabled T1-T4, close > its price + break sensitivity * ATR(ATR lookback) from that T, sampled with lookahead off (last completed T bar historically, forming T bar in real time)
Short: On a chart bar, for any active support level from enabled T1-T4, close < its price - break sensitivity * ATR(ATR lookback) from that T, sampled with lookahead off (last completed T bar historically, forming T bar in real time)
