Calculation: Adaptive Fourier model = For each enabled rolling window of log(close), subtract the least-squares linear trend, compute DFT powers over the permitted bins, rank bins by power adjusted for frequency, select and score the dominant window using spectral energy, amplitude, stability, phase agreement, and prior selection, then reconstruct its cycle from the strongest qualifying harmonics and retain its trend, phase, amplitude, stability, and fit error.
Calculation: Forecast move = Reconstructed cycle change from now to the rounded dominant-period forecast step plus the selected trend slope projected over that step with per-bar damping; forecast direction is positive or negative only when forecast move divided by ATR/close times the square root of the step exceeds the direction dead-zone parameter in magnitude.
Calculation: Confidence score = The capped weighted score of cycle stability, forecast direction, relative cycle amplitude, phase, forecast quality, ZLEMA alignment and extension, volume class, and support/resistance room, multiplied by the stability adjustment and higher-timeframe alignment adjustment; calculate separately for long and short direction.
Calculation: Volume class = Classify volume relative to its SMA using separate strong- and weak-volume-ratio parameters and the candle direction (close versus open); when volume SMA is unavailable or nonpositive, do not restrict the signal.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = On each enabled higher timeframe, use the last completed bar to compare a log-close EMA-detrended series with its projected Fourier-bin move, classified against an ATR-normalized threshold; alignment is the mean directional score, assigning full, half, or zero credit to agreement, neutral, or disagreement.
Calculation: Projected cycle levels = Project the dominant Fourier cycle and damped trend to its next qualifying trough and peak within the stability-adjusted forecast horizon; adjust a projected level toward the nearest confirmed pivot support or resistance when within the pivot proximity parameter.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a new long signal occurs when the prior bar had no active long signal, the minimum-bar cooldown has elapsed, and no long trade is active, AND an adaptive Fourier model exists, forecast direction is up and forecast move is positive, phase is bullish (near a trough or rising while not near a peak), cycle stability meets the minimum unless weak cycles are allowed, long confidence meets the entry-confidence threshold, price is above ZLEMA when that filter is enabled and is not extended above it by more than the extension-in-ATR parameter, volume passes the non-strict or strict bullish volume-class filter, cycle position is not near a projected top, and higher-timeframe alignment passes when blocking confirmation is enabled.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a new short signal occurs when the prior bar had no active short signal, the minimum-bar cooldown has elapsed, and no short trade is active, AND an adaptive Fourier model exists, forecast direction is down and forecast move is negative, phase is bearish (near a peak or falling while not near a trough), cycle stability meets the minimum unless weak cycles are allowed, short confidence meets the entry-confidence threshold, price is below ZLEMA when that filter is enabled and is not extended below it by more than the extension-in-ATR parameter, volume passes the non-strict or strict bearish volume-class filter, cycle position is not near a projected bottom, and higher-timeframe alignment passes when blocking confirmation is enabled.
Long: An adaptive Fourier model exists, the projected trough is within the stability-adjusted forecast horizon, and its projected price, after any qualifying support adjustment, is below close.
Short: An adaptive Fourier model exists, the projected peak is within the stability-adjusted forecast horizon, and its projected price, after any qualifying resistance adjustment, is above close.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low reaches or falls below the stored stop-loss level OR high reaches or exceeds a stored target level; stop loss takes priority when reached on the same bar, each target triggers at most once, and the third target closes the trade OR bars since entry exceed the trade-expiry parameter.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high reaches or exceeds the stored stop-loss level OR low reaches or falls below a stored target level; stop loss takes priority when reached on the same bar, each target triggers at most once, and the third target closes the trade OR bars since entry exceed the trade-expiry parameter.
