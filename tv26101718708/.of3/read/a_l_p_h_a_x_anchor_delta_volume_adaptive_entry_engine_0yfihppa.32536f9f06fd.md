Calculation: Anchor trend and Band 4 = Initialize bullish. On each chart bar, compute an adaptive scale: when adaptive scaling is disabled, use 1; otherwise use the minimum scale if Bollinger Bands(close, BB lookback, BB width) lie strictly inside Keltner Channels(close, Keltner EMA lookback, ATR(Keltner ATR lookback), Keltner width), the maximum scale if the percentile rank of ATR(ATR lookback) over its lookback exceeds its threshold, and 1 otherwise. Set the outer offset to ATR(ATR lookback) × (base ATR multiplier × adaptive scale + outer-band spacing count × band spacing). The bullish raw Band 4 is source price minus the offset; the bearish raw Band 4 is source price plus the offset. While bullish, flip bearish when source price is below the prior Band 4 and reset Band 4 to the bearish raw band; otherwise set Band 4 to the greater of the bullish raw band and prior Band 4. While bearish, flip bullish when source price is above the prior Band 4 and reset Band 4 to the bullish raw band; otherwise set Band 4 to the lesser of the bearish raw band and prior Band 4. Use the bullish raw band as the prior-band fallback on the first bar.
Calculation: Trend equilibrium = Midpoint of the highest high and lowest low since the latest Anchor trend flip; on a flip bar, initialize those extrema using the high and low against the newly reset Band 4.
Calculation: Estimated volume delta = EMA(estimated bullish volume, volume lookback) − EMA(estimated bearish volume, volume lookback), where estimated bullish volume is volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume when high equals low, and estimated bearish volume is volume minus estimated bullish volume.
Calculation: HVN proximity = If HVN detection is enabled, divide the current trend segment's range from its lowest low to highest high into the configured number of equal bins, assign each bar's HLC3 to a bin, and sum its volume there. When delta weighting is enabled, use estimated bullish volume in a bullish trend and estimated bearish volume in a bearish trend. Recalculate from the current segment, using at most the configured profile history cap bars. An interior bin midpoint is an HVN when its volume exceeds both adjacent bins. HVN proximity is true when the close or Band 4 is within ATR(ATR lookback) × HVN tolerance of any HVN; use instrument tick size as bin size if the segment range is zero.
Calculation: Directional confluence score(side) = Count the true conditions among six layers: Anchor trend agrees with side; HTF bias agrees with side; ADX(ADX lookback) is at least its threshold; estimated volume delta is positive for long or negative for short; close is below Trend equilibrium for long or above it for short; and HVN proximity. The HTF layer is true when its filter is disabled; otherwise, on chart bars use T1's fast and slow EMAs and close with lookahead off (the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar live): bullish requires fast EMA above slow EMA and close above fast EMA, bearish requires fast EMA below slow EMA and close below fast EMA. The ADX and volume-delta layers are true when their respective filters are disabled. The equilibrium layer is true when the PD-zone filter is disabled.
Long: On confirmed chart-bar close, Anchor trend flips bullish.
Short: On confirmed chart-bar close, Anchor trend flips bearish.
Long: On confirmed chart-bar close, Anchor trend is bullish AND low <= Band 4 + ATR(ATR lookback) × touch tolerance AND close > Band 4 AND HVN proximity.
Short: On confirmed chart-bar close, Anchor trend is bearish AND high >= Band 4 − ATR(ATR lookback) × touch tolerance AND close < Band 4 AND HVN proximity.
Long: On confirmed chart-bar close, Anchor trend is bullish AND low <= Band 4 + ATR(ATR lookback) × touch tolerance AND close > Band 4 AND Directional confluence score(long) >= minimum layers AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is in the active session) AND (no prior entry signal exists OR chart bars since the prior entry signal >= cooldown) AND entry signals are enabled.
Short: On confirmed chart-bar close, Anchor trend is bearish AND high >= Band 4 − ATR(ATR lookback) × touch tolerance AND close < Band 4 AND Directional confluence score(short) >= minimum layers AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is in the active session) AND (no prior entry signal exists OR chart bars since the prior entry signal >= cooldown) AND entry signals are enabled.
Exit: long, Anchor trend flips bearish.
Exit: short, Anchor trend flips bullish.
