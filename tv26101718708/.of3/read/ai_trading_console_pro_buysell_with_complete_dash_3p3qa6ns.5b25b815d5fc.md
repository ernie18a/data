Calculation: Long stop = initialized at entry price - ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * initial-stop multiplier; while long, if trailing is enabled, max(previous long stop, close - ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier), otherwise previous long stop
Calculation: Short stop = initialized at entry price + ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * initial-stop multiplier; while short, if trailing is enabled, min(previous short stop, close + ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier), otherwise previous short stop
Long: long-entry switch enabled AND (ORB filter disabled OR close crosses above the highest high of the opening interval of the current trading session) AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(chart bars, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) > minimum ADX) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close on chart bars < maximum VIX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close of the still-forming T1 bar > EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback, T1 bars, still-forming bar), sampled on chart bars) AND flat AND most recent entry side is not long
Short: short-entry switch enabled AND (ORB filter disabled OR close crosses below the lowest low of the opening interval of the current trading session) AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(chart bars, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) > minimum ADX) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close on chart bars < maximum VIX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close of the still-forming T1 bar < EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback, T1 bars, still-forming bar), sampled on chart bars) AND flat AND most recent entry side is not short
Exit: long, close < long stop
Exit: short, close > short stop

Calculation: opening-range low = minimum traded-instrument low during the opening period
Long: buy enabled AND (opening-range filter disabled OR close crosses above opening-range high) AND (EMA filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > buy RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > minimum ADX) AND (India VIX filter disabled OR India VIX < maximum VIX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR T1 close > EMA(T1 close, higher-timeframe EMA period)) AND no position is open AND last entry direction is not long
Short: sell enabled AND (opening-range filter disabled OR close crosses below opening-range low) AND (EMA filter disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < sell RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > minimum ADX) AND (India VIX filter disabled OR India VIX < maximum VIX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR T1 close < EMA(T1 close, higher-timeframe EMA period)) AND no position is open AND last entry direction is not short
