Calculation: Breadth HMA = HMA(breadth close, HMA lookback)
Calculation: Smoothed breadth HMA = EMA(Breadth HMA, HMA smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Fast breadth EMA = EMA(breadth close, shorter breadth EMA lookback)
Calculation: Slow breadth EMA = EMA(breadth close, longer breadth EMA lookback)
Calculation: RSI composite = (RSI(close, RSI lookback) + SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI signal lookback)) / 2
Calculation: Pivot-range condition = minimum pivot-range lookback <= bars since previous detected pivot <= maximum pivot-range lookback
Calculation: Core long-entry condition = Breadth HMA crosses above Smoothed breadth HMA AND RSI composite > RSI midpoint AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint + RSI band AND volatility-series close > volatility threshold AND Fast breadth EMA is positive
Calculation: ATR stop offset = ATR(ATR lookback, T1, still-forming bar), calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars, multiplied by ATR stop percentage
Calculation: Long stop level = MIN(chart low on the bar immediately before entry - ATR stop offset at entry, current chart low on the bar immediately before this bar - current ATR stop offset)
Calculation: Short stop level = MAX(chart high on the bar immediately before entry + ATR stop offset at entry, current chart high on the bar immediately before this bar + current ATR stop offset)
Long: Breadth HMA crosses above Smoothed breadth HMA
Short: Breadth HMA crosses below Smoothed breadth HMA
Long: Breadth HMA[reversal lookback] >= Breadth HMA[previous bar] AND Breadth HMA > Breadth HMA[previous bar]
Short: Breadth HMA[reversal lookback] <= Breadth HMA[previous bar] AND Breadth HMA < Breadth HMA[previous bar]
Long: Slow breadth EMA is positive
Short: Slow breadth EMA is nonpositive
Long: breadth close > background magnitude threshold
Short: breadth close < negative background magnitude threshold
Long: bullish-divergence toggle is enabled AND pivotlow(breadth close, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) exists AND chart low[right pivot lookback] < valuewhen(pivotlow exists, chart low[right pivot lookback], previous occurrence) AND breadth close[right pivot lookback] > valuewhen(pivotlow exists, breadth close[right pivot lookback], previous occurrence) AND pivot-range condition

Short: bearish-divergence toggle is enabled AND pivothigh(breadth close, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) exists AND chart high[right pivot lookback] > valuewhen(pivothigh exists, chart high[right pivot lookback], previous occurrence) AND breadth close[right pivot lookback] < valuewhen(pivothigh exists, breadth close[right pivot lookback], previous occurrence) AND pivot-range condition
