Calculation: Structure-window high = highest high of chart bars in the structure-window parameter since the start of the current T2 period
Calculation: Structure-window low = lowest low of chart bars in the structure-window parameter since the start of the current T2 period
Calculation: Model EMA = EMA(close, lookback, T1), sampled on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time
Calculation: ADR target = ADR(lookback, T2, last completed bars) * ADR-fraction parameter
Calculation: Upper ADR boundary = if current T2 high - current T2 low < ADR target OR current T2 close >= current T2 open then current T2 low + ADR target else current T2 high; calculated on T2 bars and mapped to chart bars using the still-forming T2 bar in real time and completed T2 values historically
Calculation: Lower ADR boundary = if current T2 high - current T2 low < ADR target OR current T2 close < current T2 open then current T2 high - ADR target else current T2 low; calculated on T2 bars and mapped to chart bars using the still-forming T2 bar in real time and completed T2 values historically
Long: model alerts enabled AND buy-day model enabled AND a chart bar on or before the current bar in the current T2 period was in the structure-window parameter and had close > Structure-window high AND close > Model EMA AND current low <= Model EMA AND current chart bar is in the entry-window parameter AND (ADR filter disabled OR Upper ADR boundary unavailable OR current high < Upper ADR boundary)
Short: model alerts enabled AND sell-day model enabled AND a chart bar on or before the current bar in the current T2 period was in the structure-window parameter and had close < Structure-window low AND close < Model EMA AND current high >= Model EMA AND current chart bar is in the entry-window parameter AND (ADR filter disabled OR Lower ADR boundary unavailable OR current low > Lower ADR boundary)
Long: model alerts enabled AND buy-day model enabled AND a chart bar on or before the current bar in the current T2 period was in the structure-window parameter and had close > Structure-window high AND close > Model EMA AND current low <= Model EMA AND current chart bar is in the entry-window parameter AND (ADR filter disabled OR Upper ADR boundary unavailable OR current high < Upper ADR boundary) AND long direction is allowed AND position is flat AND (trade-session filter disabled OR current chart bar is in the trade-session parameter in its configured timezone)
Short: model alerts enabled AND sell-day model enabled AND a chart bar on or before the current bar in the current T2 period was in the structure-window parameter and had close < Structure-window low AND close < Model EMA AND current high >= Model EMA AND current chart bar is in the entry-window parameter AND (ADR filter disabled OR Lower ADR boundary unavailable OR current low > Lower ADR boundary) AND short direction is allowed AND position is flat AND (trade-session filter disabled OR current chart bar is in the trade-session parameter in its configured timezone)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + first-target-distance parameter OR high >= entry price + second-target-distance parameter OR low <= entry price - stop-distance parameter OR (break-even-after-first-target enabled AND first target has been reached since entry AND low <= entry price)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - first-target-distance parameter OR low <= entry price - second-target-distance parameter OR high >= entry price + stop-distance parameter OR (break-even-after-first-target enabled AND first target has been reached since entry AND high >= entry price)
