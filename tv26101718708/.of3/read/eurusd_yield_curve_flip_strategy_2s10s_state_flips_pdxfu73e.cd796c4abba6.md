Calculation: N-T1-bar change(x) = the current T1 value of x minus its value at the Nth earlier new-T1-bar sample, evaluated on chart bars using values from the still-forming T1 bar
Calculation: Current regime = bullish if (N-T1-bar change(10Y yield close - 2Y yield close) > curve-move threshold AND N-T1-bar change(2Y yield close) < -leg-confirmation threshold) OR (N-T1-bar change(10Y yield close - 2Y yield close) < -curve-move threshold AND N-T1-bar change(10Y yield close) < -leg-confirmation threshold); otherwise bearish if not bullish and ((N-T1-bar change(10Y yield close - 2Y yield close) < -curve-move threshold AND N-T1-bar change(2Y yield close) > leg-confirmation threshold) OR (bear-steepener shorts enabled AND N-T1-bar change(10Y yield close - 2Y yield close) > curve-move threshold AND N-T1-bar change(10Y yield close) > leg-confirmation threshold)); otherwise neutral; insufficient current yield data or change history gives neutral
Calculation: Stored regime = Current regime on a new T1 bar if Current regime is non-neutral; otherwise the prior chart bar's Stored regime; initially neutral
Long: Current regime is bullish AND (prior chart bar's Stored regime is bearish OR (prior chart bar's Stored regime is neutral AND entries from neutral are enabled)) AND (new T1 bar OR the new-T1-bar-only filter is disabled)
Short: Current regime is bearish AND (prior chart bar's Stored regime is bullish OR (prior chart bar's Stored regime is neutral AND entries from neutral are enabled)) AND (new T1 bar OR the new-T1-bar-only filter is disabled)
Calculation: US yield curve = US 10Y yield - US 2Y yield
Calculation: US yield curve change = current US yield curve - US yield curve lookback T1 bars earlier
Calculation: US 2Y yield change = current US 2Y yield - US 2Y yield lookback T1 bars earlier
Calculation: US 10Y yield change = current US 10Y yield - US 10Y yield lookback T1 bars earlier
Long: US 2Y yield, US 10Y yield, and lookback data are available AND (new T1 rates bar OR new rates bar only is false) AND ((US yield curve change > curve threshold AND US 2Y yield change < -leg threshold) OR (US yield curve change < -curve threshold AND US 10Y yield change < -leg threshold)) AND (previous stored regime is bearish OR (trade from neutral AND previous stored regime is neutral))
Short: US 2Y yield, US 10Y yield, and lookback data are available AND (new T1 rates bar OR new rates bar only is false) AND NOT ((US yield curve change > curve threshold AND US 2Y yield change < -leg threshold) OR (US yield curve change < -curve threshold AND US 10Y yield change < -leg threshold)) AND ((US yield curve change < -curve threshold AND US 2Y yield change > leg threshold) OR (allow bear steepener AND US yield curve change > curve threshold AND US 10Y yield change > leg threshold)) AND (previous stored regime is bullish OR (trade from neutral AND previous stored regime is neutral))
