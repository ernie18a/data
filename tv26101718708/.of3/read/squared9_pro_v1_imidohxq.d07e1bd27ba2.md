Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = SMA(SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback), %K smoothing lookback), %D smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Gann anchor and direction = latest qualifying PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), where each pivot's range (high[pivot lookback] − low[pivot lookback]) > ATR(ATR lookback)[pivot lookback] × minimum pivot range multiple; if both exist, use the high pivot with downward direction when close < (latest qualifying pivot high + latest qualifying pivot low) / 2, otherwise use the low pivot with upward direction; require the chosen anchor > 0
Calculation: HTF EMA = EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1), computed on higher-timeframe T1 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (current T1 bar in real time; last completed T1 bar historically)
Long: (Gann proximity filter disabled OR abs(close − any Square-of-Nine level from Gann anchor and direction) <= close × proximity percentage / 100) AND (divergence filter disabled OR PivotLow(low, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) < PivotLow(low, divergence lookback, divergence lookback)[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotLow(Smoothed stochastic, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) > Smoothed stochastic[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotLow(Smoothed stochastic, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) < bullish stochastic divergence threshold OR PivotLow(MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), divergence lookback, divergence lookback) > MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback)[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotLow(low, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) < PivotLow(low, divergence lookback, divergence lookback)[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotLow(MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), divergence lookback, divergence lookback) < 0 OR fallback enabled AND Smoothed stochastic < bullish fallback threshold) AND (strong candle filter disabled OR close > open AND close − open >= (high − low) × minimum candle body ratio) AND (HTF alignment filter disabled OR close > HTF EMA) AND position is flat or short
Short: (Gann proximity filter disabled OR abs(close − any Square-of-Nine level from Gann anchor and direction) <= close × proximity percentage / 100) AND (divergence filter disabled OR PivotHigh(high, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) > PivotHigh(high, divergence lookback, divergence lookback)[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotHigh(Smoothed stochastic, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) < Smoothed stochastic[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotHigh(Smoothed stochastic, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) > bearish stochastic divergence threshold OR PivotHigh(MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), divergence lookback, divergence lookback) < MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback)[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotHigh(high, divergence lookback, divergence lookback) > PivotHigh(high, divergence lookback, divergence lookback)[2 × divergence lookback] AND PivotHigh(MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), divergence lookback, divergence lookback) > 0 OR fallback enabled AND Smoothed stochastic > bearish fallback threshold) AND (strong candle filter disabled OR close < open AND open − close >= (high − low) × minimum candle body ratio) AND (HTF alignment filter disabled OR close < HTF EMA) AND position is flat or long
Exit: long, (fixed ATR exits enabled AND (low <= entry price − ATR at entry signal bar × stop multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry signal bar × target multiple)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND high has reached entry price + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × trailing multiple AND low <= highest high since trailing activation − ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × trailing multiple)
Exit: short, (fixed ATR exits enabled AND (high >= entry price + ATR at entry signal bar × stop multiple OR low <= entry price − ATR at entry signal bar × target multiple)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND low has reached entry price − ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × trailing multiple AND high >= lowest low since trailing activation + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × trailing multiple)
