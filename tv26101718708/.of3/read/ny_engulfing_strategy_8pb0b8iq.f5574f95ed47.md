Calculation: body high = max(open, close)
Calculation: body low = min(open, close)
Calculation: bullish one-bar engulf = close > open AND open <= body low[1] AND close >= body high[1]
Calculation: bearish one-bar engulf = close < open AND open >= body high[1] AND close <= body low[1]
Calculation: two-bar body-range engulf = min(body low, body low[1]) <= body low[2] AND max(body high, body high[1]) >= body high[2]
Calculation: long entry level = if bullish one-bar engulf then body high[1] else body high[2]
Calculation: short entry level = if bearish one-bar engulf then body low[1] else body low[2]
Long: two prior bars available AND (bullish one-bar engulf OR (two-bar engulfing enabled AND close > open AND two-bar body-range engulf)) AND close > selected MA(close, lookback) AND long entry level > selected MA(close, lookback) AND (slope filter disabled OR selected MA(close, lookback) > selected MA(close, lookback)[1]) AND (proximity filter disabled OR abs(long entry level - selected MA(close, lookback)) <= proximity threshold) AND high >= long entry level AND no open position AND (session filter disabled OR current chart bar is within the configured session and timezone) AND trades today < daily trade limit AND (stop-after-first-win filter disabled OR no win has occurred today)
Short: two prior bars available AND (bearish one-bar engulf OR (two-bar engulfing enabled AND close < open AND two-bar body-range engulf)) AND close < selected MA(close, lookback) AND short entry level < selected MA(close, lookback) AND (slope filter disabled OR selected MA(close, lookback) < selected MA(close, lookback)[1]) AND (proximity filter disabled OR abs(short entry level - selected MA(close, lookback)) <= proximity threshold) AND low <= short entry level AND no open position AND (session filter disabled OR current chart bar is within the configured session and timezone) AND trades today < daily trade limit AND (stop-after-first-win filter disabled OR no win has occurred today)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR (forced close enabled AND current bar time >= forced-close time AND prior bar time < forced-close time)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR (forced close enabled AND current bar time >= forced-close time AND prior bar time < forced-close time)
