Calculation: EqualCloseCount(close, swing side, swing strength, equal-body reach, tolerance in ticks) = count of closes within the tolerance of the highest close for a swing high or lowest close for a swing low, among bars within min(equal-body reach, swing strength) of the swing candle
Long: On confirmed chart bars, or on chart bars when an enabled T1, T2, or T3 bar closes, a confirmed PivotLow(low, corresponding symmetric swing strength) from that bar stream has a positive lower wick band, measured from its low to the lowest min(open, close) in the full swing window, that is at least the wick/body ratio parameter times abs(close - open) of the swing candle OR EqualCloseCount(close, swing low, corresponding swing strength, equal-body reach, tolerance in ticks) is at least the minimum equal-body count; AND, when enabled, every close after the swing candle through confirmation is at or above the midpoint of the swing low and min(open, close); AND, when enabled, the swing low is below the previous confirmed swing low on that stream and the swing candle close is at or above that previous swing low; AND, when enabled, the swing candle closes above its open; AND the candidate passes the configured same-side overlap rule: it is not hidden by an overlapping higher-timeframe block, and no overlapping same-timeframe block reaches as far or farther in the rejection direction. For T1, T2, and T3, use only last completed bars of that timeframe, calculate on chart bars at its bar rollover, and process only when that timeframe exceeds the chart timeframe. OR, during an enabled alert window (or at any time when all alert windows are disabled), on a chart bar after formation, an active retained bullish block that has not already run has low below its raid candle's min(open, close) and close above that level.
Short: On confirmed chart bars, or on chart bars when an enabled T1, T2, or T3 bar closes, a confirmed PivotHigh(high, corresponding symmetric swing strength) from that bar stream has a positive upper wick band, measured from its high to the highest max(open, close) in the full swing window, that is at least the wick/body ratio parameter times abs(close - open) of the swing candle OR EqualCloseCount(close, swing high, corresponding swing strength, equal-body reach, tolerance in ticks) is at least the minimum equal-body count; AND, when enabled, every close after the swing candle through confirmation is at or below the midpoint of the swing high and max(open, close); AND, when enabled, the swing high is above the previous confirmed swing high on that stream and the swing candle close is at or below that previous swing high; AND, when enabled, the swing candle closes below its open; AND the candidate passes the configured same-side overlap rule: it is not hidden by an overlapping higher-timeframe block, and no overlapping same-timeframe block reaches as far or farther in the rejection direction. For T1, T2, and T3, use only last completed bars of that timeframe, calculate on chart bars at its bar rollover, and process only when that timeframe exceeds the chart timeframe. OR, during an enabled alert window (or at any time when all alert windows are disabled), on a chart bar after formation, an active retained bearish block that has not already run has high above its raid candle's max(open, close) and close below that level.
