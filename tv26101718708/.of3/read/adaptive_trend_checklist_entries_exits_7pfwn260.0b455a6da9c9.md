Calculation: Ultra-active = ultra-aggressive mode selected AND ADX enabled AND ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing) >= ADX minimum + ADX strength margin
Calculation: HTF state(T) = on chart bars, using request-security values with lookahead off (the developing T bar in real time and the last completed T bar historically), bullish if close(T) > EMA(close, MA lookback, T) AND close(T) > Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor, T), bearish if close(T) < both, otherwise range
Calculation: HTF bull score = sum of the separate weights for enabled T1–T4 whose HTF state is bullish
Calculation: HTF bear score = sum of the separate weights for enabled T1–T4 whose HTF state is bearish
Calculation: Enabled HTF total weight = sum of the separate weights for enabled T1–T4
Calculation: Swing lookback = ultra-active swing lookback if Ultra-active; otherwise aggressive swing lookback if aggressive mode is selected OR ultra-aggressive mode is selected; otherwise standard swing lookback
Calculation: Long stop loss = min(entry price - abs(entry price - Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor)) * chart-timeframe-selected stop-distance multiplier, Lowest(low, swing lookback))
Calculation: Short stop loss = max(entry price + abs(entry price - Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor)) * chart-timeframe-selected stop-distance multiplier, Highest(high, swing lookback))
Calculation: Risk = abs(entry price - stop loss)
Calculation: Long target i = entry price + risk * separate reward multiple i; Short target i = entry price - risk * separate reward multiple i, for targets 1, 2, and 3
Calculation: Long trailing stop = max(stop loss, highest Supertrend since entry); Short trailing stop = min(stop loss, lowest Supertrend since entry)
Long: position side is flat AND close > selected EMA or SMA(close, MA lookback) AND (Ultra-active AND close != Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor) OR NOT Ultra-active AND close > Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor)) AND (no HTFs enabled OR HTF bull score >= required HTF score fraction * enabled HTF total weight) AND NOT (ADX enabled AND ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing) < ADX minimum OR T1 enabled AND HTF state(T1) is range OR T2 enabled AND HTF state(T2) is range) AND (close > close[1] if Ultra-active, otherwise close > high[1]) AND bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled
Short: position side is flat AND close < selected EMA or SMA(close, MA lookback) AND (Ultra-active AND close != Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor) OR NOT Ultra-active AND close < Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor)) AND (no HTFs enabled OR HTF bear score >= required HTF score fraction * enabled HTF total weight) AND NOT (ADX enabled AND ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing) < ADX minimum OR T1 enabled AND HTF state(T1) is range OR T2 enabled AND HTF state(T2) is range) AND (close < close[1] if Ultra-active, otherwise close < low[1]) AND bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled
Exit: long, low <= long stop loss OR exit mode is target 1 AND high >= long target 1 OR exit mode is target 2 AND high >= long target 2 OR exit mode is target 3 AND high >= long target 3 OR exit mode is target 2 plus trailing AND (high >= long target 2 OR low <= long trailing stop) OR exit mode is trailing Supertrend AND low <= long trailing stop OR exit mode is trend reversal AND bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled AND (close < selected EMA or SMA(close, MA lookback) OR close < Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor))
Exit: short, high >= short stop loss OR exit mode is target 1 AND low <= short target 1 OR exit mode is target 2 AND low <= short target 2 OR exit mode is target 3 AND low <= short target 3 OR exit mode is target 2 plus trailing AND (low <= short target 2 OR high >= short trailing stop) OR exit mode is trailing Supertrend AND high >= short trailing stop OR exit mode is trend reversal AND bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled AND (close > selected EMA or SMA(close, MA lookback) OR close > Supertrend(ATR lookback, Supertrend factor))
