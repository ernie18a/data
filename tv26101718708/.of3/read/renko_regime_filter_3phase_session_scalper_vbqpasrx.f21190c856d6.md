Calculation: EMA separation = abs(EMA(close, shorter lookback) - EMA(close, longer lookback))
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR(lookback) / SMA(ATR(same lookback), same lookback)
Long: close > open AND close[1] > open[1] AND bars since EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer lookback) < bars since EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer lookback) AND EMA separation > minimum gap parameter AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > VWAP(close) AND (during the regular session OR (during the pre-market or afternoon session AND ATR ratio > threshold)) AND the previous chart bar did not meet the corresponding session-qualified long conditions
Short: close < open AND close[1] < open[1] AND bars since EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer lookback) < bars since EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer lookback) AND EMA separation > minimum gap parameter AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < VWAP(close) AND (during the regular session OR (during the pre-market or afternoon session AND ATR ratio > threshold)) AND the previous chart bar did not meet the corresponding session-qualified short conditions
