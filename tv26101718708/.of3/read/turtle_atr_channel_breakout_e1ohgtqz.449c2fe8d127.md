Calculation: N = ATR(N lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Channel midline = selected SMA or EMA(channel source, channel lookback) on the bars being evaluated
Calculation: Channel half-width = standard-deviation mode: standard deviation(channel source, channel lookback) * standard-deviation multiplier; ATR mode: ATR(N lookback) * ATR-width multiplier
Calculation: Upper channel = channel midline + channel half-width
Calculation: Lower channel = channel midline - channel half-width
Calculation: Long-term trend average = selected SMA or EMA(close, long-term lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend average = EMA(close, higher-timeframe lookback) on T2, sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off; it uses the last completed T2 bar historically and the still-forming T2 bar in real time
Calculation: ADX value = ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Long hard stop = entry close - stop multiple * N at entry; if stop-moves-on-add is enabled, reset to latest add price - the same distance
Calculation: Short hard stop = entry close + stop multiple * N at entry; if stop-moves-on-add is enabled, reset to latest add price + the same distance
Calculation: Last virtual breakout outcome = initially a loser; independently of real positions, open one virtual unit at the close when flat virtually and close is above the upper channel or below the lower channel; exit on an enabled hard stop, otherwise on a close below the midline for a virtual long or above it for a virtual short; use N and the stop multiple captured at entry, and classify exit P&L <= 0 as a loser
Long: On the T1 bar after a completed T1 close, that last completed T1 close is above its upper channel and the preceding T1 close was at or below its own upper channel
Short: On the T1 bar after a completed T1 close, that last completed T1 close is below its lower channel and the preceding T1 close was at or above its own lower channel
Long: Flat after exit processing, close > upper channel, longs enabled, and every enabled filter passes: channel midline > channel midline[slope lookback]; close > long-term trend average and long-term trend average > long-term trend average[1]; close > higher-timeframe trend average; ADX value >= ADX threshold; channel half-width > channel half-width[expansion lookback]; last virtual breakout outcome is a loser
Short: Flat after exit processing, close < lower channel, shorts enabled, and every enabled filter passes: channel midline < channel midline[slope lookback]; close < long-term trend average and long-term trend average < long-term trend average[1]; close < higher-timeframe trend average; ADX value >= ADX threshold; channel half-width > channel half-width[expansion lookback]; last virtual breakout outcome is a loser
Long: Flat after exit processing, close > upper channel, longs enabled, and at least one enabled slope, long-term trend, higher-timeframe trend, ADX, channel-expansion, or last-breakout filter fails
Short: Flat after exit processing, close < lower channel, shorts enabled, and at least one enabled slope, long-term trend, higher-timeframe trend, ADX, channel-expansion, or last-breakout filter fails
Long: While in a long position, close >= most recent unit's add price + add-step multiple * N captured at initial entry
Short: While in a short position, close <= most recent unit's add price - add-step multiple * N captured at initial entry
Exit: Long, hard stop enabled and (intrabar stop mode and low <= long hard stop, or close-based stop mode and close <= long hard stop) OR midline exit selected and close < channel midline OR Donchian exit selected and (intrabar exit mode and low <= lowest(low, Donchian lookback)[1], or close-based exit mode and close < lowest(low, Donchian lookback)[1]) OR trailing-N exit selected and (intrabar exit mode and low <= highest high since entry through this bar - trailing multiple * N captured at initial entry, or close-based exit mode and close < that level)
Exit: Short, hard stop enabled and (intrabar stop mode and high >= short hard stop, or close-based stop mode and close >= short hard stop) OR midline exit selected and close > channel midline OR Donchian exit selected and (intrabar exit mode and high >= highest(high, Donchian lookback)[1], or close-based exit mode and close > highest(high, Donchian lookback)[1]) OR trailing-N exit selected and (intrabar exit mode and high >= lowest low since entry through this bar + trailing multiple * N captured at initial entry, or close-based exit mode and close > that level)
