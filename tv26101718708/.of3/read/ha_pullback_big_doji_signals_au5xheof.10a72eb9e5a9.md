Calculation: HA range = HA high - HA low
Calculation: HA upper wick = HA high - max(HA open, HA close)
Calculation: HA lower wick = min(HA open, HA close) - HA low
Calculation: Consecutive long pullback count = number of immediately preceding consecutive chart bars where HA close > EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND HA close < HA open AND HA upper wick <= HA range * flat-wick tolerance percentage / 100
Calculation: Consecutive short pullback count = number of immediately preceding consecutive chart bars where HA close < EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND HA close > HA open AND HA lower wick <= HA range * flat-wick tolerance percentage / 100
Long: HA close > EMA(HA close, EMA lookback) AND Consecutive long pullback count >= minimum pullbacks AND Consecutive long pullback count >= doji-range minimum count AND HA range > the maximum of the preceding min(Consecutive long pullback count, doji-range comparison cap) HA ranges AND abs(HA close - HA open) <= HA range * maximum doji-body percentage / 100 AND HA upper wick >= HA range * minimum wick percentage / 100 AND HA lower wick >= HA range * minimum wick percentage / 100 AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= the maximum raw volume of the preceding volume-lookback chart bars) AND (doji-color preference disabled OR HA close > HA open)
