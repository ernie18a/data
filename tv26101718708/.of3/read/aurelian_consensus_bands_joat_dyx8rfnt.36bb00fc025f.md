Calculation: recency-weighted profile levels = cache confirmed chart bars up to the greater of the profile lookback and profile row count; on each bar, set the logarithmic price range from the rolling high and low and their EMAs with a span based on the rounded square root of the profile lookback and a minimum-span parameter; divide that range into the profile row count; add each cached bar’s volume times the greater of its linear-age weight and recency-span weight, clamped to a weight floor and unit cap, to every bin intersecting its low-to-high range; derive the weighted mean and standard deviation of bin indexes; use the peak bin, interpolated from its adjacent bins when interior, for VPOC; derive the consensus-area bounds from the mean plus or minus a consensus deviation multiplier and the outer bounds from the mean plus or minus a separate outer deviation multiplier, rounding and clamping indexes to the profile; convert indexes to prices and EMA-smooth all levels with the profile smoothing lookback; if profile volume is zero, use the geometric midpoint of the price range for all levels
Calculation: consensus-area midpoint = sqrt(consensus-area high × consensus-area low)
Calculation: selected consensus anchor = VPOC, consensus-area midpoint, or sqrt(VPOC × consensus-area midpoint), according to the anchor-selection parameter
Calculation: normalized profile positions = for VPOC and consensus-area midpoint separately, (close − level) divided by the distance from that level to the matching outer band on close’s side, clipped to the position cap
Calculation: consensus direction = average of the normalized VPOC and consensus-area midpoint positions
Calculation: profile coherence = coherence scale parameter × product of the normalized VPOC and consensus-area midpoint positions divided by the sum of their squares; use zero if the denominator is zero
Calculation: consensus strength = clipped absolute consensus direction × absolute profile coherence
Calculation: cumulative session pressure = cumulative sum on chart bars of volume × (close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), reset at each new T1 period
Calculation: normalized delta bias = clipped EMA(cumulative session pressure, delta smoothing lookback) / max(EMA(abs(cumulative session pressure), VWAP deviation lookback), normalization floor)
Calculation: volume node = hlc3 on the most recent chart bar whose volume equals the highest volume over the node lookback
Calculation: institutional score = clipped sum of consensus strength normalized by its cap times its score weight, absolute profile coherence times its score weight, the lesser of absolute VWAP z-score divided by its saturation parameter and its cap times its score weight, and absolute delta bias times its score weight; VWAP z-score = clipped (close − session VWAP(hlc3)) / max(stdev(hlc3 − session VWAP(hlc3), VWAP deviation lookback), minimum price increment)
