Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi OHLC = apply EMA with the pre-smoothing lookback to open, high, low, and close; form Heikin-Ashi close as the mean of those four values, open as their open/close mean on the first bar and thereafter the mean of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close, high as the maximum of smoothed high, Heikin-Ashi open, and close, and low as the minimum of smoothed low, Heikin-Ashi open, and close; apply EMA with the post-smoothing lookback to each Heikin-Ashi component.
Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction = bullish when smoothed Heikin-Ashi close is greater than or equal to smoothed Heikin-Ashi open, bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Protected swing levels = latest confirmed pivot low and pivot high of the configured structure source (raw price or smoothed Heikin-Ashi), using the structure pivot lookback on both sides; protect the low during bullish structure and the high during bearish structure, initialize the active level from the latest corresponding pivot, and on a direction flip set the newly active level from the latest corresponding pivot and clear the other level.
Calculation: Structure direction = initialize from smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction; with structure locking enabled, change bullish to bearish only when the configured break test is below protected swing low minus break buffer times ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars, and change bearish to bullish only when it is above protected swing high plus break buffer times ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars; the break test uses close in close mode, or low for bearish breaks and high for bullish breaks in wick mode; otherwise retain direction. With structure locking disabled, follow smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction.
Calculation: Selected-timeframe directions = smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction calculated on bars of each enabled selected timeframe T1–T6 and sampled on chart bars, using the last completed timeframe bar or the still-forming timeframe bar according to the timeframe confirmation setting.
Calculation: Extra-timeframe direction = on bars of T7 and sampled on chart bars, use either EMA-slope-plus-price direction (bullish when close is above EMA(close, confirmation lookback) and the EMA is greater than its previous value; bearish when close is below the EMA and it is less than its previous value; neutral otherwise) or smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction according to the configured mode; use the last completed T7 bar or the still-forming T7 bar according to the confirmation setting.
Long: the displayed trend direction is bullish: cheat candle mode is enabled and either structure direction is used for candle direction and structure direction is bullish, or raw smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction is used and is bullish; or cheat candle mode is disabled and structure direction is bullish.
Short: the displayed trend direction is bearish: cheat candle mode is enabled and either structure direction is used for candle direction and structure direction is bearish, or raw smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction is used and is bearish; or cheat candle mode is disabled and structure direction is bearish.
Long: structure direction changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: structure direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: structure direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (no selected timeframe is enabled OR the number of enabled selected timeframes with bullish direction is at least the lesser of the agreement parameter and enabled timeframe count) AND (extra-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR T7 direction is bullish) AND (configured entry mode is reversal OR EMA(smoothed Heikin-Ashi close, slow baseline lookback) is greater than its previous value) AND (impulse filter is disabled OR absolute smoothed Heikin-Ashi close minus open is greater than its previous-bar value).
Short: structure direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (no selected timeframe is enabled OR the number of enabled selected timeframes with bearish direction is at least the lesser of the agreement parameter and enabled timeframe count) AND (extra-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR T7 direction is bearish) AND (configured entry mode is reversal OR EMA(smoothed Heikin-Ashi close, slow baseline lookback) is less than its previous value) AND (impulse filter is disabled OR absolute smoothed Heikin-Ashi close minus open is greater than its previous-bar value).
