Long: on T1 bars, close[i] > close[i+1] for each i from 0 to consecutive-bar count - 1 AND Slow Stochastic(high, low, close, prose stochastic lookback) < prose threshold
Short: on T1 bars, close[i] < close[i+1] for each i from 0 to consecutive-bar count - 1 AND Fast Stochastic(high, low, close, prose stochastic lookback) > prose threshold
Calculation: Reversal direction = 1 when close[3] < close[2] AND close[1] > close[2] AND fast stochastic < slow stochastic AND fast stochastic > reversal level; -1 when close[3] > close[2] AND close[1] < close[2] AND fast stochastic > slow stochastic AND fast stochastic < reversal level; otherwise its prior value (initially 0), where fast stochastic = SMA(Stochastic(close[1], high[1], low[1], reversal lookback), K smoothing) and slow stochastic = SMA(fast stochastic, D smoothing)
Calculation: Money-index direction = 1 when SMA(net, money-index lookback) > net; -1 when SMA(net, money-index lookback) < net; otherwise its prior value (initially 0), where net is evaluated on chart bars as prior net - (open - close) + (open(T2, last completed bar) - close(T2, last completed bar)), with initial prior net 1
Long: (reverse setting is false AND Reversal direction = 1 AND Money-index direction = 1) OR (reverse setting is true AND Reversal direction = -1 AND Money-index direction = -1)
Short: (reverse setting is false AND Reversal direction = -1 AND Money-index direction = -1) OR (reverse setting is true AND Reversal direction = 1 AND Money-index direction = 1)
Exit: long, NOT (Reversal direction = 1 AND Money-index direction = 1) AND NOT (Reversal direction = -1 AND Money-index direction = -1)
Exit: short, NOT (Reversal direction = 1 AND Money-index direction = 1) AND NOT (Reversal direction = -1 AND Money-index direction = -1)
