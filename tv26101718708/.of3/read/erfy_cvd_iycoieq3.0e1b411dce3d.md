Long: On confirmation of a pivot low using pivotlow(low, pivot strength, pivot strength), the current pivot low is lower than the immediately preceding confirmed pivot low in the current CVD reset period AND estimated CVD at the current pivot low is higher than estimated CVD at the immediately preceding confirmed pivot low.
