Calculation: Break(n, d, tf) = close crosses above (d = bull) or below (d = bear) the reference level; bull reference = latest Pivot high(high, n, n) not yet crossed, or the protected high while Trend(n, tf) is bear; bear reference = latest Pivot low(low, n, n) not yet crossed, or the protected low while Trend(n, tf) is bull; the break is CHoCH when the reference is protected, otherwise BOS; protected high = latest pivot high at the latest bear break; protected low = latest pivot low at the latest bull break; for tf = T1, computed on completed T1 bars and read once, at the first chart bar after each T1 bar completes
Calculation: Trend(n, tf) = bull after the latest Break(n, bull, tf), bear after the latest Break(n, bear, tf), none before any break; for tf = T1, the last completed T1 bar value is used on every chart bar
Calculation: Displacement(tf) = abs(close - open) >= displacement ATR multiple * ATR(atr length)[1] AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, tick size) >= minimum body ratio
Calculation: FVG(d, tf) = for bull: low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= FVG width ATR multiple * ATR(atr length)[1] AND close[1] > open[1]; for bear: high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= FVG width ATR multiple * ATR(atr length)[1] AND close[1] < open[1]
Calculation: OB(d, tf) = on a bar where Break(swing length, d, tf) occurs and Displacement(tf) holds with close > open for bull or close < open for bear, and, when FVG association is required, FVG(d, tf) occurred within the last FVG window bars including this bar and after the candidate; candidate = nearest earlier bar within the last OB search length bars with close < open for bull or close > open for bear; zone = candidate's high-to-low range, or open-to-close range when body bounds are set; candidate is rejected if any bar after it and before this bar traded below zone bottom for bull (above zone top for bear), or traded back into the zone after an earlier bar closed above zone top for bull (below zone bottom for bear), counting this bar; this bar must also close above zone top for bull (below zone bottom for bear) with low >= zone bottom for bull (high <= zone top for bear)
Calculation: Sweep(d) = for bull: low crosses below a swing low level and close is above that level; for bear: high crosses above a swing high level and close is below that level; swing levels are Pivot low(low, swing length, swing length) and Pivot high(high, swing length, swing length), not yet crossed and no older than liquidity expiry; swing lows within cluster tolerance * ATR(atr length) of each other merge into the lowest low (swing highs into the highest high); when session liquidity is on, completed selected-session lows (highs) also count as swing lows (highs)
Calculation: Chain(d) = stage 0 to 3: stage 1 on Sweep(d) when Sweep(opposite d) does not occur on that bar, recording that sweep bar; stage 2 on a later bar with Break(shift, d, chart) (shift = internal length or swing length per shift setting; CHoCH only when CHoCH-only setting is on), recording that shift bar; stage 3 on Displacement(chart) with close > open for bull or close < open for bear occurring on the shift bar or a later bar, recording that displacement bar; stage returns to 0 on Break(shift, opposite d, chart), on Sweep(opposite d), when more than sequence expiry bars have passed since the sweep bar, or when a setup in direction d is taken
Calculation: Retest(d) = on this bar, some zone Z that is OB(d, tf) or FVG(d, tf), with tf = chart or T1, satisfies: Z was formed on an earlier bar; Z is not invalidated (OB only: a close, or a wick when invalidation basis is wick, beyond its distal edge, below its bottom for bull or above its top for bear); Z is younger than chart zone expiry in chart bars (chart zones) or T1 zone expiry in T1 bars (T1 zones); no earlier bar reached Z's retire point (touch, midpoint or full fill, per setting); this bar overlaps Z (low <= Z top and high >= Z bottom) and the overlap run it belongs to has index within the encounter limit (1 when first-only), with no setup from Z taken in that run; this bar closes beyond Z's near edge (close > Z top for bull, close < Z bottom for bear) with close > open for bull or close < open for bear; and this bar does not breach Z's distal edge (low >= Z bottom for bull, high <= Z top for bear)
Long: Retest(bull) AND (sequence requirement off OR (Chain(bull) = 3 AND the retested zone was formed on or after Chain(bull)'s shift bar AND this bar is after Chain(bull)'s displacement bar)) AND Trend(swing length, chart) = bull AND (premium/discount filter off OR (close <= (latest swing high + latest swing low) / 2 AND close >= latest swing low AND latest swing high > latest swing low)) AND (T1 agreement off OR Trend(swing length, T1) = bull) AND (session filter off OR bar lies in a selected session window) AND bars since last setup > cooldown AND no setup already taken earlier on this bar
Short: Retest(bear) AND (sequence requirement off OR (Chain(bear) = 3 AND the retested zone was formed on or after Chain(bear)'s shift bar AND this bar is after Chain(bear)'s displacement bar)) AND Trend(swing length, chart) = bear AND (premium/discount filter off OR (close >= (latest swing high + latest swing low) / 2 AND close <= latest swing high AND latest swing high > latest swing low)) AND (T1 agreement off OR Trend(swing length, T1) = bear) AND (session filter off OR bar lies in a selected session window) AND bars since last setup > cooldown AND no setup already taken earlier on this bar
Long: Break(swing length, bull, chart) OR Break(internal length, bull, chart) OR Break(swing length, bull, T1), where internal length < swing length
Short: Break(swing length, bear, chart) OR Break(internal length, bear, chart) OR Break(swing length, bear, T1), where internal length < swing length
Long: Sweep(bull)
Short: Sweep(bear)
Long: OB(bull, chart) OR FVG(bull, chart) forming on this bar, OR OB(bull, T1) OR FVG(bull, T1) forming on a completed T1 bar and read on the first chart bar after it completes
Short: OB(bear, chart) OR FVG(bear, chart) forming on this bar, OR OB(bear, T1) OR FVG(bear, T1) forming on a completed T1 bar and read on the first chart bar after it completes
Long: Trend(swing length, chart) = bull
Short: Trend(swing length, chart) = bear
