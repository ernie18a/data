Calculation: RSI price band(n, q) = p = 2*n - 1; gains = EMA(max(close - close[1], 0), p); losses = EMA(max(close[1] - close, 0), p); x = (n - 1) * (losses * q / (100 - q) - gains); if x >= 0, close + x, otherwise close + x * (100 - q) / q
Calculation: Reference trend = on chart bars, bullish when the external reference close is above its EMA on the last completed bars of both T1 and T2, bearish when below both; calculate each EMA over bars of its own timeframe using the same EMA lookback, and use those completed-bar values on chart bars
Calculation: Initial stop = fixed at entry; for Grand positions, the minimum low (long) or maximum high (short) over the prior stop-lookback chart bars; for Trend positions, entry price minus (long) or plus (short) the trend stop multiplier times RMA ATR at entry using the trend ATR lookback
Calculation: Long trailing stop = at partial TP1, close minus trailing ATR multiplier times ATR(trailing ATR lookback, configured smoothing); on each later chart bar before exit checks, the greater of the prior trailing stop and close minus that ATR distance
Calculation: Short trailing stop = at partial TP1, close plus trailing ATR multiplier times ATR(trailing ATR lookback, configured smoothing); on each later chart bar before exit checks, the lesser of the prior trailing stop and close plus that ATR distance
Long: Flat AND the number of bullish reversal signals from Pivot Point SuperTrend (shared left/right pivot lookback, ATR factor, ATR lookback), Supertrend (HL2 source, ATR lookback, multiplier), close-based Chandelier Exit (ATR lookback, multiplier), and Twin Range Filter (close source, separate first and second periods and range multipliers) on the current and preceding chart bars in the signal window is at least the minimum signal count AND at least one such signal occurs on the current chart bar AND (Grand RSI-touch filter disabled OR low <= the lower entry RSI band on at least one bar in the RSI-touch lookback) AND (Grand midpoint filter disabled OR current high < the average of the upper and lower entry RSI bands) AND (Grand WaveTrend filter disabled OR the WaveTrend first line is <= the higher oversold threshold on at least one bar in the WaveTrend lookback) AND (AutoTrend disabled OR Grand AutoTrend filtering disabled OR Reference trend is bullish)
Short: Flat AND the number of bearish reversal signals from Pivot Point SuperTrend (shared left/right pivot lookback, ATR factor, ATR lookback), Supertrend (HL2 source, ATR lookback, multiplier), close-based Chandelier Exit (ATR lookback, multiplier), and Twin Range Filter (close source, separate first and second periods and range multipliers) on the current and preceding chart bars in the signal window is at least the minimum signal count AND at least one such signal occurs on the current chart bar AND (Grand RSI-touch filter disabled OR high >= the upper entry RSI band on at least one bar in the RSI-touch lookback) AND (Grand midpoint filter disabled OR current low > the average of the upper and lower entry RSI bands) AND (Grand WaveTrend filter disabled OR the WaveTrend first line is >= the lower overbought threshold on at least one bar in the WaveTrend lookback) AND (AutoTrend disabled OR Grand AutoTrend filtering disabled OR Reference trend is bearish)
Long: Flat AND Trend long signals enabled AND (AutoTrend disabled OR Reference trend is bullish) AND (symbol EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, symbol EMA lookback)) AND low <= RSI price band(entry lookback, oversold threshold) AND (Trend WaveTrend filter disabled OR the WaveTrend first line is <= the higher oversold threshold on at least one of the current or preceding bars in the Trend WaveTrend lookback) AND no Grand long signal occurs on this chart bar
Short: Flat AND Trend short signals enabled AND (AutoTrend disabled OR Reference trend is bearish) AND (symbol EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, symbol EMA lookback)) AND high >= RSI price band(entry lookback, overbought threshold) AND (Trend WaveTrend filter disabled OR the WaveTrend first line is >= the lower overbought threshold on at least one of the current or preceding bars in the Trend WaveTrend lookback) AND no Grand short signal occurs on this chart bar
Exit: Long, while open before TP1, the configured stop-trigger field (low or close) <= the fixed initial stop; OR after partial TP1, the configured stop-trigger field <= entry price
Exit: Short, while open before TP1, the configured stop-trigger field (high or close) >= the fixed initial stop; OR after partial TP1, the configured stop-trigger field >= entry price
Exit: Long, before TP1, the configured TP1-trigger field (high or close) >= RSI price band(TP lookback, overbought threshold) AND the configured stop-trigger field > the fixed initial stop
Exit: Short, before TP1, the configured TP1-trigger field (low or close) <= RSI price band(TP lookback, oversold threshold) AND the configured stop-trigger field < the fixed initial stop
Exit: Long, on later chart bars after partial TP1, the configured trailing-stop trigger field (low or close) <= the long trailing stop AND the configured stop-trigger field > entry price
Exit: Short, on later chart bars after partial TP1, the configured trailing-stop trigger field (high or close) >= the short trailing stop AND the configured stop-trigger field < entry price
