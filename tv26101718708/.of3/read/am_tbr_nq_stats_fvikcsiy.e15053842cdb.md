Calculation: TBR open = open of the chart bar beginning the session
Calculation: TBR distance = TBR open × touch multiplier × sample standard deviation(100 × (close − close[1]) / close[1], lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) / 100
Long: On a chart bar whose open time is in the session, if TBR distance > 0 and neither level was touched earlier this session, the first touch is the lower level (low ≤ TBR open − TBR distance); if the same bar also touches the upper level, select the lower level only when the upper level − bar open > bar open − lower level. Scan available T2 bars in chronological order within each chart bar, including any forming T2 bar; if T2 data is unavailable, apply this rule to chart bars. A simultaneous-touch tie selects the upper level.
Short: On a chart bar whose open time is in the session, if TBR distance > 0 and neither level was touched earlier this session, the first touch is the upper level (high ≥ TBR open + TBR distance); if the same bar also touches the lower level, select the upper level when the upper level − bar open ≤ bar open − lower level. Scan available T2 bars in chronological order within each chart bar, including any forming T2 bar; if T2 data is unavailable, apply this rule to chart bars. A simultaneous-touch tie selects the upper level.
Exit: long, after a lower-level touch and on a chart bar whose open time is in the session, high ≥ TBR open on a T2 bar at or after the touching T2 bar; if the touch was classified from chart bars because T2 data was unavailable, require high ≥ TBR open on a later chart bar.
Exit: short, after an upper-level touch and on a chart bar whose open time is in the session, low ≤ TBR open on a T2 bar at or after the touching T2 bar; if the touch was classified from chart bars because T2 data was unavailable, require low ≤ TBR open on a later chart bar.
