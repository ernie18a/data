Long: long trades enabled AND position is flat AND Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback) crosses above SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback), D smoothing lookback) AND ((All must be OB/OS is off AND at least one Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback)[n] < oversold threshold for n = 0 through OB/OS lookback) OR (All must be OB/OS is on AND Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback)[OB/OS lookback] < oversold threshold)) AND EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), with fast lookback < slow lookback, AND close < EMA(close, fast lookback) AND close >= EMA(close, slow lookback) AND (reference-market filter is off OR (reference-market close on T1 > EMA(close, reference EMA lookback) on T1 on the current chart-bar sample OR the same comparison on the previous chart-bar sample)); calculate the reference-market close and EMA on T1 bars, use the last completed T1 bar while it is forming and the current T1 bar once confirmed, then sample onto chart bars.
Short: short trades enabled AND position is flat AND Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback) crosses below SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback), D smoothing lookback) AND ((All must be OB/OS is off AND at least one Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback)[n] > overbought threshold for n = 0 through OB/OS lookback) OR (All must be OB/OS is on AND Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback)[OB/OS lookback] > overbought threshold)) AND EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback), with fast lookback < slow lookback, AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback) AND close <= EMA(close, slow lookback) AND (reference-market filter is off OR (reference-market close on T1 < EMA(close, reference EMA lookback) on T1 on the current chart-bar sample OR the same comparison on the previous chart-bar sample)); calculate the reference-market close and EMA on T1 bars, use the last completed T1 bar while it is forming and the current T1 bar once confirmed, then sample onto chart bars.
Long: EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), with fast lookback < slow lookback, AND Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback) <= oversold threshold.
Short: EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback), with fast lookback < slow lookback, AND Stochastic(close, high, low, K lookback) >= overbought threshold.
Exit: long, low <= entry-signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier OR high >= entry-signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier × risk/reward ratio.
Exit: short, high >= entry-signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier OR low <= entry-signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier × risk/reward ratio.
