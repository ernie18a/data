Calculation: T-frame zones = On bars of each enabled T1/T2/T3, a bullish zone is created when close > open, close > highest(high[1], impulse lookback), and either abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement ATR multiplier * zone-mode displacement factor * zone-mode body factor or high - low >= ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement ATR multiplier * zone-mode displacement factor * zone-mode range factor; a bearish zone uses the reverse comparisons. The mode factors and search lookback are determined by the same zone-mode selection (strict increases displacement and reduces search, normal uses the base values, aggressive decreases displacement and increases search). For a bullish zone, search prior bars from nearest to farthest: use up to the cluster limit of bars with close <= open when clustering is enabled, otherwise the nearest bar with close < open; for a bearish zone use close >= open when clustering is enabled, otherwise the nearest bar with close > open. In body range mode, bullish bounds are the maximum open and minimum close, and bearish bounds are the maximum close and minimum open; in wick range mode use maximum high and minimum low. Create a zone only when its top > bottom; retain it until the selected invalidation condition is met (bullish close below bottom or low below bottom; bearish close above top or high above top). Detection runs on T bars; chart bars use the returned values with lookahead off (last completed T bar historically and the still-forming T bar in realtime).
Calculation: Active bullish zone = any stored bullish zone from an enabled T timeframe whose bounds differ from the last emitted pair for that timeframe and direction, and which has not been invalidated on chart bars by the selected close-or-wick condition; the current chart bar overlaps it when high >= bottom and low <= top. Active bearish zone is defined symmetrically.
Long: a new valid bullish zone is emitted on an enabled T timeframe OR low < lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) AND close > open AND the current bar overlaps an active bullish zone OR close > high[1] AND close > open AND the current bar overlaps an active bullish zone AND an unconfirmed low sweep in an active bullish zone occurred 1 to confirmation-window bars earlier
Short: a new valid bearish zone is emitted on an enabled T timeframe OR high > highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < open AND the current bar overlaps an active bearish zone OR close < low[1] AND close < open AND the current bar overlaps an active bearish zone AND an unconfirmed high sweep in an active bearish zone occurred 1 to confirmation-window bars earlier
