Calculation: Bearish EMA stack(T) = EMA(close, fast lookback, T) < EMA(close, mid lookback, T) AND EMA(close, mid lookback, T) < EMA(close, slow lookback, T)
Calculation: Requested T1/T2 values = EMA(close, each EMA lookback) and close computed on T1/T2 bars, and VWAP(hlc3) computed on T2 bars, returned on chart bars using request.security timing (last completed source bar historically; still-forming source bar in real time); T1 is longer than T2
Calculation: T1 bullish bias = bullish EMA stack(T1) AND close(T1) > EMA(close, mid lookback, T1)
Calculation: T1 bearish bias = bearish EMA stack(T1) AND close(T1) < EMA(close, mid lookback, T1)
Calculation: T2 bullish alignment = bullish EMA stack(T2) AND close(T2) > VWAP(hlc3, T2)
Calculation: T2 bearish alignment = bearish EMA stack(T2) AND close(T2) < VWAP(hlc3, T2)
Calculation: Long trigger = ((cross trigger enabled AND EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses above VWAP(hlc3)) OR (pullback trigger enabled AND bullish EMA stack(chart bars) AND close > VWAP(hlc3) AND low <= EMA(close, fast lookback) AND close > open)) AND (EMA slope requirement disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback) > its prior chart-bar value)
Calculation: Short trigger = ((cross trigger enabled AND EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses below VWAP(hlc3)) OR (pullback trigger enabled AND bearish EMA stack(chart bars) AND close < VWAP(hlc3) AND high >= EMA(close, fast lookback) AND close < open)) AND (EMA slope requirement disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback) < its prior chart-bar value)
Calculation: Long structure break = high > Highest(high[prior bar], swing lookback) OR (a confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot-left lookback, pivot-right lookback) exists AND close > the most recently confirmed pivot high) OR (sweep confirmation enabled AND low < Lowest(low[prior bar], sweep-confirmation lookback) AND close > open)
Calculation: Short structure break = low < Lowest(low[prior bar], swing lookback) OR (a confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot-left lookback, pivot-right lookback) exists AND close < the most recently confirmed pivot low) OR (sweep confirmation enabled AND high > Highest(high[prior bar], sweep-confirmation lookback) AND close < open)
Calculation: Long score = core score weight times the count of true conditions among T1 bullish bias, bullish EMA stack(chart bars), close > VWAP(hlc3), and long trigger, plus bonus score weight times the count of true conditions among long structure break and (T2 bullish alignment AND bullish EMA stack(chart bars) AND close > VWAP(hlc3))
Calculation: Short score = core score weight times the count of true conditions among T1 bearish bias, bearish EMA stack(chart bars), close < VWAP(hlc3), and short trigger, plus bonus score weight times the count of true conditions among short structure break and (T2 bearish alignment AND bearish EMA stack(chart bars) AND close < VWAP(hlc3))
Long: inside configured active session AND ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum ATR AND high - low <= SMA(high - low, average-candle lookback) * maximum candle multiple AND 0 < close - Lowest(low, swing lookback including current bar) <= maximum stop distance AND long trigger AND (long score >= minimum score threshold OR (C trades enabled AND long score < grade-B cutoff)) AND no long signal in the previous cooldown-length chart bars
Short: inside configured active session AND ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum ATR AND high - low <= SMA(high - low, average-candle lookback) * maximum candle multiple AND 0 < Highest(high, swing lookback including current bar) - close <= maximum stop distance AND short trigger AND (short score >= minimum score threshold OR (C trades enabled AND short score < grade-B cutoff)) AND no short signal in the previous cooldown-length chart bars
