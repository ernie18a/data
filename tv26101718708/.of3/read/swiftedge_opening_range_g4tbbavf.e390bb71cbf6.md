Calculation: Opening-range bounds = (maximum high, minimum low) over the configured opening interval of each enabled session, frozen when the first bar opens after the interval
Calculation: Configured target price(side, entry, initial risk, range bounds, range size) = entry + side × configured R multiple × initial risk; entry + side × configured ATR multiple × ATR(lookback); range high + configured extension × range size for long or range low − configured extension × range size for short; entry + side × configured points; entry × (1 + side × configured percentage / 100); the opposite range edge; or the range midpoint, according to target type; undefined unless beyond entry, and undefined for a session-end target
Calculation: Breakout initial stop(side, entry, range bounds, range size, breakout-bar extreme) = opposite range edge; range midpoint; entry − side × configured ATR multiple × ATR(lookback); entry − side × configured range multiple × range size; or breakout-bar extreme − side × minimum tick, according to stop type
Calculation: Fade initial stop(side, entry, breakout extreme) = breakout extreme − side × configured ATR multiple × ATR(lookback), or entry − side × configured ATR multiple × ATR(lookback), according to stop type
Calculation: Active stop for long = maximum of the initial stop, prior active stop, entry price after an enabled breakeven trigger, and prior close − configured trailing distance while trailing is active; for short, use the minimum and prior close + trailing distance. Breakeven triggers after a partial target 1 when enabled or after a close moves the configured R multiple of initial risk in favor; trailing distance is a configured ATR or range multiple and may be restricted to after target 1
Long: On a confirmed bar during an enabled session, after its opening range completes and before its first breakout, close > opening-range high + configured buffer × ATR(lookback) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR SMA(volume, lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, lookback) >= configured minimum relative volume)
Short: On a confirmed bar during an enabled session, after its opening range completes and before its first breakout, close < opening-range low − configured buffer × ATR(lookback) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR SMA(volume, lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, lookback) >= configured minimum relative volume)
Long: An upward breakout has no close below the opening-range high during the configured fake-out window and is classified as held when that window passes
Short: A downward breakout has no close above the opening-range low during the configured fake-out window and is classified as held when that window passes
Long: Breakout trading is enabled, entry mode is bar close, the upward breakout condition is met, no position is open, no breakout trade has been taken for the session, and the breakout initial stop is below entry
Short: Breakout trading is enabled, entry mode is bar close, the downward breakout condition is met, no position is open, no breakout trade has been taken for the session, and the breakout initial stop is above entry
Long: Breakout trading is enabled, entry mode is stop order at the range edge, the opening range is complete, no breakout has occurred, no position is open, no breakout trade has been taken for the session, high >= opening-range high + configured buffer × ATR(lookback), low > opening-range low − configured buffer × ATR(lookback), and the breakout initial stop is below entry
Short: Breakout trading is enabled, entry mode is stop order at the range edge, the opening range is complete, no breakout has occurred, no position is open, no breakout trade has been taken for the session, low <= opening-range low − configured buffer × ATR(lookback), high < opening-range high + configured buffer × ATR(lookback), and the breakout initial stop is above entry
Long: Breakout trading is enabled, entry mode is retest, a qualifying upward breakout occurred, a later bar is within the configured retest window, low <= opening-range high, no position is open, no breakout trade has been taken for the session, and the breakout initial stop is below entry
Short: Breakout trading is enabled, entry mode is retest, a qualifying downward breakout occurred, a later bar is within the configured retest window, high >= opening-range low, no position is open, no breakout trade has been taken for the session, and the breakout initial stop is above entry
Long: Fade trading is enabled, a downward breakout closes above the opening-range low within the configured fake-out window, no position is open, no fade trade has been taken for the session, the fade initial stop is below entry, and the configured fade target price is valid
Short: Fade trading is enabled, an upward breakout closes below the opening-range high within the configured fake-out window, no position is open, no fade trade has been taken for the session, the fade initial stop is above entry, and the configured fade target price is valid
Exit: Long, low <= active stop (a stop takes precedence over a target) OR, from the bar after entry, high >= configured breakout target 1 when it closes all remaining size OR high >= configured breakout target 2 after a partial target 1 OR high >= configured fade target OR an enabled fake-out exit occurs when an upward breakout closes below the opening-range high within its fake-out window OR the configured maximum holding time is reached at the bar close OR the configured session ends
Exit: Short, high >= active stop (a stop takes precedence over a target) OR, from the bar after entry, low <= configured breakout target 1 when it closes all remaining size OR low <= configured breakout target 2 after a partial target 1 OR low <= configured fade target OR an enabled fake-out exit occurs when a downward breakout closes above the opening-range low within its fake-out window OR the configured maximum holding time is reached at the bar close OR the configured session ends
