Calculation: Signal average(source, lookback, type, delay, ALMA offset, ALMA sigma) = TEMA, Hull moving average, or ALMA of source[delay], selected by type; use the same parameters for open and close, with raw or Heikin-Ashi sources as configured; when alternate resolution is enabled, calculate on T1 bars and carry each last completed T1 value onto chart bars, otherwise calculate on the current chart bar
Calculation: ATR average = SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Bullish T2 trend = EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > the configured EMA or SMA(close, baseline lookback), calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and carried onto chart bars
Calculation: Bearish T2 trend = EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < the configured EMA or SMA(close, baseline lookback), calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and carried onto chart bars
Long: Signal average(close) crosses above Signal average(open) on chart bars AND long trading is enabled AND (session filter is disabled OR the chart bar is within the configured session) AND ATR(ATR lookback) > ATR average * minimum ATR multiple AND ATR(ATR lookback) < ATR average * maximum ATR multiple AND (HTF trend filter is disabled OR Bullish T2 trend) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) >= ADX threshold) AND (cooldown is disabled OR chart bars since the latest transition to flat > cooldown length) AND (one-trade-per-leg is disabled OR no long entry has occurred since the latest bullish T2 trend flip, or since initialization if reset on flip is disabled)
Short: Signal average(close) crosses below Signal average(open) on chart bars AND short trading is enabled AND (session filter is disabled OR the chart bar is within the configured session) AND ATR(ATR lookback) > ATR average * minimum ATR multiple AND ATR(ATR lookback) < ATR average * maximum ATR multiple AND (HTF trend filter is disabled OR Bearish T2 trend) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) >= ADX threshold) AND (cooldown is disabled OR chart bars since the latest transition to flat > cooldown length) AND (one-trade-per-leg is disabled OR no short entry has occurred since the latest bearish T2 trend flip, or since initialization if reset on flip is disabled)
Exit: long, high >= (ATR mode ? entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * TP1 ATR multiple : entry price * (1 + TP1 percent / 100))
Exit: long, high >= (ATR mode ? entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * TP2 ATR multiple : entry price * (1 + TP2 percent / 100))
Exit: long, high >= (ATR mode ? entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * TP3 ATR multiple : entry price * (1 + TP3 percent / 100))
Exit: long, low <= (entry price if break-even is enabled and the long TP1 target has been reached since entry, otherwise ATR mode ? entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * stop ATR multiple : entry price * (1 - stop-loss percent / 100))
Exit: short, low <= (ATR mode ? entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * TP1 ATR multiple : entry price * (1 - TP1 percent / 100))
Exit: short, low <= (ATR mode ? entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * TP2 ATR multiple : entry price * (1 - TP2 percent / 100))
Exit: short, low <= (ATR mode ? entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * TP3 ATR multiple : entry price * (1 - TP3 percent / 100))
Exit: short, high >= (entry price if break-even is enabled and the short TP1 target has been reached since entry, otherwise ATR mode ? entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry * stop ATR multiple : entry price * (1 + stop-loss percent / 100))
