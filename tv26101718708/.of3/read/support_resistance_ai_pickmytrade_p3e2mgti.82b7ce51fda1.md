Calculation: Zone tests = On each confirmed chart bar with ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, add resistance/support zones from confirmed PivotHigh(high, left lookback, right lookback) and PivotLow(low, left lookback, right lookback); each pivot confirms after the right lookback and creates a zone born on the confirmation bar, centered on the pivot price, with half-width ATR × zone-width multiplier / 2, strength 1, and armed. Merge a pivot into the first unbroken same-role zone in creation order whose midpoint is within ATR × merge-distance multiplier; increment strength and, unless testing, recenter to the strength-weighted mean and reset width using current ATR. Otherwise create a zone. Process zones newest-first. On each bar overlapping a zone (low <= top and high >= bottom), increment touches; an armed, non-testing zone opens a test and disarms. A decisive break is close > resistance top + ATR × break-buffer multiplier or close < support bottom - ATR × break-buffer multiplier, with no cooldown before the first flip and at least the flip-cooldown bars since birth thereafter. A test resolves on decisive break, hold, or timeout, in that priority; hold is close < resistance bottom - ATR × rejection-distance multiplier or close > support top + ATR × rejection-distance multiplier. Train a break outcome for a decisive break; for a hold condition train hold; at timeout train break if resistance close > top or support close < bottom, otherwise hold. The first decisive break flips the role, resets birth bar and resolved-test count, and rearms only if price has fully left on the new approach side; the next decisive break retires the zone. A disarmed non-testing zone rearms only after price fully leaves on its approach side (high < bottom for resistance, low > top for support). After processing, while live zones exceed the live-zone cap, remove the non-testing live zone with the lowest strength-weight × strength + touches score; ties remove the earliest-created zone.
Calculation: Hold probability = At each test opening, store features [clamp(role-signed (close - close[approach lookback]) / (ATR × sqrt(approach lookback)), ±velocity clip bound), relative volume, min(prior resolved-test count, test-count cap), min(log(1 + current bar - birth bar), log-age cap), clamp(role-signed (close - EMA(close, trend lookback)) / ATR, ±stretch clip bound), min(zone width / ATR, width cap), min(strength, strength cap), min(touches, touch-count cap)]. Role-signed means positive for resistance and negative for support. Use zero for unavailable velocity or stretch; relative volume is 1 if volume or its SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback) is unavailable or the SMA is not positive, otherwise min(volume / SMA, relative-volume cap). Train online Gaussian naive Bayes on each resolved test and its stored features, with hold and break as classes; per class and feature use the sample mean and variance, the single-sample fallback variance when fewer than two samples exist, and a variance floor. Use class priors proportional to max(class count, prior pseudocount); normalize prior × product of feature likelihoods to obtain hold probability. It is available only after total resolved tests reach the warmup-sample parameter. Process resolutions immediately in zone order, so later tests on the same bar use earlier resolutions.
Long: A new support test opens with available hold probability >= conviction threshold OR a new resistance test opens with available hold probability <= 1 - conviction threshold OR a live tested support zone closes outside its band with hold probability >= conviction threshold OR a live tested resistance zone closes outside its band with hold probability <= 1 - conviction threshold.
Short: A new resistance test opens with available hold probability >= conviction threshold OR a new support test opens with available hold probability <= 1 - conviction threshold OR a live tested resistance zone closes outside its band with hold probability >= conviction threshold OR a live tested support zone closes outside its band with hold probability <= 1 - conviction threshold.
