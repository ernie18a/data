Calculation: safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum price increment), treating missing ATR as zero
Calculation: relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), using a baseline of one when the SMA is missing or nonpositive, and one when volume is missing
Calculation: body efficiency = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: eligible resistance = track confirmed pivot highs requiring pivot-strength bars on each side; on each pivot, reset the level to that pivot and touches to one if no level exists or its distance from the prior level exceeds level-match tolerance times max(ATR at the pivot, using current safe ATR if missing, minimum price increment); otherwise update the touch-count-weighted mean and increment touches; age from the pivot bar; eligible when touches meet minimum level touches and age is at most maximum level age; clear after a confirmed bullish breakout
Calculation: eligible support = track confirmed pivot lows requiring pivot-strength bars on each side; on each pivot, reset the level to that pivot and touches to one if no level exists or its distance from the prior level exceeds level-match tolerance times max(ATR at the pivot, using current safe ATR if missing, minimum price increment); otherwise update the touch-count-weighted mean and increment touches; age from the pivot bar; eligible when touches meet minimum level touches and age is at most maximum level age; clear after a confirmed bearish breakout
Calculation: breakout score = weighted sum of clamped volume, body-efficiency, directional-close, breakout-distance, range-expansion, and range-structure scores; use relative volume above its score baseline, body efficiency relative to minimum body efficiency, directional close location above its neutral baseline, distance beyond the eligible level in safe ATR units, and bar range in safe ATR units; for structure, use the clamped preferred-range-width minus (eligible resistance - eligible support) / safe ATR relative to preferred range width when both levels are eligible and resistance exceeds support, otherwise use the neutral structure score; each component is clamped to zero through one before applying its separate score weight, with score denominators floored by the denominator floor
Calculation: bullish breakout attempt = confirmed chart bar AND eligible resistance AND more than the event-cooldown bars since the prior breakout event AND close > eligible resistance + break buffer ATR * safe ATR AND close[1] <= eligible resistance + break buffer ATR * safe ATR
Calculation: bearish breakout attempt = confirmed chart bar AND eligible support AND more than the event-cooldown bars since the prior breakout event AND close < eligible support - break buffer ATR * safe ATR AND close[1] >= eligible support - break buffer ATR * safe ATR
Calculation: build-up width in ATR = (highest high over build-up lookback - lowest low over build-up lookback) / safe ATR
