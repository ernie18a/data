Calculation: Bar body = abs(close - open); bar range = high - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; body fraction = bar range > 0 ? bar body / bar range : 0.
Calculation: Volatility-scaled ATR = ATR(short ATR lookback) * clamp(ATR(short ATR lookback) / ATR(long ATR lookback), lower volatility clamp, upper volatility clamp); use 1 when the long ATR is zero.
Calculation: Wavelet bands = recursively apply the stationary wavelet transform to close at four successive levels using the selected wavelet family; smooth each approximation with EMA using the smoothing lookback multiplied by its level; leave detail bands unsmoothed.
Calculation: Wavelet alignment = +1 when approximation 1 > approximation 2 > approximation 3 > approximation 4; -1 when all inequalities are reversed; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Wavelet strength = [weight 1 * (approximation 1 - its prior-bar value) + weight 2 * (approximation 2 - its value at its lag) + weight 3 * (approximation 3 - its value at its lag) + weight 4 * (approximation 4 - its value at its lag)] / ATR(short ATR lookback).
Calculation: Normalized wavelet energy = sum of the squared ratios of each detail band to ATR(short ATR lookback); its moving average is SMA(normalized wavelet energy, energy lookback).
Calculation: Hurst estimate = clamp the slope of the least-squares fit of ln(max(abs(approximation 2 - its value at each scale lag), epsilon)) against ln(each scale lag), across the configured scale lags, to [0, 1]. Regime is trending above the upper Hurst threshold, mean reverting below the lower Hurst threshold, and random otherwise.
Calculation: Recent swing high and low = latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low using the pivot-length parameter on both sides; previous swing high and low = the preceding confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Delta ratio = native footprint delta / native footprint buy-plus-sell volume when native footprint data is available; otherwise, use the weighted sum of estimated delta from T1 and T2 divided by chart-bar volume. Each estimate is volume * [2 * (close - low) / max(high - low, epsilon) - 1]. Chart bars use the latest returned T1 and T2 values from lookahead-off requests; the latest sub-bar may still be forming in real time and is completed historically.
Calculation: Footprint absorption = abs(native footprint delta / native footprint buy-plus-sell volume) when native footprint delta opposes the chart candle direction; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Structural proximity = proximity to either MSL siege corridor, MSL POC, VAH, VAL, latest swing high, or latest swing low. Siege proximity uses the MSL engine; POC proximity is abs(close - POC) <= ATR(short ATR lookback) * POC distance parameter; VAH and VAL use their separate ATR distance parameter; swing levels use their separate ATR distance parameter. POC and value-area checks require MSL enabled; swing-level checks do not.
Calculation: Continuation score(side) = sum of these eight Boolean points: close above the long-trend SMA for bullish side or below it for bearish side; wavelet strength positive for bullish side or negative for bearish side; volume above its short-volume SMA; when Lorentzian is enabled and confidence exceeds its minimum parameter, prediction has the side's sign; when RCM is enabled, RCM permits the side; when MSL is enabled, structural proximity is true; delta ratio exceeds its positive threshold for bullish side or is below its negative threshold for bearish side; MACD line is above its signal line for bullish side or below it for bearish side.
Calculation: Reversal score(side) = the same eight-point score, replacing the long-trend SMA point with RSI below its oversold threshold for bullish side or above its overbought threshold for bearish side.
Calculation: Lorentzian prediction and confidence = construct current and historical 13-feature vectors from normalized RSI, log relative volume, wavelet strength, normalized Hurst estimate, ATR / close, relative wavelet energy, wavelet alignment, MACD line / ATR, delta ratio, normalized footprint POC distance, footprint value-area position, footprint absorption, and RCM integrity. Use the configured historical outcome delay to label each stored feature +1 or -1 when its later close return from the stored entry close exceeds the positive or negative outcome-return threshold, otherwise 0. Compare labelled features with similarity 1 / (1 + weighted squared feature distance / adaptive gamma squared), retain the top K similarities above the minimum similarity parameter from the configured memory limit, and return their similarity-weighted outcome mean and mean similarity. Adaptive gamma is the gamma parameter multiplied by an energy factor, a Hurst-regime factor, an RCM-conviction factor when RCM is enabled, and (1 + volatility multiplier * ATR / close); RCM conviction is integrity multiplied by the damage factor when structural damage exists and by 1 otherwise. Use separate configurable distance weights for the wavelet energy, wavelet alignment, delta ratio, value-area position, absorption, and RCM-integrity features; other feature weights are 1.
Calculation: Original pattern candidate = when original patterns are enabled, apply these tests in order and return the matching direction and its continuation or reversal score: (1) close > close[1] > close[2] and volume > short-volume SMA, bullish continuation; (2) close < close[1] < close[2] and volume > short-volume SMA, bearish continuation; (3) if Highest(high, compression lookback) - Lowest(low, compression lookback) < [Highest(high, wide-range lookback) - Lowest(low, wide-range lookback)] * compression ratio, return bullish continuation if close > short-pattern SMA and close > open, else bearish continuation if close < short-pattern SMA and close < open; entering this compression branch prevents later original-pattern tests even if neither flag matches; (4) low <= Lowest(low, pattern-extreme lookback) and close > close[1], bullish reversal; (5) high >= Highest(high, pattern-extreme lookback) and close < close[1], bearish reversal; (6) abs(low - low[double-pattern lag]) < ATR(short ATR lookback) * double-pattern tolerance and close > close[reversal-confirmation lag], bullish reversal; (7) abs(high - high[double-pattern lag]) < ATR(short ATR lookback) * double-pattern tolerance and close < close[reversal-confirmation lag], bearish reversal; (8) close > Highest(high, pattern-extreme lookback)[1] and volume > short-volume SMA * breakout-volume ratio, bullish continuation; (9) close < Lowest(low, pattern-extreme lookback)[1] and volume > short-volume SMA * breakout-volume ratio, bearish continuation; (10) wavelet alignment positive and close > approximation 2, bullish continuation; (11) wavelet alignment negative and close < approximation 2, bearish continuation; (12) normalized wavelet energy > its moving average * original-energy ratio and close > open, bullish continuation; (13) the same energy condition and close < open, bearish continuation.
Calculation: Candle pattern candidate = when candle patterns are enabled, apply these tests in order and return the matching direction and score: bullish absorption when body fraction < absorption body threshold, lower wick > bar body * absorption wick ratio, volume > short-volume SMA * absorption volume ratio, and close > open; bearish absorption with upper wick and close < open instead; bearish exhaustion when upper wick > bar body * exhaustion wick ratio, high >= Highest(high, pattern-extreme lookback), and close < (high + low) / 2; bullish exhaustion when lower wick exceeds that ratio, low <= Lowest(low, pattern-extreme lookback), and close > the midpoint; bullish pin bar when lower wick > bar body * pin-wick ratio, upper wick < bar body * opposite-wick ratio, and close > open; bearish pin bar with wicks and candle direction reversed; bullish engulfing when close > open, prior close < prior open, close > prior open, open < prior close, and current body > prior body; bearish engulfing with all directions reversed; bullish or bearish marubozu when body fraction > marubozu body threshold, bar range > volatility-scaled ATR * marubozu range ratio, and candle direction matches; at structural proximity with body fraction < doji threshold, return bearish reversal when close > close[doji confirmation lag], otherwise bullish reversal.
Calculation: Structure pattern candidate = when structure patterns are enabled, apply these tests in order and return the matching direction and score: bullish BOS when a swing high exists, close > swing high, close > close[1], and volume > short-volume SMA; bearish BOS when a swing low exists, close < swing low, close < close[1], and volume > short-volume SMA; bearish CHoCH when swing low and previous swing high exist, close < swing low, and latest swing high < previous swing high; bullish CHoCH when swing high and previous swing low exist, close > swing high, and latest swing low > previous swing low; bearish liquidity sweep when high > swing high, close < swing high, and close < open; bullish liquidity sweep when low < swing low, close > swing low, and close > open; bullish order-block reclaim when MSL is enabled, close is near VAL or the support siege corridor, close > open, and lower wick > bar body; bearish order-block reclaim when MSL is enabled, close is near VAH or the resistance siege corridor, close < open, and upper wick > bar body.
Calculation: VSA pattern candidate = when VSA patterns are enabled, apply these tests in order and return the matching direction and score: narrow range (bar range < volatility-scaled ATR * narrow-range ratio), declining volume (volume < short-volume SMA * declining-volume ratio), close > open and close < short-pattern SMA, bearish continuation; same narrow-range and declining-volume tests with close < open and close > short-pattern SMA, bullish continuation; buying climax when volume > long-volume SMA * climax-volume ratio, close < candle midpoint, and high >= Highest(high, pattern-extreme lookback), bearish reversal; selling climax with close > midpoint and low <= Lowest(low, pattern-extreme lookback), bullish reversal; bullish stopping volume when volume > short-volume SMA * stopping-volume ratio, bar range > volatility-scaled ATR * stopping-range ratio, close < open, low <= Lowest(low, stopping lookback), and close > low + bar range * stopping-close-position; bearish stopping volume when volume exceeds the same ratio, bar range exceeds the same range ratio, close > open, high >= Highest(high, stopping lookback), and close < high - bar range * stopping-close-position.
Calculation: Category weights start at 1. When a simulated trade opened with trade lines enabled resolves at its second target or stop, adjust its selected category weight by the learning-step parameter according to whether the resolving bar reaches the first target; clamp the weight between the lower and upper weight parameters. On a bar reaching both the second target and stop, resolve at the second target.
Calculation: Selected pattern = among nonempty enabled category candidates, choose the category with the greatest category weight * candidate score; ties prefer original, then candle, then structure, then VSA. Return no pattern unless the greatest product is positive; otherwise return that candidate's direction, category, and score.
Long: Selected pattern is bullish AND selected score >= minimum signal score AND show-pattern labels is enabled AND bars since the prior displayed pattern >= pattern-label cooldown.
Short: Selected pattern is bearish AND selected score >= minimum signal score AND show-pattern labels is enabled AND bars since the prior displayed pattern >= pattern-label cooldown.
Long: Selected pattern is bullish AND selected score >= minimum signal score AND bars since the prior generated signal >= signal cooldown AND (structural proximity is not required OR structural proximity) AND (RCM integrity is not required OR RCM is disabled OR RCM integrity >= its minimum parameter) AND RCM permits long AND (chart bar is realtime OR confirmed).
Short: Selected pattern is bearish AND selected score >= minimum signal score AND bars since the prior generated signal >= signal cooldown AND (structural proximity is not required OR structural proximity) AND (RCM integrity is not required OR RCM is disabled OR RCM integrity >= its minimum parameter) AND RCM permits short AND (chart bar is realtime OR confirmed).
Long: Dashboard is enabled AND (wavelet alignment is positive OR Lorentzian prediction exceeds its bullish-display threshold OR RCM state is bullish OR RCM bullish impulse > RCM bearish impulse OR support siege proximity is true while resistance siege proximity is false).
Short: Dashboard is enabled AND (wavelet alignment is negative OR Lorentzian prediction is below the negative bearish-display threshold OR RCM state is bearish OR RCM bearish impulse > RCM bullish impulse OR resistance siege proximity is true).
Exit: long, simulated trade lines are enabled AND high >= entry price + entry risk distance * second-target multiple OR low <= entry price - entry risk distance; entry risk distance = ATR(short ATR lookback at entry) * stop-distance multiplier.
Exit: short, simulated trade lines are enabled AND low <= entry price - entry risk distance * second-target multiple OR high >= entry price + entry risk distance; entry risk distance = ATR(short ATR lookback at entry) * stop-distance multiplier.
