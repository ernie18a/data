Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low on chart bars using separate left- and right-lookback parameters; a pivot is available after its right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Dealing-range bounds = highest confirmed swing high and lowest confirmed swing low since the configured range window starts. The leg is bullish when the high pivot is later than the low pivot, otherwise bearish. Measure the OTE zone between the shallower and deeper retracement parameters from the later extreme toward the other range extreme. If the OTE is untapped when a daily or London window ends, continue updating the bounds with new confirmed swings until a chart-bar wick overlaps the OTE, then lock them. A bullish range is invalid when low < range bottom; a bearish range is invalid when high > range top. An NY range starts at its configured window open; on invalidation, retain the unbroken extreme and reset the broken extreme to the current bar's high or low.
Calculation: Session high/low = the highest high and lowest low during the configured Asia or London session, locked after that session ends.
Calculation: Fair Value Gap on T = on T bars, bullish when the latest completed bar's low > the high two T bars earlier, with bottom at that earlier high and top at the latest low; bearish when the latest completed bar's high < the low two T bars earlier, with bottom at the latest high and top at that earlier low. Detect on each new T bar using completed T bars; on chart bars use the stored gaps. Remove a bullish gap when a completed T close < its bottom and a bearish gap when a completed T close > its top; retain no more than the configured storage cap per T.
Calculation: Selected range FVG = the newest non-inverted stored gap, created at or after the range start, that overlaps the OTE and matches its side: bullish when the OTE midpoint < the range midpoint, bearish otherwise. Search newest-first within T1, then T2, then T3; select the first timeframe with a match.
Calculation: Manipulation FVG set = chart-bar bearish FVGs for a long setup, or bullish FVGs for a short setup, found by scanning offsets i from max(0, bars since tip minus the tip-edge allowance parameter) through the manipulation scan-lookback parameter, in increasing i; require i+2 to exist. A bearish gap has high[i] < low[i+2], bottom high[i], top low[i+2]; a bullish gap has low[i] > high[i+2], bottom high[i+2], top low[i]. Rebuild the set when the tip changes.
Calculation: Long IFVG confirmation = the manipulation FVG set is nonempty, its first gap in scan order is marked inverted by a chart close > its top + minimum-displacement parameter, and every later gap is either marked inverted by that condition or has never been touched. Mark a bearish gap touched when close <= its top and high > its bottom; retain touched and inverted marks until the tip changes.
Calculation: Short IFVG confirmation = the manipulation FVG set is nonempty, its first gap in scan order is marked inverted by a chart close < its bottom - minimum-displacement parameter, and every later gap is either marked inverted by that condition or has never been touched. Mark a bullish gap touched when close >= its bottom and low < its top; retain touched and inverted marks until the tip changes.
Calculation: Long CISD level = the open of the first scanned bearish chart bar whose immediately older bar is non-bearish; ignore that boundary when the older bar's body size <= the opposing-body parameter and the next older bar is bearish. Short CISD level mirrors this with bullish and non-bullish bars. Use the manipulation scan range.
Calculation: Long CISD confirmation = close >= long CISD level + minimum-displacement parameter and no manipulation bearish FVG has close <= its top and high >= its bottom. Short CISD confirmation = close <= short CISD level - minimum-displacement parameter and no manipulation bullish FVG has close >= its bottom and low <= its top.
Calculation: Sweep tip = the lowest low since a session-low sweep or the highest high since a session-high sweep. Sweep target anchor = high of the first chart bar found scanning from the major-swing confirmation lookback through the maximum scan lookback that is a pivot low for a high sweep (low[i] < low[i-j] and low[i] <= low[i+j] for every j through the confirmation lookback), or a pivot high for a low sweep (high[i] > high[i-j] and high[i] >= high[i+j] for every such j). Sweep target for a high sweep = tip - (tip - anchor) × sweep-target retracement parameter; for a low sweep = tip + (anchor - tip) × sweep-target retracement parameter.
Long: During the configured NY AM window, while a valid dealing-range setup is hunting after its selected bullish FVG is tapped (low <= gap top) and remains available, emit long when long IFVG confirmation OR long CISD confirmation, and abs(range top - close) > abs(close - lowest low since tap). For daily and London ranges, require the delivery count to be below the per-window delivery cap; the NY range has no delivery cap.
Short: During the configured NY AM window, while a valid dealing-range setup is hunting after its selected bearish FVG is tapped (high >= gap bottom) and remains available, emit short when short IFVG confirmation OR short CISD confirmation, and abs(close - range bottom) > abs(highest high since tap - close). For daily and London ranges, require the delivery count to be below the per-window delivery cap; the NY range has no delivery cap.
Long: During the configured NY AM window, while a London- or Asia-session low sweep setup is hunting after low <= its locked session low, emit long when long IFVG confirmation OR long CISD confirmation, and abs(sweep target - close) >= abs(close - sweep tip). For an Asia low, require the London range bottom > the Asia low.
Short: During the configured NY AM window, while a London- or Asia-session high sweep setup is hunting after high >= its locked session high, emit short when short IFVG confirmation OR short CISD confirmation, and abs(close - sweep target) >= abs(sweep tip - close). For an Asia high, require the London range top < the Asia high.
Exit: long, after a dealing-range FVG long entry, low <= the recorded sweep tip OR high >= the recorded range top.
Exit: short, after a dealing-range FVG short entry, high >= the recorded sweep tip OR low <= the recorded range bottom.
Exit: long, after a session-low sweep long entry, low < the recorded sweep tip OR high > the recorded sweep target.
Exit: short, after a session-high sweep short entry, high > the recorded sweep tip OR low < the recorded sweep target.
