Calculation: SafeATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) when available, otherwise high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: PreGrabRangeATR = (Highest(high, coil lookback)[1] − Lowest(low, coil lookback)[1]) / SafeATR, sampled on the initial sweep bar; if unavailable, use one normalized ATR
Calculation: VolumeSurge = volume / max(SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback) when available, otherwise volume, volume denominator floor)
Calculation: PropulsionCharge(depthATR, barsToReclaim, wickFraction, volumeSurge, preGrabRangeATR, displacementATR) = 100 × clamp((depthWeight × clamp01(depthATR / depth reference) + speedWeight × clamp01(1 − barsToReclaim / reclaim window) + wickWeight × clamp01(wickFraction) + volumeWeight × clamp01((volumeSurge − volume baseline) / max(volume reference − volume baseline, volume-reference denominator floor)) + coilWeight × clamp01((coil looseness reference − preGrabRangeATR) / max(coil looseness reference − coil lower anchor, coil denominator floor)) + displacementWeight × clamp01(displacementATR / displacement reference)) / max(sum of the six weights, weight-sum floor), 0, 1), where clamp01(x) = min(max(x, 0), 1)
Long: After low falls below the latest confirmed pivot low for its unresolved sweep attempt, load a long spring when close > the current latest confirmed pivot low within the reclaim window AND (current pivot low − lowest low since the sweep began) / SafeATR at reclaim ≥ minimum grab depth AND PropulsionCharge((current pivot low − lowest low since sweep began) / SafeATR at reclaim, bars from sweep to reclaim, (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), VolumeSurge, PreGrabRangeATR sampled at sweep, (close − current pivot low) / SafeATR at reclaim) ≥ arming threshold AND (strong-only filter is disabled OR PropulsionCharge(...) ≥ strong-tier threshold); retire the pivot attempt at reclaim or timeout
Short: After high rises above the latest confirmed pivot high for its unresolved sweep attempt, load a short spring when close < the current latest confirmed pivot high within the reclaim window AND (highest high since the sweep began − current pivot high) / SafeATR at reclaim ≥ minimum grab depth AND PropulsionCharge((highest high since sweep began − current pivot high) / SafeATR at reclaim, bars from sweep to reclaim, (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), VolumeSurge, PreGrabRangeATR sampled at sweep, (current pivot high − close) / SafeATR at reclaim) ≥ arming threshold AND (strong-only filter is disabled OR PropulsionCharge(...) ≥ strong-tier threshold); retire the pivot attempt at reclaim or timeout
Long: For a loaded long spring, close > the reclaim bar high AND close ≥ the lowest low from sweep through reclaim AND the ignition window has not expired
Short: For a loaded short spring, close < the reclaim bar low AND close ≤ the highest high from sweep through reclaim AND the ignition window has not expired
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − (SafeATR at entry × ATR risk distance) OR high ≥ entry price + (SafeATR at entry × ATR risk distance × TP3 R multiple); if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, stop takes precedence
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + (SafeATR at entry × ATR risk distance) OR low ≤ entry price − (SafeATR at entry × ATR risk distance × TP3 R multiple); if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, stop takes precedence
