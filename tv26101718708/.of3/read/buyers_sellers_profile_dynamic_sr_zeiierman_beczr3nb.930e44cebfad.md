Calculation: Estimated volume profile(pivot bar, profile lookback, row count, minimum price increment) = use up to profile lookback bars ending at the pivot bar; divide their low-to-high range into equal-width rows, expanding the range symmetrically to at least minimum price increment × row count; allocate each bar’s nonnegative volume to rows by range overlap, using full volume for zero-range bars; estimated buy volume is allocated volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or allocated volume × zero-range buy fraction for zero-range bars
Calculation: Profile node(raw pivot, side, profile rows, ATR lookback, search distance) = among row midpoints within max(ATR at pivot × search distance, row height × row-radius multiplier) of raw pivot, select the midpoint with greatest evidence, breaking ties toward the lower midpoint; row strength = row volume / max(maximum row volume, zero-volume floor); peak quality = clamp((row volume / max(mean adjacent row volumes, maximum row volume × neighbor-volume floor fraction) − peak baseline) / (peak full-scale − peak baseline)); at an edge use the current row for the missing neighbor; directional volume = estimated buy share for support or estimated sell share for resistance; proximity = 1 − distance / search radius; evidence = node-strength weight × row strength + peak-quality weight × peak quality + directional-volume weight × directional volume + proximity weight × proximity; return selected midpoint, row strength, and evidence
Calculation: Pivot quality(raw pivot, side, swing length, volume-average lookback, minimum price increment) = at the pivot, volume score is clamp((pivot volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) − pivot-volume baseline) / (pivot-volume saturation − pivot-volume baseline)), using ratio 1 when the average is nonpositive; wick score is clamp((support lower wick or resistance upper wick) / max(pivot candle range, minimum price increment) / wick saturation); absorption score is the maximum over bars within min(swing length, absorption-radius cap) of sqrt(clamp((absorption maximum body fraction − absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) / absorption maximum body fraction) × clamp((volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) − absorption-volume baseline) / (absorption-volume saturation − absorption-volume baseline))), using current volume when the average is unavailable and ratio 1 when the average is nonpositive
Calculation: Profile-guided level(raw pivot, selected node, evidence, pivot quality, profile pull) = raw pivot + (selected node − raw pivot) × profile pull maximum × clamp(evidence / evidence pull saturation); score = score scale × (profile-score evidence weight × evidence + absorption-score weight × absorption score + pivot-volume-score weight × volume score + wick-score weight × wick score)
Calculation: Active profile-guided levels = accept a qualified candidate when no same-side active level is within max(ATR at pivot × merge distance, minimum price increment), or when its score exceeds that of the nearest same-side level within that distance; replace the nearest level or add the candidate; on a confirmed bar close remove support below its price minus max(current ATR × break distance, zero), and resistance above its price plus that buffer; then remove lowest-score levels until the combined support and resistance count is at most the maximum level count
Long: current chart bar has more than volume-average lookback + 2 × swing length + warm-up bars AND a confirmed pivot low with swing length left and right bars AND Profile node(raw pivot, support, profile rows, ATR lookback, search distance) strength >= minimum node strength AND its evidence >= minimum profile evidence AND Profile-guided level(raw pivot, selected node, evidence, Pivot quality(raw pivot, support, swing length, volume-average lookback, minimum price increment), profile pull) score >= minimum level score AND (no active support within max(ATR at pivot × merge distance, minimum price increment) OR candidate score > score of the nearest active support within that distance)
