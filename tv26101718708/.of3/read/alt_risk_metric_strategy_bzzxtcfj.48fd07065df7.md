Calculation: normalized MACD = midpoint when the highest and lowest MACD(close, faster MACD lookback, slower MACD lookback) over the MACD range lookback are equal; otherwise clamp((MACD - lowest MACD) / (highest MACD - lowest MACD), index lower bound, index upper bound); evaluated on T1 bars using the current T1 bar, then carried to chart bars with lookahead off
Calculation: fear and greed index = clamp(rescale(RMA(cycle, fear and greed smoothing lookback), index offset, index scale), index lower bound, index upper bound), where cycle is the average of (close / EMA(close, price lookback) - one) scaled as a percentage, (close - close[price lag]) / close[price lag] scaled as a percentage, negative percentage deviation of the selected volatility measure from its EMA, and percentage change of the selected Bitcoin dominance or gold measure from its lagged value; include Chaikin Money Flow scaled as a percentage in the average when volume is nonzero; evaluated on T1 bars using current T1 values with lookahead off and carried to chart bars
Calculation: social risk adjustment = (social risk / social divisor) * social weight, where social risk is zero when the absolute value of EMA(proxy + boost, social smoothing lookback) is within the neutral deadband and otherwise equals that EMA; proxy is (RSI(close, social RSI lookback) - RSI center) multiplied by the reciprocal of volatility sensitivity when ATR(close, volatility lookback) / close exceeds its SMA over the volatility baseline lookback, and by one otherwise; boost is the positive extreme boost above the upper RSI threshold plus its extension, the positive boost above the upper threshold, the negative extreme boost below the lower threshold minus its extension, the negative boost below the lower threshold, or zero; evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: risk = EMA(base risk + optional fear and greed adjustment + optional U.S. business conditions adjustment + optional social risk adjustment, risk smoothing lookback), where base risk is normalized MACD times MACD weight plus RSI(close, RSI lookback) times RSI weight plus close / SMA(close, long-term lookback) times deviation weight; fear and greed adjustment is (fear and greed index - index center) times its weight, using either Bitcoin volatility and Bitcoin dominance or volatility index and gold according to the selected mode; U.S. business conditions adjustment is (SMA(T2 close shifted by its bar offset, smoothing lookback) - index center) times its weight, using the current T2 bar with lookahead off and smoothing on chart bars; evaluated on T1 bars and carried to chart bars with lookahead off, using the current T1 bar in realtime and its completed value historically
Long: risk <= deepest buy threshold
Long: risk <= middle buy threshold AND risk > deepest buy threshold
Long: risk <= shallowest buy threshold AND risk > middle buy threshold
Exit: long, close > entry price AND risk >= first sell threshold AND no sell tier has completed since the most recent long signal
Exit: long, close > entry price AND risk >= second sell threshold AND first sell tier completed on an earlier bar since the most recent long signal AND second sell tier not completed
Exit: long, close > entry price AND risk >= third sell threshold AND second sell tier completed on an earlier bar since the most recent long signal AND third sell tier not completed
