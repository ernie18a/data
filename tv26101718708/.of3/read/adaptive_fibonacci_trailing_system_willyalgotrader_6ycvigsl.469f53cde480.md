Calculation: body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low
Calculation: volatility ratio = ATR(regime lookback) / SMA(ATR(regime lookback), volatility-average lookback)
Calculation: volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) when both are positive, otherwise neutral
Calculation: regime = ranging initially; on warmed-up confirmed chart bars, enter volatile when volatility ratio >= volatile-entry threshold; while volatile, remain volatile until volatility ratio < volatile-entry threshold × volatile-exit factor, then become trending if ADX(ADX lookback) >= trend threshold or ranging otherwise; when not volatile, enter trending if ADX >= trend threshold, leave trending for ranging if ADX < trend threshold × trend-exit factor, and otherwise retain the prior regime
Calculation: regime gate = regime is trending OR (regime is volatile AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio) OR (regime is ranging AND ADX(ADX lookback) >= ranging breakout threshold)
Calculation: valid swing anchors = confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right pivot lookbacks whose swing score >= minimum swing score, retaining the most recent swing-memory count; swing score = (distance from the pivot price to the latest prior opposing valid pivot price, or ATR(regime lookback) at the pivot if none, divided by ATR(regime lookback) at the pivot) × max(volume ratio at the pivot when volume is available, otherwise neutral volume factor, volume floor); use the latest retained high and low when at least two valid swings are retained and the high exceeds the low
Calculation: adaptive trail stops = use Fibonacci retracement levels from the valid swing low to high, with upper, midpoint, and lower retracement parameters ordered from smallest to largest; choose a retracement parameter by trailing mode, or in adaptive mode choose the smallest in ranging, midpoint in volatile, and largest in trending; volatility adjustment = clamp(1 / max(volatility ratio, volatility floor), lower adjustment bound, upper adjustment bound); bullish stop = clamp(swing low + swing range × (1 − retracement) × volatility adjustment, swing low, swing high); bearish stop = clamp(swing high − swing range × (1 − retracement) × volatility adjustment, swing low, swing high)
Calculation: trail direction = on the first eligible confirmed bar with valid stops, initialize long if close > the Fibonacci midpoint and short otherwise; thereafter ratchet a long stop upward to max(previous stop, bullish stop) and a short stop downward to min(previous stop, bearish stop); when a long closes below its stop or a short closes above its stop, count consecutive wrong-side closes and flip direction, resetting the stop to the opposite adaptive stop, if body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND penetration beyond the stop > breakout factor × ATR(regime lookback) AND regime gate, OR if the positive force-flip count is reached; reset the wrong-side count when close is no longer beyond the stop; update only on warmed-up confirmed chart bars
Calculation: confidence(side) = percentage scale × (structure weight × clamp(anchor swing score for the side / structure-score cap, normalized range) + regime weight × regime-alignment score + ADX weight × clamp(ADX / ADX confidence scale, normalized range) + volume weight × volume score + volatility weight × volatility score + confluence weight × confluence score), with weights summing to one; anchor swing score is the latest low score for long and latest high score for short, or zero if unavailable; SMA alignment means close is above SMA(trend lookback) for long or below it for short, and trail alignment means trail direction matches the side; regime-alignment score is, when trending, full score if trail-aligned, otherwise cross quality in midpoint-cross mode or trend-misaligned score; when volatile, volatile-aligned score if SMA-aligned or volatile-misaligned score otherwise; when ranging, in midpoint-cross mode clamp(cross quality − range-cross deduction + (SMA-aligned ? range-SMA bonus : zero), normalized range), otherwise range-aligned score if SMA-aligned or range-misaligned score; cross quality is selected by the cross-filter setting; volume score is neutral when volume is unavailable, otherwise volume ratio divided by the saturation ratio up to saturation, full score up to the spike threshold, then clamp(full score − (volume ratio − spike threshold) / decay span, volume floor, full score); volatility score is the normalized tent score with its peak at the volatility optimum and zero at its lower and upper bounds; confluence reference is the Fibonacci midpoint in midpoint-cross mode or the side's adaptive stop otherwise, and confluence score is clamp(full score − abs(reference − SMA(trend lookback)) / ATR(regime lookback) / confluence radius, normalized range), or neutral when inputs are unavailable
Calculation: trade levels and effective stop = risk distance is ATR(risk lookback) at entry × stop multiplier; initial stop is entry price minus the side-signed risk distance, and each target is entry price plus the side-signed risk distance × its separate target multiple; effective stop is entry price if break-even is enabled and TP1 was first touched on an earlier active bar without a stop hit, otherwise the initial stop
Long: confirmed chart-bar close AND warm-up complete AND no active position AND (confidence filtering disabled OR confidence(long) >= minimum confidence) AND ((signal-mode setting selects trail-direction changes AND trail direction changes to long) OR (signal-mode setting selects midpoint crossings AND current and prior midpoint exist AND close > current Fibonacci midpoint AND close[1] <= current Fibonacci midpoint AND (cross-filter setting allows an unfiltered cross OR (body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND close − current Fibonacci midpoint > penetration factor × ATR(regime lookback) AND (cross-filter setting does not require the regime gate OR regime gate))))
Short: confirmed chart-bar close AND warm-up complete AND no active position AND (confidence filtering disabled OR confidence(short) >= minimum confidence) AND ((signal-mode setting selects trail-direction changes AND trail direction changes to short) OR (signal-mode setting selects midpoint crossings AND current and prior midpoint exist AND close < current Fibonacci midpoint AND close[1] >= current Fibonacci midpoint AND (cross-filter setting allows an unfiltered cross OR (body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND current Fibonacci midpoint − close > penetration factor × ATR(regime lookback) AND (cross-filter setting does not require the regime gate OR regime gate))))
