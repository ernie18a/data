Calculation: Confirmed swings = on chart bars, detect pivot highs and lows from the selected wick or close source using the pivot-leg parameter on both sides, confirming after the right-side legs; store each pivot's price, direction, RSI(close, RSI lookback), and relative volume (volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback)) from the pivot bar; process a confirming high before a confirming low, append only when direction changes, otherwise replace the latest pivot only when the new pivot is more extreme, and retain the latest maximum-swings parameter pivots
Calculation: Fit score(x, target, tolerance) = max(0, score scale * (1 - abs(x - target) / tolerance))
Calculation: Primary candidate = score each available trailing impulse of 3, 4, 5, and 6 swings, zigzag of 3 and 4 swings, flat of 4 swings, and triangle of 6 swings, then choose the greatest adjusted score, breaking ties in that order. For impulse swings p0...p5, let wk=abs(pk-p(k-1)); average the available Fit scores for w2/w1, w3/w1, w4/w3, and w5/w1 using separate target and tolerance parameters for each ratio; if none are available use the neutral fib score. Set proportion base to the strong-proportion score when n>=4 and w3>=w1, otherwise the weak-proportion score; for n>=5 average that base with min(alt-score cap, abs(w2/w1-w4/w3)/alternation gap * score scale). For n>=4, momentum score is high if wave-3 RSI is in the impulse direction beyond the RSI center by the direction offset and its distance from center is at least wave-1's, medium if only one condition holds, otherwise low; for n>=6 multiply by the wave-5 penalty when wave-5 RSI distance from center exceeds wave-3's. When wave-3 relative volume is positive, score volume high if it is at least wave-1 relative volume and above the volume baseline, medium if either holds, otherwise low; blend momentum and volume scores using separate blend weights. For n<4 use the initial energy score. Impulse raw score is (fib score*fib weight + proportion score*proportion weight + energy score*momentum weight) / max(weight-sum floor, sum of weights); valid requires, when available, p2 beyond p0 in the impulse direction, not (w3<w1 and w3<w5), and p4 beyond p1 in the impulse direction; multiply invalid scores by the impulse penalty. Zigzag raw score averages available Fit scores for B/A and C/A with separate targets and tolerances; it is valid when p2 is beyond p1 opposite the impulse direction; multiply invalid scores by the correction penalty. Flat raw score averages Fit scores for B/A and C/A with separate targets and tolerances; it is valid when B is at least A times the flat-validity parameter; multiply invalid scores by the correction penalty. Triangle raw score is the number of strictly shrinking consecutive leg lengths divided by the number of comparisons, times score scale; it is valid when that count reaches the triangle-validity parameter; multiply invalid scores by the correction penalty. Weight each raw score by the applicable family prior multiplier: derive the impulse prior from whether ADX(DMI length, ADX smoothing length) exceeds the ADX threshold, use its complement for correction families, then apply the family base multiplier plus the family prior multiplier times that prior. Candidate direction is bullish when its first swing is a low and bearish when it is a high; its invalidation is p1 for impulse and p0 for all other families.
Calculation: Market structure = from the confirmed swings, let H1/H2 and L1/L2 be the latest and previous swing highs and lows. If any latest high or low is missing, state neutral. If H1>H2 and L1>L2, state BOS up when close>H1, CHoCH down when close<L1, otherwise uptrend HH/HL. If H1<H2 and L1<L2, state BOS down when close<L1, CHoCH up when close>H1, otherwise downtrend LH/LL. Otherwise state BOS up when close>H1, BOS down when close<L1, otherwise ranging.
Long: Primary candidate direction = bullish
Short: Primary candidate direction = bearish
Long: Market structure = BOS up OR market structure = CHoCH up OR market structure = uptrend HH/HL
Short: Market structure = BOS down OR market structure = CHoCH down OR market structure = downtrend LH/LL
Exit: long, Primary candidate direction = bullish AND close < primary candidate invalidation
Exit: short, Primary candidate direction = bearish AND close > primary candidate invalidation
