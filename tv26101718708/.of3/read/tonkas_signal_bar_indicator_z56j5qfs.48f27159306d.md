Calculation: Body fraction above L = if max(open, close) >= L and min(open, close) <= L, clamp((max(open, close) - L) / max(max(open, close) - min(open, close), tick size), 0, 1); otherwise 0

Calculation: Recent sell-side sweep = within the ERL lookback number of chart bars since the last day reset, a chart bar had low below the previous completed T1 low or completed Asia-session low and close above that same level
Calculation: Recent buy-side sweep = within the ERL lookback number of chart bars since the last day reset, a chart bar had high above the previous completed T1 high or completed Asia-session high and close below that same level
