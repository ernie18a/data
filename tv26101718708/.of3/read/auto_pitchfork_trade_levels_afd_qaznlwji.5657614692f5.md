Calculation: Median(x) = the line through the midpoint of P1 and P2 and the selected origin: P0, P0's bar with the midpoint of P0 and P1 prices, or the midpoint of P0 and P1 in both bar and price coordinates.
Calculation: Upper and lower warning lines(x) = lines parallel to the median, each one median-to-parallel spacing beyond the upper or lower of the parallels through P1 and P2.
Calculation: Fork width = the absolute price difference at P1's bar between P1 and the line parallel to the median through P2.
