Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) when positive, otherwise close × ATR-fallback fraction.
Calculation: Automatic pitchfork = from the selected high and low sources (default high and low), confirm pivots using separate left and right lookbacks; retain alternating pivots, replace the latest pivot with a more extreme confirmed pivot of the same kind, and append an opposite-kind pivot only when its distance from the latest pivot is at least the minimum-prominence multiplier × Safe ATR. Use the latest three pivots: for low-high-low, place the origin at the first low; for high-low-high, place it at the first pivot's bar and the mean of the first two prices. The median endpoint is the midpoint of pivots two and three; the median-line slope joins the origin to that endpoint, and the upper and lower tine offsets are the maximum and minimum residuals of pivots two and three from the median line. On the first bar of an invalidation, if re-anchoring is enabled and its cooldown has elapsed, clear the pivots, seed the new sequence with that bar's high for an up break or low for a down break, and use the re-anchor lookback for both pivot sides and the standard prominence multiplier × re-anchor prominence factor until the new fork forms; exclude pre-break pivots.
Calculation: Trending = ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing lookback equal to ADX lookback) > ADX threshold AND Efficiency Ratio(selected price source, regime ER lookback) > regime ER threshold.
Calculation: Break strength = for an up or down embedded break, add one for each condition met: (close − upper tine) / Safe ATR for an up break, or (lower tine − close) / Safe ATR for a down break, ≥ break-magnitude ATR multiple; effective volume is reliable and ≥ volume-participation multiple × SMA(effective volume, volume-average lookback); TrueRange > range-expansion multiple × Safe ATR AND ATR > its prior chart-bar value; up-break close location (close − low) / (high − low), or down-break close location (high − close) / (high − low), ≥ anti-wick fraction (use 0.5 when high = low); Efficiency Ratio(close, longer coil ER lookback)[long coil lag] ≤ coil ER threshold AND Efficiency Ratio(close, shorter coil ER lookback) > Efficiency Ratio(close, shorter coil ER lookback)[short coil lag], where the longer coil ER lookback exceeds the shorter one. Effective volume is native volume, or selected proxy volume only if native volume has never been positive; unavailable or nonpositive effective volume fails the volume condition.
Calculation: Ride state = at least the embedded-close streak length of consecutive current chart-bar closes above the automatic pitchfork median plus its half-width or below the median minus its half-width, AND Trending AND Break strength ≥ minimum ride strength; reset each directional streak when its condition fails. Evaluate using current chart-bar values, including a bar still forming; use pivots only after their right-side confirmation.
Long: Automatic pitchfork exists AND its median-line slope ≥ 0
Short: Automatic pitchfork exists AND its median-line slope < 0
Long: Trending AND close > EMA(close, trend-MA lookback)
Short: Trending AND close ≤ EMA(close, trend-MA lookback)
Long: Ride state AND up embedded break
Short: Ride state AND down embedded break
