Calculation: Zone score = average of the percentage distances of close from TEMA(close, TEMA lookback), LSMA(close, LSMA lookback), and EMA(close, EMA lookback)
Calculation: All three averages rising = TEMA > its prior-bar value AND LSMA > its prior-bar value AND EMA > its prior-bar value
Calculation: All three averages not rising = TEMA <= its prior-bar value AND LSMA <= its prior-bar value AND EMA <= its prior-bar value
Calculation: Trend state = bullish run length while all three averages are rising, bearish run length while all three averages are not rising, or neutral with zero run length otherwise; a direction change starts a run at the current bar
Calculation: Trend exhausted = trend run length > minimum trend bars * exhaustion run factor AND (absolute average of TEMA minus its value at the TEMA strength lag, LSMA minus its value at the LSMA strength lag, and EMA minus its value at the EMA strength lag, divided by ATR, < normalized strength threshold OR (bullish trend and not all three averages rising) OR (bearish trend and not all three averages not rising) OR (bullish trend and close > close at divergence lookback and RSI < its value at divergence lookback) OR (bearish trend and close < close at divergence lookback and RSI > its value at divergence lookback))
Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot high = most recently confirmed Pivot High(high, sweep pivot lookback, sweep pivot lookback)
Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot low = most recently confirmed Pivot Low(low, sweep pivot lookback, sweep pivot lookback)
Calculation: Latest bullish break level = EMA at the most recent cross of close above EMA
Calculation: Latest bearish break level = EMA at the most recent cross of close below EMA
Calculation: EMA test count = count of distinct per-bar closes testing EMA within the retest tolerance band since the test count was reset
Calculation: Smoothed Stochastic K = SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback), stochastic smoothing)
Calculation: Bullish consensus = count of these conditions meeting the consensus threshold: RSI(close, reversal RSI lookback) > RSI midpoint; Smoothed Stochastic K > stochastic midpoint OR Smoothed Stochastic K > SMA(Smoothed Stochastic K, stochastic D lookback); Stochastic RSI(close, RSI lookback, stochastic RSI lookback) > stochastic RSI midpoint; MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > MACD signal; Williams %R(high, low, close, lookback) > Williams %R midpoint; CCI(close, lookback) > zero
Calculation: Bearish consensus = count of these conditions meeting the consensus threshold: RSI(close, reversal RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint; Smoothed Stochastic K < stochastic midpoint OR Smoothed Stochastic K < SMA(Smoothed Stochastic K, stochastic D lookback); Stochastic RSI(close, RSI lookback, stochastic RSI lookback) < stochastic RSI midpoint; MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) < MACD signal; Williams %R(high, low, close, lookback) < Williams %R midpoint; CCI(close, lookback) < zero
Calculation: Long signal reference price = open on the bar a long signal occurs
Calculation: Ideal buy setup = (low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > prior-bar high AND prior-bar high - low > ATR * minimum sweep size AND close > EMA AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) OR (close within the bullish break level's retest tolerance band AND close > open AND TEMA > LSMA AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) OR (zone score <= negative oversold-zone threshold AND close > EMA AND TEMA > LSMA AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier)
Calculation: Ideal sell setup = (close within the bearish break level's retest tolerance band AND close < open AND TEMA < LSMA AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) OR (high < latest confirmed pivot high AND high > prior-bar high AND zone score > neutral-high boundary AND TEMA < LSMA) OR (EMA test count >= maximum test count AND close < EMA AND TEMA < LSMA AND close crosses below EMA)

Short: close is within [highest high over swing lookback - ATR * institutional zone width, highest high over swing lookback]
