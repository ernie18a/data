Calculation: Regression SuperTrend = Linear Regression of the selected source over a lookback, with upper and lower raw bands at the regression value plus or minus RMSE times a multiplier; RMSE is the square root of the sum of squared source-minus-regression residuals over the lookback divided by lookback minus one. Initialize the trend bullish; when bullish, the lower rail is the maximum of the current lower raw band and prior lower rail, and the upper rail is the current upper raw band; when bearish, the upper rail is the minimum of the current upper raw band and prior upper rail, and the lower rail is the current lower raw band. Switch bearish when close falls below the lower rail and bullish when close rises above the upper rail; the trend line is the lower rail when bullish and upper rail when bearish.
Calculation: Range regime = (count of Regression SuperTrend flips over the flip lookback >= flip threshold AND ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback) <= range ADX maximum) OR (EMA slope percentage < flat-slope threshold AND ADX <= range ADX maximum), where EMA slope percentage is abs(EMA(close, bias length) - EMA(close, bias length)[slope lookback]) / EMA(close, bias length) * 100, or zero when that EMA is zero.
Calculation: Hard break quality = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= hard body-ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * hard body ATR threshold.
Calculation: Active v1 distances = ATR(ATR lookback) multiplied separately by either the fallback touch-tolerance and retracement parameters, or the trend or range touch-tolerance and retracement parameters selected by the current range regime when automatic mode is enabled.
Calculation: Long PREP = when PREP is enabled and its optional state filter allows WAIT(L), arm after CCI(hlc3, CCI lookback) <= negative extreme; while armed, trigger when CCI crosses above negative trigger and the shared PREP cooldown has elapsed. A trigger clears the long arm and starts the cooldown; a short arm clears the long arm.
Calculation: Short PREP = when PREP is enabled and its optional state filter allows WAIT(S), arm after CCI(hlc3, CCI lookback) >= positive extreme; while armed, trigger when CCI crosses below positive trigger and the shared PREP cooldown has elapsed. A trigger clears the short arm and starts the cooldown; a long arm clears the short arm.
Calculation: Latest structure swing high = most recent confirmed PivotHigh(high, structure left lookback, structure right lookback).
Calculation: Latest structure swing low = most recent confirmed PivotLow(low, structure left lookback, structure right lookback).
Calculation: Structure direction = while neutral, bullish when close > latest structure swing high and bearish when close < latest structure swing low; thereafter set it to the direction of each accepted BOS or CHOCH break.
Calculation: Latest liquidity swing high = most recent confirmed PivotHigh(high, liquidity left lookback, liquidity right lookback).
Calculation: Latest liquidity swing low = most recent confirmed PivotLow(low, liquidity left lookback, liquidity right lookback).
Calculation: Entry gate passes a direction when the gate is disabled, its stored direction is neutral, or its stored direction matches and either a retest is not required or a matching retest has completed. When enabled, store the direction of the first accepted BOS/CHOCH break or liquidity sweep while inactive with no completed retest; a matching CHOCH body retest or liquidity retest completes it. Reset on gate timeout, any liquidity breakout, or a v1/v2 ENTRY signal.
Calculation: Trend-distance contraction = Falling(EMA(abs(close - Regression SuperTrend line), distance lookback), contraction lookback).
Long: Regression SuperTrend trend is bullish.
Short: Regression SuperTrend trend is bearish.
Long: close > EMA(close, bias length) AND ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback) >= ADX minimum.
Short: close < EMA(close, bias length) AND ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback) >= ADX minimum.
Long: Regression SuperTrend is bullish AND close > EMA(close, bias length) AND ADX >= ADX minimum AND ADX >= no-trade threshold.
Short: Regression SuperTrend is bearish AND close < EMA(close, bias length) AND ADX >= ADX minimum AND ADX >= no-trade threshold.
Long: Regression SuperTrend flips bullish on a confirmed bar AND (regime filter is disabled OR range regime is false).
Short: Regression SuperTrend flips bearish on a confirmed bar AND (regime filter is disabled OR range regime is false).
Exit: long, exhaustion status is enabled AND Regression SuperTrend is bullish AND Trend-distance contraction AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) is falling over its weakening lookback.
Exit: short, exhaustion status is enabled AND Regression SuperTrend is bearish AND Trend-distance contraction AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) is rising over its weakening lookback.
Long: An active bullish v1 setup was created by a bullish Regression SuperTrend flip allowed by the regime filter and passing Hard break quality when hard filtering is selected; since that flip, low <= its flip-candle body high minus the active v1 retracement distance, and the current low <= that body high plus the active v1 touch-tolerance distance, close > that body high, and (candle-direction filtering is disabled OR close > open); when PREP is required, Long PREP triggers on the setup bar and, if two-candle confirmation is enabled, also on the confirmation bar; with two-candle confirmation enabled, the setup rejection is on a confirmed bar and the immediately following confirmed bar closes above the setup bar high; with two-candle confirmation disabled, the rejection is on a confirmed bar; Entry gate passes long; no v1 entry has fired for this setup.
Short: An active bearish v1 setup was created by a bearish Regression SuperTrend flip allowed by the regime filter and passing Hard break quality when hard filtering is selected; since that flip, high >= its flip-candle body low plus the active v1 retracement distance, and the current high >= that body low minus the active v1 touch-tolerance distance, close < that body low, and (candle-direction filtering is disabled OR close < open); when PREP is required, Short PREP triggers on the setup bar and, if two-candle confirmation is enabled, also on the confirmation bar; with two-candle confirmation enabled, the setup rejection is on a confirmed bar and the immediately following confirmed bar closes below the setup bar low; with two-candle confirmation disabled, the rejection is on a confirmed bar; Entry gate passes short; no v1 entry has fired for this setup.
Long: A confirmed bullish Regression SuperTrend flip allowed by the regime filter and passing Hard break quality when hard filtering is selected creates a v2 level at that bar's close; before the v2 wait limit expires, low <= the level has retested it and close crosses above the level, with a confirmed bar if close confirmation is required; Entry gate passes long.
Short: A confirmed bearish Regression SuperTrend flip allowed by the regime filter and passing Hard break quality when hard filtering is selected creates a v2 level at that bar's close; before the v2 wait limit expires, high >= the level has retested it and close crosses below the level, with a confirmed bar if close confirmation is required; Entry gate passes short.
Long: Structure direction is bullish or bearish, close crosses above the latest structure swing high, and the CHOCH/BOS engine is enabled; if its trend-only filter is enabled, range regime is false; if its ADX filter is enabled, ADX >= ADX minimum; if its state filter is enabled, close > EMA(close, bias length) AND ADX >= ADX minimum; if close confirmation is enabled, the bar is confirmed; and Hard break quality passes when hard filtering is selected.
Short: Structure direction is bullish or bearish, close crosses below the latest structure swing low, and the CHOCH/BOS engine is enabled; if its trend-only filter is enabled, range regime is false; if its ADX filter is enabled, ADX >= ADX minimum; if its state filter is enabled, close < EMA(close, bias length) AND ADX >= ADX minimum; if close confirmation is enabled, the bar is confirmed; and Hard break quality passes when hard filtering is selected.
Long: After an accepted bullish BOS/CHOCH break and before its wait limit expires, a candle body has touched the break candle's body high (min(open, close) <= that level), and close crosses above that level before an entry has fired.
Short: After an accepted bearish BOS/CHOCH break and before its wait limit expires, a candle body has touched the break candle's body low (max(open, close) >= that level), and close crosses below that level before an entry has fired.
Long: low < latest liquidity swing low AND close > latest liquidity swing low.
Short: high > latest liquidity swing high AND close < latest liquidity swing high.
Long: During an active bullish liquidity-sweep setup and before its wait limit expires, low <= sweep level + ATR(ATR lookback) * liquidity retest tolerance AND close > sweep level; this is the first retest.
Short: During an active bearish liquidity-sweep setup and before its wait limit expires, high >= sweep level - ATR(ATR lookback) * liquidity retest tolerance AND close < sweep level; this is the first retest.
Long: After a bullish liquidity retest and before its wait limit expires, a confirmed close > the retest bar high.
Short: After a bearish liquidity retest and before its wait limit expires, a confirmed close < the retest bar low.
