Calculation: Opening range (S) = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in session S's configured opening-range window
Calculation: Asia range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured overnight range
Calculation: Initial risk = entry-bar close − entry-bar low for long, or entry-bar high − entry-bar close for short
Calculation: R level = maximum of the prior R level and the floor of (high − entry price) / initial risk for long, or (entry price − low) / initial risk for short
Calculation: Trailing stop = entry-bar low for long or entry-bar high for short until the breakeven R threshold; at that threshold, entry price plus the breakeven offset for long or minus it for short; above it, entry price plus (R level − breakeven R threshold) × initial risk for long or minus it for short
Long: On a chart bar in an enabled session's configured entry window, when flat, its opening range is complete, its entry count is below the session trade limit, no prior closed trade in the session reached the winner R threshold, and either this is its first entry or a close has returned strictly inside its opening range since the prior trade closed; and the Asia filter is disabled or the completed Asia range was breached before the London opening-range window; and close ≥ session opening-range high + breakout distance; and the EMA filter is disabled or close > EMA(close, lookback) computed on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar; and initial risk > 0
Short: On a chart bar in an enabled session's configured entry window, when flat, its opening range is complete, its entry count is below the session trade limit, no prior closed trade in the session reached the winner R threshold, and either this is its first entry or a close has returned strictly inside its opening range since the prior trade closed; and the Asia filter is disabled or the completed Asia range was breached before the London opening-range window; and close ≤ session opening-range low − breakout distance; and the EMA filter is disabled or close < EMA(close, lookback) computed on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar; and initial risk > 0
Exit: long, low ≤ trailing stop OR its session's enabled forced-close window is active
Exit: short, high ≥ trailing stop OR its session's enabled forced-close window is active
