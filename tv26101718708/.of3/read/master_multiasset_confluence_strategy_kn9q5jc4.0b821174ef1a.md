Calculation: Active swing order-block zones = on chart bars, identify swing highs and lows using the swing lookback on both sides; use the pivot bar's high and low as the zone; return the newest unmitigated bullish zone from a pivot low and bearish zone from a pivot high, marking a bullish zone mitigated when close < its bottom and a bearish zone mitigated when close > its top
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum on chart bars of signed volume delta; in intrabar mode, delta is the sum of signed volume over constituent T5 bars, including a still-forming bar when present, and otherwise is signed chart-bar volume; in bar-direction mode, delta is signed chart-bar volume; signed volume is positive when close >= open and negative otherwise
Long: low < lowest(low, divergence lookback)[1] AND ((first auxiliary series high from its current, still-forming T1 bar, mapped to chart bars) < highest(first auxiliary series high mapped to chart bars, divergence lookback)[1] OR (second auxiliary series high from its current, still-forming T2 bar, mapped to chart bars) < highest(second auxiliary series high mapped to chart bars, divergence lookback)[1] OR (third auxiliary series low from its current, still-forming T3 bar, mapped to chart bars) > lowest(third auxiliary series low mapped to chart bars, divergence lookback)[1]) AND (RSI/BB confirmation disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) < lower RSI threshold AND low < lower Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, band multiplier))) AND (order-block confirmation disabled OR (active bullish swing order-block zone exists AND low <= its top AND high >= its bottom)) AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR (low < lowest(low, CVD lookback)[1] AND CVD > lowest(CVD, CVD lookback)[1])) AND (SFP confirmation disabled OR (low < low of the last completed T4 bar AND close > low of the last completed T4 bar))
Short: high > highest(high, divergence lookback)[1] AND ((first auxiliary series low from its current, still-forming T1 bar, mapped to chart bars) > lowest(first auxiliary series low mapped to chart bars, divergence lookback)[1] OR (second auxiliary series low from its current, still-forming T2 bar, mapped to chart bars) > lowest(second auxiliary series low mapped to chart bars, divergence lookback)[1] OR (third auxiliary series high from its current, still-forming T3 bar, mapped to chart bars) < highest(third auxiliary series high mapped to chart bars, divergence lookback)[1]) AND (RSI/BB confirmation disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) > upper RSI threshold AND high > upper Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, band multiplier))) AND (order-block confirmation disabled OR (active bearish swing order-block zone exists AND low <= its top AND high >= its bottom)) AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR (high > highest(high, CVD lookback)[1] AND CVD < highest(CVD, CVD lookback)[1])) AND (SFP confirmation disabled OR (high > high of the last completed T4 bar AND close < high of the last completed T4 bar))
Exit: Long, (configured exit mode is percentage AND (high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage / 100) OR low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100))) OR (configured exit mode is ticks AND (high >= entry price + take-profit tick count × tick size OR low <= entry price - stop-loss tick count × tick size))
Exit: Short, (configured exit mode is percentage AND (low <= entry price × (1 - take-profit percentage / 100) OR high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100))) OR (configured exit mode is ticks AND (low <= entry price - take-profit tick count × tick size OR high >= entry price + stop-loss tick count × tick size))
