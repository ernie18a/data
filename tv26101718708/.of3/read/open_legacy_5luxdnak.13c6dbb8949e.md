Calculation: bullish gap = on chart bars, low > high[gap-offset lookback] AND low - high[gap-offset lookback] >= minimum gap fraction * ATR(ATR lookback); top = low, bottom = high[gap-offset lookback], and each new bullish gap replaces the prior one
Calculation: bearish gap = on chart bars, high < low[gap-offset lookback] AND low[gap-offset lookback] - high >= minimum gap fraction * ATR(ATR lookback); top = low[gap-offset lookback], bottom = high, and each new bearish gap replaces the prior one
Long: (gap-pullback method selected AND the latest stored bullish gap is untriggered, formed on a prior chart bar within gap lifetime, low <= its top, close > open, and close > its bottom) OR (EMA-trend method selected AND EMA(close, short entry lookback) > EMA(close, long entry lookback) AND close > EMA(close, short entry lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND those three comparisons were not all true on the preceding chart bar); AND (chart EMA filter not selected OR close > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback)) AND (higher-timeframe/strength extension not selected OR (chart EMA filter selected AND, on each chart bar, close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) >= strength threshold)); AND (no session filter enabled OR chart-bar open time is within an enabled session and its configured weekdays) AND chart-bar close is outside the inclusive pause window around every enabled news event; AND (chart bar is confirmed OR replay mode is enabled); AND (position is flat or short, or a long position reaches its take-profit or stop-loss level on this later chart bar)
Short: (gap-pullback method selected AND the latest stored bearish gap is untriggered, formed on a prior chart bar within gap lifetime, high >= its bottom, close < open, and close < its top) OR (EMA-trend method selected AND EMA(close, short entry lookback) < EMA(close, long entry lookback) AND close < EMA(close, short entry lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold AND those three comparisons were not all true on the preceding chart bar); AND (chart EMA filter not selected OR close < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback)) AND (higher-timeframe/strength extension not selected OR (chart EMA filter selected AND, on each chart bar, close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) >= strength threshold)); AND (no session filter enabled OR chart-bar open time is within an enabled session and its configured weekdays) AND chart-bar close is outside the inclusive pause window around every enabled news event; AND (chart bar is confirmed OR replay mode is enabled); AND (position is flat or long, or a short position reaches its take-profit or stop-loss level on this later chart bar)
Exit: long, on a later chart bar, high >= entry price + fixed take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - fixed stop-loss distance
Exit: short, on a later chart bar, low <= entry price - fixed take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + fixed stop-loss distance
