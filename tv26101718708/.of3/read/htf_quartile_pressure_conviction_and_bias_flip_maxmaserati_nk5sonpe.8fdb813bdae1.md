Calculation: Lower conviction level(bar) = low(bar) + (high(bar) - low(bar)) × lower-quartile parameter
Calculation: Upper conviction level(bar) = low(bar) + (high(bar) - low(bar)) × upper-quartile parameter, where the upper-quartile parameter is greater than the lower-quartile parameter
Calculation: Active bearish violation zone = the zone from the low to the lower conviction level of the first candle in the most recent adjacent completed T1 candle pair where the first candle closes below its open and the second candle closes above the first candle's lower conviction level; retain until replaced by a later qualifying pair
Calculation: Active bullish violation zone = the zone from the upper conviction level to the high of the first candle in the most recent adjacent completed T1 candle pair where the first candle closes at or above its open and the second candle closes below the first candle's upper conviction level; retain until replaced by a later qualifying pair
Long: On chart bars using the last completed T1 bar, (close >= open AND close >= upper conviction level) OR (close < open AND close > lower conviction level); OR on chart bars using the still-forming T1 bar, (close >= open AND close >= upper conviction level) OR (close < open AND close > lower conviction level); OR an active bearish violation zone exists AND chart-bar close > its upper bound; OR an active bullish violation zone exists AND chart-bar close > its upper bound
