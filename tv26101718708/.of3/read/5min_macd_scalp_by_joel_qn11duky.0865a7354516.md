Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD histogram using close values from T1, calculated on chart bars; use the current T1 value historically and the last completed T1 bar in realtime
Calculation: RSI = RSI using close values from T2, calculated on chart bars; use the current T2 value historically and the last completed T2 bar in realtime
Calculation: Positive MACD histogram average = over the average lookback of chart bars, sum the current MACD histogram historically or the previous chart bar's histogram in realtime for bars whose current histogram is positive, divided by the count of bars whose current histogram is positive
Calculation: Negative MACD histogram magnitude average = over the average lookback of chart bars, sum the absolute current MACD histogram historically or the absolute previous chart bar's histogram in realtime for bars whose current histogram is negative, divided by the count of bars whose current histogram is negative
Long: position is flat AND start datetime <= datetime <= end datetime AND ((trend mode uses fast/slow EMA crossover AND EMA(close, fast length) >= EMA(close, slow length) AND bars since crossover(EMA(close, fast length), EMA(close, slow length)) <= valid-cross lookback) OR (trend mode uses close/slow EMA crossover AND close > EMA(close, slow length) AND bars since crossover(close, EMA(close, slow length)) <= valid-cross lookback) OR (trend mode uses close/fast EMA crossover AND close > EMA(close, fast length) AND bars since crossover(close, EMA(close, fast length)) <= valid-cross lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI >= long minimum AND RSI <= long maximum AND (RSI direction confirmation disabled OR rising(RSI, direction lookback)))) AND MACD histogram < 0 AND abs(MACD histogram) > histogram minimum AND (highestbars(abs(MACD histogram), histogram peak lookback) = 0 OR lowestbars(abs(MACD histogram), histogram peak lookback)[1] = 0) AND (histogram average filter disabled OR abs(MACD histogram) >= (1 + above-average percentage) * negative MACD histogram magnitude average) AND (pullback filter disabled OR count(close < open bars in pullback lookback) >= pullback threshold
Short: position is flat AND start datetime <= datetime <= end datetime AND ((trend mode uses fast/slow EMA crossover AND EMA(close, fast length) < EMA(close, slow length) AND bars since crossunder(EMA(close, fast length), EMA(close, slow length)) <= valid-cross lookback) OR (trend mode uses close/slow EMA crossover AND close < EMA(close, slow length) AND bars since crossunder(close, EMA(close, slow length)) <= valid-cross lookback) OR (trend mode uses close/fast EMA crossover AND close < EMA(close, fast length) AND bars since crossunder(close, EMA(close, fast length)) <= valid-cross lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI >= short minimum AND RSI <= short maximum AND (RSI direction confirmation disabled OR falling(RSI, direction lookback)))) AND MACD histogram >= 0 AND MACD histogram > histogram minimum AND (highestbars(MACD histogram, histogram peak lookback) = 0 OR highestbars(MACD histogram, histogram peak lookback)[1] = 0) AND (histogram average filter disabled OR MACD histogram >= (1 + above-average percentage) * positive MACD histogram average) AND (pullback filter disabled OR count(close > open bars in pullback lookback) >= pullback threshold)
Exit: long, high >= entry price * (1 + long profit percentage) OR low <= entry price * (1 - long stop percentage)
Exit: short, low <= entry price * (1 - short profit percentage) OR high >= entry price * (1 + short stop percentage)
Calculation: EMA trend confirmation = bullish when the trend mode selects EMA crossover and EMA(close, fast length) >= EMA(close, slow length), or selects an EMA and close > the selected EMA; bearish when the trend mode selects EMA crossover and EMA(close, fast length) < EMA(close, slow length), or selects an EMA and close < the selected EMA; in either direction, the corresponding crossover or crossunder must have occurred within the EMA cross lookback
Calculation: Entry eligibility = chart bar time is within the configured start and end times AND, when pausing only after losses, (pause bars = 0 OR consecutive-loss count < loss limit) AND (no losing trade exists OR bars since the latest losing trade >= pause bars), otherwise (no closed trade exists OR bars since the latest closed trade >= pause bars); the consecutive-loss count resets on either EMA crossover
Long: Entry eligibility AND bullish EMA trend confirmation AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI length) on T1 >= long RSI minimum AND RSI(close, RSI length) on T1 <= long RSI maximum AND (RSI direction filter disabled OR RSI is rising over the RSI direction lookback))) AND MACD histogram(close, fast length, slow length, signal length) on T2 < 0 AND absolute MACD histogram > histogram minimum AND (absolute MACD histogram is highest over the histogram peak lookback OR the prior bar's absolute MACD histogram was lowest over that lookback) AND (histogram average filter disabled OR absolute MACD histogram >= (1 + histogram margin percentage / 100) × mean absolute negative MACD histogram over the histogram average lookback) AND (pullback filter disabled OR bearish candle count over the pullback lookback >= pullback threshold)
Short: Entry eligibility AND bearish EMA trend confirmation AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI length) on T1 >= short RSI minimum AND RSI(close, RSI length) on T1 <= short RSI maximum AND (RSI direction filter disabled OR RSI is falling over the RSI direction lookback))) AND MACD histogram(close, fast length, slow length, signal length) on T2 >= 0 AND MACD histogram > histogram minimum AND (MACD histogram is highest over the histogram peak lookback OR the prior bar's MACD histogram was highest over that lookback) AND (histogram average filter disabled OR MACD histogram >= (1 + histogram margin percentage / 100) × mean positive MACD histogram over the histogram average lookback) AND (pullback filter disabled OR bullish candle count over the pullback lookback >= pullback threshold)
Exit: Long, low <= average long entry price × (1 − long stop percentage)
Exit: Short, high >= average short entry price × (1 + short stop percentage)
