Calculation: Clamped ramp(x, lower, upper) = clamp((x - lower) / (upper - lower), 0, 1); inverse ramp = 1 - clamped ramp; band score = 0 outside its lower and upper bounds, ramps to 1 at its inner bounds, and stays 1 between the inner bounds.
Calculation: Volatility regime = dormant when ATR percentile rank and ATR / SMA(ATR, long-term lookback) are both below their dormant thresholds; otherwise low when either is below its low threshold, high when either is above its high threshold, and normal otherwise. Structure regime = trending when ADX exceeds its threshold; otherwise compressing when SMA(true range, short lookback) / SMA(true range, long lookback) is below its lower threshold, expanding when above its upper threshold, and ranging otherwise. Use separate lookbacks and thresholds; treat dormant as low for regime-selected thresholds.
Calculation: Compression score = weighted sum of inverse-ramp scores for (a) (highest(high, compression window) - lowest(low, compression window)) / ATR against regime-selected width limits, (b) stdev(close, compression window) / ATR against dispersion limits, (c) abs(SMA(close, drift lookback) - its value compression-window bars ago) / (compression window * ATR) against drift limits, (d) the compression-window range / the four-times-compression-window range against regime-selected relative-range limits, and (e) highest(true range, compression window) / compression-window range against a dominance limit. Scale the weighted sum to the score range.
Calculation: Accumulation touch statistics = within the forming range, count high touches where high is within the ATR-or-tick-based touch tolerance of the range high and min(open, close) rejects below it; count low touches analogously where max(open, close) rejects above the range low. Require the configured minimum bar separation between counted touches and cap the scan at the configured maximum. Equal-high and equal-low scores are zero with fewer than two respective touches; otherwise each is one minus the absolute difference between the two most extreme respective touch prices divided by the ATR-or-tick-based equality tolerance, clamped to the score range.
Calculation: Accumulation quality = weighted sum of (a) an inverse-ramp score for the four-times-window relative range, (b) a band score for frozen range width / ATR, (c) a ramp score for the lesser of high-touch count and low-touch count, (d) the mean of equal-high and equal-low scores, and (e) a band score for accumulation age. Use separate component weights and score boundaries.
Calculation: Liquidity pool strength = weighted sum of (a) the highest applicable tier for the level among previous-week, previous-day, previous-Asia, and last confirmed swing levels within the ATR-or-tick-based tier tolerance, with a baseline tier when none match, (b) the count of those levels within the ATR-or-tick-based cluster tolerance, ramped from zero to its configured cap, and (c) the range-boundary touch count, ramped from its configured minimum to maximum. Use separate tier values, tolerances, and weights. Previous-period and Asia levels use chart bars in the configured timezone; swing highs and lows use pivot high/low with separate left and right lookbacks and are available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Acceptance score = weighted sum of the clamped volume-beyond-level fraction, consecutive closes beyond the level, and closes beyond the level divided by excursion bars. The volume fraction is accumulated volume weighted by each bar's fraction beyond the level, divided by SMA(volume, volume lookback) times excursion bars; if that SMA is unavailable or zero, use the fraction of excursion bars that extended beyond the level instead.
Calculation: Manipulation quality = weighted sum of (a) a band score for sweep depth / ATR frozen at accumulation lock, with regime-selected bounds, (b) an inverse-ramp score for Acceptance score, (c) a ramp score for reclaim overshoot / locked ATR, (d) a ramp score for the rejection-wick fraction of the sweep-extreme bar's range, (e) Liquidity pool strength, and (f) a band score for excursion duration. For a long, reclaim overshoot is (close - raided low) / locked ATR and the rejection wick is the lower wick; for a short, use (raided high - close) / locked ATR and the upper wick.
Calculation: Distribution quality = weighted sum of (a) a ramp score for abs(close - raided level) / locked ATR, (b) zero without a qualifying imbalance, otherwise a base score plus a ramp score for imbalance size / locked ATR, (c) a base score plus a ramp score for abs(close - the opposite frozen accumulation boundary) / locked ATR, (d) a ramp score for the largest absolute candle body / candle range since manipulation confirmation, and (e) an inverse-ramp score for bars since manipulation confirmation. A qualifying long imbalance has low above high two bars earlier; a qualifying short imbalance has high below low two bars earlier; in either case its gap must meet the configured fraction of locked ATR. Use only the first qualifying imbalance and separate component weights and score boundaries.
Calculation: Directional higher-timeframe alignment = on chart bars, use EMA with the shorter lookback and EMA with the longer lookback, plus highest(high, higher-timeframe range lookback) and lowest(low, higher-timeframe range lookback), all from the last completed T1 bar. T1 must be at least the chart timeframe. Use the current chart-bar close to locate price within those higher-timeframe extremes. Combine a trend component (full when the EMA ordering agrees with the position side, neutral when equal, zero when opposed) and a premium/discount component (longs favor the lower part of the range and shorts the upper part), using separate weights and boundaries. When T1 is below the chart timeframe, use neutral alignment.
Calculation: Session prior = weighted fit of the session at accumulation lock, the session at manipulation reclaim, and the current signal-bar session. Sessions are the configured Asia, London, New York morning, New York afternoon, and outside-session windows, measured on intraday chart bars in the configured timezone. Use a separate fit parameter for every phase/session pair and separate phase weights. When session context is disabled, omit this term and renormalize the other context weights.
Calculation: Regime fit = volatility-regime fit multiplied by structure-regime fit, using a separate fit parameter for each regime state.
Calculation: Candidate target room = the band score of abs(target - signal close) / ATR when an opposing liquidity target qualifies, and the configured fallback room score otherwise. For a long, choose the nearest previous-week high, previous-day high, previous-Asia high, or confirmed swing high beyond the frozen accumulation high and at least the configured minimum risk distance above entry; reverse the levels and comparisons for a short. If none qualifies, target is entry plus or minus the configured fallback multiple of risk. Risk is based on the tick-rounded stop distance.
Calculation: Stop risk eligibility = abs(entry - stop after minimum-distance flooring but before tick rounding) / locked ATR. The raw long stop is the sweep low minus the configured ATR buffer; the raw short stop is the sweep high plus it. Enforce a minimum stop distance using the greater of the configured ATR distance and tick floor. Risk eligibility requires this ratio not to exceed the configured maximum. For risk and target calculations, use the tick-rounded stop and a risk distance at least one tick.
Calculation: Context score = weighted sum of directional higher-timeframe alignment, session prior when enabled, Regime fit, Candidate target room, and a directional position-in-range score. The position score favors the lower part of the range from frozen accumulation high to sweep low for longs and the upper part of the range from sweep high to frozen accumulation low for shorts. When session context is disabled, renormalize the remaining weights.
Calculation: Phase chain score = weighted geometric mean of Accumulation quality, Manipulation quality, and Distribution quality after normalization to the score range, multiplied by a penalty for recent opposing raids capped at the configured maximum. Structural score = the clamped phase chain score multiplied by the configured context adjustment, scaled to the score range. Phase validity requires each normalized phase quality to meet its separate floor.
Calculation: Directional analogue estimate = for each side, use its stored signal outcomes only when its library has at least the configured neighbour count. Fingerprint each outcome by normalized Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution quality, ATR percentile rank, structure regime, directional higher-timeframe alignment, pool strength, and target room. Rank stored same-side episodes by the sum across these features of log(1 + absolute feature difference); estimate success from the nearest configured neighbours weighted by inverse (1 + distance), combined with the library success fraction using the configured prior strength. Resolve stored outcomes over the configured chart-bar horizon: a bar hitting both target and stop is a loss; otherwise target hit is a win, stop hit is a loss; if neither is hit by the horizon, it is a win only when favorable excursion reaches its configured threshold and exceeds adverse excursion. Keep only the configured number of most recent outcomes per side.
