Calculation: bar range = max(high - low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Calculation: range ratio = bar range / SMA(bar range, range lookback)
Calculation: safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), using true range if ATR is unavailable, minimum tick size)
Calculation: dry-up pocket = on a confirmed chart bar where relative volume <= volume threshold and range ratio <= range threshold, the prior chart bar did not meet both conditions, and no reversal is active, create or replace the pocket if none exists, its age exceeds maximum pocket age, or its age >= pocket refresh age; set pocket high and low to highest(high) and lowest(low) over pocket window and pocket ATR to safe ATR; the pocket expires when its age > maximum pocket age
Calculation: reversal score for an active reversal = round(clamp(base score + clamp((volume threshold - min(relative volume, volume threshold)) / max(volume threshold, denominator floor) × volume score weight, 0, volume score weight) + clamp((range threshold - min(range ratio, range threshold)) / max(range threshold, denominator floor) × range score weight, 0, range score weight) + (long reversal ? clamp((close - pocket high) / safe ATR × close-response weight, 0, close-response cap) : clamp((pocket low - close) / safe ATR × close-response weight, 0, close-response cap)) + (long reversal ? clamp((close - low) / bar range × rejection score limit, 0, rejection score limit) : clamp((high - close) / bar range × rejection score limit, 0, rejection score limit)) + clamp(volatility-fit limit - abs(safe ATR / SMA(safe ATR, range lookback) - 1) × volatility-fit limit, 0, volatility-fit limit), score floor, score ceiling))
Long: on a confirmed chart bar after an active dry-up pocket forms and before it expires, with no reversal active, close > pocket high + pocket ATR × reversal-buffer ATR multiple AND close > open
Short: on a confirmed chart bar after an active dry-up pocket forms and before it expires, with no reversal active, close < pocket low - pocket ATR × reversal-buffer ATR multiple AND close < open
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, a long reversal is active, reversal score >= minimum ready score, close >= pocket low - pocket ATR × failure-shelf ATR multiple, pocket age <= maximum pocket age, and the prior chart bar's action state was not READY
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, a short reversal is active, reversal score >= minimum ready score, close <= pocket high + pocket ATR × failure-shelf ATR multiple, pocket age <= maximum pocket age, and the prior chart bar's action state was not READY
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar with a long reversal active, close < pocket low - pocket ATR × failure-shelf ATR multiple
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar with a short reversal active, close > pocket high + pocket ATR × failure-shelf ATR multiple
