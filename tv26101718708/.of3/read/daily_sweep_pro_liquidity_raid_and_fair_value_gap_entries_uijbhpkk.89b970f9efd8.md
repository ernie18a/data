Calculation: Structure bias = bullish when the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot strength, pivot strength, T1) is above the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot strength, pivot strength, T1) is above the preceding confirmed pivot low; bearish when both are below; otherwise neutral. Pivots run on T1 bars; values come from the last completed T1 bar on chart bars.
Calculation: Prior-period high/low = high/low of the last completed T1 bar or the previous completed execution-session range, according to the prior-period source setting. T1 values come from its last completed bar on chart bars; session values are computed on chart bars.
Calculation: Range-session high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured range session, computed on chart bars and available after session completion.
Calculation: T2 open = open of the still-forming T2 bar, sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Displacement body efficiency = high > low ? abs(close - open) / (high - low) : 0.
Calculation: Low-pool raid = low <= pool - raid-penetration ticks × tick size AND low[1] > pool - raid-penetration ticks × tick size AND (raid mode is wick-breach OR close >= pool).
Calculation: High-pool raid = high >= pool + raid-penetration ticks × tick size AND high[1] < pool + raid-penetration ticks × tick size AND (raid mode is wick-breach OR close <= pool).
Calculation: Gap entry price = tick-rounded selected near edge or midpoint of the gap; the near edge is the upper boundary for a bullish gap and the lower boundary for a bearish gap.
Calculation: Planned stop = lowest low from the raid bar through the FVG bar minus stop-buffer ticks × tick size for longs; highest high over those bars plus stop-buffer ticks × tick size for shorts.
Calculation: Planned target = selected prior-period high for longs or low for shorts, fixed when the gap arms.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with no active setup or position, inside the configured execution session, structure bias bullish, and current T1-period entry count below the entry cap; (weekly-open filter disabled OR low < the current T2 open); and at least one enabled, unconsumed low-side pool is breached: the latest confirmed chart-bar PivotLow(low, swing strength, swing strength), the completed Asian-range low, the completed premarket-range low, or the selected prior-period low. Range-session pools must be within the range-freshness duration; each pool must not have been consumed since its pivot, range-session, or T1-period reset; the Low-pool raid condition must hold for that pool.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with no active setup or position, inside the configured execution session, structure bias bearish, and current T1-period entry count below the entry cap; (weekly-open filter disabled OR high > the current T2 open); and at least one enabled, unconsumed high-side pool is breached: the latest confirmed chart-bar PivotHigh(high, swing strength, swing strength), the completed Asian-range high, the completed premarket-range high, or the selected prior-period high. Range-session pools must be within the range-freshness duration; each pool must not have been consumed since its pivot, range-session, or T1-period reset; the High-pool raid condition must hold for that pool.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar after a qualifying long-side raid, strictly after the raid bar and within the maximum bars from raid to gap, while still inside the execution session and structure bias remains bullish, a bullish FVG forms: low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= minimum-gap multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars AND (displacement filter disabled OR close[1] > open[1] AND Displacement body efficiency[1] >= displacement body threshold); planned risk = abs(gap entry price - planned stop) > 0 AND planned target - gap entry price > 0 AND (planned target - gap entry price) / planned risk >= minimum reward:risk.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar after a qualifying short-side raid, strictly after the raid bar and within the maximum bars from raid to gap, while still inside the execution session and structure bias remains bearish, a bearish FVG forms: high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= minimum-gap multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars AND (displacement filter disabled OR close[1] < open[1] AND Displacement body efficiency[1] >= displacement body threshold); planned risk = abs(gap entry price - planned stop) > 0 AND gap entry price - planned target > 0 AND (gap entry price - planned target) / planned risk >= minimum reward:risk.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar strictly after an armed bullish FVG, structure bias remains bullish, the execution session has not ended, the selected prior-period high target has not been reached since the gap formed through this bar, and low <= gap entry price.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar strictly after an armed bearish FVG, structure bias remains bearish, the execution session has not ended, the selected prior-period low target has not been reached since the gap formed through this bar, and high >= gap entry price.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed chart bar while long: low <= planned stop OR high >= planned target OR (execution session ends AND flatten-at-session-end is enabled).
Exit: Short, on a confirmed chart bar while short: high >= planned stop OR low <= planned target OR (execution session ends AND flatten-at-session-end is enabled).
