Calculation: Structure(T, pivot lookback) = On T bars, let A=ATR(ATR lookback), H0/H1=latest and previous confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), and L0/L1=latest and previous confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback); HH=H0>H1+A*swing buffer and LH=H0<H1-A*swing buffer when both highs exist; HL=L0>L1+A*swing buffer and LL=L0<L1-A*swing buffer when both lows exist; BOSup=H0 exists AND close[1]>H0+A*BOS buffer; BOSdown=L0 exists AND close[1]<L0-A*BOS buffer; midpoint=(H0+L0)/2 when both exist; bull score=major weight*(HL+BOSup)+minor weight*HH+midpoint weight*(close[1]>midpoint); bear score=major weight*(LH+BOSdown)+minor weight*LL+midpoint weight*(close[1]<midpoint); direction is bullish when bull score>=minimum score AND bull score>=bear score+score margin, bearish when bear score>=minimum score AND bear score>=bull score+score margin, otherwise neutral; return direction, HL, LH, BOSup, BOSdown, H0, L0. The T1 calculation runs on T1 bars through request.security and is read on chart bars with lookahead off; ATR uses the current, potentially forming T1 bar, and close[1] is the prior completed T1 close. The local calculation runs on chart bars.
Calculation: Context direction(T2) = On T2 bars, using only completed bars, let C/O/H/L be the prior bar's close/open/high/low, M=(H+L)/2, R=close[comparison offset], and E=EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1]; bull score=point weight*((C>O)+(C>M)+(C>R)+(C>E)); bear score=point weight*((C<O)+(C<M)+(C<R)+(C<E)); direction is neutral when context is disabled, bullish when bull score>=minimum score AND bull score>bear score, bearish when bear score>=minimum score AND bear score>bull score, otherwise neutral. The T2 calculation runs on T2 bars through request.security and is read on chart bars with lookahead off.
Calculation: Previous London-session levels = the highest high and lowest low during the prior completed London-session window, evaluated on chart bars using the session-window and timezone parameters; midpoint=(high+low)/2. Levels are unavailable until a prior session completes.
Calculation: London direction = neutral when London context is disabled; otherwise bullish when close>previous London-session high, bearish when close<previous London-session low, bullish when close>previous London-session midpoint, bearish when close<previous London-session midpoint, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Composite direction = let bull score=HTF weight*(Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bullish)+local weight*(Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).direction is bullish)+context weight*(Context direction(T2) is bullish)+session weight*(London direction is bullish), and bear score use the corresponding bearish comparisons; direction is bullish when bull score>=minimum score AND bull score>=bear score+score margin, bearish when bear score>=minimum score AND bear score>=bull score+score margin, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Possible bullish reversal = Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bearish AND (Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).HL OR Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).direction is bullish OR close>previous London-session high).
Calculation: Possible bearish reversal = Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bullish AND (Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).LH OR Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).direction is bearish OR close<previous London-session low).
Calculation: Confirmed bullish flip = Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bearish AND Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).HL AND Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).BOSup AND (close>Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).H0 OR close>previous London-session high OR Context direction(T2) is not bearish).
Calculation: Confirmed bearish flip = Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bullish AND Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).LH AND Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).BOSdown AND (close<Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).L0 OR close<previous London-session low OR Context direction(T2) is not bullish).
Calculation: Final direction = bullish when Confirmed bullish flip; bearish when Confirmed bearish flip; neutral when (Possible bullish reversal AND NOT Confirmed bullish flip) OR (Possible bearish reversal AND NOT Confirmed bearish flip); otherwise Composite direction.
Long: Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bullish OR Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).direction is bullish OR Context direction(T2) is bullish OR Final direction is bullish OR Possible bullish reversal
Short: Structure(T1, HTF pivot lookback).direction is bearish OR Structure(chart bars, local pivot lookback).direction is bearish OR Context direction(T2) is bearish OR Final direction is bearish OR Possible bearish reversal
