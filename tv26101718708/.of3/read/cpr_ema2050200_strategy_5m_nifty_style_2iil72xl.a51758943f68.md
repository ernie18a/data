Calculation: CPR bottom = (high(T1, last completed bar) + low(T1, last completed bar)) / 2, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: CPR top = 2 * ((high(T1, last completed bar) + low(T1, last completed bar) + close(T1, last completed bar)) / 3) - CPR bottom, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: EMA spread = EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) - EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)
Calculation: Long stop at entry = min(EMA(close, fast EMA lookback), CPR bottom) - minimum tick * stop-buffer multiplier under the EMA/CPR stop method; CPR bottom - minimum tick * stop-buffer multiplier under the CPR stop method; close - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier under the ATR stop method
Calculation: Short stop at entry = max(EMA(close, fast EMA lookback), CPR bottom) + minimum tick * stop-buffer multiplier under the EMA/CPR stop method; CPR bottom + minimum tick * stop-buffer multiplier under the CPR stop method; close + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier under the ATR stop method
Calculation: Long target at entry = entry price + abs(entry price - long stop at entry) * reward-to-risk parameter
Calculation: Short target at entry = entry price - abs(entry price - short stop at entry) * reward-to-risk parameter
Long: EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND (crossover requirement is disabled OR EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) AND close > CPR top AND close > EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND (gap-increase requirement is disabled OR (EMA spread > minimum-gap parameter AND EMA spread > EMA spread[1]))
Long: EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND (crossover requirement is disabled OR EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) AND close > CPR top AND close > EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND (gap-increase requirement is disabled OR (EMA spread > minimum-gap parameter AND EMA spread > EMA spread[1])) AND long stop at entry < close
Short: EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) < EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND (crossover requirement is disabled OR EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) AND close < CPR bottom AND close < EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND (gap-increase requirement is disabled OR (EMA spread < -minimum-gap parameter AND EMA spread < EMA spread[1]))
Short: EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) < EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND (crossover requirement is disabled OR EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) AND close < CPR bottom AND close < EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND (gap-increase requirement is disabled OR (EMA spread < -minimum-gap parameter AND EMA spread < EMA spread[1])) AND short stop at entry > close
Exit: long, low <= long stop at entry OR high >= long target at entry
Exit: short, high >= short stop at entry OR low <= short target at entry
