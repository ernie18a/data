Calculation: Atr = ATR(atr length), or the mean of (high - low) over all bars so far while ATR is undefined
Calculation: SideEnabled(support) = side setting is Bullish or Bullish AND Bearish, or side setting is Bullish OR Bearish and resistance is not active; SideEnabled(resistance) = side setting is Bearish or Bullish AND Bearish, or side setting is Bullish OR Bearish and support is not active
Calculation: Visible(score) = strong-only display is off OR score >= display threshold
Calculation: MarkAllowed(zone) = markers are enabled AND (zone has no placed marker OR marker mode is Last OR bars since zone's latest placed marker > marker gap)
Calculation: ZoneScore(zone, idle bars) = min(max(10 * (touch weight * min(zone touches / touches for full score, 1) + volume weight * VolumeFactor + wick weight * WickFactor) / (touch weight + volume weight + wick weight) * DecayFactor, 0), 10), where VolumeFactor = min((zone volume sum / bars since zone pivot bar) / SMA(volume, volume length), 1), WickFactor = min((zone rejection wick sum / zone rejection wick count) / Atr, 1), a factor is 0 when its divisor or count is not positive, and DecayFactor = 1 if decay is off, otherwise max(1 - (1 - decay floor) * min(idle bars / decay bars, 1), decay floor)
Calculation: Zone(side) for side support or resistance: opens on the bar where a pivot low (support) or pivot high (resistance) with left and right bars is confirmed, if no zone of that side is active, SideEnabled(side) holds, and there is no clash; clash = (opposite-side zone active and overlapping it) OR (overlapping any previously stored zone), checked only when overlap hiding is on; overlapping = overlap length > 0 and overlap length / smaller height >= overlap threshold; H = Atr * height multiplier, or close * height percent / 100 when height method is percent; with normalized heights top = pivot + H/2 and bottom = pivot - H/2, otherwise support spans pivot to pivot + H and resistance spans pivot - H to pivot; mid = (top + bottom) / 2; active from opening bar until it ends; support becomes broken on a bar with close < bottom and ends on a bar with close > top; resistance becomes broken on a bar with close > top and ends on a bar with close < bottom; state changes apply from the next bar; all zone processing (opening, state changes, touches) occurs only on bars where SideEnabled(side) holds; touches = number of bars through the current bar where the zone is intact or broken, its touch condition holds, and MarkAllowed(zone) holds; touch condition: support intact low <= top and close > top; support broken high >= bottom and close < bottom; resistance intact high >= bottom and close < bottom; resistance broken low <= top and close > top; zone volume sum, rejection wick sum and count, and idle bars since last update are kept as zone state
Long: SideEnabled(support) AND Zone(support) is intact AND low <= Zone(support).top AND close > Zone(support).top AND MarkAllowed(Zone(support)) AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(support), 0))
Long: SideEnabled(resistance) AND Zone(resistance) is broken AND low <= Zone(resistance).top AND close > Zone(resistance).top AND MarkAllowed(Zone(resistance)) AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(resistance), 0))
Short: SideEnabled(support) AND Zone(support) is broken AND high >= Zone(support).bottom AND close < Zone(support).bottom AND MarkAllowed(Zone(support)) AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(support), 0))
Short: SideEnabled(resistance) AND Zone(resistance) is intact AND high >= Zone(resistance).bottom AND close < Zone(resistance).bottom AND MarkAllowed(Zone(resistance)) AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(resistance), 0))
Long: (Zone(support) is active AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(support), idle bars of Zone(support))) AND |close - Zone(support).mid| <= close * proximity percent / 100) OR (a stored support zone record with stored price P has |close - P| <= close * proximity percent / 100)
Long: (Zone(support) is active AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(support), idle bars of Zone(support))) AND ZoneScore(Zone(support), idle bars of Zone(support)) >= alert threshold AND |close - Zone(support).mid| <= close * proximity percent / 100) OR (a stored support zone record with stored price P and stored score S has |close - P| <= close * proximity percent / 100 AND S >= alert threshold)
Short: (Zone(resistance) is active AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(resistance), idle bars of Zone(resistance))) AND |close - Zone(resistance).mid| <= close * proximity percent / 100) OR (a stored resistance zone record with stored price P has |close - P| <= close * proximity percent / 100)
Short: (Zone(resistance) is active AND Visible(ZoneScore(Zone(resistance), idle bars of Zone(resistance))) AND ZoneScore(Zone(resistance), idle bars of Zone(resistance)) >= alert threshold AND |close - Zone(resistance).mid| <= close * proximity percent / 100) OR (a stored resistance zone record with stored price P and stored score S has |close - P| <= close * proximity percent / 100 AND S >= alert threshold)
