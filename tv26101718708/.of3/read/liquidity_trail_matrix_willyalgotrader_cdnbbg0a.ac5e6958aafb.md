Calculation: Trailing bands and trend = initialize bullish; for bands k=1–4, distance = ATR(band ATR lookback) × base multiplier × (1 + (k−1) × spacing); in an uptrend, each band is max(source − distance, its prior value), and in a downtrend, min(source + distance, its prior value); before updating, flip bullish to bearish when source < the prior value of the selected flip band, or bearish to bullish when source > that prior value; reset all bands to source + distance on a bearish flip and source − distance on a bullish flip.
Calculation: Pending retest state = in a bullish trend, low <= a band arms or refreshes a long pending retest for the retest window, counting the touch bar; in a bearish trend, high >= a band does the same for a short pending retest; retain the deepest band touched while pending, decrement the window each chart bar, and clear pending retests on a trend flip or expiry.
Calculation: Retest score for each direction = points for deepest pending band touched (Band 2 points, Band 3 points, Band 1 points, or Band 4 points) + candle points + volume points + higher-timeframe bias points + trend-age points; long CLV = (close−low)/(high−low), short CLV = (high−close)/(high−low), using the zero-range CLV fallback when high=low; CLV > upper candle cutoff earns high candle points, CLV > lower candle cutoff earns middle points, otherwise low points; current volume > strong-volume ratio × prior-bar SMA(nz(volume, 0), volume lookback) earns strong volume points, volume > that baseline earns middle points, otherwise low points, and unavailable volume earns neutral points (use current-bar SMA if the prior-bar SMA is unavailable); on each chart bar, compare T1's last completed close with EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar): above it is bullish bias, otherwise bearish bias, with aligned, opposed, or unavailable bias earning the corresponding points; bars since the last trend flip within the inclusive trend-age bounds earn mature points, below the lower bound earn young points, and above the upper bound earn old points.
Calculation: Long initial stop = in ATR mode, entry − ATR(risk ATR lookback) × stop multiplier; otherwise, min(signal-bar low − ATR(risk ATR lookback) × wick buffer, entry − ATR(risk ATR lookback) × minimum wick-stop distance).
Calculation: Short initial stop = in ATR mode, entry + ATR(risk ATR lookback) × stop multiplier; otherwise, max(signal-bar high + ATR(risk ATR lookback) × wick buffer, entry + ATR(risk ATR lookback) × minimum wick-stop distance).
Calculation: Long target n = entry + (entry − long initial stop) × target-n risk multiple; short target n = entry − (short initial stop − entry) × target-n risk multiple, with separate multiples for each target.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until break-even is enabled and TP1 is first touched on a confirmed post-entry bar without the stop-at-bar-start being touched; then set the stop to entry beginning on the next bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with bar index >= max(warm-up ATR multiple × band ATR lookback, minimum warm-up bars), a bullish trend flip OR a bullish pending retest reclaims Band 1 (close > Band 1 and close > open), its retest score >= minimum retest score, and either no prior retest signal exists or chart bars since the prior retest signal in either direction >= shared cooldown.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with bar index >= max(warm-up ATR multiple × band ATR lookback, minimum warm-up bars), a bearish trend flip OR a bearish pending retest reclaims Band 1 (close < Band 1 and close < open), its retest score >= minimum retest score, and either no prior retest signal exists or chart bars since the prior retest signal in either direction >= shared cooldown.
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, low <= active stop captured at bar start OR high >= long target 3.
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, high >= active stop captured at bar start OR low <= short target 3.
