Calculation: Fast midpoint = average of the selected MA of close using the fast type and fast lower lookback, and the selected MA of close using the same type and fast upper lookback; both lookbacks are multiplied by the common MA length factor, and use their respective Laguerre gamma parameters if the selected type is LAGMA.
Calculation: Slow midpoint = average of the selected MA of close using the slow type and slow lower lookback, and the selected MA of close using the same type and slow upper lookback; both lookbacks are multiplied by the common MA length factor, and use their respective Laguerre gamma parameters if the selected type is LAGMA. The fast and slow types are separate; fast lower < fast upper < slow lower < slow upper lookback.
Calculation: Upper band = SMA(close, band center lookback) + EMA(true range, band range lookback) * band width multiplier.
Calculation: Lower band = SMA(close, band center lookback) - EMA(true range, band range lookback) * band width multiplier.
Calculation: Stop high = high when Renko adjustment is off, otherwise max(open, close). Stop low = low when Renko adjustment is off, otherwise min(open, close).
Calculation: Long peak = highest stop high from long entry through the current chart bar. Short trough = lowest stop low from short entry through the current chart bar.
Calculation: Long pullback event = Donchian midpoint(high, low, channel lookback) > its value slope lookback chart bars ago AND close > lower band AND close < Donchian midpoint(high, low, channel lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < lower RSI threshold, with this full condition false on the prior chart bar.
Calculation: Short pullback event = Donchian midpoint(high, low, channel lookback) < its value slope lookback chart bars ago AND close < upper band AND close > Donchian midpoint(high, low, channel lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > upper RSI threshold, with this full condition false on the prior chart bar.
Calculation: Long setup count = (long trend core ? (prior chart bar's long setup count > zero ? prior count plus one : initial setup count) : zero) + one if the long pullback event is true + one if Parabolic SAR(start, increment, maximum) > high. Long trend core means each of these conditions has held for at least the trend streak threshold consecutive chart bars: fast midpoint > slow midpoint and their difference is increasing; fast lower MA > fast upper MA and their difference is increasing; slow lower MA > slow upper MA and their difference is increasing.
Calculation: Short setup count = (short trend core ? (prior chart bar's short setup count > zero ? prior count plus one : initial setup count) : zero) + one if the short pullback event is true + one if Parabolic SAR(start, increment, maximum) < low. Short trend core means each of these conditions has held for at least the trend streak threshold consecutive chart bars: fast midpoint < slow midpoint and their difference is decreasing; fast lower MA < fast upper MA and their difference is decreasing; slow lower MA < slow upper MA and their difference is decreasing.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar while flat and neither side closed on the prior chart bar, when the configured order mode permits a long order and either maps long setups to long orders with long setup count >= entry count threshold, or swaps setup sides and maps short setups to long orders with short setup count >= entry count threshold.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar while flat and neither side closed on the prior chart bar, when the configured order mode permits a short order and either maps short setups to short orders with short setup count >= entry count threshold, or swaps setup sides and maps long setups to short orders with long setup count >= entry count threshold.
Exit: long, take profit enabled AND long peak >= entry price + entry price * take-profit percentage AND stop low <= long peak - long peak * min(trailing-profit percentage, take-profit percentage) OR stop loss enabled AND stop low <= entry price - entry price * stop-loss percentage OR trailing stop enabled AND stop low <= long peak - long peak * trailing-stop percentage OR (fast exit enabled OR (trailing stop disabled AND stop loss disabled)) AND the condition (fast midpoint on the prior chart bar < slow midpoint on the prior chart bar AND current open < slow midpoint on the prior chart bar) has just changed from false to true.
Exit: short, take profit enabled AND short trough <= entry price - entry price * take-profit percentage AND stop high >= short trough + short trough * min(trailing-profit percentage, take-profit percentage) OR stop loss enabled AND stop high >= entry price + entry price * stop-loss percentage OR trailing stop enabled AND stop high >= short trough + short trough * trailing-stop percentage OR (fast exit enabled OR (trailing stop disabled AND stop loss disabled)) AND the condition (fast midpoint on the prior chart bar > slow midpoint on the prior chart bar AND current open > slow midpoint on the prior chart bar) has just changed from false to true.
