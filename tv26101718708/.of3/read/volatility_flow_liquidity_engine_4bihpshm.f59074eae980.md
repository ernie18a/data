Calculation: Path-volatility forecast = max(0, rolling OLS forecast of annualized realized volatility from weighted EMAs of prior log return and the square root of weighted EMAs of prior squared log return, using separate shorter and longer lookbacks and a rolling calibration lookback; use annualized rolling return standard deviation when forecasting is disabled or not ready.
Calculation: Pressure = weighted mean of compression (100 minus PercentRank(ATR(volatility lookback), ranking lookback)), implied-volatility dislocation (PercentRank(abs(implied-volatility index minus selected realized volatility), ranking lookback)), basis tension (PercentRank(abs(change(100 × (related derivative price − underlying price) / underlying price, basis lookback)), ranking lookback)), convexity (PercentRank(abs(change(log(price-source return))), ranking lookback)), and inefficiency (100 × (1 − EfficiencyRatio(price source, efficiency lookback))); use separate configured factor weights, omit dislocation or basis when its external data is disabled, and renormalize over included factors. Selected realized volatility is the path-volatility forecast when enabled and ready, otherwise annualized rolling return standard deviation.
Calculation: Release direction = up score versus down score, where each score adds its configured breakout weight when close breaks above the prior lookback high or below the prior lookback low, its close-location weight when close is in the upper or lower half of the bar, and its momentum weight when change(price source, direction lookback) is nonnegative or negative respectively; ties are up.
Calculation: Flow bias = sign of the rolling z-score of synthetic signed-volume imbalance, provided its absolute z-score meets the configured threshold and its signed imbalance has the same sign; otherwise neutral. Signed imbalance uses the selected classifier: lower-timeframe tick rule (sum lower-timeframe volume by close versus open, splitting ties evenly, aggregated within each chart bar); bulk-volume classification (volume split by the normal CDF of return divided by its rolling standard deviation); or candle geometry (volume split by close location in the high-low range), falling back to geometry when tick data is unavailable. Clip the z-score to its configured bounds.
Calculation: Liquidity state = STRESSED when the standardized raw Amihud illiquidity jump reaches its configured stress threshold or the liquidity score is below its configured stress boundary; otherwise derive the score from the inverse mean percentile rank of the selected spread estimate, Amihud illiquidity, and absolute Kyle lambda, blending with the percentile rank of mean dollar volume when volume is available. Use EDGE, Abdi-Ranaldo, Corwin-Schultz, or their median as configured; volatility adjustment, when enabled, shrinks the high-low estimators' excess over EDGE according to the volatility forecast percentile. Estimator inputs use T1 values with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar live); estimators run on chart bars taking values from T1.
Calculation: Location levels = nearest resistance above and support below close among the prior completed session's POC, value-area high and low, live untested prior-session POCs, and anchored VWAP. Build each session profile by distributing bar volume across price bins spanning the high-low range; POC is the fullest bin, and the value area expands from it toward the fuller adjacent bin, ties upward, until the configured volume fraction is reached. A prior POC is live only while no bar spans it. Anchored VWAP is volume-weighted hlc3 (equal-weighted without volume), reset at the configured swing, session-open, period-open, or highest-volume-bar anchor.
Calculation: Candle-location imbalance = rolling sum of normalized close location × volume divided by rolling volume, or rolling mean normalized close location without volume; calculate on chart bars taking values from T1 when a separate liquidity calculation timeframe T1 is configured.
Calculation: Release eligibility = companion-price and implied-volatility data are available whenever their respective inputs are enabled; the highest PercentRank(Pressure, ranking lookback) over the spring-memory lookback meets the configured pressure percentile; the normalized (high − low) / ATR release score meets the configured release threshold; these conditions hold for the configured consecutive confirmation bars and the configured minimum spacing from the preceding event is met; and all enabled filters pass. The HTF filter passes when disabled or when the configured number of current-chart and higher-timeframe Ichimoku cloud biases agree with the release direction. Ichimoku bias is bullish above the displaced cloud, bearish below it, and neutral inside it; higher-timeframe calculations run on T2, T3 and T4 bars with lookahead off, using the last completed bar or the forming bar as configured. The regime filter passes when disabled or when the variance ratio is not significantly below its neutral band; compute it as variance of k-bar log returns divided by k times one-bar log-return variance, and apply the configured Lo-MacKinlay heteroskedastic significance test when enabled. The flow filter passes when disabled or flow bias does not oppose the release direction. On an expiry gamma-blast session, bypass each filter whose gamma gate is disabled. An expiry gamma-blast session is the configured weekly expiry weekday, or its last occurrence in the month when monthly-only is enabled, after the configured session start.
Long: Release eligibility AND release direction is up.
Short: Release eligibility AND release direction is down.
Long: Release eligibility AND release direction is up AND (liquidity requirement disabled OR Liquidity state is not STRESSED) AND (location gate disabled OR no resistance exists above close OR resistance minus close is at least the configured ATR buffer).
Short: Release eligibility AND release direction is down AND (liquidity requirement disabled OR Liquidity state is not STRESSED) AND (location gate disabled OR no support exists below close OR close minus support is at least the configured ATR buffer).
Long: Order-flow module enabled AND Flow bias is bullish.
Short: Order-flow module enabled AND Flow bias is bearish.
Long: PercentRank(Pressure, ranking lookback) meets the configured candle-color cutoff AND close > EMA(close, volatility lookback).
Short: PercentRank(Pressure, ranking lookback) meets the configured candle-color cutoff AND close <= EMA(close, volatility lookback).
Long: Candle-location imbalance exceeds the configured positive buyer cutoff.
Short: Candle-location imbalance is below the negative of the configured buyer cutoff.
