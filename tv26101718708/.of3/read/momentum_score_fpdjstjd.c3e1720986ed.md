Calculation: Return(n) = 100 × (close - close[n]) / close[n], where n counts chart bars.
Calculation: Benchmark return(n) = 100 × (benchmark close - benchmark close[n]) / benchmark close[n], using the selected benchmark index close sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Relative strength(n) = Return(n) - Benchmark return(n).
Calculation: Momentum persistence = c₁ × Return(shortest lookback) + c₂ × Return(second-shortest lookback) + c₃ × Return(third-shortest lookback) + c₄ × Return(longest lookback), where the four coefficient parameters sum to 1, c₁ is greater than each other coefficient, and c₂ = c₃ = c₄.
Calculation: Percentage from high = 100 × (close / Highest(high, high lookback) - 1), where the high lookback equals the longest return lookback.
Calculation: Up volume = sum of volume over the shared short lookback where close ≥ open; Down volume = sum of volume over the shared short lookback where close < open.
Calculation: Trend intensity = 100 × EMA(close, trend-short lookback) / EMA(close, trend-long lookback), when both EMAs are available and the trend-long EMA is positive.
Calculation: Weighted momentum total = sum of the 13 criterion weights for criteria that pass, otherwise zero; the weights sum to 100. Criteria: close > EMA(close, price-short lookback); close > EMA(close, price-middle lookback); close > EMA(close, price-long lookback); EMA(close, price-short lookback) > EMA(close, price-middle lookback) > EMA(close, price-long lookback); Relative strength(longest lookback) > 0; Relative strength(shortest lookback) > 0; Percentage from high ≥ -near-high distance parameter; if ATR(shared short lookback) is available, Highest(high, high lookback) - close ≤ ATR(shared short lookback), otherwise Percentage from high ≥ -ATR fallback distance parameter; Up volume > Down volume; Return(second-shortest lookback) > 0; Momentum persistence > 0; Trend intensity ≥ trend-intensity threshold when defined; SMA((high - low) / low × 100, shared short lookback) ≥ average-range threshold. All lookbacks count chart bars; EMA lookbacks are ordered trend-short < shared short < price-middle < trend-long < price-long, and the four return lookbacks are distinct and ordered shortest to longest. The shared short lookback is used for the price-short EMA, ATR, volume totals, and average range.
Long: Weighted momentum total crosses above the high-score threshold.

Exit: long, Weighted momentum total crosses below the medium-score threshold.
