Calculation: TrendlineValue(L, t) = P1 price + S × (t − P1 bar), where L is the line through anchors P1 (earlier) and P2 (later) and S = (P2 price − P1 price) ÷ (P2 bar − P1 bar)
Calculation: Anchors(scale) = alternating high and low anchors built from the matured pivots of the scale, PivotHigh(high, W, W)[pivot maturity] and PivotLow(low, W, W)[pivot maturity] with W = pivot window of the scale: the first pivot is appended; a pivot of the same type as the last anchor replaces it only if more extreme; a pivot of the opposite type is appended only if its price differs from the last anchor's price by more than ATR(ATR length) at the last anchor's bar × ATR multiplier of the scale; otherwise it is ignored
Calculation: ValidLine(P1, P2, t) = P1 bar ≥ t − lookback AND (P1 price > P2 price for resistance, P1 price < P2 price for support) AND (min slope filter off OR |S| ≥ A × min slope % ÷ 100) AND (max slope filter off OR |S| ≤ A × max slope % ÷ 100) AND (for every bar x from P1 bar + 1 to t except P2 bar, max(open, close) ≤ the line's value at x for resistance, or min(open, close) ≥ it for support) AND touches ≥ min touches AND t < extension limit; where S = (P2 price − P1 price) ÷ (P2 bar − P1 bar), A = (ATR(ATR length) at P1 bar + ATR(ATR length) at P2 bar) ÷ 2, tolerance = A × touch tolerance multiplier, touches = 2 + number of confirmed pivots of the same side at the scale's touch window with bar strictly between P1 and P2 bars and price within tolerance of the line, and extension limit = P2 bar + floor((P2 bar − P1 bar) × min(extension base multiplier + extension step per touch × (touches − 2), extension cap))
Calculation: Clash(A, C) = min(P2 bar of A, P2 bar of C) − max(P1 bar of A, P1 bar of C) is positive and exceeds overlap % ÷ 100 × (P2 bar − P1 bar) of A, or of C
Calculation: Contains(G, C) = P1 bar of C ≥ P1 bar of G AND P2 bar of C ≤ P2 bar of G AND |P1 price of C − TrendlineValue(G, P1 bar of C)| ≤ tolerance of G AND |P2 price of C − TrendlineValue(G, P2 bar of C)| ≤ tolerance of G
Calculation: Beats(C, A) = in the order touches, P2 bar, base length (P2 bar − P1 bar), |S|, the first pair that differs has the larger value for C; false if all are equal
Calculation: ActiveLines(t) = lines of the enabled scales and both sides admitted on bars before t and still active at t, where a line is active from the bar after its admission through the first of its break bar, its retirement bar, or its extension limit bar; after the break checks on each bar, each new anchor P2 added to Anchors(scale), longer scale first, forms candidate lines with each earlier anchor P1 of the same type among the most recent (scan depth of the scale − 1) such anchors, oldest first, where ValidLine(P1, P2, t) holds; a candidate C is rejected if the pivot scale is both, C is shorter-scale, and Contains(X, C) for an active longer-scale line X of the same side, or if some active line A of the same side and scale has Clash(A, C) and Beats(C, A) is false; otherwise C is admitted: active lines of the same side and scale with Clash(A, C) retire, and if the pivot scale is both and C is longer-scale, active shorter-scale lines X of the same side with Contains(C, X) retire; then, while active lines of that side and scale exceed max active lines, the oldest one retires
Calculation: RunLength(L, t) = number of consecutive bars ending at t, starting after the admission bar of L, whose close is beyond L (above for resistance, below for support)
Long: for some resistance line L in ActiveLines(t), RunLength(L, t) ≥ break count AND (volume filter off OR volume[k] > volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume length)[k] for some k from 0 to RunLength(L, t) − 1) AND (break distance filter off OR close − TrendlineValue(L, t) ≥ break distance × ATR(ATR length)) AND (MA filter off OR close > MA(close, MA type, chosen MA length))
Short: for some support line L in ActiveLines(t), RunLength(L, t) ≥ break count AND (volume filter off OR volume[k] > volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume length)[k] for some k from 0 to RunLength(L, t) − 1) AND (break distance filter off OR TrendlineValue(L, t) − close ≥ break distance × ATR(ATR length)) AND (MA filter off OR close < MA(close, MA type, chosen MA length))
Calculation: BrokenResistance(t) = the resistance line L in ActiveLines(t) with the highest TrendlineValue(L, t) among lines with RunLength(L, t) ≥ break count AND (volume filter off OR volume[k] > volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume length)[k] for some k from 0 to RunLength(L, t) − 1) AND (break distance filter off OR close − TrendlineValue(L, t) ≥ break distance × ATR(ATR length)) AND (MA filter off OR close > MA(close, MA type, chosen MA length)); none if no line qualifies
Calculation: BrokenSupport(t) = the support line L in ActiveLines(t) with the lowest TrendlineValue(L, t) among lines with RunLength(L, t) ≥ break count AND (volume filter off OR volume[k] > volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume length)[k] for some k from 0 to RunLength(L, t) − 1) AND (break distance filter off OR TrendlineValue(L, t) − close ≥ break distance × ATR(ATR length)) AND (MA filter off OR close < MA(close, MA type, chosen MA length)); none if no line qualifies
Exit: long, for some n from 1 to the negation window, close < TrendlineValue(BrokenResistance[n], t) AND close[k] ≥ TrendlineValue(BrokenResistance[n], t)[k] for every k from 1 to n − 1
Exit: short, for some n from 1 to the negation window, close > TrendlineValue(BrokenSupport[n], t) AND close[k] ≤ TrendlineValue(BrokenSupport[n], t)[k] for every k from 1 to n − 1
