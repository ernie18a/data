Calculation: AnchorOpen = open of the chart bar starting at the session anchor time in the configured timezone, held for that session
Calculation: CandleBody = abs(close - open)
Calculation: LowerWick = max(min(open, close) - low, 0)
Calculation: UpperWick = max(high - max(open, close), 0)
Calculation: Doji = high > low AND CandleBody / (high - low) <= doji-threshold parameter
Long: the wick label and anchor line are enabled on chart timeframes no greater than T2, and the bar is at or before the anchor wick-label expiry time, and close >= AnchorOpen, and max(AnchorOpen - lowest(low, chart bars from the anchor through the configured line-end time), 0) >= wick-size threshold parameter
Short: the wick label and anchor line are enabled on chart timeframes no greater than T2, and the bar is at or before the anchor wick-label expiry time, and close < AnchorOpen, and max(highest(high, chart bars from the anchor through the configured line-end time) - AnchorOpen, 0) >= wick-size threshold parameter
Long: on chart timeframes no greater than T1, a newly confirmed T1 bar received from the last completed T1 bar has a timestamp within the anchor-to-RB-cutoff window, RB display is enabled and its mode allows all blocks or this first bullish block of the session, and ((not Doji AND (candle-color filter disabled OR close > open) AND open >= AnchorOpen AND low < AnchorOpen AND close > AnchorOpen) OR (Doji AND doji handling selects the larger-wick direction AND low <= AnchorOpen AND high >= AnchorOpen AND LowerWick > UpperWick AND close > AnchorOpen)), and (wick filter allows all valid blocks OR LowerWick > CandleBody)
