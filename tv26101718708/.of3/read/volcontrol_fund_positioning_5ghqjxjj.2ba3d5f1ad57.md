Calculation: Equity log return = ln(close / prior T1 close); bond log return = ln(bond close / prior T1 bond close), calculated on T1 bars.
Calculation: Annualized EWMA volatility(return) = sqrt(annualization factor × max(short-decay EWMA variance of return², long-decay EWMA variance of return²)); initialize each variance with the first available return², then update as decay × prior variance + (1 − decay) × current return².
Calculation: Annualized window volatility(return, lookback) = sqrt(annualization factor × rolling mean(return², lookback)); short and long lookbacks are separate parameters, with the long lookback longer than the short lookback.
Calculation: Vol-control target exposure, as a fraction of capital, is the normalized weighted mean of available positive-weight components: the EWMA component uses annualized EWMA volatility of equity log returns, except in simple-weighted mode when it uses the greater of the short-window and long-window volatilities; the short-window and long-window components each use annualized window volatility of equity log returns; the multi-asset component is equity share × capped leverage from annualized EWMA volatility of (equity share × equity log return + (1 − equity share) × bond log return). Capped leverage is min(maximum leverage, target volatility / volatility), or maximum leverage when volatility is zero. Omit unavailable components; if all component weights are zero, use the EWMA component. Components require their model warm-up history. Calculated on T1 bars.
Calculation: Live exposure = target exposure from implementation-lag T1 bars ago. Calculated on T1 bars; it uses a still-forming T1 bar only when the implementation lag is zero, otherwise completed T1 bars.
Calculation: Scheduled exposure change = target exposure from implementation-lag-minus-one T1 bars ago − target exposure from implementation-lag T1 bars ago, calculated on T1 bars.
Calculation: One-T1-bar live exposure change, in percentage points = 100 × (live exposure − live exposure from one T1 bar ago), calculated on T1 bars.
Long: implementation lag ≥ 1 AND scheduled exposure change > scheduled-change deadband.
Exit: long, (implementation lag ≥ 1 AND scheduled exposure change < −scheduled-change deadband) OR (one-T1-bar live exposure change ≤ −deleveraging-alert threshold AND confirmed chart bar AND (chart is not intraday OR first chart bar of a new T1 session))
