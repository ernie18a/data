Calculation: Chart-bar momentum = LinReg(close − (((highest(high, KC lookback) + lowest(low, KC lookback)) / 2 + SMA(close, KC lookback)) / 2), KC lookback, current-bar offset).
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band AND Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band, where Bollinger bands are SMA(close, BB lookback) ± BB deviation multiplier × StDev(close, BB lookback), and Keltner bands are SMA(close, KC lookback) ± KC range multiplier × SMA(TrueRange or high − low, KC lookback). Expansion = Bollinger lower band < Keltner lower band AND Bollinger upper band > Keltner upper band. On each contiguous compressed chart-bar run, coil length counts bars, coil high and low are the run’s highest high and lowest low, and coil tightness is the lowest Bollinger half-width / Keltner half-width ratio (using a fallback parameter when the denominator is nonpositive). A release is compression on the previous chart bar and no compression on the current chart bar.
Calculation: Release direction = long when momentum > zero, or when momentum = zero and prior-bar momentum ≥ zero; otherwise short.
Calculation: HTF alignment for a side = true when the HTF filter is disabled or its EMA is unavailable; otherwise chart-bar close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) for long, or chart-bar close < that EMA for short.
Calculation: Strength score on a release = clamp(round-to-score-step of the sum of: (1) coil-length points, the return point for the first met cutoff among five descending coil-length cutoffs, else a fallback point; (2) coil-tightness points, the return point for the first met cutoff among three ascending tightness cutoffs using ≤, else a fourth point; (3) momentum-magnitude points, the return point for the first met cutoff among four descending cutoffs using abs(momentum) / highest(abs(momentum), momentum-normalization lookback), else a fifth point; (4) directional-acceleration points, the return point for the first met cutoff among four descending cutoffs using (momentum − prior momentum) for long or (prior momentum − momentum) for short, divided by the same highest-absolute-momentum value, else a fifth point; (5) volume points, the return point for the first met cutoff among four descending cutoffs using volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback), else a fifth point; (6) candle points, a first point if both abs(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ its first cutoff and (high − low) / ATR(ATR lookback) ≥ its first cutoff, otherwise a second point if either ratio meets its second cutoff, else a third point; (7) band-widening points, the return point for the first met cutoff among three descending cutoffs using Bollinger width / SMA(Bollinger width, width-average lookback), else a fourth point; (8) an alignment point if HTF alignment is true; and (9) an RSI-agreement point if RSI(close, RSI lookback) meets the long cutoff for long or the short cutoff for short). Each cutoff and point, the score floor and ceiling, and the rounding step are parameters; use the code’s fallback parameters when a ratio denominator is nonpositive.
Calculation: Cooldown passes = cooldown is disabled OR there was no previous signal OR at least the cooldown length in chart bars has elapsed since the previous signal.
Calculation: At entry, risk distance = clamp(abs(entry price − (coil low − ATR-at-entry × stop-buffer for long, or coil high + ATR-at-entry × stop-buffer for short)), ATR-at-entry × risk-floor, ATR-at-entry × risk-cap); freeze the resulting levels for the position. Stop = entry price − risk distance for long or entry price + risk distance for short. Third target = entry price + risk distance × third-target R multiple for long or entry price − risk distance × third-target R multiple for short.
Long: On chart-bar close, release AND coil length on the prior bar ≥ minimum coil length AND (strict-expansion setting disabled OR current-bar expansion) AND release direction is long AND strength score ≥ minimum strength AND (strong-only setting disabled OR strength score ≥ strong threshold) AND HTF alignment AND cooldown passes AND no active position AND no position exit on this bar AND ATR is available.
Short: On chart-bar close, release AND coil length on the prior bar ≥ minimum coil length AND (strict-expansion setting disabled OR current-bar expansion) AND release direction is short AND strength score ≥ minimum strength AND (strong-only setting disabled OR strength score ≥ strong threshold) AND HTF alignment AND cooldown passes AND no active position AND no position exit on this bar AND ATR is available.
Exit: Long, low ≤ frozen stop OR high ≥ frozen third target.
Exit: Short, high ≥ frozen stop OR low ≤ frozen third target.
