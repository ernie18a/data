Long: On T1 bars, long signals are enabled AND datetime is between the configured start and end datetimes AND close crosses above the lower Bollinger Band(close, lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, basis moving-average type) AND no long position is open AND (filters are disabled OR (volume > long volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close > SMA(close, trend lookback))).
Short: On T1 bars, short signals are enabled AND datetime is between the configured start and end datetimes AND close crosses below the upper Bollinger Band(close, lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, basis moving-average type) AND no short position is open AND (filters are disabled OR (volume > short volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close < SMA(close, trend lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold)).
Exit: Long, on T1 bars, long signals are enabled AND datetime is between the configured start and end datetimes AND a long position is open AND close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band(close, lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, basis moving-average type).
Exit: Short, on T1 bars, short signals are enabled AND datetime is between the configured start and end datetimes AND a short position is open AND close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band(close, lookback, standard-deviation multiplier, basis moving-average type).
