Calculation: Master = weighted mean of PercentRank(volume, Li) on each Ti, using separate weights W1–W5 and renormalizing over available values; use 50 if all are unavailable. T1–T5 series are evaluated on their own bars and sampled on chart bars from the current forming bar in realtime and the last completed bar historically; T1 is fastest and T5 slowest.
Calculation: RegularBearDiv(high, V, P) = true when a newly confirmed pivot high of V, using P bars on each side, has a higher price high and lower V than the preceding pivot high of V.
Calculation: RegularBullDiv(low, V, P) = true when a newly confirmed pivot low of V, using P bars on each side, has a lower price low and higher V than the preceding pivot low of V.
Long: close > open on chart bars OR close > open on any T1–T5 bars, sampled on chart bars from the current forming bar in realtime and the last completed bar historically.
Short: close < open on chart bars OR close < open on any T1–T5 bars, sampled on chart bars from the current forming bar in realtime and the last completed bar historically.
Long: RegularBullDiv(low, PercentRank(volume, Li), P) on Ti bars for any i from 1 to 5, sampled on chart bars as specified above, OR RegularBullDiv(low, Master, P) on chart bars.
Short: RegularBearDiv(high, PercentRank(volume, Li), P) on Ti bars for any i from 1 to 5, sampled on chart bars as specified above, OR RegularBearDiv(high, Master, P) on chart bars.
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND (T1 Volume Percentile <= strong lower threshold AND T5 Volume Percentile >= strong upper threshold).
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND T1 Volume Percentile <= moderate-bottom T1 threshold AND T5 Volume Percentile >= moderate-bottom T5 threshold AND NOT (T1 Volume Percentile >= strong upper threshold AND T5 Volume Percentile <= strong lower threshold) AND NOT (T1 Volume Percentile <= strong lower threshold AND T5 Volume Percentile >= strong upper threshold).
Short: chart bar is confirmed AND (T1 Volume Percentile >= strong upper threshold AND T5 Volume Percentile <= strong lower threshold).
