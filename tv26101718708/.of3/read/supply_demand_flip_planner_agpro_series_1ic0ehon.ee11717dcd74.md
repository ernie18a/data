Calculation: Supply upper bound = latest PivotHigh(high, pivot left bars, pivot right bars)
Calculation: Supply lower bound = Supply upper bound - zone depth multiple * ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot high bar
Calculation: Demand lower bound = latest PivotLow(low, pivot left bars, pivot right bars)
Calculation: Demand upper bound = Demand lower bound + zone depth multiple * ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot low bar
Calculation: Armed supply upper bound = Supply upper bound, if bars since the pivot high bar <= max zone age bars and no earlier bar since its confirmation closed above Supply upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer multiple; otherwise NA
Calculation: Armed demand lower bound = Demand lower bound, if bars since the pivot low bar <= max zone age bars and no earlier bar since its confirmation closed below Demand lower bound - ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer multiple; otherwise NA
Calculation: Active flip zone = the most recent up lock or down lock, if it still holds, else none. An up lock is a bar with close > Armed supply upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer multiple, with upper bound = Supply upper bound and lower bound = Supply lower bound on that bar, direction up. A down lock is a bar with close < Armed demand lower bound - ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer multiple, with upper bound = Demand upper bound and lower bound = Demand lower bound on that bar, direction down. A lock still holds if no earlier bar since the lock closed beyond its invalidation line (up: close < lower bound - ATR(ATR lookback) * invalidation buffer multiple; down: close > upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) * invalidation buffer multiple), and either bars since the lock <= retest window bars or a Long or Short acceptance held on an earlier bar within the last retest window bars
Calculation: Flip zone touch = Active flip zone exists AND high >= lower bound of Active flip zone AND low <= upper bound of Active flip zone
Calculation: Ready threshold = minimum ready score - confirmation bonus of the selected confirmation mode
Calculation: Flip quality score = break weight * clamp(break strength / break scale, 0, 1) + retest weight * clamp(retest depth / retest scale, 0, 1) + wick weight * clamp(wick response / wick scale, 0, 1) + body weight * clamp(abs(close - open) / (high - low) / body scale, 0, 1) + source weight * clamp(zone size / ATR(ATR lookback) at the lock bar / source scale, 0, 1) + freshness weight * clamp(1 - bars since Active flip zone lock / retest window bars, 0, 1) + volume term, where zone size = upper bound of Active flip zone - lower bound of Active flip zone, volume term = volume weight * clamp(volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) / volume scale, 0, 1) if relative volume scoring is enabled else volume fallback score, and for an up flip break strength = (close - upper bound of Active flip zone) / ATR(ATR lookback), retest depth = (upper bound of Active flip zone - low) / zone size, wick response = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), while for a down flip break strength = (lower bound of Active flip zone - close) / ATR(ATR lookback), retest depth = (high - lower bound of Active flip zone) / zone size, wick response = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low)
Long: close > Armed supply upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer multiple
Short: close < Armed demand lower bound - ATR(ATR lookback) * break buffer multiple
Long: Active flip zone direction is up AND BarsSince(Flip zone touch) <= retest recency bars AND close > upper bound of Active flip zone + ATR(ATR lookback) * acceptance buffer multiple AND Flip quality score >= Ready threshold
Short: Active flip zone direction is down AND BarsSince(Flip zone touch) <= retest recency bars AND close < lower bound of Active flip zone - ATR(ATR lookback) * acceptance buffer multiple AND Flip quality score >= Ready threshold
Exit: long, Active flip zone direction is up AND close < lower bound of Active flip zone - ATR(ATR lookback) * invalidation buffer multiple
Exit: short, Active flip zone direction is down AND close > upper bound of Active flip zone + ATR(ATR lookback) * invalidation buffer multiple
