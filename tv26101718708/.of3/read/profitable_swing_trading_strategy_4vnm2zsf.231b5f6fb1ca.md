Calculation: Long stop level = entry-signal bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar * ATR stop multiplier when ATR stops are enabled, otherwise entry-signal bar close * long stop factor
Calculation: Long target level = entry-signal bar close + (entry-signal bar close - long stop level) * risk/reward ratio
Calculation: Short stop level = entry-signal bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar * ATR stop multiplier when ATR stops are enabled, otherwise entry-signal bar close * short stop factor
Calculation: Short target level = entry-signal bar close - (short stop level - entry-signal bar close) * risk/reward ratio
Long: ((EMA(close, short lookback) crosses above EMA(close, medium lookback) AND close > EMA(close, long lookback)) OR (EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, medium lookback) AND EMA(close, medium lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND close > EMA(close, medium lookback) AND close < EMA(close, medium lookback) * upper proximity factor AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > MACD signal line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long-entry lower RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long-entry upper RSI threshold)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))
Short: ((EMA(close, short lookback) crosses below EMA(close, medium lookback) AND close < EMA(close, long lookback)) OR (EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, medium lookback) AND EMA(close, medium lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback) AND close < EMA(close, medium lookback) AND close > EMA(close, medium lookback) * lower proximity factor AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) < MACD signal line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short-entry upper RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short-entry lower RSI threshold)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))
Exit: Long, EMA(close, short lookback) crosses below EMA(close, medium lookback) OR close < EMA(close, long lookback) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long-exit RSI threshold OR low <= long stop level OR high >= long target level
Exit: Short, EMA(close, short lookback) crosses above EMA(close, medium lookback) OR close > EMA(close, long lookback) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short-exit RSI threshold OR high >= short stop level OR low <= short target level
