Calculation: ATR floor = max(ATR(ATR length), minimum price increment)
Calculation: Displacement demand score = min(total score cap, min(break component cap, max(0, (close - highest(high, displacement break lookback through prior bar)) / ATR floor) × break factor) + min(body component cap, abs(close - open) / ATR floor × body factor) + min(range component cap, (high - low) / ATR floor × range factor) + (volume confirmation enabled ? min(volume component cap, volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), volume denominator floor) × volume factor) : no-volume score) + (close > highest(high, displacement break lookback through prior bar) ? close-break bonus : 0)); displacement supply score uses max(0, (lowest(low, displacement break lookback through prior bar) - close) / ATR floor) and close < that prior low for its break component and close-break bonus
Calculation: Displacement zone boundaries = use the nearest prior opposite-color candle within the opposite-candle search lookback; the source mode sets top and bottom to full-candle extremes, body extremes, or the halfway points between corresponding wick and body extremes; if zone height is below max(ATR floor × minimum displacement-zone-height factor, minimum price increment × minimum tick-width factor), expand it symmetrically about its midpoint to that minimum height
Calculation: Accepted displacement demand zone = close > open AND high - low >= ATR floor × sensitivity-selected impulse-range factor AND abs(close - open) >= ATR floor × sensitivity-selected body factor AND (volume confirmation disabled OR (SMA(volume, volume lookback) is defined AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (close-break preference disabled OR close > highest(high, displacement break lookback through prior bar)) AND displacement demand score >= weak-zone score threshold AND a prior bearish candle exists within the opposite-candle search lookback AND its adjusted zone boundaries are valid AND no similar stored demand zone exists; displacement supply zone is the direction-reversed condition using a bearish candle, a prior bullish candle, the supply score and supply boundaries
Calculation: Similar stored zone = same direction, state earlier than invalidated, stored tier at least the candidate tier, and either both corresponding boundaries are within max(current ATR floor × merge-distance factor, minimum price increment × merge-tick factor) or overlap divided by the smaller zone height meets the overlap-merge threshold; candidate tier is valid when its score meets the valid-zone score threshold, otherwise weak
Calculation: Pivot zone volume-reaction score = max(score floor, (base-volume multiplier × pivot-volume weight + min(reaction move / ATR at pivot, reaction saturation cap) / reaction normalization scale × pivot-reaction weight) / max(pivot-volume weight + pivot-reaction weight, weight-sum floor)); base-volume multiplier is average volume in the base window ending at the pivot divided by the volume SMA at the pivot, falling back to that average if the SMA is unavailable and to the neutral multiplier if the denominator is not positive; reaction move for a pivot high is pivot high minus the lowest low from pivot through confirmation, and for a pivot low is the highest high over those bars minus pivot low
Calculation: Pivot zone boundaries = take the base window ending at the confirmed pivot; wick mode uses its high and low extremes, body mode uses its highest body high and lowest body low, and hybrid mode uses wick high with body low for supply or body high with wick low for demand; if height is below ATR floor at the pivot × minimum pivot-zone-height factor, expand symmetrically about its midpoint to that minimum; if above ATR floor at the pivot × maximum pivot-zone-height factor, retain the supply top and lower its bottom to the maximum height, or retain the demand bottom and raise its top to the maximum height
Calculation: Accepted pivot demand zone = pivot fallback enabled AND pivot zones enabled with mode including demand AND PivotLow(low, pivot-left bars, pivot-right bars) confirms AND its filtered base-volume multiplier meets the minimum pivot-volume multiplier when the pivot volume filter is enabled AND its adjusted boundaries are valid AND no similar stored demand zone exists; accepted pivot supply zone uses the corresponding confirmed PivotHigh, supply boundaries, and mode including supply
Calculation: Zone lifecycle = each accepted zone starts active; after its age meets the mitigation delay, it becomes mitigated when the selected mitigation mode is met (wick overlap, close inside, or penetration to the configured depth); an active, mitigated, held, or rejected demand zone becomes a breaker when close < bottom - ATR floor × sensitivity-selected wick tolerance, and the corresponding supply condition is close > top + ATR floor × sensitivity-selected wick tolerance; a breaker becomes invalidated when close passes the same boundary by ATR floor × breaker-confirmation factor; while mitigated and within the reaction window from mitigation, demand becomes held when high >= top + ATR floor × reaction-confirmation factor AND close > top, and supply becomes rejected when low <= bottom - ATR floor × reaction-confirmation factor AND close < bottom; held and rejected states persist until a breaker transition; zones are removed when age exceeds the zone-retention age factor × maximum zone age
Calculation: Eligible active demand = any stored demand zone whose state is active, close is between its boundaries, and tier is above weak OR weak zones are enabled OR it is a pivot zone; eligible active supply is the direction-reversed condition
Calculation: Demand core promotion = an unvalidated demand zone reaches the core-validation lookback, has active state and mid tier, and is a core candidate; core candidate means score >= core-candidate score threshold AND close broke above the prior displacement range AND the displacement volume filter passes; demand validation requires lowest(close, core-validation lookback) >= bottom - ATR floor × core-hold buffer factor AND highest(high, core-validation lookback) >= top + ATR floor × core-continuation factor; supply promotion reverses these comparisons and uses highest close and lowest low
Calculation: Demand hold confirmation = a demand zone in mitigated state, still within the reaction window from mitigation, with high >= top + ATR floor × reaction-confirmation factor AND close > top; supply rejection confirmation uses a mitigated supply zone with low <= bottom - ATR floor × reaction-confirmation factor AND close < bottom
Long: accepted displacement demand zone OR accepted pivot demand zone OR eligible active demand OR demand core promotion OR demand hold confirmation OR (zone markers enabled AND an eligible demand zone is held)
Short: accepted displacement supply zone OR accepted pivot supply zone OR eligible active supply OR supply core promotion OR supply rejection confirmation OR (zone markers enabled AND an eligible supply zone is rejected)
