Calculation: Order-block score = rounded weighted percentage of body strength, volume strength, displacement strength, and trend alignment; body strength = min(abs(close - open) / safe ATR, strength cap) / strength cap; volume strength = min(volume / safe average volume, strength cap) / strength cap; displacement strength = min(max(direction-adjusted displacement / safe ATR, 0), strength cap) / strength cap; safe ATR = ATR(ATR lookback), or minimum tick when zero; safe average volume = SMA(volume, volume lookback), or one when zero; direction-adjusted displacement = close - highest(high, displacement lookback)[1] for bullish candidates or lowest(low, displacement lookback)[1] - close for bearish candidates; trend alignment is true when bullish close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) or bearish close < it; use separate body-volume and displacement-trend weights.
Calculation: Order-block zone = high-low range of the most recent opposite-body candle within origin lookback before a qualifying displacement; retain up to the maximum-blocks-per-side parameter, discarding the oldest blocks when that limit is exceeded.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close > highest(high, displacement lookback)[1] AND close > open AND order-block score >= minimum score AND a prior bearish-body candle exists within origin lookback.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close < lowest(low, displacement lookback)[1] AND close < open AND order-block score >= minimum score AND a prior bullish-body candle exists within origin lookback.
Long: A retained bullish zone created on an earlier bar is active, close >= its base, AND ((touch mode uses wicks AND low <= its top) OR (touch mode uses closes AND close <= its top)).
Short: A retained bearish zone created on an earlier bar is active, close <= its top, AND ((touch mode uses wicks AND high >= its base) OR (touch mode uses closes AND close >= its base)).
Long: A retained bullish zone created on an earlier bar is unmitigated AND close < its base.
Short: A retained bearish zone created on an earlier bar is unmitigated AND close > its top.
