Calculation: chart-bar source and squared source = volume-weighted means of the selected price source and its square across the current chart bar’s T2 intrabars; use arithmetic means when their total volume is zero, and the chart-bar source and its square when no T2 intrabars are available; use chart bars when T2 is not lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: session VWAP = cumulative(chart-bar source × chart-bar volume weight) / cumulative chart-bar volume weight; calculate on chart bars and reset when a chart bar enters a new T1 period; the weight is chart volume or external volume at the chart timeframe.
Calculation: session weighted standard deviation = sqrt(max(cumulative(chart-bar squared source × chart-bar volume weight) / cumulative chart-bar volume weight − session VWAP², 0)); calculate on chart bars and reset when a chart bar enters a new T1 period.
Calculation: band 1 upper/lower = session VWAP ± band-1 multiplier parameter × session weighted standard deviation.
Calculation: band 2 upper/lower = session VWAP ± band-2 multiplier parameter × session weighted standard deviation.
Calculation: bar delta = sum of T2 intrabar deltas within the current chart bar, using chart bars when T2 is not lower than the chart timeframe; for the Close Location method each intrabar delta is volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or volume × sign(close − previous intrabar close) when its range is zero; for the Up/Down Bar method it is positive volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, or volume × sign(close − previous intrabar close) when close = open; the first intrabar’s previous close is its open.
Calculation: session CVD = cumulative bar delta, reset when a chart bar enters a new T1 period; CVD bar high/low = session CVD at chart-bar start plus the maximum/minimum running cumulative T2 intrabar delta within the current chart bar, including zero.
Calculation: time-of-day relative volume = current chart-bar volume weight divided by the mean volume weight of prior completed chart bars with the same time of day; an undefined value counts as zero.
