Calculation: New York opening range = maximum(high) and minimum(low) across bars in each configured New York session
Calculation: London bias = direction of the first London opening-range signal during the configured London signal window; neutral if none; reset each session day
Long: After the London session ends, (direct-break setting AND close crosses above the London opening-range high OR retest setting AND the first post-session close outside the range is above its high AND, after a later low <= that high, a close on that or a later bar is > that high AND no retest signal has fired that session)
Short: After the London session ends, (direct-break setting AND close crosses below the London opening-range low OR retest setting AND the first post-session close outside the range is below its low AND, after a later high >= that low, a close on that or a later bar is < that low AND no retest signal has fired that session)
