Calculation: smoothed RSI = RSI(source, RSI lookback), optionally smoothed by the selected EMA, SMA, or RMA over a separate smoothing lookback
Calculation: signal line = selected EMA, SMA, or RMA of smoothed RSI over signal lookback
Calculation: weekly RSI = RSI(close, weekly RSI lookback) on T1 bars, using the T1 bar still forming and sampled on chart bars
Calculation: relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) when that SMA is nonzero, otherwise na
Long: smoothed RSI crosses above extreme-low RSI level AND current chart bar is confirmed
Long: smoothed RSI crosses above oversold RSI level AND current chart bar is confirmed AND (trend filter disabled OR ((close-above-fast filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, shorter lookback)) AND (fast-above-slow filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) > SMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (fast-slope filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, shorter lookback)[slope lookback]))) AND (weekly filter disabled OR weekly RSI >= weekly RSI minimum) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= relative-volume minimum) AND (signal confirmation disabled OR smoothed RSI > signal line)
Long: smoothed RSI crosses above oversold RSI level AND current chart bar is confirmed AND (trend filter disabled OR ((close-above-fast filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, shorter lookback)) AND (fast-above-slow filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) > SMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (fast-slope filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, shorter lookback)[slope lookback]))) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= relative-volume minimum) AND (signal confirmation disabled OR smoothed RSI > signal line) AND weekly RSI >= weekly RSI minimum + weekly confirmation buffer AND relative volume >= high-conviction relative-volume threshold
