Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = an enabled hammer with lower wick >= body × dominant-wick/body threshold, upper wick <= body × opposite-wick/body maximum, and close location >= bullish hammer threshold; OR an enabled bullish engulfing with close > open, prior close < prior open, open <= prior close, and close >= prior open; OR an enabled bullish pin with lower wick >= candle range × pin-wick fraction, close location >= bullish pin threshold, and body/candle range <= pin-body fraction; OR an enabled outside reversal with high > prior high, low < prior low, close > open, and close > (high + low)/2; OR an enabled morning star with close[2] < open[2], abs(close[1] - open[1]) <= (high[1] - low[1]) × star-body fraction, close > open, and close >= (open[2] + close[2])/2. Candle range = max(high - low, minimum tick); body = abs(close - open); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); close location = (close - low)/candle range.
Calculation: Bearish reversal pattern = an enabled shooting star with upper wick >= body × dominant-wick/body threshold, lower wick <= body × opposite-wick/body maximum, and close location <= bearish shooting-star threshold; OR an enabled bearish engulfing with close < open, prior close > prior open, open >= prior close, and close <= prior open; OR an enabled bearish pin with upper wick >= candle range × pin-wick fraction, close location <= bearish pin threshold, and body/candle range <= pin-body fraction; OR an enabled outside reversal with high > prior high, low < prior low, close < open, and close < (high + low)/2; OR an enabled evening star with close[2] > open[2], abs(close[1] - open[1]) <= (high[1] - low[1]) × star-body fraction, close < open, and close <= (open[2] + close[2])/2.
Calculation: Candidate direction = bullish when the bullish reversal pattern is true; otherwise bearish when the bearish reversal pattern is true; otherwise none.
Calculation: Candidate pattern quality = wick-quality weight × (bullish ? lower wick/candle range : upper wick/candle range) + close-quality weight × (bullish ? close location : 1 - close location) + body-quality weight × (1 - body/candle range), clamped to the quality-score bounds. If both reversal patterns are true, use bullish direction.
Calculation: Key-level distance and confluence = for a bullish candidate use its low and for a bearish candidate its high; among individually enabled prior completed T1 high/low, current-session high/low, latest confirmed chart-bar pivot high/low, session VWAP, nearest configured round number, and each of two optional manual levels, find the minimum absolute distance and count levels within ATR × key-level proximity. T1 high/low values are taken from the last completed T1 bar and carried onto chart bars; pivots use separate left/right lookbacks and are confirmed after the right-side bars.
Calculation: Candidate setup quality = candidate pattern quality × (away from enabled key levels ? max(0, 1 - away-from-level penalty/100) : 1) + (near an enabled key level ? max(confluence count - 1, 0) × per-additional-level bonus : 0), clamped to the quality-score bounds; away from enabled key levels means no enabled level is within ATR × key-level proximity.
Calculation: Contextual trend on each of T2, T3, and T4 = bullish when EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) and timeframe close > the shorter-lookback EMA; bearish when both inequalities are reversed; otherwise neutral. These values use the bar still forming on each timeframe, are taken on chart bars, and are carried onto chart bars.
Calculation: Trap risk score at candidate formation = clamp(sum of the separate weights for poor location, nearby unswept swing liquidity, round-surface bounce, news-like volume/range event, contextual contradiction, repeated candidate-level tests, and distance from enabled key levels, plus the separate extra-contextual-timeframe weight for each contradiction beyond the first), to the risk-score bounds. Poor location means close's position in the rolling high/low range is within the configured half-width around its midpoint. Nearby unswept liquidity means the latest confirmed swing low is below a bullish candidate low, or swing high is above a bearish candidate high, within ATR × liquidity distance. A round-surface bounce means close is within ATR × round tolerance of the nearest configured round number and the bullish candidate low is at or above it, or the bearish candidate high is at or below it. A news-like event requires volume/SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), volume z-score over its separate z-score lookback, and true-range z-score over that lookback each to meet its separate threshold. Contextual contradiction means the candidate opposes at least the configured number of T2/T3/T4 trends. Repeated tests count prior chart bars in the touch lookback whose high/low span intersects the candidate level within ATR × touch tolerance, counting contacts only when separated by at least the minimum separation; the count must meet the over-tested threshold.
