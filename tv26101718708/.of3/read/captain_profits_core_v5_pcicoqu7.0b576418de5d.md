Calculation: selected source = close when Heikin-Ashi source is disabled, otherwise Heikin-Ashi close
Calculation: volume is fading(lookback) = volume[i] < volume[i + 1] for every i from 0 through lookback - 1
Long: position is not long AND direction setting allows long AND selected source > UT Bot ATR trail(selected source, ATR lookback, key multiplier) AND (EMA trend filter is disabled OR (close > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback))) AND (candle dominance filter is disabled OR (high - low = 0 ? 0.50 : (close - low) / (high - low)) >= dominance threshold) AND (relative volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * minimum volume multiplier) AND (breakout filter is disabled OR (strict mode AND previous bar exists AND low > open[1] AND close > high[1] AND close > open) OR (close-break mode AND previous bar exists AND close > high[1] AND close > open))
Short: position is not short AND direction setting allows short AND selected source < UT Bot ATR trail(selected source, ATR lookback, key multiplier) AND (EMA trend filter is disabled OR (close < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback))) AND (candle dominance filter is disabled OR (high - low = 0 ? 0.50 : (high - close) / (high - low)) >= dominance threshold) AND (relative volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * minimum volume multiplier) AND (breakout filter is disabled OR (strict mode AND previous bar exists AND high < open[1] AND close < low[1] AND close < open) OR (close-break mode AND previous bar exists AND close < low[1] AND close < open))
Exit: Long, position is long AND (selected source < UT Bot ATR trail(selected source, ATR lookback, key multiplier) OR (volume-fade exit is enabled AND volume is fading(fade lookback) AND close < open))
Exit: Short, position is short AND (selected source > UT Bot ATR trail(selected source, ATR lookback, key multiplier) OR (volume-fade exit is enabled AND volume is fading(fade lookback) AND close > open))
