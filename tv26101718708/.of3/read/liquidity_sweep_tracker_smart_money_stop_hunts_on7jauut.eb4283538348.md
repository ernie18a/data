Calculation: Regime ratio = ATR(atr length) / SMA(ATR(atr length), regime length, longer lookback than atr length)
Calculation: Touch count = 1 + number of bars after the pivot bar whose high (for a pivot high) or low (for a pivot low) is within touch tolerance * ATR(atr length) of the pivot level
Calculation: Pending high sweep level = level p of the most recent unswept PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) with high > p + ATR(atr length) * sweep buffer multiplier, selected only while no pending high sweep level exists and then marked swept; set on the sweep bar, it stays pending for confirmation window bars in total (sweep bar included) and is cleared on the first bar with close < p or when that window ends
Calculation: Pending low sweep level = level p of the most recent unswept PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) with low < p - ATR(atr length) * sweep buffer multiplier, selected only while no pending low sweep level exists and then marked swept; set on the sweep bar, it stays pending for confirmation window bars in total (sweep bar included) and is cleared on the first bar with close > p or when that window ends
Calculation: Sweep extreme = highest high (pending high sweep) or lowest low (pending low sweep) from the sweep bar through the current bar
Calculation: Bullish BOS = close > most recent PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND Structure bias[1] is not bullish
Calculation: Bearish BOS = close < most recent PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND Structure bias[1] is not bearish
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish if Bullish BOS, bearish if Bearish BOS, otherwise Structure bias[1]; neutral before any BOS
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume length)
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND close - open >= ATR(atr length) * displacement body multiplier
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND open - close >= ATR(atr length) * displacement body multiplier
Calculation: T1 pivot of sweep side = most recent PivotHigh(high, T1 swing lookback, T1 swing lookback) for a high sweep or PivotLow(low, T1 swing lookback, T1 swing lookback) for a low sweep, computed on T1 bars and read as of the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: T1 distance = abs(pending sweep level - T1 pivot of sweep side) / ATR(atr length)
Calculation: Unfilled FVG midpoints = midpoints of unfilled gaps, where a bullish gap is low > high[2], spanning high[2] (bottom) to low (top), filled when a later low <= top, and a bearish gap is high < low[2], spanning high (bottom) to low[2] (top), filled when a later high >= bottom
Calculation: Confidence = (Depth score * depth weight + Touch score * touch weight + Structure score * structure weight + Regime score * regime weight + Displacement score * displacement weight + Volume score * volume weight + T1 score * T1 weight) / sum of the seven weights, evaluated at the confirmation bar for the pending sweep side, where Depth score = Triangular(abs(Sweep extreme - pending sweep level) / ATR(atr length), depth ideal min, depth ideal max); Touch score = min(100, Touch count of the swept pivot at sweep bar / touch cap * 100); Structure score = 100 if Structure bias matches the trade direction (bullish for long, bearish for short), neutral structure score if Structure bias is neutral, otherwise opposed structure score; Regime score = clamp((Regime ratio - regime low reference) / (regime high reference - regime low reference) * 100, 0, 100); Displacement score = min(100, abs(Sweep extreme - close) / (ATR(atr length) * displacement ideal) * 100); Volume score = clamp((Volume ratio - 1) / volume multiplier * 100, 0, 100) if volume filter enabled, otherwise filter-off score; T1 score = max(0, 100 - T1 distance / T1 proximity reference * 100) if T1 filter enabled and T1 distance defined, otherwise filter-off score; Triangular(x) = x / depth ideal min * 100 if x < depth ideal min, 100 if x <= depth ideal max, otherwise max(0, 100 - (x - depth ideal max) / depth ideal max * 100)
Calculation: Initial long stop = max(Sweep extreme - ATR(atr length) * stop buffer multiplier * clamp(Regime ratio, min regime multiplier, max regime multiplier), close - ATR(atr length) * max stop multiplier), evaluated at the confirmation bar
Calculation: Initial short stop = min(Sweep extreme + ATR(atr length) * stop buffer multiplier * clamp(Regime ratio, min regime multiplier, max regime multiplier), close + ATR(atr length) * max stop multiplier), evaluated at the confirmation bar
Calculation: Long target = lowest unswept PivotHigh above close; if none, close + ATR(atr length) * fallback target multiplier unless opposing target is required; undefined otherwise; evaluated at the confirmation bar
Calculation: Short target = highest unswept PivotLow below close; if none, close - ATR(atr length) * fallback target multiplier unless opposing target is required; undefined otherwise; evaluated at the confirmation bar
Calculation: Long first target = lowest unfilled FVG midpoint above close and below Long target, if FVG target enabled and one exists; otherwise Long target; evaluated at the confirmation bar
Calculation: Short first target = highest unfilled FVG midpoint below close and above Short target, if FVG target enabled and one exists; otherwise Short target; evaluated at the confirmation bar
Calculation: Structural trail long = max over pivot lows formed after the entry bar of (pivot low - ATR(atr length) * trail buffer multiplier), none if no such pivot low
Calculation: Structural trail short = min over pivot highs formed after the entry bar of (pivot high + ATR(atr length) * trail buffer multiplier), none if no such pivot high
Calculation: Long stop level = max(Initial long stop, Structural trail long), and if ATR backstop enabled, max of that and close - ATR(atr length) * backstop multiplier
Calculation: Short stop level = min(Initial short stop, Structural trail short), and if ATR backstop enabled, min of that and close + ATR(atr length) * backstop multiplier
Long: close > Pending low sweep level AND Confidence >= minimum confidence AND (volume filter off OR Volume ratio at sweep bar > volume multiplier) AND (T1 filter off OR T1 distance <= T1 proximity reference) AND (no trigger required OR (engulf required AND bullish engulfing) OR (displacement required AND Bullish displacement) OR (sweep-break required AND close > high at sweep bar)) AND Long target is defined AND (R:R filter off OR (Long target - close) / (close - Initial long stop) >= min R:R)
Short: close < Pending high sweep level AND Confidence >= minimum confidence AND (volume filter off OR Volume ratio at sweep bar > volume multiplier) AND (T1 filter off OR T1 distance <= T1 proximity reference) AND (no trigger required OR (engulf required AND bearish engulfing) OR (displacement required AND Bearish displacement) OR (sweep-break required AND close < low at sweep bar)) AND Short target is defined AND (R:R filter off OR (close - Short target) / (Initial short stop - close) >= min R:R)
Exit: long, Bearish BOS AND structure exit enabled
Exit: long, low <= Long stop level
Exit: long, high >= Long first target
Exit: long, bars since entry >= time exit bars AND no bullish BOS since entry AND close <= entry price AND time exit enabled
Exit: short, Bullish BOS AND structure exit enabled
Exit: short, high >= Short stop level
Exit: short, low <= Short first target
Exit: short, bars since entry >= time exit bars AND no bearish BOS since entry AND close >= entry price AND time exit enabled
