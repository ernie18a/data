Long: flat AND datetime >= start-time parameter AND datetime <= finish-time parameter AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses above its signal line using T1 values; MACD is calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars, using completed T1 bars historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime; T1 is the longer timeframe.
Long: flat AND datetime >= start-time parameter AND datetime <= finish-time parameter AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses above its signal line using T2 values AND MACD(T1) line > its signal line; MACD is calculated on each timeframe’s bars and sampled on chart bars, using completed timeframe bars historically and the forming bar in realtime; T2 is the shorter timeframe; both use the same fast, slow, and signal lookbacks.
Calculation: trailing stop = max(previous trailing stop, trailing price source * (1 - trailing percentage)) while long; 0 while flat
Exit: Long, MACD(T1) line crosses below its signal line OR MACD(T2) line crosses below its signal line OR take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage) OR trailing-stop enabled AND low <= trailing stop OR trailing-stop disabled AND fixed-stop enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - fixed-stop percentage)
