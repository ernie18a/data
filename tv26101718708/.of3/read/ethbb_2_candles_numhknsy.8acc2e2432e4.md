Short: close(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars) < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars) < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, one chart bar ago sampled on chart bars) AND low <= SMA(close, midline lookback, chart bars) <= high AND close[1] < open[1] AND close < open

Exit: Short, high >= SMA(close, midline lookback, chart bars) + ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) * trailing multiplier
