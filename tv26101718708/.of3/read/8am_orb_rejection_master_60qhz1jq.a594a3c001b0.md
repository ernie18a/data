Calculation: Prior T3 high/low = high/low of the last completed T3 bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Previous Asia, London, and New York session highs/lows = highest high and lowest low during each preceding completed session, sampled on chart bars using New York time
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose timestamps fall within the opening range window (New York time), accumulated for the trading day
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2; opening range range = opening range high - opening range low
Calculation: Level proximity = ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × level proximity multiplier
Calculation: Resistance nearby = current high is within level proximity of any available prior T1, T2, or T3 high, or previous Asia, London, or New York session high
Calculation: Support nearby = current low is within level proximity of any available prior T1, T2, or T3 low, or previous Asia, London, or New York session low
Calculation: Upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; body = abs(close - open); candle range = high - low
Calculation: Bearish rejection candle = upper wick > body AND upper wick > lower wick AND close < open; bullish rejection candle = lower wick > body AND lower wick > upper wick AND close > open
Calculation: Candle body fraction = candle range > 0 ? body / candle range : 0
