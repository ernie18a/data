Calculation: Initial stop = low[stop lookback] minus stop buffer for a long, or high[stop lookback] plus stop buffer for a short, evaluated on the entry chart bar
Calculation: Target level i = entry price + position side × its separate target-distance parameter; final target = the last level selected by the target-count parameter
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop; when the enabled breakeven trigger is reached by favorable movement, move it to entry price + position side × breakeven offset; when target trailing is enabled, move it at the first target to entry price + position side × breakeven offset and at each later target to the previous target, only when the move tightens the stop in the position’s favor
Long: Long signals enabled AND flat AND the EMA with the shorter lookback crosses above the EMA with the longer lookback within the entry-window chart bars, including the cross bar, AND the shorter-lookback EMA remains above the longer-lookback EMA AND close is above each EMA(close, shorter lookback), EMA(close, longer lookback), EMA(EMA(close, shorter lookback), shared smoothing lookback), and EMA(EMA(close, longer lookback), shared smoothing lookback) AND this cross has not already opened a trade; confirm at bar close and enter at the next chart bar’s open only if initial stop < entry price
Short: Short signals enabled AND flat AND the EMA with the shorter lookback crosses below the EMA with the longer lookback within the entry-window chart bars, including the cross bar, AND the shorter-lookback EMA remains below the longer-lookback EMA AND close is below each EMA(close, shorter lookback), EMA(close, longer lookback), EMA(EMA(close, shorter lookback), shared smoothing lookback), and EMA(EMA(close, longer lookback), shared smoothing lookback) AND this cross has not already opened a trade; confirm at bar close and enter at the next chart bar’s open only if initial stop > entry price
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= final target OR, when the early EMA exit mode is active, close is below the fast EMA, both fast and slow EMAs, or all four EMA lines, as selected by that mode
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= final target OR, when the early EMA exit mode is active, close is above the fast EMA, both fast and slow EMAs, or all four EMA lines, as selected by that mode
