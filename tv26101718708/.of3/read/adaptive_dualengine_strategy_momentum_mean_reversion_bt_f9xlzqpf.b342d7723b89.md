Calculation: higher-timeframe trend = on chart bars, compare the still-forming T1 close with EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback, T1, still-forming bar); bullish when close is higher, bearish when lower, otherwise neutral
Calculation: mean-reversion mean = EMA(selected OHLC-derived price source, mean lookback)
Calculation: EMA-MACD score = sum signed weights: alignment weight for fast EMA above second EMA AND source above medium EMA, its negative for the inverse; long-term EMA weight for source above long-term EMA, its negative below; MACD state weight for MACD line above signal, its negative below; MACD cross weight for line crossing above signal, its negative for crossing below; RSI bias weight above its bullish level, its negative below its bearish level; ADX direction weight when ADX meets its minimum and +DI exceeds −DI, its negative when −DI exceeds +DI; volume weight when volume exceeds its moving average and close exceeds open, its negative when close is below open; and, when enabled, higher-timeframe trend weight signed by the higher-timeframe trend. Each term is zero otherwise. EMA lookbacks are successively longer from fast through second and medium to long-term; MACD fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback.
Calculation: BuySell score = sum signed weights: RSI bias weight at or above its bullish level, its negative at or below its bearish level; RSI cross weight for crossing above its buy level, its negative for crossing below its sell level; stochastic state weight when smoothed %K exceeds %D and the midpoint, its negative when both are lower; stochastic %K/%D cross weight for %K crossing above %D below the sell level, its negative for crossing below %D above the buy level; stochastic level-cross weight for %K crossing above its buy level, its negative for crossing below its sell level; MACD state weight for line above signal, its negative below; MACD cross weight for line crossing above signal, its negative for crossing below; and, when enabled, trend-filter weight for fast EMA above slow EMA with close above trend EMA, its negative for the inverse; and, when enabled, volume weight when volume exceeds its moving average and close exceeds open, its negative when close is below open. Each term is zero otherwise. MACD oscillator fast lookback is shorter than slow; BuySell trend EMA fast lookback is shorter than slow and trend lookback is longer than slow.
Calculation: entry window passes = date filter disabled or datetime within selected start and end; AND session filter disabled or bar time within selected session; AND weekday filter disabled or datetime weekday is an enabled Monday–Friday
Calculation: selected long signal = in Mean Reversion mode, or Auto mode on a chart timeframe no longer than T2, close is below mean-reversion mean minus ATR × entry-stretch multiple AND short-period RSI is below its buy level; in Both Agree mode, both scores meet their respective positive entry thresholds AND either score was below its threshold on the prior bar; in Either Signal mode, either score meets its positive entry threshold after being below it on the prior bar; in EMA MACD Only mode, EMA-MACD score meets its positive entry threshold after being below it on the prior bar; in BuySell Only mode, BuySell score meets its positive entry threshold after being below it on the prior bar; in Auto mode on a chart timeframe longer than T2, use the EMA MACD Only condition
Calculation: selected short signal = in Mean Reversion mode, or Auto mode on a chart timeframe no longer than T2, close is above mean-reversion mean plus ATR × entry-stretch multiple AND short-period RSI is above its sell level; in Both Agree mode, both scores meet their respective negative entry thresholds AND either score was above its threshold on the prior bar; in Either Signal mode, either score meets its negative entry threshold after being above it on the prior bar; in EMA MACD Only mode, EMA-MACD score meets its negative entry threshold after being above it on the prior bar; in BuySell Only mode, BuySell score meets its negative entry threshold after being above it on the prior bar; in Auto mode on a chart timeframe longer than T2, use the EMA MACD Only condition

Calculation: forecast score bias = average of EMA-MACD score divided by its normalization scale and BuySell score divided by its separate normalization scale
Calculation: forecast drift per bar = weighted mean of linear-regression slope over the forecast lookback, forecast score bias × ATR × score-drift-per-point, and selected-source change over the forecast lookback divided by that lookback; divide by the greater of the sum of the three separate weights and the minimum positive weight sum
Calculation: forecast confidence = rounded percentage of the weighted sum of absolute forecast score bias, ADX divided by its confidence normalizer and capped at one, and squared correlation of selected source with bar index over the forecast lookback; use separate weights for the three terms

Calculation: entry-time risk distance = ATR at entry × mean-reversion stop multiple in Mean Reversion mode, otherwise ATR at entry × momentum stop multiple
Calculation: long first target = entry price + ATR at entry × first-target multiple; short first target = entry price − ATR at entry × first-target multiple
Calculation: long stop level = maximum of prior stop level and entry price minus entry-time risk distance; after first target is reached, also include entry price when breakeven is enabled and current high minus current ATR × trailing multiple when trailing is enabled
Calculation: short stop level = minimum of prior stop level and entry price plus entry-time risk distance; after first target is reached, also include entry price when breakeven is enabled and current low plus current ATR × trailing multiple when trailing is enabled
Calculation: long target = current mean-reversion mean in Mean Reversion mode, otherwise entry price + entry-time risk distance × reward/risk multiple; short target = current mean-reversion mean in Mean Reversion mode, otherwise entry price − entry-time risk distance × reward/risk multiple
