Calculation: Resistance = first confirmed chart-bar pivot high, then latest confirmed pivot high at least the minimum level separation from the previously accepted resistance; pivot length bars on each side
Calculation: VWAP slope = VWAP(close) - VWAP(close)[slope lookback] on chart bars

Short: (close < Resistance AND abs(close - Resistance) <= resistance-zone thickness OR prior-day-high filter enabled AND close < high(T1, last completed bar) AND abs(close - high(T1, last completed bar)) <= prior-day-zone thickness) AND, when enabled, close < VWAP(close) AND, when enabled, VWAP slope < 0 AND, when enabled, SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, current bar still forming) < SMA(close, longer lookback, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars AND, when enabled, RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= short RSI minimum AND, when the RTH filter is enabled, within the configured RTH session and after the opening delay, and before the configured cutoff when the cutoff is enabled AND this full setup condition was false on the prior chart bar AND more than the cooldown length in chart bars has elapsed since the most recent long or short signal
Calculation: Retained support level = the first chart-timeframe pivot low confirmed by P1 bars on both sides, then each newly confirmed pivot low at least P2 points from the current level
Calculation: Retained resistance level = the first chart-timeframe pivot high confirmed by P1 bars on both sides, then each newly confirmed pivot high at least P2 points from the current level
Calculation: Entry window = (RTH-only filter disabled OR (time is within session P17 and at least P18 minutes have elapsed since its opening)) AND (RTH-only filter disabled OR cutoff disabled OR time is before cutoff hour P20 and minute P21 in New York time)
Calculation: Put spread setup = Entry window AND ((retained support level exists AND close > retained support level AND close - retained support level <= P3) OR (prior-day filter P4 enabled AND close > previous completed T1-period low AND close - previous completed T1-period low <= P5)) AND (daily-MA filter P9 disabled OR T1-period SMA of close over P10 bars > T1-period SMA of close over P11 bars) AND (VWAP-level filter P6 disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (VWAP-slope filter P7 disabled OR VWAP > VWAP from P8 chart bars ago) AND (RSI filter P12 disabled OR RSI of close over P13 chart bars <= P14)
