Calculation: Support = first confirmed chart-bar pivot low, then latest confirmed pivot low at least the minimum level separation from the previously accepted support; pivot length bars on each side
Calculation: Resistance = first confirmed chart-bar pivot high, then latest confirmed pivot high at least the minimum level separation from the previously accepted resistance; pivot length bars on each side
Calculation: VWAP slope = VWAP(close) - VWAP(close)[slope lookback] on chart bars
Long: (close > Support AND abs(close - Support) <= support-zone thickness OR prior-day-low filter enabled AND close > low(T1, last completed bar) AND abs(close - low(T1, last completed bar)) <= prior-day-zone thickness) AND, when enabled, close > VWAP(close) AND, when enabled, VWAP slope > 0 AND, when enabled, SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, current bar still forming) > SMA(close, longer lookback, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars AND, when enabled, RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= long RSI maximum AND, when the RTH filter is enabled, within the configured RTH session and after the opening delay, and before the configured cutoff when the cutoff is enabled AND this full setup condition was false on the prior chart bar AND more than the cooldown length in chart bars has elapsed since the most recent long or short signal
Short: (close < Resistance AND abs(close - Resistance) <= resistance-zone thickness OR prior-day-high filter enabled AND close < high(T1, last completed bar) AND abs(close - high(T1, last completed bar)) <= prior-day-zone thickness) AND, when enabled, close < VWAP(close) AND, when enabled, VWAP slope < 0 AND, when enabled, SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, current bar still forming) < SMA(close, longer lookback, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars AND, when enabled, RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= short RSI minimum AND, when the RTH filter is enabled, within the configured RTH session and after the opening delay, and before the configured cutoff when the cutoff is enabled AND this full setup condition was false on the prior chart bar AND more than the cooldown length in chart bars has elapsed since the most recent long or short signal
