Calculation: RSI-equivalent price level(target, RSI lookback, source) = source + (RSI lookback - 1) × (q × gains - losses) if q × losses >= gains, otherwise source + (RSI lookback - 1) × (losses - gains / q), where q = target / (100 - target), gains = RMA(max(change(source), 0), RSI lookback), and losses = RMA(max(-change(source), 0), RSI lookback)
Calculation: Cardwell regime = bull if lowest(RSI(source, RSI lookback), regime lookback) >= bull floor - tolerance and highest(RSI(source, RSI lookback), regime lookback) > bear ceiling + tolerance; bear if highest(RSI(source, RSI lookback), regime lookback) <= bear ceiling + tolerance and lowest(RSI(source, RSI lookback), regime lookback) < bull floor - tolerance; neutral otherwise
Calculation: Support rung price = RSI-equivalent price level(bull-support rung, RSI lookback, source) when Cardwell regime is bull, otherwise RSI-equivalent price level(oversold rung, RSI lookback, source)
Calculation: Resistance rung price = RSI-equivalent price level(bear-resistance rung, RSI lookback, source) when Cardwell regime is bear, otherwise RSI-equivalent price level(overbought rung, RSI lookback, source)
Long: (low <= support rung price[1] AND close > support rung price[1] AND close[1] > support rung price[1]) OR (a confirmed pivot low of RSI(source, RSI lookback), using the reversal pivot length on both sides, occurs AND RSI at the latest pivot is lower than RSI at the prior confirmed pivot low AND low at the latest pivot is higher than low at the prior pivot AND pivot-bar separation <= maximum bars between pivots)
Short: (high >= resistance rung price[1] AND close < resistance rung price[1] AND close[1] < resistance rung price[1]) OR (a confirmed pivot high of RSI(source, RSI lookback), using the reversal pivot length on both sides, occurs AND RSI at the latest pivot is higher than RSI at the prior confirmed pivot high AND high at the latest pivot is lower than high at the prior pivot AND pivot-bar separation <= maximum bars between pivots)
