Calculation: Directional bias = clamp(range-position weight × RP + EMA-trend weight × ET + candle-pressure weight × CP + midpoint-shift weight × MS, to the normalized signed range), where RP is the close's position within the compression-lookback high-low range, clamped to the normalized range and mapped to the signed range; ET is positive when EMA(close, shorter context lookback) > EMA(close, longer context lookback) and the longer EMA > its value slope-lookback bars ago, negative when both inequalities reverse, and zero otherwise; CP is the close's position within the current bar's high-low range, clamped to the normalized range and mapped to the signed range; MS is the difference between the midpoint of the recent half-window high-low range and the preceding half-window high-low range, divided by max(ATR(ATR lookback) if available, true range, minimum price increment), then clamped to the normalized signed range; half-window = max(minimum half-window, round(compression lookback × half-window fraction)).
Long: bullish side is selected OR automatic side selection AND Directional bias >= automatic-side bias threshold
Short: bearish side is selected OR automatic side selection AND Directional bias <= -automatic-side bias threshold
