Calculation: Active consolidation zone = on each eligible chart bar without a breakout, scan lookbacks from the longer lookback parameter down to the shorter lookback parameter; for each lookback L, calculate highest(high, L) and lowest(low, L) over the trailing L chart bars including the current bar, and calculate range percentage as 100 × (highest − lowest) / ((highest + lowest) / 2), or 100 when the midpoint is zero; select the first lookback whose range percentage is at most the maximum range percentage, and set the zone top to highest(high, L), zone bottom to lowest(low, L), and zone average volume to SMA(volume, L); if none qualifies, retain the existing zone until current bar index minus its last qualifying bar index is greater than the longer lookback parameter; clear the zone after a breakout, and scan only on bars after the last breakout when bars since that breakout are at least the cooldown parameter
Long: an active consolidation zone carried from the prior chart bar exists AND close > zone top AND volume >= zone average volume × breakout volume multiple
Short: an active consolidation zone carried from the prior chart bar exists AND close < zone bottom AND volume >= zone average volume × breakout volume multiple
