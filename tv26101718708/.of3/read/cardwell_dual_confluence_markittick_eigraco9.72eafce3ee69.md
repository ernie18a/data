Calculation: entry reference price = close on the chart bar before the accepted signal bar
Calculation: entry ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) on the accepted signal bar
Calculation: bullish regime = trend source > SMA(trend source, trend SMA lookback) AND RSI(regime RSI source, regime RSI lookback) is inclusively within separate bullish lower and upper bounds on each of the confirmation lookback consecutive completed bars
Calculation: bearish regime = trend source < SMA(trend source, trend SMA lookback) AND RSI(regime RSI source, regime RSI lookback) is inclusively within separate bearish lower and upper bounds on each of the confirmation lookback consecutive completed bars
Long: bullish regime
Short: bearish regime
Long: RMA(RSI(close, cross RSI lookback), fast RMA lookback) > RMA(RSI(close, cross RSI lookback), slow RMA lookback), with separate fast and slow lookbacks and the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Short: RMA(RSI(close, cross RSI lookback), fast RMA lookback) <= RMA(RSI(close, cross RSI lookback), slow RMA lookback), with separate fast and slow lookbacks and the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Long: on a confirmed chart bar close, no prior accepted signal OR at least the signal-gap lookback chart bars since the previous accepted signal, AND ((cross-plus-regime requirement disabled AND (fast RMA crossed above slow RMA on the prior chart bar OR a bullish regime has just begun)) OR (cross-plus-regime requirement enabled AND the fast RMA crossing above the slow RMA and the start of a bullish regime occur within the confluence window)), AND (HTF confirmation disabled OR the bullish regime calculated on T1 bars is bullish on the last completed T1 bar), AND (ADX chop filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback for smoothing) on the current chart bar >= ADX minimum threshold)
Short: on a confirmed chart bar close, no prior accepted signal OR at least the signal-gap lookback chart bars since the previous accepted signal, AND ((cross-plus-regime requirement disabled AND (fast RMA crossed below slow RMA on the prior chart bar OR a bearish regime has just begun)) OR (cross-plus-regime requirement enabled AND the fast RMA crossing below the slow RMA and the start of a bearish regime occur within the confluence window)), AND (HTF confirmation disabled OR the bearish regime calculated on T1 bars is bearish on the last completed T1 bar), AND (ADX chop filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback for smoothing) on the current chart bar >= ADX minimum threshold)
Exit: long, current chart bar low <= entry reference price - entry ATR * separate stop-loss multiplier OR current chart bar high >= entry reference price + entry ATR * first take-profit multiplier OR current chart bar high >= entry reference price + entry ATR * second take-profit multiplier OR current chart bar high >= entry reference price + entry ATR * third take-profit multiplier
Exit: short, current chart bar high >= entry reference price + entry ATR * separate stop-loss multiplier OR current chart bar low <= entry reference price - entry ATR * first take-profit multiplier OR current chart bar low <= entry reference price - entry ATR * second take-profit multiplier OR current chart bar low <= entry reference price - entry ATR * third take-profit multiplier
