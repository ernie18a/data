Calculation: EMA envelope upper/lower = EMA(source, channel lookback) ± STDEV(source, channel lookback) × envelope deviation parameter
Calculation: VWAP stretch layer upper/lower = anchored VWAP(hlc3) ± STDEV(close − anchored VWAP(hlc3), channel lookback) × VWAP stretch parameter
Calculation: Bullish rejection candle = close > open AND MIN(close, open) − low > ABS(close − open) × wick/body parameter AND close > low + (high − low) × bullish close-position parameter
Calculation: Bearish rejection candle = close < open AND high − MAX(close, open) > ABS(close − open) × wick/body parameter AND close < low + (high − low) × bearish close-position parameter
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, low < EMA envelope lower AND low < VWAP stretch layer lower AND close > EMA envelope lower AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= oversold threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (rejection not required OR bullish rejection candle) AND (no prior signal exists OR cooldown parameter is zero OR bars since the most recent long or short signal > cooldown parameter)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, high > EMA envelope upper AND high > VWAP stretch layer upper AND close < EMA envelope upper AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= overbought threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (rejection not required OR bearish rejection candle) AND (no prior signal exists OR cooldown parameter is zero OR bars since the most recent long or short signal > cooldown parameter)
