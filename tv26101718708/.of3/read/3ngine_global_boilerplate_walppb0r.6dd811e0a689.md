Calculation: Long stop price = if Highest(high, bars since entry) > average entry price * (1 + trailing activation parameter) then Highest(high, bars since entry) * (1 - trailing distance parameter) else configured stop price
Calculation: Short stop price = if Lowest(low, bars since entry) < average entry price * (1 - trailing activation parameter) then Lowest(low, bars since entry) * (1 + trailing distance parameter) else configured stop price
Long: configured entry price > configured stop price AND no trade has closed AND not in a short position AND bar datetime > configured start datetime AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > MA(configured source, lookback, selected type, T1, last completed bar in real time or current T1 value in historical calculations), sampled on chart bars) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) on chart bars is between configured lower and upper thresholds) AND (session filter disabled OR bar datetime is within configured session in GMT-6)
Short: configured entry price <= configured stop price AND no trade has closed AND not in a long position AND bar datetime > configured start datetime AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > MA(configured source, lookback, selected type, T1, last completed bar in real time or current T1 value in historical calculations), sampled on chart bars) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) on chart bars is between configured lower and upper thresholds) AND (session filter disabled OR bar datetime is within configured session in GMT-6)
Exit: long, low <= Long stop price OR high >= average entry price * (1 + take-profit parameter)
Exit: short, high >= Short stop price OR low <= average entry price * (1 - take-profit parameter)
