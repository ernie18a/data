Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; average body = SMA(candle body, body-average lookback).
Calculation: Lower-wick hammer shape = candle body > 0 AND lower wick >= candle body × long-wick/body parameter AND upper wick <= candle body × opposite-wick/body maximum.
Calculation: Upper-wick star shape = candle body > 0 AND upper wick >= candle body × long-wick/body parameter AND lower wick <= candle body × opposite-wick/body maximum.
Calculation: Developing session volume profile = reset each session; use a range from session open minus to session open plus range multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback, T2 bars), sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T2 bar historically, forming T2 bar live), falling back to chart ATR when unavailable; divide the range into bin-count bins and distribute each chart bar's volume evenly across bins intersecting its low-to-high range; POC is the highest-volume bin and VAL/VAH bound the value area expanded from POC to the selected fraction of total bin volume; an HVN/LVN is a close-price bin whose volume is respectively above/below its multiplier × mean nonempty-bin volume.
Calculation: Signed volume delta = when enhanced data is selected and confirmed T3 intrabars exist, sum intrabar volume for close > open and subtract it for close < open; otherwise use ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low) × volume, or zero when high = low. T3 intrabars are aggregated within each chart bar.
Calculation: CVD divergence = bullish when low = lowest(low, divergence lookback) AND session-cumulative signed volume delta > its value divergence lookback chart bars ago; bearish when high = highest(high, divergence lookback) AND session-cumulative signed volume delta < its value divergence lookback chart bars ago. CVD resets each session.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-spike multiplier.
Calculation: Swing sweeps = low sweep when low < most recently confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND volume spike; high sweep when high > most recently confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND volume spike. Pivots require the swing-pivot lookback bars on both sides to confirm.
Calculation: Reversal locations = bullish when close is within proximity multiplier × chart ATR of VAL, below VAL, within that distance of an untouched prior-session POC, at a low sweep, or in an HVN at or below POC; bearish when close is within that distance of VAH, above VAH, within that distance of an untouched prior-session POC, at a high sweep, or in an HVN at or above POC. When the sweep-required parameter is enabled, also require the corresponding sweep.
Calculation: Continuation locations = bullish when (close bin is an LVN OR close > VAH) AND NOT (close bin is an HVN AND close < VAH); bearish when (close bin is an LVN OR close < VAL) AND NOT (close bin is an HVN AND close > VAL).
Calculation: Coil node = previous chart bar was within the profile proximity of POC OR its close-price bin was an HVN.
Calculation: HTF bullish bias = T1 close > EMA(close, HTF trend lookback) AND (slope requirement disabled OR current T1 EMA >= prior T1 EMA); HTF bearish bias = T1 close < that EMA AND (slope requirement disabled OR current T1 EMA <= prior T1 EMA). Calculate on T1 bars and sample on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically, forming T1 bar live).
Calculation: Regime passes = regime mode is unrestricted OR mode is calm and ATR percentrank over volatility lookback is below the panic percentile parameter OR mode is panic and that percentrank is at least the parameter.
Calculation: Ready = chart bar index > max(body-average lookback, divergence lookback) + 2 × swing-pivot lookback + warm-up offset parameter.

Exit: long, on a bar after the close-entry, low <= entry - stop-multiple × chart ATR at entry OR high >= entry + target-multiple × chart ATR at entry OR neither barrier has been touched and bars since entry reach the horizon; if both barriers are touched on one bar, count the stop as hit.
Exit: short, on a bar after the close-entry, high >= entry + stop-multiple × chart ATR at entry OR low <= entry - target-multiple × chart ATR at entry OR neither barrier has been touched and bars since entry reach the horizon; if both barriers are touched on one bar, count the stop as hit.
