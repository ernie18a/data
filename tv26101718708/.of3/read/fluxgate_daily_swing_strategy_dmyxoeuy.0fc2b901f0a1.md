Calculation: Soft clip(x, bound) = x × max(bound, ε) / (max(bound, ε) + abs(x))
Calculation: Normalized regression curvature(src, L) = current LinearRegression(src, L) minus twice its prior-bar value plus its two-bars-prior value, divided by max(STDEV(src, L), ε)
Calculation: AdaptiveScore(N, A) = EMA(Soft clip(((direction weight × direction component + persistence weight × persistence component + curvature weight × curvature component + phase assist) × regime gate × shock gate / max(mean absolute deviation of the component blend from its SMA over the norm lookback, ε)) × score gain, score clip bound), smoothing lookback), on chart bars. Trend, gate, norm, adaptive, phase, fractal, short-volatility, and long-volatility lookbacks are each round(N times their own low-aggression to high-aggression multiplier linearly interpolated by A); smoothing lookback is the rounded interpolation of its own low-aggression and high-aggression endpoints. Log return = ln(close/close[1]); direction driver = Soft clip((EMA(OHLC4, trend lookback) minus its prior-bar value) / max(STDEV(log return, norm lookback), ε), direction clip bound); persistence driver = Correlation of the close-direction sign series and its prior-bar value over the gate lookback, replacing an unavailable result with zero; curvature driver = Soft clip(Normalized regression curvature(close, trend lookback), curvature clip bound). Direction, persistence, and curvature components are rolling Gram-Schmidt orthogonalized in that order over the norm lookback by subtracting each projection, with projection coefficient equal to rolling covariance divided by max(rolling variance, ε), then each residual is divided by max(its rolling STDEV, ε). The three weights are respectively direction weight intercept plus direction weight aggression slope × A, persistence weight intercept minus persistence weight aggression slope × A, and curvature weight intercept plus curvature weight aggression slope × (unit minus A); divide each by their sum. Efficiency ratio = abs(close minus close[gate lookback]) / sum of absolute close changes over the gate lookback. Binary entropy is the binary entropy of SMA(close-up indicator, gate lookback), normalized by log(2). Fractal dimension uses the log of path length divided by price range, divided by log(fractal lookback), with the coded invalid-range fallback, offset, and clamp bounds; regime coherence = max(zero, coherence ceiling minus fractal dimension). Regime gate = max(efficiency ratio × (unit minus binary entropy) × regime coherence, zero) raised to the gate exponent interpolated between its low-aggression and high-aggression endpoints. Phase assist = (twice normalized Stochastic %K over the phase lookback minus unit) × phase weight × its absolute value; phase weight is interpolated between its low-aggression and high-aggression endpoints. Shock gate = unit / (unit + max(short-volatility STDEV(log return) / long-volatility STDEV(log return) minus unit, zero)). Score gain is interpolated between its low-aggression and high-aggression endpoints.
Calculation: AdaptiveThresholds(N, A) = [upper, lower], calculated separately from AdaptiveScore(N, A). Adaptive lookback is the one used by AdaptiveScore. Volatility = max(STDEV(AdaptiveScore, adaptive lookback), ε); z = (AdaptiveScore − SMA(AdaptiveScore, adaptive lookback)) / volatility; skew = SMA(z cubed, adaptive lookback). k is interpolated between its low-aggression and high-aggression endpoints. Upper = k × volatility × clamp(unit − tilt sensitivity × max(skew, zero), tilt lower bound, tilt upper bound); lower = −k × volatility × clamp(unit − tilt sensitivity × max(−skew, zero), tilt lower bound, tilt upper bound).
Calculation: Risk distance at entry = ATR(ATR lookback, T1 current bar; calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using the developing T1 bar in realtime and the last available confirmed T1 value historically) × stop multiple; freeze at entry.
Long: Long entries enabled AND (AdaptiveScore(long base lookback, long aggression) crosses above the first output of AdaptiveThresholds(long base lookback, long aggression) OR that crossover occurred on the previous chart bar)
Short: Short entries enabled AND AdaptiveScore(short base lookback, short aggression) crosses below the second output of AdaptiveThresholds(short base lookback, short aggression)
Exit: long, AdaptiveScore(long base lookback, long aggression) crosses above the first output of AdaptiveThresholds(long base lookback, long aggression)
Exit: long, risk engine enabled AND (low ≤ (entry price if breakeven is enabled and a close since entry has reached entry price + risk distance × breakeven trigger R, otherwise entry price − risk distance) OR take profit is enabled AND high ≥ entry price + risk distance × take-profit R)
Exit: short, risk engine enabled AND (high ≥ (entry price if breakeven is enabled and a close since entry has reached entry price − risk distance × breakeven trigger R, otherwise entry price + risk distance) OR take profit is enabled AND low ≤ entry price − risk distance × take-profit R)
