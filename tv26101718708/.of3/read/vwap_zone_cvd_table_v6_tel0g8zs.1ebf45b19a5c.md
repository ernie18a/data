Calculation: Anchored VWAP = VWAP(HLC3, configured anchor interval), calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(max(sum(HLC3² × volume) / sum(volume) − Anchored VWAP², 0)), with sums reset at the configured anchor boundary on chart bars.
Calculation: VWAP zone = Anchored VWAP ± VWAP-zone ATR-width parameter × ATR(chart bars, shared ATR lookback).
Calculation: SD1 upper/lower zones = Anchored VWAP ± SD1 deviation multiplier × anchored VWAP standard deviation ± SD1 ATR-width parameter × ATR(chart bars, shared ATR lookback); SD2 upper/lower zones use separate SD2 deviation and ATR-width parameters.
Calculation: Eligible(z) = chart close is within zone z, inclusive, AND chart volume ≥ minimum-volume parameter.
Calculation: T1 delta = T1 volume × ((T1 close − T1 low) − (T1 high − T1 close)) / max(T1 high − T1 low, range-floor parameter), computed on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off; historical chart bars use the last completed T1 bar and realtime chart bars use the still-forming T1 bar. Sample T1 volume the same way; unavailable sampled values are zero.
Calculation: CVD zone percentage(z) = 100 × accumulated positive-delta T1 volume / (accumulated positive-delta T1 volume + accumulated negative-delta T1 volume), or 50 when the denominator is zero. Maintain separate totals for each zone; on chart bars, add sampled T1 volume when Eligible(z) and sampled T1 delta is positive or negative, respectively. When the CVD-reset parameter is enabled, clear totals at the configured anchor boundary or at a calendar-date change detected on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars with lookahead off.
Long: CVD zone percentage(VWAP zone) ≥ CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD1 upper zone) ≥ CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD1 lower zone) ≥ CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD2 upper zone) ≥ CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD2 lower zone) ≥ CVD threshold.
Short: CVD zone percentage(VWAP zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD1 upper zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD1 lower zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD2 upper zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold OR CVD zone percentage(SD2 lower zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold.
Long: Eligible(VWAP zone) AND CVD zone percentage(VWAP zone) ≥ CVD threshold OR Eligible(SD1 lower zone) AND CVD zone percentage(SD1 lower zone) ≥ CVD threshold OR Eligible(SD2 lower zone) AND CVD zone percentage(SD2 lower zone) ≥ CVD threshold.
Short: Eligible(VWAP zone) AND CVD zone percentage(VWAP zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold OR Eligible(SD1 upper zone) AND CVD zone percentage(SD1 upper zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold OR Eligible(SD2 upper zone) AND CVD zone percentage(SD2 upper zone) ≤ 100% − CVD threshold.
