Calculation: Phoenix TCI = EMA(((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, TCI baseline lookback)) / MAX(EMA(ABS(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, same TCI baseline lookback)), same TCI baseline lookback), denominator floor)) / TCI scale, TCI smoothing lookback) + TCI centerline
Calculation: Phoenix = (Phoenix TCI + MFI(HLC3, directional-flow and RSI lookback) + RSI(HLC3, same directional-flow and RSI lookback)) / 3
Calculation: Squeeze fired = (BB upper > KC upper OR BB lower < KC lower) AND at least one of the preceding squeeze lookback bars had (BB upper < KC upper AND BB lower > KC lower), where BB upper/lower = SMA(close, band lookback) plus/minus BB multiplier × STDEV(close, same band lookback), and KC upper/lower = SMA(close, same band lookback) plus/minus KC multiplier × ATR(same band lookback)
Calculation: Volatility scale = minimum volatility scale + (maximum volatility scale − minimum volatility scale) × PercentRank(ATR(volatility ATR lookback), volatility rank lookback) / 100 when adaptive ATR is enabled; otherwise 1
Calculation: Entry volatility scale = Volatility scale on the entry bar
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR(risk ATR lookback) on the entry bar
Calculation: Long trailing stop = entry price − Entry ATR × ATR stop multiple × Entry volatility scale at entry; thereafter MAX(previous long trailing stop, close − Entry ATR × ATR stop multiple × current Volatility scale)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = entry price + Entry ATR × ATR stop multiple × Entry volatility scale at entry; thereafter MIN(previous short trailing stop, close + Entry ATR × ATR stop multiple × current Volatility scale)
Long: Squeeze fired AND close > EMA(close, macro EMA input lookback × macro EMA scaling factor, chart bars) AND HMA(close, HMA lookback) > HMA(close, same HMA lookback)[1] AND Phoenix > SMA(Phoenix, signal lookback) AND Phoenix > Phoenix[1] AND LSMA(Phoenix, LSMA lookback) > TCI centerline AND minimum volatility percentile ≤ PercentRank(ATR(volatility ATR lookback), volatility rank lookback) ≤ maximum volatility percentile AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) ≥ ADX threshold)
Short: Squeeze fired AND close < EMA(close, macro EMA input lookback × macro EMA scaling factor, chart bars) AND HMA(close, HMA lookback) < HMA(close, same HMA lookback)[1] AND Phoenix < SMA(Phoenix, signal lookback) AND Phoenix < Phoenix[1] AND LSMA(Phoenix, LSMA lookback) < TCI centerline AND minimum volatility percentile ≤ PercentRank(ATR(volatility ATR lookback), volatility rank lookback) ≤ maximum volatility percentile AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) ≥ ADX threshold)
Exit: Long, close ≤ long trailing stop OR close ≥ entry price + Entry ATR × ATR take-profit multiple × Entry volatility scale OR bars held ≥ maximum hold bars OR Phoenix crosses below SMA(Phoenix, signal lookback) OR Phoenix decreases on each of the last max(1, exit-slope lookback) bar-to-bar changes
Exit: Short, close ≥ short trailing stop OR close ≤ entry price − Entry ATR × ATR take-profit multiple × Entry volatility scale OR bars held ≥ maximum hold bars OR Phoenix crosses above SMA(Phoenix, signal lookback) OR Phoenix increases on each of the last max(1, exit-slope lookback) bar-to-bar changes
