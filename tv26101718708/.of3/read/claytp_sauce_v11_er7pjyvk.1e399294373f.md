Calculation: Effective pivot lookback = base lookback, raised to the configured minimum for the applicable chart-timeframe band when automatic adjustment is enabled
Calculation: Active pivot high = latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, effective pivot lookback, effective pivot lookback), replaced by each new confirmed pivot high and inactive after the selected breakout test
Calculation: Active pivot low = latest confirmed PivotLow(low, effective pivot lookback, effective pivot lookback), replaced by each new confirmed pivot low and inactive after the selected breakout test
Calculation: Short SMA slope = SMA(close, short lookback) - SMA(close, short lookback)[1]
Calculation: Middle SMA slope = SMA(close, middle lookback) - SMA(close, middle lookback)[1]
Calculation: Long SMA slope = SMA(close, long lookback) - SMA(close, long lookback)[1]
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: Candle wick = max(high - low - candle body, 0)
Calculation: Long stop = selected stop mode's previous pivot low, adjusted toward the reference entry price by the selected swing-distance percentage when enabled; signal-bar low; or reference entry price reduced by the selected stop percentage, then reduced by the point buffer and reference entry price times the percentage buffer
Calculation: Short stop = selected stop mode's previous pivot high, adjusted toward the reference entry price by the selected swing-distance percentage when enabled; signal-bar high; or reference entry price increased by the selected stop percentage, then increased by the point buffer and reference entry price times the percentage buffer
Calculation: Long target = reference entry price + (reference entry price - long stop) * selected long reward-risk multiplier, using the long-specific multiplier when separate long/short multipliers are enabled and the common multiplier otherwise
Calculation: Short target = reference entry price - (short stop - reference entry price) * selected short reward-risk multiplier, using the short-specific multiplier when separate long/short multipliers are enabled and the common multiplier otherwise
Long: long entries enabled AND (tick mode: active pivot high > high when the stop order is placed, then price reaches the active pivot high intrabar; OR close mode: confirmed close > active pivot high and entry is next bar) AND active pivot high > active pivot low AND (selected entry-type mode permits the break: both types, BOS when the last break was not downward, or CHoCH when it was downward) AND (SMA slope filter disabled OR selected mode passes: all three slopes positive, at least two positive, short and middle positive, short positive, or close above all three SMAs) AND (SMA stacking filter disabled OR short SMA > middle SMA > long SMA) AND (slope-divergence filter disabled OR short SMA slope > middle SMA slope > long SMA slope) AND (NY-anchored VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(ohlc4, configured NY anchor)) AND (calendar-day-anchored VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(ohlc4, configured daily anchor)) AND (trading-window filter disabled OR time is within the configured window, including overnight wrap) AND (daily trade limit disabled OR positions opened today < daily trade limit) AND active pivot high has not already been used for a long order today AND (tick mode: high < active pivot high; OR close mode: strong-candle filter disabled OR close > open AND candle body >= candle wick * (1 + body-to-wick threshold)) AND current position is flat or short AND long stop < reference entry price AND long target > reference entry price AND long stop < long target
Short: short entries enabled AND (tick mode: active pivot low < low when the stop order is placed, then price reaches the active pivot low intrabar; OR close mode: confirmed close < active pivot low and entry is next bar) AND active pivot low < active pivot high AND (selected entry-type mode permits the break: both types, BOS when the last break was not upward, or CHoCH when it was upward) AND (SMA slope filter disabled OR selected mode passes: all three slopes negative, at least two negative, short and middle negative, short negative, or close below all three SMAs) AND (SMA stacking filter disabled OR short SMA < middle SMA < long SMA) AND (slope-divergence filter disabled OR short SMA slope < middle SMA slope < long SMA slope) AND (NY-anchored VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(ohlc4, configured NY anchor)) AND (calendar-day-anchored VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(ohlc4, configured daily anchor)) AND (trading-window filter disabled OR time is within the configured window, including overnight wrap) AND (daily trade limit disabled OR positions opened today < daily trade limit) AND active pivot low has not already been used for a short order today AND (tick mode: low > active pivot low; OR close mode: strong-candle filter disabled OR close < open AND candle body >= candle wick * (1 + body-to-wick threshold)) AND current position is flat or long AND short stop > reference entry price AND short target < reference entry price AND short stop > short target
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
Exit: long, new calendar day AND end-of-day close enabled
Exit: short, new calendar day AND end-of-day close enabled
Exit: long, long stop >= position entry price OR long target <= position entry price OR long stop >= long target
Exit: short, short stop <= position entry price OR short target >= position entry price OR short stop <= short target
