Long: HMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above HMA(close, longer lookback)
Short: HMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below HMA(close, longer lookback)
Long: With no bullish setup active, open < upper Bollinger Band(close, basis MA type, lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close > that band
Short: With no bearish setup active, open > lower Bollinger Band(close, basis MA type, lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close < that band
Long: A chart bar opens below EMA(close, shorter lookback) and EMA(close, longer lookback) and closes above both; then, before any T1 close inside a later chart bar falls below the setup bar low, a T1 close inside a chart bar exceeds the setup bar high. T1 values are read on chart bars; the current T1 bar may still be forming in real time.
Short: A chart bar opens above EMA(close, shorter lookback) and EMA(close, longer lookback) and closes below both; then, before any T1 close inside a later chart bar rises above the setup bar high, a T1 close inside a chart bar falls below the setup bar low. T1 values are read on chart bars; the current T1 bar may still be forming in real time.
Short: A chart bar opens above lower Bollinger Band(close, basis MA type, lookback, deviation multiplier) and closes below it, with no bearish setup active; before the bearish phase wait limit expires or a chart close falls below the setup bar low, a chart bar high comes within the bearish EMA touch tolerance of EMA(close, shorter lookback); then, before the bearish phase wait limit expires or a T2 close inside a chart bar exceeds the high of a chart bar that closed above that EMA, a T2 close inside a chart bar falls below the retest bar low. T2 values are read on chart bars; the current T2 bar may still be forming in real time.
Long: A chart bar opens below upper Bollinger Band(close, basis MA type, lookback, deviation multiplier) and closes above it, with no bullish setup active; before the bullish phase wait limit expires or a chart close falls below EMA(close, shorter lookback), a later chart bar low comes within the bullish EMA touch tolerance of that EMA; then, before the bullish phase wait limit expires or a T3 close inside a chart bar falls below the low of a chart bar that closed below that EMA, a T3 close inside a chart bar exceeds the retest bar high. T3 values are read on chart bars; the current T3 bar may still be forming in real time.
