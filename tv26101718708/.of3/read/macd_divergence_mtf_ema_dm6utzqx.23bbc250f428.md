Calculation: Bullish divergence = on chart bars, when the MACD histogram sampled from T4 crosses above zero, the just-ended negative run’s minimum histogram value (as of the prior chart bar) is greater than the stored preceding negative-run minimum, and the chart close recorded at the just-ended minimum is lower than the stored preceding close; track strict new negative minima and their chart closes, initialize current and preceding memories to zero, reset current memories on every histogram zero-cross, and store the just-ended minimum and close as preceding memories at each crossover above zero. MACD(source, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback) is calculated on T4 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T4 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically).
Calculation: Bearish divergence = on chart bars, when the MACD histogram sampled from T4 crosses below zero, the just-ended positive run’s maximum histogram value (as of the prior chart bar) is lower than the stored preceding positive-run maximum, and the chart close recorded at the just-ended maximum is higher than the stored preceding close; track strict new positive maxima and their chart closes, initialize current and preceding memories to zero, reset current memories on every histogram zero-cross, and store the just-ended maximum and close as preceding memories at each crossunder below zero. Long: Bullish divergence AND flat AND long-position switch enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND EMA(source, shorter fast EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T1 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically) > EMA(source, longer slow EMA lookback) calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T2 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically) AND (third EMA filter disabled OR EMA(source, longer slow EMA lookback) from T2 > EMA(source, longest filter EMA lookback) calculated on T3 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T3 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically)).
Short: Bearish divergence AND flat AND short-position switch enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND EMA(source, shorter fast EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T1 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically) < EMA(source, longer slow EMA lookback) calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T2 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically) AND (third EMA filter disabled OR EMA(source, longer slow EMA lookback) from T2 < EMA(source, longest filter EMA lookback) calculated on T3 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T3 bar (forming in real time, last completed historically)).
Calculation: Long trailing stop = while long, max(previous long trailing stop, Lowest(low, shared trailing lookback on chart bars) * (1 - long stop percentage / 100)); reset while flat.
Calculation: Long take-profit = while long, max(previous long take-profit, entry price + (entry price - current long trailing stop) * shared profit multiplier); reset while flat.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = while short, min(previous short trailing stop, Highest(high, shared trailing lookback on chart bars) * (1 + short stop percentage / 100)); reset to a fixed high sentinel while flat.
Calculation: Short take-profit = while short, min(previous short take-profit, entry price - (current short trailing stop - entry price) * shared profit multiplier); reset to a fixed high sentinel while flat.
Exit: Long, MACD line from T4 crosses below signal line from T4 (calculated on T4 bars and sampled onto chart bars from the current T4 bar, forming in real time and last completed historically; T4 may be the chart timeframe) OR low <= long trailing stop OR high >= long take-profit.
