Calculation: EMA cluster maximum = max of EMA(close, shortest, second-shortest, second-longest, and longest of four separate chart-bar lookbacks)
Calculation: EMA cluster minimum = min of EMA(close, shortest, second-shortest, second-longest, and longest of four separate chart-bar lookbacks)
Calculation: EMA cluster compression = EMA cluster maximum - EMA cluster minimum < (upper Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, standard-deviation multiplier) - lower Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, standard-deviation multiplier)) * compression factor
Long: close of the last completed T1 bar > EMA(close, separate trend lookback, on T1 bars, last completed bar)
Short: close of the last completed T1 bar < EMA(close, separate trend lookback, on T1 bars, last completed bar)
Long: close > EMA cluster maximum AND EMA cluster compression
Short: close < EMA cluster minimum AND EMA cluster compression
Long: close of the last completed T1 bar > EMA(close, separate trend lookback, on T1 bars, last completed bar) AND close > EMA cluster maximum AND EMA cluster compression AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume factor AND close > open
Short: close of the last completed T1 bar < EMA(close, separate trend lookback, on T1 bars, last completed bar) AND close < EMA cluster minimum AND EMA cluster compression AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume factor AND close < open
Long: position is flat or short AND close of the last completed T1 bar > EMA(close, separate trend lookback, on T1 bars, last completed bar) AND close > EMA cluster maximum AND EMA cluster compression AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume factor AND close > open
Short: position is flat or long AND close of the last completed T1 bar < EMA(close, separate trend lookback, on T1 bars, last completed bar) AND close < EMA cluster minimum AND EMA cluster compression AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume factor AND close < open
Long: position is flat AND close > open AND high > upper Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, standard-deviation multiplier)
Short: position is flat AND close < open AND low < lower Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, standard-deviation multiplier)
Exit: long, trailing stop enabled AND 100 * (highest close since entry - entry price) / entry price >= trailing activation percentage AND 100 * (highest close since entry - close) / highest close since entry >= trailing retracement percentage OR 100 * (close - entry price) / entry price >= profit target percentage OR EMA(close, shortest of the four chart-bar lookbacks) < EMA(close, second-shortest of the four chart-bar lookbacks)
Exit: short, trailing stop enabled AND 100 * (entry price - lowest close since entry) / entry price >= trailing activation percentage AND 100 * (close - lowest close since entry) / lowest close since entry >= trailing retracement percentage OR 100 * (entry price - close) / entry price >= profit target percentage OR EMA(close, shortest of the four chart-bar lookbacks) > EMA(close, second-shortest of the four chart-bar lookbacks)
Short: position is flat AND a short watch is active from a long exit caused by EMA(close, shortest lookback) < EMA(close, second-shortest lookback), with neither the trailing-stop nor profit-target condition true on that exit bar, AND close < open AND low < lower Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, standard-deviation multiplier); clear the watch after this entry or on a flat bearish bar at least the watch-window length after the exit when low >= the lower Bollinger Band
Long: position is flat AND a long watch is active from a short exit caused by EMA(close, shortest lookback) > EMA(close, second-shortest lookback), with neither the trailing-stop nor profit-target condition true on that exit bar, AND close > open AND high > upper Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, standard-deviation multiplier); clear the watch after this entry or on a flat bullish bar at least the watch-window length after the exit when high <= the upper Bollinger Band
