Calculation: Support OB = on chart bars, when a pivot low with swing-lookback bars on the left is confirmed by pivot-confirmation bars on the right, select the nearest bearish candle searched from immediately before the pivot candle through the source-candle lookback; require pivot-candle high minus source-candle low >= ATR at the source candle * displacement multiplier; center the zone on the source candle's high-low midpoint with height ATR at the source candle * zone-height multiplier; keep it active until close < its bottom and retain only the newest maximum-active-zone parameter support zones.
Calculation: Resistance OB = on chart bars, when a pivot high with swing-lookback bars on the left is confirmed by pivot-confirmation bars on the right, select the nearest bullish candle searched from immediately before the pivot candle through the source-candle lookback; require source-candle high minus pivot-candle low >= ATR at the source candle * displacement multiplier; center the zone on the source candle's high-low midpoint with height ATR at the source candle * zone-height multiplier; keep it active until close > its top and retain only the newest maximum-active-zone parameter resistance zones.
Calculation: Origin score = clamp(displacement score + structural-break score + body score + volume score, component-score floor, component-score ceiling); displacement ratio = displacement / (ATR at source candle * displacement multiplier), scored by five descending score parameters at four descending ratio-threshold parameters, using the lowest score below all thresholds; structural-break score is the origin bonus parameter when the pivot-candle close > source-candle high for support or < source-candle low for resistance, otherwise zero; body score is the same bonus when absolute pivot-candle body >= SMA(absolute close-open, separate body-average lookback) at the pivot candle * body multiplier, otherwise zero; volume score is the same bonus when pivot-candle volume >= SMA(volume, separate volume-average lookback) at the pivot candle * volume multiplier, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Location score = clamp(premium-discount score + pivot-support-resistance score + trend score + bias score, component-score floor, component-score ceiling); recent range midpoint = (highest(high, range lookback) + lowest(low, range lookback)) / two; premium-discount score is the favorable-location score when the support-zone midpoint < recent range midpoint or resistance-zone midpoint > recent range midpoint, zero otherwise, or the disabled-filter score when that filter is disabled; pivot-support-resistance score is the favorable-location score when the support-zone midpoint is within ATR at source * pivot-distance multiplier of the latest pivot low or resistance-zone midpoint is within that distance of the latest pivot high, zero when a pivot exists but does not match, or the missing-or-disabled score when no pivot exists or the filter is disabled; trend score is the favorable-trend score when close > EMA(close, trend lookback) for support or close < it for resistance, otherwise the unfavorable-trend score; bias score is the favorable-bias score when market bias matches the zone, the neutral-bias score when neutral, or zero otherwise; market bias becomes bullish after close > latest pivot high, bearish after close < latest pivot low, and otherwise retains its previous value, initially neutral.
Calculation: Reaction score = zero when support close < zone bottom or resistance close > zone top; otherwise the win score when resolved at target, loss score when resolved at invalidation, untouched score before first touch, or a coverage score after touch; coverage = clamp(maximum zone penetration / max(zone height, minimum tick) * percentage scale, percentage floor, percentage ceiling), where penetration is zone top - low for support or high - zone bottom for resistance; coverage score is the shallow-touch score below the shallow-coverage threshold, the medium-touch score below the deep-coverage threshold, the deep-touch score below the full-coverage threshold, and the full-touch score otherwise; update maximum coverage across bars; support target = zone midpoint + ATR at source * target-distance multiplier and invalidation = zone bottom - ATR at source * failure-distance multiplier; resistance target = zone midpoint - ATR at source * target-distance multiplier and invalidation = zone top + ATR at source * failure-distance multiplier; after touch, resolve as won when support high >= target or resistance low <= target, else resolve as lost when support low <= invalidation or resistance high >= invalidation.
Calculation: OB score = clamp(weighted mean of origin score, location score, and reaction score using their separate input weights, multiplied by the total-score multiplier, total-score floor, and total-score ceiling).
