Calculation: Volume Profile(chart bars grouped into T1 or T2 periods, row-count parameter, value-area fraction) = divide the period low-to-high range evenly into rows; distribute each bar’s volume equally among rows touched by its low-to-high range, using unit volume for missing or zero volume; choose the lowest-price row tied for maximum volume as POC; expand contiguously from POC by adding the adjacent row with greater volume, choosing the upper row on ties, until the value-area fraction of total volume is reached; VAH and VAL are the outer edges of included rows; each bar touches a row at most once.
Calculation: Long reference = selected VAH from the prior completed T1 or T2 Volume Profile, or the higher of both VAHs; use an available edge if the other is missing. Short reference = selected VAL from the prior completed T1 or T2 Volume Profile, or the lower of both VALs, with the same missing-value fallback. T1 is the shorter profile period and T2 the longer; profiles aggregate chart bars and use completed periods.
Calculation: Micro POC position = (POC-row midpoint − lowest low)/(highest high − lowest low), using Volume Profile on the latest min(micro-profile lookback cap, impulse lookback + balance-bar count) chart bars, with max(minimum micro-profile rows, profile row count / micro-row divisor) rows.
Calculation: Single-print row count = count of rows within the impulse low-to-high range in the current T1 profile whose chart-bar touch count is greater than zero and no greater than the maximum-touch parameter; each chart bar contributes at most one touch per row.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a long, the selected micro-balance low, impulse low, or lower of long reference and micro-balance low, minus ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer; for a short, the selected micro-balance high, impulse high, or higher of short reference and micro-balance high, plus the same buffer.
Calculation: Initial risk = max(abs(entry price − initial stop), minimum price increment).
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until the first target is reached; then entry price if moving the stop to break-even is enabled, otherwise initial stop.
Long: On a confirmed intraday chart bar no higher than T3, within the selected session if session filtering is enabled, while flat and below the current T1 signal limit, a nonstale upward setup exists: over the impulse lookback, range = highest(high) − lowest(low) is at least ATR(ATR lookback) × impulse-size parameter, close is at or above lowest(low) + range × upper-close-fraction parameter, lowest(low) < long reference, and close > long reference; the weekly-location filter, if enabled, requires close > prior completed T2 VAH and, if its open filter is enabled, the first chart-bar open of the current T2 period > that VAH. The pre-trigger balance has at least the minimum balance bars, no more than the maximum, height no greater than ATR × balance-height parameter, and has not been invalidated by a close below impulse low. On the trigger bar, require the weekly test filter if enabled (during the current T2 period a chart bar with prior close > prior T2 VAH and low ≤ VAH + ATR × test-tolerance parameter was followed by a bullish bar closing above VAH with range ≥ ATR × rejection-strength parameter), the T1 VPOC trend filter if enabled (compare adjacent values among the latest requested number of completed T1 VPOCs; require every step higher in strict mode, otherwise at least ceil(step count × required favorable-step fraction) strictly higher steps, with at least one step), close > long reference, the single-print filter if enabled (single-print row count ≥ its minimum parameter), and Micro POC position ≥ the upper-shape parameter; and either the breakout trigger is enabled and close > pre-trigger balance high and close > open, or the break-in trigger is enabled and low ≤ balance low + balance height × retracement-fraction parameter and close > balance low + balance height × midpoint-fraction parameter and close > open.
Short: On a confirmed intraday chart bar no higher than T3, within the selected session if session filtering is enabled, while flat and below the current T1 signal limit, a nonstale downward setup exists: over the impulse lookback, range = highest(high) − lowest(low) is at least ATR(ATR lookback) × impulse-size parameter, close is at or below lowest(low) + range × lower-close-fraction parameter, highest(high) > short reference, and close < short reference; the weekly-location filter, if enabled, requires close < prior completed T2 VAL and, if its open filter is enabled, the first chart-bar open of the current T2 period < that VAL. The pre-trigger balance has at least the minimum balance bars, no more than the maximum, height no greater than ATR × balance-height parameter, and has not been invalidated by a close above impulse high. On the trigger bar, require the weekly test filter if enabled (during the current T2 period a chart bar with prior close < prior T2 VAL and high ≥ VAL − ATR × test-tolerance parameter was followed by a bearish bar closing below VAL with range ≥ ATR × rejection-strength parameter), the T1 VPOC trend filter if enabled (compare adjacent values among the latest requested number of completed T1 VPOCs; require every step lower in strict mode, otherwise at least ceil(step count × required favorable-step fraction) strictly lower steps, with at least one step), close < short reference, the single-print filter if enabled (single-print row count ≥ its minimum parameter), and Micro POC position ≤ the lower-shape parameter; and either the breakout trigger is enabled and close < pre-trigger balance low and close < open, or the break-in trigger is enabled and high ≥ balance high − balance height × retracement-fraction parameter and close < balance high − balance height × midpoint-fraction parameter and close < open.
Exit: long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ entry price + initial risk × second-target R parameter OR (first target not reached AND time stop enabled AND chart bars elapsed since entry ≥ holding-bar limit). The first target is entry price + initial risk × first-target R parameter; reaching it updates the active stop as defined above.
Exit: short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ entry price − initial risk × second-target R parameter OR (first target not reached AND time stop enabled AND chart bars elapsed since entry ≥ holding-bar limit). The first target is entry price − initial risk × first-target R parameter; reaching it updates the active stop as defined above.
