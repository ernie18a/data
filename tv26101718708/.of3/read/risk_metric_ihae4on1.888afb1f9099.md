Calculation: Scale = exp(decay parameter × (chart-bar index − effective offset) × sensitivity parameter) + baseline parameter, where effective offset is the last chart-bar index if the offset parameter is zero, otherwise the offset parameter
Calculation: Risk = clamp((ln(chart close) − ln(lower-bound multiplier × M × lower scale)) / (ln(upper-bound multiplier × M × Scale) − ln(lower-bound multiplier × M × lower scale)), 0, 1), where M = SMA(close, SMA lookback parameter, selected timeframe T1, calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars using the forming T1 bar when available or its latest completed bar otherwise; if chart timeframe is selected, calculate M on chart bars; lower scale = Scale when the dynamic-lower-bound parameter is enabled, otherwise 1
Long: Risk <= entry-threshold parameter / 100, or Risk < entry-threshold parameter / 100 when selected by the entry-comparator parameter
Exit: long, Risk >= exit-threshold parameter / 100, or Risk > exit-threshold parameter / 100 when selected by the exit-comparator parameter
