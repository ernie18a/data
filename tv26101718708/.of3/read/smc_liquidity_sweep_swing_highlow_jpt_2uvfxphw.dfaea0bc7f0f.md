Calculation: long risk = entry price - min(entry-bar low, entry price - ATR(ATR lookback parameter on entry bar) * stop-distance parameter)
Calculation: short risk = max(entry-bar high, entry price + ATR(ATR lookback parameter on entry bar) * stop-distance parameter) - entry price
Long: on a closed chart bar, current low < latest confirmed unswept pivot low(low, swing-left parameter, swing-right parameter) - ATR(ATR lookback parameter) * sweep-buffer parameter, and (close-back filter disabled or close > pivot low) and (rejection-body filter disabled or close > open and (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) * 100 >= minimum-wick-percentage parameter) and (volume filter disabled or volume >= SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) * volume-multiplier parameter) and (EMA trend filter disabled or close > EMA(close, EMA-lookback parameter)) and (no prior signal or at least cooldown-parameter chart bars since the previous long or short signal)
Short: on a closed chart bar, current high > latest confirmed unswept pivot high(high, swing-left parameter, swing-right parameter) + ATR(ATR lookback parameter) * sweep-buffer parameter, and (close-back filter disabled or close < pivot high) and (rejection-body filter disabled or close < open and (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) * 100 >= minimum-wick-percentage parameter) and (volume filter disabled or volume >= SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) * volume-multiplier parameter) and (EMA trend filter disabled or close < EMA(close, EMA-lookback parameter)) and (no prior signal or at least cooldown-parameter chart bars since the previous long or short signal)
Exit: Long, long trade active and (low <= entry price - long risk or high >= entry price + long risk * first-target risk/reward parameter or high >= entry price + long risk * second-target risk/reward parameter or high >= entry price + long risk * third-target risk/reward parameter)
Exit: Short, short trade active and (high >= entry price + short risk or low <= entry price - short risk * first-target risk/reward parameter or low <= entry price - short risk * second-target risk/reward parameter or low <= entry price - short risk * third-target risk/reward parameter)
