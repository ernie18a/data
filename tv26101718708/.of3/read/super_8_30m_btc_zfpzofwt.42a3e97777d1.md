Calculation: Bollinger Band width percentage = 100 × (Bollinger upper band(close, lookback, multiplier) - Bollinger lower band(close, lookback, multiplier)) / Bollinger middle band(close, lookback, multiplier)
Calculation: Long ATR stop = max(low - ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, prior Long ATR stop) when open > prior Long ATR stop; otherwise low - ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier; use the raw value when no prior value exists
Calculation: Short ATR stop = min(high + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, prior Short ATR stop) when open < prior Short ATR stop; otherwise high + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier; use the raw value when no prior value exists
Calculation: Long stop level = average entry price × (1 - stop percentage) in Normal mode, Long ATR stop in ATR mode, or max(Long ATR stop, average entry price × (1 - stop percentage)) in Both mode
Calculation: Short stop level = average entry price × (1 + stop percentage) in Normal mode, Short ATR stop in ATR mode, or min(Short ATR stop, average entry price × (1 + stop percentage)) in Both mode
Calculation: Long take-profit level = average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) in Normal mode, max(highest(high, Donchian lookback), average entry price) in Donchian mode, or max(highest(high, Donchian lookback), average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)) in Both mode
