Calculation: Equal-level tolerance = EMA(average(abs(high - high[1]), abs(low - low[1])), movement EMA lookback) * equal-level tolerance, calculated on chart-timeframe bars and each enabled Tn; Tn calculations use the last completed Tn bar and are processed at the close of the first confirmed chart bar in the next Tn period.
Calculation: EQH formation = On each confirmed source-timeframe bar, a direct match occurs when abs(high - high[1]) < equal-level tolerance AND abs(high[1] - high[2]) >= equal-level tolerance[1] AND (the RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > 50 + strength); OR, when separated pivots are enabled, high[1] is a local peak matching the nearest prior local peak within the separated-pivot lookback, at least two source bars apart, with abs(high[1] - high[i]) < equal-level tolerance[1], all intervening highs < min(high[1], high[i]), and RSI at the newer peak > 50 + strength unless the RSI filter is disabled, provided no direct filtered EQH formed on this or the preceding source bar; the older endpoint is no more than max zone age source bars old. For Tn, use the last completed Tn bar and process at the close of the first confirmed chart bar in the next Tn period.
Calculation: EQL formation = On each confirmed source-timeframe bar, a direct match occurs when abs(low - low[1]) < equal-level tolerance AND abs(low[1] - low[2]) >= equal-level tolerance[1] AND (the RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < 50 - strength); OR, when separated pivots are enabled, low[1] is a local trough matching the nearest prior local trough within the separated-pivot lookback, at least two source bars apart, with abs(low[1] - low[i]) < equal-level tolerance[1], all intervening lows > max(low[1], low[i]), and RSI at the newer trough < 50 - strength unless the RSI filter is disabled, provided no direct filtered EQL formed on this or the preceding source bar; the older endpoint is no more than max zone age source bars old. For Tn, use the last completed Tn bar and process at the close of the first confirmed chart bar in the next Tn period.
Calculation: EQH zone boundaries = lower bound: maximum of the two endpoint opens and closes; upper bound: maximum of the two endpoint highs.
Calculation: EQL zone boundaries = lower bound: minimum of the two endpoint lows; upper bound: minimum of the two endpoint opens and closes.
Calculation: Retained active zones = zones created by EQH or EQL formations, keeping at most the stored-zone limit and deleting the oldest first; a zone becomes inactive when its source-bar age exceeds max zone age or it is swept. On closed source bars, an EQH is swept when its high exceeds the upper bound in wick mode, or its close exceeds the upper bound in body mode; an EQL is swept when its low falls below the lower bound in wick mode, or its close falls below the lower bound in body mode. In body mode, the two-close option also requires the preceding source close to have crossed beyond the same bound.
Long: EQL formation on the chart timeframe OR any enabled Tn.
Short: EQH formation on the chart timeframe OR any enabled Tn.
Long: At a confirmed chart-bar close, an EQL zone confirmed by the chart bar's open is active or was swept at this bar's close without expiring, chart low <= zone upper bound, chart high >= zone lower bound, (chart open >= zone lower bound OR prior chart close >= zone lower bound), and chart close > zone upper bound.
Short: At a confirmed chart-bar close, an EQH zone confirmed by the chart bar's open is active or was swept at this bar's close without expiring, chart low <= zone upper bound, chart high >= zone lower bound, (chart open <= zone upper bound OR prior chart close <= zone upper bound), and chart close < zone lower bound.
