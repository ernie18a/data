Calculation: ATR = ATR(atr lookback)
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: Average Body = SMA(Body, body lookback)
Calculation: Average Volume = SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Calculation: Body Ratio = Body / (high - low) if high > low, else 1
Calculation: VAH = upper edge of the value area of Volume Profile(profile lookback, profile rows, value area fraction)
Calculation: VAL = lower edge of the value area of the same Volume Profile
Calculation: POC = price of the highest-volume row of the same Volume Profile
Calculation: PDH = high of the previous RTH session, fixed at the current RTH open
Calculation: PDL = low of the previous RTH session, fixed at the current RTH open
Calculation: ONH = high of the latest completed overnight session, fixed at the current RTH open
Calculation: ONL = low of the latest completed overnight session, fixed at the current RTH open
Calculation: ORB-H = highest high of the current RTH session's opening range, defined once that range ends
Calculation: ORB-L = lowest low of the same opening range
Calculation: Monday High = highest high of the most recent completed Monday session
Calculation: Trend Up = close > EMA(close, slow lookback) AND (EMA(close, slow lookback) - EMA(close, slow lookback)[slope lookback] > 0 OR close > EMA(close, fast lookback))
Calculation: Trend Down = close < EMA(close, slow lookback) AND (EMA(close, slow lookback) - EMA(close, slow lookback)[slope lookback] < 0 OR close < EMA(close, fast lookback))
Calculation: Value Migration = Higher if the last two POC sample steps both rose, Lower if both fell, otherwise Stable; POC is sampled on bars whose index is a multiple of the sample interval and the state holds between samples
Calculation: Long Touch(L) = low between L - far touch multiple * ATR and L + near touch multiple * ATR; for any L other than VAL, also close[momentum lag] > L AND close > L - break multiple * ATR
Calculation: Long Level = the first of VAL, PDL, PDH, ONL, ONH, ORB-L, ORB-H, in that order, for which Long Touch(L) holds; undefined if none
Calculation: Short Touch(L) = high between L - near touch multiple * ATR and L + far touch multiple * ATR; for any L other than VAH, also close[momentum lag] < L AND close < L + break multiple * ATR
Calculation: Short Level = the first of VAH, PDH, PDL, ONH, ONL, ORB-H, ORB-L, in that order, for which Short Touch(L) holds; undefined if none
Calculation: Extreme Support = (Long Touch(VAL) AND close[momentum lag] > VAL AND fewer than support break count consecutive closes below VAL) OR Long Touch(ONL) OR Long Touch(PDL)
Calculation: Extreme Resistance = (Short Touch(VAH) AND close[momentum lag] < VAH) OR Short Touch(ONH) OR Short Touch(PDH)
Calculation: Long Allowed = (Value Migration is not Lower OR Extreme Support) AND (Trend Up OR Long Level is defined)
Calculation: Short Allowed = (Value Migration is not Higher OR Extreme Resistance) AND (Trend Down OR Short Level is defined)
Calculation: Long Target Clear(stop) = no candidate among VAH, POC (if above close), PDH, ONH, ORB-H (when defined) lies above close at a distance D with min target fraction * R <= D < min R:R * R, where R = close - stop
Calculation: Short Target Clear(stop) = no candidate among VAL, POC (if below close), PDL, ONL, ORB-L (when defined) lies below close at a distance D with min target fraction * R <= D < min R:R * R, where R = stop - close
Calculation: Absorption Bear = Body Ratio <= absorption body limit AND volume > Average Volume * max(absorption volume multiple, absorption volume floor) AND volume >= Highest(volume, spike lookback) AND close > open AND bars since the previous Absorption Bear > absorption cooldown
Calculation: Absorption Bull = Body Ratio <= absorption body limit AND volume > Average Volume * max(absorption volume multiple, absorption volume floor) AND volume >= Highest(volume, spike lookback) AND close <= open AND bars since the previous Absorption Bull > absorption cooldown
Calculation: Trap Buyers = close[1] > open[1] AND Body[1] > Average Body[1] * prior body multiple AND volume[1] > Average Volume[1] * prior volume multiple AND close < open[1] AND Body > Average Body * body multiple AND bars since the previous Trap Buyers > trap cooldown
Calculation: Trap Sellers = close[1] < open[1] AND Body[1] > Average Body[1] * prior body multiple AND volume[1] > Average Volume[1] * prior volume multiple AND close > open[1] AND Body > Average Body * body multiple AND bars since the previous Trap Sellers > trap cooldown
Calculation: Look Above Fail = high within LAF zone multiple * ATR of VAH, PDH, ONH or ORB-H (when defined) AND close < EMA(close, LAF EMA lookback) + LAF trend multiple * ATR AND high > Highest(high, swing lookback)[1] AND high - Highest(high, swing lookback)[1] > min wick fraction * close AND close < Highest(high, swing lookback)[1] AND close < open AND bars since the previous Look Above Fail > LAF cooldown
Calculation: Look Below Fail = low within LAF zone multiple * ATR of VAL, PDL, ONL or ORB-L (when defined) AND close > EMA(close, LAF EMA lookback) - LAF trend multiple * ATR AND low < Lowest(low, swing lookback)[1] AND Lowest(low, swing lookback)[1] - low > min wick fraction * close AND close > Lowest(low, swing lookback)[1] AND close > open AND bars since the previous Look Below Fail > LAF cooldown
Calculation: VA Re-entry Long = close >= VAL_snap AND close <= VAH_snap AND close > open AND consecutive closes below VAL_snap through the previous bar >= min bars outside AND (VAL_snap - lowest close since the later of the RTH open and the last firing) / VAL_snap >= min excursion fraction AND bars since the previous VA Re-entry Long > VA cooldown; VAH_snap and VAL_snap are VAH and VAL on the RTH-open bar
Calculation: VA Re-entry Short = close <= VAH_snap AND close >= VAL_snap AND close < open AND consecutive closes above VAH_snap through the previous bar >= min bars outside AND (highest close since the later of the RTH open and the last firing - VAH_snap) / VAH_snap >= min excursion fraction AND bars since the previous VA Re-entry Short > VA cooldown; VAH_snap and VAL_snap are VAH and VAL on the RTH-open bar
Calculation: Bear Exhaustion Score = number of true among: Body < Body[1] AND Body[1] < Body[2]; volume < volume[1] AND volume[1] < volume[2]; close > close[momentum lag] AND close < open; upper wick > wick body multiple * Body AND upper wick > wick ATR multiple * ATR, where upper wick = high - max(open, close); Absorption Bear; Look Above Fail; Trap Buyers
Calculation: Bull Exhaustion Score = number of true among: Body < Body[1] AND Body[1] < Body[2]; volume < volume[1] AND volume[1] < volume[2]; close < close[momentum lag] AND close > open; lower wick > wick body multiple * Body AND lower wick > wick ATR multiple * ATR, where lower wick = min(open, close) - low; Absorption Bull; Look Below Fail; Trap Sellers
Calculation: Near Key Level = abs(close - L) < exhaustion zone multiple * ATR for some L in VAH, VAL, PDH, PDL, ONH, ONL, ORB-H, ORB-L (when defined)
Calculation: Recent REV WARN = a REV WARN fired within the last REV WARN recency window bars; a REV WARN fires on a bar where bars since the previous REV WARN > REV WARN cooldown AND ((Near Key Level AND close > close[momentum lag] AND Bear Exhaustion Score >= exhaustion threshold) OR (Near Key Level AND close < close[momentum lag] AND Bull Exhaustion Score >= exhaustion threshold))
Calculation: Long Order Flow = Absorption Bull OR Trap Sellers OR Look Below Fail OR VA Re-entry Long
Calculation: Short Order Flow = Absorption Bear OR Trap Buyers OR Look Above Fail OR VA Re-entry Short
Calculation: Breakout = the level, direction and bar of the latest breakout registered on a confirmed bar that is not an RTH-open or overnight-open bar; an up-breakout if close > L + break multiple * ATR AND close[1] <= L + break multiple * ATR for the first L in ORB-L, ONL, PDL, VAL, ORB-H, ONH, PDH, VAH that does so, else a down-breakout if close < L - break multiple * ATR AND close[1] >= L - break multiple * ATR for the first L in ORB-H, ONH, PDH, VAH, PDL, ONL, ORB-L, VAL that does so; each registration replaces the previous breakout
Calculation: Long Setup Level = Long Level of the latest long setup start that is still alive; a long setup starts on a confirmed bar with Bars Since Entry > entry cooldown / 2 AND Allow long, where either (a) Long Level is defined AND low < Long Level AND close > Long Level AND close > open, or (b) no long setup is alive AND low < VAL + setup zone multiple * ATR AND (Look Below Fail OR Absorption Bull OR Trap Sellers), using level VAL; it stays alive until bars since its start exceed the confirmation window, or low < its level - setup expiry multiple * ATR, or a Short Agg, Retest or Confirmation entry fires, or a Long Confirmation entry fires; Setup Low = low on the start bar; Bounce Low = lowest low since the start, tracked while bars since start <= bounce window
Calculation: Short Setup Level = Short Level of the latest short setup start that is still alive; a short setup starts on a confirmed bar with Bars Since Entry > entry cooldown / 2 AND Allow short, where either (a) Short Level is defined AND high > Short Level AND close < Short Level AND close < open, or (b) no short setup is alive AND high > VAH - setup zone multiple * ATR AND (Look Above Fail OR Absorption Bear OR Trap Buyers), using level VAH; it stays alive until bars since its start exceed the confirmation window, or high > its level + setup expiry multiple * ATR, or a Long Agg, Retest or Confirmation entry fires, or a Short Confirmation entry fires; Setup High = high on the start bar; Bounce High = highest high since the start, tracked while bars since start <= bounce window
Calculation: Bars Since Entry = bars since the latest bar on which any Long or Short Agg, Retest, Confirmation or Squeeze entry rule below held, ignoring its Target Clear term
