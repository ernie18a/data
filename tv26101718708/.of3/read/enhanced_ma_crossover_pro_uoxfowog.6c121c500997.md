Calculation: daily PnL (ticks) = today's realized side-adjusted closed-trade PnL in ticks + current position side × (close − average entry price) in ticks
Calculation: long trailing stop = highest high since entry − trailing distance
Calculation: short trailing stop = lowest low since entry + trailing distance
Long: (HTF entry enabled AND crossover(HTF fast MA, HTF slow MA) on chart bars using T1 values from T1 bars [last completed T1 bar historically; still-forming T1 bar in realtime]) OR (HTF entry disabled AND crossover(fast MA, slow MA) on chart bars)
