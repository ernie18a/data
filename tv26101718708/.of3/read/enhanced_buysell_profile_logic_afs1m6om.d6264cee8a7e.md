Calculation: Estimated buy share(OHLC, estimation mode, weights, neutral-bar setting) = clamp(selected model, 0, 1), where close position = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment); body share = 0.5 + 0.5 × clamp((close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), -1, 1); direction share = 1, 0, or 0.5 according to whether close is above, below, or equal to the candle midpoint. The selected model is bar direction, close position, or the normalized weighted sum of body share, close position, and direction share; when neutral-bar splitting is selected, a doji within one minimum price increment has share 0.5. Estimated buy volume = volume × share; estimated sell volume = volume × (1 - share). If lower-timeframe reconstruction is selected, calculate on constituent T1 bars and sum into each chart bar, including any still-forming T1 bar in a still-forming chart bar; otherwise calculate from chart bars.
Calculation: Price-volume profile(window, bin count, allocation mode and weights) = divide the selected trailing chart-bar lookback or current session range into equal price bins, including the current chart bar while it is forming. Cap the window by available bars and the configured maximum, and by the fast-mode maximum when enabled. For each bar, allocate its estimated buy, sell, and total volume across bins intersecting its low-to-high range in proportion to bin scores: equal scores, or body-overlap × body-row-weight + wick-overlap × wick-row-weight, with the latter multiplied by 1 + close-bias-strength × clamp(1 - distance from bin midpoint to close / max(high - low, minimum price increment), 0, 1) in close-biased mode. Divide evenly across touched bins if the score sum is at or below the fallback floor. With lower-timeframe reconstruction, use individual T1 candle prices for current-bar row placement when selected; otherwise distribute the aggregated estimates using chart-bar prices. Fall back to chart-bar estimates if T1 data is unavailable and fallback is selected.
Calculation: VWMP(profile) = sum(bin midpoint × bin total volume) / sum(bin total volume).
Calculation: Net delta percentage(buy volume, sell volume) = 100 × (buy volume - sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume), when the denominator is positive.
Calculation: Profile net delta percentage = Net delta percentage(sum of profile-bin buy volume, sum of profile-bin sell volume).
Calculation: Historical net delta percentage = Net delta percentage(sum of estimated buy volume, sum of estimated sell volume over the selected trailing chart-bar lookback or current session). The lookback is capped by the fast-mode maximum when fast mode is enabled.
Calculation: Cluster buffer = buffer-row parameter × profile bin height.
Calculation: Top directional cluster(profile, side) = the highest-scoring contiguous cluster of adjacent bins with that side's allocated volume greater than the other side's, each bin's imbalance percentage at least the cluster threshold, each bin's total volume at least the row-volume fraction of the maximum bin volume, and cluster length at least the minimum row count; cluster score is the sum of its bin imbalance percentages.
Calculation: Eligible imbalance levels(profile) = bins whose absolute buy/sell imbalance percentage meets the minimum imbalance threshold and whose total volume meets the row-volume fraction of the maximum bin volume, ranked by imbalance percentage or by imbalance percentage × bin volume / maximum bin volume, and selected from the configured top count or the strongest bin alone.
Calculation: Structural score(profile) = sign(close - POC(profile)) + sign(close - VWMP(profile)), where sign is positive, zero, or negative for a positive, zero, or negative difference.
Calculation: Flow score(profile) = the net-delta contribution (positive weight when profile net delta percentage exceeds the separate bias threshold, negative weight when below its negative, otherwise zero) plus the winning top-cluster contribution: a larger positive or negative weight when that cluster's score meets the cluster-strength cutoff, otherwise a smaller weight; a tie or no cluster contributes zero.
Calculation: Directional bias score(profile) = Structural score(profile) + Flow score(profile).
Calculation: Bear-pressure structural failure = (bar range intersects POC(profile) ± Cluster buffer and close > POC(profile) + Cluster buffer) OR (bar range intersects VWMP(profile) ± Cluster buffer and close > VWMP(profile) + Cluster buffer) OR (a top sell cluster exists and close > its upper boundary + Cluster buffer).
Calculation: Bull-pressure structural failure = (bar range intersects POC(profile) ± Cluster buffer and close < POC(profile) - Cluster buffer) OR (bar range intersects VWMP(profile) ± Cluster buffer and close < VWMP(profile) - Cluster buffer) OR (a top buy cluster exists and close < its lower boundary - Cluster buffer).
Calculation: Bullish absorption armed state = set when low <= top sell cluster upper boundary + Cluster buffer; clear when close > that boundary + the absorption disarm-buffer multiplier × Cluster buffer, or after a bullish absorption fires.
Calculation: Bearish absorption armed state = set when high >= top buy cluster lower boundary - Cluster buffer; clear when close < that boundary - the absorption disarm-buffer multiplier × Cluster buffer, or after a bearish absorption fires.
Calculation: Bull reclaim armed state = set when close < top sell cluster upper boundary - Cluster buffer; clear after a bull reclaim fires.
Calculation: Bear reject armed state = set when close > top buy cluster lower boundary + Cluster buffer; clear after a bear reject fires.
Calculation: Bear-pressure-failure armed state = while historical net delta percentage is at or below the negative strong-pressure threshold, set when Bear-pressure structural failure is false; clear when that pressure condition ends or after a failure fires.
Calculation: Bull-pressure-failure armed state = while historical net delta percentage is at or above the strong-pressure threshold, set when Bull-pressure structural failure is false; clear when that pressure condition ends or after a failure fires.
