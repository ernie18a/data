Calculation: T1-mapped close = each advance or decline series close from the still-forming T1 bar, carried onto chart bars; missing values are zero; T1 is the selected source timeframe, which may be the chart timeframe
Calculation: T2-mapped close = each external series close from the still-forming T2 bar, carried onto chart bars
Calculation: weighted advances = sum of each advance-series T1-mapped close times its separate market-weight parameter
Calculation: weighted declines = sum of each decline-series T1-mapped close times its separate market-weight parameter
Calculation: weighted net breadth = weighted advances - weighted declines
Calculation: ratio-adjusted breadth = ratio multiplier * weighted net breadth / (weighted advances + weighted declines)
Calculation: breadth source = ratio-adjusted breadth or weighted net breadth, selected by a parameter
Calculation: breadth MACD = MACD line(breadth source, shorter oscillator lookback, longer oscillator lookback), evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: roll yield = (front-month futures T2-mapped close - volatility-index T2-mapped close) / volatility-index T2-mapped close, evaluated on chart bars
Long: breadth MACD <= -oscillator threshold AND breadth MACD > its previous chart-bar value
Short: breadth MACD >= oscillator threshold AND breadth MACD < its previous chart-bar value
Short: outside the cycle month in cycle years AND (Bollinger %B(chart-timeframe skew-series close, skew %B lookback, deviation multiplier) > skew threshold OR its previous chart-bar value > skew threshold) AND position is flat or long
Short: outside the cycle month in cycle years AND EMA(breadth MACD, shorter buy EMA lookback) > EMA(breadth MACD, longer buy EMA lookback) AND position is flat or short
Short: outside the cycle month in cycle years AND Bollinger %B(breadth-index T2-mapped close, breadth %B lookback, deviation multiplier) >= current breadth %B threshold AND its previous chart-bar value <= previous breadth %B threshold AND SMA(chart-timeframe skew-series close, skew SMA lookback) > its value slope lookback bars ago AND position is flat or long
Short: outside the cycle month in cycle years AND chart-timeframe small-cap index close > SMA(chart-timeframe small-cap index close, small-cap SMA lookback) AND SMA(chart-timeframe skew-series close, skew SMA lookback) > its value slope lookback bars ago AND position is flat or long
Long: ((year - cycle reference year) modulo cycle interval = 0 AND month = cycle month AND day is between long-window start day and end day inclusive) OR (roll yield <= roll-yield threshold AND SMA(chart-timeframe percentage-above-average series close, breadth SMA lookback) < its value breadth slope lookback bars ago AND (Bollinger %B(volatility-of-volatility T2-mapped close, first volatility %B lookback, deviation multiplier) >= volatility %B threshold OR (Bollinger %B(volatility-linked T2-mapped close, second volatility %B lookback, deviation multiplier) >= volatility %B threshold AND EMA(breadth MACD, shorter sell EMA lookback) < its previous chart-bar value)) AND Bollinger %B(chart-timeframe skew-series close, skew %B lookback, deviation multiplier) <= skew threshold AND its previous chart-bar value <= skew threshold AND chart-timeframe small-cap index close <= SMA(chart-timeframe small-cap index close, small-cap SMA lookback)) AND position is flat or short
