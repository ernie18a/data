Calculation: bar delta = volume * (close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: swept before this bar = for an upper zone, high exceeded the zone top on an earlier bar since the zone was created; for a lower zone, low fell below the zone bottom on an earlier bar since the zone was created
Calculation: upper zones = list of at most max zones upper zones, newest first, updated each bar: when PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookforward) is confirmed on this bar, a candidate zone is formed with top = high[pivot lookforward] and bottom = min(max(open, close)[pivot lookforward], top - thickness factor * ATR(ATR period)[pivot lookforward]); if overlap filter is on, the upper zones not swept before this bar that overlap the candidate (max(candidate bottom, zone bottom) <= min(candidate top, zone top)) are scanned oldest first, a scanned zone with top below candidate top is removed, and the first scanned zone with top at or above candidate top prevents adding the candidate; the candidate is added unless prevented; the oldest zone is removed when the list exceeds max zones
Calculation: lower zones = list of at most max zones lower zones, newest first, updated each bar: when PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookforward) is confirmed on this bar, a candidate zone is formed with bottom = low[pivot lookforward] and top = max(min(open, close)[pivot lookforward], bottom + thickness factor * ATR(ATR period)[pivot lookforward]); if overlap filter is on, the lower zones not swept before this bar that overlap the candidate (max(candidate bottom, zone bottom) <= min(candidate top, zone top)) are scanned oldest first, a scanned zone with bottom above candidate bottom is removed, and the first scanned zone with bottom at or below candidate bottom prevents adding the candidate; the candidate is added unless prevented; the oldest zone is removed when the list exceeds max zones
Calculation: slice delta(s) = for slice s of slice count equal slices of a zone's height, numbered 1 at the bottom, the sum over bars from the zone's creation through the current bar of bar delta * max(0, min(high, slice top) - max(low, slice bottom)) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: total absolute slice delta = sum over all slices s of |slice delta(s)|
Calculation: outer slice delta = slice delta(slice count) for an upper zone, or slice delta(1) for a lower zone
Calculation: outer share = |outer slice delta| / total absolute slice delta
Calculation: profit run = number of consecutive bars ending at the current bar, all after the entry bar, whose close is above the entry price for a long or below it for a short
Calculation: bars since entry = current bar index - entry bar index
Short: an upper zone in upper zones AND NOT swept before this bar AND no earlier short marking on the zone AND total absolute slice delta > 0 AND ((high > zone top AND outer slice delta < 0 AND outer share > absorption threshold) OR (high > zone top AND outer share < exhaustion threshold, where exhaustion threshold < absorption threshold < divergence threshold) OR (close between zone bottom and zone top AND outer slice delta > 0 AND outer share > divergence threshold) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND |bar delta| / volume > rejection threshold))
Long: a lower zone in lower zones AND NOT swept before this bar AND no earlier long marking on the zone AND total absolute slice delta > 0 AND ((low < zone bottom AND outer slice delta > 0 AND outer share > absorption threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND outer share < exhaustion threshold, where exhaustion threshold < absorption threshold < divergence threshold) OR (close between zone bottom and zone top AND outer slice delta < 0 AND outer share > divergence threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND |bar delta| / volume > rejection threshold))
Exit: long, profit run >= hold bars OR bars since entry >= window bars
Exit: short, profit run >= hold bars OR bars since entry >= window bars
