Calculation: direction score = +strong direction score if close(T1, sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime) is above EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) and EMA(close, middle lookback, T1) and EMA(close, longer lookback, T1), and +DI(DMI lookback, ADX smoothing, T1) > -DI(DMI lookback, ADX smoothing, T1); otherwise +bullish direction score if close is above all three EMAs; otherwise -strong direction score if close is below all three EMAs and -DI > +DI; otherwise -bearish direction score if close is below all three EMAs; otherwise +mixed direction score if close > EMA(shorter lookback) and close < EMA(middle lookback); otherwise -mixed direction score if close < EMA(shorter lookback) and close > EMA(middle lookback); otherwise 0, where shorter < middle < longer lookback
Calculation: trend score = +strong trend score if ADX(DMI lookback, ADX smoothing, T1) > strong ADX threshold and +DI > -DI; otherwise -strong trend score if ADX > strong ADX threshold and -DI > +DI; otherwise +trend score if ADX > medium ADX threshold and +DI > -DI; otherwise -trend score if ADX > medium ADX threshold and -DI > +DI; otherwise weak trend score if ADX > weak ADX threshold; otherwise 0
Calculation: momentum score = strong up score if RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1) > highest RSI threshold; otherwise accelerating score if RSI > next RSI threshold; otherwise bullish momentum score if RSI > next RSI threshold; otherwise 0 if RSI > middle RSI threshold; otherwise bearish momentum score if RSI > next RSI threshold; otherwise declining score if RSI > lowest RSI threshold; otherwise strong down score
Calculation: composite score = clamp(((direction score + trend score + momentum score) / 3) × factor, -score cap, score cap), where factor is the high volatility reduction factor if volatility score > higher volatility score threshold, the lower volatility reduction factor if volatility score > lower volatility score threshold, and 1 otherwise
Long: composite score > positive weak-buy threshold
Short: composite score <= negative weak-sell threshold
