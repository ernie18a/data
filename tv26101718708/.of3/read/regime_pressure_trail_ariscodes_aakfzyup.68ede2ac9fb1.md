Calculation: Bar delta volume = volume when close >= open, otherwise -volume.
Calculation: Cumulative delta volume = cumulative sum of bar delta volume.
Calculation: Volatility regime = low when ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), regime lookback) < low-volatility threshold, high when it exceeds the high-volatility threshold, and medium otherwise; the low threshold is below the high threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR trail line and direction = use close ± ATR(ATR lookback) × the regime-specific ATR multiplier; while bullish, trail at the maximum of the current lower band and prior bullish line; while bearish, trail at the minimum of the current upper band and prior bearish line; initialize bullish, flip bullish when close crosses above the prior bearish line, and flip bearish when close crosses below the prior bullish line. The ATR expansion averaging lookback is shorter than the regime lookback.
Calculation: Trend RSI = RSI(trail source, trend RSI lookback).
Calculation: Trend strength score = score scale × (trend RSI weight × min(abs(Trend RSI − Trend RSI[RSI slope lookback]) / RSI-change normalization, component ceiling) + trend volume weight × min(max(volume / SMA(volume, trend volume lookback) − 1, 0) / volume-surge normalization, component ceiling) + trend ATR weight × min(max(ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), short ATR lookback) − 1, 0) / ATR-expansion normalization, component ceiling)).
Calculation: Pressure RSI = RSI(close, pressure RSI lookback).
Calculation: Pressure score = score scale × (pressure delta weight × (if volume > 0 then abs(Bar delta volume) / volume else 0) + pressure volume weight × min(max(volume / SMA(volume, pressure volume lookback) − 1, 0) / volume-surge normalization, component ceiling) + pressure body weight × (if high > low then min(abs(close − open) / (high − low), component ceiling) else 0)).
Calculation: Entry management type = trail-based for an accepted trail-engine entry, or for a two-engine confluence entry when trend strength score at entry >= pressure score at entry; pressure-based otherwise. If both engines produce the accepted direction without requiring confluence, use the trail engine when its signal is present.
Calculation: Entry target = for trail-based long entries, entry price + ATR at entry × trail target multiplier × (trail target boost when trend strength score at entry >= strong-score threshold, otherwise 1); for trail-based short entries, entry price − the same amount; for pressure-based long entries, entry price × (1 + pressure target percentage / 100); for pressure-based short entries, entry price × (1 − pressure target percentage / 100).
Calculation: Entry stop = the adaptive ATR trail line at entry for trail-based entries; for pressure-based long entries, entry price × (1 − pressure stop percentage / 100); for pressure-based short entries, entry price × (1 + pressure stop percentage / 100).
Long: Bar delta volume >= 0.
Short: Bar delta volume < 0.
Long: close > VWAP(hlc3).
Short: close <= VWAP(hlc3).
Long: adaptive ATR trail direction is bullish.
Short: adaptive ATR trail direction is bearish.
Long: close >= open AND pressure score >= pressure-color threshold.
Short: close < open AND pressure score >= pressure-color threshold.
Long: Pressure RSI < oversold threshold.
Short: Pressure RSI > overbought threshold.
Long: on a confirmed bar while flat and after the cooldown since the previous signal, the configuration selects the trail engine without requiring confluence AND (regime arming is inactive OR volatility regime is medium or high) AND adaptive ATR trail flips bullish AND trend strength score >= trend score minimum; OR the configuration selects the pressure engine without requiring confluence AND (regime arming is inactive OR volatility regime is low or medium) AND Cumulative delta volume crosses above SMA(Cumulative delta volume, delta lookback) AND pressure score >= pressure score minimum AND close >= open AND Pressure RSI < overbought threshold; OR the configuration requires both engines to agree AND adaptive ATR trail flips bullish AND trend strength score >= trend score minimum AND Cumulative delta volume crosses above SMA(Cumulative delta volume, delta lookback) AND pressure score >= pressure score minimum AND close >= open AND Pressure RSI < overbought threshold.
Short: on a confirmed bar while flat and after the cooldown since the previous signal, no long-entry condition is true AND ((the configuration selects the trail engine without requiring confluence AND (regime arming is inactive OR volatility regime is medium or high) AND adaptive ATR trail flips bearish AND trend strength score >= trend score minimum) OR (the configuration selects the pressure engine without requiring confluence AND (regime arming is inactive OR volatility regime is low or medium) AND Cumulative delta volume crosses below SMA(Cumulative delta volume, delta lookback) AND pressure score >= pressure score minimum AND close < open AND Pressure RSI > oversold threshold) OR (the configuration requires both engines to agree AND adaptive ATR trail flips bearish AND trend strength score >= trend score minimum AND Cumulative delta volume crosses below SMA(Cumulative delta volume, delta lookback) AND pressure score >= pressure score minimum AND close < open AND Pressure RSI > oversold threshold)).
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar, high >= Entry target OR low <= Entry stop OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR trail-flip exit is enabled AND adaptive ATR trail flips bearish.
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar, low <= Entry target OR high >= Entry stop OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR trail-flip exit is enabled AND adaptive ATR trail flips bullish.
