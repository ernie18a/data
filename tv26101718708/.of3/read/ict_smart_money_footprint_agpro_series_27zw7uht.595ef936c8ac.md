Calculation: Candle range = max(high - low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: ATR basis = max(ATR(ATR lookback), true range when ATR is unavailable, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Directional close location = (close - low) / candle range for bullish candles; (high - close) / candle range for bearish candles
Calculation: Same-direction HTF-zone context = true when the current chart bar overlaps an unmitigated zone of that direction among the retained HTF zones; bullish zones are centered on confirmed T2 pivot lows and bearish zones on confirmed T2 pivot highs, each with half-height equal to half of T2 ATR(ATR lookback) times the HTF zone-height multiplier; T2 pivots use a separate left/right pivot lookback, and T2 pivot and ATR values are sampled on chart bars with lookahead disabled, using the last completed T2 bar historically and the still-forming T2 bar in realtime; zone generation must be enabled, zones are mitigated when a chart bar overlaps them, and only the configured maximum number of zones is retained
Calculation: Footprint sequence score = round(clamp(sequence base score + displacement-strength weight × clamp((candle range / ATR basis - displacement multiplier) / (displacement multiplier × (strength ceiling factor - 1)), normalization lower bound, normalization upper bound) + close-location weight × directional close location + HTF-context bonus × same-direction HTF-zone context, score lower bound, score upper bound))
Calculation: Effective sequence-score threshold = max(user minimum sequence score, selected workflow-profile floor, selected display-mode floor); the workflow floor is the selected Publish, Swing, Intraday, or Scalp floor, or the user minimum for Auto; the display floor is the selected Publish or Clean floor, or the user minimum for Full
Calculation: Bias score = on chart bars, reset to zero at each new T1 period; otherwise carry the previous bar's score, add the bullish-event weight once if any bullish sweep, displacement, or reclaim occurs, subtract the bearish-event weight once if any bearish sweep, displacement, or reclaim occurs, add the sequence weight separately for each bullish sequence trigger and confirmation, and subtract it separately for each bearish sequence trigger and confirmation
Long: sweep detection enabled AND low < latest confirmed chart-bar pivot low using the LTF pivot lookback - ATR basis × sweep-buffer fraction AND close > that pivot low AND close > open
Short: sweep detection enabled AND high > latest confirmed chart-bar pivot high using the LTF pivot lookback + ATR basis × sweep-buffer fraction AND close < that pivot high AND close < open
Long: displacement detection enabled AND close > open AND candle range > ATR basis × displacement multiplier AND close > previous bar's high AND directional close location >= displacement close-strength threshold
Short: displacement detection enabled AND close < open AND candle range > ATR basis × displacement multiplier AND close < previous bar's low AND directional close location >= displacement close-strength threshold
Long: reclaim detection enabled AND, within the reclaim lookback after a close crossed below the latest confirmed chart-bar pivot low from at or above it, close crosses above that stored pivot-low level
Short: reclaim detection enabled AND, within the reclaim lookback after a close crossed above the latest confirmed chart-bar pivot high from at or below it, close crosses below that stored pivot-high level
Long: footprint-range creation enabled AND a bullish displacement occurs AND the most recent bullish sweep occurred within the sequence lookback AND (weak-sequence filter disabled OR footprint sequence score >= effective sequence-score threshold) AND max(minimum of the sweep-bar open and close, minimum of the displacement-bar open and close) > min(sweep-bar low, displacement-bar low)
Short: footprint-range creation enabled AND a bearish displacement occurs AND the most recent bearish sweep occurred within the sequence lookback AND (weak-sequence filter disabled OR footprint sequence score >= effective sequence-score threshold) AND max(sweep-bar high, displacement-bar high) > min(maximum of the sweep-bar open and close, maximum of the displacement-bar open and close)
Long: the most recently triggered footprint sequence is bullish and unconfirmed, has not expired under the maximum sequence-lifetime parameter, and a bullish reclaim occurs no later than the reclaim lookback after the sequence trigger
Short: the most recently triggered footprint sequence is bearish and unconfirmed, has not expired under the maximum sequence-lifetime parameter, and a bearish reclaim occurs no later than the reclaim lookback after the sequence trigger
Long: bias score >= bias threshold
Short: bias score <= negative bias threshold
Long: bias score >= bias threshold AND previous bar's bias score < bias threshold
Short: bias score <= negative bias threshold AND previous bar's bias score > negative bias threshold
