Calculation: T1 close and volume = values requested from T1 on chart bars; use the current T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically. All following calculations and offsets run on chart bars taking these T1 values.
Calculation: Benchmark close = selected benchmark close requested from T1 on chart bars, using the current T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically.
Calculation: Full-history status = if the minimum-history setting meets the longest-history cutoff, require close at both the longest-history and long-moving-average lookbacks; else if it meets the long-moving-average cutoff, require close at that lookback; else if it meets the medium-history cutoff, require close at that lookback; else require close at the short-history lookback.
Calculation: Minimum-history status = if the minimum-history setting meets either the longest-history or long-moving-average cutoff, require close at both the medium- and short-history lookbacks; else if it meets the medium-history cutoff, require close at the short-history lookback; else pass.
Calculation: History score = full-history value when full-history status passes, else partial-history value when minimum-history status passes, else low-history value.
Calculation: Long-horizon excess return = close at the recent-exclusion lookback divided by close at the long return lookback, minus one; subtract the same benchmark return when relative strength uses a benchmark.
Calculation: Medium-horizon excess return = close divided by close at the medium return lookback, minus one; subtract the same benchmark return when relative strength uses a benchmark.
Calculation: Short-horizon excess return = close divided by close at the short return lookback, minus one; subtract the same benchmark return when relative strength uses a benchmark.
Calculation: High-proximity = close divided by the highest close over the high-proximity lookback.
Calculation: Relative-strength line = close divided by benchmark close when the instrument mode uses a benchmark, otherwise close; relative-strength slope = its percentage change over the RS-slope lookback.
Calculation: Relative-strength score = the weighted mean, normalized over available components, of percent-ranks over the RS-ranking lookback for long-horizon excess return, medium-horizon excess return, short-horizon excess return, high-proximity, and relative-strength slope; use separate component weights for those five components.
Calculation: Relative-strength coverage = available relative-strength components divided by the number of relative-strength components.
Calculation: EPS score = clamp((winsorized reported EPS growth minus its lower bound) divided by the range between its lower and upper bounds, to the unit interval).
Calculation: Revenue score = clamp((winsorized reported revenue growth minus its lower bound) divided by the range between its lower and upper bounds, to the unit interval).
Calculation: ROE score = clamp(winsorized reported return on equity divided by its normalization ceiling, to the unit interval); use reported TTM ROE, else FY ROE, else TTM net income divided by FQ equity when equity is positive.
Calculation: Earnings score = the weighted mean, normalized over available components, of EPS, revenue, and ROE scores; use separate component weights. Revenue growth uses FQ data when available, otherwise TTM data; EPS growth uses FQ data.
Calculation: Earnings coverage = available EPS, revenue, and ROE scores divided by the number of earnings components.
Calculation: Drawdown = (close divided by the highest close over the drawdown lookback, minus one) times one hundred; drawdown threshold = the lower of the configured maximum drawdown and negative ATR multiple times ATR divided by close times one hundred.
Calculation: Trend score = the weighted mean, normalized over available components, of clamp((close's percentage distance above the long SMA plus its lower offset) divided by its range), clamp((short SMA's percentage spread over the long SMA plus its lower offset) divided by its range), and clamp((drawdown minus drawdown threshold) divided by the greater of absolute drawdown threshold and its floor); use separate component weights.
Calculation: Trend coverage = available trend components divided by the number of trend components.
Calculation: Average dollar turnover = SMA(T1 close times T1 volume, liquidity lookback), calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Total coverage = for equities, weighted relative-strength, earnings, and trend coverage; for other modes, weighted relative-strength and trend coverage. Use separate weights for each mode and component.
Calculation: Confidence = clamp(the weighted mean of total coverage and history score to the unit interval), using separate weights for coverage and history.
Calculation: Composite score = clamp the weighted mean of available relative-strength, earnings, and trend scores to the unit interval, normalized by the sum of their active weights. Use the separate configured weights for each score; omit relative strength when its score is unavailable, its coverage is below its minimum, or its required benchmark is unavailable; include earnings only for equities when earnings data is available; include trend when trend coverage is positive; omit earnings when a required benchmark is missing and strict benchmark checking is enabled. For index mode only, apply EMA with the index-smoothing lookback to the composite score.
Calculation: Score momentum = SMA(composite score minus composite score at the score-momentum lookback, score-momentum smoothing lookback).
Long: Full-history status AND total coverage >= minimum buy coverage AND (benchmark not required OR strict benchmark checking disabled OR benchmark exists) AND confidence >= minimum buy coverage AND (safety filter disabled OR (drawdown >= drawdown threshold AND close > SMA(close, long moving-average lookback) AND (equity mode implies average dollar turnover >= minimum turnover) AND (ETF mode implies no volume data OR average dollar turnover >= minimum turnover))) AND ((composite score >= good-buy threshold AND composite score[1] >= good-buy threshold) OR (composite score >= strong-buy threshold AND composite score[1] >= strong-buy threshold AND composite score > composite score[3] AND score momentum > zero AND close >= highest(close, price-breakout lookback)[1] AND relative-strength score available AND relative-strength coverage >= minimum relative-strength coverage AND relative-strength score >= strong relative-strength floor AND trend coverage > zero AND trend score >= strong trend floor AND confidence >= max(minimum buy coverage, strong confidence floor) AND total coverage >= max(minimum buy coverage, strong coverage floor) AND drawdown >= max(drawdown threshold, strong drawdown floor) AND (not equity mode OR (earnings score available AND earnings score >= strong earnings floor))))
Short: Composite score crosses below the sell threshold.
Exit: Long or short, composite score < good-buy threshold AND composite score[1] >= good-buy threshold AND minimum-history status AND total coverage >= minimum watch coverage
