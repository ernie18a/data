Calculation: adjusted ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), high - low if ATR is unavailable, minimum price increment)
Calculation: candle range = max(high - low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: body share = abs(close - open) / candle range
Calculation: lower-wick share = (min(open, close) - low) / candle range
Calculation: upper-wick share = (high - max(open, close)) / candle range
Calculation: close location = (close - low) / candle range
Calculation: volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or neutral volume ratio when the SMA is not positive
Calculation: normalized-high score(x, weak, strong) = zero if weak equals strong; otherwise clamp((x - weak) / (strong - weak) * 100, 0, 100)
Calculation: peak score(x, weak, ideal, excessive) = normalized-high score(x, weak, ideal) when x <= ideal; otherwise clamp((excessive - x) / max(excessive - ideal, normalization epsilon) * 100, 0, 100)
Calculation: sweep penetration(side) = max((prior range low - low) / adjusted ATR for long, or (high - prior range high) / adjusted ATR for short, 0), where prior range high and low are the highest high[1] and lowest low[1] over the reference lookback
Calculation: seed wick score(side) = normalized-high score(side's rejection-direction wick share, minimum wick share * wick-score floor factor, strong wick share)
Calculation: seed depth score(side) = peak score(sweep penetration(side), minimum sweep penetration, ideal sweep penetration, excessive sweep penetration)
Calculation: seed-bar volume score = normalized-high score(volume ratio, weak volume ratio, strong volume ratio)
Calculation: stop-run seed score(side) = clamp(seed wick score(side) * seed-wick weight + seed depth score(side) * seed-depth weight + seed-bar volume score * seed-volume weight, 0, 100)
Calculation: confirmation sweep quality(side) = clamp(seed wick score(side) * confirmation-wick weight + seed depth score(side) * confirmation-depth weight, 0, 100)
Calculation: directional reclaim score(side) = normalized-high score((close - swept reference) / adjusted ATR for long, or (swept reference - close) / adjusted ATR for short, weak reclaim distance, strong reclaim distance)
Calculation: confirmation body score = normalized-high score(body share, minimum body share * body-score floor factor, strong body share)
Calculation: confirmation close-location score(side) = normalized-high score(close location for long, or 1 - close location for short, minimum close location - close-location score offset, strong close location score)
Calculation: follow-through score(side) = normalized-high score((close - high[1]) / adjusted ATR for long, or (low[1] - close) / adjusted ATR for short, weak follow-through, strong follow-through)
Calculation: directional candle score(side) = favorable-candle score when the candle closes in the reversal direction, otherwise unfavorable-candle score
Calculation: confirmation candle score(side) = clamp(confirmation body score * body weight + follow-through score(side) * follow-through weight + directional candle score(side) * directional-candle weight + confirmation close-location score(side) * close-location weight, 0, 100)
Calculation: target-room score(side) = normalized-high score(target room in ATR units, weak target room, strong target room); target is the captured opposite prior-range boundary if it lies beyond close in the reversal direction, otherwise close plus or minus adjusted ATR * target-room ATR fallback
Calculation: trend-turn score(side) = clamp(the applicable points for close above or below EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), EMA slope in the reversal direction using its value at [1], and shorter EMA above or below EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), 0, 100)
Calculation: reversal readiness score(side) = clamp(confirmation sweep quality(side) * sweep weight + directional reclaim score(side) * reclaim weight + confirmation candle score(side) * confirmation weight + seed-bar volume score * volume weight + target-room score(side) * target-room weight + trend-turn score(side) * trend weight, 0, 100)
Calculation: seed staging score threshold = max(seed-score floor, watch score - seed-score discount)
Long: on an eligible chart bar (bar-close confirmed when close confirmation is enabled), prior range high > prior range low, sweep penetration(long) >= minimum sweep penetration, lower-wick share >= minimum wick share, stop-run seed score(long) >= seed staging score threshold, no pending long candidate exists or its saved sweep low is unavailable or current low is below that saved low or its age exceeds the confirmation window, and stop-run seed score(long) >= watch score
Short: on an eligible chart bar (bar-close confirmed when close confirmation is enabled), the bullish seed branch is ineligible, prior range high > prior range low, sweep penetration(short) >= minimum sweep penetration, upper-wick share >= minimum wick share, stop-run seed score(short) >= seed staging score threshold, no pending short candidate exists or its saved sweep high is unavailable or current high is above that saved high or its age exceeds the confirmation window, and stop-run seed score(short) >= watch score
Long: on an eligible chart bar, an active pending long candidate from a downside sweep has age <= the confirmation window and seed score >= seed staging score threshold, close >= its swept reference + adjusted ATR * reclaim padding, close location >= minimum close location, body share >= minimum body share * confirmation-body factor, no prior confirmed plan exists or the plan cooldown has elapsed, and reversal readiness score(long) >= minimum readiness score
Short: on an eligible chart bar, an active pending short candidate from an upside sweep has age <= the confirmation window and seed score >= seed staging score threshold, close <= its swept reference - adjusted ATR * reclaim padding, close location <= 1 - minimum close location, body share >= minimum body share * confirmation-body factor, no prior confirmed plan exists or the plan cooldown has elapsed, and reversal readiness score(short) >= minimum readiness score
Exit: long, an unresolved active bullish plan is within its forward-render bar horizon and an eligible chart bar closes below the sweep low saved by that plan minus adjusted ATR * invalidation buffer
Exit: short, an unresolved active bearish plan is within its forward-render bar horizon and an eligible chart bar closes above the sweep high saved by that plan plus adjusted ATR * invalidation buffer
