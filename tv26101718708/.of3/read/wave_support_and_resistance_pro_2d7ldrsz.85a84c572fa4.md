Long: close crosses above a resistance level OR, on chart bars, close crosses above high(T1, last completed bar) OR, on chart bars, close crosses above high(T2, last completed bar) OR a new confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high (or is the first confirmed pivot high) OR a new confirmed pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low (or is the first confirmed pivot low) OR close first crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high before a new pivot high forms; pivot highs and lows use the same swing lookback parameter on both sides.
Short: close crosses below a support level OR, on chart bars, close crosses below low(T1, last completed bar) OR, on chart bars, close crosses below low(T2, last completed bar) OR a new confirmed pivot high is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a new confirmed pivot low is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed pivot low OR close first crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low before a new pivot low forms; pivot highs and lows use the same swing lookback parameter on both sides.
