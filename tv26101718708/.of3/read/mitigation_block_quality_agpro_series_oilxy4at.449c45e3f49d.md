Calculation: Bullish order block = full high-low range of the nearest bearish candle at a confirmed pivot low or among the swing-length bars before it; pivotlow(low, swing length, swing length) confirms the pivot.
Calculation: Bearish order block = full high-low range of the nearest bullish candle at a confirmed pivot high or among the swing-length bars before it; pivothigh(high, swing length, swing length) confirms the pivot.
Long: On the first eligible chart bar at least the reaction lookback after the first overlap of a tracked, uninvalidated bullish order block (low <= block high AND high >= block low), (max(block high, highest(high, reaction lookback)[1]) - block high) / ATR(ATR lookback) >= Grade A threshold × sensitivity multiplier; the block is uninvalidated if no intervening bar closes below its low in body-close mode or wicks below its low in wick-break mode. Evaluate on confirmed bars, or intrabar in live-updating mode.
Short: On the first eligible chart bar at least the reaction lookback after the first overlap of a tracked, uninvalidated bearish order block (high >= block low AND low <= block high), (block low - min(block low, lowest(low, reaction lookback)[1])) / ATR(ATR lookback) >= Grade A threshold × sensitivity multiplier; the block is uninvalidated if no intervening bar closes above its high in body-close mode or wicks above its high in wick-break mode. Evaluate on confirmed bars, or intrabar in live-updating mode.
