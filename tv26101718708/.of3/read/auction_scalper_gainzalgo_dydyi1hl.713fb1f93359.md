Calculation: Previous-period Volume Profile levels = on intraday chart bars, distribute each bar’s volume equally across the price bins it spans in the last completed T1 period; POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, and the value area expands from the POC toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until it covers the parameter percentage of total volume; VAH and VAL are its outer boundaries; hold these levels through the current T1 period
Calculation: Previous-period high/low = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars in the last completed T1 period, held through the current T1 period
Calculation: Bar range = max(high − low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Estimated volume imbalance = (volume × (close − low) / bar range − volume × (high − close) / bar range) / volume when volume is positive, otherwise zero
Calculation: Volume percentile rank = PercentRank(volume, rank lookback) on chart bars, using the neutral-rank parameter when unavailable
Calculation: ATR percentile rank = PercentRank(ATR(ATR lookback), the same rank lookback) on chart bars, using the neutral-rank parameter when unavailable
Calculation: Usable volume = volume is available and the maximum volume over the availability lookback is positive
Calculation: T2 bias = compare the last completed T2 close with EMA(close, EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar), sampled onto chart bars; bullish when close is above EMA, bearish when below, neutral when equal or unavailable
Long: T2 bias is bullish
Short: T2 bias is bearish
Long: Estimated volume imbalance ≥ pressure threshold
Short: Estimated volume imbalance ≤ −pressure threshold
Calculation: Outside-value streaks = consecutive chart-bar closes below prior VAL or above prior VAH through the previous bar, reset on an in-area close or T1 period change; saved sweep low/high is the minimum low/maximum high of the latest such streak and persists until another streak
Calculation: Accepted-break state = reset at a T1 period change; set bullish when prior VAH is not already in an active bullish state and close > prior VAH, close > open, and abs(close − open) ≥ acceptance body fraction × bar range; set bearish symmetrically below prior VAL; save the broken level and breakout bar; clear the corresponding retest arm if price closes beyond the broken level against the breakout by the retest tolerance × ATR, after the retest window, or after a retest signal; an opposite accepted break replaces the state and arm
Long: Previous-period VAH exists, no bullish accepted-break state was active before this bar, close > prior VAH, close > open, and abs(close − open) ≥ acceptance body fraction × bar range
Short: Previous-period VAL exists, no bearish accepted-break state was active before this bar, close < prior VAL, close < open, and abs(close − open) ≥ acceptance body fraction × bar range
Calculation: Value-edge crossing count = number of chart bars in the chop lookback on which the sign of close minus prior VAH or prior VAL differs from the previous bar’s sign
Calculation: Chop condition = accepted-break state is neutral and value-edge crossing count ≥ chop-crossing threshold
Long: Bullish accepted-break state is active, previous-period profile exists, chart bars are intraday, session filter is disabled or the bar is in session and outside the opening lockout, and Chop condition is false
Short: Bearish accepted-break state is active, previous-period profile exists, chart bars are intraday, session filter is disabled or the bar is in session and outside the opening lockout, and Chop condition is false
Calculation: Failed-auction long setup = not a T1 period-change bar and either low < prior VAL and close > prior VAL with no prior outside-value streak, or close crosses above prior VAL after a positive prior-below-VAL streak no greater than the failed-auction maximum
Calculation: Failed-auction short setup = not a T1 period-change bar and either high > prior VAH and close < prior VAH with no prior outside-value streak, or close crosses below prior VAH after a positive prior-above-VAH streak no greater than the failed-auction maximum
Calculation: Accepted-break long retest = bullish state and retest arm active, current bar is after the breakout and within the retest window, low ≤ saved breakout level + retest tolerance × ATR, and close > saved breakout level
Calculation: Accepted-break short retest = bearish state and retest arm active, current bar is after the breakout and within the retest window, high ≥ saved breakout level − retest tolerance × ATR, and close < saved breakout level
Calculation: Session score = no-session score when the session filter is disabled; otherwise use the parameter score bands for elapsed minutes since session open
Calculation: Long swing low = for a failed-auction setup, the minimum of current low and saved sweep low, using current low if no sweep low exists; for a retest setup, the minimum of current low and previous low, using current low if unavailable
Calculation: Short swing high = for a failed-auction setup, the maximum of current high and saved sweep high, using current high if no sweep high exists; for a retest setup, the maximum of current high and previous high, using current high if unavailable
Calculation: Long stop and risk = with entry equal to close, use swing low + inside-tick parameter × minimum tick size in saver mode, otherwise swing low − stop-buffer parameter × ATR; if stop ≥ entry, replace it with swing low − stop-buffer parameter × ATR, then with entry − ATR if still ≥ entry; in classic mode, if entry − stop < minimum-stop parameter × ATR, replace stop with entry − minimum-stop parameter × ATR; risk = entry − stop
Calculation: Short stop and risk = with entry equal to close, use swing high − inside-tick parameter × minimum tick size in saver mode, otherwise swing high + stop-buffer parameter × ATR; if stop ≤ entry, replace it with swing high + stop-buffer parameter × ATR, then with entry + ATR if still ≤ entry; in classic mode, if stop − entry < minimum-stop parameter × ATR, replace stop with entry + minimum-stop parameter × ATR; risk = stop − entry
Calculation: Long targets = for a failed-auction setup, TP1 is prior POC if it exceeds entry by the target-clearance parameter × risk, otherwise entry + fallback-target parameter × risk; TP2 is prior VAH if it exceeds TP1 by the target-clearance parameter × risk, otherwise TP1 + risk; for a retest setup, TP1 is prior-period high if it exceeds entry by the prior-extreme clearance parameter × risk, otherwise entry + fallback-target parameter × risk; TP2 is entry + runner parameter × risk, replaced by TP1 + runner-gap parameter × risk if TP2 ≤ TP1
Calculation: Short targets = for a failed-auction setup, TP1 is prior POC if it is below entry by the target-clearance parameter × risk, otherwise entry − fallback-target parameter × risk; TP2 is prior VAL if it is below TP1 by the target-clearance parameter × risk, otherwise TP1 − risk; for a retest setup, TP1 is prior-period low if it is below entry by the prior-extreme clearance parameter × risk, otherwise entry − fallback-target parameter × risk; TP2 is entry − runner parameter × risk, replaced by TP1 − runner-gap parameter × risk if TP2 ≥ TP1
Calculation: Directional score component = clamp(score midpoint + estimated volume imbalance × dominance multiplier) for long, or clamp(score midpoint − estimated volume imbalance × dominance multiplier) for short
Calculation: Rejection score = for failed-auction long, clamp(close-position weight × (close − low) / bar range × percentage scale + sweep weight × clamp(sweep base + sweep scale × (prior VAL − long swing low) / ATR)); for failed-auction short use (high − close) / bar range and (short swing high − prior VAH); for retest long, use clamp(close-position weight × (close − low) / bar range × percentage scale + retest-precision weight × (percentage scale × (1 − min(1, abs(low − breakout level) / max(retest tolerance × ATR, minimum tick size)))); for retest short use (high − close) / bar range and high in the precision term
Calculation: Location score = for failed-auction long, clamp(score maximum − (close − prior VAL) / ATR × location multiplier); for failed-auction short, clamp(score maximum − (prior VAH − close) / ATR × location multiplier); for either retest, clamp(score maximum − abs(close − breakout level) / ATR × location multiplier)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe score = aligned-direction score when T2 bias agrees with the position side, neutral score when T2 bias is neutral, and opposed-direction score otherwise
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp(ATR percentile rank × low-rank multiplier) below the low-rank threshold; maximum score through the high-rank threshold; above it, clamp(maximum score − (ATR percentile rank − high-rank threshold) × high-rank deduction)
Calculation: Reward-risk score = clamp((directional distance from entry to TP1 / risk) × reward-risk multiplier)
Calculation: Setup score = participation weight × volume percentile rank + dominance weight × directional score component + rejection weight × rejection score + location weight × location score + higher-timeframe weight × higher-timeframe score + volatility weight × volatility score + reward-risk weight × reward-risk score + session weight × session score
Calculation: Required setup score = effective minimum-score parameter + chop score addition when Chop condition is true + countertrend reversal addition when a failed-auction setup opposes T2 bias
Calculation: Entry eligibility = chart bars are intraday, current bar is confirmed, previous-period VAH, VAL, and POC exist, session filter is disabled or the bar is in session and elapsed minutes since open are at least the opening-lockout parameter and no greater than the entry-cutoff parameter, no trade is active, and more than the cooldown parameter chart bars have elapsed since the latest signal, stop, or TP2 event
Long: Entry eligibility; long direction cap is disabled or no long signal has occurred in the current T1 period; close > open; participation gate is disabled or volume is unusable or (volume percentile rank ≥ effective participation-percentile parameter AND estimated volume imbalance > long imbalance threshold); failed-auction long setup; long risk ≤ maximum-stop parameter × ATR; and failed-auction long setup score ≥ required setup score
Long: Entry eligibility; long direction cap is disabled or no long signal has occurred in the current T1 period; close > open; participation gate is disabled or volume is unusable or (volume percentile rank ≥ effective participation-percentile parameter AND estimated volume imbalance > long imbalance threshold); failed-auction long setup is false; accepted-break long retest; Chop condition is false; long risk ≤ maximum-stop parameter × ATR; and retest long setup score ≥ required setup score
Short: Entry eligibility; short direction cap is disabled or no short signal has occurred in the current T1 period; close < open; participation gate is disabled or volume is unusable or (volume percentile rank ≥ effective participation-percentile parameter AND estimated volume imbalance < −short imbalance threshold); failed-auction short setup; short risk ≤ maximum-stop parameter × ATR; and failed-auction short setup score ≥ required setup score
Short: Entry eligibility; short direction cap is disabled or no short signal has occurred in the current T1 period; close < open; participation gate is disabled or volume is unusable or (volume percentile rank ≥ effective participation-percentile parameter AND estimated volume imbalance < −short imbalance threshold); failed-auction short setup is false; accepted-break short retest; Chop condition is false; short risk ≤ maximum-stop parameter × ATR; and retest short setup score ≥ required setup score
Exit: long, current bar is confirmed and after the entry bar, and low ≤ long stop
Exit: long, current bar is confirmed and after the entry bar, TP1 has not previously been reached, low > long stop, and high ≥ long TP1
Exit: long, current bar is confirmed and after the entry bar, TP2 has not previously been reached, TP1 was previously reached or is reached on this bar, low > long stop, and high ≥ long TP2
Exit: short, current bar is confirmed and after the entry bar, and high ≥ short stop
Exit: short, current bar is confirmed and after the entry bar, TP1 has not previously been reached, high < short stop, and low ≤ short TP1
Exit: short, current bar is confirmed and after the entry bar, TP2 has not previously been reached, TP1 was previously reached or is reached on this bar, high < short stop, and low ≤ short TP2
