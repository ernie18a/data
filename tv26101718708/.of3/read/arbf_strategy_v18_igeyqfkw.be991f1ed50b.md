Calculation: Asia range high = highest high of chart bars timestamped within the configured Asia session
Calculation: Asia range low = lowest low of chart bars timestamped within the configured Asia session
Calculation: London opening range high = highest high of chart bars timestamped within the configured opening range interval
Calculation: London opening range low = lowest low of chart bars timestamped within the configured opening range interval
Calculation: Asia range gate passes = range gate disabled OR Asia range size is within the selected profile's separately configured minimum and maximum, measured in price units or as a percentage of close according to the profile
Long: The first confirmed chart bar close above Asia range high after the Asia session completes, during the configured bias scan window, while the Asia range gate passes
Short: The first confirmed chart bar close below Asia range low after the Asia session completes, during the configured bias scan window, while the Asia range gate passes
Calculation: HTF alignment at bias = for a long bias, each enabled filter passes when that timeframe's last completed bar close is above EMA(close, shared lookback, T1 shorter higher timeframe, last completed bar) or EMA(close, shared lookback, T2 longer higher timeframe, last completed bar); for a short bias, each enabled filter passes when the corresponding close is below its EMA; evaluate on chart bars using the last completed bars of T1 and T2
Calculation: Entry filters pass = chart bar time is on or after the configured backtest start and (end-date filter disabled or on or before the configured end) AND (weekday filter disabled or the weekday in its configured timezone is an enabled Monday-to-Friday day) AND (entry-time filter disabled or the time in its configured timezone is within its configured interval, including overnight wrap when the start is later than the end)
Long: A-mode entry is enabled AND the current confirmed bar establishes the long bias AND its time in the configured signal timezone is at or before the configured A-entry cutoff AND Entry filters pass AND long entries are enabled AND the position is flat AND (one-trade-per-Asia-session limit is disabled or no trade has occurred in the current Asia session) AND no A-mode entry has fired this session AND initial risk is greater than zero
Short: A-mode entry is enabled AND the current confirmed bar establishes the short bias AND its time in the configured signal timezone is at or before the configured A-entry cutoff AND Entry filters pass AND short entries are enabled AND the position is flat AND (one-trade-per-Asia-session limit is disabled or no trade has occurred in the current Asia session) AND no A-mode entry has fired this session AND initial risk is greater than zero
Long: B-mode entry is enabled AND the London opening range is complete AND the confirmed chart bar is within the configured maximum bars after its completion and before the configured exit hour AND the current bias is long and HTF alignment at bias passed AND close > London opening range high AND Entry filters pass AND long entries are enabled AND the position is flat AND (one-trade-per-Asia-session limit is disabled or no trade has occurred in the current Asia session) AND no B-mode entry has fired this session AND no A-mode entry was taken on this bar AND initial risk is greater than zero
Short: B-mode entry is enabled AND the London opening range is complete AND the confirmed chart bar is within the configured maximum bars after its completion and before the configured exit hour AND the current bias is short and HTF alignment at bias passed AND close < London opening range low AND Entry filters pass AND short entries are enabled AND the position is flat AND (one-trade-per-Asia-session limit is disabled or no trade has occurred in the current Asia session) AND no B-mode entry has fired this session AND no A-mode entry was taken on this bar AND initial risk is greater than zero
Calculation: Entry price = close of the entry signal bar
Calculation: Mode A initial stop = for long, Asia range low or entry price - ATR(break ATR lookback) * break ATR multiple according to the selected stop method; for short, Asia range high or entry price + ATR(break ATR lookback) * break ATR multiple according to the selected stop method
Calculation: Mode B initial stop = for long, London opening range low - configured profile-specific ABLC stop buffer; for short, London opening range high + configured profile-specific ABLC stop buffer; each profile has a separate buffer parameter
Calculation: Initial risk = absolute value of entry price - initial stop
Calculation: Single target = for long, entry price + initial risk * single-target R multiple; for short, entry price - initial risk * single-target R multiple
Calculation: TP1 = for long, entry price + initial risk * TP1 R multiple; for short, entry price - initial risk * TP1 R multiple
Calculation: TP2 = for long, entry price + initial risk * TP2 R multiple; for short, entry price - initial risk * TP2 R multiple
Calculation: TP3 = for long, entry price + initial risk * TP3 R multiple; for short, entry price - initial risk * TP3 R multiple
Calculation: TP1 reached = remains true after any long bar high >= TP1 or short bar low <= TP1
Calculation: TP2 reached = remains true after any long bar high >= TP2 or short bar low <= TP2
Calculation: Long single-mode stop = initial stop until single-mode break-even is enabled and a bar high has reached entry price + initial risk * single break-even trigger multiple; thereafter entry price
Calculation: Short single-mode stop = initial stop until single-mode break-even is enabled and a bar low has reached entry price - initial risk * single break-even trigger multiple; thereafter entry price
Calculation: Long scaled stop = start at initial stop; after TP1 is reached, retain the current stop for Keep Initial, use the higher of current stop and entry price for Break Even, or the higher of current stop and TP1 for Lock TP1; then after TP2 is reached, retain the current stop for Keep Current, use the higher of current stop and entry price for Break Even, the higher of current stop and TP1 for Lock TP1, or the higher of current stop and TP2 for Lock TP2
Calculation: Short scaled stop = start at initial stop; after TP1 is reached, retain the current stop for Keep Initial, use the lower of current stop and entry price for Break Even, or the lower of current stop and TP1 for Lock TP1; then after TP2 is reached, retain the current stop for Keep Current, use the lower of current stop and entry price for Break Even, the lower of current stop and TP1 for Lock TP1, or the lower of current stop and TP2 for Lock TP2
Calculation: Trailing distance = ATR(trailing ATR lookback) * trailing ATR multiple or initial risk * trailing R distance according to the selected trailing-distance method
Calculation: Long runner stop = long scaled stop until trailing starts after the configured TP1 or TP2 trigger; thereafter the higher of long scaled stop and the trailing stop, where the trailing stop starts at high - trailing distance and ratchets to the highest of its prior value and high - trailing distance
Calculation: Short runner stop = short scaled stop until trailing starts after the configured TP1 or TP2 trigger; thereafter the lower of short scaled stop and the trailing stop, where the trailing stop starts at low + trailing distance and ratchets to the lowest of its prior value and low + trailing distance
Exit: Long, single-target mode AND (low <= long single-mode stop OR high >= single target)
Exit: Short, single-target mode AND (high >= short single-mode stop OR low <= single target)
Exit: Long, scaled-target mode AND (low <= long scaled stop OR high >= TP1 OR high >= TP2 OR (fixed TP3 mode AND high >= TP3) OR (trailing runner mode AND low <= long runner stop))
Exit: Short, scaled-target mode AND (high >= short scaled stop OR low <= TP1 OR low <= TP2 OR (fixed TP3 mode AND low <= TP3) OR (trailing runner mode AND high >= short runner stop))
Exit: Long, time exit enabled AND chart-bar time enters the configured exit hour in the configured exit timezone
Exit: Short, time exit enabled AND chart-bar time enters the configured exit hour in the configured exit timezone
Exit: Long, end-date filter enabled AND chart-bar time is after the configured backtest end
Exit: Short, end-date filter enabled AND chart-bar time is after the configured backtest end
