Calculation: Bullish OB = On T1 bars, when close crosses above the latest available Pivot high(high, swing length, swing length) and close − open > displacement parameter × ATR(ATR length), use the nearest prior bearish candle within the OB-lookback bars as the stored zone [low, high] and mark it active, if such a candle exists; otherwise retain the stored zone and state. Deactivate it when close < its low. Chart bars use lookahead-off T1 values: the still-forming T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically.
Calculation: Bearish OB = On T1 bars, when close crosses below the latest available Pivot low(low, swing length, swing length) and open − close > displacement parameter × ATR(ATR length), use the nearest prior bullish candle within the OB-lookback bars as the stored zone [low, high] and mark it active, if such a candle exists; otherwise retain the stored zone and state. Deactivate it when close > its high. Chart bars use lookahead-off T1 values: the still-forming T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically.
Long: An active Bullish OB exists AND (no active Bearish OB exists OR |T1 close − Bullish OB low| ≤ |T1 close − Bearish OB high|)
Short: An active Bearish OB exists AND (no active Bullish OB exists OR |T1 close − Bullish OB low| > |T1 close − Bearish OB high|)
