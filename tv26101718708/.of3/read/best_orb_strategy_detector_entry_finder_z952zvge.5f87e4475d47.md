Calculation: ORBHigh(T1) = highest high from the selected session open until T1 elapses; fix when T1 completes.
Calculation: ORBLow(T1) = lowest low from the selected session open until T1 elapses; fix when T1 completes.
Calculation: ActiveORBRange = ORBHigh − ORBLow for the longest enabled completed opening range.
Calculation: BreakoutFilters(side) = volume filter disabled or its configured mode passes (increasing: volume > volume[1]; above average: volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier or × strong-volume multiplier; both: increasing and above average); AND trend filter disabled or its configured directional test passes (close vs VWAP(close), EMA(close, trend EMA lookback), EMA(close, separate custom EMA lookback), SuperTrend direction, VWAP AND EMA, or VWAP AND SuperTrend); AND FVG filter disabled or a stored same-direction FVG is within the configured proximity of the corresponding ORB boundary. A bullish FVG has low > high[2]; a bearish FVG has high < low[2]. Store gaps only when FVG collection is enabled, retaining the configured maximum count. Evaluate volume, trend, and FVG on chart bars.
Calculation: QualifiedUpBreakout = breakout detection enabled AND selected session active AND active opening range complete AND upward breakout not already active AND BreakoutFilters(long) AND, on a confirmed chart bar, either (body-close mode: current T1 close > ORBHigh(T1) × (1 + breakout buffer %) and previous T1 close ≤ that threshold) or (wick mode: current T1 high > that threshold and previous T1 high ≤ it). When chart timeframe < T1, evaluate on chart bars as T1 closes using the last completed T1 bar and its predecessor; otherwise use the current and previous confirmed chart bars. Rearm after an early return into the buffered range before minimum outside-bar commitment within the failed-break window, a stop hit, a new session, or a valid retest in cycle-tracking mode.
Calculation: QualifiedDownBreakout = breakout detection enabled AND selected session active AND active opening range complete AND downward breakout not already active AND BreakoutFilters(short) AND, on a confirmed chart bar, either (body-close mode: current T1 close < ORBLow(T1) × (1 − breakout buffer %) and previous T1 close ≥ that threshold) or (wick mode: current T1 low < that threshold and previous T1 low ≥ it). When chart timeframe < T1, evaluate on chart bars as T1 closes using the last completed T1 bar and its predecessor; otherwise use the current and previous confirmed chart bars. Rearm after an early return into the buffered range before minimum outside-bar commitment within the failed-break window, a stop hit, a new session, or a valid retest in cycle-tracking mode.
Long: QualifiedUpBreakout
Short: QualifiedDownBreakout
Long: QualifiedUpBreakout AND breakout entry mode enabled AND (pullback filter disabled OR, after breakout, low reaches the running highest high minus the configured pullback percentage and a later close ≥ that running high, OR the configured pullback timeout elapses)
Short: QualifiedDownBreakout AND breakout entry mode enabled AND (pullback filter disabled OR, after breakout, high reaches the running lowest low plus the configured pullback percentage and a later close ≤ that running low, OR the configured pullback timeout elapses)
Calculation: ValidUpRetest = after an upward breakout, before another upward retest, on a subsequent confirmed chart bar below the configured chart-timeframe limit: at least the configured minimum number of post-breakout chart bars through the current bar close above the breakout ORB high; a prior post-breakout close > breakout ORB high × (1 + minimum retest distance %); and current close > breakout ORB high, < breakout ORB high + (current active ORB high × retest buffer %), and > breakout ORB low − (current active ORB high × retest buffer %).
Calculation: ValidDownRetest = after a downward breakout, before another downward retest, on a subsequent confirmed chart bar below the configured chart-timeframe limit: at least the configured minimum number of post-breakout chart bars through the current bar close below the breakout ORB low; a prior post-breakout close < breakout ORB low × (1 − minimum retest distance %); and current close < breakout ORB low, > breakout ORB low − (current active ORB high × retest buffer %), and < breakout ORB high + (current active ORB high × retest buffer %).
Long: ValidUpRetest AND retest labels enabled AND (cycle tracking disabled OR retest count < maximum cycles)
Short: ValidDownRetest AND retest labels enabled AND (cycle tracking disabled OR retest count < maximum cycles)
Long: ValidUpRetest AND retest entry mode enabled AND, within the rejection wait limit after the retest, a confirmed T1 close > breakout ORB high × (1 + retest buffer %); when chart timeframe < T1, use the last completed T1 bar and evaluate on chart bars as T1 closes, otherwise use the confirmed chart bar. Rejection wait limit = maximum of the pullback timeout and rejection-timeout floor.
Short: ValidDownRetest AND retest entry mode enabled AND, within the rejection wait limit after the retest, a confirmed T1 close < breakout ORB low × (1 − retest buffer %); when chart timeframe < T1, use the last completed T1 bar and evaluate on chart bars as T1 closes, otherwise use the confirmed chart bar. Rejection wait limit = maximum of the pullback timeout and rejection-timeout floor.
Calculation: ORBStop(side) = long ? ORBLow − ActiveORBRange × stop buffer % : ORBHigh + ActiveORBRange × stop buffer %.
Calculation: RiskAdjustment(entry) = first adjustment factor when entry < first price threshold; second adjustment factor when entry ≥ first threshold and < second threshold; third adjustment factor otherwise.
Calculation: Target(side, n) = long ? entry + min(n × ActiveORBRange, n × abs(entry − ORBStop(side)) × RiskAdjustment(entry)) : entry − min(n × ActiveORBRange, n × abs(entry − ORBStop(side)) × RiskAdjustment(entry)); n is each configured target multiple.
Exit: Long, targets and stops enabled AND on a confirmed chart bar at or after entry, low ≤ ORBStop(long)
Exit: Short, targets and stops enabled AND on a confirmed chart bar at or after entry, high ≥ ORBStop(short)
Exit: Long, targets and stops enabled AND on a confirmed chart bar at or after entry, the highest enabled target is reached (high ≥ Target(long, n)) AND the stop is not reached on that bar
Exit: Short, targets and stops enabled AND on a confirmed chart bar at or after entry, the highest enabled target is reached (low ≤ Target(short, n)) AND the stop is not reached on that bar
