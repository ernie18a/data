Calculation: Relative strength = (T1 stock close / T1 stock close RS lookback chart bars ago) / (T1 index close / T1 index close RS lookback chart bars ago) - 1, using current still-forming T1 closes read on chart bars
Calculation: Long stop = setup candle low at entry from flat; max(previous long stop, setup candle low) at scale-in; while long, replace it with current low when the RSI exit-setup condition occurs and current low is higher than the previous stop; otherwise retain it
Long: RSI(close, RSI length) on the still-forming T3 bar, calculated on T3 bars and read on chart bars, is unavailable or above the RSI threshold, AND ((RSI on the still-forming T1 bar crosses above the RSI threshold on chart bars AND RSI on the still-forming T2 bar is above the RSI threshold) OR (RSI on the still-forming T2 bar crosses above the RSI threshold on chart bars AND RSI on the still-forming T1 bar is above the RSI threshold)) AND relative strength > relative strength threshold AND (index filter disabled OR current still-forming T1 index close > EMA(T1 index close, index EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and read on chart bars)
Long: within the confirmation window after a setup bar, on a chart bar that is not a new setup bar, current high > setup bar high AND ((flat) OR (long after a partial exit and before a scale-in)) AND ((current high - setup bar low) / current high * 100 <= maximum initial stop percent)
Exit: long, RSI on the still-forming T1 bar < support level OR (RSI < RSI threshold AND relative strength < relative strength threshold AND (RSI < support level OR RSI > support level + support buffer)), AND (long stop absent OR current low > long stop)
Exit: long, close the partial exit percentage when RSI on the still-forming T2 bar >= weekly profit RSI limit AND current close < lowest(current low one chart bar ago, profit lookback) AND no partial exit has already been taken since the last entry or scale-in
Exit: long, current low <= long stop * (1 - crash buffer percent / 100)
Exit: long, after a chart bar closes below long stop and sets the confirmation low, a subsequent chart bar reaches or falls below that low while the confirmation is pending; cancel the pending confirmation if a chart bar closes above long stop
