Calculation: Local ATR-deviation ZigZag = track the leg’s extreme high or low; while tracking highs, confirm a low pivot when low < tracked high − ATR(lookback) × local reversal multiple; while tracking lows, confirm a high pivot when high > tracked low + ATR(lookback) × local reversal multiple; require reversal size ≥ ATR(lookback) × local minimum-swing multiple unless that multiple is zero; when spike filtering is enabled and body > 0, reject a high pivot if its upper wick ≥ wick/body threshold × body and reject a low pivot if its lower wick ≥ wick/body threshold × body, where body = abs(close − open).
Calculation: T1 ATR-deviation ZigZag = aggregate chart bars into T1 bars and process each completed T1 bar on the first chart bar of the next T1 bucket; track leg extremes and confirm reversals using T1 high/low and ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar) × the separate T1 reversal multiple; require swing size ≥ that ATR × the separate T1 minimum-swing multiple unless that multiple is zero; the forming leg tracks current chart-bar extremes, with direction up while tracking highs and down while tracking lows.
Long: A confirmed local low-to-high ZigZag swing AND the direction filter allows uptrends AND (the intraday time-window filter is off, the chart is not intraday, or the swing endpoint time is inside the selected session when the mode tests the endpoint, or both swing endpoint times are inside the selected session when the mode tests the full cycle) AND (HTF context is off OR there are no completed T1 swings OR a completed T1 swing containing the local swing is upward OR no completed T1 swing contains the local swing and the forming T1 leg is upward).
Short: A confirmed local high-to-low ZigZag swing AND the direction filter allows downtrends AND (the intraday time-window filter is off, the chart is not intraday, or the swing endpoint time is inside the selected session when the mode tests the endpoint, or both swing endpoint times are inside the selected session when the mode tests the full cycle) AND (HTF context is off OR there are no completed T1 swings OR a completed T1 swing containing the local swing is downward OR no completed T1 swing contains the local swing and the forming T1 leg is downward).
Long: A confirmed upward T1 swing or an initialized forming T1 leg tracking highs.
Short: A confirmed downward T1 swing or an initialized forming T1 leg tracking lows.
Long: An accepted local uptrend has a following downtrend swing AND (HTF retracement gate is off OR both swings are contained in the same completed T1 swing, or neither is contained in a completed T1 swing) AND (the intraday time-window filter is off, the chart is not intraday, or the primary swing passes its endpoint/full-cycle time test and the countertrend endpoint is inside the session when the mode tests endpoints, or both endpoints of both swings are inside the session when the mode tests full cycles).
Short: An accepted local downtrend has a following uptrend swing AND (HTF retracement gate is off OR both swings are contained in the same completed T1 swing, or neither is contained in a completed T1 swing) AND (the intraday time-window filter is off, the chart is not intraday, or the primary swing passes its endpoint/full-cycle time test and the countertrend endpoint is inside the session when the mode tests endpoints, or both endpoints of both swings are inside the session when the mode tests full cycles).
