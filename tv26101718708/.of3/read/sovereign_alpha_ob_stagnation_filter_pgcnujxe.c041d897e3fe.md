Calculation: Momentum Z = (close - close[1] - SMA(close - close[1], lookback)) / standard deviation(close - close[1], same lookback)
Calculation: Bullish OB = on a bullish MSB, bounds from the nearest bearish candle at offset 1 through the OB search lookback, or the current bar if none; top = high[offset], bottom = low[offset]; mitigate when low < bottom; if overlap hiding is enabled, omit it when its top or bottom lies within an unmitigated OB's inclusive bounds
Calculation: Bearish OB = on a bearish MSB, bounds from the nearest bullish candle at offset 1 through the OB search lookback, or the current bar if none; top = high[offset], bottom = low[offset]; mitigate when high > top; if overlap hiding is enabled, omit it when its top or bottom lies within an unmitigated OB's inclusive bounds
Long: close > the latest confirmed, unconsumed pivot high AND close[1] <= that pivot high AND Momentum Z > the MSB momentum threshold
Short: close < the latest confirmed, unconsumed pivot low AND close[1] >= that pivot low AND Momentum Z < negative MSB momentum threshold
Long: flat AND the first OB in creation order that is unmitigated, unused, passes its directional macro filter, and meets its directional retest condition is bullish; its retest condition is low <= top AND close > bottom, and its macro filter is disabled OR every required alignment passes: on chart bars, the still-forming T1 close > EMA(close, T1 lookback, T1, still-forming bar), the still-forming T2 close > EMA(close, T2 lookback, T2, still-forming bar), and the still-forming T3 close > EMA(close, T3 lookback, T3, still-forming bar)
Short: flat AND the first OB in creation order that is unmitigated, unused, passes its directional macro filter, and meets its directional retest condition is bearish; its retest condition is high >= bottom AND close < top, and its macro filter is disabled OR every required alignment passes: on chart bars, the still-forming T1 close < EMA(close, T1 lookback, T1, still-forming bar), the still-forming T2 close < EMA(close, T2 lookback, T2, still-forming bar), and the still-forming T3 close < EMA(close, T3 lookback, T3, still-forming bar)
Long: flat AND long re-entry is armed by a long stagnation exit, has not been invalidated by close < tracked OB bottom or reset by a primary entry, AND close > tracked OB top
Short: flat AND short re-entry is armed by a short stagnation exit, has not been invalidated by close > tracked OB top or reset by a primary entry, AND close < tracked OB bottom
Calculation: Long trailing stop = unset until close >= entry price + break-even trigger; set to entry price on the first qualifying bar, then on each qualifying bar set to max(previous trailing stop, close - ATR(lookback) * multiplier); otherwise retain its previous value
Calculation: Short trailing stop = unset until close <= entry price - break-even trigger; set to entry price on the first qualifying bar, then on each qualifying bar set to min(previous trailing stop, close + ATR(lookback) * multiplier); otherwise retain its previous value
Exit: long, low <= (trailing stop unset ? entry price - hard-stop distance : max(entry price - hard-stop distance, long trailing stop)) OR high >= entry price + target distance OR stagnation exit enabled AND chart bars since entry signal >= linger bars AND tracked OB bottom <= close <= tracked OB top
Exit: short, high >= (trailing stop unset ? entry price + hard-stop distance : min(entry price + hard-stop distance, short trailing stop)) OR low <= entry price - target distance OR stagnation exit enabled AND chart bars since entry signal >= linger bars AND tracked OB bottom <= close <= tracked OB top
