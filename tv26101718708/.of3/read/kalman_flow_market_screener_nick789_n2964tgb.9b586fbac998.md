Calculation: Structure impulse on T1 = +1 when close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high, -1 when close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low, otherwise 0; pivot highs and lows use the pivot lookback on both sides and become confirmed after that many right-side bars.
Calculation: Kalman Flow basis on T1 = calculate fast and slow Kalman filters of OHLC4, each initialized at the source with error 1, process noise equal to response divided by its respective fast or slow Kalman length, and shared measurement noise; each bar, gain=(prior error+process noise)/(prior error+process noise+measurement noise), estimate=prior estimate+gain*(source-prior estimate), and error=(1-gain)*(prior error+process noise). Let activity=RMA(abs(structure impulse), shared structure lookback), bias=RMA(structure impulse, shared structure lookback), adaptation=min(1, activity*structure weight/max(structure weight, epsilon)), alpha=2/(longer baseline smoothing lookback+1)+(2/(shorter baseline smoothing lookback+1)-2/(longer baseline smoothing lookback+1))*adaptation, and agreement=1 when bias>0 and fast estimate>slow estimate or bias<0 and fast estimate<slow estimate, otherwise the disagreement coefficient. Start the basis at the fast estimate; thereafter form a candidate from prior basis+alpha*(fast estimate-prior basis)+push coefficient*bias*agreement*ATR, then, when both latest confirmed pivots exist, add anchor coefficient*activity*(pivot midpoint-candidate).
Calculation: Kalman Flow state on T1 = upper boundary=basis+ATR*boundary multiplier and lower boundary=basis-ATR*boundary multiplier; initially bullish when close>=basis, otherwise bearish; thereafter change to bullish when close>upper boundary and slope confirmation is disabled or basis>prior basis, change to bearish when close<lower boundary and slope confirmation is disabled or basis<prior basis, otherwise retain the prior direction. Trail is the lower boundary in bullish state and upper boundary in bearish state. Compute on T1 bars and merge values onto chart bars; T1 is the scan timeframe, falling back to chart timeframe when the selected scan timeframe is lower. Use the last completed T1 bar when scan-candle-close confirmation is enabled, otherwise the T1 bar still forming.
Calculation: T2 Kalman Flow direction = Kalman Flow state on the selected filter timeframe, using its last completed bar when scan-candle-close confirmation is enabled and its bar still forming otherwise; compute on T2 bars and merge values onto chart bars. T2 is the selected filter timeframe, floored at T1.
Calculation: Alert block = scan data is stale when chart time minus scan-bar time exceeds the stale-bar parameter times the T1 duration; or the enabled killzone-end guard is within the maximum of its extra buffer and one T1 duration of an enabled session end; or the enabled market-close guard applies to a non-24/7 instrument and New York time is between the configured close minus the maximum of the close buffer and one T1 duration, and the configured after-close buffer.
