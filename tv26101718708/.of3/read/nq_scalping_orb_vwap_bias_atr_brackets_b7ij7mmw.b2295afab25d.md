Calculation: daily entry count = number of submitted long and short entries since the current day began
Calculation: bars since entry = current chart-bar index minus entry chart-bar index while a position is open; 0 while flat
Long: (session filter disabled OR current time is within the configured trade session) AND daily entry count < daily entry limit AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (VWAP bias disabled OR close > session VWAP(hlc3)) AND opening range is complete AND close > highest(high during the parameterized interval beginning at 9:30 a.m. New York time)
Short: (session filter disabled OR current time is within the configured trade session) AND daily entry count < daily entry limit AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (VWAP bias disabled OR close < session VWAP(hlc3)) AND opening range is complete AND close < lowest(low during the parameterized interval beginning at 9:30 a.m. New York time)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop parameter * ATR(ATR lookback) OR high >= entry price + target parameter * ATR(ATR lookback) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry >= bar-count parameter) OR (end-of-session flattening enabled AND session filter enabled AND current time is outside the configured trade session)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop parameter * ATR(ATR lookback) OR low <= entry price - target parameter * ATR(ATR lookback) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry >= bar-count parameter) OR (end-of-session flattening enabled AND session filter enabled AND current time is outside the configured trade session)
