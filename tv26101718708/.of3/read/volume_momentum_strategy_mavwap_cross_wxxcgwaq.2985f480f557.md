Short: Mark when the moving average selected from SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, and HMA crosses below session VWAP within the inclusive trading window; submit a short entry only when short trading is enabled and the position is flat
Exit: Long, close-all time of day is reached OR (stop loss is enabled AND ((stop-loss type is percentage AND low <= entry-signal-bar close * (1 - stop-loss percentage)) OR (stop-loss type is dollar amount AND low <= entry-signal-bar close - stop-loss amount))) OR (take profit is enabled AND ((take-profit type is percentage AND high >= entry-signal-bar close * (1 + take-profit percentage)) OR (take-profit type is dollar amount AND high >= entry-signal-bar close + take-profit amount)))
Exit: Short, close-all time of day is reached OR (stop loss is enabled AND ((stop-loss type is percentage AND high >= entry-signal-bar close * (1 + stop-loss percentage)) OR (stop-loss type is dollar amount AND high >= entry-signal-bar close + stop-loss amount))) OR (take profit is enabled AND ((take-profit type is percentage AND low <= entry-signal-bar close * (1 - take-profit percentage)) OR (take-profit type is dollar amount AND low <= entry-signal-bar close - take-profit amount)))
