Calculation: Macro regime = On each chart bar, use the close and EMA(close, EMA lookback) from the last completed T1 bar, and the EMA from the T1 bar before it; bullish when close > EMA and EMA > prior EMA, bearish when close < EMA and EMA < prior EMA, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Support = greatest of the most recent confirmed chart-bar Pivot Low(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), low from the last completed T2 bar, low from the last completed T3 bar, close from the last completed T3 bar, chart-bar VWAP(hlc3), and chart-bar EMA(close, EMA lookback) that is below close; ignore missing candidates.
Calculation: Resistance = least of the most recent confirmed chart-bar Pivot High(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), high from the last completed T2 bar, high from the last completed T3 bar, close from the last completed T3 bar, chart-bar VWAP(hlc3), and chart-bar EMA(close, EMA lookback) that is above close; ignore missing candidates.
Calculation: ATR zone width = ATR(ATR lookback) * zone ATR multiplier.
Calculation: Near support = support exists AND low <= support + ATR zone width AND close >= support - ATR zone width.
Calculation: Near resistance = resistance exists AND high >= resistance - ATR zone width AND close <= resistance + ATR zone width.
Calculation: Confluence proximity = abs(close - chart-bar VWAP(hlc3)) <= ATR zone width OR abs(close - chart-bar EMA(close, EMA lookback)) <= ATR zone width.
Calculation: Body fraction = if high > low, abs(close - open) / (high - low); otherwise zero.
Calculation: Long breakout = resistance[1] exists AND close > resistance[1] AND close[1] <= resistance[1].
Calculation: Short breakout = support[1] exists AND close < support[1] AND close[1] >= support[1].
Calculation: MACD histogram rising = chart-bar MACD histogram > its value on the previous chart bar, using MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback).
Calculation: MACD histogram falling = chart-bar MACD histogram < its value on the previous chart bar, using MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback).
Calculation: Long trigger = (long breakout OR (near support AND close > high[1] AND close > open)) AND body fraction >= minimum body fraction AND MACD histogram rising AND positive DI > negative DI from chart-bar DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing).
Calculation: Short trigger = (short breakout OR (near resistance AND close < low[1] AND close < open)) AND body fraction >= minimum body fraction AND MACD histogram falling AND negative DI > positive DI from chart-bar DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing).
Calculation: Long candidate risk = close - (min(low, support if present, otherwise low) - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop buffer).
Calculation: Short candidate risk = (max(high, resistance if present, otherwise high) + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop buffer) - close.
Calculation: Long room qualified = long candidate risk > minimum price increment AND (resistance exists ? resistance - close : ATR(ATR lookback) * target R multiple) >= long candidate risk * minimum structural room multiple.
Calculation: Short room qualified = short candidate risk > minimum price increment AND (support exists ? close - support : ATR(ATR lookback) * target R multiple) >= short candidate risk * minimum structural room multiple.
Calculation: Long score = (macro regime bullish ? higher score weight : macro regime neutral ? lower score weight : no points) + (near support OR long breakout ? higher score weight : no points) + (confluence proximity ? lower score weight : no points) + (RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI midpoint ? lower score weight : no points) + (MACD histogram rising AND MACD line > MACD signal line ? lower score weight : no points) + (positive DI > negative DI AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND ADX > prior chart-bar ADX from DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) ? lower score weight : no points) + (long trigger ? lower score weight : no points) + (long room qualified ? lower score weight : no points).
Calculation: Short score = (macro regime bearish ? higher score weight : macro regime neutral ? lower score weight : no points) + (near resistance OR short breakout ? higher score weight : no points) + (confluence proximity ? lower score weight : no points) + (RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint ? lower score weight : no points) + (MACD histogram falling AND MACD line < MACD signal line ? lower score weight : no points) + (negative DI > positive DI AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND ADX > prior chart-bar ADX from DMI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) ? lower score weight : no points) + (short trigger ? lower score weight : no points) + (short room qualified ? lower score weight : no points).
Calculation: Entry price = close on the signal bar.
Calculation: Long stop = at long entry, entry price - long candidate risk on the signal bar; store this level.
Calculation: Short stop = at short entry, entry price + short candidate risk on the signal bar; store this level.
Calculation: Long target = at long entry, if resistance exists above entry price then min(entry price + long candidate risk on the signal bar * target R multiple, resistance at entry), otherwise entry price + long candidate risk on the signal bar * target R multiple; store this level.
Calculation: Short target = at short entry, if support exists below entry price then max(entry price - short candidate risk on the signal bar * target R multiple, support at entry), otherwise entry price - short candidate risk on the signal bar * target R multiple; store this level.
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND within the configured session in America/New_York AND no position is open AND no signal bar exists yet or current chart-bar index - last signal chart-bar index > cooldown bars AND long trigger AND long room qualified AND long score >= minimum signal score AND NOT short trigger.
Short: chart bar is confirmed AND within the configured session in America/New_York AND no position is open AND no signal bar exists yet or current chart-bar index - last signal chart-bar index > cooldown bars AND short trigger AND short room qualified AND short score >= minimum signal score AND NOT long trigger.
Exit: long, chart bar is confirmed AND (outside the configured session in America/New_York OR current chart-bar index - entry-bar index >= maximum bars in position OR (current chart-bar index > entry-bar index AND (low <= stored long stop OR high >= stored long target OR (close < entry price AND MACD histogram falling)))).
Exit: short, chart bar is confirmed AND (outside the configured session in America/New_York OR current chart-bar index - entry-bar index >= maximum bars in position OR (current chart-bar index > entry-bar index AND (high >= stored short stop OR low <= stored short target OR (close > entry price AND MACD histogram rising)))).
