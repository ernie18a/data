Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative(((high + low + close) / 3) × volume) / cumulative(volume), reset at each T1 anchor and accumulated on chart bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(max(0, cumulative(volume × ((high + low + close) / 3)^2) / cumulative(volume) − Anchored VWAP^2)) since the T1 anchor reset; use zero when the variance is not positive.
Calculation: Band 2 upper/lower = Anchored VWAP ± Anchored VWAP standard deviation × Band 2 multiplier × (the separate Low, Medium, or High regime scale parameter when band adaptation is enabled and the volatility regime is trusted; otherwise 1).
Calculation: Cross buffer = Anchored VWAP standard deviation × buffer parameter.
Calculation: Ordered clusters = initialize Low, Medium, and High centroids evenly from the sample minimum to maximum; assign each sample to its nearest centroid, breaking ties toward the lower centroid; replace each nonempty centroid with its assigned mean and retain empty centroids; repeat for the iteration parameter using the trailing clustering lookback, refitting every recalculation interval.
Calculation: Volatility regime and confidence = apply Ordered clusters to ATR with its separate length parameter; use the same clustering lookback, iteration parameter, and recalculation interval as volume clustering; classify current ATR on each chart bar by nearest centroid, with ascending centroids labeled Low, Medium, and High; confidence is 100 when the second-nearest distance is zero, otherwise 100 × (1 − nearest-centroid distance / second-nearest-centroid distance); the regime is trusted only when clustering is enabled, the lookback is full, and confidence meets the confidence threshold.
Calculation: Volume class = apply Ordered clusters to volume using the same clustering lookback, iteration parameter, and recalculation interval as volatility clustering; classify current volume on each chart bar by nearest centroid, with ascending centroids labeled Low, Normal, and High.
Long: ((selected style is mean reversion OR selected style is Auto and the volatility regime is untrusted, Low, or Medium) AND close crosses above Band 2 lower + Cross buffer OR (selected style is breakout OR selected style is Auto and the trusted volatility regime is High) AND close crosses above Band 2 upper + Cross buffer) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume clustering is not ready OR Volume class is Normal or High) AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the current chart bar is confirmed) AND (no prior signal OR chart bars since the last signal are at least the cooldown parameter).
Short: ((selected style is mean reversion OR selected style is Auto and the volatility regime is untrusted, Low, or Medium) AND close crosses below Band 2 upper − Cross buffer OR (selected style is breakout OR selected style is Auto and the trusted volatility regime is High) AND close crosses below Band 2 lower − Cross buffer) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume clustering is not ready OR Volume class is Normal or High) AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the current chart bar is confirmed) AND (no prior signal OR chart bars since the last signal are at least the cooldown parameter).
