Calculation: Curve state = on chart bars, use the current T1 bar in real time and the latest completed T1 bar historically, requested with lookahead off; position = clamp((chart close − highest(high, curve lookback, T1))/max(highest(high, curve lookback, T1) − lowest(low, curve lookback, T1), minimum price increment), 0, 1); state is upper when position is at least the upper boundary, lower when at most the lower boundary, otherwise middle.
Calculation: Trend state = on chart bars, use the current T2 bar in real time and the latest completed T2 bar historically, requested with lookahead off; up when EMA(close, fast lookback, T2) > EMA(close, slow lookback, T2) and the fast EMA is rising, down when the fast EMA is below the slow EMA and falling, otherwise neutral; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback.
Calculation: Value bands = VWAP(hlc3) ± standard deviation(close − VWAP(hlc3), value lookback) × band width.
Calculation: Participation = volume ≥ SMA(volume, participation lookback) × mode-adjusted participation multiplier; the multiplier is raised in strict mode and lowered in early mode.
Calculation: Initial range = the highest high and lowest low during the configured opening session, reset at each session-day boundary and usable after that session ends. Accepted upper break = close above initial range high for the mode-adjusted holding-bar requirement and participation; accepted lower break = close below initial range low for the same requirement and participation.
Calculation: Failed lower-value reclaim = VWAP bands enabled AND low below the lower value band AND close above it AND (close − low)/max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ rejection-close threshold AND participation. Failed upper-value rejection = VWAP bands enabled AND high above the upper value band AND close below it AND (high − close)/max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ rejection-close threshold AND participation.
Calculation: Demand and supply zones = chart-source, regular, or Heikin Ashi candles are scanned for compressed bases followed by directional departures. A candidate requires ATR-relative limits on base candle ranges, cluster range, body span, total body size and body-midpoint drift; incoming direction is determined from preceding candle closes and bull/bear counts. Candidate bounds use the base decision candles or the applicable reversal-origin or hidden-pair geometry. The departure must pass direction, clean-leave, excursion or strong-close, impulse, structure-break and math-score tests; volume and footprint/delta proxies contribute when enabled. Overlapping candidates are deduplicated or replaced by the score, width, reversal and age rules. Zone state tracks touches, consumption, breaks, reactions and optional age expiry. The active zone is selected by visibility tier and truth/rank score, with distance, width, proximity, reversal and recency affecting rank. A zone is fresh when selected and within its configured age limit, or when historical retention or age expiry is off.
Calculation: Near demand = a fresh demand zone exists AND close is between its bottom minus ATR × near-zone distance and its top plus that distance. Near supply is the corresponding condition for a fresh supply zone.
Calculation: Demand sweep = a fresh demand zone exists AND low is below its bottom AND close is above its top AND the close meets the bullish rejection-close threshold AND participation. Supply sweep is the inverse.
Calculation: Long auction = demand sweep OR failed lower-value reclaim OR accepted upper break. Short auction = supply sweep OR failed upper-value rejection OR accepted lower break. Long auction allowed = demand sweep OR accepted upper break OR (failed lower-value reclaim AND (matching-zone filter disabled OR near demand)); short auction allowed is the inverse.
Calculation: Long and short scores = cap at the score ceiling the sum of zone strength points capped at the strength ceiling, zone time points, freshness points, trend points, curve points, profit points and rejection points. Strength points are highest when zone math score meets the high-quality threshold and volume confirmation passes, middle when it meets the continuation or reversal threshold, otherwise lowest. Time points favor shorter bases. Freshness awards more for fewer zone tests. Trend points favor the matching trend, with partial points when neutral. Curve points favor lower curve for long and upper curve for short, with partial points in the middle. Profit points increase at the high reward-risk threshold and again at the minimum reward-risk threshold. Rejection points are the clipped excess of zone rejection score above its threshold. Reaction points are zero. The score threshold is raised in strict mode, lowered in early mode and unchanged otherwise.
Calculation: Long risk = max(close − stop, minimum price increment), where stop is fresh demand bottom − ATR × stop buffer, or low − ATR × (one plus stop buffer) without fresh demand. Short risk = max(stop − close, minimum price increment), where stop is fresh supply top + ATR × stop buffer, or high + ATR × (one plus stop buffer) without fresh supply.
Calculation: Long target = the higher of (VWAP when enabled and above close, otherwise close + long risk × minimum reward-risk) and (fresh supply bottom when above close, otherwise highest high over the curve lookback on T1). Short target = the lower of (VWAP when enabled and below close, otherwise close − short risk × minimum reward-risk) and (fresh demand top when below close, otherwise lowest low over the curve lookback on T1). Long reward-risk = max(long target − close, 0)/long risk; short reward-risk = max(close − short target, 0)/short risk.
