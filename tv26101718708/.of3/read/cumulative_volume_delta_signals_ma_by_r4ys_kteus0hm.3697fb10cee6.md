Calculation: chart-bar delta = sum of T1-bar volume signed positive when close > open, negative when close < open, otherwise positive when close > prior T1 close, negative when close < prior T1 close, otherwise retaining the prior sign (initially positive); use zero when the chart bar has no T1 bars. Use available T1 bars, including a still-forming T1 bar, to calculate each chart bar’s delta.
Long: within the processing-bar limit AND chart-bar delta >= 0 AND (abs(chart-bar delta) > SMA(abs(chart-bar delta), delta-average lookback) * delta-threshold percentage / 100 OR abs(chart-bar delta) > SMA(abs(chart-bar delta), delta-average lookback) * extreme-threshold percentage / 100).
Short: within the processing-bar limit AND chart-bar delta < 0 AND (abs(chart-bar delta) > SMA(abs(chart-bar delta), delta-average lookback) * delta-threshold percentage / 100 OR abs(chart-bar delta) > SMA(abs(chart-bar delta), delta-average lookback) * extreme-threshold percentage / 100).
