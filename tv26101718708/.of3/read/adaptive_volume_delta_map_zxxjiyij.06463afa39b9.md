Calculation: Directional volume(T1 bar) = +volume if close > open, −volume if close < open, and ±volume by close versus previous close when open = close, retaining the previous side on ties (initially negative).
Calculation: Buy/sell volume per price row = allocate each T1 bar’s positive/absolute negative directional volume in proportion to the fraction of its high-low range overlapping the row; rows use width chart-bar range/(rows per bar − 1) in automatic mode or ticks per row × minimum tick otherwise.
Long: Sum of directional volume for T1 bars within the current chart bar, including a still-forming T1 bar, > 0 OR buy volume in a price row > 0 AND a lower adjacent row exists AND buy volume in that row >= absolute sell volume in the lower adjacent row × imbalance percentage/100.
Short: Sum of directional volume for T1 bars within the current chart bar, including a still-forming T1 bar, <= 0 OR absolute sell volume in a price row > 0 AND a higher adjacent row exists AND absolute sell volume in that row >= buy volume in the higher adjacent row × imbalance percentage/100.
