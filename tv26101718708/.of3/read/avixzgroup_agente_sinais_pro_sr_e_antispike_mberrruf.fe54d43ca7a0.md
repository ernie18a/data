Calculation: Resistance levels = T1 pivot highs with left and right widths equal to the pivot lookback, calculated on T1 bars and projected to chart bars with lookahead on, including the current forming T1 bar; admit each pivot when the zone-creation volume filter is disabled or volume at the pivot bar is at least SMA(volume, volume lookback) at the pivot bar times the volume multiplier; merge each admitted pivot into the first stored level within the grouping percentage of that level, keeping the higher level, otherwise append it; retain only the latest maximum-zone-count levels; when daily caching is enabled, use the snapshot refreshed at each calendar-day boundary.
Calculation: Support levels = T1 pivot lows with left and right widths equal to the pivot lookback, calculated on T1 bars and projected to chart bars with lookahead on, including the current forming T1 bar; admit each pivot when the zone-creation volume filter is disabled or volume at the pivot bar is at least SMA(volume, volume lookback) at the pivot bar times the volume multiplier; merge each admitted pivot into the first stored level within the grouping percentage of that level, keeping the lower level, otherwise append it; retain only the latest maximum-zone-count levels; when daily caching is enabled, use the snapshot refreshed at each calendar-day boundary.
Calculation: Active resistance = first stored resistance level above chart close.
Calculation: Active support = first stored support level below chart close.
Long: Active support exists AND low <= active support AND close >= active support * (1 - anti-spike percentage) AND (live-volume filter is disabled OR chart-bar volume >= SMA(volume, live volume lookback) on chart bars including the current bar * volume multiplier).
Short: Active resistance exists AND high >= active resistance AND close <= active resistance * (1 + anti-spike percentage) AND (live-volume filter is disabled OR chart-bar volume >= SMA(volume, live volume lookback) on chart bars including the current bar * volume multiplier).
