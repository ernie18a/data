Calculation: Bullish pullback zone = [BOS-bar high - (BOS-bar high - lowest(low, impulse lookback ending on the BOS bar)) * maximum retracement, BOS-bar high - (BOS-bar high - lowest(low, impulse lookback ending on the BOS bar)) * minimum retracement]; invalidation level = BOS-bar high - (BOS-bar high - lowest(low, impulse lookback ending on the BOS bar)) * cancellation retracement.
Calculation: Bearish pullback zone = [BOS-bar low + (highest(high, impulse lookback ending on the BOS bar) - BOS-bar low) * minimum retracement, BOS-bar low + (highest(high, impulse lookback ending on the BOS bar) - BOS-bar low) * maximum retracement]; invalidation level = BOS-bar low + (highest(high, impulse lookback ending on the BOS bar) - BOS-bar low) * cancellation retracement.
Calculation: Long initial stop = the lower of signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiple and lowest(low, swing lookback), capped to no more than ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum stop ATR multiple below signal-bar close when the cap is enabled.
Calculation: Short initial stop = the higher of signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop ATR multiple and highest(high, swing lookback), capped to no more than ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum stop ATR multiple above signal-bar close when the cap is enabled.
Calculation: Long initial risk = signal-bar close - long initial stop.
Calculation: Short initial risk = short initial stop - signal-bar close.
Calculation: Long protective stop = long initial stop until the first target is reached; thereafter, raise it to the highest active level among the initial stop, entry average price when break-even is enabled, and EMA(close, fast lookback) when EMA trailing is enabled.
Calculation: Short protective stop = short initial stop until the first target is reached; thereafter, lower it to the lowest active level among the initial stop, entry average price when break-even is enabled, and EMA(close, fast lookback) when EMA trailing is enabled.
Long: Position is flat AND no prior entry exists or more than the cooldown lookback chart bars have elapsed since the last entry AND the latest qualifying bullish BOS armed a still-active setup (no later qualifying bearish BOS, no entry since, and no low below its invalidation level); that BOS occurred when close crossed above the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot-left, pivot-right), higher-timeframe bias was bullish (on chart bars, using the last completed T1 bar's close above EMA(close, HTF bias lookback) calculated on T1 bars), the ATR expansion filter was disabled or ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback) was at least the minimum ATR ratio with the baseline greater than zero, and the BOS buffer filter was disabled or BOS-bar close exceeded the pivot high plus ATR(ATR lookback) * BOS buffer multiple; current bar range intersects the bullish pullback zone; if strong confirmation is enabled, close > EMA(close, fast lookback) AND close > high[1], otherwise close > open AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback); close is between EMA(close, fast lookback) and EMA(close, mid lookback); and the EMA filter is disabled or close > EMA(close, long-term filter lookback).
Short: Position is flat AND no prior entry exists or more than the cooldown lookback chart bars have elapsed since the last entry AND the latest qualifying bearish BOS armed a still-active setup (no later qualifying bullish BOS, no entry since, and no high above its invalidation level); that BOS occurred when close crossed below the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot-left, pivot-right), higher-timeframe bias was bearish (on chart bars, using the last completed T1 bar's close below EMA(close, HTF bias lookback) calculated on T1 bars), the ATR expansion filter was disabled or ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback) was at least the minimum ATR ratio with the baseline greater than zero, and the BOS buffer filter was disabled or BOS-bar close was below the pivot low minus ATR(ATR lookback) * BOS buffer multiple; current bar range intersects the bearish pullback zone; if strong confirmation is enabled, close < EMA(close, fast lookback) AND close < low[1], otherwise close < open AND close < EMA(close, fast lookback); close is between EMA(close, fast lookback) and EMA(close, mid lookback); and the EMA filter is disabled or close < EMA(close, long-term filter lookback).
Exit: Long, high >= signal-bar close + long initial risk * first-target R multiple (close the configured target fraction).
Exit: Long, low <= long protective stop OR high >= signal-bar close + long initial risk * second-target R multiple.
Exit: Short, low <= signal-bar close - short initial risk * first-target R multiple (close the configured target fraction).
Exit: Short, high >= short protective stop OR low <= signal-bar close - short initial risk * second-target R multiple.
