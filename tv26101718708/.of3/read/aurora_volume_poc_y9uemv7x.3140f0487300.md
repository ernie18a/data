Calculation: VolumeRatio = current selected volume (chart volume or external-reference tick volume at chart timeframe) / SMA(selected volume[1], volume lookback) over previous completed chart bars, when the SMA is positive
Calculation: BodyFraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 1 when high equals low
Calculation: LowerWickFraction = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low
Calculation: UpperWickFraction = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low
Calculation: T1Weight = raw T1 intrabar volume when chart volume is selected; otherwise raw T1 intrabar volume × selected chart-bar volume / sum of raw T1 intrabar volumes in that chart bar
Calculation: UpVol = on each chart bar, sum T1Weight for T1 intrabars with close > open, plus half T1Weight when close equals open; if T1 data are unavailable, use selected chart-bar volume when chart close > open, half when equal, otherwise zero
Calculation: DownVol = on each chart bar, sum T1Weight for T1 intrabars with close < open, plus half T1Weight when close equals open; if T1 data are unavailable, use selected chart-bar volume when chart close < open, half when equal, otherwise zero
