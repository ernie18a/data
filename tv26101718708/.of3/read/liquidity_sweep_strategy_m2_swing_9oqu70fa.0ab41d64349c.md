Calculation: Session levels = highest(high) and lowest(low) across chart bars in each configured Asia, London, and initial-balance session, available after the session ends
Calculation: Long structure level = in M2 mode, the high of the nearest prior chart bar within the M2 search lookback whose close is above its low + (high - low) * M2 midpoint fraction and with an intervening chart bar before the current swept-low extreme whose high is below that candidate bar's low; in swing mode, the latest confirmed pivot high(high, swing lookback, swing lookback)
Calculation: Short structure level = in M2 mode, the low of the nearest prior chart bar within the M2 search lookback whose close is below its low + (high - low) * M2 midpoint fraction and with an intervening chart bar before the current swept-high extreme whose low is above that candidate bar's high; in swing mode, the latest confirmed pivot low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback)
Long: While the corresponding session is inactive, low < its completed session low AND after the sweep each new lower low updates the swept-low extreme and current long structure level AND close > the current long structure level AND after that break the pullback extreme is the highest high since the break AND a later chart bar has low <= swept-low extreme + (pullback high - swept-low extreme) * entry retracement fraction AND no low since the break is below the swept-low extreme
Short: While the corresponding session is inactive, high > its completed session high AND after the sweep each new higher high updates the swept-high extreme and current short structure level AND close < the current short structure level AND after that break the pullback extreme is the lowest low since the break AND a later chart bar has high >= swept-high extreme - (swept-high extreme - pullback low) * entry retracement fraction AND no high since the break is above the swept-high extreme
