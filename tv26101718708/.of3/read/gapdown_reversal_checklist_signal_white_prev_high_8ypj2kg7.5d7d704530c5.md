Calculation: prior-high condition = close >= prior regular-session high * (1 - proximity percentage / 100) OR close > prior regular-session high
Calculation: constructive MA interaction = (close >= SMA(close, shorter MA lookback, T1 current bar forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars) * (1 - proximity percentage / 100) AND close <= SMA(close, shorter MA lookback, T1 current bar forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars) * (1 + proximity percentage / 100)) OR close > SMA(close, shorter MA lookback, T1 current bar forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars) OR (close >= SMA(close, longer MA lookback, T1 current bar forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars) * (1 - proximity percentage / 100) AND close <= SMA(close, longer MA lookback, T1 current bar forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars) * (1 + proximity percentage / 100)) OR close > SMA(close, longer MA lookback, T1 current bar forming, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: RSI-MA trend condition = RSI MA disabled OR prior chart-bar RSI-MA value unavailable OR current chart-bar RSI-MA value >= prior chart-bar RSI-MA value, where RSI MA is the selected MA type of RSI(close, RSI lookback) with its separate RSI-MA lookback
Calculation: technical reversal setup = during regular-session window AND close > VWAP(hlc3) on chart bars AND close > close on the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars AND opening-range break AND prior-high condition AND (MA filter disabled OR constructive MA interaction)
Calculation: checklist score = count of satisfied conditions: close > VWAP(hlc3) on chart bars; close > close on the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars; opening-range break; prior-high condition; constructive MA interaction; RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI limit; RSI-MA trend condition
Calculation: confirmed reversal condition = technical reversal setup AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI limit AND RSI-MA trend condition
