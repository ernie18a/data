Calculation: EqualLevelTolerance = ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) when tolerance mode is ATR; otherwise tick tolerance × instrument minimum tick
Calculation: HTFSweepDirection(lookback,T) = on T bars, initially neutral and retaining the prior direction until a sweep; low < Lowest(low,lookback)[1] AND close > Lowest(low,lookback)[1] sets bullish, then high > Highest(high,lookback)[1] AND close < Highest(high,lookback)[1] sets bearish (bearish wins if both); return values to chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming T bar in realtime and the last completed T bar historically
Long: (an unswept level among the most recent max-levels-per-side equal-low levels exists, where each level is the minimum of two consecutive confirmed PivotLow(low,pivot-left lookback,pivot-right lookback) values whose absolute difference is ≤ EqualLevelTolerance, AND low < that level AND close > that level) OR HTFSweepDirection(sweep lookback,T1) = bullish OR HTFSweepDirection(sweep lookback,T2) = bullish OR HTFSweepDirection(sweep lookback,T3) = bullish
Short: (an unswept level among the most recent max-levels-per-side equal-high levels exists, where each level is the maximum of two consecutive confirmed PivotHigh(high,pivot-left lookback,pivot-right lookback) values whose absolute difference is ≤ EqualLevelTolerance, AND high > that level AND close < that level) OR HTFSweepDirection(sweep lookback,T1) = bearish OR HTFSweepDirection(sweep lookback,T2) = bearish OR HTFSweepDirection(sweep lookback,T3) = bearish
