Calculation: normalized Heikin-Ashi trend = 100 × (M(Heikin-Ashi close, MA lookback) − M(Heikin-Ashi open, MA lookback)) / (M(Heikin-Ashi high, MA lookback) − M(Heikin-Ashi low, MA lookback)), using the same selected MA type and lookback for all terms; M is selected from ALMA, HMA, SMA, SWMA, VWMA, ZLEMA, or EMA, with WMA selection also computing VWMA, and ALMA using separate offset and deviation parameters
Long: no long position is open AND the last completed T1 bar has M(Heikin-Ashi close, MA lookback) > M(Heikin-Ashi open, MA lookback), computed on T1 bars, AND normalized Heikin-Ashi trend crosses above zero on the current chart timeframe
Exit: long, normalized Heikin-Ashi trend crosses below zero on the current chart timeframe OR (ATR stop enabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR(ATR lookback, current chart timeframe at entry) × stop multiplier)
