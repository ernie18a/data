Calculation: Prior high/low(T) = high/low of the last completed T bar, sampled onto chart bars
Long: For at least one enabled prior high or low from T1, T2, or T3, close crosses above that level AND close - that level > ATR(ATR lookback) * break sensitivity AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-surge multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR ZLEMA(close, trend lookback) > ZLEMA(close, trend lookback)[1])
Short: For at least one enabled prior high or low from T1, T2, or T3, close crosses below that level AND that level - close > ATR(ATR lookback) * break sensitivity AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-surge multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR ZLEMA(close, trend lookback) <= ZLEMA(close, trend lookback)[1])
Long: For at least one enabled prior low from T1, T2, or T3, low <= that level AND close > that level AND min(open, close) - low > abs(close - open) * rejection wick/body multiplier AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-surge multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR ZLEMA(close, trend lookback) > ZLEMA(close, trend lookback)[1])
Short: For at least one enabled prior high from T1, T2, or T3, high >= that level AND close < that level AND high - max(open, close) > abs(close - open) * rejection wick/body multiplier AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-surge multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR ZLEMA(close, trend lookback) <= ZLEMA(close, trend lookback)[1])
