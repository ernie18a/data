Calculation: qualifying score = sum of the six base conditions (close > EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > SMA(close, longer trend lookback); MACD histogram > 0 AND greater than its previous chart-bar value; RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= lower threshold AND <= upper threshold; close > SMA(close, band lookback); volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier; and bullish Supertrend(high, low, close, ATR lookback, factor)) plus the relative-strength condition if its filter is enabled, otherwise 1; the MACD fast lookback is shorter than its slow lookback, and the signal lookback is a separate parameter; relative strength compares (close / close[RS lookback] - 1) with (benchmark close / benchmark close[RS lookback] - 1), using benchmark closes on chart bars.
Calculation: qualification threshold = round(enabled check count * qualification fraction), where enabled check count is the six base checks plus one if the relative-strength filter is enabled.
Calculation: qualification signal = qualifying score == enabled check count OR (qualifying score >= qualification threshold AND qualifying score != enabled check count).
Calculation: entry trigger = qualification signal AND the MACD line crosses above its signal line on the current chart bar or within the parameterized number of preceding chart bars.
