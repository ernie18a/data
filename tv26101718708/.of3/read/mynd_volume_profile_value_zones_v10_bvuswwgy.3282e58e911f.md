Calculation: VAH and VAL = On each chart bar, build a rolling volume profile from the profile-lookback number of chart bars, including the current bar: divide the highest high to the lowest low into the number-of-bins equal-width price bins, assign each bar's full volume to the bin containing the selected price (close, typical price, or OHLC4), and sum volume by bin; the POC is the lowest-price bin with the greatest volume. Starting at the POC, expand the value area by adding the adjacent bin with greater volume (choose the lower bin on a tie) until included volume reaches the total profile volume times the value-area percentage; VAH is the upper edge and VAL the lower edge of the included bins.
Long: close > VAH OR close crosses above VAL OR (close crosses above VAH AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume-confirmation average lookback) * volume-confirmation multiplier)
Short: close < VAL OR close crosses below VAH OR (close crosses below VAL AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume-confirmation average lookback) * volume-confirmation multiplier)
