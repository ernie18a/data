Calculation: failures(L) = cumulative count of pending C1 setups at level L invalidated when the primary close crosses back below L or the paired close crosses back below M for a long setup, or the primary close crosses back above L or the paired close crosses back above M for a short setup
Long: on a chart bar with no pending setup at level L, primary close > L AND (M is absent OR paired close > M) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (SMA filter is disabled OR primary close > SMA(primary close, fast lookback) AND SMA(primary close, fast lookback) >= SMA(primary close, slow lookback) AND paired close > SMA(paired close, fast lookback) AND SMA(paired close, fast lookback) >= SMA(paired close, slow lookback))
Long: on a later chart bar while a long C1 is pending at level L, primary close > L AND (M is absent OR paired close > M) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND failures(L) < failure threshold
Short: on a chart bar with no pending setup at level L, primary close < L AND (M is absent OR paired close < M) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (SMA filter is disabled OR primary close < SMA(primary close, fast lookback) AND SMA(primary close, fast lookback) <= SMA(primary close, slow lookback) AND paired close < SMA(paired close, fast lookback) AND SMA(paired close, fast lookback) <= SMA(paired close, slow lookback))
Short: on a later chart bar while a short C1 is pending at level L, primary close < L AND (M is absent OR paired close < M) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND failures(L) < failure threshold
Exit: long, primary close < L after a long entry at L
Exit: short, primary close > L after a short entry at L
