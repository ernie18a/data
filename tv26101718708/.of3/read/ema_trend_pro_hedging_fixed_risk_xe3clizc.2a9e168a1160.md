Calculation: Risk distance = ATR(chart bars, risk ATR lookback) * stop-loss multiplier
Calculation: Round-trip fee rate = 2 * fee percentage / 100
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend state(T) = neutral when ADX(T bars, shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) < state ADX threshold; bullish when ADX is at least that threshold and SuperTrend(T bars, shared factor, shared ATR lookback) direction is bullish; bearish when ADX is at least that threshold and SuperTrend direction is bearish; calculate on T bars and return values to chart bars with lookahead off (completed T bars historically, still-forming T bar in real time); T1 is shorter than T2
Long: ((EMA(close, shortest lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longest lookback) OR EMA(close, next-shortest lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longest lookback)) AND EMA(close, shortest lookback) > EMA(close, next-shortest lookback) AND EMA(close, next-shortest lookback) > EMA(close, next-longest lookback) AND +DI(shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) > -DI(shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) AND ADX(shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) > entry ADX threshold AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within configured date range) AND position is not long) OR higher-timeframe trend state(T1) is bullish OR higher-timeframe trend state(T2) is bullish
Short: ((EMA(close, shortest lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longest lookback) OR EMA(close, next-shortest lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longest lookback)) AND EMA(close, shortest lookback) < EMA(close, next-shortest lookback) AND EMA(close, next-shortest lookback) < EMA(close, next-longest lookback) AND -DI(shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) > +DI(shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) AND ADX(shared DMI lookback, shared DMI smoothing) > entry ADX threshold AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within configured date range) AND position is not short) OR higher-timeframe trend state(T1) is bearish OR higher-timeframe trend state(T2) is bearish
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + risk distance * first profit-target multiple (lowest of the three target multiples) OR high >= entry price + risk distance * second profit-target multiple (middle of the three target multiples) OR high >= entry price + risk distance * third profit-target multiple (highest of the three target multiples) OR (first target not reached by this bar AND low <= entry price - risk distance) OR (first target reached on or before this bar AND low <= entry price * (1 + round-trip fee rate))
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - risk distance * first profit-target multiple (lowest of the three target multiples) OR low <= entry price - risk distance * second profit-target multiple (middle of the three target multiples) OR low <= entry price - risk distance * third profit-target multiple (highest of the three target multiples) OR (first target not reached by this bar AND high >= entry price + risk distance) OR (first target reached on or before this bar AND high >= entry price * (1 - round-trip fee rate))
