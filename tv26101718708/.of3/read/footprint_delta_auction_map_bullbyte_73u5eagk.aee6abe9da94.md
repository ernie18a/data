Calculation: Robust ATR = max(RMA(min(TrueRange, median(TrueRange, ATR outlier lookback) × ATR outlier cap multiplier), ATR lookback), close × minimum ATR percent / percent scale), on chart bars
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), on chart bars
Calculation: Unified Auction Pressure = select the current confirmed chart bar’s native footprint delta when the footprint engine is enabled and available; otherwise, if lower-timeframe reconstruction is enabled, available, and its requested T1 is strictly below the chart timeframe, select the sum over T1 sub-bars within the chart bar of ((subclose − sublow) / max(subhigh − sublow, minimum price increment) × subvolume) minus ((subhigh − subclose) / max(subhigh − sublow, minimum price increment) × subvolume); otherwise select ((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) − (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) × volume. For the selected source, divide its signed delta by the highest absolute delta over the pressure lookback, multiply by the pressure scale, and clamp to the configured lower and upper pressure bounds; normalize each source independently. Footprint row size and value-area percentage are parameters.
Calculation: Level Confluence Score and Hits = at close, set tolerance to close × level tolerance percent / percent scale; for each enabled level, proximity is zero when its distance from close exceeds tolerance, otherwise proximity maximum score − distance / tolerance. Use current-bar footprint POC, VAH, and VAL when available; otherwise use session midpoint, session high × upper session reference factor, and session low × lower session reference factor. Also use current-session high and low; prior-day high and low from the last completed T3 bar; prior-week high and low from the last completed T4 bar; and the nearest round number, round(close / round-number step) × round-number step. T3 and T4 values are mapped to chart bars. Prior-day, prior-week, and round-number levels are included only when enabled. Weight each proximity by its level weight, using separate footprint and fallback weights for POC and value-area levels, and the configured weights for session extremes, prior-day extremes, prior-week extremes, and round numbers. Score = sum(weight × proximity) / sum(included weights) × confluence score scale; Hits = count of included levels with positive proximity.
Calculation: Initial Balance Regime, Threshold Adjustment, and Extension Scalar = form the current session’s initial-balance high and low during the configured initial-balance window; compare its range with the median of completed historical session initial-balance ranges. Classify as Trend when the range exceeds the median × trend-width ratio, Balance when it is below the median × balance-width ratio, and Neutral otherwise. Threshold Adjustment is positive for Trend, negative for Balance, and zero for Neutral. Extension Scalar is the configured Trend, Balance, or Neutral scalar.
Calculation: Composite Score = min(composite score cap, composite auction weight × min(auction score cap, abs(Unified Auction Pressure) / pressure scale × pressure points + min(score component cap, Candle body ratio / body saturation ratio) × body points + min(score component cap, ((high − low) / Robust ATR) / range saturation ratio) × range points + tier imbalance points) + composite confluence weight × Level Confluence Score). For footprint or lower-timeframe reconstruction, tier imbalance points = abs(buy volume − sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume) × tier imbalance maximum, or tier imbalance maximum × zero-volume fraction when total volume is zero. For the single-bar OHLCV proxy, tier imbalance points = abs((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) − midpoint fraction) × midpoint score multiplier × tier imbalance maximum.
Calculation: Sizing Unit = determine ATR rank as (Robust ATR − lowest Robust ATR over the sizing-regime lookback) / (highest − lowest), or the configured default rank when highest equals lowest. If automatic sizing bands are enabled and available, use the configured lower and upper nearest-rank percentiles of Robust ATR as a percent of close over the sizing-band lookback; otherwise use the manual minimum and maximum sizing percentages. Ensure the upper percentage is at least the lower percentage plus the minimum band gap. Sizing Unit = close × (minimum percentage + ATR rank × (maximum percentage − minimum percentage)) / percent scale.
Calculation: Extension Multiplier = max(reaction multiplier + extension floor increment, base extension multiplier × Initial Balance Extension Scalar × clamp(Robust ATR / SMA(Robust ATR, volatility-regime lookback), volatility scalar minimum, volatility scalar maximum)) when dynamic extension sizing is enabled; otherwise max(reaction multiplier + extension floor increment, base extension multiplier).
Calculation: Extension Level and Invalidation Level = at entry, with entry price equal to the close of the bar that fires the signal, Extension Level = entry price plus the position-side sign × Extension Multiplier × Sizing Unit, and Invalidation Level = entry price minus the position-side sign × invalidation multiplier × Sizing Unit. Freeze both levels at entry; the position-side sign is positive for long and negative for short.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, no active scenario, cooldown expired, current session’s Initial Balance formed, at least the configured minimum number of completed historical Initial Balance samples available, and Sizing Unit available; abs(Unified Auction Pressure) ≥ minimum pressure, Candle body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio, Level Confluence Hits ≥ minimum hits, Composite Score ≥ base composite threshold + Initial Balance Threshold Adjustment, Unified Auction Pressure > 0, and close > open; if the HTF filter is enabled, EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, last completed bar) > EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, preceding completed bar), with the HTF values mapped to chart bars. If next-bar confirmation is disabled, these setup conditions must hold on this bar; if enabled, they must have held on the preceding chart bar and close > close[1] on this bar, with no active scenario and cooldown expired on this bar.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, no active scenario, cooldown expired, current session’s Initial Balance formed, at least the configured minimum number of completed historical Initial Balance samples available, and Sizing Unit available; abs(Unified Auction Pressure) ≥ minimum pressure, Candle body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio, Level Confluence Hits ≥ minimum hits, Composite Score ≥ base composite threshold + Initial Balance Threshold Adjustment, Unified Auction Pressure < 0, and close < open; if the HTF filter is enabled, EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, last completed bar) < EMA(close, HTF lookback, T2, preceding completed bar), with the HTF values mapped to chart bars. If next-bar confirmation is disabled, these setup conditions must hold on this bar; if enabled, they must have held on the preceding chart bar and close < close[1] on this bar, with no active scenario and cooldown expired on this bar.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed chart bar, (high ≥ Extension Level AND (low > Invalidation Level OR abs(close[1] − Extension Level) ≤ abs(close[1] − Invalidation Level))) OR (low ≤ Invalidation Level AND (high < Extension Level OR abs(close[1] − Invalidation Level) < abs(close[1] − Extension Level)))); after either exit, apply the configured cooldown in chart bars.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed chart bar, (low ≤ Extension Level AND (high < Invalidation Level OR abs(close[1] − Extension Level) ≤ abs(close[1] − Invalidation Level))) OR (high ≥ Invalidation Level AND (low > Extension Level OR abs(close[1] − Invalidation Level) < abs(close[1] − Extension Level)))); after either exit, apply the configured cooldown in chart bars.
