Calculation: Higher-high reversal = low > low[1] AND high > high[1] AND close > high[1]
Calculation: Lower-low reversal = low < low[1] AND high < high[1] AND close < low[1]
Calculation: Long stop = low[1] on the long-entry bar
Calculation: Long target = upper Bollinger band when high >= Bollinger basis on the long-entry bar, otherwise Bollinger basis on the long-entry bar
Calculation: Short stop = high[1] on the short-entry bar
Calculation: Short target = lower Bollinger band when low <= Bollinger basis on the short-entry bar, otherwise Bollinger basis on the short-entry bar
Long: On a completed chart bar, high[1] < Bollinger lower band[1] AND close > Bollinger lower band AND close < Bollinger upper band AND Higher-high reversal AND RSI(source, lookback) crosses above the oversold threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)
Short: On a completed chart bar, low[1] > Bollinger upper band[1] AND close < Bollinger upper band AND close > Bollinger lower band AND Lower-low reversal AND RSI(source, lookback) crosses below the overbought threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)
Exit: long, close >= Long target OR close <= Long stop OR end of day for markets that close
Exit: short, close <= Short target OR close >= Short stop OR end of day for markets that close
Calculation: clock value = hour(time(T1)) × 100 + minute(time(T1))
Long: confirmed bar AND (continuous-market setting OR clock value < cutoff time) AND previous high < previous lower Bollinger Band of the selected price series AND current close > current lower Bollinger Band AND current close < current upper Bollinger Band AND current low > previous low AND current high > previous high AND current close > previous high AND RSI of the selected price series crosses above the oversold threshold AND fast EMA of the selected price series < slow EMA of the selected price series
Short: confirmed bar AND (continuous-market setting OR clock value < cutoff time) AND previous low > previous upper Bollinger Band of the selected price series AND current close < current upper Bollinger Band AND current close > current lower Bollinger Band AND current low < previous low AND current high < previous high AND current close < previous low AND RSI of the selected price series crosses below the overbought threshold AND fast EMA of the selected price series > slow EMA of the selected price series
Exit: long, current close >= (if entry-bar high >= entry-bar Bollinger middle band then entry-bar Bollinger upper band else entry-bar Bollinger middle band) OR current close <= entry-bar previous low OR (not continuous-market setting AND clock value >= cutoff time)
Exit: short, current close <= (if entry-bar low <= entry-bar Bollinger middle band then entry-bar Bollinger lower band else entry-bar Bollinger middle band) OR current close >= entry-bar previous high OR (not continuous-market setting AND clock value >= cutoff time)
