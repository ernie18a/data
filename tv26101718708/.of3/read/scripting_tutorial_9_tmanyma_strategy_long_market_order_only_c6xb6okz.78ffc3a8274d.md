Calculation: Average A = the indicator selected by type parameter A (RMA, SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA)(source A, lookback A), calculated on A's selected timeframe (chart timeframe or T1) and sampled onto chart bars; for another timeframe, historical samples use its last completed bar and realtime samples use its still-forming bar
Calculation: Average B = the indicator selected by type parameter B (RMA, SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA)(source B, lookback B), calculated on B's selected timeframe (chart timeframe or T2) and sampled onto chart bars; for another timeframe, historical samples use its last completed bar and realtime samples use its still-forming bar
Calculation: Average C = the indicator selected by type parameter C (RMA, SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA)(source C, lookback C), calculated on C's selected timeframe (chart timeframe or T3) and sampled onto chart bars; for another timeframe, historical samples use its last completed bar and realtime samples use its still-forming bar
Long: (Average A crosses above Average B AND visibility parameters A and B are enabled AND (cross-display parameter or pair trade parameter AB is enabled)) OR (Average A crosses above Average C AND visibility parameters A and C are enabled AND (cross-display parameter or pair trade parameter AC is enabled)) OR (Average B crosses above Average C AND visibility parameters B and C are enabled AND (cross-display parameter or pair trade parameter BC is enabled))
Exit: long, (Average A crosses below Average B AND visibility parameters A and B are enabled AND (cross-display parameter or pair trade parameter AB is enabled)) OR (Average A crosses below Average C AND visibility parameters A and C are enabled AND (cross-display parameter or pair trade parameter AC is enabled)) OR (Average B crosses below Average C AND visibility parameters B and C are enabled AND (cross-display parameter or pair trade parameter BC is enabled))
