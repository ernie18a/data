Calculation: body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: candle range = high - low
Calculation: upper wick = high - max(close, open)
Calculation: lower wick = min(close, open) - low
Calculation: preceding decline = close[previous bar] < close[prior-move lookback bars ago]
Calculation: preceding rise = close[previous bar] > close[prior-move lookback bars ago]
Long: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) crosses above EMA(close, middle EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long-entry RSI ceiling
Short: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) crosses below EMA(close, middle EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short-entry RSI floor
Long: EMA(close, middle EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND low <= EMA(close, middle EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, middle EMA lookback) AND close > open AND more than pullback spacing parameter bars since the previous long pullback signal, or no previous signal
