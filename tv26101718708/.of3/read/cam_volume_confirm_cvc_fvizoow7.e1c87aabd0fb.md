Calculation: VolumeMetric = apply the selected smoothing method (none, SMA, EMA, RMA, HMA, or WMA; smoothing lookback) to RMA(clamp((A != 0 ? R / A : R) * scale limit, -scale limit, scale limit), rolling lookback), where R = (high != low ? (close - open) / (high - low) : 0) * ((volume > 0 ? volume^(1 - volume boost) : 0) * volume factor + (1 - volume factor)) and A = RMA(abs(R), scaling-days lookback converted to chart bars using chart bars per trading day); for time-based bars, chart bars per trading day is max(one, round(session duration / chart-bar duration)), otherwise use the fallback session-bar count.
Long: (VolumeMetric >= long volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= RSI midline) OR (VolumeMetric >= long volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midline AND VolumeMetric[1] < long volume threshold) OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above RSI midline AND VolumeMetric < long volume threshold)
Short: (VolumeMetric <= short volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midline) OR (VolumeMetric <= short volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= RSI midline AND VolumeMetric[1] > short volume threshold) OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below RSI midline AND VolumeMetric > short volume threshold)
Exit: long, NOT (VolumeMetric >= long volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= RSI midline) AND NOT (VolumeMetric <= short volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midline) AND VolumeMetric[1] >= long volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] >= RSI midline
Exit: short, NOT (VolumeMetric >= long volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= RSI midline) AND NOT (VolumeMetric <= short volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midline) AND VolumeMetric[1] <= short volume threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] < RSI midline
