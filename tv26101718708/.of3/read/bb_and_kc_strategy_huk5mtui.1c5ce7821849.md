Calculation: bars in trade = 1 at entry; previous value + 1 on each subsequent bar while a position is open
Long: Keltner Channel upper band(close, KC lookback, KC multiplier) crosses above Bollinger Bands upper band(close, BB lookback, BB standard-deviation parameter) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)
Short: Keltner Channel lower band(close, KC lookback, KC multiplier) crosses below Bollinger Bands lower band(close, BB lookback, BB standard-deviation parameter) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)
Exit: long, bars in trade > holding-period parameter
Exit: short, bars in trade > holding-period parameter
