Calculation: T1 long bias = T1 close > EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, current bar) AND that EMA > its value on the previous T1 bar AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, current bar) ≥ long RSI threshold; use T1 values on chart bars as returned with lookahead off (developing in real time, completed historically).
Calculation: T1 short bias = T1 close < EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, current bar) AND that EMA < its value on the previous T1 bar AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, current bar) ≤ short RSI threshold; use T1 values on chart bars as returned with lookahead off (developing in real time, completed historically).
Calculation: Long local trend = chart close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND current chart-bar EMA > previous chart-bar EMA.
Calculation: Short local trend = chart close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND current chart-bar EMA < previous chart-bar EMA.
Calculation: Long retracement zone for stored impulse low L and high H = [H − (H−L) × upper retracement fraction, H − (H−L) × lower retracement fraction]; midpoint = average of the bounds.
Calculation: Short retracement zone for stored impulse low L and high H = [L + (H−L) × lower retracement fraction, L + (H−L) × upper retracement fraction]; midpoint = average of the bounds.
Calculation: Long zone confirmation = locked setup is fresh through the current chart bar, its zone was touched within the confirmation-window chart bars, current low ≤ zone top AND current high ≥ zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint, close > open, close ≥ low + max(high−low, minimum tick) × close-strength fraction, abs(close−open) ≥ max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick) × minimum-body ATR multiple, AND (low < lowest low of the prior sweep-lookback chart bars OR min(open, close)−low > abs(close−open) × wick/body ratio).
Calculation: Short zone confirmation = locked setup is fresh through the current chart bar, its zone was touched within the confirmation-window chart bars, current high ≥ zone bottom AND current low ≤ zone top AND close < zone midpoint, close < open, close ≤ high − max(high−low, minimum tick) × close-strength fraction, abs(close−open) ≥ max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick) × minimum-body ATR multiple, AND (high > highest high of the prior sweep-lookback chart bars OR high−max(open, close) > abs(close−open) × wick/body ratio).
Calculation: Long score = count of true conditions: T1 long bias; long local trend; stored long impulse range ≥ current chart ATR(ATR lookback) × strong-impulse ATR multiple; long zone confirmation; and (volume SMA(volume, volume lookback) is available AND volume > that SMA × volume multiplier AND close > open).
Calculation: Short score = count of true conditions: T1 short bias; short local trend; stored short impulse range ≥ current chart ATR(ATR lookback) × strong-impulse ATR multiple; short zone confirmation; and (volume SMA(volume, volume lookback) is available AND volume > that SMA × volume multiplier AND close < open).
Calculation: Long stop, fixed on entry bar = lowest low over the stop lookback chart bars − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer ATR multiple; long target, fixed on entry bar = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × target R multiple.
Calculation: Short stop, fixed on entry bar = highest high over the stop lookback chart bars + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer ATR multiple; short target, fixed on entry bar = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × target R multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close while flat, a locked long setup exists that was established while flat and unlocked, with T1 long bias, highest high minus lowest low over the impulse lookback chart bars ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-impulse ATR multiple, and the most recent lookback low occurring before the most recent lookback high; store those extrema, measure setup freshness from the high extreme, and require freshness through the current chart bar. The configured session filter is disabled or datetime is within its configured session when the setup is established. Require long zone confirmation AND long local trend AND long score ≥ minimum score AND entry price (current close) − long stop > minimum tick AND the same raw long fire condition was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close while flat, a locked short setup exists that was established while flat and unlocked, with T1 short bias, highest high minus lowest low over the impulse lookback chart bars ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-impulse ATR multiple, and the most recent lookback high occurring before the most recent lookback low; store those extrema, measure setup freshness from the low extreme, and require freshness through the current chart bar. The configured session filter is disabled or datetime is within its configured session when the setup is established. Require short zone confirmation AND short local trend AND short score ≥ minimum score AND short stop − entry price (current close) > minimum tick AND the same raw short fire condition was false on the previous chart bar.
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
