Calculation: Pivot levels = {P=(H+L+C)/3, R1=2P-L, R2=P+H-L, R3=H+2(P-L), R4=R3+H-L, R5=R4+H-L, S1=2P-H, S2=P-H+L, S3=L-2(H-P), S4=S3-H+L, S5=S4-H+L}, where H, L, and C are high, low, and close of the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: Long target = lowest pivot level above the entry signal bar's close, or R5 if none; set on entry and held for the position
Calculation: Short target = highest pivot level below the entry signal bar's close, or S5 if none; set on entry and held for the position
Long: Current T2 bar is within the session window parameters AND fewer than the daily signal limit parameter of position-flat signal entries have occurred on the current calendar day AND VWAP((high+low+close)/3, volume, T1-anchored, chart bars through the current bar) is defined AND there exists a pivot level P such that (low<P AND low<VWAP AND close>P AND close>VWAP) OR (close>P AND close>VWAP AND close[1]<P AND close[1]<VWAP)
Short: Current T2 bar is within the session window parameters AND fewer than the daily signal limit parameter of position-flat signal entries have occurred on the current calendar day AND VWAP((high+low+close)/3, volume, T1-anchored, chart bars through the current bar) is defined AND there exists a pivot level P such that (high>P AND high>VWAP AND close<P AND close<VWAP) OR (close<P AND close<VWAP AND close[1]>P AND close[1]>VWAP)
Exit: Long, (high>=long target AND close<long target) OR close<VWAP((high+low+close)/3, volume, T1-anchored, chart bars through the current bar)
Exit: Short, (low<=short target AND close>short target) OR close>VWAP((high+low+close)/3, volume, T1-anchored, chart bars through the current bar)
