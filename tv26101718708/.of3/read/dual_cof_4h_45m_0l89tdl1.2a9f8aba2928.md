Calculation: COF(T) = For T1 (longer) and T2 (shorter), on chart bars using the last completed T bar, initialize the held high, low, and direction from the first available T bar; on each new completed T bar, if its close is above the held high or below the held low, replace the held high and low with its high and low and set direction bullish if close >= open, otherwise bearish; otherwise retain them.
Calculation: COF entry levels(T) = COF low plus the bullish percentage parameter or bearish percentage parameter of (COF high - COF low), using separate parameters for each direction.
Calculation: COF zone-hit state(T) = Reset armed and hit states on each COF update; on chart bars without a COF update, arm immediately if the return-to-zone requirement is disabled, otherwise arm for a bearish COF when low < its entry level or for a bullish COF when high > its entry level; after arming, including on that same bar, latch hit for a bearish COF when high >= its entry level or a bullish COF when low <= its entry level; retain hit until the next COF update.
Calculation: Confirmation close(T) = On chart bars, use the last completed T3 bar, where T3 is the separately parameterized confirmation timeframe; after a zone hit and before a trade signal, update on each new completed T3 bar to its close if that close is between the current COF low and high inclusive, otherwise clear it; clear on a COF update or zone hit.
Calculation: Trade signal already fired(T) = Reset on each COF update; after a zone hit, latch true on the first chart bar with a defined confirmation close where a bullish COF has close >= confirmation close + momentum distance or a bearish COF has close <= confirmation close - momentum distance; retain until the next COF update.
Calculation: Momentum distance = the distance parameter in price units, or the distance parameter multiplied by the instrument minimum tick size when specified in ticks.
Long: On a chart bar when a new completed T1 or T2 bar becomes available, its close is above the COF high held immediately before that update or below the COF low held immediately before it, and its close >= its open.
Short: On a chart bar when a new completed T1 or T2 bar becomes available, its close is above the COF high held immediately before that update or below the COF low held immediately before it, and its close < its open.
Long: The bullish COF zone-hit state for T1 or T2 changes from false to true.
Short: The bearish COF zone-hit state for T1 or T2 changes from false to true.
Long: For a bullish COF on T1 or T2, the zone-hit state is true, the confirmation close is defined, no trade signal has fired on an earlier chart bar since the COF update, and chart close >= confirmation close + momentum distance.
Short: For a bearish COF on T1 or T2, the zone-hit state is true, the confirmation close is defined, no trade signal has fired on an earlier chart bar since the COF update, and chart close <= confirmation close - momentum distance.
