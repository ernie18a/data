Calculation: Neckline(bar) = linear interpolation between the two neckline-pivot prices at their pivot bars, extended to bar
Calculation: Pattern symmetry score = mean of time symmetry percentage and price symmetry percentage, where time symmetry percentage = 100% × (1 − absolute difference between the two adjacent pivot-bar spans ÷ the greater span), and price symmetry percentage = clamp(100% × (1 − shoulder-price difference ÷ max(head extension, ATR)), 0, 100%)
Calculation: Bearish H&S structure = the latest three confirmed pivot highs form left shoulder, head, and right shoulder in order; head price exceeds both shoulder prices; minimum head-to-shoulder price difference ≥ head-prominence ATR parameter × ATR(ATR lookback parameter) at detection; shoulder-price difference ≤ shoulder-tolerance ATR parameter × that ATR; each adjacent pivot-bar span is ≥ pivot lookback parameter and < maximum-span parameter; the latest confirmed pivot low in each interval between these pivots exists and the neckline-pivot price difference ≤ neckline-tolerance ATR parameter × that ATR; pattern symmetry score ≥ minimum-symmetry parameter
Calculation: Inverse H&S structure = the latest three confirmed pivot lows form left shoulder, head, and right shoulder in order; head price is below both shoulder prices; minimum shoulder-to-head price difference ≥ head-prominence ATR parameter × ATR(ATR lookback parameter) at detection; shoulder-price difference ≤ shoulder-tolerance ATR parameter × that ATR; each adjacent pivot-bar span is ≥ pivot lookback parameter and < maximum-span parameter; the latest confirmed pivot high in each interval between these pivots exists and the neckline-pivot price difference ≤ neckline-tolerance ATR parameter × that ATR; pattern symmetry score ≥ minimum-symmetry parameter
Calculation: TP1 = at confirmation, Neckline(confirmation bar) minus the target distance for a bearish H&S and plus the target distance for an inverse H&S; target distance is the absolute difference between head-pivot price and either the mean neckline-pivot price or Neckline(head-pivot bar), according to the configured target basis
Calculation: Stop = at confirmation, head-pivot price plus ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × stop-buffer parameter for a bearish H&S, or minus that amount for an inverse H&S
Short: on a confirmed pivot-high or pivot-low event, at least two pivot lookbacks after the preceding pattern mark, a bearish H&S structure forms
Long: on a confirmed pivot-high or pivot-low event, at least two pivot lookbacks after the preceding pattern mark, an inverse H&S structure forms AND no bearish H&S structure forms
Short: for a bearish H&S structure not previously expired or invalidated, on the first chart bar after its right-shoulder pivot where close < Neckline(current bar)
Long: for an inverse H&S structure not previously expired or invalidated, on the first chart bar after its right-shoulder pivot where close > Neckline(current bar)
Short: for a confirmed bearish H&S not previously retested or exited, more than two bars after confirmation, high ≥ Neckline(current bar) AND low ≤ Neckline(current bar)
Long: for a confirmed inverse H&S not previously retested or exited, more than two bars after confirmation, high ≥ Neckline(current bar) AND low ≤ Neckline(current bar)
Exit: Short, low ≤ TP1 OR high ≥ Stop
Exit: Long, high ≥ TP1 OR low ≤ Stop
