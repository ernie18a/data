Calculation: zone = cluster rebuilt on each new completed T1 bar from PivotHigh(T1 high, pivot strength, pivot strength) and PivotLow(T1 low, pivot strength, pivot strength) values over the last T1 lookback completed T1 bars, visited from newest to oldest bar with pivot high before pivot low; each pivot joins the earliest existing cluster whose mean is within cluster tolerance multiple × ATR(T1 bars, T1 ATR length, last completed bar) of it, otherwise starts a new cluster; zone touches = number of pivots in cluster; zone mean = mean of its pivots; zone bottom = min(lowest pivot, mean - pad); zone top = max(highest pivot, mean + pad); pad = ATR(T1 bars, T1 ATR length, last completed bar) × cluster tolerance multiple × minimum half-height factor
Calculation: qualifying zone = zone with touches ≥ minimum touches
Calculation: key levels = {high(T2, last completed bar), low(T2, last completed bar), close(T2, last completed bar), high(T3, last completed bar), low(T3, last completed bar), SMA(close, shorter SMA length, T2, last completed bar), SMA(close, middle SMA length, T2, last completed bar), SMA(close, longer SMA length, T2, last completed bar)}, with shorter SMA length < middle SMA length < longer SMA length
Calculation: distance above = smallest value greater than close among bottoms of qualifying zones and key levels - close
Calculation: distance below = close - largest value less than close among tops of qualifying zones and key levels
Calculation: risk unit = fixed stop points if stop mode is fixed, else ATR(chart bars, intraday ATR length) × stop ATR multiple; tick size if that value is not positive or undefined
Long: NOT (any qualifying zone has bottom ≤ close ≤ top) AND NOT (distance above < ATR(T1 bars, T1 ATR length, last completed bar) × danger band multiple) AND NOT (distance above / risk unit < minimum room R)
Short: NOT (any qualifying zone has bottom ≤ close ≤ top) AND NOT (distance below < ATR(T1 bars, T1 ATR length, last completed bar) × danger band multiple) AND NOT (distance below / risk unit < minimum room R)
