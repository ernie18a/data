Calculation: selected VWAP = VWAP(hlc3, volume, selected reset interval or session, accumulated on chart bars through the current chart bar; for a session basis, accumulate only during the selected session and reset at its start)
Calculation: close-location ratio = (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise the close-location fallback parameter
Calculation: body-to-range ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise the body-ratio fallback parameter
Calculation: VWAP distance in ATRs = abs(close - selected VWAP) / ATR(ATR lookback) when ATR is available and positive, otherwise the distance fallback parameter
Long: confirmed chart bar AND selected VWAP is available AND low <= selected VWAP AND close > selected VWAP AND close > open AND close-location ratio >= long close-location threshold AND body-to-range ratio >= minimum body-ratio threshold AND VWAP distance in ATRs >= minimum VWAP-distance threshold AND (volume is unavailable OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) AND selected VWAP did not reset on the current chart bar AND selected VWAP is greater than its value on the prior chart bar AND (no prior reaction OR more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior long or short reaction)
Short: confirmed chart bar AND selected VWAP is available AND high >= selected VWAP AND close < selected VWAP AND close < open AND close-location ratio <= short close-location threshold AND body-to-range ratio >= minimum body-ratio threshold AND VWAP distance in ATRs >= minimum VWAP-distance threshold AND (volume is unavailable OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) AND selected VWAP did not reset on the current chart bar AND selected VWAP is less than its value on the prior chart bar AND (no prior reaction OR more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior long or short reaction)
