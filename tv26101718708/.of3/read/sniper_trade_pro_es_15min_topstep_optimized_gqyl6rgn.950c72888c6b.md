Calculation: MFD = EMA(Momentum(close, MFD momentum lookback), MFD smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Accumulated P&L = prior accumulated P&L plus total realized P&L on every bar with no open position; otherwise unchanged
Long: datetime is within the session window AND Accumulated P&L > loss limit AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > VWAP(close) AND MFD > SMA(MFD, MFD signal lookback) AND close > open[one bar ago] AND open < close[one bar ago] AND high - low > high[one bar ago] - low[one bar ago] AND ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback) > ADX threshold
Short: datetime is within the session window AND Accumulated P&L > loss limit AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < VWAP(close) AND MFD < SMA(MFD, MFD signal lookback) AND close < open[one bar ago] AND open > close[one bar ago] AND high - low > high[one bar ago] - low[one bar ago] AND ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback) > ADX threshold
Exit: long, low <= close of the most recent risk-approved long signal bar - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier OR high >= close of the most recent risk-approved long signal bar + ATR(ATR lookback) * target multiplier OR, after a close reaches entry price + ATR(ATR lookback), low <= entry price
Exit: short, high >= close of the most recent risk-approved short signal bar + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop multiplier OR low <= close of the most recent risk-approved short signal bar - ATR(ATR lookback) * target multiplier OR, after a close reaches entry price - ATR(ATR lookback), high >= entry price
