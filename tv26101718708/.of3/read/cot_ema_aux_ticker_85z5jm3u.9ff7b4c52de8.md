Calculation: Commercial net-position change for a selected market = (Commercial long-position series close[n] - close[n+1]) - (Commercial short-position series close[n] - close[n+1]), where n is the shared COT offset parameter; calculate on T1 bars and project onto chart bars with lookahead on, using the current T1 bar when n=0.
Long: datetime is on or after the configured start date AND (COT filter is disabled OR (first selected market's Commercial net-position change > 0 AND second selected market's Commercial net-position change < 0)) AND (main EMA filter is disabled OR EMA(selected source, main lookback) > its prior chart-bar value) AND (auxiliary 1 filter is disabled OR (normal mode AND EMA(auxiliary 1 T1 close, auxiliary 1 lookback) > its prior chart-bar value OR inverse mode AND EMA(auxiliary 1 T1 close, auxiliary 1 lookback) < its prior chart-bar value)) AND (auxiliary 2 filter is disabled OR (normal mode AND EMA(auxiliary 2 T1 close, auxiliary 2 lookback) > its prior chart-bar value OR inverse mode AND EMA(auxiliary 2 T1 close, auxiliary 2 lookback) < its prior chart-bar value)); calculate auxiliary EMAs on chart bars from T1 closes mapped with lookahead off (completed T1 values historically, still-forming values in realtime).
Short: datetime is on or after the configured start date AND (COT filter is disabled OR (first selected market's Commercial net-position change < 0 AND second selected market's Commercial net-position change > 0)) AND (main EMA filter is disabled OR EMA(selected source, main lookback) < its prior chart-bar value) AND (auxiliary 1 filter is disabled OR (normal mode AND EMA(auxiliary 1 T1 close, auxiliary 1 lookback) < its prior chart-bar value OR inverse mode AND EMA(auxiliary 1 T1 close, auxiliary 1 lookback) > its prior chart-bar value)) AND (auxiliary 2 filter is disabled OR (normal mode AND EMA(auxiliary 2 T1 close, auxiliary 2 lookback) < its prior chart-bar value OR inverse mode AND EMA(auxiliary 2 T1 close, auxiliary 2 lookback) > its prior chart-bar value)); calculate auxiliary EMAs on chart bars from T1 closes mapped with lookahead off (completed T1 values historically, still-forming values in realtime).
Exit: long, low <= the low of the most recent bar with a long signal
Exit: short, high >= the high of the most recent bar with a short signal
Exit: long, close > entry price
Exit: short, close < entry price
