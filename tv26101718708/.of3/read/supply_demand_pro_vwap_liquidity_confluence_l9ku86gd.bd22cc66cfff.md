Calculation: Explosive bar = high - low > ATR(ATR lookback) × move multiplier AND abs(close - open) > (high - low) × body fraction AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier)
Calculation: Base = start with candle [2] before the zone detection bar; extend through consecutive older candles up to the base-count parameter only while each has abs(close - open) < ATR(ATR lookback)[1] × base-body factor AND is not explosive; base high and low are the highest high and lowest low of the included candles
Calculation: Demand zone = on a confirmed chart bar with an explosive bullish bar[1], top = max(base high, open[1]) and bottom = base low; the zone remains active until a chart close < bottom
Calculation: Supply zone = on a confirmed chart bar with an explosive bearish bar[1], top = base high and bottom = min(base low, open[1]); the zone remains active until a chart close > top
