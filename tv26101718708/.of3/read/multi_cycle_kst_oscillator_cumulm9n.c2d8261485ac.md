Calculation: Short-term KST = KST(selected price source, four separately parameterized ROC lookbacks and their corresponding SMA lookbacks) on chart bars, using shorter lookbacks than the intermediate-term and long-term KSTs.
Calculation: Intermediate-term KST = KST(selected price source, its own four ROC lookbacks and corresponding SMA lookbacks) on chart bars.
Calculation: Long-term KST = KST(selected price source, its own four ROC lookbacks and corresponding SMA lookbacks) on chart bars, using longer lookbacks than the short-term KST.
Calculation: Normalized KST(x) = x in raw mode; (x − SMA(short-term KST, statistical lookback)) / STDEV(short-term KST, statistical lookback) in Z mode; 0.6745 × (x − MEDIAN(short-term KST, statistical lookback)) / MEDIAN(ABS(short-term KST − MEDIAN(short-term KST, statistical lookback)), statistical lookback) in robust-Z mode; use 0 when the selected denominator is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Oscillator = Normalized KST(short-term KST); Signal = Normalized KST(SMA(short-term KST, separate signal lookback)), on chart bars.
Calculation: Weighted vote = (cross weight × Vcross + long-term weight × Vlong + price-location weight × Vprice + divergence weight × Vdiv) / max(1, sum of the four separately parameterized weights), where Vcross is +1 if Oscillator > Signal and −1 otherwise; Vlong is +1 if long-term KST is rising, −1 if falling, and 0 if unchanged; Vprice is +1 if close > selected EMA or SMA(close, trend lookback), and −1 otherwise; Vdiv is +1 for bullish divergence, −1 for bearish divergence, and 0 otherwise, with bullish taking precedence.
Calculation: Confidence = min(100, ABS(100 × Weighted vote) × (chop conviction + Efficiency Ratio(close, separate lookback) × (clean-trend conviction − chop conviction))).
Long: Oscillator crosses above Signal AND long-term KST > its prior chart-bar value AND (ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) ≥ ADX threshold if the regime filter is enabled) AND (on chart bars, short-term KST ≥ its raw SMA signal on the previous completed T1 bar if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled; calculate both on T1 bars, use neutral when unavailable) AND Confidence ≥ confidence threshold AND warmup is complete (statistical lookback bars in either normalized mode, or the maximum short-term ROC/SMA lookback plus signal lookback bars in raw mode) AND (the chart bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled).
Short: Oscillator crosses below Signal AND long-term KST < its prior chart-bar value AND (ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) ≥ ADX threshold if the regime filter is enabled) AND (on chart bars, short-term KST ≤ its raw SMA signal on the previous completed T1 bar if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled; calculate both on T1 bars, use neutral when unavailable) AND Confidence ≥ confidence threshold AND warmup is complete (statistical lookback bars in either normalized mode, or the maximum short-term ROC/SMA lookback plus signal lookback bars in raw mode) AND (the chart bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled).
Long: On confirmation of a pivot low, with divergence detection enabled and using the selected low source and Oscillator at the pivot-right bars ago, the pivot is within the maximum gap from the prior confirmed pivot low AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND the selected low is lower than at the prior pivot AND Oscillator is higher) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND the selected low is higher than at the prior pivot AND Oscillator is lower)).
Short: On confirmation of a pivot high, with divergence detection enabled and using the selected high source and Oscillator at the pivot-right bars ago, the pivot is within the maximum gap from the prior confirmed pivot high AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND the selected high is higher than at the prior pivot AND Oscillator is lower) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND the selected high is lower than at the prior pivot AND Oscillator is higher)).
Long: Short-term KST > 0 AND intermediate-term KST > 0 AND long-term KST > 0.
Short: Short-term KST < 0 AND intermediate-term KST < 0 AND long-term KST < 0.
Long: Weighted vote ≥ 0.
Short: Weighted vote < 0.
