Calculation: Manipulation candidate(T, side) = process completed bars of each source timeframe T1, T2, and T3 separately; reset on a configured context-boundary change or source-data gap; use confirmed PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T, last completed bar) and PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T, last completed bar), accepting pivots only when their confirmation window stays within one context; for a long candidate, sweep the latest unused pivot low when low penetrates it by the configured sweep ticks and use a preceding same-context pivot high as origin A, while for a short candidate reverse high and low; require A to precede the sweep, lie on the correct side of the swept pivot, and be within the maximum-leg lookback; reject a bar that sweeps both sides; set B to the sweep bar's directional extreme and retain ATR from that bar; replace a candidate on a qualifying sweep with a newer origin, or update its swept pivot and B when a later qualifying sweep makes a new directional extreme; update A to a later confirmed origin-side pivot strictly between A and B when it is on the correct side of B and within the maximum-leg lookback; use A as MSS unless the latest-internal-swing model is configured, in which case use the latest confirmed origin-side pivot formed after B; discard when the origin distance or wait limit is exceeded; reset the confirmation count and displacement state when B or MSS changes, and reset the count on any ineligible bar.
Calculation: Directional displacement(T, side) = side × (close − open) ≥ (ATR normalization enabled ? ATR(ATR lookback, T, last completed bar) at the candidate's initial sweep × minimum body ATR multiple : tick size) AND abs(close − open) / max(high − low, tick size) ≥ body-fraction parameter.
Long: On a completed T1, T2, or T3 bar, a bullish manipulation candidate has abs(A − B) ≥ max(minimum-leg ticks × tick size, ATR normalization enabled ? ATR at initial sweep × minimum ATR multiple : 0), A above the swept pivot S, and S − B ≥ sweep-penetration ticks × tick size; close > S AND close > MSS + break-buffer ticks × tick size AND bar time > B time; and confirmation mode is satisfied: if it requires multiple consecutive eligible closes, the required count is reached and at least one counted bar has Directional displacement(T, long); if it is close-through-only, no displacement is required; otherwise the current bar has Directional displacement(T, long); reject confirmation if the bearish candidate also confirms on this bar.
Short: On a completed T1, T2, or T3 bar, a bearish manipulation candidate has abs(A − B) ≥ max(minimum-leg ticks × tick size, ATR normalization enabled ? ATR at initial sweep × minimum ATR multiple : 0), A below the swept pivot S, and B − S ≥ sweep-penetration ticks × tick size; close < S AND close < MSS − break-buffer ticks × tick size AND bar time > B time; and confirmation mode is satisfied: if it requires multiple consecutive eligible closes, the required count is reached and at least one counted bar has Directional displacement(T, short); if it is close-through-only, no displacement is required; otherwise the current bar has Directional displacement(T, short); reject confirmation if the bullish candidate also confirms on this bar.
Calculation: Valid current leg(T) = the current confirmed leg is active or completed, is not invalidated, and its anchors A and B and the paired pivot are in the current context; invalidate it on a later completed source bar when the configured observation (directional wick for touch mode, close for close mode) reaches or crosses the configured measured-leg invalidation level; completion occurs when price reaches the configured terminal expansion, and an active leg expires at the configured source-bar age.
Calculation: Expansion-zone SMT(T) = on completed, aligned source bars of T and the comparison symbol, compare successive confirmed same-time PivotHigh pairs for a valid bullish current leg and successive confirmed same-time PivotLow pairs for a valid bearish current leg; require matching source-bar times, closes, and contexts, positive tick sizes, both pivots after B, pivot separation within the maximum-leg lookback, and no intervening unmatched pivot; the two symbols' pivot changes must have opposite signs and each meet the configured minimum divergence movement; calculate expansion magnitude as (current pivot price − A) / (A − B) and require it within either configured expansion band after widening each edge by configured tolerance ticks × tick size / abs(A − B); emit at most one marker per band per leg; bullish-leg PivotHigh divergence is bearish, and bearish-leg PivotLow divergence is bullish.
Short: Expansion-zone SMT(T) is bearish on a valid bullish current leg.
Long: Expansion-zone SMT(T) is bullish on a valid bearish current leg.
