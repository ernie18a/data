Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, range floor)
Calculation: Centered body = (open + close) / 2 lies between the configured lower and upper fractions of the candle range
Calculation: Upper wick ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, range floor); lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, range floor)
Calculation: Fast and long EMAs = EMA(close, fast lookback) and EMA(close, longer lookback), with the long lookback longer than the fast lookback
Calculation: Uptrend = close > fast EMA and fast EMA > long EMA; downtrend = close < fast EMA and fast EMA < long EMA
Calculation: Trend bias = long EMA minus its value at the configured slope lookback; bullish bias when trend filtering is enabled and this change exceeds close times the configured slope percentage; bearish bias when it is below the negative of that threshold
Calculation: Average volume = SMA(volume, volume-average lookback); RSI = RSI(close, RSI lookback); ATR = ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = pivot highs and lows using the same left and right swing lookback; retain the two latest confirmed highs and lows and their bar indices
Calculation: Session levels = high and low accumulated during the configured premarket session and current regular session; retain the bars when the regular-session high and low were most recently set
Calculation: Opening-range levels = high and low accumulated during the configured opening-range interval, available after that range is set
Calculation: Near a level = percentage distance from close to the level is less than the configured key-level proximity threshold
Calculation: At daily resistance/support = near an enabled premarket high or regular-session high / near an enabled premarket low or regular-session low
Calculation: At resistance/support = near the latest confirmed swing high / low, or at daily resistance/support
Calculation: Fib levels = four separately parameterized retracement fractions of the regular-session high-low range, measured from the high if it was set most recently, otherwise from the low
Calculation: Fib resistance/support = when the high was set most recently, the two shallower fib levels are resistance and the two deeper levels are support; when the low was set most recently, the two deeper levels are resistance and the two shallower levels are support
Calculation: Near a fib = fib levels are enabled and the absolute distance from close to any fib level is less than close times the configured fib proximity percentage
Calculation: Fib confluence = near a fib and that same fib level is within the fib proximity distance of a premarket high or low, regular-session high or low, or latest confirmed swing high or low
Calculation: Strict recent high/low = current high exceeds the highest high of the configured recent-high lookback ending on the prior bar by ATR times the configured margin / current low is below the lowest low of the configured recent-low lookback ending on the prior bar by that margin
Calculation: At high/low = strict recent high/low or at resistance/support
Calculation: Prior-bar-at-high/low = prior high exceeds the recent-high maximum ending two bars ago by the ATR margin, or is near the latest swing high / prior low is below the recent-low minimum ending two bars ago by the ATR margin, or is near the latest swing low
Calculation: Prior doji = prior candle body ratio is below the configured doji threshold and its body is centered
Calculation: Bullish doji-engulf = engulf signals are enabled, prior doji, close > open, close > prior high, and current body size > prior body size; bearish doji-engulf = enabled, prior doji, close < open, close < prior low, and current body size > prior body size
Calculation: Strong bullish engulf = engulf signals are enabled, current candle is bullish, open <= prior close, close >= prior open, current body exceeds prior body, prior body is less than the configured fraction of current body, and prior candle is not a doji; strong bearish engulf uses the inverse open/close comparisons and a bearish candle
Calculation: Shooting star = shooting-star/hammer signals are enabled, at high, upper wick ratio >= the configured rejection-wick threshold, and the candle body top is below the configured lower-range fraction; hammer = enabled, at low, lower wick ratio >= that threshold, and body bottom is above the configured upper-range fraction
Calculation: Doji at strict high/low = candle body ratio below the configured doji threshold, centered body, and strict recent high/low
Calculation: Volume exhaustion = exhaustion signals are enabled, current and prior volumes decline for four consecutive bars when the configured exhaustion-bar setting is at least four, otherwise for three consecutive bars, current volume is below average when the below-average requirement is enabled, and body ratio is below the configured exhaustion-body threshold
Calculation: Bearish/bullish volume exhaustion = volume exhaustion, strict recent high/low, and body ratio below the exhaustion-body threshold, with a bearish candle or doji / bullish candle or doji; bearish exhaustion is also allowed in an uptrend only near the latest swing high, and bullish exhaustion in a downtrend only near the latest swing low
Calculation: Engulf-with-exhaustion = matching doji-engulf and matching volume exhaustion on the current or prior bar
Calculation: Climactic bearish/bullish volume = spike signals are enabled, current volume >= average volume times the configured spike multiplier, strict recent high/low, and a bearish candle or upper wick above the configured strong-wick threshold / a bullish candle or lower wick above that threshold
Calculation: Bearish/bullish CHoCH = CHoCH signals are enabled, close below the latest confirmed swing low while in an uptrend / close above the latest confirmed swing high while in a downtrend
Calculation: Bearish/bullish key-level rejection = key-level rejection signals are enabled, close near the latest confirmed swing high/low, upper/lower wick ratio above the configured key-rejection wick threshold, and bearish/bullish candle
Calculation: Trendline values = extrapolate a line through the latest two confirmed swing highs/lows to the current bar; bearish trendline break = enabled, swing-high line slopes down, current close > line, and prior close <= current line; bullish trendline break = enabled, swing-low line slopes up, current close < line, and prior close >= current line
Calculation: Consecutive closes above/below the fast EMA = count preceding consecutive chart bars closing above/below that bar's fast EMA, resetting the count on any other close
Calculation: Bullish EMA pullback = EMA-pullback signals enabled, uptrend, low <= fast EMA plus the configured touch buffer, close above fast EMA by the configured decisive-close buffer, bullish candle, and the preceding above-EMA run meets the configured away-bar requirement; bearish EMA pullback uses downtrend, high >= fast EMA minus the touch buffer, close below fast EMA by the decisive-close buffer, bearish candle, and the preceding below-EMA run
Calculation: Bullish/bearish ORB breakout = ORB signals enabled, opening range set, current close crosses above its high / below its low, and volume confirmation is disabled or volume > average volume
Calculation: Bullish/bearish flag breakout = flag signals enabled, pole percentage move from close at the longer pole offset to close at the shorter pole offset / inverse move using the shorter-offset close as denominator exceeds the configured minimum pole move, with the positive-price denominator floor applied; current flag range over the shorter-offset lookback is less than the absolute pole move times the configured tightness ratio; current close breaks above the prior lookback high / below the prior lookback low; and volume confirmation is disabled or volume > average volume
Calculation: Higher-high/lower-low structure = structure signals enabled, a new confirmed swing high exceeds the preceding confirmed swing high while in an uptrend / a new confirmed swing low is below the preceding confirmed swing low while in a downtrend
Calculation: RSI overbought/oversold = RSI signals enabled and RSI >= configured overbought level / RSI <= configured oversold level
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = RSI signals enabled, current high > prior lookback highest high, current RSI < prior lookback highest RSI, and RSI above its midpoint; bullish RSI divergence = enabled, current low < prior lookback lowest low, current RSI > prior lookback lowest RSI, and RSI below its midpoint
Calculation: Bearish daily-resistance signal = at daily resistance and shooting star, bearish doji-engulf, strong bearish engulf, or body ratio below the configured doji threshold
Calculation: Bullish raw score = bullish doji-engulf base point, plus one bonus point when prior-bar-at-low or at support, plus one point each for hammer, strong bullish engulf, bullish volume exhaustion, bullish engulf-with-exhaustion, climactic bullish volume, bullish CHoCH, bullish key-level rejection, bullish trendline break, bullish EMA pullback, bullish ORB breakout, bullish flag breakout, higher-high structure, RSI oversold, bullish RSI divergence, doji at strict low, enabled-fib proximity with hammer/bullish doji-engulf/strong bullish engulf/doji, and a bullish candle with fib confluence
Calculation: Bearish raw score = bearish doji-engulf base point, plus one bonus point when prior-bar-at-high or at resistance, plus one point each for shooting star, strong bearish engulf, bearish volume exhaustion, bearish engulf-with-exhaustion, climactic bearish volume, bearish CHoCH, bearish key-level rejection, bearish trendline break, bearish EMA pullback, bearish ORB breakout, bearish flag breakout, lower-low structure, RSI overbought, bearish RSI divergence, doji at strict high, bearish daily-resistance signal, enabled-fib proximity with shooting star/bearish doji-engulf/strong bearish engulf/doji, and a bearish candle with fib confluence
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = min(corresponding raw score, configured score cap)
Calculation: Bullish qualification = bullish raw score > 0, bullish score >= score cap when enabled trend bias is bearish or otherwise >= configured minimum score, no regular long marker within the configured cooldown lookback, RSI divergence requirement disabled or bullish RSI divergence, volume-breakout requirement disabled or current volume > prior volume times one plus the configured percentage, and close > fast EMA or bullish engulf at support/prior-bar-at-low or doji at strict low with RSI oversold
