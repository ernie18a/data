Calculation: Regime center = SMA(average of the latest confirmed swing high and swing low, regime center smoothing lookback); confirm each swing using the regime swing lookback on both sides, and use current high and low until the first respective swing is confirmed.
Calculation: Regime upper and lower bands = SMA(regime center plus or minus ATR(regime volatility lookback) times regime band multiplier, regime band smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Regime state starts bullish when a confirmed chart bar closes at or above the regime center and bearish otherwise; thereafter, a confirmed close crossing above the upper band or below the lower band sets a pending direction, which becomes the state after the regime hold delay has elapsed from that cross.
Calculation: Normalized regime = (close - regime center) / max(ATR(regime volatility lookback), minimum tick).
Calculation: Regime strength = absolute value of normalized regime.
Calculation: Pressure signal = EMA((close - selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA(close, pressure MA lookback)) / max(highest absolute value of that price-to-average distance over the pressure normalization lookback, minimum tick), pressure signal smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Participation axis = midpoint of the highest-volume bin after dividing the range from lowest low to highest high over the participation lookback into the participation row count; bin each bar's close, sum its volume, and resolve tied maximum-volume bins in favor of the lower bin.
Calculation: Axis deviation = (close - participation axis) / max(ATR(regime volatility lookback), minimum tick).
Calculation: Delta wave = EMA((EMA(volume when close > open, delta lookback) - EMA(volume when close < open, delta lookback)) / EMA(max(volume, minimum volume floor), delta lookback), delta wave smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Delta slope = delta wave - delta wave one chart bar ago.
Calculation: Recent bullish structure = a confirmed bullish base-and-impulse pattern or bullish gap occurred within the structure validity lookback; the pattern is a bearish candle two bars ago followed by a bullish candle whose body is at least ATR(structure impulse lookback) one bar ago times the structure impulse size, and the gap is current low above high two bars ago with prior close above that high.
Calculation: Recent bearish structure = a confirmed bearish base-and-impulse pattern or bearish gap occurred within the structure validity lookback; the pattern is a bullish candle two bars ago followed by a bearish candle whose body is at least ATR(structure impulse lookback) one bar ago times the structure impulse size, and the gap is current high below low two bars ago with prior close below that low.
Calculation: HTF bias is computed on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar, where T1 is no shorter than the chart timeframe; bullish when EMA(close, HTF fast lookback) > EMA(close, HTF slow lookback) and T1 close > that slow EMA, bearish when both comparisons are reversed, otherwise neutral; HTF fast lookback is shorter than HTF slow lookback.
Calculation: Long score = regime-direction score weight if regime state is bullish, plus component score weight for each condition that is true: normalized regime >= 0; regime strength >= regime strength threshold; pressure signal > 0; delta wave > 0; delta slope >= 0; recent bullish structure; close > participation axis; axis deviation > 0; and HTF filter disabled, T1 invalid, or bullish HTF bias. Regime-direction score weight is twice the component score weight.
Calculation: Short score = regime-direction score weight if regime state is bearish, plus component score weight for each condition that is true: normalized regime <= 0; regime strength >= regime strength threshold; pressure signal < 0; delta wave < 0; delta slope <= 0; recent bearish structure; close < participation axis; axis deviation < 0; and HTF filter disabled, T1 invalid, or bearish HTF bias. Regime-direction score weight is twice the component score weight.
Calculation: Score spread = long score - short score.
Calculation: Long stop starts at the lower of signal-bar close - ATR(regime volatility lookback) times stop distance multiplier and regime center - ATR(regime volatility lookback) times center offset; on each long-position chart bar, raise it to the maximum of its previous value and close - ATR(regime volatility lookback) times trail distance multiplier when trailing is enabled, otherwise retain its previous value.
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close + ATR(regime volatility lookback) times target distance multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop starts at the higher of signal-bar close + ATR(regime volatility lookback) times stop distance multiplier and regime center + ATR(regime volatility lookback) times center offset; on each short-position chart bar, lower it to the minimum of its previous value and close + ATR(regime volatility lookback) times trail distance multiplier when trailing is enabled, otherwise retain its previous value.
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar close - ATR(regime volatility lookback) times target distance multiplier.
Long: Regime state is bullish.
Short: Regime state is bearish.
Long: A confirmed chart bar closes AND long score >= confluence threshold AND score spread >= minimum entry spread AND position is flat or short AND (session filter is disabled OR the chart bar is in the configured session) AND (no prior entry exists OR chart bars since the last entry >= cooldown lookback).
Short: A confirmed chart bar closes AND short score >= confluence threshold AND score spread <= negative minimum entry spread AND position is flat or long AND (session filter is disabled OR the chart bar is in the configured session) AND (no prior entry exists OR chart bars since the last entry >= cooldown lookback).
Long: Alerts are enabled AND regime state is bullish AND the prior chart bar's regime state was not bullish.
Short: Alerts are enabled AND regime state is bearish AND the prior chart bar's regime state was not bearish.
Long: Alerts are enabled AND HTF bias filter is enabled AND T1 is no shorter than the chart timeframe AND HTF bias is bullish.
Short: Alerts are enabled AND HTF bias filter is enabled AND T1 is no shorter than the chart timeframe AND HTF bias is bearish.
Long: Alerts are enabled AND long score >= confluence threshold AND score spread >= strong-signal spread threshold, where the strong-signal spread threshold is greater than the minimum entry spread.
Short: Alerts are enabled AND short score >= confluence threshold AND score spread <= negative strong-signal spread threshold, where the strong-signal spread threshold is greater than the minimum entry spread.
Long: Score heat display is enabled AND long score >= confluence threshold.
Short: Score heat display is enabled AND long score < confluence threshold AND short score >= confluence threshold.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR regime state is bearish OR score spread <= 0 OR pressure signal < 0 OR close < regime center.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR regime state is bullish OR score spread >= 0 OR pressure signal > 0 OR close > regime center.
