Calculation: adjusted internal lookback = max(internal minimum lookback, round(internal lookback * aggressiveness multiplier))
Calculation: adjusted swing lookback = max(swing minimum lookback, round(swing lookback * aggressiveness multiplier))
Calculation: performance score = optimization enabled ? clamp(mean recorded trade result / score baseline, score floor, score ceiling) : neutral score
Calculation: effective ATR buffer multiplier = base ATR buffer multiplier * (performance score below lower threshold ? low-score factor : performance score above upper threshold ? high-score factor : neutral factor)
Long: position is flat AND long entries are enabled AND ((internal structure is active AND close crosses above the latest confirmed internal pivot high using the adjusted internal lookback) OR (swing structure is active AND close crosses above the latest confirmed swing pivot high using the adjusted swing lookback)) AND (PD filter disabled OR close < (highest(high, range lookback) + lowest(low, range lookback)) / 2) AND (FVG filter disabled OR ((low > high[2] AND close[1] > high[2]) now OR one bar ago)) AND (divergence filter disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND low < low[1])) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close > SMA(close, Bollinger lookback))
Short: position is flat AND short entries are enabled AND ((internal structure is active AND close crosses below the latest confirmed internal pivot low using the adjusted internal lookback) OR (swing structure is active AND close crosses below the latest confirmed swing pivot low using the adjusted swing lookback)) AND (PD filter disabled OR close > (highest(high, range lookback) + lowest(low, range lookback)) / 2) AND (FVG filter disabled OR ((high < low[2] AND close[1] < low[2]) now OR one bar ago)) AND (divergence filter disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND high > high[1])) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close < SMA(close, Bollinger lookback))
Calculation: initial long stop = max((latest internal pivot low below entry price, or lowest(low, stop fallback lookback)) - ATR(ATR lookback) * effective ATR buffer multiplier, entry price * (1 - hard-stop cap fraction)); if this is not below entry price, use entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * fallback stop ATR multiple
Calculation: initial short stop = min((latest internal pivot high above entry price, or highest(high, stop fallback lookback)) + ATR(ATR lookback) * effective ATR buffer multiplier, entry price * (1 + hard-stop cap fraction)); if this is not above entry price, use entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * fallback stop ATR multiple
Calculation: long risk = entry price - initial long stop
Calculation: short risk = initial short stop - entry price
Calculation: long first target = entry price + long risk
Calculation: short first target = entry price - short risk
Calculation: long second target = latest swing pivot high if structural target mode is selected and that pivot is above the first target; otherwise entry price + long risk * reward multiple
Calculation: short second target = latest swing pivot low if structural target mode is selected and that pivot is below the first target; otherwise entry price - short risk * reward multiple
Calculation: long stop = entry price after the first target is hit, otherwise initial long stop; when high > high[1], raise it to max(current long stop, high - ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing ATR multiple)
Calculation: short stop = entry price after the first target is hit, otherwise initial short stop; when low < low[1], lower it to min(current short stop, low + ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing ATR multiple)
Exit: long, (first target not hit AND high >= long first target) OR (second target not hit AND high >= long second target) OR low <= long stop
Exit: short, (first target not hit AND low <= short first target) OR (second target not hit AND low <= short second target) OR high >= short stop
