Calculation: Prior ATR = ATR(ATR lookback)[1]
Calculation: VWAP and sigma = VWAP(source, anchor period) and max(VWAP standard deviation(source, anchor period), instrument minimum price increment), calculated on chart bars
Calculation: VWAP z-score = (close - VWAP) / sigma
Calculation: Bar span = max(high - low, instrument minimum price increment)
Calculation: Prior T2 high and low = high[1] and low[1] from T2; chart bars use the last completed T2 bar
Calculation: Active session levels = running high and low, plus opening-range high and low during the configured opening-range duration; opening-range levels are available after that duration
Calculation: Same-time RVOL = volume / average volume for the same clock-time slot in prior comparison sessions
Calculation: VFI raw, signal, and slope = VFI(hlc3, VFI lookback, VFI volatility lookback, VFI cutoff coefficient, VFI volume cap), EMA(VFI raw, VFI signal lookback), and VFI raw - VFI raw[1]
Calculation: Bullish VFI = VFI raw > 0 AND VFI raw >= VFI signal AND VFI slope >= minimum VFI slope; bearish VFI = VFI raw < 0 AND VFI raw <= VFI signal AND VFI slope <= -minimum VFI slope
Calculation: VFI turn up = VFI slope > reversal turn threshold AND (VFI raw >= VFI signal OR VFI slope > VFI slope[1]); turn down = VFI slope < -reversal turn threshold AND (VFI raw <= VFI signal OR VFI slope < VFI slope[1])
Calculation: VSA effort = volume / SMA(volume, VSA baseline lookback)
Calculation: Bullish VSA initiative = VSA effort >= initiative relative-volume threshold AND abs(close - open) / Prior ATR >= initiative body threshold AND close > open AND (close - low) / Bar span >= directional close threshold; bearish initiative uses close < open and (close - low) / Bar span <= 1 - directional close threshold
Calculation: Candle delta fraction = 1 when close > open, -1 when close < open, and 0 when close = open, if volume > 0; delta slope = candle delta fraction - candle delta fraction[1]
Calculation: CVD turn up = volume > 0 AND (candle delta fraction > 0 OR (delta slope >= signed-candle turn threshold AND candle delta fraction > -opposite-candle fraction limit)); turn down = volume > 0 AND (candle delta fraction < 0 OR (delta slope <= -signed-candle turn threshold AND candle delta fraction < opposite-candle fraction limit))
Calculation: T1 structure bias = on T1 bars, neutral if either latest confirmed swing pivot is older than the maximum T1-bar age; otherwise bullish when close > latest swing high + instrument minimum price increment, bearish when close < latest swing low - instrument minimum price increment, and otherwise the prior direction; chart bars use the last completed T1 bias
Calculation: Liquidity sweeps = down sweep when low <= a level - minimum sweep penetration and close > that level; up sweep when high >= a level + minimum sweep penetration and close < that level. Levels are prior T2 high/low, active session high/low, completed opening-range high/low, and confirmed chart swing high/low within maximum pivot age
Calculation: Compact pullback = highest(high, pullback envelope lookback) - lowest(low, pullback envelope lookback) <= pullback range ATR multiple * Prior ATR
Calculation: Local long pullback = Compact pullback AND (low < low[1] OR close[1] < close[2]) AND close > open; local short pullback = Compact pullback AND (high > high[1] OR close[1] > close[2]) AND close < open
Calculation: Continuation long seed event = continuation enabled AND (HTF filter disabled OR T1 structure bias bullish) AND close > VWAP AND (continuation sweep required ? down sweep : local long pullback); short mirrors this
Calculation: Reversal long seed event = reversal enabled AND (reversal sweep required ? down sweep : local long pullback); short mirrors this
Calculation: Baseline seeds = on a confirmed bar with valid context, flow, VWAP and Prior ATR, and no news block, refresh the long or short seed only when exactly one side's combined continuation/reversal seed event occurs. Long seed low is lowest(low, pullback envelope lookback) for an unswept continuation event, otherwise low; short seed high is highest(high, pullback envelope lookback) for an unswept continuation event, otherwise high. Store the prior-bar highest high or lowest low over the structure-break lookback as trigger, whether swept, and whether the seed bar exceeded the session-specific reversal VWAP excursion. Expire after maximum seed age or when price breaches the stored low/high by an instrument minimum price increment; clear on context reset, news block, invalid flow, or any confirmed signal
Calculation: Frozen continuation seeds = only in the frozen-seed timing route, create on the first one-sided continuation seed event when no same-side frozen seed exists. Store the seed low/high and trigger as max(seed high, prior-bar highest high) or min(seed low, prior-bar lowest low). Expire after maximum seed age, a seed-level breach, HTF direction loss, VWAP-side loss, context reset, news block, invalid flow, or route/continuation disablement. The first later confirmed close beyond the trigger consumes the seed even if another entry gate fails
Calculation: Accepted VWAP closes = consecutive confirmed closes above VWAP for long or below VWAP for short, while ready and not news-blocked; otherwise reset the count
Calculation: Ready = a permitted session context, valid VFI and VSA effort, valid VWAP, and valid Prior ATR. A permitted context is an enabled configured NY/Asia session when session restriction is enabled, or all-hours context otherwise; all-hours context resets at a T2 period change
Calculation: Common entry gate = Ready AND no enabled manual news pause or overlapping dated blackout AND (volatility/RVOL engine disabled OR Prior ATR >= the NY/Asia-specific minimum AND (Same-time RVOL unavailable OR Same-time RVOL >= the NY/Asia-specific minimum) AND Bar span <= Prior ATR * shock-range multiple)
Calculation: Remembered turn = a VFI turn in the seed direction, with the CVD turn condition required when enabled and volume is valid, remembered through the configured memory age (current seed bar only when memory is disabled); clear on invalid flow or opposing VFI/VSA initiative
Calculation: Baseline long structure = valid baseline long seed AND close > its trigger AND close > open AND close - open >= confirmation body ATR multiple * Prior ATR; short mirrors this
Calculation: Long continuation setup = continuation enabled AND (frozen-seed route: valid frozen long seed, a later close > its trigger, long body confirmation, z-score <= maximum continuation deviation, accepted-close count >= required count; refreshing-seed route: valid baseline long seed, baseline long structure, close > VWAP, z-score <= maximum continuation deviation, accepted-close count >= required count and seed age + 1 >= required count) AND (continuation sweep not required OR selected seed was swept) AND (flow confirmation disabled OR bullish VFI AND bullish VSA initiative AND (continuation shift not required OR Remembered turn is up)) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR Supertrend direction is bullish) AND (HTF filter disabled OR T1 structure bias bullish)
Calculation: Short continuation setup = long continuation setup with bearish directions, close < VWAP, z-score >= -maximum continuation deviation, and short-side seed, trigger, sweep, acceptance, flow and body conditions
Calculation: Reversal flow gate for long = flow confirmation disabled OR (valid baseline long seed AND NOT (bearish VFI AND bearish VSA initiative) AND NOT (VSA effort >= reversal opposing-effort threshold AND bearish VSA initiative) AND (Remembered turn is up OR (VFI turn up AND (CVD requirement disabled OR volume invalid OR CVD turn up))); short mirrors this
Calculation: Long reversal setup = reversal enabled AND valid baseline long seed AND (reversal sweep not required OR seed was swept) AND Reversal flow gate for long AND seed excursion recorded AND lower reversal z bound <= VWAP z-score <= upper reversal z bound AND baseline long structure AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR Supertrend direction is bullish); short mirrors this using its separate bounds and bearish conditions
Calculation: Long rearm gate = repeat filter disabled OR prior signal side is not long OR (selected continuation seed for a continuation setup, otherwise baseline long seed, began at or after an armed confirmed same-leg swing-low pivot). The pivot must follow the prior long signal and have depth from the pre-pivot leg high >= repeat depth ATR multiple * Prior ATR at the pivot; disarm if low breaches it by an instrument minimum price increment. Short mirrors this using a swing-high pivot and pre-pivot leg low
Long: Common entry gate AND NOT (Short continuation setup OR Short reversal setup) AND ((Long continuation setup AND Long rearm gate) OR (NOT Long continuation setup AND Long reversal setup AND Long rearm gate)), at a confirmed chart-bar close
Short: Common entry gate AND NOT (Long continuation setup OR Long reversal setup) AND ((Short continuation setup AND Short rearm gate) OR (NOT Short continuation setup AND Short reversal setup AND Short rearm gate)), at a confirmed chart-bar close
Long: On a realtime forming chart bar, the long condition above AND no prior provisional attempt this bar AND no context reset AND low >= the selected long seed low - instrument minimum price increment; invalidate the attempt if eligibility ceases and do not rearm it that bar
Short: On a realtime forming chart bar, the short condition above AND no prior provisional attempt this bar AND no context reset AND high <= the selected short seed high + instrument minimum price increment; invalidate the attempt if eligibility ceases and do not rearm it that bar
