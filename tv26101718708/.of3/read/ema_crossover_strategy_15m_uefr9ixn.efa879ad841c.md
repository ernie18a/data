Calculation: EMA intersection = F[1] + ((F[1]-S[1])/((F[1]-S[1])-(F-S)))*(F-F[1]) when abs((F[1]-S[1])-(F-S)) > tolerance, otherwise unavailable; F and S are the shorter- and longer-lookback EMA(close) values from the current, still-forming T1 bar sampled on chart bars with lookahead off
Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar) crosses above EMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar) AND close > EMA intersection AND close > VWAP(hlc3) AND ADX(lookback, lookback, T1 current bar) >= threshold AND abs(EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar) - EMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar)) >= ATR(lookback, T1 current bar) * separation multiplier
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar) crosses below EMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar) AND close < EMA intersection AND close < VWAP(hlc3) AND ADX(lookback, lookback, T1 current bar) >= threshold AND abs(EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar) - EMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar)) >= ATR(lookback, T1 current bar) * separation multiplier
Exit: long, low <= EMA intersection at entry OR high >= entry price + (entry price - EMA intersection at entry) * reward/risk ratio
Exit: short, high >= EMA intersection at entry OR low <= entry price - (EMA intersection at entry - entry price) * reward/risk ratio
