Calculation: Gate levels = On chart bars, use T1 values requested with lookahead off (completed T1 bars historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime). For each eligible T1 close in the lookback whose forward window ends before the latest T1 bar, create a bullish candidate at that close with force (maximum high in the forward window − close) / ATR(ATR lookback, T1, candidate bar) if the excursion exceeds acceleration threshold × ATR; create a bearish candidate with force (close − minimum low in the forward window) / ATR under the same threshold. Process candidate bars from most recent to oldest, bullish before bearish at each bar. Merge each candidate into the first existing cluster within cluster width × latest T1 ATR, or start a cluster; update its price to the arithmetic mean of candidate prices, total force to the sum of forces, signed force to bullish force minus bearish force, and hit count by one. Retain up to the max-level strongest clusters that meet the applicable minimum-hit parameter, selected by T1 duration relative to two duration-boundary parameters.
Long: A retained gate level has signed force / total force > direction threshold
Short: A retained gate level has signed force / total force < −direction threshold
