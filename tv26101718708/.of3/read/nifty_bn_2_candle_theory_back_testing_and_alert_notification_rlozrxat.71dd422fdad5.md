Calculation: VWAP = cumulative hlc3 * volume divided by cumulative volume on chart bars, resetting both sums on the chart bar after the first chart bar of a new T1 period, detected from the T1 current forming bar's start time
Calculation: long stop = low of the chart bar immediately preceding the entry-signal bar, retained during the position
Calculation: short stop = high of the chart bar immediately preceding the entry-signal bar, retained during the position
Long: RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) > long lower RSI threshold AND RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) <= long upper RSI threshold AND close > VWAP AND close > Supertrend lower band(Supertrend source, ATR lookback, ATR multiplier, wick setting) AND close > VWMA(VMA source, VMA lookback) AND close > PSAR(start parameter, increment parameter, maximum parameter) AND volume > volume cutoff AND previous chart bar's volume > volume cutoff AND close > open AND previous chart bar's close > previous chart bar's open AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is outside the configured session evaluated using T2's current bar) AND datetime is within the configured start and end times AND position is flat, on a confirmed chart bar
Short: RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) < short upper RSI threshold AND RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) >= short lower RSI threshold AND close < VWAP AND close < Supertrend upper band(Supertrend source, ATR lookback, ATR multiplier, wick setting) AND close < VWMA(VMA source, VMA lookback) AND close < PSAR(start parameter, increment parameter, maximum parameter) AND volume > volume cutoff AND previous chart bar's volume > volume cutoff AND close < open AND previous chart bar's close < previous chart bar's open AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is outside the configured session evaluated using T2's current bar) AND datetime is within the configured start and end times AND position is flat, on a confirmed chart bar
Long: Supertrend lower band(Supertrend source, ATR lookback, ATR multiplier, wick setting) crosses above VWAP AND VWMA(VMA source, VMA lookback) crosses above VWAP AND RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) > long lower RSI threshold AND RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) <= long upper RSI threshold AND volume > volume cutoff AND previous chart bar's volume > volume cutoff AND close > open AND previous chart bar's close > previous chart bar's open
Short: Supertrend upper band(Supertrend source, ATR lookback, ATR multiplier, wick setting) crosses below VWAP AND VWMA(VMA source, VMA lookback) crosses below VWAP AND RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) < short upper RSI threshold AND RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) >= short lower RSI threshold AND volume > volume cutoff AND previous chart bar's volume > volume cutoff AND close < open AND previous chart bar's close < previous chart bar's open
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + (entry price - long stop) * risk/reward parameter
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price - (short stop - entry price) * risk/reward parameter
