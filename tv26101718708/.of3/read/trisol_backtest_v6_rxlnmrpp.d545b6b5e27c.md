Calculation: Stop = most recent confirmed pivot low(low, left lookback, right lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: T1 percent change = 100 * (close(T1, latest available bar, possibly forming) - close(T1, preceding completed bar)) / close(T1, preceding completed bar), sampled on each chart bar
Long: close >= min(HMA(close, shorter lookback), HMA(close, longer lookback)) AND close <= max(HMA(close, shorter lookback), HMA(close, longer lookback)) AND MACD histogram(close, fast lookback longer than slow lookback, signal lookback) < 0 AND crossover(RSI(close, RSI lookback), WMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), longer RSI smoothing lookback)) AND crossover(SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), shorter RSI smoothing lookback), WMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), longer RSI smoothing lookback))
Long: close >= min(HMA(close, shorter lookback), HMA(close, longer lookback)) AND close <= max(HMA(close, shorter lookback), HMA(close, longer lookback)) AND MACD histogram(close, fast lookback longer than slow lookback, signal lookback) < 0 AND crossover(RSI(close, RSI lookback), WMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), longer RSI smoothing lookback)) AND crossover(SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), shorter RSI smoothing lookback), WMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), longer RSI smoothing lookback)) AND position is flat AND Stop is defined AND Highest(high[1], lookback bars) is defined
Long: position is long AND T1 percent change <= -downward threshold AND a partial exit has occurred during the current position AND no re-entry has occurred since that partial exit
Exit: long, low <= Stop OR high >= Highest(high[1], lookback bars) OR (T1 percent change >= upward threshold AND no partial exit has occurred during the current position)
