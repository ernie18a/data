Calculation: Session VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(sum(volume × hlc3²) / sum(volume) − session VWAP²) since session start
Calculation: Upper/lower VWAP bands = session VWAP ± band multiplier × session VWAP standard deviation
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum(hlc3 × volume) / cumulative volume from the anchor bar onward

Calculation: Bounce, retest, and anchored-VWAP risk = max(entry price − entry-bar low + stop buffer × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar, minimum risk × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar) for long; max(entry-bar high + stop buffer × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar − entry price, minimum risk × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar) for short
Calculation: Band-reversion risk = max(entry price − min(entry-bar low, lowest low during the lower-band excursion) + stop buffer × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar, minimum risk × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar) for long; max(max(entry-bar high, highest high during the upper-band excursion) + stop buffer × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar − entry price, minimum risk × ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar) for short
Calculation: Stop = entry price − setup risk for long; entry price + setup risk for short
Calculation: Bounce, retest, and anchored-VWAP target = entry price + target multiple × setup risk for long; entry price − target multiple × setup risk for short
Calculation: Band-reversion target = session VWAP at entry
Exit: long, after the entry bar on a confirmed bar when no session-end exit is due, open ≤ stop (exit at open) OR (open > stop AND low ≤ stop) OR (low > stop AND high ≥ target); stop takes priority over target
Exit: short, after the entry bar on a confirmed bar when no session-end exit is due, open ≥ stop (exit at open) OR (open < stop AND high ≥ stop) OR (high < stop AND low ≤ target); stop takes priority over target
