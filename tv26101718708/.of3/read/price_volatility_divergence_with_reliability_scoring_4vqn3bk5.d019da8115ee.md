Calculation: fear = IV, or IV - EMA(IV, vol length), or (IV / 100)^2, or (IV / 100)^2 - realized variance, as selected by fear measure, where IV = implied volatility index close
Calculation: osc = smoother(zc, smoothing length) if smoothing length > 1, else zc; zc = zscore(-fear, z window) in single-window mode, or the median of zscore(-fear, max(window floor, floor(z window / 2))), zscore(-fear, z window) and zscore(-fear, 2 * z window) in consensus mode; zscore(x, w) = (x - SMA(x, w)) / STDEV(x, w), or 0 if STDEV(x, w) is not positive, in classic mode, or (x - median(x, w)) / (1.4826 * MAD(x, w)) in robust mode; smoother = EMA or Ehlers UltimateSmoother by smoother type
Calculation: long baseline = 100 * SUM over the last baseline window bars of (osc[look-ahead] <= -stretch band AND price >= price[look-ahead] + (favourable move + cost) * ATR(ATR length)[look-ahead]) / SUM over the same bars of (osc[look-ahead] <= -stretch band), NA if the window is not full or the denominator is zero; [look-ahead] means that many bars before each summed bar
Calculation: short baseline = 100 * SUM over the last baseline window bars of (osc[look-ahead] >= stretch band AND price <= price[look-ahead] - (favourable move + cost) * ATR(ATR length)[look-ahead]) / SUM over the same bars of (osc[look-ahead] >= stretch band), NA if the window is not full or the denominator is zero; [look-ahead] means that many bars before each summed bar
Calculation: regular bullish divergence = pivot low of osc confirmed at this bar AND prior pivot low exists AND bars from prior pivot low to this pivot low <= max pivot gap AND ABS(osc at pivot low - osc at prior pivot low) >= min gap multiple * STDEV(osc, z window) AND osc at pivot low > osc at prior pivot low AND price at pivot low < price at prior pivot low AND (NOT triple-pivot mode OR (second prior pivot low exists AND osc at prior pivot low > osc at second prior pivot low AND price at prior pivot low < price at second prior pivot low)); pivot low = confirmed low of osc with pivot bars on each side, located pivot bars back, osc and price read at that bar
Calculation: hidden bullish divergence = pivot low of osc confirmed at this bar AND prior pivot low exists AND bars from prior pivot low to this pivot low <= max pivot gap AND ABS(osc at pivot low - osc at prior pivot low) >= min gap multiple * STDEV(osc, z window) AND osc at pivot low < osc at prior pivot low AND price at pivot low > price at prior pivot low AND (NOT triple-pivot mode OR (second prior pivot low exists AND osc at prior pivot low < osc at second prior pivot low AND price at prior pivot low > price at second prior pivot low))
Calculation: regular bearish divergence = pivot high of osc confirmed at this bar AND prior pivot high exists AND bars from prior pivot high to this pivot high <= max pivot gap AND ABS(osc at pivot high - osc at prior pivot high) >= min gap multiple * STDEV(osc, z window) AND osc at pivot high < osc at prior pivot high AND price at pivot high > price at prior pivot high AND (NOT triple-pivot mode OR (second prior pivot high exists AND osc at prior pivot high < osc at second prior pivot high AND price at prior pivot high > price at second prior pivot high)); pivot high defined as pivot low with high in place of low

Calculation: signal direction = +1 if fear gate AND regime gate AND time gate AND (regular bullish divergence OR (hidden enabled AND hidden bullish divergence)); -1 if fear gate AND regime gate AND time gate AND (regular bearish divergence OR (hidden enabled AND hidden bearish divergence)); 0 otherwise; where fear gate = fear gate off OR percent rank of fear over percentile window bars >= min fear percentile; regime gate = regime gate off OR (external regime state is within its valid range AND round(external regime state) <= 0); time gate = time gate off OR the time-of-day slot significance test of one-bar price change passes for the current slot (slot minutes, slot level, slot z threshold)
Calculation: track record confirmed = (resolved count >= min resolved signals) AND (100 * wins / resolved count - Wilson half-width(wins, resolved count, deflated z) - mean baseline > 0); over signal events s with signal direction d != 0 at s and s at least look-ahead bars before the current bar: resolved count = number of such events; wins = number of such events with d * (price at look-ahead bars after s - price at s) >= (favourable move + cost) * ATR(ATR length) at s; mean baseline = average, over such events with a defined baseline, of long baseline at s if d = +1 or short baseline at s if d = -1; Wilson half-width in percentage points; deflated z = Bonferroni-deflated two-sided 95% normal quantile for deflation tests
