Calculation: Attention Weights = softmax over k=1..N of -squared Euclidean distance divided by softmax temperature, comparing the current and k-bars-ago feature vectors ((close-close[1])/ATR(ATR lookback) when ATR>0 else 0, (high-low)/ATR(ATR lookback)-1 when ATR>0 else 0, (volume-SMA(volume, volume lookback))/StDev(volume, volume lookback) when StDev>0 else 0).
Calculation: Attention Forecast = sum over k=1..N of Attention Weight[k] * ((close[k-1]-close[k])/ATR(ATR lookback)[k]), using zero when ATR at k is zero.
Calculation: Attention Conviction = clamp(1 + sum over k=1..N of Attention Weight[k]*ln(Attention Weight[k]) / ln(N), 0, 1).
Calculation: Trend Score = EMA(Attention Forecast, score-smoothing lookback).
Calculation: R Ledger = reset to zero at each T1 boundary; after updating any active simulated trade on each confirmed chart bar, start a simulated trade at the signal close only if none is active, with stop distance equal to stop multiplier times ATR(ATR lookback) at entry, floored at the instrument minimum tick, and target distance equal to stop distance times reward-risk. On each later confirmed chart bar, check the stop before the target: for a long, low at or below stop books -1R, otherwise high at or above target books reward-risk R; for a short, high at or above stop books -1R, otherwise low at or below target books reward-risk R. Clear the simulated trade when either level is reached.
Long: Trend Score crosses above zero.
Short: Trend Score crosses below zero.
Long: Trend Score crosses above the score threshold AND Attention Conviction exceeds the conviction floor AND (HTF confirmation is disabled OR chart close is above EMA(close, EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars) AND the configured session filter passes AND the bar is outside enabled news blackout windows AND R Ledger is greater than -(configured daily-loss percentage / risk-per-trade percentage) AND the signal count in the current T1 period is below its cap AND the most recent signal was not long AND the number of chart bars since the most recent signal is at least the cooldown AND the chart bar is confirmed.
Short: Trend Score crosses below the negative score threshold AND Attention Conviction exceeds the conviction floor AND (HTF confirmation is disabled OR chart close is below EMA(close, EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars) AND the configured session filter passes AND the bar is outside enabled news blackout windows AND R Ledger is greater than -(configured daily-loss percentage / risk-per-trade percentage) AND the signal count in the current T1 period is below its cap AND the most recent signal was not short AND the number of chart bars since the most recent signal is at least the cooldown AND the chart bar is confirmed.
