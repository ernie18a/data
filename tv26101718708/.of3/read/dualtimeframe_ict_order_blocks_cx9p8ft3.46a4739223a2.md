Calculation: Primary OB zones = On primary timeframe bars (T1 when custom, otherwise chart bars; T1 uses its still-forming bar in real time and completed bars historically), create bullish zones when close > the latest uncrossed confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback), and bearish zones when close < the latest uncrossed confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback); when FVG confirmation is required, also require low > high[FVG lookback] for bullish zones or high < low[FVG lookback] for bearish zones. Search up to the OB search limit among bars after the pivot and before the break, and select the eligible lowest-low bullish candidate or highest-high bearish candidate; candidates are unrestricted when the liquidity-sweep filter is off, otherwise require candidate low < a prior confirmed PivotLow or candidate high > a prior confirmed PivotHigh. Use the candidate candle's high-low range, or, when multi-candle zones are enabled, the full range from candidate through pivot. If an oversized multi-candle zone is disallowed, replace it with the contiguous bearish/doji sequence from the bullish candidate or bullish/doji sequence from the bearish candidate, falling back to the candidate candle if still oversized; reject zones exceeding maximum ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) unless oversized zones are allowed. Invalidate bullish zones when the configured wick/body low < bottom and bearish zones when the configured wick/body high > top; breakers reverse effective direction. Keep the configured newest primary zones per direction; disable an invalidated zone overlapping a newer active zone of the same timeframe; combine overlapping time-price rectangles of the same timeframe and effective direction when overlap percentage > overlap threshold, unless breaker zones are hidden; use union bounds and the configured combination limit, and mark a combined zone broken if both inputs are breakers or its effective-direction wick breach occurs.
Calculation: Primary OB signal times = For each valid, non-breaker selected or combined primary zone, use the earliest chart bar within the signal history limit with datetime > zone creation and datetime <= zone render end (dynamic through the current bar or fixed from zone start by the configured extension), low <= bottom + lower entry-band fraction × (top - bottom), and high >= bottom + upper entry-band fraction × (top - bottom). A combined zone uses the earliest stored constituent signal time when present; otherwise use its own earliest qualifying bar.
Long: Chart bar datetime = a bullish primary OB signal time
Short: Chart bar datetime = a bearish primary OB signal time
