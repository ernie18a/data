Calculation: AnchorEvent = (anchor method is calendar AND a new calendar period of T1 starts on this bar) OR (anchor method is earnings AND an earnings announcement is on this bar) OR (anchor method is dividends AND a dividend event is on this bar) OR (anchor method is splits AND a split event is on this bar) OR (anchor method is custom date AND this bar is the first bar at or after the custom date-time) OR ((anchor method is pivot low OR anchor method is latest pivot) AND PivotLow(low, pivot left length, pivot right length) is confirmed on this bar) OR ((anchor method is pivot high OR anchor method is latest pivot) AND PivotHigh(high, pivot left length, pivot right length) is confirmed on this bar) OR (anchor method is volume high AND volume > 0 AND volume = Highest(volume, volume lookback)) OR (anchor method is volume spike AND (LN(max(volume, 1)) - SMA(LN(max(volume, 1)), volume lookback)[1]) / STDEV(LN(max(volume, 1)), volume lookback)[1] >= volume spike threshold) OR (anchor method is gap AND (open > high[1] * (1 + min gap percent / 100) OR open < low[1] * (1 - min gap percent / 100)))
Calculation: AnchorOrigin = the bar of the latest true AnchorEvent, shifted back by pivot right length when anchor method is pivot low, pivot high, or latest pivot
Calculation: AnchoredVWAP = volume-weighted average of ((high + low + close) / 3) over bars from AnchorOrigin through the current bar
Calculation: VolWeightedSD = sqrt(max(sum over bars from AnchorOrigin through the current bar of ((high + low + close) / 3)^2 * volume / sum of volume over the same bars - AnchoredVWAP^2, 0))
Calculation: DevSamples = the most recent max samples values of (((high + low + close) / 3) - AnchoredVWAP) / AnchoredVWAP * 100, one per bar from AnchorOrigin through the previous bar, each using the AnchoredVWAP of its own bar
Calculation: Percentile(p) = p-th percentile of DevSamples, p in percent, by linear interpolation between adjacent sorted values
Calculation: UpperOuter = AnchoredVWAP * (1 + Percentile(outer percentile) / 100) if band method is empirical percentile AND sample count of DevSamples >= min samples; AnchoredVWAP * (1 + outer multiplier / 100) if band method is percentage; AnchoredVWAP + VolWeightedSD * outer multiplier otherwise
Calculation: LowerOuter = AnchoredVWAP * (1 + Percentile(100 - outer percentile) / 100) if band method is empirical percentile AND sample count of DevSamples >= min samples; AnchoredVWAP * (1 - outer multiplier / 100) if band method is percentage; AnchoredVWAP - VolWeightedSD * outer multiplier otherwise
Long: close crosses above AnchoredVWAP AND (NOT volume confirmation enabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume confirmation length) * volume confirmation multiplier) AND bars since AnchorOrigin >= min bars after anchor AND NOT AnchorEvent
Short: close crosses below AnchoredVWAP AND (NOT volume confirmation enabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume confirmation length) * volume confirmation multiplier) AND bars since AnchorOrigin >= min bars after anchor AND NOT AnchorEvent
Long: close[1] > AnchoredVWAP[1] AND low <= AnchoredVWAP AND close > AnchoredVWAP AND bars since AnchorOrigin >= min bars after anchor AND NOT AnchorEvent
Short: close[1] < AnchoredVWAP[1] AND high >= AnchoredVWAP AND close < AnchoredVWAP AND bars since AnchorOrigin >= min bars after anchor AND NOT AnchorEvent
Short: close[1] > UpperOuter[1] AND close <= UpperOuter AND bars since AnchorOrigin >= min bars after anchor AND NOT AnchorEvent
Long: close[1] < LowerOuter[1] AND close >= LowerOuter AND bars since AnchorOrigin >= min bars after anchor AND NOT AnchorEvent
