Calculation: Long-lookback cRVOL = current session’s cumulative volume through the current, possibly forming chart bar divided by the mean cumulative volume through the same chart-bar slot in prior completed sessions over the long lookback; use configured active-contract history when its minimum-history requirement is met, otherwise continuous history
Calculation: Short-lookback cRVOL = current session’s cumulative volume through the current, possibly forming chart bar divided by the mean cumulative volume through the same chart-bar slot in prior completed sessions over the short lookback; use configured active-contract history when its minimum-history requirement is met, otherwise continuous history
Calculation: Upward breakout = on an intraday chart during the configured session and before the configured cutoff, the current chart-bar close crosses above an enabled previous completed T1-bar high, completed opening-range high, or enabled manual level; each level can trigger once upward per T1 period
Calculation: Downward breakout = on an intraday chart during the configured session and before the configured cutoff, the current chart-bar close crosses below an enabled previous completed T1-bar low, completed opening-range low, or enabled manual level; each level can trigger once downward per T1 period
Long: Upward breakout AND long-lookback cRVOL ≥ in-play threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL ≥ short-lookback confirmation threshold
Long: Upward breakout AND long-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold
Long: Upward breakout AND NOT (long-lookback cRVOL ≥ in-play threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL ≥ short-lookback confirmation threshold) AND NOT (long-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold)
Short: Downward breakout AND long-lookback cRVOL ≥ in-play threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL ≥ short-lookback confirmation threshold
Short: Downward breakout AND long-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold
Short: Downward breakout AND NOT (long-lookback cRVOL ≥ in-play threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL ≥ short-lookback confirmation threshold) AND NOT (long-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold AND short-lookback cRVOL < baseline threshold)
