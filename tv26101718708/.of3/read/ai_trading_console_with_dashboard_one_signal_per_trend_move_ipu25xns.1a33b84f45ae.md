Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum high during the session’s opening-range interval
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum low during the session’s opening-range interval
Calculation: EMA bullish = EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: HTF bullish = close of the still-forming T1 bar, sampled on chart bars, > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Stop = for a long, entry price − ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) at entry × initial-stop multiplier, raised to the highest close − ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × trailing-stop multiplier since entry when trailing is enabled; for a short, entry price + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) at entry × initial-stop multiplier, lowered to the lowest close + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × trailing-stop multiplier since entry when trailing is enabled
Long: buy enabled AND no open position AND last entry direction is not long AND (ORB disabled OR close crosses above opening-range high) AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA bullish) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, lookback) > long RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(high, low, close, lookback, smoothing) > ADX threshold) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close < VIX threshold) AND (HTF filter disabled OR HTF bullish)
Short: sell enabled AND no open position AND last entry direction is not short AND (ORB disabled OR close crosses below opening-range low) AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, lookback) < short RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(high, low, close, lookback, smoothing) > ADX threshold) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close < VIX threshold) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close of the still-forming T1 bar, sampled on chart bars, < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars)
Long: EMA filter disabled OR EMA bullish
Short: EMA filter enabled AND NOT EMA bullish
Long: HTF bullish
Short: NOT HTF bullish
Exit: long, close < Stop
Exit: short, close > Stop
Calculation: opening-range high = highest traded-instrument high during the opening range
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest traded-instrument low during the opening range
Calculation: long stop = maximum of the previous long stop and current traded-instrument close minus ATR(traded-instrument high, low, close, ATR period) times the trailing-stop multiple, initialized to entry close minus ATR(traded-instrument high, low, close, ATR period) times the initial-stop multiple
Calculation: short stop = minimum of the previous short stop and current traded-instrument close plus ATR(traded-instrument high, low, close, ATR period) times the trailing-stop multiple, initialized to entry close plus ATR(traded-instrument high, low, close, ATR period) times the initial-stop multiple
Long: buy signals enabled AND flat AND previous entry direction was not long AND (opening-range filter disabled OR traded-instrument close crosses above opening-range high) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR EMA(traded-instrument close, fast EMA period) > EMA(traded-instrument close, slow EMA period)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(traded-instrument close, RSI period) > long RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(traded-instrument high, low, close, DI length, smoothing length) > minimum ADX) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close < maximum VIX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR T1 close > EMA(T1 close, T1 EMA period))
Short: sell signals enabled AND flat AND previous entry direction was not short AND (opening-range filter disabled OR traded-instrument close crosses below opening-range low) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR EMA(traded-instrument close, fast EMA period) < EMA(traded-instrument close, slow EMA period)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(traded-instrument close, RSI period) < short RSI threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(traded-instrument high, low, close, DI length, smoothing length) > minimum ADX) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close < maximum VIX) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR T1 close < EMA(T1 close, T1 EMA period))
Exit: Long, traded-instrument close < long stop
Exit: Short, traded-instrument close > short stop
