Calculation: midnight open = open of the first chart bar of each calendar day in the parameterized timezone
Calculation: upper boundary = midnight open + buffer-tick parameter × tick size
Calculation: lower boundary = midnight open − buffer-tick parameter × tick size
Long: On a chart bar within any enabled session window, while flat and before any trade in the current uninterrupted enabled-session period, after a bar in that period has high ≥ lower boundary and low ≤ upper boundary, close > upper boundary
Short: On a chart bar within any enabled session window, while flat and before any trade in the current uninterrupted enabled-session period, after a bar in that period has high ≥ lower boundary and low ≤ upper boundary, close < lower boundary
Exit: long, (take-profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price + take-profit ticks × tick size) OR (initial stop enabled AND low ≤ entry price − stop-loss ticks × tick size) OR (trailing stop enabled AND highest high since entry ≥ entry price + trailing activation ticks × tick size AND low ≤ highest high since entry) OR (highest high since entry ≥ entry price + breakeven activation ticks × tick size AND low ≤ entry price + breakeven offset ticks × tick size)
Exit: short, (take-profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price − take-profit ticks × tick size) OR (initial stop enabled AND high ≥ entry price + stop-loss ticks × tick size) OR (trailing stop enabled AND lowest low since entry ≤ entry price − trailing activation ticks × tick size AND high ≥ lowest low since entry) OR (lowest low since entry ≤ entry price − breakeven activation ticks × tick size AND high ≥ entry price − breakeven offset ticks × tick size)
