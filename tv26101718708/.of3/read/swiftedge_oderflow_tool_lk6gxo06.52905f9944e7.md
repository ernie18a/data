Calculation: Bar delta = on each chart bar, sum the volume of each T1 bar within it as positive when that T1 bar's close is greater than or equal to its open and negative otherwise; use T1 bar values as they stand, including a T1 bar still forming. If no T1 bars are available, use chart-bar volume as positive when chart close is greater than or equal to chart open and negative otherwise.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of bar delta on chart bars
