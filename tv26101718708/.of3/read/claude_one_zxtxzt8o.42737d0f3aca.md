Calculation: Long Chandelier stop = if close[1] > previous long stop then max(highest(high, chandelier lookback) - ATR(chandelier lookback) * Chandelier multiplier, previous long stop) else highest(high, chandelier lookback) - ATR(chandelier lookback) * Chandelier multiplier; initial previous stop is zero
Calculation: Short Chandelier stop = if close[1] < previous short stop then min(lowest(low, chandelier lookback) + ATR(chandelier lookback) * Chandelier multiplier, previous short stop) else lowest(low, chandelier lookback) + ATR(chandelier lookback) * Chandelier multiplier; initial previous stop is zero
Long: position is flat AND (session filter is disabled OR bar time is within the configured GMT weekday session) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) >= minimum ADX) AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND (abs(low - EMA(close, fast lookback)) <= ATR(stop lookback) * pullback distance OR low <= EMA(close, fast lookback)) AND lowest(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI dip lookback) <= oversold level AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above long trigger level AND close > open
Short: position is flat AND (session filter is disabled OR bar time is within the configured GMT weekday session) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) >= minimum ADX) AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND (abs(high - EMA(close, fast lookback)) <= ATR(stop lookback) * pullback distance OR high >= EMA(close, fast lookback)) AND highest(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI dip lookback) >= overbought level AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below short trigger level AND close < open
Exit: Long, low <= max(entry price - ATR(stop lookback) * initial stop multiplier, Long Chandelier stop) OR (RSI exit enabled AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below neutral RSI level) OR bar time is at or after configured GMT close hour OR (session filter enabled AND bar time is outside the configured GMT weekday session)
Exit: Short, high >= min(entry price + ATR(stop lookback) * initial stop multiplier, Short Chandelier stop) OR (RSI exit enabled AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above neutral RSI level) OR bar time is at or after configured GMT close hour OR (session filter enabled AND bar time is outside the configured GMT weekday session)
