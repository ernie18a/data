Calculation: Channel ATR = ATR(high, low, close, channel ATR lookback) on chart bars.
Calculation: Trend efficiency = Efficiency Ratio(close, trend efficiency lookback) on chart bars.
Calculation: Adaptive basis = KAMA(close, adaptive basis lookback, faster smoothing length, slower smoothing length) on chart bars.
Calculation: Channel boundaries = adaptive basis ± channel ATR × (base width + (1 − clamp(trend efficiency, 0, 1)) × low-efficiency expansion).
Calculation: Adaptive trend state = bullish when close is above the adaptive basis, the adaptive basis change over the slope lookback divided by channel ATR is at least the minimum positive slope, and trend efficiency is at least its minimum; bearish when close is below the basis, normalized slope is at most the negative minimum slope, and trend efficiency meets its minimum. From a neutral state, choose bullish if its condition holds, otherwise bearish if its condition holds, otherwise remain neutral. From bullish, remain bullish until close is below the lower channel boundary for the trend-break confirmation lookback consecutive chart bars, then become bearish if the bearish condition holds or neutral otherwise. From bearish, remain bearish until close is above the upper boundary for that many consecutive bars, then become bullish if the bullish condition holds or neutral otherwise. Reset state age when state changes; a non-neutral state is confirmed after it persists for the trend-break confirmation lookback.
Calculation: Bar confirmation gate = true on a confirmed chart bar when bar-close confirmation is enabled, and true on every chart bar otherwise.
Calculation: Zone half-width = max(ATR(high, low, close, zone ATR lookback) on T1 bars, taking the last completed T1 value onto chart bars, or channel ATR if unavailable, multiplied by the zone-width multiplier; instrument minimum tick size multiplied by its separate multiplier).
Calculation: Zone deduplication tolerance = zone half-width × deduplication multiplier.
Calculation: Nearest resistance = the lowest of the last completed T1 high, the highest highs over shorter and longer T1 lookbacks ending at the last completed T1 bar, and the last completed T2, T3, and T4 highs that is greater than chart close plus zone deduplication tolerance. Calculate these values on their respective timeframe bars and take their last completed values onto chart bars.
Calculation: Nearest support = the highest of the last completed T1 low, the lowest lows over shorter and longer T1 lookbacks ending at the last completed T1 bar, and the last completed T2, T3, and T4 lows that is less than chart close minus zone deduplication tolerance. Calculate these values on their respective timeframe bars and take their last completed values onto chart bars.
Calculation: Pivot archive = the latest three confirmed Pivot Highs and latest three confirmed Pivot Lows, using separate left and right pivot lookbacks, with their pivot-bar indices.
Calculation: Wedge boundaries = straight-line projections through the latest two pivot highs and latest two pivot lows; wedge start is the later of the second latest high and low pivot bars. Measure both projected lines, their slopes, and the distance between them at wedge start and the current bar.
Calculation: Ascending wedge candidate = wedge age is no greater than its maximum age; the latest two pivot highs and lows are each rising; both projected slopes are positive and the lower slope is greater than the upper slope; current width exceeds minimum tick size; and current width is no greater than start width multiplied by the compression parameter.
Calculation: Descending wedge candidate = wedge age is no greater than its maximum age; the latest two pivot highs and lows are each falling; both projected slopes are negative and the upper slope is less negative than the lower slope; current width exceeds minimum tick size; and current width is no greater than start width multiplied by the compression parameter.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders neckline = the average of the latest two pivot lows; inverse head-and-shoulders neckline = the average of the latest two pivot highs.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders candidate = the latest three pivot highs are chronological, the outer highs differ by no more than the shoulder-tolerance parameter times channel ATR, the middle high exceeds both outer highs by at least the head-dominance parameter times channel ATR, and adaptive trend state is bullish.
Calculation: Inverse head-and-shoulders candidate = the latest three pivot lows are chronological, the outer lows differ by no more than the shoulder-tolerance parameter times channel ATR, the middle low is below both outer lows by at least the head-dominance parameter times channel ATR, and adaptive trend state is bearish.
Calculation: Bull flag candidate = adaptive trend state is confirmed bullish; the highest high over the flag lookback ending on the previous chart bar minus the lowest low over the pole lookback shifted back by the flag lookback is at least channel ATR times the minimum-pole parameter; the prior flag-range width is at most the pole height times the maximum-retracement parameter; the linear-regression slope of close over the flag lookback is nonpositive; and close is within that prior flag range.
Calculation: Cup-and-handle candidate = adaptive trend state is neutral or bullish; cup depth, calculated as the highest high over the cup lookback ending on the previous chart bar minus the lowest low over the cup lookback including the current bar, is at least channel ATR times the minimum-depth parameter; close and the prior handle high are each no lower than the cup rim minus cup depth times the rim-tolerance parameter; and (cup rim minus the lowest low over the handle lookback including the current bar) divided by cup depth is between zero and the maximum-handle-depth parameter.
Long: adaptive trend state is bullish.
Short: adaptive trend state is bearish.
Long: adaptive trend state changes to bullish AND the bar confirmation gate is true.
Short: adaptive trend state changes to bearish AND the bar confirmation gate is true.
Short: the previous adaptive trend state is bullish AND close is below the lower channel boundary for the trend-break confirmation lookback consecutive chart bars AND the bar confirmation gate is true.
Long: the previous adaptive trend state is bearish AND close is above the upper channel boundary for the trend-break confirmation lookback consecutive chart bars AND the bar confirmation gate is true.
Long: close crosses above nearest resistance plus zone half-width AND the bar confirmation gate is true.
Short: close crosses below nearest support minus zone half-width AND the bar confirmation gate is true.

Long: inverse head-and-shoulders candidate held for the pattern-maturity lookback on consecutive chart bars through the previous bar AND close crosses above the inverse head-and-shoulders neckline AND the bar confirmation gate is true AND at least the shared pattern-cooldown lookback has elapsed since the previous inverse head-and-shoulders break, if any, AND the head pivot differs from the one at the previous break, if any.
Short: ascending wedge candidate held for the pattern-maturity lookback on consecutive chart bars through the previous bar AND close is below the projected lower wedge boundary AND the bar confirmation gate is true AND at least the shared pattern-cooldown lookback has elapsed since the previous ascending-wedge break, if any, AND the wedge starts after the previous ascending-wedge break, if any.
Long: descending wedge candidate held for the pattern-maturity lookback on consecutive chart bars through the previous bar AND close is above the projected upper wedge boundary AND the bar confirmation gate is true AND at least the shared pattern-cooldown lookback has elapsed since the previous descending-wedge break, if any, AND the wedge starts after the previous descending-wedge break, if any.
Long: bull flag candidate held for the pattern-maturity lookback on consecutive chart bars through the previous bar AND close is above the prior flag-range high AND the bar confirmation gate is true AND at least the shared pattern-cooldown lookback has elapsed since the previous bull-flag break, if any.
Long: cup-and-handle candidate held for the pattern-maturity lookback on consecutive chart bars through the previous bar AND close is above the prior handle high AND the bar confirmation gate is true AND at least the shared pattern-cooldown lookback has elapsed since the previous cup-and-handle break, if any.
