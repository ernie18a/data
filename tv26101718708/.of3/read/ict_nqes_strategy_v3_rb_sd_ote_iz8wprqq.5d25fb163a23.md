Calculation: Opening range high and low = high and low of the latest intraday chart bar starting at the New York session open
Calculation: Bullish order block = close > open AND close - open > ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement ATR multiple AND open[1] > close[1]
Calculation: Bearish order block = open > close AND open - close > ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement ATR multiple AND open[1] < close[1]
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high[2] AND close[1] > open[1]
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = high < low[2] AND open[1] > close[1]
Calculation: Bullish rejection block = high > low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum wick fraction AND min(open, close) - low > (high - max(open, close)) * wick dominance multiplier
Calculation: Bearish rejection block = high > low AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum wick fraction AND high - max(open, close) > (min(open, close) - low) * wick dominance multiplier
Calculation: OTE zone = let swing high = Highest(high, OTE lookback), swing low = Lowest(low, OTE lookback), and swing range = swing high - swing low; (close > swing low + swing range * equilibrium ratio AND close >= swing low + swing range * lower OTE retracement AND close <= swing low + swing range * upper OTE retracement) OR (close <= swing low + swing range * equilibrium ratio AND close >= swing high - swing range * upper OTE retracement AND close <= swing high - swing range * lower OTE retracement)
Calculation: Upper first standard-deviation level = open of the latest New York session opening chart bar + StDev(close, standard-deviation lookback) * first standard-deviation multiplier
Calculation: Lower first standard-deviation level = open of the latest New York session opening chart bar - StDev(close, standard-deviation lookback) * first standard-deviation multiplier
