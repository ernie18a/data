Calculation: ORB high and low = high and low of the last completed T1 bar spanning the ORB build session, sampled on chart bars when that session ends
Calculation: candle body percentage = 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0
Calculation: confirmed-entry eligibility = on or after the backtest start date AND ORB ready AND within the entry session AND chart bar confirmed AND no open position AND (one-trade-per-day restriction disabled OR no trade taken that day)
Calculation: possible-alert eligibility = possible alerts enabled AND ORB ready AND within the entry session AND no open position AND (one-trade-per-day restriction disabled OR no trade taken that day)
Calculation: long VWAP/trend filters pass = (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND (T2 trend filter disabled OR (on chart bars, the last completed T2 bar's close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar) AND (EMA slope not required OR EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar) > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2, preceding completed bar)))
Calculation: short VWAP/trend filters pass = (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND (T2 trend filter disabled OR (on chart bars, the last completed T2 bar's close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar) AND (EMA slope not required OR EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar) < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2, preceding completed bar)))
Calculation: long stochastic filter passes = stochastic filter disabled OR (Stochastic %K(stochastic lookback, K smoothing) > Stochastic %D(stochastic lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing) AND Stochastic %K(stochastic lookback, K smoothing) > 50)
Calculation: short stochastic filter passes = stochastic filter disabled OR (Stochastic %K(stochastic lookback, K smoothing) < Stochastic %D(stochastic lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing) AND Stochastic %K(stochastic lookback, K smoothing) < 50)
Calculation: fresh bullish ORB breakout = confirmed-entry eligibility AND standard breakout mode AND close crosses above ORB high + confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND close > open AND candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND long VWAP/trend filters pass
Calculation: fresh bearish ORB breakout = confirmed-entry eligibility AND standard breakout mode AND close crosses below ORB low - confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND close < open AND candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage AND short VWAP/trend filters pass
Calculation: long stop price = ORB low - stop-loss tick buffer in standard breakout mode, or entry-bar low - stop-loss tick buffer in liquidity-sweep mode
Calculation: short stop price = ORB high + stop-loss tick buffer in standard breakout mode, or entry-bar high + stop-loss tick buffer in liquidity-sweep mode
Calculation: long target price = entry price + (entry price - long stop price) * reward-to-risk parameter
Calculation: short target price = entry price - (short stop price - entry price) * reward-to-risk parameter
Long: possible-alert eligibility AND high >= ORB high + possible-alert tick buffer AND long VWAP/trend filters pass AND no long possible alert earlier that day
Short: possible-alert eligibility AND low <= ORB low - possible-alert tick buffer AND short VWAP/trend filters pass AND no short possible alert earlier that day
Long: fresh bullish ORB breakout AND retest disabled AND two-close confirmation disabled AND long stochastic filter passes
Long: fresh bullish ORB breakout on the immediately preceding chart bar AND two-close confirmation enabled AND retest disabled AND confirmed-entry eligibility AND close > ORB high + confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND long VWAP/trend filters pass AND long stochastic filter passes
Long: retest enabled AND confirmed-entry eligibility AND a fresh bullish ORB breakout armed a retest within the maximum retest chart bars AND (two-close confirmation disabled OR the immediately following chart bar had confirmed-entry eligibility, closed above ORB high + confirmed-breakout tick buffer, and passed long VWAP/trend filters) AND no close since arming was below ORB high AND current bar is after the arming bar AND low <= ORB high + confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND close > ORB high + confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND close > open AND long VWAP/trend filters pass AND long stochastic filter passes
Long: liquidity-sweep mode AND confirmed-entry eligibility AND low < ORB low AND close > ORB low AND (high <= low OR 100 * (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum rejection-wick percentage) AND long VWAP/trend filters pass AND long stochastic filter passes
Short: fresh bearish ORB breakout AND retest disabled AND two-close confirmation disabled AND short stochastic filter passes
Short: fresh bearish ORB breakout on the immediately preceding chart bar AND two-close confirmation enabled AND retest disabled AND confirmed-entry eligibility AND close < ORB low - confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND short VWAP/trend filters pass AND short stochastic filter passes
Short: retest enabled AND confirmed-entry eligibility AND a fresh bearish ORB breakout armed a retest within the maximum retest chart bars AND (two-close confirmation disabled OR the immediately following chart bar had confirmed-entry eligibility, closed below ORB low - confirmed-breakout tick buffer, and passed short VWAP/trend filters) AND no close since arming was above ORB low AND current bar is after the arming bar AND high >= ORB low - confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND close < ORB low - confirmed-breakout tick buffer AND close < open AND short VWAP/trend filters pass AND short stochastic filter passes
Short: liquidity-sweep mode AND confirmed-entry eligibility AND high > ORB high AND close < ORB high AND (high <= low OR 100 * (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum rejection-wick percentage) AND short VWAP/trend filters pass AND short stochastic filter passes
Exit: long, low <= long stop price OR high >= long target price OR management session ends
Exit: short, high >= short stop price OR low <= short target price OR management session ends
