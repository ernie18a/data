Calculation: ZScore(x, z-score lookback) = (x - SMA(x, z-score lookback)) / population standard deviation(x, z-score lookback), or the z-score-zero fallback when standard deviation is zero
Calculation: SessionComposite = EMA((ZScore(source, z-score lookback) * price-weight + ZScore(volume, z-score lookback) * volume-weight + ZScore(source - VWAP(HLC3), z-score lookback) * VWAP-distance-weight) / (sum of the three weights when nonzero, otherwise the zero-total-weight denominator), smoothing lookback
Calculation: RollingComposite = EMA((ZScore(source, z-score lookback) * price-weight + ZScore(volume, z-score lookback) * volume-weight + ZScore(source - RollingVWAP(source, volume, rolling-VWAP lookback), z-score lookback) * VWAP-distance-weight) / (sum of the three weights when nonzero, otherwise the zero-total-weight denominator), smoothing lookback
Calculation: OpeningRangeHigh = highest high during the configured opening-range window of the current session, retained afterward and unavailable until a bar exists in that window
Calculation: PremarketHigh = highest high during the configured premarket window of the current session, or unavailable if no premarket bars exist
