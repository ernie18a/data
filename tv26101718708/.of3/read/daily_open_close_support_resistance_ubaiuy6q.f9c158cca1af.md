Calculation: Nearest support/resistance = cluster T1 bar opens and closes on T1 bars, assigning each price to the nearest existing cluster within ATR(ATR length) × merge distance (ties go to the last matching cluster), updating the cluster price to the running mean and incrementing its count; retain clusters with count ≥ minimum touches; nearest support is the highest retained price below close and nearest resistance is the lowest retained price at or above close.
Calculation: Touches nearest support = nearest support exists AND high ≥ nearest support − ATR(ATR length) × touch tolerance AND low ≤ nearest support + ATR(ATR length) × touch tolerance.
Calculation: Touches nearest resistance = nearest resistance exists AND high ≥ nearest resistance − ATR(ATR length) × touch tolerance AND low ≤ nearest resistance + ATR(ATR length) × touch tolerance.
Long: (Touches nearest support AND NOT Touches nearest support[1]) OR (nearest resistance[1] exists AND close > nearest resistance[1] AND close[1] ≤ nearest resistance[1])
Short: (Touches nearest resistance AND NOT Touches nearest resistance[1]) OR (nearest support[1] exists AND close < nearest support[1] AND close[1] ≥ nearest support[1])
