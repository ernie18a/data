Calculation: Body percentage = if high = low then 0 else 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low)
Calculation: Eligible key levels = configured SMA(close, separate lookbacks) on chart bars; prior completed T1 high/low and prior completed session high/low; configured Fibonacci retracements between confirmed T2 pivot highs/lows, using a separate pivot lookback; and unmitigated T2 pivot highs/lows admitted when T2 volume > SMA(T2 volume, swing-volume lookback) and > previous T2 volume. Calculate T2 pivots and volume SMA on T2 bars and map them to chart bars with lookahead off; the volume test uses the T2 bar still forming.
Long: On chart-bar close, for any eligible level L > 0, ((low <= L + touch buffer OR close[1] < L) AND close > L AND close > open) AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR Body percentage >= minimum body percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, signal-volume lookback) on chart bars) AND the configured bullish VWAP-bias mode passes: off, close > VWAP(hlc3), VWAP(hlc3) > VWAP(hlc3)[slope lookback], or both price and slope conditions
Short: On chart-bar close, for any eligible level L > 0, ((high >= L - touch buffer OR close[1] > L) AND close < L AND close < open) AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR Body percentage >= minimum body percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, signal-volume lookback) on chart bars) AND the configured bearish VWAP-bias mode passes: off, close < VWAP(hlc3), VWAP(hlc3) < VWAP(hlc3)[slope lookback], or both price and slope conditions
Exit: Long, low <= the entry-triggering key level - stop offset OR high >= the next key level above entry price
Exit: Short, high >= the entry-triggering key level + stop offset OR low <= the next key level below entry price
