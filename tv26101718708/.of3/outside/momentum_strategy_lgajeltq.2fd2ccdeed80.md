Calculation: relative strength = traded instrument close / crypto benchmark close (P6) when listed on Coinbase, Binance, FTX, or Bitstamp; otherwise traded instrument close / stock benchmark close (P5)
Long: traded instrument close > highest prior traded instrument close over P1 bars AND traded instrument close - open > P7 × (open - high) AND EMA(traded instrument close, P3) is rising AND RSI(traded instrument close, P8) > P9 AND (traded instrument root is SPX or BTCUSD OR (relative strength > highest prior relative strength over P2 bars AND EMA(relative strength, P3) is rising))
Exit: long, traded instrument close < EMA(traded instrument close, P4) when the position is not in its trending state OR traded instrument close < EMA(traded instrument close, P3) when the position is in its trending state OR relative strength < EMA(relative strength, P3)
