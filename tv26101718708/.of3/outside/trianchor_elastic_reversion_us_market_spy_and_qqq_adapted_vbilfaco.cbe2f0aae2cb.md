Calculation: Close-in-range = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick)
Calculation: Opening gap percentage = 100 × (open − previous close) / previous close on the first regular-session bar of intraday charts and on every non-intraday bar; 0 on other intraday bars
Calculation: Three-index mean robust Z-score = (Robust Z-score(SPY close, Z lookback) + Robust Z-score(QQQ close, Z lookback) + Robust Z-score(IWM close, Z lookback)) / 3
