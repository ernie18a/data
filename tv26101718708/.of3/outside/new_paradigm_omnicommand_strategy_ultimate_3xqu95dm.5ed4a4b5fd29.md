Calculation: Expected move = T1 open × (T1 close of VXN for NQ, VIX for ES or stocks, or GVZ for Gold ÷ 100) × sqrt(1 ÷ trading days per year)
Long: chart close crosses above the configured chart-timeframe SMA or EMA AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average period) × volume-spike multiplier AND (time filter disabled OR time is within configured New York trading hours) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX with the configured period > minimum ADX threshold) AND chart close > EMA(T2 close, higher-timeframe EMA period) AND RSI(chart close, RSI period) < long RSI threshold AND T1 open + expected move − chart close > wall-clearance threshold
Short: chart close crosses below the configured chart-timeframe SMA or EMA AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average period) × volume-spike multiplier AND (time filter disabled OR time is within configured New York trading hours) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX with the configured period > minimum ADX threshold) AND chart close < EMA(T2 close, higher-timeframe EMA period) AND RSI(chart close, RSI period) > short RSI threshold AND chart close − (T1 open − expected move) > wall-clearance threshold
