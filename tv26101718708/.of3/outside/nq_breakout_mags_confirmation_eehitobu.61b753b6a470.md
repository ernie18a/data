Calculation: T3 structure bias = bullish when the latest two T3 Pivot Highs and latest two T3 Pivot Lows are both rising; bearish when both are falling; neutral otherwise
Calculation: NQ breakout levels = enabled previous T1 and T2 highs and lows plus up to the configured number of latest T3 Pivot Highs and Pivot Lows per side, excluding levels farther than the maximum level distance from the setup close and merging levels within the merge distance
Calculation: MAGS confirmation levels = previous T1 high and low plus up to the configured number of latest T3 Pivot Highs and Pivot Lows per side, retaining levels within the configured percentage of the MAGS close and removing levels within the configured percentage of the MAGS close from an earlier level
Calculation: Latest MAGS breakout direction = upward when the MAGS close crosses above a confirmation level by the configured percentage, downward when it crosses below, and otherwise the latest direction remains unchanged
Long: During the configured trading window and below the daily trade limit, the close crosses above an unfinished NQ breakout level plus the breakout distance, or close confirmation is disabled and the high crosses above that threshold; the NQ direction filter permits long; each enabled MAGS confirmation agrees, with structure accepting bullish or neutral T3 bias and breakout requiring the latest MAGS breakout direction to be upward
Short: During the configured trading window and below the daily trade limit, the close crosses below an unfinished NQ breakout level minus the breakout distance, or close confirmation is disabled and the low crosses below that threshold; the NQ direction filter permits short; each enabled MAGS confirmation agrees, with structure accepting bearish or neutral T3 bias and breakout requiring the latest MAGS breakout direction to be downward
