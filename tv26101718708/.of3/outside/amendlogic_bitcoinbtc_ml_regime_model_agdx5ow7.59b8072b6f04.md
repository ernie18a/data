Calculation: logistic model probability = 1 / (1 + exp(-clip(intercept + sum of each learned coefficient times its clipped model feature, plus or minus the sigmoid cap))); initialize the intercept and coefficients at zero; on each confirmed chart bar after the feature-normalization window, prediction horizon, replay span, and warm-up buffer are available, replay the configured number of consecutive prior feature vectors beginning at the prediction horizon, label each by whether the close at its horizon exceeds its sample close, and update the intercept and coefficients by gradient descent using the learning-rate and L2-regularization parameters; trust this probability only after the minimum training-update count is reached, enough resolved predictions fill the reliability window, and their rolling hit rate meets the minimum hit-rate parameter, where a hit means the probability at the prediction horizon earlier was above the probability midpoint exactly when the current close is above the close at that horizon.
Calculation: bullish probability = use the logistic model probability when trusted, otherwise use the fallback probability, then, when demand sensing is enabled, blend that base probability with the sensing probability using the demand-sensing weight; fallback probability = probability midpoint plus fallback scale times (fallback trend weight times traded-instrument direction relative to its T1-equivalent EMA, plus TIP direction relative to its T1-equivalent EMA, plus U.S. Dollar Index direction opposite its T1-equivalent EMA direction) divided by the fallback divisor, with each direction equal to positive one when its stated condition holds and negative one otherwise; sensing probability = logistic of the sensing slope times the sum of the short-return feature and short-return/volume interaction divided by the sensing divisor, using the sigmoid cap.
