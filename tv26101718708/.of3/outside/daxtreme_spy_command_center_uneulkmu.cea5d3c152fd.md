Calculation: opening-range high = highest traded-instrument high during session window P1 each day
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest traded-instrument low during session window P1 each day
Calculation: CVD = daily-reset cumulative sum of volume × sign(close − open), where sign is positive, negative, or zero according to close versus open
Calculation: maximum EMA-distance limit = P9, P10, P11, P12, P13, or P14 for the selected option-expiry bucket, respectively
Calculation: minimum VWAP-distance limit = P15, P16, P17, P18, P19, or P20 for the selected option-expiry bucket, respectively
Calculation: EMA extension pass = extension filter disabled OR abs(close − EMA(close, P2)) / close × 100 ≤ maximum EMA-distance limit
Calculation: VWAP separation pass = chop filter disabled OR abs(close − VWAP((high + low + close) / 3)) / close × 100 ≥ minimum VWAP-distance limit
Calculation: bullish base = close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) AND close > EMA(close, P2) AND (trend filter disabled OR (EMA(close, P2) > its prior chart-bar value AND its prior chart-bar value > its value two chart bars ago AND EMA(close, P2) > EMA(close, P3))) AND EMA extension pass AND VWAP separation pass
Calculation: bearish base = close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) AND close < EMA(close, P2) AND (trend filter disabled OR (EMA(close, P2) < its prior chart-bar value AND its prior chart-bar value < its value two chart bars ago AND EMA(close, P2) < EMA(close, P3))) AND EMA extension pass AND VWAP separation pass
Calculation: bullish ORB retest = opening range complete AND (retest requirement disabled AND close crosses above opening-range high OR retest requirement enabled AND a close crossover above opening-range high with bullish base has armed the setup during the current day, with no qualifying bearish breakout or bullish entry since, AND low ≤ opening-range high AND close > opening-range high)
Calculation: bearish ORB retest = opening range complete AND (retest requirement disabled AND close crosses below opening-range low OR retest requirement enabled AND a close crossunder below opening-range low with bearish base has armed the setup during the current day, with no qualifying bullish breakout or bearish entry since, AND high ≥ opening-range low AND close < opening-range low)
Calculation: bullish pullback rejection = pullback-rejection requirement disabled OR (low ≤ EMA(close, P2) AND close > EMA(close, P2) AND close > open)
Calculation: bearish pullback rejection = pullback-rejection requirement disabled OR (high ≥ EMA(close, P2) AND close < EMA(close, P2) AND close < open)
Option: buy call, bullish ORB retest AND bullish pullback rejection AND bullish base AND the count of true conditions among (close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3), close > EMA(close, P2), EMA(close, P2) > its prior chart-bar value AND its prior chart-bar value > its value two chart bars ago, EMA(close, P2) > EMA(close, P3), bullish ORB retest, bullish pullback rejection, RSI(close, P4) on T1 > P6, RSI(close, P5) on T2 > P6, CVD > its prior chart-bar value, EMA extension pass) ≥ P8 AND (one-signal-per-side-per-day limit disabled OR no earlier call signal during the current day)
Option: buy put, bearish ORB retest AND bearish pullback rejection AND bearish base AND the count of true conditions among (close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3), close < EMA(close, P2), EMA(close, P2) < its prior chart-bar value AND its prior chart-bar value < its value two chart bars ago, EMA(close, P2) < EMA(close, P3), bearish ORB retest, bearish pullback rejection, RSI(close, P4) on T1 < P7, RSI(close, P5) on T2 < P7, CVD < its prior chart-bar value, EMA extension pass) ≥ P8 AND (one-signal-per-side-per-day limit disabled OR no earlier put signal during the current day)
Exit: long call, call signal active during the current day AND ((EMA exit enabled AND close < EMA(close, P2)) OR (VWAP exit enabled AND close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3)))
