Calculation: normalized breadth = (advancing issues - declining issues) / (advancing issues + declining issues + unchanged issues) when advancing and declining issues share a T1 observation, treating unavailable unchanged issues as zero and requiring a positive denominator
Calculation: cumulative breadth = cumulative sum of normalized breadth from January 1 of year P1, adding only available normalized breadth observations
Calculation: confirmed breadth regime = raw regime after P4 consecutive confirmed T1 observations with the same raw regime; raw regime is green when cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2) >= P3 and cumulative breadth exceeds each of the prior P5 observations, amber when cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2) >= P3 and the green condition is false, and red when cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2) < P3
Calculation: bearish breadth divergence = a confirmed chart pivot high in traded instrument price with P6 bars left and P7 bars right, higher than the prior confirmed pivot high, while cumulative breadth at the current pivot is lower than at the prior pivot
Long: current year >= P1 AND latest breadth observation is no more than P8 calendar days old AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P2 AND confirmed breadth regime is green
Exit: long, current year >= P1 AND latest breadth observation is no more than P8 calendar days old AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P2 AND confirmed breadth regime is amber
Short: current year >= P1 AND latest breadth observation is no more than P8 calendar days old AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P2 AND confirmed breadth regime is red
Long: confirmed chart bar with a valid T1 breadth observation AND current year >= P1 AND latest breadth observation is no more than P8 calendar days old AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P2 AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P9 AND cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2) >= P3 AND cumulative breadth exceeds each of the prior P5 observations AND (SMA(normalized breadth, P10) - SMA(SMA(normalized breadth, P10), P9)) / STDEV(SMA(normalized breadth, P10), P9) > P11 AND STDEV(SMA(normalized breadth, P10), P9) > 0 AND close > EMA(close, P12) AND no prior kickoff or more than P13 chart bars since prior kickoff
Short: confirmed chart bar with a valid T1 breadth observation AND current year >= P1 AND latest breadth observation is no more than P8 calendar days old AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P2 AND bearish breadth divergence AND confirmed breadth regime is not green
Short: confirmed chart bar with a valid T1 breadth observation AND current year >= P1 AND latest breadth observation is no more than P8 calendar days old AND valid breadth observations since January 1 of year P1 > P9 AND confirmed breadth regime is red AND (cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2) - SMA(cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2), P9)) / STDEV(cumulative breadth - EMA(cumulative breadth, P2), P9) < P14 AND an outside-green bearish breadth divergence occurred within P15 chart bars AND this condition was false on the prior chart bar
