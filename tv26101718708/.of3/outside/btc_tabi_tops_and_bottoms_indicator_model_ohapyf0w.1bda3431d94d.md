Calculation: Rolling percentile rank(value, lookback) = count of the current and preceding lookback bars whose value is no greater than the current value, divided by lookback.
Calculation: TABI score = EMA of the percentage-scaled weighted mean of: percentile rank(Bitcoin close / SMA(Bitcoin close, long price window)); percentile rank(Bitcoin close / SMA(Bitcoin close, medium price window)); percentile rank((SMA(Bitcoin close, short Pi-cycle window) − Pi-cycle multiplier × SMA(Bitcoin close, long Pi-cycle window)) / SMA(Bitcoin close, long Pi-cycle window)); percentile rank(Bitcoin close / SMA(Bitcoin close, Puell window)); RSI(Bitcoin close, RSI window) normalized to 0–1; percentile rank(log(Bitcoin close) − linear regression(log(Bitcoin close), lookback, current bar)); percentile rank(ATR(Bitcoin close, ATR window) / SMA(ATR(Bitcoin close), ATR mean window)); percentile rank(Bitcoin MVRV Z-Score on T1, or 0.5 if missing); one minus percentile rank(Bitcoin dominance on T1), or 0.5 if missing; percentile rank((Bitcoin close − SMA(Bitcoin close, NUPL window)) / Bitcoin close); percentile rank(SMA(Bitcoin close, holder short window) / SMA(Bitcoin close, holder long window)); percentile rank(clamp(((Bitcoin close / Bitcoin close at the SOPR short horizon) − 1) / (abs((Bitcoin close / Bitcoin close at the SOPR long horizon) − 1) + SOPR epsilon), −SOPR clip bound, SOPR clip bound)); percentile rank(Bitcoin close / sqrt(EMA(Bitcoin close, reserve-risk EMA window) × SMA(Bitcoin close, reserve-risk SMA window))); percentile rank(RSI(Bitcoin close, monthly RSI window) on the previous completed T2 bar, normalized to 0–1, or 0.5 if missing); percentile rank(ETH/BTC close on T1, or 0.5 if missing); percentile rank(Bitcoin market capitalization on T1 / (USDT market capitalization on T1 + USDC market capitalization on T1, or zero if missing + SSR denominator offset)), or 0.5 if Bitcoin or USDT market capitalization is missing; percentile rank(EMA((Coinbase BTC/USD close on T1 − Binance BTC/USDT close on T1) / Binance BTC/USDT close on T1, expressed as a percentage, with zero premium if either price is missing, Coinbase premium EMA window)); percentile rank(SMA(hash rate on T1, or Bitcoin close if hash rate is missing, hash-rate short window) / SMA(the same source, hash-rate long window)). Each metric has its own weight; divide the sum of weighted metrics by the sum of their weights before percentage scaling. Use the shared percentile-rank lookback and the TABI-score EMA smoothing parameter.
Long: TABI score <= accumulation ceiling OR TABI score crosses above neutral boundary
Short: TABI score >= sell-zone floor OR TABI score crosses below neutral boundary
Exit: long, TABI score >= reduce-size floor AND TABI score < sell-zone floor
