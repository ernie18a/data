Calculation: Long re-entry block = active after a long stop-loss exit; clear when the prior bar's traded fast EMA >= traded slow EMA AND NOT(current traded fast EMA < traded slow EMA AND external cryptocurrency Others dominance fast EMA > external cryptocurrency Others dominance slow EMA), or after any new entry.
