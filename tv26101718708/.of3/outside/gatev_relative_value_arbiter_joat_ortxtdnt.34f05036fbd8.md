Calculation: Rolling log-return beta = rolling correlation of traded and peer log returns over the beta lookback × standard deviation of traded log returns over that lookback ÷ standard deviation of peer log returns over that lookback.
Calculation: Peer-adjusted log-price residual = ln(traded closing price) − [SMA(ln(traded closing price), beta lookback) + rolling log-return beta × (ln(peer closing price) − SMA(ln(peer closing price), beta lookback))].
Calculation: Relative-value z-score = (peer-adjusted log-price residual − SMA(peer-adjusted log-price residual, z-score lookback)) ÷ standard deviation(peer-adjusted log-price residual, z-score lookback).
Calculation: Valid peer pair = rolling correlation of traded and peer log returns over the beta lookback ≥ minimum correlation AND standard deviation(peer-adjusted log-price residual, z-score lookback) is positive.
Calculation: Relative-value z-score change = current relative-value z-score − prior-bar relative-value z-score.
Calculation: Chart-mark spacing condition = no prior chart long or short mark OR chart bars since the most recent chart long or short mark ≥ chart-mark cooldown parameter.
Calculation: Reversion-band entry = confirmed bar AND valid peer pair AND absolute relative-value z-score ≤ exit threshold AND NOT (prior bar had a valid peer pair AND absolute prior-bar relative-value z-score ≤ exit threshold).
Long: confirmed bar AND chart-mark spacing condition AND valid peer pair AND relative-value z-score ≤ −entry threshold AND relative-value z-score is rising.
Short: confirmed bar AND chart-mark spacing condition AND valid peer pair AND relative-value z-score ≥ entry threshold AND relative-value z-score is falling.
Exit: Long, reversion-band entry.
Exit: Short, reversion-band entry.
Calculation: Kalman-standardized residual = (peer-adjusted log-price residual − updated filter mean) ÷ √max(updated filter variance, variance floor); each bar, process variance = process-noise base + absolute relative-value z-score change × process-noise multiplier, measurement variance = max(standard deviation(peer-adjusted log-price residual, z-score lookback)², variance floor), predicted mean = prior updated mean (initially the current peer-adjusted log-price residual), predicted variance = prior updated variance + process variance (initially initial filter variance), gain = predicted variance ÷ (predicted variance + measurement variance), updated filter mean = predicted mean + gain × (peer-adjusted log-price residual − predicted mean), and updated filter variance = (1 − gain) × predicted variance.
Calculation: Stationarity score = clamp(scale − absolute change in rolling log-return beta over the drift lookback × beta-drift weight − positive part of the negative change in rolling correlation over the drift lookback × correlation-drift weight − absolute standardized skewness of the peer-adjusted log-price residual over the z-score lookback × skewness weight − positive part of raw kurtosis of that residual over the z-score lookback minus the kurtosis baseline × kurtosis weight, from zero to scale).
Calculation: Mean-reversion pressure = clamp(−relative-value z-score change × sign(relative-value z-score) × reversion-velocity weight + (absolute relative-value z-score − entry threshold) × excess-distance weight + stationarity score × stationarity weight + positive part of clamped negative linear-regression value of the peer-adjusted log-price residual change over the z-score lookback divided by max(absolute linear-regression value of the peer-adjusted log-price residual over that lookback, variance floor), scaled and clamped from negative scale to scale) × regression-speed weight, from zero to scale).
Calculation: Kalman-cheap state = confirmed bar AND valid peer pair AND Kalman-standardized residual < −entry threshold AND mean-reversion pressure > pressure threshold.
Calculation: Kalman-rich state = confirmed bar AND valid peer pair AND Kalman-standardized residual > entry threshold AND mean-reversion pressure > pressure threshold.
Long: Kalman-cheap state AND NOT Kalman-cheap state on the prior bar.
Short: Kalman-rich state AND NOT Kalman-rich state on the prior bar.
