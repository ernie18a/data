Calculation: Qualifying EP bar = latest chart bar where (instrument close / previous chart bar close − 1) × 100 ≥ minimum EP change, provided its age is at most maximum EP age
Calculation: Outperformance since qualifying EP bar = 100 × ((instrument close − instrument close on qualifying EP bar) / instrument close on qualifying EP bar − (benchmark close − benchmark close on qualifying EP bar) / benchmark close on qualifying EP bar)
Long: a qualifying EP bar exists AND outperformance since qualifying EP bar ≥ minimum outperformance AND (highest instrument high over high lookback bars − instrument close) / highest instrument high over high lookback bars × 100 ≤ maximum distance below high AND EMA(instrument close, fast EMA length) > EMA(instrument close, middle EMA length) > EMA(instrument close, slow EMA length) AND instrument close ≤ EMA(instrument close, fast EMA length) × (1 + cloud tolerance / 100) AND instrument close ≥ EMA(instrument close, middle EMA length) × (1 − cloud tolerance / 100) AND instrument volume ≤ SMA(instrument volume, volume average length) × maximum pullback volume ratio
Calculation: Breakout level = highest instrument high after the qualifying EP bar through the first bar meeting the Long setup condition, or that bar’s high if it is the EP bar; retain until the Long setup condition is false for the clearing streak number of consecutive chart bars
Long: a breakout level is active AND instrument close > breakout level
