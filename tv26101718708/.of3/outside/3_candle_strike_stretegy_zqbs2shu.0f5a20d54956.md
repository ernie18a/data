Calculation: Bullish strike pattern = each candle in the configured preceding candle count closes at or below its open AND current close > previous candle open
Calculation: Bearish strike pattern = each candle in the configured preceding candle count closes at or above its open AND current close < previous candle open
Calculation: Smoothed regression slope = EMA(Linear Regression(close, configured regression period) - prior-bar Linear Regression(close, configured regression period), configured slope smoothing period)
Long: Bullish strike pattern
Short: Bearish strike pattern
Long: chart time is within the configured trading date range and entry session AND current high - current low <= ATR(T1, configured ATR period) * configured range limit AND (restricted-session filter is disabled OR chart time is outside the configured restricted session) AND ((three-candle entry is enabled AND Bullish strike pattern AND fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) > medium SMMA(close, configured medium SMMA period) AND fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) > prior-bar fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) AND absolute value of Smoothed regression slope > configured trend threshold) OR (MA-reversal entry is enabled AND fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) < slow SMMA(close, configured slow SMMA period) AND medium SMMA(close, configured medium SMMA period) < slow SMMA(close, configured slow SMMA period) AND EMA(close, configured fast EMA period) > fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) AND Bullish strike pattern AND the fast EMA and fast SMMA crossed in either direction within the configured bar limit))
Short: chart time is within the configured trading date range and entry session AND current high - current low <= ATR(T1, configured ATR period) * configured range limit AND (restricted-session filter is disabled OR chart time is outside the configured restricted session) AND ((three-candle entry is enabled AND Bearish strike pattern AND fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) < medium SMMA(close, configured medium SMMA period) AND fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) < prior-bar fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) AND absolute value of Smoothed regression slope > configured trend threshold) OR (MA-reversal entry is enabled AND fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) > slow SMMA(close, configured slow SMMA period) AND medium SMMA(close, configured medium SMMA period) > slow SMMA(close, configured slow SMMA period) AND EMA(close, configured fast EMA period) < fast SMMA(close, configured fast SMMA period) AND Bearish strike pattern AND the fast EMA and fast SMMA crossed in either direction within the configured bar limit))
Exit: long, Bearish strike pattern OR chart time is outside the configured entry session
Exit: short, Bullish strike pattern OR chart time is outside the configured entry session
