Calculation: Daily reference price = (open + close) / 2 on the bar crossing the reference time
Long: HMA(close, HMA length) > its prior value AND close crosses above HMA(close, HMA length) AND HMA slope direction is unchanged on the current and previous bars AND at least the cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous signal AND bar is confirmed AND (BOS filter disabled OR (close > highest high of the previous structure lookback bars AND close > open AND (stretch filter disabled OR (daily reference price is captured AND close <= daily reference price - stretch distance)))) AND (session filter disabled OR (daily reference price is captured AND New York time is within the configured session start and end))
Short: HMA(close, HMA length) <= its prior value AND close crosses below HMA(close, HMA length) AND HMA slope direction is unchanged on the current and previous bars AND at least the cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous signal AND bar is confirmed AND (BOS filter disabled OR (close < lowest low of the previous structure lookback bars AND close < open AND (stretch filter disabled OR (daily reference price is captured AND close >= daily reference price + stretch distance)))) AND (session filter disabled OR (daily reference price is captured AND New York time is within the configured session start and end))
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR(ATR length) at entry * stop-loss multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR length) at entry * stop-loss multiple * risk-reward ratio
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(ATR length) at entry * stop-loss multiple OR low <= entry price - ATR(ATR length) at entry * stop-loss multiple * risk-reward ratio
