Calculation: Relative EMA spread = 100 × (EMA(closing price, momentum fast length) − EMA(closing price, momentum slow length)) / EMA(closing price, momentum slow length)
Calculation: Normalized momentum = if highest(relative EMA spread, normalization lookback) > lowest(relative EMA spread, normalization lookback), then 2 × (relative EMA spread − lowest(relative EMA spread, normalization lookback)) / (highest(relative EMA spread, normalization lookback) − lowest(relative EMA spread, normalization lookback)) − 1, else 0
Option: buy call, closing price > EMA(closing price, base EMA length) AND EMA(closing price, fast EMA length) > EMA(closing price, slow EMA length) AND normalized momentum > 0 AND normalized momentum > EMA(normalized momentum, momentum smoothing length) AND normalized momentum < overbought threshold AND (volume surge is not required OR (trading volume ≥ SMA(trading volume, volume average period) × volume surge threshold AND closing price > opening price)) AND the most recent signal was not a buy signal
Option: buy put, closing price < EMA(closing price, base EMA length) AND EMA(closing price, fast EMA length) < EMA(closing price, slow EMA length) AND normalized momentum < 0 AND normalized momentum < EMA(normalized momentum, momentum smoothing length) AND normalized momentum > oversold threshold AND (volume surge is not required OR (trading volume ≥ SMA(trading volume, volume average period) × volume surge threshold AND closing price < opening price)) AND the most recent signal was not a sell signal
