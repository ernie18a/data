Calculation: Extended trend score = sum a bullish vote when both MACD line and signal line from MACD(close, fast period, slow period, signal period) are positive, a bearish vote when both are negative, otherwise a neutral vote; a bullish, bearish, or neutral vote according as SMA(close, spread-average period) is above, below, or equal to SMA(open, spread-average period); a bullish, bearish, or neutral vote according as close is above, below, or equal to close at the momentum lookback; a bullish vote when close is above SMA(close, trend-average period), otherwise a bearish vote; a bullish, bearish, or neutral vote according as on-balance volume is above, below, or equal to its SMA over the OBV-average period; and, when relative strength is enabled and the traded instrument is not Bitcoin-based, a bullish vote if close divided by selected benchmark close is above its SMA over the relative-strength-average period, otherwise a bearish vote, or a neutral vote when relative strength is disabled
Calculation: Filtered annualized realized volatility = EMA(standard deviation(log(close / prior close), volatility lookback) × square root(annualization factor) × percentage scale, smoothing period) when volatility smoothing is enabled; otherwise standard deviation(log(close / prior close), volatility lookback) × square root(annualization factor) × percentage scale
Long: No long position is open AND extended trend score stays at or above the long score threshold for at least the confirmation period of consecutive chart bars AND filtered annualized realized volatility is at or below the selected volatility cap (automatic profile cap or manual cap) AND (close - SMA(close, trend-average period)) / standard deviation(close, trend-average period) is at or below the buy-extension threshold
Long: No long position is open AND extended trend score is at or above the long score threshold on the first bar qualifying for the confirmation period AND (filtered annualized realized volatility is above the selected volatility cap (automatic profile cap or manual cap) OR (close - SMA(close, trend-average period)) / standard deviation(close, trend-average period) is above the buy-extension threshold)
Exit: Long, extended trend score stays at or below the exit score threshold for at least the confirmation period of consecutive chart bars AND filtered annualized realized volatility is at or below the selected volatility cap (automatic profile cap or manual cap) AND (close - SMA(close, trend-average period)) / standard deviation(close, trend-average period) is at or above the sell-extension threshold
Exit: Long, extended trend score is at or below the exit score threshold on the first bar qualifying for the confirmation period AND (filtered annualized realized volatility is above the selected volatility cap (automatic profile cap or manual cap) OR (close - SMA(close, trend-average period)) / standard deviation(close, trend-average period) is below the sell-extension threshold)
