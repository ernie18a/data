Calculation: Relative volume = traded instrument volume on T1 / SMA(traded instrument volume on T1, P1)
Calculation: VWAP trend = VWAP filter disabled OR VWAP unavailable OR traded instrument close on T1 > VWAP(traded instrument close on T1)
Calculation: Bullish alignment = EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P2) > EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P3) AND EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P3) > EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P4) AND traded instrument close on T1 > EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P5) AND VWAP trend
Calculation: High reference = wick-based references enabled ? traded instrument high on T1 : max(traded instrument close on T1, traded instrument open on T1)
Calculation: Low reference = wick-based references enabled ? traded instrument low on T1 : min(traded instrument close on T1, traded instrument open on T1)
Long: RSI(traded instrument close on T1 / broad-market benchmark close on T1, P6) > P17 AND ((Bullish alignment AND (Highest(High reference, P8) - traded instrument close on T1) / Highest(High reference, P8) * 100 <= P9 AND Relative volume >= P10 AND traded instrument close on T1 crosses above Highest(High reference from prior bars, P1)) OR (Bullish alignment AND Low reference >= EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P3) * P11 AND High reference <= EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P2) * P12 AND Relative volume < P13) OR (Bullish alignment AND Low reference <= EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P3) * P14 AND Low reference >= EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P3) * P11 AND MFI(traded instrument close on T1, P7) > P15) OR (traded instrument close on T1 crosses above EMA(traded instrument close on T1, P5) AND Relative volume >= P16 AND VWAP trend))
