Calculation: Weighted score = ((if ROC(chart close, ROC period) > ROC threshold then 1 else -1) + (if RSI(chart close, RSI period) > RSI threshold then 1 else -1) + (if EMA(chart close, fast EMA period) > EMA(chart close, slow EMA period) then 1 else -1) + (if chart close > SMA(chart close, SMA period) then 1 else -1)) / 4 × clamp(sum for i=1..5 over valid windows of (correlation weight i × Pearson correlation(chart close, Bitcoin close at T1, correlation period i)) / sum for i=1..5 over valid windows of correlation weight i, -1, 1), using 0 if the denominator is 0; a window is valid when its count of aligned nonmissing closes is at least ceiling(correlation period i × minimum valid observation ratio) and its Pearson correlation is defined
Long: Weighted score >= 0 AND (Weighted score < neutral lower bound OR Weighted score > neutral upper bound)
Short: Weighted score < 0 AND (Weighted score < neutral lower bound OR Weighted score > neutral upper bound)
