Calculation: DXY range impulse = DXY high on T1 − DXY low on T1 > EMA(DXY high on T1 − DXY low on T1, P10) × P11
Calculation: bullish divergence event = a new confirmed DXY pivot high or traded-instrument pivot low using P2 bars on each side AND the two latest confirmed DXY pivot highs are rising AND the two latest confirmed traded-instrument pivot lows are rising AND the latest DXY pivot high and traded-instrument pivot low are within P7 minutes of each other AND (trend filter disabled OR DXY close on T1 > EMA(DXY close on T1, P1)) AND (push filter disabled OR DXY close on T1 > highest prior DXY highs on T1 over P4 bars) AND more than P3 chart bars have passed since the previous bullish divergence event
Calculation: bearish divergence event = a new confirmed DXY pivot low or traded-instrument pivot high using P2 bars on each side AND the two latest confirmed DXY pivot lows are falling AND the two latest confirmed traded-instrument pivot highs are falling AND the latest DXY pivot low and traded-instrument pivot high are within P7 minutes of each other AND (trend filter disabled OR DXY close on T1 < EMA(DXY close on T1, P1)) AND (push filter disabled OR DXY close on T1 < lowest prior DXY lows on T1 over P4 bars) AND more than P3 chart bars have passed since the previous bearish divergence event
Calculation: bullish divergence active = a bullish divergence event occurred within the last P8 chart bars
Calculation: bearish divergence active = a bearish divergence event occurred within the last P8 chart bars
Calculation: bullish lead = DXY close on T1 < lowest prior DXY lows on T1 over P9 bars AND DXY range impulse AND traded-instrument close > lowest prior traded-instrument lows over P9 bars
Calculation: bearish lead = DXY close on T1 > highest prior DXY highs on T1 over P9 bars AND DXY range impulse AND traded-instrument close < highest prior traded-instrument highs over P9 bars
Calculation: bullish gold score = (1 if DXY close on T1 < EMA(DXY close on T1, P1), otherwise 0) + (1 if bullish divergence active, otherwise 0) + (1 if bullish lead, otherwise 0)
Calculation: bearish gold score = (1 if DXY close on T1 > EMA(DXY close on T1, P1), otherwise 0) + (1 if bearish divergence active, otherwise 0) + (1 if bearish lead, otherwise 0)
Long: bullish divergence event OR (bullish divergence active AND NOT bearish divergence active) OR bullish lead OR bullish gold score > bearish gold score
Short: bearish divergence event OR (bearish divergence active AND NOT bullish divergence active) OR bearish lead OR bearish gold score > bullish gold score
