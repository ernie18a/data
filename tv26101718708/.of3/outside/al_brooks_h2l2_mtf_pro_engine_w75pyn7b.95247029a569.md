Calculation: High 2 = a non-inside chart bar closing above EMA(chart close, P1) with a higher high than the prior bar, followed later by a non-inside bar closing above that EMA with a lower high than its prior bar, then by a non-inside bar closing above that EMA with a higher high than its prior bar; reset before processing a bar that closes below that EMA or whose high equals the highest chart high over P3 bars
Calculation: Low 2 = a non-inside chart bar closing below EMA(chart close, P1) with a lower low than the prior bar, followed later by a non-inside bar closing below that EMA with a higher low than its prior bar, then by a non-inside bar closing below that EMA with a lower low than its prior bar; reset before processing a bar that closes above that EMA or whose low equals the lowest chart low over P3 bars
Long: High 2 AND chart high > chart low AND (chart close - chart low) / (chart high - chart low) >= P4 AND chart low <= EMA(chart close, P1) * P5 AND (P2 disabled OR prior completed T1 close > prior completed T1 EMA over P1 bars)
Short: Low 2 AND chart high > chart low AND (chart high - chart close) / (chart high - chart low) >= P4 AND chart high >= EMA(chart close, P1) * P6 AND (P2 disabled OR prior completed T1 close < prior completed T1 EMA over P1 bars)
