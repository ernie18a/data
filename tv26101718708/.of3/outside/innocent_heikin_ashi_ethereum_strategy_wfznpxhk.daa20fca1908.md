Calculation: long target = long signal close + (long signal close - Lowest Low(ETH low, P3) on the long signal candle) * P5
Long: confirmed chart candle AND a prior red PVSRA candle since the latest long signal or chart start had chart open and close < EMA(chart close, P1) AND at least P4 red PVSRA candles since the latest long signal or chart start had chart close < EMA(chart close, P1) AND PVSRA color(ETH volume, ETH high, ETH low, ETH close, ETH open) = green AND chart open > EMA(chart close, P1) AND (chart close < EMA(chart close, P2) OR chart close > EMA(chart close, P2))
Short: PVSRA color(ETH volume, ETH high, ETH low, ETH close, ETH open) = blue OR green AND chart open > EMA(chart close, P1) AND chart open > EMA(chart close, P2)
Exit: long, chart low <= Lowest Low(ETH low, P3) on the long signal candle OR chart high >= long target OR the candle immediately after the latest long signal has PVSRA color red OR violet OR the candle immediately after the latest pending short signal has PVSRA color green OR the candle immediately after the latest short signal has PVSRA color red AND chart open and close > EMA(chart close, P2) AND at least P4 green PVSRA candles since the latest successful sell exit or chart start had chart close > EMA(chart close, P2)
