Calculation: financial conditions composite = EMA of the weighted sum of eight scores sampled on T1: Z-score of the log return of the US equity ETF close over the equity return window; Z-score of the log return of the global equity ETF close over the equity return window; negative Z-score of the investment-grade bond ETF close divided by the high-yield bond ETF close; negative Z-score of the 2-year yield; Z-score of the 10-year yield minus the 2-year yield; negative Z-score of the dollar index; negative Z-score of the volatility index; and negative Z-score of the T-bill ETF close divided by the US equity ETF close. Each Z-score standardizes its input by subtracting its SMA and dividing by its standard deviation over the normalization lookback, with zero when the standard deviation is zero. Optionally clip each score to the winsorization limit. Weights are equal or inverse-volatility, where each inverse-volatility weight is the reciprocal of the component score's standard deviation over the weight lookback raised to the inverse-volatility exponent; normalize weights to sum to one. Apply the composite smoothing length.
Long: (financial conditions composite crosses above the long entry threshold AND financial conditions composite > financial conditions composite[slope lookback] AND (price filter disabled OR traded instrument close > SMA(traded instrument close, price SMA length))) OR financial conditions composite >= always-long floor
Short: financial conditions composite crosses below the short entry threshold AND financial conditions composite < financial conditions composite[slope lookback] AND (price filter disabled OR traded instrument close < SMA(traded instrument close, price SMA length))
Exit: Long, financial conditions composite crosses below the long exit threshold
Exit: Short, financial conditions composite crosses above the short exit threshold OR financial conditions composite < forced short-exit level
