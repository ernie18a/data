Calculation: Yield spread = EMA(base-currency 10Y yield at T1 − quote-currency 10Y yield at T1, spread smoothing length), using the US real yield for the US leg when real yields are selected
Calculation: Z-score(value, normalization length) = (value − SMA(value, normalization length)) / standard deviation(value, normalization length), or zero when the standard deviation is zero
Long: rolling Pearson correlation(yield spread, FX closing price, correlation length) ≥ minimum correlation AND Z-score(yield spread, normalization length) > Z-score(yield spread, normalization length) divergence lookback bars ago AND Z-score(FX closing price, normalization length) < Z-score(FX closing price, normalization length) divergence lookback bars ago AND absolute difference between the two Z-scores ≥ minimum Z-score gap
