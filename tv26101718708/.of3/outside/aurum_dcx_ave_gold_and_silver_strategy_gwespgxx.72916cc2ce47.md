Calculation: Directional Convexity Index = 100 × sqrt((DX / 100) × ((1 − convexity weight) × clamp(abs(close − close N DCX bars ago) / sum(abs(close changes), N DCX bars), efficiency floor, 1) + convexity weight × (1 + (RMA(max(second difference of close, 0), N DCX bars) − RMA(max(−second difference of close, 0), N DCX bars)) / (RMA(max(second difference of close, 0), N DCX bars) + RMA(max(−second difference of close, 0), N DCX bars))) / 2))
