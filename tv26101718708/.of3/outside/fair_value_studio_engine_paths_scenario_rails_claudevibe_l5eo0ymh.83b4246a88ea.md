Calculation: Valuation metric per share = in manual mode, use the configured per-share value when positive; otherwise divide the configured financial metric by shares outstanding. In automatic mode, select net income for financial businesses; EBITDA for REIT or real-estate, utility, or energy, mineral, oil, mining, or metal businesses; free cash flow for technology, software, or semiconductor businesses with positive free cash flow; otherwise net income when positive, else free cash flow when positive, else EBITDA; divide the selected financial measure by shares outstanding. Optionally smooth feed-derived values with an SMA over the configured report length. When clamping is enabled and its reference SMA is available, clamp the result to that SMA multiplied by the configured lower and upper bands; the reference is an SMA of the pre-clamp per-share value over the configured bar length.
Calculation: Enterprise basis = the configured enterprise setting in manual mode; otherwise enabled for REIT or real-estate, utility, or energy, mineral, oil, mining, or metal businesses, or when neither net income nor free cash flow is positive.
Calculation: Enterprise net debt per share = the configured manual net-debt-per-share value when a positive manual per-share value is used; otherwise divide total debt minus cash and short-term investments by shares outstanding, treating missing debt or cash as zero and an unavailable result as zero.
Calculation: Normalized multiple = SMA(close / valuation metric per share, configured bar length) when manual mode is active, or in automatic mode when net income or free cash flow is positive and debt / EBITDA is not above the configured leverage limit; otherwise unavailable. Debt / EBITDA is unavailable when debt or EBITDA is missing or EBITDA is nonpositive.
Calculation: Effective valuation multiple = in manual mode, the configured multiple; otherwise select a separate configured base multiple for financial businesses, heavy businesses, profitable technology businesses with positive free cash flow, other profitable businesses, businesses with positive free cash flow, and all remaining businesses, in that priority order. In automatic mode, increase the base multiple by the configured uplift when gross profit / revenue is above the configured margin cutoff, then clamp it to the configured minimum and maximum. Use this multiple in Fixed mode, the normalized multiple in Normal mode, or their mean in Blend mode; when the normalized multiple is unavailable, use the base multiple.
Calculation: Fair value = valuation metric per share × effective valuation multiple − enterprise net debt per share when enterprise basis is enabled; unavailable when the per-share metric is nonpositive or the result is nonpositive.
Calculation: Normal value = valuation metric per share × normalized multiple − enterprise net debt per share when enterprise basis is enabled; unavailable when the per-share metric is nonpositive, the normalized multiple is unavailable, or the result is nonpositive.
Long: crossunder(close, fair value)
Short: crossover(close, fair value)
Long: crossunder(close, normal value)
Short: crossover(close, normal value)
