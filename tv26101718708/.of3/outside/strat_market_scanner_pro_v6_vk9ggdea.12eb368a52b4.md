Calculation: Inside bar = scanned instrument's bar high <= preceding bar high AND bar low >= preceding bar low
Calculation: Outside bar = scanned instrument's bar high > preceding bar high AND bar low < preceding bar low
Calculation: 2-up bar = scanned instrument's bar high > preceding bar high AND bar low >= preceding bar low
Calculation: 2-down bar = scanned instrument's bar low < preceding bar low AND bar high <= preceding bar high
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment count(side) = number of scanned instrument bars on T2, T3, T4, and T5 whose close >= open for long or close < open for short
Long: ((current T1 bar is 2-up AND (previous T1 bar is inside OR previous T1 bar is 2-down OR previous T1 bar is outside)) OR (previous T1 bar is inside AND current T1 bar is outside AND current close > current open) OR (two-bars-prior T1 bar is 2-down AND previous T1 bar is inside AND current T1 bar is inside) OR (two-bars-prior T1 bar is outside AND previous T1 bar is 2-down AND current T1 bar is inside)) AND higher-timeframe alignment count(long) >= P1 AND (NOT P2 OR scanned instrument's T1 volume > SMA(scanned instrument's T1 volume, P3))
Short: ((current T1 bar is 2-down AND (previous T1 bar is inside OR previous T1 bar is 2-up OR previous T1 bar is outside)) OR (previous T1 bar is inside AND current T1 bar is outside AND current close < current open) OR (two-bars-prior T1 bar is 2-up AND previous T1 bar is inside AND current T1 bar is inside) OR (two-bars-prior T1 bar is outside AND previous T1 bar is 2-up AND current T1 bar is inside)) AND higher-timeframe alignment count(short) >= P1 AND (NOT P2 OR scanned instrument's T1 volume > SMA(scanned instrument's T1 volume, P3))
