Calculation: currency breakout count(currency, direction) = number of its seven major pairs whose latest breakout in the active session indicates that direction (strength: pair close above the previous completed session high for a base currency or below its low for a quote currency; weakness: below the low for a base currency or above the high for a quote currency)
Calculation: currency holding count(currency, direction) = number of its seven major pairs whose current close remains beyond the previous completed session boundary in that direction (strength: above the high for a base currency or below the low for a quote currency; weakness: below the low for a base currency or above the high for a quote currency)
Long: for each of AUD, CAD, CHF, EUR, GBP, JPY, NZD, and USD, an active session AND no signal yet for that currency in the session AND currency breakout count(currency, strength) >= breakout threshold AND currency holding count(currency, strength) >= holding threshold
