Calculation: standardized price change = z-score of one-bar closing-price change over P1 bars, undefined when its standard deviation is zero
Calculation: market-gold link = Pearson correlation of gold and market completed closing-price changes, using up to P13 valid pairs at T3 when available, otherwise up to P14 valid pairs at T2, and requiring at least P2 pairs with nonzero variance
Calculation: dollar direction factor = sign of the dollar-gold link when its absolute value is at least P3, otherwise inverse
Calculation: yield direction factor = sign of the yield-gold link when its absolute value is at least P3, otherwise inverse when P15 is enabled and same-direction when disabled
Calculation: oil direction factor = sign of the oil-gold link when its absolute value is at least P3, otherwise neutral
Calculation: aligned market change = market direction factor × standardized price change for that market and timeframe
Calculation: fast external direction = at T1, neutral when dollar and yield aligned changes both meet P7 and disagree, otherwise the direction of the first qualifying dollar, yield, or linked-oil change, in that order; qualification requires absolute aligned change at least P7
Calculation: comparison external direction = at a timeframe, neutral when dollar and yield aligned changes both meet P12 and disagree, otherwise the direction of the first qualifying dollar, yield, or linked-oil change, in that order; qualification requires absolute aligned change at least P12
Calculation: weighted external push = weighted average of T1 aligned changes that meet P7 and match the fast external direction, using separate dollar, yield, and oil weights P4, P5, and P6, excluding unlinked oil, when total weight is positive
Calculation: directional unpriced move = weighted external push minus gold standardized price change at T1 when the difference points in the fast external direction, otherwise zero
Calculation: instability state = initially inactive; activates when dollar-gold Pearson correlation over up to P14 valid T2 pairs reaches P9, deactivates when it falls to or below P9 − P10, and otherwise retains its prior state
