Calculation: upper edge = max(low of newest candle, low of oldest candle) of a fair value gap spanning three candles
Calculation: lower edge = min(high of newest candle, high of oldest candle) of a fair value gap spanning three candles
Calculation: midpoint = (upper edge + lower edge) / 2
Calculation: flip bar = first bar after the gap completed with close below lower edge for a bullish gap, or close above upper edge for a bearish gap
Calculation: midpoint reached = some bar from setup start through the current bar has low <= midpoint <= high, where setup start is the bar the gap completed, or the bar after the flip bar once a flip bar exists
Calculation: HTF gate = the gap completed in the HTF window immediately before the current HTF window AND NOT (window midpoint < reference AND midpoint > window open) AND NOT (window midpoint > reference AND midpoint < window open), where window midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 and window open = first open of the window in which the gap completed; reference = index-1 open at daily session start P9 (current value) on T1 and T2 charts, or chart open at weekly session start P10 (value when the gap completed) on T3 chart, where the gap is hidden on T3 chart if that reference is undefined; HTF window = P5 minutes on T1 chart or P6 minutes on T2 chart in blocks aligned to midnight GMT+3, or P7 minutes on T3 chart in blocks starting at P8 GMT+3 truncated at midnight with no window between midnight and P8
Calculation: shown = bars since the gap's oldest candle <= P1 AND (HTF switch is off OR HTF gate), where HTF switch = P2 on T1 chart, P3 on T2 chart, P4 on T3 chart
Long: chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND a bullish fair value gap completed at or before the current bar AND shown AND no bar from completion through the current bar has close below lower edge AND no bar from completion through the current bar has (midpoint reached AND close above upper edge)
Long: chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND a bearish fair value gap completed at or before the current bar AND shown AND flip bar at or before the current bar AND no bar before the flip bar has (midpoint reached AND close below lower edge) AND no bar after the flip bar through the current bar has (midpoint reached AND (close above upper edge OR close below lower edge))
Short: chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND a bearish fair value gap completed at or before the current bar AND shown AND no bar from completion through the current bar has close above upper edge AND no bar from completion through the current bar has (midpoint reached AND close below lower edge)
Short: chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND a bullish fair value gap completed at or before the current bar AND shown AND flip bar at or before the current bar AND no bar before the flip bar has (midpoint reached AND close above upper edge) AND no bar after the flip bar through the current bar has (midpoint reached AND (close above upper edge OR close below lower edge))
Exit: long, chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND midpoint reached AND close above upper edge AND (bullish fair value gap with no flip bar at or before the current bar OR bearish fair value gap with flip bar at or before the current bar)
Exit: long, chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND bearish fair value gap with flip bar at or before the current bar AND midpoint reached AND close below lower edge
Exit: short, chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND midpoint reached AND close below lower edge AND (bearish fair value gap with no flip bar at or before the current bar OR bullish fair value gap with flip bar at or before the current bar)
Exit: short, chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND bullish fair value gap with flip bar at or before the current bar AND midpoint reached AND close above upper edge
