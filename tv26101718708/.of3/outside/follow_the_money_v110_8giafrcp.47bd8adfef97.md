Calculation: Comparison benchmark = configured semiconductor benchmark for AEHR, MU, and ONTO; configured industrial benchmark for ETN and VRT; configured utility benchmark for CEG; configured default benchmark otherwise
Calculation: Fast moving average = selected EMA or SMA of traded instrument closing price over the fast average length
Calculation: Long moving average = SMA of traded instrument closing price over the long average length
Calculation: Relative strength = (traded instrument close / its close at the relative-strength lookback − 1) − (comparison benchmark close / its close at the same lookback − 1)
Calculation: Volume expansion = (traded instrument volume − its SMA over the volume lookback) / that SMA, expressed as a percentage; zero when that SMA is zero
Calculation: Bar close position = (traded instrument close − low) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low
Calculation: Institutional score = standard score points for each of: fast moving average > long moving average; long moving average > its value at the slope lookback; volume expansion >= the volume expansion threshold; bar close position >= the close-position threshold; OBV > its SMA over the score averaging length; Accumulation/Distribution Line > its SMA over the score averaging length; relative strength is positive and at or below the very-strong relative-strength threshold; secondary score points for each of: close > fast moving average; bar close position >= the close-position threshold; ATR < its SMA over the score averaging length; full-volatility score points if ATR > that SMA; very-strong relative-strength score points instead of standard score points if relative strength > the very-strong relative-strength threshold; bull-market score points if comparison benchmark close > its SMA over the fast market length and that SMA > its SMA over the slow market length
Long: position is flat AND the bar is confirmed if the confirmed-bar filter is enabled AND bars since exit >= the cooldown period AND fast moving average > long moving average AND long moving average > its value at the slope lookback AND closing price has remained above the fast moving average for at least the required consecutive-bar count AND (breakout filter is disabled OR close > highest prior close over the breakout lookback OR close > highest prior close over the near-high lookback multiplied by the near-high factor) AND volume expansion >= the volume expansion threshold AND relative strength is positive AND comparison benchmark close > its SMA over the fast market length AND that SMA > its SMA over the slow market length AND institutional score >= the minimum score
Long: after the first entry stage and before the second entry stage AND open-position return from average entry price >= the add trigger percentage AND close > fast moving average AND relative strength is positive AND the bar is confirmed if the confirmed-bar filter is enabled
