## Description

🇬🇧 United Kingdom · English

Key Levels and Sessions Coach draws the price levels that traders watch every single day and explains, in plain language, what price is doing around them.

WHAT IT SHOWS
- Previous Day High / Low (PDH / PDL) and today's Daily Open (DO)
- Previous Week High / Low (PWH / PWL)
- Previous Month High / Low (PMH / PML)
- Round numbers (psychological levels), automatic step or your own step
- Asia, London and New York session boxes with their high and low (1h and lower)
- Liquidity sweep markers: price pokes above PDH or below PDL and closes back inside

COACH PANEL
- Where price is versus yesterday's and last week's range (breakout, breakdown or inside)
- Intraday bias based on the daily open
- Nearest resistance and support with distance in %
- Which sessions are open now, plus a London + New York overlap warning
- 11 languages, selectable in the settings

CLEAN CHART
- Fit to window: levels far from the visible candles are hidden, so the price scale stays tight. They are still listed in the panel.
- The coach panel moves automatically to the free corner (top right or bottom right), so it never hides the levels.
- Close labels are staggered so they never overlap.
- Session boxes and sweep markers show only the last days you choose (default 3).

HOW TO USE
1. Start with the bigger picture: monthly and weekly levels.
2. Use PDH / PDL and the daily open to plan the day.
3. A break above PDH that holds on a retest shows strength. A sweep that closes back inside can be a trap.
4. Levels are zones, not exact lines. Wait for a candle close.

ALERTS
Cross above PDH, cross below PDL, cross above PWH, cross below PWL, daily open cross, PDH sweep, PDL sweep.

NOTES
- Levels use confirmed higher timeframe data, past levels do not repaint.
- Sessions follow real local times with daylight saving (Tokyo, London, New York) and can be edited.
- Works on crypto, forex, stocks, indices and commodities.

Educational tool, not financial advice.

ЧТО ПОКАЗЫВАЕТ

- Блоки сессий Азии, Лондона и Нью-Йорка (1ч и ниже)
- Отметки снятия ликвидности у PDH / PDL

ПАНЕЛЬ ТРЕНЕРА
- Положение цены относительно диапазона вчера и прошлой недели
- Внутридневной уклон по дневному открытию
- Ближайшие сопротивление и поддержка с расстоянием в %

ЧИСТЫЙ ГРАФИК

Начните с месячных и недельных уровней, затем планируйте день по PDH / PDL и дневному открытию. Уровни - это зоны, а не точные линии. Ждите закрытия свечи.

type SessState
    box        bx
    float      hi
    float      lo
    bool       active = false
    array<box> bxs
    array<int> tms

pruneSess(SessState s) =>
    while s.tms.size() > 0 and time - s.tms.first() > histMs
        box.delete(s.bxs.shift())
        s.tms.shift()
    s.tms.size()

procSess(SessState s, bool inS, color c, string key) =>
    if inS
        if not s.active
            s.active := true
            s.hi := high
            s.lo := low
            s.bx := box.new(bar_index, high, bar_index, low, border_color = color.new(c, 50), bgcolor = color.new(c, 92), text = tr(key), text_color = color.new(c, 20), text_size = size.tiny, text_halign = text.align_left, text_valign = text.align_top)
            s.bxs.push(s.bx)
            s.tms.push(time)
        else
            s.hi := math.max(s.hi, high)
            s.lo := math.min(s.lo, low)
            box.set_top(s.bx, s.hi)
            box.set_bottom(s.bx, s.lo)
            box.set_right(s.bx, bar_index)
    else if s.active
        s.active := false
    s.active

var SessState stA = SessState.new(bxs = array.new<box>(), tms = array.new<int>())
