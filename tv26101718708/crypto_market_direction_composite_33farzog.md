## Description

# Crypto Market Direction Composite

[b]Overview[/b]

The Crypto Market Direction Composite is a multi-timeframe market regime model designed to identify bullish, neutral, and bearish conditions within the cryptocurrency market.

The model combines information from three independent market horizons:

* **5-Day Component** – Short-term positioning and pullbacks
* **10-Day Component** – Intermediate trend confirmation
* **20-Day Component** – Long-term market regime

These components are combined into a single Bull Market Score ranging from 0 to 100.

The indicator is designed to help traders distinguish between temporary market noise and meaningful shifts in trend structure.

---

[b]Research Foundation[/b]

The model was developed using historical analysis of total cryptocurrency market capitalization (CRYPTOCAP:TOTAL).

[b]5-Day Analysis[/b]

The 5-day dataset exhibited:

* Higher market noise
* Weak trend persistence
* Mild mean-reversion tendencies

Historical observations showed that extended short-term rallies were often followed by consolidation or temporary weakness.

As a result, the 5-day component is used primarily as a timing mechanism rather than a directional forecasting tool.

---

[b]10-Day Analysis[/b]

The 10-day dataset exhibited:

* Reduced market noise
* Consistent positive drift
* Improved trend persistence

Historical observations showed that the market tended to advance regardless of recent candle direction, suggesting that intermediate-term momentum contributes positively to future market performance.

The 10-day component acts as a trend confirmation layer.

---

[b]20-Day Analysis[/b]

The 20-day dataset produced the strongest directional signals.

Historical observations included:

* 58.7% of 20-day periods closing higher
* Average 20-day return of +3.84%
* Positive momentum persistence following consecutive bullish periods

The 20-day component forms the primary market regime filter and contributes the largest weighting within the model.

---

[b]Model Structure[/b]

The Bull Market Score is constructed using:

| Component     | Weight |
| ------------- | ------ |
| 20-Day Regime | 50%    |
| 10-Day Trend  | 30%    |
| 5-Day Timing  | 20%    |

The weighting reflects the relative predictive strength observed within the historical datasets.

---

[b]Interpretation[/b]

[b]Bull Market Score[/b]

| Score  | Interpretation     |
| ------ | ------------------ |
| 75-100 | Strong Bull Market |
| 60-75  | Bullish            |
| 40-60  | Neutral            |
| 25-40  | Bearish            |
| 0-25   | Strong Bear Market |

Higher scores indicate stronger alignment between long-term trend, intermediate momentum, and short-term positioning.

Lower scores indicate increasing trend weakness and deterioration in market structure.

---

[b]Bullish Pullback Setup[/b]

One of the primary signals generated by the model is the Bullish Pullback Setup.

Conditions:

* 20-Day Regime Bullish
* 10-Day Trend Bullish
* 5-Day Component Weakening

This combination suggests that the broader market remains healthy while short-term participants are temporarily taking profits.

Historically, these conditions have frequently occurred during ongoing bull markets.

---

[i]Important Notes[/i]

The Bull Market Score should not be interpreted as a literal statistical probability of future price movement.

Instead, it represents a confidence score derived from historical multi-timeframe market behaviour.

The indicator is intended for:

* Market regime analysis
* Portfolio allocation decisions
* Swing trading
* Crypto macro trend identification

The indicator is not intended to provide financial advice or guarantee future performance.

Past market behaviour may not persist in future market conditions.

## Source Code

````pine
//@version=6
indicator("Crypto Market Direction Composite", overlay=true)

// Inputs
macroLen = input.int(80, "20D Macro Trend Length")
midLen   = input.int(40, "10D Intermediate Trend Length")
shortLen = input.int(20, "5D Short-Term Length")
rsiLen   = input.int(14, "RSI Length")

// Core indicators
macroEMA = ta.ema(close, macroLen)
midEMA   = ta.ema(close, midLen)
shortEMA = ta.ema(close, shortLen)
rsi      = ta.rsi(close, rsiLen)

// Returns
ret5  = close / close[5] - 1
ret10 = close / close[10] - 1
ret20 = close / close[20] - 1

// 20D Macro Score
macroScore = 0.0
macroScore += close > macroEMA ? 35 : -35
macroScore += ret20 > 0 ? 35 : -35
macroScore += macroEMA > macroEMA[20] ? 30 : -30

// 10D Intermediate Score
midScore = 0.0
midScore += close > midEMA ? 35 : -35
midScore += ret10 > 0 ? 35 : -35
midScore += rsi > 50 ? 30 : -30

// 5D Timing Score
shortScore = 0.0
shortScore += close > shortEMA ? 30 : -30
shortScore += ret5 > 0 ? 30 : -30
shortScore += rsi < 35 ? 25 : rsi > 70 ? -25 : 0
shortScore += close < shortEMA and macroScore > 0 ? 15 : 0

// Weighted composite
score =
     macroScore * 0.50 +
     midScore   * 0.30 +
     shortScore * 0.20

bullProbability = math.min(math.max(50 + score / 2, 0), 100)
bearProbability = 100 - bullProbability

// Regime
strongBull = bullProbability >= 75
bull       = bullProbability >= 60 and bullProbability < 75
neutral    = bullProbability > 40 and bullProbability < 60
bear       = bullProbability <= 40 and bullProbability > 25
strongBear = bullProbability <= 25

// Plots
plot(macroEMA, "Macro EMA", linewidth=2)
plot(midEMA, "Intermediate EMA")
plot(shortEMA, "Short-Term EMA")

plotshape(strongBull, title="Strong Bull", style=shape.labelup, text="Strong Bull", location=location.belowbar)
plotshape(bull, title="Bull", style=shape.triangleup, text="Bull", location=location.belowbar)

plotshape(strongBear, title="Strong Bear", style=shape.labeldown, text="Strong Bear", location=location.abovebar)
plotshape(bear, title="Bear", style=shape.triangledown, text="Bear", location=location.abovebar)

// Pullback buy setup
pullbackBuy =
     macroScore > 30 and
     midScore > 20 and
     shortScore < 0 and
     rsi < 55

plotshape(pullbackBuy, title="Bullish Pullback", style=shape.circle, text="Pullback", location=location.belowbar)

// Dashboard
var table dash = table.new(position.top_right, 2, 7)

if barstate.islast
    table.cell(dash, 0, 0, "Bull Probability")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 0, str.tostring(bullProbability, "#") + "%")

    table.cell(dash, 0, 1, "Bear Probability")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 1, str.tostring(bearProbability, "#") + "%")

    table.cell(dash, 0, 2, "20D Macro")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 2, str.tostring(macroScore, "#"))

    table.cell(dash, 0, 3, "10D Trend")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 3, str.tostring(midScore, "#"))

    table.cell(dash, 0, 4, "5D Timing")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 4, str.tostring(shortScore, "#"))

    table.cell(dash, 0, 5, "RSI")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 5, str.tostring(rsi, "#.0"))

    table.cell(dash, 0, 6, "Regime")
    table.cell(dash, 1, 6,
         strongBull ? "Strong Bull" :
         bull ? "Bull" :
         neutral ? "Neutral" :
         bear ? "Bear" :
         "Strong Bear")

// Alerts
alertcondition(strongBull, "Strong Bull Market", "Crypto market direction is strongly bullish")
alertcondition(bull, "Bull Market", "Crypto market direction is bullish")
alertcondition(bear, "Bear Market", "Crypto market direction is bearish")
alertcondition(strongBear, "Strong Bear Market", "Crypto market direction is strongly bearish")
alertcondition(pullbackBuy, "Bullish Pullback Setup", "Macro trend bullish, short-term pullback detected")
````
