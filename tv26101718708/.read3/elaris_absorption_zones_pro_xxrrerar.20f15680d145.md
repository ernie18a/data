Calculation: Range high = Highest(high[1], range lookback)
Calculation: Range low = Lowest(low[1], range lookback)
Calculation: Bear breakout reference level = last confirmed swing high when the enabled swing-high break condition is true; otherwise range high
Calculation: Bull breakout reference level = last confirmed swing low when the enabled swing-low break condition is true; otherwise range low
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) when SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0, otherwise 0
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume - SMA(volume, volume lookback)) / standard deviation(volume, volume lookback) when the standard deviation > 0, otherwise 0
Calculation: Candle body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 1
Calculation: Bullish absorption zone bounds = bottom: low of the signal bar; top: min(bull breakout reference level, low of the signal bar + ATR(ATR lookback) * zone thickness)
Calculation: Bearish absorption zone bounds = bottom: max(bear breakout reference level, high of the signal bar - ATR(ATR lookback) * zone thickness); top: high of the signal bar
Calculation: Active absorption zones = zones created on absorption signal bars when zone creation is enabled, retaining at most the newest maximum-zones count across both sides and removing bullish zones after close < bottom and bearish zones after close > top when broken-zone removal is enabled
