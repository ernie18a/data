Calculation: Bull structure(S) = current low from S > the most recently confirmed pivot low of low from S, using equal left/right structure-pivot-strength bars; for S=T1 or T2, calculate on chart bars using security-mapped S values (last completed S bar historically, still-forming S bar in real time); chart source uses chart bars directly. T1 is longer than T2, and both are higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bear structure(S) = current high from S < the most recently confirmed pivot high of high from S, using equal left/right structure-pivot-strength bars; for S=T1 or T2, calculate on chart bars using security-mapped S values (last completed S bar historically, still-forming S bar in real time); chart source uses chart bars directly. T1 is longer than T2, and both are higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Directional count = count of higher highs plus lower lows across the latest direction-lookback adjacent chart-bar pairs.
Calculation: Pullback depth = for a long position, (highest high over the impulse lookback − current low) / (highest high − lowest low); for a short position, (current high − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low), using chart bars.
Calculation: Favorable move = for a long position, current high − prior chart-bar close; for a short position, prior chart-bar close − current low.
Long: flat AND directional count ≥ consolidation threshold AND Bull structure(T1) AND Bull structure(T2) AND Bull structure(chart) AND prior chart-bar range > 0 AND (min(prior open, prior close) − prior low) / (prior high − prior low) ≥ rejection-wick fraction AND current range > 0 AND close > open AND (close − open) / (high − low) ≥ strong-body fraction AND SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) ≥ volume-ratio threshold
Short: flat AND directional count ≥ consolidation threshold AND Bear structure(T1) AND Bear structure(T2) AND Bear structure(chart) AND prior chart-bar range > 0 AND (prior high − max(prior open, prior close)) / (prior high − prior low) ≥ rejection-wick fraction AND current range > 0 AND close < open AND (open − close) / (high − low) ≥ strong-body fraction AND SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) ≥ volume-ratio threshold
Long: in a long position AND directional count ≥ consolidation threshold AND highest high over the impulse lookback > lowest low over the impulse lookback AND Pullback depth ≤ maximum pullback fraction AND close > prior chart-bar high
Short: in a short position AND directional count ≥ consolidation threshold AND highest high over the impulse lookback > lowest low over the impulse lookback AND Pullback depth ≤ maximum pullback fraction AND close < prior chart-bar low
Exit: Long, directional count ≥ consolidation threshold AND ((no continuation signal since entry AND chart bars since entry ≥ no-move duration AND Favorable move < ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum favorable-move ATR fraction) OR (a continuation signal has occurred since entry AND chart bars since the most recent continuation signal ≥ continuation-failure duration) OR (a pivot low is confirmed on the current chart bar using equal left/right exit-pivot-strength bars AND close < that pivot low))
