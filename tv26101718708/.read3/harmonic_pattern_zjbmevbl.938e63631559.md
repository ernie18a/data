Calculation: Confirmed pivot sequence = on confirmed chart bars, form Pivot High(high, depth, depth) and Pivot Low(low, depth, depth); append alternating pivots only when their price distance is at least ATR(lookback) × minimum pivot-swing parameter, replace the latest same-side pivot when the new one is more extreme, and retain at most the pivot-memory parameter.
Calculation: Harmonic ratios = AB/XA = absolute AB price distance ÷ absolute XA price distance; BC/AB = absolute BC distance ÷ absolute AB distance; CD/BC = absolute CD distance ÷ absolute BC distance; XD/XA = absolute XD distance ÷ absolute XA distance; XC/XA = absolute XC distance ÷ absolute XA distance; CD/XC = absolute CD distance ÷ absolute XC distance; CD/AB = absolute CD distance ÷ absolute AB distance.
Calculation: Effective ratio tolerance = base ratio-tolerance parameter multiplied by the strict-mode factor in Strict mode, the research-mode factor in Research mode, or unchanged in Balanced mode. AB=CD tolerance = clamp(effective ratio tolerance × AB=CD tolerance factor, AB=CD minimum tolerance, AB=CD maximum tolerance). Top-pattern tolerance = clamp(effective ratio tolerance × top-tolerance factor, top-tolerance minimum, top-tolerance maximum).
Calculation: Target-fit score(value, target, tolerance) = clamp(score maximum − abs(value − target) ÷ max(abs(target) × tolerance, positive denominator floor) × score scale, score minimum, score maximum). Range-fit score(value, lower, upper) = score minimum outside the range; otherwise clamp(score maximum − abs(value − range midpoint) ÷ max(range half-width, positive denominator floor) × range penalty, score minimum, score maximum).
Calculation: Swing-time symmetry score = shorter of the two compared pivot-leg durations ÷ longer duration × score maximum. Harmonic patterns compare the A-to-B and C-to-D durations; Three Drives compares the first and third drive durations; 5-0 also uses the harmonic comparison.
Calculation: Stable HTF bias = on chart bars, compare the last completed T1 close with EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar); bullish bias when close is above EMA and bearish bias when close is below EMA.
Calculation: Directional context score = clamp(context baseline plus enabled contributions, score minimum, score maximum). Bullish candle means close > open and close > (high + low)/2; bearish candle means close < open and close < (high + low)/2. Bullish RSI means RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above its previous chart-bar value and above the bullish RSI threshold; bearish RSI means it is below its previous value and below the bearish RSI threshold. Volume expansion means volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier. VWAP context is bullish when close ≥ VWAP(hlc3) and bearish when close ≤ VWAP(hlc3). ATR regime passes when ATR(lookback) ≥ SMA(ATR(lookback), ATR-regime lookback) × ATR-regime factor. Add the configured positive or negative score contribution for each enabled candle, RSI, volume, HTF-bias, VWAP, and ATR-regime test; the HTF contribution uses Stable HTF bias.
Calculation: Harmonic score = harmonic ratio score × harmonic ratio weight + PRZ tightness score × harmonic tightness weight + swing-time symmetry score × harmonic symmetry weight + directional context score × harmonic context weight. ABC=CD score = ABC=CD ratio score × ABC=CD ratio weight + PRZ tightness score × ABC=CD tightness weight + swing-time symmetry score × ABC=CD symmetry weight + directional context score × ABC=CD context weight.
Calculation: PRZ levels = for Gartley, X-A retracement, B-C projection, and AB=CD levels using their separate Gartley PRZ ratios; for Bat, the corresponding Bat ratios; for Alternate Bat, X-A extension, B-C projection, and AB=CD levels using Alternate Bat ratios; for Butterfly, Crab, Deep Crab, Cypher, and 5-0, the respective model-specific levels. For AB=CD at each of its three CD/AB targets, use the corresponding AB=CD level, B-C projection, and X-A level based on XD/XA, using an X-A extension when the pivot direction is extended and a retracement otherwise. An X-A retracement is measured from X toward A; an X-A extension is measured from X away from A. A B-C projection is measured from C away from B; an AB=CD level is measured from C in the direction of the AB leg; an X-C retracement is measured from C toward X. Apply the bullish or bearish direction to each level.
Calculation: PRZ bounds and tightness = take the minimum and maximum of the model's three PRZ levels, pad both sides by max(ATR(lookback) × PRZ-padding parameter, minimum tick size × PRZ minimum-tick multiple), and calculate clamp(score maximum − PRZ width ÷ max(ATR(lookback), XA size × PRZ tightness denominator factor) × PRZ tightness penalty, score minimum, score maximum). D is near the PRZ when its distance from the interval is at most ATR(lookback) × maximum D-to-PRZ distance parameter.
Calculation: Pattern entry and stop = entry is the detection-bar close in Signal Close mode or the D-pivot price in Pattern D mode. For a bullish pattern, raw stop = PRZ lower bound − ATR(lookback) × stop-padding parameter; for a bearish pattern, raw stop = PRZ upper bound + ATR(lookback) × stop-padding parameter. If the raw stop is not beyond entry, use entry minus/plus max(ATR(lookback) × max(stop-padding parameter, minimum stop-ATR factor), minimum tick size × stop minimum-tick multiple) for bullish/bearish patterns respectively.
Calculation: Extra-pattern scores = Three Drives score is Three Drives ratio score × its ratio weight + PRZ tightness × its tightness weight + directional context × its context weight + its swing-time symmetry score × its symmetry weight. Double-pattern score = clamp(double baseline − double similarity penalty × shoulder-level relative difference ÷ top-pattern tolerance + directional context × extra-pattern context weight, score minimum, score maximum). Triple-pattern score uses its separate baseline and similarity penalty in the same formula. Head-and-shoulders score = clamp(its baseline − shoulder penalty × shoulder relative difference ÷ top-pattern tolerance − neckline penalty × neckline ATR ratio + directional context × extra-pattern context weight, score minimum, score maximum). Triangle/wedge score = clamp(its baseline − contraction penalty × final-to-initial range ratio + directional context × extra-pattern context weight, score minimum, score maximum).
