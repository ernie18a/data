Calculation: Neutral zone = ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR fraction, fixed-points parameter, or close × percentage parameter ÷ 100, according to selected zone mode, on the timeframe being evaluated
Calculation: Chart trend state = bullish when MA(selected price field, shorter MA lookback) − MA(selected price field, longer MA lookback) > neutral zone on each of the last confirmation lookback chart bars; bearish when the difference < −neutral zone on each of those bars; neutral otherwise; use the same selected MA type (SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA) for both MAs, with the shorter lookback less than the longer lookback
Calculation: Ribbon displacement = EMA(series that is −ATR(ATR lookback) × offset parameter when chart trend state is bullish, +ATR(ATR lookback) × offset parameter when bearish, and 0 when neutral, smoothing lookback) on chart bars
Long: Chart trend state = bullish
Short: Chart trend state = bearish
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, chart trend state = bullish and chart trend state on the prior chart bar ≠ bullish and (session gate disabled or chart bar is within the configured session in the configured timezone) and (HTF gate disabled or the last completed T1 bar has MA(close, shorter MA lookback, T1) − MA(close, longer MA lookback, T1) > neutral zone computed on T1)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, chart trend state = bearish and chart trend state on the prior chart bar ≠ bearish and (session gate disabled or chart bar is within the configured session in the configured timezone) and (HTF gate disabled or the last completed T1 bar has MA(close, shorter MA lookback, T1) − MA(close, longer MA lookback, T1) < −neutral zone computed on T1)
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, chart trend state = bullish and (session gate disabled or chart bar is within the configured session in the configured timezone) and low ≤ MA(selected price field, shorter MA lookback) + ribbon displacement and close > MA(selected price field, shorter MA lookback) + ribbon displacement and close > open
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, chart trend state = bearish and (session gate disabled or chart bar is within the configured session in the configured timezone) and high ≥ MA(selected price field, longer MA lookback) + ribbon displacement and close < MA(selected price field, longer MA lookback) + ribbon displacement and close < open
