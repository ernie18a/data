Calculation: MA ratio = abs((fast MA / slow MA > 0 ? fast MA / slow MA : slow MA / fast MA) - 1), where fast and slow are separately selected SMA, EMA, DEMA, or TEMA of close with separate lookbacks and the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Effective trend = fast MA above slow MA is bullish and below is bearish, inverted when reverse-trend mode is enabled; reset fast-direction reversal state whenever the fast-above-slow state changes; while fast-direction reversal is enabled, a change in whether fast MA rose from the prior bar sets reversal state and inverts the trend, except that an enabled ratio gate with MA ratio at or below its threshold clears reversal state and uses the direct trend; otherwise retain the current trend state
Calculation: Selected MACD histogram = according to the selector, the maximum of the last five T1 MACD histograms when the latest is positive, their minimum otherwise, their average, or the latest; calculate MACD from the configured source using separate fast EMA, slow EMA, and signal EMA lookbacks on T1 bars with the current T1 bar, then select on chart bars from T1 values
Calculation: Upper and lower tick thresholds = in fixed mode, separate fixed up and down levels; in moving-average mode, EMA of the scaled tick source carried forward on nonnegative bars and EMA of the scaled tick source carried forward on negative bars, using separate uptick and downtick lookbacks; the source is the current T1 histogram when chart timeframe equals T1 and otherwise the selected histogram multiplied by the original or reversal tick multiplier according to fast-direction reversal state
Calculation: Non-neutral tick = (selected MACD histogram > upper tick threshold OR selected MACD histogram < lower tick threshold) AND ((selected MACD histogram > 0 AND effective trend is not bullish) OR (selected MACD histogram <= 0 AND effective trend is not bearish) )
Calculation: Signal ordinal = count of consecutive non-neutral ticks, starting at one and resetting to zero on a neutral tick; reset to zero when the completed trade-group count reaches its configured maximum
Calculation: Entry time eligible = bar time is from configured start-date midnight through end-date midnight in the configured timezone AND (weekday filter is disabled OR the weekday is enabled) AND ((primary session filter is enabled and the bar is in its session) OR (secondary session filter is enabled and the bar is in its session) OR primary session filter is disabled) AND (it is not Friday with the Friday session filter enabled, or the bar is in the Friday session) AND (it is not Sunday with the Sunday session filter enabled, or the bar is in the Sunday session) AND the bar time is outside the configured pre-event and post-event windows for every avoidance-enabled economic event type
Calculation: Countertrend price gates = reset buy and sell thresholds when flat; after a long order, clear the sell threshold and set the buy threshold to signal close × (1 − countertrend distance) if unset or old buy threshold / (1 − countertrend distance) <= signal close; after a short order, clear the buy threshold and set the sell threshold to signal close × (1 + countertrend distance) if unset or old sell threshold / (1 + countertrend distance) <= signal close; long is allowed when the buy threshold is unset or close >= it, and short when the sell threshold is unset or close <= it
Calculation: Exit levels = at each entry, anchor to signal-bar close; separately select take-profit and stop-loss distances from squeezed parameters when squeeze state is active, otherwise reversal parameters when fast-direction reversal state is active, otherwise original parameters; long target and stop are anchor × (1 + take-profit distance) and anchor × (1 − stop-loss distance), and short target and stop are anchor × (1 − take-profit distance) and anchor × (1 + stop-loss distance); squeeze state activates when enabled, after MA ratio has exceeded the minimum difference since the latest fast-above-slow state change and MA ratio is below its separate squeeze threshold, and resets at that state change; on squeeze activation, re-anchor levels to current close
Calculation: Trailing stop = when enabled, a long activates after high reaches entry signal close × (1 + trigger) and trails the highest price since activation by entry signal close × offset; a short activates after low reaches entry signal close × (1 − trigger) and trails the lowest price since activation by entry signal close × offset
Long: effective trend is bullish AND non-neutral tick AND signal ordinal is within the separately configured first and last signal ordinals AND next entry ordinal since the latest fast-above-slow state change is within the separately configured first and last trade ordinals AND completed trade groups since that state change are below the configured maximum AND MA ratio is strictly between its separately configured minimum and maximum AND the buy countertrend price gate allows close AND entry time eligible
Short: effective trend is bearish AND non-neutral tick AND signal ordinal is within the separately configured first and last signal ordinals AND next entry ordinal since the latest fast-above-slow state change is within the separately configured first and last trade ordinals AND completed trade groups since that state change are below the configured maximum AND MA ratio is strictly between its separately configured minimum and maximum AND the sell countertrend price gate allows close AND entry time eligible
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR low reaches the active long trailing stop OR fast-direction reversal state changes to active OR avoidance window is active while close-on-news is enabled OR configured Friday or Saturday close session ends while close-at-session-end is enabled
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR high reaches the active short trailing stop OR fast-direction reversal state changes to active OR avoidance window is active while close-on-news is enabled OR configured Friday or Saturday close session ends while close-at-session-end is enabled
