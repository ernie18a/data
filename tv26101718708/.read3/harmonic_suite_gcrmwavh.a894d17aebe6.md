Calculation: Alternating pivots = PivotHigh(high, pivot-left lookback, pivot-right lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot-left lookback, pivot-right lookback), keeping the latest five alternating confirmed pivots; replace the latest high with a higher confirmed high or the latest low with a lower confirmed low, and append only an opposite-side pivot
Calculation: Bullish ratios = XA = A - X, AB = A - B, BC = C - B, CD = C - D, XD = A - D; AB/XA = AB / XA, BC/AB = BC / AB, CD/BC = CD / BC, XD/XA = XD / XA
Calculation: Bearish ratios = XA = X - A, AB = B - A, BC = B - C, CD = D - C, XD = D - A; AB/XA = AB / XA, BC/AB = BC / AB, CD/BC = CD / BC, XD/XA = XD / XA
Calculation: NearRatio(value, target) = target > 0 AND abs(value - target) / target <= ratio tolerance
Calculation: InRatioRange(value, lower, upper) = value >= lower * (1 - ratio tolerance) AND value <= upper * (1 + ratio tolerance)
Calculation: HarmonicPatternMatch(ratios) = InRatioRange(BC/AB, common BC/AB lower bound, common BC/AB upper bound) AND ((Gartley enabled AND NearRatio(AB/XA, Gartley AB/XA target) AND InRatioRange(CD/BC, Gartley CD/BC lower bound, Gartley CD/BC upper bound) AND NearRatio(XD/XA, Gartley XD/XA target)) OR (Bat enabled AND InRatioRange(AB/XA, Bat AB/XA lower bound, Bat AB/XA upper bound) AND InRatioRange(CD/BC, Bat CD/BC lower bound, Bat CD/BC upper bound) AND NearRatio(XD/XA, Bat XD/XA target)) OR (Butterfly enabled AND NearRatio(AB/XA, Butterfly AB/XA target) AND InRatioRange(CD/BC, Butterfly CD/BC lower bound, Butterfly CD/BC upper bound) AND InRatioRange(XD/XA, Butterfly XD/XA lower bound, Butterfly XD/XA upper bound)) OR (Crab enabled AND InRatioRange(AB/XA, Crab AB/XA lower bound, Crab AB/XA upper bound) AND InRatioRange(CD/BC, Crab CD/BC lower bound, Crab CD/BC upper bound) AND NearRatio(XD/XA, Crab XD/XA target)))
Calculation: LastHarmonicDirection(T) = direction of the most recent harmonic completion calculated on T bars and requested on chart bars from the current T bar (forming in realtime; last completed T bar historically)
Calculation: Fresh(T) = a harmonic signal exists on T AND bar_index(T) - last-signal bar_index(T) <= stale lookback, calculated on T bars
