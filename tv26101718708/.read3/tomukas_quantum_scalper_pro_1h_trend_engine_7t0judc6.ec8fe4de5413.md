Long: close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, trend lookback) > EMA(close, longer macro lookback) AND (low <= EMA(close, shorter pullback lookback) OR low <= EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND EMA(close, fastest lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slower signal lookback) AND MACD line(close, faster lookback, slower lookback, signal lookback) crosses above its signal line AND RSI(close, lookback) > buy threshold AND +DI(lookback) > -DI(lookback) AND ADX(lookback) > minimum strength threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, lookback))
Short: close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, trend lookback) < EMA(close, longer macro lookback) AND (high >= EMA(close, shorter pullback lookback) OR high >= EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND EMA(close, fastest lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slower signal lookback) AND MACD line(close, faster lookback, slower lookback, signal lookback) crosses below its signal line AND RSI(close, lookback) < sell threshold AND -DI(lookback) > +DI(lookback) AND ADX(lookback) > minimum strength threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, lookback))
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR(lookback) * stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR(lookback) * target multiplier
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(lookback) * stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR(lookback) * target multiplier
