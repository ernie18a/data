Calculation: Prior-period high = high(T2, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Prior-period low = low(T2, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Level distance(level, percentage) = abs(level) * percentage / 100
Calculation: Premarket high = highest chart-bar high during the current configured premarket session, available after the session ends until the next day starts
Calculation: Premarket low = lowest chart-bar low during the current configured premarket session, available after the session ends until the next day starts
Calculation: Latest confirmed swing high = latest confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, same swing lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Latest confirmed swing low = latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, same swing lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: In prior-period mid-zone(level) = prior-period high and low exist AND prior-period high > prior-period low AND abs(level - (prior-period high + prior-period low) / 2) < (prior-period high - prior-period low) * mid-range width percentage / 100
Calculation: Bullish sweep-reclaim(level) = low < level - Level distance(level, sweep buffer percentage) AND close > level + Level distance(level, sweep buffer percentage)
Calculation: Bearish sweep-reclaim(level) = high > level + Level distance(level, sweep buffer percentage) AND close < level - Level distance(level, sweep buffer percentage)
Long: close(T1, forming bar) > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, forming bar), sampled on chart bars
Short: close(T1, forming bar) < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, forming bar), sampled on chart bars
Long: during either configured signal session AND (Bullish sweep-reclaim(prior-period low) OR Bullish sweep-reclaim(premarket low) OR Bullish sweep-reclaim(latest confirmed swing low))
Short: during either configured signal session AND (Bearish sweep-reclaim(prior-period high) OR Bearish sweep-reclaim(premarket high) OR Bearish sweep-reclaim(latest confirmed swing high))
Long: during either configured signal session AND a bullish setup is pending at level x from an earlier chart bar, armed during a signal session only when no bullish setup was pending and choosing the first matching sweep level in order prior-period low, premarket low, latest confirmed swing low AND current bar index is later than the setup bar index AND current bar index minus setup bar index is no greater than the maximum wait bars AND no chart bar from setup through current has closed below x - Level distance(x, invalidation percentage) AND low <= x + Level distance(x, retest tolerance percentage) AND close > x AND (directional-body filter disabled OR close > open) AND (prior-bar break filter disabled OR high > prior chart bar high) AND (mid-range filter disabled OR NOT In prior-period mid-zone(x)) AND (HTF bias filter disabled OR close(T1, forming bar) > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, forming bar))
Short: during either configured signal session AND a bearish setup is pending at level x from an earlier chart bar, armed during a signal session only when no bearish setup was pending and choosing the first matching sweep level in order prior-period high, premarket high, latest confirmed swing high AND current bar index is later than the setup bar index AND current bar index minus setup bar index is no greater than the maximum wait bars AND no chart bar from setup through current has closed above x + Level distance(x, invalidation percentage) AND high >= x - Level distance(x, retest tolerance percentage) AND close < x AND (directional-body filter disabled OR close < open) AND (prior-bar break filter disabled OR low < prior chart bar low) AND (mid-range filter disabled OR NOT In prior-period mid-zone(x)) AND (HTF bias filter disabled OR close(T1, forming bar) < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, forming bar))
