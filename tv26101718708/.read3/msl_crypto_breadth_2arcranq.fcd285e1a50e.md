Calculation: Participation = 100 × count(valid selected basket members whose T1 close > selected MA type(close, MA lookback) on T1 bars) / count(valid selected basket members), when valid count ≥ min(minimum live feeds, selected basket size); valid members have both close and MA. Compute participation and signal tests on chart bars from T1 values, using the still-forming T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically; calculate MAs on T1 bars.
Long: Participation crosses above the midpoint threshold on chart bars.
Long: ROC(reference-symbol close from T1, comparison lookback) ≤ −reference-move threshold AND change(Participation, comparison lookback) ≥ breadth-gap threshold AND this combined condition was false on the previous chart bar AND at least one comparison lookback of chart bars has elapsed since the previous bullish divergence.
Short: ROC(reference-symbol close from T1, comparison lookback) ≥ reference-move threshold AND change(Participation, comparison lookback) ≤ −breadth-gap threshold AND this combined condition was false on the previous chart bar AND at least one comparison lookback of chart bars has elapsed since the previous bearish divergence.
Exit: long, Participation crosses below the midpoint threshold on chart bars.
