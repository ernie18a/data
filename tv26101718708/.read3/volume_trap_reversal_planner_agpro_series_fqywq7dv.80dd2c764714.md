Calculation: Adjusted ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) with missing values treated as zero, TrueRange, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Upper reference = Highest(high[1], trap reference lookback)
Calculation: Lower reference = Lowest(low[1], trap reference lookback)
Calculation: Candle range = max(high - low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Body share = abs(close - open) / candle range
Calculation: Upper wick share = (high - max(open, close)) / candle range
Calculation: Lower wick share = (min(open, close) - low) / candle range
Calculation: Bull close location = (close - low) / candle range
Calculation: Bear close location = (high - close) / candle range
Calculation: Relative volume = volume with missing values treated as zero, divided by SMA(volume with missing values treated as zero, volume baseline lookback); use the current volume if the SMA is missing and use one if the resulting baseline is zero
Calculation: Effective trap thresholds = base spike RVOL plus the sensitivity-selected RVOL offset; the sensitivity-selected wick threshold; max(base sweep ATR buffer plus the sensitivity-selected sweep offset, zero); base recovery ATR buffer plus the sensitivity-selected recovery offset
Calculation: Confirmation thresholds = sensitivity-mode-selected body-share threshold, close-location threshold, and ATR buffer
Calculation: Normalized high(value, weak, strong) = zero if strong equals weak, otherwise clamp((value - weak) / (strong - weak) × 100, 0, 100)
Calculation: Trap score(RVOL, wick share, close location, penetration ATR) = clamp(Normalized high(RVOL, effective spike RVOL × weak-volume fraction, max(full-score RVOL, effective spike RVOL + full-score RVOL margin)) × volume weight + Normalized high(wick share, weak wick threshold, strong wick threshold) × wick weight + Normalized high(close location, weak recovery threshold, strong recovery threshold) × recovery weight + Normalized high(penetration ATR, effective sweep buffer, strong sweep threshold) × sweep weight + (setup bonus if RVOL ≥ effective spike RVOL, otherwise Normalized high(RVOL, setup weak-volume threshold, effective spike RVOL) × setup weight), 0, base score cap)
Calculation: Confirmation score(body share, close location, follow-through ATR) = clamp(Normalized high(body share, weak confirmation body threshold, strong confirmation body threshold) × body weight + Normalized high(close location, weak confirmation close threshold, strong confirmation close threshold) × close weight + Normalized high(follow-through ATR, weak follow-through threshold, strong follow-through threshold) × follow-through weight, 0, confirmation score cap)
Calculation: Long trap score = Trap score(Relative volume, Lower wick share, Bull close location, (Lower reference - low) / Adjusted ATR)
Calculation: Short trap score = Trap score(Relative volume, Upper wick share, Bear close location, (high - Upper reference) / Adjusted ATR)
Calculation: Long risk edge = trap-bar low - trap-bar Adjusted ATR × risk-edge ATR buffer
Calculation: Short risk edge = trap-bar high + trap-bar Adjusted ATR × risk-edge ATR buffer
Calculation: Long target = max(trap-bar upper reference, trap-bar close + trap-bar Adjusted ATR × minimum target-room ATR, trap-bar close + trap-bar Adjusted ATR × fallback-target ATR)
Calculation: Short target = min(trap-bar lower reference, trap-bar close - trap-bar Adjusted ATR × minimum target-room ATR, trap-bar close - trap-bar Adjusted ATR × fallback-target ATR)
Long: When the side setting permits long, the upper and lower references are ready, the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar, and the bar is at least the event-spacing parameter after the prior trap event: the downside probe (low < lower reference - Adjusted ATR × effective sweep buffer) AND close > lower reference + Adjusted ATR × effective recovery buffer AND lower wick share ≥ effective wick threshold AND Relative volume ≥ effective spike RVOL AND NOT (the corresponding upside trap raw condition); OR both downside and upside trap raw conditions hold AND Long trap score ≥ Short trap score; AND Long trap score ≥ trap-watch score
Short: When the side setting permits short, the upper and lower references are ready, the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar, and the bar is at least the event-spacing parameter after the prior trap event: the upside probe (high > upper reference + Adjusted ATR × effective sweep buffer) AND close < upper reference - Adjusted ATR × effective recovery buffer AND upper wick share ≥ effective wick threshold AND Relative volume ≥ effective spike RVOL AND NOT (the corresponding downside trap raw condition); OR both downside and upside trap raw conditions hold AND Short trap score > Long trap score; AND Short trap score ≥ trap-watch score
Long: Watch labels are enabled AND the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND no new trap event occurs AND Relative volume ≥ effective spike RVOL AND the side setting permits long AND low < lower reference - Adjusted ATR × effective sweep buffer AND Long trap score ≥ trap-watch score AND no event label was printed within the label-cooldown parameter bars
Short: Watch labels are enabled AND the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND no new trap event occurs AND Relative volume ≥ effective spike RVOL AND NOT (the side setting permits long AND low < lower reference - Adjusted ATR × effective sweep buffer) AND the side setting permits short AND high > upper reference + Adjusted ATR × effective sweep buffer AND Short trap score ≥ trap-watch score AND no event label was printed within the label-cooldown parameter bars
Long: After an active long trap event and before it has been confirmed, failed, or expired, on a later bar within the confirmation window, the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND close > open AND Body share ≥ confirmation body threshold AND Bull close location ≥ confirmation close-location threshold AND close > max(trap-bar open, trap-bar close) + trap-bar Adjusted ATR × confirmation ATR buffer AND close ≥ Long risk edge AND clamp(Long trap score + Confirmation score(Body share, Bull close location, (close - lower reference at trap) / Adjusted ATR), 0, 100) ≥ minimum reversal score
Short: After an active short trap event and before it has been confirmed, failed, or expired, on a later bar within the confirmation window, the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND close < open AND Body share ≥ confirmation body threshold AND Bear close location ≥ confirmation close-location threshold AND close < min(trap-bar open, trap-bar close) - trap-bar Adjusted ATR × confirmation ATR buffer AND close ≤ Short risk edge AND clamp(Short trap score + Confirmation score(Body share, Bear close location, (upper reference at trap - close) / Adjusted ATR), 0, 100) ≥ minimum reversal score
Exit: long, An active long trap has not previously failed or expired AND the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND close < Long risk edge
Exit: short, An active short trap has not previously failed or expired AND the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND close > Short risk edge
Exit: long, An active long trap was confirmed on an earlier bar and has not failed or reached its target AND the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND close ≥ Long risk edge AND high ≥ Long target
Exit: short, An active short trap was confirmed on an earlier bar and has not failed or reached its target AND the bar-close confirmation gate permits the bar AND close ≤ Short risk edge AND low ≤ Short target
