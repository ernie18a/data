Calculation: Normalized separation = (EMA(close, short EMA lookback) - EMA(close, long EMA lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: Normalized slope = (EMA(close, long EMA lookback) - EMA(close, long EMA lookback)[slope lookback]) / ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: Regime = after the minimum warmup bars, form a Bull candidate when ADX(DMI lookback, ADX smoothing) >= trend-entry ADX threshold, normalized separation >= trend separation threshold, and normalized slope >= trend slope threshold; form a Bear candidate when ADX >= trend-entry ADX threshold, normalized separation <= -trend separation threshold, and normalized slope <= -trend slope threshold; form a Range candidate when ADX <= trend-exit ADX threshold, absolute normalized separation <= range separation threshold, and absolute normalized slope <= range slope threshold; otherwise form no candidate. Confirm Bull or Bear after the trend confirmation count consecutive bars with that candidate, or Range after the range confirmation count consecutive bars with that candidate. Set the regime to a confirmed candidate; otherwise retain a prior Bull while ADX >= trend-exit ADX threshold and normalized separation and slope are positive, retain a prior Bear while ADX >= trend-exit ADX threshold and both are negative, or retain a prior Range while ADX < trend-entry ADX threshold, absolute normalized separation < range holding separation threshold, and absolute normalized slope < range holding slope threshold; otherwise set Transition. The regime is undefined before warmup.
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, Regime on chart bars = Bull AND the prior chart bar's Regime is defined and is not Bull
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, Regime on chart bars = Bear AND the prior chart bar's Regime is defined and is not Bear
Exit: Long, on a confirmed chart bar, Regime on chart bars = Range OR Regime on chart bars = Transition
Exit: Short, on a confirmed chart bar, Regime on chart bars = Range OR Regime on chart bars = Transition
