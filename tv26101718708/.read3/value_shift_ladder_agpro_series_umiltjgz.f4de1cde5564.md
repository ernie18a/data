Calculation: Ladder state = Build a rolling volume profile from the configured lookback by dividing its high-low range into configured price bins and distributing each bar’s volume equally across bins touched by its low-low range; select the highest-volume qualifying bin, then successive highest-volume qualifying bins up to the shelf-count limit, excluding bins within the minimum ATR separation of a selected bin. Match each selected price to a nonbroken shelf within the minimum ATR separation and update its price by confidence-weighted blending; otherwise create a Held shelf in creation order. A Held shelf becomes Broken after the configured number of recent closes each lie beyond its ATR break distance, adjusted by confidence and age multipliers. A Broken shelf becomes Reclaimed after the configured number of closes in its ATR reclaim band within the configured dwell window; a Reclaimed shelf becomes Held after its age threshold while close remains in that band. Active shelves are Held or Reclaimed shelves within the configured ATR proximity of close. State is Reclaiming if any shelf becomes Reclaimed on the bar, otherwise Broken if any becomes Broken, otherwise Building if exactly one active shelf exists, otherwise Rising if at least two active shelves in creation order have nondecreasing prices with at least one increase, Falling if they have nonincreasing prices with at least one decrease, Mixed if neither condition holds, and Neutral if none exist.
