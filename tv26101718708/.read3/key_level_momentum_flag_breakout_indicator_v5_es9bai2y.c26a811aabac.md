Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA(close, fast EMA lookback)
Calculation: Slow EMA = EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)
Calculation: VWAP = VWAP(close)
Calculation: ATR = ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: Volume SMA = SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD histogram(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback)
Calculation: Bull trend = close > VWAP AND Fast EMA > Slow EMA AND close > Fast EMA
Calculation: Bear trend = close < VWAP AND Fast EMA < Slow EMA AND close < Fast EMA
Calculation: Bull key proximity = |close - premarket high| <= ATR * key-distance multiplier OR |close - last completed T1 high| <= ATR * key-distance multiplier OR close > premarket high OR close > last completed T1 high OR close > VWAP
Calculation: Bear key proximity = |close - premarket low| <= ATR * key-distance multiplier OR |close - last completed T1 low| <= ATR * key-distance multiplier OR close < premarket low OR close < last completed T1 low OR close < VWAP
Calculation: Signal allowed = session-only filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured signal window in its configured timezone

Short: Fast EMA <= Slow EMA
Long: MACD histogram > 0
Short: MACD histogram <= 0
