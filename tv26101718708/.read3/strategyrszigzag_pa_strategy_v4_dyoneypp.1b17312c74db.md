Calculation: ZigZag pivots = On selected regular or Heikin-Ashi bars, mark a down reversal when the previous bar is up (close >= open) and the current bar is down (close <= open), unless the previous direction was already down; emit the highest high over the pivot-lookback parameter. Otherwise, mark an up reversal when the previous bar is down and the current bar is up, unless the previous direction was already up; emit the lowest low over the pivot-lookback parameter. Otherwise emit no pivot. Direction is down after an up-to-down reversal, up after a down-to-up reversal, and otherwise carries forward from an initially neutral state. Calculate on chart bars or on T1 bars; in T1 mode use the still-forming T1 bar and sample its returned value on the chart bar where each new T1 interval begins.
Calculation: Pattern points = X, A, B, C, and D are the five most recent nonempty ZigZag pivots, ordered oldest to newest.

Calculation: BCD = abs(C-D)/abs(B-C)
Calculation: FibLevel(rate) = D - abs(D-C)*rate when D>C, otherwise D + abs(D-C)*rate
