Calculation: Bullish probability = 100 × (weighted HTF bias + sum of the three separate HTF weight parameters) / (2 × sum of those weights), where weighted HTF bias sums each timeframe's weight times 1 if its still-forming close is above EMA(close, shared EMA lookback, that timeframe), −1 if below, or 0 if equal; T1 is longer than T2, and T2 is longer than T3; values are taken on chart bars from the current bar of each timeframe
Long: Bullish probability > bullish threshold AND within the configured session AND ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR lookback) AND ((close > EMA(close, shared EMA lookback) AND close > VWAP(hlc3) AND low <= EMA(close, shared EMA lookback)) OR (chart-bar time is after session start plus opening-range duration AND an opening range has formed AND close > highest(high, chart bars in the opening range from session start through its configured duration) AND close > VWAP(hlc3)))
Short: Bullish probability < bearish threshold AND within the configured session AND ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR lookback) AND ((close < EMA(close, shared EMA lookback) AND close < VWAP(hlc3) AND high >= EMA(close, shared EMA lookback)) OR (chart-bar time is after session start plus opening-range duration AND an opening range has formed AND close < lowest(low, chart bars in the opening range from session start through its configured duration) AND close < VWAP(hlc3)))
Calculation: Long stop = low of the latest bar satisfying a long-entry condition − ATR(ATR lookback) on that bar
Calculation: Long risk = close of the latest bar satisfying a long-entry condition − long stop
Calculation: Short stop = high of the latest bar satisfying a short-entry condition + ATR(ATR lookback) on that bar
Calculation: Short risk = short stop − close of the latest bar satisfying a short-entry condition
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= (close of the latest bar satisfying a long-entry condition + long risk × first-target reward/risk parameter) OR high >= (close of the latest bar satisfying a long-entry condition + long risk × runner reward/risk parameter)
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= (close of the latest bar satisfying a short-entry condition − short risk × first-target reward/risk parameter) OR low <= (close of the latest bar satisfying a short-entry condition − short risk × runner reward/risk parameter)
