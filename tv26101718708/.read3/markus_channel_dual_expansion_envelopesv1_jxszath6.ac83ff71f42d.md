Calculation: Selected base moving average = manually selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of close over the center lookback, or, in adaptive mode, the candidate with the lowest score, where score = SMA((candidate − close)^2, center lookback) + noise penalty × SMA((change in candidate)^2, center lookback); ties retain priority EMA, SMA, WMA, RMA, VWMA, and the selection switches from the prior candidate only when the lowest score is less than the prior candidate's score × (1 − switching hysteresis).
Calculation: Liquidity weighted mean = sum(adjusted close × activity weight) / sum(activity weight) over the center lookback, or SMA(close, center lookback) if the weight sum is zero; adjusted close = close + wick adjustment coefficient × (lower wick − upper wick); activity weight = clamp(volume ratio over center lookback / max(true range / SMA(true range, center lookback), relative-range floor), activity-weight floor, activity-weight ceiling)^activity-weight exponent, where volume ratio over center lookback = volume / SMA(volume, center lookback) or one if that average is zero, and true-range ratio = true range / SMA(true range, center lookback) or one if that average is at most tick size.
Calculation: Center line = selected base moving average × (1 − liquidity blend) + liquidity weighted mean × liquidity blend.
Calculation: Tick-volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or one if that average is zero.
Calculation: Upper and lower Markus bands = center line ± standard deviation(close, center lookback) × Bollinger multiplier × (1 + asymmetry strength × RMA(respective wick, center lookback) / max(ATR(short ATR lookback), tick size)), using the upper wick for the upper band and lower wick for the lower band.
Calculation: Dynamic Keltner multiplier = clamp(minimum Keltner multiplier + (tick-volume ratio − neutral volume ratio) × volume sensitivity, minimum Keltner multiplier, maximum Keltner multiplier).
Calculation: Markus channel width = upper Markus band − lower Markus band.
Calculation: Average Markus channel width = SMA(Markus channel width, expansion lookback).
Calculation: Expansion volume boost = 1 + max(0, tick-volume ratio − neutral volume ratio) × envelope volume sensitivity.
Calculation: ATR normalization factor = clamp(ATR(short ATR lookback) / ATR(longer ATR lookback), lower normalization bound, upper normalization bound), or one if the longer-lookback ATR is zero.
Calculation: Expansion envelope offset = average Markus channel width × envelope width fraction × envelope multiplier × expansion volume boost × ATR normalization factor; upper and lower expansion envelope = respective Markus band ± this offset.
Calculation: Upper trigger latch = on an upper Markus-band expansion, the envelope upper in dynamic mode or max(previous latch, envelope upper) in peak-hold mode; otherwise max(upper Markus band, previous latch − average Markus channel width / expansion lookback), using the upper Markus band if there is no previous latch. Lower trigger latch = on a lower Markus-band expansion, the envelope lower in dynamic mode or min(previous latch, envelope lower) in peak-hold mode; otherwise min(lower Markus band, previous latch + average Markus channel width / expansion lookback), using the lower Markus band if there is no previous latch.
Calculation: Upper and lower trigger levels = EMA(respective trigger latch, trigger smoothing lookback) ± average Markus channel width × trigger buffer multiplier × expansion volume boost × ATR normalization factor.
Calculation: Selected crossover boundary = center line for the midline target; the respective upper or lower Markus band for the Markus-band target; the respective upper or lower expansion envelope for the expansion-envelope target; or the respective upper or lower trigger level for the trigger-channel target.
Calculation: Tension = EMA((close > center line ? 1 : −1) × abs(close − close[path-efficiency lookback]) / max(SMA(abs(change in close), path-efficiency lookback) × path-efficiency lookback, tick size), tension smoothing lookback).
Long: high >= upper Markus band AND Markus channel width > Markus channel width[1] AND volume > volume[1].
Short: low <= lower Markus band AND Markus channel width > Markus channel width[1] AND volume > volume[1].
Long: close crosses above the selected crossover boundary AND (crossover filter disabled OR (tension > crossover tension threshold AND (volume expansion not required OR tick-volume ratio > neutral volume ratio))).
Short: close crosses below the selected crossover boundary AND (crossover filter disabled OR (tension < −crossover tension threshold AND (volume expansion not required OR tick-volume ratio > neutral volume ratio))).
Long: close > center line.
Short: close <= center line.
Long: candle coloring enabled AND tension coloring enabled AND tension > regime-color tension threshold.
Short: candle coloring enabled AND tension coloring enabled AND tension < −regime-color tension threshold.
