Calculation: Active zones = same-side confirmed swing pivots on chart bars, using separate left-lookback and right-confirmation parameters; merge a pivot into a same-side zone within cluster tolerance × ATR, otherwise pair it with the first buffered same-side pivot within that tolerance; set bounds from pivot extremes, padded to minimum height and capped at maximum height in ATR units; use the highest-volume pivot as the midline when volume-anchor mode is selected and it lies within the bounds, otherwise use the geometric midpoint; start paired zones with two touches, add touches for merged pivots and overlapping retests separated by the retest interval, retain pre-flip touches when a zone flips, expire unpaired pivots after the buffer age, and remove zones that break without flipping or exceed the cull distance in ATR units.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing levels = latest confirmed pivot high and low, computed on T1 bars with separate left-lookback and right-confirmation parameters and mapped to chart bars with gaps off and lookahead off; use the T1 bar still forming in realtime and the latest completed T1 bar historically.
Calculation: Confluence score = int(min(score cap, base score - touch decay / (1 + (max(total touches, minimum touches) - minimum touches) × touch growth) + (higher-timeframe alignment enabled and zone midline within alignment tolerance × ATR of the latest higher-timeframe pivot matching the zone side ? alignment bonus : 0) + (round-number boost enabled and zone midline within round-number tolerance × ATR of a calculated round-number level ? round-number bonus : 0) + (volume boost enabled and the zone formed or was retested on volume above its volume-average threshold ? volume bonus : 0))).
Long: no displayed active zone is at or testing price AND at least one displayed support zone is below close AND no displayed resistance zone is above close.
Short: no displayed active zone is at or testing price AND at least one displayed resistance zone is above close AND no displayed support zone is below close.
Long: a resistance zone not created on the current bar closes above its upper boundary AND role flipping is enabled.
Short: a support zone not created on the current bar closes below its lower boundary AND role flipping is enabled.
Long: a support zone has confluence score at least the rejection-score threshold AND low <= its upper boundary AND close > its upper boundary AND close > open.
Short: a resistance zone has confluence score at least the rejection-score threshold AND high >= its lower boundary AND close < its lower boundary AND close < open.
