Calculation: Seller zone = [T1 open of the bar still forming + min(SMA(T1 high - T1 low, shorter lookback) from the last completed T1 bar, SMA(T1 high - T1 low, longer lookback) from the last completed T1 bar) × projection factor, T1 open of the bar still forming + max(SMA(T1 high - T1 low, shorter lookback) from the last completed T1 bar, SMA(T1 high - T1 low, longer lookback) from the last completed T1 bar) × projection factor]; these T1 values are mapped to chart bars
Calculation: Buyer zone = [T1 open of the bar still forming − max(SMA(T1 high - T1 low, shorter lookback) from the last completed T1 bar, SMA(T1 high - T1 low, longer lookback) from the last completed T1 bar) × projection factor, T1 open of the bar still forming − min(SMA(T1 high - T1 low, shorter lookback) from the last completed T1 bar, SMA(T1 high - T1 low, longer lookback) from the last completed T1 bar) × projection factor]; these T1 values are mapped to chart bars
Long: high >= Buyer zone lower boundary AND low <= Buyer zone upper boundary AND NOT (high[1] >= Buyer zone lower boundary[1] AND low[1] <= Buyer zone upper boundary[1])
Short: high >= Seller zone lower boundary AND low <= Seller zone upper boundary AND NOT (high[1] >= Seller zone lower boundary[1] AND low[1] <= Seller zone upper boundary[1])
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, close > Seller zone upper boundary AND close[1] <= Seller zone upper boundary[1]
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, close < Buyer zone lower boundary AND close[1] >= Buyer zone lower boundary[1]
