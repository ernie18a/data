Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (EMA(volume, short lookback) − EMA(volume, long lookback)) / EMA(volume, long lookback)
Calculation: Long PSAR distance = signal-bar close − Parabolic SAR(high, low, start, increment, maximum) on the signal bar
Calculation: Short PSAR distance = Parabolic SAR(high, low, start, increment, maximum) − signal-bar close on the signal bar
Long: MACD histogram(MACD source, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, oscillator averaging type, signal averaging type)[1] < 0 AND MACD histogram(MACD source, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, oscillator averaging type, signal averaging type) > 0 AND close > EMA(EMA source, lookback) AND Volume oscillator < 0 AND Parabolic SAR(high, low, start, increment, maximum) is in an uptrend
Short: MACD histogram(MACD source, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, oscillator averaging type, signal averaging type)[1] > 0 AND MACD histogram(MACD source, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, oscillator averaging type, signal averaging type) < 0 AND close < EMA(EMA source, lookback) AND Volume oscillator > 0 AND Parabolic SAR(high, low, start, increment, maximum) is in a downtrend
Exit: long, long position was active one bar ago AND (close crosses above signal-bar close + Long PSAR distance OR high crosses above signal-bar close + Long PSAR distance OR close crosses below signal-bar close − Long PSAR distance OR low crosses below signal-bar close − Long PSAR distance)
Exit: short, short position was active one bar ago AND (close crosses below signal-bar close − Short PSAR distance OR low crosses below signal-bar close − Short PSAR distance OR close crosses above signal-bar close + Short PSAR distance OR high crosses above signal-bar close + Short PSAR distance)
