Calculation: HTFRangeAverage = mean of high minus low over the most recent completed T1 bars, up to the range-average lookback parameter; evaluated on chart bars taking values from T1.
Calculation: KeyLevelTouch(a,b) = the interval from min(a,b) minus HTFRangeAverage times the key-level touch tolerance to max(a,b) plus that tolerance overlaps an active zone generated from completed T1 bars. Zones are pivot-high wicks, pivot-low wicks, bullish or bearish FVGs, and full ranges of the nearest prior opposing candles when a T1 candle's body exceeds the prior-body average times the displacement multiplier. Pivot zones use the key-level pivot length on both sides; FVGs use the configured gap separation; opposing candles are searched within the configured lookback. A zone deactivates when a chart bar spans it or closes across it from one side to the other; retain only the newest configured zone count.
Calculation: CISDLevel(side) = the open of the oldest candle in the most recent contiguous run of bullish candles for a short setup or bearish candles for a long setup, searching the configured number of chart bars; undefined if no such run is found.
Calculation: ManagedStop(side) = the most extreme chart-bar high since the TBS body sweep plus ATR(chart bars, stop ATR lookback) times the stop buffer for a short, or the most extreme low since the sweep minus that amount for a long; after TP1, use entry price instead if breakeven is enabled.
Calculation: CRTTargets(side, entry) = for a short, TP1 is the CRT midpoint if below entry, TP2 is the CRT low if below entry, and TP3 is the CRT low minus CRT range times the extension multiplier; for a long, TP1 is the midpoint if above entry, TP2 is the CRT high if above entry, and TP3 is the CRT high plus CRT range times the extension multiplier.
Short: On a later T1 bar while a CRT range awaits manipulation, high(T1, last completed bar) > CRT high AND close(T1, last completed bar) < CRT high AND (low(T1, last completed bar) >= CRT low OR high(T1, last completed bar) - CRT high >= CRT low - low(T1, last completed bar)).
Long: On a later T1 bar while a CRT range awaits manipulation, low(T1, last completed bar) < CRT low AND close(T1, last completed bar) > CRT low AND (high(T1, last completed bar) <= CRT high OR CRT low - low(T1, last completed bar) > high(T1, last completed bar) - CRT high).
Short: While a bearish CRT manipulation is active, a chart bar closes above the latest confirmed pivot high AND a chart bar closes below the latest bullish candle's low; require the confirmation setting to be off or the confirming bar to be closed.
Long: While a bullish CRT manipulation is active, a chart bar closes below the latest confirmed pivot low AND a chart bar closes above the latest bearish candle's high; require the confirmation setting to be off or the confirming bar to be closed.
Short: When enabled, a confirmed chart bar forms a pivot high using the swing-pivot length on both sides.
Long: When enabled, a confirmed chart bar forms a pivot low using the swing-pivot length on both sides.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a non-overlapping compressed range persists for at least the compression lookback, then close breaks above its fixed high; compression means highest(high, lookback) minus lowest(low, lookback) < ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) times the compression factor times sqrt(lookback).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a non-overlapping compressed range persists for at least the compression lookback, then close breaks below its fixed low; compression means highest(high, lookback) minus lowest(low, lookback) < ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) times the compression factor times sqrt(lookback).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the latest centered pivot high using the sweep-pivot length on both sides has not been close-broken or swept before, high exceeds it, close returns below it, and at least the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous bearish sweep.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, the latest centered pivot low using the sweep-pivot length on both sides has not been close-broken or swept before, low falls below it, close returns above it, and at least the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous bullish sweep.
Short: With previous completed T2 high and low available, current chart high > that high AND current chart close < that high, OR current chart close < that low; retain the last assigned bias when none of these conditions fires.
Long: With previous completed T2 high and low available, current chart close > that high, OR current chart low < that low AND current chart close > that low AND NOT (current chart high > that high AND current chart close < that high); retain the last assigned bias when none of these conditions fires.
Short: After bearish TBS confirmation, on a chart bar where close < low of the preceding chart bar, with the short model enabled or all models selected, the CRT manipulation is active, the key-level requirement is disabled or KeyLevelTouch(CRT high, manipulation high) was true at manipulation, the current time is outside configured blocked hours for the selected season and timezone, and the confirmation setting is off or the bar is closed; fire once per setup.
Long: After bullish TBS confirmation, on a chart bar where close > high of the preceding chart bar, with the long model enabled or all models selected, the CRT manipulation is active, the key-level requirement is disabled or KeyLevelTouch(CRT low, manipulation low) was true at manipulation, the current time is outside configured blocked hours for the selected season and timezone, and the confirmation setting is off or the bar is closed; fire once per setup.
Short: After bearish TBS confirmation, on a chart bar where close < CISDLevel(short), with the short model enabled or all models selected, the CRT manipulation is active, the key-level requirement is disabled or KeyLevelTouch(CRT high, manipulation high) was true at manipulation, the current time is outside configured blocked hours for the selected season and timezone, and the confirmation setting is off or the bar is closed; fire once per setup.
Long: After bullish TBS confirmation, on a chart bar where close > CISDLevel(long), with the long model enabled or all models selected, the CRT manipulation is active, the key-level requirement is disabled or KeyLevelTouch(CRT low, manipulation low) was true at manipulation, the current time is outside configured blocked hours for the selected season and timezone, and the confirmation setting is off or the bar is closed; fire once per setup.
Short: After bearish TBS confirmation and a chart-bar close below the MSS reference, a bearish FVG forms, with the short model enabled or all models selected, the CRT manipulation active, the key-level requirement disabled or KeyLevelTouch(CRT high, manipulation high) true at manipulation, the current time outside configured blocked hours for the selected season and timezone, and the confirmation setting off or the bar closed; fire once per setup.
Long: After bullish TBS confirmation and a chart-bar close above the MSS reference, a bullish FVG forms, with the long model enabled or all models selected, the CRT manipulation active, the key-level requirement disabled or KeyLevelTouch(CRT low, manipulation low) true at manipulation, the current time outside configured blocked hours for the selected season and timezone, and the confirmation setting off or the bar closed; fire once per setup.
Exit: Long, while trade tracking is enabled, an active managed long trade reaches a later chart bar and low <= ManagedStop(long), or the stop is not reached and high reaches a valid TP1, valid TP2, or TP3 from CRTTargets(long, entry). Check the stop before targets on each bar.
Exit: Short, while trade tracking is enabled, an active managed short trade reaches a later chart bar and high >= ManagedStop(short), or the stop is not reached and low reaches a valid TP1, valid TP2, or TP3 from CRTTargets(short, entry). Check the stop before targets on each bar.
