Calculation: ATR length = base ATR length when adaptive ATR is off; otherwise max(1, round(base ATR length × duration(T2) / chart-bar duration)); evaluate ATR on chart bars
Calculation: Session VWAP bands = VWAP ± sqrt(max(volume-weighted mean of HLC3² − VWAP², 0)), where VWAP is the volume-weighted mean of HLC3; reset sums at each T1 boundary and calculate on chart bars using the current, still-forming T1 session
Calculation: Candle anatomy = body abs(close − open), range high − low, upper wick high − max(open, close), lower wick min(open, close) − low
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise 0
Calculation: Doji = range > 0 AND body ≤ range × doji body percentage
Calculation: Bullish setup candle = low ≤ lowest(low, high/low lookback) AND (Doji OR (range > 0 AND body ≤ range × small-body percentage AND lower wick ≥ body × lower-wick/body multiple AND upper wick ≤ body))
Calculation: Bearish setup candle = high ≥ highest(high, high/low lookback) AND (Doji OR (range > 0 AND body ≤ range × small-body percentage AND upper wick ≥ body × upper-wick/body multiple AND lower wick ≤ body))
Long: Bullish setup candle
Short: Bearish setup candle
Long: Bullish setup candle[1] AND close position ≥ 1 − close-position percentage AND high − low ≤ ATR(ATR length) × maximum candle-size multiple AND ((trigger mode is engulfing-only AND close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND open ≤ close[1] AND close ≥ open[1]) OR (trigger mode allows structural triggers AND ((close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND open ≤ close[1] AND close ≥ open[1]) OR (low > low[1] AND close > high[1])))) AND (both VWAP filters are disabled OR close[1] < current lower Session VWAP band) AND the full bullish confirmation predicate was false on the previous chart bar
Short: Bearish setup candle[1] AND close position ≤ close-position percentage AND high − low ≤ ATR(ATR length) × maximum candle-size multiple AND ((trigger mode is engulfing-only AND close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND open ≥ close[1] AND close ≤ open[1]) OR (trigger mode allows structural triggers AND ((close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND open ≥ close[1] AND close ≤ open[1]) OR (high < high[1] AND close < low[1])))) AND (both VWAP filters are disabled OR close[1] > current upper Session VWAP band) AND the full bearish confirmation predicate was false on the previous chart bar
Exit: Long, low ≤ signal-bar low − ATR(signal bar) × ATR-buffer percentage − spread OR high ≥ signal-bar high + ATR(signal bar) × ATR-buffer percentage + max(signal-bar range + 2 × ATR(signal bar) × ATR-buffer percentage, ATR(signal bar) × stop-ATR multiple) × take-profit risk multiple − spread
Exit: Short, high ≥ signal-bar high + ATR(signal bar) × ATR-buffer percentage + spread OR low ≤ signal-bar low − ATR(signal bar) × ATR-buffer percentage − max(signal-bar range + 2 × ATR(signal bar) × ATR-buffer percentage, ATR(signal bar) × stop-ATR multiple) × take-profit risk multiple + spread
