Calculation: Bearish candle = close < open AND open − close > candle-body fraction parameter × (high − low)
Calculation: Long stop price = max(previous long stop price, close × (1 − trailing-offset parameter / 100)) when trailing is enabled and close profit percentage >= trailing-activation parameter; otherwise previous long stop price; initialize at entry to entry price × (1 − stop-loss parameter / 100)
Calculation: Short stop price = min(previous short stop price, close × (1 + trailing-offset parameter / 100)) when trailing is enabled and close profit percentage >= trailing-activation parameter; otherwise previous short stop price; initialize at entry to entry price × (1 + stop-loss parameter / 100)
Long: (EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slow lookback) OR MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) line crosses above its signal line OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the prior bar < RSI oversold parameter AND current RSI > RSI oversold parameter AND current RSI > prior RSI OR close > highest high over the prior breakout lookback bars, excluding the current bar OR low <= EMA(close, fast lookback) × long pullback factor parameter AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback) AND bullish candle OR prior close <= current lower Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, deviation parameter) AND close > current lower Bollinger Band AND bullish candle) AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier parameter AND (ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing parameter) filter disabled OR ADX > ADX threshold parameter) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI upper-entry parameter
Short: (EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slow lookback) OR MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) line crosses below its signal line OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the prior bar > RSI overbought parameter AND current RSI < RSI overbought parameter AND current RSI < prior RSI OR close < lowest low over the prior breakout lookback bars, excluding the current bar OR high >= EMA(close, fast lookback) × short pullback factor parameter AND close < EMA(close, fast lookback) AND bearish candle OR prior close >= current upper Bollinger Band(close, band lookback, deviation parameter) AND close < current upper Bollinger Band AND bearish candle) AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier parameter AND (ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing parameter) filter disabled OR ADX > ADX threshold parameter) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI lower-entry parameter
Exit: long, low <= long stop price OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit parameter / 100) OR EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slow lookback) OR chart bars since entry >= maximum-hold-bars parameter
Exit: short, high >= short stop price OR low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit parameter / 100) OR EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slow lookback) OR chart bars since entry >= maximum-hold-bars parameter
