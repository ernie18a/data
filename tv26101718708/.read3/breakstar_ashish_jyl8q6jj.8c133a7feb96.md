Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: Upper wick = high - max(close, open)
Calculation: Lower wick = min(close, open) - low
Calculation: Candle range = high - low
Calculation: Volume ratio = if SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > 0 then volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) else 1
Calculation: Upper reversal band = HMA(close, structure lookback) + band multiplier * STDEV(close, dispersion lookback)
Calculation: Lower reversal band = HMA(close, structure lookback) - band multiplier * STDEV(close, dispersion lookback)
Calculation: Average candle range = SMA(candle range, dispersion lookback)
Calculation: Inside bar = high < high[1] AND low > low[1]
Calculation: Morning Star = star pattern enabled AND close[2] < open[2] AND candle body[2] >= first-candle body multiplier * SMA(candle body, star-context lookback) AND candle body[1] <= star-body multiplier * candle body[2] AND close > open AND close[2] < close[2 + star-context lookback] AND close >= open[2] - (open[2] - close[2]) * penetration threshold AND (star volume is informational OR volume ratio >= volume surge multiplier)
Calculation: Evening Star = star pattern enabled AND close[2] > open[2] AND candle body[2] >= first-candle body multiplier * SMA(candle body, star-context lookback) AND candle body[1] <= star-body multiplier * candle body[2] AND close < open AND close[2] > close[2 + star-context lookback] AND close <= open[2] + (close[2] - open[2]) * (1 - penetration threshold) AND (star volume is informational OR volume ratio >= volume surge multiplier)
Calculation: Bullish reversal = Morning Star OR (lower wick >= wick-to-body multiple * candle body AND upper wick <= candle body AND candle body > 0 AND close[1] < close[reversal-trend lookback] AND low <= lower reversal band AND low <= lowest(low, fresh-extreme lookback) AND NOT inside bar) OR (close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close > open[1] AND open < close[1] AND low <= lower reversal band AND average candle range > 0 AND candle range >= engulf-range multiple * average candle range)
Calculation: Bearish reversal = Evening Star OR (upper wick >= wick-to-body multiple * candle body AND lower wick <= candle body AND candle body > 0 AND close[1] > close[reversal-trend lookback] AND high >= upper reversal band AND high >= highest(high, fresh-extreme lookback) AND NOT inside bar) OR (close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close < open[1] AND open > close[1] AND high >= upper reversal band AND average candle range > 0 AND candle range >= engulf-range multiple * average candle range)
Calculation: Long profit in ATR = (close - entry price) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Calculation: Short profit in ATR = (entry price - close) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Calculation: Long invalidation level = on long entry, (if Morning Star then min(low, low[1], low[2]) else low) - initial-buffer ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback); on each later bar with trailing enabled, max(previous level, if profit locking is enabled and long profit in ATR >= tight-trail ATR threshold then max(highest high since entry - trail-distance ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), close[1]) else if profit locking is enabled and long profit in ATR >= breakeven ATR threshold then max(highest high since entry - trail-distance ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), entry price) else highest high since entry - trail-distance ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)); otherwise retain previous level
Calculation: Short invalidation level = on short entry, (if Evening Star then max(high, high[1], high[2]) else high) + initial-buffer ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback); on each later bar with trailing enabled, min(previous level, if profit locking is enabled and short profit in ATR >= tight-trail ATR threshold then min(lowest low since entry + trail-distance ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), close[1]) else if profit locking is enabled and short profit in ATR >= breakeven ATR threshold then min(lowest low since entry + trail-distance ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), entry price) else lowest low since entry + trail-distance ATR multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)); otherwise retain previous level
Long: position is flat or short AND bars since last signal >= cooldown bars AND (Morning Star OR (close > highest(close, breakout lookback)[1] AND (breakout volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ratio >= volume surge multiplier)))
Short: position is flat or long AND bars since last signal >= cooldown bars AND (Evening Star OR (close < lowest(close, breakout lookback)[1] AND (breakout volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ratio >= volume surge multiplier)))
Exit: long, (stop-hit flag enabled AND close < long invalidation level) OR (pattern exits enabled AND long profit in ATR >= minimum pattern-exit profit AND Bearish reversal)
Exit: short, (stop-hit flag enabled AND close > short invalidation level) OR (pattern exits enabled AND short profit in ATR >= minimum pattern-exit profit AND Bullish reversal)
