Calculation: Shelf = price level with touch count and strength; each PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length) or PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length) price X joins the first shelf in creation order with abs(shelf price - X) <= cluster distance * ATR(ATR length), setting shelf price = (shelf price * touch count + X) / (touch count + 1) and adding one touch; otherwise a shelf is created at X with one touch; each join or creation adds high-volume weight to strength if volume[pivot length] >= SMA(volume, volume average length)[pivot length] * volume multiple, else base weight
Calculation: Reaction = reaction of a shelf with price P at bar t, where B = reaction sensitivity * ATR(ATR length) and in = (low <= P + band fraction * B) AND (high >= P - band fraction * B); HELD if in AND close[1] > P AND close >= P + B; LOST if (close[1] > P AND close < P - B) OR (close[1] < P AND close > P + B AND no LOST on an earlier bar); RECLAIM if close[1] < P AND close > P + B AND LOST on an earlier bar; REJECT if in AND close[1] < P AND close <= P - B; TOUCH if in; otherwise the previous Reaction
Calculation: Strongest = the Shelf with the greatest strength among shelves with touch count >= minimum touch count AND (fresh-only switch is off OR no LOST on or before bar t); ties go to the earliest-created shelf
Calculation: Marked = Reaction of Strongest at bar t if it is HELD, RECLAIM, REJECT, or LOST AND that shelf's Reaction at bar t differs from its Reaction at bar t-1 AND it differs from the last Reaction marked on that shelf AND bars since that shelf's last mark >= tag cooldown; otherwise none; a mark sets the last marked Reaction and mark bar of that shelf

Short: Marked = REJECT OR Marked = LOST
