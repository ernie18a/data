Calculation: Dial exposure = in binary mode, 1 after the gate turns on and the trend mean is rising, otherwise 0; the gate turns on after a shared confirmation count of consecutive closes above SMA(close, trend-mean lookback) and turns off after the same count at or below it, and the mean is rising when SMA(close, trend-mean lookback) > SMA(close, trend-mean lookback)[slope lookback]; in single-mean graded mode, clamp((close − SMA(close, trend-mean lookback)) / (ramp-width parameter × ATR(ramp lookback)), 0, 1); in four-mean graded mode, average clamp((close − SMA(close, each of four lookbacks)) / (ensemble-width parameter × sqrt(each lookback / longest lookback) × ATR(ramp lookback)), 0, 1), with the four lookbacks ordered shortest to longest; for graded modes with quantization enabled, hold the prior rung until raw exposure differs by at least the activation fraction of a rung, then round raw exposure to the nearest rung.
Calculation: Effective exposure = with the sell engine disabled or in dial-only mode, dial exposure; in band-only mode, 1 − sold rungs / rung-count parameter, where sold rungs increase when the source crosses above the Nadaraya-Watson upper band and decrease when it crosses below the lower band, bounded by zero and the rung-count parameter; in dial-gated mode, dial exposure when source ≥ the Nadaraya-Watson smoother, otherwise the greater of prior gated exposure and dial exposure; in both-cut mode, the lesser of dial exposure and band-only exposure. The Nadaraya-Watson Envelope uses the source, bandwidth, MAE lookback, and separate upper and lower multipliers.
Calculation: Target weight = 0 when SMA(close, trend-mean lookback) is unavailable; otherwise min(floor parameter + (1 − floor parameter) × scale parameter × effective exposure, full exposure).
Long: position side is flat AND bar is confirmed AND effective exposure > 0
Exit: long, bar is confirmed AND effective exposure = 0
