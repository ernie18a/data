Calculation: LegState(L) = 0 if high[L] > highest(high, L); else 1 if low[L] < lowest(low, L); else LegState(L)[1]
Calculation: LegPivot(L, +1) = high[L] where LegState(L) = 0 and LegState(L)[1] = 1; LegPivot(L, -1) = low[L] where LegState(L) = 1 and LegState(L)[1] = 0; otherwise na
Calculation: Pivot(L, d) = LegPivot(L, d) under the leg-transition option, or pivothigh(high, L, L) for d = +1 and pivotlow(low, L, L) for d = -1 under the symmetric option
Calculation: Break(L, d) = the first bar after the latest Pivot(L, d) is confirmed on which close is beyond that pivot (above for +1, below for -1; high or low under the wick option); L is Ls (short pivot length) or Ll (long pivot length), with Ll > Ls
Calculation: StructTrend(L) = +1 on Break(L, +1) bars; -1 on Break(L, -1) bars; otherwise StructTrend(L)[1]
Calculation: Supported(d) = on a Break(Ll, d) bar where StructTrend(Ll)[1] = -d, the latest Pivot(Ll, -d) is higher than the prior Pivot(Ll, -d) (lower for d = -1) and formed after the latest Pivot(Ll, d)
Calculation: Disp(d) = close > open for +1 (close < open for -1) AND abs(close - open) > SMA(abs(close - open), body length) x body multiplier AND abs(close - open) > ATR(atr length) x ATR multiplier AND close lies in the top (+1) or bottom (-1) close-position fraction of the bar range
Calculation: RecentDisp(d) = Disp(d) on any of the last displacement window bars
Calculation: Sweep(+1) = low < LowLevel AND close > LowLevel AND close > open; Sweep(-1) = high > HighLevel AND close < HighLevel AND close < open; LowLevel = most recent equal-low level once one has formed, else Pivot(Ll, -1); HighLevel = most recent equal-high level once one has formed, else Pivot(Ll, +1); an equal-low level forms when a new Pivot(Ll, -1) is within ATR(atr length) x equal tolerance x equal range factor of the previous one and is set to the lower of the two (equal-high mirrored)
Calculation: RecentSweep(d) = Sweep(d) on any of the last sweep window bars
Calculation: SwingLegDir = +1 on Break(Ll, +1) bars; -1 on Break(Ll, -1) bars; otherwise SwingLegDir[1]
Calculation: SwingLegBottom = for SwingLegDir = +1, the latest Pivot(Ll, -1) at the start break (lowest low of the last fallback window bars if none); for -1, the lowest low since the start break. SwingLegTop = for +1, the highest high since the start break; for -1, the latest Pivot(Ll, +1) at the start break (highest high of the last fallback window bars if none)
Calculation: InOTE(d) = SwingLegDir = d AND close between SwingLegTop - OTE high ratio x leg range and SwingLegTop - OTE low ratio x leg range for +1, or between SwingLegBottom + OTE low ratio x leg range and SwingLegBottom + OTE high ratio x leg range for -1; leg range = SwingLegTop - SwingLegBottom
Calculation: RetraceHalf(d) = SwingLegDir = d AND close at or below the leg midpoint for +1, or at or above it for -1
Calculation: HtfTrend = +1 if last completed T1 bar close > EMA(close, longer EMA length, T1, last completed bar), -1 if below, otherwise 0
Calculation: OB(d) = zone created on each first bar where close is beyond the latest LegPivot(OB length, d) since that pivot (above for +1, below for -1); for +1 the zone spans from the low of the lowest-low bar since that pivot up by min(that bar range, ATR(atr length) x ATR multiplier); for -1 it spans from the high of the highest-high bar since that pivot down by the same amount; active until close (or wick, per invalidation option) passes its far edge (bottom for +1, top for -1) or the age limit
Calculation: InOB(d) = an active OB(d) overlaps the bar range (low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom)
Calculation: OBBreak(d) = an active OB(d) is invalidated on this bar (close or wick, per invalidation option, beyond its far edge)
Calculation: FVG(d) on T2 bars, last completed bar: for +1, low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= ATR(atr length) x max(volatility threshold, minimum size multiplier), zone spans high[2] up to low; for -1, high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= same, zone spans high down to low[2]; active until close passes its far edge
Calculation: InFVG(d) = an active FVG(d) overlaps the bar range
Calculation: EntryPrice(d) = close under the market option; under the Fibonacci option, if the swing leg is valid, SwingLegTop - entry ratio x leg range for +1 or SwingLegBottom + entry ratio x leg range for -1, else close; under the zone option, midpoint of the newest active OB(d), else of the newest active FVG(d), else close
Calculation: InitialStop(d) = under the structure option: for +1, min(low of latest Sweep(+1) bar if any, bar low, bottom of newest active OB(+1) if any) - ATR(atr length) x stop buffer, and for -1 the mirror with max, high and top, plus; under the ATR option: EntryPrice(d) - d x ATR(atr length) x max(stop buffer, minimum ATR multiplier); under the zone option: bottom of newest active OB(+1) - ATR(atr length) x stop buffer (top plus for -1), else the structure option
Calculation: Risk(d) = d x (EntryPrice(d) - InitialStop(d))
Calculation: Target(d, k) = under the risk-multiple option EntryPrice(d) + d x R_k x Risk(d); under the smart-money option the smart level k if beyond EntryPrice(d) (k = 1) or beyond Target(d, k-1) (k > 1), else EntryPrice(d) + d x R_k x Risk(d); under the hybrid option the smart level for k = 1 and 2 and the risk-multiple value for k = 3; smart level k = 1 is Pivot(Ls, d); k = 2 is Pivot(Ll, d), or if not beyond entry the most recent equal-high level for +1 (equal-low for -1); k = 3 is SwingLegTop + extension ratio x leg range for +1 (SwingLegBottom - extension ratio x leg range for -1)
Calculation: Stop(d) = InitialStop(d), replaced by EntryPrice(d) under the breakeven option once price has reached Target(d, 1) on an earlier bar since entry (high for +1, low for -1)
Calculation: EntryOK(d) = Risk(d) > 0 AND Risk(d) <= ATR(atr length) x max stop multiple AND abs(Target(d, 2) - EntryPrice(d)) >= minimum reward-to-risk x Risk(d)
Calculation: FilterOK(d) = (sweep filter off OR RecentSweep(d)) AND (order-block filter off OR InOB(d)) AND (imbalance filter off OR InFVG(d)) AND (displacement filter off OR RecentDisp(d)) AND (RSI filter off OR RSI(close, RSI length) within the d band, long band for +1 and short band for -1)
Calculation: RecentStruct(d) = StructTrend(Ll) = d AND the latest Break(Ll, d) occurred within the structure window bars
Calculation: StrictSignal(d) = RecentStruct(d) AND RecentDisp(d) AND RetraceHalf(d) AND InOTE(d) AND (InOB(d) OR InFVG(d)) AND RecentSweep(d) AND (HTF filter off OR HtfTrend = d)
Calculation: Score(d) = 2 x [StructTrend(Ll) = d] + 1 x [StructTrend(Ls) = d] + 2 x [HTF filter off OR HtfTrend = d] + 2 x [latest Break(Ll, .) within the structure window is a BOS in direction d, i.e. StructTrend(Ll)[1] = d at that break] + 1 x [latest Break(Ll, .) within the structure window is a CHoCH in direction d that was not Supported(d), or a CHoCH in direction d occurs on this bar] + 2 x [RecentSweep(d)] + 1 x [InOB(d)] + 1 x [InFVG(d)] + 2 x [InOTE(d)] + 1 x [RecentDisp(d)] + 1 x [volume term on AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) x volume multiplier] + 1 x [EMA term on AND EMA(close, shorter EMA length) and close vs EMA(close, middle EMA length) and EMA(close, longer EMA length) both in direction d]
Calculation: Setup(d) = bar is closed AND no position is open AND bar closes in direction d AND ((strict-mode flag AND StrictSignal(d)) OR (score-mode flag AND Score(d) >= minimum score AND InOTE(d))) AND FilterOK(d) AND EntryOK(d)
Calculation: Grab(+1) = low < P AND min(open, close) > P AND close[1] >= P AND P - low >= ATR(atr length)[1] x sweep depth multiplier AND min(open, close) - low >= wick fraction x (high - low), where P is a confirmed pivot-low level from the grab pivot length, each level consumed at its first penetration; Grab(-1) mirrors this with confirmed pivot-high levels, high > P, max(open, close) < P, close[1] <= P and the upper wick
Calculation: TrendBreak(+1) = close > the value at this bar of the down-trendline joining the prior Pivot(Ll, +1) and the latest Pivot(Ll, +1), when the latest is lower and at least trend window bars after the prior; TrendBreak(-1) = close < the up-trendline joining Pivot(Ll, -1) pivots when the latest is higher and at least trend window bars after the prior; the line is removed at its first break or when a new pivot does not qualify
Long: Setup(+1)
Long: Break(Ls, +1) OR Break(Ll, +1) OR Sweep(+1) OR Grab(+1) OR TrendBreak(+1) OR OBBreak(-1)
Short: Setup(-1) AND NOT Setup(+1)
Short: Break(Ls, -1) OR Break(Ll, -1) OR Sweep(-1) OR Grab(-1) OR TrendBreak(-1) OR OBBreak(+1)
Exit: long, low <= Stop(+1)
Exit: long, high >= Target(+1, 3)
Exit: short, high >= Stop(-1)
Exit: short, low <= Target(-1, 3)
