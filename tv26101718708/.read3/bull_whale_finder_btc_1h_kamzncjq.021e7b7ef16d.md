Calculation: Trimmed mean range = mean of high - low over the range-lookback bars, including the current bar, after removing the trim-count smallest and largest ranges, using the same count from each end and capping it to retain at least one range.
Calculation: Expansion bar = high - low > 0 AND high - low >= trimmed mean range * range-expansion multiplier AND high - low >= close * minimum-range fraction AND (high - close) / (high - low) <= maximum-close-to-high fraction.
Calculation: Expansion count = 1 on the entry expansion bar; increment on each subsequent confirmed expansion bar while long; reset when flat. The add, runner-protection, first-partial-exit, and second-partial-exit thresholds are consecutive in that order.
Calculation: Long stop = close - ATR(ATR lookback) * initial-stop multiple at entry; while long, ratchet upward to max(previous stop, low - stop buffer) on each confirmed expansion bar; when the highest high since entry has risen from entry price by at least the MFE trigger fraction, ratchet to max(previous stop, entry price * (1 + lock fraction)); at the runner-protection threshold, also ratchet to max(current stop, entry price, the saved low from the add-threshold expansion bar - stop buffer).
Long: Expansion bar AND NOT (long position AND (expansion count = first-partial-exit threshold OR expansion count = second-partial-exit threshold)).
Long: flat AND confirmed chart bar AND Expansion bar AND close > SMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close > SMA(close, longer lookback) AND SMA(close, longer lookback) > SMA(close, longer lookback)[slope lookback] AND chart close > SMA(close, same longer lookback, T1, current possibly still-forming T1 bar), evaluated on chart bars AND abs(low - SMA(close, longer lookback)) / low <= low-to-long-SMA proximity parameter AND abs(SMA(close, shorter lookback) - SMA(close, longer lookback)) / SMA(close, shorter lookback) <= moving-average proximity parameter.
