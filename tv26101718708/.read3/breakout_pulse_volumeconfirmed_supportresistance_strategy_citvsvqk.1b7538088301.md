Long: close crosses above the most recently confirmed pivot high of high using separate left and right lookbacks AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, lookback)) AND datetime is within the configured start and end dates, inclusive
Short: close crosses below the most recently confirmed pivot low of low using separate left and right lookbacks AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, lookback)) AND datetime is within the configured start and end dates, inclusive
Exit: long, low <= the stop fixed on the entry signal bar (most recently confirmed pivot low minus ATR(lookback) * buffer when structural-stop mode is enabled and a pivot low exists, otherwise signal-bar close minus ATR(lookback) * stop multiple) OR high >= signal-bar close plus ATR(lookback) * target multiple
Exit: short, high >= the stop fixed on the entry signal bar (most recently confirmed pivot high plus ATR(lookback) * buffer when structural-stop mode is enabled and a pivot high exists, otherwise signal-bar close plus ATR(lookback) * stop multiple) OR low <= signal-bar close minus ATR(lookback) * target multiple
