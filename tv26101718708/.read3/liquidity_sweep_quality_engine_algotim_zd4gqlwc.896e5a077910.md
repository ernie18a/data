Calculation: Active liquidity levels = the newest level-capacity confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) and PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) levels combined, aged from the pivot bar and removed when older than level-memory bars or after their first sweep
Calculation: SQS(sweep side, level) = the number of true conditions among: for a swing-high sweep, high - level >= wick ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback), high - close >= rejection ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback), volume confirmation enabled AND volume >= volume multiple * SMA(volume, volume lookback), close <= prior-body level; for a swing-low sweep, level - low >= wick ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback), close - low >= rejection ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback), volume confirmation enabled AND volume >= volume multiple * SMA(volume, volume lookback), close >= prior-body level; prior-body level = min(open[1], close[1]) + body fraction * (max(open[1], close[1]) - min(open[1], close[1]))
Long: An active swing-high level is swept (high > level AND close < level) AND (SQS(swing-high sweep, level) >= minimum quality score OR (weak sweeps enabled AND SQS(swing-high sweep, level) > 0)) AND this sweep has the highest SQS among qualifying sweeps on the bar, with the newest level winning ties
Short: An active swing-low level is swept (low < level AND close > level) AND (SQS(swing-low sweep, level) >= minimum quality score OR (weak sweeps enabled AND SQS(swing-low sweep, level) > 0)) AND this sweep has the highest SQS among qualifying sweeps on the bar, with the newest level winning ties
