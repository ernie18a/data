Calculation: Gaussian trend bands = let basis = SMMA(Gaussian(DEMA(close, DEMA lookback), Gaussian lookback, sigma), smoothing lookback), where Gaussian(x, lookback, sigma) is the weighted average of x[i] for i from zero through lookback minus one with weight exp(-0.5 * ((i - (lookback - 1) / 2) / sigma)^2); upper band = basis + standard deviation(basis, deviation lookback) * upper multiplier; lower band = basis - standard deviation(basis, deviation lookback) * lower multiplier
Calculation: Dow direction = 1 when close > SMA(close, shorter lookback) > SMA(close, longer lookback), volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume threshold, SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0, and ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 > NATR threshold; -1 when close < SMA(close, shorter lookback) < SMA(close, longer lookback) and the same volume and NATR conditions hold; otherwise 0
Calculation: Alligator direction = 1 when Alligator lips > Alligator teeth > Alligator jaw and close > Alligator lips; -1 when Alligator lips < Alligator teeth < Alligator jaw and close < Alligator lips; otherwise retain the prior direction, initially 0; Alligator lips, teeth, and jaw are SMMA(hl2, lookback) with lips lookback shorter than teeth lookback, which is shorter than jaw lookback
Calculation: KAMA direction = 1 when KAMA(close, shorter lookback, fast smoothing, slow smoothing) crosses above KAMA(close, longer lookback, fast smoothing, slow smoothing); -1 when it crosses below; otherwise retain the prior direction, initially 0; the fast lookback is shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: VWMA direction = 1 when close crosses above VWMA(close, lookback); -1 when close crosses below; otherwise retain the prior direction, initially 0
Calculation: ATR trail regime = 1 when the fast ATR trailing stop is above the medium ATR trailing stop and the medium ATR trailing stop is above the slow ATR trailing stop; -1 when the fast ATR trailing stop is below the medium ATR trailing stop and the medium ATR trailing stop is below the slow ATR trailing stop; otherwise 0; each ATR trailing stop uses close, its own ATR period, and its own multiplier, with fast period and multiplier shorter and smaller than medium, and medium shorter and smaller than slow
Calculation: ATR trail signal direction = 1 when ATR trail regime becomes 1 from a prior value <= 0; -1 when ATR trail regime becomes -1 from a prior value >= 0; otherwise retain the prior direction, initially 0
Calculation: Gaussian trend direction = 1 when close > upper Gaussian trend band; -1 when close < lower Gaussian trend band; otherwise 0
Calculation: Non-Dow long filter = CCI(close, CCI lookback) > lower CCI threshold and rising, ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 >= minimum ATR percentage, (close - Alligator lips) / minimum tick size >= minimum distance in ticks, and Alligator lips > Alligator teeth > Alligator jaw and close > Alligator lips
Calculation: Non-Dow short filter = CCI(close, CCI lookback) < upper CCI threshold and falling, ATR(ATR lookback) / close * 100 >= minimum ATR percentage, (Alligator lips - close) / minimum tick size >= minimum distance in ticks, and Alligator lips < Alligator teeth < Alligator jaw and close < Alligator lips
Calculation: Long confirmation weight = sum the higher confirmation weight once for each of Dow direction and ATR trail signal direction that is long, and the lower confirmation weight once for each of Gaussian trend direction, KAMA direction, VWMA direction, and Alligator direction that is long; count each signal only when the non-Dow long filter passes
Calculation: Short confirmation weight = sum the higher confirmation weight once for each of Dow direction and ATR trail signal direction that is short, and the lower confirmation weight once for each of Gaussian trend direction, KAMA direction, VWMA direction, and Alligator direction that is short; count each signal only when the non-Dow short filter passes
Long: flat AND Long confirmation weight >= minimum confirmation weight
Short: flat AND Short confirmation weight >= minimum confirmation weight
