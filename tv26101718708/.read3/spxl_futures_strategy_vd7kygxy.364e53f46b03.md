Calculation: MA Rating(s,T1) = the mean of 15 scores: one for each of SMA(close,L1…L6), EMA(close,L1…L6), HMA(close,H), VWMA(close,L2), and the Ichimoku score; each moving-average score is +1 when close is above it, -1 when below it, and 0 when equal. L1…L6 are increasing lookbacks shared by SMA and EMA; H is shorter than L1. Calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars with default lookahead off; a forming T1 value is used in realtime and completed values historically.
Calculation: Ichimoku score = +1 when Span A > Span B AND close > Span A AND close < Base AND close[1] < Conversion AND close > Conversion; -1 when Span B > Span A AND close < Span B AND close > Base AND close[1] > Conversion AND close < Conversion; otherwise 0. Conversion, Base, Span A, and Span B are the unshifted Ichimoku lines using increasing short, medium, and long lookbacks; Span A is the mean of Conversion and Base.
Calculation: k = 1 when the chart timeframe is T3 and the chart bar is realtime or last; otherwise k = 0. An index [n] on a sampled series means n chart bars ago.
Calculation: Adjusted cutoff for each of the four index series = the shared overbought cutoff if both RSI(close,R,T3) and MFI(hlc3,volume,R,T3) of the first index series are above the shared momentum overbought threshold; otherwise that series' individual cutoff. RSI and MFI are calculated on T3 bars and sampled on chart bars at offset k; on intraday charts they use lookahead on and the current, possibly forming, T3 bar, otherwise the default requested value.
Calculation: Risk sell filter = ((T3-mapped volatility-futures close - T3-mapped volatility-index close) / T3-mapped volatility-index close <= yield threshold) AND ((breadth SMA - breadth SMA[k+3]) / 3 < 0) AND (Bollinger %B(T3-mapped volatility-of-volatility close, its lookback, band multiplier)[k] >= BBR threshold OR Bollinger %B(T3-mapped volatility-ETF close, its lookback, band multiplier)[k] >= BBR threshold) AND Bollinger %B(T3-mapped skew close, its lookback, band multiplier)[k] <= skew threshold AND Bollinger %B(T3-mapped skew close, its lookback, band multiplier)[k+1] <= skew threshold. The breadth SMA is SMA(T3-mapped breadth close, its lookback) on chart bars; all Bollinger %B and yield calculations also run on chart bars using T3 values merged with default lookahead off. The three BBR lookbacks are separate and increasing; the band multiplier is shared.
Long: MA-rating mode AND, for each of the four index series, MA Rating(series,T1)[k] > its adjusted cutoff AND ((MA Rating(series,T1)[k+1] > its adjusted cutoff AND MA Rating(series,T1)[k] > its adjusted cutoff) OR MA Rating(series,T1)[k] >= 0) AND NOT Risk sell filter[k].
Short: MA-rating mode AND (at least one of the four index series has MA Rating(series,T1)[k] <= its adjusted cutoff OR Risk sell filter[k]).
Long: EMA-trend mode AND, for each of the four index series, T2 close[k] > EMA(close,EMA lookback,T2)[k]. The close and EMA are calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with default lookahead off; a forming T2 value is used in realtime and completed values historically.
Short: EMA-trend mode AND at least one of the four index series has T2 close[k] <= EMA(close,EMA lookback,T2)[k].
Exit: Long or short, strategy enabled AND (datetime > end-date parameter OR (chart timeframe is T3 AND datetime is within the start-date and end-date parameters AND short-on-sell is disabled AND ((MA-rating mode AND (at least one of the four index series has MA Rating(series,T1)[k] <= its adjusted cutoff OR Risk sell filter[k])) OR (EMA-trend mode AND at least one of the four index series has T2 close[k] <= EMA(close,EMA lookback,T2)[k])))).
Calculation: Technical rating = arithmetic mean of the available scores for six simple moving averages, six exponential moving averages, a Hull moving average, a volume-weighted moving average, and an Ichimoku rating; each moving average has its own period parameter, and Ichimoku has conversion, base, and second-span period parameters; assign the shared positive, negative, or neutral score when close is above, below, or equal to each moving average; the Ichimoku score is positive when Span A > Span B AND close > Span A AND close < base AND prior close < conversion AND close > conversion, negative when Span B > Span A AND close < Span B AND close > base AND prior close > conversion AND close < conversion, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Effective moving-average rating threshold for each index = its own baseline threshold unless T3 S&P 500 futures RSI and MFI, each with its own period parameter, both exceed the shared overbought cutoff, in which case it is the shared overbought rating threshold.
Calculation: Volatility-breadth sell filter = (front-month VIX futures close − VIX index close) / VIX index close <= its cutoff AND the T3 simple moving average of the percentage of stocks above their moving average is lower than its value at the trend-lookback parameter bars earlier AND (VVIX Bollinger %B using its period parameter OR VXX Bollinger %B using its period parameter is at least the shared volatility %B cutoff) AND current and preceding T3 SKEW Bollinger %B, using its own period parameter, are each at or below the SKEW %B cutoff; Bollinger %B uses the shared band-deviation parameter.
Long: Using the moving-average-rating method, the volatility-breadth sell filter is false AND, for each of S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones, and Russell 2000 futures, its current T1 technical rating is above its effective threshold AND (its preceding T1 rating is above its effective threshold OR its current rating is at least the neutral rating level).
Short: Using the moving-average-rating method, any of the four current T1 technical ratings is at or below its effective threshold OR the volatility-breadth sell filter holds OR (the filter is false AND all four current ratings are above their effective thresholds AND at least one current rating is below the neutral rating level and its preceding T1 rating is at or below its effective threshold).
Long: Using the EMA-trend method, T2 close of each of S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones, and Russell 2000 futures is above its EMA with the shared period parameter.
Short: Using the EMA-trend method, T2 close of any of S&P 500, Nasdaq 100, Dow Jones, or Russell 2000 futures is at or below its EMA with the shared period parameter.
