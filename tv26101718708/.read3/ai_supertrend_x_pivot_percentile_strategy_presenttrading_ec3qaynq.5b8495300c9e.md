Calculation: SuperTrend(T, length, factor) = trend (up or down) and line of a SuperTrend on chart bars, where basis = MA(close(T, last completed bar) * volume, length, MA type) / MA(volume, length, MA type), upper band = basis + factor * ATR(length, T, last completed bar), lower band = basis - factor * ATR(length, T, last completed bar), with standard SuperTrend band-carry and trend-flip rules
Calculation: KnnLabel(SuperTrend S, price lookback, prediction lookback, neighbors, data points) = 1 if all of the neighbors chart bars, among the last max(neighbors, data points) chart bars, whose line of S is nearest to the current line of S have WMA(close, price lookback) > WMA(line of S, prediction lookback) at their own bar; 0 if all of them have WMA(close, price lookback) <= WMA(line of S, prediction lookback) at their own bar; otherwise neutral; price lookback is shorter than prediction lookback
Calculation: PivotTrend = (bull count - bear count) + weak weight * (weak bull count - weak bear count) on chart bars, where each count is summed over lookbacks equal to base length times increasing multipliers; H = PercentileNearestRank(high, lookback, upper percentile); L = PercentileNearestRank(low, lookback, lower percentile); HH = PercentileNearestRank(high, range lookback, upper percentile); LL = PercentileNearestRank(low, range lookback, lower percentile); bull count = [H > HH] + [L > HH]; bear count = [H < LL] + [L < LL]; weak bull count = [L > LL and L < HH]; weak bear count = [H > LL and H < HH]; [x] is 1 if x is true else 0; range lookback is longer than each lookback
Calculation: DMI(T, ADX length) = ADX, +DI, -DI on chart bars, using high, low, close of the last completed T bar, where prior means one chart bar earlier: +DM = (high - prior high) if (high - prior high) > (prior low - low) and > 0, else 0; -DM = (prior low - low) if (prior low - low) > (high - prior high) and > 0, else 0; TR = abs(high - low) + abs(high - prior close) + abs(low - prior close); +DI = 100 * WMA(+DM, ADX length) / WMA(TR, ADX length); -DI = 100 * WMA(-DM, ADX length) / WMA(TR, ADX length); ADX = WMA(100 * abs(+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI), ADX length)
Long: trend of SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1) = up AND KnnLabel(SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1), price lookback1, prediction lookback1, neighbors1, data points1) = 1 AND trend of SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2) = up AND KnnLabel(SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2), price lookback2, prediction lookback2, neighbors2, data points2) = 1 AND ADX of DMI(T3, ADX length) > ADX threshold AND +DI of DMI(T3, ADX length) > -DI of DMI(T3, ADX length) AND PivotTrend > 0
Short: trend of SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1) = down AND KnnLabel(SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1), price lookback1, prediction lookback1, neighbors1, data points1) = 0 AND trend of SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2) = down AND KnnLabel(SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2), price lookback2, prediction lookback2, neighbors2, data points2) = 0 AND ADX of DMI(T3, ADX length) > ADX threshold AND -DI of DMI(T3, ADX length) > +DI of DMI(T3, ADX length) AND PivotTrend < 0

Long: trend of SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2) = up AND trend of SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2)[1] = down AND KnnLabel(SuperTrend(T2, length2, factor2), price lookback2, prediction lookback2, neighbors2, data points2) = 1
Short: trend of SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1) = down AND trend of SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1)[1] = up AND KnnLabel(SuperTrend(T1, length1, factor1), price lookback1, prediction lookback1, neighbors1, data points1) = 0
