Short: crosses below close and Lower Bollinger Band(close, lookback, standard deviation multiplier) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > entry threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, lookback) * volume spike multiplier AND close < Supertrend(lookback, multiplier)
Exit: long, crosses below MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback) and its signal line( signal lookback) OR close < close - ATR(lookback) * trailing stop multiplier OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) < exit threshold
Exit: short, crosses above MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback) and its signal line( signal lookback) OR close > close + ATR(lookback) * trailing stop multiplier OR ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) < exit threshold
Option: buy call, close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger length parameter, Bollinger multiplier parameter) AND ADX(DI length parameter, ADX smoothing parameter) > ADX entry threshold parameter AND volume > SMA(volume, volume average length parameter) * volume spike multiplier parameter AND close > Supertrend(close, Supertrend multiplier parameter, Supertrend length parameter)
Option: buy put, close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger length parameter, Bollinger multiplier parameter) AND ADX(DI length parameter, ADX smoothing parameter) > ADX entry threshold parameter AND volume > SMA(volume, volume average length parameter) * volume spike multiplier parameter AND close < Supertrend(close, Supertrend multiplier parameter, Supertrend length parameter)
Option: sell call, MACD(close, MACD fast length parameter, MACD slow length parameter, MACD signal length parameter) line crosses below its signal line OR close < close - ATR(ATR length parameter) * ATR stop multiplier parameter OR ADX(DI length parameter, ADX smoothing parameter) < ADX exit threshold parameter
Option: sell put, MACD(close, MACD fast length parameter, MACD slow length parameter, MACD signal length parameter) line crosses above its signal line OR close > close + ATR(ATR length parameter) * ATR stop multiplier parameter OR ADX(DI length parameter, ADX smoothing parameter) < ADX exit threshold parameter
