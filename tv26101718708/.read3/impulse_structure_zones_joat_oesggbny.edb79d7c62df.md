Calculation: price-change z-score = ((close - close[1]) - SMA(close - close[1], z-score lookback)) / StDev(close - close[1], z-score lookback), or zero when standard deviation is zero
Calculation: session filter = session filter disabled or datetime is inside an enabled Asia, London, or New York session, AND datetime is outside any enabled exclusion session, using configured session intervals and UTC offset
Calculation: long filter = (EMA filter disabled or close > EMA(close, long lookback)) AND (VWAP filter disabled or close > VWAP(hl2)) AND (ATR-band filter disabled or close <= EMA(close, long lookback) + ATR-band multiplier × EMA(TrueRange, long lookback))
Calculation: short filter = (EMA filter disabled or close < EMA(close, long lookback)) AND (VWAP filter disabled or close < VWAP(hl2)) AND (ATR-band filter disabled or close >= EMA(close, long lookback) - ATR-band multiplier × EMA(TrueRange, long lookback))
Long: price-change z-score > z-score threshold
Short: price-change z-score < negative z-score threshold
Long: bar is confirmed AND price-change z-score > z-score threshold AND close > open AND long filter AND session filter AND close > Highest(close, origin scan lookback)[1]
Short: bar is confirmed AND price-change z-score < negative z-score threshold AND close < open AND short filter AND session filter AND close < Lowest(close, origin scan lookback)[1]
Calculation: bullish zone = created by a confirmed bullish impulse; upper and lower boundaries are max(open, close) and min(open, close) on the nearest preceding bearish candle within the origin scan lookback, falling back to the prior bar if none; use high and low instead when full-candle zones are enabled
Calculation: bearish zone = created by a confirmed bearish impulse; upper and lower boundaries are max(open, close) and min(open, close) on the nearest preceding bullish candle within the origin scan lookback, falling back to the prior bar if none; use high and low instead when full-candle zones are enabled
Calculation: eligible bullish zone = bullish zone not previously fired, not mitigated by close < lower boundary (or low < lower boundary in wick mode), and whose age in bars has not exceeded the maximum zone age
Calculation: eligible bearish zone = bearish zone not previously fired, not mitigated by close > upper boundary (or high > upper boundary in wick mode), and whose age in bars has not exceeded the maximum zone age
Long: bar is confirmed AND an eligible bullish zone exists AND low <= its upper boundary AND low >= its lower boundary AND close > open AND close - low > (upper boundary - lower boundary) × rejection percentage / 100 AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(abs(close - open), (high - low) × wick-denominator floor multiplier) >= minimum wick-to-body ratio AND long filter AND session filter
Short: bar is confirmed AND an eligible bearish zone exists AND high >= its lower boundary AND high <= its upper boundary AND close < open AND high - close > (upper boundary - lower boundary) × rejection percentage / 100 AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(abs(close - open), (high - low) × wick-denominator floor multiplier) >= minimum wick-to-body ratio AND short filter AND session filter
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-loss multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-loss multiplier × reward-risk multiple
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-loss multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-loss multiplier × reward-risk multiple
