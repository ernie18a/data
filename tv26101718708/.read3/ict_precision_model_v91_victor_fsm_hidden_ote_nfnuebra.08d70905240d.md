Calculation: Bullish FVG = on the evaluated timeframe, low > high[2], with bounds [high[2], low]; use chart bars for the setup and still-forming bars of T3, T4, and T5 for context, carrying their values onto chart bars.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = on the evaluated timeframe, high < low[2], with bounds [high, low[2]]; use chart bars for the setup and still-forming bars of T3, T4, and T5 for context, carrying their values onto chart bars.
Calculation: Active bullish context zone = the latest bullish FVG from T3, T4, or T5, replacing the prior zone when a new one is detected; if multiple are detected together, choose the longest timeframe. Keep it active until a chart-bar close is below its lower bound.
Calculation: Active bearish context zone = the latest bearish FVG from T3, T4, or T5, replacing the prior zone when a new one is detected; if multiple are detected together, choose the longest timeframe. Keep it active until a chart-bar close is above its upper bound.
Calculation: Current-day midpoint = (highest chart-bar high since the current day began + lowest chart-bar low since the current day began) / 2.
Long: Within the setup-wait limit, a chart-bar sequence starts with a bullish manipulation bar during a configured UTC signal window, while the per-day signal count is below its limit, the bars since any prior manipulation meet the cooldown, and no bullish setup is active; the bar has at least one of: low < the prior completed T1 bar low minus the tick buffer and close > that low; low < the prior completed T2 bar low minus the tick buffer and close > that low; low < the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) minus the tick buffer and close > that pivot; low < the prior chart bar's current-day running low minus the tick buffer and close > that running low; or its range overlaps the active bullish context zone. T1 is the prior-day reference timeframe, T2 the prior-week reference timeframe, and T1 is shorter than T2; the tick buffer is the instrument tick size times its buffer parameter. After that bar, a bearish reference candle has low >= the manipulation bar low and high <= the manipulation bar high times (1 + the manipulation-edge percentage parameter); use the latest such candle's open as the reference. A later bullish candle closes above that open. Then, on or after that shift, a bullish candle has abs(close - open) > SMA(abs(close - open), body-average lookback) times the displacement multiplier and close > Highest(high, swing lookback)[1], using the same swing lookback as the pivots. On or after that displacement, a chart-bar bullish FVG forms. The current bar's range intersects the OTE zone from dispHigh - (dispHigh - manipulation low) times the upper OTE fraction to dispHigh - (dispHigh - manipulation low) times the lower OTE fraction, where the lower fraction is less than the upper fraction; the sequence has completed within the setup-wait limit. On the current bar, the manipulation low is below the current-day midpoint, the per-day signal count is below its limit, UTC time is in a configured signal window, close > open, close > the FVG midpoint, and open >= the FVG lower bound. If bullish and bearish manipulation candidates both qualify on the starting bar, bearish takes precedence.
Short: Within the setup-wait limit, a chart-bar sequence starts with a bearish manipulation bar during a configured UTC signal window, while the per-day signal count is below its limit, the bars since any prior manipulation meet the cooldown, and no bearish setup is active; the bar has at least one of: high > the prior completed T1 bar high plus the tick buffer and close < that high; high > the prior completed T2 bar high plus the tick buffer and close < that high; high > the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) plus the tick buffer and close < that pivot; high > the prior chart bar's current-day running high plus the tick buffer and close < that running high; or its range overlaps the active bearish context zone. T1 is the prior-day reference timeframe, T2 the prior-week reference timeframe, and T1 is shorter than T2; the tick buffer is the instrument tick size times its buffer parameter. After that bar, a bullish reference candle has high <= the manipulation bar high and low >= the manipulation bar low times (1 - the manipulation-edge percentage parameter); use the latest such candle's open as the reference. A later bearish candle closes below that open. Then, on or after that shift, a bearish candle has abs(close - open) > SMA(abs(close - open), body-average lookback) times the displacement multiplier and close < Lowest(low, swing lookback)[1], using the same swing lookback as the pivots. On or after that displacement, a chart-bar bearish FVG forms. The current bar's range intersects the OTE zone from dispLow + (manipulation high - dispLow) times the lower OTE fraction to dispLow + (manipulation high - dispLow) times the upper OTE fraction, where the lower fraction is less than the upper fraction; the sequence has completed within the setup-wait limit. On the current bar, the manipulation high is above the current-day midpoint, the per-day signal count is below its limit, UTC time is in a configured signal window, close < open, close < the FVG midpoint, and open <= the FVG upper bound.
