Calculation: pair-cross score(F) = add one unit if any shorter-lookback series crosses above a longer-lookback series, and independently subtract one unit if any crosses below; count each direction at most once per bar.
Calculation: level-cross score(F) = for each series in F, add one unit if it crosses above any of its upper threshold parameters and independently subtract one unit if it crosses below any of its lower threshold parameters; count each direction at most once per series per bar.
Calculation: volume oscillator = 100 × (moving average of volume at the shorter lookback − moving average of volume at the longer lookback) / moving average of volume at the longer lookback; the two averages use the selected SMA or EMA method. Its contribution is zero unless a threshold is crossed; use the first crossing in the ordered threshold list, testing upward before downward at each threshold. On an up candle, an upward crossing adds one unit and a downward crossing subtracts one; on a down candle, reverse these signs; on a doji, add zero.
Calculation: scaled BOP = BOP × scale + offset; smooth it at each of four progressively longer lookbacks using the selected SMA or EMA method.
Calculation: selected stochastic K = SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, selected lookback), selected smoothing lookback); each selected lookback has its corresponding smoothing parameter. For each of the four progressively longer lookbacks, K = SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback), smoothing lookback), and D = SMA(selected stochastic K, its separate D lookback).
Calculation: compound score = prior compound score plus the volume-oscillator contribution; pair-cross score and level-cross score for CMO; pair-cross score for stochastic K; one unit if any of the three shortest K series is at least its corresponding D, and independently minus one unit if any is below its D; pair-cross and level-cross scores for RSI, MFI, RVI, and smoothed scaled BOP; pair-cross scores for SMA and EMA; plus one unit when SAR is at or below close and otherwise minus one; plus one unit when Supertrend direction is negative and otherwise minus one; plus floor(close / upper Bollinger Band) plus an offset when close is above the middle band, otherwise minus floor(close / lower Bollinger Band) minus an offset; plus one unit when COG(close, lookback) is above its SMA and otherwise minus one. Initialize the score at zero. Each indicator family uses four progressively longer, separately parameterized lookbacks; threshold levels for each series are separate parameters. For the shortest RSI, MFI, RVI, and smoothed scaled BOP series, the endpoint levels are tested with an unoriented cross in both the upward and downward tests.
Calculation: price-score covariance = rounded covariance, over the same rolling window, of compound score and (open + close) / 2, divided by its scale parameter.
Calculation: score-high = highest(compound score divided by its scale parameter, lookback); score-low = lowest(compound score divided by its scale parameter, lookback).
Calculation: score-high covariance = covariance of compound score and score-high over the covariance window; score-low covariance = covariance of compound score and score-low over that window. For each, use the rolling highest value when the covariance is positive and the rolling lowest value otherwise.
Calculation: price-score covariance high = highest(price-score covariance, lookback).
Calculation: point 1 = previous price-score covariance equals its previous rolling high AND current price-score covariance is below its current rolling high AND current price-score covariance is at least its cutoff AND SMA(close, lookback) is falling over its lookback.
Calculation: point 2 = previous price-score covariance equals its previous rolling high AND current price-score covariance is below its current rolling high AND current price-score covariance is at least its separate cutoff AND SMA(close, lookback) is rising over its lookback.
Calculation: point 3 = previous score-high covariance equals its previous selected extreme AND current score-high covariance is below its current selected extreme AND current score-high covariance is at least its cutoff AND SMA(close, lookback) is rising over its lookback.
Calculation: point 5 = previous score-low covariance equals its previous selected extreme AND current score-low covariance is below its current selected extreme AND current score-low covariance is at least its cutoff AND SMA(close, lookback) is falling over its lookback.
Calculation: point 6 = previous score-low covariance equals its previous selected extreme AND current score-low covariance is above its current selected extreme AND current score-low covariance is at most its cutoff AND SMA(close, lookback) is falling over its lookback.
Calculation: point 7 = previous score-high covariance equals its previous selected extreme AND current score-high covariance is below its current selected extreme AND current score-high covariance is at least its separate cutoff AND SMA(close, lookback) is falling over its lookback.
Calculation: sell command = previous Supertrend direction is negative and current direction is positive, OR current direction is positive AND (point 2 OR point 3); use separate Supertrend factor and ATR-length parameters from those used in the compound score.
Calculation: intermediate selling enabled = initially false; set true when open-trade count equals its prior-bar value, set false when it is one less than its prior-bar value, and otherwise retain its prior value.
Calculation: latest open-trade entry price = entry price of the most recently opened trade.
Long: point 1 OR point 5 OR point 6 OR point 7, AND SMA(close, lookback) is below average entry price times the separately parameterized drawdown factor.
Long: sell command is false AND open-trade count is below the parallel-entry limit AND ((point 1 AND position is flat) OR ((point 1 OR point 5 OR point 6 OR point 7) AND position is long AND SMA(close, lookback) is below average entry price times the drawdown factor)).
Exit: long, sell command AND average entry price × (1 + profit target percentage / percent scale) < SMA(close, lookback).
Exit: long, sell command AND latest open-trade entry price × (1 + intermediate profit percentage / percent scale) < SMA(close, lookback) AND SMA(close, lookback) < average entry price × intermediate drawdown factor AND intermediate selling is enabled.
Exit: long, (point 2 OR point 3) AND sell command AND (average entry price × (1 + profit target percentage / percent scale) < SMA(close, lookback) OR (latest open-trade entry price × (1 + profit target percentage / percent scale) < SMA(close, lookback) AND average entry price × the separately parameterized marker discount factor > SMA(close, lookback))).
