Calculation: Close spread ratio = (close of the last completed T1 bar - close of the last completed T2 bar) / close of the last completed T2 bar, sampled on chart bars; T1 is shorter than T2
Long: position is flat AND HMA(close, lookback) > HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND close > HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND Close spread ratio > decision threshold AND Ichimoku Span A(high, low, conversion lookback, base lookback) > Ichimoku Span B(high, low, span B lookback) AND open < close AND MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback) > EMA(MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback), signal lookback)
Short: position is flat AND HMA(close, lookback) < HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND close < HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND Close spread ratio < decision threshold AND Ichimoku Span A(high, low, conversion lookback, base lookback) < Ichimoku Span B(high, low, span B lookback) AND open > close AND MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback) < EMA(MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback), signal lookback)
Exit: long, (HMA(close, lookback) < HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND close < HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND Close spread ratio < decision threshold) OR open profit < stop-loss parameter OR open profit > target parameter
Exit: short, (HMA(close, lookback) > HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND close > HMA(close, lookback)[1] AND Close spread ratio > decision threshold) OR open profit < stop-loss parameter OR open profit > target parameter
