Calculation: Pivot(kind, n) = price and bar time of the n-th most recent pivot of kind confirmed up to the last completed T1 bar, where kind is high or low, a high pivot is PivotHigh(high, leftBars, rightBars) and a low pivot is PivotLow(low, leftBars, rightBars) on T1 bars; n = 0 is the most recent
Calculation: CorrelatedPivot(kind, p) = price of the correlated-instrument pivot of the same kind, computed like Pivot on the correlated instrument's T1 bars with the same leftBars and rightBars, whose bar time is nearest to p's bar time among those confirmed up to the last completed T1 bar, provided the time difference is at most toleranceBars × duration of T1; otherwise na
Long: PivotLow(low, leftBars, rightBars, T1, last completed bar) is not na AND Pivot(low, 0).price < Pivot(low, 1).price AND CorrelatedPivot(low, Pivot(low, 0)) > CorrelatedPivot(low, Pivot(low, 1))
Short: PivotHigh(high, leftBars, rightBars, T1, last completed bar) is not na AND Pivot(high, 0).price > Pivot(high, 1).price AND CorrelatedPivot(high, Pivot(high, 0)) < CorrelatedPivot(high, Pivot(high, 1))
