Calculation: Fusion = EMA(the weighted mean of MA(close, selected MA type, MA lookback) and VWAP(hlc3, volume, selected VWAP mode, VWAP lookback) values from T1, T2, and T3, using a shared weight for each MA and a shared weight for each VWAP; use selected-source close if total weight is zero; bypass EMA when its lookback is at its minimum. VWAP mode selects rolling volume-weighted hlc3 over its lookback or cumulative volume-weighted hlc3 from each date boundary. Use configured chart OHLC or Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Compute MA and VWAP on their own timeframe bars and carry values to chart bars, using the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and otherwise the current bar.
Calculation: Volatility buffer = EMA(ATR(selected-source OHLC, ATR lookback), fusion smoothing lookback) × volatility multiplier, bypassing EMA when its lookback is at its minimum; calculate ATR on chart bars.
Calculation: Trend state = bullish after selected-source close is above Fusion + Volatility buffer and above selected-source open for the confirmation count of consecutive chart bars; bearish after close is below Fusion − Volatility buffer and below open for that many consecutive chart bars; otherwise retain the previous state, initially neutral.
Long: Trend state changes to bullish OR (trend state is bullish AND selected-source close crosses above Fusion AND an unconsumed close cross below Fusion occurred during the current bullish state).
Short: Trend state changes to bearish OR (trend state is bearish AND selected-source close crosses below Fusion AND an unconsumed close cross above Fusion occurred during the current bearish state).
