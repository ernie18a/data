Calculation: Resistance line = the straight line through the latest two confirmed pivot highs, each confirmed using the pivot lookback on both sides, if the newer pivot is lower and the pivots are at least the minimum pivot span apart; extend the line by bar index, reject it if any close from the newer pivot through its confirmation bar is above it, and consume it on the first close above it by ATR(ATR lookback) × break buffer, even if filters block the signal.
Calculation: Support line = the straight line through the latest two confirmed pivot lows, each confirmed using the pivot lookback on both sides, if the newer pivot is higher and the pivots are at least the minimum pivot span apart; extend the line by bar index, reject it if any close from the newer pivot through its confirmation bar is below it, and consume it on the first close below it by ATR(ATR lookback) × break buffer, even if filters block the signal.
Calculation: Smoothed AO = EMA(SMA((high + low) / 2, shorter lookback) − EMA((high + low) / 2, longer lookback), smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Initial risk distance = the greater of the distance from signal close to the selected initial stop and ATR(ATR lookback) × risk-floor multiple; the long initial stop is signal close − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR-stop multiple or lowest(low, swing lookback) − ATR(ATR lookback) × swing padding, and the short initial stop is signal close + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR-stop multiple or highest(high, swing lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × swing padding, according to stop mode.
Calculation: Long stop level = entry price − initial risk distance initially; if break-even is enabled, ratchet it to at least entry price once high reaches entry price + initial risk distance × break-even trigger; if trailing is enabled, once high has reached entry price + initial risk distance × trail-start threshold, ratchet it on each bar to at least the greater of its prior value and close − ATR(ATR lookback) × trail distance.
Calculation: Short stop level = entry price + initial risk distance initially; if break-even is enabled, ratchet it to at most entry price once low reaches entry price − initial risk distance × break-even trigger; if trailing is enabled, once low has reached entry price − initial risk distance × trail-start threshold, ratchet it on each bar to at most the lesser of its prior value and close + ATR(ATR lookback) × trail distance.
Long: long entries enabled AND resistance line is valid, unconsumed, and no older than the line-age limit AND close > resistance line + ATR(ATR lookback) × break buffer AND (momentum filter disabled OR smoothed AO > its previous-bar value) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume average unavailable OR volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier).
Short: short entries enabled AND support line is valid, unconsumed, and no older than the line-age limit AND close < support line − ATR(ATR lookback) × break buffer AND (momentum filter disabled OR smoothed AO < its previous-bar value) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume average unavailable OR volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier).
Exit: long, low <= long stop level OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + initial risk distance × take-profit R) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry >= time limit).
Exit: short, high >= short stop level OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price − initial risk distance × take-profit R) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry >= time limit).
