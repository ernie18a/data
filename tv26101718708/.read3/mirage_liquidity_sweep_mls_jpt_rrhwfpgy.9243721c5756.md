Calculation: long risk at entry = max(entry price - (bullish swept pivot low at entry - ATR at entry × stop buffer), ATR at entry × minimum-risk ATR multiple)
Calculation: short risk at entry = max((bearish swept pivot high at entry + ATR at entry × stop buffer) - entry price, ATR at entry × minimum-risk ATR multiple)
Calculation: long stop = entry price if break-even is enabled and a subsequent confirmed chart bar after the signal bar reached entry price + long risk × TP1 reward multiple while its low stayed above the initial long stop; otherwise entry price - long risk
Calculation: short stop = entry price if break-even is enabled and a subsequent confirmed chart bar after the signal bar reached entry price - short risk × TP1 reward multiple while its high stayed below the initial short stop; otherwise entry price + short risk
Calculation: long setup = bullish sweep and bullish score >= minimum score and (no prior signal or bars since the most recent signal > signal cooldown) and (displacement not required or close > open and high - low >= ATR × displacement range multiple and (body filter disabled or (high > low and abs(close - open) / (high - low) × 100 >= minimum body percentage))) and (MSS not required or close > latest confirmed pivot high with equal left and right MSS pivot lengths) and chart bar confirmed
Calculation: short setup = bearish sweep and bearish score >= minimum score and (no prior signal or bars since the most recent signal > signal cooldown) and (displacement not required or close < open and high - low >= ATR × displacement range multiple and (body filter disabled or (high > low and abs(close - open) / (high - low) × 100 >= minimum body percentage))) and (MSS not required or close < latest confirmed pivot low with equal left and right MSS pivot lengths) and chart bar confirmed

Short: high > an unconsumed pivot high confirmed with equal left and right liquidity swing lengths and formed within the liquidity validity lookback, and close < that pivot high; consume a candidate on its first close above the level or first sweep, and use the newest qualifying pivot if multiple qualify
Long: close > latest confirmed pivot high with equal left and right MSS pivot lengths

Short: on chart bars, close of T1's last completed bar < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar), where the EMA is calculated on T1 bars

Short: a bearish FVG occurred within FVG validity lookback

Short: short setup and position is flat and ATR > 0 and (long setup is false or bullish score <= bearish score)
