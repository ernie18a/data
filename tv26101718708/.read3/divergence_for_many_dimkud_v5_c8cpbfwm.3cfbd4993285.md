Calculation: BullishRegularDivergence(I) = at a confirmed pivot low from the selected price source, an endpoint within the pivot and bar search limits has I(endpoint) > I(pivot) and price(endpoint) < price(pivot); I and close stay on or above their straight lines from pivot to endpoint at every intervening bar; unless the endpoint is unconfirmed, I(endpoint) > I(previous bar) or close(endpoint) > close(previous bar); pivot-to-endpoint distance exceeds the minimum separation parameter
Calculation: BearishRegularDivergence(I) = at a confirmed pivot high from the selected price source, an endpoint within the pivot and bar search limits has I(endpoint) < I(pivot) and price(endpoint) > price(pivot); I and close stay on or below their straight lines from pivot to endpoint at every intervening bar; unless the endpoint is unconfirmed, I(endpoint) < I(previous bar) or close(endpoint) < close(previous bar); pivot-to-endpoint distance exceeds the minimum separation parameter
Calculation: VolumeWeightedMACDHistogram = d - EMA(d, signal lookback), where d = EMA(volume × close, shorter lookback) / EMA(volume, shorter lookback) - EMA(volume × close, longer lookback) / EMA(volume, longer lookback)
Calculation: SmoothedBalanceOfPower = TEMA(EMA((bull terms - bear terms) / 3, smoothing lookback), TEMA lookback), where denominator = high - low if high ≠ low, otherwise 0.01; bull terms = (high - open) / denominator + (close - low) / denominator + (close > open ? (close - open) / denominator : 0); bear terms = (open - low) / denominator + (high - close) / denominator + (open > close ? (open - close) / denominator : 0)
Calculation: EntryFilter(side) = datetime is within the configured start-inclusive, stop-exclusive interval AND every enabled filter for side passes: the enabled channel filter passes; for each RSI, MFI, CCI, and Chande Momentum Oscillator range filter, either neither side's range is enabled or side's range is enabled and its value is within its bounds; the enabled MFI rising/falling filter passes; the enabled ATR and volume comparisons pass; the enabled Supertrend direction filter passes
