Calculation: normalized range = 100 × (high-low - lowest(high-low, range lookback)) / (highest(high-low, range lookback) - lowest(high-low, range lookback)); use the midpoint when the denominator is zero
Calculation: recent bullish sweep = within the sweep-age parameter confirmed chart bars, a confirmed chart bar has low below and close above any of the latest stored pivot low using swing left/right lookbacks, a pivot low confirmed on that bar using the shorter symmetric lookback, or a pivot low confirmed on that bar using the longer symmetric lookback
Calculation: recent bearish sweep = within the sweep-age parameter confirmed chart bars, a confirmed chart bar has high above and close below any of the latest stored pivot high using swing left/right lookbacks, a pivot high confirmed on that bar using the shorter symmetric lookback, or a pivot high confirmed on that bar using the longer symmetric lookback
Calculation: OB score(direction) = base-score parameter plus separate bonus parameters for a recent same-direction sweep, a directional current-bar body exceeding ATR(lookback) × displacement multiplier × stronger-body multiplier, a same-direction MSS within the MSS-score window, a same-direction FVG, aligned higher-timeframe EMAs, and normalized range[1] meeting the high-range threshold; the shorter- and longer-lookback EMAs are computed on T1 and sampled on chart bars from the current T1 bar with lookahead off
Calculation: zone overlap ratio = (min(zone tops) - max(zone bottoms)) / min(zone ranges), when the smaller zone range is positive
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, close > open AND close - open > ATR(lookback) × displacement multiplier AND close[1] < open[1] AND open[1] - close[1] > ATR(lookback) × OB-body multiplier AND normalized range[1] >= minimum-range-percentile parameter AND OB score(long) >= selected minimum-grade score AND (no stored bullish zone overlaps the candidate zone [close[1], open[1]] above the overlap-threshold parameter OR the candidate score exceeds the score of the first such stored zone)
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, close < open AND open - close > ATR(lookback) × displacement multiplier AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[1] - open[1] > ATR(lookback) × OB-body multiplier AND normalized range[1] >= minimum-range-percentile parameter AND OB score(short) >= selected minimum-grade score AND (no stored bearish zone overlaps the candidate zone [open[1], close[1]] above the overlap-threshold parameter OR the candidate score exceeds the score of the first such stored zone)
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, low < the latest stored pivot low using the shorter symmetric lookback AND close > that pivot low, and on that bar or within the MSS-confirmation window close > the pivot high captured at the sweep AND close - open > ATR(lookback) × displacement multiplier
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, high > the latest stored pivot high using the shorter symmetric lookback AND close < that pivot high, and on that bar or within the MSS-confirmation window close < the pivot low captured at the sweep AND open - close > ATR(lookback) × displacement multiplier
