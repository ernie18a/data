Calculation: average candle body = SMA(abs(close - open), body lookback)
Calculation: estimated candle delta = volume * (close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR ratio lookback)
Calculation: initial long stop = low of the sell-side sweep bar
Calculation: initial short stop = high of the buy-side sweep bar
Calculation: long target 1 = latest confirmed swing high when dynamic targets are enabled and that high is above entry, otherwise entry + abs(entry - initial long stop) * target 1 multiple
Calculation: short target 1 = latest confirmed swing low when dynamic targets are enabled and that low is below entry, otherwise entry - abs(entry - initial short stop) * target 1 multiple
Calculation: long stop = initial long stop; after high reaches long target 1, ratchet to the maximum of the previous stop, entry when break-even is enabled, and the selected trailing level: high - ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier for ATR mode, latest confirmed swing low for swing mode, or highest(high, Chandelier lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier for Chandelier mode
Calculation: short stop = initial short stop; after low reaches short target 1, ratchet to the minimum of the previous stop, entry when break-even is enabled, and the selected trailing level: low + ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier for ATR mode, latest confirmed swing high for swing mode, or lowest(low, Chandelier lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing multiplier for Chandelier mode
Calculation: long target 3 = entry + abs(entry - initial long stop) * target 3 multiple
Calculation: short target 3 = entry - abs(entry - initial short stop) * target 3 multiple
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, low < low of the previous completed T1 bar AND close > low of the previous completed T1 bar, followed within the CISD window by close crossing above the latest confirmed swing high AND (MSS is disabled OR abs(close - open) > average candle body * MSS body multiplier OR low > high[2]) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1]) AND (divergence filter is disabled OR the selected bullish divergence is confirmed: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI at the latest confirmed swing low while the sweep low is below that swing low, MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > MACD line at the latest confirmed swing low while the sweep low is below that swing low, or both as selected) AND (session filter is disabled OR the bar is within the configured session) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume on the sweep bar >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) on the sweep bar * RVOL threshold) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close >= EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND (delta filter is disabled OR estimated candle delta > 0) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(DMI lookback) > ADX threshold AND +DI > -DI) AND (ATR filter is disabled OR ATR ratio >= minimum ATR ratio) AND (correlation filter is disabled OR, using the reference currency index's previous completed chart-timeframe bar, direct alignment has its close above EMA(close, trend lookback) or inverse alignment has its close below that EMA) AND (news filter is disabled OR the bar is outside the configured blackout window) AND no same-side entry accepted by the entry model occurred within the same-side cooldown lookback
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, high > high of the previous completed T1 bar AND close < high of the previous completed T1 bar, followed within the CISD window by close crossing below the latest confirmed swing low AND (MSS is disabled OR abs(close - open) > average candle body * MSS body multiplier OR high < low[2]) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1]) AND (divergence filter is disabled OR the selected bearish divergence is confirmed: RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI at the latest confirmed swing high while the sweep high is above that swing high, MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) < MACD line at the latest confirmed swing high while the sweep high is above that swing high, or both as selected) AND (session filter is disabled OR the bar is within the configured session) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume on the sweep bar >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) on the sweep bar * RVOL threshold) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close <= EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND (delta filter is disabled OR estimated candle delta < 0) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX(DMI lookback) > ADX threshold AND -DI > +DI) AND (ATR filter is disabled OR ATR ratio >= minimum ATR ratio) AND (correlation filter is disabled OR, using the reference currency index's previous completed chart-timeframe bar, direct alignment has its close below EMA(close, trend lookback) or inverse alignment has its close above that EMA) AND (news filter is disabled OR the bar is outside the configured blackout window) AND no same-side entry accepted by the entry model occurred within the same-side cooldown lookback
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target 3
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target 3
