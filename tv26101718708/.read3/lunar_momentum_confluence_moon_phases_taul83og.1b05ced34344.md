Calculation: lunar phase fraction = positive modulo of (chart-bar datetime minus reference new-moon datetime), measured in days, by synodic-month length, divided by synodic-month length
Calculation: lunar bias = negative cosine of one full cycle times lunar phase fraction
Calculation: lunar phase bucket = floor((lunar phase fraction + half a phase bin) times phase count) modulo phase count
Calculation: new-moon event = current lunar phase bucket is the first bucket and differs from the previous chart bar's bucket
Calculation: full-moon event = current lunar phase bucket is the halfway bucket and differs from the previous chart bar's bucket
Calculation: lunar long score = positive part of lunar bias times lunar score scale
Calculation: lunar short score = positive part of negative lunar bias times lunar score scale
Calculation: relative volume = volume divided by max(SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), volume-baseline floor)
Calculation: bar range = max(high minus low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: candle body = absolute value of close minus open
Calculation: bullish close location = (close minus low) divided by bar range
Calculation: bearish close location = (high minus close) divided by bar range
Calculation: HTF bullish alignment = on chart bars, using lookahead-off T1 values (latest completed T1 bar historically and the T1 bar still forming live), T1 close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1) and EMA(close, fast EMA lookback, T1) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback, T1)
Calculation: HTF bearish alignment = on chart bars, using lookahead-off T1 values (latest completed T1 bar historically and the T1 bar still forming live), T1 close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1) and EMA(close, fast EMA lookback, T1) < EMA(close, slow EMA lookback, T1)
Calculation: bull momentum score = clamp the sum of trend score weight if (close > EMA(selected source, trend EMA lookback) and EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(selected source, slow EMA lookback) and EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) > its previous chart-bar value), slow-EMA score weight if close > EMA(selected source, slow EMA lookback), RSI score weight if RSI(selected source, RSI lookback) >= bull RSI threshold, RSI-extension score weight if RSI(selected source, RSI lookback) > bull RSI threshold + RSI score offset, MACD score weight if MACD histogram(selected source, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) > 0 and >= its previous chart-bar value, VWAP score weight if close > session VWAP(typical price), participation score weight if (relative volume >= relative-volume threshold and close > open and bullish close location >= close-location threshold and candle body >= ATR(ATR lookback) times body-to-ATR threshold), and HTF score weight if the HTF filter is disabled or HTF bullish alignment; clamp between score floor and score ceiling
Calculation: bear momentum score = clamp the sum of trend score weight if (close < EMA(selected source, trend EMA lookback) and EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) < EMA(selected source, slow EMA lookback) and EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) < its previous chart-bar value), slow-EMA score weight if close < EMA(selected source, slow EMA lookback), RSI score weight if RSI(selected source, RSI lookback) <= bear RSI threshold, RSI-extension score weight if RSI(selected source, RSI lookback) < bear RSI threshold - RSI score offset, MACD score weight if MACD histogram(selected source, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) < 0 and <= its previous chart-bar value, VWAP score weight if close < session VWAP(typical price), participation score weight if (relative volume >= relative-volume threshold and close < open and bearish close location >= close-location threshold and candle body >= ATR(ATR lookback) times body-to-ATR threshold), and HTF score weight if the HTF filter is disabled or HTF bearish alignment; clamp between score floor and score ceiling; use the same component weights as for bull momentum score
Calculation: long confluence = clamp(bull momentum score times momentum share + lunar long score times lunar share + full-moon event bonus when a full-moon event occurs, between score floor and score ceiling
Calculation: short confluence = clamp(bear momentum score times momentum share + lunar short score times lunar share + new-moon event bonus when a new-moon event occurs, between score floor and score ceiling
Calculation: long initial stop = lowest low over swing lookback on the entry bar minus ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar times ATR stop buffer; hold fixed from entry
Calculation: long risk = max(entry price minus long initial stop, minimum tick size times minimum risk ticks)
Calculation: long second target = entry price plus long risk times second-target R multiple; hold fixed from entry
Calculation: short initial stop = highest high over swing lookback on the entry bar plus ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry bar times ATR stop buffer; hold fixed from entry
Calculation: short risk = max(short initial stop minus entry price, minimum tick size times minimum risk ticks)
Calculation: short second target = entry price minus short risk times second-target R multiple; hold fixed from entry
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND long side is enabled AND (no prior long signal OR current chart-bar index minus prior long-signal bar index exceeds the cooldown parameter) AND lunar bias > lunar-bias threshold AND bull momentum score >= minimum momentum score AND long confluence >= minimum confluence score AND (close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback bars and close > open OR lowest low over the pullback lookback bars including current <= EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) and close > EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) and close > open and close > previous close) AND (HTF filter is disabled or HTF bullish alignment)
Short: chart bar is confirmed AND short side is enabled AND (no prior short signal OR current chart-bar index minus prior short-signal bar index exceeds the cooldown parameter) AND lunar bias < negative lunar-bias threshold AND bear momentum score >= minimum momentum score AND short confluence >= minimum confluence score AND (close < lowest low over the preceding breakout lookback bars and close < open OR highest high over the pullback lookback bars including current >= EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) and close < EMA(selected source, fast EMA lookback) and close < open and close < previous close) AND (HTF filter is disabled or HTF bearish alignment)
Exit: long, long position is open AND (low <= long initial stop captured at entry OR high >= long second target captured at entry)
Exit: short, short position is open AND (high >= short initial stop captured at entry OR low <= short second target captured at entry)
