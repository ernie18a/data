Calculation: Prior T1 high/low = high/low of the last completed T1 bar, mapped to chart bars.
Calculation: Local prior high/low = Highest(high[1], sweep lookback) / Lowest(low[1], sweep lookback).
Calculation: Latest chart swing high/low = the most recent confirmed Pivot High/Low on chart bars, using the execution pivot lookback for both left and right bars.
Calculation: Internal trend = direction of the latest BOS, initially neutral.
Calculation: Bullish zone = on an upward BOS, the bounds of the nearest preceding bearish candle within the zone lookback, using either its full high-low range or its open-close body as selected; retain until the source candle exceeds the zone-expiry age or close falls below the bottom by the stop buffer. Mark external if, at creation, a PDL sweep, confirmed break above prior T1 high, or local low sweep occurred within the external-zone lookback. Bearish zone is the corresponding nearest preceding bullish candle on a downward BOS; retain until the source candle exceeds the zone-expiry age or close rises above the top by the stop buffer, and mark external if a PDH sweep, confirmed break below prior T1 low, or local high sweep occurred within the external-zone lookback.
Calculation: Entry reference = selected market close, zone midpoint, near edge, or far edge; near/far edges are top/bottom for a bullish zone and bottom/top for a bearish zone.
Calculation: Bullish progression against setup = when progression lookback is at or below its branch threshold, close < previous close < close two bars ago; otherwise Falling(close, progression lookback). Bearish progression against setup is the corresponding rising condition, otherwise Rising(close, progression lookback).
Calculation: Bias = selected bullish, bearish, or lateral override when set; otherwise bullish on a PDL sweep, bearish on a PDH sweep, bullish on a confirmed close above prior T1 high by breakout-points parameter times point-size parameter, or bearish on a confirmed close below prior T1 low by that distance, in that precedence. Otherwise use the selected bias method: PD levels only gives neutral; HTF swings gives bullish when chart close exceeds the latest confirmed swing highs on both T2 and T3 bars, or bearish when it is below both latest confirmed swing lows; HTF moving averages gives bullish when the current, still-forming T2 and T3 closes exceed their selected SMA or EMA, or bearish when both are below. Calculate HTF pivots on their respective bars using the shared HTF pivot lookback; T2 is shorter than T3. Otherwise bias is neutral.
Calculation: Bullish initial stop = the lower of bullish-zone bottom minus stop-buffer ticks and entry reference minus bullish-zone range times stop-coverage parameter; risk distance = absolute difference from entry reference. Bearish initial stop = the higher of bearish-zone top plus stop-buffer ticks and entry reference plus bearish-zone range times stop-coverage parameter; risk distance = absolute difference from entry reference.
Calculation: Bullish final target = the lower of entry reference plus risk distance times default-RR parameter and the nearest valid obstacle above entry reference among prior T1 high, latest chart swing high, and bearish-zone bottom, each reduced by target-buffer ticks; ignore obstacles and disable adjustment when configured. Bearish final target = the higher of entry reference minus risk distance times default-RR parameter and the nearest valid obstacle below entry reference among prior T1 low, latest chart swing low, and bullish-zone top, each increased by target-buffer ticks; ignore obstacles and disable adjustment when configured.
Calculation: Bullish first/second targets = entry reference plus the respective first/second target fraction times the distance to the bullish final target; bearish first/second targets = entry reference minus the respective fraction times the distance to the bearish final target. The first fraction is less than the second.
Calculation: Bullish active stop starts at the bullish initial stop; once high reaches the entry-time latest chart swing high, increase the current stop by the configured first-break trail amount if enabled; once high reaches entry plus the configured progress threshold times the distance to the final target, raise the stop to at least entry plus the configured breakeven offset if enabled. Apply the first-break update before the progress update when both occur on one bar, and retain the resulting stop. Bearish active stop is symmetric using low, the entry-time latest chart swing low, and decreases in stop price.
Long: low < prior T1 low AND close > prior T1 low OR low < local prior low AND close > local prior low OR close crosses above the latest chart swing high plus BOS buffer.
Short: high > prior T1 high AND close < prior T1 high OR high > local prior high AND close < local prior high OR close crosses below the latest chart swing low minus BOS buffer.
Long: bullish bias AND an active bullish zone AND entry reference is within the zone AND bullish risk distance divided by point-size parameter is at most the maximum-stop parameter AND bullish final-target distance divided by risk distance is at least the minimum-RR parameter AND (enabled anticipated entry with a zone touch and either an external zone or internal zones allowed, OR enabled confirmed entry within the confirmation window after a zone touch and either BOS confirmation is disabled or an upward BOS occurs) AND progression cancellation is false AND (MA confluence is disabled or low <= selected SMA or EMA(close, moving-average lookback) AND close > that average) AND the configured symbol, session, news, manual, macro, daily trade-count, daily loss, weekly consecutive-loss, and second-trade filters permit entry AND enabled no-pullback filtering does not block the long.
Short: bearish bias AND an active bearish zone AND entry reference is within the zone AND bearish risk distance divided by point-size parameter is at most the maximum-stop parameter AND bearish final-target distance divided by risk distance is at least the minimum-RR parameter AND (enabled anticipated entry with a zone touch and either an external zone or internal zones allowed, OR enabled confirmed entry within the confirmation window after a zone touch and either BOS confirmation is disabled or a downward BOS occurs) AND progression cancellation is false AND (MA confluence is disabled or high >= selected SMA or EMA(close, moving-average lookback) AND close < that average) AND the configured symbol, session, news, manual, macro, daily trade-count, daily loss, weekly consecutive-loss, and second-trade filters permit entry AND enabled no-pullback filtering does not block the short.
Exit: long, low <= bullish active stop OR high >= bullish first target OR high >= bullish second target OR high >= bullish final target.
Exit: short, high >= bearish active stop OR low <= bearish first target OR low <= bearish second target OR low <= bearish final target.
