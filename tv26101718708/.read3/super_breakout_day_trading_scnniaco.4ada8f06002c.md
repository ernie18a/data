Calculation: pre-session high = maximum high of chart bars from the start of the configured pre-session through the current chart bar; reset at the first chart bar whose T2 session time falls in the pre-session and retain outside the pre-session
Calculation: Bollinger basis = SMA(close, Bollinger lookback)
Calculation: upper band = upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger deviation multiplier)
Calculation: volume flow = VFI(HLC3, volume, long VFI lookback, medium cutoff-volatility lookback, cutoff coefficient, volume-cap factor)
Calculation: smoothed volume flow = EMA(volume flow, short signal lookback)
Long: the current chart bar has a present time(T1, configured trade session) AND close[pre-session lag] > pre-session high AND (bars since any Long exit condition > exit cooldown OR (close > Bollinger basis AND close < standard deviation(close, Bollinger lookback) * larger deviation multiplier)) AND (close crosses above upper band OR (close[short price lag] > upper band AND close > EMA(close, Bollinger lookback) AND (volume flow > lowest flow threshold OR smoothed volume flow > lowest flow threshold) AND (volume flow < middle flow threshold OR smoothed volume flow < middle flow threshold))) AND close < close[long price lag] * larger long-lag price cap multiplier AND close < close[short price lag] * smaller short-lag price cap multiplier; pre-session lag < short price lag < long price lag; short SMA lookback < Bollinger lookback; short signal lookback < medium cutoff-volatility lookback < long VFI lookback
Exit: Long, close crosses below lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger deviation multiplier) OR volume flow > highest flow threshold OR smoothed volume flow > highest flow threshold OR (low < Parabolic SAR(acceleration start, acceleration increment, maximum acceleration) AND close < SMA(close, short SMA lookback))
