Calculation: Active liquidity zones = Process confirmed pivot highs then pivot lows on each chart bar, using pivot lookback bars on both sides. A pivot creates a resistance zone at its price for a pivot high or a support zone for a pivot low, with touch count one and unswept state. Merge it into the first existing zone whose absolute level difference is less than the pivot price multiplied by the merge threshold divided by 100; set the merged level to the average and increment its touch count, retaining its direction and swept state. After adding a zone, remove the oldest zones until the count is at most the maximum active zones. A zone becomes swept after its first sweep that passes the volume filter and then cannot signal again.
