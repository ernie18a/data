Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high, using equal left and right swing lookbacks in Swing mode or equal left and right internal lookbacks in Internal mode, if that pivot has not previously been crossed
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low, using equal left and right swing lookbacks in Swing mode or equal left and right internal lookbacks in Internal mode, if that pivot has not previously been crossed
Long: on chart bars when T1 changes, the current T1 bar's low > the T1 bar two bars earlier's high AND the gap is at least chart-bar ATR(true range, lookback) times the minimum gap multiplier; use T1 values with lookahead off (the current T1 bar in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically)
Short: on chart bars when T1 changes, the current T1 bar's high < the T1 bar two bars earlier's low AND the gap is at least chart-bar ATR(true range, lookback) times the minimum gap multiplier; use T1 values with lookahead off (the current T1 bar in realtime and the last completed T1 bar historically)
