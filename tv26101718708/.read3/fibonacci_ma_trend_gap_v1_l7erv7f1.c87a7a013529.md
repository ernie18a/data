Calculation: Trend mean = arithmetic mean of the first N selected moving averages of source, each with its own lookback; the selected type is SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, VWMA, RMA/SMMA, or DEMA.
Calculation: Oscillator = (close² − trend mean²) / trend mean² when trend mean ≠ 0.
Long: SMA(oscillator, short signal lookback) > SMA(oscillator, long signal lookback) OR a newly confirmed PivotLow(oscillator, left lookback, right lookback) is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low and low at its pivot bar is lower than low at the preceding pivot bar OR a newly confirmed PivotLow(oscillator, left lookback, right lookback) is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low and low at its pivot bar is higher than low at the preceding pivot bar.
Short: SMA(oscillator, short signal lookback) ≤ SMA(oscillator, long signal lookback) OR a newly confirmed PivotHigh(oscillator, left lookback, right lookback) is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high and high at its pivot bar is higher than high at the preceding pivot bar OR a newly confirmed PivotHigh(oscillator, left lookback, right lookback) is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high and high at its pivot bar is lower than high at the preceding pivot bar.
