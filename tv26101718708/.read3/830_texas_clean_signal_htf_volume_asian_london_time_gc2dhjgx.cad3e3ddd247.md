Calculation: Opening-range high = highest(high on chart bars) during the first T3 interval after the U.S. cash-session open in America/Chicago
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest(low on chart bars) during the same T3 interval
Long: At the end of the T3 opening interval, its final chart-bar close > VWAP(close) on that bar
Short: At the end of the T3 opening interval, its final chart-bar close < VWAP(close) on that bar
Long: The opening-interval final close < its VWAP(close) AND close > Opening-range high AND close > VWAP(close)
Short: The opening-interval final close > its VWAP(close) AND close < Opening-range low AND close < VWAP(close)
Long: The T3 opening interval is complete AND close > VWAP(close) AND close crosses above Opening-range high AND close > EMA(high, shared chart EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, same lookback) AND (RSI(close, lookback) > threshold OR MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slower lookback, signal lookback) > its previous chart-bar value) AND (if HTF trend is enabled, EMA(close, shorter HTF EMA lookback, T1) > EMA(close, longer HTF EMA lookback, T1) AND EMA(close, shorter HTF EMA lookback, T2) > EMA(close, longer HTF EMA lookback, T2)) AND (if volume confirmation is enabled, volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (the opening-interval final close > its VWAP(close) OR the opening-interval final close < its VWAP(close)); T1 is shorter than T2; HTF EMA values are calculated on T1 and T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming HTF bar in real time and confirmed values historically; the shorter and longer HTF EMA lookbacks are shared across T1 and T2
Short: The T3 opening interval is complete AND close < VWAP(close) AND close crosses below Opening-range low AND close < EMA(low, shared chart EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, same lookback) AND (RSI(close, lookback) < threshold OR MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slower lookback, signal lookback) < its previous chart-bar value) AND (if HTF trend is enabled, EMA(close, shorter HTF EMA lookback, T1) < EMA(close, longer HTF EMA lookback, T1) AND EMA(close, shorter HTF EMA lookback, T2) < EMA(close, longer HTF EMA lookback, T2)) AND (if volume confirmation is enabled, volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier) AND (the opening-interval final close > its VWAP(close) OR the opening-interval final close < its VWAP(close)); T1 is shorter than T2; HTF EMA values are calculated on T1 and T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming HTF bar in real time and confirmed values historically; the shorter and longer HTF EMA lookbacks are shared across T1 and T2
