Calculation: Direction(T) = 1 when close(T) > SMA(close(T), shared MA lookback) and round(ADX(T, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing)) >= minimum ADX; -1 when close(T) < SMA(close(T), shared MA lookback) and round(ADX(T, ADX lookback, ADX smoothing)) >= minimum ADX; otherwise 0. Calculate on each timeframe’s bars; chart bars use T1 and T2 values from lookahead-off, meaning the last completed T bar on historical bars and the still-forming T bar in real time. Use the same MA lookback on the chart timeframe, T1, and T2; use the same parameter for ADX lookback and smoothing.
Calculation: Confluence = when chart direction is 1 or -1, the number of chart, T1, and T2 directions equal to chart direction; otherwise 0.
Calculation: A pending alignment event begins at an alignment event’s chart-bar close and remains pending until the close moves at least the outcome distance multiplier times current chart ATR(ATR lookback) in the event direction, or the outcome window of chart bars elapses.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, chart direction = 1 AND (Confluence = 3 OR ((Confluence != Confluence[1] OR chart direction != chart direction[1]) AND no pending alignment event after checking its resolution on this bar)).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, chart direction = -1 AND (Confluence = 3 OR ((Confluence != Confluence[1] OR chart direction != chart direction[1]) AND no pending alignment event after checking its resolution on this bar)).
