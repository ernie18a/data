Calculation: Projected volume(T, chart-bar offset) = volume requested from the T bar available at each chart bar, projected onto chart bars and indexed by chart-bar offset
Calculation: SMA-event close difference = ValueWhen(close > SMA(close, SMA lookback), close, older occurrence) - ValueWhen(close > SMA(close, SMA lookback), close, newer occurrence), on chart bars; older occurrence index > newer occurrence index
Calculation: Candle flag = IF((IF(T1 close[older close offset] > T1 open[prior-bar offset], 1, 0) + IF(T2 close[older close offset] > T2 current open, 1, 0)) > IF(T3 close[prior-bar offset] > T3 open[prior-bar offset], 1, 0), 1, 0), with comparisons evaluated on their timeframe bars and projected to chart bars; T1 and T2 use the current timeframe bar, T3 uses the last completed bar, and older close offset > prior-bar offset
Long: Projected volume(T1, longer chart-bar offset A) > volume threshold 1 * Projected volume(T1, shorter chart-bar offset) AND Projected volume(T1, longer chart-bar offset B) > volume threshold 3 * Projected volume(T1, shorter chart-bar offset) AND Projected volume(T5, shorter chart-bar offset A) > volume threshold 2 * Projected volume(T5, longer chart-bar offset A) AND Projected volume(T5, longer chart-bar offset B) > volume threshold 2 * Projected volume(T5, shorter chart-bar offset B) AND (ATR(high, low, close, boundary ATR lookback) > upper ATR threshold OR ATR(high, low, close, boundary ATR lookback) < lower ATR threshold) AND Candle flag = 0 AND BarsSince(RSI(close, RSI length) <= RSI threshold) <= oversold recency limit AND SMA-event close difference > distance threshold AND T4 ATR(high, low, close, shorter ATR lookback) < T4 ATR(high, low, close, longer ATR lookback), evaluated on the current T4 bar and projected to chart bars AND BarsSince(close > SMA(close, SMA lookback)) < above-SMA recency limit AND datetime >= start timestamp AND datetime < end timestamp
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - close at the most recent bar satisfying the long-entry condition * long stop-loss percentage / 100 OR high >= entry price + close at the most recent bar satisfying the long-entry condition * long take-profit percentage / 100
