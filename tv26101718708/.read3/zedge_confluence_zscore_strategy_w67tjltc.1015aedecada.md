Calculation: smoothed composite = recursively smooth C with alpha 2/(round(L)+1), initialized to C, where C = (momentum weight * ZScore(ROC(source, momentum lookback), z-score lookback) + RSI weight * ZScore(RSI(source, RSI lookback), z-score lookback) + volume weight * ZScore(volume/SMA(volume, volume-average lookback), z-score lookback))/(sum of the three weights, or 1 if their sum is 0); L = max(round(max smoothing length - (max smoothing length - min smoothing length) * PercentRank(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-rank lookback)/100), 1) when adaptive smoothing is enabled, otherwise base smoothing length
Long: long trades enabled AND account equity > 0 AND ATR(ATR lookback) > 0 AND close > 0 AND ((zero-cross mode selected AND smoothed composite crosses above 0) OR (threshold-reversion mode selected AND smoothed composite crosses above the long-entry threshold))
Short: short trades enabled AND account equity > 0 AND ATR(ATR lookback) > 0 AND close > 0 AND ((zero-cross mode selected AND smoothed composite crosses below 0) OR (threshold-reversion mode selected AND smoothed composite crosses below the short-entry threshold))
Long: divergence detection enabled AND a pivot low of the smoothed composite is confirmed using separate left-bars and right-bars parameters AND the low at the latest confirmed pivot is below the low at the preceding confirmed pivot AND the smoothed composite at the latest pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot
Short: divergence detection enabled AND a pivot high of the smoothed composite is confirmed using separate left-bars and right-bars parameters AND the high at the latest confirmed pivot is above the high at the preceding confirmed pivot AND the smoothed composite at the latest pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot
Exit: long, smoothed composite crosses below the long exit level OR (ATR stop enabled AND low reaches the stop set at the latest long-entry signal: that bar's close minus that bar's ATR(ATR lookback) times the stop multiplier)
Exit: short, smoothed composite crosses above the short exit level OR (ATR stop enabled AND high reaches the stop set at the latest short-entry signal: that bar's close plus that bar's ATR(ATR lookback) times the stop multiplier)
