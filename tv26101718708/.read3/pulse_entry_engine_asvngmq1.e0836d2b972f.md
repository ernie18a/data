Calculation: Distance ratio = if SMA(abs(Special K(source, primary lookback, secondary lookback) − its signal line), distance lookback) > 0 then abs(Special K − its signal line) / SMA(abs(Special K − its signal line), distance lookback) else 0
Calculation: Signal score(side) = clamp(15 + 40 × min(distance ratio / mode-selected extreme stretch threshold, 1) + 15 if the zero filter is inactive or Special K is below zero for long or above zero for short + 20 if the EMA filter is inactive or close is below EMA(close, EMA lookback) for long or above it for short + 10 if distance ratio >= mode-selected distance threshold, 0, 100)
Calculation: HTF allowed(side) = HTF filter off, or the selected HTF mode is EMA short lookback and T1 close >= EMA(T1 close, short HTF EMA lookback), or EMA long lookback and T1 close >= EMA(T1 close, long HTF EMA lookback), or Engine and T1 Special K(close, primary lookback, secondary lookback) >= its signal line for long; use <= for short. Calculate on T1 and sample on chart bars, using the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR(high, low, close, risk lookback) × stop multiplier, fixed at entry
Calculation: Long TP1 = entry price + risk distance × reward multiple × first-target fraction
Calculation: Short TP1 = entry price − risk distance × reward multiple × first-target fraction
Calculation: Long TP3 = entry price + risk distance × reward multiple
Calculation: Short TP3 = entry price − risk distance × reward multiple
Long: Special K(source, primary lookback, secondary lookback) < its signal line AND distance ratio >= mode-selected distance threshold AND (zero filter inactive or Special K < 0) AND (EMA filter inactive or close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND Signal score(long) >= mode-selected minimum score AND HTF allowed(long) AND signal state is not long AND (live intrabar confirmation selected or bar confirmed) AND (projection system disabled or no active projection awaiting TP1); reset signal state to neutral when reset is enabled, distance ratio <= reset threshold, and neither side's setup passes its filters, HTF bias, and score
Short: Special K(source, primary lookback, secondary lookback) > its signal line AND distance ratio >= mode-selected distance threshold AND (zero filter inactive or Special K > 0) AND (EMA filter inactive or close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND Signal score(short) >= mode-selected minimum score AND HTF allowed(short) AND signal state is not short AND (live intrabar confirmation selected or bar confirmed) AND (projection system disabled or no active projection awaiting TP1); reset signal state to neutral when reset is enabled, distance ratio <= reset threshold, and neither side's setup passes its filters, HTF bias, and score
Exit: long, projection system enabled AND active projection AND bar after entry AND (low <= entry price − risk distance OR high >= Long TP3 OR (Long TP1 reached and a new long signal occurs))
Exit: short, projection system enabled AND active projection AND bar after entry AND (high >= entry price + risk distance OR low <= Short TP3 OR (Short TP1 reached and a new short signal occurs))
