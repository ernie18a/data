Calculation: Recursive filtered volatility = abs(change(source)) when rising(source, band lookback) or falling(source, band lookback); otherwise its previous value.
Calculation: Band volatility(x) = ATR(band lookback), standard deviation(source, band lookback), SMA(high − low, band lookback), recursive filtered volatility, or x, according to the selected method (ATR, STDEV, AHLR, RFV, or Classic), respectively.
Calculation: Upper band = max(source, previous upper band with source as the initial value) − 2/(band lookback + 1) × band volatility(abs(source − previous upper band with source as the initial value)).
Calculation: Lower band = min(source, previous lower band with source as the initial value) + 2/(band lookback + 1) × band volatility(abs(source − previous lower band with source as the initial value)).
Calculation: CF direction turns up when the current high is a highest high over the CF pivot lookback alone, turns down when the current low is a lowest low over that lookback alone, and otherwise retains its direction; when both extremes occur, select the current high if direction is up and the current low otherwise. CF pivot is the highest selected high in the uninterrupted up-direction run, the lowest selected low in the uninterrupted down-direction run, or the latest selected point while direction is neutral.
Calculation: CF count starts at zero, increments on bars without a CF pivot change, and on a pivot change increments only if the prior count is greater than zero and the pivot is within the inclusive current CF range; otherwise it resets to zero. When count reaches the CF range lookback, initialize the range to the highest high and lowest low over that lookback; while count is greater, expand the range to include each bar's high and low.
Calculation: Long stop = entry-bar low − ATR(stop lookback) on the entry bar × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop = entry-bar high + ATR(stop lookback) on the entry bar × stop multiplier.
Long: crosses below(low, lower band) AND (band-direction filter disabled OR lower band[n] > lower band[n+1] for every n from 0 to direction lookback − 1) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) < RSI oversold threshold) AND (MFI filter disabled OR MFI(MFI source, MFI lookback) < MFI oversold threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (CF filter disabled OR CF count < CF range lookback) AND (opposite-side entries enabled OR position is flat or long).
Short: crosses above(high, upper band) AND (band-direction filter disabled OR upper band[n] < upper band[n+1] for every n from 0 to direction lookback − 1) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(RSI source, RSI lookback) > RSI overbought threshold) AND (MFI filter disabled OR MFI(MFI source, MFI lookback) > MFI overbought threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (CF filter disabled OR CF count < CF range lookback) AND (opposite-side entries enabled OR position is flat or short).
Exit: long, low < long stop OR high > entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward/risk parameter.
Exit: short, high > short stop OR low < entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward/risk parameter.
