Calculation: tracked swing levels = confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing-pivot lookback bars on each side) and Pivot High(high, swing-pivot lookback bars on each side), retaining the newest tracked-level limit per side; consume a level when a chart bar pierces it, spans it, and its penetration depth passes the ATR depth gate
Calculation: bullish reclaim setup = on chart bars, test only the highest retained swing low strictly above low; a sweep requires low < level, high >= level, and level - low within the minimum and maximum wick-depth ATR bounds (unless the maximum-wick cap is disabled); reclaim requires close > level on the sweep bar or within the reclaim-window chart bars after it; for delayed reclaim, swept wick extreme is the lowest low from sweep through reclaim
Calculation: bearish reclaim setup = on chart bars, test only the lowest retained swing high strictly below high; a sweep requires high > level, low <= level, and high - level within the minimum and maximum wick-depth ATR bounds (unless the maximum-wick cap is disabled); reclaim requires close < level on the sweep bar or within the reclaim-window chart bars after it; for delayed reclaim, swept wick extreme is the highest high from sweep through reclaim
Calculation: trade risk = min(max(abs(entry price - raw stop), minimum-risk ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback)), maximum-risk ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback)), where entry price is reclaim-bar close and raw stop is swept low extreme - stop-buffer ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback) for long or swept high extreme + stop-buffer ATR multiple * ATR(ATR lookback) for short
Long: bullish reclaim setup AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available on the reclaim bar AND long direction is enabled AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume is missing OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) is missing or <= 0 OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-spike multiple) AND (T1 trend alignment is disabled OR EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar is missing OR chart close > that EMA) AND (dual-side blocking is disabled OR no bearish reclaim setup occurs on this bar) AND the bullish and bearish setups do not both pass their enabled filters and direction restrictions on this bar AND no prior signal exists or elapsed chart bars since the prior signal >= cooldown bars AND no tracked position is open AND no tracked position was resolved on this bar
Short: bearish reclaim setup AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available on the reclaim bar AND short direction is enabled AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume is missing OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) is missing or <= 0 OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-spike multiple) AND (T1 trend alignment is disabled OR EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar is missing OR chart close < that EMA) AND (dual-side blocking is disabled OR no bullish reclaim setup occurs on this bar) AND the bullish and bearish setups do not both pass their enabled filters and direction restrictions on this bar AND no prior signal exists or elapsed chart bars since the prior signal >= cooldown bars AND no tracked position is open AND no tracked position was resolved on this bar
Exit: long, while the position is open on a chart bar after entry, low <= entry price - trade risk OR high >= entry price + trade risk * third-target R multiple OR elapsed chart bars since entry >= trade-timeout bars
Exit: short, while the position is open on a chart bar after entry, high >= entry price + trade risk OR low <= entry price - trade risk * third-target R multiple OR elapsed chart bars since entry >= trade-timeout bars
