Calculation: Oriented leader close = (inverse leader ? -1 : 1) × leader market close[configured lead-lag bars ago], using selected chart price if leader data is unavailable
Calculation: Leader oscillator = optionally smooth the selected z-score with the configured EMA or Ultimate Smoother when smoothing is enabled; the z-score is either (oriented leader close − rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation or (oriented leader close − rolling median) / (1.4826 × rolling median absolute deviation), using either the normalization lookback or the median of z-scores at half, one, and twice that lookback
Long: On a chart bar confirming a pivot low of the Leader oscillator using the configured pivot lookback on both sides, the latest oscillator low is higher than the preceding oscillator low and the selected chart price at the latest pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot; if three pivots are configured, the preceding oscillator low is also higher than the one before it and its chart price is lower; alternatively, when hidden divergence is enabled, the latest oscillator low is lower and its chart price is higher, with the same additional ordering in three-pivot mode; the latest pivot pair is no more than the configured maximum gap apart and its oscillator difference in absolute value is at least the configured minimum gap multiplied by the current standard deviation of the Leader oscillator over the normalization lookback; and (correlation gate disabled or absolute rolling correlation of selected chart price and oriented leader close over the correlation lookback is at least the configured minimum) and (time-of-day gate disabled or the absolute slot z-score of selected chart price change is at least the configured significance threshold)
Short: On a chart bar confirming a pivot high of the Leader oscillator using the configured pivot lookback on both sides, the latest oscillator high is lower than the preceding oscillator high and the selected chart price at the latest pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot; if three pivots are configured, the preceding oscillator high is also lower than the one before it and its chart price is higher; alternatively, when hidden divergence is enabled, the latest oscillator high is higher and its chart price is lower, with the same additional ordering in three-pivot mode; the latest pivot pair is no more than the configured maximum gap apart and its oscillator difference in absolute value is at least the configured minimum gap multiplied by the current standard deviation of the Leader oscillator over the normalization lookback; and (correlation gate disabled or absolute rolling correlation of selected chart price and oriented leader close over the correlation lookback is at least the configured minimum) and (time-of-day gate disabled or the absolute slot z-score of selected chart price change is at least the configured significance threshold)
