Calculation: VolumeProfile = bin volumes over an equal-width span of bins (count = bin count) from Lowest(low, lookback) * (1 - pad) to Highest(high, lookback) * (1 + pad); each bar's volume is split into a body share (body bias) spread over bins in proportion to overlap with the open-to-close range and a wick share (1 - body bias) spread equally over the bins from low to high; when close lies inside the current span, all bins are multiplied by exp(-1/lookback) before the current bar is added; when close lies outside it, the span is re-anchored to the current bounds and the bins are rebuilt from the last lookback bars, bar k bars back weighted by exp(-k/lookback)
Calculation: PointOfControl = center price of the bin with the greatest VolumeProfile volume (lowest such bin on ties)
Calculation: ValueAreaBins = starting from the PointOfControl bin, repeatedly add the adjacent existing bin with the greater VolumeProfile volume (upper bin on ties) until the total VolumeProfile volume of the included bins is at least value-area share * total VolumeProfile volume
Calculation: ValueAreaHigh = upper price edge of the highest bin in ValueAreaBins
Calculation: ValueAreaLow = lower price edge of the lowest bin in ValueAreaBins
Calculation: ValueLocation = EMA(limit(PiecewiseLinear(close), -50, 50), smoothing length), where PiecewiseLinear(close) is 0 at PointOfControl, +25 at ValueAreaHigh, -25 at ValueAreaLow, linear between these points, linear above ValueAreaHigh up to +50 at max(Highest(high, lookback), ValueAreaHigh), and linear below ValueAreaLow down to -50 at min(Lowest(low, lookback), ValueAreaLow)
Long: ValueLocation <= -stretch threshold AND EMA(ValueLocation, turn length) > EMA(ValueLocation, turn length)[1]
Short: ValueLocation >= stretch threshold AND EMA(ValueLocation, turn length) < EMA(ValueLocation, turn length)[1]
