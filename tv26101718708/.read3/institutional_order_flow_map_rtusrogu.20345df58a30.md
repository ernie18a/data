Calculation: lower rejection percent = 100 x (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: upper rejection percent = 100 x (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: signed volume delta = (close - open) / (high - low) x volume, or 0 when high = low
Calculation: normalized flow = 100 x SUM(signed volume delta, flow lookback) / HIGHEST(ABS(SUM(signed volume delta, flow lookback)), normalization lookback), or 0 when the denominator is 0
Calculation: zone score(side, pivot bar) = MIN(volume score + rejection score + trend score, total score cap), where volume score = MIN(ROUND(volume[pivot bar] / MAX(SMA(volume, volume lookback) on the confirmation bar, volume floor) x volume score weight), volume score cap), rejection score = MIN(ROUND(rejection percent at pivot bar x rejection score multiplier), rejection score cap), and trend score = aligned trend points when confirmation-bar close is above EMA(trend lookback) for a buy zone or below it for a sell zone, otherwise unaligned trend points; use lower rejection percent for buy zones and upper rejection percent for sell zones
Calculation: zone center = (high[pivot bar] + low[pivot bar]) / 2; zone half-height = ATR(ATR lookback) on the confirmation bar x zone height parameter / 2; zone bounds = center plus or minus zone half-height
Calculation: active zones are qualifying zones whose mitigation condition has not occurred: low <= lower bound for buy zones, high >= upper bound for sell zones; highest active zone score is the maximum score of active zones, or 0 if none
Long: volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) x volume multiplier AND lower rejection percent >= minimum wick percent AND close > open, OR normalized flow > flow threshold, OR a confirmed pivot low with pivot lookback bars on each side has zone score(buy, pivot bar) >= minimum zone score, OR highest active buy-zone score > highest active sell-zone score
Short: volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) x volume multiplier AND upper rejection percent >= minimum wick percent AND close < open, OR normalized flow < -flow threshold, OR a confirmed pivot high with pivot lookback bars on each side has zone score(sell, pivot bar) >= minimum zone score, OR highest active sell-zone score > highest active buy-zone score
