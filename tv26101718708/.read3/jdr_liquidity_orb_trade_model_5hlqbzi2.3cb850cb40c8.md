Calculation: session opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars accumulated from regular-session start through the first chart bar at or after the T2 boundary; lock when that bar completes
Calculation: previous-session opening-range high/low = finalized session opening range from the immediately preceding regular session
Calculation: overnight-session high/low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the overnight session
Calculation: candle-body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low)
Long: signal display enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND regular session AND the latest low sweep occurred within the confirmation-window parameter chart bars AND close > its selected level AND low < at least one of the last completed T1 bar's low sampled onto chart bars, overnight-session low, completed current-session opening-range low, or previous-session opening-range low; each new sweep resets the window, and if multiple levels are swept on one bar select the last matching level in that listed order; AND (trend filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (displacement filter disabled OR (close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × body-to-ATR threshold AND candle-body fraction >= body-fraction threshold AND close > previous chart-bar high)) AND (FVG filter disabled OR low > high[FVG lookback parameter bars ago]) AND fewer than the per-session maximum accepted entries have occurred AND (no prior accepted entry exists OR at least the cooldown parameter chart bars have elapsed since the latest accepted entry)
Short: signal display enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND regular session AND the latest high sweep occurred within the confirmation-window parameter chart bars AND close < its selected level AND high > at least one of the last completed T1 bar's high sampled onto chart bars, overnight-session high, completed current-session opening-range high, or previous-session opening-range high; each new sweep resets the window, and if multiple levels are swept on one bar select the last matching level in that listed order; AND (trend filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)) AND (displacement filter disabled OR (close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × body-to-ATR threshold AND candle-body fraction >= body-fraction threshold AND close < previous chart-bar low)) AND (FVG filter disabled OR high < low[FVG lookback parameter bars ago]) AND fewer than the per-session maximum accepted entries have occurred AND (no prior accepted entry exists OR at least the cooldown parameter chart bars have elapsed since the latest accepted entry)
Exit: Long, high >= entry + (entry - low of entry bar) × risk-reward parameter OR low <= low of entry bar
Exit: Short, low <= entry - (high of entry bar - entry) × risk-reward parameter OR high >= high of entry bar
