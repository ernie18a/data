Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Volume spike buy event = volume > SMA(volume, long volume lookback) * buy spike multiplier AND smoothed RSI < volume buy RSI level AND (volume[1] > SMA(volume, short volume lookback) OR volume[2] > SMA(volume, short volume lookback)) AND (no prior volume spike buy event OR at least volume spacing bars since the previous volume spike buy event)
Calculation: Volume spike sell event = volume > SMA(volume, long volume lookback) * sell spike multiplier AND smoothed RSI > volume sell RSI level AND (no prior volume spike sell event OR at least volume spacing bars since the previous volume spike sell event)
Calculation: RSI divergence buy event = smoothed RSI < divergence buy RSI level AND close > Lowest(close, divergence lookback) AND close < Lowest(close, divergence lookback) * (1 + buy low distance percentage / 100) AND smoothed RSI > Lowest(smoothed RSI, divergence lookback) AND smoothed RSI > smoothed RSI[1] AND (no prior RSI divergence buy event OR at least divergence spacing bars since the previous RSI divergence buy event)
Calculation: RSI divergence sell event = smoothed RSI > divergence sell RSI level AND close < Highest(close, divergence lookback) AND close > Highest(close, divergence lookback) * (1 - sell high distance percentage / 100) AND smoothed RSI < Highest(smoothed RSI, divergence lookback) AND (no prior RSI divergence sell event OR at least divergence spacing bars since the previous RSI divergence sell event)
Long: ((volume signals enabled AND NOT divergence signals enabled AND volume spike buy event) OR (divergence signals enabled AND NOT volume signals enabled AND RSI divergence buy event) OR (volume signals enabled AND divergence signals enabled AND ((volume spike buy event AND an RSI divergence buy event occurred no more than the confirmation window bars ago, including the current bar) OR (RSI divergence buy event AND a volume spike buy event occurred no more than the confirmation window bars ago, including the current bar)))) AND (no prior long signal OR at least overall spacing bars since the previous long signal)
Short: ((volume signals enabled AND NOT divergence signals enabled AND volume spike sell event) OR (divergence signals enabled AND NOT volume signals enabled AND RSI divergence sell event) OR (volume signals enabled AND divergence signals enabled AND ((volume spike sell event AND an RSI divergence sell event occurred no more than the confirmation window bars ago, including the current bar) OR (RSI divergence sell event AND a volume spike sell event occurred no more than the confirmation window bars ago, including the current bar)))) AND (no prior short signal OR at least overall spacing bars since the previous short signal)
