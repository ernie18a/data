Calculation: Chart swings = alternating high and low pivots from rolling highest(high, shorter lookback) and lowest(low, shorter lookback), or confirmed pivot highs and lows using separate left and confirmation lookbacks; optional additional swing streams use longer lookbacks. Evaluate on confirmed chart bars; nested candidates may skip intervening pivots only when each skipped pivot remains within its proposed leg.
Calculation: XABCD match = use X,A,B,C,D swing points, with long side when D is a low and short side when D is a high; require A and C to lie on the trade side of B, and D to reverse past C. Match family-specific ratio bands, expanding each lower bound by the error tolerance and each upper bound by the error tolerance: Gartley, Butterfly, Bat, Crab, Alternate Bat, Deep Crab, White Swan, Black Swan, Shark, Nen Star, Leonardo, Partizan, Anti-Gartley, Anti-Butterfly, Anti-Bat, Anti-Crab, Anti-Shark, Anti-Cypher, and Anti-Nen Star each use separate AB/XA, BC/AB, CD/BC, and AD/XA bands; Cypher uses separate AB/XA, XC/XA, and CD/XC completion bands; AB=CD uses separate BC/AB, CD/BC, and AD/AB bands; 5-0 uses separate AB/XA, BC/AB, CD/BC extension, and CD/BC completion bands. Intersect the applicable completion bands, require D within their intersection, and for an extension CD/BC band require D beyond B. Use confirmed or developing D pivots according to the selected swing and D modes; evaluate developing D at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Double top or bottom = use contained alternating swing legs with lead,A,B,C; for a double bottom set long side and for a double top set short side. Require A and C to be same-side extrema, B to be the intervening opposite extreme, height=(B-C)*side positive, abs(A-C) <= height * classical symmetry tolerance, and (lead-B)*side > 0; after C, require high >= B for a bottom or low <= B for a top, and pattern duration within its minimum and maximum bar parameters.
Calculation: Triangle = use X,A,B,C swing points, with X and B defining one boundary and A and C the other. Require contracting boundaries, upper boundary at the current bar above the lower boundary, AB divided by the initial width and BC divided by AB each at least the minimum retracement parameter, and duration and C-to-breakout bars within their limits. Classify as ascending when highs are flat within classical symmetry tolerance and lows rise, descending when lows are flat and highs fall, or symmetrical when highs fall and lows rise. Project both boundaries linearly to the current bar; side is long above the upper boundary and short below the lower boundary, confirmed by close.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders pattern = use lead, left shoulder, first neckline pivot, head, second neckline pivot, and right shoulder, allowing the configured contained skipped pivots and right-shoulder tail. For regular head-and-shoulders use short side; for inverse use long side. Require the head to exceed both shoulders, both shoulders to be within the shoulder-difference parameter of the head depth, the lead to lie beyond the first neckline, neckline pivots within error tolerance, each shoulder retracement within its shoulder-ratio bands, the head to be the extreme of the maximum-pattern lookback at the break, and a break beyond the outer neckline by high for inverse or low for regular. Require pattern duration within its limits.
Calculation: Three-drive pattern = use five alternating drive and retracement pivots, with long side when the last drive ends at a low and short side when it ends at a high. Require drives two and three to extend their preceding retracements within separate drive-ratio bands, the second retracement to be within its band relative to drive two, and the three drive ends and two retracement ends to progress in the trade direction. When a preceding pivot exists, require the first retracement to fall within its own band relative to the first drive. Require pattern duration within its limits and the last pivot within the pivot-confirmation window.
Calculation: Candle confirmation = bullish body and either close above prior high with open at or below prior close, or a lower wick at least the wick-to-body parameter times the body with close above the bar midpoint; bearish body and either close below prior low with open at or above prior close, or an upper wick at least that parameter times the body with close below the midpoint.
Calculation: Pattern entry and targets = entry level is D price for XABCD patterns or the formation trigger for classical patterns, plus side times the entry percentage of the selected leg. For XABCD patterns the standard basis uses AD for AB=CD, Black Swan, Bat, Anti-Cypher, Anti-Bat, and Anti-Nen Star, and CD for other families; the all-CD basis uses CD, and the XABCD-AD basis uses AD for XABCD patterns other than AB=CD. For classical patterns use the formation unit: double reversal height times its multiplier, head-and-shoulders depth, triangle width, or the Three-drive unit. Targets use entry plus side times initial risk times their separate reward-to-risk parameters when enabled; otherwise use the formation reference plus side times the selected leg times each target percentage.
Calculation: Stop price = initial long stop at the adverse lower validation-zone edge and initial short stop at its upper edge, expanded by the zone-padding percentage of CD; manual mode instead places the stop one manual-stop percentage of absolute entry beyond entry. After Target 1, break-even mode moves the stop by its configured percentage of the entry-to-Target-1 distance. Trailing mode, starting after entry or Target 1 as configured, places it the trail-distance parameter times the entry-to-Target-1 distance behind the best favorable high or low since entry. Never loosen a stop; enforce at least one tick of initial risk.
Calculation: Eligible pattern = enabled XABCD or classical match detected on a confirmed chart bar, within the configured pattern duration, minimum C-to-D duration where applicable, and minimum height; for AB=CD also require its configured CD/AB duration band. Require the selected direction and, when enabled, current close above the prior chart-bar EMA or SMA for long or below it for short. Require the configured session and detection start time, D inside the validation zone, and current close not beyond its adverse edge. If enabled, require the close within the padded validation zone and on the permitted side of the calculated entry level. For XABCD patterns only, apply the configured minimum quality score, defined as the mean of defining-ratio scores that equal one inside each nominal band and decay linearly to zero across the error tolerance; if enabled, require D-bar volume at least its multiplier times SMA(volume, longer lookback) evaluated at D. Exclude blocked same-name-and-side active patterns, previously detected geometry, and families disabled by the configured past-trade win-rate filter. Require active-pattern and per-bar pattern counts below their configured limits.
Long: an eligible pattern has long side.
Short: an eligible pattern has short side.
Long: a pending eligible long pattern remains valid AND the configured trigger is met: close >= entry level for close confirmation; high >= entry level for high/low touch; or low <= validation-zone upper edge AND bullish candle confirmation for candle confirmation. Reject if the configured invalidation source (close or low) is below the validation-zone lower edge, if open >= Target 1, or, except for high/low touch mode, if close >= Target 1.
Short: a pending eligible short pattern remains valid AND the configured trigger is met: close <= entry level for close confirmation; low <= entry level for high/low touch; or high >= validation-zone lower edge AND bearish candle confirmation for candle confirmation. Reject if the configured invalidation source (close or high) is above the validation-zone upper edge, if open <= Target 1, or, except for high/low touch mode, if close <= Target 1.
Exit: long, configured stop source (close or low) <= stop price OR high >= Target 2 OR Target 1 reached when configured to finish at Target 1; a stop takes precedence over a target on the same bar.
Exit: short, configured stop source (close or high) >= stop price OR low <= Target 2 OR Target 1 reached when configured to finish at Target 1; a stop takes precedence over a target on the same bar.
