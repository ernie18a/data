Calculation: FastCoralChange = Coral Trend(source, Coral smoothing length, fast Coral constant D) - Coral Trend(source, Coral smoothing length, fast Coral constant D)[1]
Calculation: SlowCoralChange = Coral Trend(source, Coral smoothing length, slow Coral constant D) - Coral Trend(source, Coral smoothing length, slow Coral constant D)[1]
Calculation: MacdValue = EMA(source, shorter MACD length) - EMA(source, longer MACD length)
Calculation: MacdAverage = SMA(MacdValue, MACD signal length)
Calculation: PivotHighPct = EMA(100 * close / (last confirmed pivot high of high with pivot left length and pivot right length), pivot smoothing length)
Calculation: PivotLowPct = EMA(100 * (last confirmed pivot low of low with pivot left length and pivot right length) / close, pivot smoothing length)
Calculation: OBLowValue = high if (high - low) >= OB volatility multiple * ATR(OB volatility ATR length), else low
Calculation: OBHighValue = low if (high - low) >= OB volatility multiple * ATR(OB volatility ATR length), else high
Calculation: BullOBZone = zone (low, high) formed on each bar where close crosses above the last confirmed pivot high of high with swing pivot length or with internal pivot length, counting only the first crossing of each pivot; low = lowest OBLowValue over the bars from that pivot's bar through this bar, high = high of the bar holding that low; if the pivot is max pivot age or more bars back, low = OBLowValue and high = OBHighValue of this bar
Calculation: BearOBZone = zone (low, high) formed on each bar where close crosses below the last confirmed pivot low of low with swing pivot length or with internal pivot length, counting only the first crossing of each pivot; high = highest OBHighValue over the bars from that pivot's bar through this bar, low = low of the bar holding that high; if the pivot is max pivot age or more bars back, low = OBLowValue and high = OBHighValue of this bar
Calculation: BullOBInRange = some stored BullOBZone has low <= close <= high + ATR(ATR length) * OB proximity multiplier, where stored BullOBZones are the newest OB storage limit zones not yet removed, and a zone is removed on any bar where close < its low
Calculation: BearOBInRange = some stored BearOBZone has low - ATR(ATR length) * OB proximity multiplier <= close <= high, where stored BearOBZones are the newest OB storage limit zones not yet removed, and a zone is removed on any bar where close > its high
Calculation: BullConfluence = IF(FastCoralChange > 0, wCoral, 0) + IF(FastCoralChange > 0 AND FastCoralChange[1] <= 0, wCoralFlip, 0) + IF(SlowCoralChange > 0, wSlowCoral, 0) + IF(PivotHighPct > PivotLowPct, wPivot, 0) + IF(PivotHighPct > strong bull threshold, wPivotStrong, 0) + IF(HTF pivot enabled AND PivotHighPct(T1, last completed bar) > PivotLowPct(T1, last completed bar), wHtfPivot, 0) + IF(MacdValue > MacdAverage, wMacd, 0) + IF(MacdValue crosses above MacdAverage, wMacdCross, 0) + IF(BullOBInRange, wOB, 0) + IF(VWAP enabled AND close > VWAP(close), wVwap, 0) + IF(VWAP enabled AND close > VWAP(close) AND Rising(VWAP(close), VWAP rising length), wVwapMomentum, 0)
Calculation: BullMaintenance = BullConfluence - IF(FastCoralChange > 0 AND FastCoralChange[1] <= 0, wCoralFlip, 0) - IF(MacdValue crosses above MacdAverage, wMacdCross, 0)
Calculation: BearConfluence = IF(FastCoralChange < 0, wCoral, 0) + IF(FastCoralChange < 0 AND FastCoralChange[1] >= 0, wCoralFlip, 0) + IF(SlowCoralChange < 0, wSlowCoral, 0) + IF(PivotLowPct > PivotHighPct, wPivot, 0) + IF(PivotLowPct > strong bear threshold, wPivotStrong, 0) + IF(HTF pivot enabled AND PivotLowPct(T1, last completed bar) > PivotHighPct(T1, last completed bar), wHtfPivot, 0) + IF(MacdValue < MacdAverage, wMacd, 0) + IF(MacdValue crosses below MacdAverage, wMacdCross, 0) + IF(BearOBInRange, wOB, 0) + IF(VWAP enabled AND close < VWAP(close), wVwap, 0) + IF(VWAP enabled AND close < VWAP(close) AND Falling(VWAP(close), VWAP rising length), wVwapMomentum, 0)
Calculation: BearMaintenance = BearConfluence - IF(FastCoralChange < 0 AND FastCoralChange[1] >= 0, wCoralFlip, 0) - IF(MacdValue crosses below MacdAverage, wMacdCross, 0)
Calculation: EntryATR = ATR(ATR length) evaluated on the entry bar
Calculation: LongInitialStop = IF structure SL enabled THEN MIN(MAX(entry price - SL multiple * EntryATR, lowest low over the entry bar and prior swing lookback length bars - structure buffer * EntryATR, low of newest stored BullOBZone - structure buffer * EntryATR if one is stored), entry price - min stop distance * EntryATR) ELSE entry price - SL multiple * EntryATR
Calculation: ShortInitialStop = IF structure SL enabled THEN MAX(MIN(entry price + SL multiple * EntryATR, highest high over the entry bar and prior swing lookback length bars + structure buffer * EntryATR, high of newest stored BearOBZone + structure buffer * EntryATR if one is stored), entry price + min stop distance * EntryATR) ELSE entry price + SL multiple * EntryATR
Calculation: LongRisk = entry price - LongInitialStop
Calculation: ShortRisk = ShortInitialStop - entry price
Calculation: HighestHighSinceEntry = highest high over the bars after the entry bar through the current bar
Calculation: LowestLowSinceEntry = lowest low over the bars after the entry bar through the current bar
Calculation: LongStopLevel = IF trailing stop enabled AND HighestHighSinceEntry[1] >= entry price + TP3 multiple * LongRisk THEN entry price + TP2 multiple * LongRisk ELSE IF trailing stop enabled AND HighestHighSinceEntry[1] >= entry price + TP2 multiple * LongRisk THEN entry price + TP1 multiple * LongRisk ELSE IF trailing stop enabled AND HighestHighSinceEntry[1] >= entry price + TP1 multiple * LongRisk THEN entry price ELSE LongInitialStop
Calculation: ShortStopLevel = IF trailing stop enabled AND LowestLowSinceEntry[1] <= entry price - TP3 multiple * ShortRisk THEN entry price - TP2 multiple * ShortRisk ELSE IF trailing stop enabled AND LowestLowSinceEntry[1] <= entry price - TP2 multiple * ShortRisk THEN entry price - TP1 multiple * ShortRisk ELSE IF trailing stop enabled AND LowestLowSinceEntry[1] <= entry price - TP1 multiple * ShortRisk THEN entry price ELSE ShortInitialStop
Calculation: LongDecayThreshold = IF decay threshold mode is absolute THEN decay absolute threshold ELSE decay percent threshold / 100 * BullMaintenance on the entry bar
Calculation: ShortDecayThreshold = IF decay threshold mode is absolute THEN decay absolute threshold ELSE decay percent threshold / 100 * BearMaintenance on the entry bar
Calculation: LongTPGate = decay gate is none OR (decay gate is TP1 AND HighestHighSinceEntry >= entry price + TP1 multiple * LongRisk) OR (decay gate is TP2 AND HighestHighSinceEntry >= entry price + TP2 multiple * LongRisk)
Calculation: ShortTPGate = decay gate is none OR (decay gate is TP1 AND LowestLowSinceEntry <= entry price - TP1 multiple * ShortRisk) OR (decay gate is TP2 AND LowestLowSinceEntry <= entry price - TP2 multiple * ShortRisk)
Long: FastCoralChange > 0 AND (FastCoralChange[1] <= 0 OR NOT Coral flip required) AND PivotHighPct > PivotLowPct AND MacdValue > MacdAverage AND (NOT OB required OR BullOBInRange) AND (NOT slow Coral required OR SlowCoralChange > 0) AND (NOT (HTF pivot enabled AND HTF pivot required) OR PivotHighPct(T1, last completed bar) > PivotLowPct(T1, last completed bar)) AND (NOT (VWAP enabled AND VWAP required) OR close > VWAP(close)) AND BullConfluence >= min score AND position side is not long
Short: FastCoralChange < 0 AND (FastCoralChange[1] >= 0 OR NOT Coral flip required) AND PivotLowPct > PivotHighPct AND MacdValue < MacdAverage AND (NOT OB required OR BearOBInRange) AND (NOT slow Coral required OR SlowCoralChange < 0) AND (NOT (HTF pivot enabled AND HTF pivot required) OR PivotLowPct(T1, last completed bar) > PivotHighPct(T1, last completed bar)) AND (NOT (VWAP enabled AND VWAP required) OR close < VWAP(close)) AND BearConfluence >= min score AND position side is not short
Exit: long, low <= LongStopLevel
Exit: long, single TP exit mode AND trailing stop disabled AND high >= entry price + single TP target multiple * LongRisk
Exit: long, partial exit mode AND trailing stop disabled AND partial exit percentages sum to at least 100 AND high >= entry price + TP3 multiple * LongRisk
Exit: long, decay exit enabled AND for all k from 0 to decay confirmation bars minus 1: bars since entry > k AND LongTPGate[k] AND BullMaintenance[k] < LongDecayThreshold
Exit: short, high >= ShortStopLevel
Exit: short, single TP exit mode AND trailing stop disabled AND low <= entry price - single TP target multiple * ShortRisk
Exit: short, partial exit mode AND trailing stop disabled AND partial exit percentages sum to at least 100 AND low <= entry price - TP3 multiple * ShortRisk
Exit: short, decay exit enabled AND for all k from 0 to decay confirmation bars minus 1: bars since entry > k AND ShortTPGate[k] AND BearMaintenance[k] < ShortDecayThreshold
