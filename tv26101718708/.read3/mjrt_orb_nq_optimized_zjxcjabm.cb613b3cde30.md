Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum high of chart bars in the capture window for the current day
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars in the capture window for the current day
Long: The first post-capture-window breach is above the opening-range high (the high breach takes precedence if both boundaries are breached on the same bar), and a bar since that breach has low at or below the opening-range high and closes outside the opening range, and that retest remains set until a signal or daily reset, and during the display window close > open and (close < opening-range low or close > opening-range high)
Short: The first post-capture-window breach is below the opening-range low, and a bar since that breach has high at or above the opening-range low and closes outside the opening range, and that retest remains set until a signal or daily reset, and during the display window close < open and (close < opening-range low or close > opening-range high)
