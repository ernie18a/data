Calculation: Unit-bounded value(x) = clamp x to the unit interval
Calculation: Reversal score(side, entry) = score scale × clamp((trend weight × clamp((directional EMA gap / score ATR + trend offset) / trend divisor) + RSI weight × clamp(abs(RSI − score midpoint) / score denominator × RSI conviction multiplier) + volatility weight × clamp(1 − PercentRank(ATR, rank lookback) / rank normalization + proximity weight × clamp(1 − abs(entry − fast EMA) / score ATR / proximity scale) + momentum weight × clamp((directional change of fast EMA over slope lookback / score ATR + momentum offset) / momentum divisor)) / sum of the five weights); score is zero when the weight sum is zero; score ATR is ATR or the instrument minimum price increment when ATR is nonpositive
Calculation: Star tier(score) = four stars at or above the four-star cutoff, three at or above the three-star cutoff, two at or above the two-star cutoff, one at or above the one-star cutoff, otherwise zero
Calculation: Risk distance(entry, side) = clamp(abs(entry − raw stop), ATR × minimum risk multiple, ATR × maximum risk multiple); raw stop is entry − ATR × stop multiple in long ATR-stop mode, entry + ATR × stop multiple in short ATR-stop mode, lowest low over the swing lookback − ATR × swing buffer in long swing-stop mode, or highest high over the swing lookback + ATR × swing buffer in short swing-stop mode; calculate at entry and hold fixed
Calculation: Stop(entry, side) = entry − side × risk distance(entry, side), with long side positive and short side negative
Calculation: Target(entry, side, R multiple) = entry + side × risk distance(entry, side) × R multiple
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, RSI crosses above the selected RSI trigger level (the RSI midline in midline mode or the oversold level in exit-oversold mode), the trend filter is disabled or the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, Star tier(Reversal score(long, close)) ≥ minimum star tier, ATR is available, no trade is active, no trade exit occurred on this bar, and bars since the last accepted signal ≥ cooldown bars
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, RSI crosses below the selected RSI trigger level (the RSI midline in midline mode or the overbought level in exit-overbought mode), the trend filter is disabled or the fast EMA is below the slow EMA, Star tier(Reversal score(short, close)) ≥ minimum star tier, ATR is available, no trade is active, no trade exit occurred on this bar, and bars since the last accepted signal ≥ cooldown bars
Exit: long, low ≤ Stop(entry, long) OR high ≥ Target(entry, long, TP3 R multiple) OR bars since entry ≥ time-stop bars
Exit: short, high ≥ Stop(entry, short) OR low ≤ Target(entry, short, TP3 R multiple) OR bars since entry ≥ time-stop bars
