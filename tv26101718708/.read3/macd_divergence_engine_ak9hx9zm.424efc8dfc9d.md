Calculation: normalized MACD = (MA(source, shorter lookback, oscillator MA type) - MA(source, longer lookback, oscillator MA type)) / scale, where scale is 1, ATR(normalization lookback), stdev(raw MACD, normalization lookback), or source/100 according to the normalization setting; replace a nonpositive or unavailable scale with 1
Calculation: higher-timeframe momentum bias = +1 when MACD > its EMA signal and MACD > 0, -1 when MACD < its EMA signal and MACD < 0, otherwise 0; MACD = EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) - EMA(close, longer lookback, T1), with the signal EMA using the signal lookback; calculate on T1 bars and sample onto chart bars with lookahead off and gaps filled, using the still-forming T1 bar on realtime chart bars; T1 is the configured higher timeframe, manual or automatically selected from the chart timeframe
Calculation: zero-line gate and score(direction, class, oscillator segment, endpoint values, mode) = pure when the whole segment is strictly below zero for bullish regular or bearish hidden candidates, or strictly above zero for bullish hidden or bearish regular candidates; ends when both endpoints are on that required side; eligible in unrestricted mode, when ends in endpoint-only mode, or when pure in full-segment mode; score is 1 when pure, otherwise 0.5 when ends, otherwise 0
Calculation: candidate score(direction, hidden, c, p, Oc, Op, Pc, Pp, zeroScore) = 100 * clip(sum(weight * factor) / sum(weights)), or 0 when the weight sum is nonpositive; clip(x) = min(max(x, 0), 1); c and p are current and prior pivot offsets in bars from confirmation, with p > c; weights are separate parameters for magnitude, extremity, spacing, regime fit, extension, volume, higher-timeframe bias, momentum shift, zero-line integrity, and structure; magnitude = sqrt(clip((abs(Pc-Pp)/max(ATR(ATR-score lookback)[c], tick size))/2) * clip(abs(Oc-Op)/max(stdev(normalized MACD, twice normalization lookback), positive epsilon)); extremity = clip(abs(Oc)/(max(stdev(normalized MACD, twice normalization lookback), positive epsilon)*1.5)), then use clip(1-extremity/1.2) for hidden candidates and extremity otherwise; spacing = clip(1-abs((p-c)-max((minimum pivot gap+maximum pivot gap)/2,1))/max((minimum pivot gap+maximum pivot gap)/2,1)); trend power on the confirmation chart bar = clip(0.5*Efficiency Ratio(close, ER lookback)+0.5*clip(ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback)/45)); up trend means DI+ > DI- and close > EMA(close, structural trend lookback), down trend means DI+ <= DI- and close <= that EMA; for regime fit, with-trend means up for bullish or down for bearish, against-trend means down for bullish or up for bearish; hidden fit is clip(1.2*trend power) with trend, 0.05 against trend, or 0.4*trend power otherwise; regular fit is 0.85 with trend, 0.85*clip(1-trend power) against trend, or 0.60 otherwise; pivot extension = (close[c]-EMA(close, structural trend lookback)[c])/ATR(ATR-score lookback)[c], signed as -extension for bullish and extension for bearish; extension factor is clip((1-signed extension)/3) for hidden candidates and clip(signed extension/3) otherwise; volume ratio = SMA(volume, volume lookback)[c]/SMA(volume, volume lookback)[p]; volume factor is clip((ratio-0.6)/0.8) for hidden candidates or clip((1.4-ratio)/0.8) otherwise when both averages are positive, and 0.5 otherwise; higher-timeframe factor is 0.5 when disabled, otherwise 1 when the bias agrees with direction, 0.5 when bias is 0, or 0.1 when it opposes direction; on the confirmation chart bar, histogram = normalized MACD - MA(normalized MACD, signal lookback, signal MA type); momentum factor is 0.6 when the histogram turns up (bullish: current > prior and prior <= two bars ago) or down (bearish: current < prior and prior >= two bars ago), plus 0.4 when normalized MACD is above its signal for bullish or below for bearish; zero-line factor is zeroScore; pivot structure = 0.6*RSI factor + 0.4*clip(rejection wick fraction/0.5), where RSI factor is clip((45-RSI(close, RSI lookback)[c])/20) for bullish or clip((RSI(close, RSI lookback)[c]-55)/20) for bearish, rejection wick fraction is (min(open[c],close[c])-low[c])/max(high[c]-low[c],tick size) for bullish or (high[c]-max(open[c],close[c]))/max(high[c]-low[c],tick size) for bearish
Long: a confirmed normalized-MACD pivot low, after the configured left and right pivot bars, passes the configured bar-close gate; among its prior confirmed pivot lows within scan depth, at least one eligible pairing has gap >= max(minimum pivot gap, left pivot bars + right pivot bars) and <= maximum pivot gap, and the maximum candidate score is >= minimum score; eligible pairings are either (regular divergences enabled AND current price low < prior price low AND current oscillator low > prior oscillator low AND zero-line gate passes for bullish regular) OR (hidden divergences enabled AND current price low > prior price low AND current oscillator low < prior oscillator low AND zero-line gate passes for bullish hidden)
Short: a confirmed normalized-MACD pivot high, after the configured left and right pivot bars, passes the configured bar-close gate; among its prior confirmed pivot highs within scan depth, at least one eligible pairing has gap >= max(minimum pivot gap, left pivot bars + right pivot bars) and <= maximum pivot gap, and the maximum candidate score is >= minimum score; eligible pairings are either (regular divergences enabled AND current price high > prior price high AND current oscillator high < prior oscillator high AND zero-line gate passes for bearish regular) OR (hidden divergences enabled AND current price high < prior price high AND current oscillator high > prior oscillator high AND zero-line gate passes for bearish hidden)
