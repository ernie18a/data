Calculation: LastSwingHigh = most recent non-NA PivotHigh(high, pivotBars, pivotBars)
Calculation: LastSwingLow = most recent non-NA PivotLow(low, pivotBars, pivotBars)
Calculation: UpBreak = close > LastSwingHigh AND close[1] <= LastSwingHigh
Calculation: DownBreak = close < LastSwingLow AND close[1] >= LastSwingLow
Calculation: MarketStructure = -1 if DownBreak, 1 if UpBreak, else MarketStructure[1]
Calculation: Equilibrium = (H + L) / 2, where H = LastSwingHigh if not na, else Highest(high, rangeFallbackBars), and L = LastSwingLow if not na, else Lowest(low, rangeFallbackBars)
Calculation: InBullOB = low is between OB low and OB high, each extended by obProximity * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod), for some bull OB not removed through this bar; a bull OB forms on a bar with UpBreak AND close - open > dispMult * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod), taking as its candle the nearest of the obSearchBars prior bars with close < open, with OB high and OB low = that candle's high and low; it is removed on a bar where its candle is more than obLifetime bars old or close < OB low
Calculation: InBearOB = high is between OB low and OB high, each extended by obProximity * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod), for some bear OB not removed through this bar; a bear OB forms on a bar with DownBreak AND open - close > dispMult * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod), taking as its candle the nearest of the obSearchBars prior bars with close > open, with OB high and OB low = that candle's high and low; it is removed on a bar where its candle is more than obLifetime bars old or close > OB high
Calculation: InBullFVG = low is between FVG bottom and FVG top for some bull FVG not removed through this bar; a bull FVG forms on a bar with low - high[2] > fvgMinGap * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod), with bottom = high[2] and top = low; it is removed on a bar where it is more than fvgLifetime bars after formation or low <= bottom
Calculation: InBearFVG = high is between FVG bottom and FVG top for some bear FVG not removed through this bar; a bear FVG forms on a bar with low[2] - high > fvgMinGap * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod), with bottom = high and top = low[2]; it is removed on a bar where it is more than fvgLifetime bars after formation or high >= bottom
Calculation: LongConfluence = number of true among (MarketStructure = 1, InBullOB, InBullFVG, close < Equilibrium, close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, htfEmaPeriod, T1, last completed bar))
Calculation: ShortConfluence = number of true among (MarketStructure = -1, InBearOB, InBearFVG, close > Equilibrium, close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, htfEmaPeriod, T1, last completed bar))
Long: LongConfluence >= confluenceMin AND close - open > bodyMult * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod) AND close > close[1] AND ADX(high, low, close, adxPeriod) >= adxThreshold AND position side != long
Short: ShortConfluence >= confluenceMin AND open - close > bodyMult * ATR(high, low, close, atrPeriod) AND close < close[1] AND ADX(high, low, close, adxPeriod) >= adxThreshold AND position side != short
