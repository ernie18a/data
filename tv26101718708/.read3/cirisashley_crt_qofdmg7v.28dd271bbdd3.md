Calculation: Displacement = (high - low) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR-size multiplier AND (if high > low then 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low) else 0) >= minimum-body percentage
Calculation: Inside T1 range = on chart bars, for every i from 1 through the inside-bar lookback, close[i] >= low(T1, last completed bar) AND close[i] <= high(T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: Killzone = UTC hour >= London-open parameter AND UTC hour < London-close parameter OR UTC hour >= NY-open parameter AND UTC hour < NY-close parameter
Calculation: Asia session = UTC hour >= Asia-open parameter OR UTC hour < Asia-close parameter
Calculation: Asia range high = maximum chart-bar high during each Asia session, frozen at the first chart bar outside the session
Calculation: Asia range low = minimum chart-bar low during each Asia session, frozen at the first chart bar outside the session
Calculation: Opening-range window = on chart bars, UTC minute-of-day minus opening-hour parameter * 60 >= 0 AND UTC minute-of-day minus opening-hour parameter * 60 < opening-range duration
Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum chart-bar high during the opening-range window, frozen at the first chart bar outside the window
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum chart-bar low during the opening-range window, frozen at the first chart bar outside the window
