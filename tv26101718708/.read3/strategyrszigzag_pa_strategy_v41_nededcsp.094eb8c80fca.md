Calculation: ZigZag pivot = use regular or Heikin-Ashi OHLC according to the candle-source parameter; calculate on chart bars unless the alternate-timeframe parameter selects T1, in which case calculate on T1 bars using the bar still forming and sample the security value on chart bars when time(T1) changes. An up candle has close >= open; a down candle has close <= open. Direction becomes down after an up candle followed by a down candle, becomes up after a down candle followed by an up candle, and otherwise retains its prior value (initially neutral). The pivot is highest(high, pivot-lookback parameter) after an up-to-down transition when prior direction is not down, lowest(low, pivot-lookback parameter) after a down-to-up transition when prior direction is not up, and NA otherwise.
Calculation: X, A, B, C, D = the fifth, fourth, third, second, and most recent non-NA ZigZag pivot values, respectively.
Calculation: XAB = abs(B - A) / abs(X - A)
Calculation: XAD = abs(A - D) / abs(X - A)
Calculation: ABC = abs(B - C) / abs(A - B)
Calculation: BCD = abs(C - D) / abs(B - C)

Calculation: FibLevel(rate) = D - abs(D - C) * rate when D > C; otherwise D + abs(D - C) * rate. Target 1 and Target 2 each have separate entry-rate, take-profit-rate, and stop-loss-rate parameters.
