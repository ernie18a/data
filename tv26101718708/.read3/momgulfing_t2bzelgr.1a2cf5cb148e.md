Calculation: Bullish pattern low = minimum low among all bars in the matching bullish pattern
Calculation: Bearish pattern high = maximum high among all bars in the matching bearish pattern
Calculation: Long risk = close - bullish pattern low
Calculation: Short risk = bearish pattern high - close
Calculation: On each long signal, long stop = bullish pattern low if position is flat or short; otherwise bullish pattern low + stop-increase parameter × long risk
Calculation: On each long signal, long target = close + reward/risk parameter × long risk if position is flat or short; otherwise close + (reward/risk parameter - target-decrease parameter) × long risk
Calculation: On each short signal, short stop = bearish pattern high if position is flat or long; otherwise bearish pattern high - stop-increase parameter × short risk
Calculation: On each short signal, short target = close - reward/risk parameter × short risk if position is flat or long; otherwise close - (reward/risk parameter - target-decrease parameter) × short risk
