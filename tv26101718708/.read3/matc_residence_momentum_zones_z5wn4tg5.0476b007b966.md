Calculation: Score percentage(x) = clamp(x * score scale + neutral percentage, minimum percentage, maximum percentage).
Calculation: Trend percentage = Score percentage of the first matching score: close above EMA(close, long trend lookback) and EMA(close, short trend lookback) > EMA(close, medium trend lookback) > EMA(close, long-term intermediate lookback): strong bullish trend score; close below the long-lookback EMA and the three EMAs ordered oppositely: strong bearish trend score; otherwise close above or below the intermediate-lookback EMA with short EMA above or below medium EMA: medium directional trend score; otherwise short EMA above or below medium EMA: weak directional trend score; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: GAE state on timeframe T = a state initialized bullish when close >= linear regression(hl2, GAE regression lookback) and bearish otherwise; use ATR(GAE ATR lookback) bands around that regression, with the bullish band below and bearish band above by GAE band multiplier. On a confirmed T bar, switch bearish to bullish when close > prior rail, or bullish to bearish when close < prior rail; otherwise trail the bullish rail upward with max(prior rail, bullish band) or the bearish rail downward with min(prior rail, bearish band).
Calculation: GAE percentage = the fraction of bullish GAE states across the four distinct timeframes T1, T2, T3, and T4, multiplied by maximum percentage. Evaluate each state on chart bars from the last completed bar of its timeframe.
Calculation: Price-volume momentum percentage = the weighted sum of EMA(correlation(change(close), change(volume), price-volume correlation lookback) transformed to a percentage, EMA(normalize(ATR(ATR lookback) / EMA(ATR(ATR lookback), volatility baseline lookback) * (volume / EMA(volume, volume baseline lookback)), impulse normalization lookback), impulse smoothing lookback), and EMA(normalize(ROC(close, direction lookback), direction normalization lookback), direction smoothing lookback); use separate parameters for the three component weights.
Calculation: Volume-efficiency percentage = the bullish score when EMA(percentrank(max(close - close[1], 0) / max(volume when close > open, minimum volume), efficiency rank lookback), efficiency smoothing lookback) exceeds its bearish counterpart and EMA(green volume, green volume lookback) is nonincreasing while EMA(red volume, red volume lookback) is nondecreasing; the bearish score uses the reverse conditions; otherwise derive the percentage from the difference between the two efficiency values, scaled and clamped.
Calculation: Ichimoku percentage = Score percentage of a score formed from close above the cloud top or below the cloud bottom, conversion line above or below base line, leading span A above or below leading span B, and close above or below close[Chikou displacement]; conversion, base, and leading span B use separate lookbacks.
Calculation: RSI-structure percentage = Score percentage of a score that uses the latest confirmed RSI pivot high or low as resistance or support: RSI above resistance with smoothed RSI acceleration positive gives the bullish break score; RSI below support with acceleration negative gives the bearish break score; otherwise use (RSI(close, RSI lookback) - neutral RSI) / RSI scale + smoothed RSI acceleration * acceleration weight. Smooth acceleration with EMA over its separate lookback.
Calculation: HAP percentage = a release score when RSI(close, HAP RSI lookback) exits its SMA(HAP band lookback) plus or minus standard deviation bands and close is above or below a price band formed from SMA(close, HAP price lookback), its standard deviation, and the RSI band displacement; otherwise use the above-band score, below-band score, squeeze score, or neutral score, in that order. A squeeze is RSI band width below its SMA over the HAP band lookback; release is a squeeze on the prior bar and no squeeze now.
Calculation: Regression-flow percentage = a clamped directional score from the EMA-smoothed projection of linreg(hl2, regression-flow lookback, offsets 0, 1, and 2), its slope, and its curve. Direction is whether that smoothed projected value rises or falls. Flow power is the smoothed weighted sum of normalized absolute slope, normalized absolute curve, clamped absolute close-to-core distance divided by max(EMA(abs(close - core), regression-flow lookback), ATR(flow ATR lookback)), and normalized ATR; compare it with its EMA over the flow-average lookback.
Calculation: Market-structure percentage = Score percentage of a score using the latest two alternating confirmed pivot highs and lows: latest high above or at/below prior high and latest low above or at/below prior low; add a bullish score when close exceeds the latest pivot high or a bearish score when close is below the latest pivot low.
Calculation: OTE-structure percentage = bullish or bearish OTE score when the latest four alternating confirmed pivots form, respectively, a rising low-high impulse or falling high-low impulse; otherwise neutral percentage.
Calculation: Power-zone bullish and bearish scores = pivot-fibonacci contribution when close is inside the zone between the configured lower and upper retracements of the highest high and lowest low over the power-zone swing lookback and on the bullish or bearish side of the latest support or resistance pivot; plus the applicable pin-bar or engulfing-candle score; plus capped relative-volume contribution when volume / SMA(volume, power-zone volume lookback) exceeds its threshold and the candle direction agrees; plus support or resistance strength contribution after its test count reaches the configured minimum. A pin bar compares wick lengths with body size and requires candle range above an ATR fraction. An engulfing candle compares current and prior candle direction, body boundaries, and body sizes. Support and resistance test counts increment when high or low is within the configured fractional tolerance of the latest pivot, excluding the pivot bar.
Calculation: Liquidity percentage = Score percentage of the bullish rejection score for a low below the prior liquidity-lookback low followed by close above it and a close-to-low distance above ATR(liquidity ATR lookback) times the rejection multiplier; the bearish rejection reverses those comparisons. Without a rejection, use the bullish score when close exceeds the prior lookback high, the bearish score when close is below the prior lookback low, and otherwise neutral.
Calculation: CVD percentage = Score percentage of the clamped difference between the mean of three normalized series: EMA(merged-volume delta, CVD smoothing lookback), EMA(cumulative merged-volume delta, CVD trend lookback), and EMA(cumulative merged-volume OBV step, CVD trend lookback). Merged volume sums volume over the merge lookback; delta is positive or negative merged volume according to merged close versus merged open; OBV step uses merged close versus prior merged close.
Calculation: Harmonic percentage = bullish or bearish harmonic score from the latest five alternating structure pivots when the last four legs satisfy the corresponding ABCD ratio, Gartley-like, Bat-like, or Butterfly-like ratio ranges, using the configured harmonic tolerance; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Volatility percentage = Score percentage of a directional score: when ATR(ATR lookback) / EMA(ATR(ATR lookback), volatility regime lookback) exceeds the expansion threshold, use the bullish or bearish expansion score according to close versus EMA(close, medium trend lookback); when below the contraction threshold use neutral; otherwise use the directional trend score.
Calculation: Fibonacci-pullback percentage = Score percentage of a directional pullback score from the latest confirmed pivot impulse. For an upward impulse use (impulse high - close) / impulse range; for a downward impulse use (close - impulse low) / impulse range. Within the configured healthy retracement interval, assign the configured score for the applicable retracement subinterval when a reaction agrees with the impulse (candle direction, close versus prior close, or smoothed RSI acceleration); beyond the maximum retracement use the configured invalidation score with direction reversed; otherwise use neutral. This calculation is neutral when Fibonacci pullback use is disabled.
Calculation: Core percentage = weighted mean of trend, GAE, price-volume momentum, volume-efficiency, and Ichimoku percentages using separate component weights.
Calculation: Structure-group percentage = weighted mean of RSI-structure, HAP, regression-flow, market-structure, and OTE-structure percentages using separate component weights.
Calculation: Power-group percentage = weighted mean of power-zone, liquidity, CVD, harmonic, and volatility percentages using separate component weights.
Calculation: Final percentage = the weighted mean of core, structure-group, and power-group percentages using their respective weight sums, with Fibonacci-pullback percentage and its separate weight included only when enabled; clamp to the percentage range. Final edge = final percentage - EMA(final percentage, final-average lookback).
Calculation: Bull agreement = count of bullish votes for trend percentage at or above its bullish threshold, at least the configured number of bullish GAE states, and each of price-volume momentum, volume-efficiency, Ichimoku, RSI-structure, HAP, regression-flow, market-structure, OTE-structure, power-zone, liquidity, CVD, harmonic, and volatility percentages at or above their bullish thresholds, plus a Fibonacci bonus vote when enabled and its bullish reaction score meets the bonus requirement. Bear agreement uses the corresponding bearish thresholds, bearish GAE states, and bearish Fibonacci bonus.
Calculation: Net-force root = signed square root of the absolute sum of each component percentage minus the neutral percentage, including all component percentages and Fibonacci percentage. Force basis = EMA(close, force basis lookback); force distance = abs(net-force root) * ATR(force ATR lookback) * force ATR multiplier. Bull force line = force basis - force distance * force cloud multiplier; bear force line = force basis + force distance * force cloud multiplier. Force bullish condition = net-force root >= force signal threshold and close > (bull force line when above the threshold, otherwise force basis). Force bearish condition = net-force root <= negative force signal threshold and close < (bear force line when below the negative threshold, otherwise force basis).
Long: bar is confirmed AND final percentage >= bullish threshold AND bull agreement >= prime agreement requirement AND bull agreement > bear agreement AND (edge filter disabled OR final edge > minimum edge) AND (net-force filter disabled OR force bullish condition) AND more than the configured signal cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior long signal, or no prior long signal exists, AND no long signal is currently active.
Short: bar is confirmed AND final percentage <= bearish threshold AND bear agreement >= prime agreement requirement AND bear agreement > bull agreement AND (edge filter disabled OR final edge < negative minimum edge) AND (net-force filter disabled OR force bearish condition) AND more than the configured signal cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior short signal, or no prior short signal exists, AND no short signal is currently active.
Exit: long, a long signal is active AND final percentage < neutral percentage.
Exit: short, a short signal is active AND final percentage > neutral percentage.
Long: bullish power-zone score >= power-zone signal threshold AND prior bar's bullish power-zone score was below that threshold AND more than the configured power-signal cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior bullish power-zone event, or no prior event exists.
Short: bearish power-zone score >= power-zone signal threshold AND prior bar's bearish power-zone score was below that threshold AND more than the configured power-signal cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior bearish power-zone event, or no prior event exists.
Long: bar is confirmed AND force bullish condition.
Short: bar is confirmed AND force bearish condition.
