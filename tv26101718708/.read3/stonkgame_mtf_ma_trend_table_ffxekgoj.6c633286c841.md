Calculation: AdaptiveZ(x) = (x - SMA(x, N)) / population standard deviation(x, N), where N is the smaller of the maximum sample and the number of valid observations; unavailable when N is below the minimum sample.
Calculation: State(T1) = on T1 bars, let M be the selected moving average of close over the selected horizon’s MA lookback (the shared method is EMA, SMA, WMA, or VWMA, with EMA fallback if VWMA is unavailable); let n be the larger of the minimum slope interval and the rounded product of the MA lookback and slope-window fraction; D = AdaptiveZ(log(close / M)); S = AdaptiveZ(log(M / M[n]) / n); E = clip(abs(M - M[n]) / (SMA(abs(change(M)), n) * n), 0, 1); multiply S by efficiency floor + E * (1 - efficiency floor) when efficiency adjustment is enabled, otherwise by 1 (use multiplier 1 if E is unavailable); clip D and adjusted S to ± the state Z-score limit, then take their normalized weighted sum, using equal weights if both weights are zero; apply EMA with the state-smoothing length when smoothing is enabled, otherwise use the raw weighted sum; unavailable unless D and S are available. Chart bars use the selected source timeframe’s last completed or still-forming value, according to the completed-source-bars setting.
Long: On confirmed chart bars, State(T1) crosses above the direction level while its bullish direction arm is set, or crosses above the negative recovery level while its bullish recovery arm is set. The direction arm is initially unset, is set when State(T1) reaches or falls below the negative of the smaller of the direction-reset cap and the direction level times the direction-reset fraction, and resets after its event. The recovery arm is initially unset, is set when State(T1) reaches or falls below the negative recovery level times the recovery-arm factor, and resets after its event.
Short: On confirmed chart bars, State(T1) crosses below the negative direction level while its bearish direction arm is set, or crosses below the recovery level while its bearish recovery arm is set. The direction arm is initially unset, is set when State(T1) reaches or exceeds the direction-reset cap or the direction level times the direction-reset fraction, whichever is smaller, and resets after its event. The recovery arm is initially unset, is set when State(T1) reaches or exceeds the recovery level times the recovery-arm factor, and resets after its event.
