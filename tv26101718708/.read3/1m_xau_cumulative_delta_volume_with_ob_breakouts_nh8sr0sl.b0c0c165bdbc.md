Calculation: Profile range = highest high and lowest low on chart bars during the configured profile session and timezone; available after the session ends, reset at the next session start, and held until then.
Calculation: Support bounds = profile low ± ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) × zone thickness parameter; resistance bounds = profile high ± the same ATR value × zone thickness parameter.
Calculation: Intrabar delta = on each chart bar, sum signed volume from completed T1 bars within that chart bar; sign is positive when close > open, negative when close < open, otherwise the sign of close minus prior T1 close, otherwise the prior T1 bar's nonzero sign, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Recent delta = sum of intrabar delta over the recent-delta lookback of chart bars.

Long: Profile range ready AND close > resistance upper bound AND ((close - resistance upper bound) / resistance upper bound) × 100 >= breakout-move parameter.
Short: Profile range ready AND close < support lower bound AND ((support lower bound - close) / support lower bound) × 100 >= breakout-move parameter.
Long: After the latest upward breakout in the current profile session and before a downward breakout or the next profile-session start, a later chart bar has high >= resistance upper bound AND low <= resistance upper bound AND close > resistance upper bound.
Short: After the latest downward breakout in the current profile session and before an upward breakout or the next profile-session start, a later chart bar has high >= support lower bound AND low <= support lower bound AND close < support lower bound.
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss parameter / 100) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit parameter / 100).
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss parameter / 100) OR low <= entry price × (1 - take-profit parameter / 100).
