Calculation: envelope distance = Highest(ATR(trend length), trend length × envelope lookback multiple) × envelope multiplier

Calculation: lower envelope = adjusted centerline - envelope distance
Calculation: directional state = initialize neutral; change to bullish when close crosses above the upper envelope; change to bearish when close crosses below the lower envelope; otherwise retain the prior state
Calculation: normalized deviation = (close - adjusted centerline) / envelope distance, or zero when envelope distance is zero
Calculation: smoothed oscillator = EMA(normalized deviation, oscillator smoothing length)
Calculation: oscillator signal = EMA(smoothed oscillator, signal smoothing length)
Calculation: momentum histogram = smoothed oscillator - oscillator signal
Calculation: bullish momentum confirmation = smoothed oscillator > oscillator signal AND momentum histogram > its prior value
Calculation: bearish momentum confirmation = smoothed oscillator < oscillator signal AND momentum histogram < its prior value

Calculation: mature bearish momentum = smoothed oscillator < -minimum momentum AND bearish momentum confirmation

Calculation: bearish trend expansion = momentum histogram falling AND smoothed oscillator < oscillator signal AND close < adjusted centerline

Calculation: hot bearish market = directional state bearish AND bearish trend expansion AND absolute adjusted-centerline slope > ATR(trend length) × hot-market slope factor
Calculation: market chop = absolute normalized deviation < chop deviation threshold AND absolute momentum histogram < minimum momentum × chop momentum factor
Calculation: adaptive regime = classify trend when ADX(regime length) >= trend ADX threshold AND absolute normalized deviation >= trend deviation threshold; classify high-volatility trend when trend applies and EMA(ATR(short volatility length), short smoothing length) > EMA(ATR(short volatility length), long smoothing length); classify range when ADX is below trend ADX threshold × range factor OR market chop applies; otherwise classify transition; confirm a candidate regime for the regime confirmation length before changing the retained regime
Calculation: higher-timeframe direction = on T1 bars, bullish when close > adjusted centerline and adjusted centerline is rising, bearish when close < adjusted centerline and adjusted centerline is falling, otherwise neutral; sample on chart bars using the forming T1 bar or last completed T1 bar according to the higher-timeframe bar selection parameter

Calculation: structure direction = initialize neutral; change to bullish on a bullish structural break and bearish on a bearish structural break; bullish breaks from neutral or bullish state are BOS and from bearish state are CHOCH; bearish breaks from neutral or bearish state are BOS and from bullish state are CHOCH
Calculation: strong opposing structure for a long = structure direction bearish AND bearish trend is strong AND (higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR higher-timeframe direction bearish); strong opposing structure for a short = structure direction bullish AND bullish trend is strong AND (higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR higher-timeframe direction bullish)
