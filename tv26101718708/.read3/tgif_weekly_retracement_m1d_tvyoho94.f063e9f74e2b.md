Calculation: Model-period anchors = current T1-period high and low, updated on confirmed chart bars; the later extreme is the terminus and the other is the origin; freeze anchors while a confirming swing holds and resume tracking after its closing-price break.
Calculation: Validated arrays = for each enabled T1, T2 (longer than T1), and T3 (shorter than T1), the most recent premium and discount array from last completed timeframe bars, calculated on those timeframe bars and read on chart bars. A bullish FVG requires low[1] > high[3], low[1] - high[3] >= minimum array height × ATR(ATR lookback)[1], and close[2] - open[2] >= displacement body × ATR(ATR lookback)[1]; its zone is [high[3], low[1]], replaced by [close[3], open[3]] when close[3] < open[3]. A bearish FVG requires high[1] < low[3], low[3] - high[1] >= minimum array height × ATR(ATR lookback)[1], and open[2] - close[2] >= displacement body × ATR(ATR lookback)[1]; its zone is [high[1], low[3]], replaced by [close[3], open[3]] when close[3] > open[3]. A bullish VI requires open[1] > close[2] and open[1] - close[2] >= minimum array height × ATR(ATR lookback)[1], with zone [close[2], open[1]]; a bearish VI requires open[1] < close[2] and close[2] - open[1] >= minimum array height × ATR(ATR lookback)[1], with zone [open[1], close[2]]. Retain each latest array until a chart-bar close passes through its far edge.
Calculation: Delivered = the terminus reached at least the delivery-depth fraction of a preexisting opposite-side array's height from the approached edge: a high terminus into a premium array from below, or a low terminus into a discount array from above.
Calculation: Draw spent = after the terminus candle and before the setup window, the deepest pullback from the terminus reached the retracement lower-bound fraction of the model-period range; reset this measure when the terminus changes.
Long: The model-period low printed later than its high AND the current chart bar and a fresh PivotLow(low, left bars, right bars, T4, last completed bar) are within the setup window (Thursday at or after the configured start hour or Friday, in the configured timezone) and the same model period AND no unbroken confirming swing is active AND the chart close is above the pivot low AND model-period range > 0 AND draw is not spent AND (delivery requirement is disabled OR the low terminus is Delivered into a discount array) AND (recent-terminus requirement is disabled OR the low formed Thursday or Friday).
Short: The model-period high printed later than its low AND the current chart bar and a fresh PivotHigh(high, left bars, right bars, T4, last completed bar) are within the setup window (Thursday at or after the configured start hour or Friday, in the configured timezone) and the same model period AND no unbroken confirming swing is active AND the chart close is below the pivot high AND model-period range > 0 AND draw is not spent AND (delivery requirement is disabled OR the high terminus is Delivered into a premium array) AND (recent-terminus requirement is disabled OR the high formed Thursday or Friday).
