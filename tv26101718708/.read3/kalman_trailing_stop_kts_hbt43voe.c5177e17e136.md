Calculation: Kalman level and velocity = 2-state Kalman filter of close using variance(close, noise lookback) as observation variance and variance(close, noise lookback) × 10^((responsiveness − 50) / 10) as process variance; when volume weighting is enabled, divide observation variance by clamp(volume / median(volume, volume lookback), lower volume-ratio bound, upper volume-ratio bound); optionally use a 3-state filter that also observes normalized range position, (close − lowest(low, oscillator lookback)) / (highest(high, oscillator lookback) − lowest(low, oscillator lookback)), or 0.5 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Smoothed true range = EMA(true range, noise lookback)
Calculation: Kalman residual deviation = sqrt(EMA((close − Kalman level)^2, noise lookback))
Calculation: Kalman bands = Kalman level ± stop-distance multiplier × smoothed true range
Calculation: Kalman trend direction and trailing stop = initialize direction long when close > Kalman level, otherwise short, and initialize the stop to the corresponding lower or upper band; while long, flip short when close < previous stop − signal offset × smoothed true range, close < previous close, and slope confirmation is disabled or Kalman velocity < 0; on that flip set stop to the lesser of the upper band and rolling low + stop-distance multiplier × smoothed true range; otherwise set target to the greater of previous stop and lower band, add the slope-push amount when Kalman velocity > 0 and close > previous close, optionally tighten to close − oscillator cushion × smoothed true range when the normalized oscillator exceeds its upper extreme, and when the effective floor (the greater of latest internal swing low − structural padding × smoothed true range and rolling high − stop-distance multiplier × smoothed true range) exceeds target, set target to the lesser of that floor and close − close cushion × smoothed true range; when smoothed true range rises, relax target toward the previous stop by the increase in smoothed true range × stop-distance multiplier × relaxation factor, bounded by the lower band; while short, apply the symmetric rules using the upper band, rolling low, latest internal swing high, and lower oscillator extreme; optionally interpolate from the previous stop to target with a normalized logistic sigmoid over the transition length when the target change exceeds the smoothing threshold × smoothed true range
Calculation: Clamped return kurtosis = clamp(EMA((change(close) − EMA(change(close), noise lookback))^4, noise lookback) / EMA((change(close) − EMA(change(close), noise lookback))^2, noise lookback)^2, lower kurtosis bound, upper kurtosis bound)
Calculation: Dynamic sweep multiplier = base sweep multiplier × sqrt(clamped return kurtosis / normal kurtosis reference)
Calculation: Latest short-term swing high and low = most recently detected short-term pivot high and pivot low
Calculation: Pullback average volume = average volume since the latest short-term swing high for long setups or swing low for short setups, through the prior bar; reset on a new corresponding pivot
Long: first initialized bar with close > Kalman level OR prior Kalman trend direction short AND close > previous stop + signal offset × smoothed true range AND close > previous close AND (slope confirmation disabled OR Kalman velocity > 0)
Short: first initialized bar with close ≤ Kalman level OR prior Kalman trend direction long AND close < previous stop − signal offset × smoothed true range AND close < previous close AND (slope confirmation disabled OR Kalman velocity < 0)
Long: low = lowest(low, max(minimum plunger lookback, round(plunger lookback fraction × cycle lookback))) AND close > open AND high − low > 0 AND (high − close) / (high − low) ≤ wick percentage AND high − low ≥ ATR(cycle lookback) / range divisor AND low < Kalman level − dynamic sweep multiplier × Kalman residual deviation
Short: high = highest(high, max(minimum plunger lookback, round(plunger lookback fraction × cycle lookback))) AND close < open AND high − low > 0 AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≤ wick percentage AND high − low ≥ ATR(cycle lookback) / range divisor AND high > Kalman level + dynamic sweep multiplier × Kalman residual deviation
Long: Kalman trend direction short AND close > open AND true range > EMA(true range, warning lookback) × warning multiplier
Short: Kalman trend direction long AND close < open AND true range > EMA(true range, warning lookback) × warning multiplier
Long: close crosses above latest short-term swing high AND prior pullback average volume < median(volume, volume lookback) × dry-up threshold AND volume > prior pullback average volume AND long position is active AND Kalman trailing stop ≥ entry price AND scale-in cooldown has expired
Short: close crosses below latest short-term swing low AND prior pullback average volume < median(volume, volume lookback) × dry-up threshold AND volume > prior pullback average volume AND short position is active AND Kalman trailing stop ≤ entry price AND scale-in cooldown has expired
Exit: Long, take-profit is enabled AND high ≥ entry price + take-profit multiplier × smoothed true range OR intraday filter is enabled AND the bar is outside the configured session
Exit: Short, take-profit is enabled AND low ≤ entry price − take-profit multiplier × smoothed true range OR intraday filter is enabled AND the bar is outside the configured session
