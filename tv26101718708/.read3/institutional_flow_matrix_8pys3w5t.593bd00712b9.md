Calculation: Bullish structure break = on a confirmed bar, close crosses above the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high; bearish structure break = on a confirmed bar, close crosses below the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low. A pivot is confirmed after its separate left and right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Market structure state = bullish after a bullish structure break, bearish after a bearish structure break, and otherwise the previous state; initially neutral.
Calculation: Bullish trend = Supertrend(ATR multiplier, ATR lookback) is bullish AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, longer trend lookback); bearish trend = Supertrend is bearish AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < EMA(close, longer trend lookback).
Calculation: Directional bias = on confirmed bars, becomes bullish if (a bullish structure break AND bullish trend) OR (market structure state is bullish AND the shorter trend EMA crosses above the longer trend EMA AND bullish trend); becomes bearish under the corresponding bearish conditions; if neutral, becomes bullish or bearish when market structure and trend agree; otherwise retains its previous state, initially neutral. Bullish candidates take precedence over bearish candidates.
Long: A confirmed pivot high exceeds the previous confirmed pivot high, or a confirmed pivot low exceeds the previous confirmed pivot low; when equal-swing filtering is enabled, exclude pivots within the equal-swing threshold of the previous pivot, measured using ATR at the pivot bar.
Short: A confirmed pivot high is below the previous confirmed pivot high, or a confirmed pivot low is below the previous confirmed pivot low; when equal-swing filtering is enabled, exclude pivots within the equal-swing threshold of the previous pivot, measured using ATR at the pivot bar.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Directional bias is bullish.
Short: Directional bias is bearish.
Long: Directional bias changes from not bullish to bullish on a confirmed bar.
Short: Directional bias changes from not bearish to bearish on a confirmed bar.
Calculation: Normalized momentum = (EMA(close, shorter oscillator lookback) - EMA(close, longer oscillator lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback), or zero when ATR is zero; momentum signal = EMA(normalized momentum, signal lookback).
Calculation: Bullish momentum = EMA(close, shorter oscillator lookback) > EMA(close, longer oscillator lookback) AND normalized momentum > momentum signal AND normalized momentum > 0; bearish momentum = the opposite comparisons.
Long: Normalized momentum crosses above its signal.
Short: Normalized momentum crosses below its signal.
Long: Bullish momentum AND normalized momentum - momentum signal > 0.
Short: Bearish momentum AND normalized momentum - momentum signal < 0.
Calculation: Premium boundary = rolling highest high - max(rolling highest high - rolling lowest low, minimum price increment) × premium fraction; discount boundary = rolling lowest low + max(rolling highest high - rolling lowest low, minimum price increment) × discount fraction; rolling extrema use the range lookback.
Calculation: Active order blocks = on an order-block trigger, select the nearest prior opposing candle within the scan lookback that meets the minimum candle-range-to-ATR ratio, minimum body-to-range ratio, and, when volume confirmation is enabled, volume >= its SMA × volume multiplier (or the SMA is unavailable). Use the candle wick or body as zone bounds. Reject a candidate if an intervening candle within the pre-mitigation scan limit breached the opposite zone edge using the selected close or wick method. Triggers are bullish or bearish structure breaks, or enabled displacement events where the confirmed candle has the matching direction, body >= ATR × displacement multiplier, and close beyond the prior bar's high or low; a displacement event occurs only when that condition changes from false to true. Ignore duplicate active same-side zones whose two bounds are each within the duplicate-zone tick tolerance. Keep at most the configured number of newest zones. On confirmed bars, deactivate a bullish zone when close or low (selected mitigation method) falls below its bottom, and a bearish zone when close or high rises above its top; expire zones after the analytical-age limit from creation.
Calculation: Active order-block touch(side) = high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top for any active order block of that side, on a bar after the zone's creation bar.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = bullish-bias points when directional bias is bullish + bullish-structure points when market structure state is bullish + bullish-momentum points when bullish momentum is true + bullish-zone points when an active bullish order block is touched + discount-location points when close <= discount boundary; bearish confluence score uses the corresponding bearish conditions and premium location (close >= premium boundary).
Long: Bullish order-block trigger AND a qualifying bullish order block is created.
Short: Bearish order-block trigger AND a qualifying bearish order block is created.
Long: On a confirmed bar, low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) × FVG size multiplier.
Short: On a confirmed bar, high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= ATR(ATR lookback) × FVG size multiplier.
Long: On a confirmed bar, directional bias is bullish AND market structure state is bullish AND (bullish-zone-touch requirement is disabled OR an active bullish order block is touched) AND (discount-location requirement is disabled OR close <= discount boundary) AND (momentum requirement is disabled OR bullish momentum is true) AND bullish confluence score >= minimum score, the requirements were false on the previous bar, and no prior bullish signal exists or the bullish signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: On a confirmed bar, directional bias is bearish AND market structure state is bearish AND (bearish-zone-touch requirement is disabled OR an active bearish order block is touched) AND (premium-location requirement is disabled OR close >= premium boundary) AND (momentum requirement is disabled OR bearish momentum is true) AND bearish confluence score >= minimum score, the requirements were false on the previous bar, and no prior bearish signal exists or the bearish signal cooldown has elapsed.
Long: On a confirmed bar, an active bullish order-block touch changes from false to true AND directional bias is bullish.
Short: On a confirmed bar, an active bearish order-block touch changes from false to true AND directional bias is bearish.
Long: Directional bias changes to bullish AND bullish momentum is true.
Short: Directional bias changes to bearish AND bearish momentum is true.
