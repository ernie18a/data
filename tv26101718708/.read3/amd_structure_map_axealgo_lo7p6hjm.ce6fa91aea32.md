Calculation: Accumulation high = highest(high, seed lookback) on the range-start chart bar, held until the range resolves
Calculation: Accumulation low = lowest(low, seed lookback) on the range-start chart bar, held until the range resolves
Calculation: Accumulation volume baseline = SMA(volume, seed lookback) on the range-start chart bar, held until the range resolves; use current bar volume if the baseline is missing or nonpositive
Long: Before the active Accumulation range resolves or expires, fewer than two Manipulation sweeps have confirmed and either none has confirmed or the latest swept the high; a chart bar's low reaches at least sweep-depth parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) below Accumulation low with volume greater than Accumulation volume baseline × sweep-volume parameter, and that excursion closes on the same or a later chart bar strictly between Accumulation low and Accumulation high
Short: Before the active Accumulation range resolves or expires, fewer than two Manipulation sweeps have confirmed and either none has confirmed or the latest swept the low; a chart bar's high reaches at least sweep-depth parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) above Accumulation high with volume greater than Accumulation volume baseline × sweep-volume parameter, and that excursion closes on the same or a later chart bar strictly between Accumulation low and Accumulation high
Long: Before the active Accumulation range resolves or expires, its latest confirmed Manipulation swept the low, and a chart bar closes above Accumulation high + breakout-distance parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) with volume greater than Accumulation volume baseline × breakout-volume parameter
Short: Before the active Accumulation range resolves or expires, its latest confirmed Manipulation swept the high, and a chart bar closes below Accumulation low − breakout-distance parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) with volume greater than Accumulation volume baseline × breakout-volume parameter
Long: On chart bars, close of the last completed T1 bar > EMA(close, EMA lookback, computed on T1 bars, last completed bar; chart bars take its last completed value)
Short: On chart bars, close of the last completed T1 bar <= EMA(close, EMA lookback, computed on T1 bars, last completed bar; chart bars take its last completed value)
