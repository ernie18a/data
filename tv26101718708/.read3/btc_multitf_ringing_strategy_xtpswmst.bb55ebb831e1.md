Calculation: Impulse amplitude = (high(T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) - low(T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars)) * oscillation decay factor
Calculation: Strong impulse = abs(EMA(close, primary EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) - its value on the previous chart bar) / ATR(ATR lookback, T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) > impulse threshold AND ATR(ATR lookback, T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) > volatility multiplier * SMA(ATR(ATR lookback, T1 still-forming bar), ATR averaging lookback, sampled on chart bars)
Long: Strong impulse AND close(T2 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) < min(low(T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) + Impulse amplitude * pullback fraction, EMA(close, primary EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars)) AND chart close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T3 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) * long trend multiplier AND prior entry signals in the current uninterrupted run of strong-impulse chart bars < maximum entries per impulse
Short: Strong impulse AND close(T2 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) > max(high(T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) - Impulse amplitude * pullback fraction, EMA(close, primary EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars)) AND chart close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T3 still-forming bar, sampled on chart bars) * short trend multiplier AND prior entry signals in the current uninterrupted run of strong-impulse chart bars < maximum entries per impulse
Exit: long, low(chart bar) <= active long-entry signal's close(T2) - stop ATR multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback, T2, sampled on chart bars at that signal) OR high(chart bar) >= active long-entry signal's close(T2) * (1 + partial gain / 100) OR for any swing index from 1 through maximum swings, high(chart bar) >= active long-entry signal's close(T2) + its Impulse amplitude * oscillation decay factor^swing index
Exit: short, high(chart bar) >= active short-entry signal's close(T2) + stop ATR multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback, T2, sampled on chart bars at that signal) OR low(chart bar) <= active short-entry signal's close(T2) * (1 - partial gain / 100) OR for any swing index from 1 through maximum swings, low(chart bar) <= active short-entry signal's close(T2) - its Impulse amplitude * oscillation decay factor^swing index
