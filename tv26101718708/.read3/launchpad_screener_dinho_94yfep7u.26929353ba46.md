Calculation: MA spread (%) = (max(SMA(close, short MA period), SMA(close, medium MA period), SMA(close, long MA period)) - min(SMA(close, short MA period), SMA(close, medium MA period), SMA(close, long MA period))) / close * 100, where short MA period < medium MA period < long MA period
Calculation: MA proximity (%) = abs(close - (SMA(close, short MA period) + SMA(close, medium MA period) + SMA(close, long MA period)) / 3) / close * 100
Calculation: Short and medium MA flatness (%) = (STDEV(SMA(close, short MA period), short flatness lookback) / SMA(SMA(close, short MA period), short flatness lookback) * 100 + STDEV(SMA(close, medium MA period), short flatness lookback) / SMA(SMA(close, medium MA period), short flatness lookback) * 100) / 2
Calculation: Long MA flatness (%) = STDEV(SMA(close, long MA period), long flatness lookback) / SMA(SMA(close, long MA period), long flatness lookback) * 100, where long flatness lookback > short flatness lookback
Calculation: Total score = max(0, compression score weight * (1 - MA spread (%) / MA spread threshold)) + max(0, proximity score weight * (1 - MA proximity (%) / MA proximity threshold)) + max(0, short flatness score weight * (1 - Short and medium MA flatness (%) / short flatness threshold)) + max(0, long flatness score weight * (1 - Long MA flatness (%) / long flatness threshold)) + (if volume < SMA(volume, volume lookback) then volume score weight else max(0, volume score weight * (1 - (volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) - baseline volume multiple) / volume score scale))) + (if ROC(ATR(ATR period), ATR ROC lookback) < 0 then ATR score weight else max(0, ATR score weight * (1 + ROC(ATR(ATR period), ATR ROC lookback) / ATR ROC score scale))
Calculation: Active compression = at least minimum matching criteria of (MA spread (%) < MA spread threshold, MA proximity (%) < MA proximity threshold, Short and medium MA flatness (%) < short flatness threshold, Long MA flatness (%) < long flatness threshold, volume < SMA(volume, volume lookback), ROC(ATR(ATR period), ATR ROC lookback) < 0) AND Total score >= minimum total score
Calculation: Consecutive active bars = count of consecutive chart bars satisfying Active compression through the current bar, reset to zero when Active compression is false
Long: (Active compression AND NOT Active compression[1]) OR (Consecutive active bars[1] >= minimum consecutive active chart bars AND close > max(SMA(close, short MA period)[1], SMA(close, medium MA period)[1], SMA(close, long MA period)[1]) AND volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] * activation volume multiplier)
