Calculation: Candle body percentage = 0 when high = low; otherwise abs(close - open) / (high - low) * 100.
Calculation: ATR ratio = 0 when SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback) = 0; otherwise ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = EMA(close, short-term lookback, T1 current bar) and EMA(close, medium-term lookback, T1 current bar), sampled on chart bars with lookahead off and gaps filled forward; the current T1 bar may still be forming in real time, while historical values use the latest completed T1 bar available.
Calculation: Bull score = number of true conditions on the current chart bar: EMA(close, short-term lookback) > EMA(close, medium-term lookback), with short-term lookback < medium-term lookback; each EMA is greater than its values at every one of the preceding slope lookback bars; EMA200 filter disabled or close > EMA(close, long-term lookback), with long-term lookback > medium-term lookback; in the current or preceding pullback lookback bars, low <= EMA(close, short-term lookback) + pullback distance * ATR(ATR lookback) or low <= EMA(close, medium-term lookback) + pullback distance * ATR(ATR lookback); close > open and candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage; RSI(close, RSI lookback) > bullish RSI level; ADX(DMI lookback, smoothing lookback) >= minimum ADX, with DMI lookback and smoothing lookback equal; +DI(DMI lookback) > -DI(DMI lookback); ATR ratio >= minimum ATR ratio and ATR ratio <= maximum ATR ratio; higher-timeframe filter disabled or T1 short-term EMA > T1 medium-term EMA.
Calculation: Bear score = number of true conditions on the current chart bar: EMA(close, short-term lookback) < EMA(close, medium-term lookback), with short-term lookback < medium-term lookback; each EMA is less than its values at every one of the preceding slope lookback bars; EMA200 filter disabled or close < EMA(close, long-term lookback), with long-term lookback > medium-term lookback; in the current or preceding pullback lookback bars, high >= EMA(close, short-term lookback) - pullback distance * ATR(ATR lookback) or high >= EMA(close, medium-term lookback) - pullback distance * ATR(ATR lookback); close < open and candle body percentage >= minimum body percentage; RSI(close, RSI lookback) < bearish RSI level; ADX(DMI lookback, smoothing lookback) >= minimum ADX, with DMI lookback and smoothing lookback equal; -DI(DMI lookback) > +DI(DMI lookback); ATR ratio >= minimum ATR ratio and ATR ratio <= maximum ATR ratio; higher-timeframe filter disabled or T1 short-term EMA < T1 medium-term EMA.
Long: At chart bar close, (strict mode enabled and all ten bullish score conditions are true, or strict mode disabled and Bull score >= minimum score) and the bearish score does not qualify by the same strict-mode or minimum-score rule; session filter disabled or datetime is within its session; blackout filter disabled or datetime is outside its blackout window; and (show-all-signals enabled or ((no prior signal exists or current chart-bar index - most recent signal bar index >= cooldown parameter) and (no bullish pullback bar has previously been used or the latest bullish EMA-proximity bar index differs from the last bullish pullback bar used for a signal))).
Long: At chart bar close, the Long conditions hold and Bull score >= separate strong-signal score parameter.
Short: At chart bar close, (strict mode enabled and all ten bearish score conditions are true, or strict mode disabled and Bear score >= minimum score) and the bullish score does not qualify by the same strict-mode or minimum-score rule; session filter disabled or datetime is within its session; blackout filter disabled or datetime is outside its blackout window; and (show-all-signals enabled or ((no prior signal exists or current chart-bar index - most recent signal bar index >= cooldown parameter) and (no bearish pullback bar has previously been used or the latest bearish EMA-proximity bar index differs from the last bearish pullback bar used for a signal))).
Short: At chart bar close, the Short conditions hold and Bear score >= separate strong-signal score parameter.
