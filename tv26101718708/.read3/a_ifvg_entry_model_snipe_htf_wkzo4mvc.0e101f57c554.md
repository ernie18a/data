Calculation: Long risk = close − (lowest low since the most recent sweep of a confirmed pivot low − ATR(lookback) × stop-buffer parameter)
Calculation: Short risk = (highest high since the most recent sweep of a confirmed pivot high + ATR(lookback) × stop-buffer parameter) − close
Long: A sweep of a confirmed pivot low occurred within the setup window (low < the latest confirmed pivot low and close > that pivot low) AND a bullish displacement occurred within the setup window (close > open and abs(close − open) > ATR(lookback) × displacement-strength parameter) AND a bullish IFVG occurred within the setup window (low[n] > high[m] OR close > high[n], where m is a longer lag than n) AND close > open AND close > high[n] AND ((SNIPE mode is enabled AND its long cooldown has elapsed AND (the minimum-RR filter is disabled OR (long risk > 0 AND highest(high, shorter liquidity-target lookback) − close > long risk × minimum-RR parameter))) OR (HTF runner mode is enabled AND on chart bars using the still-forming T1 bar, close(T1) > EMA(close, lookback, T1) AND its long cooldown has elapsed AND (the minimum-RR filter is disabled OR (long risk > 0 AND highest(high, longer runner-target lookback) − close > long risk × minimum-RR parameter))))
Short: A sweep of a confirmed pivot high occurred within the setup window (high > the latest confirmed pivot high and close < that pivot high) AND a bearish displacement occurred within the setup window (close < open and abs(close − open) > ATR(lookback) × displacement-strength parameter) AND a bearish IFVG occurred within the setup window (high[n] < low[m] OR close < low[n], where m is a longer lag than n) AND close < open AND close < low[n] AND ((SNIPE mode is enabled AND its short cooldown has elapsed AND (the minimum-RR filter is disabled OR (short risk > 0 AND close − lowest(low, shorter liquidity-target lookback) > short risk × minimum-RR parameter))) OR (HTF runner mode is enabled AND on chart bars using the still-forming T1 bar, close(T1) < EMA(close, lookback, T1) AND its short cooldown has elapsed AND (the minimum-RR filter is disabled OR (short risk > 0 AND close − lowest(low, longer runner-target lookback) > short risk × minimum-RR parameter))))
