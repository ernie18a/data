Calculation: MFI = Money Flow Index(hlc3, MFI lookback)
Calculation: Divergence lookback = the separate lookback parameter selected by the chart timeframe, using a fallback parameter when no configured case matches
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback), stochastic smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Peak price tracker = close if MFI reaches its highest value in the divergence lookback on this bar or the prior tracker is undefined; otherwise max(close, previous peak price tracker)
Calculation: Peak MFI tracker = MFI if MFI reaches its highest value in the divergence lookback on this bar or the prior tracker is undefined; otherwise max(MFI, previous peak MFI tracker)
Calculation: Trough price tracker = close if MFI reaches its lowest value in the divergence lookback on this bar or the prior tracker is undefined; otherwise min(close, previous trough price tracker)
Calculation: Trough MFI tracker = MFI if MFI reaches its lowest value in the divergence lookback on this bar or the prior tracker is undefined; otherwise min(MFI, previous trough MFI tracker)
Calculation: Pending pivot high = each confirmed Pivot High(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback), retained until its age from the pivot bar exceeds the level-lifetime parameter or it breaks; when duplicate filtering is enabled, reject a new level if its distance from any pending pivot high is less than the new pivot price times the duplicate-distance percentage
Calculation: Pending pivot low = each confirmed Pivot Low(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback), retained until its age from the pivot bar exceeds the level-lifetime parameter or it breaks; when duplicate filtering is enabled, reject a new level if its distance from any pending pivot low is less than the new pivot price times the duplicate-distance percentage
Calculation: Active bullish level = a pending pivot high crossed by a qualifying bullish impulse; remove it if, during the following confirmation-bars parameter bars, close < level or low <= level, or after retest
Calculation: Active bearish level = a pending pivot low crossed by a qualifying bearish impulse; remove it if, during the following confirmation-bars parameter bars, close > level or high >= level, or after retest
Long: close crosses above a pending pivot high AND close > open AND high > low AND the bullish candle body is at least the minimum breakout-body percentage of the high-low range
Short: close crosses below a pending pivot low AND close < open AND high > low AND the bearish candle body is at least the minimum breakout-body percentage of the high-low range
Long: an active bullish level exists AND more than the retest-delay parameter bars have elapsed since its breakout AND low <= level plus the retest-buffer amount
Short: an active bearish level exists AND more than the retest-delay parameter bars have elapsed since its breakout AND high >= level minus the retest-buffer amount
Long: trough price tracker at the previous bar < trough price tracker at the previous previous bar AND trough MFI tracker at the previous bar < MFI at the previous bar AND MFI >= MFI at the previous bar AND (smoothed stochastic < oversold threshold OR smoothed stochastic at the previous bar < oversold threshold)
Short: peak price tracker at the previous bar > peak price tracker at the previous previous bar AND peak MFI tracker at the previous bar > MFI at the previous bar AND MFI <= MFI at the previous bar AND (smoothed stochastic > overbought threshold OR smoothed stochastic at the previous bar > overbought threshold)
Long: trough MFI tracker = its value at the previous previous bar AND its value at the previous previous bar != its value at the previous previous previous bar AND (smoothed stochastic < oversold threshold OR smoothed stochastic at the previous bar < oversold threshold)
Short: peak MFI tracker = its value at the previous previous bar AND its value at the previous previous bar != its value at the previous previous previous bar AND (smoothed stochastic > overbought threshold OR smoothed stochastic at the previous bar > overbought threshold)
