Calculation: Raw ease = midpoint change / box ratio, where midpoint is the high-low midpoint or selected close source, box ratio = (volume / selected volume scale) / (high - low), and raw ease is zero when box ratio is zero; selected volume scale is max(SMA(nz(volume), normalization lookback), volume-scale floor) in automatic mode or manual volume scale otherwise
Calculation: Smoothed ease = raw ease when smoothing lookback is one; otherwise SMA(raw ease, smoothing lookback) or Ultimate Smoother(raw ease, smoothing lookback), according to the selected smoother
Calculation: Normalized ease = classic z-score or robust z-score of smoothed ease over the normalization lookback, according to the selected normalization method
Calculation: Ease oscillator = output scale × tanh(clamp(normalized ease × gain / (scale multiplier × max(scale floor, percentile(abs(normalized ease × gain), scale lookback, scale percentile), using fallback scale when unavailable)), -input cap, input cap))
