Calculation: Adjusted volume = min(volume, (EMA(volume, volume-average lookback)[1] if available, else volume) * volume-spike cap multiplier) when adaptive averaging is enabled; otherwise volume
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / selected EMA, RMA, or SMA(adjusted volume, volume-average lookback) when that average is greater than zero; otherwise unavailable
Calculation: Bull close strength = (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low; otherwise 0.5
Calculation: Bear close strength = (high - close) / (high - low) when high > low; otherwise 0.5
Calculation: Uptrend MA context = close > EMA(close, long-term context lookback) AND EMA(close, short-term context lookback) > EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) > EMA(close, long-term context lookback) AND EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) > its prior-bar value
Calculation: Downtrend MA context = close < EMA(close, long-term context lookback) AND EMA(close, short-term context lookback) < EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) < EMA(close, long-term context lookback) AND EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) < its prior-bar value
Long: close > highest(high[1], breakout lookback) AND close > open AND Bull close strength >= close-strength threshold AND Relative volume >= strong relative-volume threshold
Short: close < lowest(low[1], breakout lookback) AND close < open AND Bear close strength >= close-strength threshold AND Relative volume >= strong relative-volume threshold
Short: high > highest(high[1], breakout lookback) AND (Relative volume <= weak relative-volume threshold OR Bull close strength < close-strength threshold)
Long: low < lowest(low[1], breakout lookback) AND (Relative volume <= weak relative-volume threshold OR Bear close strength < close-strength threshold)
Long: MA context enabled AND Uptrend MA context AND low <= EMA(close, short-term context lookback) AND close > EMA(close, short-term context lookback) AND close > EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) AND close > open AND Bull close strength >= close-strength threshold AND Relative volume > weak relative-volume threshold
Short: MA context enabled AND Downtrend MA context AND high >= EMA(close, short-term context lookback) AND close < EMA(close, short-term context lookback) AND close < EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) AND close < open AND Bear close strength >= close-strength threshold AND Relative volume > weak relative-volume threshold
Exit: long, MA context enabled AND Uptrend MA context AND low <= EMA(close, short-term context lookback) AND close >= EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) AND close < open AND Bear close strength >= close-strength threshold AND Relative volume >= strong relative-volume threshold
Exit: short, MA context enabled AND Downtrend MA context AND high >= EMA(close, short-term context lookback) AND close <= EMA(close, middle-term context lookback) AND close > open AND Bull close strength >= close-strength threshold AND Relative volume >= strong relative-volume threshold
