Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 * log10(SMA(true range, lookback) / (highest(high, lookback) - lowest(low, lookback))) / log10(lookback), or the zero-range fallback parameter when the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Adaptive trailing band = use a minimum length, maximum length, and volatility lookback selected by chart interval; calculate the adaptive length by scaling the standard deviation of close/close[1] - 1 over the volatility lookback between its rolling lowest and highest values, then rounding between the minimum and maximum lengths (use the minimum if unavailable or the range is zero). Let the secondary length be the rounded adaptive length times its length-fraction parameter, floored at one. Smooth true range with that secondary length using alpha 1/length, initialized by its simple average; let K be its short-lookback SMA and A be the smaller of K divided by its secondary-length SMA and its cap parameter (use one if the denominator is not positive). Let D be DEMA(close, adaptive length), E be the midpoint of highest(high, adaptive length) and lowest(low, adaptive length), and F be the smaller of ((SMA(volume, short flow-average length) + volume) / 2) divided by EMA of the average of the short, medium, and long volume SMAs (use one if the denominator is not positive), and its cap parameter. Let C = (D * F + E * (2 - F)) / 2 and Q = A * F. The ordinary upper and lower bands are C + K * Q + half of ATR(true range, band-offset lookback), and C - K * Q - half of that ATR. For the alternate bullish and bearish cues, calculate the ratio of short to long Bollinger bandwidths, clamp its product with a multiplier parameter between lower and upper cap parameters, and call the result M. A bullish cue is SMA(close - close[1], short cue length) > SMA(close - close[1], long cue length) + stdev(close - close[1], long cue length) * M, and either the corresponding short-versus-long SMA expansion condition for ATR(1) or for volume is true. The bearish cue uses the symmetric less-than condition. On either cue, replace the bands with close plus or minus K times the band-width parameter. Maintain a direction initialized to zero and a prior trail initialized to zero: a bearish cue sets direction short and trail to the upper band; a bullish cue sets direction long and trail to the lower band. Otherwise, with zero direction, initialize long on close above the upper band with bullish DMI flow and the enabled long filters, or initialize short symmetrically below the lower band. In long direction, use the greater of the prior trail and lower band; if close falls below it with bearish DMI flow, switch short and set trail to the upper band, otherwise keep that greater value. In short direction, use the lesser of the prior trail and upper band; if close rises above it with bullish DMI flow, switch long and set trail to the lower band, otherwise keep that lesser value. Each source-specific lookback, multiplier, cap, fallback, and threshold is a separate parameter.
Long: position side is not long AND close > adaptive trailing band AND ADX(flow DI length, flow ADX smoothing) > flow threshold AND +DI(flow DI length) > -DI(flow DI length) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI length, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold) AND (Choppiness filter disabled OR Choppiness Index(lookback) < Choppiness threshold) AND (DMI direction filter disabled OR +DI(DI length) > -DI(DI length)) AND (Hurst filter disabled OR (Hurst Exponent(log(close), lookback) > Hurst threshold AND NOT (SMA(ATR(1), shorter volatility lookback) / SMA(ATR(1), longer volatility lookback) > volatility-expansion threshold when the denominator is positive)).
Short: position side is not short AND close < adaptive trailing band AND ADX(flow DI length, flow ADX smoothing) > flow threshold AND -DI(flow DI length) > +DI(flow DI length) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI length, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold) AND (Choppiness filter disabled OR Choppiness Index(lookback) < Choppiness threshold) AND (DMI direction filter disabled OR -DI(DI length) > +DI(DI length)) AND (Hurst filter disabled OR (Hurst Exponent(log(close), lookback) > Hurst threshold AND NOT (SMA(ATR(1), shorter volatility lookback) / SMA(ATR(1), longer volatility lookback) > volatility-expansion threshold when the denominator is positive)).
