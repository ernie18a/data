Calculation: equilibrium = linear regression(selected price source, equilibrium lookback, zero offset) when selected, otherwise EMA(selected price source, equilibrium lookback)
Calculation: oscillator = clamp((selected price source − equilibrium) / ATR(ATR lookback), ± deviation limit) in raw mode; otherwise the standard Z-score of that clipped deviation over the standardization lookback, or robust Z = robust-Z scale factor × (clipped deviation − rolling median) / rolling median absolute deviation; use zero when ATR or the normalization divisor is unavailable or zero
Calculation: volatility ratio = volatility level / SMA(volatility level, volatility-average lookback), using the external volatility-index close on chart bars in external mode or ATR(ATR lookback) otherwise; use the neutral ratio if the average is unavailable or nonpositive
Calculation: adaptive signal bands = ±(raw-band parameter in raw mode, otherwise standardized-band parameter) × clamp(volatility ratio, minimum band multiplier, maximum band multiplier)
Calculation: higher-timeframe bias = bullish when T1 close > EMA(close, T1, HTF EMA lookback), otherwise bearish; evaluate on T1 bars and sample onto chart bars with lookahead off, using the last completed T1 value historically and the still-forming T1 bar in realtime
Calculation: MTF bullish agreement = count of T2, T3, and T4 closes > their respective EMA(close, MTF EMA lookback); MTF bearish agreement = count of those closes ≤ their respective EMA; evaluate on each T timeframe and sample onto chart bars with lookahead off, using last completed values historically and still-forming bars in realtime
Calculation: ranging = ADX(ADX length, ADX smoothing) < ADX trend threshold
Calculation: bullish divergence = divergence detection enabled AND a confirmed pivot low, evaluated after the pivot-right lookback, is spaced from the previous confirmed pivot low by the minimum-to-maximum pivot interval AND ((regular divergence enabled AND current pivot low < previous pivot low AND oscillator at current pivot > oscillator at previous pivot + minimum oscillator gap) OR (hidden divergence enabled AND current pivot low > previous pivot low AND oscillator at current pivot < oscillator at previous pivot − minimum oscillator gap))
Calculation: bearish divergence = divergence detection enabled AND a confirmed pivot high, evaluated after the pivot-right lookback, is spaced from the previous confirmed pivot high by the minimum-to-maximum pivot interval AND ((regular divergence enabled AND current pivot high > previous pivot high AND oscillator at current pivot < oscillator at previous pivot − minimum oscillator gap) OR (hidden divergence enabled AND current pivot high < previous pivot high AND oscillator at current pivot > oscillator at previous pivot + minimum oscillator gap))
Calculation: secondary spread point = 1 when the secondary-spread filter is enabled, a secondary instrument price is available and positive, and the percent spread of chart close to secondary instrument price ≥ spread threshold; otherwise 0
Calculation: long confluence score = count of bullish higher-timeframe bias, ranging, a cooldown-accepted bullish divergence pivot within the recent-divergence lookback measured from its pivot bar, volatility ratio at or below its neutral level, and secondary spread point
Calculation: short confluence score = count of bearish higher-timeframe bias, ranging, a cooldown-accepted bearish divergence pivot within the recent-divergence lookback measured from its pivot bar, volatility ratio at or below its neutral level, and secondary spread point
Long: oscillator < 0
Short: oscillator ≥ 0
Long: higher-timeframe bias is bullish
Short: higher-timeframe bias is bearish
Long: bullish divergence
Short: bearish divergence
Long: oscillator crosses above the lower adaptive signal band AND (clearance filter disabled OR oscillator ≥ lower adaptive signal band + clearance buffer) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bullish) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ranging) AND (session skip disabled OR chart is not intraday OR bars since session boundary > session-skip count) AND chart bar index ≥ max(raw-mode ATR lookback or standardization lookback, one beyond equilibrium lookback) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is confirmed) AND long confluence score ≥ minimum confluence AND (MTF gate disabled OR MTF bullish agreement ≥ minimum MTF agreement)
Short: oscillator crosses below the upper adaptive signal band AND (clearance filter disabled OR oscillator ≤ upper adaptive signal band − clearance buffer) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bearish) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ranging) AND (session skip disabled OR chart is not intraday OR bars since session boundary > session-skip count) AND chart bar index ≥ max(raw-mode ATR lookback or standardization lookback, one beyond equilibrium lookback) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is confirmed) AND short confluence score ≥ minimum confluence AND (MTF gate disabled OR MTF bearish agreement ≥ minimum MTF agreement)
