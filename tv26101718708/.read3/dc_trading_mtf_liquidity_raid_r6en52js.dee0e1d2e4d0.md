Calculation: Active liquidity levels = confirmed chart Pivot High(high, chart pivot lookback) and Pivot Low(low, chart pivot lookback), plus, when enabled and T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, Pivot High(high, T1 pivot lookback) and Pivot Low(low, T1 pivot lookback) computed on T1 bars and imported on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar; when equal-level detection is enabled, merge a new chart pivot with the newest active same-side chart level within ATR(ATR lookback) × equal-level tolerance, using the higher price for highs and lower for lows; add new levels after that bar’s sweep checks; remove levels on their first wick breach, chart-level age limit, or oldest-level capacity, and retain breached levels for pending confirmation only while they remain within taken-level capacity; T1 levels have no age limit and use their own active-level capacity.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an active sellside level L is breached with low < L and close > L, and either wick confirmation is selected or, within the confirmation window after the breach, a later confirmed chart bar closes above the breach bar’s high before any close below its low, while the breached level remains retained.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an active buyside level L is breached with high > L and close < L, and either wick confirmation is selected or, within the confirmation window after the breach, a later confirmed chart bar closes below the breach bar’s low before any close above its high, while the breached level remains retained.
