Calculation: Net pressure = volume × (close - low) / (high - low + range denominator epsilon) - volume × (high - close) / (high - low + range denominator epsilon)
Calculation: Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD = EMA((EMA(close, shorter lookback) - EMA(close, longer lookback)) × power(if SMA(volume, volume lookback) > 0 then volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) else 1, volume effect) × (1 + clamp((if SMA(abs(Net pressure), volume lookback) > 0 then Net pressure / SMA(abs(Net pressure), volume lookback) else 0) × pressure effect, -maximum pressure influence, maximum pressure influence)), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: MACD histogram = Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD - EMA(Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD, signal lookback)
Calculation: Bullish MACD divergence = current confirmed pivotlow(low, max(half divergence lookback, minimum left pivot bars), divergence lookback) < its previous confirmed pivotlow(low, same lookbacks) AND current confirmed pivotlow(Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD, same lookbacks) > its previous confirmed pivotlow(Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD, same lookbacks), with both current pivots confirmed on the same bar
Calculation: Bearish MACD divergence = current confirmed pivothigh(high, max(half divergence lookback, minimum left pivot bars), divergence lookback) > its previous confirmed pivothigh(high, same lookbacks) AND current confirmed pivothigh(Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD, same lookbacks) < its previous confirmed pivothigh(Volume-pressure-adjusted MACD, same lookbacks), with both current pivots confirmed on the same bar
Calculation: Bullish histogram divergence = current confirmed pivotlow(low, max(half divergence lookback, minimum left pivot bars), divergence lookback) < its previous confirmed pivotlow(low, same lookbacks) AND current confirmed pivotlow(MACD histogram, same lookbacks) > its previous confirmed pivotlow(MACD histogram, same lookbacks), with both current pivots confirmed on the same bar
Calculation: Bearish histogram divergence = current confirmed pivothigh(high, max(half divergence lookback, minimum left pivot bars), divergence lookback) > its previous confirmed pivothigh(high, same lookbacks) AND current confirmed pivothigh(MACD histogram, same lookbacks) < its previous confirmed pivothigh(MACD histogram, same lookbacks), with both current pivots confirmed on the same bar
Long: MACD histogram >= 0 OR Bullish MACD divergence OR Bullish histogram divergence
Short: MACD histogram < 0 OR Bearish MACD divergence OR Bearish histogram divergence
