Calculation: T1 reference values = prior completed T1 bar high, low, close, and ATR(daily ATR lookback); current T1 open = open of the still-forming T1 bar. Take these values on chart bars. T1 is the daily timeframe placeholder.
Calculation: Opening-range bounds = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the opening-range session and timezone parameters; reset each new T1 day and lock on the first chart bar after a bar in that session.
Calculation: Opening-range ratio = (opening-range high − opening-range low) / prior completed T1 ATR(daily ATR lookback), when both are available.
Calculation: Gap percentage = (current T1 open − prior completed T1 close) / prior completed T1 close × 100, or zero when the prior close is zero.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative VWAP(hlc3, volume) on chart bars, reset each new T1 day. VWAP distance = (close − session VWAP) / session VWAP × 100 when VWAP is available and nonzero, or zero otherwise.
Calculation: CPR bias = +1 when chart close is above the CPR upper level, −1 when below the lower level, else zero; CPR pivot = (prior completed T1 high + prior completed T1 low + prior completed T1 close) / 3, bottom = (prior high + prior low) / 2, top = 2 × pivot − bottom, and the upper and lower levels are the maximum and minimum of top and bottom.
Calculation: Market-structure bias = +1 when the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low both exceed their preceding respective pivots, −1 when both are lower, else zero; use the same pivot lookback parameter on the left and right.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = sum, since the start of the current T1 day, of volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) on chart bars when high exceeds low, else zero for that bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) on chart bars when the average is positive, else 1. Momentum = ROC(close, momentum lookback) on chart bars. Chart ATR average = SMA(ATR(chart ATR lookback), ATR smoothing length) on chart bars.
Calculation: Pressure = clamp to [0, 100] the pressure midpoint plus: positive or negative pressure increment according to whether close is above available session VWAP; positive or negative pressure increment according to whether cumulative volume delta is positive; a signed pressure increment × min(abs(momentum) / pressure momentum scale, 1) according to whether momentum is positive; a signed pressure increment when relative volume exceeds its pressure gate, positive if close > open and negative otherwise; and a signed pressure increment according to whether close exceeds current T1 open.
Calculation: Bull points and bear points = respectively add clamp-to-[0,1](gap percentage / gap scoring scale) to bull when gap percentage is positive, otherwise clamp-to-[0,1](−gap percentage / gap scoring scale) to bear; do the same for VWAP distance using the VWAP scoring scale. Add the unit point weight to bull when close exceeds the available opening-range high, or to bear when close is below its low; add it according to positive or negative CPR bias and market-structure bias. Add clamp-to-[0,1](abs(momentum) / momentum point scale) to the side matching momentum's sign. Add (pressure − pressure midpoint) / pressure midpoint to bull when pressure exceeds the midpoint, otherwise (pressure midpoint − pressure) / pressure midpoint to bear. When prior completed T1 ATR is nonzero, form trend alignment as (close − current T1 open) / (prior completed T1 ATR × trend-distance scale); add its positive value, clamped to [0,1], to bull when positive, or its negation, clamped to [0,1], to bear when negative.
Calculation: Sideways points = add the opening-range compression weight when opening-range ratio is below the narrow-range ratio parameter; add the common compression weight for each of these conditions: relative volume below its sideways gate, absolute VWAP distance below its sideways gate, and chart ATR below chart ATR average × ATR contraction ratio.
Calculation: Total points = bull points + bear points + sideways points. When total points are positive, each probability is its corresponding points / total points × 100; when total points are zero, use the respective bull, bear, and sideways zero-total fallback probability parameters.
Calculation: Confidence grade = round(clamp to [0, 100] of (maximum of bull, bear, and sideways probabilities × confidence dominance weight + clarity × confidence clarity scale)); clarity = clamp to [0,1](abs(opening-range ratio − narrow-range ratio parameter) / wide-range clarity scale) when the ratio is available, else the missing-range clarity parameter.
Long: opening range is locked AND bull probability > bullish-probability threshold AND confidence grade ≥ confidence-grade threshold
Short: opening range is locked AND bear probability > bearish-probability threshold AND confidence grade ≥ confidence-grade threshold
Long: opening-range high is available AND close crosses above opening-range high
Short: opening-range low is available AND close crosses below opening-range low
Long: session VWAP is available AND close crosses above session VWAP
Short: session VWAP is available AND close crosses below session VWAP
