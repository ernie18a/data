Calculation: Mapped levels = active levels from confirmed external pivot highs/lows, equal highs/lows, order blocks, fair value gaps, and highs/lows of the last completed bars of T1 and T2 (T2 longer than T1), with T1/T2 values sampled on chart bars; merge levels within level-merge tolerance × ATR(level ATR lookback) and retain at most the level-capacity parameter
Calculation: Cumulative flow = cumulative sum on chart bars of ((close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) × volume when SMA(volume, volume-availability lookback) is available and positive, otherwise (close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Selected event = the first true condition in this order, evaluated on chart bars: upward sweep (low < nearest active mapped level below close within sweep-distance parameter × ATR(level ATR lookback), close > that level, and lower wick ≥ nearest-rank percentile(lower wick, wick lookback, wick percentile threshold)); downward sweep (high > nearest active mapped level above close within sweep-distance parameter × ATR(level ATR lookback), close < that level, and upper wick ≥ nearest-rank percentile(upper wick, wick lookback, wick percentile threshold)); bullish divergence (when a confirmed external pivot low with pivot-length bars on both sides confirms, its low < the preceding confirmed external pivot low and cumulative flow at its pivot bar > cumulative flow at the preceding pivot bar); bearish divergence (when a confirmed external pivot high confirms, its high > the preceding confirmed external pivot high and cumulative flow at its pivot bar < cumulative flow at the preceding pivot bar); upward shift (close > the most recent confirmed external pivot high not already broken upward); downward shift (close < the most recent confirmed external pivot low not already broken downward); climax (when volume SMA and its percentile are available, volume ≥ nearest-rank percentile(volume, climax volume/range lookback, volume percentile threshold) AND high − low ≥ nearest-rank percentile(high − low, climax volume/range lookback, range percentile threshold); otherwise, high − low ≥ nearest-rank percentile(high − low, climax volume/range lookback, range percentile threshold) AND abs(close − open) ≥ nearest-rank percentile(abs(close − open), climax body lookback, body percentile threshold), with direction upward if close > open and downward otherwise); squeeze release (Bollinger Bands(close, squeeze lookback, deviation multiplier) are wholly inside Keltner Channels centered on SMA(close, squeeze lookback) with width ATR(squeeze lookback) × channel multiplier for at least the minimum squeeze duration on consecutive preceding bars, and the current bar is no longer inside; direction upward if close > SMA(close, squeeze lookback) and downward otherwise); upward rejection (bullish engulfing: close > open, prior close < prior open, close ≥ prior open, open ≤ prior close; OR bullish pin: lower wick ≥ its wick percentile threshold, lower wick > abs(close − open) × pin-body multiplier, and close > (high + low) / 2; and close is within level-merge tolerance × ATR(level ATR lookback) of an active mapped level); downward rejection (bearish engulfing: close < open, prior close > prior open, close ≤ prior open, open ≥ prior close; OR bearish pin: upper wick ≥ its wick percentile threshold, upper wick > abs(close − open) × pin-body multiplier, and close < (high + low) / 2; and close is within level-merge tolerance × ATR(level ATR lookback) of an active mapped level)
Calculation: Event eligible = a selected event exists AND the chart bar is confirmed AND ATR(level ATR lookback) > 0 AND the efficiency-ratio calibration lookback has elapsed with its rank and wick percentiles available AND no event of the same class, in either direction, fired within the event-separation lookback
Long: Event eligible AND selected event direction is upward
Short: Event eligible AND selected event direction is downward
