Calculation: Volume source = selected volume, high-low range, or automatic selection of volume when cumulative volume is positive and high-low range otherwise.
Calculation: Flow score = MFI score + A/D-line score + Williams %R score, using shared upper, upper-middle, lower-middle, and lower score weights. MFI is 100 when its negative-flow sum is zero, otherwise 100 - 100/(1 + positive-flow sum/negative-flow sum); each flow sum is the sum over the shared momentum lookback of volume source × close when close change is respectively positive or negative. MFI score uses upper, shared midpoint, and lower MFI thresholds, assigning the corresponding score weights above the upper threshold, above the midpoint, at or above the lower threshold, and otherwise. A/D line is the cumulative sum of zero when high equals low, otherwise ((2 × close - low - high)/(high - low)) × volume source. Its rolling highest and lowest values use the shared flow lookback and are each retained until the corresponding rolling extreme changes; A/D-line score uses the upper weight at or above the retained high, the upper-middle weight above their midpoint, the lower-middle weight above the retained low, and otherwise the lower weight. Williams %R uses the shared momentum lookback, or its midpoint threshold when highest high equals lowest low; its score uses separate upper, midpoint, and lower Williams %R thresholds with the shared score weights above the upper threshold, above the midpoint, at or above the lower threshold, and otherwise.
Calculation: StochRSI composite = mean(K, D), where K = SMA(StochRSI(ohlc4, shared momentum lookback, stochastic lookback), smoothing lookback) and D = SMA(K, smoothing lookback).
Long: sign(Flow score) + sign(OBV(ohlc4, volume source) - EMA(OBV(ohlc4, volume source), shared flow lookback)) + sign(StochRSI composite - shared midpoint threshold) > 0
Short: sign(Flow score) + sign(OBV(ohlc4, volume source) - EMA(OBV(ohlc4, volume source), shared flow lookback)) + sign(StochRSI composite - shared midpoint threshold) < 0
