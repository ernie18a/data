Calculation: Rally state(EMA) = (session filter disabled or chart bar is within the configured session) AND Had undercut(EMA) AND close > EMA
Calculation: Active episode(EMA) = a Rally state(EMA) transition from false to true occurred on any of the preceding 1 through confirmation-window chart bars
Long: show-undercut-rally enabled AND (Active episode(EMA on T1, shorter lookback) AND (green confirmation disabled or close > open) AND close > EMA on T1, shorter lookback AND close < open(T1, bar still forming) OR Active episode(EMA on T1, longer lookback) AND (green confirmation disabled or close > open) AND close > EMA on T1, longer lookback AND close < open(T1, bar still forming) OR Active episode(EMA on T2, separate lookback) AND (green confirmation disabled or close > open) AND close > EMA on T2, separate lookback) AND the corresponding signal has not already occurred in this T1 period
