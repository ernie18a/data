Calculation: Active macro channel = confirm a candidate high when it is at least all more recent highs and strictly above all older highs, and a candidate low when it is at most all more recent lows and strictly below all older lows, using the selected pivot lookback; the adaptive lookback is round(adaptive base / clamp(ATR(short ATR period) / ATR(long ATR period), ratio floor, ratio ceiling)), otherwise use the selected fixed preset or custom lookback. Connect consecutive opposite-type pivots only when their bar distance is at least the minimum segment length and their absolute price distance is at least the minimum ATR multiple times ATR(short ATR period); otherwise retain the prior pivot. Extend a same-type high pivot to a higher high or a same-type low pivot to a lower low. Fit a straight line between each segment's pivot prices; at most the sample-limit parameter evenly spaced bars determine separate upper and lower deviation percentiles. Each active-channel half-width is max(its deviation percentile times the channel-width multiplier, the minimum-width ATR multiple times ATR(short ATR period)). The active channel extends from the last confirmed pivot using the current close or, in non-repaint mode, the last completed bar's close; its direction is up after a low pivot and down after a high pivot. Calculate higher-timeframe directions on T1 and T2 bars and use each timeframe's last completed bar; T1 is shorter than T2.
Calculation: Micro regression channel = weighted least-squares regression of close[i + lag] on x=i for i from zero through the micro lookback minus one, with weight=decay^i and lag selected by non-repaint mode. Its direction is up when atan(-regression slope / ATR(short ATR period)) in degrees exceeds the angle threshold, down when it is below the negative threshold, and flat otherwise. Its R² is the squared weighted correlation; it is visible when R² is at least the minimum-fit parameter. Its upper and lower bands are the fitted value at x=0 plus or minus the weighted root-mean-square residual times the micro-width multiplier.
Calculation: Navigator score(side) = zero without an active channel; otherwise sum the alignment budget when both macro and micro directions match side, the channel-position budget times the favorable channel-position percentage divided by 100, the fit-quality budget times micro R² when the micro channel is visible (zero otherwise), and the per-timeframe agreement budget for each of T1 and T2 whose direction matches side. Favorable channel-position percentage is 100 minus 100*(close-active lower)/(active upper-active lower) for long, and 100*(close-active lower)/(active upper-active lower) for short. Clamp each component to its budget and the total to the score scale, whose ceiling equals the sum of the component budgets. If strict MTF mode is enabled and MTF points are below the full dual-timeframe budget, cap the total at entry threshold minus the strict-cap margin.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction down AND close crosses above the active upper channel boundary AND long Navigator score >= minimum signal score AND structural cooldown satisfied.
Short: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction up AND close crosses below the active lower channel boundary AND short Navigator score >= minimum signal score AND structural cooldown satisfied.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction up AND close is between the active lower channel boundary and that boundary plus the pullback ATR fraction times ATR(short ATR period) AND the same pullback condition was false on the prior chart bar AND long Navigator score >= minimum signal score AND structural cooldown satisfied.
Short: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction down AND close is between the active upper channel boundary minus the pullback ATR fraction times ATR(short ATR period) and that boundary AND the same pullback condition was false on the prior chart bar AND short Navigator score >= minimum signal score AND structural cooldown satisfied.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction up AND close crosses above the micro upper band AND the micro channel is visible AND bars since active segment start are between the early-segment minimum and maximum, inclusive AND long Navigator score >= minimum signal score AND structural cooldown satisfied.
Short: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction down AND close crosses below the micro lower band AND the micro channel is visible AND bars since active segment start are between the early-segment minimum and maximum, inclusive AND short Navigator score >= minimum signal score AND structural cooldown satisfied.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction up AND long Navigator score crosses above the entry threshold AND entry cooldown satisfied.
Short: confirmed chart bar AND active macro direction down AND short Navigator score crosses above the entry threshold AND entry cooldown satisfied.
