Calculation: Active order-block zones = On each bar, create a bullish zone from [low[1], high[1]] when close[1] < open[1], close > open, and abs(close - open) >= ATR(lookback) * minimum flip-body threshold; create a bearish zone from [low[1], high[1]] when close[1] > open[1], close < open, and abs(close - open) >= ATR(lookback) * minimum flip-body threshold. Merge each new zone into the first active zone of the same direction with positive overlap, overlap at least the minimum overlap fraction of the smaller zone, and merged height <= ATR(lookback) * maximum merged-height threshold; otherwise add it. After creating or merging zones, a bullish zone is touched when low <= its top and is removed when low <= its bottom; a bearish zone is touched when high >= its bottom and is removed when high >= its top.
Long: A bullish order-block zone is created OR low <= the top of any active bullish order-block zone.
Short: A bearish order-block zone is created OR high >= the bottom of any active bearish order-block zone.
