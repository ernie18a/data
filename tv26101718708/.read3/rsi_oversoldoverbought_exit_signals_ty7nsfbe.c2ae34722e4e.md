Calculation: DivergenceRange(event) = bars since event is between the lower and upper divergence-range parameters
Long: signal markers enabled AND RSI(source, RSI lookback) crosses above the oversold parameter AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR current bar confirmed)
Short: signal markers enabled AND RSI(source, RSI lookback) crosses below the overbought parameter AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR current bar confirmed)
Long: divergence detection enabled AND pivot low of RSI(source, RSI lookback), using the left and right pivot lookbacks, is confirmed AND RSI at the pivot (right pivot lookback bars ago) is higher than RSI at the previous confirmed pivot low AND low at the pivot is lower than low at the previous confirmed pivot low AND DivergenceRange(previous bar's confirmed pivot low)
Short: divergence detection enabled AND pivot high of RSI(source, RSI lookback), using the left and right pivot lookbacks, is confirmed AND RSI at the pivot (right pivot lookback bars ago) is lower than RSI at the previous confirmed pivot high AND high at the pivot is higher than high at the previous confirmed pivot high AND DivergenceRange(previous bar's confirmed pivot high)
