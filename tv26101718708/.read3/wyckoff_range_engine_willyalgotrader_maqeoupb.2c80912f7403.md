Calculation: Effort = S / SMA(S, effort baseline length), where S is volume or high - low according to the effort source setting
Calculation: MeanEffort(a, b) = average of Effort over the bars after bar a up to and including bar b
Calculation: PeakEffort(a, b) = maximum of Effort over the bars after bar a up to and including bar b
Calculation: Bounded(x) = min(1, max(0, x))
Calculation: Turn = close-based zigzag turning point: a high turn has price = highest high since the previous low turn's bar and is confirmed on bar t when close(t) < price - swing reversal multiple * ATR(structure ATR length); a low turn has price = lowest low since the previous high turn's bar and is confirmed when close(t) > price + swing reversal multiple * ATR(structure ATR length)
Calculation: Run(P) = for a low turn P, step back over earlier turns while the earlier high turn is above the next later high turn and the low turn between them is above price(P); run start = last high turn reached; RunLen = abs(price(run start) - price(P)) / ATR(structure ATR length); RunBars = bars from run start to P; RunSteps = number of steps; for a high turn P, swap highs and lows and reverse both comparisons
Calculation: ClimaxScore(P) = w1 * Bounded(MeanEffort(run start, P) - o1) + w2 * Bounded((PeakEffort(run start, P) - o1) / s2) + w3 * Bounded((RunLen - o2) / s3) + w4 * Bounded((RunLen / RunBars) / s4), with run start, RunLen and RunBars from Run(P)
Calculation: Climax(P) = ClimaxScore(P) >= climax score threshold AND PeakEffort(run start, P) >= climax peak threshold AND RunSteps >= trend context count
Calculation: RangePosition(p) = (p - B) / (T - B) for accumulation, (T - p) / (T - B) for distribution, where B and T are the bottom and top of the Range
Calculation: STSeen = a climax-side turn (low for accumulation, high for distribution) with 0 <= RangePosition <= ST zone has been confirmed since the Range opened
Calculation: Range = accumulation Range (candidate P a low turn, reaction H a high turn) or distribution Range (P a high turn, H a low turn), opened on bar t when no Range is open and, for candidate P, H is confirmed on t with abs(H - price(P)) >= min range height multiple * ATR(structure ATR length), abs(H - price(P)) >= AR min retrace * run distance of P, and t - bar(P) <= AR max bars; candidate P = the first turn with Climax(P) true while no Range or candidate is open (a candidate stays open until it opens a Range or t - bar(P) exceeds AR max bars), replaced by each later deeper turn of the same kind before opening, whose depth is added to its run distance (run distance = abs(price(run start) - price(P)) plus added depths); climax edge = price(P), reaction edge = H, B and T set accordingly; before any ST or Spring, a climax-side turn beyond the climax edge that closes back inside it moves the climax edge to its price when the prior-ST requirement is on, and a reaction-side turn beyond the reaction edge moves the reaction edge to its price
Calculation: RangeOpen = the Range was not closed on an earlier bar by LPS/LPSY, by a reaction-side turn with RangePosition < 0 while a climax-side turn is pending, or by a reaction-side turn beyond the reaction edge whose height from the climax edge exceeds max range height * ATR before any ST or Spring; and on this bar none of these closes it: bars since its climax bar > range lifetime while no SOS/SOW; close beyond the climax edge by more than decisive break multiple * ATR while no SOS/SOW; a pending climax-side turn with RangePosition < 0 is not reclaimed (no close with RangePosition >= 0) within reclaim deadline bars of its confirmation; close with RangePosition < midline level after SOS/SOW and before LPS/LPSY; bars since SOS/SOW > LPS window with no LPS/LPSY
Calculation: Stop = initial stop at entry. Long: Wick = min(entry bar low - wick buffer multiple * ATR(stop ATR length), entry price - wick minimum multiple * ATR(stop ATR length)); ATR = entry price - stop multiple * ATR(stop ATR length); Structure = min(event level - wick buffer multiple * ATR(stop ATR length), entry price - wick minimum multiple * ATR(stop ATR length)), where event level = price of the LPS or Spring turn, or the reaction edge for SOS; the stop mode setting selects one. Short: same with max and plus, entry bar high, price of the LPSY or UTAD turn, or the reaction edge for SOW
Calculation: Target(k) = for long, entry price + k * (entry price - Stop); for short, entry price - k * (Stop - entry price)
Calculation: ActiveStop = if break-even setting is on and Target(TP1 multiple) was reached on an earlier bar since entry, then for long entry price + break-even offset multiple * ATR(stop ATR length at entry), for short entry price - break-even offset multiple * ATR(stop ATR length at entry); otherwise Stop
Long: accumulation RangeOpen AND no SOS yet AND (STSeen OR prior-ST requirement off) AND (a low turn is confirmed on this bar with RangePosition of its price < 0 and close RangePosition >= 0, OR close RangePosition >= 0 on this bar is the first such close after the latest low turn with RangePosition of its price < 0 and this bar is within reclaim deadline bars of that turn's confirmation)
Long: accumulation RangeOpen AND no SOS yet AND no pending Spring AND last turn is a low turn AND (STSeen OR Spring occurred) AND close > reaction edge + SOS close-beyond multiple * ATR(structure ATR length) AND (MeanEffort(bar of last low turn, this bar) >= SOS effort threshold OR abs(close - price of last low turn) / ATR(structure ATR length) >= SOS leg length threshold), where AND replaces OR when the SOS confirmation setting is AND
Long: accumulation RangeOpen AND a low turn is confirmed on this bar with its bar after the SOS bar AND RangePosition of its price >= LPS floor
Short: distribution RangeOpen AND no SOW yet AND (STSeen OR prior-ST requirement off) AND (a high turn is confirmed on this bar with RangePosition of its price < 0 and close RangePosition >= 0, OR close RangePosition >= 0 on this bar is the first such close after the latest high turn with RangePosition of its price < 0 and this bar is within reclaim deadline bars of that turn's confirmation)
Short: distribution RangeOpen AND no SOW yet AND no pending UTAD AND last turn is a high turn AND (STSeen OR UTAD occurred) AND close < reaction edge - SOS close-beyond multiple * ATR(structure ATR length) AND (MeanEffort(bar of last high turn, this bar) >= SOS effort threshold OR abs(price of last high turn - close) / ATR(structure ATR length) >= SOS leg length threshold), where AND replaces OR when the SOS confirmation setting is AND
Short: distribution RangeOpen AND a high turn is confirmed on this bar with its bar after the SOW bar AND RangePosition of its price >= LPS floor
Exit: long, a bar after entry AND high >= Target(TP3 multiple)
Exit: long, a bar after entry AND low <= ActiveStop AND (high < Target(TP1 multiple) OR TP1 was reached on an earlier bar since entry)
Exit: short, a bar after entry AND low <= Target(TP3 multiple)
Exit: short, a bar after entry AND high >= ActiveStop AND (low > Target(TP1 multiple) OR TP1 was reached on an earlier bar since entry)
