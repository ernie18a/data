Short: EMA(close, shortest trend lookback) < EMA(close, middle trend lookback) AND EMA(close, middle trend lookback) < EMA(close, longest trend lookback) AND close < EMA(close, long-term baseline lookback) AND MACD line(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback) < MACD signal line(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short RSI threshold AND DI−(high, low, close, DI lookback) > DI+(high, low, close, DI lookback) AND ADX(high, low, close, DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close < EMA(close, higher-timeframe EMA lookback, T1 current bar sampled on chart bars)
Exit: Long, low <= close[1] - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)[1] * stop multiplier OR high >= close[1] + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)[1] * target multiplier
Exit: Short, high >= close[1] + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)[1] * stop multiplier OR low <= close[1] - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)[1] * target multiplier
