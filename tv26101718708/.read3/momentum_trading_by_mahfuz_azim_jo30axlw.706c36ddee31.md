Long: The configured entry trigger occurs on the prior chart bar (upper-channel crossing, QQE trailing-line crossing, or centerline crossing, as selected) AND all enabled long-entry filters pass on that prior bar AND, when first-in-trend-only is enabled, this is the first qualified long trigger since the last qualified short trigger. For the QQE trailing-line trigger, an enabled threshold filter also requires the QQE RSI signal to be outside the threshold channel.
Short: The configured entry trigger occurs on the prior chart bar (lower-channel crossing, QQE trailing-line crossing, or centerline crossing, as selected) AND all enabled short-entry filters pass on that prior bar AND, when first-in-trend-only is enabled, this is the first qualified short trigger since the last qualified long trigger. For the QQE trailing-line trigger, an enabled threshold filter also requires the QQE RSI signal to be outside the threshold channel.
Long-entry MA filter: When the single-ribbon branch applies, close > medium MA AND medium MA > slow MA AND fast MA > medium MA; when the second, longer ribbon is active, medium MA > scaled medium MA AND close > fast MA AND fast MA > medium MA. Fast, medium, and slow MAs have separate type and lookback parameters; the scaled medium MA uses the medium MA type and its lookback multiplied by the second-ribbon multiplier.
Short-entry MA filter: When the single-ribbon branch applies, close < medium MA AND medium MA < slow MA AND fast MA < medium MA; when the second, longer ribbon is active, medium MA < scaled medium MA AND close < fast MA AND fast MA < medium MA. Fast, medium, and slow MAs have separate type and lookback parameters; the scaled medium MA uses the medium MA type and its lookback multiplied by the second-ribbon multiplier.
Long-entry directional filter: When the single-ribbon branch applies, the medium MA is rising over the direction lookback; when the second, longer ribbon is active, both the medium MA and scaled medium MA are rising over the direction lookback AND close > medium MA.
Short-entry directional filter: When the single-ribbon branch applies, the medium MA is falling over the direction lookback; when the second, longer ribbon is active, both the medium MA and scaled medium MA are falling over the direction lookback AND close < medium MA.
