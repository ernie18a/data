Calculation: Working ATR = max(nz(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum price increment), minimum price increment).
Calculation: Candle range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); body efficiency = abs(close - open) / candle range; close location = (close - low) / candle range; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Developing profile, on confirmed chart bars, uses chart bars since the latest T1 boundary, including the current bar, capped at a maximum profile-bar lookback. Use the running session low and high until the cap is reached, then the lowest low and highest high over the capped lookback. Divide the range into a profile-row-count number of equal bins using row step max(range / row count, minimum price increment). For each bar, distribute max(volume, zero) equally across the clamped bins from its low through its high. The control is the midpoint of the first bin with maximum volume. Expand the value area from that bin by adding the adjacent bin with greater volume until included volume reaches total volume times the coverage parameter; on a tie, add the lower bin. The resulting outer edges are the raw lower and upper bounds. Raw center is their midpoint; raw half-width is max((upper bound - lower bound) / a width divisor, working ATR times a minimum-width factor). Initialize center and half-width from these raw values. On each confirmed chart bar, update each by adding a clamped step equal to (raw value - prior value) times a shared update-gain parameter. Limit center steps by max(working ATR times a center-step factor, minimum price increment times a center-step tick factor). Limit half-width expansion and contraction using separate limits, each the maximum of working ATR times its factor and minimum price increment times its tick factor. Keep half-width at least minimum price increment times a width-floor factor. Profile upper and lower bounds are center plus and minus half-width. The profile is calculated on chart bars, not T1 bars.
Calculation: Direction score = the sum of five separately parameterized weighted states: normalized directional state (plus DI minus minus DI divided by their sum, or zero if the sum is zero), alignment state (positive, negative, or zero according to EMA(close, shorter lookback) versus EMA(close, longer lookback)), momentum state (clamp(EMA(change(close), momentum lookback) / working ATR / momentum scale, negative unit, positive unit)), displacement state (clamp((close - EMA(hlc3, balance lookback)) / working ATR / displacement scale, negative unit, positive unit)), and value-position state (positive unit above the profile upper bound, negative unit below the lower bound, otherwise clamp((close - profile center) / max(profile half-width, minimum price increment), negative unit, positive unit); zero when the profile is unavailable). The shorter EMA lookback is less than the longer EMA lookback.
Calculation: Dynamic trendline = EMA(WMA(parameterized multiplier times WMA(close, shorter trend lookback) minus WMA(close, longer trend lookback), trend blend lookback), trendline smoothing lookback); the shorter trend lookback is less than the longer trend lookback. Trend slope = (dynamic trendline - dynamic trendline[1]) / working ATR; prior trend slope is trend slope[1], or zero when unavailable.
Calculation: Compressed = highest(high, compression range lookback) - lowest(low, compression range lookback) <= working ATR times a compression-range factor AND compression efficiency <= a compression-efficiency threshold. Compression efficiency = abs(close - close[compression path lag]) divided by the sum of absolute close changes across that path, or one when the path is no greater than the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Bullish response = (low < lowest(low, sweep lookback)[1] AND close > lowest(low, sweep lookback)[1] AND close > open OR close > highest(high, breakout lookback)[1] AND close > open OR profile available AND close > profile upper bound AND close[1] > profile upper bound[1] OR low <= dynamic trendline + working ATR times a trendline-proximity factor AND close > dynamic trendline AND close > open AND lower wick > upper wick AND trend slope >= negative trend-slope tolerance AND trend slope >= prior trend slope - prior-slope tolerance AND direction score > bullish reversal threshold) AND lower wick >= upper wick times a wick-ratio parameter.
Calculation: Bearish response = (high > highest(high, sweep lookback)[1] AND close < highest(high, sweep lookback)[1] AND close < open OR close < lowest(low, breakout lookback)[1] AND close < open OR profile available AND close < profile lower bound AND close[1] < profile lower bound[1] OR high >= dynamic trendline - working ATR times a trendline-proximity factor AND close < dynamic trendline AND close < open AND upper wick > lower wick AND trend slope <= trend-slope tolerance AND trend slope <= prior trend slope + prior-slope tolerance AND direction score < negative bearish reversal threshold) AND upper wick >= lower wick times the wick-ratio parameter.
Calculation: Bullish vote count = count of true conditions: close > open; close > close[1]; EMA(change(close), momentum lookback) > its prior value; EMA(close, shorter reference lookback) > EMA(close, longer reference lookback); plus DI > minus DI; direction score > a positive vote threshold; close > profile control; and body efficiency >= a body-efficiency threshold AND close location >= an upper-close threshold.
Calculation: Bearish vote count = count of true conditions: close < open; close < close[1]; EMA(change(close), momentum lookback) < its prior value; EMA(close, shorter reference lookback) < EMA(close, longer reference lookback); minus DI > plus DI; direction score < a negative vote threshold; close < profile control; and body efficiency >= the body-efficiency threshold AND close location <= a lower-close threshold.
Calculation: Cloud center = VWMA(hlc3, cloud lookback), falling back to EMA(hlc3, cloud lookback) when unavailable. Cloud deviation = max(stdev(hlc3, cloud lookback), working ATR times a deviation floor factor), with unavailable standard deviation replaced by working ATR. Effective volume is volume when available and positive, otherwise one. Cloud participation = clamp(EMA(effective volume times ((clamp((close - low) / candle range, zero, one) - a location midpoint parameter) times a direction multiplier parameter, cloud participation lookback) / EMA(effective volume, cloud participation lookback), negative unit, positive unit), or zero when the denominator is no greater than a minimum participation denominator. Cloud slope = clamp(nz((cloud center - cloud center[1]) / working ATR, zero), negative unit, positive unit). Cloud displacement = clamp((close - cloud center) / cloud deviation, negative unit, positive unit). Cloud direction is the weighted sum of cloud participation, cloud slope, and cloud displacement using separate weight parameters. Cloud basis = EMA(hlc3, cloud basis lookback); cloud basis slope = nz((cloud basis - cloud basis[1]) / working ATR, zero).
Calculation: Bullish cloud raw condition = cloud direction >= a positive cloud threshold AND cloud basis slope >= negative cloud-slope tolerance AND close >= cloud center.
Calculation: Bearish cloud raw condition = cloud direction <= a negative cloud threshold AND cloud basis slope <= cloud-slope tolerance AND close <= cloud center.
Long: position side is not long AND confirmed chart bar AND profile available AND NOT compressed AND (volume unavailable or volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) >= a participation threshold) AND bullish response AND bullish vote count >= a minimum vote count AND direction score >= a positive entry threshold AND bullish vote count > bearish vote count.
Short: position side is not short AND confirmed chart bar AND profile available AND NOT compressed AND (volume unavailable or volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) >= the participation threshold) AND bearish response AND bearish vote count >= the minimum vote count AND direction score <= a negative entry threshold AND bearish vote count > bullish vote count.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND bullish cloud raw condition AND bullish cloud raw condition[1].
Short: confirmed chart bar AND bearish cloud raw condition AND bearish cloud raw condition[1].
Long: dynamic trendline >= dynamic trendline[1].
Short: dynamic trendline < dynamic trendline[1].
