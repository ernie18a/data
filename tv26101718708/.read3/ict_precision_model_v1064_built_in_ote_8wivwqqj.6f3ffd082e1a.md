Expire any nonflat state when its age exceeds P_maxSetupBars. In bullish manipulation state, replace the reference open on each bearish bar with low >= manipulation low AND high <= manipulation high * P_bullReferenceHighFactor; in bearish manipulation state, replace it on each bullish bar with high <= manipulation high AND low >= manipulation low * P_bearReferenceLowFactor. A bullish shift is close > the reference open AND close > open; a bearish shift is close < the reference open AND close < open. Shift to displacement-waiting state on that condition. From bullish displacement-waiting state, require close > open, strong body, and close > Highest(high, P_swing)[1], then save high and low and enter bullish FVG-waiting state; bearish requires close < open, strong body, and close < Lowest(low, P_swing)[1].
