Calculation: Gated ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) if available, otherwise max(high-low, minimum price increment), minimum price increment)
Calculation: Engine direction = on each confirmed chart bar, 1 if EMA(source, shorter lookback) > EMA(source, longer lookback) AND close > EMA(source, longer lookback) + Gated ATR * gate multiplier AND close > VWAP(source) AND EMA(source, shorter lookback) > its previous chart-bar value (using itself if unavailable); otherwise -1 if EMA(source, shorter lookback) < EMA(source, longer lookback) AND close < EMA(source, longer lookback) - Gated ATR * gate multiplier AND close < VWAP(source) AND EMA(source, shorter lookback) < its previous chart-bar value (using itself if unavailable); otherwise its previous value, initially 0; unchanged on unconfirmed bars
Long: confirmed chart bar AND engine direction = 1 AND previous chart-bar engine direction != 1
Short: confirmed chart bar AND engine direction = -1 AND previous chart-bar engine direction != -1
Calculation: Plan entry price = close on setup bar
Calculation: Long risk = max(abs(plan entry price - min(lowest(low, swing lookback) on setup bar, plan entry price - Gated ATR on setup bar * ATR stop multiplier)), minimum price increment * risk-floor multiple)
Calculation: Short risk = max(abs(plan entry price - max(highest(high, swing lookback) on setup bar, plan entry price + Gated ATR on setup bar * ATR stop multiplier)), minimum price increment * risk-floor multiple)
Exit: Long, confirmed chart bar after setup bar AND ((low <= plan entry price - Long risk AND stop has not previously been touched since setup) OR (high >= plan entry price + Long risk * target 1 R multiple AND target 1 has not previously been touched since setup) OR (high >= plan entry price + Long risk * target 2 R multiple AND target 2 has not previously been touched since setup) OR (high >= plan entry price + Long risk * target 3 R multiple AND target 3 has not previously been touched since setup))
