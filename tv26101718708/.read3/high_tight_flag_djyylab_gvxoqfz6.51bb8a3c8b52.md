Calculation: flag pole low = earliest low attaining the minimum low among the maximum-pole-length bars before a candidate pole top
Calculation: flag pole top = the candidate top bar's high, strictly above every high from the flag pole low through the preceding bar
Long: On completed chart bars, after a flag confirms, signal on its first subsequent bar with high > its flag pole top, unless the flag was previously invalidated or expired; if that bar also has low < flag pole top × (1 − maximum flag depth), signal only when the flag pole top is no farther from open than the invalidation level. Confirm the flag on the first completed bar when no flag is active, choosing the most recent qualifying candidate top: the number of bars from flag pole low to top is between minimum and maximum pole lengths, inclusive; flag pole top / flag pole low − 1 >= minimum pole gain; the number of bars after the top is between minimum and maximum flag lengths, inclusive; every such bar's high <= the top and their lowest low >= top × (1 − maximum flag depth). If volume is available, require mean pole volume > 0 and mean flag volume <= flag-volume multiple × mean pole volume; also require maximum pole volume >= pole-volume multiple × mean volume over the pre-pole volume lookback when that many preceding bars exist and their mean volume is positive. Skip volume conditions when volume is unavailable. A flag expires after more than the maximum flag length without a breakout, with a breakout checked first on that bar.
Exit: Long, within the failure-window bars after its breakout, close <= that flag's pole top
