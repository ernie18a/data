Calculation: High episode = consecutive confirmed chart bars with RSI(close, P_RSI) >= P_OB; retain the highest high and highest RSI and their bars until the first bar with RSI(close, P_RSI) < P_OB; update only on strictly higher values, retaining the earlier bar on ties.
Calculation: Low episode = consecutive confirmed chart bars with RSI(close, P_RSI) <= P_OS; retain the lowest low and lowest RSI and their bars until the first bar with RSI(close, P_RSI) > P_OS; update only on strictly lower values, retaining the earlier bar on ties.
Calculation: Exhaustion score(side) = enabled depth score (P_depthWeight if the high episode peak RSI < P_deepLevel or the low episode minimum RSI > RSI scale maximum - P_deepLevel, otherwise P_deepWeight) + enabled volume score P_volumeWeight when volume at the extreme bar > SMA(volume, P_volumeMA) at that bar + enabled fade score P_fadeWeight when completion bar index - peak RSI bar index <= P_fadeWindow + enabled divergence score P_divergenceWeight when the high episode extreme is higher and its peak RSI lower than those of the previous completed high episode, or the low episode extreme is lower and its minimum RSI higher than those of the previous completed low episode + enabled wick score P_wickWeight when the high episode upper wick or low episode lower wick > P_wickBodyRatio * body; body = abs(close - open), upper wick = high - max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) - low; compare divergence with the previous completed episode of the same side, whether or not it formed a zone.
Calculation: Exhaustion zone(side) = at episode completion, form a high zone from the episode extreme high to max(open, close) on its extreme bar, or a low zone from min(open, close) on its extreme bar to the episode extreme low; use ATR(P_ATR) on the completion bar to pad thickness below P_minThicknessATR * ATR(P_ATR), and reject if the resulting height > P_maxHeightATR * ATR(P_ATR); reject if the extreme bar is more than P_history bars before completion; create only if that side's zones are enabled and score filtering is disabled or Exhaustion score(side) >= P_minScore; retain at most P_maxZones newest zones per side, removing the oldest on overflow; a zone breaks when close > its top for a high zone or close < its bottom for a low zone; otherwise remove it once age > P_zoneMaxAge; its first touch is a close within its inclusive bounds before removal, with age <= P_zoneMaxAge and no earlier touch.
Long: a low exhaustion zone forms OR an untouched low exhaustion zone's first touch occurs OR close > the top of an existing high exhaustion zone.
Short: a high exhaustion zone forms OR an untouched high exhaustion zone's first touch occurs OR close < the bottom of an existing low exhaustion zone.
