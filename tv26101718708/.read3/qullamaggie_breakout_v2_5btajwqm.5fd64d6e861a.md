Calculation: swing stop = while flat, the latest confirmed PivotLow(chart low, L, L); while long, update to the latest confirmed PivotLow(chart low, L, L) until its previous-bar value is at least the entry price, then hold the prior swing stop
Calculation: MA trail level = the latest chart or T1-bar low, according to the selected trailing-stop timeframe, where low crosses below the selected first or second moving average since the most recent flat-to-long transition; the moving average is EMA or SMA(close, its separate lookback, T1, current still-forming bar)
Calculation: exit stop = the MA trail level if it exists, swing stop is at or below it, and the previous chart close is above both the MA trail level and the selected moving average; otherwise the swing stop
Long: chart high >= the latest confirmed PivotHigh(chart high, H, H) AND datetime > start-date parameter AND datetime < end-date parameter AND (MA filter disabled OR latest confirmed PivotHigh value > EMA or SMA(close, longest moving-average lookback, T1, current still-forming bar)) AND (ADR filter disabled OR latest confirmed PivotHigh value - prior-bar swing stop < ADR percentage parameter * SMA((T2 high - T2 low) / abs(T2 low) * 100, ADR lookback, T2, current still-forming bar) / 100)
Exit: long, chart low <= exit stop
