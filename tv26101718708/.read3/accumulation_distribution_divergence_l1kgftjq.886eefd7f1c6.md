Long: A/D oscillator crosses above its balance level
Short: A/D oscillator crosses below its balance level
Long: On a newly confirmed pivot low with equal left/right pivot lookback, the current pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low and the oscillator value at the current pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot, with pivot separation within the pivot-gap lookback; OR the current pivot low is above the preceding confirmed pivot low and the oscillator value at the current pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot, within the same pivot-gap lookback
