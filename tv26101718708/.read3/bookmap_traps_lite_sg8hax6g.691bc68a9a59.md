Calculation: T1 volume rate = volume / (high - low) on each T1 intrabar when high > low, otherwise 0; use T1 intrabars available within the current chart bar, including a still-forming intrabar when available.
Calculation: Effective T1 volume = T1 volume rate × clamp(T1 volume rate / SMA(T1 volume rate, volume-rate lookback parameter, T1, current intrabar), lower ratio parameter, upper ratio parameter) when that SMA is positive; otherwise T1 volume rate.
Calculation: Raw trapped-buyer volume = sum over current-chart-bar T1 intrabars of effective T1 volume × upper-wick overlap / T1 range × clamped T1 close position, where upper-wick overlap = max(min(T1 high, chart high) - max(T1 low, max(chart open, chart close)), 0), T1 range = T1 high - T1 low, and clamped T1 close position = clamp((T1 close - T1 low) / T1 range, lower strength parameter, upper strength parameter); include only positive-range, positive-rate intrabars with positive overlap when chart range > 0, chart upper wick / chart range × 100% >= minimum wick-percent parameter, and either deep-wick-only is off or overlap midpoint > chart body top + chart upper wick / 2.
Calculation: Raw trapped-seller volume = sum over current-chart-bar T1 intrabars of effective T1 volume × lower-wick overlap / T1 range × (1 - clamped T1 close position), where lower-wick overlap = max(min(T1 high, min(chart open, chart close)) - max(T1 low, chart low), 0), T1 range = T1 high - T1 low, and clamped T1 close position = clamp((T1 close - T1 low) / T1 range, lower strength parameter, upper strength parameter); include only positive-range, positive-rate intrabars with positive overlap when chart range > 0, chart lower wick / chart range × 100% >= minimum wick-percent parameter, and either deep-wick-only is off or overlap midpoint < chart low + chart lower wick / 2.
Calculation: Trap pattern vector = ((close - open) / safe ATR, upper wick / chart range, lower wick / chart range, (close - low) / chart range, chart range / safe ATR, raw trapped-buyer volume, raw trapped-seller volume), where safe ATR = ATR(ATR lookback parameter) if positive, otherwise ATR fallback parameter; for zero chart range use 0 for both wick ratios and range/ATR and 0.5 for close position.
Calculation: Side-specific kNN success fraction = successful outcomes / valid neighbors among up to neighbor-count parameter nearest prior chart-bar pattern vectors in the training-window parameter of prior chart bars; normalize each feature difference by its range across training vectors, floored at the distance-floor parameter, and rank by the sum of squared normalized differences. Buyer-trap candidates require a prior upper-wick trap and have a successful outcome when the next chart bar closes below that trap bar's close; seller-trap candidates require a prior lower-wick trap and have a successful outcome when the next chart bar closes above that trap bar's close. Score only when training-vector count and valid-neighbor count are each at least the minimum-sample parameter.
Calculation: ML-adjusted trapped-buyer volume = raw trapped-buyer volume × side-specific buyer kNN success fraction × ML volume multiplier parameter when ML is enabled and the buyer score is available; otherwise raw trapped-buyer volume. ML-adjusted trapped-seller volume = raw trapped-seller volume × side-specific seller kNN success fraction × ML volume multiplier parameter when ML is enabled and the seller score is available; otherwise raw trapped-seller volume.
Calculation: Display threshold = percentile_linear_interpolation(cached trap volumes, 100% - top-trap-share parameter).
Long: Current chart bar is within the maximum-lookback parameter of the latest chart bar AND ML-adjusted trapped-seller volume >= minimum trapped-volume parameter AND ML-adjusted trapped-seller volume >= display threshold.
Short: Current chart bar is within the maximum-lookback parameter of the latest chart bar AND ML-adjusted trapped-buyer volume >= minimum trapped-volume parameter AND ML-adjusted trapped-buyer volume >= display threshold.
