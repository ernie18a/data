Calculation: Bullish trend = EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback).
Calculation: Bearish trend = EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback).
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR(ATR lookback) / close converted to percent when close is nonzero, else zero.
Calculation: ATR activity = ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percentage parameter.
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume > SMA(volume, volume baseline lookback) * volume expansion multiplier OR (standard deviation of volume over the volume baseline lookback > 0 AND (volume - SMA(volume, volume baseline lookback)) / standard deviation of volume over the volume baseline lookback > volume z-score threshold).
Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(close position * close-position weight + body direction * body-direction weight + body power * (body direction - neutral body-direction value) * body-power weight + buy-share offset, buy-share floor, buy-share ceiling), where close position = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), body power = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment), and body direction is the bullish-body value when close > open, bearish-body value when close < open, and neutral-body value otherwise.
Calculation: Pressure delta = rolling sum over the pressure lookback of volume * (estimated buy share - (one - estimated buy share)), divided by rolling sum of volume over the pressure lookback and converted to percent when that denominator is positive, else zero.
Calculation: VWAP distance in ATR = (close - VWAP((high + low + close) / three)) / ATR(ATR lookback) when ATR is positive, else zero.
Calculation: VWAP discount condition = VWAP distance in ATR < negative auction-band parameter AND close > open.
Calculation: VWAP premium condition = VWAP distance in ATR > auction-band parameter AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = (min(open, close) - low) > max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) * wick-to-body parameter AND close > open AND close > low + max(high - low, minimum price increment) * bullish close-location parameter.
Calculation: Bearish rejection = (high - max(open, close)) > max(abs(close - open), minimum price increment) * wick-to-body parameter AND close < open AND close < low + max(high - low, minimum price increment) * bearish close-location parameter.
Calculation: Downside sweep = low < Lowest(low excluding the current bar, structure lookback) AND close > Lowest(low excluding the current bar, structure lookback) AND close > open.
Calculation: Upside sweep = high > Highest(high excluding the current bar, structure lookback) AND close < Highest(high excluding the current bar, structure lookback) AND close < open.
Calculation: Compression = (highest high over the compression lookback - lowest low over the compression lookback) / close converted to percent when close is nonzero, else zero, is less than ATR percentage * range-compression parameter AND abs(EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) - EMA(close, longer EMA lookback)) < ATR(ATR lookback) * EMA-compression parameter.
Calculation: Bullish expansion break = previous bar was compressed AND close > Highest(high excluding the current bar, expansion lookback) AND volume expansion.
Calculation: Bearish expansion break = previous bar was compressed AND close < Lowest(low excluding the current bar, expansion lookback) AND volume expansion.
Calculation: Market regime = bullish when bullish trend AND close >= VWAP((high + low + close) / three), bearish when bearish trend AND close <= that VWAP, and neutral otherwise; previous regime initializes as neutral.
Calculation: Bullish continuation probability = (transition prior count + count since initialization of bars whose previous regime was bullish and current regime is bullish) / (two transition prior counts + those bullish-to-bullish bars + bars whose previous regime was bullish and current regime is bearish), converted to percent; use the neutral-probability parameter if the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Bearish continuation probability = (transition prior count + count since initialization of bars whose previous regime was bearish and current regime is bearish) / (two transition prior counts + those bearish-to-bearish bars + bars whose previous regime was bearish and current regime is bullish), converted to percent; use the neutral-probability parameter if the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Neutral-regime break probability = the larger of (transition prior count + count since initialization of neutral-to-bullish transitions) and (transition prior count + count since initialization of neutral-to-bearish transitions), divided by two transition prior counts plus both transition counts and converted to percent; use the neutral-probability parameter if the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish score = clamp the sum of trend weight when bullish trend, VWAP-side weight when close >= VWAP((high + low + close) / three), ATR-activity weight when ATR activity, DMI weight when ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold and +DI > -DI, RSI-momentum weight when RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI midpoint and RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= its previous-bar value, volume-expansion weight when volume expansion, strong-pressure weight when pressure delta > strong-pressure threshold otherwise weak-pressure weight when pressure delta > neutral-pressure boundary, CVD-alignment weight when CVD(volume * (estimated buy share - (one - estimated buy share))) > its EMA(CVD signal lookback) and that EMA >= its previous-bar value, auction-location weight when VWAP discount condition, rejection weight when bullish rejection, sweep weight when downside sweep, expansion-break weight when bullish expansion break, continuation weight when bullish continuation probability > continuation threshold otherwise flat-break weight when neutral-regime break probability > flat-break threshold AND market regime is bullish, and zero for each unmet condition, between the score floor and score ceiling.
Calculation: Bearish score = clamp the sum of trend weight when bearish trend, VWAP-side weight when close <= VWAP((high + low + close) / three), ATR-activity weight when ATR activity, DMI weight when ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold and -DI > +DI, RSI-momentum weight when RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint and RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= its previous-bar value, volume-expansion weight when volume expansion, strong-pressure weight when pressure delta < negative strong-pressure threshold otherwise weak-pressure weight when pressure delta < negative neutral-pressure boundary, CVD-alignment weight when CVD(volume * (estimated buy share - (one - estimated buy share))) < its EMA(CVD signal lookback) and that EMA <= its previous-bar value, auction-location weight when VWAP premium condition, rejection weight when bearish rejection, sweep weight when upside sweep, expansion-break weight when bearish expansion break, continuation weight when bearish continuation probability > continuation threshold otherwise flat-break weight when neutral-regime break probability > flat-break threshold AND market regime is bearish, and zero for each unmet condition, between the score floor and score ceiling.
Long: (confirmation parameter disabled OR current bar is confirmed) AND (no previous long or short signal OR current bar index - most recent long or short signal bar index > signal cooldown parameter) AND ATR activity AND (trend filter disabled OR bullish trend) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND bullish score >= setup-score threshold AND bullish score > bearish score + score-separation parameter.
Short: (confirmation parameter disabled OR current bar is confirmed) AND (no previous long or short signal OR current bar index - most recent long or short signal bar index > signal cooldown parameter) AND ATR activity AND (trend filter disabled OR bearish trend) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND bearish score >= setup-score threshold AND bearish score > bullish score + score-separation parameter.
