Calculation: RSI B = RSI(shared selected source, independent lookback B) on chart bars
Calculation: RSI C = RSI(shared selected source, independent lookback C) on chart bars
Calculation: RSI D = RSI(separate selected source, independent lookback D) on bars of the chart timeframe or T1, sampled on chart bars from the last completed selected-timeframe bar when confirmed-only is enabled and from the forming bar otherwise
Long: RSI A, RSI B, and RSI C are each at or below the shared oversold threshold, and, if RSI D is enabled and required for confluence, RSI D is at or below the custom fourth oversold threshold when enabled or the shared oversold threshold otherwise, and the RSI D turning filter is disabled or RSI D > RSI D[1]; confluence signals are enabled and first-alignment-only is disabled or this same alignment was false on the preceding chart bar; OR, for any of RSI A, RSI B, or RSI C whose display and divergence detection are enabled, or RSI D whose enable, plot, and divergence detection are enabled, a pivot low of that RSI exists, the number of bars since the preceding-bar pivot-low event is within the minimum and maximum range parameters, and either low[right] < valuewhen(not na(pivotlow(RSI, left, right)), low[right], 1) AND RSI[right] > valuewhen(not na(pivotlow(RSI, left, right)), RSI[right], 1), or low[right] > valuewhen(not na(pivotlow(RSI, left, right)), low[right], 1) AND RSI[right] < valuewhen(not na(pivotlow(RSI, left, right)), RSI[right], 1)
