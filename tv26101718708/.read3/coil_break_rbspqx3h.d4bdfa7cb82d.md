Calculation: trend-qualified = trend filter disabled OR selected SMA/EMA(close, trend lookback) is unavailable OR close > selected SMA/EMA(close, trend lookback)
Calculation: inside-bar state = an inside bar matches the selected definition: high <= previous high AND low >= previous low; OR max(open, close) <= max(open[1], close[1]) AND min(open, close) >= min(open[1], close[1]); OR high <= max(open[1], close[1]) AND low >= min(open[1], close[1]); require strict inequalities instead when strict edges are selected. Each qualifying bar stores its high, low, previous bar high, and formation bar, resetting any prior setup. A close below the stored low cancels it; otherwise it remains active through the selected breakout-window length after formation.
Calculation: bull-flag state = a pole starts or resets when high >= highest(high, pole lookback) AND high - lowest(low, pole lookback) >= the selected minimum pole size, measured either as ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum ATR pole size or lowest(low, pole lookback) × minimum pole percentage / 100. Store the pole high, pole low, and SMA(volume, pole lookback). On later bars, track the lowest low and highest high since the pole; the breakout ceiling uses earlier flag bars, excluding the current bar. Compute flag volume average from completed bars since the pole. Invalidate when the tracked low < pole high - (pole high - pole low) × maximum retracement percentage / 100, or flag age > maximum flag length. The flag is active after its pole bar through the maximum flag length; its volume check passes when disabled, volume or either average is unavailable, or flag volume average < pole volume average.
Calculation: tight-box state = tight when highest(high, tight-window) - lowest(low, tight-window) <= either ATR(ATR lookback) × maximum ATR box height or lowest(low, tight-window) × maximum box-height percentage / 100, according to the selected unit. A fresh tight window is tight now and not tight on the previous bar. When fresh, form a box only if no box is stored or the prior box is done; if the optional near-high filter is enabled, require the window high >= highest(high, maximum box length × near-high window multiple) × (1 - near-high tolerance percentage / 100), unless that reference is unavailable. Store the window high and low without updating them. A close below the stored low or box age plus tight-window length > maximum box length ends the box; breakout is checked against the previously stored box before those current-bar invalidations.
Calculation: armed setup = trend-qualified AND no entry trigger fires on this bar AND at least one enabled setup is pending: an uncanceled inside-bar setup within its breakout window, a non-invalidated bull flag after its pole bar through its maximum flag length, or a stored tight box not ended by its current-bar invalidation checks.
Long: inside-bar setup enabled AND inside-bar state active after its formation bar AND current bar is not an inside bar AND (when close breakout is selected, close > selected stored inside-bar high or previous bar high; otherwise high > that level) AND trend-qualified AND (closed-bar-only triggering is disabled OR the bar is closed)
Long: bull-flag setup enabled AND bull-flag state active AND flag age >= minimum flag length AND (when flag-high breakout is selected, close > earlier flag-bar high, falling back to pole high if unavailable; otherwise close > pole high; use high instead of close when intrabar breakout is selected) AND (volume-dry-up filter disabled OR volume or either average is unavailable OR flag volume average < pole volume average) AND trend-qualified AND (closed-bar-only triggering is disabled OR the bar is closed)
Long: tight-box setup enabled AND a previously stored box is active before current-bar invalidation checks AND (when close breakout is selected, close > stored box high; otherwise high > stored box high) AND trend-qualified AND (closed-bar-only triggering is disabled OR the bar is closed)
