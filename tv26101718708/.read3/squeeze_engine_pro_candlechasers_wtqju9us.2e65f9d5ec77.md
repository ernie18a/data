Calculation: Raw momentum = Momentum(close, Classic momentum lookback) in Classic; otherwise, the linear-regression endpoint over the channel lookback of close − ((highest(high, channel lookback) + lowest(low, channel lookback))/2 + SMA(close, channel lookback))/2.
Calculation: Momentum acceleration = EMA((raw momentum − raw momentum[1]) − (raw momentum[1] − raw momentum[2]), acceleration-smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Squeeze tier = Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channel at the tight width (deep), else normal width (medium), else wide width (loose), else none; Bollinger Bands use SMA(close, BB lookback) ± mode-specific BB multiplier × StDev(close, BB lookback); Keltner Channel uses SMA(close, KC lookback) ± SMA(true range or high − low, KC lookback) × width; Classic uses one shared squeeze lookback and tests only medium width, while PRO/Early use separate BB and KC lookbacks and test any width, with wide > normal > tight.
Calculation: Fresh release = not in squeeze, a squeeze release occurred within the recent-release lookback, and the immediately preceding squeeze lasted at least the minimum squeeze duration; release means the current chart bar is out of squeeze and the prior chart bar was in squeeze.
Calculation: Bull trend = chart close > EMA(close, trend lookback) and (Classic or higher-timeframe filter disabled or the close of the last completed T1 bar > EMA(close, same trend lookback, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar)).
Calculation: Bear trend = chart close < EMA(close, trend lookback) and (Classic or higher-timeframe filter disabled or the close of the last completed T1 bar < EMA(close, same trend lookback, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar)).
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback).
Calculation: DMI = DMI(positive-DI lookback, ADX-smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Long/short score = nearest-integer rounded percentage of directional points divided by maximum available points; directional points are squeeze points when squeeze scoring is active, trend weight when the corresponding bull/bear trend holds, full momentum weight when momentum is rising/falling and acceleration is positive/negative or slope-only weight when merely rising/falling, relative-volume points, and directional ADX weight when enabled and ADX ≥ its minimum with +DI > −DI for long or −DI > +DI for short. Squeeze points are zero outside squeeze/release, use the deep/medium/loose weight in squeeze, and on a fresh release use the extended-release weight if completed duration ≥ release-duration multiplier × minimum squeeze duration, otherwise the shorter-release weight. Relative-volume points are zero unless its score filter is enabled and volume and its average are valid and positive; then use the full weight if relative volume ≥ its minimum, the partial weight if relative volume ≥ its minimum × partial-volume fraction, else zero. Maximum available points are base maximum weight plus maximum squeeze weight when squeeze scoring is active, maximum volume weight when relative-volume scoring is active, and maximum ADX weight when the ADX filter is enabled.
Calculation: Entry gate = chart bar confirmed, position flat, ATR(ATR lookback) > 0, and more than the cooldown lookback bars have elapsed since the last exit.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = max(signal-bar ATR × Classic stop multiple in Classic or PRO/Early stop multiple otherwise, instrument minimum price increment); long initial stop = entry price − risk distance; short initial stop = entry price + risk distance; entry price is the signal-bar close.
Calculation: PRO/Early target = entry price ± initial risk distance × reward-to-risk multiple, positive for long and negative for short.
Calculation: Optional PRO/Early trailing stop = long max(current stop, max(entry price, highest high on bars after entry) − current ATR × trail multiple), or short min(current stop, min(entry price, lowest low on bars after entry) + current ATR × trail multiple); update after that bar’s exit check and apply from the next bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, Entry gate holds, Classic engine is selected, a squeeze release occurs, the squeeze episode has not already had an entry, and either Classic slope mode is enabled and raw momentum is rising, or it is disabled and raw momentum > 0.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, Entry gate holds, Classic engine is selected, a squeeze release occurs, the squeeze episode has not already had an entry, and either Classic slope mode is enabled and raw momentum is falling, or it is disabled and raw momentum < 0.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, Entry gate holds, engine is PRO/Early, setup is eligible (no-squeeze entries enabled or fresh release or Early mode with squeeze duration ≥ minimum squeeze duration), zero-side requirement is disabled or raw momentum > 0, bull trend holds, relative-volume filter is disabled or volume/average is invalid or relative volume ≥ its minimum, ADX filter is disabled or ADX ≥ its minimum and +DI > −DI, impulse holds (Early mode: raw momentum rising; otherwise stronger-confirmation setting: raw momentum rises on each of the configured slope-confirmation intervals and acceleration > 0; otherwise: raw momentum rising and acceleration > 0), and long score ≥ score threshold. Also require either the no-squeeze path (no-squeeze entries enabled, not in squeeze, not fresh release, and prior chart bar was not long-ready) or no earlier entry in the current squeeze episode.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, Entry gate holds, engine is PRO/Early, setup is eligible (no-squeeze entries enabled or fresh release or Early mode with squeeze duration ≥ minimum squeeze duration), zero-side requirement is disabled or raw momentum < 0, bear trend holds, relative-volume filter is disabled or volume/average is invalid or relative volume ≥ its minimum, ADX filter is disabled or ADX ≥ its minimum and −DI > +DI, impulse holds (Early mode: raw momentum falling; otherwise stronger-confirmation setting: raw momentum falls on each of the configured slope-confirmation intervals and acceleration < 0; otherwise: raw momentum falling and acceleration < 0), and short score ≥ score threshold. Also require either the no-squeeze path (no-squeeze entries enabled, not in squeeze, not fresh release, and prior chart bar was not short-ready) or no earlier entry in the current squeeze episode; if long entry also qualifies, suppress short entry.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, squeeze duration ≥ minimum squeeze duration, raw momentum is rising and exceeds its value at the configured earlier lookback, bull trend holds, and this early bullish setup condition was false on the prior chart bar.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, squeeze duration ≥ minimum squeeze duration, raw momentum is falling and is below its value at the configured earlier lookback, bear trend holds, and this early bearish setup condition was false on the prior chart bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with a confirmed pivot low using the configured left and right pivot lookbacks, the pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low, raw momentum at the pivot is above raw momentum at the preceding pivot, and the pivot-bar distance is at most the maximum pivot distance.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with a confirmed pivot high using the configured left and right pivot lookbacks, the pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high, raw momentum at the pivot is below raw momentum at the preceding pivot, and the pivot-bar distance is at most the maximum pivot distance.
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, low ≤ current stop, or in PRO/Early high ≥ target, or in Classic raw momentum falls for the configured Classic fade lookback, or in PRO/Early with fade exit enabled raw momentum falls for the configured PRO/Early fade lookback.
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, high ≥ current stop, or in PRO/Early low ≤ target, or in Classic raw momentum rises for the configured Classic fade lookback, or in PRO/Early with fade exit enabled raw momentum rises for the configured PRO/Early fade lookback.
