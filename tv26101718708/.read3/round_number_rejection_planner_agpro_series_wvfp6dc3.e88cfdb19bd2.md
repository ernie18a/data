Calculation: Round step = configured manual step, or the step paired with the first satisfied descending abs(close) price threshold among seven thresholds, with the eighth step as fallback
Calculation: Round level = round(close / round step) * round step
Calculation: Active ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) when available, otherwise true range, minimum tick)
Calculation: Round band width = Active ATR * band-width ATR multiple
Calculation: Interaction count = count over the reaction lookback of bars where high >= Round level - Round band width and low <= Round level + Round band width
Calculation: Wick quality(side) = min(max((side-directed wick / max(high - low, minimum tick)) / wick saturation, 0), 1) * wick score weight, where side-directed wick is min(open, close) - low for long and high - max(open, close) for short
Calculation: Close quality(side) = min(max((side-directed close distance / Active ATR) / close-distance saturation, 0), 1) * close score weight, where side-directed close distance is close - Round level for long and Round level - close for short
Calculation: Failed reference = current distance from Round level / Active ATR <= maximum active distance AND abs(Round level - Round level[1]) <= max(minimum tick * tick tolerance, Round step * step tolerance) AND ((close > Round level + invalidation buffer AND close[1] < Round level - invalidation buffer) OR (close < Round level - invalidation buffer AND close[1] > Round level + invalidation buffer)), where invalidation buffer = Active ATR * invalidation-buffer ATR multiple
Calculation: Rejection score(side) = min(max(Wick quality(side) + Close quality(side) + distance score + interaction score + volume score + volatility score, 0), score ceiling), where distance score = min(max(1 - abs(close - Round level) / (Active ATR * maximum active distance), 0), 1) * distance score weight; interaction score = min(Interaction count / interaction saturation, 1) * interaction score weight; volume score = relative-volume weight * min(max(relative volume / relative-volume saturation, 0), 1) when enabled, otherwise the disabled-volume score, with relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) when that average is positive and available, otherwise 1; volatility score = min(Round step / (Active ATR * volatility divisor), 1) * volatility score weight
Calculation: Failure risk(side) = min(max((Failed reference ? failed-reference penalty : 0) + (score ceiling - Close quality(side)) * close-risk weight + min(Interaction count, risk interaction cap) * interaction-risk weight, 0), score ceiling)
Calculation: Required score = max(monitor score + monitor margin, the confirmation-mode threshold, where the lower threshold is max(base ready score - faster-mode adjustment, score floor), the middle threshold is the base ready score, and the higher threshold is min(base ready score + stricter-mode adjustment, score ceiling))
Long: bar_index > max(reaction lookback, volume lookback, ATR lookback) + warm-up bars AND Active ATR > 0 AND Round step > 0 AND abs(close - Round level) / Active ATR <= maximum active distance AND high >= Round level - Round band width AND low <= Round level + Round band width AND close > Round level + Round band width * close-location fraction AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= minimum wick fraction AND Rejection score(long) >= Required score AND Failure risk(long) < failure-risk threshold
Short: bar_index > max(reaction lookback, volume lookback, ATR lookback) + warm-up bars AND Active ATR > 0 AND Round step > 0 AND abs(close - Round level) / Active ATR <= maximum active distance AND high >= Round level - Round band width AND low <= Round level + Round band width AND close < Round level - Round band width * close-location fraction AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= minimum wick fraction AND Rejection score(short) >= Required score AND Failure risk(short) < failure-risk threshold
