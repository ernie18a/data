Calculation: supply profile S(b) = sum over the last lookback bars of supply strength times smear weight added to bin b; supply strength = volume[i] × range[i] × ((lookback − i) / lookback)^age-decay power × (upper wick[i] × wick weight + abs(close[i] − open[i]) × body weight × supply bias), where range[i] = high[i] − low[i], upper wick[i] = max(high[i] − max(open[i], close[i]), 0), and supply bias is 1 when close[i] ≤ open[i] and counterbar bias otherwise; assign each strength around floor((clamp(supply anchor, lowest low, lowest low + bin step × bin count) − lowest low) / bin step), where supply anchor is high[i] − wick-anchor fraction × upper wick[i] when upper wick[i] > 0 and high[i] − fallback-anchor fraction × range[i] otherwise, bin step = max(highest high − lowest low, minimum price increment × bin count) / bin count, and smear weight is exp(−(abs(offset) / smear radius)^2) for valid offsets from −smear radius to +smear radius, or 1 at offset 0 when smear radius is zero; skip zero-range bars.
Calculation: demand profile D(b) = sum over the last lookback bars of demand strength times smear weight added to bin b; demand strength = volume[i] × range[i] × ((lookback − i) / lookback)^age-decay power × (lower wick[i] × wick weight + abs(close[i] − open[i]) × body weight × demand bias), where range[i] = high[i] − low[i], lower wick[i] = max(min(open[i], close[i]) − low[i], 0), and demand bias is 1 when close[i] ≥ open[i] and counterbar bias otherwise; assign each strength around floor((clamp(demand anchor, lowest low, lowest low + bin step × bin count) − lowest low) / bin step), where demand anchor is low[i] + wick-anchor fraction × lower wick[i] when lower wick[i] > 0 and low[i] + fallback-anchor fraction × range[i] otherwise, using the same bin step and smear weights as the supply profile; skip zero-range bars.
Calculation: pressure imbalance = (sum over bins b of normalized demand profile D(b) − sum over bins b of normalized supply profile S(b)) / (sum over bins b of normalized demand profile D(b) + sum over bins b of normalized supply profile S(b)), where each profile is divided by its maximum bin value, or is zero when that maximum is zero; pressure imbalance is zero when its denominator is zero.
Long: there exists a bin b with D(b) > 0 OR pressure imbalance > bias threshold
Short: there exists a bin b with S(b) > 0 OR pressure imbalance < −bias threshold
