Calculation: Selected-cycle bias = at each selected-cycle rollover, set bullish if the last completed cycle closes above the preceding completed cycle high, or closes inside its range while both its high and low are lower; set bearish if it closes below the preceding low, or closes inside that range while both its high and low are higher; retain the prior bias when the completed cycle is inside the preceding range; otherwise set neutral. While the selected cycle forms, invalidate bullish bias if its low <= the preceding completed low, and invalidate bearish bias if its high >= the preceding completed high.
Calculation: Cycle SMT = compare the current forming cycle extreme with the preceding completed cycle extreme for the traded instrument and each configured correlated asset. For direct correlation, high or low extremes must move in opposite directions; for inverse correlation, compare the traded instrument high with the asset low, or the traded instrument low with the asset high, and require movement in the same direction. The paired extremes cannot both be unchanged. Include higher-cycle SMT when its extreme equals the main-cycle extreme, and lower-cycle or T1/T2 SMT when its current or preceding extreme aligns with the main-cycle extreme, including a prior divergence retained at that extreme. T1/T2 values are taken from their still-forming bars on chart bars.
Calculation: Cycle sweep = the current forming cycle trades beyond the preceding completed cycle high or low and its close returns through that level; apply the same condition to correlated-asset cycle prices and closes.
Long: Selected-cycle bias is bullish.
Short: Selected-cycle bias is bearish.
Long: A first bullish CISD confirmation, using the last completed chart-bar close, AND (main-cycle SSL SMT OR at least two of main-cycle low sweep, low SMT, and an unfilled bullish main-cycle FVG tap OR a correlated asset has a low sweep and low SMT on the same pair if directly correlated, or a high sweep and low SMT if inversely correlated) AND (the confluence is in the current main cycle OR it was in the immediately preceding main cycle and the CISD reference candle predates the current main-cycle start).
Short: A first bearish CISD confirmation, using the last completed chart-bar close, AND (main-cycle BSL SMT OR at least two of main-cycle high sweep, high SMT, and an unfilled bearish main-cycle FVG tap OR a correlated asset has a high sweep and high SMT on the same pair if directly correlated, or a low sweep and high SMT if inversely correlated) AND (the confluence is in the current main cycle OR it was in the immediately preceding main cycle and the CISD reference candle predates the current main-cycle start).
