Calculation: selected OHLC = standard OHLC or Heikin-Ashi OHLC, according to the source setting
Calculation: signal bars = T1 bars when the chart timeframe is shorter than T1; otherwise chart bars. Use completed signal bars for signals and the current forming signal bar for forming alerts.
Calculation: FRD qualifying setup = the highest high over the greater of the peak-age limit and extreme lookback is within the peak-age limit; at least the minimum run length of closes exceed their preceding closes among the minimum run length plus pause allowance bars ending at that high; and at least one bar from that high through the current signal bar is red. Red is close below prior close, close below open, or both, according to the red-definition setting.
Calculation: FGD qualifying setup = the lowest low over the greater of the trough-age limit and extreme lookback is within the trough-age limit; at least the minimum run length of closes are below their preceding closes among the minimum run length plus pause allowance bars ending at that low; and at least one bar from that low through the current signal bar is green. Green is close above prior close, close above open, or both, according to the red-definition setting.
Calculation: normalized MA slope = (selected SMA or EMA(close, MA lookback, T2) − its value slope lookback T2 bars ago) / ATR(ATR lookback, T2); use completed T2 values when the chart timeframe is shorter than T2 and current T2 values otherwise.
Calculation: MA cross event = when the chart timeframe is shorter than T2, a cross of T2 close and the selected T2 MA on the last completed T2 bar, detected at the next T2 bar; otherwise, a chart close crossing the current T2 MA on a confirmed chart bar.
Short: While the current signal bar is forming in real time, prior completed signal bars have at least the minimum run length of consecutive higher closes, their highest high over that run is at least the highest high over the extreme lookback, and current close meets the selected red definition against prior signal close and/or current signal-bar open.
Long: While the current signal bar is forming in real time, prior completed signal bars have at least the minimum run length of consecutive lower closes, their lowest low over that run is at most the lowest low over the extreme lookback, and current close meets the selected green definition against prior signal close and/or current signal-bar open.
Short: When MA confirmation is enabled, an FRD qualifying setup has a current completed red-definition signal bar and its peak has not already produced a confirmed FRD.
Long: When MA confirmation is enabled, an FGD qualifying setup has a current completed green-definition signal bar and its trough has not already produced a confirmed FGD.
Short: An FRD qualifying setup has not already fired for its peak, chart close is below the T2 MA reference, and either MA confirmation is disabled and the current completed signal bar is red by the selected definition, or MA confirmation is enabled and a downward MA cross occurred now or remains pending within the cross-gap at a current red-definition signal day; when slope filtering is enabled, normalized MA slope is below the maximum slope or unavailable.
Long: An FGD qualifying setup has not already fired for its trough, chart close is above the T2 MA reference, and either MA confirmation is disabled and the current completed signal bar is green by the selected definition, or MA confirmation is enabled and an upward MA cross occurred now or remains pending within the cross-gap at a current green-definition signal day; when slope filtering is enabled, normalized MA slope is above the negative maximum slope or unavailable.
Short: On a confirmed signal bar (T1 when chart timeframe is shorter than T1, otherwise chart bars), the close is above the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, swing strength, swing strength); signal only the first close above each pivot, resetting when a newer pivot is confirmed.
Long: On a confirmed signal bar (T1 when chart timeframe is shorter than T1, otherwise chart bars), the close is below the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing strength, swing strength); signal only the first close below each pivot, resetting when a newer pivot is confirmed.
Short: When trigger-line entry is enabled, FRD confirmation close is above its stored trigger, and the first later confirmed chart bar before zone-duration expiry closes below that trigger, unless cancelled by a close above the setup peak or an FGD confirmation; when no-touch filtering is enabled, no bar since confirmation, including the current bar, has reached the stored sell-zone lower bound. In signal-day zone mode, or automatic mode when confirmation close ≤ FRD high and FRD close − confirmation close ≤ maximum zone distance × chart ATR, the trigger and lower bound are FRD low and FRD close; otherwise they are confirmation-bar low and min(T2 MA reference, max(confirmation close, T2 MA reference − zone half-width × chart ATR)).
Long: When trigger-line entry is enabled, FGD confirmation close is below its stored trigger, and the first later confirmed chart bar before zone-duration expiry closes above that trigger, unless cancelled by a close below the setup trough or an FRD confirmation; when no-touch filtering is enabled, no bar since confirmation, including the current bar, has reached the stored buy-zone upper bound. In signal-day zone mode, or automatic mode when confirmation close ≥ FGD low and confirmation close − FGD close ≤ maximum zone distance × chart ATR, the trigger and upper bound are FGD high and FGD close; otherwise they are confirmation-bar high and max(T2 MA reference, min(confirmation close, T2 MA reference + zone half-width × chart ATR)).
