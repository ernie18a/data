Calculation: bearish-run open = open of the first chart bar with close < open after a chart bar with close >= open
Calculation: bullish-run open = open of the first chart bar with close > open after a chart bar with close <= open
Calculation: CRT reference range = high and low of the previous completed T1 bar sampled onto chart bars, the previous completed T2 bar sampled onto chart bars or previous completed anchored T2 interval aggregated from chart bars, the previous completed T3 interval aggregated from chart bars, or the completed configured morning window aggregated from chart bars
Calculation: CRT sweep state = reset sweep states and signal flags when the reference range changes; a low sweep begins when low < range low and tracks the lowest low and bearish-run open at each new low; a high sweep begins when high > range high and tracks the highest high and bullish-run open at each new high; invalidate a low sweep if range low minus lowest low exceeds the maximum sweep depth times range width, and invalidate a high sweep if highest high minus range high exceeds the maximum sweep depth times range width
Calculation: strong-up regime = in ADX mode, ADX with the same configured lookback for both lengths on the last completed T4 bar is at least the configured threshold and the short-lookback EMA is above the intermediate-lookback EMA which is above the long-lookback EMA on that bar; in ribbon mode, chart-bar EMA with the shorter lookback is above chart-bar EMA with the longer lookback; otherwise false
Calculation: strong-down regime = in ADX mode, ADX with the same configured lookback for both lengths on the last completed T4 bar is at least the configured threshold and the short-lookback EMA is below the intermediate-lookback EMA which is below the long-lookback EMA on that bar; in ribbon mode, chart-bar EMA with the shorter lookback is less than or equal to chart-bar EMA with the longer lookback; otherwise false
Calculation: CRT risk distance = maximum of the distance from entry to the sweep extreme plus the configured buffer and the configured minimum stop; buffer, minimum stop, and maximum stop are expressed either as configured percentages of ATR with the configured lookback on the last completed T1 bar or as configured pip distances
Calculation: ORB risk distance = maximum of the absolute distance from entry to the opening-range midpoint and the configured minimum stop, using the same stop-distance units as the CRT risk distance
Calculation: CRT targets = let side be +1 for long and -1 for short, midpoint be the reference-range midpoint, opposite edge be the range high for long or range low for short, and width be range high minus range low; midpoint R is (midpoint - entry) times side divided by CRT risk distance; if midpoint R is below the configured minimum TP1 R and the configured action is to use the range end, TP1 is the opposite edge, TP2 is opposite edge plus side times extension times width, and TP3 is opposite edge plus side times twice the extension times width; otherwise TP1 is midpoint, TP2 is opposite edge, and TP3 is opposite edge plus side times extension times width; extension is the configured extension or its built-in fallback when configured extension is zero
Calculation: ORB targets = for each of the three separately configured R-multiple targets, entry plus side times ORB risk distance times that target
Calculation: active stop = initial long stop is entry minus risk distance and initial short stop is entry plus risk distance; after TP1 move it to entry if configured; after TP2 move it to TP1 if enabled, otherwise move it monotonically to entry unless breakeven moves are disabled; with ATR trailing enabled after TP2, update the long stop to the greater of its current value and close minus the configured trail multiple times chart-bar ATR, or the short stop to the lesser of its current value and close plus that amount
Long: a valid unsignaled CRT low-sweep signal from an enabled reference range, with close > range low and close > open for close-back-inside confirmation, or close > max(range low, bearish-run open at the latest sweep low) and close > open for CISD confirmation; for completed-T2-close confirmation, the prior chart bar closed strictly inside the swept range, the range then changed, and the current close is strictly above range low and below its midpoint while low has not broken the sweep low
Short: a valid unsignaled CRT high-sweep signal from an enabled reference range, with close < range high and close < open for close-back-inside confirmation, or close < min(range high, bullish-run open at the latest sweep high) and close < open for CISD confirmation; for completed-T2-close confirmation, the prior chart bar closed strictly inside the swept range, the range then changed, and the current close is strictly below range high and above its midpoint while high has not broken the sweep high
Long: the first regime-permitted signal in priority order previous T1 CRT, previous T2 CRT, morning CRT, previous T3 CRT, then ORB is long; the chart bar is confirmed and is a weekday bar within the configured entry session and outside the configured news window when that block is enabled; there is no open position, no position closed on the current bar, and daily trades and losses are below their configured limits; regime mode is off or strong-up regime is true for ORB, and regime mode is off or strong-down regime is false for CRT; high minus low is no greater than the configured spike multiple times the prior chart-bar ATR; if the extreme-volatility filter is enabled, chart-bar ATR is no greater than its configured multiple times its configured simple moving average; the selected setup's risk distance is no greater than the configured maximum stop; the risk-based lot size, rounded down to the configured lot increment using risk amount divided by risk distance times contract value in account currency, is at least the minimum lot; for CRT, midpoint R is > 0 and the distance to the opposite range edge divided by risk distance is at least the configured minimum R; if the minimum-TP1 action is skip, midpoint R is at least the configured minimum TP1 R; if the daily premium/discount filter is enabled, close is no greater than the midpoint of the previous completed T1 high and low
Short: the first regime-permitted signal in priority order previous T1 CRT, previous T2 CRT, morning CRT, previous T3 CRT, then ORB is short; the chart bar is confirmed and is a weekday bar within the configured entry session and outside the configured news window when that block is enabled; there is no open position, no position closed on the current bar, and daily trades and losses are below their configured limits; regime mode is off or strong-down regime is true for ORB, and regime mode is off or strong-up regime is false for CRT; high minus low is no greater than the configured spike multiple times the prior chart-bar ATR; if the extreme-volatility filter is enabled, chart-bar ATR is no greater than its configured multiple times its configured simple moving average; the selected setup's risk distance is no greater than the configured maximum stop; the risk-based lot size, rounded down to the configured lot increment using risk amount divided by risk distance times contract value in account currency, is at least the minimum lot; for CRT, midpoint R is > 0 and the distance to the opposite range edge divided by risk distance is at least the configured minimum R; if the minimum-TP1 action is skip, midpoint R is at least the configured minimum TP1 R; if the daily premium/discount filter is enabled, close is no less than the midpoint of the previous completed T1 high and low
Long: close > the high of the completed configured opening range aggregated from chart bars, the long ORB signal has not already occurred in that range, and ORB is enabled
Short: close < the low of the completed configured opening range aggregated from chart bars, the short ORB signal has not already occurred in that range, and ORB is enabled
Exit: long, on a chart bar after the entry bar, low <= the active stop carried into the bar
Exit: short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, high >= the active stop carried into the bar
Exit: long, on a chart bar after the entry bar, the first target is not yet hit AND high >= TP1 AND low > the active stop carried into the bar AND no fixed TP3 target or configured time exit is reached on that bar
Exit: short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, the first target is not yet hit AND low <= TP1 AND high < the active stop carried into the bar AND no fixed TP3 target or configured time exit is reached on that bar

Exit: short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, the second target is not yet hit AND low <= TP2 AND high < the active stop carried into the bar AND no fixed TP3 target or configured time exit is reached on that bar

Exit: short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, fixed TP3 mode is enabled AND low <= TP3 AND high < the active stop carried into the bar
Exit: long, on a chart bar after the entry bar, configured flat time has been reached before the end of the configured time window OR a new T1 period begins, AND low > the active stop carried into the bar AND no fixed TP3 target is reached on that bar
Exit: short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, configured flat time has been reached before the end of the configured time window OR a new T1 period begins, AND high < the active stop carried into the bar AND no fixed TP3 target is reached on that bar
