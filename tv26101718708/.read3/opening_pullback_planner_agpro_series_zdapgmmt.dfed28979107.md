Calculation: High-good normalized score = clamp((value − weak boundary) / (strong boundary − weak boundary) × score maximum, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Low-good normalized score = clamp((weak boundary − value) / (weak boundary − strong boundary) × score maximum, 0, score maximum).
Calculation: Peak score = high-good normalized score from the low boundary to the ideal when value ≤ ideal; otherwise low-good normalized score from the ideal to the high boundary.
Calculation: Opening drive quality score = weighted sum, using separate component weights, of the high-good normalized opening-drive range / max(chart-bar ATR, opening-drive range), the drive-side close location within the opening-drive range scaled to the score maximum, and the high-good normalized mean opening-drive volume / chart-bar SMA(volume, volume lookback).
Calculation: Pullback zone = the retracement band between the opening-drive extreme retraced by the shallow and deep retracement parameters; its midpoint is the mean of its boundaries.
Calculation: Continuation trigger = opening-drive extreme plus the drive-side ATR buffer.
Calculation: Failure line = the deep pullback-zone boundary beyond the drive-side ATR risk buffer.
Calculation: Target guide = opening-drive extreme plus the drive-side opening-drive-range multiple.
Calculation: Pullback quality score = the clamped weighted sum, using separate component weights, of peak score of the maximum retracement depth since the pullback first touched the zone (shallow boundary, ideal depth, deep boundary plus depth-score tolerance), recovery position within the zone scaled to the score maximum, high-good normalized chart-bar volume / volume baseline, peak score of chart-bar ATR / chart-bar SMA(ATR, ATR-baseline lookback) (low, ideal, and high volatility-fit parameters), and high-good normalized reward/risk ratio. Reward/risk ratio = drive-side distance from close to target guide / max(drive-side distance from close to failure line, risk-distance floor); normalize it between separate weak and strong reward/risk parameters.
