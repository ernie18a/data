Calculation: ATR percentile = (ATR(ATR lookback) - lowest(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR normalization lookback)) / (highest(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR normalization lookback) - lowest(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR normalization lookback)); use the neutral midpoint if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Displacement ATR threshold = clamp(displacement base + (SMA(total wick / absolute body, calibration lookback) - wick/body baseline) * wick/body slope, lower displacement bound, upper displacement bound), with total wick / absolute body set to its fallback when body is zero; when automatic calibration is disabled, use the selected instrument-specific or manual threshold
Calculation: Minimum body/range percentage = clamp(body percentage base + ATR percentile * ATR percentile slope, lower body percentage bound, upper body percentage bound); when automatic calibration is disabled, use the selected instrument-specific or manual threshold
Calculation: Bullish sweep severity = GRIND when low[grind lookback] and low[one bar ago] are below the lowest low of prior sweep lookback bars and close is above that level; otherwise, when low is below that level and close is above it, classify (prior lowest low - low) / ATR as MINOR, MAJOR, or FLUSH by the sweep depth thresholds; otherwise NONE
Calculation: Bearish sweep severity = GRIND when high[grind lookback] and high[one bar ago] are above the highest high of prior sweep lookback bars and close is below that level; otherwise, when high is above that level and close is below it, classify (high - prior highest high) / ATR as MINOR, MAJOR, or FLUSH by the sweep depth thresholds; otherwise NONE
Calculation: KP RSI = RSI of volume-weighted positive and negative close changes, where each change is multiplied by sqrt(volume / SMA(volume, volume SMA lookback)); blend the fast and slow EMAs of each side using ATR percentile as the fast weight when adaptive KP is enabled, or an equal blend otherwise
Calculation: KP Fisher score = clamp(score midpoint + Fisher transform of the KP RSI rescaled to the unit interval and bounded just inside its endpoints * KP Fisher multiplier, score range)
Calculation: Bullish divergence active = a confirmed Pivot Low(low, divergence pivot lookback, divergence pivot lookback) is lower than the prior confirmed pivot low and at least the minimum oscillator agreement count among KP RSI, CCI(close, divergence CCI lookback), and Stochastic(close, high, low, divergence Stochastic lookback), sampled at the pivots, is higher at the new pivot; remains active for divergence pivot lookback * divergence active duration after confirmation when divergence is enabled
Calculation: Bearish divergence active = a confirmed Pivot High(high, divergence pivot lookback, divergence pivot lookback) is higher than the prior confirmed pivot high and at least the minimum oscillator agreement count among KP RSI, CCI(close, divergence CCI lookback), and Stochastic(close, high, low, divergence Stochastic lookback), sampled at the pivots, is lower at the new pivot; remains active for divergence pivot lookback * divergence active duration after confirmation when divergence is enabled
Calculation: Directional confluence score = clamp(weighted average of Efficiency Ratio(close, DER lookback), AMVS score, ATR percentile score, COFP score, and KP Fisher score, using their separate weights; use zero when the weights sum to zero; add the direction bonus for each of AMVS and COFP aligned with the direction and the divergence bonus when the corresponding divergence is active; clamp to the score range). AMVS = ((close - close[AMVS lookback]) / AMVS lookback) / ATR; AMVS score = clamp(score midpoint + AMVS * AMVS score multiplier, score range). ATR percentile score = ATR percentile * score scale. COFP = SMA(sign(close - open) * volume * abs(close - open) / (high - low + minimum price increment), COFP lookback) / highest(abs(that SMA), COFP lookback * COFP normalization multiplier), using zero when the denominator is zero; COFP score = clamp(score midpoint + COFP * COFP score multiplier, score range). Efficiency Ratio is abs(close - close[DER lookback]) / sum(abs(change(close)), DER lookback), using zero when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Order-block quality score = quality body/ATR weight * clamp((abs(close - open) / max(ATR, minimum price increment)) / body/ATR scale, unit interval) + quality body/range weight * clamp(abs(close - open) / (high - low), unit interval) + quality gap weight * bullish or bearish gap indicator + quality volume weight * clamp((volume / SMA(volume, volume SMA lookback)) / volume scale, unit interval), using the neutral volume score when that average is zero, + quality sweep weight * clamp(sweep depth / ATR / sweep depth scale, unit interval); bullish gap is low > high[gap lookback], bearish gap is high < low[gap lookback]
Calculation: Order-block grade = A+ when order-block quality score is at least the A+ threshold, A when at least the A threshold, B when at least the B threshold, otherwise C
Calculation: Gate score = count of direction-aligned checks: close from T1 > HMA(close from T1, HMA lookback) for long or <= for short; close from T2 > HMA(close from T2, HMA lookback) for long or <= for short; and sign(close - close[return lookback]) from each of T3, T4, and T5 is positive for long or negative for short. These timeframe values are sampled on chart bars with lookahead off: the current forming timeframe bar in realtime and the last completed value historically
Calculation: Bullish OB qualification = signal timing permits AND close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR * displacement ATR threshold AND body/range percentage >= minimum body/range percentage AND close > latest confirmed Pivot High(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) AND Gate score for long >= gate minimum AND (sweep filter disabled OR bullish sweep severity >= minimum sweep severity) AND (volume vacuum filter disabled OR SMA(volume, vacuum lookback) < SMA(volume, vacuum baseline lookback) * vacuum threshold) AND (compression filter disabled OR (highest(high, compression lookback) - lowest(low, compression lookback)) / ATR < compression threshold) AND (confluence filter disabled OR directional confluence score for long >= confluence minimum) AND a prior bearish candle exists within OB search lookback AND its order-block grade >= minimum grade; the zone is the high-to-low range of the nearest such prior candle
Calculation: Bearish OB qualification = signal timing permits AND close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR * displacement ATR threshold AND body/range percentage >= minimum body/range percentage AND close < latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) AND Gate score for short >= gate minimum AND (sweep filter disabled OR bearish sweep severity >= minimum sweep severity) AND (volume vacuum filter disabled OR SMA(volume, vacuum lookback) < SMA(volume, vacuum baseline lookback) * vacuum threshold) AND (compression filter disabled OR (highest(high, compression lookback) - lowest(low, compression lookback)) / ATR < compression threshold) AND (confluence filter disabled OR directional confluence score for short >= confluence minimum) AND a prior bullish candle exists within OB search lookback AND its order-block grade >= minimum grade; the zone is the high-to-low range of the nearest such prior candle
Calculation: Effective SL ATR multiplier = selected SL ATR multiplier * (VRI scale base + ATR percentile * VRI scale slope) when VRI scaling is enabled, otherwise selected SL ATR multiplier
Long: KP RSI > KP direction midpoint
Short: KP RSI <= KP direction midpoint
Long: Bullish divergence active
Short: Bearish divergence active AND NOT Bullish divergence active
Long: signal bar is confirmed or intrabar signaling is enabled AND position is flat AND the newest touched unmitigated bullish OB has low <= top - (top - bottom) * entry fraction AND close > bottom; its source candle age does not exceed the OB lifetime by more than one bar, and it has not been invalidated by the configured count of consecutive closes below bottom
Short: signal bar is confirmed or intrabar signaling is enabled AND position is flat AND the newest touched unmitigated bearish OB has high >= bottom + (top - bottom) * entry fraction AND close < top; its source candle age does not exceed the OB lifetime by more than one bar, and it has not been invalidated by the configured count of consecutive closes above top
Exit: Long, confirmed bar AND (low <= stop OR (low > stop AND high >= entry price + TP3 risk multiple * abs(entry price - stop)))
Exit: Short, confirmed bar AND (high >= stop OR (high < stop AND low <= entry price - TP3 risk multiple * abs(entry price - stop)))
