Calculation: StoredLevel_s = pivot low(low, piv_left, piv_right) for long or pivot high(high, piv_left, piv_right) for short, confirmed piv_right bars after its bar, stored with that pivot bar as born bar unless a stored level of the same side lies within merge_k * ATR(atr_len) of its price; retained while bars since born bar <= max_age AND it is among the newest max_levels stored levels of its side
Calculation: Valid_s(level, t) = level is StoredLevel_s AND level has no earlier signal AND on this confirmed bar the level is crossed (long: low crosses below level; short: high crosses above level) AND depth is between dmin and dmax, where depth = (level - low) / ATR(atr_len) for long or (high - level) / ATR(atr_len) for short AND pen_mode accepts the bar kind (body if min(open, close) < level for long or max(open, close) > level for short, otherwise wick) AND no earlier confirmed close beyond level by brk_k * ATR(atr_len) (below level for long, above for short) AND ((first_only is on AND no earlier crossing of level) OR (first_only is off AND no sweep of level within max(sweep_timeout, window) bars))
Calculation: Swept_s(level, t) = Valid_s(level, t) AND (multi_all is on OR level has the least depth among valid levels of its side on this bar) AND NOT (dual_sweep_block is on AND a valid level of the opposite side exists on this bar); a sweep stores bar t, its extreme (low for long, high for short), its depth and its kind
Calculation: Reclaim_s(level, t) = level was Swept_s at bar s AND 0 <= t - s <= window AND (for long: close >= level + buf_k * ATR(atr_len) AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= loc_min AND abs(close - open) / ATR(atr_len) >= body_min AND (dir_on is off OR close > open) AND (wick_on is off OR t != s OR (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= wick_min); for short: close <= level - buf_k * ATR(atr_len) AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= loc_min AND abs(close - open) / ATR(atr_len) >= body_min AND (dir_on is off OR close < open) AND (wick_on is off OR t != s OR (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= wick_min))
Calculation: Quality_s(level, t) = round(min(100, max(0, D1 + C1 + W1 + F1 + V1 + X1 + M1 + K1))), where clip(x) = min(1, max(0, x)); D = stored sweep depth; s = stored sweep bar; Kd = stored sweep kind; Att = crossings of level through the sweep bar; Age = t - born bar; for long: Lp = (close - low) / (high - low), Wn = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low); for short: Lp = (high - close) / (high - low), Wn = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low); Bd = abs(close - open) / ATR(atr_len); Dx = abs(close - level) / ATR(atr_len); Rg = (high - low) / ATR(atr_len); D1 = wD * clip(1 - abs(clip((D - dmin) / (dmax - dmin)) - dc) / dh); C1 = wC * clip((Lp - cf) / (1 - cf)); W1 = wW * (t = s ? clip(Wn / wn) : max(bw if Kd is wick else bb, clip(Wn / wn) * wm)); F1 = wF * (1 - clip(Age / max_age)) ^ fe; V1 = wV * (1 if vol_on is off, vu if volume is unavailable, else clip((volume / SMA(volume, vol_len) - va) / vb)); X1 = wX * (db * clip(Bd / bn) + (1 - db) * clip(Dx / dn)); M1 = wM * (mr * clip(Rg <= r0 ? Rg / r0 : 1 - (Rg - r0) / r1) + (1 - mr) * clip((ATR(atr_len) / SMA(ATR(atr_len), regime_len) - ra) / rb)); K1 = k1 if Att <= 1, k2 if Att = 2, else k3
Calculation: Candidate_s(t) = stored levels with Reclaim_s(level, t) AND Quality_s(level, t) >= qmin AND no earlier signal from the level AND (consume_on is off OR not chosen on an earlier bar)
Calculation: Chosen_s(t) = the candidate with highest Quality_s, ties to smaller abs(close - level) / ATR(atr_len), when select_quality is on; otherwise the candidate with smallest abs(close - level) / ATR(atr_len)
Calculation: Stop_s = anchor -/+ ATR(atr_len) at entry bar * m, with minus for long and plus for short, where entry price = close of the signal bar; anchor = entry price and m = stop_mult when stop_sweep is off; anchor = sweep extreme of the chosen level at the signal bar (low for long, high for short) and m = stop_buf when stop_sweep is on
Calculation: Target_s = entry price + abs(entry price - Stop_s) * rr for long; entry price - abs(Stop_s - entry price) * rr for short
Long: Candidate_long(t) is non-empty AND allow_long is on AND (chart_on is off OR (close > EMA(close, chart_len) AND (slope_on is off OR EMA(close, chart_len) > EMA(close, chart_len)[1]))) AND (htf_on is off OR (close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, htf_len, T1, last completed bar) AND (slope_on is off OR EMA(close, htf_len, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, htf_len, T1, previous completed bar)))) AND (vol_on is off OR vr_min <= ATR(atr_len) / SMA(ATR(atr_len), regime_len) <= vr_max) AND (eff_on is off OR Kaufman efficiency ratio(close, eff_len) >= eff_min) AND (trade_block is off OR not in position) AND (exit_allow is on OR no exit on this bar) AND (cool_on is off OR bars since last executed signal > cool_bars, where that signal is long, or of either side if cool_any is on) AND (clust_on is off OR no earlier executed long signal OR abs(close - close at last executed long signal) / ATR(atr_len) >= clust_k) AND (Candidate_short(t) is empty OR (dual_reclaim_block is off AND (conf_long_pri is on OR (conf_hq is on AND Quality of Chosen_long(t) > Quality of Chosen_short(t)))))
Short: Candidate_short(t) is non-empty AND allow_short is on AND (chart_on is off OR (close < EMA(close, chart_len) AND (slope_on is off OR EMA(close, chart_len) < EMA(close, chart_len)[1]))) AND (htf_on is off OR (close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, htf_len, T1, last completed bar) AND (slope_on is off OR EMA(close, htf_len, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, htf_len, T1, previous completed bar)))) AND (vol_on is off OR vr_min <= ATR(atr_len) / SMA(ATR(atr_len), regime_len) <= vr_max) AND (eff_on is off OR Kaufman efficiency ratio(close, eff_len) >= eff_min) AND (trade_block is off OR not in position) AND (exit_allow is on OR no exit on this bar) AND (cool_on is off OR bars since last executed signal > cool_bars, where that signal is short, or of either side if cool_any is on) AND (clust_on is off OR no earlier executed short signal OR abs(close - close at last executed short signal) / ATR(atr_len) >= clust_k) AND (Candidate_long(t) is empty OR (dual_reclaim_block is off AND (conf_short_pri is on OR (conf_hq is on AND Quality of Chosen_short(t) > Quality of Chosen_long(t)))))
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND low <= Stop_long AND (stop_first is on OR high < Target_long)
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND high >= Target_long AND (stop_first is off OR low > Stop_long)
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND high >= Stop_short AND (stop_first is on OR low > Target_short)
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND low <= Target_short AND (stop_first is off OR high < Stop_short)
