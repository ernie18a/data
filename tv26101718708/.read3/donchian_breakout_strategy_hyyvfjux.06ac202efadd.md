Calculation: initial tight stop level = lowest(low, tight lookback) while the position is long and entry price > lowest(low, tight lookback); while the position is long and entry price <= lowest(low, tight lookback), retain its value from the preceding bar; otherwise lowest(low, tight lookback)
Calculation: tight trailing stop = if the position is long, entry price <= lowest(low, tight lookback), and lowest(low, wide lookback) > initial tight stop level, then lowest(low, wide lookback); otherwise initial tight stop level
Calculation: active stop = tight trailing stop when the tight stop option is enabled; otherwise lowest(low, wide lookback)
Long: ((close trigger selected AND close >= highest(high, upper lookback)[1]) OR (wick trigger selected AND high >= highest(high, upper lookback)[1])) AND (current timeframe MA filter disabled OR highest(high, upper lookback) > MA(close, selected type, current timeframe lookback)) AND (higher timeframe MA filter disabled OR highest(high, upper lookback) > MA(close, selected type, higher timeframe lookback, T1 bar still forming, value taken on chart bars)) AND (slope filter disabled OR MA(close, selected type, slope lookback, T2 bar still forming, calculated on T2 bars and value taken on chart bars) > its value on the preceding T2 bar) AND (ADR filter disabled OR (highest(high, upper lookback) - lowest(low, lower lookback)) / highest(high, upper lookback) * 100 < ADR percentage * SMA((high - low) / abs(low) * 100, ADR lookback, T3 bar still forming, calculated on T3 bars and value taken on chart bars) / 100) AND datetime > start datetime AND datetime < finish datetime AND (close trigger selected OR slope MA is rising now OR slope MA was not rising on the preceding chart bar)
Exit: Long, (close trigger selected AND close <= active stop[1]) OR (wick trigger selected AND low <= active stop[1])
