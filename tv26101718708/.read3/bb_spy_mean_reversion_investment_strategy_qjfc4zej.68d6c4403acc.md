Calculation: Signal-band multiplier = band multiplier × clamp(1 + VOV gain × (VOV / SMA(VOV, VOV lookback) − 1), minimum scale, maximum scale) when adaptive multiplier and adaptive signal bands are enabled, otherwise band multiplier; VOV = StDev(ROC(close, one-bar lookback), VOV lookback), using a VOV ratio of 1 when SMA(VOV, VOV lookback) is zero
Calculation: Regime pass = selected regime is none OR (selected regime is trend AND PercentRank(2 × band multiplier × StDev(close, Bollinger lookback), rank lookback) > trend width threshold AND abs((SMA(close, Bollinger lookback) − SMA(close, Bollinger lookback)[slope lookback]) / max(2 × band multiplier × StDev(close, Bollinger lookback), minimum tick)) > slope threshold) OR (selected regime is mean reversion AND PercentRank(2 × band multiplier × StDev(close, Bollinger lookback), rank lookback) < 100 − trend width threshold)
Calculation: Bias = direction of the first base band-cross setup, then remains unchanged until an opposite base setup; base setups use the band-cross, candle, and EMA comparisons in the Long and Short rules, including the short ADX comparison, without regime filtering
Calculation: Risk ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the current, still-forming T1 bar and sampled on chart bars
Long: strategy direction permits long AND ((close crosses above the lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Signal-band multiplier) AND close > open AND close > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND (regime filtering is off for longs OR Regime pass)) OR (Bias is long AND position is not long AND (keep-rearm enabled OR position was long on the preceding chart bar and is not long now)))
Short: strategy direction permits short AND ((close crosses below the upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Signal-band multiplier) AND close < open AND close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) AND ADX(high, low, close, DMI lookback, smoothing lookback) > minimum ADX AND (regime filtering is off for shorts OR Regime pass)) OR (Bias is short AND position is not short AND (keep-rearm enabled OR position was short on the preceding chart bar and is not short now)))
Exit: Long, (ATR exits enabled AND (low ≤ (if close − entry price ≥ breakeven trigger × ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR then max(entry price − ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR, entry price) else entry price − ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR) OR high ≥ entry price + ATR take-profit multiplier × Risk ATR)) OR (time exit enabled AND ((bars since long entry ≥ minimum hold AND close − entry price < profit gate × ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR) OR bars since long entry ≥ minimum hold + extension))
Exit: Short, (ATR exits enabled AND (high ≥ (if entry price − close ≥ breakeven trigger × ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR then min(entry price + ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR, entry price) else entry price + ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR) OR low ≤ entry price − ATR take-profit multiplier × Risk ATR)) OR (time exit enabled AND ((bars since short entry ≥ minimum hold AND entry price − close < profit gate × ATR stop multiplier × Risk ATR) OR bars since short entry ≥ minimum hold + extension))
