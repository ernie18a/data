Calculation: Trend(T) = close(T) > EMA(close, trend length, T) AND EMA(close, fast length, T) > EMA(close, slow length, T); calculate on T bars and sample on chart bars using the current forming T bar in real time and the last completed T bar historically. Fast, slow, and trend lengths are separate parameters.
Calculation: Bear trend(T) = close(T) < EMA(close, trend length, T) AND EMA(close, fast length, T) < EMA(close, slow length, T); calculate and sample as for Trend(T).
Calculation: Selected VWAP = session VWAP(HLC3, T1) OR RTH-anchored VWAP(HLC3, T4), according to the selected mode; calculate on the stated timeframe bars and sample on chart bars using the current available bar in real time and the last completed bar historically. VWAP bands are one volume-weighted standard deviation above and below the selected VWAP.
Calculation: Momentum = close(T1) - close(T1, momentum lookback minutes ago); strong momentum means its absolute value exceeds ATR(T1, ATR length) times the momentum-strength parameter. Bull momentum is strong positive momentum; bear momentum is strong negative momentum.
Calculation: VOLD% = (NYSE UVOL - NYSE DVOL) / (NYSE UVOL + NYSE DVOL) × 100. A/D slope and VOLD% slope compare current values with values at the internals-velocity lookback on T4 bars. Hard rising or falling slopes meet their separate minimum-move parameters. Smoothed TICK is SMA(NYSE TICK close, TICK smoothing length) on T4 bars. True cumulative TICK is the session sum of NYSE TICK close; its short and long slopes compare it with its values at the respective lookbacks on T4 bars. These external series use the current available T4 bar in real time and the last completed T4 bar historically.
Calculation: Long internal support points are awarded separately when NYSE internals are available and (A/D is positive without a hard falling slope, or the enabled hard rising A/D slope qualifies); (VOLD% meets its positive threshold without a hard falling slope, or the enabled hard rising VOLD% slope qualifies); and smoothed TICK and both cumulative-TICK slopes are positive. Short support is the sign-reversed counterpart. Long internal opposition counts available components when A/D is at or below its negative moderate threshold or has a hard falling slope while below its positive moderate threshold, VOLD% is at or below its negative threshold or has a hard falling slope while below its positive threshold, or smoothed TICK is at or below its negative dim threshold with a falling short cumulative-TICK slope; exclude A/D or VOLD% opposition when its hard slope is rising. Short opposition is the sign-reversed counterpart.
Calculation: Relative strength = the traded instrument's percent change from its RTH open minus ES price's percent change from its RTH open. Relative-strength slope is current relative strength minus relative strength at the internals-velocity lookback. NQ-leading is a relative strength above its positive threshold without a hard falling slope, or a hard rising slope while relative strength is not below its negative threshold. NQ-lagging is the sign-reversed counterpart. Exceptional long or short leadership meets its separate relative-strength threshold and has no opposing hard slope. Calculate on T4 bars; sample on chart bars using the current available bar in real time and the last completed bar historically.
Calculation: Long score = long-trend(T1) × trend weight + long-trend(T2) × component weight + close(T1) above selected VWAP × component weight + bull momentum × component weight + RSI(close(T1), RSI length) within its long range × component weight + each enabled long internal support point × component weight + enabled NQ-leading point × component weight. Short score uses bear trend, below VWAP, bear momentum, its separate RSI range, short internal support, and NQ-lagging. The trend, confirmation, and regime calculations use the same separate fast, slow, and trend EMA lengths.
Calculation: Score maximum = sum of enabled component weights. When the maximum equals the full score maximum, the GO and caution thresholds are their respective threshold parameters; otherwise each is the greater of its minimum scaled threshold and the ceiling of score maximum times its respective scaling parameter.
Calculation: Long GO core = NYSE session is active AND required score data are available AND long score exceeds short score AND long score meets the GO threshold AND the score advantage meets its minimum parameter AND (core gate is disabled OR (long-trend(T1) AND close(T1) above selected VWAP)) AND VIX percent change over the VIX support window is below the long conflict threshold AND long opposing internals are below the opposition downgrade count, except exceptional long leadership reduces the specified two-component opposition case by one, AND not all internals oppose. Short GO core is the sign-reversed counterpart. VIX is the external VIX series on T4 bars, sampled using the current available bar in real time and the last completed bar historically.
Calculation: Completed-close activation, when selected, requires Long GO core or Short GO core on a completed T1 bar and keeps the signal active only while that core remains valid; otherwise activation uses the live core condition.
Calculation: One-minute volume profile = on completed T4 bars, distribute each bar's volume evenly among the price bins its high-low range touches; POC is the highest-volume bin, and the value area expands from POC toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until the configured percentage of volume is reached. The selected window is current RTH, prior completed RTH, or the configured rolling T4-bar window.
Calculation: Long required stop distance = the greater of the ATR noise floor and the positive distance to the structure-first stop; use the nearest valid level below close(T1) among the latest confirmed swing low, slow EMA(T1), selected VWAP, and enabled manual support, then place the stop beyond that level by the ATR structure buffer. If no positive structural stop exists, use ATR(T1) times the fallback stop multiplier. Short required stop distance is the sign-reversed counterpart using swing high, slow EMA, VWAP, and enabled manual resistance.
Calculation: Long target distance uses the nearer of the scalp target and the nearest enabled level above close(T1): swing high, prior RTH high, completed initial-balance high, prior confirmed RTH high, value-area high, or manual resistance. In a qualifying continuation checkpoint context, use the nearest eligible destination at or beyond the minimum-room distance, capped by the scalp target. Without scalp mode, use the nearest enabled level or the ATR projection fallback. Short target distance uses the sign-reversed levels. Long room is distance to the nearest enabled obstacle, except continuation checkpoint context uses target distance; short room is the counterpart.
Calculation: Long risk block = scalp mode AND (long required stop distance exceeds the selected maximum stop OR long target distance / long required stop distance is below the minimum reward-risk ratio OR long room is below minimum room) OR hard bearish cumulative-TICK divergence OR (long direction AND price is near swing resistance AND near the upper VWAP band AND (hard bearish divergence OR weakening bull momentum OR exhaustion risk or climax) AND not strong bull continuation AND not fresh long expansion). Short risk block is the sign-reversed counterpart. Weakening momentum means current absolute momentum is below its value at the earlier comparison offset.
Calculation: Long extension veto = enabled extension veto AND long GO core AND not long risk block AND the count of active long flags meets the minimum: close is beyond the configured fraction of the RTH low-to-high range, level climax or exhaustion climax, VWAP stretch meets the active ATR threshold, close is within the ATR distance of value-area high, close is within the ATR interaction distance of manual resistance, or hard bearish divergence; in a continuation regime, require a serious flag (climax, hard divergence, or VWAP stretch). Short flags use the RTH high, value-area low, manual support, and bullish divergence.
Long: Long GO core AND completed-close activation is not selected OR long GO core is confirmed under completed-close activation, AND NOT long risk block AND NOT long extension veto.
Short: Short GO core AND completed-close activation is not selected OR short GO core is confirmed under completed-close activation, AND NOT short risk block AND NOT short extension veto.
Long: NYSE session is active AND required score data are available AND long score exceeds short score AND long score meets the caution threshold AND score advantage is at least one point AND not Long.
Short: NYSE session is active AND required score data are available AND short score exceeds long score AND short score meets the caution threshold AND score advantage is at least one point AND not Short.
Long: Completed-close activation is selected AND Long GO core AND the completed T1 close has not yet confirmed it.
Short: Completed-close activation is selected AND Short GO core AND the completed T1 close has not yet confirmed it.
Long: T1 high is at or above its highest high over the price-extreme lookback AND true cumulative TICK on T4 has fallen over both the short and long cumulative-TICK lookbacks during the NYSE session.
Short: T1 low is at or below its lowest low over the price-extreme lookback AND true cumulative TICK on T4 has risen over both the short and long cumulative-TICK lookbacks during the NYSE session.
Long: On T1, at least the reversal-signal threshold of these crosses occurred within the reversal lookback: close crosses above fast EMA, fast EMA crosses above slow EMA, fast EMA crosses above trend EMA, close crosses above trend EMA; the latest qualifying bullish cross is no older than the latest bearish cross. A reversal is confirmed while this condition holds and close remains above slow EMA for the confirmation-bar count, or after a qualifying held pullback to slow EMA within the pullback lookback.
Short: On T1, at least the reversal-signal threshold of these crosses occurred within the reversal lookback: close crosses below fast EMA, fast EMA crosses below slow EMA, fast EMA crosses below trend EMA, close crosses below trend EMA; the latest qualifying bearish cross is more recent than the latest bullish cross. A reversal is confirmed while this condition holds and close remains below slow EMA for the confirmation-bar count, or after a qualifying held pullback to slow EMA within the pullback lookback.
