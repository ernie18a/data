Calculation: Candle spread = max(high - low, high-low minimum price increment)
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / candle spread
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume baseline lookback)[1]
Calculation: Directional efficiency = abs(close[1] - close[range lookback + 1]) / sum(abs(change(close)), range lookback)[1], or 1 when the denominator is unavailable or nonpositive
Calculation: Active range = in Automatic mode, highest high and lowest low over the preceding range lookback bars, activated when their width is within the minimum and maximum width-to-prior-ATR bounds, directional efficiency is at most its maximum, and each boundary has at least the minimum distinct visits; in Manual mode, user-specified support and resistance activate at the selected time when ATR is available and bypass discovery filters; freeze support, resistance, and prior-bar ATR at activation, track one range at a time, replace any pending sweep with each new accepted sweep, allow at most one retest per qualified breakout, and end tracking on failure or an unqualified escape beyond the escape-distance-to-frozen-ATR threshold
Long: at chart-bar close within an active range, sweep cooldown elapsed, low < support - frozen ATR * boundary buffer and low >= support - frozen ATR * maximum sweep depth, high <= resistance + frozen ATR * boundary buffer, support < close < resistance, Close location >= spring close-location minimum, and (volume mode is price-only OR current volume and prior-bar volume baseline are available and positive)
Short: at chart-bar close within an active range, sweep cooldown elapsed, high > resistance + frozen ATR * boundary buffer and high <= resistance + frozen ATR * maximum sweep depth, low >= support - frozen ATR * boundary buffer, support < close < resistance, Close location <= upthrust close-location maximum, and (volume mode is price-only OR current volume and prior-bar volume baseline are available and positive)
Long: at chart-bar close within an active range, the most recent pending sweep is a spring within the sweep-test window, low > that sweep's low and low <= support + frozen ATR * sweep-test boundary distance, support < close < resistance, Close location >= spring-test close-location minimum, candle spread <= frozen ATR * maximum quiet spread, and (volume mode is price-only OR volume is valid, Relative volume <= maximum test relative volume, and current volume < volume on the spring-sweep bar)
Short: at chart-bar close within an active range, the most recent pending sweep is an upthrust within the sweep-test window, high < that sweep's high and high >= resistance - frozen ATR * sweep-test boundary distance, support < close < resistance, Close location <= upthrust-test close-location maximum, candle spread <= frozen ATR * maximum quiet spread, and (volume mode is price-only OR volume is valid, Relative volume <= maximum test relative volume, and current volume < volume on the upthrust-sweep bar)
Long: at chart-bar close within an active range, close > resistance + frozen ATR * boundary buffer, close > open, Close location >= SOS close-location minimum, candle spread >= frozen ATR * minimum breakout spread, and (volume mode is price-only OR volume is valid and Relative volume >= minimum breakout relative volume)
Short: at chart-bar close within an active range, close < support - frozen ATR * boundary buffer, close < open, Close location <= SOW close-location maximum, candle spread >= frozen ATR * minimum breakout spread, and (volume mode is price-only OR volume is valid and Relative volume >= minimum breakout relative volume)
Long: at chart-bar close during an upward-breakout track, before any retest and within the breakout-retest window, low is within frozen ATR * retest boundary distance of resistance, close > resistance + frozen ATR * boundary buffer, Close location >= LPS close-location minimum, candle spread <= frozen ATR * maximum quiet spread, and (volume mode is price-only OR volume is valid and Relative volume <= maximum test relative volume)
Short: at chart-bar close during a downward-breakout track, before any retest and within the breakout-retest window, high is within frozen ATR * retest boundary distance of support, close < support - frozen ATR * boundary buffer, Close location <= LPSY close-location maximum, candle spread <= frozen ATR * maximum quiet spread, and (volume mode is price-only OR volume is valid and Relative volume <= maximum test relative volume)
Exit: long, at chart-bar close during an upward-breakout track, close < resistance - frozen ATR * breakout-failure distance
Exit: short, at chart-bar close during a downward-breakout track, close > support + frozen ATR * breakout-failure distance
Long: on chart bars when confirmed context is enabled and T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed T1 bar's close and EMA and the preceding T1 bar's EMA, close > EMA(close, context EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) and that EMA > EMA(close, context EMA lookback, T1, one bar before the last completed bar)
Short: on chart bars when confirmed context is enabled and T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed T1 bar's close and EMA and the preceding T1 bar's EMA, close < EMA(close, context EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) and that EMA < EMA(close, context EMA lookback, T1, one bar before the last completed bar)
