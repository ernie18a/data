Calculation: bullish score = count of true conditions: close > session VWAP; RSI(close, main RSI lookback) > RSI midpoint; MACD(close, short lookback, long lookback, signal lookback) line > signal line; EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback); ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback); volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close > open; RSI(close, confirmation RSI lookback, T1) > RSI midpoint using chart bars with values from T1's still-forming bar; and, when enabled, correlation(close, Dollar Index close, correlation lookback) <= correlation threshold AND Dollar Index close < EMA(Dollar Index close, separate lookback).
Calculation: bearish score = count of true conditions: close < session VWAP; RSI(close, main RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint; MACD(close, short lookback, long lookback, signal lookback) line < signal line; EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback); ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback); volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close < open; RSI(close, confirmation RSI lookback, T1) < RSI midpoint using chart bars with values from T1's still-forming bar; and, when enabled, correlation(close, Dollar Index close, correlation lookback) <= correlation threshold AND Dollar Index close > EMA(Dollar Index close, separate lookback).
Calculation: effective minimum score = round(configured minimum score × active factor count / eight-factor baseline), where the Dollar Index factor is omitted from the score and active factor count when disabled.
Calculation: average range = SMA(high - low, lookback, T2, last completed bar), with chart bars taking values from T2.
Calculation: risk distance = ATR(lookback) on the entry bar × stop multiplier.
Long: crossover(EMA(close, shorter lookback), EMA(close, longer lookback)), accepted intrabar or only after the chart bar closes according to the close-confirmation setting, AND bullish score >= effective minimum score AND (Gold-only guard disabled OR symbol metadata passes Gold recognition) AND (session filter disabled OR chart is non-intraday OR chart time is within either configured session window in the configured timezone) AND (ADR filter disabled OR chart is non-intraday OR average range <= 0 OR (high of T2's still-forming bar - low of T2's still-forming bar) / average range × 100 < exhaustion threshold) AND (no prior entry OR chart bars since the latest entry >= minimum spacing).
Short: crossunder(EMA(close, shorter lookback), EMA(close, longer lookback)), accepted intrabar or only after the chart bar closes according to the close-confirmation setting, AND bearish score >= effective minimum score AND (Gold-only guard disabled OR symbol metadata passes Gold recognition) AND (session filter disabled OR chart is non-intraday OR chart time is within either configured session window in the configured timezone) AND (ADR filter disabled OR chart is non-intraday OR average range <= 0 OR (high of T2's still-forming bar - low of T2's still-forming bar) / average range × 100 < exhaustion threshold) AND (no prior entry OR chart bars since the latest entry >= minimum spacing).
Long: bullish score >= bearish score (confluence panel BUY marking).
Short: bullish score < bearish score (confluence panel SELL marking).
Long: close > session VWAP (price-above-VWAP marking).
Short: close <= session VWAP (price-below-VWAP marking).

Short: RSI(close, main RSI lookback) <= RSI midpoint (bear-colored RSI marking).

Long: DI+(DI lookback) > DI-(DI lookback) (up-arrow marking).
Short: DI+(DI lookback) <= DI-(DI lookback) (down-arrow marking).
Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) (bullish EMA marking).
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback) <= EMA(close, longer lookback) (bearish EMA marking).
Long: volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) (high-volume marking).
Long: RSI(close, confirmation RSI lookback, T1) > RSI midpoint using chart bars with values from T1's still-forming bar (bull-colored confirmation RSI marking).
Short: RSI(close, confirmation RSI lookback, T1) <= RSI midpoint using chart bars with values from T1's still-forming bar (bear-colored confirmation RSI marking).

Short: correlation(close, Dollar Index close, correlation lookback) <= correlation threshold AND Dollar Index close >= EMA(Dollar Index close, separate lookback) (bear-colored inverse Dollar Index marking).
Long: an open trade exists AND low <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND high >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND low > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND no long or short entry signal occurs on the bar (EMA retest marking).
Short: an open trade exists AND low <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND high >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) <= EMA(close, longer lookback) AND high < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND no long or short entry signal occurs on the bar (EMA retest marking).
Calculation: long active stop = entry price - risk distance until the first target was reached on a prior bar; afterward, entry price if breakeven management is enabled, otherwise entry price - risk distance.
Calculation: short active stop = entry price + risk distance until the first target was reached on a prior bar; afterward, entry price if breakeven management is enabled, otherwise entry price + risk distance.
Exit: long, low <= long active stop OR high >= entry price + 3 × risk distance; if both occur on the same bar, the stop is processed first.
Exit: short, high >= short active stop OR low <= entry price - 3 × risk distance; if both occur on the same bar, the stop is processed first.
