Calculation: Globex open = the open of the T2 bar starting at the Globex session anchor in the configured timezone, sampled on chart bars whether or not that T2 bar is complete; retain it until the next Globex anchor and treat it as unavailable until captured.
Calculation: Midnight open = the open of the T2 bar starting at the Midnight session anchor in the configured timezone, sampled on chart bars whether or not that T2 bar is complete; retain it until the next Globex anchor and treat it as unavailable until captured.
Calculation: NY equity open = the open of the T2 bar starting at the NY equity session anchor in the configured timezone, sampled on chart bars whether or not that T2 bar is complete; retain it until the next Globex anchor and treat it as unavailable until captured.
Calculation: Fair Value Gap zones = on T3 bars, bullish when high[2] < low, with bottom = high[2] and top = low; bearish when low[2] > high, with bottom = high and top = low[2]. Chart bars take values from the current T3 bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Unbroken pivot high = the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, the same lookback parameter on each side), available when its confirmation-bar close was less than or equal to the pivot and no earlier selected BOS-mode break above it occurred; replace it when a newer pivot high confirms.
Calculation: Unbroken pivot low = the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, the same lookback parameter on each side), available when its confirmation-bar close was greater than or equal to the pivot and no earlier selected BOS-mode break below it occurred; replace it when a newer pivot low confirms.
Long: At the NY-open event, when all three session opens are available, Globex open < Midnight open AND Midnight open < NY equity open; retain this bias until the next Globex-open event.
Short: At the NY-open event, when all three session opens are available, Globex open >= Midnight open AND Midnight open >= NY equity open; retain this bias until the next Globex-open event.
Long: close > EMA(close, EMA lookback parameter, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars.
Short: close < EMA(close, EMA lookback parameter, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars.
Long: For any available session open, the selected open-break mode is Close and close[1] < the open AND close > the open, OR the selected open-break mode is Wick/Touch and close[1] <= the open AND high > the open; AND the open-break EMA filter is disabled or close > EMA(close, EMA lookback parameter, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars.
Short: For any available session open, the selected open-break mode is Close and close[1] > the open AND close < the open, OR the selected open-break mode is Wick/Touch and close[1] >= the open AND low < the open; AND the open-break EMA filter is disabled or close < EMA(close, EMA lookback parameter, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars.
Long: When the BOS/FVG signal is enabled, an unbroken pivot high exists AND the selected BOS break mode is Close and close > the pivot high OR the selected BOS break mode is Wick/Touch and high > the pivot high; AND a Fair Value Gap created 1 through the maximum-age parameter chart bars ago is below or at the pivot high, with pivot high minus gap top <= the maximum-distance parameter; if direction matching is enabled the gap is bullish; if the unmitigated-gap filter is enabled, no chart bar through the signal bar has overlapped the gap (high >= gap bottom AND low <= gap top); and the BOS EMA filter is disabled or close > EMA(close, its separate lookback parameter, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars; and the BOS open-break filter is disabled or a selected-mode upward break of any available session open occurs on this bar.
Short: When the BOS/FVG signal is enabled, an unbroken pivot low exists AND the selected BOS break mode is Close and close < the pivot low OR the selected BOS break mode is Wick/Touch and low < the pivot low; AND a Fair Value Gap created 1 through the maximum-age parameter chart bars ago is at or above the pivot low, with gap bottom minus pivot low <= the maximum-distance parameter; if direction matching is enabled the gap is bearish; if the unmitigated-gap filter is enabled, no chart bar through the signal bar has overlapped the gap (high >= gap bottom AND low <= gap top); and the BOS EMA filter is disabled or close < EMA(close, its separate lookback parameter, T1, current bar still forming), calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars; and the BOS open-break filter is disabled or a selected-mode downward break of any available session open occurs on this bar.
