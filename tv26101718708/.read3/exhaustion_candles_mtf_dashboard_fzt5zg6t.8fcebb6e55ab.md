Calculation: Rejection body = abs(close - open), except use minimum tick when abs(close - open) is zero
Calculation: Upper-wick exhaustion = high - low > SMA(high - low, average lookback) * range multiplier AND (volume spike not required OR volume > SMA(volume, same average lookback) * volume multiplier) AND high - max(close, open) > rejection body * wick/body ratio AND high - max(close, open) > min(close, open) - low
Calculation: Lower-wick exhaustion = high - low > SMA(high - low, average lookback) * range multiplier AND (volume spike not required OR volume > SMA(volume, same average lookback) * volume multiplier) AND min(close, open) - low > rejection body * wick/body ratio AND min(close, open) - low > high - max(close, open)
Calculation: Most recent exhaustion direction = none if neither wick exhaustion has occurred; otherwise upper-wick if its bars-since count is no greater than the lower-wick count, else lower-wick; a missing count is treated as larger than any valid count
Long: Lower-wick exhaustion on chart bars OR, on chart bars, the selected current state of any of T1, T2, or T3 is lower-wick exhaustion OR the most recent exhaustion direction of any of T1, T2, or T3 is lower-wick; each T calculation runs on its own bars and is sampled on chart bars from its last completed bar when confirmed-bars-only is enabled, otherwise from its still-forming bar
