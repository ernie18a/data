Calculation: Body(R) = abs(close - open) on source bars R
Calculation: Upper wick(R) = high - max(open, close) on source bars R
Calculation: Lower wick(R) = min(open, close) - low on source bars R
Calculation: Average body(R) = SMA(Body(R), average-body lookback)
Calculation: Hammer shape(R) = Body(R) <= Average body(R) * small-body multiple AND Lower wick(R) >= Body(R) * long-wick ratio AND Upper wick(R) <= Body(R) * short-wick maximum ratio
Calculation: Inverted hammer shape(R) = Body(R) <= Average body(R) * small-body multiple AND Upper wick(R) >= Body(R) * long-wick ratio AND Lower wick(R) <= Body(R) * short-wick maximum ratio
Calculation: Harami inside(R) = max(open, close) <= max(open[1], close[1]) AND min(open, close) >= min(open[1], close[1]) AND Body(R) <= Body(R)[1] * harami-body ratio
Calculation: Midpoint(R, n) = (open[n] + close[n]) / 2
Calculation: Bullish engulfing(R) = close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND open <= close[1] AND close >= open[1]
Calculation: Bearish engulfing(R) = close[1] > open[1] AND close < open AND open >= close[1] AND close <= open[1]
Calculation: Bullish hammer(R) = Hammer shape(R) AND close[1] < SMA(close, trend lookback)[1]
Calculation: Bearish hanging man(R) = Hammer shape(R) AND close[1] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[1]
Calculation: Bullish inverted hammer(R) = Inverted hammer shape(R) AND close[1] < SMA(close, trend lookback)[1]
Calculation: Bearish shooting star(R) = Inverted hammer shape(R) AND close[1] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[1]
Calculation: Bullish harami(R) = Harami inside(R) AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[2] < SMA(close, trend lookback)[2]
Calculation: Bearish harami(R) = Harami inside(R) AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[2] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[2]
Calculation: Bullish morning star(R) = close[2] < open[2] AND Body(R)[2] >= Average body(R)[2] * large-body multiple AND Body(R)[1] <= Body(R)[2] * star-middle-body ratio AND close > open AND close > Midpoint(R, 2) AND close[3] < SMA(close, trend lookback)[3]
Calculation: Bearish evening star(R) = close[2] > open[2] AND Body(R)[2] >= Average body(R)[2] * large-body multiple AND Body(R)[1] <= Body(R)[2] * star-middle-body ratio AND close < open AND close < Midpoint(R, 2) AND close[3] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[3]
Calculation: Bullish piercing line(R) = close[1] < open[1] AND Body(R)[1] >= Average body(R)[1] * large-body multiple AND close > open AND open < close[1] AND close > Midpoint(R, 1) AND close < open[1] AND close[2] < SMA(close, trend lookback)[2]
Calculation: Bearish dark cloud cover(R) = close[1] > open[1] AND Body(R)[1] >= Average body(R)[1] * large-body multiple AND close < open AND open > close[1] AND close < Midpoint(R, 1) AND close > open[1] AND close[2] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[2]
Calculation: Bullish three white soldiers(R) = close > open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[2] > open[2] AND close > close[1] AND close[1] > close[2] AND open > open[1] AND open < close[1] AND open[1] > open[2] AND open[1] < close[2] AND Body(R) >= (high - low) * minimum body-range ratio AND Body(R)[1] >= (high[1] - low[1]) * minimum body-range ratio AND Body(R)[2] >= (high[2] - low[2]) * minimum body-range ratio
Calculation: Bearish three black crows(R) = close < open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[2] < open[2] AND close < close[1] AND close[1] < close[2] AND open < open[1] AND open > close[1] AND open[1] < open[2] AND open[1] > close[2] AND Body(R) >= (high - low) * minimum body-range ratio AND Body(R)[1] >= (high[1] - low[1]) * minimum body-range ratio AND Body(R)[2] >= (high[2] - low[2]) * minimum body-range ratio
Calculation: Reliable(p) = for each chart-bar pattern p separately, count its occurrences only after the evaluation-forward lookback has elapsed; grade each as correct when close then is higher than its occurrence close for a bullish pattern or lower for a bearish pattern; use the mean of correct outcomes among the latest rolling-window matured occurrences; reliable when its count is below the minimum tracked occurrences or its mean is at least the minimum reliability threshold
Calculation: Chart selected pattern direction = direction of the first reliable chart pattern in this order: bullish engulfing, bullish hammer, bullish inverted hammer, bullish harami, bullish morning star, bullish piercing line, bullish three white soldiers, bearish engulfing, bearish hanging man, bearish shooting star, bearish harami, bearish evening star, bearish dark cloud cover, bearish three black crows; zero if none
Calculation: MTF selected pattern direction(Tn) = direction of the first detected pattern in the chart selected pattern order, evaluated on Tn bars and using its last completed bar sampled on chart bars; do not apply reliability gating
Calculation: MTF agreement count(direction) = number of enabled T1-T3 whose MTF selected pattern direction equals direction
Calculation: MTF gate passes = MTF gating is not selected OR MTF is disabled OR no T1-T3 is enabled OR MTF agreement count(Chart selected pattern direction) >= minimum HTFs required to gate
Calculation: Volume confirmation passes = volume confirmation is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume-confirmation lookback) * volume-confirmation multiple
