Calculation: Accepted swing high/low = a strict high/low pivot using separate left- and right-bar lookbacks, confirmed after the right-bar lookback, and accepted when no prior same-side pivot exists or its distance from the prior accepted pivot exceeds ATR(ATR lookback) × pivot-distance multiplier.
Calculation: Context bias = on chart bars using T1 values, set bullish when close(T1, current forming bar) − close(T1, context lookback bars ago) > ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) × context tolerance; set bearish when that difference is less than the negative threshold; otherwise retain the prior bias.
Calculation: Structure direction for each of T1–T7 = on chart bars using the last completed bars of that timeframe, bullish when the latest two completed highs and lows both rise, bearish when both fall, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Weighted structure score = sum of each T1–T7 structure direction multiplied by its separate timeframe weight, divided by the sum of those weights.
Calculation: Trend direction = Context bias when non-neutral; otherwise chart-timeframe structure direction from the latest two accepted swing highs and lows.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe structure direction = bullish when the latest two accepted swing highs and lows both rise, bearish when both fall, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Swing position percentile = 100 × (close − latest accepted swing low) / (latest accepted swing high − latest accepted swing low), when the high exceeds the low.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = accepted swing highs and lows; merge an unswept same-side pool when its price is within ATR(ATR lookback) × equal-high/low tolerance, replacing its price with the mean of the two prices.
Calculation: Sweep quality = min(100, 40 × sweep depth / ATR(ATR lookback) + 60 × candle body / ATR(ATR lookback)).
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback).
Calculation: Impulse class = WEAK when candle body/range is below the profile's hollow threshold; otherwise ABSORBED when the opposing wick exceeds body × absorption-wick multiple and the close is in the specified opposite half of the range; otherwise STRONG when body/range meets the profile's strong threshold and volume ratio meets its strong-volume threshold; otherwise SOLID when body/range meets the profile's solid threshold; otherwise HOLLOW. Direction is candle direction except ABSORBED, whose direction is the attempted direction defined by its dominant wick.
Calculation: Active kill zone = chart-bar time is within a configured Asian, London, NY AM, or NY PM window and outside the lunch window.
Calculation: Confluence percent = 100 × the number of true factors / 15. Factors: close within ATR × OB-proximity parameter of an active OB; close within ATR × FVG-proximity parameter of an active FVG; close within ATR × VWAP-proximity parameter of anchored VWAP; active kill zone; a BOS or MSS within the validation-freshness parameter; close above EMA( close, EMA lookback) when Context bias is bullish or below it when bearish; chart structure direction equals non-neutral Context bias; volume exceeds its moving average; close within ATR × OB-proximity parameter of either previous completed T9 high or low; close within ATR × VWAP-proximity parameter of the previous completed anchor VWAP; close within ATR × OB-proximity parameter of an active BB; close within ATR × FVG-proximity parameter of an active BPR; swing position percentile is within the swing-proximity parameter of either extreme; swing position percentile is within the OTE discount or premium bounds when OTE is enabled; swing position percentile is outside the equilibrium bounds.
Calculation: Continuation tier and direction = direction is Context bias when known, otherwise the sign of the weighted structure score. Score is the clamped 0–100 result of the following raw points divided by the score-normalization parameter and multiplied by the chart-timeframe multiplier: bias points are (base bias points when absolute weighted structure score meets its threshold and Context bias is known, otherwise zero, plus absolute weighted structure score × bias bonus points) × the fresh BOS-displacement, fresh plain-BOS, or other-event quality multiplier; confluence percent × confluence weight; timing points when a kill zone is active; impulse points for a trend-aligned STRONG or SOLID candle; liquidity points when no unswept pool blocks the bias-direction path within the ATR path-distance parameter, or, for neutral direction, when no pools exist; volume points from separate descending thresholds and point values; the highest applicable bonus among aligned BB retest, continuation delivery state, and aligned active-zone rejection; and inducement points when a fresh sweep opposite Context bias precedes a fresh BOS in the bias direction. Map score to INACTIVE, EARLY, FORMING, BUILDING, or CONFIRMED using the ordered tier thresholds. Then apply the continuation modifiers: consolidation and lunch cap at EARLY; blocking liquidity and validation conflict each lower one tier; an active sweep not already counted as inducement and the configured NY overlap each raise one tier, up to CONFIRMED.
Calculation: Reversal tier and direction = no tier and neutral direction unless (a) the latest non-stale CHoCH, MSS, or BB retest is within the reversal freshness window and opposes non-neutral Context bias, and (b) an opposite-Context-bias sweep is within the sweep recency window and its quality meets the sweep-quality threshold. Otherwise, direction is the validation event direction. Score is the clamped 0–100 result of path-specific raw points divided by the path maximum and multiplied by the chart-timeframe multiplier: path-specific VAL freshness points (separate age-point schedules for MSS, CHoCH, and BB retest, followed by linear decay to zero); sweep quality × sweep weight; confluence percent × confluence weight; reversal-aligned STRONG or SOLID impulse points plus the capped absorption-pattern bonus when fresh; volume points from separate descending thresholds and point values; FVG points when a same-direction FVG formed after the validation anchor within the freshness window; sweep points when the qualifying prior sweep is present; kill-zone points when active; and SMT points for a same-direction divergence within its recency window. Map score using the ordered tier thresholds; consolidation caps at BUILDING and lunch caps at EARLY.
