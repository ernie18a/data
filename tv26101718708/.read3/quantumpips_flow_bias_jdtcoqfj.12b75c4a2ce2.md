Calculation: Bull volume percentage = 100 × sum(volume when close > open, half volume when close = open, otherwise 0, over the delta lookback) / sum(volume when close > open, half volume when close = open, otherwise 0, plus volume when close < open, half volume when close = open, otherwise 0, over the delta lookback), when the delta lookback is available and total allocated volume > 0
Long: EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), where the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback, AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, fast lookback)[1]) AND Bull volume percentage > bull threshold
Short: EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback), where the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback, AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, fast lookback)[1]) AND Bull volume percentage < bear threshold
