Calculation: Confirmed swing state = retain the latest and preceding pivot highs and lows; pivot high = pivot high(high, swing lookback, swing lookback), pivot low = pivot low(low, swing lookback, swing lookback); each pivot becomes known after the right-side lookback bars complete.
Calculation: Displacement score(direction) = body points + ATR points + alignment points. Body fraction = abs(close[1] - open[1]) / (high[1] - low[1]) when the range is positive, otherwise zero; award points using three descending configured cutoffs and their corresponding awards. ATR multiple = (high[1] - low[1]) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) when ATR is positive, otherwise zero; award points using three descending configured cutoffs and their corresponding awards. Alignment points use close[2] versus open[2] and current close versus open: award the configured full amount if both candles align with direction, the configured partial amount if one aligns, otherwise none.
Calculation: Volume score(direction) = dominance points + relative-volume points + rising-volume points. For each T1 intrabar within chart bar [1], classify volume as bullish when close > open and bearish otherwise; dominance = volume in the requested direction / total volume, or zero if total volume is zero. Award dominance points using a strict upper cutoff followed by two descending inclusive cutoffs, each with its configured award. Relative volume = volume[1] / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback), or zero if the average is not positive; award points using a strict upper cutoff followed by two descending inclusive cutoffs, each with its configured award. Add the configured rising-volume award when volume[1] > volume[2] > volume[3].
Calculation: Contextual score(direction, top, bottom) = premium-discount points + higher-timeframe nesting points + sweep points + session points. On chart bars, position = (((top + bottom) / 2) - lowest(low, range lookback)) / (highest(high, range lookback) - lowest(low, range lookback)); if the range is positive, bullish points are the configured full award when position <= the configured midpoint fraction, otherwise round((1 - position) * the configured scale); bearish points are the full award when position >= that fraction, otherwise round(position * that scale); use no points for this component if the range is zero. For nesting, use request.security with lookahead_off on T2: current T2 high/low and high/low two T2 bars ago; historical values are available at the T2 bar close and realtime values may use the forming T2 bar. A bullish T2 gap exists when current low > high[2], with zone [high[2], current low]; a bearish T2 gap exists when current high < low[2], with zone [current high, low[2]]. Award the configured nesting points when the current FVG zone is contained in a T2 gap of the same direction. Award configured sweep points if, on any of the preceding bars within the sweep window, bullish low < that bar's latest swing low and close > that swing low, or bearish high > that bar's latest swing high and close < that swing high. Convert the chart bar's opening datetime to New York time; award configured session points when time is at or after the configured Asian start through midnight, or within any of the configured London, New York AM, or New York PM start/end intervals.
Calculation: Structural score(direction) = market-structure points + EMA points + break points. Bullish structure means latest swing high > preceding swing high and latest swing low > preceding swing low; bearish structure means both latest swings are lower; otherwise structure is neutral. Award the configured aligned, opposed, or neutral points according to direction. Award configured EMA points when bullish close > EMA(close, EMA lookback), or bearish close < EMA(close, EMA lookback). A bullish break occurs when close > latest swing high, close[1] <= that same high, and the high differs from the previously broken high; bearish break is the reverse using the latest swing low. Retain the latest break direction and bar. If its age is within the configured break lookback, award configured matching-direction points or configured opposing-direction points; otherwise award the configured no-recent-break points.
Calculation: FVG grade(direction, top, bottom) = first passing grade in A, B, C order, or D if none pass. A, B, and C each pass when every axis whose requirement toggle is enabled has a score at least its own minimum; each grade and axis has a separate requirement toggle and minimum. The axis scores are Displacement score(direction), Volume score(direction), Contextual score(direction, top, bottom), and Structural score(direction).
Long: low > high[2] AND FVG grade(bullish, low, high[2]) >= configured minimum display grade AND (the FVG has not been mitigated since formation by the configured method, where wick means low < bottom and close means close < bottom, OR show mitigated history is enabled)
Short: high < low[2] AND FVG grade(bearish, low[2], high) >= configured minimum display grade AND (the FVG has not been mitigated since formation by the configured method, where wick means high > top and close means close > top, OR show mitigated history is enabled)
