Calculation: regular-session VWAP = VWAP(hlc3, volume, regular session), calculated on chart bars for each market series
Calculation: overnight-session VWAP = VWAP(hlc3, volume, overnight session), calculated on chart bars for each market series
Calculation: opening-range high and low = high and low of the opening T1 bar, calculated on T1 bars and carried to chart bars from the forming T1 bar through its completion
Calculation: regular-session VWAP cross count = selected close-based or wick-based regular-session VWAP crosses since the regular-session start, excluding that session's first bar and resetting at each session start
Calculation: upward reclaim = the configured reclaim mode's consecutive-close test above regular-session VWAP, or its cross-and-hold test: current close above VWAP and (previous bar in regular session with previous close above prior VWAP, or previous close at or below prior VWAP)
Calculation: downward reclaim = the configured reclaim mode's consecutive-close test below regular-session VWAP, or its cross-and-hold test: current close below VWAP and (previous bar in regular session with previous close below prior VWAP, or previous close at or above prior VWAP)
Long: close of any displayed equity-index market > that market's regular-session VWAP OR close > that market's overnight-session VWAP OR close > that market's prior regular-session VWAP
Short: close of any displayed equity-index market < that market's regular-session VWAP OR close < that market's overnight-session VWAP OR close < that market's prior regular-session VWAP
Long: close of any displayed equity-index market > its opening-range high
Short: close of any displayed equity-index market < its opening-range low
Long: during the regular session within the recent-session window, EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slow lookback)
Short: during the regular session within the recent-session window, EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slow lookback)
Long: during the regular session within the recent-session window and outside its first bar, close crosses above regular-session VWAP in close mode OR (wick mode and low <= regular-session VWAP and high >= regular-session VWAP and previous close < prior VWAP and close > regular-session VWAP)
Short: during the regular session within the recent-session window and outside its first bar, close crosses below regular-session VWAP in close mode OR (wick mode and low <= regular-session VWAP and high >= regular-session VWAP and previous close > prior VWAP and close < regular-session VWAP)
Long: during the regular session within the recent-session window and outside its first bar, regular-session VWAP crosses above overnight-session VWAP
Short: during the regular session within the recent-session window and outside its first bar, regular-session VWAP crosses below overnight-session VWAP
Long: during the regular session and configured trade window within the recent-session window, an upward reclaim has occurred since the session started and has not reset at the configured consecutive-close acceptance threshold below VWAP, EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback) with separate lookbacks ordered short-to-long and each EMA > its prior-bar value, low <= regular-session VWAP + touch buffer, close > regular-session VWAP, regular-session VWAP cross count < its configured maximum, and volume > prior-bar volume if rising-volume confirmation is enabled
Short: during the regular session and configured trade window within the recent-session window, a downward reclaim has occurred since the session started and has not reset at the configured consecutive-close acceptance threshold above VWAP, EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback) with separate lookbacks ordered short-to-long and each EMA < its prior-bar value, high >= regular-session VWAP - touch buffer, close < regular-session VWAP, regular-session VWAP cross count < its configured maximum, and volume > prior-bar volume if rising-volume confirmation is enabled
Long: position is flat, during the configured trade window within the recent-session window, regular-session VWAP cross count < its configured maximum, and high >= the high of the latest long hold candle + minimum price increment, after that hold candle's pending setup with reclaimed-up state, bullish EMA alignment, trade-window eligibility, recent-session eligibility, and cross-count allowance remaining valid; round(((hold-candle high + minimum price increment) - (hold-candle low - stop buffer)) / minimum price increment) <= maximum stop distance
Short: position is flat, during the configured trade window within the recent-session window, regular-session VWAP cross count < its configured maximum, and low <= the low of the latest short hold candle - minimum price increment, after that hold candle's pending setup with reclaimed-down state, bearish EMA alignment, trade-window eligibility, recent-session eligibility, and cross-count allowance remaining valid; round(((hold-candle high + stop buffer) - (hold-candle low - minimum price increment)) / minimum price increment) <= maximum stop distance
Exit: long, low <= latest long hold-candle low - stop buffer OR high >= entry price + profit-target distance
Exit: short, high >= latest short hold-candle high + stop buffer OR low <= entry price - profit-target distance
