Calculation: Adaptive envelope(L) = let m=EMA(close,L), n=abs(close-m)/max(abs(m), minimum tick), d=EMA(abs(sin(a*n^2+b*n)*cos(a*n^2+b*n))*max(abs(m),minimum tick),L), upper=EMA(max(m+d,close),L), and lower=EMA(min(close,m-d),L), where a and b are separate parameters.
Calculation: Directional signal(L, side) = falling ended for upper-minus-lower envelope over L bars AND, for long, close above the lower envelope or, for short, close below the upper envelope; in either case, neither envelope lower rising nor envelope upper falling over max(one bar, floor(L/fifth)) bars.
Calculation: MLMA state = bullish when close is above MLMA plus its mean absolute error band and MLMA is rising; bearish when close is below MLMA minus that band and MLMA is falling; otherwise retain the previous state. MLMA and its bands use the configured kernel regression, source, regression mode, kernel settings, window, forecast, and multiplier. A non-chart MLMA timeframe uses its last completed bar, sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Signal quality(direction) = weighted sum of MLMA context, alignment across current and three higher-timeframe MLMA states, momentum, volatility, volume, and order-block context scores, minus the capped loss-streak penalty, clamped to zero through one hundred. MLMA context scores trend alignment, directional MLMA slope, close on the directional side of MLMA, valid MLMA-or-supportive-block context, and proximity to a supportive block. Momentum scores directional RSI, candle direction, SMA slope, and candle body-to-range ratio. Volume and volatility scores use their configured ratio bands. Order-block context scores the best nearby supportive block and subtracts the strongest nearby opposing block.
Calculation: No-trade condition = strict no-trade filtering enabled AND (consolidation OR either enabled envelope wedge OR ranging regime with absolute three-bar MLMA slope divided by ATR below its threshold OR ATR divided by average ATR below its threshold OR MLMA state opposing the direction OR close inside a cloud whose width divided by ATR is below its threshold). Consolidation compares the absolute ten-bar close change divided by ATR with its threshold; regime is trending when the absolute ten-bar close change exceeds half the average ATR, otherwise ranging.
Calculation: Order-block volatility = the configured order-block ATR, smoothed absolute close-to-MLMA deviation, or high-minus-low; smooth it using the configured smoothing lookback. Consolidation compares the absolute ten-bar close change divided by ATR to its threshold.
Calculation: Swing low (lookback) = the lowest qualifying low among bars one through lookback, where a qualifying low is below its neighboring lows and, when available, the next up to five lows. Swing high is the corresponding highest qualifying high.
Calculation: Stop level(side, entry, swing, ATR, order blocks) = the configured ATR-distance, buffered swing, nearby order-block, or entry-percentage stop; use the ATR-distance stop if the swing is unavailable or the selected order-block stop is unavailable or nonpositive. The order-block stop scans up to the three latest same-side blocks within the configured proximity and offsets the block boundary by the configured ATR buffer.
Calculation: Target levels(side, entry, swing, ATR) = up to the configured number of levels at entry plus the long-side or minus the short-side distance. Base distance is selected from ATR times its configured factor, absolute entry-to-swing distance, the greater of an ATR distance and a fraction of entry-to-swing distance, or another configured ATR distance. Multiply the base distance by the configured spacing factor raised to each level index and that index's configured Fibonacci extension.
Long: The configured signal-combination mode selects primary and secondary Directional signal(long) independently, requires both to agree, or selects only the primary, AND signal quality is at least the configured minimum and the No-trade condition is false when the quality engine is enabled.
Short: The configured signal-combination mode selects primary and secondary Directional signal(short) independently, requires both to agree, or selects only the primary, AND signal quality is at least the configured minimum and the No-trade condition is false when the quality engine is enabled.
Long: The previous bar has order-block volatility above its smoothed value times the configured multiplier AND its candle is bullish AND the current bar is not consolidating AND the previous-bar RSI is between its configured bounds AND, if enabled, previous-bar volume exceeds its average times the configured threshold AND, if enabled, the previous close is above the previous MLMA.
Short: The previous bar has order-block volatility above its smoothed value times the configured multiplier AND its candle is bearish AND the current bar is not consolidating AND the previous-bar RSI is between its configured bounds AND, if enabled, previous-bar volume exceeds its average times the configured threshold AND, if enabled, the previous close is below the previous MLMA.
Long: A stored bullish order block is retested while intact: the bar range touches the block within the configured ATR tolerance, close remains above its lower boundary within that tolerance, price previously departed above the block by the configured ATR distance, and the minimum bars since block creation and prior retest have elapsed; the block is not already being tested, current volume exceeds its configured average-volume ratio, block strength exceeds its threshold, retest count is below its maximum, and the bullish reaction condition holds. In trend-aligned mode, require bullish MLMA state when enabled and ATR-to-average-ATR ratio within its configured bounds when enabled. Retest confidence passes the configured adaptive threshold and is not clustered by the configured time and price windows.
Short: A stored bearish order block is retested while intact: the bar range touches the block within the configured ATR tolerance, close remains below its upper boundary within that tolerance, price previously departed below the block by the configured ATR distance, and the minimum bars since block creation and prior retest have elapsed; the block is not already being tested, current volume exceeds its configured average-volume ratio, block strength exceeds its threshold, retest count is below its maximum, and the bearish reaction condition holds. In trend-aligned mode, require bearish MLMA state when enabled and ATR-to-average-ATR ratio within its configured bounds when enabled. Retest confidence passes the configured adaptive threshold and is not clustered by the configured time and price windows.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below Stop level(long, entry price, swing low, ATR, order blocks).
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds Stop level(short, entry price, swing high, ATR, order blocks).
Exit: long, high reaches or exceeds any unhit Target level(long, entry price, swing low, ATR); close the remaining position when all target levels have been hit.
Exit: short, low reaches or falls below any unhit Target level(short, entry price, swing high, ATR); close the remaining position when all target levels have been hit.
