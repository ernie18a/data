Calculation: Kalman velocity and velocity variance = two-state constant-velocity Kalman filter of log(max(source price, price floor)) × price scale; initialize on the first bar with position equal to transformed source, initial velocity, and initial position variance, initial position-velocity covariance, and initial velocity variance; on each chart bar predict position = prior position + prior velocity, velocity = prior velocity, position variance = prior position variance + prior position-velocity covariance + prior position-velocity covariance + prior velocity variance + process noise, position-velocity covariance = prior position-velocity covariance + prior velocity variance, and velocity variance = prior velocity variance + process noise; innovation variance = predicted position variance + measurement noise; position gain = predicted position variance / innovation variance; velocity gain = predicted position-velocity covariance / innovation variance; innovation = transformed source − predicted position; update position = predicted position + position gain × innovation, velocity = predicted velocity + velocity gain × innovation, position variance = (one − position gain) × predicted position variance, position-velocity covariance = (one − position gain) × predicted position-velocity covariance, and velocity variance = predicted velocity variance − velocity gain × predicted position-velocity covariance
Calculation: Bull significance = Kalman velocity − sigma multiplier × sqrt(max(Kalman velocity variance, variance floor)) > zero
Calculation: Bear significance = Kalman velocity + sigma multiplier × sqrt(max(Kalman velocity variance, variance floor)) < zero
Long: (Bull significance[confirmation lag] AND NOT Bull significance[confirmation lag plus one bar] AND (no prior long cross OR bars since prior long cross >= same-direction signal spacing)) OR (NOT Bull significance[previous chart bar] AND NOT Bear significance[previous chart bar] AND Bull significance)
Short: (Bear significance[confirmation lag] AND NOT Bear significance[confirmation lag plus one bar] AND (no prior short cross OR bars since prior short cross >= same-direction signal spacing)) OR (NOT Bull significance[previous chart bar] AND NOT Bear significance[previous chart bar] AND Bear significance)
Exit: long, Bull significance[previous chart bar] AND NOT Bull significance AND NOT Bear significance AND current chart bar is confirmed
Exit: short, Bear significance[previous chart bar] AND NOT Bull significance AND NOT Bear significance AND current chart bar is confirmed
