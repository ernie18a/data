Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum(high) over chart bars in the opening-range session, retained afterward
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum(low) over chart bars in the opening-range session, retained afterward
Long: in the breakout-window session AND no earlier signal in the session AND opening-range high is defined AND close > opening-range high
Short: in the breakout-window session AND no earlier signal in the session AND opening-range low is defined AND close < opening-range low
