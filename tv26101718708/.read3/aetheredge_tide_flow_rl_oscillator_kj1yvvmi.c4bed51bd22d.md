Calculation: Safe ATR = max(nz(ATR(ATR lookback), nz(TrueRange, close * ATR fallback factor)), positive floor)
Calculation: Participation impulse = nz(((close - close[1]) / Safe ATR) * nz(volume / max(SMA(volume, flow lookback), positive floor), 1))
Calculation: Flow oscillator = oscillator scale * clamp(EMA(Participation impulse, flow lookback) / (normalization multiplier * max(STDEV(EMA(Participation impulse, flow lookback), normalization lookback), positive floor)), -1, 1)
Calculation: Momentum oscillator = oscillator scale * clamp(EMA(Participation impulse, momentum lookback) / (normalization multiplier * max(STDEV(EMA(Participation impulse, momentum lookback), normalization lookback), positive floor)), -1, 1)
Calculation: Learned fade edge = Q(current state, fade) - Q(current state, wait), where current state is overflow side (Flow > overflow threshold, Flow < -overflow threshold, or neither) combined with whether Momentum oscillator > Momentum oscillator[1]; initialize each state's wait and fade Q-values with separate initial-value parameters; choose fade when its Q-value exceeds wait's; update the prior state/action by Q += learning rate * (reward + discount * max action Q(current state) - prior Q), with reward 0 for wait and, for fade, -(close-close[1])/Safe ATR for bullish overflow, +(close-close[1])/Safe ATR for bearish overflow, or -flat-flow penalty * abs((close-close[1])/Safe ATR) for neither
Calculation: Divergence reliability(side, extreme) = success count / (success count + failure count) for each bullish-or-bearish and extreme-or-not-extreme bucket, initialized with the same prior-count parameter for successes and failures; at the outcome horizon after each pivot, count success when close is above the pivot close for bullish or below it for bearish, otherwise count failure; extreme means Flow at the pivot is below -overflow threshold for bullish or above overflow threshold for bearish
Long: (RL enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND chart bar index > warm-up bars AND Flow[1] > overflow threshold AND Flow < Flow[1] AND Learned fade edge > 0) OR (divergences enabled AND a newly confirmed Flow pivot low is higher than the previous Flow pivot low AND its pivot-bar low is lower than the previous pivot-bar low AND Divergence reliability(bullish, extreme if Flow at the pivot < -overflow threshold) >= minimum reliability)
Short: (RL enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND chart bar index > warm-up bars AND Flow[1] < -overflow threshold AND Flow > Flow[1] AND Learned fade edge > 0) OR (divergences enabled AND a newly confirmed Flow pivot high is lower than the previous Flow pivot high AND its pivot-bar high is higher than the previous pivot-bar high AND Divergence reliability(bearish, extreme if Flow at the pivot > overflow threshold) >= minimum reliability)
