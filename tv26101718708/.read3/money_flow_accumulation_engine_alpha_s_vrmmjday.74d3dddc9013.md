Calculation: MFI flow = (MFI(MFI source, money-flow lookback) - MFI center) × MFI scale
Calculation: Money-flow multiplier = if high != low then ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), else zero
Calculation: CMF ratio = sum(money-flow multiplier × volume, CMF lookback) / max(sum(volume, CMF lookback), volume-sum floor)
Calculation: CMF flow = clamp(CMF ratio × CMF scale, -flow cap, flow cap)
Calculation: OBV flow = clamp((OBV - EMA(OBV, OBV trend lookback)) / StDev(OBV, OBV trend lookback) × OBV scale, -flow cap, flow cap), using zero when the standard deviation is zero
