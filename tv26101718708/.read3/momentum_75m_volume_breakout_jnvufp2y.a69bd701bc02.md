Calculation: Volume breakout threshold = EMA(volume, volume lookback) + volume deviation multiplier × standard deviation(volume, same lookback), on chart bars
Long: (RoC(close, longest lookback in T1 bars, current T1 bar) > longest-horizon RoC threshold OR RoC(close, middle-length lookback in T1 bars, current T1 bar) > middle-horizon RoC threshold OR RoC(close, shortest lookback in T1 bars, current T1 bar) > shortest-horizon RoC threshold) AND ATR(ATR lookback, T1, current T1 bar) / close(T1, current T1 bar) × 100 > ATR percentage threshold AND (chart-bar close > EMA(chart-bar close, shortest EMA lookback) × (1 + EMA stretch percentage / 100) OR chart-bar close > EMA(chart-bar close, second-shortest EMA lookback) × (1 + EMA stretch percentage / 100) OR chart-bar close > EMA(chart-bar close, second-longest EMA lookback) × (1 + EMA stretch percentage / 100) OR chart-bar close > EMA(chart-bar close, longest EMA lookback) × (1 + EMA stretch percentage / 100) OR chart-bar close > highest(high, T1 high lookback, ending on the last completed T1 bar) × (1 + high breakout percentage / 100)) AND chart-bar close > chart-bar open AND chart-bar volume > Volume breakout threshold; T1 values are sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (the current T1 bar is developing live and is historically exposed on the final chart bar of its period)
