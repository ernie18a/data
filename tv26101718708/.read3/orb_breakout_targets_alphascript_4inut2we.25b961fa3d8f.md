Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the opening-range duration of the selected session
Calculation: Opening range low = lowest low during the opening-range duration of the selected session
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2
Long: opening range formed AND close >= opening range midpoint
Short: opening range formed AND close < opening range midpoint
Long: opening range formed AND selected session active AND confirmed bar close > opening range high AND no earlier bullish breakout in the session
Short: opening range formed AND selected session active AND confirmed bar close < opening range low AND no earlier bearish breakout in the session
