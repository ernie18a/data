Calculation: Prior channel low/high = lowest(low)/highest(high) over the previous channel-lookback chart bars, excluding the current bar, optionally smoothed by the selected moving average using its separate smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Extreme age = chart bars since the most recent raw low/high that set the prior channel low/high, excluding the current bar.
Long: On chart-bar close, (enabled same-bar variant: low < prior channel low AND close > prior channel low AND prior-channel-low extreme age >= minimum age OR enabled +1 variant: low[1] < prior channel low[1] AND close[1] < prior channel low[1] AND close > prior channel low[1] AND extreme age[1] >= minimum age) AND (HTF filter disabled OR T1 is below the chart timeframe OR last completed T1 close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback)[1] >= ADX threshold; both lookbacks use the same parameter) AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available AND no short candidate simultaneously passes its enabled setup and filters AND NOT (signal lock enabled AND this is the latest chart bar). The HTF filter runs on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar; use standard-symbol data on non-standard charts.
Short: On chart-bar close, (enabled same-bar variant: high > prior channel high AND close < prior channel high AND prior-channel-high extreme age >= minimum age OR enabled +1 variant: high[1] > prior channel high[1] AND close[1] > prior channel high[1] AND close < prior channel high[1] AND extreme age[1] >= minimum age) AND (HTF filter disabled OR T1 is below the chart timeframe OR last completed T1 close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback)[1] >= ADX threshold; both lookbacks use the same parameter) AND ATR(ATR lookback) is available AND no long candidate simultaneously passes its enabled setup and filters AND NOT (signal lock enabled AND this is the latest chart bar). The HTF filter runs on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar; use standard-symbol data on non-standard charts.
Calculation: Stop price = at entry, for a long, signal-bar low (or the lower of signal-bar and preceding-bar lows if the +1 variant qualifies) − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop buffer; for a short, signal-bar high (or the higher of signal-bar and preceding-bar highs if the +1 variant qualifies) + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop buffer.
Calculation: TP3 level = entry price plus (for long) or minus (for short) abs(entry price − stop price) × TP3 risk multiple.
Exit: long, from the bar after entry, low <= stop price OR high >= TP3 level; if both occur on the same bar, the stop takes precedence.
Exit: short, from the bar after entry, high >= stop price OR low <= TP3 level; if both occur on the same bar, the stop takes precedence.
