Long: datetime is within the selected session (inclusive bounds; unrestricted if all sessions are selected) AND close > open(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; longest timeframe) AND close > open(T2, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; second-longest timeframe) AND close > open(T3, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; second-shortest timeframe) AND close > open(T4, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; shortest timeframe)
Short: datetime is within the selected session (inclusive bounds; unrestricted if all sessions are selected) AND close <= open(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; longest timeframe) AND close <= open(T2, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; second-longest timeframe) AND close <= open(T3, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; second-shortest timeframe) AND close <= open(T4, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; shortest timeframe)
Calculation: Long trailing stop = max(close × (1 − long-loss percentage), long trailing stop[1]) while long; otherwise long-stop reset parameter
Calculation: Short trailing stop = min(close × (1 + short-loss percentage), short trailing stop[1]) while short; otherwise short-stop reset parameter
Exit: Long, (trailing stop enabled AND low <= long trailing stop) OR (end-of-day close enabled AND datetime = configured daily close timestamp)
Exit: Short, (trailing stop enabled AND high >= short trailing stop) OR (end-of-day close enabled AND datetime = configured daily close timestamp)
