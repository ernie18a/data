Calculation: Shown(T) = T is enabled AND T is at or above the chart timeframe AND distance in slots from the lowest slot at or above the chart timeframe to T <= rung limit
Calculation: BullCandidate(T, n) = high_T[n+2] < low_T[n] AND n >= 1 AND n+2 < buffer length of T AND low_T[n] - high_T[n+2] >= min height fraction * close AND low_T[n] >= close / range ratio AND high_T[n+2] <= close * range ratio AND MIN over j in 0..n-1 of (low_T[j] if wick fill else close_T[j]) > low_T[n] - fill fraction * (low_T[n] - high_T[n+2])
Calculation: BearCandidate(T, n) = low_T[n+2] > high_T[n] AND n >= 1 AND n+2 < buffer length of T AND low_T[n+2] - high_T[n] >= min height fraction * close AND low_T[n+2] >= close / range ratio AND high_T[n] <= close * range ratio AND MAX over j in 0..n-1 of (high_T[j] if wick fill else close_T[j]) < high_T[n] + fill fraction * (low_T[n+2] - high_T[n])
Calculation: BottomsBetween(T, a, b) = number of n >= 1 where (BullCandidate(T, n) AND a < high_T[n+2] < b) OR (BearCandidate(T, n) AND a < high_T[n] < b)
Calculation: TopsBetween(T, a, b) = number of n >= 1 where (BullCandidate(T, n) AND a < low_T[n] < b) OR (BearCandidate(T, n) AND a < low_T[n+2] < b)
Calculation: BullishZoneDrawn(T) = exists n >= 1 where BullCandidate(T, n) AND ((high_T[n+2] <= close AND close <= low_T[n] AND direction label enabled) OR (high_T[n+2] > close AND BottomsBetween(T, close, high_T[n+2]) < zones per side) OR (low_T[n] < close AND TopsBetween(T, low_T[n], close) < zones per side))
Calculation: BearishZoneDrawn(T) = exists n >= 1 where BearCandidate(T, n) AND ((high_T[n] <= close AND close <= low_T[n+2] AND direction label enabled) OR (high_T[n] > close AND BottomsBetween(T, close, high_T[n]) < zones per side) OR (low_T[n+2] < close AND TopsBetween(T, low_T[n+2], close) < zones per side))
Long: Shown(T1) AND BullishZoneDrawn(T1)

Long: Shown(T4) AND BullishZoneDrawn(T4)
Short: Shown(T1) AND BearishZoneDrawn(T1)
