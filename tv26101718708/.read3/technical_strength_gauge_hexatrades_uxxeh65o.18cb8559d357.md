Calculation: Moving-average vote = one positive vote when close is above the moving average, one negative vote when close is below it, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Moving-average family vote = mean of that family’s moving-average votes across five separately configured lookbacks, omitting unavailable values; a positive or negative mean gives the family direction
