Calculation: Structural levels on T1 and T2 = On each last completed bar, create a level at the preceding bar's close rounded to the nearest instrument minimum price increment when the pair is green-red (A, resistance), red-green (V, support), green-green (bullish gap, support), or red-red (bearish gap, resistance); a doji creates no level. Check existing levels before creating new ones. A close below a support or above a resistance flips its side and makes it Fresh. A Fresh support is rejected when low <= level + half the instrument minimum price increment and close > level; a Fresh resistance is rejected when high >= level - half the instrument minimum price increment and close < level. Rejection makes the level Unfresh. On a T1 bar, if several supports or resistances are rejected, use the lowest support and highest resistance to open at most one setup per side, while making every rejected level Unfresh. Retain only the most recent parameterized level-storage limit of T1 levels.
Calculation: T1/T2 confirmations = Each T1 support rejection opens a long setup and each resistance rejection opens a short setup. For each setup, scan completed T2 bars from the open of its T1 rejection bar; the first T2 bar whose low <= the rejected support level plus touch tolerance or whose high >= the rejected resistance level minus touch tolerance is its touch bar. At that touch, lock the most recently formed T2 A level for a long setup or V level for a short setup, whether Fresh or Unfresh; if none exists, the setup cannot confirm. A later T2 close strictly above the locked A level confirms long, or strictly below the locked V level confirms short. Advance T1 and T2 calculations on their own last completed bars. Keep setups in parallel without expiry; remove a setup if a T1 close crosses its rejected level, when it confirms, or when it is the oldest setup dropped after its side reaches the parameterized pending-setup limit.
Calculation: T1/T2 trend = Initially undefined; thereafter retain the direction of the latest confirmation in chronological T2-bar order. If long and short confirmations occur on the same T2 bar, use short.
Long: T1/T2 trend = bullish
Short: T1/T2 trend = bearish
