Calculation: First-session high = high of the first chart bar in the breakout-candle session of each T1 period, carried forward until the next T1 period
Calculation: First-session low = low of the first chart bar in the breakout-candle session of each T1 period, carried forward until the next T1 period
Long: close > First-session high AND close[1] < First-session high[1] AND a new T2 bar AND datetime within the backtest date range and trading session AND long trading enabled AND long trades taken in the current T1 period < maximum long trades per T1 period
Short: close < First-session low AND close[1] > First-session low[1] AND a new T2 bar AND datetime within the backtest date range and trading session AND short trading enabled AND short trades taken in the current T1 period < maximum short trades per T1 period
Exit: Long, datetime outside the backtest date range or trading session OR (datetime within the backtest date range and trading session AND (close < EMA(close, lookback) OR close[1] < First-session low OR (first-session stop enabled AND close < First-session high AND a new T2 bar) OR (long fixed stop enabled AND close < entry price - long stop distance) OR (long target enabled AND close > entry price + long target distance)))
Exit: Short, datetime outside the backtest date range or trading session OR (datetime within the backtest date range and trading session AND (close > EMA(close, lookback) OR close[1] > First-session high OR (first-session stop enabled AND close > First-session low AND a new T2 bar) OR (short fixed stop enabled AND close > entry price + short stop distance) OR (short target enabled AND close < entry price - short target distance)))
