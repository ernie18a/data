Calculation: On each chart bar, raw trail bands are EMA(close, trail EMA lookback) ± ATR(ATR lookback) × base multiplier × adaptive factor; when enabled, the adaptive factor is the low factor below the low ATR-percentile cutoff, the high factor above the high cutoff, and the neutral factor otherwise, using ATR percentile rank over its lookback. With ratcheting enabled, the lower band in long direction is max(raw lower band, previous lower band), and the upper band in short direction is min(raw upper band, previous upper band); reset the active band to raw on a direction flip. Direction turns long when close > previous upper band, turns short when close < previous lower band, and otherwise persists.
Calculation: Regime score = ADX score × ADX weight + inverted Choppiness score × Choppiness weight + R² score × R² weight; ADX score = min(ADX(high, low, close, ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback) / ADX normalization × score scale, score scale); inverted Choppiness score = clamp(score scale − Choppiness Index(high, low, true range, Choppiness lookback), 0, score scale); R² score = R²(close, bar_index, regression lookback) × score scale.
Calculation: Quality score(side) = quality scale × (HTF points + volume points + RSI points + regime score × regime weight + break points) / (base denominator + valid HTF maximum points + valid volume maximum points). HTF points are the maximum when the last completed T1 close and EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) agree with the side, neutral points when equal, and zero otherwise; score and denominator contribution are zero when HTF scoring is off or data is unavailable. Volume points equal the maximum points when volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume threshold, otherwise zero; score and denominator contribution are zero when volume scoring is off or volume data is unavailable. RSI points equal the maximum points when RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above its midpoint for long or below it for short, otherwise zero. Break points = min(abs(close − prior upper band for long or prior lower band for short) / ATR, break distance cap) / break distance cap × break maximum points.
Calculation: Entry is the signal-bar close. Initial stop distance = ATR at entry × selected stop multiplier; round stop and target levels to tick size, with at least one tick of distance and separation between targets. Long stop = entry − stop distance; short stop = entry + stop distance. Long TP1/TP2/TP3 = entry + stop distance × their respective target multipliers; short TP1/TP2/TP3 = entry − stop distance × their respective target multipliers. After TP1 is first touched, if the position remains open and break-even is enabled, effective stop becomes entry from the following bar.
Calculation: Warm-up bars = maximum of ATR lookback, trail EMA lookback, regression lookback, ADX lookback × ADX warm-up multiplier, Choppiness lookback, and adaptive ATR-percentile lookback when adaptive is enabled or volume lookback otherwise, plus warm-up offset.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close after warm-up, previous direction is short AND close > previous upper trail band AND ATR > 0 AND quality score(long) ≥ minimum quality score AND (choppy skip is disabled OR regime score ≥ choppy cutoff) AND (hard HTF alignment is disabled OR last completed T1 close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar)) AND, when the range filter is enabled, previous ATR > 0 AND high − low ≤ previous ATR × maximum range multiple AND, when the extension filter is enabled, previous ATR > 0 AND abs(close − EMA(close, trail EMA lookback)) ≤ previous ATR × maximum extension multiple AND no long position is open.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close after warm-up, previous direction is long AND close < previous lower trail band AND ATR > 0 AND quality score(short) ≥ minimum quality score AND (choppy skip is disabled OR regime score ≥ choppy cutoff) AND (hard HTF alignment is disabled OR last completed T1 close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar)) AND, when the range filter is enabled, previous ATR > 0 AND high − low ≤ previous ATR × maximum range multiple AND, when the extension filter is enabled, previous ATR > 0 AND abs(close − EMA(close, trail EMA lookback)) ≤ previous ATR × maximum extension multiple AND no short position is open.
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, low ≤ effective stop AND open < TP3.
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, TP1 is first touched: not previously reached AND (open ≥ TP1 OR (high ≥ TP1 AND NOT (low ≤ effective stop AND open < TP3))).
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, TP2 is first touched: not previously reached AND (open ≥ TP2 OR (high ≥ TP2 AND NOT (low ≤ effective stop AND open < TP3))).
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, TP3 is first touched: not previously reached AND (open ≥ TP3 OR (high ≥ TP3 AND low > effective stop)).
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, high ≥ effective stop AND open > TP3.
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, TP1 is first touched: not previously reached AND (open ≤ TP1 OR (low ≤ TP1 AND NOT (high ≥ effective stop AND open > TP3))).
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, TP2 is first touched: not previously reached AND (open ≤ TP2 OR (low ≤ TP2 AND NOT (high ≥ effective stop AND open > TP3))).
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, TP3 is first touched: not previously reached AND (open ≤ TP3 OR (low ≤ TP3 AND high < effective stop)).
