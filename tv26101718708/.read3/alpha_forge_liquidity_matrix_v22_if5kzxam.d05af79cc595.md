Calculation: Upper/lower liquidity zones = pivot highs/lows of T1 high/low sampled on chart bars, using the swing lookback on both sides; initialize each pivot’s weight from the base weight plus optional relative-volume weighting at the pivot, merge levels within ATR × merge-distance parameter × sensitivity multiplier using a weighted-average level and combined weight, update weights by the configured rejection-volume increment or decay, and select the nearest upper level above close and lower level below close; zone half-height = ATR × zone-height parameter. T1 values use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Upper rejection strength = (high − close) / max(high − (upper level − zone half-height), minimum tick size) when high exceeds the upper boundary, otherwise zero; lower rejection strength = (close − low) / max((lower level + zone half-height) − low, minimum tick size) when low is below the lower boundary, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Upper base sweep event = an upper level exists, high exceeds its upper boundary, and close is below the upper boundary in wick-reclaim mode or below the level in close-reclaim modes; upper wick is at least ATR × minimum sweep-wick parameter × structural-exit multiplier; upper rejection strength is at least rejection-close parameter × structural-exit multiplier; zone weight meets the minimum sweep-weight parameter; volume meets its threshold when volume confirmation is enabled; datetime is in either configured session when session filtering is enabled; close is at or below EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, bar still forming) when strict trend filtering is selected; and no prior upper base sweep occurred within the cooldown parameter. ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, bar still forming) and SMA(volume, volume lookback, T1, bar still forming) are sampled on chart bars; relative volume is T1 volume divided by that SMA, using the neutral relative-volume value when the SMA is missing or zero.
Calculation: Lower base sweep event = a lower level exists, low is below its lower boundary, and close is above the lower boundary in wick-reclaim mode or above the level in close-reclaim modes; lower wick is at least ATR × minimum sweep-wick parameter × structural-exit multiplier; lower rejection strength is at least rejection-close parameter × structural-exit multiplier; zone weight meets the minimum sweep-weight parameter; volume meets its threshold when volume confirmation is enabled; datetime is in either configured session when session filtering is enabled; close is at or above EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, bar still forming) when strict trend filtering is selected; and no prior lower base sweep occurred within the cooldown parameter.
Calculation: Upper sweep = upper base sweep event outside follow-through mode; in follow-through mode, a later chart bar within the follow-through window has high no greater than the sweep high plus minimum tick size, close below the sweep level, and close below the sweep close.
Calculation: Lower sweep = lower base sweep event outside follow-through mode; in follow-through mode, a later chart bar within the follow-through window has low no less than the sweep low minus minimum tick size, close above the sweep level, and close above the sweep close.
Long: long entries enabled AND lower sweep OR long entries enabled AND short entries disabled AND opposite warnings enabled AND upper sweep
Short: short entries enabled AND upper sweep OR short entries enabled AND long entries disabled AND opposite warnings enabled AND lower sweep
Exit: Long, long entries enabled AND structural exits enabled AND upper base sweep event AND upper zone weight meets the separate structural-exit weight parameter AND close < open AND close < upper level − ATR × structural-exit buffer parameter
Exit: Short, short entries enabled AND structural exits enabled AND lower base sweep event AND lower zone weight meets the separate structural-exit weight parameter AND close > open AND close > lower level + ATR × structural-exit buffer parameter
