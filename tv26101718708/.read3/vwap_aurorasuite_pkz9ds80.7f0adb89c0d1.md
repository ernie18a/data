Calculation: normalized sentiment = the sum of the available votes divided by their count; each vote is 1 when its comparison is true, −1 when the reverse comparison is true, and 0 on equality: close versus VWAP(hlc3, volume, T1 reset), that VWAP versus its value sentiment lookback bars ago, close versus the selected London or US session VWAP, close versus low AVWAP, and close versus high AVWAP; the VWAPs reset or accumulate on chart bars, using the still-forming T1 period for the T1 reset
