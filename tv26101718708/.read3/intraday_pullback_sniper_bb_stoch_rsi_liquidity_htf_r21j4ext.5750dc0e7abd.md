Calculation: Bollinger Bands(source, lookback, deviation) = SMA(source, lookback) ± deviation × standard deviation(source, lookback), calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Stoch RSI K = SMA(Stochastic(RSI(close, RSI lookback), Stoch lookback), K smoothing), calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Swing points(short lookback) and swing points(long lookback) are confirmed pivot highs and lows with the corresponding number of bars on each side; highs and lows alternate, and a same-side point replaces the current one only when more extreme. Apply the configured backstep and minimum-leg filter: zero when off, deviation × ATR(ATR lookback) in ATR mode, or close × deviation / 100 in percent mode; on T2 bars, ATR mode uses the mean high-low range over its configured averaging lookback.
Calculation: A structure break on a timeframe's bars occurs when close is above the latest unconsumed confirmed long-lookback swing high or below the latest unconsumed confirmed long-lookback swing low; consume the broken level. The prevailing structure direction classifies a break as BOS or CHOCH, and either is an MSB.
Calculation: Bias(T1) uses the last completed T1 bar: EMA bias is bullish when its close is above EMA(close, long lookback) and bearish when below; structure bias is the direction of its latest structure break. The configured bias source selects EMA, structure, or both; when both disagree, structure defines the turning direction and the configured transition policy determines whether that direction or neither is allowed.
Calculation: Entry structure(T2) uses confirmed short-lookback pivots from completed T2 candles read inside chart bars. A higher low followed by a higher high is a bullish sequence; a lower high followed by a lower low is bearish. A structure-break condition is chart close above the latest confirmed T2 swing high or below the latest confirmed T2 swing low. A sequence qualifies if completed after setup arming or within the configured lead before it; the signal check uses structure known before processing the current chart bar's T2 candles.
Calculation: Selected liquidity levels are configured significant and/or simple long-lookback swing highs and lows from source timeframes T3 and onward, ranked by distance separately by side and kind, with the configured count retained. A source-timeframe close through a level removes it from the active levels; significance is established when a later swing exceeds the preceding swing in the defending direction. On chart bars, a bullish sweep is rollingLowest(low, sweep lookback) below a selected low level while close is above it; a bearish sweep is rollingHighest(high, sweep lookback) above a selected high level while close is below it. Source levels use the current source-timeframe bar until it closes.
Calculation: Bullish setup zones are the configured OR of an existing bullish order block or breaker, a significant bullish sweep, a previously recorded bullish chart structure break within its freshness lookback, a bar within ATR tolerance of the current session low, a bar within ATR tolerance of an enabled session opening-range edge, or the Bollinger zone. Bearish zones mirror these conditions using bearish blocks, sweeps, breaks, session highs, and opening-range edges. Order blocks use the wick range of the nearest opposing-close candle within the search lookback before a qualifying chart structure break, subject to the configured source and displacement filters; a close through a block flips it into an opposite-side breaker when configured, otherwise removes it, and a close through a breaker removes it. Session and opening-range proximity means the bar's high-low range intersects the level's ATR tolerance band.
Calculation: An armed setup is refreshed by each qualifying setup after the signal check and expires when its chart-bar age reaches the configured expiry converted to chart bars by ceiling(expiry count / chart-bar duration in minutes), with the configured minimum expiry floor. A signal requires a setup armed on an earlier chart bar, consumes both armed setups, and starts cooldown; a new qualifying setup on the signal bar may arm for a later signal.
Calculation: Long confluence count is the count of bullish or turning-up bias, recent oversold Stoch RSI, current bullish Bollinger rejection, confirmed bullish entry sequence, bullish sweep, close inside a bullish order block or breaker, and open session. Short confluence count mirrors these conditions with bearish or turning-down bias, overbought Stoch RSI, bearish rejection, bearish sequence and sweep, bearish block or breaker, and open session. No confluence direction is shown when both counts are zero; ties favor long.
Long: The configured bias is bullish or turning up.
Short: The configured bias is bearish or turning down.
Long: An accepted swing high is above its prior accepted swing high (higher high) or an accepted swing low is above its prior accepted swing low (higher low), using the configured chart-timeframe label degree.
Short: An accepted swing high is at or below its prior accepted swing high (lower high) or an accepted swing low is at or below its prior accepted swing low (lower low), using the configured chart-timeframe label degree.
Long: A bullish chart structure break passes the configured order-block source and displacement filters and an opposing-close candle exists within the search lookback, or a bearish order block closes above its upper boundary while configured to become a bullish breaker.
Short: A bearish chart structure break passes the configured order-block source and displacement filters and an opposing-close candle exists within the search lookback, or a bullish order block closes below its lower boundary while configured to become a bearish breaker.
Long: Long confluence count > 0 AND long confluence count ≥ short confluence count.
Short: Short confluence count > long confluence count.
Long: low ≤ lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close ≤ lower Bollinger Band + Bollinger band width × rejection fraction AND lowest(Stoch RSI K, extreme lookback) ≤ oversold threshold AND (strict bias is disabled OR configured bias permits long) AND (setup-zone filter is disabled OR a configured bullish setup zone is present).
Short: high ≥ upper Bollinger Band AND close < upper Bollinger Band AND close ≥ upper Bollinger Band − Bollinger band width × rejection fraction AND highest(Stoch RSI K, extreme lookback) ≥ overbought threshold AND (strict bias is disabled OR configured bias permits short) AND (setup-zone filter is disabled OR a configured bearish setup zone is present).
Long: An earlier long setup is armed and unexpired AND (entry structure is disabled OR the configured entry mode's bullish structure condition is met: break, higher-low-then-higher-high sequence, or both) AND (entry structure is enabled in break mode OR current bullish Bollinger rejection is present) AND (strict bias is disabled OR configured bias permits long) AND (liquidity sweep is not required OR bullish sweep is present) AND (an enabled session is open within its configured hours OR no session is enabled) AND chart bars since the last signal > ceiling(cooldown count / chart-bar duration in minutes).
Short: An earlier short setup is armed and unexpired AND (entry structure is disabled OR the configured entry mode's bearish structure condition is met: break, lower-high-then-lower-low sequence, or both) AND (entry structure is enabled in break mode OR current bearish Bollinger rejection is present) AND (strict bias is disabled OR configured bias permits short) AND (liquidity sweep is not required OR bearish sweep is present) AND (an enabled session is open within its configured hours OR no session is enabled) AND chart bars since the last signal > ceiling(cooldown count / chart-bar duration in minutes) AND the long signal condition is false.
Long: Chart close > the latest unconsumed confirmed long-lookback swing high on chart bars.
Short: Chart close < the latest unconsumed confirmed long-lookback swing low on chart bars.
Long: Bullish sweep is true on the current chart bar AND was false on the prior chart bar.
Short: Bearish sweep is true on the current chart bar AND was false on the prior chart bar.
