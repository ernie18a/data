Calculation: VolumeProfile(period, rows) = histogram of volume over rows equal price bins spanning the lowest low to the highest high of the period's bars, built from the finer of chart bars and T2 bars within the period (only bars inside the session window when custom session is enabled); each bar's volume, or 1 per bar when the period's total volume is zero, is split across the bins its high-to-low range covers in proportion to overlap
Calculation: ValueAreaHigh(period, rows, share) = top edge of the highest bin in the value area of VolumeProfile(period, rows), which starts at the bin with the largest volume (lowest such bin on ties) and repeatedly adds the adjacent bin with more volume (upper bin on ties) until included volume is at least share * total volume
Calculation: ValueAreaLow(period, rows, share) = bottom edge of the lowest bin in the same value area
Calculation: BullGap(f) = low_T4[f] > high_T4[f+2] AND low_T4[f] - high_T4[f+2] >= gap multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T4)[f]
Calculation: BearGap(f) = high_T4[f] < low_T4[f+2] AND low_T4[f+2] - high_T4[f] >= gap multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T4)[f]
Calculation: Aligned(bottom, top) = at least one enabled level L satisfies bottom - tolerance <= L <= top + tolerance, where tolerance = tolerance multiple * ATR(ATR lookback, T4)[0] and L is one of ValueAreaHigh(T1, last completed bar, rows, share), ValueAreaLow(T1, last completed bar, rows, share), ValueAreaHigh(T3, last completed bar, rows, share), ValueAreaLow(T3, last completed bar, rows, share), or the center of the VolumeProfile(T1, last completed bar, rows) bin with the largest volume (lowest such bin on ties)
