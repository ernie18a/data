Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars in the configured opening-range window, finalized when the window ends
Long: Opening range is locked AND the configured breakout trigger (close in close mode or high in wick mode) > opening-range high AND (minimum-range multiplier is zero OR opening-range high - opening-range low >= ATR(high, low, close, lookback) on chart bars × minimum-range multiplier) AND (first-break-per-side option is disabled OR no earlier upward breakout occurred in the current opening-range session)
Short: Opening range is locked AND the configured breakout trigger (close in close mode or low in wick mode) < opening-range low AND (minimum-range multiplier is zero OR opening-range high - opening-range low >= ATR(high, low, close, lookback) on chart bars × minimum-range multiplier) AND (first-break-per-side option is disabled OR no earlier downward breakout occurred in the current opening-range session)
