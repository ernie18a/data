Calculation: Confirmed swing pools = PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) and PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback), clustered separately by side when prices are within pool-tolerance × ATR(ATR lookback); each pool price is the running mean, only the newest pool-count pools per side are kept, and a pool is sweep-eligible only after a bar following its latest update.
Calculation: Sweep events = on confirmed chart bars, an unswept eligible high pool is swept when high > pool price and, if close-back is required, close < pool price; an unswept eligible low pool is swept when low < pool price and, if required, close > pool price. Each pool is swept once; the latest event sets bearish direction for a high sweep and bullish direction for a low sweep, with a high sweep taking precedence if both occur on one bar.
Calculation: Displacement = close > open or close < open, respectively, and abs(close - open) / ATR(ATR lookback) >= displacement threshold.
Calculation: Zones = on confirmed chart bars, a bullish FVG zone has bounds [high[2], low] when FVG detection is enabled, low > high[2], low - high[2] >= minimum gap × ATR, and bullish displacement; a bearish FVG has bounds [high, low[2]] when high < low[2], low[2] - high >= minimum gap × ATR, and bearish displacement. A bullish order-block zone has bounds [low[1], high[1]] when OB detection is enabled, bullish displacement, and close[1] < open[1]; a bearish order block has the same bounds when bearish displacement and close[1] > open[1]. Suppress an order block when an FVG of the same direction lies fully inside it; retain at most the newest live-zone limit.
Calculation: Dealing equilibrium = (Highest(high, dealing-range lookback) + Lowest(low, dealing-range lookback)) / 2, calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Signed delta = zero when order flow is off or volume history is absent; otherwise, on chart bars, sum each available T1 intrabar's volume as positive when its close > open, negative when its close < open, and zero when equal, including a still-forming intrabar when available. Use volume × (2 × (close - low) / (high - low) - 1) instead when bar-proxy mode is selected or intrabar data is unavailable or invalid; use zero when high = low. T1 is a lower timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Delta fraction = clamp(Signed delta / volume, -1, 1) when order flow is active and volume > 0, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = cumulative sum of Signed delta on chart bars.
Calculation: Zone score = min(100, 100 × total / sum of component weights), or zero if the sum of weights is zero. For a zone at formation, total is: displacement weight × min(1, (abs(close - open) / ATR) / (2 × displacement threshold)); plus participation weight × min(1, max(0, RVOL - 1)) when volume history exists, otherwise half that weight, where RVOL = volume / max(SMA(volume, RVOL lookback), epsilon); plus sweep weight when the latest sweep direction matches the zone and its age is at most the sweep-to-displacement window; plus imbalance weight × min(1, (FVG height / ATR) / imbalance-saturation parameter), using zero FVG height for an order block; plus HTF weight when zone direction matches the available bias from the last completed T2 close versus EMA(close, T2, HTF EMA lookback); plus premium-discount weight when enabled and a bullish zone has close <= Dealing equilibrium or a bearish zone has close > Dealing equilibrium, half that weight when disabled or unavailable, otherwise zero; plus volume-profile weight × a profile bonus; plus order-flow weight × max(0, Delta fraction × zone direction) when active, otherwise half that weight. Calculate the rolling chart-bar Volume Profile from the profile lookback and bin-count parameters by distributing each bar's volume equally across bins intersecting its low-high range; expand value area from the POC toward the higher-volume adjacent bin until the value-area fraction is reached. A prior POC becomes an untested POC when a new POC differs by more than one bin width and remains untested until a later bar spans its price. The profile bonus is one when the POC or an untested POC lies inside the zone, otherwise 0.6 when the zone overlaps value area, otherwise zero; when profile use is off or volume history is absent, use 0.5.
Calculation: Setup R:R = min(abs(target - entry) / abs(entry - stop), maximum R:R), when risk is positive. For a bullish zone, entry is its top and stop is its bottom minus stop-buffer × ATR; for a bearish zone, entry is its bottom and stop is its top plus stop-buffer × ATR. Target is the nearest unswept opposing pool, or the nearest untested POC when that target mode is selected; if the selected target is unavailable, or fixed-R mode is selected, use entry plus or minus fixed-R × risk in the zone direction.
