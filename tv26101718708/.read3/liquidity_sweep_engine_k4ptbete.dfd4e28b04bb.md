Calculation: ATR = ATR(lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = sign(EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar) - EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, last completed bar)), evaluated on chart bars using the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = VWAP(hlc3) on chart bars, reset at each T2 period
Calculation: Lower-timeframe delta = the sum of signed volume over completed T3 bars within each confirmed chart bar, plus the prior chart bar’s sum; signed volume is positive when close > open and negative when close < open
Calculation: Sweep levels = confirmed chart-bar swing highs/lows, higher-timeframe swing highs/lows from the last completed T1 bar, equal highs/lows formed by merging nearby swing levels, highs/lows of the last completed T4, T5 and T6 bars, configured session and opening-range highs/lows, round-price levels, and session volume-profile value-area highs/lows
Calculation: Level quality = capped weighted sum of normalized level weight, touch count, age, ATR distance to the directional extreme in a lookback, and a bonus for proximity to a round-price level
Calculation: Confluence score = true applicable checks divided by applicable checks: relative volume at the sweep meets its threshold; lower-timeframe delta agrees; displacement has occurred; structure shift has occurred; a qualifying fair value gap has formed after the pierce; T1 trend agrees; swept level is in discount for a long or premium for a short relative to the midpoint of the lookback high-low range; ATR percentile rank meets its threshold; sweep extreme is below VWAP for a long or above VWAP for a short; current bar is in an active window when intraday; SMT divergence agrees when comparison-instrument high/low data are available. Relative-volume, delta and SMT checks are captured at the sweep; displacement, structure shift and fair value gap checks persist once seen during confirmation; other checks use the current chart bar.
Calculation: Sweep target = from unpierced forward liquidity levels compatible with the position side and opposing unfilled fair value gap edges beyond entry, each with ATR offset and at least minimum reward/risk; a major level meets the configured weight or touch threshold. For standard confluence choose the nearest eligible pool; for high confluence choose the nearest major pool, or nearest eligible pool if none; for very high confluence choose the next major pool if available, otherwise the nearest major or eligible pool. If none exists use the next round-price level, or the minimum reward target if unavailable. Floor reward/risk to a whole multiple and cap it.
Calculation: Front-market target = the nearest eligible forward liquidity pool as defined for Sweep target, floored to a whole reward/risk multiple and capped; use the minimum reward target if no pool is eligible
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, while flat and past the post-exit cooldown, select the highest-quality eligible support-level sweep: price pierces below the level, the deepest penetration is within the configured ATR limits, and a close reclaims above the level within the reclaim window. A too-shallow pierce updates the level as a touch without signaling; a too-deep pierce or missed reclaim retires it; each level signals at most once. The level quality meets its minimum. The setup remains valid only until its confirmation window expires or price falls below the sweep extreme. Within that window, including the sweep bar, displacement or a structure shift occurs, Confluence score meets its configured threshold, the bar is outside scheduled-news windows when the intraday timeframe is within the configured ceiling, the stop wick fits within the fixed ATR risk including its buffer, and a target is available by the Sweep target rule. Displacement is a bullish candle whose body meets its ATR threshold and whose close is in the configured upper part of its range; structure shift is a close above the last confirmed chart-bar pivot high. A qualifying bullish fair value gap has low above high two bars earlier by at least its ATR threshold.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, while flat and past the post-exit cooldown, select the highest-quality eligible resistance-level sweep: price pierces above the level, the deepest penetration is within the configured ATR limits, and a close reclaims below the level within the reclaim window. A too-shallow pierce updates the level as a touch without signaling; a too-deep pierce or missed reclaim retires it; each level signals at most once. The level quality meets its minimum. The setup remains valid only until its confirmation window expires or price rises above the sweep extreme. Within that window, including the sweep bar, displacement or a structure shift occurs, Confluence score meets its configured threshold, the bar is outside scheduled-news windows when the intraday timeframe is within the configured ceiling, the stop wick fits within the fixed ATR risk including its buffer, and a target is available by the Sweep target rule. Displacement is a bearish candle whose body meets its ATR threshold and whose close is in the configured lower part of its range; structure shift is a close below the last confirmed chart-bar pivot low. A qualifying bearish fair value gap has high below low two bars earlier by at least its ATR threshold.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, when flat with no active front-market idea and ATR is available, higher-timeframe trend is bullish, or trend is neutral and close >= anchored VWAP
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, when flat with no active front-market idea and ATR is available, higher-timeframe trend is bearish, or trend is neutral and close < anchored VWAP
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= fixed stop OR high >= selected target OR elapsed chart bars >= hold-period multiple * min(planned reward/risk, hold cap)
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= fixed stop OR low <= selected target OR elapsed chart bars >= hold-period multiple * min(planned reward/risk, hold cap)
