Calculation: Relative strength(A, B, n) = (A / A[n]) / (B / B[n]) - 1, where A and B are close series from T1; current values use the still-forming T1 bar and calculations are evaluated on chart bars using T1 values
Calculation: Relative strength change(A, B, n, k) = Relative strength(A, B, n) - Relative strength(A[k], B[k], n)
Calculation: Upper market aligned = Relative strength(industry ETF close, sector ETF close, relative strength lookback) > 0 AND Relative strength(sector ETF close, broad market ETF close, relative strength lookback) > 0
Calculation: Upper market building = Relative strength change(industry ETF close, sector ETF close, relative strength lookback, lag) > 0 AND Relative strength change(sector ETF close, broad market ETF close, relative strength lookback, lag) > 0 AND Relative strength(industry ETF close, sector ETF close, relative strength lookback) > -near-positive allowance AND Relative strength(sector ETF close, broad market ETF close, relative strength lookback) > -near-positive allowance
Calculation: Upper market support = upper market aligned OR upper market building
Calculation: Early breakout event = T1 close > Highest(high[1], early breakout lookback, T1) AND prior T1 close <= Highest(high[2], early breakout lookback, T1), evaluated on chart bars using T1 values
Calculation: Major breakout event = T1 close > Highest(high[1], major breakout lookback, T1) AND prior T1 close <= Highest(high[2], major breakout lookback, T1), evaluated on chart bars using T1 values
Calculation: Early setup condition = 0 <= 100 * (Highest(high[1], early breakout lookback, T1) - T1 close) / Highest(high[1], early breakout lookback, T1) <= maximum early setup distance AND Relative strength change(stock close, industry ETF close, relative strength lookback, lag) > 0 AND T1 close > SMA(close, trend SMA lookback, T1) AND EMA(close, fast EMA lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, fast EMA lookback, T1)[lag] AND SMA(close, trend SMA lookback, T1) >= SMA(close, trend SMA lookback, T1)[lag] AND upper market support; current T1 values use the still-forming bar, and the condition is evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: Early setup active = early setup condition is true on the current chart bar or within the preceding setup memory bars
Calculation: Qualifying major breakout = major breakout event AND Relative strength(stock close, industry ETF close, relative strength lookback) > 0 AND upper market support
Calculation: Trend failure = persistent trend was active before the current chart bar AND T1 close < primary breakout level * (1 - trend failure tolerance / 100) AND T1 close < SMA(close, trend SMA lookback, T1), evaluated on chart bars using the still-forming T1 values
Calculation: Persistent trend = starts on a qualifying major breakout while inactive, stores Highest(high[1], major breakout lookback, T1) as the primary breakout level, and remains active until trend failure; state is tracked on chart bars
Calculation: Breakout holding = the most recent early breakout event or qualifying major breakout occurred 1 through breakout holding bars ago on chart bars, and no trend failure occurred after it
Long: NOT trend failure AND (persistent trend OR (early breakout event AND Relative strength change(stock close, industry ETF close, relative strength lookback, lag) > 0 AND upper market support) OR breakout holding OR early setup active OR (100 * (Highest(high, base lookback, T1) - Lowest(low, base lookback, T1)) / T1 close <= maximum base width AND Relative strength change(stock close, industry ETF close, relative strength lookback, lag) > 0 AND T1 close > base trend proximity factor * SMA(close, trend SMA lookback, T1)) OR 100 * (T1 close / SMA(close, trend SMA lookback, T1) - 1) > high extension threshold); evaluated on chart bars using the still-forming T1 values; setup and breakout ages count chart bars
Exit: long, trend failure
