Calculation: opening-range high = highest high of chart bars in the opening-range interval, reset each day and frozen when the interval ends
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest low of chart bars in the opening-range interval, reset each day and frozen when the interval ends
Calculation: tick-rounded distance(d) = max(1, round((d / points-per-price-point) / minimum tick)) × minimum tick
Long: confirmed chart bar AND opening range locked AND within the configured trade session and time zone AND position flat AND (daily trade limit disabled OR no trade yet today) AND long entries enabled AND ((close-break mode AND close > opening-range high) OR (wick-break mode AND high > opening-range high))
Short: confirmed chart bar AND opening range locked AND within the configured trade session and time zone AND position flat AND (daily trade limit disabled OR no trade yet today) AND short entries enabled AND ((close-break mode AND close < opening-range low) OR (wick-break mode AND low < opening-range low))
Exit: long, low <= entry price - tick-rounded distance(stop-loss parameter) OR high >= entry price + tick-rounded distance(take-profit parameter) OR (highest high since trailing activation >= entry price + tick-rounded distance(trailing-activation parameter) AND low <= highest high since trailing activation - tick-rounded distance(trailing-offset parameter)) OR (end-of-day close enabled AND current chart bar outside the configured trade session AND previous chart bar within it)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + tick-rounded distance(stop-loss parameter) OR low <= entry price - tick-rounded distance(take-profit parameter) OR (lowest low since trailing activation <= entry price - tick-rounded distance(trailing-activation parameter) AND high >= lowest low since trailing activation + tick-rounded distance(trailing-offset parameter)) OR (end-of-day close enabled AND current chart bar outside the configured trade session AND previous chart bar within it)
