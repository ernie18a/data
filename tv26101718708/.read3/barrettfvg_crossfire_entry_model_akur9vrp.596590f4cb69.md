Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = [high[gap spacing], low] when low > high[gap spacing] and gap size as a percentage of close meets the minimum gap; create only on confirmed chart bars within the configured history window, excluding bars that cross or immediately follow a calendar day or week boundary; after the configured mitigation delay, shrink its upper edge using low or close from that many bars ago, according to the FVG mitigation setting, and discard it when the upper edge <= lower edge.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = [high, low[gap spacing]] when high < low[gap spacing] and gap size as a percentage of close meets the minimum gap; create only on confirmed chart bars within the configured history window, excluding bars that cross or immediately follow a calendar day or week boundary; after the configured mitigation delay, shrink its lower edge using high or close from that many bars ago, according to the FVG mitigation setting, and discard it when the lower edge >= upper edge.
Calculation: Crossfire zone = the positive-width intersection of a new gap and an unfilled opposite-direction gap, oriented in the new gap's direction and assigned the configured initial star count; consume both gaps. A new gap first intersects and flips live opposite-direction zones, replacing each with its positive-width intersection and incrementing its star count, then intersects unfilled opposite-direction gaps. Consume the new gap if it flips or strikes any gap; otherwise retain it as an unfilled gap. When enabled, merge a new base zone into an overlapping live same-direction zone by union, retaining the existing star count. A bullish zone is mitigated when its selected wick or close is <= its bottom; a bearish zone is mitigated when its selected wick or close is >= its top.
Calculation: Zone retest = a live zone created before the current confirmed chart bar whose band overlaps the current bar's high-low range after the preceding bar did not overlap it.
Calculation: Active VWAP = NY-session anchored VWAP during the NY session after the configured real-time grace period from its anchor; otherwise use TDO anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Entry price = close on the signal bar.
Calculation: Long stop = min(selected long reference - stop buffer, entry price - minimum tick), where the reference is the lowest low over the structure lookback excluding the signal bar or the retested bullish zone bottom, according to the SL method.
Calculation: Short stop = max(selected short reference + stop buffer, entry price + minimum tick), where the reference is the highest high over the structure lookback excluding the signal bar or the retested bearish zone top, according to the SL method.
Calculation: Long take-profit = entry price + abs(entry price - long stop) * target R multiple; short take-profit = entry price - abs(entry price - short stop) * target R multiple.
Long: Zone retest is bullish.
Short: Zone retest is bearish.
Long: (BOS requirement disabled AND a bullish zone retest has at least the minimum star count) OR (BOS requirement enabled AND the most recent qualifying bullish retest is within the BOS window AND close crosses above the most recently confirmed chart-bar Pivot High(high, BOS pivot lookback) plus the BOS buffer in ticks); AND the displacement filter is disabled or high - low >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum range multiple AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body ratio (use zero when high = low) AND the directional-close requirement is disabled or close > open; AND the VWAP filter is disabled or Active VWAP is available and close > Active VWAP; AND the session gate is disabled or bar time is in either configured New York time window; AND there is no prior long entry or current bar index - last long entry bar index >= the cooldown; evaluate on confirmed chart bars.
Short: (BOS requirement disabled AND a bearish zone retest has at least the minimum star count) OR (BOS requirement enabled AND the most recent qualifying bearish retest is within the BOS window AND close crosses below the most recently confirmed chart-bar Pivot Low(low, BOS pivot lookback) minus the BOS buffer in ticks); AND the displacement filter is disabled or high - low >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum range multiple AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body ratio (use zero when high = low) AND the directional-close requirement is disabled or close < open; AND the VWAP filter is disabled or Active VWAP is available and close < Active VWAP; AND the session gate is disabled or bar time is in either configured New York time window; AND there is no prior short entry or current bar index - last short entry bar index >= the cooldown; evaluate on confirmed chart bars.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= long stop OR high >= long take-profit.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= short stop OR low <= short take-profit.
