Calculation: Flow delta = classified buy volume − classified sell volume; classify volume using the selected estimator: sum T1 intrabar volume by candle direction (split flat bars evenly), Bulk Volume Classification from the configured price change and its return standard deviation, or candle-range close-location weighting; substitute unit volume for missing or zero volume. Aggregate T1 intrabars into each chart bar, using currently available intrabars, including forming bars.
Calculation: OFI z-score = selected classic rolling mean-and-standard-deviation z-score or robust rolling median-and-MAD z-score of flow delta; use the configured stationarization lookback, return zero when the selected denominator is zero, and clamp to the configured bounds.
Calculation: Consensus = mean of the short-, medium-, and long-lookback EMAs of OFI z-score; the lookbacks are ordered short < medium < long. Consensus alignment is true when all three EMA signs agree and are nonzero.
Calculation: Cascade direction = buy excitation − sell excitation; net cascade = cascade direction divided by total excitation, or zero when total excitation is zero; cascade strength = percentile rank of total excitation over its configured window. Each excitation starts at zero and updates recursively as prior excitation times exponential decay plus excitation gain times same-side imbalance when absolute flow imbalance exceeds the configured event threshold, otherwise only decaying.
Calculation: Cross-asset confirmation = the sign of the reference asset’s prior completed-bar z-score of volume-weighted close-location delta matches the current OFI z-score sign, and the absolute prior z-score exceeds its configured threshold. Calculate the reference asset delta and its rolling mean-and-standard-deviation z-score on chart-timeframe bars with lookahead off; use the prior bar’s z-score. If cross-asset confirmation is disabled, it passes.
Calculation: Expected move in ATR = nonnegative Kyle lambda times flow delta divided by ATR; Kyle lambda is the nonnegative correlation of flow delta and configured price change times their price-change standard deviation, divided by flow-delta standard deviation, over the configured regression lookback.
Calculation: Liquidity stress = mean of the rolling percentile ranks of flow toxicity, thin-book vacuum, and Amihud illiquidity. Flow toxicity is rolling sum of absolute flow delta divided by rolling sum of classified volume; thin-book vacuum is true range divided by ATR and relative volume, with the configured relative-volume floor; relative volume is effective volume divided by its configured moving average. Amihud illiquidity is absolute configured-price return divided by configured price times effective volume, or zero when its denominator is nonpositive. Use the configured toxicity, relative-volume, ATR, and percentile-rank windows; effective volume substitutes unit volume for missing or zero volume.
Calculation: Regime = the highest of the following scores, breaking ties in this order: trend, squeeze, cascade blast, mean reversion, liquidity shock. Trend score weights absolute consensus, adds an alignment bonus when consensus is aligned, adds a cascade term when cascade direction and consensus have the same sign, and adds a capped absolute expected move in ATR term. Squeeze score adds squeeze sensitivity when Bollinger width is below the ATR-channel width, plus a compression term based on the positive difference between a configured compression target and true range divided by ATR. Cascade-blast score weights cascade strength, adds a release bonus when squeeze was on at the configured prior-bar lag and is now off, and weights absolute net cascade. Mean-reversion score adds a divergence bonus when configured-price change and consensus signs differ and absolute consensus exceeds its threshold, plus weighted absolute VWAP stretch in ATR and weighted RSI extremity. Liquidity-shock score weights liquidity stress and adds a weighted positive excess of true range divided by ATR over its configured threshold. Use configured lookbacks, widths, weights, thresholds, caps, and bonuses; calculate on chart bars using the current bar and prior bars.
Calculation: Flow bias = bullish if consensus exceeds its configured positive threshold or buy pressure reaches its configured threshold; otherwise bearish if consensus is below the negative consensus threshold or sell pressure reaches its configured threshold; otherwise neutral. Buy pressure is the configured bounded transformation of OFI z-score mapped to a symmetric pressure scale; sell pressure is its complement.
Calculation: Cascade triggers = bullish when the closed-bar gate passes (if confirmation is enabled), cascade direction is positive, cascade strength exceeds its threshold, OFI z-score exceeds its threshold, and cross-asset confirmation passes; bearish uses the corresponding negative comparisons. A trigger occurs only when its condition is true and was false on the prior chart bar. When confirmation is disabled, evaluate on the forming chart bar; otherwise evaluate on closed chart bars.
Calculation: VSA volume = chart volume, or, when time-of-day normalization is enabled, chart volume divided by the updated EMA of volume for the same time-of-day slot. Slot size and EMA session memory are configured parameters; update the slot EMA using the current volume. VSA spread rank and volume rank are percentile ranks of high − low and VSA volume over the configured history window.
Long: OFI z-score >= 0 OR expected move in ATR > 0 OR cascade direction > 0 OR flow bias is bullish OR (regime is mean reversion AND flow bias is bearish AND neither cascade trigger is true) OR (volume exists and is positive AND low <= highest-prior-bar lookback low AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= shakeout close-location threshold AND VSA volume rank >= event-volume percentile threshold) OR (close < prior close AND VSA spread rank >= wide-spread percentile threshold AND VSA volume rank >= stopping-volume percentile threshold AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= stopping-volume close-location threshold) OR (close < prior close AND VSA volume >= highest VSA volume over its configured window AND VSA spread rank >= climax-spread percentile threshold). For zero bar range, use the VSA close-location fallback.
Short: OFI z-score < 0 OR expected move in ATR < 0 OR cascade direction < 0 OR flow bias is bearish OR (regime is mean reversion AND flow bias is bullish AND neither cascade trigger is true) OR (volume exists and is positive AND high >= highest-prior-bar lookback high AND (close − low) / (high − low) <= upthrust close-location threshold AND VSA volume rank >= event-volume percentile threshold) OR (close > prior close AND VSA volume >= highest VSA volume over its configured window AND VSA spread rank >= climax-spread percentile threshold). For zero bar range, use the VSA close-location fallback.
Exit: none. Entering the opposite side closes the current position.
Option: none.
