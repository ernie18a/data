Calculation: T1 undercut/reclaim = on chart bars sampling current/forming T1 values, at least one of the separate short and intermediate EMA lookbacks has low below its EMA on any of the last T1-lookback chart bars, current close above that EMA, and, when the shared green-reclaim filter is enabled, current close above open.
Calculation: T1 tap = on chart bars sampling current/forming T1 values, low above and within the shared tap percentage above either the short- or intermediate-lookback EMA, and close above that EMA.
Calculation: T2 undercut/reclaim = when T2 detection is enabled, on chart bars sampling current/forming T2 values, low below the separate T2 EMA on any of the last T2-lookback chart bars, current close above that EMA, and, when the shared green-reclaim filter is enabled, current close above open.
Calculation: T2 tap = when T2 detection is enabled, on chart bars sampling current/forming T2 values, low above and within the shared tap percentage above the T2 EMA, and close above that EMA.
Calculation: U&R signal = a T1 or T2 undercut/reclaim, or a T1 or T2 tap when no T1 or T2 undercut/reclaim is present.
Calculation: U&R score = one point each for current T1 close in or above the configured upper portion of its range, T1 relative volume at or above the separate U&R-score threshold, lower wick above the configured minimum portion of range, sector proxy close above open, and a T2 undercut/reclaim.
Calculation: T1 relative volume = rounded 100 times selected current T1 volume divided by SMA(volume[1], separate relative-volume lookback, T1, completed T1 bars), sampled on chart bars; selected volume is current volume, or, in projected mode after the configured elapsed-time threshold, current volume plus current volume divided by elapsed time times remaining session time.
Calculation: Pattern body = abs(close - open) from confirmed T1 OHLC sampled on chart bars; historical offsets are chart-bar offsets in those sampled series.
Calculation: Pattern range = high - low from confirmed T1 OHLC sampled on chart bars; historical offsets are chart-bar offsets in those sampled series.
Calculation: Pattern lower wick = min(open, close) - low from confirmed T1 OHLC sampled on chart bars; historical offsets are chart-bar offsets in those sampled series.
Calculation: Pattern upper wick = high - max(open, close) from confirmed T1 OHLC sampled on chart bars; historical offsets are chart-bar offsets in those sampled series.
Calculation: Bullish Engulfing = current close > open AND prior close < prior open AND current open <= min(prior open, prior close) AND current close >= max(prior open, prior close) AND current body >= prior body times an engulfing-body multiplier AND prior body >= prior range times a prior-body minimum fraction AND current body >= current range times a current-body minimum fraction.
Calculation: Dragonfly Doji = current range > 0 AND current body <= current range times a maximum-body fraction AND current lower wick >= current range times a minimum-lower-wick fraction AND current upper wick <= current range times a maximum-upper-wick fraction.
Calculation: Morning Star = candle two chart bars ago closes below its open AND its range > 0 AND its body >= its range times a minimum-body fraction AND prior candle body <= its body times a maximum-middle-body fraction AND current candle closes above its open AND current close >= the bearish candle's body bottom plus its body times a close fraction AND current body >= current range times a minimum-body fraction.
Calculation: Piercing Line = prior close < prior open AND prior range > 0 AND prior body >= prior range times a minimum-body fraction AND current open < prior low AND current close > current open AND current close >= prior bearish body bottom plus prior body times a penetration fraction AND current close < prior open AND current body >= current range times a minimum-body fraction.
Calculation: Hammer = current range > 0 AND current body <= current range times a maximum-body fraction AND current lower wick >= current body times a wick multiplier AND current upper wick <= current range times a maximum-upper-wick fraction AND min(current open, current close) >= current low plus current range times a body-position fraction AND prior close < prior open.
Calculation: Bullish Harami = prior close < prior open AND prior range > 0 AND prior body >= prior range times a minimum-body fraction AND current close > current open AND current open > min(prior open, prior close) AND current close < max(prior open, prior close) AND current body <= prior body times a maximum-body fraction.
Calculation: Three White Soldiers = the three consecutive candles close above their opens; each body >= its range times a minimum-body fraction and each upper wick <= its range times a maximum-wick fraction; each candle after the first opens strictly inside the prior candle's body; and each close is above the preceding close.
