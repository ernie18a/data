Calculation: Bullish structural origin = the latest confirmed pivot low using the shared pivot left and right lookbacks, with its ATR(structural ATR lookback) sampled on the confirmation bar; replace the origin with each later lower low and retain the sampled ATR.
Calculation: Bearish structural origin = the latest confirmed pivot high using the shared pivot left and right lookbacks, with its ATR(structural ATR lookback) sampled on the confirmation bar; replace the origin with each later higher high and retain the sampled ATR.
Calculation: Bullish destination = the highest high from the parameterized destination scan offset before the bullish origin through setup activation, then each later higher high while the setup is active.
Calculation: Bearish destination = the lowest low from the parameterized destination scan offset before the bearish origin through setup activation, then each later lower low while the setup is active.
Calculation: Pullback trigger(side, previous destination, current destination, origin, retracement coefficient) = the first touched Fibonacci retracement level, with long touch when low ≤ level and short touch when high ≥ level; on live chart bars update the destination from the chart high or low before testing; on historical chart bars without enabled intrabar data, test the updated level on an extension bar and the unchanged level otherwise; with historical intrabar checking enabled, process completed T1 bars in order on each chart bar, update the destination from each intrabar high or low, and test both the pre-update and post-update levels, choosing the post-update level if both are touched.
Long: the bullish setup is initially inactive, has reached its age limit since its origin, or its previous position hit its stop loss; bars since bullish origin ≥ minimum displacement duration; (close − bullish origin) / bars since bullish origin ≥ bullish origin ATR × displacement ATR multiplier.
Short: the bearish setup is initially inactive, has reached its age limit since its origin, or its previous position hit its stop loss; bars since bearish origin ≥ minimum displacement duration; (bearish origin − close) / bars since bearish origin ≥ bearish origin ATR × displacement ATR multiplier.
Long: a bullish pullback is pending; Pullback trigger(long, previous bullish destination, current bullish destination, bullish origin, retracement coefficient) returns a level; bars since the last destination extension ≤ entry-wait duration; the absolute difference between the returned level and (bullish origin − ATR(stop lookback at entry) × stop offset multiplier) ≤ ATR(stop lookback at entry) × stop-distance cap; and same-side position blocking is disabled or no long position is open.
Short: a bearish pullback is pending; Pullback trigger(short, previous bearish destination, current bearish destination, bearish origin, retracement coefficient) returns a level; bars since the last destination extension ≤ entry-wait duration; the absolute difference between the returned level and (bearish origin + ATR(stop lookback at entry) × stop offset multiplier) ≤ ATR(stop lookback at entry) × stop-distance cap; and same-side position blocking is disabled or no short position is open.
Exit: Long, low ≤ bullish origin − ATR(stop lookback at entry) × stop offset multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + absolute(entry price − stop price) × reward-risk multiplier OR chart bars since entry ≥ position-duration limit.
Exit: Short, high ≥ bearish origin + ATR(stop lookback at entry) × stop offset multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − absolute(entry price − stop price) × reward-risk multiplier OR chart bars since entry ≥ position-duration limit.
