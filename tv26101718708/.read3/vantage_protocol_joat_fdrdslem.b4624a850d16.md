Calculation: efficiency gate = initially closed; open when SMA(correlation(close, bar index, efficiency lookback)^2, R² smoothing lookback) > SMA(smoothed R², efficiency lookback) + threshold multiplier × stdev(smoothed R², efficiency lookback); close when open and smoothed R² < that threshold × hysteresis factor; otherwise retain prior state
Calculation: chop score = 1 when SUM(abs(close - close[1]), chop lookback) = 0, otherwise 1 - abs(close - close[chop lookback]) / SUM(abs(close - close[1]), chop lookback)
Calculation: regime = bullish when efficiency gate is open, chop score ≤ chop threshold, and H-infinity filter(close, filter order, noise, disturbance) - its prior-bar value ≥ 0; bearish when the first two conditions hold and that difference < 0; ranging otherwise
Calculation: CVD slope = EMA(cumulative sum of signed volume, CVD smoothing lookback) - its prior-bar value; signed volume is volume when close > open, -volume when close < open, and 0 otherwise; cumulative sum starts at 0
Calculation: volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), using 1 as the denominator when the SMA is 0
Long: regime is bullish
Short: regime is bearish
Long: position is flat AND regime is bullish AND JMA(Laguerre RSI(close, Laguerre alpha), momentum JMA period, momentum JMA phase, momentum JMA power) > bull momentum threshold AND CVD slope > 0 AND volume ratio ≥ minimum volume ratio AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is within the configured session in its configured time zone) AND cooldown bars have elapsed since the latest exit or chart start
Short: position is flat AND regime is bearish AND JMA(Laguerre RSI(close, Laguerre alpha), momentum JMA period, momentum JMA phase, momentum JMA power) < bear momentum threshold AND CVD slope < 0 AND volume ratio ≥ minimum volume ratio AND (session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is within the configured session in its configured time zone) AND cooldown bars have elapsed since the latest exit or chart start
Exit: long, regime is not bullish OR JMA(Laguerre RSI(close, Laguerre alpha), momentum JMA period, momentum JMA phase, momentum JMA power) < long momentum exit threshold OR low ≤ entry price - DEMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR smoothing lookback) × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + DEMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR smoothing lookback) × take-profit multiplier
Exit: short, regime is not bearish OR JMA(Laguerre RSI(close, Laguerre alpha), momentum JMA period, momentum JMA phase, momentum JMA power) > short momentum exit threshold OR high ≥ entry price + DEMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR smoothing lookback) × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price - DEMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR smoothing lookback) × take-profit multiplier
