Calculation: Fib regime = direction of EMA(EMA(hlc3, basis lookback), basis lookback) versus its prior bar, carrying the prior direction when equal; on T1 use the last completed bar, and count flips in chart bars.
Calculation: Trail price and direction = initialize direction bullish when close > EMA(close, trail lookback), otherwise bearish, with price EMA(close, trail lookback) minus/plus ATR(trail ATR lookback) × trail distance; while bullish, a close below the prior trail flips bearish and resets the trail above the EMA only when EMA(close, alpha fast lookback) < EMA(close, alpha slow lookback), otherwise hold the prior trail, and without a flip use the greater of the prior trail and EMA minus ATR distance; while bearish, a close above the prior trail flips bullish and resets the trail below the EMA only when EMA(close, alpha fast lookback) > EMA(close, alpha slow lookback), otherwise hold the prior trail, and without a flip use the lesser of the prior trail and EMA plus ATR distance.
Calculation: Bull score = count of enabled bullish nodes: macro Hull > its value two chart bars ago, fast Hull > its value two chart bars ago, bullish trail direction, active bullish CHoCH, close within 1.5 active-zone ranges of either zone edge with active bullish direction, normalized momentum > momentum threshold, bullish Ultron gate, and volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier; the volume node also counts for the bear score.
Calculation: Bear score = count of enabled bearish nodes: macro Hull < its value two chart bars ago, fast Hull < its value two chart bars ago, bearish trail direction, active bearish CHoCH, close within 1.5 active-zone ranges of either zone edge with active bearish direction, normalized momentum < negative momentum threshold, bearish Ultron gate, and volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Normalized momentum = (close − close[momentum lookback]) / ATR(trail ATR lookback).
Calculation: Bullish Ultron gate = gate disabled OR Fib regime bullish OR close > EMA(close, gate EMA lookback); bearish Ultron gate = gate disabled OR Fib regime bearish OR close < EMA(close, gate EMA lookback).
Calculation: Base bullish Fib gate = each selected LTF/HTF source has bullish Fib regime, enabled LTF/HTF band filters have close above their respective double-EMA hlc3 basis, and enabled LTF/HTF cooldowns have bullish regime or chart bars since that source's flip greater than its cooldown; Base bearish Fib gate uses bearish regimes and close below the respective bases. Calculate HTF values on T1 and use its last completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish trigger Fib gate = base bullish Fib gate OR enabled soft override with bull score ≥ override score and bullish trail direction; bearish trigger Fib gate = base bearish Fib gate OR enabled soft override with bear score ≥ override score and bearish trail direction.
Calculation: Active zone confidence = count divided by nine of matching T1 CHoCH direction, corresponding base Fib gate clear, nearest unbroken low/high liquidity pivot within four ATRs, price position below 0.45/above 0.55 of trailing high-low range, volume confirmation, directional normalized momentum, NY AM or London-NY session, matching HTF Fib regime, and active matching-direction AOI; evaluate on a CHoCH event and retain until the next event.
