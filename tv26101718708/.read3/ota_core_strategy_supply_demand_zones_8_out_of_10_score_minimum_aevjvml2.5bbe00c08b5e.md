Calculation: Zone score = minimum of the score cap and the sum of tight-base points (base length at or below the short-base threshold: high points; otherwise at or below the long-base threshold: low points; otherwise zero), explosive-leg points (mean leg body / mean base body at or above the high ratio threshold: high points; otherwise at or above the low ratio threshold: low points; otherwise zero; ratio is zero when mean base body is zero), freshness points (zero tests: high points; one test: low points; otherwise zero), close-strength points, volume-spike points, and ATR-expansion points. Close-strength points apply when the most recent-in-time leg candle has positive range and its close location from low is at or above the demand threshold or at or below the supply threshold. Volume-spike points apply when mean volume over the volume lookback beginning at the base start is positive and volume on that leg candle exceeds it times the volume multiplier. ATR-expansion points apply when current-bar ATR is positive and summed leg ranges exceed ATR times leg length times the range-expansion multiplier. Each component's thresholds and point values are separate parameters.
Calculation: Eligible demand zone = a base of 1 through the maximum base length bars immediately preceding a bullish leg of 1 through the maximum leg length bars, with the leg's most recent-in-time bar offset from the maximum leg length through lookback length minus maximum base length minus maximum leg length minus two; skip a base whose end offset is at or beyond the lookback length. Every leg candle has close > open and abs(close-open)/close * 100 at least the minimum leg move (zero when close <= 0). Mean base body is less than mean leg body / the leg-to-base body ratio, and every base body is less than mean leg body times the maximum base-body fraction. Proximal is the maximum max(open,close) over the base; distal is the minimum low over the base. Tests count bars more recent than the leg whose low <= proximal and high >= distal. Mitigated means any such bar closes below distal. The zone passes when its score is at least the minimum score, (mitigated-zone display is enabled or it is not mitigated), and (fresh-only is disabled or tests = 0). Only the first zone-display cap passing candidates in scan order are drawn; the highest-scoring passing candidate, first on ties, supplies the summary setup.
Calculation: Eligible supply zone = a base of 1 through the maximum base length bars immediately preceding a bearish leg of 1 through the maximum leg length bars, with the leg's most recent-in-time bar offset from the maximum leg length through lookback length minus maximum base length minus maximum leg length minus two; skip a base whose end offset is at or beyond the lookback length. Every leg candle has close < open and abs(close-open)/close * 100 at least the minimum leg move (zero when close <= 0). Mean base body is less than mean leg body / the leg-to-base body ratio, and every base body is less than mean leg body times the maximum base-body fraction. Proximal is the minimum min(open,close) over the base; distal is the maximum high over the base. Tests count bars more recent than the leg whose low <= distal and high >= proximal. Mitigated means any such bar closes above distal. The zone passes when its score is at least the minimum score, (mitigated-zone display is enabled or it is not mitigated), and (fresh-only is disabled or tests = 0). Only the first zone-display cap passing candidates in scan order are drawn; the highest-scoring passing candidate, first on ties, supplies the summary setup.
Calculation: Live demand bounds = proximal highest(max(open,close), rolling-zone lookback), distal lowest(low, rolling-zone lookback). Live supply bounds = proximal lowest(min(open,close), rolling-zone lookback), distal highest(high, rolling-zone lookback).
Calculation: Demand approach = close is above live demand proximal and at most live demand proximal times (1 + approach distance), and close is at least live demand distal. Supply approach = close is below live supply proximal and at least live supply proximal times (1 - approach distance), and close is at most live supply distal.
Calculation: IV/HV ratio = IV proxy / historical volatility when historical volatility > 0, otherwise the fallback ratio; historical volatility = stdev(log(close/close[1]), historical-volatility lookback) * sqrt(annualization factor) * 100; IV proxy = ATR(close, ATR lookback) / close * sqrt(annualization factor) * 100 when close > 0, otherwise historical volatility.
Calculation: Rounded strike(price) = nearest multiple of the high-price strike increment when price is at least the high-price threshold; otherwise nearest multiple of the mid-price strike increment when price is at least the mid-price threshold; otherwise nearest multiple of the low-price strike increment.
Calculation: Spread width(price) = rounded raw width, where raw width is price times spread-width percent / 100; use the high-price strike increment above the high-price threshold, the mid-price strike increment above the mid-price threshold, and otherwise the greater of rounded raw width and the minimum low-price width.
Calculation: Option delta inputs = spot close, volatility IV proxy / 100, risk-free-rate parameter, and time to expiry equal to the midpoint of the minimum and maximum DTE divided by the year-length parameter. Select delta-target strikes by bisection between the configured lower and upper multiples of spot for the configured iteration count, then round the strike.
Long: eligible demand zone AND not mitigated OR close <= live demand proximal AND close >= live demand distal OR Demand approach AND NOT Demand approach[1]
Short: eligible supply zone AND not mitigated OR close >= live supply proximal AND close <= live supply distal OR Supply approach AND NOT Supply approach[1]
Exit: long, close < distal of an identified demand zone
Exit: short, close > distal of an identified supply zone
Option: sell put, demand zone or live demand area or first bar of Demand approach AND IV/HV ratio >= rich threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; short-leg strike targets the negative short-leg put-delta parameter
Option: buy put, demand zone or live demand area or first bar of Demand approach AND IV/HV ratio >= rich threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; long-leg strike targets the negative long-leg put-delta parameter
Option: buy call, demand zone or live demand area or first bar of Demand approach AND IV/HV ratio < rich threshold AND IV/HV ratio > cheap threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; strike is Rounded strike(demand distal - demand zone width * stop buffer percent / 100)
Option: buy call, demand zone or live demand area or first bar of Demand approach AND IV/HV ratio <= cheap threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; strike is Rounded strike(demand distal - demand zone width * stop buffer percent / 100)
Option: sell call, demand zone or live demand area or first bar of Demand approach AND IV/HV ratio <= cheap threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; strike is Rounded strike(demand distal - demand zone width * stop buffer percent / 100 + Spread width(close))
Option: sell call, supply zone or live supply area or first bar of Supply approach AND IV/HV ratio >= rich threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; short-leg strike targets the short-leg call-delta parameter
Option: buy call, supply zone or live supply area or first bar of Supply approach AND IV/HV ratio >= rich threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; long-leg strike targets the long-leg call-delta parameter
Option: buy put, supply zone or live supply area or first bar of Supply approach AND IV/HV ratio < rich threshold AND IV/HV ratio > cheap threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; strike is Rounded strike(supply distal + supply zone width * stop buffer percent / 100)
Option: buy put, supply zone or live supply area or first bar of Supply approach AND IV/HV ratio <= cheap threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; strike is Rounded strike(supply distal + supply zone width * stop buffer percent / 100)
Option: sell put, supply zone or live supply area or first bar of Supply approach AND IV/HV ratio <= cheap threshold AND expiry DTE is within the configured range; strike is Rounded strike(supply distal + supply zone width * stop buffer percent / 100 - Spread width(close))
