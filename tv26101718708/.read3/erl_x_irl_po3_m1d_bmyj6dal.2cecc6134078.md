Calculation: T1 dealing range = confirmed T1 swing highs and lows using the T1 swing-strength parameter on each side of a pivot, from completed T1 candles; use the latest pivots, or the highest and lowest pivots in the T1 lookback when range holding is enabled. Follow the range on closed chart bars. When held, keep its edges until the reclaim allowance is exceeded by consecutive chart closes beyond an edge; keep that edge spent, reset the far edge to the last opposite T1 swing before the break, and track the new near edge on chart bars until a post-break T1 swing confirms.
Calculation: Peer level = the extreme peer high or low in the T1 swing-confirmation window around the corresponding range swing, using last completed T1 candles. A peer takes that level when a later peer T1 candle or closed chart bar trades through it.
Calculation: OTE = the band between the configured retracement fractions of the leg from the lowest low for a long setup or highest high for a short setup, from just before the first sweep breach through the MSS bar, to the first qualifying opposite swing after the sweep and beyond the MSS reference. Confirm that swing using either completed T1 swing pivots or the configured chart swing; the chart option uses a two-candle swing. If ratcheting is enabled, remeasure at higher qualifying swings until price trades into the band.
Calculation: PD-array count = the number of distinct FVG, volume imbalance, suspension block, inversion gap, breaker, and OTE candidates formed after the sweep and counted after MSS; count each type once. A suspension block replaces the FVG and volume imbalance of its triplet, and an FVG absorbs a volume imbalance on either seam. Removing an array after a close through it does not reduce the count.
Calculation: Breaker = in the chart-bar leg from the last far-side chart swing before the sweep to the sweep extreme, find a candle against the setup whose real body meets the configured ATR body floor, whose gap surrounds it, and whose close passes through the latest prior chart swing on that side. Its block is the wick-to-wick range of the first opposite-close candle within the configured preceding-bar search and its consecutive same-color run, up to the configured block-length limit. It confirms when a close passes through the block toward the setup; apply the optional rally-away filter using block body height.
Long: A closed chart bar wicks below the active, locked T1 range low after the prior chart close was above it; the range width meets the minimum-range multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback), price has not closed above the range high, and the range low has not already been used for a setup. The wick is reclaimed within the configured T1 reclaim allowance converted to chart bars. If the failed-push filter is enabled, require a confirmed T1 swing high after the range high that reaches the configured fraction of the range, or a range-high swing that raided the prior extreme and closed back inside.
Short: A closed chart bar wicks above the active, locked T1 range high after the prior chart close was below it; the range width meets the minimum-range multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback), price has not closed below the range low, and the range high has not already been used for a setup. The wick is reclaimed within the configured T1 reclaim allowance converted to chart bars. If the failed-push filter is enabled, require a confirmed T1 swing low after the range low that reaches the configured fraction of the range, or a range-low swing that raided the prior extreme and closed back inside.
Long: After a sellside sweep and before its expiry, a closed chart bar closes above the latest confirmed chart swing high formed after the failed-push swing and below the range high, closes back above the swept range low without closing above the range high, and is at least the configured displacement multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) above the sweep low extreme. If enabled, require an SMT: at least one corresponding peer low has not taken its peer level. If enabled, require confirmation inside the configured New York session window. A range low can produce only one confirmed setup.
Short: After a buyside sweep and before its expiry, a closed chart bar closes below the latest confirmed chart swing low formed after the failed-push swing and above the range low, closes back below the swept range high without closing below the range low, and is at least the configured displacement multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) below the sweep high extreme. If enabled, require an SMT: at least one corresponding peer high has not taken its peer level. If enabled, require confirmation inside the configured New York session window. A range high can produce only one confirmed setup.
Long: After a qualifying sellside sweep and bullish MSS, a bullish FVG forms on consecutive closed chart bars, its height meets the configured gap-floor multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback), and it is unmitigated when counted and not absorbed by a suspension block or its own seam volume imbalance.
Short: After a qualifying buyside sweep and bearish MSS, a bearish FVG forms on consecutive closed chart bars, its height meets the configured gap-floor multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback), and it is unmitigated when counted and not absorbed by a suspension block or its own seam volume imbalance.
Long: After a qualifying sellside sweep and bullish MSS, a bullish volume imbalance forms on consecutive closed chart bars when the current candle body is above the prior candle body, the current low overlaps the prior high, and the body gap meets the configured gap-floor multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback); it is unmitigated when counted and not absorbed by its suspension block or an FVG seam.
Short: After a qualifying buyside sweep and bearish MSS, a bearish volume imbalance forms on consecutive closed chart bars when the current candle body is below the prior candle body, the current high overlaps the prior low, and the body gap meets the configured gap-floor multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback); it is unmitigated when counted and not absorbed by its suspension block or an FVG seam.
Long: After a qualifying sellside sweep and bullish MSS, three consecutive bullish chart candles have rising gaps between their bodies, the block height meets the configured gap-floor multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback), and the block is unmitigated when counted.
Short: After a qualifying buyside sweep and bearish MSS, three consecutive bearish chart candles have falling gaps between their bodies, the block height meets the configured gap-floor multiple of ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback), and the block is unmitigated when counted.
Long: After a qualifying sellside sweep and bullish MSS, an opposite-direction FVG formed after the failed-push swing is closed through in the bullish direction; count the nearest qualifying gap once.
Short: After a qualifying buyside sweep and bearish MSS, an opposite-direction FVG formed after the failed-push swing is closed through in the bearish direction; count the nearest qualifying gap once.

Short: A bearish breaker confirms under the Breaker calculation.
