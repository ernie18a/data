Calculation: ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: Displacement = at least one of the latest displacement-window chart bars has |close - open| >= displacement-body multiplier * ATR
Calculation: High-side sweep credit = on chart bars, high breaches an unswept chart swing high, prior rolling high, extreme-range candle high, prior completed session high, or prior completed T1/T2/T3 high; close must return below the breached level on that bar or within the sweep-confirmation window. The extreme from breach through confirmation must remain unbreached. Swing and extreme-range-candle levels expire after the sweep-freshness window; session and T1/T2/T3 levels expire after twice that window. An extreme-range candle has high - low > data-range multiplier * ATR. Retain only the configured number of latest swing and data-candle levels. A swing uses a symmetric swing-lookback pivot; a rolling high excludes the current bar.
Calculation: Low-side sweep credit = the inverse of High-side sweep credit: low breaches an unswept chart swing low, prior rolling low, extreme-range candle low, prior completed session low, or prior completed T1/T2/T3 low; close must return above the level within the sweep-confirmation window, and the breach-through-confirmation low must remain unbreached. A rolling low excludes the current bar.
Calculation: Long delivery = chart high-low overlaps a live bullish fair value gap from any of the four higher timeframes T1, T4, T5, T6 above the chart timeframe; the gap is formed on T bars where low > high[2] and close[1] > open[1], and remains live until a close below its lower edge. The tap must be within the sweep-freshness window; when sweep requirement is Required, it must also be within the sweep-to-gap pairing window of a low-side sweep. Evaluate on the current T bar when confirmed, and return values to chart bars with lookahead_off; historical evaluation may expose that T bar before its close.
Calculation: Short delivery = the inverse of Long delivery, using bearish T1/T4/T5/T6 fair value gaps formed where high < low[2] and close[1] < open[1], remaining live until a close above the upper edge; when sweep requirement is Required, pair the tap with a high-side sweep.
Calculation: Long SMT = a low-side sweep occurs while the configured correlated symbol has no recent-low breach in its latest correlation-lag window. On the correlated symbol, test the last completed chart-timeframe bar against its own prior low over a window of twice the chart swing lookback plus one; check for such breaches on the latest correlation-lag-window chart bars.
Calculation: Short SMT = a high-side sweep occurs while the configured correlated symbol has no recent-high breach in its latest correlation-lag window. On the correlated symbol, test the last completed chart-timeframe bar against its own prior high over a window of twice the chart swing lookback plus one; check for such breaches on the latest correlation-lag-window chart bars.
Calculation: Clear upside target = an unswept data-candle high or prior completed session/T1/T2/T3 high is above close by at least the target-distance parameter times ATR, or two unswept swing highs are within the equal-pool-tolerance parameter times ATR and both are that far above close. Clear downside target is the inverse using lows.
Calculation: Setup grade = one point for the IFVG inversion plus one point each for the direction-matched sweep, delivery, displacement, clear target, and SMT checks.
Calculation: Entry-economy gate = in one-trade-at-a-time mode, block a same-direction entry while its lane is held until the selected Trade Is Done At threshold, a stop, or the stall-duration parameter without a new SD rung; zero stall duration disables stall release. Block an opposite-direction entry while a lane is held unless the selected reversal proof passes: it must meet the reversal-grade parameter, meet displacement when that filter is enabled, and close beyond the far boundary of every live opposite zone; the structure-shift choice additionally requires a close beyond the prior bar's rolling extreme over the configured leg lookback. The Never choice blocks opposite entries while a lane is held. All Entries disables these lane blocks. In Graded sweep mode, also block a same-direction entry while an unfinished same-direction setup is active or within the entry-spacing parameter of its last print. In Both detection mode, a same-direction candidate overlapping or within the ATR-scaled zone-proximity parameter of a live zone merges with it and bypasses these blocks. A strictly higher-grade candidate from the same sweep replaces the earlier setup before its first SD rung is reached, or at any time in All Entries mode.
Calculation: SD unit = the absolute distance from the swing extreme to entry, the IFVG zone height, the full displacement-leg range, or the entry-to-stop distance, according to the selected SD source; if zero, fall back to swing-extreme-to-entry distance, then zone height.
Long: long direction enabled AND a confirmed chart-bar bearish fair value gap (low[2] > high) closes above the gap's upper edge within the gap-inversion window of its confirmed formation AND the gap meets both the minimum-tick and minimum-ATR-height parameters AND the selected detection mode admits it (standalone gap, same-direction gaps joined within one swing leg and inverted through their full union, or both) AND long setup grade is at least the selected minimum grade AND (sweep requirement is Required and a low-side sweep sponsors the gap within the sweep-to-gap pairing window, or sweep requirement is Graded and grade is at least two) AND Entry-economy gate passes AND, when the killzone filter is enabled, the print bar is in an enabled session. Grade and gates are evaluated when the setup prints; if printing is delayed, use the first confirmed close through the gap as the entry price. A gap can print only while within its inversion window.
Short: short direction enabled AND a confirmed chart-bar bullish fair value gap (low > high[2]) closes below the gap's lower edge within the gap-inversion window of its confirmed formation AND the gap meets both the minimum-tick and minimum-ATR-height parameters AND the selected detection mode admits it (standalone gap, same-direction gaps joined within one swing leg and inverted through their full union, or both) AND short setup grade is at least the selected minimum grade AND (sweep requirement is Required and a high-side sweep sponsors the gap within the sweep-to-gap pairing window, or sweep requirement is Graded and grade is at least two) AND Entry-economy gate passes AND, when the killzone filter is enabled, the print bar is in an enabled session. Grade and gates are evaluated when the setup prints; if printing is delayed, use the first confirmed close through the gap as the entry price. A gap can print only while within its inversion window.
Exit: long, the setup has not reached its TP AND a confirmed chart-bar close is below the IFVG zone's lower edge; OR the selected Trade Is Done At threshold is reached, with an SD rung reached when high touches entry + its selected rung multiplier * SD unit, or TP reached when close is at or above the nearest eligible unswept swing high, data-candle high, or prior completed session/T1/T2/T3 high beyond the target-distance parameter times ATR, falling back to the zone's upper edge plus that distance when no such pool exists.
Exit: short, the setup has not reached its TP AND a confirmed chart-bar close is above the IFVG zone's upper edge; OR the selected Trade Is Done At threshold is reached, with an SD rung reached when low touches entry - its selected rung multiplier * SD unit, or TP reached when close is at or below the nearest eligible unswept swing low, data-candle low, or prior completed session/T1/T2/T3 low beyond the target-distance parameter times ATR, falling back to the zone's lower edge minus that distance when no such pool exists.
