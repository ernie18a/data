Calculation: D(I, kind) = for each enabled indicator series I (MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), MACD histogram using those same MACD lookbacks, RSI(close, lookback), SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback), smoothing lookback), CCI(close, lookback), Momentum(close, lookback), OBV(close, volume), VW-MACD as VWMA(close, shorter VW-MACD lookback) minus VWMA(close, longer VW-MACD lookback), Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, lookback), MFI(high, low, close, volume, lookback), or the external indicator source), true when a requested divergence kind matches one of the most recent maximum-pivot-count confirmed pivot lows (positive kinds) or highs (negative kinds), with pivot-period bars on each side, within the maximum-bars limit. For each candidate, let n = current chart-bar index minus stored pivot position plus pivot period; require n greater than the minimum-offset parameter. Let s = 0 when unconfirmed divergences are allowed, otherwise s = 1. Positive kinds also require unconfirmed divergences allowed OR I > I[1] OR close > close[1]; negative kinds require unconfirmed divergences allowed OR I < I[1] OR close < close[1]. The comparison price is close in Close source mode, otherwise low for positive kinds and high for negative kinds; compare it at s with the candidate pivot value. Positive regular requires I[s] > I[n] and comparison price at s < pivot value; positive hidden requires I[s] < I[n] and comparison price at s > pivot value. Negative regular requires I[s] < I[n] and comparison price at s > pivot value; negative hidden requires I[s] > I[n] and comparison price at s < pivot value. For positive kinds, every intervening bar must have I and close on or above their respective straight lines between s and n; for negative kinds both must be on or below. D is true for at most one matching candidate per indicator and kind. The pivot-price source, pivot period, maximum-pivot-count, maximum-bars, minimum-offset, confirmation mode, enabled indicators, and divergence-type selection are parameters.
Calculation: D_count = number of true D(I, kind) results across enabled indicators and divergence kinds permitted by the divergence-type selection, counting each indicator-kind pair once.
Long: At least one enabled indicator has a positive regular or positive hidden divergence AND D_count is at least the minimum-divergence-count parameter.
Short: At least one enabled indicator has a negative regular or negative hidden divergence AND D_count is at least the minimum-divergence-count parameter.
