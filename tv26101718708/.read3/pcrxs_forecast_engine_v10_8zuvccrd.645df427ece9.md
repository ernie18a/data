Calculation: normalized oscillator = if ATR(close, ATR lookback) > 0 then 100 × (WMA(close, shorter lookback) − WMA(close, longer lookback)) / ATR(close, ATR lookback), otherwise WMA(close, shorter lookback) − WMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: cycle length = initial cycle length until three confirmed pivot highs exist; thereafter, after each new confirmed pivot high, clamp the mean of the two distances between the latest three pivot highs to the minimum and maximum cycle lengths; pivot highs use the same left and right pivot lookback
Calculation: cycle phase = 2π × bars since the latest confirmed pivot high / cycle length
Calculation: cycle amplitude = max(latest confirmed pivot-high price − latest confirmed pivot-low price, ATR(close, ATR lookback) × amplitude floor multiplier) / 2; initialize both pivot prices to close
Calculation: halving cycle value = cos(2π × max(current time − Bitcoin fourth halving time, 0) / halving period − halving phase offset)
Calculation: Bitcoin futures basis = if the BTC futures close is available and exceeds the futures validity threshold, then (BTC futures close − close) / close × 100, otherwise 0
Calculation: global index bullish count = count of Nikkei, Hang Seng, DAX, FTSE, and CAC 40 closes above their respective EMA(close, index EMA lookback); global risk-on means this count is at least the risk-on count threshold, and global risk-off means it is at most the risk-off count threshold
Calculation: projected price(offset) = trend × trend weight + short-cycle projection × short-cycle weight + medium-cycle projection × medium-cycle weight + macro adjustment + halving adjustment + USDT dominance adjustment + BTC dominance adjustment + futures adjustment + global risk adjustment + Asian index adjustment, where trend = close + linear regression slope(close, regression lookback) × offset; short-cycle projection = trend + cycle amplitude × sin(cycle phase + 2π × offset / cycle length + π); medium-cycle projection = trend + cycle amplitude × medium-cycle amplitude multiplier × sin(cycle phase / medium-cycle phase divisor + 2π × offset / (cycle length × medium-cycle phase divisor) + π); halving adjustment = cycle amplitude × halving cycle value × halving adjustment multiplier; macro adjustment sums: if S&P 500 close > its EMA(close, macro EMA lookback), positive S&P multiplier × offset × close / 100, otherwise negative S&P multiplier × offset × close / 100; if U.S. dollar index close > its EMA(close, macro EMA lookback), negative dollar multiplier × offset × close / 100, otherwise positive dollar multiplier × offset × close / 100; if gold close > its EMA(close, macro EMA lookback), negative gold multiplier × offset × close / 100, otherwise positive gold multiplier × offset × close / 100; USDT dominance adjustment uses its percentage change from the USDT momentum lookback when its value exceeds the USDT momentum validity threshold, otherwise zero, multiplied by the falling or rising USDT multiplier and offset × close / 100, positive when USDT dominance is below its value at the USDT direction lookback and negative when above; BTC dominance adjustment uses its percentage change from the BTC dominance momentum lookback when its value exceeds the BTC dominance momentum validity threshold, otherwise zero, multiplied by the bullish or bearish BTC dominance multiplier and offset × close / 100, positive when BTC dominance is above its EMA if the traded base currency is Bitcoin or below its EMA otherwise, negative otherwise; futures adjustment is Bitcoin futures basis × contango/backwardation multiplier × close / 100 when basis exceeds the contango threshold and is not above the overleveraged threshold, or is below the negative backwardation threshold, and is negative Bitcoin futures basis × overleveraged multiplier × close / 100 when basis exceeds the overleveraged threshold, otherwise zero; global risk adjustment is positive global risk-on multiplier × offset × close / 100 when global risk-on, negative global risk-off multiplier × offset × close / 100 when global risk-off, otherwise zero; Asian index adjustment is positive Asian multiplier × offset × close / 100 when both Nikkei and Hang Seng closes exceed their respective EMA(close, index EMA lookback), negative Asian multiplier × offset × close / 100 when neither does, otherwise zero. All external series in this calculation use chart-timeframe values.
Long: (crosses above(normalized oscillator, −standard deviation(normalized oscillator, oscillator deviation lookback)) AND close on chart bars > EMA(close, shorter structural lookback, T1 values on chart bars using lookahead-off; the T1 value is developing live and historical values appear on the final chart bar of each T1 bar) AND that EMA > EMA(close, longer structural lookback, T1 values on chart bars using lookahead-off; the T1 value is developing live and historical values appear on the final chart bar of each T1 bar) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI ceiling AND MACD histogram(close, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback) >= its value one chart bar ago AND Chaikin Money Flow(lookback) > long flow floor AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume factor AND halving cycle value > negative long halving threshold AND USDT dominance <= EMA(USDT dominance, dominance EMA lookback) AND (traded base currency is Bitcoin AND BTC dominance > EMA(BTC dominance, dominance EMA lookback) OR traded base currency is not Bitcoin AND BTC dominance < EMA(BTC dominance, dominance EMA lookback)) AND Bitcoin futures basis <= overleveraged threshold) OR (projected price(min(short forecast horizon, forecast horizon)) > close × upward forecast multiplier AND projected price(min(long forecast horizon, forecast horizon)) > close) OR (there exists a forecast offset i from step size through forecast horizon minus step size in step-size increments such that projected price(i) is below both projected price(i − step size) and projected price(min(i + step size, forecast horizon)), with fewer than the label limit local extrema at preceding offsets
Short: (crosses below(normalized oscillator, standard deviation(normalized oscillator, oscillator deviation lookback)) AND close on chart bars < EMA(close, shorter structural lookback, T1 values on chart bars using lookahead-off; the T1 value is developing live and historical values appear on the final chart bar of each T1 bar) AND that EMA < EMA(close, longer structural lookback, T1 values on chart bars using lookahead-off; the T1 value is developing live and historical values appear on the final chart bar of each T1 bar) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI floor AND MACD histogram(close, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback) <= its value one chart bar ago AND Chaikin Money Flow(lookback) < short flow ceiling AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume factor AND halving cycle value < short halving ceiling AND USDT dominance >= EMA(USDT dominance, dominance EMA lookback) AND (traded base currency is Bitcoin AND BTC dominance <= EMA(BTC dominance, dominance EMA lookback) OR traded base currency is not Bitcoin AND BTC dominance >= EMA(BTC dominance, dominance EMA lookback)) AND NOT (USDT dominance > highest(USDT dominance, near-high lookback) × near-high ratio AND USDT dominance < its value USDT direction lookback bars ago)) OR (projected price(min(short forecast horizon, forecast horizon)) < close × downward forecast multiplier AND projected price(min(long forecast horizon, forecast horizon)) < close) OR (there exists a forecast offset i from step size through forecast horizon minus step size in step-size increments such that projected price(i) is above both projected price(i − step size) and projected price(min(i + step size, forecast horizon)), with fewer than the label limit local extrema at preceding offsets
Exit: Long, crosses below(normalized oscillator, 0)
Exit: Short, crosses above(normalized oscillator, 0)
