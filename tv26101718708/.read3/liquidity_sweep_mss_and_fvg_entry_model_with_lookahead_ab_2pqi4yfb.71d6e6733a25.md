Calculation: Buy-side liquidity pool = on chart bars, the nearest enabled level above close from T1 high of the last completed T1 bar, or T1 high of the bar still forming with lookahead on when selected, and the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback); disable the T1 source when equal-only is selected and include the pivot source only when enabled, requiring the latest two confirmed pivot highs to be within the equal-level tolerance when equal-only is selected.
Calculation: Sell-side liquidity pool = on chart bars, the nearest enabled level below close from T1 low of the last completed T1 bar, or T1 low of the bar still forming with lookahead on when selected, and the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback); disable the T1 source when equal-only is selected and include the pivot source only when enabled, requiring the latest two confirmed pivot lows to be within the equal-level tolerance when equal-only is selected.
Calculation: Sweep extreme = the lowest low from the sell-side sweep through the bullish displacement bar, or the highest high from the buy-side sweep through the bearish displacement bar.
Calculation: Limit price = bullish FVG upper edge for proximal or lower edge for distal; bearish FVG lower edge for proximal or upper edge for distal; otherwise the midpoint of the gap edges. Bullish FVG edges are low and high[2]; bearish FVG edges are low[2] and high.
Calculation: Protective stop = sweep extreme minus ATR(ATR lookback) times stop-buffer parameter for long positions, or plus it for short positions.
Calculation: Target = the opposing liquidity pool captured on the sweep bar when opposing-pool mode is selected and that pool is beyond the limit price; otherwise the limit price plus or minus the R-multiple parameter times abs(limit price - protective stop), according to position side.
Long: (kill-zone restriction disabled OR datetime is in an enabled session window in the configured timezone) AND (daily-loss lock disabled OR strategy P&L since the current T1 boundary is greater than negative daily-loss limit) AND (flattening disabled OR datetime is outside the flatten window) AND low < sell-side liquidity pool AND that pool has not previously been swept AND NOT(high > buy-side liquidity pool AND that pool has not previously been swept) OR after that sell-side sweep and within the sweep-to-MSS limit, abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement multiplier AND close > latest confirmed Pivot High(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) OR after that bullish displacement, low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= minimum-gap-thickness parameter * tick size AND high[2] >= sweep extreme.
Short: (kill-zone restriction disabled OR datetime is in an enabled session window in the configured timezone) AND (daily-loss lock disabled OR strategy P&L since the current T1 boundary is greater than negative daily-loss limit) AND (flattening disabled OR datetime is outside the flatten window) AND high > buy-side liquidity pool AND that pool has not previously been swept AND NOT(low < sell-side liquidity pool AND that pool has not previously been swept) OR after that buy-side sweep and within the sweep-to-MSS limit, abs(close - open) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement multiplier AND close < latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) OR after that bearish displacement, low[2] > high AND low[2] - high >= minimum-gap-thickness parameter * tick size AND low[2] <= sweep extreme.
Long: while fewer than the unfilled-order duration parameter bars have elapsed since a valid bullish FVG, low <= limit price AND limit price > protective stop.
Short: while fewer than the unfilled-order duration parameter bars have elapsed since a valid bearish FVG, high >= limit price AND limit price < protective stop.
Exit: Long, low <= protective stop OR high >= target OR flattening is enabled AND datetime is within the flatten window in the configured timezone.
Exit: Short, high >= protective stop OR low <= target OR flattening is enabled AND datetime is within the flatten window in the configured timezone.
