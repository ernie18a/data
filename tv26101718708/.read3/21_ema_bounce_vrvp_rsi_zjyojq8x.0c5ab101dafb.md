Calculation: Shorter EMA = EMA(close, shorter lookback) on T1 bars using the still-forming T1 bar, with its value taken on chart bars
Calculation: Longer EMA = EMA(close, longer lookback) on T1 bars using the still-forming T1 bar, with its value taken on chart bars
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = (min(open, close) - low > abs(close - open) × pin-wick multiplier parameter AND high - max(open, close) < abs(close - open) × other-wick limit parameter) OR (prior close < prior open AND close > prior open AND open <= prior close)
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = (high - max(open, close) > abs(close - open) × pin-wick multiplier parameter AND min(open, close) - low < abs(close - open) × other-wick limit parameter) OR (prior close > prior open AND close < prior open AND open >= prior close)
Long: Shorter EMA > Longer EMA AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI midpoint parameter AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI upper parameter AND low <= Shorter EMA × (1 + touch-threshold parameter) AND close > Shorter EMA AND (pattern requirement parameter is disabled OR Bullish candle pattern)
Short: Shorter EMA < Longer EMA AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint parameter AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI lower parameter AND high >= Shorter EMA × (1 - touch-threshold parameter) AND close < Shorter EMA AND (pattern requirement parameter is disabled OR Bearish candle pattern)
