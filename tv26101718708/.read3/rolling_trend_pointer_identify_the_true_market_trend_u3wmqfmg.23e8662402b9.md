Calculation: raw VWAP distance = close >= Rolling VWAP(open, volume, VWAP lookback) ? high - Rolling VWAP(open, volume, VWAP lookback) : low - Rolling VWAP(open, volume, VWAP lookback)
Calculation: smoothed VWAP distance = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, or unsmoothed raw VWAP distance, using the smoothing lookback
Calculation: positive boundary = 2 × channel multiplier × sqrt(selected weighted variance of stored positive raw VWAP distances); select weighted population variance or weighted mean squared residuals according to the channel mode; weight each chart-bar sample by its positive volume, or 1 when volume is unavailable or nonpositive; boundary is available after the positive sample count reaches the VWAP lookback
Calculation: negative boundary = -2 × channel multiplier × sqrt(selected weighted variance of the absolute values of stored negative raw VWAP distances); select weighted population variance or weighted mean squared residuals according to the channel mode; weight each chart-bar sample by its positive volume, or 1 when volume is unavailable or nonpositive; boundary is available after the negative sample count reaches the VWAP lookback
Calculation: Anchored VWAP slope(T) = Anchored VWAP(source, T, accumulated on chart bars through the current forming T period) - Anchored VWAP(source, T, accumulated on chart bars through the current forming T period)[slope lookback chart bars]; T1 is the primary anchor period and T2, T3, and T4 are successively longer optional confirmation periods
Long: smoothed VWAP distance > positive boundary
Short: smoothed VWAP distance < negative boundary
Long: Anchored VWAP slope(T1) >= 0 AND (T2 confirmation disabled OR Anchored VWAP slope(T2) >= 0) AND (T3 confirmation disabled OR Anchored VWAP slope(T3) >= 0) AND (T4 confirmation disabled OR Anchored VWAP slope(T4) >= 0) AND (synchronized mode disabled OR smoothed VWAP distance >= 0)
Short: Anchored VWAP slope(T1) < 0 AND (T2 confirmation disabled OR Anchored VWAP slope(T2) < 0) AND (T3 confirmation disabled OR Anchored VWAP slope(T3) < 0) AND (T4 confirmation disabled OR Anchored VWAP slope(T4) < 0) AND (synchronized mode disabled OR smoothed VWAP distance < 0)
