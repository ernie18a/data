Calculation: Micro and meso resistance/support lines = on chart bars, join same-side pivot highs/lows with a line in configured log-price or price space; use separate pivot lengths, anchor counts, minimum pivot gaps, slope limits, touch tolerances, and minimum-touch parameters for micro and meso. Count touches when a pivot wick is within the line’s touch tolerance; count violations and broken lines from the configured body edge or pivot extreme crossing the tolerated line. Rank eligible lines by touch count and span, penalizing violations, breaks, excessive slope, and distance from close; deduplicate by slope, price, and ATR separation, then retain the configured top lines. Line quality is a bounded score from touch count, age, normalized slope, ATR distance from close, and violations, using separate score weights, scales, exponents, and decay parameters. Project each selected line on chart bars from its anchor price and slope.
Calculation: Mega resistance/support lines = use pivot highs/lows and ATR from T2, requested with gaps off and lookahead off; calculate and select lines on chart bars from the T2 series, using the T2 bar still forming in real time and the latest completed T2 bar historically. Join same-side pivots separated by the configured minimum T2 bars, in configured log-price or price space, with the configured maximum slope per day; count wick touches and body-edge violations using the greater of the percentage tolerance and the ATR-based tolerance. Rank eligible lines by touch count, span, configured length and extreme-anchor bonuses, and break penalty; prefer candidates within the configured violation limit, otherwise use the best-scoring candidate, then fall back to the oldest and newest macro pivots if no candidate exists. Project the selected line to chart-bar time; macro fallback pivots use chart bars and their separately configured parameters.
Long: Signals enabled AND chart timeframe <= T1 AND (a selected micro resistance line breaks upward on its first broken bar AND its quality score >= the signal quality threshold AND the configured micro-break confluence filter passes, if enabled; OR a selected micro resistance line retests within its micro retest window, has not already retested, and its quality score >= the signal quality threshold; OR an enabled meso resistance line breaks upward on its first broken bar or retests within its meso retest window without a prior retest, with quality score >= the signal quality threshold; OR an enabled mega resistance line breaks upward on its first broken bar or retests within its mega retest window without a prior retest). For each scale, resistance break means configured break source > line * (1 + that scale’s break tolerance); the break source is close, HL2, or high. Resistance retest means low <= stored broken line * (1 + retest tolerance) AND close > stored broken line. Retests require a confirmed chart bar when close-only mode is enabled. Breaks require a confirmed chart bar in close-only mode, a prior-bar unbroken state, and an active line; retests require a recorded break within the configured window and an uncompleted retest. Micro and meso breaks and retests require more than the configured cooldown since the prior same-scale, same-event signal; mega breaks and retests have the same cooldown. The micro-break confluence filter is disabled or requires an unbroken same-side micro line and meso line within the configured ATR band and midpoint distance; its higher setting additionally requires another unbroken micro line within that band and distance. Micro and meso line calculations run on chart bars.

For each scale, support break means configured break source < line * (1 - that scale’s break tolerance); the break source is close, HL2, or low. Breaks require a confirmed chart bar in close-only mode, a prior-bar unbroken state, and an active line; retests require a recorded break within the configured window and an uncompleted retest.
