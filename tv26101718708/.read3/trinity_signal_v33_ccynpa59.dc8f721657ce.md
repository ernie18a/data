Calculation: Bull momentum = Momentum(close, longer momentum lookback) > 0 AND Momentum(close, shorter momentum lookback) > 0
Calculation: Bear momentum = Momentum(close, longer momentum lookback) < 0 AND Momentum(close, shorter momentum lookback) < 0
Calculation: Bull score = count of true conditions: close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3); EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback); RSI(close, RSI lookback) is within the bull RSI range; high-volume condition; close > SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) AND SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) > SMA(close, longer SMA lookback)
Calculation: Bear score = count of true conditions: close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3); EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback); RSI(close, RSI lookback) is within the bear RSI range; high-volume condition; close < SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) AND SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) < SMA(close, longer SMA lookback)
Calculation: Raw bullish condition = Bull score >= minimum score AND (momentum filter disabled OR Bull momentum)
Calculation: Raw bearish condition = Bear score >= minimum score AND (momentum filter disabled OR Bear momentum)
Long: close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) OR EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) OR close > SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) AND SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) > SMA(close, longer SMA lookback) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= overbought cutoff AND (RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold cutoff OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) is within the bull RSI range) OR Bull momentum OR high-volume condition OR Bull score >= minimum score
Short: close <= VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) OR EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) <= EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) OR close < SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) AND SMA(close, shorter SMA lookback) < SMA(close, longer SMA lookback) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > overbought cutoff OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= oversold cutoff AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= overbought cutoff AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) is outside the bull RSI range AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) is within the bear RSI range OR Bear momentum OR Raw bearish condition
Exit: long, trim signals enabled AND Raw bullish condition AND (RSI(close, RSI lookback) > overbought cutoff OR close >= upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger multiplier) × upper-band proximity multiplier) AND trim cooldown elapsed since chart start or the prior trim signal
Exit: short, trim signals enabled AND Raw bearish condition AND (RSI(close, RSI lookback) < oversold cutoff OR close <= lower Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger multiplier) × lower-band proximity multiplier) AND trim cooldown elapsed since chart start or the prior trim signal
