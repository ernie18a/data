Calculation: absolute candle body = ABS(close - open)
Calculation: upper wick = high - MAX(close, open)
Calculation: lower wick = MIN(close, open) - low
Long: (low < low(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; T1 longer than T2) OR (secondary timeframe levels enabled AND low < low(T2, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars))) AND each of the prior N consecutive chart bars has close < open AND close > open AND (lower wick > absolute candle body OR absolute candle body > upper wick * P) AND (displacement filter disabled OR close - open > ATR(K) * D) AND (sweep-depth filter disabled OR low(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars) - low > S) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, E)) AND (range filter disabled OR high - low > ATR(K) * R)
Short: (high > high(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars; T1 longer than T2) OR (secondary timeframe levels enabled AND high > high(T2, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars))) AND each of the prior N consecutive chart bars has close > open AND close < open AND (upper wick > absolute candle body OR absolute candle body > lower wick * P) AND (displacement filter disabled OR open - close > ATR(K) * D) AND (sweep-depth filter disabled OR high - high(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars) > S) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, E)) AND (range filter disabled OR high - low > ATR(K) * R)
