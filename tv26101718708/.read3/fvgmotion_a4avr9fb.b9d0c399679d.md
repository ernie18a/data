Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the opening-range interval
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low during the opening-range interval
Long: During the trade window, when flat with no active setup, the selected sweep direction is sell-side (a bar low < opening-range low; the first sweep if the first-sweep-only parameter is enabled, otherwise the latest sweep), and a bullish FVG forms (current low > high[2] and current low − high[2] ≥ minimum-FVG-size parameter; bottom = high[2], top = current low); enter while flat when no bar has closed below the stored FVG bottom and close > max(previous-bar high, FVG-maker-candle high)
Short: During the trade window, when flat with no active setup, the selected sweep direction is buy-side (a bar high > opening-range high; the first sweep if the first-sweep-only parameter is enabled, otherwise the latest sweep), and a bearish FVG forms (low[2] > current high and low[2] − current high ≥ minimum-FVG-size parameter; bottom = current high, top = low[2]); enter while flat when no bar has closed above the stored FVG top and close < min(previous-bar low, FVG-maker-candle low)
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry + target-distance parameter OR (breakeven has not been armed and low ≤ entry − stop-distance parameter) OR (a bar's high has reached entry + breakeven-trigger parameter and low ≤ entry)
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry − target-distance parameter OR (breakeven has not been armed and high ≥ entry + stop-distance parameter) OR (a bar's low has reached entry − breakeven-trigger parameter and high ≥ entry)
