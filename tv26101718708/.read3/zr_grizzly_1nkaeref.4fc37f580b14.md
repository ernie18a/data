Calculation: T1 swing range = highest(high, swing lookback, T1) - lowest(low, swing lookback, T1)
Calculation: T1 long shallow level = highest(high, swing lookback, T1) - T1 swing range * shallow retracement parameter
Calculation: T1 long deep level = highest(high, swing lookback, T1) - T1 swing range * deep retracement parameter
Calculation: T1 short shallow level = lowest(low, swing lookback, T1) + T1 swing range * shallow retracement parameter
Calculation: T1 short deep level = lowest(low, swing lookback, T1) + T1 swing range * deep retracement parameter
Calculation: long confirmation level 1 = signal-bar low + (signal-time T1 highest high - signal-bar low) * confirmation fraction 1
Calculation: long confirmation level 2 = signal-bar low + (signal-time T1 highest high - signal-bar low) * confirmation fraction 2
Calculation: short confirmation level 1 = signal-bar high - (signal-bar high - signal-time T1 lowest low) * confirmation fraction 1
Calculation: short confirmation level 2 = signal-bar high - (signal-bar high - signal-time T1 lowest low) * confirmation fraction 2
Calculation: long stop = at entry, highest(high, swing lookback, T1) - T1 swing range * selected Fib stop extension parameter if the selected Fib level is available and below entry price; otherwise entry price * (1 - fixed stop percentage / 100)
Calculation: short stop = at entry, lowest(low, swing lookback, T1) + T1 swing range * selected Fib stop extension parameter if the selected Fib level is available and above entry price; otherwise entry price * (1 + fixed stop percentage / 100)
Long: On configured signal timeframe T1 (or chart timeframe if unset), calculate on T1 bars and sample values on chart bars with lookahead off (forming T1 bar in realtime, completed historically): T1 swing range > 0 AND ((T1 low <= T1 long shallow level AND T1 low[1] > T1 long shallow level) OR (T1 low <= T1 long deep level AND T1 low[1] > T1 long deep level)) AND (trend filter disabled OR chart close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2), calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (forming T2 bar in realtime, completed historically)) AND (validation disabled OR, calculated on T3 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (forming T3 bar in realtime, completed historically), the lowest-low bar within the swing lookback is more recent than the highest-high bar)
Short: On configured signal timeframe T1 (or chart timeframe if unset), calculate on T1 bars and sample values on chart bars with lookahead off (forming T1 bar in realtime, completed historically): T1 swing range > 0 AND ((T1 high >= T1 short shallow level AND T1 high[1] < T1 short shallow level) OR (T1 high >= T1 short deep level AND T1 high[1] < T1 short deep level)) AND (trend filter disabled OR chart close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T2), calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (forming T2 bar in realtime, completed historically)) AND (validation disabled OR, calculated on T3 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (forming T3 bar in realtime, completed historically), the highest-high bar within the swing lookback is more recent than the lowest-low bar)
Long: After a filtered long Sniper signal, on a later chart bar, for any pending signal whose respective level has not triggered: (signal-time T1 highest high - signal-bar low) > 0 AND ((close >= long confirmation level 1 AND close[1] < long confirmation level 1) OR (close >= long confirmation level 2 AND close[1] < long confirmation level 2)); trigger each level only once per signal
Short: After a filtered short Sniper signal, on a later chart bar, for any pending signal whose respective level has not triggered: (signal-bar high - signal-time T1 lowest low) > 0 AND ((close <= short confirmation level 1 AND close[1] > short confirmation level 1) OR (close <= short confirmation level 2 AND close[1] > short confirmation level 2)); trigger each level only once per signal
Exit: long, chart low <= long stop OR chart high >= entry price + reward-risk parameter * (entry price - long stop)
Exit: short, chart high >= short stop OR chart low <= entry price - reward-risk parameter * (short stop - entry price)
