Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR(shorter ATR lookback) / ATR(longer ATR lookback), or unavailable if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Peak drawdown = (highest(high, drawdown lookback) - close) / highest(high, drawdown lookback), or unavailable if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Range-low distance = (close - lowest(low, range lookback)) / lowest(low, range lookback), or unavailable if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Range-high distance = (highest(high, range lookback) - close) / highest(high, range lookback), or unavailable if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Range position = (close - lowest(low, range lookback)) / (highest(high, range lookback) - lowest(low, range lookback)), or range midpoint if the denominator is zero
Calculation: EMA distance in ATR = (close - EMA(close, EMA lookback)) / ATR(shorter ATR lookback), or unavailable if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume average lookback), or unavailable if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Regime stress score = neutral score if volatility ratio, peak drawdown, or range-low distance is unavailable; otherwise SMA(stress volatility weight × Normalized score(volatility ratio, stress volatility lower bound, stress volatility upper bound) + stress drawdown weight × Normalized score(peak drawdown, stress drawdown lower bound, stress drawdown upper bound) + stress rebound weight × Normalized score(range-low distance, stress rebound lower bound, stress rebound upper bound), signal smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Price location score = neutral score if range-low distance or range-high distance is unavailable; otherwise SMA(location position weight × Normalized score(range position, location position lower bound, location position upper bound) + location low-distance weight × Normalized score(range-low distance, location low-distance lower bound, location low-distance upper bound) + location high-distance weight × (score maximum - Normalized score(range-high distance, location high-distance lower bound, location high-distance upper bound)), signal smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Bullish overreaction score = neutral score if RSI(close, RSI lookback) is unavailable; otherwise SMA(bullish RSI weight × Normalized score(score maximum - RSI(close, RSI lookback), bullish RSI lower bound, bullish RSI upper bound) + bullish EMA-distance weight × Normalized score(-EMA distance in ATR, bullish EMA-distance lower bound, bullish EMA-distance upper bound), signal smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Bearish overreaction score = neutral score if RSI(close, RSI lookback) is unavailable; otherwise SMA(bearish RSI weight × Normalized score(RSI(close, RSI lookback), bearish RSI lower bound, bearish RSI upper bound) + bearish EMA-distance weight × Normalized score(EMA distance in ATR, bearish EMA-distance lower bound, bearish EMA-distance upper bound), signal smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Pressure score = neutral score if relative volume is unavailable; otherwise SMA(pressure volume weight × Normalized score(relative volume, pressure volume lower bound, pressure volume upper bound) + pressure volatility weight × Normalized score(volatility ratio, pressure volatility lower bound, pressure volatility upper bound), signal smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Bullish reversal score = EMA(bullish reversal raw score, exhaustion smoothing lookback), where bullish reversal raw score = bullish location weight × (score maximum - price location score) + bullish overreaction weight × bullish overreaction score + bullish regime weight × regime stress score + bullish pressure weight × pressure score
