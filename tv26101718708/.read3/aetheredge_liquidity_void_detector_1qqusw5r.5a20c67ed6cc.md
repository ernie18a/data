Calculation: gap magnitude = abs(open - close[1]) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), epsilon)
Calculation: upward sweep = high > highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= wick threshold AND high - close >= ATR(ATR lookback) × rejection threshold AND close < open
