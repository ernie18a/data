Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), instrument minimum tick)
Calculation: Upper channel = EMA(hlc3, basis lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) * channel ATR multiplier
Calculation: Lower channel = EMA(hlc3, basis lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) * channel ATR multiplier
Calculation: Bull bias = EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(hlc3, basis lookback)
Calculation: Bear bias = EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) < EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(hlc3, basis lookback)
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), or the configured fallback when the SMA is not positive
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), regime lookback), or the configured fallback when the SMA is not positive
Calculation: Regime score = trend-regime score if CHOP(regime lookback) < chop threshold AND abs((EMA(hlc3, basis lookback) - EMA(hlc3, basis lookback) at slope lookback ago) / safe ATR) > slope threshold AND (fraction of bars in the slope lookback with close above EMA(hlc3, basis lookback) > persistence threshold OR fraction with close below EMA(hlc3, basis lookback) > persistence threshold); otherwise expansion-regime score if ATR expansion > expansion threshold AND CHOP(regime lookback) < chop threshold + expansion chop allowance; otherwise quiet-regime score if CHOP(regime lookback) >= chop threshold AND ATR expansion < quiet ATR threshold; otherwise neutral-regime score
Calculation: Normalized score component(x, lower bound, upper bound) = clamp((x - lower bound) / (upper bound - lower bound), to the unit interval)
Calculation: Long score = score scale factor * (relative-volume weight * Normalized score component(Relative volume, relative-volume score lower bound, relative-volume score upper bound) + channel-extension weight * Normalized score component((close - Upper channel) / safe ATR, extension score lower bound, extension score upper bound) + momentum weight * Normalized score component(ROC(close, momentum lookback), momentum score lower bound, momentum score upper bound) + candle-body weight * Normalized score component(abs(close - open) / safe ATR, candle-body score lower bound, candle-body score upper bound) + trend-gap weight * Normalized score component((EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) - EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) / safe ATR, trend-gap score lower bound, trend-gap score upper bound) + regime weight * Regime score)
Calculation: Short score = score scale factor * (relative-volume weight * Normalized score component(Relative volume, relative-volume score lower bound, relative-volume score upper bound) + channel-extension weight * Normalized score component((Lower channel - close) / safe ATR, extension score lower bound, extension score upper bound) + momentum weight * Normalized score component(-ROC(close, momentum lookback), momentum score lower bound, momentum score upper bound) + candle-body weight * Normalized score component(abs(close - open) / safe ATR, candle-body score lower bound, candle-body score upper bound) + trend-gap weight * Normalized score component((EMA(close, slow EMA lookback) - EMA(close, fast EMA lookback)) / safe ATR, trend-gap score lower bound, trend-gap score upper bound) + regime weight * Regime score)
Calculation: Long initial risk = abs(entry-bar close - (min(entry-bar low, EMA(hlc3, basis lookback)) - safe ATR at entry bar * initial stop ATR multiple))
Calculation: Short initial risk = abs((max(entry-bar high, EMA(hlc3, basis lookback)) + safe ATR at entry bar * initial stop ATR multiple) - entry-bar close)
