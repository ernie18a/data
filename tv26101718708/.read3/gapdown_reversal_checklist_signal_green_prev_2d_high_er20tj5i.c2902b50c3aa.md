Calculation: opening range high = Highest(high of chart bars in the opening-range window), fixed when the window ends
Calculation: prior T1 close = close of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: prior T1 high = high of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: constructive MA = for either SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 bar still forming), sampled on chart bars, or SMA(close, longer lookback, T1 bar still forming), sampled on chart bars, close >= that SMA * (1 - near threshold / 100) AND close <= that SMA * (1 + near threshold / 100) OR close > that SMA
Calculation: checklist score = count of these seven true conditions: close > VWAP(hlc3); close > prior T1 close; opening range is complete AND close > opening range high; close >= prior T1 high * (1 - near threshold / 100) OR close > prior T1 high; constructive MA; RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold; RSI-MA type is None OR its prior chart-bar value is unavailable OR selected MA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-MA lookback) >= its value on the prior chart bar
Long: within the regular-session window AND (close > VWAP(hlc3) AND close > prior T1 close AND opening range is complete AND close > opening range high AND (close >= prior T1 high * (1 - near threshold / 100) OR close > prior T1 high) AND (MA filter is not required OR constructive MA) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold AND (RSI-MA type is None OR its prior chart-bar value is unavailable OR selected MA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-MA lookback) >= its value on the prior chart bar) OR checklist score >= score threshold)
