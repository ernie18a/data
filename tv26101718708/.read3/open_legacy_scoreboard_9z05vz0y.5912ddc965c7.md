Calculation: Active bullish gap = the latest prior chart bar where low > high[gap-separation lag] and low - high[gap-separation lag] >= minimum-gap ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback), with lower boundary high[gap-separation lag] and upper boundary low; it is valid through the gap-lifetime parameter, is consumed after a bullish pullback signal, and is replaced by a newer bullish gap.
Calculation: Active bearish gap = the latest prior chart bar where high < low[gap-separation lag] and low[gap-separation lag] - high >= minimum-gap ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback), with lower boundary high and upper boundary low[gap-separation lag]; it is valid through the gap-lifetime parameter, is consumed after a bearish pullback signal, and is replaced by a newer bearish gap.
Calculation: Qualified long setup = ((selected entry method is gap pullback AND low <= active bullish gap upper boundary AND close > open AND close > active bullish gap lower boundary) OR (selected entry method is EMA trend AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold, with this full condition true on the current chart bar and false on the previous chart bar)) AND (trend filtering is disabled OR (close > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) AND (sensitivity requires only the EMA trend filter OR (sensitivity requires the higher-timeframe trend filter AND the last completed T1 bar close > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) >= ADX strength threshold))); the T1 values are taken on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Qualified short setup = ((selected entry method is gap pullback AND high >= active bearish gap lower boundary AND close < open AND close < active bearish gap upper boundary) OR (selected entry method is EMA trend AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold, with this full condition true on the current chart bar and false on the previous chart bar)) AND (trend filtering is disabled OR (close < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) AND (sensitivity requires only the EMA trend filter OR (sensitivity requires the higher-timeframe trend filter AND the last completed T1 bar close < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) >= ADX strength threshold))) AND NOT Qualified long setup; the T1 values are taken on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Entry window = no enabled session OR the chart bar start datetime is within an enabled session's configured days and times; AND the chart bar close is outside every enabled news interval centered on its configured event time.
Long: Qualified long setup AND Entry window AND position is flat or short.
Short: Qualified short setup AND Entry window AND position is flat or long.
Exit: Long, on a chart bar after the entry bar, high >= entry price + take-profit pips × points-per-pip × minimum tick size OR low <= entry price - stop-loss pips × points-per-pip × minimum tick size OR an enabled session configured to close trades reaches its end OR an enabled news blackout starts.
Exit: Short, on a chart bar after the entry bar, low <= entry price - take-profit pips × points-per-pip × minimum tick size OR high >= entry price + stop-loss pips × points-per-pip × minimum tick size OR an enabled session configured to close trades reaches its end OR an enabled news blackout starts.
