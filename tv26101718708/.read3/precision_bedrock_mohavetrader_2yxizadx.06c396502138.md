Calculation: Candle metrics = body percentage, close-position percentage, upper-wick percentage, and lower-wick percentage, calculated from open, high, low, and close; when high = low, use 0, 50, 0, and 0 respectively.
Calculation: Rail trend = +1 when close crosses above the prior bar's highest high over the rail lookback; otherwise -1 when close crosses below the prior bar's lowest low over the rail lookback; otherwise retain the prior value, initially 0. An up transition is an upper break with prior trend -1; a down transition is a lower break with prior trend +1.
Calculation: Rail position = 100 × clamp((close − lowest(low, rail lookback)) / (highest(high, rail lookback) − lowest(low, rail lookback)), 0, 1); use 50 when the rail range is no greater than the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Equilibrium = lower rail + rail range × clamp(0.5 + (average(EMA(raw position, equilibrium lookback), if available VWMA(raw position, equilibrium lookback) else WMA(raw position, equilibrium lookback)) − 0.5) × equilibrium shift, equilibrium edge buffer, 1 − equilibrium edge buffer); raw position = clamp((hlc3 − lower rail) / rail range, 0, 1), or 0.5 when the rail range is no greater than the minimum price increment. The average combines EMA and VWMA-or-WMA values.
Calculation: Smoothed momentum = EMA(EMA(close, momentum smoothing) − EMA(close, momentum smoothing)[momentum lag], momentum smoothing).
Calculation: Smoothed gamma = smoothed momentum − smoothed momentum[gamma lag].
Calculation: Momentum-up state = prior momentum-up state AND smoothed momentum > −EMA(abs(smoothed momentum), state-buffer lookback) × state-buffer fraction, OR NOT prior momentum-up state AND smoothed momentum > EMA(abs(smoothed momentum), state-buffer lookback) × state-buffer fraction; initially false.
Calculation: Gamma-up state = prior gamma-up state AND smoothed gamma > −EMA(abs(smoothed gamma), state-buffer lookback) × state-buffer fraction, OR NOT prior gamma-up state AND smoothed gamma > EMA(abs(smoothed gamma), state-buffer lookback) × state-buffer fraction; initially false.
Calculation: Momentum state code = 0 when momentum-up and gamma-up; 1 when momentum-up and not gamma-up; 2 when neither is up; otherwise 3.
Calculation: Participation score = neutral participation score if volume average is unavailable; otherwise, if volume/average volume meets the volume-stress threshold and candle body percentage is below its limit, or bar range/average range meets the range-expansion threshold, clamp(volume ratio / stress scale × 100, 0, 100); otherwise clamp(volume ratio / normal scale × 100, normal floor, normal ceiling). Both averages use the participation lookback; an unavailable ratio is treated as zero for the stress and expansion comparisons.
Calculation: Gamma reversal score = the configured highest, second, third, or fallback gamma score: for bearish, respectively, momentum-up and not gamma-up; neither up; both up; otherwise; for bullish, respectively, not momentum-up and gamma-up; both up; neither up; otherwise.
Calculation: Reversal score(side) = close-pressure weight × close pressure + wick weight × side's opposing wick percentage + body weight × (body percentage when the candle closes against that side, otherwise body percentage × opposite-body factor) + participation weight × participation score + gamma weight × gamma reversal score(side) + rail weight × clamp(side's rail score, 0, 100). Bullish close pressure is close-position percentage and bullish rail score is (lower-rail threshold − rail position) / rail-score span × 100; bearish close pressure is 100 − close-position percentage and bearish rail score is (rail position − upper-rail threshold) / rail-score span × 100.
Calculation: Chop suppression = chop filter enabled AND ((abs(smoothed momentum) < EMA(abs(smoothed momentum), chop-energy lookback) × momentum dead-energy fraction AND abs(smoothed gamma) < EMA(abs(smoothed gamma), chop-energy lookback) × gamma dead-energy fraction) OR the sum over the flip lookback of bars whose momentum state code differs from the prior bar is at least the state-flip threshold).
Long: reversal warnings enabled AND at least the warning cooldown has elapsed since either warning AND NOT chop suppression AND rail trend = −1 AND NOT (lower break on this bar AND prior rail trend = +1) AND close ≤ equilibrium AND (rail-zone requirement disabled OR rail position ≤ lower-rail proximity threshold) AND (equilibrium > equilibrium[1] AND equilibrium[1] ≥ equilibrium[2] OR (equilibrium − equilibrium[1]) / rail range ≥ −equilibrium slope tolerance OR lower-wick percentage ≥ rejection-wick threshold OR (close > open AND body percentage ≥ rejection-body threshold) OR close-position percentage ≥ bullish rejection threshold) AND Reversal score(bullish) ≥ reversal-score threshold.
Short: reversal warnings enabled AND at least the warning cooldown has elapsed since either warning AND NOT chop suppression AND rail trend = +1 AND NOT (upper break on this bar AND prior rail trend = −1) AND close ≥ equilibrium AND (rail-zone requirement disabled OR rail position ≥ upper-rail proximity threshold) AND (equilibrium < equilibrium[1] AND equilibrium[1] ≤ equilibrium[2] OR (equilibrium − equilibrium[1]) / rail range ≤ equilibrium slope tolerance OR upper-wick percentage ≥ rejection-wick threshold OR (close < open AND body percentage ≥ rejection-body threshold) OR close-position percentage ≤ bearish rejection threshold) AND Reversal score(bearish) ≥ reversal-score threshold.
