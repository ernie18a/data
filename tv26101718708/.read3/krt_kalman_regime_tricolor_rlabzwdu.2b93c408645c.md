Calculation: Kalman LLT(close, q, noise lookback, warm-up count, fallback variance, observation variance floor, initial covariance) = recursive two-state local-linear-trend Kalman filter of log(close), initialized with level=log(close), slope=0, diagonal covariance=initial covariance; use process variance q × R, where R is the rolling median of squared log returns over noise lookback after the sample count exceeds warm-up count, otherwise the current squared log return or fallback variance, floored at observation variance floor.
Calculation: Kalman slope t-statistic = filtered slope / sqrt(max(filtered slope variance, slope-variance floor)).
Calculation: T1 flip levels = exp(predicted log-level + (± trend hysteresis threshold × sqrt(max(filtered slope variance, slope-variance floor)) − predicted slope) / slope gain) from Kalman LLT(close, qFast, noise lookback, warm-up count, fallback variance, observation variance floor, initial covariance); minus gives the lower level and plus gives the upper level.
Calculation: Confirmed T1 trend state = on T1 bars, initialize long, switch to short when Kalman slope t-statistic from Kalman LLT(close, qFast, noise lookback, warm-up count, fallback variance, observation variance floor, initial covariance) < −trend hysteresis threshold, switch to long when it > trend hysteresis threshold, and otherwise retain state; on chart bars use the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Realtime trend state = on chart bars, initialize long, switch to short when close < the lower T1 flip level or to long when close > the upper T1 flip level, using flip levels from the still-forming T1 bar; otherwise retain state.
Calculation: Ready bull regime(T2) = on chart bars, use the Kalman slope t-statistic from Kalman LLT(close, qGate, noise lookback, warm-up count, fallback variance, observation variance floor, initial covariance) on the last completed T2 bar; regime state starts inactive, activates when the statistic > gate-entry threshold, deactivates when it < −gate-exit threshold, and becomes ready after remaining active for the installation-age parameter in hours.
Calculation: Ready bear regime(T2) = on chart bars, use the Kalman slope t-statistic from Kalman LLT(close, qGate, noise lookback, warm-up count, fallback variance, observation variance floor, initial covariance) on the last completed T2 bar; regime state starts inactive, activates when the statistic < −gate-entry threshold, deactivates when it > gate-exit threshold, and becomes ready after remaining active for the installation-age parameter in hours.
Long: Realtime trend state changes from short to long OR confirmed T1 trend state changes from short to long OR (ready bull regime changes from inactive to active AND confirmed T1 trend state is long)
Short: Realtime trend state changes from long to short OR confirmed T1 trend state changes from long to short OR (ready bear regime changes from inactive to active AND confirmed T1 trend state is short)
