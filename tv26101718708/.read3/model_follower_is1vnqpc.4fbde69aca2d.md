Calculation: Structure = Initialize upper and lower bounds to the first bar’s high and low, with direction unset and origin at that bar; a bearish pullback is the latest bar with close < open and close < prior low, retaining its low and updating it to any subsequent lower low, and a bullish pullback is the latest bar with close > open and close > prior high, retaining its high and updating it to any subsequent higher high; only pullbacks after the origin qualify; close > upper bound when direction is unset or a bearish pullback qualifies sets direction up, upper bound to the current high, lower bound to the pullback low if qualified, and origin to the pullback bar if qualified; close < lower bound symmetrically sets direction down, lower bound to the current low, upper bound to the pullback high if qualified, and origin to the pullback bar if qualified; while direction is up, extend the upper bound to each higher high, and while direction is down, extend the lower bound to each lower low; the T1 instance is calculated on T1 bars and returned to chart bars with lookahead off, using the still-forming T1 bar in real time.
Long: Local Structure direction = up OR T1 Structure direction = up
Short: Local Structure direction = down OR T1 Structure direction = down
