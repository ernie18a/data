Calculation: Opening range high = highest high of chart bars from the regular-session open through the selected opening-range duration; hold fixed after that window ends
Calculation: Opening range low = lowest low of chart bars from the regular-session open through the selected opening-range duration; hold fixed after that window ends
Calculation: Nearest resistance = lowest retained confirmed swing pivot high or high of the last completed T1 bar, each strictly above close; T1 values are taken on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: Nearest support = highest retained confirmed swing pivot low or low of the last completed T1 bar, each strictly below close; T1 values are taken on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: Body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high = low
Long: opening range is locked AND current bar is in the regular session AND (signal-session filter is disabled OR current bar is in its signal window) AND no earlier long signal since the session reset or the latest close below opening range high AND ((break mode AND high > opening range high) OR (close mode AND open > opening range high AND close > opening range high)) AND (break mode OR (body filter disabled OR body fraction >= minimum body fraction) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))) AND (S/R filter disabled OR nearest resistance is absent OR nearest resistance - close >= target distance)
Short: opening range is locked AND current bar is in the regular session AND (signal-session filter is disabled OR current bar is in its signal window) AND no earlier short signal since the session reset or the latest close above opening range low AND ((break mode AND low < opening range low) OR (close mode AND open < opening range low AND close < opening range low)) AND (break mode OR (body filter disabled OR body fraction >= minimum body fraction) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback))) AND (S/R filter disabled OR nearest support is absent OR close - nearest support >= target distance)
