Calculation: State(T, ADX threshold) = bullish when close > EMA(close, fast length) AND EMA(close, fast length) > EMA(close, slow length) AND Supertrend(trend factor, trend ATR length) is bullish AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI midline AND MACD histogram(close, MACD fast length, MACD slow length, MACD signal length) > its prior value AND ADX(DMI length, ADX smoothing) >= ADX threshold; bearish when the inverse strict comparisons hold; otherwise flat. Compute the current chart state on current chart bars using the shared ADX minimum. Compute each enabled T1/T2/T3 state on that timeframe’s own bars using its last completed bar, then sample it onto chart bars; use that slot’s separate ADX minimum when per-timeframe ADX minimums are enabled, otherwise use the shared minimum.
Calculation: Total enabled timeframes = the current chart timeframe plus each enabled T1/T2/T3.
Calculation: Active timeframe count = the number of enabled timeframes whose state is not flat.
Calculation: Bull count = the number of enabled timeframes whose state is bullish.
Calculation: Bear count = the number of enabled timeframes whose state is bearish.
Calculation: Required alignment = the greater of active timeframe count minus allowed disagreements and the smaller of total enabled timeframes and the adaptive vote floor, when the adaptive denominator is enabled; otherwise, the greater of total enabled timeframes minus allowed disagreements and the positive vote floor.
Calculation: Effective weighted denominator = the sum of weights for all enabled timeframes, except when the adaptive denominator is enabled and active timeframe count meets the adaptive timeframe floor, in which case it is the sum of weights for enabled non-flat timeframes. Each timeframe has its own weight.
Calculation: Weighted bull percentage = the weights of enabled bullish timeframes divided by the effective weighted denominator, expressed as a percentage; zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Weighted bear percentage = the weights of enabled bearish timeframes divided by the effective weighted denominator, expressed as a percentage; zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Mean ADX = the arithmetic mean of ADX(DMI length, ADX smoothing) on the current chart bars and each enabled T1/T2/T3’s last completed bar. HTF ADX is computed on that timeframe’s own bars and sampled onto chart bars.
Calculation: Reached profit tier count = the number of enabled profit-ladder tiers already reached during the open position; each tier has its own gain threshold and remains reached for that trade once signed unrealized percentage return from entry, based on close and entry price, first meets the threshold. The count is zero when the ladder is disabled.
Calculation: Effective exit Supertrend factor = the configured trailing-exit factor unless the ladder and progressive stop tightening are enabled; when both are enabled, use the greater of configured factor × (1 − per-tier tightening fraction × reached profit tier count) and configured factor × the exit-factor floor fraction.
Calculation: Weakening = falling(ADX(DMI length, ADX smoothing), weakening ADX lookback) on chart bars OR (histogram decay enabled AND MACD histogram declines on each consecutive comparison across the histogram-decay lookback on chart bars).
Long: State on current chart bars is bullish.
Short: State on current chart bars is bearish.
Long: State(T1, last completed T1 bar) is bullish AND T1 is enabled.
Short: State(T1, last completed T1 bar) is bearish AND T1 is enabled.
Long: State(T2, last completed T2 bar) is bullish AND T2 is enabled.
Short: State(T2, last completed T2 bar) is bearish AND T2 is enabled.
Long: State(T3, last completed T3 bar) is bullish AND T3 is enabled.
Short: State(T3, last completed T3 bar) is bearish AND T3 is enabled.
Long: The equal-weight gate is selected AND Bull count >= Required alignment AND Bull count is nonzero, OR the weighted gate is selected AND Weighted bull percentage >= minimum weighted confluence percentage; T3 is not enabled with a bearish state under the opposition veto; the average-ADX gate is disabled or Mean ADX >= its minimum; the ADX-rising requirement is disabled or chart ADX is rising over its lookback; this complete gate has remained open for the entry-confirmation length of consecutive chart bars and has just become confirmed; position side is not already long; volume confirmation is disabled or volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier; cooldown is disabled, no long trailing-stop exit has occurred, or the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the last long trailing-stop exit.
Short: The equal-weight gate is selected AND Bear count >= Required alignment AND Bear count is nonzero, OR the weighted gate is selected AND Weighted bear percentage >= minimum weighted confluence percentage; T3 is not enabled with a bullish state under the opposition veto; the average-ADX gate is disabled or Mean ADX >= its minimum; the ADX-rising requirement is disabled or chart ADX is rising over its lookback; this complete gate has remained open for the entry-confirmation length of consecutive chart bars and has just become confirmed; position side is not already short; volume confirmation is disabled or volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier; cooldown is disabled, no short trailing-stop exit has occurred, or the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the last short trailing-stop exit.
Exit: long, position side is long AND Supertrend(Effective exit Supertrend factor, trailing-exit ATR length) direction changes from bullish on the prior chart bar to bearish on the current chart bar.
Exit: short, position side is short AND Supertrend(Effective exit Supertrend factor, trailing-exit ATR length) direction changes from bearish on the prior chart bar to bullish on the current chart bar.
Long: position side is long AND Weakening AND (position side was not long on the prior chart bar OR Weakening was false on the prior chart bar).
Short: position side is short AND Weakening AND (position side was not short on the prior chart bar OR Weakening was false on the prior chart bar).
Long: Bull count > Bear count AND Bull count is nonzero AND Bull count < Required alignment AND this condition was false on the prior chart bar.
Short: Bear count > Bull count AND Bear count is nonzero AND Bear count < Required alignment AND this condition was false on the prior chart bar.
