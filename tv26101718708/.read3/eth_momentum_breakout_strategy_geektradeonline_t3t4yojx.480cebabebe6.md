Calculation: Recent valid squeeze = Bollinger Bands with an SMA basis and standard-deviation width are strictly inside Keltner Channels with an SMA basis and ATR width for at least the squeeze-duration parameter consecutive chart bars, and have since released within the release-age parameter number of chart bars.
Calculation: Candle body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Momentum = endpoint linear regression of close - SMA(close, momentum lookback), using the same lookback for the regression.
Calculation: Entry risk scale = unscaled when adaptive scaling is disabled; otherwise, at entry use the hot scale when PercentRank(ATR(base lookback), volatility-rank lookback) exceeds the hot threshold and the side-adjusted trend score exceeds the hot trend threshold; else use the cold scale when volatility rank is below the cold threshold or the side-adjusted trend score is below the cold trend threshold; otherwise use unscaled. The long trend score is (close - EMA(close, adaptive trend lookback)) / ATR(base lookback); the short trend score is (EMA(close, adaptive trend lookback) - close) / ATR(base lookback).
Calculation: Long stop = max(prior long stop, entry price - entry ATR x stop distance x entry risk scale, breakeven level when enabled and activated (entry price + entry ATR x breakeven offset, activated when close - entry price >= entry ATR x breakeven trigger), and ATR trail when enabled (close - current ATR x regular trail distance before partial TP or tight trail distance after partial TP, each x entry risk scale); after partial TP in runner mode, also include the lowest low over the structure lookback. Initialize at the entry stop and ratchet only upward.
Calculation: Short stop = min(prior short stop, entry price + entry ATR x stop distance x entry risk scale, breakeven level when enabled and activated (entry price - entry ATR x breakeven offset, activated when entry price - close >= entry ATR x breakeven trigger), and ATR trail when enabled (close + current ATR x regular trail distance before partial TP or tight trail distance after partial TP, each x entry risk scale); after partial TP in runner mode, also include the highest high over the structure lookback. Initialize at the entry stop and ratchet only downward.
Calculation: Long favorable excursion = greatest close - entry price since entry, floored at zero.
Calculation: Short favorable excursion = greatest entry price - close since entry, floored at zero.

Short: flat AND short direction enabled AND Recent valid squeeze AND Momentum < the negative momentum threshold AND Momentum < its value on the previous chart bar AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) x volume multiplier AND close < lower Bollinger Band - ATR(base lookback) x breakout buffer AND Candle body fraction >= minimum body fraction AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > oversold threshold when the RSI filter is enabled AND ATR(base lookback) as a percentage of close >= minimum ATR percentage AND cooldown has elapsed (use the shortened cooldown after a prior partial TP when smart cooldown is enabled) AND, when the regime filter is enabled, each enabled subfilter passes: ADX(DI lookback, smoothing, T1 current bar calculated on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars) is within its bounds; chart close is within the maximum percentage distance of EMA(close, long-term lookback, T1 current bar calculated on T1 bars and sampled onto chart bars); and ATR(regime ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(regime ATR lookback), volatility-history lookback), calculated on T1 bars using its current bar and sampled onto chart bars, is within its bounds AND, when the trend filter is enabled, close < EMA(close, trend lookback, T2 current bar calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars) - ATR(trend lookback, T2 current bar calculated on T2 bars and sampled onto chart bars) x EMA buffer, and when slope confirmation is enabled that EMA is below its value slope lookback T2 bars earlier AND, when fundamental filters are enabled, each available T3 current-bar value sampled onto chart bars passes: active addresses > chart-bar SMA of sampled active addresses, large-transaction count > chart-bar SMA of sampled large-transaction count, and sentiment < the complement of the bullish sentiment threshold on the sentiment scale AND, when the session filter is enabled, chart-bar opening datetime is within the configured exchange-time session.
