Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum of high, or maximum of open and close when body source is selected, during the configured opening-range session; freeze when the session ends.
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum of low, or minimum of open and close when body source is selected, during the configured opening-range session; freeze when the session ends.
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2; width = opening-range high − opening-range low.
Calculation: Range-width percentile = percentage of prior sessions in the configured lookback whose opening-range width divided by ATR(normalization lookback, T1, last completed bar) is strictly below the current session's ratio; evaluate on chart bars when the opening range completes, and use a neutral score component when the rank is unavailable.
Calculation: Trend bias = bullish when the last completed T2 close is above EMA(close, trend lookback, T2, last completed bar) and that EMA is at least its value one completed T2 bar earlier; bearish when the close is below that EMA and the EMA is at most its value one completed T2 bar earlier; neutral otherwise. Evaluate on chart bars using completed T2 bars.
Calculation: Chart-bar volume = sum of volume from available T3 bars inside the chart bar when lower-timeframe data is enabled, otherwise chart-bar volume; use one unit when volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Chart-bar aggression delta = sum of volume × aggression estimate over available T3 bars inside the chart bar, falling back to the chart bar when lower-timeframe data is unavailable; the estimate is close-location value (2×close − high − low)/(high − low), body ratio (close − open)/(high − low), or their average, as selected; for a zero-range bar use sign(close − previous close).
Calculation: Session VWAP = VWAP(hlc3, chart-bar volume, anchored at the configured opening-range session start), evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Extension level j = opening-range high + opening-range width × extension factor j for upper levels, or opening-range low − opening-range width × extension factor j for lower levels; factors are either a selected Fibonacci set or three separate parameters.
Calculation: Signal score = score scale × weighted mean of: clamp((chart-bar volume / SMA(chart-bar volume, volume lookback) − volume-score start ratio) / (high-volume multiple × full-score multiplier − volume-score start ratio), 0, 1); directional close location, using (close − low)/max(high − low, minimum price increment) for long and (high − close)/max(high − low, minimum price increment) for short; clamp((close − breakout edge) × direction / ATR(signal lookback) / displacement full-score distance, 0, 1); range-width percentile for failed-break fades or its inverse for breakouts and retests, neutral when unavailable; trend alignment as full, neutral, or zero for aligned, neutral, or opposing trend bias; clamp((chart-bar aggression delta / chart-bar volume) × direction / delta full-score level, 0, 1); and session VWAP alignment as full when close is on the directional side, zero otherwise, neutral when unavailable. Each component has its own weight parameter.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry − direction × risk; risk = clamp((entry − raw stop) × direction, risk-floor multiple × ATR(signal lookback), risk-cap multiple × ATR(signal lookback)). For a failed-break fade, raw stop is the failed break's extreme − direction × stop buffer × ATR(signal lookback); otherwise select the opposite range edge, range midpoint, entry − direction × ATR stop distance, or signal-bar extreme − direction × stop buffer × ATR(signal lookback).
Calculation: Target candidates for breakouts and retests are the three extension levels in the position direction; for failed-break fades they are the range midpoint, opposite range edge, and first extension beyond that edge. In extension-target mode, use a candidate only when it is at least the configured distance from entry and configured spacing beyond the previous target; otherwise use entry + direction × risk × that target's R multiple if it is beyond the previous target, or previous target + direction × configured fallback fraction × risk. In R-target mode use the same R-multiple and fallback rule.
Calculation: Active stop starts at the initial stop; after target one it moves to entry when breakeven is enabled; after target two, when runner trailing is enabled, it updates to the more protective of the current stop and close − trail multiplier × ATR(trail lookback) for a long, or close + trail multiplier × ATR(trail lookback) for a short. Updates apply from the next bar.
Long: A new close above the opening-range high by more than break-buffer × ATR(signal lookback), after a close not beyond that buffered edge, while the completed range is live and within the configured signal window; OR live price is above the opening-range high while range-position bar coloring is enabled; OR, on an eligible confirmed chart bar, an enabled upward breakout with its per-side break count within its limit, an enabled upward retest within its separate chart-bar window with no earlier retest or failure for that break, low at or below opening-range high + retest tolerance × ATR(signal lookback), close above the edge and open, or an enabled downward failed break within its separate chart-bar window with no earlier failure for that break and close at least fail-depth × opening-range width above the lower edge; AND either score ≥ minimum score, counter-trend blocking is disabled or trend bias is not bearish, and session signal count is below its limit, OR weak markers are enabled and score is below minimum, counter-trend blocking rejects the signal, or the session signal limit is reached.
Short: A new close below the opening-range low by more than break-buffer × ATR(signal lookback), after a close not beyond that buffered edge, while the completed range is live and within the configured signal window; OR live price is below the opening-range low while range-position bar coloring is enabled; OR, on an eligible confirmed chart bar, an enabled downward breakout with its per-side break count within its limit, an enabled downward retest within its separate chart-bar window with no earlier retest or failure for that break, high at or above opening-range low − retest tolerance × ATR(signal lookback), close below the edge and open, or an enabled upward failed break within its separate chart-bar window with no earlier failure for that break and close at least fail-depth × opening-range width below the upper edge; AND either score ≥ minimum score, counter-trend blocking is disabled or trend bias is not bullish, and session signal count is below its limit, OR weak markers are enabled and score is below minimum, counter-trend blocking rejects the signal, or the session signal limit is reached.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ target one OR high ≥ target two OR high ≥ target three OR configured maximum holding bars have elapsed OR the configured trading window has ended OR a new opening-range session has started.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ target one OR low ≤ target two OR low ≤ target three OR configured maximum holding bars have elapsed OR the configured trading window has ended OR a new opening-range session has started.
