Calculation: Session green(session) = session close > session open in open-close mode; otherwise session close > previous completed same-session close, falling back to session open if unavailable; session open and close are the first chart-bar open and last chart-bar close.
Calculation: First NY break = first Asia high hit when bar high >= its level, Asia low hit when bar low <= its level, London high hit when bar high >= its level, or London low hit when bar low <= its level during the current NY session; simultaneous hits use that listed priority.
Calculation: ALN break rate(level) = 100 × completed historical NY sessions in the configured statistics window matching the current ALN pattern that hit level / all such sessions; undefined for a zero denominator.
Calculation: ALN conditional break rate(level | opposite level first) = 100 × completed historical NY sessions in the configured statistics window matching the current ALN pattern that hit level and had the opposite level as First NY break / such sessions with the opposite level as First NY break; undefined for a zero denominator.
Calculation: SSS bucket count(pair) = number of completed historical Asia-London-NY cycles in the configured statistics window whose Asia and London Session green results match pair.
Calculation: SSS NY green rate(pair) = 100 × completed historical cycles in pair with a green NY session / SSS bucket count(pair); undefined for a zero denominator.
Long: ALN enabled AND after the current cycle's London session closes and before the next Asia session starts, London high > Asia high AND London low >= Asia low AND NOT (current NY session's First NY break is London low AND ALN break rate(London high) and ALN conditional break rate(London high | London low first) are defined AND their difference is >= the kill-switch drop threshold).
Short: ALN enabled AND after the current cycle's London session closes and before the next Asia session starts, London low < Asia low AND London high <= Asia high AND NOT (current NY session's First NY break is London high AND ALN break rate(London low) and ALN conditional break rate(London low | London high first) are defined AND their difference is >= the kill-switch drop threshold).
Long: SSS enabled AND the current cycle's Asia and London sessions are complete AND SSS bucket count(current Asia and London Session green results) >= the minimum sample size AND SSS NY green rate(current Asia and London Session green results) >= the confirmation threshold.
Short: SSS enabled AND the current cycle's Asia and London sessions are complete AND SSS bucket count(current Asia and London Session green results) >= the minimum sample size AND SSS NY green rate(current Asia and London Session green results) <= 100 − the confirmation threshold.
