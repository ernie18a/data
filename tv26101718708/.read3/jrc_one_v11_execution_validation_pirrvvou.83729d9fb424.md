Calculation: Mapped levels = previous regular-session high/low, current premarket high/low, completed opening-range high/low, and confirmed chart-bar pivot high/low with equal left/right lookbacks; obtain session levels from T1 bars and take their values on chart bars as available, merge levels within configured tick tolerance, and scan in that order.
Calculation: Pivot zones = confirmed chart-bar pivot highs/lows expanded by the greater of one tick or ATR at the pivot times the zone-width parameter; create a high-pivot zone when close is at or below its top and a low-pivot zone when close is at or above its bottom; remove a zone when close breaks its far edge or its configured age expires.
Calculation: Stop = lowest low since setup began minus frozen chart ATR times the stop-buffer parameter for a long, or highest high since setup began plus frozen chart ATR times the stop-buffer parameter for a short; a break/retest setup starts with the mapped level as its extreme, and a sweep setup starts with the sweep-bar extreme; round outward to ticks.
Calculation: Target = from modeled entry, the lesser of maximum gross reward multiple times price risk and the distance to the nearest favorable mapped level or corresponding active pivot-zone edge minus frozen chart ATR times the clearance parameter; if there is no obstacle, use the maximum gross reward multiple; round inward to ticks.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close within configured test dates and an enabled New York regular-session weekday signal window, with required data available and T2 above the chart timeframe, no active position, queued plan, or same-bar exit, daily confirmed-plan cap not reached, cooldown elapsed, and mapped level/direction unused that day; bullish bias (last completed T2 close > EMA(close, shorter lookback, T2, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T2, last completed bar)); first eligible candidate in mapped-level order and configured overlap priority; and either (close crosses above the level, RVOL(volume, preceding regular-session chart-bar lookback) >= breakout threshold, breakout distance <= maximum extension times chart ATR, then a later candle touches the level band and closes above the level, with volume below breakout-bar volume if the quiet-retest filter is enabled, then a still-later candle closes above the retest high with close > open and close > anchored VWAP(hlc3, volume, configured anchor) and EMA(close, shorter chart lookback) > EMA(close, longer chart lookback)) or (low < level minus chart ATR times minimum sweep penetration, close > level, close > open, and close >= candle midpoint, then a later candle touches the level band and closes above the level, then a still-later candle closes above the retest high with close > open); in both paths bias remains bullish, setup does not fail or exceed its configured lifetime or confirmation window, entry extension is within its configured maximum, stop distance meets its minimum tick and maximum ATR limits, modeled costs/price risk are within their maximum, and net reward/total stop risk meets its minimum.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close within configured test dates and an enabled New York regular-session weekday signal window, with required data available and T2 above the chart timeframe, no active position, queued plan, or same-bar exit, daily confirmed-plan cap not reached, cooldown elapsed, and mapped level/direction unused that day; bearish bias (last completed T2 close < EMA(close, shorter lookback, T2, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer lookback, T2, last completed bar)); first eligible candidate in mapped-level order and configured overlap priority; and either (close crosses below the level, RVOL(volume, preceding regular-session chart-bar lookback) >= breakout threshold, breakout distance <= maximum extension times chart ATR, then a later candle touches the level band and closes below the level, with volume below breakout-bar volume if the quiet-retest filter is enabled, then a still-later candle closes below the retest low with close < open and close < anchored VWAP(hlc3, volume, configured anchor) and EMA(close, shorter chart lookback) < EMA(close, longer chart lookback)) or (high > level plus chart ATR times minimum sweep penetration, close < level, close < open, and close <= candle midpoint, then a later candle touches the level band and closes below the level, then a still-later candle closes below the retest low with close < open); in both paths bias remains bearish, setup does not fail or exceed its configured lifetime or confirmation window, entry extension is within its configured maximum, stop distance meets its minimum tick and maximum ATR limits, modeled costs/price risk are within their maximum, and net reward/total stop risk meets its minimum.
Long: A queued long plan reaches its configured eligible bar after the signal bar and delay, before fill-wait expiry and in the same test date and session window; the modeled open plus adverse half-spread and slippage, rounded up to a tick, is above the stop and below the target, within the configured level-extension maximum, has stop distance within minimum tick and maximum ATR limits, modeled costs/price risk within their maximum, and net reward/total stop risk meeting its minimum; bias on the preceding chart bar is bullish.
Short: A queued short plan reaches its configured eligible bar after the signal bar and delay, before fill-wait expiry and in the same test date and session window; the modeled open minus adverse half-spread and slippage, rounded down to a tick, is below the stop and above the target, within the configured level-extension maximum, has stop distance within minimum tick and maximum ATR limits, modeled costs/price risk within their maximum, and net reward/total stop risk meeting its minimum; bias on the preceding chart bar is bearish.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR the regular session ends OR configured test dates end.
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR the regular session ends OR configured test dates end.
