Calculation: Fibonacci swing pair = store the latest high equal to the highest high over the swing lookback including the current bar when its distance above the lowest low in that window is at least the minimum swing ATR multiple times ATR; store the latest low equal to the lowest low in that window when its distance below the highest high is at least the same threshold; the pair is valid only when both are stored at nonzero bar indices.
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement levels = swing high − each of three separate retracement parameters × (swing high − swing low).
Calculation: Fibonacci upswing = valid swing pair AND the stored swing high is later than the stored swing low.
Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci mark = Fibonacci enabled AND valid swing pair AND both swing points are at least the minimum swing age AND Fibonacci upswing AND the current low is within the ATR-scaled zone of any enabled retracement level AND (lower wick divided by candle range is at least the wick threshold OR the candle is bullish and closes above the first touched enabled level in level order). Treat wick fraction as zero when candle range is zero.
Calculation: Bearish Fibonacci mark = Fibonacci enabled AND valid swing pair AND both swing points are at least the minimum swing age AND NOT Fibonacci upswing AND the current high is within the ATR-scaled zone of any enabled retracement level AND (upper wick divided by candle range is at least the wick threshold OR the candle is bearish and closes below the first touched enabled level in level order). Treat wick fraction as zero when candle range is zero.
Calculation: Bullish ribbon alignment = at least one fewer than the alignment threshold of the adjacent chosen moving-average pairs, using eight independently parameterized lookbacks, have each average above the next.
Calculation: Bearish ribbon alignment = at least one fewer than the alignment threshold of the adjacent chosen moving-average pairs, using eight independently parameterized lookbacks, have each average below the next.
Calculation: Bullish T1 filter = on chart bars, the EMA of close at the fast T1 lookback is above the EMA at the slow T1 lookback AND the current T1 close is above the fast EMA; use values from the still-forming T1 bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Bearish T1 filter = on chart bars, the EMA of close at the fast T1 lookback is below the EMA at the slow T1 lookback AND the current T1 close is below the fast EMA; use values from the still-forming T1 bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Buy/sell volume ratio = volume on bullish candles divided by the greater of volume on bearish candles and the ratio floor.
Calculation: Bullish order-flow mark = order-flow enabled AND ((Cumulative Volume Delta, using volume signed positive on bullish candles, negative on bearish candles, and zero otherwise, is above its moving average and price closes below its previous close while CVD rises OR buy/sell volume ratio exceeds its neutral ratio plus the CVD threshold) AND close is above VWAP).
Calculation: Bearish order-flow mark = order-flow enabled AND ((Cumulative Volume Delta, using volume signed positive on bullish candles, negative on bearish candles, and zero otherwise, is below its moving average and price closes above its previous close while CVD falls OR buy/sell volume ratio is below its neutral ratio minus the CVD threshold) AND close is below VWAP.
Calculation: Long signal score = the sum of binary values for each of these conditions: T1 filter disabled or bullish T1 filter; ribbon disabled or bullish ribbon alignment; order-flow filter disabled or close above VWAP; Fibonacci filter disabled or Fibonacci upswing; ADX above its threshold.
Calculation: Short signal score = the sum of binary values for each of these conditions: T1 filter disabled or bearish T1 filter; ribbon disabled or bearish ribbon alignment; order-flow filter disabled or close below VWAP; Fibonacci filter disabled or NOT Fibonacci upswing; ADX above its threshold.
Calculation: Buy-volume percentage = the percentage of volume on bullish candles among bullish- and bearish-candle volume over the volume-pressure lookback; use the neutral percentage when that total volume is zero.
