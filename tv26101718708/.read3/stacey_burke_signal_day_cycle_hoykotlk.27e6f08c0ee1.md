Calculation: Outside day = at the first chart bar of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 high > the preceding T1 high AND its low < the preceding T1 low.
Calculation: First outside high = at the first chart bar of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 high > the preceding T1 high AND its close < the preceding T1 high AND its close > the preceding T1 low AND its low >= the preceding T1 low.
Calculation: First outside low = at the first chart bar of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 low < the preceding T1 low AND its close > the preceding T1 low AND its close < the preceding T1 high AND its high <= the preceding T1 high.
Calculation: Day Zero = at the first chart bar of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not an outside day AND (it is a first outside high AND the immediately preceding completed T1 bar was a first outside low OR it is a first outside low AND the immediately preceding completed T1 bar was a first outside high).
Calculation: Cycle day and direction = at the first chart bar of each new T1 bar, count consecutive completed T1 bars with high > the preceding T1 high AND low >= the preceding T1 low as an up cycle, or low < the preceding T1 low AND high <= the preceding T1 high as a down cycle; otherwise reset the count and direction.
Long: Outside day AND last completed T1 close > its open.
Short: Outside day AND last completed T1 close <= its open.
Long: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 close > its open AND the preceding completed T1 close < its open AND (the swing filter is disabled OR the preceding T1 low <= the lowest low over the swing-lookback parameter bars ending on that bar).
Short: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 close < its open AND the preceding completed T1 close > its open AND (the swing filter is disabled OR the preceding T1 high >= the highest high over the swing-lookback parameter bars ending on that bar).
Long: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not FRD or FGD AND it is not Day Zero AND the preceding completed T1 bar was FGD.
Short: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not FRD or FGD AND it is not Day Zero AND the preceding completed T1 bar was FRD.
Long: Cycle direction is down AND cycle day equals the fade-stage parameter, immediately after the continuation-stage parameter.
Short: Cycle direction is up AND cycle day equals the fade-stage parameter, immediately after the continuation-stage parameter.
Long: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not FRD or FGD AND it is not Day Zero AND the preceding completed T1 bar was not FRD or FGD AND cycle direction is up AND cycle day equals the continuation-stage parameter.
Short: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not FRD or FGD AND it is not Day Zero AND the preceding completed T1 bar was not FRD or FGD AND cycle direction is down AND cycle day equals the continuation-stage parameter.
Long: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not FRD or FGD AND it is not Day Zero AND the preceding completed T1 bar was not FRD or FGD AND cycle direction is down AND cycle day equals the late-fade-stage parameter, immediately after the fade-stage parameter.
Short: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is not FRD or FGD AND it is not Day Zero AND the preceding completed T1 bar was not FRD or FGD AND cycle direction is up AND cycle day equals the late-fade-stage parameter, immediately after the fade-stage parameter.
Long: On chart bars at the start of each new T1 bar, the last completed T1 bar is Wednesday AND its close >= the highest completed T1 close of that week, including that bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close > the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot-strength bars left, pivot-strength bars right) AND no earlier close since that pivot was confirmed exceeded it.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close < the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, pivot-strength bars left, pivot-strength bars right) AND no earlier close since that pivot was confirmed fell below it.
Long: During an active up cycle, before the first strike-zone tag of the forming T1 bar, chart-bar low <= max(last completed T1 high, forming T1 high) - projection-multiple parameter × level-size parameter.
Short: During an active down cycle, before the first strike-zone tag of the forming T1 bar, chart-bar high >= min(last completed T1 low, forming T1 low) + projection-multiple parameter × level-size parameter.
Exit: long, valid long bias has not already been invalidated AND chart-bar close < the low of the most recent completed T1 bar that established or renewed the bias.
Exit: short, valid short bias has not already been invalidated AND chart-bar close > the high of the most recent completed T1 bar that established or renewed the bias.
