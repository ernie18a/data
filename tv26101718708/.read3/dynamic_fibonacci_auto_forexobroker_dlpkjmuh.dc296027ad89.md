Calculation: Latest confirmed swing high and low = most recent PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), respectively, using their pivot-center bars; both use the same pivot lookback.
Calculation: Swing trend = uptrend when the latest swing low's pivot-center bar precedes the latest swing high's; downtrend when it follows; otherwise undefined.
Calculation: Golden Zone = interval between the shallower and deeper Fibonacci retracements of the latest swing range, measured from the swing high in an uptrend and from the swing low in a downtrend; the shallower ratio is less than the deeper ratio, and the zone is undefined unless both pivots exist and the swing high exceeds the swing low.
Calculation: Bullish bounce = bar low <= Golden Zone upper boundary AND bar high >= Golden Zone lower boundary AND close > open AND close > (Golden Zone upper boundary + Golden Zone lower boundary) / 2.
Calculation: Bearish bounce = bar low <= Golden Zone upper boundary AND bar high >= Golden Zone lower boundary AND close < open AND close < (Golden Zone upper boundary + Golden Zone lower boundary) / 2.
Long: Bullish bounce AND uptrend AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND chart bar is confirmed AND chart bars since chart bar 0 or the prior confirmed filtered signal of either side >= cooldown parameter.
Long: Bullish bounce AND NOT (uptrend AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND chart bar is confirmed AND chart bars since chart bar 0 or the prior confirmed filtered signal of either side >= cooldown parameter).
Short: Bearish bounce AND downtrend AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND chart bar is confirmed AND chart bars since chart bar 0 or the prior confirmed filtered signal of either side >= cooldown parameter.
Short: Bearish bounce AND NOT (downtrend AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND chart bar is confirmed AND chart bars since chart bar 0 or the prior confirmed filtered signal of either side >= cooldown parameter).
