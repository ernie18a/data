Calculation: reference ATR = ATR(T1, reference ATR lookback) requested onto chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T1 bar historically, still-forming T1 bar in realtime); use ATR(chart bars, same lookback) when unavailable.
Calculation: qualified level = enabled T1/T2/T3 PivotHigh(high, higher-timeframe pivot lookback) or PivotLow(low, higher-timeframe pivot lookback), each calculated on its source-timeframe bars and requested onto chart bars with lookahead off (last completed source bar historically, still-forming source bar in realtime); merge pivots within the merge tolerance across polarities, anchor price to the highest-timeframe pivot, retain the existing polarity, and count distinct source timeframes; expire levels by age and, at each storage-side capacity, evict the least-reacted level, oldest first on ties; qualify when source-timeframe count and reaction count meet their respective thresholds. A reaction counts when, after the chart-bar range first overlaps the level's touch band, max(level-low) for resistance or max(high-level) for support reaches max(reference ATR × reaction-distance multiple, tick size) before the reaction deadline.
Calculation: TAP event = on a confirmed chart bar within the configured session when session gating is enabled, a qualified level is touched by the chart-bar range and is not broken, with high <= level + touch tolerance + overshoot for resistance or low >= level - touch tolerance - overshoot for support; touch tolerance is a tick parameter and overshoot = max(reference ATR × overshoot multiple, zero); direction is long if close[1] > level, otherwise short. For each path, choose the eligible level with the smallest absolute distance from close, with resistance winning ties across storage sides.
Calculation: RECLAIM event = a qualified level closes beyond level + touch tolerance + overshoot for resistance or below level - touch tolerance - overshoot for support; before the break deadline, a confirmed chart PivotLow(low, far-side structure pivot lookback) forms above a broken resistance plus touch tolerance or a confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, far-side structure pivot lookback) forms below a broken support minus touch tolerance; then, on a chart bar within the session when session gating is enabled, the level's touch band is revisited. Direction is long if close[1] > level, otherwise short; the event flips the level's polarity and expires if the retest deadline passes.
Calculation: accumulation range = after the latest TAP or RECLAIM event, start with that bar's high and low; while forming, expand the range only while its width stays <= max(reference ATR × accumulation-width multiple, tick size); freeze it after the minimum formation time, then wait for a later chart bar to close outside it; discard if formation and breakout do not both occur within the total time limit. If both event types occur together, use the RECLAIM event's level. When the volume filter is enabled, require breakout volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiple; SMA uses chart bars.
Calculation: trendline channel breaks = on chart bars, join the latest two confirmed PivotHigh(high, channel pivot lookback) values for a descending channel or PivotLow(low, channel pivot lookback) values for an ascending channel, extending the line by its slope; require descending pivots and each of the latest two highs below the preceding two highs, or ascending pivots and each of the latest two lows above the preceding two lows, unless a prior channel was broken or invalidated; invalidate a descending candidate if any scanned low is above its line, or an ascending candidate if any scanned high is below its line; a descending channel breaks upward when close > its line, and an ascending channel breaks downward when close < its line.
Calculation: displayed stop and target = on a confluence signal when stop/target display is enabled, use the latest confirmed chart PivotLow(low, stop-structure pivot lookback) below entry close for a long stop reference, else the accumulation range low for an accumulation entry, else the stored setup level, else entry-bar low; long stop = min(reference, entry-bar low) - stop buffer, and target = entry close + (entry close - stop) × target multiple. For shorts, use the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, stop-structure pivot lookback) above entry close, else the accumulation range high for an accumulation entry, else the stored setup level, else entry-bar high; short stop = max(reference, entry-bar high) + stop buffer, and target = entry close - (stop - entry close) × target multiple. Track them only when risk is positive.
Long: on a confirmed chart bar within the configured session when session gating is enabled, with the long signal cooldown elapsed, either a long TAP or RECLAIM event was armed after the long arm cooldown and remains within its BOS waiting window, and close crosses above the last confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, BOS pivot lookback) plus the BOS buffer; OR an enabled accumulation range formed after a TAP or RECLAIM event closes above its frozen high within its time limit and passes its optional volume filter.
Short: on a confirmed chart bar within the configured session when session gating is enabled, with the short signal cooldown elapsed, either a short TAP or RECLAIM event was armed after the short arm cooldown and remains within its BOS waiting window, and close crosses below the last confirmed chart PivotLow(low, BOS pivot lookback) minus the BOS buffer; OR an enabled accumulation range formed after a TAP or RECLAIM event closes below its frozen low within its time limit and passes its optional volume filter.
Long: on a confirmed bar, either a Long condition fires and a descending channel broke upward within the trendline lookback window, or a previous completed T4/T5 high or low (T5 inclusion optional; key-level display enabled) was touched by the chart-bar range within the major-level window and close is above the most recently touched level, or a pending Long condition is within its forward window and a descending channel breaks upward on this bar. If multiple major levels are touched on one bar, select T4 high, T4 low, T5 high, then T5 low.
Short: on a confirmed bar, either a Short condition fires and an ascending channel broke downward within the trendline lookback window, or a previous completed T4/T5 high or low (T5 inclusion optional; key-level display enabled) was touched by the chart-bar range within the major-level window and close is below the most recently touched level, or a pending Short condition is within its forward window and an ascending channel breaks downward on this bar. If multiple major levels are touched on one bar, select T4 high, T4 low, T5 high, then T5 low.
Exit: long, a displayed long stop/target is active AND (low <= stop OR high >= target).
Exit: short, a displayed short stop/target is active AND (high >= stop OR low <= target).
