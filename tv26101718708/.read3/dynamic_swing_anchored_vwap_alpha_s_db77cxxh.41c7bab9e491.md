Calculation: Swing direction = bullish when the bar of the running highest high is later than the bar of the running lowest low; bearish otherwise. Update the running high only on a higher high and the running low only on a lower low, starting from the first chart bar.
Calculation: Swing-anchored VWAP = smoothed price-volume sum divided by smoothed volume sum. On a swing-direction change, seed the sums from the latest swing extreme (extreme price × its bar's volume, and that volume); otherwise seed them on the first chart bar with HLC3 × volume and volume. Each chart bar, update the sums as (1 − alpha) × prior sum + alpha × current HLC3 × volume, and (1 − alpha) × prior volume sum + alpha × current volume. Alpha = 1 − exp(−ln(2) / max(minimum divisor, tracking speed)). Tracking speed is the rounded value of base tracking speed divided by (ATR(ATR lookback) / RMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR lookback)) raised to volatility sensitivity, clamped between minimum and maximum tracking speeds, when volatility adaptation is enabled; otherwise use base tracking speed. Use a neutral volatility ratio when average ATR is not positive.
Long: Swing direction is bullish OR close crosses above Swing-anchored VWAP.
Short: Swing direction is bearish OR close crosses below Swing-anchored VWAP.
