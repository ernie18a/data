Calculation: Bullish setup origin = lowest low from the preceding confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) through the bullish BOS bar when the interval is shorter than the origin-search cap; otherwise use the BOS-bar low
Calculation: Bearish setup origin = highest high from the preceding confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) through the bearish BOS bar when the interval is shorter than the origin-search cap; otherwise use the BOS-bar high
Calculation: Bullish fib extreme = high at the bullish BOS, updated to a higher high on bars with no confirmed PivotHigh until the first confirmed PivotHigh, then frozen
Calculation: Bearish fib extreme = low at the bearish BOS, updated to a lower low on bars with no confirmed PivotLow until the first confirmed PivotLow, then frozen
Calculation: Active setup = side and origin of the most recent BOS, cleared when close is strictly beyond its origin and reset by every later BOS
Long: low < the most recent confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND close > that pivot low
Short: high > the most recent confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) AND close < that pivot high
Long: low > high[FVG gap separation] AND close crosses above the most recent confirmed PivotHigh series on this bar or within the preceding BOS crossover-window bars AND chart-bar distance from the prior bullish BOS exceeds the same-side BOS cooldown
Short: high < low[FVG gap separation] AND close crosses below the most recent confirmed PivotLow series on this bar or within the preceding BOS crossover-window bars AND chart-bar distance from the prior bearish BOS exceeds the same-side BOS cooldown
Long: active setup is bullish and has not alerted AND (HTF-alignment requirement is disabled OR close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) on chart bars) AND (liquidity-sweep requirement is disabled OR a bullish sweep occurred after the preceding BOS and on or before this BOS) AND (retracement requirement is disabled OR since this BOS a bar had low <= bullish fib extreme - (bullish fib extreme - bullish setup origin) * retracement level AND close >= bullish setup origin)
Short: active setup is bearish and has not alerted AND (HTF-alignment requirement is disabled OR close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) on chart bars) AND (liquidity-sweep requirement is disabled OR a bearish sweep occurred after the preceding BOS and on or before this BOS) AND (retracement requirement is disabled OR since this BOS a bar had high >= bearish fib extreme + (bearish setup origin - bearish fib extreme) * retracement level AND close <= bearish setup origin)
