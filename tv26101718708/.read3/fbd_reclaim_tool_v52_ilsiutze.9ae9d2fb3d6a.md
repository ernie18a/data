Calculation: armed major supports = supports that produced a Long flush mark during the current New York calendar day; clear them at the day change.
Calculation: armed major resistances = resistances that produced a Short sweep mark during the current New York calendar day; clear them at the day change.
Calculation: long reclaim duration = on chart bars taking values from T1 intrabars, accumulate each intrabar’s elapsed duration from setup start when its close is at or above the reclaimed support; when closes are below support, accumulate dip duration and reset reclaim duration once dip duration reaches the long dip-reset duration; reset dip duration when a close is at or above support. Use the still-forming T1 intrabar in real time and completed T1 intrabars otherwise.
Calculation: short reclaim duration = on chart bars taking values from T1 intrabars, accumulate each intrabar’s elapsed duration from setup start when its close is at or below the reclaimed resistance; when closes are above resistance, accumulate pop duration and reset reclaim duration once pop duration reaches the short dip-reset duration; reset pop duration when a close is at or below resistance. Use the still-forming T1 intrabar in real time and completed T1 intrabars otherwise.
Calculation: long stop = entry price minus minimum stop distance, replaced by the next major support below the swept support if next-level stops are enabled and that support is below the fixed stop.
Calculation: short stop = entry price plus minimum stop distance, replaced by the next major resistance above the swept resistance if next-level stops are enabled and that resistance is above the fixed stop.
Calculation: long target = the nearest major resistance above entry price if major-level targets are enabled and it is at least the minimum target distance above entry; otherwise entry price plus minimum target distance.
Calculation: short target = the nearest major support below entry price if major-level targets are enabled and it is at least the minimum target distance below entry; otherwise entry price minus minimum target distance.
Long: Long trading is enabled AND no long position is open AND no long setup is pending or the selected support is below the pending support AND the selected lowest qualifying major support is unarmed AND chart-bar low <= support minus flush depth.
Short: Short trading is enabled AND no short position is open AND no short setup is pending or the selected resistance is above the pending resistance AND the selected highest qualifying major resistance is unarmed AND chart-bar high >= resistance plus flush depth.
Long: Long trading is enabled AND no long position is open AND a pending setup at the selected lowest qualifying major support was started when chart-bar low <= support minus flush depth if unarmed or chart-bar low <= support if armed AND setup age <= flush validity AND long reclaim duration >= long acceptance duration.
Short: Short trading is enabled AND no short position is open AND a pending setup at the selected highest qualifying major resistance was started when chart-bar high >= resistance plus flush depth if unarmed or chart-bar high >= resistance if armed AND setup age <= flush validity AND short reclaim duration >= short acceptance duration.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
