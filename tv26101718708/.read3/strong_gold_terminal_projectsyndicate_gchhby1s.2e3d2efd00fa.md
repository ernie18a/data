Calculation: timeframe score(T) = the equal-weight mean of Supertrend direction score, EMA-count score, RSI score, MACD score, ADX/DI score, and range-position score, computed on T bars: Supertrend direction maps to opposite unit scores; EMA-count score maps the count of close above the fast, middle, and slow EMAs linearly from negative to positive unit score; RSI is centered and linearly scaled, clipped to unit score bounds; MACD histogram sign sets score direction, with full magnitude when its slope agrees and half magnitude otherwise; ADX/DI score is DI direction multiplied by ADX scaled to its saturation level; range-position score maps close's position between the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback linearly from negative to positive unit score, or zero for a zero-width range. EMA lookbacks are ordered fast < middle < slow; DMI uses the same length for DI and ADX. Heatmap Supertrend has separate ATR-lookback and factor parameters. The five timeframe scores use separate timeframe parameters; each is computed on its timeframe's still-forming bar and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off. Score normalization, MACD score magnitudes, and lookbacks are parameters.
Calculation: composite pressure = 100 × weighted mean of the five timeframe scores; the first two weights are equal, the third is lower than the equal fourth and fifth weights when higher-timeframe weighting is enabled, and all weights are equal when it is disabled.
Long: chart-bar close crosses above the high of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars
Short: chart-bar close crosses below the low of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars

Short: chart-timeframe Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish

Short: composite pressure <= -pressure threshold AND composite pressure[1] > -pressure threshold
