Calculation: T1 effort zone = For each T1 candle supplied within a chart bar, including the still-forming T1 candle when available, let body = abs(open - close), lower rejection = abs(low - close), upper rejection = abs(high - close), and effort = their maximum; ties choose body, then lower rejection, then upper rejection. If close > open or close = open and close > the previous T1 close, body and lower rejection give a bullish zone and upper rejection a bearish zone. If close < open or close = open and close < the previous T1 close, lower rejection gives a bullish zone and body and upper rejection a bearish zone. A flat candle unchanged from the previous T1 close gives no zone. Store effort and bounds: bullish body has bottom=open and top=open + effort × zone-width fraction parameter; bearish body has bottom=open - effort × zone-width fraction parameter and top=open; bullish lower rejection has bottom=low and top=max(open, low + effort × zone-width fraction parameter); bearish upper rejection has bottom=min(open, high - effort × zone-width fraction parameter) and top=high.
Calculation: T1 zone strength = Using the prior processed T1 effort records over a history-lookback parameter measured in chart-bar equivalents, let maximum volume and maximum effort be their respective maxima, average effort be the mean effort, and weighted-average volume = sum(volume × effort / volume-normalization parameter) / sum(effort). Assign major strength if volume > maximum volume AND effort > maximum effort; otherwise assign moderate strength if effort > maximum effort AND volume > weighted-average volume × high-volume ratio parameter; otherwise assign minor strength if volume > weighted-average volume × the same high-volume ratio parameter AND effort >= maximum effort × minimum-range ratio parameter. Otherwise assign minor strength if (volume > maximum volume OR volume >= weighted-average volume × absorption-volume ratio parameter) AND effort <= average effort × absorption-range ratio parameter AND (the preceding processed T1 effort event had a directional strength OR volume > maximum volume × extreme-volume ratio parameter) AND volume >= maximum volume × the same extreme-volume ratio parameter. Otherwise assign no directional strength.
Calculation: Directional weighted volume = On each chart bar, sum the signed volume × effort / volume-normalization parameter for processed T1 effort events over the history-lookback parameter, positive for bullish zones and negative for bearish zones; use the latest available T1 events, including a still-forming T1 candle when available.
Long: A bullish T1 effort zone is created with minor, moderate, or major strength.
Short: A bearish T1 effort zone is created with minor, moderate, or major strength.
Long: Directional weighted volume > 0.
Short: Directional weighted volume <= 0.
Long: On a subsequent chart bar within the trap-window parameter after creation, an active bearish T1 effort zone of any strength that has not already been trapped has close >= its top + its stored effort.
Short: On a subsequent chart bar within the trap-window parameter after creation, an active bullish T1 effort zone of any strength that has not already been trapped has close <= its bottom - its stored effort.
Long: A bearish T1 effort zone that was trapped within the trap-window parameter after creation has close < its bottom - its stored effort on a subsequent chart bar within that window.
Short: A bullish T1 effort zone that was trapped within the trap-window parameter after creation has close > its top + its stored effort on a subsequent chart bar within that window.
Long: For the first time after creation, a major bullish T1 effort zone is tested by a chart bar whose low <= the zone top.
Short: For the first time after creation, a major bearish T1 effort zone is tested by a chart bar whose high >= the zone bottom.
