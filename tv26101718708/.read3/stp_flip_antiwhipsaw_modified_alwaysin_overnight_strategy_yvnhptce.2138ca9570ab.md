Calculation: Trail bands = EMA(close, trail lookback) ± ATR(ATR lookback) × base ATR multiplier × adaptive factor; when adaptive adjustment is enabled, choose the factor by PercentRank(ATR, volatility-rank lookback) using separate low and high rank cutoffs and low, neutral, and high factors; when ratcheting is enabled, reset the relevant band to its raw value on a direction flip, otherwise ratchet the long lower band upward with max(raw lower band, prior lower band) and the short upper band downward with min(raw upper band, prior upper band); the other band remains raw.
Calculation: Trail direction = remain neutral until bar index reaches max(ATR lookback, trail lookback, regime lookback) plus warm-up buffer; thereafter, long when close > prior upper trail band, short when close < prior lower trail band, otherwise retain prior direction.
Calculation: Breakout distance = close − prior upper trail band when trail direction is long; prior lower trail band − close when trail direction is short; zero when neutral.
Calculation: Breakout strength = when ATR > 0, min(abs(breakout distance) / ATR, configured breakout cap) / configured breakout cap × configured score ceiling; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Market regime score = weighted sum of min(ADX(adx lookback) / configured ADX normalization × configured score ceiling, configured score ceiling), clamp(configured score ceiling − ChoppinessIndex(high, low, close, choppiness lookback), 0, configured score ceiling), and R²(close, regime lookback) × configured score ceiling, using separate component weights.
Calculation: Signal quality(direction) = weighted mean of higher-timeframe bias, volume confirmation, RSI direction, market regime score, and breakout strength using configured component scores and weights; higher-timeframe bias compares close(T1, last completed bar) with EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar), volume confirmation compares volume with its moving average times the volume threshold when volume and its average are positive, and RSI direction compares RSI(close, momentum lookback) with its midpoint; omit the higher-timeframe and volume components from the denominator when their filters are disabled or their data is unavailable.
Calculation: Long trend alignment = EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback), and, when slow-EMA slope is required, EMA(close, slow lookback) > its prior value; fast and slow lookbacks are separate, with the fast lookback shorter.
Calculation: Short trend alignment = EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback), and, when slow-EMA slope is required, EMA(close, slow lookback) < its prior value; fast and slow lookbacks are separate, with the fast lookback shorter.
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR(ATR lookback) ≥ SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), expansion lookback) × expansion threshold, when the ATR average is positive.
Long: Trail direction is long.
Short: Trail direction is short.
Long: Trail direction changes from short to long after warm-up AND the chart bar is confirmed AND Signal quality(long) ≥ minimum quality AND (hard choppy-signal skip is disabled OR Market regime score ≥ choppy cutoff) AND (trend alignment filter is disabled OR Long trend alignment) AND (volatility expansion filter is disabled OR ATR expansion) AND (minimum-breakout filter is disabled OR abs(breakout distance) / ATR ≥ minimum breakout threshold).
Short: Trail direction changes from long to short after warm-up AND the chart bar is confirmed AND Signal quality(short) ≥ minimum quality AND (hard choppy-signal skip is disabled OR Market regime score ≥ choppy cutoff) AND (trend alignment filter is disabled OR Short trend alignment) AND (volatility expansion filter is disabled OR ATR expansion) AND (minimum-breakout filter is disabled OR abs(breakout distance) / ATR ≥ minimum breakout threshold).
Calculation: Trading-period P/L = closed P/L since the most recent configured P/L reset + current open P/L.
Calculation: Daily lock = reset at each configured P/L reset; once Trading-period P/L reaches an active profit target or loss limit, remain locked until the next reset.
Calculation: Profit-trailing stop = while enabled, arm when open P/L reaches the configured arming threshold, or immediately if that threshold is nonpositive; initialize the favorable-price peak from the entry bar high for long or low for short, then update it to the highest high for long or lowest low for short; use the configured fixed-point or ATR-multiple distance, or the progressive ATR distance when progressive locking is enabled; for progressive locking, select the lock amount for the highest reached profit stage, convert it to points using point value × absolute position size, and combine the resulting entry-price lock with the peak-based stop using max for long or min for short; optionally guard the stop at breakeven and ratchet it only in the favorable direction; calculate from completed bars and activate the submitted stop on the following bar.
Exit: long, per-trade profit limit is enabled and its target is active and open P/L ≥ target OR per-trade loss limit is enabled and its limit is active and open P/L ≤ −limit OR flatten-on-daily-limit is enabled and Daily lock is active OR flatten-at-session-end is enabled and the prior chart bar was in the configured trading window and the current bar is outside it OR profit trailing is enabled and armed and low ≤ active profit-trailing stop.
Exit: short, per-trade profit limit is enabled and its target is active and open P/L ≥ target OR per-trade loss limit is enabled and its limit is active and open P/L ≤ −limit OR flatten-on-daily-limit is enabled and Daily lock is active OR flatten-at-session-end is enabled and the prior chart bar was in the configured trading window and the current bar is outside it OR profit trailing is enabled and armed and high ≥ active profit-trailing stop.
