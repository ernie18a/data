Calculation: Smoothed efficiency = EMA(EfficiencyRatio(close, slow base lookback), efficiency smoothing length)
Calculation: Fast ribbon average = EMA(close, adaptive mode ? max(fast minimum length, fast base lookback × (maximum adaptive stretch − smoothed efficiency)) : fast base lookback); fast base lookback is shorter than slow base lookback
Calculation: Slow ribbon average = EMA(close, adaptive mode ? max(slow minimum length, slow base lookback × (maximum adaptive stretch − smoothed efficiency)) : slow base lookback)
Calculation: Ribbon direction = up when fast ribbon average > slow ribbon average, otherwise down
Calculation: Ribbon upper edge = max(fast ribbon average, slow ribbon average)
Calculation: Ribbon lower edge = min(fast ribbon average, slow ribbon average)
Calculation: Ribbon width = ribbon upper edge − ribbon lower edge
Calculation: Trend age = number of chart bars since the most recent ribbon direction change
Calculation: Ribbon flip count = SUM(1 when ribbon direction differs from its prior chart bar, chop lookback)
Calculation: Last qualified start direction = ribbon direction on the most recent bar where trend age >= confirmation lookback and close is beyond the ribbon edge in that direction; update even when chop suppresses the start marker
Calculation: Smoothed trend strength = EMA(clamp(S, 0, 1), strength smoothing length), where separation = 0 if ATR(ATR lookback) <= 0, otherwise min(abs(fast ribbon average − slow ribbon average) / ATR(ATR lookback), separation cap) / separation cap; signed volume ratio = SUM(volume on up-close bars and negative volume otherwise, volume lookback) / (SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume lookback); direction-adjusted volume support = clamp(signed volume ratio in the ribbon direction, 0, 1); when volume scoring is enabled and current volume and its moving average are available with a positive average, S = volume-mode separation weight × separation + volume-mode efficiency weight × efficiency strength multiplier × smoothed efficiency + volume support weight × direction-adjusted volume support; otherwise S = no-volume separation weight × separation + no-volume efficiency weight × efficiency strength multiplier × smoothed efficiency
Long: On a closed chart bar with ATR available, either ribbon direction is up AND trend age >= confirmation lookback AND close > ribbon upper edge AND the last qualified start direction before this bar is not up AND ribbon flip count <= maximum flips; OR ribbon direction is up AND no ribbon direction change occurs on this bar AND trend age >= minimum pullback age AND the last qualified start direction is up AND this is the first close above the ribbon upper edge after an earlier bar in this trend reached the pullback depth into the ribbon and closed at or below the upper edge AND close > open AND smoothed trend strength >= steady-strength threshold AND ribbon flip count <= maximum flips AND bars since the prior pullback >= minimum pullback spacing
Short: On a closed chart bar with ATR available, either ribbon direction is down AND trend age >= confirmation lookback AND close < ribbon lower edge AND the last qualified start direction before this bar is not down AND ribbon flip count <= maximum flips; OR ribbon direction is down AND no ribbon direction change occurs on this bar AND trend age >= minimum pullback age AND the last qualified start direction is down AND this is the first close below the ribbon lower edge after an earlier bar in this trend reached the pullback depth into the ribbon and closed at or above the lower edge AND close < open AND smoothed trend strength >= steady-strength threshold AND ribbon flip count <= maximum flips AND bars since the prior pullback >= minimum pullback spacing
