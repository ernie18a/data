Calculation: Structural direction and pivot levels = use confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left/right lookbacks of half the fractal-length parameter, rounded down; each new pivot replaces that side's level and resets it to unbroken; a level is consumed and structural direction becomes bullish or bearish only when its configured close-or-wick break test and trend filter pass; direction starts neutral and otherwise persists.
Calculation: Entry zone = for bullish setups, lower bound = setup high − range × deeper retracement fraction and upper bound = setup high − range × shallower retracement fraction; for bearish setups, lower bound = setup low + range × shallower retracement fraction and upper bound = setup low + range × deeper retracement fraction; range is setup high − setup low. Bullish setup anchors are the break-bar high and latest confirmed pivot low; bearish setup anchors are the latest confirmed pivot high and break-bar low.
Long: (close > latest unbroken confirmed pivot high when close-break mode is enabled, otherwise high > that pivot high) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA of close over its lookback) AND ((prior structural direction is bearish AND reversal engine is enabled) OR (prior structural direction is bullish AND continuation engine is enabled)).
Short: (close < latest unbroken confirmed pivot low when close-break mode is enabled, otherwise low < that pivot low) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA of close over its lookback) AND ((prior structural direction is bullish AND reversal engine is enabled) OR (prior structural direction is bearish AND continuation engine is enabled)).
Long: At least one enabled engine has a latest bullish setup AND close is greater than or equal to that setup's lower entry-zone bound AND close is less than or equal to its upper entry-zone bound.
Short: At least one enabled engine has a latest bearish setup AND close is greater than or equal to that setup's lower entry-zone bound AND close is less than or equal to its upper entry-zone bound.
