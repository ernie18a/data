Calculation: Intraday extrema = maximum high and minimum low of T1 bars in the premarket and current regular-session intervals, respectively; evaluate on chart bars using available T1 values, including a T1 bar still forming, and reset by session date.
Calculation: Opening-range outputs(d) = max high and min low of completed T1 bars from session open through duration d; on each later completed T1 bar through session close, track upward and downward states, resetting them and their event times each session date. An idle or failed side enters breakout on a close beyond its range edge; an active side fails on a close back inside beyond the failure-buffer percentage of range width; otherwise, a later bar touching the edge changes breakout to retest, and a still later close beyond the edge changes retest to confirmed. A failed side can rearm on a later breakout. Net direction is the active side with the latest event when both sides are active (ties favor upward), the sole active side when only one is active, or neutral otherwise; when the sole active side is in breakout and the opposite side has failed, its net stage is retest. Emit a confirmation event on the first confirmation for each side per session date, and on later re-confirmations only when the re-alert setting is enabled. Use three separate ascending duration parameters dA, dB, dC and one shared failure-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Opening-range status(d) = Inside until session open plus d; afterward, Bullish when current chart close exceeds the range high, Bearish when it is below the range low, and Inside otherwise.
Calculation: Session VWAP deviation = square root of the sum, over chart bars in the current session through the current chart bar, of volume × (close − VWAP(close))², divided by the greater of one and summed volume; reset by session date. VWAP and this deviation use chart bars, including the current bar while forming.
Calculation: Relative strength = the traded instrument's percentage change from its last completed T2 close minus the benchmark's percentage change from its last completed T2 close. Current closes are chart-timeframe values on chart bars; benchmark close and VWAP may be from a forming chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: MA50 ATR distance = (current chart close − selected EMA or SMA using the third of five separate increasing lookback parameters, on the last completed T2 bar) / ATR using its separate lookback parameter on the last completed T2 bar.
Calculation: MA-structure contribution = the score mapped from the count of the five selected T2 moving averages below current chart close (all, four, three, two, one, or none map to separate score parameters) plus the score mapped from the count of adjacent pairs where the shorter-lookback average exceeds the longer-lookback average (all pairs, all but one, half, one, or none map to separate score parameters). All five use one shared EMA-or-SMA type parameter and their separate increasing lookbacks; T2 values are from the last completed bar.
Calculation: Intraday contribution = opening-range contribution + VWAP-location contribution + failure contribution + session-phase contribution. Opening-range contribution is the clamped sum of: for dB, separate positive or negative score parameters for upward or downward confirmed and retest net stages, otherwise a smaller signed score when its status is Bullish or Bearish; and for dA, one signed score when its net stage is confirmed, otherwise one signed score when its status is Bullish or Bearish. Clamp this component to its separate cap. VWAP-location contribution uses ordered branches: close at or above VWAP plus the nearer deviation band; else close at or above VWAP; else close at or above VWAP minus the near-VWAP band; else close at or below VWAP minus the nearer deviation band; else. Each branch has its own score parameter, with bearish branches negative. The nearer deviation band is session VWAP deviation times its multiplier; the near-VWAP band is last-completed-T2 ATR times its factor when ATR is positive, otherwise session VWAP deviation times its fallback factor. Failure contribution subtracts a dB upward-side failure weight and adds a dB downward-side failure weight, and subtracts a dA upward-side failure weight and adds a dA downward-side failure weight; clamp to its separate cap. Session-phase contribution uses dB net direction and elapsed time since session open: when direction is non-neutral and elapsed time is at or below the early-window parameter and net stage is confirmed, add the signed early weight; else when elapsed time reaches the late-window parameter, subtract the signed late weight; else when elapsed time reaches the middle-window parameter and net stage is not confirmed, subtract the signed middle weight.
Calculation: Key-level contribution = the sum of prior-T2-range, premarket-range, and session-position contributions, clamped to its separate cap. For each available prior-T2 range and premarket range, add the key-level unit when close is above its high, subtract it when close is below its low, otherwise add it when close is at or above the midpoint and subtract it otherwise. For an available session range with high above low, add the unit when (close − session low) / (session high − session low) reaches the separate upper-position threshold; otherwise subtract it when that fraction is at or below the separate lower-position threshold.
Calculation: Benchmark-regime contribution = zero when benchmark percentage change is unavailable; otherwise add the benchmark-VWAP weight when benchmark close is at or above benchmark VWAP and subtract it otherwise, then add the strong positive-change weight when benchmark percentage change exceeds its threshold, the smaller positive-change weight when it is positive, subtract the strong weight when it is below the negative threshold, or subtract the smaller weight when it is negative. Clamp to its separate cap. Benchmark close and VWAP are chart-timeframe values on chart bars and may be forming; benchmark percentage change uses its last completed T2 close.
Calculation: Relative-strength contribution = zero when relative strength is unavailable; otherwise map relative strength above descending high, middle, and low thresholds to separate positive weights, and below the corresponding negative thresholds to their negative weights, with zero between thresholds. Add the bonus weight when dB status is Bullish, benchmark close is below benchmark VWAP, and relative strength is positive; subtract it when dB status is Bearish, benchmark close is at or above benchmark VWAP, and relative strength is negative. Clamp to its separate cap.
Calculation: Pre-room contribution = MA-structure contribution + intraday contribution + key-level contribution + period-open contribution + benchmark-regime contribution + relative-strength contribution. Period-open contribution adds the open-unit when close is at or above the open of the current T3 period and adds it again when close is at or above the open of the current T4 period; use the current period's forming-bar open once that period has started, otherwise the preceding bar's open.
Calculation: Target-room contribution = when last-completed-T2 ATR is positive, find the nearest resistance above close among prior-T2 high, available premarket high, prior completed T3 high, prior completed T4 high, and VWAP plus the farther deviation band; find the nearest support below close from the corresponding lows and VWAP minus that band. If pre-room contribution is positive and resistance exists, subtract the largest, middle, or smallest room penalty as resistance distance divided by ATR falls below successive ascending room thresholds; add the room reward when that ratio exceeds the far-room threshold. If pre-room contribution is negative and support exists, add the corresponding penalties for support distance divided by ATR below those thresholds, and subtract the reward when the ratio exceeds the far-room threshold. Otherwise this contribution is neutral.
Calculation: Directional bias score = pre-room contribution + target-room contribution + extension contribution. Extension contribution uses MA50 ATR distance: beyond the high extension threshold, score against its sign using the high-extension weight; otherwise beyond the middle threshold, score against its sign using the middle-extension weight; otherwise at or beyond the low threshold, score with its sign using the low-extension weight; otherwise neutral. The thresholds are separate and ordered low to high.
Long: directional bias score reaches the bullish-label threshold OR any dA, dB, or dC net opening-range stage is upward OR any such net stage is neutral, neither side has failed, and its opening-range status is Bullish OR an upward confirmation event occurs for dA or dB OR dC status changes to Bullish from the prior non-Inside status on a confirmed chart bar after dC ends and before session close OR close is at or above VWAP(close) OR close is above the high of the last completed T2 bar OR premarket high and low are available and close is above premarket high OR session high and low are available, session high exceeds session low, and close is at or above their midpoint OR MA50 ATR distance is positive and its absolute value is at or below the middle extension threshold OR relative strength exceeds the low relative-strength threshold OR close is at or above the current T3 period open OR close is at or above the current T4 period open OR close exceeds any of the five selected moving averages from the last completed T2 bar OR dC status is Bullish and differs from the prior non-Inside status on a confirmed chart bar during the regular session after dC ends and before session close.
Short: directional bias score reaches the negative of the bullish-label threshold OR any dA, dB, or dC net opening-range stage is downward OR any such net stage is neutral, neither side has failed, and its opening-range status is Bearish OR a downward confirmation event occurs for dA or dB OR dC status changes to Bearish from the prior non-Inside status on a confirmed chart bar after dC ends and before session close OR close is below VWAP(close) OR close is below the low of the last completed T2 bar OR premarket high and low are available and close is below premarket low OR session high and low are available, session high exceeds session low, and close is below their midpoint OR MA50 ATR distance is non-positive and its absolute value is at or below the middle extension threshold OR relative strength is below the negative low relative-strength threshold OR close is below the current T3 period open OR close is below the current T4 period open OR close is at or below any of the five selected moving averages from the last completed T2 bar OR dC status is Bearish and differs from the prior non-Inside status on a confirmed chart bar during the regular session after dC ends and before session close.
