Calculation: rolling normalization(x, length, gain) = clamp to [0,1] of sigmoid((x - EMA(x, length)) / max(StDev(x, length), floor) * gain)
Calculation: log return = ln(max(selected price source, price floor) / max(selected price source[one bar ago], price floor))
Calculation: filtered price = recursive one-step Kalman filter of selected price source, initialized with the first source value and covariance; use separate process-noise and measurement-noise parameters
Calculation: volatility-state transition = clamp to [0,1] of SMA(abs(state - state[one bar ago]), transition lookback), where state is 1 when rolling standard deviation of log return exceeds its rolling median, otherwise 0
Calculation: Hawkes pressure = rolling normalization of recursive jump state; jump state uses absolute log return divided by its rolling standard deviation when absolute log return exceeds jump multiplier times that standard deviation, otherwise 0, and the recursive state uses separate decay and offset parameters
Calculation: wavelet energy = rolling normalization of (log source - 2 * log source[lag A] + log source[lag B]) squared, with the second difference formed from the corresponding lagged log-source values
Calculation: spectral purity = rolling normalization of the maximum of absolute differences between SMA(log return, short period) and SMA(log return, twice that period), at three successive period scales, divided by StDev(log return, noise period)
Calculation: DFA strength = rolling normalization of StDev(cumulative sum(log return - SMA(log return, drift period)) - linear regression of that cumulative sum, regression period, zero offset) / max(StDev(log return, DFA period), floor)
Calculation: subatomic mass = rolling normalization of absolute log return * (1 + mass coefficient * wavelet energy)
Calculation: subatomic spin = tanh(sign(log return) * min(Hawkes jump state, spin cap) * spin coefficient)
Calculation: subatomic charge = tanh(charge coefficient * SMA((log return - SMA(log return, skew period)) cubed, skew period) / max(StDev(log return, skew period) cubed, floor))
Calculation: subatomic lifetime = rolling normalization of 1 / max(StDev(log return, lifetime period), floor)
Calculation: Kalman lock = 1 - rolling normalization(abs((selected price source - filtered price[one bar ago]) / max(selected price source, price floor)), model memory, lock gain)
Calculation: drift model = tanh(drift multiplier * EMA(log return, trend length) / volatility basis) * DFA strength, where volatility basis is StDev(log return, volatility period)
Calculation: Kalman model = tanh(Kalman velocity multiplier * (filtered price - filtered price[Kalman lag]) / max(max(selected price source, price floor) * volatility basis, floor)) * Kalman lock * spectral purity
Calculation: wave model = tanh(wave impulse multiplier * (log selected price source - log selected price source[wave lag]) / volatility basis) * wavelet energy * spectral purity * (1 - wave Hawkes coefficient * Hawkes pressure)
Calculation: RSI model = tanh((RSI(selected price source, RSI length) - RSI midpoint) / RSI scale) * (RSI base weight + RSI purity weight * spectral purity)
Calculation: spin model = subatomic spin * subatomic mass * (spin base weight + spin lifetime weight * subatomic lifetime)
Calculation: charge model = subatomic charge * (1 - Hawkes pressure) * (charge base weight + charge purity weight * spectral purity)
Calculation: shock risk = clamp to [0,1] of Hawkes weight * Hawkes pressure + transition weight * volatility-state transition + lifetime weight * (1 - subatomic lifetime)
Calculation: model confidence = clamp to [0,1] of lock weight * Kalman lock + purity weight * spectral purity + DFA weight * DFA strength + mass weight * subatomic mass + lifetime weight * subatomic lifetime - shock weight * shock risk
Calculation: directional score = tanh(ensemble multiplier * weighted mean of spin model, drift model, wave model, Kalman model, charge model, and RSI model) * (score base weight + confidence weight * model confidence); use separate input weights for the six models
Calculation: signal threshold = base threshold when adaptive threshold is disabled; otherwise clamp the greater of base threshold and the rolling percentile of absolute directional score to the configured threshold bounds
Calculation: model coordination = abs(spin model + drift model + wave model + Kalman model) / max(abs(spin model) + abs(drift model) + abs(wave model) + abs(Kalman model), floor)
Calculation: model dispersion = 1 - model coordination
Calculation: manifold quality = coordination weight * model coordination + purity weight * spectral purity + DFA weight * DFA strength + lifetime weight * subatomic lifetime
Calculation: signal stack = tanh(stack multiplier * (spin model + drift model + wave model + Kalman model))
Calculation: direction-aligned value of x = x * sign of directional score, using positive one when directional score is zero
Calculation: union qualification = alpha 1 OR alpha 2 OR alpha 3 OR alpha 4 OR alpha 5 OR alpha 6 OR alpha 7 OR alpha 8 OR alpha 9 OR alpha 10 OR alpha 11 OR alpha 12; each alpha is the following conjunction, with each rolling percentile rank a separate parameter and all percentiles calculated over the union lookback: alpha 1, spin model is not used: Kalman model > its percentile and drift model > its percentile and manifold quality > its percentile; alpha 2, drift model > its percentile AND spin model > its percentile AND subatomic mass > its percentile; alpha 3, Kalman model > its percentile AND Kalman lock < its percentile AND direction-aligned spin model > its percentile; alpha 4, direction-aligned directional score > its percentile AND Kalman lock < its percentile AND manifold quality > its percentile; alpha 5, direction-aligned Kalman model > its percentile AND DFA strength > its percentile AND spin model > its percentile; alpha 6, direction-aligned drift model > its percentile AND Kalman lock < its percentile AND spin model > its percentile; alpha 7, direction-aligned Kalman model > its percentile AND DFA strength > its percentile AND direction-aligned spin model > its percentile; alpha 8, direction-aligned directional score > its percentile AND spectral purity > its percentile AND shock risk < its percentile; alpha 9, manifold quality > its percentile AND model coordination > its percentile AND model dispersion < its percentile AND subatomic lifetime > its percentile; alpha 10, direction-aligned wave model > its percentile AND wavelet energy > its percentile AND Hawkes pressure < its percentile AND direction-aligned Kalman model > its percentile; alpha 11, direction-aligned charge model > its percentile AND DFA strength > its percentile AND model confidence > its percentile AND shock risk < its percentile; alpha 12, direction-aligned drift model > its percentile AND direction-aligned spin model > its percentile AND wavelet energy > its percentile AND spectral purity > its percentile
Long: bar index > max(model memory, union lookback) AND long signals enabled AND model confidence >= minimum confidence AND shock risk <= maximum shock risk AND model dispersion <= maximum dispersion AND model coordination >= minimum coordination AND (union filter disabled OR union qualification) AND (directional score crosses above signal threshold OR (continuation signals enabled AND directional score > signal threshold AND signal stack crosses above zero))
Short: bar index > max(model memory, union lookback) AND short signals enabled AND model confidence >= minimum confidence AND shock risk <= maximum shock risk AND model dispersion <= maximum dispersion AND model coordination >= minimum coordination AND (union filter disabled OR union qualification) AND (directional score crosses below negative signal threshold OR (continuation signals enabled AND directional score < negative signal threshold AND signal stack crosses below zero))
