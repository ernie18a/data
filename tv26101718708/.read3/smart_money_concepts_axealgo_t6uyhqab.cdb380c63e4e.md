Calculation: Structure displacement = max(abs(close - close[clamp(bars since pivot, minimum offset, maximum offset)]), abs(close - pivot price)) / ATR(ATR lookback)
Long: On a closed bar, close > the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high from the shorter- or longer-lookback structure stream AND Structure displacement >= minimum displacement AND (the break changes that stream's direction from bearish to bullish OR no same-direction structure marking from that stream was emitted within the BOS throttle)
Short: On a closed bar, close < the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low from the shorter- or longer-lookback structure stream AND Structure displacement >= minimum displacement AND (the break changes that stream's direction from bullish to bearish OR no same-direction structure marking from that stream was emitted within the BOS throttle)
