Calculation: Short EMA = EMA(close, short EMA lookback).
Calculation: Core upper = max(EMA(close, first core EMA lookback), EMA(close, second core EMA lookback)); both core EMA lookbacks are longer than the short EMA lookback.
Calculation: Core lower = min(EMA(close, first core EMA lookback), EMA(close, second core EMA lookback)).
Calculation: ATR = ATR(ATR lookback).
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume MA lookback) when the SMA is positive, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish = higher-timeframe filter disabled OR, on chart bars, close from the last completed T1 bar > max(EMA(close, first core EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar), EMA(close, second core EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar)) OR SuperTrend direction on the last completed T1 bar is bullish.
Calculation: Bullish OB retest = OB reference enabled AND a bullish displacement breakout has established a fresh bullish block within the OB maximum age, where the breakout close exceeds the prior OB swing-lookback high by the required ATR displacement and a prior bearish candle within that lookback supplies the block low and open; current low <= block open + ATR * OB retest buffer AND current close >= block low - ATR * OB retest buffer.
Calculation: Bullish OB reclaim = bullish OB retest AND close > block open AND close > open.
Calculation: Recent bullish OBV divergence = bullish OBV divergence enabled AND a confirmed price pivot low and OBV pivot low occur together, with price lower and OBV higher than at the preceding paired pivot lows, and the pivot is within the divergence maximum age.
Calculation: Recent bearish OBV divergence = bearish OBV divergence blocking enabled AND a confirmed price pivot high and OBV pivot high occur together, with price higher and OBV lower than at the preceding paired pivot highs, and the pivot is within the divergence maximum age.
Calculation: Buy stage = buy signals increase the stage, sell-reduce signals decrease it to a floor of zero, and a bar without a bullish active trend resets it to zero.
Calculation: Sell stage = sell-reduce signals increase the stage; a bar without a bullish active trend resets it, and a buy signal resets it when sell-stage reset is enabled and the resulting buy stage exceeds the first stage.
Calculation: Active long cycle = true after a buy signal until an exit signal or a bar without a bullish active trend.
Calculation: Last buy price = close of the most recent buy signal in the current active trend; reset when the bullish active trend ends.
Calculation: Average entry price = weighted average of buy-signal closes, weighted by the applicable trial, setup, or add size parameter; reset on exit or when the bullish active trend ends.
Calculation: Peak high = highest high since the active long cycle began, updated on each chart bar and reset on exit or when the bullish active trend ends.
Calculation: Core confirmed = latched true after a breakout buy condition or stable closes above Core upper; reset when the bullish active trend ends.
Calculation: Early-sell DI+ falling = DI+ is falling over the early-sell DI lookback.
Calculation: Exhaustion DI+ falling = DI+ is falling over the exhaustion-filter DI lookback when that filter is enabled.
Calculation: RSI overbought = RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= the overbought threshold when the RSI filter is enabled.
Calculation: Near cycle high = the cycle-high filter is enabled AND high >= highest(high, cycle-high lookback) - ATR * cycle-high buffer.
Calculation: Momentum exhausted = RSI overbought OR exhaustion DI+ falling OR near cycle high AND (RSI overbought OR exhaustion DI+ falling OR recent bearish OBV divergence).
Calculation: Bullish OBV risk clear = NOT recent bearish OBV divergence.
Calculation: Bear pressure = close < open AND volume ratio >= bear-pressure volume multiplier AND (close < Short EMA OR open - close >= ATR * bear-candle body parameter).
Calculation: Recent bear pressure = a bear-pressure bar occurred within the volume-price lookback.
Calculation: Bullish reclaim volume = close > open AND close > previous chart-bar high AND volume ratio >= bullish reclaim volume threshold.
Calculation: Volume-price add permitted = volume-price filter disabled OR no recent bear pressure OR bullish reclaim volume.
Calculation: Support touched = lowest(low - Short EMA, pullback lookback) <= ATR * pullback distance OR lowest(low - Core upper, pullback lookback) <= ATR * pullback distance.
Calculation: Not deeply pulled back = highest(high, pullback lookback) - close <= ATR * maximum pullback-from-high parameter.
Calculation: Add pullback permitted = not deeply pulled back AND Short EMA >= its previous chart-bar value.
Calculation: SuperTrend safe = SuperTrend direction is bullish AND close > SuperTrend line AND close - SuperTrend line >= ATR * minimum SuperTrend distance.
Calculation: Add trend healthy = SuperTrend safe AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND (ADX-rising requirement disabled OR ADX >= its previous chart-bar value) AND (DI requirement disabled OR DI+ > DI-).
Calculation: Reclaim trend healthy = SuperTrend safe AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND (DI requirement disabled OR DI+ > DI-).
Calculation: Setup trend healthy = SuperTrend direction is bullish AND SuperTrend line < close AND (DI requirement disabled OR DI+ > DI-).
Calculation: Core reduction raw = close < Core lower AND previous chart-bar close < previous chart-bar Core lower.
Calculation: Structure risk = structure-risk requirement disabled OR core reduction raw OR (SuperTrend direction is bullish AND close < SuperTrend line).
Calculation: EMA20 sell confirmation = highest(close - Short EMA, EMA20 sell-confirmation lookback) < -ATR * EMA20 sell-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Weak reduction raw = SuperTrend direction is bullish AND structure risk AND (EMA20 light sell enabled AND EMA20 sell confirmation OR early sell enabled AND (DI-cross requirement disabled OR DI- > DI+) AND early-sell DI+ falling AND close < Short EMA - ATR * EMA20 sell-buffer parameter OR early sell enabled AND close > previous chart-bar close AND close < Short EMA AND volume ratio <= weak-rally maximum volume multiplier AND early-sell DI+ falling).
Calculation: No recent risk = no weak reduction raw, core reduction raw, or bearish SuperTrend bar within the recent-risk lookback.
Calculation: No recent sell churn = no weak reduction raw or core reduction raw within the post-sell buy-block lookback.
Calculation: Breakout buy condition = Higher-timeframe bullish AND SuperTrend direction is bullish AND close > Core upper AND (volume ratio >= breakout volume multiplier OR lowest(close - Core upper, stable-close lookback) > 0).
Calculation: Pullback buy condition = Higher-timeframe bullish AND Add trend healthy AND Volume-price add permitted AND close > Short EMA AND close > open AND volume ratio <= pullback maximum volume multiplier AND Support touched AND Not deeply pulled back AND No recent risk.
Calculation: Bullish OB reference buy condition = Higher-timeframe bullish AND SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lowest(close - Short EMA, breakout-hold lookback) > 0 AND close > Short EMA AND close > open AND No recent risk AND Bullish OBV risk clear AND (Bullish OB reclaim OR Recent bullish OBV divergence).
Calculation: Initial trial buy condition = trial buy enabled by buy stage being zero AND (Higher-timeframe bullish AND SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lowest(close - Short EMA, breakout-hold lookback) > 0 AND close < Core upper AND volume ratio >= trial minimum volume ratio AND (ADX requirement disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND DI+ - DI- >= minimum DI spread AND Short EMA > its previous chart-bar value AND No recent risk OR Breakout buy condition OR buy stage is zero AND SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lowest(close - Short EMA, breakout-hold lookback) > 0 AND close > open AND DI+ - DI- >= minimum DI spread AND (fresh-trend ADX requirement disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND (close > previous chart-bar high OR close > Short EMA + ATR * EMA reclaim buffer parameter) OR buy stage is zero AND Higher-timeframe bullish AND SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lowest(close - Short EMA, breakout-hold lookback) > 0 AND close > open AND close > Core lower AND close >= Core upper - ATR * core-reclaim tolerance parameter AND close > previous chart-bar high AND volume ratio >= trial minimum volume ratio AND DI+ - DI- >= minimum DI spread AND ADX >= minimum ADX OR Bullish OB reference buy condition).
Calculation: OB-reference add progress = buy stage is at least the first stage AND close > Last buy price + ATR * OB-reference add progression parameter AND Bullish OB reference buy condition.
Calculation: Second-stage add progress = buy stage is the first stage AND close > Core upper + ATR * second-add core buffer AND Short EMA > its previous chart-bar value AND Core upper >= its previous chart-bar value AND close > Last buy price + ATR * second-add progression parameter AND close > Short EMA + ATR * EMA reclaim buffer parameter AND close > highest(high of prior chart bars, second-add breakout lookback) AND close > open AND volume ratio >= bullish reclaim volume threshold.
Calculation: Later-add progress = buy stage exceeds the first stage AND close > Last buy price + ATR * move-add progression parameter AND ((close > previous chart-bar high AND close > open AND volume ratio >= bullish reclaim volume threshold) OR (close > highest(high of prior chart bars, breakout-add lookback) AND volume ratio >= breakout volume multiplier)).
Calculation: Add progress = if buy stage is the first stage, second-stage add progress OR OB-reference add progress; otherwise, later-add progress OR OB-reference add progress.

Calculation: Trend add = buy stage is at least the first stage AND buy stage < maximum buy stages AND stage cooldown elapsed since the last buy AND Core confirmed AND Add trend healthy AND Volume-price add permitted AND Add pullback permitted AND Add progress AND close > Short EMA AND close >= previous chart-bar close AND close - Short EMA <= ATR * trend-add maximum distance AND No recent risk AND Bullish OBV risk clear AND NOT Momentum exhausted.
Calculation: Setup add condition = Higher-timeframe bullish AND Setup trend healthy AND Volume-price add permitted AND close > Short EMA AND close >= open AND highest(high, setup lookback) - lowest(low, setup lookback) <= ATR * setup maximum range AND lowest(close - Short EMA, setup lookback) >= -ATR * setup hold tolerance parameter AND Short EMA >= its previous chart-bar value AND Core upper >= its previous chart-bar value AND Support touched AND close - Short EMA <= ATR * late-add maximum distance AND volume ratio <= breakout volume multiplier AND No recent risk.

Calculation: Reclaim add = buy stage is at least the first stage AND buy stage < maximum buy stages AND reclaim-add cooldown elapsed since the last buy AND (Core confirmed OR close > Core upper) AND Add pullback permitted AND Add progress AND (Reclaim buy condition OR Bullish OB reclaim) AND Bullish OBV risk clear AND NOT Momentum exhausted.
Calculation: Pullback add = buy stage is at least the first stage AND buy stage < maximum buy stages AND stage cooldown elapsed since the last buy AND Core confirmed AND Add pullback permitted AND Add progress AND (Pullback buy condition OR Bullish OB reclaim) AND Bullish OBV risk clear AND NOT Momentum exhausted.
Calculation: Momentum take-profit raw = momentum take-profit enabled AND SuperTrend direction is bullish AND buy stage > 0 AND (highest(high, momentum swing-high lookback) - close) / ATR >= momentum drop parameter AND (close - Last buy price) / ATR >= minimum momentum profit parameter AND (DI- > DI+ OR Early-sell DI+ falling) AND close > Core upper.

Calculation: Hard stop raw = hard stop enabled AND Active long cycle AND buy stage > 0 AND close <= Average entry price - ATR * hard-stop distance parameter.
Calculation: Trailing stop raw = trailing stop enabled AND Active long cycle AND buy stage > 0 AND (Peak high - Average entry price) / ATR >= trailing activation parameter AND close <= Peak high - ATR * trailing distance parameter AND (close < Short EMA OR DI- > DI+ OR Recent bearish OBV divergence).

Calculation: Trend exit raw = SuperTrend direction is bearish OR (higher-timeframe filter enabled AND NOT Higher-timeframe bullish).
