Calculation: Analysis RSI = RSI(close, RSI lookback) computed on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars, using the forming T1 bar in real time and the latest completed T1 bar historically
Calculation: T2 RSI = RSI(close, RSI lookback) computed on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars, using the forming T2 bar in real time and the latest completed T2 bar historically
Calculation: Stochastic RSI close = SMA(Stochastic(Analysis RSI, Analysis RSI, Analysis RSI, stochastic lookback), smoothing lookback) computed on chart bars
Calculation: Chart Stochastic RSI high = SMA(Stochastic(RSI(high, RSI lookback), same series for source, high, and low, stochastic lookback), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Chart Stochastic RSI low = SMA(Stochastic(RSI(low, RSI lookback), same series for source, high, and low, stochastic lookback), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Oscillator upper = Chart Stochastic RSI high if Chart Stochastic RSI high > Stochastic RSI close, otherwise the prior Stochastic RSI close
Calculation: Oscillator lower = min(Chart Stochastic RSI low, Chart Stochastic RSI high) if that minimum < Stochastic RSI close, otherwise the prior Stochastic RSI close
Calculation: Oscillator direction = bullish if Oscillator upper > its prior value; otherwise bearish if Oscillator lower < its prior value; otherwise bearish if Oscillator upper < its prior value; otherwise bullish if Oscillator lower > its prior value; otherwise neutral
