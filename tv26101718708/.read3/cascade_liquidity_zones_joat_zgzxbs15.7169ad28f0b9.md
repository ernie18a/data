Calculation: Impulse score at bar b = clamp(score floor, score ceiling, body weight × (high[b] − low[b] > 0 ? abs(close[b] − open[b]) / (high[b] − low[b]) : 0) + volume weight × min(volume ratio, volume-ratio cap) / volume-ratio cap + range weight × min(ATR ratio, ATR-ratio cap) / ATR-ratio cap), where volume ratio = volume[b] / SMA(volume, score-volume lookback)[b] if that average is positive, otherwise 1, and ATR ratio = (high[b] − low[b]) / ATR(ATR lookback)[b] if ATR is positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Demand zone = a zone created on a confirmed PivotLow(low, zone pivot lookback, zone pivot lookback), with bottom = pivot low and top = pivot low + ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-width parameter, using ATR on the confirmation bar; create only if the pivot-bar impulse score is positive and cluster admission passes. Admit if no active demand-zone midpoint is within ATR(ATR lookback) × separation parameter of the candidate midpoint; otherwise compare only the newest active matching zone, rejecting the candidate if that zone's score is at least the candidate's, or replacing it otherwise. Keep at most the maximum-zones parameter, removing the oldest on overflow. A created zone is fresh until swept.
Calculation: Supply zone = a zone created on a confirmed PivotHigh(high, zone pivot lookback, zone pivot lookback), with top = pivot high and bottom = pivot high − ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-width parameter, using ATR on the confirmation bar; create only if the pivot-bar impulse score is positive and cluster admission passes. Admit if no active supply-zone midpoint is within ATR(ATR lookback) × separation parameter of the candidate midpoint; otherwise compare only the newest active matching zone, rejecting the candidate if that zone's score is at least the candidate's, or replacing it otherwise. Keep at most the maximum-zones parameter, removing the oldest on overflow. A created zone is fresh until swept.
Calculation: Sweep stop at entry = for a long sweep, the associated demand-zone bottom − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer parameter in zone mode, otherwise the entry-bar low − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer parameter; for a short sweep, the associated supply-zone top + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer parameter in zone mode, otherwise the entry-bar high + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Sweep target at entry = entry price + abs(entry price − sweep stop) × reward-risk parameter for a long sweep; entry price − abs(entry price − sweep stop) × reward-risk parameter for a short sweep.
Long: (sweep mode selected AND bar confirmed AND a fresh demand zone exists AND low < zone bottom AND (zone bottom > 0 ? 100 × (zone bottom − low) / zone bottom : 0) ≥ minimum penetration parameter AND close > zone bottom AND (wick filter disabled OR (high > low ? 100 × (close − low) / (high − low) : 0) ≥ minimum rejection parameter) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ SMA(volume, sweep-volume lookback) × sweep-volume multiple) AND current bar index ≥ prior long signal bar index + sweep cooldown) OR (failure-entry mode selected AND bar confirmed AND a latest confirmed PivotLow(low, failure pivot lookback, failure pivot lookback) exists AND low < pivot low AND close > pivot low AND low[1] ≥ pivot low AND pivot low − low ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × failure-wick ATR parameter AND (high > low ? 100 × (close − low) / (high − low) : 0) ≥ failure rejection parameter AND (failure volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ SMA(volume, sweep-volume lookback) × failure-volume multiple) AND current bar index ≥ prior long signal bar index + failure cooldown).
Short: (sweep mode selected AND bar confirmed AND a fresh supply zone exists AND high > zone top AND (zone top > 0 ? 100 × (high − zone top) / zone top : 0) ≥ minimum penetration parameter AND close < zone top AND (wick filter disabled OR (high > low ? 100 × (high − close) / (high − low) : 0) ≥ minimum rejection parameter) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ SMA(volume, sweep-volume lookback) × sweep-volume multiple) AND current bar index ≥ prior short signal bar index + sweep cooldown) OR (failure-entry mode selected AND bar confirmed AND a latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, failure pivot lookback, failure pivot lookback) exists AND high > pivot high AND close < pivot high AND high[1] ≤ pivot high AND high − pivot high ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × failure-wick ATR parameter AND (high > low ? 100 × (high − close) / (high − low) : 0) ≥ failure rejection parameter AND (failure volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ SMA(volume, sweep-volume lookback) × failure-volume multiple) AND current bar index ≥ prior short signal bar index + failure cooldown).
Exit: Long, a sweep-mode simulated long position is already open AND (high ≥ its stored sweep target OR low ≤ its stored sweep stop).
Exit: Short, a sweep-mode simulated short position is already open AND (low ≤ its stored sweep target OR high ≥ its stored sweep stop).
