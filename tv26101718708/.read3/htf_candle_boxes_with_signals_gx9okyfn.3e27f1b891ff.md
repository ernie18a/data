Calculation: Opening ATR = ATR(lookback, T2 bars; use the value mapped to the chart bar when T1 opens, last completed on historical bars and potentially forming in realtime)
Calculation: Volume Oscillator = percentage Volume Oscillator(volume, shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback) on chart bars
Long: During a T1 candle, its low reaches at least Opening ATR × wick threshold below its open before or on the first chart-bar close inside the zone from T1 open − Opening ATR × zone multiplier to T1 open + Opening ATR × zone multiplier; a later confirmed chart bar closes above the zone after the previous chart bar closed inside it; the chart-bar time is at least the buffer duration after T1 opens and at least the cooldown duration after the prior signal; if the volume filter is selected, Volume Oscillator is at least its threshold; if the last-candles filter is selected, all specified chart bars before the first zone-touch close have close below open
Short: During a T1 candle, its high reaches at least Opening ATR × wick threshold above its open before or on the first chart-bar close inside the zone from T1 open − Opening ATR × zone multiplier to T1 open + Opening ATR × zone multiplier; a later confirmed chart bar closes below the zone after the previous chart bar closed inside it; the chart-bar time is at least the buffer duration after T1 opens and at least the cooldown duration after the prior signal; if the volume filter is selected, Volume Oscillator is at least its threshold; if the last-candles filter is selected, all specified chart bars before the first zone-touch close have close above open
