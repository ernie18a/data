Calculation: Adaptive RSI levels = overbought EMA(clamp(SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), adaptive lookback) + StDev(RSI(close, RSI lookback), adaptive lookback) × ((adaptive percentile − percentile midpoint) / percentile scale), overbought lower bound, overbought upper bound), adaptive smoothing lookback); oversold EMA(clamp(SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), adaptive lookback) − StDev(RSI(close, RSI lookback), adaptive lookback) × ((adaptive percentile − percentile midpoint) / percentile scale), oversold lower bound, oversold upper bound), adaptive smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Selected RSI levels = adaptive RSI levels in Adaptive mode; custom overbought and oversold parameters in Custom mode; preset level pairs in the selected preset; Auto selects the T3 intraday preset when the chart timeframe is intraday T3, otherwise the preset for the instrument's asset class (crypto, forex, or other)
Calculation: ADX-adjusted Bollinger bands = SMA(close, band lookback) ± band deviation × StDev(close, band lookback) × (1 + ADX(ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) / ADX normalization scale × ADX influence)
Calculation: ADX zone thresholds = upper: SMA(upper ADX-adjusted Bollinger band, zone smoothing lookback) + zone offset × (SMA(upper ADX-adjusted Bollinger band, zone smoothing lookback) − SMA(lower ADX-adjusted Bollinger band, zone smoothing lookback)); lower: SMA(lower ADX-adjusted Bollinger band, zone smoothing lookback) − zone offset × (SMA(upper ADX-adjusted Bollinger band, zone smoothing lookback) − SMA(lower ADX-adjusted Bollinger band, zone smoothing lookback))
Calculation: Higher-timeframe RSI values = RSI(close, RSI lookback) calculated on T1 and T2 bars and mapped to chart bars using the last completed timeframe bar historically and the still-forming timeframe bar in real time
Long: close crosses below the lower ADX zone threshold AND (no prior ADX long zone-entry signal OR current bar index − prior ADX long zone-entry bar index ≥ signal cooldown)
Short: close crosses above the upper ADX zone threshold AND (no prior ADX short zone-entry signal OR current bar index − prior ADX short zone-entry bar index ≥ signal cooldown)
Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above the selected oversold level AND (close[1] < lower ADX zone threshold[1] OR RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] < selected oversold level[1]) AND (ADX confirmation disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) > strong ADX threshold) AND (MTF confirmation disabled OR T1 RSI < selected oversold level AND T2 RSI < selected oversold level)
Short: RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below the selected overbought level AND (close[1] > upper ADX zone threshold[1] OR RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] > selected overbought level[1]) AND (ADX confirmation disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, ADX smoothing) > strong ADX threshold) AND (MTF confirmation disabled OR T1 RSI > selected overbought level AND T2 RSI > selected overbought level)
Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) < selected oversold level AND T1 RSI < selected oversold level AND T2 RSI < selected oversold level
Short: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > selected overbought level AND T1 RSI > selected overbought level AND T2 RSI > selected overbought level
