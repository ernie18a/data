Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), instrument minimum tick size).
Calculation: An active recovery plan starts on a qualifying update when a new plan is allowed and either the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows are both higher, with close below the latest pivot low minus safe ATR times max(minimum damage depth, sensitivity damage buffer), or both pivot highs and lows are lower, with close above the latest pivot high plus safe ATR times max(minimum damage depth, sensitivity damage buffer). Use PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) and PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback); store the broken pivot as the rail and that bar’s low or high as the damage extreme. A new plan is allowed when no plan is active, the prior plan failed, the prior plan expired and its recovery-window wait has elapsed, or its age exceeds the recovery window times the plan reset multiplier. Close-confirmed mode qualifies only confirmed bars; live-updating mode also qualifies the forming bar. The latest plan’s side remains active until a new plan starts.
Calculation: Recovery has occurred when, after the active plan starts and before it expires or fails, a qualifying close is above the bullish plan’s rail plus safe ATR times the recovery buffer, or below the bearish plan’s rail minus safe ATR times the recovery buffer; this status persists until the plan ends.
Long: the active recovery plan is bullish AND recovery has not failed
Short: the active recovery plan is bearish AND recovery has not failed
Exit: long, recovery has occurred AND recovery has not previously failed AND close < the bullish plan’s damage-bar low − safe ATR × risk-edge distance
Exit: short, recovery has occurred AND recovery has not previously failed AND close > the bearish plan’s damage-bar high + safe ATR × risk-edge distance
