Calculation: opening-range high = highest(high) among chart bars in the configured opening-range window of each session
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest(low) among chart bars in the configured opening-range window of each session
Calculation: long risk distance = close - (configured range stop ? opening-range low : close - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier)
Calculation: short risk distance = (configured range stop ? opening-range high : close + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier) - close
Long: range is locked AND close[1] < opening-range low AND close > opening-range low
Long: datetime is within the configured session AND range is locked AND close > opening-range high AND (range-size filter is disabled OR (ATR(ATR lookback) is defined AND opening-range high - opening-range low >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum range multiple AND opening-range high - opening-range low <= ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum range multiple)) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiple) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > session VWAP(HLC3)) AND (false-breakout confirmation is disabled OR the number of chart bars since the most recent close at or below the opening-range high >= confirmation bars) AND trades taken this session day < daily trade limit
Short: range is locked AND close[1] > opening-range high AND close < opening-range high
Exit: long, high >= close + long risk distance * partial-profit multiple AND no earlier partial exit occurred during this position
Exit: long, low <= (breakeven stop enabled AND a partial exit has occurred ? close of the first chart bar with a long position : (configured range stop ? opening-range low : close - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier)) OR high >= close + long risk distance * target multiple OR datetime is outside the configured session AND the previous chart bar's datetime was within it
Exit: short, low <= close - short risk distance * partial-profit multiple AND no earlier partial exit occurred during this position
Exit: short, high >= (breakeven stop enabled AND a partial exit has occurred ? close of the first chart bar with a short position : (configured range stop ? opening-range high : close + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR stop multiplier)) OR low <= close - short risk distance * target multiple OR datetime is outside the configured session AND the previous chart bar's datetime was within it
