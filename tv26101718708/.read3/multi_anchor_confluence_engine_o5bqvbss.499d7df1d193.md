Calculation: relative-strength ratio = 100 + 10 × (relative value − SMA(relative value, lookback)) / StDev(relative value, lookback), or 100 if StDev is unavailable or zero; relative value is the weighted mean of 100 × each enabled market’s selected OHLC-derived price divided by each reference series’ separately selected OHLC-derived value, across the equity-index level, dollar-index value, 10-year Treasury yield, and 5-year Treasury yield, using a separate weight parameter for each reference series on chart bars.
Long: the number of enabled market series with relative-strength ratio ≥ 100 is at least the number whose relative-strength ratio is not ≥ 100
Short: the number of enabled market series with relative-strength ratio ≥ 100 is less than the number whose relative-strength ratio is not ≥ 100
