Calculation: VWAP deviation oscillator = optional EMA or Ultimate Smoother of classic or robust MAD Z-score of (price source − session-anchored VWAP), using either the normalization lookback or the median of Z-scores at max(5, floor(half the lookback)), the lookback, and twice the lookback; use the oscillator-smoothing lookback when greater than one; equals zero when VWAP is unavailable
Calculation: High-pivot oscillator value = highest(VWAP deviation oscillator, 2 × price-pivot length + 1) at each confirmed high-source pivot
Calculation: Low-pivot oscillator value = lowest(VWAP deviation oscillator, 2 × price-pivot length + 1) at each confirmed low-source pivot
Calculation: Oscillator-gap threshold = minimum-gap multiplier × standard deviation(VWAP deviation oscillator, oscillator-gap lookback)
Long: VWAP deviation oscillator ≤ −overbought sigma OR a confirmed pivot low of the VWAP deviation oscillator ≤ −extreme-zone sigma OR (a confirmed low-source pivot is lower than the most recent prior confirmed low-source pivot, the current low-pivot oscillator value is greater than the prior value by at least the oscillator-gap threshold, the pivots are no more than the maximum pivot separation apart, and the current value ≤ −overbought sigma) OR (a confirmed low-source pivot is higher than the most recent prior confirmed low-source pivot, the current low-pivot oscillator value is lower than the prior value by at least the oscillator-gap threshold, and the pivots are no more than the maximum pivot separation apart)
Short: VWAP deviation oscillator ≥ overbought sigma OR a confirmed pivot high of the VWAP deviation oscillator ≥ extreme-zone sigma OR (a confirmed high-source pivot is higher than the most recent prior confirmed high-source pivot, the current high-pivot oscillator value is lower than the prior value by at least the oscillator-gap threshold, the pivots are no more than the maximum pivot separation apart, and the current value ≥ overbought sigma) OR (a confirmed high-source pivot is lower than the most recent prior confirmed high-source pivot, the current high-pivot oscillator value is greater than the prior value by at least the oscillator-gap threshold, and the pivots are no more than the maximum pivot separation apart)
