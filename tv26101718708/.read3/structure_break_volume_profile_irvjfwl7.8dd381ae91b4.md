Calculation: CHoCH POC = midpoint of the lowest-price row with greatest total volume in an equal-row profile with a tunable row count, spanning the prior structure’s active pivot low to pivot high and chart bars from the earlier active pivot bar through the bar before CHoCH; allocate each bar’s volume among rows in proportion to each row’s overlap with its high-low range clipped to the profile, or wholly to the close’s row when there is no positive overlap and the close is within the profile; undefined if the price span or total allocated volume is zero
Long: the selected break price (close or high) > the latest confirmed pivot high(high, symmetric pivot lookback) that has not previously been broken
Short: the selected break price (close or low) < the latest confirmed pivot low(low, symmetric pivot lookback) that has not previously been broken
Long: the latest CHoCH was bullish AND CHoCH POC exists AND time > latest CHoCH time AND low <= CHoCH POC <= high AND the prior bar did not satisfy the after-CHoCH POC-touch condition
Short: the latest CHoCH was bearish AND CHoCH POC exists AND time > latest CHoCH time AND low <= CHoCH POC <= high AND the prior bar did not satisfy the after-CHoCH POC-touch condition
