Calculation: T1 order-block ranges = on a bullish shift, the high and low of the nearest bearish T1 candle among the preceding order-block-search lookback bars; on a bearish shift, the high and low of the nearest bullish T1 candle among those bars; bullish shift = close on T1 crosses above highest(high[1], shift lookback), bearish shift = close crosses below lowest(low[1], same shift lookback); evaluate shifts on the current/forming T1 bar and retain the latest range of each direction.
Long: A qualifying bullish grab occurs when low < an active confirmed PivotLow(pivot lookback, pivot lookback) level and close >= that level; the level has not expired, been invalidated by a close below it, or reached the maximum-grabs-per-level limit, and (HTF order-block filter disabled OR the grab-bar low is within the latest bullish T1 order-block range). AND a bullish CSD for that grab is confirmed by close > the grab-bar high within the CSD-confirmation lookback. AND (OTE-only disabled AND the current bar is that CSD-confirmation bar OR OTE-only enabled AND breaker display enabled AND a post-grab bullish FVG overlaps the bullish breaker from the bar before the grab AND a generated bullish zone is first touched at or after the CSD plus the touch-wait bars AND current close > highest(high from the CSD-confirmation bar through that zone's first-touch bar)); a bullish FVG has low > high two bars before it, and overlaps the breaker whose preceding-to-grab bar closes above its open when the FVG range overlaps that bar's low-high range; a generated zone is the breaker zone or, when FVG display is enabled, a post-grab FVG zone; bullish zone touch = low <= zone top.
Short: A qualifying bearish grab occurs when high > an active confirmed PivotHigh(pivot lookback, pivot lookback) level and close <= that level; the level has not expired, been invalidated by a close above it, or reached the maximum-grabs-per-level limit, and (HTF order-block filter disabled OR the grab-bar high is within the latest bearish T1 order-block range). AND a bearish CSD for that grab is confirmed by close < the grab-bar low within the CSD-confirmation lookback. AND (OTE-only disabled AND the current bar is that CSD-confirmation bar OR OTE-only enabled AND breaker display enabled AND a post-grab bearish FVG overlaps the bearish breaker from the bar before the grab AND a generated bearish zone is first touched at or after the CSD plus the touch-wait bars AND current close < lowest(low from the CSD-confirmation bar through that zone's first-touch bar)); a bearish FVG has high < low two bars before it, and overlaps the breaker whose preceding-to-grab bar closes below its open when the FVG range overlaps that bar's low-high range; a generated zone is the breaker zone or, when FVG display is enabled, a post-grab FVG zone; bearish zone touch = high >= zone bottom.
