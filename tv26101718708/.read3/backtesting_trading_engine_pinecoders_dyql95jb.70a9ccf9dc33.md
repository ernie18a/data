Calculation: Bullish Ichimoku filter = (Tenkan > Kijun AND Senkou A > Senkou B AND close > max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B)) OR ((Tenkan > Kijun AND Kijun > max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B) AND Tenkan > max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B) AND close > max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B)) AND rising(Kijun, 1) AND rising(Tenkan, 1)); Tenkan = Donchian midpoint(Tenkan lookback), Kijun = Donchian midpoint(Kijun lookback), Senkou A = average(Tenkan, Kijun), Senkou B = Donchian midpoint(Senkou B lookback), displaced values are those from the displacement lookback ago
Calculation: Bearish Ichimoku filter = (Tenkan < Kijun AND Senkou A < Senkou B AND close < max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B)) OR ((Tenkan < Kijun AND Kijun < max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B) AND Tenkan < max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B) AND close < max(displaced Senkou A, displaced Senkou B)) AND falling(Kijun, 1) AND falling(Tenkan, 1)); Tenkan = Donchian midpoint(Tenkan lookback), Kijun = Donchian midpoint(Kijun lookback), Senkou A = average(Tenkan, Kijun), Senkou B = Donchian midpoint(Senkou B lookback), displaced values are those from the displacement lookback ago
Calculation: Bullish MA Squeeze state = abs(EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) - EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) >= ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR threshold AND EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)
Calculation: Bearish MA Squeeze state = abs(EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) - EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)) >= ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR threshold AND EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) < EMA(close, slow EMA lookback)
Calculation: Long entry stop = round-to-tick(the selected stop: previous external indicator value when positive and not 1, 2, or 3; min(open, close) - ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiple; min(open, close) × (1 - stop percentage / 100); or max(min(open, close) - fixed stop value, 0))
Calculation: Short entry stop = round-to-tick(the selected stop: negative previous external indicator value made positive when negative and not -1, -2, or -3; max(open, close) + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiple; max(open, close) × (1 + stop percentage / 100); or max(open, close) + fixed stop value)
Calculation: X = abs(entry price - selected entry stop)
Calculation: Long in-trade stop candidate = the selected value among favorable high since entry - X × trailing multiple; favorable high since entry × (1 - trailing percentage / 100); favorable high since entry - fixed trailing value; Donchian midpoint(lookback); average of the prior two rounded min(open, close) values - ATR(shared stop lookback) × shared stop multiple; Chandelier Exit long stop(shared stop lookback, shared stop multiple); Volatility Stop(shared stop lookback, shared stop multiple) when it is in an uptrend, otherwise the prior stop; or low[1]
Calculation: Short in-trade stop candidate = the selected value among favorable low since entry + X × trailing multiple; favorable low since entry × (1 + trailing percentage / 100); favorable low since entry + fixed trailing value; Donchian midpoint(lookback); average of the prior two rounded max(open, close) values + ATR(shared stop lookback) × shared stop multiple; Chandelier Exit short stop(shared stop lookback, shared stop multiple); Volatility Stop(shared stop lookback, shared stop multiple) when it is in a downtrend, otherwise the prior stop; or high[1]
Calculation: Active in-trade stop = selected entry stop on the entry bar; afterward, keep the prior stop until the selected kick condition passes, then use the tighter of the prior stop and the selected candidate. Long kick conditions are candidate > entry stop; candidate > max(round-to-tick(entry price + X × kick multiple), entry stop); candidate > max(round-to-tick(entry price × (1 + kick percentage / 100)), entry stop); or candidate > max(round-to-tick(entry price + kick value), entry stop). Short kick conditions use < and the corresponding entry price minus X multiple, percentage, or value, with min(..., entry stop). If the long candidate is above high or the short candidate is below low, retain the prior stop. Round the resulting stop to tick size and floor it at zero.
