Calculation: High Low Index = SMA(the percentage of configured instruments making new highs among those making new highs or new lows, over the smoothing lookback); score each instrument +1 when its current high exceeds the prior chart bar's highest high over the lookback and -1 when its current low is below the prior chart bar's lowest low when the source is High/Low, or compare current close with both prior extremes when the source is Close; score 0 otherwise, carry forward the prior percentage when no instrument makes a new high or low, and use the initial percentage parameter at initialization.
Calculation: Raw LR slope = LinReg(close, regression lookback, offset) - its value on the prior chart bar.
Calculation: Smoothed LR slope = EMA(raw LR slope, slope smoothing lookback).
Calculation: LR slope signal = SMA(smoothed LR slope, signal lookback).
Calculation: Long protective stop = seed at prior low - ATR(true range, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier; before trailing activation, use the minimum of the stop value bars-since-entry ago and prior low, adjusted upward by (entry price - that minimum) * stop adjustment percentage; after trailing activation, use the long trailing stop.
Calculation: Short protective stop = seed at prior high + ATR(true range, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier; before trailing activation, use the maximum of the stop value bars-since-entry ago and prior high, adjusted downward by (that maximum - entry price) * stop adjustment percentage; after trailing activation, use the short trailing stop.
Calculation: Long target = prior high + abs(current high - long protective stop) while positioned.
Calculation: Short target = prior low - abs(short protective stop - current low) while positioned.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = max(prior long protective stop, current low - ATR(true range, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier).
Calculation: Short trailing stop = min(prior short protective stop, current high + ATR(true range, ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier).
Long: Flat AND within the configured entry date range and weekday session AND (entry-pause filter disabled OR outside its configured interval) AND MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, source MA type, signal MA type) > its prior chart-bar value AND High Low Index > its prior chart-bar value AND percentage-scaled abs(smoothed LR slope) > trend threshold AND raw LR slope > LR slope signal AND raw LR slope > 0 AND High Low Index < long entry cutoff.
Short: Flat AND within the configured entry date range and weekday session AND (entry-pause filter disabled OR outside its configured interval) AND MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, source MA type, signal MA type) < its prior chart-bar value AND High Low Index < its prior chart-bar value AND percentage-scaled abs(smoothed LR slope) > trend threshold AND raw LR slope < LR slope signal AND raw LR slope < 0 AND High Low Index > short entry cutoff.
Exit: Long, early-exit option enabled AND close > entry price AND ((prior-bar High Low Index > long overbought exit threshold AND current High Low Index < that threshold) OR (MACD histogram < its prior chart-bar value AND High Low Index < its prior chart-bar value AND percentage-scaled abs(smoothed LR slope) > trend threshold AND raw LR slope < LR slope signal AND raw LR slope < 0 AND High Low Index > short entry cutoff)).
Exit: Short, early-exit option enabled AND close < entry price AND ((prior-bar High Low Index < short oversold exit threshold AND current High Low Index > that threshold) OR (MACD histogram > its prior chart-bar value AND High Low Index > its prior chart-bar value AND percentage-scaled abs(smoothed LR slope) > trend threshold AND raw LR slope > LR slope signal AND raw LR slope > 0 AND High Low Index < long entry cutoff)).
Exit: Long, trailing option disabled AND within the configured stop-order date range and session AND (low <= long protective stop OR high >= long target).
Exit: Short, trailing option disabled AND within the configured stop-order date range and session AND (high >= short protective stop OR low <= short target).
Exit: Long, trailing option enabled AND trailing stop not yet activated AND within the configured stop-order date range and session AND low <= long protective stop.
Exit: Short, trailing option enabled AND trailing stop not yet activated AND within the configured stop-order date range and session AND high >= short protective stop.
Exit: Long, trailing option enabled AND within the configured trailing-order date range and session AND (high > long target OR trailing stop was active on the prior chart bar) AND low <= long trailing stop.
Exit: Short, trailing option enabled AND within the configured trailing-order date range and session AND (low < short target OR trailing stop was active on the prior chart bar) AND high >= short trailing stop.

Calculation: High-low index = SMA of the record-high percentage over P2 bars, using selected constituent data on T1; a constituent breaks high when its high or close exceeds its prior P1-bar highest high, and breaks low when its low or close falls below its prior P1-bar lowest low, with a high break taking precedence; record-high percentage is positive breaks divided by all positive or negative breaks, carried forward when there are none and initialized at P3; the selected source is P4.
Calculation: Linear regression slope = current linear regression of close over P5 bars minus its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Smoothed linear regression slope = EMA of linear regression slope over P6 bars.
Calculation: Linear regression slope signal = SMA of smoothed linear regression slope over P7 bars.
