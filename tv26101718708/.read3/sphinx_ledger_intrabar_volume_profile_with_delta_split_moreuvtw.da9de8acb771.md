Calculation: Developing session volume profile = Recompute on chart bars from current-session T1 intrabars, including the still-forming intrabar; divide each intrabar’s volume equally among equal-width bins touched by its low-to-high range across the current session’s low-to-high range (profile-row-count parameter). POC is the center of the highest-volume bin, with ties going to the lowest-price bin. Expand the value area from POC by adding the higher-volume adjacent bin until it reaches the value-area-percentage parameter; ties go to the upper bin. VAH and VAL are the upper and lower boundaries of the included bins.
Long: close crosses above developing POC OR close crosses above developing VAH OR close crosses above developing VAL
Short: close crosses below developing POC OR close crosses below developing VAH OR close crosses below developing VAL
