Calculation: Structure-break direction = bullish when a later source bar closes above the latest unused confirmed PivotHigh(high, scale lookback, scale lookback) without also closing below the latest unused confirmed PivotLow(low, scale lookback, scale lookback); bearish when it closes below that PivotLow without also closing above that PivotHigh; otherwise none. Consume each broken pivot once until a newer pivot replaces it. Keep separate state and lookback parameters for Local and Major scales, with Local shorter than Major. Calculate independently on chart bars and T1/T2 bars; use only the last completed T1/T2 bar and consume it at the first following chart-bar close.
Calculation: Origin relative volume = origin candle volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback, source timeframe, prior completed bar), using only candles before the origin candle.
Calculation: Minimum FVG width = max(minimum gap tick count × minimum price increment, gap ATR multiple × ATR(source timeframe, ATR lookback)); omit the ATR term when its filter is disabled.
Calculation: Level-oriented close = (close − level) × orientation, where orientation is positive for a high level and negative for a low level.
Calculation: Previous level-oriented close = (previous completed close of the same reaction lane − level) × the level orientation.
Calculation: Sweep wick penetration = high − level for a high level, or level − low for a low level.
Calculation: Level touch = high >= level − touch band AND low <= level + touch band.
