Calculation: Price-row ownership = split each bar's volume by its close position within its high-low range, then distribute each side across equal-width rows spanning the rolling highest high to lowest low in proportion to each bar's overlap with each row; use the row-count and lookback parameters
Calculation: Buyer shelf = highest row below close where buyer volume divided by total row volume meets the dominance threshold; seller shelf = lowest row above close where seller volume divided by total row volume meets the dominance threshold
Calculation: Volume center = midpoint of the first price row where cumulative total row volume reaches half of total volume; use the range midpoint if no row qualifies
Calculation: Normalized fuel = normalize volume × absolute change in close over its rolling maximum and minimum; use zero if the maximum is not greater than the minimum
Calculation: Defense win rate = prior target wins divided by prior exited defenses for the same side and ownership tier; classify tiers by the lower and upper dominance cutoffs, and count a stop as the outcome when stop and target are both hit on one bar
Calculation: Equal-high/low pool = average of confirmed pivot highs/lows clustered within ATR × pool tolerance, using the pivot-left, pivot-right, recent-pivot-count, minimum-touch, and pools-per-side parameters
Calculation: Long stop = buyer shelf lower edge − ATR × stop buffer; if not below entry price, use entry price − ATR × fallback stop multiple
Calculation: Short stop = seller shelf upper edge + ATR × stop buffer; if not above entry price, use entry price + ATR × fallback stop multiple
Calculation: Long target = qualifying volume center or rolling range high, according to target model, if above entry by at least ATR × minimum target distance and no more than long risk × maximum R; otherwise entry price + long risk × reward-to-risk multiple
Calculation: Short target = qualifying volume center or rolling range low, according to target model, if below entry by at least ATR × minimum target distance and no more than short risk × maximum R; otherwise entry price − short risk × reward-to-risk multiple
Long: volume center is in the lower half of the range and net buyer mass percentage exceeds the bullish threshold
Short: volume center is in the upper half of the range and net buyer mass percentage is below the bearish threshold
Long: a confirmed low-pool pivot sweep has low below a resting equal-low pool and close above it
Short: a confirmed high-pool pivot sweep has high above a resting equal-high pool and close below it
Long: relative volume, volume divided by SMA(volume, common lookback), is at least the relative-volume threshold AND relative range, (high − low) divided by SMA(high − low, common lookback), is at most the relative-range ceiling AND (close − low) / (high − low) is at least the midpoint; use the midpoint when high equals low
Short: relative volume, volume divided by SMA(volume, common lookback), is at least the relative-volume threshold AND relative range, (high − low) divided by SMA(high − low, common lookback), is at most the relative-range ceiling AND (close − low) / (high − low) is below the midpoint; use the midpoint when high equals low
Long: on a confirmed bar, the selected buyer shelf is touched by low and close finishes above its top edge AND ATR is available AND prior exited defenses for this side and ownership tier are fewer than the minimum samples or their win rate meets the minimum probability AND balance alignment is disabled or volume center is in the lower half of the range AND minimum fuel is disabled or normalized fuel meets it AND the last signal was at least the cooldown bars ago or there was no prior signal AND the selected row differs from the last fired buyer row or that row was cleared after low rose above its top edge by ATR
Short: on a confirmed bar, the selected seller shelf is touched by high and close finishes below its bottom edge AND ATR is available AND prior exited defenses for this side and ownership tier are fewer than the minimum samples or their win rate meets the minimum probability AND balance alignment is disabled or volume center is in the upper half of the range AND minimum fuel is disabled or normalized fuel meets it AND the last signal was at least the cooldown bars ago or there was no prior signal AND the selected row differs from the last fired seller row or that row was cleared after high fell below its bottom edge by ATR AND no long signal occurs on the same bar
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low is at or below the long stop OR high is at or above the long target
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high is at or above the short stop OR low is at or below the short target
