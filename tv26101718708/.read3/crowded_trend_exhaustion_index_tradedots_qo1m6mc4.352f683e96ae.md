Calculation: Crowding score = clamp(0, 100, the weighted average using eight separate weights of: clamp(25 × abs(close − EMA(close, trend EMA lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback), 0, 100), or 0 when ATR is 0; clamp(25 × abs(close − anchored VWAP(hlc3, anchor period)) / ATR(ATR lookback), 0, 100), or 0 when ATR is 0 or VWAP is unavailable; clamp(10 × streak, 0, 100), where streak adds 1 when close rises and the trend EMA rises or close falls and the trend EMA falls, otherwise subtracts 10, floored at 0; RSI percentile over the percentile lookback when the trend EMA rises, 100 minus that percentile when it falls, or 50 when flat; volume percentile over the percentile lookback scored 100 at or above 90, 60 at or above 75, 30 at or above 50, otherwise 0; ATR percentile over the percentile lookback, defaulting to 50 if unavailable; failed-wick score; and divergence score. The trend EMA rises or falls when its current value is respectively above or below its prior value. Failed-wick score uses a counter set to 5 when the trend EMA rises and the upper wick exceeds twice the body, or when it falls and the lower wick exceeds twice the body; otherwise the counter decreases by 1 to a floor of 0. Its score is 0 at counter 0, otherwise min(100, 25 × counter). Divergence score uses a counter with the same update and scoring: set it to 5 when the trend EMA rises and high equals its highest value over the divergence lookback while RSI is not at its highest over that lookback, or when the trend EMA falls and low equals its lowest over that lookback while RSI is not at its lowest; otherwise decrease it by 1 to a floor of 0. Divide the weighted sum by max(sum of the eight weights, 0.01); all calculations use confirmed chart bars.
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar the trend EMA rises AND the Crowding score crosses above the crowded threshold OR the climax risk threshold
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar the trend EMA falls AND the Crowding score crosses above the crowded threshold OR the climax risk threshold
