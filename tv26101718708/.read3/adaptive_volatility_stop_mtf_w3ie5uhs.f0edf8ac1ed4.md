Calculation: Adaptive volatility stop = on each engine’s bars, compute efficiency ratio as EMA(abs(source − source[n]) / sum(abs(source − source[1]), n), smoothing lookback), normalize it to its rolling minimum and maximum over the normalization lookback (use the neutral midpoint until warmed up), and form upper and lower thresholds from its rolling mean ± max(threshold offset, rolling standard deviation × volatility fraction), clamped to [0,1]. Compute adaptive alpha as (ER × (2/(fast bound+1) − 2/(slow bound+1)) + 2/(slow bound+1))²; clamp its reciprocal between the configured lower and upper adaptive ATR-length bounds. Map normalized ER linearly from the slow multiplier at the lower threshold, through the midpoint multiplier at the mean, to the fast multiplier at the upper threshold. The mode selects adaptive length, adaptive multiplier, both, or neither; otherwise use the base ATR length and multiplier. ATR is RMA(TrueRange(OHLC), selected length). The path factor is the clamp of sqrt(EMA(return², fast path window)/EMA(return², slow path window)) + max(−EMA(return, fast path window)/ATR,0) × downside sensitivity + max(EMA(return, fast path window)/ATR,0) × upside sensitivity; blend it with one by the path weight when path scaling is enabled, otherwise use one. Stop distance is selected multiplier × ATR × blended factor. Initialize bullish with source − distance; while bullish, trail at max(previous stop, source − distance) and flip bearish when source < stop, setting stop to source + distance. While bearish, trail at min(previous stop, source + distance) and flip bullish when source > stop, setting stop to source − distance. ER lookbacks may be scaled inversely to chart-bar duration relative to reference timeframe T4, with configured base lengths and minimums; the resulting lengths are shared by all engines. The chart engine runs on chart bars; T1, T2, and T3 engines run on their aggregated bars and are sampled on chart bars from the current higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: MTF score = sum of each chart, T1, T2, and T3 adaptive-stop direction as +1 when bullish or −1 when bearish, multiplied by its separate timeframe weight, with higher-timeframe weights larger.
Calculation: Hawkes score lift = zero when clustering detection is disabled or intensity/baseline ratio ≤ warning multiple; otherwise max lift × min(1,(ratio − warning multiple)/lift span). A shock occurs when abs(log(close/close[1])) exceeds shock multiple × standard deviation of log returns over the short volatility window. Baseline is SMA(shock indicator, baseline window); intensity starts at zero and updates as previous intensity × exp(−decay) + excitation when shocked. Ratio is intensity/baseline when baseline > 0, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Signal eligible = abs(MTF score) > min(score-floor cap, enabled choppy cutoff + enabled Hawkes score lift), where a disabled choppy filter contributes zero.
Calculation: Volume passes = volume filter disabled OR positive-volume bar fraction below the reliability threshold OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback).
Calculation: MTF score momentum = MTF score − MTF score[n chart bars ago], with n optionally scaled inversely to chart-bar duration relative to T4 using its separate base length and minimum.
