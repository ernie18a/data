Calculation: MACD score = the preceding bar's total score on its execution timeframe, with unavailable prior values treated as zero; MACD line = EMA(close, fast lookback) - EMA(close, slow lookback), signal line = EMA(MACD line, signal lookback), and histogram = MACD line - signal line; the direction component is direction score + histogram score when the MACD line rises and the histogram rises, direction score when the MACD line rises and the histogram is unchanged, direction score - histogram score when the MACD line rises and the histogram falls, -(direction score + histogram score) when the MACD line falls and the histogram falls, -direction score when the MACD line falls and the histogram is unchanged, -(direction score - histogram score) when the MACD line falls and the histogram rises, histogram score when the MACD line is unchanged and the histogram rises, -histogram score when the MACD line is unchanged and the histogram falls, and zero when both are unchanged; the current cross score is cross score when the MACD line is at least the signal line and the prior MACD line is below the prior signal line, negative cross score when the MACD line is at most the signal line and the prior MACD line is above the prior signal line, and zero otherwise; total score on each bar is the direction component plus the current cross score plus the prior bar's cross contribution multiplied by the cross-decay parameter.
Calculation: Blended score = (T1 MACD score × T1 weight + chart-timeframe MACD score × chart-timeframe weight) / (T1 weight + chart-timeframe weight); calculate the T1 MACD score on T1 bars and carry it onto chart bars; each MACD score uses the preceding completed bar of its execution timeframe.
Long: on chart bars, blended score one chart bar ago > 0 AND blended score two chart bars ago < 0.
Short: on chart bars, blended score one chart bar ago < 0 AND blended score two chart bars ago > 0.
