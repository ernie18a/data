Calculation: Divergence(indicator, lookback, spacing) = ValidPivot(low, lookback, spacing) AND low at the pivot bar is below low at the pivot bar of the previous ValidPivot(low, lookback, spacing) AND indicator at the pivot bar is above indicator at the pivot bar of that previous ValidPivot
Calculation: StarCount(lookback, spacing, ideal length) = 0 if low at the pivot bar is below low at the pivot bar of the previous ValidPivot(low, lookback, spacing); otherwise the number of true items among: Divergence(RSI(close, RSI length), lookback, spacing); Divergence(MACD histogram(close, MACD fast length, MACD slow length, MACD signal length), lookback, spacing); volume at the pivot bar is above SMA(volume, volume average length) * volume factor; |(pivot bar - pivot bar of the previous ValidPivot(low, lookback, spacing)) - ideal length| <= regularity tolerance * ideal length; HighPosition = RIGHT
Calculation: ProjectedLength(lookback, spacing, average count, ideal length) = rounded mean of the latest average count intervals between consecutive pivot bars of ValidPivot(low, lookback, spacing), using all such intervals if fewer exist, or ideal length if none exist
Calculation: HighPosition = updated at each ValidPivot(low, DCL lookback, DCL spacing): let P1 be the pivot bar of the previous ValidPivot(low, DCL lookback, DCL spacing), P2 the pivot bar of the one before it, and H the pivot bar of the latest ValidPivot(high, high-pivot lookback, high-pivot spacing); if P2 < H < P1, then LEFT when (H - P2) / (P1 - P2) is below lower fraction threshold, RIGHT when it is at or above upper fraction threshold, otherwise MID; if P2 < H < P1 does not hold, HighPosition is unchanged
Calculation: BullRegime = NOT (close < SMA(close, longer MA length) AND SMA(close, shorter MA length) < SMA(close, shorter MA length)[slope lookback])
Calculation: MABreakout = close > SMA(close, breakout MA length) AND a close below SMA(close, breakout MA length) occurred on or after the latest ValidPivot(low, DCL lookback, DCL spacing) bar AND no close above SMA(close, breakout MA length) occurred between the first such close below and this bar
Calculation: RedHealth = (HighPosition = LEFT OR bars since latest DCL pivot bar > overdue factor * ProjectedLength(DCL lookback, DCL spacing, DCL average count, DCL ideal length) OR bars since latest ICL pivot bar > overdue factor * ProjectedLength(ICL lookback, ICL spacing, ICL average count, ICL ideal length) OR NearInvalidation) AND NOT (HighPosition = RIGHT AND MABreakout is true on some bar from the latest DCL ValidPivot bar through this bar AND NOT NearInvalidation), where NearInvalidation = close < near-fail factor * low at the latest DCL pivot bar AND BullRegime
