Calculation: Active liquidity levels = PivotHigh(high, strength1, strength1) on T1 bars, PivotHigh(high, strength2, strength2) on T2 bars, and PivotHigh(high, strength3, strength3) on T3 bars as buy-side levels; matching PivotLow(low, strength1, strength1), PivotLow(low, strength2, strength2), and PivotLow(low, strength3, strength3) as sell-side levels. Compute on T bars and return values on chart bars with lookahead off (historical updates use completed T bars; realtime uses the forming T bar). Include only enabled T values no coarser than the chart. A new level within mergeTolerance × ATR(chart bars, atrLookback) of an unswept opposite-side level merges into it first; otherwise merge it into an unswept same-side level within that distance; both retain the existing price. Otherwise add it. Keep at most maxLevels per side, removing the oldest excess. A level remains active while any of its source T values is enabled and no coarser than the chart, and until swept or, when closeBackRequired and dropBroken are enabled, closed strictly beyond.
Long: An active sell-side level L exists with low < L.price AND (closeBackRequired is false OR close >= L.price)
Short: An active buy-side level L exists with high > L.price AND (closeBackRequired is false OR close <= L.price)
