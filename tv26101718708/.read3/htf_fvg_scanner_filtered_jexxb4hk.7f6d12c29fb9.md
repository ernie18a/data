Calculation: T1/T2/T3 FVG zones = On bars of each T, scan offsets i from 1 through FVG scan lookback minus 3 in ascending order and take the first qualifying bullish and bearish gap separately. Bullish: low[i] > high[i+2], zone [high[i+2], low[i]]; bearish: high[i] < low[i+2], zone [high[i], low[i+2]]. Require i no greater than that T's maximum age, gap size at least the common minimum gap and that T's minimum floor, and gap / max(ATR(T, ATR lookback), minimum tick) between that T's minimum and maximum gap/ATR ratios. Reject a gap if any close[j], j from 0 through i+1, is below its lower boundary for bullish or above its upper boundary for bearish. Mark it mitigated if any of those bars overlaps the zone; otherwise mark it active. Calculated on T bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off: historical values use the last completed T bar, and realtime values may use the T bar still forming.
Calculation: T1/T2/T3 trend = bullish when T close > EMA(T close, shorter EMA lookback) and that EMA > EMA(T close, longer EMA lookback); bearish when T close < the shorter-lookback EMA and that EMA < the longer-lookback EMA. Calculated on T bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off; historical values use the last completed T bar, and realtime values may use the T bar still forming.
Calculation: FVG score = the separate T1, T2, or T3 timeframe weight + the active-state points or mitigated-state points + the aligned-trend bonus + a distance/ATR tier bonus + a gap/ATR tier bonus. Distance/ATR tier thresholds are separate parameters in ascending order; use the corresponding separate points for the first threshold met, otherwise zero. Gap/ATR tier thresholds are separate parameters in descending order; use the corresponding separate points for the first threshold met, otherwise zero. Distance is absolute distance from the T close to the zone midpoint divided by max(ATR(T, ATR lookback), minimum tick); gap/ATR is zone width divided by the same safe ATR.
Calculation: Primary score = FVG score + continuation bonus when the zone's direction agrees with its T trend + a distance bonus or penalty: use separate bonuses for distance at or below each of three ascending raw-distance thresholds, otherwise use the separate farther-distance penalty; add the separate mitigated-state bonus when mitigated. For clusters, distance is measured from chart close to cluster midpoint and ATR is T2 ATR.
Calculation: T1/T2 clusters = for each direction, require both T1 and T2 zones of that direction; cluster upper boundary is the lesser zone upper boundary and cluster lower boundary is the greater zone lower boundary; the intersection exists when its lower boundary is no greater than its upper boundary. Cluster distance is absolute chart-close distance to its midpoint; cluster width is its upper minus lower boundary. Cluster score is the FVG score using T2's timeframe weight, active state, T2 ATR, and T2 trend, plus a separate cluster bonus. Cluster primary score is its primary score using T2 trend, plus a separate cluster primary bonus.
Calculation: Primary zone = On each chart bar, consider the T1 bullish, T1 bearish, T2 bullish, T2 bearish, T3 bullish, T3 bearish, T1/T2 bullish cluster, then T1/T2 bearish cluster, in that order. Start candidate score at zero; replace the candidate only when its primary score meets the separate minimum-primary-score parameter and exceeds the current candidate score. The selected candidate is the highest qualifying score, with ties retaining the earlier candidate in that order. If primary locking is enabled, lock the current candidate when chart close is inside its zone, or when EQ-touch locking is enabled and bullish low <= midpoint or bearish high >= midpoint. Keep the locked zone and its direction and trend until chart close invalidates it (bullish close below its lower boundary or bearish close above its upper boundary) or chart bars since lock reach the separate timeout; then use the current candidate. For a cluster, use T2 trend. The candidate and lock are evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Chart body = max(abs(close - open), minimum tick); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: T4 BOS confirmation = bullish when close[1] > highest(high[2], BOS-confirmation lookback); bearish when close[1] < lowest(low[2], BOS-confirmation lookback). Calculated on T4 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using completed T4 bars.
Calculation: T5 trigger state = On confirmed T5 bars, wait for a primary zone and arm when the bar overlaps it. Reset to waiting if the primary disappears, its close invalidates the zone, or the armed setup exceeds its timeout; timeout is measured from arming and applies before triggering. While armed for a bullish primary, a sweep is low < lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) and close > that prior low; for a bearish primary, high > highest(high[1], sweep lookback) and close < that prior high. On a sweep, freeze the bullish BOS reference as highest(high[1], BOS lookback), or the bearish reference as lowest(low[1], BOS lookback). Confirm BOS when a later confirmed close exceeds the frozen bullish reference or falls below the frozen bearish reference. Within the micro-FVG window after BOS, require a confirmed bullish gap low > high[2] or bearish gap high < low[2], with gap / max(ATR(T5, trigger ATR lookback), minimum tick) at least the separate minimum micro-gap/ATR parameter. Set the micro-FVG entry to its midpoint and enter ready state. If the micro-FVG window expires, re-arm and require a new sweep and BOS. Trigger on a later confirmed T5 bar whose range contains the entry midpoint. The triggered state persists until the primary disappears or is invalidated. Calculated on T5 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off.
