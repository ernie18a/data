Calculation: entry risk = ATR(lookback, chart bars at entry) × stop-distance parameter
Calculation: entry target distance = ATR(lookback, T1 bars, last completed bar sampled on chart bars at entry) × target-distance parameter, using the same ATR lookback parameter
Long: long side enabled AND flat AND cooldown elapsed AND the last completed T1 high has not already produced a long entry since it last changed AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND ((retest disabled AND close > high from the last completed T1 bar sampled on chart bars + ATR(lookback, chart bars) × break-buffer parameter AND open ≤ that level AND (breakout-body parameter = 0 OR abs(close − open) > ATR(lookback, chart bars) × breakout-body parameter) AND entry risk > 0) OR (retest enabled AND a qualifying breakout with those level, open, close, and body conditions is pending AND low ≤ high from the last completed T1 bar sampled on chart bars AND the pending setup has not been cancelled after a non-touch bar with elapsed chart bars > retest-bars parameter AND entry risk > 0)) AND (minimum-stop parameter = 0 OR entry risk ≥ ATR(lookback, chart bars at entry) × minimum-stop parameter) AND entry target distance / entry risk ≥ minimum R:R parameter AND (maximum R:R parameter = 0 OR entry target distance / entry risk ≤ maximum R:R parameter)
Short: short side enabled AND flat AND cooldown elapsed AND the last completed T1 low has not already produced a short entry since it last changed AND T1 is higher than the chart timeframe AND ((retest disabled AND close < low from the last completed T1 bar sampled on chart bars − ATR(lookback, chart bars) × break-buffer parameter AND open ≥ that level AND (breakout-body parameter = 0 OR abs(close − open) > ATR(lookback, chart bars) × breakout-body parameter) AND entry risk > 0) OR (retest enabled AND a qualifying breakout with those level, open, close, and body conditions is pending AND high ≥ low from the last completed T1 bar sampled on chart bars AND the pending setup has not been cancelled after a non-touch bar with elapsed chart bars > retest-bars parameter AND entry risk > 0)) AND (minimum-stop parameter = 0 OR entry risk ≥ ATR(lookback, chart bars at entry) × minimum-stop parameter) AND entry target distance / entry risk ≥ minimum R:R parameter AND (maximum R:R parameter = 0 OR entry target distance / entry risk ≤ maximum R:R parameter)
Exit: long, low ≤ (if trailing-R parameter > 0 AND highest high since entry − entry price ≥ trailing-R parameter × entry risk then max(entry price − entry risk, highest high since entry − trailing-R parameter × entry risk) else entry price − entry risk) OR high ≥ entry price + entry target distance OR elapsed chart bars since entry > max-hold parameter
Exit: short, high ≥ (if trailing-R parameter > 0 AND entry price − lowest low since entry ≥ trailing-R parameter × entry risk then min(entry price + entry risk, lowest low since entry + trailing-R parameter × entry risk) else entry price + entry risk) OR low ≤ entry price − entry target distance OR elapsed chart bars since entry > max-hold parameter
