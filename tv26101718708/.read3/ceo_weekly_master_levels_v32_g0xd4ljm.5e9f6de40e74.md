Calculation: Current T1 high/low = on the first T2 trading day, developing RTH high/low from chart bars; afterward, the first completed T2 RTH bar’s high/low in the current T1 period, held from the last completed T2 bar
Calculation: T1 midpoint = (current T1 high + current T1 low) / 2
Calculation: Fresh level touch = chart-bar high/low overlaps the level ± touch tolerance; a new touch event requires a prior bar whose range is entirely outside the level ± (touch tolerance × release multiplier); the touch remains fresh through the recency parameter
Calculation: Reaction continuation void = after a fresh ML/MH touch, an RTH close at or beyond the T1 midpoint voids that continuation path until a later RTH close returns to the valid side or a new touch; evaluate each bar using the void state from before its close
Calculation: Flip confirmation count = the longer consecutive-close count when chart timeframe is at or below the timeframe-threshold parameter, otherwise the shorter count
Long: On RTH chart bars, with the flip family enabled and permitted on the first T1 trading day, a confirmed MH flip (consecutive closes above current T1 high; invalidated by a close at or below it) is fresh within the recency parameter, has not triggered, and close > SMA(close, lookback) AND close > open AND close > close[1] AND close > current T1 high AND no long signal was accepted within the cooldown parameter
Long: On RTH chart bars, with the flip family enabled and permitted on the first T1 trading day, a confirmed MH flip has a fresh pullback touch (wick overlaps current T1 high ± touch tolerance and close remains above it), has not triggered, and close > SMA(close, lookback) AND close > current T1 high AND close > open AND no MH-momentum candidate qualifies on this bar AND no long signal was accepted within the cooldown parameter
Long: On RTH chart bars, with the reaction family enabled and permitted on the first T1 trading day, a fresh ML touch has not triggered, and ((close crosses above SMA(close, lookback) AND close > current T1 low) OR (the reaction continuation is not voided AND close > SMA(close, lookback) AND (close crosses above current T1 low OR (close > current T1 low AND close > open)))); no MH-momentum or MH-pullback candidate qualifies on this bar AND no long signal was accepted within the cooldown parameter
Short: On RTH chart bars, with the flip family enabled and permitted on the first T1 trading day, a confirmed ML flip (consecutive closes below current T1 low; invalidated by a close at or above it) is fresh within the recency parameter, has not triggered, and close < SMA(close, lookback) AND close < open AND close < close[1] AND close < current T1 low AND no short signal was accepted within the cooldown parameter
Short: On RTH chart bars, with the flip family enabled and permitted on the first T1 trading day, a confirmed ML flip has a fresh pullback touch (wick overlaps current T1 low ± touch tolerance and close remains below it), has not triggered, and close < SMA(close, lookback) AND close < current T1 low AND close < open AND no ML-momentum candidate qualifies on this bar AND no short signal was accepted within the cooldown parameter
Short: On RTH chart bars, with the reaction family enabled and permitted on the first T1 trading day, a fresh MH touch has not triggered, and ((close crosses below SMA(close, lookback) AND close < current T1 high) OR (the reaction continuation is not voided AND close < SMA(close, lookback) AND (close crosses below current T1 high OR (close < current T1 high AND close < open)))); no ML-momentum or ML-pullback candidate qualifies on this bar AND no short signal was accepted within the cooldown parameter
Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer lookback)
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer lookback)
Long: On RTH chart bars, close crosses above current T1 low OR current T1 high
Short: On RTH chart bars, close crosses below current T1 high OR current T1 low

Short: On chart bars, close(T3) crosses below SMA(close, lookback, T3) when T3 is at least the chart timeframe; use the developing T3 bar in live evaluation and the last completed T3 bar historically
