Calculation: Session range = highest(high) and lowest(low) of chart bars within each named session in America/Chicago; midpoint = (session high + session low) / 2; reference range = completed Asia range in London, completed London range in New York, or completed New York range in Asia.
Calculation: Pivot bias(source bars, lookback) = bullish close-break if close > the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, lookback, lookback); otherwise, if that pivot high exists and high > it, upper-wick bias if upper wick >= wick/body parameter * abs(close - open) OR upper wick >= max(high - low, pivot range floor) * wick/range parameter; if bias remains neutral and the latest confirmed PivotLow exists, bearish close-break if close < it, else lower-wick bias if low < it and lower wick >= wick/body parameter * abs(close - open) OR lower wick >= max(high - low, pivot range floor) * wick/range parameter; otherwise neutral. Upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low. Calculate on T1 or T2 bars; chart bars use the current forming requested-timeframe bar with lookahead off. The T1 lookback is separate; T2 dashboard lookbacks are separate, with longest > middle > shortest.
Calculation: Delta = volume * ((close - low) - (high - close)) / max(high - low, delta range floor) when volume is available and cumulative volume is positive, otherwise close - open; smoothed delta = SMA(delta, longer lookback); delta baseline = SMA(smoothed delta, shorter lookback), with shorter < longer.
Calculation: VSA = bullish rejection when relative volume > high-volume threshold, body < range * narrow-spread fraction, lower wick > upper wick * wick ratio, and lower wick > body; bearish rejection uses the corresponding upper-wick conditions. VSA = bullish wide-spread when relative volume > high-volume threshold, body > range * wide-spread fraction, and close >= open; bearish wide-spread uses close < open. Otherwise VSA is other. Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, VSA lookback) when volume and its average are available and cumulative volume is positive, otherwise neutral; body = abs(close - open), range = max(high - low, VSA range floor), upper wick = high - max(open, close), and lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Exhaustion bands = EMA(close, EMA lookback) ± ATR(ATR lookback) * exhaustion multiplier.
Calculation: Stop level = for London breakouts, current London midpoint in midpoint mode, otherwise London low for long or Asia high for short; for New York breakouts, completed London midpoint in midpoint mode, otherwise London low for long or London high for short; for Asia breakouts, completed New York midpoint in midpoint mode, otherwise New York low for long or New York high for short. For a fade short, stop = reference high + (reference high - stop midpoint); for a fade long, stop = reference low - (stop midpoint - reference low). Stop midpoint is current London midpoint for London signals, completed London midpoint for New York signals, or completed New York midpoint for Asia signals.
Long: During London, New York, or Asia, the current session signal is enabled and no earlier signal has been recorded in that session; for New York, time is outside the news blackout window; and ((high > reference high AND T1 pivot bias is bullish close-break AND smoothed delta is positive AND smoothed delta > delta baseline AND VSA is not bullish wide-spread) OR (high <= reference high AND low < reference low AND NOT (T1 pivot bias is bearish close-break AND smoothed delta is negative AND smoothed delta < delta baseline AND VSA is not bearish wide-spread) AND (T1 pivot bias is bullish close-break OR VSA is bullish rejection OR low < lower exhaustion band)).
Short: During London, New York, or Asia, the current session signal is enabled and no earlier signal has been recorded in that session; for New York, time is outside the news blackout window; and ((high > reference high AND NOT (T1 pivot bias is bullish close-break AND smoothed delta is positive AND smoothed delta > delta baseline AND VSA is not bullish wide-spread) AND (T1 pivot bias is bearish close-break OR VSA is bearish rejection OR high > upper exhaustion band) OR (high <= reference high AND low < reference low AND T1 pivot bias is bearish close-break AND smoothed delta is negative AND smoothed delta < delta baseline AND VSA is not bearish wide-spread)).
Exit: long, low < stop level OR datetime is at or after the end of the signal's session.
Exit: short, high > stop level OR datetime is at or after the end of the signal's session.
Long: (T2 pivot bias with the longest lookback is bullish close-break AND T2 pivot bias with the middle lookback is bullish close-break) OR (T2 pivot bias with the middle lookback is bullish close-break AND T2 pivot bias with the shortest lookback is bullish close-break).
Short: (T2 pivot bias with the longest lookback is bearish close-break AND T2 pivot bias with the middle lookback is bearish close-break) OR (T2 pivot bias with the middle lookback is bearish close-break AND T2 pivot bias with the shortest lookback is bearish close-break).
