Calculation: Minimum penetration = 0 when disabled; otherwise penetration multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback parameter, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = high[pivot strength] > every high at offsets pivot strength + 1 through 2 × pivot strength AND high[pivot strength] >= every high at offsets 0 through pivot strength − 1, using completed T1 bars
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = low[pivot strength] < every low at offsets pivot strength + 1 through 2 × pivot strength AND low[pivot strength] <= every low at offsets 0 through pivot strength − 1, using completed T1 bars
Calculation: Active swing high = confirmed swing high until a completed T1 bar has high > its level + minimum penetration, then remove it whether or not the bar qualifies as a sweep
Calculation: Active swing low = confirmed swing low until a completed T1 bar has low < its level − minimum penetration, then remove it whether or not the bar qualifies as a sweep
Calculation: In killzone = T1 bar open time is within either configured session in the configured timezone
Calculation: Level age = number of completed T1 bars since the swing level was confirmed
Calculation: Sweep entry proxy = swept level in level-entry mode; otherwise, for a low sweep, sweep-bar low + (1 − retracement parameter) × (sweep-bar high − sweep-bar low), or for a high sweep, sweep-bar high − (1 − retracement parameter) × (sweep-bar high − sweep-bar low)
Calculation: Sweep stop = sweep-bar low − stop buffer parameter × ATR(ATR lookback parameter, T1, last completed bar) for a low sweep, or sweep-bar high + stop buffer parameter × ATR(ATR lookback parameter, T1, last completed bar) for a high sweep
Calculation: Nearest opposite target = lowest active swing high above the sweep-bar high for a low sweep, or highest active swing low below the sweep-bar low for a high sweep
Calculation: Sweep R:R = absolute distance from sweep entry proxy to nearest opposite target ÷ absolute distance from sweep entry proxy to sweep stop; undefined if no target exists or risk is zero
