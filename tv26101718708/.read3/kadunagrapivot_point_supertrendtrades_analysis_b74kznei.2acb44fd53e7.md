Calculation: pivot supertrend direction = on each bar, update the center to (2 × prior center + confirmed pivot) / 3 when a pivot confirms, initializing it to the pivot; use the confirmed pivot high if both a pivot high and low confirm, otherwise the confirmed pivot low. Form base bands at center ± band multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback). The lower trailing band is max(lower base band, prior lower trailing band) when prior close is above the prior lower trailing band, otherwise the lower base band; the upper trailing band is min(upper base band, prior upper trailing band) when prior close is below the prior upper trailing band, otherwise the upper base band. Direction is bullish when close is above the prior upper trailing band, bearish when close is below the prior lower trailing band, and otherwise retains prior direction; missing prior bands are zero and initial direction is bullish. Pivot highs and lows use equal left and right lookbacks.
Long: pivot supertrend direction is bullish AND its prior bar value was bearish
Short: pivot supertrend direction is bearish AND its prior bar value was bullish
Long: pivot supertrend direction is bullish AND its prior bar value was bearish AND close > selected moving average(selected source, lookback) AND (reference-market trend confirmation is disabled OR reference-market pivot supertrend direction is bullish; use the same pivot supertrend parameters)
Short: pivot supertrend direction is bearish AND its prior bar value was bullish AND close < selected moving average(selected source, lookback) AND (reference-market trend confirmation is disabled OR reference-market pivot supertrend direction is bearish; use the same pivot supertrend parameters)
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100)
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100)
Long: Pivot Point Supertrend (pivot length, ATR length, ATR multiplier) turns bullish AND close is above moving average (source, type, length) AND (direction basis is the chart instrument OR Bitcoin Pivot Point Supertrend is bullish)
Short: Pivot Point Supertrend (pivot length, ATR length, ATR multiplier) turns bearish AND close is below moving average (source, type, length) AND (direction basis is the chart instrument OR Bitcoin Pivot Point Supertrend is bearish)
