Long: SMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above SMA(close, longer lookback) AND low <= support level
Short: SMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below SMA(close, longer lookback) AND high >= resistance level
Exit: long, MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback) crosses below its signal line (signal lookback) OR high >= resistance level
Exit: short, MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback) crosses above its signal line (signal lookback) OR low <= support level
