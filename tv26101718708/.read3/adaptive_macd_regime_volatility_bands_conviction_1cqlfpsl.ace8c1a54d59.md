Calculation: Core MACD = MACD of the selected price source, or its VWMA when volume weighting is enabled and current and prior volume are available; use the selected fixed lengths, or in ER-adaptive mode set fast length to round(fast maximum − (fast maximum − fast minimum) × Efficiency Ratio(selected source, ER lookback)), or in cycle-adaptive mode set fast length to round(MESA dominant-cycle period(hl2) × cycle-to-fast ratio) clamped to the shared fast minimum and maximum; in either adaptive mode, round fast length × the slow ratio and fast length × the signal ratio for the slow and signal lengths. Apply EMA with the per-bar lengths; MACD line is fast EMA − slow EMA, signal line is EMA of the MACD line, and histogram is MACD line − signal line. Fixed fast, slow, and signal lengths, the ER and cycle modes, the slow ratio, and the signal ratio are separate settings.
Calculation: Normalized histogram = Z-score of the core MACD histogram over its normalization lookback; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Volatility-cluster score = Z-score over the histogram normalization lookback of a recursively decayed absolute log return: current score input = previous score input × exp(−decay parameter) + abs(change(log(close))).
Calculation: Regime = Trend Bull or Trend Bear when ADX, using the same lookback for DI and smoothing, exceeds its threshold AND Efficiency Ratio(selected source, ER lookback) exceeds its threshold; classify as Trend Bull when close > EMA(close, regime trend lookback), otherwise Trend Bear. If not trending, classify as Volatile when the volatility-cluster score exceeds its threshold, otherwise Range.
Calculation: Outer price bands = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source over the band lookback ± its volume-weighted population standard deviation × the outer-deviation multiplier; use volume as weight when positive and otherwise use a unit weight.
Calculation: CVD direction = sign of current cumulative signed volume minus cumulative signed volume at a separate lookback of chart bars ago; per chart bar, sum lower-timeframe signed volume, assigning positive volume when lower-timeframe close >= open and negative volume otherwise, and use signed chart-bar volume if no lower-timeframe bars are available. CVD availability requires current and prior chart-bar volume.
Calculation: MTF state(T) = sign of (EMA(close, MTF fast lookback, T) − EMA(close, MTF slow lookback, T)) − EMA(MTF MACD line, MTF signal lookback, T). Use these same separate MTF lengths on T1, T2, T3, T4, and T5. Calculate on each T timeframe and sample onto chart bars with lookahead off; the current T bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Weighted direction score = weighted mean of: clamp(normalized histogram / shared saturation scale, −1, 1); mean signed MTF states across T1–T4 when MTF is enabled; CVD direction when available; the OI score when open-interest data is available; clamp(Z-score of traded close − reference-underlying close over the basis lookback / the shared saturation scale, −1, 1) when that reference is available; a positive regime score for Trend Bull, its negative for Trend Bear, and zero otherwise; sign(cross-asset close − its value at the cross-asset lookback) × the cross-asset correlation sign when available; and the most recent fade direction within the fade-persistence lookback when fade scoring is enabled. OI score is positive buildup score when close and open interest both rise, negative buildup score when close falls and open interest rises, smaller positive cover score when close rises and open interest falls, smaller negative unwind score when both fall, and zero otherwise. Use separate weights for core MACD, MTF, CVD, OI, basis, regime, cross-asset, and fade; divide by the sum of included weights. Reference-underlying, volatility-index, open-interest, and cross-asset values are sampled on chart bars with lookahead off.
Calculation: Conviction = rounded absolute weighted direction score scaled to the conviction range, multiplied by a separate volatility-spike discount when the live volatility-index Z-score exceeds its spike threshold.
Calculation: Trade verdict = LONG when the weighted direction score is positive, conviction meets the minimum, regime is not Trend Bear, and no long veto holds; SHORT when the score is negative, conviction meets the minimum, regime is not Trend Bull, and no short veto holds; otherwise no verdict. Long vetoes are: enabled MTF bullish-state count below the minimum alignment; available CVD direction negative; available OI showing falling close with rising open interest; live volatility-index Z-score above its spike threshold; absolute normalized histogram below its flat-zone threshold; or enabled T5 state negative. Short vetoes mirror these: enabled MTF bearish-state count below minimum alignment; available CVD direction positive; available OI showing rising close with rising open interest; the same volatility-index and flat-zone vetoes; or enabled T5 state positive. Zero CVD or T5 state is not opposing.
Long: Core MACD line crosses above its signal line.
Short: Core MACD line crosses below its signal line.
Long: A newly confirmed pivot low is below the prior confirmed pivot low and normalized histogram at the new pivot is above normalized histogram at the prior pivot.
Short: A newly confirmed pivot high is above the prior confirmed pivot high and normalized histogram at the new pivot is below normalized histogram at the prior pivot.
Long: Low <= lower outer price band AND close > lower outer price band AND regime is not Trend Bear AND the chart bar is confirmed when close confirmation is enabled.
Short: High >= upper outer price band AND close < upper outer price band AND regime is not Trend Bull AND the chart bar is confirmed when close confirmation is enabled.
Long: Trade verdict is LONG AND position is flat or short AND close confirmation is disabled or the chart bar is confirmed.
Short: Trade verdict is SHORT AND position is flat or long AND close confirmation is disabled or the chart bar is confirmed.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + risk distance × second-target multiple OR low <= entry price after any post-entry high reaches entry price + risk distance OR low <= entry price − risk distance before that target is reached; risk distance = ATR at entry × stop multiplier.
Exit: short, low <= entry price − risk distance × second-target multiple OR high >= entry price after any post-entry low reaches entry price − risk distance OR high >= entry price + risk distance before that target is reached; risk distance = ATR at entry × stop multiplier.
Option: buy call, option suggestions are enabled AND trade verdict is LONG.
Option: buy put, option suggestions are enabled AND trade verdict is SHORT.
