Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the opening-range session, locked when the session ends and reset at the next day's opening-range start
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low during the opening-range session, locked when the session ends and reset at the next day's opening-range start
Long: After the opening range is locked and during the trading session, if retest confirmation is off, signal on the first bar whose selected breakout source (close or high) > opening-range high; if retest confirmation is on, signal on the first bar within the retest window after the first such break where low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high; at most one signal per day
Short: After the opening range is locked and during the trading session, if retest confirmation is off, signal on the first bar whose selected breakout source (close or low) < opening-range low; if retest confirmation is on, signal on the first bar within the retest window after the first such break where high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low; at most one signal per day
