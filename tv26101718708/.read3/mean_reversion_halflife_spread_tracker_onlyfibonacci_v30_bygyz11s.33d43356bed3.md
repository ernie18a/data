Calculation: Spread ratio = close / SMA(close, moving-average lookback) in single-asset mode, or close / secondary asset close in pair mode when the denominator is positive.
Calculation: Smoothed Z-Score = EMA((Spread ratio − SMA(Spread ratio, z-score lookback)) / StDev(Spread ratio, z-score lookback), smoothing lookback), using zero for the inner value when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Average closure duration = mean of up to the sample-limit most recent completed threshold-breach durations, counted in chart bars from the first bar with Smoothed Z-Score >= the upper threshold or <= the lower threshold while no breach is active through the first subsequent bar with Smoothed Z-Score <= 0 for an upper breach or >= 0 for a lower breach, inclusive; undefined until a breach completes.
Long: Spread ratio and Smoothed Z-Score are defined AND no signal is active AND Smoothed Z-Score crosses above the lower threshold, does not also cross below the upper threshold on that bar, and remains strictly above the lower threshold at each of the next confirmation-parameter chart-bar closes.
Short: Spread ratio and Smoothed Z-Score are defined AND no signal is active AND Smoothed Z-Score crosses below the upper threshold and remains strictly below the upper threshold at each of the next confirmation-parameter chart-bar closes.
Exit: Long, Smoothed Z-Score is defined and >= 0 OR signal duration, counted in chart bars including the signal bar, exceeds twice the available average closure duration OR an active threshold-breach duration, counted in chart bars including its first bar, exceeds the available average closure duration.
Exit: Short, Smoothed Z-Score is defined and <= 0 OR signal duration, counted in chart bars including the signal bar, exceeds twice the available average closure duration OR an active threshold-breach duration, counted in chart bars including its first bar, exceeds the available average closure duration.
