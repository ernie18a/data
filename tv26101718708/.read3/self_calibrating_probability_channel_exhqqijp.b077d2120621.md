Calculation: Forecast centre = prior-bar forecast path + prior-bar drift; select EMA(price source, EMA lookback) with path change over drift lookback as drift, linear regression(price source, regression lookback) with regression slope, anchored VWAP(price source, volume, selected calendar anchor) with path change over drift lookback, Kalman filter(level and velocity), or an inverse exponentially updated absolute-error-weighted ensemble of those paths and drifts.
Calculation: Lower and upper 90% and 95% prediction bounds = forecast centre + (prior ATR when volatility normalization is enabled, otherwise 1) × the corresponding rolling quantile of signed one-step forecast errors over the error lookback, using adaptively calibrated tail probabilities.
Calculation: Upper anomaly threshold = upper 95% prediction bound + anomaly-depth parameter × max(upper 95% bound − upper 90% bound, ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-gap parameter).
Calculation: Lower anomaly threshold = lower 95% prediction bound − anomaly-depth parameter × max(lower 90% bound − lower 95% bound, ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-gap parameter).
Long: Available forecast-error samples ≥ minimum-sample parameter AND price source crosses above the upper anomaly threshold.
Short: Available forecast-error samples ≥ minimum-sample parameter AND price source crosses below the lower anomaly threshold.
Calculation: Up breach-quality score = count of true conditions: (price source − upper 95% prediction bound) / ATR(ATR lookback) > displacement parameter; (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick size) > close-location parameter; volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-surge parameter; high − low > ATR(ATR lookback) × range-expansion parameter; close > most recent confirmed pivot high with pivot lookback on both sides.
Long: Available forecast-error samples ≥ minimum-sample parameter AND price source crosses above the upper anomaly threshold AND up breach-quality score ≥ minimum-quality-score parameter.
Calculation: Down breach-quality score = count of true conditions: (lower 95% prediction bound − price source) / ATR(ATR lookback) > displacement parameter; (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick size) > close-location parameter; volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-surge parameter; high − low > ATR(ATR lookback) × range-expansion parameter; close < most recent confirmed pivot low with pivot lookback on both sides.
Short: Available forecast-error samples ≥ minimum-sample parameter AND price source crosses below the lower anomaly threshold AND down breach-quality score ≥ minimum-quality-score parameter.
