Calculation: failure quality score = depth weight × clamp(score ceiling − abs(depth ATR − target depth ATR) × depth penalty, score floor, score ceiling) + wick weight × clamp(rejection wick / max(bar high − bar low, minimum tick) × wick scale, score floor, score ceiling) + reclaim weight × clamp(inside distance / max(opening-range span × reclaim fraction, minimum tick) × score ceiling, score floor, score ceiling) + speed weight × clamp(score ceiling − bars to reclaim × speed penalty, score floor, score ceiling) + outside-close weight × clamp(score ceiling − outside-close count × outside-close penalty, score floor, score ceiling); depth ATR = distance from the opening-range boundary to the probe extreme divided by ATR-safe; ATR-safe = ATR(ATR lookback) with unavailable values replaced by zero, floored at minimum tick; opening-range span = max(opening-range high − opening-range low, minimum tick); rejection wick = upper wick for an upside probe or lower wick for a downside probe; inside distance = opening-range high − close for an upside probe or close − opening-range low for a downside probe; outside-close count = confirmed closes beyond the probed boundary during the attempt; when volume weighting is enabled, blend this base score and clamp(relative volume / volume normalization × score ceiling, score floor, score ceiling) using separate base-score and volume-score weights; relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), using the configured fallback when the average is unavailable or nonpositive
