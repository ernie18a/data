Calculation: Zone(q) = close(T1, last completed bar) * exp(H * SMA(LogRet, N) + sqrt(H) * StDev(LogRet, N) * NormInv(q))
Calculation: VolRatio = ATR(shorter lookback) / ATR(longer lookback)
Calculation: InWindow(s, e) = s <= time of day in New York < e
