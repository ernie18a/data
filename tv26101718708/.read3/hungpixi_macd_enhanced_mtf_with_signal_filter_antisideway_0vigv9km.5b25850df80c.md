Calculation: MACD score = D + H + C, where M = MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback), S = EMA(M, signal lookback), and histogram = M − S; D is indicator-direction score plus histogram-direction score: if M > M[1], indicator-direction score is I + J when histogram > histogram[1], I when equal, or I − J when lower; if M < M[1], it is −(I + J) when histogram < histogram[1], −I when equal, or −(I − J) when higher; otherwise it is 0; histogram-direction score is J when M = M[1] and histogram rises, −J when M = M[1] and histogram falls, otherwise 0; C = current crossing contribution + decay parameter × prior C (zero if unavailable), where current crossing contribution is cross-score parameter on a cross above S, negative cross-score parameter on a cross below S, otherwise 0; I, J, and the cross-score parameter are separate parameters
Calculation: Blended score = (weight parameter selected by chart timeframe × MACD score calculated on T1 bars, using the T1 bar still forming when the lookahead parameter is enabled and otherwise the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars + MACD score calculated on chart bars) / (weight parameter + 1), where T1 is the higher timeframe selected from the chart timeframe
Calculation: Uptrend = EMA(close, shorter trend EMA lookback) calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and sampled on chart bars > EMA(close, longer trend EMA lookback) calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and sampled on chart bars AND the absolute difference between those EMAs > trend separation parameter
Calculation: Downtrend = EMA(close, shorter trend EMA lookback) calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and sampled on chart bars < EMA(close, longer trend EMA lookback) calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and sampled on chart bars AND the absolute difference between those EMAs > trend separation parameter
Long: crosses above Blended score and zero AND ATR(filter lookback, chart bars) > minimum ATR parameter AND Uptrend
Long: crosses above Blended score and zero AND ATR(filter lookback, chart bars) > minimum ATR parameter AND Downtrend
Long: crosses above Blended score and zero AND ATR(filter lookback, chart bars) > minimum ATR parameter AND Uptrend AND crosses above Blended score and positive strong-signal threshold
Short: crosses below Blended score and zero AND ATR(filter lookback, chart bars) > minimum ATR parameter AND Downtrend
Short: crosses below Blended score and zero AND ATR(filter lookback, chart bars) > minimum ATR parameter AND Uptrend
Short: crosses below Blended score and zero AND ATR(filter lookback, chart bars) > minimum ATR parameter AND Downtrend AND crosses below Blended score and negative strong-signal threshold
Exit: long, low <= entry price − ATR(exit lookback, chart bars) × stop multiplier
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(exit lookback, chart bars) × stop multiplier
