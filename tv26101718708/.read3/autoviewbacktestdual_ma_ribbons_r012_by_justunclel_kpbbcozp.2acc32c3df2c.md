Calculation: Effective lookback = base lookback unchanged when the common lookback multiplier is disabled; otherwise base lookback × multiplier
Calculation: MA(close, lookback, type, gamma) = the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, SMMA, DEMA, TEMA, Laguerre MA, Hull MA, zero-lag EMA, TMA, or Super Smoother; gamma applies to Laguerre MA
Calculation: Fast average = mean of MA(close, effective shortest lookback, fast type, lower gamma) and MA(close, effective second-shortest lookback, fast type, upper gamma); lower and upper share the fast type
Calculation: Slow average = mean of MA(close, effective second-longest lookback, slow type, lower gamma) and MA(close, effective longest lookback, slow type, upper gamma); lower and upper share the slow type; fast and slow types are separate; lower gamma is shared by both lower MAs, upper gamma by both upper MAs, and lower gamma < upper gamma
Calculation: Bullish fast-slow count = consecutive chart-bar count where fast average > slow average and fast average - slow average > fast average[1] - slow average[1]; reset otherwise
Calculation: Bearish fast-slow count = consecutive chart-bar count where fast average < slow average and fast average - slow average < fast average[1] - slow average[1]; reset otherwise
Calculation: Bullish fast-ribbon count = consecutive chart-bar count where fast lower MA > fast upper MA and fast lower MA - fast upper MA > fast lower MA[1] - fast upper MA[1]; reset otherwise
Calculation: Bearish fast-ribbon count = consecutive chart-bar count where fast lower MA < fast upper MA and fast lower MA - fast upper MA < fast lower MA[1] - fast upper MA[1]; reset otherwise
Calculation: Bullish slow-ribbon count = consecutive chart-bar count where slow lower MA > slow upper MA and slow lower MA - slow upper MA > slow lower MA[1] - slow upper MA[1]; reset otherwise
Calculation: Bearish slow-ribbon count = consecutive chart-bar count where slow lower MA < slow upper MA and slow lower MA - slow upper MA < slow lower MA[1] - slow upper MA[1]; reset otherwise
Calculation: Bullish setup = bullish fast-slow count, bullish fast-ribbon count, and bullish slow-ribbon count are each at least the shared persistence threshold
Calculation: Bearish setup = bearish fast-slow count, bearish fast-ribbon count, and bearish slow-ribbon count are each at least the shared persistence threshold
Calculation: Adjusted high = high in standard mode, otherwise max(open, close) in Renko mode
Calculation: Adjusted low = low in standard mode, otherwise min(open, close) in Renko mode
Calculation: Highest adjusted high since long entry = running maximum of adjusted high from the entry bar through the current bar
Calculation: Lowest adjusted low since short entry = running minimum of adjusted low from the entry bar through the current bar
Calculation: Effective trailing-profit fraction = min(trailing-profit fraction, target-profit fraction)
Long: configured test date interval AND selected side mode permits long AND (normal direction mode AND bullish setup AND NOT bullish setup[1] OR reverse direction mode AND bearish setup AND NOT bearish setup[1]) AND no script trade is active AND neither side's close trigger fired on the previous bar
Short: configured test date interval AND selected side mode permits short AND (normal direction mode AND bearish setup AND NOT bearish setup[1] OR reverse direction mode AND bullish setup AND NOT bullish setup[1]) AND no script trade is active AND neither side's close trigger fired on the previous bar
Exit: long, configured test date interval AND after the entry bar AND (take profit enabled AND highest adjusted high since long entry >= entry price × (1 + target-profit fraction) AND adjusted low <= highest adjusted high since long entry × (1 - effective trailing-profit fraction) OR stop loss enabled AND adjusted low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR trailing stop enabled AND adjusted low <= highest adjusted high since long entry × (1 - trailing-stop fraction) OR (opposite-exit setting enabled OR (trailing stop disabled AND stop loss disabled)) AND fast average[1] < slow average[1] AND open < slow average[1] AND NOT (fast average[2] < slow average[2] AND open[1] < slow average[2]))
