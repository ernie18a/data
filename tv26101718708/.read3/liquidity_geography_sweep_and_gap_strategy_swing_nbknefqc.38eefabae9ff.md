Calculation: Regime EMA travel in ATR = abs(EMA(close, regime lookback) - EMA(close, regime lookback)[regime lookback]) / ATR(ATR lookback), on chart bars
Calculation: Round-number step = 10^floor(log10(close)) or manual step, selected by step mode
