Calculation: Volume profile = On each chart bar, use the current and prior profile-lookback chart bars; divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into equal profile bins, applying a minimum tick-range floor. Distribute each bar’s volume equally across every bin intersecting its low–high range, weighted by recency-decay raised to bars ago when recency weighting is enabled and equally otherwise. POC is the bin with the greatest total volume; HVN-2 is the distinct bin with the second-greatest total volume.
Calculation: Hurst proxy = For close over the Hurst lookback, calculate the range of cumulative deviations from SMA(close, Hurst lookback), divided by standard deviation(close, Hurst lookback), then take the logarithm of that ratio divided by the logarithm of lookback divided by the Hurst scaling factor; clamp to the Hurst bounds. Use neutral Hurst when history, the ratio, or standard deviation is insufficient.
Calculation: Classified pockets = On each chart bar, find interior volume-profile bins strictly below both neighbors, at or below the selected volume percentile, and with bin volume divided by mean neighboring-bin volume no greater than the local-minimum ratio. Expand each candidate across contiguous bins at or below the percentile threshold; keep pockets meeting the minimum bin-width and ATR-width requirements, and use their outer bin-center prices as bounds. For each pocket, calculate mean in-pocket volume, mean volume in the wall bands beyond each edge, and touch density as the number of bars in the touch lookback whose low–high ranges intersect its bins divided by the lookback. Let HTF confluence mean its midpoint is within ATR times the confluence factor of a previous completed high or low from an enabled T1 or T2, sampled on chart bars. For pockets above close, use the upper wall as the far wall; below close, use the lower wall. Calculate wall strength as the normalized sigmoid of far-wall volume divided by pocket depth, vacuum as one minus the lesser of far-wall volume divided by near-wall volume and one (or one when far-wall volume is zero), reversal touch score as one minus the lesser of touch density times its scaling factor and one, and breakthrough touch score as the lesser of that product and one. Normalize classification weights by their sum, using one if the sum is zero. Reversal score weights wall strength, inverse vacuum, inverse Hurst, HTF confluence, and reversal touch score; breakthrough score weights inverse wall strength, vacuum, Hurst, and breakthrough touch score. Classify ties as reversal, and retain the nearest midpoint to close for each above/below reversal/breakthrough category.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, lookback), or one when the average is nonpositive.
Long: A reversal pocket below exists AND (close crosses below its upper edge when close confirmation is enabled OR high is at least its upper edge and close is below it when disabled) AND (volume ratio is below the volume-decrease threshold OR ROC filter is enabled and ROC(close, lookback) is at least zero).
Long: A breakthrough pocket above exists AND (close crosses above its lower edge when close confirmation is enabled OR low is at most its lower edge and close is above it when disabled) AND (momentum filter is disabled OR close > EMA(close, lookback)) AND (volume ratio is above the volume-increase threshold OR ROC filter is disabled OR ROC(close, lookback) > 0).
Short: A reversal pocket above exists AND (close crosses above its lower edge when close confirmation is enabled OR low is at most its lower edge and close is above it when disabled) AND (volume ratio is below the volume-decrease threshold OR ROC filter is enabled and ROC(close, lookback) is at most zero).
Short: A breakthrough pocket below exists AND (close crosses below its upper edge when close confirmation is enabled OR high is at least its upper edge and close is below it when disabled) AND (momentum filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close, lookback)) AND (volume ratio is above the volume-increase threshold OR ROC filter is disabled OR ROC(close, lookback) < 0).
Exit: long, low is below the lower edge of the reversal pocket below the entry OR high reaches the nearest POC or HVN-2 above the entry.
Exit: short, high is above the upper edge of the reversal pocket above the entry OR low reaches the nearest POC or HVN-2 below the entry.
