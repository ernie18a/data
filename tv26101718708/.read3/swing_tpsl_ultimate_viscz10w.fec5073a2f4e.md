Calculation: Final TP% and final SL% = select the configured sample source: pivot-only uses the configured primary method on its rolling buffer; per-bar-only uses the per-bar EMA; blended uses the mean of the trimmed pivot-buffer mean and the per-bar EMA. The primary method is the pivot-buffer mean, the EMA of successive pivot samples with alpha 2/(EMA weight period + 1), the trimmed pivot-buffer mean, or the mean of the trimmed pivot-buffer mean and per-bar EMA. Per-bar TP = EMA(100 × (high − open) / open, per-bar lookback); per-bar SL = EMA(100 × (open − low) / open, per-bar lookback). TP samples = 100 × (confirmed pivot high − close) / close; SL samples = 100 × (close − confirmed pivot low) / close; add only positive samples and retain the latest buffer-size samples. For a trimmed mean, sort the buffer and remove max(1, round(buffer size × trim percentage / 100)) samples from each end when the buffer has at least four samples; otherwise use its mean, and use the mean if trimming leaves no samples. No pivot samples gives a pivot-buffer value of zero. Confirmed chart pivots use separate fast, mid, and slow lookbacks, with fast shorter than mid and mid shorter than slow. Optional higher-timeframe pivots use a separate lookback on T1 bars, with the latest available T1 value mapped to chart bars (last completed T1 bar historically, still-forming T1 bar in real time). Update the last sampled swing high/low only when the corresponding pivot price and its TP/SL sample are positive.
