Calculation: Seasonal cohorts = on confirmed T1 closes, anchor each forecast year at its first market trading day; for each prior year use the nearest T1 bar to the same month and day within the anchor-search tolerance, choosing the more recent bar on ties. Reserve the most recent holdout years only when lookback years reach the holdout activation threshold; their count is the rounded lookback-years fraction clamped between the holdout minimum and maximum. Earlier years form the training cohort. Use last completed T1 bars.
Calculation: Seasonal states = for each trading-day offset from the forecast-year origin, calculate the mean across training years of 100 × (next T1 close / current T1 close − 1), and confidence as 100 × the larger of positive and negative sample counts divided by valid samples. Smooth each mean using available values at the previous, current, and next offsets. The direction threshold is the larger of mean absolute smoothed value times the direction factor and the direction floor. State is long when confidence meets its threshold and the smoothed value exceeds the direction threshold; short when it is below the negative threshold; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Seasonal windows = group consecutive equal non-neutral states; bridge a same-side neutral gap no larger than the gap parameter before duration filtering. Discard segments shorter than the larger of the minimum segment length and minimum trade duration, and segments whose exit offset minus entry offset is less than the minimum trade duration. Merge same-side overlaps and direct adjacencies; retain only segments with valid projected entry and exit timestamps in chronological order, up to the zone cap. Project dates from the forecast-year origin using the market trading-day calendar.
Calculation: Window statistics = for each window and training-year anchor, use the historical T1 closes at its entry and exit offsets and the inclusive period low and high. Directional return is 100 × side × (exit close / entry close − 1); MAE and MFE are the adverse and favorable excursion from entry close to the period low and high, as percentages of entry close, floored at zero. From valid samples calculate win rate, mean return, mean MAE, mean MFE, population return standard deviation, and SQN = sqrt(sample count) × mean return / standard deviation when positive. Raw score = win rate + mean return × return weight − mean MAE × drawdown weight + SQN × SQN weight, treating unavailable SQN as zero.
Calculation: Wilson lower bound = the one-sided Wilson lower confidence bound for successes and sample count, using the configured confidence level.
Calculation: Plateau grade = score each of the six neighboring windows formed by shifting entry, exit, or both by one offset; divide each valid neighbor score by the selected window score. Count ratios below the weak-neighbor threshold. Grade Strong when valid-neighbor count, minimum ratio, average ratio, and weak-neighbor count pass their Strong thresholds; otherwise Valid, Weak, or Reject when their respective thresholds pass, and Reject otherwise.
Calculation: AutoQ grade and score = raw-edge grade from raw-score bands; sample grade from sample count, sample coverage against training years, and Wilson lower bound of rounded successes; outlier grade from mean MFE / absolute mean return and SQN, with dispersion unavailable; separation grade from the side's raw score minus the opposite side's raw score; tradeability grade from whether the window is expired, pending, or its elapsed-duration ratio. Overall grade is the lowest of those grades and the stability grade. Final score is raw score plus the configured grade bonuses and a clamped separation adjustment, clamped to the configured score bounds. Require the strength-dependent minimum grade and score.
Calculation: Tradeable seasonal zone = a structurally valid window with at least one valid sample, reference samples from all lookback years meeting the smaller of lookback years and the reference sample cap, reference win rate meeting the minimum win rate, and reference mean return meeting the reference return floor. At zero quality strength these baseline gates suffice. At positive strength also require training samples meeting the strength-tiered sample minimum, training win rate meeting the larger of the minimum win rate and strength-based win-rate floor, training mean return meeting the strength-based return floor, mean MAE no greater than its maximum, and AutoQ grade and score passing. Also require training samples to meet the exploration sample threshold, Wilson lower bound to meet its threshold, and median return to be positive; require AutoQ to pass at or above the review strength. At or above the plateau strength, reject a Reject plateau grade. At or above the holdout strength, reject an adequately sampled failed holdout, where adequate depth meets its sample threshold and failed means median return is nonpositive and success rate is below its threshold. Holdout status is confirmed when median return is positive, success rate meets its threshold, and the 75th-percentile MFE exceeds the 75th-percentile MAE; otherwise it is mixed unless failed.
Calculation: Operational exit timestamp = the earlier of the projected seasonal-window exit and the projected date obtained by advancing its entry by the maximum holding-duration parameter on the market trading-day calendar.
Long: a tradeable seasonal zone has long side AND current T1 timestamp is on or after its projected entry timestamp AND on or before its operational exit timestamp.
Short: a tradeable seasonal zone has short side AND current T1 timestamp is on or after its projected entry timestamp AND on or before its operational exit timestamp.
Exit: long, current T1 timestamp is after that zone's operational exit timestamp.
Exit: short, current T1 timestamp is after that zone's operational exit timestamp.
