Calculation: Unswept pivot levels = confirmed chart-bar pivot highs and lows using the same pivot-lookback parameter on both sides; each level’s price and origin are the pivot bar’s high or low and bar. Create levels only when the optional chart-bar session filter passes, retain at most the max-levels-per-side parameter of newest unswept levels on each side, and mark a pivot high swept when chart high or close exceeds its price, or a pivot low swept when chart low or close falls below its price, according to the sweep-mode parameter.
Calculation: Level strength = pivot-bar volume, chart bars since the pivot-origin bar, or touch count, selected by the strength-metric parameter. Touch count starts at the unit-count parameter and increments when min(abs(chart high − level price), abs(chart low − level price)) <= ATR(chart bars, shared ATR-lookback parameter) × touch-distance parameter.
Calculation: Buy-side weight = sum(max(level strength, unit-count parameter)) over unswept pivot-low levels whose chart-bar age since origin is <= the bias-lookback parameter.
Calculation: Sell-side weight = sum(max(level strength, unit-count parameter)) over unswept pivot-high levels whose chart-bar age since origin is <= the bias-lookback parameter.
Calculation: Bias percentage = 100 × (sell-side weight − buy-side weight) / (sell-side weight + buy-side weight) when their sum is positive; otherwise 0.
Long: Bias percentage < −bias-threshold parameter OR (current-timeframe FVG detection is enabled AND the chart bar is confirmed AND low > high[n] AND low − high[n] >= ATR(chart bars, shared ATR-lookback parameter) × shared minimum-gap-size parameter) OR (higher-timeframe FVG detection is enabled AND, on chart bars when a new T1 bar begins, low of the last completed T1 bar > high of the T1 bar FVG-comparison-offset parameter bars before it AND the gap is >= ATR(chart bars, shared ATR-lookback parameter) × shared minimum-gap-size parameter) OR (IFVG conversion is enabled AND an unexpired, un-inverted bearish FVG from either detector has chart close > its upper boundary).
Short: Bias percentage > bias-threshold parameter OR (current-timeframe FVG detection is enabled AND the chart bar is confirmed AND high < low[n] AND low[n] − high >= ATR(chart bars, shared ATR-lookback parameter) × shared minimum-gap-size parameter) OR (higher-timeframe FVG detection is enabled AND, on chart bars when a new T1 bar begins, high of the last completed T1 bar < low of the T1 bar FVG-comparison-offset parameter bars before it AND the gap is >= ATR(chart bars, shared ATR-lookback parameter) × shared minimum-gap-size parameter) OR (IFVG conversion is enabled AND an unexpired, un-inverted bullish FVG from either detector has chart close < its lower boundary).
