Calculation: short-range high = highest(high) during the first short-duration parameter after each selected weekday session or enabled pre-session window opens, using last completed T2 bars sampled on chart bars when the chart timeframe is above T2, otherwise chart bars
Calculation: short-range low = lowest(low) over the same interval
Calculation: long-range high = highest(high) during the first long-duration parameter after each selected weekday session or enabled pre-session window opens, using last completed T2 bars sampled on chart bars when the chart timeframe is above T2, otherwise chart bars; the long-duration parameter is greater than the short-duration parameter
Calculation: long-range low = lowest(low) over the same interval
Calculation: T1 close input = close of the last completed T1 bar sampled on chart bars when the chart timeframe is at or below T1; otherwise the previous chart-bar close
Long: during a selected weekday range window after the short range is complete and before either short-range boundary has broken, the selected break-confirm comparison is above the short-range high (chart high for touch, chart close for chart-close confirmation, or T1 close input for T1-close confirmation)
Short: during a selected weekday range window after the short range is complete and before either short-range boundary has broken, the selected break-confirm comparison is below the short-range low (chart low for touch, chart close for chart-close confirmation, or T1 close input for T1-close confirmation)
Long: during a selected weekday range window after the long range is complete, either no long-range boundary has broken or the lower boundary broke first and the upper boundary is the first opposite-side break; the selected break-confirm comparison is above the long-range high
Short: during a selected weekday range window after the long range is complete, either no long-range boundary has broken or the upper boundary broke first and the lower boundary is the first opposite-side break; the selected break-confirm comparison is below the long-range low
Long: during a selected weekday range window after the long range is complete and when the chart timeframe is at or below T1, the T1 close input is above the long-range high and no earlier same-side T1-close tag occurred in that range window
Short: during a selected weekday range window after the long range is complete and when the chart timeframe is at or below T1, the T1 close input is below the long-range low and no earlier same-side T1-close tag occurred in that range window
Long: during a selected weekday range window after the long range is complete, chart close is above the long-range high and no earlier same-side chart-close tag occurred in that range window
Short: during a selected weekday range window after the long range is complete, chart close is below the long-range low and no earlier same-side chart-close tag occurred in that range window
