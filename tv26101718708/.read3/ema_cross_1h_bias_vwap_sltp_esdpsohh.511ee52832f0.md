Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > VWAP(close) AND close(T1, sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time) > EMA(close, separate HTF lookback, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time)
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < VWAP(close) AND close(T1, sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time) < EMA(close, separate HTF lookback, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in real time)
Exit: Long, low <= VWAP(close) on the long signal bar OR high >= close on the long signal bar + (close on the long signal bar - VWAP(close) on the long signal bar)
Exit: Short, high >= VWAP(close) on the short signal bar OR low <= close on the short signal bar - (VWAP(close) on the short signal bar - close on the short signal bar)
