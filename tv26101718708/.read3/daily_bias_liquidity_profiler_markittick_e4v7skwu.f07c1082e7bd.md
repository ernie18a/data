Calculation: T1 day type = Consolidation if Average Daily Range(T1, ADR lookback, preceding completed T1 bars) is unavailable or zero; otherwise Expansion if (completed T1 high − completed T1 low) / that Average Daily Range ≥ expansion threshold, Consolidation if ≤ consolidation threshold, and Reversal otherwise.
Calculation: Candle body fraction(open, high, low, close) = 0 if high = low, otherwise abs(close − open) / (high − low).
Calculation: Probability zone bounds = current T1 open ± Average Daily Range(T1, ADR lookback, last completed T1 bars) × zone multiplier; the 95% and 99% zones use separate multipliers. These bounds are read on chart bars using the current T1 open and completed T1 bars.
Long: On the first chart bar of a new T1 period, the most recent pattern-lookback completed T1 periods are all Consolidation days AND the current T1 open is below the midpoint of the previous completed T1 high and low, OR they are all Reversal days AND the current T1 open is below the previous completed T1 close.
Short: On the first chart bar of a new T1 period, the most recent pattern-lookback completed T1 periods are all Consolidation days AND the current T1 open is at or above the midpoint of the previous completed T1 high and low, OR they are all Reversal days AND the current T1 open is at or above the previous completed T1 close.
Long: On chart bars, the prior completed chart bar low is below the previous completed T1 low AND its close is above that low.
Short: On chart bars, the prior completed chart bar high is above the previous completed T1 high AND its close is below that high.
Long: Session sweep detection is enabled AND the prior completed chart bar low is below an Asia-session or London-session low AND its close is above that same low AND Candle body fraction(open[1], high[1], low[1], close[1]) ≥ minimum sweep body fraction.
Short: Session sweep detection is enabled AND the prior completed chart bar high is above an Asia-session or London-session high AND its close is below that same high AND Candle body fraction(open[1], high[1], low[1], close[1]) ≥ minimum sweep body fraction.
Long: FVG confluence and the 95% zone are enabled AND low[1] > high[3] on chart bars AND the prior completed chart bar's low or high is within the 95% zone, OR the 99% zone is enabled AND that low or high is within the 99% zone. The zones use the current T1 open and last completed T1 values.
Short: FVG confluence and the 95% zone are enabled AND high[1] < low[3] on chart bars AND the prior completed chart bar's low or high is within the 95% zone, OR the 99% zone is enabled AND that low or high is within the 99% zone. The zones use the current T1 open and last completed T1 values.
