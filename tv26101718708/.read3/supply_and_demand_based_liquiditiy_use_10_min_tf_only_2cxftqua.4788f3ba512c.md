Calculation: Demand zones = ranges [low, high] of the most recent bearish candle within the scan-lookback parameter when the zone-creation setting is enabled, the cooldown is clear, consecutive bullish chart bars equal the impulse-count parameter, and volume[1] > SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] × volume threshold; start the cooldown when such an opposite candle is found, even if its range overlaps an existing demand zone, and add it only if it overlaps none. Each bar, latch left after low > top and liquidity-qualified after front sell-side liquidity > top; emit a test event when freshness is enabled, the zone was left, and low <= top; delete when close < bottom or on a test event. Process zones before discarding the oldest if their count exceeds the zone-retention limit.
Calculation: Supply zones = ranges [low, high] of the most recent bullish candle within the scan-lookback parameter when the zone-creation setting is enabled, the cooldown is clear, consecutive bearish chart bars equal the impulse-count parameter, and volume[1] > SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] × volume threshold; start the cooldown when such an opposite candle is found, even if its range overlaps an existing supply zone, and add it only if it overlaps none. Each bar, latch left after high < bottom and liquidity-qualified after front buy-side liquidity < bottom; emit a test event when freshness is enabled, the zone was left, and high >= bottom; delete when close > top or on a test event. Process zones before discarding the oldest if their count exceeds the zone-retention limit.
Calculation: Marked demand zones = retained, liquidity-qualified demand zones with top < close, ranked by descending top and limited to the nearest-zone-count parameter; a zone is armed when front sell-side liquidity is absent or <= top.
Calculation: Marked supply zones = retained, liquidity-qualified supply zones with bottom > close, ranked by ascending bottom and limited to the nearest-zone-count parameter; a zone is armed when front buy-side liquidity is absent or >= bottom.
