Calculation: Long signal trendline = the straight line through the two most recent qualifying pivot lows since the most recent qualifying pivot high, extended to the current chart bar
Calculation: Short signal trendline = the straight line through the two most recent qualifying pivot highs since the most recent qualifying pivot low, extended to the current chart bar
Calculation: Signal zone = [signal pivot price - ATR(ATR lookback) * zone buffer, signal pivot price + ATR(ATR lookback) * zone buffer], with ATR evaluated on the signal confirmation bar
Calculation: Supply zone = signal zone from a qualifying pivot high; starts active, becomes inactive when high > its upper bound, and reactivates on a later bar when close < its upper bound
Calculation: Demand zone = signal zone from a qualifying pivot low; starts active, becomes inactive when low < its lower bound, and reactivates on a later bar when close > its lower bound
Long: Chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND PivotLow(low, left lookback, right lookback) is confirmed AND volume[right lookback] > SMA(volume, shared average lookback)[right lookback] * volume multiplier AND (high[right lookback] - low[right lookback]) > SMA(high - low, shared average lookback)[right lookback] * range multiplier AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close[right lookback] > EMA(close, EMA lookback)[right lookback])
Short: Chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND PivotHigh(high, left lookback, right lookback) is confirmed AND volume[right lookback] > SMA(volume, shared average lookback)[right lookback] * volume multiplier AND (high[right lookback] - low[right lookback]) > SMA(high - low, shared average lookback)[right lookback] * range multiplier AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close[right lookback] < EMA(close, EMA lookback)[right lookback])
Exit: long, Chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND a long signal trendline exists AND close < long signal trendline
Exit: short, Chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND a short signal trendline exists AND close > short signal trendline
Exit: long, Chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND mitigation zones are enabled AND ghost exits are enabled AND a supply zone was inactive before the current bar and remains inactive after its reactivation check AND high >= the zone lower bound AND low <= the zone upper bound
Exit: short, Chart timeframe is T1, T2, or T3 AND mitigation zones are enabled AND ghost exits are enabled AND a demand zone was inactive before the current bar and remains inactive after its reactivation check AND low <= the zone upper bound AND high >= the zone lower bound
