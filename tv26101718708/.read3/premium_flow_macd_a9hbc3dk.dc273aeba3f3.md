Calculation: Active low sweep = set when low < lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback bars; remain valid on that bar and the next maximum post-sweep-bars chart bars; restart the count on each new low sweep; clear after a long signal or when the count exceeds the maximum post-sweep bars.
Calculation: Active high sweep = set when high > highest high over the preceding sweep lookback bars; remain valid on that bar and the next maximum post-sweep-bars chart bars; restart the count on each new high sweep; clear after a short signal or when the count exceeds the maximum post-sweep bars.
Calculation: Projected MACD histogram = MACD histogram + (linear regression of MACD histogram over the projection slope lookback evaluated now − that regression value one bar ago) × projection bars.
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low + epsilon).
Long: Active low sweep AND close > open AND (candle body ratio > displacement ratio OR volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) × volume multiplier) AND ((MACD histogram(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, signal EMA lookback) < zero tolerance AND projected MACD histogram > 0) OR MACD histogram crosses above 0) AND MACD histogram < maximum entry histogram distance AND MACD histogram > its value one chart bar ago AND (close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is confirmed).
Short: Active high sweep AND close < open AND (candle body ratio > displacement ratio OR volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) × volume multiplier) AND ((MACD histogram(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, signal EMA lookback) > −zero tolerance AND projected MACD histogram < 0) OR MACD histogram crosses below 0) AND MACD histogram > −maximum entry histogram distance AND MACD histogram < its value one chart bar ago AND (close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is confirmed).
