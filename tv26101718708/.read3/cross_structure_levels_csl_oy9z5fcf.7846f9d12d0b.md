Calculation: Cross level price = close(T1, bar still forming) in confirmation-close mode, or (fast MA + slow MA) / 2 in MA-cross-value mode; each MA is the selected EMA or SMA of close over its respective lookback using the selected session data from T1.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar within the configured America/New_York active session and, when today-only is enabled, on the current date, with bullish levels enabled, crossover(fast MA, slow MA), using T1 values from the bar still forming and sampled on chart bars.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar within the configured America/New_York active session and, when today-only is enabled, on the current date, with bearish levels enabled, crossunder(fast MA, slow MA), using T1 values from the bar still forming and sampled on chart bars.
Long: With retests enabled, for each active bullish level P created by a Long condition, after a prior chart-bar close > P + invalidation buffer and before it has been retested, chart-bar low <= P AND chart-bar close > P.
Short: With retests enabled, for each active bearish level P created by a Short condition, after a prior chart-bar close < P - invalidation buffer and before it has been retested, chart-bar high >= P AND chart-bar close < P.
