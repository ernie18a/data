Calculation: RS rating = clamp(RS midpoint + RS multiplier × ((close / close[short-return lookback] − 1) × 100), 0, 100)
Calculation: score = count of true conditions among: close > SMA(close, short MA lookback), SMA(close, medium MA lookback), and SMA(close, long MA lookback); SMA(close, short MA lookback) > SMA(close, medium MA lookback) > SMA(close, long MA lookback); SMA(close, long MA lookback)[k] > SMA(close, long MA lookback)[k+1] for every k from 0 through slow-MA-uptrend bar count − 1; close ≥ lowest(low, annual lookback) × (1 + percent-above-annual-low / 100); close ≥ highest(high, annual lookback) × (1 − percent-below-annual-high / 100); RS rating ≥ minimum RS rating; volume ≥ SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier; close > close[annual lookback] and close > close[short-return lookback]. The short, medium, and long MA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest.
Long: score equals the total number of criteria
Exit: long, score ≤ exit-score threshold
