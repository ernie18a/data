Calculation: turtle soup = on a confirmed source bar, long when low < a retained confirmed pivot low and close > that level, short when high > a retained confirmed pivot high and close < that level; scan chart bars when enabled using the chart pivot lookback, and scan each configured higher timeframe T1–T5 on its own bars using the separate higher-timeframe pivot lookback, reading only its last completed bar on chart bars; retain levels up to the per-source active-level limit and soups up to the pending-soup limit, dropping the oldest at each limit; if multiple levels qualify on one source bar, use the level with the latest pivot origin
Long: a confirmed chart bar forms a bullish FVG when low > high[2] and low - high[2] > ATR(short lookback on chart bars) × minimum gap ATR multiplier, and its zone passes the configured same-direction overlap filter when enabled
Short: a confirmed chart bar forms a bearish FVG when high < low[2] and low[2] - high > ATR(short lookback on chart bars) × minimum gap ATR multiplier, and its zone passes the configured same-direction overlap filter when enabled
Long: a retained bearish FVG becomes a bullish IFVG when a confirmed chart close > its top, and the inverted zone passes the configured same-direction overlap filter when enabled
Short: a retained bullish FVG becomes a bearish IFVG when a confirmed chart close < its bottom, and the inverted zone passes the configured same-direction overlap filter when enabled
Calculation: delivery state and reclaimed opening = on confirmed chart bars, track runs of bullish bodies (close > open) and bearish bodies (close < open), ignoring doji bars; after a run reaches the minimum-run parameter, retain its first open; bullish candidate when close > latest bearish-run open + ATR(long lookback on chart bars) × reclaim-clearance parameter, bearish candidate when close < latest bullish-run open - the same product; update state and reclaimed opening when a candidate's direction differs from the state or its opening differs from the reclaimed opening, giving the bullish candidate priority if both update; otherwise retain state and opening
Long: delivery state updates to bullish
Short: delivery state updates to bearish
Calculation: running sweep extreme = for a long soup, the lowest of its source-bar low and subsequent chart lows while pending or held; for a short soup, the highest of its source-bar high and subsequent chart highs while pending or held
Long: a bullish FVG or bullish IFVG forms 1 through the entry-window parameter chart bars after a matching long soup is registered, its entire zone lies above the soup level plus the entry-clearance parameter in minimum ticks, it passes the same-direction overlap filter when enabled, the current bar is permitted by the configured New York session gate, and the delivery filter is disabled or delivery state is not bearish or close > reclaimed opening; use the most recent eligible soup and consume it on this entry
Short: a bearish FVG or bearish IFVG forms 1 through the entry-window parameter chart bars after a matching short soup is registered, its entire zone lies below the soup level minus the entry-clearance parameter in minimum ticks, it passes the same-direction overlap filter when enabled, the current bar is permitted by the configured New York session gate, and the delivery filter is disabled or delivery state is not bullish or close < reclaimed opening; use the most recent eligible soup and consume it on this entry
Long: a previously held qualifying long setup is within the hold-window parameter chart bars, delivery state is not bearish or close > reclaimed opening, and the current bar is permitted by the configured New York session gate; only the latest held setup remains eligible
Short: a previously held qualifying short setup is within the hold-window parameter chart bars, delivery state is not bullish or close < reclaimed opening, and the current bar is permitted by the configured New York session gate; only the latest held setup remains eligible
Calculation: initial stop = for long, min(running sweep extreme - stop-buffer parameter × minimum tick, entry price - minimum tick); for short, max(running sweep extreme + stop-buffer parameter × minimum tick, entry price + minimum tick)
Calculation: target price = for long, entry price + abs(entry price - initial stop) × target-R parameter; for short, entry price - abs(entry price - initial stop) × target-R parameter
Calculation: breakeven price = entry price + (target price - entry price) × breakeven fraction for long, or entry price - (entry price - target price) × breakeven fraction for short; disabled when the fraction is zero
Calculation: active stop = entry price after price first reaches breakeven price (long high >= breakeven price, short low <= breakeven price), otherwise initial stop; manage only the latest entry signal and replace its levels on every newer entry signal
Exit: long, on a subsequent confirmed chart bar, low <= active stop or (high >= target price and low > active stop)
Exit: short, on a subsequent confirmed chart bar, high >= active stop or (low <= target price and high < active stop)
