Calculation: Trend = on T1 bars, classify the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar as Super Bullish if close > EMA(close, fast lookback, T1) and EMA(close, fast lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, medium lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, major lookback, T1); else Bullish if that EMA stack holds; else Sideway Up if close > EMA(close, major lookback, T1) and (EMA(close, fast lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, medium lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) >= EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) or close >= EMA(close, filter lookback, T1)); else Sideway Down if close < EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) and close > EMA(close, major lookback, T1) and not (EMA(close, fast lookback, T1) <= EMA(close, medium lookback, T1) <= EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) <= EMA(close, filter lookback, T1)); else Super Bear if close < EMA(close, major lookback, T1); else Downtrend if close < EMA(close, filter lookback, T1); else Sideway. The separate EMA lookbacks are ordered fast to major from shorter to longer. Trend is computed on T1 bars and read on chart bars from the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar.
Calculation: Swing legs = count of confirmed pivot highs during a bullish Trend or confirmed pivot lows during a bearish Trend, using the same left and right lookbacks; reset the count whenever the bullish, bearish, or neutral regime changes. Computed on T1 bars and read on chart bars from the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar.
Calculation: Too far to chase = bullish or bearish Trend and (in ATR mode, directional distance from EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) divided by ATR(ATR lookback, T1) exceeds its ATR threshold; in percent mode, directional distance divided by EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) times 100 exceeds its percent threshold) OR active bullish or bearish regime age in T1 bars >= its threshold OR Swing legs >= its threshold. Directional distance is close minus EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) in a bullish Trend and EMA(close, slow lookback, T1) minus close in a bearish Trend. Computed on T1 bars and read on chart bars from the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar.
Long: Trend is Super Bullish, Bullish, or Sideway Up AND close >= EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) AND close >= EMA(close, major lookback, T1) AND not Too far to chase
Long: Trend is Super Bullish, Bullish, or Sideway Up now AND was not Super Bullish, Bullish, or Sideway Up on the prior chart bar
Long: Trend is Super Bullish, Bullish, or Sideway Up AND close >= EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) AND low <= EMA(close, fast lookback, T1) AND high >= EMA(close, fast lookback, T1)
Short: Trend is Downtrend, Super Bear, or Sideway Down now AND was not Downtrend, Super Bear, or Sideway Down on the prior chart bar
Exit: long, close < EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) for at least the cut-threshold consecutive T1 bars
Exit: short, close < EMA(close, filter lookback, T1) for at least the cut-threshold consecutive T1 bars
