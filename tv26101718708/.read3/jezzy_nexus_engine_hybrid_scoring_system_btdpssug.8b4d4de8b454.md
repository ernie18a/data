Calculation: Session = chart timeframe is T3 OR bar open time (UTC) is in [London open, London close) OR bar open time (UTC) is in [New York open, New York close)
Calculation: Bullish CISD+FVG = close > Lowest(low, swing lookback)[1] AND low - high[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum FVG (ATR multiple)
Calculation: Bearish CISD+FVG = close < Highest(high, swing lookback)[1] AND low[2] - high >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum FVG (ATR multiple)
Calculation: Lower rejection candle = lower wick >= body * wick ratio AND body >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum body (ATR multiple) AND lower wick >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum wick (ATR multiple), where body = |close - open| and lower wick = min(open, close) - low
Calculation: Upper rejection candle = upper wick >= body * wick ratio AND body >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum body (ATR multiple) AND upper wick >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum wick (ATR multiple), where body = |close - open| and upper wick = high - max(open, close)
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = for some i from 0 to engulfing lookback - 1: close[i] > open[i] AND close[i+1] < open[i+1] AND close[i] > open[i+1] AND open[i] < close[i+1] AND close[i] - open[i] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum engulfing body (ATR multiple)
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = for some i from 0 to engulfing lookback - 1: close[i] < open[i] AND close[i+1] > open[i+1] AND close[i] < open[i+1] AND open[i] > close[i+1] AND open[i] - close[i] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum engulfing body (ATR multiple)
Calculation: Bull IFVG touch = for some bull IFVG zone remaining after step (c) of this bar, and some k from 0 to IFVG touch lookback - 1, low[k] <= zone top AND high[k] >= zone bottom; where, on each bar in the order (a), (b), (c): (a) if Bearish CISD+FVG is true, add to a zone list a zone with top = low[2], bottom = high and midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2, evaluated on this bar, keeping only the newest zone capacity zones; (b) move each zone in the zone list whose close > midpoint into a bull IFVG list, keeping only the newest IFVG capacity zones; (c) delete each bull IFVG zone whose close < bottom
Calculation: Bear IFVG touch = for some bear IFVG zone remaining after step (c) of this bar, and some k from 0 to IFVG touch lookback - 1, low[k] <= zone top AND high[k] >= zone bottom; where, on each bar in the order (a), (b), (c): (a) if Bullish CISD+FVG is true, add to a zone list a zone with top = low, bottom = high[2] and midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2, evaluated on this bar, keeping only the newest zone capacity zones; (b) move each zone in the zone list whose close < midpoint into a bear IFVG list, keeping only the newest IFVG capacity zones; (c) delete each bear IFVG zone whose close > top
Calculation: Bull point count = number of the following seven that hold: (1) MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, MACD signal lookback) line > its signal line; (2) at least two of: Parabolic SAR(slowest start, slowest increment, SAR max) < close, Parabolic SAR(middle start, middle increment, SAR max) < close, Parabolic SAR(fastest start, fastest increment, SAR max) < close, where the slowest, middle and fastest parameter sets have increasing start and increment; (3) close[i] > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback)[i] for every i from 0 to confirmation count - 1, AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback); (4) chart timeframe is greater than T2 OR EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar); (5) Bullish CISD+FVG; (6) Bull IFVG touch; (7) close > Linear regression(low, LinReg low lookback) AND Linear regression(low, LinReg low lookback) > Linear regression(low, LinReg low lookback)[1]
Calculation: Bear point count = number of the following seven that hold: (1) MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, MACD signal lookback) line < its signal line; (2) at least two of: Parabolic SAR(slowest start, slowest increment, SAR max) > close, Parabolic SAR(middle start, middle increment, SAR max) > close, Parabolic SAR(fastest start, fastest increment, SAR max) > close, where the slowest, middle and fastest parameter sets have increasing start and increment; (3) close[i] < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback)[i] for every i from 0 to confirmation count - 1, AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback); (4) chart timeframe is greater than T2 OR EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar); (5) Bearish CISD+FVG; (6) Bear IFVG touch; (7) close < Linear regression(high, LinReg high lookback) AND Linear regression(high, LinReg high lookback) < Linear regression(high, LinReg high lookback)[1]
