Calculation: Setup quality = round(100 × (sweep-depth weight × min(1, normalized sweep depth ÷ sweep-depth cap) + displacement weight × min(1, normalized displacement ÷ displacement cap) + imbalance weight × min(1, normalized FVG size ÷ imbalance cap) + speed weight × max(0, 1 − bars from sweep to shift ÷ maximum bars sweep-to-MSS))), where normalized sweep depth = abs(sweep extreme − swept pivot) ÷ max(ATR(ATR lookback), instrument minimum tick size), normalized displacement = abs(shift-bar close − captured internal level) ÷ the same normalizer, and normalized FVG size = FVG width ÷ the same normalizer, or zero if no FVG exists.
Long: On a completed T1 bar (the scan timeframe, or the chart timeframe if unset), while scanning with no active setup or trade and no terminal result still held, the latest confirmed, untaken pivot low of the liquidity swing length is swept (low < pivot low AND close > pivot low) AND the latest confirmed pivot high of the separate internal swing length is above close.
Short: On a completed T1 bar, while scanning with no active setup or trade and no terminal result still held, the latest confirmed, untaken pivot high of the liquidity swing length is swept (high > pivot high AND close < pivot high) AND the latest confirmed pivot low of the separate internal swing length is below close AND no qualifying long sweep occurs on that bar.
Long: After a qualifying long sweep, within the maximum bars sweep-to-MSS and before any close < sweep low, close > the internal pivot high captured at the sweep; choose the most recent bullish three-bar FVG with low[j] > high[j+2] for j from zero through min(bars from sweep to shift + FVG search buffer, maximum FVG search bars), using low[j] as entry, or use the captured internal pivot high if none exists; set stop to sweep low; require entry > stop, abs(entry − stop) > max(ATR(ATR lookback), instrument minimum tick size) × minimum stop distance in ATR, and setup quality ≥ minimum quality.
Short: After a qualifying short sweep, within the maximum bars sweep-to-MSS and before any close > sweep high, close < the internal pivot low captured at the sweep; choose the most recent bearish three-bar FVG with high[j] < low[j+2] over the same search range, using high[j] as entry, or use the captured internal pivot low if none exists; set stop to sweep high; require entry < stop, abs(entry − stop) > max(ATR(ATR lookback), instrument minimum tick size) × minimum stop distance in ATR, and setup quality ≥ minimum quality.
Long: After a valid long setup is mapped, on a completed T1 bar within the maximum bars setup-to-entry, close ≥ stop AND low ≤ entry.
Short: After a valid short setup is mapped, on a completed T1 bar within the maximum bars setup-to-entry, close ≤ stop AND high ≥ entry.
Exit: long, on a later completed T1 bar while open, low ≤ sweep low OR (high ≥ entry + abs(entry − sweep low) × take-profit R AND low > sweep low) OR (bars in trade > maximum bars in trade AND low > sweep low AND high < entry + abs(entry − sweep low) × take-profit R); stop takes precedence when stop and target are both touched.
Exit: short, on a later completed T1 bar while open, high ≥ sweep high OR (low ≤ entry − abs(entry − sweep high) × take-profit R AND high < sweep high) OR (bars in trade > maximum bars in trade AND high < sweep high AND low > entry − abs(entry − sweep high) × take-profit R); stop takes precedence when stop and target are both touched.
