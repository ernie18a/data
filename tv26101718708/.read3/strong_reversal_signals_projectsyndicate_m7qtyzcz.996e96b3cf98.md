Calculation: liquidity levels = selected Macro Pivots uses the latest confirmed pivot high and low with equal left and right lookbacks of floor(pivot-length parameter / 2); selected HTF Previous Bar uses the previous completed T1 bar high and low, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars at each T1 start; selected Session High/Low uses the latest completed configured session’s high and low, accumulated on chart bars and held after the session ends; a new level resets its swept flag, an opposite sweep replaces a pending sweep, and an HTF or session level reset clears a pending sweep
Calculation: rejection-wick fractions = upper (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) and lower (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), with both fractions zero when high equals low
Calculation: reversal strength = score-scale parameter × weighted mean of trend weight × clamp((directional EMA separation / chart ATR + trend offset parameter) / trend divisor parameter), wick weight × clamp(sweep-bar rejection-wick fraction / wick divisor parameter), calm weight × clamp(1 - PercentRank(chart ATR, percentile lookback parameter) / percentile divisor parameter), proximity weight × clamp(1 - max(directional EMA alignment, 0) / proximity divisor parameter), and sweep weight × clamp(abs(sweep extreme - swept level) / (chart ATR × sweep divisor parameter)); clamp each factor to the unit interval, divide the weighted sum by the sum of the five separate weights, or use zero when their sum is zero; chart ATR is ATR(chart lookback parameter), falling back to cumulative mean(high - low) from the first chart bar when ATR is unavailable; directional EMA separation is EMA(close, shorter lookback) - EMA(close, longer lookback) for short and the reverse for long; directional EMA alignment is (EMA(close, longer lookback) - entry close) / chart ATR for short and (entry close - EMA(close, longer lookback)) / chart ATR for long
Calculation: star tier = highest qualifying tier threshold parameter’s tier, or tier zero if none qualifies; thresholds descend from tier four to tier one
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, after an unswept liquidity low is swept by close < liquidity low and lower rejection-wick fraction × 100 >= rejection-wick threshold, enter at close when the bar low is above the running sweep low; require ADR(T2, ADR lookback parameter, completed bars only) > 0, calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, and star tier(reversal strength for long using entry close and the sweep wick, extreme, and level) >= minimum-stars parameter
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, after an unswept liquidity high is swept by close > liquidity high and upper rejection-wick fraction × 100 >= rejection-wick threshold, enter at close when the bar high is below the running sweep high; require ADR(T2, ADR lookback parameter, completed bars only) > 0, calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, and star tier(reversal strength for short using entry close and the sweep wick, extreme, and level) >= minimum-stars parameter
Exit: Long, on a confirmed chart bar after entry, low <= entry price - ADR(T2, ADR lookback parameter, completed bars only, sampled on chart bars, fixed at entry) × stop-percentage parameter / 100 OR TP1 has been reached since entry and high >= entry price + ADR at entry × TP2-percentage parameter / 100 OR elapsed chart bars since entry >= expiry-bars parameter
Exit: Short, on a confirmed chart bar after entry, high >= entry price + ADR(T2, ADR lookback parameter, completed bars only, sampled on chart bars, fixed at entry) × stop-percentage parameter / 100 OR TP1 has been reached since entry and low <= entry price - ADR at entry × TP2-percentage parameter / 100 OR elapsed chart bars since entry >= expiry-bars parameter
