Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum(high) over chart bars during the opening-range duration from session start
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum(low) over chart bars during the opening-range duration from session start
Long: During the session after the opening-range duration, close > opening-range high AND no earlier post-formation bar in the session closed above the opening-range high
Short: During the session after the opening-range duration, close < opening-range low AND no earlier post-formation bar in the session closed below the opening-range low
