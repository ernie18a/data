Calculation: Distribution-day count = count of market-index T1 bars within the configured lookback where the index close fell by at least the decline threshold and volume exceeded prior T1-bar volume, excluding a candidate day when the current index close is at least that day’s close times one plus the rally-expiry threshold; a zero rally-expiry threshold disables expiry.
Calculation: Power Trend = a state initialized OFF; on each market-index T1 bar, turn OFF if the shorter-lookback EMA of close is below the middle-lookback SMA of close, or if index close is below that SMA and below the highest high over the breaker lookback by at least the breaker drawdown threshold; otherwise turn ON when index low has held above that EMA for its required consecutive-bar count, that EMA has held above the middle SMA for its separate required consecutive-bar count, the middle SMA is rising, and index close is above its prior T1-bar close; otherwise retain the prior state. The turn-off condition takes priority.
Calculation: Market state = unknown if index close, the shorter-lookback EMA, the middle- or longer-lookback SMA, Power Trend, or distribution-day count is unavailable; otherwise confirmed uptrend if Power Trend is ON and distribution-day count is at or below its contained-days threshold; otherwise correction if Power Trend is OFF while index close is below the middle SMA, or distribution-day count exceeds its correction threshold; otherwise under pressure.
Calculation: CAN SLIM score = (number of passed checks, number of evaluable checks) from the following checks. T1 price and volume calculations run on T1 bars and are sampled onto chart bars, using the still-forming T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically. Quarterly and annual report sequences advance when revenue changes if revenue is available, otherwise when EPS changes. Use diluted EPS when selected and available, otherwise basic EPS. A growth percentage is (current value / comparison value - 1), expressed as a percentage, and is evaluable only when both values exist and the comparison value is positive. Checks: (1) current quarterly EPS growth versus the same fiscal quarter a year earlier meets the quarterly EPS threshold, or comparison EPS is nonpositive and current EPS is positive; (2) the prior quarter’s EPS growth versus the same fiscal quarter a year earlier meets that same threshold, or its comparison EPS is nonpositive and prior-quarter EPS is positive; if the prior-quarter requirement is disabled, this check passes; (3) current quarterly revenue growth versus the same fiscal quarter a year earlier meets the separate quarterly revenue threshold; (4) every required consecutive annual EPS growth period among the latest annual reports meets the annual EPS threshold; (5) latest annual return on equity meets its threshold; (6) latest quarterly total debt / total equity does not exceed its threshold, with positive equity; (7) T1 close is no farther below the highest T1 high over the configured year-high lookback than the permitted percentage; (8) latest annual float shares do not exceed the float threshold; (9) quarterly shares outstanding growth versus the same fiscal quarter a year earlier does not exceed the dilution threshold; (10) over the configured T1 lookback, the sum of volume on up-closes divided by the sum of volume on down-closes meets its threshold, using the source’s fixed fallback ratio when down-volume is zero and up-volume is positive; (11) T1 close is above both the middle- and longer-lookback SMAs; (12) on chart bars, the T1 stock-close / T1 market-index-close ratio is no farther below its chart-bar high over the source-selected year-equivalent lookback than the permitted percentage; this check is evaluable only on one of the three supported non-intraday chart aggregations and when sufficient chart history exists; (13) the stock’s T1 weighted momentum score exceeds the market index’s score by the required margin, where each score sums returns at four successively longer, equally spaced lookbacks, with the shortest-lookback return weighted twice as heavily as each other return; (14) SMA of T1 close times volume over the middle lookback meets the dollar-volume threshold and T1 close meets the separate minimum-price threshold; (15) market-index close is above both the shorter-lookback EMA and middle-lookback SMA, and the middle-lookback SMA is above the longer-lookback SMA; (16) Power Trend is ON; (17) distribution-day count does not exceed its contained-days threshold. An unavailable required input makes its check unevaluable; unevaluable checks are excluded from both score counts.
Long: the CAN SLIM score’s passed-check count meets the minimum-pass parameter, its evaluable-check count meets the minimum-coverage parameter, and passed checks divided by evaluable checks meets the minimum-pass-percentage parameter, AND the market gate is disabled or the market state is not blocked by the selected gate mode (correction only, or correction and under pressure).
Long: the CAN SLIM score meets the same minimum-pass, minimum-coverage, and minimum-pass-percentage parameters, AND the market gate is enabled AND the market state is blocked by the selected gate mode (correction only, or correction and under pressure).
