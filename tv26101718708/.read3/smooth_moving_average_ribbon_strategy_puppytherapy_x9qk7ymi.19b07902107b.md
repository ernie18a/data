Calculation: Heikin-Ashi OHLC = HA close is (open + high + low + close) / 4; HA open is open + close / 2 when the prior HA open is unavailable, otherwise (prior HA open + prior HA close) / 2; HA high is the maximum of high, HA open, and HA close; HA low is the minimum of low, HA open, and HA close
Calculation: Smoothed MA variant(source, lookback) = SMA(source, lookback) when the prior WMA(source, lookback) is unavailable; otherwise (prior WMA(source, lookback) × (lookback − 1) + source) / lookback
Calculation: Selected MA(source, lookback, type) = the selected SMA, EMA, DEMA, TEMA, WMA, VWMA, smoothed MA variant, Hull MA, least-squares MA, ALMA, KAMA, T3, zero-lag EMA, Henderson MA, Ahrens MA, or Jurik MA; least-squares MA has an offset parameter, ALMA has separate offset and sigma parameters, KAMA has separate fast and slow parameters, T3 has a factor parameter, and Jurik MA has separate phase and power parameters
Calculation: Ribbon open and close = optionally transform chart OHLC to Heikin-Ashi OHLC; apply the selected MA variant independently to each OHLC series for the selected number of repeated passes, using one ribbon MA type and lookback; optionally transform the resulting OHLC to Heikin-Ashi OHLC again; take the resulting open and close
Calculation: Trend-filter MA = selected MA of Heikin-Ashi close when the HA-source toggle is on, otherwise the selected data-field source, using a filter MA type and lookback separate from the ribbon MA parameters
Calculation: Long filter = filter disabled OR (filter enabled AND ((rising/falling logic enabled AND trend-filter MA is rising over the trend lookback) OR (rising/falling logic disabled AND HA-source enabled AND Heikin-Ashi close ≠ 0) OR (rising/falling logic disabled AND HA-source disabled AND close > trend-filter MA)))
Calculation: Short filter = filter disabled OR (filter enabled AND ((rising/falling logic enabled AND trend-filter MA is falling over the trend lookback) OR (rising/falling logic disabled AND HA-source enabled AND Heikin-Ashi close ≠ 0) OR (rising/falling logic disabled AND HA-source disabled AND close < trend-filter MA)))
Long: Ribbon close crosses above ribbon open AND Long filter
Short: Ribbon close crosses below ribbon open AND Short filter
Exit: Long, ribbon close crosses below ribbon open AND NOT Short filter
Exit: Short, ribbon close crosses above ribbon open AND NOT Long filter
