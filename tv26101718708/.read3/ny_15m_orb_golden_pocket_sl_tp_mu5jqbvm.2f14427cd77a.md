Calculation: Opening range high = maximum chart-bar high during each configured opening-range session, held until the next session
Calculation: Opening range low = minimum chart-bar low during each configured opening-range session, held until the next session
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close - open) / (high - low) on each chart bar when high > low, otherwise 0
