Calculation: Active zones = chart-timeframe swing-pivot zones around confirmed pivot highs and lows, plus T1 and T2 swing-pivot zones; order-block zones from an opposite-color prior candle followed by a move exceeding the move threshold, volume exceeding the average-volume lookback times the volume multiplier, and a close beyond the highs or lows of bars [2] through [5]; volume-POC zones at the center of the highest-volume bin across the price bins spanning the lookback highs and lows, classified by close relative to the center; fair-value-gap zones where low > high[2] or high < low[2], the middle candle body exceeds its ATR threshold, and the gap exceeds its price-relative size threshold; resistance at a lookback high and support at a lookback low when at least the required number of highs or lows fall within the close-relative equality tolerance; T1 and T2 prior highs and lows, and premarket highs and lows; gap zones when low > high[1] or high < low[1] and gap size exceeds its ATR threshold; and anchored-VWAP zones, classified support when close < anchored VWAP and resistance otherwise. Swing-zone widths use ATR times the configured width and mode scale, with separate T1 and T2 width multipliers; low-price mode uses close times the configured percentage width. Order-block zones span the prior candle body; volume-POC zones span the highest-volume bin; gap zones span the gap; other zone widths use their detector's configured width. T1, T2, and T3 values are requested on chart bars with lookahead off: historical chart bars use the latest completed bar of each timeframe and realtime chart bars use its forming bar. T1 and T2 swing pivots use their respective timeframe bars; T3 bias uses its timeframe bars. Zones of the same polarity whose midpoints fall within the merge distance are merged. In-zone bars increment touches and update average touch volume relative to its moving average. After the configured confirmation bars from a touch, a close beyond the midpoint in the zone's expected bounce direction records a bounce. A close below a support bottom or above a resistance top records a violation; after the flip threshold the zone reverses polarity, and at the invalidation threshold it is removed. A touched fair-value-gap zone is removed after its pending bounce check resolves when filled gaps are not kept. When zone capacity is reached, evict the lowest-score unprotected zone first; protect T1 and T2 swing zones and key levels when possible.
Calculation: Adaptive zone weight = base weight unless the observed bounce and break counts each reach the learning sample threshold; otherwise clamp(base weight * (mean factor for first-bounce outcomes / mean factor for first-break outcomes) ^ dampening exponent, base weight * lower multiplier, base weight * upper multiplier). Apply separately to touch-volume, timeframe-origin, confluence, freshness, and structure factors; structure weight is zero when market structure is disabled.
Calculation: Zone score = clamp(100 * (touch-count factor * touch-count weight + touch-volume factor * adaptive touch-volume weight + creation-volume factor * creation-volume weight + timeframe-origin factor * adaptive timeframe weight + confluence factor * adaptive confluence weight + freshness factor * adaptive freshness weight + regime-fit factor * regime weight + structure-fit factor * adaptive structure weight - violation penalty * violation weight) / total active weights + bounce-history bonus + flipped-zone bonus, zero, maximum score). Touch-count factor is capped touches divided by its cap; volume factors are capped volume ratios divided by their caps; confluence factor is capped distinct detector sources plus overlapping zones divided by its cap; freshness is full when untouched and otherwise declines by bars since last touch to its floor. Timeframe-origin factors distinguish T1, T2, T3, and other origins. Regime fit favors support in bullish regimes and resistance in bearish regimes, gives neutral fit in range, and uses its configured squeeze and opposing-regime factors. Structure fit favors support in bullish structure and resistance in bearish structure, gives neutral fit in range, and penalizes the opposing structure. Violation penalty increases with violations to its cap. Bounce-history bonus applies after the minimum observed outcomes and scales the difference of bounce share from neutral; flipped-zone bonus applies after the flip-count threshold.
Calculation: Market regime = squeeze when Bollinger Band Width percentile rank over its lookback is below its threshold AND ATR-as-percent-of-close percentile rank is below its threshold; otherwise strong bull when ADX exceeds its strong threshold AND the configured EMA slope is above its strong bullish threshold; otherwise weak bull when ADX exceeds its weak threshold AND EMA slope is above its weak bullish threshold; otherwise strong bear when ADX exceeds its strong threshold AND EMA slope is below its strong bearish threshold; otherwise weak bear when ADX exceeds its weak threshold AND EMA slope is below its weak bearish threshold; otherwise range. EMA slope compares EMA(close, regime EMA lookback) with its value at the configured slope lookback.
Calculation: Market structure = range initially; on confirmed chart bars, close above the latest unbroken chart-timeframe pivot high sets bullish structure and close below the latest unbroken pivot low sets bearish structure. Pivot highs and lows use the configured left and right lookbacks. An upward break is a change-of-character event when prior structure was bearish and otherwise a continuation break; a downward break is a change-of-character event when prior structure was bullish and otherwise a continuation break.
Calculation: Long plan levels = entry at selected support-zone top plus ATR times the long entry offset; stop at its bottom minus ATR times the long stop offset; target at the nearest resistance-zone midpoint above entry whose score meets the target-zone threshold; risk = absolute value of entry minus stop; reward = target minus entry; reward-to-risk = reward / risk.
Calculation: Short plan levels = entry at selected resistance-zone bottom minus ATR times the short entry offset; stop at its top plus ATR times the short stop offset; target at the nearest support-zone midpoint below entry whose score meets the target-zone threshold; risk = absolute value of stop minus entry; reward = entry minus target; reward-to-risk = reward / risk.
