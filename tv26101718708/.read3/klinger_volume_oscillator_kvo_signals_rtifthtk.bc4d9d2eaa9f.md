Calculation: KVO spread = Klinger Volume Oscillator(fast EMA lookback, longer slow EMA lookback) - EMA(Klinger Volume Oscillator(fast EMA lookback, longer slow EMA lookback), separate shorter trigger lookback)
Calculation: Cross strength = absolute change in KVO spread over the strength lookback divided by KVO standard deviation over the normalization lookback, expressed as a percentage
Long: KVO spread crosses above zero AND KVO standard deviation over the normalization lookback is positive and available AND (zero-line filter is off OR KVO is below the negative signed zero-line-distance percentage of the cross-bar standard deviation) AND (run-up-gap filter is off OR the highest value of negative KVO spread over the run-up lookback before the cross, divided by the cross-bar standard deviation, meets the minimum run-up-gap percentage) AND (cross-strength filter is off OR Cross strength meets the minimum cross-strength threshold) AND (Donchian midline slope filter is off OR the current Donchian midline(high, low, channel lookback) minus its value at the slope lookback is at least negative flat tolerance in ticks times the instrument's minimum price tick) AND, after the confirmation delay, KVO spread stayed above zero on every bar from the cross through the signal bar AND signal-bar KVO spread is at least the minimum close-through percentage of signal-bar KVO standard deviation AND (signal-candle filter is off OR signal-bar close > open)
Short: KVO spread crosses below zero AND KVO standard deviation over the normalization lookback is positive and available AND (zero-line filter is off OR KVO is above the signed zero-line-distance percentage of the cross-bar standard deviation) AND (run-up-gap filter is off OR the highest value of KVO spread over the run-up lookback before the cross, divided by the cross-bar standard deviation, meets the minimum run-up-gap percentage) AND (cross-strength filter is off OR Cross strength meets the minimum cross-strength threshold) AND (Donchian midline slope filter is off OR the current Donchian midline(high, low, channel lookback) minus its value at the slope lookback is at most flat tolerance in ticks times the instrument's minimum price tick) AND, after the confirmation delay, KVO spread stayed below zero on every bar from the cross through the signal bar AND signal-bar KVO spread is at most the negative minimum close-through percentage of signal-bar KVO standard deviation AND (signal-candle filter is off OR signal-bar close < open)
