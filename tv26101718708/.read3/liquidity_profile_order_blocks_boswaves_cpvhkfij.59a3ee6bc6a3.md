Calculation: Order-block zones = on a confirmed pivot, bullish when Pivot Low(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) is confirmed and confirmation-bar close minus pivot low is at least ATR(ATR lookback)[pivot lookback] times the impulse multiple; bearish when Pivot High(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) is confirmed and pivot high minus confirmation-bar close is at least that amount. Bullish bounds are the lowest low and highest max(open, close); bearish bounds are the lowest min(open, close) and highest high, each across the base-candle lookback ending at the pivot. Symmetrically expand or contract bounds about their midpoint to clamp zone height between the minimum and maximum ATR multiples, using pivot-bar ATR floored at minimum tick.
Calculation: Eligible zones = remove the newest existing same-side overlapping zone when overlap removal is enabled; when at the zone limit, remove the oldest zone first. Invalidate bullish zones when the selected close or wick breaches the bottom, and bearish zones when it breaches the top. A zone starts unarmed and arms on a confirmed bar intersecting it when the previous chart bar did not; it stays armed until its signal, then disarms.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an armed bullish zone intersects the bar (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top), close > zone midpoint, AND (close[1] <= zone midpoint OR low <= zone midpoint).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an armed bearish zone intersects the bar (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top), close < zone midpoint, AND (close[1] >= zone midpoint OR high >= zone midpoint).
