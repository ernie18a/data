Calculation: Session VWAP deviation = sqrt(Σ from session start through current bar of [volume × (typical price − session VWAP on that bar)^2] / Σ from session start through current bar of volume), where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Upper VWAP band = session VWAP + band-deviation parameter × session VWAP deviation
Calculation: Lower VWAP band = session VWAP − band-deviation parameter × session VWAP deviation
Long: During the New York session from its open plus the delay parameter through session close, at least one of |low − session VWAP| ≤ proximity parameter × minimum tick, |high − session VWAP| ≤ proximity parameter × minimum tick, or low ≤ session VWAP ≤ high; close > upper VWAP band; close > open; close ≥ low + (high − low) × close-percentage parameter / 100; and low > lower VWAP band
Short: During the New York session from its open plus the delay parameter through session close, at least one of |low − session VWAP| ≤ proximity parameter × minimum tick, |high − session VWAP| ≤ proximity parameter × minimum tick, or low ≤ session VWAP ≤ high; close < lower VWAP band; close < open; close ≤ low + (high − low) × (100 − close-percentage parameter) / 100; and high < upper VWAP band
