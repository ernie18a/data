Calculation: Volatility bucket = compare ATR(ATR lookback)[1] / max(close[1], minimum price increment) with its lower, middle, and upper nearest-rank percentiles over the volatility lookback; assign the lowest, next, next, or highest bucket when below the corresponding cutoff, otherwise the highest bucket.
Calculation: Volatility adjustment factors = when the volatility fingerprint is disabled, use factor 1 for all adjustments; when enabled, use the bucket-specific band factor for the band multiplier, smoothing length, stall threshold, slope threshold, BPS threshold, breakout multiple, volume average, and range percentile, and use separate bucket-specific ATR-weight and price-move-weight factors for those weights; adjusted smoothing length is at least 1.
Calculation: Dynamic trendline = ATR is ATR(ATR lookback)[1]; price move is min(abs(close[1] - close[2]) / max(close[2], minimum price increment), nearest-rank percentile of that same move over the price-move lookback at the price-move cap percentile); raw half-band is (effective band multiplier + ATR / close[1] × effective ATR weight + price move × effective price-move weight) × ATR; smooth raw half-band recursively with alpha 2 / (effective smoothing length + 1), initializing to raw half-band; upper and lower bands are (high[1] + low[1]) / 2 plus or minus the smoothed half-band; initialize the trendline to the upper band, then set it to max(lower band, prior trendline) when close[1] > prior trendline, otherwise min(upper band, prior trendline).
Calculation: Filter guard = any enabled filter is flat AND breakout override is not engaged; stall is abs(Dynamic trendline[1] - Dynamic trendline[2]) < ATR × effective stall threshold; slope is abs(LinReg(Dynamic trendline[1], regression length, offset 0) - LinReg(Dynamic trendline[1], regression length, offset 1)) / max(ATR, minimum price increment) < effective slope threshold; volume is volume[1] <= SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] × effective volume factor; range is high[1] - low[1] <= nearest-rank percentile of the prior-bar high-low range series over the range lookback at the range percentile × effective range factor; BPS delta is abs(Dynamic trendline[1] - Dynamic trendline[2]) / max(close[2], minimum price increment) × 10000 < effective BPS threshold; breakout override is enabled AND abs(close[1] - close[2]) > ATR × effective breakout multiple.
Calculation: Displayed direction = initialize bullish; when Filter guard is true, retain the prior displayed direction; otherwise set bullish when close[1] > Dynamic trendline[1], bearish otherwise.
Long: Displayed direction changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Displayed direction changes from bullish to bearish.
