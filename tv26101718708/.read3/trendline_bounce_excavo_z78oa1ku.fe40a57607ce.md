Calculation: Validated support/resistance line = use the most recent stored quality PivotLow(low, quality lookback, quality lookback) pivots for support or PivotHigh(high, quality lookback, quality lookback) pivots for resistance, up to the pivot-buffer capacity; for each anchor pivot and each other pivot, define slope = (priceB - priceA) / (barB - barA) and projected price = priceA + slope * (bar - barA). Count same-side pivots within ATR(ATR lookback) * touch-tolerance multiplier of the projected price; retain candidates with count >= minimum touches, abs(slope) <= ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum-slope multiplier, and all same-side pivots on the nonbreaking side of the line within tolerance. Choose the candidate with the greatest touch count for each anchor. A resistance line requires every quality pivot high <= projected price + tolerance; a support line requires every quality pivot low >= projected price - tolerance. Reject lines whose first and last touch pivots are less than minimum span bars apart, whose last-touch price differs from current close by more than ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum-distance multiplier, or whose sampled highs (resistance) exceed projected price + tolerance or sampled lows (support) fall below projected price - tolerance between first and last touches; sample with bar steps max(1, floor(span / sample-count)). Process anchors newest first, accept at most maximum lines per side, and do not reuse touch pivots from an accepted line.
Long: The latest quality-pivot touch of a validated support line is within quality lookback bars of the current bar.
Short: The latest quality-pivot touch of a validated resistance line is within quality lookback bars of the current bar.
Long: (the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, fast lookback, fast lookback) is within fast lookback bars of the current bar and within ATR(ATR lookback) * touch-tolerance multiplier of a validated support line OR current low is within ATR(ATR lookback) * touch-tolerance multiplier of a validated support line's projected price OR the latest quality-pivot touch of a validated support line is within quality lookback bars of the current bar) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND (ATR regime filter disabled OR PercentRank(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-rank lookback) / 100 < ATR-rank threshold) AND (bar confirmed OR repainting enabled); fast lookback is shorter than quality lookback.
Short: (the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, fast lookback, fast lookback) is within fast lookback bars of the current bar and within ATR(ATR lookback) * touch-tolerance multiplier of a validated resistance line OR current high is within ATR(ATR lookback) * touch-tolerance multiplier of a validated resistance line's projected price OR the latest quality-pivot touch of a validated resistance line is within quality lookback bars of the current bar) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND (ATR regime filter disabled OR PercentRank(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-rank lookback) / 100 < ATR-rank threshold) AND (bar confirmed OR repainting enabled); fast lookback is shorter than quality lookback.
