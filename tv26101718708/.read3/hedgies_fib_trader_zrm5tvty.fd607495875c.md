Calculation: swing anchors = on chart bars, retain each latest non-null T1 pivot high or low using the same left/right lookback parameter, requested with lookahead on from the T1 bar still forming; after the shared stale-age limit, counted in chart bars, replace each stale anchor with its corresponding rolling T1 highest or lowest using the fallback lookback parameter, requested with lookahead off from the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = for each of four separately configured retracement fractions, bullish level = swing high - (swing high - swing low) * fraction; bearish level = swing low + (swing high - swing low) * fraction.
Calculation: bullish candle pattern = (close > open AND abs(close - open) > (high - low) * body-fraction parameter) OR (close > open AND open - low > abs(close - open) * wick-to-body parameter) OR (close > open AND abs(close - open) > abs(close[1] - open[1]) AND close[1] < open[1]).
Calculation: bearish candle pattern = (close < open AND abs(close - open) > (high - low) * body-fraction parameter) OR (close < open AND high - open > abs(close - open) * wick-to-body parameter) OR (close < open AND abs(close - open) > abs(close[1] - open[1]) AND close[1] > open[1]).
Calculation: initial risk distance = ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback parameter) on the entry-signal bar * stop-distance multiplier.
Long: flat AND swing high > swing low AND T1 close from the last completed T1 bar > EMA(T1 close, trend lookback parameter) from the T1 bar still forming, both sampled on chart bars AND chart close is within ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback parameter) * fib-zone-width multiplier of any bullish Fibonacci level AND bullish candle pattern AND (EMA confirmation disabled OR chart close > EMA(chart close, separate confirmation lookback parameter)) AND RSI(chart close, RSI lookback parameter) < configured overbought threshold AND RSI(chart close, RSI lookback parameter) > separate lower RSI-bound parameter AND (session filter disabled OR bar hour >= configured session start hour AND bar hour < configured session end hour) AND datetime is within configured start and end date parameters AND flat.
Short: flat AND swing high > swing low AND T1 close from the last completed T1 bar < EMA(T1 close, trend lookback parameter) from the T1 bar still forming, both sampled on chart bars AND chart close is within ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback parameter) * fib-zone-width multiplier of any bearish Fibonacci level AND bearish candle pattern AND (EMA confirmation disabled OR chart close < EMA(chart close, separate confirmation lookback parameter)) AND RSI(chart close, RSI lookback parameter) > configured oversold threshold AND RSI(chart close, RSI lookback parameter) < separate upper RSI-bound parameter AND (session filter disabled OR bar hour >= configured session start hour AND bar hour < configured session end hour) AND datetime is within configured start and end date parameters AND flat.
Exit: Long, low <= entry-signal close - initial risk distance OR high >= entry-signal close + initial risk distance * first target ratio OR high >= entry-signal close + initial risk distance * second target ratio OR (EOD close enabled AND bar hour = configured close hour AND bar minute >= configured close minute).
Exit: Short, high >= entry-signal close + initial risk distance OR low <= entry-signal close - initial risk distance * first target ratio OR low <= entry-signal close - initial risk distance * second target ratio OR (EOD close enabled AND bar hour = configured close hour AND bar minute >= configured close minute).
