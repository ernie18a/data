Calculation: Reaction ATR = ATR(reaction lookback) when session adaptation is off; otherwise use the recursively updated true-range average with alpha 1/reaction lookback for the active New York, London, or Asia session, in that priority order, falling back to ATR(reaction lookback).
Calculation: Bullish reaction = close > open AND (lower wick >= body × rejection ratio AND close location >= strong-close threshold OR range >= Reaction ATR × displacement multiple AND body/range >= displacement-body threshold OR close > highest(high, microstructure lookback, excluding current bar) OR relative-volume reactions are enabled AND volume >= SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) × volume multiple AND close location >= strong-close threshold).
Calculation: Bearish reaction = close < open AND (upper wick >= body × rejection ratio AND close location <= 1 − strong-close threshold OR range >= Reaction ATR × displacement multiple AND body/range >= displacement-body threshold OR close < lowest(low, microstructure lookback, excluding current bar) OR relative-volume reactions are enabled AND volume >= SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) × volume multiple AND close location <= 1 − strong-close threshold).
Calculation: Active zones(T) = on T bars, support bottom is the low of the latest confirmed pivot low with pivot-strength bars on each side, and support top is max(min(open, close) at that pivot, bottom + ATR(zone lookback) at that pivot × zone padding); resistance top is the high of the latest confirmed pivot high with the same confirmation, and resistance bottom is min(max(open, close) at that pivot, top − ATR(zone lookback) at that pivot × zone padding). Replace each zone on a newer confirmed pivot of its type and remove support when close < support bottom or resistance when close > resistance top. For T other than the chart interval, calculate on T bars and sample the returned values on chart bars with lookahead off: completed T values historically and the forming T value in real time.
Calculation: Bullish zone defense(T) = a valid active zone with close > top AND ((Bullish reaction AND (low <= top OR close[1] <= top)) OR (low <= top AND close[1] > top AND close > open)).
Calculation: Bearish zone defense(T) = a valid active zone with close < bottom AND ((Bearish reaction AND (high >= bottom OR close[1] >= bottom)) OR (high >= bottom AND close[1] < bottom AND close < open)).
Calculation: First zone defense(T, side) = the first confirmed bullish or bearish zone defense since that zone became active or was replaced; reset when the zone is invalidated or replaced. A defense is confirmed only on a closed chart bar when close confirmation is enabled.
Long: On chart interval T1, first bullish defense of the active chart support zone AND NOT first bearish defense of the active chart resistance zone.
Short: On chart interval T1, first bearish defense of the active chart resistance zone AND NOT first bullish defense of the active chart support zone.
Exit: long, after a long marker, the support zone is replaced or close < the support bottom saved at that marker.
Exit: short, after a short marker, the resistance zone is replaced or close > the resistance top saved at that marker.
Calculation: Bias pair(T) = close(T) and EMA(close(T), bias lookback), calculated on T bars and sampled on chart bars; use the last completed T bar when confirmed-bias mode is enabled, otherwise use the forming T bar.
Long: For each displayed bias row at T1–T7, or the enabled dynamic chart-timeframe row when that interval is not represented, bias close >= bias EMA.
Short: For each displayed bias row at T1–T7, or the enabled dynamic chart-timeframe row when that interval is not represented, bias close < bias EMA.
Long: Among enabled majority rows T2–T7, at least one is enabled, bullish-row count >= min(minimum matching biases, max(enabled-row count, 1)), and bullish-row count > bearish-row count.
Short: Among enabled majority rows T2–T7, at least one is enabled, bearish-row count >= min(minimum matching biases, max(enabled-row count, 1)), and bearish-row count > bullish-row count.
Calculation: Structure AMD phase = on confirmed chart bars, maintain phase 0, +1, −1, +2, or −2, bullish/bearish armed states, and last context bar. Use chart zones plus the configured context-zone sources. Reset phase and armed states when context age exceeds its maximum. Then process, in order: both support and resistance first defenses set phase 0 and disarm both sides; support defense arms bullish and clears bearish (resetting a negative phase to 0); resistance defense arms bearish and clears bullish (resetting a positive phase to 0); a bullish sweep of any selected support zone (low < bottom, close > top, and Bullish reaction) alone sets phase +1 and arms bullish, while a bearish sweep of any selected resistance zone (high > top, close < bottom, and Bearish reaction) alone sets phase −1 and arms bearish; simultaneous sweeps choose +1 when close >= open, otherwise −1. A close crossing above a selected resistance's prior-bar top while bullish is armed sets phase +2 if expansion is not required or bullish expansion holds; a close crossing below a selected support's prior-bar bottom while bearish is armed sets phase −2 if expansion is not required or bearish expansion holds. Bullish expansion is bullish displacement or close > open with bullish microstructure shift; bearish expansion is bearish displacement or close < open with bearish microstructure shift. Simultaneous distributions choose +2 when close >= open, otherwise −2. If neither distribution occurs, an opposing structural break with opposite expansion resets a nonzero phase to 0 and disarms both sides. Update last context bar on each defense, sweep, distribution, or context failure. For context intervals, calculate zones on their bars and sample them on chart bars with lookahead off.
Long: Structure AMD phase is +1 or +2.
Short: Structure AMD phase is −1 or −2.
