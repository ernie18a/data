Calculation: MA alignment score = using MA(close, selected MA type, each of seven ordered MA lookbacks), let count be the number of true comparisons in close > MA1, MA1 > MA2, …, MA6 > MA7; assign the positive full-alignment magnitude if all are true, the negative full-alignment magnitude if all corresponding comparisons are reversed, otherwise, if partial alignment is enabled, assign the positive outer-partial magnitude when count > upper count threshold, the negative outer-partial magnitude when count < lower count threshold, the positive inner-partial magnitude when count > middle count threshold, or the negative inner-partial magnitude otherwise; assign zero when partial alignment is disabled. The ordered MA lookbacks increase, and the upper, middle, and lower count thresholds decrease.
Calculation: High/low event = +1 when high equals highest(high, high/low lookback), else -1 when low equals lowest(low, high/low lookback), else 0; evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Direction(x) = rising when x > x[1], falling when x < x[1], otherwise the prior Direction(x), or neutral if unavailable; evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Yearly-high eligibility = not yearly filter enabled OR ((H0 unavailable OR H1 unavailable OR H0 > H1) AND (H0 unavailable OR H2 unavailable OR H0 > H2)) OR (H0 unavailable OR (H1 unavailable AND close > H0) OR (H1 available AND H2 unavailable AND close > max(H0,H1)) OR (H1 and H2 available AND close > max(H0,min(H1,H2)))), where H0 is the high of the last completed T1 bar mapped onto chart bars, H1 = H0[shorter history lag in chart bars], and H2 = H0[longer history lag in chart bars]; the longer history lag exceeds the shorter.
Calculation: Yearly-low eligibility = not yearly filter enabled OR ((L0 unavailable OR L1 unavailable OR L0 < L1) AND (L0 unavailable OR L2 unavailable OR L0 < L2)) OR (L0 unavailable OR (L1 unavailable AND close < L0) OR (L1 available AND L2 unavailable AND close < min(L0,L1)) OR (L1 and L2 available AND close < min(L0,max(L1,L2)))), where L0 is the low of the last completed T1 bar mapped onto chart bars, L1 = L0[shorter history lag in chart bars], and L2 = L0[longer history lag in chart bars]; the longer history lag exceeds the shorter.
Long: Supertrend(multiplier, length) direction is bullish AND Direction(rolling sum of MA alignment score over the shared sum lookback) is rising AND Direction(rolling sum of High/low event over the shared sum lookback) is rising AND Yearly-high eligibility AND datetime is within the inclusive start and end time parameters.
Short: Supertrend(multiplier, length) direction is bearish AND Direction(rolling sum of MA alignment score over the shared sum lookback) is falling AND Direction(rolling sum of High/low event over the shared sum lookback) is falling AND Yearly-low eligibility AND datetime is within the inclusive start and end time parameters.
Exit: long, Supertrend(multiplier, length) direction is bearish.
Exit: short, Supertrend(multiplier, length) direction is bullish.
