Calculation: A = Selected MA(type A, lookback A), calculated on chart bars when timeframe A is the chart timeframe or on T1 bars otherwise; chart bars use the last completed T1 value when T1 applies
Calculation: B = Selected MA(type B, lookback B or shared fallback lookback when B is zero), calculated on chart bars when timeframe B is the chart timeframe or on T2 bars otherwise; chart bars use the last completed T2 value when T2 applies
Calculation: C = Selected MA(type C, lookback C or shared fallback lookback when C is zero), calculated on chart bars when timeframe C is the chart timeframe or on T3 bars otherwise; chart bars use the last completed T3 value when T3 applies
Calculation: A direction = A is greater than or equal to its value on the previous bar of its calculation timeframe; when T1 applies, calculate on T1 bars and use the last completed T1 result on chart bars
Calculation: B direction = B is greater than or equal to its value on the previous bar of its calculation timeframe; when T2 applies, calculate on T2 bars and use the last completed T2 result on chart bars
Calculation: C direction = C is greater than or equal to its value on the previous bar of its calculation timeframe; when T3 applies, calculate on T3 bars and use the last completed T3 result on chart bars
