Calculation: Long support = on chart bars at T1 or lower, with T1 < T2 < T3, close[T1, last completed bar] > high[T2, last completed bar] OR close[T1, last completed bar] > high[T3, last completed bar]
Calculation: Short support = close[T1, last completed bar] < low[T2, last completed bar] OR close[T1, last completed bar] < low[T3, last completed bar]
Calculation: Failed upward break = (high[T2, current forming bar] > high[T2, last completed bar] AND close[T1, last completed bar] < high[T2, last completed bar]) OR (high[T3, current forming bar] > high[T3, last completed bar] AND close[T1, last completed bar] < high[T3, last completed bar])
Calculation: Failed downward break = (low[T2, current forming bar] < low[T2, last completed bar] AND close[T1, last completed bar] > low[T2, last completed bar]) OR (low[T3, current forming bar] < low[T3, last completed bar] AND close[T1, last completed bar] > low[T3, last completed bar])
Calculation: Long stop = on a flat-to-long entry, max(low[T2, last completed bar], low[T3, last completed bar], low[T1, last completed bar] if Failed upward break); on a short-to-long reversal, max(low[T2, last completed bar], low[T3, last completed bar]); while long, max(previous long stop, low[T2, last completed bar], low[T3, last completed bar], low[T1, last completed bar] if Failed upward break); if 3-override is enabled and a T1, T2, or T3 bar is forming an outside bar (current high > its prior completed high AND current low < its prior completed low), replace the stop with the current low of the smallest such timeframe
Calculation: Short stop = on a flat-to-short entry, min(high[T2, last completed bar], high[T3, last completed bar], high[T1, last completed bar] if Failed downward break); on a long-to-short reversal, min(high[T2, last completed bar], high[T3, last completed bar]); while short, min(previous short stop, high[T2, last completed bar], high[T3, last completed bar], high[T1, last completed bar] if Failed downward break); if 3-override is enabled and a T1, T2, or T3 bar is forming an outside bar (current high > its prior completed high AND current low < its prior completed low), replace the stop with the current high of the smallest such timeframe
Long: flat AND Long support OR short AND high[T1, current forming bar] > Short stop AND (Long support OR neutral-state option disabled)
Short: flat AND NOT Long support AND Short support OR long AND low[T1, current forming bar] < Long stop AND (Short support OR neutral-state option disabled)
Exit: long, low[T1, current forming bar] < Long stop AND neutral-state option enabled AND NOT Short support
Exit: short, high[T1, current forming bar] > Short stop AND neutral-state option enabled AND NOT Long support
