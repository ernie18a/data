Calculation: crossframe score = crossframe primary weight * (EMA(close, crossframe shorter length, T1, last completed bar)[1] - EMA(close, crossframe longer length, T1, last completed bar)[1]) / ATR(structure ATR length) + crossframe secondary weight * (EMA(close, crossframe shorter length, T2, last completed bar)[1] - EMA(close, crossframe longer length, T2, last completed bar)[1]) / ATR(structure ATR length) + crossframe local weight * (EMA(close, crossframe shorter length) - EMA(close, crossframe longer length)) / ATR(structure ATR length)
Calculation: trend = (EMA(close, trend shorter length) - EMA(close, trend longer length)) / ATR(structure ATR length)
Calculation: auction point of control = midpoint of the auction bin with the largest total volume over the last auction lookback length bars, where the price range runs from the lowest low to the highest high of those bars and is split into auction bin count equal bins, and each bar's volume is spread across the bins its high-low range overlaps in proportion to the overlap
Calculation: auction score = (SMA(volume, auction shorter volume length) / SMA(volume, auction longer volume length) - 1) * auction volume weight + trend * auction trend weight + (close - auction point of control) / ATR(structure ATR length) * auction distance weight
Calculation: step midline = IF (close > step midline[1] + inner ATR multiplier * ATR(structure ATR length) OR close < step midline[1] - inner ATR multiplier * ATR(structure ATR length)) THEN SMA(close, step basis length) ELSE step midline[1]
Calculation: momentum score = clamp(wave weight * (WaveTrend(hlc3, wave channel length, wave average length) - SMA(WaveTrend(hlc3, wave channel length, wave average length), wave signal length)) + (RSI(close, RSI length) - EMA(RSI(close, RSI length), RSI smoothing length)) + (RSI(close, RSI length) - 50) / RSI scale + momentum trend weight * trend, -100, 100)
Calculation: momentum signal = EMA(momentum score, momentum signal length)
Calculation: absorption range = range with fields range high, range low, range equilibrium, range bull balance and range start; bar bull share = bullish bar share if close >= open, else bearish bar share; on each bar with volume > SMA(volume, absorption baseline length) * absorption multiplier, if range start is absent, bars since range start > absorption life bars, close > range high[1], or close < range low[1], then range high = high, range low = low, range equilibrium = (high + low + close) / 3, range bull balance = bar bull share, range start = current bar; otherwise range high = max(range high[1], high), range low = min(range low[1], low), range equilibrium = (range equilibrium[1] + (high + low + close) / 3) / 2, range bull balance = (range bull balance[1] + bar bull share) / 2; an absorption range is active while bars since range start <= absorption life bars
Calculation: ATR percentile = percent rank(ATR(volatility ATR length), volatility percentile lookback)
Calculation: volatility score = (ATR percentile - 50) + volatility direction weight * (EMA(high - low if close > open else 0, directional range length) - EMA(high - low if close < open else 0, directional range length)) / ATR(volatility ATR length)
Long: crossframe score > crossframe threshold AND auction score > auction threshold AND close > auction point of control AND close > step midline AND step midline >= step midline[1] AND close > most recent pivot high(high, pivot left length, pivot right length) AND (no active absorption range OR (close >= absorption range equilibrium AND absorption range bull balance >= 0.5)) AND ATR percentile >= min ATR percentile AND ATR percentile <= max ATR percentile AND volatility score > -volatility threshold
Long: position side is not long AND crossframe score > crossframe threshold AND auction score > auction threshold AND close > auction point of control AND close > step midline AND step midline >= step midline[1] AND close > most recent pivot high(high, pivot left length, pivot right length) AND (no active absorption range OR (close >= absorption range equilibrium AND absorption range bull balance >= 0.5)) AND ATR percentile >= min ATR percentile AND ATR percentile <= max ATR percentile AND volatility score > -volatility threshold AND momentum score crosses above momentum signal AND momentum score > momentum threshold
Short: crossframe score < -crossframe threshold AND auction score < -auction threshold AND close < auction point of control AND close < step midline AND step midline <= step midline[1] AND close < most recent pivot low(low, pivot left length, pivot right length) AND (no active absorption range OR (close <= absorption range equilibrium AND absorption range bull balance <= 0.5)) AND ATR percentile >= min ATR percentile AND ATR percentile <= max ATR percentile AND volatility score < volatility threshold
Short: position side is not short AND crossframe score < -crossframe threshold AND auction score < -auction threshold AND close < auction point of control AND close < step midline AND step midline <= step midline[1] AND close < most recent pivot low(low, pivot left length, pivot right length) AND (no active absorption range OR (close <= absorption range equilibrium AND absorption range bull balance <= 0.5)) AND ATR percentile >= min ATR percentile AND ATR percentile <= max ATR percentile AND volatility score < volatility threshold AND momentum score crosses below momentum signal AND momentum score < -momentum threshold
Exit: long, crossframe score < 0 OR momentum score < 0 OR auction score < 0 OR close < step midline
Exit: short, crossframe score > 0 OR momentum score > 0 OR auction score > 0 OR close > step midline
