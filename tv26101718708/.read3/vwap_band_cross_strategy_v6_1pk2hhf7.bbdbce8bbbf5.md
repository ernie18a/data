Calculation: Upper band = VWAP(source) + standard deviation(source, lookback) * band multiplier
Calculation: Lower band = VWAP(source) - standard deviation(source, lookback) * band multiplier
Long: Entry is enabled AND the selected entry trigger crosses above VWAP, the upper band, or the lower band AND long trading and the current weekday are enabled AND (RSI confirmation is disabled OR RSI(close, lookback) < long oversold threshold) AND (EMA confirmation is disabled OR close > EMA(close, lookback)) AND (MACD confirmation is disabled OR (MACD line(close, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback) > signal line AND histogram > 0))
Short: Entry is enabled AND the selected entry trigger crosses below the lower band or upper band, or crosses above VWAP AND short trading and the current weekday are enabled AND (RSI confirmation is disabled OR RSI(close, lookback) > short overbought threshold) AND (EMA confirmation is disabled OR close < EMA(close, lookback)) AND (MACD confirmation is disabled OR (MACD line(close, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback) < signal line AND histogram < 0))
Exit: Long, exit strategy is enabled AND ((fixed-tick bracket is selected AND (high >= entry price + take-profit tick parameter * minimum tick OR low <= entry price - stop-loss tick parameter * minimum tick)) OR (ATR bracket is selected AND (high >= entry price + ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier)) OR (capped ATR bracket is selected AND (high >= min(entry price + ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier, entry price * (1 + cap percentage / 100)) OR low <= max(entry price - ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier, entry price * (1 - cap percentage / 100))))
Exit: Short, exit strategy is enabled AND ((fixed-tick bracket is selected AND (low <= entry price - take-profit tick parameter * minimum tick OR high >= entry price + stop-loss tick parameter * minimum tick)) OR (ATR bracket is selected AND (low <= entry price - ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier)) OR (capped ATR bracket is selected AND (low <= max(entry price - ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier, entry price * (1 - cap percentage / 100)) OR high >= min(entry price + ATR(lookback) at entry * multiplier, entry price * (1 + cap percentage / 100))))
Exit: Long, exit strategy is enabled AND VWAP-cross exit is selected AND close crosses below VWAP

Exit: Short, exit strategy is enabled AND upper-band-cross exit is selected AND close crosses below the upper band
