Calculation: V anchors = on chart bars, find the rolling-lookback highest high and the lowest low since it; require the top to be at least the minimum downtrend bars plus minimum uptrend bars old and the bottom to be at least the minimum uptrend bars old; refresh for a newer top or a low below the current bottom. The downtrend baseline is the straight line in log-price space from bottom to top; its offset is the maximum log high above that baseline. The uptrend slope is the minimum log slope from bottom to any later chart-bar low.
Calculation: V-Fib ratio(price) = (ln(price) − log-price downtrend baseline at the current chart bar) / (maximum log high-above-baseline distance / Fib-fraction parameter).
Calculation: Uptrend rate = minimum log slope from V-bottom to a later chart-bar low × bars per day × percent conversion.
Calculation: Downtrend rate = log slope of the line from V-bottom to downtrend top raised by the maximum log high-above-baseline distance × bars per day × percent conversion.
Calculation: V-trend = uptrend rate + downtrend rate.
Calculation: Lead(price) = 100 × (ln(price) − log-price uptrend line at the current chart bar) / uptrend slope; zero when the uptrend slope is zero.
Calculation: Support rebound armed = track V-Fib ratios of T1 intrabar highs on chart bars, including any still-forming intrabar; after a peak, arm when a ratio has fallen from that peak by at least peak × minimum pullback ratio and a later ratio rises from the resulting trough by at least the minimum rebound. Reset on changed V anchors; reset the trough if a new peak exceeds the prior peak by more than the minimum rebound.
Calculation: Support trail stop = project the steepest unbroken log-linear support line through a pair of chart-bar lows, with slope greater than the uptrend slope and no later chart low below it after its intersection with the uptrend line; subtract the greater of the minimum support trail gap and the maximum drawdown from the support start through T1 intrabar highs and lows. It exists only when the current close is at or above the support line.
Calculation: Entry trail gap = (entry price − entry trail price) / entry price; for a trend entry, entry trail price is the greater of entry price × (1 − trend trail cap / 100) and V-bottom low; for a support entry, it is the greater of entry price × (1 − support trail cap / 100) and support trail stop.
Calculation: Trend-entry lock = set on trend entry; release when current-chart-bar V-Fib ratio reaches the unlock threshold or the rate-based exit condition is true.
Long: No position is open AND the trend-entry lock is clear AND Uptrend rate >= minimum uptrend rate AND Downtrend rate <= maximum downtrend rate AND V-trend >= minimum V-trend AND Technical Rating on the current chart bar >= minimum entry rating AND, on a chart bar, a T1 intrabar high (including any still-forming intrabar) has V-Fib ratio >= minimum entry ratio and <= maximum entry ratio while Lead(T1 intrabar low) >= minimum entry leading gain; use the first qualifying intrabar high as entry price.
Long: Support entries are enabled AND no position is open AND no trend-entry intrabar qualifies on this chart bar AND Technical Rating on the current chart bar >= minimum entry rating AND Support trail stop exists AND Support rebound armed AND, on a chart bar, a T1 intrabar high at or after arming has V-Fib ratio > maximum entry ratio; use the first qualifying intrabar high as entry price.
Long: Support trail stop exists AND Technical Rating on the current chart bar >= minimum entry rating AND current-chart-bar V-Fib ratio > maximum entry ratio AND Support rebound armed AND no entry occurred on this chart bar.
Exit: Long, on a chart bar, low <= highest price since entry (initialized at entry price) × (1 − entry trail gap); when a new chart high occurs and T1 intrabars are available, process their highs and lows in order and exit when an intrabar low reaches the updated stop.
Exit: Long, Uptrend rate < keep uptrend rate AND Uptrend rate < uptrend rate at entry + exit uptrend difference.
Exit: Long, V-Fib-based exits are enabled AND V-Fib ratio(current chart high) >= exit V-Fib ratio.
