Calculation: VWAP = VWAP(source, T1 reset interval, chart bars through the current bar)
Calculation: VWAP σ = distance from VWAP(source, T1 reset interval) to its upper standard-deviation band
Long: (volume > 0 AND SMA(volume, volume lookback) is defined AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × spike threshold AND ((direction mode selects VWAP AND close > VWAP) OR (direction mode selects candle AND close > open) OR (direction mode selects close-to-close AND close > close[1])) AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the current bar is confirmed)) OR crossover(close, VWAP) OR (VWAP σ is defined AND close < VWAP − band-width multiplier × VWAP σ)
Short: (volume > 0 AND SMA(volume, volume lookback) is defined AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × spike threshold AND ((direction mode selects VWAP AND close < VWAP) OR (direction mode selects candle AND close < open) OR (direction mode selects close-to-close AND close < close[1])) AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the current bar is confirmed)) OR crossunder(close, VWAP) OR (VWAP σ is defined AND close > VWAP + band-width multiplier × VWAP σ)
