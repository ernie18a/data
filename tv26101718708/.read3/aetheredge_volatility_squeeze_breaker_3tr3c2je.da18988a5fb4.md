Calculation: Keltner upper/lower channel = SMA(close, channel lookback) +/- SMA(true range when selected, otherwise high - low, same channel lookback) * channel multiplier
Calculation: squeeze = (Bollinger lower band(close, BB lookback, BB deviation) > Keltner lower channel(wide squeeze multiplier) AND Bollinger upper band(close, BB lookback, BB deviation) < Keltner upper channel(wide squeeze multiplier)) OR the same comparisons using the medium squeeze multiplier OR the same comparisons using the tight squeeze multiplier
Calculation: momentum = Linear Regression(close - average(average(highest(high, momentum lookback), lowest(low, momentum lookback)), SMA(close, momentum lookback)), momentum lookback, linear-regression offset
Long: squeeze is false AND squeeze was true on at least one of the preceding bars within the release lookback AND momentum > 0 AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND close > Bollinger upper band(close, BB lookback, BB deviation) on the previous bar
Short: squeeze is false AND squeeze was true on at least one of the preceding bars within the release lookback AND momentum <= 0 AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND close < Bollinger lower band(close, BB lookback, BB deviation) on the previous bar
