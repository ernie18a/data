Long: On the current chart bar, enter when the absolute difference between its open and the last completed chart bar's close, divided by that close, is at most the maximum gap; the position is flat; and on the last completed chart bar RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above its threshold, CMF(high, low, close, volume, CMF lookback) is positive, OBV(close, volume) is above its value at the slope lookback bars ago, EMA(close, shorter lookback) is above its value at the slope lookback bars ago, the close is above EMA(close, shorter lookback) and that EMA is above EMA(close, longer lookback), ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) divided by close is between its lower and upper bounds, close is above open, and volume is at least SMA(volume, volume lookback) times the volume multiplier; also, the chart bar before the last completed bar closed above its open.
Exit: long, high reaches the entry price times one plus the take-profit fraction OR low reaches the entry price times one minus the stop-loss fraction.
