Calculation: Supply/demand zones = bands centered on confirmed pivot highs/lows, respectively, with height ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) × zone-height multiplier; create only when the pivot candle range is at least ATR × minimum-swing multiplier and the same-side spacing from the previous accepted zone is at least the minimum-zone-distance parameter; merge overlapping unbroken zones of the same side from the same session, recentering the merged band; mark broken when close or the corresponding wick crosses beyond its edge, according to the invalidation setting.
Long: confirmed pivot low OR close is within close × proximity percentage / 100 of the midpoint of any unbroken demand zone.
Short: confirmed pivot high OR close is within close × proximity percentage / 100 of the midpoint of any unbroken supply zone.
