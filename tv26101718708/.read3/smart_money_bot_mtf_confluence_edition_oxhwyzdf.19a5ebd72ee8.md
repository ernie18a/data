Calculation: Demand zone top/bottom = high[momentum lookback] and low[momentum lookback] on the latest chart bar where momentum lookback consecutive bars close above open and the current high - low > ATR(ATR lookback) * momentum candle size; retain these levels until the next qualifying bar
Calculation: Supply zone top/bottom = high[momentum lookback] and low[momentum lookback] on the latest chart bar where momentum lookback consecutive bars close below open and the current high - low > ATR(ATR lookback) * momentum candle size; retain these levels until the next qualifying bar
Calculation: Long stop = demand zone bottom - ATR(ATR lookback) * stop buffer, sampled at the signal bar
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - long stop) * risk/reward ratio
Calculation: Short stop = supply zone top + ATR(ATR lookback) * stop buffer, sampled at the signal bar
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar close - (short stop - signal-bar close) * risk/reward ratio
Long: low <= demand zone top AND low >= demand zone bottom AND close > open AND previous-bar close < previous-bar open AND close > previous-bar open AND close > previous-bar close AND close > long stop AND (HTF filter disabled OR (on chart bars using the still-forming bars of T1 and T2, T1 close > EMA(close, shared HTF EMA lookback, T1, still-forming bar) AND T2 close > EMA(close, same HTF EMA lookback, T2, still-forming bar)))
Short: high >= supply zone bottom AND high <= supply zone top AND close < open AND previous-bar close > previous-bar open AND close < previous-bar open AND close < previous-bar close AND short stop > close AND (HTF filter disabled OR (on chart bars using the still-forming bars of T1 and T2, T1 close <= EMA(close, shared HTF EMA lookback, T1, still-forming bar) AND T2 close <= EMA(close, same HTF EMA lookback, T2, still-forming bar)))
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR (since entry, high has reached entry price + trailing activation distance AND low <= highest high since activation - trailing offset distance) OR (breakeven enabled AND since entry, high has exceeded entry price + breakeven activation distance AND low <= entry price)
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR (since entry, low has reached entry price - trailing activation distance AND high >= lowest low since activation + trailing offset distance) OR (breakeven enabled AND since entry, low has fallen below entry price - breakeven activation distance AND high >= entry price)
