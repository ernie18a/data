Calculation: normalized option flow = (weighted call pressure across the ATM strike and symmetric near, middle, and far strike offsets from ATM − weighted put pressure across the same strikes) / (absolute weighted call pressure + absolute weighted put pressure), or 0 when the denominator is 0; each pressure term is option volume × +1 when current option close > previous chart-bar close, otherwise option volume × −1, × the weight for its absolute strike offset, and is 0 if current close, previous close, or volume is unavailable
Calculation: intrabar volume imbalance = (up volume − down volume) / (up volume + down volume), or unavailable when the sum is 0; on each chart bar, sum T1 intrabar volume from the second intrabar onward into up volume when its close ≥ the preceding T1 close, otherwise into down volume; T1 is the configured lower timeframe, and its current bar may still be forming on a live chart bar; unavailable when intrabar data is unavailable or the layer is off
Calculation: ATM implied-volatility proxy = ATM call-option close + ATM put-option close, divided by (annualization-coefficient parameter × underlying close × square root of the greater of time to selected expiry in years and minimum-expiry-fraction parameter), expressed as a percentage; use the configured ATM strike, or the initial chart close rounded to the strike-step grid when ATM is automatic; selected expiry is the configured manual date or current time advanced in the symbol timezone to the configured expiry weekday, including the current day
Calculation: directional score = momentum-weight parameter × sign(change(close, momentum lookback) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), denominator-floor parameter)) × min(abs(change(close, momentum lookback) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), denominator-floor parameter)), score-cap parameter) + basis-weight parameter × clamp(change(futures close − close, basis lookback) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), denominator-floor parameter), −score-cap parameter, score-cap parameter) + flow-weight parameter × (flow layer enabled AND (skew gate disabled OR put-option close at ATM minus middle strike offset − call-option close at ATM plus middle strike offset is unavailable OR (normalized option flow > 0 AND that difference ≤ 0) OR (normalized option flow < 0 AND that difference ≥ 0)) ? normalized option flow : 0) + intrabar-weight parameter × (intrabar volume imbalance if available, otherwise 0)
Option: buy call, directional score > direction-gate parameter AND (ATM implied-volatility proxy unavailable OR EMA(ATM implied-volatility proxy, IV slope lookback) unavailable OR ATM implied-volatility proxy ≥ EMA(ATM implied-volatility proxy, IV slope lookback))
Option: buy put, directional score < −direction-gate parameter AND (ATM implied-volatility proxy unavailable OR EMA(ATM implied-volatility proxy, IV slope lookback) unavailable OR ATM implied-volatility proxy ≥ EMA(ATM implied-volatility proxy, IV slope lookback))
