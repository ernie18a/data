Calculation: Opening-range low = min(open, close) on the opening-range bar when body range is enabled, otherwise low on that bar
Calculation: Close position percentage = 100 × (close − low) ÷ (high − low), or 50 when high = low
Calculation: Upper-wick percentage = 100 × (high − max(open, close)) ÷ (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Lower-wick percentage = 100 × (min(open, close) − low) ÷ (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Most recent swing high = latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot strength, pivot strength)
Calculation: Most recent swing low = latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot strength, pivot strength)
