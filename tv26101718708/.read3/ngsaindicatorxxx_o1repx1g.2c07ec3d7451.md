Calculation: Average slope (%) = (SMA(source, average lookback) - SMA(source, average lookback)[slope lookback]) / SMA(source, average lookback)[slope lookback] × 100
Calculation: Distance from average (%) = (source - SMA(source, average lookback)) / SMA(source, average lookback) × 100
Calculation: Uptrend structure = latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) > preceding confirmed PivotHigh AND latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) > preceding confirmed PivotLow
Calculation: Downtrend structure = latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) < preceding confirmed PivotHigh AND latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) < preceding confirmed PivotLow
Calculation: Lower-high structure = latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) < preceding confirmed PivotHigh
Calculation: Accumulation = SMA(volume when close >= open, otherwise zero, volume lookback) > SMA(volume when close < open, otherwise zero, volume lookback) × directional-volume ratio
Calculation: Distribution = SMA(volume when close < open, otherwise zero, volume lookback) > SMA(volume when close >= open, otherwise zero, volume lookback) × directional-volume ratio
Calculation: Range expansion = ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), pivot lookback) × range-expansion multiplier
Calculation: Churning = volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND abs(close - open) / open < SMA((high - low) / low, pivot lookback) × low-body multiplier
Calculation: Relative performance (%) = ((close / benchmark close) - (close / benchmark close)[relative-strength lookback]) / (close / benchmark close)[relative-strength lookback] × 100, using benchmark data at the chart timeframe
Calculation: Average is rising when average slope (%) > slope threshold; falling when average slope (%) < -slope threshold; flat when neither
Calculation: Stage 1 score = stage-1 rising-average weight × indicator(average is flat) + stage-1 no-trend-structure weight × indicator(NOT uptrend structure AND NOT downtrend structure) + stage-1 near-average weight × indicator(distance from average (%) < distance band AND distance from average (%) > -distance band) + stage-1 accumulation weight × indicator(accumulation) + stage-1 no-range-expansion weight × indicator(NOT range expansion) + stage-1 upward-curl weight × indicator(average is flat AND average slope (%) - average slope (%)[slope lookback] > 0)
Calculation: Stage 2 score = stage-2 rising-average weight × indicator(average is rising) + stage-2 above-average weight × indicator(close > SMA(close, average lookback)) + stage-2 uptrend-structure weight × indicator(uptrend structure) + stage-2 relative-strength weight × indicator(relative-strength filter enabled AND relative performance (%) > 0) + stage-2 no-distribution weight × indicator(NOT distribution)
Calculation: Stage 3 score = stage-3 flat-average weight × indicator(average is flat) + stage-3 distribution weight × indicator(distribution) + stage-3 lower-high weight × indicator(lower-high structure) + stage-3 range-expansion weight × indicator(range expansion) + stage-3 churning weight × indicator(churning) + stage-3 downward-curl weight × indicator(average is flat AND average slope (%) - average slope (%)[slope lookback] < 0)
Calculation: Stage 4 score = stage-4 falling-average weight × indicator(average is falling) + stage-4 below-average weight × indicator(close < SMA(close, average lookback)) + stage-4 downtrend-structure weight × indicator(downtrend structure) + stage-4 negative-relative-strength weight × indicator(relative-strength filter enabled AND relative performance (%) <= 0) + stage-4 distribution weight × indicator(distribution)
Calculation: Raw stage = stage with the greatest stage score, resolving ties in order stage 1, then stage 2, then stage 3, then stage 4
Calculation: Stage = raw stage when it equals each raw stage over the stage-confirmation lookback, including the current chart bar; otherwise retain the previous Stage. Initialize Stage to stage 1.
Calculation: Market-index mean true range = SMA(max(index high - index low, abs(index high - prior-bar index close), abs(index low - prior-bar index close)), index true-range lookback)
Calculation: Market-index stage = stage 2 when index close > SMA(index close, index average lookback) AND market-index average is rising; stage 4 when index close < that SMA AND market-index average is falling; stage 3 when market-index average is flat AND index close > that SMA; otherwise stage 1
Calculation: Market-index RSI is falling when RSI(index close, market-index RSI lookback) < that RSI[decline interval] AND that RSI[decline interval] < that RSI[twice the decline interval]
Calculation: Market breadth score = market-danger weight when market-index stage is stage 4 OR (market-index stage is stage 3 AND market-index RSI is falling), plus market-climax weight when market-index distance from its SMA >= index-overextension threshold AND market-index RSI >= index RSI-warning level AND market-index RSI is falling, plus market-warning weight when market-index RSI >= index RSI-warning level OR market-index mean true range > SMA(market-index mean true range, index volatility lookback) × index volatility-expansion multiplier OR market-index average is flat, plus falling-index-average weight when market-index average is falling
Calculation: Extreme overextension = distance from average (%) >= overextension threshold × extreme-overextension multiplier
Calculation: Parabolic blowoff = (close - close[short parabolic lookback selected by chart timeframe]) / close[short parabolic lookback selected by chart timeframe] >= short-gain threshold OR (close - close[long parabolic lookback selected by chart timeframe]) / close[long parabolic lookback selected by chart timeframe] >= long-gain threshold; the long lookback is longer than the short lookback
Calculation: Volume climax = volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume-climax multiplier
Calculation: Stage-2 breakout close = close on a transition from stage 1 to stage 2; retain it while Stage is stage 2 and clear it on leaving stage 2
Calculation: Gain since stage-2 breakout (%) = (close - stage-2 breakout close) / stage-2 breakout close × 100, or zero when no stage-2 breakout close is stored
Calculation: Stage-2 highest high = high on a transition from stage 1 to stage 2, then the highest high since that transition while Stage is stage 2; clear it on leaving stage 2
Calculation: Chandelier stop = stage-2 highest high - ATR(chandelier ATR lookback) × chandelier multiplier
Calculation: Chandelier stop hit = close < chandelier stop AND previous-bar close >= previous-bar chandelier stop
Calculation: Exhaustion score = overextension-mild weight when distance from average (%) >= overextension threshold, plus overextension-strong weight when distance from average (%) >= overextension threshold × strong-overextension multiplier, plus overextension-extreme weight when extreme overextension, plus short-parabolic weight when short-window gain >= short-gain threshold, plus long-parabolic weight when long-window gain >= long-gain threshold, plus volume-climax weight when volume climax, plus exhaustion-candle weight when high - low > 0 AND (high - max(close, open)) / (high - low) >= upper-wick threshold AND close < (high + low) / 2, plus overbought-RSI weight when RSI(close, exhaustion RSI lookback) > overbought threshold, plus failed-swing weight when the prior bar made a high >= Highest(high, failed-swing lookback)[prior bar] AND close < low[prior bar] AND close < open, plus short-average-cross weight when close crosses below SMA(close, short-average lookback), plus chandelier-hit weight when chandelier stop hit
Calculation: Exhaustion score window = maximum exhaustion score over the current bar and the preceding score-clustering lookback bars
Calculation: Weak-market breadth = relative-strength/breadth filter enabled AND market breadth score >= weak-market breadth threshold
Calculation: Strong booking threshold = weak-market strong-score threshold when weak-market breadth, otherwise normal-market strong-score threshold
Calculation: Caution booking threshold = weak-market caution-score threshold when weak-market breadth, otherwise normal-market caution-score threshold
Calculation: Market emergency = breadth filter enabled AND (market-danger condition OR market-climax condition) AND exhaustion score window >= positive-score threshold
Calculation: Stage-2 profit gate = Stage is stage 2 AND gain since stage-2 breakout (%) >= minimum breakout gain
Calculation: Strong profit booking = Stage-2 profit gate AND (exhaustion score window >= strong booking threshold OR extreme overextension OR (parabolic blowoff AND volume climax) OR chandelier stop hit OR market emergency)
Calculation: Profit-signal cooldown is satisfied when no prior strong or caution profit signal exists OR bars since the prior signal >= profit-signal cooldown lookback
Calculation: Caution profit booking = Stage-2 profit gate AND NOT strong profit booking AND exhaustion score window >= caution booking threshold AND profit-signal cooldown is satisfied

Short: Stage = stage 4

Exit: Short, Stage = stage 1 AND previous Stage = stage 4
