Calculation: Long stop at entry = lowest(low, swing lookback) − stop buffer × tick size OR close − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR stop multiple, using the shared stop basis; fixed at entry
Calculation: Short stop at entry = highest(high, swing lookback) + stop buffer × tick size OR close + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR stop multiple, using the shared stop basis; fixed at entry
Long: On a confirmed chart bar close, no tracked signal is active after current-bar exits and any T2 reset; the chart bar's opening time is within the configured session and timezone; on chart bars, the last completed T1 bar's close > its EMA(close, long-term lookback, calculated on T1 bars) AND that EMA > its value one T1 bar earlier; the current low <= the selected pullback reference (VWAP(hlc3) or EMA(close, short-term lookback)) OR the prior chart bar's low <= the prior reference; close > the current reference; close > open if configured; and close − long stop at entry > one tick
Short: On a confirmed chart bar close, no tracked signal is active after current-bar exits and any T2 reset; the chart bar's opening time is within the configured session and timezone; on chart bars, the last completed T1 bar's close < its EMA(close, long-term lookback, calculated on T1 bars) AND that EMA < its value one T1 bar earlier; the current high >= the selected pullback reference (VWAP(hlc3) or EMA(close, short-term lookback)) OR the prior chart bar's high >= the prior reference; close < the current reference; close < open if configured; and short stop at entry − close > one tick
Exit: Long, a new T2 bar begins OR, on a confirmed chart bar close, low <= fixed long stop OR high >= entry price + target-2 R multiple × (entry price − long stop)
Exit: Short, a new T2 bar begins OR, on a confirmed chart bar close, high >= fixed short stop OR low <= entry price − target-2 R multiple × (short stop − entry price)
