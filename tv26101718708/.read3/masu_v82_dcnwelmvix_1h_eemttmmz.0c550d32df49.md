Calculation: Nadaraya-Watson ribbon = eight endpoint estimates of the selected source, each the weighted mean of current and prior source values using Gaussian kernel weights; all share a lookback and adaptive bandwidth scale, with eight strictly increasing bandwidth parameters

Calculation: Bullish smart-money event = bullish order block OR bullish fair value gap OR low liquidity sweep OR close crossing above the most recent confirmed pivot high; bullish order block means close > open AND prior close < prior open AND high − close < (high − low) × wick fraction; bullish fair value gap means low > high two bars ago AND low − high two bars ago > ATR × gap-size parameter; low liquidity sweep means low < prior rolling low AND close > open AND close > prior rolling low; pivot high uses the configured left and right pivot lookbacks
Calculation: Bearish smart-money event = bearish order block OR bearish fair value gap OR high liquidity sweep OR close crossing below the most recent confirmed pivot low; bearish order block means close < open AND prior close > prior open AND close − low < (high − low) × wick fraction; bearish fair value gap means high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago − high > ATR × gap-size parameter; high liquidity sweep means high > prior rolling high AND close < open AND close < prior rolling high; pivot low uses the configured left and right pivot lookbacks
Calculation: ELM probability = logistic of the weighted sum of seven normalized features plus bias: RSI, rate of change, distance from long-term EMA, ADX, shortest-bandwidth relative slope, bullish-minus-bearish ribbon ordering count, and volume relative to its average; initialize each feature weight and bias separately, then on confirmed chart bars update them by decayed gradient descent using the error between the predicted probability and whether close after the target lookahead is above current close, multiplied by the corresponding lagged feature
