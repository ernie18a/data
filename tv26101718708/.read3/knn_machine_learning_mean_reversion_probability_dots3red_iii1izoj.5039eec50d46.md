Calculation: PriorZScore(x, lookback) = (x - SMA(x[1], lookback)) / STDEV(x[1], lookback)
Calculation: Above and below reversion probabilities = for each bar, form five features: 100 × (close − basis) / max(basis, floor), (close − basis) / max(basis + band multiplier × STDEV(close, basis lookback) − basis, floor), RSI(close, RSI lookback) − 50, abs(close − open) / max(high − low, floor), and −volume / max(SMA(volume[1], volume lookback), 1); apply PriorZScore with the search lookback to each feature. For candidate offsets every reversion lookback from the reversion lookback through search lookback plus reversion lookback, compute the Minkowski-p distance between the current and candidate five-feature vectors. Label a candidate +1 when its close is above basis and a low touches or crosses basis within the next reversion lookback bars, −1 when its close is below basis and a high touches or crosses basis within that period, and 0 otherwise. Select the k nearest candidates; let sigma be the greater of a positive floor and the distance at sorted index min(max(1, floor(k/2)), candidate count − 1), using zero-based indexing. Weight each selected candidate by exp(−distance^bandwidth / (2 × sigma²)). Above probability is the weight sum for +1 labels divided by the weight sum for +1 labels and 0 labels whose first feature is positive. Below probability is the weight sum for −1 labels divided by the weight sum for −1 labels and 0 labels whose first feature is not positive.
Long: crosses above(below reversion probability, probability threshold) AND close < basis AND (extension gate disabled OR close < basis − gate multiplier × ATR(gate lookback)) AND no long signal since the most recent close above basis OR close < basis AND below reversion probability > bar-color factor × probability threshold
Short: crosses above(above reversion probability, probability threshold) AND close > basis AND (extension gate disabled OR close > basis + gate multiplier × ATR(gate lookback)) AND no short signal since the most recent close below basis OR close >= basis AND above reversion probability > bar-color factor × probability threshold
