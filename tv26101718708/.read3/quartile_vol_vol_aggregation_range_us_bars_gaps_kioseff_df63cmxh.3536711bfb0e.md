Calculation: Range US events and level = choose separate trend-move and reversal-move parameters in custom mode, or divide the initial chart-bar close by separate trend and reversal divisors in automatic mode; initialize the level to the first T1 high and direction up; on each chart bar, process its T1 intrabars in order, including the still-forming T1 bar when available. While direction is up, emit an up-trend event and add the trend move to the level if T1 high > level + trend move before any move has been emitted, or T1 high >= level + trend move afterward; then emit a down-reversal event and subtract the reversal move from the updated level if T1 low < level - reversal move. Next, while direction is down, emit a down-trend event and subtract the trend move from the level if T1 low <= level - trend move; then emit an up-reversal event and add the reversal move to the updated level if T1 high > level + reversal move. Update the level and direction after each event before the next comparison; the latest level is the Range US level.
Long: (Range US event = up trend OR Range US event = up reversal) OR close >= Range US level OR (low > high[1] AND abs(low / high[1] - 1) > gap percentage / 100)
Short: (Range US event = down trend OR Range US event = down reversal) OR close < Range US level OR (high < low[1] AND abs(low[1] / high - 1) > gap percentage / 100)
