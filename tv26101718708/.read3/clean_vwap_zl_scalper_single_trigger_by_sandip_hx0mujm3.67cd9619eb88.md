Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the most recent signal direction is not short AND close < ZLEMA(source, zero-lag lookback) AND close < VWAP(hlc3) AND (high >= VWAP(hlc3) OR high >= VWAP(hlc3) + StDev(hlc3, deviation lookback) * VWAP band multiplier OR high >= ZLEMA(source, zero-lag lookback)) AND close < open AND close <= open[1]
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) on signal bar * stop ATR multiplier OR high >= signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) on signal bar * stop ATR multiplier * target risk-reward multiplier
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + ATR(ATR lookback) on signal bar * stop ATR multiplier OR low <= signal-bar close - ATR(ATR lookback) on signal bar * stop ATR multiplier * target risk-reward multiplier
