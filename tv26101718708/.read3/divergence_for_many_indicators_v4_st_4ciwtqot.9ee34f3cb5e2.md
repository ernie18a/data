Calculation: Divergence series = enabled MACD line and MACD histogram (same fast, slower, and signal lookbacks), RSI(close, lookback), SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback), smoothing lookback), CCI(close, lookback), Momentum(close, lookback), OBV(close, volume), VW-MACD(close, shorter and longer lookbacks), Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, lookback), MFI(high, low, close, volume, lookback), or an enabled external series sourced from the data fields.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = for any divergence series I and selected regular and/or hidden type, let e be 0 in no-confirm mode and 1 otherwise; there is a prior confirmed pivot low of the selected pivot source (close or low), formed with the pivot-period parameter on each side, among the maximum-pivot-count and maximum-bars-back limits, with distance d greater than the minimum-separation parameter. For regular type, I[e] > I[d] and the selected endpoint price (close or low) < the pivot low; for hidden type, I[e] < I[d] and the selected endpoint price > the pivot low. Also, no-confirm mode or I > I[1] or close > close[1], and on every intervening bar I and close are each at or above the straight line between their endpoint values.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = for any divergence series I and selected regular and/or hidden type, let e be 0 in no-confirm mode and 1 otherwise; there is a prior confirmed pivot high of the selected pivot source (close or high), formed with the pivot-period parameter on each side, among the maximum-pivot-count and maximum-bars-back limits, with distance d greater than the minimum-separation parameter. For regular type, I[e] < I[d] and the selected endpoint price (close or high) > the pivot high; for hidden type, I[e] > I[d] and the selected endpoint price < the pivot high. Also, no-confirm mode or I < I[1] or close < close[1], and on every intervening bar I and close are each at or below the straight line between their endpoint values.
Long: the number of divergences across enabled series and selected types is at least the minimum-divergence parameter AND bullish divergence
Short: the number of divergences across enabled series and selected types is at least the minimum-divergence parameter AND bearish divergence
Exit: Long, low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss parameter) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit parameter)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss parameter) OR low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit parameter)
