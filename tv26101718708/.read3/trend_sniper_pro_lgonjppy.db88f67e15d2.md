Calculation: Trend price breakout = abs(close - close[1]) > ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * trend target multiplier * (1 + effective trend value markup / 100) OR (open-close range breakout enabled AND high - low > range breakout factor * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * trend target multiplier); effective trend value markup is the crypto-mode trend value markup when crypto mode is enabled, otherwise the ordinary trend value markup.
Calculation: Reversal price breakout = abs(close - close[1]) > ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * reversal target multiplier * (1 + effective reversal value markup / 100) OR (open-close range breakout enabled AND high - low > range breakout factor * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * reversal target multiplier); effective reversal value markup is the crypto-mode reversal value markup when crypto mode is enabled, otherwise the ordinary reversal value markup.
Calculation: High price breakout = abs(close - close[1]) > ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * high breakout multiplier OR (open-close range breakout enabled AND high - low > range breakout factor * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * high breakout multiplier).
Calculation: Trend volume breakout = volume is missing OR moving average(volume, selected volume average type, volume lookback) * (1 + effective trend volume markup / 100) < volume; effective trend volume markup is the crypto-mode trend volume markup when crypto mode is enabled, otherwise the ordinary trend volume markup.
Calculation: Reversal volume breakout = volume is missing OR moving average(volume, selected volume average type, volume lookback) * (1 + effective reversal volume markup / 100) < volume; effective reversal volume markup is the crypto-mode reversal volume markup when crypto mode is enabled, otherwise the ordinary reversal volume markup.
Calculation: High volume breakout = volume is not missing AND moving average(volume, selected volume average type, volume lookback) * high volume multiplier < volume.
Calculation: Breakout state = +1 when close > close[1] AND ((Supertrend is bullish AND trend price breakout AND trend volume breakout) OR (Supertrend is bearish AND reversal price breakout AND reversal volume breakout) OR high volume breakout OR high price breakout); -1 when close < close[1] AND ((Supertrend is bearish AND trend price breakout AND trend volume breakout) OR (Supertrend is bullish AND reversal price breakout AND reversal volume breakout) OR high volume breakout OR high price breakout); otherwise 0. Supertrend uses separate multiplier and lookback parameters.
Long: Breakout state = +1
Short: Breakout state = -1
Calculation: Most recent breakout state = current breakout state when it is nonzero, otherwise the previous most recent breakout state; initially 0.
Calculation: Long target line = high + selected value markup / 100 * TrueRange(high, low, close[1]) when Breakout state = +1, otherwise its previous nonmissing value; select the trend value markup when Supertrend is bullish and the reversal value markup when bearish.
Calculation: Short target line = low - selected value markup / 100 * TrueRange(high, low, close[1]) when Breakout state = -1, otherwise its previous nonmissing value; select the trend value markup when Supertrend is bearish and the reversal value markup when bullish.
Calculation: Long candle stop line = low - ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * stop factor when Breakout state = +1, otherwise its previous nonmissing value.
Calculation: Short candle stop line = high + ATR(close, main ATR lookback) * stop factor when Breakout state = -1, otherwise its previous nonmissing value.
Calculation: Long price signal = (if Supertrend is bullish then high else low crosses above the previous Long target line AND (Most recent breakout state > 0 OR reversal-break bypass is enabled) AND close > the previous Short target line) OR (if Supertrend is bullish then high else low crosses above the previous Short candle stop line AND (Most recent breakout state < 0 OR reversal-break bypass is enabled)) OR (Breakout state = +1 AND if Supertrend is bullish then high else low > the previous Long target line).
Calculation: Short price signal = (if Supertrend is bearish then low else high crosses below the previous Short target line AND (Most recent breakout state < 0 OR reversal-break bypass is enabled) AND close < the previous Long target line) OR (if Supertrend is bullish then low else high crosses below the previous Long candle stop line AND (Most recent breakout state > 0 OR reversal-break bypass is enabled)) OR (Breakout state = -1 AND if Supertrend is bearish then low else high < the previous Short target line).
Calculation: Long exit signal = (if Supertrend is bullish then close else low crosses below the previous Short target line) OR (if Supertrend is bullish then close else low crosses below the previous Long candle stop line).
Calculation: Short exit signal = (if Supertrend is bearish then close else high crosses above the previous Long target line) OR (if Supertrend is bearish then close else high crosses above the previous Short candle stop line).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe high = high of the last completed T1 bar, calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe low = low of the last completed T1 bar, calculated on T1 bars and mapped to chart bars.
Calculation: Yearly high filter = long-term high/low check disabled OR (((Higher-timeframe high is missing OR its value at the first historical chart-bar offset is missing OR Higher-timeframe high > that offset value) AND (Higher-timeframe high is missing OR its value at the second, longer historical chart-bar offset is missing OR Higher-timeframe high > that offset value)) OR (Higher-timeframe high is missing OR (its first-offset value is missing ? close > Higher-timeframe high : its second-offset value is missing ? close > max(Higher-timeframe high, first-offset value) : close > max(Higher-timeframe high, min(first-offset value, second-offset value))))).
Calculation: Yearly low filter = long-term high/low check disabled OR (((Higher-timeframe low is missing OR its value at the first historical chart-bar offset is missing OR Higher-timeframe low < that offset value) AND (Higher-timeframe low is missing OR its value at the second, longer historical chart-bar offset is missing OR Higher-timeframe low < that offset value)) OR (Higher-timeframe low is missing OR (its first-offset value is missing ? close < Higher-timeframe low : its second-offset value is missing ? close < min(Higher-timeframe low, first-offset value) : close < min(Higher-timeframe low, max(first-offset value, second-offset value))))).
Calculation: Alignment score = count 1 for each true comparison among close > the shortest of seven separately parameterized, successively longer SMA or EMA values of close, and each of the six comparisons that a shorter value is greater than the next longer value; score is +1 when all seven comparisons are true, -1 when close and all seven values descend in that order, otherwise when partial alignment is enabled it is the positive strong-score parameter if score > the high partial threshold, the negative strong-score parameter if score < the low partial threshold, the positive weak-score parameter if score > the middle partial threshold, or the negative weak-score parameter otherwise; if partial alignment is disabled it is 0.
Calculation: Alignment sum = sum of Alignment score over the alignment lookback.
Calculation: MA-based supertrend direction uses OHLC4 and a separately parameterized SMA or EMA lookback; lower and upper bands start at the moving average minus and plus ATR(custom ATR lookback) * band multiplier, respectively. Ratchet the lower band to max(current lower band, previous lower band) when the moving average exceeds the previous lower band; ratchet the upper band to min(current upper band, previous upper band) when the moving average is below the previous upper band. Direction starts at +1, changes from -1 to +1 when the moving average exceeds the previous upper band, changes from +1 to -1 when it falls below the previous lower band, and otherwise persists.
Calculation: Long stop price = in the Breakout state > 0 or long-position branch, use the previous Long stop price if close - stop multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) < previous Long stop price OR close < previous Short stop price, otherwise use close - stop multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback); in the Breakout state < 0 or short-position branch, use the previous Long stop price if close - compound multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) > previous Long stop price OR close > previous Long stop price, otherwise use close - compound multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback); otherwise use close - stop multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback). The first branch takes precedence.
Calculation: Short stop price = in the Breakout state > 0 or long-position branch, use the previous Short stop price if close + compound multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) < previous Short stop price OR close < previous Short stop price, otherwise use close + compound multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback); in the Breakout state < 0 or short-position branch, use the previous Short stop price if close + stop multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback) > previous Short stop price OR close > previous Long stop price, otherwise use close + stop multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback); otherwise use close + stop multiplier * ATR(close, main ATR lookback). The first branch takes precedence.
Calculation: Compound entry gate = position is flat OR (position is long AND close > Short stop price) OR (position is short AND close < Long stop price).
Long: Long price signal AND Yearly high filter AND Breakout state != -1 AND (highest(high, shorter high/low lookback) = highest(high, longer high/low lookback) OR long-term high/low check disabled) AND (Alignment sum > alignment threshold OR MA alignment check disabled) AND (MA-based supertrend direction = +1 OR MA supertrend check disabled) AND PercentRank(OHLC4, alignment lookback) > long rank threshold OR (Long price signal AND Yearly high filter AND Breakout state != -1 AND cumulative strategy net profit >= its floor parameter); also require datetime at or after January 1 of (current year minus the backtest-window-years parameter), the compound entry gate, and the direction setting to permit long entries.
Short: Short price signal AND Yearly low filter AND Breakout state != +1 AND (lowest(low, shorter high/low lookback) = lowest(low, longer high/low lookback) OR long-term high/low check disabled) AND (Alignment sum < -alignment threshold OR MA alignment check disabled) AND (MA-based supertrend direction = -1 OR MA supertrend check disabled) AND PercentRank(OHLC4, alignment lookback) < short rank threshold OR (Short price signal AND Yearly low filter AND Breakout state != +1 AND cumulative strategy net profit >= its floor parameter); also require datetime at or after January 1 of (current year minus the backtest-window-years parameter), the compound entry gate, and the direction setting to permit short entries.
Exit: long, low <= the previous Long stop price OR (exit-on-signal enabled AND Long exit signal AND Breakout state != +1 AND datetime at or after January 1 of (current year minus the backtest-window-years parameter))
Exit: short, high >= the previous Short stop price OR (exit-on-signal enabled AND Short exit signal AND Breakout state != -1 AND datetime at or after January 1 of (current year minus the backtest-window-years parameter))
