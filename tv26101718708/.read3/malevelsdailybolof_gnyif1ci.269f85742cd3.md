Calculation: MA set = five EMAs of close on T1’s last completed bar and three SMAs of close on T1’s last completed bar, carried onto chart bars; the five EMA lookbacks increase from shortest to longest, and the three SMA lookbacks match the longest three EMA lookbacks.
Calculation: Wide band = (ATR of chart bars over its lookback from the previous completed chart bar, or current close × fallback factor if unavailable) × tolerance multiplier × wide-band multiplier; narrow band = the same ATR or fallback base × tolerance multiplier × narrow-band multiplier; the wide-band multiplier exceeds the narrow-band multiplier.
Long: For any available M in MA set, (selected setup mode uses touch/reclaim AND ((low ≤ M AND close > M AND close > open) OR (close[1] ≤ M AND close > M))) OR (selected setup mode uses bands AND M − wide band ≤ low ≤ M + narrow band AND close > M AND close > open) OR (proximity-signal toggle is enabled AND M > 0 AND low > M AND the distance from M to low as a percentage of M ≤ proximity threshold AND close > open).
Short: For any available M in MA set, (selected setup mode uses touch/reclaim AND ((high ≥ M AND close < M AND close < open) OR (close[1] ≥ M AND close < M))) OR (selected setup mode uses bands AND M − narrow band ≤ high ≤ M + wide band AND close < M AND close < open) OR (proximity-signal toggle is enabled AND M > 0 AND high < M AND the distance from high to M as a percentage of M ≤ proximity threshold AND close < open).
