Calculation: multi-timeframe TEMA average = arithmetic mean of TEMA(close, TEMA lookback) calculated on T1, T2, T3, T4, and T5, using each timeframe’s bar still forming and taking its value on chart bars
Calculation: multi-timeframe SMA average = arithmetic mean of SMA(close, SMA lookback) calculated on T1, T2, T3, T4, and T5, using each timeframe’s bar still forming and taking its value on chart bars
Long: multi-timeframe TEMA average > multi-timeframe SMA average
Short: multi-timeframe TEMA average <= multi-timeframe SMA average
