Calculation: guarded ATR = max(ATR(ATR length) if available, otherwise True Range, minimum tick size)
Calculation: candle range = max(high - low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: candle body efficiency = abs(close - open) / candle range
Calculation: long close location = clamp((close - low) / candle range, close-location lower bound, close-location upper bound)
Calculation: short close location = close-location upper bound - long close location
Calculation: long trend = EMA(close, fast EMA length) > EMA(close, slow EMA length) AND EMA(close, slow EMA length) - (EMA(close, slow EMA length)[slope lookback] if available, otherwise EMA(close, slow EMA length)) >= nonnegative slope threshold AND close >= EMA(close, fast EMA length)
Calculation: short trend = EMA(close, fast EMA length) < EMA(close, slow EMA length) AND EMA(close, slow EMA length) - (EMA(close, slow EMA length)[slope lookback] if available, otherwise EMA(close, slow EMA length)) <= nonpositive slope threshold AND close <= EMA(close, fast EMA length)
Calculation: long stop proxy = lowest(low[1], invalidation proxy lookback) - guarded ATR * ATR stop buffer
Calculation: short stop proxy = highest(high[1], invalidation proxy lookback) + guarded ATR * ATR stop buffer
Calculation: long risk in ATR = abs(close - long stop proxy) / guarded ATR
Calculation: short risk in ATR = abs(close - short stop proxy) / guarded ATR
Calculation: risk-fit score for risk in ATR within its bounds = with lower bound min(minimum risk ATR, maximum risk ATR), upper bound max(minimum risk ATR, maximum risk ATR), and target clamp(risk-fit target ATR, lower bound, upper bound): if risk in ATR <= target, use full risk-fit points when target equals lower bound, otherwise lower risk-fit points + (risk in ATR - lower bound) / (target - lower bound) * (full risk-fit points - lower risk-fit points); otherwise use full risk-fit points when target equals upper bound, otherwise full risk-fit points - (risk in ATR - target) / (upper bound - target) * upper-range risk-fit penalty
Calculation: volume activation score = if volume is present and positive and SMA(volume with missing values replaced by zero, volume lookback) is present and positive, clamp((volume / SMA - weak volume-ratio threshold) / (strong volume-ratio threshold - weak volume-ratio threshold) * volume normalization scale, lower volume-score bound, upper volume-score bound); otherwise missing-volume score
Calculation: long activation score = (long trend ? full trend points : close > EMA(close, slow EMA length) ? partial trend points : weak trend points) * trend score weight + (candle body efficiency * body activation weight + long close location * close-location activation weight) * candle score weight + risk-fit score(long risk in ATR) * risk score weight + volume activation score * volume score weight
Calculation: short activation score = (short trend ? full trend points : close < EMA(close, slow EMA length) ? partial trend points : weak trend points) * trend score weight + (candle body efficiency * body activation weight + short close location * close-location activation weight) * candle score weight + risk-fit score(short risk in ATR) * risk score weight + volume activation score * volume score weight
Calculation: effective activation-score floor = manual activation-score floor for Balanced Progress or Custom; max(manual floor, Cleaner Swing floor) for Cleaner Swing; max(manual floor - Active Intraday relaxation, Active Intraday minimum floor) for Active Intraday
Calculation: effective maximum tracking bars = max(raw maximum tracking bars, minimum tracking-bars floor), where raw maximum is the input for Balanced Progress or Custom, max(input, Cleaner Swing minimum) for Cleaner Swing, and min(input, Active Intraday cap) for Active Intraday
Calculation: active progress cycle = a selected candidate activation starts a cycle at that bar's close; the cycle remains active until a later confirmed chart bar touches its stored invalidation proxy (long cycle: low <= proxy; short cycle: high >= proxy) or its age reaches effective maximum tracking bars
Calculation: long activation candidate = progress-side setting permits long AND activation-mode setting accepts the range-break condition (close > highest(high[1], structure lookback) AND close > open), the trend-pulse condition (long trend AND close > EMA(close, fast EMA length) AND low <= EMA(close, fast EMA length) AND long close location >= pulse close-location threshold), or both according to its selected mode; long stop proxy < close; long risk in ATR is inclusively between min(minimum risk ATR, maximum risk ATR) and max(minimum risk ATR, maximum risk ATR); long activation score >= effective activation-score floor
Calculation: short activation candidate = progress-side setting permits short AND activation-mode setting accepts the range-break condition (close < lowest(low[1], structure lookback) AND close < open), the trend-pulse condition (short trend AND close < EMA(close, fast EMA length) AND high >= EMA(close, fast EMA length) AND short close location >= pulse close-location threshold), or both according to its selected mode; short stop proxy > close; short risk in ATR is inclusively between min(minimum risk ATR, maximum risk ATR) and max(minimum risk ATR, maximum risk ATR); short activation score >= effective activation-score floor
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, after more than structure lookback chart bars are available, no active progress cycle remains, the long activation candidate is valid, and either the short activation candidate is invalid or long activation score >= short activation score
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, after more than structure lookback chart bars are available, no active progress cycle remains, the short activation candidate is valid, and either the long activation candidate is invalid or short activation score > long activation score
