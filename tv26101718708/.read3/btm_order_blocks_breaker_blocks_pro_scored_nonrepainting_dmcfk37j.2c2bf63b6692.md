Calculation: Bullish structure break = on chart bars, after a confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot span, pivot span) becomes the latest unbroken swing high, the configured source (close or high) is strictly above it; each swing triggers once, until a newer confirmed pivot replaces it. Bearish structure break = the corresponding confirmed Pivot Low(low, pivot span, pivot span) with configured source (close or low) strictly below it. Structure direction becomes the side of the latest break and starts neutral. When configured, evaluate breaks and zone events only on confirmed bars.
Calculation: Bullish OB origin = the lowest low from the broken swing-high bar through the bar before the break, limited by the OB search lookback; bearish OB origin = the highest high over the corresponding swing-low range. Use the origin candle unless configured to use the last opposite candle: if the origin candle is not already opposite-colored, select the first opposite-colored older candle within the opposite-candle search length, or keep the origin candle if none exists. With wick range, bullish zone top is the selected candle's high and bottom is the lower of its low and the origin low; bearish zone top is the higher of its high and the origin high and bottom is its low. With body range, use the selected candle's open/close range.
Calculation: A bullish OB is intact when every close after its selected candle through the break is at or above its bottom; a bearish OB is intact when every such close is at or below its top. Displacement exists when any of those bars has absolute close-open at least the displacement ATR multiple times ATR(ATR length) on that bar. A bullish/bearish FVG exists when the corresponding three-candle fair value gap occurs between the break and selected candle. A bullish/bearish liquidity sweep exists when the origin low/high strictly exceeds the most recent eligible retained confirmed pivot low/high preceding the origin candle. Volume qualifies when volume at the selected candle is at least the volume multiple times SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) there, with a positive available average. OB quality score is the count of displacement, FVG, sweep, and qualifying volume. An OB qualifies only if intact, its configured required displacement/FVG/sweep/volume filters pass (missing volume data passes the volume filter), its score meets the minimum score, its height is at most the maximum zone-height multiple times ATR(ATR length) on the break bar, and, when configured, it does not overlap an active nonbreaker OB of the same direction.
Calculation: A zone is active after its birth bar until it fails. On a later bar, a bullish support zone fails when the configured failure source (close or low) is strictly below its bottom; a bearish resistance zone fails when the configured source (close or high) is strictly above its top. A failed OB becomes a breaker with the opposite direction if breaker creation is enabled and, when required, the failure bar's absolute close-open is at least the displacement ATR multiple times ATR(ATR length); otherwise the failed OB is removed. A failed breaker is removed. When configured, remove an OB after its first retest.
Calculation: An active bullish support zone is retested when low <= its top and the prior bar's low > its top; an active bearish resistance zone is retested when high >= its bottom and the prior bar's high < its bottom. Retests are evaluated only after the zone's birth bar and only when the zone has not failed on that bar.
Long: bullish structure break OR the latest structure break is bullish OR an active bullish OB or bullish breaker exists OR a qualified bullish OB is created OR a bearish OB fails and creates a bullish breaker OR an active bullish OB or bullish breaker is retested
Short: bearish structure break OR the latest structure break is bearish OR an active bearish OB or bearish breaker exists OR a qualified bearish OB is created OR a bullish OB fails and creates a bearish breaker OR an active bearish OB or bearish breaker is retested
