Long: close >= Bollinger upper band(BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) AND close[1] < Bollinger upper band(BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) on the current bar AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long RSI threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) > ADX threshold AND ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) > ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback)[1] AND +DI(ADX lookback) > -DI(ADX lookback)
Short: close <= Bollinger lower band(BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) AND close[1] > Bollinger lower band(BB lookback, BB deviation multiplier) on the current bar AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short RSI threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) > ADX threshold AND ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) > ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback)[1] AND -DI(ADX lookback) > +DI(ADX lookback)
Calculation: Long risk = min(entry price - low on long-entry signal bar, entry price * maximum risk percentage / 100)
Calculation: Short risk = min(high on short-entry signal bar - entry price, entry price * maximum risk percentage / 100)
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - Long risk OR high >= entry price + reward-risk multiple * Long risk OR hour(datetime) = exit hour AND minute(datetime) >= exit minute
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + Short risk OR low <= entry price - reward-risk multiple * Short risk OR hour(datetime) = exit hour AND minute(datetime) >= exit minute
