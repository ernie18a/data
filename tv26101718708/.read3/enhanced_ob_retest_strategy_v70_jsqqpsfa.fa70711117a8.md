Calculation: Zones overlap = both zones exist AND NOT (bottom A > top B OR top A < bottom B); touching bounds overlap.
Calculation: Bullish OB zone = on chart bars using current mapped T1 security values (lookahead on: current T1 bar, still forming live), when T1 close > highest(T1 high shifted one chart bar, over the swing-lookback chart bars) AND 100 * abs(T1 close - swing high) / nz(swing high, T1 close) >= minimum-breakout input / 100, select the most recent prior bearish chart candle within the OB-search lookback; if found and abs(mapped T1 open[idx] - mapped T1 close[idx]) >= minimum-OB-size input / 100 * chart ATR(ATR lookback), set zone bounds to the min and max of those mapped T1 values, replacing the prior bullish zone or unioning bounds if they overlap. After candidate creation, a bearish zone becomes the bullish zone when chart close > its top + flip ATR multiple * chart ATR(ATR lookback), clearing the bearish zone and any prior bullish zone.
Calculation: Bearish OB zone = on chart bars using current mapped T1 security values (lookahead on: current T1 bar, still forming live), when T1 close < lowest(T1 low shifted one chart bar, over the swing-lookback chart bars) AND 100 * abs(T1 close - swing low) / nz(swing low, T1 close) >= minimum-breakout input / 100, select the most recent prior bullish chart candle within the OB-search lookback; if found and abs(mapped T1 open[idx] - mapped T1 close[idx]) >= minimum-OB-size input / 100 * chart ATR(ATR lookback), set zone bounds to the min and max of those mapped T1 values, replacing the prior bearish zone or unioning bounds if they overlap. After candidate creation and bullish-flip handling, a bullish zone becomes the bearish zone when chart close < its bottom - flip ATR multiple * chart ATR(ATR lookback), clearing the bullish zone and any prior bearish zone.
Calculation: Upper wick zone = the interval from max(open, close) to high on the latest chart bar where high > max(open, close); retain until chart close > its top.
Calculation: Lower wick zone = the interval from low to min(open, close) on the latest chart bar where low < min(open, close); retain until chart close < its bottom.
Calculation: Trade available = bullish and bearish OB zones do not overlap AND position is flat AND there is no prior entry or the chart bars since the most recent entry are at least the cooldown parameter.
Long: longs enabled AND backtest start datetime reached AND bullish OB use enabled AND Trade available AND close is strictly between the bullish OB zone bounds AND (zone top - close) / (zone top - zone bottom) >= minimum-retrace input / 100 AND (zone top - close) / (zone top - zone bottom) <= maximum-entry-depth input / 100
Short: shorts enabled AND backtest start datetime reached AND bearish OB use enabled AND Trade available AND close is strictly between the bearish OB zone bounds AND (close - zone bottom) / (zone top - zone bottom) >= minimum-retrace input / 100 AND (close - zone bottom) / (zone top - zone bottom) <= maximum-entry-depth input / 100

Calculation: Long initial stop = entry-signal chart close - trailing ATR multiple * chart ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar.
Calculation: Short initial stop = entry-signal chart close + trailing ATR multiple * chart ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initially the long initial stop; on each chart bar with a long position, max(previous stop, close - trailing ATR multiple * chart ATR(ATR lookback)); when breakeven is enabled and close >= average entry price + (average entry price - long initial stop), max that stop with average entry price.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initially the short initial stop; on each chart bar with a short position, min(previous stop, close + trailing ATR multiple * chart ATR(ATR lookback)); when breakeven is enabled and close <= average entry price - (short initial stop - average entry price), min that stop with average entry price.
Exit: Long, low <= long trailing stop
Exit: Short, high >= short trailing stop
