Calculation: Opening gap = open of the current RTH opening bar − close of the prior RTH session's last bar
Calculation: Minimum gap = configured points threshold in points mode; current RTH opening price × configured percent / 100 in percent mode; configured ATR multiple × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar) in ATR mode, using the T1 value on chart bars and falling back to the configured points threshold if unavailable
Long: On the confirmed RTH opening bar, a prior RTH close exists AND Opening gap <= 0
Short: On the confirmed RTH opening bar, a prior RTH close exists AND Opening gap > 0
Long: abs(Opening gap) >= Minimum gap AND Opening gap <= 0 AND on a confirmed chart bar inside the configured RTH session before the detection cutoff AND the opening range is locked AND low < Highest(high over chart bars from the RTH opening bar through the configured opening-range lock) AND close > Lowest(low over chart bars from the RTH opening bar through the configured opening-range lock) AND no earlier qualifying low sweep occurred this session
Short: abs(Opening gap) >= Minimum gap AND Opening gap > 0 AND on a confirmed chart bar inside the configured RTH session before the detection cutoff AND the opening range is locked AND high > Highest(high over chart bars from the RTH opening bar through the configured opening-range lock) AND close < Highest(high over chart bars from the RTH opening bar through the configured opening-range lock) AND no earlier qualifying high sweep occurred this session
Long: abs(Opening gap) >= Minimum gap AND on a confirmed chart bar inside the configured RTH session before the detection cutoff AND the current and previous chart pivot lows (PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length)) are centered in this RTH session AND the current pivot low < the previous pivot low AND the opening range is locked or SMT inside the opening range is enabled AND both-directions mode is enabled or Opening gap <= 0 AND, at the current and previous chart pivot-low center bars, the low of each eligible comparison index sampled on chart bars is higher now than before; if both-symbol mode is enabled and two comparison indices are eligible, both must be higher, otherwise at least one must be higher (eligible indices differ from the chart's index family and have both samples available)
Short: abs(Opening gap) >= Minimum gap AND on a confirmed chart bar inside the configured RTH session before the detection cutoff AND the current and previous chart pivot highs (PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length)) are centered in this RTH session AND the current pivot high > the previous pivot high AND the opening range is locked or SMT inside the opening range is enabled AND both-directions mode is enabled or Opening gap > 0 AND, at the current and previous chart pivot-high center bars, the high of each eligible comparison index sampled on chart bars is lower now than before; if both-symbol mode is enabled and two comparison indices are eligible, both must be lower, otherwise at least one must be lower (eligible indices differ from the chart's index family and have both samples available)
