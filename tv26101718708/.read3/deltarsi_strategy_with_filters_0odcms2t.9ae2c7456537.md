Calculation: Polynomial RSI derivative = derivative at the newest point of a degree-order least-squares polynomial fit to the last window values of RSI(close, RSI lookback)
Calculation: Long trailing stop = max(entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage), close * (1 - stop-loss percentage), previous long trailing stop)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = min(entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage), close * (1 + stop-loss percentage), previous short trailing stop)
Long: Long entries enabled AND datetime within the configured test interval AND ((long-entry mode selects zero-crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses above zero) OR (long-entry mode selects signal-line crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses above EMA(polynomial RSI derivative, signal lookback)) OR (long-entry mode selects direction change AND polynomial RSI derivative > polynomial RSI derivative[1] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] < polynomial RSI derivative[2] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] < 0) AND (long-entry filters are not applied OR (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume multiplier * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(shorter lookback) > ATR(longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) > lower RSI bound AND RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) < upper RSI bound))
Short: Short entries enabled AND datetime within the configured test interval AND ((short-entry mode selects zero-crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses below zero) OR (short-entry mode selects signal-line crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses below EMA(polynomial RSI derivative, signal lookback)) OR (short-entry mode selects direction change AND polynomial RSI derivative < polynomial RSI derivative[1] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] > polynomial RSI derivative[2] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] > 0)) AND (short-entry filters are not applied OR (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume multiplier * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(shorter lookback) > ATR(longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) > lower RSI bound AND RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) < upper RSI bound))
Exit: long, (exit mode selects zero-crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses below zero OR exit mode selects signal-line crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses below EMA(polynomial RSI derivative, signal lookback) OR exit mode selects direction change AND polynomial RSI derivative < polynomial RSI derivative[1] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] > polynomial RSI derivative[2] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] > 0) AND (exit filters are not applied OR (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume multiplier * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(shorter lookback) > ATR(longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) > lower RSI bound AND RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) < upper RSI bound)) AND no qualifying short entry OR datetime outside the configured test interval OR stop loss enabled AND low <= Long trailing stop OR take profit enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, (exit mode selects zero-crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses below zero OR exit mode selects signal-line crossing AND polynomial RSI derivative crosses below EMA(polynomial RSI derivative, signal lookback) OR exit mode selects direction change AND polynomial RSI derivative < polynomial RSI derivative[1] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] > polynomial RSI derivative[2] AND polynomial RSI derivative[1] > 0) AND (exit filters are not applied OR (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume multiplier * SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR(shorter lookback) > ATR(longer lookback)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) > lower RSI bound AND RSI(close, filter RSI lookback) < upper RSI bound)) AND no qualifying long entry OR datetime outside the configured test interval OR stop loss enabled AND high >= Short trailing stop OR take profit enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage)
