Calculation: Prior RTH high/low = highest(high)/lowest(low) of T1 bars in the preceding completed RTH session, calculated on T1 bars and read on chart bars
Calculation: Latest RTH close = close of the latest T1 bar in the current or most recent RTH session, including a still-forming T1 bar, held constant outside RTH and read on chart bars
Long: Latest RTH close > Prior RTH high
Short: Latest RTH close < Prior RTH low
