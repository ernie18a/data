Long: On a confirmed bar, close > a tracked, unbroken confirmed swing-high level + ATR(lookback) * break-buffer multiplier AND close[1] <= that level
Short: On a confirmed bar, close < a tracked, unbroken confirmed swing-low level - ATR(lookback) * break-buffer multiplier AND close[1] >= that level
Calculation: entry price = close of the retest bar
Calculation: stop = for a long, min(min(entry-bar low, preceding-bar low, broken resistance) - ATR-at-entry * stop-buffer multiplier, entry price - ATR-at-entry * minimum-stop multiplier); for a short, max(max(entry-bar high, preceding-bar high, broken support) + ATR-at-entry * stop-buffer multiplier, entry price + ATR-at-entry * minimum-stop multiplier)
Calculation: stop distance = abs(entry price - stop)
Calculation: third target = entry price + position direction * stop distance * third-target multiple, where position direction is +1 for long and -1 for short
Long: On a confirmed bar, a tracked broken-up level has not failed by a close below level - ATR(lookback) * break-buffer multiplier AND minimum retest delay <= bars since break <= maximum retest window AND low <= level + ATR(lookback) * retest-zone multiplier AND close > level AND close > open AND (strong-break filter is disabled OR abs(breakout close - breakout open) >= ATR-at-break * body-strength multiplier OR (breakout volume > 0 AND breakout volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)-at-break * volume multiplier)) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND stop distance <= ATR-at-entry * maximum-stop multiplier AND position side is not long
Short: On a confirmed bar, a tracked broken-down level has not failed by a close above level + ATR(lookback) * break-buffer multiplier AND minimum retest delay <= bars since break <= maximum retest window AND high >= level - ATR(lookback) * retest-zone multiplier AND close < level AND close < open AND (strong-break filter is disabled OR abs(breakout close - breakout open) >= ATR-at-break * body-strength multiplier OR (breakout volume > 0 AND breakout volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)-at-break * volume multiplier)) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close, trend lookback)) AND stop distance <= ATR-at-entry * maximum-stop multiplier AND position side is not short
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= third target
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= third target
