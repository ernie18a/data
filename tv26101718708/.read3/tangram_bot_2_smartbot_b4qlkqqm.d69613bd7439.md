Calculation: Moving-average signal = +1 when the short-period SMA or EMA of its selected, shifted source crosses above the long-period SMA or EMA of its separately selected, shifted source in crossover mode, or is above it in level mode; -1 for the corresponding cross below or below condition; otherwise 0.
Calculation: HiLo signal = +1 in stair-direction mode when low > HiLo Activator(high, low, lookback), and -1 otherwise; in direction-change mode, +1 when close > SMA(high, lookback) and close[1] < SMA(low, lookback)[1], or -1 when close < SMA(low, lookback) and close[1] > SMA(high, lookback)[1]; otherwise 0.
Calculation: MACD signal = +1 when the MACD line crosses above its signal line in crossover mode or is above it in level mode; -1 when it crosses below in crossover mode or is less than or equal to it in level mode; otherwise 0. When the MACD filter is enabled, a positive signal requires the MACD line to be at least the filter threshold, and a negative signal requires it to be at most the threshold. The MACD line is the selected SMA or EMA of the source at the short lookback minus that at the long lookback; the signal line is the same selected average of the MACD line at its separate lookback.
Calculation: ADX signal = +1 when +DI crosses above -DI in crossover mode or +DI > -DI in level mode; -1 when -DI crosses above +DI in crossover mode or -DI > +DI in level mode; otherwise 0. Either signal is valid only when ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds, inclusive, and satisfies the selected ADX trend setting: unrestricted, rising, or falling. Use the separate DI lookback and ADX smoothing parameters.
Calculation: Stochastic signal = +1 when smoothed %K crosses above %D in crossover mode or %K > %D in level mode; -1 when it crosses below or %K < %D; otherwise 0. A positive signal requires %K <= its minimum threshold, and a negative signal requires %K >= its maximum threshold. %K is SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback), K smoothing); %D is SMA(%K, D smoothing).
Calculation: VWAP signal = +1 on close crossing above VWAP in breakout mode, or close > VWAP in above/below mode; -1 on crossing below in breakout mode, or close < VWAP in above/below mode. In the reversed above/below mode, +1 requires close < VWAP and -1 requires close > VWAP; otherwise 0.
Calculation: RSI signal = +1 when RSI(source, lookback) crosses above its oversold threshold in crossover mode or is below that threshold in level mode; -1 when it crosses below its separately configured overbought threshold in crossover mode or is above it in level mode; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bollinger signal = +1 when the selected source crosses above the lower Bollinger Band in crossover mode or is below it in level mode; -1 when it crosses below the upper band in crossover mode or is above it in level mode; otherwise 0. Use the selected source, lookback, and deviation multiplier.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = let previous stop be nz(previous ATR trailing stop, 0) and loss be ATR(lookback) times the multiplier; use max(previous stop, close - loss) when both close and close[1] exceed the previous stop, min(previous stop, close + loss) when both are below it, close - loss when close exceeds it, and close + loss otherwise.
Calculation: ATR signal = +1 when close crosses above the ATR trailing stop in direction-change mode or close > the stop in trend-following mode; -1 when close crosses below it or close < it, respectively; otherwise 0.
Calculation: SAR signal = +1 when close crosses above Parabolic SAR(acceleration factor, increment, maximum factor) in direction-change mode or close > it in trend-following mode; -1 when close crosses below it or close < it, respectively; otherwise 0.
Long: Long entries enabled AND datetime is after the configured backtest start and before its configured end AND datetime is within the entry session AND current-day strategy P&L is below its gain limit and above the negative loss limit AND the chart bar is confirmed AND at least one entry-enabled signal is active AND every entry-enabled moving-average, HiLo, MACD, ADX, stochastic, VWAP, RSI, Bollinger, ATR, and SAR signal is +1 AND repeat prevention is disabled or the previous chart bar did not have every entry-enabled signal positive.
Short: Short entries enabled AND datetime is after the configured backtest start and before its configured end AND datetime is within the entry session AND current-day strategy P&L is below its gain limit and above the negative loss limit AND the chart bar is confirmed AND at least one entry-enabled signal is active AND every entry-enabled moving-average, HiLo, MACD, ADX, stochastic, VWAP, RSI, Bollinger, ATR, and SAR signal is -1 AND repeat prevention is disabled or the previous chart bar did not have every entry-enabled signal negative.
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured backtest date range and flatten session AND (all-exits mode is selected and every exit-enabled signal is -1, OR any-exit mode is selected and at least one exit-enabled signal is -1).
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured backtest date range and flatten session AND (all-exits mode is selected and every exit-enabled signal is +1, OR any-exit mode is selected and at least one exit-enabled signal is +1).
Exit: long, fixed stop is enabled and low <= entry price - fixed stop distance OR fixed target is enabled and high >= entry price + fixed target distance OR partial target is enabled and high >= entry price + partial target distance OR a partial exit has filled and break-even-after-partial is enabled and low <= entry price OR trailing activation and offset are enabled and highest high since entry >= entry price + activation distance and low <= highest high since entry - trailing offset.
Exit: short, fixed stop is enabled and high >= entry price + fixed stop distance OR fixed target is enabled and low <= entry price - fixed target distance OR partial target is enabled and low <= entry price - partial target distance OR a partial exit has filled and break-even-after-partial is enabled and high >= entry price OR trailing activation and offset are enabled and lowest low since entry <= entry price - activation distance and high >= lowest low since entry + trailing offset.
Exit: long, datetime is outside the configured backtest date range or flatten session OR current-day strategy P&L >= its gain limit OR current-day strategy P&L <= negative its loss limit.
Exit: short, datetime is outside the configured backtest date range or flatten session OR current-day strategy P&L >= its gain limit OR current-day strategy P&L <= negative its loss limit.
