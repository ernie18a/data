Calculation: PSMA raw = 100 × the count of integer MA lengths from the PSMA minimum length through the PSMA maximum length, inclusive, for which the PSMA source field > SMA(PSMA source field, MA length), divided by the number of lengths in that range.
Calculation: SupOsc = clamp((close − trend band) / (upper band − lower band), −1, 1); base upper/lower bands = hl2 ± ATR(ATR length) × ATR multiplier; upper band = min(base upper band, prior upper band) if prior close < prior upper band, otherwise base upper band; lower band = max(base lower band, prior lower band) if prior close > prior lower band, otherwise base lower band; trend switches bullish when close > prior upper band, bearish when close < prior lower band, and otherwise retains its prior direction; trend band is the lower band when bullish and upper band when bearish.
Calculation: StochSup %K and direction = T1 close sampled onto chart bars, using the last completed T1 bar when the close-wait setting is enabled and the still-forming T1 bar otherwise; calculate RSI, StochRSI, and trend bands on chart bars; %K = SMA((RSI − Lowest(RSI, K period)) / (Highest(RSI, K period) − Lowest(RSI, K period)) × 100, K smoothing), where RSI uses the RSI length; upper/lower bands start at %K ± StochSup multiplier and trail as upper = upper base if upper base < prior upper or prior %K > prior upper, otherwise prior upper, and lower = lower base if lower base > prior lower or prior %K < prior lower, otherwise prior lower; direction starts bearish, then if the prior trend band equals the prior upper band direction is bullish only when %K > upper band, otherwise direction is bearish only when %K < lower band; trend band is lower when bullish and upper when bearish.
Calculation: Effort normalization(value, average) = score 1 when value / average > cutoff 1, otherwise score 2 when > cutoff 2, continuing through seven descending ratio cutoffs and their corresponding scores, with score 8 as the fallback.
Calculation: Relative Rate of Flow = 2 × (100 − 100 / (1 + bulls / bears)) − 100, where bulls and bears are the selected moving averages of positive and absolute negative flow, using the RROF flow length and selected MA type; flow = price score × volume score / 100; price score is the mean of 2 × (close − low) / (high − low) × 100 − 100, (close − open) / (high − low) × 100, sign(close − open) × Effort normalization(abs(close − open), MA(abs(close − open), normalization lookback, lookback MA type)) × 100, 2 × (close − Lowest(low, short-range lookback)) / (Highest(high, short-range lookback) − Lowest(low, short-range lookback)) × 100 − 100, (close − close[1]) / (Highest(high, short-range lookback) − Lowest(low, short-range lookback)) × 100, and sign(close − close[1]) × Effort normalization(abs(close − close[1]), MA(abs(close − close[1]), normalization lookback, lookback MA type)) × 100; volume score = Effort normalization(volume, MA(volume, normalization lookback, lookback MA type)) × 100; lookback MA type is SMA in Simple mode and otherwise the selected MA type.
Calculation: Smoothed Relative Rate of Flow = MA(Relative Rate of Flow, RROF smoothing length, selected MA type).
Long: StochSup direction flips bullish AND StochSup %K < StochSup midpoint AND SupOsc crosses above negative SupOsc crossing magnitude AND Pearson correlation( LCO source field, bar number, LCO length) < LCO lower level AND Smoothed Relative Rate of Flow > Smoothed Relative Rate of Flow[1] AND Williams %R(close, Willy length)[1] < Willy oversold level AND PSMA raw < PSMA buy level AND EMA(PSMA raw, PSMA smoothing length) < PSMA buy level.
Short: StochSup direction flips bearish AND StochSup %K > StochSup midpoint AND SupOsc crosses below positive SupOsc crossing magnitude AND Pearson correlation(LCO source field, bar number, LCO length) > LCO upper level AND Smoothed Relative Rate of Flow <= Smoothed Relative Rate of Flow[1] AND Williams %R(close, Willy length)[1] > Willy overbought level AND PSMA raw > PSMA sell level AND EMA(PSMA raw, PSMA smoothing length) > PSMA sell level.
