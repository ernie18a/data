Calculation: ZigZag pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the pivot-depth parameter on both sides; replace the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type, or append an opposite-type pivot when its percentage distance from the latest pivot meets the effective deviation; if both pivot types confirm together, choose the one farther from the latest pivot, or the high when the sequence is empty. Effective deviation = pivot-deviation parameter × (one + ATR(ATR lookback) / close × deviation-volatility multiplier). Pivots are processed on confirmed chart bars and dated at their pivot bars.
Calculation: Fib retracement(start, end, retracement) = absolute value of (retracement − end) / absolute value of (end − start).
Calculation: Bullish wave sequence = successive ZigZag pivots W0 low, W1 high above W0, W2 low above W0 and below W1, W3 high above W1 with W3−W2 at least the minimum W3/W1 ratio × (W1−W0), W4 low above W1 and below W3, and W5 high above W3; accept each stage only after its pivot and within the maximum-bars-per-wave limit measured from the prior stage pivot. Accept W5 only when W3−W2 is at least the smaller of W1−W0 and W5−W4. Reset the sequence when a required pivot fails its conditions or a stage times out.
Calculation: Bearish wave sequence = successive ZigZag pivots W0 high, W1 low below W0, W2 high below W0 and above W1, W3 low below W1 with W2−W3 at least the minimum W3/W1 ratio × (W0−W1), W4 high below W1 and above W3, and W5 low below W3; accept each stage only after its pivot and within the maximum-bars-per-wave limit measured from the prior stage pivot. Accept W5 only when W2−W3 is at least the smaller of W0−W1 and W4−W5. Reset the sequence when a required pivot fails its conditions or a stage times out.
Calculation: W2 confidence = prioritized score lookup for Fib retracement(W0, W1, W2) against its separate target-and-score parameters using effective Fibonacci tolerance; otherwise use its configured broad-range fallback score when within its configured bounds, else zero.
Calculation: W4 confidence = the weighted sum of the prioritized W4 retracement score lookup, the prioritized W3/W1 size-ratio score lookup, and the W3 volume score, clamped to the confidence range. W3 volume score is the configured full score when the W3 volume spike passes, the configured reduced score when the volume filter is enabled and the spike fails, or the configured no-filter score when the volume filter is disabled. The W3 size-ratio and W4 retracement score tables and the three W4 score weights are separate parameters.
Calculation: W5 Fibonacci score = sum of four prioritized target-and-score lookups: Fib retracement(W0, W1, W2), W3/W1 size ratio, Fib retracement(W2, W3, W4), and W5/W1 size ratio; use effective Fibonacci tolerance and clamp to the confidence range. Each lookup has its own target-and-score parameters and fallback range parameters.
Calculation: W5 wave-ratio score = sum of configured score points for each condition met: W3 size is at least the smaller of W1 and W5; W2/W1 size ratio is within its configured bounds; W4 size is less than W2 size; and W5 size is no greater than the configured extension factor times W3 size, with the extension factor selected by the wave-extension setting. Clamp to the confidence range.
Calculation: W5 time-symmetry score = the confidence ceiling minus the median of the absolute deviations of W1, W3, and W5 durations from their mean, each divided by that mean and scaled to the confidence range; use zero when the mean duration is zero, and clamp to the confidence range.
Calculation: W5 confidence = W5 Fibonacci score × normalized Fibonacci score weight + W5 wave-ratio score × normalized ratio score weight + W5 time-symmetry score × normalized time score weight; normalize the three separate score-weight parameters by their sum, use their configured fallback weights when the sum is zero, add the configured regime adjustment when the matching regime is valid or subtract it otherwise, then clamp to the confidence range.
Calculation: Dynamic confidence threshold = the base threshold when volatility-adjustment is disabled; otherwise the lesser of the confidence cap and the base threshold plus the lesser of the maximum volatility adjustment and ATR(ATR lookback) / close × the volatility-adjustment multiplier. W2, W4, and W5 use separate base thresholds.
Long: Bullish wave sequence reaches W2 AND W2 confidence ≥ dynamic W2 confidence threshold AND (regime filter disabled OR bullish regime valid) AND chart ADX ≥ effective ADX threshold × choppiness multiplier AND (liquidity filter disabled OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > minimum average volume) AND long signals permitted AND current chart bar index − most recent signal bar ≥ cooldown bars AND chart bars elapsed ≥ the greater of the slow trend EMA lookback and warm-up lookback AND chart bar confirmed.
Long: Bullish wave sequence reaches W4 AND W4 confidence ≥ dynamic W4 confidence threshold AND (regime filter disabled OR bullish regime valid) AND chart ADX ≥ effective ADX threshold × choppiness multiplier AND (liquidity filter disabled OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > minimum average volume) AND W3 volume gate passes AND close ≥ W4 price AND W4 entries enabled AND long signals permitted AND current chart bar index − most recent signal bar ≥ cooldown bars AND chart bars elapsed ≥ the greater of the slow trend EMA lookback and warm-up lookback AND chart bar confirmed.
Short: Bearish wave sequence reaches W2 AND W2 confidence ≥ dynamic W2 confidence threshold AND (regime filter disabled OR bearish regime valid) AND chart ADX ≥ effective ADX threshold × choppiness multiplier AND (liquidity filter disabled OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > minimum average volume) AND short signals permitted AND current chart bar index − most recent signal bar ≥ cooldown bars AND chart bars elapsed ≥ the greater of the slow trend EMA lookback and warm-up lookback AND chart bar confirmed.
Short: Bearish wave sequence reaches W4 AND W4 confidence ≥ dynamic W4 confidence threshold AND (regime filter disabled OR bearish regime valid) AND chart ADX ≥ effective ADX threshold × choppiness multiplier AND (liquidity filter disabled OR SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) > minimum average volume) AND W3 volume gate passes AND close ≤ W4 price AND W4 entries enabled AND short signals permitted AND current chart bar index − most recent signal bar ≥ cooldown bars AND chart bars elapsed ≥ the greater of the slow trend EMA lookback and warm-up lookback AND chart bar confirmed.
Exit: long, bullish wave sequence reaches W5 AND W5 confidence ≥ dynamic W5 confidence threshold AND (RSI divergence filter disabled OR W5 price > W3 price AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) at W5 pivot < RSI(close, RSI lookback) at W3 pivot) AND chart bars elapsed ≥ the greater of the slow trend EMA lookback and warm-up lookback AND chart bar confirmed.
Exit: short, bearish wave sequence reaches W5 AND W5 confidence ≥ dynamic W5 confidence threshold AND (RSI divergence filter disabled OR W5 price < W3 price AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) at W5 pivot > RSI(close, RSI lookback) at W3 pivot) AND chart bars elapsed ≥ the greater of the slow trend EMA lookback and warm-up lookback AND chart bar confirmed.
