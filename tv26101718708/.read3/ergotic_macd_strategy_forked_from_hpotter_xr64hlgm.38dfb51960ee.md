Calculation: Price trend = selected price field when smoothing is disabled, otherwise EMA(selected price field, smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Flow index = RSI(sum(volume * (change(selected price field) <= 0 ? 0 : selected price field), flow lookback), sum(volume * (change(selected price field) >= 0 ? 0 : selected price field), flow lookback))
Long: datetime is within the configured date window AND MACD(selected price field, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) line is above its signal line (or equal while the retained direction is long), with direction optionally reversed and initially neutral; AND RSI(selected price field, RSI lookback) <= upper RSI threshold; AND Flow index <= upper flow threshold; AND (RSI trend filter disabled OR rising(RSI(selected price field, RSI lookback), RSI trend lookback)); AND (flow trend filter disabled OR rising(Flow index, flow trend lookback)); AND (price trend filter disabled OR rising(Price trend, price trend lookback)); AND the trailing-stop condition is false
Short: (datetime is within the configured date window AND MACD(selected price field, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) line is below its signal line (or equal while the retained direction is short), with direction optionally reversed and initially neutral; AND RSI(selected price field, RSI lookback) >= lower RSI threshold; AND Flow index >= lower flow threshold; AND (price trend filter disabled OR falling(Price trend, price trend lookback))) OR (long trailing-stop tracking is active AND the trailing-stop source from T1, mapped onto chart bars using the last completed T1 bar historically and the still-forming T1 bar in realtime, is at or below its highest value since first reaching entry price × (1 + trigger parameter), multiplied by (1 - trail-stop parameter))
Exit: Long, low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss parameter) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit parameter)
