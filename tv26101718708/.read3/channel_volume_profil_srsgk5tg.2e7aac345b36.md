Calculation: Channel basis = Linear Regression(close, regression lookback)
Calculation: Channel slope = SMA(Linear Regression(close, regression lookback, current bar) - Linear Regression(close, regression lookback, previous bar), slope smoothing length)
Calculation: Upper channel offset = max(Highest(high - channel basis, channel and profile lookback) × channel width multiplier, minimum rail offset in ticks × minimum tick size)
Calculation: Lower channel offset = min(Lowest(low - channel basis, channel and profile lookback) × channel width multiplier, -minimum rail offset in ticks × minimum tick size)
Calculation: Channel range = max(upper channel offset - lower channel offset, minimum channel range in ticks × minimum tick size)
Calculation: Projected upper and lower rails at offset n = channel basis - channel slope × n + respective channel offset
Calculation: Qualifying pivot high or low = confirmed Pivot High(high) or Pivot Low(low), using separate left and right lookbacks, whose distance from close at the pivot bar is at least ATR(ATR lookback) × pivot distance multiplier; the latest qualifying pivots are the swing and liquidity references
Calculation: Lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / max(abs(close - open), minimum tick size)
Calculation: Upper wick ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / max(abs(close - open), minimum tick size)
Calculation: IDM marker = qualifying pivot low in rising structure or qualifying pivot high in falling structure; structure starts from channel slope and flips on a close beyond the latest qualifying opposite pivot; the selected IDM validation mode requires a candidate sweep beyond the pivot by ATR(ATR lookback) × IDM sweep tolerance with a close back across the pivot, may additionally require a same-bar break of structure in the trend direction, or permits an unswept candidate
Calculation: Bullish sweep = (low < latest qualifying pivot low - ATR(ATR lookback) × pivot sweep tolerance AND close > that pivot low OR low <= channel-anchored Volume Profile VAL AND close > VAL OR low <= projected lower rail + channel range × rail touch tolerance AND close > projected lower rail) AND lower wick ratio >= wick rejection multiplier × sweep wick threshold multiplier
Calculation: Bearish sweep = (high > latest qualifying pivot high + ATR(ATR lookback) × pivot sweep tolerance AND close < that pivot high OR high >= channel-anchored Volume Profile VAH AND close < VAH OR high >= projected upper rail - channel range × rail touch tolerance AND close < projected upper rail) AND upper wick ratio >= wick rejection multiplier × sweep wick threshold multiplier
Calculation: Bullish A score = lower-rail sweep weight if low <= projected lower rail + channel range × rail touch tolerance and close > projected lower rail, plus VAL sweep weight if low <= VAL and close > VAL, plus full-wick weight if lower wick ratio >= wick rejection multiplier or otherwise partial-wick weight if it >= partial-wick threshold multiplier × wick rejection multiplier, plus volume weight if volume > SMA(volume, signal volume lookback) × signal volume multiplier, plus POC-reclaim weight if low < channel-anchored Volume Profile POC and close > POC, plus IDM-confluence weight if an IDM marker occurred within the IDM confluence lookback
Calculation: Bearish A score = upper-rail sweep weight if high >= projected upper rail - channel range × rail touch tolerance and close < projected upper rail, plus VAH sweep weight if high >= VAH and close < VAH, plus full-wick weight if upper wick ratio >= wick rejection multiplier or otherwise partial-wick weight if it >= partial-wick threshold multiplier × wick rejection multiplier, plus volume weight if volume > SMA(volume, signal volume lookback) × signal volume multiplier, plus POC-rejection weight if high > channel-anchored Volume Profile POC and close < POC, plus IDM-confluence weight if an IDM marker occurred within the IDM confluence lookback
Long: bullish sweep
Short: bearish sweep

Short: confirmed bar AND high >= projected upper rail - channel range × rail touch tolerance AND close < projected upper rail AND upper wick ratio >= wick rejection multiplier
Long: bullish sweep AND bullish A score >= minimum A score AND (candle direction filter disabled OR close >= open) AND (signal volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, signal volume lookback) × signal volume multiplier)
Short: bearish sweep AND bearish A score >= minimum A score AND (candle direction filter disabled OR close <= open) AND (signal volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, signal volume lookback) × signal volume multiplier)
