Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) if available, otherwise true range, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Base high = highest(high[1], base lookback); base low = lowest(low[1], base lookback)
Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative volume lookback) when the SMA is positive, otherwise 1
Calculation: Breakout score = round(clamp(28 + clamp(body-to-range ratio at breakout * 35, 0, 28) + clamp(relative volume at breakout * 16, 0, 24) + clamp(abs(breakout close - breakout base edge) / safe ATR at breakout * 24, 0, 24) + clamp(breakout base range / safe ATR at breakout * 8, 0, 14), 0, 100))
Long: (base high and base low are available AND base high - base low is between safe ATR * minimum base-range ATR multiplier and safe ATR * maximum base-range ATR multiplier AND close > base high + safe ATR * breakout-buffer ATR multiplier AND close[1] <= base high + safe ATR * breakout-buffer ATR multiplier AND body-to-range ratio >= minimum breakout body ratio AND relative volume >= minimum relative volume) OR (an active bullish breakout has breakout score >= minimum ready score AND close >= its invalidation boundary AND breakout age <= maximum breakout age)
Short: (base high and base low are available AND base high - base low is between safe ATR * minimum base-range ATR multiplier and safe ATR * maximum base-range ATR multiplier AND close < base low - safe ATR * breakout-buffer ATR multiplier AND close[1] >= base low - safe ATR * breakout-buffer ATR multiplier AND body-to-range ratio >= minimum breakout body ratio AND relative volume >= minimum relative volume) OR (an active bearish breakout has breakout score >= minimum ready score AND close <= its invalidation boundary AND breakout age <= maximum breakout age)
Exit: long, an active bullish breakout has close < its invalidation boundary
Exit: short, an active bearish breakout has close > its invalidation boundary
