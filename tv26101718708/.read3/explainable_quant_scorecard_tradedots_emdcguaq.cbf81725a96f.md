Calculation: Rolling min-max score(x, lookback) = score midpoint if highest(x, lookback) equals lowest(x, lookback), otherwise score maximum × (x − lowest(x, lookback)) / (highest(x, lookback) − lowest(x, lookback))
Calculation: Trend direction = 1 if EMA(close, short-term trend lookback) > EMA(close, long-term trend lookback), −1 if less, otherwise 0; calculate on chart bars, including the current bar
Calculation: Trend score = clamp(Rolling min-max score(EMA(close, short-term trend lookback) − EMA(close, short-term trend lookback)[slope lag], trend normalization lookback), score minimum, score maximum)
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(Rolling min-max score(ROC(close, momentum lookback), generic normalization lookback), score minimum, score maximum)
Calculation: Volatility regime score = lower volatility score if PercentRank(Bollinger Band Width(close, Bollinger lookback, standard-deviation multiplier), generic normalization lookback) is below the lower volatility rank threshold; higher volatility score if above the upper volatility rank threshold; otherwise the midpoint score; Bollinger Band Width is zero when its basis is zero
Calculation: Participation score = clamp(volume percentile rank × (alignment + (1 − alignment) × neutral participation factor), score minimum, score maximum), where volume percentile rank is PercentRank(volume, generic normalization lookback) or the score midpoint if volume is unavailable; CLV is the score midpoint if high equals low, otherwise score maximum × (close − low) / (high − low); alignment is CLV / score maximum when trend direction is 1, (score maximum − CLV) / score maximum when trend direction is −1, and the alignment midpoint when trend direction is 0
Calculation: Relative-strength score = clamp(PercentRank(risk-adjusted relative return, relative-strength percentile lookback), score minimum, score maximum), where relative return is ROC(close, relative-strength return lookback) minus benchmark return; benchmark return is zero if its current or lagged close is unavailable or its lagged close is zero, otherwise it is ROC of the benchmark close sampled from the last completed chart-timeframe bar over the relative-strength return lookback; risk-adjusted relative return is relative return divided by StDev(ROC(close, one bar), relative-strength volatility lookback) / 100, or zero if that volatility is zero; calculate the returns and percentile rank on chart bars
Calculation: Exhaustion score = clamp(score maximum − (absolute(close − EMA(close, short-term trend lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback)) × exhaustion penalty, score minimum, score maximum); use zero distance when ATR is zero
Calculation: Multi-timeframe alignment score = average across T1, T2, and T3 of score maximum when that timeframe’s EMA(close, short-term trend lookback) direction matches trend direction, score midpoint when its direction is zero and does not match, and score minimum otherwise; calculate each EMA direction on that timeframe’s bars and use its last completed bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Effective weights equal the base weights except when ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) is at least the trend ADX threshold, when trend and momentum weights are multiplied by the trend-regime boost and the exhaustion weight by the trend-regime exhaustion factor; otherwise, if ADX is at most the range ADX threshold, multiply trend and momentum weights by the range-regime trend factor and the exhaustion weight by the range-regime exhaustion boost; all other weights remain at base values
Calculation: Composite score = clamp(sum of each effective weight × its trend, momentum, volatility regime, participation, relative-strength, exhaustion, or multi-timeframe alignment score, divided by the greater of the sum of effective weights and the minimum weight denominator, score minimum, score maximum)
