Calculation: Base = selected moving average(close * volume, first SuperTrend lookback) / selected moving average(volume, first SuperTrend lookback), using the same selected moving-average type in numerator and denominator
Calculation: ST1 = SuperTrend(Base, ATR(first SuperTrend lookback), first multiplier)
Calculation: ST2 = SuperTrend(Base, ATR(second SuperTrend lookback), second multiplier)
Calculation: WeightedKNN(x, values, labels, k) = weighted mean of labels for the k nearest values by absolute distance, with each weight equal to 1 / (distance + distance-offset parameter)
Calculation: Classifier1 = WeightedKNN(current ST1, ST1 values from the current chart bar through n−1 bars ago, labels equal to 1 when WMA(close, first price lookback) > WMA(ST1, first prediction lookback) and 0 otherwise on the corresponding bars, k), where n = max(k, data-count parameter)
Calculation: Classifier2 = WeightedKNN(current ST2, ST2 values from the current chart bar through n−1 bars ago, labels equal to 1 when WMA(close, second price lookback) > WMA(ST2, second prediction lookback) and 0 otherwise on the corresponding bars, k), using the same k and n as Classifier1
Long: (AI signals enabled AND Classifier1 = 1 AND Classifier1[1] != 1) OR (AI signals enabled AND Classifier2 = 1 AND Classifier2[1] != 1) OR (AI signals enabled AND ST1 direction changes from bearish to bullish AND Classifier1 = 1) OR (AI signals enabled AND ST2 direction changes from bearish to bullish AND Classifier2 = 1) OR (long trading enabled AND ST1 direction is bullish AND Classifier1 = 1 AND ST2 direction is bullish AND Classifier2 = 1)

Exit: Long, long trading enabled AND low <= ST1 - ATR(first SuperTrend lookback) * first multiplier AND NOT (AI signals enabled AND ((ST1 direction changes from bearish to bullish AND Classifier1 = 1) OR (ST2 direction changes from bearish to bullish AND Classifier2 = 1)))
