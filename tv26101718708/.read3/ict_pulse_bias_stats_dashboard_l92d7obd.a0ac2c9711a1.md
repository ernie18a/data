Calculation: session liquidity-taken flags = for each configured session, reset its high and low flags at session start; set and persist the high flag after any chart-bar high exceeds the most recently completed occurrence’s high, and set and persist the low flag after any chart-bar low falls below its low; update those stored levels to the current occurrence’s maximum high and minimum low when it ends
Calculation: active-session midpoint = midpoint of the running high and low of the first active configured session; undefined when no configured session is active
Calculation: bullish score = count of true conditions: for each of T1, T2, and T3, chart close is at or above the midpoint of the last completed bar’s high-low range when that range is valid and nonzero; any session low-taken flag is true; chart close is at or above the active-session midpoint; T1, T2, and T3 are separate timeframes ordered from shorter to longer, with completed-bar values sampled on chart bars
Calculation: bearish score = count of true conditions: for each of T1, T2, and T3, chart close is below the midpoint of the last completed bar’s high-low range when that range is valid and nonzero; any session high-taken flag is true; chart close is below the active-session midpoint; T1, T2, and T3 are separate timeframes ordered from shorter to longer, with completed-bar values sampled on chart bars
Long: bullish score > bearish score
Short: bearish score > bullish score
