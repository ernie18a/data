Calculation: ATR percent = ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) / close × 100 when close > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR percent / SMA(ATR percent, volatility averaging lookback), or 1 when that average is not positive.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close - close[directional lookback]) / (SMA(abs(change(close)), efficiency averaging lookback) × efficiency averaging lookback), or 0 when the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Price regime = explosive when volatility ratio exceeds its explosive threshold or efficiency ratio exceeds its explosive threshold; otherwise active when either exceeds its active threshold; otherwise quiet when both are below their quiet thresholds; otherwise normal.
Calculation: Average volume = when automatic tuning is enabled, SMA(volume, the regime lookback) with fallback to SMA(volume, manual volume lookback), where the normal lookback is the automatic base lookback, the quiet lookback is max(quiet factor × base lookback, base lookback + one bar), and the active and explosive lookbacks are each max(minimum adaptive lookback, round(their respective factor × base lookback)); otherwise SMA(volume, manual volume lookback).
Calculation: Volume spike multiplier = when automatic tuning is enabled, the base multiplier in normal regime, clamp(base multiplier × quiet factor, minimum multiplier, maximum multiplier) in quiet regime, clamp(base multiplier × active factor, minimum multiplier, maximum multiplier) in active regime, and clamp(base multiplier × explosive factor, minimum multiplier, maximum multiplier) in explosive regime; otherwise the manual multiplier.
Calculation: Zone merge threshold = when automatic tuning is enabled, clamp(ATR percent × automatic threshold factor, max(base threshold × lower threshold factor, minimum lower threshold), max(base threshold × upper threshold factor, minimum upper threshold)); otherwise the manual merge threshold.
Calculation: Zone bounds = level price ± level price × effective thickness percent / 200; effective thickness percent is the clamped automatic base thickness times the clamped square root of chart timeframe seconds divided by seconds per minute when automatic appearance is enabled, otherwise it is the clamped volume-interpolated thickness when volume-based widths are enabled, or the fixed thickness when they are disabled.
Calculation: Active volume zones = support or resistance levels created by the volume-zone conditions below, with candidate levels within the zone merge threshold of any active level merged into that level; a level is active while its age does not exceed its maximum age. In automatic tuning, maximum age is the regime-adjusted base age multiplied by (volume age base + volume age scale × level volume / maximum active level volume) × (1 + min(test count − one, maximum test-age bonus) × test-age factor), rounded and floored at the minimum age; otherwise it is the manual maximum age. Ages increase by one chart bar.
Calculation: Active swing lines = confirmed swing pivot levels not expired by age; automatic maximum age is the regime-adjusted base age, otherwise it is the manual maximum age. A swing line reverses type when close crosses its price: resistance to support above it, support to resistance below it.
Long: (low = lowest(low, extreme lookback) AND volume > Average volume × Volume spike multiplier AND no active volume zone is within Zone merge threshold of low) OR (swing lines are enabled AND PivotLow(low, left bars, right bars) is confirmed AND its price is farther than Zone merge threshold from every active volume zone and every active support swing line) OR (close > the top of an active resistance volume zone) OR (close > the price of an active resistance swing line).
Short: (NOT (low = lowest(low, extreme lookback) AND volume > Average volume × Volume spike multiplier) AND high = highest(high, extreme lookback) AND volume > Average volume × Volume spike multiplier AND no active volume zone is within Zone merge threshold of high) OR (swing lines are enabled AND PivotHigh(high, left bars, right bars) is confirmed AND its price is farther than Zone merge threshold from every active volume zone and every active resistance swing line) OR (close < the bottom of an active support volume zone) OR (close < the price of an active support swing line).
