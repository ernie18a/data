Calculation: Touch tolerance = ATR(ATR lookback) on chart bars × touch-tolerance multiplier.
Calculation: Trendline value = first anchor price + (second anchor price − first anchor price) / (second anchor bar − first anchor bar) × (current chart bar − first anchor bar). Support trendlines join an earlier confirmed pivot low to a higher confirmed pivot low; resistance trendlines join an earlier confirmed pivot high to a lower confirmed pivot high. Candidate anchors must be separated by at least twice the swing length, and the first anchor must be within the maximum line span. Accept a candidate only if every chart-bar close since its first anchor stays on the safe side of the line within the touch tolerance. Count confirmed same-side pivots within the touch tolerance as touches, including both anchors; choose the candidate with the most touches, then the longest span, using the retained pivot-history capacity. When a new confirmed pivot is within tolerance of an active line, retain that line and add a touch; otherwise build a line only when none is active or its first anchor is older than the maximum line span. Deactivate a line when its break entry occurs.
Calculation: Safety line = for a bounce entry, initialize to the entry trendline value minus (long) or plus (short) ATR(ATR lookback) × bounce-buffer multiplier, then on each later chart bar use the greater (long) or lesser (short) of the prior safety line and the original entry trendline value minus (long) or plus (short) that buffer. For a break entry, initialize to the most recent confirmed pivot low (long) or pivot high (short); on each later chart bar, raise it to a newly confirmed pivot low only when that low is above the prior safety line and below close, or lower it to a newly confirmed pivot high only when that high is below the prior safety line and above close.
Long: flat AND bounce setup enabled AND support trendline age ≥ minimum signal age AND low ≤ support trendline + touch tolerance AND close > support trendline AND close > open AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar).
Short: flat AND bounce setup enabled AND resistance trendline age ≥ minimum signal age AND high ≥ resistance trendline − touch tolerance AND close < resistance trendline AND close < open AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar).
Long: flat AND break setup enabled AND resistance trendline age ≥ minimum signal age AND close > resistance trendline AND a most recent confirmed pivot low exists below close by no more than break-distance multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar) AND NOT (the long bounce condition) AND NOT (the short bounce condition).
Short: flat AND break setup enabled AND support trendline age ≥ minimum signal age AND close < support trendline AND a most recent confirmed pivot high exists above close by no more than break-distance multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar) AND NOT (the long bounce condition) AND NOT (the short bounce condition) AND NOT (break setup enabled AND resistance trendline age ≥ minimum signal age AND close > resistance trendline AND a most recent confirmed pivot low exists below close by no more than break-distance multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar)).
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND close < safety line.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND close > safety line.
