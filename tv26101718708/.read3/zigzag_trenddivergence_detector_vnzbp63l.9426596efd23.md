Calculation: Zigzag pivot directions = form alternating price pivots from rolling-window high and low extrema using a lookback parameter, using close for both extrema or high and low according to a source parameter; replace a same-side pivot only with a more extreme price. At each price pivot record the selected oscillator value. Assign price and oscillator directions +2 or +1 for a higher or not-higher high, and -2 or -1 for a lower or not-lower low, comparing with the prior pivot in the same direction. Select the latest pivot or the prior pivot according to a confirmation parameter. The oscillator is selected by a source parameter from CCI(close, length), CMO(close, length), COG(close, length), ADX(high, low, close, long lookback, short smoothing), MACD histogram(close, fast, slow, signal length equal to fast), MACD line(close, fast, slow, signal length equal to fast), MFI(close, length), MOM(close, length), ROC(close, length), RSI(close, length), TSI(close, fast, slow), Williams %R(high, low, close, length), normalized Bollinger, Keltner, Donchian, or adaptive Donchian channel position, or an external source. Channel calculations use length and channel-multiplier parameters; the adaptive Donchian channel carries its prior values until high reaches or exceeds the prior upper channel or low reaches or falls below the prior lower channel, then uses the current Donchian channel.
Calculation: Pivot-based trend direction = initialize positive; each bar calculate ATR(length) times a multiplier, a candidate buy stop as the minimum of the previous history-length price pivots excluding the latest pivot minus that amount, and a candidate sell stop as their maximum plus that amount. Flip positive to negative when the prior close is below the prior buy stop; flip negative to positive when the prior close is above the prior sell stop. While positive, set the buy stop to the greater of its prior value and the candidate buy stop; while negative, set the sell stop to the lesser of its prior value and the candidate sell stop. Set the other stop to its candidate value.
Calculation: Divergence class = continuation when price direction equals oscillator direction and either the price direction has magnitude 2 and half the price direction equals trend direction at the selected pivot, or the price direction has magnitude 1 and differs from trend direction at that pivot; regular divergence when price direction is twice oscillator direction and oscillator direction equals trend direction at that pivot; hidden divergence when oscillator direction is twice price direction and price direction is opposite trend direction at that pivot; otherwise indeterminate.
Calculation: Sentiment bias = positive when price and oscillator directions are both +2; negative when both are -2; otherwise negative when price direction is positive, positive when price direction is negative, and zero otherwise.
Long: pivot-based trend direction is positive OR a selected Zigzag pivot event has a non-indeterminate divergence class and positive sentiment bias
Short: pivot-based trend direction is negative OR a selected Zigzag pivot event has a non-indeterminate divergence class and negative sentiment bias
