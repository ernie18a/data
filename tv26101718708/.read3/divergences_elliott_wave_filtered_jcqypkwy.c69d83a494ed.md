Calculation: Normalized ROC = 50 + ROC scale × ROC(close, ROC lookback) / StDev(ROC(close, ROC lookback), ROC normalization lookback) when the standard deviation is positive; otherwise 50.
Calculation: Hurst = clamp(log(R/S) / log(lookback), Hurst minimum, Hurst maximum), where returns are ln(close[n] / close[n+1]), R is the range of cumulative deviations from their mean, S = StDev(close, lookback), and Hurst is neutral when history or R/S is insufficient.
Calculation: Normalized entropy = −sum(p × ln(p)) / ln(bin count), where p are frequencies of log returns ln(close[n] / close[n+1]) in equal-width bins spanning the lookback’s minimum to maximum return; use zero when history or return range is insufficient.
Calculation: Pivot-line crossing = for each intermediate bar between P1 and P2, deviation of low (bullish) or high (bearish) from the straight line between pivot prices; reject only when a deviation has the opposite sign from the preceding nonzero deviation, initializing the preceding deviation to zero and updating it on every intermediate bar.
Calculation: Oscillator concordance(P1, P2, type) = count of enabled oscillators agreeing with the divergence, with RSI, StochRSI and MFI requiring the P1 value to be below its separate oversold threshold for regular bullish divergence or above its separate overbought threshold for regular bearish divergence; ROC has no zone requirement. Regular bullish requires each counted value at P2 > P1, regular bearish < P1, hidden bullish < P1, and hidden bearish > P1. Use RSI(close, RSI lookback), StochRSI(close, StochRSI lookback, K smoothing), MFI(hlc3, MFI lookback), and Normalized ROC with their separate lookbacks.
Calculation: Wave state for each degree = from chart-bar confirmed pivots, scan that degree’s separate left/right window ranges, retaining the most extreme high and low pivot per bar and ordering them by pivot bar; accept a swing only when its percent move from the preceding accepted swing meets that degree’s minimum, replace adjacent same-type swings with the more extreme one, and retain the configured swing limit. Classify the latest swings in this priority: impulse, diagonal, W3, W2, W4, corrective C, triangle E, neutral. Impulse uses six swings: wave 2 stays beyond the origin, wave 3 extends beyond wave 1, wave 3 is not shorter than both waves 1 and 5, and wave 4 does not overlap wave 1; its quality combines capped wave-3 pace relative to waves 1 and 5, normalized channel-slope alignment, and wave-2/wave-4 slope alternation using separate weights. Diagonal uses the same six-swing checks except wave 4 may overlap wave 1, and its boundary slopes must differ; use its separate quality parameter. W3 uses four swings with first and third legs in the same direction and third leg greater than first. W2 uses three swings with a retracement that reverses wave 1, stays beyond its origin, and meets its separate retracement bounds. W4 uses five swings with waves 1 and 3 in the same direction, wave 4 retracing wave 3 within its separate bounds without overlapping wave 1. Corrective C uses four swings when the B retracement is below its limit and the final leg is nonzero. Triangle E uses five alternating swings with lower successive highs and higher successive lows. Return position, quality, and trend direction; use separate position-score parameters for each regular and hidden wave position, and a neutral score and direction for unclassified states. Require impulse and diagonal quality to meet the minimum impulse-quality parameter.
Calculation: Wave context(P2, type, direction) = for Small, Medium, and Large wave states whose latest swing is within their separate proximity limits from P2, use the state; otherwise use neutral score, zero quality, no position, and no trend. For hidden types map W5/Diagonal to the hidden W5 score, W2 to hidden W2, W4 to hidden W4, corrective C to hidden C, W3 to hidden W3, and other positions to hidden neutral. Adjust each degree score as neutral + (position score − neutral) × max(quality, quality floor); combine with separate degree weights, then add a cross-degree modifier: triple agreement when all adjusted scores exceed the high threshold, dual agreement when any two do, otherwise apply the separate conflict penalty when Small exceeds its strong threshold and Medium or Large is below the low threshold (the stronger penalty applies when both are low). Clamp to separate score bounds. The wave filter passes when disabled; when enabled, require allowed position toggles for the selected degree mode (any, Small, Medium, Large, or all). Trend probability is neutral without a Medium or Large trend; otherwise use separate regular-opposition or hidden-alignment probabilities against the Medium trend if present, else Large, and adjust when Medium and Large trends agree using separate trend adjustment parameters.
Calculation: Divergence posterior(P1, P2, type, direction) = logistic(prior log odds + sum of each separate component weight × logit(component probability)). The components are concordance divided by enabled oscillator count (or neutral if none), clamp(abs(RSI(P2) − RSI(P1)) / StDev(RSI, RSI deviation lookback) / (Z-score threshold × Z-score scale), probability bounds), Hurst mapped from neutral according to its distance from neutral and the Hurst mean-reversion weight, normalized entropy clamped to probability bounds, Wave context score, overextension probability, and Wave context trend probability. Bullish overextension compares the absolute P2-low-to-highest-intervening-pivot-high leg, normalized by that high, with the absolute P1-low-to-highest-prior-pivot-high leg normalized by that high; bearish overextension uses corresponding lowest intervening and prior pivot lows and P1/P2 highs. Use neutral ratio if either comparison pivot is unavailable, the configured fallback for a zero prior leg, and clamp((ratio − 1) / overextension scale, probability bounds).
