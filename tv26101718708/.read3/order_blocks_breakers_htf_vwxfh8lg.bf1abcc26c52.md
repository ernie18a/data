Calculation: Stored OB zones = On bars of each enabled T1/T2/T3, use the previous completed T bar’s high and low as zone bounds when the current T bar’s close is above the previous high after a bearish previous bar, or below the previous low after a bullish previous bar; on chart bars, store only when its returned source timestamp is newly non-na (prior chart-bar value is na or different), the matching direction toggle is enabled, and the minimum-size filter is disabled or 100 × zone height / ATR(ATR-length parameter, T, current bar) >= minimum-size parameter; use 1 when ATR is na or <= 0; retain at most 3 × zone-cap parameter newest zones per T.
Calculation: Stored-zone state = On chart bars, each stored zone starts unmitigated and unbroken; convert an unbroken original bullish zone to a bearish breaker when close < bottom, or an unbroken original bearish zone to a bullish breaker when close > top; otherwise, if unmitigated, mark it mitigated on the first Tap (bullish low <= top or bearish high >= bottom) or first Close inside (close >= bottom AND close <= top), according to the mitigation parameter; breakers remain eligible regardless of mitigation.
Calculation: Bullish-zone overlap = On chart bars, low <= top AND high >= bottom for any stored original bullish zone that is unmitigated, or any stored original bearish zone that is a breaker.
Calculation: Bearish-zone overlap = On chart bars, low <= top AND high >= bottom for any stored original bearish zone that is unmitigated, or any stored original bullish zone that is a breaker.
Long: On chart bars, for any enabled T1/T2/T3, current T bar close (forming in realtime) > previous completed T bar high AND previous completed T bar close < previous completed T bar open AND its returned source timestamp is newly non-na (prior chart-bar value is na or different).
Short: On chart bars, for any enabled T1/T2/T3, current T bar close (forming in realtime) < previous completed T bar low AND previous completed T bar close > previous completed T bar open AND its returned source timestamp is newly non-na (prior chart-bar value is na or different).
