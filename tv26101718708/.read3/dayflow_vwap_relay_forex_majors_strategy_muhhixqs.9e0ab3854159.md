Calculation: Anchored VWAP and its upper and lower standard-deviation bands = VWAP(hlc3, band-width parameter, reset at each T1 boundary), evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: Residual = chart close − anchored VWAP
Calculation: Expansion = STDEV(Residual, signal lookback parameter) / max(positive epsilon floor, STDEV(chart close, same signal lookback parameter))
Calculation: VWAP slope = LinearRegression(anchored VWAP, signal lookback parameter, offset 0) − LinearRegression(anchored VWAP, same signal lookback parameter, offset 1), evaluated on chart bars
Calculation: Micro flow = mean sign of close changes across up to the micro-span most recent transitions in the T2 intrabar array for the current chart bar; +1 for an increase, −1 for a decrease, 0 for no change; zero when fewer than two closes are available. T2 is lower than the chart timeframe; use its latest available completed bars, and calculate on chart bars.
Long: long side is permitted AND (session filter is disabled OR chart bar is within the configured session in exchange time) AND (no trade has closed OR chart bars since the latest close ≥ cooldown parameter) AND ((Expansion < expansion gate parameter AND Residual ≤ percentile(Residual, signal lookback parameter, lower-percentile parameter) AND Micro flow > micro-flow gate parameter) OR (Expansion ≥ expansion gate parameter AND chart close > anchored VWAP upper band AND VWAP slope > 0 AND Micro flow > micro-flow gate parameter))
Short: short side is permitted AND (session filter is disabled OR chart bar is within the configured session in exchange time) AND (no trade has closed OR chart bars since the latest close ≥ cooldown parameter) AND ((Expansion < expansion gate parameter AND Residual ≥ percentile(Residual, signal lookback parameter, upper-percentile parameter) AND Micro flow < −micro-flow gate parameter) OR (Expansion ≥ expansion gate parameter AND chart close < anchored VWAP lower band AND VWAP slope < 0 AND Micro flow < −micro-flow gate parameter))
Exit: Long, chart low ≤ entry price − nearest-tick-rounded(ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback parameter) × stop multiple parameter) OR chart high ≥ entry price + nearest-tick-rounded(ATR(chart bars, same ATR lookback parameter) × target multiple parameter)
Exit: Short, chart high ≥ entry price + nearest-tick-rounded(ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback parameter) × stop multiple parameter) OR chart low ≤ entry price − nearest-tick-rounded(ATR(chart bars, same ATR lookback parameter) × target multiple parameter)
