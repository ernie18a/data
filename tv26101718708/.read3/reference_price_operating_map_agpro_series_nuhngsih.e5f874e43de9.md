Calculation: ReferenceState(level, reset event) = maintain independent status and side, initially Untouched; on each confirmed chart bar with ATR(lookback) > 0 and abs(close-level) <= ATR(lookback) * far-distance, define D = ATR(lookback) * touch-distance, touch = abs(close-level) <= D OR low <= level + D AND high >= level - D, and rejection = touch AND (max(high-low, minimum price increment) - abs(close-open)) / max(high-low, minimum price increment) > wick-ratio threshold AND abs(close-level) > D * rejection-clearance fraction; Untouched + touch -> FirstTouch and side = (close > level); FirstTouch or Tested + rejection -> Rejected and side = (close > level); FirstTouch or Tested + not touch -> Held and reset count when (close > level) matches side, otherwise increment opposite-close count and become Reclaimed with side = (close > level) when strict-reclaim is false or count >= reclaim-bars; Held, Reclaimed, or Rejected + touch -> Tested and reset count; otherwise, for those states, opposite-side closes increment count and become Reclaimed with updated side at the same confirmation threshold, while same-side closes reset count and leave status unchanged; reset status and count when the reference period changes; freeze status and count when ATR(lookback) <= 0 or abs(close-level) > ATR(lookback) * far-distance.
Long: (ReferenceState(open(T1, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars), T1 period change) has status Held or Reclaimed AND bullish side) OR (ReferenceState(open(T2, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars), T2 period change) has status Held or Reclaimed AND bullish side) OR (ReferenceState(open(T3, still-forming bar sampled on chart bars), T3 period change) has status Held or Reclaimed AND bullish side) OR (ReferenceState(close(T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars), T1 period change) has status Held or Reclaimed AND bullish side)
