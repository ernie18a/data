Calculation: Safe ATR = max(nz(ATR(ATR lookback), nz(True Range, close * ATR fallback fraction)), ATR floor)
Calculation: Body displacement = abs(close - open) / Safe ATR
Calculation: OB features, computed at formation = [clamp((origin candle high - origin candle low) / Safe ATR, 0, OB size cap), clamp(Body displacement, 0, body displacement cap), clamp(tanh(clamp((EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) - EMA(close, longer trend lookback)) / Safe ATR * direction, -tanh input bound, tanh input bound)), -trend alignment bound, trend alignment bound), clamp((RSI(close, momentum lookback) - RSI center) / momentum normalization scale * direction, -momentum feature bound, momentum feature bound), clamp(origin candle volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), positive numerical floor), 0, volume ratio cap)]
Calculation: Stored OB quality = 1 / (1 + exp(-clamp(intercept + sum(weight[i] * feature[i]), -quality logit bound, quality logit bound)))
Calculation: Online quality model = initialize intercept and feature weights to zero; at each OB resolution set target y to 0 if a bullish OB closes below its bottom or a bearish OB closes above its top, otherwise set y to 1 if it retested and closes through its far edge, or at the resolve horizon set y to 1 iff it retested; with p = 1 / (1 + exp(-clamp(intercept + sum(weight[i] * feature[i]), -quality logit bound, quality logit bound))) and error = p - y, update intercept to intercept - learning rate * error and each weight[i] to weight[i] - learning rate * (error * feature[i] + regularization * weight[i]); resolve multiple OBs newest first
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close crosses above highest(high, structure lookback)[1] AND Body displacement > displacement threshold AND a bearish candle exists among the preceding origin lookback bars
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close crosses below lowest(low, structure lookback)[1] AND Body displacement > displacement threshold AND a bullish candle exists among the preceding origin lookback bars
Long: On a confirmed chart bar whose index exceeds the warmup length, an unresolved bullish OB has not previously been retested AND low <= its origin candle high AND its stored OB quality >= quality threshold
Short: On a confirmed chart bar whose index exceeds the warmup length, an unresolved bearish OB has not previously been retested AND high >= its origin candle low AND its stored OB quality >= quality threshold
Long: For the chart-symbol scan on chart bars, or the screener scan on current T1 bars, the most recent event between close crossing above highest(high, structure lookback)[1] and close crossing below lowest(low, structure lookback)[1] is the upward cross; T1 results are mapped onto chart bars with lookahead off and use the current T1 bar, which may still be forming in real time

Long: At least one unresolved OB exists AND the unresolved OB nearest to close by abs(close - (OB top + OB bottom) / 2) / Safe ATR is bullish; ties select the oldest OB
Short: At least one unresolved OB exists AND the unresolved OB nearest to close by abs(close - (OB top + OB bottom) / 2) / Safe ATR is bearish; ties select the oldest OB
Calculation: break of structure = up on a bar where close crosses above the highest high of the structure length bars before the current bar; down on a bar where close crosses below the lowest low of those bars
Calculation: order block = at each break of structure, if among the origin lookback bars before the current bar there is a nearest bar whose close is below its open (for an up break) or above its open (for a down break), an order block is formed on the current bar with direction = the break direction, top = that bar's high, bottom = that bar's low, and origin candle = that bar
Calculation: order block quality = sigmoid(bias + sum over i=1..5 of weight_i * feature_i), where feature_1 = min(size cap, (origin candle high - origin candle low) / ATR(ATR length)), feature_2 = min(displacement cap, abs(close - open) / ATR(ATR length)), feature_3 = tanh((EMA(close, fast EMA length) - EMA(close, slow EMA length)) / ATR(ATR length)) * direction, feature_4 = (RSI(close, RSI length) - 50) / 50 * direction, feature_5 = min(volume ratio cap, origin candle volume / SMA(volume, volume average length)); features are evaluated on the formation bar; direction = +1 for an up break and -1 for a down break; bias and all weights start at 0 and are updated when order blocks resolve, so quality is computed at formation with weights updated by resolutions on earlier bars; when an order block resolves, error = sigmoid(bias + sum over i of weight_i * stored feature_i) - label, bias = bias - learning rate * error, and weight_i = weight_i - learning rate * (error * stored feature_i + L2 regularization * weight_i); an order block resolves on the first bar where close is below its bottom (up) or above its top (down), or, after a touch, close is above its top (up) or below its bottom (down), or its age reaches resolve horizon bars; touched = low <= top (up) or high >= bottom (down) on any bar from formation through the current bar; label = 1 if touched and the resolving close is not below its bottom (up) or above its top (down), else 0
Long: for an order block, direction = up AND low <= order block top AND low > order block top on every bar from its formation to the previous bar AND bars since formation <= resolve horizon AND order block quality >= quality threshold AND bar index > warmup bars
Short: for an order block, direction = down AND high >= order block bottom AND high < order block bottom on every bar from its formation to the previous bar AND bars since formation <= resolve horizon AND order block quality >= quality threshold AND bar index > warmup bars
Long: most recent break of structure on the chart timeframe = up
Short: most recent break of structure on the chart timeframe = down

Short: for a screened symbol, most recent break of structure on T1 = down
