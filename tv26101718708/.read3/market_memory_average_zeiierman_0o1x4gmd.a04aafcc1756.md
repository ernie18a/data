Calculation: Market Memory Average = EMA of current close multiplied by one plus the similarity-weighted mean of the long-lookback ROC of the top-ranked historical states, expressed as a decimal; rank eligible states by exp(−sensitivity × distance), where distance is the sum of absolute differences in short-, medium-, and long-lookback ROC divided by their respective scale parameters, normalized RSI multiplied by its scale parameter, ATR as a percentage of close divided by its scale parameter, and relative volume divided by its scale parameter; relative volume is volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or 1 when that SMA is zero; use RSI(close, RSI lookback) normalized to a fraction and ATR(ATR lookback); compare the current state with historical states from pattern length plus one bars ago through scan depth, limited by available history; use the top-matches parameter states and weight their long-lookback ROC by their similarity; the ROC lookbacks are ordered short, medium, and long; smooth with the smoothing lookback
Long: close crosses above Market Memory Average OR Market Memory Average > Market Memory Average[slope lookback]
Short: close crosses below Market Memory Average OR Market Memory Average < Market Memory Average[slope lookback]
