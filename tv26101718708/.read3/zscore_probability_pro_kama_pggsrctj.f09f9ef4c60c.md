Calculation: Price center = selected KAMA(close, lookback, fast length, slow length), HMA(close, lookback), EMA(close, lookback), or ALMA(close, lookback, ALMA offset, ALMA sigma)
Calculation: Price residual(price) = selected price − price center, 100 × (price − price center) / max(abs(price center), epsilon), or 100 × ln(price / max(price center, epsilon))
Calculation: Raw standardized residual(price) = selected Price residual(price) / stdev(Price residual(close), lookback), or MAD scale constant × (Price residual(price) − median(Price residual(close), lookback)) / median(abs(Price residual(close) − median(Price residual(close), lookback)), lookback); use zero when the selected denominator is zero
Calculation: Smoothed standardized residual = selected ALMA, HMA, EMA, or no smoothing of Raw standardized residual(close), using a separate smoothing lookback
Calculation: Smoothed variance-ratio Hurst estimate = EMA(clamp(ln(max(variance(close − close[lag], lookback), epsilon) / max(variance(close − close[1], lookback), epsilon)) / (2 × ln(lag)), 0, 1), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = on the bar a pivot low of Smoothed standardized residual is confirmed using separate left and right pivot lookbacks, the latest and preceding such pivots are no more than the maximum pivot gap apart, the low at the latest pivot bar is lower than at the preceding pivot bar, and Smoothed standardized residual at the latest pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot; if the zone filter is enabled, the latest pivot value must also be below the negative lower Z threshold
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = on the bar a pivot high of Smoothed standardized residual is confirmed using separate left and right pivot lookbacks, the latest and preceding such pivots are no more than the maximum pivot gap apart, the high at the latest pivot bar is higher than at the preceding pivot bar, and Smoothed standardized residual at the latest pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot; if the zone filter is enabled, the latest pivot value must also be above the lower Z threshold
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = on the bar a pivot low of Smoothed standardized residual is confirmed using separate left and right pivot lookbacks, the latest and preceding such pivots are no more than the maximum pivot gap apart, the low at the latest pivot bar is higher than at the preceding pivot bar, and Smoothed standardized residual at the latest pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence = on the bar a pivot high of Smoothed standardized residual is confirmed using separate left and right pivot lookbacks, the latest and preceding such pivots are no more than the maximum pivot gap apart, the high at the latest pivot bar is lower than at the preceding pivot bar, and Smoothed standardized residual at the latest pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot
Calculation: Higher-timeframe smoothed standardized residual = Smoothed standardized residual calculated on T1 bars with the current T1 bar, mapped onto chart bars
Calculation: Bull score = sum of the reversal weight when Smoothed standardized residual crosses above the negative higher Z threshold; the divergence weight when divergence detection is enabled and either enabled regular or enabled hidden bullish divergence occurs; the slope weight when Price center > Price center[slope lookback]; the regime weight when the Hurst filter is enabled and (Hurst estimate < reverting threshold and (regular bullish divergence or Z reversal) or Hurst estimate > trending threshold and (hidden bullish divergence or (Price center > Price center[slope lookback] and Smoothed standardized residual < 0)); the higher-timeframe weight when confirmation is enabled and (Higher-timeframe smoothed standardized residual < negative lower Z threshold or (it > 0 and is below its value on the preceding chart bar)); and the volume weight when volume confirmation is enabled and volume is available and its Z-score exceeds the volume threshold
Calculation: Bear score = sum of the reversal weight when Smoothed standardized residual crosses below the higher Z threshold; the divergence weight when divergence detection is enabled and either enabled regular or enabled hidden bearish divergence occurs; the slope weight when Price center < Price center[slope lookback]; the regime weight when the Hurst filter is enabled and (Hurst estimate < reverting threshold and (regular bearish divergence or Z reversal) or Hurst estimate > trending threshold and (hidden bearish divergence or (Price center < Price center[slope lookback] and Smoothed standardized residual > 0)); the higher-timeframe weight when confirmation is enabled and (Higher-timeframe smoothed standardized residual > lower Z threshold or (it < 0 and is above its value on the preceding chart bar)); and the volume weight when volume confirmation is enabled and volume is available and its Z-score exceeds the volume threshold
Calculation: Total component weight = reversal weight + divergence weight + slope weight + regime weight + higher-timeframe weight + volume weight
Calculation: Bull percentage = 100 × Bull score / Total component weight, or 0 when Total component weight is zero
Calculation: Bear percentage = 100 × Bear score / Total component weight, or 0 when Total component weight is zero
