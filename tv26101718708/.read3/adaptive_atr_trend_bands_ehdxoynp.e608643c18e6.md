Calculation: Basis = selected EMA, SMA, or VWMA of chart-bar close over the basis lookback.
Calculation: Final upper band = Basis + ATR(ATR lookback) × band multiplier if no prior final upper band exists, or if that raw upper band is below the prior final upper band, or prior close is above the prior final upper band; otherwise the prior final upper band.
Calculation: Final lower band = Basis − ATR(ATR lookback) × band multiplier if no prior final lower band exists, or if that raw lower band is above the prior final lower band, or prior close is below the prior final lower band; otherwise the prior final lower band.
Calculation: Basis slope = Basis − Basis[chart-timeframe-selected lag].
Calculation: Adaptive slope cutoff = ATR × clamp(EMA(abs(Basis slope) / ATR, chart-timeframe-selected smoothing lookback) × slope scale, slope floor, slope ceiling), using zero for abs(Basis slope) / ATR when ATR is zero.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR / EMA(ATR, chart-timeframe-selected smoothing lookback) when that EMA is positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR expansion cutoff = clamp(EMA(ATR ratio, chart-timeframe-selected smoothing lookback) × expansion scale, expansion floor, expansion ceiling).
Calculation: Bullish filters = ATR ratio ≥ adaptive ATR expansion cutoff AND Basis slope > adaptive slope cutoff AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) on T1's last completed bar, sampled on chart bars, ≤ RSI lower threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR T2's last completed close, sampled on chart bars, > EMA(close, EMA lookback) on T2's last completed bar, sampled on chart bars).
Calculation: Bearish filters = ATR ratio ≥ adaptive ATR expansion cutoff AND Basis slope < −adaptive slope cutoff AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) on T1's last completed bar, sampled on chart bars, ≥ RSI upper threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR T2's last completed close, sampled on chart bars, < EMA(close, EMA lookback) on T2's last completed bar, sampled on chart bars).
Calculation: Trend state updates only when close confirmation is disabled or the chart bar is confirmed; from neutral it becomes bullish when close ≥ Basis and bearish otherwise; from bearish it becomes bullish when close > the prior final upper band (or current raw upper band if the prior band is unavailable) and bullish filters hold; from bullish it becomes bearish when close < the prior final lower band (or current raw lower band if the prior band is unavailable) and bearish filters hold; otherwise it retains its prior state. When the close-confirmation setting is enabled and the chart bar is unconfirmed, it retains its prior state.
Calculation: Chart bars since last signal = the initial no-signal sentinel if no signal has occurred, otherwise current chart-bar index minus the last signal's chart-bar index.
Long: Trend state changes from bearish to bullish AND the last emitted signal side was not long AND chart bars since last signal ≥ minimum signal spacing.
Short: Trend state changes from bullish to bearish AND the last emitted signal side was not short AND chart bars since last signal ≥ minimum signal spacing.
