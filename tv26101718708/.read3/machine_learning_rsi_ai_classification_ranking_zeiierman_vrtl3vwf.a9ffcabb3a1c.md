Calculation: normalizedRange(x, length) = (x − lowest(x, length)) / (highest(x, length) − lowest(x, length)); when the denominator is zero, return the midpoint of the normalized range
Calculation: RSI fingerprint = (RSI(source, base length) normalized to its scale; normalizedRange(RSI(source, base length) − RSI(source, base length)[step], feature lookback); normalizedRange(RSI(source, base length) − 2 × RSI(source, base length)[step] + RSI(source, base length)[2 × step], feature lookback); distance of RSI from its midpoint normalized to half its scale; percentile rank of RSI over the feature lookback normalized to its scale; normalizedRange(standard deviation of RSI over its separate lookback, feature lookback); normalizedRange(RSI(source, rounded half base length with its minimum) − RSI(source, twice base length), feature lookback); normalizedRange(EMA(RSI(source, base length), regime length) − RSI midpoint, feature lookback))
Calculation: labeled sample bank = on each confirmed chart bar, store the RSI fingerprint from the learning horizon ago with a label from source − source[horizon]: strongest positive class above the strong ATR-scaled threshold, weaker positive class above the weak threshold, weak positive class above zero, corresponding negative classes below the negative thresholds, otherwise neutral; use ATR from the horizon ago and the learning-sensitivity parameter, retain the configured memory depth, and scan every configured spacing of stored rows
Calculation: analog engine = use either manual feature weights or auto weights; auto weights are Fisher discriminant scores for positive versus negative labeled samples, scaled from a minimum weight to a maximum weight and blended with prior weights at the adaptation speed after the minimum sample count; distance to each scanned fingerprint is the sum of feature weights times log(unit offset + absolute feature difference); keep the configured number of smallest distances; weight each neighbor label by the reciprocal of unit offset plus distance; analog score is weighted mean label, agreement is the weighted share voting with the score direction, and tightness is clamp(unit − mean distance / (weight sum × distance scale), zero, one); bias is bullish above the positive score threshold, bearish below the negative threshold, otherwise neutral
Calculation: market context = trend force is absolute difference between fast and slow EMAs of source divided by ATR; raw chop is trend force below its threshold; RSI slope is RSI above its step-lagged value; slope fit is RSI slope for bullish bias or its inverse for bearish bias; stretched is RSI above the upper extreme for bullish bias or below the lower extreme for bearish bias; volatility rank is the percentile rank of ATR over its lookback, healthy between its lower and upper bounds; oscillator regime is EMA of RSI; smoothed RSI direction is whether its short EMA is above its prior value
Calculation: adaptive Supertrend direction = smooth the clamped analog score by EMA; conviction drive is the clamped weighted sum of absolute smoothed score, analog tightness, and agreement, reduced by the chop factor when raw chop is enabled; adaptive multiplier is base multiplier × (unit + adaptivity × (unit − conviction drive)); form source-centered bands using that multiplier and Supertrend ATR; ratchet the lower band to the greater of its current value and prior lower band when prior close is above the prior lower band, otherwise reset it; ratchet the upper band to the lesser of its current value and prior upper band when prior close is below the prior upper band, otherwise reset it; initialize direction bullish, flip bearish to bullish when close exceeds the prior upper band, and flip bullish to bearish when close falls below the prior lower band
Calculation: stance = when bias is bullish or bearish and enabled trend, volatility, and chop filters pass, set stance to that bias; otherwise retain prior stance; stance age resets on a stance change and increments otherwise; an early flip is a stance change when any of the preceding configured bars also changed stance
Calculation: rank = clamp to the configured rank range the sum of weighted analog agreement, tightness, structure (slope fit and not stretched), Supertrend alignment, volatility health, RSI regime fit, smoothed RSI direction, and capped stance age, minus capped penalties for raw chop, stretch, early flip, and having fewer neighbors than requested; rank is zero for neutral bias
Calculation: confidence = clamp to the configured confidence range: weighted agreement + weighted tightness + capped stance age contribution + slope-fit contribution − early-flip penalty − proportional penalty for having fewer neighbors than requested; confidence is zero for neutral bias
Long: adaptive Supertrend direction is bullish OR stance changes to bullish AND rank meets its minimum AND confidence meets its minimum AND the configured cooldown since the prior signal has elapsed AND the chart bar is confirmed
