Calculation: opening-range high = highest high during the configured opening-range window
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest low during the configured opening-range window
Calculation: opening-range size = opening-range high - opening-range low
Calculation: combined prior/current RTH VWAP = sum(hlc3 * volume) over the previous completed RTH session and current RTH session so far, divided by summed volume over those bars
Calculation: market positioning score = for each of RTH VWAP, overnight VWAP, combined prior/current RTH VWAP, high-of-day VWAP, and low-of-day VWAP, add 1 when close is above the level, subtract 1 when below, and add 0 when equal or unavailable; also add 1 when close is above the opening-range high, subtract 1 when below the opening-range low, and add 0 otherwise
Calculation: planned long entry price = selected RTH-open- or Globex-open-anchored VWAP + shared tick offset
Calculation: planned short entry price = selected RTH-open- or Globex-open-anchored VWAP - shared tick offset
Calculation: initial long stop = configured fixed-tick stop, or planned long entry price minus configured ATR-multiple distance using ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar), or selected entry VWAP minus buffer, or opening-range low minus buffer (fixed-tick fallback if unavailable), or lowest low over the configured swing lookback minus buffer, according to the selected stop mode
Calculation: initial short stop = configured fixed-tick stop, or planned short entry price plus configured ATR-multiple distance using ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar), or selected entry VWAP plus buffer, or opening-range high plus buffer (fixed-tick fallback if unavailable), or highest high over the configured swing lookback plus buffer, according to the selected stop mode
Calculation: planned long risk = planned long entry price - initial long stop
Calculation: planned short risk = initial short stop - planned short entry price
Calculation: position risk = absolute difference between actual fill price and initial stop
Calculation: long target = planned long entry price plus configured R multiple times planned long risk, or configured opening-range multiple times opening-range size, or configured fixed-tick distance, or disabled, according to the selected target mode
Calculation: short target = planned short entry price minus configured R multiple times planned short risk, or configured opening-range multiple times opening-range size, or configured fixed-tick distance, or disabled, according to the selected target mode
Calculation: trailing distance = configured point distance or configured R multiple times position risk, according to the selected trail mode
Calculation: active long stop = initial long stop, raised to actual fill price when breakeven is enabled and high reaches fill price plus position risk; when trailing is enabled and the highest high since entry reaches fill price plus configured activation R times position risk, ratchet the stop to at least that highest high minus trailing distance
Calculation: active short stop = initial short stop, lowered to actual fill price when breakeven is enabled and low reaches fill price minus position risk; when trailing is enabled and the lowest low since entry reaches fill price minus configured activation R times position risk, ratchet the stop to at most that lowest low plus trailing distance

Short: close < RTH-anchored VWAP, evaluated separately on the chart market and each configured correlated market

Short: configured direction permits short AND opening-range window has closed AND a chart bar since then closed below opening-range low AND current close < selected entry VWAP AND flat AND within configured entry window AND fills this RTH session < configured maximum AND configured weekday is allowed AND month is not excluded AND (opening-range size filter is disabled OR opening-range size meets configured minimum and optional maximum in the selected points or ATR-relative measure) AND (positioning-score filter is disabled OR chart market positioning score <= negative configured entry threshold) AND (correlated-market confirmation is disabled OR selected correlated-market positioning score(s) meet configured short threshold in the selected one/both/either mode) AND short time cutoff is not active OR current local time is before the configured cutoff AND short month exclusion is not active AND short weekday exclusion is not active AND (prop-trading halt is inactive) AND planned short risk > 0 AND stop distance is within configured bounds
Exit: Long, low <= active long stop OR (target is enabled and not suppressed by the enabled remove-target-when-trailing rule after trail activation AND high >= long target) OR forced-flat switch is enabled and configured local flat time has arrived
Exit: Short, high >= active short stop OR (target is enabled and not suppressed by the enabled remove-target-when-trailing rule after trail activation AND low <= short target) OR forced-flat switch is enabled and configured local flat time has arrived
