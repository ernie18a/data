Calculation: Opening range high/low = maximum high/minimum low during the configured session window and timezone; use after the session ends and retain until the next session starts. At each session start reset the range, swept-side flags, active setup and primary FVG, and T1-entry-use flag.
Calculation: Pressure delta on a bar = volume * (((close - low) - (high - close)) / max(high - low, range floor)).
Calculation: Primary FVG = on T1 bars, after each setup confirmation, scan from that bar through the FVG scan length inclusive. For a bullish setup, qualifying gaps have low > high[FVG offset parameter] and low - high[FVG offset parameter] >= ATR(T1, shared ATR lookback) * minimum FVG ATR multiple; their zone is [high[FVG offset parameter], low], and choose the zone with the lowest midpoint. For a bearish setup, qualifying gaps have high < low[FVG offset parameter] and low[FVG offset parameter] - high >= ATR(T1, shared ATR lookback) * minimum FVG ATR multiple; their zone is [high, low[FVG offset parameter]], and choose the zone with the highest midpoint. Keep the first zone on midpoint ties; lock the choice after the scan and leave it unavailable if no gap qualifies. Each new confirmation clears and restarts the scan.
Long: On T1 bars after the opening range completes, a range-high sweep (high > opening range high AND close < opening range high) followed by a range-low sweep (low < opening range low AND close > opening range low) sets or restarts a bullish candidate and its confirmation window; the latest eligible second-side sweep sets the candidate direction. From that sweep through the confirmation-window length inclusive, require close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR(T1, shared ATR lookback) * displacement ATR multiplier AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, range floor) >= minimum body fraction AND close > the highest high over the structure lookback bars before the current bar AND pressure delta > 0 AND abs(pressure delta) >= SMA(abs(pressure delta), T1 delta lookback) * T1 delta strength multiplier. Each T1 bar satisfying these confirmation conditions, including consecutive bars, starts a new setup and restarts the FVG scan. Emit the breakout marking on T2 chart bars only when the projected T1 confirmation changes from false to true; use the last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime.

From that sweep through the confirmation-window length inclusive, require close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR(T1, shared ATR lookback) * displacement ATR multiplier AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, range floor) >= minimum body fraction AND close < the lowest low over the structure lookback bars before the current bar AND pressure delta < 0 AND abs(pressure delta) >= SMA(abs(pressure delta), T1 delta lookback) * T1 delta strength multiplier.
