Calculation: Fast ribbon = average(MA(close, fast lower lookback × length multiplier, fast MA type, lower gamma when required), MA(close, fast upper lookback × length multiplier, fast MA type, upper gamma when required)), on chart bars
Calculation: Slow ribbon = average(MA(close, slow lower lookback × length multiplier, slow MA type, lower gamma when required), MA(close, slow upper lookback × length multiplier, slow MA type, upper gamma when required)), on chart bars
Calculation: Fast ribbon spread = Fast ribbon − Slow ribbon
Calculation: Fast line spread = MA(close, fast lower lookback × length multiplier, fast MA type, lower gamma when required) − MA(close, fast upper lookback × length multiplier, fast MA type, upper gamma when required), on chart bars
Calculation: Slow line spread = MA(close, slow lower lookback × length multiplier, slow MA type, lower gamma when required) − MA(close, slow upper lookback × length multiplier, slow MA type, upper gamma when required), on chart bars
Calculation: Rising-positive count(X) = prior count + one when X > 0 and X > X[1], otherwise zero, on chart bars
Calculation: Falling-negative count(X) = prior count + one when X < 0 and X < X[1], otherwise zero, on chart bars
Calculation: Adjusted high = max(open, close) when Renko adjustment is enabled, otherwise high
Calculation: Adjusted low = min(open, close) when Renko adjustment is enabled, otherwise low
Calculation: Maximum adjusted high since long entry = highest adjusted high from the entry bar through the current bar
Calculation: Minimum adjusted low since short entry = lowest adjusted low from the entry bar through the current bar
Long: close > EMA(close, bar-color EMA lookback), on chart bars
Short: close <= EMA(close, bar-color EMA lookback), on chart bars
Long: Rising-positive count(Fast line spread) >= count threshold
Short: Falling-negative count(Fast line spread) >= count threshold
Long: Rising-positive count(Slow line spread) >= count threshold
Short: Falling-negative count(Slow line spread) >= count threshold
Long: At a confirmed chart-bar close, while flat, neither side closed on the prior bar, within the configured test date window, and on the first bar where Rising-positive count(Fast ribbon spread), Rising-positive count(Fast line spread), and Rising-positive count(Slow line spread) all meet the count threshold, with long orders enabled in an unflipped mode; or, in flipped mode, on the first bar where Falling-negative count(Fast ribbon spread), Falling-negative count(Fast line spread), and Falling-negative count(Slow line spread) all meet the count threshold
Short: At a confirmed chart-bar close, while flat, neither side closed on the prior bar, within the configured test date window, and on the first bar where Falling-negative count(Fast ribbon spread), Falling-negative count(Fast line spread), and Falling-negative count(Slow line spread) all meet the count threshold, with short orders enabled in an unflipped mode; or, in flipped mode, on the first bar where Rising-positive count(Fast ribbon spread), Rising-positive count(Fast line spread), and Rising-positive count(Slow line spread) all meet the count threshold
Exit: long, within the configured test date window AND ((trailing stop enabled AND adjusted low <= Maximum adjusted high since long entry × (1 − trailing-stop fraction)) OR (take profit enabled AND Maximum adjusted high since long entry >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) AND adjusted low <= Maximum adjusted high since long entry × (1 − min(trailing-profit fraction, take-profit fraction))) OR (stop loss enabled AND adjusted low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction)) OR ((opposite-cross exit enabled OR trailing stop disabled AND stop loss disabled) AND Fast ribbon[1] < Slow ribbon[1] AND open < Slow ribbon[1] AND NOT (Fast ribbon[2] < Slow ribbon[2] AND open[1] < Slow ribbon[2])))
Exit: short, within the configured test date window AND ((trailing stop enabled AND adjusted high >= Minimum adjusted low since short entry × (1 + trailing-stop fraction)) OR (take profit enabled AND Minimum adjusted low since short entry <= entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) AND adjusted high >= Minimum adjusted low since short entry × (1 + min(trailing-profit fraction, take-profit fraction))) OR (stop loss enabled AND adjusted high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction)) OR ((opposite-cross exit enabled OR trailing stop disabled AND stop loss disabled) AND Fast ribbon[1] > Slow ribbon[1] AND open > Slow ribbon[1] AND NOT (Fast ribbon[2] > Slow ribbon[2] AND open[1] > Slow ribbon[2])))
