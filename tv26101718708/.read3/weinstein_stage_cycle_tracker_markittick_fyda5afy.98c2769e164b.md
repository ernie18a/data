Calculation: Stage source = selected price source, either raw or smoothed by the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA over the smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Stage slope = 100 * (SMA(Stage source, MA lookback) - SMA(Stage source, MA lookback)[slope lookback]) / SMA(Stage source, MA lookback)[slope lookback], calculated on the bars being analyzed; undefined if history is insufficient or the earlier average is zero.
Calculation: T1 bias = on T1 bars, bullish when Stage slope > flat slope threshold and Stage source > SMA(Stage source, MA lookback), bearish when Stage slope < -flat slope threshold and Stage source <= SMA(Stage source, MA lookback), otherwise neutral; each chart bar uses the value from the last completed T1 bar.
Calculation: Prior positive-volume average = mean of positive volume values among the preceding volume lookback chart bars, excluding the current bar; undefined if none are positive.
Calculation: Stage = on each chart-bar update, let M = SMA(Stage source, MA lookback), rising mean Stage slope > flat slope threshold, falling means Stage slope < -flat slope threshold, non-rising means Stage slope <= flat slope threshold, and non-falling means Stage slope >= -flat slope threshold; up breakout means Stage source > Highest(high, range lookback)[1] AND Stage source > M AND non-falling; down breakdown means Stage source < Lowest(low, range lookback)[1] AND Stage source < M AND non-rising. If Stage slope is undefined, Stage is undefined. Without a prior established Stage, assign Stage 2 when rising AND Stage source > M, Stage 4 when falling AND Stage source <= M, otherwise leave Stage undefined. From Stage 1, assign Stage 4 on down breakdown, Stage 2 on up breakout, otherwise remain Stage 1. From Stage 2, assign Stage 4 on down breakdown; otherwise assign Stage 3 when non-rising AND Stage slope has been rising at least once since Stage 2 began; otherwise assign Stage 1 when non-rising AND Stage source <= M AND Stage slope has not been rising since Stage 2 began; otherwise remain Stage 2. From Stage 3, assign Stage 4 on down breakdown, Stage 2 on up breakout, otherwise remain Stage 3. From Stage 4, assign Stage 2 on up breakout; otherwise assign Stage 1 when non-falling AND Stage slope has been falling at least once since Stage 4 began; otherwise assign Stage 3 when non-falling AND Stage source >= M AND Stage slope has not been falling since Stage 4 began; otherwise remain Stage 4.

Short: Stage = 4

Short: on a closed chart bar, Stage[1] is established AND Stage = 4 AND Stage[1] != 4 AND (HTF confirmation is disabled OR T1 is lower than the chart timeframe OR T1 bias is bearish) AND (trend-strength filter is disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback)[1] >= ADX threshold)
