Calculation: StochRSI K+D(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing, T) = SMA(StochRSI(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, T), K smoothing) + SMA(SMA(StochRSI(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, T), K smoothing), D smoothing), calculated on T bars using the still-forming T bar
Calculation: Aggregate score = (StochRSI K+D(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing, T1) + StochRSI K+D(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing, T2) + StochRSI K+D(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing, T3) + StochRSI K+D(close, RSI lookback, Stoch lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing, T4)) * 100 / 800, calculated on chart bars from the T1, T2, T3, and T4 values
Long: Low watch is armed when aggregate score <= low threshold while neither extreme watch is active; signal when low watch is active and aggregate score >= mid level, including the arming bar
Short: High watch is armed when aggregate score >= high threshold while neither extreme watch is active; signal when high watch is active and aggregate score <= mid level, including the arming bar
Long: After high watch triggers at aggregate score <= mid level, signal when aggregate score has not made a new running low for the confirmation lookback, counting the trigger bar
Short: After low watch triggers at aggregate score >= mid level, signal when aggregate score has not made a new running high for the confirmation lookback, counting the trigger bar
