Calculation: Signed close-change streak = positive consecutive count when close > close[1], negative consecutive count when close < close[1], otherwise 0
Calculation: Connors RSI = (RSI(close, RSI lookback) + RSI(signed close-change streak, streak RSI lookback) + percent rank(close - close[1], percentile lookback)) / 3; compute on T1 or T2 bars, with chart bars reading that timeframe’s current bar (still forming in realtime)
Calculation: Upper Keltner band = EMA(close, Keltner basis lookback) + Keltner multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback); compute on T1 bars, with chart bars reading the current T1 bar (still forming in realtime)
Calculation: Lower Keltner band = EMA(close, Keltner basis lookback) - Keltner multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback); compute on T1 bars, with chart bars reading the current T1 bar (still forming in realtime)
Long: On chart bars where time(T1) changes, Connors RSI(T1, current bar) ≤ oversold threshold AND ((Connors RSI(T2, current bar) ≥ lower regime boundary AND Connors RSI(T2, current bar) ≤ upper regime boundary AND low(T1, current bar) < lower Keltner band(T1, current bar) AND close(T1, current bar) > open(T1, current bar) AND close(T1, current bar) - open(T1, current bar) ≥ candle body fraction × (high(T1, current bar) - low(T1, current bar))) OR (Connors RSI(T2, current bar) > upper regime boundary AND (close(T1, current bar) ≤ lower Keltner band(T1, current bar) × long pullback factor OR (high(T1, current bar) > upper Keltner band one T1 bar ago AND close(T1, current bar) < upper Keltner band(T1, current bar)))))
Long: On chart bars where time(T1) changes, the long condition above AND at least the minimum trade spacing number of chart bars have elapsed since the previous entry order
Short: On chart bars where time(T1) changes, Connors RSI(T1, current bar) ≥ overbought threshold AND ((Connors RSI(T2, current bar) ≥ lower regime boundary AND Connors RSI(T2, current bar) ≤ upper regime boundary AND high(T1, current bar) > upper Keltner band(T1, current bar) AND close(T1, current bar) < open(T1, current bar) AND open(T1, current bar) - close(T1, current bar) ≥ candle body fraction × (high(T1, current bar) - low(T1, current bar))) OR (Connors RSI(T2, current bar) < lower regime boundary AND (close(T1, current bar) ≥ upper Keltner band(T1, current bar) × short pullback factor OR (low(T1, current bar) < lower Keltner band one T1 bar ago AND close(T1, current bar) > lower Keltner band(T1, current bar)))))
Short: On chart bars where time(T1) changes, the short condition above AND at least the minimum trade spacing number of chart bars have elapsed since the previous entry order
Exit: Long, stop-loss enabled AND (current bar low ≤ entry signal T1 close - stop ATR multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal) OR (take-profit enabled AND current bar high ≥ entry signal T1 close + take-profit ratio × stop ATR multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND highest high since entry ≥ entry price + trailing activation multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal) AND current bar low ≤ highest high since entry - trailing offset multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal)))
Exit: Short, stop-loss enabled AND (current bar high ≥ entry signal T1 close + stop ATR multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal) OR (take-profit enabled AND current bar low ≤ entry signal T1 close - take-profit ratio × stop ATR multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND lowest low since entry ≤ entry price - trailing activation multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal) AND current bar high ≥ lowest low since entry + trailing offset multiplier × ATR(T1, ATR lookback at entry signal)))
Exit: Long, the short condition above without the trade-spacing restriction AND at least the minimum hold number of chart bars have elapsed since long entry
Exit: Short, the long condition above without the trade-spacing restriction AND at least the minimum hold number of chart bars have elapsed since short entry
