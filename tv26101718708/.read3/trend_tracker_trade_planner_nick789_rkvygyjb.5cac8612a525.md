Calculation: Trend state and tracker = basis is EMA(source, fast lookback) in Scalp mode; in Trend mode it is the average of EMA(source, slow lookback) and EMA(source, max(slow lookback × trend length factor, fast lookback + trend length offset)); Scalp and Trend modes use their corresponding configured lookback rules. Volatility is ATR(ATR lookback) or SMA(True Range, ATR lookback); upper and lower bands are basis ± volatility × ATR multiplier. On chart bars, initialize bullish at the lower band; on T2–T8 bars, initialize bullish at the lower band when source ≥ basis, otherwise bearish at the upper band. Thereafter, become bullish when source > prior upper band, bearish when source < prior lower band, otherwise retain prior direction. The tracker is max(lower band, prior tracker when prior direction was bullish, otherwise lower band) in a bullish state, and min(upper band, prior tracker when prior direction was bearish, otherwise upper band) in a bearish state. On chart bars, use adaptive-selected parameters when available, otherwise manual parameters; on T2–T8 bars use manual parameters. T2–T8 states are calculated on their own bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off, using the forming timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Adaptive tracker settings = select among the Cartesian grid of candidate fast lookbacks, ATR lookbacks, and ATR multipliers, each formed from its start parameter plus a grid index times its step parameter. A candidate records TP1, TP2, TP3, and stop touches from its tracker flips and trade-plan levels, and counts a completed setup at the next flip. After the warmup threshold and minimum setup count, score candidates by the selected metric: TP1, TP2, or TP3 hit rate; inverse stop hit rate; net hit score (sum of TP hits minus stop hits × stop penalty, divided by setups); or balanced score (weighted TP hit rates minus stop hit rate × stop penalty). Best Score selects the highest eligible score. Stable Zone selects the non-boundary candidate maximizing the mean eligible score among adjacent grid candidates minus their population standard deviation × stability penalty. If no eligible score exists, use manual parameters.
Calculation: Trend flow = for each chart bar, sum lower-timeframe T1 volume where close > open and subtract volume where close < open, when T1 data is available and T1 is lower than the chart timeframe; otherwise apply the same signed-volume rule to chart bars. Dojis contribute zero. Cumulative trend delta resets when chart trend direction changes and adds each chart bar’s signed volume. Trend delta percentage = 100% × cumulative trend delta / cumulative buy-plus-sell volume, or zero when that volume is zero.
Calculation: Trade-plan levels = entry is the close on the setup creation bar; risk is the greater of the selected raw stop distance and the risk-floor multiple × minimum price increment. The raw distance is entry minus tracker for a long or tracker minus entry for a short when tracker-based risk is selected, otherwise fixed-stop ATR multiplier × ATR. Initial stop is entry minus risk for a long or entry plus risk for a short. Each of the three target levels is entry plus risk × its separate target ratio for a long or entry minus risk × its separate target ratio for a short. When trailing is enabled, the long stop is the greater of the prior stop and tracker; the short stop is the lesser of the prior stop and tracker.

Long: Exhaustion markers are enabled AND (strict band breach is disabled OR low < lower band) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-climax multiplier AND close > low + (high − low) × wick sensitivity AND trend delta percentage > its prior chart-bar value AND trend state is bearish.
Short: Exhaustion markers are enabled AND (strict band breach is disabled OR high > upper band) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-climax multiplier AND close < high − (high − low) × wick sensitivity AND trend delta percentage < its prior chart-bar value AND trend state is bullish.

Exit: short, low ≤ target 1 OR low ≤ target 2 OR low ≤ target 3 OR high ≥ stop.
