Calculation: Regime = initialize as range; count consecutive bars where LinReg(close, regime lookback, current offset) minus LinReg(close, regime lookback, prior offset) is positive (up) or negative (down) while (Highest(high, regime lookback) minus Lowest(low, regime lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback) exceeds the regime swing threshold; switch to that direction when its count reaches the regime confirmation length, switch to range when normalized swing is at or below the threshold, otherwise retain the prior regime.
Calculation: Descending pivot-high line = line through the latest and preceding confirmed pivot highs, using trendline pivot strength on both sides, extrapolated to the current bar when the latest pivot high is lower than the preceding one.
Calculation: Ascending pivot-low line = line through the latest and preceding confirmed pivot lows, using trendline pivot strength on both sides, extrapolated to the current bar when the latest pivot low is higher than the preceding one.
Calculation: Clustered pivot levels = on each confirmed pivot using support/resistance pivot strength on both sides, use the pivot high if present, otherwise the pivot low; merge it into the first existing level within ATR(ATR lookback) times the cluster-zone factor, replacing that level with the touch-count-weighted mean, or add a new level if none qualifies.
Calculation: Nearest support = the highest clustered pivot level at or below close.
Calculation: Nearest resistance = the lowest clustered pivot level at or above close.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = RSI divergence display enabled AND a new, not previously marked, aligned pair of latest confirmed price and RSI pivot lows, within the pivot-alignment tolerance, where the latest price pivot low is below its preceding pivot low and the latest RSI pivot low is above its preceding pivot low, OR hidden divergence display enabled AND such a pair where the latest price pivot low is above its preceding pivot low and the latest RSI pivot low is below its preceding pivot low; use RSI(close, RSI lookback) and divergence pivot strength on both sides.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = RSI divergence display enabled AND a new, not previously marked, aligned pair of latest confirmed price and RSI pivot highs, within the pivot-alignment tolerance, where the latest price pivot high is above its preceding pivot high and the latest RSI pivot high is below its preceding pivot high, OR hidden divergence display enabled AND such a pair where the latest price pivot high is below its preceding pivot high and the latest RSI pivot high is above its preceding pivot high; use RSI(close, RSI lookback) and divergence pivot strength on both sides.
Long: Ichimoku display enabled AND Senkou Span A(tenkan lookback, kijun lookback) > Senkou Span B(span-B lookback).
Short: Ichimoku display enabled AND Senkou Span A(tenkan lookback, kijun lookback) <= Senkou Span B(span-B lookback).
Long: Regime is up.
Short: Regime is down.
Long: Bullish RSI divergence.
Short: Bearish RSI divergence.
Long: confirmed bar AND low > high[gap offset] AND (low minus high[gap offset]) as a percentage of close >= minimum gap percentage.
Short: confirmed bar AND high < low[gap offset] AND (low[gap offset] minus high) as a percentage of close >= minimum gap percentage.
Long: ((Regime is up or down AND close crosses above the descending pivot-high line AND close > max(Senkou Span A(tenkan lookback, kijun lookback)[displacement bars ago], Senkou Span B(span-B lookback)[displacement bars ago])) OR (Regime is range AND low <= Nearest support + ATR(ATR lookback) times cluster-zone factor AND bullish body position >= body-position threshold, where bullish body position is (min(open, close) minus low) / (high minus low), or 0.5 when high = low) OR (Bullish RSI divergence AND low <= Nearest support + ATR(ATR lookback) times cluster-zone factor)) AND (session filter disabled OR current chart bar is within the configured session) AND no prior long composite signal within the signal-cooldown length of cooldown multiplier times trendline pivot strength.
Short: ((Regime is up or down AND close crosses below the ascending pivot-low line AND close < min(Senkou Span A(tenkan lookback, kijun lookback)[displacement bars ago], Senkou Span B(span-B lookback)[displacement bars ago])) OR (Regime is range AND high >= Nearest resistance - ATR(ATR lookback) times cluster-zone factor AND bearish body position >= body-position threshold, where bearish body position is (high minus max(open, close)) / (high minus low), or 0.5 when high = low) OR (Bearish RSI divergence AND high >= Nearest resistance - ATR(ATR lookback) times cluster-zone factor)) AND (session filter disabled OR current chart bar is within the configured session) AND no prior short composite signal within the signal-cooldown length of cooldown multiplier times trendline pivot strength.
