Calculation: Indicator streams I(h) use five increasing lookbacks H1 < H2 < H3 < H4 < H5: RSI, MFI, CMO, Momentum, and COG of the principle source at h; EMA(ADX(DMI(high, low, close, DMI lookback, ADX smoothing)), h); EMA(BOP(open, high, low, close), h); EMA(Stochastic(principle source, high, low, stochastic lookback), h); EMA(SWMA(principle source), h); EMA(TSI(principle source, TSI short lookback, TSI long lookback), h); EMA(Williams %R(Williams lookback), h); and EMA of Accumulation/Distribution, Intraday Intensity Index, Negative Volume Index, Positive Volume Index, On-Balance Volume, Price Volume Trend, and True Range at h.
Calculation: Indicator gap(I,h) = I(h) - SMA(I(H5), band-basis lookback); price gap(h) = EMA(price source,h) - SMA(EMA(price source,H5),band-basis lookback); volume gap(h) = EMA(volume,h) - SMA(EMA(volume,H5),band-basis lookback).
Calculation: Price-linked score(I) = sum over h in {H1,H2,H3,H4} of weight(h) × StDev(indicator gap(I,h), correlation lookback) × StDev(price gap(h), correlation lookback) × Correlation(indicator gap(I,h), price gap(h), correlation lookback); the four weights decrease as h increases.
Calculation: Volume-linked score(I) = sum over h in {H1,H2,H3,H4} of weight(h) × StDev(indicator gap(I,h), correlation lookback) × StDev(volume gap(h), correlation lookback) × Correlation(indicator gap(I,h), volume gap(h), correlation lookback), using the same weights as the price-linked score.
Calculation: Compound graph = sum over all indicator streams I of Price-linked score(I) × Volume-linked score(I) × Correlation(Price-linked score(I), Volume-linked score(I), correlation lookback).
Calculation: Cross score updates from its prior value by adding a pair-specific weight for the first true bullish event, or, if none is true, subtracting a pair-specific weight for the first true bearish event; pair priority is (H1,H2), (H1,H3), (H1,H4), (H1,H5), (H2,H3), (H2,H4), (H2,H5), (H3,H4), (H3,H5), (H4,H5). Each event is a crossover or crossunder, respectively, between any indicator streams at that pair; all bullish pairs precede all bearish pairs. Bearish events for the last three pairs exclude the True Range stream. If no event is true, the score is unchanged. Pair weights decrease in the stated order.
Calculation: Positive-signal graph = price signal × volume signal × Correlation(price signal, volume signal, correlation lookback), where price signal = StDev(Cross score - Cross score[1], correlation lookback) × StDev(price gap(H1), correlation lookback) × Correlation(StDev(price gap(H1), correlation lookback), StDev(Cross score - Cross score[1], correlation lookback), correlation lookback), and volume signal is the same formula using volume gap(H1).
Calculation: Signal strength = StDev(Compound graph - SMA(Compound graph, band-basis lookback), correlation lookback) × StDev(Positive-signal graph - SMA(Positive-signal graph, band-basis lookback), correlation lookback) × Correlation(StDev(Compound graph - SMA(Compound graph, band-basis lookback), correlation lookback), StDev(Positive-signal graph - SMA(Positive-signal graph, band-basis lookback), correlation lookback), correlation lookback).
Calculation: Buy trigger A = crosses(Signal strength - Signal strength[1], 0) AND falling(selected MA(average source, correlation lookback), falling lookback).
Calculation: Buy trigger B = crosses(Compound graph - Compound graph[1], 0).
Calculation: Buy trigger C = crosses(the second successive difference of Compound graph, 0).
Calculation: Buy trigger D = crosses(the third successive difference of Compound graph, 0).
Calculation: Buy trigger E = crosses(the fourth successive difference of Compound graph, 0).
Calculation: Buy trigger F = crosses(the fifth successive difference of Compound graph, 0).
Calculation: Sell trigger = Supertrend direction changes from negative to positive OR (Supertrend direction is positive AND any of Buy triggers A–F is true).
Long: crossover(EMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback), SMMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback)) AND SMMA(trend source, longer trend lookback) <= SMMA(trend source, intermediate trend lookback).
Long: (Buy trigger E OR Buy trigger F OR crossover(EMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback), SMMA(trend source, intermediate trend lookback))) AND position is flat AND datetime is within the configured entry window AND lower-point source < latest recorded swing low.
Long: (Buy trigger A AND selected MA(average source, correlation lookback) < average entry price OR (Buy trigger C OR Buy trigger E OR Buy trigger F) AND selected MA(average source, correlation lookback) < average entry price × (1 - drawdown threshold)) AND position is long AND datetime is within the configured entry window AND lower-point source < latest recorded swing low.
Exit: long, SMA(intermediate-sell source, intermediate-sell lookback) > minimum recorded swing-entry price × (1 + swing profit threshold) AND swing selling is enabled AND a swing high was recorded on the prior bar AND a swing low is recorded AND average entry price > selected MA(average source, correlation lookback).
Exit: long, rising(downtrend price-rise source, downtrend price-rise lookback) AND SMA(downtrend sell source, downtrend sell lookback) < current recorded high entry price × 100 / (100 + downtrend threshold) AND downtrend selling is enabled AND selected MA(average source, correlation lookback) < average entry price × (1 - drawdown threshold).
Exit: long, (Sell trigger AND profit target price < selected MA(average source, correlation lookback) AND a swing high was recorded on the prior bar AND (Buy trigger B OR Buy trigger C OR Buy trigger D OR Buy trigger F) AND EMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback) < SMMA(trend source, intermediate trend lookback)) OR (crossunder(EMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback), SMMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback)) AND profit target price < selected MA(average source, correlation lookback) AND SMMA(trend source, shorter trend lookback) > current recorded swing high).
