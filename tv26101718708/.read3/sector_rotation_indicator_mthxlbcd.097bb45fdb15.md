Calculation: Asset relative score = 100 × (EMA(close, score fast lookback, T1 current bar) / EMA(close, score slow lookback, T1 current bar) − 1); compute on T1 bars and map to chart bars, or compute on chart bars with day-scaled lookbacks when intraday-adapted scoring is enabled.
Calculation: Kernel deviation = mean of (close − K)/K for the Epanechnikov, logistic, and cosine estimates K, each the weighted mean of close[i] over i=0..bandwidth; with u=(i/bandwidth)², weights are 0.75×(1−u), 1/(exp(u)+2+exp(−u)), and cos(πi/(2×bandwidth))×(1−i/bandwidth), respectively. Use the configured kernel source bars and sampling stride.
Long: Kernel deviation > SMA(Kernel deviation, kernel mid lookback), using the EMA fallback when configured for the sub-hour kernel calculation.
Short: Kernel deviation <= SMA(Kernel deviation, kernel mid lookback), using the EMA fallback when configured for the sub-hour kernel calculation.

Short: Chart close crosses below EMA(close, alert slow lookback, T1 current bar mapped to chart bars) OR EMA(close, alert fast lookback, T1 current bar mapped to chart bars) crosses below EMA(close, alert slow lookback, T1 current bar mapped to chart bars).
Long: The selected asset changes to the higher-scoring asset in a two-asset comparison; on initial selection choose the higher score, with ties choosing the second asset. In a multi-asset comparison, choose the highest-scoring selected asset, with ties favoring the first in selection order. Require S&P 500 close >= EMA(close, cycle lower lookback, T1 current bar). For multi-asset comparisons in a bullish regime (S&P 500 close > SMA(close, regime lookback, T1 current bar)), require candidate score > active asset score + abs(active asset score) × rotation buffer. When a high-risk zone is active, require the candidate to have lower volatility than the active asset or a recent risk event within the guard window; otherwise require candidate score > active asset score + guard gap. When a calm zone is active and high risk is not, require higher candidate volatility or a recent calm-zone entry within the calm window; otherwise require candidate score > active asset score + calm gap. Volatility is standard deviation of close/close[1] − 1 over the volatility lookback on T1 bars.
Long: The selected asset's relative score >= the moderate-dominance threshold.
Short: The selected asset's relative score < the negative-equilibrium threshold.
Exit: Long, S&P 500 close < EMA(close, regime EMA lookback, T1 current bar) OR S&P 500 close <= SMA(close, regime lookback, T1 current bar).
Exit: Long, Bubble Risk Model score >= extreme-risk threshold + extreme-zone offset AND (S&P 500 close < EMA(close, regime EMA lookback, T1 current bar) OR S&P 500 close <= SMA(close, regime lookback, T1 current bar)) AND IXR relative score > IXT relative score.
