Calculation: Custom oscillator(source, lookback, volume weighting) = 50 + 50 × RMA(x × w, lookback) / RMA(abs(x) × w, lookback), where H = highest(source, lookback), L = lowest(source, lookback), x = H-L if H > H[1], -(H-L) if L < L[1], otherwise source-source[1], and w = volume when volume weighting is enabled or 1 otherwise; if the denominator is zero, the oscillator equals 50.
Calculation: Micro oscillator = the custom oscillator on chart bars, or, when higher-timeframe mode is selected, oscillator values computed on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off (last completed T2 bar historically and the forming T2 bar in real time).
Calculation: Four macro moving averages = moving averages of close computed on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars, with separately selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, or HMA types and separate lookbacks ordered shortest to longest. Use lookahead on when chart-bar confirmation is disabled (final T1 values on historical chart bars and the forming T1 bar in real time); use lookahead off when enabled (latest available completed T1 value historically and the forming T1 bar in real time). Sample T1 close with the same lookahead mode.
Calculation: Bullish macro alignment = shortest T1 moving average > next-shortest T1 moving average > next-longest T1 moving average > longest T1 moving average.
Calculation: Bearish macro alignment = shortest T1 moving average < next-shortest T1 moving average < next-longest T1 moving average < longest T1 moving average.
Calculation: Bullish macro trend = bullish macro alignment AND T1 close > shortest T1 moving average.
Calculation: Bearish macro trend = bearish macro alignment AND T1 close < shortest T1 moving average.
Calculation: Signal confirmation = chart-bar confirmation is disabled OR the chart bar is confirmed.
Calculation: Micro RSI setup states = long full-cycle activates when the micro oscillator is at or below the oversold threshold and clears when it is at or above the overbought threshold; short full-cycle activates at or above the overbought threshold and clears at or below the oversold threshold; long shallow activates on a cross below the overbought threshold and clears at or below 50 or at or above the overbought threshold; short shallow activates on a cross above the oversold threshold and clears at or above 50 or at or below the oversold threshold; long midline activates after the oscillator has reached the overbought threshold and crosses below 50, and clears at or below the oversold threshold or at or above the overbought threshold; short midline activates after it has reached the oversold threshold and crosses above 50, and clears at or above the overbought threshold or at or below the oversold threshold. Any same-side micro signal clears all three same-side setups after that bar's signal conditions are evaluated.
Calculation: Qualifying divergence pivots = matching confirmed pivots of the selected price source and micro oscillator on the same chart bar, using the same parameterized left and right pivot window; the oscillator pivot must be at least the parameterized minimum distance from 50, and qualifying pivots of the same side must be separated by at least the parameterized minimum bar distance. Compare each new qualifying pivot with the preceding qualifying pivot of the same side.
Long: Bullish macro alignment is true and was false on the prior chart bar AND signal confirmation.
Short: Bearish macro alignment is true and was false on the prior chart bar AND signal confirmation.
Long: Bullish macro trend is true and was false on the prior chart bar AND signal confirmation.
Short: Bearish macro trend is true and was false on the prior chart bar AND signal confirmation.
Long: Bullish macro trend AND signal confirmation AND ((long full-cycle setup AND micro oscillator crosses above the overbought threshold) OR (long shallow setup AND micro oscillator crosses above the overbought threshold) OR (long midline setup AND micro oscillator crosses above 50)).
Short: Bearish macro trend AND signal confirmation AND ((short full-cycle setup AND micro oscillator crosses below the oversold threshold) OR (short shallow setup AND micro oscillator crosses below the oversold threshold) OR (short midline setup AND micro oscillator crosses below 50)).
Long: Regular-divergence option enabled AND signal confirmation AND a new qualifying low pivot has a lower price-source value and a higher oscillator value than the preceding qualifying low pivot.
Short: Regular-divergence option enabled AND signal confirmation AND a new qualifying high pivot has a higher price-source value and a lower oscillator value than the preceding qualifying high pivot.
Long: Hidden-divergence option enabled AND bullish macro trend AND signal confirmation AND a new qualifying low pivot has a higher price-source value and a lower oscillator value than the preceding qualifying low pivot.
Short: Hidden-divergence option enabled AND bearish macro trend AND signal confirmation AND a new qualifying high pivot has a lower price-source value and a higher oscillator value than the preceding qualifying high pivot.
