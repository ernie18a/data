Calculation: Support/resistance zones = use PivotHigh and PivotLow of the selected high/low source, each with the pivot lookback on both sides; on each confirmed pivot, prepend the high pivot if present, otherwise the low pivot, and discard pivots older than the pivot retention lookback. For each stored pivot in newest-first order, seed a candidate zone at its price; include each stored pivot whose distance to the candidate's nearest boundary is at most (Highest(high, channel-width lookback) - Lowest(low, channel-width lookback)) × maximum channel width percent / 100, expanding the bounds and adding pivot-strength weight per included pivot. Add touch-strength weight for each bar from the current bar through the pivot retention lookback ago whose high or low lies within the candidate. Keep candidates with strength at least minimum strength × pivot-strength weight; repeatedly select the strongest candidate and discard candidates whose upper or lower boundary lies inside it. Sort selected zones by strength descending, up to zone capacity; use only the first active-zone-count zones for break decisions.
Calculation: Most recent breakout level = the upper boundary crossed by the latest upward zone break or the lower boundary crossed by the latest downward zone break.
Long: For any of the first active-zone-count zones, close[1] <= its upper boundary AND close > its upper boundary.
Short: For any of the first active-zone-count zones, close[1] >= its lower boundary AND close < its lower boundary.
Long: (close[1] <= an active zone's upper boundary AND close > that boundary OR a most recent breakout level exists AND close < that level AND close[1] >= that level) AND MA1 and MA2 are enabled AND MA1 > MA2, where MA1 is its configured SMA or EMA of close with the shorter lookback and MA2 is its configured SMA or EMA of close with the longer lookback AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI oversold threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI overbought threshold AND ATR(ATR lookback) > ATR(ATR lookback) × volatility multiplier AND hour(datetime) >= trading start hour AND hour(datetime) <= trading end hour AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier.
Short: (close[1] >= an active zone's lower boundary AND close < that boundary OR a most recent breakout level exists AND close > that level AND close[1] <= that level) AND MA1 and MA2 are enabled AND MA1 < MA2, where MA1 is its configured SMA or EMA of close with the shorter lookback and MA2 is its configured SMA or EMA of close with the longer lookback AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI oversold threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI overbought threshold AND ATR(ATR lookback) > ATR(ATR lookback) × volatility multiplier AND hour(datetime) >= trading start hour AND hour(datetime) <= trading end hour AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier.
Exit: long, RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long RSI exit threshold OR MA2 crosses above MA1 OR low <= close[1] - ATR(ATR lookback)[1] × stop-distance multiplier OR high >= close[1] + ATR(ATR lookback)[1] × stop-distance multiplier × reward ratio.
Exit: short, RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short RSI exit threshold OR MA1 crosses above MA2 OR high >= close[1] + ATR(ATR lookback)[1] × stop-distance multiplier OR low <= close[1] - ATR(ATR lookback)[1] × stop-distance multiplier × reward ratio.
