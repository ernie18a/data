Calculation: Short score = count of true conditions: EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, medium lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback), with fast < medium < slow, AND EMA(close, fast lookback) < its value at the slope lookback AND EMA(close, medium lookback) < its value at the slope lookback; close < EMA(close, medium lookback); RSI(close, RSI lookback) < the short RSI threshold AND RSI > the oversold threshold AND RSI < its previous-bar value; MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram <= its previous-bar value AND bars since MACD-line crossover below signal line <= the MACD recency-window parameter; (Smoothed %K crosses below Smoothed %D AND Smoothed %K > stochastic oversold threshold) OR (Smoothed %K < Smoothed %D AND Smoothed %K > stochastic oversold threshold AND Smoothed %K < stochastic overbought threshold); (close < Bollinger basis AND previous close >= current Bollinger basis) OR (high >= Bollinger upper band AND close < Bollinger upper band); ADX(adx lookback) >= ADX minimum AND -DI(adx lookback) > +DI(adx lookback); close < open AND open - close > (high - low) * candle-body ratio parameter

Short: Short setup AND NOT Long setup
