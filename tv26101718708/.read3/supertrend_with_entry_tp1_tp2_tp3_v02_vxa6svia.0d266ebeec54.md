Calculation: Upper breakout boundary(n) = the line through the two latest confirmed pivot highs projected to bar n, unless a parallel channel from the line through the two latest confirmed pivot lows has positive width, in which case use that offset line; confirm pivots with the pivot lookback on each side.
Calculation: Lower breakout boundary(n) = the line through the two latest confirmed pivot lows projected to bar n, unless a parallel channel from the line through the two latest confirmed pivot highs has positive width, in which case use that offset line; confirm pivots with the pivot lookback on each side.
Calculation: TP3 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) ATR at the setup bar × stop ATR multiplier × TP3 risk/reward.
Calculation: MTF trend score(T) = score midpoint if ADX(T) is below the ADX range threshold or either indicator is unavailable; otherwise score midpoint plus min(ADX(T), score midpoint) in a Supertrend uptrend, or minus min(ADX(T), score midpoint) in a Supertrend downtrend. Use the chart Supertrend ATR lookback and factor and ADX DI lookback and smoothing; evaluate on chart bars using T's current bar, which can still be forming in real time.
Long: Supertrend direction is uptrend.
Short: Supertrend direction is downtrend.
Long: ADX is available and +DI(ADX DI lookback) > -DI(ADX DI lookback).
Short: ADX is available and -DI(ADX DI lookback) > +DI(ADX DI lookback).
Long: For any of T1, T2, T3, T4, or T5, on chart bars using that timeframe's current bar, MTF trend score(T) > score midpoint + neutral-zone half-width.
Short: For any of T1, T2, T3, T4, or T5, on chart bars using that timeframe's current bar, MTF trend score(T) < score midpoint − neutral-zone half-width.
Long: (Supertrend direction changes from downtrend to uptrend OR breakout signals are enabled AND close > upper breakout boundary(current bar) AND close[1] <= upper breakout boundary(previous bar) OR Supertrend re-entry is enabled AND a prior long setup reached TP3 AND Supertrend direction is uptrend AND low <= Supertrend AND close > Supertrend OR EMA re-entry is enabled AND a prior long setup reached TP3 AND close crossed below the re-entry EMA on an earlier bar while Supertrend was up AND close crosses above the re-entry EMA while Supertrend is up OR aggressive EMA re-entry is enabled AND a prior long setup reached TP3 AND Supertrend direction is uptrend AND low <= the re-entry EMA AND close > the re-entry EMA) AND (signal ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > signal ADX threshold) AND (EMA direction filter is disabled OR close > EMA(close, direction-filter lookback)).
Short: (Supertrend direction changes from uptrend to downtrend OR breakout signals are enabled AND close < lower breakout boundary(current bar) AND close[1] >= lower breakout boundary(previous bar) OR Supertrend re-entry is enabled AND a prior short setup reached TP3 AND Supertrend direction is downtrend AND high >= Supertrend AND close < Supertrend OR EMA re-entry is enabled AND a prior short setup reached TP3 AND close crossed above the re-entry EMA on an earlier bar while Supertrend was down AND close crosses below the re-entry EMA while Supertrend is down OR aggressive EMA re-entry is enabled AND a prior short setup reached TP3 AND Supertrend direction is downtrend AND high >= the re-entry EMA AND close < the re-entry EMA) AND (signal ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > signal ADX threshold) AND (EMA direction filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close, direction-filter lookback)).
