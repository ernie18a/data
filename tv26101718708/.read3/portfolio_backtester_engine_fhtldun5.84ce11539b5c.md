Calculation: Long entry stop = 0 when no entry stop is selected; minimum(open, close) - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiple for an ATR stop; or minimum(open, close) * (1 - stop percentage) for a percentage stop.
Calculation: Short entry stop = +infinity when no entry stop is selected; maximum(open, close) + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiple for an ATR stop; or maximum(open, close) * (1 + stop percentage) for a percentage stop.
Calculation: Initial stop gap = absolute difference between entry price and selected entry stop.
Calculation: Smoothed log rate of change = EMA(100 * ln(source / source[lookback]), smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Long candidate stop = highest high since entry - initial stop gap * multiple for an X-multiple stop; highest high since entry * (1 - percentage) for a percentage stop; highest high since entry - fixed amount for a fixed-value stop; entry price for a break-even stop; average of minimum(open, close) from the previous two bars - ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiple for an ATR stop; or previous bar low for a last-low stop.
Calculation: Short candidate stop = lowest low since entry + initial stop gap * multiple for an X-multiple stop; lowest low since entry * (1 + percentage) for a percentage stop; lowest low since entry + fixed amount for a fixed-value stop; entry price for a break-even stop; average of maximum(open, close) from the previous two bars + ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiple for an ATR stop; or previous bar high for a last-high stop.
Calculation: Long in-trade stop = entry stop on the entry bar; thereafter, max(previous in-trade stop, long candidate stop) when the selected kick-in condition holds, otherwise the previous in-trade stop; keep the previous stop if the candidate exceeds the current high. The selected kick-in condition is: candidate stop > entry stop; candidate stop > max(entry price + initial stop gap * multiple, entry stop); candidate stop > max(entry price * (1 + percentage), entry stop); candidate stop > max(entry price + fixed amount, entry stop); or highest high since entry > max(entry price * (1 + percentage), entry stop).
Calculation: Short in-trade stop = entry stop on the entry bar; thereafter, min(previous in-trade stop, short candidate stop) when the selected kick-in condition holds, otherwise the previous in-trade stop; keep the previous stop if the candidate is below the current low. The selected kick-in condition is: candidate stop < entry stop; candidate stop < min(entry price - initial stop gap * multiple, entry stop); candidate stop < min(entry price * (1 - percentage), entry stop); candidate stop < min(entry price - fixed amount, entry stop); or lowest low since entry < min(entry price * (1 - percentage), entry stop).
Long: When the RSI-cross entry strategy is selected, RSI(close, entry lookback) crosses above the lower entry level with normal signal mapping, or crosses below the upper entry level with reversed signal mapping; long trading is enabled; datetime is within the date range if date filtering is enabled; equity > 0; the drawdown circuit breaker has not triggered; and either the position is flat with all enabled entry filters passing, or a long position is open, pyramiding is enabled, the add limit is not reached, the selected pyramiding condition holds, and all enabled entry filters pass if required for pyramiding. The pyramiding condition is close > last entry price + initial stop gap * multiple; close > last entry price * (1 + percentage); close > last entry price + price increment; current entry source and side match the last entry; current entry source or side differs from the last entry; or any opportunity, according to the selected mode. A short position also opens long when an enabled reversal follows an opposite-signal exit or a short-exit RSI crossover.
Short: When the RSI-cross entry strategy is selected, RSI(close, entry lookback) crosses below the upper entry level with normal signal mapping, or crosses above the lower entry level with reversed signal mapping; short trading is enabled; datetime is within the date range if date filtering is enabled; equity > 0; the drawdown circuit breaker has not triggered; and either the position is flat with all enabled entry filters passing, or a short position is open, pyramiding is enabled, the add limit is not reached, the selected pyramiding condition holds, and all enabled entry filters pass if required for pyramiding. The pyramiding condition is close < last entry price - initial stop gap * multiple; close < last entry price * (1 - percentage); close < last entry price - price increment; current entry source and side match the last entry; current entry source or side differs from the last entry; or any opportunity, according to the selected mode. A long position also opens short when an enabled reversal follows an opposite-signal exit or a long-exit RSI crossunder.
Exit: Long, low <= long entry stop OR (trailing stop enabled AND low < long in-trade stop from the preceding bar, using the current stop on the entry bar) OR (Smoothed log rate of change was rising over its rise lookback on the previous bar AND is no longer rising over that lookback) OR RSI(close, exit lookback) crosses below the lower long-exit level OR close > entry price + initial stop gap * X-multiple target OR close > entry price * (1 + price-gain percentage) OR in-trade equity >= account equity + account equity * equity-gain percentage.
Exit: Short, high >= short entry stop OR (trailing stop enabled AND high > short in-trade stop from the preceding bar, using the current stop on the entry bar) OR (Smoothed log rate of change was falling over its fall lookback on the previous bar AND is no longer falling over that lookback) OR RSI(close, exit lookback) crosses above the upper short-exit level OR close < entry price - initial stop gap * X-multiple target OR close < entry price * (1 - price-gain percentage) OR in-trade equity >= account equity + account equity * equity-gain percentage.
