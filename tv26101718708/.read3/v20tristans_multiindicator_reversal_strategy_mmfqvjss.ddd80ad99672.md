Calculation: Bearish aligned count = count of true conditions among RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI overbought level AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1]; MACD histogram < 0 OR MACD line crosses below signal line; Williams %R(high, low, close, Williams %R lookback) > Williams %R overbought level AND Williams %R(high, low, close, Williams %R lookback) < Williams %R(high, low, close, Williams %R lookback)[1]; close > upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger Band lookback, deviation) OR close[1] > upper Bollinger Band(close, Bollinger Band lookback, deviation)[1]
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle = close > open AND close - open > (high - close) * body-to-opposing-wick multiplier AND open - low > (high - low) * minimum-wick fraction
Calculation: Bearish reversal candle = close < open AND open - close > (close - low) * body-to-opposing-wick multiplier AND high - open > (high - low) * minimum-wick fraction
Calculation: Standard long signal condition = bullish aligned count >= minimum aligned indicators AND divergence confirmation disabled AND (volume confirmation not required OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (reversal candle confirmation not required OR bullish reversal candle) AND within selected trading session
Calculation: Standard short signal condition = bearish aligned count >= minimum aligned indicators AND divergence confirmation disabled AND (volume confirmation not required OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (reversal candle confirmation not required OR bearish reversal candle) AND within selected trading session
Calculation: Long signal condition = (signals flipped AND standard short signal condition) OR (signals not flipped AND standard long signal condition)
Calculation: Short signal condition = (signals flipped AND standard long signal condition) OR (signals not flipped AND standard short signal condition)
Long: Long signal condition AND chart bar confirmed
Short: Short signal condition AND chart bar confirmed
Long: bullish aligned count >= setup aligned-count threshold AND NOT long signal condition AND within selected trading session
Short: bearish aligned count >= setup aligned-count threshold AND NOT short signal condition AND within selected trading session
Long: bullish aligned count >= minimum aligned indicators
Short: bullish aligned count < minimum aligned indicators AND bearish aligned count >= minimum aligned indicators
Exit: Long, stop-loss enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100) OR take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage / 100)
Exit: Short, stop-loss enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) OR take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage / 100)
