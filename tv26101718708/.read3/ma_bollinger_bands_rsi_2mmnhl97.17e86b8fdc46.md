Calculation: ATR stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier
Calculation: Long fixed stop = signal-bar close − ATR stop distance on the signal bar
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close + (signal-bar close − long fixed stop) × risk-reward
Calculation: Short fixed stop = signal-bar close + ATR stop distance on the signal bar
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar close − (short fixed stop − signal-bar close) × risk-reward
Calculation: Long trailing stop = running maximum since long entry of (lowest(low, trailing lookback) − ATR stop distance)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = running minimum since short entry of (highest(high, trailing lookback) + ATR stop distance)
Calculation: New direction signal = first valid long signal since the last valid short signal, or first valid short signal since the last valid long signal; the first valid signal counts
Long: open < lower Bollinger Band(close, selected moving-average type, MA lookback, Bollinger lookback, band multiplier) AND close > lower Bollinger Band(close, selected moving-average type, MA lookback, Bollinger lookback, band multiplier) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above RSI neutral on the current bar or within the preceding RSI cross lookback bars AND volume is neither extreme-high nor extreme-low AND (date filter disabled OR datetime < start date OR datetime > end date) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within trading session) AND bar is confirmed
Short: open > upper Bollinger Band(close, selected moving-average type, MA lookback, Bollinger lookback, band multiplier) AND close < upper Bollinger Band(close, selected moving-average type, MA lookback, Bollinger lookback, band multiplier) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below RSI neutral on the current bar or within the preceding RSI cross lookback bars AND volume is neither extreme-high nor extreme-low AND (date filter disabled OR datetime < start date OR datetime > end date) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within trading session) AND bar is confirmed
Exit: Long, (stop-loss enabled AND low <= long fixed stop) OR (stop-loss disabled AND trailing stop enabled AND low <= long trailing stop) OR (stop-loss disabled AND trailing stop disabled AND new direction signal AND low <= upper Bollinger Band(close, selected moving-average type, MA lookback, Bollinger lookback, band multiplier)) OR (take-profit enabled AND high >= long target)
Exit: Short, (stop-loss enabled AND high >= short fixed stop) OR (stop-loss disabled AND trailing stop enabled AND high >= short trailing stop) OR (stop-loss disabled AND trailing stop disabled AND new direction signal AND high >= lower Bollinger Band(close, selected moving-average type, MA lookback, Bollinger lookback, band multiplier)) OR (take-profit enabled AND low <= short target)
