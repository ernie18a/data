Calculation: On each chart bar, adaptive upper and lower bands = basis ± effective multiplier × STDEV(source, band lookback); basis is the configured SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA, or in adaptive mode VWMA when relative volume exceeds its threshold, otherwise EMA when ADX exceeds its threshold, otherwise SMA; relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) when both are positive, otherwise its fallback; effective multiplier = base multiplier × EMA(raw dynamic factor, smoothing lookback) when dynamic scaling is enabled, otherwise base multiplier; raw dynamic factor is the weighted mean of ADX magnitude, relative-volume magnitude, and RSI magnitude, using their corresponding weights; ADX magnitude and weight are each clamp(ADX / ADX scale, their respective ranges); relative-volume magnitude and weight are each clamp(relative volume, their respective ranges); RSI magnitude = RSI magnitude baseline + abs(RSI(source, RSI lookback) − RSI midpoint) / RSI magnitude divisor × RSI elasticity; RSI weight = min(RSI weight cap, RSI base weight + abs(RSI − RSI midpoint) / RSI weight divisor); use the configured ADX fallback and RSI midpoint for unavailable values, and the configured neutral factor for unavailable or nonpositive raw or smoothed factors.
