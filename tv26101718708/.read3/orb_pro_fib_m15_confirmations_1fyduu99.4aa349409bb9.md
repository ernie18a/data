Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low across chart bars in either of two configured session windows, reset at each window's start
Long: After the current range's first breakout is confirmed upward by the configured number of consecutive chart-bar closes above its high, and before any signal for that range, close > RMA(close, RMA lookback) AND RMA(close, RMA lookback) > RMA(close, same lookback)[slope lag] AND Ichimoku conversion line(high, low, shorter lookback) > Ichimoku base line(high, low, longer lookback) AND opening-range high - opening-range low > ATR(ATR lookback) * range-width multiplier
Short: After the current range's first breakout is confirmed downward by the configured number of consecutive chart-bar closes below its low, and before any signal for that range, close < RMA(close, RMA lookback) AND RMA(close, RMA lookback) < RMA(close, same lookback)[slope lag] AND Ichimoku conversion line(high, low, shorter lookback) < Ichimoku base line(high, low, longer lookback) AND opening-range high - opening-range low > ATR(ATR lookback) * range-width multiplier
