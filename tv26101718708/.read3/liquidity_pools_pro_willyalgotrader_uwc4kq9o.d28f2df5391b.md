Calculation: Pivot left lookback = if adaptive pivots are enabled, round(clamp(adaptive base / max(volatility floor, ATR(fast ATR lookback) / ATR(slow ATR lookback)), adaptive minimum, adaptive maximum)); otherwise manual pivot lookback. ATR and pivot calculations use chart bars.
Calculation: Signal warm-up = max(warm-up minimum, risk ATR lookback + warm-up offset) chart bars.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = After signal warm-up, create pools from confirmed chart-bar PivotHigh(high, pivot left lookback, right-confirmation lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot left lookback, right-confirmation lookback). Match each pivot to the first tracked active pool of the same side with absolute pivot-price-to-level distance ≤ ATR(fast ATR lookback) × equality tolerance multiplier and current bar index − last pivot index ≤ maximum pool age; otherwise create a pool. On a match, set level = (old level × touch count + pivot price) / (touch count + one), add pivot-bar volume to cumulative volume, increment touch count, and set last pivot index to the pivot bar. Set bounds to level ± ATR(fast ATR lookback) × equality tolerance multiplier × zone half-width fraction. Add each later-index chart bar's volume when its range intersects the stored bounds. When HTF confluence is enabled, set the pool's HTF bonus once if the pivot price is within ATR(fast ATR lookback) × equality tolerance multiplier × HTF confluence multiplier of the corresponding PivotHigh(high) or PivotLow(low) with the HTF pivot lookback on both sides, using the last completed T1 bar and sampling its value on chart bars. After signal evaluation, remove swept pools when swept-zone retention is disabled; otherwise retain them. If tracked pool count exceeds capacity, remove the oldest swept pool, or the active pool with lowest strength if none is swept.
Calculation: Pool strength = clamp(min(touch point cap, (touch count − one) × points per extra touch) + recency point cap × decay base^(bars since last pivot / strength half-life) + (when volume weighting is enabled and median tracked-pool cumulative volume is positive, min(volume point cap, ln(max(volume-ratio floor, pool cumulative volume / median tracked-pool cumulative volume)) × volume log scale), otherwise zero) + HTF bonus, strength floor, strength ceiling).
Calculation: Entry price = signal-bar close. Risk distance = ATR(risk ATR lookback) × SL ATR multiplier, or (signal-bar close − low for long / high − signal-bar close for short) + ATR(risk ATR lookback) × wick buffer when wick-based stop placement is selected and this distance is positive and at least ATR(risk ATR lookback) × minimum wick-distance fraction; otherwise use the ATR distance. TP1 and TP3 prices = entry price ± risk distance × their separate target-risk multipliers, upward for long and downward for short.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar at or after signal warm-up, when sweep signals are enabled, any active low pool is at least the right-confirmation lookback plus one chart bar past its last pivot, low < pool lower bound, pool strength ≥ minimum signal strength, and close > pool level if body reversal is required; omit the close condition otherwise.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar at or after signal warm-up, when sweep signals are enabled, any active high pool is at least the right-confirmation lookback plus one chart bar past its last pivot, high > pool upper bound, pool strength ≥ minimum signal strength, and close < pool level if body reversal is required; omit the close condition otherwise.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low ≤ entry price − risk distance unless break-even is enabled and TP1 was first touched on an earlier bar, in which case low ≤ entry price; OR high ≥ entry price + risk distance × TP3 target-risk multiplier.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high ≥ entry price + risk distance unless break-even is enabled and TP1 was first touched on an earlier bar, in which case high ≥ entry price; OR low ≤ entry price − risk distance × TP3 target-risk multiplier.
