Calculation: ATM high = highest(high, configured ATM range window)
Calculation: ATM low = lowest(low, configured ATM range window)
Calculation: minimum significant FVG size = max(minimum gap ticks × tick size, ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum gap ATR multiplier)
Calculation: reversal stop = at entry, if sweep swing mode, long: lowest(low, from ATM sweep through entry) − stop buffer in ticks × tick size; short: highest(high, from ATM sweep through entry) + stop buffer in ticks × tick size; otherwise, long: bearish FVG lower bound − stop buffer in ticks × tick size; short: bullish FVG upper bound + stop buffer in ticks × tick size
Calculation: reversal target = if opposing ATM mode, long: ATM high; short: ATM low; otherwise, long: entry price + abs(entry price − reversal stop) × fixed-RR parameter; short: entry price − abs(entry price − reversal stop) × fixed-RR parameter
Calculation: continuation stop = long: lowest(low, from FVG tap through current bar) − stop buffer in ticks × tick size; short: highest(high, from FVG tap through current bar) + stop buffer in ticks × tick size
Calculation: continuation target = long: entry price + (entry price − continuation stop); short: entry price − (continuation stop − entry price)
Long: on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window, before any ATM sweep that session, low < ATM low and high ≤ ATM high
Short: on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window, before any ATM sweep that session, high > ATM high and low ≥ ATM low
Long: after an ATM low sweep and before bullish MSS confirmation, on a confirmed bar, close > the level fixed at the sweep: the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot strength, pivot strength) if its pivot age ≤ maximum pivot age, otherwise highest(high[1], fallback lookback)
Short: after an ATM high sweep and before bearish MSS confirmation, on a confirmed bar, close < the level fixed at the sweep: the latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, pivot strength, pivot strength) if its pivot age ≤ maximum pivot age, otherwise lowest(low[1], fallback lookback)
Long: after an ATM high sweep, with continuation enabled, on a confirmed bar in an enabled ATM execution window, before any hard switch, high ≥ ATM high + configured switch distance
Short: after an ATM low sweep, with continuation enabled, on a confirmed bar in an enabled ATM execution window, before any hard switch, low ≤ ATM low − configured switch distance
Long: after an ATM low sweep, on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window, before the high reaches ATM high and, when continuation is enabled, before the low reaches ATM low − configured switch distance; the reversal timing filter allows entry; the newest retained active bearish Fair Value Gap formed since the ATM range started and within the configured chart-bar age has high < low[2] and low[2] − high ≥ minimum significant FVG size; close > its upper boundary and close[1] ≤ its upper boundary; reversal stop < close and reversal target > close
Short: after an ATM high sweep, on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window, before the low reaches ATM low and, when continuation is enabled, before the high reaches ATM high + configured switch distance; the reversal timing filter allows entry; the newest retained active bullish Fair Value Gap formed since the ATM range started and within the configured chart-bar age has low > high[2] and low − high[2] ≥ minimum significant FVG size; close < its lower boundary and close[1] ≥ its lower boundary; reversal stop > close and reversal target < close
Long: after a hard switch to continuation long, on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window later than the switch bar, the latest uninvalidated bullish Fair Value Gap for an enabled source timeframe overlaps the chart bar, was formed at or after the ATM sweep and within the configured elapsed-time age; for T1, calculate low > high[2] on confirmed chart bars when the chart timeframe is T1; for T2, use low > high[2] from the last completed T2 bar on chart bars; chart close ≥ the gap lower bound
Short: after a hard switch to continuation short, on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window later than the switch bar, the latest uninvalidated bearish Fair Value Gap for an enabled source timeframe overlaps the chart bar, was formed at or after the ATM sweep and within the configured elapsed-time age; for T1, calculate high < low[2] on confirmed chart bars when the chart timeframe is T1; for T2, use high < low[2] from the last completed T2 bar on chart bars; chart close ≤ the gap upper bound
Long: after a qualifying bullish Fair Value Gap tap, on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window later than the tap bar, close > the highest high from the hard-switch bar through the bar before the tap, and continuation stop < close
Short: after a qualifying bearish Fair Value Gap tap, on a confirmed chart bar in an enabled ATM execution window later than the tap bar, close < the lowest low from the hard-switch bar through the bar before the tap, and continuation stop > close
Exit: long, after a reversal long entry and on a later confirmed bar, low ≤ reversal stop OR high ≥ reversal target
Exit: short, after a reversal short entry and on a later confirmed bar, high ≥ reversal stop OR low ≤ reversal target
Exit: short, while a reversal short is active, on a confirmed bar in an enabled ATM execution window after an ATM high sweep, high ≥ ATM high + configured switch distance
Exit: long, while a reversal long is active, on a confirmed bar in an enabled ATM execution window after an ATM low sweep, low ≤ ATM low − configured switch distance
