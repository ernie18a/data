Calculation: Estimated buy volume = sum of nz(volume, 0) × (close − low) ÷ (high − low) over completed T1 bars within each chart bar, evaluated on chart bars at close; use the chart bar alone when T1 aggregation is not selected; omit zero-range bars when zero-range skipping is enabled, otherwise assign half their volume.
Calculation: Estimated sell volume = sum of nz(volume, 0) × (high − close) ÷ (high − low) over completed T1 bars within each chart bar, evaluated on chart bars at close; use the chart bar alone when T1 aggregation is not selected; omit zero-range bars when zero-range skipping is enabled, otherwise assign half their volume.
Calculation: Contested volume = min(Estimated buy volume, Estimated sell volume)
Calculation: Net delta = Estimated buy volume − Estimated sell volume
Calculation: Seasonal log baseline = average of the per-slot EMA(log1p(Contested volume), seasonal-memory parameter) for the current and adjacent clock-time slots, evaluated on chart bars using prior sessions.
Calculation: Seasonal baseline volume = exp(Seasonal log baseline) − 1
Calculation: Raw time-of-day residual = log1p(Contested volume) − Seasonal log baseline
Calculation: Session adjustment = session-normalization parameter × n ÷ (n + session-prior-count parameter) × mean of prior current-session chart-bar Raw time-of-day residuals, each clipped to ±session-clamp parameter; n is the number of prior chart bars in the session.
Calculation: Time-of-day trigger source = nz(Raw time-of-day residual − Session adjustment, 0)
Calculation: Bubble signal = confirmed chart bar AND (intrabar data is not required OR T1 is not active OR at least one T1 bar is available) AND ((effective normalization is Standard AND EMA(Contested volume, smoothing lookback) > 0 AND highest(EMA(Contested volume, smoothing lookback), trigger lookback)[1] > lowest(EMA(Contested volume, smoothing lookback), trigger lookback)[1] AND (activity floor is disabled OR EMA(Contested volume, smoothing lookback) ≥ activity floor × SMA(EMA(Contested volume, smoothing lookback), activity baseline lookback)) AND PercentRank(EMA(Contested volume, smoothing lookback), trigger lookback) ≥ trigger percentile threshold) OR (effective normalization is Time Of Day AND chart bars are intraday, non-tick, and meet the minimum supported interval AND Contested volume > 0 AND current-slot history covers the warm-up sessions parameter AND Raw time-of-day residual is valid for at least the trigger lookback consecutive chart bars AND (activity floor is disabled OR Seasonal baseline volume is available AND Contested volume ≥ activity floor × Seasonal baseline volume) AND PercentRank(Time-of-day trigger source, trigger lookback) ≥ trigger percentile threshold)); when Time Of Day normalization is selected but unavailable, effective normalization is Standard.
Long: Bubble signal AND Net delta ≥ 0
Short: Bubble signal AND Net delta < 0
