Calculation: Effective time = on realtime bars, the earlier of current time and chart-bar close; otherwise chart-bar close, adjusted to the later of the latest T1 tick time and effective time minus the stale-tick grace parameter
Calculation: T1 tick rate = current chart bar's T1 tick count / max(effective time minus chart-bar open, minimum elapsed-duration parameter); calculated on chart bars from current T1 ticks
Calculation: T1 volume rate = current chart bar's T1 tick volume / max(effective time minus chart-bar open, minimum elapsed-duration parameter); calculated on chart bars from current T1 ticks
Calculation: T2, T3, and T4 are successively longer scales; for each T, seconds per candle = max((start time of newest T candle minus start time of the T candle its separate T candle-lookback parameter bars earlier) / that lookback, effective time minus start time of newest T candle); T tick rate = trades per T candle / seconds per candle; T volume rate = mean volume of that many prior completed T candles / seconds per candle; calculated on chart bars using T data, with the newest T candle still forming
Calculation: Relative rate(r) = r / EMA(r[1], shared speed-baseline lookback), evaluated on chart bars and initialized to normal speed on the first valid rate
Calculation: Scale speed(T) = the relative tick rate, relative volume rate, or their geometric mean, as selected by the speed-basis parameter
Calculation: Scale direction(T) = price-based direction or volume delta, as selected by the direction-basis parameter; for T1, price direction is (upticks minus downticks) / (upticks plus downticks), and volume delta is (buy volume minus sell volume) / total volume, with tick direction classifying volume, unchanged ticks inheriting the prior side, and valid bid/ask quotes overriding that classification at or beyond the quotes or on either side of their midpoint; for T2, T3, and T4, price direction is net change in candle opens / sum of absolute changes in candle opens over that scale's separate candle lookback, and volume delta is the sum of each open change's sign times the preceding completed candle's volume / the sum of those volumes; zero denominators yield zero
Calculation: Composite direction-speed score = sum over enabled T1, T2, T3, and T4 with valid speed and direction of that scale's separate weight times its direction times min(its speed, shared speed cap), divided by the sum of those weights; undefined if the weight sum is zero
Calculation: Concordant high-speed state(direction) = at least the minimum-scale-count parameter of enabled scales have valid speed and direction, and every enabled scale with valid speed and direction has direction matching the requested direction by more than the directional deadband parameter and speed greater than the surge-speed threshold parameter
Long: on a non-tick chart, the composite direction-speed score crosses above the velocity threshold parameter OR the concordant high-speed state for up is true and was false on the previous chart bar
Short: on a non-tick chart, the composite direction-speed score crosses below the negative of the same velocity threshold parameter OR the concordant high-speed state for down is true and was false on the previous chart bar
