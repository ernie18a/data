Calculation: Qualified swing high/low = PivotHigh/PivotLow of chart-bar highs/lows using the configured lookback on each side, filtered to the highest high/lowest low over the configured liquidity lookback ending at the pivot when that filter is enabled; the pivot is confirmed after its right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Minimum displacement size = configured ATR multiplier × ATR(chart bars, configured lookback), or configured tick count × tick size, according to the configured size mode.
Calculation: BodyTop(candle) = max(open, close).
Calculation: BodyBottom(candle) = min(open, close).
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, low < the latest confirmed qualified swing low AND close > that swing low.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, high > the latest confirmed qualified swing high AND close < that swing high.
Calculation: Bullish zone = [first-candle high, current low] for a fair value gap, or [first-candle close, current open] for a qualifying suspension block, which takes precedence over a fair value gap. For a fair value gap when body-gap absorption is enabled, lower edge = min(lower edge, first-candle BodyTop) if middle-candle BodyBottom > first-candle BodyTop; upper edge = max(upper edge, current BodyBottom) if current BodyBottom > middle-candle BodyTop.
Calculation: Bearish zone = [current high, first-candle low] for a fair value gap, or [current open, first-candle close] for a qualifying suspension block, which takes precedence over a fair value gap. For a fair value gap when body-gap absorption is enabled, upper edge = max(upper edge, first-candle BodyBottom) if middle-candle BodyTop < first-candle BodyBottom; lower edge = min(lower edge, current BodyTop) if current BodyTop < middle-candle BodyBottom.
Calculation: Candidate retention = test each tracked candidate for inversion at confirmed chart-bar close before pruning it; after a failed inversion check, discard it if its age exceeds the configured grace interval or its same-direction rank exceeds the configured active-candidate limit.
Calculation: Retained IFVG zones = newest configured number per direction, additionally dropping a zone when the optional distance gate is enabled and the nearer zone edge is farther from close than the configured ATR multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, the latest sellside sweep remains within its configured validity window AND (current low > first-candle high AND current low − first-candle high ≥ minimum displacement size OR suspension-block detection is enabled AND the first, middle, and current candles are bullish AND each later open > the preceding close AND both adjoining time gaps are within the configured continuity tolerance AND current open − first-candle close ≥ minimum displacement size) AND, when overlap skipping is enabled, the resulting zone does not strictly overlap a tracked candidate or retained IFVG zone.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, the latest buyside sweep remains within its configured validity window AND (current high < first-candle low AND first-candle low − current high ≥ minimum displacement size OR suspension-block detection is enabled AND the first, middle, and current candles are bearish AND each later open < the preceding close AND both adjoining time gaps are within the configured continuity tolerance AND first-candle close − current open ≥ minimum displacement size) AND, when overlap skipping is enabled, the resulting zone does not strictly overlap a tracked candidate or retained IFVG zone.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, a tracked bearish candidate is tested before that bar's age/cap pruning AND close > its bearish zone upper edge.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, a tracked bullish candidate is tested before that bar's age/cap pruning AND close < its bullish zone lower edge.
Calculation: T1 swing pools = highs/lows of the middle bars of three consecutive completed T1 bars when the middle high ≥ both neighboring highs or the middle low ≤ both neighboring lows; construct T1 bars from chart bars and evaluate pools on chart bars as each T1 interval completes. Remove a high pool when chart-bar high > its price and a low pool when chart-bar low < its price.
Calculation: Configured stop =, when stop management is enabled, the selected long/short level among the lowest low/highest high from the candidate's displacement bar through IFVG confirmation, the lower/upper body edge of the candle making that extreme, or the lower/upper IFVG zone edge.
Calculation: Target = nearest untaken T1 swing high strictly above a long entry or swing low strictly below a short entry when external-pool targeting is enabled and T1 is a valid higher target timeframe; otherwise, if furthest-R fallback is enabled, R targets are enabled, a stop exists, risk > 0, and at least one R multiple is configured, entry ± abs(entry − stop) × greatest configured R multiple; otherwise no target. Entry = IFVG confirmation close.
Long: For an unresolved tracked long setup created at a bullish IFVG confirmation when setup tracking is enabled and, if the break-even filter is enabled, confirmation close < the highest high from the originating buyside sweep through bearish displacement; after the confirmation bar, on a confirmed chart-bar close, (a stop exists AND low ≤ configured stop) OR (the stop condition is false AND a target exists AND ((target is a T1 pool AND high > target) OR (target is an R level AND high ≥ target))). Retain setups only within the configured setup-retention limit.
Short: For an unresolved tracked short setup created at a bearish IFVG confirmation when setup tracking is enabled and, if the break-even filter is enabled, confirmation close > the lowest low from the originating sellside sweep through bullish displacement; after the confirmation bar, on a confirmed chart-bar close, (a stop exists AND high ≥ configured stop) OR (the stop condition is false AND a target exists AND ((target is a T1 pool AND low < target) OR (target is an R level AND low ≤ target))). Retain setups only within the configured setup-retention limit.
