Calculation: Trifecta bullish state = RSI(close, Trifecta RSI lookback) > Trifecta RSI threshold AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback) is positive AND Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, flow lookback) is positive; fast lookback < slow lookback
Calculation: Trifecta bearish state = RSI(close, Trifecta RSI lookback) < Trifecta RSI threshold AND MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback) is negative AND Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, flow lookback) is negative
Calculation: MT score = bullish-vote count minus bearish-vote count: for three Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback) series ordered longest to shortest, with K and D smoothing lengths equal within each pair and descending across the pairs, add a bullish vote for each K ≥ D and a bearish vote for each K ≤ D; for Stochastic(RSI, RSI, RSI, Stoch-RSI lookback) with equal K and D smoothing lengths, use K/D (or the configured fallback ratio when D is zero) for its percentile rank over the rank lookback, adding a bullish vote when rank ≥ rank midpoint and a bearish vote when rank < rank midpoint; also add a bullish vote when the smoothed RSI-range oscillator > its lower cutoff and a bearish vote when it < its upper cutoff, where the oscillator is SMA(RSI(close, oscillator RSI lookback) − lowest(RSI, range lookback), smoothing lookback) / (epsilon + SMA(highest(RSI, range lookback) − lowest(RSI, range lookback), smoothing lookback)) × scale
Calculation: MCU score = bullish-vote count minus bearish-vote count across three groups: structure uses conversion and base lines formed as midpoints of highest high and lowest low over ordered short, medium, and long cloud lookbacks; span A is the mean of conversion and base, span B is the long-lookback midpoint, and cloud top/bottom use span A and B from the displacement lookback ago; bullish votes are close > cloud top, conversion > base, and close > close[displacement lookback ago], with bearish votes using the corresponding < comparisons. Volatility uses (ATR(MCU ATR lookback) / close) / SMA(ATR(MCU ATR lookback) / close, ATR-ratio average lookback); bullish votes are ratio > expansion cutoff AND close > previous close, ratio > stronger expansion cutoff AND close > previous close, and close > EMA(close, volatility EMA lookback), with bearish votes using the corresponding < comparisons; stronger expansion cutoff > expansion cutoff. Squeeze momentum uses Bollinger bounds SMA(close, shared channel lookback) ± standard deviation(close, shared channel lookback) × Bollinger multiplier and Keltner bounds EMA(close, shared channel lookback) ± ATR(shared channel lookback) × Keltner multiplier; squeeze is on when Bollinger bounds are strictly inside Keltner bounds, and fires when it is off after having been on within the recent-squeeze lookback. Momentum is linear regression of close minus the mean of the mean of highest high and lowest low over the momentum lookback and SMA(close, momentum lookback), using the same momentum and regression lookback; bullish votes are momentum positive, momentum positive and rising from the previous bar, and squeeze fired with positive momentum, with bearish votes using negative and falling momentum.
