Calculation: Effective volume = native volume, or configured external-symbol volume if native volume has never appeared and external volume is available; missing or negative values become zero.
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR(ATR lookback) when positive, otherwise close × fallback fraction.
Calculation: Shock memory = prior memory × exp(−decay) + 1 on a true-range shock, otherwise prior memory × exp(−decay); a shock is true range above its moving average × shock multiple.
Calculation: Regime = CHAOS when shock-memory percentile rank reaches its threshold and a shock occurred within the true-range average lookback; otherwise STRONG TREND when ADX and Efficiency Ratio clear their strong floors and ADX is rising, or the prior regime was STRONG TREND and both clear their hysteresis floors; otherwise TREND when ADX and Efficiency Ratio clear their trend floors; otherwise MEAN REVERSION when Variance Ratio is unavailable or below 1, ADX is below its trend floor, and Efficiency Ratio is below its reversion cutoff; otherwise NEUTRAL. Strong-trend classification takes precedence over TREND.
Calculation: Kill state = CHAOS, or an intraday chart bar in either configured exchange-time exclusion window, or ATR divided by its moving average exceeding the volatility-spike threshold, or ADX change over its configured lag exceeding the jump threshold. Extreme volatility = ATR percentile rank over its lookback reaches its threshold.
Calculation: Major levels = VWAP of hlc3 reset each T1 session, T2 period, and T3 period; rolling volume-profile POC, VAH, and VAL from chart bars using configured lookback, bins, and value-area fraction; and high/low of the last completed T1 and T2 bars. For the profile, assign each bar's volume to the bin containing hlc3, select the maximum-volume bin as POC, then expand the value area from POC toward the adjacent higher-volume bin until its volume target is reached; ties expand upward.
Calculation: Swing trend anchored VWAP(L) = VWAP(high) reset at confirmed rolling pivot highs or VWAP(low) reset at confirmed rolling pivot lows; use the low-anchored VWAP while the rolling swing state is bullish and the high-anchored VWAP while bearish. The state turns bullish when high equals its rolling highest high and bearish when low equals its rolling lowest low; if both occur on one bar, bearish takes precedence.
Calculation: Swing flip = a change in the short-lookback swing state from bearish to bullish or bullish to bearish. Confirm a pivot high when the prior bar was the rolling highest high and the current high is lower; confirm a pivot low when the prior bar was the rolling lowest low and the current low is higher. The flip pivot is the latest confirmed low for a bullish flip or high for a bearish flip.
Calculation: Level proximity = absolute distance from close to a level below proximity parameter × Safe ATR. Level grade, taking the highest applicable grade, is A+ for T3 VWAP, POC, or long-lookback swing VWAP; A for VAH, VAL, or T2 VWAP; B for medium-lookback swing VWAP or completed T1 high/low; C for T1-session VWAP.
Calculation: Flip grade = parameterized score bands and weights applied to the highest nearby level grade, pivot-leg extension from T1-session VWAP measured in Safe ATR, effective-volume/moving-average ratio, same-direction flips within the recency window on either configured lower timeframe, and pivot-leg range per bar divided by Safe ATR; cap the velocity input at its configured cap and map the score through tier cutoffs to S/A/B/C/D. A bullish extension is (T1-session VWAP at the pivot − pivot low)/Safe ATR; a bearish extension is (pivot high − T1-session VWAP at the pivot)/Safe ATR.
Calculation: Signed flow = sum over available lower-timeframe bars of volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1), using zero for zero-range bars; when lower-timeframe flow is disabled or unavailable, use effective volume × (2 × clamp(NormalCDF(change(close)/stdev(change(close), fallback lookback)), 0, 1) − 1). Flow direction is positive when cumulative signed flow is at least its EMA and negative otherwise. Toxicity = SMA(abs(buy volume − sell volume), toxicity lookback) / SMA(buy volume + sell volume, toxicity lookback), where buy volume is effective volume × clamp(0.5 + 0.5 × signed flow/effective volume, 0, 1) and sell volume is the remainder.
Calculation: MTF flow z-score on each rung = clamp((d − median(d, lookback))/(1.4826 × MAD(d, lookback)), −4, 4), where d = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close))/(high − low), or zero for zero-range bars; use volume of one when native volume is missing or zero. Classify each rung positive above the neutral threshold, negative below its negative, otherwise neutral; MTF alignment is the sum for the chart rung and configured T6/T7 rungs. MTF opposition is alignment at or below the negative opposition threshold for longs, or at or above it for shorts. T6/T7 values come from chart-bar requests with lookahead off: historical bars use the last completed requested bar and realtime bars may use the forming bar.
Calculation: HTF bias on each of T4 and T5 = +1 when close > EMA and EMA ≥ its prior requested-timeframe value, −1 when close < EMA and EMA ≤ its prior value, otherwise 0; HTF sum is their sum, or zero when HTF bias is disabled. Values come from chart-bar requests with lookahead off: historical bars use the last completed requested bar and realtime bars may use the forming bar; T5 is longer than T4.
Calculation: Price-leading state = negative smoothed Lévy area when its absolute-value percentile rank reaches the configured threshold after warmup. Lévy area over its lookback is the sum of prior cumulative standardized flow × current standardized price change minus prior cumulative standardized price change × current standardized flow, divided by the lookback; standardize signed flow and price change by their lookback standard deviations and smooth the area with its configured EMA.
Calculation: Pivot length = configured manual length, or the rounded target-structure duration divided by chart-bar duration, bounded by configured limits. Build upper and lower convex-hull edges from the most recent configured count of confirmed pivot highs and lows; valid upper edges slope down and lower edges slope up within configured ATR-per-bar slope bounds. Edge touch count is the number of retained pivots within touch tolerance × Safe ATR of the edge.
Calculation: Hull break = first confirmed chart-bar close beyond a valid projected upper edge plus break buffer × Safe ATR, or lower edge minus that buffer, with bar range at least displacement × Safe ATR, body/range at least its threshold, and close position on the break side at least the anti-wick threshold; for a lower-edge break use (high − close)/range. Reset the one-break-per-edge state when that edge changes.
Calculation: Effective continuation event = raw upper-edge break long and lower-edge break short in chase mode; in fade mode, an upper break becomes short when, after at least one and no more than the configured fade-window bars, close falls below its saved break line minus break buffer × Safe ATR, and a lower break becomes long when close rises above its saved line plus that buffer. Track the highest high after an upper break and lowest low after a lower break as the respective failure extremes; consume each failed break after it fires.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = max(pivot length plus its configured offset, minimum signal gap) chart bars; after each confirmed directional signal, suppress further directional signals until this gap has elapsed. A continuation is also suppressed within this gap after an opposite-direction swing flip.
Calculation: Optional mode mute = when edge gating is enabled and the rolling outcome count reaches max(minimum samples, half the rolling window), mute a mode below its lower win-rate threshold and unmute it at or above its upper threshold. Count simulated target-before-stop outcomes as wins, stop-before-target as losses, both hit on the same bar as wins, and timeouts as neither; exclude timeouts from the rolling win rate.
Calculation: Conformal favorable/adverse widths = the configured quantiles of recent forward-horizon favorable/adverse excursions, each measured from its origin close and normalized by Safe ATR, multiplied by current Safe ATR; available only when conformal exits are enabled and the required sample count is reached.
Calculation: Price volatility width = ATR at the configured volatility lookback, or close × square root of recursively EMA-smoothed squared log returns at that lookback, as selected. When regime-asymmetric widths are enabled, use separate configured target and stop multipliers for each regime; otherwise both multipliers are one.
Calculation: Latched stop = for flips, flip pivot minus/plus the configured ATR offset, replaced by conformal adverse width from the pivot when available; for failed-break continuations, the failure extreme; for chase continuations, the nearer protective value of the corresponding hull edge and close minus/plus stop multiple × regime stop multiplier × price volatility width, falling back to that close-based value if no edge exists. Other missing stops use close minus/plus conformal adverse width when available, otherwise the close-based stop width.
Calculation: Latched TP2 = for flips, close plus/minus profit multiple × regime target multiplier × price volatility width × flip target fraction, or conformal favorable width × flip target fraction when available, then multiplied by the TP2 multiplier; for chase continuations use the same formula without the flip fraction. For failed-break continuations with an opposite hull edge, use close plus/minus the distance to that edge × TP2 multiplier. If target snapping is enabled, replace TP2 with the nearest POC, VAH, VAL, T3 VWAP, T2 VWAP, long- or medium-lookback swing VWAP, or completed T1/T2 high/low within snap tolerance × Safe ATR; equal-distance ties keep the first level in that order.
Calculation: Flip candidate = enabled bullish/bearish swing flip with extension at least its minimum and grade meeting its minimum (OFF suppresses); it must be near any major level or have grade S/A, and must not be in the kill state. Suppress flips in STRONG TREND; when flow veto is enabled, also suppress a flip only if toxicity reaches its threshold and flow opposes its side; suppress when the enabled MTF gate opposes its side or its mode is muted by enabled edge gating.
Calculation: Continuation candidate = enabled effective continuation event with edge touch count at least the minimum strength, mode not muted by enabled edge gating, and no MTF opposition. Fade mode requires regime other than STRONG TREND and bypasses HTF and price-leading filters; chase mode requires STRONG TREND or TREND, and when HTF agreement is required its HTF sum must have the trade's sign. Suppress continuation in CHAOS, extreme volatility, or toxic flow; in chase mode also suppress when the enabled price-leading filter detects price leading. A long continuation requires no recent bearish swing flip; a short continuation requires no recent bullish swing flip.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND signal cooldown clear AND (bullish flip candidate OR (no flip candidate on either side AND bullish continuation candidate)).
Short: confirmed chart bar AND signal cooldown clear AND (bearish flip candidate OR (no flip candidate on either side AND bearish continuation candidate)).
Exit: Long, confirmed chart bar while a long signal is latched AND (low ≤ its latched stop OR high ≥ its latched TP2).
Exit: Short, confirmed chart bar while a short signal is latched AND (high ≥ its latched stop OR low ≤ its latched TP2).
