Calculation: Alternating swing levels = On chart bars when T1 advances, use the last completed T1 bar; set swing state to high when high[lookback] > highest(high, lookback, T1, last completed bars), else to low when low[lookback] < lowest(low, lookback, T1, last completed bars); save the candidate price only when the state changes, testing high first. Both sides use the same lookback.
Calculation: Bullish OB bounds = The wick or body range, according to the body-selection parameter, of the most recent T1 candle with the lowest low or body low strictly between the saved swing high and the break bar; ties select the most recent candle.
Calculation: Bearish OB bounds = The wick or body range, according to the body-selection parameter, of the most recent T1 candle with the highest high or body high strictly between the saved swing low and the break bar; ties select the most recent candle.
Long: On a chart bar when T1 advances, the last completed T1 close > the latest saved swing high, and that swing has not previously been crossed; mark a bullish OB using Bullish OB bounds.
Short: On a chart bar when T1 advances, the last completed T1 close < the latest saved swing low, and that swing has not previously been crossed; mark a bearish OB using Bearish OB bounds.
Long: On a chart bar when T1 advances, max(open, close) of the last completed T1 bar > the upper bound of an unbroken bearish OB; mark a bullish breaker.
Short: On a chart bar when T1 advances, min(open, close) of the last completed T1 bar < the lower bound of an unbroken bullish OB; mark a bearish breaker.
