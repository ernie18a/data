Calculation: entry price = on the fill bar, min(open, EMA(close, pullback lookback) - entry-depth parameter × ATR(ATR lookback)) for a long, or max(open, EMA(close, pullback lookback) + entry-depth parameter × ATR(ATR lookback)) for a short

Calculation: trade risk = min(max(abs(entry price - structural stop), ATR(ATR lookback) on the fill bar × minimum-risk parameter), ATR(ATR lookback) on the fill bar × maximum-risk parameter)
Calculation: long stop = entry price - trade risk
Calculation: short stop = entry price + trade risk
Calculation: long TP3 = entry price + trade risk × third-target R parameter
Calculation: short TP3 = entry price - trade risk × third-target R parameter
