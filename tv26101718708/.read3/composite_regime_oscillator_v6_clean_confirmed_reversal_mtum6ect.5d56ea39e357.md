Calculation: Scaled score(x, lower bound, upper bound) = midpoint percentage score if upper bound equals lower bound; otherwise percentage-scaled clamp of (x - lower bound) / (upper bound - lower bound) to the unit interval
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR(fast ATR lookback) / ATR(slow ATR lookback), undefined when the slow ATR is zero
Calculation: High drawdown = (highest(high, drawdown lookback) - close) / highest(high, drawdown lookback), undefined when the highest high is zero
Calculation: Low rebound = (close - lowest(low, range lookback)) / lowest(low, range lookback), undefined when the lowest low is zero
Calculation: Baseline EMA distance in ATR units = (close - EMA(close, baseline EMA lookback)) / ATR(fast ATR lookback), undefined when the ATR is zero
Calculation: Range location = (close - lowest(low, range lookback)) / (highest(high, range lookback) - lowest(low, range lookback)), or midpoint when the range is zero
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), undefined when the volume average is zero
Calculation: Distance from range low = (close - lowest(low, range lookback)) / lowest(low, range lookback), undefined when the lowest low is zero
Calculation: Distance from range high = (highest(high, range lookback) - close) / highest(high, range lookback), undefined when the highest high is zero
Calculation: Regime stress = midpoint percentage score if ATR ratio, high drawdown, or low rebound is undefined; otherwise SMA(regime ATR weight * Scaled score(ATR ratio, ATR-ratio lower bound, ATR-ratio upper bound) + regime drawdown weight * Scaled score(high drawdown, drawdown lower bound, drawdown upper bound) + regime rebound weight * Scaled score(low rebound, rebound lower bound, rebound upper bound), score smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Price location = midpoint percentage score if distance from range low or distance from range high is undefined; otherwise SMA(location range weight * Scaled score(range location, location lower bound, location upper bound) + location low-distance weight * Scaled score(distance from range low, low-distance lower bound, low-distance upper bound) + location high-distance weight * complement of Scaled score(distance from range high, high-distance lower bound, high-distance upper bound), score smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Long overreaction = midpoint percentage score if RSI is undefined; otherwise SMA(long RSI weight * Scaled score(RSI complement, long RSI lower bound, long RSI upper bound) + long distance weight * Scaled score(negative baseline EMA distance in ATR units, distance lower bound, distance upper bound), score smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Short overreaction = midpoint percentage score if RSI is undefined; otherwise SMA(short RSI weight * Scaled score(RSI(close, RSI lookback), short RSI lower bound, short RSI upper bound) + short distance weight * Scaled score(baseline EMA distance in ATR units, distance lower bound, distance upper bound), score smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Pressure = midpoint percentage score if relative volume is undefined; otherwise SMA(pressure volume weight * Scaled score(relative volume, volume lower bound, volume upper bound) + pressure ATR weight * Scaled score(ATR ratio, ATR-ratio lower bound, ATR-ratio upper bound), score smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Long exhaustion score = EMA(long location-complement weight * complement of price location + long overreaction weight * long overreaction + long regime weight * regime stress + long pressure weight * pressure, exhaustion smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Short exhaustion score = EMA(short location weight * price location + short overreaction weight * short overreaction + short regime-complement weight * complement of regime stress + short pressure weight * pressure, exhaustion smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Strong uptrend = close > EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow trend EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback) > EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback)[EMA slope lookback]
Calculation: Strong downtrend = close < EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback) < EMA(close, slow trend EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback) < EMA(close, fast trend EMA lookback)[EMA slope lookback]
Calculation: Strong trend = ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback as smoothing length) > strong-trend threshold
Calculation: Long reversal blocked = strong downtrend AND strong trend AND ((ADX cooling required AND NOT falling(ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback as smoothing length), ADX cooling lookback)) OR (ADX cooling not required AND rising(ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback as smoothing length), ADX cooling lookback)))
Calculation: Short reversal blocked = strong uptrend AND strong trend AND ((ADX cooling required AND NOT falling(ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback as smoothing length), ADX cooling lookback)) OR (ADX cooling not required AND rising(ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback as smoothing length), ADX cooling lookback)))
Calculation: Long base = long exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND (trigger confirmation disabled OR ((close > high[prior-bar lookback] OR (close > open AND close[prior-bar lookback] < open[prior-bar lookback]) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above oversold RSI threshold OR rising(close, price-turn lookback)) AND (EMA reclaim/loss not required OR close crosses above EMA(close, baseline EMA lookback) OR close > high[prior-bar lookback]))) AND NOT long reversal blocked
Calculation: Short base = short exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND (trigger confirmation disabled OR ((close < low[prior-bar lookback] OR (close < open AND close[prior-bar lookback] > open[prior-bar lookback]) OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below overbought RSI threshold OR falling(close, price-turn lookback)) AND (EMA reclaim/loss not required OR close crosses below EMA(close, baseline EMA lookback) OR close < low[prior-bar lookback]))) AND NOT short reversal blocked
Long: Long exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND long reversal blocked
Short: Short exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND short reversal blocked
Long: Long base AND NOT Long base[prior-bar lookback] AND no long signal in the preceding cooldown lookback bars
Short: Short base AND NOT Short base[prior-bar lookback] AND no short signal in the preceding cooldown lookback bars
