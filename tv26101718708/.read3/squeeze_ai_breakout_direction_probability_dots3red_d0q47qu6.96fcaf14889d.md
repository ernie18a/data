Calculation: Squeeze compression = the complement of Bollinger Band width divided by Keltner Channel width when Keltner width is positive, otherwise zero; Bollinger Bands = SMA(close, BB lookback) plus or minus BB multiplier times STDEV(close, BB lookback); Keltner Channel = SMA(close, KC lookback) plus or minus KC multiplier times ATR(close, KC lookback).
Calculation: Squeeze feature vector = (min(shared feature upper bound, elapsed squeeze bars / duration scale), maximum squeeze compression clipped between compression lower bound and shared feature upper bound, min(shared feature upper bound, mean per-bar volume ratio / shared volume-volatility scale), (LINREG(close, momentum lookback, current endpoint) minus LINREG(close, momentum lookback, immediately preceding regression endpoint)) / max(ATR(close, ATR lookback), positive ATR floor) clipped between momentum lower bound and shared feature upper bound, min(shared feature upper bound, ATR(close, ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(close, ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback) / shared volume-volatility scale). Per-bar volume ratio is volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) when that SMA is positive, otherwise the volume fallback ratio. During a squeeze, use elapsed bars, maximum compression, and mean volume ratio so far; outside a squeeze, the live query uses zero duration, the stored maximum compression (initially zero), and the volume fallback ratio. Live queries use current chart-bar values, including a still-forming bar; release queries use the confirmed release bar.
Calculation: KNN upward share = the fraction of upward outcomes among the nearest min(neighbor count, stored sample count) examples by Euclidean distance between squeeze feature vectors, with equal-distance examples ordered oldest first; return a tie when stored sample count is below the minimum sample count. At a qualifying squeeze release, store its feature vector and release close as the single pending example; after the outcome lookback bars, classify it upward if close is above release close and downward otherwise, append it to history, and discard the oldest examples beyond the training sample cap. A newer qualifying release replaces a pending example before it matures.
Long: (confirmed bar AND a squeeze that began with Bollinger Bands fully inside the Keltner Channel ends after at least the minimum squeeze duration AND release KNN upward share is at least half) OR (last chart bar AND stored sample count meets the minimum sample count AND live KNN upward share is at least half).
Short: (confirmed bar AND a squeeze that began with Bollinger Bands fully inside the Keltner Channel ends after at least the minimum squeeze duration AND release KNN upward share is below half) OR (last chart bar AND stored sample count meets the minimum sample count AND live KNN upward share is below half).
