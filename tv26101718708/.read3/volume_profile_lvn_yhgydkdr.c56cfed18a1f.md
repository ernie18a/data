Calculation: LVN zones = For each current chart bar, divide the selected session-reset or configured lookback window's highest high to lowest low into configured rows; allocate each positive-volume, nonzero-range bar's volume to each row in proportion to the overlap with its high-low range; retain rows whose volume exceeds the configured percentage of the maximum row volume and is strictly below every row within the configured neighborhood on both sides, where neighborhood size is the floored configured percentage of rows bounded below by the smallest positive row count; merge adjacent retained rows, expand each zone by the configured number of rows, keep up to the configured node count nearest to the current close by midpoint, and classify zones with midpoint below the close as support and the rest as resistance.
Long: (bar range intersects a support LVN zone AND close > its upper boundary) OR (close crosses above the upper boundary of a resistance LVN zone from the previous bar AND volume > 0)
Short: (bar range intersects a resistance LVN zone AND close < its lower boundary) OR (close crosses below the lower boundary of a support LVN zone from the previous bar AND volume > 0)
