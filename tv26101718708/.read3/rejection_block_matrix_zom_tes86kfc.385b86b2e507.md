Calculation: Lower-wick share = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low
Calculation: Upper-wick share = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low
Calculation: Block height = clamp(abs(source extreme - source candle body edge), ATR(ATR lookback) at the source bar * minimum block-height parameter, ATR(ATR lookback) at the source bar * maximum block-height parameter)
Calculation: Demand blocks = zones created on a confirmed pivot low whose pivot bar is swing-confirmation bars earlier and has lower-wick share >= wick-share threshold, or on a bar with low < the latest confirmed pivot low, close > that pivot low, and lower-wick share >= wick-share threshold; each zone has bottom = source low and top = source low + block height, is born on its detection bar, expires when age > maximum-age parameter, and becomes inactive when close < bottom
Calculation: Supply blocks = zones created on a confirmed pivot high whose pivot bar is swing-confirmation bars earlier and has upper-wick share >= wick-share threshold, or on a bar with high > the latest confirmed pivot high, close < that pivot high, and upper-wick share >= wick-share threshold; each zone has top = source high and bottom = source high - block height, is born on its detection bar, expires when age > maximum-age parameter, and becomes inactive when close > top
Calculation: The oldest demand or supply blocks are removed when their combined count exceeds the maximum-blocks parameter
Long: a demand block is created OR a later bar touches an active demand block (high >= its bottom AND low <= its top) and closes above its top AND close > open OR active demand block count > active supply block count OR (active demand block count = active supply block count AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback))
Short: a supply block is created OR a later bar touches an active supply block (high >= its bottom AND low <= its top) and closes below its bottom AND close < open OR active supply block count > active demand block count OR (active supply block count = active demand block count AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback))
