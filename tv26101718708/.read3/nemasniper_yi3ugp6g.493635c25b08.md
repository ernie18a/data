Calculation: Gaussian range EMA = EMA(weighted mean of (highest + lowest) / 2, where for each i from 0 to Gaussian length minus one, highest and lowest are the extrema of ALMA(100 * change(source, change lookback) / source, ALMA lookback, offset, sigma) over the current and preceding i chart bars, and weight = exp(-0.5 * ((i - (Gaussian length - 1)) / (2 * sigma))^2)); sigma is the greater of the minimum sigma and the standard deviation of close over the volatility lookback when adaptive, or the fixed sigma otherwise, using fixed sigma if the standard deviation is unavailable
Long: ALMA(100 * change(source, change lookback) / source, ALMA lookback, offset, sigma) >= Gaussian range EMA AND (RSI+CMO filter disabled OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) > its value on the prior chart bar AND CMO(close, CMO lookback) > its value on the prior chart bar)) AND (EMA filter disabled OR (close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) > its value on the prior chart bar)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR (current T1 close sampled on chart bars > EMA(close, same EMA lookback, T1) for the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar AND that EMA value > its value sampled on the prior chart bar)) AND this combined condition changed from false to true on the current chart bar AND chart bar is confirmed AND more than cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior signal or no prior signal exists AND position side is not long
