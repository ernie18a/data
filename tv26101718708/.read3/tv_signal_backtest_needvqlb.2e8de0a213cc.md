Calculation: vote(positive condition, negative condition) = -1 if the negative condition is true, otherwise +1 if the positive condition is true, otherwise 0
Calculation: moving-average rating = (sum of 14 votes + 0) / 15; each vote is +1 when chart close >= SMA(close, lookback, T1 current still-forming bar), otherwise -1, with T1 values sampled on chart bars; the 14 lookbacks comprise two separate six-lookback sets ordered shortest to longest with corresponding values equal, plus two separate lookbacks, one shorter than the shortest and one equal to the second-shortest in both sets
Calculation: oscillator rating = sum of 11 votes / 11; calculate the indicators on T1 current still-forming bars and sample their values on chart bars; [1] means the prior chart-bar sample of a T1 value. The votes are: vote(RSI below its lower threshold and rising, RSI above its upper threshold and falling), using one RSI lookback; vote(Stochastic K below its lower threshold and above Stochastic D, Stochastic K above its upper threshold and below Stochastic D), using a Stochastic lookback, a K-smoothing parameter, and a separate D lookback; vote(CCI below its lower threshold and falling, CCI above its upper threshold and rising), using a CCI lookback; vote(Awesome Oscillator above zero and rising, Awesome Oscillator below zero and falling), using separate short and long lookbacks with short < long; vote(Momentum(close, lookback) >= zero, Momentum(close, lookback) <= zero); vote(MACD line >= zero, MACD line <= zero), where MACD line = EMA(close, fast lookback) - EMA(close, slow lookback), with fast < slow; vote(StochRSI K below its lower threshold and above StochRSI D, StochRSI K above its upper threshold and below StochRSI D), sharing the RSI and Stochastic lookbacks, with StochRSI K smoothing using the Stochastic D parameter and StochRSI D smoothing using the Stochastic K-smoothing parameter; vote(Williams %R below its lower threshold and rising, Williams %R above its upper threshold and falling), using a separate lookback; vote(Ultimate Oscillator >= its upper threshold, Ultimate Oscillator <= its lower threshold), using separate short, medium, and long lookbacks ordered short < medium < long; vote(ADX >= its minimum threshold and (+DI / -DI - 1) > 0, ADX >= its minimum threshold and (+DI / -DI - 1) < 0), using separate DI lookback and ADX smoothing parameters; vote(Bears Power < zero and rising, Bulls Power > zero and falling), where Bears Power = T1 low - EMA(T1 close, lookback) and Bulls Power = T1 high - the same EMA; each upper and lower threshold pair has separate parameters
Calculation: rating = (moving-average rating + oscillator rating) / 2
