Calculation: Confirmed swings = ATR-threshold zigzag highs and lows on chart bars, confirmed after the configured swing-confirmation delay; a more extreme same-direction candidate replaces the prior candidate, and an opposite turn confirms the swing.
Calculation: SNR levels = confirmed swing highs and lows; when body levels are used, resistance is the highest close of a bullish candle followed by a bearish candle near a swing high, and support is the lowest close of a bearish candle followed by a bullish candle near a swing low; also include close-to-open gaps above the configured size floor. Keep the close-to-open band around body levels, merge same-side levels within the SNR clustering tolerance, and discard levels already closed through. A close beyond the far band edge plus the level tolerance flips a level once to the opposite side when flips are enabled; otherwise remove it.
Calculation: SNR proof = initial touch count plus qualifying later touches and misses. A touch is a chart-bar range intersecting the level band expanded by the configured ATR tolerance, after price has departed by the configured ATR distance and the touch cooldown has elapsed. A miss is a confirmed swing that turns beyond the touch tolerance but within the configured miss window from the near edge of the band, after departure and cooldown. A touch makes the level nonfresh; a miss does not.
Calculation: Storyline resistance/support = the latest confirmed PivotHigh(max(open, close), pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and PivotLow(min(open, close), pivot lookback, pivot lookback), respectively, calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars with lookahead off.
Calculation: HTF bias = EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, last completed T1 bar) for bullish bias, and the reverse for bearish bias; the shorter lookback is less than the longer lookback.
Calculation: Strictness-adjusted minimum QML score = for the loose preset, max(loose score floor, configured minimum score minus loose offset); for the strict preset, min(strict score cap, configured minimum score plus strict offset); otherwise the configured minimum score. Strictness-adjusted reaction tolerance and BOS body weight use their separately configured preset values.
Calculation: Direct sweep quality = 0 unless the head exceeds the shoulder in the reversal direction by at least ATR at the head times the minimum sweep depth; otherwise combine the capped head sweep depth divided by ATR at the head times the sweep depth scale with a reclaim score of full credit when the head closes back across the shoulder and the configured partial credit otherwise.
Calculation: Liquidity-pool sweep quality = the best quality among opposite-side pivot pools with at least the configured minimum touches, formed before the head and within the pool clustering tolerance of the shoulder, that the head wick sweeps and closes back across; combine capped sweep depth divided by ATR at the head times the pool depth scale with capped pool touches divided by the pool touch scale. Sweep quality = neutral midpoint when the sweep filter is disabled; otherwise max(weighted direct and pool sweep quality, direct sweep quality times its minimum weight).
Calculation: FVG confluence quality = neutral midpoint when its filter is disabled; otherwise the maximum over the configured recent unmitigated FVGs formed after the head and no later than the current bar, matching the setup direction and within the level tolerance of the shoulder, of weighted capped gap size divided by current ATR and the gap size scale plus weighted max(minimum freshness, one minus gap age divided by the freshness scale); zero when none match.
Calculation: BOS quality = body-or-wick mode quality times the strictness-adjusted BOS body weight, plus capped first-break depth divided by current ATR and the BOS depth scale times the BOS depth weight, plus max(minimum time quality, one minus bars from head to first break divided by the BOS timeout) times the remaining weight.
Calculation: Displacement quality = capped BOS-candle body divided by ATR at that candle, displacement scale, and displacement cap; multiply by full directional credit when the candle closes in the setup direction and the configured partial credit otherwise.
Calculation: Volume/body quality = weighted capped head volume divided by current SMA(volume, volume lookback) and head-volume scale, plus weighted capped BOS volume divided by that SMA and BOS-volume scale, plus weighted capped BOS body-to-range ratio divided by its scale.
Calculation: RSI divergence quality = for a bullish setup, capped RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the head minus RSI at the shoulder divided by the divergence scale when the head low is below the shoulder and head RSI is higher; for a bearish setup, use the shoulder RSI minus head RSI when the head high is above the shoulder and head RSI is lower; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Regime multiplier = neutral when regime filtering is disabled; otherwise use the ranging multiplier when ADX from DMI(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) is below its threshold, else the volatile multiplier when ATR(close, ATR lookback)/close divided by its SMA over the regime lookback exceeds the high threshold, else the compressed multiplier when that ratio is below the low threshold, else one.
Calculation: HTF bias quality = neutral midpoint when the HTF bias filter is disabled or off; otherwise full credit when the bias matches the setup direction and zero otherwise.
Calculation: QML score = round(min(score cap, regime multiplier times the sum of sweep weight times sweep quality, BOS weight times BOS quality, FVG weight times FVG confluence quality, displacement weight times displacement quality, volume weight times volume/body quality, divergence weight times RSI divergence quality, and HTF weight times HTF bias quality)).
Calculation: Bullish accepted QML = the last five confirmed swings in chronological order are low, high, low shoulder, high neck, low head; head is below shoulder; when reversal context is required, the high before the shoulder is above the neck; structure height from that earlier high to the head is at least ATR at the head times the minimum structure height; the first subsequent break above neck plus ATR at the break times the minimum BOS distance occurs within the BOS timeout, using close when body-close BOS is required and otherwise the high; no intervening confirmed low is below the shoulder and no intervening confirmed high is above the neck; current close is above the shoulder; QML score meets the strictness-adjusted minimum; the shoulder is not a recently used same-side level when freshness filtering is enabled; and no active bullish QML has a level within the duplicate-level tolerance.
Calculation: Bearish accepted QML = the last five confirmed swings in chronological order are high, low, high shoulder, low neck, high head; head is above shoulder; when reversal context is required, the low before the shoulder is below the neck; structure height from the earlier low to the head is at least ATR at the head times the minimum structure height; the first subsequent break below neck minus ATR at the break times the minimum BOS distance occurs within the BOS timeout, using close when body-close BOS is required and otherwise the low; no intervening confirmed high is above the shoulder and no intervening confirmed low is below the neck; current close is below the shoulder; QML score meets the strictness-adjusted minimum; the shoulder is not a recently used same-side level when freshness filtering is enabled; and no active bearish QML has a level within the duplicate-level tolerance.
Calculation: Live QML side and freshness = an accepted QML keeps its setup side until, more than two chart bars after its BOS, a close crosses the QML level by the configured level tolerance against that side, which flips it once. A QML remains fresh until retested or invalidated; remove it from fresh status when its configured age or ATR-distance limit is exceeded, with zero disabling that limit.
Calculation: Reaction(isBull, level, optional zone bounds) = range > 0 and the configured reaction mode holds: Touch Only requires low at or below the bullish zone top (or level) plus ATR times level tolerance times the strictness-adjusted reaction tolerance, or high at or above the bearish zone bottom (or level) minus that tolerance; Wick Rejection requires the bullish lower-wick-to-range ratio or bearish upper-wick-to-range ratio to meet the configured wick ratio times reaction tolerance and close on the correct side of level; Close Reclaim requires close beyond level by ATR times the configured reclaim distance times reaction tolerance, open and close on the correct side, and either a correctly directed candle or the configured opposing-wick comparison; Displacement Confirmation requires a correctly directed candle closing on the correct side, body at least ATR times the configured minimum body size times the displacement multiplier times reaction tolerance, and body-to-range ratio at least the configured minimum.
Calculation: Active trendline values = projected values on chart bars of the latest active descending resistance line joining consecutive confirmed pivot highs or ascending support line joining consecutive confirmed pivot lows. Pivot sources are max(open, close) or high for highs and min(open, close) or low for lows, as configured; a resistance line requires descending anchors and the prior close above the newer anchor, and a support line requires ascending anchors and the newer anchor above the prior close.
Long: A higher-timeframe support rejection occurs when chart-bar low is at or below the latest T2 support plus ATR times rejection tolerance and close is above support plus that tolerance times the configured reclaim fraction; while that rejection is pending, chart close above the latest confirmed swing high confirms and locks the bullish storyline.
Short: A higher-timeframe resistance rejection occurs when chart-bar high is at or above the latest T2 resistance minus ATR times rejection tolerance and close is below resistance minus that tolerance times the configured reclaim fraction; while that rejection is pending, chart close below the latest confirmed swing low confirms and locks the bearish storyline.
Long: A bullish live QML side is fresh and not stale.
Short: A bearish live QML side is fresh and not stale.
Long: A fresh bullish live QML side is not stale, more than two chart bars have elapsed after its BOS, the current range intersects the QML level expanded by the configured ATR retest tolerance, and Reaction(true, QML level, QML zone bounds) holds.
Short: A fresh bearish live QML side is not stale, more than two chart bars have elapsed after its BOS, the current range intersects the QML level expanded by the configured ATR retest tolerance, and Reaction(false, QML level, QML zone bounds) holds.
Long: A confirmed chart bar satisfies Reaction(true, support level, no zone bounds) within the configured reaction window after a qualifying touch of that level, SNR proof meets the reaction minimum, and the bullish signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: A confirmed chart bar satisfies Reaction(false, resistance level, no zone bounds) within the configured reaction window after a qualifying touch of that level, SNR proof meets the reaction minimum, and the bearish signal cooldown has elapsed.
Long: A fresh, nonstale bullish live QML has a newly confirmed swing low after its BOS, with the swing low above the QML level and no higher than QML level plus the fraction of the distance from QML level to BOS level specified by the shoulder-depth parameter plus SNR clustering tolerance; a support within SNR clustering tolerance of that swing low has proof at least the shoulder SNR minimum.
Short: A fresh, nonstale bearish live QML has a newly confirmed swing high after its BOS, with the swing high below the QML level and no lower than QML level minus the fraction of the distance from QML level to BOS level specified by the shoulder-depth parameter minus SNR clustering tolerance; a resistance within SNR clustering tolerance of that swing high has proof at least the shoulder SNR minimum.
Long: A fresh bullish QML level is within ATR times the confluence-distance parameter of the active lower trendline and the current range intersects the QML level expanded by that tolerance, OR a fresh bullish SNR support with proof at least the trendline-SNR minimum is within that tolerance of the active lower trendline and the current range intersects the support expanded by that tolerance.
Short: A fresh bearish QML level is within ATR times the confluence-distance parameter of the active upper trendline and the current range intersects the QML level expanded by that tolerance, OR a fresh bearish SNR resistance with proof at least the trendline-SNR minimum is within that tolerance of the active upper trendline and the current range intersects the resistance expanded by that tolerance.
Long: The current candle is bullish, the previous candle is bearish, the configured body-engulf or full-wick-engulf condition holds, current body size is at least ATR times the minimum engulfing size, and current body size exceeds previous body size.
Short: The current candle is bearish, the previous candle is bullish, the configured body-engulf or full-wick-engulf condition holds, current body size is at least ATR times the minimum engulfing size, and current body size exceeds previous body size.
Long: A bearish engulfing zone, bounded by the previous candle body or full range as configured, closes above its upper bound more than one bar after formation, failed zones are retained, and the zone has not failed before.
Short: A bullish engulfing zone, bounded by the previous candle body or full range as configured, closes below its lower bound more than one bar after formation, failed zones are retained, and the zone has not failed before.
