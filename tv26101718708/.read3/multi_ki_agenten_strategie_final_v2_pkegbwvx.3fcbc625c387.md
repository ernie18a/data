Long: ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) < volatility-ratio threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, middle lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND count of bullish votes >= minimum agent votes AND count of bearish votes = 0; bullish votes are EMA ordering bullish, RSI(close, RSI lookback) > upper RSI threshold, close > close[1] AND volume_active, close > highest(high, structure lookback)[1], and close <= lowest(low, demand lookback) + ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) * demand-zone ATR fraction; bearish votes are EMA ordering bearish, RSI(close, RSI lookback) < lower RSI threshold, close < close[1] AND volume_active, close < lowest(low, structure lookback)[1], and close >= highest(high, supply lookback) - ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) * supply-zone ATR fraction. EMA lookbacks are ordered shorter < middle < longer; structure, demand, and supply lookbacks are separate parameters.
Short: ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(close, high, low, ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) < volatility-ratio threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, middle lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND count of bearish votes >= minimum agent votes AND count of bullish votes = 0; bullish and bearish votes are as defined in the Long rule.
