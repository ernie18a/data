Calculation: OpeningRangeHigh = highest chart-bar high during the configured opening session; reset each T1 day and make available after the session ends.
Calculation: LongTradeCount = 0 during the opening session or at T1 day end; otherwise prior count + 1 when flat and ORB is ready and open < OpeningRangeHigh and high > OpeningRangeHigh and R1 > OpeningRangeHigh; otherwise prior count; initialize at 0.
Calculation: HalfPivotLevels = R0.5 = OpeningRangeHigh + abs(R1 - OpeningRangeHigh) / 2; R1.5 = R1 + abs(R2 - R1) / 2; R2.5 = R2 + abs(R3 - R2) / 2; R3.5 = R3 + abs(R4 - R3) / 2; R4.5 = R4 + abs(R5 - R4) / 2.
Calculation: InitialStop = min(entry price, entry price * (1 - (stop-loss percentage > 0 ? stop-loss percentage / 100 : 99999)) in percentage mode, or the low of the signal bar's prior chart bar in previous-low mode.
Calculation: TrailingStop = 0 on entry or while flat; while long, let p be the prior trailing stop and choose the first matching value in this order: R4 if high > R5 and R4 > p; R3.5 if high > R4.5 and R3.5 > p; R3 if high > R4 and R3 > p; R2.5 if high > R3.5 and R2.5 > p; R2 if high > R3 and R2 > p; R1.5 if high > R2.5 and R1.5 > p; R1 if high > R2 and R1 > p; R0.5 if high > R1.5 and R0.5 > p; OpeningRangeHigh if high > R0.5 and OpeningRangeHigh > p; otherwise p.
Long: ORB is ready for the current T1 day AND open < OpeningRangeHigh AND high > OpeningRangeHigh AND Traditional Pivot Points R1(high, low, close, T1, last completed bar) > OpeningRangeHigh AND flat AND LongTradeCount[1] < max trades per day AND time > backtest start time AND time < backtest end time.
Exit: long, low <= max(InitialStop, TrailingStop) OR the chart bar closes at the close time of the still-forming T1 bar.
