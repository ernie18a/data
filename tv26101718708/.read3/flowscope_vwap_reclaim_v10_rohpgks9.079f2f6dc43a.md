Calculation: VWAP zone width = min(max(ATR(ATR lookback) × zone multiplier, minimum zone width), maximum zone width)
Calculation: VWAP cross count = rolling sum over cross-count lookback of 1 on active-session bars where close crosses session VWAP, otherwise 0
Long: During the active session, close crosses above session VWAP
Short: During the active session, close crosses below session VWAP
Long: During the active session, since the latest bullish cross, no close has been below session VWAP and no earlier long trigger has occurred, either the current bar is within the retest timeout or a retest was already recorded, the lowest close over the hold lookback was above session VWAP on at least one bar since that cross, low <= session VWAP + VWAP zone width, high >= session VWAP - VWAP zone width, close > session VWAP, and close > open
Short: During the active session, since the latest bearish cross, no close has been above session VWAP and no earlier short trigger has occurred, either the current bar is within the retest timeout or a retest was already recorded, the highest close over the hold lookback was below session VWAP on at least one bar since that cross, high >= session VWAP - VWAP zone width, low <= session VWAP + VWAP zone width, close < session VWAP, and close < open
Long: During the active session, a bullish retest high is stored from a prior bar and current high > its stored value on the preceding bar; the value is set or updated by each bullish retest confirmation, cleared by a new bullish cross, a close below session VWAP while its setup is active, or a prior long trigger, and retained through bearish crosses; the chop filter is disabled or VWAP cross count < chop threshold; the volume filter is disabled or Relative Volume(volume, volume lookback) >= elevated-volume threshold
Short: During the active session, a bearish retest low is stored from a prior bar and current low < its stored value on the preceding bar; the value is set or updated by each bearish retest confirmation, cleared by a new bearish cross, a close above session VWAP while its setup is active, or a prior short trigger, and retained through bullish crosses; the chop filter is disabled or VWAP cross count < chop threshold; the volume filter is disabled or Relative Volume(volume, volume lookback) >= elevated-volume threshold
