Calculation: Intrabar buy fraction = for each subbar, use 1 when its close is at or above ask, 0 when at or below bid, and linear interpolation between them when valid quotes are used; otherwise use close location within range, body direction (1 for up, 0 for down, one-half for flat), or their weighted blend according to the classification mode, with the blend weight as a parameter. Clamp to [0,1].
Calculation: Intrabar profile = on each chart bar, aggregate its completed subbars from lower timeframe T1, using T2 if T1 returns none. Divide the chart bar's range into the configured number of equal rows. Allocate each subbar's volume to intersected rows in proportion to overlap divided by the greater of its range and minimum tick, assigning all volume to its row when it intersects only one. Row buy volume is allocated volume times intrabar buy fraction; row sell volume is allocated volume times one minus that fraction. Bar volume is the sum of subbar volumes; bar delta is the sum of subbar volume times (twice the buy fraction minus one).
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of bar delta on chart bars, initialized at zero.
Calculation: Profile features = volume POC is the center of the lowest row with maximum volume; POC location is its row-center position divided by row count. Shape is B when the first and last rows at or above a parameter fraction of maximum row volume are separated by at least a parameter number of rows and at least a parameter number of intervening rows are below a parameter fraction of maximum row volume. Otherwise shape is P when POC location is at or above an upper threshold, b when at or below a lower threshold, and D otherwise. Upper excess is clamp(1 minus the mean volume of the top two rows divided by mean row volume, 0, 1); lower excess uses the bottom two rows.
Calculation: Longest diagonal imbalance run = a buy imbalance at row i occurs when buy volume at i is positive and greater than sell volume at i-1 times the imbalance ratio; a sell imbalance at i occurs when sell volume at i is positive and greater than buy volume at i+1 times the imbalance ratio. Count consecutive same-side imbalances from the lowest row upward; retain the longest run and its side and ending row, keeping the first run on ties and prioritizing buy when both sides qualify at one row.
Calculation: Relative volume = bar volume divided by SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), using bar volume when the average is unavailable. Effort without result = clamp((relative volume minus 1) divided by effort-volume scale, 0, 1) times clamp(1 minus absolute(close-open) divided by bar range, 0, 1). Delta dominance = absolute(bar delta) divided by bar volume.
Calculation: Long fade score = clamp(long-wick weight × clamp((lower wick / bar range) / wick scale, 0, 1) + effort weight × effort without result + trap weight × (clamp(delta dominance / delta scale, 0, 1) if bar delta is negative and close is above the bar midpoint, else 0) + excess weight × lower excess + shape weight × (clamp((midpoint of POC location minus POC location) / POC-shape scale, 0, 1) if shape is b, else 0) + divergence weight × (1 if current CVD is above the lowest CVD in the preceding divergence lookback, else 0) + imbalance weight × (clamp(longest buy-run length / run-length scale, 0, 1) if its ending row is in the configured lower region, else 0), 0, score cap). Each weight, scale, and region threshold is a parameter.
Calculation: Short fade score = clamp(short-wick weight × clamp((upper wick / bar range) / wick scale, 0, 1) + effort weight × effort without result + trap weight × (clamp(delta dominance / delta scale, 0, 1) if bar delta is positive and close is below the bar midpoint, else 0) + excess weight × upper excess + shape weight × (clamp((POC location minus midpoint of POC location) / POC-shape scale, 0, 1) if shape is P, else 0) + divergence weight × (1 if current CVD is below the highest CVD in the preceding divergence lookback, else 0) + imbalance weight × (clamp(longest sell-run length / run-length scale, 0, 1) if its ending row is in the configured upper region, else 0), 0, score cap). Each weight, scale, and region threshold is a parameter.
Long: On a profile row i above the lowest row, buy volume at i > sell volume at i-1 × imbalance ratio.
Short: On a profile row i below the highest row, sell volume at i > buy volume at i+1 × imbalance ratio.
Long: On a completed chart bar with at least one subbar, high > low, and positive bar volume: low < lowest(low, extreme lookback) on the preceding chart bar AND (mean-extension filter disabled OR low <= EMA(close, mean lookback) - extension multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback)) AND lower wick >= upper wick AND NOT (high > highest(high, extreme lookback) on the preceding chart bar AND (mean-extension filter disabled OR high >= EMA(close, mean lookback) + extension multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback)) AND upper wick >= lower wick) AND long fade score >= minimum score AND shape != B AND no long or short signal within the cooldown lookback chart bars.
Short: On a completed chart bar with at least one subbar, high > low, and positive bar volume: high > highest(high, extreme lookback) on the preceding chart bar AND (mean-extension filter disabled OR high >= EMA(close, mean lookback) + extension multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback)) AND upper wick >= lower wick AND short fade score >= minimum score AND shape != B AND no long or short signal within the cooldown lookback chart bars.
