Calculation: Trend strength on chart bars = clamp(score minimum, score maximum, score midpoint + clamp((EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) - EMA(close, longer trend lookback)) / ATR(volatility lookback), -normalization cap, normalization cap) * trend weight + (RSI(close, momentum lookback) - score midpoint) * momentum weight), with the longer trend lookback greater than the shorter trend lookback
Calculation: Initial risk = if the swing stop anchor is selected and the distance to the opposite range extreme is less than ATR(volatility lookback) * swing distance limit, that distance + ATR(volatility lookback) * swing stop buffer; otherwise ATR(volatility lookback) * stop multiplier; the distance is entry - range low for longs and range high - entry for shorts
Calculation: Long stop = entry - initial risk at entry; on each later confirmed chart bar, raise the prior stop to the greater of itself and high - ATR(volatility lookback) * trail distance when trailing mode is enabled or trailing after break-even is enabled and break-even was armed before that bar; then raise it to at least entry when the break-even trigger or first partial target is reached
Calculation: Short stop = entry + initial risk at entry; on each later confirmed chart bar, lower the prior stop to the lesser of itself and low + ATR(volatility lookback) * trail distance when trailing mode is enabled or trailing after break-even is enabled and break-even was armed before that bar; then lower it to at most entry when the break-even trigger or first partial target is reached
Calculation: Qualifying loss = a stop exit before the partial target when break-even is not armed and stop profit is no greater than the minimum winning stop-profit distance; stop profit is stop - entry for longs and entry - stop for shorts
Long: confirmed chart bar AND flat after trade management AND latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) has not triggered a long AND latest confirmed PivotHigh and PivotLow exist AND range high - range low >= ATR(volatility lookback) * minimum range height AND bar index - range high bar <= maximum range age AND close > range high + ATR(volatility lookback) * breakout buffer AND trend strength >= score midpoint AND trend strength >= long strength threshold AND close <= EMA(high, ribbon lookback) + ATR(volatility lookback) * maximum overextension AND (MTF agreement disabled OR the same trend-strength calculation on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars from the T1 bar still forming, >= score midpoint) AND (cooldown disabled OR latest qualifying loss was not long OR bars since that loss >= loss cooldown)
Short: confirmed chart bar AND flat after trade management AND latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) has not triggered a short AND latest confirmed PivotHigh and PivotLow exist AND range high - range low >= ATR(volatility lookback) * minimum range height AND bar index - range low bar <= maximum range age AND close < range low - ATR(volatility lookback) * breakout buffer AND trend strength < score midpoint AND 100 - trend strength >= short strength threshold AND close >= EMA(low, ribbon lookback) - ATR(volatility lookback) * maximum overextension AND (MTF agreement disabled OR the same trend-strength calculation on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars from the T1 bar still forming, < score midpoint) AND (cooldown disabled OR latest qualifying loss was not short OR bars since that loss >= loss cooldown)
Exit: Long, later confirmed chart bar AND low <= long stop
Exit: Long, later confirmed chart bar AND partial target enabled AND partial target not previously reached AND high >= entry + initial risk * partial target multiple
Exit: Long, later confirmed chart bar AND fixed-target mode AND low > long stop AND high >= entry + initial risk * full target multiple
Exit: Short, later confirmed chart bar AND high >= short stop
Exit: Short, later confirmed chart bar AND partial target enabled AND partial target not previously reached AND low <= entry - initial risk * partial target multiple
Exit: Short, later confirmed chart bar AND fixed-target mode AND high < short stop AND low <= entry - initial risk * full target multiple
