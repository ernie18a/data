Calculation: Price percentile = 100 × percentile rank of the oldest close among the up-to-percentile-lookback closes, ranked against those closes.
Calculation: TP factor = average(volume factor, price factor), where volume factor is high-volume factor if volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) > high-volume threshold, else medium-volume factor if > medium-volume threshold, else low-volume factor if > low-volume threshold, else fallback volume factor; price factor is high-percentile factor if price percentile > high-percentile threshold, else medium-percentile factor if > medium-percentile threshold, else low-percentile factor if > low-percentile threshold, else fallback percentile factor.
Long: On a confirmed bar, Z-score(Bull Bear Power(high, low, EMA(close, EMA lookback)), Z-score lookback) crosses above the entry threshold.
Short: On a confirmed bar, Z-score(Bull Bear Power(high, low, EMA(close, EMA lookback)), Z-score lookback) crosses below the negative entry threshold.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar the Z-score crosses below zero OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × first target ATR multiplier × TP factor OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × second target ATR multiplier × TP factor OR high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × third target ATR multiplier × TP factor.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar the Z-score crosses above zero OR low <= entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × first target ATR multiplier × TP factor OR low <= entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × second target ATR multiplier × TP factor OR low <= entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × third target ATR multiplier × TP factor.
