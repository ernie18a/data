Calculation: Selected T1 reversal zones = On T1 bars, using the current T1 bar (still forming in real time), calculate ATR(lookback), range=max(high−low, minimum price increment), upper wick=high−max(open,close), and lower wick=min(open,close)−low. A supply seed has upper wick ≥ range × minimum wick fraction and high−close ≥ ATR × minimum reaction distance; a demand seed has lower wick ≥ range × minimum wick fraction and close−low ≥ ATR × minimum reaction distance. For each stored zone with center c and radius r, count a supply rejection when |high−c|≤r and close<c and the supply wick and reaction tests hold; count a demand rejection when |low−c|≤r and close>c and the demand tests hold. Count each zone at most once per T1 bar; if both rejection tests hold, increment both side counts and use the high as the tip when the upper wick is at least as large, otherwise the low. Update its center to the hit-count-weighted mean of its old center and tip. For each seed, choose the high for supply or low for demand; if both seed tests hold, choose the tip with the larger wick, using the high on a tie. Match the seed to the nearest stored center within max(existing radius, ATR × clustering range); on a match not already counted this bar, update its center to the hit-count-weighted mean, set its radius to min(existing radius, ATR × clustering range), and increment its hit and chosen-side counts. Otherwise create a zone with that tip, radius ATR × clustering range, and one hit. Keep at most the configured cluster capacity, discarding the oldest. From zones with hits ≥ required touches, select up to the configured output count by descending score min(hits, touch-score cap) × touch-score weight − |close−center|/max(ATR, minimum price increment); ties favor older zones. Classify a selected zone as flip if it has both supply and demand hits, supply if it has supply hits only, and demand otherwise. Use the configured T1 history window. Return T1 results to chart bars with lookahead off.

Short: a selected T1 reversal zone is classified as supply
