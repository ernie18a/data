Calculation: Composite = the arithmetic mean of EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, current possibly forming bar), EMA(close, EMA lookback, T2, current possibly forming bar), EMA(close, EMA lookback, T3, current possibly forming bar), and EMA(close, EMA lookback, T4, current possibly forming bar), each sampled on chart bars, when the aggregation mode is Simple; their weighted mean using separate weights W1, W2, W3, and W4 when Weighted; when Consensus, let each direction be 1 if its EMA value is at least its value on the prior chart bar and -1 otherwise, then use the arithmetic mean if the absolute direction sum is at least the consensus threshold, else use the weighted mean
Calculation: Brick size = ATR(ATR lookback, T5, current possibly forming bar), sampled on chart bars, multiplied by the ATR multiplier when ATR sizing is enabled; otherwise the fixed brick size
Calculation: Renko state = initialize level to Composite and direction to neutral; on each later chart bar, if Composite minus level is at most negative brick size, lower level by floor((level - Composite) / brick size) bricks and set direction short; else if Composite minus level is at least the reversal brick multiple times brick size, raise level by floor((Composite - level) / brick size) bricks and set direction long; otherwise retain level and direction
Calculation: Initial long stop = close on the first long-entry signal bar * (1 - stop-loss fraction)
Calculation: Initial short stop = close on the first short-entry signal bar * (1 + stop-loss fraction)
Long: Renko direction is long AND Renko direction on the prior chart bar is not long
Short: Renko direction is short AND Renko direction on the prior chart bar is not short
Exit: Long, close <= Initial long stop OR (trailing enabled AND the close has reached average entry price * (1 + trailing activation fraction) during this position AND close <= highest high since this position opened * (1 - trailing offset fraction)) OR (trailing disabled AND close >= average entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction))
Exit: Short, close >= Initial short stop OR (trailing enabled AND the close has reached average entry price * (1 - trailing activation fraction) during this position AND close >= lowest low since this position opened * (1 + trailing offset fraction)) OR (trailing disabled AND close <= average entry price * (1 - take-profit fraction))
