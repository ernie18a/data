Calculation: DelayedClose = close when Sum(volume, 0 through short-distance lookback) / Sum(volume, short-distance lookback + 1 through short-distance lookback + long-distance lookback) > volume-ratio threshold; otherwise its previous value
Calculation: MeanTrueRange = SMA(max(high - low, abs(high - close[1]), abs(low - close[1])), ATR lookback)
Long: chart-bar datetime >= timestamp(from year, from month, from day, 00:00) AND chart-bar datetime <= timestamp(thru year, thru month, thru day, 23:59) AND ((long trigger mode selects the first lower band AND DelayedClose crosses below EMA(close, EMA lookback) - MeanTrueRange) OR (long trigger mode selects the second lower band AND DelayedClose crosses below EMA(close, EMA lookback) - MeanTrueRange × first Fibonacci-ratio parameter) OR (long trigger mode selects the third lower band AND DelayedClose crosses below EMA(close, EMA lookback) - MeanTrueRange × second Fibonacci-ratio parameter) OR (long trigger mode selects the fourth lower band AND DelayedClose crosses below EMA(close, EMA lookback) - MeanTrueRange × third Fibonacci-ratio parameter) OR (long trigger mode is disabled AND low != low[1]))
Exit: Long, DelayedClose crosses EMA(close, EMA lookback) + MeanTrueRange × second Fibonacci-ratio parameter OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage parameter / 100) OR low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss percentage parameter / 100) OR (a trailing stop is activated when high reaches entry price × (1 + (take-profit percentage parameter + trail-activation increment parameter) / 100) AND thereafter low <= highest high since activation minus the trailing-offset parameter)
