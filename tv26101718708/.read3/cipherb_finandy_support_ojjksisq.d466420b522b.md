Calculation: Fractal divergences(X, upper cutoff, lower cutoff, limited) = a top (bottom) fractal occurs when X at the center is strictly higher (lower) than each of the fractal half-width samples before and after it; compare the center price high (low) and X value with those at the previous qualifying top (bottom); regular bearish = higher high and lower X at a top, regular bullish = lower low and higher X at a bottom, hidden bearish = lower high and higher X at a top, hidden bullish = higher low and lower X at a bottom; when limited, require X at a top to be at least the upper cutoff and X at a bottom to be at most the lower cutoff.

Calculation: Smoothed price slope = (EMA(close, slope EMA lookback, T1, mapped to chart bars from lookahead-off security values) - that mapped value slope lag chart bars ago) / slope lag.
