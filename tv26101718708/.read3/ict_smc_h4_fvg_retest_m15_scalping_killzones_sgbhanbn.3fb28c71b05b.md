Calculation: near-touch buffer = 0 when near-touch is disabled; otherwise max(minimum tick distance, ATR(chart bars, near-touch ATR lookback) × near-touch percentage).
Calculation: selected active FVG = among confirmed, active, untriggered FVGs, choose by priority: new valid retest touch, retest-ready, continuation-ready, eligible touch, fresh tag, then other; ties use the smallest touch gap for a new retest touch, otherwise the smallest distance from close to the zone (zero inside the zone). A bearish zone is invalidated, when full-close invalidation is enabled, by a confirmed chart-bar close above its upper boundary; a bullish zone by a confirmed chart-bar close below its lower boundary. A stopped retest may reactivate only when re-arming is enabled, the confirmed stop bar closes no further through the zone than its far boundary, and the re-entry cap permits it; another attempt requires a fresh touch.
Calculation: accepted FVG touch = exact overlap (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) OR, when near-touch is enabled, a bullish near-touch (low > zone top AND low <= zone top + near-touch buffer) OR a bearish near-touch (high < zone bottom AND high >= zone bottom - near-touch buffer); when the correct-side filter is enabled, a bearish touch also requires previous close <= zone bottom AND current open <= zone bottom + near-touch buffer, and a bullish touch requires previous close >= zone top AND current open >= zone top - near-touch buffer.
Long: retest module enabled AND selected zone is active, untriggered, and a confirmed bullish T1 Fair Value Gap (third T1 bar low > first T1 bar high; zone bounds are the first bar high and third bar low, using completed T1 bars sampled on chart bars) AND (killzone filter disabled OR current time is in an enabled session window) AND ((direct-entry model AND accepted FVG touch now AND (LTF-FVG requirement disabled OR bullish chart-timeframe Fair Value Gap AND, if enabled, its size meets the configured minimum)) OR (retest model AND a fresh accepted touch within the configured validity window AND (departure occurred OR direct tap is allowed) AND ((wick-tag trigger AND fresh accepted touch) OR (rejection-close trigger AND close > zone top) OR (LTF-FVG trigger AND bullish chart-timeframe Fair Value Gap whose size meets the configured minimum when that filter is enabled)))) AND (execution-close validation disabled OR chart bar confirmed) AND (trade blocking disabled OR no active trade or pending order). Strict departure requires a chart-bar close above the zone; tolerant departure requires a chart-bar high above it.
Short: retest module enabled AND selected zone is active, untriggered, and a confirmed bearish T1 Fair Value Gap (third T1 bar high < first T1 bar low; zone bounds are the third bar high and first bar low, using completed T1 bars sampled on chart bars) AND (killzone filter disabled OR current time is in an enabled session window) AND ((direct-entry model AND accepted FVG touch now AND (LTF-FVG requirement disabled OR bearish chart-timeframe Fair Value Gap AND, if enabled, its size meets the configured minimum)) OR (retest model AND a fresh accepted touch within the configured validity window AND (departure occurred OR direct tap is allowed) AND ((wick-tag trigger AND fresh accepted touch) OR (rejection-close trigger AND close < zone bottom) OR (LTF-FVG trigger AND bearish chart-timeframe Fair Value Gap whose size meets the configured minimum when that filter is enabled)))) AND (execution-close validation disabled OR chart bar confirmed) AND (trade blocking disabled OR no active trade or pending order). Strict departure requires a chart-bar close below the zone; tolerant departure requires a chart-bar low below it.
Long: instant-tap entry enabled AND a fresh accepted touch of a live bullish FVG, including one already traded, subject to its tap cap; if several live FVGs qualify, choose the one whose midpoint is nearest the chart-bar close; AND (trade blocking disabled OR no active trade or pending order). No departure, retest trigger, or chart-bar close is required.
