Long: Chaikin Oscillator(fast lookback, slow lookback) crosses above 0 AND close > SMA(close, lookback)
Short: Chaikin Oscillator(fast lookback, slow lookback) crosses below 0 AND close < SMA(close, lookback)
Exit: long, low <= close on long signal bar - ATR(lookback) on long signal bar * stop multiplier OR high >= close on long signal bar + ATR(lookback) on long signal bar * take-profit multiplier
Exit: short, high >= close on short signal bar + ATR(lookback) on short signal bar * stop multiplier OR low <= close on short signal bar - ATR(lookback) on short signal bar * take-profit multiplier
