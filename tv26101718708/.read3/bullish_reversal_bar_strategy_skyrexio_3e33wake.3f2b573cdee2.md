Long: A signal bar with datetime >= trade-start time and datetime <= trade-stop time has close > (high + low) / 2, low = lowest(low, reversal lookback), and high < Williams Alligator Jaw(hl2, longest lookback, longest lag), high < Williams Alligator Teeth(hl2, intermediate lookback, intermediate lag), and high < Williams Alligator Lips(hl2, shortest lookback, shortest lag); the AO filter is disabled or Awesome Oscillator(hl2, shorter lookback, longer lookback) < its value one bar ago; the MFI filter is disabled or at least one bar in the MFI squat window ending at the signal bar has Market Facilitation Index(high, low, volume) < its value one bar ago and volume > volume[1]; a later bar's high crosses above the signal bar's high plus one minimum tick.
Calculation: Alligator-Fractal trend = persistent state, initially neutral; retain the latest confirmed Williams up-fractal high and down-fractal low, using the same fractal lookback on each side; store the up-fractal high as the up activation level whenever it exceeds current Williams Alligator Teeth, and store the down-fractal low as the down activation level whenever it is below Teeth; when high crosses above the stored up activation level, set trend up, clear that activation level, and set the down activation level to the latest down-fractal low; when low crosses below the stored down activation level, set trend down, clear that activation level, and set the up activation level to the latest up-fractal high; clear the up activation level while trend is up and the down activation level while trend is down.
Exit: Long, while the initial stop is active, low <= the signal bar's low OR the initial stop has been disabled by a down-to-up Alligator-Fractal trend change during the position and Alligator-Fractal trend changes from up to down.
