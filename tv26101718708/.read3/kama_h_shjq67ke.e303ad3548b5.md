Calculation: Efficiency ratio = EMA(abs(source − source[lookback]) / sum(abs(source − source[1]), lookback), smoothing length); use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: KAMA anchor = KAMA(source, efficiency-ratio lookback, faster smoothing period, slower smoothing period, efficiency-ratio smoothing length)[1].
Calculation: ATR-safe = max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum price tick).
Calculation: Dead-zone width = ATR(ATR lookback) × dead-zone multiplier × (2 − efficiency ratio).
Calculation: Confirmed regime = bullish after the configured number of consecutive completed chart bars with close above KAMA anchor + dead-zone width, remaining bullish until the same number of consecutive bars close below KAMA anchor − dead-zone width; bearish follows the inverse rule; initially neutral.
Calculation: Adaptive bands = on chart bars, record each low below KAMA anchor and high above it as a penetration in ATR-safe units, retaining the latest configured number of penetrations per side; each learned multiplier starts at its initial multiplier and, once the minimum sample count is reached, equals the mean penetration × sensitivity clamped to its minimum and maximum. The lower band is (KAMA anchor − dead-zone width × lower-band multiplier) × (1 − learning weight) + (KAMA anchor − ATR-safe × learned lower multiplier) × learning weight; the upper band uses the corresponding upper-band parameters and plus signs.
Calculation: Pressure z-score = standardize over the normalization lookback the product of percent change from cost basis to close and relative volume, where relative volume is volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) when that SMA is positive and otherwise 1; cost basis is the longer-lookback rolling VWAP of hlc3 and volume × longer-window weight plus the shorter-lookback rolling VWAP × shorter-window weight, with the longer lookback exceeding the shorter and the weights summing to 1. Set the z-score to zero when standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: DMFI oscillator = EMA(2 × smoothed MFI − MFI of smoothed MFI, DMFI smoothing length) − 50. Smoothed MFI is EMA of the MFI over the MFI lookback, using hlc3 × volume as flow on rising hlc3 bars and falling hlc3 bars separately; the MFI of smoothed MFI uses the DMFI lookback and sums smoothed MFI on rising and falling smoothed-MFI bars. For either MFI, use a positive-to-negative flow ratio of 100 when negative flow is zero.
Long: On a completed chart bar, low ≤ lower adaptive band AND confirmed regime is bearish AND (buy throttle disabled OR no prior long signal since the latest transition into bullish regime OR abs(low − close of prior long signal) ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × buy separation) AND (ER buy filter disabled OR efficiency ratio ≤ ER maximum for buys) AND (pain gate disabled OR pressure z-score ≤ −z-score override threshold OR lowest pressure z-score in the pain lookback ≤ −capitulation threshold) AND (DMFI gate disabled OR DMFI oscillator ≤ DMFI gate threshold) AND (lower-low filter disabled OR no prior long signal since the latest transition into bullish regime OR low < the lowest low recorded through the prior long signal).
Exit: long, On a completed chart bar, high ≥ upper adaptive band AND confirmed regime is bullish AND (sell throttle disabled OR no sell since the latest confirmed bearish regime OR bars since prior sell ≥ round(base sell cooldown × (1 + min(maximum cooldown multiplier − 1, max(0, (close − KAMA anchor) / ATR-safe) × cooldown scale per ATR)))) AND (pain gate disabled OR pressure z-score ≥ z-score override threshold OR highest pressure z-score in the pain lookback ≥ euphoria threshold) AND (higher-high filter disabled OR no prior sell since the latest transition into bearish regime OR high > the highest high recorded through the prior sell signal).
