Calculation: Structure(T, L) = on T bars, keep the latest confirmed pivot high and low (each confirmed with L bars on both sides), a bias initially neutral, crossed flags initially false, and latest CHoCH and BOS directions initially neutral; a new pivot replaces its level and clears its crossed flag; then, in order, if close is above the uncrossed latest pivot high, mark it crossed, set bias bullish, and update CHoCH direction to bullish if prior bias was neutral or bearish, otherwise update BOS direction to bullish; if close is below the uncrossed latest pivot low, mark it crossed, set bias bearish, and update CHoCH direction to bearish if prior bias was neutral or bullish, otherwise update BOS direction to bearish; event directions persist until updated. Evaluate on each timeframe’s bars and read on chart bars through security with lookahead off: the timeframe bar can still be forming in real time, while historical values appear when it completes.
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND ((Structure(T2, L) CHoCH direction = bullish AND differs from its value on the prior chart bar) OR (Structure(T2, L) BOS direction = bullish AND differs from its value on the prior chart bar))
Short: chart bar is confirmed AND ((Structure(T2, L) CHoCH direction = bearish AND differs from its value on the prior chart bar) OR (Structure(T2, L) BOS direction = bearish AND differs from its value on the prior chart bar))
