Calculation: Trend-timeframe series = EMA, MACD, ADX, SuperTrend, and trend scores calculated on T1 bars (T1 may be the chart timeframe) and sampled on chart bars using the forming T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically (lookahead off).
Calculation: Entry-timeframe series = RSI, OBV, volume oscillator, volume ratio, ATR, structure bias, and entry scores calculated on T2 bars (T2 may be the chart timeframe) and sampled on chart bars using the forming T2 bar in real time and the last completed T2 bar historically (lookahead off).
Calculation: Trend bullish score, trend bearish score, and trend count = counts of enabled bullish EMA, MACD, ADX, and SuperTrend conditions; enabled bearish conditions; and enabled trend indicators, respectively.
Calculation: Entry bullish score, entry bearish score, and entry count = counts of enabled bullish RSI, OBV, volume, ATR, and structure conditions; enabled bearish conditions; and enabled entry indicators, respectively.
Calculation: Volume Oscillator = SMA(volume, shorter volume lookback) - SMA(volume, longer volume lookback).
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, longer volume lookback).
Calculation: Structure bias = initialize as NONE; set LONG when close > latest confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), else set SHORT when close < latest confirmed Pivot Low(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), otherwise retain the previous bias.
Long: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) OR MACD(close, shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, MACD signal lookback) line > signal line AND MACD line > MACD baseline parameter OR ADX(high, low, close, ADX/DI lookback) > ADX threshold AND +DI(high, low, close, ADX/DI lookback) > -DI(high, low, close, ADX/DI lookback) OR SuperTrend(high, low, close, ATR multiplier, ATR lookback) is bullish OR trend bullish score > trend bearish score OR trend bullish score = trend count OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold OR OBV(close, volume) > SMA(OBV(close, volume), OBV SMA lookback) AND OBV(close, volume) is rising OR Volume Oscillator > volume oscillator threshold AND Volume ratio > volume ratio threshold OR ATR(high, low, close, entry ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(high, low, close, entry ATR lookback), ATR mean lookback) OR structure bias = LONG OR entry bullish score > entry bearish score OR entry bullish score = entry count.
Short: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) OR MACD(close, shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, MACD signal lookback) line < signal line AND MACD line < MACD baseline parameter OR ADX(high, low, close, ADX/DI lookback) > ADX threshold AND -DI(high, low, close, ADX/DI lookback) > +DI(high, low, close, ADX/DI lookback) OR SuperTrend(high, low, close, ATR multiplier, ATR lookback) is bearish OR trend bearish score > trend bullish score OR trend bearish score = trend count OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold OR OBV(close, volume) < SMA(OBV(close, volume), OBV SMA lookback) AND OBV(close, volume) is falling OR Volume Oscillator < volume oscillator threshold AND Volume ratio < volume ratio threshold OR ATR(high, low, close, entry ATR lookback) <= SMA(ATR(high, low, close, entry ATR lookback), ATR mean lookback) OR structure bias = SHORT OR entry bearish score > entry bullish score OR entry bearish score = entry count.
