Calculation: FVG strength(gap size, direction) = rounded percentage of these four checks that pass: gap size >= ATR(ATR lookback) × smaller gap multiple; gap size >= ATR(ATR lookback) × larger gap multiple; high[1] - low[1] >= ATR(ATR lookback) × displacement multiple; and, for bullish direction, close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback), or, for bearish direction, close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: Strength category enabled(s) = (s < low/medium cutoff AND low-strength category enabled) OR (s >= low/medium cutoff AND s < medium/strong cutoff AND medium-strength category enabled) OR (s >= medium/strong cutoff AND s < strong/very-strong cutoff AND strong-strength category enabled) OR (s >= strong/very-strong cutoff AND very-strong category enabled)
Long: bullish FVG enabled AND low > high[2] AND FVG strength(low - high[2], bullish) >= minimum strength AND Strength category enabled(FVG strength(low - high[2], bullish))
Short: bearish FVG enabled AND high < low[2] AND FVG strength(low[2] - high, bearish) >= minimum strength AND Strength category enabled(FVG strength(low[2] - high, bearish))
