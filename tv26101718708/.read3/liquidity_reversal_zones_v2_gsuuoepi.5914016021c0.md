Calculation: signed volume delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume weighted by each lower-timeframe close position in its range, or signed by its candle direction, blended by lower-timeframe coverage with volume × (2×close−high−low)/(high−low), using that proxy when lower-timeframe data is unavailable
Calculation: CVD = rolling sum of signed volume delta or EMA of signed volume delta; CVD slope = clamp(Z-score over the CVD period of CVD minus its value flow-slope bars ago divided by 2, −1, 1)
Calculation: reversal flow = clamp((EMA over 2 bars of the weighted sum of CVD-slope change, OBV-momentum change, and OBV-traffic-state change) + buy/sell-pressure influence × smoothed buy/sell balance, divided by 1 plus buy/sell-pressure influence, −1, 1); OBV momentum is the clamped Z-score of EMA(OBV) minus SMA(OBV), and traffic state compares cumulative volume signed by changes in (open+high+low+close)/4 with its EMA
Calculation: CVD bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low and CVD at that pivot is higher; CVD bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high and CVD at that pivot is lower
Calculation: extremity = clamp((−soft(mean of the selected, clipped Z-scores of close, ROC, RSI, VWAP, MACD difference, Bollinger %B, Stochastic, ATR, volume, Sharpe, Sortino, and Omega divided by its clipping parameter) + MFI weight × (50−MFI)/50 − CMF weight × CMF) divided by 1 plus both weights, −1, 1); selected components are Z-scored over the extremity lookback, with RSI, Bollinger %B, and Stochastic enabled by default
Calculation: bullish sweep strength = the greatest weighted strength among enabled low-side liquidity levels; bearish sweep strength = the greatest weighted strength among enabled high-side levels. Levels are latest confirmed pivot lows/highs, rolling lows/highs excluding the current bar, prior completed highs/lows at T1, T2, and T3, and prior completed T1 POC. A sweep pierces a level within the reclaim window and closes back across it on the piercing bar or after a prior close beyond it; piercing depth must meet its ATR threshold, and a consumed level cannot trigger again until price rebuilds liquidity beyond it. Strength is (base + depth factor × clamp(depth in ATR, 0, cap)) × source weight × level quality; equal pivots and nearby round levels increase pivot quality.
Calculation: sweep direction = bullish when bullish sweep strength exceeds bearish sweep strength and is positive; bearish when bearish sweep strength exceeds bullish sweep strength and is positive; otherwise neutral
Calculation: rejection = clamp(1.5 × lower-wick fraction + positive close-location value, 0, 1) − clamp(1.5 × upper-wick fraction + positive inverse close-location value, 0, 1)
Calculation: higher-timeframe direction = the configured weighted extremity proxy, or (50−RSI(14))/50 when the proxy is disabled; close, RSI, and EMA(50) come from the last completed T1 bar when closed-bar mode is enabled, otherwise from the T1 bar still forming, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: directional score = 100 × soft(direction × event gate × amplitude × score sensitivity / 100), where soft(x)=x/sqrt(1+x²)
Calculation: direction = the active-weight-normalized weighted sum of extremity, reversal flow, decaying regular CVD divergence, sweep direction, rejection, higher-timeframe direction, normalized inverse funding-rate Z-score, normalized inverse spot-perpetual basis Z-score, and decaying matching RSI-and-OBV divergence; apply the configured maximum penalty for a strong trend, opposing higher-timeframe trend, or opposing hidden CVD divergence
Calculation: event gate = clamp(base gate + sweep weight × same-side sweep strength after level boosts + absorption weight × greater of volume/range absorption and same-side delta absorption + rejection weight × same-side rejection + enabled overextension, climax, killzone, Wyckoff spring/upthrust, and squeeze-stress contributions, 0, cap). Absorption is high relative volume with a quiet range, adjusted by Amihud price-change-per-volume; delta absorption is a sweep with insufficiently confirming signed volume delta.
Calculation: amplitude = volume multiplier × mean of the side-specific open-interest quadrant multiplier, Parkinson-volatility regime multiplier, VWAP/session-context multiplier, and efficiency-ratio chop multiplier; open-interest quadrants use price change and normalized change in aggregated available open interest, and the chop multiplier is reduced when Kaufman Efficiency Ratio is below its threshold
Calculation: medium threshold is absolute score at least the configured fixed medium level, or both at least the configured absolute floor and at or above its configured rolling percentile rank; strong threshold is the analogous fixed strong level or rolling strong percentile
