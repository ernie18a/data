Calculation: Premarket high = highest high on chart bars before the configured regular session since the start of the day
Calculation: Premarket low = lowest low on chart bars before the configured regular session since the start of the day
Calculation: Bullish displacement = during the configured regular session, close > open, close - open >= ATR(ATR lookback) * body multiple, close > high[1], and (range filter disabled OR high - low >= minimum range)
Calculation: Bearish displacement = during the configured regular session, close < open, open - close >= ATR(ATR lookback) * body multiple, close < low[1], and (range filter disabled OR high - low >= minimum range)
Long: during the configured regular session, either Premarket low exists AND low < Premarket low AND close > Premarket low, OR Premarket high exists AND close > Premarket high
Short: during the configured regular session, either Premarket high exists AND high > Premarket high AND close < Premarket high, OR Premarket low exists AND close < Premarket low
Long: during the configured regular session, either since the latest same-day PML sweep failure, with no later PML break or prior long signal and no earlier retest since that failure, Premarket low exists AND low <= Premarket low + retest tolerance AND close > Premarket low AND close < open, OR since the latest same-day PMH break, with no later PMH sweep failure, PML sweep failure, or prior long signal and no earlier retest since that break, Premarket high exists AND low <= Premarket high + retest tolerance AND close > Premarket high
Short: during the configured regular session, either since the latest same-day PMH sweep failure, with no later PMH break or prior short signal and no earlier retest since that failure, Premarket high exists AND high >= Premarket high - retest tolerance AND close < Premarket high AND close > open, OR since the latest same-day PML break, with no later PMH sweep failure, PML sweep failure, or prior short signal and no earlier retest since that break, Premarket low exists AND high >= Premarket low - retest tolerance AND close < Premarket low
Long: bullish displacement AND (either a PML sweep-fail path remains active after its latest same-day PML sweep failure, a qualifying retest occurred on a prior bar since that failure, and no later PML break or long signal occurred, OR a PMH-break path remains active after its latest same-day PMH break, a qualifying retest occurred on a prior bar since that break, and no later PMH sweep failure, PML sweep failure, or long signal occurred) AND (one-signal-per-side-per-day restriction disabled OR no earlier long signal that day)
Short: bearish displacement AND (either a PMH sweep-fail path remains active after its latest same-day PMH sweep failure, a qualifying retest occurred on a prior bar since that failure, and no later PMH break or short signal occurred, OR a PML-break path remains active after its latest same-day PML break, a qualifying retest occurred on a prior bar since that break, and no later PMH sweep failure, PML sweep failure, or short signal occurred) AND (one-signal-per-side-per-day restriction disabled OR no earlier short signal that day)
