Calculation: bounded Z-score(x, lookback, cap) = clamp(ZScore(x, lookback), -cap, cap) / cap
Calculation: composite score = scale × (trend weight × trend + momentum weight × bounded Z-score(ROC(close, momentum lookback), normalization lookback, cap) + mean-reversion weight × mean reversion + volume weight × bounded Z-score(volume, normalization lookback, cap) × sign(ROC(close, momentum lookback)) + slope weight × bounded Z-score(LinReg(close, short lookback, current bar) − LinReg(close, short lookback, previous bar), normalization lookback, cap)), where trend = 0 if ATR(ATR lookback) is zero, otherwise clamp((EMA(close, shorter lookback) − EMA(close, longer lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback), −cap, cap) / cap, and mean reversion = 0 if ATR(ATR lookback) is zero, otherwise clamp((close − VWMA(close, short lookback)) / ATR(ATR lookback), −cap, cap) / cap
Long: composite score > long score threshold AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer lookback), on confirmed chart bars while flat
Exit: Long, in normal exit mode, low ≤ max(entry price − ATR stop multiple × ATR(ATR lookback), entry price × (1 − hard stop percentage)) OR high ≥ entry price + target ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) OR composite score < exit score threshold OR close < EMA(close, shorter lookback); evaluate the score and close conditions on confirmed chart bars
Exit: Long, in runner mode, low ≤ entry price × (1 − hard stop percentage) OR, on confirmed chart bars, current T1 close < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, current T1 bar) AND that EMA < its value on the preceding T1 bar; T1 values are sampled on chart bars from the current T1 bar, which is forming in realtime and completed in historical data
