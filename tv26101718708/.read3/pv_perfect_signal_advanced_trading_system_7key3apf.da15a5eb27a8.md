Calculation: Elevated volume = volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-spike multiplier × strong-volume factor
Calculation: Rising volume = current volume is greater than each volume in the preceding volume-trend lookback
Calculation: Rising-price low-volume divergence = close > close[price-divergence lookback] AND SMA(volume, divergence-volume lookback) < SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × low-volume ratio
Calculation: Falling-price low-volume divergence = close < close[price-divergence lookback] AND SMA(volume, divergence-volume lookback) < SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × low-volume ratio
Calculation: Bull trend state = EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, medium lookback) AND close > EMA(close, long lookback) AND SuperTrend(ATR lookback, multiplier) is bullish, where short, medium, and long lookbacks are ordered from shortest to longest
Calculation: Bear trend state = EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, medium lookback) AND close < EMA(close, long lookback) AND SuperTrend(ATR lookback, multiplier) is bearish, where short, medium, and long lookbacks are ordered from shortest to longest
Calculation: Sideways market = Bollinger Band Width(close, lookback, deviation multiplier) < width threshold OR (sideways filter enabled AND ADX(DMI lookback, DMI smoothing lookback) <= ADX threshold)
Calculation: Supply zone = nearest active confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), with zones expiring after the persistence duration and only the latest zone-count zones retained; its test count increases when it becomes nearest during the current scan, its distance from close is less than close × zone-test tolerance, and close < zone price × (1 + supply-test overshoot); use the count from before the current-bar increment
Calculation: Demand zone = nearest active confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), with zones expiring after the persistence duration and only the latest zone-count zones retained; its test count increases when it becomes nearest during the current scan, its distance from close is less than close × zone-test tolerance, and close > zone price × (1 - demand-test overshoot); use the count from before the current-bar increment
Calculation: At supply = close is within the zone-tolerance percentage of the supply-zone price AND supply-zone test count >= minimum zone tests
Calculation: At demand = close is within the zone-tolerance percentage of the demand-zone price AND demand-zone test count >= minimum zone tests
Calculation: Near supply = close is between supply-zone price × (1 - zone-tolerance percentage × lower near-zone multiplier) and supply-zone price × (1 + zone-tolerance percentage × upper near-zone multiplier)
Calculation: Near demand = close is between demand-zone price × (1 - zone-tolerance percentage × lower near-zone multiplier) and demand-zone price × (1 + zone-tolerance percentage × upper near-zone multiplier)
Calculation: Demand-zone bullish candle = (At demand OR Near demand) AND abs(close - open) > ATR(ATR lookback) × big-candle multiplier AND close > open AND Elevated volume AND NOT Sideways market
Calculation: Supply-zone bearish candle = (At supply OR Near supply) AND abs(close - open) > ATR(ATR lookback) × big-candle multiplier AND close < open AND Elevated volume AND NOT Sideways market
Calculation: Bullish V-shaped reversal = percentage rise from Lowest(low, V lookback) > V-recovery threshold × rebound fraction AND percentage change from Highest(high, V lookback) to close < -V-recovery threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above extreme-oversold level and below rebound RSI ceiling AND Elevated volume AND Rising volume AND close > close[1] AND NOT Rising-price low-volume divergence AND NOT Sideways market
Calculation: Bullish extreme-RSI recovery = RSI(close, RSI lookback) was below extreme-oversold level on any of the preceding extreme-history lookback bars AND RSI has risen on each of the preceding RSI-slope lookback transitions AND Elevated volume AND close > close[1] AND NOT Rising-price low-volume divergence AND NOT Sideways market
Calculation: Bearish inverse-V reversal = percentage rise from Lowest(low, V lookback) > V-recovery threshold AND percentage change from Highest(high, V lookback) to close < -V-recovery threshold × decline fraction AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above decline RSI floor and below extreme-overbought level AND Elevated volume AND Rising volume AND close < close[1] AND NOT Falling-price low-volume divergence AND NOT Sideways market
Calculation: Bearish extreme-RSI decline = RSI(close, RSI lookback) was above extreme-overbought level on any of the preceding extreme-history lookback bars AND RSI has fallen on each of the preceding RSI-slope lookback transitions AND Elevated volume AND close < close[1] AND NOT Falling-price low-volume divergence AND NOT Sideways market
Calculation: Bullish points = base-point weight × sum of these true conditions: Bull trend state; close > EMA(close, short lookback) AND close > VWAP(close); EMA(close, short lookback) crosses above EMA(close, medium lookback); RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above the RSI midpoint and below the upper RSI bound; volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-spike multiplier AND NOT Rising-price low-volume divergence; low > SuperTrend(ATR lookback, multiplier) value; plus advanced-point weight × sum of Demand-zone bullish candle and (Bullish V-shaped reversal OR Bullish extreme-RSI recovery)
Calculation: Bearish points = base-point weight × sum of these true conditions: Bear trend state; close < EMA(close, short lookback) AND close < VWAP(close); EMA(close, short lookback) crosses below EMA(close, medium lookback); RSI(close, RSI lookback) is below the RSI midpoint and above the lower RSI bound; volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume-spike multiplier AND NOT Falling-price low-volume divergence; high < SuperTrend(ATR lookback, multiplier) value; plus advanced-point weight × sum of Supply-zone bearish candle and (Bearish inverse-V reversal OR Bearish extreme-RSI decline)
Calculation: Trading time allowed = opening-time filter disabled OR minutes since configured market-open time >= post-open wait duration
Long: Bullish points >= minimum score AND Bearish points < opposing-score limit AND bars since last signal >= inter-signal interval AND Trading time allowed AND EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, short lookback)[1] AND close > close[1] AND NOT Sideways market AND NOT Rising-price low-volume divergence AND (NOT Bear trend state OR (volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × counter-trend volume multiplier AND Rising volume))
