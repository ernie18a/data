Calculation: Stop OHLC = chart OHLC, or Heikin-Ashi OHLC when enabled; when Renko mode is enabled, stop high = max(stop open, stop close) and stop low = min(stop open, stop close)
Calculation: Long peak = highest(stop high, bars since long entry + 1)
Calculation: Short trough = lowest(stop low, bars since short entry + 1)
Calculation: Active test period = (automatic start enabled AND (((chart interval >= interval cutoff AND chart interval <= interval ceiling) AND elapsed chart bars > round(A * (chart interval in hours)^2 - B * (chart interval in hours) + C)) OR (chart interval < interval cutoff AND elapsed chart bars > round(D / chart interval in minutes)))) OR (automatic start disabled AND test-start datetime <= datetime AND datetime <= test-stop datetime)
Long: longs enabled AND active test period AND ((flip disabled AND SMA(open, MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars) crosses below SMA(close, same MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars)) OR (flip enabled AND SMA(open, MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars) crosses above SMA(close, same MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars))); T1 is the alternate resolution (chart interval multiplied by the resolution multiplier), or the chart timeframe when alternate resolution is disabled
Short: shorts enabled AND active test period AND ((flip disabled AND SMA(open, MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars) crosses above SMA(close, same MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars)) OR (flip enabled AND SMA(open, MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars) crosses below SMA(close, same MA lookback, T1 bars; current/forming bar with lookahead on, evaluated on chart bars))); T1 is the alternate resolution (chart interval multiplied by the resolution multiplier), or the chart timeframe when alternate resolution is disabled
Exit: long, active test period AND ((take profit enabled AND long peak >= entry price + entry price * take-profit percentage AND stop low <= long peak - long peak * min(trailing-profit percentage, take-profit percentage)) OR (stop loss enabled AND stop low <= entry price - entry price * stop-loss percentage) OR (trailing stop enabled AND stop low <= long peak - long peak * trailing-stop percentage))
Exit: short, active test period AND ((take profit enabled AND short trough <= entry price - entry price * take-profit percentage AND stop high >= short trough + short trough * min(trailing-profit percentage, take-profit percentage)) OR (stop loss enabled AND stop high >= entry price + entry price * stop-loss percentage) OR (trailing stop enabled AND stop high >= short trough + short trough * trailing-stop percentage))
