Calculation: T1 range = highest(high, lookback, on T1 bars ending at the last completed T1 bar, sampled on each chart bar) - lowest(low, same lookback, on T1 bars ending at the last completed T1 bar, sampled on each chart bar)
Calculation: Fibonacci midpoint = highest(high, same lookback, on T1 bars ending at the last completed T1 bar, sampled on each chart bar) - T1 range * retracement fraction
Long: (direct-signal enable parameter is on AND crossover(close[1], Fibonacci midpoint) AND close[1] > EMA(close, longer lookback)) OR (reversed-signal enable parameter is on AND crossunder(close[1], Fibonacci midpoint) AND open[1] > Fibonacci midpoint AND close[1] > EMA(close, longer lookback)) OR (scalping-signal enable parameter is on AND EMA(close, longer lookback)[1] - low[1] >= T1 range[1] * distance fraction AND, on each of the previous candle-count chart bars, close > open AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback))
Short: (direct-signal enable parameter is on AND crossunder(close[1], Fibonacci midpoint) AND close[1] < EMA(close, longer lookback)) OR (reversed-signal enable parameter is on AND crossover(close[1], Fibonacci midpoint) AND open[1] < Fibonacci midpoint AND close[1] < EMA(close, longer lookback)) OR (scalping-signal enable parameter is on AND high[1] - EMA(close, longer lookback)[1] >= T1 range[1] * distance fraction AND, on each of the previous candle-count chart bars, close < open AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback))
