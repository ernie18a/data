Calculation: relative close ratio = close of the shorter of T1 and T2 divided by close of the longer of T1 and T2, minus 1; map both requested closes onto chart bars using the last completed requested-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming bar in realtime, using the next longer timeframe when T1 and T2 are equal
Calculation: transformed ratio = the selected transform of the relative close ratio: identity; leaky ReLU, max(x, 0) - alpha * max(-x, 0); step, 1 when x >= 0 and -1 otherwise; Swish, x / (1 + exp(-x)); or the custom z-score, where mean = cumulative sum of x / chart bar index and output = (x - mean) / sqrt((x - mean / (chart bar index - 1))^2)
Calculation: normalized oscillator = 2 * (Karobein Oscillator(selected source, lookback) - its all-time low) / (its all-time high - its all-time low) - 1, calculated on chart bars
Long: transformed ratio crosses above the sensitivity parameter AND normalized oscillator > 0 AND no long signal has occurred since the most recent short signal or initialization AND the current bar's closing timestamp differs from the previous long signal's closing timestamp
Short: transformed ratio crosses below negative sensitivity AND normalized oscillator < 0 AND no short signal has occurred since the most recent long signal or initialization AND the current bar's closing timestamp differs from the previous short signal's closing timestamp
Exit: Long, the chart-bar count since the signal changed to long equals the holding-period parameter
Exit: Short, the chart-bar count since the signal changed to short equals the holding-period parameter
Exit: Long, trailing stops are enabled AND price reaches the trailing stop after it activates at the entry-signal bar's high plus an activation-rate parameter times that high, with the stop tracking the post-activation high at the trailing-offset parameter below it
Exit: Short, trailing stops are enabled AND price reaches the trailing stop after it activates at the entry-signal bar's low minus an activation-rate parameter times that low, with the stop tracking the post-activation low at the trailing-offset parameter above it
