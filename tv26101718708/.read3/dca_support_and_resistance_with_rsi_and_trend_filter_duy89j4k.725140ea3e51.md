Calculation: Osc(source, length) = RSI(source, length) if oscillator type is RSI; MFI(source, length) if oscillator type is MFI; ConnorsRSI(source, length) if oscillator type is Connors RSI
Calculation: TFValue(T, expr) = expr computed on bars of timeframe T using data through the last completed T bar, refreshed when each T bar opens
Calculation: Support(lookback) = low of the most recent bar whose low equals the lowest low over the lookback bars ending at that bar
Calculation: Resistance(lookback) = high of the most recent bar whose high equals the highest high over the lookback bars ending at that bar
Calculation: WTBull(level) = crossover(WaveTrend(source, channel length, average length), SMA(WaveTrend(source, channel length, average length), signal length)) AND WaveTrend(source, channel length, average length) < level
Calculation: WTBear(level) = crossunder(WaveTrend(source, channel length, average length), SMA(WaveTrend(source, channel length, average length), signal length)) AND WaveTrend(source, channel length, average length) > level
Calculation: BullDivergence = pivot low of RSI(divergence source, divergence RSI length) with left and right pivot lookbacks is confirmed on this bar AND bars since the previous pivot low are between min range and max range AND ((low at pivot < low at previous pivot AND RSI at pivot > RSI at previous pivot) OR (low at pivot > low at previous pivot AND RSI at pivot < RSI at previous pivot))
Calculation: BearDivergence = pivot high of RSI(divergence source, divergence RSI length) with left and right pivot lookbacks is confirmed on this bar AND bars since the previous pivot high are between min range and max range AND ((high at pivot > high at previous pivot AND RSI at pivot < RSI at previous pivot) OR (high at pivot < high at previous pivot AND RSI at pivot > RSI at previous pivot))
Calculation: Oversold = (WT disabled AND divergence disabled AND crossover(Osc(close, RSI length), oversold threshold)) OR (Osc(close, RSI length) < oversold threshold AND ((WT enabled AND WTBull(WT oversold level)) OR (divergence enabled AND BullDivergence)))
Calculation: Overbought = (WT disabled AND divergence disabled AND crossunder(Osc(close, RSI length), overbought threshold)) OR (Osc(close, RSI length) > overbought threshold AND ((WT enabled AND WTBear(WT overbought level)) OR (divergence enabled AND BearDivergence)))
Calculation: GaussianTrend(length) = (score, trend line): G_k = GaussianFilter(close, length + k, Gaussian sigma) for k = 0 to Gaussian step count; V = selected Gaussian mode aggregate (average, median, or mode) of G_0 to G_K; half band = SMA(high - low, volatility length) * band distance; upper = V + half band; lower = V - half band; trend flag turns on when close crosses above upper, turns off when close crosses below lower, otherwise keeps its value; trend line = lower if trend flag is on, else upper; score = fraction of k in 1 to K with G_k > G_0
Calculation: TrendState(length) = state s, initially unset, updated in this order by each enabled filter among correlation, Schaff, MACD, ADX, Gaussian: correlation filter: s = 1 if Correlation(close, bar index, length) > 0, else -1; Schaff filter: s = 1 if (s unset or s > 0) AND STC does not decrease AND STC > STC uptrend threshold, else -1 if (s unset or s < 0) AND STC does not increase AND STC < STC downtrend threshold, else 0, where STC = Schaff Trend Cycle(close, STC length, STC sensitivity, STC fast length, STC slow length) minus its midpoint; MACD filter: s = 1 if (s unset or s > 0) AND MACD histogram(MACD source, MACD fast length, MACD slow length, MACD signal length) > 0, else -1 if (s unset or s < 0), else 0; ADX filter: s = 1 if (s unset or s > 0) AND DI+ > DI-, else -1 if (s unset or s < 0), else 0, where DI+ and DI- = DMI(ADX DI length, ADX smoothing length); Gaussian filter: s = 1 - score if (s unset or s > 0) AND close > trend line, else -score if (s unset or s < 0), else 0, where (score, trend line) = GaussianTrend(Gaussian length)
Calculation: FastOsc = TFValue(T1, Osc(close, fast RSI length))
Calculation: SlowOsc = TFValue(T2, Osc(close, slow RSI length))
Calculation: FastTrend = TFValue(T1, TrendState(fast RSI length))
Calculation: SlowTrend = TFValue(T2, TrendState(slow RSI length))
Calculation: FastWTBull = TFValue(T1, WTBull(fast WT oversold level))
Calculation: FastWTBear = TFValue(T1, WTBear(fast WT overbought level))
Calculation: SlowWTBull = TFValue(T2, WTBull(slow WT oversold level))
Calculation: SlowWTBear = TFValue(T2, WTBear(slow WT overbought level))
Calculation: SlowEMA = TFValue(T2, EMA(close, switch EMA length))
Calculation: FastUptrend = FastTrend > 0 OR (FastOsc < fast oversold threshold AND -FastTrend >= fast trend enter strength/100)
Calculation: FastDowntrend = FastTrend < 0 OR (FastOsc > fast overbought threshold AND FastTrend >= fast trend enter strength/100)
Calculation: SlowUptrend = SlowTrend > 0 OR (SlowOsc < slow oversold threshold AND -SlowTrend >= slow trend enter strength/100)
Calculation: SlowDowntrend = SlowTrend < 0 OR (SlowOsc > slow overbought threshold AND SlowTrend >= slow trend enter strength/100)
Calculation: FastOversold = FastOsc < fast oversold threshold AND WT enabled AND FastWTBull AND fast entry signal enabled AND a T1 bar has completed since the last entry
Calculation: FastOverbought = FastOsc > fast overbought threshold AND WT enabled AND FastWTBear AND fast entry signal enabled AND a T1 bar has completed since the last entry
Calculation: LongFullExit = (FastWTBear AND WT enabled AND fast full-sell-on-signal enabled AND close > entry price * (1 + fast signal profit percent/100)) OR (SlowWTBear AND WT enabled AND slow full-sell-on-signal enabled AND close > entry price * (1 + slow signal profit percent/100)) OR (Overbought AND ((fast trend filter enabled AND fast full-sell-on-wrong-direction enabled AND NOT FastUptrend AND (slow trend filter disabled OR NOT SlowUptrend)) OR (slow trend filter enabled AND slow full-sell-on-wrong-direction enabled AND NOT SlowUptrend)) AND close > entry price * (1 + trend profit percent/100)) OR (Overbought AND bars since last exit >= cooldown bars AND abs(position size) < base entry size AND close > entry price * (1 + profit percent/100)) OR (Overbought AND (FastTrend > trend bound AND FastTrend < fast trend exit strength/100 OR SlowTrend > trend bound AND SlowTrend < slow trend exit strength/100) AND close > entry price * (1 + trend profit percent/100))
Calculation: ShortFullExit = (FastWTBull AND fast full-sell-on-signal enabled AND WT enabled AND close < entry price * (1 - fast signal profit percent/100)) OR (WT enabled AND SlowWTBull AND slow full-sell-on-signal enabled AND close < entry price * (1 - slow signal profit percent/100)) OR (Oversold AND ((NOT FastDowntrend AND fast full-sell-on-wrong-direction enabled AND fast trend filter enabled) OR (NOT SlowDowntrend AND slow full-sell-on-wrong-direction enabled AND slow trend filter enabled)) AND close < entry price * (1 - trend profit percent/100)) OR (Oversold AND bars since last exit >= cooldown bars AND abs(position size) < base entry size AND close < entry price * (1 - profit percent/100)) OR (Oversold AND (FastTrend > -fast trend exit strength/100 OR SlowTrend > -slow trend exit strength/100) AND close < entry price * (1 - trend profit percent/100))
Long: (Oversold OR FastOversold) AND (S/R filter disabled OR close >= Support(lookback)) AND (BB filter disabled OR close < BollingerLower(close, BB length, BB lower multiplier)) AND (fast trend filter disabled OR FastUptrend) AND (slow trend filter disabled OR SlowUptrend OR open entry count < slow threshold entries OR FastOversold) AND NOT (SlowWTBear AND WT enabled AND slow full-sell-on-signal enabled) AND (fast trend filter disabled OR FastOsc < fast overbought threshold) AND (slow trend filter disabled OR SlowOsc < slow overbought threshold) AND bars since last entry >= cooldown bars AND no exit on this bar AND position side is not short AND (trade mode is LONG OR (trade mode is BOTH AND (close >= SlowEMA OR position side is long)))
Short: (Overbought OR FastOverbought) AND (S/R filter disabled OR close <= Resistance(lookback)) AND (BB filter disabled OR close > BollingerUpper(close, BB length, BB upper multiplier)) AND (fast trend filter disabled OR FastDowntrend) AND (slow trend filter disabled OR SlowDowntrend OR open entry count < slow threshold entries OR FastOverbought) AND NOT (SlowWTBull AND WT enabled AND slow full-sell-on-signal enabled) AND (fast trend filter disabled OR FastOsc > fast oversold threshold) AND (slow trend filter disabled OR SlowOsc > slow oversold threshold) AND bars since last entry >= cooldown bars AND no exit on this bar AND position side is not long AND (trade mode is SHORT OR (trade mode is BOTH AND (close <= SlowEMA OR position side is short)))
Exit: long, LongFullExit
Exit: long, Overbought AND partial sell percent > 0 AND bars since last exit >= cooldown bars AND abs(position size) > base entry size AND close > entry price * (1 + profit percent/100) AND NOT LongFullExit
Exit: long, Overbought AND partial sell percent > 0 AND bars since last exit >= cooldown bars AND abs(position size) > base entry size AND abs(position size) / base entry size >= panic entries AND close > entry price * (1 + panic profit percent/100) AND close <= entry price * (1 + profit percent/100) AND NOT LongFullExit
Exit: short, ShortFullExit
Exit: short, Oversold AND partial sell percent > 0 AND bars since last exit >= cooldown bars AND abs(position size) > base entry size AND (close < entry price * (1 - profit percent/100) OR (SlowWTBear AND open entry count >= panic entries AND close < entry price * (1 - panic profit percent/100))) AND NOT ShortFullExit
Exit: short, Oversold AND partial sell percent > 0 AND bars since last exit >= cooldown bars AND abs(position size) > base entry size AND abs(position size) / base entry size >= panic entries AND close >= entry price * (1 - profit percent/100) AND close < entry price * (1 - panic profit percent/100) AND NOT (SlowWTBear AND open entry count >= panic entries) AND NOT ShortFullExit
