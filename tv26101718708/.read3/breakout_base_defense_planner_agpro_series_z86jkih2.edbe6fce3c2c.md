Calculation: Base high = Highest(high[1], base lookback)
Calculation: Base low = Lowest(low[1], base lookback)
Calculation: Base range = Base high - Base low
Calculation: ATR floor = max(nz(ATR(ATR lookback), True Range), minimum tick size)
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative volume lookback), or one when the average is not positive
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Locked broken edge = Base high for a bullish breakout or Base low for a bearish breakout, stored when the base is locked
Calculation: Locked base height = Base range, stored when the base is locked
Calculation: Locked defense zone = the locked broken edge plus and minus ATR floor at lock × defense-zone width in ATR, stored when the base is locked
Calculation: Locked failure level = lower defense-zone boundary minus ATR floor at lock × failure buffer in ATR for a bullish base; upper boundary plus that amount for a bearish base
Calculation: Locked room rail = max(locked broken edge + locked base height × room projection multiple, Highest(high[1], structure room lookback)) for a bullish base; min(locked broken edge - locked base height × room projection multiple, Lowest(low[1], structure room lookback)) for a bearish base, stored when the base is locked
Calculation: Directional close distance = close - locked broken edge for a bullish base; locked broken edge - close for a bearish base
Calculation: Defense score = round(clamp(score base + clamp(width score cap - abs(locked base height / ATR floor - ideal base height in ATR) × width score slope, score component floor, width score cap) + clamp(abs(close - locked broken edge) / ATR floor × break-distance weight, score component floor, break-distance cap) + clamp(defense-close offset + directional close distance / ATR floor × defense-close weight, score component floor, defense-close cap) + (high and low overlap the locked defense zone ? retest bonus : clamp(retest bonus - abs(directional close distance / ATR floor) × retest penalty, score component floor, retest cap)) + clamp((relative volume - volume-score offset) × volume-score weight, score component floor, volume-score cap) + clamp(abs(locked room rail - close) / ATR floor × room-score weight, score component floor, room-score cap), score component floor, score ceiling))
Long: confirmed bar AND (Base range is between ATR floor × minimum base range in ATR and ATR floor × maximum base range in ATR AND close > Base high + ATR floor × breakout buffer in ATR AND close[1] <= Base high + ATR floor × breakout buffer in ATR AND candle body ratio >= minimum breakout body ratio AND relative volume >= minimum relative volume OR a bullish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND low <= upper boundary of locked defense zone AND close > locked broken edge AND close > open AND the bullish defense-hold condition was false on the previous bar OR a bullish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND high >= lower boundary of locked defense zone AND low <= upper boundary of locked defense zone AND NOT bullish defense-hold AND this is the first pressure event or more than the pressure cooldown bars after the previous pressure event OR a bullish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND defense score >= minimum ready score AND base is not lost AND base age <= maximum base age AND ready state was false on the previous bar)
Short: confirmed bar AND (Base range is between ATR floor × minimum base range in ATR and ATR floor × maximum base range in ATR AND close < Base low - ATR floor × breakout buffer in ATR AND close[1] >= Base low - ATR floor × breakout buffer in ATR AND candle body ratio >= minimum breakout body ratio AND relative volume >= minimum relative volume OR a bearish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND high >= lower boundary of locked defense zone AND close < locked broken edge AND close < open AND the bearish defense-hold condition was false on the previous bar OR a bearish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND high >= lower boundary of locked defense zone AND low <= upper boundary of locked defense zone AND NOT bearish defense-hold AND this is the first pressure event or more than the pressure cooldown bars after the previous pressure event OR a bearish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND defense score >= minimum ready score AND base is not lost AND base age <= maximum base age AND ready state was false on the previous bar)
Exit: long, bullish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND close < locked failure level
Exit: short, bearish base is locked AND confirmed bar AND close > locked failure level
