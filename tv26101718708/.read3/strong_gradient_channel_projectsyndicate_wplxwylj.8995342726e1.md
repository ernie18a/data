Calculation: Buy fraction(bar) = clamp(close-location fraction in close-location mode, body-direction fraction in body-direction mode, or the blend of those fractions using the blend percentage in blend mode, 0, 1), where close-location fraction = (close − low) / max(high − low, tick size), and body-direction fraction = 1 if close > open, 0 if close < open, otherwise one-half.
Calculation: Bar buy volume and sell volume = on each chart bar, sum each completed T1 intrabar’s volume times its buy fraction and sell fraction; if no T1 intrabars are available, use chart-bar volume times the chart-bar buy fraction and its complement. T1 is the selected lower timeframe; values are aggregated onto chart bars.
Calculation: Bar delta = bar buy volume − bar sell volume.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of bar delta from the start of loaded history, calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Local profile = on each chart bar, split its low-to-high range into a parameterized number of equal bins and divide each T1 intrabar’s volume equally among the bins intersecting its low-to-high range; POC location is the center of the maximum-volume bin as a fraction of the bin count; shape is bimodal if at least a parameterized count of bins between the first and last bins with volume at least a parameterized fraction of maximum each have volume below a separate parameterized fraction of maximum, otherwise upper-heavy P if POC location is at least a parameterized upper threshold, lower-heavy b if at most a separate lower threshold, or D.
Calculation: Fade score(side) = clamp(wick weight × clamp((side-facing wick / (high − low)) / wick saturation, 0, 1) + effort weight × clamp((bar buy volume + bar sell volume) / SMA(volume, average-volume lookback) − relative-volume baseline, 0, 1) / relative-volume scale × clamp(1 − abs(close − open) / (high − low), 0, 1) + trap weight × (side is short and bar delta > 0 and close < (high + low) / 2, or side is long and bar delta < 0 and close > (high + low) / 2 ? clamp((abs(bar delta) / (bar buy volume + bar sell volume)) / delta-dominance saturation, 0, 1) : 0) + profile weight × (side is short and shape is upper-heavy P ? clamp((POC location − neutral POC location) / POC-location distance, 0, 1) : side is long and shape is lower-heavy b ? clamp((neutral POC location − POC location) / POC-location distance, 0, 1) : 0) + divergence weight × (side is short and CVD < Highest(CVD, divergence lookback)[1], or side is long and CVD > Lowest(CVD, divergence lookback)[1] ? 1 : 0), 0, score upper bound).
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, low < Lowest(low, extreme lookback)[1] AND low <= LinReg(source, channel lookback) − lower multiplier × StDev(source, channel lookback) AND lower wick >= upper wick AND Local profile is not bimodal AND Fade score(long) >= conviction floor AND at least the cooldown period has elapsed since the previous fade signal.
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, high > Highest(high, extreme lookback)[1] AND high >= LinReg(source, channel lookback) + upper multiplier × StDev(source, channel lookback) AND upper wick >= lower wick AND Local profile is not bimodal AND Fade score(short) >= conviction floor AND at least the cooldown period has elapsed since the previous fade signal.
