Calculation: Ribbon average(source, line) = selected moving-average method(source, base lookback + fixed step × (line − 1)); all lines use the same method, base lookback, and step
Calculation: LLAMA forecast = the weighted mean of sign(close[i−1] − close[i]) for i from 1 through the forecast lookback, with weight 1 / (1 + ln(1 + |RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback) − RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[i]|) + ln(1 + |RSI(close, longer RSI lookback) − RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[i]|)); the longer RSI lookback is twice the shorter
Calculation: LLAMA average(source, base lookback) = recursive smoothing of source with alpha 2 / (effective lookback + 1), where effective lookback = max(base lookback × (1 + effective-length range × LLAMA forecast), minimum effective lookback), initialized at source
Calculation: T1 ribbon bias = Ribbon average(source, first line) > Ribbon average(source, last visible line), calculated on T1 bars and read on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar
Long: On confirmed chart-bar close, (Ribbon average(source, first line)[1] > Ribbon average(source, last visible line)[1] AND Ribbon average(source, first line)[2] <= Ribbon average(source, last visible line)[2] OR close[1] > Ribbon average(source, first line)[1] AND close[2] <= Ribbon average(source, first line)[2]) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback)[1] >= ADX minimum) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume[1] >= SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] × volume multiplier) AND (T1 filter disabled OR T1 ribbon bias)
Short: On confirmed chart-bar close, (Ribbon average(source, first line)[1] <= Ribbon average(source, last visible line)[1] AND Ribbon average(source, first line)[2] > Ribbon average(source, last visible line)[2] OR close[1] < Ribbon average(source, first line)[1] AND close[2] >= Ribbon average(source, first line)[2]) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same smoothing lookback)[1] >= ADX minimum) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume[1] >= SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] × volume multiplier) AND (T1 filter disabled OR NOT T1 ribbon bias)
