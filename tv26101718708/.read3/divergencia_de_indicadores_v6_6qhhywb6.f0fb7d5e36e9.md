Calculation: Divergence(I,K) = Let s=0 when confirmation is not awaited, otherwise s=1. A candidate is one of the most recent maximum-pivot-count confirmed pivot lows for positive regular/hidden divergence or pivot highs for negative regular/hidden divergence, with center n bars ago, minimum pivot distance < n <= maximum bars; use PivotLow(close if pivot source is Close, otherwise low, pivot period, pivot period) or PivotHigh(close if pivot source is Close, otherwise high, pivot period, pivot period). Require, unless confirmation is not awaited, I > I[1] OR close > close[1] for positive divergence, or I < I[1] OR close < close[1] for negative divergence. Positive regular: I[s] > I[n] AND P[s] < pivot-low value; positive hidden: I[s] < I[n] AND P[s] > pivot-low value; negative regular: I[s] < I[n] AND P[s] > pivot-high value; negative hidden: I[s] > I[n] AND P[s] < pivot-high value. P is close if pivot source is Close, otherwise low for positive and high for negative. For positive divergence, at every bar strictly between s and n, I and close are each at least their straight-line interpolation between offsets s and n; for negative divergence, each is at most its interpolation.
Calculation: Indicator sources = MACD line and MACD histogram of close using the same fast, slow, and signal lookbacks; RSI(close, lookback); SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback), smoothing lookback); CCI(close, lookback); Momentum(close, lookback); OBV(close, volume); VWMA(close, volume, shorter lookback) - VWMA(close, volume, longer lookback); Chaikin Money Flow(close, high, low, volume, lookback); MFI(close, lookback); Cumulative Volume Delta from lower-timeframe T1 bars anchored to T2, using current forming data and returned on chart bars; and up/down volume delta from current forming lower-timeframe T1 bars, returned on chart bars. MACD histogram uses the same MACD parameters as the MACD line.
Calculation: Selected divergence(I,K) = I is enabled AND K is allowed by the regular/hidden divergence setting AND Divergence(I,K).
Calculation: Divergence count = number of true Selected divergence(I,K) conditions across all enabled indicator sources and all four divergence types, including up/down volume delta.
Calculation: Visible positive divergence = any Selected divergence(I,K) for positive regular or positive hidden divergence among MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI, smoothed stochastic, CCI, Momentum, OBV, VWMA difference, Chaikin Money Flow, MFI, and Cumulative Volume Delta.
Calculation: Visible negative divergence = any Selected divergence(I,K) for negative regular or negative hidden divergence among MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI, smoothed stochastic, CCI, Momentum, OBV, VWMA difference, Chaikin Money Flow, MFI, and Cumulative Volume Delta.
Long: Visible positive divergence AND Divergence count >= minimum number of divergences
Short: Visible negative divergence AND Divergence count >= minimum number of divergences
