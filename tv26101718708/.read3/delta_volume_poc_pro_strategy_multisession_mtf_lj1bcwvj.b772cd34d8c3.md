Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA(close, trend lookback)
Long: low of the still-forming T1 bar < lowest(low[1], shared swing lookback, T1 bars) AND close of the still-forming T1 bar > lowest(low[1], shared swing lookback, T1 bars) AND confirmed chart bar AND (trend filter disabled OR chart close > Trend EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR chart time is within an enabled session)
Short: high of the still-forming T1 bar > highest(high[1], shared swing lookback, T1 bars) AND close of the still-forming T1 bar < highest(high[1], shared swing lookback, T1 bars) AND confirmed chart bar AND (trend filter disabled OR chart close < Trend EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR chart time is within an enabled session)
Exit: Long, low <= entry price reduced by the stop-loss percentage OR high >= entry price increased by the take-profit percentage OR (trailing stop enabled AND high has reached the entry price increased by the trailing activation percentage AND low <= highest high since activation reduced by the trailing offset percentage of entry price)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price increased by the stop-loss percentage OR low <= entry price reduced by the take-profit percentage OR (trailing stop enabled AND low has reached the entry price reduced by the trailing activation percentage AND high >= lowest low since activation increased by the trailing offset percentage of entry price)
