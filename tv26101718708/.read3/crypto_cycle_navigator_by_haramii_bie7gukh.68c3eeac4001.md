Calculation: chart RSI = RSI(close, RSI lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: chart short EMA = EMA(close, short EMA lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: T1 close = current T1 close requested on chart bars without lookahead; T1 may still be forming
Calculation: T1 short EMA = EMA(close, short EMA lookback) on the current T1 bar, requested on chart bars without lookahead; T1 may still be forming
Calculation: T1 medium EMA = EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) on the current T1 bar, requested on chart bars without lookahead; T1 may still be forming
Calculation: T2 RSI = RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the current T2 bar, requested on chart bars without lookahead; T2 may still be forming
Calculation: cycle %K = Stochastic %K(close, high, low, cycle lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: zone range = max(highest(high, zone lookback) - lowest(low, zone lookback), minimum price increment) on chart bars
Calculation: lower inner zone = lowest(low, zone lookback) + zone range × lower-zone fraction
Calculation: upper inner zone = lowest(low, zone lookback) + zone range × upper-zone fraction
Calculation: accumulation score = min(score cap, (low-cycle weight if cycle %K < low-cycle cutoff, else 0) + (deep-cycle weight if cycle %K < deep-cycle cutoff, else 0) + (weak-T2-RSI weight if T2 RSI < RSI low/recovery threshold, else 0) + (rising-T2-RSI weight if T2 RSI > RSI low/recovery threshold and T2 RSI > its value one chart bar ago, else 0) + (weak-chart-RSI weight if chart RSI < RSI low/recovery threshold, else 0) + (lower-zone weight if close < lower inner zone, else 0))
Calculation: overextension score = min(score cap, (lower-cycle-position weight if cycle %K > lower top-cycle cutoff, else 0) + (higher-cycle-position weight if cycle %K > higher top-cycle cutoff, else 0) + (elevated-RSI weight if chart RSI > elevated-RSI cutoff, else 0) + (extreme-RSI weight if chart RSI > extreme-RSI cutoff, else 0) + (upper-zone weight if close > upper inner zone, else 0))
Calculation: trend state = 1 when close > the previous chart bar's (EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier); otherwise -1 when close < the previous chart bar's (EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier); otherwise the previous trend state, initially 1
Long: (((T1 close > T1 short EMA and T1 short EMA > T1 medium EMA) or (T1 close > T1 short EMA and RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the current T1 bar > early-T1 RSI threshold)) and accumulation score >= mid-score threshold and chart RSI crosses above RSI low/recovery threshold) or (((T1 close > T1 short EMA and T1 short EMA > T1 medium EMA) or (T1 close > T1 short EMA and RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the current T1 bar > early-T1 RSI threshold)) and T2 RSI > RSI low/recovery threshold and T2 RSI > its value one chart bar ago and close crosses above chart short EMA and chart RSI > confirmation RSI threshold) or (T1 close > T1 short EMA and T1 short EMA > T1 medium EMA and close of current T2 bar > EMA(close, short EMA lookback) on the current T2 bar and T2 RSI > weekly-strong RSI threshold and low <= chart short EMA and close > chart short EMA and chart RSI < pullback RSI ceiling and close > open) or (T1 close > T1 short EMA and T1 short EMA > T1 medium EMA and close of current T2 bar > EMA(close, short EMA lookback) on the current T2 bar and T2 RSI > weekly-strong RSI threshold and close > highest(high, breakout lookback) one chart bar ago and chart RSI < upper RSI threshold)) and (no prior long signal or more than cooldown chart bars since the prior long signal)
Exit: long, ((overextension score >= mid-score threshold and high > upper inner zone) or (overextension score >= high-score threshold and chart RSI > upper RSI threshold) or (close crosses below chart short EMA and overextension score >= low-score threshold) or ((T1 close < T1 short EMA and T1 short EMA < T1 medium EMA) and not (T2 RSI > RSI low/recovery threshold and T2 RSI > its value one chart bar ago)) or (close crosses below EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) and trend state = -1)) and (no prior exit signal or more than cooldown chart bars since the prior exit signal)
