Long: long trading enabled AND selected fast/shorter moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the fast lookback) crosses above selected medium/longer moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the medium lookback) AND (slow confirmation disabled OR close > selected slow confirmation moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the slow lookback)) AND (MACD confirmation disabled OR (MACD line (MACD source, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback) crosses above its signal line (signal lookback) AND (zero-line filter unrestricted OR (configured zero-line side is above AND MACD line > 0) OR (configured zero-line side is below AND MACD line < 0))))
Short: short trading enabled AND selected fast/shorter moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the fast lookback) crosses below selected medium/longer moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the medium lookback) AND (slow confirmation disabled OR close < selected slow confirmation moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the slow lookback)) AND (MACD confirmation disabled OR (MACD line (MACD source, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback) crosses below its signal line (signal lookback) AND (zero-line filter unrestricted OR (configured zero-line side is above AND MACD line > 0) OR (configured zero-line side is below AND MACD line < 0))))
Exit: long, (low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) on entry signal bar * stop-loss multiplier) OR (high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) on entry signal bar * take-profit multiplier) OR (MA-cross exit enabled AND selected exit fast/shorter moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the exit fast lookback) crosses below selected exit medium/longer moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the exit medium lookback)) OR (max-bars exit enabled AND full bars since entry signal >= maximum-bars parameter)
Exit: short, (high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) on entry signal bar * stop-loss multiplier) OR (low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) on entry signal bar * take-profit multiplier) OR (MA-cross exit enabled AND selected exit fast/shorter moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the exit fast lookback) crosses above selected exit medium/longer moving average (selected EMA, SMA, or WMA of the MA source using the exit medium lookback)) OR (max-bars exit enabled AND full bars since entry signal >= maximum-bars parameter)
