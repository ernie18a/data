Calculation: Equilibrium = average of the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, left strength, right strength) and PivotLow(low, left strength, right strength), when both exist and the high is above the low.
Calculation: Midnight Open = open of the first chart bar at 00:00 New York time, held until the next such bar.
Calculation: Automatic bias = majority of bullish votes from close below Midnight Open and close below Equilibrium versus bearish votes from close above either level; ties or no votes are mixed.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap multiple, OR three time-contiguous bullish bars with each later open above the preceding close AND open - close[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap multiple.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap multiple, OR three time-contiguous bearish bars with each later open below the preceding close AND close[2] - open >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap multiple.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = a bearish FVG zone after a confirmed close above its upper boundary; bearish IFVG = a bullish FVG zone after a confirmed close below its lower boundary.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND close - open >= ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement multiple AND close > the latest confirmed swing high; bearish displacement = close < open AND open - close >= ATR(ATR lookback) * displacement multiple AND close < the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = the high-to-low range of the consecutive down-close run immediately before a bullish displacement; bearish order-block zone = the high-to-low range of the consecutive up-close run immediately before a bearish displacement. The run must end within the run-recency parameter; when the rally-away filter is enabled, the displacement close must be at least the rally multiple times the run's height beyond its far edge.
Calculation: Bullish Breaker = a bearish order-block zone after a later confirmed close above its upper boundary; bearish Breaker = a bullish order-block zone after a later confirmed close below its lower boundary.
Long: A live bullish Breaker overlaps a live bullish IFVG on a confirmed chart bar AND the bias gate is off OR the configured bias is bullish or mixed.
Short: A live bearish Breaker overlaps a live bearish IFVG on a confirmed chart bar AND the bias gate is off OR the configured bias is bearish or mixed.
