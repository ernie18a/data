Calculation: long entry stop = lowest(low, swing lookback) − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR buffer on the entry bar in swing mode; otherwise VWAP(source) on the entry bar
Calculation: short entry stop = highest(high, swing lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR buffer on the entry bar in swing mode; otherwise VWAP(source) on the entry bar
Calculation: long target = entry price + max(entry price − long entry stop, minimum tick) × target risk multiple
Calculation: short target = entry price − max(short entry stop − entry price, minimum tick) × target risk multiple
Calculation: long active stop = the stored long entry stop in swing mode or current VWAP(source) in VWAP mode; after the first target, if trailing is enabled, use the maximum of entry price, EMA(close, fast lookback), and the baseline stop
Calculation: short active stop = the stored short entry stop in swing mode or current VWAP(source) in VWAP mode; after the first target, if trailing is enabled, use the minimum of entry price, EMA(close, fast lookback), and the baseline stop
Long: long trades enabled AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar is within the configured session) AND position is flat AND cooldown after the prior close has elapsed AND T1 bias close > VWAP(source, T1) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1) AND (HTF EMA filter disabled OR T2 close > EMA(close, separate HTF lookback, T2)) AND low <= EMA(close, slow lookback) when slow-touch is enabled, otherwise low <= EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close crosses above EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND (entry VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(source)) AND (RVOL filter disabled OR SMA(volume, RVOL lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, RVOL lookback) >= RVOL threshold); sample T1 and T2 values on chart bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time
Short: short trades enabled AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar is within the configured session) AND position is flat AND cooldown after the prior close has elapsed AND T1 bias close < VWAP(source, T1) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1) < EMA(close, longer lookback, T1) AND (HTF EMA filter disabled OR T2 close < EMA(close, separate HTF lookback, T2)) AND high >= EMA(close, slow lookback) when slow-touch is enabled, otherwise high >= EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close crosses below EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND (entry VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(source)) AND (RVOL filter disabled OR SMA(volume, RVOL lookback) > 0 AND volume / SMA(volume, RVOL lookback) >= RVOL threshold); sample T1 and T2 values on chart bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time
Exit: long, low <= long active stop OR (first target not previously hit AND high >= long target) OR (time stop enabled AND first target not hit AND configured holding-bar threshold reached AND close <= entry price)
Exit: short, high >= short active stop OR (first target not previously hit AND low <= short target) OR (time stop enabled AND first target not hit AND configured holding-bar threshold reached AND close >= entry price)
