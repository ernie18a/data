Calculation: T1 resistance = latest non-na PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T1, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars
Long: on a confirmed chart bar while flat, T1 support exists AND ((T2 latest non-na PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T2, last completed bar) exists AND abs(T1 support - T2 pivot low) <= confirmation tolerance × confirmation-tolerance multiplier) OR (T3 latest non-na PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T3, last completed bar) exists AND abs(T1 support - T3 pivot low) <= confirmation tolerance × confirmation-tolerance multiplier)) AND (low <= T1 support + entry buffer OR previous chart-bar low <= T1 support + entry buffer) AND close > open AND close > previous chart-bar close AND close > T1 support
Short: on a confirmed chart bar while flat, T1 resistance exists AND ((T2 latest non-na PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T2, last completed bar) exists AND abs(T1 resistance - T2 pivot high) <= confirmation tolerance × confirmation-tolerance multiplier) OR (T3 latest non-na PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback, T3, last completed bar) exists AND abs(T1 resistance - T3 pivot high) <= confirmation tolerance × confirmation-tolerance multiplier)) AND (high >= T1 resistance - entry buffer OR previous chart-bar high >= T1 resistance - entry buffer) AND close < open AND close < previous chart-bar close AND close < T1 resistance
Exit: Long, on a bar after the entry signal, low <= T1 support at entry - stop buffer OR high >= entry-signal close + (entry-signal close - (T1 support at entry - stop buffer)) × reward/risk multiple
Exit: Short, on a bar after the entry signal, high >= T1 resistance at entry + stop buffer OR low <= entry-signal close - ((T1 resistance at entry + stop buffer) - entry-signal close) × reward/risk multiple
