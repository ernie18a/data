Calculation: Volume delta = volume × abs(close − open) / (high − low) × sign(close − open), or zero when high = low.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume delta from the start of the series.
Calculation: EMA ribbon score = sum positive votes for each configured adjacent EMA pair where the shorter-lookback EMA exceeds the longer-lookback EMA, and for close above the shortest-lookback EMA; otherwise use negative votes. The ribbon lookbacks are separate and ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Bollinger width position = (Bollinger band width − its lowest value over the position lookback) / max(its highest value minus its lowest value over that lookback, the minimum price increment). Squeeze ended = the prior bar's width position was below the squeeze threshold and the current position is at or above it.
Calculation: Regime score = ADX component × ADX weight + CHOP component × CHOP weight + Bollinger component × Bollinger weight; ADX component = min(ADX(ADX lookback, the same ADX smoothing lookback) / ADX scale × regime score cap, regime score cap); CHOP component = max(zero, CHOP ceiling − CHOP(CHOP lookback)); Bollinger component = Bollinger width position × regime score scale. Trending means score above the trend threshold; ranging means score below the range threshold.
Calculation: HTF score = T1 weight × T1 score + T2 weight × T2 score + T3 weight × T3 score. On chart bars, use last completed bars from T1, T2, and T3. For T1 and T2, raw score adds a bullish vote or bearish vote for HMA(close, effective primary lookback) rising versus its preceding completed bar, and for bullish or bearish Supertrend(effective factor, Supertrend ATR lookback); it also adds a bullish vote when RSI(close, RSI lookback) exceeds the HTF upper RSI threshold, a bearish vote when below the HTF lower RSI threshold, and a neutral vote otherwise. T3 uses the same HMA and RSI tests but no Supertrend test. Each timeframe score is the positive bias score when its raw score is positive, the negative bias score when negative, and the neutral bias score otherwise.
Calculation: Confidence(direction) = min(abs(HTF score) / HTF normalization × maximum HTF points, maximum HTF points) + (trending ? trend points : regime score above the confidence regime threshold ? moderate regime points : zero) + (abs(EMA ribbon score) at least the high ribbon threshold ? high ribbon points : at least the low ribbon threshold ? low ribbon points : zero) + (abs(RSI − chart RSI midpoint) at least the high RSI deviation threshold ? high RSI points : at least the low RSI deviation threshold ? low RSI points : zero) + (SMA(abs(volume delta), volume average lookback) > zero and abs(volume delta) / that average above the volume intensity threshold ? high volume points : base volume points) + (squeeze ended ? squeeze points : zero) + (directional CVD divergence ? CVD points : zero) + (sweep confluence enabled and a directional liquidity sweep ? sweep points : zero).
Calculation: Active bullish FVG zones form when low exceeds high at the gap-separation offset; their bounds are that high and current low, and a zone remains active until low reaches or passes its lower bound. Active bearish FVG zones form when high is below low at the gap-separation offset; their bounds are current high and that low, and a zone remains active until high reaches or passes its upper bound. A close is inside a zone when it is between its bounds, inclusive.
Calculation: RSI bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low exists on the current bar, preceding price and RSI pivot lows exist, the latest price pivot low is below the preceding price pivot low, and the latest RSI pivot low is above the preceding RSI pivot low. RSI bearish divergence uses confirmed pivot highs, with the latest price pivot high above the preceding one and the latest RSI pivot high below the preceding one. Use RSI(close, RSI lookback) and the same divergence pivot strength on both sides.
Calculation: CVD bullish divergence = a confirmed CVD pivot low exists on the current bar, preceding CVD and close pivot lows exist, the latest close pivot low is below the preceding close pivot low, and the latest CVD pivot low is above the preceding CVD pivot low. CVD bearish divergence uses confirmed pivot highs, with the latest close pivot high above the preceding one and the latest CVD pivot high below the preceding one. Use the same divergence pivot strength for CVD and close pivots.
Calculation: Swept prior high = current high exceeds the highest high over the sweep lookback bars before the current bar and current close is below that level. Swept prior low = current low is below the lowest low over those prior bars and current close is above that level.
Calculation: Support and resistance levels are registered from confirmed pivot lows and highs using equal left and right pivot strengths. Merge each pivot into the first existing level within ATR(ATR lookback) × merge distance, updating the level to the touch-count-weighted average and increasing its touch count; otherwise create a level. Cap the level count at the configured maximum, removing the least-touched level and, on ties, the oldest. A long S/R block is any level with at least the minimum touch count at or above close and within ATR × the S/R block distance; a short S/R block is any such level at or below close and within that distance.
Calculation: Volume profile levels use the configured lookback bars and bin count across their lowest low to highest high. Allocate each bar's volume equally among all bins spanned by its low and high. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, with ties choosing the lowest-price bin. Starting at POC, expand the value area toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until accumulated volume reaches the configured value-area fraction; ties choose the upper bin. The upper and lower value-area edges are the outer bin boundaries. A long VP block is any of POC, upper edge, or lower edge at or above close and within ATR(ATR lookback) × VP block distance; a short VP block is any of those levels at or below close and within that distance.
Calculation: Resistance zone = highest(high, zone lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR zone-width multiplier. Support zone = lowest(low, zone lookback) − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR zone-width multiplier.
Calculation: Stop multiplier at entry = adaptive stop mode ? (trending ? trending stop multiple : ranging ? ranging stop multiple : transitional stop multiple) : fixed stop multiple. Initial stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) at entry × stop multiplier at entry.
Calculation: Long trail stop starts at the initial long stop and, after the first target, updates each chart bar to max(previous long trail stop, high − current ATR(ATR lookback) × trail ATR multiplier). Short trail stop starts at the initial short stop and, after the first target, updates each chart bar to min(previous short trail stop, low + current ATR(ATR lookback) × trail ATR multiplier).
Long: selected moving average(close, effective primary lookback) > its prior chart-bar value AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > chart RSI midpoint AND volume delta > zero AND EMA ribbon score ≥ long ribbon threshold AND Supertrend(effective factor, Supertrend ATR lookback) is bullish AND (HTF suppression disabled OR HTF score ≥ long HTF threshold) AND (context filter disabled OR traded ticker matches the context asset OR context asset close > EMA(context asset close, context EMA lookback, chart timeframe, current/forming bar)) AND (FVG filter disabled or inactive OR close is outside every active bearish FVG) AND NOT (strict range filter enabled AND ranging AND ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) < range ADX ceiling AND CHOP(CHOP lookback) > range CHOP floor) AND abs(close − resistance zone) > ATR(ATR lookback) × resistance clearance multiple AND (body filter disabled OR (high > low AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > minimum body fraction)) AND bars since the last confirmed signal ≥ signal spacing AND Confidence(long) ≥ effective minimum confidence AND (S/R filter disabled OR no long S/R block) AND (VP filter disabled OR no long VP block) AND (confirmation disabled OR these long conditions held for the confirmation count of consecutive chart bars).
Short: selected moving average(close, effective primary lookback) ≤ its prior chart-bar value AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < chart RSI midpoint AND volume delta < zero AND EMA ribbon score ≤ short ribbon threshold AND Supertrend(effective factor, Supertrend ATR lookback) is bearish AND (HTF suppression disabled OR HTF score ≤ short HTF threshold) AND (context filter disabled OR traded ticker matches the context asset OR context asset close ≤ EMA(context asset close, context EMA lookback, chart timeframe, current/forming bar)) AND (FVG filter disabled or inactive OR close is outside every active bullish FVG) AND NOT (strict range filter enabled AND ranging AND ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX smoothing lookback) < range ADX ceiling AND CHOP(CHOP lookback) > range CHOP floor) AND abs(close − support zone) > ATR(ATR lookback) × support clearance multiple AND (body filter disabled OR (high > low AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > minimum body fraction)) AND bars since the last confirmed signal ≥ signal spacing AND Confidence(short) ≥ effective minimum confidence AND (S/R filter disabled OR no short S/R block) AND (VP filter disabled OR no short VP block) AND (confirmation disabled OR these short conditions held for the confirmation count of consecutive chart bars).

Short: RSI bearish divergence = true.
