Calculation: Pressure source = previous completed chart bar OHLCV when confirmed-bar mode is enabled; otherwise the current chart bar still forming.
Calculation: Adjusted bar range = pressure-source high - pressure-source low when nonzero, otherwise minimum tick size.
Calculation: Relative volume = 1 when SMA(pressure-source volume, volume-baseline lookback, chart bars) is zero; otherwise pressure-source volume / SMA(pressure-source volume, volume-baseline lookback, chart bars).
Calculation: Relative-volume factor = min(relative volume, volume cap) / volume cap.
Calculation: Trend bias = when ATR dead-zone mode is enabled, zero if abs(pressure-source close - EMA(pressure-source close, trend EMA lookback, chart bars)) < ATR(trend ATR lookback, chart bars including the current forming bar) * ATR dead-zone multiplier, otherwise positive unit if the difference is positive and negative unit otherwise; when disabled, positive unit if pressure-source close is above that EMA, negative unit if below, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Smoothed pressure = EMA(EMA(((((pressure-source close - pressure-source low) - (pressure-source high - pressure-source close)) / adjusted bar range) * relative-volume factor * CLV weight) + (((pressure-source close - pressure-source open) / adjusted bar range) * body-pressure weight) + ((zero if adjusted bar range is zero, otherwise clamp((pressure-source close - previous pressure-source close) / adjusted bar range, -direction clamp limit, direction clamp limit)) * relative-volume factor * direction weight) + (trend bias * trend weight), shorter score-smoothing lookback), longer pressure lookback), on chart bars.
Calculation: Pressure reading = zero when STDEV(smoothed pressure, normalization lookback, chart bars) is zero; otherwise clamp(((smoothed pressure - SMA(smoothed pressure, normalization lookback, chart bars)) / STDEV(smoothed pressure, normalization lookback, chart bars)) * normalization scale, -normalization cap, normalization cap), on chart bars.
Calculation: TTM Squeeze active = SMA(pressure-source close, Bollinger lookback, chart bars) + Bollinger multiplier * STDEV(pressure-source close, Bollinger lookback, chart bars) < EMA(pressure-source close, Keltner lookback, chart bars) + Keltner multiplier * ATR(Keltner ATR lookback, chart bars including the current forming bar) AND SMA(pressure-source close, Bollinger lookback, chart bars) - Bollinger multiplier * STDEV(pressure-source close, Bollinger lookback, chart bars) > EMA(pressure-source close, Keltner lookback, chart bars) - Keltner multiplier * ATR(Keltner ATR lookback, chart bars including the current forming bar).
Calculation: Pressure growing during squeeze = TTM Squeeze active AND abs(pressure reading - pressure reading from pressure-slope lookback chart bars ago) > minimum pressure change AND ((pressure reading > zero AND pressure change > zero) OR (pressure reading < zero AND pressure change < zero)).
Long: EMA(pressure reading, signal-line lookback, chart bars) crosses above zero OR (pressure reading > confluence pressure threshold AND (TTM Squeeze active OR (TTM Squeeze active on the previous chart bar AND NOT TTM Squeeze active))) OR (((NOT TTM Squeeze active OR NOT show-pressure-trend setting) AND pressure reading > neutral boundary) OR (TTM Squeeze active AND show-pressure-trend setting AND pressure growing during squeeze AND pressure reading > zero)).
Short: EMA(pressure reading, signal-line lookback, chart bars) crosses below zero OR (pressure reading < -confluence pressure threshold AND (TTM Squeeze active OR (TTM Squeeze active on the previous chart bar AND NOT TTM Squeeze active))) OR (((NOT TTM Squeeze active OR NOT show-pressure-trend setting) AND pressure reading < -neutral boundary) OR (TTM Squeeze active AND show-pressure-trend setting AND pressure growing during squeeze AND pressure reading < zero)).
