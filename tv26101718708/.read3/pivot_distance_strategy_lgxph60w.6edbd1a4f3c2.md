Calculation: Pivot distance = 100 × close / latest confirmed pivot high − 100 × latest confirmed pivot low / close; pivots use separate left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Squeeze threshold = the selected fixed percentage, or 100 × ATR(threshold lookback) / close × squeeze multiplier, or 2 × standard deviation(Pivot distance, Bollinger lookback) × Bollinger multiplier × Bollinger squeeze ratio, or 2 × Keltner range × Keltner multiplier × Keltner squeeze ratio; Keltner range = ATR(Keltner lookback) when true range is selected, otherwise SMA(standard deviation(Pivot distance, Keltner lookback), Keltner lookback).
Calculation: Breakout threshold = the selected fixed percentage, or 100 × ATR(threshold lookback) / close × breakout multiplier, or 2 × standard deviation(Pivot distance, Bollinger lookback) × Bollinger multiplier × Bollinger breakout ratio, or 2 × Keltner range × Keltner multiplier × Keltner breakout ratio; Keltner range = ATR(Keltner lookback) when true range is selected, otherwise SMA(standard deviation(Pivot distance, Keltner lookback), Keltner lookback).
Calculation: T1 pivot distance = 100 × current T1 close / latest confirmed T1 pivot high − 100 × latest confirmed T1 pivot low / current T1 close; pivots use the separate left and right lookbacks on T1 bars. T1 close and pivots use the still-forming T1 bar and are sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: T1 bias = under the selected HTF method, Long if T1 pivot distance is outside the T1 squeeze threshold and above the T1 breakout threshold, Short if it is outside the T1 squeeze threshold and below the negative T1 breakout threshold, otherwise Squeeze; or, for the zero method, Long if the T1 fast MA is above zero and Short if below zero, otherwise Squeeze; or, for the dual-MA method, Long if the T1 fast MA is above the T1 slow MA and Short if below, otherwise Squeeze. T1 thresholds are the separate fixed HTF percentages or 100 × ATR(T1, threshold lookback) / T1 close × the separate HTF ATR multipliers. T1 MAs use separate selected types and lookbacks, with the slow lookback longer than the fast; they are calculated on T1 bars using the still-forming bar and sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Long cooldown active = cooldown enabled and fewer than the cooldown lookback chart bars have elapsed since a long stop-loss exit, an enabled breakout-cross exit in breakout mode, or an enabled trailing-stop exit.
Calculation: Short cooldown active = cooldown enabled and fewer than the cooldown lookback chart bars have elapsed since a short stop-loss exit, an enabled breakout-cross exit in breakout mode, or an enabled trailing-stop exit.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the highest close − ATR(risk lookback) × trailing distance since long-entry profit first reached ATR(risk lookback) × activation distance; reset on a new long entry or exit.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the lowest close + ATR(risk lookback) × trailing distance since short-entry profit first reached ATR(risk lookback) × activation distance; reset on a new short entry or exit.
Long: (breakout mode selected AND Pivot distance > Squeeze threshold OR zero-cross mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses above zero OR dual-MA mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses above MA(Pivot distance, slow type, longer lookback)) AND trade mode permits long AND long cooldown inactive AND (HTF filter disabled OR T1 bias is Long).
Short: (breakout mode selected AND Pivot distance < −Squeeze threshold OR zero-cross mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses below zero OR dual-MA mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses below MA(Pivot distance, slow type, longer lookback)) AND trade mode permits short AND short cooldown inactive AND (HTF filter disabled OR T1 bias is Short).
Long: breakout mode selected AND abs(Pivot distance[1]) ≤ Squeeze threshold AND Pivot distance > Breakout threshold.
Short: breakout mode selected AND abs(Pivot distance[1]) ≤ Squeeze threshold AND Pivot distance < −Breakout threshold.
Long: breakout mode selected AND NOT (abs(Pivot distance) ≤ Squeeze threshold) AND NOT (abs(Pivot distance[1]) ≤ Squeeze threshold AND (Pivot distance > Breakout threshold OR Pivot distance < −Breakout threshold)) AND Pivot distance ≥ 0.
Short: breakout mode selected AND NOT (abs(Pivot distance) ≤ Squeeze threshold) AND NOT (abs(Pivot distance[1]) ≤ Squeeze threshold AND (Pivot distance > Breakout threshold OR Pivot distance < −Breakout threshold)) AND Pivot distance < 0.
Long: zero-cross mode selected AND (MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses above zero OR (neither that MA crossing above nor crossing below zero AND Pivot distance ≥ 0)).
Short: zero-cross mode selected AND (MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses below zero OR (neither that MA crossing above nor crossing below zero AND Pivot distance < 0)).
Long: dual-MA mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) > MA(Pivot distance, slow type, longer lookback).
Short: dual-MA mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) ≤ MA(Pivot distance, slow type, longer lookback).
Long: HTF filter enabled AND T1 bias is Long.
Short: HTF filter enabled AND T1 bias is Short.
Long: HTF filter enabled AND T1 bias changes to Long from its prior chart-bar value.
Short: HTF filter enabled AND T1 bias changes to Short from its prior chart-bar value.
Exit: long, HTF filter enabled AND T1 bias is Short or Squeeze.
Exit: short, HTF filter enabled AND T1 bias is Long or Squeeze.
Exit: long, Long-only mode selected AND (breakout mode selected AND Pivot distance < 0 OR zero-cross mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses below zero OR dual-MA mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses below MA(Pivot distance, slow type, longer lookback)).
Exit: short, Short-only mode selected AND (breakout mode selected AND Pivot distance > 0 OR zero-cross mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses above zero OR dual-MA mode selected AND MA(Pivot distance, fast type, fast lookback) crosses above MA(Pivot distance, slow type, longer lookback)).
Exit: long, breakout-cross exit enabled AND breakout mode selected AND Pivot distance crosses below Breakout threshold.
Exit: short, breakout-cross exit enabled AND breakout mode selected AND Pivot distance crosses above −Breakout threshold.
Exit: long, trailing stop enabled AND a long trailing stop exists AND close ≤ long trailing stop.
Exit: short, trailing stop enabled AND a short trailing stop exists AND close ≥ short trailing stop.
Exit: long, (stop loss enabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR(risk lookback) × stop multiple OR take profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price + ATR(risk lookback) × target multiple) AND (trailing stop disabled OR no long trailing stop exists) AND no HTF forced long exit.
Exit: short, (stop loss enabled AND high ≥ entry price + ATR(risk lookback) × stop multiple OR take profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR(risk lookback) × target multiple) AND (trailing stop disabled OR no short trailing stop exists) AND no HTF forced short exit.
