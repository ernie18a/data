Calculation: FVG zones on chart bars use the previous three completed bars [3], [2], and [1]: a bullish zone spans [high[3], low[1]] when low[1] - high[3] > 0, is between separate minimum and maximum ATR multiples of ATR(ATR lookback)[1], close[2] > open[2], and the volume filter is disabled or volume[2] > SMA(volume, volume lookback)[2] * volume multiple; a bearish zone spans [high[1], low[3]] when low[3] - high[1] > 0, is between those same ATR multiples, close[2] < open[2], and the same volume condition holds.
Calculation: An unfilled bullish FVG zone is removed when a later chart bar has low <= its lower boundary; an unfilled bearish FVG zone is removed when a later chart bar has high >= its upper boundary.
Calculation: Distance from close to an FVG zone = 0 when close is within the zone, zone top - close when close > zone top, and zone bottom - close when close < zone bottom.
Calculation: T1 bias is bullish when T1 close > EMA(close, bias lookback, T1), bearish when T1 close < EMA(close, bias lookback, T1), and neutral when equal; EMA is calculated on T1 and mapped to chart bars, using the last completed T1 bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming T1 bar in realtime.
Long: The nearest unfilled FVG zone among all unfilled zones whose distance from close <= maximum zone distance and whose side matches T1 bias when the bias filter is enabled is bullish (earliest-created zone wins ties), OR an unfilled bullish FVG zone whose distance from close <= maximum zone distance and whose T1 bias is bullish when the bias filter is enabled overlaps the current bar (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) and no earlier bar both met these visibility conditions and overlapped that zone.
Short: The nearest unfilled FVG zone among all unfilled zones whose distance from close <= maximum zone distance and whose side matches T1 bias when the bias filter is enabled is bearish (earliest-created zone wins ties), OR an unfilled bearish FVG zone whose distance from close <= maximum zone distance and whose T1 bias is bearish when the bias filter is enabled overlaps the current bar (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) and no earlier bar both met these visibility conditions and overlapped that zone.
