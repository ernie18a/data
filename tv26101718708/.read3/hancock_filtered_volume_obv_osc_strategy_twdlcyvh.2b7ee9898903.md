Calculation: Filtered OBV oscillator = raw OBV starts at zero and each bar carries its prior value when volume filtering is enabled and volume < abs((volume + volume-filter lookback) / 2 - stdev(volume, volume-filter lookback) * volume-filter multiplier), otherwise it adds sign(change(source)) * volume to its prior value; return raw OBV - selected MA(raw OBV, oscillator signal lookback, MA-type parameter) when the oscillator is enabled, else raw OBV; ALMA uses its offset and sigma parameters.
Calculation: Fast channel lookback = slow channel lookback * fast-length percentage / 100
Long: high > highest(high, slow channel lookback)[1] AND Filtered OBV oscillator > highest(Filtered OBV oscillator, slow channel lookback)[1] AND (oscillator disabled OR Filtered OBV oscillator > 0)
Long: datetime >= timestamp(start year, start month, start day, 00:00) AND position is flat AND trade-direction parameter allows long AND high > highest(high, slow channel lookback)[1] AND Filtered OBV oscillator > highest(Filtered OBV oscillator, slow channel lookback)[1] AND (oscillator disabled OR Filtered OBV oscillator > 0)
Short: low < lowest(low, slow channel lookback)[1] AND Filtered OBV oscillator < lowest(Filtered OBV oscillator, slow channel lookback)[1] AND (oscillator disabled OR Filtered OBV oscillator < 0)
Short: datetime >= timestamp(start year, start month, start day, 00:00) AND position is flat AND trade-direction parameter allows short AND low < lowest(low, slow channel lookback)[1] AND Filtered OBV oscillator < lowest(Filtered OBV oscillator, slow channel lookback)[1] AND (oscillator disabled OR Filtered OBV oscillator < 0)
Exit: Long, low < lowest(low, fast channel lookback)[1] AND Filtered OBV oscillator < lowest(Filtered OBV oscillator, fast channel lookback)[1]
Exit: Short, high > highest(high, fast channel lookback)[1] AND Filtered OBV oscillator > highest(Filtered OBV oscillator, fast channel lookback)[1]
Calculation: Long protective stop = on a long entry signal, initialize tracked high to that bar's low, trail threshold to its close + (its close / leverage) * trail-threshold percentage / 100, and stop to its close - (its close / leverage) * stop-loss percentage / 100; while long, when high exceeds tracked high, update tracked high and, if it exceeds the trail threshold, set stop to tracked high - (tracked high / leverage) * trail-minimum percentage / 100; otherwise retain prior values.
Calculation: Short protective stop = on a short entry signal, initialize tracked low to that bar's high, trail threshold to its close - (its close / leverage) * trail-threshold percentage / 100, and stop to its close + (its close / leverage) * stop-loss percentage / 100; while short, when low falls below tracked low, update tracked low and, if it falls below the trail threshold, set stop to tracked low + (tracked low / leverage) * trail-minimum percentage / 100; otherwise retain prior values.
Exit: Long, low <= Long protective stop
Exit: Short, high >= Short protective stop
