Calculation: opening-range high = highest high of chart bars in each configured session's opening-range interval.
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest low of chart bars in each configured session's opening-range interval.
Calculation: opening-range width = opening-range high - opening-range low.
Calculation: minimum breakout distance = zero, the minimum point-distance parameter, or opening-range width times the minimum range-percentage parameter divided by 100, according to the configured distance filter.
Calculation: initial stop price = for a long, opening-range low minus the range-stop buffer, entry-bar close minus max(ATR(ATR lookback, T1, calculated on T1 bars and returned on chart bars using the forming T1 bar in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically, lookahead off) times the ATR stop multiplier, minimum ATR stop distance), or entry-bar close minus the fixed stop distance; for a short, opening-range high plus the range-stop buffer, entry-bar close plus the corresponding ATR distance, or entry-bar close plus the fixed stop distance, according to the configured stop mode.
Calculation: initial risk = abs(entry price - initial stop price).
Calculation: target price = for a long, entry price plus the fixed target distance or initial risk times the target risk/reward parameter; for a short, entry price minus the corresponding distance, according to the configured target mode.
Calculation: managed stop price = entry price after the break-even trigger fires, otherwise initial stop price.
Long: for an enabled session, opening range locked AND current time within the breakout-validity duration after session end AND close > opening-range high AND close - opening-range high >= minimum breakout distance AND (first-break-only filter disabled OR no earlier qualifying upward breakout in the session) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar confirmed) AND ((immediate-entry mode selected AND no active trade in the session) OR retest-entry mode selected).
Short: for an enabled session, opening range locked AND current time within the breakout-validity duration after session end AND close < opening-range low AND opening-range low - close >= minimum breakout distance AND (first-break-only filter disabled OR no earlier qualifying downward breakout in the session) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar confirmed) AND ((immediate-entry mode selected AND no active trade in the session) OR retest-entry mode selected).
Long: retest-entry mode selected AND a qualifying upward breakout is pending for the session AND low has reached or fallen below its opening-range high since that breakout AND close > opening-range high AND no active trade in the session AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar confirmed).
Short: retest-entry mode selected AND a qualifying downward breakout is pending for the session AND high has reached or risen above its opening-range low since that breakout AND close < opening-range low AND no active trade in the session AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar confirmed).
Exit: Long, trade-level setting enabled AND active long trade in the session AND chart bar after entry bar AND (high >= target price OR low <= managed stop price OR (elapsed chart bars >= maximum bars in trade AND high < target price AND low > managed stop price)).
Exit: Short, trade-level setting enabled AND active short trade in the session AND chart bar after entry bar AND (low <= target price OR high >= managed stop price OR (elapsed chart bars >= maximum bars in trade AND low > target price AND high < managed stop price)).
Exit: Long, active long trade AND break-even/partial setting enabled AND chart bar after entry bar AND break-even trigger not previously fired AND high - entry price >= initial risk times the break-even trigger R multiple.
Exit: Short, active short trade AND break-even/partial setting enabled AND chart bar after entry bar AND break-even trigger not previously fired AND entry price - low >= initial risk times the break-even trigger R multiple.
