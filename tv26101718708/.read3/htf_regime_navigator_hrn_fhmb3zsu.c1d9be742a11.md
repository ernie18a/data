Calculation: Active EMA values = each active EMA(close, its independent EMA length, T1–T6, current T bar while forming, sampled on chart bars); an EMA slope is its percentage change from its value the slope lookback bars earlier on its own timeframe, qualifying only when its magnitude exceeds the minimum slope threshold.
Calculation: Effective EMA order = EMA length × timeframe duration / chart-bar duration; rank included signal EMAs from shortest to longest effective length, collapse equal lengths, and use the first configured stack depth.
Calculation: Trading range = latest confirmed Williams fractal high and low on T7 using the range-fractal wings; if the pair is invalid and fallback is enabled, use the highest high and lowest low over the range-context lookback on T7, otherwise the range is invalid. T7 values are sampled on chart bars; its current bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Volume baseline = the configured SMA of volume, or, for intraday bars when session-relative mode is selected, the exponential mean of volume from the same session slot over the configured number of prior sessions. Up-volume share = sum(volume where close > open) / [sum(volume where close > open) + sum(volume where close < open)] over the configured chart-bar lookback; use 0.5 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Qualified spring = a low below the valid range low by no more than the maximum penetration percentage of range height, with volume on that undercut passing the configured qualification (any volume, below baseline, or above the configured multiple of baseline; missing volume or baseline passes), followed by a close above range low within the reclaim window, while the range is not broken down and more than the cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous spring.
Calculation: Qualified upthrust = a high above the valid range high by no more than the maximum penetration percentage of range height, with volume on that overshoot passing the configured qualification (any volume, below baseline, or above the configured multiple of baseline; missing volume or baseline passes), followed by a close below range high within the reclaim window, while the range is not broken up and more than the cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous upthrust.
Calculation: Confirmed regular bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed Williams pivot low on T9 has a lower price than the preceding pivot low and its RSI(close, RSI length, T9) value at the pivot is higher; confirmed regular bearish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed pivot high has a higher price and its RSI value at the pivot is lower. T9 pivot and RSI values are sampled on chart bars; pivots confirm after their right-side wings.
Calculation: Recent spring, upthrust, bullish divergence, or bearish divergence = the corresponding event occurred within the configured event-memory window in chart bars.
Calculation: Regime = breakout up when the current T7 close exceeds range high by the breakout buffer percentage; breakout down when it falls below range low by that buffer. A breakout is TRENDING UP or TRENDING DOWN when MA confirmation is disabled, or when at least the configured number of active EMAs are on the breakout side and at least the greater of one or that number minus one qualify with the matching slope; zero active EMAs cannot confirm. Otherwise, with a valid range, count bullish evidence from a prior T7 close above the buffered range midpoint, up-volume share above its threshold, recent spring, and recent bullish divergence; count bearish evidence from the mirrored prior close, low up-volume share, recent upthrust, and recent bearish divergence. More bullish evidence gives ACCUMULATION, more bearish gives DISTRIBUTION, and a tie or invalid range gives NEUTRAL RANGE.
Calculation: MA structure score = clamp((active EMAs with close above them − active EMAs with close below them) × score scale / active EMA count) to the configured score range; use zero when no EMA is active.
Calculation: Regime score = configured positive or negative trend score for TRENDING UP or TRENDING DOWN, configured positive or negative range score for ACCUMULATION or DISTRIBUTION, and zero for NEUTRAL RANGE.
Calculation: Entry-value score = clamp the range-position score to the configured score range; range position is (close − range low) / range height × percent scale, scored as midpoint minus position for bullish regimes, position minus midpoint for bearish regimes, and midpoint minus position for a neutral regime.
Calculation: Divergence score = configured bullish-divergence points for a recent bullish divergence plus configured bearish-divergence points for a recent bearish divergence, clamped to the configured score range.
Calculation: Wyckoff score = configured spring points for a recent spring plus configured upthrust points for a recent upthrust plus (up-volume share − neutral share) × volume-share score scale, clamped to the configured score range.
Calculation: Discretionary score = the mean of the four user-entered external assessments (macro liquidity, upcoming catalysts, insider or institutional flow, and analyst consensus target); map each assessment’s five ordered choices from the configured strongest-positive score through neutral to the configured strongest-negative score.
Calculation: Net score = clamp the weighted mean of MA structure, regime, entry-value, divergence, Wyckoff, and discretionary scores to the configured score range; use zero when the sum of their separate weights is zero.
Calculation: Bull probability = round((100 − base probability before rounding) × (50 + net score / 2) / 100), where base probability before rounding is the greater of the base-case floor and base-case prior minus absolute net score times prior sensitivity. Rounded base probability is the rounded base probability before rounding; bear probability is 100 minus rounded bull and base probabilities, with any negative remainder subtracted from base probability and bear probability set to zero.
Calculation: Fib anchors = the configured source: trading-range high and low; latest confirmed Williams swing high and low on T8 using the independent swing lookback; or chart-bar highest high and lowest low over the fib lookback. Swap anchors if high is below low. T8 pivots are sampled on chart bars and confirm after their right-side wings.
Calculation: Display fib direction = forced up or down when selected; otherwise it follows regime direction, or retains its prior direction while net score remains within the configured hysteresis band, switching up above the positive boundary and down below the negative boundary. Initial direction is up. The golden zone is between the configured canonical retracement bounds; up orientation measures retracement from the high and down orientation from the low.
Calculation: Signal fib direction = display fib direction when selected; otherwise use regime direction, then the direction implied by which fib anchor formed last, then range position relative to the midpoint when anchor timing is unknown, and up if none is available. The signal zone is between its configured shallow and deep retracement percentages using that orientation.
Calculation: EMA-fib confluence = at least one, or all, selected active EMAs lie within the golden zone extended by the configured tolerance percentage of fib range; apply the optional price-in-zone requirement and optional selected-EMA slope filter. Slope direction is positive, negative, or nonzero as configured; selected slopes must all pass or at least one must pass as configured, and no selected slopes passes.
Calculation: Confluence direction = bullish when the configured source is selected EMA slopes with more rising than falling, price above more active EMAs than below, both slope and price tests passing, a nonnegative regime direction, or fib orientation; bearish otherwise. For fib orientation, a confluence slope-direction setting of positive or negative determines direction, while either or a disabled slope filter follows fib orientation.
Calculation: Signal EMA status = ABOVE when close exceeds the EMA by the configured test tolerance and the bar does not straddle it; BELOW when close is below the EMA by the test tolerance multiplied by the separate below-tolerance multiplier; otherwise TESTING. Apply this to active signal EMAs. A long structure requires at least one included EMA and none BELOW. A symmetric short structure requires no included EMA ABOVE; an asymmetric short structure requires all included EMAs BELOW, except EMA 1 may be TESTING if at least one included EMA is BELOW.
Calculation: Fresh long or short license = an enabled reclaim route within its reclaim-memory window or among the first configured number of EMA 1 test episodes after reclaim; or an enabled qualified fresh stack; or an enabled recent spring for long or upthrust for short. A fresh stack is the required strict order newly formed after the configured unstacked duration, after an opposite stack within its qualification window, or on any restack, according to the selected qualification mode; its license lasts for the configured stack-memory window. Count a qualifying test episode only when the candidate gates are open, cooldown permits action, and the signal state can act.
Calculation: Long candidate = signal engine enabled AND long structure AND slope gate AND optional stack-order gate AND configured signal-zone reference passes AND optional strict price-in-zone gate AND optional strict EMA-fib-confluence gate AND optional higher-timeframe veto passes AND optional regime agreement passes AND optional score threshold passes. Slope gate passes when slope mode is off or no slope EMA is selected; otherwise the selected EMAs must all rise or a majority must rise as configured, and alignment must have begun within its freshness window unless a fresh license bypasses it while strict no-bypass mode is off. A fresh license bypasses the signal-zone gate only in Reclaim + Test mode when strict no-bypass mode is off. The strict confluence gate requires bullish confluence, unless its slope-direction setting is Either.
Calculation: Short candidate = signal engine enabled AND short structure AND the mirrored slope and stack gates AND configured signal-zone reference passes AND optional strict price-in-zone gate AND optional strict EMA-fib-confluence gate AND optional higher-timeframe veto passes AND optional regime agreement passes AND optional score threshold passes. A fresh license bypasses the slope gate, and the signal-zone gate only in Reclaim + Test mode, only while strict no-bypass mode is off. The strict confluence gate requires bearish confluence, unless its slope-direction setting is Either.
Calculation: Signal-zone reference passes when the configured reference is price, EMA 1, or either; if EMA 1 is selected but disabled, use price. In strict no-bypass mode the signal-zone reference is required. The separate strict price-in-zone gate requires close inside the signal zone.
Calculation: Long trigger = trigger-volume gate passes AND the configured entry mode: in Reclaim + Test mode, the reclaim bar triggers if enabled, or the first EMA 1 test-episode bar triggers with a fresh license; in Testing EMA 1 mode, EMA 1 is tested; in Testing Any Signal EMA mode, at least one included EMA is TESTING; in Reclaim All mode, long structure is newly recovered. An EMA 1 test is within the configured close tolerance or has a low at/below and close at/above EMA 1. Trigger-volume gate is disabled, passes for missing volume or baseline, or requires trigger-bar volume above its configured multiple of baseline.
Calculation: Short trigger = trigger-volume gate passes AND the configured entry mode: in Reclaim + Test mode, the reclaim bar triggers if enabled, or the first EMA 1 test-episode bar triggers with a fresh license; in Testing EMA 1 mode, EMA 1 is tested; in Testing Any Signal EMA mode, at least one included EMA is TESTING; in Reclaim All mode, short structure is newly recovered. An EMA 1 test is within the configured close tolerance or has a high at/above and close at/below EMA 1. Trigger-volume gate is disabled, passes for missing volume or baseline, or requires trigger-bar volume above its configured multiple of baseline.
Calculation: Long and short signal scores = the lesser of the score cap and the sum of side-specific EMA-status points, slope-alignment and freshness points, signal-zone or golden-pocket points, and volume points. EMA points distinguish full or partial structure from the proportion of included EMAs on the side; slope points distinguish fresh alignment, older alignment, and a majority in the side’s direction; zone points distinguish golden-pocket and signal-zone location; volume points distinguish missing volume, volume above baseline, volume near baseline, and lower volume. Apply the optional minimum-score gate to the corresponding side’s score.
Calculation: Selected stop = configured lowest signal EMA or EMA 1, padded by the invalidation buffer on the risk side; qualified spring low for long or upthrust high for short; or the configured fib retracement, custom fib percentage, or protective fib level. Protective fib level is the lower of the selected pair for long and the higher for short; fib stops have no invalidation buffer.
Calculation: Long invalidation level = selected stop when invalidation reference is Match Stop Reference; otherwise the configured EMA 1 or lowest signal EMA padded below price by the invalidation buffer, falling back to the lowest signal EMA if EMA 1 is unavailable. Short invalidation level is the mirrored EMA 1 or highest signal EMA padded above price, with the same stop-reference option.
Calculation: Long setup = a long candidate that does not trigger while the long signal state can act; short setup is the mirror. A setup marking occurs only on the transition from no setup to setup.
Calculation: Long confirmation = a long candidate and trigger with no active long cooldown, followed by the configured number of consecutive chart-bar closes retaining long structure, including the trigger bar; after the trigger, candidate gates need not remain open. Short confirmation is the mirror. Confirmations and signal-end markings are bar-close gated.
Calculation: Bullish probability action = bull probability reaches the actionable threshold; bearish probability action = bear probability reaches it while bull probability is below it, since the action row prioritizes bull when both qualify.
Calculation: Zone-aligned bias = close is inside the golden zone AND (sticky bullish fib direction with actionable bull probability OR sticky bearish fib direction with actionable bear probability).
