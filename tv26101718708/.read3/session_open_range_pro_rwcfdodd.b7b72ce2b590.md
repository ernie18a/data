Calculation: OpeningRangeHigh(session window, duration) = maximum high of chart bars within the configured-timezone session window whose datetime is less than duration after session open
Calculation: OpeningRangeLow(session window, duration) = minimum low of chart bars within the configured-timezone session window whose datetime is less than duration after session open
Long: (session 1 enabled AND session 1 breakout tint enabled AND close > OpeningRangeHigh(session window 1, duration 1)) OR (session 2 enabled AND session 2 breakout tint enabled AND close > OpeningRangeHigh(session window 2, duration 2)) OR (session 3 enabled AND session 3 breakout tint enabled AND close > OpeningRangeHigh(session window 3, duration 3)) OR (session 4 enabled AND session 4 breakout tint enabled AND close > OpeningRangeHigh(session window 4, duration 4))
Short: (session 1 enabled AND session 1 breakout tint enabled AND close < OpeningRangeLow(session window 1, duration 1)) OR (session 2 enabled AND session 2 breakout tint enabled AND close < OpeningRangeLow(session window 2, duration 2)) OR (session 3 enabled AND session 3 breakout tint enabled AND close < OpeningRangeLow(session window 3, duration 3)) OR (session 4 enabled AND session 4 breakout tint enabled AND close < OpeningRangeLow(session window 4, duration 4))
Long: previous-day levels enabled AND close > high[1] of T1 (last completed bar, taken on chart bars)
Short: previous-day levels enabled AND close < low[1] of T1 (last completed bar, taken on chart bars)
