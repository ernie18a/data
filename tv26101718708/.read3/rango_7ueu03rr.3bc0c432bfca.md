Calculation: Weighted mean = sum(close[i] × abs(close[i] − close[i+1]) / close[i+1]) divided by the sum of those weights over the lookback, using valid adjacent closes with nonzero close[i+1]; use close when the weight sum is zero.
Calculation: Range state = active when the maximum absolute distance of closes in the lookback from the weighted mean is no greater than ATR(ATR lookback) × effective width, after sufficient bars and when ATR is positive. Effective width is base width × clamp(STDEV(change(close), volatility lookback) / SMA(STDEV(change(close), volatility lookback), volatility average lookback), lower clamp, upper clamp) when adaptive mode is enabled, and base width otherwise; use a ratio of one when the average volatility is zero. On transition to active, set range top and bottom to weighted mean ± ATR × effective width; retain them until the range becomes inactive.
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = persistent confirmed pivot highs/lows using separate left and right lookbacks. A pivot’s strength is the greater of one and the count of prior closes within the touch tolerance over the strength lookback. Merge a new level into an existing level within the merge tolerance, retaining its price and type and taking the greater strength.
Long: no bullish signal was active on the prior bar AND (close > upper LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close[1] ≤ that current upper value OR the range is active AND close > range top AND close[1] ≤ range top OR close crosses above a resistance level with strength ≥ strength threshold) AND cooldown has elapsed since the last volume-qualified breakout trigger AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ≥ volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (the range is active OR (close > lower LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close < upper LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) × (1 + extension fraction))).
Short: no bearish signal was active on the prior bar AND (close < lower LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close[1] ≥ that current lower value OR the range is active AND close < range bottom AND close[1] ≥ range bottom OR close crosses below a support level with strength ≥ strength threshold) AND cooldown has elapsed since the last volume-qualified breakout trigger AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ≥ volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (the range is active OR (close < upper LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close > lower LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) × (1 − extension fraction))).

Short: bearish signal is active AND close ≥ the midline of LinearRegressionChannel(source, channel lookback, deviation multiplier) AND close < its upper line AND no pullback-sell marking was active on the prior bar.
