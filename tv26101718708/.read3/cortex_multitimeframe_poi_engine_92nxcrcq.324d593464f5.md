Calculation: NewBar(T) = the last completed T bar differs from the last completed T bar at the previous chart bar
Calculation: OverlapRatio(T) = average over i=1..B-1 of max(0, min(high[i], high[i+1]) - max(low[i], low[i+1])) / min(high[i] - low[i], high[i+1] - low[i+1]), on T bars
Calculation: POI(T, s) = with Ifc = most recent bar i=1..B where s*(close[i] - open[i]) < 0: distal = low[Ifc] if s=+1 else high[Ifc]; proximal = max(open[Ifc], close[Ifc]) if s=+1 else min(open[Ifc], close[Ifc]); on T bars
Calculation: Impulse(T, s) = s*(close - open) > 0 AND abs(close - open) >= departure multiple * ATR(n) AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= min directional share AND close beyond Highest(high[1..B]) (s=+1) or Lowest(low[1..B]) (s=-1) by buffer * ATR(n), on T bars
Calculation: Candidate(T, s) = Impulse(T, s) AND (average over i=1..B of abs(close[i] - open[i]) / (high[i] - low[i]) <= max body ratio) AND (Highest(high[1..B]) - Lowest(low[1..B]) <= max base range * ATR(n)[1]) AND (OverlapRatio(T) >= min overlap combined with abs(close[1] - close[B]) <= max drift * ATR(n)[1] by AND in Strict, by OR in Balanced, omitted in Loose) AND (proximal - distal of POI(T, s)) <= max width(T)
Calculation: OriginTime(T, s) = bar time of Ifc at the latest bar where Candidate(T, s) is true (AM layers: AmCandidate(T, s))
Calculation: StructLevel(T, s) = value at the latest Candidate(T, s) bar of the last confirmed pivot high (s=+1) or low (s=-1) of strengths P_L and P_R; the structure method may instead use Highest(high[B+1..B+S]) or Lowest(low[B+1..B+S]), or the more extreme of pivot and that rolling value
Calculation: FirstBreak(T, s) = Break(T, s) AND (barssince(Candidate(T, s)) = 0 OR NOT Break(T, s)[1]), where Break(T, s) = barssince(Candidate(T, s)) <= W AND s*(close - StructLevel(T, s)) > buffer * ATR(n)
Calculation: Pullback(T, s) = s=+1: Highest(high[B+1..B+R]) - Lowest(low[1..B]) >= min retrace(T); s=-1: Highest(high[1..B]) - Lowest(low[B+1..B+R]) >= min retrace(T)
Calculation: LiqSweep(T, s) = s=+1: Lowest(low[1..B]) < last confirmed pivot low of strength SW AND close[1] > that pivot low; s=-1: Highest(high[1..B]) > last confirmed pivot high of strength SW AND close[1] < that pivot high
Calculation: Imbalance(T, s) = s=+1: low > high[2]; s=-1: high < low[2]
Calculation: RestLiq(T, s) = s=+1: Highest(high[B+1..B+S]) > close; s=-1: Lowest(low[B+1..B+S]) < close
Calculation: Quality(T, s) = min(100, 30*FirstBreak(T,s) + BasePts(T) + 15*Pullback(T,s) + (15 if candidate body >= strong multiple * ATR(n) else 12) + 10*LiqSweep(T,s) + 10*Imbalance(T,s) + 5*RestLiq(T,s)), where BasePts(T) = 15 if OverlapRatio(T) >= min overlap AND abs(close[1] - close[B]) <= max drift * ATR(n)[1] else 10; Pullback, candidate body, BasePts, LiqSweep and Imbalance (on or after the bar) are taken at the latest Candidate bar; FirstBreak and RestLiq at the current bar
Calculation: Trigger(T, s) = FirstBreak(T, s) AND Confirmations(T, s) AND Quality(T, s) >= threshold(mode), where Confirmations = Strict: Pullback(T,s) AND (LiqSweep(T,s) OR Imbalance(T,s)); Custom: each selected one of Pullback, Imbalance, LiqSweep must hold; Loose and Balanced: none; Confirmations taken at the latest Candidate bar
Calculation: AmCandidate(T, s) = candidate bar in session AND bars 1..B all in session AND average over 1..B of abs(close[i] - open[i]) / (high[i] - low[i]) <= AM body limit AND Highest(high[1..B]) - Lowest(low[1..B]) <= AM range limit * ATR(n)[1] AND (Strict only: OverlapRatio(T) >= min overlap) AND s*(close - open) > 0 AND abs(close - open) >= AM departure multiple * ATR(n) AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= AM directional share AND close beyond base extreme by AM buffer * ATR(n) AND (s=+1: close > proximal of POI(T, s); s=-1: close < proximal of POI(T, s)) AND (proximal - distal of POI(T, s)) / tick <= max ticks
Calculation: AmFirstBreak(T, s) = AmBreak(T, s) AND (barssince(AmCandidate(T, s)) = 0 OR NOT AmBreak(T, s)[1]), where AmBreak(T, s) = barssince(AmCandidate(T, s)) <= pending window AND (current bar in session OR post-session allowed AND barssince(AmCandidate(T, s)) <= post-session bars) AND s*(close - AmStructLevel(T, s)) > AM buffer * ATR(n); pending window = max(AM break window, post-session bars) if post-session allowed else AM break window; AmStructLevel = value at the latest AmCandidate bar of the last confirmed pivot high (s=+1) or low (s=-1) of strength M
Calculation: AmQuality(T, s) = min(100, 30*AmFirstBreak(T,s) + (OverlapRatio(T) >= min overlap ? 15 : 10) + 15*Pullback(T,s) + (15 if candidate body >= AM strong multiple * ATR(n) else 12) + 10*Imbalance(T,s) + 5*RestLiq(T,s)), with overlap, Pullback, candidate body, Imbalance and RestLiq taken at the latest AmCandidate bar
Calculation: AmTrigger(T, s) = AmFirstBreak(T, s) AND (Strict: Pullback(T,s) AND Imbalance(T,s); Custom: each selected one of Pullback, Imbalance must hold; Loose and Balanced: none; taken at the latest AmCandidate bar) AND AmQuality(T, s) >= threshold(mode) AND (s=+1: low >= distal of POI(T, s) at the latest AmCandidate bar; s=-1: high <= distal of POI(T, s) at the latest AmCandidate bar)
Calculation: StructBreak(T, m) = m=+1: close crosses above (last confirmed pivot high of strength Q + buffer * ATR(n)); m=-1: close crosses below (last confirmed pivot low of strength Q - buffer * ATR(n)); AM layers: only on bars in session; Q is the breaker structure strength (AM layers: their swing strength)
Calculation: Block(T, m) = at the latest StructBreak(T, m) bar, the most recent bar i=1..K with m*(close[i] - open[i]) < 0 (AM layers: bars in session only); top = high[i]; bottom = low[i]
Calculation: LiveBlock(T, m) = latest Block(T, m) created on an earlier bar, not yet broken by Breaker(T, -m), at most Ma bars old, and for AM layers created on the current session day; origin = bar time of its candle
Calculation: Breaker(T, s) = LiveBlock(T, -s) exists AND s*(close - edge) > buffer * ATR(n), where edge = top of LiveBlock if s=+1 and bottom if s=-1; AM layers: current bar in session or within post-session bars of the block's creation
