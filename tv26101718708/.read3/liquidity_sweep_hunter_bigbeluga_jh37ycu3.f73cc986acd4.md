Calculation: volume change = (volume - volume[1]) / volume * 100
Calculation: active support levels = low of each pivot low with strength L whose volume change L bars ago exceeds volume threshold; active resistance levels = high of each pivot high with strength L; remove either level after a confirmed bar has low < level AND high > level
Long: on a confirmed bar, low < an active support level AND close > that level
Short: on a confirmed bar, high > an active resistance level AND close < that level
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar, low < an active support level AND high > that level
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar, low < an active resistance level AND high > that level
