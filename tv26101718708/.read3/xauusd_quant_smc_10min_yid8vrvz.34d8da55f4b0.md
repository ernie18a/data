Calculation: BOS range high = highest(high, BOS lookback, T3 bars), captured from the current, possibly still-forming T3 bar when a BOS occurs and held until the next BOS
Calculation: BOS range low = lowest(low, BOS lookback, T3 bars), captured from the current, possibly still-forming T3 bar when a BOS occurs and held until the next BOS
Calculation: BOS active = true after crossover(close from the current, possibly still-forming T3 bar of T3 sampled on chart bars, highest(high, BOS lookback, T3 bars) sampled on chart bars at the prior chart bar) when both T1 and T2 closes are above their EMAs, or after crossunder(close from the current, possibly still-forming T3 bar of T3 sampled on chart bars, lowest(low, BOS lookback, T3 bars) sampled on chart bars at the prior chart bar) otherwise; reset at each day boundary and after either entry
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement level = when both T1 and T2 closes are above their EMAs, BOS range high - (BOS range high - BOS range low) × retracement ratio; otherwise, BOS range low + (BOS range high - BOS range low) × retracement ratio
Long: T1 close from the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar sampled on chart bars > EMA(close, trend lookback, T1 bars) sampled on chart bars AND T2 close from the current, possibly still-forming T2 bar sampled on chart bars > EMA(close, trend lookback, T2 bars) sampled on chart bars AND BOS active AND crossunder(chart-bar low, Fibonacci retracement level) AND hour(datetime in GMT+1) >= session start AND hour(datetime in GMT+1) < session end AND day-to-date closed-trade profit as a percentage of initial capital > daily loss floor AND week-to-date closed-trade profit as a percentage of initial capital > weekly loss floor AND week-to-date closed-trade profit as a percentage of initial capital < weekly profit target AND entries during the current day < daily entry limit
Short: T1 close from the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar sampled on chart bars < EMA(close, trend lookback, T1 bars) sampled on chart bars AND T2 close from the current, possibly still-forming T2 bar sampled on chart bars < EMA(close, trend lookback, T2 bars) sampled on chart bars AND BOS active AND crossover(chart-bar high, Fibonacci retracement level) AND hour(datetime in GMT+1) >= session start AND hour(datetime in GMT+1) < session end AND day-to-date closed-trade profit as a percentage of initial capital > daily loss floor AND week-to-date closed-trade profit as a percentage of initial capital > weekly loss floor AND week-to-date closed-trade profit as a percentage of initial capital < weekly profit target AND entries during the current day < daily entry limit
Exit: long, chart-bar low <= BOS range low captured at entry OR chart-bar high >= BOS range high captured at entry + (BOS range high captured at entry - BOS range low captured at entry) × target extension ratio
Exit: short, chart-bar high >= BOS range high captured at entry OR chart-bar low <= BOS range low captured at entry - (BOS range high captured at entry - BOS range low captured at entry) × target extension ratio
