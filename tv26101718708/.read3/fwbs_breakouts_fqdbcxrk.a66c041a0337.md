Calculation: Resistance = On T1 chart bars, initialize span high and low to current close; for each of the prior consolidation-lookback bars whose close differs from current close by less than the consolidation close-range percentage of current close, expand the span with that bar's high and low. Divide the span into bucket-count equal buckets; among the upper edge-bucket-count buckets, count qualifying prior highs in each bucket using inclusive boundaries. Return the midpoint of the bucket with the greatest count, choosing the lowest bucket on ties; if all counts are zero, return span high.
Calculation: Support = On T1 chart bars, initialize span high and low to current close; for each of the prior consolidation-lookback bars whose close differs from current close by less than the consolidation close-range percentage of current close, expand the span with that bar's high and low. Divide the span into bucket-count equal buckets; among the lower edge-bucket-count buckets, count qualifying prior lows in each bucket using inclusive boundaries. Return the midpoint of the bucket with the greatest count, choosing the lowest bucket on ties; if all counts are zero, return span low.
Calculation: Middle-third boundary = Resistance - 2/3 * (Resistance - Support)
Long: On T1 chart bars, close > Resistance AND close[1] <= Resistance AND high > low AND (high - close) / (high - low) < maximum upper-wick fraction AND volume > volume[1] * (1 + minimum volume increase percentage / 100) AND close = Highest(close, breakout high lookback) AND minimum breakout percentage <= (close - close[1]) / close[1] * 100 <= maximum breakout percentage AND (close - Middle-third boundary) / close * 100 <= maximum stop-loss percentage
