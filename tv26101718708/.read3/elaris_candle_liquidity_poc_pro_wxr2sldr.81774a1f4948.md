Calculation: Candle POC price = the midpoint of the lowest-index bin with maximum accumulated volume among BinCount equal bins spanning the chart bar from its low through max(high − low, minimum price increment); for each completed T1 bar contained in the confirmed chart bar, accumulate source volume × overlap(bin, source low–high) / max(source high − source low, minimum price increment) in each bin, where overlap = max(0, min(bin high, source high) − max(bin low, source low)); use the chart bar itself as the source when lower-timeframe precision is disabled or no T1 bars are available.
Calculation: POC delta = the accumulated delta in the maximum-volume bin; for each source bar used for Candle POC price, delta = source volume × ((source close − source low) / source range − (source high − source close) / source range), and each bin accumulates delta × overlap(bin, source low–high) / source range, where source range = max(source high − source low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: POC concentration = volume accumulated in the maximum-volume bin / max(sum of volume across all bins, profile volume floor) × 100.
Calculation: Volume power = chart-bar volume / SMA(chart-bar volume, volume average lookback) when the SMA is positive, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Strong POC = POC concentration >= minimum concentration AND Volume power >= minimum volume ratio.
Calculation: POC bias = POC delta >= 0 when delta-based bias is selected; otherwise chart-bar close >= chart-bar open.
Calculation: Untested strong POC levels = the newest MaxUntestedPerSide strong POC levels of each bias, created on earlier confirmed chart bars while untested-level tracking is enabled and not spanned by any subsequent confirmed chart bar through the bar before the current bar; remove each level after the first confirmed chart bar spans its price.
Long: Strong POC AND POC bias OR untested-level tracking is enabled AND current chart-bar high >= any untested bullish POC price AND current chart-bar low <= that price.
Short: Strong POC AND NOT POC bias OR untested-level tracking is enabled AND current chart-bar high >= any untested bearish POC price AND current chart-bar low <= that price.
