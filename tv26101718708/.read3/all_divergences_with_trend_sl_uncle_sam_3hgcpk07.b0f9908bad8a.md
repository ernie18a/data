Calculation: MA trend state = long when close > MA(close, MA lookback, MA type), short when close < MA(close, MA lookback, MA type), otherwise retain the previous state (initially neutral)
Calculation: Bullish regular divergence(price, oscillator, pivot period, minimum span, pivot limit, maximum bars) = oscillator[1] > oscillator[span] AND price[1] < price[span] for a confirmed close pivot low among the latest pivot limit pivots within maximum bars, with span greater than minimum span; oscillator[1] > oscillator[2] OR price[1] > price[2]; and, on every intervening bar, price and oscillator are at or above the straight lines joining their pivot values to their values at bar 1
Calculation: Bearish regular divergence(price, oscillator, pivot period, minimum span, pivot limit, maximum bars) = oscillator[1] < oscillator[span] AND price[1] > price[span] for a confirmed close pivot high among the latest pivot limit pivots within maximum bars, with span greater than minimum span; oscillator[1] < oscillator[2] OR price[1] < price[2]; and, on every intervening bar, price and oscillator are at or below the straight lines joining their pivot values to their values at bar 1
Calculation: Logistic Dominance(source, rate, lookback, iteration count) = sign(mean(V)) * standard deviation(V), where base = rate * source / Highest(high, lookback) * (1 - source / Highest(high, lookback)); initialize value to base, append value to V each iteration, then set value = rate * abs(base[i]) * (1 - base[i])
Calculation: Smoothed balance of power(high, low, open, close, range floor, smoothing lookback, triple smoothing lookback) = TEMA(EMA(BOP, smoothing lookback), triple smoothing lookback), where range = high - low if high != low else range floor; BOP = ((high-open)/range + (close-low)/range + max(close-open,0)/range - (open-low)/range - (high-close)/range - max(open-close,0)/range) / 3
