Calculation: ALMA deviation bias(src, ALMA length, deviation length, offset, sigma, deviation factor) = calculate ALMA(src, ALMA length, offset, sigma) and raw upper/lower bands at ALMA ± deviation factor × StDev(src, deviation length); trail the upper band as raw upper when raw upper is below prior upper or prior src is above prior upper, otherwise prior upper; trail the lower band as raw lower when raw lower is above prior lower or prior src is below prior lower, otherwise prior lower; when prior spine equals prior upper, choose lower as spine if src is above current upper and upper otherwise; otherwise choose upper if src is below current lower and lower otherwise; bias is bullish when lower is chosen and bearish when upper is chosen. Initialize missing prior bands to current raw bands and missing prior spine to current trailed upper.
Calculation: Five-horizon vote state(T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, vote threshold) = bullish when the sum of +1 for each bullish bias and −1 for each bearish bias is at least the vote threshold, bearish when the sum is at most the negative vote threshold, and neutral otherwise; calculate on chart bars from the last completed bar of each timeframe, sampled and held on chart bars.
Calculation: Bias persistence length = the number of consecutive timeframe bars ending at the evaluated bar with the same bias.
