Calculation: opening-range high = maximum high of chart bars in the configured opening-range interval
Calculation: opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars in the configured opening-range interval
Calculation: opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2
Calculation: retest depth (%) = long: 100 × (opening-range high − low) / (opening-range high − opening-range midpoint); short: 100 × (high − opening-range low) / (opening-range midpoint − opening-range low)
Long: the first close outside the opening range in the current T2 period was above the opening-range high AND this is a later chart bar AND no entry has occurred in the current T2 period AND low < opening-range high AND min(open, close) > opening-range high AND retest depth (%) ≥ minimum wick percentage parameter AND retest depth (%) ≤ maximum wick percentage parameter AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(lookback parameter, smoothing parameter) on chart bars > ADX threshold parameter) AND (T1 VWAP filter disabled OR chart-bar close > VWAP(hlc3, T1 bar still forming, sampled on chart bars))
Short: the first close outside the opening range in the current T2 period was below the opening-range low AND this is a later chart bar AND no entry has occurred in the current T2 period AND high > opening-range low AND max(open, close) < opening-range low AND retest depth (%) ≥ minimum wick percentage parameter AND retest depth (%) ≤ maximum wick percentage parameter AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(lookback parameter, smoothing parameter) on chart bars > ADX threshold parameter) AND (T1 VWAP filter disabled OR chart-bar close < VWAP(hlc3, T1 bar still forming, sampled on chart bars))
Exit: long, partial take-profit enabled AND partial target not previously hit AND chart-bar close ≥ entry price + (entry price − opening-range midpoint) × partial-R parameter; close the configured partial percentage
Exit: short, partial take-profit enabled AND partial target not previously hit AND chart-bar close ≤ entry price − (opening-range midpoint − entry price) × partial-R parameter; close the configured partial percentage
Exit: long, chart-bar high ≥ entry price + (entry price − opening-range midpoint) × take-profit-R parameter OR (chart-bar low ≤ opening-range midpoint AND NOT (partial take-profit enabled AND trailing stop enabled AND partial target hit)) OR (partial take-profit enabled AND trailing stop enabled AND partial target hit AND chart-bar low ≤ opening-range high)
Exit: short, chart-bar low ≤ entry price − (opening-range midpoint − entry price) × take-profit-R parameter OR (chart-bar high ≥ opening-range midpoint AND NOT (partial take-profit enabled AND trailing stop enabled AND partial target hit)) OR (partial take-profit enabled AND trailing stop enabled AND partial target hit AND chart-bar high ≥ opening-range low)
