Calculation: Bullish gap(i) = Low[i] > High[i+2] AND Low[i] - High[i+2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap multiple AND Low[i] - High[i+2] <= ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum gap multiple, with ATR evaluated on the bar where Bullish gap is evaluated
Calculation: Bearish gap(i) = Low[i+2] > High[i] AND Low[i+2] - High[i] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap multiple AND Low[i+2] - High[i] <= ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum gap multiple, with ATR evaluated on the bar where Bearish gap is evaluated
Calculation: Untouched level = pivot high or pivot low computed on bars of an enabled timeframe T1 to T5 (pivot left bars, pivot right bars, lagged one bar of that timeframe; chart bars take the value of the last completed bar of that timeframe), or session high or low of an enabled session window (high and low of chart bars inside the window in UTC, recorded at the first bar after the window ends), recorded at or before this bar and not swept on an earlier bar; a new level within merge tolerance of an untouched level on the same side is not recorded
Calculation: Taken(L) = (L is a low level AND Low < L AND (Close > L OR sweep mode is any breach)) OR (L is a high level AND High > L AND (Close < L OR sweep mode is any breach))
Calculation: Long sweep = FVG detection is on AND some untouched level L has Taken(L) AND (L is a low level OR reversal-only switch is off) AND (session-only switch is off OR L is a session level)
Calculation: Short sweep = FVG detection is on AND some untouched level L has Taken(L) AND (L is a high level OR reversal-only switch is off) AND (session-only switch is off OR L is a session level)
Long: (Long sweep AND Bullish gap(i) for some i from 0 to backward window) OR (BarsSince(Long sweep) <= forward window AND Bullish gap(0) AND bars back to the latest earlier bar where Bullish gap(0) held, infinite if none, >= BarsSince(Long sweep))
Short: (Short sweep AND Bearish gap(i) for some i from 0 to backward window) OR (BarsSince(Short sweep) <= forward window AND Bearish gap(0) AND bars back to the latest earlier bar where Bearish gap(0) held, infinite if none, >= BarsSince(Short sweep))
