Long: On a confirmed close above the previous bar’s high or below its low, if anchoring immediately and the candle closes at or above its open; OR, if anchoring after an inside bar, on a confirmed close within the previous bar’s high-low range when the previous candle closed above its previous high or below its previous low and that breakout candle closed at or above its open.
Short: On a confirmed close above the previous bar’s high or below its low, if anchoring immediately and the candle closes below its open; OR, if anchoring after an inside bar, on a confirmed close within the previous bar’s high-low range when the previous candle closed above its previous high or below its previous low and that breakout candle closed below its open.
Calculation: range upper and lower bounds = the anchor candle’s high and low, extended to the greatest high and least low reached while the range remains open; test for a close outside the bounds before extending them, using live updates or confirmed bars according to the expansion method.
Calculation: estimated candle buy and sell volume = for an up candle, volume times a clamped buy share based on a baseline share plus body fraction times a body weight minus upper-wick fraction times a wick weight; sell volume is the remainder. For a down candle, use the same formula for sell share with the lower wick fraction, and buy volume is the remainder. Split doji volume equally. Use max(high minus low, minimum price increment) as the denominator for body and wick fractions, and clamp shares between minimum-share and maximum-share parameters.
Calculation: range-bin count = clamp(round((range upper bound minus range lower bound) / (minimum price increment times bin-width parameter)), minimum-bin-count parameter, maximum-bin-count parameter); divide the range evenly into that many bins.
Calculation: estimated buy or sell volume per range bin = sum, over positive-volume bars from the anchor through the current bar, of that bar’s estimated buy or sell volume times the fraction of its high-low range overlapping the bin; use max(high minus low, minimum price increment) as the overlap denominator.
Long: For each positive-volume bin in an open or just-ended range, estimated buy volume per bin is greater than or equal to estimated sell volume per bin.
Short: For each positive-volume bin in an open or just-ended range, estimated sell volume per bin is greater than estimated buy volume per bin.
Exit: long, a bullish range closes below its lower bound.
Exit: short, a bearish range closes above its upper bound.
