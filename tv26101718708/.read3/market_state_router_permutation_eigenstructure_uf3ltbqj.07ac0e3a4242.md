Calculation: Normalized impulse(x) = clamp(EMA(x, smoothing lookback) × sqrt(smoothing lookback) / standard deviation(x, volatility lookback), −1, 1).
Calculation: Local impulse = Normalized impulse(log(close / close[1]), smoothing lookback, volatility lookback), on chart bars.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = Kaufman efficiency ratio(close, efficiency lookback), on chart bars.
Calculation: Ordered-structure rank = PercentRank(normalized permutation entropy(close, embedding dimension 4, embedding delay, permutation lookback), percentile baseline), on chart bars and on a 0–100 scale.
Calculation: Systemic concentration = 0.5 × clamp(((largest eigenvalue / sum of eigenvalues) − 1/5) / (1 − 1/5), 0, 1) + 0.5 × (1 − clamp(−Σ(pᵢ × ln(pᵢ)) / ln(5), 0, 1)), where pᵢ = max(eigenvalueᵢ, 0) / sum(eigenvalues), and the eigenvalues come from the correlation matrix of synchronized traded-instrument and external returns over the correlation lookback.
Calculation: Absorption percentile = PercentRank of the latest systemic concentration among the latest percentile-baseline synchronized observations, divided by 100; update on complete synchronized observations and hold otherwise.
Calculation: Factor impulse = Normalized impulse of the first principal component’s weighted sum of rolling z-scores of synchronized log returns for the traded instrument, broad equity prices, long-term Treasury prices, the US dollar index, and the implied-volatility index. Use the dominant eigenvector of their correlation matrix over the correlation lookback, orient it so the broad equity loading is nonnegative, and update on complete synchronized observations while holding the last value otherwise.
Calculation: Target-factor impulse = factor impulse × clamp(abs(traded-instrument loading) × sqrt(5), 0, 1) × (−1 if the traded-instrument loading is negative, otherwise 1).
