Calculation: ORB high = highest high of bars in the configured ORB window.
Calculation: ORB low = lowest low of bars in the configured ORB window.
Calculation: ORB range = ORB high - ORB low.
Calculation: ORB range measure = ORB range in minimum ticks, ORB range / ATR(ATR lookback), or ORB range / close * 100, according to the configured unit.
Calculation: Long breakout buffer = long buffer parameter * minimum tick size in tick units, ORB range * long buffer parameter / 100 in ORB-percent units, or ATR(ATR lookback) * long buffer parameter in ATR units.
Calculation: Short breakout buffer = short buffer parameter * minimum tick size in tick units, ORB range * short buffer parameter / 100 in ORB-percent units, or ATR(ATR lookback) * short buffer parameter in ATR units.
Calculation: Long entry trigger = ORB high + long breakout buffer, shifted down by the configured spread when the bid/ask trigger setting applies to longs; spread = spread parameter * minimum tick size in tick units or spread parameter in price units.
Calculation: Short entry trigger = ORB low - short breakout buffer, shifted up by the configured spread when the bid/ask trigger setting applies to shorts; spread = spread parameter * minimum tick size in tick units or spread parameter in price units.
Calculation: Previous close = close of the last completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Common entry gate = the ORB is enabled and complete; the configured weekday is allowed; local time in the configured timezone is before the entry cutoff; position is flat; the ORB range measure is between its minimum and maximum when the range filter is enabled; volume >= SMA(volume, volume lookback) * minimum relative-volume multiple when the volume filter is enabled; ADX(ADX lookback, ADX lookback) >= minimum ADX when the ADX filter is enabled; high - low <= ATR(ATR lookback) * maximum breakout-candle multiple; and configured per-ORB and daily entry-count limits, daily planned-risk limit, and stop-after-first-win/loss controls permit entry. A per-ORB allowance permits entry according to its configured one-total, once-per-direction, maximum-count, or unlimited rule; the daily risk limit is disabled when zero, and first-win/loss checks use closed-trade profit, with profit > 0 a win and profit <= 0 a loss.
Calculation: Long directional filter = long direction is enabled globally and for the ORB; the optional EMA filter passes when close > EMA(close, EMA lookback), EMA(close, EMA lookback) > its prior chart-bar value, or both, according to its configured mode; the optional VWAP filter passes when close > VWAP(hlc3), VWAP(hlc3) > its prior chart-bar value, or both, according to its configured mode; and the optional previous-close filter passes when close > Previous close or ORB low > Previous close, according to its configured mode.
Calculation: Short directional filter = short direction is enabled globally and for the ORB; the optional EMA filter passes when close < EMA(close, EMA lookback), EMA(close, EMA lookback) < its prior chart-bar value, or both, according to its configured mode; the optional VWAP filter passes when close < VWAP(hlc3), VWAP(hlc3) < its prior chart-bar value, or both, according to its configured mode; and the optional previous-close filter passes when close < Previous close or ORB high < Previous close, according to its configured mode.
Long: high >= Long entry trigger AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter; evaluate the gate when arming the resting touch entry, with no long touch entry already armed for this ORB.
Short: low <= Short entry trigger AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter; evaluate the gate when arming the resting touch entry, with no short touch entry already armed for this ORB.
Long: close > ORB high + long breakout buffer AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Short: close < ORB low - short breakout buffer AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Calculation: Close-breakout setup = the first post-completion close beyond a buffered ORB boundary, recording its direction and chart-bar index and arming an ORB retest.
Long: the close-breakout setup is long, the current bar is within the retest-expiry parameter from its setup bar, high <= ORB high + ORB range * maximum-extension parameter, low <= ORB high, and the configured retest confirmation is touch-only, close > ORB high, or a rejection candle with close > open and close > ORB high; AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Short: the close-breakout setup is short, the current bar is within the retest-expiry parameter from its setup bar, low >= ORB low - ORB range * maximum-extension parameter, high >= ORB low, and the configured retest confirmation is touch-only, close < ORB low, or a rejection candle with close < open and close < ORB low; AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = after a long close-breakout setup, low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum-FVG-size parameter AND (outside-ORB requirement is disabled OR high[2] >= ORB high); FVG bottom = high[2], FVG top = low.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = after a short close-breakout setup, high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum-FVG-size parameter AND (outside-ORB requirement is disabled OR low[2] <= ORB low); FVG bottom = high, FVG top = low[2].
Calculation: Bullish FVG entry level = FVG top for near-edge depth, (FVG top + FVG bottom) / 2 for midpoint depth, or FVG bottom for full-fill depth, according to the configured depth.
Calculation: Bearish FVG entry level = FVG bottom for near-edge depth, (FVG top + FVG bottom) / 2 for midpoint depth, or FVG top for full-fill depth, according to the configured depth.
Long: a stored bullish FVG is within the FVG-expiry parameter, low <= Bullish FVG entry level, and high >= Bullish FVG entry level AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Short: a stored bearish FVG is within the FVG-expiry parameter, low <= Bearish FVG entry level, and high >= Bearish FVG entry level AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Short: high > ORB high + long breakout buffer AND close < ORB high AND (sweep confirmation is close-back-inside OR close < open) AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Long: low < ORB low - short breakout buffer AND close > ORB low AND (sweep confirmation is close-back-inside OR close > open) AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Calculation: First-break state = the first post-completion bar where exactly one of high >= ORB high + long breakout buffer or low <= ORB low - short breakout buffer is true; record its side and bar index and keep updating its extreme to the highest high for a high break or lowest low for a low break until consumed.
Short: First-break state is a high break, close < ORB high, current bar is within the failure-timeout parameter from the first-break bar, and the recorded high extreme <= ORB high + ORB range * maximum-extension parameter AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Long: First-break state is a low break, close > ORB low, current bar is within the failure-timeout parameter from the first-break bar, and the recorded low extreme >= ORB low - ORB range * maximum-extension parameter AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Long: First-break state is a high break AND (configured continuation is touch and high >= ORB high + long breakout buffer, close-outside and close > ORB high + long breakout buffer, or retest-holds and low <= ORB high AND close > ORB high) AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Short: First-break state is a low break AND (configured continuation is touch and low <= ORB low - short breakout buffer, close-outside and close < ORB low - short breakout buffer, or retest-holds and high >= ORB low AND close < ORB low) AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Short: First-break state is a high break, chart-bar index > first-break bar, close < ORB high, current bar is within the failure-timeout parameter, and no configured continuation condition is true AND Common entry gate AND Short directional filter.
Long: First-break state is a low break, chart-bar index > first-break bar, close > ORB low, current bar is within the failure-timeout parameter, and no configured continuation condition is true AND Common entry gate AND Long directional filter.
Calculation: Stop-reference extreme = for touch entries, ORB low for longs and ORB high for shorts; for breakout-close, ORB-retest, sweep, and continuation entries, signal-bar low for longs and signal-bar high for shorts; for FVG entries, FVG bottom for longs and FVG top for shorts; for failed-break entries, the recorded first-break extreme.
Calculation: Structural stop = the configured stop model: long/short signal-candle low/high minus/plus stop buffer; reference price minus/plus ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR-stop multiplier; ORB midpoint; ORB low/high minus/plus stop buffer; Stop-reference extreme minus/plus stop buffer; reference price minus/plus ORB range * stop-percentage parameter / 100; or FVG bottom/top minus/plus stop buffer. Reference price is the intended touch-entry level for touch entries and signal-bar close for other entries. Clamp the long stop to at most reference price - minimum tick and the short stop to at least reference price + minimum tick.
Calculation: Active stop = Structural stop, shifted away from the position by the configured spread when the bid/ask stop setting applies; optionally tighten the structural stop to entry price plus/minus breakeven-offset ticks when high >= entry price + original risk * breakeven-activation R for longs or low <= entry price - original risk * breakeven-activation R for shorts; optionally, on confirmed bars, tighten it to best close since entry minus/plus entry ATR * trail multiplier after best close reaches entry price plus/minus original risk * trail-activation R. Ratchet only in the favorable direction. Original risk is the absolute difference between entry reference and the selected corrected or original stop used for risk calculations.
Calculation: Active target = for non-fade entries, the configured target model; for fade entries, the configured fade-target preference. Fixed R is target-calculation entry plus/minus absolute risk * R parameter; ORB projection is ORB high plus ORB range * projection multiplier for longs or ORB low minus ORB range * projection multiplier for shorts; ATR target is target-calculation entry plus/minus ATR(ATR lookback) * target ATR multiplier; midpoint is ORB midpoint; boundary target is ORB high for longs or ORB low for shorts. For touch entries, target-calculation entry is the intended level or spread-shifted chart trigger according to the configured entry-calculation price; for other entries it is signal-bar close. Fixed-R risk uses the configured corrected spread-adjusted stop or original structural stop and the configured stop-calculation price.
Exit: Long position, low <= Active stop OR high >= Active target OR (time exit is enabled AND chart bars since entry >= time-exit parameter) OR (forced close is enabled AND local time in the configured timezone >= forced-close time).
Exit: Short position, high >= Active stop OR low <= Active target OR (time exit is enabled AND chart bars since entry >= time-exit parameter) OR (forced close is enabled AND local time in the configured timezone >= forced-close time).
