Calculation: Fibonacci upswing = both qualifying swing points exist and their bar indices are greater than zero, and the saved swing high is later than the saved swing low
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = saved swing high − each of three separately configured retracement ratios, ordered shallow to deep, × (saved swing high − saved swing low)
Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci bounce = Fibonacci enabled AND both saved swing points exist with bar indices greater than zero and are at least the configured confirmation age; Fibonacci upswing; at least one enabled level is within Fibonacci zone ATR × ATR of the current low; and the lower-wick fraction of the bar range is at least the minimum wick fraction OR (close > open AND close is above the first touched enabled level in shallow-to-deep order)
Calculation: Bearish Fibonacci rejection = Fibonacci enabled AND both saved swing points exist with bar indices greater than zero and are at least the configured confirmation age; NOT Fibonacci upswing; at least one enabled level is within Fibonacci zone ATR × ATR of the current high; and the upper-wick fraction of the bar range is at least the minimum wick fraction OR (close < open AND close is below the first touched enabled level in shallow-to-deep order)
Calculation: Bar buy volume = volume when close > open, otherwise zero; bar sell volume = volume when close < open, otherwise zero
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of bar buy volume minus bar sell volume
Calculation: Bar volume ratio = bar buy volume ÷ max(bar sell volume, configured volume floor)
Calculation: Bullish order-flow signal = order flow enabled AND ((cumulative volume delta > SMA(cumulative volume delta, CVD lookback) AND close < previous close AND cumulative volume delta > previous cumulative volume delta) OR (bar volume ratio > one plus CVD threshold)) AND close > session-reset VWAP

Calculation: Buying-pressure percentage = volume from bars with close > open divided by volume from bars with close > open or close < open, over the volume lookback; use 50% if that denominator is zero
