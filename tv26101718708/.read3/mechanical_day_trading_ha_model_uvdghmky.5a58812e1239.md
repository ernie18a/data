Calculation: Heikin Ashi body-to-range ratio = abs(Heikin Ashi close - Heikin Ashi open) / (Heikin Ashi high - Heikin Ashi low)
Calculation: Selected trend is bullish = Supertrend(factor parameter, ATR lookback parameter) is bullish when Supertrend mode is selected OR close > PSAR(start parameter, increment parameter, maximum parameter) when PSAR mode is selected
Calculation: Selected trend is bearish = Supertrend(factor parameter, ATR lookback parameter) is bearish when Supertrend mode is selected OR close < PSAR(start parameter, increment parameter, maximum parameter) when PSAR mode is selected
Long: Heikin Ashi close > Heikin Ashi open AND Heikin Ashi body-to-range ratio > neutral-body percentage / 100 AND EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, middle lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND close > EMA(close, short lookback) AND selected trend is bullish AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(hlc3, T1 anchor, chart bars)) AND position is flat, on confirmed chart bars
Short: Heikin Ashi close < Heikin Ashi open AND Heikin Ashi body-to-range ratio > neutral-body percentage / 100 AND EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, middle lookback) AND EMA(close, middle lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback) AND close < EMA(close, short lookback) AND selected trend is bearish AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(hlc3, T1 anchor, chart bars)) AND position is flat, on confirmed chart bars
Exit: long, exit mode selects all triggers AND (selected trend becomes bearish (bearish now and not bearish on the previous chart bar) OR Heikin Ashi close < Heikin Ashi open OR close crosses below EMA(close, middle lookback)) OR exit mode selects trend flip AND selected trend becomes bearish (bearish now and not bearish on the previous chart bar) OR exit mode selects Heikin Ashi bearishness AND Heikin Ashi close < Heikin Ashi open OR exit mode selects EMA cross AND close crosses below EMA(close, middle lookback)), on confirmed chart bars
Exit: short, exit mode selects all triggers AND (selected trend becomes bullish (bullish now and not bullish on the previous chart bar) OR Heikin Ashi close > Heikin Ashi open OR close crosses above EMA(close, middle lookback)) OR exit mode selects trend flip AND selected trend becomes bullish (bullish now and not bullish on the previous chart bar) OR exit mode selects Heikin Ashi bullishness AND Heikin Ashi close > Heikin Ashi open OR exit mode selects EMA cross AND close crosses above EMA(close, middle lookback)), on confirmed chart bars
