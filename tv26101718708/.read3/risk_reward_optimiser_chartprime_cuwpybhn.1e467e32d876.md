Calculation: selected comparison(x, y, threshold) = x crosses above y, x > threshold, or x < threshold, according to its selected comparison mode.
Calculation: stop level at entry = entry price - abs(entry price - selected stop candidate on the entry bar), where the candidate is low - ATR(its lookback) × its multiplier, lowest(low, its lookback), or low - abs(SUM(VWAP(true range), volume-sum lookback) × volume / SUM(volume, same lookback)) × its multiplier, according to the selected stop method.
Long: date filtering is disabled or bar datetime is within the selected interval, AND (external mode and selected external plot equals its threshold OR internal mode and every enabled condition holds: selected comparison(Aroon Up(high, lookback), Aroon Down(low, same lookback), its threshold); selected comparison(Awesome Oscillator((high + low) / 2, shorter lookback, longer lookback) - its prior-bar value, 0, its threshold); selected comparison(RSI(close, lookback), SMA(RSI(close, same lookback), same lookback), its threshold); selected comparison(MACD line(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback), MACD signal(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, same signal lookback), its threshold); selected comparison(SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, lookback), K smoothing), SMA(SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, same lookback), same K smoothing), D smoothing), its threshold); selected comparison(Stochastic RSI %K(close, lookback, K smoothing), SMA(Stochastic RSI %K(close, same lookback, same K smoothing), D smoothing), its threshold); selected moving average(close, shorter lookback, its selected type) crosses above selected moving average(close, longer lookback, its separately selected type); Supertrend direction(factor, ATR lookback) crosses below zero). Each filter has its own enabled setting; each selected comparison has its own mode and threshold.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + one of the seven separately configured reward multiples × abs(entry price - stop level at entry) OR low <= stop level at entry.
