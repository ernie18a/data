Long: MACD line crosses its signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 AND low <= VWMA(close, lookback) AND Choppiness Index(lookback) < threshold AND ADX(lookback) > threshold AND no prior exit or bars since last exit >= cooldown length AND position is flat AND long direction is enabled
Short: MACD line crosses its signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 AND high >= VWMA(close, lookback) AND Choppiness Index(lookback) < threshold AND ADX(lookback) > threshold AND no prior exit or bars since last exit >= cooldown length AND position is flat AND short direction is enabled
Exit: Long, high >= entry price × (1 + entry-signal ATR percentage × take-profit multiplier / 100) OR low <= max(entry price × (1 − entry-signal ATR percentage × stop-loss multiplier / 100), entry price × (1 − hard-stop percentage / 100)); exit levels are fixed from fill price using ATR percentage captured on the entry signal bar
Exit: Short, low <= entry price × (1 − entry-signal ATR percentage × take-profit multiplier / 100) OR high >= min(entry price × (1 + entry-signal ATR percentage × stop-loss multiplier / 100), entry price × (1 + hard-stop percentage / 100)); exit levels are fixed from fill price using ATR percentage captured on the entry signal bar
