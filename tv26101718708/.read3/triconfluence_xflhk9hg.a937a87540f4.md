Calculation: opening-range high = highest high of chart bars in the T1 opening-range window
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest low of chart bars in the T1 opening-range window
Calculation: initial-balance high = highest high of chart bars in the T2 initial-balance window
Calculation: initial-balance low = lowest low of chart bars in the T2 initial-balance window
Long: opening and initial-balance ranges are set AND high crosses above opening-range high AND the first chart bar in the opening-range window closes above its open AND high crosses above initial-balance high
Short: opening and initial-balance ranges are set AND low crosses below opening-range low AND the first chart bar in the opening-range window closes at or below its open AND low crosses below initial-balance low
