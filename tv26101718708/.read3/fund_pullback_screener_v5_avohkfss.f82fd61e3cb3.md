Calculation: Pivot support = latest confirmed PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback)
Calculation: Pivot distance = (close - pivot support) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Calculation: SMA distance = (close - SMA(close, shorter lookback)) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Calculation: Trend = ((SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar) exists AND SMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar) exists AND close(T1 current bar) > SMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar) AND SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar) > SMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar)) OR (SMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar) unavailable AND close(T1 current bar) > SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar)); T1 values are sampled on chart bars from the current T1 bar with lookahead off, and T1 may be the chart timeframe
Calculation: Entry proximity = (pivot support exists AND pivot distance <= entry proximity in ATR AND pivot distance >= -allowed undercut in ATR) OR (SMA distance <= entry proximity in ATR AND SMA distance >= -allowed undercut in ATR)
Long: Trend AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= lower RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= upper RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND Entry proximity
Long: Trend AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < watch RSI threshold AND ((pivot support exists AND pivot distance <= watch proximity in ATR AND pivot distance >= -allowed undercut in ATR) OR (SMA distance <= watch proximity in ATR AND SMA distance >= -allowed undercut in ATR)) AND NOT (RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= lower RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= upper RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND Entry proximity)
