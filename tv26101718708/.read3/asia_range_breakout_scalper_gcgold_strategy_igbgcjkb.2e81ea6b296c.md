Calculation: Asia high = highest(high) on chart bars in the configured Asia session (New York time)
Calculation: Asia low = lowest(low) on chart bars in the configured Asia session (New York time)
Long: the latest Asia session has ended AND (session filter disabled OR the chart bar is in the configured trade window) AND close > Asia high + breakout buffer ticks × tick size AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND the configured daily trade limit has not been reached
Short: the latest Asia session has ended AND (session filter disabled OR the chart bar is in the configured trade window) AND close < Asia low − breakout buffer ticks × tick size AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND the configured daily trade limit has not been reached
Exit: long, (trailing disabled AND low <= entry price − stop ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback)) OR high >= entry price + take-profit ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) OR (session filter enabled AND close-at-trade-end enabled AND previous chart bar was in the trade window AND current chart bar is outside it)
Exit: short, (trailing disabled AND high >= entry price + stop ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback)) OR low <= entry price − take-profit ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) OR (session filter enabled AND close-at-trade-end enabled AND previous chart bar was in the trade window AND current chart bar is outside it)
