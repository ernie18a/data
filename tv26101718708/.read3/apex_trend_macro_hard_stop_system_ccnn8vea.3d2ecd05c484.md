Calculation: Long trailing stop = initialize at long entry to highest(close, ATR lookback) − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier; on subsequent bars, max(highest(close, ATR lookback) − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, previous long trailing stop)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initialize at short entry to lowest(close, ATR lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier; on subsequent bars, min(lowest(close, ATR lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, previous short trailing stop)
Long: position is flat AND close crosses above upper Bollinger Band(close, squeeze lookback, band multiplier) AND Squeeze Momentum(high, low, close, squeeze lookback) > 0 AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume factor AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback))
Short: position is flat AND close crosses below lower Bollinger Band(close, squeeze lookback, band multiplier) AND Squeeze Momentum(high, low, close, squeeze lookback) < 0 AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume factor AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) > ADX threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback))
Exit: Long, close crosses below long trailing stop OR (EMA filter enabled AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback))
Exit: Short, close crosses above short trailing stop OR (EMA filter enabled AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback))
