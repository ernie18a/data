Calculation: Bullish node = On chart bars, import PivotLow(low, pivot lookback on both sides) calculated on each configured other timeframe T1’s bars from its last completed bar; for each new pivot, merge its price into the last-created low-pivot node within ATR(ATR lookback) × node tolerance, keep that node’s price, increase strength up to the strength cap, and refresh its age, or create a strength-one node; remove nodes when chart-bar age exceeds the node-age limit; select the highest remaining node below close with strength ≥ minimum node strength.
Calculation: Bearish node = On chart bars, import PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback on both sides) calculated on each configured other timeframe T1’s bars from its last completed bar; for each new pivot, merge its price into the last-created high-pivot node within ATR(ATR lookback) × node tolerance, keep that node’s price, increase strength up to the strength cap, and refresh its age, or create a strength-one node; remove nodes when chart-bar age exceeds the node-age limit; select the lowest remaining node above close with strength ≥ minimum node strength.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a bullish node exists AND abs(low − bullish node) ≤ ATR(ATR lookback) × node tolerance AND (rejection filter disabled OR (high > low AND 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ rejection-wick percentage)) AND close > open
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a bearish node exists AND abs(high − bearish node) ≤ ATR(ATR lookback) × node tolerance AND (rejection filter disabled OR (high > low AND 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ rejection-wick percentage)) AND close < open
Calculation: Long stop at entry = bullish node at entry − ATR(ATR lookback) at entry × stop buffer
Calculation: Short stop at entry = bearish node at entry + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry × stop buffer
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar, low ≤ long stop at entry OR high ≥ entry price + abs(entry price − long stop at entry) × third-target risk multiple
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar, high ≥ short stop at entry OR low ≤ entry price − abs(short stop at entry − entry price) × third-target risk multiple
