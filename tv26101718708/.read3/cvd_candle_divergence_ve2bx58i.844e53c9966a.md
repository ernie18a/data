Calculation: CVD(T1, T2, session P1, timezone P2) = on each chart bar, start from the prior CVD close; on each T1 bar where the T2 session time changes, reset the running total and CVD open to a fresh session start; add T1 volume as positive when T1 close > T1 open, negative when T1 close < T1 open, otherwise positive when T1 close > prior T1 close, negative when T1 close < prior T1 close, and retain the prior sign when equal (initially positive); CVD close is the final running total after processing all available T1 bars in the chart bar, including a forming T1 bar
Long: chart close < chart open AND CVD(T1, T2, session P1, timezone P2) close > CVD(T1, T2, session P1, timezone P2) open
Short: chart close > chart open AND CVD(T1, T2, session P1, timezone P2) close < CVD(T1, T2, session P1, timezone P2) open
