Calculation: Watched swing highs = confirmed pivot highs of high using the same pivot-lookback parameter on the left and right, accepted when their distance from every watched swing high is at least ATR(ATR-lookback parameter) times the minimum-spacing parameter on the confirmation bar; remove a level after a qualifying high sweep or when its pivot age exceeds the maximum-age parameter.
Calculation: Watched swing lows = confirmed pivot lows of low using the same pivot-lookback parameter on the left and right, accepted when their distance from every watched swing low is at least ATR(ATR-lookback parameter) times the minimum-spacing parameter on the confirmation bar; remove a level after a qualifying low sweep or when its pivot age exceeds the maximum-age parameter.
Calculation: Qualifying high sweep = high > a watched swing high and close < that level, with volume > SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) * volume-multiplier parameter if the volume filter is enabled, and high - max(close, open) > 0 and high - max(close, open) >= abs(close - open) * wick/body parameter if the wick filter is enabled; remove each qualifying level, and use the sweep bar's high as its wick.
Calculation: Qualifying low sweep = low < a watched swing low and close > that level, with volume > SMA(volume, volume-lookback parameter) * volume-multiplier parameter if the volume filter is enabled, and min(close, open) - low > 0 and min(close, open) - low >= abs(close - open) * wick/body parameter if the wick filter is enabled; remove each qualifying level, and use the sweep bar's low as its wick.
Calculation: Long initial stop = low of the qualifying sweep bar - ATR(entry bar, ATR-lookback parameter) * stop-distance parameter.
Calculation: Long risk = entry price - Long initial stop.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + Long risk * reward:risk parameter.
Calculation: Short initial stop = high of the qualifying sweep bar + ATR(entry bar, ATR-lookback parameter) * stop-distance parameter.
Calculation: Short risk = Short initial stop - entry price.
Calculation: Short target = entry price - Short risk * reward:risk parameter.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price if breakeven management is enabled and, since entry, high has reached entry price + (Long target - entry price) * breakeven-trigger fraction parameter; otherwise Long initial stop.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price if breakeven management is enabled and, since entry, low has reached entry price - (entry price - Short target) * breakeven-trigger fraction parameter; otherwise Short initial stop.
Long: position is flat AND long entries are enabled AND (next-bar confirmation is disabled and a qualifying low sweep occurs on this chart bar, OR next-bar confirmation is enabled and a qualifying low sweep occurred on the previous chart bar while flat and close > (low[1] + close[1]) / 2) AND (session restriction is disabled or this chart bar is within the configured session window) AND Long risk >= ATR(entry bar, ATR-lookback parameter) * minimum-stop-distance parameter.
Short: position is flat AND short entries are enabled AND (next-bar confirmation is disabled and a qualifying high sweep occurs on this chart bar, OR next-bar confirmation is enabled and a qualifying high sweep occurred on the previous chart bar while flat and close < (high[1] + close[1]) / 2) AND (session restriction is disabled or this chart bar is within the configured session window) AND Short risk >= ATR(entry bar, ATR-lookback parameter) * minimum-stop-distance parameter.
Exit: long, low <= Long stop OR high >= Long target
Exit: short, high >= Short stop OR low <= Short target
