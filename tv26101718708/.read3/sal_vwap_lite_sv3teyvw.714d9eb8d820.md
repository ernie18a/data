Calculation: upper wick = high - max(open, close)
Calculation: lower wick = min(open, close) - low
Long: T1 close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed historical T1 bar or the still-forming realtime T1 bar AND (strict mode is off OR T2 close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed historical T2 bar or the still-forming realtime T2 bar) AND SMA(close, shorter lookback) > SMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > SMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) on chart bars AND upper wick[1] >= minimum prior-wick size AND RSI(close, lookback) > bullish RSI threshold
Short: T1 close <= VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed historical T1 bar or the still-forming realtime T1 bar AND (strict mode is off OR T2 close <= VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) calculated on T2 bars and sampled on chart bars from the last completed historical T2 bar or the still-forming realtime T2 bar) AND SMA(close, shorter lookback) < SMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < SMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3) on chart bars AND lower wick[1] >= minimum prior-wick size AND RSI(close, lookback) < bearish RSI threshold
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance
