Calculation: Stored zone pivots = confirmed PivotHigh(pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and PivotLow(pivot lookback, pivot lookback) values, newest first; when a new pivot is added to a list containing more than one item, remove items older than the zone-history limit.
Calculation: Zone width = ATR(ATR lookback) times the zone-width multiplier, or close times the zero-ATR width factor when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Bullish tested-zone break = clustered-zone engine enabled AND a bullish candle AND close > Highest(high, pivot lookback)[1] AND, among stored pivot highs, scan newest first to the first price >= close; use the preceding index as cutoff, index zero if the first price qualifies, or the last index if none qualifies; let the level be the maximum price through the cutoff; require cutoff index >= minimum pivot tests, at least minimum pivot tests through the cutoff with prices from level minus zone width through level, open <= level, and Highest(high, pivot lookback)[1] < level; the zone age begins at the oldest counted pivot.
Calculation: Bearish tested-zone break = clustered-zone engine enabled AND a bearish candle AND close < Lowest(low, pivot lookback)[1] AND, among stored pivot lows, scan newest first to the first price <= close; use the preceding index as cutoff, index zero if the first price qualifies, or the last index if none qualifies; let the level be the minimum price through the cutoff; require cutoff index >= minimum pivot tests, at least minimum pivot tests through the cutoff with prices from level through level plus zone width, open >= level, and Lowest(low, pivot lookback)[1] > level; the zone age begins at the oldest counted pivot.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = structure engine enabled AND the latest confirmed swing high is active AND (close > that swing high for close confirmation, otherwise high > that swing high) AND close > open; each same-direction entry consumes that swing high, and a later confirmed pivot high activates a new one.
Calculation: Bearish structure break = structure engine enabled AND the latest confirmed swing low is active AND (close < that swing low for close confirmation, otherwise low < that swing low) AND close < open; each same-direction entry consumes that swing low, and a later confirmed pivot low activates a new one.
Calculation: Bullish breakout level = bullish zone level when a bullish tested-zone break is valid, otherwise the latest confirmed swing high.
Calculation: Bearish breakout level = bearish zone level when a bearish tested-zone break is valid, otherwise the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: HTF aligned long = higher-timeframe filter disabled OR its value unavailable OR chart-bar close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar), with chart bars taking that T1 value.
Calculation: HTF aligned short = higher-timeframe filter disabled OR its value unavailable OR chart-bar close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar), with chart bars taking that T1 value.
Calculation: Breakout score(direction, tests, age, body ratio, range/ATR, volume ratio, short momentum, long momentum, price, clean distance, zone width, RSI, confluence, HTF alignment) = clamp the sum of the following components to the score floor and ceiling, then round to the score precision: test points are high-test points when tests >= high-test cutoff, middle-high-test points when tests == middle-high test count, middle-test points when tests == middle test count, otherwise base-test points; age points use the high, middle, and low age cutoffs and their respective points; body-ratio points use the high, middle, and low body-ratio cutoffs and their respective points; range/ATR points use the high, middle, and low range/ATR cutoffs and their respective points; volume-ratio points use the top, high, middle, and low volume-ratio cutoffs and their respective points; short momentum is close minus close[short momentum lookback] and long momentum is close minus close[long momentum lookback], each magnitude divided by price times the momentum normalization factor; if both align with direction, add the lesser of the two-momentum cap and their magnitudes times their respective two-momentum weights; if either aligns, add the lesser of the one-momentum cap and short-momentum magnitude times the one-momentum weight; otherwise subtract the momentum penalty; if clean distance > zone width times the clean-distance fraction and RSI(close, RSI lookback) aligns with direction using the upper RSI threshold for long or lower RSI threshold for short, add the clean-and-RSI points; if only clean distance qualifies, add the clean-only points; add the confluence points when both zone and structure breaks agree, and the HTF points when HTF aligned.
Calculation: Bullish strength = Breakout score(long, bullish zone test count or structure test-count baseline, bullish zone age or latest swing-high age, abs(close - open) / (high - low) or zero-range body fallback, (high - low) / ATR(ATR lookback) or zero-ATR range fallback, volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) or zero-average volume fallback, short momentum, long momentum, close, close - bullish breakout level, zone width, RSI(close, RSI lookback), bullish zone break AND bullish structure break, HTF aligned long).
Calculation: Bearish strength = Breakout score(short, bearish zone test count or structure test-count baseline, bearish zone age or latest swing-low age, abs(close - open) / (high - low) or zero-range body fallback, (high - low) / ATR(ATR lookback) or zero-ATR range fallback, volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback) or zero-average volume fallback, short momentum, long momentum, close, bearish breakout level - close, zone width, RSI(close, RSI lookback), bearish zone break AND bearish structure break, HTF aligned short).
Calculation: Bullish candidate = (bullish tested-zone break OR bullish structure break) AND bullish breakout level exists AND bullish strength >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR bullish strength >= strong-strength threshold) AND HTF aligned long AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior taken signal OR at least the cooldown number of chart bars since the prior taken signal) AND flat; evaluate at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Bearish candidate = (bearish tested-zone break OR bearish structure break) AND bearish breakout level exists AND bearish strength >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR bearish strength >= strong-strength threshold) AND HTF aligned short AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior taken signal OR at least the cooldown number of chart bars since the prior taken signal) AND flat; evaluate at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Long stop, fixed at entry = entry price minus clamp(abs(entry price - (bullish breakout level - zone width - ATR(ATR lookback) times stop-buffer multiplier)), ATR(ATR lookback) times minimum-risk multiplier, ATR(ATR lookback) times maximum-risk multiplier).
Calculation: Short stop, fixed at entry = entry price plus clamp(abs(entry price - (bearish breakout level + zone width + ATR(ATR lookback) times stop-buffer multiplier)), ATR(ATR lookback) times minimum-risk multiplier, ATR(ATR lookback) times maximum-risk multiplier).
Calculation: Long TP3, fixed at entry = entry price + (entry price - long stop) times TP3 R multiple.
Calculation: Short TP3, fixed at entry = entry price - (short stop - entry price) times TP3 R multiple.
Long: Bullish candidate AND (NOT bearish candidate OR bullish strength >= bearish strength).
Short: Bearish candidate AND (NOT bullish candidate OR bearish strength > bullish strength).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long TP3.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short TP3.
