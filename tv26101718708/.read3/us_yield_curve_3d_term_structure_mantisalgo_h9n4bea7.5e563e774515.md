Calculation: Treasury curve shift = unavailable unless the current and prior T1 closes of the 10Y and 2Y Treasury yields are positive; otherwise let d10 = current 10Y yield close − prior T1 10Y yield close, d2 = current 2Y yield close − prior T1 2Y yield close, and ds = d10 − d2; unchanged if abs(ds) < stability threshold; bearish steepening if ds > 0 and d10 ≥ d2; bullish steepening if ds > 0 and d10 < d2; bearish flattening if ds ≤ 0 and d2 ≥ d10; otherwise bullish flattening. Current T1 closes are sampled on chart bars and may be from a T1 bar still forming; prior closes are from the last completed T1 bar.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND Treasury curve shift changes from a different state on the prior chart bar to bullish steepening or bullish flattening.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND Treasury curve shift changes from a different state on the prior chart bar to bearish steepening or bearish flattening.
