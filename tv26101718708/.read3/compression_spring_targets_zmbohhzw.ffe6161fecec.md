Calculation: T1 range ratio = (high - low of the last completed T1 bar) / mean(high - low of the preceding comparison-lookback completed T1 bars), calculated on chart bars at each new T1 block when the comparison-lookback bars exist and both means are positive
Calculation: T1 volume ratio = max(sum(volume within the last completed T1 bar), 1) / mean(max(sum(volume within each of the preceding comparison-lookback completed T1 bars), 1)), calculated on chart bars at each new T1 block when the comparison-lookback bars exist and both means are positive
Calculation: Active spring state stores the count, highest high, and lowest low of completed T1 compression bars, where compression means T1 range ratio <= compression maximum and T1 volume ratio >= compression minimum; compression resets the gap count, non-compression increments it, breakout is evaluated against the state before updating it, and the state is discarded after a non-breakout bar makes the gap count exceed the maximum
Long: On chart bars at each new T1 block, active spring compression count >= minimum compression blocks AND the last completed T1 bar closes above active spring high in close mode or its high is above active spring high in wick mode AND T1 range ratio >= breakout range minimum AND (breakout volume filter is disabled OR T1 volume ratio >= breakout volume minimum)
Short: On chart bars at each new T1 block, active spring compression count >= minimum compression blocks AND the last completed T1 bar closes below active spring low in close mode or its low is below active spring low in wick mode AND T1 range ratio >= breakout range minimum AND (breakout volume filter is disabled OR T1 volume ratio >= breakout volume minimum)
