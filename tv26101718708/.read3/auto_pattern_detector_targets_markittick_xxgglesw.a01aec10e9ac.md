Calculation: Pivot high/low sequences = on each bar, record high[Pivot-left lookback] when it is strictly greater than every other high at offsets 1 through twice that lookback, and record low[Pivot-left lookback] when it is strictly lower than every other low at those offsets; assign each pivot index as current bar index minus the separate Pivot-right lookback; order each sequence newest detected first.
Calculation: Projection((i1,p1),(i2,p2),i) = p1 when i1=i2, otherwise p1+(p2-p1)*(i-i1)/(i2-i1).
Calculation: Pivot slope(older,newer) = 0 when their indices match, otherwise (newer price-older price)/max(1,newer index-older index).
Calculation: Near(a,b,tolerance) = abs(a-b) <= ((a+b)/2)*tolerance.
Calculation: Size valid(height) = height > max(close*minimum-size percentage, ATR(ATR lookback)*minimum ATR multiple).
Calculation: Stop(anchor,long,E,T) = E-abs(T-E)*target-distance stop fraction when target-based stops are enabled, otherwise anchor-ATR(current bar)*stop buffer; Stop(anchor,short,E,T) = E+abs(T-E)*target-distance stop fraction when target-based stops are enabled, otherwise anchor+ATR(current bar)*stop buffer.
Calculation: BreakUp(B) = the earliest matching bar, scanning from older to newer, among the previous max(scan lookback,pivot-right lookback)+scan padding bars, with offset at least one, where (close if close-only breakout is enabled, otherwise high) exceeds B at that bar plus (ATR(ATR lookback) at that bar*ATR breakout multiple if ATR breakout is enabled, otherwise B*percentage breakout threshold); if full-body breakout is enabled and unconfirmed-pattern display is disabled, that bar's open and close must also exceed the threshold; unless unconfirmed-pattern display is enabled, each of the following confirmation-count minus one bars must close above its own projected B plus margin.
Calculation: BreakDown(B) = the earliest matching bar, scanning from older to newer, among the previous max(scan lookback,pivot-right lookback)+scan padding bars, with offset at least one, where (close if close-only breakout is enabled, otherwise low) is below B at that bar minus (ATR(ATR lookback) at that bar*ATR breakout multiple if ATR breakout is enabled, otherwise B*percentage breakout threshold); if full-body breakout is enabled and unconfirmed-pattern display is disabled, that bar's open and close must also be below the threshold; unless unconfirmed-pattern display is enabled, each of the following confirmation-count minus one bars must close below its own projected B minus margin.
Calculation: Pattern accepted(E,S,T,b) = unconfirmed-pattern display is enabled OR ((volume spike is not required OR volume at b > SMA(volume,max(1,min(volume lookback,available bars))) at b*volume multiplier) AND abs(E-S)>0 AND max(0,abs(T-E)-(slippage ticks*minimum price tick+E*commission percentage/100*2))/abs(E-S) >= minimum risk-reward). E is the open of breakout bar b.
Calculation: Pattern precedence = double top, double bottom, triple top, triple bottom, head-and-shoulders, inverse head-and-shoulders, bullish flag or pennant, bearish flag or pennant, falling wedge, rising wedge, bullish triangle, bearish triangle, bullish rectangle, bearish rectangle, cup-and-handle, inverse cup-and-handle.
Calculation: First eligible(P) = P is enabled and no higher-priority enabled pattern in Pattern precedence passes its structure, breakout, size, and validation conditions.
Calculation: Mark gate(start) = bar index exceeds the history-gate threshold AND at least two pivot highs and two pivot lows exist AND the current bar is confirmed AND (no prior marked start exists OR abs(start-prior marked start)>pattern cooldown).
