Calculation: Bar scenario = inside when the bar breaks neither prior high nor prior low, 2u when it breaks only the prior high, 2d when it breaks only the prior low, and outside when it breaks both; equality counts as a break or non-break according to the equality parameter.
Calculation: Timeframe state = bar scenario, close-versus-open direction, and whether close is above the prior high or below the prior low, calculated on each timeframe’s bars and sampled on chart bars; omit timeframes below the chart timeframe; for higher timeframes use the last completed bar when higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, otherwise the bar still forming; use the current chart bar for an equal timeframe. T1 and T2 are the first two rows, T3 is the decision row, and T4 is the fourth row.
Calculation: Regime leaning = bullish when at least one timeframe is readable and either all readable timeframes close above open, or at least three are readable, exactly one does not close above open, and every readable T1/T2 closes above open; bearish under the symmetric conditions; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Regime grade direction = regime leaning, except neutral when readable T3 is an inside bar or readable T1 and T2 have opposite bullish/bearish close-versus-open directions.
Calculation: Conditional regime direction = regime grade direction after replacing the chart-timeframe row, when it matches a row, with a hypothetical break state for the specified side: 2u, bullish close-versus-open, and bullish In Force for up; 2d, bearish close-versus-open, and bearish In Force for down.
Calculation: ATR = SMA(TrueRange, ATR lookback) on the selected ATR timeframe T5, using its last completed bar and sampling it on chart bars; use chart bars when T5 is below the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Setup risk in ATR = selected inside-bar range divided by ATR plus the structural buffer multiplier in structural-stop mode; use the trigger-distance multiplier in ATR-from-trigger mode.
Calculation: Inside-bar setup = an inside bar under the configured equality rule, with trigger generation enabled, ATR available, and risk filter disabled or setup risk in ATR at or below its limit; require the prior bar to be outside when 3-1-only is enabled; require a completed bar when closed-bar-only is enabled; anchor to the latest inside bar or the first inside bar of a consecutive run according to the anchor parameter. The selected bar’s high and low are the trigger levels. The long stop is its low minus the structural buffer times ATR, or its high minus the trigger-distance multiplier times ATR; the short stop is its high plus the structural buffer times ATR, or its low plus the trigger-distance multiplier times ATR.
Calculation: Relative strength(first lookback) = ((close / close[first lookback]) / (benchmark close / benchmark close[first lookback]) - 1) * 100 on chart bars, using the current chart bar and benchmark closes sampled to chart bars.
Calculation: Trigger break = up when a watched setup’s close is above its high trigger on a completed bar in close mode, or the bar’s high is above it in wick mode; down is symmetric. In wick mode, if both triggers are crossed, use the close to select a side only when it closes beyond that side’s trigger; otherwise no break resolves.
Long: Regime leaning is bullish.
Short: Regime leaning is bearish.
Long: Regime grade direction is bullish.
Short: Regime grade direction is bearish.
Long: The chart timeframe matches a T1–T4 row and conditional regime direction for an up break is bullish.
Short: The chart timeframe matches a T1–T4 row and conditional regime direction for a down break is bearish.
Long: A setup is being watched and trigger break is up and closed-bar-only markings are disabled or the current bar is complete.
Short: A setup is being watched and trigger break is down and closed-bar-only markings are disabled or the current bar is complete.
Long: A setup is being watched and trigger break is up and the regime alert filter is disabled or regime leaning is bullish, and the relative-strength filter is disabled or relative strength is disabled or benchmark is the traded instrument or relative strength(first lookback) > 0.
Short: A setup is being watched and trigger break is down and the regime alert filter is disabled or regime leaning is bearish, and the relative-strength filter is disabled or relative strength is disabled or benchmark is the traded instrument or relative strength(first lookback) < 0.
Exit: long, low <= long stop after the break bar, or stop is not hit and targets are enabled and target 1 has not been reached and high >= entry price + target 1 multiple * initial risk, or stop is not hit and no target has been reached and a completed bar closes below the high trigger.
Exit: short, high >= short stop after the break bar, or stop is not hit and targets are enabled and target 1 has not been reached and low <= entry price - target 1 multiple * initial risk, or stop is not hit and no target has been reached and a completed bar closes above the low trigger.
