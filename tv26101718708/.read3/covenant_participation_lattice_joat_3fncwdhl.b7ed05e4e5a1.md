Calculation: Core axis = stage 3, where volume-profile POC assigns each bar's volume over a rolling lookback to the row containing its close across the rolling high-low range divided into a parameterized row count, with row width at least the minimum price increment; stage 1 = EMA(prior stage 1 + clamp(POC - prior stage 1, -ATR(lookback) × stability-band parameter, ATR(lookback) × stability-band parameter), smoothing lookback), initialized to the first POC; stage 2 = stage 1 if |stage 1 - prior stage 2| > ATR(lookback) × stability-band parameter, else prior stage 2; stage 3 = stage 2 if |stage 2 - prior stage 3| > ATR(lookback) × stability-band parameter, else prior stage 3; stages 2 and 3 are initialized to stage 1.
Calculation: Clamped ATR deviation = clamp((close - core axis) / max(ATR(lookback), minimum price increment), -deviation-limit parameter, deviation-limit parameter)
Calculation: Value midpoint = (volume-profile value-area high + volume-profile value-area low) / 2
Calculation: Balance tilt = volume share of profile rows above the value-area high minus volume share of profile rows below the value-area low; both shares are zero when total profile volume is zero
Long: alerts enabled AND chart bar confirmed AND (close crosses above core axis OR clamped ATR deviation >= deviation-limit parameter × threshold-fraction parameter OR close crosses above volume-profile value-area high OR close crosses above value midpoint OR balance tilt crosses above zero)
Short: alerts enabled AND chart bar confirmed AND (close crosses below core axis OR clamped ATR deviation <= -deviation-limit parameter × threshold-fraction parameter OR close crosses below volume-profile value-area low OR close crosses below value midpoint OR balance tilt crosses below zero)
