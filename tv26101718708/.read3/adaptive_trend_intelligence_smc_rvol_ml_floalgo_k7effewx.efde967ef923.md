Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend = SuperTrend(hl2, ATR(long lookback), adaptive multiplier); adaptive multiplier is the base multiplier scaled by ATR(short lookback)/ATR(long lookback) and bounded by separate lower and upper multiplier bounds when adaptation is enabled, otherwise it is the base multiplier; the short lookback is half the long lookback subject to a minimum lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed SuperTrend direction = SuperTrend direction after a direction change persists until the noise-filter count reaches its lookback; a zero noise-filter lookback confirms the change immediately.
Calculation: Directional volume percentile = min(percentile cap, directional volume / rolling median(directional volume, lookback) × percentile scale); bullish directional volume = volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick), bearish directional volume = volume × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick), and the percentile defaults to the neutral percentile when its median is not positive.
Calculation: SMC swing chain and direction = smoothed Stochastic %K identifies a high pivot when it crosses below the overbought level after tracking the highest high while above that level, and a low pivot when it crosses above the oversold level after tracking the lowest low while below it; accept alternating pivots, update a same-side high only with a higher pivot high and a same-side low only with a lower pivot low, classify each high as HH if above the prior accepted high and LH otherwise, and each low as HL if above the prior accepted low and LL otherwise; direction becomes bullish when the latest high and low are HH and HL, bearish when they are LH and LL, and otherwise retains its prior direction, initially neutral.
Calculation: SMC break levels = after an accepted or updated pivot, in bullish SMC direction set the prior high as the pending BOS level and the prior low as the pending CHoCH level; in bearish direction set the prior low as the pending BOS level and the prior high as the pending CHoCH level; a close beyond a pending level triggers its event once and clears it, and a CHoCH reverses SMC direction and clears both levels.
Calculation: Online ML probability = sigmoid of the weighted sum plus intercept from separate bullish and bearish online logistic models using normalized candle body, upper and lower wick, range, volume, consecutive candle direction, delta volume, signed-body momentum, ATR slope, volume trend, SuperTrend distance, trend duration, RSI, Bollinger position, directional volume percentile, and SMC bias; update weights from resolved prior flip outcomes using a resolved-sample window, recency decay, learning rate, training epochs, L2 regularization, and weight clipping; probability is unavailable until the minimum resolved-sample count. These model settings, the feature lookback, and signal-history limit are separate parameters.
Long: Adaptive SuperTrend direction is bullish.
Short: Adaptive SuperTrend direction is bearish.
Long: Confirmed SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (RVOL filter disabled OR volume is positive AND sufficient bars exist for the RVOL lookback AND bullish directional volume percentile ≥ minimum percentile) AND (ML probability unavailable OR ML probability ≥ minimum probability) AND (SMC filter disabled OR SMC direction is neutral or bullish).
Short: Confirmed SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (RVOL filter disabled OR volume is positive AND sufficient bars exist for the RVOL lookback AND bearish directional volume percentile ≥ minimum percentile) AND (ML probability unavailable OR ML probability ≥ minimum probability) AND (SMC filter disabled OR SMC direction is neutral or bearish).
Long: An accepted high pivot is HH (no prior accepted high OR pivot high > prior accepted high).
Short: An accepted high pivot is LH (pivot high ≤ prior accepted high).
Long: An accepted low pivot is HL (no prior accepted low OR pivot low > prior accepted low).
Short: An accepted low pivot is LL (pivot low ≤ prior accepted low).
Long: On a confirmed bar, bullish SMC direction AND close > pending BOS level.
Short: On a confirmed bar, bearish SMC direction AND close < pending BOS level.
Long: On a confirmed bar, bearish SMC direction AND close > pending CHoCH level.
Short: On a confirmed bar, bullish SMC direction AND close < pending CHoCH level.
