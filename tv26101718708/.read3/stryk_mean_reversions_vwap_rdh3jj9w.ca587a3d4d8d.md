Calculation: Selected VWAP = VWAP(source, selected session or calendar anchor, or rolling T3-duration window of chart bars).
Calculation: VWAP σ = sqrt(VWAP(source², same anchor/window) − selected VWAP²), using chart bars.
Calculation: Volume delta = footprint buy volume − sell volume when available; otherwise sum lower-timeframe T4 volume signed positive for up closes, negative for down closes, and zero for equal closes; if no T4 bars are available, sign chart-bar volume by close versus open, then versus prior close on ties.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the recent-session average for the same time-of-day slot, falling back to a moving average of chart-bar volume until enough slot history is available.
Calculation: T1 delta share = cumulative volume delta divided by cumulative volume in the current forming T1 candle, calculated from chart bars and read on chart bars.
Calculation: Profile POC and value area = distribute each bar's volume across price bins spanning its rounded high-low range, using a parameterized bin width and slice cap; POC is the highest-volume bin, and value area grows from POC by adding the heavier adjacent bin until the parameterized volume share is covered, with ties choosing the upper bin.
Calculation: T1 profile = profile of the last N T1 candles including the current forming candle, calculated on T1 bars and read on chart bars.
Calculation: Delta% color value = sign(x) × min(1, |x| × calibration × volume gate × streak multiplier), where x is T1 delta share or its parameterized blend with chart-bar delta share; calibration is signed percentile rank of |x| or scaled and clipped x, the volume gate tapers at a volume-percentile floor, and the streak multiplier boosts repeated same-direction high-rank confirmed bars.
