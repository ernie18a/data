Calculation: Macro segment chain = Confirm PivotHigh(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback); initialize from the first confirmed pivot; accept a later pivot only if its type is opposite the last accepted pivot, its pivot bar is at least minimum segment bars later, and its price differs by at least minimum segment move ATR × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) on the detection bar; otherwise retain the last accepted pivot. Each completed segment joins successive accepted pivots, with direction up from low to high or down from high to low. The active segment starts at the last accepted pivot, has direction opposite its pivot type, and has age equal to current bar index minus pivot bar; its endpoint is the current close or previous close in closed-bar mode.
Calculation: Active macro channel = From the active segment start, calculate the slope to its endpoint and project the basis line to the current bar; calculate the maximum positive distance of sampled segment highs or lows from the basis, sampling every max(1, ceiling(segment length / deviation sample cap)) bars; set channel half-width to max(channel deviation multiplier × maximum distance, minimum band ATR × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)); upper and lower bands are projected basis plus and minus half-width.
Calculation: Micro regression channel = Fit a least-squares line to close over the regression lookback on chart bars, with observations ordered oldest to newest; basis is the fitted value at the current bar, residual standard deviation is square root of SSE / (regression lookback − 2), upper and lower bands are basis plus and minus regression deviation multiplier × residual standard deviation, R² is the coefficient of determination, and angle is atan(regression slope / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)) × 180 / π.
Calculation: Micro direction = Up when Micro regression channel angle exceeds the micro direction threshold, down when it is below the negative threshold, and flat otherwise; the displayed direction requires Micro regression channel R² ≥ minimum R².
Calculation: Trend bias = From the last up to bias segment count completed segments plus the active segment, sum each up and down segment's duration in bars × max(abs(segment angle), angle weight floor) into up and down weights; up ratio = up weight / total weight. Net move ATR = (newest price − oldest price) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), using the first included completed segment's start and last included completed segment's end, replacing newest price with current close when an active segment exists and using its start as oldest price if no completed segment is included. If total weight is zero, bias is Neutral; otherwise, in order: Strong Bull if net move ATR > strong net move threshold and up ratio ≥ strong bull override ratio, Strong Bear if net move ATR < negative strong net move threshold and up ratio ≤ strong bear override ratio, Strong Bull if up ratio ≥ strong bull ratio, Bullish if up ratio ≥ bull ratio or (net move ATR > bull net move threshold and up ratio ≥ bull net override ratio), Strong Bear if up ratio ≤ strong bear ratio, Bearish if up ratio ≤ bear ratio or (net move ATR < negative bear net move threshold and up ratio ≤ bear net override ratio), else Mixed.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe macro direction = Active direction from Macro segment chain computed on T1 and T2 bars and sampled on chart bars from each timeframe's last completed bar; T2 is longer than T1.
Long: An active Macro segment chain segment exists AND its active direction is up
Short: An active Macro segment chain segment exists AND its active direction is down
Long: Micro regression channel is visible AND Micro direction is up
Short: Micro regression channel is visible AND Micro direction is down
Long: Active macro direction is up AND Micro direction is up OR active macro direction is down AND Micro direction is up
Short: Active macro direction is down AND Micro direction is down OR active macro direction is up AND Micro direction is down
Long: Higher-timeframe macro direction on T1 is up OR higher-timeframe macro direction on T2 is up
Short: Higher-timeframe macro direction on T1 is down OR higher-timeframe macro direction on T2 is down
Long: Trend bias is Strong Bull OR Bullish
Short: Trend bias is Strong Bear OR Bearish
Long: An active macro segment exists AND active macro direction is down AND close crosses above the active macro upper band
Short: An active macro segment exists AND active macro direction is up AND close crosses below the active macro lower band
Long: Active macro direction is up AND close is between the active macro lower band and that band plus pullback ATR fraction × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) AND this condition was false on the prior bar
Short: Active macro direction is down AND close is between the active macro upper band minus pullback ATR fraction × ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) and that band AND this condition was false on the prior bar

Short: Active macro direction is down AND close crosses below the Micro regression channel lower band AND Micro regression channel R² ≥ minimum R² AND active segment age is between early segment minimum age and early segment maximum age
