Calculation: Order-block pattern = on chart bars, for lookback L ≥ 2, bullish when close[L−1] < open[L−1] and close[i] ≥ open[i] for every i from 0 to L−2; bearish when close[L−1] > open[L−1] and close[i] ≤ open[i] for every i from 0 to L−2; no pattern for L = 1.
Calculation: Order-block max-volume zone = for each confirmed chart-bar pattern after at least L+1 chart bars exist, use the oldest pattern bar’s high and low as the block range; divide it into grid-count equal bins and sum, per bin, each completed T1 intrabar’s volume multiplied by the fraction of its high-low range overlapping the bin. This runs on chart bars using T1 intrabars within the oldest pattern bar. The zone is the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest-price bin on ties; retain no block if total bin volume is zero. A block remains active through the chart bar on which it is checked for removal, then is removed on a confirmed bar if the selected mitigation mode’s source (close, or low for bullish/high for bearish) is below the bullish threshold high − p × (high − low), or above the bearish threshold low + p × (high − low), respectively, where p is the selected mitigation fraction; also remove the oldest blocks when count exceeds floor(block-capacity parameter / grid-count) + 1.
Long: confirmed bullish order-block pattern after at least lookback + 1 chart bars exist OR an active bullish order block exists AND close is within its max-volume zone OR an active bullish order block exists AND the chart bar is confirmed AND low ≤ the zone bottom AND close ≥ the zone bottom.
Short: confirmed bearish order-block pattern after at least lookback + 1 chart bars exist OR an active bearish order block exists AND close is within its max-volume zone OR an active bearish order block exists AND the chart bar is confirmed AND high ≥ the zone top AND close ≤ the zone top.
