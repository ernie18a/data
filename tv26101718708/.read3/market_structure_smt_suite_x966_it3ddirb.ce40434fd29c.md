Calculation: NormalHighs(T, lookback) = highs of the last lookback completed T bars that are greater than every newer completed T-bar high.
Calculation: NormalLows(T, lookback) = lows of the last lookback completed T bars that are lower than every newer completed T-bar low.
Calculation: BearishCISD(T2, delivery gap, run scan limit, minimum delivery count) = the highest open in the most recent contiguous bearish-delivery run found by scanning back from the last completed T2 bar, with its first member within the delivery gap; genuine bearish bars count, and an inside bar joins the run only when adjacent to a genuine bearish bar; valid only when the genuine-bar count meets the minimum delivery count.
Calculation: BullishCISD(T2, delivery gap, run scan limit, minimum delivery count) = the lowest open in the most recent contiguous bullish-delivery run found by scanning back from the last completed T2 bar, with its first member within the delivery gap; genuine bullish bars count, and an inside bar joins the run only when adjacent to a genuine bullish bar; valid only when the genuine-bar count meets the minimum delivery count.
Calculation: PriorPivotHigh(T2, delivery-run end, swing strength, swing search) = the nearest older T2 pivot high within the swing search, with a high greater than the swing-strength newer highs and greater than or equal to the swing-strength older highs.
Calculation: PriorPivotLow(T2, delivery-run end, swing strength, swing search) = the nearest older T2 pivot low within the swing search, with a low lower than the swing-strength newer lows and lower than or equal to the swing-strength older lows.
Calculation: BullishCandidate(T1, T2, T3) = per normal low from completed T1 bars, create a candidate when the last completed T2 bar's selected sweep measure is below the low minus sweep tolerance and the preceding completed T2 bar's measure was at or above that threshold; record the sweep low and the containing T3 window; a later qualifying violation that makes the low lower resets its extreme and CISD stage. Expire after the effective candidate lifetime from the first sweep, after the effective reclaim lifetime while awaiting CISD, or after the effective swing-break lifetime from CISD confirmation; consume the reference when the candidate is accepted. A relaxed competition-window confirmation timeframe may fill missing structure after a later bar.
Calculation: BearishCandidate(T1, T2, T3) = per normal high from completed T1 bars, create a candidate when the last completed T2 bar's selected sweep measure is above the high plus sweep tolerance and the preceding completed T2 bar's measure was at or below that threshold; record the sweep high and the containing T3 window; a later qualifying violation that makes the high higher resets its extreme and CISD stage. Expire after the effective candidate lifetime from the first sweep, after the effective reclaim lifetime while awaiting CISD, or after the effective swing-break lifetime from CISD confirmation; consume the reference when the candidate is accepted. A relaxed competition-window confirmation timeframe may fill missing structure after a later bar.
Calculation: DeliveryQuality = max(0, 1 - (first delivery offset - one-bar offset) * gap-quality penalty) + max(0, 1 - abs(genuine delivery count - target count) * count-quality penalty).
Calculation: CandidateScore = DeliveryQuality * delivery weight + normalized sweep depth * sweep weight + normalized CISD displacement * CISD weight + normalized swing displacement * swing weight + normalized projection range * range weight; normalize by ATR(T2, ATR lookback, last completed bar) when positive, otherwise by max(instrument tick size, projection range).
Calculation: CompetitionAccepted(candidate) = protected-extremes mode is enabled, or no winner is recorded for its T3 window, or that window is still open and the candidate score exceeds its recorded winner score.
