Calculation: Bullish gap pullback = the current bar pulls into the latest unconsumed bullish gap formed on an earlier chart bar, no older than the gap-lifetime lookback; the formation bar has low > high[gap-separation lookback] AND low - high[gap-separation lookback] >= minimum gap ATR multiple * ATR(shared indicator lookback), its lower boundary is that formation bar's high[gap-separation lookback] and its upper boundary is that formation bar's low, and the current bar has low <= the gap's upper boundary AND close > open AND close > the gap's lower boundary
Calculation: Bearish gap pullback = the current bar pulls into the latest unconsumed bearish gap formed on an earlier chart bar, no older than the gap-lifetime lookback; the formation bar has high < low[gap-separation lookback] AND low[gap-separation lookback] - high >= minimum gap ATR multiple * ATR(shared indicator lookback), its lower boundary is that formation bar's high and its upper boundary is that formation bar's low[gap-separation lookback], and the current bar has high >= the gap's lower boundary AND close < open AND close < the gap's upper boundary
Calculation: Long signal = (gap-pullback method selected AND bullish gap pullback OR trend method selected AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) AND RSI(close, shared indicator lookback) > RSI threshold, with this trend condition false on the previous chart bar) AND (selected sensitivity bypasses filtering OR (close > EMA(close, filter EMA lookback) AND (selected sensitivity requires only the chart-timeframe filter OR (on chart bars, close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, filter EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND ADX(shared indicator lookback, shared indicator lookback) >= ADX threshold))))
Calculation: Entry gate = chart timeframe satisfies the configured timeframe lock AND chart bar is confirmed AND chart bar opens inside at least one enabled session on an enabled weekday AND (session-end closing is disabled OR the bar closes inside that same session with more than one chart-bar duration remaining before its end) AND bar close time is outside every configured news interval from event time minus blackout duration inclusive to event time plus blackout duration exclusive
Calculation: Session/news close = (session-end closing is enabled AND chart bar opens inside an enabled session AND closes outside that session) OR (bar close time is within a configured news interval and the previous chart bar's close time was outside all configured news intervals)
Calculation: Take-profit distance = round(take-profit pips * points per pip) * instrument minimum tick
Calculation: Stop-loss distance = round(stop-loss pips * points per pip) * instrument minimum tick
Long: position is flat or short AND Entry gate AND Long signal
Short: position is flat or long AND Entry gate AND (gap-pullback method selected AND bearish gap pullback OR trend method selected AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) AND RSI(close, shared indicator lookback) < RSI threshold, with this trend condition false on the previous chart bar) AND (selected sensitivity bypasses filtering OR (close < EMA(close, filter EMA lookback) AND (selected sensitivity requires only the chart-timeframe filter OR (on chart bars, close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, filter EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND ADX(shared indicator lookback, shared indicator lookback) >= ADX threshold)))) AND NOT Long signal
Exit: long, high >= entry price + Take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - Stop-loss distance OR Session/news close
Exit: short, low <= entry price - Take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + Stop-loss distance OR Session/news close
