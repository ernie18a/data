Calculation: Seasonal return samples = On chart bars at each T1 boundary, append the percentage return of the last completed T1 bar when its calendar month is the current calendar month and current year minus its year is at most the year-span parameter.
Calculation: Seasonal win rate = 100 × the number of positive values among the most recent at most sample-count-parameter seasonal returns, divided by their count; use 0 when there are no returns.
Calculation: Seasonal profit factor = sum of positive values among the most recent at most sample-count-parameter seasonal returns divided by the sum of the absolute values of nonpositive returns; when that denominator is 0, use the sum of positive returns if positive, otherwise 1.
Long: Bollinger Bandwidth(close, lookback, deviation-multiplier) on T2 bars using the still-forming T2 bar, read on chart bars (use 0 if the T2 SMA is unavailable or 0), ≥ minimum bandwidth AND Seasonal win rate ≥ minimum win rate AND Seasonal profit factor ≥ minimum profit factor
