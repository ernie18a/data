Calculation: effective tick size = instrument minimum tick or configured manual tick size
Calculation: candle range = high - low
Calculation: candle body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: upper wick = high - max(open, close)
Calculation: lower wick = min(open, close) - low
Calculation: wick body = max(candle body, effective tick size)
Calculation: small body = candle range > 0 AND candle body <= maximum body fraction * candle range
Calculation: bullish reversal candle = (enabled Hammer: small body AND lower wick >= dominant-wick/body parameter * wick body AND lower wick >= dominant-wick/range parameter * candle range AND upper wick <= opposite-wick/body parameter * wick body; OR enabled Bullish Spinning Top: small body AND both wicks >= spinning-wick/body parameter * wick body; OR enabled Bullish Engulfing: close > open AND prior close < prior open AND close >= prior open AND open <= prior close) AND (bullish close not required OR close >= open)
Calculation: bearish reversal candle = (enabled Shooting Star/Inverted Hammer: small body AND upper wick >= dominant-wick/body parameter * wick body AND upper wick >= dominant-wick/range parameter * candle range AND lower wick <= opposite-wick/body parameter * wick body; OR enabled Bearish Spinning Top: small body AND both wicks >= spinning-wick/body parameter * wick body; OR enabled Bearish Engulfing: close < open AND prior close > prior open AND close <= prior open AND open >= prior close) AND (bearish close not required OR close <= open)
Calculation: downward context = context mode is off, or (EMA context selected and close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND that EMA < its value at the slope lookback), or (swing context selected and low <= lowest(low, swing lookback)); either mode uses the OR of the EMA and swing conditions
Calculation: upward context = context mode is off, or (EMA context selected and close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND that EMA > its value at the slope lookback), or (swing context selected and high >= highest(high, swing lookback)); either mode uses the OR of the EMA and swing conditions
Calculation: long reversal = bullish reversal candle AND downward context AND NOT bearish reversal candle
Calculation: short reversal = bearish reversal candle AND upward context AND NOT bullish reversal candle
Calculation: long entry level = prior chart bar high or reversal bar high, as selected
Calculation: short entry level = prior chart bar low or reversal bar low, as selected
Calculation: long stop = reversal low - stop buffer * effective tick size, or long entry level - fixed stop distance * effective tick size, as selected by stop basis
Calculation: short stop = reversal high + stop buffer * effective tick size, or short entry level + fixed stop distance * effective tick size, as selected by stop basis
Calculation: long target = long entry level + target-R parameter * (long entry level - long stop), or long entry level + candle range, as selected by target basis
Calculation: short target = short entry level - target-R parameter * (short stop - short entry level), or short entry level - candle range, as selected by target basis
Calculation: entry eligibility = no open position or pending entry AND trade cap disabled or not reached (filled trades counted and reset at each T2 boundary) AND cooldown disabled or elapsed since last close AND entry window disabled or current time inside it AND force-flat window disabled or current time outside it AND bar-close-only disabled or chart bar confirmed AND no stop/target exit already latched this chart bar
Calculation: long setup = long reversal AND long side permitted AND each enabled filter passes: HTF filter uses the last completed T1 bar on chart bars, with separate fast and slow EMA lookbacks; trade-agreement requires fast EMA > slow EMA, reversal-agreement requires fast EMA <= slow EMA; structure requires long entry level > highest(high, structure lookback) shifted back by the exclusion lookback; RSI(close, RSI lookback) <= oversold threshold; stretch requires close <= selected SMA or EMA(close, stretch lookback) - minimum stretch * ATR(ATR lookback, T2, last completed bar), with T2 values taken on chart bars
Calculation: short setup = short reversal AND short side permitted AND each enabled filter passes: HTF filter uses the last completed T1 bar on chart bars, with separate fast and slow EMA lookbacks; trade-agreement requires fast EMA <= slow EMA, reversal-agreement requires fast EMA > slow EMA; structure requires short entry level < lowest(low, structure lookback) shifted back by the exclusion lookback; RSI(close, RSI lookback) >= overbought threshold; stretch requires close >= selected SMA or EMA(close, stretch lookback) + minimum stretch * ATR(ATR lookback, T2, last completed bar), with T2 values taken on chart bars
Long: long side permitted AND long reversal
Short: short side permitted AND short reversal
Long: long setup AND entry eligibility
Short: short setup AND entry eligibility
Long: pending long stop entry from a long setup registered while entry eligibility held, long entry level > long stop, and no-chase is disabled or the last winning trade is not a long within its chase window or long entry level < that trade's exit; on a later chart bar within the validity window, a processed sub-bar high >= long entry level
Short: pending short stop entry from a short setup registered while entry eligibility held, short entry level < short stop, and no-chase is disabled or the last winning trade is not a short within its chase window or short entry level > that trade's exit; on a later chart bar within the validity window, a processed sub-bar low <= short entry level
Exit: long, the first chronological processed sub-bar has low <= long stop or high >= long target; if both occur in one sub-bar, use the configured stop-first or target-first priority; OR elapsed chart bars since entry >= maximum hold; OR the chart bar is in the enabled force-flat window
Exit: short, the first chronological processed sub-bar has high >= short stop or low <= short target; if both occur in one sub-bar, use the configured stop-first or target-first priority; OR elapsed chart bars since entry >= maximum hold; OR the chart bar is in the enabled force-flat window
Long: when intrabar resolution is enabled, process available sub-bars of the current chart bar chronologically, using T3 then T4 then T5 and falling back to chart-bar OHLC; when disabled, use chart-bar OHLC; process at chart-bar close if bar-close-only is enabled
Short: when intrabar resolution is enabled, process available sub-bars of the current chart bar chronologically, using T3 then T4 then T5 and falling back to chart-bar OHLC; when disabled, use chart-bar OHLC; process at chart-bar close if bar-close-only is enabled
