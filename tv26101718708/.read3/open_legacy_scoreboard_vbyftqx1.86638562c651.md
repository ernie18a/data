Calculation: Bullish FVG pullback = the current bar is later than, and within the selected sensitivity’s maximum-age parameter of, the most recent unconsumed bullish gap; that gap formed when low > high[2] and low - high[2] >= the selected sensitivity’s minimum-gap ATR-multiple parameter × ATR(ATR lookback); current low <= the gap’s upper boundary, close > open, and close > its lower boundary; consume the gap after a signal and replace it with each newly formed bullish gap.
Calculation: Bearish FVG pullback = the current bar is later than, and within the selected sensitivity’s maximum-age parameter of, the most recent unconsumed bearish gap; that gap formed when high < low[2] and low[2] - high >= the selected sensitivity’s minimum-gap ATR-multiple parameter × ATR(ATR lookback); current high >= the gap’s lower boundary, close < open, and close < its upper boundary; consume the gap after a signal and replace it with each newly formed bearish gap.
Calculation: Bullish EMA/RSI trend regime = EMA(close, fast lookback for selected sensitivity) > EMA(close, slow lookback for selected sensitivity) AND close > EMA(close, fast lookback for selected sensitivity) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Bearish EMA/RSI trend regime = EMA(close, fast lookback for selected sensitivity) < EMA(close, slow lookback for selected sensitivity) AND close < EMA(close, fast lookback for selected sensitivity) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, when position side is not long, enter at the bar close if (Entry Method is FVG Pullback AND Bullish FVG pullback OR Entry Method is EMA Trend AND Bullish EMA/RSI trend regime is true now and was false on the prior chart bar); AND (Sensitivity is Aggressive OR Sensitivity is Balanced and close > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) OR Sensitivity is Conservative and close > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) AND, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close > EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) >= ADX threshold); AND (both session filters are disabled OR an enabled session’s configured weekday and time window includes the bar time in the configured timezone); AND the bar close time is outside the configured pause window around each enabled news event.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, when position side is not short, enter at the bar close if (Entry Method is FVG Pullback AND Bearish FVG pullback OR Entry Method is EMA Trend AND Bearish EMA/RSI trend regime is true now and was false on the prior chart bar); AND (Sensitivity is Aggressive OR Sensitivity is Balanced and close < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) OR Sensitivity is Conservative and close < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) AND, on chart bars, the last completed T1 close < EMA(close, trend-filter lookback) calculated on T1 bars and sampled from the last completed T1 bar AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing) >= ADX threshold); AND (both session filters are disabled OR an enabled session’s configured weekday and time window includes the bar time in the configured timezone); AND the bar close time is outside the configured pause window around each enabled news event.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, high >= entry price + take-profit price distance OR low <= entry price - stop-loss price distance.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed chart bar after the entry bar, low <= entry price - take-profit price distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss price distance.
