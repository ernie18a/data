Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close[ER lookback]) / sum(abs(close[i] − close[i+1]), i=0..ER lookback−1), or zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Feature vector = [zscore(close − close[1], feature Z lookback), zscore(close − close[momentum lookback], feature Z lookback), 2 × Efficiency ratio − 1, RSI(close, RSI lookback)/100 − 0.5, clip((close − EMA(close, MA-distance lookback))/ATR(ATR lookback), ±MA-distance clip bound), zscore(volume, feature Z lookback)]; each zscore is (value − SMA(value, lookback))/StDev(value, lookback), or zero if StDev is zero, and the return, momentum, and volume zscores are clipped to a shared zscore clip bound.
Calculation: Barrier training labels = On each confirmed chart bar, start a sample unless OU half-life(close, OU lookback) exceeds the vertical-barrier horizon; store its feature vector, opening probability, entry close, and ATR. Its barriers are entry close ± barrier-width × opening ATR. On a confirmed bar, the first high or low touch labels the sample 1 or 0; if both are touched on the same bar, label 1 when close ≥ entry close and otherwise 0. If neither is touched by the vertical-barrier horizon, discard the sample without training. Count only resolved barrier labels toward warm-up.
Calculation: Online logistic probability = sigmoid(intercept + sum(weight[i] × feature[i])); initialize weights to zero. For each resolved label y, recompute this probability on the stored features, set error = probability − y, and make one gradient update: intercept -= learning rate × uniqueness weight × error; each feature weight -= learning rate × uniqueness weight × (error × feature + L2 × weight). Uniqueness weight = 1/(1 + the sample's recorded overlap concentration); clamp updated weights to the configured weight limit.
Calculation: Lorentzian k-NN probability = mean label among the k nearest resolved samples in the capped recent memory, using distance sum(log(1 + abs(current feature − stored feature))) across features; unavailable until memory contains at least k samples.
Calculation: Model probability = sigmoid(Kish factor × (logit(Online logistic probability) + logit(Lorentzian k-NN probability))) when k-NN blending is enabled and available, otherwise Online logistic probability; clip probabilities to an interior numerical epsilon before logit. Shrink this result toward 0.5 by min(1, resolved-label count in the current regime / warm-up label count).
Calculation: Volatility-clustering score = recursively update H as prior H × exp(−decay parameter) + abs(change(log(close))), starting at zero; standardize H by its rolling mean and standard deviation over the normalization lookback, or use zero when the deviation is zero.
Calculation: Regime = volatile when the volatility-clustering score exceeds its threshold and the trend condition is false; otherwise, when ADX(DMI lookback) exceeds its threshold and Efficiency ratio exceeds its trend threshold, classify as uptrend if close > EMA(close, regime EMA lookback), else downtrend; otherwise classify as range.
Calculation: Conformal band = retain the capped recent absolute errors abs(resolved label − opening Model probability). On each resolution, miss = 1 when that error exceeds the width recorded at sample opening, else 0; update working miss rate by ACI step × (target miss rate − miss), and update recent miss rate by EWMA rate × (miss − recent miss rate). Effective miss rate = clipped(working miss rate + PID gain × (target miss rate − recent miss rate)). When the retained error count reaches its minimum, width is their linear-interpolated percentile at 1 − effective miss rate; lower and upper bounds are Model probability minus and plus width, clipped to [0,1].
Calculation: Calibrated probability = when isotonic recalibration is enabled and at least two eligible reliability bins exist, apply their PAV-pooled, sample-count-weighted observed label frequencies to Model probability by linear interpolation between bin midpoints, clamping beyond the outer midpoints; otherwise use Model probability. Reliability bins are equal-width over [0,1], populated by each sample's opening Model probability and resolved label, and eligible at the minimum samples-per-bin parameter.
Calculation: MTF agreement = on chart bars, request each of T1, T2, and T3 close versus its EMA using the shared MTF EMA lookback and lookahead_off; each bias is positive above the EMA, negative below, or zero at equality. Count positive and negative biases separately. Historical values use the last completed requested bar as made available by lookahead_off; realtime values can use the requested bar still forming.
Calculation: Participation direction = sign of cumulative bar delta minus its value at the participation lookback, where each bar delta is volume if close ≥ open and −volume otherwise.
Calculation: Brier score = mean squared difference between each resolved label and its opening Model probability over all resolved samples.
Calculation: Directional conviction = round the weighted sum of model confidence, regime fit, MTF agreement, calibration quality, and participation scores. Model confidence = min(1, abs(Model probability − 0.5)/0.5). Regime fit is the configured aligned-trend score in a matching trend, range score in range, and mismatch score otherwise. MTF agreement is the fraction of the three biases matching the candidate direction when MTF is enabled, else 0.5. Calibration quality = clip((Brier reference − Brier score)/calibration span, 0, 1), or zero before a Brier score exists. Participation score is the configured matching, neutral, or opposing score for matching, zero, or opposing participation direction.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND resolved labels ≥ warm-up count AND lower conformal bound > 0.5 + signal margin AND calibrated probability ≥ 0.5 AND bars since the preceding signal ≥ minimum gap AND (regime veto disabled OR regime is neither volatile nor downtrend) AND (MTF veto disabled OR MTF disabled OR positive-bias count ≥ minimum agreement) AND (conviction veto disabled OR directional conviction ≥ minimum conviction) AND (participation veto disabled OR participation direction is zero or positive).
Short: confirmed chart bar AND resolved labels ≥ warm-up count AND upper conformal bound < 0.5 − signal margin AND calibrated probability ≤ 0.5 AND bars since the preceding signal ≥ minimum gap AND (regime veto disabled OR regime is neither volatile nor uptrend) AND (MTF veto disabled OR MTF disabled OR negative-bias count ≥ minimum agreement) AND (conviction veto disabled OR directional conviction ≥ minimum conviction) AND (participation veto disabled OR participation direction is zero or negative).
