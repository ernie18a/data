Calculation: ready(T) = T-bar count >= max(efficiency lookback + one, ATR lookback + ATR baseline lookback - one, volume lookback), where ATR baseline lookback = ATR lookback x ATR baseline multiplier.
Calculation: adaptive average(T) = KAMA(close, efficiency lookback, fast adaptive length, max(slow adaptive length, next integer after fast adaptive length)), calculated on T bars.
Calculation: efficiency score(T) = if signed efficiency is enabled, score center + signed-efficiency scale x Kaufman Efficiency Ratio(close, efficiency lookback) x sign(close - close[efficiency lookback]); otherwise score maximum x that ratio; the ratio is zero when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: slope score(T) = clamp(score center + slope scale x (adaptive average(T) - adaptive average(T)[1]) / ATR(ATR lookback), score minimum, score maximum); use score center when ATR is zero.
Calculation: location score(T) = clamp(score center + location scale x (close - adaptive average(T)) / ATR(ATR lookback), score minimum, score maximum); use score center when ATR is zero.
Calculation: ATR ratio(T) = ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR baseline lookback); use the neutral ATR ratio when the baseline is zero.
Calculation: volatility score(T) = expanded-up score when ATR ratio(T) > expansion threshold, close > adaptive average(T), and adaptive average(T) > adaptive average(T)[1]; expanded-down score when ATR ratio(T) > expansion threshold, close < adaptive average(T), and adaptive average(T) < adaptive average(T)[1]; expanded-neutral score when ATR ratio(T) > expansion threshold otherwise; contracted score when ATR ratio(T) < contraction threshold; otherwise normal score.
Calculation: volume score(T) = neutral score when volume context is disabled or volume, its SMA(volume, volume lookback), or a positive average is unavailable; otherwise clamp(volume base score + relative-volume scale x volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), volume denominator floor), score minimum, score maximum).
Calculation: higher-timeframe score(T) = neutral score when higher-timeframe context is disabled or context timeframe T1 is not longer than T; otherwise, using calculations on T1 bars and the last completed T1 bar on each T bar, use aligned-up score if its close is above its KAMA and that KAMA is above the preceding completed T1 KAMA, aligned-down score if its close is below its KAMA and that KAMA is below the preceding completed T1 KAMA, and neutral score otherwise.
Calculation: composite score(T) = clamp(efficiency weight x efficiency score(T) + slope weight x slope score(T) + location weight x location score(T) + volatility weight x volatility score(T) + volume weight x volume score(T) + higher-timeframe weight x higher-timeframe score(T), score minimum, score maximum).
Calculation: regime(T) = neutral while not ready(T); otherwise retain the prior regime while its held-bar count is positive and below minimum hold; then, from Uptrend, remain Uptrend when composite score(T) >= effective up-exit, change to Downtrend when composite score(T) <= down-entry, and otherwise become Range; from Downtrend, remain Downtrend when composite score(T) <= effective down-exit, change to Uptrend when composite score(T) >= up-entry, and otherwise become Range; from Range, enter Uptrend at or above up-entry, enter Downtrend at or below down-entry, and otherwise remain Range. Effective up-exit = max(min(up-exit, up-entry), down-entry); effective down-exit = min(max(down-exit, down-entry), up-entry).
Calculation: row regime(T) = regime(T) calculated on T bars and read on chart bars from the last completed T bar.
Long: regime(chart) = Uptrend OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 1 AND T2 > chart timeframe AND row regime(T2) = Uptrend) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 2 AND T3 > chart timeframe AND row regime(T3) = Uptrend) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 3 AND T4 > chart timeframe AND row regime(T4) = Uptrend).
Short: regime(chart) = Downtrend OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 1 AND T2 > chart timeframe AND row regime(T2) = Downtrend) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 2 AND T3 > chart timeframe AND row regime(T3) = Downtrend) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 3 AND T4 > chart timeframe AND row regime(T4) = Downtrend).
Exit: long, (ready(chart) AND regime(chart) = Range) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 1 AND T2 > chart timeframe AND ready(T2, last completed bar) AND row regime(T2) = Range) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 2 AND T3 > chart timeframe AND ready(T3, last completed bar) AND row regime(T3) = Range) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 3 AND T4 > chart timeframe AND ready(T4, last completed bar) AND row regime(T4) = Range).
Exit: short, (ready(chart) AND regime(chart) = Range) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 1 AND T2 > chart timeframe AND ready(T2, last completed bar) AND row regime(T2) = Range) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 2 AND T3 > chart timeframe AND ready(T3, last completed bar) AND row regime(T3) = Range) OR (dashboard enabled AND row count includes row 3 AND T4 > chart timeframe AND ready(T4, last completed bar) AND row regime(T4) = Range).
