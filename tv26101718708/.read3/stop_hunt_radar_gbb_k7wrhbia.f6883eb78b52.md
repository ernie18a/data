Calculation: Sweep ATR = ATR(ATR lookback), or cumulative mean(high - low) when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Pool tolerance = ATR × ATR tolerance in ATR mode, or close × percentage tolerance / 100 in percentage mode.
Calculation: Tracked pool levels = confirmed Pivot Low prices for sell-side pools and confirmed Pivot High prices for buy-side pools. Create pools only when the pivot confirmation bar passes the lookback filter; start pool age at the pivot bar. Merge a new pivot into the first same-side armed pool within pool tolerance, updating its level to the mean of its cluster pivots; otherwise create a pool. Retire an unswept armed pool when its age reaches maximum life. At the per-side pool cap, remove the farthest pool outside the display radius if any; otherwise remove the lowest heat-scoring pool. A zero display radius disables the outside-radius test.
Calculation: Pool heat score = 0 when the active weights sum to 0; otherwise clamp 10 × (cluster weight × min(cluster count / max(cluster full-score count, 1), 1) + age weight × min(pool age / max(age full-score bars, 1), 1) + confluence weight × min(0.6 × session match + 0.5 × round-number match, 1) + volume weight × min(max formation relative volume / max(volume full-score multiple, 1), 1)) / sum of active weights to [0, 10]. Session match is a pool level within pool tolerance of a last completed T1 or T2 high or low, mapped onto chart bars. Round-number match is a pool level within half the pool tolerance of a multiple of the selected round-number step; when the step is automatic, use price magnitude / 200 for adapted forex and price magnitude / 10 otherwise. Formation relative volume is pivot-bar volume / SMA(volume, volume-baseline length) on the pivot confirmation bar, or 1 when that baseline is not positive; retain the largest value in a merged pool. Set volume weight to zero when asset adaptation is enabled and no positive volume has appeared.
Long: A tracked sell-side pool is swept when low < pool level - penetration multiple × sweep ATR and close > pool level; or, if grace bars are enabled, after a close below the pool level, a later close above it within the grace window reclassifies the break as a sweep. On a subsequent chart bar, while the pool remains in its watch window, close > pool level + reclaim-confirmation multiple × sweep ATR.
Short: A tracked buy-side pool is swept when high > pool level + penetration multiple × sweep ATR and close < pool level; or, if grace bars are enabled, after a close above the pool level, a later close below it within the grace window reclassifies the break as a sweep. On a subsequent chart bar, while the pool remains in its watch window, close < pool level - reclaim-confirmation multiple × sweep ATR.
