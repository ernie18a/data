Calculation: Pivot lookback = clamp(round(ATR(close, ATR lookback) / SMA(True Range, range lookback) * lookback scale), minimum pivot lookback, maximum pivot lookback)
Calculation: Pressure balance = 100 * (2 * close - high - low) / (high - low + pressure epsilon)
Calculation: Historical power(price, age) = min(power cap, min(price shock cap, price / ATR(ATR lookback) at pivot bar if positive, otherwise current ATR if positive, otherwise neutral value) * min(volume ratio cap, selected pivot volume / selected average volume if positive, otherwise neutral value) * (1 if age <= power lookback, otherwise reduced weight); selected pivot volume is volume at pivot bar if positive, otherwise current volume; selected average volume is SMA(volume, volume lookback) at pivot bar if positive, otherwise current SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Long: Pressure balance > buy pressure threshold OR confirmed pivot low(Pivot lookback, Pivot lookback) AND Historical power(pivot low price, Pivot lookback) >= power threshold
Short: Pressure balance <= sell pressure threshold OR confirmed pivot high(Pivot lookback, Pivot lookback) AND Historical power(pivot high price, Pivot lookback) >= power threshold
