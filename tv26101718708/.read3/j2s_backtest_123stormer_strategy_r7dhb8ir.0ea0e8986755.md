Calculation: Three-bar signal range = highest(high, 3) - lowest(low, 3), evaluated on the signal chart bar
Long: Long entries enabled AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive range) AND high[2] > high[1] AND low[2] > low[1] AND (inside-bar filter enabled AND high < high[1] AND low > low[1] OR inside-bar filter disabled AND low > low[1]) AND (both EMA filters enabled AND each of low[2], low[1], and low is above both EMA(source, shorter lookback, T1, bar still forming) and EMA(source, longer lookback, T1, bar still forming) OR only the shorter EMA filter enabled AND each of those lows is above that EMA OR only the longer EMA filter enabled AND each of those lows is above that EMA); compare the EMA values on chart bars using the current T1 value; no signal if neither EMA filter is enabled. For each signal, place a stop entry at its high, valid until the chart-bar age equals the entry-bar limit; enter when price reaches or exceeds that high before cancellation.
Short: Short entries enabled AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive range) AND high[2] < high[1] AND low[2] < low[1] AND (inside-bar filter enabled AND high < high[1] AND low > low[1] OR inside-bar filter disabled AND high < high[1]) AND (both EMA filters enabled AND each of high[2], high[1], and high is below both EMA(source, shorter lookback, T1, bar still forming) and EMA(source, longer lookback, T1, bar still forming) OR only the shorter EMA filter enabled AND each of those highs is below that EMA OR only the longer EMA filter enabled AND each of those highs is below that EMA); compare the EMA values on chart bars using the current T1 value; no signal if neither EMA filter is enabled. For each signal, place a stop entry at its low, valid until the chart-bar age equals the entry-bar limit; enter when price reaches or falls below that low before cancellation.
Exit: long, low <= the signal bar's lowest(low, 3) plus one minimum price increment OR high >= the signal bar's high + target-multiple parameter × three-bar signal range OR chart bars since entry = close-bar limit OR (date filter enabled AND datetime is outside the configured range AND the previous chart bar was inside it); on the date-range exit, cancel pending entry orders.
Exit: short, high >= the signal bar's highest(high, 3) plus one minimum price increment OR low <= the signal bar's low - target-multiple parameter × three-bar signal range OR chart bars since entry = close-bar limit OR (date filter enabled AND datetime is outside the configured range AND the previous chart bar was inside it); on the date-range exit, cancel pending entry orders.
