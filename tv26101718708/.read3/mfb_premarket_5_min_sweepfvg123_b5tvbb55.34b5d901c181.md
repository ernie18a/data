Calculation: Asian session high/low = maximum(high)/minimum(low) of chart bars in the Asian session of the current New York day, retained until the next New York day
Calculation: London session high/low = maximum(high)/minimum(low) of chart bars in the London session of the current New York day, retained until the next New York day
Calculation: Opening session high/low = maximum(high)/minimum(low) of chart bars in the opening session of the current New York day, retained until the next New York day
Calculation: Upper key level = max(high of the last completed T1 bar, Asian session high, London session high, opening session high, high of the last completed T2 bar), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Lower key level = min(low of the last completed T1 bar, Asian session low, London session low, opening session low, low of the last completed T2 bar), sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Key-level intention = direction of the latest chart-bar close crossing above any constituent high or below any constituent low used in the key levels; retain until another such cross, with above-cross priority if both occur on one bar
Calculation: Low-sweep priming = active after low <= Lower key level; expires when more than the level-touch lookback chart bars elapse since the latest such bar, and resets after any IFVG or FVG-123 signal
Calculation: High-sweep priming = active after high >= Upper key level; expires when more than the level-touch lookback chart bars elapse since the latest such bar, and resets after any IFVG or FVG-123 signal
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high[n], with top = low and bottom = high[n]
Calculation: Bearish FVG = high < low[n], with top = low[n] and bottom = high
Calculation: Long retrace state = arm when EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above EMA(close, longer lookback), or, when the cross requirement is disabled, when the shorter-lookback EMA > the longer-lookback EMA while unarmed; mark retraced after low <= the shorter-lookback EMA; reset on a cross below or after a long signal
Calculation: Short retrace state = arm when EMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below EMA(close, longer lookback), or, when the cross requirement is disabled, when the shorter-lookback EMA < the longer-lookback EMA while unarmed; mark retraced after high >= the shorter-lookback EMA; reset on a cross above or after a short signal
Long: Long retrace state is retraced AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) OR IFVG is enabled AND an active bearish FVG formed with top <= Lower key level while low-sweep priming was active AND a confirmed chart bar closes above its top while low-sweep priming is active AND no earlier IFVG or FVG-123 signal occurred on that chart bar OR an active, unfinished bullish FVG formed within the maximum FVG age has a later chart bar overlap its zone and close >= its bottom, then a still-later confirmed chart bar closes above its top AND the latest key-level intention is bearish AND low-sweep priming is active AND no earlier IFVG or FVG-123 signal occurred on that chart bar
Short: Short retrace state is retraced AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) OR IFVG is enabled AND an active bullish FVG formed with bottom >= Upper key level while high-sweep priming was active AND a confirmed chart bar closes below its bottom while high-sweep priming is active AND no earlier IFVG or FVG-123 signal occurred on that chart bar OR an active, unfinished bearish FVG formed within the maximum FVG age has a later chart bar overlap its zone and close <= its top, then a still-later confirmed chart bar closes below its bottom AND the latest key-level intention is bullish AND high-sweep priming is active AND no earlier IFVG or FVG-123 signal occurred on that chart bar
