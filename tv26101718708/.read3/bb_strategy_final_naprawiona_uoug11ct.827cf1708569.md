Long: low < lower Bollinger Band(close, BB lookback, BB multiplier) * (1 - chart-timeframe breakout threshold / 100), with separate threshold parameters for chart timeframes T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, and T6, and a separate fallback parameter for other chart timeframes
Short: high > upper Bollinger Band(close, BB lookback, BB multiplier) * (1 + chart-timeframe breakout threshold / 100), with separate threshold parameters for chart timeframes T1, T2, T3, T4, T5, and T6, and a separate fallback parameter for other chart timeframes
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage) OR exit-on-touch enabled AND high >= SMA(close, BB lookback) OR exit-on-touch disabled AND take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage) OR exit-on-close enabled AND close crosses below SMA(close, BB lookback)
Exit: short, stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR exit-on-touch enabled AND low <= SMA(close, BB lookback) OR exit-on-touch disabled AND take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) OR exit-on-close enabled AND close crosses above SMA(close, BB lookback)
