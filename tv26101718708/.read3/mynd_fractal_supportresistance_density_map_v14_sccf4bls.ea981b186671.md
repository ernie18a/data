Calculation: Top-N density zones = On the first chart bar, set the anchor to close and bin width to either a manual-width parameter or ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) times an ATR multiplier; map prices to the nearest bin, clamped to the configured number of bins on each side of the anchor. On each chart bar, multiply every bin score by a decay parameter. Add a separate weight for each of three confirmed symmetric pivot scales (shortest, intermediate, longest lookbacks; weights increase by scale), at each pivot high or low, multiplied by 1 when volume weighting is off or the current confirmation-bar SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) or pivot-bar volume is unavailable or nonpositive, otherwise by min(volume cap, pivot-bar volume / current confirmation-bar SMA(volume, volume-average lookback)). Rank bins by score; repeatedly select the highest remaining bin as a zone center and merge positive-score bins within the merge-distance parameter, summing their scores, until selecting the configured number of zones. A zone's expanded range is its center ± half the bin width ± bin width times the zone-tolerance parameter / 100. Count a touch for a zone's center bin each time close moves from outside to inside its expanded range; it qualifies when its touch count meets the minimum-touch parameter, or that parameter is zero.
Long: A qualifying Top-N zone is exited with close above its expanded range after price entered that same ranked zone and was inside it on the previous bar.
Short: A qualifying Top-N zone is exited with close below its expanded range after price entered that same ranked zone and was inside it on the previous bar.
