Calculation: IB high = highest high of chart bars in the IB session, using the configured timezone
Calculation: IB low = lowest low of chart bars in the IB session, using the configured timezone
Calculation: IB range = IB high - IB low
Long: After the IB is locked, during the RTH session and outside the IB session, the first processed bar or T1 sub-bar has high > IB high; if that same bar also has low < IB low, the up-break takes priority. Use T1 bars as they form when the intrabar walk is enabled and available; otherwise use chart bars.
Short: After the IB is locked, during the RTH session and outside the IB session, the first processed bar or T1 sub-bar has low < IB low and high ≤ IB high. Use T1 bars as they form when the intrabar walk is enabled and available; otherwise use chart bars.
Long: On the first post-break T1 sub-bar or chart bar whose low ≤ IB high, the maximum since the up-break of 1 + (high - IB high) / IB range is at least the minimum-extension parameter, and, when enabled, the break chart bar is in the break window, the current chart-bar EMA(close, regime lookback) minus that EMA from the regime-slope lookback chart bars ago is > 0, no bearish MACD divergence was confirmed between break and retest (a confirmed MACD pivot high is below the prior MACD pivot high while the chart high at its pivot is above the prior pivot high; use separate left and right pivot lookbacks), and no MACD slope kill applies: Highest(MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback), slope lookback) ≥ the MACD extreme parameter AND current MACD line < its value slope-lookback chart bars ago AND NOT (immediate-slope reprieve is enabled AND current MACD line > its value immediate-slope-lookback chart bars ago).
Short: On the first post-break T1 sub-bar or chart bar whose high ≥ IB low, the maximum since the down-break of 1 + (IB low - low) / IB range is at least the minimum-extension parameter, and, when enabled, the break chart bar is in the break window, the current chart-bar EMA(close, regime lookback) minus that EMA from the regime-slope lookback chart bars ago is < 0, no bullish MACD divergence was confirmed between break and retest (a confirmed MACD pivot low is above the prior MACD pivot low while the chart low at its pivot is below the prior pivot low; use separate left and right pivot lookbacks), and no MACD slope kill applies: Lowest(MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback), slope lookback) ≤ minus the MACD extreme parameter AND current MACD line > its value slope-lookback chart bars ago AND NOT (immediate-slope reprieve is enabled AND current MACD line < its value immediate-slope-lookback chart bars ago).
Long: When RSI entry is enabled, after a valid up-break retest and within the entry-window parameter in chart bars, RSI(close, RSI lookback) on T1 bars as they form (or chart bars if the intrabar walk is disabled or unavailable) crosses down through the long RSI threshold: the prior value is above the threshold and the current value is at or below it.
Short: When RSI entry is enabled, after a valid down-break retest and within the entry-window parameter in chart bars, RSI(close, RSI lookback) on T1 bars as they form (or chart bars if the intrabar walk is disabled or unavailable) crosses up through the short RSI threshold: the prior value is below the threshold and the current value is at or above it.
