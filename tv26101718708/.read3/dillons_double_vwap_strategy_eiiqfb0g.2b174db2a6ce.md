Calculation: Upper outer VWAP band = VWAP(source, anchor period) + outer-band multiplier × volume-weighted standard deviation(source, anchor period)
Calculation: Lower outer VWAP band = VWAP(source, anchor period) − outer-band multiplier × volume-weighted standard deviation(source, anchor period)
Calculation: Long trailing stop = if highest(high, bars since most recent entry) > average entry price × (1 + trailing activation %) then highest(high, bars since most recent entry) × (1 − trailing distance %) else average entry price × (1 − stop-loss %)
Calculation: Short trailing stop = if lowest(low, bars since most recent entry) < average entry price × (1 − trailing activation %) then lowest(low, bars since most recent entry) × (1 + trailing distance %) else average entry price × (1 + stop-loss %)
Long: ((EMA filter enabled AND position is not short AND close > EMA(source, lookback, T1, last completed bar)) OR (EMA filter disabled AND position is not long)) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is outside the configured excluded session in GMT-6) AND ADX(high, low, close, DI lookback, ADX smoothing) < ADX threshold AND crossunder(low, lower outer VWAP band) AND close > lower outer VWAP band
Short: ((EMA filter enabled AND position is not long AND close < EMA(source, lookback, T1, last completed bar)) OR (EMA filter disabled AND position is not short)) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is outside the configured excluded session in GMT-6) AND ADX(high, low, close, DI lookback, ADX smoothing) < ADX threshold AND crossover(high, upper outer VWAP band) AND close < upper outer VWAP band
Exit: long, close crosses Long trailing stop OR high > average entry price × (1 + take-profit %) OR (early VWAP exit enabled AND crossover(high, upper outer VWAP band))
Exit: short, close crosses Short trailing stop OR low > average entry price × (1 − take-profit %) OR (early VWAP exit enabled AND crossunder(low, lower outer VWAP band))
