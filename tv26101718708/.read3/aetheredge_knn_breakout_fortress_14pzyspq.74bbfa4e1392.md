Calculation: Zones = On chart bars, every refresh interval, collect each bar’s high, low, and close over the lookback; weight highs and lows by min(volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback), volume-weight cap) when both volume and its average are positive, otherwise by fallback weight; weight closes by that weight × close-weight multiplier; initialize the requested number of centroids at rounded midpoint quantiles of the sorted observations, then repeat the requested number of passes assigning observations to the nearest centroid (ties to the first) and replacing each centroid with its assigned weighted mean; for each centroid with assigned-observation count ≥ minimum cluster density, add a zone unless its center is within duplicate-distance ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) of an active zone; set its half-width to zone-width multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback) at creation, classify it as resistance if its center is above close at creation and support otherwise, and deactivate it when broken.
Long: There exists an active resistance zone such that close > its upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) × breakout buffer AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier.
Short: There exists an active support zone such that close < its lower bound − ATR(ATR lookback) × breakout buffer AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) × volume multiplier.
