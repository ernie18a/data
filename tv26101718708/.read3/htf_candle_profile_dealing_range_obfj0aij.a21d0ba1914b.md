Calculation: T1 EQ = (high + low) / 2 of the last completed T1 candle
Long: On chart bars using the forming T1 candle, low < open AND close > open
Short: On chart bars using the forming T1 candle, high > open AND close < open
Exit: Long, forming T1 close < T1 EQ
Exit: Short, forming T1 close > T1 EQ
