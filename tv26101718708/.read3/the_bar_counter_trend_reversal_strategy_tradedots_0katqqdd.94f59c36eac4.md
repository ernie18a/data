Long: On a confirmed bar, falling(close, fall-count) AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR rising(volume, fall-count)) AND (channel confirmation is disabled OR (volume confirmation enabled AND high > the upper line of the selected Bollinger Bands or Keltner Channels(channel source, channel lookback, channel multiplier)) OR (volume confirmation disabled AND low < the lower line of the selected Bollinger Bands or Keltner Channels(channel source, channel lookback, channel multiplier))) AND no Long signal has already occurred since the most recent bar where close > close[1] (initially eligible).
Short: On a confirmed bar, rising(close, rise-count) AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR rising(volume, rise-count)) AND (channel confirmation is disabled OR (volume confirmation enabled AND low < the lower line of the selected Bollinger Bands or Keltner Channels(channel source, channel lookback, channel multiplier)) OR (volume confirmation disabled AND high > the upper line of the selected Bollinger Bands or Keltner Channels(channel source, channel lookback, channel multiplier))) AND no Short signal has already occurred since the most recent bar where close < close[1] (initially eligible).
