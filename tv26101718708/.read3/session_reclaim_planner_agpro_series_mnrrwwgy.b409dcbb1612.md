Calculation: ReferenceHigh = maximum high of bars in the completed configured build session; available after that session ends.
Calculation: ReferenceLow = minimum low of bars in the completed configured build session; rails are ready only when ReferenceHigh > ReferenceLow.
Calculation: EffectiveATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback), with missing values treated as zero, TrueRange, minimum price increment).
Calculation: VolumeRatio = volume / SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback), or the fallback ratio if that SMA is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: HighGood(x, weak, strong) = clamp((x - weak) / (strong - weak) * score scale, 0, score scale).
Calculation: Peak(x, low, ideal, high) = if x <= ideal then clamp((x - low) / (ideal - low) * score scale, 0, score scale), otherwise clamp((high - x) / (high - ideal) * score scale, 0, score scale).
Calculation: RiskEdge(side, level, sweepExtreme, EffectiveATR) = for long, min(sweepExtreme, level - pocket ATR * EffectiveATR) - risk-buffer ATR * EffectiveATR; for short, max(sweepExtreme, level + pocket ATR * EffectiveATR) + risk-buffer ATR * EffectiveATR. sweepExtreme is the lowest low for long or highest high for short from the selected sweep through reclaim, updated on valid monitor-session bars.
Calculation: Target(side, level, riskEdge, ReferenceHigh, ReferenceLow) = selected target model's opposite rail, R target, or hybrid; opposite rail is ReferenceHigh for long or ReferenceLow for short; R target = level + side sign * max(abs(level - riskEdge), minimum price increment) * R multiple; midpoint target = level + side sign * max(abs(ReferenceHigh - ReferenceLow), minimum price increment) * range fraction; hybrid target is min(max(opposite rail, midpoint target), R target) for long and max(min(opposite rail, midpoint target), R target) for short.
Calculation: AcceptedCloses = consecutive valid chart-bar closes on the reclaimed side of the rail since activation, including the reclaim close; reset to zero on a close on the other side and cap at the required acceptance closes.
Calculation: ReclaimScore = clamp(close weight * HighGood(side sign * (close - level) / max(EffectiveATR, minimum price increment), close-score weak, close-score strong) + acceptance weight * clamp(AcceptedCloses / required acceptance closes * score scale, 0, score scale) + participation weight * (if VolumeRatio is unavailable then neutral participation score else HighGood(VolumeRatio, volume-score weak, volume-score strong)) + room weight * HighGood(abs(Target - close) / max(abs(level - riskEdge), minimum price increment), room-score weak, room-score strong) + range-fit weight * Peak(abs(ReferenceHigh - ReferenceLow) / max(EffectiveATR, minimum price increment), range-fit low, range-fit ideal, range-fit high), 0, score scale).
Long: On an intraday chart bar during the configured monitor session after the completed build session, on a valid close (confirmed when close confirmation is enabled), with no active plan or reclaim watch and with the selected rail mode allowing the low rail, the low is at least the sweep-depth threshold * EffectiveATR below ReferenceLow; if both rails meet the sweep threshold on that bar, choose the greater normalized sweep depth, choosing the low rail on a tie; then, no later than the reclaim-window parameter in chart bars after that sweep, close >= ReferenceLow + reclaim-distance threshold * EffectiveATR. A new build session clears the watch and active plan; a watch clears when its age exceeds the reclaim window; an active plan clears on a valid close after its age exceeds the plan-expiry parameter.
Long: First valid close of an active long reclaim plan that is not failed or target-reached, has not previously reached plan review, has AcceptedCloses >= required acceptance closes, and has ReclaimScore >= plan-review threshold.
Short: On an intraday chart bar during the configured monitor session after the completed build session, on a valid close (confirmed when close confirmation is enabled), with no active plan or reclaim watch and with the selected rail mode allowing the high rail, the high is at least the sweep-depth threshold * EffectiveATR above ReferenceHigh; if both rails meet the sweep threshold on that bar, choose the greater normalized sweep depth, choosing the low rail on a tie; then, no later than the reclaim-window parameter in chart bars after that sweep, close <= ReferenceHigh - reclaim-distance threshold * EffectiveATR. A new build session clears the watch and active plan; a watch clears when its age exceeds the reclaim window; an active plan clears on a valid close after its age exceeds the plan-expiry parameter.
Short: First valid close of an active short reclaim plan that is not failed or target-reached, has not previously reached plan review, has AcceptedCloses >= required acceptance closes, and has ReclaimScore >= plan-review threshold.
Exit: long, an active long reclaim plan has not failed and a valid close <= its RiskEdge.
Exit: short, an active short reclaim plan has not failed and a valid close >= its RiskEdge.
