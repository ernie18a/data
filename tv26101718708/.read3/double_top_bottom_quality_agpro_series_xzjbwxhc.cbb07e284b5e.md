Calculation: Neckline = for a top candidate Lowest(low, B1 - B2), and for a bottom candidate Highest(high, B1 - B2), both evaluated on the bar where the candidate forms
Calculation: OuterExtreme = max(P1, P2) for a top candidate, and min(P1, P2) for a bottom candidate
Calculation: Depth = min(|P1 - Neckline|, |P2 - Neckline|), evaluated on the bar where the candidate forms
Calculation: Target = Neckline + Depth for a bottom candidate, and Neckline - Depth for a top candidate
Calculation: Candidate = forms on a bar where a new pivot of a side is confirmed (top side: PivotHigh(high, pivot length, pivot length); bottom side: PivotLow(low, pivot length, pivot length)) and the two most recent pivots of that side are P1 at bar B1 (later) and P2 at bar B2 (earlier), if B1 - B2 >= min bars between pivots AND B1 - B2 <= max bars between pivots AND |P1 - P2| <= equality tolerance * ATR AND Depth >= min depth * ATR AND the cooldown test passes AND the region test passes, with ATR evaluated on the forming bar; the cooldown test fails only if the most recent confirmed candidate of the same side was confirmed within cooldown bars and |P1 - its Neckline| <= cooldown proximity * ATR; the region test fails only if a Waiting or Active candidate of the same side that formed on an earlier bar has later pivot price Q with |Q - P1| < region proximity * ATR
Calculation: Waiting = candidates formed on or before the current bar that, on every bar from formation through the bar before the current bar, satisfy: for a bottom, low >= OuterExtreme - invalidation tolerance * ATR and close <= Neckline; for a top, high <= OuterExtreme + invalidation tolerance * ATR and close >= Neckline; and bars since B1 <= pending lifetime * max bars between pivots
Calculation: Score = round(max(0, 1 - |P1 - P2| / (equality tolerance * ATR)) * factor points) + round(min(1, max(0, (volume / max(SMA(volume, volume average length), 1) - 1) / (break volume multiplier - 1))) * factor points) + round(min(1, Depth / ATR / depth normalization) * factor points), evaluated for a candidate on the current bar
Calculation: Active = candidates confirmed on an earlier bar that are still tracked and open at the start of the current bar, where a candidate is confirmed on the bar its Long condition (bottom) or Short condition (top) holds, and is tracked only if on that bar bars since B1 <= pending lifetime * max bars between pivots; an open candidate closes on a bar where its Target is reached (bottom: high >= Target; top: low <= Target) or, if not reached, where min(|close - Neckline|, |close - Target|) > stale distance * ATR; after each bar, if more than maximum active open candidates remain across both sides, the earliest-formed open candidates close; closed candidates stay closed
Calculation: Hit = the earliest-formed candidate in Active whose Target is reached on the current bar (bottom: high >= Target; top: low <= Target), or none
Long: some bottom candidate in Waiting has low >= OuterExtreme - invalidation tolerance * ATR AND close > Neckline AND Score >= minimum quality score
Short: some top candidate in Waiting has high <= OuterExtreme + invalidation tolerance * ATR AND close < Neckline AND Score >= minimum quality score
Exit: long, Hit is a bottom
Exit: short, Hit is a top
