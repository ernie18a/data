Calculation: Interpolated HTF Keltner bands = on chart bars, linearly interpolate between consecutive T1 values of EMA(close, band lookback, T1, last completed bar) ± ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) × multiplier; use the same band lookback, ATR lookback, and multiplier as for the chart-timeframe bands
Long: close < EMA(close, band lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) × multiplier AND close < interpolated HTF lower Keltner band AND NOT (close[1] < (EMA(close, band lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) × multiplier)[1] AND close[1] < interpolated HTF lower Keltner band[1])
Short: close > EMA(close, band lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × multiplier AND close > interpolated HTF upper Keltner band AND NOT (close[1] > (EMA(close, band lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × multiplier)[1] AND close[1] > interpolated HTF upper Keltner band[1])
