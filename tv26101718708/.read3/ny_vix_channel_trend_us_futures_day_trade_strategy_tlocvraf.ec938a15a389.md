Calculation: VIX unit = selected volatility-index open or close from T1, returned to chart bars from the T1 bar still forming, with configured source selection and fallback to the alternate source when unavailable, divided by 100 and by the square root of the annualization-period parameter
Long: On a chart bar after the first chart bar opening at or after session start plus the window-length parameter has closed, that first bar's close is above the session-opening bar's open, position is flat, and no entry has occurred this session
Short: On a chart bar after the first chart bar opening at or after session start plus the window-length parameter has closed, that first bar's close is below the session-opening bar's open, position is flat, and no entry has occurred this session
Exit: long, close >= session-opening bar's open * (1 + VIX unit sampled at session start) OR low <= entry price * (1 - current VIX unit * stop-factor parameter) OR high >= entry price * (1 + current VIX unit * target-factor parameter) AND low > entry price * (1 - current VIX unit * stop-factor parameter) OR chart-bar time >= session close - exit-lead parameter
Exit: short, close <= session-opening bar's open * (1 - VIX unit sampled at session start) OR high >= entry price * (1 + current VIX unit * stop-factor parameter) OR low <= entry price * (1 - current VIX unit * target-factor parameter) AND high < entry price * (1 + current VIX unit * stop-factor parameter) OR chart-bar time >= session close - exit-lead parameter
Calculation: volatility unit = (T1 volatility-index price selected by source parameter and price-field parameter / 100) / sqrt(annualization parameter)
Exit: long, instrument low <= average entry price × (1 − volatility unit × stop factor) OR instrument high >= average entry price × (1 + volatility unit × target factor) OR instrument close >= session open × (1 + volatility unit)
Exit: short, instrument high >= average entry price × (1 + volatility unit × stop factor) OR instrument low <= average entry price × (1 − volatility unit × target factor) OR instrument close <= session open × (1 − volatility unit)
