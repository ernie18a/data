Calculation: Watched high = highest high over the preceding rolling-lookback bars, excluding the current bar, or the latest confirmed pivot high using separate left- and right-bar parameters, according to the selected level model
Calculation: Watched low = lowest low over the preceding rolling-lookback bars, excluding the current bar, or the latest confirmed pivot low using separate left- and right-bar parameters, according to the selected level model
Calculation: Break tolerance = minimum tick size × tick-count parameter or ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × ATR-multiplier parameter, according to the selected tolerance mode
Calculation: Selected RVOL = volume divided by the median volume from prior completed sessions at the same intraday slot over the selected session lookback, or volume divided by SMA(volume, prior-volume lookback)[1], according to the selected volume baseline; RVOL is unavailable when the selected baseline is unavailable or nonpositive
Calculation: Candle range = high − low
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close − open)
Calculation: Previous candle body = abs(close[1] − open[1])
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close)
Calculation: Lower wick = min(open, close) − low
Calculation: Upward close location = (close − low) / candle range when candle range > 0, otherwise 0.5
Calculation: Downward close location = (high − close) / candle range when candle range > 0, otherwise 0.5
Calculation: Body efficiency = candle body / candle range when candle range > 0, otherwise 0
Calculation: Doji = candle range > 0 AND candle body <= doji body-to-range parameter × candle range

Calculation: A prior up-break event remains active until an eligible bar closes below its stored level minus its stored tolerance or its age in chart bars reaches the evaluation-horizon parameter; a prior down-break event remains active until an eligible bar closes above its stored level plus its stored tolerance or reaches that horizon. Event resolution is checked on the current bar before new break marks; a bar is eligible when session restriction is disabled or its time is within the configured session and time zone
Calculation: Up-break event = completed chart bar AND (session restriction disabled OR bar time within configured session and time zone) AND ATR(ATR lookback parameter) > 0 AND watched high exists AND (confirmed-pivot model not selected OR watched pivot high is unconsumed) AND close > watched high + break tolerance AND close[1] <= watched high + break tolerance AND no up-break event remains active after current-bar resolution AND no prior up-break mark exists or chart bars since the prior up-break mark >= directional cooldown parameter
