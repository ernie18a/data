Calculation: Ichimoku periods = use separate configurable conversion, base, and Span B lookbacks in classic mode; in adaptive mode derive the base lookback from the Ehlers dominant-cycle period times its multiplier, then derive conversion and Span B lookbacks from the base using their configured ratios and bounds.
Calculation: Cloud bias = bullish when the selected price source is above the maximum of Senkou A and Senkou B from the displacement lookback ago; bearish when below their minimum; otherwise neutral. Chart values use chart bars and chart high/low for Ichimoku; the selected source is configurable.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe cloud bias for each of T1, T2, and T3 = Ichimoku bias using separate classic conversion, base, and Span B lookbacks and displacement, calculated on that timeframe's bars from its last completed bar, then applied on chart bars.
Calculation: Market structure = direction of the most recent close that broke an unbroken confirmed pivot high or low; pivots use the configured swing lookback on both sides. A newly confirmed pivot resets its corresponding broken flag. A break against the prior direction is a Change of Character; otherwise it is a Break of Structure.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in the selected price source over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes over that lookback; zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Volatility-cluster z-score = z-score of a recursively decayed sum of absolute one-bar changes in log close, using separate decay and normalization lookback parameters; it is zero when the rolling standard deviation is zero. Regime is Trend when ADX exceeds its threshold and efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold; otherwise Volatile when the z-score exceeds its threshold; otherwise Range.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average over its lookback; unavailable when cumulative volume or the average is not positive.
Calculation: CVD direction = sign of cumulative signed volume minus its value at the configured comparison lookback. Signed volume is the sum within each chart bar of lower-timeframe T4 bar volume signed positive when that bar closes at or above its open and negative otherwise; use signed chart-bar volume if no T4 bars are available. T4 values include available intrabars of the current chart bar.
Calculation: Cross-asset momentum direction = sign of the change in cross-asset close over its lookback multiplied by its configured sign; unavailable when no cross-asset symbol is configured. Volatility-index spike = volatility-index close z-score over its lookback is at least its configured threshold; unavailable when no volatility-index symbol is configured.
Calculation: Signal strength = rounded percentage of the weighted mean of these chart-bar components, using separate configured weights: Tenkan/Kijun alignment with cloud bias; selected source above its displacement-lookback value aligned with bias; displaced Senkou A versus Senkou B aligned with bias; current Senkou A versus Senkou B aligned with bias; Kijun slope aligned with bias; matching higher-timeframe cloud count divided by the number of configured higher timeframes; cloud thickness in ATR units, clamped using its normalization parameter; structure alignment; and relative volume above its minimum, clamped using its normalization parameter. Use configured neutral scores when a component is disabled or unavailable, and return zero when cloud bias is neutral.
Calculation: Conviction = rounded, clamped weighted score using separate configured weights for signal strength, regime, higher-timeframe agreement, CVD direction, and cross-asset momentum. Regime scores use separate parameters for Trend, Range, and Volatile. Directional agreement scores use the configured full-match and mismatch scores; unavailable optional data uses its configured neutral score. Higher-timeframe agreement is the matching cloud-bias count divided by the number of configured higher timeframes, or its neutral score when disabled.
Calculation: Learned strength cutoff = evaluate each cloud-bias flip after the configured judging window; it wins when favorable high-minus-signal-close for a long, or signal-close-minus-favorable low for a short, reaches the configured ATR target times ATR at the signal. Bin signal strengths using the configured bin width. Once the total completed evaluations reaches the configured minimum history count, add bins from highest to lowest strength; retain a bin's lower bound while its cumulative win rate meets the configured target and the configured minimum observation count, and stop after adding a lower bin makes the rate fail. Use this cutoff when auto-learning is enabled and available; otherwise use the manual minimum-strength parameter.
