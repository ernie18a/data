Calculation: Volatility distance = root mean square of standardized values of the first configured number of these components: standard deviation of simple returns over the analysis lookback; standard deviation of (volume − SMA(volume, analysis lookback)) / SMA(volume, analysis lookback) over the analysis lookback; standard deviation of (high − low) / close over the analysis lookback; standard deviation of correlation(close, volume, analysis lookback) over the analysis lookback; standard deviation of (close − (high + low) / 2) / (high − low + epsilon) over the analysis lookback. Each component is standardized against its mean and population standard deviation over the preceding analysis-lookback chart bars; zero-standard-deviation components contribute zero.
Calculation: Jump event = Volatility distance > jump threshold × sensitivity AND bars since the previous jump > jump cooldown.
Calculation: Hawkes intensity = 1 on a jump event; otherwise previous Hawkes intensity × decay parameter; initialize at zero.
Calculation: Volatility component = jump event OR Hawkes intensity > intensity factor × sensitivity.
Calculation: Binned flow score(source, target) = binary entropy of the fraction of pairs assigned to the same bin, pairing source[i − entropy lag] with target[i] for i from entropy lag through analysis lookback − 1; bin source and target independently into the configured number of equal-width bins over their respective ranges. Return zero if fewer than analysis lookback + entropy lag bars are available or either range is zero.
Calculation: Net information flow = EMA(Binned flow score(log(volume + pseudocount), simple returns), flow-smoothing lookback) − EMA(Binned flow score(simple returns, log(volume + pseudocount)), flow-smoothing lookback), using the analysis lookback arrays.
Calculation: Regime = trending when EMA(HurstExponent(log(close / close[1]), analysis lookback), regime-smoothing lookback) > trend threshold; mean-reverting when it is < mean-reversion threshold; otherwise random.
Calculation: Regime component = (Regime is trending AND close > SMA(close, 20-period lookback) AND close > close[1]) OR (Regime is mean-reverting AND (close > Bollinger upper band(close, 20-period lookback, deviation multiplier) OR close < Bollinger lower band(close, 20-period lookback, deviation multiplier))) OR (Regime is random AND Bollinger band width / middle band < squeeze threshold AND (close > prior-bar Bollinger upper band OR close < prior-bar Bollinger lower band)).
Calculation: Flow component = Net information flow > entropy threshold × sensitivity OR Net information flow < −entropy threshold × sensitivity OR volume > SMA(volume, 20-period lookback) × (1 + sensitivity × volume factor).
Calculation: Active component count = number of true values among Volatility component, Regime component, and Flow component.
Calculation: Signal gate = Active component count ≥ required component count AND bars since the previous long or short signal > signal cooldown.
Long: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback) OR (Signal gate AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)).
Short: RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold AND close < EMA(close, EMA lookback) OR (Signal gate AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)).
Exit: Long, bars since entry > holding period OR (close − entry price) / entry price > profit threshold OR (close − entry price) / entry price < −loss threshold.
Exit: Short, bars since entry > holding period OR (entry price − close) / entry price > profit threshold OR (entry price − close) / entry price < −loss threshold.
