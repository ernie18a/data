Calculation: Ribbon direction = +1 when HMA(selected price source, short lookback) > KAMA(selected price source, reference lookback, KAMA fast lookback, KAMA slow lookback), -1 when it is lower, otherwise 0
Calculation: Adaptive stop direction = initialize bullish with stop at close minus distance; on each bar, distance = adaptive ATR multiplier × RMA(TrueRange, adaptive length), where adaptive length is the reciprocal of the squared interpolation between the fast and slow smoothing constants using EMA(ER(close, stop ER lookback), stop ER smoothing lookback), clamped between the fast bound minus its lower offset (minimum length) and the slow bound plus its upper offset. The multiplier tightens above its upper efficiency threshold and widens below its lower threshold. While bullish, trail the stop at the greater of its prior value and close minus distance; while bearish, trail at the lesser of its prior value and close plus distance. Flip bearish when close is below the bullish stop and reset it to close plus distance; flip bullish when close is above the bearish stop and reset it to close minus distance. Apply separately on chart bars and on T2, T3, and T4 bars; those higher-timeframe values use lookahead off, so realtime bars can use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and historical bars use the latest available completed bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = count of bullish adaptive stop directions among T2, T3, and T4 when ribbon direction is bullish, or count of bearish directions when ribbon direction is bearish; zero when ribbon direction is neutral. T2, T3, and T4 are distinct derived higher timeframes in increasing order.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe multiplier = 1 when higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled or ribbon direction is neutral; otherwise use 1 plus the all-agree bonus when all three agree, 1 when two agree, the one-agree multiplier when one agrees, and the zero-agree multiplier when none agree.
Calculation: Volume-profile POC, VAH, and VAL = on each configured anchor change, reset bins across a range centered on the anchor bar's close with half-width equal to the configured ATR multiple; divide that range into the configured number of bins. For each chart bar overlapping the range, distribute its volume equally across every touched bin, or add one per touched bin in no-volume mode. POC is the center of the highest-volume bin, with the first tied bin selected. Starting at POC, expand the value area until it contains the configured fraction of total bin volume, adding the higher adjacent bin first on ties; VAH and VAL are the resulting outer bin edges.
Calculation: Prior-session references = the preceding bar's session VWAP and POC, captured on each configured VWAP/profile anchor change and held until the next change.
Calculation: Flow agreement and toxicity = compute order-flow imbalance on chart bars from adjacent T1 sub-bar close changes within each chart bar, summing their signs weighted by T1 volume when volume weighting is enabled, or by one otherwise, and dividing by total weight. If a chart bar has no adjacent T1 sub-bars, use the sign of its close change weighted by chart-bar volume or one. T1 is lower than the chart timeframe; use available T1 sub-bars inside each chart bar, including a still-forming sub-bar in realtime. Use borrowed-symbol volume when configured, otherwise chart-symbol volume. Volume weighting follows the configured mode; auto mode enables it when the observed positive-volume proportion reaches its configured threshold, unless the chart is an index/economic series without borrowed volume. Flow agreement is clamp to [0,1] of ribbon direction times the imbalance z-score over the flow lookback divided by its scale, or zero when the standard deviation is zero or direction is neutral. Toxicity is SMA(abs(weighted signed flow)/total weight, flow lookback), using zero when total weight is zero.
Calculation: Effort-move score = use volume when volume weighting is enabled and available, otherwise one; normalize it against its SMA over the effort lookback using configurable ratio cutoffs and output levels. Compute normalized price movement as the clamped average of close location within the bar range, body divided by bar range, signed normalized body size, close location within the short-range lookback, close change divided by that range, and signed normalized close change; use zero for terms with a zero range. Set effort/result ratio to 100 times absolute normalized movement divided by normalized volume, or zero when normalized volume is zero. Assign movement quality by configurable thresholds for exceptional movement with high volume, high-efficiency movement without that condition, and low-efficiency compression; otherwise use the neutral quality. Return zero when direction is neutral or movement sign disagrees with ribbon direction; otherwise return clamp to [0,1] of absolute normalized movement divided by its scale times movement quality.
Calculation: Prior-level score = accept a prior reference when close crosses above either prior-session reference in bullish ribbon direction or below either in bearish direction; accepted score applies. Otherwise choose the nearest reference ahead in the ribbon direction and measure its distance from close in ATR units. Use the neutral score when direction is neutral or no obstacle exists; otherwise assign the near-obstacle, intermediate, or clear score by configured distance thresholds.
Calculation: Location score = weighted average of session VWAP gradient, swing-anchored VWAP position, value-area position, and prior-level score using their separate subweights; use zero if their weight sum is zero. Session VWAP gradient is clamp[0,1](ribbon direction × (VWAP(hlc3, configured anchor period) - its value gradient-lookback bars ago) / gradient lookback / ATR × slope gain). Swing VWAP resets at each confirmed pivot high or low using the same configurable left and right pivot lookback; its component is the mean of the directional close-versus-VWAP test and clamp[0,1] of its directional slope over the configured anchored-VWAP slope lag, ATR-normalized and multiplied by slope gain. The value-area component is 0.4 when VAH or VAL is unavailable; otherwise it is 1 outside value in the ribbon direction, 0.4 inside value, and 0 outside value against direction; use zero for neutral direction. ATR uses the configured ATR lookback.
Calculation: Conviction = clamp[0,1] of the weighted average of flow agreement, effort-move score, Efficiency Ratio(close, score ER lookback), and location score, using their separate weights and zero when their weight sum is zero, multiplied by the toxicity factor and higher-timeframe multiplier. The toxicity factor is 1 when disabled; otherwise it decreases from 1 according to the configured VPIN thresholds and damping factor.
Calculation: Signal gate = conviction >= configured minimum conviction AND not within the configured initial bar count after a calendar-date change when the session filter is enabled on an intraday chart.
