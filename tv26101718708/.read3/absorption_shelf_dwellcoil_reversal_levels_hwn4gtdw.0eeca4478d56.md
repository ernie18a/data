Calculation: Dwell = for each price level, the sum over the last dwell window bars whose low-high range covers the level of 1 / (number of levels from that bar's low to its high), where a level is a price bin of width bin width
Calculation: Band = each maximal run of adjacent levels whose Dwell is at least the hot-level threshold; its low is the lower edge of its lowest level, its high the upper edge of its highest level, and its anchor the Dwell-weighted mean of its level midpoints
Calculation: Coil = 1 - clamp(ATR(high, low, close, fast ATR lookback) / ATR(high, low, close, slow ATR lookback), 0, 1)
Calculation: Containment = (number of the last containment lookback closes, current close included, that are >= shelf low - containment buffer * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) and <= shelf high + containment buffer * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)) / containment lookback
Calculation: Shelf = a tracked record of a band's low, high, anchor and stage; each bar each band is matched to the earliest-tracked unmatched shelf whose previous low-high range overlaps the band's low-high range, and that shelf takes the band's low, high and anchor; a band with no match opens a shelf in forming stage while fewer than max tracked shelves are tracked; a shelf unmatched for more than ghost retention bars is dropped, and a spent shelf unmatched for more than containment lookback bars is dropped
Calculation: Shelf stage = forming until the first bar with Coil >= min coil AND Containment >= min containment fraction, then active; an active shelf becomes flipped on a bar where (Approach = +1 AND Upper break) OR (Approach = -1 AND Lower break), and spent on a bar where (Approach = +1 AND Lower break) OR (Approach = -1 AND Upper break); flipped and spent are final
Calculation: Approach = +1 if close[1] >= shelf anchor, else -1, set at the first bar since activation whose low-high range overlaps the shelf's low-high range, and kept thereafter
Calculation: Upper break = close > shelf high + flip distance * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) AND low[1] <= shelf high + flip distance * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Calculation: Lower break = close < shelf low - flip distance * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) AND high[1] >= shelf low - flip distance * ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback)
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low) if high > low, else 0.5
Calculation: Volume measure = volume, or if the time-of-day option is on, volume / EMA(volume, session memory) over bars in the same time-of-day slot of slot size minutes, or 1 when that EMA is not positive
Long: for some shelf, Shelf stage = active AND Approach = +1 AND Upper break
Short: for some shelf, Shelf stage = active AND Approach = -1 AND Lower break
Long: volume > 0 AND low <= Lowest(low, new-extreme lookback)[1] AND Close position >= shakeout close-position limit AND PercentRank(Volume measure, VSA lookback) >= trap volume percentile threshold
Long: volume > 0 AND close < close[1] AND PercentRank(high - low, VSA lookback) >= stopping spread percentile threshold AND PercentRank(Volume measure, VSA lookback) >= stopping volume percentile threshold AND Close position >= stopping close-position limit
