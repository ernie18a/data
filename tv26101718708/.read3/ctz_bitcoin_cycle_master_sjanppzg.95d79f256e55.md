Calculation: StochRSI %K = SMA(StochRSI(close, RSI lookback, StochRSI lookback), %K smoothing)
Calculation: StochRSI %D = SMA(StochRSI %K, %D smoothing)
Long: close > Confirmation EMA
Short: close <= Confirmation EMA
Long: an accepted pivot low using the shorter pivot lookback (no prior candidate, or at least the shorter minimum spacing since the prior candidate, or a lower pivot low) is followed by both close > Confirmation EMA and StochRSI %K crossing above StochRSI %D with lowest(StochRSI %K, oversold lookback) < oversold threshold; latch each condition after the pivot and emit once both have occurred
Long: an accepted pivot low using the longer pivot lookback (no prior candidate, or at least the longer minimum spacing since the prior candidate, or a lower pivot low) is followed by both close > Confirmation EMA and StochRSI %K crossing above StochRSI %D with lowest(StochRSI %K, oversold lookback) < oversold threshold; latch each condition after the pivot and emit once both have occurred
Long: mark the running lowest low of each calendar year provisionally; at year rollover finalize it unless its date is within the minimum spacing of the prior finalized yearly low, in which case keep only the lower low
Long: mark the running lowest low provisionally while time is from the next anchored cycle target minus the snap window through the target plus the settle delay; the first target is the anchor date, and later targets are the last selected long-scale low plus the running mean interval between selected long-scale lows
Long: after the anchor date plus the settle delay, select the confirmed long-scale low nearest the anchor, snapping to the deepest confirmed long-scale low within the snap window; for later targets, after target plus settle delay select the nearest confirmed long-scale low after the previous selected low, snap to the deepest within the snap window if it is after the previous selected low, and replace the selection with any deeper confirmed long-scale low within that window before it closes
Short: an accepted pivot high using the shorter pivot lookback (no prior candidate, or at least the shorter minimum spacing since the prior candidate, or a higher pivot high) is followed by both close < Confirmation EMA and StochRSI %K crossing below StochRSI %D with highest(StochRSI %K, overbought lookback) > overbought threshold; latch each condition after the pivot and emit once both have occurred
Short: an accepted pivot high using the longer pivot lookback (no prior candidate, or at least the longer minimum spacing since the prior candidate, or a higher pivot high) is followed by both close < Confirmation EMA and StochRSI %K crossing below StochRSI %D with highest(StochRSI %K, overbought lookback) > overbought threshold; latch each condition after the pivot and emit once both have occurred
Calculation: Long-scale high position ratio = (chart-bar offset of the running highest high since the last confirmed long-scale low) / expected long-cycle length in chart bars
Long: chart bars since the last confirmed long-scale low >= the early cycle-age fraction of expected long-cycle length AND long-scale high position ratio >= the late-position fraction
Short: chart bars since the last confirmed long-scale low >= the early cycle-age fraction of expected long-cycle length AND long-scale high position ratio <= the early-position fraction
