Calculation: lower retracement level = highest(high, Fibonacci lookback) - (highest(high, Fibonacci lookback) - lowest(low, Fibonacci lookback)) * lower-level ratio
Long: close crosses above upper retracement level + ATR(ATR lookback) * breakout buffer multiplier
Short: close crosses below lower retracement level - ATR(ATR lookback) * breakout buffer multiplier
