Calculation: selectable indicator series = OHLC4=(open+high+low+close)/4; three independently configured RSI(source, lookback), three independently configured moving averages(type, source, lookback; type is EMA, SMA, WMA, VWMA, HMA, RMA, or DEMA); ADX, DI+, and DI− from DMI with separate directional and ADX-smoothing lookbacks; Stochastic %K and %D(lookback, K smoothing, D smoothing); Bollinger basis, upper, and lower bands(source, lookback, multiplier); Keltner upper and lower bands(close, basis lookback, ATR lookback, multiplier); MACD, signal, and histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback); and EMA(volume, lookback). Each timeframe-configurable indicator is calculated on its selected timeframe and sampled on chart bars, using the still-forming timeframe bar live and its last completed value in historical bars. Other selectable values are open, high, low, close, volume, and an imported indicator series.
Long: During the configured session if session filtering is enabled, at least one of the three long tests is enabled and every enabled test is true; each test compares a left value chosen from open, high, low, close, OHLC4, or a selectable indicator series with a right value chosen from a selectable indicator series or a numeric parameter, using its configured operator: crosses above, crosses below, greater than, or less than.
Short: During the configured session if session filtering is enabled, at least one of the three short tests is enabled and every enabled test is true; each test compares a left value chosen from open, high, low, close, OHLC4, or a selectable indicator series with a right value chosen from a selectable indicator series or a numeric parameter, using its configured operator: crosses above, crosses below, greater than, or less than.
Calculation: stop distance in ticks = selected stop mode's stop percentage × close / 100, ATR(shared lookback) × stop ATR multiplier, or stop point amount; target distance in ticks = independently selected target mode's target percentage × close / 100, ATR(shared lookback) × target ATR multiplier, or target point amount.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop distance × minimum tick OR high ≥ entry price + target distance × minimum tick OR, if trailing is enabled and price has reached entry price + activation distance in ticks × minimum tick, low reaches the highest high since activation − trailing offset in ticks × minimum tick.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop distance × minimum tick OR low ≤ entry price − target distance × minimum tick OR, if trailing is enabled and price has reached entry price − activation distance in ticks × minimum tick, high reaches the lowest low since activation + trailing offset in ticks × minimum tick.
