Calculation: T1 high, low, and close samples = values from the still-forming T1 bar when the current-bar option is selected, or from the last completed T1 bar otherwise, mapped onto chart bars.
Calculation: Predicted price = the mean of the selected close samples for each chart-bar offset i from zero through the data-point lookback minus one; for each i, let d[j] = abs(close sample[j] - close sample[j+1]) for j from zero through i, and select close sample[i+1] when d[i] is less than the smallest distance in d[0..i] if i+1 is no greater than the neighbor-count parameter, or less than the distance at zero-based rank neighbor-count parameter otherwise; select close sample[i] in all other cases.
Calculation: Comparison close = the current chart bar's T1 close sample when the adjustment option is enabled, otherwise the preceding chart bar's T1 close sample.
Long: (add-on selection is None AND predicted price < comparison close) OR (add-on selection is Pivot Point AND predicted price < comparison close AND highestbars(T1 high sample, pivot lookback) = 0) OR (add-on selection is Z-Score AND predicted price < comparison close AND T1 close sample > SMA(T1 close sample, z-score lookback))
Short: (add-on selection is None AND predicted price > comparison close) OR (add-on selection is Pivot Point AND predicted price > comparison close AND lowestbars(T1 low sample, pivot lookback) = 0) OR (add-on selection is Z-Score AND predicted price > comparison close AND T1 close sample < SMA(T1 close sample, z-score lookback))
