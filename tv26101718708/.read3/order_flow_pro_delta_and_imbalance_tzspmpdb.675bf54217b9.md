Calculation: bar delta = if close > open then volume, if close < open then -volume, otherwise 0
Calculation: cumulative delta = prior cumulative delta + bar delta, initialized at zero
Calculation: normalized cumulative delta = if highest(abs(cumulative delta), normalization lookback) > 0 then cumulative delta / highest(abs(cumulative delta), normalization lookback) * highest(abs(bar delta), normalization lookback) * normalization scale, otherwise 0
Calculation: signed body fraction = (close - open) / (high - low + range epsilon)
Calculation: bullish imbalance = close > open AND volume > 0 AND signed body fraction > imbalance threshold
Calculation: bearish imbalance = close < open AND volume > 0 AND signed body fraction < -imbalance threshold
Calculation: bullish imbalance streak = bullish imbalance on each of the current and preceding imbalance streak length minus one bars
Calculation: bearish imbalance streak = bearish imbalance on each of the current and preceding imbalance streak length minus one bars
Long: bar delta > 0 OR cumulative delta > 0 OR normalized cumulative delta > normalized cumulative delta[1] OR bullish imbalance streak OR (bar delta > SMA(bar delta, delta MA lookback) * signal multiplier AND volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * high-volume multiplier) OR bar delta > SMA(bar delta, delta MA lookback) * pressure multiplier OR bar delta > SMA(bar delta, delta MA lookback) * extreme-pressure multiplier OR bar index > warm-up threshold AND pivot low(close, equal left and right pivot lookbacks) exists AND pivot high(bar delta, equal left and right pivot lookbacks) exists AND close[shorter comparison lookback] < close[longer comparison lookback]
Short: bar delta <= 0 OR cumulative delta <= 0 OR normalized cumulative delta <= normalized cumulative delta[1] OR bearish imbalance streak OR (bar delta < -SMA(bar delta, delta MA lookback) * signal multiplier AND volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) * high-volume multiplier) OR bar delta < -SMA(bar delta, delta MA lookback) * pressure multiplier OR bar delta < -SMA(bar delta, delta MA lookback) * extreme-pressure multiplier OR bar index > warm-up threshold AND pivot high(close, equal left and right pivot lookbacks) exists AND pivot low(bar delta, equal left and right pivot lookbacks) exists AND close[shorter comparison lookback] > close[longer comparison lookback]
