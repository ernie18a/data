Calculation: Buy fraction(T) = NormalCDF((close − close[1]) / StDev(close − close[1], BVC lookback)), or 0.5 if the standard deviation is zero; in triangular mode, 1 − TriangularCDF(open; low, mode=close, high), with open and close clamped to [low, high] and the close/open comparison determining the fraction when high=low
Calculation: Signed volume(T) = volume × (2 × buy fraction(T) − 1) × min(volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), relative-volume cap) when relative-volume weighting is enabled; otherwise omit the relative-volume factor
Calculation: Control(T) = clamp(adjusted weighted mean, −1, 1); the raw weighted mean uses signed-volume balance over the short delta lookback, signed-volume balance over a longer CVD lookback, optional VWAP and EMA lenses, and optional CMF. The VWAP lens is the mean of clamp((close − session VWAP)/(ATR × VWAP-location scale), −1, 1) and clamp((session VWAP − session VWAP[vwap-slope lookback])/ATR, −1, 1). The EMA lens weights the mean of +1 when fast EMA > middle EMA, middle EMA > slow EMA, and close > slow EMA (−1 otherwise) with clamp((fast EMA − fast EMA[EMA-slope lookback])/ATR, −1, 1). Fast, middle, and slow EMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest. Disabled optional lenses have zero weight; remaining weights are renormalized. Adjust raw control by the absorption multiplier when abs(raw control) exceeds its threshold and either abs(close − close[absorption-progress lookback])/ATR is below its threshold or VSA effort-result reports high-volume compression. If Kyle scaling is enabled, multiply by impact floor + impact weight × clamp(Kyle-λ R², 0, 1); otherwise use 1.
Calculation: Delta(T) = sum(signed volume(T), short delta lookback) / sum(volume(T), short delta lookback); CVD(T) = sum(signed volume(T), longer CVD lookback) / sum(volume(T), longer CVD lookback)
Calculation: Kyle-λ R²(T) = R² of close change regressed on signed volume over the Kyle lookback
Calculation: Strength(T) = PercentRank(abs(Control(T)), normalization lookback). Calculate Control and Strength on T bars; use the last completed T bar when freeze is enabled and the still-forming T bar otherwise. Sample these values on chart bars for cross-timeframe calculations.
Calculation: Path consensus = weighted mean of Control(T) × Strength(T)/100 over the configured consensus set, using equal weights or weights proportional to the square root of timeframe duration. The set is either the configured fixed set or the chart timeframe plus configured nearest-higher rungs from T1…T7, ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Turbulence percentile = PercentRank(SMA(((return − SMA(return, turbulence lookback)) / StDev(return, turbulence lookback))², turbulence-smoothing lookback), normalization lookback), using chart-timeframe returns
Calculation: Path clarity = round(100 × abs(2 × P(up) − 1) × (1 − correlation-discount × max(0, mean available correlation of adjacent T controls over the correlation lookback)) × clamp((KaufmanEfficiencyRatio(chart close, regime lookback) − lower ER threshold)/(upper ER threshold − lower ER threshold), 0, 1) × turbulence factor). Turbulence factor is 1 when disabled; otherwise max(minimum factor, 1 − turbulence-damp weight × max(0, (turbulence percentile − turbulence threshold)/(100 − turbulence threshold))). Correlations and path calculations run on chart bars using sampled T values.
Calculation: P(up) = 1 / (1 + exp(−log-odds gain × Path consensus))
Calculation: Path = UP when Path clarity ≥ chop threshold and P(up) ≥ 0.5; DOWN when Path clarity ≥ chop threshold and P(up) < 0.5; otherwise CHOP
Calculation: VPIN percentile = PercentRank(SMA(abs(unweighted signed volume), VPIN lookback) / SMA(volume, VPIN lookback), normalization lookback), using the selected buy/sell split (BVC when using an external flow source)
Calculation: Lower-timeframe signed delta = signed-volume balance from order-flow-source open, close, and volume on T8 sub-bars, where T8 is lower than the chart timeframe
Calculation: VSA event(T) = VSA event from order-flow-source high, low, close, and volume, with separate event parameters, calculated on T bars
Calculation: Initial Balance high and low = highest high and lowest low accumulated during the configured opening interval of each session
