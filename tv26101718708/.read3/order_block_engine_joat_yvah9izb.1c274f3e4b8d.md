Calculation: Last swing high = most recent confirmed Pivot high(high, pivot length, pivot length)
Calculation: Last swing low = most recent confirmed Pivot low(low, pivot length, pivot length)
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume length) if SMA(volume, volume length) > 0, else 1
Calculation: Tier points(x, tiers) = points of the highest threshold in tiers that x reaches, or the lowest tier's points if x reaches none
Calculation: Grade = Tier points(abs(close - broken swing level) / ATR(high, low, close, ATR length), displacement tiers) + Tier points(Volume ratio, volume tiers) + Tier points(abs(close - open) / (high - low), body tiers) on the break bar, where broken swing level = Last swing high for a Bullish break and Last swing low for a Bearish break, and the body ratio is 0 when high = low
Calculation: Bullish break = bar where close > open AND close - Last swing high > displacement multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR length) AND (Volume ratio >= volume multiplier OR SMA(volume, volume length) <= 0) AND no bar from the confirmation of the current Last swing high up to the previous bar meets these same conditions
Calculation: Bearish break = bar where close < open AND Last swing low - close > displacement multiple * ATR(high, low, close, ATR length) AND (Volume ratio >= volume multiplier OR SMA(volume, volume length) <= 0) AND no bar from the confirmation of the current Last swing low up to the previous bar meets these same conditions
Calculation: Bullish OB = for a Bullish break bar, the nearest bar among the order-block search depth bars before it with close < open; OB top = its high, OB bottom = its low, OB grade = Grade on the break bar; none if no such bar
Calculation: Bearish OB = for a Bearish break bar, the nearest bar among the order-block search depth bars before it with close > open; OB top = its high, OB bottom = its low, OB grade = Grade on the break bar; none if no such bar
Calculation: Live bullish OB = Bullish OB created on or before the current bar, among the max zones kept most recently created OBs (bullish or bearish) up to the current bar, whose bottom was not closed below on any bar after its creation and before the current bar, and that did not satisfy low <= top AND close > bottom AND close > open AND OB grade >= minimum grade on any bar after its creation and before the current bar
Calculation: Live bearish OB = Bearish OB created on or before the current bar, among the max zones kept most recently created OBs (bullish or bearish) up to the current bar, whose top was not closed above on any bar after its creation and before the current bar, and that did not satisfy high >= bottom AND close < top AND close < open AND OB grade >= minimum grade on any bar after its creation and before the current bar
Calculation: Long stop = bottom of the newest Live bullish OB created on an earlier bar that satisfies low <= top AND close > bottom AND close > open AND grade >= minimum grade on the entry bar, minus ATR(high, low, close, ATR length) * stop buffer, with ATR taken on the entry bar
Calculation: Short stop = top of the newest Live bearish OB created on an earlier bar that satisfies high >= bottom AND close < top AND close < open AND grade >= minimum grade on the entry bar, plus ATR(high, low, close, ATR length) * stop buffer, with ATR taken on the entry bar
Long: low <= top AND close > bottom AND close > open AND OB grade >= minimum grade, all for a Live bullish OB created on an earlier bar
Long: bottom <= close AND close <= top, for a Live bullish OB
Short: high >= bottom AND close < top AND close < open AND OB grade >= minimum grade, all for a Live bearish OB created on an earlier bar
Short: bottom <= close AND close <= top, for a Live bearish OB
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND (low <= Long stop OR high >= entry price + (entry price - Long stop) * take-profit multiple)
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND (high >= Short stop OR low <= entry price - (Short stop - entry price) * take-profit multiple)
