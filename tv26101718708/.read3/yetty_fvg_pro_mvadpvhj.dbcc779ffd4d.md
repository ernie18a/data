Calculation: Zone break(direction, top, bottom, confirmation parameter) = for an upward break, high > top for wick confirmation, close > bottom for inside-zone confirmation, close > (top + bottom) / 2 for midpoint confirmation, or close > top for full-zone confirmation; for a downward break, low < bottom for wick confirmation, close < top for inside-zone confirmation, close < (top + bottom) / 2 for midpoint confirmation, or close < bottom for full-zone confirmation
