Calculation: Buyside liquidity levels = add each confirmed PivotHigh(high, mode-selected swing-pivot lookback, mode-selected swing-pivot lookback); merge a new pivot into the oldest active level within merge-tolerance × ATR(ATR lookback), using the higher price, or add a new level if none matches; cap stored levels per side at the level-cap by removing the oldest inactive level first, otherwise the oldest stored level; deactivate a level after a sweep or break.
Calculation: Sellside liquidity levels = add each confirmed PivotLow(low, mode-selected swing-pivot lookback, mode-selected swing-pivot lookback); merge a new pivot into the oldest active level within merge-tolerance × ATR(ATR lookback), using the lower price, or add a new level if none matches; cap stored levels per side at the level-cap by removing the oldest inactive level first, otherwise the oldest stored level; deactivate a level after a sweep or break.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score on a sweep bar = 1 if the EMA-stack filter is enabled and EMA(close, shorter EMA length) > EMA(close, middle EMA length) > EMA(close, longer EMA length), otherwise 0, plus 1 if the RVOL filter is enabled and SMA(volume, RVOL average length) > 0 and volume / SMA(volume, RVOL average length) ≥ RVOL threshold, otherwise 0; shorter EMA length < middle EMA length < longer EMA length.
Calculation: Bearish confluence score on a sweep bar = 1 if the EMA-stack filter is enabled and EMA(close, shorter EMA length) < EMA(close, middle EMA length) < EMA(close, longer EMA length), otherwise 0, plus 1 if the RVOL filter is enabled and SMA(volume, RVOL average length) > 0 and volume / SMA(volume, RVOL average length) ≥ RVOL threshold, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Pending sweep setup = on each confirmed chart bar, resolve an existing setup before registering a new one; expire it when bars since its sweep exceed the confirmation window, cancel a long setup on close below its sweep-bar low or a short setup on close above its sweep-bar high, and clear it when close exceeds a long setup's sweep-bar high or falls below a short setup's sweep-bar low, even if its stored confluence score is below minimum confluence; if no setup remains, store an SSL sweep as long with its bullish confluence score or a BSL sweep as short with its bearish confluence score; if both sweep on the same bar, choose long when close > open and short otherwise; ignore sweeps for pending setup while one remains.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close > an active buyside liquidity level OR (there exists an active sellside liquidity level L such that low < L - minimum-pierce-ATR × ATR(ATR lookback) AND close > L) OR (an earlier pending long setup is within the confirmation window, close > its sweep-bar high, and its stored bullish confluence score ≥ minimum confluence).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close < an active sellside liquidity level OR (there exists an active buyside liquidity level L such that high > L + minimum-pierce-ATR × ATR(ATR lookback) AND close < L) OR (an earlier pending short setup is within the confirmation window, close < its sweep-bar low, and its stored bearish confluence score ≥ minimum confluence).
