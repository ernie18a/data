Calculation: CVD momentum = CVD - CVD[lookback]
Calculation: VWAP dispersion = sqrt(max(sum(volume * HLC3^2) / sum(volume) - VWAP(HLC3, volume, configured anchor)^2, 0)) within the configured anchor; zero when anchored volume is zero

Calculation: Upper wick/body ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / abs(close - open) when abs(close - open) > close * doji threshold factor; otherwise the configured doji fallback ratio
