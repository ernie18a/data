Calculation: Completed session range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in each configured session, fixed when that session ends
Calculation: Pivot alignment distance = absolute difference between the chart pivot bar and correlated-symbol pivot bar
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, for at least one lower reference (the low of the last completed T1 or T2 bar, or the low of a completed configured session range), open > level AND low < level AND close > level; no chart close since the latest T1, T2, or session-level reset has been below any current lower reference, and no bullish sweep has occurred since that reset. T1 and T2 values are the last completed bars, carried onto chart bars.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, for at least one upper reference (the high of the last completed T1 or T2 bar, or the high of a completed configured session range), open < level AND high > level AND close < level; no chart close since the latest T1, T2, or session-level reset has been above any current upper reference, and no bearish sweep has occurred since that reset. T1 and T2 values are the last completed bars, carried onto chart bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, low > high[FVG gap lookback] (bullish gap bounds are high[FVG gap lookback] to current low) OR close > the upper bound of an active, noninverted bearish FVG.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, high < low[FVG gap lookback] (bearish gap bounds are current high to low[FVG gap lookback]) OR close < the lower bound of an active, noninverted bullish FVG.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close > the latest confirmed, unused chart PivotHigh(high, structure pivot lookback, structure pivot lookback), and the most recent earlier down-close candle is within the OB source lookback; the bullish OB spans that source candle's low to high. OR close > the upper bound of an active bearish OB while breaker conversion is enabled.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close < the latest confirmed, unused chart PivotLow(low, structure pivot lookback, structure pivot lookback), and the most recent earlier up-close candle is within the OB source lookback; the bearish OB spans that source candle's low to high. OR close < the lower bound of an active bullish OB while breaker conversion is enabled.
Calculation: Bullish CISD delivery level = opening price of the first bar in the latest active consecutive down-close series; its first bar sweeps below the latest confirmed chart PivotLow(low, CISD sweep pivot length, CISD sweep pivot length) when the swing-sweep filter is enabled, or below the prior bar's low when disabled. Consecutive down-close bars extend the series; a later nonconsecutive qualifying sweep replaces it.
Calculation: Bearish CISD delivery level = opening price of the first bar in the latest active consecutive up-close series; its first bar sweeps above the latest confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, CISD sweep pivot length, CISD sweep pivot length) when the swing-sweep filter is enabled, or above the prior bar's high when disabled. Consecutive up-close bars extend the series; a later nonconsecutive qualifying sweep replaces it.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close > the active bullish CISD delivery level within the CISD lifetime from its first bar, unless an active bearish CISD also confirms on that bar; a CISD confirmation cancels the opposite pending series, and bearish confirmation takes precedence if both qualify.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close < the active bearish CISD delivery level within the CISD lifetime from its first bar; a CISD confirmation cancels the opposite pending series.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, current chart low < latest confirmed chart PivotLow(low, SMT pivot length, SMT pivot length) AND current correlated-symbol low > the prior confirmed correlated-symbol pivot low; the latest chart and correlated-symbol pivot lows are within the SMT alignment parameter, and no earlier chart bar since the chart pivot became latest has undercut it. Correlated-symbol values use the same chart timeframe.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, current chart high > latest confirmed chart PivotHigh(high, SMT pivot length, SMT pivot length) AND current correlated-symbol high < the prior confirmed correlated-symbol pivot high; the latest chart and correlated-symbol pivot highs are within the SMT alignment parameter, and no earlier chart bar since the chart pivot became latest has exceeded it. Correlated-symbol values use the same chart timeframe.
