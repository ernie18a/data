Calculation: Main trend = on chart bars, track a persistent trend and a watch direction; with no watch, start an up watch at the rolling highest high when it exceeds its value two bars ago, otherwise start a down watch at the rolling lowest low when it is below its value two bars ago; while watching up, set trend up and clear the watch when the rolling highest high exceeds the watched high, or clear the watch when the rolling lowest low is below its value two bars ago; while watching down, set trend down and clear the watch when the rolling lowest low is below the watched low, or clear the watch when the rolling highest high is above its value two bars ago; otherwise retain the trend.
Calculation: Adaptive TP percentage = base TP percentage recursively increased once for each DCA layer after the initial layer, multiplying by 1 plus the TP increment divided by 100 in percentage scaling mode, or adding the TP increment in fixed scaling mode.
Calculation: Adaptive DCA distance = base DCA percentage recursively increased once for each DCA layer after the initial layer, multiplying by 1 plus the DCA increment divided by 100 in percentage scaling mode, or adding the DCA increment in fixed scaling mode.
Calculation: Adaptive target price = average entry price multiplied by 1 plus adaptive TP percentage divided by 100 for a long position, or 1 minus adaptive TP percentage divided by 100 for a short position.
Calculation: Entry filter passes = no filter is selected, or the selected reward-space condition holds (prior rolling highest high exceeds close multiplied by 1 plus adaptive TP percentage divided by 100 for long, or prior rolling lowest low is below close multiplied by 1 minus adaptive TP percentage divided by 100 for short), or the selected entry-quality condition holds (the corresponding reward-space condition and the candle's close-to-low fraction for long or high-to-close fraction for short is below the entry-quality fraction threshold), or the selected RSI-extremity condition holds, or the selected fair-value condition holds (close at or below WMA(close, fair-value lookback) for long or at or above it for short), or the selected trend-direction condition holds (main trend up for long or down for short).
Calculation: Effective PNL = realized PNL on the open position plus unrealized PNL.
