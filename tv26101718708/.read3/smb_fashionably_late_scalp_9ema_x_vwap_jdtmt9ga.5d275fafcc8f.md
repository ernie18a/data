Calculation: T1-period low = lowest low since the start of the current T1 period
Calculation: T1-period high = highest high since the start of the current T1 period
Calculation: EMA slope (%) = 100 × (EMA(close, EMA lookback) − EMA(close, EMA lookback)[EMA slope lookback]) / EMA(close, EMA lookback), with lookbacks in chart bars
Calculation: VWAP slope (%) = 100 × (session VWAP(hlc3, volume, T1) − session VWAP(hlc3, volume, T1)[VWAP slope lookback]) / session VWAP(hlc3, volume, T1), with lookback in chart bars
Calculation: Raw cross trigger = (session VWAP − (1 − 2 / (EMA lookback + 1)) × EMA(close, EMA lookback)) / (2 / (EMA lookback + 1))
Calculation: Long trigger = max(Raw cross trigger, close + minimum price increment)
Calculation: Short trigger = min(Raw cross trigger, close − minimum price increment)
Calculation: Stop anchor = session VWAP for anticipatory entries or when the VWAP-to-extreme stop anchor is selected; otherwise signal-bar close
Calculation: Cross price = session VWAP for anticipatory entries or when VWAP at cross is selected; otherwise signal-bar close
Calculation: Long stop = Stop anchor − stop fraction × (Stop anchor − T1-period low), fixed at the signal bar
Calculation: Short stop = Stop anchor + stop fraction × (T1-period high − Stop anchor), fixed at the signal bar
Calculation: Long target = Cross price + (Cross price − T1-period low), fixed at the signal bar
Calculation: Short target = Cross price − (T1-period high − Cross price), fixed at the signal bar
Long: Longs enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND position flat AND (no prior position OR current bar index − last position or exit bar index ≥ cooldown lookback) AND EMA slope (%) > EMA slope threshold AND VWAP slope (%) ≤ VWAP slope threshold AND (turn requirement disabled OR chart bars since T1-period low was last set ≥ turn lookback) AND (session restriction disabled OR bar time is within either configured New York session window) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (flat veto disabled OR longest consecutive run of flat EMA-slope bars since the latest new T1-period high or low < max(1, round(flat-veto duration / max(chart-bar minutes, 1)))) AND ((anticipatory stop-entry mode AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) < session VWAP AND T1-period low < session VWAP AND Raw cross trigger > close AND Long stop < Long trigger AND Long target > Long trigger AND (reward:risk floor is nonpositive OR (Long target − Long trigger) / max(Long trigger − Long stop, minimum price increment) ≥ reward:risk floor) [arm a stop entry at Long trigger] OR next-open market-entry mode AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) crosses above session VWAP AND T1-period low < session VWAP AND Long stop < close AND Long target > close AND (reward:risk floor is nonpositive OR (Long target − close) / max(close − Long stop, minimum price increment) ≥ reward:risk floor))
Short: Shorts enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND position flat AND (no prior position OR current bar index − last position or exit bar index ≥ cooldown lookback) AND EMA slope (%) < −EMA slope threshold AND VWAP slope (%) ≥ −VWAP slope threshold AND (turn requirement disabled OR chart bars since T1-period high was last set ≥ turn lookback) AND (session restriction disabled OR bar time is within either configured New York session window) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback)) AND (flat veto disabled OR longest consecutive run of flat EMA-slope bars since the latest new T1-period high or low < max(1, round(flat-veto duration / max(chart-bar minutes, 1)))) AND ((anticipatory stop-entry mode AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) > session VWAP AND T1-period high > session VWAP AND Raw cross trigger < close AND Short stop > Short trigger AND Short target < Short trigger AND (reward:risk floor is nonpositive OR (Short trigger − Short target) / max(Short stop − Short trigger, minimum price increment) ≥ reward:risk floor) [arm a stop entry at Short trigger] OR next-open market-entry mode AND EMA(close, EMA lookback) crosses below session VWAP AND T1-period high > session VWAP AND Short stop > close AND Short target < close AND (reward:risk floor is nonpositive OR (close − Short target) / max(Short stop − close, minimum price increment) ≥ reward:risk floor))
Exit: long, low ≤ Long stop OR high ≥ Long target
Exit: short, high ≥ Short stop OR low ≤ Short target
