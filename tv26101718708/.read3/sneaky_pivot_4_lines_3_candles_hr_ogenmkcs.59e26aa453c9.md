Calculation: Doug lower anchor = Doug lower swing level if present and candle 1 low ≤ that level × (1 + touch tolerance / 100), otherwise Doug prior-session low
Calculation: Doug upper anchor = Doug upper swing level if present and candle 1 high ≥ that level × (1 − touch tolerance / 100), otherwise Doug prior-session high
Calculation: CryptoBosh lower anchor = CryptoBosh lower swing level if present and candle 1 low ≤ that level × (1 + touch tolerance / 100), otherwise CryptoBosh prior-session low
Calculation: CryptoBosh upper anchor = CryptoBosh upper swing level if present and candle 1 high ≥ that level × (1 − touch tolerance / 100), otherwise CryptoBosh prior-session high
Calculation: Doug long stop = selected Doug stop reference (lower anchor, candle 1 low, or min(candle 1 low, candle 2 low)) × (1 − Doug stop buffer / 100)
Calculation: Doug short stop = selected Doug stop reference (upper anchor, candle 1 high, or max(candle 1 high, candle 2 high)) × (1 + Doug stop buffer / 100)
Calculation: CryptoBosh long stop = selected CryptoBosh stop reference (lower anchor, candle 1 low, or min(candle 1 low, candle 2 low)) × (1 − CryptoBosh stop buffer / 100)
Calculation: CryptoBosh short stop = selected CryptoBosh stop reference (upper anchor, candle 1 high, or max(candle 1 high, candle 2 high)) × (1 + CryptoBosh stop buffer / 100)
Calculation: Long setup target = selected nearest or far opposite line × (1 − version-specific target buffer / 100), or, in fixed-R mode, entry + fixed-R multiple × (entry − stop); the far line is the upper swing level if present, otherwise the prior-session high
Calculation: Short setup target = selected nearest or far opposite line × (1 + version-specific target buffer / 100), or, in fixed-R mode, entry − fixed-R multiple × (stop − entry); the far line is the lower swing level if present, otherwise the prior-session low
