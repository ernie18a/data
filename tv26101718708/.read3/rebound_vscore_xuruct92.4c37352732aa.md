Calculation: Bars/day(T) = for bars of T, convert duration using separate long and medium duration cutoff/divisor parameters, returning the reciprocal of the matching divisor or one for day-length bars; for shorter bars, count bars per day and, at each day boundary, update the average from the prior average and completed-day count using separate smoothing weights, initializing it from the running count.
Calculation: Long-pattern metrics(T) = on bars of T, use the current T bar (including the forming bar in realtime) and map results to chart bars; find the newest highest high in the lookback and the lowest low since it, updating the stored top and bottom when newer; update the pattern when the current low is below the stored bottom, or when the top is at least the minimum downtrend plus minimum uptrend bars old and the bottom is at least the minimum uptrend bars old. Let D be the greatest high above the line between top and bottom, and S the smallest slope from the bottom to any later low. Return V-Fib=(high−line)/(D/Fib-unit fraction), rebound rate=100×S/bottom low×Bars/day(T), prior-leg rate=100×(top high+D−bottom low)/(top index−bottom index)/top high×Bars/day(T), V-Trend=rebound rate+prior-leg rate, and leading gain=((low−projected line from bottom with slope S)/(S×Bars/day(T)))×rebound rate; leading gain is zero if S is zero.
Calculation: Short-pattern metrics(T) = on bars of T, use the current T bar (including the forming bar in realtime) and map results to chart bars; find the newest lowest low in the lookback and the highest high since it, updating the stored bottom and peak when newer; update the pattern when the current high is above the stored peak, or when the bottom is at least the minimum downtrend plus minimum uptrend bars old and the peak is at least the minimum uptrend bars old. Let D be the greatest low below the line between bottom and peak, and S the greatest slope from the peak to any later high. Return V-Fib=(line−low)/(D/Fib-unit fraction), post-peak rate=100×S/peak high×Bars/day(T), prior-leg rate=100×(bottom low−D−peak high)/(bottom index−peak index)/bottom low×Bars/day(T), V-Trend=post-peak rate+prior-leg rate, and leading gain=((high−projected line from peak with slope S)/(S×Bars/day(T)))×post-peak rate; leading gain is zero if S is zero.
Calculation: Readiness(rate, V-Trend, leading gain, V-Fib) = normalized weighted average of zone fraction, trend fraction, and lead fraction using separate zone, trend, and lead weights; zone fraction is zero for invalid V-Fib, V-Fib/lower-zone-bound below the lower bound, one inside the inclusive entry zone, and max(0,(unlock-bound−V-Fib)/(unlock-bound−upper-zone-bound)) above it; trend fraction is the minimum of clamp01(1+(rate−minimum-rate)/minimum-rate) and clamp01(1+(V-Trend−minimum-V-Trend)/minimum-rate); lead fraction is clamp01(1+(leading gain−minimum-leading-gain)/minimum-leading-gain). For short-pattern metrics, pass the negated post-peak rate, V-Trend, and leading gain.
Calculation: Integrated long and short scores = score scale times the normalized weighted average of the respective Readiness values on T1, T2, and T3, using separate T1/T2/T3 score weights; V-Score = integrated long score−integrated short score.
Calculation: Entry-filter score(profile) = score scale times the normalized weighted average of the respective Readiness values on T1, T2, and T3, using separate T1/T2/T3 weights for each fast and slow profile.
Long: Long-pattern V-Fib on any of T1, T2, or T3 is between the inclusive minimum and maximum entry V-Fib bounds.
Short: Short-pattern V-Fib on any of T1, T2, or T3 is between the inclusive minimum and maximum entry V-Fib bounds.
Long: Long-pattern V-Trend on any of T1, T2, or T3 is at least the minimum V-Trend.
Short: Short-pattern V-Trend on any of T1, T2, or T3 is at most the negative minimum V-Trend.

Short: Integrated short score is at least the maximum CL score threshold.
