Calculation: Long retest confirmation = after a close above ORB high, low at or below ORB high plus retest tolerance sets the retest state; while retested, a close above ORB high confirms it, and confirmation remains valid until consumed by a long signal or reset at the next ORB session; state updates can occur on the same bar
Calculation: Short retest confirmation = after a close below ORB low, high at or above ORB low minus retest tolerance sets the retest state; while retested, a close below ORB low confirms it, and confirmation remains valid until consumed by a short signal or reset at the next ORB session; state updates can occur on the same bar
