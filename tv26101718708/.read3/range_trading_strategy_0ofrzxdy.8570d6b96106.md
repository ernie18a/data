Calculation: Pivot high = max(high, high[1]); its bar is the current bar if high > high[1], otherwise the previous bar
Calculation: Pivot low = min(low, low[1]); its bar is the current bar if low < low[1], otherwise the previous bar
Calculation: Eligible range levels = the previous completed T1 bar's high and low when the still-forming T1 close is within them, checked on chart bars when T1 changes; or the previous completed configured session's high and low, checked on chart bars when the session changes; when special ranges are enabled, also create a range high at each stored pivot low above a mitigated normal range low and a range low at each stored pivot high below a mitigated normal range high when a new normal range forms; discard a special range if its extant extreme is touched on its creation bar or the next chart bar
Long: A pending bullish setup exists from the latest eligible range-low mitigation (low <= range low; each new mitigation replaces the previous bullish setup), initialized with that bar's pivot high and updated to a later pivot high when high > the mitigated range low AND low < the mitigated range low AND pivot high > the mitigated range low; close > its current confirmation level AND (maximum-bar-distance filtering is disabled OR current bar index - confirmation pivot bar index <= maximum bars), OR reverse-on-stop-loss is enabled AND the previous position side was short AND the current position side is flat or long AND high >= the short stop recorded at entry AND a pivot low is available; set the stop to the pivot low at confirmation for a pending-signal entry or the current pivot low for a reversal entry
Short: A pending bearish setup exists from the latest eligible range-high mitigation (high >= range high; each new mitigation replaces the previous bearish setup), initialized with that bar's pivot low and updated to a later pivot low when high > the mitigated range high AND low < the mitigated range high AND pivot low < the mitigated range high; close < its current confirmation level AND (maximum-bar-distance filtering is disabled OR current bar index - confirmation pivot bar index <= maximum bars), OR reverse-on-stop-loss is enabled AND the previous position side was long AND the current position side is flat or short AND low <= the long stop recorded at entry AND a pivot high is available; set the stop to the pivot high at confirmation for a pending-signal entry or the current pivot high for a reversal entry
Exit: Long, low <= the long stop recorded at entry OR (take-profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + (entry price - long stop) × risk-reward parameter)
Exit: Short, high >= the short stop recorded at entry OR (take-profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - (short stop - entry price) × risk-reward parameter)
