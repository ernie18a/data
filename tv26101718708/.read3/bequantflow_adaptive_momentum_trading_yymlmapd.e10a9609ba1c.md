Calculation: Bull score = percentage formed from the current bull contribution plus stored prior chart-bar bull contributions, divided by the correspondingly weighted true ranges; a bar’s bull contribution is TR(high, low, prior close) × (close − low)/(high − low), or zero when high = low. Use equal weights when recency weighting is off; otherwise weight stored contributions in ascending recency order and give the current bar the next weight.
Calculation: Bear score = percentage formed from the current bear contribution plus stored prior chart-bar bear contributions, divided by the correspondingly weighted true ranges; a bar’s bear contribution is TR(high, low, prior close) × (high − close)/(high − low), or zero when high = low. Use the same weighting mode and weights as for the bull score.
Calculation: Cross level = (bull score + bear score)/2 at each confirmed chart-bar cross in either direction.
Calculation: Upper noise band = mean of recorded cross levels + (standard deviation of recorded cross levels × band deviation multiplier) when there are at least two levels; otherwise use the configured fallback midpoint plus the configured fallback deviation.
Calculation: Lower noise band = mean of recorded cross levels − (standard deviation of recorded cross levels × band deviation multiplier) when there are at least two levels; otherwise use the configured fallback midpoint minus the configured fallback deviation.
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry-bar low − ATR(short stop lookback) × long stop multiple.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initial long stop at entry; thereafter, on confirmed chart bars without a new long trigger, max(previous long trailing stop, close − ATR(short stop lookback) × long stop multiple).
Calculation: Long hard-stop price = long trailing stop − ATR(buffer lookback).
Calculation: Initial short stop = entry-bar high + ATR(short stop lookback) × short stop multiple.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initial short stop at entry; thereafter, on confirmed chart bars without a new short trigger, min(previous short trailing stop, close + ATR(short stop lookback) × short stop multiple).
Calculation: Short hard-stop price = short trailing stop + ATR(buffer lookback).
Calculation: Long partial target = entry close + absolute distance from entry close to initial long stop.
Calculation: Short partial target = entry close − absolute distance from entry close to initial short stop.
Long: Bull score > upper noise band AND bear score < lower noise band.
Short: Bear score > upper noise band AND bull score < lower noise band.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, bull score crosses above bear score, the cross level is above the upper noise band or below the lower noise band, recorded cross-level count ≥ cross history lookback, position is not long, AND (trend filter is off OR close > the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)).
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with no bullish cross, recorded cross-level count ≥ cross history lookback, AND (trend filter is off OR close > the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)), bull score > upper noise band, AND (position is flat OR position is not long and, on the prior chart bar, bars since bull score last exceeded the upper noise band ≥ breakout quiet lookback).
Long: After a bullish cross whose cross level is within the noise bands, on a confirmed chart bar bull score > bear score, the pending bullish cross has accumulated at least the confirmation lookback in chart bars, close > open, recorded cross-level count ≥ cross history lookback, position is not long, AND (trend filter is off OR close > the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)); cancel the pending cross if bull score is no longer above bear score, and reset its bar count to zero if close ≤ open when the count has reached the confirmation lookback.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a ghost position is active after a calendar-date change, the long stop remains active, open < close, AND (trend filter is off OR close > the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)).
Long: On a confirmed chart bar where the long stop exits, an opposing short trigger was flagged within the reversal window, short trades are enabled, AND (trend filter is off OR close < the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, bear score crosses above bull score, the cross level is above the upper noise band or below the lower noise band, no earlier primary entry branch applies, recorded cross-level count ≥ cross history lookback, short trades are enabled, position is not short, AND (trend filter is off OR close < the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with no bullish cross and no earlier primary entry branch applying, recorded cross-level count ≥ cross history lookback, short trades are enabled, AND (trend filter is off OR close < the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)), bear score > upper noise band, AND (position is flat OR position is not short and, on the prior chart bar, bars since bear score last exceeded the upper noise band ≥ breakout quiet lookback).
Short: After a bearish cross whose cross level is within the noise bands, on a confirmed chart bar bear score > bull score, the pending bearish cross has accumulated at least the confirmation lookback in chart bars, close < open, recorded cross-level count ≥ cross history lookback, short trades are enabled, position is not short, AND (trend filter is off OR close < the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)); cancel the pending cross if bear score is no longer above bull score, and reset its bar count to zero if close ≥ open when the count has reached the confirmation lookback.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a ghost position is active after a calendar-date change, the short stop remains active, open > close, short trades are enabled, AND (trend filter is off OR close < the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar where the short stop exits, an opposing long trigger was flagged within the reversal window, AND (trend filter is off OR close > the selected SMA, EMA, or DEMA(close, trend lookback)).
Exit: Long, long stop active AND (low < long hard-stop price OR (chart bar confirmed AND close < long trailing stop)) OR partial exits enabled AND high ≥ long partial target OR intraday square-off enabled AND a T1 intrabar available within the current chart bar, including a forming T1 bar, falls within the configured square-off minute while the long stop is active.
Exit: Short, short stop active AND (high > short hard-stop price OR (chart bar confirmed AND close > short trailing stop)) OR partial exits enabled AND low ≤ short partial target OR intraday square-off enabled AND a T1 intrabar available within the current chart bar, including a forming T1 bar, falls within the configured square-off minute while the short stop is active.
