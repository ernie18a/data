Calculation: For each trade signal, entry = close. Setup bounds are zone bounds for a zone setup; for a long sweep they are min(low, swept pool level) and close; for a short sweep they are close and max(high, swept pool level); for a momentum setup they are lowest(low, P_stopLookback) and close for long, or close and highest(high, P_stopLookback) for short. Initial stop = lower bound - ATR(P_ATR) * P_stopBuffer for long, or upper bound + ATR(P_ATR) * P_stopBuffer for short. Risk = max(abs(entry - initial stop), ATR(P_ATR) * P_minRiskMultiplier). Candidate targets are latest and previous swing highs/lows, T1/T2 equilibrium levels, Point of Control, prior high/low levels from T8–T10, and active liquidity-pool levels, retaining duplicates. For long, take the nearest three candidates > entry + ATR(P_ATR) * P_targetBuffer; for short, take the nearest three candidates < entry - ATR(P_ATR) * P_targetBuffer. Fill any missing target with entry plus or minus risk for target one, then the preceding target plus or minus risk for each later missing target. After target one is reached, the stop becomes entry.
