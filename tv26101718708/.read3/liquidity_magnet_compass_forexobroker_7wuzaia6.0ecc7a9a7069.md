Calculation: retained liquidity zones = the most recent zone-limit confirmed PivotHigh(high, pivot-lookback, pivot-lookback) and PivotLow(low, pivot-lookback, pivot-lookback) zones combined; each remains unswept until swept
Calculation: north magnet = min(number of unswept retained pivot-high zones above close × zone-count weight + proximity weight / normalized nearest-high distance × proximity multiplier, magnet cap), where normalized nearest-high distance = (nearest such zone − close) / ATR(ATR-lookback), or distance fallback if none
Calculation: south magnet = min(number of unswept retained pivot-low zones below close × zone-count weight + proximity weight / normalized nearest-low distance × proximity multiplier, magnet cap), where normalized nearest-low distance = (close − nearest such zone) / ATR(ATR-lookback), or distance fallback if none
Long: an unswept retained pivot-low zone exists with low < zone price AND zone price − low ≥ ATR(ATR-lookback) × sweep-size parameter AND (close > zone price OR rejection-close requirement is disabled) OR north magnet > south magnet + compass margin OR north magnet ≥ magnet-color threshold
Short: an unswept retained pivot-high zone exists with high > zone price AND high − zone price ≥ ATR(ATR-lookback) × sweep-size parameter AND (close < zone price OR rejection-close requirement is disabled) OR south magnet > north magnet + compass margin OR south magnet ≥ magnet-color threshold
