Long: no open position AND (close crosses above the highest high of the current day's premarket bars OR close crosses above high(T1, last completed bar, sampled on chart bars))
Short: no open position AND (close crosses below the lowest low of the current day's premarket bars OR close crosses below low(T1, last completed bar, sampled on chart bars))
Exit: long, close crosses above high(T1, bar still forming, sampled on chart bars)
Exit: short, close crosses below low(T1, bar still forming, sampled on chart bars)
