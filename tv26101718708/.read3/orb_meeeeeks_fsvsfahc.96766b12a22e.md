Calculation: Opening range = highest chart-bar high and lowest chart-bar low within each configured active window, reset on each calendar-day change and locked at each window end; rolling-window membership uses the current T3 bar while it is forming, and range values use chart bars
Calculation: Signed bar volume = volume × sign(close − open)
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of signed bar volume, reset on each calendar-day change
Calculation: Initial long stop = selected opening-range low, signal-bar low, entry price − ATR(stop lookback) × stop multiplier, entry price − opening-range width × range multiplier, or VWAP(hlc3), then subtract the configured tick or point offset
Calculation: Initial short stop = selected opening-range high, signal-bar high, entry price + ATR(stop lookback) × stop multiplier, entry price + opening-range width × range multiplier, or VWAP(hlc3), then add the configured tick or point offset
Calculation: Target distance i = selected entry risk × target i R multiple, ATR(target lookback) × target i ATR multiplier, opening-range width × target i range multiplier, or target i fixed tick or point distance; evaluate on the signal chart bar, with separate parameters for each target; long target i = entry price + distance i; short target i = entry price − distance i; if all targets are disabled, enable target 1
Long: Opening range is locked AND close crosses above opening-range high
Short: Opening range is locked AND close crosses below opening-range low
Long: Opening range is locked AND low ≤ opening-range high AND close > opening-range high AND, earlier the same calendar day, close crossed above opening-range high while long filters passed, with no later filtered downward breakout or long entry
Short: Opening range is locked AND high ≥ opening-range low AND close < opening-range low AND, earlier the same calendar day, close crossed below opening-range low while short filters passed, with no later filtered upward breakout or short entry
Long: Long direction is allowed AND ((retest is required AND low ≤ opening-range high AND close > opening-range high AND an earlier same-day upward breakout passed the long filters, with no later filtered downward breakout or long entry) OR (retest is not required AND close crosses above opening-range high)) AND (bias RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, separate bias lookback, T1, lookahead off; current T1 bar in realtime, sampled on chart bars) > long bias threshold) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND (order-flow filter is disabled OR (EMA(signed bar volume, delta lookback) > minimum delta strength AND cumulative volume delta > its previous chart-bar value)) AND (entry RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, separate entry lookback, T2, lookahead off; current T2 bar in realtime, sampled on chart bars) > separate long entry threshold) AND (session filter is disabled OR within configured trading session) AND (one signal per direction per calendar day is disabled OR no long signal has occurred that day) AND close − initial long stop > minimum tick size
Short: Short direction is allowed AND ((retest is required AND high ≥ opening-range low AND close < opening-range low AND an earlier same-day downward breakout passed the short filters, with no later filtered upward breakout or short entry) OR (retest is not required AND close crosses below opening-range low)) AND (bias RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, separate bias lookback, T1, lookahead off; current T1 bar in realtime, sampled on chart bars) < separate short bias threshold) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND (order-flow filter is disabled OR (EMA(signed bar volume, delta lookback) < −minimum delta strength AND cumulative volume delta < its previous chart-bar value)) AND (entry RSI filter is disabled OR RSI(close, separate entry lookback, T2, lookahead off; current T2 bar in realtime, sampled on chart bars) < separate short entry threshold) AND (session filter is disabled OR within configured trading session) AND (one signal per direction per calendar day is disabled OR no short signal has occurred that day) AND initial short stop − close > minimum tick size
Exit: long, (break-even is disabled OR no enabled target has been touched) AND low ≤ initial long stop OR break-even is enabled AND an enabled target has been touched AND low ≤ entry price OR high ≥ highest-index enabled long target OR return-inside-opening-range exit is enabled AND close < opening-range high OR VWAP invalidation exit is enabled AND close < VWAP(hlc3) OR session filter and session-end closing are enabled AND the trading session just ended
Exit: short, (break-even is disabled OR no enabled target has been touched) AND high ≥ initial short stop OR break-even is enabled AND an enabled target has been touched AND high ≥ entry price OR low ≤ highest-index enabled short target OR return-inside-opening-range exit is enabled AND close > opening-range low OR VWAP invalidation exit is enabled AND close > VWAP(hlc3) OR session filter and session-end closing are enabled AND the trading session just ended
