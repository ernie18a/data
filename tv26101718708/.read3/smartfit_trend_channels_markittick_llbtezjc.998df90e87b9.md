Calculation: Active regression channel = linear regression of the selected filter of close[1] over completed chart bars from the active anchor through the prior completed bar; filter choices are raw close, SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA(WMA(source, filter lookback), filter lookback), VWMA(VWMA(VWMA(source, filter lookback), filter lookback), filter lookback), HMA, LLAMA(source, n) = SMA(source, n) + (source - source[n]) / 2, or a scalar Kalman filter. Regression length is capped by a maximum-history parameter. Upper and lower bands at the regression endpoint are the endpoint plus or minus the deviation parameter times the sample standard deviation of regression residuals; fit is Pearson correlation of source with time. Initialize the anchor at the first chart bar. Confirmed swing highs and lows are strict extremes over a window of 2 × pivot-lookback + 1 chart bars; the pivot lookback is auto-selected from chart timeframe or uses a manual parameter. After a new breakout meeting minimum bar-count and absolute-fit thresholds, regardless of the ADX filter, reset the anchor to the latest low pivot for an upper breakout or latest high pivot for a lower breakout if that pivot is after the existing anchor and before the breakout and its candidate-segment absolute fit meets the threshold; otherwise reset at the breakout-evaluation bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close[1] > active upper channel band AND active segment length >= minimum fit bars AND |active Pearson fit| >= minimum fit quality AND NOT (close[2] > previous upper channel band AND previous segment length >= minimum fit bars AND |previous Pearson fit| >= minimum fit quality) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback) on the confirmed chart bar >= ADX threshold).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close[1] < active lower channel band AND active segment length >= minimum fit bars AND |active Pearson fit| >= minimum fit quality AND NOT (close[2] < previous lower channel band AND previous segment length >= minimum fit bars AND |previous Pearson fit| >= minimum fit quality) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback, same ADX lookback) on the confirmed chart bar >= ADX threshold).
