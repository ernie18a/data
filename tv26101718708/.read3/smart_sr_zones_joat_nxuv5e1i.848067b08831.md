Calculation: Active support and resistance zones = confirm PivotLow(low, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) and PivotHigh(high, left pivot lookback, right pivot lookback) only when ATR(high, low, close, shared normalization lookback) is positive; retain the most recent pivot-memory capacity per side with pivot-bar volume (use the volume fallback if missing or nonpositive) and lower wick for lows or upper wick for highs. In pivot order, seed each unused pivot and add later unused pivots within ATR × cluster-distance multiplier of the seed price and at least minimum pivot spacing from every cluster member. Keep clusters with at least minimum pivot count; calculate center as the pivot-price weighted mean, where weight = (weight base + shared volume/wick coefficient × normalized pivot volume + shared volume/wick coefficient × normalized wick) / (decay base + max(minimum pivot age, bars since pivot) / age-decay scale), normalized volume is pivot volume / SMA(volume, shared normalization lookback) or its fallback when the average is nonpositive, and normalized wick is wick / ATR. Set zone top and bottom to center ± ATR × zone half-width multiplier; retain the strongest maximum-zones-per-side clusters. Rebuild clusters each bar, drop zones no longer represented, and exclude zones already marked broken within the archive-match distance; a zone is active only while its top and bottom are positive and its close has not broken it.
Calculation: Broken zones = formerly active support zones with a close below bottom or resistance zones with a close above top, retaining side and death bar, including zones whose previous close was already beyond the boundary; do not include zones dropped because their cluster disappeared. Merge same-side archived zones within ATR × cluster-distance multiplier × archive-merge coefficient and retain the most recent archive-capacity zones. Exclude reformed clusters within that same distance from active zones.
Calculation: Bounce cooldown passes when no support or resistance bounce signal occurred during the prior cooldown bars at a zone center within ATR × cluster-distance multiplier; retain only the most recent cooldown-history-capacity signal centers.
Long: An active support zone has low <= top AND close >= bottom AND close >= open AND the bounce cooldown passes.
Long: An active support zone has low <= top AND close > bottom.
Long: A broken resistance zone has bar_index - death bar > 0 AND bar_index - death bar <= retest window AND low <= top AND close >= bottom AND close >= open.
Long: An active resistance zone that existed before the current bar has close > top AND close[1] <= top.
Short: An active resistance zone has high >= bottom AND close <= top AND close <= open AND the bounce cooldown passes.
Short: An active resistance zone has high >= bottom AND close < top.
Short: A broken support zone has bar_index - death bar > 0 AND bar_index - death bar <= retest window AND high >= bottom AND close <= top AND close <= open.
Short: An active support zone that existed before the current bar has close < bottom AND close[1] >= bottom.
