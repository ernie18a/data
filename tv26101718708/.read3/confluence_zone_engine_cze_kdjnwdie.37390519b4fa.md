Calculation: Quality = quality weight * clamp(1 - range / (tightness scale * close), 0, 1) + (1 - quality weight) * clamp(1 - age / max anchor age, 0, 1), where range = highest high minus lowest low since the anchor bar and age = bars since the anchor bar
Calculation: Level = the set of price values on each bar: for each live chart-timeframe anchor (age <= max anchor age AND Quality >= quality threshold), where anchors are PivotHigh(high, L, L) and PivotLow(low, L, L) for each enabled pivot length L, starting on the bar that confirms the pivot, at the pivot's high or low: AVWAP(typical price, anchor) since the anchor, tagged pivot-high or pivot-low by anchor type and counted once; POC, VAH and VAL of the anchored volume profile since the anchor, with POC counted POC weight times and VAH and VAL each counted VA weight times; plus POC of each expired chart anchor that no bar in the last naked lookback bars straddled (low <= POC <= high), counted naked weight times; plus AVWAP(typical price, anchor) of the same pivot anchors on each of T1, T2, T3, computed on that timeframe's bars and read on chart bars as the value of the last completed bar of that timeframe, tagged by anchor type and counted once
Calculation: Band = the (lowest, highest) price of the chosen run, where the Level values sorted ascending are greedily partitioned into runs (each run starts at the lowest unassigned value and includes the following values within confluence tolerance * close of that start value), the chosen run is the one with the largest total count, and it is used only if its total count >= min weighted count
Calculation: Zone = on each bar where Band exists: if an Active zone (not Pending as of the previous bar) has midpoint within merge tolerance * close of the Band midpoint, its band becomes the union of its band and Band and its last-confirmation bar becomes this bar; otherwise a new Active zone is created with band = Band and last-confirmation bar = this bar; a zone is Pending when bars since its last confirmation > zone timeout
Calculation: Resolution = the event on the first bar where a zone not yet Resolved is Pending and bars since its last confirmation >= outcome bars; the zone becomes Resolved with role Support if (close - midpoint) / midpoint * 100 > outcome threshold, role Resistance if (close - midpoint) / midpoint * 100 < -outcome threshold, otherwise role Chop, where midpoint is the zone's band midpoint on that bar
Calculation: Lean = for a zone created on this bar, (count of pivot-low AVWAP Levels in Band minus count of pivot-high AVWAP Levels in Band) plus side, where side = +1 if zone midpoint < close, -1 if zone midpoint > close, else 0, and side is 0 unless |close - zone midpoint| <= lean distance * ATR(ATR length)
Long: exists a Resolved zone with role Support such that (close[1] < zone low OR close[1] > zone high) AND zone low <= close <= zone high, on a confirmed bar
Short: exists a Resolved zone with role Resistance such that (close[1] < zone low OR close[1] > zone high) AND zone low <= close <= zone high, on a confirmed bar
Long: exists a Resolved zone with role Resistance such that close > zone high * (1 + flip threshold) for flip bars consecutive confirmed bars ending on this bar, none before its resolution or latest flip
Short: exists a Resolved zone with role Support such that close < zone low * (1 - flip threshold) for flip bars consecutive confirmed bars ending on this bar, none before its resolution or latest flip
Long: exists Resolution with role Support
Short: exists Resolution with role Resistance
Long: exists a zone created on this bar with Lean >= lean threshold
Short: exists a zone created on this bar with Lean <= -lean threshold
Long: exists a Resolved zone with role Support and zone midpoint <= close AND NOT (exists a Resolved zone with role Resistance and zone midpoint >= close)
Short: exists a Resolved zone with role Resistance and zone midpoint >= close AND NOT (exists a Resolved zone with role Support and zone midpoint <= close)
Exit: long, exists a Resolved zone with role Support and close < zone low - break buffer * close
Exit: short, exists a Resolved zone with role Resistance and close > zone high + break buffer * close
