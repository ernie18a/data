Calculation: Bull trend = EMA(close, short EMA lookback) > EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, short EMA lookback, T1) > EMA(close, medium EMA lookback, T1) AND close > EMA(close, medium EMA lookback, T1); calculate the T1 EMAs on T1 bars and sample them on chart bars, using the last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in real time; short < medium < long lookback
Calculation: Bear trend = EMA(close, short EMA lookback) < EMA(close, medium EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, long EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, short EMA lookback, T1) < EMA(close, medium EMA lookback, T1) AND close < EMA(close, medium EMA lookback, T1); calculate and sample the T1 EMAs as for Bull trend
Calculation: Strong ADX = ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > ADX strength threshold
Calculation: Long confidence = trend weight if Bull trend, plus ADX weight if Strong ADX, plus DI weight if +DI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > -DI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback), plus RSI weight if RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long RSI lower threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI upper threshold, plus MACD weight if MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) > MACD signal line AND MACD histogram > 0, plus volume weight if volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) × volume multiplier, plus ATR expansion weight if ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR average lookback) × ATR expansion multiplier, plus sweep weight if low < lowest(low[1], swing lookback) AND close > lowest(low[1], swing lookback), plus FVG weight if low > high[gap lookback] AND close[1] > high[gap lookback], plus body weight if close > open AND close − open > ATR(ATR lookback) × body ATR fraction
Calculation: Short confidence = trend weight if Bear trend, plus ADX weight if Strong ADX, plus DI weight if -DI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > +DI(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback), plus RSI weight if RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI lower threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short RSI upper threshold, plus MACD weight if MACD line(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) < MACD signal line AND MACD histogram < 0, plus volume weight if volume > SMA(volume, volume average lookback) × volume multiplier, plus ATR expansion weight if ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR average lookback) × ATR expansion multiplier, plus sweep weight if high > highest(high[1], swing lookback) AND close < highest(high[1], swing lookback), plus FVG weight if high < low[gap lookback] AND close[1] < low[gap lookback], plus body weight if close < open AND open − close > ATR(ATR lookback) × body ATR fraction
Calculation: Adaptive ATR factor = high-ADX ATR factor if ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > higher ADX threshold; otherwise medium-ADX ATR factor if ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing lookback) > ADX strength threshold; otherwise low-ADX ATR factor
Calculation: Long stop distance = absolute value of entry-bar close − (entry-bar low − entry-bar ATR(ATR lookback) × entry-bar adaptive ATR factor)
Calculation: Short stop distance = absolute value of entry-bar close − (entry-bar high + entry-bar ATR(ATR lookback) × entry-bar adaptive ATR factor)
Calculation: Armed swing high = latest confirmed ta.pivothigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback) whose value changed; arm it when confirmed and disarm it after a long signal if Structure display is enabled
Calculation: Armed swing low = latest confirmed ta.pivotlow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback) whose value changed; arm it when confirmed and disarm it after a short signal if Structure display is enabled
Long: flat at the start of the chart bar AND the selected breakout price (high for Wicks, close for Body) > armed swing high AND Long confidence >= confidence threshold AND Bull trend AND Strong ADX
Short: flat at the start of the chart bar AND the selected breakout price (low for Wicks, close for Body) < armed swing low AND Short confidence >= confidence threshold AND Bear trend AND Strong ADX
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + RR ratio × Long stop distance × selected target multiple OR (stop trigger is Close AND close <= entry price − Long stop distance) OR (stop trigger is High/Low AND low <= entry price − Long stop distance)
Exit: Short, low <= entry price − RR ratio × Short stop distance × selected target multiple OR (stop trigger is Close AND close >= entry price + Short stop distance) OR (stop trigger is High/Low AND high >= entry price + Short stop distance)
