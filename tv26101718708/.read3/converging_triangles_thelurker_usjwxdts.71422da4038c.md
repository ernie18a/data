Calculation: ProjectedPrice(p1,b1,p2,b2,b) = p2+(p2-p1)*(b-b2)/(b2-b1) on arithmetic scale; on log scale when p1 and p2 are positive, p2*exp((ln(p2)-ln(p1))*(b-b2)/(b2-b1)).
Calculation: UpperSource = close in close mode, high in wick mode; LowerSource = close in close mode, low in wick mode.
Calculation: ActiveTriangle(P, enabled) = enabled and a frozen structure formed from a falling projected line through two confirmed pivot highs and a rising projected line through two confirmed pivot lows, each pivot confirmed using P bars on each side; each pivot pair's bar span is at least MinSpan and at most P*SpanMultiplier; UpperSource never exceeds its projected line by more than Epsilon and LowerSource never falls below its projected line by more than Epsilon from the first pivot through detection; the pivot ranges overlap by at least MinOverlap bars; and w/(w-wNext) is between MinApex and MaxApex, where w = upper line price now - lower line price now and wNext is the same width one bar later. Freeze both lines at detection; test breaks on subsequent confirmed bars through the rounded projected apex bar, and expire the structure after that bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, for at least one enabled scale P_L, P_M, or P_S with an ActiveTriangle, UpperSource > upper line price*(1+Epsilon) and (LowerSource is not below lower line price*(1-Epsilon) or UpperSource-upper line price >= lower line price-LowerSource).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, for at least one enabled scale P_L, P_M, or P_S with an ActiveTriangle, LowerSource < lower line price*(1-Epsilon) and (UpperSource is not above upper line price*(1+Epsilon) or lower line price-LowerSource > UpperSource-upper line price).
