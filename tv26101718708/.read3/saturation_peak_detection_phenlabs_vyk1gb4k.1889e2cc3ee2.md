Calculation: bull strength = round(clamp((neutral momentum − trough RSI-space momentum) × bull extremity weight, 0, bull extremity cap) + clamp((bull recovery percentage − adaptive pullback) × pullback score weight + pullback score base, 0, pullback score cap) + clamp((volume at trough / (SMA(volume, volume average lookback) + epsilon) − 1) × volume score weight, 0, volume score cap) + divergence bonus when divergence filtering is enabled, a prior bull saturation exists, the current trough-bar low is below the prior accepted trough-bar low, and the current rolling momentum trough is above the prior accepted trough momentum)
