Calculation: Structural phase(T) = bullish expansion or correction, bearish expansion or correction, or neutral; calculated on T bars using ATR(ATR lookback), separate compression and large-swing thresholds, an expansion factor, and an optional candle-body-to-ATR momentum filter; sampled onto chart bars from the last completed T bar historically and the forming T bar in realtime.
Long: Structural phase(T1) is bullish AND close(T1, sampled onto chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime) >= EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, sampled the same way).
Short: (Structural phase(T1) is bearish AND close(T1, sampled onto chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime) <= EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, sampled the same way)) OR (Structural phase(T1) is bullish AND close(T1, sampled onto chart bars from the last completed T1 bar historically and the forming T1 bar in realtime) >= EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, sampled the same way) AND T1 bullish expansion AND chart close > T1 structural low + ATR(chart, ATR lookback) * large-swing threshold AND NOT ((T1 upward move AND chart close - T1 swing low < ATR(chart, ATR lookback) * compression threshold) OR (T1 downward move AND T1 swing high - chart close < ATR(chart, ATR lookback) * compression threshold)) AND Structural phase(T2) is bullish correction, sampled onto chart bars from the last completed T2 bar historically and the forming T2 bar in realtime).
