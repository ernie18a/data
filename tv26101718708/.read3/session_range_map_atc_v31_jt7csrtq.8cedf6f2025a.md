Long: chart close crosses above the highest high of chart bars in the previous completed regular session OR chart close crosses above the highest high of chart bars in the current pre-market session OR opening range enabled AND opening range complete AND chart close > the highest high of chart bars from regular-session open through the opening-range duration + minimum tick
Short: chart close crosses below the lowest low of chart bars in the previous completed regular session OR chart close crosses below the lowest low of chart bars in the current pre-market session OR opening range enabled AND opening range complete AND chart close < the lowest low of chart bars from regular-session open through the opening-range duration - minimum tick
