Calculation: Stop = for a long CISD, the nearest chart-bar low below its CISD level that is strictly lower than the lows on both sides for the selected swing strength; for a short CISD, the nearest chart-bar high above its CISD level that is strictly higher than the highs on both sides; search strengths from minimum to maximum, then offsets from nearest to farthest within the stop lookback.
Calculation: Target = CISD level + (long ? 1 : -1) × abs(CISD level − Stop) × risk/reward parameter
Long: On chart bars, a candidate created when the still-forming T1 bar starts from its last completed candle with close <= open and abs(close − open) >= minimum OB body size; within the candidate age limit, chart close >= that candle's open AND (previous chart close < that open OR this is the candidate-creation bar); bullish signals enabled; and, if first-active-only is enabled, no active bullish CISD.
Short: On chart bars, a candidate created when the still-forming T1 bar starts from its last completed candle with close > open and abs(close − open) >= minimum OB body size; within the candidate age limit, chart close <= that candle's open AND (previous chart close > that open OR this is the candidate-creation bar); bearish signals enabled; and, if first-active-only is enabled, no active bearish CISD.
Exit: Long, automatic SL/TP removal enabled AND Stop exists AND (low <= Stop OR high >= Target)
Exit: Short, automatic SL/TP removal enabled AND Stop exists AND (high >= Stop OR low <= Target)
