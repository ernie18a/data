Calculation: T1 first chart-bar open and close = on chart bars, the open and close of the first chart bar in each T1 interval, held until the next T1 interval
Calculation: Latest bullish order-block zone = the range from low[1] to high[1] when close[1] < open[1] AND close > high[1], retaining the most recent such range
Calculation: Latest bearish order-block zone = the range from low[1] to high[1] when close[1] > open[1] AND close < low[1], retaining the most recent such range
Long: T1 first chart-bar close > T1 first chart-bar open AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND RSI(close, lookback) > long threshold AND close crosses above EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND (close <= lowest(low, lookback) * support multiplier OR close is within the latest bullish order-block zone)
Short: T1 first chart-bar close < T1 first chart-bar open AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND close < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND RSI(close, lookback) < short threshold AND close crosses below EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND (close >= highest(high, lookback) * resistance multiplier OR close is within the latest bearish order-block zone)
