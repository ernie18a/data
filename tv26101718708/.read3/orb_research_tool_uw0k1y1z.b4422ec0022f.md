Calculation: Opening-range high = highest(high, T1 bars from the New York session open until the opening-range duration ends)
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest(low, T1 bars from the New York session open until the opening-range duration ends)
Long: position is flat AND weekday and month filters pass AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR chart close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND ((T1 first-close breakout mode is enabled AND the opening range is locked AND this is the first T1 close above Opening-range high today) OR (T1 retest mode is enabled AND the opening range is locked AND a T1 close above Opening-range high has occurred today on or before this bar AND this is the first retest today with low <= Opening-range high + retest buffer AND close >= Opening-range high) OR (chart-close breakout mode is enabled AND the opening range is locked AND chart close > Opening-range high))
Short: position is flat AND weekday and month filters pass AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR chart close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND ((T1 first-close breakout mode is enabled AND the opening range is locked AND this is the first T1 close below Opening-range low today) OR (T1 retest mode is enabled AND the opening range is locked AND a T1 close below Opening-range low has occurred today on or before this bar AND this is the first retest today with high >= Opening-range low - retest buffer AND close <= Opening-range low) OR (chart-close breakout mode is enabled AND the opening range is locked AND chart close < Opening-range low))
Exit: Long, low <= Opening-range low(T1, last completed bar) - stop buffer OR high >= entry price + (entry price - (Opening-range low(T1, last completed bar) - stop buffer)) OR datetime is at or after the force-flat time in New York
Exit: Short, high >= Opening-range high(T1, last completed bar) + stop buffer OR low <= entry price - ((Opening-range high(T1, last completed bar) + stop buffer) - entry price) OR datetime is at or after the force-flat time in New York
