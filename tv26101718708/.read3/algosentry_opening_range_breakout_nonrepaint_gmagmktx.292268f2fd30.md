Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured session window and timezone; reset at session start and freeze at session end.
Calculation: Session volume POC = on chart bars, divide each included bar's volume equally among equal-price rows spanning the included bars' minimum low to maximum high; use the midpoint of the lowest-price row with greatest allocated volume, with a configurable row count, over the selected window from session start or trading-day start through session end; freeze at session end and make unavailable unless the resulting price is within the opening range and the included price span is nonzero.
Long: After the opening range freezes and within the configurable chart-bar tracking limit, the first confirmation close above the opening-range high, with no prior long break in the session and either two-direction allowance enabled or no prior short break; use chart close at chart-bar close when confirmation is on the chart timeframe, otherwise use the last completed T1 close on chart bars when a new T1 bar begins.
Short: After the opening range freezes and within the configurable chart-bar tracking limit, the first confirmation close below the opening-range low, with no prior short break in the session and either two-direction allowance enabled or no prior long break; use chart close at chart-bar close when confirmation is on the chart timeframe, otherwise use the last completed T1 close on chart bars when a new T1 bar begins.
Long: After a long break, the first confirmed chart bar strictly after that break and within the tracking limit, while the retest sequence is enabled and before any prior long retest, whose low is at or below any enabled retest zone: opening-range high, opening-range high minus opening-range width times the configurable depth percentage, or available session volume POC.
Short: After a short break, the first confirmed chart bar strictly after that break and within the tracking limit, while the retest sequence is enabled and before any prior short retest, whose high is at or above any enabled retest zone: opening-range low, opening-range low plus opening-range width times the configurable depth percentage, or available session volume POC.
Long: After a long retest, the first confirmed chart bar strictly after that retest and within the tracking limit, with no prior long trend, whose close is above the opening-range high.
Short: After a short retest, the first confirmed chart bar strictly after that retest and within the tracking limit, with no prior short trend, whose close is below the opening-range low.
