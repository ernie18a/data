Calculation: Market structure = confirmed pivot highs and lows using a pivot lookback on each side; track the running extreme in the current trend, arm a continuation BOS level after a new extreme and the configured count of consecutive countertrend candles, and confirm BOS when close reaches or exceeds that level in the trend direction. Close reaching or exceeding the opposite CHoCH protect level flips the trend. In adjusted-points mode, update the protect level from confirmed pivots; bound swing searches by the swing-search cap. When sweep detection is enabled, a wick through a BOS or CHoCH level followed by a close back across it marks a sweep instead of the corresponding break.
Calculation: Active parent and child fib ranges = parent range from the saved trend-origin extreme to the running extreme of the current trend cycle, locked at a trend change; child range from the current protect swing to the running extreme of the current leg, locked at BOS and restarted from the new protect swing. Use a forming range when available, otherwise the latest locked range.
Calculation: Fib level(high, low, direction, ratio) = direction is bullish ? high - ratio × (high - low) : low + ratio × (high - low). A fib zone is the interval between its two Fib levels.
Calculation: Parent Fib VWAP = anchored VWAP of HLC3 and volume from the parent fib start.
Calculation: ALMA SuperTrend direction = ALMA(close, ALMA length, offset, sigma) with upper and lower bands at ALMA ± factor × standard deviation(close, SD lookback); ratchet the upper band down and lower band up using their prior values and prior close. Direction switches bullish above the upper band or bearish below the lower band, and otherwise persists. Calculate on T1 bars; chart bars take T1 values with lookahead off, using the forming T1 bar in real time.
Long: Market structure trend = bullish OR market structure marks a downside liquidity sweep OR parent fib is valid and bullish OR active child fib is valid and bullish OR ALMA SuperTrend direction = bullish OR (parent or active child fib is valid and bullish AND the bar range overlaps its golden zone or deep zone) OR close crosses above the parent Fib VWAP OR close crosses above the parent or active child fib 0% level OR a bearish parent or active child fib reaches its 100% level.
Short: Market structure trend = bearish OR market structure marks an upside liquidity sweep OR parent fib is valid and bearish OR active child fib is valid and bearish OR ALMA SuperTrend direction = bearish OR (parent or active child fib is valid and bearish AND the bar range overlaps its golden zone or deep zone) OR close crosses below the parent Fib VWAP OR close crosses below the parent or active child fib 0% level OR a bullish parent or active child fib reaches its 100% level.
