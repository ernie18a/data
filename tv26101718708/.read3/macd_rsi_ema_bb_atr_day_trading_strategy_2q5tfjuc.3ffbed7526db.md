Calculation: Initial risk distance = ATR(ATR lookback) on the entry-signal bar * initial stop multiplier
Calculation: Long trailing stop = close - ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing-stop multiplier when undefined; otherwise the greater of its prior value and that value on each long-position chart bar; reset to undefined when the trailing-stop exit or close-session exit occurs
Calculation: Short trailing stop = close + ATR(ATR lookback) * trailing-stop multiplier when undefined; otherwise the lesser of its prior value and that value on each short-position chart bar; reset to undefined when the trailing-stop exit or close-session exit occurs
Long: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar calculated on T1 and carried to chart bars) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar calculated on T1 and carried to chart bars) AND MACD line of MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback) crosses above its signal line AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long lower RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI overbought threshold AND close > VWAP(hlc3) AND close > open AND high > high[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier AND ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) * ATR-average multiplier AND bar time is within the trading-session parameter
Short: EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar calculated on T1 and carried to chart bars) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback, T1 still-forming bar calculated on T1 and carried to chart bars) AND MACD line of MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback) crosses below its signal line AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI oversold threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < short upper RSI threshold AND close < VWAP(hlc3) AND close < open AND low < low[1] AND volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume multiplier AND ATR(ATR lookback) > SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR-average lookback) * ATR-average multiplier AND bar time is within the trading-session parameter
Exit: long, low <= entry-signal-bar close - initial risk distance OR high >= entry-signal-bar close + initial risk distance * reward ratio OR close <= long trailing stop OR bar time is within the close-session parameter
Exit: short, high >= entry-signal-bar close + initial risk distance OR low <= entry-signal-bar close - initial risk distance * reward ratio OR close >= short trailing stop OR bar time is within the close-session parameter
