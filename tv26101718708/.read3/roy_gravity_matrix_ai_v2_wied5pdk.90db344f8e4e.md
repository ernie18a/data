Calculation: Score = sum over i=1..7 and j=1..5 of W_ij × Direction(T_i,P_j); T1..T7 are separate timeframes ordered shortest to longest, P1..P5 are separate EMA lookbacks ordered shortest to longest, and each W_ij is a separate positive parameter
Calculation: Trigger timeframe Q = T1 when main timeframe M is T1 or T2; otherwise Q is the immediately shorter timeframe than M, with M selected from T1..T7
Long: For any T_i and P_j, Direction(T_i,P_j)=+1 AND EMA(close,P_j,T_i) has not crossed above or below any EMA(close,P_k,T_i), k≠j, within L T_i bars; OR, for any T_i and distinct P_i,P_j, EMA(close,P_i,T_i) crossed above EMA(close,P_j,T_i) within L T_i bars; OR Score>0; OR Direction(T6,P3)=+1 AND Direction(T7,P3)=+1 AND ((Direction(M,P2)=+1 AND Direction(Q,P2)=+1) OR Direction(M,P2)=-1); OR Score>0 AND Direction(M,P2)=+1 AND Direction(M,P3)=+1 AND Direction(Q,P2)=+1
