Calculation: T1 SuperTrend direction = on each chart bar, use hl2 and ATR(ATR lookback) from the T1 bar still forming to form lower and upper bands as hl2 − multiplier × ATR and hl2 + multiplier × ATR; update the lower band to max(current lower band, prior chart bar's lower band) when the prior chart bar's sampled T1 close is above the prior lower band, otherwise use the current lower band; update the upper band to min(current upper band, prior chart bar's upper band) when the prior chart bar's sampled T1 close is below the prior upper band, otherwise use the current upper band; direction is bullish when the current chart bar's sampled T1 close is above the prior upper band, bearish when below the prior lower band, and otherwise the prior direction, defaulting to bullish. Use the chart SuperTrend's ATR lookback and multiplier.
Long: chart-bar close crosses above the current SuperTrend line OR chart-bar SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND T1 SuperTrend direction is bullish
Short: chart-bar close crosses below the current SuperTrend line OR chart-bar SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND T1 SuperTrend direction is bearish
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit tick offset OR low <= entry price − stop-loss tick offset
Exit: short, low <= entry price − take-profit tick offset OR high >= entry price + stop-loss tick offset
