Calculation: Range Filter = on chart bars, let R = EMA(EMA(abs(close − close[1]), sampling period), 2 × sampling period − 1) × range multiplier; when R is unavailable, use close; otherwise, if close > prior Range Filter use max(prior Range Filter, close − R), else use min(prior Range Filter, close + R), with the initial prior value equal to close
Calculation: Range-filter direction = 1 when Range Filter > its prior value, −1 when Range Filter < its prior value, and its prior direction otherwise, initially 0
Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi = on each input timeframe, first EMA-smooth open, high, low, and close using the shared pre-smoothing length; set HA close to their average, HA open to the average of smoothed open and close on the first bar and thereafter the average of prior HA open and prior HA close, HA high to their maximum with smoothed high, and HA low to their minimum with smoothed low; EMA-smooth each HA value using the shared post-smoothing length
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = for a long, max(entry price − trailing-stop ATR multiple × ATR at entry, highest since entry of close − trailing-stop ATR multiple × ATR); for a short, min(entry price + trailing-stop ATR multiple × ATR at entry, lowest since entry of close + trailing-stop ATR multiple × ATR)
Long: position is flat AND chart bar index > 4 × range-filter sampling period AND ATR and Range Filter range are available AND no exit has occurred or at least the cooldown number of chart bars have elapsed since the last exit AND direction mode permits long AND Range-filter direction = 1 AND Smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= open on the current chart bar AND, if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, Smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= open on the last completed bar of higher timeframe T1 AND the same bullish trend agreement did not hold on the preceding chart bar AND, if the SMI filter is enabled, SMI(lookback, first smoothing length, second smoothing length) < SMI threshold
Short: position is flat AND chart bar index > 4 × range-filter sampling period AND ATR and Range Filter range are available AND no exit has occurred or at least the cooldown number of chart bars have elapsed since the last exit AND direction mode permits short AND Range-filter direction = −1 AND Smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < open on the current chart bar AND, if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, Smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < open on the last completed bar of higher timeframe T1 AND the same bearish trend agreement did not hold on the preceding chart bar AND, if the SMI filter is enabled, SMI(lookback, first smoothing length, second smoothing length) > −SMI threshold
Exit: Long, low <= long ATR trailing stop OR (take-profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit multiple × trailing-stop ATR multiple × ATR at entry) OR (Range-filter-flip exit is enabled AND Range-filter direction = −1)
Exit: Short, high >= short ATR trailing stop OR (take-profit is enabled AND low <= entry price − take-profit multiple × trailing-stop ATR multiple × ATR at entry) OR (Range-filter-flip exit is enabled AND Range-filter direction = 1)
