Calculation: Untapped swing-high pools = confirmed pivot highs of high, using the swing-strength lookback on both sides, retained up to the pool-memory limit and removed from eligibility after any chart bar has high > their price
Calculation: Untapped swing-low pools = confirmed pivot lows of low, using the swing-strength lookback on both sides, retained up to the pool-memory limit and removed from eligibility after any chart bar has low < their price
Calculation: Prior-period levels = high and low of the last completed bar of T1, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Buy pool = the nearest price above close among untapped swing-high pools and, when enabled, the prior-period high
Calculation: Sell pool = the nearest price below close among untapped swing-low pools and, when enabled, the prior-period low
Calculation: Side pool quality(side) = the cluster score from the count of untapped swing pools of that side within the ATR-based equal-level tolerance of the nearest pool, normalized by the touch-count parameter and clamped to the score floor and ceiling; if the nearest pool is a prior-period level, use the greater of that score and the prior-pool quality floor; use the score floor if no pool exists
Calculation: Side proximity score(side) = the nearest pool's distance from close in ATR, mapped linearly from the score ceiling at zero distance to the score floor at or beyond the maximum draw distance, and clamped to the score range; use the score floor if no pool exists
Calculation: Side trend-context score(side) = the aligned score when close is on that side of EMA(close, trend lookback) and the EMA is sloping that way over the slope lookback; otherwise use the same-side-without-slope score if close is on that side of the EMA, the opposing-alignment score if close and EMA slope are both opposite, or the neutral score; aligned score > same-side-without-slope score > neutral score > opposing-alignment score
Calculation: Side freshness score(side) = the prior-pool freshness parameter for a selected prior-period level; otherwise clamp the score ceiling scaled down linearly by the selected swing pool's age in chart bars over the freshness window to the score range; use the score floor if no pool exists
Calculation: Side draw score(side) = clamp(the weighted sum of Side pool quality(side), Side proximity score(side), Side trend-context score(side), and Side freshness score(side), using separate weights that total one and satisfy quality weight > proximity weight > trend-context weight > freshness weight)
Long: at least one buy or sell pool exists AND Buy pool draw score - Sell pool draw score > the balance-gap parameter
Short: at least one buy or sell pool exists AND Sell pool draw score - Buy pool draw score > the balance-gap parameter
