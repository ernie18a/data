Calculation: Volume percentile rank = percentile rank of current volume among the current and preceding bars in the rank window
Calculation: Range percentile rank = percentile rank of current high minus low among the current and preceding bars in the rank window
Calculation: Close-position class = High when ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low) >= the close-location threshold; Low when it is <= the negative of that threshold; otherwise Middle, using zero for a zero-range bar
Calculation: Volume effort class = High Effort when volume rank >= either high-effort rank threshold; Normal when it is >= the low-volume rank threshold and below both high-effort thresholds; otherwise Low Effort
Calculation: Spread class = Ultra Wide when range rank >= the ultra-wide threshold; otherwise Wide when >= the wide threshold; otherwise Normal when >= the narrow-spread cutoff; otherwise Narrow when >= the ultra-narrow threshold; otherwise Ultra Narrow
Calculation: VSA result category = Strong Upward for an up bar with High close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; Moderate Upward for an up bar with High close-position and Normal spread; Questionable Upward for an up bar with Middle close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; Weak Upward for an up bar with Middle or High close-position and Narrow or Ultra Narrow spread; Poor Upward for an up bar with Low close-position; Strong Downward for a down bar with Low close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; Moderate Downward for a down bar with Low close-position and Normal spread; Questionable Downward for a down bar with Middle close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; Weak Downward for a down bar with Middle or Low close-position and Narrow or Ultra Narrow spread; Poor Downward for a down bar with High close-position; Upward Bias for an unchanged close with High close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; Downward Bias for an unchanged close with Low close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; Neutral Wide for an unchanged close with Middle close-position and Wide or Ultra Wide spread; otherwise Neutral
Calculation: Rejection type = Upper when upper-wick/range >= the rejection-wick threshold, upper wick > lower wick, and body/range <= the maximum rejection-body ratio; Lower when lower-wick/range >= the rejection-wick threshold, lower wick > upper wick, and body/range <= the maximum rejection-body ratio; otherwise none, using zero for ratios on a zero-range bar
Calculation: Effort-result category = Bullish Efficiency for High Effort with Strong Upward result; Bearish Efficiency for High Effort with Strong Downward result; Possible Hidden Weakness for High Effort with an upward result other than Strong Upward; Possible Hidden Strength for High Effort with a downward result other than Strong Downward; Easy Upward Movement for Low Effort with Strong Upward result; Easy Downward Movement for Low Effort with Strong Downward result; Constructive Upward for Normal effort with Strong or Moderate Upward result; Constructive Downward for Normal effort with Strong or Moderate Downward result; otherwise Other
Calculation: Trend direction = ZigZag direction from high and low using a pivot-depth parameter
Calculation: VSA background score = sum over the background lookback of per-bar scores, assigning the bullish-efficiency weight to Bullish Efficiency, easy-upward weight to Easy Upward Movement, constructive-upward weight to Constructive Upward, the negative bearish-efficiency weight to Bearish Efficiency, the negative easy-downward weight to Easy Downward Movement, the negative constructive-downward weight to Constructive Downward, and zero otherwise; add the trend-bonus parameter when trend influence is enabled and trend direction is bullish, or subtract it when enabled and trend direction is not bullish
Calculation: VSA background = Strong when VSA background score > the background threshold; Weak when it is < the negative of that threshold; otherwise Neutral
Calculation: Stop-volume active = true from the first bar where (log(close / close[return lag]) - SMA(log(close / close[return lag]), z-score lookback)) / population standard deviation(log(close / close[return lag]), z-score lookback) <= the activation threshold, and remains true thereafter
Long: VSA result category is Strong Upward, Moderate Upward, Questionable Upward, Weak Upward, Poor Upward, or Upward Bias; or effort-result category is Bullish Efficiency, Possible Hidden Strength, Easy Upward Movement, or Constructive Upward; or VSA background score > 0
Short: VSA result category is Strong Downward, Moderate Downward, Questionable Downward, Weak Downward, Poor Downward, or Downward Bias; or effort-result category is Bearish Efficiency, Possible Hidden Weakness, Easy Downward Movement, or Constructive Downward; or VSA background score < 0
Long: On the previous bar, a down bar had Narrow or Ultra Narrow spread and either the No Supply traditional mode was enabled with its volume below each of the two preceding bars, or that mode was disabled with Low Effort or volume below each of the two preceding bars and Middle close-position; current close > previous close; current trend direction is bullish or bearish
Short: On the previous bar, an up bar had Narrow or Ultra Narrow spread and either the No Demand traditional mode was enabled with its volume below each of the two preceding bars, or that mode was disabled with Low Effort or volume below each of the two preceding bars and Middle close-position; current close < previous close; current trend direction is bullish or bearish
Short: Previous bar had Upper rejection, Middle close-position, Normal, Wide, or Ultra Wide spread, and Weak background; if the Upthrust trend filter is enabled, previous trend direction was bearish; current close < previous close
Long: Previous bar had Lower rejection, Middle or High close-position, Normal, Wide, or Ultra Wide spread, and Strong background; if the Shakeout trend filter is enabled, previous trend direction was bullish; current close > previous close
Long: Down bar, High Effort, Narrow, Ultra Narrow, or Normal spread, Middle close-position, Stop-volume active, and Strong background
Short: Down bar, High Effort, Narrow, Ultra Narrow, or Normal spread, Middle close-position, Stop-volume active, and VSA background is not Strong
