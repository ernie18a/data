Calculation: Selected oscillator = the selected one of RSI(close, oscillator lookback), MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback), CCI(hlc3, oscillator lookback), Stoch RSI(close, oscillator lookback) smoothed by SMA(smoothing lookback), or MFI(hlc3, oscillator lookback)
Calculation: Bullish divergence = on a confirmed bar, the current selected-oscillator pivot low, confirmed after the right pivot lookback, follows the previous selected-oscillator pivot low by between the minimum and maximum pivot spacing; low at the current pivot is at least the minimum price-move multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) below low at the previous pivot, and the current oscillator pivot is at least the oscillator-difference multiple × stdev(selected oscillator, oscillator-deviation lookback) above the previous oscillator pivot
Calculation: Bearish divergence = on a confirmed bar, the current selected-oscillator pivot high, confirmed after the right pivot lookback, follows the previous selected-oscillator pivot high by between the minimum and maximum pivot spacing; high at the current pivot is at least the minimum price-move multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) above high at the previous pivot, and the current oscillator pivot is at least the oscillator-difference multiple × stdev(selected oscillator, oscillator-deviation lookback) below the previous oscillator pivot
Calculation: Bullish divergence count = rolling sum of confirmed bullish divergences over the divergence-memory lookback
Calculation: Bearish divergence count = rolling sum of confirmed bearish divergences over the divergence-memory lookback
Calculation: Normalized EMA slope = (EMA(close, trend lookback) - EMA(close, trend lookback)[slope lookback]) / ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: Strong slope = abs(normalized EMA slope) >= slope sensitivity × SMA(abs(normalized EMA slope), slope-average lookback)
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND normalized EMA slope > 0 AND strong slope AND bearish divergence count >= minimum aligned divergences AND bearish divergence count > bullish divergence count
Calculation: Bearish trend = close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND normalized EMA slope < 0 AND strong slope AND bullish divergence count >= minimum aligned divergences AND bullish divergence count > bearish divergence count
Long: bullish divergence AND bearish trend
Long: FVG requirement enabled AND an active setup from a bullish divergence confirmed during a bearish trend is 1 to FVG-window bars old AND the setup has not been cancelled by a close below its divergence pivot low when break cancellation is enabled AND low > high[gap-reference lag] AND the intervening candle closes above its open AND low - high[gap-reference lag] >= minimum FVG-size multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) AND abs(close[1] - open[1]) >= displacement-body multiple × ATR(ATR lookback), on a confirmed bar
Short: bearish divergence AND bullish trend
Short: FVG requirement enabled AND an active setup from a bearish divergence confirmed during a bullish trend is 1 to FVG-window bars old AND the setup has not been cancelled by a close above its divergence pivot high when break cancellation is enabled AND high < low[gap-reference lag] AND the intervening candle closes below its open AND low[gap-reference lag] - high >= minimum FVG-size multiple × ATR(ATR lookback) AND abs(close[1] - open[1]) >= displacement-body multiple × ATR(ATR lookback), on a confirmed bar
