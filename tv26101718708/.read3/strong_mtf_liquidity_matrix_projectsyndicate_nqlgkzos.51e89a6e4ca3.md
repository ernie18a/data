Calculation: OB strength(displacement, zone height, age, ATR) = if strength ranking is off, 5; otherwise min(strength ceiling, max(0, displacement score + swing score + age score + zone score)). With positive ATR, displacement score is a descending step function of displacement/ATR with four descending parameter thresholds and five descending parameter scores; without positive ATR it is a parameter default. Swing score is a parameter. Age score is a descending step function of age with three ascending parameter cutoffs and four descending parameter scores. With positive ATR, zone score uses zone height/ATR: a parameter score applies inside a parameter inner interval, another inside a wider parameter interval, otherwise a third; without positive ATR it is a parameter default.
Calculation: FVG strength(gap, ATR) = if strength ranking is off, 5; with positive ATR, a descending step function of gap/ATR with four descending parameter thresholds and five descending parameter scores; otherwise a parameter default.
Calculation: OB zone bounds(top, bottom, ATR, price) = if zone normalization is off, (top, bottom); otherwise midpoint=(top+bottom)/2 and height is ATR × the ATR-height parameter or price × the percentage-height parameter / 100, according to the selected height method; bounds=(midpoint+height/2, midpoint-height/2).
Long: On chart bars, using each T1, T2, T3, and T4 bar series with its still-forming bar and last completed bars, a bullish FVG or bullish OB passes its strength filter and per-side cap. Bullish FVG: the newer bar's low is above the bar two bars older's high; if the optional ATR filter is enabled, the gap is at least ATR(14, T, still-forming bar) × its separate minimum-gap parameter; no more recent bar has mitigated it under the selected Touch, Full Fill, or 50% Fill mode (bullish thresholds are the newer low, older high, or gap midpoint, respectively), unless FVG mitigation is off; FVG strength is at least the minimum-strength parameter. Bullish OB: a pivot low is no higher than the lows of the swing-length bars on each side; scanning older bars through the OB lookback finds a nearest older bearish candle (close < open) whose range is positive and whose low is below the pivot low by more than its range × the OB displacement parameter; no more recent bar closes below the normalized OB zone bottom, unless OB mitigation is off; OB strength, using displacement=the still-forming T close minus the candle low, zone height=candle high minus candle low, and age=its bar offset, is at least the minimum-strength parameter. Keep at most the separate FVG and OB per-side cap parameters, in newest-first scan order.
Short: On chart bars, using each T1, T2, T3, and T4 bar series with its still-forming bar and last completed bars, a bearish FVG or bearish OB passes its strength filter and per-side cap. Bearish FVG: the newer bar's high is below the bar two bars older's low; if the optional ATR filter is enabled, the gap is at least ATR(14, T, still-forming bar) × its separate minimum-gap parameter; no more recent bar has mitigated it under the selected Touch, Full Fill, or 50% Fill mode (bearish thresholds are the newer high, older low, or gap midpoint, respectively), unless FVG mitigation is off; FVG strength is at least the minimum-strength parameter. Bearish OB: a pivot high is no lower than the highs of the swing-length bars on each side; scanning older bars through the OB lookback finds a nearest older bullish candle (close > open) whose range is positive and whose high is above the pivot high by more than its range × the OB displacement parameter; no more recent bar closes above the normalized OB zone top, unless OB mitigation is off; OB strength, using displacement=the candle high minus the still-forming T close, zone height=candle high minus candle low, and age=its bar offset, is at least the minimum-strength parameter. Keep at most the separate FVG and OB per-side cap parameters, in newest-first scan order.
