Calculation: Active profile = configured intraday profile, or automatic profile when chart timeframe >= profile-threshold parameter; otherwise scalp profile.
Calculation: Effective sweep-to-MSS limit = intraday profile ? min(configured sweep-to-MSS limit, intraday cap) : configured sweep-to-MSS limit.
Calculation: Effective MSS-to-entry limit = intraday profile ? min(configured MSS-to-entry limit, intraday cap) : configured MSS-to-entry limit.
Calculation: Effective entry session = configured NY session in intraday profile; otherwise configured killzone session.
Calculation: Stop buffer = max(configured tick buffer, intraday profile ? configured ATR fraction * ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars) : zero).
Calculation: Liquidity pools = enabled previous completed T1 and T2 high/low, sampled on chart bars at each period boundary; enabled configured session highs/lows, recorded at session end; and enabled equal highs/lows from consecutive confirmed swing pivots whose prices differ by <= configured tolerance * ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars), recorded at the higher high or lower low. Ignore a new same-side pool within configured deduplication distance * ATR of an unswept pool; discard the oldest pool when the configured capacity is exceeded. A high pool is swept when chart high > pool + configured penetration * ATR; a low pool is swept when chart low < pool - configured penetration * ATR.
Calculation: Eligible setup sweep = a one-sided pool sweep while ADX(ADX length, ADX length, chart bars) >= configured minimum when the ADX filter is enabled, and no cooldown is active. A both-sided sweep always cancels the setup and starts cooldown; an opposite-side sweep within the configured reversal window cancels it and starts cooldown when the two-sided filter is enabled. A same-side sweep rearms the setup; its extreme tracks the highest high for a short setup or lowest low for a long setup through the MSS bar.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = confirmation filter passes AND the latest confirmed, unbroken pivot high using the configured MSS pivot length is available AND (close > that pivot high OR a new pivot high is confirmed and highest(close, MSS pivot length) > that pivot high). Each pivot can be broken once.
Calculation: Bearish structure break = confirmation filter passes AND the latest confirmed, unbroken pivot low using the configured MSS pivot length is available AND (close < that pivot low OR a new pivot low is confirmed and lowest(close, MSS pivot length) < that pivot low). Each pivot can be broken once.
Calculation: Active FVG zone = after MSS, bind the most recent matching chart-bar FVG formed on or after the sweep, or the first matching FVG that forms later; bullish FVG when low > high[FVG span], with bounds high[FVG span] to low; bearish FVG when high < low[FVG span], with bounds high to low[FVG span].
Calculation: Entry level = bullish FVG upper edge or bearish FVG lower edge in edge mode; otherwise the midpoint of the matching FVG.
Calculation: Stop price = short setup extreme + stop buffer; long setup extreme - stop buffer.
Calculation: Entry score = configured common score weight for each true item: swept pool is T1, T2, or equal-high/low; sweep candle closes below the last swept high pool for shorts or above the last swept low pool for longs; enabled correlated-instrument divergence at the sweep (short: its current chart-bar high < its prior-lookback highest high; long: its current chart-bar low > its prior-lookback lowest low); current bias alignment; current midnight-open alignment (short close > midnight open, long close < midnight open); effective entry session; MSS occurred; MSS candle body > configured impulse fraction * its high-low range; FVG zone is bound; and nearest unswept opposing pool offers reward >= stop risk * configured fallback-RR.
Calculation: Target price at entry = nearest unswept opposing pool beyond the entry if its reward >= stop risk; otherwise the latest confirmed configured swing-length pivot in the trade direction if its reward >= stop risk; otherwise entry price +/- stop risk * configured fallback-RR in the trade direction.
Long: close > EMA(close, configured bias length, T3, last completed bar); the EMA value is sampled on chart bars.
Short: close <= EMA(close, configured bias length, T3, last completed bar); the EMA value is sampled on chart bars.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: eligible low-pool sweep AND bullish structure break within the effective sweep-to-MSS limit AND active bullish FVG zone remains valid (close >= its lower bound, or, before a zone is bound, close >= the setup extreme) AND the first later-bar zone tap occurs within the effective MSS-to-entry limit (low <= entry level AND close > zone lower bound) AND confirmation filter passes AND (killzone filter disabled OR current bar is in the effective entry session) AND (news filter disabled OR current bar is outside configured blackout windows) AND (date filter disabled OR chart time >= configured start) AND flat position AND longs enabled AND entry score >= configured minimum AND stop price < entry level.
Short: eligible high-pool sweep AND bearish structure break within the effective sweep-to-MSS limit AND active bearish FVG zone remains valid (close <= its upper bound, or, before a zone is bound, close <= the setup extreme) AND the first later-bar zone tap occurs within the effective MSS-to-entry limit (high >= entry level AND close < zone upper bound) AND confirmation filter passes AND (killzone filter disabled OR current bar is in the effective entry session) AND (news filter disabled OR current bar is outside configured blackout windows) AND (date filter disabled OR chart time >= configured start) AND flat position AND shorts enabled AND entry score >= configured minimum AND stop price > entry level.
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price.
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price.
