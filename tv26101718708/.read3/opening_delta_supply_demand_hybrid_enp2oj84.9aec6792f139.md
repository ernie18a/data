Calculation: Signed volume delta = the latest volume-delta value from intrabar timeframe T2 for the current chart bar, including any still-forming T2 bar; if it is zero and candle-delta fallback is enabled, use chart-bar volume with the sign of close versus open, or, when close equals open, the sign of close versus close[1], or zero if those are equal.
Calculation: RVOL = volume / SMA(volume, RVOL lookback), or the neutral RVOL value when that SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Candle quality = let range be max(high - low, minimum tick size), body be abs(close - open) / range, close position be (close - low) / range, and upper and lower wick ratios be (high - max(open, close)) / range and (min(open, close) - low) / range; for positive signed volume delta use close position + body + wick contribution factor * lower wick ratio, for negative delta use 1 - close position + body + wick contribution factor * upper wick ratio, and for zero delta use the neutral quality value.
Calculation: Session dominance = reset buyer and seller pressure to zero outside the session and on its first chart bar; on each other session chart bar, multiply each prior pressure by 1 - decay rate, then add abs(signed volume delta) times the RVOL weight times the candle-quality weight to buyer pressure for positive delta or seller pressure for negative delta. Each weight is max(its value, the weight floor) when its weighting switch is enabled, otherwise one. Return 100 * (buyer pressure - seller pressure) / (buyer pressure + seller pressure), or zero when their sum is zero.
Calculation: Opening threshold = max(abs(signed volume delta on the first session chart bar), minimum opening threshold), retained for the session.
Calculation: Supply/demand zones = on chart bars, accumulate the current forming T3 range high and low and sample high, low, and volume from intrabar timeframe T1; if no T1 intrabars are available, add chart volume to total sample volume. Divide the range into resolution intervals. From the range high downward, accumulate sample volume for bars whose highs fall in each successive interval; the first level where cumulative volume exceeds the zone-volume-threshold percentage of total sample volume defines supply from that level to the range high. From the range low upward, do the same with sample lows to define demand from the range low to that level. When retest hiding is enabled, mark a supply zone retested after a later close above its top, or a demand zone retested after a later close below its bottom.
Calculation: Demand-zone rejection = at least one enabled demand zone created on an earlier chart bar is touched (high >= zone bottom and low <= zone top), is not retested when the untested-only filter is enabled, and either close is above its top when the close-outside-zone filter is enabled or close > open and its lower wick is nonzero when that filter is disabled.
Calculation: Supply-zone rejection = at least one enabled supply zone created on an earlier chart bar is touched (high >= zone bottom and low <= zone top), is not retested when the untested-only filter is enabled, and either close is below its bottom when the close-outside-zone filter is enabled or close < open and its upper wick is nonzero when that filter is disabled.
Long: Hybrid signals are enabled AND in session AND demand-zone rejection AND at least one enabled confirmation is true (signed volume delta > 0; or session dominance > dominance threshold; or session dominance[1] < 0 AND session dominance >= flip threshold; or signed volume delta > 0 AND abs(signed volume delta) >= opening threshold * challenge factor; or not the first session bar AND signed volume delta > 0 AND abs(signed volume delta) >= opening threshold * superior-delta factor AND candle quality >= minimum quality for superior delta AND RVOL >= minimum RVOL for superior delta) AND NOT (opposite-control blocking is enabled AND session dominance < -dominance threshold) AND (no prior long signal OR current chart bar index - prior long signal bar index > signal cooldown). Each confirmation applies only when its corresponding confirmation switch is enabled.
