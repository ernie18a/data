Calculation: Entry eligibility = no active trade AND chart bars since the last entry signal >= cooldown AND today's entry-signal count < daily trade limit
Calculation: Maximum stop distance = maximum loss per trade / (contracts × point value)
Calculation: Long stop = max(entry-signal-bar low − ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR stop buffer, entry-signal-bar close − maximum stop distance)
Calculation: Long target = entry-signal-bar close + (entry-signal-bar close − long stop) × reward-to-risk ratio
Calculation: Short stop = min(entry-signal-bar high + ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR stop buffer, entry-signal-bar close + maximum stop distance)
Calculation: Short target = entry-signal-bar close − (short stop − entry-signal-bar close) × reward-to-risk ratio
Long: Entry eligibility AND the weekday is enabled for long entries AND NY time < entry cutoff AND (NY session restriction is disabled OR the current bar is in the configured NY session) AND close > VWAP(typical price, volume, longer anchor T2, chart bars through the current bar in the still-forming T2 period) AND ((low[1] <= VWAP(typical price, volume, shorter anchor T1, chart bars through the previous bar) AND close > VWAP(typical price, volume, shorter anchor T1, chart bars through the current bar in the still-forming T1 period)) OR (low[1] <= VWAP(typical price, volume, NY-session anchor, chart bars through the previous bar) AND close > VWAP(typical price, volume, NY-session anchor, chart bars through the current bar in the current NY session)))
Short: Entry eligibility AND the weekday is enabled for short entries AND NY time < entry cutoff AND (NY session restriction is disabled OR the current bar is in the configured NY session) AND close < VWAP(typical price, volume, longer anchor T2, chart bars through the current bar in the still-forming T2 period) AND ((high[1] >= VWAP(typical price, volume, shorter anchor T1, chart bars through the previous bar) AND close < VWAP(typical price, volume, shorter anchor T1, chart bars through the current bar in the still-forming T1 period)) OR (high[1] >= VWAP(typical price, volume, NY-session anchor, chart bars through the previous bar) AND close < VWAP(typical price, volume, NY-session anchor, chart bars through the current bar in the current NY session)))
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR NY session restriction is enabled AND the current bar is outside the configured NY session AND the previous chart bar was inside it
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR NY session restriction is enabled AND the current bar is outside the configured NY session AND the previous chart bar was inside it
