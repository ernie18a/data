Calculation: level match(a,b) = abs(a-b) <= average(a,b) * level-symmetry tolerance
Calculation: linear projection(x1,y1,x2,y2,x) = y1 + (y2-y1)/(x2-x1) * (x-x1); if x2=x1, return y1
Calculation: pivot slope = (newer pivot price - older pivot price) / max(1, newer pivot index - older pivot index)
Calculation: HTF bullish bias = EMA(close, shorter HTF lookback, T1 bar still forming) > EMA(close, longer HTF lookback, T1 bar still forming) AND T1 close still forming > the shorter EMA; HTF bearish bias reverses both comparisons. Compute these on T1 bars and map their current values to chart bars.
Calculation: formation completion score = double top or bottom base score + correct-side bonus + near-boundary bonus; head-and-shoulders base score + correct-side bonus + near-boundary bonus; triangle base score + large-base bonus when base/ATR exceeds its threshold, otherwise small-base bonus, + near-boundary bonus; flag base score + near-boundary bonus; wedge base score + the lesser of its score cap and projected-boundary gap/ATR times its width-score factor. A correct-side bonus applies below the neckline for bearish formations and above it for bullish formations; a near-boundary bonus applies within the ARMED distance.
Calculation: candidate confidence score = the lesser of its score cap and the sum of: a high-completion score when completion reaches the high-completion threshold, otherwise a lower completion score when it reaches the minimum-completion threshold; an HTF-alignment score when the HTF layer is enabled and aligned, or its disabled-layer score when disabled; a chop-pass score; a phase score, higher for FIRED than ARMED; a volume-coil score when enabled, volume is no greater than its maximum ratio of its moving average, and that average is positive; a risk/reward score when target-to-stop distance divided by entry-to-stop distance meets the minimum; and a height score when formation height meets its ATR multiple.
Calculation: candidate rank = candidate confidence score + formation completion score * completion-rank weight + phase-rank bonus, with the FIRED bonus greater than the ARMED bonus and no bonus for FORMING.
Calculation: selected candidate = active candidate with the highest rank; ties retain the first in this order: double top/bottom, head-and-shoulders, triangles, flags, wedges.
Calculation: eligible event = selected candidate on a confirmed chart bar whose pattern, phase, or start index differs from the preceding emitted event, and either no FIRED event has occurred or at least the cooldown interval has elapsed since the preceding FIRED event.
Calculation: bullish candidate = any enabled setup below, using confirmed pivot highs/lows with equal left and right pivot lookbacks; each setup's phase is FIRED when its stated breakout comparison holds, otherwise ARMED when its close is within the ARMED ATR distance of its stated boundary, otherwise FORMING. Double bottom: the latest pivot low follows the latest pivot high, which follows the previous pivot low; the two lows match by level match; their intervening high is the neckline; neckline minus the average lows exceeds ATR. Target is neckline plus that height; stop is the entry open minus target-to-entry distance times the stop fraction. Inverted head-and-shoulders: the latest trough is the right shoulder, the previous trough is lower than both shoulders, and the shoulders match; project the line through the two intervening pivot highs to the current bar as neckline; average intervening highs minus head trough exceeds ATR. Target is neckline plus that height; stop is the entry open minus target-to-entry distance times the stop fraction. Triangle: project the latest two pivot highs and latest two pivot lows to the current bar as upper and lower boundaries; their separation at the older low pivot is the base, which exceeds ATR, and the upper boundary is above the lower. Ascending means abs(upper slope) is below close times the flat-slope tolerance and lower slope is positive; descending means upper slope is negative and abs(lower slope) is below that tolerance. The bullish branch applies when close exceeds upper boundary plus the breakout ATR margin, or when close has not broken below the lower boundary by that margin and the triangle is ascending or upper slope is negative. Its target is upper boundary plus base and its stop is the lower boundary. Bull flag: from the older of the second-most-recent high and low pivots, span exceeds the minimum pole span; pole is highest high minus lowest low over the lesser of span and the pole lookback cap, and exceeds ATR times the minimum pole multiple; both pivot slopes are negative. Upper projected pivot-high line is the boundary; target is boundary plus pole and stop is the projected pivot-low line. Falling wedge: projected upper boundary exceeds projected lower boundary; both pivot slopes are negative and lower slope is less than upper slope. Target is the older of the two latest pivot highs and stop is the projected lower boundary.
Calculation: bearish candidate = any enabled setup below, using the same confirmed pivots, projections, symmetry tolerance, and shared ATR lookback as the bullish candidate. Double top: the latest pivot high follows the latest pivot low, which follows the previous pivot high; the two highs match by level match; their intervening low is the neckline; average highs minus neckline exceeds ATR. Close below neckline minus the breakout ATR margin is FIRED; otherwise proximity to the neckline defines ARMED. Target is neckline minus that height; stop is the entry open plus target-to-entry distance times the stop fraction. Head-and-shoulders: the latest peak is the right shoulder, the previous peak is higher than both shoulders, and the shoulders match; project the line through the two intervening pivot lows to the current bar as neckline; head peak minus average intervening lows exceeds ATR. Close below neckline minus the breakout ATR margin is FIRED; otherwise proximity defines ARMED. Target is neckline minus that height; stop is the entry open plus target-to-entry distance times the stop fraction. Triangle uses the shared projected boundaries and base: the bearish branch applies only when the bullish branch does not apply and close is below the lower boundary minus the breakout ATR margin or the triangle is descending. Its target is lower boundary minus base and its stop is the upper boundary. Bear flag: pole meets the bullish-candidate pole conditions and both pivot slopes are positive; lower projected pivot-low line is the boundary. Close below it minus the breakout ATR margin is FIRED; otherwise proximity defines ARMED. Target is boundary minus pole and stop is the projected pivot-high line. Rising wedge: projected upper boundary exceeds projected lower boundary; both pivot slopes are positive and lower slope is greater than upper slope. Close below the lower boundary minus the breakout ATR margin is FIRED; otherwise proximity defines ARMED. Target is the older of the two latest pivot lows and stop is the projected upper boundary.
Long: selected bullish candidate is ARMED or FIRED, the chop filter is disabled or Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) < chop maximum, and eligible event holds; when FIRED, any enabled hard HTF filter requires HTF bullish bias and target-to-stop risk/reward meets its minimum.
Long: selected bullish candidate is FIRED, close breaks its boundary by more than the breakout ATR margin, the chop filter is disabled or Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) < chop maximum, any enabled hard HTF filter requires HTF bullish bias, target-to-stop risk/reward meets its minimum, and eligible event holds.
Short: selected bearish candidate is ARMED or FIRED, the chop filter is disabled or Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) < chop maximum, and eligible event holds; when FIRED, any enabled hard HTF filter requires HTF bearish bias and target-to-stop risk/reward meets its minimum.
Short: selected bearish candidate is FIRED, close breaks its boundary by more than the breakout ATR margin, the chop filter is disabled or Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) < chop maximum, any enabled hard HTF filter requires HTF bearish bias, target-to-stop risk/reward meets its minimum, and eligible event holds.
