Calculation: Each enabled moving average = its independently selected EMA, SMA, WMA, VWMA, RMA, or HMA of close using its own lookback and timeframe; calculate on chart bars when chart timeframe is selected, otherwise calculate on its T1, T2, or T3 bars and use the forming timeframe bar in realtime or last completed timeframe bar historically on chart bars.
Calculation: HTF KAMA = KAMA(close, length, fast parameter, slow parameter) calculated on T4 bars and used on chart bars from the forming T4 bar in realtime or the last completed T4 bar historically.
Calculation: HTF VWAP = VWAP(hlc3) calculated on T5 bars and used on chart bars from the forming T5 bar in realtime or the last completed T5 bar historically.
Long: At least one moving average is enabled, close > each enabled moving average, and each enabled shorter-lookback moving average > each enabled longer-lookback moving average; if the DMI filter is enabled, +DI(DI lookback) > -DI(DI lookback) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > threshold; if the HTF KAMA filter is enabled, close > HTF KAMA; if the HTF VWAP filter is enabled, close > HTF VWAP; position side is not long.
Short: At least one moving average is enabled, close < each enabled moving average, and each enabled shorter-lookback moving average < each enabled longer-lookback moving average; if the DMI filter is enabled, -DI(DI lookback) > +DI(DI lookback) AND ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback) > threshold; if the HTF KAMA filter is enabled, close < HTF KAMA; if the HTF VWAP filter is enabled, close < HTF VWAP; position side is not short.
