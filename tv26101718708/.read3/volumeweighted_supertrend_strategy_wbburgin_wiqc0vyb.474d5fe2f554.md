Calculation: VWAP-based Supertrend(source, lookback, factor, ATR lookback) = raw lower and upper bands are VWMA(source, lookback) minus and plus factor * ATR(ATR lookback); final lower is raw lower if raw lower > nz(final lower[1]) or source[1] < nz(final lower[1]), otherwise nz(final lower[1]); final upper is raw upper if raw upper < nz(final upper[1]) or source[1] > nz(final upper[1]), otherwise nz(final upper[1]); missing prior bands are zero; direction is down if ATR(ATR lookback)[1] is unavailable, otherwise if line[1] = final upper[1], direction is up iff close > final upper and down otherwise, otherwise direction is down iff close < final lower and up otherwise; line is final lower when direction is up and final upper when direction is down.
Long: (Supertrend(volume, factor, lookback) < volume AND VWAP-based Supertrend direction changes from down to up) OR (Supertrend(volume, factor, lookback) direction changes from down to up AND VWAP-based Supertrend line < source), using the same factor and lookback for both supertrends.
Exit: long, (Supertrend(volume, factor, lookback) >= volume AND VWAP-based Supertrend direction changes from up to down) OR (Supertrend(volume, factor, lookback) direction changes from up to down AND VWAP-based Supertrend line >= source), using the same factor and lookback for both supertrends.
