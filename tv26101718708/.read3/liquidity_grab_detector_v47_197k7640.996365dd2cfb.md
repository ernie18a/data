Calculation: Sell/buy pools = Add swing-high/swing-low pivots confirmed by the swing lookback, prior highs/lows of T2, optionally prior highs/lows of T3, and equal highs/lows when the latest two same-side swing pivots differ by less than ATR(short lookback) × equal-level threshold. Create zones centered on each level with half-width ATR(short lookback) × effective pool half-width; merge into the nearest same-side pool within ATR(short lookback) × merge distance, updating its midpoint by hit-count-weighted average and widening it by effective pool half-width × (one plus the hit-count expansion factor). Swing, prior-period, and equal-level pools have separate source weights. Start each pool with one hit and full health; decrement health while close is inside the zone by volume divided by (SMA(volume, volume lookback) × health-consumption divisor), times the health-consumption scale. Increment hits on spaced touches that close on the pool’s expected side of its midpoint. Remove pools past maximum age, beyond the per-side pool limit, or after successive closes beyond the far zone edge. Effective pool half-width, minimum penetration, and equal-level threshold use their manual parameters when auto-calibration is off; when on, derive them respectively as clamp(base + wick/body ratio × factor, lower, upper) × loose-bias multiplier, clamp(wick/body ratio × factor, lower, upper) × tight-bias multiplier, and clamp(base + mean normalized ATR percentile × factor, lower, upper) × loose-bias multiplier. Wick/body ratio is SMA(total candle wicks, calibration lookback) / SMA(abs(close − open), calibration lookback), defaulting to its fallback when the body average is zero; normalized ATR percentile is the mean over that lookback of (ATR(short lookback) − lowest ATR) / (highest ATR − lowest ATR), with its fallback when the denominator is zero. Loose and tight bias multipliers depend on the selected sensitivity bias.
Calculation: Sweep score = (sum of each factor × its regime-specific weight / sum of weights) × session multiplier × coil multiplier × pool-age multiplier × volume-vacuum multiplier. Factors are: clamp(one − abs(penetration − score penetration target) / max(score penetration tolerance, score penetration floor), zero, one); clamp((reclaim − reclaim-score lower bound) / (reclaim-score upper bound − lower bound), zero, one); clamp(wick ratio / wick saturation, zero, one); one for a candle in the sweep direction or the wrong-body score otherwise; pool significance = hit factor × hit-count score + health factor × normalized health score + source-weight factor × source tier score, where hit factor and health factor are clamped normalized values and source tier score depends on two source-weight thresholds; clamp(abs(impulse-VWAP stretch) / stretch saturation, zero, one); neutral HTF score if the HTF filter is off, otherwise one for trend agreement and zero for disagreement; neutral volume score if unavailable, otherwise a clamped normalization of volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback); one for a recent micro-structure trend change in the sweep direction and zero otherwise; clamp favorable MSS distance / MSS-distance saturation, zero, one; and neutral channel score if either bounding pool is unavailable, otherwise an edge-position score outside the channel thresholds and a middle score inside them. Base weights apply outside the enabled regimes. Trend regime uses separate weights for HTF, MSS, pool significance, channel position, and wick ratio; range regime uses separate weights for pool significance, channel position, HTF, MSS, and wick ratio; coil regime uses separate weights for penetration, reclaim, pool significance, and HTF. Regime is coil when ADX(ADX lookback) is below the coil threshold and the percentile rank of Bollinger Band width is below its threshold; trend when ADX exceeds its trend threshold and regime is not coil; range when neither applies. Bollinger Band width is (upper band − lower band) / middle band. Session multiplier is the current UTC session quality only in Multiplier mode, otherwise neutral; session gate passes unless mode is Gate and current session quality is below the gate threshold. Coil multiplier applies only in an enabled coil regime. Pool-age multiplier is a clamped base plus a power of clamped pool age / age saturation, scaled by an age factor. Volume-vacuum multiplier applies when volume / its selected moving average stays below the calibrated threshold for at least the configured consecutive-bar count; select the longer or shorter vacuum average according to whether volume coefficient of variation exceeds its threshold. Volume coefficient of variation is volume standard deviation / volume mean over the calibration lookback; the calibrated vacuum threshold is a clamped base plus clamped coefficient of variation × factor. For a sell sweep, HTF agreement means T1 close below EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1 bar still forming); for a buy sweep it means T1 close above that EMA. Sample T1 values on chart bars with lookahead off. Micro-structure changes use confirmed swing pivots and a persistent trend state; MSS levels are the prior-bar lowest low or highest high over the BOS lookback. Channel position uses the nearest active sell-pool midpoint above close and buy-pool midpoint below close.
Calculation: SFP exhaustion percentile = PercentRank(percent-scaled tanh(exhaustion input), SFP percentile lookback). Exhaustion input is EMA(impulse stretch + optional day-VWAP stretch × day-stretch weight + wick skew × wick weight + max(volume Z-score, zero) × sign(impulse stretch) × volume weight, exhaustion smoothing lookback) × exhaustion scale + curvature weight × negative(ROC(EMA(close, price EMA lookback), price ROC lookback) − EMA(ROC(EMA(close, price EMA lookback), price ROC lookback), curvature smoothing lookback)[one bar ago]). Impulse stretch and day-VWAP stretch are (close − respective VWAP) / max(ATR(long lookback), minimum price increment); the day VWAP is volume-weighted hlc3 accumulated on chart bars during the current T2 period, which is still forming. Wick skew is (upper wick − lower wick) / true range. Volume Z-score is ZScore(volume, volume Z-score lookback). The day-VWAP term is omitted when disabled.
Long: (chart bar closes AND session gate passes AND there is an eligible buy pool with age within its limit, untouched signal state, and no pending confirmation, AND either (confirmation window is zero AND (spike sweep OR enabled grind sweep) AND score filter is disabled or sweep score ≥ score threshold AND the applicable type score threshold is met AND bars since any sweep signal ≥ sweep cooldown; OR (confirmation window is positive AND this pool had a raw sweep within that window, has not expired or been invalidated, current close > pool top, score filter is disabled or its saved initial sweep score ≥ score threshold, the current confirmation candle’s type score threshold is met, and bars since any sweep signal ≥ sweep cooldown)). A buy-pool spike sweep is low < pool bottom AND close > pool midpoint AND max(pool bottom − low, zero) / ATR(short lookback) > effective minimum penetration AND close > low. A buy-pool grind sweep, when enabled and no spike sweep occurred, requires a prior close within the grind lookback below pool bottom − ATR(short lookback) × grind offset, current close > pool bottom AND close < pool top + ATR(short lookback) × maximum grind close distance, and close > open. For the immediate path, use spike penetration and reclaim (close − low) / (high − low) as percentages; for a grind path, use grind penetration and the greater of spike reclaim or the grind reclaim floor. The immediate type threshold is the grind threshold for a grind path; otherwise it is the applicable absorption, rejection, divergence, or unclassified threshold, classified respectively by volume Z-score and lower-wick/true-range and reclaim thresholds, lower-wick/true-range and reclaim and bullish-body thresholds, or volume Z-score and reclaim and lower-wick/true-range thresholds. The confirmation path classifies the current candle using its lower-wick/true-range, volume Z-score, bullish-body state, and the fixed confirmation reclaim value. Sweep score inputs include the pool’s hit count, health, and source weight; current age; impulse stretch; volume ratio; micro-structure change; MSS distance; and channel position. (OR SFP: SFP is enabled AND an unexpired long arm exists, created when exhaustion percentile ≤ long-arm threshold AND ((impulse stretch < negative minimum stretch AND max(volume Z-score, zero) > positive-volume threshold) OR a buy sweep signal), with no active long arm or long SFP cooldown at arming. While armed, including the arming bar, close > prior-bar highest high over the BOS lookback AND bars since either SFP trigger ≥ global SFP cooldown AND session gate passes AND chart bar closes. An arm lasts for its configured TTL, counts down while not refreshed, and is cleared by its trigger.)
