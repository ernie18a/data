Long: On each confirmed chart bar, low > high[2] AND bullish gap size in points >= chart minimum-gap parameter.
Short: On each confirmed chart bar, high < low[2] AND bearish gap size in points >= chart minimum-gap parameter.
Long: When HTF zones are enabled, on chart bars once per newly closed T1 bar, low of the last completed T1 bar > high of the T1 bar two bars before it AND bullish gap size in points >= HTF minimum-gap parameter.
Short: When HTF zones are enabled, on chart bars once per newly closed T1 bar, high of the last completed T1 bar < low of the T1 bar two bars before it AND bearish gap size in points >= HTF minimum-gap parameter.
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar the close is below the lower boundary of an active bullish chart-timeframe or T1 FVG zone.
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar the close is above the upper boundary of an active bearish chart-timeframe or T1 FVG zone.
