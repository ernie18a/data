Calculation: LiquidityLevel(side, class) = latest confirmed chart-bar pivot high/low for swing levels; midpoint of the latest two confirmed same-side pivots when their difference is within ATR(chart bars, ATR lookback) times equal-level tolerance for equal levels; last completed bar high/low from T1 or T2, sampled on chart bars; or running day high/low through the previous chart bar, reset at each day boundary, for developing session levels
Calculation: RaidRank(level importance, sweep depth, ATR, tick size) = level importance times importance coefficient minus sweep depth divided by max(ATR, tick size) times depth penalty, when sweep depth is greater than ATR times sweep-buffer parameter and no greater than ATR times maximum-sweep-depth parameter; otherwise invalid
Calculation: EffectiveReclaimWindow = max(minimum reclaim bars, round(base reclaim bars times the multiplier for the current UTC-hour session, selected from the configured New York, London, or other-session windows))
Calculation: DirectionalFlow = EMA of volume times ((close - low) - (high - close)) divided by max(high - low, tick size), over the flow-smoothing lookback, divided by max(EMA(volume, flow-smoothing lookback), volume floor), treating missing volume as zero
Calculation: RejectionWick(side) = maximum since the raid of max(min(open, close) - low, 0) / max(high - low, tick size) for long, or max(high - max(open, close), 0) / max(high - low, tick size) for short
Calculation: FVGConfluence(side, level) = true when FVG confluence is enabled and the latest same-side gap is within its maximum age, remains active, and the level lies within its gap zone expanded by ATR times the near-gap tolerance; a bullish gap has low above high at the configured gap-comparison lag and a preceding configured impulse-bar body at least ATR times the impulse threshold, with zone [that high, low] and remains active while low is above its bottom; a bearish gap has high below low at the configured gap-comparison lag and the same impulse-body condition, with zone [high, that low] and remains active while high is below its top
Calculation: RecoveryScore(side, importance, depthATR, bars since raid, effective window, maximum rejection wick, maximum RVOL, close-back ATR, ATR regime, FVG confluence) = clamp to the configured score range of importance weight times min(importance / importance normalization, 1), plus depth weight times min(depthATR / depth normalization, 1), plus speed weight times (1 - min(bars since raid, effective window) / effective window), plus wick weight times min(maximum rejection wick / wick target, 1), plus RVOL weight times min(maximum RVOL / RVOL target, 1), plus flow weight times min(max(side sign times DirectionalFlow, 0) / flow target, 1), plus close-back weight times min(close-back ATR / close-back normalization, 1), plus FVG weight when FVGConfluence is true; depth weight uses the high-volatility weight when ATR exceeds SMA(ATR, volatility lookback) times the high-volatility threshold and otherwise the normal weight; speed weight uses the low-volatility weight when ATR is below that SMA times the low-volatility threshold and otherwise the normal weight; depthATR is the maximum excursion beyond the raided level divided by current ATR, close-back ATR is max(close - level, 0) / ATR for long or max(level - close, 0) / ATR for short, and maximum RVOL and rejection wick are measured from the raid through the current bar
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, low sweeps below an enabled LiquidityLevel(long, class) by more than ATR times the sweep-buffer parameter and no more than ATR times the maximum-sweep-depth parameter; select the eligible low with the highest RaidRank, retaining the first in order swing, equal, T1, T2, developing session on a tie; mark the bullish sweep and create or replace its pending state only when none is pending or the new level importance exceeds the pending level importance
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, high sweeps above an enabled LiquidityLevel(short, class) by more than ATR times the sweep-buffer parameter and no more than ATR times the maximum-sweep-depth parameter; select the eligible high with the highest RaidRank, retaining the first in order swing, equal, T1, T2, developing session on a tie; mark the bearish sweep and create or replace its pending state only when none is pending or the new level importance exceeds the pending level importance

Short: a bearish raid is pending and no bullish raid is pending
