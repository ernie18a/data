Calculation: Base(Tn) = high(Tn) > low(Tn) AND 100 * ((high(Tn) - low(Tn)) - abs(close(Tn) - open(Tn))) / (high(Tn) - low(Tn)) >= that timeframe’s base-wick threshold
Calculation: Impulse(Tn) = when strict impulse mode is enabled, 100 * abs(close(Tn) - open(Tn)) / (high(Tn) - low(Tn)) >= that timeframe’s impulse-body threshold (use 0 when the range is 0); otherwise NOT Base(Tn)
Calculation: IBI zone(Tn) = on confirmed chart bars when Tn data updates, using the last completed Tn bar, start a cluster on a Base(Tn) bar immediately after an Impulse(Tn) bar; accumulate subsequent base bars, discard the cluster if their count exceeds the maximum, complete it on a non-base Impulse(Tn) bar, and leave it pending through other bars. The ending bar makes the zone demand if close > open, otherwise supply. For demand, top = greatest base body high, bottom = least base low, entry = least base body low; for supply, top = greatest base high, bottom = least base body low, entry = greatest base body high.
Calculation: Active zone(Tn) = a formed zone remains active until a confirmed chart bar has min(open, close) < its bottom for demand or max(open, close) > its top for supply
Long: On the last chart bar, an active demand zone on an enabled Tn has positive height, close > entry, abs(close - entry) <= proximity multiple * abs(top - bottom), and ranks among the nearest plan-limit eligible zones for Tn by abs(close - entry), with ties in zone creation order
Short: On the last chart bar, an active supply zone on an enabled Tn has positive height, close < entry, abs(close - entry) <= proximity multiple * abs(close - entry), and ranks among the nearest plan-limit eligible zones for Tn by abs(close - entry), with ties in zone creation order
Long: When trade tracking is enabled, on a confirmed chart bar an active demand zone on an enabled Tn has positive height, close > entry, abs(close - entry) <= proximity multiple * abs(top - bottom), bars since its last armed trade >= cooldown, no armed or active trade exists for that zone, and (loss blocking is disabled OR the zone has no prior loss); after arming, fill on a later confirmed chart bar when low <= entry
Short: When trade tracking is enabled, on a confirmed chart bar an active supply zone on an enabled Tn has positive height, close < entry, abs(close - entry) <= proximity multiple * abs(top - bottom), bars since its last armed trade >= cooldown, no armed or active trade exists for that zone, and (loss blocking is disabled OR the zone has no prior loss); after arming, fill on a later confirmed chart bar when high >= entry
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar after fill, min(open, close) < lowest zone wick low - buffer ticks * minimum tick size OR (configured target count <= 3 AND high >= entry + abs(top - bottom) * target step * configured target count)
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar after fill, max(open, close) > highest zone wick high + buffer ticks * minimum tick size OR (configured target count <= 3 AND low <= entry - abs(top - bottom) * target step * configured target count)
