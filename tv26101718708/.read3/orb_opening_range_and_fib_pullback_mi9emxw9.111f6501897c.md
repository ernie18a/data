Calculation: ORHigh = maximum high of chart bars timestamped within the configured session start and opening-range duration, per local calendar day
Calculation: ORLow = minimum low of chart bars timestamped within the configured session start and opening-range duration, per local calendar day
Calculation: ORBullish = close of the last opening-range chart bar > open of the first opening-range chart bar
Calculation: ORRange = ORHigh − ORLow
Calculation: FibLevel(retracement parameter) = ORHigh − ORRange × retracement parameter when ORBullish, otherwise ORLow + ORRange × retracement parameter; shallow, intermediate, and deep retracement parameters are in increasing order
Calculation: FibZone = ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × zone-tolerance parameter
Calculation: VolumeAverage = SMA(volume, volume-average lookback parameter)
Calculation: CandleBody = max(abs(close − open), minimum tick size)
Long: Fib signals enabled AND the current chart bar is after completion of the opening range, outside the opening range, and at or before the configured signal-window end time AND ORRange > 0 AND ORBullish AND ((shallow level enabled AND abs(low − FibLevel(shallow retracement parameter)) ≤ FibZone) OR (intermediate level enabled AND abs(low − FibLevel(intermediate retracement parameter)) ≤ FibZone) OR (deep level enabled AND abs(low − FibLevel(deep retracement parameter)) ≤ FibZone)) AND ((close > open AND min(open, close) − low ≥ CandleBody × wick-to-body parameter) OR (close > open AND close ≥ open[1] AND open ≤ close[1] AND close[1] < open[1]))
Short: Fib signals enabled AND the current chart bar is after completion of the opening range, outside the opening range, and at or before the configured signal-window end time AND ORRange > 0 AND NOT ORBullish AND ((shallow level enabled AND abs(high − FibLevel(shallow retracement parameter)) ≤ FibZone) OR (intermediate level enabled AND abs(high − FibLevel(intermediate retracement parameter)) ≤ FibZone) OR (deep level enabled AND abs(high − FibLevel(deep retracement parameter)) ≤ FibZone)) AND ((close < open AND high − max(open, close) ≥ CandleBody × wick-to-body parameter) OR (close < open AND close ≤ open[1] AND open ≥ close[1] AND close[1] > open[1]))
Long: ORB signals enabled AND the current chart bar is after completion of the opening range, outside the opening range, and at or before the configured signal-window end time AND close > ORHigh AND volume > VolumeAverage × heavy-volume multiplier AND (repeat-signals parameter enabled OR no prior long ORB signal on this local calendar day)
Short: ORB signals enabled AND the current chart bar is after completion of the opening range, outside the opening range, and at or before the configured signal-window end time AND close < ORLow AND volume > VolumeAverage × heavy-volume multiplier AND (repeat-signals parameter enabled OR no prior short ORB signal on this local calendar day)
