Long: A lower-wick setup, evaluated on chart bars using the still-forming T1 bar, where (min(T1 open, T1 close) - T1 low) / abs(T1 open - T1 close) >= the minimum wick-to-body ratio AND T1 low < the lower Bollinger Band(source, lookback, deviation multiplier, T1), sets a pending price to the configured T1 close, open, high, or low; while that order is unexpired, chart-bar high >= its pending price AND chart-bar close > T1 swing low.
Short: An upper-wick setup, evaluated on chart bars using the still-forming T1 bar, where (T1 high - max(T1 open, T1 close)) / abs(T1 open - T1 close) >= the minimum wick-to-body ratio AND T1 high > the upper Bollinger Band(source, lookback, deviation multiplier, T1), sets a pending price to the configured T1 close, open, high, or low; while that order is unexpired, chart-bar low <= its pending price AND chart-bar close < T1 swing high.
Exit: long, chart-bar high >= the upper Bollinger Band(source, lookback, deviation multiplier, T1 still-forming bar) OR chart-bar low <= T1 swing low.
Exit: short, chart-bar low <= the lower Bollinger Band(source, lookback, deviation multiplier, T1 still-forming bar) OR chart-bar high >= T1 swing high.
