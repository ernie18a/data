Calculation: RVOL = volume / SMA(volume, lookback)
Long: (RSI(close, lookback) crosses above oversold level AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses above signal line) OR (RSI(close, lookback) crosses above oversold level AND RVOL crosses above long RVOL threshold) OR (MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses above signal line AND RVOL crosses above long RVOL threshold)
Short: (RSI(close, lookback) crosses below overbought level AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses below signal line) OR (RSI(close, lookback) crosses below overbought level AND RVOL crosses below short RVOL threshold) OR (MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback) line crosses below signal line AND RVOL crosses below short RVOL threshold)
