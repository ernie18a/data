Calculation: anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 within each T4 period; volume-weighted deviation = square root of the cumulative volume-weighted product of (HLC3 − prior VWAP) and (HLC3 − current VWAP), divided by cumulative volume; evaluate on chart bars using the still-forming T4 period
Calculation: absorption score = signed clamp(floor(abs(volume × (close − open) / (high − low, or minimum tick if zero)) / (minimum delta × intensity scale) × level scale), minimum level, maximum level), or zero unless volume ≥ minimum volume, the signed delta exceeds the minimum delta, candle direction matches the sign, abs(close − open) / close × percentage scale is below the body limit, close position (close − low) / (high − low, or minimum tick if zero) meets the direction-specific close-location limit, close is on the corresponding side of EMA(close, absorption EMA lookback), and the bar intersects the corresponding VWAP deviation band; positive bands use VWAP plus the nearer and farther band multipliers, negative bands use VWAP minus them
Calculation: swing retracement score = negative retracement weight when the latest upward swing's open is above its upper retracement level and close is between its lower and upper retracement levels; positive retracement weight when the latest downward swing's open is below its lower retracement level and close is between its lower and upper retracement levels; otherwise zero; levels use separate retracement parameters and the latest completed swing high and low
Calculation: key-level reaction score = positive resistance weight if open < resistance and abs(close − resistance) ≤ key-level tolerance, negative support weight if open > support and abs(close − support) ≤ key-level tolerance, and at a neutral level within that tolerance add the positive weight if open < neutral or the negative weight if open > neutral; add the swing retracement score
Calculation: prior-period volume-profile reaction score = negative daily-value weight if open > prior completed T5 value-area low and abs(close − it) ≤ profile tolerance; positive daily-value weight if open < prior completed T5 value-area high and abs(close − it) ≤ profile tolerance; apply the corresponding separately parameterized weekly weights to prior completed T6 levels; value-area levels come from volume distributed across evenly spaced samples of each bar's high-low range into nearest-tick bins
Calculation: RSI level score = floor((RSI(close, RSI lookback) − RSI midpoint) / RSI step) × points per step above the midpoint, or the negative floor of the distance below it divided by the step, times points per step
Calculation: stochastic score = clamp the specified upper or lower linear score of the smoothed Stochastic %K, add the bearish cross adjustment when smoothed %K crosses below smoothed %D above the upper extreme, or subtract the bullish cross adjustment when it crosses above smoothed %D below the lower extreme; smoothed %K and %D are separate SMAs of Stochastic %K with separate lookbacks
Calculation: CCI score = capped positive bucket score above the upper CCI threshold, capped negative bucket score below the lower CCI threshold, or zero otherwise; bucket size, thresholds, and cap are parameters; CCI uses HLC3 and its lookback
Calculation: MACD score = clamp the signed scaled excess of abs(MACD histogram / StDev(MACD histogram, normalization lookback)) above its extreme threshold, or zero within the threshold; add the divergence adjustment when close rises while the histogram falls and the score is positive, subtract it when close falls while the histogram rises and the score is negative; clamp to the score limits
Calculation: DMI trend score = clamp(((positive DI − negative DI) / trend normalizer) × ((ADX − ADX threshold) / ADX scale) × score scale) when ADX exceeds its threshold, or zero otherwise; use separate DMI lookback and smoothing parameters
Calculation: opposing volume-delta streak score = positive base plus streak increment per consecutive bar when volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1) is positive and close < open; negative of the corresponding amount when that delta is negative and close > open; otherwise zero and reset both streaks
Calculation: RSI divergence score = positive divergence weight when a confirmed swing high exceeds the prior confirmed swing high while its RSI is lower, or negative divergence weight when a confirmed swing low is lower while its RSI is higher; otherwise zero
Calculation: correlated-instrument divergence score = positive divergence weight when a confirmed traded-instrument swing high exceeds its prior high while the correlated instrument's corresponding high is lower, or negative divergence weight when a confirmed traded-instrument swing low is lower while the correlated instrument's corresponding low is higher; otherwise zero or zero when no correlated instrument is selected
Calculation: base score = BB extreme score + RSI level score + opposing volume-delta streak score + prior-period volume-profile reaction score + absorption score + stochastic score + CCI score + MACD score + DMI trend score + key-level reaction score + RSI divergence score + correlated-instrument divergence score; BB extreme score adds points per step for each close above EMA(close, BB lookback) + StDev(close, BB lookback) × step number × deviation step, subtracts them for each close below the corresponding lower level, and uses steps through the rounded maximum-deviation-to-step ratio
Calculation: adjusted score = chart-mapped base score from T1 (or chart bars if T1 is unset) + T2 base score × higher-timeframe scale when T2 is set + T3 base score × extra-timeframe scale when T3 is set; each base score is evaluated on its own timeframe bars and chart bars take values from the still-forming timeframe bar; then multiply by the trend factor selected from score sign and EMA slope direction, where slope = (EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) − its value trend-slope lookback bars ago) / trend-slope lookback, strength = clamp(slope amplification × abs(slope) / ATR(ATR lookback), zero, strength cap), pro factor = one plus strength × pro scale, and contra factor = one minus strength × contra scale; multiply by one plus the SMMA(close, SMMA lookback) deviation in pip units × SMMA percent-per-pip when score sign matches close's side of that SMMA; multiply by the ADX factor selected by separate low and high ADX thresholds; round to the score increment
Long: adjusted score < the long threshold above EMA(close, filter EMA lookback) when close is above that EMA, otherwise < the separately parameterized long threshold below it
Short: adjusted score > the short threshold above EMA(close, filter EMA lookback) when close is above that EMA, otherwise > the separately parameterized short threshold below it
Exit: long, low ≤ the signal-bar close for that position − stop distance or high ≥ that close + target distance
Exit: short, high ≥ the signal-bar close for that position + stop distance or low ≤ that close − target distance
