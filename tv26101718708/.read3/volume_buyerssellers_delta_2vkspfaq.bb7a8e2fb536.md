Calculation: Buyer volume = selected volume method: volume if close >= open else 0; volume × (close − low) / (high − low) if high > low else volume × location fallback ratio; or volume × (bull body fraction + upper wick fraction × wick weight), where bull body fraction is abs(close − open) / (high − low) when close >= open and high > low, body fallback fraction when close >= open and high = low, otherwise 0; upper wick fraction is (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise wick fallback fraction
Calculation: Seller volume = selected volume method: volume if close < open else 0; volume × (1 − (close − low) / (high − low)) if high > low else volume × (1 − location fallback ratio); or volume × (bear body fraction + lower wick fraction × wick weight), where bear body fraction is abs(close − open) / (high − low) when close < open and high > low, body fallback fraction when close < open and high = low, otherwise 0; lower wick fraction is (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise wick fallback fraction
Calculation: Buyer percentage = 100 × sum(Buyer volume, pressure lookback) / sum(Buyer volume + Seller volume, pressure lookback), or 50 when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Seller percentage = 100 × sum(Seller volume, pressure lookback) / sum(Buyer volume + Seller volume, pressure lookback), or 50 when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Delta = Buyer percentage − Seller percentage
Calculation: Consecutive buying bars = count of consecutive bars where Buyer volume > Seller volume, reset to zero otherwise; Consecutive selling bars = count of consecutive bars where Seller volume > Buyer volume, reset to zero otherwise
Long: Buyer percentage >= P1 threshold OR Buyer percentage > strong pressure threshold OR Delta > delta threshold OR divergence signals are enabled AND low < Lowest(low[1], divergence lookback) AND Delta > Delta[divergence lookback] (use zero if unavailable) AND Delta < 0 OR Consecutive buying bars > display streak threshold OR Consecutive buying bars >= sustained buying threshold
