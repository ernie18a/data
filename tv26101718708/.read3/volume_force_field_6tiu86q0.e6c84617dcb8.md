Calculation: Selected value centre = selected Rolling VWAP(price source, centre length, volume feed), KAMA(price source, centre length), or EMA(price source, centre length)
Calculation: Bounded tanh(x) = tanh(clamp(x, -saturation limit, saturation limit))
Calculation: Net pull = Bounded tanh(EMA((value-gravity weight / max(sum of value-gravity and drift weights, positive epsilon)) * ((Rolling VWAP(price source, recent-cluster window, volume feed) - close) / ATR(ATR length)) + (drift weight / max(sum of value-gravity and drift weights, positive epsilon)) * ((selected value centre - close) / ATR(ATR length)), force smoothing))
Calculation: Smoothed pull acceleration = EMA(Net pull - Net pull[1], force smoothing)
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or the midpoint when high equals low
Calculation: VSA volume = volume when time-of-day normalization is off; otherwise volume divided by the session EMA for the same time-of-day slot, using current volume when the slot baseline is uninitialized and one when the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive
Calculation: Field density = Gaussian-smoothed histogram of price-source observations weighted by the selected volume feed over the field lookback; the grid spans the lowest low to highest high, and bandwidth is max(bandwidth factor * standard deviation(price source, field lookback) * field lookback raised to the negative bandwidth exponent * bandwidth multiplier, minimum-bin-width factor * bin width)
Calculation: Density gradient = next-bin density minus current-bin density at the lower grid edge, current-bin density minus previous-bin density at the upper edge, and half the next-bin density minus previous-bin density at interior bins
Calculation: Field pull(p) = (value-gravity weight / max(sum of value-gravity and drift weights, positive epsilon)) * Bounded tanh(Density gradient(p) / (peak field density * gradient scale)) + (drift weight / max(sum of value-gravity and drift weights, positive epsilon)) * Bounded tanh((selected value centre - p) / max(sqrt(SMA((price source - selected value centre)^2, centre length)), ATR(ATR length) * minimum sigma factor))
Long: crosses above(Net pull, strong-pull threshold) OR Net pull > strong-pull threshold OR crosses above(Net pull, zero) OR crosses below(Smoothed pull acceleration, zero) OR (Net pull >= strong-pull threshold AND Net pull * Smoothed pull acceleration > zero) OR (volume is available AND low <= Lowest(low, VSA extreme lookback)[1] AND Close location >= shakeout close-location threshold AND PercentRank(VSA volume, VSA history window) >= VSA medium percentile threshold) OR (volume is available AND close < close[1] AND PercentRank(high - low, VSA history window) >= VSA medium percentile threshold AND PercentRank(VSA volume, VSA history window) >= VSA high-volume percentile threshold AND Close location >= stopping close-location threshold) OR (volume is available AND close < close[1] AND VSA volume >= Highest(VSA volume, VSA history window) AND PercentRank(high - low, VSA history window) >= climax range-percentile threshold) OR (for a field grid level p, Field density(p) / peak field density > field display-density threshold AND Field pull(p) >= zero)
Short: crosses below(Net pull, -strong-pull threshold) OR Net pull < -strong-pull threshold OR crosses below(Net pull, zero) OR crosses above(Smoothed pull acceleration, zero) OR (Net pull <= -strong-pull threshold AND Net pull * Smoothed pull acceleration > zero) OR (volume is available AND high >= Highest(high, VSA extreme lookback)[1] AND Close location <= upthrust close-location threshold AND PercentRank(VSA volume, VSA history window) >= VSA medium percentile threshold) OR (volume is available AND close > close[1] AND VSA volume >= Highest(VSA volume, VSA history window) AND PercentRank(high - low, VSA history window) >= climax range-percentile threshold) OR (for a field grid level p, Field density(p) / peak field density > field display-density threshold AND Field pull(p) < zero)
