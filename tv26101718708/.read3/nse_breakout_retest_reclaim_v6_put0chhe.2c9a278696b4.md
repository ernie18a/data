Calculation: day pace RVOL = current day cumulative chart-bar volume divided by the mean of available historical cumulative-volume values at chart-bar offsets equal to the current in-session bar count multiplied by successive positive integers through the comparison-session-count parameter
Calculation: setup zone = saved breakout level plus or minus ATR(ATR lookback parameter) from the setup bar multiplied by the zone-width parameter
Calculation: active setup = a setup signal not followed by an entry or invalidation; invalidate it if no retest occurs by more than the setup-wait parameter bars after setup, more than the reclaim-window parameter bars elapse after retest, or a bar closes below the saved lower zone boundary or session VWAP
Long: within the configured session after skipping the configured initial in-session bar count, with no active setup, close crosses above an enabled level (the high of the last completed T1 bar, or the highest high over the long-term lookback ending at the last completed T1 bar, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars; or the high of the first session bars using the shorter or longer opening-range bar-count parameter); the current forming T1 bar's open and last completed T1 bar's close are available and the close is nonzero; the gap percentage ((current forming T1 open - last completed T1 close) / last completed T1 close) × 100 is between the gap lower and upper parameters; close > session VWAP(hlc3) and session VWAP(hlc3) > its prior chart-bar value; volume / SMA(volume, bar-RVOL lookback parameter) >= breakout-RVOL parameter; day pace RVOL >= day-pace parameter if enabled; and high - low <= ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × breakout-size parameter
Long: an active setup with no prior retest, the bar overlaps the saved setup zone (low <= zone upper boundary AND high >= zone lower boundary), close >= zone lower boundary, and close >= session VWAP(hlc3)
Long: an active setup with a prior retest, bars since retest are between one and the reclaim-window parameter inclusive, close > saved zone upper boundary, close > high of the retest bar, volume / SMA(volume, bar-RVOL lookback parameter) >= reclaim-RVOL parameter, and close > session VWAP(hlc3)
