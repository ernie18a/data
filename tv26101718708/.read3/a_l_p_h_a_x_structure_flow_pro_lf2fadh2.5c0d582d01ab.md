Calculation: Momentum Z = (change(close) - SMA(change(close), lookback)) / StDev(change(close), lookback), or zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, lookback) when the SMA is positive, otherwise the fallback volume ratio.
Calculation: Tracked swing high/low = the latest confirmed Pivot High(high, pivot lookback, pivot lookback) / Pivot Low(low, pivot lookback, pivot lookback), respectively; replace each with every newly confirmed pivot and clear it after a bullish/bearish MSB, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high[2] AND low - high[2] >= ATR(lookback) * minimum gap factor; its zone is [high[2], low] and is filled when a later low <= its top. Bearish FVG = high < low[2] AND low[2] - high >= ATR(lookback) * minimum gap factor; its zone is [high, low[2]] and is filled when a later high >= its bottom. When FVG tracking is enabled, retain up to the FVG retention limit, removing the oldest zones first.
Calculation: HPZ order blocks = on each MSB, find the nearest opposite-color candle among the preceding OB lookback bars; its high and low define the block. On the MSB bar, block score = clamp(abs(Momentum Z) * momentum weight + PercentRank(volume, rank lookback) * volume weight + Body-to-range ratio * body weight + block range / ATR(lookback) * range weight, score floor, score ceiling). The block is HPZ when its score >= HPZ threshold. A bullish block is mitigated when low < its bottom; a bearish block is mitigated when high > its top. Retain up to the OB retention limit, removing the oldest blocks first; when overlap suppression is enabled, skip a block if either boundary lies inside an unmitigated prior block.
Calculation: Nearby bullish/bearish HPZ count = number of unmitigated bullish/bearish HPZ blocks whose range, expanded on both sides by ATR(lookback) * proximity factor, contains close.
Calculation: Bull SMC score = clamp((bullish MSB ? MSB points : bullish BMS ? BMS points : 0) + (bullish sweep ? sweep points : 0) + (nearby bullish HPZ count >= 2 ? two-HPZ points : nearby bullish HPZ count = 1 ? one-HPZ points : 0) + (unfilled bearish FVGs above close >= 2 ? two-FVG points : unfilled bearish FVGs above close = 1 ? one-FVG points : 0) + (Momentum Z > high positive-Z threshold ? strong-Z points : Momentum Z > medium positive-Z threshold ? medium-Z points : Momentum Z > low positive-Z threshold ? mild-Z points : 0) + (Volume ratio > spike threshold ? spike-volume points : Volume ratio > high-volume threshold ? high-volume points : Volume ratio > above-average threshold ? above-average-volume points : 0) + (close > open AND Body-to-range ratio > strong-body threshold ? strong-body points : close > open ? directional-candle points : 0) - (Momentum Z < negative medium positive-Z threshold ? opposing-momentum penalty : 0) - (nearby bearish HPZ count >= 2 ? opposing-HPZ penalty : 0), score floor, score ceiling); high, medium, and low positive-Z thresholds are ordered high > medium > low, and volume thresholds are ordered spike > high-volume > above-average.
Calculation: Bear SMC score = clamp((bearish MSB ? MSB points : bearish BMS ? BMS points : 0) + (bearish sweep ? sweep points : 0) + (nearby bearish HPZ count >= 2 ? two-HPZ points : nearby bearish HPZ count = 1 ? one-HPZ points : 0) + (unfilled bullish FVGs below close >= 2 ? two-FVG points : unfilled bullish FVGs below close = 1 ? one-FVG points : 0) + (Momentum Z < negative high positive-Z threshold ? strong-Z points : Momentum Z < negative medium positive-Z threshold ? medium-Z points : Momentum Z < negative low positive-Z threshold ? mild-Z points : 0) + (Volume ratio > spike threshold ? spike-volume points : Volume ratio > high-volume threshold ? high-volume points : Volume ratio > above-average threshold ? above-average-volume points : 0) + (close < open AND Body-to-range ratio > strong-body threshold ? strong-body points : close < open ? directional-candle points : 0) - (Momentum Z > medium positive-Z threshold ? opposing-momentum penalty : 0) - (nearby bullish HPZ count >= 2 ? opposing-HPZ penalty : 0), score floor, score ceiling).
Long: close crosses above the tracked swing high AND Momentum Z > MSB threshold.
Short: close crosses below the tracked swing low AND Momentum Z < -MSB threshold.
Long: BMS detection is enabled AND close crosses above the tracked swing high AND Body-to-range ratio > BMS body threshold.
Short: BMS detection is enabled AND close crosses below the tracked swing low AND Body-to-range ratio > BMS body threshold.
Long: liquidity-sweep detection is enabled AND low < Lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) AND close > Lowest(low[1], sweep lookback) AND close > open.
Short: liquidity-sweep detection is enabled AND high > Highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < Highest(high[1], sweep lookback) AND close < open.
Long: bullish FVG forms.
Short: bearish FVG forms.
Long: SMC engine is enabled AND Bull SMC score >= minimum SMC score AND (volume filter is disabled OR Volume ratio >= minimum volume ratio) AND (bullish MSB OR bullish sweep) AND at least the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the prior long SMC signal.
Short: SMC engine is enabled AND Bear SMC score >= minimum SMC score AND (volume filter is disabled OR Volume ratio >= minimum volume ratio) AND (bearish MSB OR bearish sweep) AND at least the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the prior short SMC signal.
Exit: Long, bearish MSB.
Exit: Short, bullish MSB.
