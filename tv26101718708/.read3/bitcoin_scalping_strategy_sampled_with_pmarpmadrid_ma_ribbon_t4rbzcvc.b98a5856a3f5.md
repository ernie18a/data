Calculation: price-to-MA ratio percentile = 100 × count of preceding ratios abs(selected price source / MA(selected price source, MA lookback, ratio-MA type [SMA/EMA/HMA/RMA/VWMA])) that are <= the current ratio, divided by min(number of prior bars, percentile lookback); available only when at least MA lookback prior bars exist
Long: for all eighteen progressively longer, equally spaced short lookbacks below the shared long lookback, MA(close, short lookback, ribbon type [EMA/SMA]) > MA(close, long lookback, ribbon type) AND MA(close, short lookback, ribbon type) >= its prior-bar value AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold AND close[1] <= open[1]
Short: for the first ten and last seven of those eighteen short lookbacks, MA(close, short lookback, ribbon type [EMA/SMA]) < MA(close, long lookback, ribbon type) AND MA(close, short lookback, ribbon type) < its prior-bar value
Exit: short, price-to-MA ratio percentile <= lower percentile threshold OR low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 - take-profit fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction)
