Calculation: Structure range = absolute difference between the latest confirmed chart-bar pivot high and pivot low, each using the structure swing-length parameter for left and right bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high using the structure swing-length parameter for left and right bars; this marks an upward BOS/CHoCH and sets the structure direction bullish.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low using the structure swing-length parameter for left and right bars; this marks a downward BOS/CHoCH and sets the structure direction bearish.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, price intersects an active, unused bullish order block and close > open, the most recent structure break was upward, Structure range > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum swing-range parameter, and the last completed T1 bar's EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback); the EMAs are calculated on T1 bars and their last completed values are sampled on chart bars. The order block is the high-low range of the nearest down-close chart bar within the order-block search-lookback parameter before an upward structure break; price intersects it when low ≤ its top and high ≥ its bottom.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, price intersects an active, unused bearish order block and close < open, the most recent structure break was downward, Structure range > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum swing-range parameter, and the last completed T1 bar's EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback); the EMAs are calculated on T1 bars and their last completed values are sampled on chart bars. The order block is the high-low range of the nearest up-close chart bar within the order-block search-lookback parameter before a downward structure break; price intersects it when high ≥ its bottom and low ≤ its top.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, the previous chart bar's low is below the latest confirmed pivot low using the swept swing-length parameter for left and right bars, current close is above that pivot low, close > open, close > close[1], and close − low[1] > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum trap-size parameter.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the previous chart bar's high is above the latest confirmed pivot high using the swept swing-length parameter for left and right bars, current close is below that pivot high, close < open, close < close[1], and high[1] − close > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum trap-size parameter.
Long: On chart bars, a bullish sweep on the last completed T2 bar is sampled and its direction differs from the prior chart bar's sampled T2 sweep direction. The sweep is calculated on T2 bars: the setup bar's low is below the latest confirmed pivot low using the swept swing-length parameter for left and right bars, and the next bar closes above that pivot low, above its open and prior close, with close − setup-bar low > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum trap-size parameter.
Short: On chart bars, a bearish sweep on the last completed T2 bar is sampled and its direction differs from the prior chart bar's sampled T2 sweep direction. The sweep is calculated on T2 bars: the setup bar's high is above the latest confirmed pivot high using the swept swing-length parameter for left and right bars, and the next bar closes below that pivot high, below its open and prior close, with setup-bar high − close > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum trap-size parameter.
Long: On chart bars, a bullish sweep on the last completed T3 bar is sampled and its direction differs from the prior chart bar's sampled T3 sweep direction. The sweep is calculated on T3 bars: the setup bar's low is below the latest confirmed pivot low using the swept swing-length parameter for left and right bars, and the next bar closes above that pivot low, above its open and prior close, with close − setup-bar low > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum trap-size parameter.
Short: On chart bars, a bearish sweep on the last completed T3 bar is sampled and its direction differs from the prior chart bar's sampled T3 sweep direction. The sweep is calculated on T3 bars: the setup bar's high is above the latest confirmed pivot high using the swept swing-length parameter for left and right bars, and the next bar closes below that pivot high, below its open and prior close, with setup-bar high − close > ATR(ATR lookback parameter) × minimum trap-size parameter.
