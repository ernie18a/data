Calculation: confirmed status starts as the initial raw status and thereafter changes only after the candidate raw status holds for max(confirmation-bars, 2) consecutive chart bars; raw status is risk-off if close <= EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, calculated on T1 bars using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars) OR close <= SMA(close, T1 SMA lookback, calculated on T1 bars using the last completed T1 bar and sampled on chart bars), risk-on if close > both of those averages AND close > EMA(close, shorter chart lookback) AND close > EMA(close, longer chart lookback) AND close > EMA(close, shorter T2 lookback, calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and sampled on chart bars) AND close > EMA(close, longer T2 lookback, calculated on T2 bars using the last completed T2 bar and sampled on chart bars), and neutral otherwise; T1 is the shorter and T2 the longer of the two non-chart timeframes, and each shorter/longer EMA lookback pair uses separate parameters
Long: confirmed status = risk-on AND confirmed status[1] != risk-on
Short: confirmed status = risk-off AND confirmed status[1] != risk-off
