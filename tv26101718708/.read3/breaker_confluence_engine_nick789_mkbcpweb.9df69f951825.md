Calculation: MSS swing sequence = confirmed pivothigh(high, MSS left lookback, MSS right lookback) and pivotlow(low, MSS left lookback, MSS right lookback) on T1 bars, stored newest first; append opposite-direction pivots and replace the newest same-direction pivot only when more extreme. Select D as the third-newest swing if it has the target direction, otherwise the second-newest; C is the next older swing and E the next newer swing.
Calculation: Gap Z-score = (gap size − mean gap size for the same time-of-day slot) / standard deviation of those gap sizes; use 0 when standard deviation is 0. Gap size is low − high[2] for bullish gaps and low[2] − high for bearish gaps.
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume[1] − mean volume[1] for the same time-of-day slot) / standard deviation of those volumes; use 0 when standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Breaker zone = pivot candle high and low in pivot-candle mode; in candle-run mode, top and bottom are the maximum and minimum of the latest contiguous same-direction candle run between D and C within the search depth, using candle bodies when body mode is enabled and wicks otherwise. Midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2.
Long: In pivot-candle mode, on T1 bars using the current T1 bar and sampling source values onto chart bars with lookahead off, close crosses above the high of the most recent confirmed pivot-low candle, and the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session.
Short: In pivot-candle mode, on T1 bars using the current T1 bar and sampling source values onto chart bars with lookahead off, close crosses below the low of the most recent confirmed pivot-high candle, and the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session.
Long: In candle-run mode, on T1 bars using the current T1 bar and sampling source values onto chart bars with lookahead off, D is an MSS swing high, close > D.price, E.price < C.price, C.time ≠ D.time, no bullish MSS break has occurred since the most recent bearish MSS break (or initialization), a consecutive bullish candle run exists between D and C within the search depth, and the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session.
Short: In candle-run mode, on T1 bars using the current T1 bar and sampling source values onto chart bars with lookahead off, D is an MSS swing low, close < D.price, E.price > C.price, C.time ≠ D.time, no bearish MSS break has occurred since the most recent bullish MSS break (or initialization), a consecutive bearish candle run exists between D and C within the search depth, and the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session.
Long: On T1 bars, a most recent confirmed pivot-low candle exists, the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session, close < that candle’s high and close ≥ that high − ATR(potential ATR length, T1) × potential ATR multiplier, and this condition was false on the previous T1 bar.
Short: On T1 bars, a most recent confirmed pivot-high candle exists, the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session, close > that candle’s low and close ≤ that low + ATR(potential ATR length, T1) × potential ATR multiplier, and this condition was false on the previous T1 bar.
Long: On T1 bars using the current T1 bar and sampling source values onto chart bars with lookahead off, the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session, and low > high[2], and either T1 differs from the chart timeframe, chart-FVG ranking is disabled, or both configured gap and volume rank filters pass.
Short: On T1 bars using the current T1 bar and sampling source values onto chart bars with lookahead off, the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session, and high < low[2], and either T1 differs from the chart timeframe, chart-FVG ranking is disabled, or both configured gap and volume rank filters pass.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, T1 differs from the chart timeframe, the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session, low > high[2], and chart-FVG ranking is disabled or both configured gap and volume rank filters pass.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, T1 differs from the chart timeframe, the session filter is disabled or the bar is within the configured New York session, high < low[2], and chart-FVG ranking is disabled or both configured gap and volume rank filters pass.
Long: On a chart bar for a bullish breaker, open > midpoint, open < top, and close > top, with no earlier retest, cancellation (close < midpoint and close > bottom), or mitigation since the breaker formed; mitigation is low < bottom in pivot-candle mode or close < bottom in candle-run mode.
Short: On a chart bar for a bearish breaker, open < midpoint, open > bottom, and close < bottom, with no earlier retest, cancellation (close > midpoint and close < top), or mitigation since the breaker formed; mitigation is high > top in pivot-candle mode or close > top in candle-run mode.
