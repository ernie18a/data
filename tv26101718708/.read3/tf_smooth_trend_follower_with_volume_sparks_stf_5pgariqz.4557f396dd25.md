Calculation: trailing trend state = source (high + low + 2 × close) / 4; distance = max(HMA(high − low, range length), minimum tick); factor = base factor when ATR adaptation is off, otherwise base factor × ATR(ATR length) / SMA(ATR(ATR length), ATR-average length), using ratio 1 if the average is unavailable or nonpositive and clamping between the lesser and greater adaptive-factor limits; provisional bands = source ± factor × distance; ratchet the lower band to its provisional value if it exceeds its prior value or prior close is below the prior lower band, otherwise keep the prior lower band; ratchet the upper band to its provisional value if it is below its prior value or prior close is above the prior upper band, otherwise keep the prior upper band; direction is down when prior distance is unavailable, otherwise it switches from down to up when close > upper band and stays down otherwise, or switches from up to down when close < lower band and stays up otherwise.
Long: prior direction is down AND close > upper band
Short: prior direction is up AND close < lower band
