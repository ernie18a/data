Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = top low and bottom high[2] at formation.
Calculation: Bearish FVG zone = top low[2] and bottom high at formation.
Calculation: Zone direction = bullish for a bullish FVG and bearish for a bearish FVG; it reverses on its first inversion and cannot invert again.
Calculation: A zone is stored until evicted as oldest when the zone count exceeds its limit, removed after its age exceeds the validity lookback when expiry deletion is enabled, or removed after its first mitigation when mitigation deletion is enabled; its age is the number of chart bars since formation.
Calculation: Zone mitigation level = midpoint of its top and bottom in midpoint mode; top for bullish direction or bottom for bearish direction in proximal mode; bottom for bullish direction or top for bearish direction in distal mode.
Long: low > high[2] AND (high[2] != 0 ? (low - high[2]) / high[2] * 100 : 0) >= minimum gap percentage AND (ATR filter disabled OR low - high[2] >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap/ATR ratio) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (confirmed-candle-only disabled OR current bar is confirmed) AND bullish FVG detection enabled.
Short: high < low[2] AND (low[2] != 0 ? (low[2] - high) / low[2] * 100 : 0) >= minimum gap percentage AND (ATR filter disabled OR low[2] - high >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum gap/ATR ratio) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, EMA lookback)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier) AND (confirmed-candle-only disabled OR current bar is confirmed) AND bearish FVG detection enabled.
Long: an inversion signal is enabled AND a stored bearish FVG zone has not previously inverted AND (close > its top in close-through mode OR high > its top in wick-through mode).
Short: an inversion signal is enabled AND a stored bullish FVG zone has not previously inverted AND (close < its bottom in close-through mode OR low < its bottom in wick-through mode).
Long: a stored zone has bullish direction and has not been mitigated since creation or its most recent inversion AND low <= its mitigation level AND high >= its bottom.
Short: a stored zone has bearish direction and has not been mitigated since creation or its most recent inversion AND high >= its mitigation level AND low <= its top.
