Calculation: Smoothed flow(T) = (EMA(HA-open, shorter flow lookback) + EMA(HA-close, shorter flow lookback)) / 2 on T bars; HA-close = (EMA(open, longer flow lookback) + EMA(high, longer flow lookback) + EMA(low, longer flow lookback) + EMA(close, longer flow lookback)) / 4; HA-open = (prior HA-open + prior HA-close) / 2, seeded on the first T bar with (EMA(open, longer flow lookback) + EMA(close, longer flow lookback)) / 2; the flow lookbacks are shared across T1, T2, and T3
Calculation: Trend line(T) = Smoothed flow(T) when line smoothing is disabled, otherwise EMA(EMA(Smoothed flow(T) sampled onto chart bars, line lookback(T)), line lookback(T)); Smoothed flow(T) is computed on T bars and sampled without future lookahead, using the last completed T bar historically and the still-forming T bar in real time; line EMAs run on chart bars; line lookback(T) = clamp(truncate(T duration / max(chart-bar duration, minimum duration) * smoothing strength), minimum line lookback, maximum line lookback)
Long: close > Trend line(T1) AND Trend line(T2) > Trend line(T1) AND (close > Trend line(T3) OR bullish trend was latched on the prior chart bar)
Short: close < Trend line(T1) AND Trend line(T2) < Trend line(T1) AND (close < Trend line(T3) OR bearish trend was latched on the prior chart bar)
