Calculation: Gaussian midline = Gaussian filter(configured source, poles, sampling period, reduced-lag mode, fast-response mode)
Calculation: Filtered true range = Gaussian filter(TrueRange, same poles, sampling period, same modes)
Calculation: Gaussian upper band = Gaussian midline + filtered true range * band multiplier
Calculation: Gaussian lower band = Gaussian midline - filtered true range * band multiplier
Long: Long trading enabled AND Gaussian midline > Gaussian midline[1] AND close > Gaussian upper band AND (StochRSI K(configured source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing) > upper threshold OR StochRSI K(configured source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing) < lower threshold) AND (baseline filter disabled OR close > configured EMA or SMA(close, baseline lookback)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(lookback, same lookback) > ADX threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar sampled on chart bars) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar sampled on chart bars)) AND datetime within configured trading interval AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within configured entry session) AND weekday enabled for trading
Short: Short trading enabled AND Gaussian midline < Gaussian midline[1] AND close < Gaussian lower band AND (StochRSI K(configured source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing) > upper threshold OR StochRSI K(configured source, RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing) < lower threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR EMA(close, longer lookback, T1 current bar sampled on chart bars) > EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1 current bar sampled on chart bars)) AND datetime within configured trading interval AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within configured entry session) AND weekday enabled for trading
Exit: Long, (datetime within configured trading interval AND close crosses below Gaussian upper band) OR (forced day close enabled AND bar time within configured end-of-day exit window) OR (ATR stop enabled AND low <= entry-signal close - ATR(lookback at entry signal) * stop multiple) OR (ATR target enabled AND high >= entry-signal close + ATR(lookback at entry signal) * stop multiple * reward-to-risk ratio)
Exit: Short, (datetime within configured trading interval AND close crosses above Gaussian lower band) OR (forced day close enabled AND bar time within configured end-of-day exit window) OR (ATR stop enabled AND high >= entry-signal close + ATR(lookback at entry signal) * stop multiple) OR (ATR target enabled AND low <= entry-signal close - ATR(lookback at entry signal) * stop multiple * reward-to-risk ratio)
