Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high[swing lookback] > highest(high, swing lookback) or low[swing lookback] < lowest(low, swing lookback), respectively, when the swing state changes to that side.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = on the first close above an unbroken confirmed swing high, the high-low range of the lowest-low candle after that swing and before the break, if its height <= ATR(OB ATR lookback) × OB size limit; create only within the OB creation-distance from the latest bar.
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = on the first close below an unbroken confirmed swing low, the high-low range of the highest-high candle after that swing and before the break, if its height <= ATR(OB ATR lookback) × OB size limit; create only within the OB creation-distance from the latest bar.
Calculation: Demand zone = when at least the required bullish candle count in the momentum span have abs(close - open) >= SMA(abs(close - open), body lookback) × body multiple and close > open, use the high-low range of the candle momentum span + 1 bars ago, clamp its height to ATR(S&D ATR lookback) × S&D size limit, and require more than the spacing parameter since the prior demand zone.
Calculation: Supply zone = when at least the required bearish candle count in the momentum span have abs(close - open) >= SMA(abs(close - open), body lookback) × body multiple and close <= open, use the high-low range of the candle momentum span + 1 bars ago, clamp its height to ATR(S&D ATR lookback) × S&D size limit, and require more than the spacing parameter since the prior supply zone.
Calculation: Eligible zones = on each enabled source timeframe, retain up to the storage cap newest zones per type, then the configured zone count per type; merge zones of the same type and direction when their time-price rectangles' intersection area divided by union area exceeds the overlap threshold, using the union price range, latest nonempty break time, and larger source timeframe (reclamping merged S&D zones). Calculate zones on source-timeframe bars; chart bars read the latest T1 values (last completed T1 bar historically, forming T1 bar in realtime).
Long: (live eligible demand zone AND low < zone top AND zone age >= minimum zone age × source-timeframe duration AND chart bars since prior demand retest > retest cooldown) OR (live eligible bullish order-block zone AND low < zone top AND chart bars since prior bullish order-block retest > retest cooldown)
Short: (live eligible supply zone AND high > zone bottom AND zone age >= minimum zone age × source-timeframe duration AND chart bars since prior supply retest > retest cooldown) OR (live eligible bearish order-block zone AND high > zone bottom AND chart bars since prior bearish order-block retest > retest cooldown)
Long: (eligible supply zone invalidated this chart bar above its top using the S&D invalidation mode—wick: high > top; close: max(open, close) > top—and current bar time - zone start time > minimum zone age × chart-timeframe duration) OR (eligible bearish order-block zone invalidated this chart bar above its top using the OB invalidation mode—wick: high > top; close: max(open, close) > top—and current bar time - zone start time > minimum zone age × chart-timeframe duration)
Short: (eligible demand zone invalidated this chart bar below its bottom using the S&D invalidation mode—wick: low < bottom; close: min(open, close) < bottom—and current bar time - zone start time > minimum zone age × chart-timeframe duration) OR (eligible bullish order-block zone invalidated this chart bar below its bottom using the OB invalidation mode—wick: low < bottom; close: min(open, close) < bottom—and current bar time - zone start time > minimum zone age × chart-timeframe duration)
