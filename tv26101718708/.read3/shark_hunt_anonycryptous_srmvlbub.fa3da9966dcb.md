Calculation: Zone hold percentage = 100 when there are no touches; otherwise round((touches − 1)/touches × 100).
Calculation: Bullish price failure = low < latest confirmed pivot low (pattern pivot lookback on each side) AND close > that pivot AND previous low >= that pivot.
Calculation: Bearish price failure = high > latest confirmed pivot high (pattern pivot lookback on each side) AND close < that pivot AND previous high <= that pivot.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = close > open AND previous open > previous close AND close > previous open AND open < previous close.
Calculation: Bearish absorption = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close.
Calculation: Bullish wick trap = lower wick > body × wick-to-body parameter AND lower wick > ATR(ATR lookback) × wick-to-ATR parameter.
Calculation: Bearish wick trap = upper wick > body × wick-to-body parameter AND upper wick > ATR(ATR lookback) × wick-to-ATR parameter.
Calculation: Liquidity zones form at confirmed pivot lows and highs using equal left and right zone pivot lookbacks, adjusted shorter, unchanged, or longer by the zone-significance parameter. A bullish zone spans the pivot low to that low plus ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-width parameter; a bearish zone spans the pivot high minus that amount to the pivot high, using ATR on the confirmation bar. Keep zones with positive impulse score; within ATR × minimum-separation parameter, keep the higher-score zone, retaining the existing zone on a tie. Remove the oldest zone when the per-side zone-count parameter is exceeded. A fresh zone can flip sides on a confirmed close beyond its outer boundary when zone flipping is enabled. Count touches while fresh when price enters the zone; a zone is eligible when untouched or its hold percentage is at least the minimum-hold parameter.

Calculation: Zone backtest stop = for a long, entry price − ATR(ATR lookback) × (zone-width parameter + zone-stop-buffer parameter) in zone-stop mode, otherwise signal-bar low − ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-stop-buffer parameter; for a short, entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) × (zone-width parameter + zone-stop-buffer parameter) in zone-stop mode, otherwise signal-bar high + ATR(ATR lookback) × zone-stop-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Zone backtest target = for a long, entry price + abs(entry price − zone backtest stop) × zone risk/reward parameter; for a short, entry price − abs(zone backtest stop − entry price) × zone risk/reward parameter.
Calculation: Failure backtest stop = for a long, signal-bar low − ATR(ATR lookback) × failure-stop-buffer parameter in wick-stop mode, otherwise latest confirmed pivot low − ATR(ATR lookback) × failure-stop-buffer parameter; for a short, signal-bar high + ATR(ATR lookback) × failure-stop-buffer parameter in wick-stop mode, otherwise latest confirmed pivot high + ATR(ATR lookback) × failure-stop-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Failure backtest target = for a long, entry price + abs(entry price − failure backtest stop) × failure risk/reward parameter; for a short, entry price − abs(failure backtest stop − entry price) × failure risk/reward parameter.
Exit: long, low <= zone backtest stop OR high >= zone backtest target.
Exit: short, high >= zone backtest stop OR low <= zone backtest target.
