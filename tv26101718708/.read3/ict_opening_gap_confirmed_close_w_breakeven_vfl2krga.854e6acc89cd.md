Calculation: Opening gap bounds = on the first chart bar of each T1 interval, upper = max(open, close[1]) and lower = min(open, close[1]); retain until the next T1 interval.
Calculation: Cross count = reset to zero with prior side neutral at each T2 boundary; on chart bars with defined gap bounds, increment and store the outside-gap side when close > upper bound or close < lower bound and that side differs from the stored side; otherwise leave the count and stored side unchanged.
Long: flat AND no entry has been taken in the current T2 interval AND cross count = required-cross parameter AND close > opening gap upper bound.
Short: flat AND no entry has been taken in the current T2 interval AND cross count = required-cross parameter AND close < opening gap lower bound.
Exit: Long, datetime hour and minute in New York equal the exit-time parameter OR close < opening gap lower bound OR (breakeven enabled AND high >= entry price + trigger parameter × minimum tick AND low <= entry price + offset parameter × minimum tick) OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + target parameter × minimum tick).
Exit: Short, datetime hour and minute in New York equal the exit-time parameter OR close > opening gap upper bound OR (breakeven enabled AND low <= entry price − trigger parameter × minimum tick AND high >= entry price − offset parameter × minimum tick) OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price − target parameter × minimum tick).
