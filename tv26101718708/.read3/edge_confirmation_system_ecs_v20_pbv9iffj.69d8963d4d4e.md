Calculation: percentage EMA spread = 100 * (EMA(close, shorter momentum lookback) - EMA(close, longer momentum lookback)) / EMA(close, longer momentum lookback)
Calculation: normalized momentum = if highest(percentage EMA spread, normalization lookback) > lowest(percentage EMA spread, normalization lookback), then 2 * ((percentage EMA spread - lowest(percentage EMA spread, normalization lookback)) / (highest(percentage EMA spread, normalization lookback) - lowest(percentage EMA spread, normalization lookback))) - 1; otherwise 0
Long: close > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, intermediate trend lookback) AND normalized momentum > 0 AND normalized momentum > EMA(normalized momentum, smoothing lookback) AND normalized momentum < overbought threshold AND (volume surge requirement is disabled OR (volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume surge multiplier AND close > open)) AND position side is not long
Short: close < EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < EMA(close, intermediate trend lookback) AND normalized momentum < 0 AND normalized momentum < EMA(normalized momentum, smoothing lookback) AND normalized momentum > oversold threshold AND (volume surge requirement is disabled OR (volume >= SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume surge multiplier AND close < open)) AND position side is not short
Exit: Long, (normalized momentum < 0 OR normalized momentum >= overbought threshold) AND normalized momentum < EMA(normalized momentum, smoothing lookback)
Exit: Short, (normalized momentum > 0 OR normalized momentum <= oversold threshold) AND normalized momentum > EMA(normalized momentum, smoothing lookback)
