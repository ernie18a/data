Calculation: Signal score = sum of the enabled Heikin Ashi, Supertrend, and UT Bot votes; each vote is +1 when bullish, -1 when bearish, and 0 when neutral. Heikin Ashi is bullish when its close > its open and bearish when its close < its open; Supertrend(ATR factor parameter, ATR length parameter) votes by its direction; UT Bot(Heikin Ashi close, key-value parameter, ATR-period parameter) is bullish when Heikin Ashi close > its ATR trailing stop and bearish when Heiken Ashi close < its ATR trailing stop.
Long: Signal score > 0 OR MA(close, shortest lookback, first MA type parameter) crosses above MA(close, middle lookback, second MA type parameter) OR MA(close, middle lookback, second MA type parameter) crosses above MA(close, longest lookback, third MA type parameter)
Short: Signal score < 0 OR MA(close, shortest lookback, first MA type parameter) crosses below MA(close, middle lookback, second MA type parameter) OR MA(close, middle lookback, second MA type parameter) crosses below MA(close, longest lookback, third MA type parameter)
