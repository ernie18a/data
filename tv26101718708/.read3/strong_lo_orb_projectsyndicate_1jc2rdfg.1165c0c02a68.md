Calculation: opening-range high and low = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars in the configured opening-range session, finalized when that session ends; opening-range midpoint = arithmetic mean of those bounds; opening-range width = high minus low; session anchor open = open of the first chart bar in that session; ADR reference = SMA(high minus low, ADR lookback, T2, last completed bar, calculated on T2 bars), sampled at the opening-range session start.
Calculation: breakout score(direction) = clamp(round the sum of body points, candle-range points, volume points, momentum points, penetration points, RSI points, HTF points, session-bias points, first-entry points, and coil points to the score rounding increment, score lower bound, score upper bound). On chart bars, body ratio = abs(close minus open) / (high minus low), or body-ratio fallback if high equals low; body points use body-high points at or above the body-high threshold, else body-mid points at or above the body-mid threshold, else body-low points at or above the body-low threshold, else body-floor points. Candle-range ratio = (high minus low) / ATR(ATR lookback), or ATR-ratio fallback if ATR is zero; candle-range points use the corresponding high, mid, low, or floor points at their respective thresholds. Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), or volume-ratio fallback if that SMA is not positive; volume points use the corresponding high, mid, or low points at their respective thresholds, else add nothing. Short and long momentum are close minus close at the shorter and longer momentum lookbacks; momentum points are both-momentum points if both changes agree with direction, one-momentum points if exactly one agrees, otherwise the neither-momentum points. Penetration ratio = distance of close beyond the opening-range edge in the breakout direction / ATR(ATR lookback), or penetration fallback if ATR is zero; penetration points use the corresponding high, mid, or low points at their respective thresholds, else add nothing. RSI points are added when RSI(close, RSI lookback) is above the long RSI threshold for long direction or below the short RSI threshold for short direction. HTF points are added when the HTF filter is disabled or price is aligned with its EMA; session-bias points are added when close is above the session anchor open for long direction or below it for short direction; first-entry points are added when no entry has yet occurred in the opening-range session. Coil ratio = opening-range width / ADR reference, or coil-ratio fallback if ADR is not positive; coil points are added when this ratio is between the coil-score lower and upper bounds, inclusive.
Calculation: protective-stop risk distance = min(max(abs(entry price minus raw stop), ATR(ATR lookback) at entry multiplied by minimum-risk ATR multiple), ATR(ATR lookback) at entry multiplied by maximum-risk ATR multiple). Raw stop uses the configured basis: for long, ATR basis is entry price minus ATR at entry times ATR-stop multiple, midpoint basis is opening-range midpoint minus ATR at entry times stop-buffer multiple, and opposite-edge basis is opening-range low minus ATR at entry times stop-buffer multiple; for short, use the corresponding plus operations and opening-range high for the opposite edge.
Calculation: protective stop = entry price minus risk distance for long, or entry price plus risk distance for short; this level is fixed at entry.
Calculation: third target = entry price plus risk distance times the third-target R multiple for long, or entry price minus risk distance times the third-target R multiple for short; this level is fixed at entry.
Long: on a chart bar close, opening range is formed AND bar is inside the configured trade window AND close crosses above opening-range high (close > opening-range high AND prior chart-bar close <= opening-range high) AND (break-buffer filter disabled OR close minus opening-range high >= break-buffer ATR multiple times ATR(ATR lookback)) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars)) AND (ADR gate disabled OR ADR reference is valid AND opening-range width as a percentage of ADR reference is between the configured lower and upper range percentages, inclusive) AND breakout score(long) >= minimum score AND (strong-only filter disabled OR breakout score(long) >= strong threshold) AND entries since the opening-range session began are below the configured daily maximum AND no position is active AND no exit occurred on this chart bar AND ATR is available.
Short: on a chart bar close, opening range is formed AND bar is inside the configured trade window AND close crosses below opening-range low (close < opening-range low AND prior chart-bar close >= opening-range low) AND (break-buffer filter disabled OR opening-range low minus close >= break-buffer ATR multiple times ATR(ATR lookback)) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar, calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars)) AND (ADR gate disabled OR ADR reference is valid AND opening-range width as a percentage of ADR reference is between the configured lower and upper range percentages, inclusive) AND breakout score(short) >= minimum score AND (strong-only filter disabled OR breakout score(short) >= strong threshold) AND entries since the opening-range session began are below the configured daily maximum AND no position is active AND no exit occurred on this chart bar AND ATR is available.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= protective stop
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= third target AND low > protective stop
Exit: long, after the entry bar, trade window has just ended AND low > protective stop AND high < third target
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= protective stop
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= third target AND high < protective stop
Exit: short, after the entry bar, trade window has just ended AND high < protective stop AND low > third target
