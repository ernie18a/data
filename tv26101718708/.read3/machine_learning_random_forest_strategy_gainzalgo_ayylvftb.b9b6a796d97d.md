Calculation: Bullish probability = mean bullish-class leaf probability across classifier stumps trained on feature rows labeled whether the next chart-bar close exceeds the current close; each stump uses one randomly selected feature and a threshold chosen for greatest Gini-impurity reduction among sampled training values; features are the selected RSI, MFI, Stochastic, or scaled close Z-score anchor, scaled correlation(close, bar index, trend lookback), and scaled momentum(close, momentum lookback)/ATR(momentum lookback)
Calculation: Effective threshold = base probability threshold + (adaptive mode enabled and mean stored simulated-trade percentage return < 0 ? min(abs(mean return)/loss-scale parameter, maximum threshold boost) : 0)
Long: Bullish probability crosses above the effective threshold AND at least the signal-cooldown lookback bars have elapsed since the previous long signal AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback) >= ADX threshold)
Short: 1 - bullish probability crosses above the effective threshold AND at least the signal-cooldown lookback bars have elapsed since the previous short signal AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(ADX lookback) >= ADX threshold)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target ATR multiple * ATR(TP/SL lookback, entry bar) OR low <= entry price - stop ATR multiple * ATR(TP/SL lookback, entry bar) OR chart bars since entry >= trade-lifetime lookback
Exit: short, low <= entry price - target ATR multiple * ATR(TP/SL lookback, entry bar) OR high >= entry price + stop ATR multiple * ATR(TP/SL lookback, entry bar) OR chart bars since entry >= trade-lifetime lookback
