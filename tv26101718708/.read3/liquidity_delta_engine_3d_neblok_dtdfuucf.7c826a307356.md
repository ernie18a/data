Calculation: bar delta = on each chart bar, sum T1 intrabar volume as positive when intrabar close > open and negative when close < open, splitting volume equally between positive and negative when close = open; if no T1 intrabars are available, use volume * (2 * (close - low) / (high - low) - 1), with the fraction set to 0.5 when high = low. Signal conditions run on confirmed chart bars and use completed T1 intrabars within each chart bar.
Calculation: cumulative delta = cumulative sum of bar delta.
Calculation: active liquidity zones = on each chart bar, first test rejections against zones active at the start of the bar; then deactivate demand zones when the configured zone-break price is below their bottom and supply zones when it is above their top; then create zones from confirmed chart-bar PivotLow(low, zone pivot strength, zone pivot strength) or PivotHigh(high, zone pivot strength, zone pivot strength) when volume at the pivot is at least SMA(volume, average lookback) at the pivot times the minimum pivot-volume multiple. For a pivot low, set the zone bottom to the pivot low and its top to the pivot low plus the body-to-low distance clamped between ATR(ATR lookback) times the minimum and maximum zone-height multiples; for a pivot high, set the top to the pivot high and its bottom to the pivot high minus the body-to-high distance clamped by the same bounds. Use the confirmation bar's ATR and the pivot bar's open and close for these calculations. Do not create a zone that overlaps an active zone of the same side. Retain broken zones only when configured; after creation, if the zone count exceeds the maximum, remove the oldest inactive zone if one exists, otherwise the oldest zone. Newly created zones are available starting on the next chart bar.
Long: absorption enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND bar delta < 0 AND -bar delta >= SMA(abs(bar delta), average lookback) * ABS delta multiple AND volume >= SMA(volume, average lookback) * ABS volume multiple AND close >= (high + low) / 2 AND low <= Lowest(low, extreme lookback) AND (no prior bullish absorption signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Short: absorption enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND bar delta > 0 AND bar delta >= SMA(abs(bar delta), average lookback) * ABS delta multiple AND volume >= SMA(volume, average lookback) * ABS volume multiple AND close <= (high + low) / 2 AND high >= Highest(high, extreme lookback) AND (no prior bearish absorption signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Long: exhaustion enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND low <= Lowest(low, extreme lookback) AND abs(bar delta) <= SMA(abs(bar delta), average lookback) * exhaustion delta multiple AND close >= (high + low) / 2 AND the sum of bar delta over the prior delta-sum lookback chart bars < 0 AND (no prior bullish exhaustion signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Short: exhaustion enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND high >= Highest(high, extreme lookback) AND abs(bar delta) <= SMA(abs(bar delta), average lookback) * exhaustion delta multiple AND close <= (high + low) / 2 AND the sum of bar delta over the prior delta-sum lookback chart bars > 0 AND (no prior bearish exhaustion signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Long: divergence enabled AND on a confirmed chart bar confirming a PivotLow(low, divergence pivot length, divergence pivot length), the pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low, cumulative delta at this pivot is above cumulative delta at the preceding pivot, the pivot bars are no more than the maximum pivot-gap bars apart, and (no prior bullish divergence signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Short: divergence enabled AND on a confirmed chart bar confirming a PivotHigh(high, divergence pivot length, divergence pivot length), the pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high, cumulative delta at this pivot is below cumulative delta at the preceding pivot, the pivot bars are no more than the maximum pivot-gap bars apart, and (no prior bearish divergence signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Long: zone rejection enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND bar delta > 0 AND, for any active demand zone, low is at least its bottom and at most its top AND close > its top AND (no prior bullish zone-rejection signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
Short: zone rejection enabled AND confirmed chart bar AND bar delta < 0 AND, for any active supply zone, high is at least its bottom and at most its top AND close < its bottom AND (no prior bearish zone-rejection signal OR more than the signal cooldown chart bars have elapsed since it).
