Calculation: Rolling volume profile = distribute each bar’s volume evenly across the equal price bins touched by its low-to-high range over the profile lookback; the bins span the lowest low to highest high, POC is the highest-volume bin, and VAH and VAL are the profile’s value-area boundaries.
Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) if available, else TrueRange if available, else close × ATR fallback fraction, ATR floor).
Calculation: Acceptance probability(direction, edge) = sigmoid of a logistic regression over seven features: edge-bin volume / maximum bin volume; clipped tanh((close − close[momentum lag]) / safe ATR × direction); clipped(volume / SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) − 1); clipped((close − POC) / safe ATR × direction); clipped tanh((EMA(close, shorter lookback) − EMA(close, longer lookback)) / safe ATR × direction); clipped(safe ATR / SMA(safe ATR, volatility-average lookback) − 1); and clipped((VAH − VAL) / safe ATR). Use the coded feature-specific clipping bounds and logistic-input bound. Initialize all weights at zero; on each confirmed edge break, store its features and update its probability on resolution with online logistic gradient descent at the learning-rate parameter and L2 parameter. Resolve on confirmed chart bars: an upward break is accepted at close ≥ edge + acceptance-distance parameter × safe ATR and rejected at close ≤ edge − rejection-distance parameter × safe ATR; reverse these comparisons for a downward break. The first condition reached labels the event; discard it if neither is reached within the resolution-horizon parameter.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = threshold-base parameter + selected-arm index × threshold-step parameter. Select an arm by UCB within the current volatility regime: rank safe ATR over the regime lookback and assign one of three regimes using the two regime cutoffs; choose the arm with the greatest mean reward + exploration parameter × sqrt(log(total regime pulls + 1) / arm pulls), assigning untried arms a score above tried arms and breaking ties toward the lower index. On event resolution, update the stored regime-arm mean reward using a running average: reward 1 for a correct stored breakout or rejection call, negative wrong-call penalty for an incorrect call, and the no-call reward otherwise; increment its pull count.
Long: on a confirmed chart bar with bar index greater than the warmup parameter, close crosses above VAH and Acceptance probability(upward, VAH) ≥ Adaptive threshold, OR close crosses below VAL and Acceptance probability(downward, VAL) ≤ 1 − Adaptive threshold.
Short: on a confirmed chart bar with bar index greater than the warmup parameter, close crosses below VAL and Acceptance probability(downward, VAL) ≥ Adaptive threshold, OR close crosses above VAH and Acceptance probability(upward, VAH) ≤ 1 − Adaptive threshold.
