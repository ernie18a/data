Calculation: Double-top setup = On a newly confirmed PivotHigh(high, LeftPivotBars, RightPivotBars), scan up to PivotMemory earlier confirmed pivot highs newest first. Select the first pair whose span is between MinPeakSpan and MaxPeakSpan, peak difference is at most PeakTolerance × ATR(ATRLength), no intervening high exceeds the higher peak, and the higher peak minus the lowest intervening low is at least MinHeightATR × formation-bar ATR. The lowest intervening low is the neckline. Stop scanning if the span exceeds MaxPeakSpan or an intervening high exceeds the higher peak.
Calculation: Double-bottom setup = On a newly confirmed PivotLow(low, LeftPivotBars, RightPivotBars), scan up to PivotMemory earlier confirmed pivot lows newest first. Select the first pair whose span is between MinPeakSpan and MaxPeakSpan, low difference is at most PeakTolerance × ATR(ATRLength), no intervening low is below the lower peak, and the highest intervening high minus the lower peak is at least MinHeightATR × formation-bar ATR. The highest intervening high is the neckline. Stop scanning if the span exceeds MaxPeakSpan or an intervening low is below the lower peak.
Calculation: Pattern score = round(PeakMatchWeight × max(0, 1 − abs(P2 − P1)/(PeakTolerance × formation-bar ATR)) + (directional RSI divergence ? RSIDivergenceWeight : 0) + (both pivot-centered SUM(volume, PivotVolumeBars) values are positive ? (second sum < first sum ? VolumeDivergenceWeight : 0) : half of VolumeDivergenceWeight) + HeightWeight × min(1, PatternHeight/(FullHeightATRScale × formation-bar ATR)) + SymmetryWeight × min(L1,L2)/max(max(L1,L2), one bar) + PriorTrendWeight × min(1, PriorTrendMove/max(PatternHeight, instrument minimum price increment))). Directional RSI divergence is RSI(close, RSILength) at P2 below P1 for tops or above P1 for bottoms. PriorTrendMove is max(0, P1 minus the lowest low in PriorTrendLookback bars before P1) for tops, or max(0, the highest high in those bars minus P1) for bottoms. L1 and L2 are the bar distances from P1 to the neckline and from the neckline to P2.
Calculation: Initial stop = (long ? min(P1,P2) : max(P1,P2)) − DirectionSign × StopBuffer × formation-bar ATR, where DirectionSign is +1 for long and −1 for short.
Calculation: Target 1 = neckline + DirectionSign × PatternHeight × Target1Multiplier, where DirectionSign is +1 for long and −1 for short.
Calculation: Target 2 = neckline + DirectionSign × PatternHeight × Target2Multiplier, where DirectionSign is +1 for long and −1 for short.
Calculation: Active stop = entry price if breakeven tracking is enabled and Target 1 was previously hit; otherwise Initial stop.
Long: Double-bottom detection and long-signal toggles enabled AND a Double-bottom setup has Pattern score ≥ MinimumSignalScore, has not been invalidated by a low below Initial stop, is no older than BreakExpiry + one bar, and on its first post-formation break evaluation the bar closes above the neckline with low ≥ Initial stop AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, TrendEMALength)) AND (volume surge filter disabled OR SMA(volume, VolumeAverageLength) ≤ 0 OR volume ≥ SMA(volume, VolumeAverageLength) × BreakVolumeMultiplier).
Short: Double-top detection and short-signal toggles enabled AND a Double-top setup has Pattern score ≥ MinimumSignalScore, has not been invalidated by a high above Initial stop, is no older than BreakExpiry + one bar, and on its first post-formation break evaluation the bar closes below the neckline with high ≤ Initial stop AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, TrendEMALength)) AND (volume surge filter disabled OR SMA(volume, VolumeAverageLength) ≤ 0 OR volume ≥ SMA(volume, VolumeAverageLength) × BreakVolumeMultiplier).
Exit: Long, low ≤ Active stop OR (high ≥ Target 1 AND Target 1 was not previously hit) OR high ≥ Target 2
Exit: Short, high ≥ Active stop OR (low ≤ Target 1 AND Target 1 was not previously hit) OR low ≤ Target 2
