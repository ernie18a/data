Calculation: Resistance zone = [high at confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback), max(open, close) at that pivot]; if its height is below minimum-zone-height parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot, extend its bottom down to that height; cap its height at maximum-zone-height parameter × ATR at the pivot.
Calculation: Support zone = [min(open, close) at confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback), low at that pivot]; if its height is below minimum-zone-height parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot, extend its top up to that height; cap its height at maximum-zone-height parameter × ATR at the pivot.
Calculation: Merge a new zone into the first active zone that overlaps it or is within merge-distance parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) at the pivot; use the union, capped at maximum-zone-height parameter × ATR, anchored at the highest high for resistance or lowest low for support; accept only if the clamped zone still contains the new pivot wick, and assign it the new zone's side.
Calculation: A broken zone stays active with its side reversed only when the zone-break-action parameter selects reversal; otherwise it becomes inactive.
Long: An active resistance zone breaks upward when close > zone top on a confirmed bar or high > zone top on the current bar, as selected by the break-confirmation parameter.
Short: An active support zone breaks downward when close < zone bottom on a confirmed bar or low < zone bottom on the current bar, as selected by the break-confirmation parameter.
