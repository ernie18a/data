Calculation: Price Action Index = ((SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, stochastic lookback), smoothing) - 50) / 50) × Stochastic(D, D, D, stochastic lookback) when source is close; otherwise replace the first stochastic with Stochastic(source, source, source, stochastic lookback). D is selected from Standard Deviation(source, dispersion lookback), Variance(source, dispersion lookback), Standard Deviation(source, dispersion lookback) / SMA(source, dispersion lookback), -SMA(source, dispersion lookback) / Standard Deviation(source, dispersion lookback), -SMA(source, dispersion lookback)^2 / Standard Deviation(source, dispersion lookback)^2, Variance(source, dispersion lookback)^2 / SMA(source, dispersion lookback), Standard Deviation(source, dispersion lookback)^2 / SMA(source, dispersion lookback)^2, or Highest(high, dispersion lookback) - Lowest(low, dispersion lookback). Calculate on chart bars at the chart timeframe when no other timeframe is selected; otherwise calculate on T1 bars and sample on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar, or from the current T1 bar when repainting is enabled (still-forming in realtime).

Short: Price Action Index < -straddle-area parameter
