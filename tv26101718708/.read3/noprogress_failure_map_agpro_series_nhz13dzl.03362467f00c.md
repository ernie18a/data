Calculation: Safe ATR = max(nz(ATR(ATR length), true range), minimum tick size)
Calculation: Candle body ATR = abs(close - open) / Safe ATR
Calculation: Trend slope = EMA(close, trend length) - EMA(close, trend length)[slope lookback]
Calculation: Effective minimum progress ATR = min(minimum progress ATR, progress accepted ATR)
Calculation: Effective accepted progress ATR = max(progress accepted ATR, effective minimum progress ATR + progress ATR separation)
Calculation: Effective progress score threshold = max(progress accepted score, min(failure review score, progress accepted score - score separation) + score separation)
Calculation: Tracking limit bars = max(maximum tracking bars, effective evaluation bars + tracking margin)
Calculation: Active progress window = starts on a long or short trigger and ends on a confirmed risk shelf crossing, accepted progress, or confirmed elapsed bars > tracking limit bars
Calculation: Risk shelf = for a long setup, lowest(low[1], trigger lookback) at trigger - Safe ATR at trigger * risk shelf buffer ATR; for a short setup, highest(high[1], trigger lookback) at trigger + Safe ATR at trigger * risk shelf buffer ATR
Calculation: Favorable travel ATR = for a long setup, max(highest high since trigger - trigger close, 0) / trigger Safe ATR; for a short setup, max(trigger close - lowest low since trigger, 0) / trigger Safe ATR
Calculation: Close progress ATR = for a long setup, max(close - trigger close, 0) / trigger Safe ATR; for a short setup, max(trigger close - close, 0) / trigger Safe ATR
Calculation: Progress score = travel weight * clamp(Favorable travel ATR / Effective accepted progress ATR * 100, 0, 100) + efficiency weight * (if Favorable travel ATR > 0 then clamp(Close progress ATR / Favorable travel ATR * 100, 0, 100) else 0) + volume weight * clamp((volume readiness - minimum volume ratio * weak-volume multiplier) / (minimum volume ratio * strong-volume multiplier - minimum volume ratio * weak-volume multiplier) * 100, 0, 100) + time weight * clamp((tracking limit bars - elapsed bars) / (tracking limit bars - effective evaluation bars) * 100, 0, 100) + reclaim weight * (if trigger side was lost then if reclaimed then reclaimed score else lost score else held score), where volume readiness is 1 if volume is missing or its SMA(nz(volume, 0), volume baseline length) <= 0, otherwise nz(volume, 0) / SMA(nz(volume, 0), volume baseline length)
Long: no active progress window and (no prior closed window or bars since its terminal bar >= effective trigger cooldown) and (evaluation side selects long or (evaluation side is Auto and close >= EMA(close, trend length) and Trend slope >= 0)) and (trigger model is Range Break and close > highest(high[1], trigger lookback) and close[1] <= highest(high[1], trigger lookback) or trigger model is Impulse Close and close > open and close > high[1] and Candle body ATR >= impulse body ATR and close >= EMA(close, trend length) or trigger model is Trend Reclaim and close > EMA(close, trend length) and close[1] <= EMA(close, trend length)[1]) and (confirmation mode is live updating or bar is confirmed)
Short: no active progress window and (no prior closed window or bars since its terminal bar >= effective trigger cooldown) and (evaluation side selects short or (evaluation side is Auto and (close < EMA(close, trend length) or Trend slope < 0))) and (trigger model is Range Break and close < lowest(low[1], trigger lookback) and close[1] >= lowest(low[1], trigger lookback) or trigger model is Impulse Close and close < open and close < low[1] and Candle body ATR >= impulse body ATR and close <= EMA(close, trend length) or trigger model is Trend Reclaim and close < EMA(close, trend length) and close[1] >= EMA(close, trend length)[1]) and (confirmation mode is live updating or bar is confirmed)
Long: an active long setup has previously closed below its trigger price, has not already reclaimed it, and the current confirmed close is >= its trigger price, before any earlier failed or accepted-progress event
Short: an active short setup has previously closed above its trigger price, has not already reclaimed it, and the current confirmed close is <= its trigger price, before any earlier failed or accepted-progress event
Long: an active long setup has not previously reached accepted progress, current low > its risk shelf, Favorable travel ATR >= Effective accepted progress ATR, Progress score >= Effective progress score threshold, and the bar is confirmed
Short: an active short setup has not previously reached accepted progress, current high < its risk shelf, Favorable travel ATR >= Effective accepted progress ATR, Progress score >= Effective progress score threshold, and the bar is confirmed
Exit: long, an active long setup and a confirmed bar with low <= its risk shelf
Exit: short, an active short setup and a confirmed bar with high >= its risk shelf
