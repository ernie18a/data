Calculation: Previous T1 high = high of the previous completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Previous T1 low = low of the previous completed T1 bar, sampled on chart bars
Calculation: Latest confirmed major pivot high/low = most recent confirmed chart-bar PivotHigh(high, major pivot lookback, major pivot lookback) / PivotLow(low, major pivot lookback, major pivot lookback)
Calculation: Latest confirmed minor pivot high/low = most recent confirmed chart-bar PivotHigh(high, minor pivot lookback, minor pivot lookback) / PivotLow(low, minor pivot lookback, minor pivot lookback), with major pivot lookback longer than minor pivot lookback
Calculation: Bullish sweep(level) = low < level AND close > level AND close > open AND (level - low) / max(high - low, denominator floor) >= wick ratio
Calculation: Bearish sweep(level) = high > level AND close < level AND close < open AND (high - level) / max(high - low, denominator floor) >= wick ratio
Calculation: Any bullish liquidity sweep = Bullish sweep(previous T1 low) OR Bullish sweep(latest confirmed major pivot low) OR Bullish sweep(latest confirmed minor pivot low)
Calculation: Any bearish liquidity sweep = Bearish sweep(previous T1 high) OR Bearish sweep(latest confirmed major pivot high) OR Bearish sweep(latest confirmed minor pivot high)
Calculation: Bullish MTF count = count of T1–T5 where close > EMA(close, shorter MTF lookback) > EMA(close, longer MTF lookback); bearish MTF count = count where close < EMA(close, shorter MTF lookback) < EMA(close, longer MTF lookback). Evaluate each EMA and close on its T timeframe bars, sampled on chart bars with lookahead off: the still-forming T bar in real time and the last completed available T bar historically.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND close - open > ATR(displacement ATR lookback) * displacement multiplier
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND open - close > ATR(displacement ATR lookback) * displacement multiplier
Calculation: Bullish core setup = (session gate disabled OR current chart bar is in either configured session in Asia/Dubai) AND Linear Regression Slope(close, regression lookback) > 0 AND Chandelier Exit direction(Chandelier lookback, ATR multiplier) is bullish AND (Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) <= chop threshold OR bullish displacement) AND (any bullish liquidity sweep OR (bullish MTF count >= MTF alignment threshold AND Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) <= chop threshold AND bullish displacement AND (prior chart-bar close < prior chart-bar open OR low <= prior chart-bar low)))
Calculation: Bearish core setup = (session gate disabled OR current chart bar is in either configured session in Asia/Dubai) AND Linear Regression Slope(close, regression lookback) < 0 AND Chandelier Exit direction(Chandelier lookback, ATR multiplier) is bearish AND (Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) <= chop threshold OR bearish displacement) AND (any bearish liquidity sweep OR (bearish MTF count >= MTF alignment threshold AND Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) <= chop threshold AND bearish displacement AND (prior chart-bar close > prior chart-bar open OR high >= prior chart-bar high)))
Calculation: Bullish weak setup = any bullish liquidity sweep AND (Linear Regression Slope(close, regression lookback) > 0 OR Chandelier Exit direction(Chandelier lookback, ATR multiplier) is bullish) AND Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) <= chop threshold
Calculation: Bearish weak setup = any bearish liquidity sweep AND (Linear Regression Slope(close, regression lookback) < 0 OR Chandelier Exit direction(Chandelier lookback, ATR multiplier) is bearish) AND Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) <= chop threshold
Calculation: Bullish chop setup = any bullish liquidity sweep AND Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) > chop threshold AND NOT bullish displacement
Calculation: Bearish chop setup = any bearish liquidity sweep AND Choppiness Index(choppiness lookback) > chop threshold AND NOT bearish displacement
Calculation: Long spacing gate = initially open; after a gated bullish core, weak, or chop setup, it is closed until more than the signal spacing has elapsed; any gated bearish core, weak, or chop setup resets it open
Calculation: Short spacing gate = initially open; after a gated bearish core, weak, or chop setup, it is closed until more than the signal spacing has elapsed; any gated bullish core, weak, or chop setup resets it open
Long: (long spacing gate AND (bullish core setup OR bullish weak setup)) OR (barssince(low <= previous T1 low when available OR low <= latest confirmed major pivot low when available) <= macro recency lookback AND close > open AND Linear Regression Slope(close, regression lookback) > 0 AND (Chandelier Exit direction(Chandelier lookback, ATR multiplier) is bullish OR bullish displacement) AND current bar index - last bullish swing signal bar > swing spacing)
Short: (short spacing gate AND (bearish core setup OR bearish weak setup)) OR (barssince(high >= previous T1 high when available OR high >= latest confirmed major pivot high when available) <= macro recency lookback AND close < open AND Linear Regression Slope(close, regression lookback) < 0 AND (Chandelier Exit direction(Chandelier lookback, ATR multiplier) is bearish OR bearish displacement) AND current bar index - last bearish swing signal bar > swing spacing)
