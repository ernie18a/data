Calculation: Chart Supertrend = Supertrend(factor, ATR lookback) on chart bars
Calculation: T1 Supertrend = Supertrend(same factor, same ATR lookback, current T1 bar including its still-forming value in realtime, sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: Trend average = SMA(close, lookback)
Calculation: Selected low-bar open and close = open and close of the most recent lowest-low bar among the preceding bars 1 through N, if its low is below the current low; otherwise (0, 0), where N is bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated an uptrend
Calculation: Selected high-bar open and close = open and close of the most recent highest-high bar among the preceding bars 1 through N, if its high is above the current high; otherwise (0, 0), where N is bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated a downtrend
Long: bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated a downtrend > minimum age AND bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated a downtrend < maximum age AND T1 Supertrend indicates an uptrend AND close > Trend average AND Selected low-bar close < Selected low-bar open AND close > Selected low-bar open AND close > open
Short: bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated an uptrend > minimum age AND bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated an uptrend < maximum age AND T1 Supertrend indicates a downtrend AND close < Trend average AND Selected high-bar close > Selected high-bar open AND close < Selected high-bar open AND close < open
Long: bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated a downtrend > minimum age AND bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated a downtrend < maximum age AND Chart Supertrend indicates an uptrend AND T1 Supertrend indicates an uptrend AND close > Trend average AND Selected low-bar close < Selected low-bar open AND close > Selected low-bar open AND close > open AND outside the configured session window AND flat AND no long entry since the most recent transition to an uptrend, or initialization
Short: bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated an uptrend > minimum age AND bars since Chart Supertrend last indicated an uptrend < maximum age AND Chart Supertrend indicates a downtrend AND T1 Supertrend indicates a downtrend AND close < Trend average AND Selected high-bar close > Selected high-bar open AND close < Selected high-bar open AND close < open AND outside the configured session window AND flat AND no short entry since the most recent transition to a downtrend, or initialization
Exit: Long, low <= Chart Supertrend line captured at entry OR high >= entry price + (entry price - Chart Supertrend line captured at entry) * risk-reward ratio
Exit: Short, high >= Chart Supertrend line captured at entry OR low <= entry price - (Chart Supertrend line captured at entry - entry price) * risk-reward ratio
