Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = when low > high[2], with lower bound high[2], upper bound low, and age counted in chart bars from creation.
Calculation: Bearish FVG zone = when high < low[2], with lower bound high, upper bound low[2], and age counted in chart bars from creation.
Calculation: Bar range = max(high - low, tick size).
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) when that SMA is positive, otherwise 0.
Calculation: ATR expansion = bar range / max(ATR(ATR lookback), tick size).
Calculation: Delta pressure = EMA((((close - low) - (high - close)) / bar range) * volume / max(SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), 1) * 100, delta-smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Bullish wick rejection = price overlaps a bullish zone, close > zone midpoint, close > open, and (min(open, close) - low) / bar range > rejection-wick fraction.
Calculation: Bearish wick rejection = price overlaps a bearish zone, close < zone midpoint, close < open, and (high - max(open, close)) / bar range > rejection-wick fraction.
Calculation: Bullish touch reprice = price overlaps a bullish zone, close > its lower bound, close > open, and (close > zone midpoint OR low <= zone midpoint).
Calculation: Bearish touch reprice = price overlaps a bearish zone, close < its upper bound, close < open, and (close < zone midpoint OR high >= zone midpoint).
Calculation: Bullish VWAP score = when VWAP scoring is disabled, neutral VWAP score; otherwise, when close >= VWAP(typical price), above-VWAP score; otherwise, when low <= VWAP(typical price) - STDEV(close - VWAP(typical price), VWAP-deviation lookback) * VWAP-deviation multiplier, outer-band score; otherwise, below-VWAP score.
Calculation: Bearish VWAP score = when VWAP scoring is disabled, neutral VWAP score; otherwise, when close <= VWAP(typical price), above-VWAP score; otherwise, when high >= VWAP(typical price) + STDEV(close - VWAP(typical price), VWAP-deviation lookback) * VWAP-deviation multiplier, outer-band score; otherwise, below-VWAP score.
Calculation: Zone freshness = clamp(1 - zone age / maximum zone age, 0, 1).
Calculation: Zone-size score = clamp((zone upper bound - zone lower bound) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), tick size), minimum zone-size ratio, maximum zone-size ratio) / maximum zone-size ratio.
Calculation: Displacement score = clamp((ATR expansion - minimum ATR expansion) / displacement-score scale, 0, 1).
Calculation: Volume score = clamp((relative volume - 1) / volume-score scale, 0, 1).
Calculation: Bullish score = zone freshness * freshness weight + zone-size score * zone-size weight + displacement score * displacement weight + volume score * volume weight + bullish VWAP score * VWAP weight + (delta pressure > delta cutoff AND delta pressure > its prior-bar value ? delta weight : 0) + (bullish wick rejection ? wick-rejection weight : bullish touch reprice ? touch-reprice weight : 0).
Calculation: Bearish score = zone freshness * freshness weight + zone-size score * zone-size weight + displacement score * displacement weight + volume score * volume weight + bearish VWAP score * VWAP weight + (delta pressure < -delta cutoff AND delta pressure < its prior-bar value ? delta weight : 0) + (bearish wick rejection ? wick-rejection weight : bearish touch reprice ? touch-reprice weight : 0).
Calculation: Bullish-zone failure candidate = previously unfailed bullish zone and close < its lower bound - ATR(ATR lookback) * failure-buffer ATR multiplier, zone age > minimum failure age, and relative volume >= minimum relative volume; its score is the bullish score.
Calculation: Bearish-zone failure candidate = previously unfailed bearish zone and close > its upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) * failure-buffer ATR multiplier, zone age > minimum failure age, and relative volume >= minimum relative volume; its score is the bearish score.
Calculation: Select events by scanning zones in creation order with winning score initialized to zero; replace the winner only when a candidate score is strictly greater than the current winning score, keeping the earlier event on ties. A failure candidate sets the winning score to max(its score, score threshold) after selection. Reprice candidates require score >= the score threshold selected by signal strictness.
Long: A selected bullish reprice candidate has a bullish zone that overlaps the bar (high >= its lower bound AND low <= its upper bound), is not failed and does not fail on this bar (close >= its lower bound - ATR(ATR lookback) * failure-buffer ATR multiplier), has bullish wick rejection OR bullish touch reprice, and has bullish score >= score threshold; OR a bearish-zone failure candidate is selected; AND there is no prior signal OR current bar index - prior signal bar > signal cooldown. Any selected long, short, or zone-failure event updates the prior signal bar.
Short: A selected bearish reprice candidate has a bearish zone that overlaps the bar (high >= its lower bound AND low <= its upper bound), is not failed and does not fail on this bar (close <= its upper bound + ATR(ATR lookback) * failure-buffer ATR multiplier), has bearish wick rejection OR bearish touch reprice, and has bearish score >= score threshold; OR a bullish-zone failure candidate is selected; AND there is no prior signal OR current bar index - prior signal bar > signal cooldown. Any selected long, short, or zone-failure event updates the prior signal bar.
