Calculation: ATR percentage (on scan timeframe T1 bars) = ATR(high, low, close, volatility lookback) / close * 100 when close > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume ratio (on T1 bars) = SMA(volume, shorter volume lookback) / SMA(volume, longer volume lookback) when the longer volume average > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: High distance percentage (on T1 bars) = (close / Highest(high, high lookback) - 1) * 100 when the highest high > 0, otherwise -100.
Calculation: Setup score (on T1 bars) = the sum of five values, each 1 when its condition is true and 0 otherwise: close > SMA(close, short trend lookback) AND SMA(close, short trend lookback) > SMA(close, medium trend lookback) AND SMA(close, medium trend lookback) > SMA(close, long trend lookback); RSI(close, momentum lookback) >= lower RSI bound AND RSI(close, momentum lookback) <= upper RSI bound; Volume ratio >= volume surge multiplier AND SMA(volume, longer volume lookback) > 0; ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percent AND ATR percentage <= maximum ATR percent; High distance percentage >= -maximum distance below high AND High distance percentage <= -minimum distance below high. The trend lookbacks satisfy short < medium < long; the volume lookbacks satisfy shorter < longer.
Long: On chart bars, setup score from T1 bars >= minimum qualifying score AND (trend filter disabled OR (market-trend close from T2 is available AND its SMA with the regime lookback on T2 is available AND market-trend close from T2 > that SMA)) AND (volatility filter disabled OR (market-volatility close from T2 is available AND market-volatility close from T2 < volatility threshold). Requested T1 and T2 values use lookahead off: the requested bar still forming in real time and the last completed requested bar historically.
Calculation: setup score for the scanned instrument at T1 = count of met conditions: close > SMA(close, short trend period) AND SMA(close, short trend period) > SMA(close, medium trend period) AND SMA(close, medium trend period) > SMA(close, long trend period); RSI(close, RSI period) >= RSI lower threshold AND RSI(close, RSI period) <= RSI upper threshold; SMA(volume, short volume period) / SMA(volume, long volume period) >= volume surge multiplier AND SMA(volume, long volume period) > 0; close > 0 AND ATR(ATR period) / close * 100 >= minimum ATR percent AND ATR(ATR period) / close * 100 <= maximum ATR percent; highest(high, high lookback) > 0 AND (1 - close / highest(high, high lookback)) * 100 >= minimum percent below high AND (1 - close / highest(high, high lookback)) * 100 <= maximum percent below high
Long: setup score >= minimum score AND (QQQ regime filter disabled OR QQQ close at T2 > SMA(QQQ close at T2, QQQ regime SMA period)) AND (VIX filter disabled OR VIX close at T2 < VIX threshold)
