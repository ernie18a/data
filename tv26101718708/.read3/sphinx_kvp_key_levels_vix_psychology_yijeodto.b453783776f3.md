Calculation: Active T1 swings = pivots with configured equal left/right strength on T1 bars, sampled on chart bars from the last completed T1 bar; in primary mode retain one active pivot per side, admitting a new pivot after the active pivot is touched and reanchoring the opposite side to the leg extreme, or in all-pivots mode retain the configured most recent unbroken pivots per side; remove on a wick through when wick mitigation is enabled, otherwise on a close beyond when broken-level mitigation is enabled.
Calculation: Range-anchor open/high/low = open at the configured anchor time and running high/low accumulated on chart bars until the next anchor.
Calculation: Range budget = positive manual historical-range override or the configured median, SMA, or EMA of high-low over its lookback on T2 bars using last completed T2 bars; select historical range, implied range, or the larger of both, falling back to historical range when implied range is unavailable; implied range = volatility-index percentage locked at the anchor multiplied by range-anchor open and divided by the square root of the annualization factor.
Long: Entry checklist enabled AND at least one automatic filter enabled (swing, range budget, or wall when live gamma walls exist) AND (swing filter disabled OR swing source enabled on a chart timeframe no greater than T1 AND an active T1 swing low is first touched or traded through (low <= swing low), then a confirmed chart-bar close is above it within the rejection window, with the setup remaining valid for the configured duration after rejection and cancelled if price trades below the lowest low from sweep through rejection) AND (range filter disabled OR sweep-tracking low extreme, if present, otherwise current low, minus (range-anchor high - Range budget) <= configured room limit in points or as a percentage of Range budget) AND (wall filter disabled OR no live gamma wall exists OR a live option gamma wall is within configured tolerance of the sweep-tracking low extreme, if present, otherwise current low, and its magnitude is at least the configured ratio of the largest live wall of its type; use any wall or puts as configured for longs).
Short: Entry checklist enabled AND at least one automatic filter enabled (swing, range budget, or wall when live gamma walls exist) AND (swing filter disabled OR swing source enabled on a chart timeframe no greater than T1 AND an active T1 swing high is first touched or traded through (high >= swing high), then a confirmed chart-bar close is below it within the rejection window, with the setup remaining valid for the configured duration after rejection and cancelled if price trades above the highest high from sweep through rejection) AND (range filter disabled OR (range-anchor low + Range budget) minus the sweep-tracking high extreme, if present, otherwise current high, <= configured room limit in points or as a percentage of Range budget) AND (wall filter disabled OR no live gamma wall exists OR a live option gamma wall is within configured tolerance of the sweep-tracking high extreme, if present, otherwise current high, and its magnitude is at least the configured ratio of the largest live wall of its type; use any wall or calls as configured for shorts).
