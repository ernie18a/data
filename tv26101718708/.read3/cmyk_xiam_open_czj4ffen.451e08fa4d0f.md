Calculation: Centered RMI(close, momentum lookback, EMA lookback) = 0 when EMA(max(close[momentum lookback] - close, 0), EMA lookback) = 0; otherwise 50 - 100 / (1 + EMA(max(close - close[momentum lookback], 0), EMA lookback) / EMA(max(close[momentum lookback] - close, 0), EMA lookback))
Calculation: Short-term force = SMA(change(SMA(close, shared price and volume lookback), 1) / SMA(selected volume series, shared price and volume lookback)[1], shorter force lookback)
Calculation: Long-term force = SMA(change(SMA(close, shared price and volume lookback), 1) / SMA(selected volume series, shared price and volume lookback)[1], longer force lookback)
Calculation: Bull phase = active after crossover(short-term force, long-term force) with long-term force < 0; inactive after crossunder(short-term force, long-term force) with long-term force < 0 or crossunder(short-term force, long-term force) with long-term force > 0; otherwise retain the previous state; initially unset
Calculation: Bear phase = active after crossunder(short-term force, long-term force) with long-term force > 0; inactive after crossover(short-term force, long-term force) with long-term force > 0 or crossover(short-term force, long-term force) with long-term force < 0; otherwise retain the previous state; initially unset
Calculation: Bull exit sweep = SMA(1 when crossunder(short-term force, long-term force) and long-term force < 0, exit lookback; otherwise 0) > 0 and bull phase = 0
Calculation: Bear exit sweep = SMA(1 when crossover(short-term force, long-term force) and long-term force > 0, exit lookback; otherwise 0) > 0 and bear phase = 0
Long: (active RMI < active lower threshold and SMA(close, phase lookback) > close and close < the close on the most recent bar when slow RMI crossed above its lower threshold) or (active RMI < active lower threshold and slow RMI < slow lower threshold and bull phase is active), where active and slow RMI use respectively shorter and longer momentum and EMA lookbacks, with separate upper and lower thresholds
Short: (active RMI > active upper threshold and SMA(close, phase lookback) < close and close > the close on the most recent bar when slow RMI crossed below its upper threshold) or (active RMI > active upper threshold and slow RMI > slow upper threshold and bear phase is active), where active and slow RMI use respectively shorter and longer momentum and EMA lookbacks, with separate upper and lower thresholds
Exit: Short, bull phase = 0 and bull exit sweep was true two bars ago and false one bar ago
Exit: Long, bear phase = 0 and bear exit sweep was true two bars ago and false one bar ago
