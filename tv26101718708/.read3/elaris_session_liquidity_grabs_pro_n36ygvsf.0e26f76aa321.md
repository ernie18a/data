Calculation: SessionHigh(session) = maximum high from the first through latest bar in the configured session, retained after the session ends until the next session begins
Calculation: SessionLow(session) = minimum low from the first through latest bar in the configured session, retained after the session ends until the next session begins
Calculation: BodyPercent = when high > low, 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low); otherwise 0
Long: close > EMA(close, EMA lookback)
Short: close <= EMA(close, EMA lookback)
Long: There exists an enabled configured session such that the current bar is outside that session, SessionLow(session) is available, and either the configured sweep mode tests wicks and low < SessionLow(session) and close > SessionLow(session), or it tests closes and close < SessionLow(session); and abs(SessionLow(session) - low) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum sweep-distance multiple; and the close-back-inside requirement is disabled or close > SessionLow(session); and the volume filter is disabled or volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier; and the EMA trend filter is disabled or close > EMA(close, EMA lookback); and there is no prior long signal or the current bar index minus the prior long signal bar index is at least the cooldown bars; and the outside-session-only filter is disabled or the current bar is outside every configured session; and the strong reversal-close requirement is disabled or close > open and close - low >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum displacement multiple and BodyPercent >= minimum body percentage
Short: There exists an enabled configured session such that the current bar is outside that session, SessionHigh(session) is available, and either the configured sweep mode tests wicks and high > SessionHigh(session) and close < SessionHigh(session), or it tests closes and close > SessionHigh(session); and abs(high - SessionHigh(session)) >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum sweep-distance multiple; and the close-back-inside requirement is disabled or close < SessionHigh(session); and the volume filter is disabled or volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier; and the EMA trend filter is disabled or close < EMA(close, EMA lookback); and there is no prior short signal or the current bar index minus the prior short signal bar index is at least the cooldown bars; and the outside-session-only filter is disabled or the current bar is outside every configured session; and the strong reversal-close requirement is disabled or close < open and high - close >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum displacement multiple and BodyPercent >= minimum body percentage
