Calculation: Swing-high level = high[right swing lookback] when strictly greater than the highs of the left swing lookback bars before it and right swing lookback bars after it; confirm after the right swing lookback bars, retain at most the newest level-cap levels, and retire each on its first break test, including a test rejected by the volume filter.
Calculation: Swing-low level = low[right swing lookback] when strictly lower than the lows of the left swing lookback bars before it and right swing lookback bars after it; confirm after the right swing lookback bars, retain at most the newest level-cap levels, and retire each on its first break test, including a test rejected by the volume filter.
Calculation: Bull volume = on each chart bar, sum volume across its T1 sub-bars where close > open, including a still-forming T1 sub-bar when available.
Calculation: Bear volume = on each chart bar, sum volume across its T1 sub-bars where close < open, including a still-forming T1 sub-bar when available.
Long: At least one active swing-high level has current chart-bar high > level in Wick mode or current chart-bar close > level in Close mode AND (volume filter disabled OR bull dominance > threshold), where bull dominance = bull volume / (bull volume + bear volume), or 0.5 when total volume is zero.
Short: At least one active swing-low level has current chart-bar low < level in Wick mode or current chart-bar close < level in Close mode AND (volume filter disabled OR bear dominance > threshold), where bear dominance = 1 - bull dominance, with bull dominance as defined above, AND no active swing-high level satisfies the Long breakout and filter conditions on that chart bar.
