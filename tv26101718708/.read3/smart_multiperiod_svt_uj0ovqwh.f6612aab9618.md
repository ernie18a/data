Calculation: VWAP(source, T) = running sum of source × volume ÷ running sum of volume over chart bars, restarted at the start of each T bar still forming
Calculation: VolStdev(source, T) = square root of MAX(0, VWAP(source squared, T) − VWAP(source, T) squared)
Calculation: UpperBand(multiplier, T) = VWAP(source, T) + multiplier × VolStdev(source, T)
Calculation: LowerBand(multiplier, T) = VWAP(source, T) − multiplier × VolStdev(source, T)
Calculation: Mature(T) = the T bar containing the chart bar has closed by the current time OR the chart bar's datetime is at or past a threshold for T within that T bar
Long: Mature(T1) AND close > VWAP(source, T1)
Short: Mature(T1) AND close <= VWAP(source, T1)
Long: T2 enabled AND Mature(T2) AND close > VWAP(source, T2)
Short: T2 enabled AND Mature(T2) AND close <= VWAP(source, T2)
Long: Mature(T1) AND inner bands of T1 defined AND close > UpperBand(inner multiplier, T1)
Short: Mature(T1) AND inner bands of T1 defined AND close < LowerBand(inner multiplier, T1)
Long: T2 enabled AND Mature(T2) AND inner bands of T2 defined AND close > UpperBand(inner multiplier, T2)
Short: T2 enabled AND Mature(T2) AND inner bands of T2 defined AND close < LowerBand(inner multiplier, T2)
Long: outer bands of T1 defined AND close > UpperBand(outer multiplier, T1)
Short: outer bands of T1 not defined OR close <= UpperBand(outer multiplier, T1)
Long: inner bands of T1 defined AND close > UpperBand(inner multiplier, T1)
Short: inner bands of T1 not defined OR close <= UpperBand(inner multiplier, T1)
Long: close > VWAP(source, T1)
Short: close <= VWAP(source, T1)
Long: inner bands of T1 defined AND close > LowerBand(inner multiplier, T1)
Short: inner bands of T1 not defined OR close <= LowerBand(inner multiplier, T1)
Long: outer bands of T1 defined AND close > LowerBand(outer multiplier, T1)
Short: outer bands of T1 not defined OR close <= LowerBand(outer multiplier, T1)
Long: outer bands of T2 defined AND close > UpperBand(outer multiplier, T2)
Short: outer bands of T2 not defined OR close <= UpperBand(outer multiplier, T2)
Long: inner bands of T2 defined AND close > UpperBand(inner multiplier, T2)
Short: inner bands of T2 not defined OR close <= UpperBand(inner multiplier, T2)
Long: close > VWAP(source, T2)
Short: close <= VWAP(source, T2)
Long: inner bands of T2 defined AND close > LowerBand(inner multiplier, T2)
Short: inner bands of T2 not defined OR close <= LowerBand(inner multiplier, T2)
Long: outer bands of T2 defined AND close > LowerBand(outer multiplier, T2)
Short: outer bands of T2 not defined OR close <= LowerBand(outer multiplier, T2)
