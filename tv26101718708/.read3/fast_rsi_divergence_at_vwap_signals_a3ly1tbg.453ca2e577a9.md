Calculation: Bull reference = selected bull price source and RSI recorded when RSI enters oversold; refresh on each long signal and expire after the maximum divergence lookback
Calculation: Bear reference = selected bear price source and RSI recorded when RSI enters overbought; refresh on each short signal and expire after the maximum divergence lookback
Long: a bull reference exists within the maximum divergence lookback AND the selected bull price source (low in wick mode, close in close mode) is below its reference price AND ((reference price / current bull price source) - 1) × 100 is at least the minimum price extension AND current RSI minus reference RSI is at least the minimum RSI difference AND (RSI is at least its prior-bar value, if RSI-turn confirmation is enabled) AND (close is above open, if candle-direction confirmation is enabled) AND (if the VWAP filter is enabled, low touches or crosses below the lower session VWAP standard-deviation band in wick-touch mode, or close is at or below that band in close-outside mode) AND (the current bar is confirmed, if candle-close confirmation is enabled) AND (there was no prior long signal or bars since the prior long signal exceed the cooldown parameter)
Short: a bear reference exists within the maximum divergence lookback AND the selected bear price source (high in wick mode, close in close mode) is above its reference price AND ((current bear price source / reference price) - 1) × 100 is at least the minimum price extension AND reference RSI minus current RSI is at least the minimum RSI difference AND (RSI is at most its prior-bar value, if RSI-turn confirmation is enabled) AND (close is below open, if candle-direction confirmation is enabled) AND (if the VWAP filter is enabled, high touches or crosses above the upper session VWAP standard-deviation band in wick-touch mode, or close is at or above that band in close-outside mode) AND (the current bar is confirmed, if candle-close confirmation is enabled) AND (there was no prior short signal or bars since the prior short signal exceed the cooldown parameter)
