Calculation: Structure direction = bullish after a bullish structure break, bearish after a bearish structure break, and neutral until either; a bullish break occurs when close > the latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing confirmation length, swing confirmation length) and the prior chart-bar close <= that swing high; a bearish break occurs when close < the latest confirmed PivotLow(low, swing confirmation length, swing confirmation length) and the prior chart-bar close >= that swing low
Calculation: HTF trend = 1 when chart close > EMA(close, HTF EMA length, T1 mapped to chart bars with lookahead off) and that EMA > its value HTF slope comparison lookback T1 bars ago; -1 when both comparisons are reversed; otherwise 0; lookahead off uses the current T1 bar while it forms in real time and the last completed T1 bar on historical chart bars
Calculation: Liquidity pool is active until close > its price + ATR(ATR length) * touch buffer for a buy-side pool, close < its price - ATR(ATR length) * touch buffer for a sell-side pool, or its age exceeds pool expiry multiple times the freshness window; a confirmed swing high creates a buy-side pool and a confirmed swing low creates a sell-side pool
Calculation: Pool score = max(0, 1 - abs(pool price - close) / (max(ATR(ATR length), minimum tick) * maximum scored reach ATR)) * distance score weight + min(pool birth volume ratio / full volume score reference, 1) * volume score weight + max(0, 1 - pool age / freshness window) * freshness score weight + min(touch count, touch count cap) / touch count cap * touch score weight + (pool price on its side of close ? location score weight : 0) + (HTF trend agrees with pool side ? trend score weight : HTF trend is neutral ? neutral trend score : 0); pool birth volume ratio = volume at the confirmed swing bar / SMA(volume, volume baseline lookback) at that bar; touch count increases on each active chart bar where high >= buy-side pool price - ATR(ATR length) * touch buffer or low <= sell-side pool price + ATR(ATR length) * touch buffer
Calculation: Net liquidity pull = maximum score of active buy-side pools above close, or zero if none, minus maximum score of active sell-side pools below close, or zero if none
Calculation: Bullish FVG forms when low > high[FVG lookback]; its gap is from high[FVG lookback] to low and remains active until selected mitigation mode is satisfied: any touch when low <= gap top and high >= gap bottom, or close-through when close < gap bottom; only the most recent FVGs up to the retained FVG count are kept
Calculation: Bearish FVG forms when high < low[FVG lookback]; its gap is from high to low[FVG lookback] and remains active until selected mitigation mode is satisfied: any touch when high >= gap bottom and low <= gap top, or close-through when close > gap top; only the most recent FVGs up to the retained FVG count are kept
Long: a sell-side pool is active and unswept, and (sweep mode requires close back inside and low < pool price and close > pool price, or sweep mode is wick-only and low <= pool price)
Short: a buy-side pool is active and unswept, and (sweep mode requires close back inside and high > pool price and close < pool price, or sweep mode is wick-only and high >= pool price)
Long: structure direction is bullish OR HTF trend = 1 OR net liquidity pull >= dominance threshold OR a bullish FVG forms OR a bullish FVG is active
Short: structure direction is bearish OR HTF trend = -1 OR net liquidity pull <= -dominance threshold OR a bearish FVG forms OR a bearish FVG is active
