Calculation: Favourability score = the arithmetic mean of the defined per-axis scores: for each active axis, count same-direction historical pivot samples in the current equal-width bin and divide by the largest count in any bin, then multiply by the score scale; before counting, restrict samples to those in the current bins of every other active axis. Use confirmed ZigZag pivot highs from bullish SuperTrend runs and pivot lows from bearish runs, sample each axis at the pivot bar, and retain up to the history-limit parameter. Axes are PercentRank(volume, volume lookback), time of day scaled linearly from datetime milliseconds since UTC midnight to the score scale (intraday bars only), (close − Lowest(low, range lookback)) / max(Highest(high, range lookback) − Lowest(low, range lookback), minimum tick size) scaled to the score scale, and an optional custom source when its loaded-history and current values stay within the score scale. Use the bin-count parameter; average only scores with samples. Calculate on chart bars using the current bar's axis values and the active SuperTrend direction; use pivot-bar values for historical samples. Undefined when no axis score is defined.
Long: SuperTrend(high, low, close, ATR period, multiplier) changes direction from bearish to bullish.
Short: SuperTrend(high, low, close, ATR period, multiplier) changes direction from bullish to bearish.
Exit: long, SuperTrend is bullish AND Favourability score is defined AND (Favourability score >= full-match threshold OR crosses above upper exit-favourability threshold OR crosses below upper exit-favourability threshold OR (crosses above lower exit-favourability threshold AND Favourability score <= upper exit-favourability threshold) OR crosses below lower exit-favourability threshold)
Exit: short, SuperTrend is bearish AND Favourability score is defined AND (Favourability score >= full-match threshold OR crosses above upper exit-favourability threshold OR crosses below upper exit-favourability threshold OR (crosses above lower exit-favourability threshold AND Favourability score <= upper exit-favourability threshold) OR crosses below lower exit-favourability threshold)
