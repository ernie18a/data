Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open)
Calculation: Upper wick = high - max(close, open)
Calculation: Lower wick = min(close, open) - low
Calculation: ATR stop distance = ATR(ATR lookback) * stop-distance multiplier
Long: position is flat AND time(datetime, GMT) is within an enabled London, New York, or Asian interval, using separate start and end parameters for each session (the Asian interval may cross midnight) AND entries since the current bar's day began < daily trade limit AND at least minimum confirmations of these six conditions are true: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > long RSI floor AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long RSI ceiling AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1]; close > lower(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)) AND close[1] <= lower(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)); close > EMA(close, EMA lookback); close > middle(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)) OR (close > lower(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)) AND close < middle(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long middle-band RSI cutoff); (Lower wick > Candle body * long-wick/body ratio AND Upper wick < Candle body * short-wick/body ratio) OR (Candle body < (high - low) * doji body/range ratio AND close > open); low < low[1] AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] < bullish-divergence RSI cutoff
Short: position is flat AND time(datetime, GMT) is within an enabled London, New York, or Asian interval, using separate start and end parameters for each session (the Asian interval may cross midnight) AND entries since the current bar's day began < daily trade limit AND at least minimum confirmations of these six conditions are true: RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1]; close < upper(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)) AND close[1] >= upper(BollingerBands(close, band lookback, band width)); close < EMA(close, EMA lookback); (Upper wick > Candle body * long-wick/body ratio AND Lower wick < Candle body * short-wick/body ratio) OR (Candle body < (high - low) * doji body/range ratio AND close < open); high > high[1] AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] > short RSI threshold; RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] > overbought threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < overbought threshold
Exit: Long, low <= entry-signal bar close - ATR stop distance on the entry-signal bar OR high >= entry-signal bar close + ATR stop distance on the entry-signal bar * reward-ratio parameter
Exit: Short, high >= entry-signal bar close + ATR stop distance on the entry-signal bar OR low <= entry-signal bar close - ATR stop distance on the entry-signal bar * reward-ratio parameter
