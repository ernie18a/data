Calculation: SSL bands(lookback, T) = HMA(close, lookback) ± EMA(True Range, lookback) × multiplier, calculated on T bars using the still-forming bar and carried to chart bars
Long: chart close > upper SSL band(first SSL lookback, T1)
Short: chart close < lower SSL band(first SSL lookback, T1)
Long: chart close > upper SSL band(second SSL lookback, T2)
Short: chart close < lower SSL band(second SSL lookback, T2)
