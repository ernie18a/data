Calculation: Opening-range high/low = maximum high/minimum low of chart bars during the configured opening window from regular-session start, fixed when that window ends.
Calculation: Afternoon-range high/low = maximum high/minimum low of chart bars during the configured afternoon window, fixed when that window ends.
Calculation: Premarket high/low = maximum high/minimum low of chart bars during the configured premarket window.
Calculation: Volume surge = volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) * volume-multiple parameter.
Calculation: Bullish RSI confirmation = RSI(close, confluence RSI lookback) < overbought parameter AND RSI(close, confluence RSI lookback) > its prior value.
Calculation: Bearish RSI confirmation = RSI(close, confluence RSI lookback) > oversold parameter AND RSI(close, confluence RSI lookback) <= its prior value.
Calculation: ADX confirmation = ADX(DMI lookback) >= ADX threshold.
Calculation: VWAP touch distance = ATR(ATR lookback) * VWAP-proximity parameter.
Calculation: Equal-high/low pools = two successive confirmed pivot highs/lows within the configured tolerance create a pool at the higher high/lower low; retain each pool until a close above/below it, respectively. Tolerance is ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR-multiple parameter in ATR mode, otherwise close * percentage parameter / 100.
Calculation: Weekly sweep high/low = on Monday in the configured timezone, the previous completed T2 bar's high/low; otherwise, the current T2 week's high/low. Chart bars take these values from T2 without forward look-ahead; the live value may use the forming T2 bar, while historical values update at the T2 bar's close.

A close below the range high cancels a pending setup; a fully eligible confirmation suppressed as grade C also clears it.

A close above the range low cancels a pending setup; a fully eligible confirmation suppressed as grade C also clears it.

A close above the range high resets that one-signal restriction.

A close below the range low resets that one-signal restriction.
