Calculation: Oscillator = RSI(source, length) when oscillator type is RSI; SMA(Stochastic(close, high, low, length), smoothing length) when oscillator type is Stochastic; MACD histogram(close, fast length, slow length, signal length) when oscillator type is MACD histogram; CCI(hlc3, length) when oscillator type is CCI; MFI(hlc3, length) when oscillator type is MFI; ROC(source, length) when oscillator type is ROC; Williams %R(length) when oscillator type is Williams %R; OBV when oscillator type is OBV; external oscillator series otherwise
Calculation: Range = Highest(Oscillator, normalisation lookback) - Lowest(Oscillator, normalisation lookback), on the current bar
Calculation: Rank = PercentRank(Oscillator, normalisation lookback)
Calculation: ATR = ATR(ATR lookback)
Calculation: OscillatorGap = abs(Oscillator at C - Oscillator at E)
Calculation: PriceGap = abs(pivot price at C - pivot price at E), where pivot price is low for low pivots and high for high pivots
Calculation: LongSwing = Highest(high over bars strictly between E and C) - max(low at E, low at C)
Calculation: ShortSwing = min(high at E, high at C) - Lowest(low over bars strictly between E and C)
Long: PivotLow(low, left pivot bars, right pivot bars) is not NA, with C the bar right pivot bars ago, AND there exists an earlier pivot low E among the pivot memory most recent pivot lows with min span <= C - E <= max span AND ((regular enabled AND low at C < low at E AND Oscillator at C > Oscillator at E AND (zone mode is off OR (zone mode is adaptive AND Rank at E <= outer percent for E AND Rank at C <= outer percent for C) OR (zone mode is fixed AND Oscillator at E < oversold for E AND Oscillator at C < oversold for C)) AND 100 * (regular divergence weight * min(OscillatorGap / (divergence scale * Range), 1) + regular zone weight * max(0, (zone scale - Rank at E) / zone scale) + regular swing weight * min(LongSwing / (ATR scale * ATR at C), 1) + regular price weight * min(PriceGap / (ATR scale * ATR at C), 1)) >= min score) OR (hidden enabled AND low at C > low at E AND Oscillator at C < Oscillator at E AND 100 * (hidden divergence weight * min(OscillatorGap / (divergence scale * Range), 1) + hidden swing weight * min(LongSwing / (ATR scale * ATR at C), 1) + hidden price weight * min(PriceGap / (ATR scale * ATR at C), 1)) >= min score)) AND OscillatorGap >= min divergence percent / 100 * Range AND PriceGap >= min separation * ATR at C AND LongSwing >= min swing * ATR at C AND (LOS disabled OR for every bar j strictly between E and C: low at j >= straight line from (E, low at E) to (C, low at C) evaluated at j AND Oscillator at j >= straight line from (E, Oscillator at E) to (C, Oscillator at C) evaluated at j)
