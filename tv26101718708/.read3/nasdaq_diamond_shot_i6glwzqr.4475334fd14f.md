Calculation: Average candle range = SMA(high - low, range lookback)
Long: flat AND during the configured session on a configured weekday AND (one-shot mode disabled OR no earlier entry on the current configured-timezone calendar day) AND close >= open AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) * 100 >= minimum body percentage AND high - low >= average candle range AND high - low <= average candle range * maximum range multiple AND (minimum candle-size filter disabled OR (high - low) / close * 100 >= minimum candle-size percentage) AND (DEMA filter disabled OR close > DEMA(close, DEMA lookback))
Short: flat AND during the configured session on a configured weekday AND (one-shot mode disabled OR no earlier entry on the current configured-timezone calendar day) AND close < open AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) * 100 >= minimum body percentage AND high - low >= average candle range AND high - low <= average candle range * maximum range multiple AND (minimum candle-size filter disabled OR (high - low) / close * 100 >= minimum candle-size percentage) AND (DEMA filter disabled OR close < DEMA(close, DEMA lookback))
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + (high - low on entry bar) * take-profit fraction OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - (high - low on entry bar) * take-profit fraction OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration
