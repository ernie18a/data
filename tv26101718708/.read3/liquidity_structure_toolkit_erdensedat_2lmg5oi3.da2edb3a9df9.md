Calculation: Doji = abs(close - open) <= (high - low) * doji-body fraction
Calculation: Major pivots = PivotHigh(high, major sensitivity, major sensitivity) and PivotLow(low, major sensitivity, major sensitivity), calculated on T1; chart bars take values from T1's still-forming bar; track levels subject to the active-level limit
Calculation: Break pivots = PivotHigh(high, break half-length, break half-length) and PivotLow(low, break half-length, break half-length), calculated on chart bars; break half-length is half the break fractal length rounded up to an odd length
Long: (close-break mode AND close > latest unbroken break pivot high) OR (wick-break mode AND high > latest unbroken break pivot high) OR (a retained major pivot low not swept before this bar is touched with low <= its level AND close > its level, and this bar is the first bullish non-doji bar at or after the most recent such touch within the PA lookback)
Short: (close-break mode AND close < latest unbroken break pivot low) OR (wick-break mode AND low < latest unbroken break pivot low) OR (a retained major pivot high not swept before this bar is touched with high >= its level AND close < its level, and this bar is the first bearish non-doji bar at or after the most recent such touch within the PA lookback)
