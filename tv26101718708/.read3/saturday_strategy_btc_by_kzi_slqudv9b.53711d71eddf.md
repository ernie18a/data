Long: On each chart bar, the day is Saturday AND the last completed daily bar's close > its open
Short: On each chart bar, the day is Saturday AND the last completed daily bar's close <= its open
Exit: Long, close >= entry price × long exit multiplier OR the day is Sunday through Friday
Exit: Short, close <= entry price × short exit multiplier OR the day is Sunday through Friday
Long: previous T1 close of selected instrument > previous T1 open of selected instrument AND day of week is Saturday
Short: previous T1 close of selected instrument <= previous T1 open of selected instrument AND day of week is Saturday
Exit: long, day of week is not Saturday
Exit: short, day of week is not Saturday
