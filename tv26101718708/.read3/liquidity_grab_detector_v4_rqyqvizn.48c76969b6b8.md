Calculation: Active sell-side and buy-side liquidity pools = configured confirmed chart-bar pivot highs and lows, previous completed T1 highs and lows, optionally previous completed T2 highs and lows, optionally equal highs and lows whose latest two confirmed same-side pivots differ by less than the equal-level ATR threshold, and enabled higher-timeframe pivot levels; merge levels within ATR times the merge distance, set the merged midpoint to the hit-count-weighted mean of source prices, and set zone half-width to the greater of ATR times the configured half-width and the minimum-tick floor, expanded by the configured hit-count factor up to its cap. A pool is active while its state is unconsumed and its age does not exceed the maximum; remove a sell-side pool after consecutive closes above its top and a buy-side pool after consecutive closes below its bottom.
Calculation: Pool hit count increases on a spaced touch of its zone that closes on the rejection side of its midpoint; pool health starts at full health and decreases on each close inside the zone in proportion to volume divided by its volume-average capacity, floored at zero. Pool source priority is retained from the highest-priority merged source.
Calculation: Regime = coil when enabled ADX is below its coil threshold and Bollinger Band width percentile is below its threshold; otherwise trend when enabled ADX is above its trend threshold; otherwise range. Sweep-score component weights use configured base weights, with trend weights adjusted toward higher-timeframe direction and market-structure shift and away from pool significance, channel position, and wick dominance; range weights adjusted toward pool significance, channel position, and wick dominance and away from higher-timeframe direction and market-structure shift; and coil weights adjusted toward penetration, reclaim, pool significance, and higher-timeframe direction.
Calculation: Sweep score = age penalty × session multiplier × coil multiplier × 100 × the weighted mean of: penetration score, clamp(1 − abs(penetration in ATR − target penetration) / max(penetration half-width, penetration floor), 0, 1); reclaim score, normalized reclaim percentage; wick score, clamp(wick-to-true-range ratio / wick saturation, 0, 1); body score, full score for a candle body in the signal direction and the configured lesser score otherwise; pool significance, 0.4 × clamp(hit count / hit scale, 0, 1) + 0.3 × normalized pool health + 0.3 × source-priority score; stretch score, clamp(abs(impulse AVWAP distance in ATR) / stretch scale, 0, 1); higher-timeframe score, neutral score when the filter is disabled, full score when enabled and direction agrees, otherwise zero; volume score, neutral score when unavailable, otherwise normalized volume / volume average; micro-CHoCH score, full score if the micro trend changes in the signal direction within its lookback, otherwise zero; market-structure-shift score, clamp(distance beyond the prior lookback extreme in ATR / saturation distance, 0, 1); and channel score, neutral when either nearest active pool is missing, normalized distance from the lower or upper channel edge outside the configured middle band, otherwise the configured middle score. Use separate configured weights by component and regime. Age penalty = clamp(base age penalty + clamp(pool age / age scale, 0, 1)^age exponent × age penalty range, minimum age penalty, maximum age penalty). Apply the current UTC session weight only in multiplier mode and the coil bonus only in coil regime. The higher-timeframe direction compares the current T3 close with EMA(close, higher-timeframe lookback, T3, current forming bar); chart bars take these values from T3. Session gating passes when mode is off or multiplier, or when the current session weight meets the gate threshold in gate mode.
Calculation: Reversal type = ABS when volume z-score exceeds its ABS threshold, wick ratio exceeds its ABS threshold, and reclaim exceeds its ABS threshold; otherwise REJ when wick ratio, reclaim, and signal-direction body exceed their REJ thresholds; otherwise DIV when volume z-score and reclaim exceed their DIV thresholds and wick ratio is below its DIV threshold; otherwise EXH. Type-specific minimum sweep scores are separate parameters; the grind path uses its own type and minimum score.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume − SMA(volume, z-score lookback)) / standard deviation(volume, z-score lookback).
Calculation: SFP exhaustion score = score scale × tanh(score input); score input = exhaustion coefficient × EMA(impulse stretch + optional daily-AVWAP coefficient × daily stretch + wick-skew coefficient × wick skew + volume coefficient × max(volume z-score, 0) × sign(impulse stretch), exhaustion lookback) + curvature coefficient × negative(ROC(EMA(close, price-EMA lookback), ROC lookback) − EMA(ROC(EMA(close, price-EMA lookback), ROC lookback), curvature lookback)[1]). Impulse stretch and daily stretch are (close − respective AVWAP) / max(ATR(ATR lookback), minimum tick); daily AVWAP is the volume-weighted average of hlc3 over chart bars since the current date began; wick skew is (upper wick − lower wick) / true range, or its neutral value when true range is zero. Impulse AVWAP resets at each configured N-bar extreme. All coefficients, lookbacks, score scale, and tanh input clipping limits are separate parameters.
Calculation: SFP percentile rank = percent rank of the SFP exhaustion score over its configured lookback.
Long: A qualifying bullish sweep of an active buy-side pool is marked. A spike sweep has low below the pool bottom, close above its midpoint, ATR-normalized penetration above the minimum, and positive reclaim; or an enabled grind sweep, when no spike sweep occurs, has a prior close within the grind lookback below the pool bottom by the configured ATR distance, current close above the bottom and below the top plus the configured ATR distance, and close above open. For a grind, use wick penetration and the greater of wick reclaim or the configured grind reclaim floor, and use the grind type; otherwise use spike penetration and wick reclaim and classify the reversal type. The pool must be unconsumed and have no pending confirmation. The age-adjusted sweep score must meet its type minimum and, when score filtering is enabled, the configured minimum; the shared sweep cooldown must pass. When confirmation window is zero, mark only the highest-scoring qualifying pool on the bar. When it is positive, save the base score on the raw sweep and mark only if, one or more bars later and within the window, close exceeds the pool top, the base score passes the optional score filter and the confirmation-bar reversal type minimum, and the shared sweep cooldown passes; the pending sweep is discarded on expiry or if close falls below the prior bar low.

Long: SFP is enabled, the chart bar is confirmed, a bullish SFP arm is active within its TTL, close is above the highest high over the configured BOS lookback excluding the current bar, the global SFP cooldown and long-side cooldown pass, and session gating passes. A bullish arm is created only when no long arm is active and its cooldown is clear, and either the SFP percentile rank is at or below the long percentile threshold and impulse stretch is below its negative threshold with positive volume z-score above its threshold, or a bullish sweep is marked. The arm is cleared on trigger and the long-side cooldown is set.
Short: SFP is enabled, the chart bar is confirmed, a bearish SFP arm is active within its TTL, close is below the lowest low over the configured BOS lookback excluding the current bar, the global SFP cooldown and short-side cooldown pass, and session gating passes. A bearish arm is created only when no short arm is active and its cooldown is clear, and either the SFP percentile rank is at or above the short percentile threshold and impulse stretch is above its threshold with positive volume z-score above its threshold, or a bearish sweep is marked. The arm is cleared on trigger and the short-side cooldown is set.
