Calculation: Rail lookback(T) = the timeframe-adaptive base lookback for T multiplied by the rail-width parameter, rounded and floored at the minimum-lookback parameter.
Calculation: Rail range(T) = highest(high, rail lookback(T)) - lowest(low, rail lookback(T)); upper rail = highest(high, rail lookback(T)); lower rail = lowest(low, rail lookback(T)).
Calculation: Rail trend(T) = on each T bar, set bullish when close crosses above highest(high, rail lookback(T))[1], set bearish when close crosses below lowest(low, rail lookback(T))[1], otherwise retain the previous state; initialize neutral. The chart calculation updates on the chart bar; T1–T9 table values use each T's bars with lookahead off, so they use the last completed T bar historically and may use the still-forming T bar in real time.
Calculation: Adaptive equilibrium = let p = clamp((HLC3 - lower rail) / rail range, 0, 1) when rail range exceeds the minimum price increment, otherwise 0.5; let v be VWMA(p, equilibrium lookback), falling back to WMA(p, equilibrium lookback) if unavailable; let a = average(EMA(p, equilibrium lookback), v); let c = 0.5 + (a - 0.5) × equilibrium shift; let b = clamp(c, equilibrium buffer percentage / 100, 1 - equilibrium buffer percentage / 100); equilibrium = lower rail + rail range × b when rail range exceeds the minimum price increment, otherwise average(upper rail, lower rail).
Calculation: Close location (%) = (close - low) / (high - low) × 100 when high > low, otherwise the neutral close-location parameter.
Calculation: Body percentage = abs(close - open) / (high - low) × 100 when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Upper wick percentage = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) × 100 when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Lower wick percentage = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) × 100 when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume baseline lookback) when the average is positive, otherwise unavailable.
Calculation: Relative range = (high - low) / SMA(high - low, range baseline lookback) when the average is positive, otherwise unavailable.
Calculation: Channel position (%) = clamp((close - lower rail) / rail range × 100, 0, 100) when rail range exceeds the minimum price increment, otherwise the neutral channel-position parameter.
Calculation: Normalized equilibrium slope (%) = (equilibrium - equilibrium[1]) / rail range × 100 when rail range exceeds the minimum price increment, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Reversal pressure score(side) = close-pressure weight × (100 - close location for short, close location for long) + wick weight × (upper wick percentage for short, lower wick percentage for long) + body weight × (body percentage when the candle closes in side's direction, otherwise body percentage × opposite-direction body factor) + participation weight × participation score + equilibrium weight × equilibrium score(side) + rail-position weight × clamp((channel position - upper score origin) / channel score span × 100 for short, (lower score origin - channel position) / channel score span × 100 for long, 0, 100). Participation score = neutral participation score if relative volume is unavailable; otherwise clamp(relative volume / stress-volume scale × 100, 0, 100) when (relative volume >= volume-stress threshold and body percentage < stress-body threshold) or relative range >= expanded-range threshold, else clamp(relative volume / ordinary-volume scale × 100, lower participation bound, upper participation bound). Equilibrium score(short) = high score on a downward curl, otherwise the descending-slope score bands selected by normalized equilibrium slope thresholds; equilibrium score(long) = high score on an upward curl, otherwise the mirrored ascending-slope score bands. A downward curl is equilibrium < equilibrium[1] and equilibrium[1] <= equilibrium[2]; an upward curl is equilibrium > equilibrium[1] and equilibrium[1] >= equilibrium[2].
Calculation: Pending retest(side) = the latest eligible confirmed chart-bar continuation or trend-change break in that side's direction, storing the crossed level: highest(high, chart rail lookback)[1] for long, lowest(low, chart rail lookback)[1] for short. A break is eligible only when the plan engine and that side are enabled, the selected source allows its type, no same-side plan is active, and no stop or target exit occurred on the break bar. A new eligible break replaces the pending setup. It expires when bars since the break exceed the retest-expiry parameter, and is cancelled if the rail trend changes away from its side or any intervening close is below equilibrium for long or above equilibrium for short.
Calculation: Stop distance(side) = with the structural stop model, max(minimum price increment, entry price minus floor((entry-bar low - ATR(ATR lookback) × structure buffer) / minimum price increment) × minimum price increment for long; max(minimum price increment, ceil((entry-bar high + ATR(ATR lookback) × structure buffer) / minimum price increment) × minimum price increment minus entry price for short); with the ATR model, max(minimum price increment, round(ATR(ATR lookback) × ATR stop multiplier / minimum price increment) × minimum price increment); with the fixed-tick model, fixed stop ticks × minimum price increment.
Calculation: Stop price(side) = structural stop from the structural stop model; otherwise entry price minus stop distance for long or plus stop distance for short. Target distance = fixed target ticks × minimum price increment with the fixed-tick model; otherwise max(minimum price increment, round(stop distance × reward-risk parameter / minimum price increment) × minimum price increment). Target price = entry price plus target distance for long or minus target distance for short.

Long: With the plan engine enabled and long direction allowed, a valid pending long retest exists; on a confirmed chart bar after its breakout and within its expiry, no same-side plan is active and no stop or target exit occurs on this bar; rail trend is bullish; low <= pending retest level; close > pending retest level; close > open; close location >= bullish retest close-location threshold; close > equilibrium; pending retest level > equilibrium; if the equilibrium-slope filter is enabled, equilibrium > equilibrium[1]; if the structural stop model is selected, stop distance(long) <= ATR(ATR lookback) × maximum structure-stop parameter; and if the flat-rails filter is enabled, rail range >= stop distance(long) × minimum rail-width parameter.
Short: With the plan engine enabled and short direction allowed, a valid pending short retest exists; on a confirmed chart bar after its breakout and within its expiry, no same-side plan is active and no stop or target exit occurs on this bar; rail trend is bearish; high >= pending retest level; close < pending retest level; close < open; close location <= bearish retest close-location threshold; close < equilibrium; pending retest level < equilibrium; if the equilibrium-slope filter is enabled, equilibrium < equilibrium[1]; if the structural stop model is selected, stop distance(short) <= ATR(ATR lookback) × maximum structure-stop parameter; and if the flat-rails filter is enabled, rail range >= stop distance(short) × minimum rail-width parameter.

Exit: Short, high >= stop price(short) or (high < stop price(short) and low <= target price(short)); if stop and target are both touched on the same bar, exit at the stop.
