Calculation: session opening-range high = highest(high, opening-range lookback) over the first opening-range lookback bars of each configured session, held until the next session; calculate separately for the chart and each external-market series
Calculation: session opening-range low = lowest(low, opening-range lookback) over the first opening-range lookback bars of each configured session, held until the next session; calculate separately for the chart and each external-market series
Calculation: live external-market count = count of enabled non-chart market series with available close and high data
Calculation: upward basket votes = count of live enabled external-market series that have closed above their own session opening-range high or below their own session opening-range low, with each series’ optional inversion applied; votes persist until the session ends
Calculation: downward basket votes = count of live enabled external-market series that have closed below their own session opening-range low or above their own session opening-range high, with each series’ optional inversion applied; votes persist until the session ends
Long: During the configured session, on the first confirmed chart close above the session opening-range high, if ATR(ATR lookback) > 0
Short: During the configured session, on the first confirmed chart close below the session opening-range low, if ATR(ATR lookback) > 0
Long: During the configured session, on a confirmed bar with ATR(ATR lookback) > 0 and no confirmed signal already fired that session, if the latest chart impulse accepted by the optional volume gate is upward, the current bar passes that gate, and either live external-market count = 0 or upward basket votes ≥ minimum basket confirmations; when enabled, the volume gate requires volume ≥ minimum relative volume × SMA(volume, volume lookback), and passes if volume or the SMA is unavailable or the SMA ≤ 0
Short: During the configured session, on a confirmed bar with ATR(ATR lookback) > 0 and no confirmed signal already fired that session, if the latest chart impulse accepted by the optional volume gate is downward, the current bar passes that gate, and either live external-market count = 0 or downward basket votes ≥ minimum basket confirmations; when enabled, the volume gate requires volume ≥ minimum relative volume × SMA(volume, volume lookback), and passes if volume or the SMA is unavailable or the SMA ≤ 0
Exit: long, after a confirmed long signal and before invalidation, during that session on a confirmed close below the session opening-range low
Exit: short, after a confirmed short signal and before invalidation, during that session on a confirmed close above the session opening-range high
