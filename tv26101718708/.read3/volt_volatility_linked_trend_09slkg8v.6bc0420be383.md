Calculation: Long stop = entry price - initial-stop multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) at entry; after each later T1 completion without a stop hit, update on chart bars when T1 advances to max(previous long stop, entry price, Highest(high(T1, last completed bar), since entry) - chandelier multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar))
Long: on chart bars when T1 advances, flat position AND close(T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) >= RSI midpoint AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) <= upper RSI threshold AND volume(T1, last completed bar) > SMA(volume, volume lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Long: on the last chart bar while T1 is forming, flat position AND close(T1, forming bar) > EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, forming bar) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, forming bar) >= RSI midpoint AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, forming bar) <= upper RSI threshold AND volume(T1, forming bar) > SMA(volume, volume lookback, T1, forming bar)
Short: on chart bars when T1 advances, close(T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) >= lower RSI threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) <= RSI midpoint AND volume(T1, last completed bar) > SMA(volume, volume lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Exit: long, on chart bars when T1 advances, low(T1, last completed bar) <= long stop in effect before the current T1 bar's stop update
