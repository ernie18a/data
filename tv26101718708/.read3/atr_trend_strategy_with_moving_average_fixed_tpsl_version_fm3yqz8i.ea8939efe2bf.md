Calculation: Trend State = uptrend after the selected upward confirmation price is above the previous-bar selected moving average(source, MA lookback, MA type) plus ATR multiplier × previous-bar selected moving average(true range, ATR lookback, ATR type) for the confirmation-bar parameter consecutive bars; downtrend after the selected downward confirmation price is below the corresponding lower band for that many consecutive bars; otherwise retain the previous state, initially neutral
Calculation: Trend MFE = 100 × (highest high during the trend − trend-start close) / trend-start close for an uptrend, or 100 × (trend-start close − lowest low during the trend) / trend-start close for a downtrend
Calculation: Trend MAE = 100 × (trend-start close − lowest low during the trend) / trend-start close for an uptrend, or 100 × (highest high during the trend − trend-start close) / trend-start close for a downtrend
Calculation: Take-profit percentage = TP parameter when auto TP/SL is disabled; otherwise, the interpolated percentile of completed-trend MFE values at the percentile parameter, undefined until values exist
Calculation: Stop-loss percentage = SL parameter when auto TP/SL is disabled; otherwise, the interpolated percentile of completed-trend MAE values at the percentile parameter, undefined until values exist
Long: (selected entry mode is trend-transition and Trend State changes to uptrend OR selected entry mode is moving-average pullback and Trend State is uptrend and crossunder(low, selected moving average(source, MA lookback, MA type)[1]) and no pullback signal has occurred since the position last became flat) AND (ATR filter is disabled OR selected moving average(true range, ATR lookback, ATR type)[1] > SMA(true range, ATR filter lookback))
Short: (selected entry mode is trend-transition and Trend State changes to downtrend OR selected entry mode is moving-average pullback and Trend State is downtrend and crossover(high, selected moving average(source, MA lookback, MA type)[1]) and no pullback signal has occurred since the position last became flat) AND (ATR filter is disabled OR selected moving average(true range, ATR lookback, ATR type)[1] > SMA(true range, ATR filter lookback))
Exit: Long, take-profit and stop-loss percentages are defined AND (high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage / 100) OR low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100))
Exit: Short, take-profit and stop-loss percentages are defined AND (low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage / 100) OR high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100))
