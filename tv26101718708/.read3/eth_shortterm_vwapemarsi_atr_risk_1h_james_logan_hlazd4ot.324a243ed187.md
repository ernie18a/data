Long: EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) > EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP((high + low + close) / 3)) AND (pullback filter disabled OR close >= EMA(close, pullback lookback) AND low <= EMA(close, pullback lookback)) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses above the long RSI threshold AND (session filter disabled OR bar datetime is within the configured session)
Short: EMA(close, shorter trend lookback) < EMA(close, longer trend lookback) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP((high + low + close) / 3)) AND (pullback filter disabled OR close <= EMA(close, pullback lookback) AND high >= EMA(close, pullback lookback)) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) crosses below the short RSI threshold AND (session filter disabled OR bar datetime is within the configured session)
Exit: Long, low <= close - ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier OR (trailing stop enabled AND low <= close - ATR(ATR lookback) × trailing multiplier) OR high >= close + ATR(ATR lookback) × take-profit multiplier OR bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade
Exit: Short, high >= close + ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier OR (trailing stop enabled AND high >= close + ATR(ATR lookback) × trailing multiplier) OR low <= close - ATR(ATR lookback) × take-profit multiplier OR bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade
