Calculation: opening-range high = maximum(high) over opening-range bars
Calculation: opening-range low = minimum(low) over opening-range bars
Long: after the opening range forms and while the ORB window is active, close crosses above opening-range high AND no breakout has yet occurred that day
Short: after the opening range forms and while the ORB window is active, close crosses below opening-range low AND no breakout has yet occurred that day
Long: after a bullish breakout arms a retest, while that retest remains armed, low is at or below opening-range high AND low is at or above opening-range low
Short: after a bearish breakout arms a retest, while that retest remains armed, high is at or above opening-range low AND high is at or below opening-range high
