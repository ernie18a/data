Calculation: FVG strength(direction) = round(100 × score / maximum score), where score starts at 0 and gains one for each of: directional gap size ≥ ATR(ATR lookback) × small-gap multiplier; directional gap size ≥ ATR × large-gap multiplier; and high[1] − low[1] ≥ ATR × displacement multiplier. Bullish gap size = low − high[2]; bearish gap size = low[2] − high. Maximum score starts at 3. When volume scoring is enabled, add one to the maximum and add one to the score if volume[1] > SMA(volume, volume lookback)[1] × volume multiplier. When trend scoring is enabled, add one to the maximum and add one to the score if, for bullish direction, close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) > EMA(close, longer EMA lookback), or, for bearish direction, close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) < EMA(close, longer EMA lookback). When structure scoring is enabled, add one to the maximum and add one to the score if, for bullish direction, close > highest(high, structure lookback)[1], or, for bearish direction, close < lowest(low, structure lookback)[1]. When sweep scoring is enabled, add one to the maximum and add one to the score if, for bullish direction, low[1] < lowest(low, sweep lookback)[2] AND close[1] > open[1], or, for bearish direction, high[1] > highest(high, sweep lookback)[2] AND close[1] < open[1]. The shorter and longer EMA lookbacks are separate parameters.
Long: bullish FVG display enabled AND low > high[2] AND bullish FVG strength ≥ minimum strength percentage
Short: bearish FVG display enabled AND high < low[2] AND bearish FVG strength ≥ minimum strength percentage
