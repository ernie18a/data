Calculation: Active accumulation range = on confirmed chart bars, after full percentile warm-up and the cooldown since the previous cycle end, start a candidate only when close > 0, PercentRank(DonchianWidth(short lookback), distribution lookback) <= compression threshold, and DonchianWidth >= minimum range-width fraction × close. Starting with the current Donchian window, repeatedly remove its oldest bar while this reduces the current width by more than the tail-trim fraction and the oldest-bar offset remains greater than the minimum-maturity parameter. In pivot mode, use the highest confirmed pivot high and lowest confirmed pivot low within the retained window, falling back separately to its highest high and lowest low; in absolute mode use those window extrema. Reject if the resulting width is below the minimum range width or close is outside the boundaries. Set tolerance to the candidate width × tolerance fraction and retain it for the range. Record the range start at the oldest retained bar and ATR(ATR lookback) from the preceding bar. While accumulating, update absolute boundaries with highs and lows, clamped to the frozen tolerance; in pivot mode update only to new confirmed pivots within the frozen tolerance whose pivot bars are in the range. Expire when age from the range start, or from a re-arm bar after a rejected direction, exceeds the maximum-age parameter. Discard on simultaneous boundary breaches or on a filtered breach whose close is outside the range; retain the range after a filtered breach whose close is inside. A sweep requires age at least the minimum-maturity parameter.
Calculation: Sweep extreme = lowest low from a lower-boundary breach through its return bar for a long, or highest high from an upper-boundary breach through its return bar for a short.
Calculation: Long stop = round down to the nearest tick (sweep extreme − stop-buffer parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) on the bar before range start).
Calculation: Short stop = round up to the nearest tick (sweep extreme + stop-buffer parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) on the bar before range start).
Calculation: Long target = round to the nearest tick (range high + (fib-extension parameter − 1) × (range high − sweep extreme)).
Calculation: Short target = round to the nearest tick (range low − (fib-extension parameter − 1) × (sweep extreme − range low)).
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an active accumulation range has a low below range low − frozen tolerance; the breach bar is in either configured session window if the session filter is enabled; the excursion is within the optional cap from the prior chart bar's TrueRange percentile, frozen at breach and rechecked against the cumulative extreme; and, if the liquidity filter is enabled, the required cluster of confirmed pivot lows near the lower boundary and near each other exists within its lookback. The close returns inside the range on the breach bar or within the return-deadline parameter, with no upper-boundary breach during the pending sweep; a re-armed range has not already swept this same side; if higher-timeframe bias is enabled, the last completed T1 bar's close is above its EMA(close, long lookback), calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars; and Long target − close >= one tick.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an active accumulation range has a high above range high + frozen tolerance; the breach bar is in either configured session window if the session filter is enabled; the excursion is within the optional cap from the prior chart bar's TrueRange percentile, frozen at breach and rechecked against the cumulative extreme; and, if the liquidity filter is enabled, the required cluster of confirmed pivot highs near the upper boundary and near each other exists within its lookback. The close returns inside the range on the breach bar or within the return-deadline parameter, with no lower-boundary breach during the pending sweep; a re-armed range has not already swept this same side; if higher-timeframe bias is enabled, the last completed T1 bar's close is below its EMA(close, long lookback), calculated on T1 bars and sampled on chart bars; and close − Short target >= one tick.
Exit: Long, from the chart bar after entry, low <= Long stop OR high >= Long target OR (bars since entry >= distribution-timeout parameter AND low > Long stop AND high < Long target); if both stop and target are touched on one bar, classify the exit at the stop.
Exit: Short, from the chart bar after entry, high >= Short stop OR low <= Short target OR (bars since entry >= distribution-timeout parameter AND high < Short stop AND low > Short target); if both stop and target are touched on one bar, classify the exit at the stop.
