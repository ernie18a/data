Calculation: recent-NG-visit(T) = at least one of the previous visit-lookback chart bars has a close from T, requested with lookahead_off and sampled on chart bars, above the chop-zone high
Calculation: expanding-volume(T) = current volume from T, requested with lookahead_off and sampled on chart bars (the T bar may still be forming in real time), > SMA of that sampled volume over the volume lookback on chart bars * the expansion multiplier
Calculation: climax-volume(T) = volume from T one chart bar ago > the value one chart bar ago of SMA of sampled T volume over the volume lookback on chart bars * the climax multiplier AND current sampled T volume < sampled T volume one chart bar ago * the remaining-volume fraction; T values are requested with lookahead_off and the current T bar may still be forming in real time
Calculation: bull-reclaim(T) = T signals enabled AND active level set enabled AND chop-zone high > 0 AND chop-zone low > 0 AND current T close >= chop-zone low AND T close one chart bar ago < chop-zone low AND (TICK confirmation disabled OR current chart-bar TICK close >= long TICK threshold) AND (volume exhaustion disabled OR climax-volume(T)); T closes are requested with lookahead_off and sampled on chart bars, and the current T bar may still be forming in real time
Calculation: bull-continuation(T) = T signals enabled AND active level set enabled AND chop-zone high > 0 AND chop-zone low > 0 AND T close one chart bar ago < chop-zone high AND current T close > chop-zone high AND (TICK confirmation disabled OR current chart-bar TICK close >= long TICK threshold) AND recent-SG-visit(T) AND (volume expansion disabled OR expanding-volume(T)); T closes are requested with lookahead_off and sampled on chart bars, and the current T bar may still be forming in real time
Calculation: bear-reclaim(T) = T signals enabled AND active level set enabled AND chop-zone high > 0 AND chop-zone low > 0 AND T close one chart bar ago > chop-zone high AND current T close <= chop-zone high AND (TICK confirmation disabled OR current chart-bar TICK close <= negative long TICK threshold) AND recent-NG-visit(T) AND (volume expansion disabled OR expanding-volume(T)); T closes are requested with lookahead_off and sampled on chart bars, and the current T bar may still be forming in real time
Calculation: bear-continuation(T) = T signals enabled AND active level set enabled AND chop-zone high > 0 AND chop-zone low > 0 AND T close one chart bar ago > chop-zone low AND current T close < chop-zone low AND (TICK confirmation disabled OR current chart-bar TICK close <= negative long TICK threshold) AND recent-NG-visit(T) AND (volume expansion disabled OR expanding-volume(T)); T closes are requested with lookahead_off and sampled on chart bars, and the current T bar may still be forming in real time
Calculation: bull-confirmed = bull-reclaim(T1) OR bull-reclaim(T2) OR bull-continuation(T1) OR bull-continuation(T2)
