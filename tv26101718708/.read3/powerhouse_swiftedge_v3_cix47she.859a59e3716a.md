Calculation: TrendScore = EMA(clamp(slope weight × (KAMA(close, adaptive lookback, fast length, slow length) − KAMA(close, adaptive lookback, fast length, slow length)[slope lookback]) / ATR(ATR lookback) + distance weight × (close − KAMA(close, adaptive lookback, fast length, slow length)) / (distance scale × ATR(ATR lookback)), score lower bound, score upper bound), score smoothing lookback); use zero as the raw score when ATR is zero
Calculation: EstimatedCVD = cumulative sum of estimated volume delta on chart bars, summing all automatic lower-timeframe T9 candles within each chart bar as positive volume when close >= open and negative volume otherwise; if no T9 candles are available, use chart-bar volume times (twice the fraction of the bar range below close minus one), or zero when high = low
Calculation: Risk = if the stop mode is ATR or the opposite confirmed swing is unavailable, fixed stop ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback); otherwise clamp(abs(entry price − opposite confirmed swing) + swing buffer ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback), minimum stop ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback), maximum stop ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback))
Calculation: TargetDistance(target parameter) = target parameter × Risk when targets use R multiples, target parameter × ATR(ATR lookback) when targets use ATR multiples, or target parameter when targets use points
