Calculation: EMA cross count = count, over the prior cross lookback completed chart bars, of changes in (close > EMA(close, EMA lookback))
Calculation: Long initial stop = in structure mode, the low of the nearest prior bar with bearish Heikin-Ashi color within the structure lookback minus the structure buffer; in fixed mode, signal-bar close minus the fixed stop distance; undefined if structure mode finds no such bar
Calculation: Short initial stop = in structure mode, the high of the nearest prior bar with bullish Heikin-Ashi color within the structure lookback plus the structure buffer; in fixed mode, signal-bar close plus the fixed stop distance; undefined if structure mode finds no such bar
Calculation: Long active stop = initialize to long initial stop; while long, if break-even is enabled and close >= entry price + break-even trigger distance, set to entry price + break-even offset distance if undefined, otherwise to max(previous active stop, entry price + break-even offset distance); otherwise retain previous active stop
Calculation: Short active stop = initialize to short initial stop; while short, if break-even is enabled and close <= entry price - break-even trigger distance, set to entry price - break-even offset distance if undefined, otherwise to min(previous active stop, entry price - break-even offset distance); otherwise retain previous active stop
Long: position is flat AND Heikin-Ashi candles are bullish on each of the preceding HA confirmation lookback completed chart bars AND close[1] > EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar opening datetime is within the configured session) AND (trade limit disabled OR entries taken on chart bars since the current forming T1 bar began < maximum entries per T1 interval) AND (loss limit disabled OR losing trades closed on chart bars since the current forming T1 bar began < maximum losing trades per T1 interval) AND ATR(ATR lookback)[1] > 0 AND ATR(ATR lookback)[1] >= SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR average lookback)[1] * ATR health multiplier AND (box range filter disabled OR (ATR(ATR lookback)[1] > 0 AND Highest(high, box lookback)[1] - Lowest(low, box lookback)[1] >= ATR(ATR lookback)[1] * box ATR multiplier)) AND (cross-chop filter disabled OR EMA cross count <= maximum allowed crosses) AND (slope filter disabled OR (ATR(ATR lookback)[1] > 0 AND abs(EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] - EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1 + slope lookback]) >= ATR(ATR lookback)[1] * minimum slope ATR multiplier AND EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] > EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1 + slope lookback]))
Short: position is flat AND Heikin-Ashi candles are bearish on each of the preceding HA confirmation lookback completed chart bars AND close[1] < EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar opening datetime is within the configured session) AND (trade limit disabled OR entries taken on chart bars since the current forming T1 bar began < maximum entries per T1 interval) AND (loss limit disabled OR losing trades closed on chart bars since the current forming T1 bar began < maximum losing trades per T1 interval) AND ATR(ATR lookback)[1] > 0 AND ATR(ATR lookback)[1] >= SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR average lookback)[1] * ATR health multiplier AND (box range filter disabled OR (ATR(ATR lookback)[1] > 0 AND Highest(high, box lookback)[1] - Lowest(low, box lookback)[1] >= ATR(ATR lookback)[1] * box ATR multiplier)) AND (cross-chop filter disabled OR EMA cross count <= maximum allowed crosses) AND (slope filter disabled OR (ATR(ATR lookback)[1] > 0 AND abs(EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] - EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1 + slope lookback]) >= ATR(ATR lookback)[1] * minimum slope ATR multiplier AND EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1] < EMA(close, EMA lookback)[1 + slope lookback]))
Exit: long, (flip exit enabled AND current Heikin-Ashi candle is bearish) OR (long active stop is defined AND low <= long active stop) OR (take profit enabled AND high >= signal-bar close + take-profit distance)
Exit: short, (flip exit enabled AND current Heikin-Ashi candle is bullish) OR (short active stop is defined AND high >= short active stop) OR (take profit enabled AND low <= signal-bar close - take-profit distance)
