Calculation: Ranging = ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) < ranging threshold
Calculation: Uptrend = not Ranging AND (close > SMA(close, slow SMA lookback) when available, otherwise close > EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) when available, otherwise close > open[fallback offset])
Calculation: Downtrend = not Ranging AND (close < SMA(close, slow SMA lookback) when available, otherwise close < EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) when available, otherwise close < open[fallback offset])
Calculation: Adaptive pivot lookback = clamp(round(base swing lookback / volatility ratio), minimum pivot lookback, maximum pivot lookback), where volatility ratio = ATR(ATR lookback) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), volatility average lookback), or 1 when that SMA is 0
Calculation: Structural swing high and low = stateful levels from confirmed PivotHigh(high, Adaptive pivot lookback, Adaptive pivot lookback) and PivotLow(low, Adaptive pivot lookback, Adaptive pivot lookback). Use each pivot bar's time. On a pivot high after the current swing low during a downtrend, replace the swing high and clear pending high if no swing high exists or the pivot is higher; otherwise store it as pending high. On other pivot highs, promote a pending low during an uptrend, then set the swing high to the pivot. Symmetrically, on a pivot low after the current swing high during an uptrend, replace the swing low and clear pending low if no swing low exists or the pivot is lower; otherwise store it as pending low. On other pivot lows, promote a pending high during a downtrend, then set the swing low to the pivot. Before extending levels to current-bar extremes, snapshot them as the prior swing high and low. Extend the high to new highs in an uptrend, the low to new lows in a downtrend, and both to new extremes while ranging. If the low is at or above the high, reset them to the highest high and lowest low over the recovery lookback and clear pending levels
Calculation: Swing range = abs(structural swing high - structural swing low)
Calculation: Golden pocket = the interval between the two retracement levels measured from the swing high in an uptrend, or from the swing low in a downtrend, using the upper and lower pocket retracement parameters. Deep value = the corresponding deep retracement level using the deep-value retracement parameter
Calculation: T2 pocket overlap = on chart bars, the low and high of the still-forming T2 bar overlap the golden pocket
Calculation: T2 trap = T2 pocket overlap AND T2 volume > SMA(volume, volume-average lookback, T2) * volume multiplier, OR the low of the still-forming T2 bar < prior swing low AND its close > prior swing low, OR its high > prior swing high AND its close < prior swing high; evaluate on chart bars using the still-forming T2 bar values
Calculation: TradeReady = not Ranging AND ((Uptrend AND the close of the still-forming T1 bar > SMA(close, slow SMA lookback, T1)) OR (not Uptrend AND the close of the still-forming T1 bar < SMA(close, slow SMA lookback, T1))) AND T2 pocket overlap AND a T2 trap occurred within the chart-bar confirmation window AND reward-risk ratio >= reward-risk threshold, where reward-risk ratio = abs(target - close) / abs(close - deep value) when the denominator is positive, otherwise 0, and target = structural swing high in an uptrend or structural swing low otherwise. Evaluate on chart bars using the still-forming T1 and T2 bar values
Long: bar is confirmed AND prior swing low exists AND low < prior swing low AND close > prior swing low AND ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) >= ranging threshold AND close > SMA(close, slow SMA lookback)
Short: bar is confirmed AND prior swing high exists AND high > prior swing high AND close < prior swing high AND ADX(DI lookback, ADX smoothing) >= ranging threshold AND close < SMA(close, slow SMA lookback)
Long: TradeReady AND not TradeReady[1] AND Uptrend
Short: TradeReady AND not TradeReady[1] AND not Uptrend
