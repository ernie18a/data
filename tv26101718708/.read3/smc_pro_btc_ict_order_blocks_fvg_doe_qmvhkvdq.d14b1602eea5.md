Calculation: Latest pivot high = most recent confirmed PivotHigh(high, swing lookback, swing lookback)
Calculation: Latest pivot low = most recent confirmed PivotLow(low, swing lookback, swing lookback)
Calculation: Structure bias(close, high, low, swing lookback) = persistent direction, set to bullish when close > latest pivot high, bearish when close < latest pivot low, otherwise unchanged
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = [low, high] of the nearest preceding bearish candle within OB lookback when close crosses above latest pivot high; valid until close < its lower edge
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = [low, high] of the nearest preceding bullish candle within OB lookback when close crosses below latest pivot low; valid until close > its upper edge
Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = latest [high[2], low] formed when low > high[2] AND close[1] > open[1]; valid until close < its lower edge
Calculation: Bearish FVG zone = latest [high, low[2]] formed when high < low[2] AND close[1] < open[1]; valid until close > its upper edge
Calculation: Long stop = lower edge of the bullish order-block zone × (1 - stop buffer / 100) if the signal bar intersects a valid bullish order-block zone (low <= its upper edge AND close >= its lower edge); otherwise lower edge of the bullish FVG zone × (1 - stop buffer / 100)
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - long stop) × reward/risk multiple
Calculation: Short stop = upper edge of the bearish order-block zone × (1 + stop buffer / 100) if the signal bar intersects a valid bearish order-block zone (high >= its lower edge AND close <= its upper edge); otherwise upper edge of the bearish FVG zone × (1 + stop buffer / 100)
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar close - (short stop - signal-bar close) × reward/risk multiple
Long: Structure bias(swing lookback, T1) = bullish AND Structure bias(swing lookback, T2) = bullish (each calculated on its timeframe bars and sampled on chart bars using lookahead-off values: last completed bar historically and the forming bar in realtime) AND a bullish liquidity sweep occurred within sweep-memory lookback bars (low < latest pivot low AND close > latest pivot low) AND ((bullish order-block zone is valid AND low <= its upper edge AND close >= its lower edge) OR (bullish FVG zone is valid AND low <= its upper edge AND close >= its lower edge)) AND close > open AND (premium/discount filter disabled OR close < latest pivot low + (latest pivot high - latest pivot low) × premium/discount threshold) AND position is flat
Short: Structure bias(swing lookback, T1) = bearish AND Structure bias(swing lookback, T2) = bearish (each calculated on its timeframe bars and sampled on chart bars using lookahead-off values: last completed bar historically and the forming bar in realtime) AND a bearish liquidity sweep occurred within sweep-memory lookback bars (high > latest pivot high AND close < latest pivot high) AND ((bearish order-block zone is valid AND high >= its lower edge AND close <= its upper edge) OR (bearish FVG zone is valid AND high >= its lower edge AND close <= its upper edge)) AND close < open AND (premium/discount filter disabled OR close > latest pivot high - (latest pivot high - latest pivot low) × premium/discount threshold) AND position is flat
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
