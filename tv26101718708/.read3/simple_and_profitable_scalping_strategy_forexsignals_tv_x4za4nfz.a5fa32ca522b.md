Calculation: EMA fan-out gap = ATR(ATR lookback) * fan-out ATR multiplier
Long: datetime >= start date AND datetime < end date AND position side is not long AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close > EMA(close, shorter trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND EMA(close, shorter entry lookback) > EMA(close, medium entry lookback) AND EMA(close, medium entry lookback) > EMA(close, longer entry lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter entry lookback)[n] - EMA fan-out gap > EMA(close, longer entry lookback)[n] for every n from 0 to fan-out lookback minus 1 AND low[1] > EMA(close, shorter entry lookback)[1] AND low < EMA(close, shorter entry lookback)
Short: datetime >= start date AND datetime < end date AND position side is not short AND EMA(close, shorter trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close < EMA(close, shorter trend lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND EMA(close, shorter entry lookback) < EMA(close, medium entry lookback) AND EMA(close, medium entry lookback) < EMA(close, longer entry lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter entry lookback)[n] + EMA fan-out gap < EMA(close, longer entry lookback)[n] for every n from 0 to fan-out lookback minus 1 AND high[1] < EMA(close, shorter entry lookback)[1] AND high > EMA(close, shorter entry lookback)
Exit: long, position side[1] is flat AND close < EMA(close, longer entry lookback)
Exit: short, position side[1] is flat AND close > EMA(close, longer entry lookback)
