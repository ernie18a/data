Calculation: Reference line = (1 − EMA blend) × recursive volume-weighted average + EMA blend × EMA(source, volume lookback); seed the recursive average with source, then use weight min(volume × sensitivity / Sum(volume, volume lookback), 1), or zero when the sum is zero, to update it as (1 − weight) × prior average + weight × source.
Calculation: ATR-normalized distance = (close − reference line) / ATR(master lookback).
Calculation: ATR compression percentile = 100 × (SMA(TrueRange, coil ATR lookback) − Lowest(SMA(TrueRange, coil ATR lookback), percentile lookback)) / (Highest(SMA(TrueRange, coil ATR lookback), percentile lookback) − Lowest(SMA(TrueRange, coil ATR lookback), percentile lookback)); use the midpoint percentile when the highest and lowest values are equal.
Calculation: Ratcheting stop uses loss = multiplier × SMA(TrueRange, stop lookback); when higher-timeframe sizing is selected, use SMA(TrueRange, stop lookback, T1, last completed bar), sampled on chart bars. In always-on mode, initialize the prior stop as close; if close and close[1] are both above the prior stop, set the stop to max(prior stop, close − loss); if both are below, set it to min(prior stop, close + loss); otherwise set it to close − loss when close is above the prior stop, or close + loss otherwise. In signal-armed mode, a long coil fade arms the stop at close − loss and a short coil fade at close + loss; while armed long, update it to max(prior stop, close − loss), and while armed short, update it to min(prior stop, close + loss). The real stop and manual visual stop use their separately configured lengths and multipliers.
Long: EMA(ATR-normalized distance, distance smoothing) > flat-distance threshold (up-colored line and candles).
Short: EMA(ATR-normalized distance, distance smoothing) < −flat-distance threshold (down-colored line and candles).
Long: an enabled long candle override is active, or an enabled long override fires while the effective sign is not already long on a coil fade, reversal, volume confirmation, or bullish spike (volume ≥ spike multiple × SMA(volume, spike-average lookback) and close ≥ open); keep the override until close > reference line or the hold duration expires.
Short: an enabled short candle override is active, or an enabled short override fires while the effective sign is not already short on a coil fade, reversal, volume confirmation, or bearish spike (volume ≥ spike multiple × SMA(volume, spike-average lookback) and close < open); keep the override until close < reference line or the hold duration expires.
Long: ATR compression percentile crosses from at or below the coil threshold to above it, and, from that release bar until bars since release reach the release-window parameter, no coil fade has fired for the release, the long side is permitted, and ATR-normalized distance ≤ −minimum stretch and the maximum stretch ceiling is disabled or ATR-normalized distance ≥ −maximum stretch ceiling.
Short: ATR compression percentile crosses from at or below the coil threshold to above it, and, from that release bar until bars since release reach the release-window parameter, no coil fade has fired for the release, the short side is permitted, and ATR-normalized distance ≥ minimum stretch and the maximum stretch ceiling is disabled or ATR-normalized distance ≤ maximum stretch ceiling.
Long: after a long coil fade, before another coil fade, not already volume-confirmed, and within the confirmation window after the fire, SMA(volume, recent-volume lookback) ≥ the SMA(volume, baseline lookback) from the bar before the fire × (1 + surge percentage / 100).
Short: after a short coil fade, before another coil fade, not already volume-confirmed, and within the confirmation window after the fire, SMA(volume, recent-volume lookback) ≥ the SMA(volume, baseline lookback) from the bar before the fire × (1 + surge percentage / 100).
Long: since ATR-normalized distance last returned to within the reset distance of zero, its minimum reached at most −leg-stretch threshold; at least the minimum confluence count is true among RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] ≤ oversold threshold and current RSI > prior RSI; low[1] ≤ reference line[1] − band multiple × ATR(master lookback)[1] and close > reference line − band multiple × ATR(master lookback); confirmed PivotLow(low, left bars, right bars); and volume ≥ volume-climax multiple × SMA(volume, reversal-volume lookback). Fire once per leg, then re-arm on the reset.
Short: since ATR-normalized distance last returned to within the reset distance of zero, its maximum reached at least the leg-stretch threshold; at least the minimum confluence count is true among RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] ≥ overbought threshold and current RSI < prior RSI; high[1] ≥ reference line[1] + band multiple × ATR(master lookback)[1] and close < reference line + band multiple × ATR(master lookback); confirmed PivotHigh(high, left bars, right bars); and volume ≥ volume-climax multiple × SMA(volume, reversal-volume lookback). Fire once per leg, then re-arm on the reset.
Long: while the always-on trail is active, close > ratcheting stop; or, while the signal-armed trail is active, its direction is long.
Short: while the always-on trail is active, close ≤ ratcheting stop; or, while the signal-armed trail is active, its direction is short.

Exit: short, in signal-armed mode the prior-bar armed short real stop disarms because close > its prior stop, or the prior-bar armed short manual visual stop disarms because close > its prior stop.
