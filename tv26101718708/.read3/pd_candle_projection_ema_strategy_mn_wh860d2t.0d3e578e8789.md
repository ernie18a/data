Calculation: UT ATR trailing stop(source, multiplier, ATR lookback) = let stop be the previous value, initialized to 0; if source > stop AND source[1] > stop, max(stop, source - multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback)); else if source < stop AND source[1] < stop, min(stop, source + multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback)); else if source > stop, source - multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback); otherwise source + multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback)
Long: (short EMA and intermediate EMA enabled AND crossover(EMA(short EMA source, shorter lookback), EMA(intermediate EMA source, intermediate lookback))[1] OR short EMA and longest EMA enabled AND crossover(EMA(short EMA source, shorter lookback), EMA(longest EMA source, longest lookback))[1]) AND close[1] < open[1] AND high > high[1] AND close > open
Short: (short EMA and intermediate EMA enabled AND crossunder(EMA(short EMA source, shorter lookback), EMA(intermediate EMA source, intermediate lookback))[1] OR short EMA and longest EMA enabled AND crossunder(EMA(short EMA source, shorter lookback), EMA(longest EMA source, longest lookback))[1]) AND close[1] > open[1] AND low < low[1] AND close < open
