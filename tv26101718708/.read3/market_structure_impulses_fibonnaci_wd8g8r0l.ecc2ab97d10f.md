Calculation: External impulse = direction, origin, and live extreme tracked from confirmed ZigZag pivots; ZigZag uses high/low or candle-body extrema and confirms chart-bar reversals at an ATR-multiple or percentage threshold. Extend on a new favorable extreme; reverse when an opposite pivot retraces at least the external threshold, or immediately when price breaks the origin.
Calculation: External retracement = adverse excursion from the live external impulse extreme, using the selected high/low or candle-body source from bars after the extreme bar, divided by the absolute distance between the extreme and origin.
Calculation: Pivot sweep = after a confirmed pivot, price pierces its level and closes back across it within the sweep window; the sweep wick is the highest high or lowest low from first pierce through reclaim.
Long: On a closed chart bar, the external impulse is bullish, the slow MACD on T1 meets the selected bullish filter (line above zero, line above signal, or both), external retracement is at least the pullback lower threshold and below the pullback upper threshold, the selected price source touched the pullback band within the trigger window, the fast chart-bar MACD line is above its signal, and no signal has fired for this external impulse extreme.
Short: On a closed chart bar, the external impulse is bearish, the slow MACD on T1 meets the selected bearish filter (line below zero, line below signal, or both), external retracement is at least the pullback lower threshold and below the pullback upper threshold, the selected price source touched the pullback band within the trigger window, the fast chart-bar MACD line is below its signal, and no signal has fired for this external impulse extreme.
Long: On a closed chart bar within the trigger window after a low pivot sweep, low has not broken the sweep wick, close is above the swept level, and fast chart-bar MACD line is above its signal; if the divergence filter is enabled, the lowest fast MACD line from first pierce through reclaim must be above its value at the swept pivot.
Short: On a closed chart bar within the trigger window after a high pivot sweep, high has not broken the sweep wick, close is below the swept level, and fast chart-bar MACD line is below its signal; if the divergence filter is enabled, the highest fast MACD line from first pierce through reclaim must be below its value at the swept pivot.
Calculation: Slow MACD is calculated on T1 bars; chart bars use its last completed bar when T1 is higher than the chart timeframe, and the current chart bar when T1 is the chart timeframe.
