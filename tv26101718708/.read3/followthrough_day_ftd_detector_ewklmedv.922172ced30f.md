Long: On chart bars, start a rally attempt when close > close[1] and low is at or near lowest(low, recent-low lookback), setting its starting low to min(low, low[1]) and its day count to 1; increment the count on each subsequent bar; reset the attempt if low < its starting low or its count exceeds the maximum rally-day parameter; signal when the attempt is valid, its count is >= the minimum rally-day parameter and <= the maximum rally-day parameter, (close - close[1]) / close[1] * 100 >= the minimum-gain parameter, and the higher-volume requirement is disabled or volume > volume[1].
