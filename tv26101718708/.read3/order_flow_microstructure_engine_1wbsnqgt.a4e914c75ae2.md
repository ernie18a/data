Calculation: signed flow per chart bar = sum of lower-timeframe T1 subbar volumes times their signed classification weight: positive strong weight if close > open, negative strong weight if close < open, otherwise positive or negative strong weight if close is respectively above or below the previous T1 close, otherwise prior nonzero sign times mixed weight, otherwise mixed weight times close location value when its absolute value exceeds the fallback threshold, else zero; close location value = ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), or zero for zero range. Sum positive and negative terms separately as buy and sell volume. When native footprint data is selected and available, use its ask volume, bid volume, and delta instead. Flow is valid when native data is available or the classified T1 subbar fraction exceeds its validity threshold.
Calculation: smoothed aggressor ratio = recursive adaptive smoothing of (buy volume - sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume), using alpha = slow alpha + (fast alpha - slow alpha) * Kaufman Efficiency Ratio(close, lookback); alternatively use SMA of the aggressor ratio. Acceleration = change(smoothed aggressor ratio, acceleration lookback). Signed-volume cumulative delta = cumulative signed flow since its configured reset event. Normalized bar delta = clip(signed flow / volume, -1, 1) when flow is valid and volume is positive, else zero. Normalized cumulative delta = x / (1 + abs(x)), where x = cumulative delta / (SMA(volume, lookback) * scale factor), when flow is valid and average volume is positive, else zero.
Calculation: footprint pattern code = positive strong when top-half bin volume fraction >= strong-top threshold and signed flow > 0; else positive mild when that fraction >= mild-top threshold and signed flow > 0; else negative strong when the fraction <= strong-bottom threshold and signed flow < 0; else negative mild when the fraction <= mild-bottom threshold and signed flow < 0; else negative mild when the fraction >= sell-at-top threshold and signed flow < 0; else positive mild when the fraction <= buy-at-bottom threshold and signed flow > 0; else zero. Bins divide the chart-bar range by the bin count, or use the configured percentage of close with minimum tick size; allocate reconstructed subbar flow by typical price, or native footprint rows by their midpoint. Top-half fraction is the volume in bins at or above floor(bin count / 2) divided by total bin volume.
Calculation: auction contribution = positive or negative directional support when value-area volume / total active flow volume is respectively at least the efficient threshold or at most the inefficient threshold; efficient support follows the dominant buy or sell volume, inefficient support reverses it, and intermediate efficiency contributes zero. Value area expands outward from the highest-volume bin until it contains the configured fraction of total volume, adding the higher-volume adjacent bin first.
Calculation: multi-level flow imbalance = clip(sum over occupied footprint bins of exp(-abs(bin center - close) / (bin width * depth-decay parameter)) * (buy volume - sell volume) / sum of the same weights times (buy volume + sell volume), -1, 1).
Calculation: directional event intensity = (buy intensity - sell intensity) / (buy intensity + sell intensity), with each intensity decayed each chart bar and incremented by one on its respective side when absolute signed flow exceeds its event multiplier times SMA(abs(signed flow), event lookback); zero when total intensity is zero.
Calculation: absorption signal = sign(signed flow) * clip(realized impact / predicted impact - 1, -1, 1), where realized impact = abs(close - open), predicted impact = impact coefficient * ATR(lookback) * sqrt(abs(signed flow) / SMA(volume, lookback)); unavailable when predicted impact is nonpositive or required data is unavailable.
Calculation: MOC imbalance = cumulative signed flow / cumulative chart-bar volume during the configured market-on-close interval, reset each session and unavailable outside the active interval before its first value; clamp it to [-1,1] when used as a factor. Completed interval imbalance is retained for confluence checks.
Calculation: paired-instrument flow proxy = clip(change(paired-instrument close, lookback) / ATR(paired-instrument, lookback), -1, 1) times its momentum coefficient plus clip(directional paired-instrument volume / SMA(paired-instrument volume, lookback), -1, 1) times its volume coefficient; directional volume is positive when close > open, negative when close < open, else zero. Read paired-instrument data on chart bars.
Calculation: regime score = smoothed aggressor ratio times its weight + normalized bar delta times its weight + normalized cumulative delta times its weight + clip(acceleration times its scale, -1, 1) times its weight + clamped MOC imbalance times its weight + footprint pattern code / pattern scale times its weight. Regime code is positive strong above the strong-bull threshold, positive mild above the bull threshold, negative strong below the strong-bear threshold, negative mild below the bear threshold, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Bayesian posterior = sigmoid(logit(prior) + sum of factor-specific likelihood weights times logit(sigmoid(factor-specific gain times factor))). Factors are smoothed aggressor ratio, normalized bar delta, normalized cumulative delta, scaled acceleration, clamped MOC imbalance, footprint pattern code / pattern scale, auction contribution, multi-level flow imbalance, directional event intensity, absorption signal, and paired-instrument flow proxy. Multiply the first six flow-family contributions and multi-level flow imbalance by the decorrelation shrink when decorrelation is enabled: shrink = 1 / (1 + (family count - 1) * mean correlation), where mean correlation is the average of all pairwise rolling correlations among smoothed aggressor ratio, normalized bar delta, normalized cumulative delta, scaled acceleration, and footprint pattern code / pattern scale, each clamped to [0,1]. Do not shrink the other factors. Posterior = 1 / (1 + exp(-total log odds)).
Calculation: higher-timeframe posterior proxy = sigmoid(momentum coefficient * change(close, momentum lookback) / ATR(lookback) + supertrend-direction coefficient * Supertrend direction + volume-direction coefficient * directional volume / SMA(volume, lookback)); volume direction is positive when close > open and negative otherwise. Compute this proxy on each higher-timeframe bar and sample it on chart bars with lookahead off: use completed higher-timeframe bars historically and the forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime. Use separate timeframes T2 and T3 for the two higher-timeframe checks.
Calculation: thrash score = min(1, decayed regime-change total / maximum thrash total), summing over the configured chart-bar lookback with each event weighted by decay factor to the age of the event. In directional mode a change between opposite nonzero regime signs counts fully and a change between neutral and nonneutral counts half; in any-change mode any regime-code change counts fully.
Calculation: cofire count and maximum = among the following dimensions, count each eligible dimension in the maximum and each eligible dimension agreeing with the nonzero Bayesian direction in the count: smoothed aggressor ratio beyond its magnitude threshold; signed flow beyond its volume-relative threshold; normalized cumulative delta beyond its threshold; nonzero footprint pattern; completed MOC imbalance beyond its threshold; sweep on the current bar; efficient directional auction; multi-level flow imbalance beyond its threshold; directional event intensity beyond its threshold; paired-instrument direction beyond its threshold. Both counts are zero when Bayesian direction is zero.
Calculation: effective direction = sign(Bayesian posterior - neutral probability) when Bayesian mode is enabled, otherwise sign(regime score). Base confidence = twice the absolute distance of the Bayesian posterior from neutral probability when Bayesian mode is enabled, otherwise min(1, abs(regime score) / confidence scale). Confidence = clip((base confidence - elevated-risk penalty - thrash score * maximum thrash penalty + confluence bonus + horizon bonus) * higher-timeframe multiplier, 0, 1). Elevated risk is VPIN above its toxicity threshold or the percentile rank of SMA(abs(close - close[1]) / abs(signed flow), impact lookback) above its thin-liquidity threshold. Confluence bonus is zero when thrash exceeds its suppression threshold, otherwise, when enabled and at least three dimensions are eligible, min(bonus cap, cofire count * bonus per dimension). Horizon bonus is positive when posterior and its medium- and long-lookback EMAs have the same nonzero sign, negative when their nonzero signs split, and zero otherwise. Higher-timeframe directions are positive above the bull posterior threshold, negative below the bear posterior threshold, and neutral otherwise. In required-alignment mode, multiplier is zero on any nonneutral disagreement, else one plus the alignment bonus when aligned with a nonneutral first higher-timeframe direction, else one. In preferred-alignment mode, disagreement uses the disagreement multiplier and alignment uses the alignment bonus under the same alignment condition; ignored mode uses one. Tier code is assigned by the configured descending confidence thresholds.
