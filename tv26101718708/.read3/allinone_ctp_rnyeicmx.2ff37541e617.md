Calculation: Effective volume = volume when present and positive, otherwise one unit.
Calculation: Volume-profile levels = choose row height as max(seed range / target row count, tick size) or fixed ticks per row × tick size; allocate each bar’s effective volume across overlapping bins in proportion to overlap, or assign all volume to the HLC3 bin in single-price mode; if a bar spans more than the bin-span limit, assign its volume to the midpoint bin. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin. Starting at that bin, expand the value area toward the side with greater volume across the configured adjacent-bin comparison span, choosing upward on ties and adding bins until accumulated volume reaches total volume × the configured value-area fraction or the expansion limit; VAH is the upper edge of the highest included bin and VAL is the lower edge of the lowest included bin.
Calculation: Session profile levels = Volume-profile levels of confirmed chart bars since each configured calendar-day profile reset, with row-size seed range from the last completed T2 bar’s high minus low.
Calculation: Weekly profile levels = Volume-profile levels of confirmed chart bars since each calendar-week boundary, with row-size seed range from the last completed T3 bar’s high minus low.
Calculation: Structure profile levels = Volume-profile levels of confirmed chart bars since the latest main swing BOS or CHoCH, with row-size seed range = ATR(ATR lookback) × structure seed multiplier.
Calculation: Long-term POC = POC of a rolling volume profile of completed T2 bars over the configured lookback, updated on chart bars; at initialization, its row-size seed range is Highest(high, lookback, T2, bar still forming) − Lowest(low, lookback, T2, bar still forming).
Calculation: Valid swing-high pivot = PivotHigh(high, main pivot lookback) where pivot high − low[main pivot lookback] > ATR(ATR lookback) × pivot ATR sensitivity, and, when swing-size filtering is enabled, pivot high − Lowest(low, main pivot lookback × structure range multiplier) ≥ SMA(ATR(main pivot lookback), average swing lookback) × swing-size multiplier × main pivot lookback × swing-size normalization factor.
Calculation: Valid swing-low pivot = PivotLow(low, main pivot lookback) where high[main pivot lookback] − pivot low > ATR(ATR lookback) × pivot ATR sensitivity, and, when swing-size filtering is enabled, Highest(high, main pivot lookback × structure range multiplier) − pivot low ≥ SMA(ATR(main pivot lookback), average swing lookback) × swing-size multiplier × main pivot lookback × swing-size normalization factor.
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close).
Calculation: Lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Candle range = max(high − low, tick size).

Short: close crosses below session-profile VAL OR close crosses below session-profile POC OR close crosses below weekly-profile VAL OR close crosses below structure-profile POC OR close crosses below long-term POC.
Long: the most recent valid swing-high pivot is unbroken and close > its level + ATR(ATR lookback) × break-displacement multiplier and (main volume filter is disabled OR volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume > SMA(effective volume, volume-average lookback) × volume threshold multiplier), and either the main structure trend is not bearish or this upward break condition has held for the configured consecutive-bar count while the trend is bearish.
Short: the most recent valid swing-low pivot is unbroken and close < its level − ATR(ATR lookback) × break-displacement multiplier and (main volume filter is disabled OR volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume > SMA(effective volume, volume-average lookback) × volume threshold multiplier), and either the main structure trend is not bullish or this downward break condition has held for the configured consecutive-bar count while the trend is bullish.
Long: a confirmed PivotHigh(high, internal pivot lookback) is more than ATR(ATR lookback) × internal duplicate threshold from the most recent valid swing-high level, or no such swing-high level exists; then close > the most recent internal pivot-high level + ATR(ATR lookback) × break-displacement multiplier and the main volume condition passes, with no main swing BOS or CHoCH on this bar.
Short: a confirmed PivotLow(low, internal pivot lookback) is more than ATR(ATR lookback) × internal duplicate threshold from the most recent valid swing-low level, or no such swing-low level exists; then close < the most recent internal pivot-low level − ATR(ATR lookback) × break-displacement multiplier and the main volume condition passes, with no main swing BOS or CHoCH on this bar.

Long: (CISD is disabled AND the current bar has a latest confirmed pivot low within the configured swing lookback, low < that pivot low, close > that pivot low, lower wick > ATR(ATR lookback) × minimum-wick ATR parameter, lower wick > candle range × minimum-wick fraction, (sweep-depth filter is disabled OR pivot low − low > ATR(ATR lookback) × sweep-depth parameter), (trend filter is disabled OR close < EMA(close, fast lookback)), and (volume filter is disabled OR volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR volume > SMA(effective volume, volume-average lookback))) OR (CISD is enabled AND the prior bar satisfies those same bullish sweep conditions and current close > the prior bar’s pivot low); AND (BOS/CHoCH confluence filter is disabled OR a bullish main BOS or CHoCH occurred within the configured chart-bar confluence window).

Short: after a selected session’s configured opening interval has ended and its range is locked, the first chart-bar close below the range low, where the range uses available T1 bar lows within the interval on chart bars (including a forming T1 bar) and falls back to overlapping chart-bar lows if T1 intrabars are unavailable.
