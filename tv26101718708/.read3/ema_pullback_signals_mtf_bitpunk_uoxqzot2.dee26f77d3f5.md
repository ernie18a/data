Calculation: Setup direction = on T1’s last completed bar, long if close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback) and that EMA > its value slope lookback T1 bars earlier; short if close < that EMA and it < its value slope lookback T1 bars earlier; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Fast zone = [EMA(close, fast EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) − zone width × ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar), EMA(close, fast EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) + zone width × ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar)].
Calculation: Trend zone = [EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) − zone width × ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar), EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) + zone width × ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar)].
Calculation: Momentum confirmation = for setup side s (+1 long, −1 short), (high > low AND s × (close − open) / (high − low) ≥ body fraction AND (s = +1 ? close − low : high − close) / (high − low) ≥ extreme fraction) OR (s × (MACD histogram(close, fast MACD lookback, slow MACD lookback, signal lookback) − its previous chart-bar value) > 0 AND s × (Heikin-Ashi close − Heikin-Ashi open) > 0); calculate MACD and Heikin-Ashi from chart bars.
Calculation: Reclaim state = on confirmed chart bars with available prices and setup data, a standard chart and a valid compatible pairing with higher timeframe T1, and trend EMA lookback > fast EMA lookback, track idle, departed, pending, and consumed states. Reset state, spacing reference, and prior-zone contacts if these requirements fail, setup direction is neutral, or setup direction changes. From idle, become departed when setup direction is long and chart low is above both zone upper edges, or setup direction is short and chart high is below both lower edges, and Efficiency Ratio(close, fast EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) ≥ departure-efficiency threshold. From departed or consumed, start a pending attempt when the chart bar overlaps either zone (low ≤ zone upper edge and high ≥ zone lower edge): if no spacing ATR is stored, overlap suffices; otherwise require a fresh overlap with at least one zone (the prior chart bar did not overlap that same zone), long spacing of prior best favorable confirmed close − current low or short spacing of current high − prior best favorable confirmed close ≥ rearm distance × stored ATR, and setup Efficiency Ratio ≥ departure-efficiency threshold. On starting an attempt, store the current T1 bar index; choose the Trend zone if overlapped, otherwise the Fast zone. While pending, any Trend-zone overlap upgrades the active zone and does not restart the window; expire when current T1 bar index − stored start index ≥ confirmation-window length. The active edge is the upper edge of the chosen zone for long setup direction and its lower edge for short. A pending attempt can confirm on its first contact bar. If not confirmed, a later separated chart bar returns state to departed when setup Efficiency Ratio ≥ departure-efficiency threshold, otherwise idle. On directional reclaim with Momentum confirmation, consume the attempt and store current setup ATR and close as the spacing reference, even if the extension limit skips the entry. While consumed, update the reference with each more favorable confirmed close. A pending retouch does not restart its window; a newly started attempt gets a new window.
Long: Reclaim state is pending, setup direction is long, close > active edge, close > open, close > prior chart-bar close, Momentum confirmation, and (close − active edge) / ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) ≤ maximum entry extension.
Short: Reclaim state is pending, setup direction is short, close < active edge, close < open, close < prior chart-bar close, Momentum confirmation, and (active edge − close) / ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) ≤ maximum entry extension.
