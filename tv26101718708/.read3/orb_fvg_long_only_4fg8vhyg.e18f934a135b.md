Calculation: opening-range high = maximum high of chart bars in the configured opening-range session, fixed when the session ends
Calculation: opening-range low = minimum low of chart bars in the configured opening-range session, fixed when the session ends
Calculation: long stop = the selected opening-range low, opening-range midpoint, or entry price - ATR(ATR lookback, chart bars at entry) * ATR stop multiplier; store only when the trade-plan setting is enabled and entry price > long stop
Long: opening-range session has ended AND close > opening-range high AND close[1] <= opening-range high AND chart bar is within the configured trade session AND chart bar is confirmed at close AND (daily-bias filter disabled OR close > open of the current, still-forming T1 bar sampled on chart bars) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, EMA lookback, current, still-forming T1 bar sampled on chart bars)) AND (trend-day filter disabled OR (if the last completed T1 bar's high > low then (close - low) / (high - low) else zero-range close-position fallback) >= trend close-position threshold) AND (loss-streak filter disabled OR the number of consecutive preceding completed T1 bars with close < low + (high - low) * weak-day threshold is below the loss-streak limit) AND (one-trade-per-day limit disabled OR no long signal has occurred earlier on the current date) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback, chart bars) * volume multiplier) AND (FVG requirement disabled OR low > high[2] occurred within the FVG lookback in chart bars)
Exit: long, after a long signal with stored valid levels, high >= entry price + (entry price - long stop) * target-2 risk/reward OR low <= long stop
