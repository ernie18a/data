Calculation: ATR trailing stop and direction = initialize bullish with stop at close − ATR(ATR lookback) × stop multiplier; while bullish, set stop to max(previous stop, close − ATR × stop multiplier) if close > previous stop, otherwise retain previous stop, then flip bearish and reset stop to close + ATR × stop multiplier if close < resulting stop; while bearish, set stop to min(previous stop, close + ATR × stop multiplier) if close < previous stop, otherwise retain previous stop, then flip bullish and reset stop to close − ATR × stop multiplier if close > resulting stop; otherwise retain direction; flips are changes from the prior direction, with no signal on initialization

Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(max(volume-weighted mean(configured VWAP source squared) − VWAP(configured source)^2, 0)) over the VWAP accumulation interval
Calculation: VWAP extension = abs(close − VWAP(configured source)) / VWAP standard deviation; undefined when VWAP is unavailable or its standard deviation is nonpositive
