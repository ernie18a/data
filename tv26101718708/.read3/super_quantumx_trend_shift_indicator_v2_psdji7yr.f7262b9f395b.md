Calculation: Primary HVN target = close at the most recent chart bar where volume equals highest(volume, primary HVN lookback), captured on each signal bar
Calculation: Aggressive HVN target = close at the most recent chart bar where volume equals highest(volume, aggressive HVN lookback), captured on each signal bar
Calculation: First Fib target = at each signal, for a long signal, T1 lowest(low, Fib lookback) + (T1 highest(high, Fib lookback) - T1 lowest(low, Fib lookback)) * first extension factor; for a short signal, T1 highest(high, Fib lookback) - (T1 highest(high, Fib lookback) - T1 lowest(low, Fib lookback)) * first extension factor; calculated on T1 bars including the still-forming bar and returned to chart bars
Calculation: Second Fib target = at each signal, for a long signal, T1 lowest(low, Fib lookback) + (T1 highest(high, Fib lookback) - T1 lowest(low, Fib lookback)) * second extension factor; for a short signal, T1 highest(high, Fib lookback) - (T1 highest(high, Fib lookback) - T1 lowest(low, Fib lookback)) * second extension factor; calculated on T1 bars including the still-forming bar and returned to chart bars
Long: Supertrend(multiplier, lookback) direction is bullish AND MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) line > signal line AND RSI(close, lookback) > RSI threshold AND volume > 0 AND fast selected MA(close, fast MA type, fast lookback) > slow selected MA(close, slow MA type, slow lookback) AND close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close > open[1] AND (ATR filter disabled OR high - low > ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier) AND (cooldown disabled OR current bar index - most recent signal bar index > cooldown lookback; initial most recent signal bar index is zero)
Short: Supertrend(multiplier, lookback) direction is bearish AND MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) line < signal line AND RSI(close, lookback) < RSI threshold AND volume > 0 AND fast selected MA(close, fast MA type, fast lookback) <= slow selected MA(close, slow MA type, slow lookback) AND close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close < open[1] AND (ATR filter disabled OR high - low > ATR(ATR lookback) * ATR multiplier) AND (cooldown disabled OR current bar index - most recent signal bar index > cooldown lookback; initial most recent signal bar index is zero)
Exit: long, high >= Primary HVN target OR high >= Aggressive HVN target OR high >= First Fib target OR high >= Second Fib target OR low <= Supertrend(multiplier, lookback) value
Exit: short, low <= Primary HVN target OR low <= Aggressive HVN target OR low <= First Fib target OR low <= Second Fib target OR high >= Supertrend(multiplier, lookback) value
