Calculation: Volume excess = volume * P_volumeScale / Highest(volume, P_volumeLookback) - EMA(volume * P_volumeScale / Highest(volume, P_volumeLookback), P_volumeSmoothing).
Calculation: High-volume candle = Volume excess > 0 AND Volume excess >= P_highVolumeFraction * Highest(Volume excess, P_volumeLookback).
Calculation: Directional high-volume bar = High-volume candle AND close != open.
Calculation: Bearish sweep level = the highest qualifying level among the prior P_sweepLookback completed T1 highs, where the current forming T1 high exceeds the level and its open and close are at or below it, or its open is above and close is below it; do not use older levels past a prior high above the current T1 high; default to the previous T1 high if none qualifies. Bullish sweep level is the mirrored lowest qualifying prior T1 low, with the current forming T1 low below the level and its open and close at or above it, or its open below and close above it; default to the previous T1 low if none qualifies.
Calculation: Bearish sweep zone qualifies = since the current T1 high last changed, at least P_zoneCandles chart bars closed between the bearish sweep level and current T1 high, at least one Directional high-volume bar overlapped that interval, and the interval height is no greater than P_sweepBodyFactor times the largest absolute chart-bar body among those closes. Bullish sweep zone qualifies by mirroring the levels, close interval, overlap, and height.
Calculation: Bearish CISD level = at a chart bar making the current T1 high above the previous completed T1 high, use the current T2 open if the current T2 candle is bullish and the preceding completed T2 candle is bearish or doji. Otherwise, search back up to P_CISDSearch completed T2 candles for a bullish candle immediately newer than a bearish or doji candle, stopping if an intervening T2 high exceeds the current T2 high; use the lowest open among bullish candles from the matched candle through the current T2 candle. If that level exceeds the current T2 open, use the current T2 open when its close is at or above its open, or its low when its close is below its open; retain the existing level if no candidate is found. Bullish CISD level is the mirror: at a chart bar making the current T1 low below the previous completed T1 low, use the current T2 open if the current T2 candle is bearish and the preceding completed T2 candle is bullish or doji; otherwise search back up to P_CISDSearch completed T2 candles for a bearish candle immediately newer than a bullish or doji candle, stopping if an intervening T2 low is below the current T2 low; use the highest open among bearish candles from the matched candle through the current T2 candle. If that level is below the current T2 open, use the current T2 open when its close is at or below its open, or its high when its close is above its open; retain the existing level if no candidate is found.
Calculation: Bearish CISD becomes active when the current T2 close is below the Bearish CISD level, after the selected T2 candle's start time, on a confirmed chart bar closing exactly with the T2 bar, and the Bearish sweep zone qualifies; reset it on a new T1 high setup and clear it if a chart high exceeds that setup high. Bullish CISD becomes active when the current T2 close is above the Bullish CISD level under the same T2-close and chart-confirmation conditions and the Bullish sweep zone qualifies; reset it on a new T1 low setup and clear it if a chart low falls below that setup low. T1 and T2 current bars are built from chart bars and may still be forming before their respective closes.
Calculation: Three-timeframe bullish alignment = on each of T6, T7, and T8, DMI positive DI > negative DI AND MACD line > signal line, using the same DMI and MACD lookbacks on all three timeframes. Three-timeframe bearish alignment uses the opposite comparisons. These calculations run on T6, T7, and T8 bars; chart bars read their current values, including values from bars still forming.
Calculation: Delayed cloud bounds = for each of two Ichimoku clouds calculated on chart bars, the lower and upper of Span A and Span B at a lag equal to that cloud's Kijun lookback. Use separate Tenkan, Kijun, and Span B lookbacks for each cloud; the second cloud uses correspondingly longer lookbacks tied to the first by P_cloudScale.
Calculation: Up SK pattern = close[2] < open[2] AND close[1] > open[1] AND max(open, close)[1] > max(open, close)[2] AND volume[1] <= volume[2]. Down SK pattern = close[1] < open[1] AND close[2] > open[2] AND min(open, close)[2] > min(open, close)[1] AND volume[1] <= volume[2].
Calculation: Active wall = when the latest qualifying High-volume candle ranges from each of T3, T4, and T5 overlap, their intersection; otherwise, the high-low range of the most recently completed window among P_sessionA, P_sessionB, and P_sessionC that contained a chart bar. Wall candles are evaluated on T3, T4, and T5 bars, with chart bars reading current values that may include a still-forming bar.
Calculation: Alignment box = the high-low range accumulated on chart bars during the current Three-timeframe bullish or bearish alignment, including the current bar. Retain it beyond P_alignmentAge bars only while delayed Span B of the first Ichimoku cloud lies between EMA(close, P_fastEMA) and EMA(close, P_slowEMA) for P_cloudHold consecutive bars and a fresh CISD trigger occurred within P_CISDWindow bars; otherwise reset it. Reset after a breakout signal. The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback.
Long: Bearish CISD becomes active AND it was inactive on the preceding chart bar.
Short: Bearish CISD becomes active AND it was inactive on the preceding chart bar.
Long: P_confirmationBars after a bullish CISD activation, the bullish CISD remains active AND its level equals the level P_confirmationBars bars ago.
Short: P_confirmationBars after a bearish CISD activation, the bearish CISD remains active AND its level equals the level P_confirmationBars bars ago.
Long: Three-timeframe bullish alignment begins AND chart close is above both Delayed cloud bounds AND the current chart bar is within P_alignmentAge bars of the latest alignment bar AND a bullish or bearish CISD activation occurred within P_CISDWindow bars AND (no Active wall exists OR Alignment box top exceeds Active wall top) AND the Active wall is not wholly contained within the Alignment box.
Short: Three-timeframe bearish alignment begins AND chart close is below both Delayed cloud bounds AND the current chart bar is within P_alignmentAge bars of the latest alignment bar AND a bullish or bearish CISD activation occurred within P_CISDWindow bars AND (no Active wall exists OR Alignment box bottom is below Active wall bottom) AND the Active wall is not wholly contained within the Alignment box.
Long: The latest breakout signal was long within P_alignmentAge bars AND the Up SK pattern occurs AND chart close is outside both Delayed cloud bounds AND the current chart bar is within P_SKWindow bars of the latest alignment bar AND no stuck-cloud exit has occurred since that breakout.
Short: The latest breakout signal was short within P_alignmentAge bars AND the Down SK pattern occurs AND chart close is outside both Delayed cloud bounds AND the current chart bar is within P_SKWindow bars of the latest alignment bar AND no stuck-cloud exit has occurred since that breakout.
Exit: Long, a breakout signal occurred within P_alignmentAge bars AND either SK pattern occurs AND chart close is within either Delayed cloud bounds AND no stuck-cloud exit has occurred since that breakout.
Exit: Short, a breakout signal occurred within P_alignmentAge bars AND either SK pattern occurs AND chart close is within either Delayed cloud bounds AND no stuck-cloud exit has occurred since that breakout.
