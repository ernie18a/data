Calculation: Frozen value profile = on a shorter event lookback of completed chart bars, allocate each bar’s footprint volume using separate parameters for the share placed evenly across evenly spaced prices from its footprint VAL to VAH and the share placed at its footprint POC; bin across the bars’ price range, Gaussian-smooth, select separated local peaks meeting parameterized height thresholds up to a cluster-count parameter, split clusters at intervening valleys, and calculate each cluster’s POC and value area by adding adjacent bins in descending volume order until the parameterized value-area fraction is included; select the cluster containing the previous close, or the cluster with the nearest POC, and freeze its VAL, VAH, POC, and neighboring cluster POCs until the next refresh.
Calculation: Active value profile = on a longer lookback of completed chart bars, bin footprint row volumes across the bars’ price range, Gaussian-smooth, select separated local peaks meeting parameterized average-height, maximum-height, and local-mass thresholds up to a cluster-count parameter, enhance volume around selected peaks, and split clusters at intervening valleys; within each cluster use the enhanced-volume maximum as POC and add adjacent raw-volume bins in descending volume order until the parameterized value-area fraction is included; select the cluster containing the current close, or the cluster range containing it, or the cluster with the nearest POC; return its VAL, VAH, POC, and neighboring cluster POCs.
Calculation: Auction scores(profile) = relative flow is current footprint total volume, or candle volume when footprint is unavailable, divided by the SMA of prior-bar flow over a lookback; delta fraction is footprint delta divided by footprint total; range factor is (high - low) / ATR(lookback); outside-volume share is footprint volume with row midpoint above profile VAH or below profile VAL divided by footprint total; hold fraction is the fraction of current and preceding acceptance-lookback closes above VAH or below VAL. Normalize each continuous input by a clamped linear mapping with parameterized bounds. Up/down participation scores are weighted sums of normalized relative flow and normalized positive/negative delta; expansion score is normalized range factor; up/down acceptance scores are weighted sums of normalized outside-volume share, the indicator that current-bar footprint POC is beyond the corresponding boundary, and hold fraction; final up/down scores are weighted sums of participation, expansion, and acceptance scores. Use separate parameters for each distinct weight, bound, and threshold.
Calculation: Event state = initialize in balance; in balance prioritize upper rejection, lower rejection, upper break, then lower break, otherwise enter compression when its compression condition holds; in compression prioritize upper break, lower break, upper rejection, then lower rejection; upper/lower breaks enter the corresponding probe state; a probe fails and rotates toward POC when its close returns to or inside its frozen value boundary, or becomes accepted when its break, acceptance-lookback closes, and final score meet the acceptance conditions; an accepted state fails on the same return condition or migrates when high/low reaches the neighboring POC within its ATR-distance parameter; a downward/upward rotation enters the opposite probe on a break in that direction or returns to balance at POC or after its timeout; migration or an expired probe/accepted lock returns to balance on profile refresh.
Long: active value profile is available AND ((high > active VAH AND close > active VAH) OR (high > active VAH AND close <= active VAH) OR (high >= active VAH - ATR(lookback) * rejection-distance parameter AND close < active VAH AND close < open AND final up score < acceptance threshold) OR (low < active VAL AND close >= active VAL) OR (low <= active VAL + ATR(lookback) * rejection-distance parameter AND close > active VAL AND close > open AND final down score < acceptance threshold)) OR event state emits an upward probe, upward acceptance, upward migration, downward failure, or downward rejection.
Short: active value profile is available AND ((low < active VAL AND close < active VAL) OR (low < active VAL AND close >= active VAL) OR (low <= active VAL + ATR(lookback) * rejection-distance parameter AND close > active VAL AND close > open AND final down score < acceptance threshold) OR (high > active VAH AND close <= active VAH) OR (high >= active VAH - ATR(lookback) * rejection-distance parameter AND close < active VAH AND close < open AND final up score < acceptance threshold)) OR event state emits a downward probe, downward acceptance, downward migration, upward failure, or upward rejection.
Long: event state is a probe up AND high > frozen VAH AND close > frozen VAH AND the acceptance-lookback closes are above frozen VAH AND final up score >= acceptance threshold.
Short: event state is a probe down AND low < frozen VAL AND close < frozen VAL AND the acceptance-lookback closes are below frozen VAL AND final down score >= acceptance threshold.
