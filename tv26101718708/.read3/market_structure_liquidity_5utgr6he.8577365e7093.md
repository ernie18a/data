Calculation: ATR-safe = ATR(ATR lookback) when positive, otherwise close times the fallback fraction
Calculation: Efficiency ratio(close, lookback) = abs(close − close[lookback]) / sum(abs(close − close[1]), lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when the range is zero
Calculation: Close position up = (close − low) / (high − low), and close position down = (high − close) / (high − low); both equal one half when the range is zero
Calculation: Displacement threshold = true-range/ATR-safe percentile rank over its lookback ≥ its floor when adaptive displacement is enabled, otherwise true range ≥ its ATR multiple times ATR-safe
Calculation: Coil release = long-lookback efficiency ratio at its specified lag ≤ its threshold AND short-lookback efficiency ratio > that ratio at its specified lag
Calculation: Displacement = (displacement threshold AND body ratio ≥ its minimum) OR coil release
Calculation: Pivot quality = one half times clamp(1 − abs(latest pivot − previous same-side pivot)/(close times its scale factor), 0, 1) plus one half times clamp(1 − pivot age/its recency horizon, 0, 1); use one half if the pivots or age are unavailable
Calculation: Protected swing high = latest swing pivot high when a newly confirmed swing pivot low is below the previous swing pivot low; protected swing low = latest swing pivot low when a newly confirmed swing pivot high is above the previous swing pivot high; each persists until updated
Calculation: Validated bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing pivot high AND displacement passes when that filter is enabled AND close position up ≥ its minimum when the clean-close filter is enabled AND latest-high pivot quality ≥ its minimum when the pivot-quality filter is enabled AND (prior structure bias is not bearish OR protected-swing filtering is disabled OR protected swing high is unavailable OR close > protected swing high)
Calculation: Validated bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed swing pivot low AND displacement passes when that filter is enabled AND close position down ≥ its minimum when the clean-close filter is enabled AND latest-low pivot quality ≥ its minimum when the pivot-quality filter is enabled AND (prior structure bias is not bullish OR protected-swing filtering is disabled OR protected swing low is unavailable OR close < protected swing low)
Calculation: Structure bias starts neutral, becomes bullish after a validated bullish break, becomes bearish after a validated bearish break, and otherwise persists
Calculation: Internal structure bias starts neutral, becomes bullish when close crosses above the latest confirmed internal pivot high, becomes bearish when close crosses below the latest confirmed internal pivot low, and otherwise persists
Calculation: Equal-high level = the higher of the latest two confirmed swing pivot highs when their distance is within the equal-pivot tolerance times ATR-safe; equal-low level = the lower of the latest two confirmed swing pivot lows under the same tolerance; each persists until updated
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = (high > equal-high level AND close < equal-high level) OR (high > latest confirmed swing pivot high AND close < that pivot AND close < open); bearish liquidity sweep = (low < equal-low level AND close > equal-low level) OR (low < latest confirmed swing pivot low AND close > that pivot AND close > open)
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = bar low > high at the FVG separation lag AND the gap size ≥ its minimum ATR multiple times ATR-safe; bearish fair value gap = bar high < low at that lag AND the gap size ≥ its minimum ATR multiple times ATR-safe
Calculation: Order-block quality = round(100 times (displacement contribution + body-ratio contribution + volume-z contribution + recent-sweep contribution)); the contributions are respectively their configured weights times min(1, impulse-in-ATR/its scale), min(1, origin-candle body ratio/its scale), min(1, max(0, (origin-candle volume z-score + its offset)/its scale)), and 1 if a same-direction sweep occurred within its recency window, otherwise 0
Calculation: Bullish order block = zone from the nearest bearish candle within the origin scan before a validated bullish break, using its full high-low range or candle body according to the zone setting, retained when quality ≥ its minimum; bearish order block is symmetric using the nearest bullish candle before a validated bearish break. Merge overlapping same-side zones, retain the higher quality, and remove the lowest-quality zone when the zone-count limit is exceeded
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume − SMA(volume, volume lookback)) / standard deviation(volume, volume lookback), using proxy volume only when instrument volume is unavailable
Calculation: Confluence count = number of profile point of control, nearest untested point of control above and below price, equal-high and equal-low levels, swing-range midpoint, and latest swing pivot high and low within cluster tolerance times ATR-safe of the order-block midpoint
Calculation: Volume node = classify the order-block midpoint's rolling volume-profile bin as high-volume when its volume ≥ the configured percentile of nonempty bin volumes, low-volume when ≤ the configured low percentile, otherwise middle; assign each bar's volume to the bin of its typical price
Calculation: Premium/discount position = (close − latest swing pivot low) / (latest swing pivot high − latest swing pivot low); bullish alignment requires position < one half and bearish alignment requires position > one half
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on chart bars, set bullish when chart close > the latest confirmed pivot high and bearish when chart close < the latest confirmed pivot low; calculate the pivots on bars of T1 from the last completed T1 bar, and otherwise retain the prior bias
Calculation: Regime = trending when ADX(ADX lookback) > its threshold AND efficiency ratio(close, regime lookback) > its threshold; volatility-clustered when the z-score of the exponentially smoothed absolute log return, normalized over its lookback, exceeds its threshold; trend reward applies only when trending, not volatility-clustered, and structure-aligned
Calculation: Reversal confirmation is bullish when the latest confirmed price pivot low is below its previous pivot low while the corresponding RSI pivot low is above its previous RSI pivot low, or RSI ≤ its oversold threshold; bearish confirmation is symmetric at pivot highs or RSI ≥ its overbought threshold
Calculation: Zone score = clamp(quality times its weight + confluence count times its weight + high-volume-node bonus or low-volume-node penalty + premium/discount alignment bonus + higher-timeframe alignment bonus or penalty + structure alignment bonus or penalty + reversal-confirmation bonus + aligned-trend bonus − volatility-cluster penalty, 0, score ceiling), then subtract min(maximum age penalty, zone age/decay window times maximum age penalty) and, when enabled with enough same-direction historical analogs, add (distance-weighted analog probability − one half) times its weight; clamp again to 0 and the score ceiling. Analog probability is the weighted mean of past same-direction zone outcomes, with each weight 1/(1+d²), where d is the sum across quality, directional structure alignment, normalized regime, efficiency ratio, regime-change score, and normalized volume z-score of log(1 + absolute feature difference)
Calculation: Best setup = highest-scoring untagged order block whose structure and premium/discount align; long setup uses bullish zones and short setup bearish zones. A zone is tagged after price first overlaps it
