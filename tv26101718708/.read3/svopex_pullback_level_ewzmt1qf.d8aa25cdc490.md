Calculation: Active short pullback level = On each confirmed chart bar with close > open, if close[1] < open[1] and min(open[1], close[1]) < LOWEST(min(open, close), pullback lookback)[2], replace the level with max(open, close) of the oldest consecutive prior candle with close < open, scanning back from the prior bar through the walk-back cap; keep it active through its first confirmed green-body break where max(open, close) > the level, then deactivate it.
Calculation: Active long pullback level = On each confirmed chart bar with close < open, if close[1] > open[1] and max(open[1], close[1]) > HIGHEST(max(open, close), pullback lookback)[2], replace the level with min(open, close) of the oldest consecutive prior candle with close > open, scanning back from the prior bar through the walk-back cap; keep it active through its first confirmed red-body break where min(open, close) < the level, then deactivate it.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close > open AND max(open, close) > active short pullback level AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the current, still-forming T1 bar >= SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-SMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the current, still-forming T1 bar.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close < open AND min(open, close) < active long pullback level AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the current, still-forming T1 bar <= SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI-SMA lookback) calculated on T1 bars using the current, still-forming T1 bar.
