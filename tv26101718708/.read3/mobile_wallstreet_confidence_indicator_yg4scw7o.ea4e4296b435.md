Calculation: MTF direction at T = bullish when EMA(close, fast EMA lookback) > EMA(close, slow EMA lookback), close > the slow EMA, and the slow EMA > its prior value; bearish when all three comparisons are reversed; otherwise neutral. Evaluated on chart bars using T values, with the last completed T bar when the closed-bar setting is on and the still-forming T bar otherwise.
Calculation: Bullish MTF count = number of bullish directions among T3, T4, T5, and T6.
Calculation: Bearish MTF count = number of bearish directions among T3, T4, T5, and T6.
Calculation: Bullish MTF agreement = bullish MTF count >= required MTF count AND bullish MTF count > bearish MTF count.
Calculation: Bearish MTF agreement = bearish MTF count >= required MTF count AND bearish MTF count > bullish MTF count.
Calculation: Execution values = open, high, low, close, and volume from T1, sampled on chart bars from the still-forming T1 bar.
Calculation: Prior structure high/low = highest high/lowest low over the structure lookback, excluding the current T1 bar.
Calculation: Execution ATR = ATR(ATR lookback, T1, still-forming bar).
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); candle range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Bullish body confirmation = close > open AND candle body >= Execution ATR * body-size threshold.
Calculation: Bearish body confirmation = close < open AND candle body >= Execution ATR * body-size threshold.
Calculation: Buy zone = [lowest low over the zone lookback on T2, lowest low + ATR(ATR lookback, T2) * zone thickness]; sell zone = [highest high over the zone lookback on T2 - ATR(ATR lookback, T2) * zone thickness, highest high]. These values are sampled on chart bars from the still-forming T2 bar.
Calculation: Recent buy-zone touch = a T1 bar range overlapped the buy zone within the zone-exit lookback; recent sell-zone touch = a T1 bar range overlapped the sell zone within the zone-exit lookback.
Calculation: Buy-zone exit = the prior chart bar had a recent buy-zone touch AND current close > buy-zone high AND close > open; sell-zone exit = the prior chart bar had a recent sell-zone touch AND current close < sell-zone low AND close < open.
Calculation: Buy post-retest = close crossed above buy-zone high within the post-retest lookback, at least one chart bar ago, AND current low <= buy-zone high + Execution ATR * retest tolerance AND close > buy-zone high AND close > open; sell post-retest = the reverse at sell-zone low, using current high >= sell-zone low - Execution ATR * retest tolerance AND close < sell-zone low AND close < open.
Calculation: Buy-zone filter = zone-exit requirement is off OR buy-zone exit OR buy post-retest; sell-zone filter = zone-exit requirement is off OR sell-zone exit OR sell post-retest.
Calculation: Zone bullish event = buy-zone exit OR buy post-retest; zone bearish event = sell-zone exit OR sell post-retest.
Calculation: Bullish structure clearance = close > prior structure high OR prior structure high - close >= Execution ATR * structure-clearance parameter; bearish structure clearance = close < prior structure low OR close - prior structure low >= Execution ATR * structure-clearance parameter.
Calculation: Retracement depth passes = highest high minus lowest low over the configured recent T1 bars >= Execution ATR * minimum retracement-depth parameter.
Calculation: Fractal ribbon bullish = EMA of T1 RSI values on chart bars using the fractal fast lookback > EMA of those values using the fractal slow lookback AND the fast EMA > RSI midpoint AND (T1 close > last confirmed T1 pivot high OR T1 close > T1 slow EMA). The pivot uses separate left and right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Fractal ribbon bearish = fractal ribbon bullish with all directional comparisons reversed, using the last confirmed T1 pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish ribbon = EMA(bullish confidence - bearish confidence, ribbon fast lookback) > EMA(bullish confidence - bearish confidence, ribbon slow lookback) AND the fast EMA > ribbon threshold AND fractal ribbon bullish; bearish ribbon = the reverse comparisons AND fractal ribbon bearish.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of one for each true condition: bullish MTF agreement; bullish MTF count > bearish MTF count; T3 bullish; T4 bullish; T5 or T6 bullish; T1 close > fast EMA; fast EMA > slow EMA; T1 close > slow EMA; slow EMA > its prior value; middle EMA > long EMA; T1 close > middle EMA; Hull MA > its prior value; T1 close > Hull MA; T1 RSI > RSI midpoint; RSI > its prior value; MACD line > signal line; MACD histogram > zero; CCI > zero; stochastic K > D; stochastic K > midpoint; MFI > midpoint; Williams %R > its midpoint; ADX > trend threshold AND +DI > -DI; SAR < close; OBV > its prior value; VWMA > its prior value; close > VWMA; close > Bollinger basis; close > midpoint of Bollinger basis and upper band; close > midpoint of prior Donchian upper and lower bands; close > prior structure high; bullish body confirmation; candle body >= Execution ATR * body-size threshold; lower wick > upper wick; fractal ribbon bullish; bullish structure clearance. Indicators use T1 values on chart bars; EMA, Hull MA, RSI, MACD, CCI, stochastic, MFI, Williams %R, DMI/ADX, SAR, OBV, VWMA, Bollinger Bands, and Donchian bands use their respective lookback parameters.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of one for each true condition: bearish MTF agreement; bearish MTF count > bullish MTF count; T3 bearish; T4 bearish; T5 or T6 bearish; T1 close < fast EMA; fast EMA < slow EMA; T1 close < slow EMA; slow EMA < its prior value; middle EMA < long EMA; T1 close < middle EMA; Hull MA < its prior value; T1 close < Hull MA; T1 RSI < RSI midpoint; RSI < its prior value; MACD line < signal line; MACD histogram < zero; CCI < zero; stochastic K < D; stochastic K < midpoint; MFI < midpoint; Williams %R < its midpoint; ADX > trend threshold AND -DI > +DI; SAR > close; OBV < its prior value; VWMA < its prior value; close < VWMA; close < Bollinger basis; close < midpoint of Bollinger basis and lower band; close < midpoint of prior Donchian upper and lower bands; close < prior structure low; bearish body confirmation; candle body >= Execution ATR * body-size threshold; upper wick > lower wick; fractal ribbon bearish; bearish structure clearance. Uses the same indicators and sampling as the bullish score.
Calculation: Bullish confidence = rounded percentage of bullish score normalized by its number of terms; bearish confidence = rounded percentage of bearish score normalized by its number of terms.
Calculation: Bullish rank = rounded bullish score divided by the rank-normalization parameter; bearish rank = rounded bearish score divided by the rank-normalization parameter.
Calculation: Bullish body ratio = candle body / candle range; upper-wick ratio = upper wick / candle range; lower-wick ratio = lower wick / candle range.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND prior close < prior open AND current body high >= prior body high AND current body low <= prior body low; bearish engulfing is the reverse.
Calculation: Doji = bullish body ratio <= doji threshold.
Calculation: Bullish marubozu = close > open AND bullish body ratio >= marubozu body threshold AND upper-wick ratio <= marubozu wick threshold AND lower-wick ratio <= marubozu wick threshold; bearish marubozu is the reverse.
Calculation: Hammer = lower wick >= candle body * hammer wick ratio AND upper wick <= candle body * hammer opposite-wick ratio AND close > low + candle range * hammer close-location threshold; shooting star reverses the wick comparisons and requires close < low + candle range * shooting-star close-location threshold.
Calculation: Bullish resistance break = close > sell-zone high AND prior close <= prior sell-zone high AND bullish body confirmation; bearish support break = close < buy-zone low AND prior close >= prior buy-zone low AND bearish body confirmation.
Calculation: Bull trap = a bullish resistance break occurred within the trap lookback, at least one chart bar ago, AND close < sell-zone low AND close < open; bear trap = a bearish support break occurred within the trap lookback, at least one chart bar ago, AND close > buy-zone high AND close > open.
Calculation: Bull flag = close - close[flag lookback] > Execution ATR * impulse threshold AND close > highest high over the flag lookback excluding the current bar AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND bullish body confirmation; bear flag reverses these comparisons.
Calculation: Double bottom = abs(low - prior structure low) <= Execution ATR * double-pattern tolerance AND close > open AND close > fast EMA AND recent buy-zone touch; double top uses prior structure high, close < open, close < fast EMA, and recent sell-zone touch.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > prior structure high AND bullish body confirmation; bearish structure break = close < prior structure low AND bearish body confirmation.
Calculation: Active pattern name, side, and score = initialize to no pattern, neutral, and zero; then apply these assignments in order, with each later matching assignment replacing the earlier one: bullish structure break gets long side and structure-break score; bearish structure break gets short side and structure-break score; doji with recent buy-zone touch gets long side and doji-at-support score, else doji with recent sell-zone touch gets short side and doji-at-resistance score, else doji with either confidence >= doji-exhaustion threshold gets neutral side and exhaustion score; hammer with recent buy-zone touch gets long side and hammer score; shooting star with recent sell-zone touch gets short side and shooting-star score; double bottom gets long side and a score selected by whether close > prior structure high; double top gets short side and a score selected by whether close < prior structure low; bull flag gets long side and a score selected by buy post-retest; bear flag gets short side and a score selected by sell post-retest; bullish engulfing gets long side and a score selected in order by buy post-retest, recent buy-zone touch, or neither; bearish engulfing gets short side and the analogous sell conditions; bullish marubozu gets long side and a score selected in order by bullish MTF agreement plus fractal ribbon bullish, buy post-retest, or neither; bearish marubozu uses the bearish equivalents; bear trap gets long side and trap score; bull trap gets short side and trap score; buy post-retest gets long side and retest score; sell post-retest gets short side and retest score.
Calculation: Pattern reversal bullish = active pattern side is long AND its name contains reversal, doji, hammer, double bottom, or trap; pattern reversal bearish uses short side and reversal, doji, shooting star, double top, or trap. Pattern continuation on either side = that side AND not its reversal condition.
Calculation: Pattern compatibility long = pattern-compatibility gate is off OR active pattern side is not short OR active pattern score < opposite-pattern blocking score; pattern compatibility short is the reverse.
Calculation: Long core = bullish MTF agreement AND bullish confidence >= minimum confidence AND bullish rank >= minimum rank AND retracement depth passes AND bullish structure clearance AND (body-confirmation requirement is off OR bullish body confirmation) AND buy-zone filter AND pattern compatibility long AND (fractal-ribbon gate is off OR fractal ribbon bullish).
Calculation: Short core = bearish MTF agreement AND bearish confidence >= minimum confidence AND bearish rank >= minimum rank AND retracement depth passes AND bearish structure clearance AND (body-confirmation requirement is off OR bearish body confirmation) AND sell-zone filter AND pattern compatibility short AND (fractal-ribbon gate is off OR fractal ribbon bearish).
Calculation: Long early candidate = early-signal display enabled AND not long core AND bullish MTF count >= early minimum count AND T3 and T4 bullish AND bearish MTF count <= early maximum count AND bullish confidence >= early confidence AND bullish rank >= minimum rank AND fractal ribbon bullish AND (body-confirmation requirement is off OR bullish body confirmation) AND buy-zone filter AND pattern compatibility long.
Calculation: Short early candidate = early-signal display enabled AND not short core AND bearish MTF count >= early minimum count AND T3 and T4 bearish AND bullish MTF count <= early maximum count AND bearish confidence >= early confidence AND bearish rank >= minimum rank AND fractal ribbon bearish AND (body-confirmation requirement is off OR bearish body confirmation) AND sell-zone filter AND pattern compatibility short.
Calculation: Long strong candidate = strong-signal display enabled AND long core AND bullish confidence >= strong confidence AND bullish rank >= strong rank AND bullish MTF count >= required MTF count AND fractal ribbon bullish AND (strong-retest requirement is off OR buy post-retest); short strong candidate is the bearish equivalent.
Calculation: Long regular candidate = normal-signal display enabled AND long core AND not long strong candidate; short regular candidate is the bearish equivalent.
Calculation: Long signal event = a long early, regular, or strong candidate changes from false on the prior chart bar to true now AND passes its repeat gate. The early repeat gate allows when one-signal-per-trend is off or the last printed side is not long or its strength is below early; the regular and strong gates use their corresponding strength levels. Each gate also requires the configured bars since the last printed long signal, except a regular or strong upgrade bypasses cooldown when the last printed strength is lower. Strength order is early, regular, strong.
Calculation: Short signal event = the corresponding short candidate changes from false on the prior chart bar to true now AND passes the mirrored repeat gate using the last printed side, strength, and bars since the last printed short signal.
Calculation: Printed signal side = among long and short signal events, select the side with the higher event strength; on equal strength select long when bullish confidence >= bearish confidence, otherwise short.
Calculation: Bullish sentiment score = projection of points: zone bullish event, bullish resistance break or active bull-flag/breakout-retest pattern, buy post-retest or bullish breakout-retest pattern, qualifying long pattern, bullish reversal pattern, bullish continuation pattern, bullish confidence > bearish confidence, bullish MTF count > bearish MTF count, bullish ribbon, fractal ribbon bullish, and any long strong/core/early candidate. Use the code-assigned weights for each term.
Calculation: Bearish sentiment score = the mirrored weighted sum of zone bearish event, bearish support break or active bear-flag/breakdown-retest pattern, sell post-retest or bearish breakdown-retest pattern, qualifying short pattern, bearish reversal pattern, bearish continuation pattern, bearish confidence > bullish confidence, bearish MTF count > bullish MTF count, bearish ribbon, fractal ribbon bearish, and any short strong/core/early candidate, using the same corresponding weights.
Calculation: Active sentiment side = long when bullish sentiment score >= sentiment minimum AND exceeds bearish sentiment score by the sentiment margin; short when bearish sentiment score >= sentiment minimum AND exceeds bullish sentiment score by that margin; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Active plan side = long if any long strong candidate, long core, or long early candidate; otherwise short if any short strong candidate, short core, or short early candidate; otherwise active sentiment side.
Calculation: Forecast side = active plan side when non-neutral; otherwise active sentiment side when non-neutral; otherwise active pattern side when non-neutral and its score >= forecast pattern-score threshold; otherwise long when bullish confidence > bearish confidence, short when bearish confidence > bullish confidence, or neutral on a tie.
Calculation: Bullish projection score = sum the code-assigned weights for active long sentiment, bullish zone event, buy-zone exit, buy post-retest, bullish breakout event, bullish breakout-retest, active long pattern (higher weight above the pattern-rank threshold), bullish reversal pattern, bullish continuation pattern, bullish MTF agreement, bullish MTF count > bearish MTF count, bullish confidence > bearish confidence, fractal ribbon bullish, bullish ribbon, and the strongest available long strong/core/early candidate. Bearish projection score uses the mirrored conditions and corresponding weights.
Calculation: Projected direction = bullish projection score minus bearish projection score; strong bullish above the strong projection threshold, bullish above the bullish projection threshold, strong bearish below the negative strong threshold, bearish below the negative bullish threshold, otherwise neutral.
