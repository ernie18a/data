Calculation: For an N-candle pattern, C1 through CN are consecutive chart bars, oldest to newest, with CN = bar[1] and C1 = bar[N].
Calculation: TrendUp(N) = selected trend price[N+1] > selected moving average[N+1]; when both moving averages are selected, use the shorter-lookback average > the longer-lookback average, except use MA1 when their lengths match or both are symmetrical; TrendDown(N) = NOT TrendUp(N). MA1 and MA2 have separate type, source, and lookback parameters; supported types are SMA, EMA, VWMA, WMA, HMA, SWMA, SMMA, ALMA, and linear regression, with separate ALMA parameters and linear-regression offsets where applicable.
Calculation: Tall(Cn) = selected candle size >= tall multiplier × mean(selected candle size over the tall sample lookback bars immediately preceding Cn); selected size is high − low or abs(close − open).
Calculation: Doji(Cn) = abs(close − open) / (high − low) <= doji body tolerance. Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; body midpoint = (open + close) / 2; body position = (body midpoint − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Engulfs(C2,C1) = C2 strictly encloses C1 by body, range, or either, according to the engulfing setting; equality at the boundaries is allowed only when inclusive engulfing is enabled. Within(x,a,b) = x lies between a and b, with endpoints included only when the open/close inclusion setting is enabled.
Calculation: In BREAKOUT mode, a detected pattern's upper and lower targets are respectively the highest high and lowest low of its pattern candles; within the breakout-candle limit, a current close >= the upper target confirms an upward breakout and a current close <= the lower target confirms a downward breakout.
Long: In CLASSIC mode, an enabled and displayed bullish pattern is detected: Hammer or Takuri Line (downtrend; hammer-shaped candle, discarded wick <= hammer tolerance × abs(body), used wick >= 2 × abs(body), or >= 3 × abs(body) for Takuri); Hammer Inverted (downtrend; prior candle bearish and tall, latest candle has the inverted hammer shape); White Long Day (bullish candle whose selected range or body is >= 3 × the prior tall-sample average); Gapping Up Doji (uptrend, Doji, and candle low > prior candle high); Dragonfly Doji; Bullish Engulfing or Last Engulfing Bottom (opposite trend contexts respectively); Bullish Doji Star, Bullish Harami, Bullish Harami Cross, Homing Pigeon, Bullish Kicking, Bullish Separating Lines, Bullish Meeting Lines, Matching Low, Piercing, Above Stomach, Tweezers Bottom, Rising Window, Bullish Abandoned Baby, Upside Gap Three Methods, Three Inside Up, Three Outside Up, Bullish Side-by-Side, Morning Star or Morning Doji Star, Stick Sandwich, Upside Tasuki Gap, Three White Soldiers, Three Stars in the South, Bullish Tri-Star, Unique Three River Bottom, Concealing Baby Swallow, Bullish Three Line Strike, Bullish Breakaway, Ladder Bottom, Mat Hold, or Rising Three Methods, each when its candlestick-pattern conditions are met; OR, in BREAKOUT mode, an enabled and displayed pattern confirms an upward breakout.
Short: In CLASSIC mode, an enabled and displayed bearish pattern is detected: Shooting Star or Hanging Man (uptrend; hammer-shaped candle, discarded wick <= hammer tolerance × abs(body), used wick >= 2 × abs(body)); Shooting Star (Two Lines) (uptrend, prior candle bullish, latest inverted hammer-shaped candle with used wick >= 3 × abs(body)); Black Long Day (bearish candle whose selected range or body is >= 3 × the prior tall-sample average); Gapping Down Doji (downtrend, Doji, and candle high < prior candle low); Gravestone Doji; Bearish Engulfing or Last Engulfing Top (opposite trend contexts respectively); Dark Cloud Cover, Bearish Doji Star, Bearish Harami, Bearish Harami Cross, Bearish Kicking, Bearish Separating Lines, Bearish Meeting Lines, On Neck, In Neck, Below Stomach, Thrusting, Tweezers Top, Two Black Gapping, Falling Window, Bearish Abandoned Baby, Advance Block, Collapsing Doji, Deliberation, Downside Gap Three Methods, Three Inside Down, Three Outside Down, Bearish Side-by-Side, Evening Star or Evening Doji Star, Downside Tasuki Gap, Identical Black Crows, Three Black Crows, Bearish Tri-Star, Two Crows, Upside Gapping Two Crows, Bearish Three Line Strike, Bearish Breakaway, or Falling Three Methods, each when its candlestick-pattern conditions are met; OR, in BREAKOUT mode, an enabled and displayed pattern confirms a downward breakout.
