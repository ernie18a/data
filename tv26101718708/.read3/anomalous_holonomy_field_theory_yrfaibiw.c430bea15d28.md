Calculation: Bias = IF(close > EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), 1, IF(close < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback), -1, 0))
Calculation: Core = Bias * base weight * IF(Bias * (close - EMA(close, longer EMA lookback)) > 0, longer EMA factor, 1) + IF(Bias * ROC(close, ROC lookback) > 0, ROC(close, ROC lookback) / ROC divisor, 0) + IF(volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiple, Bias * volume bonus, 0) + MIN(VWAP clamp, MAX(-VWAP clamp, 100 * (close - VWAP(hlc3)) / close))
Calculation: Strength = score amplifier * (Core + IF(Core > 0, IF(RSI(close, RSI lookback) < strong oversold level, strong RSI weight, IF(RSI(close, RSI lookback) < mild oversold level, mild RSI weight, 0)), IF(Core < 0, -IF(RSI(close, RSI lookback) > strong overbought level, strong RSI weight, IF(RSI(close, RSI lookback) > mild overbought level, mild RSI weight, 0)), 0)) + IF(Core > 0 AND MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, MACD signal lookback).line > MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, MACD signal lookback).signal, MACD weight, IF(Core < 0 AND MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, MACD signal lookback).line < MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, MACD signal lookback).signal, -MACD weight, 0)))
Calculation: Cutoff = sensitivity * mode multiplier * IF(NATR(ATR lookback) < PercentileNearestRank(NATR(ATR lookback), percentile lookback, low percentile), low volatility factor, IF(NATR(ATR lookback) > PercentileNearestRank(NATR(ATR lookback), percentile lookback, high percentile), high volatility factor, 1))
Calculation: SessionHour = hour(datetime, session timezone)
Calculation: EntryGate = (NOT smart entry timing enabled OR bars since last entry >= minimum bars between entries) AND entries today < daily entry cap AND (IF(session start < session end, SessionHour >= session start AND SessionHour < session end, SessionHour >= session start OR SessionHour < session end)) AND (weekend trading enabled OR dayofweek(datetime, session timezone) is a weekday)
Calculation: StopLong = MAX(entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) * long stop ATR multiple * stop mode multiplier, MIN(Lowest(low, swing lookback), (high[1] + low[1] + close[1]) / 3 - pivot fraction * (Highest(high, range lookback) - Lowest(low, range lookback)))), with all terms taken at the entry bar
Calculation: StopShort = MIN(entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) * short stop ATR multiple * stop mode multiplier, MAX(Highest(high, swing lookback), (high[1] + low[1] + close[1]) / 3 + pivot fraction * (Highest(high, range lookback) - Lowest(low, range lookback)))), with all terms taken at the entry bar
Long: Strength >= Cutoff * strong entry factor AND position is flat AND EntryGate AND (NOT confirmation filter enabled OR (close > EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > confirmation RSI lower bound AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < confirmation RSI upper bound))
Short: Strength <= -Cutoff * strong entry factor AND position is flat AND EntryGate AND (NOT confirmation filter enabled OR (close < EMA(close, shorter EMA lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > confirmation RSI lower bound AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < confirmation RSI upper bound))
Long: Strength >= Cutoff AND EntryGate
Short: Strength <= -Cutoff AND EntryGate
Exit: long, bars since entry > max bars in trade
Exit: short, bars since entry > max bars in trade
Exit: long, low <= StopLong
Exit: short, high >= StopShort
Exit: long, high >= entry price + (entry price - StopLong) * target multiple
Exit: short, low <= entry price - (StopShort - entry price) * target multiple
Exit: long, advanced exits enabled AND highest high since entry >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry bar * trail activation multiple AND low <= highest high since entry - ATR(ATR lookback) at entry bar * trail offset multiple
Exit: short, advanced exits enabled AND lowest low since entry <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback) at entry bar * trail activation multiple AND high >= lowest low since entry + ATR(ATR lookback) at entry bar * trail offset multiple
