Calculation: MA slope = moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) - moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback)[slope lookback]
Calculation: usable ATR = max(ATR(ATR lookback) when available, otherwise true range, minimum tick)
Calculation: retest band = usable ATR * band-width multiplier selected by the sensitivity parameter
Calculation: invalidation level for long = moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) - retest band * invalidation multiple
Calculation: invalidation level for short = moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) + retest band * invalidation multiple
Calculation: bar range = max(high - low, minimum tick)
Calculation: close location = clamp((close - low) / bar range, 0, 1)
Calculation: lower wick length = max(min(open, close) - low, 0)
Calculation: upper wick length = max(high - max(open, close), 0)
Calculation: relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback) when volume and its average are available and the average is positive, otherwise the relative-volume fallback parameter
Calculation: reaction score = clamp(slope weight * clamp(abs(MA slope / usable ATR) * slope-score scale, score floor, score ceiling) + depth weight * clamp(score ceiling - abs(retest extreme - moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback)) / retest band * depth-distance penalty - penetration / retest band * penetration penalty, score floor, score ceiling) + wick weight * clamp((lower wick length for long retests or upper wick length for short retests) / bar range * wick-score scale, score floor, score ceiling) + close weight * clamp((close location for long retests or 1 - close location for short retests) * score ceiling + (favorable-close bonus when close is on the favorable side of the moving average, otherwise minus wrong-side-close penalty), score floor, score ceiling) + volume weight * clamp((relative volume - relative-volume base) / relative-volume span * score ceiling, score floor, score ceiling), score floor, score ceiling); retest extreme is low for long and high for short, and penetration is max(MA - low, 0) for long or max(high - MA, 0) for short
Long: moving average and its slope history and ATR are available AND close > moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) AND MA slope > usable ATR * significant-slope parameter
Short: moving average and its slope history and ATR are available AND close < moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) AND MA slope < -usable ATR * significant-slope parameter
Long: bullish trend condition AND highest(close - moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback), retest lookback) > usable ATR * prior-move-away multiplier selected by the sensitivity parameter AND low <= moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) + retest band AND close >= moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) - retest band
Short: bearish trend condition AND highest(moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) - close, retest lookback) > usable ATR * prior-move-away multiplier selected by the sensitivity parameter AND high >= moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) - retest band AND close <= moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) + retest band
Long: bullish retest condition AND close >= moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) + retest band * confirmation offset AND reaction score >= max(READY-score parameter - WATCH-score gap parameter, WATCH-score floor parameter) AND no prior planner event or chart bars since the prior planner event >= cooldown parameter, on a completed chart bar
Short: bearish retest condition AND close <= moving average(price source, selected moving-average type, moving-average lookback) - retest band * confirmation offset AND reaction score >= max(READY-score parameter - WATCH-score gap parameter, WATCH-score floor parameter) AND no prior planner event or chart bars since the prior planner event >= cooldown parameter, on a completed chart bar
Exit: long, an active long retest plan is within the retest lookback AND close < its stored invalidation level, on a completed chart bar
Exit: short, an active short retest plan is within the retest lookback AND close > its stored invalidation level, on a completed chart bar
Long: an active long retest plan is within the retest lookback AND close >= its stored invalidation level AND close - its trigger close >= usable ATR * follow-through parameter, on a completed chart bar
Short: an active short retest plan is within the retest lookback AND close <= its stored invalidation level AND its trigger close - close >= usable ATR * follow-through parameter, on a completed chart bar
