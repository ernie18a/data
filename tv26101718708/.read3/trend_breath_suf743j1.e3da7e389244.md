Calculation: Source OHLC = regular or Heikin-Ashi OHLC, according to the source setting
Calculation: Fast smoothed range = EMA(Williams %R(source high, source low, source close, fast lookback; midpoint when the range is zero), fast smoothing)
Calculation: Slow smoothed range = EMA(Williams %R(source high, source low, source close, slow lookback; midpoint when the range is zero), slow smoothing)
Calculation: Bullish trend tally = count of: (EMA(source close, trend lookback) - its value slope lookback chart bars ago) / ATR(ATR lookback) >= minimum slope; consecutive chart-bar closes above that EMA >= persistence lookback; ADX(DMI lookback) >= trend strength threshold AND +DI(DMI lookback) > -DI(DMI lookback); last completed T1 close > EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback) AND that EMA > its value on the preceding completed T1 bar; slow smoothed range > its midpoint. Compute the T1 values on T1 bars and sample the last completed T1 bar on chart bars.
Calculation: Bearish trend tally = count of: (EMA(source close, trend lookback) - its value slope lookback chart bars ago) / ATR(ATR lookback) <= -minimum slope; consecutive chart-bar closes below that EMA >= persistence lookback; ADX(DMI lookback) >= trend strength threshold AND -DI(DMI lookback) > +DI(DMI lookback); last completed T1 close < EMA(close, T1 EMA lookback) AND that EMA < its value on the preceding completed T1 bar; slow smoothed range < its midpoint. Compute the T1 values on T1 bars and sample the last completed T1 bar on chart bars.
Calculation: Largest bearish candle body = highest(max(source open - source close, 0), shock lookback)
Calculation: Largest bullish candle body = highest(max(source close - source open, 0), shock lookback)
Calculation: Initial long stop = min((for a pullback, lowest source low over swing lookback ending on the signal bar; for an exhaustion reversal, lowest source low in the qualifying oversold run) - ATR at signal bar * stop buffer, entry price - ATR at signal bar * minimum stop multiple)
Calculation: Initial short stop = max((for a pullback, highest source high over swing lookback ending on the signal bar; for an exhaustion reversal, highest source high in the qualifying overbought run) + ATR at signal bar * stop buffer, entry price + ATR at signal bar * minimum stop multiple)
Calculation: Risk distance = abs(entry price - initial stop)
Calculation: Long target 1 = entry price + risk distance * first target multiple
Calculation: Long target 2 = entry price + risk distance * second target multiple, where the second target multiple is greater than the first
Calculation: Short target 1 = entry price - risk distance * first target multiple
Calculation: Short target 2 = entry price - risk distance * second target multiple, where the second target multiple is greater than the first
Long: (fast smoothed range crosses above the Williams %R lower bound plus pullback threshold while source close > EMA(source close, trend lookback), bullish trend tally >= minimum trend score, and the long pullback is armed; then within the next pullback confirmation bars source close > source open while those trend conditions remain true, fast smoothed range remains above that boundary, and largest bearish candle body < shock multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback)) OR (fast and slow smoothed ranges both remain within the upper exhaustion zone for at least the minimum zone duration; when the combined zone ends with ADX(DMI lookback) < maximum exhaustion ADX, then within the next reversal confirmation bars source close > the high of the zone-exit bar, largest bearish candle body < shock multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback), and the upper exhaustion zone has not re-entered); AND more than the long cooldown bars have elapsed since the last long report and more than the opposite-lock bars since the last short report; AND no simultaneous short report; AND upside tracking is enabled; AND the configured signal evaluation gate is satisfied. The long pullback starts armed, is disarmed by any long report, and is rearmed when fast smoothed range returns to or below the pullback boundary.
Short: (fast smoothed range crosses below the Williams %R upper bound minus pullback threshold while source close < EMA(source close, trend lookback), bearish trend tally >= minimum trend score, and the short pullback is armed; then within the next pullback confirmation bars source close < source open while those trend conditions remain true, fast smoothed range remains below that boundary, and largest bullish candle body < shock multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback)) OR (fast and slow smoothed ranges both remain within the lower exhaustion zone for at least the minimum zone duration; when the combined zone ends with ADX(DMI lookback) < maximum exhaustion ADX, then within the next reversal confirmation bars source close < the low of the zone-exit bar, largest bullish candle body < shock multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback), and the lower exhaustion zone has not re-entered); AND more than the short cooldown bars have elapsed since the last short report and more than the opposite-lock bars since the last long report; AND no simultaneous long report; AND downside tracking is enabled; AND the configured signal evaluation gate is satisfied. The short pullback starts armed, is disarmed by any short report, and is rearmed when fast smoothed range returns to or above the pullback boundary.
Exit: long, on a permitted evaluation bar after the entry bar, before target 1 has been reached, source low <= initial long stop OR source high >= long target 2; after target 1 has been reached, source low <= entry price OR source high >= long target 2
Exit: short, on a permitted evaluation bar after the entry bar, before target 1 has been reached, source high >= initial short stop OR source low <= short target 2; after target 1 has been reached, source high >= entry price OR source low <= short target 2
