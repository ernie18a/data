Calculation: Trend score = clip((current EMA − EMA from a longer lookback ago) / (current ATR × scale), −1, 1), using the same timeframe bar as the state.
Calculation: Momentum score = clip((RSI − 50) / 25, −1, 1), using the same timeframe bar as the state.
Calculation: Direction score = 0.55 × trend score + 0.45 × momentum score.
Calculation: Confirmed state direction = bullish when all scores exist, quiet-volatility threshold is below hot-volatility threshold, trend and momentum are not both at least the conflict threshold in magnitude with opposite signs, and direction score ≥ 0.45 × directional threshold; bearish when those conditions hold and direction score ≤ −0.45 × directional threshold; otherwise neutral. For each enabled, unique reference timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, use its last completed bar (or the chart bar once confirmed).
Long: Current chart bar is confirmed AND at least one enabled, unique reference timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe exists AND every such timeframe has a bullish confirmed state AND the previous chart bar did not have every such timeframe in a bullish confirmed state.
Short: Current chart bar is confirmed AND at least one enabled, unique reference timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe exists AND every such timeframe has a bearish confirmed state AND the previous chart bar did not have every such timeframe in a bearish confirmed state.
