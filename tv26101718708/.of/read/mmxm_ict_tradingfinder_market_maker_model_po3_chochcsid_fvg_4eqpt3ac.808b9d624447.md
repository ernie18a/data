Calculation: Higher-timeframe sweep = On each last completed higher-timeframe bar, confirm symmetric pivot highs and lows and retain their prices and timestamps. Examine all retained pivots if there are at least two, or the most recent configured number if more than that number exist. For each unconsumed, uninvalidated pivot high, a sweep occurs when high > pivot high - ATR × coefficient AND close < pivot high + ATR × coefficient; otherwise invalidate it when close > pivot high + ATR × coefficient. For each unconsumed, uninvalidated pivot low, a sweep occurs when low < pivot low + ATR × coefficient AND close > pivot low - ATR × coefficient; otherwise invalidate it when close < pivot low - ATR × coefficient. Consume swept pivots. If several same-side pivots sweep on one bar, select the oldest examined pivot. ATR is calculated on the higher timeframe. Emit each sweep once on the chart bar receiving that completed higher-timeframe result.
Calculation: Structure level = Maintain the latest chart-timeframe pivot high and low confirmed with a longer left lookback than right lookback. On a higher-timeframe low sweep whose selected pivot timestamp differs from the preceding low sweep's selected timestamp, set the bullish structure level and its originating bar to the latest confirmed chart pivot high and arm it. On a higher-timeframe high sweep whose selected pivot timestamp differs from the preceding high sweep's selected timestamp, set the bearish structure level and its originating bar to the latest confirmed chart pivot low and arm it. Initialize each level and originating bar to zero and each side armed. Disarm a side immediately after its structure decision; a qualifying new sweep rearms it.
Long: Bullish structure level is armed AND close >= bullish structure level AND current bar index - bullish structure level's originating bar index <= structure validity period.
Short: Bearish structure level is armed AND previous bar's close <= bearish structure level AND previous bar index - bearish structure level's originating bar index <= structure validity period.
