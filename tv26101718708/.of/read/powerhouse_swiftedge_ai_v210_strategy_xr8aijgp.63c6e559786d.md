Calculation: Trend(T) = +1 if the chart close is above both the EMA of close and session VWAP of typical price on timeframe T; -1 if it is below both; otherwise 0. The selected timeframes are higher than or equal to the chart timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the completed higher-timeframe bar with historical lookahead, including its final values from the start of that bar; in real time use the bar still forming. If either requested indicator is unavailable, substitute its chart-timeframe equivalent.
Calculation: Momentum = 100 × (close - previous close) / previous close.
Calculation: Momentum threshold = base threshold × (1 + 2 × volatility), where volatility is ATR / close; if ATR is unavailable after the first bar, use high - low in place of ATR.
Calculation: Preliminary threshold = momentum threshold × preliminary factor × (1 - volatility / 2).
Calculation: Latest pivot high or low = the most recently confirmed pivot high or low, respectively, using equal numbers of bars on both sides; update it on the confirmation bar. A cross above means the first series is greater than the second now and was less than or equal to it on the previous bar; a cross below reverses those inequalities.
Calculation: Prediction score(T) = Trend(T) + one half if the change in T's close over a momentum lookback is positive, minus one half if negative, plus one half if T's ATR exceeds its SMA. Use the same higher-timeframe bar and lookahead behavior as Trend(T); substitute chart-timeframe ATR or its SMA when unavailable and zero for unavailable momentum. Evaluate each selected timeframe independently.
Calculation: Signal state = the direction, bar index, and restriction-timeframe trend recorded by the most recent main entry signal; initially the direction is neutral and the signal distance requirement is satisfied. Update all three after each main entry signal. The restriction timeframe follows the same bar and lookahead behavior as Trend(T).
Long: Momentum filter is disabled OR momentum > momentum threshold; AND bars since the last main entry signal >= minimum signal distance; AND higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR Trend(selected higher timeframe) = +1; AND lower-timeframe filter is disabled OR Trend(selected lower timeframe) = +1; AND volume filter is disabled OR current volume > its longer-period SMA AND the shorter-period volume SMA > its previous value; AND breakout filter is disabled OR close > the highest high over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar; AND repeated-signal restriction is disabled OR last main entry direction != long OR restriction-timeframe trend != its value at the last main entry AND restriction-timeframe trend != +1.
Short: Momentum filter is disabled OR momentum < -momentum threshold; AND bars since the last main entry signal >= minimum signal distance; AND higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR Trend(selected higher timeframe) = -1; AND lower-timeframe filter is disabled OR Trend(selected lower timeframe) = -1; AND volume filter is disabled OR current volume > its longer-period SMA AND the shorter-period volume SMA > its previous value; AND breakout filter is disabled OR close < the lowest low over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar; AND repeated-signal restriction is disabled OR last main entry direction != short OR restriction-timeframe trend != its value at the last main entry AND restriction-timeframe trend != -1.
Long: Momentum filter is enabled AND momentum > preliminary threshold AND momentum < momentum threshold OR momentum filter is disabled AND bars since the last main entry signal >= minimum signal distance AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR Trend(selected higher timeframe) = +1) AND (lower-timeframe filter is disabled OR Trend(selected lower timeframe) = +1) AND (volume filter is disabled OR current volume > its longer-period SMA AND the shorter-period volume SMA > its previous value) AND (breakout filter is disabled OR close > the highest high over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar).
Short: Momentum filter is enabled AND momentum < -preliminary threshold AND momentum > -momentum threshold OR momentum filter is disabled AND bars since the last main entry signal >= minimum signal distance AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR Trend(selected higher timeframe) = -1) AND (lower-timeframe filter is disabled OR Trend(selected lower timeframe) = -1) AND (volume filter is disabled OR current volume > its longer-period SMA AND the shorter-period volume SMA > its previous value) AND (breakout filter is disabled OR close < the lowest low over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar).
Long: High crosses above the latest pivot low AND close > open.
Short: Low crosses below the latest pivot high AND close < open.
Long: High crosses above the previous bar's latest pivot high AND close > open.
Short: Low crosses below the previous bar's latest pivot low AND close < open.
Long: Trend(T) = +1.
Short: Trend(T) = -1.
Long: Prediction score(T) > one half.
Short: Prediction score(T) < minus one half.
Exit: long, high >= the main long signal bar's close + take-profit distance OR low <= the main long signal bar's close - stop-loss distance.
Exit: short, low <= the main short signal bar's close - take-profit distance OR high >= the main short signal bar's close + stop-loss distance.
