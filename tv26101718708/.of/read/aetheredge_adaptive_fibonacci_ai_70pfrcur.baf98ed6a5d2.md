Calculation: Swing-based Fibonacci levels = retracement and extension prices projected from the latest confirmed swing high and low
Calculation: Breakout probability = percentage of prior touches of a level followed by a close beyond it in the approach direction; use a neutral probability when there were no prior touches
Long: close is below a Fibonacci level AND price is near the level AND (breakout probability is low OR breakout probability is high)
Short: close is above a Fibonacci level AND price is near the level AND (breakout probability is low OR breakout probability is high)
Exit: Long, high reaches an upward Fibonacci extension
Exit: Short, low reaches a downward Fibonacci extension
