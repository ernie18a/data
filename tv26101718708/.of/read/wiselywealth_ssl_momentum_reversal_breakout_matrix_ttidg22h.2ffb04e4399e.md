Calculation: SSL state = Apply the same selectable moving-average type and lookback to high and low. Set state to +1 when close is above the high moving average, otherwise to -1 when close is below the low moving average, otherwise retain the previous state; initialize to +1. The SSL line is the low moving average in state +1 and the high moving average in state -1.
Calculation: Normalized channel width = absolute difference between the high and low moving averages divided by ATR when ATR is positive; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Directional score = For direction d, where d is +1 for long and -1 for short, use independent configurable weights and thresholds as follows. Let scale(x,a,b) = clamp((x-a)/(b-a), 0, 1), or zero when b <= a; let range = max(high-low, minimum price increment); body = abs(close-open)/range; close location = clamp((close-low)/range, 0, 1) for long or clamp((high-close)/range, 0, 1) for short; breakout = max(close-high moving average, 0)/ATR for long or max(low moving average-close, 0)/ATR for short, and zero when ATR is not positive; RSI displacement = d times (RSI minus the RSI midpoint); DI dominance = clamp(d times (positive DI minus negative DI) / max(positive DI plus negative DI, a small positive floor), 0, 1); relative volume = volume / SMA(volume) when both are available and the SMA is positive; opposing wick = (high-max(open,close))/range for long or (min(open,close)-low)/range for short; extension = abs(close-SSL line)/ATR when ATR is positive, otherwise zero. Add: a reversal weight when SSL state changed to d from -d, otherwise a smaller non-reversal weight; scaled breakout times its weight; body times its weight; close location times its weight; scaled RSI displacement times its weight; an RSI momentum weight when RSI moves in direction d; a DI direction weight when d times (positive DI minus negative DI) is positive; ADX scaled from the ADX threshold to that threshold plus a configurable offset times its weight; DI dominance times its weight; an ADX momentum weight when ADX rises; relative volume scaled between configurable bounds times its weight, or a configurable fallback when relative volume is unavailable; normalized channel width scaled between configurable multiples of the channel-width threshold times its weight; and a channel-expansion weight when normalized channel width rises. Subtract opposing wick times its penalty weight when opposing wick exceeds its threshold, and subtract extension scaled between configurable bounds times its penalty weight. Clamp the result between configurable score bounds and round to the nearest integer. Use the same weights and thresholds for both directions. Select the ADX threshold, channel-width threshold, and minimum score together from a configurable signal profile.
Long: On a completed chart bar, SSL state changes from -1 to +1 AND RSI is above its midpoint AND positive DI is above negative DI AND ADX is above its profile threshold AND normalized channel width is at least its profile threshold AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR the SSL state of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is +1) AND long directional score is at least the profile minimum score.
Short: On a completed chart bar, SSL state changes from +1 to -1 AND RSI is below its midpoint AND negative DI is above positive DI AND ADX is above its profile threshold AND normalized channel width is at least its profile threshold AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR the SSL state of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is -1) AND short directional score is at least the profile minimum score.
