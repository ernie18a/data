Calculation: Liquidity trendline = EMA of the configured price source over the lookback, then SMA of that EMA over the same lookback; add and subtract an EMA of absolute close-to-basis distance over three times the lookback, multiplied by a scale factor, to form upper and lower bands. Trend switches bullish when close exceeds the upper band and bearish when close falls below the lower band; otherwise it persists. The line is the lower band in a bullish trend and the upper band otherwise.
Calculation: Heatmap trail = EMA of true range times a scale factor. Bull stop is high minus this offset; bear stop is low plus it. The bull stop carries forward as the maximum of its current value and prior bull stop when prior close exceeded prior bull stop; otherwise it resets to its current value. The bear stop carries forward as the minimum of its current value and prior bear stop when prior close was below prior bear stop; otherwise it resets to its current value. Trend turns bullish when close exceeds the prior bear stop, bearish when close falls below the prior bull stop, and otherwise persists. The trail is the bull stop in a bullish trend and the bear stop otherwise.
Calculation: BASS bullish votes = count of positive MACD histogram, RSI minus 50, smoothed stochastic K minus D, Awesome Oscillator, Alligator lips minus jaw, and, when enabled, VWMA minus SMA of typical price. Bearish votes count the corresponding negative values. BASS direction requires at least the configured vote threshold and more votes in that direction than the opposite direction. The configured threshold may require all votes.
Calculation: Volume-adjusted price index = 100 × SMA(volume × (2×close−high−low)/(high−low), lookback) / SMA(volume, lookback); use zero for the per-bar price term when high equals low. Its long condition is positive and, when slope confirmation is enabled, no lower than its prior value; its short condition is negative and, when enabled, no higher than its prior value.
Calculation: All requested-timeframe values use their configured timeframe, which can be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe. With lookahead off, use the available current bar while that requested-timeframe bar is forming and its completed value after it closes.
Long: Trigger-timeframe close is above the liquidity trendline and heatmap trail when trigger confirmation requires both, or above at least one when it requires one; the count of bullish trigger, confirmation, and enabled third-timeframe states is at least the configured minimum and exceeds the bearish count; ADX passes its configured minimum and optional non-falling requirement when its filter is enabled; and each enabled bias, BASS, and mandatory VAPI filter is bullish. Each timeframe state is bullish when its close is above the liquidity trendline or heatmap trail, according to the configured one-or-both confirmation rule. Bias is bullish when the configured EMA and/or VWAP conditions hold: fast EMA above slow EMA and/or close above session VWAP. VAPI is mandatory only when its filter is enabled in mandatory mode; in warning mode it does not restrict entry. Enter only when not already long.
Short: Trigger-timeframe close is below the liquidity trendline and heatmap trail when trigger confirmation requires both, or below at least one when it requires one; the count of bearish trigger, confirmation, and enabled third-timeframe states is at least the configured minimum and exceeds the bullish count; ADX passes its configured minimum and optional non-falling requirement when its filter is enabled; and each enabled bias, BASS, and mandatory VAPI filter is bearish. Each timeframe state is bearish when its close is below the liquidity trendline or heatmap trail, according to the configured one-or-both confirmation rule. Bias is bearish when the configured EMA and/or VWAP conditions hold: fast EMA below slow EMA and/or close below session VWAP. VAPI is mandatory only when its filter is enabled in mandatory mode; in warning mode it does not restrict entry. Enter only when not already short.
Exit: Long, when smart exits are enabled and the configured exit mode is opposite signal, alignment loss, or either: the short trigger and timeframe-count conditions hold, or the bullish timeframe count falls below the configured minimum.
Exit: Short, when smart exits are enabled and the configured exit mode is opposite signal, alignment loss, or either: the long trigger and timeframe-count conditions hold, or the bearish timeframe count falls below the configured minimum.
