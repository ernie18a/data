Calculation: Timeframe sampling = For each configurable calculation timeframe, use the chart bar if equal to the chart timeframe, the last completed bar if higher, or the last available completed intrabar within the chart bar if lower. Apply each calculation in its own timeframe before sampling. Trigger, confirmation, optional third confirmation, ADX, bias, oscillator, volume-pressure and both volume-context timeframes are independently configurable.
Calculation: Liquidity line = Let basis be SMA of EMA of the selected OHLC price source, using the same lookback for both. Deviation is a multiplier times EMA of abs(close minus basis), with a longer lookback. Upper and lower bands are basis plus and minus deviation. Initialize trend to neutral; set it positive when close > upper, negative when close < lower, and otherwise retain it. The line is the lower band when trend is positive and the upper band otherwise.
Calculation: Heat trail = Offset is a multiplier times EMA of true range. Bull stop is high minus offset; bear stop is low plus offset. Initialize the rising trail to bull stop and the falling trail to bear stop. Subsequently, if previous close > previous rising trail, rising trail is max(bull stop, previous rising trail), otherwise bull stop; if previous close < previous falling trail, falling trail is min(bear stop, previous falling trail), otherwise bear stop. Initialize trend positive; set it positive if close > previous falling trail, otherwise negative if close < previous rising trail, otherwise retain it. Heat trail is the rising trail for positive trend and the falling trail otherwise.
Calculation: Timeframe direction counts = For each active trigger, confirmation and optional third timeframe, compare close with its liquidity line and heat trail. The trigger qualifies in a direction when both comparisons or, under the alternative configurable mode, at least one comparison supports that direction. Each confirmation timeframe independently qualifies when both comparisons or, under its configurable mode, at least one supports that direction. Count qualifying timeframes separately for each direction; the required count is the configured minimum capped at the number of active timeframes.
Calculation: Oscillator direction counts = On the oscillator timeframe count the positive and negative values separately among: MACD histogram; RSI minus its midpoint; smoothed stochastic K minus its SMA signal, with the stochastic and two smoothing lookbacks configurable; short-lookback SMA of (high+low)/2 minus its longer-lookback SMA; short-lookback SMMA of (high+low)/2 minus its longer-lookback SMMA; and, optionally, VWMA minus SMA of (high+low+close)/3 using equal lookbacks. Multiply each component by its own nonnegative configurable scale before counting. The required count is configurable or all enabled components, capped at their number.
Calculation: Volume pressure = Sum over a configurable lookback of volume times (2*close-high-low)/(high-low), divided by the sum of volume over that lookback and multiplied by 100; use zero for a bar whose high equals low and use zero when the volume sum is zero.
Calculation: Volume zone = On each volume-context timeframe, retain (high+low+close)/3 from the most recent bar whose volume exceeds a configurable multiple of its volume SMA; initialize it to the current (high+low+close)/3 until such a bar occurs. The volume midpoint is VWMA of (high+low+close)/3; the normal midpoint is its SMA with the same lookback.
Long: On any active trigger or confirmation timeframe, close > liquidity line.
Short: On any active trigger or confirmation timeframe, close < liquidity line.
Long: On any active trigger or confirmation timeframe, close > heat trail.
Short: On any active trigger or confirmation timeframe, close < heat trail.
Long: On any active trigger or confirmation timeframe, close > liquidity line AND close > heat trail, OR under the configurable at-least-one mode, close > liquidity line OR close > heat trail.
Short: On any active trigger or confirmation timeframe, close < liquidity line AND close < heat trail, OR under the configurable at-least-one mode, close < liquidity line OR close < heat trail.
Long: On the bias timeframe, shorter-lookback close EMA > longer-lookback close EMA.
Short: On the bias timeframe, shorter-lookback close EMA < longer-lookback close EMA.
Long: On the bias timeframe, close > daily-session VWAP of (high+low+close)/3.
Short: On the bias timeframe, close < daily-session VWAP of (high+low+close)/3.
Long: Positive oscillator count >= required oscillator count AND positive oscillator count > negative oscillator count.
Short: Negative oscillator count >= required oscillator count AND negative oscillator count > positive oscillator count.
Long: Volume pressure > 0 AND, if its slope requirement is enabled, volume pressure >= its previous value.
Short: Volume pressure < 0 AND, if its slope requirement is enabled, volume pressure <= its previous value.
Long: On either volume-context timeframe, close > volume midpoint AND volume midpoint > normal midpoint.
Short: On either volume-context timeframe, close < volume midpoint AND volume midpoint < normal midpoint.
Long: On either volume-context timeframe, abs(close minus volume zone) <= a configurable multiple of ATR AND close > volume zone.
Short: On either volume-context timeframe, abs(close minus volume zone) <= a configurable multiple of ATR AND close < volume zone.
Long: Trigger qualifies long AND long timeframe count >= required timeframe count AND long timeframe count > short timeframe count AND, if enabled, ADX >= its threshold AND, if required, ADX >= its previous value AND, if enabled, the selected EMA bias, VWAP bias or both are long AND, if enabled, positive oscillator count >= required oscillator count AND positive oscillator count > negative oscillator count AND, if mandatory volume-pressure filtering is enabled, volume pressure > 0 AND, if required, volume pressure >= its previous value.
Short: Trigger qualifies short AND short timeframe count >= required timeframe count AND short timeframe count > long timeframe count AND, if enabled, ADX >= its threshold AND, if required, ADX >= its previous value AND, if enabled, the selected EMA bias, VWAP bias or both are short AND, if enabled, negative oscillator count >= required oscillator count AND negative oscillator count > positive oscillator count AND, if mandatory volume-pressure filtering is enabled, volume pressure < 0 AND, if required, volume pressure <= its previous value.
Exit: long, if smart exits are enabled, short trigger qualifies AND short timeframe count >= required timeframe count AND short timeframe count > long timeframe count, OR long timeframe count < required timeframe count; select either or both alternatives according to the configured exit mode.
Exit: short, if smart exits are enabled, long trigger qualifies AND long timeframe count >= required timeframe count AND long timeframe count > short timeframe count, OR short timeframe count < required timeframe count; select either or both alternatives according to the configured exit mode.
