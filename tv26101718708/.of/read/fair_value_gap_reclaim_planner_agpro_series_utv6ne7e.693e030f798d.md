Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago AND current low − high two bars ago ≥ configured minimum gap size (ATR × configured size filter) AND current volume ≥ configured minimum volume.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago − current high ≥ configured minimum gap size (ATR × configured size filter) AND current volume ≥ configured minimum volume.
Calculation: Reclaim reference = active gap midpoint, or its outer edge when full-edge mode is selected.
Calculation: Reclaim quality score = capped sum of normalized contributions from close distance beyond the reclaim reference, rejection wick, candle body size, fill progress closeness to 50%, gap size, gap freshness, and relative volume; omit the relative-volume contribution when disabled. Relative volume = current volume / its configured lookback average.
Long: confirmed bar AND ((bullish fair value gap AND (fresh-gap replacement enabled OR no active gap)) OR (active bullish gap AND interaction with its zone within the configured touch lookback AND close > reclaim reference AND previous close ≤ reclaim reference AND no active reclaim AND reclaim quality score ≥ configured ready threshold)).
Short: confirmed bar AND ((bearish fair value gap AND (fresh-gap replacement enabled OR no active gap)) OR (active bearish gap AND interaction with its zone within the configured touch lookback AND close < reclaim reference AND previous close ≥ reclaim reference AND no active reclaim AND reclaim quality score ≥ configured ready threshold)).
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND active bullish gap AND close < its lower edge minus the configured ATR invalidation buffer.
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND active bearish gap AND close > its upper edge plus the configured ATR invalidation buffer.
