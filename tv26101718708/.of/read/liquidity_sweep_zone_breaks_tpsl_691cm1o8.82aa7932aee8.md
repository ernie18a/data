Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the corresponding highs/lows of N bars before and after it; use four N values ordered from shortest to longest.
Calculation: Sweep zone = the range from the breached pivot level to the breach bar’s wick extreme; create it after a confirmed sweep only if no close from the breach bar through the confirmation bar has crossed beyond that extreme. Keep it active until its first later close beyond the extreme.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR × a multiplier, or entry price × a percentage; stop-loss is entry price minus/plus risk distance for long/short. Take-profit levels are entry price plus/minus risk distance × each of three risk-reward ratios for long/short.
Long: A latest unswept confirmed pivot low is breached (low < pivot low OR close < pivot low), then within the confirmation window a later bar closes above the pivot low AND above its open; cancel the pending setup if an opposite pivot high is breached or the window expires. OR an active sell-sweep zone closes above its wick extreme.
Short: A latest unswept confirmed pivot high is breached (high > pivot high OR close > pivot high), then within the confirmation window a later bar closes below the pivot high AND below its open; cancel the pending setup if an opposite pivot low is breached or the window expires. OR an active buy-sweep zone closes below its wick extreme.
Exit: long, low <= stop-loss OR high >= the furthest enabled take-profit level
Exit: short, high >= stop-loss OR low <= the furthest enabled take-profit level
