Calculation: Sweep setups = Independently for four progressively longer pivot lengths, track the latest confirmed pivot high and low, using equal numbers of bars on each side. Track previously breached pivot prices separately for highs and lows. A high above the latest pivot high starts a pending sell setup if that price has never been breached; a low below the latest pivot low starts a pending buy setup if that price has never been breached. Record the breached level, bar, and that bar's high or low. Each new breach replaces the pending setup and cancels the opposite setup; process a high breach before a low breach. A setup expires after the confirmation window. A breach cannot confirm on its own bar.
Calculation: Sweep zones = On a confirmed sweep, create a zone from its breached pivot level to the breach bar's high for a sell sweep or low for a buy sweep, provided no close from the breach bar through the confirmation bar is beyond that high or low respectively. Reject a zone if any previously created zone of the same sweep direction, including a broken zone, has a pivot level within a price tolerance. Each accepted zone becomes eligible for a break after its confirmation bar and remains eligible until its first break.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at the entry bar multiplied by a parameter, or entry price multiplied by a percentage parameter. Entry price is the signal bar's close. The profit target is entry price plus risk distance times the selected reward-to-risk parameter for a long, or minus it for a short. Select the last enabled target in target order, where target reward-to-risk parameters are progressively larger.
Long: A pending buy setup is within its confirmation window AND close > its breached pivot low AND close > open OR close > the breach-bar high of an eligible sell-sweep zone.
Short: A pending sell setup is within its confirmation window AND close < its breached pivot high AND close < open OR close < the breach-bar low of an eligible buy-sweep zone.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= entry price - risk distance OR high >= profit target).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= entry price + risk distance OR low <= profit target).
