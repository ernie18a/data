Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low at the center of a symmetric lookback window, recognized only after the right-hand window has completed; use close instead of high or low for support and resistance pivots.
Calculation: Trendline slope = a selectable method: ATR divided by the pivot lookback and multiplied by a slope multiplier; standard deviation of close divided by the pivot lookback and multiplied by the multiplier; or the absolute covariance of close and bar index divided by the variance of bar index, divided by 2 and multiplied by the multiplier. The covariance and variance use simple moving averages over the pivot lookback; floor the variance denominator at 0.000001.
Calculation: Upper trendline = initialize to zero; on each confirmed high pivot set it to the pivot high and store the current trendline slope; otherwise subtract the stored slope each bar. Its breakout level is the upper trendline minus the stored slope times the pivot lookback. Reset its breakout state to zero on a new high pivot; otherwise set the state to one when close exceeds the breakout level and retain it until the next high pivot.
Calculation: Lower trendline = initialize to zero; on each confirmed low pivot set it to the pivot low and store the current trendline slope; otherwise add the stored slope each bar. Its breakout level is the lower trendline plus the stored slope times the pivot lookback. Reset its breakout state to zero on a new low pivot; otherwise set the state to one when close is below the breakout level and retain it until the next low pivot.
Calculation: Signed volume = volume when close is above open and negative volume when close is below open; on an equal open and close retain the previous bar's sign, initially positive.
Calculation: Support = the latest confirmed pivot low of close for which current signed volume exceeds the highest signed volume divided by 2.5 over the volume lookback, including the current bar. Resistance = the latest confirmed pivot high of close for which current signed volume is below the lowest signed volume divided by 2.5 over that lookback. Resistance top = resistance plus ATR multiplied by a zone-width multiplier, both captured when resistance is created. Support and resistance persist until replaced; their creation requires support/resistance boxes to be enabled.
Calculation: Resistance break = low crosses above resistance top. Resistance hold = high crosses below resistance. Support hold = low crosses above support. Support break = high crosses below support. A cross above requires the current value to be greater and the previous value to be less than or equal to its respective level; a cross below requires the current value to be less and the previous value to be greater than or equal to its respective level.
Calculation: Zone middle = the average of an EMA of close and an HMA of close with a shorter lookback. Zone top and bottom = zone middle plus and minus the SMA of high minus low. Initialize the zone state bearish; on a completed bar switch it bullish when low is above zone top and the previous low was below the previous zone top, and switch it bearish when high is below zone bottom and the previous high was above the previous zone bottom. Record the current low or high respectively as the trend initiation level at each state change.
Calculation: Smoothed Heikin Ashi open and close = apply a selectable moving-average type and lookback separately to open, high, low and close; calculate Heikin Ashi close as their average and Heikin Ashi open as the average of the previous Heikin Ashi open and close, initialized to the average of the smoothed open and close; then apply a selectable moving-average type and lookback separately to those Heikin Ashi open and close series. The available moving averages are SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, RMA, HMA, DEMA and TEMA.
Calculation: Impulse MACD = let the middle be twice the EMA of typical price minus the EMA of that EMA, and let the upper and lower bounds be the RMA of high and low, all using the same lookback. The impulse value is middle minus upper when middle is above upper, middle minus lower when middle is below lower, and zero otherwise. Its signal is an SMA of the impulse value with a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Bullish EMA swing condition = close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND previous close > previous short EMA AND medium EMA > previous medium EMA AND long EMA > previous long EMA AND RSI > bullish threshold. Bearish EMA swing condition = close < short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA AND previous close < previous short EMA AND medium EMA < previous medium EMA AND long EMA < previous long EMA AND RSI < bearish threshold. All EMAs and RSI use close.
Calculation: Bullish confluence = each enabled filter passes: close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; zone state is bullish; smoothed Heikin Ashi close >= smoothed Heikin Ashi open; impulse value > impulse signal AND impulse value > zero; upper trendline breakout state rises from zero to one; resistance break OR support hold. Bearish confluence = each enabled filter passes: close < short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA; zone state is bearish; smoothed Heikin Ashi close < smoothed Heikin Ashi open; impulse value < impulse signal AND impulse value < zero; lower trendline breakout state rises from zero to one; support break OR resistance hold.
Long: Upper trendline breakout state rises from zero to one.
Short: Lower trendline breakout state rises from zero to one.
Long: Resistance break OR support hold.
Short: Support break OR resistance hold.
Long: Bullish EMA swing condition is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Short: Bearish EMA swing condition is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Long: Zone state is bullish AND trend initiation level exists AND ((close > current trend initiation level AND previous close < previous trend initiation level) OR (low > current trend initiation level AND previous low < previous trend initiation level)) AND bars since the last bullish or bearish zone retest exceed the retest-spacing threshold.
Short: Zone state is bearish AND trend initiation level exists AND ((previous close > current trend initiation level AND close < previous trend initiation level) OR (previous high > current trend initiation level AND high < previous trend initiation level)) AND bars since the last bullish or bearish zone retest exceed the retest-spacing threshold.
Long: Smoothed Heikin Ashi close >= smoothed Heikin Ashi open.
Short: Smoothed Heikin Ashi close < smoothed Heikin Ashi open.
Long: Bullish confluence is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Short: Bearish confluence is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Long: Medium EMA > long EMA AND zone state is bullish.
Short: Medium EMA < long EMA AND zone state is bearish.
