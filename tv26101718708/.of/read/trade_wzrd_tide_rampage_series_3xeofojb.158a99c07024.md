Calculation: Ownership profile = On each chart bar, divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over a lookback including the current bar into equal price rows. For each nonzero-range bar in the lookback, split its volume into buyer volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and seller volume = volume − buyer volume. Allocate each part to every overlapping row in proportion to the overlap length divided by the bar's range. Ignore bars with missing volume or zero range. A row's ownership share is its side's allocated volume divided by its total allocated volume.
Calculation: Defending shelves = The long shelf is the highest row whose buyer ownership share is at least the dominance threshold and whose upper edge is below the current close. The short shelf is the lowest row whose seller ownership share is at least the dominance threshold and whose lower edge is above the current close. Recalculate both from the current profile on every bar.
Calculation: Volume center = The midpoint of the first row, scanning upward, where cumulative profile volume reaches half the profile's total volume; use the range midpoint if total volume is zero. Balance position is the center's fractional position within the profile range.
Calculation: Fuel = Normalize volume × absolute close-to-previous-close change to its rolling minimum and maximum over a lookback including the current bar; use zero when the value is unavailable or the maximum does not exceed the minimum.
Calculation: Shelf eligibility = Remember the row index of the most recent signal separately for each side. A shelf is eligible if its index differs from the remembered index or no index is remembered. After evaluating signals, clear the long index when low is above the current profile's upper edge for that remembered index plus ATR; clear the short index when high is below the current profile's lower edge for that remembered index minus ATR. Remember a new index only when its signal fires.
Calculation: Tier win rate = Divide shelf ownership into ordered dominance tiers using increasing thresholds. Maintain separate attempts and target-hit wins for each side and tier. A tier passes if its attempts are below the minimum sample count OR its wins divided by attempts is at least the minimum win rate. Record an outcome only when the currently tracked trade hits its stop or target; a new signal replaces the tracked trade without recording an outcome for the previous one. When both stop and target are hit on the same bar, record a stop.
Calculation: Trade levels = At a defense signal, use the signal close as entry and freeze the levels. The long stop is the long shelf's lower edge minus an ATR buffer; the short stop is the short shelf's upper edge plus an ATR buffer. If the stop is on the wrong side of entry, replace it with entry minus ATR for a long or entry plus ATR for a short. Risk is the absolute distance from entry to stop. The target is the selected volume center or opposite range edge if it is in the profitable direction, farther than a minimum ATR distance, and no farther than a maximum risk multiple; otherwise use entry plus or minus a fixed risk multiple. The fixed-risk target mode always uses that fallback.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = Maintain a limited rolling list of confirmed pivot highs and, separately, pivot lows, using left and right confirmation lengths. On each bar, cluster each list oldest first: add each pivot to the first cluster whose running mean is within an ATR-based tolerance, updating that mean, or create a cluster. Maintain a limited number of persistent high and low pool slots. Remove a high slot when close exceeds its frozen price or no current high cluster remains within tolerance; mirror this for a low slot. In cluster order, add clusters with at least the minimum touch count and price above close for highs or below close for lows, provided no existing same-side slot is within tolerance and capacity remains. Freeze the cluster mean as the slot price and mark it unswept. After a sweep, mark the slot swept; remove swept slots after a maximum age or when close passes through their price. A subsequently recreated slot is unswept.
Long: A long defending shelf exists AND low <= its upper edge AND close > its upper edge AND shelf eligibility passes AND ATR is available AND the long tier passes AND (balance filter is disabled OR balance position < midpoint) AND fuel >= minimum fuel AND bars since either defense signal >= cooldown, or no earlier defense signal exists.
Short: A short defending shelf exists AND high >= its lower edge AND close < its lower edge AND shelf eligibility passes AND ATR is available AND the short tier passes AND (balance filter is disabled OR balance position > midpoint) AND fuel >= minimum fuel AND bars since either defense signal >= cooldown, or no earlier defense signal exists AND the long defense signal is false.
Long: Relative volume >= absorption threshold AND relative high-low range <= absorption threshold AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= midpoint; use midpoint for the close position when high equals low. Relative volume and range divide the current values by their respective same-length simple moving averages, using one when the corresponding average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Short: Relative volume >= absorption threshold AND relative high-low range <= absorption threshold AND (close − low) / (high − low) < midpoint; use midpoint for the close position when high equals low. Relative volume and range divide the current values by their respective same-length simple moving averages, using one when the corresponding average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Long: An unswept low liquidity pool exists AND low < its frozen price AND close > its frozen price.
Short: An unswept high liquidity pool exists AND high > its frozen price AND close < its frozen price.
Exit: long, a later bar than the defense entry AND (low <= frozen stop OR high >= frozen target), with the stop taking precedence if both are reached.
Exit: short, a later bar than the defense entry AND (high >= frozen stop OR low <= frozen target), with the stop taking precedence if both are reached.
Exit: long, opposite-signal close is enabled AND the short defense signal is true.
Exit: short, opposite-signal close is enabled AND the long defense signal is true.
