Calculation: Ownership range = highest high and lowest low over a lookback; divide it into price rows. For each bar, split volume between buyers and sellers in proportion to the close’s position within its high-low range, then allocate each side’s volume to every overlapped row in proportion to the overlap. A row’s buyer (seller) ownership = its buyer (seller) volume divided by total volume in that row.
Calculation: Buyer shelf = highest row whose buyer ownership meets the dominance threshold and whose top is below the close; seller shelf = lowest row whose seller ownership meets the threshold and whose bottom is above the close.
Calculation: Volume center = midpoint of the row where cumulative total row volume first reaches half of total volume, scanning rows from the range low upward.
Calculation: Kinetic fuel = normalize current volume × absolute change in close against its rolling minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Tier win rate = wins divided by closed trades for the same side and shelf ownership tier; tiers are ownership below 75%, from 75% to below 90%, and at least 90%.
Calculation: Absorption = volume is at least 2 times its 30-bar average AND bar range is at most 0.7 times its 30-bar average; bullish absorption has the close in the upper half of the bar, bearish absorption has it below the midpoint.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = at least two confirmed swing highs, or at least two confirmed swing lows, clustered within an ATR-scaled tolerance; swing detection uses left and right bars. A swing is confirmed only after the right bars have completed.
Long: A buyer shelf exists AND low <= its top AND close > its top AND it is not the same buyer shelf as the last fired long defense AND (same-side tier has fewer than the minimum samples OR its win rate >= the minimum win probability) AND (balance alignment is disabled OR volume center is below the range midpoint) AND (minimum fuel is zero OR kinetic fuel >= minimum fuel) AND cooldown since the last signal has elapsed AND ATR is available AND the bar is confirmed; OR bullish absorption; OR low < a resting equal-low pool AND close > that pool.
Short: A seller shelf exists AND high >= its bottom AND close < its bottom AND it is not the same seller shelf as the last fired short defense AND (same-side tier has fewer than the minimum samples OR its win rate >= the minimum win probability) AND (balance alignment is disabled OR volume center is above the range midpoint) AND (minimum fuel is zero OR kinetic fuel >= minimum fuel) AND cooldown since the last signal has elapsed AND ATR is available AND the bar is confirmed AND no long signal fires on the same bar; OR bearish absorption; OR high > a resting equal-high pool AND close < that pool.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low <= stop price OR high >= target price; if both are reached on the same bar, stop takes precedence.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high >= stop price OR low <= target price; if both are reached on the same bar, stop takes precedence.
Exit: Long, an opposite short signal fires and opposite-signal closing is enabled.
Exit: Short, an opposite long signal fires and opposite-signal closing is enabled.
