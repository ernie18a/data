Calculation: Three moving averages = independently selected EMA, SMA, WMA, HMA, TEMA, DEMA, or ZLEMA of close, each with an independently configurable length; their conventional ordering is shortest, intermediate, and longest, respectively. ZLEMA is EMA of 2 × close − close from round((length − 1) / 2) bars ago.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = with source = Heikin-Ashi close, loss = ATR × configurable multiplier, and previous stop = zero when unavailable: if current and previous source are both above the previous stop, max(previous stop, source − loss); otherwise, if both are below it, min(previous stop, source + loss); otherwise, if current source is above the previous stop, source − loss; otherwise, source + loss.
Calculation: Bias score = sum of enabled votes: Heikin-Ashi contributes +1 when its close > its open, −1 when its close < its open, otherwise 0; Supertrend contributes +1 in its bullish direction and −1 in its bearish direction; the ATR trailing stop contributes +1 when Heikin-Ashi close > the stop, −1 when Heikin-Ashi close < the stop, otherwise 0. Each vote can be independently enabled or disabled; a disabled vote contributes 0.
Long: Shortest moving average crosses above intermediate moving average OR intermediate moving average crosses above longest moving average OR bias score > 0.
Short: Shortest moving average crosses below intermediate moving average OR intermediate moving average crosses below longest moving average OR bias score < 0.
