Calculation: Adaptive trend bands = Let midpoint be (high + low) / 2 and distance be ATR multiplied by a factor. The upper band is the smaller of midpoint + distance and its previous value when previous close is below the previous upper band; otherwise it is midpoint + distance. The lower band is the larger of midpoint - distance and its previous value when previous close is above the previous lower band; otherwise it is midpoint - distance. Set trend to bullish when close is above the previous upper band, bearish when close is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retain its previous state, initially bearish. The trend line is the lower band in a bullish state and the upper band otherwise.
Calculation: Adaptive oscillator = Clamp (close - adaptive trend line) / (upper adaptive trend band - lower adaptive trend band) to [-1, 1]. Its adaptive average equals its previous value plus oscillator squared divided by the ATR lookback, multiplied by the difference between oscillator and its previous adaptive average; initialize it to the oscillator.
Calculation: Moving-average direction = Bullish when the selected colored moving average is greater than each of its values over its direction lookback, bearish when it is less than each of them, and otherwise retain its previous direction, initially bullish.
Calculation: Three-average direction = Bullish when the fast moving average > medium moving average > slow moving average, bearish when fast < medium < slow, and neutral otherwise. If one of the three filters is disabled, compare the other two in the same order. If two are disabled, compare the colored average with the remaining average: bullish if greater, bearish otherwise. If all three are disabled, the three-average filter passes in either direction.
Calculation: Supertrend direction = Form lower and upper bands as midpoint minus and plus a factor times ATR. Retain the greater of the current lower band and previous trailing lower band when previous close > previous trailing lower band; otherwise reset it to the current lower band. Retain the smaller of the current upper band and previous trailing upper band when previous close < previous trailing upper band; otherwise reset it to the current upper band. Set direction bullish when close > previous trailing upper band, bearish when close < previous trailing lower band, and otherwise retain the previous direction, initially bullish.
Calculation: Outside-in bullish condition = Fast moving average crosses above the colored moving average AND the lowest low in the lookback occurred before the current bar AND that low was below the lower Bollinger Band at the low's bar. Outside-in bearish condition = Fast moving average crosses below the colored moving average AND the highest high in the lookback occurred before the current bar AND that high was above the upper Bollinger Band at the high's bar.
Calculation: Three-average bullish state = Moving-average direction is bullish AND three-average direction is bullish AND close has been above the fast moving average at least once during the current uninterrupted run of those two bullish directions. Three-average bearish state = Moving-average direction is bearish AND three-average direction is bearish AND close has been below the fast moving average at least once during the current uninterrupted run of those two bearish directions.
Calculation: Bullish base signal = According to the selected mode, the first bar of the three-average bullish state; OR the first bar when moving-average direction and Supertrend direction are both bullish; OR the first bar when the three-average bullish state and bullish Supertrend direction coincide; OR the outside-in bullish condition, optionally requiring bullish three-average or Supertrend direction; OR a fast moving average cross above the colored moving average, optionally requiring bullish three-average or Supertrend direction; OR the first bar of bullish moving-average direction. Apply only the selected mode. The combined three-average and Supertrend mode requires only bullish moving-average direction and bullish Supertrend direction when all three filters are disabled.
Calculation: Bearish base signal = According to the selected mode, the first bar of the three-average bearish state; OR the first bar when moving-average direction and Supertrend direction are both bearish; OR the first bar when the three-average bearish state and bearish Supertrend direction coincide; OR the outside-in bearish condition, optionally requiring bearish three-average or Supertrend direction; OR a fast moving average cross below the colored moving average, optionally requiring bearish three-average or Supertrend direction; OR the first bar of bearish moving-average direction. Apply only the selected mode. The combined three-average and Supertrend mode requires only bearish moving-average direction and bearish Supertrend direction when all three filters are disabled.
Calculation: Signal buffer = A bullish base signal sets the buffer bullish; a bearish base signal sets it bearish, taking precedence if both occur. It retains its direction until an opposite base signal or the corresponding filtered entry, which clears it.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = Sum of typical price multiplied by volume divided by sum of volume over a rolling time window, retaining at least a specified minimum number of chart bars. Retain the last direction of its comparison with the medium moving average when they are equal.
Calculation: Chaikin volatility = Rate of change of the EMA of high minus low.
Calculation: Entry stop = At a filtered entry, store close minus a factor times that bar's high-low range for a long, or close plus that amount for a short.
Calculation: RSI extremes = Set the overbought state when RSI exceeds its upper threshold and retain it until the long exit condition occurs. Set the oversold state when RSI falls below its lower threshold and retain it until the short exit condition occurs; update both states before evaluating exits.
Long: Close crosses above the adaptive trend line.
Short: Close crosses below the adaptive trend line.
Long: Bullish base signal.
Short: Bearish base signal.
Long: Signal buffer is bullish AND the last unequal comparison of rolling VWAP with the medium moving average is above AND Chaikin volatility > its threshold AND close - open > a factor times the standard deviation of close - open AND (adaptive oscillator = 1 OR its previous value = 1) AND the oscillator at an earlier lookback bar < 0 AND close > open.
Short: Signal buffer is bearish AND the last unequal comparison of rolling VWAP with the medium moving average is below AND Chaikin volatility > its threshold AND close - open < negative one times a factor times the standard deviation of close - open AND (adaptive oscillator = -1 OR its previous value = -1) AND the oscillator at an earlier lookback bar > 0 AND close < open.
Exit: Long, (overbought state is set AND RSI < a lower exit threshold) OR low < the stored long entry stop OR (adaptive oscillator multiplied by a factor < its adaptive average AND adaptive average > 0 AND adaptive average > a threshold).
Exit: Short, (oversold state is set AND RSI > a higher exit threshold) OR high > the stored short entry stop OR (adaptive oscillator multiplied by a factor > its adaptive average AND adaptive average < 0 AND adaptive average < a threshold).
