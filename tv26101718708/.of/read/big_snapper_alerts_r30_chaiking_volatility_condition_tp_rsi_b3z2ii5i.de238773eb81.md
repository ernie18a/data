Calculation: Selected moving averages = the configured moving average of close; fast, medium, and slow use progressively longer lookbacks. The coloured moving average uses its separately configured type and lookback.
Calculation: Supertrend = a direction state that turns bullish when close exceeds the prior upper trailing ATR band and bearish when close falls below the prior lower trailing ATR band.
Calculation: Super oscillator = normalized close minus its ATR trailing stop, clipped to [-1, 1]; its signal is an adaptive moving average of that oscillator, with adaptation proportional to the squared oscillator.
Calculation: Chaikin Volatility = rate of change of the EMA of high minus low.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 over the configured rolling time window.
Long: a long marker occurs OR (rolling VWAP > medium moving average AND a long marker has occurred since the most recent short marker AND Chaikin Volatility > its configured lower bound AND close - open > standard deviation(close - open, longer lookback) × configured spike factor AND (Super oscillator = 1 OR its prior-bar value = 1) AND its value four bars ago < 0 AND close > open)
Short: a short marker occurs OR (rolling VWAP < medium moving average AND a short marker has occurred since the most recent long marker AND Chaikin Volatility > its configured lower bound AND close - open < -standard deviation(close - open, longer lookback) × configured spike factor AND (Super oscillator = -1 OR its prior-bar value = -1) AND its value four bars ago > 0 AND close < open)
Exit: long, low < entry price - configured stop factor × (entry bar high - entry bar low) OR (RSI has exceeded its configured overbought threshold and RSI < its configured take-profit threshold) OR (Super oscillator × configured oscillator factor < its signal AND signal > 0 AND signal > configured signal threshold)
Exit: short, high > entry price + configured stop factor × (entry bar high - entry bar low) OR (RSI has fallen below its configured oversold threshold and RSI > its configured take-profit threshold) OR (Super oscillator × configured oscillator factor > its signal AND signal < 0 AND signal < configured signal threshold)
