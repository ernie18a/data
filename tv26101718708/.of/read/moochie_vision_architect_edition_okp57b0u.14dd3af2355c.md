Calculation: Lower VWAP deviation band = VWAP − (standard deviation of close × deviation multiplier)
Calculation: Upper VWAP deviation band = VWAP + (standard deviation of close × deviation multiplier)
Calculation: Synthetic velocity = (current close − prior close) × (current volume ÷ simple moving average of volume)
Long: low ≤ lower VWAP deviation band AND synthetic velocity < −(standard deviation of synthetic velocity × threshold multiplier)
Short: high ≥ upper VWAP deviation band AND synthetic velocity > standard deviation of synthetic velocity × threshold multiplier
