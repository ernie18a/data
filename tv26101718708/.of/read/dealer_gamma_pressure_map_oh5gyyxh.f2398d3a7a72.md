Calculation: Flip = EMA of the chart-timeframe VWMA of close, or EMA on the chart timeframe of the last completed higher-timeframe VWMA of close; use the same VWMA lookback as the close standard deviation.
Calculation: Volatility = ATR or close standard deviation; if the selected value is zero or unavailable, use close standard deviation.
Calculation: Upper and lower zones = Flip plus or minus half the volatility multiplier times Volatility; upper and lower acceleration levels = Flip plus or minus the full volatility multiplier times Volatility.
Calculation: Gamma velocity = current (close - Flip) / Volatility minus its previous-bar value.
Calculation: Cooldown = bars since the last emitted signal of the same class, initialized as bars since bar index zero. Breakout long and breakout short have separate cooldowns; both reversal directions share one cooldown, and both fade directions share one cooldown. All use the same cooldown period.
Long: Close crosses above the highest high of the preceding breakout lookback bars AND close > upper zone AND gamma velocity > 0 AND ADX > trend threshold AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier AND breakout-long cooldown >= cooldown period.
Short: Close crosses below the lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback bars AND close < lower zone AND gamma velocity < 0 AND ADX > trend threshold AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier AND breakout-short cooldown >= cooldown period.
Long: Lowest low over the reversal lookback, including the current bar, < current lower acceleration level AND close crosses above lower acceleration level AND gamma velocity > 0 AND RSI < lower RSI threshold AND reversal cooldown >= cooldown period.
Short: Highest high over the reversal lookback, including the current bar, > current upper acceleration level AND close crosses below upper acceleration level AND gamma velocity < 0 AND RSI > upper RSI threshold AND reversal cooldown >= cooldown period.
Long: ADX < choppy threshold AND lowest low over the fade lookback, including the current bar, <= current lower acceleration level AND close crosses above lower zone AND gamma velocity > 0 AND fade cooldown >= cooldown period.
Short: ADX < choppy threshold AND highest high over the fade lookback, including the current bar, >= current upper acceleration level AND close crosses below upper zone AND gamma velocity < 0 AND fade cooldown >= cooldown period.
Calculation: The breakout lookback is longer than the reversal lookback, which is longer than the fade lookback; the choppy ADX threshold is below the trend ADX threshold; the lower RSI threshold is below the upper RSI threshold.
