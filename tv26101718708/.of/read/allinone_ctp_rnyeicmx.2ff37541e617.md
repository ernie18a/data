Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is the high or low of a bar with a specified number of bars on each side, confirmed only after the right-side bars have completed. Each decision uses its own pivot length; the internal structure pivot length is shorter than the swing structure pivot length.
Calculation: Swing structure levels = the latest confirmed pivot high and low that pass both the pivot-bar range > ATR times a sensitivity multiplier and, if enabled, pivot height >= average swing times a size multiplier. For a high, pivot height is pivot high minus the lowest low over twice the pivot length ending on the confirmation bar; for a low, it is the highest high over that window minus pivot low. Average swing is an SMA of ATR calculated with the pivot length. Replace a level when a newer valid pivot is confirmed; clear it after a break in its direction.
Calculation: Swing structure break = close beyond the corresponding swing structure level by an ATR displacement AND, if the volume filter is enabled and volume is positive, volume > a multiple of the SMA of volume, using 1 in place of nonpositive or missing volume in that SMA. Initially the structure direction is neutral; set it to the direction of each break. A break opposite the current structure direction requires the break condition to hold for two consecutive bars; otherwise it requires one bar.
Calculation: Internal structure levels = the latest confirmed shorter-length pivot high and low whose distance from the corresponding current swing structure level exceeds an ATR multiple, or for which that swing level is absent. Replace each level on a qualifying pivot and clear it after an internal break in its direction.
Calculation: Internal structure break = close beyond the corresponding internal structure level by the same ATR displacement used for swing structure AND the swing structure volume filter passes AND no swing structure break occurs on the current bar.
Calculation: Rejection range = max(high - low, minimum price increment). Upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of lower-timeframe bars starting within a configured interval after a configured session open, using the completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar. If the configured precision timeframe is not lower than the chart timeframe or intrabars are unavailable, use overlapping chart bars. Initialize the range on the chart bar containing the session open, falling back to that chart bar's high and low if no eligible intrabar exists; lock it on the first chart bar whose start is at or after the interval end. Keep only the configured number of most recent ranges. If multiple configured session opens occur in one chart bar, create only the first by configured session priority.
Long: Fast close EMA >= slow close EMA.
Short: Fast close EMA < slow close EMA.
Long: Close >= a separate close EMA.
Short: Close < a separate close EMA.
Long: Close >= anchored VWAP, calculated from a selectable close, HLC3, or OHLC4 source and volume, with nonpositive or missing volume replaced by 1 and a configurable calendar anchor.
Short: Close < anchored VWAP, calculated with the same source, volume substitution, and anchor.
Long: Fast close EMA crosses above slow close EMA.
Short: Fast close EMA crosses below slow close EMA.
Long: Swing structure break above the swing high level.
Short: Swing structure break below the swing low level.
Long: Internal structure break above the internal high level.
Short: Internal structure break below the internal low level.
Long: Low < latest confirmed swing-failure pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND pivot age from its pivot bar <= the lookback AND lower wick > ATR times a wick multiplier AND lower wick > rejection range times a wick fraction AND, if the sweep-depth filter is enabled, pivot low - low > ATR times a sweep multiplier AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close < fast close EMA AND, if volume confirmation is enabled and volume is positive, volume > its SMA with nonpositive or missing volume replaced by 1. If next-bar confirmation is enabled, emit the decision on the next bar only if its close > the swept pivot low.
Short: High > latest confirmed swing-failure pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND pivot age from its pivot bar <= the lookback AND upper wick > ATR times a wick multiplier AND upper wick > rejection range times a wick fraction AND, if the sweep-depth filter is enabled, high - pivot high > ATR times a sweep multiplier AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close > fast close EMA AND, if volume confirmation is enabled and volume is positive, volume > its SMA with nonpositive or missing volume replaced by 1. If next-bar confirmation is enabled, emit the decision on the next bar only if its close < the swept pivot high.
Long: A retained opening range is locked AND its close has never previously broken either boundary after locking AND close > its range high.
Short: A retained opening range is locked AND its close has never previously broken either boundary after locking AND close < its range low.
