Calculation: Buy volume and sell volume = sum the volumes of the completed lower-timeframe bars within the confirmed chart bar, assigning each lower-timeframe bar to buy when its close >= open and otherwise to sell. Delta = buy volume - sell volume; delta dominance = absolute delta / (buy volume + sell volume), or zero when the denominator is zero. Relative volume = (buy volume + sell volume) / chart-bar volume SMA, using the current bar and a configurable lookback; use the numerator as the denominator if the SMA is unavailable, and return zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Footprint stack = divide the chart bar's high-low range into equal price rows. Allocate each completed lower-timeframe bar's volume equally to every row from its low's row through its high's row, clamping row indices to the chart-bar range; classify the allocated volume as buy or sell by the lower-timeframe bar's close >= open. Scan rows from low to high. A buy imbalance occurs when row buy volume > zero AND > the configurable imbalance ratio times the greater of the preceding row's sell volume and a small positive floor. Otherwise, a sell imbalance occurs when row sell volume > zero AND > the same ratio times the greater of the following row's buy volume and the floor. The first and last rows lack a buy and sell comparison respectively. Count consecutive same-direction imbalances, resetting both counts on a row without an imbalance. The stack direction is the direction of the longest run; retain the first run when lengths tie.
