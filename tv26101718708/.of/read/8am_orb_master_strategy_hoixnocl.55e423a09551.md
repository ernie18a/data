Calculation: Opening range high and low = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the opening-range window in New York time; reset on each new calendar day and lock the range when a bar opens exactly at the window end.
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2.
Calculation: Bounce zone width = (opening range high - opening range low) × a sensitivity parameter.
Long: Opening range is locked AND no long or short signal has occurred today AND (previous bar low <= opening range midpoint AND previous bar high >= opening range midpoint OR absolute(previous bar low - opening range midpoint) <= bounce zone width OR absolute(previous bar high - opening range midpoint) <= bounce zone width) AND close > opening range midpoint AND close > open AND close > EMA of close AND volume > SMA of volume × a volume multiplier.
Short: Opening range is locked AND no long or short signal has occurred today AND (previous bar low <= opening range midpoint AND previous bar high >= opening range midpoint OR absolute(previous bar low - opening range midpoint) <= bounce zone width OR absolute(previous bar high - opening range midpoint) <= bounce zone width) AND close < opening range midpoint AND close < open AND close < EMA of close AND volume > SMA of volume × a volume multiplier.
