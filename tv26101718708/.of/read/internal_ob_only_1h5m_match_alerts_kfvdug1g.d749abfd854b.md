Calculation: Parsed high/low = high/low, except when bar range is at least twice the volatility measure, in which case parsed high = low and parsed low = high; volatility measure = ATR or cumulative mean true range, according to the selected filter
Calculation: Internal order block = on a close crossing above the latest internal swing high, create a bullish block using the bar with the lowest parsed low from that swing pivot up to the crossing bar; on a close crossing below the latest internal swing low, create a bearish block using the bar with the highest parsed high over that interval; an internal swing high/low is the high/low at the lookback offset when it exceeds the highs/is below the lows of the intervening lookback bars
Calculation: Active internal order blocks = created blocks not mitigated; remove a bearish block when its mitigation source exceeds its high, or a bullish block when its mitigation source falls below its low; mitigation source is close or high for bearish blocks and close or low for bullish blocks, according to the selected mitigation setting
Calculation: Internal OB bias = bullish when at least 2 of the 3 most recently created active internal order blocks are bullish; bearish when at least 2 are bearish; otherwise neutral
Long: Internal OB bias is bullish OR higher-timeframe internal OB bias is bullish AND chart-timeframe internal OB bias is bullish AND its prior value was not bullish
Short: Internal OB bias is bearish OR higher-timeframe internal OB bias is bearish AND chart-timeframe internal OB bias is bearish AND its prior value was not bearish
