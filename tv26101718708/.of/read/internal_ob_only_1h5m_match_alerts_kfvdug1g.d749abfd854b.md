Calculation: Parsed bar = use low as parsed high and high as parsed low when bar range is at least a configurable multiple of volatility; otherwise use high and low respectively. Volatility is either ATR or cumulative true range divided by bar index.
Calculation: Pivot = maintain a leg state, initially bearish. If the high a lookback ago is greater than every high since then, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low a lookback ago is less than every low since then, set it bullish. On a change to bearish, record the high from the lookback bar as the latest high pivot; on a change to bullish, record its low as the latest low pivot. Reset the corresponding pivot's crossed flag when it is recorded.
Calculation: Order blocks = on the first close crossover above the latest uncrossed high pivot, mark that pivot crossed and create a bullish block using the parsed high and low of the first bar with the minimum parsed low from the pivot bar inclusive to the crossover bar exclusive. On the first close crossunder below the latest uncrossed low pivot, mark that pivot crossed and create a bearish block using the parsed high and low of the first bar with the maximum parsed high over the corresponding interval. Keep blocks newest first, discarding the oldest above a configurable capacity. Delete a bullish block when the selected mitigation source, either low or close, is below its stored parsed low; delete a bearish block when the selected mitigation source, either high or close, is above its stored parsed high. Chart-timeframe block creation is enabled by a configurable switch; the two other-timeframe calculations always create blocks.
Calculation: Order-block bias = neutral unless at least a configurable number of active blocks exist; otherwise bullish or bearish when at least a configurable threshold among the most recent blocks have that direction. Evaluate blocks independently on the chart timeframe and on two other timeframes, one longer than the other. For either other timeframe, relative to the chart timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in realtime if it is higher; use the last available intrabar if it is lower; use the current bar if equal.
Long: A bullish chart-timeframe order block is created.
Short: A bearish chart-timeframe order block is created.
Long: Chart-timeframe order-block bias is bullish OR longer-timeframe order-block bias is bullish OR shorter-timeframe order-block bias is bullish.
Short: Chart-timeframe order-block bias is bearish OR longer-timeframe order-block bias is bearish OR shorter-timeframe order-block bias is bearish.
Long: Longer-timeframe order-block bias is bullish AND shorter-timeframe order-block bias changes to bullish from a non-bullish value.
Short: Longer-timeframe order-block bias is bearish AND shorter-timeframe order-block bias changes to bearish from a non-bearish value.
