Long: Supertrend direction changes from down to up OR close crosses above the upper breakout boundary OR (after TP3 was reached on a long setup, Supertrend is up, and low <= Supertrend and close > Supertrend) OR (after TP3 was reached on a long setup, Supertrend is up, a prior close crossed below the re-entry EMA, and close crosses above it) OR (after TP3 was reached on a long setup, Supertrend is up, low <= re-entry EMA, and close > re-entry EMA); and (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > its threshold); and (EMA 200 filter disabled OR close > EMA 200
Short: Supertrend direction changes from up to down OR close crosses below the lower breakout boundary OR (after TP3 was reached on a short setup, Supertrend is down, high >= Supertrend, and close < Supertrend) OR (after TP3 was reached on a short setup, Supertrend is down, a prior close crossed above the re-entry EMA, and close crosses below it) OR (after TP3 was reached on a short setup, Supertrend is down, high >= re-entry EMA, and close < re-entry EMA); and (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > its threshold); and (EMA 200 filter disabled OR close < EMA 200
Calculation: Upper breakout boundary = rising line through the two latest confirmed pivot lows, offset upward by the distance between the latest pivot high and that line at the pivot high's bar; if this channel is invalid, use the line through the two latest confirmed pivot highs. Lower breakout boundary = falling line through the two latest confirmed pivot highs, offset downward by the distance between that line and the latest pivot low at the pivot low's bar; if this channel is invalid, use the line through the two latest confirmed pivot lows. Confirm pivots after the required bars to their right have completed; compare each boundary with its value on the prior bar.
Calculation: Re-entry EMA = exponential moving average of close, with a shorter lookback than the EMA 200 filter
Exit: Long, low <= stop-loss level OR high >= TP1 level OR high >= TP2 level OR high >= TP3 level OR a Short condition
Exit: Short, high >= stop-loss level OR low <= TP1 level OR low <= TP2 level OR low <= TP3 level OR a Long condition
