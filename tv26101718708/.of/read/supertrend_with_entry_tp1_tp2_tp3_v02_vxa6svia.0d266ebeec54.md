Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at the signal bar multiplied by the stop-loss multiplier; use the same ATR length as Supertrend.
Calculation: Pivot support and resistance = lines through the two most recent confirmed pivot lows and highs respectively, using the same left and right pivot lookback; extend each line to the current and previous bars. Confirm each pivot only after its right-hand bars have elapsed.
Calculation: Upper breakout boundary = pivot support shifted upward through the latest confirmed pivot high if that high is above support at its pivot bar; otherwise pivot resistance. Lower breakout boundary = pivot resistance shifted downward through the latest confirmed pivot low if that low is below resistance at its pivot bar; otherwise pivot support. For both the current and previous boundary values, use the pivots known on the current bar.
Calculation: Re-entry state = after a signal, record its side and entry-bar risk distance; clear both re-entry states and both EMA pullback states. On a later bar without a signal, arm re-entry for that side when its third target is reached, unless its stop is reached before the first target. Clear its EMA pullback state when the third target is reached. While long re-entry is armed and Supertrend is up, arm the long EMA pullback after close crosses below the re-entry EMA; while short re-entry is armed and Supertrend is down, arm the short EMA pullback after close crosses above that EMA. Evaluate re-entry signals before updating these states.
Long: (Supertrend changes from down to up OR optional breakout is enabled AND close > upper breakout boundary AND previous close <= previous upper breakout boundary OR optional Supertrend re-entry is enabled AND long re-entry is armed AND Supertrend is up AND low <= Supertrend AND close > Supertrend OR optional EMA re-entry is enabled AND long re-entry and long EMA pullback are armed AND Supertrend is up AND close crosses above the re-entry EMA OR optional aggressive EMA re-entry is enabled AND long re-entry is armed AND Supertrend is up AND low <= re-entry EMA AND close > re-entry EMA) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > signal threshold) AND (long-term EMA filter is disabled OR close > long-term EMA).
Short: (Supertrend changes from up to down OR optional breakout is enabled AND close < lower breakout boundary AND previous close >= previous lower breakout boundary OR optional Supertrend re-entry is enabled AND short re-entry is armed AND Supertrend is down AND high >= Supertrend AND close < Supertrend OR optional EMA re-entry is enabled AND short re-entry and short EMA pullback are armed AND Supertrend is down AND close crosses below the re-entry EMA OR optional aggressive EMA re-entry is enabled AND short re-entry is armed AND Supertrend is down AND high >= re-entry EMA AND close < re-entry EMA) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > signal threshold) AND (long-term EMA filter is disabled OR close < long-term EMA).
Long: Supertrend is up.
Short: Supertrend is down.
Long: +DI > -DI.
Short: -DI > +DI.
Long: Higher or lower timeframe Supertrend is up AND its ADX >= range threshold AND min(ADX, score cap) > neutral-zone half-width; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: Higher or lower timeframe Supertrend is down AND its ADX >= range threshold AND min(ADX, score cap) > neutral-zone half-width; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Exit: long, no entry signal on the current bar AND first target has not previously been reached AND low <= entry price - risk distance.
Exit: short, no entry signal on the current bar AND first target has not previously been reached AND high >= entry price + risk distance.
Exit: long, no entry signal on the current bar AND no stop was reached before the first target AND high >= entry price + risk distance multiplied by a target risk/reward parameter; evaluate each of the three target parameters separately and mark each only once.
Exit: short, no entry signal on the current bar AND no stop was reached before the first target AND low <= entry price - risk distance multiplied by a target risk/reward parameter; evaluate each of the three target parameters separately and mark each only once.
