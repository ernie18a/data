Calculation: Directional volume oscillator = Select a price range from close only, open and close, or high and low. Set the assignment size to a fixed amount, ATR, or close divided by a parameter. Starting with previous assigned price zero, set the assigned price to the range high if it is strictly greater than the previous assigned price plus the assignment size; otherwise set it to the range low if it is strictly less than the previous assigned price minus the assignment size; otherwise retain the previous assigned price. Set direction to up or down when the assigned price rises or falls, otherwise retain its previous direction. Sum volume since the most recent direction change, resetting the sum to current volume on a change; optionally use true range instead of volume and optionally divide the sum by the number of bars since the change. If oscillation is enabled, negate the result when direction is down.
Calculation: G-channel trend = Starting with both boundaries at zero, update the upper boundary to max(OHLC4, previous upper) minus (previous upper minus previous lower) divided by a lookback; update the lower boundary to min(OHLC4, previous lower) plus the same fraction. A lower-boundary event occurs when the previous lower boundary is below the previous close AND the current lower boundary is above the current close. An upper-boundary event occurs when the previous upper boundary is below the previous close AND the current upper boundary is above the current close. The trend is bullish when bars since the upper-boundary event are less than or equal to bars since the lower-boundary event; otherwise it is bearish once both event counts exist.
Long: Directional volume oscillator crosses above zero.
Short: Directional volume oscillator crosses below zero.
Long: Directional volume oscillator crosses above zero AND G-channel trend is bullish AND ADX is above its threshold when the ADX filter is enabled AND datetime is strictly inside the enabled date bounds AND position side is not long.
Short: Directional volume oscillator crosses below zero AND G-channel trend is bearish AND ADX is above its threshold when the ADX filter is enabled AND datetime is strictly inside the enabled date bounds AND position side is not short.
Exit: long, datetime is strictly inside the enabled date bounds AND high is greater than or equal to entry price plus a profit-target percentage of entry price; apply up to four independently parameterized targets and their configured position fractions.
Exit: short, datetime is strictly inside the enabled date bounds AND low is less than or equal to entry price minus a profit-target percentage of entry price; apply up to four independently parameterized targets and their configured position fractions.
Exit: long, datetime is strictly inside the enabled date bounds AND close is below entry price minus a stop-loss percentage of entry price.
Exit: short, datetime is strictly inside the enabled date bounds AND close is above entry price plus a stop-loss percentage of entry price.
