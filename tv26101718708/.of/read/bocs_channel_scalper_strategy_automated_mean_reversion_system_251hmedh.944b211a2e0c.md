Calculation: Normalized volatility = standard deviation of (close − lowest low over the normalization lookback) / (highest high over the same lookback − lowest low) over a separate volatility lookback.
Calculation: Volatility upper and lower = (detection lookback − bars since the most recent highest and lowest normalized volatility, respectively, within the detection lookback plus the current bar) / detection lookback.
Calculation: Channel duration = max(1, bars since volatility lower crossed above volatility upper), using 0 when no such cross has occurred. When volatility upper crosses above volatility lower and channel duration exceeds a minimum, create a channel with top equal to the highest high and bottom equal to the lowest low over the channel duration including the current bar. If overlapping channels are disabled, create it only if its price interval does not overlap any existing channel. Keep channels newest first. On each bar, process channels newest first; if close exceeds a channel's top plus a breakout offset or falls below its bottom minus that offset, remove that channel and stop processing further channels on that bar.
Calculation: Long zone boundary = channel bottom + entry-zone fraction × (channel top − channel bottom). Short zone boundary = channel top − entry-zone fraction × (channel top − channel bottom).
Calculation: ATR filter value = ATR on the selected timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed higher-timeframe ATR on historical bars, including the current higher-timeframe bar only on its final chart bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe ATR within the chart bar.
Calculation: Entry reference = the relevant zone boundary when the entry signal occurs. Fix the channel bounds and range from that signal for the associated exit orders. Fixed-point target distance and channel-breakout and stop offsets are price-distance parameters.
Long: Long entries enabled AND a channel is being processed AND low ≤ long zone boundary AND position is flat AND (no previous long signal OR bars since previous long signal ≥ cooldown) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR filter value ≥ minimum ATR).
Short: Short entries enabled AND a channel is being processed AND high ≥ short zone boundary AND position is flat AND (no previous short signal OR bars since previous short signal ≥ cooldown) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR filter value ≥ minimum ATR).
Exit: long, low ≤ entry channel bottom − stop offset OR high ≥ entry reference + (fixed-point target distance OR entry channel range × target fraction, according to selected target method).
Exit: short, high ≥ entry channel top + stop offset OR low ≤ entry reference − (fixed-point target distance OR entry channel range × target fraction, according to selected target method).
