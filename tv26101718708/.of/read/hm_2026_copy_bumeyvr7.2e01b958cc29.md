Calculation: Adaptive lengths = If adaptation is enabled, divide ATR by an SMA of ATR with a longer lookback; use 1 when that SMA is not positive. Raise the ratio, floored at a positive minimum, to the negative adaptation-strength parameter, clamp it between configured multiplier bounds, multiply each base length by the result, round, and clamp to that calculation's minimum and a common maximum. Otherwise use the base lengths.
Calculation: RSI = RSI of a selected price source. With adaptation enabled, calculate gains and losses using recursive averages with the current adaptive RSI length as the smoothing denominator, initialized with the first gain or loss; use the neutral midpoint if both averages are zero and the maximum RSI if only average loss is zero. Otherwise use standard RSI with the fixed base length.
Calculation: Fast RSI average = EMA of RSI with the shorter moving-average length. With adaptation enabled, update the EMA recursively using the current adaptive length; otherwise use the fixed base length.
Calculation: Slow RSI average = WMA of RSI with the longer moving-average length, using the current adaptive length when adaptation is enabled and the fixed base length otherwise.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot = An RSI low or high with the configured number of bars on each side. Evaluate it on the confirmation bar, and pair it with the price low or high at the pivot bar. Compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind, including pivots that produced no signal.
Calculation: Regime slope = Percentage change from the fitted value at the beginning to the fitted value at the end of a linear regression of close over the regime lookback. If automatic scaling is enabled, derive the lookback from a configurable calibration duration divided by chart-bar duration, round and clamp it, and scale the regime thresholds by the square root of the actual lookback duration divided by the calibration duration; otherwise use the fixed lookback and thresholds.
Calculation: Composite score = A weighted sum of RSI, clamped momentum, regime score, and directional volume score. Clamped momentum is the neutral midpoint plus a configurable multiple of fast RSI average minus slow RSI average, clamped to the score range. Regime score is the maximum, minimum, or midpoint for bullish, bearish, or other regime. Directional volume score is the midpoint plus a configurable weight times the clamped positive excess of volume divided by its SMA over unity, multiplied by the sign of close minus open; use a volume ratio of unity when cumulative volume is not positive or its SMA is not positive. Substitute the midpoint for unavailable RSI and zero for an unavailable moving-average spread.
Long: Fast RSI average crosses above slow RSI average AND (cumulative volume is not positive OR volume is greater than a configured multiple of its SMA) AND (the optional RSI midpoint filter is disabled OR RSI is above its midpoint) AND (no prior long signal of the enabled signal type exists OR bars since that signal are at least that type's cooldown). Scalp and swing are separately enabled signal types with separate per-direction cooldowns; the swing cooldown is longer and each cooldown is a configured duration converted to chart bars by rounding up and floored at one bar.
Short: Fast RSI average crosses below slow RSI average AND (cumulative volume is not positive OR volume is greater than a configured multiple of its SMA) AND (the optional RSI midpoint filter is disabled OR RSI is below its midpoint) AND (no prior short signal of the enabled signal type exists OR bars since that signal are at least that type's cooldown). Scalp and swing use separate per-direction cooldowns as above.
Long: RSI is at or below the deep-oversold threshold AND previous RSI was above that threshold, substituting the RSI midpoint for an unavailable previous RSI.
Short: RSI is at or above the deep-overbought threshold AND previous RSI was below that threshold, substituting the RSI midpoint for an unavailable previous RSI.
Long: A confirmed RSI low pivot and its preceding RSI low pivot are no more than the maximum gap apart AND the current pivot's price low is below the previous pivot's price low AND the current pivot's RSI minus the previous pivot's RSI is at least the minimum RSI difference AND their absolute percentage price displacement is at least the configured minimum AND (the selected tier is All OR the previous pivot's RSI is at or below the oversold threshold AND (the selected tier is Standard OR displacement is at least the premium threshold)).
Short: A confirmed RSI high pivot and its preceding RSI high pivot are no more than the maximum gap apart AND the current pivot's price high is above the previous pivot's price high AND the previous pivot's RSI minus the current pivot's RSI is at least the minimum RSI difference AND their absolute percentage price displacement is at least the configured minimum AND (the selected tier is All OR the previous pivot's RSI is at or above the overbought threshold AND (the selected tier is Standard OR displacement is at least the premium threshold)).
Long: Fast RSI average is greater than slow RSI average.
Short: Fast RSI average is less than or equal to slow RSI average.
Long: Regime slope is at or above the bullish regime threshold.
Short: Regime slope is below the bullish regime threshold AND at or below the bearish regime threshold.
Long: Composite score is at or above the bullish score threshold.
Short: Composite score is below the bullish score threshold AND at or below the bearish score threshold.
