Calculation: Signed bar volume = volume × (2 × buy fraction − 1), where buy fraction is the standard normal CDF of close-to-close return divided by its rolling standard deviation; multiply by relative volume when volume weighting is enabled.
Calculation: Control score = a normalized weighted mean of (1) short-lookback signed volume divided by volume, (2) longer-lookback signed volume divided by volume, (3) session VWAP location relative to close and VWAP slope, (4) fast/mid/slow EMA ordering and fast EMA slope, and (5) volume-weighted close location within each bar; damp the score when its magnitude is strong but price progress over the absorption lookback is small or an absorption event occurs, then scale it by the fitted price-impact R². Calculate separately on each configured timeframe's aggregated bars, using the last completed bar when frozen and otherwise the forming bar. The delta lookback is shorter than the CVD lookback; EMA lengths are ordered fast < mid < slow.
Calculation: Control strength = percentile rank of the absolute control score within that timeframe's own rolling history.
Calculation: Path direction = sign of the weighted mean of each included timeframe's control score × its control strength; include the chart timeframe and the configured nearest higher timeframes, or all configured timeframes when selected. Path clarity = absolute logistic conviction of that mean, reduced by positive inter-timeframe correlation, the chart-timeframe efficiency-ratio regime gate, and the extreme-turbulence damp. The path is CHOP when clarity is below its threshold; otherwise its direction is the sign of the mean.
Calculation: Control momentum = current chart-timeframe control score minus its value at the momentum lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = close is below its value at the divergence lookback and control score is above its value then. Bearish divergence = close is above its value then and control score is below its value then.
Calculation: Top VSA exhaustion = an upthrust, buying climax, or no-demand event. Bottom VSA exhaustion = stopping volume, shakeout, selling climax, or no-supply event.
Calculation: Initial Balance high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening window for the session.
Long: Any configured timeframe's control score > 0 AND its control strength ≥ the neutral-band threshold; OR path clarity ≥ the chop threshold AND path direction is up; OR chart-timeframe control newly aligns with the up path while its strength ≥ the neutral-band threshold; OR bullish divergence; OR bottom VSA exhaustion AND control momentum > its positive threshold; OR close > Initial Balance high.
Short: Any configured timeframe's control score < 0 AND its control strength ≥ the neutral-band threshold; OR path clarity ≥ the chop threshold AND path direction is down; OR chart-timeframe control newly aligns with the down path while its strength ≥ the neutral-band threshold; OR bearish divergence; OR top VSA exhaustion AND control momentum < its negative threshold; OR close < Initial Balance low.
