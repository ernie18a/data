Calculation: Confluence zones = Form configured levels from the previous completed daily bar’s high, low, and close; previous completed weekly high and low; optionally previous completed monthly high and low; configured daily pivots; and 38.2%, 50%, and 61.8% retracements between the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and low. Higher-timeframe levels use completed bars. Daily pivots use the previous completed daily bar: Classic uses P=(H+L+C)/3, R1=2P−L, S1=2P−H, R2=P+(H−L), S2=P−(H−L); Fibonacci uses P and P±0.382(H−L), P±0.618(H−L); Camarilla uses C±(H−L)×1.1/4 and C±(H−L)×1.1/2. Reference is the average range of prior completed daily bars on intraday charts, otherwise chart-timeframe ATR. Sort levels by price and group consecutive levels when each level is within the configured tolerance times the reference of the group’s lowest level; retain groups with at least two distinct sources. Expand each retained group’s low and high by 0.03 times the reference.
Long: Current bar high ≥ zone low AND current bar low ≤ zone high AND previous chart bar close > zone high, for any confluence zone
Short: Current bar high ≥ zone low AND current bar low ≤ zone high AND previous chart bar close < zone low, for any confluence zone
