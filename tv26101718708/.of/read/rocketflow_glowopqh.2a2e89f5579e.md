Calculation: All calculations use chart-timeframe bars. Use configurable positive numerical constants and lookback lengths wherever thresholds, offsets, or multipliers are specified below. A lag refers to completed chart bars. Use a configurable positive minimum denominator where division could otherwise be undefined.
Calculation: Donchian midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over its lookback. The conversion line uses a shorter lookback than the base line; the second cloud span uses a longer lookback than both. The first cloud span is the average of conversion and base lines. Cloud top and bottom are the maximum and minimum of the two spans lagged by the cloud displacement; cloud thickness is their difference. Future cloud is bullish when the unlagged first span exceeds the unlagged second span.
Calculation: ATR = Wilder-smoothed true range. RSI slope = RSI of close minus its value at a configurable lag. MACD histogram slope = MACD histogram minus its value at a configurable lag. Directional momentum = (positive DI minus negative DI) minus that spread at a configurable lag; calculate DI with Wilder smoothing. Momentum composite = max(RSI slope, 0) + max(MACD histogram slope, 0) + max(directional momentum, 0).
Calculation: Gap = (cloud bottom - close) / ATR; cloud size = cloud thickness / ATR; compression = (highest high - lowest low over a compression lookback) / ATR; range = (high - low) / ATR; body = abs(close - open) / ATR; body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low); close position = (close - low) / (high - low); upper wick fraction = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low). Impulse = body fraction * close position * range. Conversion slope = (conversion line - its lagged value) / ATR; base gap = (base line - close) / ATR. Cloud penetration = 0 if high < cloud bottom, otherwise (min(high, cloud top) - cloud bottom) / cloud thickness. Cloud travel = range / max(cloud size, a positive floor).
Calculation: Pressure = max(RSI slope, a positive floor) * max(range, a positive floor) / max(gap * compression, a positive floor); pressure slope = pressure minus its lagged value. Coil quality = compression / max(gap, a positive floor). Cloud arrival estimate = gap / max((close - lagged close) / ATR, a positive floor).
Calculation: Clean thrust = range >= its threshold AND impulse >= its threshold AND close position >= its threshold AND gap <= its threshold. Ignition = RSI slope >= its threshold AND momentum composite >= its threshold. Strong rescue = close position >= its threshold AND body >= its threshold AND RSI slope >= its threshold AND gap <= its threshold. Violent engagement = cloud penetration >= its threshold AND range >= its threshold AND body >= its threshold AND (close position >= its high threshold OR (close position >= its lower threshold AND impulse >= its threshold AND upper wick fraction <= its threshold) OR (close position >= its lower threshold AND base gap <= its negative threshold AND RSI slope >= its threshold)). Rip = (violent engagement OR (strong rescue AND range >= its threshold AND impulse >= its threshold)) AND RSI slope >= its threshold AND gap <= its threshold AND (pressure slope >= its threshold OR ignition OR strong rescue).
Calculation: Displacement quality = range >= its threshold OR impulse >= its threshold OR (close position >= its threshold AND upper wick fraction <= its threshold) OR (range >= its lower threshold AND base gap <= its negative threshold AND RSI slope >= its threshold). Structure quality = conversion slope >= 0 OR impulse >= its threshold OR close position >= its threshold OR base gap <= its negative threshold.
Calculation: Overrun trap = cloud size <= its threshold AND cloud travel >= its threshold AND cloud penetration >= its threshold AND NOT rip AND NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust. Shallow engagement trap = high >= cloud bottom AND cloud penetration <= its threshold AND cloud travel <= its threshold AND base gap > its negative threshold AND conversion slope < its threshold AND NOT violent engagement. Rejection probe = high >= cloud bottom AND cloud penetration >= its threshold AND close position <= its threshold AND upper wick fraction >= its threshold AND NOT rip AND NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust. Weak probe = high >= cloud bottom AND cloud penetration <= its threshold AND base gap < 0 AND impulse < its threshold AND NOT ignition AND NOT rip AND NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust. Soft pop = high >= cloud bottom AND cloud penetration <= its threshold AND cloud travel < its threshold AND conversion slope <= its threshold AND RSI slope < its threshold AND NOT rip AND NOT violent engagement AND NOT strong rescue.
Calculation: Rocket base = (close < cloud bottom, or optionally high < cloud bottom) AND gap > 0 AND gap <= the rocket gap limit AND compression <= the rocket compression limit AND close > open AND close position >= its threshold AND body fraction >= its threshold.
Calculation: Highest-tier rocket = rocket base AND displacement quality AND structure quality AND NOT overrun trap AND NOT shallow engagement trap AND ((gap <= its highest-tier limit AND pressure slope >= its threshold AND range >= its threshold AND impulse >= its threshold AND close position >= its threshold AND RSI slope >= its threshold AND coil quality >= its threshold AND momentum composite >= its threshold) OR rip).
Calculation: Confirmed rocket = rocket base AND NOT highest-tier rocket AND NOT overrun trap AND NOT shallow engagement trap AND NOT rejection probe AND NOT weak probe AND NOT soft pop AND NOT (NOT rip AND NOT clean thrust AND NOT future cloud bullish AND conversion slope <= 0 AND base gap >= its threshold AND cloud travel < its threshold AND cloud penetration <= its threshold) AND NOT (high < cloud bottom AND cloud penetration < its conversion threshold AND NOT clean thrust AND NOT rip) AND NOT (RSI slope < its threshold AND close position <= its threshold AND upper wick fraction >= its threshold AND impulse < its threshold AND NOT rip AND NOT violent engagement AND NOT strong rescue) AND pressure slope >= its threshold AND pressure >= the confirmed pressure threshold AND gap <= its confirmed limit AND range >= its threshold AND close position >= its threshold AND RSI slope >= its threshold AND impulse >= its threshold AND (ignition OR cloud travel >= its threshold).
Calculation: Early rocket = rocket base AND NOT highest-tier rocket AND NOT confirmed rocket AND NOT overrun trap AND NOT shallow engagement trap AND NOT rejection probe AND NOT weak probe AND NOT soft pop AND NOT (NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust AND NOT future cloud bullish AND cloud size >= its threshold AND upper wick fraction >= its threshold) AND NOT (cloud penetration >= its threshold AND cloud size <= its threshold AND NOT future cloud bullish AND NOT violent engagement AND NOT clean thrust AND NOT strong rescue AND RSI slope < its threshold) AND NOT (base gap >= its threshold AND conversion slope <= 0 AND close position <= its threshold AND upper wick fraction >= its threshold AND RSI slope < its threshold AND NOT rip AND NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust) AND NOT (base gap >= its overhead threshold AND RSI slope < its threshold AND cloud penetration <= its threshold AND NOT rip AND NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust) AND NOT (high < cloud bottom AND NOT displacement quality AND NOT structure quality AND NOT rip AND NOT strong rescue AND NOT clean thrust) AND pressure >= the early pressure threshold AND gap <= its early limit AND range >= its threshold AND (cloud penetration >= its conversion threshold OR clean thrust OR strong rescue) AND ((pressure slope >= its threshold AND RSI slope >= its threshold) OR clean thrust OR strong rescue).
Calculation: Final rocket = (highest-tier rocket OR confirmed rocket OR early rocket) AND bars since the most recent raw rocket on a prior bar > the cooldown, or no prior raw rocket exists. Count every raw rocket for cooldown, including rockets suppressed by cooldown.
Calculation: Pre-rocket score = sum of six independently tiered scores: gap gets the highest score at or below its nearest threshold, a lower score at or below its middle threshold, a still lower score at or below the pre-rocket gap limit, otherwise zero; cloud arrival estimate is scored similarly using short, medium, and maximum arrival thresholds; momentum gets the highest score if RSI slope or momentum composite reaches its high threshold, a middle score if RSI slope reaches its middle threshold, a lower score if RSI slope reaches its minimum, otherwise zero; candle gets descending scores for (close position and body fraction both above their high thresholds), close position above its middle threshold, and close position above its low threshold; structure gets a high score if close > conversion line AND conversion slope >= 0, a lower score if either close > conversion line OR conversion slope >= its negative threshold; compression gets a high score at or below the rocket compression limit and a lower score at or below the looser pre-rocket compression limit. Cap the sum at its maximum score.
Long: Final rocket = true.
Long: Final rocket = false AND (close < cloud bottom, or optionally high < cloud bottom) AND gap > the rocket gap limit AND gap <= the larger pre-rocket gap limit AND compression <= the larger pre-rocket compression limit AND close > open AND close position >= its threshold AND body fraction >= its threshold AND RSI slope >= its minimum AND cloud arrival estimate <= its maximum AND NOT ((upper wick fraction >= its threshold AND close position <= its threshold) OR (base gap >= its threshold AND RSI slope < its threshold) OR (conversion slope <= its negative threshold AND NOT strong rescue)) AND pre-rocket score >= its minimum.
Calculation: Rocket campaign = the period after the latest final rocket through a configurable maximum age, ending on its first danger condition or replacing it on a new final rocket. Store that rocket's close, low, ATR, tier, and stop = min(lowest low over the stop lookback - signal ATR * tier-specific buffer, signal close - signal ATR * tier-specific minimum distance). A push has occurred once (highest high since the rocket - rocket close) / current ATR >= the push threshold. A pullback arms re-entry when a push has occurred AND low > stored stop + current ATR * buffer AND close position >= its threshold AND (low <= conversion line OR low <= base line OR close <= rocket close + current ATR * buffer). Danger = close < stored stop OR (low < stored stop AND close < stored rocket low).
Long: Rocket campaign is active AND a pullback was armed on an earlier bar AND no re-entry has occurred in this campaign AND danger = false AND close > previous high AND close > conversion line AND RSI slope >= its re-entry threshold AND impulse >= its re-entry threshold.
Exit: long, rocket campaign is active AND danger = true.
Exit: long, after a final rocket, low <= its stored rocket stop + rocket-bar ATR * trailing-rise multiplier * bars since that rocket, for the first time after that rocket.
Calculation: Support ATR = SMA of true range. Support stop = lowest low over its support lookback - support ATR * buffer. Frozen stop = lowest support stop over a reclaim window ending on the previous bar. While the support virtual position is flat, arm a reclaim when low < frozen stop; capture that frozen stop, the break bar, and the lowest low since the break. Disarm when the break age exceeds the reclaim window or a valid support entry occurs. A new break on the expiry bar may immediately arm a new reclaim.
Calculation: Support entry stop = min(current support stop, lowest low over the reclaim window, and, for a reclaim entry, the lowest low since its captured break). Support risk = entry close - support entry stop. A support virtual position opens at the signal close. On each later bar, first close it if low <= the active stop from the preceding bar; otherwise record whether high has reached entry + one risk or entry + two risks, and update the trailing stop to the maximum of its previous value and current support stop. The active stop for the next bar is the initial stop before the first target, max(initial stop, entry price) after the first target, and max(initial stop, entry price, trailing stop) after the second target. A stop exit and new entry can occur on the same bar.
Long: Support virtual position is flat AND reclaim is armed AND break age <= reclaim window AND close > captured frozen stop AND close > open AND (trend filter disabled OR close > its SMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND support risk > a configurable minimum price increment.
Long: Support virtual position is flat AND support ATR > 0 AND close > support stop AND (close - support stop) / support ATR <= its coil-distance limit AND close > open AND close > previous close AND (trend filter disabled OR close > its SMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND support risk > a configurable minimum price increment.
Exit: long, low crosses below support stop.
