Calculation: Session = bars whose opening time in the exchange timezone is between the configured session start and end, inclusive. The first session candle is the first bar entering the session, also reset on the first in-session bar of a new trading day.
Calculation: High break = the first bar after the first session candle in each session whose high > first session candle high + ATR × break multiplier; retain its bar index until the next session.
Calculation: Low break = the first bar after the first session candle in each session whose low < first session candle low − ATR × break multiplier; retain its bar index until the next session.
Calculation: Long stop = current low − ATR × stop multiplier; short stop = current high + ATR × stop multiplier. Recalculate on every bar.
Calculation: Long target = current close + (current close − long stop) × reward-to-risk multiplier × target fraction; short target = current close − (short stop − current close) × reward-to-risk multiplier × target fraction. Recalculate on every bar. When multiple targets are enabled, use three increasing target fractions; otherwise use the largest fraction.
Calculation: Trailing stop = when enabled, for a long, if close − entry signal close >= (current long target using the largest fraction − entry signal close) × activation fraction, set the stop to close − ATR × trailing multiplier if that level > current long stop and either no trailing stop is active or the level > the previous trailing stop. For a short, if entry signal close − close >= (entry signal close − current short target using the largest fraction) × activation fraction, set the stop to close + ATR × trailing multiplier if that level < current short stop and either no trailing stop is active or the level < the previous trailing stop. Retain the last submitted trailing stop until changed or the position closes; reset it when flat.
Long: In session AND a high break has occurred AND bars since the high break >= minimum delay AND bars since the high break <= maximum delay AND low >= first session candle high − ATR × retest multiplier AND low <= first session candle high + ATR × retest multiplier AND close > first session candle high AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier).
Short: In session AND a low break has occurred AND bars since the low break >= minimum delay AND bars since the low break <= maximum delay AND high >= first session candle low − ATR × retest multiplier AND high <= first session candle low + ATR × retest multiplier AND close < first session candle low AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of close) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier).
Exit: long, price <= the most recently submitted long stop OR price >= a most recently submitted long target OR price <= the most recently submitted long trailing stop OR current bar is outside the session AND previous bar was in session.
Exit: short, price >= the most recently submitted short stop OR price <= a most recently submitted short target OR price >= the most recently submitted short trailing stop OR current bar is outside the session AND previous bar was in session.
Calculation: Exit orders calculated at a bar close become active on the following bar and remain active until replaced or filled. With multiple targets, submit three stop-limit exit brackets in increasing target order, reserving respectively the first target fraction, the second target fraction, and all remaining position quantity; each bracket has the same stop. With a single target, reserve the entire position in one bracket. Submit the trailing stop after these brackets, subject to remaining unreserved position quantity. Entry signals can reverse an opposite position; same-side signals are still marked but do not create another entry.
