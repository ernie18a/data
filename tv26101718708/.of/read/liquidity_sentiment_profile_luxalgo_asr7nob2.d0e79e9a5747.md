Calculation: Profile rows = Divide the price range into a parameterized number of equal-width rows. For a completed higher-timeframe anchor period, use its high and low and its chart bars; calculate this profile at the start of the next anchor period. For the still-forming higher-timeframe anchor period, use the high and low of its chart bars after the first bar, but include the first bar in the volume calculations; calculate this profile only after the first bar.
Calculation: Row total volume and bullish volume = For each chart bar whose high is at least the row's lower boundary and whose low is below its upper boundary, add volume multiplied by row width divided by the bar's high-low range to row total volume; if the range is zero, add the full volume instead. Add the same amount to row bullish volume only when the bar's close is greater than its open. Treat missing volume as zero.
Long: Current chart-bar volume is nonzero AND profile price range is positive AND row bullish volume is greater than half of row total volume
Short: Current chart-bar volume is nonzero AND profile price range is positive AND row bullish volume is less than or equal to half of row total volume
