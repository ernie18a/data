Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume from the anchor bar through the current chart bar, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3; for the first-available-bar and rolling anchors, replace nonpositive or missing volume with 1.
Calculation: Comparison level = any of: anchored VWAP reset at the start of a calendar period; anchored VWAP from the first available chart bar; anchored VWAP from a configurable timestamp; anchored VWAP from the start of a configurable number of calendar periods ago; anchored VWAP reset when the current high or low equals or exceeds the corresponding rolling chart-bar extreme after a full lookback; anchored VWAP reset on a new calendar-period high or low; anchored VWAP reset on the confirmation bar of a pivot high or low with configurable left and right lookbacks; anchored VWAP reset when open exceeds the previous high by a configurable percentage or falls below the previous low by a configurable percentage; anchored VWAP reset when the absolute percentage gap between open and previous close meets a configurable threshold AND volume exceeds its chart-bar simple moving average times a configurable multiplier; the high or low so far in a forming higher-timeframe calendar bar; or the high or low of the last completed higher-timeframe calendar bar.
Calculation: Touch level = anchored VWAP reset at the start of a calendar period, anchored VWAP from the first available chart bar, or anchored VWAP reset when the current low equals or falls below the rolling chart-bar low after a full lookback.
Long: close >= comparison level
Short: close < comparison level
Long: low <= touch level * (1 + touch sensitivity percentage / 100) AND high >= touch level * (1 - touch sensitivity percentage / 100) AND close >= touch level
Short: low <= touch level * (1 + touch sensitivity percentage / 100) AND high >= touch level * (1 - touch sensitivity percentage / 100) AND close < touch level
