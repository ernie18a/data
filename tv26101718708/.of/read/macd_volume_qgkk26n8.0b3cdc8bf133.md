Calculation: Delta = up volume minus down volume estimated from lower-timeframe bars (lower than the chart timeframe), including available intrabars of the current chart bar.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative Delta within the current anchor period, including the current, possibly forming, anchor-period bar.
Long: Delta > 0 OR cumulative volume delta > its value at the start of the anchor period
Short: Delta < 0 OR cumulative volume delta <= its value at the start of the anchor period
