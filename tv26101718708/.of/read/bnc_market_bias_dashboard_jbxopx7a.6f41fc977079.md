Calculation: Per-timeframe bias score = Sum five component scores: EMA stack (+2 for strictly descending EMA lengths in bullish order through the longest EMA; −2 for bearish ordering through the second-longest; otherwise +1 if close is above both long EMAs, −1 if below both, else 0); RSI (+2 above the upper bullish zone, +1 above the middle zone, −1 above the lower support zone, else −2); Stochastic RSI (−1 in the upper extreme, +1 in the upper-middle band, −1 in the lower-middle band, else −2); Ichimoku (+2 above the cloud, −2 below it, otherwise +1 above the base line, else −1); Keltner (−1 above the upper band, +1 below the lower band, otherwise +1 above the basis, else −1). EMA lookbacks increase in the stated stack order; RSI zones increase in order. For non-chart timeframes, use aggregated bars: higher-timeframe values are from the last completed bar and lower-timeframe values from the latest available bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Overall bias score = weighted mean of the selected per-timeframe bias scores, with greater weight for longer timeframes.
Calculation: Bull trendline break = price crosses above an active line joining successively lower pivot highs, after it has remained unbroken for the configured minimum bars. Bear trendline break = price crosses below an active line joining successively higher pivot lows, after it has remained unbroken for the configured minimum bars. The selected close or high/low price source is used for the break.
Long: Any per-timeframe bias score ≥ 3 OR rounded overall bias score ≥ 3
Short: Any per-timeframe bias score ≤ −3 OR rounded overall bias score ≤ −3
Long: Overall bias score crosses upward through the negative strong threshold AND a bull trendline break occurred within the confirmation window, OR a bull trendline break occurs while the overall bias score crossed upward through the negative strong threshold within the confirmation window
Short: Overall bias score crosses downward through the positive strong threshold AND a bear trendline break occurred within the confirmation window, OR a bear trendline break occurs while the overall bias score crossed downward through the positive strong threshold within the confirmation window
Long: Overall bias score crosses upward through the negative soft threshold
Short: Overall bias score crosses downward through the positive soft threshold
