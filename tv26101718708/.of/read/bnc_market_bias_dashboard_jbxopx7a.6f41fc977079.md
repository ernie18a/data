Calculation: Timeframe score = sum of EMA, RSI, stochastic RSI, Ichimoku and Keltner points, evaluated on each selected timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar. Use the chart bar when the timeframes match.
Calculation: EMA points = using five close EMAs ordered from shortest to longest, +2 if all five descend in value from shortest to longest; otherwise -2 if the shortest four ascend in value from shortest to longest; otherwise +1 if close is above both longest EMAs; otherwise -1 if close is below both longest EMAs; otherwise 0.
Calculation: RSI points = +2 if close RSI is at or above the upper threshold; otherwise +1 if at or above the middle threshold; otherwise -1 if at or above the lower threshold; otherwise -2.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI points = apply an SMA to the stochastic of close RSI; assign -1 if at or above the upper threshold, otherwise +1 if at or above the middle threshold, otherwise -1 if at or above the lower threshold, otherwise -2.
Calculation: Ichimoku points = +2 if close is above the displaced cloud, otherwise -2 if below it, otherwise +1 if above the undisplaced baseline, otherwise -1. The cloud consists of the average of conversion and baseline and the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback, both displaced forward by the baseline lookback; conversion uses a shorter lookback than baseline.
Calculation: Keltner points = -1 if close is above the upper EMA-and-ATR channel, otherwise +1 if below its lower channel, otherwise +1 if above its EMA center, otherwise -1.
Calculation: Overall score = weighted mean of the scores of enabled timeframes, with nondecreasing weights as timeframe increases; use zero if none are enabled.
Calculation: Trendlines = on the chart timeframe, confirm pivot highs and lows after an equal number of bars on each side. Connect consecutive confirmed descending pivot highs for a downtrend line and consecutive confirmed ascending pivot lows for an uptrend line. Accept a line only when its normalized angle is strictly inside the configured range; optionally reject it if the selected break source crossed it between the first pivot and either the second pivot or the confirmation bar. Normalize slope as price slope multiplied by a fixed chart-height scale and divided by the current highest-high-minus-lowest-low range over the chart-index-limited lookback; angle is the arctangent of that normalized slope, rounded to the configured precision. On confirmation set the line value to its extrapolated value at the confirmation bar plus one slope increment; subsequently add one slope increment per bar. A newly confirmed pivot with a changed line slope makes the corresponding line unavailable for that bar; otherwise the line is unavailable when the previous break source was beyond the previous line. Retain the last accepted line until replaced.
Calculation: Bull break = the high or close, according to the selected break source, crosses above the downtrend line AND the number of bars since that line was last unavailable exceeds the minimum unbroken-bar count.
Calculation: Bear break = the low or close, according to the selected break source, crosses below the uptrend line AND the number of bars since that line was last unavailable exceeds the minimum unbroken-bar count.
Long: Any enabled timeframe score is at or above the positive signal threshold OR the rounded overall score is at or above that threshold.
Short: Any enabled timeframe score is at or below the negative signal threshold OR the rounded overall score is at or below that threshold.
Long: Overall score crosses above the negative soft threshold.
Short: Overall score crosses below the positive soft threshold.
Long: Signals are enabled AND ((overall score crosses above the negative strong threshold AND a bull break occurred within the confirmation window, including the current bar) OR (a bull break occurs AND the overall score crossed above the negative strong threshold within the confirmation window, including the current bar)).
Short: Signals are enabled AND ((overall score crosses below the positive strong threshold AND a bear break occurred within the confirmation window, including the current bar) OR (a bear break occurs AND the overall score crossed below the positive strong threshold within the confirmation window, including the current bar)).
