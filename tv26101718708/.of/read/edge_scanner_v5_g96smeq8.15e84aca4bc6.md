Calculation: Range high = highest high over the lookback on the scan timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current scan-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Range low = lowest low over the same lookback on the scan timeframe; use the current scan-timeframe bar.
Long: close ≤ range low + (range high − range low) × edge-zone percentage AND close > (range high + range low) / 2
Short: close ≥ range high − (range high − range low) × edge-zone percentage AND close < (range high + range low) / 2
