Calculation: Regime average = Apply the selected RMA, SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, DEMA, or TEMA to sqrt(close). Let b be the result and a = 2/(1 + average length); core = b * (b * a + nz(previous b, 0)). Let r = full-length ATR / half-length ATR, or 1 if the denominator is zero; regime average = core * (1 - r) + nz(previous core, 0) * r. The half-length ATR uses the rounded half of the full length, with a minimum of one bar.
Calculation: Trend state = Initially 0; set to 1 when close > regime average + ATR * band multiplier, set to -1 when close < regime average - ATR * band multiplier, and otherwise retain the previous state.
Calculation: Band width = 2 * Bollinger multiplier * standard deviation of close; compression means band width < its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier.
Calculation: Oscillator composite = Mean of three values, each clamped to [-1, 1]: 2 * (Bollinger %B - 0.5), CCI of close divided by a normalization constant, and ROC of close divided by a multiple of the longer-lookback standard deviation of that ROC. Use %B = 0.5 when the Bollinger span is zero and normalized ROC = 0 when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: Volume intensity = Clamp(volume / SMA(volume) - 1, -1, 1), or 0 when the volume SMA is zero. Candle direction = 1 if close > open, -1 if close < open, otherwise 0. Volume displacement = candle direction * abs(close - open) / (high - low) * volume intensity, or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Displacement = EMA(weighted sum of oscillator composite and volume displacement). Its upper threshold is a positive multiple of its longer-lookback standard deviation; its lower threshold is the negative of that threshold.
Calculation: Structure score = Maintain a state initially 0; set it to 0 if the high one swing lookback ago > the highest high of the current swing lookback, otherwise set it to 1 if the low one swing lookback ago < the lowest low of the current swing lookback, otherwise retain the previous state. The score is 1 when the state is 1 and -1 otherwise.
Calculation: Confluence score = On the chart timeframe, average: 1 if close > EMA(close), otherwise -1; oscillator composite; clamp((band width / its longer-lookback SMA - 1) * volatility multiplier, -1, 1), or 0 if the SMA is zero; structure score; and candle direction * min(1, volume intensity). On the higher timeframe, average: 1 if close > EMA(close), otherwise -1; and 1 if Bollinger %B > 0.5, -1 if %B < 0, otherwise 0, using %B = 0.5 when its span is zero. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming, with historical lookahead: its final score is applied to all chart bars within it. Combine the two scores with the higher-timeframe weight greater than the chart-timeframe weight, replace a missing higher-timeframe score with 0, map the weighted score from [-1, 1] to [0, 100], round, and clamp to [0, 100]. The EMA length matches the regime average length; the Bollinger settings match those used by the oscillator composite.
Calculation: Bullish entry setup = trend state = 1 AND band width >= its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier AND confluence score >= long entry threshold AND displacement > its upper threshold AND (session filter disabled OR UTC hour >= a session start AND UTC hour < its end) AND (FVG filter disabled OR at least one prior bar in the FVG lookback has low > high two bars before it AND the intervening close > that earlier high).
Calculation: Bearish entry setup = trend state = -1 AND band width >= its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier AND confluence score <= short entry threshold AND displacement < its lower threshold AND (session filter disabled OR UTC hour >= a session start AND UTC hour < its end) AND (FVG filter disabled OR at least one prior bar in the FVG lookback has high < low two bars before it AND the intervening close < that earlier low).
Calculation: Entry risk distance = ATR on the signal bar * stop multiplier; target distance = entry risk distance * reward-to-risk multiplier. The entry reference price is the signal bar close.
Calculation: Long stop = entry reference price - entry risk distance; when trailing is enabled, update it on each bar in position to the maximum of its previous value and close - current ATR * trailing multiplier. Short stop = entry reference price + entry risk distance; when trailing is enabled, update it to the minimum of its previous value and close + current ATR * trailing multiplier. Updated stops apply to subsequent bars.
Long: Bullish entry setup = true AND previous bar's bullish entry setup = false.
Short: Bearish entry setup = true AND previous bar's bearish entry setup = false.
Long: Trend state = 1 AND band width >= its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier.
Short: Trend state = -1 AND band width >= its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier.
Long: Displacement > its upper threshold.
Short: Displacement < its lower threshold.
Long: Confluence score >= long bias threshold.
Short: Confluence score <= short bias threshold.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry reference price + target distance OR trend state = -1 OR band width < its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier OR displacement < its lower threshold.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry reference price - target distance OR trend state = 1 OR band width < its longer-lookback SMA * compression multiplier OR displacement > its upper threshold.
