Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range window in New York time; finalize it on the first bar outside that window and reset it when the exchange calendar day changes. Its midpoint is (high + low) / 2 and its range is high - low.
Calculation: Key highs and lows = highs and lows of the last completed bars of three successively longer higher timeframes, together with the highs and lows of the most recently completed chart-bar sessions for each of three configured New York time windows. Update each session level on the first bar outside its session.
Calculation: Proximity = ATR multiplied by a proximity parameter. A high is near a key high when its absolute distance from that level is <= proximity; a low is near a key low when its absolute distance from that level is <= proximity.
Calculation: Upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; body = abs(close - open); range = high - low. Body fraction is body / range when range > 0, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Rejection score = sum of configured weights for each true condition: price is near a key level; the directional wick exceeds both the body and the opposite wick AND the candle closes in the rejection direction; close is on the rejection side of the EMA; volume exceeds its SMA multiplied by a volume parameter; RSI is beyond the directional RSI threshold; body fraction >= a body-quality threshold; price is near a key level from either of the two longer higher timeframes. The first three weights are equal and greater than the volume weight; the volume weight is greater than the equal RSI and body-quality weights; those weights are greater than the longer-timeframe level weight.
