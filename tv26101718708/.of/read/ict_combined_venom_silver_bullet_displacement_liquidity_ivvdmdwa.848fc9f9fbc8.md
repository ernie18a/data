Calculation: Body high = max(open, close); body low = min(open, close).
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when low > high two bars earlier, with lower edge at that earlier high and upper edge at current low; bearish when high < low two bars earlier, with lower edge at current high and upper edge at that earlier low. If volume-imbalance extension is enabled, for a bullish gap replace the lower edge with the first bar's body high when the middle bar's body low exceeds it, and replace the upper edge with the last bar's body low when it exceeds the middle bar's body high. For a bearish gap replace the upper edge with the first bar's body low when it exceeds the middle bar's body high, and replace the lower edge with the last bar's body high when it is below the middle bar's body low.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = highs and lows of the last completed higher-timeframe periods and of the most recently completed specified sessions. A level is available for another sweep only when its value changes.
Calculation: Premarket grid = the last completed specified session's low plus a selectable fraction of its high-low range; equilibrium is its midpoint.
Calculation: Session confirmation level = on the first trading-window bar that breaks only one side of the preceding opening range, find the most recent prior candle within the lookback with a positive body for a low break or a negative body for a high break. If it is the immediately preceding candle, use the current open; otherwise use the higher of the opens of the two bars immediately after that candle for a low break, or the lower for a high break. Measure staleness from the bar supplying that open.
Calculation: First presented gap = the first qualifying fair value gap per specified session and day, whose middle candle is inside that session and whose raw wick-to-wick width is at least the minimum. Use completed lower-timeframe bars when the chart timeframe is higher, otherwise completed chart bars; use the selected extended or unextended gap edges.
Calculation: Entry raid = the lowest low of the recent raid window is below the lowest low of the preceding lookback window, or a low liquidity sweep occurred within the recent raid window; reverse highs and inequalities for a high raid. The raid extreme is the lowest low or highest high of the recent raid window.
Calculation: Entry zone = independently for each direction, retain the latest opposing fair value gap as an inversion candidate. During a setup window, replace any existing zone with, in priority order: today's first presented gap when its last close outside the gap changes from below to above for a long or above to below for a short; a same-direction fair value gap of at least the minimum raw width when the corresponding entry raid exists; or the latest opposing gap, no older than the raid lookback, when the corresponding raid exists and close crosses beyond its far edge in the trade direction. Closes inside a first presented gap do not change its last outside side. Remove an unfilled zone when close passes its far edge against the trade, its age exceeds the limit, or the setup window ends. Test a retest before replacing or expiring the zone, and only after its creation bar.
Calculation: Entry level = the zone's near edge or midpoint. For a confirmed retest, entry price is close; for a limit retest, it is min(open, entry level) for a long or max(open, entry level) for a short.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a raid-based zone, the raid extreme or zone far edge, offset against the trade by a buffer; for a first presented flip, the selected buffered zone far edge, buffered recent swing extreme constrained to be no tighter than the far edge, or fixed distance from entry. A confirmed retest stop is extended beyond the retest wick by the buffer unless the retest-wick stop is selected, in which case it is that buffered wick. A limit entry with retest-wick stop selected uses the buffered far edge.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, either a fixed distance or a multiple of the absolute entry-to-initial-stop distance.
Calculation: Opening-session gap = the span between the previous completed regular session's close and the current session's open, provided its width exceeds the minimum. Its midpoint is tagged when low reaches it after a gap up or high reaches it after a gap down, including the formation bar.
Long: During a trading window, after its first opening-range low break without a simultaneous high break, close >= the unexpired session confirmation level and that level has not previously confirmed.
Short: During a trading window, after its first opening-range high break without a simultaneous low break, close <= the unexpired session confirmation level and that level has not previously confirmed.
Long: A completed bar forms a bullish fair value gap AND the middle bar closes above its open AND its selected absolute body size or high-low range exceeds a multiple of the rolling standard deviation of that same measure AND the confirming bar is inside an allowed trading window when the window restriction is enabled.
Short: A completed bar forms a bearish fair value gap AND the middle bar closes below its open AND its selected absolute body size or high-low range exceeds a multiple of the rolling standard deviation of that same measure AND the confirming bar is inside an allowed trading window when the window restriction is enabled.
Long: Low < an available liquidity low AND close > that level.
Short: High > an available liquidity high AND close < that level.
Long: Close < premarket equilibrium AND the previous close was not below the previous equilibrium.
Short: Close > premarket equilibrium AND the previous close was not above the previous equilibrium.
Long: At a selected premarket grid level, the count of downward wick interactions reaches the required count: low <= level AND body low > level; reset the downward count on an upward wick interaction, a body spanning the level, or a bar wholly below it; reset both counts each day and optionally allow only one signal per level per day.
Short: At a selected premarket grid level, the count of upward wick interactions reaches the required count: high >= level AND body high < level; reset the upward count on a downward wick interaction, a body spanning the level, or a bar wholly above it; reset both counts each day and optionally allow only one signal per level per day.
Long: A bullish fair value gap has raw width >= the minimum AND the middle bar's body low > the first bar's body high AND the last bar's body low > the middle bar's body high AND the bar is within the selected scan scope.
Short: A bearish fair value gap has raw width >= the minimum AND the first bar's body low > the middle bar's body high AND the middle bar's body low > the last bar's body high AND the bar is within the selected scan scope.
Long: A completed bar intersects the opening-session gap AND its demand polarity was set by the close before its first intersection being above the midpoint AND its body low remains >= midpoint for the required number of intersections without an earlier body-low violation.
Short: A completed bar intersects the opening-session gap AND its supply polarity was set by the close before its first intersection being <= the midpoint AND its body high remains <= midpoint for the required number of intersections without an earlier body-high violation.
Short: A completed bar intersects a demand-polarity opening-session gap AND its body low < midpoint for the first time.
Long: A completed bar intersects a supply-polarity opening-session gap AND its body high > midpoint for the first time.
Long: Inside an entry window, an existing long entry zone is retested after its creation bar AND low <= entry level AND, if confirmed retests are selected, close > entry level AND initial stop < entry price AND optional maximum risk, daily loss limit and per-window signal limit are satisfied AND the opening-session gap gate is disabled or its current-day gap is not an untagged gap up within the configured distance of close.
Short: Inside an entry window, an existing short entry zone is retested after its creation bar AND high >= entry level AND, if confirmed retests are selected, close < entry level AND initial stop > entry price AND optional maximum risk, daily loss limit and per-window signal limit are satisfied AND the opening-session gap gate is disabled or its current-day gap is not an untagged gap down within the configured distance of close.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until a later bar survives the stop and target tests and either reaches the optional favorable break-even distance or is the first bar after entry and closes in profit when that option is enabled; thereafter active stop = entry price.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= target OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period; test stop before target, and for a limit entry also test its initial stop on the entry bar.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= target OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period; test stop before target, and for a limit entry also test its initial stop on the entry bar.
