Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Change-of-state level after an opening-range high raid = open of the most recent bearish candle found within a lookback before the raid; if the two preceding opens are used, take the lower. After an opening-range low raid, use the most recent bullish candle's open; if the two preceding opens are used, take the higher.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during its session, sealed when that session ends.
Calculation: Displacement = the prior candle's selected size measure (absolute open-close distance or high-low range) exceeds its rolling standard deviation times a strength factor.
Calculation: Prior day, week, and month liquidity levels = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Asia and London liquidity levels = high and low of the most recently completed session range.
Calculation: First presented fair value gap = first qualifying bullish or bearish fair value gap of the session, detected from one-minute bars (lower timeframe than the chart when the chart is above one minute; use completed one-minute bars).
Calculation: Premarket equilibrium = midpoint of the high and low of the premarket session range, locked when that session ends.
Calculation: Raid = price trades beyond the high or low of a recent lookback range, or sweeps a liquidity level by trading beyond it and closing back on its original side.
Calculation: Inverted fair value gap = an opposing fair value gap whose far boundary is crossed by a candle close.
Long: During a trading window, the opening-range low is raided without the high being raided, and a candle closes above the change-of-state level before it becomes stale.
Short: During a trading window, the opening-range high is raided without the low being raided, and a candle closes below the change-of-state level before it becomes stale.
Long: A bullish displacement candle is followed by a bullish fair value gap; the gap and displacement are confirmed at the close of the third pattern candle. Short: A bearish displacement candle is followed by a bearish fair value gap; the gap and displacement are confirmed at the close of the third pattern candle.
Long: A liquidity low is swept (low < level and close > level). Short: A liquidity high is swept (high > level and close < level).
Long: A bullish first presented fair value gap forms; use the first qualifying gap in the session, detected on one-minute bars (lower timeframe than the chart when applicable; completed bars).
Short: A bearish first presented fair value gap forms; use the first qualifying gap in the session, detected on one-minute bars (lower timeframe than the chart when applicable; completed bars).
Long: A bullish suspension block forms when a bullish fair value gap also has a body gap at each end. Short: A bearish suspension block forms when a bearish fair value gap also has a body gap at each end.
Long: Price crosses from at or above premarket equilibrium to below it. Short: Price crosses from at or below premarket equilibrium to above it.
Long: A premarket range grid level is tagged by the low while the candle body stays above it for the required consecutive interactions. Short: A premarket range grid level is tagged by the high while the candle body stays below it for the required consecutive interactions.
Long: Within an enabled Silver Bullet window, after a recent low raid, a bullish fair value gap forms and its zone is retested: price reaches its near edge or midpoint and either closes back above that level or, for limit-on-touch entries, touches it. Also allow a retest setup after a recent low raid when a bearish fair value gap inverts on a close above its top. Also allow a bullish first-presented fair value gap flip when a close moves from below the gap to above its top, followed by the same retest condition. The retest must occur after the zone forms and before it expires; the zone must not have been invalidated by a close through its far edge. Apply enabled opening-range-gap gate, stop-width, daily-loss, and per-window signal limits.
Short: Within an enabled Silver Bullet window, after a recent high raid, a bearish fair value gap forms and its zone is retested: price reaches its near edge or midpoint and either closes back below that level or, for limit-on-touch entries, touches it. Also allow a retest setup after a recent high raid when a bullish fair value gap inverts on a close below its bottom. Also allow a bearish first-presented fair value gap flip when a close moves from above the gap to below its bottom, followed by the same retest condition. The retest must occur after the zone forms and before it expires; the zone must not have been invalidated by a close through its far edge. Apply enabled opening-range-gap gate, stop-width, daily-loss, and per-window signal limits.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= target OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period; if stop and target are both touched on one bar, stop takes precedence. Move stop to entry when the configured favorable price distance is reached or, if enabled, when the first bar after entry closes in profit.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= target OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period; if stop and target are both touched on one bar, stop takes precedence. Move stop to entry when the configured favorable price distance is reached or, if enabled, when the first bar after entry closes in profit.
