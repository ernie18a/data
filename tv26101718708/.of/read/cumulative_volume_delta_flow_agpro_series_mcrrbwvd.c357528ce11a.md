Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative signed volume, reset at each new regular session. For each chart bar, signed volume is the sum of volume from lower-timeframe bars (lower than the chart timeframe; use completed sub-bars), signed + for close above open, − for close below open, and 0 otherwise. If that sum is zero, use chart-bar volume signed by its candle direction; for a doji, use +0.5 if the lower wick is longer, −0.5 if the upper wick is longer, and 0 if equal.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows in the same configured number of chart bars on each side; confirm after the bars on the right have completed. Pair each pivot with CVD at its pivot bar.
Calculation: CVD change = current CVD − prior chart bar CVD.
Calculation: CVD change standard deviation = rolling standard deviation of CVD change over the configured lookback.
