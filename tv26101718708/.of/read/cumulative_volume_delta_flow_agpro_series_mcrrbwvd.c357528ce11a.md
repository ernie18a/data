Calculation: Bar delta = choose one mode: (1) sum sign(close − open) × volume over all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; (2) chart-bar volume × polarity, where polarity is sign(close − open), or for a doji is +0.5 if the lower wick is longer than the upper wick, −0.5 if the upper wick is longer, and 0 otherwise; or (3) use the lower-timeframe sum when nonzero and otherwise use the chart-bar polarity result. Treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of bar delta, initialized to zero; optionally reset to the current bar delta at the start of each configured session, day, or week.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side; confirm it only after the right-side bars complete. For equal highs or lows, select the rightmost occurrence. Pair each pivot with cumulative volume delta at the pivot bar, and retain the latest two confirmed pivots of each kind.
Calculation: Delta change = cumulative volume delta minus its previous-bar value, using zero for a missing previous value. Delta-change deviation = rolling population standard deviation of delta change.
Calculation: Cooldown = the number of chart bars since the most recent enabled regular, hidden, or exhaustion signal of either direction; initialize the last signal bar to a sufficiently early bar. Evaluate all signals on a bar before updating the last signal bar.
Calculation: Flow trend = up when cumulative volume delta is above its EMA and above its value a configurable number of chart bars earlier; down when it is below both; otherwise flat. Use zero for a missing earlier value.
Calculation: Signal age = chart bars since the most recent enabled divergence or exhaustion signal of the specified direction. A signal is fresh when its age is at most the cooldown parameter.
Long: On a completed chart bar, cooldown >= its threshold AND a new low pivot is confirmed AND the latest low pivot is lower than the previous low pivot AND its paired cumulative volume delta is higher than the previous low pivot's paired cumulative volume delta.
Long: On a completed chart bar, cooldown >= its threshold AND a new low pivot is confirmed AND the latest low pivot is higher than the previous low pivot AND its paired cumulative volume delta is lower than the previous low pivot's paired cumulative volume delta.
Long: On a completed chart bar, cooldown >= its threshold AND delta-change deviation > 0 AND delta change < −(exhaustion threshold × delta-change deviation) AND close > open.
Short: On a completed chart bar, cooldown >= its threshold AND a new high pivot is confirmed AND the latest high pivot is higher than the previous high pivot AND its paired cumulative volume delta is lower than the previous high pivot's paired cumulative volume delta.
Short: On a completed chart bar, cooldown >= its threshold AND a new high pivot is confirmed AND the latest high pivot is lower than the previous high pivot AND its paired cumulative volume delta is higher than the previous high pivot's paired cumulative volume delta.
Short: On a completed chart bar, cooldown >= its threshold AND delta-change deviation > 0 AND delta change > exhaustion threshold × delta-change deviation AND close < open.
Long: Bullish signal age <= cooldown threshold AND (no bearish signal has occurred OR bullish signal age <= bearish signal age).
Short: Bearish signal age <= cooldown threshold AND (no bullish signal has occurred OR bearish signal age < bullish signal age).
Long: Flow trend is up.
Short: Flow trend is down.
