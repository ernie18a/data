Calculation: Zero-lag EMA = EMA(close + (close − close[floor((lookback − 1) / 2)]), lookback); the main trend uses a longer lookback than the fast trigger
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback bars before the current bar
Long: (chart bar is confirmed AND close > prior range high + breakout buffer AND fast zero-lag EMA > main zero-lag EMA AND close > main zero-lag EMA AND main zero-lag EMA rise > ATR × slope threshold AND absolute distance between fast and main zero-lag EMAs > ATR × separation threshold AND high − low > ATR × candle-range threshold AND close > previous high AND close > open AND absolute distance from close to main zero-lag EMA ≥ ATR × line-block threshold AND previous range high − previous range low > compression threshold OR close is outside the prior range AND no long signal has printed in the current trend AND cooldown has elapsed) OR (a buy signal on a higher-timeframe bar changes from absent to present) OR (a buy signal on a lower-timeframe bar changes from absent to present AND no lower-timeframe long signal has printed in the current trend)
Short: (chart bar is confirmed AND close < prior range low − breakout buffer AND fast zero-lag EMA < main zero-lag EMA AND close < main zero-lag EMA AND main zero-lag EMA fall < −ATR × slope threshold AND absolute distance between fast and main zero-lag EMAs > ATR × separation threshold AND high − low > ATR × candle-range threshold AND close < previous low AND close < open AND absolute distance from close to main zero-lag EMA ≥ ATR × line-block threshold AND previous range high − previous range low > compression threshold OR close is outside the prior range AND no short signal has printed in the current trend AND cooldown has elapsed) OR (a sell signal on a higher-timeframe bar changes from absent to present) OR (a sell signal on a lower-timeframe bar changes from absent to present AND no lower-timeframe short signal has printed in the current trend)
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry, high ≥ entry price + configured target distance
Exit: Short, on a bar after entry, low ≤ entry price − configured target distance
