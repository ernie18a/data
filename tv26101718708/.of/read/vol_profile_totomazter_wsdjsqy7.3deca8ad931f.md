Calculation: Estimated bullish volume = volume × (0.5 + direction × body/range × 0.5), where body = |close − open|, range = high − low, direction = 1 when close ≥ open and −1 otherwise, and body/range = 0.5 when range = 0; estimated bearish volume = volume − estimated bullish volume
Long: estimated bullish volume > estimated bearish volume
Short: estimated bullish volume ≤ estimated bearish volume
