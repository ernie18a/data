Calculation: MACD = faster-length EMA of close minus slower-length EMA of close, calculated on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar
Calculation: MACD signal line = signal-length EMA of MACD, calculated on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar
Calculation: RSI = RSI of close on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar
Long: On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, MACD crosses above the MACD signal line AND RSI is above its buy-level parameter
Short: On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, MACD crosses below the MACD signal line
