Calculation: Signal bars = aggregated bars on the reversal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar historically and the bar still forming in real time.
Calculation: Bullish crossover count = number of these four pairs that crossed upward within the reversal lookback on signal bars: close and short EMA, short EMA and medium EMA, short EMA and long EMA, close and long EMA. An upward crossover requires the first value to be greater than the second now and less than or equal to it on the preceding bar. Bearish crossover count uses downward crosses with the comparisons reversed. The short, medium, and long EMA lookbacks increase in that order.
Calculation: Bullish reversal state = bullish crossover count at least the required count AND (bearish crossover count below the required count OR the most recent bullish crossover is at least as recent as the most recent bearish crossover). Bearish reversal state = bearish crossover count at least the required count AND (bullish crossover count below the required count OR the most recent bearish crossover is more recent than the most recent bullish crossover).
Calculation: Bullish confirmation = bullish reversal state AND (the close has remained above the medium EMA for the required consecutive signal bars OR, after the bullish reversal state first became true, a signal bar had low at or below medium EMA plus an ATR-based buffer and close above medium EMA, that test occurred within the pullback lookback, and the current close is above medium EMA). Bearish confirmation reverses the comparisons, using high at or above medium EMA minus the ATR-based buffer and close below medium EMA. Each confirmation is latched until its corresponding reversal state becomes false; a new confirmation occurs when that latch changes from false to true.
Long: Bullish reversal state is true OR a new bullish confirmation occurred within the confirmation display lookback on signal bars.
Short: Bearish reversal state is true OR a new bearish confirmation occurred within the confirmation display lookback on signal bars.
