Calculation: For direction d = +1 for bullish and -1 for bearish, the reference is the latest confirmed pivot low for d = +1 or pivot high for d = -1, using equal numbers of bars on both sides. ATR is the chart-timeframe ATR, falling back to true range before ATR is available and floored at the minimum price increment. Candle range is max(high - low, minimum price increment); close position is (close - low) / candle range; upper wick is high - max(open, close); lower wick is min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: A reference is touched when low <= reference + ATR * zone-width parameter AND high >= reference - ATR * zone-width parameter. Its distance is abs(close - reference) if touched, otherwise a large sentinel value. Bullish components count close > support, close >= open, lower wick >= upper wick, and close position >= 0.55. Bearish components count close < resistance, close <= open, upper wick >= lower wick, and close position <= 0.45. Each true comparison contributes one.
Calculation: A new reaction replaces the previous active reaction. On its bar, store direction d, close E, and bar index. For a bullish reaction, zone bottom = min(low, support - ATR * zone-width parameter); for a bearish reaction, zone top = max(high, resistance + ATR * zone-width parameter). Invalidation is zone bottom - ATR * buffer parameter for bullish reactions or zone top + ATR * buffer parameter for bearish reactions. Initialize best progress and worst adverse excursion to zero, next-bar score to zero, and outcome to pending.
Calculation: Clamp all scores to [0, 100]. On the reaction bar, reaction score = 38 * directional close position + 32 * directional wick / candle range + 15 * abs(close - open) / candle range + 15 * direction agreement, where directional close position is close position for bullish reactions and 1 - close position for bearish reactions, directional wick is lower wick for bullish reactions and upper wick for bearish reactions, and direction agreement is 1 when d * (close - open) >= 0, otherwise 0. Volume score = clamp(100 * relative volume / 1.70), where relative volume is volume / its SMA when that SMA is positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: On every bar from the reaction bar onward, best progress is the maximum so far of 0 and (high - E) / current ATR for bullish reactions or (E - low) / current ATR for bearish reactions. Worst adverse excursion is the maximum so far of 0 and (E - low) / current ATR for bullish reactions or (high - E) / current ATR for bearish reactions. On the first bar after the reaction, set next-bar score = clamp(100 * d * (close - E) / (current ATR * 0.55)) and retain it. Progress score = clamp(100 * best progress / max(target-distance parameter, 0.25)). Defense score = 0 if invalidated, otherwise clamp(100 - 38 * worst adverse excursion / max(buffer parameter + zone-width parameter, 0.10)). Time score = clamp(100 - 42 * bars elapsed / tracking-length parameter, 25, 100). Follow-through score = clamp(0.24 * reaction score + 0.28 * progress score + 0.18 * next-bar score + 0.12 * volume score + 0.12 * defense score + 0.06 * time score).
Calculation: An active reaction is invalidated when d * (close - invalidation) < 0. Its pending outcome becomes failed on invalidation; otherwise it becomes continued when bars elapsed > 0 AND follow-through score >= score threshold AND progress score >= 48; otherwise it becomes expired when bars elapsed >= tracking-length parameter AND follow-through score < score threshold. Once set, an outcome remains unchanged until a new reaction replaces it.
Long: The bar is closed AND bars since the previous reaction >= cooldown parameter OR no previous reaction exists; AND support is touched AND bullish components >= required component count AND support distance <= resistance distance.
Short: The bar is closed AND bars since the previous reaction >= cooldown parameter OR no previous reaction exists; AND resistance is touched AND bearish components >= required component count AND resistance distance < support distance.
Long: The bar is closed AND the active reaction is bullish AND its outcome was pending before this bar AND close >= invalidation AND bars elapsed > 0 AND follow-through score >= score threshold AND progress score >= 48.
Short: The bar is closed AND the active reaction is bearish AND its outcome was pending before this bar AND close <= invalidation AND bars elapsed > 0 AND follow-through score >= score threshold AND progress score >= 48.
Exit: long, the bar is closed AND the active reaction is bullish AND its outcome was pending before this bar AND close < invalidation.
Exit: short, the bar is closed AND the active reaction is bearish AND its outcome was pending before this bar AND close > invalidation.
