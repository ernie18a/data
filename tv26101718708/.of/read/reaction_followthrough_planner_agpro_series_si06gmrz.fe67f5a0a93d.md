Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low with the selected lookback number of bars on each side; becomes available when the right-side bars have completed. The latest confirmed swing high is resistance; the latest confirmed swing low is support.
Calculation: Reaction tolerance = ATR × reaction-zone-width parameter; a support/resistance is touched when the bar’s low–high range overlaps its level ± reaction tolerance.
Calculation: Bullish reaction components = count of: close > support, close ≥ open, lower wick ≥ upper wick, close position within bar range ≥ 0.55. Bearish reaction components = count of: close < resistance, close ≤ open, upper wick ≥ lower wick, close position within bar range ≤ 0.45. Required component count is controlled by response mode: fast uses the lowest threshold, balanced the middle threshold, strict the highest.
Calculation: Close position within bar range = (close − low) / (high − low); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Bullish reaction zone bottom = min(low, support − reaction tolerance); bearish reaction zone top = max(high, resistance + reaction tolerance). Bullish reaction zone top = max(support + reaction tolerance, min(open, close)); bearish reaction zone bottom = min(resistance − reaction tolerance, max(open, close)).
Calculation: Bullish invalidation = bullish reaction zone bottom − ATR × invalidation-buffer parameter; bearish invalidation = bearish reaction zone top + ATR × invalidation-buffer parameter.
Calculation: Reaction score = clamp(0–100, 38 × close defense + 32 × wick defense + 15 × body share + 15 × body alignment). For bullish reactions, close defense is close position and wick defense is lower wick / bar range; for bearish reactions they are 1 − close position and upper wick / bar range. Body share = abs(close − open) / bar range; body alignment = 1 if the candle direction agrees with the reaction, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(0–100, volume / SMA(volume, volume-baseline lookback) / 1.70 × 100); reaction score and volume score are fixed at the reaction bar.
Calculation: Best progress = maximum favorable high/low excursion from reaction close, divided by current ATR, since the reaction bar. Progress score = clamp(0–100, best progress / target-ATR parameter × 100). Next-bar score = clamp(0–100, directional close progress on the first bar after the reaction / (ATR × 0.55) × 100); zero if progress is negative.
Calculation: Worst adverse excursion = maximum adverse low/high excursion from reaction close, divided by current ATR, since the reaction bar. Defense score = 0 if invalidated; otherwise clamp(0–100, 100 − worst adverse excursion / max(invalidation-buffer parameter + reaction-zone-width parameter, 0.10) × 38).
Calculation: Time-decay score = clamp(25–100, 100 − bars elapsed / follow-through-track-bars parameter × 42). Follow-through score = clamp(0–100, 0.24 × reaction score + 0.28 × progress score + 0.18 × next-bar score + 0.12 × volume score + 0.12 × defense score + 0.06 × time-decay score).
Long: Confirmed bar AND cooldown since the last reaction has elapsed AND support is touched AND bullish reaction components ≥ required component count AND (resistance is not touched OR abs(close − support) ≤ abs(close − resistance)).
Short: Confirmed bar AND cooldown since the last reaction has elapsed AND resistance is touched AND bearish reaction components ≥ required component count AND abs(close − resistance) < abs(close − support).
Long: A bullish reaction is active AND confirmed bar AND bars elapsed > 0 AND close ≥ bullish invalidation AND follow-through score ≥ minimum-score parameter AND progress score ≥ 48.
Short: A bearish reaction is active AND confirmed bar AND bars elapsed > 0 AND close ≤ bearish invalidation AND follow-through score ≥ minimum-score parameter AND progress score ≥ 48.
Exit: Long, confirmed close < bullish invalidation.
Exit: Short, confirmed close > bearish invalidation.
