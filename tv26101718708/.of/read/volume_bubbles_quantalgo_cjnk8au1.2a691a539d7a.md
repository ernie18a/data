Calculation: Net volume delta = sum of signed volumes of lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including the lower-timeframe bar still forming. Assign positive volume when a lower-timeframe close is above its open and negative volume when below; if equal, use the sign of its close change from the previous lower-timeframe close, retaining the previous lower-timeframe sign if unchanged.
Calculation: Volume threshold and absolute-delta threshold = linearly interpolated percentiles of chart-bar volume and absolute net volume delta, respectively, over each of three lookbacks including the current bar; the lookbacks are short, medium, and long. Use the lowest cluster-tier percentile for both series.
Long: The selected minimum number of the three lookbacks has current volume >= its volume threshold, or has current absolute net volume delta >= its absolute-delta threshold, according to the selected detection method: volume only, delta only, both, or either; AND close >= open for candle classification, net volume delta > 0 for delta classification, or both conditions for combined classification. If delta is unavailable, use volume-only detection and candle classification.
Short: The selected minimum number of the three lookbacks has current volume >= its volume threshold, or has current absolute net volume delta >= its absolute-delta threshold, according to the selected detection method: volume only, delta only, both, or either; AND close < open for candle classification, net volume delta < 0 for delta classification, or both conditions for combined classification. If delta is unavailable, use volume-only detection and candle classification.
