Calculation: Daily strong start = the current 1D bar’s open is above the previous completed 1D bar’s close, and the current 1D bar’s low is at least 99.5% of that previous close; use the still-forming 1D bar.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume; use the shared lookback for SVRO and reversal conditions.
Calculation: SVRO contraction phase = EMA(shorter) > EMA(medium) > EMA(longer); close is at least the prior lookback low increased by the minimum prior-uptrend percentage; within the most recent 30 chart bars, including the current bar, there is a bar whose close-to-open gain meets the minimum move and whose volume meets the minimum volume; current high-low range as a percentage of low is at most the contraction maximum; current volume is below the volume average times the SVRO dry-up factor; and close is at or above the primary EMA or no more than the maximum pierce percentage below it.
Calculation: Inside bar = current high is below the previous bar’s high and current low is above the previous bar’s low.
Calculation: Reversal base = EMA(medium) > EMA(longer); primary EMA is above its value three bars earlier; close is at least the minimum pullback percentage below the highest high of the last 20 bars; at least the minimum count of the last seven bars, including the current bar, closed below their opens; and close is within the maximum proximity percentage of the primary EMA.
Long: previous bar had an SVRO contraction phase AND daily strong start AND (current volume > volume average OR previous bar was an inside bar)
Long: reversal base AND (daily strong start OR (current volume >= volume average times the stop-volume factor AND current high-low range as a percentage of low <= stop-volume maximum AND close is in the upper 30% of the current bar) OR (close < open AND open-to-close decline as a percentage of open >= climax-volume minimum AND current volume >= volume average times the climax-volume factor))
Exit: Long, close < stop; for SVRO, stop = entry price × (1 − 1%); for reversal, stop = low of the pullback bar
