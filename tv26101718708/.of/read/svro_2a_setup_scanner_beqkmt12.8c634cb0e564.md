Calculation: Strong start = reference-timeframe open > previous reference-timeframe close AND reference-timeframe low >= previous reference-timeframe close × a fraction. The reference timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; on historical bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. On a chart of the reference timeframe, use the current bar. A higher-timeframe bar may still be forming in real time.
Calculation: Contraction phase = primary close EMA > longer close EMA AND longer close EMA > longest close EMA AND (close - lowest low over the prior-uptrend lookback, including the current bar) / lowest low >= minimum gain fraction AND at least one bar within the recent-event lookback, including the current bar, has (close - open) / open >= minimum move fraction AND volume >= minimum volume AND (high - low) / low <= maximum range fraction AND volume < volume SMA × dry-volume factor AND close >= primary close EMA × an EMA-pierce factor.
Calculation: Reversal base = longer close EMA > longest close EMA AND primary close EMA > its value a specified number of bars ago AND (highest high over the pullback lookback, including the current bar - close) / highest high >= minimum pullback fraction AND the number of bars with close < open over the bearish-bar lookback, including the current bar, >= minimum bearish-bar count AND abs(close - primary close EMA) / primary close EMA <= maximum EMA-distance fraction.
Long: Contraction phase on the previous bar AND strong start AND (volume > volume SMA OR (previous bar high < the high two bars ago AND previous bar low > the low two bars ago)).
Long: Reversal base AND (strong start OR (volume >= volume SMA × stop-volume factor AND (high - low) / low <= maximum stop-volume range fraction AND close >= high - (high - low) × upper-range fraction) OR (close < open AND (open - close) / open >= minimum climax-drop fraction AND volume >= volume SMA × climax-volume factor)).
Exit: long entered on a contraction-phase entry, close < entry-signal bar low - entry-signal bar close × stop fraction.
Exit: long entered on a reversal entry, close < low of the bar preceding the entry-signal bar.
