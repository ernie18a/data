Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = On the chart timeframe and each enabled higher timeframe (using that timeframe’s current bar, which may still be forming), confirm a swing high after the configured lookback; when a later close exceeds it, use the range of the lowest-low candle between the swing and break as the zone. Discard it if its height exceeds the configured ATR multiple. It remains live until the configured wick-or-close invalidation price falls below its bottom.
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = On the chart timeframe and each enabled higher timeframe (using that timeframe’s current bar, which may still be forming), confirm a swing low after the configured lookback; when a later close falls below it, use the range of the highest-high candle between the swing and break as the zone. Discard it if its height exceeds the configured ATR multiple. It remains live until the configured wick-or-close invalidation price rises above its top.
Calculation: Demand zone = On the chart timeframe and each enabled higher timeframe (using that timeframe’s current bar, which may still be forming), when all four consecutive candles have bullish bodies at least the configured fraction of the current average body over the longer lookback, use the range of the candle immediately preceding those four as the zone, clamp its height to the configured ATR multiple, and require the configured minimum spacing from the prior demand zone. It remains live until the configured wick-or-close invalidation price falls below its bottom.
Calculation: Supply zone = On the chart timeframe and each enabled higher timeframe (using that timeframe’s current bar, which may still be forming), when all four consecutive candles have bearish bodies at least the configured fraction of the current average body over the longer lookback, use the range of the candle immediately preceding those four as the zone, clamp its height to the configured ATR multiple, and require the configured minimum spacing from the prior supply zone. It remains live until the configured wick-or-close invalidation price rises above its top.
Long: A live demand or bullish order-block zone is retested (low < zone top), and more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous retest of that zone type; for demand, the zone is at least the configured minimum age in its own timeframe.
Short: A live supply or bearish order-block zone is retested (high > zone bottom), and more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous retest of that zone type; for supply, the zone is at least the configured minimum age in its own timeframe.
Long: A supply or bearish order-block zone is invalidated on this bar, and its age exceeds the configured minimum in chart bars.
Short: A demand or bullish order-block zone is invalidated on this bar, and its age exceeds the configured minimum in chart bars.
