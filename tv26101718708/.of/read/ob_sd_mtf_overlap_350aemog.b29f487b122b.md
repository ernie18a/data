Calculation: Zone timeframe data = chart-timeframe bars or the last completed bar of an enabled higher timeframe; evaluate decisions on confirmed chart bars.
Calculation: Swing high and low = maintain a swing state; switch to high when the high swing-length bars ago exceeds every high since then, otherwise switch to low when the low swing-length bars ago is below every low since then. Record a new swing only when the state switches, and allow each recorded swing to be broken once.
Calculation: Bullish order block = on the first close above the latest swing high, take the high and low of the lowest-low bar between the swing and the break, excluding both; retain it if its height does not exceed an ATR multiple. Bearish order block = on the first close below the latest swing low, use the high and low of the highest-high bar over the same interval, subject to the same size test. On ties use the oldest matching bar.
Calculation: Demand zone = when every bar in a momentum window has close > open and body size >= a multiple of the current bar's average body size, and the distance from the previous demand creation exceeds the zone-spacing parameter, use the high and low of the bar immediately before the bar preceding the momentum window. Supply zone = the same construction when every qualifying bar has close <= open, with separate spacing from the previous supply creation. Clamp each zone symmetrically to a maximum ATR multiple. The body-size average and ATR use longer lookbacks than the momentum window.
Calculation: Zone state = calculate zones independently on each enabled timeframe, retaining the most recent configured number per type and timeframe. A bullish order block or demand zone is invalidated when low, or optionally min(open, close), falls below its bottom; a bearish order block or supply zone is invalidated when high, or optionally max(open, close), rises above its top. Remove an invalidated bullish order block if a later high exceeds its top, and remove an invalidated bearish order block if a later low falls below its bottom. Merge same-type zones whose time and price ranges overlap, repeatedly using their union; pad supply and demand price ranges by an ATR fraction for overlap testing and clamp merged supply and demand zones again. A merged zone's start is the earliest component start and its break time is the latest component break time, if any. Only zones originating within the configured historical processing window qualify.
Calculation: Eligible zone = a non-invalidated zone; for supply and demand, its age must also reach the minimum zone age measured in its owning timeframe. A retest requires more than the cooldown number of chart bars since the previous retest of the same zone type; the cooldown is shared across zones of that type.
Long: A bullish order block is created OR a demand zone becomes eligible.
Long: Chart low < the top of an eligible bullish order block or demand zone AND its retest cooldown has elapsed.
Long: A bearish order block or supply zone is invalidated on the current chart bar AND its lifetime exceeds the minimum break age measured in chart bars.
Short: A bearish order block is created OR a supply zone becomes eligible.
Short: Chart high > the bottom of an eligible bearish order block or supply zone AND its retest cooldown has elapsed.
Short: A bullish order block or demand zone is invalidated on the current chart bar AND its lifetime exceeds the minimum break age measured in chart bars.
