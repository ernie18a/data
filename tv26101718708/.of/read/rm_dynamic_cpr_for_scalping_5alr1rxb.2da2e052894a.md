Calculation: CPR levels = From the last completed bar of a higher timeframe: pivot = (high + low + close) / 3; bottom central = (high + low) / 2; top central = 2 × pivot − bottom central; R1 = 2 × pivot − low; S1 = 2 × pivot − high
Long: close > top central OR close > R1
Short: close < bottom central OR close < S1
