Calculation: Radar lines = Apply short- and long-lookback EMAs to (open + close) / 2 on bars where close > open, ignoring other bars; smooth each EMA with the same SMA. Upper and lower radar are their maximum and minimum; radar midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Opening high and low = High and low of the first chart bar of the exchange trading day, or of the first completed bar of a configurable source timeframe on that day. The source timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use completed source bars, with the last available source bar selected within each chart bar. Initialize only when the first source bar's values for the current day are available; reset at the start of each trading day.
Calculation: Signal state = Reset to neutral at the start of each trading day. On each confirmed chart bar, set it to short if close < opening low AND close < lower radar AND absolute candle body > sum of upper and lower wicks AND short radar line < long radar line; set it to long if close > opening high AND close > upper radar AND absolute candle body > sum of upper and lower wicks AND short radar line > long radar line. Then set a short state to long if close > upper radar AND short radar line > long radar line; set a long state to short if close < lower radar AND short radar line < long radar line. Otherwise retain the state.
Calculation: Momentum score = Clamp to a symmetric maximum and minimum the weighted sum of (close minus close at an impulse lookback) / ATR and ((close minus previous close) minus (previous close minus the close before it)) / ATR. If ATR is unavailable or zero, use zero for both normalized terms.
Calculation: Stop = On a new signal, initialize the long stop to upper radar + a fraction of (upper radar - radar midpoint), or the short stop to lower radar - a fraction of (radar midpoint - lower radar). Activate it only if it is below the long entry close or above the short entry close. Reset stop and trade state at the start of each trading day. On later confirmed bars, first test the existing stop for exit. If it is not broken, calculate a long candidate as the maximum of the existing stop and current initial long-stop formula, or a short candidate as the minimum of the existing stop and current initial short-stop formula. When optional profit protection is active, also include the entry price plus, for long, or minus, for short, a configurable fraction of the maximum favorable excursion since entry; include the latest confirmed pivot low minus an ATR buffer for long, or pivot high plus an ATR buffer for short, only if that pivot occurred after entry. Update the stop only if the candidate tightens it and remains strictly below the current close for long or above it for short. Profit protection activates on a confirmed bar when the maximum favorable excursion as a percentage of entry reaches its threshold AND the momentum score reaches the positive threshold for long or the negative threshold for short; it stays active until exit or a new entry. Track favorable excursion using highs for long and lows for short, including the entry bar.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND signal state = long AND previous chart bar's signal state != long.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND signal state = short AND previous chart bar's signal state != short.
Long: Continuation enabled AND a stop exit occurred earlier in the current trading day AND no protected trade is active AND signal state = long AND close > upper radar AND initial long stop < close AND the current confirmed chart bar is later than the stop-exit bar.
Short: Continuation enabled AND a stop exit occurred earlier in the current trading day AND no protected trade is active AND signal state = short AND close < lower radar AND initial short stop > close AND the current confirmed chart bar is later than the stop-exit bar.
Long: Close > upper radar.
Short: Close < lower radar.
Long: Short radar line > long radar line.
Short: Short radar line <= long radar line.
Long: Momentum score >= positive momentum-state threshold.
Short: Momentum score <= negative momentum-state threshold.
Exit: long, active stop AND confirmed chart bar later than the entry bar AND close < previous stop.
Exit: short, active stop AND confirmed chart bar later than the entry bar AND close > previous stop.
