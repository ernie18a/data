Calculation: Market memory average = EMA of close × (1 + weighted historical momentum / 100). After more than scan depth + pattern length bars, scan historical bars from pattern length + 1 bars ago through scan depth bars ago. For each, compute distance as |current shortest ROC − historical shortest ROC| / 2 + |current middle ROC − historical middle ROC| / 4 + |current longest ROC − historical longest ROC| / 6 + 2 × |current RSI / 100 − historical RSI / 100| + |current ATR / close × 100 − historical ATR / close × 100| / 2 + |current relative volume − historical relative volume| / 1.5. Relative volume is volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the SMA is zero. Weight each historical bar by 100 × exp(−distance × sensitivity). Weighted historical momentum is the weighted mean of the longest ROC among the highest-weighted matches; it is undefined when total weight is zero.
Long: Market memory average > market memory average at the slope lookback.
Short: Market memory average < market memory average at the slope lookback.
