Calculation: Smoothed market-memory average = EMA of close × (1 + similarity-weighted mean of the 5-bar ROC of the top-matching historical bars ÷ 100); rank prior bars from pattern-length + 1 through scan depth by similarity, where similarity = 100 × exp(−sensitivity × distance), and distance = |1-bar ROC difference|/2 + |3-bar ROC difference|/4 + |5-bar ROC difference|/6 + 2×|RSI difference| + |ATR% difference|/2 + |relative-volume difference|/1.5. RSI is divided by 100; ATR% = ATR/close × 100; relative volume = volume/SMA(volume). Use the same chart-timeframe bars for all calculations. The slope is the smoothed average minus its value at the longer slope-detection lookback.
Long: close crosses above the smoothed market-memory average OR slope > 0
Short: close crosses below the smoothed market-memory average OR slope < 0
