Calculation: Main moving average = configured EMA, SMA, WMA, VWMA, or VWAP; VWAP uses HLC3
Calculation: Linear regression slope = linreg(close, lookback, 0) − linreg(close, lookback, 1) on the configured slope timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); use the latest value, which is the forming bar in realtime and the last completed bar in historical data
Long: Flat AND close crosses above the main moving average AND (slope filter enabled AND linear regression slope > 0 OR slope filter disabled AND (daily filter disabled OR previous completed daily close > previous completed daily open)) AND not past the Friday cutoff; save this bar’s low and main moving average + entry offset as the stop-entry trigger; while setup remains active, enter long when price reaches or exceeds the saved trigger; cancel the setup if close crosses below the main moving average before entry
Short: Flat AND close crosses below the main moving average AND (slope filter enabled AND linear regression slope < 0 OR slope filter disabled AND (daily filter disabled OR previous completed daily close < previous completed daily open)) AND not past the Friday cutoff; save this bar’s high and main moving average − entry offset as the stop-entry trigger; while setup remains active, enter short when price reaches or falls below the saved trigger; cancel the setup if close crosses above the main moving average before entry
Exit: long, low <= saved setup-bar low OR high >= entry price + any of six progressively larger target distances OR activation reached (high >= entry price + activation distance) AND close crosses below the main moving average OR Friday cutoff reached
Exit: short, high >= saved setup-bar high OR low <= entry price − any of six progressively larger target distances OR activation reached (low <= entry price − activation distance) AND close crosses above the main moving average OR Friday cutoff reached
