Calculation: SmoothedHA = Heiken Ashi built from smoothed OHLC. Step 1: s_o, s_h, s_l, s_c = Wilder RMA of open, high, low, close with a medium lookback. Step 2: ha_c = (s_o + s_h + s_l + s_c) / 4. ha_o = (s_o + s_c) / 2 on the first bar, otherwise (ha_o[1] + ha_c[1]) / 2. Step 3: ha_o_s = WMA of ha_o and ha_c_s = WMA of ha_c, both with a lookback shorter than the RMA lookback. HA_up = ha_c_s >= ha_o_s; HA_down = ha_c_s < ha_o_s. HA_turn_up = HA_up on this bar AND HA_down on the previous bar. HA_turn_down = HA_down on this bar AND HA_up on the previous bar. Both are evaluated on chart-timeframe bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: VolSpike(bar) = volume > SMA(volume) * multiplier (>1). VolRecent = a VolSpike occurred within the last N bars, counting the current bar.
Calculation: Trix(len) = 10000 * (e3 - e3[1]) / e3[1], where e3 = EMA applied three times in succession to close with lookback len. Trix_fast uses a shorter len than Trix_slow. Trix_bull = Trix_fast > Trix_slow.
Calculation: DailyPivots = from the last completed bar of a higher timeframe (daily; the previous day's H, L, C, held constant through the current day): P = (H + L + C) / 3, R1 = 2P - L, R2 = P + (H - L), S1 = 2P - H, S2 = P - (H - L).
Calculation: tol = fraction (<1) of the chart-timeframe Wilder ATR. near_S = |low - S1| <= tol OR |low - S2| <= tol OR |close - S1| <= tol. near_R = |high - R1| <= tol OR |high - R2| <= tol OR |close - R1| <= tol.
Calculation: ADX_rising_strong = Wilder ADX > ADX[1] AND ADX > threshold.
Long: (Trend mode: EMA_short > EMA_mid > SMA_long > SMA_longest (short-to-long lookback ordering on close) AND HA_turn_up AND ADX_rising_strong AND +DI > -DI AND VolRecent) OR (Counter mode: near_S AND HA_turn_up AND Trix_bull)
Short: (Trend mode: EMA_short < EMA_mid < SMA_long < SMA_longest AND HA_turn_down AND ADX_rising_strong AND -DI > +DI AND VolRecent) OR (Counter mode: near_R AND HA_turn_down AND NOT Trix_bull)
