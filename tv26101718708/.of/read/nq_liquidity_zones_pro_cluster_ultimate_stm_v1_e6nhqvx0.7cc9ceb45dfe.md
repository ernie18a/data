Calculation: Zones = on bars of a separate timeframe (chosen independently of the chart timeframe, aggregated from the data; the bar still forming is the newest bar), recomputed at every chart bar. P = (high+low+close)/3 of each aggregated bar, over the most recent N aggregated bars. Thickness = ATR of the aggregated bars * width factor. RelVol of a bar = its volume / simple average of aggregated volume over a lookback. Candidate centers are the P values of the N bars, scanned newest to oldest; skip a candidate if it lies within Thickness of an already accepted center. Cluster of a candidate = all bars in the window whose P is within Thickness of the candidate. Score = sum of RelVol over cluster bars. AvgRelVol = Score / cluster bar count. Age = bars since the newest cluster bar. Accept the candidate as a zone if Score >= min score AND AvgRelVol >= min relative volume AND Age <= max age (max age is not larger than N). Zone top = center + Thickness/2; zone bottom = center - Thickness/2. TopZones = the few accepted zones with the highest Score (descending rank, small fixed count).
Long: for any zone in TopZones, chart close > zone top AND previous chart close <= zone top
Short: for any zone in TopZones, chart close < zone bottom AND previous chart close >= zone bottom
