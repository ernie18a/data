Calculation: Tenkan-sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the shorter Ichimoku lookback
Calculation: Kijun-sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback than Tenkan-sen
Calculation: Leading Span A = midpoint of Tenkan-sen and Kijun-sen
Calculation: Leading Span B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback longer than Kijun-sen
Calculation: Cloud at price = Leading Spans A and B from the Ichimoku displacement bars ago
Calculation: Lagging cloud = Leading Spans A and B from twice the Ichimoku displacement bars ago
Calculation: Chikou clearance = current close is above the high from the Ichimoku displacement bars ago and above the lagging cloud, or below that bar's low and below the lagging cloud
Calculation: Cloud thickness = absolute difference between the cloud spans divided by ATR
Calculation: Rolling high/low = highest high / lowest low over a lookback; prior rolling high/low excludes the current bar
Calculation: Fractal level = most recent confirmed pivot high / pivot low, using equal left and right pivot widths
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = current low is above the high two bars ago; bearish imbalance = current high is below the low two bars ago
Calculation: Ichimoku directional score = sum of selected directional Ichimoku conditions, with each condition weighted equally or by its configured weight; price/cloud, Chikou/cloud, Tenkan/cloud, Kijun/cloud, cloud-flip, and B1/S1 conditions receive the higher weight when weighted scoring is selected. Optional automatic bias uses price versus cloud on a higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar unless fully confirmed HTF evaluation is selected, in which case it uses the last completed bar.
Long: A B1 marker fires when Tenkan-sen >= Kijun-sen AND close > cloud high AND current close > the high from the Ichimoku displacement bars ago AND current close > lagging cloud high AND Leading Span A > Leading Span B AND this full condition was false on the prior bar
Long: An IB marker fires when Ichimoku directional score >= adjusted long threshold + sensitivity gap AND the prior bar's score state was not long; adjusted long threshold is the long threshold minus manual and optional automatic bias, floored at zero
Long: A combined marker fires when all selected directional conditions are bullish in strict mode, net score > 0 in majority mode, or net score >= selected condition count × threshold percentage in percentage mode, AND every selected cloud-thickness pass condition passes, AND the combined condition was false on the prior bar
Long: A selected directional background signal changes to bullish when its bullish comparison is true and its prior state was not bullish: close > Tenkan-sen OR close > Kijun-sen OR close > cloud high OR current close > the delayed bar's high OR current close > delayed Tenkan-sen OR current close > delayed Kijun-sen OR current close > lagging cloud high OR Tenkan-sen > Kijun-sen OR Tenkan-sen > cloud high OR Kijun-sen > cloud high OR Leading Span A > Leading Span B OR (Leading Span A > Leading Span B AND current low > cloud high) OR (Tenkan-sen > Kijun-sen AND both are above cloud high) OR B1 condition OR selected fractal-break state is bullish OR selected imbalance state is bullish OR close > either cloud span OR Tenkan-sen > either cloud span OR Kijun-sen > either cloud span OR current close > either lagging cloud span
Long: Selected thin, normal, or thick cloud pass condition is true when cloud thickness at price or ahead is respectively below, within, or above the configured thickness bounds
Long: A pullback continuation setup triggers when regime is bullish trend or bullish pullback, location is bullish Kijun support, bullish cloud edge, bullish imbalance, or bullish value, and a bullish reclaim, fractal-break state, or close-above-cloud state is true on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Long: A breakout acceptance setup triggers when regime is bullish trend or transition, a recent bar broke and closed above the prior rolling high, current close remains at or above that breakout level and current low is no lower than the level minus the ATR buffer, and location is near the cloud edge, near a fractal break, or above the cloud; these conditions hold on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Long: A sweep reclaim setup triggers when current low is below the prior rolling low or prior range low AND current close is above or within the configured ATR distance of the prior range low, or a bullish reclaim is true; context is transition, pullback, or range and location is near the cloud edge, in an imbalance, at mid-range, or near a range edge; trigger holds on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Long: A range reversion setup triggers when regime is range AND close is within the configured ATR distance of the current rolling range low AND a bullish reclaim or bullish fractal-break state holds on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Short: A S1 marker fires when Tenkan-sen <= Kijun-sen AND close < cloud low AND current close < the low from the Ichimoku displacement bars ago AND current close < lagging cloud low AND Leading Span A < Leading Span B AND this full condition was false on the prior bar
Short: An IS marker fires when Ichimoku directional score <= -(adjusted short threshold + sensitivity gap) AND the prior bar's score state was not short; adjusted short threshold is the short threshold plus manual and optional automatic bias, floored at zero
Short: A combined marker fires when all selected directional conditions are bearish in strict mode, net score < 0 in majority mode, or net score <= -(selected condition count × threshold percentage) in percentage mode, AND every selected cloud-thickness pass condition passes, AND the combined condition was false on the prior bar
Short: A selected directional background signal changes to bearish when its bearish comparison is true and its prior state was not bearish: close < Tenkan-sen OR close < Kijun-sen OR close < cloud low OR current close < the delayed bar's low OR current close < delayed Tenkan-sen OR current close < delayed Kijun-sen OR current close < lagging cloud low OR Tenkan-sen < Kijun-sen OR Tenkan-sen < cloud low OR Kijun-sen < cloud low OR Leading Span A < Leading Span B OR (Leading Span A < Leading Span B AND current high < cloud low) OR (Tenkan-sen < Kijun-sen AND both are below cloud low) OR S1 condition OR selected fractal-break state is bearish OR selected imbalance state is bearish OR close < either cloud span OR Tenkan-sen < either cloud span OR Kijun-sen < either cloud span OR current close < either lagging cloud span
Short: A pullback continuation setup triggers when regime is bearish trend or bearish pullback, location is bearish Kijun resistance, bearish cloud edge, bearish imbalance, or bearish value, and a bearish reclaim, fractal-break state, or close-below-cloud state is true on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Short: A breakout acceptance setup triggers when regime is bearish trend or transition, a recent bar broke and closed below the prior rolling low, current close remains at or below that breakout level and current high is no higher than the level plus the ATR buffer, and location is near the cloud edge, near a fractal break, or below the cloud; these conditions hold on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Short: A sweep reclaim setup triggers when current high is above the prior rolling high or prior range high AND current close is below or within the configured ATR distance of the prior range high, or a bearish reclaim is true; context is transition, pullback, or range and location is near the cloud edge, in an imbalance, at mid-range, or near a range edge; trigger holds on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Short: A range reversion setup triggers when regime is range AND close is within the configured ATR distance of the current rolling range high AND a bearish reclaim or bearish fractal-break state holds on both the current and prior bars; no-trade and cooldown filters are clear
Exit: Long, low <= the active trade's stop price OR high >= its second target
Exit: Short, high >= the active trade's stop price OR low <= its second target
