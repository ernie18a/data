Calculation: Ichimoku lines = Tenkan is the midpoint of the rolling high and low over the shorter lookback; Kijun uses a longer lookback; Senkou A is their midpoint; Senkou B is the rolling high-low midpoint over a still longer lookback. The cloud at price uses Senkou A and B delayed by the displacement; the cloud at Chikou uses them delayed by twice the displacement. Chikou is the current close compared with values delayed by the displacement.
Calculation: Directional states = Assign +1 for each bullish comparison, -1 for its bearish counterpart, and 0 otherwise: close versus Tenkan; close versus Kijun; close above the upper or below the lower cloud at price; close above the displaced high or below the displaced low; close versus displaced Tenkan; close versus displaced Kijun; close above the upper or below the lower cloud at Chikou; Tenkan versus Kijun; Tenkan above the upper or below the lower cloud at price; Kijun above the upper or below the lower cloud at price; current Senkou A versus current Senkou B; close, Tenkan, and Kijun each versus displaced Senkou A and displaced Senkou B; close versus each of Senkou A and Senkou B delayed by twice the displacement. The strong bullish state is Tenkan >= Kijun AND close above the cloud at price AND close above the displaced high AND close above the cloud at Chikou AND current Senkou A > Senkou B; the strong bearish state reverses every comparison, using the displaced low. A price-filtered cloud state requires current Senkou A > Senkou B AND low above the cloud at price, or current Senkou A < Senkou B AND high below the cloud at price. A cloud-filtered Tenkan-Kijun state requires Tenkan > Kijun AND both above the cloud at price, or Tenkan < Kijun AND both below it. The imbalance state persists at +1 after low > high two bars earlier and at -1 after high < low two bars earlier until the opposite event. The fractal-break state persists at +1 after high exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high and at -1 after low falls below the latest confirmed pivot low; an upside break takes precedence if both occur. A pivot requires the configured number of bars on each side for confirmation.
Calculation: Confluence score = Sum the directional states for close versus Tenkan, Kijun and cloud; Chikou versus price, Tenkan, Kijun and cloud; Tenkan versus Kijun and cloud; Kijun versus cloud; current Senkou A versus B; the strong state; and close, Tenkan, Kijun and Chikou versus each Senkou span. Use equal weights or give the cloud comparisons, current Senkou comparison and strong state a greater weight. Add manual bias and, optionally, a signed bias determined by close above or below the cloud on a selected timeframe. For a higher timeframe use either the still-forming bar or the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last available completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. Apply the same timeframe rules to directional states displayed on a selected timeframe.
Calculation: Combined score = Sum the selected distinct directional states, excluding cloud-thickness filters. Cloud thickness is the absolute Senkou A-B difference divided by ATR, either at price or ahead. Every selected thickness filter must pass its configured thin, normal or thick threshold. Strict mode requires every selected directional state to agree; majority mode requires a positive or negative sum; threshold mode requires the signed sum to reach the configured fraction of the number of selected directional states.
Calculation: Signal stop = Freeze at entry the selected long stop at the entry bar low, the minimum low over a short lookback including that bar, the lower cloud edge, Kijun, or the entry bar low minus an ATR multiple; use the corresponding maximum high, upper cloud edge, or high plus an ATR multiple for a short. If the selected stop is on the wrong side of entry, replace it with entry minus or plus an ATR-based fallback. Signal risk is the absolute difference between entry and stop; the final target is entry plus risk times a target multiple for a long, or entry minus that amount for a short.
Long: Any bullish directional state = +1.
Short: Any bearish directional state = -1.
Long: A confirmed pivot low exists.
Short: A confirmed pivot high exists.
Long: Confluence score >= max(0, long threshold - manual bias - optional signed timeframe bias) + sensitivity gap.
Short: Confluence score <= -(max(0, short threshold + manual bias + optional signed timeframe bias) + sensitivity gap) AND the long confluence condition is false.
Long: At least one directional state is selected AND every selected cloud-thickness filter passes AND the selected combination mode gives a bullish result.
Short: At least one directional state is selected AND every selected cloud-thickness filter passes AND the selected combination mode gives a bearish result.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= frozen signal stop OR high >= frozen final target OR elapsed bars >= outcome horizon.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= frozen signal stop OR low <= frozen final target OR elapsed bars >= outcome horizon.
