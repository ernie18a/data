Calculation: Support reclaim = low < lowest low of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = low < lowest low of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND RSI > lowest RSI of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND RSI < an upper RSI threshold.
Calculation: Bollinger reclaim = low < lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower Bollinger Band.
Calculation: Oversold reclaim = lowest RSI over a recent lookback < a lower RSI threshold AND RSI > a recovery threshold AND RSI > previous RSI.
Calculation: Structure shift = (close > highest high of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close > fast EMA AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram) OR (close > fast EMA AND low > previous low AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram).
Calculation: Regime permitted = higher-timeframe close >= higher-timeframe long EMA OR higher-timeframe short EMA >= higher-timeframe long EMA OR higher-timeframe RSI >= a danger threshold OR higher-timeframe RSI > its value on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Crash permitted = percentage drop from the highest high over the crash lookback <= max(effective initial-entry drop threshold × crash-drop multiplier, ATR / close × 100 × ATR multiplier) OR volume <= volume SMA × crash-volume multiplier OR close >= fast EMA.
Calculation: Initial-entry score = sum of one point each for qualifying initial drop, recent oversold RSI, momentum recovery, price recovery, oversold reclaim, Bollinger reclaim, strong lower wick, improving MACD histogram, and volume >= volume SMA; add two points each for support reclaim, bullish divergence, and structure shift. A strong lower wick has lower-wick percentage >= its threshold.
Calculation: First-addition technical score = sum of one point each for Bollinger reclaim, strong lower wick, close > open, improving MACD histogram, and oversold reclaim; add two points each for bullish divergence and support reclaim.
Calculation: First-addition technical confirmation = (bullish divergence AND RSI > previous RSI) OR (Bollinger reclaim AND (strong lower wick OR close > open OR MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram)) OR ((support reclaim OR strong lower wick) AND (RSI > previous RSI OR volume >= volume SMA)) OR first-addition technical score >= its technical threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe recovery = ((higher-timeframe RSI < its recovery threshold AND higher-timeframe RSI > its value on the preceding chart bar AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe open) OR higher-timeframe MACD histogram > its value on the preceding chart bar) AND (close > open OR close > fast EMA).
Calculation: Capitulation = volume > volume SMA × spike multiplier AND lower-wick percentage >= its threshold AND high − low > ATR × range multiplier.
Calculation: Capitulation recovery = capitulation OR (previous bar’s capitulation AND close > open AND close > (previous high + previous low) / 2).
Calculation: Structure recovery = structure shift AND RSI > previous RSI.
Calculation: Second-addition technical score = two points each for higher-timeframe recovery, capitulation recovery, and structure recovery; one point each for strong lower wick, bullish engulfing, volume > volume SMA × spike multiplier, and higher-timeframe MACD histogram > its value on the preceding chart bar. Bullish engulfing means close > open AND close > previous high AND open <= previous close.
Calculation: Second-addition technical confirmation = higher-timeframe recovery OR capitulation recovery OR structure recovery OR second-addition technical score >= its technical threshold.
