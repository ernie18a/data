Calculation: Swing high and swing low = the most recent qualifying high and low, respectively; a high qualifies when it equals the highest high over the swing lookback including the current bar and its distance from the lowest low over that window is at least a threshold times ATR; a low qualifies symmetrically. Store each qualifying value and its bar index.
Calculation: Upswing = both swing values exist, both indices are after the first bar, and the swing-high index is later than the swing-low index.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = swing high minus each enabled retracement ratio times (swing high minus swing low). When several levels are touched, select the level with the smallest retracement ratio.
Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci bounce = both swing indices are old enough AND upswing AND the absolute distance from low to an enabled Fibonacci level is at most a zone-width multiplier times ATR AND (lower wick divided by high minus low is at least a threshold OR close > open AND close > the selected touched level); use zero for the wick ratio when high equals low.
Calculation: Bearish Fibonacci rejection = both swing indices are old enough AND upswing is false AND the absolute distance from high to an enabled Fibonacci level is at most a zone-width multiplier times ATR AND (upper wick divided by high minus low is at least a threshold OR close < open AND close < the selected touched level); use zero for the wick ratio when high equals low.
Calculation: Ribbon bullish count = number of adjacent pairs, among moving averages ordered from shortest to longest lookback, for which the shorter-lookback average > the longer-lookback average; bearish count uses <. The averages use the same selectable EMA, SMA, or HMA type.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of volume times (high + low + close) / 3 divided by cumulative volume since the start of the day; use close when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bars with close > open minus volume on bars with close < open; unchanged-close bars contribute zero.
Calculation: Buy-to-sell volume ratio = current volume if close > open, otherwise zero, divided by the greater of one and current volume if close < open, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Bullish order flow = (cumulative volume delta > its SMA AND close < previous close AND cumulative volume delta > its previous value OR buy-to-sell volume ratio > upper threshold) AND close > daily VWAP.
Calculation: Bearish order flow = (cumulative volume delta < its SMA AND close > previous close AND cumulative volume delta < its previous value OR buy-to-sell volume ratio < lower threshold) AND close < daily VWAP.
Calculation: Long confluence score = sum of one point each for: higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA > long EMA AND higher-timeframe close > short EMA; ribbon filter disabled OR bullish ribbon count >= alignment threshold minus one; order-flow filter disabled OR close > daily VWAP; Fibonacci filter disabled OR upswing; ADX > threshold. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Short confluence score = sum of one point each for: higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA < long EMA AND higher-timeframe close < short EMA; ribbon filter disabled OR bearish ribbon count >= alignment threshold minus one; order-flow filter disabled OR close < daily VWAP; Fibonacci filter disabled OR upswing is false; ADX > threshold. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Buy volume percentage = sum of volume on close > open bars divided by the sum of volume on close > open and close < open bars over a lookback; use a neutral percentage when that denominator is zero.
Calculation: Signal-bar stop and target distances = ATR on the entry signal bar multiplied by their respective stop and target multipliers; trailing distance = signal-bar ATR multiplied by the trailing multiplier.
Long: On a completed chart bar, (ADX > threshold AND short EMA crosses above medium EMA AND close > long EMA AND volume > volume SMA times multiplier OR close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback AND volume > volume SMA times multiplier AND close > long EMA OR ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above oversold threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA > long EMA AND higher-timeframe close > short EMA) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR bullish ribbon count >= alignment threshold minus one) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR close > daily VWAP) AND (Fibonacci filter disabled OR upswing) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within the configured session).
Short: On a completed chart bar, (ADX > threshold AND short EMA crosses below medium EMA AND close < long EMA AND volume > volume SMA times multiplier OR close < lowest low over the preceding breakout lookback AND volume > volume SMA times multiplier AND close < long EMA OR ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below overbought threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA < long EMA AND higher-timeframe close < short EMA) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR bearish ribbon count >= alignment threshold minus one) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR close < daily VWAP) AND (Fibonacci filter disabled OR upswing is false) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within the configured session).
Long: Fibonacci analysis enabled AND bullish Fibonacci bounce.
Short: Fibonacci analysis enabled AND bearish Fibonacci rejection.
Long: Long confluence score > short confluence score.
Short: Short confluence score > long confluence score.
Long: Bullish ribbon count >= alignment threshold minus one.
Short: Bullish ribbon count < alignment threshold minus one AND bearish ribbon count >= alignment threshold minus one.
Long: Order-flow analysis enabled AND bullish order flow.
Short: Order-flow analysis enabled AND bullish order flow is false AND bearish order flow.
Long: Buy volume percentage > upper threshold.
Short: Buy volume percentage < lower threshold.
Long: RSI > upper threshold AND close > open.
Short: RSI < lower threshold AND close < open.
Long: For each displayed comparison timeframe, absolute difference between its short and long EMAs >= its ATR times a threshold AND short EMA > long EMA. Use the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and the last available lower-timeframe bar when it is lower.
Short: For each displayed comparison timeframe, absolute difference between its short and long EMAs >= its ATR times a threshold AND short EMA <= long EMA. Use the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and the last available lower-timeframe bar when it is lower.
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close minus stop distance OR trailing disabled AND high >= signal-bar close plus target distance OR trailing enabled AND high has reached entry price plus trailing distance AND low <= highest price since trailing activation minus trailing distance.
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close plus stop distance OR trailing disabled AND low <= signal-bar close minus target distance OR trailing enabled AND low has reached entry price minus trailing distance AND high >= lowest price since trailing activation plus trailing distance.
