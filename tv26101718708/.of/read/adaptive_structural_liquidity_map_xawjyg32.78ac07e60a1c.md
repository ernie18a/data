Calculation: Active structural liquidity pools = On each completed chart bar, create upper pools from confirmed pivot highs and lower pools from confirmed pivot lows using short-, medium-, and long-lookback pivots (short < medium < long); assign horizon-specific seed weights, with longer horizons weighted more heavily. Merge a new pivot into the nearest same-side pool within an ATR-based merge distance by replacing its price with the seed-weighted average; otherwise add a pool. Remove upper pools when close exceeds pool price plus an ATR-based clearance buffer, lower pools when close falls below pool price minus that buffer, and pools older than the maximum age; if capacity is exceeded, remove the oldest pools.
Calculation: Relative volume = min(volume / SMA(volume, 34), 3), or 1 when volume or the SMA is unavailable or the SMA is zero.
Long: There exists an active lower pool p such that low < p − ATR × sweep-penetration parameter AND close > p AND relative volume ≥ 1.20
Short: There exists an active upper pool p such that high > p + ATR × sweep-penetration parameter AND close < p AND relative volume ≥ 1.20
