Calculation: Active liquidity pools = Maintain an ordered list of upper and lower pools, each with a price, evidence, and creation bar. On each confirmed bar, process newly confirmed pivot highs and lows in order from shortest to longest pivot lookback, using equal left and right lookbacks for each pivot. For each pivot, merge it into the nearest existing pool on the same side if its distance is at most an ATR-based merge threshold; otherwise create a pool at the pivot price. A new pool starts with evidence weighted by pivot lookback, with longer lookbacks receiving greater weight. When merging, set the pool price to the evidence-weighted average of its old price and the pivot price, add the pivot's weight to its evidence, and retain its creation bar. Then, for each pool, multiply its evidence by a decay factor and add proximity times a weighted sum of a constant, capped relative volume, capped side-specific wick length divided by ATR, and capped side-specific favorable close-to-close movement divided by ATR. Proximity is max(0, min(1, 1 - abs(close - pool price) / an ATR-based evidence radius)). The upper wick is high - max(open, close), the lower wick is min(open, close) - low; favorable movement is max(close - previous close, 0) for upper pools and max(previous close - close, 0) for lower pools. Relative volume is volume divided by its simple moving average, capped at a threshold, or 1 if volume or its average is unavailable or the average is zero; cap it again at the evidence-term threshold. Use ATR floored at a minimum price increment throughout. Remove upper pools when close exceeds their price plus an ATR-based clearance buffer, lower pools when close falls below their price minus that buffer, and pools whose age exceeds a maximum. If the pool count exceeds a maximum, remove the oldest pools first. Perform sweep comparisons after these updates.
Calculation: Armed structural high and low = The latest confirmed pivot high and low, respectively, using the medium of the three pivot lookbacks and equal left and right lookbacks. Each new confirmed pivot replaces and arms its corresponding level. A close above an armed high disarms the high; a close below an armed low disarms the low. Evaluate breaks after updating levels and before disarming them.
Long: Close > armed structural high on a confirmed bar
Long: Low < an active lower pool price - an ATR-based penetration threshold AND close > that pool price AND relative volume >= a threshold
Short: Close < armed structural low on a confirmed bar
Short: High > an active upper pool price + an ATR-based penetration threshold AND close < that pool price AND relative volume >= a threshold
