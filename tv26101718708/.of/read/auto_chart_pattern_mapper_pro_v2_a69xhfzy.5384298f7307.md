Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed by equal left and right strengths. Maintain an alternating list within the lookback window and maximum list size; replace consecutive pivots of the same type only when the new high is at least as high or the new low is at least as low. Process a high before a low when both confirm on the same bar. For structure, separately retain the latest two confirmed highs and latest two confirmed lows without this alternating-list replacement.
Calculation: Near(a,b,t) = max(abs(a),abs(b)) > 0 AND abs(a-b) <= max(abs(a),abs(b)) × t.
Calculation: Pattern geometry = for the latest four alternating pivots, let high A and high B be the earlier and later highs and low A and low B the earlier and later lows. Their slopes are price differences divided by bar-index differences. A high or low pair is flat when Near at the matching tolerance; rising when its later value is higher and its slope exceeds the positive ATR-based slope threshold; falling when its later value is lower and its slope is below the negative threshold. Sufficient height means the range of the latest three pivot prices is at least the minimum pattern-height multiple of ATR. An upward or downward impulse means the price difference between the fourth-most-recent and sixth-most-recent pivots is respectively at least the positive impulse multiple of ATR or at most its negative.
Calculation: Pattern classification = evaluate the following in order and select only the first match. Let y1 through y5 be the five latest pivot prices in chronological order, with the last four available even when a fifth is absent; H and L denote pivot types. Cup and handle: H-L-H-L-H, Near(y1,y3) at expanded tolerance, y2 below min(y1,y3) by more than the minimum pattern-height multiple of ATR, y4 above y2 by more than a retracement fraction of min(y1,y3)-y2 and below min(y1,y3), and y5 at least y3 minus an ATR allowance. Inverted cup and handle: L-H-L-H-L and the mirrored inequalities. Head and shoulders: H-L-H-L-H, y3 above both y1 and y5 by more than a shoulder-separation multiple of ATR, and Near(y1,y5) and Near(y2,y4) at the larger shoulder tolerance. Inverse head and shoulders: L-H-L-H-L with mirrored inequalities. Triple top: H-L-H-L-H with Near(y1,y3) and Near(y3,y5) at matching tolerance. Triple bottom: L-H-L-H-L with the same Near tests. Double top: latest three types H-L-H, Near(y3,y5) at matching tolerance, and y4 at most min(y3,y5) minus the minimum pattern-height multiple of ATR. Double bottom: latest three types L-H-L, Near(y3,y5), and y4 at least max(y3,y5) plus that multiple. Bullish pennant: upward impulse, falling highs, rising lows and sufficient height. Bearish pennant: downward impulse with the same converging geometry. Bullish flag: upward impulse, falling highs and lows, and absolute slope difference at most the parallel-slope multiple of ATR. Bearish flag: downward impulse, rising highs and lows, and the same slope-difference test. Rising wedge: rising highs and lows, low slope greater than high slope, and sufficient height. Falling wedge: falling highs and lows, high slope less than low slope, and sufficient height. Ascending triangle: flat highs, rising lows and sufficient height. Descending triangle: falling highs, flat lows and sufficient height. Symmetrical triangle: falling highs, rising lows and sufficient height. Rectangle: flat highs and lows and sufficient height. The expanded cup tolerance is greater than the matching tolerance; the shoulder tolerance is greater than the expanded cup tolerance.
Long: The selected pattern is cup and handle, inverse head and shoulders, triple bottom, double bottom, bullish pennant, bullish flag, falling wedge or ascending triangle OR the selected pattern is symmetrical triangle or rectangle AND close >= (high B + low B) / 2.
Short: The selected pattern is inverted cup and handle, head and shoulders, triple top, double top, bearish pennant, bearish flag, rising wedge or descending triangle OR the selected pattern is symmetrical triangle or rectangle AND close < (high B + low B) / 2.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe close > its shorter-period EMA AND the shorter-period EMA > the longer-period EMA.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe close < its shorter-period EMA AND the shorter-period EMA < the longer-period EMA.
Long: The latest confirmed swing high > the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed swing low > the preceding confirmed swing low OR close > the latest confirmed swing high.
Short: The latest confirmed swing high < the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed swing low < the preceding confirmed swing low OR close < the latest confirmed swing low, AND neither bullish structure condition is true.
Long: A previous or current bar has low > high two bars earlier AND close >= the high two bars before the most recent such bar.
Short: A previous or current bar has high < low two bars earlier AND close <= the low two bars before the most recent such bar AND the bullish gap condition is false.
