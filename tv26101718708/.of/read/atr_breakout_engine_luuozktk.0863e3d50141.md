Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback on the chart timeframe (variant: ATR of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe)
Calculation: SessionOpen = open of the first bar of the current session
Calculation: RefLong = SessionOpen (variants: prior session close, or prior session high); RefShort = SessionOpen (variants: prior session close, or prior session low)
Calculation: LongLevel = RefLong + k * ATR; ShortLevel = RefShort - k * ATR, with k a positive multiple
Long: high crosses above LongLevel (previous bar high <= LongLevel AND current high > LongLevel) AND bar is inside the session time window AND close > SessionOpen AND previous bar close > previous bar open AND ATR <= the nearest-rank percentile of ATR over a lookback of bars AND no long signal has yet fired in the current session AND (optional) volume > its simple moving average AND (optional) fast ATR < slow ATR (shorter vs longer lookback; variant: fast ATR > slow ATR) AND (optional) ADX > previous bar ADX (variants: ADX >= a minimum, or ADX within a min-max band) AND (optional) bar range <= lowest range of the last N bars (variant: inside bar, i.e. high <= previous high AND low >= previous low) AND (optional) volume / its simple moving average >= a multiple
Short: low crosses below ShortLevel (previous bar low >= ShortLevel AND current low < ShortLevel) AND bar is inside the session time window AND close < SessionOpen AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND ATR <= the nearest-rank percentile of ATR over a lookback of bars AND no short signal has yet fired in the current session AND (optional) volume > its simple moving average AND (optional) fast ATR < slow ATR (shorter vs longer lookback; variant: fast ATR > slow ATR) AND (optional) ADX > previous bar ADX (variants: ADX >= a minimum, or ADX within a min-max band) AND (optional) bar range <= lowest range of the last N bars (variant: inside bar) AND (optional) volume / its simple moving average >= a multiple
