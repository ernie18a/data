Calculation: Delta = sum of lower-timeframe intrabar volume for bars with close >= open minus volume for bars with close < open; use intrabars within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of Delta, reset at the selected session boundary; rising means current value > value 3 chart bars ago, falling means < value 3 chart bars ago.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume - rolling mean volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume.
Calculation: Buy imbalance = Delta / volume >= the imbalance threshold; sell imbalance = (1 - Delta / volume) >= the threshold.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = volume >= rolling average volume × absorption multiplier AND high - low <= ATR × range multiplier AND Delta < 0 AND close >= (high + low) / 2. Bearish absorption uses Delta > 0 AND close <= (high + low) / 2, with the same other conditions.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the selected session boundary.
Calculation: Value mean = rolling volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Long flow score = count of: Delta > 0; cumulative delta rising; close > session VWAP; close > value mean; volume z-score > 0.5; buy imbalance OR bullish absorption. Short flow score = count of: Delta < 0; cumulative delta falling; close < session VWAP; close <= value mean; volume z-score > 0.5; sell imbalance OR bearish absorption.
Calculation: Swing structure = compare the two latest confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows on the chart timeframe. Bullish bias if both latest pivots are higher; bearish bias if both are lower, or latest high is lower, or latest low is higher. Require two highs and two lows. OTE zone uses the latest swing high-low leg and the configured retracement range; bullish zone retraces down from the high, bearish zone retraces up from the low. Apply the configured swing-order check when enabled.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe OTE zones = the same swing structure and retracement calculation on each of two higher timeframes; use lookahead_off values (developing higher-timeframe values update live, historically become available when completed). A zone is directional according to that timeframe's bias.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when current low > high 2 chart bars earlier; bearish when current high < low 2 chart bars earlier. Require gap size >= ATR × minimum-gap multiplier. A gap is large when its height >= ATR × minimum-zone multiplier. It flips when a chart-bar close crosses its midpoint (or the configured full-fill boundary); after flipping, a bullish gap is bearish and a bearish gap is bullish. A flipped gap expires after the configured number of chart bars, or is invalidated if price closes back through its far edge.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG retest = for an unexpired flipped bearish gap classified as large, high reaches or exceeds its top and close finishes above its top. Bearish IFVG retest = for an unexpired flipped bullish gap classified as large, low reaches or falls below its bottom and close finishes below its bottom.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND buy imbalance on this and the previous chart bar OR bullish absorption OR close inside bullish chart-timeframe OTE zone OR close inside bullish higher-timeframe OTE zone OR bullish IFVG flip OR bullish IFVG retest OR (long flow score >= configured minimum AND long flow score > short flow score AND (location gate disabled OR any bullish OTE zone contains close OR bullish IFVG retest))
Short: confirmed chart bar AND sell imbalance on this and the previous chart bar OR bearish absorption OR close inside bearish chart-timeframe OTE zone OR close inside bearish higher-timeframe OTE zone OR bearish IFVG flip OR bearish IFVG retest OR (short flow score >= configured minimum AND short flow score > long flow score AND (location gate disabled OR any bearish OTE zone contains close OR bearish IFVG retest))
