Long: MACD crosses above zero AND short-lookback EMA(volume) > longer-lookback EMA(volume) AND MACD > its signal line
Short: MACD crosses below zero AND short-lookback EMA(volume) > longer-lookback EMA(volume) AND MACD < its signal line
Exit: long, low <= lowest low over the stop lookback OR high >= entry price + (entry price - lowest low over the stop lookback) × reward multiple OR short-entry condition
Exit: short, high >= highest high over the stop lookback OR low <= entry price - (highest high over the stop lookback - entry price) × reward multiple OR long-entry condition
