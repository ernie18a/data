Calculation: Watched swing levels = For each independently configured higher timeframe, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. A swing high is a high that is at least as high as every high in an equal-length window before it and strictly higher than every high in the window after it; a swing low is defined symmetrically. Confirm a swing only after the subsequent window has completed. Maintain the most recently confirmed swing high and low independently, replacing the corresponding watched level whenever a new swing is confirmed. After a bullish break, clear the watched high; after a bearish break, clear the watched low. Update newly confirmed swings before checking for breaks.
Long: The completed higher-timeframe close > its watched swing high.
Short: The completed higher-timeframe close < its watched swing low AND the completed higher-timeframe close is not > its watched swing high.
