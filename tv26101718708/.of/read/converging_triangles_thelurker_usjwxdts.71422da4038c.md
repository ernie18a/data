Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low (or close, if configured) that is greater/less than the corresponding values in the configured number of bars on both sides; confirm it only after those bars complete.
Calculation: Upper/lower boundary = the straight line through the two most recent confirmed swing highs/lows, extrapolated to the current bar using the configured linear or logarithmic price scale.
Long: A valid triangle exists AND the configured upward break price (close, or low in wick mode) is below the lower boundary AND the configured downward break price (close, or high in wick mode) is above the upper boundary AND the configured downward break price is above the upper boundary AND, if both boundaries are breached, the upper-boundary excursion is at least the lower-boundary excursion. A valid triangle has an upper boundary with negative slope and a lower boundary with positive slope; each joins pivots separated by at least 3 bars and no more than the configured maximum span; no intervening or subsequent configured break price breaches either boundary; both boundaries are unbroken and overlap for at least the configured minimum; and their projected intersection is between the configured minimum and maximum bars ahead. Evaluate independently for long-, medium-, and short-lookback pivots.
Short: A valid triangle exists AND the configured downward break price (close, or high in wick mode) is above the upper boundary AND the configured upward break price (close, or low in wick mode) is below the lower boundary AND the configured upward break price is below the lower boundary AND, if both boundaries are breached, the lower-boundary excursion is greater than the upper-boundary excursion. A valid triangle has an upper boundary with negative slope and a lower boundary with positive slope; each joins pivots separated by at least 3 bars and no more than the configured maximum span; no intervening or subsequent configured break price breaches either boundary; both boundaries are unbroken and overlap for at least the configured minimum; and their projected intersection is between the configured minimum and maximum bars ahead. Evaluate independently for long-, medium-, and short-lookback pivots.
