Calculation: Boundary price = For anchors (s, ps) and (e, pe), the price at bar b is pe + (pe - ps) × (b - e) / (e - s); if logarithmic mode is selected and both anchor prices are positive, use pe × exp((ln(pe) - ln(ps)) × (b - e) / (e - s)).
Calculation: Break sources = Use close for both boundaries in close mode; use high for the upper boundary and low for the lower boundary in wick mode. An upper violation is upper source > upper boundary × (1 + 0.00000001); a lower violation is lower source < lower boundary × (1 - 0.00000001).
Calculation: Live triangle = Maintain independent layers with longer, intermediate, and shorter pivot confirmation lengths. On each closed bar, for each layer and side, take the two most recently confirmed pivot highs or lows, using high and low respectively or close for both if close-only pivots are selected. Each pivot uses the layer's length on both sides. A new anchor pair is accepted if its span is at least 3 bars and at most the layer length × a maximum-span multiplier, its upper slope is negative or its lower slope is positive, and its boundary has no corresponding violation between the anchors or from the second anchor through the current bar. Cache rejected anchor pairs; retain the previous accepted side when a new pair is rejected. Mark an accepted side broken when its boundary is violated; replace it only when a different pair is accepted. After updating sides and resolving any existing live triangle, form a triangle if the layer has no live triangle, both accepted sides are unbroken, min(upper second-anchor bar, lower second-anchor bar) - max(upper first-anchor bar, lower first-anchor bar) is at least the minimum overlap, and current width / (current width - next-bar width) is within the minimum and maximum bars-to-apex parameters. Freeze both boundaries and set the expiry bar to the formation bar plus the rounded bars-to-apex. On subsequent closed bars, test for violations before expiry; if neither boundary is violated and the current bar is later than the expiry bar, remove the triangle. A resolved triangle stops being live, and a new triangle may form on the same bar.
Long: A live triangle's upper boundary is violated AND (its lower boundary is not violated OR upper source - upper boundary >= lower boundary - lower source).
Short: A live triangle's lower boundary is violated AND (its upper boundary is not violated OR lower boundary - lower source > upper source - upper boundary).
