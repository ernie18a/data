Calculation: Channel midline = SMA or EMA of a selected price source; channel half-width = a multiplier times the standard deviation of that source over the midline lookback, or a multiplier times ATR using a separately selected lookback. Upper band = midline + half-width; lower band = midline - half-width.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend average = EMA of higher-timeframe closes; on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the bar completing on the current chart bar; on a live chart bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Previous breakout result = maintain a separate virtual position, initially flat, and initialize its last result as a loss. When flat, enter long at the close above the upper band or short at the close below the lower band, recording that close and an ATR-based stop. Close the virtual position when its enabled stop is reached using the selected intrabar or close test, or when a long close is below the midline or a short close is above it. If the stop is reached, use its level as the exit price; otherwise use the close. The result is a loss when directional exit price minus entry price is at most zero. After closing, a new virtual position may open on the same bar.
Calculation: Position stop = initial entry close minus the stop multiplier times entry-bar ATR for a long, or plus that amount for a short. If stop movement on additions is enabled, reset it to the latest addition close minus or plus the same multiplier times entry-bar ATR, respectively.
Calculation: Trailing exit level = highest high since long entry minus a multiplier times entry-bar ATR, or lowest low since short entry plus a multiplier times entry-bar ATR; include the current bar in the extreme.
Long: Flat AND close > upper band AND, for an entry, each enabled filter passes: midline > midline at the slope lookback; close > trend SMA or EMA AND trend average > its previous value; close > higher-timeframe trend average; ADX >= threshold; channel half-width > half-width at the expansion lookback; previous breakout result = loss. A filtered long breakout is also marked when close > upper band AND at least one enabled filter fails.
Short: Flat AND close < lower band AND, for an entry, each enabled filter passes: midline < midline at the slope lookback; close < trend SMA or EMA AND trend average < its previous value; close < higher-timeframe trend average; ADX >= threshold; channel half-width > half-width at the expansion lookback; previous breakout result = loss. A filtered short breakout is also marked when close < lower band AND at least one enabled filter fails.
Long: Long position AND additions enabled AND unit count < maximum AND close >= latest entry or addition close + addition multiplier times entry-bar ATR.
Short: Short position AND additions enabled AND unit count < maximum AND close <= latest entry or addition close - addition multiplier times entry-bar ATR.
Exit: Long, stop enabled AND (low <= position stop if intrabar testing is selected, otherwise close <= position stop) OR midline exit selected AND close < midline OR Donchian exit selected AND (low <= lowest low over the exit lookback ending at the previous bar if intrabar testing is selected, otherwise close < that lowest low) OR trailing exit selected AND (low <= trailing exit level if intrabar testing is selected, otherwise close < trailing exit level).
Exit: Short, stop enabled AND (high >= position stop if intrabar testing is selected, otherwise close >= position stop) OR midline exit selected AND close > midline OR Donchian exit selected AND (high >= highest high over the exit lookback ending at the previous bar if intrabar testing is selected, otherwise close > that highest high) OR trailing exit selected AND (high >= trailing exit level if intrabar testing is selected, otherwise close > trailing exit level).
