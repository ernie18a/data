Calculation: Range = max(high - low, a small positive floor); body percentage = abs(close - open) / range; upper wick percentage = (high - max(open, close)) / range; lower wick percentage = (min(open, close) - low) / range.
Calculation: Volatility Z-score = (true range - its rolling SMA) / its rolling standard deviation; volume Z-score = (volume - its rolling SMA) / its rolling standard deviation. Return zero when the corresponding standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / max(rolling SMA of volume, a small positive floor); delta = volume × ((close - low) - (high - close)) / range; delta ratio = delta / max(volume, a small positive floor).
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = high two bars ago when it exceeds the highs of the current bar and the immediately preceding and third preceding bars; confirmed swing low is defined symmetrically. Retain the most recently confirmed swing levels.
Calculation: Prior-session high, low, open and close are taken from the latest completed relevant session: the first session for bars in the second session, the second session for bars in the third session or its preceding window, and the third session for bars in its following window. If windows overlap, choose them in that order for levels. A high or low is tagged when the current bar overlaps a band around that level whose half-width is ATR multiplied by a proximity parameter.
Calculation: Bull bias is active when session bias is enabled and, after a configurable delay from its completion, a relevant completed session closed above its open during its associated following session or window. Bear bias uses close below open. Evaluate overlapping windows independently, so both biases can be active. Long bias permission holds when the optional hard bias filter or session bias is disabled, or bear bias is inactive; short bias permission is symmetric.
Calculation: Bullish session fade = prior-session low is tagged AND close > prior-session low AND lower wick percentage > its threshold AND volume ratio > its fade threshold. Bearish session fade uses the prior-session high, close below it and upper wick percentage. Bullish session break = prior-session high is tagged AND close > that high AND close > open AND volume ratio > its break threshold; bearish session break uses the prior-session low, close below it and close < open. The break volume threshold is higher than the fade volume threshold.
Calculation: Bullish delta divergence = divergence detection enabled AND low equals the rolling lowest low including the current bar AND delta > the previous bar's rolling lowest delta AND volume ratio > its divergence threshold. Bearish delta divergence uses the rolling highest high and delta < the previous bar's rolling highest delta. Replace an unavailable prior rolling delta extreme with zero.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = RSI divergence detection enabled AND low equals the rolling lowest low including the current bar AND RSI > the previous bar's rolling lowest RSI. Bearish RSI divergence uses the rolling highest high AND RSI < the previous bar's rolling highest RSI. Replace an unavailable prior RSI extreme with zero.
Calculation: Bullish stop hunt = stop-hunt detection enabled AND low < the lowest low over the preceding lookback AND close > open AND close > the previous low AND volume ratio > its stop-hunt threshold. Bearish stop hunt = detection enabled AND high > the highest high over the preceding lookback AND close < open AND close < the previous high AND volume ratio > the same threshold.
Calculation: Bullish trap = trap detection enabled AND low < each of the preceding two lows AND close > the previous high. Bearish trap = trap detection enabled AND high > each of the preceding two highs AND close < the previous low.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = absorption detection enabled AND volume Z-score >= its vector threshold AND volatility Z-score < its absorption threshold AND close > open. Bearish absorption uses close < open.
Calculation: Bullish vector = long bias permission AND ((close > open AND volatility Z-score >= its vector threshold AND volume Z-score >= its vector threshold AND body percentage >= its minimum AND delta ratio > its positive vector threshold AND the optional signal-time window condition holds AND the optional structure-break condition holds) OR bullish trap OR bullish stop hunt). For the structure-break condition, choose either close > latest confirmed swing high or high > the highest high over the preceding structure lookback. Bearish vector is symmetric: close < open, delta ratio < the negative vector threshold, and either close < latest confirmed swing low or low < the lowest low over the preceding structure lookback.
Calculation: Bullish score is the weighted sum of these true conditions: low < the lowest low of a short preceding lookback AND close > that low AND volume ratio > its spring threshold; bullish delta divergence; bullish RSI divergence; bullish stop hunt; close > previous close AND volume < previous volume AND range < ATR multiplied by a narrow-range factor; delta ratio > its positive score threshold; volume ratio > its score threshold; bullish trend alignment; abs(close - highest high of the preceding structure lookback) <= ATR multiplied by a proximity factor; close > open; bullish session fade; bullish session break; bull bias. Bearish score is symmetric: high > the highest high of the short preceding lookback AND close < that high AND volume ratio > the spring threshold; bearish delta divergence; bearish RSI divergence; bearish stop hunt; close < previous close AND volume < previous volume AND range < ATR multiplied by the narrow-range factor; delta ratio < the negative score threshold; the same volume ratio condition; bearish trend alignment; distance from the preceding structure-lookback lowest low within the ATR proximity; close < open; bearish session fade; bearish session break; bear bias. The spring and delta-divergence weights are equal and largest; RSI-divergence, stop-hunt, trend-alignment and fade weights are equal and smaller; narrow-range, delta-imbalance, structure-proximity, bar-direction and session-break weights are equal and smaller again; the volume-ratio weight is smaller still; bias has its own weight. The score delta threshold is stricter than the vector delta threshold; the score volume threshold equals the delta-divergence and fade volume thresholds and is below the stop-hunt and session-break volume thresholds.
Calculation: Bullish trend alignment = (VWAP alignment disabled OR close > session VWAP AND VWAP > previous VWAP) AND (EMA alignment disabled OR EMA > previous EMA) AND (higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR higher-timeframe close > its EMA). Bearish alignment reverses the comparisons. Higher-timeframe calculations use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish pre-signal = count of bars whose bullish score >= the minimum score within the persistence window, including the current bar, >= the required count AND bars since the last bullish pre-signal > the same-direction cooldown, or no such signal exists AND bars since the last bearish pre-signal > the opposite-direction lock, or no such signal exists AND the optional signal-time window condition holds AND long bias permission holds. Bearish pre-signal is symmetric. Evaluate both using the previous bars' stored signal times, then update both signal times.
Long: bullish vector OR bullish pre-signal OR bullish stop hunt OR bullish delta divergence OR bullish absorption OR bullish session fade OR bullish session break OR bull bias.
Short: bearish vector OR bearish pre-signal OR bearish stop hunt OR bearish delta divergence OR bearish absorption OR bearish session fade OR bearish session break OR bear bias.
