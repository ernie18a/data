Calculation: Range = max(high − low, small positive floor)
Calculation: Volatility Z-score = (ATR(1) − rolling mean of ATR(1)) / rolling standard deviation of ATR(1)
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − rolling mean of volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / rolling mean of volume
Calculation: Delta ratio = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / Range
Calculation: Body percentage = abs(close − open) / Range × 100
Calculation: Upper wick percentage = (high − max(open, close)) / Range × 100; lower wick percentage = (min(open, close) − low) / Range × 100
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close exceeds the most recent confirmed swing high; a confirmed swing high is higher than the highs two bars before and two bars after. Bearish structure break = close is below the most recent confirmed swing low, defined symmetrically.
Calculation: Bullish manipulation conditions = lower wick percentage exceeds its threshold and volume ratio exceeds its threshold, OR volume Z-score meets its threshold while volatility Z-score is below its threshold and close > open, OR low is below the prior rolling lookback low while close > open and close > prior low and volume ratio exceeds its threshold, OR low is below the prior two lows and close exceeds the prior high, OR price makes a rolling lookback low while delta is above its prior rolling lookback minimum and volume ratio exceeds its threshold, OR price makes a rolling lookback low while RSI is above its prior rolling lookback minimum.
Calculation: Bearish manipulation conditions = upper wick percentage exceeds its threshold and volume ratio exceeds its threshold, OR volume Z-score meets its threshold while volatility Z-score is below its threshold and close < open, OR high is above the prior rolling lookback high while close < open and close < prior high and volume ratio exceeds its threshold, OR high is above the prior two highs and close is below the prior low, OR price makes a rolling lookback high while delta is below its prior rolling lookback maximum and volume ratio exceeds its threshold, OR price makes a rolling lookback high while RSI is below its prior rolling lookback maximum.
Calculation: Bullish pre-signal score = weighted sum of: spring (low below prior rolling short-window low, close back above it, volume ratio above threshold), bullish cumulative-delta divergence, bullish RSI divergence, bullish stop hunt, no supply (close above prior close, volume below prior volume, range below ATR(10) fraction), bullish delta imbalance, elevated volume ratio, bullish VWAP and EMA alignment, proximity to prior rolling structure high, close > open, bullish prior-session-low fade, bullish prior-session-high break, and bullish prior-session bias.
Calculation: Bearish pre-signal score = weighted sum of: upthrust (high above prior rolling short-window high, close back below it, volume ratio above threshold), bearish cumulative-delta divergence, bearish RSI divergence, bearish stop hunt, no demand (close below prior close, volume below prior volume, range below ATR(10) fraction), bearish delta imbalance, elevated volume ratio, bearish VWAP and EMA alignment, proximity to prior rolling structure low, close < open, bearish prior-session-high fade, bearish prior-session-low break, and bearish prior-session bias.
Calculation: Prior-session levels = high, low, and midpoint of the latest completed relevant session; relevant session is the prior Asia session during London, prior London session during NY pre-market and regular hours, and prior NY regular session during after-hours. Session bias is bullish when the prior relevant session closed above its open and bearish when it closed below its open.
Calculation: Prior-session level tag = current bar range intersects the level within an ATR(14)-scaled proximity band. Bullish low fade = prior-session low tag, close above the level, lower wick percentage above threshold, and volume ratio above threshold. Bearish high fade = prior-session high tag, close below the level, upper wick percentage above threshold, and volume ratio above threshold. Bullish high break = prior-session high tag, close above the level, volume ratio above threshold, and close > open. Bearish low break = prior-session low tag, close below the level, volume ratio above threshold, and close < open.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend filter = higher-timeframe close above its EMA for bullish alignment, or below its EMA for bearish alignment; use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
