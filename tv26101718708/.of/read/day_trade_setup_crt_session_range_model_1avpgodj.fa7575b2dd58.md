Calculation: Active session range = For each enabled session window, on the last completed higher-timeframe bar at the window's final bar, select the bar with the greatest weighted score among the window's consecutive bars; break ties in favor of the most recent bar. The score is body weight × abs(close − open) + wick weight × (high − max(open, close) + min(open, close) − low). Use the high and low of the most recently selected bar across the enabled windows until a newer selection is available. Session windows, their lengths, and weights are parameters.
Calculation: Sweep cooldown = The number of chart bars since the most recent qualifying long or short sweep, including sweeps whose labels are hidden. Both directions share the same cooldown; before any qualifying sweep, the cooldown is unrestricted.
Long: Active session range exists AND (low < active session range low if wick-break mode is enabled, otherwise close < active session range low) AND (close > active session range low if close-return filtering is enabled) AND high − low > ATR × minimum range multiplier AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) >= minimum wick ratio AND (no previous qualifying sweep exists OR sweep cooldown > cooldown parameter).
Short: Active session range exists AND (high > active session range high if wick-break mode is enabled, otherwise close > active session range high) AND (close < active session range high if close-return filtering is enabled) AND high − low > ATR × minimum range multiplier AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) >= minimum wick ratio AND (no previous qualifying sweep exists OR sweep cooldown > cooldown parameter).
