Calculation: Signal bar = the current completed chart bar when the chart timeframe equals the pattern timeframe; otherwise, the last completed higher-timeframe bar, evaluated only on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar. All pattern OHLC, ATR, and trend EMA values below use this bar and its preceding bars.
Calculation: Phase = bullish if signal-bar close > trend EMA AND trend EMA > its value a configured number of pattern-timeframe bars earlier; bearish if signal-bar close < trend EMA AND trend EMA < its earlier value; neutral otherwise. A phase change clears both direction locks; a signal locks its direction and, if configured, clears the opposite direction lock.
Calculation: VWAP comparison price and VWAP = current chart close and current chart-timeframe session VWAP, or signal-bar close and session VWAP of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on a chart at the pattern timeframe always use current chart close and current chart-timeframe session VWAP.
Calculation: Pending setup = the latest same-direction signal, replaced by a new same-direction signal and optionally canceled by an opposite signal; discard it when its age in chart bars exceeds the configured expiry or after it triggers. On creation, retain the signal bar's high, low, and ATR. The long planned entry is signal high for breakout mode or signal high minus a configured fraction of signal range for pullback mode; the short planned entry is signal low for breakout mode or signal low plus that fraction of signal range for pullback mode. The long stop is signal low less a configured percentage buffer, or signal high minus a configured ATR multiple; the short stop is signal high plus a configured percentage buffer, or signal low plus a configured ATR multiple. Create a pending setup only if long planned entry > long stop or short planned entry < short stop, respectively; process creation and cancellation before checking triggers, including on the signal bar.
Long: Chart timeframe equals the pattern timeframe or is lower AND signal bar is available as specified AND previous signal-timeframe close < previous open AND signal-bar close > open AND ((strict mode AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open) OR (loose mode AND close >= previous open AND close > previous close)) AND (wick filter disabled OR low < previous low) AND (ATR filter disabled OR absolute body >= ATR multiplied by a configured factor) AND (body filter disabled OR absolute body >= previous absolute body multiplied by a configured factor) AND (trend filter disabled OR signal-bar close > trend EMA) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR signal-bar close > EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bias bar) AND (slope filter disabled OR trend EMA > its configured earlier value) AND (EMA distance filter disabled OR trend EMA != 0 AND absolute difference between signal-bar close and trend EMA divided by trend EMA <= configured percentage) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR VWAP comparison price > VWAP) AND (VWAP distance filter disabled OR VWAP != 0 AND absolute difference between VWAP comparison price and VWAP divided by VWAP <= configured percentage) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is in the configured session) AND (weekday filter disabled OR chart-bar weekday is allowed) AND (phase filter disabled OR bullish direction lock is clear).
Short: Chart timeframe equals the pattern timeframe or is lower AND signal bar is available as specified AND previous signal-timeframe close > previous open AND signal-bar close < open AND ((strict mode AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open) OR (loose mode AND close <= previous open AND close < previous close)) AND (wick filter disabled OR high > previous high) AND (ATR filter disabled OR absolute body >= ATR multiplied by a configured factor) AND (body filter disabled OR absolute body >= previous absolute body multiplied by a configured factor) AND (trend filter disabled OR signal-bar close < trend EMA) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR signal-bar close < EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bias bar) AND (slope filter disabled OR trend EMA < its configured earlier value) AND (EMA distance filter disabled OR trend EMA != 0 AND absolute difference between signal-bar close and trend EMA divided by trend EMA <= configured percentage) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR VWAP comparison price < VWAP) AND (VWAP distance filter disabled OR VWAP != 0 AND absolute difference between VWAP comparison price and VWAP divided by VWAP <= configured percentage) AND (session filter disabled OR chart-bar time is in the configured session) AND (weekday filter disabled OR chart-bar weekday is allowed) AND (phase filter disabled OR bearish direction lock is clear).
Long: A valid pending long setup exists AND ((breakout mode AND chart-bar high >= planned entry) OR (pullback mode AND chart-bar low <= planned entry)).
Short: A valid pending short setup exists AND ((breakout mode AND chart-bar low <= planned entry) OR (pullback mode AND chart-bar high >= planned entry)).
