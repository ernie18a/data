Long: Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close on either selected timeframe.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close <= longer-period EMA of close on either selected timeframe.
Long: The most recent crossover between the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs of close on the longer selected timeframe was upward.
Short: The most recent crossover between the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs of close on the longer selected timeframe was downward.
Short: Close > EMA of close on the shorter selected timeframe.
Calculation: Selected-timeframe values = EMA or close calculated on the selected timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar in historical data and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar within the chart bar.
