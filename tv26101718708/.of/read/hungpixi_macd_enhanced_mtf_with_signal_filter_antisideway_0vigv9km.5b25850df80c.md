Calculation: MACD directional score = Let M = EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback), S = EMA(M, signal lookback), and H = M − S. Add the indicator-direction weight when M rises (subtract it when M falls); add the histogram-direction weight when H rises (subtract it when H falls). If M is unchanged, use only the signed histogram-direction weight. Add a cross score on an upward M/S crossover and subtract it on a downward crossover; each bar’s cross score also includes 0.6 times the previous bar’s cross score.
Calculation: MTF score = (higher-timeframe MACD directional score × timeframe-dependent weight + chart-timeframe MACD directional score) ÷ (timeframe-dependent weight + 1). The higher timeframe is the configured timeframe above the chart timeframe; use its forming bar when lookahead is enabled, otherwise its last completed bar.
Long: MTF score > 0 AND previous MTF score ≤ 0 AND ATR > the minimum entry ATR threshold AND ((short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe > long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold) OR (short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe < long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold))
Short: MTF score < 0 AND previous MTF score ≥ 0 AND ATR > the minimum entry ATR threshold AND ((short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe < long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold) OR (short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe > long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold))
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: long, MTF score < 0 AND previous MTF score ≥ 0 AND ATR > the minimum entry ATR threshold AND ((short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe < long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold) OR (short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe > long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold))
Exit: short, MTF score > 0 AND previous MTF score ≤ 0 AND ATR > the minimum entry ATR threshold AND ((short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe > long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold) OR (short-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe < long-lookback EMA on the 30-minute timeframe AND their absolute difference > the minimum EMA-difference threshold))
