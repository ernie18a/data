Calculation: Active fair value gaps = A bullish gap forms when low > high two bars earlier AND their difference >= ATR multiplied by a minimum-size parameter; its top is the current low and its bottom is the earlier high. A bearish gap forms when high < low two bars earlier AND their difference >= ATR multiplied by the same minimum-size parameter; its top is the earlier low and its bottom is the current high. Track gaps separately by direction. After updating gaps on each bar, remove bullish gaps when close < bottom and bearish gaps when close > top; remove gaps older than the maximum-age parameter when that parameter is positive, then retain only the newest maximum-count parameter gaps per direction.
Calculation: Gap stage = Each gap starts at stage 1 and can advance at most one stage per bar, starting on the bar after formation. For a bullish gap, advance from stages 1 through 8 respectively when low <= top, close > top, close < top, low <= bottom, close > bottom, close > top, close < top, and close < bottom. For a bearish gap, advance respectively when high >= bottom, close < bottom, close > bottom, high >= top, close < top, close < bottom, close > bottom, and close > top. Stage 9 resets to stage 1 on the next bar.
Calculation: Gap directional volumes = For each gap, sum volume from its formation bar onward separately for bars with close > open and bars with close < open; exclude bars with close = open. The classification is available starting on the bar after formation.
Long: At least one active bullish gap exists OR at least one active bearish gap is at stage 5 or 6 OR at least one active gap has available bullish directional volume > bearish directional volume multiplied by the dominance threshold.
Short: At least one active bearish gap exists OR at least one active bullish gap is at stage 5 or 6 OR at least one active gap has available bearish directional volume > bullish directional volume multiplied by the dominance threshold.
