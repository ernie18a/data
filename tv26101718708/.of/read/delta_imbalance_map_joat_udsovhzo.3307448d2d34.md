Calculation: Participation tilt = buyer volume percentage minus seller volume percentage for the previous completed chart bar, using all completed lower-timeframe bars within it. Assign each lower-timeframe bar's volume to buyers if close > open, to sellers if close < open, and half to each if equal. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, assign the chart bar's volume to buyers if close >= open, otherwise to sellers. If total volume is zero, both percentages are equal.
Calculation: Gap score = min(100, 30 × gap size / max(minimum gap size, price increment) + 0.7 × max(directional participation tilt, 0) + 12 × abs(previous close − previous open) / max(ATR, price increment) + 0.12 × current volume percentile rank). Directional participation tilt is participation tilt for a bullish gap and its negative for a bearish gap. Minimum gap size = ATR × gap-size threshold; use current high − low when ATR is unavailable and the midpoint percentile when volume percentile rank is unavailable.
Calculation: Tracked zones = accepted gaps with their boundaries, score, direction, birth bar, and active or resolved state. Before accepting a new gap, if overlap filtering is enabled, examine tracked zones from newest to oldest whose price intervals strictly overlap it: remove each resolved zone or zone with a lower score; reject the new gap immediately upon encountering any other overlapping zone. Previously removed zones remain removed even if the new gap is rejected. Add an accepted gap as active, removing the oldest tracked zone if the capacity is exceeded. Then resolve active zones older than the lifetime threshold or, after their birth bar, touched or filled according to the selected mode: bullish touch when low < zone top, bullish full fill when low <= zone bottom, bearish touch when high > zone bottom, bearish full fill when high >= zone top. Remove resolved zones if retaining them is disabled. A bullish gap's bottom is the high two bars ago and its top is current low; a bearish gap's bottom is current high and its top is the low two bars ago.
Calculation: Average active tilt = average participation tilt recorded at creation across active tracked zones, or zero when there are none.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago AND gap size >= minimum gap size AND abs(participation tilt) >= tilt threshold AND current volume percentile rank >= volume-rank threshold AND the new bullish gap is accepted by the tracked-zone overlap procedure.
Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND gap size >= minimum gap size AND abs(participation tilt) >= tilt threshold AND current volume percentile rank >= volume-rank threshold AND the new bearish gap is accepted by the tracked-zone overlap procedure.
Long: Average active tilt > positive tilt threshold.
Short: Average active tilt < negative tilt threshold.
Long: Active bullish-zone count >= active bearish-zone count.
Short: Active bullish-zone count < active bearish-zone count.
