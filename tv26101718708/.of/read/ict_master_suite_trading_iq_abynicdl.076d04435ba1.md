Calculation: Three-bar bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago; zone is between that prior high and current low
Calculation: Three-bar bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago; zone is between current high and that prior low
Calculation: Swing high/low = a pivot with three bars on each side
Calculation: Swing structure, fair value gaps, breaker blocks, and liquidity sweeps are calculated on the chart timeframe or the configured timeframe, whichever is higher; decisions use completed bars
Long: After a bullish liquidity sweep, a bullish fair value gap forms above the sweep low; its upper boundary is nearest the selected retracement level between the sweep low and the highest high since the sweep; enter at that boundary
Short: After a bearish liquidity sweep, a bearish fair value gap forms below the sweep high; its lower boundary is nearest the selected retracement level between the sweep high and the lowest low since the sweep; enter at that boundary
Long: A lower swing low is followed by a higher swing high and price closes at or above that high; a bullish fair value gap formed since the lower swing low overlaps a bearish breaker block formed since that swing low; enter at the overlap point nearest current price
Short: A higher swing high is followed by a lower swing low and price closes at or below that low; a bearish fair value gap formed since the higher swing high overlaps a bullish breaker block formed since that swing high; enter at the overlap point nearest current price
Long: During the configured session, price raids the session low; after the session, price is above a bullish breaker block formed since the raid and below the session high; enter at the nearest qualifying breaker block
Short: During the configured session, price raids the session high; after the session, price is below a bearish breaker block formed since the raid and above the session low; enter at the nearest qualifying breaker block
Long: During the configured session, higher-timeframe bias is bullish and a bullish fair value gap forms during the session
Short: During the configured session, higher-timeframe bias is bearish and a bearish fair value gap forms during the session

Short: Price retraces to the selected Fibonacci level of a downward swing
Exit: Long, price reaches the selected profit target or falls to the selected stop level
Exit: Short, price reaches the selected profit target or rises to the selected stop level
