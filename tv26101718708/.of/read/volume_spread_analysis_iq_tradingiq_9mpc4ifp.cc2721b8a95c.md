Calculation: Volume rank = percentile rank of current volume within the trailing volume window, including the current bar.
Calculation: Spread rank = percentile rank of current high minus low within the trailing spread window, including the current bar; use the same window length as volume rank.
Calculation: Close location = ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low. High close means close location >= the close-location threshold; low close means close location <= the negative close-location threshold, unless already classified as high; middle close means neither.
Calculation: Narrow spread = spread rank < the narrow-spread threshold. Wide spread = spread rank >= the wide-spread threshold. Normal spread = spread rank >= the narrow-spread threshold AND spread rank < the wide-spread threshold.
Calculation: Low volume = volume rank < the low-volume threshold. High volume = volume rank >= the high-volume threshold. Normal volume = volume rank >= the low-volume threshold AND volume rank < the high-volume threshold.
Calculation: Upper rejection = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= the rejection-wick threshold AND upper wick > lower wick AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) <= the rejection-body threshold; ratios are zero when high equals low. Lower rejection reverses the wick comparison and applies the same thresholds to the lower wick.
Calculation: Background score = rolling sum of per-bar upward scores minus rolling sum of per-bar downward scores, plus a trend adjustment when enabled. An upward score is the high-effort strong-result weight if close > previous close AND high close AND wide spread AND high volume; the low-effort strong-result weight if the same price conditions hold AND low volume; the normal-effort strong-result weight if close > previous close AND high close AND (wide spread OR normal spread) AND normal volume; otherwise zero. The downward score uses close < previous close AND low close with the same spread and volume conditions and respective weights. The high-effort weight is greater than the normal-effort weight, which is greater than the low-effort weight. When trend adjustment is enabled, add its weight for an upward imported zigzag direction and subtract it for any other direction. The imported zigzag algorithm is not provided in READ, so its direction cannot be reproduced from the supplied source.
Calculation: Strong background = background score > the background threshold. Weak background = background score < the negative background threshold.
Calculation: Downward-move score = the z-score of log(close / close from a prior bar), using a rolling mean and population standard deviation. Stopping-volume activation starts when this score <= a negative threshold and remains active thereafter: the source's reset condition cannot occur after activation.

Short: Previous close > the close before it AND previous spread was narrow AND ((previous volume was low OR previous volume was less than each of the two preceding volumes) AND previous close location was middle OR traditional rules are enabled AND previous volume was less than each of the two preceding volumes) AND close < previous close AND current imported zigzag direction is upward or downward.
Long: Previous bar had a lower rejection AND previous close location was middle or high AND previous spread was normal or wide AND previous background was strong AND (trend alignment is disabled OR previous imported zigzag direction was upward) AND close > previous close.
Short: Previous bar had an upper rejection AND previous close location was middle AND previous spread was normal or wide AND previous background was weak AND (trend alignment is disabled OR previous imported zigzag direction was downward) AND close < previous close.
Long: Close < previous close AND volume is high AND spread is narrow or normal AND close location is middle AND stopping-volume activation is active AND background is strong.
Short: Close < previous close AND volume is high AND spread is narrow or normal AND close location is middle AND stopping-volume activation is active AND background is not strong.
