Calculation: Volatility = simple moving average of true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs or lows that are extrema over equal numbers of preceding and following bars; recognize each pivot only after the following bars have closed. Keep a limited number of recent high and low pivots within a lookback window.
Calculation: Boundary = straight line through two retained same-side pivots. At creation, reject it if any intervening or subsequent high exceeds an upper boundary, or any low falls below a lower boundary, by more than a volatility-based tolerance. Remove a retained boundary when the current bar violates it by that tolerance or its first anchor leaves the lookback. Keep a limited number per side.
Calculation: Two-boundary formation = on a new pivot, pair the retained upper and lower boundaries nearest the close, up to a limited number per side. Require their anchor intervals to overlap by at least a fraction of the longer interval; do not reuse an active boundary or a previously resolved pair. The start is the later first anchor, and the span is the longer anchor interval. Normalize each slope by current volatility and take its arctangent as its angle. Classify the pair by flat-angle and parallel-angle tolerances, overlap width ratio, and whether its projected intersection lies within a multiple of the span: two flat sides form a rectangle; a flat upper side and converging rising lower side form an ascending triangle; a flat lower side and converging falling upper side form a descending triangle; opposite converging slopes form a symmetrical triangle if their absolute angles satisfy a symmetry ratio; same-direction converging slopes form a rising or falling wedge; approximately parallel rising or falling slopes form a channel. Rectangles and channels must also satisfy width-ratio and divergence limits. A falling channel after a sufficiently large upward close-to-close move over a prior lookback is a bull flag; a rising channel after a sufficiently large downward move is a bear flag; a symmetrical triangle after a sufficiently large move in either direction is a pennant. These three require the span to be no longer than a multiple of the prior-move lookback. For any side classified as flat, its absolute projected travel since its first anchor must be within both a fraction of initial formation height and a multiple of volatility. Admit only formations whose relevant family is enabled and whose age and projected lifetime limits have not expired; evict the oldest when the active capacity is exceeded. Lifetime ends at the projected intersection if ahead, otherwise at a multiple of the span, further limited by the permitted width band, the search window, and, for flags and pennants, the brief-duration limit. When only the brief-duration limit expires, reclassify a flag as its underlying channel or a pennant as a symmetrical triangle if that family is enabled and its other lifetime limits remain valid. A formation completes on the first eligible bar whose close is beyond either boundary; check expiry first.
Calculation: Neckline formation = on confirmation of a new high or low pivot, test the two most recent same-side pivots first for a double top or bottom: their prices must match within a volatility tolerance and the most extreme confirmed opposite-side pivot strictly between them must lie beyond both in the opposite direction. Otherwise test the three most recent same-side pivots for head and shoulders or its inverse: the middle extreme must exceed both shoulders by a volatility threshold, the shoulders must match within a volatility tolerance, and the most extreme opposite-side pivot between each shoulder and the head must lie beyond both adjacent extremes. A double's neckline is horizontal through its intervening pivot; a head-and-shoulders neckline joins its two intervening pivots. Formation height is the distance from the head to the neckline at the head, or from the farther double extreme to its neckline; require positive height no greater than a volatility multiple. Reject a neckline if a subsequent bar through the current bar pierces it on the breakout side by more than a volatility tolerance, if its first extreme exceeds the search window, if its age exceeds a multiple of the distance between its first and last extremes, or if its neckline is already an active formation boundary. Admit enabled families and evict the oldest neckline formation when active capacity is exceeded. A top completes on the first eligible close below its neckline; a bottom completes on the first eligible close above it; check expiry first.
Calculation: Formation risk unit = the formation height frozen at completion, optionally capped by a multiple of the simple average of true range from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; when that timeframe is not higher than the chart timeframe, use the current completed chart bar instead. For two-boundary formations, height is the boundary separation at the later first anchor. If the cap is unavailable or disabled, use uncapped height.
Calculation: Completion stop = at completion, freeze either entry price minus the breakout direction times a fixed multiple of the risk unit, or entry price minus the breakout direction times the greater of a risk-unit floor and the distance to structure. Structure is the opposite boundary at completion for a two-boundary formation, or the farthest peak or trough for a neckline formation. Breakout direction is positive for an upward completion and negative for a downward completion.
Calculation: Completion target = entry price plus breakout direction times a configured multiple of the frozen risk unit; each enabled target tier has its own multiple.
Long: an active falling wedge, ascending triangle, bull flag, double bottom, or inverse head and shoulders is admitted.
Short: an active rising wedge, descending triangle, bear flag, double top, or head and shoulders is admitted.
Long: an unexpired two-boundary formation has close above its upper boundary OR an unexpired double bottom or inverse head and shoulders has close above its neckline.
Short: an unexpired two-boundary formation has close below its lower boundary OR an unexpired double top or head and shoulders has close below its neckline.
Exit: long, a bar after an upward completion has high at or above any enabled completion target OR low at or below the completion stop.
Exit: short, a bar after a downward completion has low at or below any enabled completion target OR high at or above the completion stop.
