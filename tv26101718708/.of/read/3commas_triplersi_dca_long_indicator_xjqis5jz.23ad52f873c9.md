Calculation: Safety-order level = base entry price × (1 − initial step fraction × sum of step-scale^i for i from 0 through filled safety orders); the base entry price is the close on the base-entry bar. Calculate the level before processing that bar's entry or safety order.
Calculation: Average entry price = total order notional / total acquired quantity. The base order quantity is base-order notional / base entry price. Each successive safety-order notional is first safety-order notional × volume-scale^(number of previously filled safety orders), and its quantity is its notional / min(open, safety-order level). Update the average immediately after each safety order.
Long: No open deal AND RSI of the last completed higher-timeframe bar crosses above its threshold; enter at close.
Long: Open deal AND filled safety orders < maximum safety orders AND low <= safety-order level AND (RSI of the last completed longer higher-timeframe bar crosses above its threshold OR shorter-lookback RSI of the last completed shorter higher-timeframe bar crosses above its threshold); fill at min(open, safety-order level). The shorter higher timeframe is the base-entry RSI timeframe; the longer higher-timeframe RSI uses the same lookback as the base-entry RSI. Process at most one safety order per bar.
Exit: long, high >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction); evaluate after the base entry and any safety order on the bar.
