Calculation: Selected OHLCV = current chart bar OHLCV, or previous chart bar OHLCV when confirmed mode is enabled; all selected series use the same shift.
Calculation: Normalized pressure = clamp(33.3 × (P − SMA(P, normalization lookback)) / standard deviation(P, normalization lookback), −100, 100), or 0 when the standard deviation is 0; P is an EMA of an EMA of raw pressure, using the score-smoothing and pressure-smoothing lookbacks respectively. Raw pressure = 35 × close-location value × volume factor + 25 × body pressure + 25 × direction × volume factor + 15 × trend bias. Close-location value = (2 × selected close − selected high − selected low) / range; body pressure = (selected close − selected open) / range; range = selected high − selected low, replaced by the instrument's minimum price increment when zero. Volume factor = min(selected volume / SMA(selected volume, volume lookback), 3) / 3, with the ratio replaced by 1 when the average is zero. Direction = clamp((selected close − previous selected close) / range, −1, 1). Trend bias with the ATR dead zone enabled is 0 when abs(selected close − EMA(selected close, trend lookback)) < ATR(current chart bars, ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, otherwise 1 when the difference is positive and −1 when it is not; with the dead zone disabled it is 1, −1, or 0 according to whether the difference is positive, negative, or zero.
Calculation: Squeeze active = Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band AND Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band. Bollinger bands are SMA(selected close, Bollinger lookback) ± Bollinger multiplier × standard deviation(selected close, Bollinger lookback). Keltner bands are EMA(selected close, Keltner lookback) ± Keltner multiplier × ATR(current chart bars, Keltner lookback).
Calculation: Squeeze fired = squeeze active on the previous chart bar AND squeeze inactive on the current chart bar.
Calculation: Growing pressure = abs(normalized pressure − normalized pressure three chart bars earlier) > 5 AND normalized pressure and that difference have the same nonzero sign.
Long: (squeeze active OR squeeze fired) AND normalized pressure > positive confluence threshold.
Short: (squeeze active OR squeeze fired) AND normalized pressure < negative confluence threshold.
Long: (squeeze inactive OR squeeze pressure-trend display disabled) AND (normalized pressure > positive neutral threshold OR normalized pressure >= positive extreme threshold).
Short: (squeeze inactive OR squeeze pressure-trend display disabled) AND (normalized pressure < negative neutral threshold OR normalized pressure <= negative extreme threshold).
Long: squeeze active AND squeeze pressure-trend display enabled AND growing pressure AND normalized pressure > 0.
Short: squeeze active AND squeeze pressure-trend display enabled AND growing pressure AND normalized pressure < 0.
