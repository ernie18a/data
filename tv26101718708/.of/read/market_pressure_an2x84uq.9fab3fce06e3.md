Calculation: Normalized pressure = the z-score of a pressure score, scaled by 33.3 and clipped to [-100, 100]. The score is the sum of: close-location value × relative-volume factor × 35; candle body divided by bar range × 25; clamped close-to-previous-close change divided by bar range × relative-volume factor × 25; and trend bias × 15. Relative-volume factor = min(volume / average volume, 3) / 3. Trend bias is +1 above the trend EMA, −1 below it, and 0 within the ATR dead zone around it. Smooth the score with a short-lookback EMA, then a longer-lookback EMA; normalize against its rolling mean and standard deviation. Use the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Squeeze active = Bollinger Bands are inside Keltner Channels; use a shorter lookback for the Bollinger Band basis and deviation, and a longer lookback for the Keltner EMA and ATR. Use the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Squeeze fired = squeeze was active on the preceding bar and is inactive on the current bar.
Long: Normalized pressure > upper neutral boundary OR (squeeze active AND normalized pressure > positive pressure threshold) OR (squeeze fired AND normalized pressure > positive pressure threshold)
Short: Normalized pressure < lower neutral boundary OR (squeeze active AND normalized pressure < negative pressure threshold) OR (squeeze fired AND normalized pressure < negative pressure threshold)
