Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend = SuperTrend using an ATR-based band and either a fixed multiplier or an adaptive multiplier. For the adaptive multiplier, divide a base multiplier by short-term ATR / long-term ATR; use a ratio of 1 if either ATR is nonpositive. Multiply the result by a widening factor when ADX is below its lower threshold, or by a tightening factor when ADX is above its upper threshold; otherwise leave it unchanged. Clamp the result between the multiplier limits. The widening factor is greater than 1 and the tightening factor is less than 1.
Calculation: Timeframe trend = Adaptive SuperTrend direction on the selected timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar. Apply the same rule independently to each dashboard timeframe. A chart-timeframe calculation uses the current chart bar.
Calculation: Confirmed pivots = High or low pivots with equal left and right lookbacks, available only after the right lookback has elapsed. Keep the latest and previous confirmed high and low and the bar of each latest pivot. A bullish break occurs when close is above the latest pivot high and the previous close is at or below that same level; a bearish break occurs when close is below the latest pivot low and the previous close is at or above that same level. Clear the broken pivot price after a break, retaining its bar and the previous pivot.
Calculation: Order block = On a bullish break, scan backward from the preceding bar through the smaller of the bars since the latest pivot low and a maximum lookback. Select the nearest bearish candle whose low two bars later minus its high is greater than current average absolute candle body times a gap multiplier, AND whose immediately following candle's close minus open is at least current average absolute candle body times a displacement multiplier. Either test can be disabled. If no candle qualifies, select the nearest bearish candle whose low equals the minimum low in the scan. A candidate is accepted if the optional sweep filter is disabled or the scan minimum is below the previous pivot low or no previous pivot low exists, AND the optional discount filter is disabled or the candidate midpoint is below the midpoint of the latest pivot high and low; substitute current high for a missing pivot high and the scan minimum for a missing pivot low. On a bearish break, mirror the procedure: scan since the latest pivot high; select the nearest bullish candle with its low minus the high two bars later greater than the gap threshold and the immediately following candle's open minus close at least the displacement threshold; otherwise select the nearest bullish candle at the scan maximum high. The optional sweep requires that maximum to exceed the previous pivot high, or that no previous pivot high exists; the optional premium filter requires the candidate midpoint above the midpoint of the latest pivot high and low, substituting the scan maximum for a missing pivot high and current low for a missing pivot low. Use a simple moving average of absolute candle bodies for the average, replacing zero with 1. The block boundaries are the candidate candle's high and low.
Calculation: Active order blocks = Maintain bullish and bearish blocks separately, adding accepted blocks on their break bars. For a bullish block, the mitigation level is either its midpoint or upper boundary; for a bearish block it is either its midpoint or lower boundary. Count a bullish touch when current low is at or below the mitigation level and previous low is above it; count a bearish touch when current high is at or above the mitigation level and previous high is below it. Count only bars later than the candidate candle. Evaluate touches before removal. After touch evaluation, remove a bullish block if close is below its lower boundary, low is below its lower boundary minus a configurable fraction of block height, or its touch count reaches the configured limit; remove a bearish block if close is above its upper boundary, high is above its upper boundary plus that fraction, or its touch count reaches the limit. After removals, discard the oldest blocks exceeding the per-side capacity.
