Calculation: Bullish reversal = a bar closes below the lows of exactly all but one bar in a lookback that includes itself; record its high and low and reset its confirmation state. Starting on that bar, invalidate the candidate if close falls below its recorded low. Confirm it on the first bar within the confirmation window whose close exceeds its recorded high. Confirmation consumes the candidate even if the optional volume filter fails. The reversal signal occurs when confirmation happens and the optional filter is disabled or volume exceeds its SMA. A new candidate replaces any previous candidate.
Calculation: Bearish reversal = a bar closes above the highs of exactly all but one bar in a lookback that includes itself; record its high and low and reset its confirmation state. Starting on that bar, invalidate the candidate if close rises above its recorded high. Confirm it on the first bar within the confirmation window whose close falls below its recorded low. Confirmation consumes the candidate even if the optional volume filter fails. The reversal signal occurs when confirmation happens and the optional filter is disabled or volume exceeds its SMA. A new candidate replaces any previous candidate.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of 2 for RSI below its oversold threshold, 2 for RSI below its more extreme oversold threshold, 1 for RSI above its previous value, 1 for MACD line below zero, 2 for MACD line above its previous value, 2 for MACD histogram above its previous value, 2 for lower wick divided by bar range above its threshold, 1 for absolute body divided by bar range below its threshold, 1 for close above open, 2 if any high in the preceding recovery lookback exceeds current close multiplied by one plus the recovery percentage, and 4 for a bullish reversal signal. Wick and body ratios are zero when the bar range is zero. The recovery contribution requires the full lookback to exist. The MACD fast EMA length is shorter than its slow EMA length.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of 2 for RSI above its overbought threshold, 2 for RSI above its more extreme overbought threshold, 1 for RSI below its previous value, 1 for MACD line above zero, 2 for MACD line below its previous value, 2 for MACD histogram below its previous value, 2 for upper wick divided by bar range above the same wick threshold used by the bullish score, 1 for close below open, and 1 for a bearish reversal signal. The wick ratio is zero when the bar range is zero. The MACD fast EMA length is shorter than its slow EMA length.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low from the position-opening bar through the specified number of preceding bars, multiplied by one minus the stop buffer percentage; fix this level when the position opens.
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus the risk-reward multiplier times the difference between entry price and long stop; fix this level when the position opens.
Long: Bullish score >= entry score threshold AND datetime within the configured date range AND position is flat.
Long: Bullish score >= score-label threshold.
Long: Bullish reversal signal occurs.
Short: Bearish score >= score-label threshold.
Short: Bearish reversal signal occurs.
Exit: Long, bearish score >= exit score threshold.
Exit: Long, risk-reward exits enabled AND low <= long stop OR risk-reward exits enabled AND high >= long target.
