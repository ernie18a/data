Calculation: Session values = Use a higher timeframe than the chart. At the first chart bar of each session, fix the session open and the Supertrend, its direction, ATR, and Laguerre PPO from the last completed higher-timeframe bar. If ATR is unavailable or nonpositive, use the average of available completed-session ranges over the ATR lookback, or the latest completed-session range if that average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Laguerre PPO = For the last completed higher-timeframe bar and each of a faster and slower gamma, apply the following recursive filter to HL2, initializing previous states to zero: L0=(1−gamma)×HL2+gamma×previous L0; L1=−gamma×L0+previous L0+gamma×previous L1; L2=−gamma×L1+previous L1+gamma×previous L2; L3=−gamma×L2+previous L2+gamma×previous L3; filter=(L0+2×L1+2×L2+L3)/6. PPO=100×(fast filter−slow filter)/slow filter, or zero when the slow filter is zero. The faster gamma is smaller.
Calculation: Arrow levels = For each session, build a ladder from the session open using five separately parameterized ATR offsets on each side and the open itself. Rank the five levels on the pullback side by a weighted average of clamped abs(session open−Supertrend)/(ATR×2), momentum alignment score, and clamped 1−abs(level's unscaled ATR offset)/0.9; round the score to one decimal place on a 0–10 scale. Momentum alignment scores 1 when PPO is positive in a long regime or nonpositive in a short regime, and 0.4 otherwise. Select the configured number of highest-scoring levels, breaking ties from the outermost level inward. The target is the configured number of ladder steps from the selected level in the trend direction, capped at the outermost level. The stop is the next ladder level outward, or for the outermost level an additional ATR-scaled offset beyond it.
Long: Current chart bar is the first bar of a session AND session open > last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend AND (momentum filter is disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe Laguerre PPO > 0).
Short: Current chart bar is the first bar of a session AND session open < last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend AND (momentum filter is disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe Laguerre PPO <= 0).
Long: Session open > last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend AND low <= a selected long arrow level AND high >= that level AND (momentum filter is disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe Laguerre PPO > 0).
Short: Session open < last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend AND high >= a selected short arrow level AND low <= that level AND (momentum filter is disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe Laguerre PPO <= 0).
Exit: long, low <= the stop associated with the entry arrow level OR high >= its target OR close < last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend.
Exit: short, high >= the stop associated with the entry arrow level OR low <= its target OR close > last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend.
