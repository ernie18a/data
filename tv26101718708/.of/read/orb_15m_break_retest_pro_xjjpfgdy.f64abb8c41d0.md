Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range window; opening-range close = close of its final bar
Calculation: Opening-range close position = (opening-range close − opening-range low) / (opening-range high − opening-range low)
Calculation: Opening-range width = opening-range high − opening-range low
Calculation: Opening-range direction allowance = long allowed when opening-range close position is at least the configured bearish threshold; short allowed when it is at most the configured bullish threshold
Calculation: Breakout volume expansion = volume > volume SMA over the configured lookback × configured multiplier
Long: Flat position AND within an enabled trade window AND opening range is ready AND opening-range width is within configured bounds AND daily and per-session trade limits allow a trade AND outside any enabled news blackout AND close > VWAP AND close > EMA of close on a higher timeframe (using that timeframe’s currently forming bar) AND long opening-range direction allowance AND breakout volume expansion AND a prior close > opening-range high + configured acceptance distance occurred within the configured bar limit AND no intervening close < opening-range high invalidated that setup, expired, or was entered AND low <= prior breakout level + configured retest tolerance AND close > prior breakout level
Short: Flat position AND within an enabled trade window AND opening range is ready AND opening-range width is within configured bounds AND daily and per-session trade limits allow a trade AND outside any enabled news blackout AND close < VWAP AND close < EMA of close on a higher timeframe (using that timeframe’s currently forming bar) AND short opening-range direction allowance AND breakout volume expansion AND a prior close < opening-range low − configured acceptance distance occurred within the configured bar limit AND no intervening close > opening-range low invalidated that setup, expired, or was entered AND high >= prior breakout level − configured retest tolerance AND close < prior breakout level
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − configured stop distance or (high >= entry price + configured breakeven trigger distance and low <= entry price + configured breakeven offset) or high >= entry price + configured target distance or optional trailing stop is hit
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + configured stop distance or (low <= entry price − configured breakeven trigger distance and high >= entry price − configured breakeven offset) or low <= entry price − configured target distance or optional trailing stop is hit
