Calculation: OR High/Low = highest high and lowest low of the bars in the opening-range window at the start of the trading session (completed once the window ends); OR width = OR High - OR Low
Calculation: OR close position = (close of the last bar of the opening-range window - OR Low) / OR width
Calculation: VWAP = day-anchored volume-weighted average of (high+low+close)/3
Calculation: HTF EMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe than the chart, taking the value of the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Vol avg = simple moving average of volume over a lookback on the chart timeframe
Long: filters (the same on the break bar and the entry bar, except the volume and acceptance-buffer tests, which apply to the break bar only): bar is inside the trade window after the opening range has completed AND OR width is between a minimum and a maximum AND close > VWAP AND close > HTF EMA AND OR close position >= lower threshold AND daily trade count < daily maximum AND no trade yet taken in this session AND flat. Break bar: filters AND volume > Vol avg * multiplier (multiplier > 1) AND close > OR High + acceptance buffer. Entry: bar is the break bar or a later bar within a maximum number of bars after it AND no bar since the break bar has closed below OR High AND low <= OR High + retest tolerance AND close > OR High AND filters
Short: filters (the same on the break bar and the entry bar, except the volume and acceptance-buffer tests, which apply to the break bar only): bar is inside the trade window after the opening range has completed AND OR width is between a minimum and a maximum AND close < VWAP AND close < HTF EMA AND OR close position <= upper threshold (upper threshold > lower threshold) AND daily trade count < daily maximum AND no trade yet taken in this session AND flat. Break bar: filters AND volume > Vol avg * multiplier (multiplier > 1) AND close < OR Low - acceptance buffer. Entry: bar is the break bar or a later bar within a maximum number of bars after it AND no bar since the break bar has closed above OR Low AND high >= OR Low - retest tolerance AND close < OR Low AND filters
Exit: long, high >= entry + target distance OR low <= stop level, where stop level = entry - stop distance, replaced by entry + small offset once high since entry has reached entry + breakeven trigger (target distance > stop distance; breakeven trigger < target distance)
Exit: short, low <= entry - target distance OR high >= stop level, where stop level = entry + stop distance, replaced by entry - small offset once low since entry has reached entry - breakeven trigger (target distance > stop distance; breakeven trigger < target distance)
