Calculation: Fair value gap = On a confirmed chart bar, a bullish gap has bottom = high two bars earlier and top = current low when current low > high two bars earlier; otherwise a bearish gap has bottom = current high and top = low two bars earlier when current high < low two bars earlier. Require (top - bottom) / current close >= the minimum gap fraction. Exclude the current bar and the bar immediately after a calendar-day change on intraday charts, and the current bar and the bar immediately after a calendar-week change on intraday and daily charts. Detect gaps only within the configured lookback from the last dataset bar.
Calculation: Crossfire zones = Maintain unconsumed gaps and active zones in creation order. Before processing each new gap, update each waiting gap using the bar three bars earlier, only when that bar is later than the gap's creation bar: for a bullish gap lower its remaining top to the greater of its bottom and that bar's low or close; for a bearish gap raise its remaining bottom to the lesser of its top and that bar's high or close. Use the configured wick-or-close source; remove a gap when its remaining top <= its remaining bottom. For each new gap, first flip every active opposite-direction zone having a strictly positive overlap with it: deactivate the old zone and create a zone in the new gap's direction with bottom = max of the two bottoms and top = min of the two tops. Then, in newest-first order, match every waiting opposite-direction gap having a strictly positive overlap with the new gap; remove each matched waiting gap and create a zone in the new gap's direction from the overlap. If zone combining is enabled, instead expand the first active same-direction zone that overlaps or touches that overlap to their union. If the new gap matched or flipped any zone or gap, consume it; otherwise store it as a waiting gap. After these updates, process active zones created before the current bar: a zone is touched when current high >= its bottom AND current low <= its top; initialize its previous-touch state to false and update it after checking for a retest. Then deactivate a bullish zone when current low or close <= its bottom, or a bearish zone when current high or close >= its top, using the configured wick-or-close source. Apply the configured capacity limits by discarding the oldest waiting gaps and oldest zone segments after each bar.
Long: A bullish crossfire zone is created or flipped to bullish OR an active bullish crossfire zone created before the current bar is touched AND its previous-touch state is false.
Short: A bearish crossfire zone is created or flipped to bearish OR an active bearish crossfire zone created before the current bar is touched AND its previous-touch state is false.
