Calculation: Prior high = high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Prior low = low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Prior close = close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Range = prior high − prior low
Calculation: R5 = prior high ÷ prior low × prior close
Calculation: R4 = prior close + range × 1.1 ÷ 2
Calculation: S5 = prior close − (R5 − prior close)
Calculation: S4 = prior close − range × 1.1 ÷ 2
Short: high ≥ R5 AND close < R5
Long: low ≤ S5 AND close > S5
Exit: short, low ≤ R4
Exit: long, high ≥ S4
