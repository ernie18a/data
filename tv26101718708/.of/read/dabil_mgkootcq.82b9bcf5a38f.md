Calculation: Stop distance = configured stop-loss distance
Calculation: Target distance = configured take-profit distance
Long: Close of the last completed bar on the longest timeframe is greater than the close of the preceding bar on that timeframe AND the last completed bars are bullish on each of the three successively shorter higher timeframes AND the current chart-timeframe bar is bullish
Short: Close of the last completed bar on the longest timeframe is less than the close of the preceding bar on that timeframe AND the last completed bars are bearish on each of the three successively shorter higher timeframes AND the current chart-timeframe bar is bearish
Exit: long, low is less than or equal to entry price minus stop distance OR high is greater than or equal to entry price plus target distance
Exit: short, high is greater than or equal to entry price plus stop distance OR low is less than or equal to entry price minus target distance
