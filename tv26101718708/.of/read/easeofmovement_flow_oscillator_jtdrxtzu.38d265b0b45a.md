Calculation: Ease oscillator = soft-bounded, adaptively scaled standardized ease, where ease is the smoothed change in the high/low midpoint divided by ((volume / average volume over the normalization lookback) / (high − low)); standardize using median and median absolute deviation
Long: Ease oscillator crosses above its positive signal band (current > band AND previous ≤ band) AND cumulative volume > 0 OR a confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low, its standardized ease is higher than at that preceding pivot low, and the pivots are no farther apart than the maximum pivot gap
Short: Ease oscillator crosses below its negative signal band (current < −band AND previous ≥ −band) AND cumulative volume > 0 OR a confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high, its standardized ease is lower than at that preceding pivot high, and the pivots are no farther apart than the maximum pivot gap
