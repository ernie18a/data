Calculation: Standardized ease = Choose high-low midpoint or close as price. Distance is its change from the preceding bar; range is high minus low. Divide volume by either a fixed scale or max(rolling mean volume over the normalization lookback, 1), then divide by range. Raw ease is distance divided by this box ratio; use zero if range or box ratio is zero. Smooth raw ease with either an SMA or Ehlers UltimateSmoother, or leave it unsmoothed when the smoothing length is one. Standardize using either (ease minus rolling mean) / rolling population standard deviation, or (ease minus rolling median) / (1.4826 × rolling median of the series of absolute deviations from each bar's own rolling median), over the normalization lookback. Use zero if the selected denominator is zero, or for the robust method if MAD is at most a small positive tolerance.
Calculation: Ease oscillator = 100 × tanh(clamp(standardized ease × 33 / display scale, -10, 10)), where display scale is 1.2 × max(40, rolling linearly interpolated percentile of abs(standardized ease × 33)); use 100 for the percentile before it is available.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the sum is zero. The optional regime filter passes when disabled, when set to trending and the ratio is at least its threshold, or when set to reverting and the ratio is below its threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is a pivot over equal left and right pivot lookbacks, recognized only after the right lookback has elapsed. Compare consecutive confirmed pivots of the same kind and sample standardized ease at their pivot bars.
Calculation: VSA volume = either volume or volume divided by its updated time-of-day slot baseline. The baseline is an EMA maintained separately for each clock-time slot, initialized with the first available volume in that slot and updated with the current volume before division; use a ratio of one if the updated baseline is unavailable or nonpositive. VSA volume rank and range rank are rolling percentile ranks over the same history lookback. Close location is (close - low) / (high - low), or one-half when the range is zero. Prior extreme is the highest high or lowest low over the extreme lookback ending on the preceding bar.
