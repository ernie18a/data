Calculation: Volatility range = Average True Range on chart bars.
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = union of confirmed chart-timeframe swing lows/highs, confirmed higher-timeframe swing lows/highs (using the last completed higher-timeframe bar), bullish/bearish order blocks (last opposite candle before a qualifying volume-backed impulsive close beyond recent highs/lows), volume point-of-control bands (highest-volume price bin over a lookback), bullish/bearish three-bar gaps with an impulsive middle candle, equal-low/equal-high clusters, anchored volume-weighted average price bands, prior-period and premarket high/low bands, and qualifying unfilled opening gaps. Supports are bullish-side zones; resistances are bearish-side zones. A zone is removed after repeated closes through its far boundary; a filled gap zone is removed when configured to do so.
Calculation: Zone score = weighted normalized touch count, touch volume, creation volume, origin timeframe, overlapping-zone/source confluence, freshness, regime fit, and (when enabled) structure fit; subtract a penalty for closes through the zone, add historical bounce-rate and polarity-flip bonuses, and clamp to 0–100. After sufficient bounce and break observations, adapt the touch-volume, origin-timeframe, confluence, freshness, and structure weights in proportion to their relative averages on bounces versus breaks, bounded by their configured limits. Grade A = score at least the A threshold; Grade B = score at least the B threshold and below the A threshold.
Calculation: Market regime = squeeze when Bollinger bandwidth rank and ATR-percent rank are both in their lowest configured ranges; otherwise classify strong/weak bull or bear from ADX and EMA slope, with range as the remainder.
Calculation: Market structure = bull after a confirmed close above the latest confirmed swing high; bear after a confirmed close below the latest confirmed swing low; otherwise retain the prior state (initially range). A break is counted once until a new swing of that side is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle = bullish engulfing (current body covers the prior bearish body and is larger), bullish pin bar (lower wick meets its configured range share, body is small, and close is in the upper half), or inside-bar break (prior bar is inside the bar before it and current close exceeds prior high). Bearish reversal candle is the mirrored condition. A trigger remains valid for its configured number of bars after a confirmed bar.
Calculation: Trade-plan long levels = entry just above selected support-zone top; stop below its bottom by an ATR buffer; target is the nearest qualifying resistance-zone midpoint above entry. Trade-plan short levels = entry just below selected resistance-zone bottom; stop above its top by an ATR buffer; target is the nearest qualifying support-zone midpoint below entry.
Calculation: Reward-to-risk = distance from entry to target divided by distance from entry to stop.
Long: A support zone exists.
Short: A resistance zone exists.
Long: A confirmed close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high.
Short: A confirmed close falls below the latest confirmed swing low.
Long: A support zone has score above the trade-plan threshold, price is above its top and within the configured ATR distance, regime is bullish, range, or squeeze, and (if the structure gate is enabled) structure is not bear; the highest-scoring eligible support is selected; a qualifying resistance midpoint lies above its entry and reward-to-risk meets the configured minimum.
Short: A resistance zone has score above the trade-plan threshold, price is below its bottom and within the configured ATR distance, regime is bearish, range, or squeeze, and (if the structure gate is enabled) structure is not bull; the highest-scoring eligible resistance is selected; a qualifying support midpoint lies below its entry and reward-to-risk meets the configured minimum, and no long plan qualifies.
Long: A bullish reversal candle trigger is still valid and price is within the configured ATR distance of an eligible long plan's support zone.
Short: A bearish reversal candle trigger is still valid and price is within the configured ATR distance of an eligible short plan's resistance zone.
Exit: long, low is at or below the plan stop or high is at or above the plan target; if both occur in one bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high is at or above the plan stop or low is at or below the plan target; if both occur in one bar, exit at the stop.
