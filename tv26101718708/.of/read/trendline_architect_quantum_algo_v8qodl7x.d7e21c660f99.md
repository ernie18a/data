Calculation: Trendlines = On each closed chart bar, confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side. Pair each new pivot with the immediately preceding pivot of the same kind, then update the preceding pivot even if no line is created. A low-pivot pair defines support; a high-pivot pair defines resistance. Create the straight line through the pair only if their separation is at least the pivot length and at most the maximum anchor span, every close between and including the anchors is at or above support minus a fraction of current ATR or at or below resistance plus that fraction, and no active same-side line has both a current value and slope within their respective ATR-based duplicate tolerances. If the same-side active-line limit is reached, remove the line with the fewest touches, breaking ties by the oldest first anchor. Initialize the new line as forming with its two anchor touches. Process new resistance before new support, then process registered lines newest first. Evaluate each line by extrapolating its anchors to the current bar. For an active support line, a touch occurs when low is within the ATR-based touch tolerance of the line and close is above it; for resistance, a touch occurs when high is within that tolerance and close is below it. Increment the touch count on every touching bar and confirm the line when its count reaches the confirmation threshold. A support break is close below the line minus an ATR-based breakout buffer; a resistance break is close above the line plus that buffer. If a break occurs while the confirmed-only filter is enabled and the line was forming at the start of the bar, remove it; otherwise mark it broken and start its retest watch, regardless of breakout grade. After processing the bar's touch and break, remove an active line whose age from its first anchor exceeds the expiry limit. On subsequent bars, remove a broken line after the retest window expires or immediately after its first retest. A support retest occurs when high is at least the line minus the ATR-based touch tolerance and close is below the line; a resistance retest occurs when low is at most the line plus that tolerance and close is above the line.
Calculation: Breakout grade = A score starts with a base point and gains one point each when volume z-score exceeds its threshold, absolute close-to-line distance divided by ATR exceeds its threshold, and absolute candle body divided by high-low exceeds its threshold; use zero for the body ratio when high equals low and for the volume z-score when its standard deviation is unavailable or zero. Volume z-score uses the same-lookback simple moving average and standard deviation of volume. Map the score to an ordered grade, with the highest score receiving the highest grade, the next score the middle grade, and lower scores the lowest grade.
Long: An active support line is touched AND its updated touch count is at least the confirmation threshold.
Short: An active resistance line is touched AND its updated touch count is at least the confirmation threshold.
Long: An active resistance line breaks AND the confirmed-only filter is disabled OR an active resistance line breaks AND it was confirmed at the start of the bar; in either case, breakout grade must be at least the minimum grade.
Short: An active support line breaks AND the confirmed-only filter is disabled OR an active support line breaks AND it was confirmed at the start of the bar; in either case, breakout grade must be at least the minimum grade.
Long: A broken resistance line is retested within its retest window.
Short: A broken support line is retested within its retest window.
