Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across equal lookback windows before and after it; retain the latest pivot type, replacing a same-type pivot only with a more extreme one.
Calculation: Active structural extreme = highest high since the latest low pivot, or lowest low since the latest high pivot.
Calculation: Consecutive bullish/bearish candles = each candle in the selected consecutive-bar window closes above/below its open.
Calculation: Directional volume score = count of: relative volume at or above its threshold; volume at or above its moving average times its thrust threshold; OBV above/below its longer-lookback value for bullish/bearish direction; and a bullish/bearish candle.
Calculation: Directional recovery score = count of: reversal magnitude threshold met; close above/below the fast EMA; at least all but one of the selected recent closes higher/lower than their preceding closes; at least all but one of the selected recent bars making higher highs with non-lower lows/lower lows with non-higher highs; breakout beyond the prior recovery-lookback high/low by close or intrabar high/low; and directional volume score at or above its minimum.
Long: The latest confirmed pivot is a low.
Long: Latest confirmed pivot is a high and no provisional reversal is active, and the selected consecutive candles are bullish, and (the close is above the prior breakout-lookback high if the breakout filter is enabled), and (the close is above the fast EMA if the EMA filter is enabled), and the rise from the active structural extreme meets the configured ATR or percentage reversal threshold, and (directional volume score meets its minimum if volume confirmation is required).
Long: A bearish provisional reversal is active and directional recovery score meets its threshold, with volume score confirmation if required; OR a bearish provisional reversal is active and the high exceeds its provisional anchor.
Short: The latest confirmed pivot is a high.
Short: Latest confirmed pivot is a low and no provisional reversal is active, and the selected consecutive candles are bearish, and (the close is below the prior breakout-lookback low if the breakout filter is enabled), and (the close is below the fast EMA if the EMA filter is enabled), and the fall from the active structural extreme meets the configured ATR or percentage reversal threshold, and (directional volume score meets its minimum if volume confirmation is required).
Short: A bullish provisional reversal is active and directional recovery score meets its threshold, with volume score confirmation if required; OR a bullish provisional reversal is active and the low falls below its provisional anchor.
