Calculation: Confirmed structure = On each bar, confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of left and right bars. Process a high before a low if both confirm. Initialize the latest pivot with the first confirmed pivot; replace a same-type latest pivot only with a more extreme pivot, and replace it with every opposite-type pivot. Each initialization or replacement resets provisional state. After processing pivots, structural direction is up after a latest pivot low and down after a latest pivot high. Structural extreme starts at the latest pivot price and updates with each current high in an up direction or current low in a down direction, including the current bar.
Calculation: Directional volume score = When the volume layer is enabled, sum one point each for volume / SMA(volume) >= a relative-volume threshold, volume >= SMA(volume) * a volume-thrust multiplier, OBV above its value at a lookback for up direction or below it for down direction, and close > open for up direction or close < open for down direction. The score is zero when the layer is disabled.
Calculation: Provisional state = Initially inactive. After confirmed structure and its current extreme are updated, an inactive state becomes up on a bullish provisional reversal or down on a bearish provisional reversal; its anchor is the structural extreme and its extreme is the current high for up or current low for down. Update an active up extreme with the current high or an active down extreme with the current low before evaluating recovery. A recovery flip changes direction, sets the anchor to the prior provisional extreme, and sets the extreme to the current high for up or current low for down. After score-based flips, process anchor invalidation in this order: if direction is up and low < anchor and no bullish recovery flip occurred on this bar, flip down; then if direction is down and high > anchor and no bearish recovery flip occurred on this bar, flip up. Apply the same anchor and extreme updates on invalidation. Evaluate these steps in order on every bar.
Calculation: Bullish provisional reversal = Structural direction is down AND provisional state is inactive AND each candle in a reversal lookback has close > open AND (breakout filter disabled OR close > highest high of the preceding breakout lookback) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA(close)) AND ((close - structural extreme) / ATR >= an ATR threshold OR (close - structural extreme) / structural extreme >= a percentage threshold; use AND instead of OR if selected) AND (volume layer disabled OR reversal volume requirement disabled OR up directional volume score >= a minimum).
Calculation: Bearish provisional reversal = Structural direction is up AND provisional state is inactive AND each candle in a reversal lookback has close < open AND (breakout filter disabled OR close < lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA(close)) AND ((structural extreme - close) / ATR >= an ATR threshold OR (structural extreme - close) / structural extreme >= a percentage threshold; use AND instead of OR if selected) AND (volume layer disabled OR reversal volume requirement disabled OR down directional volume score >= a minimum).
Calculation: Recovery score = Sum one point for each satisfied condition in the chosen direction: reversal distance from the current provisional extreme divided by ATR >= a recovery ATR threshold OR the same distance divided by that extreme >= a recovery percentage threshold, using AND instead of OR if selected; close above EMA(close) for up or below it for down; at least max(1, recovery lookback - 1) closes in the recovery lookback higher than their preceding close for up or lower for down; at least that many bars in the same lookback with high > preceding high AND low >= preceding low for up, or high <= preceding high AND low < preceding low for down; close OR high above the highest high of the preceding recovery breakout lookback for up, or close OR low below the lowest low for down; directional volume score >= its minimum. Reversal distance is close minus provisional extreme for up and provisional extreme minus close for down. Use the same EMA length as the provisional reversal filter.
Calculation: Bullish recovery flip = Recovery enabled AND provisional state is down AND (volume layer disabled OR recovery volume requirement disabled OR up directional volume score >= its minimum) AND up recovery score >= a threshold.
Calculation: Bearish recovery flip = Recovery enabled AND provisional state is up AND (volume layer disabled OR recovery volume requirement disabled OR down directional volume score >= its minimum) AND down recovery score >= a threshold.
Long: Provisional state is up OR (provisional state is inactive AND structural direction is up).
Short: Provisional state is down OR (provisional state is inactive AND structural direction is down).
Long: Close > shorter SMA(close) AND close > longer SMA(close).
Short: Close < shorter SMA(close) AND close < longer SMA(close).
