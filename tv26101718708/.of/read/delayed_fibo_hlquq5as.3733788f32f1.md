Calculation: Volume-adjusted price = close when the sum of volume over a shorter lookback divided by the sum over the immediately preceding longer lookback exceeds a threshold; otherwise, retain the prior volume-adjusted price. The short lookback includes the current bar; the longer lookback excludes it.
Calculation: Fibonacci bands = EMA of close ± a multiple of the simple moving average of true range; band distances increase from level 1 through level 4.
Long: Volume-adjusted price crosses below the selected lower Fibonacci band (level 1, 2, 3, or 4) OR the lower-band entry is disabled and low differs from the prior bar's low.
Exit: Long, volume-adjusted price crosses either above or below the level 3 upper Fibonacci band OR price reaches the take-profit percentage above entry price OR price reaches the stop-loss percentage below entry price OR, after reaching the trailing activation percentage above entry price, price falls to the highest price reached since entry (zero trailing offset).
