Calculation: Session levels = running high and low of each configured session, retained after the session ends until replaced by the next occurrence. Evaluate sessions in the configured timezone.
Calculation: Confirmed swing = a chart-bar high or low that is a pivot against configurable numbers of bars on its left and right; record the pivot bar's time when it is confirmed.
Calculation: Dealing ranges = track the highest confirmed swing high and lowest confirmed swing low during each configured daily and London range window, accepting pivots whose pivot time is at or after the range start. Reset the London tracker at London start and the daily tracker at Asia start. At London end, copy both ranges into extension ranges; update their extremes with newly confirmed swings until the current bar overlaps the preceding bar's retracement zone or the final configured session ends, then retain the copies. Use the live London range during London and its extension otherwise; use the live daily range during London or Asia and its extension otherwise. For the separate killzone range, reset at its start, update extremes from confirmed swings while active, and, when the origin extreme is broken, retain the other extreme and replace the broken extreme with the current low for a bullish leg or current high for a bearish leg; the replacement bar starts a new range. A leg is bullish when its high's pivot time is later than its low's and bearish when the reverse is true. A bullish range is broken when low < its bottom; a bearish range is broken when high > its top.
Calculation: Retracement zone = the interval between two configurable retracement fractions of a dealing range, measured down from the top for a bullish leg and up from the bottom otherwise. For daily and London ranges, clear the zone when the origin extreme is broken and retain the last calculated zone when the range is inactive; reset it at the corresponding range-window start. For the killzone range, clear it when the range is invalidated.
Calculation: Fair value gap = for three consecutive bars, a bullish gap has newest low > oldest high and bounds [oldest high, newest low]; a bearish gap has newest high < oldest low and bounds [newest high, oldest low]. For each configured higher timeframe, use its last completed bar and the two preceding completed bars. Store gaps newest-last, remove a bullish gap when a later completed bar on that timeframe closes below its bottom and a bearish gap when it closes above its top, and discard the oldest when the configured storage limit is exceeded.
Calculation: Selected range gap = among stored gaps whose recorded higher-timeframe formation time is at or after the range start, overlap the retracement zone, and are bullish when the zone midpoint is below the range midpoint and bearish otherwise, select the newest gap on the highest-priority higher timeframe, falling back to successively lower-priority higher timeframes. For daily and London selection use the unextended tracker extremes for the range midpoint; for the killzone use its current extremes.
Calculation: Manipulation leg = for each hunting setup, the tip is the lowest low since a long setup began or the highest high since a short setup began. On the start bar, whenever the tip makes a new extreme, and on the bar immediately after the tip, scan backward from the bar immediately after the tip, bounded by a configurable maximum lookback, through the first strict local swing high for a long or swing low for a short, inclusive. A strict local swing exceeds or falls below the highs or lows of a configurable equal number of neighboring bars on each side. Collect every bearish chart-timeframe fair value gap encountered for a long, or bullish gap for a short, newest first; reset their inversion and touch flags on each rescan. Also find the first scanned bearish candle for a long or bullish candle for a short whose immediately preceding candle is not of that direction, ignoring that preceding candle when its body is at most a configurable threshold and the candle before it has the setup direction; the selected candle's open is the confirmation level.
Calculation: Confirmation = the collected gap list is nonempty AND its first gap has been inverted by a close beyond its far edge by more than the displacement threshold AND every subsequent gap before the first uninverted gap is inverted AND the first uninverted gap, if any, has never been touched since the latest rescan; OR the close reaches or exceeds the confirmation level plus the displacement threshold for a long, or reaches or falls below the confirmation level minus that threshold for a short, AND no collected gap is currently touched. For inversion, retain the flag once a close exceeds a bearish gap's top plus displacement for a long or falls below a bullish gap's bottom minus displacement for a short. For persistent touch, a bearish gap is touched when close <= its top AND high > its bottom; a bullish gap is touched when close >= its bottom AND low < its top. For confirmation-level blocking, use the same conditions with inclusive high and low comparisons. Clear the confirmation level as soon as its displacement condition is met, even if a gap blocks confirmation.
Calculation: Range setup = independently for each dealing range, reset at the configured entry-window start and, unless triggered, its end. During the entry window, an idle setup starts hunting when the selected bullish gap exists AND low <= its top, or when high >= the selected gap's bottom otherwise. A hunting setup resets if no selected gap exists or if the current selected gap is bullish AND high >= the applicable range top, or bearish AND low <= the applicable range bottom. A triggered setup also resets on that delivery condition or when its stop is reached; count deliveries and allow daily and London setups to start hunting only while their delivery count is below its configured limit. The killzone setup has no delivery-count limit. Use the extension range extremes for daily and London delivery and targets, and the current killzone extremes for the killzone. Evaluate resets, new hunts, tip updates and confirmation in that order, allowing a new hunt and confirmation on the same bar.
Calculation: Sweep setup = independently track each high and low of the two configured earlier sessions. Reset at entry-window start; a triggered setup completes when its stop or target is strictly breached, remaining completed until reset. During the entry window, an idle high-level setup starts hunting when high >= the session high and an idle low-level setup starts hunting when low <= the session low. On confirmation, a failed reward-to-risk test completes the setup. Evaluate each session level as its latest tracked value; the later session's engulfment of the earlier session does not suppress signals because that check occurs after signal generation.
Calculation: Sweep target = search backward from a configurable swing-radius offset through a configurable maximum lookback for the first major swing. For a swept high, require the candidate low to be strictly below each more recent neighboring low and no higher than each older neighboring low; for a swept low, require the candidate high to be strictly above each more recent neighboring high and no lower than each older neighboring high. In both cases use the candidate bar's high as the major swing price. Target = tip + configurable retracement fraction × (major swing price - tip).
Long: During the configured entry window, a bullish range setup is hunting AND confirmation is true AND abs(close - tip) < abs(range top - close).
Short: During the configured entry window, a bearish range setup is hunting AND confirmation is true AND abs(close - tip) < abs(range bottom - close).
Long: During the configured entry window, a swept-low setup is hunting AND confirmation is true AND abs(close - tip) <= abs(sweep target - close).
Short: During the configured entry window, a swept-high setup is hunting AND confirmation is true AND abs(close - tip) <= abs(sweep target - close).
Exit: long, for a triggered range setup during the entry window, low <= entry tip OR high >= current applicable range top.
Exit: short, for a triggered range setup during the entry window, high >= entry tip OR low <= current applicable range bottom.
Exit: long, for a triggered swept-low setup, low < entry tip OR high > target fixed at entry.
Exit: short, for a triggered swept-high setup, high > entry tip OR low < target fixed at entry.
