Calculation: Dealing-range OTE = For each daily, London, and NY killzone range, use the highest confirmed swing high and lowest confirmed swing low; the later extreme sets leg direction, and the OTE is the configurable deep-retracement band measured back from that extreme.
Calculation: Aligned higher-timeframe FVG = A bullish gap exists when the latest completed higher-timeframe bar's low is above the high two bars before it; a bearish gap exists when its high is below the low two bars before it. Keep gaps until a completed bar on that timeframe closes beyond the far edge. Select the newest un-inverted gap overlapping the OTE, formed since the range began, and matching the OTE side; check configured higher timeframes in priority order.
Calculation: Manipulation confirmation = For a long, collect bearish chart-timeframe FVGs around the latest tip swing; an inverse-FVG confirmation occurs when at least one exists and every one closes above its top by the configurable displacement. For a short, collect bullish FVGs and require every one to close below its bottom by that displacement. Alternatively, CISD confirms long when close is at least the displacement above the open of the first candle in the latest qualifying consecutive bearish run; it confirms short when close is at least the displacement below the open of the first candle in the latest qualifying consecutive bullish run. A CISD confirmation is invalid while any collected opposing FVG is overlapped by the current bar.
Calculation: ERL target = From the swept tip, use the major swing found by the configured swing lookback; for a high sweep, target the configured retracement fraction from the tip toward that swing's high; for a low sweep, target the same fraction from the tip toward that swing's high.
Long: During the NY AM killzone, a selected bullish aligned OTE FVG is tapped; while hunting, update the tip to each new low; Manipulation confirmation fires; close is nearer the range's upper extreme target than the tip stop (risk < reward). OR during the NY AM killzone, an Asia or London session low is swept; while hunting, update the tip to each new low; Manipulation confirmation fires; close-to-tip risk is no greater than target reward.
Short: During the NY AM killzone, a selected bearish aligned OTE FVG is tapped; while hunting, update the tip to each new high; Manipulation confirmation fires; close is nearer the range's lower extreme target than the tip stop (risk < reward). OR during the NY AM killzone, an Asia or London session high is swept; while hunting, update the tip to each new high; Manipulation confirmation fires; close-to-tip risk is no greater than target reward.
Exit: long, low <= tip stop OR high >= range upper extreme target OR high >= ERL target
Exit: short, high >= tip stop OR low <= range lower extreme target OR low <= ERL target
