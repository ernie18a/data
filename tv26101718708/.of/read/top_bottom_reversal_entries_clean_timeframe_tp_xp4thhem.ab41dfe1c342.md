Calculation: Candle range = high - low; lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / candle range and upper wick ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / candle range, both zero when candle range is zero.
Calculation: Long score = twice the number of true conditions among (RSI <= oversold threshold OR RSI crosses above the oversold threshold OR RSI < a higher rebound threshold AND RSI > previous RSI), (lower wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND close > low + half the candle range), and (low < lowest low of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low), plus the number of true conditions among (low <= lower Bollinger Band OR close <= lower Bollinger Band + ATR times a proximity multiplier), (close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous open AND open <= previous close), close > previous close, and candle range > ATR times a minimum-range multiplier.
Calculation: Short score = twice the number of true conditions among (RSI >= overbought threshold OR RSI crosses below the overbought threshold OR RSI > a lower reversal threshold AND RSI < previous RSI), (upper wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND close < high - half the candle range), and (high > highest high of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close < that highest high), plus the number of true conditions among (high >= upper Bollinger Band OR close >= upper Bollinger Band - ATR times a proximity multiplier), (close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous open AND open >= previous close), close < previous close, and candle range > ATR times a minimum-range multiplier.
Long: Long score >= score threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier) AND (hard EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (no previous long signal OR bars since previous long signal >= cooldown) AND (alternation disabled OR most recent signal is not long).
Short: Short score >= score threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier) AND (hard EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (no previous short signal OR bars since previous short signal >= cooldown) AND (alternation disabled OR most recent signal is not short).
Calculation: Signals are evaluated at bar close using signal history through the preceding bar; if both occur on the same bar, both are recorded and the most recent signal side becomes short. Entry price is the signal-bar close. Each new signal replaces the active target and stop with levels based on its entry price and signal-bar ATR; exit checks begin on subsequent bars. Target and stop ATR multipliers are configurable, including as functions of chart timeframe.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - signal-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + signal-bar ATR times final-target multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + signal-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - signal-bar ATR times final-target multiplier.
