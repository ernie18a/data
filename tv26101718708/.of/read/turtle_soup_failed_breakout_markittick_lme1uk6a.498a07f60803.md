Calculation: Channel low and channel high = respectively the lowest low and highest high over the preceding channel lookback bars, optionally smoothed with a selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or HMA. The low and high extreme ages are the number of bars from the current bar back to the corresponding raw extreme in that preceding window.
Calculation: Long candidate = ATR is available AND (ADX filter disabled OR previous-bar ADX >= threshold) AND (trend filter disabled or its timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe OR last completed higher-or-equal-timeframe close > its EMA) AND ((same-bar setup enabled AND low extreme age >= minimum age AND low < channel low AND close > channel low) OR (next-bar setup enabled AND previous low extreme age >= minimum age AND previous low < previous channel low AND previous close < previous channel low AND close > previous channel low)).
Calculation: Short candidate = ATR is available AND (ADX filter disabled OR previous-bar ADX >= threshold) AND (trend filter disabled or its timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe OR last completed higher-or-equal-timeframe close < its EMA) AND ((same-bar setup enabled AND high extreme age >= minimum age AND high > channel high AND close < channel high) OR (next-bar setup enabled AND previous high extreme age >= minimum age AND previous high > previous channel high AND previous close > previous channel high AND close < previous channel high)).
Calculation: Initial stop = on the entry bar, for a long, low minus ATR multiplied by a buffer; for a short, high plus ATR multiplied by the buffer. If the enabled next-bar setup qualifies for that direction, use the lower low or higher high of the entry bar and preceding bar respectively, even if the same-bar setup also qualifies. Keep the stop fixed after entry.
Calculation: Final target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a selected multiple of the absolute distance between entry price and initial stop. Entry price is the signal bar close.
Long: Long candidate AND NOT short candidate, evaluated at bar close.
Short: Short candidate AND NOT long candidate, evaluated at bar close.
Exit: long, short candidate AND NOT long candidate at bar close OR, after the entry bar, low <= initial stop OR high >= final target.
Exit: short, long candidate AND NOT short candidate at bar close OR, after the entry bar, high >= initial stop OR low <= final target.
