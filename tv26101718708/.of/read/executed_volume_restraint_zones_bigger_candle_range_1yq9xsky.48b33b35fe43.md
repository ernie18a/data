Calculation: Volume-profile point of control = divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over the profile lookback, including the current bar, into equal price bins; use max(highest high − lowest low, a price-increment parameter) as the range. For each bin, sum each bar's volume multiplied by the overlap of its low–high range with the bin, divided by max(bar high − bar low, the price-increment parameter). The point of control is the center of the first bin with the highest positive total; if no bin has positive volume, use the current close.
