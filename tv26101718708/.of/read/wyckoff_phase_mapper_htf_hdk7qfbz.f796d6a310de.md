Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the maximum/minimum across the configured number of bars before and after its pivot bar; it becomes known after those later bars complete.
Calculation: Climax = on the pivot bar, volume exceeds its configured multiple of average volume OR high minus low exceeds its configured multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Phase state = independently on each configured higher timeframe, using its bar still forming: start in phase 0. In phase 0, a confirmed pivot low with fast EMA below slow EMA and a Climax starts accumulation phase 1 and records that low as SC; a confirmed pivot high with fast EMA above slow EMA and a Climax starts distribution phase -1 and records that high as BC. In phase 1, the next confirmed pivot high starts phase 2 and records it as AR; in phase -1, the next confirmed pivot low starts phase -2 and records it as AR. In phase 2, a confirmed pivot low below SC starts phase 3 and records Spring; a confirmed pivot high above AR starts phase 4 and records SOS. In phase 3, a confirmed pivot high above AR starts phase 4 and records SOS. In phase 4, a confirmed pivot low above SC starts phase 5 and records LPS. In phase -2, a confirmed pivot high above BC starts phase -3 and records UTAD; a confirmed pivot low below AR starts phase -4 and records SOW. In phase -3, a confirmed pivot low below AR starts phase -4 and records SOW. In phase -4, a confirmed pivot high below BC starts phase -5 and records LPSY. Phase 5 resets to 0 when close exceeds AR by the configured expiry percentage; phase -5 resets to 0 when close falls below AR by that percentage. In any positive phase, a close below SC by the configured invalidation percentage sets a broken flag; in any negative phase, a close above BC by that percentage sets it. A broken structure resets to phase 0 when the fast and slow EMA trend reverses (fast EMA above slow EMA for positive phases, below for negative phases).
Long: phase 0 AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND confirmed pivot low AND (Climax); OR phase 1 AND confirmed pivot high; OR phase 2 AND confirmed pivot low < SC; OR (phase 2 OR phase 3) AND confirmed pivot high > AR; OR phase 4 AND confirmed pivot low > SC
Short: phase 0 AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND confirmed pivot high AND (Climax); OR phase -1 AND confirmed pivot low; OR phase -2 AND confirmed pivot high > BC; OR (phase -2 OR phase -3) AND confirmed pivot low < AR; OR phase -4 AND confirmed pivot high < BC
