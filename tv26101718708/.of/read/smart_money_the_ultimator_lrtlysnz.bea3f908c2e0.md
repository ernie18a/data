Calculation: Bar measures = spread is high minus low; close position is (close minus low) divided by spread, or the midpoint if spread is zero. Effort is volume, or spread times a scaling parameter if volume is missing or zero. Relative effort is effort divided by its moving average, or one if that average is zero. Trend score is the close change over a lookback divided by ATR times that lookback, scaled and bounded. Uptrend and downtrend require the score to exceed positive and negative thresholds, respectively. Regime retains the latest non-neutral trend direction and changes to the campaign direction on acceptance.
Calculation: Adaptive windows = multiply each base bar count by the ratio of longer-period ATR to shorter-period ATR, bound the multiplier and truncate to an integer; use the neutral multiplier when the shorter ATR is zero. The symmetric pivot confirmation length is a separately bounded and truncated multiple of that ATR ratio. A pivot is recognized only after its right-hand confirmation bars have elapsed.
Calculation: Campaign = process the following state updates in order on each bar. While inactive, retain the latest preliminary support candidate when downtrend AND relative effort exceeds its threshold AND low is near the rolling lowest low; retain the latest preliminary supply candidate when uptrend AND relative effort exceeds its threshold AND high is near the rolling highest high. Start a campaign only when exactly one climax qualifies: a top climax requires uptrend, high at the rolling highest high, elevated relative effort and spread relative to ATR, and at least one of a low close position, maximum rolling relative effort, or a larger spread threshold; a bottom climax mirrors trend, extreme and close position. Record climax bar, extreme, effort and spread. A top climax has bullish campaign direction if the retained regime is up or the long moving average slopes up, otherwise bearish; a bottom climax has bearish direction if the retained regime is down or that average does not slope up, otherwise bullish. The trend lookback and the rolling extreme lookback may differ; the moving-average slope compares its current value with its earlier value.
Calculation: Campaign continuation = in phase A, confirm absorption within its minimum and maximum elapsed-bar window when relative effort is sufficiently high, the current extreme has not exceeded the climax by more than an ATR allowance, and close has rebounded from the climax by an ATR threshold; deactivate after the window if unconfirmed. After absorption, track the most extreme opposite-side high or low that moves at least an ATR threshold from the climax; deactivate if no such candidate appears within its deadline. Confirm the candidate and enter phase B when price returns within an ATR allowance of the climax, current effort and spread are each below their respective fractions of climax effort and spread, enough bars have elapsed since the climax, and close is sufficiently far from the candidate. Set the range edges to that candidate and the current climax-side extreme; initialize the same-edge test count. On every phase B bar, count confirmed pivots near the corresponding range edge as same-edge or opposite-edge tests; count each close in either outer range zone as a traversal. Expand each range edge toward the current extreme, capped by a fraction of the current range height, updating the upper edge before the lower edge.
Calculation: Phase C event = after the phase B range update, a bullish probe requires bullish campaign direction, low below the lower edge by between the minimum and maximum ATR penetration allowances, close at or above that edge, and at least a threshold number of these conditions: close position in the upper portion, relative effort below its threshold, close above open, and spread below its ATR threshold. A bearish probe mirrors price direction, close position and candle direction. A qualifying probe immediately sets phase C and records its extreme and event bar; it does not require phase B maturity. If the penetration and close-back conditions are not both met, a terminal test instead sets phase C when phase B has lasted at least its adaptive minimum, the same-edge test count reaches its minimum, price is within an ATR allowance of the direction's range edge, and relative effort is below its quiet threshold. After a probe, record a completed retest when enough bars have passed since the last event, relative effort is below its retest threshold, and the probe extreme holds; update the last event bar.
Calculation: Maturity score = weighted sum of the current trend score being nonzero, qualifying climax, confirmed opposite-side move, minimum initial test count, phase at least C, recorded strength event, recorded last-point event, and phase E. Validation score = weighted sum of nonzero trend score, confirmed absorption, range height between ATR bounds, sufficient same-edge tests, sufficient opposite-edge tests, an always-true component, sufficient traversals, phase C outcome set, recorded strength event, and recorded last-point event. Evaluate both after phase C and retest updates but before the current bar's strength, last-point and acceptance updates; retain these values through entry evaluation on that bar.
Calculation: Strength event = while phase C outcome is set, enough bars have elapsed since the last event, and validation and maturity meet their strength thresholds, require directional dominance AND directional strength. Bullish dominance is a high close position OR close above the upper range edge; bearish dominance is a low close position OR close below the lower edge. Strength requires relative effort above a minimum AND either spread and relative effort both above their higher thresholds OR directional close change over a lookback at least an ATR threshold. Record the event, its bar and close, enter phase D, and update the last event bar.
Calculation: Last-point event = in phase D, after the minimum bars since the strength event and before any prior last-point event, require relative effort below its threshold, spread at or below its ATR threshold, and the directional pullback extreme within an ATR allowance of the broken range edge while holding that edge within the same allowance. Record the event and update the last event bar. In phase D, count consecutive closes back through the range midpoint against the campaign direction; at the demotion threshold return to phase B, clear the outcome and reset that counter, retaining previously recorded event fields. In phase D, track consecutive closes beyond the range in the campaign direction; enter phase E when their elapsed duration and the previously calculated maturity meet an acceptance threshold, with a lower duration allowed if a last-point event exists. In phase E deactivate on a close back through the midpoint or a maximum campaign age. Also deactivate in phase A after consecutive closes beyond the climax by an ATR allowance reach their invalidation threshold, or in any active phase when bars since the last event reach the adaptive inactivity limit.
Calculation: Entry readiness = count whether the outcome is set, phase is at least C, the probe retest is complete, strength is recorded, last point is recorded, maturity meets the selected strictness threshold, validation meets its readiness threshold, close is within an ATR allowance of the range midpoint, and the last event is sufficiently recent. An entry requires an active campaign, no prior entry in that campaign, an outcome set, maturity at or above the selected threshold, and readiness at or above its threshold. Conservative mode accepts the current last-point event; standard mode accepts the current probe or last-point event; aggressive mode accepts the current probe or a current strength event only when no probe has been recorded. Mark the first qualifying entry and prevent subsequent entries in the same campaign.

Short: Uptrend AND relative effort exceeds the preliminary-supply threshold AND high is near the rolling highest high AND a qualifying climax starts within the candidate's maximum age AND the retained candidate is preliminary supply.
