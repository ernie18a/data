Calculation: Expansion = at least a threshold count of the three most recently completed higher-timeframe bars have absolute bodies divided by the ATR of the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar greater than or equal to an expansion threshold AND that bar's (range minus absolute body) divided by its range is less than or equal to one minus a wick threshold; use zero for a body-to-ATR ratio when ATR is not positive and one for the wick ratio when the range is not positive.
Calculation: Session reversal = the retained high of the first session exceeds the last completed higher-timeframe period's high AND the retained close of that session is below that high, OR its retained low is below that period's low AND its retained close is above that low. Session highs and lows accumulate during each session and reset at its start; the retained close updates at its end.
Calculation: Bullish second-session reversal = the retained low of the second session is below the retained low of the first session AND the retained close of the second session is above the retained low of the first session. Bearish second-session reversal = the retained high of the second session is above the retained high of the first session AND the retained close of the second session is below the retained high of the first session. Session highs, lows, and closes are retained as in Session reversal.
Calculation: Retained gap = on each chart bar, compare the still-forming higher-timeframe bar with the bar two higher-timeframe bars earlier. A bullish candidate exists when the earlier low exceeds the current high, with those prices as its upper and lower bounds. A bearish candidate exists when the earlier high is below the current low, with those prices as its lower and upper bounds. Process the bullish candidate first, then the bearish candidate: replace the retained gap if none exists or the candidate midpoint is strictly closer to the chart close than the retained midpoint. Retain its direction and bounds otherwise, without clearing them when filled.
Long: Expansion AND the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar's close > its open.
Short: Expansion AND the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar's close < its open.
Long: Bullish second-session reversal OR (NOT bearish second-session reversal AND Session reversal AND the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar's close > its open).
Short: (Bearish second-session reversal AND NOT bullish second-session reversal) OR (NOT bullish second-session reversal AND NOT bearish second-session reversal AND Session reversal AND the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar's close < its open).
Long: Retained gap direction = bullish.
Short: Retained gap direction = bearish.
