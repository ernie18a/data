Calculation: WaveTrend fast = EMA of the channel index over the averaging lookback, where channel index = (source − EMA(source, channel lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(source − EMA(source, channel lookback)), channel lookback)); WaveTrend slow = SMA of WaveTrend fast over the smoothing lookback. The chart source is a configurable OHLC-derived price.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA((close − open) / (high − low) × multiplier, lookback) − offset.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA of the stochastic oscillator of RSI over its stochastic lookback; RSI uses either the selected price or its logarithm. Optionally replace K with the average of K and its SMA over a second smoothing lookback.
Calculation: A confirmed oscillator top or bottom is an oscillator value two bars ago strictly greater or strictly less, respectively, than each of the two oscillator values on either side. Its price is the high or low, respectively, two bars ago. Compare it with the most recent qualifying same-type fractal whose confirmation occurred at least two bars before the current confirmation. When a threshold applies, both fractals must satisfy it. Exclude zero-valued fractals.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed bottom with lower price and higher oscillator than the previous qualifying bottom; hidden bullish divergence = higher price and lower oscillator. Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed top with higher price and lower oscillator than the previous qualifying top; hidden bearish divergence = lower price and higher oscillator.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe WaveTrend difference = WaveTrend fast minus WaveTrend slow, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles use the last completed bar; Heikin-Ashi close is the average of OHLC and Heikin-Ashi open is the average of the preceding Heikin-Ashi open and close.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the signal bar's low and the preceding low minus ATR multiplied by a stop factor; long target = signal-bar close + reward-to-risk factor × (signal-bar close − long stop). Short stop = the higher of the signal bar's high and the preceding high plus ATR multiplied by a stop factor; short target = signal-bar close − reward-to-risk factor × (short stop − signal-bar close). Fix these prices at the entry signal.
Long: WaveTrend fast crosses above WaveTrend slow AND WaveTrend slow <= oversold threshold.
Short: WaveTrend fast crosses below WaveTrend slow AND WaveTrend slow >= overbought threshold.
Long: Regular bullish divergence of WaveTrend slow with either of two oversold fractal thresholds, OR regular bullish divergence of RSI with its oversold fractal threshold, OR regular bullish divergence of Stochastic RSI K without a threshold.
Short: Regular bearish divergence of WaveTrend slow with either of two overbought fractal thresholds, OR regular bearish divergence of RSI with its overbought fractal threshold, OR regular bearish divergence of Stochastic RSI K without a threshold.
Long: Hidden bullish divergence of WaveTrend slow or RSI, optionally without an oversold fractal threshold, OR hidden bullish divergence of WaveTrend slow with its second oversold fractal threshold, OR hidden bullish divergence of Stochastic RSI K without a threshold.
Short: Hidden bearish divergence of WaveTrend slow or RSI, optionally without an overbought fractal threshold, OR hidden bearish divergence of WaveTrend slow with its second overbought fractal threshold, OR hidden bearish divergence of Stochastic RSI K without a threshold.
Long: Money flow > bullish threshold AND WaveTrend slow < bullish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses above WaveTrend slow AND higher-timeframe WaveTrend difference > bullish threshold.
Short: Money flow < bearish threshold AND WaveTrend slow > bearish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses below WaveTrend slow AND higher-timeframe WaveTrend difference < bearish threshold.
Long: WaveTrend slow <= bullish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses above WaveTrend slow AND both Heikin-Ashi closes > their opens on two higher timeframes, one higher than the other.
Short: WaveTrend slow >= bearish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses below WaveTrend slow AND both Heikin-Ashi closes <= their opens on two higher timeframes, one higher than the other.
Exit: long, low <= fixed long stop OR high >= fixed long target.
Exit: short, high >= fixed short stop OR low <= fixed short target.
