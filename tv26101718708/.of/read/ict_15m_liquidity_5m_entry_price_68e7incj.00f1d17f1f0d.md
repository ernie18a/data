Calculation: Liquidity levels = Enabled previous-day high and low from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar; highs and lows of each enabled, most recently completed configurable session, calculated from chart bars in the New York timezone; and the latest confirmed pivot highs and lows from each enabled higher timeframe, as known on its last completed bar. Pivot left and right lengths are equal, with independently configurable lengths for each timeframe.
Calculation: Setup bar = The last completed bar of a higher timeframe, processed once at the start of each new bar of that timeframe. Its displacement is directional when its close is respectively above or below its open and its absolute body is at least a configurable multiple of the average absolute body over a configurable lookback ending on the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Setup gap = A bullish gap has setup-bar low above the high two higher-timeframe bars earlier, with bounds at those two prices; a bearish gap has setup-bar high below the low two higher-timeframe bars earlier, with bounds at those two prices. Retain the latest gap of each direction, updating it after processing both setup states.
Calculation: Setup state = Maintain separate bullish and bearish states and armed setups. On each setup bar, process bullish sweep, reclaim and confirmation in that order, then bearish sweep, reclaim and confirmation in that order. A newly entered state is processed immediately on the same setup bar. When idle and not armed, a bullish sweep is setup-bar low below any enabled liquidity low; select the highest swept low, record it and the sweep low. A bearish sweep is setup-bar high above any enabled liquidity high; select the lowest swept high, record it and the sweep high. Enter reclaim state unless the sweep bar closes respectively above or below the selected level, in which case enter confirmation state. In reclaim state, increment its age, update the bullish minimum low or bearish maximum high, and enter confirmation state with age zero if the close reclaims the level; otherwise return to idle when age exceeds the configurable reclaim limit. In confirmation state, increment its age; directional displacement arms the corresponding setup and returns its state to idle, otherwise return to idle when age exceeds the configurable confirmation limit. Test reclaim and displacement before their respective age expiry tests. The recorded sweep extreme is the armed setup's invalidation level. Arming either side disarms the other side; bearish processing occurs last if both arm on the same chart bar.
Calculation: Setup zone = At confirmation, use the latest previously stored opposing gap if a bullish close is above its upper bound or a bearish close is below its lower bound; otherwise use the current setup bar's same-direction gap if present; otherwise the setup has no zone.
Calculation: Pullback state = On each chart bar, process an armed bullish setup before an armed bearish setup, including on its arming bar. Disarm and clear its pullback if the bullish low is below its invalidation level or the bearish high is above its invalidation level, or if chart bars since arming exceed a configurable setup limit. Otherwise record the first bullish pullback when close is below open OR low is below the previous chart-bar low OR the bar overlaps the setup zone; record the first bearish pullback when close is above open OR high is above the previous chart-bar high OR the bar overlaps the setup zone. At that moment freeze the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high for bullish or pivot low for bearish, using equal configurable left and right pivot lengths. If no pivot exists, the frozen level remains unavailable. Disarm and clear the pullback when chart bars since the pullback exceed a configurable pullback limit. Clear the pullback after its signal; disarming by opposite-side arming does not clear it.
Calculation: Entry displacement = Chart-bar close is respectively above or below open and absolute body is at least a configurable multiple of the average absolute body over a configurable lookback ending on the preceding chart bar.
Long: Bullish setup remains armed AND bullish pullback is recorded AND current bar is later than the pullback bar AND close is above the frozen pivot high AND close is above open AND optional bullish entry displacement condition is satisfied AND optional configurable New York session filter is satisfied AND no earlier signal exists OR, grouped with all preceding conditions, chart bars since the most recent signal exceed the configurable shared cooldown.
Short: Bearish setup remains armed AND bearish pullback is recorded AND current bar is later than the pullback bar AND close is below the frozen pivot low AND close is below open AND optional bearish entry displacement condition is satisfied AND optional configurable New York session filter is satisfied AND no earlier signal exists OR, grouped with all preceding conditions, chart bars since the most recent signal exceed the configurable shared cooldown.
