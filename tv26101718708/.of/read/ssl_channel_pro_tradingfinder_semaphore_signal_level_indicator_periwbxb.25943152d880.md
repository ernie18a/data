Calculation: SSL state = bullish when close is above the moving average of highs, bearish when close is below the moving average of lows, otherwise retain the previous state; both moving averages use the same type and lookback.
Calculation: Normalized channel width = absolute difference between the high-based and low-based moving averages divided by ATR.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Signal score = the 0–100 bounded sum of transition, momentum, directional trend, channel regime, relative-volume participation, and context scores, less candle-wick and SSL-extension risk penalties. Context uses local SSL slope and price distance, or—when higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled—higher-timeframe SSL alignment.
Long: On a completed bar, SSL state changes from bearish to bullish AND RSI is above its midpoint AND ADX is above its configured threshold AND +DI > -DI AND normalized channel width is at least its configured threshold AND higher-timeframe SSL state is bullish if confirmation is enabled AND long signal score is at least its configured minimum.
Short: On a completed bar, SSL state changes from bullish to bearish AND RSI is below its midpoint AND ADX is above its configured threshold AND -DI > +DI AND normalized channel width is at least its configured threshold AND higher-timeframe SSL state is bearish if confirmation is enabled AND short signal score is at least its configured minimum.
