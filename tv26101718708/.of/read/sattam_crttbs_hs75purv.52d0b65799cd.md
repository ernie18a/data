Calculation: Higher-timeframe range state = Use only the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Start a range from its high and low when no range is active, the previous range reached its target, or it was invalidated. While awaiting manipulation, retain the range through inside bars; otherwise replace it with the latest completed higher-timeframe bar when its close is outside the range or the maximum wait has elapsed. A high sweep requires higher-timeframe high > range high AND close < range high; a low sweep requires higher-timeframe low < range low AND close > range low. If both occur, select the larger excursion, with ties selecting the high sweep. The selected sweep sets the direction and manipulation extreme. The setup ends immediately if the sweep bar touches the opposite range boundary, or later if a chart bar touches that boundary; it is invalidated if a subsequent completed higher-timeframe bar closes beyond the swept boundary. Update the manipulation extreme on subsequent higher-timeframe bars that extend it.
Calculation: Key-level confluence = On completed higher-timeframe bars, create zones from confirmed pivot-high upper wicks, confirmed pivot-low lower wicks, three-bar gaps between the first and third bars, and the full range of the most recent opposite-direction candle within a lookback preceding a displacement candle whose body exceeds a multiple of the average preceding body size. Retain only the most recent configured number of zones. Deactivate a zone if chart high > zone top AND chart low < zone bottom, OR chart close > zone top AND previous chart close < zone bottom, OR chart close < zone bottom AND previous chart close > zone top. At manipulation, confluence exists if the sweep interval overlaps an active zone after expanding the interval by a multiple of the mean high-minus-low range of recent completed higher-timeframe bars. Freeze this result for the setup.
Calculation: Body-sweep confirmation = During an active range setup with optional key-level confluence, a bearish setup becomes pending when chart close > the latest confirmed chart pivot high; a bullish setup becomes pending when close < the latest confirmed chart pivot low. Record the sweep bar's high or low and the opening price of the oldest candle in the most recent consecutive run of bullish candles for a bearish setup, or bearish candles for a bullish setup, searching backward through a bounded lookback. While pending, update the sweep extreme with each chart bar. Confirmation occurs when a bearish setup closes below the low of the latest bullish candle, or a bullish setup closes above the high of the latest bearish candle; the sweep and confirmation can occur on the same bar. At confirmation, record the latest confirmed pivot low for a bearish setup or pivot high for a bullish setup as the structure reference. Reset all confirmation and model states when the range changes or ceases to be active.
Calculation: Entry-model condition = After body-sweep confirmation, while the range remains active, optional key-level confluence holds, and the current hour is outside the configured blocked hours, evaluate each enabled model once per setup. Model 1 requires bearish close < previous low or bullish close > previous high. The directional-leg model requires bearish close < the recorded bullish-run opening or bullish close > the recorded bearish-run opening. The structure-and-gap model first requires bearish close < the recorded pivot low or bullish close > the recorded pivot high; then record the first bearish gap with high < low two bars earlier or bullish gap with low > high two bars earlier. Trigger when bearish high >= the gap's lower edge or bullish low <= the gap's upper edge; this can occur on the gap bar. Use close as entry price for the first two models and the touched gap edge for the third. Evaluate the models in that order; if several trigger together, the last model supplies the entry price.
Calculation: Trade levels = At each entry-model event, replace the tracked trade. For a long, initial stop = min(recorded sweep low, current low) minus an ATR multiple; target 1 = range midpoint if above entry; target 2 = range high if above entry; target 3 = range high plus a multiple of range height. For a short, initial stop = max(recorded sweep high, current high) plus an ATR multiple; target 1 = range midpoint if below entry; target 2 = range low if below entry; target 3 = range low minus a multiple of range height. Starting after the entry bar, check the stop before targets. If target 1 is reached, optionally move the stop to entry for subsequent bars. Targets 1 and 2 do not close the tracked trade; target 3 or the stop does.
Calculation: Internal sweep pivot = The latest chart high or low that is the highest or lowest within a symmetric lookback window, confirmed after the right-hand lookback. A pivot becomes crossed when a later close exceeds a high pivot or falls below a low pivot. Each pivot can produce one sweep, subject to a separate same-direction cooldown.
Calculation: Compression range = When the highest high minus the lowest low over a lookback is less than a multiple of a longer-lookback ATR times the square root of that lookback, freeze those extremes as the range, provided the lookback starts after the previous compression range ended. Keep the range until close leaves it; accept the breakout only if the range has lasted at least the lookback length.

Long: Close > previous completed higher-timeframe day high AND NOT (high > that high AND close < that high) AND NOT (low < previous completed higher-timeframe day low AND close > that low).

Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= current stop OR (high < current stop AND low <= target 3).
