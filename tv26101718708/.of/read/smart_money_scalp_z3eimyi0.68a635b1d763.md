Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low that remains the highest/lowest across equal lookback windows on both sides
Calculation: Entry zone = the range between the 0.382 and 0.618 retracements from the last confirmed opposite pivot to the CHoCH bar’s high (bullish) or low (bearish)
Long: price breaks above the latest confirmed pivot high AND the previous break direction was down
Long: price enters the active bullish entry zone
Short: price breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low AND the previous break direction was up
Short: price enters the active bearish entry zone
Exit: long, price reaches the 1.382–1.618 extension zone from the last confirmed pivot low to the bullish CHoCH bar’s high
Exit: short, price reaches the 1.382–1.618 extension zone from the last confirmed pivot high to the bearish CHoCH bar’s low
