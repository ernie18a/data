Calculation: Smoothed signal envelopes (for each length) = EMA of close; transform the absolute close-to-EMA distance by dividing by the absolute EMA, applying sin(0.68x² + 1.79x) × cos(0.68x² + 1.79x), taking the absolute value, restoring price scale, and EMA-smoothing the result; form upper/lower bands around the close EMA, then EMA-smooth max(upper band, close) and min(close, lower band) to obtain the upper and lower smoothed envelopes. Primary length is longer than secondary length.
Calculation: Envelope trigger = the smoothed-envelope range was falling on the previous bar and is no longer falling; neither smoothed envelope is rising/falling into a wedge.
Calculation: MLMA = kernel-regression estimate of close; upper and lower bands are the estimate plus/minus a multiple of the rolling mean absolute deviation. Bullish state requires close above the upper band and a rising estimate; bearish state requires close below the lower band and a falling estimate; otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Order-block retest = a touch of a live bullish or bearish volatility block within an ATR tolerance, with close not breaking the block; require a prior clean departure, sufficient bars since the prior touch, volume and block-strength thresholds, retest-count limit, and reaction within the block. Apply any selected trend-alignment and volatility filters, then the advanced confidence and clustering filters.
Calculation: Signal quality = weighted score of MLMA context, chart and higher-timeframe trend alignment, momentum, volatility, volume, and order-block context, less loss-streak penalty. No-trade filter rejects consolidation, envelope wedges, compressed volatility, trend conflict, or a narrow cloud containing close.
Long: primary envelope trigger AND close above the primary lower smoothed envelope OR (dual-length enabled AND secondary envelope trigger AND close above the secondary lower smoothed envelope), subject to the selected mode: Independent accepts either length, Consensus requires both, Primary Priority uses only primary; signal quality passes its minimum and no-trade filters do not reject.
Short: primary envelope trigger AND close below the primary upper smoothed envelope OR (dual-length enabled AND secondary envelope trigger AND close below the secondary upper smoothed envelope), subject to the selected mode: Independent accepts either length, Consensus requires both, Primary Priority uses only primary; signal quality passes its minimum and no-trade filters do not reject.
Long: bullish order-block retest passes its retest and advanced filters.
Short: bearish order-block retest passes its retest and advanced filters.
Long: close above the MLMA upper band and MLMA estimate is rising, changing the MLMA state to bullish.
Short: close below the MLMA lower band and MLMA estimate is falling, changing the MLMA state to bearish.
Exit: long, low <= the active stop-loss price OR all active take-profit levels have been reached.
Exit: short, high >= the active stop-loss price OR all active take-profit levels have been reached.
