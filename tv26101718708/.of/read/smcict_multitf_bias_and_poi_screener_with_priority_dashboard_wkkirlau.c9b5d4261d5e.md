Calculation: Valid FVG = on its source timeframe, a bullish gap has current low > high two bars earlier, or a bearish gap has current high < low two bars earlier; require the middle candle to close beyond the gap and meet the configured body-size and gap-size filters. Keep the latest valid gap until it is invalidated by a close or wick through its far boundary, or exceeds its age limit. Anchor and Primary FVGs use higher-timeframe bars; Local FVGs use lower-timeframe bars. Use completed bars for higher timeframes and the latest completed lower-timeframe bar available within each chart bar.
Calculation: Valid Order Block = on a higher timeframe, a close breaks above the prior range high or below the prior range low; use the most recent opposite-color candle as the zone. Keep the latest zone until a close passes through its far boundary or it exceeds its age limit. Use completed bars.
Long: At least one valid bullish POI exists: a bullish FVG from Anchor, Primary, or Local, or a bullish Order Block; select the valid bullish POI nearest the close (zero distance when price is inside its zone).
Short: At least one valid bearish POI exists: a bearish FVG from Anchor, Primary, or Local, or a bearish Order Block; select the valid bearish POI nearest the close (zero distance when price is inside its zone).
