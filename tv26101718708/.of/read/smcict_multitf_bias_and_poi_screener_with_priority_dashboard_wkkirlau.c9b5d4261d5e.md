Calculation: Timeframe sampling = Calculate each bias and zone on its configured timeframe. On historical chart bars, a higher-timeframe request returns the last completed higher-timeframe bar; a lower-timeframe request returns the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. The anchor, primary, local, and order-block timeframes are configurable and may each be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Structure direction = Confirm pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side. Track the latest two confirmed highs and lows. If either latest swing is missing, direction is neutral. Otherwise, if both latest swings are higher than their predecessors and close is not below the latest low, direction is long; else if both are lower and close is not above the latest high, direction is short; else if close is above the latest high, direction is long; else if close is below the latest low, direction is short; otherwise direction is neutral.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = Compare a shorter EMA of close with a longer EMA. EMA direction is long when the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA and, if the slope filter is enabled, the longer EMA's change over its slope lookback is greater than ATR times the slope threshold; it is short for the inverse comparisons; otherwise it is neutral. Select EMA direction alone, structure direction alone, their direction only when both agree and are nonneutral, or EMA direction unless a nonneutral structure direction opposes it.
Calculation: Main bias = Select the anchor bias, the primary bias, or their common nonneutral direction when both agree. The primary-with-anchor-filter mode also selects the primary bias; an opposing anchor does not change it.
Calculation: Fair value gap = On each configured anchor, primary, and local timeframe, retain the latest valid gap independently for each direction. A long gap has current low above high two bars earlier and boundaries at those prices; a short gap has current high below low two bars earlier and boundaries at those prices. Gap width must be at least ATR times the minimum-width parameter. If close confirmation is enabled, the middle close must be above the earlier high for a long gap or below the earlier low for a short gap. If the body filter is enabled, the middle bar's signed percentage body must exceed a parameter times the moving average of absolute percentage bodies for a long gap, or its negation must exceed that threshold for a short gap. Replace the prior same-direction gap when a new one qualifies. Remove a long gap when close or low, according to the configured invalidation source, falls below its lower boundary; remove a short gap when close or high rises above its upper boundary. Remove either gap when its age, counted from the first of its three bars, exceeds its timeframe's maximum age.
Calculation: Order block = On the configured order-block timeframe, compare close with the highest high and lowest low of a preceding lookback. A close above the high selects the most recent bearish candle as a long block; otherwise a close below the low selects the most recent bullish candle as a short block. Its boundaries are that candle's high and low, and its width must exceed ATR times a minimum-width parameter. Replace the previous block when a new qualifying block is found. Remove a long block when close falls below its lower boundary, a short block when close rises above its upper boundary, or either when its age measured from its source candle exceeds the maximum age.
Calculation: Directional setup = Among enabled valid gaps of the corresponding direction on the primary and local timeframes, the anchor timeframe when that context is enabled, and the enabled valid order block of that direction, select the zone with minimum distance from chart close to the nearest boundary, using zero inside the zone. Break ties in the order anchor gap, primary gap, local gap, order block. A setup exists when at least one such zone exists.
Long: Main bias = long OR anchor, primary, or local timeframe bias = long OR anchor, primary, or local structure direction = long OR long directional setup exists.
Short: Main bias = short OR anchor, primary, or local timeframe bias = short OR anchor, primary, or local structure direction = short OR short directional setup exists.
