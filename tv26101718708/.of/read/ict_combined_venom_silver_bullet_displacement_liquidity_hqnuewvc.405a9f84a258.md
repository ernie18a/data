Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of a configurable New York opening session, fixed after that session; maintain independent state for each opening-session and subsequent trading-session pair.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation level = after the first trading-session low below the opening-range low, provided the high has not exceeded the opening-range high, find the nearest earlier bullish candle within a configurable lookback; use the higher open of the two candles immediately after it, or the current open if it is the preceding candle. Anchor the level to the candle supplying that open, preferring the earlier candle on a tie. The bearish level mirrors this using a first high above the range, no low below it, the nearest earlier bearish candle, and the lower open.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = highs and lows of the last completed higher-timeframe bars at three progressively longer periods, and highs and lows of each of two most recently completed configurable New York sessions. Track each level separately and reset its signal state when its value changes.
Calculation: Premarket grid = the most recently completed configurable New York premarket session's low plus each configured fraction of its high-minus-low range, including its extremes and midpoint; retain it until the next valid range completes.
Calculation: Grid rejection count = for each grid level and New York calendar day, count upward wick touches with high >= level and max(open, close) < level, or downward wick touches with low <= level and min(open, close) > level. A body spanning the level resets both counts; an upward touch resets the downward count and vice versa; a bar entirely above the level resets the upward count, and a bar entirely below resets the downward count. Signal when the relevant count equals the configured tap count, optionally only once per level per day.
Calculation: First presented gap = the first bullish or bearish three-bar fair value gap whose middle bar falls in each configurable New York session and whose wick-to-wick width meets the minimum; use completed lower-timeframe bars, or completed chart bars when the source and chart timeframes match, and recognize it at the close of the chart bar containing its confirmation. Its raw bullish bounds are high two source bars ago and current low; bearish bounds are current high and low two source bars ago. Optionally widen the lower bullish bound to the first bar's body high if the middle body's low is above it, and the upper bullish bound to the current body's low if it is above the middle body's high; mirror these rules for bearish gaps.
Calculation: Opening gap = the interval between the prior completed configurable New York regular session's last close and the current session's first open, when their absolute difference exceeds a minimum. Set its side on its first intersecting chart bar: demand if the previous close is above its midpoint, otherwise supply. Reset its state when the gap's upper bound changes.
Calculation: Zone body test = for the current day's first presented gap, starting after its formation bar, or for the opening gap starting on its formation bar, count chart bars whose high-low range intersects the zone and whose body stays on the appropriate side of its midpoint: min(open, close) >= midpoint for demand or max(open, close) <= midpoint for supply. Signal holding once when the count reaches the configured tap count. Signal violation once on the first intersecting bar with min(open, close) < midpoint for demand or max(open, close) > midpoint for supply; stop testing that zone after violation. A first presented bullish gap is demand and a bearish gap is supply.
Long: During the corresponding trading session, a bullish confirmation level exists and has not yet been confirmed AND close >= that level AND its anchor age is at most the configured staleness limit; confirmation can occur on the raid bar.
Short: During the corresponding trading session, a bearish confirmation level exists and has not yet been confirmed AND close <= that level AND its anchor age is at most the configured staleness limit; confirmation can occur on the raid bar.
Long: The preceding chart bar closes above its open AND its selected body size or high-low range exceeds a configurable multiple of the rolling standard deviation of that same measure through that bar AND current low > high two bars ago AND, if configured, the current bar is in a trading session.
Short: The preceding chart bar closes below its open AND its selected body size or high-low range exceeds a configurable multiple of the rolling standard deviation of that same measure through that bar AND current high < low two bars ago AND, if configured, the current bar is in a trading session.
Long: Current low < a liquidity low AND close > that level AND that level has not previously signaled since its value last changed.
Short: Current high > a liquidity high AND close < that level AND that level has not previously signaled since its value last changed.
Long: Close < the premarket grid midpoint AND the preceding bar was not below its then-current midpoint.
Short: Close > the premarket grid midpoint AND the preceding bar was not above its then-current midpoint.
Long: A configured premarket grid level's downward rejection count equals the configured tap count AND its optional one-signal-per-level-per-day restriction permits the signal.
Short: A configured premarket grid level's upward rejection count equals the configured tap count AND its optional one-signal-per-level-per-day restriction permits the signal.

Short: A bearish first presented gap forms.

Short: A supply opening gap passes its zone body test for holding OR a demand opening gap fails its zone body test.

Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND the gap width meets the minimum AND the first body's low > the middle body's high AND the middle body's low > the current body's high AND the current bar is within the configured scan session, if one is selected.
