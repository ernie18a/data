Calculation: Net USD liquidity = Federal Reserve total assets − Treasury General Account − reverse repo, using last completed higher-timeframe bars
Calculation: Normalized liquidity = (net USD liquidity − its rolling low) / (its rolling high − its rolling low), using the same lookback as normalized BTC
Calculation: Normalized BTC = (BTC close − its rolling low) / (its rolling high − its rolling low), using last completed higher-timeframe bars and the same lookback as normalized liquidity
Calculation: Liquidity divergence = normalized liquidity − normalized BTC
Long: liquidity divergence > 0
Short: liquidity divergence < 0
