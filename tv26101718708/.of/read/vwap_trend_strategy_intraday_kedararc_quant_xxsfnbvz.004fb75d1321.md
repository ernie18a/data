Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each trading session.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × |close − open| / open; if open is unavailable, use close as the denominator.
Long: Position is flat or short AND bars since the last long entry are at least the minimum spacing, or no previous long entry exists AND (the one-entry-per-side-per-day restriction is disabled OR no long entry has occurred today) AND (outside market session OR minutes since the first detected in-session bar are at least the cooldown) AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe close EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA AND ((volume >= volume SMA × participation multiplier AND (close crosses above session VWAP OR (retest mode is enabled AND close > session VWAP AND lowest low over the retest lookback including the current bar <= current session VWAP))) OR (momentum exception is enabled AND close > session VWAP AND (open <= session VWAP OR low <= session VWAP) AND volume >= volume SMA × momentum multiplier AND candle body percentage >= body threshold)).
Short: Position is flat or long AND bars since the last short entry are at least the minimum spacing, or no previous short entry exists AND (the one-entry-per-side-per-day restriction is disabled OR no short entry has occurred today) AND (outside market session OR minutes since the first detected in-session bar are at least the cooldown) AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe close EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA AND ((volume >= volume SMA × participation multiplier AND (close crosses below session VWAP OR (retest mode is enabled AND close < session VWAP AND highest high over the retest lookback including the current bar >= current session VWAP))) OR (momentum exception is enabled AND close < session VWAP AND (open >= session VWAP OR high >= session VWAP) AND volume >= volume SMA × momentum multiplier AND candle body percentage >= body threshold)).
Exit: long, close < session VWAP AND shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA.
Exit: short, close > session VWAP AND shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA.
Exit: long, ATR stop is enabled AND low <= previous bar's close − previous bar's ATR × stop multiplier.
Exit: short, ATR stop is enabled AND high >= previous bar's close + previous bar's ATR × stop multiplier.
Exit: long, ATR stop is enabled AND low <= current close − current ATR × stop multiplier.
Exit: short, ATR stop is enabled AND high >= current close + current ATR × stop multiplier.
