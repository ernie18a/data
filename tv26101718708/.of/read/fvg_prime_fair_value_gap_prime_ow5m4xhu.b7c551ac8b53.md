Calculation: Bullish gap = on a completed bar, low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > high two bars earlier AND, if the ATR size filter is enabled, low − high two bars earlier >= ATR × gap-size multiplier; top = low, bottom = high two bars earlier.
Calculation: Bearish gap = on a completed bar, high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < low two bars earlier AND, if the ATR size filter is enabled, low two bars earlier − high >= ATR × gap-size multiplier; top = low two bars earlier, bottom = high.
Calculation: Tracked gaps = enabled bullish and bearish gaps in creation order; after adding a gap, remove the oldest if the configured maximum count is exceeded. Retained mitigated gaps count toward this maximum. A gap is active until removed or mitigated; check mitigation only on completed bars after its creation bar, after checking its signal. For a bullish gap, mitigation occurs when low <= top, midpoint, or bottom for the selected touch, midpoint, or full-fill mode, respectively. For a bearish gap, mitigation occurs when high >= bottom, midpoint, or top for the respective mode. Midpoint = (top + bottom) / 2. A gap can produce at most one signal.
Long: A bullish gap forms.
Short: A bearish gap forms.
Long: On a completed bar after creation, an active bullish gap has not previously signaled AND low <= its top AND (first-touch mode is selected OR close > its top AND close > open) AND (the EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier).
Short: On a completed bar after creation, an active bearish gap has not previously signaled AND high >= its bottom AND (first-touch mode is selected OR close < its bottom AND close < open) AND (the EMA filter is disabled OR close <= EMA of close) AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier).
