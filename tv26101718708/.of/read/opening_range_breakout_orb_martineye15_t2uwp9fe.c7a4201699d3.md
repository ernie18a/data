Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall in the configured session and timezone; lock the range on the first bar outside that session and retain it until the next session starts. Range size = high minus low; percentage size = 100 × range size / midpoint, or zero if midpoint is zero. Reset all breakout, retest, trade-taken, position and target-hit state at the start of each opening-range session.
Calculation: Breakout test = close above the opening-range high for long or below the opening-range low for short, alternatively high above the opening-range high for long or low below the opening-range low for short. Evaluate on completed chart bars outside the opening-range session. A new breakout occurs when the test is true and that side's breakout flag is false; set the flag even if an entry filter fails. Without retest, the new breakout is the signal; in all-breakouts mode reset the flag when close, or high in wick mode, is at or below the high for long, or close, or low in wick mode, is at or above the low for short. With retest, after a new breakout, mark a retest on a later bar with low at or below the high for long or high at or above the low for short; a subsequent bar satisfying the breakout test is the signal. In all-breakouts mode reset that side's breakout and retest flags after the signal, even if an entry filter fails. In first-breakout mode retain the flags until the next session.
Calculation: Entry price = signal bar close in close-confirmation mode, otherwise the opening-range high for long or low for short. At entry, fix the stop at the opposite opening-range boundary, the opening-range midpoint, entry minus or plus an ATR multiple, entry minus or plus a fixed tick distance, or entry minus or plus a percentage of entry, respectively for long or short. Fix each configured target at entry plus or minus a distance equal to a multiple of absolute entry-to-stop risk, a multiple of opening-range size, a multiple of entry-bar ATR, a fixed tick distance, or a percentage of entry, respectively for long or short. Tick size is a price increment parameter.
Long: The opening range is locked AND its session-start weekday is enabled AND range size > 0 AND each enabled minimum or maximum range-size filter passes, using ticks or percentage size AND a long breakout signal occurs AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > its simple moving average) AND the tracked position is flat AND (one-trade-per-session is disabled OR no entry has occurred since the session began) AND (all-breakouts mode is enabled OR no long entry has occurred since the session began).
Short: The opening range is locked AND its session-start weekday is enabled AND range size > 0 AND each enabled minimum or maximum range-size filter passes, using ticks or percentage size AND a short breakout signal occurs AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > its simple moving average) AND the tracked position is flat AND (one-trade-per-session is disabled OR no entry has occurred since the session began) AND (all-breakouts mode is enabled OR no short entry has occurred since the session began) AND no long entry occurs on the same bar.
Exit: Long, completed bar low <= entry-fixed stop OR completed bar high >= entry-fixed final configured target.
Exit: Short, completed bar high >= entry-fixed stop OR completed bar low <= entry-fixed final configured target.
