Long: close crosses above HMA AND Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open AND close > Supertrend AND volume >= volume SMA AND (close > POC OR close > EMA) AND RSI < 70 AND CCI > 0
Short: close crosses below lower-timeframe HMA calculated from the still-forming lower-timeframe bar AND lower-timeframe RSI calculated from the still-forming lower-timeframe bar < 45
Long: close crosses above lower-timeframe HMA calculated from the still-forming lower-timeframe bar AND lower-timeframe RSI calculated from the still-forming lower-timeframe bar > 50
Exit: long, close crosses below Supertrend OR (close crosses below HMA AND RSI > 75)
