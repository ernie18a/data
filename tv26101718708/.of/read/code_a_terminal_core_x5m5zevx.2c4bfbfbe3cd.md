Calculation: Trend projection = previous Hull moving average of the selected price source plus its change over a lookback ending on the previous bar divided by that lookback.
Calculation: Wave = EMA of (typical price minus its EMA) divided by 0.015 times the EMA of the absolute deviation from that EMA; use the same channel length for both inner EMAs and a separate smoothing length for the outer EMA.
Calculation: Wave signal = SMA of Wave; wave difference = Wave minus wave signal, rounded to one decimal place.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA of ((close - open) / (high - low)) multiplied by a scale, minus an offset.
Calculation: Fast and slow stochastic = separately smoothed stochastic oscillators of close, high and low; the fast oscillator has the shorter lookback and both use the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Trend score = WMA of the sum of +1 when close exceeds a historical close and -1 otherwise, over a consecutive range of historical offsets, multiplied by 100/12.
Calculation: Up count = consecutive bars with close greater than close at a fixed earlier offset; down count = consecutive bars with close less than close at that offset. Each count resets to zero otherwise. Up run and down run equal their respective current count minus its value at the second most recent bar where that count decreased from its preceding value.
Calculation: Bullish trend = Hull moving average greater than both its trend projection and its previous value; bearish trend = Hull moving average less than or equal to its trend projection and less than its previous value.
Calculation: Long crossover gate = wave crossing above its signal on the current bar or at a fixed earlier offset when the wave crossover filter is enabled, OR the medium EMA of close crossing above the long EMA of close when the EMA crossover filter is enabled. Short crossover gate = wave crossing below its signal on the current bar or at the same earlier offset when the wave crossover filter is enabled, OR the short EMA of close crossing below the medium EMA of close when the EMA crossover filter is enabled. When the combined crossover filter is enabled, replace OR between the wave and EMA components with AND. When both crossover filters are disabled, both gates are true.
Calculation: Long duplicate gate = true initially, reset to true whenever bullish trend is false, and set to false after a long signal when duplicate removal is enabled. Short duplicate gate = true initially, reset to true whenever bearish trend is false, and set to false after a short signal when duplicate removal is enabled.
Long: At least one signal filter is enabled AND long crossover gate = true AND long duplicate gate = true AND (high/low filter disabled OR high > previous high) AND (series filter disabled OR up run <= maximum run) AND (PSAR bias filter disabled OR close > PSAR) AND (candle momentum filter disabled OR (open > previous open AND close > previous close)) AND (shadowless filter disabled OR min(open, close) = low) AND (difference filter disabled OR wave difference >= positive difference threshold) AND (trend filter disabled OR bullish trend = true) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= minimum volume) AND (trend score filter disabled OR trend score > previous trend score OR trend score >= upper extreme OR trend score <= lower extreme) AND ((wave signal > 0 AND (money flow filter disabled OR money flow > 0) AND (second band filter disabled OR wave signal < upper second band)) OR (wave signal < 0 AND (stochastic filter disabled OR fast stochastic > slow stochastic) AND (first band filter disabled OR wave signal < lower first band))).
Short: At least one signal filter is enabled AND short crossover gate = true AND short duplicate gate = true AND (high/low filter disabled OR low < previous low) AND (series filter disabled OR down run <= maximum run) AND (PSAR bias filter disabled OR close < PSAR) AND (candle momentum filter disabled OR (open < previous open AND close < previous close)) AND (shadowless filter disabled OR max(open, close) = high) AND (difference filter disabled OR wave difference <= negative difference threshold) AND (trend filter disabled OR bearish trend = true) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= minimum volume) AND (trend score filter disabled OR trend score < previous trend score OR trend score <= lower extreme OR trend score >= upper extreme) AND ((wave signal < 0 AND (money flow filter disabled OR money flow <= 0) AND (second band filter disabled OR wave signal > lower second band)) OR (wave signal > 0 AND (stochastic filter disabled OR fast stochastic <= slow stochastic) AND (first band filter disabled OR wave signal > upper first band))).
Long: Bullish trend = true.
Short: Bearish trend = true.
Long: Down count >= exhaustion threshold.
Short: Up count >= exhaustion threshold.
Long: Close > PSAR.
Short: Close <= PSAR.
Exit: long, at least one exit filter is enabled AND (candle color exit disabled OR close < open) AND (candle difference exit disabled OR wave difference <= exit threshold).
Exit: short, at least one exit filter is enabled AND (candle color exit disabled OR close > open) AND (candle difference exit disabled OR wave difference >= exit threshold).
