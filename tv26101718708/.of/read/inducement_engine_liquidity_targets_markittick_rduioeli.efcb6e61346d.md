Calculation: Confirmed pivot = at the current bar, the high or low n bars ago is strictly greater than or less than, respectively, every corresponding high or low at offsets 1 through 2n except offset n; the current bar is excluded.
Calculation: Structural bounds = initialize both bounds to zero; on each bar update the upper bound to a newly confirmed pivot high if it is higher, and the lower bound to a newly confirmed pivot low if it is lower or the bound is zero. Structure becomes active once both bounds are nonzero. After processing sweeps and filters, a filtered short signal resets the upper bound to the latest confirmed pivot high; a filtered long signal resets the lower bound to the latest confirmed pivot low. Apply both resets if both signals occur.
Calculation: Pending levels = after updating structural bounds, append each newly confirmed pivot strictly between the bounds when structure is active, recording whether it is a high or low. Keep a chronological list including broken levels; if its size exceeds the memory limit, remove the oldest element once per bar. Then mark every unbroken high level broken when the previous bar's low is below it, and every unbroken low level broken when the previous bar's high is above it. A level can be broken on the bar it is registered.
Calculation: Filtered long signal = a low pending level is newly broken AND, if enabled, the current bar is in the configured session AND the previous close is above the higher-timeframe EMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND current ATR is above its SMA AND the previous close is above the midpoint of the previous bar's high and low AND at least one of the current and preceding two bars has its previous bar's low above its high three bars ago; each filter is independently optional.
Calculation: Filtered short signal = a high pending level is newly broken AND, if enabled, the current bar is in the configured session AND the previous close is below the higher-timeframe EMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND current ATR is above its SMA AND the previous close is below the midpoint of the previous bar's high and low AND at least one of the current and preceding two bars has its previous bar's high below its low three bars ago; each filter is independently optional.
Long: Previous bar's high > any unbroken pending low level.
Short: Previous bar's low < any unbroken pending high level.
