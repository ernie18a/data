Calculation: Bar delta = sum of volume × sign(close − previous close) over the completed lower-timeframe bars inside the chart bar; use the preceding lower-timeframe close across chart-bar boundaries. Delta percentage = bar delta / chart-bar volume, or zero when chart-bar volume is zero.
Calculation: Profile reset = the start of a new period of the configured higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar, OR confirmation of a chart-timeframe swing pivot in the opposite direction from the last swing reset. A pivot is confirmed after the same number of bars on each side; process a confirmed low before a confirmed high if both occur together. Cumulative delta resets to zero on a profile reset, then adds each chart bar's bar delta.
Calculation: Developing profile = accumulate lower-timeframe volume from the reset bar through the current chart bar. At reset, set a fixed row height from a price-increment parameter or chart ATR divided by a parameter, subject to a minimum price increment; align the initial row base downward from the reset bar's open. For each completed lower-timeframe bar, divide its volume equally among every price row from its low's row through its high's row, growing the row array up or down up to a row-count limit. The point of control is the midpoint of the lowest-priced row tied for maximum volume. Starting there, expand the value area one adjacent row at a time toward the larger-volume neighbor, choosing the upper neighbor on ties, until the included volume reaches the configured fraction of total volume or no rows remain. The value-area low and high are the outer row boundaries. The point of void is the midpoint of the lowest-priced minimum-volume row inside the value area excluding the point-of-control row; it is unavailable if no such row exists.
Calculation: Signal origin = the current value-area edge for an enabled sweep, the current point of control for an enabled bounce, or the current point of void for an enabled void break; when several raw models match, void takes precedence over bounce, and bounce over sweep, regardless of their wick filter. For each side, remember the origin and chart bar of the most recent signal of that side. A same-side signal is eligible when there is no previous signal, OR the bars since it are at least the cooldown, OR the absolute change in origin is at least the current risk ATR.
Calculation: Long stop = the entry-bar low minus a risk-ATR multiple, OR, in node-stop mode, min(entry-bar low, signal origin) minus a risk-ATR buffer. Short stop = the entry-bar high plus a risk-ATR multiple, OR, in node-stop mode, max(entry-bar high, signal origin) plus a risk-ATR buffer. Round stops to the configured price increment and freeze them at entry.
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus a configured multiple of entry price minus long stop; in node-target mode use the nearest entry-bar point of control, value-area high, or point of void above entry instead if its distance exceeds a risk-ATR threshold. Short target = entry price minus a configured multiple of short stop minus entry price; in node-target mode use the nearest entry-bar point of control, value-area low, or point of void below entry instead if its distance exceeds the same risk-ATR threshold. Round targets to the configured price increment and freeze them at entry.
Long: Direction permits long AND no profile reset on the current bar AND same-side signal is eligible AND (momentum filter disabled OR high − low <= risk ATR × momentum multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime falls within the configured session) AND (delta filter disabled OR bar delta > 0) AND (divergence filter disabled OR (low < lowest low of the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND cumulative delta > lowest cumulative delta of those preceding bars)) AND ((sweep enabled AND low < value-area low AND close > value-area low AND close > open AND (wick filter disabled OR lower wick / bar range >= wick threshold)) OR (bounce enabled AND low <= point of control AND close > point of control AND close > open AND low > value-area low AND (wick filter disabled OR lower wick / bar range >= wick threshold)) OR (void enabled AND close > point of void AND previous close <= current point of void AND close > open)).
Short: Direction permits short AND no profile reset on the current bar AND same-side signal is eligible AND (momentum filter disabled OR high − low <= risk ATR × momentum multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime falls within the configured session) AND (delta filter disabled OR bar delta < 0) AND (divergence filter disabled OR (high > highest high of the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND cumulative delta < highest cumulative delta of those preceding bars)) AND ((sweep enabled AND high > value-area high AND close < value-area high AND close < open AND (wick filter disabled OR upper wick / bar range >= wick threshold)) OR (bounce enabled AND high >= point of control AND close < point of control AND close < open AND high < value-area high AND (wick filter disabled OR upper wick / bar range >= wick threshold)) OR (void enabled AND close < point of void AND previous close >= current point of void AND close < open)).
Long: Delta percentage >= whale threshold AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier.
Short: Delta percentage <= negative whale threshold AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier.
Long: Delta percentage > candle-delta threshold.
Short: Delta percentage < negative candle-delta threshold.
Long: Cumulative delta > every cumulative-delta value in the preceding regime-lookback bars.
Short: Cumulative delta <= at least one cumulative-delta value in the preceding regime-lookback bars.
Exit: long, opposite-signal closing enabled AND short signal occurs.
Exit: short, opposite-signal closing enabled AND long signal occurs.
Exit: long, current bar is later than the signal bar AND no new signal occurs on the current bar AND (low <= frozen long stop OR high >= frozen long target).
Exit: short, current bar is later than the signal bar AND no new signal occurs on the current bar AND (high >= frozen short stop OR low <= frozen short target).
