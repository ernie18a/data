Calculation: Developing volume profile = Accumulate volume from lower-timeframe bars within the current profile anchor; sign each bar’s volume by the sign of its close minus the previous lower-timeframe close, then distribute it evenly across the profile price rows intersected by its high–low range. Use completed lower-timeframe bars; the feed is lower than the chart timeframe. Reset at the configured higher-timeframe period boundary or confirmed chart-timeframe swing reversal.
Calculation: POC, value area, and point of void = POC is the price row with greatest accumulated volume. Starting at POC, expand the value area one adjacent row at a time, choosing the higher-volume adjacent row, until it contains the configured share of profile volume; its bounds are VAL and VAH. Point of void is the lowest-volume row inside the value area, excluding POC.
Calculation: Bar delta = Sum of signed lower-timeframe volume within the chart bar.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = Running sum of bar delta since the current profile anchor began.
Calculation: Long stop = For the configured ATR mode, signal-bar low minus ATR times the configured stop multiple; for the origin-node mode, the lower of signal-bar low and origin node minus ATR times the configured node buffer.
Calculation: Short stop = For the configured ATR mode, signal-bar high plus ATR times the configured stop multiple; for the origin-node mode, the higher of signal-bar high and origin node plus ATR times the configured node buffer.
Calculation: Long target = In next-node mode, the nearest of POC, POV, or VAH above entry if its distance exceeds 0.3 ATR; otherwise entry plus entry-to-stop risk times the configured reward-to-risk multiple.
Calculation: Short target = In next-node mode, the nearest of POC, POV, or VAL below entry if its distance exceeds 0.3 ATR; otherwise entry minus stop-to-entry risk times the configured reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: (low < VAL AND close > VAL AND close > open AND lower-wick share of bar range meets configured minimum, OR low <= POC AND close > POC AND close > open AND low > VAL AND lower-wick share meets configured minimum, OR close > POV AND previous close <= POV AND close > open) AND (CVD divergence disabled OR low < lowest prior low over configured lookback AND current CVD > lowest prior CVD over that lookback) AND (bar-delta confirmation disabled OR bar delta > 0) AND (momentum filter disabled OR high - low <= ATR times configured maximum) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is inside configured session) AND long direction enabled AND not a new profile anchor bar AND no same-direction signal at a node within 1 ATR during the configured cooldown.
Short: (high > VAH AND close < VAH AND close < open AND upper-wick share of bar range meets configured minimum, OR high >= POC AND close < POC AND close < open AND high < VAH AND upper-wick share meets configured minimum, OR close < POV AND previous close >= POV AND close < open) AND (CVD divergence disabled OR high > highest prior high over configured lookback AND current CVD < highest prior CVD over that lookback) AND (bar-delta confirmation disabled OR bar delta < 0) AND (momentum filter disabled OR high - low <= ATR times configured maximum) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is inside configured session) AND short direction enabled AND not a new profile anchor bar AND no same-direction signal at a node within 1 ATR during the configured cooldown. The wick condition applies only to sweep and POC-bounce alternatives.
Exit: Long, short signal OR low <= long stop OR high >= long target; if stop and target are both touched on the same bar, exit at stop.
Exit: Short, long signal OR high >= short stop OR low <= short target; if stop and target are both touched on the same bar, exit at stop.
