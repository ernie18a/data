Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars ago and the gap is at least the configured ATR multiple; if displacement is required, the middle bar is bullish, its body is at least the configured ATR multiple, and its body/range ratio meets the configured minimum
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars ago and the gap is at least the configured ATR multiple; if displacement is required, the middle bar is bearish, its body is at least the configured ATR multiple, and its body/range ratio meets the configured minimum
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low is at least the configured ATR multiple below the latest confirmed swing low, and (if reclaim is required) close is above that swing low
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = high is at least the configured ATR multiple above the latest confirmed swing high, and (if reclaim is required) close is below that swing high
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the configured number of bars on each side; latest and prior refer to the two most recent confirmed pivots
Calculation: Bullish BOS = latest confirmed swing high > prior confirmed swing high; bearish BOS = latest confirmed swing low < prior confirmed swing low
Calculation: Discount/premium midpoint = midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high and swing low; discount = close below it; premium = close above it
Calculation: Displacement candle = candle body is at least the configured ATR multiple, body/range ratio meets the configured minimum, and candle direction matches the displacement direction
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the most recent opposite-color candle within the configured lookback before a bullish/bearish displacement candle; bullish retest = low enters its high-low range and close > its low; bearish retest = high enters its range and close < its high; retest is valid for fewer than 100 bars after the block
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback
Calculation: Volatility ratio = short-term ATR / longer-term ATR; compressed = ratio below configured threshold
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG CE tap = for an unmitigated bullish/bearish FVG, low <= / high >= its midpoint; record the tap and its FVG score on that bar
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG score = logistic(-0.60 + weighted sum of logit factor scores), clamped to 0.04 and the configured maximum; factors are gap-size/ATR (weight 1.2), fast/slow EMA trend (1.0), close beyond the chart-timeframe 200-period EMA by the configured ATR buffer (1.0), middle-bar relative volume (0.8), gap midpoint in discount/premium (1.5), aligned BOS (1.3), active killzone (0.7), and volatility regime (0.9); factor scores and size-ratio bands use the source-defined values
