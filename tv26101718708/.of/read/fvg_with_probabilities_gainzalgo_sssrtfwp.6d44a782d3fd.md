Calculation: All calculations use chart-timeframe bars. Parameters include thresholds, lookbacks, session boundaries, feature switches, scoring weights and score clamps. Evaluate each bar in source order; unavailable values make comparisons false.
Calculation: A confirmed swing high or low is a pivot with the same parameterized number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. Bullish structure means the latest confirmed swing high exceeds the preceding swing high; bearish structure means the latest confirmed swing low is below the preceding swing low. The range midpoint is the average of the latest confirmed swing high and low.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish displacement at an offset means that bar closes above or below its open respectively, its absolute body divided by current-bar ATR is at least the body threshold, and its absolute body divided by its range is at least the body-to-range threshold. Use a positive minimum denominator for zero ranges or ATR.
Calculation: A bullish gap has top = current low and bottom = high two bars ago; a bearish gap has top = low two bars ago and bottom = current high. Record a newly detected gap only if its interval does not overlap any retained gap. Its birth bar, direction, bounds and creation-time score are retained. Initially it is untapped. On each bar, an untapped bullish gap is tapped when low <= midpoint; an untapped bearish gap is tapped when high >= midpoint. Mark it tapped and retain the highest creation-time score among gaps tapped in each direction on that bar. Process taps before deletion or inversion.
Calculation: For each retained non-inverted gap, a full fill is low <= bottom for a bullish gap or high >= top for a bearish gap. On a full fill, invert it if inversion tracking is enabled, close is beyond the far boundary by at least the ATR-distance threshold, and each enabled opposing-displacement, opposing-structure and session filter passes. An inverted gap changes direction and, if the redraw option is enabled, its bounds become the inversion bar's body. Delete a fully filled gap that does not invert. Delete an inverted gap when close breaks its new far boundary. Delete gaps older than the maximum age after processing their current-bar tap; retained gaps, including inverted gaps, prevent overlapping new gaps.
Calculation: Gap score is computed once at creation as a clamped logistic function of an intercept plus a weighted sum of logits of eight bounded factor values. The factors are parameterized piecewise mappings of gap size/current ATR, directional short-versus-long EMA alignment, close versus the longest chart-timeframe EMA plus or minus an ATR buffer, previous-bar volume divided by its volume SMA, gap midpoint versus the swing-range midpoint, directional structure, session membership, and current ATR divided by a longer-lookback ATR. Use the neutral midpoint factor when the swing range is unavailable. The score's factor thresholds, factor outputs, weights, intercept and clamps are parameters.
Calculation: A bullish sweep requires both latest swing levels available, low below the latest swing low by at least an ATR multiple, and, when reclaim is required, close above that swing low. A bearish sweep mirrors this at the latest swing high. Sweep detection must be enabled.
Calculation: On bullish displacement, create a bullish order block from the high and low of the nearest bearish candle within the lookback; on bearish displacement, create a bearish block from the nearest bullish candle. Suppress duplicate bounds across both directions and retain only the newest parameterized maximum number of blocks. A bullish retest has low within the block bounds and close above its bottom; a bearish retest has high within the bounds and close below its top. The block's age is measured from its source candle and must be below the freshness limit. Order-block detection must be enabled.
Calculation: Directional triggers are the preceding bar's first gap-midpoint tap, order-block retest and sweep. The trend trigger is the OR of enabled triggers. If gap-tap triggering is enabled and a same-direction gap tap occurred, its highest score must also meet the signal-score threshold, even when another trigger is present. Reversal signals use the preceding sweep regardless of the individual trigger switches. The optional top-tier filter requires a trend signal, at least two of the three raw preceding-bar triggers, expanding volatility, and at least two same-direction displacement bars among the current and two preceding bars.
Calculation: Shared signal context requires current volume divided by its volume SMA >= the relative-volume threshold, current ATR divided by longer-lookback ATR >= the compression threshold, membership in an enabled required session, and exclusion from enabled opening, closing and lunch blackout intervals. Session and blackout comparisons use the configured timezone. The longest EMA is calculated on the chart timeframe, despite its higher-timeframe name.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago AND gap size >= ATR multiple AND (displacement filter disabled OR preceding bar is bullish displacement) AND new gap does not overlap a retained gap.
Short: Current high < low two bars ago AND gap size >= ATR multiple AND (displacement filter disabled OR preceding bar is bearish displacement) AND new gap does not overlap a retained gap.
Long: A bearish gap fully fills AND close > its top AND close - top >= ATR-distance threshold AND inversion tracking is enabled AND each enabled bullish-displacement, bullish-structure and session filter passes.
Short: A bullish gap fully fills AND close < its bottom AND bottom - close >= ATR-distance threshold AND inversion tracking is enabled AND each enabled bearish-displacement, bearish-structure and session filter passes.

Short: Bearish sweep.
