Calculation: Pivot = (high + low + close)/3 of the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; HRange = that same bar's high - low; R1 = Pivot + f1*HRange; R2 = Pivot + f2*HRange; S1 = Pivot - f1*HRange; S2 = Pivot - f2*HRange (f1, f2 are Fibonacci ratios, f1 < f2)
Calculation: BodyRatio = abs(close - open)/(high - low) (0 if high == low)
Long: evaluated on bar close; (close crosses above Pivot (close > Pivot and previous close <= Pivot) AND volume > k * SMA(volume, lookback) AND BodyRatio >= threshold) OR (close crosses above Pivot) OR (close > Pivot for exactly N consecutive bars including the current bar, a run broken by any close <= Pivot, true only on the Nth bar) AND the previous accepted entry signal was not Long (signals must alternate with Short) AND bars since the last accepted Long signal >= minimum rest period
Short: evaluated on bar close; (close crosses below Pivot (close < Pivot and previous close >= Pivot) AND volume > k * SMA(volume, lookback) AND BodyRatio >= threshold) OR (close crosses below Pivot) OR (close < Pivot for exactly N consecutive bars including the current bar, a run broken by any close >= Pivot, true only on the Nth bar) AND the previous accepted entry signal was not Short (signals must alternate with Long) AND bars since the last accepted Short signal >= minimum rest period
Exit: long, Short condition is true (reverse) OR high crosses above R2 (high > R2 and previous high <= R2), evaluated on bar close
Exit: short, Long condition is true (reverse) OR low crosses below S2 (low < S2 and previous low >= S2), evaluated on bar close
Exit: long (partial take-profit, once per position), high crosses above R1 (high > R1 and previous high <= R1), evaluated on bar close
Exit: short (partial take-profit, once per position), low crosses below S1 (low < S1 and previous low >= S1), evaluated on bar close
