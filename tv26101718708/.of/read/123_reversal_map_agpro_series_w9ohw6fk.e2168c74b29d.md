Calculation: Pivot chain = On each confirmed chart bar, confirm pivot highs and lows using the configured numbers of left and right bars; a pivot high is at least as high as every left-side high and higher than every right-side high, and a pivot low is at most as low as every left-side low and lower than every right-side low. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low. Append each pivot to the chain unless it has the same type as the latest pivot, in which case replace that pivot only if the new one is more extreme.
Calculation: Active structure = When the pivot chain changes, examine its latest three pivots P1, P2 and P3. A candidate is bullish for low-high-low and bearish for high-low-high. Accept it when P3's bar differs from the active structure's P3 bar, ATR is positive, abs(P2-P1) >= ATR times the minimum leg parameter, the directional distance from P1 to P3 >= ATR times the minimum hold parameter, and the retracement of the P1-P2 leg by P3 is between the minimum and maximum retracement parameters inclusive. Use ATR on the confirmation bar. An accepted candidate replaces the active structure and is initially unbroken and unretested. Store P1, P2, P3 and their bar indices; P2 is the neckline. On each confirmed bar, including the acceptance bar, expire an unbroken structure if bars since P3 exceed the maximum age, or invalidate it if its direction is bullish and low < P1 - current ATR times the invalidation buffer, or bearish and high > P1 + current ATR times the invalidation buffer. Expiration and invalidation take precedence over a break. A break stores the break bar and pocket boundaries equal to P2 plus and minus the break-bar ATR times the pocket-width parameter. Until retested, invalidate a broken bullish structure if close < P3 - current ATR times the invalidation buffer, or a broken bearish structure if close > P3 + current ATR times the invalidation buffer; invalidation takes precedence over a retest. Check for a retest on the break bar as well as subsequent bars. Once retested, retain the structure until another candidate replaces it.
Long: A bullish active structure is accepted AND it is not expired or invalidated on the acceptance bar.
Short: A bearish active structure is accepted AND it is not expired or invalidated on the acceptance bar.
Long: An active bullish structure is unbroken AND not expired or invalidated AND close > P2 + current ATR times the break-buffer parameter.
Short: An active bearish structure is unbroken AND not expired or invalidated AND close < P2 - current ATR times the break-buffer parameter.
Long: An active bullish structure is broken and unretested AND not invalidated AND bars since the break <= the retest-window parameter AND low <= the stored upper pocket boundary AND close >= the stored lower pocket boundary AND close > P2.
Short: An active bearish structure is broken and unretested AND not invalidated AND bars since the break <= the retest-window parameter AND high >= the stored lower pocket boundary AND close <= the stored upper pocket boundary AND close < P2.
