Calculation: LongScore = weighted sum of six boolean components, each worth its weight when true. Weights in descending order: (1) trend = EMA(fast) > EMA(slow) AND close > EMA(trend), with fast period < slow period < trend period; (2) momentum = RSI > midline (50) AND RSI > previous bar RSI; (3, equal weights) close > VWAP (hlc3 volume-weighted, reset each day); volume > SMA(volume) * multiplier (multiplier > 1); volatility = (ATR / close) >= minimum AND (high - low) <= ATR * exhaustion multiple; (4, lowest) higher-timeframe bias = close > EMA of closes on a timeframe higher than the chart, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming (aggregated up to the current chart bar).
Calculation: ShortScore = mirror of LongScore: EMA(fast) < EMA(slow) AND close < EMA(trend); RSI < 50 AND RSI < previous RSI; close < VWAP; same volume and volatility components; higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA. Weights are identical to LongScore.
Long: low <= EMA(fast) AND close > EMA(fast) AND close > open AND close > previous close AND close > previous high AND ADX >= minimum AND LongScore >= minimum score AND bars since the last signal (long or short) > cooldown AND (optional) bar time inside the allowed trading session
Short: high >= EMA(fast) AND close < EMA(fast) AND close < open AND close < previous close AND close < previous low AND ADX >= minimum AND ShortScore >= minimum score AND bars since the last signal (long or short) > cooldown AND (optional) bar time inside the allowed trading session
