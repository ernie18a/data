Calculation: Drawdown = (highest close over the risk lookback - close) / highest close over the risk lookback, expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Market volatility = maximum of the annualized rolling standard deviation of log(close / previous close) and the annualized current-bar Parkinson estimate, log(high / low) / (2 × sqrt(log(2))), both expressed as percentages.
Calculation: Risk allocation = when risk scaling is enabled, clamp to the allocation bounds the product of: (1) the minimum of the bounded target-volatility / max(market volatility, volatility floor) allocation and the bounded target-drawdown / drawdown allocation, with the latter set to full allocation when drawdown is at or below a drawdown threshold; and (2) a risk adjustment factor. Risk utilization is the maximum of reference equity fraction × market volatility / target volatility and reference equity fraction × drawdown / target drawdown. Above the upper utilization threshold, the adjustment factor is max(adjustment floor, exp(-decay coefficient × (utilization - 1))); below the lower utilization threshold, it is min(adjustment ceiling, 1 + growth coefficient × log(1 / utilization)); otherwise it is 1. When risk scaling is disabled, risk allocation is the bounded product of full allocation, clamp(target volatility / max(market volatility, alternative volatility floor), scalar floor, scalar ceiling), and (1 - drawdown penalty), where the penalty is min(1, (drawdown - target drawdown) / target drawdown) when drawdown exceeds target drawdown and 0 otherwise.
Long: Drawdown < lower drawdown display threshold.
Short: Drawdown >= higher drawdown display threshold.
Long: Risk allocation > upper risk allocation threshold.
Short: Risk allocation < lower risk allocation threshold.
