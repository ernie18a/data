Calculation: Previous day high = high of the last completed daily bar; daily timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Previous day low = low of the last completed daily bar; daily timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Daily open = open of the daily bar still forming; daily timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe
Long: close >= daily open OR (low < previous day low AND close > previous day low)
Short: close < daily open OR (high > previous day high AND close < previous day high)
