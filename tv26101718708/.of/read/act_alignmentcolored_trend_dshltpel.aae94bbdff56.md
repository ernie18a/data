Calculation: Ladder = four successively longer timeframes: State 1, State 2, Direction 1, and Direction 2. State 1 is at least the chart timeframe; the others are higher. Use each still-forming bar and its last completed bar.
Calculation: Up break at a ladder timeframe = forming high > last completed high AND forming close > last completed high.
Calculation: Down break at a ladder timeframe = forming low < last completed low AND forming close < last completed low.
Calculation: Inside at a ladder timeframe = forming high <= last completed high AND forming low >= last completed low.
Calculation: Failed up break at a ladder timeframe = forming high > last completed high AND forming close >= last completed low AND forming close <= last completed high AND (forming low >= last completed low OR forming close > forming open).
Calculation: Failed down break at a ladder timeframe = forming low < last completed low AND forming close >= last completed low AND forming close <= last completed high AND (forming high <= last completed high OR forming close <= forming open).
Long: Close > the still-forming open of any ladder timeframe.
Short: Close < the still-forming open of any ladder timeframe.
Long: Up break at any ladder timeframe.
Short: Down break at any ladder timeframe.
Long: Close > both Direction opens AND NOT (Direction 1 inside AND chart high < last completed Direction 1 high AND chart low > last completed Direction 1 low AND Direction 2 inside) AND NOT ((Direction 1 failed up break AND Direction 2 up break) OR (Direction 1 up break AND Direction 2 failed up break)).
Short: Close < both Direction opens AND NOT (Direction 1 inside AND chart high < last completed Direction 1 high AND chart low > last completed Direction 1 low AND Direction 2 inside) AND NOT ((Direction 1 failed down break AND Direction 2 down break) OR (Direction 1 down break AND Direction 2 failed down break)).
Long: Close is strictly between the two still-forming Direction opens AND State 2 up break AND chart high >= last completed State 2 high AND chart low > last completed State 2 low AND close >= last completed State 2 high.
Short: Close is strictly between the two still-forming Direction opens AND State 2 down break AND chart low <= last completed State 2 low AND chart high < last completed State 2 high AND close <= last completed State 2 low.
