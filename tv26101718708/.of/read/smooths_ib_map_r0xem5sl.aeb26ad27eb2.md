Calculation: Initial balance high and low = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars from the bar opening exactly at the configured initial balance start through the bar opening exactly at its end, inclusive, on weekdays, resetting at each start; the balance is formed on the end bar.
Long: Initial balance is formed AND bar opening time is after the initial balance end and at or before the data cutoff in America/New_York time AND a bar after formation and before the current bar has high > initial balance high AND low <= initial balance high AND close > initial balance high AND no prior long condition has occurred since the initial balance start.
Short: Initial balance is formed AND bar opening time is after the initial balance end and at or before the data cutoff in America/New_York time AND a bar after formation and before the current bar has low < initial balance low AND high >= initial balance low AND close < initial balance low AND no prior short condition has occurred since the initial balance start.
