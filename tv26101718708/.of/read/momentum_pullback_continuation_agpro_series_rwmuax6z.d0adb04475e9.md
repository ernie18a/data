Calculation: Bull context = fast EMA > base EMA > slow EMA, normalized base-EMA slope >= minimum slope, and smoothed rate of change >= minimum momentum; bear context = the inverse inequalities and thresholds. The base-EMA slope is its change over the momentum-slope lookback divided by ATR. Smoothed rate of change = EMA of close rate of change.
Calculation: Reset pocket = [base EMA − ATR × pocket width, fast EMA + ATR × 0.10] in bull context; [fast EMA − ATR × 0.10, base EMA + ATR × pocket width] in bear context. A pocket touch occurs when the bar's high-low range intersects the pocket. Recent reset = valid pocket and a touch within the reset review window.
Calculation: Invalidation rail = min(pocket bottom, slow EMA) − ATR × risk buffer in bull context; max(pocket top, slow EMA) + ATR × risk buffer in bear context.
Calculation: Continuation score (0–100) = 20% trend score + 22% momentum score + 24% reset-quality score + 18% recovery score + 8% volume score + 8% target-room score. Each component is normalized and clamped to 0–100. Trend score = 55% normalized directional base-EMA slope + 45% normalized fast-to-slow EMA spread, with normalization ranges −0.02 to 0.12 ATR and 0.15 to 2.50 ATR. Momentum score = 55% normalized directional smoothed rate of change + 45% normalized directional change in smoothed rate of change over the slope lookback, with ranges −0.15 to 1.25 and −0.14 to 0.22. Reset-quality score = 68% pullback-depth score + 32% pocket score; depth score peaks at ideal reset depth and falls to zero at the shallow and deep limits, while pocket score is 100 after a recent touch, 80 on a touch, 45 for a valid pocket, otherwise 0. Recovery score = 58% normalized directional close distance from fast EMA (−0.28 to 0.55 ATR) + 42% normalized directional close location in the bar (0.42 to 0.82). Volume score = normalized-low volume/current-to-average-volume ratio (0.60 to 1.45) × 65% + 35 when no qualified trigger; otherwise normalized-high ratio (0.75 to 1.35). Target-room score = normalized-high distance to target guide in ATR (0.25 to clean-room threshold). Normalized-high maps the weak-to-strong range linearly to 0–100; normalized-low maps good-to-bad inversely; both clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Pullback depth = (lookback highest high − current low) / ATR in bull context; (current high − lookback lowest low) / ATR in bear context. Target guide = lookback highest high + ATR × 0.35 in bull context; lookback lowest low − ATR × 0.35 in bear context. Target room = (target guide − close) / ATR in bull context; (close − target guide) / ATR in bear context.
Calculation: Qualified trigger = recent reset, close above fast EMA, close above open, and close location >= required location in bull context; recent reset, close below fast EMA, close below open, and close location <= 1 − required location in bear context. Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: READY = active momentum context, not invalidated, qualified trigger, continuation score >= READY threshold, and target room >= 0.30 ATR. WATCH = active context, not invalidated, recent reset, score >= WATCH threshold, and not READY. WEAK RESET = active context, recent reset, not invalidated, and (score < WATCH threshold or pullback depth > deep-reset limit). Momentum marker = active context, no recent reset, and score >= WATCH threshold − 5.
Long: Bull context and (recent reset OR (no recent reset AND continuation score >= WATCH threshold − 5)).
Short: Bear context and (recent reset OR (no recent reset AND continuation score >= WATCH threshold − 5)).
Exit: Long, recent reset and close < invalidation rail.
Exit: Short, recent reset and close > invalidation rail.
