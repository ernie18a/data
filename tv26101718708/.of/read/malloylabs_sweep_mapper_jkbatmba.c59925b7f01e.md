Calculation: Liquidity zones = Maintain an ordered list of zones. On each bar, process a confirmed pivot high and then a confirmed pivot low, each using equal numbers of left and right bars. For each pivot, find the oldest existing zone whose level differs from the pivot by less than a configured percentage of the pivot. If found, replace its level with the average of its level and the pivot, increment its touch count, and retain its direction and swept state. Otherwise append a zone at the pivot level with one touch and an unswept state; pivot highs create resistance and pivot lows create support. After each pivot, if the zone count exceeds a configured maximum, remove the oldest zone. After processing pivots, examine zones newest first; mark an unswept zone swept when its corresponding sweep condition and the optional volume filter pass. A swept zone remains swept even if a later pivot merges into it.
Calculation: Zone width offset = zone level multiplied by half a configured percentage.
Calculation: Sweep offset = the relevant zone level or EMA multiplied by a configured percentage.
Calculation: Volume filter = volume exceeds its simple moving average multiplied by a configured multiplier when volume confirmation is enabled; otherwise true.
Long: An unswept support zone exists AND low < zone level - zone width offset - sweep offset AND close > zone level AND close > open AND volume filter.
Short: An unswept resistance zone exists AND high > zone level + zone width offset + sweep offset AND close < zone level AND close < open AND volume filter.
Long: An unswept support zone exists AND low < zone level - zone width offset - sweep offset AND close > zone level AND close > open AND volume filter AND (EMA alignment disabled OR close > intermediate-length EMA of close) AND (multi-touch filter disabled OR zone touch count >= configured minimum).
Short: An unswept resistance zone exists AND high > zone level + zone width offset + sweep offset AND close < zone level AND close < open AND volume filter AND (EMA alignment disabled OR close < intermediate-length EMA of close) AND (multi-touch filter disabled OR zone touch count >= configured minimum).
Long: EMA sweep display enabled AND low < EMA of close - sweep offset AND close > EMA of close AND close > open AND volume filter, for any of the shorter-, intermediate-, or longer-length EMAs.
Short: EMA sweep display enabled AND high > EMA of close + sweep offset AND close < EMA of close AND close < open AND volume filter, for any of the shorter-, intermediate-, or longer-length EMAs.
Long: close > intermediate-length EMA of close.
Short: close <= intermediate-length EMA of close.
