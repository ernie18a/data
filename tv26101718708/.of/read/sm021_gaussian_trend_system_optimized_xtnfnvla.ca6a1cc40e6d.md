Calculation: Gaussian channel = Let the configurable price source be S, the pole count be N, and the sampling period be P. Set beta = (1 - cos(2*pi/P))/(1.414^(2/N) - 1), alpha = sqrt(beta^2 + 2*beta) - beta, and lag = integer((P - 1)/(2*N)). For each of S and true range, optionally replace input X with 2*X - X[lag] for reduced lag. Independently pass each input through N cascaded recursive stages initialized to zero: stage[i] = alpha*current_input + (1-alpha)*previous_stage[i], where current_input is the current value of the preceding stage, or X for the first stage; replace unavailable input with zero. Optionally average the Nth and first stages for fast response. The resulting filtered price is the channel center; upper and lower bands are the center plus and minus a configurable multiplier times the resulting filtered true range.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA of the stochastic oscillator applied to RSI of a configurable price source, using separate RSI, stochastic, and smoothing lookbacks.
Long: Gaussian channel center > previous center.
Short: Gaussian channel center < previous center.
Long: price source > previous price source AND price source > channel center AND price source < upper band.
Long: price source > previous price source AND price source >= upper band AND the preceding bar-color condition is false.
Long: price source <= previous price source AND price source > channel center AND the preceding bar-color conditions are false.
Short: price source < previous price source AND price source < channel center AND price source > lower band AND the preceding bar-color conditions are false.
Short: price source < previous price source AND price source <= lower band AND the preceding bar-color conditions are false.
Short: price source >= previous price source AND price source < channel center AND the preceding bar-color conditions are false.
Long: long trading enabled AND outside the enabled forced-close window AND channel center > previous center AND close > upper band AND (Stochastic RSI K > upper threshold OR Stochastic RSI K < lower threshold) AND (baseline filter disabled OR close > EMA or SMA baseline) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe shorter EMA > higher-timeframe longer EMA) AND timestamp within configured date range AND (session filter disabled OR timestamp within configured session) AND weekday enabled.
Short: short trading enabled AND outside the enabled forced-close window AND channel center < previous center AND close < lower band AND (Stochastic RSI K > upper threshold OR Stochastic RSI K < lower threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe shorter EMA < higher-timeframe longer EMA) AND timestamp within configured date range AND (session filter disabled OR timestamp within configured session) AND weekday enabled.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = EMAs of higher-than-chart-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical backtesting.
Exit: long OR short, forced-close option enabled AND timestamp within configured forced-close window.
Exit: long, outside the enabled forced-close window AND timestamp within configured date range AND close < upper band AND previous close >= previous upper band.
Exit: short, outside the enabled forced-close window AND timestamp within configured date range AND close > lower band AND previous close <= previous lower band.
Calculation: Risk levels = On each corresponding entry signal, set the stop and target from that signal bar's close and ATR: long stop = close - ATR times stop multiplier, long target = close + ATR times stop multiplier times reward-to-risk ratio; short stop = close + ATR times stop multiplier, short target = close - ATR times stop multiplier times reward-to-risk ratio. Retain the levels for the associated position until replaced by a subsequent corresponding entry signal.
Exit: long, ATR stop enabled AND low <= long stop OR ATR target enabled AND high >= long target.
Exit: short, ATR stop enabled AND high >= short stop OR ATR target enabled AND low <= short target.
