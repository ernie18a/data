Calculation: ScaledOBV = if (highest OBV - lowest OBV over a fixed lookback) is 0 then 0, else (OBV - lowest OBV over lookback) / (highest OBV - lowest OBV over lookback) * highest volume over the same lookback; OBV is the standard cumulative on-balance volume.
Calculation: OBV_MA = moving average of ScaledOBV (lookback is separate from the ribbon lookbacks).
Calculation: FastRibbon, SlowRibbon = exponential moving averages of ScaledOBV; FastRibbon lookback is shorter than SlowRibbon lookback.
Calculation: OBV pivot low/high = ScaledOBV is the lowest/highest among N bars on each side of a bar; it is confirmed N bars later, so the confirmation bar is the current bar and the pivot bar is N bars back. Price pivot low/high uses the same N on low/high; if no price pivot exists at that pivot bar, use that bar's low/high.
Long: (ScaledOBV crosses above OBV_MA on the current bar) OR (FastRibbon > SlowRibbon) OR (bullish divergence: an OBV pivot low is confirmed on the current bar, a previous confirmed OBV pivot low exists, current OBV pivot low value > previous OBV pivot low value AND the price low at the current pivot bar < the price low at the previous pivot bar AND the bars between the two confirmation bars <= a fixed window)
Short: (ScaledOBV crosses below OBV_MA on the current bar) OR (FastRibbon < SlowRibbon) OR (bearish divergence: an OBV pivot high is confirmed on the current bar, a previous confirmed OBV pivot high exists, current OBV pivot high value < previous OBV pivot high value AND the price high at the current pivot bar > the price high at the previous pivot bar AND the bars between the two confirmation bars <= a fixed window)
