Calculation: RSI pivot = a primary close-based RSI low or high with the specified left and right lookbacks, evaluated when the right lookback has completed; the pivot price is the low or high of the pivot bar.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low AND its price low is lower than the preceding pivot's price low AND the number of bars between the two pivot confirmations is within the specified minimum and maximum lookback limits.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low AND its price low is higher than the preceding pivot's price low AND the number of bars between the two pivot confirmations is within the specified minimum and maximum lookback limits.
