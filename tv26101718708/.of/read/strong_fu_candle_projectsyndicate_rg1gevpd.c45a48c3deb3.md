Calculation: Candle measures = range = high − low; body = abs(close − open); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low. Previous-bar measures use the preceding bar on the calculation timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = previous low − low >= minimum grab AND ((close > previous high AND close > previous open AND close − previous high >= minimum engulf depth) OR (attempted patterns enabled AND close > max(previous open, previous close) AND close <= previous high AND close − max(previous open, previous close) >= minimum engulf depth)).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = high − previous high >= minimum grab AND ((close < previous low AND close < previous open AND previous low − close >= minimum engulf depth) OR (attempted patterns enabled AND close < min(previous open, previous close) AND close >= previous low AND min(previous open, previous close) − close >= minimum engulf depth)). If both directions qualify, select bullish. Distinguish full from attempted patterns when tracking changes in pattern type.
Calculation: Pattern filters = range >= minimum range AND body >= minimum body AND body <= maximum body AND (rejection filter disabled OR opposite wick <= body) AND (EMA filter disabled OR bullish close > EMA of close OR bearish close < EMA of close) AND (fractal filter disabled OR bullish low < lowest low over the preceding lookback bars OR bearish high > highest high over the preceding lookback bars) AND (body-inside-wick filter disabled OR body-inside-wick detection enabled AND previous range > 0 AND previous body <= configurable fraction of previous range AND previous upper wick > 0 AND previous lower wick > 0). For bullish patterns the opposite wick is the upper wick; for bearish patterns it is the lower wick.
Calculation: Quality score = sum of five graded components, capped at a configurable maximum. Grade body/range against four descending thresholds, assigning five descending awards; grade opposite wick/body and grab wick/body each against three ascending thresholds, assigning four descending awards; grade close position against three descending thresholds, assigning four descending awards. Close position is (close − low)/range for bullish patterns and (high − close)/range for bearish patterns. The grab wick is the lower wick for bullish patterns and the upper wick for bearish patterns. Add one award when range/ATR lies within its configured inclusive bounds and another when volume/SMA(volume) >= its configured threshold. Use zero for body/range and close position when range is zero, a configurable fallback above the largest grading threshold for wick/body when body is zero, zero for range/ATR when ATR is not positive, and a configurable neutral fallback for volume/SMA(volume) when average volume is not positive.
Calculation: Qualified pattern = pattern filters pass AND quality score >= minimum score AND quality score >= the cutoff for the selected minimum tier. Evaluate on the completed chart bar; for an enabled higher timeframe, evaluate on its last completed bar and require its signed full-or-attempted pattern type to differ from the preceding higher-timeframe result.
Calculation: Setup entry = selected setup-bar value: midpoint of open and close, midpoint of high and low, or close. Long stop = setup-bar low − configurable buffer; short stop = setup-bar high + configurable buffer. Risk = abs(setup entry − stop). Profit targets = setup entry plus risk multiplied by a configurable target multiple for long positions, or minus that product for short positions. Each target has its own multiple.
Long: qualified bullish pattern OR (fade mode selected AND qualified bearish pattern).
Short: qualified bearish pattern OR (fade mode selected AND qualified bullish pattern).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= any profit target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= any profit target.
