Calculation: FU quality score = body/range score (0.5, 1, 2, 2.5, or 3 for increasing body/range bands at 0.4, 0.5, 0.6, and 0.7) + opposite-wick/body score (0, 1, 1.5, or 2 for decreasing ratios at 1, 0.5, and 0.2) + sweep-wick/body score (0, 1, 1.5, or 2 for decreasing ratios at 1, 0.6, and 0.4) + close-position score (0, 1, 1.5, or 2 for increasing positions at 0.75, 0.85, and 0.9) + 0.5 if candle range/ATR is between 0.8 and 2, and +0.5 if volume is at least its moving average; cap at 10. Tier C/B/A/S begins at scores 0/5/6.5/8.
Calculation: Entry price = setup candle body midpoint, range midpoint, or close, according to the selected entry mode.
Calculation: Stop price = setup candle low minus buffer for long positions, or setup candle high plus buffer for short positions.
Calculation: Risk = absolute difference between entry price and stop price; target prices are entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk multiplied by the selected target R multiple.
Long: Confirmed setup candle sweeps below the prior candle low by at least the grab threshold and either closes above the prior high and prior open by at least the engulf threshold, or (if attempted setups are enabled) closes above the prior candle body top but at or below its high by at least the engulf threshold; setup range and body meet their minimums and body does not exceed its maximum; quality score meets the minimum score and tier; if enabled, opposite wick is no larger than body, close is above EMA, recent-low sweep filter is met, and the prior candle meets the body-inside-wick condition; and direction is Reversal with a bullish setup or Fade with a bearish setup.
Short: Confirmed setup candle sweeps above the prior candle high by at least the grab threshold and either closes below the prior low and prior open by at least the engulf threshold, or (if attempted setups are enabled) closes below the prior candle body bottom but at or above its low by at least the engulf threshold; setup range and body meet their minimums and body does not exceed its maximum; quality score meets the minimum score and tier; if enabled, opposite wick is no larger than body, close is below EMA, recent-high sweep filter is met, and the prior candle meets the body-inside-wick condition; and direction is Reversal with a bearish setup or Fade with a bullish setup.
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= entry price + risk × selected TP1, TP2, or TP3 multiple.
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= entry price - risk × selected TP1, TP2, or TP3 multiple.
