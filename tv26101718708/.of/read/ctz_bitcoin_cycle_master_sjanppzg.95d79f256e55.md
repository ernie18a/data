Calculation: StochRSI %K = SMA of the stochastic of RSI(close), using separate RSI, stochastic, and smoothing lookbacks; %D = SMA of %K with a separate smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Bullish StochRSI event = %K crosses above %D AND the lowest %K over a lookback including the current bar is below an oversold threshold. Bearish StochRSI event = %K crosses below %D AND the highest %K over a lookback including the current bar is above an overbought threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed cycle low = a symmetric price pivot low is detected AND its pivot bar is sufficiently far from the previously accepted pivot low OR its low is below that pivot's low; from detection onward, retain whether close has exceeded EMA(close) and whether a bullish StochRSI event has occurred, resetting both when another pivot is accepted; confirmation occurs once both have occurred. Calculate independent short-lookback and longer-lookback versions, with a longer minimum pivot spacing for the longer-lookback version.
Calculation: Confirmed cycle high = a symmetric price pivot high is detected AND its pivot bar is sufficiently far from the previously accepted pivot high OR its high is above that pivot's high; from detection onward, retain whether close has fallen below EMA(close) and whether a bearish StochRSI event has occurred, resetting both when another pivot is accepted; confirmation occurs once both have occurred. Calculate independent short-lookback and longer-lookback versions, with a longer minimum pivot spacing for the longer-lookback version.
Calculation: Long-cycle low = after a configurable anchor timestamp and settlement delay, select the confirmed longer-lookback cycle low nearest the anchor, optionally replacing it with the deepest confirmed longer-lookback cycle low within a symmetric time window around the anchor. After the selected low's timestamp plus a target duration and settlement delay, select the confirmed longer-lookback cycle low after the previous selected low nearest the target, optionally replacing it with the deepest confirmed longer-lookback cycle low within a symmetric time window around the selected candidate if that replacement is after the previous low. Set the target duration to the rounded average time between selected lows. During the remaining time through the target plus the window radius, replace the selected low when a deeper confirmed longer-lookback cycle low within that window is found. This engine makes no further initial selections after that window.
Long: Close > EMA(close).
Short: Close <= EMA(close).
Long: A short-lookback OR longer-lookback cycle low confirms.
Short: A short-lookback OR longer-lookback cycle high confirms.
Long: A new calendar year begins OR low < the running lowest low of the current calendar year.
Long: A calendar year ends AND its lowest low is more than a minimum time after the previously finalized annual low OR its lowest low is lower than the previously finalized annual low.
Long: The current annual low has not yet been confirmed AND the current bar is after that low AND close > EMA(close) AND a bullish StochRSI event occurs.
Long: A long-cycle low is selected OR replaced.
Long: After the initial long-cycle low is selected and before the next selection, time is between the next target minus its window radius and the target plus the settlement delay AND the window has just opened OR low is below the lowest low recorded since that window opened.
Long: Bars since the last confirmed longer-lookback cycle low's pivot >= a lower fraction of the expected cycle length AND the position of the highest high recorded since confirmation, measured in bars from that pivot and divided by the expected cycle length, >= an upper fraction.
Short: Bars since the last confirmed longer-lookback cycle low's pivot >= a lower fraction of the expected cycle length AND the position of the highest high recorded since confirmation, measured in bars from that pivot and divided by the expected cycle length, <= the lower fraction.
Short: Time is between the start and end fractions of a projected high window measured from the most recently accepted longer-lookback cycle-low pivot time using its expected cycle duration OR from the current calendar year's running-low time using a calendar-year duration OR from the selected long-cycle-low time using its current target duration.
