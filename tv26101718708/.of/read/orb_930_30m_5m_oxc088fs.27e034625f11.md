Calculation: Opening zone = the high and low of the completed session-opening window, accumulated from chart bars. Use the shorter window when the chart timeframe is lower than that window; use the longer window when the chart timeframe is at least the shorter window and no higher than the longer window. Clear the zone at the session-timezone calendar-day rollover and replace it when its window completes.
Calculation: Overnight zone = the high and low of the completed earlier overnight window, accumulated from chart bars; clear it at the session-timezone calendar-day rollover and replace it when its window completes.
Calculation: Engulfing run = consecutive chart bars classified as up when close >= open and down otherwise. The base is the immediately preceding opposite-classified bar. An up run engulfs its base when the maximum open or close in the run >= the base high; a down run engulfs its base when the minimum open or close in the run <= the base low. The base overlaps a zone when base high >= zone low AND base low <= zone high.
Long: Opening zone exists AND the chart is the designated faster chart using the shorter opening window OR the designated slower chart using the longer opening window; AND no opening-zone breakout has occurred since that zone formed AND close > opening-zone high.
Short: Opening zone exists AND the chart is the designated faster chart using the shorter opening window OR the designated slower chart using the longer opening window; AND no opening-zone breakout has occurred since that zone formed AND close < opening-zone low.
Long: Chart is the designated slower chart AND overnight zone exists AND no overnight-zone breakout has occurred since that zone formed AND close > overnight-zone high.
Short: Chart is the designated slower chart AND overnight zone exists AND no overnight-zone breakout has occurred since that zone formed AND close < overnight-zone low.
Long: Opening zone exists AND current bar opens at or after the session opening time AND the base overlaps the opening zone AND an up run first engulfs that base under these conditions AND run length <= maximum permitted length AND, on either designated breakout chart, no opening-zone breakout occurred before the current bar.
Short: Opening zone exists AND current bar opens at or after the session opening time AND the base overlaps the opening zone AND a down run first engulfs that base under these conditions AND run length <= maximum permitted length AND, on either designated breakout chart, no opening-zone breakout occurred before the current bar.
Long: Chart is the designated faster chart AND the current shorter opening zone's first breakout was upward AND no retest entry has been taken for that zone AND the base overlaps that zone AND an up run engulfs its base; entry price = base high.
Short: Chart is the designated faster chart AND the current shorter opening zone's first breakout was downward AND no retest entry has been taken for that zone AND the base overlaps that zone AND a down run engulfs its base; entry price = base low.
Exit: long, bar opens after the retest entry bar AND (low <= entry price minus stop distance OR high >= entry price plus target distance).
Exit: short, bar opens after the retest entry bar AND (high >= entry price plus stop distance OR low <= entry price minus target distance).
