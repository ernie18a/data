Calculation: Previous regular-session close = the close of the last regular-session bar before the current calendar day, carried forward from the day boundary.
Calculation: Premarket high = the highest high since the start of the current premarket session, carried forward after that session ends.
Calculation: Opening-range high = the highest high of the regular-session bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range window, frozen after that window ends.
Calculation: Gap percentage = 100 × (close − previous regular-session close) / previous regular-session close; use 0 if the previous regular-session close is not positive.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), including the current bar; use 0 if the volume SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Retest state = reset at each calendar-day boundary; each primary long signal arms it, clears any recorded touch and prior retest, and counts as the first bar after the breakout. From the next bar onward, record a touch when low ≤ opening-range high × (1 + buffer percentage / 100) AND close ≥ opening-range high × (1 − buffer percentage / 100). A touch remains recorded until a retest signal or a new primary long signal. Allow only one retest signal per primary long signal.
Long: Regular session AND opening-range window complete AND close > opening-range high AND previous close ≤ previous bar's opening-range high AND gap percentage ≥ minimum gap percentage AND close > previous regular-session close AND (premarket filter disabled OR premarket high unavailable OR close > premarket high) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (EMA filter disabled OR shorter-period EMA(close) > longer-period EMA(close)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > RSI threshold) AND (early-session filter disabled OR elapsed regular-session time ≤ early-session limit).
Long: Retest state armed AND at least one bar has elapsed since the primary long signal AND a touch is recorded AND no retest has fired since that primary signal AND close > opening-range high AND close > open AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (EMA filter disabled OR shorter-period EMA(close) > longer-period EMA(close)) AND (early-session filter disabled OR regular session AND elapsed regular-session time ≥ opening-range duration AND elapsed regular-session time ≤ early-session limit).
