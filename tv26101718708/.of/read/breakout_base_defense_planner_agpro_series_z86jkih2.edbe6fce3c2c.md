Calculation: Base = the highest high and lowest low over a lookback of completed bars preceding the current bar; base height is their difference. Relative volume is current volume divided by its moving average, or 1 when that average is not positive. Body ratio is the absolute difference between close and open divided by the bar's high-low range.
Calculation: Active base = on each qualifying bullish or bearish breakout, replace any existing base with the current base and record its direction, breakout bar and breakout-bar ATR. The defense edge is the locked high for a bullish base and the locked low for a bearish base. The defense zone extends an ATR-scaled distance above and below the edge; the failure shelf is an additional ATR-scaled distance beyond the zone, below it for a bullish base and above it for a bearish base. Use the breakout-bar ATR to lock these levels. Clear the active base after a close beyond its failure shelf or when its age exceeds the maximum age; evaluate that bar's decisions before clearing it.
Calculation: Room reference = at the breakout, project a multiple of locked base height beyond the defense edge in the breakout direction. For a bullish base take the higher of this projection and the highest high over a room lookback of bars preceding the breakout; for a bearish base take the lower of the projection and the corresponding lowest low. Keep the result fixed for the active base.
Calculation: Defense hold = for a bullish active base, low is at or below the zone top AND close is above the defense edge AND close is above open; for a bearish active base, high is at or above the zone bottom AND close is below the defense edge AND close is below open.
Calculation: Defense score = round and clamp to 0–100 the sum of: 10; clamp(16 − 6 × abs(base height / current ATR − 1.6), 0, 16); clamp(12 × abs(close − defense edge) / current ATR, 0, 16); clamp(10 + 10 × directional distance, 0, 20); 16 if the current bar overlaps the defense zone, otherwise clamp(16 − 5 × abs(directional distance), 0, 16); clamp(12 × (relative volume − 0.5), 0, 12); and clamp(3.5 × abs(room reference − close) / current ATR, 0, 10). Directional distance is (close − edge) / current ATR for a bullish base and (edge − close) / current ATR for a bearish base.
Long: Base height is between the minimum and maximum ATR-scaled limits inclusive AND close is above the base high plus an ATR-scaled breakout buffer AND previous close is at or below that same current-bar threshold AND body ratio is at or above its minimum AND relative volume is at or above its minimum.
Short: Base height is between the minimum and maximum ATR-scaled limits inclusive AND close is below the base low minus an ATR-scaled breakout buffer AND previous close is at or above that same current-bar threshold AND body ratio is at or above its minimum AND relative volume is at or above its minimum.
Long: The active base is bullish AND defense hold is true AND defense hold was false on the previous bar.
Short: The active base is bearish AND defense hold is true AND defense hold was false on the previous bar.
Long: The active base is bullish AND its age is at or below the maximum age AND close is at or above its failure shelf AND defense score is at or above the ready threshold.
Short: The active base is bearish AND its age is at or below the maximum age AND close is at or below its failure shelf AND defense score is at or above the ready threshold.
Exit: long, the active base is bullish AND close is below its failure shelf.
Exit: short, the active base is bearish AND close is above its failure shelf.
