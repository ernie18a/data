Calculation: Active pattern channel = two converging boundary lines fitted to three alternating zigzag pivots each (pivot highs for the upper line, pivot lows for the lower); each middle pivot deviates from its line by less than an adaptive tolerance. The lines’ bar ranges overlap by at least half of the longer range, their shorter range is at least half the longer range, and the chart close is between the lines when the pattern forms. Zigzag pivots require the same left and right lookback; use only confirmed pivots. The adaptive tolerance is a base tolerance scaled by 1 + 0.5 × ATR volatility z-score, where ATR uses a shorter lookback than its moving-average and standard-deviation baseline; clamp the z-score to [−1, 2].
Calculation: Projected channel boundaries = the upper and lower pattern lines extended to the current chart bar; channel width = upper boundary − lower boundary.
Calculation: Strong bullish breakout = body-length z-score exceeds its threshold AND the lower quartile of the candle body is above the upper channel boundary. Strong bearish breakout = body-length z-score exceeds its threshold AND the upper quartile of the candle body is below the lower channel boundary. Body-length z-score uses a rolling mean and standard deviation of candle body length over the same lookback.
Calculation: Breakout targets and stop = breakout close ± channel width scaled by a duration factor for targets 1 and 2; target 3 uses breakout close ± (1.618 × channel width × duration factor + ATR volatility adjustment); stop uses breakout close on the opposite side by channel width times the stop multiplier. The duration factor is current pattern duration divided by the average duration of previously completed patterns, clamped to [0.5, 2]. The ATR volatility adjustment is absolute clamped ATR volatility z-score times current ATR. Use the same shorter-term ATR and longer-term baseline as for adaptive tolerance.
Long: confirmed chart bar closes above the projected upper channel boundary AND (strong bullish breakout OR this is the second consecutive confirmed bar closing outside the channel).
Short: confirmed chart bar closes below the projected lower channel boundary AND (strong bearish breakout OR this is the second consecutive confirmed bar closing outside the channel).
Exit: long, low ≤ stop AND previous low > stop OR high ≥ target 3 AND previous high < target 3
Exit: short, high ≥ stop AND previous high < stop OR low ≤ target 3 AND previous low > target 3
