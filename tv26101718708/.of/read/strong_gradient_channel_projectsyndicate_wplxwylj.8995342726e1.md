Calculation: Buy share = for each bar, blend its close-location fraction within its high-low range with 1 for an up body, 0 for a down body, or 0.5 for a doji; use the configured classification mode and blend weight. Sum lower-timeframe volume weighted by buy share and sell share over the chart bar; use confirmed bars of a lower timeframe.
Calculation: Candle profile shape = divide the chart bar’s price range into 14 equal rows, allocate each confirmed lower-timeframe bar’s volume equally among the rows its range overlaps, and locate the maximum-volume row. Shape is bimodal if the span between the first and last rows with at least 70% of maximum volume is at least 3 rows and at least 2 intervening rows have less than 35% of maximum volume; otherwise shape is P if the maximum row center is at or above 62% of the range, b if at or below 38%, and D otherwise.
Calculation: Fade conviction score = clamp to 0–10 the sum of 1.8 × wick score, 2 × effort score, 2 × trap score, 1.6 × shape score, and 1.6 × divergence score; multiply by 0.55 for bimodal shape. Effort score = clamp((bar volume / 20-bar average volume − 1) / 1.5, 0, 1) × clamp(1 − body size / bar range, 0, 1). For a short candidate, wick score = clamp(upper wick / bar range / 0.50, 0, 1), trap score = clamp(abs(bar delta) / bar volume / 0.50, 0, 1) only if bar delta is positive and close is below the bar midpoint, shape score = clamp((profile POC location − 0.50) / 0.35, 0, 1) only for P shape, and divergence score = 1 if cumulative volume delta is below its prior lookback high, otherwise 0. For a long candidate, use the lower wick; trap score applies only if bar delta is negative and close is above the midpoint; shape score = clamp((0.50 − profile POC location) / 0.35, 0, 1) only for b shape; divergence score = 1 if cumulative volume delta is above its prior lookback low, otherwise 0. Other inapplicable component scores are 0.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with confirmed lower-timeframe data and positive bar range and volume, the low is below the prior local-lookback low AND the low pierces the lower regression wall (linear regression of the configured price source over the channel lookback minus the lower multiplier times its standard deviation) AND lower wick is at least upper wick AND shape is not bimodal AND fade conviction score meets the configured minimum AND the signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with confirmed lower-timeframe data and positive bar range and volume, the high is above the prior local-lookback high AND the high pierces the upper regression wall (linear regression of the configured price source over the channel lookback plus the upper multiplier times its standard deviation) AND upper wick is at least lower wick AND shape is not bimodal AND fade conviction score meets the configured minimum AND the signal cooldown has elapsed.
