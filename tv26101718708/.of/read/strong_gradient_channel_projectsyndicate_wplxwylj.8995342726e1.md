Calculation: Regression channel = Fit a least-squares line to the selected price source over the channel lookback, including the current bar. At the current bar, its upper and lower walls are the line endpoint plus and minus their respective widths. Choose widths as separate multipliers of the population standard deviation of regression residuals, the largest high-minus-line and line-minus-low deviations over the window, or separate multipliers of three times ATR.
Calculation: Profile buy ratio = Divide the range from the minimum low-minus-regression-line deviation to the maximum high-minus-regression-line deviation over the channel window into equal rows. Distribute each bar's volume uniformly over its high-low range among overlapping rows; ignore zero-range bars. Classify each bar's distributed volume using its whole-bar buy fraction: either clamped close location (close minus low divided by high minus low, with the range floored at the minimum price increment), body direction (1 for close above open, 0 for close below open, otherwise 0.5), or a weighted blend of the two. The ratio is total allocated buy volume divided by total allocated volume; use 0 if the denominator is 0.
Calculation: Fade candidate = A chart bar with at least one completed lower-timeframe bar, high greater than low, and positive reconstructed volume. Reconstructed volume is the sum of the lower-timeframe volumes when intrabar classification is enabled; otherwise, or when no lower-timeframe bars are available, it is chart-bar volume. A short candidate has high above the highest high of the preceding extreme lookback, high at or above the current linear-regression endpoint plus an upper multiplier of the population standard deviation of the selected source, and upper wick at least as long as lower wick. A long candidate has low below the lowest low of the preceding extreme lookback, low at or below the current linear-regression endpoint minus a lower multiplier of the population standard deviation of the selected source, and lower wick at least as long as upper wick. The regression and standard deviation use the same channel lookback; if both candidates occur, select the short candidate.
Long: Fade candidate is long.
Short: Fade candidate is short.
Long: Regression channel and profile are available AND close is below the lower channel wall AND profile buy ratio is at least 0.5.
Short: Regression channel and profile are available AND close is above the upper channel wall AND profile buy ratio is at most 0.5.
Long: Regression channel and profile are available AND neither reversal-watch condition holds AND regression slope is greater than 0.
Short: Regression channel and profile are available AND neither reversal-watch condition holds AND regression slope is at most 0.
