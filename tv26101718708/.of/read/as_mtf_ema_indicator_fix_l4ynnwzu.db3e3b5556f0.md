Long: (close crosses above the EMA of close computed on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar, i.e. previous close <= previous EMA AND current close > current EMA) OR (low > high of 2 bars earlier) OR (close > chart-timeframe EMA of close, whose lookback is shorter than that of the higher-timeframe EMA)
Short: (close crosses below the EMA of close computed on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar, i.e. previous close >= previous EMA AND current close < current EMA) OR (high < low of 2 bars earlier) OR (close <= chart-timeframe EMA of close, whose lookback is shorter than that of the higher-timeframe EMA)
