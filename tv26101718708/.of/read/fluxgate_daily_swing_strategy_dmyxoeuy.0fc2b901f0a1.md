Calculation: Side-specific score and thresholds = Calculate independently for long and short using separately parameterized base lengths and aggression. Derive trend, gate, normalization, adaptation, channel, fractal, short-volatility and long-volatility lookbacks from the base length and aggression; higher aggression shortens them. The short-volatility lookback is shorter than the long-volatility lookback. Let return = ln(close / previous close), and let softclip(x, limit) = limit × x / (limit + abs(x)). Direction = softclip((EMA of (open + high + low + close) / 4 minus its previous value) / standard deviation of returns, direction limit). Persistence = correlation over the gate lookback between the current and previous values of a series equal to 1 when close > previous close and -1 otherwise; replace unavailable correlation with 0. Curvature = softclip((linear-regression fitted close minus twice its previous value plus its value two bars ago) / standard deviation of close, curvature limit), using the trend lookback. Orthogonalize these three drivers in order: retain direction; subtract its rolling covariance projection from persistence; subtract the rolling covariance projections onto direction and the residual persistence from curvature. Divide each resulting series by its rolling standard deviation, using the normalization lookback. Blend the three normalized series with aggression-dependent weights, divided by their sum. Efficiency = absolute net close change over the gate lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or 0 if the denominator is 0. Entropy = negative binary entropy of the gate-lookback frequency of close > previous close, divided by ln(2), clamped to [0, 1]. Fractal cohesion = 2 minus clamp(1 + ln(path length / close range) / ln(fractal lookback), 1, 2), where path length is the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over the fractal lookback and close range is the highest minus lowest close over that lookback; use cohesion 0 when the range is 0. Gate = (efficiency × (1 - entropy) × fractal cohesion) raised to an aggression-dependent exponent. Channel position = clamp((close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low), 0, 1) over the channel lookback; signed phase = 2 × channel position - 1; phase assist = signed phase × abs(signed phase) × an aggression-dependent weight. Shock gate = 1 / (1 + max(0, short-lookback return standard deviation / long-lookback return standard deviation - 1)). Deviation normalization = SMA over the normalization lookback of abs(blended driver - its contemporaneous normalization-lookback SMA). Raw score = (blended driver + phase assist) × gate × shock gate × aggression-dependent gain / deviation normalization. Score = EMA of softclip(raw score, score limit), with an aggression-dependent smoothing length. Score deviation and score mean are the standard deviation and SMA of score over the adaptation lookback. Skew = adaptation-lookback SMA of ((score - score mean) / score deviation) cubed. Long threshold = aggression-dependent threshold multiplier × score deviation × clamp(1 - skew sensitivity × max(skew, 0), minimum tilt, maximum tilt). Short threshold = negative threshold multiplier × score deviation × clamp(1 - skew sensitivity × max(-skew, 0), minimum tilt, maximum tilt). Floor standard deviations and denominators at a small positive constant; use 0 for division by zero where applicable.
Calculation: Long signal = long-side score > its long threshold AND previous long-side score <= its previous long threshold.
Calculation: Short signal = short-side score < its short threshold AND previous short-side score >= its previous short threshold.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR on the selected anchor timeframe multiplied by the stop multiplier. For a higher anchor timeframe, historical bars use the last completed anchor bar as mapped with lookahead off; a still-forming anchor bar can update in real time. For a lower anchor timeframe, use the last available intrabar. The initial stop and target use the risk distance observed when the filled position is first detected; the breakeven trigger uses the current risk distance.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus initial risk distance; if breakeven is enabled and any completed bar since entry has (close - entry price) / current risk distance >= breakeven trigger, raise the stop to at least entry price for subsequent bars. Short stop = entry price plus initial risk distance; if breakeven is enabled and any completed bar since entry has (entry price - close) / current risk distance >= breakeven trigger, lower the stop to at most entry price for subsequent bars.
Long: Long entries enabled AND (long signal OR previous bar's long signal).
Short: Short entries enabled AND short signal.
Exit: long, short signal OR long signal.
Exit: short, long signal.
Exit: long, risk engine enabled AND (low <= long stop OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + initial risk distance × profit multiple)).
Exit: short, risk engine enabled AND (high >= short stop OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price - initial risk distance × profit multiple)).
