Calculation: Detrended cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero otherwise, minus its EMA.
Calculation: Divergence strength = 100 × absolute difference between detrended cumulative volume delta at the current and previous same-type pivots / the range of detrended cumulative volume delta over a lookback ending on the confirmation bar; use 1 as the denominator if the range is zero, and clamp the result to 0–100.
Long: A symmetric pivot low is confirmed on the current closed bar AND its low is below the previous confirmed pivot low AND detrended cumulative volume delta at the current pivot is above its value at the previous pivot low AND divergence strength is at least the minimum strength.
Short: A symmetric pivot high is confirmed on the current closed bar AND its high is above the previous confirmed pivot high AND detrended cumulative volume delta at the current pivot is below its value at the previous pivot high AND divergence strength is at least the minimum strength.
