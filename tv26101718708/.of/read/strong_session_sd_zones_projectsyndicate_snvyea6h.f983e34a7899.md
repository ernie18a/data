Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high that is the highest in a symmetric window of the configured swing length on the chart timeframe, confirmed after that many later bars; confirmed swing low = the corresponding lowest low.
Calculation: Supply zone = a band centered on the confirmed swing high, with thickness equal to the current chart-timeframe ATR times the configured zone-height factor. Demand zone = the corresponding band centered on the confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Zone invalidation = supply is broken when high (wick mode) or close (close mode) exceeds its upper boundary; demand is broken when low (wick mode) or close (close mode) falls below its lower boundary.
Long: A confirmed swing low forms a demand zone AND its candle range is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple AND enough chart bars have elapsed since the last accepted demand zone; OR close is within the configured percentage of price from the midpoint of any unbroken demand zone.
Short: A confirmed swing high forms a supply zone AND its candle range is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple AND enough chart bars have elapsed since the last accepted supply zone; OR close is within the configured percentage of price from the midpoint of any unbroken supply zone.
