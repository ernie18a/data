Calculation: Tracked pivot levels = Confirm pivot highs and lows after an equal number of bars on each side, using a lookback selected by chart timeframe. Maintain separate chronological lists for highs and lows. When a new pivot of one side is confirmed, remove that side's levels whose age since confirmation exceeds the maximum age, append the new level with its confirmation time, and retain only the most recent configured number of levels. Optionally remove each level when it qualifies for an SFP.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high equals low.
Calculation: Retest levels = For each level qualifying for an SFP, optionally store the level, its bullish or bearish side, and the SFP bar and time. On each later bar, remove levels older than the maximum age and remove each level after its first qualifying retest.
Long: On a closed bar, at least one tracked pivot low has low < level AND level - low >= sweep threshold × ATR AND close > level AND close position >= close-position threshold AND, if enabled, volume >= volume SMA AND, if enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND, if enabled, the bar's UTC hour is within either configured session window, inclusive of its start and exclusive of its end.
Short: On a closed bar, at least one tracked pivot high has high > level AND high - level >= sweep threshold × ATR AND close < level AND close position <= 1 - close-position threshold AND, if enabled, volume >= volume SMA AND, if enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND, if enabled, the bar's UTC hour is within either configured session window, inclusive of its start and exclusive of its end.
Long: On a closed bar after the SFP bar, a stored bullish retest level has level - retest tolerance × ATR <= low <= level + retest tolerance × ATR AND close > level.
Short: On a closed bar after the SFP bar, a stored bearish retest level has level - retest tolerance × ATR <= high <= level + retest tolerance × ATR AND close < level.
