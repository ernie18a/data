Calculation: Trend = 100 × (MA(Heikin-Ashi close) − MA(Heikin-Ashi open)) / max(ε, MA(Heikin-Ashi high) − MA(Heikin-Ashi low)); use the same configurable moving-average method and lookback for all four series.
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = cumulative volume-weighted average of the configured price source, plus or minus a configurable multiple of its cumulative volume-weighted population standard deviation; reset at the configured day boundary or session start. Use volume 1 when volume is missing or zero.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot low in price is lower than the previous pivot low while RSI at that pivot is higher; when the divergence-zone filter is enabled, at least one of those pivot RSI values must be oversold. Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot high in price is higher than the previous pivot high while RSI at that pivot is lower; when that filter is enabled, at least one pivot RSI value must be overbought.
Long: Trend crosses above zero (current > 0 AND previous <= 0) AND the lower outer VWAP deviation band was touched within the configured lookback, no earlier than the configured minimum bars after reset (touch mode is configured as wick reaches band, close beyond band, or wick reaches band and close returns inside) AND (RSI-extreme filter disabled OR RSI was oversold within its configured lookback) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish RSI divergence occurred within its configured lookback) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR trend calculated from the higher-timeframe bar, including a bar still forming, is > 0) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR is at least its configured minimum).
Short: Trend crosses below zero (current < 0 AND previous >= 0) AND the upper outer VWAP deviation band was touched within the configured lookback, no earlier than the configured minimum bars after reset (touch mode is configured as wick reaches band, close beyond band, or wick reaches band and close returns inside) AND (RSI-extreme filter disabled OR RSI was overbought within its configured lookback) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish RSI divergence occurred within its configured lookback) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR trend calculated from the higher-timeframe bar, including a bar still forming, is < 0) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR is at least its configured minimum).
