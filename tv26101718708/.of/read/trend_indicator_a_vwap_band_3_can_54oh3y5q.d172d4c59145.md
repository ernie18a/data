Calculation: Heikin-Ashi OHLC = close is (open + high + low + close) / 4; open is the average of the preceding Heikin-Ashi open and close, initialized to (open + close) / 2; high is the maximum of high, Heikin-Ashi open and Heikin-Ashi close; low is their minimum.
Calculation: Trend = 100 × (MA of Heikin-Ashi close − MA of Heikin-Ashi open) / max(epsilon, MA of Heikin-Ashi high − MA of Heikin-Ashi low). The same selected MA and length are used for each component; available implementations are ALMA, HMA, SMA, VWMA and EMA, with EMA also used for the other displayed MA choices.
Calculation: Confirmation trend = Trend calculated after mapping the other timeframe's Heikin-Ashi OHLC onto chart bars and applying the MA on chart bars. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last intrabar available in each chart bar.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation bands = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source and that mean ± a multiplier times the volume-weighted population standard deviation of the source. Replace missing or zero volume with one. Reset at each New York calendar day or at each configured session start, according to the selected anchor.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish divergence = the latest confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND its RSI is higher than the RSI at the preceding pivot. A pivot uses equal numbers of bars on either side and is detected only after the right-hand bars have elapsed. If the optional extreme filter is enabled, RSI at either pivot is at or below the oversold threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed bearish divergence = the latest confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high AND its RSI is lower than the RSI at the preceding pivot. A pivot uses equal numbers of bars on either side and is detected only after the right-hand bars have elapsed. If the optional extreme filter is enabled, RSI at either pivot is at or above the overbought threshold.
Calculation: VWAP direction state = reset to neutral until bars since the VWAP reset are at least the greater of the warm-up length and slope lookback. Thereafter set bullish when the selected test is true, bearish when its opposite is true, and otherwise retain the preceding state if holding is enabled or set neutral. The tests are selected from (close − anchored VWAP) / ATR above a positive threshold, (anchored VWAP − anchored VWAP at the slope lookback) / ATR above a positive threshold, or both; the bearish tests use the corresponding negative thresholds. Use zero for both ratios when ATR is not positive.
Long: Trend crosses above zero AND bars since the VWAP reset are at least the minimum age AND within the lesser of the band lookback and bars since reset, a bar satisfies the selected lower-band test: low ≤ lower band, close ≤ lower band, or low ≤ lower band AND close > lower band AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI ≤ oversold threshold within its lookback) AND (divergence filter disabled OR confirmed bullish divergence occurred within its lookback) AND (confirmation filter disabled OR confirmation trend > 0) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR ≥ minimum) AND (closed-bar-only setting disabled OR chart bar is closed).
Short: Trend crosses below zero AND bars since the VWAP reset are at least the minimum age AND within the lesser of the band lookback and bars since reset, a bar satisfies the selected upper-band test: high ≥ upper band, close ≥ upper band, or high ≥ upper band AND close < upper band AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI ≥ overbought threshold within its lookback) AND (divergence filter disabled OR confirmed bearish divergence occurred within its lookback) AND (confirmation filter disabled OR confirmation trend < 0) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR ≥ minimum) AND (closed-bar-only setting disabled OR chart bar is closed).
Long: Trend > 0.
Short: Trend ≤ 0.
Long: VWAP direction state = bullish.
Short: VWAP direction state = bearish.
Long: Confirmed bullish divergence.
Short: Confirmed bearish divergence.
Long: RSI ≤ oversold threshold.
Short: RSI ≥ overbought threshold.
