Calculation: Liquidity pools = retain a configurable number of the most recent confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows separately; each pivot uses the same number of bars on both sides. Mark a high pivot taken when a subsequent or confirmation bar has high greater than its price, and a low pivot taken when low is less than its price. On each bar, the upper pool is the lowest untaken high pivot above close, and the lower pool is the highest untaken low pivot below close. Optionally include the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar as candidates; select them only when strictly closer to close than the corresponding pivot. For each selected pool, count untaken pivots on its side within an ATR-multiple tolerance of its price; retain the selected pivot's age in bars, or identify the pool as a prior-period pool.
Calculation: Side scores = for each pool, distance is its absolute distance from close divided by ATR; proximity is clamp(100 - 100 × distance / maximum distance, 0, 100). Strength is clamp(100 × touch count / 4, 0, 100), with a minimum of 72 for a prior-period pool. Freshness is clamp(100 - 100 × pivot age / freshness window, 0, 100), or 62 for a prior-period pool. Adjusted strength is 0.7 × strength + 0.3 × freshness. Trend is 100 for the upper side when close is above EMA and EMA is rising over its slope lookback, 64 when close is above EMA otherwise, 28 when close is below EMA and EMA is falling, and 50 otherwise; reverse above/below and rising/falling for the lower side. Range position is clamp((close - lowest low) / max(highest high - lowest low, ATR), 0, 1), using the same range lookback for both extremes. Range score is 100 × (1 - range position) for the upper side and 100 × range position for the lower side. Each side's score is clamp(0.28 × proximity + 0.26 × adjusted strength + 0.26 × trend + 0.20 × range score, 0, 100); a missing pool has score 0. Use true range when ATR is unavailable and floor ATR at a configurable positive price increment.
Long: upper pool exists AND upper side score >= lower side score
Short: lower pool exists AND lower side score > upper side score
