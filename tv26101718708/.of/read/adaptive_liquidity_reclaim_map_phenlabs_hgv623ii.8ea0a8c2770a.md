Calculation: Active swing low/high = the latest confirmed pivot low/high. A pivot is the lowest low/highest high over its specified left and right bars, allowing equal values on the left but requiring strictly higher lows/lower highs on the right; it becomes active on its confirmation bar. Each new pivot resets its direction's used flag; the first corresponding reclaim signal sets that flag until the next pivot.
Calculation: Adaptive impulse threshold = base impulse parameter × clamp(ATR / SMA of ATR, 0.65, 1.50); use a ratio of 1 when ATR or its SMA is unavailable or the SMA is not positive.
Long: Low < active swing low AND close > active swing low AND close > open AND ATR > 0 AND abs(close − open) / ATR >= adaptive impulse threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND active swing low's used flag is false AND close − low > minimum price increment parameter.
Short: High > active swing high AND close < active swing high AND close < open AND ATR > 0 AND abs(close − open) / ATR >= adaptive impulse threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND active swing high's used flag is false AND high − close > minimum price increment parameter.
