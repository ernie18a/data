Calculation: RSI candle close = RSI of the source price; RSI candle open = RSI of the prior bar’s source price using the same RSI lookback; RSI candle high and low = max and min of RSI candle close and open.
Calculation: RSI Supertrend trend = compute true range from RSI candle high/low and prior RSI candle close, smooth it with an RMA ATR, and form upper/lower bands around the RSI candle midpoint using the ATR and a multiplier. Trail the upper band to the lower of the current basic upper band and prior upper band unless prior RSI close is above the prior upper band; trail the lower band to the higher of the current basic lower band and prior lower band unless prior RSI close is below the prior lower band. Trend switches from bullish to bearish when RSI candle close is below the trailing lower band, and from bearish to bullish when RSI candle close is above the trailing upper band; otherwise it retains its prior trend.
Long: RSI Supertrend trend = bullish AND prior RSI Supertrend trend = bearish
Short: RSI Supertrend trend = bearish AND prior RSI Supertrend trend = bullish
