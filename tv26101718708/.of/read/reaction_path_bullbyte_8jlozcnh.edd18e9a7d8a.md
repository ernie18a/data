Calculation: Fair Price and half-band = over a rolling window, the weighted average of (high + low + 2 × close) / 4 and the weighted mean absolute deviation from that average; each bar’s weight is 1 − abs(close − open) / (high − low). The half-band has a minimum of two minimum ticks.
Calculation: Pressure Centre = weighted average over a shorter rolling window of (close − low) / (high − low), using the Fair Price candle weights. Pressure Migration = current Pressure Centre − Pressure Centre from an earlier offset; previous Migration is calculated the same way one bar earlier. The offset is shorter than the Pressure Centre lookback.
Calculation: Trend efficiency = abs(close − close from a longer lookback) / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback; trend direction is the sign of the net change.
Calculation: Body efficiency = abs(close − open) / (high − low). Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; wick ratios = respective wick / range.
Calculation: Speed ratio = current range / average range of the preceding short window. Volume ratio = current volume / average volume of a longer preceding window; use 1 when volume’s coefficient of variation over that longer window is below a threshold.
Calculation: Directional dominance = count of bullish (for long) or bearish (for short) bars in the recent short window / its length. Directional efficiency = mean body efficiency of those bullish or bearish bars. Fair Price depth = greatest penetration of the preceding short window’s lows below Fair Price, or highs above Fair Price, divided by current half-band.
Calculation: Expansion = current range is at least the maximum range of the preceding short window and at least an ATR-scaled floor. ATR = average true range.
Calculation: Normal bar = current bar is not both larger than a multiple of the preceding short-window average range and below a body-efficiency threshold, and is not the bar immediately after such a spike when its close is within the spike bar’s range.
Calculation: Path Level 2 = entry + (long) or − (short) ATR × configured target distance. Reaction invalidation = signal-bar low − (long) or high + (short) ATR × configured distance. Continuation invalidation = Fair Price captured on the signal bar.
Long: Confirmed bar, no active position, rearm ready, and no locked long signal event; no active matching failure-memory block; reaction: close is below Fair Price by at least the displacement threshold AND mean bullish-bar efficiency and bullish-bar dominance meet their thresholds AND lower wick ratio exceeds upper wick ratio AND lower wick exceeds upper wick AND speed ratio, volume ratio, and depth below Fair Price meet their thresholds AND bar is normal AND Pressure Migration is positive AND (Migration exceeds the positive stall threshold OR Pressure Centre is building toward or above its building threshold OR current Migration exceeds the positive stall threshold while previous Migration is at or below the negative stall threshold); OR continuation: close is above Fair Price plus half-band AND Expansion AND upper wick ratio is below its threshold AND body efficiency > 0.5 AND speed ratio and volume ratio meet their thresholds AND trend direction is positive AND trend efficiency meets its threshold AND Migration is positive AND (Pressure Centre is building or Migration exceeds the positive stall threshold) AND bar is normal.
Short: Confirmed bar, no active position, rearm ready, and no locked short signal event; no active matching failure-memory block; reaction: close is above Fair Price by at least the displacement threshold AND mean bearish-bar efficiency and bearish-bar dominance meet their thresholds AND upper wick ratio exceeds lower wick ratio AND upper wick exceeds lower wick AND speed ratio, volume ratio, and depth above Fair Price meet their thresholds AND bar is normal AND Pressure Migration is negative AND (Migration is below the negative stall threshold OR Migration is negative while current Migration is at or below the negative stall threshold and previous Migration was at or above the positive stall threshold); OR continuation: close is below Fair Price minus half-band AND Expansion AND lower wick ratio is below its threshold AND body efficiency > 0.5 AND speed ratio and volume ratio meet their thresholds AND trend direction is negative AND trend efficiency meets its threshold AND Migration is negative AND Migration is below the negative stall threshold AND bar is normal.
Exit: Long, low <= long invalidation OR high >= Path Level 2; if both occur on one bar, invalidation takes precedence.
Exit: Short, high >= short invalidation OR low <= Path Level 2; if both occur on one bar, invalidation takes precedence.
