Calculation: Use closed chart bars. Let division by zero or a missing denominator return the stated fallback. Use independently configurable thresholds, lookbacks, multipliers and limits; the fair-price and volume lookbacks are longer than the candle-character lookback. Start evaluating after the fair-price lookback plus the migration offset plus the candle-character lookback plus the ATR lookback have elapsed.
Calculation: Range = high - low; body efficiency = abs(close - open) / range, fallback 1; weight = 1 - body efficiency; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; wick ratios = corresponding wick / range, fallback 0.
Calculation: Fair price = weighted mean of (high + low + 2 * close) / 4 over the fair-price lookback, using weight; if total weight is zero, use the current bar's (high + low + 2 * close) / 4. Fair half-band = weighted mean of the absolute differences between those prices and fair price, floored at a positive price increment; if total weight is zero, use half the absolute difference between close and fair price before applying the floor. Displacement = (close - fair price) / fair half-band, fallback 0.
Calculation: Pressure centre = weighted mean of (close - low) / range over the pressure lookback, using weight and a midpoint fallback for zero range or zero total weight. Migration = current pressure centre minus pressure centre at the migration offset; previous migration is the preceding bar's migration.
Calculation: Exhaustion = pressure centre at or beyond either extreme threshold. Reversal = no exhaustion AND current migration at or beyond either signed stall threshold AND previous migration at or beyond the opposite signed stall threshold. Building = no exhaustion AND no reversal AND migration > 0 AND (pressure centre >= building threshold OR pressure centre >= building threshold minus approach threshold AND pressure centre < building threshold AND pressure centre > pressure centre at the migration offset). Upward movement = no exhaustion AND no reversal AND no building AND migration > stall threshold. Downward movement = no exhaustion AND no reversal AND no building AND migration < -stall threshold. Upward reaction pressure = migration > 0 AND (upward movement OR building OR reversal). Downward reaction pressure = migration < 0 AND (downward movement OR reversal). Upward continuation pressure = migration > 0 AND (building OR upward movement). Downward continuation pressure = migration < 0 AND downward movement.
Calculation: Speed = current range / mean range of the preceding candle-character lookback, fallback 1. Volume ratio = current volume / mean volume of the preceding volume lookback, fallback 1, if the coefficient of variation of volume over the current volume lookback exceeds its reliability threshold; otherwise 1. Treat missing volume as zero in the volume averages and reliability calculation.
Calculation: Directional efficiency for a side = mean body efficiency of bars closing in that direction in the current candle-character lookback, fallback 0. Directional dominance = count of those bars / candle-character lookback. Doji bars belong to neither side. Downward depth = maximum of 0 and (current fair price - prior low) / current fair half-band over the preceding candle-character lookback; upward depth substitutes (prior high - current fair price). Use 0 as the division fallback.
Calculation: Normal bar = NOT (current range > spike multiplier * mean preceding range AND current body efficiency < spike efficiency threshold) AND NOT (previous range > spike multiplier * current mean preceding range AND previous body / max(previous range, positive price increment) < spike efficiency threshold AND close >= previous low AND close <= previous high).
Calculation: Trend displacement = close minus close at the trend lookback; trend efficiency = abs(trend displacement) / sum of absolute successive close changes over that lookback, fallback 0. Expansion = current range >= maximum range of the preceding candle-character lookback AND current range >= ATR * expansion multiplier.
Calculation: On entry, save the signal family, direction, entry-bar ATR and fair price. Target = entry price plus direction * target multiplier * entry-bar ATR. Reaction invalidation = entry-bar low minus invalidation multiplier * entry-bar ATR for a long, or entry-bar high plus that distance for a short; continuation invalidation = entry-bar fair price. Direction is +1 for long and -1 for short. On each later bar, check invalidation and target before any extension; invalidation takes precedence if both are touched. If neither is touched, a same-direction continuation gate with continuation pressure extends the target by direction * extension multiplier * current ATR, up to an extension-count limit, regardless of entry family and without adaptive or event-lock filters.
Calculation: On invalidation, increment a capped consecutive-failure count and record the invalidation bar's absolute displacement, migration, side-specific directional efficiency and depth, speed, volume ratio, pressure centre and range / ATR, together with the failed direction and family. Reset the count on target success or when its expiry lookback is exceeded. Clear the recorded failure on target success, when its expiry lookback is exceeded, or when abs(displacement) < displacement-reset threshold OR abs(migration) < migration-reset threshold OR migration moves beyond the opposite-direction reset threshold OR range / ATR > recorded range / ATR * range-reset multiplier. After invalidation, permit another entry only when that behavioural reset occurs; expiry alone does not rearm it. A target success immediately rearms it.
Calculation: Failure similarity = mean across the eight recorded features of 1 - clamp(abs(current feature - recorded feature) / that feature's tolerance, 0, 1). A candidate passes the memory filter when no failure is recorded OR its direction differs OR its family differs OR failure similarity < similarity threshold minus failure count * similarity tightening. Increase the reaction displacement, directional-efficiency and depth thresholds, and both families' speed and volume thresholds, by their respective tightening increments times the failure count; retain the original strict displacement comparison as well as the tightened minimum comparison.
Calculation: Lock the most recently emitted direction and family until its behavioural reset; a different direction or family is not blocked and replaces the lock when emitted. A long reaction lock resets when displacement > positive reset threshold AND migration <= 0; a short reaction lock resets when displacement < negative reset threshold AND migration >= 0. A long continuation lock resets when close <= fair price + fair half-band AND migration <= -stall threshold; a short continuation lock resets when close >= fair price - fair half-band AND migration >= stall threshold.
Calculation: Long reaction gate = displacement < -base displacement threshold AND abs(displacement) >= tightened displacement threshold AND long directional efficiency >= tightened efficiency threshold AND long dominance >= dominance threshold AND lower wick ratio > upper wick ratio AND lower wick > upper wick AND speed >= tightened speed threshold AND volume ratio >= tightened volume threshold AND downward depth >= tightened depth threshold AND normal bar. Short reaction gate = displacement > base displacement threshold AND abs(displacement) >= tightened displacement threshold AND short directional efficiency >= tightened efficiency threshold AND short dominance >= dominance threshold AND upper wick ratio > lower wick ratio AND upper wick > lower wick AND speed >= tightened speed threshold AND volume ratio >= tightened volume threshold AND upward depth >= tightened depth threshold AND normal bar.
Calculation: Long continuation gate = close > fair price + fair half-band AND expansion AND upper wick ratio < continuation wick threshold AND body efficiency > continuation body threshold AND speed >= applicable speed threshold AND volume ratio >= applicable volume threshold AND trend displacement > 0 AND trend efficiency >= trend efficiency threshold AND normal bar. Short continuation gate = close < fair price - fair half-band AND expansion AND lower wick ratio < continuation wick threshold AND body efficiency > continuation body threshold AND speed >= applicable speed threshold AND volume ratio >= applicable volume threshold AND trend displacement < 0 AND trend efficiency >= trend efficiency threshold AND normal bar. Use base speed and volume thresholds for target extensions and tightened thresholds for entries.
Long: No active setup AND no setup closed on the current bar AND rearmed AND long reaction gate AND upward reaction pressure AND long reaction passes the memory filter AND the long reaction event is unlocked.
Short: No active setup AND no setup closed on the current bar AND rearmed AND short reaction gate AND downward reaction pressure AND short reaction passes the memory filter AND the short reaction event is unlocked.
Long: No active setup AND no setup closed on the current bar AND rearmed AND long continuation gate AND upward continuation pressure AND long continuation passes the memory filter AND the long continuation event is unlocked.
Short: No active setup AND no setup closed on the current bar AND rearmed AND short continuation gate AND downward continuation pressure AND short continuation passes the memory filter AND the short continuation event is unlocked.
Exit: long, low <= invalidation OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= invalidation OR low <= target.
