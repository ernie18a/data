Calculation: Average entry = total cost of the base order and filled averaging orders divided by their total quantity
Calculation: Averaging-order price = base entry price × (1 − that order’s deviation from base entry)
Calculation: Take-profit price = average entry × (1 + take-profit percentage)
Long: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, flat AND RSI is below its threshold, OR in a long position with an unfilled averaging order AND close is at or below the next order’s price; averaging-order deviations are progressively larger for each of the five orders
Exit: long, on a completed chart-timeframe bar close is at or above the take-profit price
