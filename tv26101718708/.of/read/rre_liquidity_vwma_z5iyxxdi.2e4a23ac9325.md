Calculation: Volume-weighted moving average = sum(close × volume) over a lookback divided by sum(volume) over the same lookback
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low that is the lowest across equal lookback windows before and after it
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high that is the highest across equal lookback windows before and after it
Long: newly confirmed pivot low AND close > volume-weighted moving average
Short: newly confirmed pivot high AND close < volume-weighted moving average
