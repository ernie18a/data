Calculation: Direction = +1 when the short-term EMA > base EMA > long-term EMA and base EMA slope / ATR >= minimum slope; -1 when the short-term EMA < base EMA < long-term EMA and base EMA slope / ATR <= -minimum slope; otherwise retain the most recent nonzero direction for at most the context-holding lookback while direction × (close - long-term EMA) >= -ATR × (max(band width, rail buffer) + context buffer), then set it to 0. Base EMA slope is the change over a slope lookback. Apply any selected directional restriction before retaining the direction.
Calculation: Band interaction = direction != 0 AND low <= base EMA + ATR × band width AND high >= base EMA - ATR × band width. Recent band interaction means an interaction occurred on the current bar or within the acceptance lookback, regardless of its direction.
Calculation: Invalidation rail = for direction +1, min(base EMA - ATR × band width - ATR × rail buffer, long-term EMA - ATR × rail buffer × rail factor); for direction -1, max(base EMA + ATR × band width + ATR × rail buffer, long-term EMA + ATR × rail buffer × rail factor).
Calculation: Acceptance score = rounded, bounded weighted sum of trend, pullback, close, volume, and target-room scores. For an active EMA stack and accepted close, trend score is a weighted sum of full stack score and slope score; slope score is the clamped linear normalization of direction × base EMA slope / ATR from minimum slope to minimum slope plus a slope span. Pullback depth is max(direction × (short-term EMA - directional extreme), 0) / ATR, where the directional extreme is low for +1 and high for -1; its score rises linearly from zero at zero depth to full at ideal depth, then falls linearly to zero at maximum depth, clamped to that range. Close score is the accepted-close score. Volume score is volume / volume SMA / required volume ratio, clamped to the score range. Target obstruction is the highest high for +1 or lowest low for -1 over a lookback ending at the previous bar, falling back to the current high or low when unavailable. Target-room score is full if direction × (close - obstruction) > 0; otherwise it is the clamped linear normalization of max(direction × (obstruction - close), 0) / ATR from minimum room to clean room. Use a volume ratio of 1 when the volume SMA is unavailable or nonpositive, and a small positive denominator floor for zero ATR or candle range.
Long: direction = +1 AND short-term EMA > base EMA > long-term EMA AND base EMA slope / ATR >= minimum slope AND recent band interaction AND close > base EMA AND (close - low) / candle range >= required close location AND close >= open AND close >= invalidation rail AND acceptance score >= accepted threshold AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume / volume SMA >= required volume ratio) AND (strict confirmation disabled OR slope score >= strict slope threshold AND target-room score >= strict room threshold).
Short: direction = -1 AND short-term EMA < base EMA < long-term EMA AND base EMA slope / ATR <= -minimum slope AND recent band interaction AND close < base EMA AND (high - close) / candle range >= required close location AND close <= open AND close <= invalidation rail AND acceptance score >= accepted threshold AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume / volume SMA >= required volume ratio) AND (strict confirmation disabled OR slope score >= strict slope threshold AND target-room score >= strict room threshold).
