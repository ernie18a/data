Calculation: Supply and demand zones = On confirmation of a symmetric pivot high or low, respectively, create a zone centered on the pivot if the distance from the pivot high to the lowest low of the pivot-length window ending now, or from the highest high of that window to the pivot low, respectively, is at least a departure multiple of current ATR. Fix its half-height at a multiple of ATR on the creation bar and its birth at the pivot bar. Reject a new zone if its center is within an ATR-scaled deduplication distance of any stored zone on that side, including dead zones; when the per-side capacity is exceeded, remove the oldest zone. Each bar, record the current bar as the latest touch whenever low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom. If the latest touch is earlier than the current bar and within the confirmation window, increment the zone's retest count and clear its touch if, since that touch, supply has reached at least a bounce distance below its bottom or demand has reached at least a bounce distance above its top, OR, when wick confirmation is enabled, the touch bar's high penetrated the supply top and closed at or below it, or its low penetrated the demand bottom and closed at or above it, by the required current-ATR-scaled wick distance. Mark a zone dead permanently when its age exceeds the enabled expiry or close breaks beyond its outer edge by the ATR-scaled break buffer. If flipping is enabled, each bar a zone meets the break condition, attempt to create an opposite-side zone at its center using current ATR and the current bar as birth. Dead zones remain eligible for deduplication, touches, retests, breaks, flips, proximity and signals until removed by capacity.
Calculation: Eligible zones = If confirmed-only mode is enabled AND at least one stored zone on either side has the required retest count, use only zones meeting that count; otherwise use all stored zones. For each side, distance is the minimum distance from close to a zone interval, zero inside it; penetration is the maximum overlap length of the current high-low interval with a zone. Use a large sentinel distance and zero penetration when that side has no eligible zone. The nearest zone on each side is the eligible zone whose center is closest to close, with the oldest winning a tie.
Calculation: Resolved side = Compare the nearest-by-center supply and demand zones by distance from close to their intervals, choosing supply on a tie. If that chosen zone exists and close <= its bottom AND high >= its bottom, choose supply; otherwise if close >= its top AND low <= its top, choose demand. Otherwise, if only one side has distance <= the ATR-scaled proximity threshold, choose that side. If both or neither do, choose the side with greater penetration when penetration resolution is enabled; if tied or disabled, choose supply when trend resolution is enabled AND the shorter EMA of close is below the longer EMA, or demand when it is above; otherwise choose supply when supply distance <= demand distance, and demand otherwise.
Calculation: Base long and base short = A doji has high > low, absolute close-open <= a fraction of high-low, and high-low >= an ATR multiple; in strict mode both upper and lower wick lengths must also be >= their respective fraction of high-low. Both bases require a doji AND at least one side's zone distance <= the ATR-scaled proximity threshold. Base long additionally requires demand penetration > 0 AND, if previous-candle flip is enabled, previous close < previous open, otherwise resolved side = demand AND close >= open. Base short additionally requires supply penetration > 0 AND, if previous-candle flip is enabled, previous close > previous open, otherwise resolved side = supply AND close <= open.
Calculation: Quality checks = On the signal bar use the nearest-by-center eligible zone on the relevant side. For demand, the four checks are: zone bottom - low >= the ATR-scaled wick threshold AND close > zone bottom; close <= zone bottom + the edge-band fraction of zone height; no recorded touch OR bars since recorded touch >= the freshness threshold; highest high over the approach lookback - low >= the ATR-scaled approach threshold. For supply, they are: high - zone top >= the ATR-scaled wick threshold AND close < zone top; close >= zone top - the edge-band fraction of zone height; no recorded touch OR bars since recorded touch >= the freshness threshold; high - lowest low over the approach lookback >= the ATR-scaled approach threshold. A missing zone fails wick and band and passes freshness. Strict auto-tightening raises the wick, approach and freshness thresholds and lowers the band fraction, subject to their respective caps and floors.
Calculation: Zone strength = For the nearest-by-center eligible zone, capped retest count plus capped bounce score plus an age score plus an integrity score; a missing zone has strength zero. If its recorded touch is earlier than the current bar, the bounce score is the capped ratio to current ATR of touch-bar close minus the minimum of current close and subsequent historical lows for supply, or the maximum of current close and subsequent historical highs minus touch-bar close for demand, using a capped lookback; otherwise it is zero. The age score takes one of three decreasing fixed levels according to two increasing age thresholds; the integrity score is a fixed positive amount for a zone not marked dead and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Quality pass = Off mode passes. Soft mode requires at least its configured number of the four relevant quality checks. Strict mode requires either at least the greater of its own and the soft-mode required counts AND at least one of wick or band, OR the relevant side's nearest-by-center zone is also the nearer of the two nearest zones by interval distance, its strength >= the rescue threshold, AND at least one of wick or band. Strict mode's strength threshold is not lower than the soft-mode count requirement only for the count of quality checks; its separate strength gate uses its configured threshold.
Calculation: Pre-gate long and short = Use the corresponding current base, or when next-bar confirmation is enabled use the preceding bar's base AND current close > preceding close + current ATR times the confirmation multiplier for long, or current close < preceding close - current ATR times that multiplier for short. Require the corresponding quality pass AND at least the configured number of bars since the last same-side pre-gate signal, if any. If both sides qualify on the same bar, retain only the resolved side. Record the surviving signal's bar before applying higher-timeframe or strength gates, even if those later gates fail.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe zones = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, detect symmetric pivot highs and lows; retain the latest confirmed pivot of each side and make its fixed band from that pivot price plus and minus a multiple of higher-timeframe ATR at confirmation. Update each band when a new pivot of its side is confirmed.
Long: Pre-gate long = true AND (higher-timeframe trend gate disabled OR shorter EMA of higher-timeframe close > longer EMA of higher-timeframe close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (higher-timeframe zone gate disabled OR current low <= higher-timeframe demand band top AND current high >= its bottom) AND (quality mode = off OR strength of nearest eligible demand zone >= the mode's strength threshold OR strength of nearest eligible supply zone >= the mode's strength threshold).
Short: Pre-gate short = true AND (higher-timeframe trend gate disabled OR shorter EMA of higher-timeframe close < longer EMA of higher-timeframe close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (higher-timeframe zone gate disabled OR current low <= higher-timeframe supply band top AND current high >= its bottom) AND (quality mode = off OR strength of nearest eligible demand zone >= the mode's strength threshold OR strength of nearest eligible supply zone >= the mode's strength threshold).
Calculation: Exit levels = At each permitted entry signal, set the long stop to the signal doji low minus a price buffer and the short stop to the signal doji high plus that buffer; use the preceding bar as the doji when next-bar confirmation is enabled. Set the long target to signal-bar close plus a reward multiple of max(signal-bar close - long stop, minimum price increment), and the short target to signal-bar close minus that multiple of max(short stop - signal-bar close, minimum price increment). Retain these levels until replaced by another same-side entry signal.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
