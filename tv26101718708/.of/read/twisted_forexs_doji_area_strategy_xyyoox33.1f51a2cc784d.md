Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Doji = current bar has positive range, body no greater than a fraction of its range, and range at least a minimum multiple of ATR; in strict mode, each wick must also be at least a minimum fraction of range.
Calculation: Supply and demand zones = bands centered on confirmed swing highs and lows, respectively, with half-height a multiple of ATR; create a zone only when price has departed the swing by at least a minimum ATR multiple, and skip centers within a minimum ATR distance of an existing same-side zone. A zone becomes inactive for display after a close beyond its edge by a buffer or after its age limit. Retests are counted when price touches a zone and, within a limited number of bars, bounces away by a minimum ATR distance or makes a sufficiently deep wick rejection. When confirmed zones are required, use them if any exist; otherwise use all zones.
Calculation: Zone proximity = distance from the close to the nearest edge of a zone on each side; a close inside a zone has zero distance. Zone penetration = greatest overlap depth between the current bar and any zone on that side.
Calculation: Trend = fast EMA compared with slow EMA, with the fast EMA using a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Doji-side resolution = choose the only nearby side if just one is within the ATR-based proximity threshold; otherwise, if enabled and penetration differs, choose the side with greater penetration; otherwise, if enabled, choose supply when the fast EMA is below the slow EMA and demand when it is above; otherwise choose the nearer side (ties choose supply). Override this using the nearest zone overall: a touch of its lower edge from below selects supply, and a touch of its upper edge from above selects demand.
Long: Doji AND close is within the ATR-based proximity threshold of at least one zone AND the current bar overlaps a demand zone AND (previous-candle flip is enabled AND the previous bar is bearish OR previous-candle flip is disabled AND resolved side is demand AND close is at least the current bar midpoint) AND (next-bar confirmation is disabled OR the preceding bar met the long conditions and current close exceeds preceding close by the confirmation ATR multiple) AND the long signal cooldown has elapsed AND any enabled quality, zone-strength, higher-timeframe trend, and higher-timeframe demand-zone filters pass AND long trading is allowed.
Short: Doji AND close is within the ATR-based proximity threshold of at least one zone AND the current bar overlaps a supply zone AND (previous-candle flip is enabled AND the previous bar is bullish OR previous-candle flip is disabled AND resolved side is supply AND close is at most the current bar midpoint) AND (next-bar confirmation is disabled OR the preceding bar met the short conditions and current close is below preceding close by the confirmation ATR multiple) AND the short signal cooldown has elapsed AND any enabled quality, zone-strength, higher-timeframe trend, and higher-timeframe supply-zone filters pass AND short trading is allowed.
Calculation: Quality filter = for each side, count these four checks: wick penetrates beyond the nearest zone edge by a minimum ATR multiple and closes back inside; close lies in the zone's corresponding edge band; the zone has not been touched within the minimum cooldown; and the approach move over a lookback meets a minimum ATR multiple. Soft mode requires at least its configured count. Strict mode requires at least the greater of its configured count and the soft count, plus either the wick or edge-band check; strict mode may tighten thresholds. Strict mode also permits a qualifying edge-anchored signal when the selected side's zone strength reaches its rescue threshold. Zone strength combines capped retest count, capped bounce distance in ATR units, zone age, and a penalty for broken zones.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA compared with slow EMA on a higher-timeframe, potentially still-forming bar; its fast EMA uses a shorter lookback. Higher-timeframe swing zones = bands around the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing high and low, sized by higher-timeframe ATR; evaluate overlap with the current chart-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, current low is at or below the signal doji low minus the stop buffer OR current high is at or above entry price plus the reward multiple times the entry-to-stop risk.
Exit: short, current high is at or above the signal doji high plus the stop buffer OR current low is at or below entry price minus the reward multiple times the entry-to-stop risk.
