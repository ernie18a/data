Calculation: Fair value gap = On each enabled watched timeframe, a bullish gap has low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > high two bars earlier, with top = low and bottom = high two bars earlier; a bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < low two bars earlier, with top = low two bars earlier and bottom = high. Its formation time is the opening time of the bar two bars earlier. Watched timeframes include a shortest seed timeframe and longer timeframes. Relative to the chart, a watched timeframe may be lower, equal, or higher. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar; for an equal timeframe use the current bar.
Calculation: Active gap = A gap remains active until chart low, or chart close when close-based mitigation is selected, is strictly below bullish top minus a mitigation fraction of gap height; or until chart high, or chart close when close-based mitigation is selected, is strictly above bearish bottom plus that fraction of gap height. Process new gaps and cluster construction before mitigation on each chart bar.
Calculation: Cluster = Keep a bounded newest-first pool of unique gaps, identified by timeframe, formation time and direction; discard the oldest pooled gap when the pool exceeds its limit. Add longer-timeframe gaps before seed-timeframe gaps. On every chart bar, process active, unclustered seed gaps from oldest to newest. For each seed, select the newest active gap from each longer timeframe that has the same direction and overlaps the seed, including touching boundaries. If exclusivity is enabled, exclude gaps belonging to any existing cluster; if permanent consumption is also enabled, exclude gaps previously consumed by a cluster. Create a cluster when the number of selected gaps including the seed meets the minimum; permanently consume selected longer-timeframe gaps when both options are enabled. Keep a bounded newest-first cluster list and discard its oldest cluster when over the limit. After mitigation, a cluster with at least two active members has top = minimum active-member top, bottom = maximum active-member bottom, and latest formation time = the greatest formation time its active members have had while the cluster was maintained. When fewer than two members remain active, retain the last range and latest formation time for the current bar and the next bar, then delete the cluster. Cluster members retain their gap state even if removed from the pool.
Calculation: Visible cluster = A retained cluster is visible when its enabled time filter passes AND its enabled price filter passes, OR when edge wake-up is enabled and the distance from close to the cluster range is at most a specified multiple of chart-timeframe ATR. The time filter passes when current bar time minus the cluster's latest formation time is at most the specified time window. The price filter passes when the cluster range overlaps the range from close minus an ATR multiple to close plus that multiple; it also passes if ATR is unavailable or not positive. Edge wake-up requires positive available ATR; distance is zero inside the range, close minus top above it, and bottom minus close below it.
Long: A visible bullish cluster exists.
Short: A visible bearish cluster exists.
