Calculation: High levels = confirmed pivot highs, keeping the most recent configurable number, plus the high of each of two optional higher timeframes, using their last completed bars; replace each higher-timeframe level when its new bar starts. Pivot confirmation uses configurable left and right bar counts. Low levels are defined analogously using pivot lows and higher-timeframe lows. Add new levels before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: High-level sweep = for an active high level, high > level and close <= level on the same bar, or high > level and close > level followed by close <= level within a configurable maximum number of bars counting the initial bar. While waiting, close <= level completes the sweep; otherwise retire the level when the maximum window expires. If the maximum window is one bar, retire the level immediately when high > level and close > level. A swept level remains active. Suppress a sweep signal if that level was also swept on the immediately preceding bar, including a suppressed sweep.
Calculation: Low-level sweep = for an active low level, low < level and close >= level on the same bar, or low < level and close < level followed by close >= level within a configurable maximum number of bars counting the initial bar. While waiting, close >= level completes the sweep; otherwise retire the level when the maximum window expires. If the maximum window is one bar, retire the level immediately when low < level and close < level. A swept level remains active. Suppress a sweep signal if that level was also swept on the immediately preceding bar, including a suppressed sweep.
Calculation: Sweep depth = absolute difference between the level and the highest high for a high-level sweep or lowest low for a low-level sweep over the configurable sweep window ending on the current bar, divided by current ATR; depth is undefined when ATR <= 0.
Long: An active low level has an unsuppressed low-level sweep AND sweep depth is undefined OR an active low level has an unsuppressed low-level sweep AND sweep depth >= configurable minimum depth.
Short: An active high level has an unsuppressed high-level sweep AND sweep depth is undefined OR an active high level has an unsuppressed high-level sweep AND sweep depth >= configurable minimum depth.
