Calculation: Swing high/low level = A local high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars to its right; retain the configured number of most recent levels on each side. A swept level remains active and may be swept again; a broken level is retired.
Calculation: Previous-period high/low = The high/low of the previous day or week, taken from the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: A tracked swing low, previous-day low, or previous-week low is swept: low < level and close >= level on the same bar, OR price first trades below the level and a bar closes >= level within the configured close-back window; the lowest low over that window is at least the configured minimum sweep depth below the level, measured in ATR.
Short: A tracked swing high, previous-day high, or previous-week high is swept: high > level and close <= level on the same bar, OR price first trades above the level and a bar closes <= level within the configured close-back window; the highest high over that window is at least the configured minimum sweep depth above the level, measured in ATR.
