Calculation: SessionHigh/SessionLow = for each of three consecutive intraday sessions (early, middle, late) of the current day, the highest high / lowest low of the bars inside that session. All levels and sequence states reset at each new day. Each session's level is used only on bars outside that session. Every level runs its own independent sequence.
Calculation: SweepState (high side, one per SessionHigh) = stage 0 until a bar outside that session has high > SessionHigh. That bar is the sweep bar and starts stage 1, with Ext = its high. In stage 1, Ext = max(Ext, high); each time Ext is raised, CHOCHShort is recomputed. If close < CHOCHShort, move to stage 2 with PullExt = that bar's low. In stage 2, if high > Ext, set Ext = high and return to stage 1 without recomputing CHOCHShort. Otherwise PullExt = min(PullExt, low). Low side (one per SessionLow) is the mirror image: low < SessionLow starts the sweep, Ext = lowest low, close > CHOCHLong moves to stage 2, PullExt = highest high since the CHOCH bar, and low < Ext returns to stage 1.
Calculation: CHOCHShort = M2 level: scanning back from the bar before the Ext bar over a limited lookback, take the nearest bar whose close is below the midpoint of its own range, provided at least one bar strictly between it and the Ext bar has low > that bar's high. The level is that bar's low. If none is found, no CHOCH is possible. Alternative method: the most recent confirmed swing low (pivot with equal bars on each side). CHOCHLong is the mirror image: nearest bar with close above its range midpoint, with some bar in between whose high < that bar's low, and the level is that bar's high. Alternative: the most recent confirmed swing high.
Calculation: FibEntry = 50% retracement of the Ext-to-PullExt leg. Short: Ext - 0.5*(Ext - PullExt). Long: Ext + 0.5*(PullExt - Ext).
Long: SweepState on a SessionLow is in stage 2 AND current bar is after the bar that set PullExt AND PullExt > Ext AND low <= FibEntry. Entry price = FibEntry.
Short: SweepState on a SessionHigh is in stage 2 AND current bar is after the bar that set PullExt AND PullExt < Ext AND high >= FibEntry. Entry price = FibEntry.
Exit: long, low <= Ext (stop) OR high >= entry price + 2*(entry price - Ext) (target)
Exit: short, high >= Ext (stop) OR low <= entry price - 2*(Ext - entry price) (target)
