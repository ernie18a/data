Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = Track the highest high while moving up and the lowest low while moving down; confirm a pivot when close reverses from that extreme by the ATR-based reversal threshold (ATR length and threshold multiplier are parameters). Let p1 be the latest confirmed pivot, p2 the prior pivot, and so on; extP is the current unconfirmed extreme.
Calculation: Bullish wave count = With upward direction: Wave B takes priority when the prior count at the last direction flip was Wave A, p2 > p1, and (extP − p1)/(p2 − p1) is 0.30–0.85; otherwise Wave 5 when p3 > p5, p1 > p4, p2 > p4, and p4−p5 and p2−p3 are positive; otherwise Wave 3 when p1 > p3 and p2 > p3; otherwise Wave 1. With downward direction, use the bearish wave count definition.
Calculation: Bearish wave count = With downward direction, Wave A when at least six pivots exist, p4 > p6, p3 > p5, p2 > p5, p1 > p3, the lengths p5−p6, p3−p4, and p1−p2 are positive, and p3−p4 is not shorter than both other lengths; otherwise Wave 4 when p2 > p4, p1 > p3, p1 > p2, and p3 > p4; otherwise Wave C when p3 > p1 > p2 and (p1−p2)/(p3−p2) is 0.30–0.95; otherwise Wave 2 when p1 > p2.
Calculation: Entry target zone = For Wave 2, [p1−0.618×(p1−p2), p1−0.500×(p1−p2)]. For Wave 4, let l3=p1−p2 and l1=p3−p4; zone is [max(p1−0.500×l3,p3), p1−0.382×l3]. For Wave C, let r=(p1−p2)/(p3−p2), lenA=p3−p2; if r≥0.85, zone is [p2−0.236×lenA,p2], otherwise [p1−1.618×lenA,p1−1.000×lenA].
Calculation: Bullish reversal bar = Bullish engulfing (close>open, prior close<prior open, close≥prior open, open≤prior close), or hammer (body>0, lower wick>2×body, upper wick<body), or recovery (close>prior high and prior close<prior open).
Calculation: Bearish reversal bar = Bearish engulfing (close<open, prior close>prior open, close≤prior open, open≥prior close), or shooting star (body>0, upper wick>2×body, lower wick<body), or break low (close<prior low and prior close>prior open).
Calculation: Bear structure = Downward direction, at least five confirmed pivots, p1<p3<p5, p2<p4, and extP<p2; retain this state until close exceeds the latest bearish structure-break level, initially p1 and updated to a lower p1 on each confirmed downward pivot.
Long: (Wave 2 OR Wave 4 OR Wave C) AND close≥entry target zone lower bound AND close≤entry target zone upper bound AND bullish reversal bar
Short: Bear structure
Exit: Long, Wave 5 AND close≥Wave 5 target lower bound AND bearish reversal bar
Exit: Long, active Wave 2/Wave 4/Wave C count AND close below its invalidation level (Wave 2: p2; Wave 4: p3; Wave C: p1)
Exit: Short, close>bear structure-break level
