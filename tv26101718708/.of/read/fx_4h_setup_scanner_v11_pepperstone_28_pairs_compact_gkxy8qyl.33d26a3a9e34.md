Calculation: All prices and Bollinger Bands are evaluated on the last completed scan-timeframe bar, with preceding bars in the same timeframe; the scan timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); previous body = abs(previous close - previous open).
Calculation: Bullish signal candle = close > open AND ((high > previous high AND low < previous low) OR (previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND body > previous body) OR (previous close < previous open AND previous body > 0 AND body > 0 AND abs(body - previous body) / previous body <= body tolerance AND abs(open - previous close) <= price tolerance AND abs(close - previous open) <= price tolerance)).
Calculation: Bearish signal candle = close < open AND ((high > previous high AND low < previous low) OR (previous close > previous open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND body > previous body) OR (previous close > previous open AND previous body > 0 AND body > 0 AND abs(body - previous body) / previous body <= body tolerance AND abs(open - previous close) <= price tolerance AND abs(close - previous open) <= price tolerance)).
Calculation: Bollinger touch count = number of the upper, middle and lower Bollinger Bands for which low <= band AND high >= band on the signal candle.
Long: high > low AND bullish signal candle AND Bollinger touch count < 2 AND close >= low + (high - low) * close retracement fraction AND (close > middle Bollinger Band + continuation price buffer OR (high < middle Bollinger Band AND low <= lower Bollinger Band on the signal candle or any candle within the preceding reversal lookback)).
Short: high > low AND bearish signal candle AND Bollinger touch count < 2 AND close <= high - (high - low) * close retracement fraction AND (close < middle Bollinger Band - continuation price buffer OR (low > middle Bollinger Band AND high >= upper Bollinger Band on the signal candle or any candle within the preceding reversal lookback)).
