Calculation: Bollinger middle = simple moving average of close; upper/lower bands = middle ± a configurable multiple of close standard deviation over the same lookback
Calculation: Bullish signal candle = bullish outside bar (high > prior high and low < prior low) OR bullish engulfing candle (prior bearish, current bullish, open ≤ prior close, close ≥ prior open, current body > prior body) OR bullish railroad candle (prior bearish, current bullish, both bodies nonzero and relative body-size difference within a configurable tolerance, and current open/close each within a configurable price tolerance of prior close/open respectively); bearish signal candle is the inverse
Calculation: Signal close retracement = (close − low) / (high − low) for bullish candles; (high − close) / (high − low) for bearish candles
Calculation: Outer-band involvement = signal candle or any of the configurable number of preceding candles has low ≤ lower band for bullish setups, or high ≥ upper band for bearish setups; use each candle’s corresponding band
Calculation: Two-band touch = candle range intersects at least two of the upper, middle, and lower Bollinger Bands
Long: high > low AND bullish signal candle AND NOT two-band touch AND signal close retracement ≥ configurable minimum AND ((close > Bollinger middle + configurable pip buffer) OR (high < Bollinger middle AND lower-band involvement))
Short: high > low AND bearish signal candle AND NOT two-band touch AND signal close retracement ≥ configurable minimum AND ((close < Bollinger middle − configurable pip buffer) OR (low > Bollinger middle AND upper-band involvement))
