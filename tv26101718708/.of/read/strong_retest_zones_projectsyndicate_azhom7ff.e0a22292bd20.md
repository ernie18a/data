Calculation: Zone state = Maintain one pivot-low zone and one pivot-high zone. When its side is enabled and has no active zone, create a zone on confirmation of a pivot using configurable left and right pivot lengths. Optionally require the confirmation bar to be within a configurable window of the end of the dataset. Zone height is a configurable multiple of current ATR or a configurable percentage of current close; use cumulative mean high-minus-low until ATR is available. A normalized zone is centered on the pivot; otherwise a pivot-low zone extends upward from the pivot and a pivot-high zone extends downward. Optionally reject a new zone if it overlaps the other active zone or an archived zone by at least a configurable fraction of the smaller zone's height. In mutually exclusive mode, enable a side only while the other side has no active zone; otherwise enable the selected sides independently. A zone starts holding, becomes broken when close crosses beyond its far boundary, and retires when its age from the pivot bar reaches a configurable maximum or, after breaking, close crosses back beyond its opposite boundary. At retirement, archive it if its score passes the visibility filter, except discard an unbroken zone with no counted touches. Retain at most a configurable number of archived zones, deleting the oldest first. Process the pivot-low side before the pivot-high side on each bar.
Calculation: Zone score = At creation set touches, wick sum and wick count to zero, volume sum to current volume, and last-touch bar to the confirmation bar. On each subsequent active bar before retirement add current volume; on an eligible touch increment touches and wick count, add max(zone top minus low, 0) for an upward rejection or max(high minus zone bottom, 0) for a downward rejection, and set last-touch bar to the current bar. Divide touches by a configurable normalization and cap at 1; divide average accumulated volume per bar of age since the pivot by current volume SMA and cap at 1, or use 0 if unavailable or nonpositive; divide average rejection wick by current ATR and cap at 1, or use 0 if unavailable or nonpositive. Take their configurable weighted average, or 0 if the weight sum is zero, multiply by 10 and by an optional linear idle-decay factor that falls from 1 to a configurable floor over a configurable number of bars since last touch, and clamp to 0–10. For a touch's visibility test, include that touch and current volume and use zero idle age. Freeze the score on retirement.
Calculation: Eligible touch = Touch marking is enabled AND the zone has no existing marker OR touch marking is set to keep only the latest marker OR the number of bars since its latest existing marker exceeds a configurable gap. Apply the enabled condition to all alternatives. A marker is created only if the score visibility filter passes; retain a configurable maximum number of markers per zone.
Long: An enabled active pivot-low zone is holding AND its age is below the maximum AND low <= zone top AND close > zone top AND the touch is eligible AND the post-touch score passes the optional minimum-score filter.
Long: An enabled active pivot-high zone is broken AND its age is below the maximum AND low <= zone top AND close > zone top AND the touch is eligible AND the post-touch score passes the optional minimum-score filter.
Short: An enabled active pivot-high zone is holding AND its age is below the maximum AND high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND the touch is eligible AND the post-touch score passes the optional minimum-score filter.
Short: An enabled active pivot-low zone is broken AND its age is below the maximum AND high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND the touch is eligible AND the post-touch score passes the optional minimum-score filter.
Long: An active visible or archived pivot-low zone exists AND absolute distance from close to its midpoint <= a configurable percentage of close.
Short: An active visible or archived pivot-high zone exists AND absolute distance from close to its midpoint <= a configurable percentage of close.
