Calculation: Price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Filtered price = low-pass output L, where high-pass output P = price - previous price + (1 - high-pass alpha) × previous P and L = low-pass alpha × P + (1 - low-pass alpha) × previous L; initialize missing previous P to zero and missing previous L to current P.
Calculation: Fractal dimension for a lookback = clamp(log10(n) / (log10(n) + log10(d / path)), 1, 2), where n is the lookback subject to a minimum of 16 bars, path is the sum of absolute consecutive price changes across those n bars, and d is the absolute difference between the current and oldest price; replace zero or unavailable path or d with 1e-10, and use 1.5 if the denominator is zero. Fractal score = average of 3 - 2 × fractal dimension over short, core, and long lookbacks, respectively.
Calculation: Hurst exponent = the average of the available adjacent slopes of log(rescaled range) against log(lookback) across three increasing lookbacks, clamped to [0.05, 0.95]; use 0.5 if neither slope is available. For each lookback, rescaled range is the range of cumulative deviations of close from its lookback SMA, accumulated from the current bar backward, divided by the lookback standard deviation of close; treat a zero range or standard deviation as a zero rescaled range and exclude any slope involving it. Hurst score = 2 × (Hurst exponent - 0.5).
Calculation: Normalized entropy = Shannon entropy divided by log(number of bins), clamped to [0, 1]. Maintain integer bin counts initialized to zero; on each bar multiply each count by 1 - 2 / (entropy lookback + 1) and round to the nearest integer, then increment the bin of the current price change. Map that change into equally spaced bins using the minimum and maximum price changes over the entropy lookback, clamping the bin index to the valid range; use 1e-12 for a zero range and skip the increment when the range or change is unavailable. Entropy probabilities are counts divided by total counts, or zero when the total is zero. Entropy score = 1 - 2 × normalized entropy.
Calculation: Momentum score = EMA of the robust z-score of filtered price, clamped to [-3, 3] and divided by 3. The z-score uses a short-lookback median and the short-lookback median of the bar-by-bar absolute difference between filtered price and its contemporaneous rolling median; divide the current deviation by 1.4826 times that median absolute deviation, substituting the short-lookback standard deviation of filtered price when the deviation is zero, and return zero when the denominator is zero. Apply a minimum lookback of 5 bars.
Calculation: Microtrend score = zero when the optional lower-timeframe sampler is disabled; otherwise sample the last completed lower-timeframe close within each chart bar, calculate its chart-bar change and chart-bar rolling standard deviation over a lookback equal to the greater of 20 bars and half the short lookback, and divide the short SMA of those changes by that standard deviation; return zero for zero standard deviation, otherwise clamp the result to [-3, 3] and divide by 3.
Calculation: Trend pillar = (momentum score + fractal score + Hurst score) / 3. Trend bias = clamp((Hurst score + 1 - 2 × fractal rank) / 2, -1, 1), where fractal rank is the current core-lookback fractal dimension's position between its rolling minimum and maximum over the core lookback, clamped to [0, 1], or 0.5 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Composite oscillator = EMA of clamp(weighted trend pillar + weighted entropy score + weighted microtrend score, -1, 1). Raw trend, entropy, and microtrend weights are clamp(0.60 + 0.40 × trend bias, 0.10, 0.95), clamp(0.40 - 0.40 × trend bias, 0.05, 0.90), and clamp(0.20 + 0.30 × trend bias, 0, 0.50), respectively. Set the microtrend weight to zero when its sampler is disabled; scale all weights by 0.95 divided by their sum.
Calculation: Confidence = clamp(1 - dispersion, 0, 1), where dispersion is the absolute difference between trend pillar and entropy score when the lower-timeframe sampler is disabled, or the average of that difference and the absolute difference between trend pillar and microtrend score when enabled.
Calculation: Chop = absolute composite oscillator <= neutral threshold OR Hurst exponent < persistence threshold OR normalized entropy > entropy ceiling.
Long: Composite oscillator < negative contrarian extreme threshold.
Short: Composite oscillator > positive contrarian extreme threshold.
Long: Composite oscillator > positive thrust threshold AND confidence > thrust confidence threshold.
Short: Composite oscillator < negative thrust threshold AND confidence > thrust confidence threshold.
Long: Composite oscillator crosses below negative mean-reversion threshold AND chop.
Short: Composite oscillator crosses above positive mean-reversion threshold AND chop.
Long: Composite oscillator > positive neutral threshold AND trend pillar > 0 AND NOT chop.
Short: Composite oscillator < negative neutral threshold AND trend pillar < 0 AND NOT chop.
Long: Composite oscillator crosses above positive neutral threshold AND NOT chop.
Short: Composite oscillator crosses below negative neutral threshold AND NOT chop.
Long: Composite oscillator crosses above positive trend-entry threshold AND trend pillar > 0 AND NOT chop AND confidence > trend-entry confidence threshold.
Short: Composite oscillator crosses below negative trend-entry threshold AND trend pillar < 0 AND NOT chop AND confidence > trend-entry confidence threshold.
Exit: Long, absolute composite oscillator <= neutral threshold OR confidence < exit confidence threshold.
Exit: Short, absolute composite oscillator <= neutral threshold OR confidence < exit confidence threshold.
Exit: Long, composite oscillator >= zero OR composite oscillator >= positive mean-reversion target threshold.
Exit: Short, composite oscillator <= zero OR composite oscillator <= negative mean-reversion target threshold.
