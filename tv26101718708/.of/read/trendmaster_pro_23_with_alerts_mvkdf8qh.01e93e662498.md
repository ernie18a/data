Calculation: Signal MA = EMA, SMA, or SMMA of close, using the same MA type for the shorter and longer periods.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic K = SMA of the raw stochastic oscillator; D = SMA of K using the same smoothing period.
Calculation: Stop price = entry-signal close adjusted by a configured percentage against the position, OR the most recent intersection value of the shorter and longer signal MAs, OR the signal-bar low-band MA for a long or high-band MA for a short. The band MA is an EMA, SMA, or SMMA of low or high, respectively. At an MA cross, intersection value = shorter MA - change in shorter MA × (shorter MA - longer MA) / (change in shorter MA - change in longer MA).
Calculation: Target price = position entry price + position direction × risk-reward ratio × absolute value of (position entry price - stop price), where position direction is +1 for long and -1 for short.
Long: Shorter signal MA crosses above longer signal MA AND Bollinger upper band minus lower band > ATR with the Bollinger lookback × Bollinger deviation multiplier AND close > Bollinger middle band AND RSI > long threshold AND MACD line > signal line AND smoothed stochastic K > oversold threshold AND K > D AND ADX > strength threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > chart-timeframe trend MA).
Short: Shorter signal MA crosses below longer signal MA AND Bollinger upper band minus lower band > ATR with the Bollinger lookback × Bollinger deviation multiplier AND close < Bollinger middle band AND RSI < short threshold AND MACD line < signal line AND smoothed stochastic K < overbought threshold AND K < D AND ADX > strength threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < chart-timeframe trend MA).
Exit: Long, shorter signal MA crosses below longer signal MA AND Bollinger upper band minus lower band > ATR with the Bollinger lookback × Bollinger deviation multiplier AND close < Bollinger middle band AND RSI < short threshold AND MACD line < signal line AND smoothed stochastic K < overbought threshold AND K < D AND ADX > strength threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < chart-timeframe trend MA).
Exit: Short, shorter signal MA crosses above longer signal MA AND Bollinger upper band minus lower band > ATR with the Bollinger lookback × Bollinger deviation multiplier AND close > Bollinger middle band AND RSI > long threshold AND MACD line > signal line AND smoothed stochastic K > oversold threshold AND K > D AND ADX > strength threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > chart-timeframe trend MA).
Exit: Long, stop enabled AND low <= stop price OR target enabled AND high >= target price.
Exit: Short, stop enabled AND high >= stop price OR target enabled AND low <= target price.
Exit: Long, bar opening hour and minute equal the configured market-close hour and minute.
Exit: Short, bar opening hour and minute equal the configured market-close hour and minute.
