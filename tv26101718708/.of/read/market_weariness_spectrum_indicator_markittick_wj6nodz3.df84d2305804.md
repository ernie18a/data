Calculation: Weariness = SMA of the weighted average of three dimensions, with weights divided by their sum. Use one primary lookback throughout: (1) 100 times the min-max normalization of SMA(volume) / (SMA(abs(close - open)) + 1e-10); (2) 100 times the fraction of bars where abs(close - open) < 0.4 * ATR; (3) 100 times the min-max normalization of SMA(volume / (ATR + 1e-10)). Both normalizations use rolling minima and maxima over three times the primary lookback and add 1e-10 to the maximum-minus-minimum denominator. The final SMA uses a separate smoothing period.
Long: Weariness two bars ago >= recovery threshold AND weariness one bar ago < recovery threshold AND the highest weariness over the longer confirmation lookback ending one bar ago > exhaustion threshold.
Short: Weariness one bar ago < weariness two bars ago AND weariness two bars ago < weariness three bars ago AND weariness one bar ago > recovery threshold AND the highest weariness over the shorter confirmation lookback ending one bar ago > exhaustion threshold.
Calculation: The shorter confirmation lookback is the confirmation parameter plus 2 bars; the longer confirmation lookback is the same parameter plus 7 bars. The exhaustion threshold is higher than the recovery threshold.
