Calculation: Body = absolute value of close minus open; upper wick = high minus max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) minus low.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close AND body >= previous body times the engulfing ratio. Bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close AND body >= previous body times the engulfing ratio.
Calculation: Hammer = body > 0 AND lower wick >= body times the pin ratio AND upper wick <= half the body. Shooting star = body > 0 AND upper wick >= body times the pin ratio AND lower wick <= half the body.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = enabled bullish engulfing OR enabled hammer. Bearish pattern = enabled bearish engulfing OR enabled shooting star.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend components use the last completed higher-timeframe bar: fast EMA above slow EMA for bullish or below for bearish; and slow EMA greater than its value a trend-lookback number of chart bars earlier for bullish or less for bearish. The fast EMA has a shorter period than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Bullish structure = the latest confirmed pivot high > the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the latest confirmed pivot low > the preceding confirmed pivot low. Bearish structure uses < for both. Confirm pivots using equal numbers of chart bars on each side.
Calculation: Pullback depth = highest high minus lowest low over the pullback lookback, including the evaluated bar. Larger range = highest high minus lowest low over twice that lookback.
Calculation: Long stop = minimum of the entry-bar low over the mode-specific stop lookback minus an ATR buffer and entry price minus an ATR multiple. Short stop = maximum of the entry-bar high over the mode-specific stop lookback plus an ATR buffer and entry price plus an ATR multiple. The trend-pullback stop lookback equals the pullback lookback; the mean-reversion stop lookback is separately parameterized. Freeze stops at entry.
Calculation: Trend-pullback first and second targets = entry price plus, for long, or minus, for short, the entry-to-stop risk times the respective target risk multiple; the second multiple is larger. Mean-reversion target = entry-bar Bollinger middle band. Freeze targets at entry.
Calculation: Reward-to-risk = target distance in the trade direction divided by entry-to-stop risk when risk > 0; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Signal spacing = chart bars since the most recent long or short signal; daily signal count = number of bars with at least one long or short signal on the current calendar day, reset when the day of month changes. Evaluate both directions before updating either counter; simultaneous signals count once. Spacing and daily cap are mode-specific.
Long: Trend-pullback mode AND on the previous chart bar bullish pattern AND at least two of bullish higher-timeframe EMA ordering, bullish structure, and bullish higher-timeframe EMA slope AND pullback-lookback lowest low <= fast chart-timeframe EMA plus ATR times touch tolerance AND pullback depth >= ATR times minimum depth AND pullback depth <= larger range times maximum depth fraction AND current high > previous high AND reward-to-risk >= rejection threshold AND signal spacing >= minimum spacing AND daily signal count < daily cap.
Short: Trend-pullback mode AND on the previous chart bar bearish pattern AND at least two of bearish higher-timeframe EMA ordering, bearish structure, and bearish higher-timeframe EMA slope AND pullback-lookback highest high >= fast chart-timeframe EMA minus ATR times touch tolerance AND pullback depth >= ATR times minimum depth AND pullback depth <= larger range times maximum depth fraction AND current low < previous low AND reward-to-risk >= rejection threshold AND signal spacing >= minimum spacing AND daily signal count < daily cap.
Long: Mean-reversion mode AND previous low <= previous lower Bollinger band AND previous RSI <= oversold threshold AND enabled hammer on the previous bar AND close > lower Bollinger band AND reward-to-risk >= rejection threshold AND signal spacing >= minimum spacing AND daily signal count < daily cap.
Short: Mean-reversion mode AND previous high >= previous upper Bollinger band AND previous RSI >= overbought threshold AND enabled shooting star on the previous bar AND close < upper Bollinger band AND reward-to-risk >= rejection threshold AND signal spacing >= minimum spacing AND daily signal count < daily cap.
Exit: long, low <= frozen long stop OR high >= frozen first target OR trend-pullback mode AND high >= frozen second target OR trend-pullback mode AND first target was hit on an earlier bar AND close < trailing EMA.
Exit: short, high >= frozen short stop OR low <= frozen first target OR trend-pullback mode AND low <= frozen second target OR trend-pullback mode AND first target was hit on an earlier bar AND close > trailing EMA.
