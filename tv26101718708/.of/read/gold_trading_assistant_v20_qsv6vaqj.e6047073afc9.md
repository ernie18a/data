Calculation: Bullish trend = at least two of: fast EMA > slow EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; current-chart confirmed pivot highs and lows form higher highs and higher lows; slow EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is above its value the configured number of higher-timeframe bars earlier

Calculation: Day-mode stop-loss = for a long, the lower of the lowest low over the configured pullback lookback minus the configured ATR buffer and entry price minus the configured ATR floor; for a short, the higher of the highest high over that lookback plus the ATR buffer and entry price plus the ATR floor
Calculation: Scalp-mode stop-loss = for a long, the lower of the lowest low over the configured extreme lookback minus the configured ATR buffer and entry price minus the configured ATR floor; for a short, the higher of the highest high over that lookback plus the ATR buffer and entry price plus the ATR floor
Calculation: Day-mode second profit target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the distance from entry to stop-loss multiplied by the configured second reward-to-risk multiple
Long: Day mode AND previous bar had a bullish reversal pattern AND previous bar had a bullish trend AND previous bar had a bullish pullback AND current high > previous high; OR Scalp mode AND previous bar had a hammer with previous high ≥ previous upper Bollinger Band and previous RSI ≥ configured overbought level AND current close > current lower Bollinger Band; AND first-target reward-to-risk ratio ≥ configured rejection threshold; AND configured bar-close confirmation passes; AND bars since the last signal ≥ configured spacing and signals today < configured daily cap
