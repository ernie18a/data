Calculation: Bullish engulfing = previous close < previous open AND close > open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close AND close - open > previous open - previous close AND high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close > previous high.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = previous close > previous open AND close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND open - close > previous close - previous open AND high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close < previous low.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of (typical price - EMA of typical price) divided by a scale factor times the EMA of the absolute difference between typical price and its EMA; the typical-price EMA and absolute-difference EMA share a lookback, and the final EMA uses a separate lookback.
Calculation: Trading window = configured weekday and intraday time comparisons in the specified timezone; a weekday is allowed after its start time, and the final trading day is allowed through its cutoff time.
Calculation: Bullish gap = the low of a completed block of chart bars exceeds the high of the block two blocks earlier; bearish gap = the high of that completed block is below the low of the block two blocks earlier. Blocks are consecutive, equal-sized and anchored to the first chart bar. A bullish gap remains active until, at a subsequent block boundary, close < its lower edge; a bearish gap remains active until, at a subsequent block boundary, close > its upper edge.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry-bar low for a long Bollinger or WaveTrend trade and entry-bar high for its short counterpart; the captured higher-timeframe low or high for an EMA-confirmation trade, falling back to the entry-bar low or high if unavailable; the first active touched bullish gap's lower edge minus a buffer for a long gap trade, or the first active touched bearish gap's upper edge plus a buffer for a short gap trade. Risk = signal-bar close minus initial stop for a long, or initial stop minus signal-bar close for a short.
Long: Bullish engulfing.
Short: Bearish engulfing.
Long: Short-lookback close EMA crosses above long-lookback close EMA.
Short: Short-lookback close EMA crosses below long-lookback close EMA.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, WaveTrend crosses below its oversold threshold on any of the configured timeframes AND the chart timeframe is the base timeframe or its own WaveTrend is below the oversold threshold. The other WaveTrend timeframes are progressively higher than the base timeframe and use their still-forming bars.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, WaveTrend crosses above its overbought threshold on any of the configured timeframes AND the chart timeframe is the base timeframe or its own WaveTrend is above the overbought threshold. The other WaveTrend timeframes are progressively higher than the base timeframe and use their still-forming bars.
Long: Bullish engulfing AND open < chart-timeframe lower Bollinger band AND a higher-timeframe close < its lower Bollinger band was detected within the subsequent configured chart-bar window. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar at the configured detection point; consume the window when an allowed trade is submitted.
Short: Bearish engulfing AND open > chart-timeframe upper Bollinger band AND a higher-timeframe close > its upper Bollinger band was detected within the subsequent configured chart-bar window. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar at the configured detection point; consume the window when an allowed trade is submitted.
Long: Bullish engulfing AND Supertrend is up AND a higher-timeframe short-lookback close EMA crossed above its long-lookback close EMA at the start of a new higher-timeframe bar within the subsequent configured chart-bar window. Use the last completed higher-timeframe values and capture the higher-timeframe low for the stop; consume the window when an allowed trade is submitted.
Short: Bearish engulfing AND Supertrend is down AND a higher-timeframe short-lookback close EMA crossed above its long-lookback close EMA at the start of a new higher-timeframe bar within the subsequent configured chart-bar window. Use the last completed higher-timeframe values and capture the higher-timeframe high for the stop; consume the window when an allowed trade is submitted.
Long: Short-lookback close EMA crosses above long-lookback close EMA AND a higher-timeframe bullish engulfing was detected on the chart bar after a new higher-timeframe bar began within the subsequent configured chart-bar window. Use the last completed higher-timeframe values and capture the higher-timeframe low for the stop; consume the window when an allowed trade is submitted.
Short: Short-lookback close EMA crosses below long-lookback close EMA AND a higher-timeframe bearish engulfing was detected on the chart bar after a new higher-timeframe bar began within the subsequent configured chart-bar window. Use the last completed higher-timeframe values and capture the higher-timeframe high for the stop; consume the window when an allowed trade is submitted.
Long: Bullish engulfing AND, on the current bar or within a configured number of preceding chart bars, all configured WaveTrend timeframes were below the oversold threshold AND at least one crossed below that threshold on a confirmed chart bar. Use still-forming bars for the higher timeframes; consume the window on the first qualifying engulfing even outside the trading window.
Short: Bearish engulfing AND, on the current bar or within a configured number of preceding chart bars, all configured WaveTrend timeframes were above the overbought threshold AND at least one crossed above that threshold on a confirmed chart bar. Use still-forming bars for the higher timeframes; consume the window on the first qualifying engulfing even outside the trading window.
Long: Bullish engulfing AND Supertrend is up AND low >= an active bullish gap's lower edge AND high <= that gap's upper edge.
Short: Bearish engulfing AND Supertrend is down AND low >= an active bearish gap's lower edge AND high <= that gap's upper edge.
Exit: Long or short, current bar is outside the trading window AND previous bar was inside it.
Exit: Long, before the first profit target is reached, short-lookback close EMA crosses below long-lookback close EMA.
Exit: Short, before the first profit target is reached, short-lookback close EMA crosses above long-lookback close EMA.
Exit: Long, low <= initial stop OR high >= entry signal close + risk for half the entry quantity OR high >= entry signal close + twice risk for the remaining quantity; after high reaches position average entry price + risk, move the remaining stop to position average entry price and set its target to position average entry price + twice risk.
Exit: Short, high >= initial stop OR low <= entry signal close - risk for half the entry quantity OR low <= entry signal close - twice risk for the remaining quantity; after low reaches position average entry price - risk, move the remaining stop to position average entry price and set its target to position average entry price - twice risk.
