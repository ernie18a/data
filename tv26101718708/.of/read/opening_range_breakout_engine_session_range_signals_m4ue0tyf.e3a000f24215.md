Calculation: Trend score = clamp(score midpoint + distance weight × clamp((shorter-period EMA of close − longer-period EMA of close) / ATR, −distance limit, distance limit) + momentum weight × (RSI of close − RSI midpoint), score minimum, score maximum); use zero for the normalized EMA distance when ATR is not positive. The optional agreement score uses the same calculation on another timeframe: for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Range high and range low = the latest independently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, using equal left and right pivot lookbacks. Record each pivot's actual bar index, and reset its used flag when a new pivot of that type is confirmed.
Calculation: Initial risk = at entry, in swing-stop mode, use entry price − range low + ATR buffer for a long, or range high − entry price + ATR buffer for a short, if the unbuffered distance is less than the maximum ATR distance; otherwise use ATR × stop multiplier. Freeze risk and ATR-derived entry levels at entry.
Calculation: Trade management = enter at the signal bar's close and manage the position starting on the next bar. Initialize the long stop to entry price − initial risk and the short stop to entry price + initial risk. On each subsequent bar, first tighten the long stop to max(previous stop, high − current ATR × trail multiplier), or the short stop to min(previous stop, low + current ATR × trail multiplier), when trailing mode is selected or trailing after break-even is enabled and break-even was already active before this bar. Next, if enabled and not already active, activate break-even when high reaches entry price + initial risk × break-even trigger for a long, or low reaches entry price − initial risk × break-even trigger for a short. Also activate break-even when the enabled first partial target is reached. On activation, tighten the stop to at least entry price for a long or at most entry price for a short. Check the updated stop before the full target; after a full exit, evaluate entries on the same bar, with long taking priority. Record a loss for cooldown only when the stop closes a trade before its first partial target, break-even was not activated, and stop profit does not exceed a price-increment-based profit threshold; retain the bar and side of the latest such loss.
Long: Position is flat AND the latest range high has not been used for a long entry AND both range extremes exist AND range high − range low >= current ATR × minimum range multiplier AND bars since the range-high pivot <= maximum age AND close > range high + current ATR × breakout buffer AND trend score >= score midpoint AND trend score >= long strength threshold AND (agreement disabled OR agreement score >= score midpoint) AND close <= EMA of highs + current ATR × maximum stretch multiplier AND (cooldown disabled OR latest loss was not long OR bars since latest loss >= cooldown).
Short: Position is flat AND the latest range low has not been used for a short entry AND both range extremes exist AND range high − range low >= current ATR × minimum range multiplier AND bars since the range-low pivot <= maximum age AND close < range low − current ATR × breakout buffer AND trend score < score midpoint AND score maximum − trend score >= short strength threshold AND (agreement disabled OR agreement score < score midpoint) AND close >= EMA of lows − current ATR × maximum stretch multiplier AND (cooldown disabled OR latest loss was not short OR bars since latest loss >= cooldown).
Exit: long, first partial target enabled AND first partial target not previously reached AND high >= entry price + initial risk × partial-target multiple.
Exit: short, first partial target enabled AND first partial target not previously reached AND low <= entry price − initial risk × partial-target multiple.
Exit: long, low <= updated stop OR (fixed-target mode AND low > updated stop AND high >= entry price + initial risk × full-target multiple).
Exit: short, high >= updated stop OR (fixed-target mode AND high < updated stop AND low <= entry price − initial risk × full-target multiple).
