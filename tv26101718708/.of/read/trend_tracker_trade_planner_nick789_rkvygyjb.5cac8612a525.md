Calculation: Price source = a selectable OHLC price source, defaulting to (open + high + low + close) / 4.
Calculation: Tracker = choose either the shorter EMA of the price source as the basis or the average of a shorter and a longer EMA as the basis. Upper and lower bands are basis plus and minus an ATR multiplier times either Wilder ATR or the SMA of true range. Initialize the chart tracker direction bullish and its line at the lower band. On each subsequent bar, set direction bullish if the source exceeds the previous upper band, bearish if it is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retain the previous direction. In a bullish direction, the line is the maximum of the lower band and the previous line if the previous direction was bullish, otherwise the lower band. In a bearish direction, it is the minimum of the upper band and the previous line if the previous direction was bearish, otherwise the upper band. For the higher-timeframe tracker, initialize direction by comparing source >= basis; otherwise use the same rules with manual parameters. On historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on live chart bars use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Adaptive tracker = optionally run a grid of tracker candidates with progressively longer EMA and ATR lengths and progressively larger ATR multipliers. In the averaged-EMA mode, candidates share the manual longer EMA. After a warmup, start a simulated setup at each candidate direction flip, using that bar's close, candidate tracker and ATR, and the same initial risk and target calculations as below. Record each target and stop independently when touched, including on the entry bar; finalize the setup on the next candidate flip. After a minimum number of finalized setups, select the candidate with the highest selected score: a target hit rate, one minus the stop hit rate, the sum of target hits less a configurable stop penalty divided by setups, or a weighted sum giving successively more weight to farther target hits less the stop penalty, divided by setups. Alternatively select an interior candidate maximizing the mean score of eligible adjacent grid candidates including itself minus a configurable multiple of their population standard deviation. Break ties by first grid order. Use the selected candidate's current direction, line, bands and ATR; until a selection is available use the manual tracker. A change in the selected candidate can itself change the active direction.
Calculation: Trend volume delta = since the latest active tracker direction change, sum buy volume minus sell volume, including the current bar. Buy volume is the volume of up candles and sell volume is the volume of down candles; doji volume is excluded. Optionally sum completed lower-timeframe candles within each chart bar; fall back to the chart candle when lower-timeframe data is unavailable. Delta percentage is delta divided by buy plus sell volume, or zero when their sum is zero.
Calculation: Pending higher-timeframe signal = a chart direction flip that disagrees with the higher-timeframe direction, retained until the chart direction changes, the configured wait is exceeded, or higher-timeframe agreement first opens. The flip bar has elapsed wait zero.
Calculation: Initial risk = at setup entry, the larger of a configurable minimum risk and either a multiple of the entry-bar ATR or the signed distance from entry close to the tracker line: entry minus tracker for a long, tracker minus entry for a short. Target levels are entry plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, initial risk times each of three configurable reward ratios. The initial stop is entry minus risk for a long and entry plus risk for a short. Optionally update the stop on every bar, including the entry bar, to the greater of its previous value and the current tracker for a long, or the lesser for a short. Target and stop touches are recorded independently, including on the entry bar, until replaced by a new setup.
Long: Active chart tracker direction = bullish.
Short: Active chart tracker direction = bearish.
Long: Chart tracker direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe tracker direction = bullish).
Short: Chart tracker direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe tracker direction = bearish).
Long: Pending higher-timeframe signal side = bullish.
Short: Pending higher-timeframe signal side = bearish.
Long: Pending higher-timeframe signal side = bullish AND higher-timeframe agreement first opens before the configured wait is exceeded.
Short: Pending higher-timeframe signal side = bearish AND higher-timeframe agreement first opens before the configured wait is exceeded.
Long: A bullish filtered flip or delayed higher-timeframe signal occurred within the configured delta wait AND chart tracker direction remains bullish AND trend volume delta >= 0; use the first qualifying bar.
Short: A bearish filtered flip or delayed higher-timeframe signal occurred within the configured delta wait AND chart tracker direction remains bearish AND trend volume delta <= 0; use the first qualifying bar.
Long: Chart tracker direction = bearish AND volume > its SMA multiplied by a volume threshold AND close > low + (high - low) multiplied by a rejection threshold AND delta percentage > previous delta percentage AND (band-breach filter is disabled OR low < lower band).
Short: Chart tracker direction = bullish AND volume > its SMA multiplied by a volume threshold AND close < high - (high - low) multiplied by a rejection threshold AND delta percentage < previous delta percentage AND (band-breach filter is disabled OR high > upper band).
Exit: long, high >= any target level OR low <= stop level.
Exit: short, low <= any target level OR high >= stop level.
Exit: long, chart tracker direction = bullish AND volume > its SMA multiplied by a volume threshold AND close < high - (high - low) multiplied by a rejection threshold AND delta percentage < previous delta percentage AND (band-breach filter is disabled OR high > upper band).
Exit: short, chart tracker direction = bearish AND volume > its SMA multiplied by a volume threshold AND close > low + (high - low) multiplied by a rejection threshold AND delta percentage > previous delta percentage AND (band-breach filter is disabled OR low < lower band).
