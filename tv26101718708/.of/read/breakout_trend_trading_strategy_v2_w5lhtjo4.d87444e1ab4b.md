Calculation: Bullish bar = close > previous close AND ((Supertrend is bullish AND absolute close change > ATR × trend breakout multiplier AND volume > Hull moving average of volume × trend volume multiplier) OR (Supertrend is bearish AND absolute close change > ATR × reversal breakout multiplier AND volume > Hull moving average of volume × reversal volume multiplier) OR volume > Hull moving average of volume × high-volume multiplier OR absolute close change > ATR × high-breakout multiplier); an unavailable volume satisfies either trend or reversal volume comparison, but not the high-volume comparison.
Calculation: Bearish bar = close < previous close AND ((Supertrend is bearish AND absolute close change > ATR × trend breakout multiplier AND volume > Hull moving average of volume × trend volume multiplier) OR (Supertrend is bullish AND absolute close change > ATR × reversal breakout multiplier AND volume > Hull moving average of volume × reversal volume multiplier) OR volume > Hull moving average of volume × high-volume multiplier OR absolute close change > ATR × high-breakout multiplier); an unavailable volume satisfies either trend or reversal volume comparison, but not the high-volume comparison.
Calculation: Bullish target channel = most recent bullish bar high plus its true range multiplied by the trend target markup if Supertrend was bullish on that bar, otherwise by the reversal target markup.
Calculation: Bullish stop channel = most recent bullish bar low minus its ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish target channel = most recent bearish bar low minus its true range multiplied by the trend target markup if Supertrend was bearish on that bar, otherwise by the reversal target markup.
Calculation: Bearish stop channel = most recent bearish bar high plus its ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier.
Long: bullish bar = true
Short: bearish bar = true
Exit: long, signal exits enabled AND bullish bar = false AND ((close if Supertrend is bearish, otherwise low) crosses below the previous bar's bearish target channel OR crosses below the previous bar's bullish stop channel)
Exit: short, signal exits enabled AND bearish bar = false AND ((close if Supertrend is bullish, otherwise high) crosses above the previous bar's bullish target channel OR crosses above the previous bar's bearish stop channel)
