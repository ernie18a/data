Calculation: Trend/reverse price breakout = absolute change in close from the prior bar exceeds its ATR-based threshold; the trend and reverse thresholds are equal. High price breakout = the same close-change comparison using a larger ATR-based threshold.
Calculation: Trend/reverse volume breakout = volume exceeds its volume moving average by its configured markup, or volume is unavailable. Both use the same moving-average lookback. High volume breakout = volume exceeds that moving average times its configured high-volume multiple.
Calculation: Bar state = bullish when close > prior close and ((supertrend direction is bearish and trend price and volume breakouts hold) OR (supertrend direction is bullish and reverse price and volume breakouts hold) OR high volume breakout OR high price breakout); bearish when close < prior close and ((supertrend direction is bullish and trend price and volume breakouts hold) OR (supertrend direction is bearish and reverse price and volume breakouts hold) OR high volume breakout OR high price breakout); otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Up/down target and stop channels = on a bullish bar state, up target is high plus a configured percentage of the greater of the bar range and its gaps from prior close, and up stop is low minus ATR times a configured factor; on a bearish bar state, down target is low minus that percentage of the same range measure, and down stop is high plus ATR times that factor. Each channel retains its most recent non-null value.
Calculation: New high/low filter = highest/lowest over the shorter lookback equals highest/lowest over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Moving-average alignment score = sum over the configured lookback of the per-bar alignment score. Per bar, score the comparisons of close and consecutively longer moving averages (5, 10, 20, 30, 50, 100, 200 bars): fully bullish stack = 1; fully bearish stack = -1; otherwise, if partial alignment is enabled, score by the count of bullish comparisons: >5 gives 0.5, <2 gives -0.5, >3 gives 0.25, otherwise -0.25; if disabled, score 0.
Calculation: Yearly high/low filter = use the last completed higher-timeframe 12-month bar's high and low; compare them with the corresponding values from 365 and 730 chart bars earlier. High filter holds if current yearly high exceeds both available older highs, or close exceeds the applicable nested maximum of those highs, or the filter is disabled. Low filter holds if current yearly low is below both available older lows, or close is below the applicable nested minimum of those lows, or the filter is disabled. Missing older yearly values make their comparisons pass.
Calculation: Buy/sell signal = buy: supertrend direction is not bearish AND ((low crosses above prior bar's up target channel AND (last non-neutral bar state is bullish OR reverse breakouts are ignored) AND close > prior bar's down target channel) OR (low crosses above prior bar's down stop channel AND (last non-neutral bar state is bearish OR reverse breakouts are ignored))); sell: supertrend direction is not bullish AND ((high crosses below prior bar's down target channel AND (last non-neutral bar state is bearish OR reverse breakouts are ignored) AND close < prior bar's up target channel) OR (high crosses below prior bar's up stop channel AND (last non-neutral bar state is bullish OR reverse breakouts are ignored)). A cross requires the comparison to change from false to true between consecutive bars.
Calculation: Entry prerequisites = within configured backtest date window AND corresponding yearly high/low filter AND corresponding new high/low filter AND corresponding moving-average alignment filter. High alignment filter: summed score > configured threshold; low alignment filter: summed score < negative configured threshold. Each filter is disabled when its corresponding option is off.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = if prerequisites hold, long eligibility holds when supertrend direction is bearish and bar state is non-neutral, or the buy signal holds; short eligibility holds when supertrend direction is bullish and bar state is non-neutral, or the sell signal holds. Also require the corresponding moving-average supertrend direction and percent rank filter, unless disabled or cumulative strategy net profit is nonnegative; require weekly supertrend direction bearish for long and bullish for short when those filters are enabled. Weekly and monthly supertrend use higher-timeframe bars still forming. Percent rank compares OHLC4 with its values over the configured lookback; long requires >50 and short <50.
Long: bar state is bullish
Short: bar state is bearish
Long: entry eligibility holds AND the configured trade type permits reverse, breakout, or pullback entry AND the configured trade direction permits long AND (reverse entry with buy signal OR breakout entry with bearish supertrend direction and bullish bar state OR pullback entry with bearish supertrend direction and bearish bar state) AND the close satisfies the compound-entry condition, unless immediate compounding is enabled AND not (range condition AND Bollinger-band range exits enabled)
Short: entry eligibility holds AND the configured trade type permits reverse, breakout, or pullback entry AND the configured trade direction permits short AND (reverse entry with sell signal OR breakout entry with bullish supertrend direction and bearish bar state OR pullback entry with bullish supertrend direction and bullish bar state) AND the close satisfies the compound-entry condition, unless immediate compounding is enabled AND not (range condition AND Bollinger-band range exits enabled)
Long: range condition AND Bollinger-band range exits enabled AND close crosses above the lower Bollinger Band
Short: range condition AND Bollinger-band range exits enabled AND close crosses below the upper Bollinger Band
Exit: long, (stop-buy signal AND (exit-on-signal enabled OR (exit-on-signal-in-range enabled AND range condition))) OR range short-entry condition OR low <= long stop band
Exit: short, stop-sell signal AND (exit-on-signal enabled OR (exit-on-signal-in-range enabled AND range condition)) OR high >= short stop band
Calculation: Stop-buy/sell signal = stop-buy: crossunder of (close if supertrend is bearish, otherwise low) below either the prior down target channel or prior up stop channel; stop-sell: crossover of (close if supertrend is bullish, otherwise high) above either the prior up target channel or prior down stop channel.
Calculation: Range condition = a position is open and both ATR-based stop/compound bands equal their values from the configured number of bars earlier; while Bollinger-band range exits are enabled, the close must also remain between the prior bands.
Calculation: Long/short stop bands = derive the target from the reverse signal close, breakout target channel, pullback stop channel, or current bar low/high as applicable; offset it by ATR times the configured stop or compound factor according to bar-state and position side. Update bands recursively: while long, both bands cannot decrease; while short, neither band can increase. When trailing stops are enabled in range, update the bands in the same restrictive direction.
