Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, resetting at each daily boundary; the daily low and high reset at the same boundary.
Calculation: Percent change = 100 × (current value − value at the lookback) / current value; the VWAP lookback is longer than the EMA lookback.
Calculation: Flat-run length = the longest consecutive run since the most recent strict new daily low or high for which the absolute EMA percent change does not exceed its slope threshold; reset it at each daily boundary and each strict new daily extreme.
Calculation: Stop = anchor − stop fraction × (anchor − daily low) for longs, or anchor + stop fraction × (daily high − anchor) for shorts. In anticipatory mode the anchor is daily VWAP; in reactive mode choose daily VWAP or signal-bar close. Fix the stop at the signal bar.
Calculation: Target = cross reference + (cross reference − daily low) for longs, or cross reference − (daily high − cross reference) for shorts. The cross reference is daily VWAP in anticipatory mode; in reactive mode choose daily VWAP or signal-bar close. Fix the target at the signal bar.
Calculation: Raw trigger = (daily VWAP − (1 − alpha) × EMA) / alpha, where alpha = 2 / (EMA length + 1). The long trigger is the greater of raw trigger and close plus minimum price increment; the short trigger is the lesser of raw trigger and close minus minimum price increment.
Calculation: Entry reference = trigger in anticipatory mode, otherwise signal-bar close. Long reward-to-risk = (target − entry reference) / max(entry reference − stop, minimum price increment); short reward-to-risk = (entry reference − target) / max(stop − entry reference, minimum price increment).
Long: Longs enabled AND position flat AND bars since the most recent bar in a position or the bar that became flat >= cooldown AND EMA percent change > EMA slope threshold AND VWAP percent change <= VWAP slope threshold AND (turn filter disabled OR bars since the most recent strict daily low >= turn lookback) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is in either configured New York session window) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (flat veto disabled OR flat-run length < max(1, rounded veto duration divided by max(chart bar duration, 1 minute))) AND daily low < daily VWAP AND (minimum reward-to-risk <= 0 OR long reward-to-risk >= minimum reward-to-risk) AND ((anticipatory mode AND EMA < daily VWAP AND raw trigger > close AND stop < long trigger AND target > long trigger) OR (reactive mode AND EMA crosses above daily VWAP AND stop < close AND target > close)). In anticipatory mode this is also a long arm marking; place or refresh a buy stop at the long trigger for the next bar and cancel it if the condition no longer holds. In reactive mode enter at the next bar's open.
Short: Shorts enabled AND position flat AND bars since the most recent bar in a position or the bar that became flat >= cooldown AND EMA percent change < negative EMA slope threshold AND VWAP percent change >= negative VWAP slope threshold AND (turn filter disabled OR bars since the most recent strict daily high >= turn lookback) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is in either configured New York session window) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (flat veto disabled OR flat-run length < max(1, rounded veto duration divided by max(chart bar duration, 1 minute))) AND daily high > daily VWAP AND (minimum reward-to-risk <= 0 OR short reward-to-risk >= minimum reward-to-risk) AND ((anticipatory mode AND EMA > daily VWAP AND raw trigger < close AND stop > short trigger AND target < short trigger) OR (reactive mode AND EMA crosses below daily VWAP AND stop > close AND target < close)). In anticipatory mode this is also a short arm marking; place or refresh a sell stop at the short trigger for the next bar and cancel it if the condition no longer holds. In reactive mode enter at the next bar's open.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed target.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed target.
