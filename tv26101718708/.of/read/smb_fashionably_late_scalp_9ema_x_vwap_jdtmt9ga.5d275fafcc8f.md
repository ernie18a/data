Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of each bar’s (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the start of each day
Calculation: EMA slope change = (current EMA − EMA at the shorter slope lookback) / current EMA
Calculation: VWAP slope change = (current VWAP − VWAP at the longer slope lookback) / current VWAP
Calculation: Long stop = VWAP − stop fraction × (VWAP − current day low); short stop = VWAP + stop fraction × (current day high − VWAP)
Calculation: Long target = cross price + (cross price − current day low); short target = cross price − (current day high − cross price)
Long: (anticipate mode: EMA < VWAP AND projected price at which the next EMA meets VWAP > close; react mode: EMA crosses above VWAP) AND EMA slope change > EMA slope threshold AND VWAP slope change ≤ VWAP slope threshold AND bars since current day low ≥ turn lookback (if turn required) AND current time is in an allowed session window (if session filter enabled) AND stop < entry reference < target AND reward/risk ≥ minimum (unless minimum is disabled) AND volume > its longer-lookback average (if volume filter enabled) AND EMA has not remained flat for the veto duration (if flat veto enabled) AND flat position AND re-entry cooldown elapsed
Short: (anticipate mode: EMA > VWAP AND projected price at which the next EMA meets VWAP < close; react mode: EMA crosses below VWAP) AND EMA slope change < −EMA slope threshold AND VWAP slope change ≥ −VWAP slope threshold AND bars since current day high ≥ turn lookback (if turn required) AND current time is in an allowed session window (if session filter enabled) AND target < entry reference < stop AND reward/risk ≥ minimum (unless minimum is disabled) AND volume > its longer-lookback average (if volume filter enabled) AND EMA has not remained flat for the veto duration (if flat veto enabled) AND flat position AND re-entry cooldown elapsed
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target
