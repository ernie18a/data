Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right swing lengths; retain the most recent 30 of each side. A pool is eligible while its pivot age is at most the validity lookback and it has not been taken. On each bar, mark an eligible low pool taken if close < its level or low < its level AND close > its level; mark an eligible high pool taken if close > its level or high > its level AND close < its level. Evaluate newest pools first; if several are swept on one bar, use the newest swept level for scoring and risk.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < an eligible low pool AND close > its level. Bearish sweep = high > an eligible high pool AND close < its level.
Calculation: Bullish structure = close > the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish structure = close < the latest confirmed pivot low. These pivots use equal left and right structure lengths, independently of the liquidity swing length.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND high - low >= ATR × range multiplier AND (body filter disabled OR abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction). Bearish displacement = close < open AND the same range and body conditions; the body fraction is zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars ago. Bearish gap = high < low two bars ago. A directional gap is recent when its latest occurrence is within the gap-validity lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Directional score = min(100, 0.25 × sweep component + 0.20 × displacement component + 0.20 × structure component + 0.10 × volume component + 0.10 × higher-timeframe component + 0.10 × range-position component + 0.05 × gap component). Sweep component = min(100, 100 × sweep depth / ATR) when the directional sweep exists and ATR > 0, otherwise 0; depth is swept low level - low for bullish and high - swept high level for bearish. Displacement component = 100 if directional displacement exists, otherwise 20, even when displacement is not required. Structure component = 100 if its filter is disabled or directional structure exists, otherwise 15. Volume component = 100 if its filter is disabled or volume > 0 AND volume SMA > 0 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier; otherwise 35. Higher-timeframe component = 100 if its filter is disabled or the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for bullish or below its EMA for bearish; otherwise 20. Range-position component = 100 if its filter is disabled or close is at or below the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low for bullish or at or above it for bearish; otherwise 20. Gap component = 100 if its filter is disabled or a directional gap is recent; otherwise 20.
Calculation: Qualified signal = directional sweep AND directional score >= minimum score AND bars since the last qualified signal of either direction > cooldown, or no prior qualified signal exists; AND directional displacement if required; AND directional structure if required. Evaluate both directions before updating the last qualified signal bar; if both qualify, retain bullish only when its score > bearish score, otherwise retain bearish. Update the last qualified signal bar even when a position is already open.
Calculation: Entry risk = max(entry close - (swept low level - entry-bar ATR × stop buffer), entry-bar ATR × minimum risk multiplier) for a long, or max((swept high level + entry-bar ATR × stop buffer) - entry close, entry-bar ATR × minimum risk multiplier) for a short. Initial stop = entry price minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short. First and final targets = entry price plus risk × the respective target multiplier for a long or minus it for a short; the final target multiplier is greater than the first. If break-even is enabled, move the stop to entry when the first target is touched on a bar after the latest qualified signal bar, provided the initial stop is not touched on that bar. Check the updated stop on that same bar.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: Bullish gap.
Short: Bearish gap.
Long: Bullish structure.
Short: Bearish structure AND bullish structure is false.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA.
Long: Qualified bullish signal AND position is flat AND ATR > 0.
Short: Qualified bearish signal AND position is flat AND ATR > 0.
Exit: Long, low <= current stop OR final-target closing is enabled AND high >= final target.
Exit: Short, high >= current stop OR final-target closing is enabled AND low <= final target.
