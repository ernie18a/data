Calculation: Bar delta = sum over completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero when close = open.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of chart-bar delta, optionally reset at each exchange-day session boundary; use its EMA for divergence comparisons.
Calculation: Data quality = SMA of completeness multiplied by a configurable volume-type ceiling, where completeness = min(1, number of available lower-timeframe bars divided by the expected number per chart bar); use zero until positive chart-bar volume has been observed, and zero for unavailable completeness. The ceiling represents the volume type and is an external configuration because it cannot be determined from the data fields.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low of the selected price source, confirmed after the configured number of right-hand chart bars; use high and low for wick pivots or close for both sides of close pivots. Compare each new pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same side, even if that preceding pivot did not produce a decision. Sample smoothed CVD and volume at each pivot. Evaluate decisions on the confirmation bar.
Long: Data quality >= minimum quality AND the previous chart bar's average absolute bar delta > 0 AND its absolute bar delta >= a multiple of that average AND its ATR > 0 AND its high - low <= a fraction of that ATR AND its bar delta < 0 AND current close > open. The average absolute bar delta and ATR use the same lookback.

ATR and average absolute bar delta use the same lookback as absorption.
