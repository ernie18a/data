Calculation: Bar delta = sum of lower-timeframe completed intrabar volume, positive when intrabar close > open, negative when close < open, and zero when equal.
Calculation: CVD = prior committed CVD plus bar delta; reset at each session start or carry across sessions, according to accumulation mode. Commit on chart-bar close.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot high/low = high/low (or close, by pivot-source setting) that is a pivot using the configured left and right chart bars; it is known after the right bars complete. CVD pivot = smoothed CVD at the matching price-pivot bar.
Calculation: Data-quality gate = smoothed completeness-adjusted volume trust reaches the minimum accepted grade; block when volume is absent, and cap trust for tick-volume symbol types.
Long: Data-quality gate passes AND ((confirmed price pivot low is lower than the prior price pivot low AND matching CVD pivot is higher than the prior CVD pivot low) OR (Show Hidden is enabled AND confirmed price pivot low is higher than the prior price pivot low AND matching CVD pivot is lower than the prior CVD pivot low)) AND same-side pivot spacing is between the configured minimum and maximum bars AND absolute CVD-pivot difference meets the configured minimum relative to average absolute bar delta AND pivot volume meets the configured minimum relative to its 20-bar average (or that average is zero) AND the price swing from the preceding opposite pivot meets the configured minimum relative to ATR; OR data-quality gate passes AND the previous chart bar had negative bar delta whose absolute value meets the configured minimum relative to average absolute bar delta and whose range is at most the configured fraction of ATR AND the current chart bar closes above its open.
Short: Data-quality gate passes AND ((confirmed price pivot high is higher than the prior price pivot high AND matching CVD pivot is lower than the prior CVD pivot high) OR (Show Hidden is enabled AND confirmed price pivot high is lower than the prior price pivot high AND matching CVD pivot is higher than the prior CVD pivot high)) AND same-side pivot spacing is between the configured minimum and maximum bars AND absolute CVD-pivot difference meets the configured minimum relative to average absolute bar delta AND pivot volume meets the configured minimum relative to its 20-bar average (or that average is zero) AND the price swing from the preceding opposite pivot meets the configured minimum relative to ATR; OR data-quality gate passes AND the previous chart bar had positive bar delta whose absolute value meets the configured minimum relative to average absolute bar delta and whose range is at most the configured fraction of ATR AND the current chart bar closes below its open.
