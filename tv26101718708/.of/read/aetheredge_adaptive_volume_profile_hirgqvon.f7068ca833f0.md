Calculation: Value-area profile = On completed chart-timeframe bars, divide the rolling lookback price range into equal bins and distribute each bar’s volume evenly across the bins intersecting its low–high range. The POC is the highest-volume bin; expand from it toward the heavier adjacent bin until the target volume fraction is included. VAH and VAL are the outer boundaries of the included bins.
Calculation: Acceptance probability = Online logistic regression probability from these direction-adjusted features: edge-bin volume divided by maximum bin volume; tanh of close momentum divided by ATR; volume divided by its moving average minus one; close-to-POC distance divided by ATR; tanh of the spread between shorter- and longer-lookback EMAs divided by ATR; ATR divided by its moving average minus one; and value-area width divided by ATR. Train on edge events resolved when price either continues beyond the edge by an ATR multiple or returns beyond the edge by an ATR multiple; discard unresolved events at the horizon. Update the logistic model online from resolved labels.
Calculation: Acceptance threshold = For each ATR-percentile volatility tercile, select among threshold arms using the highest UCB score (mean reward plus exploration bonus); threshold equals the base threshold plus the selected arm’s step. Reward correct calls positively, incorrect calls negatively, and unresolved calls with a small positive reward; update arm means from resolved events.
Long: On a completed bar after warmup, close crosses above VAH AND acceptance probability >= acceptance threshold
Long: On a completed bar after warmup, close crosses below VAL AND acceptance probability <= 1 - acceptance threshold
Short: On a completed bar after warmup, close crosses below VAL AND acceptance probability >= acceptance threshold
Short: On a completed bar after warmup, close crosses above VAH AND acceptance probability <= 1 - acceptance threshold
