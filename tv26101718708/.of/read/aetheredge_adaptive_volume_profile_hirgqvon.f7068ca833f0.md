Calculation: Volume profile = On each bar, divide the rolling profile lookback's low-to-high range into equal price bins, using a minimum bin width. Distribute each bar's volume equally among all bins touched by its low-to-high range. The point of control is the center of the first highest-volume bin. Starting at that bin, add the heavier adjacent bin until the configured fraction of total volume is included; choose the upper bin on ties. The value-area high is the upper boundary of the highest included bin; the value-area low is the lower boundary of the lowest included bin. Clamp price-to-bin indices to the profile range.
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR, falling back to true range and then a small fraction of close when unavailable, with a positive minimum.
Calculation: Break features = At the crossing bar, form seven features: volume at the crossed edge's bin divided by maximum bin volume; tanh of close change over a momentum lookback divided by safe ATR and multiplied by break direction; volume divided by its moving average minus one; close minus point of control divided by safe ATR and multiplied by break direction; tanh of the difference between shorter and longer close EMAs divided by safe ATR and multiplied by break direction; safe ATR divided by its longer moving average minus one; and value-area width divided by safe ATR. Clamp the features to their respective configured bounds. Break direction is positive for an upward cross and negative for a downward cross.
Calculation: Acceptance probability = Sigmoid of the dot product of break features and online logistic-regression weights plus an intercept; clamp the sigmoid input to a finite range. Initialize all weights to zero. Register every value-area crossing, including crossings before warmup, with its edge level, direction, features, probability, selected threshold and volatility regime. On every bar, after calculating that bar's probability and registering new events, process pending events newest first. An upward event is accepted when close is at least its stored edge plus an acceptance distance times current safe ATR, or rejected when close is at most its stored edge minus a rejection distance times current safe ATR; reverse the inequalities and directions for a downward event. Check acceptance before rejection and both before timeout. For each resolved event, update the intercept by subtracting learning rate times prediction error and update each feature weight by subtracting learning rate times the sum of prediction error times the stored feature and an L2 penalty on that weight. Recompute prediction with the current weights for each update. Discard unresolved events at the resolution horizon without training.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = Divide the rolling percentile rank of safe ATR into low, middle and high volatility regimes using configured boundaries. For each regime, maintain a count and mean reward for each threshold option, initially zero. On each bar select the option maximizing mean reward plus an exploration coefficient times sqrt(log(total regime count + 1) / option count); give untried options priority and break ties by the lowest option index. The threshold is the base threshold plus the selected index times the threshold step. When an event resolves, update the count and mean reward of the regime and option stored at registration: reward a correct breakout or rejection call, penalize an incorrect call, and give a smaller positive reward if neither call fired. A breakout call fired when its stored probability was at least its stored threshold; a rejection call fired when probability was at most one minus that threshold. If both fired, a correct call takes priority in the reward. Timed-out events do not update the threshold. Select the current threshold before processing resolutions on the current bar.
Calculation: Upward crossing = Close is above the current value-area high AND previous close is at or below the previous value-area high. Downward crossing = Close is below the current value-area low AND previous close is at or above the previous value-area low. If both crossings occur on the same bar, use the downward-crossing features for the probability used by both signals, but register one upward event using upward-crossing features.
Long: Bar index is greater than the warmup length AND ((upward crossing AND acceptance probability >= adaptive threshold) OR (downward crossing AND acceptance probability <= 1 - adaptive threshold)).
Short: Bar index is greater than the warmup length AND ((downward crossing AND acceptance probability >= adaptive threshold) OR (upward crossing AND acceptance probability <= 1 - adaptive threshold)).
