Calculation: Order block = On each closed bar, update the latest confirmed swing high and low, using equal numbers of bars on either side; a new pivot resets its side's broken flag. An upward break occurs once per latest swing high when close > swing high + ATR × break buffer; a downward break occurs once per latest swing low when close < swing low − ATR × break buffer. For an upward break, search from the broken pivot forward through the origin lookback, capped at the break bar, and select the last candle with close < open; for a downward break select the last candle with close > open. Create the corresponding bullish or bearish block with top = origin high and bottom = origin low only if origin ATR > 0 and origin range >= origin ATR × minimum impulse multiplier. Keep at most the configured number of blocks per side, removing the oldest first.
Calculation: Block mitigation = Starting on the block's creation bar, track the minimum low for a bullish block, initialized to its top, or the maximum high for a bearish block, initialized to its bottom. For positive block height, bullish mitigation = max(0, (top − tracked minimum low) / height) and bearish mitigation = max(0, (tracked maximum high − bottom) / height); otherwise mitigation = 0. Remove a block before evaluating entries when mitigation >= invalidation threshold. Block extension settings do not affect entry eligibility.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = According to the selected pattern: long wick requires high > low AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) >= 0.5; engulfing requires close > open AND close > previous open AND open <= previous close; strong close requires high > low AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= 0.75; wick plus close requires both long wick and strong close.
Calculation: Bearish rejection = According to the selected pattern: long wick requires high > low AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) >= 0.5; engulfing requires close < open AND close < previous open AND open >= previous close; strong close requires high > low AND (high − close) / (high − low) >= 0.75; wick plus close requires both long wick and strong close.
Long: On a closed bar, for the newest qualifying bullish block, low <= block top AND low >= block bottom AND block mitigation <= entry cap AND the block has not previously signaled AND bullish rejection AND (midpoint filter disabled OR close >= (block top + block bottom) / 2) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > 0 AND high − low >= ATR × expansion multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA > 0 AND volume / volume SMA >= volume multiplier). Mark that block as signaled.
Short: On a closed bar, for the newest qualifying bearish block, high >= block bottom AND high <= block top AND block mitigation <= entry cap AND the block has not previously signaled AND bearish rejection AND (midpoint filter disabled OR close <= (block top + block bottom) / 2) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > 0 AND high − low >= ATR × expansion multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume SMA > 0 AND volume / volume SMA >= volume multiplier). Mark that block as signaled.
