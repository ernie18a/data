Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; it becomes known only after those later bars complete
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = on a confirmed close above the latest unbroken swing high by at least an ATR-based buffer / below the latest unbroken swing low by at least an ATR-based buffer, use the last bearish / bullish candle from the pivot bar through the configured bounded lookback toward the break; its high-low range must meet the configured minimum ATR multiple. The candle's high and low define the zone.
Calculation: Mitigation percentage = for a bullish zone, (zone top − lowest low since zone creation) / zone height × 100; for a bearish zone, (highest high since zone creation − zone bottom) / zone height × 100. Track the extreme cumulatively and do not let mitigation decrease.
Calculation: Bullish rejection pattern = the selected condition: lower wick / candle range ≥ 0.5; or a bullish candle closing above the prior open and prior close, with its open ≤ prior close; or (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ 0.75; or both the lower-wick and strong-close conditions.
Calculation: Bearish rejection pattern = the selected condition: upper wick / candle range ≥ 0.5; or a bearish candle closing below the prior open and prior close, with its open ≥ prior close; or (high − close) / (high − low) ≥ 0.75; or both the upper-wick and strong-close conditions.
Long: confirmed bar, price low is within a live bullish zone, zone mitigation percentage ≤ configured entry cap, that zone has not signaled before, bullish rejection pattern, close ≥ zone midpoint if that filter is enabled, high − low ≥ configured ATR multiple if ATR expansion filter is enabled, and volume ≥ configured multiple of its rolling average if volume filter is enabled; signal only the newest qualifying bullish zone on the bar
Short: confirmed bar, price high is within a live bearish zone, zone mitigation percentage ≤ configured entry cap, that zone has not signaled before, bearish rejection pattern, close ≤ zone midpoint if that filter is enabled, high − low ≥ configured ATR multiple if ATR expansion filter is enabled, and volume ≥ configured multiple of its rolling average if volume filter is enabled; signal only the newest qualifying bearish zone on the bar
