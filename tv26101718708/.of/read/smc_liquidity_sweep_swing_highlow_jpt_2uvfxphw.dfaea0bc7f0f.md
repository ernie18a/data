Calculation: Liquidity levels = the most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using configurable left and right bar counts. A newly confirmed pivot replaces its corresponding level and resets that level to unswept. Mark a level swept whenever its sweep conditions through the volume filter are met, even if the trend or cooldown filter blocks the signal.
Calculation: Sweep conditions = for a high level, the current bar is later than the pivot bar, the level is unswept, and high > level + ATR × sweep buffer; for a low level, the current bar is later than the pivot bar, the level is unswept, and low < level − ATR × sweep buffer.
Calculation: Trade levels = on each signal, set entry to close. For a long, set stop to min(low, entry − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier) and risk to entry − stop; for a short, set stop to max(high, entry + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier) and risk to stop − entry. Set three targets to entry plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, risk × each respective configurable reward multiple. A new signal replaces the existing trade levels. The tracked trade becomes inactive at the third target or stop, including on the entry bar; the first and second targets do not deactivate it.
Long: Low-level sweep conditions AND (close-back filter disabled OR close > low liquidity level) AND (rejection-body filter disabled OR (close > open AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) >= minimum wick fraction)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (no previous signal OR bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown).
Short: High-level sweep conditions AND (close-back filter disabled OR close < high liquidity level) AND (rejection-body filter disabled OR (close < open AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) >= minimum wick fraction)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (no previous signal OR bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown).
Exit: long, tracked trade active AND (high >= any of the three long targets OR low <= long stop).
Exit: short, tracked trade active AND (low <= any of the three short targets OR high >= short stop).
