Calculation: Bullish score = mean over the signal window of positive directional and volatility/volume weighted scores. Directional contributions are positive for ADX above its minimum with +DI > −DI, bullish Supertrend, close > EMA, MACD line > signal line and zero, or close above both Ichimoku spans with conversion line > base line; otherwise zero. Volatility/volume contributions are positive for ATR > its longer-term average times an expansion factor, close > VWAP, volume > its longer-term average times a factor, OBV > its EMA, or CMF > a positive threshold; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Bearish score = mean over the signal window of the absolute value of negative directional and volatility/volume weighted scores. Directional contributions are negative for ADX above its minimum with −DI > +DI, bearish Supertrend, close < EMA, MACD line < signal line and zero, or close below both Ichimoku spans with conversion line < base line; otherwise zero. Volatility/volume contributions are negative for ATR < its longer-term average times a contraction factor, close < VWAP, volume < its longer-term average times a factor, OBV < its EMA, or CMF < a negative threshold; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over a short lookback minus ATR times a multiplier; use a longer lookback when the prior candle's body exceeds ATR times an impulse factor. Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) times the reward/risk factor.
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over a short lookback plus ATR times a multiplier; use a longer lookback when the prior candle's body exceeds ATR times an impulse factor. Short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) times the reward/risk factor.
Long: Bullish score >= entry threshold AND current time is in either allowed session AND prior candle close > prior candle open if the anti-pullback filter is enabled AND cooldown since last closed trade has elapsed (or no trade has closed) AND open trade count < its limit AND 0 < close − long stop <= ATR times the maximum stop-distance factor
Long: Bullish score >= entry threshold AND current time is in either allowed session AND prior candle close > prior candle open if the anti-pullback filter is enabled AND (open trade count >= its limit OR cooldown since last closed trade has not elapsed)
Short: Bearish score >= entry threshold AND current time is in either allowed session AND prior candle close < prior candle open if the anti-pullback filter is enabled AND cooldown since last closed trade has elapsed (or no trade has closed) AND open trade count < its limit AND 0 < short stop − close <= ATR times the maximum stop-distance factor
Short: Bearish score >= entry threshold AND current time is in either allowed session AND prior candle close < prior candle open if the anti-pullback filter is enabled AND (open trade count >= its limit OR cooldown since last closed trade has not elapsed)
Exit: Long, Bearish score >= entry threshold OR low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: Short, Bullish score >= entry threshold OR high >= short stop OR low <= short target
