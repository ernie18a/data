Calculation: Directional score = sum of five signed components: ADX contributes positive weight when ADX > its threshold AND +DI > -DI, negative weight when ADX > its threshold AND -DI > +DI; Supertrend contributes positive weight in an uptrend and negative weight in a downtrend; EMA contributes positive weight when close > EMA and negative weight when close < EMA; MACD contributes positive weight when MACD > its signal AND MACD > 0, negative weight when MACD < its signal AND MACD < 0; Ichimoku contributes positive weight when close > both unshifted leading spans AND conversion line > base line, negative weight when close < both unshifted leading spans AND conversion line < base line. Each component is otherwise 0. Weights decrease in the order listed. The conversion, base, and second span lookbacks increase in that order; the first span is the average of the conversion and base lines.
Calculation: Volume-volatility score = sum of five signed components: ATR contributes positive weight when ATR > its SMA times an upper multiplier and negative weight when ATR < its SMA times a lower multiplier; VWAP contributes positive weight when close > session VWAP of typical price and negative weight when close < VWAP; volume contributes positive weight when volume > its SMA times an upper multiplier and negative weight when volume < its SMA times a lower multiplier; OBV contributes positive weight when OBV > its EMA and negative weight when OBV < its EMA; CMF contributes positive weight when CMF > a positive threshold and negative weight when CMF < its negative counterpart. Each component is otherwise 0. Weights decrease in the order listed and match the corresponding ranked directional weights. Each upper multiplier is greater than 1 and each lower multiplier is less than 1. CMF is SMA of volume times ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), divided by SMA of volume over the same lookback.
Calculation: Bull score = the sum over a rolling window, including the current bar, of the positive directional score and positive volume-volatility score, treating negative group scores as 0, divided by the full window length.
Calculation: Bear score = the sum over the same window of the absolute values of the negative directional score and negative volume-volatility score, treating positive group scores as 0, divided by the full window length.
Calculation: Stop lookback = the longer lookback when the impulse option is enabled AND the absolute previous candle body > current ATR times an impulse multiplier; otherwise the shorter lookback.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the stop lookback including the current bar minus ATR times a stop multiplier. Short stop = highest high over the same lookback plus ATR times the stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = signal close + (signal close - long stop) times the reward-to-risk ratio. Short target = signal close - (short stop - signal close) times the reward-to-risk ratio. Store the stop and target when an entry condition is met; replace them if another entry condition for that side occurs.
Long: Bull score >= entry threshold AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in either configured session) AND open trade count < maximum AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR previous close > previous open) AND (no trade has closed OR bars since the latest close >= cooldown) AND close - long stop > 0 AND close - long stop <= ATR times maximum stop-distance multiplier.
Short: Bear score >= entry threshold AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in either configured session) AND open trade count < maximum AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR previous close < previous open) AND (no trade has closed OR bars since the latest close >= cooldown) AND short stop - close > 0 AND short stop - close <= ATR times maximum stop-distance multiplier.
Long: Bull score >= entry threshold AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in either configured session) AND (open trade count >= maximum OR bars since the latest close < cooldown AND a trade has closed) AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR previous close > previous open).
Short: Bear score >= entry threshold AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in either configured session) AND (open trade count >= maximum OR bars since the latest close < cooldown AND a trade has closed) AND (anti-pullback filter disabled OR previous close < previous open).
Exit: long, bear score >= entry threshold OR low <= stored long stop OR high >= stored long target.
Exit: short, bull score >= entry threshold OR high >= stored short stop OR low <= stored short target.
