Calculation: Composite score = weighted sum of normalized CHO and normalized momentum, with the CHO weight greater than the momentum weight. CHO is the difference between short-lookback and long-lookback close changes divided by their sum, or zero if their sum is zero; normalize it by shifting and scaling it to the momentum score's scale. Momentum is the close change over its lookback; normalize it to its rolling minimum and maximum, using the midpoint of the scale if they are equal.
Calculation: Candle pattern = the first matching condition in this order: bullish engulfing if close > open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close; bearish engulfing if close < open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close; hammer if lower wick > body times a multiplier AND upper wick < body times a smaller multiplier AND high > low; shooting star if upper wick > body times the hammer's larger multiplier AND lower wick < body times its smaller multiplier AND high > low; inside bar if high <= previous high AND low >= previous low; pin bar if either wick > body times the hammer's larger multiplier AND body < candle range times a fraction; morning star if close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND previous body <= previous range times a fraction AND close > open AND close > midpoint of the body two bars ago; evening star if close two bars ago > open two bars ago AND previous body <= previous range times the morning star fraction AND close < open AND close < midpoint of the body two bars ago; large bullish body if body > ATR times a multiplier AND close > open; large bearish body if body > ATR times the same multiplier AND close < open. Body = absolute value of close minus open; upper wick = high minus the greater of open and close; lower wick = the lesser of open and close minus low; range = high minus low.
Long: Composite score >= upper state threshold
Short: Composite score < lower state threshold
Long: Composite score > previous composite score
Short: Composite score < previous composite score
Long: Low < lowest low over the liquidity lookback ending at the previous bar AND close > that lowest low
Short: High > highest high over the liquidity lookback ending at the previous bar AND close < that highest high
Long: Candle pattern = bullish engulfing OR candle pattern = hammer OR candle pattern = morning star OR candle pattern = large bullish body
Short: Candle pattern = bearish engulfing OR candle pattern = shooting star OR candle pattern = evening star OR candle pattern = large bearish body
