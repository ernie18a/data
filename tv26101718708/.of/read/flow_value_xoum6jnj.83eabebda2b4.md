Calculation: RSI reversal target price = on the higher timeframe, use the previous completed bar’s close and RSI-length smoothed gains and losses to solve for the price that reaches the overbought or oversold RSI threshold: q = threshold / (100 − threshold); required rise = (q × prior smoothed loss − prior smoothed gain) × (RSI length − 1); required fall = (prior smoothed gain / q − prior smoothed loss) × (RSI length − 1). Target price is prior close plus the rise if rise > 0, otherwise prior close minus the fall if fall > 0, otherwise prior close. Use higher-timeframe data with lookahead off; on a forming higher-timeframe bar, use its developing values.
Calculation: Active higher-timeframe bullish FVG = created when the current low > the high two bars earlier, initially spanning [high two bars earlier, current low]; invalidate when a later low < its bottom, otherwise lower its top to that low if the low is below its top. Active higher-timeframe bearish FVG = created when the current high < the low two bars earlier, initially spanning [current high, low two bars earlier]; invalidate when a later high > its top, otherwise raise its bottom to that high if the high is above its bottom. Use the latest active gap and higher-timeframe data with lookahead off; on a forming higher-timeframe bar, use its developing values.
Long: oversold RSI reversal target price ≥ active bullish FVG bottom AND oversold RSI reversal target price ≤ active bullish FVG top
Short: overbought RSI reversal target price ≥ active bearish FVG bottom AND overbought RSI reversal target price ≤ active bearish FVG top
