Calculation: Average entry price = total quote currency spent on the base entry and filled averaging orders divided by total units acquired; the base entry fills at its chart-bar close, and averaging order k fills at its chart-bar close with quote currency size equal to first averaging-order size × size multiplier^(k−1).
