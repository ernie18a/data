Calculation: Direction = +1 for bullish and -1 for bearish; directional comparisons reverse for bearish conditions. Evaluate on each completed chart bar. All named thresholds, weights, lookbacks, switches, and multipliers are parameters unless stated otherwise.
Calculation: Structure = for each enabled filter, close is on the directional side of the corresponding short EMA, medium EMA, long EMA, or long SMA; when the medium EMA filter is enabled, the short EMA is also on its directional side; when the long EMA filter is enabled, the medium EMA is also on its directional side.
Calculation: VWAP alignment = close is on the directional side of session VWAP of typical price AND, in slope mode, VWAP is moving in that direction over its lookback; in retest mode also require the bar's directional extreme to reach within an ATR-based tolerance of VWAP. Bias-only mode requires only the close comparison.
Calculation: Money-flow alignment = enabled MFI is beyond its directional threshold and moving in the direction since the preceding bar AND, if enabled, CMF is on the directional side of zero.
Calculation: RSI alignment = enabled RSI is beyond its directional threshold, its one-bar change reaches the directional minimum, and RSI remains inside its directional overbought or oversold limit.
Calculation: MACD alignment = enabled MACD satisfies the selected combination of a directional signal-line cross or a still-directional MACD after a cross within the freshness lookback, and a histogram of the directional sign that is moving in that direction; the combinations are cross only, histogram only, either, or both. Alignment also holds if the histogram has moved in the direction for two consecutive bars, irrespective of its sign. The MACD fast lookback is shorter than its slow lookback.
Calculation: Candle alignment = body size as a percentage of high minus low reaches its minimum AND close position within the range reaches its directional threshold; use body percentage zero and close position one-half for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Support and resistance = retain the most recent limited number of confirmed pivot lows and highs, respectively, and discard levels older than the maximum age measured from the pivot bar. A pivot is confirmed after its specified right-hand bars. Support is the highest retained pivot low strictly below current close; resistance is the lowest retained pivot high strictly above current close. Proximity is a parameter multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Support bounce = close minus support is at most proximity AND low is at most support plus half the proximity AND close is above open. Resistance rejection = resistance minus close is at most proximity AND high is at least resistance minus half the proximity AND close is below open.
Calculation: Low sweep = low is below support AND close is above support AND volume exceeds its moving average times the breakout volume multiplier AND body percentage reaches its minimum AND, if the candle filter is enabled, bullish candle alignment holds. High sweep is the symmetric condition using resistance, high, and bearish candle alignment.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = higher-timeframe close is on the directional side of each enabled higher-timeframe EMA and session VWAP. Higher-timeframe momentum = higher-timeframe MACD histogram is moving in the direction compared with its value on the preceding chart bar AND higher-timeframe RSI is beyond its directional midpoint. Higher-timeframe requests use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Flat MACD = absolute histogram is below a fraction of its moving average absolute histogram, substituting zero if that average is unavailable. Chop = ATR as a percentage of close is below its minimum when the ATR filter is enabled OR flat MACD AND either close is within an ATR-based distance of both the medium EMA and VWAP, or the enabled EMA-compression filter finds the short and medium EMAs within its ATR-based distance.
Calculation: Directional score = sum the configured weights for directional structure, VWAP alignment, money-flow alignment, RSI alignment, MACD alignment, support bounce or resistance rejection, enabled higher-timeframe alignment, and enabled higher-timeframe momentum; add the sweep weight for the directional sweep and the ADX weight when ADX reaches its threshold; subtract the chop-zone weight when the enabled RSI chop-zone filter finds RSI within its inclusive bounds. VWAP alignment contributes even when the VWAP filter is disabled. The ADX contribution applies even when its filter is disabled. The sweep and ADX weights and RSI penalty are fixed contributions, distinct from the configurable component weights.
Calculation: Directional gate = support bounce or resistance rejection OR directional VWAP retest with VWAP moving in the direction OR directional short-EMA cross of the medium EMA with directional structure OR directional sweep OR close on the directional side of and within an ATR-based distance of the short EMA with directional VWAP alignment. The source's breakout and reclaimed-level conditions cannot occur: resistance is always selected strictly above current close and support strictly below it, so neither level can be recorded as broken.
Calculation: Ordinary readiness = directional gate AND directional structure AND enabled MACD alignment AND, in conservative mode, enabled VWAP and RSI alignment. Also require no chop, enabled ATR and volume filters to pass, and each enabled anti-chase filter to pass: close must not be farther on the directional side of the medium EMA or VWAP than the respective ATR-based limit. The ADX filter does not restrict readiness.
Calculation: Early readiness = early entries enabled AND short EMA crosses the medium EMA in the direction AND close is on the directional side of VWAP AND VWAP moves in the direction AND RSI reaches its directional early threshold AND, if enabled, the directional MACD cross occurred within the freshness lookback AND either directional structure holds or the breakout-mode switch is enabled AND no chop AND enabled ATR and volume filters pass AND the enabled directional anti-chase filters pass AND MACD is not flat.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = directional score exceeds the opposite score by at least the minimum gap and is strictly greater AND, if required, exceeds its own previous-bar score, using zero when unavailable AND the shared number of bars since the last long or short signal reaches the cooldown, or no signal has occurred AND, if trend reset is enabled, no same-direction signal has occurred since close last moved across the short EMA to the opposite side. A same-direction signal disables eligibility until that reset; reset is applied before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Directional signal = signal eligibility AND either ordinary readiness with score at least the ordinary threshold OR early readiness with score at least the ordinary threshold less the fixed early-entry discount OR higher-timeframe alignment and, when enabled, higher-timeframe momentum with either ordinary readiness and score at least the strong threshold or early readiness and score at least the strong threshold less the same discount. The strong threshold is higher than the ordinary threshold.
Long: bullish directional signal OR bullish structure OR bullish VWAP alignment OR bullish money-flow alignment OR bullish RSI alignment OR bullish MACD alignment OR low sweep OR support bounce OR enabled higher-timeframe bullish alignment with bearish alignment false OR enabled higher-timeframe bullish momentum OR enabled bullish candle alignment OR bullish score at least its ordinary threshold.
Short: bearish directional signal OR bearish structure with bullish structure false OR bearish VWAP alignment with bullish VWAP alignment false OR bearish money-flow alignment with bullish money-flow alignment false OR bearish RSI alignment with bullish RSI alignment false OR bearish MACD alignment with bullish MACD alignment false OR high sweep with low sweep false OR resistance rejection with low sweep, high sweep, and support bounce false OR enabled higher-timeframe bearish alignment with bullish alignment false OR enabled higher-timeframe bearish momentum with bullish momentum false OR enabled bearish candle alignment with bullish candle alignment false OR bearish score at least its ordinary threshold.
