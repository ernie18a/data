Calculation: Bull structure = close above each enabled EMA; when EMA 50 is enabled, EMA 20 is above EMA 50; when EMA 200 is enabled, EMA 50 is above EMA 200; and close is above enabled SMA 200.
Calculation: Bear structure = inverse of bull structure.
Calculation: VWAP direction = price above/below VWAP, with rising/falling VWAP required in Standard mode, a VWAP retest hold/fail required in Strict mode, and no slope requirement in Bias Only mode. VWAP slope compares current VWAP with an earlier VWAP.
Calculation: RSI confirmation = RSI above/below its directional threshold, changing by at least the minimum RSI delta in that direction, and below its overbought/above its oversold threshold. RSI chop = RSI within the configured middle band.
Calculation: MFI confirmation = MFI above/below its directional threshold and rising/falling; when CMF alignment is enabled, CMF must also be positive/negative. CMF = volume-weighted sum of the close's position within each bar's range over its lookback, divided by total volume over that lookback.
Calculation: MACD confirmation = configured cross/fresh-cross and/or histogram condition, OR two consecutive histogram increases/decreases; a fresh cross is within the configured number of bars. Fast MACD length is shorter than slow MACD length.
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = latest confirmed pivot lows/highs, limited to the configured count and age. A pivot is confirmed using the configured left and right bars. Nearest support/resistance is the closest retained level below/above close.
Calculation: Support/resistance context = support bounce OR reclaimed resistance OR bullish breakout; bearish context = resistance rejection OR lost support OR bearish breakdown. A bounce/rejection occurs near the nearest level with the bar touching within half the ATR-based proximity and closing directionally beyond the level. Reclaim/loss occurs after a prior break and a retest within 0.5% of the level. Breakout/breakdown requires a close crossing the nearest level, then within the confirmation window a close beyond it by 0.15 ATR, above-average breakout volume, and a qualifying directional candle.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price wicks beyond the nearest level and closes back across it, with above-average breakout volume and a qualifying directional candle. A qualifying candle has a sufficiently large body as a percentage of range and closes in the directional part of its range.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close above/below its 200-period EMA and VWAP, subject to enabled components. Higher-timeframe momentum = higher-timeframe MACD histogram rising/falling and RSI above/below 50. Use the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Chop = (close near both EMA 50 and VWAP AND MACD histogram small relative to its longer-term average absolute value) OR (EMA 20 and EMA 50 compressed relative to ATR AND MACD histogram small) OR ATR percentage below its minimum.
Calculation: Buy gate = support bounce OR reclaimed resistance OR bullish VWAP retest OR EMA 20 crossing above EMA 50 while bull structure holds OR bullish breakout OR low sweep OR close above EMA 20 within 1 ATR while VWAP is bullish. Sell gate is the directional inverse.
Calculation: Bull score = weighted sum of bull structure, bullish VWAP, MFI, RSI, MACD, support/resistance context, and higher-timeframe bias; add configured higher-timeframe momentum weight when enabled, 2 for a low sweep, and 1 when ADX meets its threshold; subtract 1 when RSI is in chop. Bear score is the directional inverse, using a high sweep.
Calculation: Score win = directional score exceeds the opposite score by at least the minimum score gap and, when required, is rising versus the prior bar.
Long: Confirmed bar AND ((normal buy gate AND (conservative mode: bull structure AND enabled VWAP, MACD, and RSI confirmations; aggressive mode: MACD confirmation AND (bull structure OR enabled breakout-against-structure)) AND buy score threshold) OR (early entry enabled AND EMA 20 crosses above EMA 50 AND close above rising VWAP AND RSI meets early-bull threshold AND not chop AND fresh bullish MACD cross when required AND (bull structure OR breakout-against-structure enabled) AND not MACD-flat AND early buy score threshold)) AND ATR and volume filters pass AND not overextended above EMA 50 or VWAP AND bull score wins AND cooldown elapsed AND bullish signal reset is clear; strong-signal threshold hits additionally require higher-timeframe bullish bias and, when enabled, bullish higher-timeframe momentum. Also long on bullish breakout or low liquidity sweep.
Short: Confirmed bar AND ((normal sell gate AND (conservative mode: bear structure AND enabled VWAP, MACD, and RSI confirmations; aggressive mode: MACD confirmation AND (bear structure OR enabled breakdown-against-structure)) AND sell score threshold) OR (early entry enabled AND EMA 20 crosses below EMA 50 AND close below falling VWAP AND RSI meets early-bear threshold AND not chop AND fresh bearish MACD cross when required AND (bear structure OR breakout-against-structure enabled) AND not MACD-flat AND early sell score threshold)) AND ATR and volume filters pass AND not overextended below EMA 50 or VWAP AND bear score wins AND cooldown elapsed AND bearish signal reset is clear; strong-signal threshold hits additionally require higher-timeframe bearish bias and, when enabled, bearish higher-timeframe momentum. Also short on bearish breakdown or high liquidity sweep.
