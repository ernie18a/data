Calculation: Chart-bar delta = if lower-timeframe data is used and available, sum the signed volumes of all completed lower-timeframe bars within the completed chart bar, assigning each lower-timeframe bar +volume, -volume, or 0 according as its close is above, below, or equal to the preceding lower-timeframe close; otherwise use volume × (2 × close - high - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Participation threshold = a positive fixed threshold if selected; otherwise max(standard deviation of chart-bar delta over its lookback × a multiplier, 1).
Calculation: Participation bars = accumulate consecutive completed chart bars until the absolute cumulative delta reaches or exceeds the current participation threshold; record their maximum high, minimum low, final close, total delta, and total volume, then reset the accumulator. Use only completed participation bars in subsequent calculations.
Calculation: Regime = initially range; update only on a participation-bar close after enough participation bars exist. Net move is the latest participation close minus the close one regime lookback earlier; path is the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; efficiency is abs(net move) / path, or 0 if path is 0; relative move is the latest absolute close change divided by path / lookback, or 0 if the denominator is 0. If relative move exceeds its threshold, regime is high volatility; otherwise, if efficiency is at least the trend threshold, regime is uptrend when net move > 0 and downtrend otherwise; otherwise, if efficiency is at most the lower range threshold, regime is range; otherwise it is transition.
Calculation: Cumulative-delta slope = cumulative participation-bar delta minus its value one slope lookback earlier; initially 0 and updated only when enough participation bars have closed.
Calculation: Flow toxicity = sum of absolute participation-bar deltas divided by sum of participation-bar volumes over its lookback, or 0 if that volume sum is 0; initially 0 and updated only when enough participation bars have closed.
Calculation: Imbalance levels = on each participation-bar close, compare the latest bar with the bar two participation bars earlier. Create a bullish level from the earlier high to the latest low if the latest low is higher; create a bearish level from the latest high to the earlier low if the latest high is lower. Require the gap size to be at least a multiple of current chart-bar ATR and the absolute delta of the intervening participation bar to be at least a fraction of the current participation threshold. Initialize its strength to 1 and its state to fresh.
Calculation: Level lifecycle = starting with the next participation bar, update every nondead level on each participation-bar close. For a bullish level, a low at or below its upper bound is a touch; if low also reaches the lower bound, mark it dead and set strength to 0 when close is below the lower bound and delta is at most minus a surge fraction of the current threshold; otherwise mark it breaker and reset strength to the breaker strength when close is above the upper bound and delta is at least an absorption fraction of the threshold; otherwise mark it tapped and subtract the deep-touch decay. For a partial touch, subtract deep-touch decay if low reaches the midpoint, otherwise light-touch decay, and change fresh to tapped. For a bearish level, mirror high and low, upper and lower bounds, comparison directions, and delta signs. After adjudication subtract time decay, flooring strength at 0; mark a zero-strength level dead unless it is a breaker.
Calculation: Trail and leading strength = in an uptrend select the highest midpoint below the current chart close among nondead bullish levels whose strength meets the minimum; in a downtrend select the lowest midpoint above close among qualifying bearish levels. The selected midpoint is the trail and its level strength is the leading strength. If no level qualifies, or the regime is not trending, the trail and leading strength are unavailable.
Calculation: Confidence = logistic(L), where logistic(L) = 1 / (1 + exp(-L)); L is (leading strength - 0.5) × 3, or -1 if leading strength is unavailable, plus 1 in a trend, -0.5 in transition, or -1.2 otherwise; plus 0.9 × sign(cumulative-delta slope) in an uptrend, minus 0.9 × sign(cumulative-delta slope) in a downtrend, and 0 otherwise; minus 1.1 if flow toxicity exceeds its threshold. Use only the supplied bar data, so unavailable external bias and volatility-index contributions are 0.
Calculation: Position stop = initialize to the trail at entry; on each subsequent completed chart bar, before testing exit, update to max(previous stop, current trail) for a long or min(previous stop, current trail) for a short when the trail is available; otherwise retain the previous stop.
Long: regime = uptrend.
Short: regime = downtrend.
Long: position = flat AND a participation bar has just closed AND regime = uptrend AND trail is available AND confidence >= entry-confidence threshold AND cumulative-delta slope > 0.
Short: position = flat AND a participation bar has just closed AND regime = downtrend AND trail is available AND confidence >= entry-confidence threshold AND cumulative-delta slope < 0.
Exit: long, close < position stop OR regime != uptrend.
Exit: short, close > position stop OR regime != downtrend.
