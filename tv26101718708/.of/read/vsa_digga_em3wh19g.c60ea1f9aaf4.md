Calculation: Spread = high − low; close position = (close − low) / spread when spread > 0, otherwise one-half.
Calculation: Narrow base = spread > 0 AND spread <= nearest-rank low-percentile spread over the lookback including the current bar AND (volume filtering is disabled OR volume <= nearest-rank low-percentile volume over the same lookback including the current bar). Evaluate only after the chart has at least the larger of the percentile and swing lookbacks plus two prior bars.
Calculation: Demand base = narrow base AND (close > EMA of close when the EMA filter is enabled, otherwise close < previous close) AND (close position <= one-half when the close-bias filter is enabled).
Calculation: Supply base = narrow base AND (close < EMA of close when the EMA filter is enabled, otherwise close > previous close) AND (close position >= one-half when the close-bias filter is enabled).
Calculation: Base tags = enabled conditions counted separately. For demand: close < previous close AND volume < each of the previous two volumes; low < previous low AND close position >= one-half AND low-percentile volume AND a stopping-volume or spring event within the context lookback including the current bar; a stopping-volume event within that context lookback; a spring event within that context lookback. For supply: close > previous close AND volume < each of the previous two volumes; a buying-climax event within the context lookback; an upthrust event within the context lookback. For either side: spread < every spread in the longer narrow-range lookback of previous bars; otherwise spread < every spread in the shorter narrow-range lookback of previous bars; high <= previous high AND low >= previous low. The volume-based tags are disabled when volume filtering is disabled.
Calculation: Stopping-volume event = close < previous close AND spread >= nearest-rank high-percentile spread AND volume >= nearest-rank high-percentile volume AND close position >= one-half. Buying-climax event = close > previous close AND spread >= that high-percentile spread AND volume >= nearest-rank ultra-high-percentile volume AND high > highest high of the prior swing lookback. Upthrust event = high > that prior swing high AND close position <= one-third AND volume >= that high-percentile volume. Spring event = low < lowest low of the prior swing lookback AND close > that prior swing low AND close position >= one-half. Percentiles use the common lookback including the event bar; the ultra-high volume percentile is higher than the high-volume percentile. Low-percentile volume means volume <= the low-percentile volume threshold.
Calculation: Forming zone = a demand or supply base with at least the configured minimum number of enabled base tags; if both bases occur, use demand. Its initial midpoint is (high + low) / 2 and its height is the greater of spread and a configured multiple of the nearest-rank median spread over the percentile lookback; top and bottom are midpoint plus and minus half the height. Process existing zones newest first before creating a zone on each closed bar. If merging is enabled, a same-side base overlapping a forming zone extends its top to the greater of its current top and the bar high and its bottom to the lesser of its current bottom and the bar low; retain its original birth bar. Otherwise cancel a forming demand zone when close < bottom, or a forming supply zone when close > top, or either when its age reaches the departure limit; cancellation takes precedence over confirmation. A same-side base overlapping a confirmed zone suppresses creation of a new zone. A merge into any forming zone also suppresses creation. Confirmed zones expire when the configured untouched-bar limit is reached, unless expiration is disabled; remove a confirmed demand zone when close < bottom and a confirmed supply zone when close > top. A newly confirmed zone's last-touch bar is its confirmation bar; thereafter update it when the current bar overlaps the zone and the previous bar did not. Before creating a zone, remove oldest zones as needed to respect the configured live-zone limit.
Long: Demand base.
Short: Supply base.
Long: A forming demand zone is not merged or cancelled on the current bar AND close > its top.
Short: A forming supply zone is not merged or cancelled on the current bar AND close < its bottom.
