Calculation: Candle efficiency = absolute chart-bar close minus open divided by the sum of absolute close-minus-open moves of all lower-timeframe bars within that chart bar, including any lower-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Average efficiency = simple moving average of candle efficiency over a lookback period.
Long: chart close >= chart open AND candle efficiency >= average efficiency × strength multiplier
Short: chart close < chart open AND candle efficiency >= average efficiency × strength multiplier
