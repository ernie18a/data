Calculation: Swing high and swing low = confirmed pivot high and pivot low using independently parameterized left and right strengths; use close for both sources or high for swing highs and low for swing lows. The pivot becomes available on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Trend = initially neutral; on each bar, update the latest confirmed swing high and low, then set trend to up if the selected high source is above the latest swing high, and set trend to down if the selected low source is below the latest swing low. Retain the previous trend otherwise; down takes precedence if both breaks occur. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher or lower timeframe, using the bar still forming for another timeframe.
Long: A swing high is confirmed AND its value is above the preceding swing high.
Long: A swing low is confirmed AND its value is greater than or equal to the preceding swing low.
Short: A swing high is confirmed AND its value is less than or equal to the preceding swing high.
Short: A swing low is confirmed AND its value is below the preceding swing low.
Long: Trend is up.
Short: Trend is down.
