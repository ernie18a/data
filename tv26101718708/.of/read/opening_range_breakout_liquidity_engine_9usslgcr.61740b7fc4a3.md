Calculation: Session = either each calendar day in a configured timezone or a configured daily session window; start a new session on entry into the window or a calendar-day change.
Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low of session bars whose opening times are within the configured opening-range duration from the first session bar; the range is complete on the first subsequent in-session bar. Opening-range direction = compare that subsequent bar's close with the first session bar's open.
Calculation: Accepted break = the first qualifying long or short opening-range break in a session; record its direction and close as the break entry price. Reset all break, failure and retest state at each new session.
Calculation: Break stop = the opposite opening-range edge, the opening-range midpoint, or the break entry price minus for a long break or plus for a short break a configured multiple of ATR, selected by configuration. Break risk = the greater of the absolute difference between break entry price and break stop and a configured minimum price increment. Break target = the broken edge plus for a long break or minus for a short break a configured multiple of the greater of opening-range width and that minimum increment, or the break entry price plus for a long break or minus for a short break a configured multiple of break risk, selected by configuration. Freeze stop and target at the accepted break.
Calculation: Failure extreme = highest high since and including an accepted long break, or lowest low since and including an accepted short break, updated through the failure bar. Freeze it on the first failure.
Calculation: Previous-session high and low = the highest high and lowest low of the last completed session. Previous-period high and low = the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe period.
Long: In session AND opening range complete AND no earlier accepted break this session AND close > opening-range high AND (confirmation disabled OR previous bar close > opening-range high) AND (direction filter disabled OR close of the first bar after the opening range > first session bar open).
Short: In session AND opening range complete AND no earlier accepted break this session AND close < opening-range low AND (confirmation disabled OR previous bar close < opening-range low) AND (direction filter disabled OR close of the first bar after the opening range < first session bar open).
Long: In session AND accepted short break this session AND no earlier failure of that break AND close > opening-range low.
Short: In session AND accepted long break this session AND no earlier failure of that break AND close < opening-range high.
Long: In session AND accepted long break this session AND no earlier failure or retest of that break AND low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high.
Short: In session AND accepted short break this session AND no earlier failure or retest of that break AND high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low.
Long: (Previous-session low - low >= configured ATR multiple AND close > previous-session low OR previous-period low - low >= configured ATR multiple AND close > previous-period low) AND no high-level sweep on the same bar.
Short: Previous-session high - negative high <= negative configured ATR multiple AND close < previous-session high OR previous-period high - negative high <= negative configured ATR multiple AND close < previous-period high.
Exit: long breakout position, in session AND (high >= break target OR low <= break stop), with target taking precedence if both are touched.
Exit: short breakout position, in session AND (low <= break target OR high >= break stop), with target taking precedence if both are touched.
Exit: long fade position, in session AND (high >= opening-range high OR low <= failure extreme), with target taking precedence if both are touched.
Exit: short fade position, in session AND (low <= opening-range low OR high >= failure extreme), with target taking precedence if both are touched.
Exit: long breakout position, new session begins AND neither break target nor break stop was touched previously.
Exit: short breakout position, new session begins AND neither break target nor break stop was touched previously.
