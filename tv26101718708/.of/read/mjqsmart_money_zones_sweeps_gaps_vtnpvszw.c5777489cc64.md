Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot whose high or low is extreme over the same configurable number of bars on each side; it becomes available after the right-hand bars complete. Use separate lookbacks for chart structure, sweeps, and higher-timeframe structure.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, start neutral; before updating confirmed pivots, set bullish when close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high, or bearish when close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low. While still neutral, set bullish if the two latest swing highs and lows are both rising, or bearish if both are falling. Retain the last bias otherwise.
Calculation: Sweep body = absolute close minus open, or a configurable minimum price increment when open equals close.
Calculation: Zone base = the bar two bars before the current detection bar plus consecutive earlier bars, up to a configurable maximum base length, whose ranges are at most a configurable multiple of the current detection bar's ATR. Base high and low are the highest high and lowest low of those bars.
Calculation: Active zones = calculate on chart bars and, separately, on the last completed bars of each enabled higher timeframe. A demand zone has top at base high and bottom at the lower of base low and the preceding bar's low; a supply zone has bottom at base low and top at the higher of base high and the preceding bar's high. Optionally cap its width by an ATR multiple while preserving its proximal edge. Add a zone only if its top exceeds its bottom, its oldest base bar differs from that of the newest same-side zone, and it does not overlap any active same-side zone. Keep a configurable maximum number per side, removing the oldest first. On each detection bar, before arming or adding zones, remove a demand zone if low or close, according to the configured failure mode, is below its bottom; remove a supply zone if high or close, according to that mode, is above its top. After a configurable grace period, an armed demand zone is touched when low is at or below its top, and an armed supply zone when high is at or above its bottom; remove it on touch if configured, otherwise retain it as tested. After these checks, arm a demand zone when high is at least its top plus the departure ATR multiple times current ATR, and arm a supply zone when low is at most its bottom minus that amount. Higher-timeframe zones may be configured to remove on first touch.
Calculation: Visible zone = an active zone whose proximal edge is within a configurable chart-ATR distance of chart close, with the distance filter optionally disabled. Process enabled higher timeframes in configured priority order before the chart timeframe; suppress a zone fully contained in an already visible same-side higher-priority zone when its height is at least a configurable fraction of that higher-priority zone's height.
Long: Higher-timeframe close crosses above its latest confirmed swing high.
Short: Higher-timeframe close crosses below its latest confirmed swing low.
Long: Chart close crosses above the latest confirmed chart-structure swing high.
Short: Chart close crosses below the latest confirmed chart-structure swing low.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias is bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias is bearish.
Long: Chart low is below the latest confirmed sweep swing low AND close is above it AND swing low minus low is at least a configurable ATR multiple AND (lower wick is at least a configurable multiple of sweep body OR engulfing is enabled AND close is above open AND previous close is below previous open AND close is at least previous open) AND (bias alignment is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bullish).
Short: Chart high is above the latest confirmed sweep swing high AND close is below it AND high minus swing high is at least a configurable ATR multiple AND (upper wick is at least a configurable multiple of sweep body OR engulfing is enabled AND close is below open AND previous close is above previous open AND close is at most previous open) AND (bias alignment is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bearish).
Long: Chart low is above the high two bars earlier AND (bias alignment is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bearish).
Short: Chart high is below the low two bars earlier AND (bias alignment is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is not bullish).
Long: A visible demand zone exists AND its preceding departure bar's range is at least a configurable multiple of the detection bar's ATR AND the anchor bar's range is at most another configurable multiple of that ATR AND the departure close is above its open AND its high is above base high.
Short: A visible supply zone exists AND its preceding departure bar's range is at least a configurable multiple of the detection bar's ATR AND the anchor bar's range is at most another configurable multiple of that ATR AND the departure close is below its open AND its low is below base low.
