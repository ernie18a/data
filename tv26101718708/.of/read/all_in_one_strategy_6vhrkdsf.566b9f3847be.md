Calculation: RSI average = EMA(RSI, RSI length).
Calculation: Fast RSI DEMA = 2 × EMA(RSI average, fast length) − EMA(EMA(RSI average, fast length), fast length); slow RSI DEMA uses the same formula with the slower length.
Calculation: Stochastic K = an SMA, EMA, or WMA of stochastic(close, high, low); stochastic D = SMA(K).
Calculation: Overall change = (close − open at the price lookback) / open at the price lookback.
Calculation: Recursive high = current high if the price lookback is zero or current high > previous high; otherwise the previous bar's recursive high. Recursive low = current low if the price lookback is zero or current low < previous low; otherwise the previous bar's recursive low.
Calculation: Dip = overall change < 0 AND recursive high > open at the price lookback AND recursive low < open at the price lookback AND recursive high > previous open AND recursive low < previous open AND previous close < previous open.
Calculation: Peak = overall change > 0 AND recursive high > open at the price lookback AND recursive low > open at the price lookback AND recursive high > previous open AND recursive low < previous open AND previous close > previous open.
Calculation: High RSI reference = rounded mean of RSI values above a threshold from the current bar through the high-reference lookback; undefined if none qualify.
Calculation: Low RSI reference = rounded mean of RSI values below a threshold sampled at positive multiples of a fixed bar stride through the low-reference lookback; undefined if none qualify.
Calculation: Bullish RSI trend = fast RSI DEMA > slow RSI DEMA AND RSI average >= previous RSI average. Bearish RSI trend = fast RSI DEMA < slow RSI DEMA AND RSI average <= previous RSI average.
Calculation: RSI trend state = bullish when the bullish RSI trend holds, bearish when the bearish RSI trend holds, otherwise the previous state; initially neutral.
Long: RSI trend state = bullish.
Short: RSI trend state = bearish.
