Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within a configured opening window in New York time; reset at each opening window and retain the completed range until the next window.
Calculation: Target levels = enabled, available, nonzero levels consisting of opens of still-forming higher-timeframe bars; highs, lows and high-low midpoints of last completed higher-timeframe bars; session opens and previous-session highs, lows and midpoints; highs, lows and midpoints of the latest session only while that session is inactive; and the latest designated weekday's high, low and midpoint, including while that day's higher-timeframe bar is still forming. Sort all enabled levels, including still-forming higher-timeframe and active-session highs, lows and midpoints, by price. Starting with the lowest ungrouped level, merge successive levels whose distance from the current group average is at most a configured price threshold, updating the average after each addition. A merged level is eligible only if every constituent is one of the target levels listed above.
Calculation: Upper target = the lowest eligible merged level strictly above the opening-range high, recalculated on each bar after an upward break.
Calculation: Lower target = the highest eligible merged level strictly below the opening-range low, recalculated on each bar after a downward break.
Long: Opening range is complete AND chart bar is within the selected parent session AND close > opening-range high AND no prior upward break has occurred since the current opening window began.
Short: Opening range is complete AND chart bar is within the selected parent session AND close < opening-range low AND no prior downward break has occurred since the current opening window began.
Long: Opening range is complete AND close >= (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2.
Short: Opening range is complete AND close < (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2.
Exit: long, an upward break has occurred since the current opening window began AND upper target exists AND high >= upper target AND no target has been reached since that window began.
Exit: short, a downward break has occurred since the current opening window began AND lower target exists AND low <= lower target AND no target has been reached since that window began.
