Calculation: Bar range = high − low; use zero body efficiency when the range is zero.
Calculation: Pressure = clamp(100 × EMA((close − open) / bar range × volume) / EMA(abs((close − open) / bar range × volume)), −100, 100); use zero when the denominator is zero. Volume impulse = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Auction center = SMA(typical price × volume) / SMA(volume), or typical price when the denominator is zero. Auction upper and lower bounds = auction center ± a deviation multiplier × standard deviation of typical price. Discount = close below the lower bound AND close above low plus a fraction of bar range. Premium = close above the upper bound AND close below high minus the same fraction of bar range. Reclaim = close above the lower bound AND previous close at or below the previous lower bound. Reject = close below the upper bound AND previous close at or above the previous upper bound.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high and low = most recent pivot high and low confirmed by equal numbers of bars on both sides. Upward break = close above the current confirmed swing high AND previous close at or below that same swing high AND absolute candle body greater than an ATR multiple AND volume impulse greater than 1. Downward break is symmetric using the confirmed swing low. Structure bias starts at zero and becomes bullish on an upward break or bearish on a downward break, retaining its last state between breaks. Low sweep = low below the confirmed swing low AND close above it; high sweep = high above the confirmed swing high AND close below it. Bullish gap = low above the high two bars earlier AND close above open; bearish gap = high below the low two bars earlier AND close below open.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = low below previous low AND close above open AND pressure above previous pressure AND volume impulse above an absorption threshold. Bearish absorption = high above previous high AND close below open AND pressure below previous pressure AND volume impulse above the same absorption threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe basis = EMA of close on the higher timeframe, using its last completed bar. The higher-timeframe long pass is enabled when the filter is disabled OR close is above the basis; the short pass is enabled when the filter is disabled OR close is below the basis.
Calculation: Long score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of weights for: short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND close > medium EMA; close > session VWAP AND VWAP > previous VWAP; MACD histogram > 0 AND RSI above its bullish threshold AND positive DI > negative DI; pressure above its positive threshold AND volume impulse above its pressure threshold; bullish absorption; bullish structure bias OR low sweep OR bullish gap; discount OR reclaim; higher-timeframe long pass; ATR-percentage percentile rank above its expansion threshold AND not premium; plus ADX clamped to its upper limit and scaled to a contribution capped at the expansion weight. Use a neutral percentile rank before the rank is available. The corresponding short score uses reversed comparisons, a bearish RSI threshold, negative pressure threshold, bearish absorption, bearish structure bias OR high sweep OR bearish gap, premium OR reject, higher-timeframe short pass, and expansion AND not discount. Both scores use the same respective weights: trend > structure > pressure > momentum > VWAP = auction > absorption > higher-timeframe pass > expansion; the ADX contribution has the same maximum as the expansion weight. The MACD fast EMA is shorter than its slow EMA; the chart trend EMAs are ordered short, medium, long.
Long: Long score at or above its entry threshold AND long score greater than short score by more than a score margin AND higher-timeframe long pass AND premium is false.
Short: Short score at or above its entry threshold AND short score greater than long score by more than the same score margin AND higher-timeframe short pass AND discount is false.
Exit: long, short score > long score OR close < medium EMA OR (premium AND pressure < 0).
Exit: short, long score > short score OR close > medium EMA OR (discount AND pressure > 0).
Long: Long score > short score.
Short: Short score > long score.
Long: Close > higher-timeframe basis.
Short: Close < higher-timeframe basis.
Long: Structure bias is bullish.
Short: Structure bias is bearish.
Long: Upward break OR bullish absorption.
Short: Downward break OR bearish absorption.
Long: (upward break OR bullish gap OR bullish absorption) AND long score at or above the zone threshold AND (structure bias is nonnegative OR low sweep).
Short: (downward break OR bearish gap OR bearish absorption) AND short score at or above the zone threshold AND (structure bias is nonpositive OR high sweep).
