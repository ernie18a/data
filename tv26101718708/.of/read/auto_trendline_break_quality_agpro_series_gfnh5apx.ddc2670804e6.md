Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a bar whose low/high is a local extreme over the configured number of bars on each side; confirm only after the bars to its right are complete (major pivot lookback is longer than minor)
Calculation: Rising support line = line through the two most recent confirmed pivot lows when the later low is higher; falling resistance line = line through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs when the later high is lower; accept a line only when its absolute slope divided by ATR meets the configured minimum
Long: For any retained major or minor falling resistance line, close > its projected value AND previous close <= its value projected at the previous bar AND the selected confirmation mode passes (close-only, volume relative to average volume meets its configured threshold, or close-to-line distance relative to ATR meets its configured threshold)
Short: For any retained major or minor rising support line, close < its projected value AND previous close >= its value projected at the previous bar AND the selected confirmation mode passes (close-only, volume relative to average volume meets its configured threshold, or close-to-line distance relative to ATR meets its configured threshold)
