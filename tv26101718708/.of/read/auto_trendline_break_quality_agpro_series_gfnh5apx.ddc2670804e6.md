Calculation: Trendlines = For each of two pivot lengths, a longer major length and a shorter minor length, confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side. Connect each newly confirmed pivot to the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type and length: retain ascending pivot-low lines and descending pivot-high lines only when absolute slope per bar divided by current ATR is at least a minimum threshold. Project each line using its pivot-to-pivot slope. Keep separate pivot-low and pivot-high line queues, each capped at a maximum count by removing the oldest line, including broken lines; major and enabled minor lines share each queue. A retained line is active until its first accepted break.
Calculation: Break confirmation = Depending on the selected mode, require either no additional condition, current volume divided by its moving average at least a threshold, or absolute distance from close to the projected line divided by ATR at least a threshold. In volume mode, pass if the volume average is not positive.
Calculation: First retest = The first bar within the minimum-to-maximum post-break bar window whose absolute close-to-projected-line distance is at most an ATR multiple; a line can have at most one retest.
Long: Close is above an active descending pivot-high trendline AND previous close is at or below that line projected to the previous bar AND break confirmation passes.
Long: A first retest of a previously upward-broken descending pivot-high trendline occurs AND close is above that line.
Short: Close is below an active ascending pivot-low trendline AND previous close is at or above that line projected to the previous bar AND break confirmation passes.
Short: A first retest of a previously downward-broken ascending pivot-low trendline occurs AND close is below that line.
