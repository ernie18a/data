Calculation: Gap score = 100 × gap difference / the rolling maximum gap difference over a lookback. Gap difference is 100 × (current low − high two bars ago) / current low if the previous close > previous open; otherwise it is 100 × (low two bars ago − current high) / current high.
Calculation: Bullish gap = [high two bars ago, current low] when high two bars ago < current low AND high two bars ago < previous high AND low two bars ago < current low AND gap score > threshold. Bearish gap = [current high, low two bars ago] when low two bars ago > current high AND low two bars ago > previous low AND high two bars ago > current high AND gap score > threshold. Create enabled gaps after the warm-up. Before evaluating entries on each bar, remove bullish gaps whose bottom is crossed below by the selected mitigation source and bearish gaps whose top is crossed above by it. Then traverse gaps newest to oldest and remove a gap if any other remaining gap's top lies strictly inside it. If more than the maximum number of gaps remain, remove the oldest.
Calculation: Gap volume and shares = at gap creation, sum lower-timeframe volume in the last completed chart bar separately for lower-timeframe closes above and below their preceding lower-timeframe closes. Total volume is their sum. If the sum is zero or unavailable, use the previous chart bar's volume and allocate its bullish share by clamping (previous close − previous low) / max(previous high − previous low, a small positive value) to [0, 1]; allocate the remainder to bearish volume. Bullish share is the rounded percentage of total volume assigned bullish, or half if total volume is zero; bearish share is the independently rounded complementary percentage. Store these values with the gap.
Calculation: Mitigation = for a bullish gap, clamp 100 × (gap top − source) / gap height to [0, 100]; for a bearish gap, clamp 100 × (source − gap bottom) / gap height to [0, 100]. The source is optionally close, otherwise low for bullish gaps and high for bearish gaps. Gap age is current bar index minus creation bar index.
Long: Position is flat AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown, or no prior entry exists, AND the newest qualifying bullish gap differs from the last traded bullish gap. A qualifying bullish gap has current high >= gap bottom AND current low <= gap top AND mitigation >= long threshold AND, if enabled, close > open AND, if enabled, total volume >= long minimum AND (long maximum is disabled OR total volume <= long maximum) AND, if enabled, gap age >= long minimum age AND, if enabled, bullish share >= long minimum bullish share AND bearish share <= long maximum bearish share.
Short: Position is flat AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown, or no prior entry exists, AND the newest qualifying bearish gap differs from the last traded bearish gap. A qualifying bearish gap has current high >= gap bottom AND current low <= gap top AND mitigation >= short threshold AND, if enabled, close < open AND, if enabled, total volume >= short minimum AND (short maximum is disabled OR total volume <= short maximum) AND, if enabled, gap age >= short minimum age AND, if enabled, bearish share >= short minimum bearish share AND bullish share <= short maximum bullish share.
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage).
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage).
