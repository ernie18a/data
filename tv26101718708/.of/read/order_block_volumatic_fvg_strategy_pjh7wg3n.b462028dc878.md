Calculation: Bullish FVG = high two bars ago < current low AND high two bars ago < prior high AND low two bars ago < current low AND FVG size score > threshold
Calculation: Bearish FVG = low two bars ago > current high AND low two bars ago > prior low AND high two bars ago > current high AND FVG size score > threshold
Calculation: FVG size score = diff / nearest-rank 100th percentile of diff over a lookback × 100, where diff = (current low − high two bars ago) / current low × 100 if prior close > prior open, otherwise (low two bars ago − current high) / current high × 100
Calculation: FVG mitigation percentage = for a bullish FVG, 0% if source ≥ top, 100% if source ≤ bottom, otherwise (top − source) / (top − bottom) × 100; for a bearish FVG, 0% if source ≤ bottom, 100% if source ≥ top, otherwise (source − bottom) / (top − bottom) × 100. Source is close, or low for bullish and high for bearish FVGs.
Calculation: FVG volume shares = sum volume of lower-timeframe bars within the last completed chart bar with close > prior close (bull volume) or close < prior close (bear volume); if their total is zero, allocate the prior chart bar's volume between bull and bear by the prior close's position within its high-low range. Bull% = rounded bull volume / total × 100; Bear% = 100 − Bull%.
Long: Current bar intersects an active bullish FVG with mitigation percentage ≥ long threshold, age ≥ minimum age, total volume ≥ minimum and ≤ maximum (maximum disabled when zero), Bull% ≥ minimum and Bear% ≤ maximum; if candle confirmation is enabled, close > open; the FVG is the most recently created qualifying bullish FVG and has not previously triggered a long; position is flat and cooldown since last entry has elapsed
Short: Current bar intersects an active bearish FVG with mitigation percentage ≥ short threshold, age ≥ minimum age, total volume ≥ minimum and ≤ maximum (maximum disabled when zero), Bear% ≥ minimum and Bull% ≤ maximum; if candle confirmation is enabled, close < open; the FVG is the most recently created qualifying bearish FVG and has not previously triggered a short; position is flat and cooldown since last entry has elapsed
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR trailing is active and price ≤ the trailing stop; trailing activates when high ≥ entry price × (1 + profit-trigger percentage) and follows the highest price with the configured percentage gap
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR trailing is active and price ≥ the trailing stop; trailing activates when low ≤ entry price × (1 − profit-trigger percentage) and follows the lowest price with the configured percentage gap
