Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each higher-timeframe anchor period and including its still-forming bar; typical price is (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Anchored deviation = square root of the cumulative sum, since the same anchor reset, of volume times (typical price minus the anchored VWAP calculated on that bar) squared, divided by cumulative volume. Upper and lower bands are anchored VWAP plus and minus the same positive multiple of this deviation.
Calculation: Normalized VWAP slope = (anchored VWAP minus its value a lookback ago) / (upper band minus lower band); use zero when the historical VWAP is unavailable or band width is not positive.
Calculation: Anchored volume profile = reset the bucket volumes at each higher-timeframe anchor period. On each chart bar, expand the period high and low to include that bar; if the range is positive, allocate all its volume to the bucket containing its typical price on an equally spaced grid over the current range, clamping to the first or last bucket. Retain previous bucket volumes at their indices when the range expands. POC is the current-grid midpoint of the highest-volume bucket, choosing the lowest index on ties. Starting at POC, expand the value area one adjacent bucket at a time toward the greater adjacent volume, choosing the upper bucket on ties when available, until a specified fraction of accumulated profile volume is included or no further bucket can be added. Value area high and low are the current-grid midpoints of its highest and lowest buckets. The profile includes the still-forming higher-timeframe anchor bar.
Calculation: Band-to-profile tolerance = current anchor-period high minus low, multiplied by a fraction. The same fraction is used in the close-to-band comparisons.
Calculation: Sustained trend = close is above anchored VWAP on the current bar and at both a shorter and a longer lookback, OR close is below anchored VWAP on all three bars; the longer lookback is twice the shorter lookback.
Calculation: Bullish confluence = (close <= lower band times (1 + tolerance fraction) AND (absolute difference between lower band and POC < band-to-profile tolerance OR absolute difference between lower band and value area low < band-to-profile tolerance)) OR (normalized VWAP slope > positive slope threshold AND absolute difference between close and anchored VWAP < a fraction of band width AND sustained trend) OR (normalized VWAP slope > positive slope threshold AND close > anchored VWAP AND close < upper band times (1 - tolerance fraction)).
Long: normalized VWAP slope > positive slope threshold
Long: bullish confluence = true
Short: normalized VWAP slope < negative slope threshold
Short: bullish confluence = false AND ((close >= upper band times (1 - tolerance fraction) AND (absolute difference between upper band and POC < band-to-profile tolerance OR absolute difference between upper band and value area high < band-to-profile tolerance)) OR (normalized VWAP slope < negative slope threshold AND absolute difference between close and anchored VWAP < the same fraction of band width used for bullish confluence AND sustained trend) OR (normalized VWAP slope < negative slope threshold AND close < anchored VWAP AND close > lower band times (1 + tolerance fraction)))
