Calculation: WaveTrend line = EMA of ((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(|HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel length)|, channel length)), using an average length longer than the channel length; both lengths are scaled together by a factor derived from annualized realized volatility (standard deviation of log returns, smoothed by EMA, mapped to a 0–1 profile factor).
Calculation: WaveTrend signal line = SMA of the WaveTrend line over a shorter length.
Calculation: Adaptive outer overbought and oversold levels = respectively the upper and lower percentiles of the WaveTrend line over a lookback, clamped to minimum and maximum magnitudes; inner levels = outer levels × an inner-level ratio.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator low/high pivot = a local low/high with the configured number of bars on each side; associated price low/high = the lowest low/highest high in that pivot window. A pivot pair is valid when the two same-side pivots are no farther apart than the configured maximum distance. Regular bullish divergence = valid low-pivot pair, newer price low lower than prior price low, newer oscillator low higher than prior oscillator low, and at least one oscillator low below the inner oversold level while below zero. Hidden bullish divergence = valid low-pivot pair, newer price low higher than prior price low, newer oscillator low lower than prior oscillator low, and newer oscillator low below zero. Regular bearish divergence = valid high-pivot pair, newer price high higher than prior price high, newer oscillator high lower than prior oscillator high, and at least one oscillator high above the inner overbought level while above zero. Hidden bearish divergence = valid high-pivot pair, newer price high lower than prior price high, newer oscillator high higher than prior oscillator high, and newer oscillator high above zero.
Long: WaveTrend line crosses above its signal line OR regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence.
Short: WaveTrend line crosses below its signal line OR regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence.
