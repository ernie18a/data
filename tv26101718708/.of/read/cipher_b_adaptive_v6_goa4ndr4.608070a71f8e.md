Calculation: Volatility factor = clamp((smoothed annualized standard deviation of chart-bar log close returns − lower volatility threshold) / max(upper volatility threshold − lower volatility threshold, minimum span), 0, 1); replace missing first log return with zero and use the midpoint of the thresholds until the smoothed volatility is available. A manual profile may replace the factor with a fixed value.
Calculation: Adaptive WaveTrend = Let price be (high + low + close) / 3. Multiply the channel length and the longer average length by 1 + volatility factor × length-stretch parameter when adaptation is enabled; otherwise use their base lengths. For each variable-length EMA, initialize with its first source value and update as previous EMA + 2 / (current length + 1) × (source − previous EMA). Compute the channel EMA of price, the channel EMA of the absolute distance between price and its channel EMA, and the normalized deviation as that distance with its original sign divided by the deviation EMA times a scaling parameter; use zero when the deviation EMA is zero. The oscillator is the average-length EMA of the normalized deviation; its signal is the shorter-length SMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Inner overbought and oversold levels = In adaptive mode, take the upper and complementary lower nearest-rank percentiles of the oscillator over a lookback, falling back separately to the corresponding fixed level when unavailable; clamp the upper value between positive minimum and maximum magnitudes and the lower value between their negatives. In symmetric mode, replace both with opposite signs of the larger absolute magnitude. In fixed mode, multiply each fixed level by 1 + volatility factor × level-stretch parameter. Multiply each resulting outer level by an inner-level ratio less than 1.
Calculation: Oscillator pivot pair = On the bar that confirms a pivot high or low in the oscillator, compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type. Each pivot has the same parameterized number of bars on each side; its associated price low or high is the lowest low or highest high across that entire pivot window. The current pivot oscillator value is from the pivot bar, while the inner levels are from the confirmation bar. Evaluate the decision on the confirmation bar.
Long: Adaptive WaveTrend oscillator crosses above its signal.
Short: Adaptive WaveTrend oscillator crosses below its signal.
Long: A confirmed oscillator low pivot and the preceding low pivot are within the maximum pivot distance AND the current window low is lower than the preceding window low AND the current oscillator pivot is higher than the preceding oscillator pivot AND (the zone filter is disabled OR (the current oscillator pivot is below zero AND (the current oscillator pivot is below the current inner oversold level OR the preceding oscillator pivot is below the current inner oversold level))).
Short: A confirmed oscillator high pivot and the preceding high pivot are within the maximum pivot distance AND the current window high is higher than the preceding window high AND the current oscillator pivot is lower than the preceding oscillator pivot AND (the zone filter is disabled OR (the current oscillator pivot is above zero AND (the current oscillator pivot is above the current inner overbought level OR the preceding oscillator pivot is above the current inner overbought level))).
Long: A confirmed oscillator low pivot and the preceding low pivot are within the maximum pivot distance AND the current window low is higher than the preceding window low AND the current oscillator pivot is lower than the preceding oscillator pivot AND the current oscillator pivot is below zero.
Short: A confirmed oscillator high pivot and the preceding high pivot are within the maximum pivot distance AND the current window high is lower than the preceding window high AND the current oscillator pivot is higher than the preceding oscillator pivot AND the current oscillator pivot is above zero.
