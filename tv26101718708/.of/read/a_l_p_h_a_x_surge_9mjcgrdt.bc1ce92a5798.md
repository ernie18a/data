Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after equal lookbacks on both sides; retain the most recently confirmed level
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Dealing-range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over a rolling lookback
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA bias = fast EMA of close > slow EMA of close, both on a higher-timeframe bar still forming; the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the rolling high/low midpoint and rolling close average; bullish when above zero and rising, bearish when below zero and falling
Calculation: Volume pressure = bullish when smoothed estimated buying volume exceeds smoothed estimated selling volume, its further-smoothed difference is positive, and OBV exceeds its value at a prior lookback; bearish when all three comparisons are reversed. Estimate buying volume as volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume when high = low; selling volume is the remainder
Calculation: ADX = smoothed directional-movement index
Calculation: Long confluence score = sum of one vote each for a recent bullish sweep, bullish structural trend, close at/below the dealing-range midpoint, bullish higher-timeframe EMA bias, bullish squeeze momentum, and bullish volume pressure
Calculation: Short confluence score = sum of one vote each for a recent bearish sweep, bearish structural trend, close at/above the dealing-range midpoint, bearish higher-timeframe EMA bias, bearish squeeze momentum, and bearish volume pressure
Long: low < most recently confirmed swing low − minimum ATR-scaled wick and close > that swing low and close > open OR close > most recently confirmed swing high with prior structural trend bearish (bullish CHoCH) or bullish (bullish BOS) OR a long entry setup is true
Short: high > most recently confirmed swing high + minimum ATR-scaled wick and close < that swing high and close < open OR close < most recently confirmed swing low with prior structural trend bullish (bearish CHoCH) or bearish (bearish BOS) OR a short entry setup is true
Long: recent bullish sweep (current or prior bar) AND bullish structural trend AND (enabled bullish CHoCH or enabled bullish continuation while structural trend is bullish) AND long confluence score ≥ minimum AND (disabled higher-timeframe filter or fast higher-timeframe EMA > slow higher-timeframe EMA) AND (disabled premium/discount filter or close ≤ dealing-range midpoint) AND (disabled VWAP filter or close > session VWAP) AND (disabled ADX filter or ADX ≥ threshold) AND (disabled session filter or within configured session) AND same-direction cooldown elapsed; setup was false on prior bar
Short: recent bearish sweep (current or prior bar) AND bearish structural trend AND (enabled bearish CHoCH or enabled bearish continuation while structural trend is bearish) AND short confluence score ≥ minimum AND (disabled higher-timeframe filter or fast higher-timeframe EMA < slow higher-timeframe EMA) AND (disabled premium/discount filter or close ≥ dealing-range midpoint) AND (disabled VWAP filter or close < session VWAP) AND (disabled ADX filter or ADX ≥ threshold) AND (disabled session filter or within configured session) AND same-direction cooldown elapsed; setup was false on prior bar
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ first target OR high ≥ full target OR (trailing stop enabled and low ≤ trailing stop); evaluate after entry, with trailing-stop exits only after more than one bar in the trade
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ first target OR low ≤ full target OR (trailing stop enabled and high ≥ trailing stop); evaluate after entry, with trailing-stop exits only after more than one bar in the trade
Calculation: Long stop = most recently tracked swing low (or entry-bar low if sweep anchoring is disabled) − ATR buffer fixed at entry; short stop = most recently tracked swing high (or entry-bar high if sweep anchoring is disabled) + ATR buffer fixed at entry
Calculation: First and full targets = entry price ± entry-bar ATR × respective target multipliers (add for long, subtract for short)
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize from tracked swing low − entry-bar ATR × trail multiplier for long, or tracked swing high + entry-bar ATR × trail multiplier for short; thereafter advance to max(previous stop, current low − current ATR × trail multiplier) for long, or min(previous stop, current high + current ATR × trail multiplier) for short
