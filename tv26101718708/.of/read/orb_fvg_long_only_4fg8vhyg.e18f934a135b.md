Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configurable opening-range session, reset each day and locked when that session ends; session times use America/New_York.
Calculation: Prior-day close position = (prior completed daily bar's close − low) / (high − low), or the midpoint of the range of possible positions if high equals low.
Calculation: Weak-day streak = count of consecutive completed daily bars, ending with the prior day, whose close is below their low plus a configurable fraction of their high-low range; reset the count to zero after any other day.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current chart bar's low > high two chart bars earlier; retain the bar index of the most recent occurrence, regardless of subsequent gap mitigation.
Calculation: Entry stop = opening range low, opening range midpoint, or entry price minus a configurable multiple of chart-timeframe ATR, selected by parameter and fixed at entry.
Calculation: Entry risk = entry price − entry stop; take-profit levels = entry price + entry risk multiplied by the respective configurable reward-to-risk ratios, fixed at entry.
Long: Opening range is locked AND current close > opening range high AND previous close <= opening range high AND current bar is in the configurable trade session in America/New_York AND (daily bias filter disabled OR close > daily open) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > daily EMA of close) AND (prior-day trend filter disabled OR prior-day close position >= configurable threshold) AND (weak-day streak filter disabled OR weak-day streak < configurable threshold) AND (daily entry limit disabled OR no earlier long signal has occurred today) AND (fair value gap filter disabled OR bars since the most recent bullish fair value gap <= configurable lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > configurable multiple of chart-timeframe volume SMA). Daily open and daily EMA use the higher timeframe's last completed bar on historical chart bars, except at the daily bar's close when that just-completed bar is used; on a live bar they use the still-forming daily bar.
Exit: long, trade-plan display enabled at entry AND entry risk > 0 AND low <= entry stop.
Exit: long, trade-plan display enabled at entry AND entry risk > 0 AND high >= first take-profit level.
Exit: long, trade-plan display enabled at entry AND entry risk > 0 AND high >= second take-profit level.
