Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of source price × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each selected anchor boundary
Calculation: Volume-weighted standard deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative sum of source price² × volume / cumulative volume − Anchored VWAP²); upper and lower 2σ bands = Anchored VWAP ± 2 × volume-weighted standard deviation
Calculation: Cross quality score = (directional momentum score + volume score + optional sigma confluence bonus) / maximum possible score × 100; directional momentum score = min(max((RSI − 50) for an upward cross or (50 − RSI) for a downward cross, 0) / 25 × 40, 40); volume score = min(volume / volume SMA × 10, 30); optional bonus = 5 when the prior bar touched the opposing 2σ band, otherwise 0; maximum possible score = 70 without the bonus or 75 when enabled
Long: prior close ≥ prior Anchored VWAP AND close < Anchored VWAP AND Cross quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND bars since last plotted cross ≥ minimum bar cooldown AND (no prior plotted cross price OR abs(close − last plotted cross price) ≥ ATR × minimum ATR cooldown)
Short: prior close ≤ prior Anchored VWAP AND close > Anchored VWAP AND Cross quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND bars since last plotted cross ≥ minimum bar cooldown AND (no prior plotted cross price OR abs(close − last plotted cross price) ≥ ATR × minimum ATR cooldown)
