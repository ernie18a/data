Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of source price times volume divided by cumulative volume since the selected session, week, or month boundary; use close if cumulative volume is zero. The source is a price series derived from the available bar fields.
Calculation: Weighted deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative sum of source price squared times volume divided by cumulative volume minus anchored VWAP squared); use zero if cumulative volume is zero. Outer bands equal anchored VWAP plus or minus the outer-band multiplier times weighted deviation.
Calculation: Cross quality = 100 times (momentum score + volume score + confluence score) divided by (70 + 5 if confluence is enabled, otherwise 70). For an upward cross, momentum score = min(40, max(RSI(close) - 50, 0) / 25 * 40); for a downward cross, use min(40, max(50 - RSI(close), 0) / 25 * 40). Volume score = min(30, volume / SMA(volume) / 3 * 30), using a volume ratio of 1 if the volume SMA is not positive. Confluence score is 5 if confluence is enabled and the previous low is at or below the previous lower outer band for an upward cross, or the previous high is at or above the previous upper outer band for a downward cross; otherwise it is zero.
Calculation: Qualified cross = close crosses anchored VWAP AND cross quality is at least the minimum quality threshold AND the number of bars since the last qualified cross is at least the cooldown length AND either no previous qualified cross exists or the absolute difference between close and the close at the last qualified cross is at least the cooldown multiplier times current ATR. Track qualified crosses in both directions together; before ATR is available, use current true range.
Long: Close crosses above anchored VWAP AND the cross is qualified.
Short: Close crosses below anchored VWAP AND the cross is qualified.
Long: Close crosses below anchored VWAP AND the cross is qualified AND cross quality is at least the strong-cross threshold.
Short: Close crosses above anchored VWAP AND the cross is qualified AND cross quality is at least the strong-cross threshold.
Long: Close is below anchored VWAP AND absolute distance from anchored VWAP divided by ATR is at least the fade-distance threshold AND the current bar is not a qualified cross with quality at least the strong-cross threshold.
Short: Close is above anchored VWAP AND absolute distance from anchored VWAP divided by ATR is at least the fade-distance threshold AND the current bar is not a qualified cross with quality at least the strong-cross threshold.
Long: Close is above anchored VWAP.
Short: Close is at or below anchored VWAP.
