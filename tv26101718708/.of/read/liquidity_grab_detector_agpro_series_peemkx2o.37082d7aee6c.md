Calculation: Liquidity levels = Maintain separate ordered high and low registries. Add enabled confirmed swing pivots, completed session highs and lows when the session ends, and the last completed bars' highs and lows at the start of each of two higher timeframes, the second longer than the first. A swing pivot has equal numbers of preceding and following bars. When adding a level, merge it into the first same-side level within an ATR-based tolerance, retaining the higher high or lower low and the original registration bar; otherwise append it. Timestamp pivots at the pivot bar, session levels at the session's final bar, and higher-timeframe levels at the current bar. Before detection, remove oldest levels while their age exceeds the lookback, then remove oldest levels until each registry is within its size limit. After a confirmation, remove from its side's registry the levels swept on the confirmation bar, even if the original sweep occurred earlier.
Calculation: Sweep candidate = On a completed bar, collect all registered highs for which high > level AND previous high <= level, or all registered lows for which low < level AND previous low >= level. The count must be at least the minimum and the difference between the maximum and minimum collected levels must be at most an ATR multiple. If enabled, volume must be greater than its SMA multiplied by a volume factor, and the rejection wick must be at least an ATR multiple: high minus max(open, close) for highs, or min(open, close) minus low for lows. Bars since the most recent confirmation must be at least the cooldown. If both sides qualify on the same bar, retain only the side with more swept levels; retain highs on a tie.
Calculation: Confirmed grab = Queue each sweep candidate with its side, bar and swept-level minimum and maximum. On each completed bar, including the candidate bar, confirm and remove each pending high sweep if the reversal filter is disabled OR close < its swept-level minimum; confirm and remove each pending low sweep if the filter is disabled OR close > its swept-level maximum. Confirmation must occur no later than the reversal-window limit; remove unconfirmed candidates at that limit. If multiple candidates confirm on one bar, only the last in queue order determines the confirmed side and cluster for that bar. Pending candidates can confirm during cooldown.
Calculation: Active setup = The latest confirmed grab sets the setup side, confirmation bar and target, replacing any previous setup. For a low sweep, target = swept-level maximum + confirmation-bar ATR multiplied by a target-height factor; for a high sweep, target = swept-level minimum - confirmation-bar ATR multiplied by that factor. Starting on the next bar, mark the target reached when high >= the low-sweep target or low <= the high-sweep target. Expire the setup when bars since confirmation exceed the setup-duration limit.
Long: A low-sweep grab is confirmed.
Short: A high-sweep grab is confirmed.
Long: The active setup is a low-sweep setup AND its target has not been reached AND bars since confirmation <= the reversal-window limit plus the setup extension.
Short: The active setup is a high-sweep setup AND its target has not been reached AND bars since confirmation <= the reversal-window limit plus the setup extension.
Long: The cumulative number of confirmed low-sweep grabs > the cumulative number of confirmed high-sweep grabs.
Short: The cumulative number of confirmed high-sweep grabs > the cumulative number of confirmed low-sweep grabs.
Long: The latest confirmed grab is a low-sweep grab.
Short: The latest confirmed grab is a high-sweep grab.
