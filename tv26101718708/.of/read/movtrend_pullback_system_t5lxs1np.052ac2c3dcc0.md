Calculation: Trend line = linear regression of close over a lookback, evaluated at the current bar.
Calculation: Trend slope = current trend line minus its value a lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: Gap proximity = close is within an ATR-multiple distance of either boundary or inside any of the most recent retained gap zones. A bullish gap zone spans high two bars ago to current low when current low > high two bars ago; a bearish gap zone spans current high to low two bars ago when current high < low two bars ago. Retain the most recent configured number of zones, including new zones on the current bar.
Calculation: Gated upper stretch = close > trend line + ATR multiplier × ATR AND, if the corresponding filter is enabled and mandatory, ATR percentile rank over its lookback >= its threshold, gap proximity is true, volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier with volume SMA > 0, and trend slope > 0 AND trend slope < trend slope a deceleration lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: Gated lower stretch = close < trend line − ATR multiplier × ATR AND, if the corresponding filter is enabled and mandatory, ATR percentile rank over its lookback >= its threshold, gap proximity is true, volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier with volume SMA > 0, and trend slope < 0 AND trend slope > trend slope a deceleration lookback number of bars ago.
Long: Trend slope > 0 AND low <= trend line AND close > trend line.
Short: Trend slope < 0 AND high >= trend line AND close < trend line.
Exit: long, close > trend line + ATR multiplier × ATR AND NOT (trend slope > 0 AND low <= trend line AND close > trend line) AND NOT (trend slope < 0 AND high >= trend line AND close < trend line) AND (gated upper stretch is false OR first-occurrence-only is disabled OR both gated upper stretch and gated lower stretch were false on the previous bar).
Exit: short, close < trend line − ATR multiplier × ATR AND NOT (trend slope > 0 AND low <= trend line AND close > trend line) AND NOT (trend slope < 0 AND high >= trend line AND close < trend line) AND (gated lower stretch is false OR first-occurrence-only is disabled OR both gated upper stretch and gated lower stretch were false on the previous bar).
