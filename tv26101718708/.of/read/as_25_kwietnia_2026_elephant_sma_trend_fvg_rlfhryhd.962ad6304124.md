Calculation: Candle range = high - low
Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low
Long: high - low > ATR × multiplier AND body-to-range ratio > minimum body fraction AND close > open OR low > high two chart bars ago OR close > SMA(close, short lookback) OR SMA(close, short lookback) > its prior-bar value OR SMA(close, medium lookback) > SMA(close, long lookback) AND NOT (abs(close - SMA(close, long lookback)) / SMA(close, long lookback) < range threshold) OR close crosses above the EMA of close calculated on a higher timeframe bar still forming
Short: high - low > ATR × multiplier AND body-to-range ratio > minimum body fraction AND close < open OR high < low two chart bars ago OR close <= SMA(close, short lookback) OR SMA(close, short lookback) <= its prior-bar value OR SMA(close, medium lookback) < SMA(close, long lookback) AND NOT (abs(close - SMA(close, long lookback)) / SMA(close, long lookback) < range threshold) OR close crosses below the EMA of close calculated on a higher timeframe bar still forming
