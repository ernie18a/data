Calculation: Strong candle = abs(close - open) > configured fraction of (high - low)
Calculation: Bullish CISD = Strong candle AND (a requested aggregation stream’s current close > its current open and its previous close < its previous open); use the current requested bar, which may still be forming, and the previous requested bar. Each stream’s timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bearish CISD = Strong candle AND (a requested aggregation stream’s current close < its current open and its previous close > its previous open); use the current requested bar, which may still be forming, and the previous requested bar. Each stream’s timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high (prior close at or below that level); bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low (prior close at or above that level). Pivots require the configured number of bars on both sides for confirmation.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = the most recently mitigated bullish fair value gap, where a bullish fair value gap is formed when current low > high two bars ago and the gap exceeds the configured minimum; it becomes mitigated when low <= its upper boundary. Bearish IFVG = the most recently mitigated bearish fair value gap, where a bearish fair value gap is formed when current high < low two bars ago and the gap exceeds the configured minimum; it becomes mitigated when high >= its lower boundary.
Long: Chart timeframe is one of the indicator’s designated low timeframes AND low < rolling lowest low (configured lookback, including the current bar) AND bullish structure break after a bearish trend AND a bullish IFVG exists and low <= its upper boundary AND close > open AND Bullish CISD
Short: Chart timeframe is one of the indicator’s designated low timeframes AND high > rolling highest high (configured lookback, including the current bar) AND bearish structure break after a bullish trend AND a bearish IFVG exists and high >= its lower boundary AND close < open AND Bearish CISD
