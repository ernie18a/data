Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low with the specified number of bars on each side required by the pivot-high or pivot-low calculation; it becomes available only after the right-side bars have completed. Use separate shorter internal, longer swing-structure, and swing-point pivot lengths.
Calculation: Structure break = for either internal or swing-structure pivots, the close first exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high or falls below the latest confirmed pivot low since that pivot was confirmed. Each new pivot resets its corresponding broken flag. Structure bias starts neutral and becomes bullish on an upward break or bearish on a downward break; if both occur on one bar, bearish is assigned last.
Calculation: Active order blocks = on an internal upward break, scan preceding bars back to the latest confirmed internal pivot low, or over the internal pivot length if unavailable, capped by a maximum scan length and available history; select the most recent bar with the lowest low and create a bullish zone from its low to max(open, close). On an internal downward break, similarly scan back to the latest confirmed internal pivot high and create a bearish zone from min(open, close) to the high of the most recent bar with the highest high. Require zone top > bottom; optionally reject zones overlapping an existing same-side zone, including touching edges. Keep at most the configured number per side, removing the oldest first. After creation and before touch detection, remove bullish zones when close < bottom or, under wick mitigation, low < bottom; remove bearish zones when close > top or, under wick mitigation, high > top.
Calculation: Order-block touch = for any surviving bullish zone, low <= zone top AND previous bar low > that same top; for any surviving bearish zone, high >= zone bottom AND previous bar high < that same bottom. Record the latest touch bar per side and clear it after its corresponding qualified setup.
Calculation: Qualified setup filter = aggressive requires no additional filter; balanced requires swing-structure bias to be neutral or aligned with the setup OR close to be on the corresponding side of the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing-structure pivot high and low; strict requires both. The corresponding side is close <= midpoint for long and close >= midpoint for short.
Calculation: Gap data = chart-timeframe high, low and ATR, or values requested from another timeframe with historical lookahead disabled and gaps filled. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within each chart bar. Index the resulting series by chart bars, including the two-bar offset. Track displayed gaps per side, optionally rejecting a new zone that overlaps an existing same-side zone, including touching edges; retain at most the configured number per side, deleting the oldest first. After creation, remove bullish gaps when close < bottom, low <= bottom, or low <= midpoint according to the selected close, wick, or midpoint mitigation; remove bearish gaps when close > top, high >= top, or high >= midpoint respectively. The no-mitigation option retains them until the count limit removes them.
Long: An internal or swing-structure upward break occurs.
Short: An internal or swing-structure downward break occurs.
Long: A swing-point pivot high is confirmed AND its value >= the previous confirmed swing-point pivot high, OR no previous swing-point pivot high exists.
Short: A swing-point pivot high is confirmed AND its value < the previous confirmed swing-point pivot high.
Long: A swing-point pivot low is confirmed AND its value > the previous confirmed swing-point pivot low.
Short: A swing-point pivot low is confirmed AND its value <= the previous confirmed swing-point pivot low, OR no previous swing-point pivot low exists.
Long: A bullish order-block touch occurs.
Short: A bearish order-block touch occurs.
Long: An internal upward break occurs AND the preceding internal structure bias is not bearish AND the latest bullish order-block touch is within the configured validity window AND the selected qualified setup filter passes.
Short: An internal downward break occurs AND the preceding internal structure bias is not bullish AND the latest bearish order-block touch is within the configured validity window AND the selected qualified setup filter passes.
Long: Gap-data low > gap-data high two chart bars earlier AND the difference >= gap-data ATR multiplied by the configured volatility threshold.
Short: Gap-data high < gap-data low two chart bars earlier AND the difference >= gap-data ATR multiplied by the configured volatility threshold.
