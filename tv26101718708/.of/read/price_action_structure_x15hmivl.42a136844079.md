Calculation: Confirmed structural pivot highs/lows = highs/lows with the configured number of bars on both sides; usable only after the right-side bars complete. Internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots; each new pivot replaces the latest of its type.
Calculation: Swing-point labels = each confirmed pivot high compared with the previous confirmed pivot high (higher = HH, lower = LH), and each confirmed pivot low compared with the previous confirmed pivot low (higher = HL, lower = LL); use their separately configured lookback.
Calculation: Active bullish order block = the zone from the low to the greater of open and close of the lowest-low candle scanned from the latest internal pivot low through an internal bullish break. Active bearish order block = the zone from the lesser of open and close to the high of the highest-high candle scanned from the latest internal pivot high through an internal bearish break. Limit each scan to its configured maximum; remove a zone when the configured mitigation comparison is met (bullish: close below its low or low below its low; bearish: close above its high or high above its high).
Calculation: Active swing-range midpoint = the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing pivot high and latest confirmed swing pivot low.
Calculation: FVG = bullish when the selected timeframe's current low exceeds its high two bars earlier; bearish when its current high is below its low two bars earlier. Use the current chart timeframe or the configured other timeframe; for another timeframe, use security data with lookahead off (the current bar may still be forming in real time). If the volatility threshold is enabled, require gap size >= ATR × threshold.
Long: close > latest unbroken internal pivot high OR close > latest unbroken swing pivot high OR a confirmed swing pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed swing pivot high OR a confirmed swing pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed swing pivot low OR low <= active bullish order-block high AND prior-bar low > active bullish order-block high OR bullish FVG forms OR a bullish setup confirms
Long: bullish setup confirms when a bullish order-block touch occurred within the configured setup window AND an internal bullish BOS occurs (close above the latest unbroken internal pivot high, with prior internal trend not bearish) AND (quality is Aggressive OR (quality is Balanced AND (swing bias is nonnegative OR close <= active swing-range midpoint)) OR (quality is Strict AND swing bias is nonnegative AND close <= active swing-range midpoint)). Swing bias is the direction of the latest swing break, initially neutral. A bullish order-block touch is valid only while its zone remains active.
Short: close < latest unbroken internal pivot low OR close < latest unbroken swing pivot low OR a confirmed swing pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed swing pivot high OR a confirmed swing pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed swing pivot low OR high >= active bearish order-block low AND prior-bar high < active bearish order-block low OR bearish FVG forms OR a bearish setup confirms
Short: bearish setup confirms when a bearish order-block touch occurred within the configured setup window AND an internal bearish BOS occurs (close below the latest unbroken internal pivot low, with prior internal trend not bullish) AND (quality is Aggressive OR (quality is Balanced AND (swing bias is nonpositive OR close >= active swing-range midpoint)) OR (quality is Strict AND swing bias is nonpositive AND close >= active swing-range midpoint)). Swing bias is the direction of the latest swing break, initially neutral. A bearish order-block touch is valid only while its zone remains active.
