Calculation: Active support and resistance levels = On each confirmed chart bar outside the configured blocked sessions, process each enabled timeframe in order. A confirmed pivot high creates resistance and a confirmed pivot low creates support; each pivot uses the same number of bars on either side. Other timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe: use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, carrying the last returned pivot value forward between updates. For each returned pivot, find the newest active level of the same kind within a configurable multiple of chart-timeframe ATR. Optionally reduce that multiple when shorter-lookback ATR exceeds a configurable multiple of longer-lookback ATR. If a level is found, replace its price with the average of its price and the pivot price; otherwise create a new level. Remove the oldest stored level when the configured capacity is exceeded. After processing pivots, invalidate each active resistance when the selected break price exceeds it and invalidate each active support when the selected break price falls below it, provided the optional volume filter passes. The selected break price is either close or, respectively, high for resistance and low for support. The volume filter passes when volume is at least a configurable multiple of its moving average.
Long: Selected break price > active resistance AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= configurable multiple of moving average volume)
Short: Selected break price < active support AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= configurable multiple of moving average volume)
