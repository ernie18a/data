Calculation: Fair value gap = On a confirmed chart bar outside the first two bars of a new day on intraday charts and the first two bars of a new week on intraday or daily charts, a bullish gap has bottom = high two bars ago and top = current low when top > bottom; a bearish gap has bottom = current high and top = low two bars ago when top > bottom. Require gap size divided by current close to be at least the minimum percentage. Create gaps only within the configured trailing chart-bar lookback. Keep unconsumed gaps as bases, subject to the configured maximum count, discarding the oldest first. Starting after a fixed delay, update each bullish base's top to max(bottom, min(top, the delayed bar's low or close)) and each bearish base's bottom to min(top, max(bottom, the delayed bar's high or close)); remove a base when top <= bottom.
Calculation: Crossfire zone = Process each new gap first against every active opposite-direction zone and then against every opposite-direction base. A strict positive overlap flips a zone into a new zone with the gap's direction, overlap bounds, and one more star; or consumes a base and creates a zone with the gap's direction, overlap bounds, and one star. If zone combining is enabled, a new base-derived zone instead expands the first overlapping active zone of the same direction to their union, retaining its existing star count. A gap that creates or flips any zone is consumed; otherwise retain it as a base. Process zones in creation order and discard the oldest when the configured maximum count is exceeded. Starting on the bar after creation, a zone is touched when high >= its bottom AND low <= its top; its first such bar and each subsequent transition from untouched to touched are retests. After checking for a retest, end the entire zone chain when a bullish zone's low or close <= its bottom or a bearish zone's high or close >= its top, according to the selected mitigation method.
Calculation: Captured retest = The last retest in zone creation order on the current bar, including its direction, stars, and far side; the far side is the bottom for a bullish zone and the top for a bearish zone. A qualifying captured retest has stars >= the minimum. A qualifying retest replaces the pending retest of its direction; it remains pending through the configured number of chart bars after the retest, including the retest bar, and is cleared after a same-direction entry. Zone mitigation does not clear a pending retest.
Calculation: Structure break = On chart bars, track the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using equal numbers of bars on both sides. An upward break occurs when close crosses above the latest pivot high plus a price buffer; a downward break occurs when close crosses below the latest pivot low minus a price buffer. For a crossover, compare the current close and current pivot threshold with their respective previous-bar values.
Calculation: Active VWAP = The cumulative sum of chart-bar typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each configured day-open anchor or at the first bar; use the equivalent VWAP reset at the configured New York session anchor instead when inside that session and the elapsed time since its most recent anchor is at least the grace period. The New York VWAP also initializes on the first bar.
Calculation: Displacement = High - low >= ATR multiplied by the minimum range factor AND ATR > 0 AND absolute(close - open) / (high - low) >= the minimum body ratio, with the ratio set to zero when high = low; optionally also require close > open for a long or close < open for a short.
Calculation: Stop and target = At each entry, use the entry close and either the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over the configured lookback excluding the entry bar, or the far side saved with the latest qualifying same-direction retest; fall back to the structure reference if that far side is unavailable. Subtract a price buffer for a long stop or add it for a short stop. If the stop is not beyond the entry in the adverse direction, place it one price increment beyond the entry. Set the target at entry plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a configured multiple of the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Retain these levels until replaced by another entry or reached.
Long: An active bullish zone has a retest.
Short: An active bearish zone has a retest.
Long: A qualifying bullish captured retest occurs when structure confirmation is disabled OR a bullish retest is pending AND an upward structure break occurs when structure confirmation is enabled; AND displacement passes if enabled AND close > active VWAP if the VWAP filter is enabled AND the bar is within a configured trading session if the session filter is enabled AND bars since the previous long entry >= the cooldown, or no previous long entry exists.
Short: A qualifying bearish captured retest occurs when structure confirmation is disabled OR a bearish retest is pending AND a downward structure break occurs when structure confirmation is enabled; AND displacement passes if enabled AND close < active VWAP if the VWAP filter is enabled AND the bar is within a configured trading session if the session filter is enabled AND bars since the previous short entry >= the cooldown, or no previous short entry exists.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= target).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= target).
