Calculation: HTF bar = consecutive chart bars aggregated into one bar of a higher timeframe than the chart (open of first, highest high, lowest low, close of last); only completed HTF bars are used, never the one still forming.
Calculation: Demand zone = [low, high] of the latest completed bearish HTF bar (close < open), created when a later completed bullish HTF bar (close >= open) closes above that bearish bar's high; at most one zone per bearish bar. It stays active until a chart bar closes below the zone low.
Calculation: Supply zone = [low, high] of the latest completed bullish HTF bar (close >= open), created when a later completed bearish HTF bar (close < open) closes below that bullish bar's low; at most one zone per bullish bar. It stays active until a chart bar closes above the zone high.
Calculation: Bullish engulf = chart-bar close > high of the most recent bearish chart bar (close < open) that has not already been engulfed; that bar is then marked engulfed, and its low is the engulfed low. Bearish engulf = chart-bar close < low of the most recent bullish chart bar (close > open) that has not already been engulfed; that bar is then marked engulfed, and its high is the engulfed high.
Long: Bullish engulf on the current chart bar AND the previous chart bar's open lies within [low, high] of any active demand zone. Enter at the close, with stop = engulfed low.
Short: Bearish engulf on the current chart bar AND the previous chart bar's open lies within [low, high] of any active supply zone. Enter at the close, with stop = engulfed high.
Exit: long, low <= stop (engulfed low at entry) OR high >= entry + fixed multiple * (entry - stop) OR Short condition is true
Exit: short, high >= stop (engulfed high at entry) OR low <= entry - fixed multiple * (stop - entry) OR Long condition is true
