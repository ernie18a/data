Calculation: Aggregated candle = OHLCV range of a group of chart bars; evaluate the last completed group when a new group begins. The group timeframe is the chart timeframe multiplied by the aggregation factor.
Calculation: Demand zone = range of the most recent bearish aggregated candle when a later bullish aggregated candle closes above that bearish candle’s high. A bullish aggregated candle has close >= open.
Calculation: Supply zone = range of the most recent bullish aggregated candle when a later bearish aggregated candle closes below that bullish candle’s low. A bearish aggregated candle has close < open.
Long: Trading is within the configured date and UTC-hour ranges AND trade mode permits longs AND the current close is above the high of the most recent bearish chart candle whose engulfing has not already triggered AND the previous chart bar’s open is inside an active demand zone AND that bearish candle’s low is defined
Short: Trading is within the configured date and UTC-hour ranges AND trade mode permits shorts AND the current close is below the low of the most recent bullish chart candle whose engulfing has not already triggered AND the previous chart bar’s open is inside an active supply zone AND that bullish candle’s high is defined
Exit: Long, low <= the low of the engulfed bearish candle OR high >= the configured target (fixed target price, or entry price plus the configured risk multiple times the distance from entry to that candle’s low)
Exit: Short, high >= the high of the engulfed bullish candle OR low <= the configured target (fixed target price, or entry price minus the configured risk multiple times the distance from that candle’s high to entry)
