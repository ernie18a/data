Calculation: Support and resistance levels = On the calculation timeframe, identify a fractal low when the low three bars ago is lower than each of the two preceding lows, each of the next two lows is successively higher, and the volume at the fractal low exceeds its volume SMA. Identify a fractal high using the opposite high comparisons and the same volume condition. On confirmation, set support to the lower of the fractal-low bar's open and close, and resistance to the higher of the fractal-high bar's open and close; retain each level until its next fractal. When the calculation timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed calculation bar; when lower, use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar. On the same timeframe, use the current completed bar.
Long: datetime is within the inclusive trading window AND position is flat or short AND close crosses below support, with previous close >= previous support.
Exit: long, datetime is within the inclusive trading window AND close crosses above resistance, with previous close <= previous resistance.
