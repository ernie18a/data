Calculation: Panel bars = a bounded history of higher-timeframe bars, newest first, including the bar still forming; index 0 is the forming bar.
Calculation: Reference ATR = ATR of the forming higher-timeframe bar; if unavailable or nonpositive, use the mean true range across the available panel bars.
Calculation: Zone bounds = the original bounds, or their midpoint plus and minus half a configurable height; height is a multiple of reference ATR or a percentage of the forming higher-timeframe close.
Calculation: Gap strength = a configurable stepwise score based on gap size divided by reference ATR; if strength ranking is disabled, use a configurable constant score.
Calculation: Order-block strength = a configurable constant plus configurable stepwise scores for displacement divided by reference ATR, candidate age in panel bars, and candidate range divided by reference ATR; clamp the result to a configurable range. If strength ranking is disabled, use a configurable constant score.
Calculation: Bullish gap = for panel bar i, its low minus the high of bar i+2; its original bounds are those two prices. A subsequent bar is any bar with index less than i.
Calculation: Bearish gap = for panel bar i, the low of bar i+2 minus its high; its original bounds are those two prices. A subsequent bar is any bar with index less than i.
Calculation: Gap mitigation level = the original near edge, far edge, or midpoint, selected by a configurable mitigation method.
Calculation: Swing low or high = a panel bar whose low or high is respectively no higher or no lower than every bar within a configurable number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Bullish order-block candidate = the first bearish panel bar found when scanning older bars from a swing low within a configurable lookback whose range is positive and whose swing-low price minus candidate low exceeds a configurable multiple of its range; its original bounds are its high and low. Its strength displacement is the forming panel close minus its low.
Calculation: Bearish order-block candidate = the first bullish panel bar found when scanning older bars from a swing high within a configurable lookback whose range is positive and whose high minus swing-high price exceeds a configurable multiple of its range; its original bounds are its high and low. Its strength displacement is its high minus the forming panel close.
Long: Bullish gap > 0 AND gap >= a configurable multiple of reference ATR when the ATR filter is enabled AND no subsequent panel low <= its mitigation level when mitigation removal is enabled AND gap strength >= a configurable minimum AND its rank among qualifying bullish gaps scanned newest first <= a configurable limit.
Short: Bearish gap > 0 AND gap >= a configurable multiple of reference ATR when the ATR filter is enabled AND no subsequent panel high >= its mitigation level when mitigation removal is enabled AND gap strength >= a configurable minimum AND its rank among qualifying bearish gaps scanned newest first <= a configurable limit.
Long: Bullish order-block candidate exists AND no more recent panel close < its zone lower bound when mitigation removal is enabled AND its strength >= a configurable minimum AND its rank among qualifying bullish order blocks scanned by swing from newest to oldest <= a configurable limit.
Short: Bearish order-block candidate exists AND no more recent panel close > its zone upper bound when mitigation removal is enabled AND its strength >= a configurable minimum AND its rank among qualifying bearish order blocks scanned by swing from newest to oldest <= a configurable limit.
