Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest over equal numbers of bars before and after it; it becomes available only after the bars to its right have completed.
Calculation: Structure trend = initially neutral; update to bullish when close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high and to bearish when close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. A crossing compares both the current and previous close with the corresponding current and previous pivot levels.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe structure trends = structure trend calculated independently on three increasingly long timeframes using the same pivot length. Relative to the chart timeframe, each can be higher or lower: use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, or the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. A higher-timeframe bar still forming is used on a realtime chart bar.
Calculation: Up/down volume ratio = sum of volume on bars whose close exceeds the previous close divided by sum of volume on bars whose close is below the previous close over the same lookback; use the neutral ratio parameter when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed reversal pivot low AND close > that pivot low; bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed reversal pivot high AND close < that pivot high. A sweep is trend-valid when close is below the trend EMA for a bullish sweep or above it for a bearish sweep.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = latest confirmed reversal pivot low < preceding reversal pivot low AND RSI at the latest pivot > RSI at the preceding pivot; bearish divergence = latest confirmed reversal pivot high > preceding reversal pivot high AND RSI at the latest pivot < RSI at the preceding pivot. Use the RSI value at each pivot bar.
Calculation: Bullish ATR reclaim = low < baseline EMA − ATR × extension multiplier AND close > baseline EMA − ATR × inner-band multiplier; bearish ATR rejection = high > baseline EMA + ATR × extension multiplier AND close < baseline EMA + ATR × inner-band multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish failed breakdown = low < previous low AND close > previous low AND close > open; bearish failed breakout = high > previous high AND close < previous high AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish reversal score = capped sum of configurable weights for these true conditions: trend-valid bullish sweep, or a smaller weight for a bullish sweep that is not trend-valid; bullish divergence; lower wick > body × wick multiplier AND lower wick > ATR × wick ATR multiplier; volume / volume SMA >= climax threshold; bullish ATR reclaim; close > previous high OR bullish failed breakdown; previous Williams %R <= oversold threshold AND current Williams %R > previous Williams %R; count of bullish or neutral multi-timeframe structure trends >= agreement threshold; low <= latest confirmed reversal pivot low + ATR × proximity multiplier; RSI below a bullish fade threshold AND RSI > previous RSI; up/down volume ratio < lower ratio threshold; record or rolling-lookback maximum volume AND close < trend EMA. If body is zero, substitute the minimum price increment parameter for body. Record and rolling-maximum volume contribute only on eligible chart timeframes.
Calculation: Bearish reversal score = capped sum of configurable weights for these true conditions: trend-valid bearish sweep, or a smaller weight for a bearish sweep that is not trend-valid; bearish divergence; upper wick > body × wick multiplier AND upper wick > ATR × wick ATR multiplier; volume / volume SMA >= climax threshold; bearish ATR rejection; close < previous low OR bearish failed breakout; previous Williams %R >= overbought threshold AND current Williams %R < previous Williams %R; count of bearish or neutral multi-timeframe structure trends >= agreement threshold; high >= latest confirmed reversal pivot high − ATR × proximity multiplier; RSI above a bearish fade threshold AND RSI < previous RSI; up/down volume ratio > upper ratio threshold; record or rolling-lookback maximum volume AND close > trend EMA. If body is zero, substitute the minimum price increment parameter for body. Record and rolling-maximum volume contribute only on eligible chart timeframes.
Calculation: Bullish reversal signal = bullish core AND bullish reversal score >= minimum score AND no earlier bullish reversal signal since the latest confirmed reversal pivot low AND bars since the previous bullish reversal signal > reversal cooldown, if one exists AND (bars since the latest bearish structure change > structure cooldown OR no bearish structure change exists OR bullish sweep is trend-valid OR structure cooldown is disabled) AND (trend filter is disabled OR strong bearish trend is absent OR bullish sweep is trend-valid OR bullish ATR reclaim) AND (extension filter is disabled OR close < trend EMA OR bullish ATR reclaim). Bullish core = bullish sweep OR bullish ATR reclaim OR bullish failed breakdown. Strong bearish trend = close < short EMA AND short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA AND ADX > trend-strength threshold.
Calculation: Bearish reversal signal = bearish core AND bearish reversal score >= minimum score AND no earlier bearish reversal signal since the latest confirmed reversal pivot high AND bars since the previous bearish reversal signal > reversal cooldown, if one exists AND (bars since the latest bullish structure change > structure cooldown OR no bullish structure change exists OR bearish sweep is trend-valid OR structure cooldown is disabled) AND (trend filter is disabled OR strong bullish trend is absent OR bearish sweep is trend-valid OR bearish ATR rejection) AND (extension filter is disabled OR close > trend EMA OR bearish ATR rejection). Bearish core = bearish sweep OR bearish ATR rejection OR bearish failed breakout. Strong bullish trend = close > short EMA AND short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA AND ADX > trend-strength threshold.
Calculation: Momentum score = rounded, bounded weighted sum of normalized RSI, the fraction of short, medium and long EMAs below close, normalized MACD histogram, direction-adjusted relative volume, direction-adjusted body size relative to ATR, and normalized up/down volume ratio. Normalize the MACD histogram using the rolling maximum absolute histogram; cap relative volume and body size before normalization; invert the latter two components on bearish candles; clamp the volume-ratio component to its range.
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high AND preceding structure trend is not bullish.
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low AND preceding structure trend is not bearish.
Long: volume > volume SMA × high-volume multiplier AND close >= open.
Short: volume > volume SMA × high-volume multiplier AND close < open.
Long: bullish reversal signal.
Short: bearish reversal signal.
Long: bullish reversal signal is false AND bullish core AND bullish reversal score >= setup threshold.
Short: bearish reversal signal is false AND bearish core AND bearish reversal score >= setup threshold.
Long: any multi-timeframe structure trend is bullish.
Short: any multi-timeframe structure trend is bearish.
Long: RSI > midpoint threshold OR RSI <= oversold threshold.
Short: RSI <= midpoint threshold OR RSI >= overbought threshold.
Long: momentum score > midpoint threshold.
Short: momentum score < midpoint threshold.
Long: up/down volume ratio >= upper dashboard threshold.
Short: up/down volume ratio <= lower dashboard threshold.
Long: Williams %R <= oversold threshold.
Short: Williams %R >= overbought threshold.
