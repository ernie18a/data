Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of an equal configurable number of bars on both sides; it becomes available after the bars to its right complete.
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the configured opening interval, fixed after that interval ends.
Long: During the market session and 4:00–17:00 New York time, price trades below the latest unswept confirmed swing low and closes above it, the close-to-low distance is at least a configurable ATR multiple, and the configured sweep cooldown has elapsed OR exactly the configured number of bars after such a sweep, close is above the sweep bar’s low and above open OR low is at or below session VWAP and close is above session VWAP OR the opening range is locked and close crosses above its high from at or below it OR the opening range is locked and low touches or crosses its high while close finishes above it OR EMA with the shorter lookback is above EMA with the longer lookback, close is above session VWAP, close is within a configurable ATR distance of either EMA with the longer lookback, session VWAP, or a locked opening-range high/low, and close is above open.
Short: During the market session and 4:00–17:00 New York time, price trades above the latest unswept confirmed swing high and closes below it, the high-to-close distance is at least a configurable ATR multiple, and the configured sweep cooldown has elapsed OR exactly the configured number of bars after such a sweep, close is below the sweep bar’s high and below open OR high is at or above session VWAP and close is below session VWAP OR the opening range is locked and close crosses below its low from at or above it OR the opening range is locked and high touches or crosses its low while close finishes below it OR EMA with the shorter lookback is below EMA with the longer lookback, close is below session VWAP, close is within a configurable ATR distance of either EMA with the longer lookback, session VWAP, or a locked opening-range high/low, and close is below open.
