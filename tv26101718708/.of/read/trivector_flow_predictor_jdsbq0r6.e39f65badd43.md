Calculation: Convention = Evaluate on each chart bar; [1] means the preceding chart bar. Adjustable lengths, thresholds, weights, switches, price-rounding increment and multipliers are parameters. A crossover requires the current value to be strictly above the reference and the previous value to be at or below it; a crossunder is symmetric. Round prices to the configured price increment. Use the selected OHLC-derived source, normally HLC3.
Calculation: Fused trend = EMA of the weighted sum of zero-lag EMA, double EMA, Hull moving average, Kaufman adaptive moving average and a recursive filter of the source. The zero-lag EMA input is source plus its change over half the core lookback. The Kaufman smoothing factor is the square of the efficiency-weighted interpolation between fast and slow smoothing constants. The recursive filter starts at source and subsequently adds alpha times the difference between source and its previous value, where alpha is derived from the core lookback using (cos(omega)+sin(omega)-1)/cos(omega), omega being one full rotation divided by the lookback. The slow trend is an EMA of the fused trend with a longer lookback.
Calculation: Efficiency = absolute source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes over that lookback, bounded between zero and one; use zero if the denominator is zero. Fractal energy = one minus efficiency.
Calculation: Volatility unit = the greatest of ATR, a scaled moving average of close times the square root of squared log(high/low) divided by four times log(2), and a scaled moving average of close times the square root of max(half squared log(high/low) minus (two times log(2) minus one) times squared log(close/open), zero), multiplied by a profile factor. Use zero for invalid logarithms. Volatility ratio = volatility unit divided by its longer moving average, bounded to configured limits.
Calculation: Phase = EMA of (close minus fused trend) divided by volatility unit; phase velocity = phase minus its previous value; pressure = EMA of the one-bar fused-trend change divided by volatility unit; extension = absolute phase. Use zero when the volatility unit is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive rail = fused trend minus volatility unit times an adaptive distance in an up state, or plus that distance in a down state. The distance is a parameter multiplied by factors selected by high or low ADX and high or low efficiency. Initialize the state as up and the preceding rail as the current up-state candidate. In an up state, switch down and reset the rail to the down-state candidate when close is below the preceding rail; otherwise retain up and set the rail to the greater of the up-state candidate and preceding rail. In a down state, switch up and reset to the up-state candidate when close is above the preceding rail; otherwise retain down and set the rail to the lesser of the down-state candidate and preceding rail.
Calculation: Candle measures = range is max(high minus low, price increment); body is absolute close minus open; body ratio is body divided by range; normalized body is body divided by volatility unit, or zero if volatility is zero. Bullish close position is (close minus low)/range and bearish close position is (high minus close)/range. A quality bullish candle has close above open, bullish close position and body ratio at or above their thresholds; a quality bearish candle is symmetric. A bullish displacement has close above open, normalized body and bullish close position at or above their displacement thresholds; bearish displacement is symmetric. Volume ratio is volume divided by its moving average, or one if the average is zero. Normalized volume pressure is the bounded EMA of volume times (close minus open)/range divided by average volume, or zero if average volume is zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = for each of two higher timeframes, compare its close with its close EMA; count bullish and bearish agreements separately. On historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off; on a live bar use the higher-timeframe bar still forming. The two higher timeframes may have different durations and use the same EMA length.
Calculation: Compression = the preceding-bar rolling high minus preceding-bar rolling low, each over the compression lookback, divided by the current volatility unit, is at or below its threshold OR efficiency is below its compression threshold. Use a fallback ratio above the threshold when volatility is zero. The prior compression test uses the preceding bar's compression result. A confirmed swing high or low is a pivot with equal left and right confirmation lookbacks; use the latest pivot that was confirmed before the current bar when testing a structure break.
Calculation: Directional triggers = Apply each enabled trigger's own optional maximum-extension threshold. Bullish pulse: the minimum phase over the setup lookback is below the negative pullback threshold AND phase crosses above zero AND close is above fused trend AND phase velocity is positive. Bullish ignition: close exceeds the preceding-bar rolling high plus a volatility buffer AND previous close is at or below that same high AND prior compression is true AND bullish displacement is true AND phase velocity is positive. Bullish sweep: low is below the preceding-bar rolling low over the sweep lookback by more than a volatility buffer AND close is above that low AND the candle is quality bullish AND phase velocity is positive. Bullish rail flip: rail state changes from down to up AND close is above fused trend AND phase velocity is positive AND bullish close position is at or above its threshold. Bullish structure break: close exceeds the latest prior confirmed swing high plus a volatility buffer AND previous close is at or below that same swing high AND close is above fused trend AND phase velocity is positive. Bullish fair-value gap: low is above high two bars ago AND normalized body is at or above its threshold AND close is above open AND close is above fused trend AND phase velocity is positive. Bullish retest: within the retest lookback after the latest enabled bullish structure break, low is at or below its broken swing high plus a volatility tolerance AND close is above that level AND close is above open AND close is above fused trend AND phase velocity is nonnegative. Bearish triggers reverse highs and lows, comparison directions, candle directions, rail states and phase signs. Record the broken level and bar whenever the underlying buffered structure break occurs, even if its trend or velocity conditions fail. Pulse and retest share an extension threshold; each other trigger type has its own.
Calculation: Regimes = Chop is efficiency below its minimum OR ADX below its chop threshold AND fractal energy above its threshold. Impulse is ADX and efficiency at or above their impulse thresholds AND compression is false. Compression regime is compression true AND volatility ratio below its threshold. Expansion is volatility ratio above its threshold AND ADX above every ADX value in its rising lookback. Reversal risk is extension and fractal energy above their respective thresholds.
Calculation: Directional scores = For each direction add parameterized points for: close beyond the bias EMA, otherwise close beyond a shorter EMA; fused trend beyond its slower EMA with pressure in that direction, otherwise pressure in that direction; rail state in that direction; directional movement indicator stronger than its opposite; higher-timeframe agreement count, or fixed points if that filter is disabled; the first active trigger in priority order ignition, structure break, fair-value gap, retest, pulse, sweep, rail flip; phase and phase velocity both in that direction, otherwise phase velocity alone; directional close position in its higher or lower threshold band; displacement, otherwise a smaller directional body above its threshold; normalized volume pressure in its stronger or weaker directional threshold band; bounded ADX points; rising-ADX points; bounded efficiency points; bounded one-minus-fractal-energy points; volume-filter points if disabled or passed; points for the applicable extension band; and expansion points. Subtract the chop penalty, otherwise the high-fractal-energy penalty, and bound the result to the score range. Each tier, trigger and component has its own points. The base directional score uses the same components but excludes trigger, displacement, rising-ADX and expansion points.
Calculation: Directional bias = Set bullish when the bullish base score is at least the bearish base score plus the dominance threshold; otherwise set bearish when the bearish base score is at least the bullish base score plus that threshold; otherwise retain the previous bias, initialized neutral.
Calculation: Dynamic minimum score = base minimum score plus a chop adjustment minus an impulse adjustment plus a compression-regime adjustment, bounded to configured limits.
Calculation: Forecast = Calculate linear-regression slope of source over its lookback and regression quality as one minus the standard deviation of the rolling regression residual divided by the standard deviation of source, bounded between zero and one; use zero quality if the denominator is zero. Normalize and bound regression slope by volatility unit. Form a bounded flow vector from a weighted sum of bounded directional-score difference, phase and phase velocity, pressure, directional-indicator difference, quality-adjusted regression slope, signed rail state, signed higher-timeframe agreement difference when enabled, and normalized volume pressure. Forecast move equals volatility unit times flow vector times the square root of forecast horizon divided by its scaling parameter, forecast strength, the applicable impulse/compression/chop/default regime multiplier, a reversal-risk multiplier when applicable, and a regression-quality multiplier. Forecast price is rounded close plus forecast move. Forecast confidence is bounded absolute flow-vector magnitude plus regression-quality and impulse contributions minus a chop penalty.
Calculation: Major signal = A directional candidate requires an enabled directional trigger AND its score at or above the dynamic minimum AND its score at least the opposite score plus the dominance threshold AND the cooldown since the last major signal has elapsed AND the absolute close change since that signal is at least a volatility-unit multiple AND either same-direction signals are allowed or the last major signal had the opposite direction. Apply enabled filters: close beyond the bias EMA and session VWAP in the direction of the candidate; sufficient higher-timeframe agreements; sufficient volume ratio; a quality directional candle OR an ignition, rail-flip, structure-break, fair-value-gap or retest trigger; forecast confidence at or above its threshold AND rounded forecast price on or beyond close in the candidate direction, OR a directional sweep trigger; and volatility unit at least a fraction of average candle range OR normalized body at least a fraction of the displacement threshold. VWAP falls back to the bias EMA when unavailable. If both candidates occur, select only the one with the strictly greater directional score; if tied, select neither. Update the last major signal's bar, close and direction on every selected major signal, whether or not it creates a trade entry.
Calculation: Trade levels = On entry, round close to obtain entry price. Initial long stop is the rounded minimum of rail and entry minus a volatility-unit multiple; initial short stop is the rounded maximum of rail and entry plus that multiple. Initial risk is the greater of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and that volatility multiple. The two fixed targets are entry plus risk times their respective reward multiples for a long, or minus those amounts for a short, rounded to the price increment; the second target uses the larger reward multiple. On each subsequent bar, first record whether high has reached the first long target or low has reached the first short target. If trailing is enabled, raise the long stop to the greater of its prior value and rounded min(rail, close minus a volatility multiple), or lower the short stop to the lesser of its prior value and rounded max(rail, close plus that multiple). If breakeven is enabled and the first target has been touched, raise the long stop to at least entry or lower the short stop to at most entry. Evaluate exits after these updates and before entries; an opposite major signal can therefore exit and reverse on the same bar.

Short: bearish directional bias
Long: rounded forecast price above close
Short: rounded forecast price below close
Short: bullish directional bias AND extension above its exhaustion threshold AND RSI of source above its upper threshold AND bullish close position below its exhaustion threshold
Long: bearish directional bias AND extension above its exhaustion threshold AND RSI of source below its lower threshold AND bearish close position below its exhaustion threshold
