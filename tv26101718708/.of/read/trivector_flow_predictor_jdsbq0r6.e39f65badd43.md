Calculation: Adaptive core = EMA(0.30×ZLEMA + 0.22×DEMA + 0.20×HMA + 0.18×KAMA + 0.10×two-pole smoother of HLC3, 2 bars); slow core = EMA(adaptive core, a longer lookback than the core).
Calculation: Volatility unit = the greatest of ATR, 0.70×smoothed Parkinson volatility, and 0.70×smoothed Garman–Klass volatility, scaled by the selected market profile.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar source changes over that lookback, capped at 1.
Calculation: Phase = 3-bar EMA of (close − adaptive core) / volatility unit; phase velocity = current phase − prior phase.
Calculation: Long trigger = any of: (phase was below a negative pullback threshold, then crosses above zero, with close above the adaptive core and rising phase); (close breaks above the prior lookback range high plus a volatility buffer after compression, with bullish displacement and rising phase); (low sweeps below the prior lookback low plus a volatility buffer, then close reclaims that low with a bullish quality candle and rising phase); (pulse rail flips bullish with close above the adaptive core, rising phase, and bullish close location); (close breaks above the last confirmed swing high plus a volatility buffer, with close above the adaptive core and rising phase); (bullish fair-value-gap displacement with close above the adaptive core and rising phase); or (within the retest window, price retests a broken swing high and closes above it on an up candle, with close above the adaptive core and non-falling phase). Apply the configured maximum extension for each trigger type.
Calculation: Short trigger = the directional inverse of the long trigger: phase pullback above a positive threshold then crosses below zero; compression breakout below the prior range low; sweep above the prior range high then close below it; bearish pulse-rail flip; break below the last confirmed swing low; bearish fair-value-gap displacement; or retest of a broken swing low followed by a close below it. Apply the configured maximum extension for each trigger type.
Calculation: Directional scores = capped sums of trend, rail, directional movement, multi-timeframe agreement, trigger, phase, candle, displacement, volume-delta, ADX, efficiency, extension, and volatility-expansion components, less a chop penalty. Long and short scores use directionally corresponding components. Dynamic minimum score rises in chop and falls in impulse conditions.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = count of the two higher-timeframe closes above their respective EMAs for bullish agreement, or below for bearish agreement; use the latest available higher-timeframe bars (completed historically, possibly still forming in real time).
Calculation: Entry eligibility = confirmed bar if close confirmation is enabled; configured EMA, VWAP, volume, candle-quality, efficiency, prediction-alignment, and noise-floor filters pass; configured cooldown and minimum price-move since the last major signal pass; and same-direction entries are allowed or the last major signal was the opposite side.
Calculation: Initial long invalidation = the lower of the entry price and pulse rail, reduced by at least the configured volatility stop distance; initial short invalidation = the higher of entry and pulse rail, increased by at least that distance. While open, trail long invalidation upward using the greater of its prior value and the lower of pulse rail or close minus a volatility buffer; trail short invalidation downward using the lesser of its prior value and the higher of pulse rail or close plus a volatility buffer. After TP1 is touched, move invalidation at least to entry. TP2 is entry plus (long) or minus (short) initial risk multiplied by the configured TP2 reward/risk ratio.
Long: flat or short position AND long trigger AND long score ≥ dynamic minimum score AND long score ≥ short score plus the configured dominance margin AND entry eligibility AND (no simultaneous short signal or long score > short score).
Short: flat or long position AND short trigger AND short score ≥ dynamic minimum score AND short score ≥ long score plus the configured dominance margin AND entry eligibility AND (no simultaneous long signal or short score > long score).
Long: short bias AND extension exceeds its exhaustion threshold AND RSI is overbought AND bullish close location is weak.
Short: long bias AND extension exceeds its exhaustion threshold AND RSI is oversold AND bearish close location is weak.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND (short signal OR close ≤ active long invalidation OR long score below the configured confidence threshold while short score exceeds long score OR close ≥ TP2 when TP2 exits are enabled).
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND (long signal OR close ≥ active short invalidation OR short score below the configured confidence threshold while long score exceeds short score OR close ≤ TP2 when TP2 exits are enabled).
