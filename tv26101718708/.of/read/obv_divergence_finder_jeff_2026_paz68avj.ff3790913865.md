Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of volume on bars where close rises from the prior bar, minus volume where close falls, with zero added when close is unchanged
Calculation: Close pivot low = a close lower than the closes of the specified number of bars immediately before and after it, confirmed when those following bars are complete
Calculation: Close pivot high = a close higher than the closes of the specified number of bars immediately before and after it, confirmed when those following bars are complete
Long: Current confirmed close pivot low < previous confirmed close pivot low AND OBV at current pivot > OBV at previous pivot
Short: Current confirmed close pivot high > previous confirmed close pivot high AND OBV at current pivot < OBV at previous pivot
