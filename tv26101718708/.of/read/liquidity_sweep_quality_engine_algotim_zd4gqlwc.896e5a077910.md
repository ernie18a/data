Calculation: Active levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side; register the high before the low when both confirm on the same bar, retain at most 12 levels in insertion order by evicting the oldest on insertion, and expire levels when their age from the pivot bar exceeds the level-memory parameter. Remove every level swept on the current bar, even if its score does not qualify.
Calculation: Sweep score = sum of four binary points: penetration beyond the level >= a penetration multiplier times ATR; distance from the sweep extreme to close >= a rejection multiplier times ATR; volume confirmation is enabled AND volume >= a volume multiplier times the simple moving average of volume; and close >= the previous candle body's midpoint for a low sweep OR close <= that midpoint for a high sweep.
Calculation: Selected sweep = the qualifying swept level with the highest sweep score; a score qualifies when it >= the minimum score OR weak sweeps are enabled AND it >= 1. Break score ties by selecting the most recently registered level.
Long: Selected sweep is a high level AND high > level AND close < level.
Short: Selected sweep is a low level AND low < level AND close > level.
