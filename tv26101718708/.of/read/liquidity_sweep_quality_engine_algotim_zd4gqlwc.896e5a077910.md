Calculation: Active confirmed swing highs/lows = pivots formed on the chart timeframe using equal left and right lookbacks; register each only after right-side confirmation, retain it until swept or older than the level-memory limit, and when the registry is full discard the oldest level before registering another. A high pivot is a bar whose high is the highest in that window; a low pivot is a bar whose low is the lowest in that window.
Calculation: Sweep quality score (SQS) = sum of four 1-point checks: for a high sweep, high minus level ≥ minimum ATR-multiple penetration, high minus close ≥ minimum ATR-multiple rejection, volume ≥ volume-multiple × its moving average when volume confirmation is enabled, and close ≤ midpoint of the prior bar’s body; for a low sweep, level minus low ≥ minimum ATR-multiple penetration, close minus low ≥ minimum ATR-multiple rejection, the same volume check, and close ≥ midpoint of the prior bar’s body. ATR and volume moving average use chart-timeframe bars.
Long: high > active confirmed swing high AND close < that swing-high level AND (SQS ≥ minimum quality score OR (SQS < minimum quality score AND weak-sweep display enabled AND SQS ≥ 1))
Short: low < active confirmed swing low AND close > that swing-low level AND (SQS ≥ minimum quality score OR (SQS < minimum quality score AND weak-sweep display enabled AND SQS ≥ 1))
