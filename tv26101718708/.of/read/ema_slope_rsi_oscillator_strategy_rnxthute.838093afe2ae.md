Calculation: Moving averages = independently selectable SMA, EMA, DEMA, TEMA, WMA, VWMA, smoothed WMA, SWMA, or HMA of a selectable OHLC price source; the short moving average has a shorter length than the long moving average. Smoothed WMA = (previous-bar WMA × (length − 1) + current source) / length, initialized with SMA when the previous WMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Normalized slope = (100 / π) × arctan((long moving average − long moving average a configurable number of bars ago) / max(standard deviation of that difference over a configurable lookback, a small positive floor)).
Calculation: Acceleration = min(50 × raw acceleration / highest raw acceleration over a configurable lookback, 50), or 0 if that highest value is 0; raw acceleration = absolute one-bar change in normalized slope × slope-difference bar count × 2.
Calculation: Centered RSI = SMA of RSI of a selectable OHLC price source minus its midpoint.
Calculation: Stretch = 100 × (long moving average − short moving average) / short moving average.
Calculation: Zone boundary = a positive configurable threshold when the zone is enabled, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed smoothed-RSI pivot low has a simultaneous price-low pivot lower than the price-low pivot stored with any of the preceding configurable number of smoothed-RSI pivot lows AND its RSI value is higher than that prior RSI pivot. Bearish RSI divergence uses simultaneous price-high and RSI pivot highs, with a higher price high and lower RSI high. Use equal configurable left and right pivot widths; store a missing price pivot with its RSI pivot and treat comparisons with it as false. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion = acceleration ≥ entry acceleration threshold AND normalized slope < negative zone boundary AND normalized slope < previous normalized slope AND centered RSI > centered RSI a configurable lookback ago AND centered RSI > centered RSI twice that lookback ago AND absolute difference between normalized slope and centered RSI ≥ configurable gap. Bearish exhaustion reverses all directional comparisons.
Calculation: Oscillator confirmation = after centered RSI crosses normalized slope in a direction while the zone is disabled or absolute normalized slope > zone boundary, retain that direction through a configurable maximum number of bars including the cross bar. A valid opposite cross replaces the pending direction; a cross outside the zone blocked by the optional past-zone filter cancels the pending wait in its own direction. The past-zone filter blocks an upward cross when both series > positive zone boundary and a downward cross when both series < negative zone boundary. Confirmation occurs when both series move in the cross direction versus their previous values. Clear both waits after an oscillator entry.
Calculation: Cross-exit confirmation = after centered RSI crosses normalized slope, retain the cross direction through a configurable maximum number of bars including the cross bar; an opposite cross replaces it. Confirmation requires both series to move in the cross direction versus their previous values AND the current candle to have that direction. Clear both waits after a cross exit.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on the first bar the position is observed, initialize at that bar's close minus entry ATR × entry multiplier for a long, or plus that amount for a short. After the applicable entry-type grace period, update the long stop to max(previous stop, high − trailing ATR × applicable multiplier), or the short stop to min(previous stop, low + trailing ATR × applicable multiplier). Use separate multipliers and grace periods for standard, divergence, and oscillator entries.
Long: Normalized slope > 0 when the zone display is disabled, or normalized slope > positive zone boundary when it is enabled.
Short: Normalized slope < 0 when the zone display is disabled, or normalized slope < negative zone boundary when it is enabled.
Long: Acceleration > 0 AND close > open.
Short: Acceleration > 0 AND close < open.
Long: Centered RSI < negative configurable oversold threshold.
Short: Centered RSI > positive configurable overbought threshold.
Long: Bullish RSI divergence.
Short: Bearish RSI divergence.
Long: Bullish exhaustion.
Short: Bearish exhaustion.

Short: Position side is flat or long AND (zone disabled OR absolute normalized slope > zone boundary) AND (candle filter disabled OR current close < current open AND previous close < previous open) AND ((zone-cross entries enabled AND centered RSI does not cross normalized slope in either direction AND (normalized slope crosses below negative zone boundary OR normalized slope < negative zone boundary AND previous normalized slope − normalized slope ≥ minimum delta) AND stretch ≤ maximum stretch AND (trend filter disabled OR short moving average < long moving average)) OR (acceleration entries enabled AND acceleration ≥ entry acceleration threshold AND previous normalized slope − normalized slope ≥ minimum delta AND normalized slope < previous normalized slope AND normalized slope < negative zone boundary AND stretch ≤ maximum stretch AND (trend filter disabled OR short moving average < long moving average)) OR (RSI-divergence entries enabled AND bearish RSI divergence AND (exhaustion confirmation disabled OR bearish exhaustion)) OR (exhaustion entries enabled AND bearish exhaustion) OR (oscillator entries enabled AND downward oscillator confirmation)).

Short: Position side is long AND cross exits and cross reversals are enabled AND downward cross-exit confirmation AND (zone disabled OR absolute normalized slope > zone boundary) AND (candle filter disabled OR current close < current open AND previous close < previous open).

Exit: short, cross exits enabled AND upward cross-exit confirmation.

Exit: short, oscillator entry active AND bars since entry > 0 AND upward oscillator confirmation.

Exit: short, standard entry active AND zone re-entry exit enabled AND normalized slope crossed from < negative zone boundary to ≥ negative zone boundary after entry AND normalized slope has remained ≥ negative zone boundary for more than the configurable confirmation count.
Exit: long, applicable trailing stop is active AND bars since entry > applicable grace period AND close < trailing stop.
Exit: short, applicable trailing stop is active AND bars since entry > applicable grace period AND close > trailing stop.
Calculation: A divergence entry is active when either divergence entry condition was true at entry and RSI-divergence entries are enabled; an oscillator entry is active when its oscillator condition was true at entry and oscillator entries are enabled. Both classifications can be active simultaneously. Otherwise the entry is standard. Divergence and oscillator trailing stops are active whenever their respective entry classifications are active; the standard trailing stop additionally requires its own enable setting. Opposite-side entry conditions reverse the position and suppress the old side's exits on that bar.
