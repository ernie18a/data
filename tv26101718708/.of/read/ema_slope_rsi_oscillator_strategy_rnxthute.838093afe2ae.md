Calculation: Normalized slope = (100 / π) × atan((long moving average − its value several bars earlier) / max(standard deviation of that difference over a longer lookback, a small positive floor)); the short moving average uses a shorter lookback than the long moving average.
Calculation: Centered RSI = smoothed RSI − 50.
Calculation: Slope delta = normalized slope − its previous value.
Calculation: Normalized acceleration = absolute one-bar change in normalized slope, multiplied by the smoothing factor, scaled by 2, and normalized so the highest value over a rolling lookback maps to 50 (capped at 50).
Calculation: MA percentage difference = (long moving average − short moving average) / short moving average × 100.
Calculation: Bullish RSI pivot divergence = at a confirmed pivot low, the bar's low is below any of the stored prior pivot lows while its smoothed RSI pivot low is higher than that prior pivot's; pivot detection uses equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bearish RSI pivot divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, the bar's high is above any of the stored prior pivot highs while its smoothed RSI pivot high is lower than that prior pivot's; pivot detection uses equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish slope-RSI exhaustion = normalized acceleration meets its threshold AND slope delta is negative AND normalized slope is below the negative NTZ boundary AND centered RSI is above its values one and two slope-RSI lookbacks earlier AND absolute distance between centered RSI and normalized slope meets its gap threshold.
Calculation: Bearish slope-RSI exhaustion = normalized acceleration meets its threshold AND slope delta is positive AND normalized slope is above the NTZ boundary AND centered RSI is below its values one and two slope-RSI lookbacks earlier AND absolute distance between centered RSI and normalized slope meets its gap threshold.
Long: bullish RSI pivot divergence OR bullish slope-RSI exhaustion OR (position is flat or short AND (NTZ filtering is disabled OR absolute normalized slope is outside the NTZ) AND (last-bar filtering is disabled OR current and previous bars close above their opens) AND ((NTZ-cross entry is enabled AND centered RSI does not cross normalized slope on this bar AND (normalized slope crosses above the NTZ boundary OR normalized slope is above it with positive slope delta meeting the minimum magnitude) AND (long MA is not above short MA by more than the stretch limit) AND (trend confirmation is disabled OR short MA > long MA)) OR (acceleration entry is enabled AND normalized acceleration meets its entry threshold AND positive slope delta meets its minimum magnitude AND normalized slope is above the NTZ boundary AND (long MA is not above short MA by more than the stretch limit) AND (trend confirmation is disabled OR short MA > long MA)) OR (RSI divergence entry is enabled AND bullish RSI pivot divergence AND (slope-RSI confirmation is disabled OR bullish slope-RSI exhaustion)) OR (slope-RSI divergence entry is enabled AND bullish slope-RSI exhaustion) OR (oscillator entry is enabled AND a centered-RSI cross above normalized slope was armed outside the NTZ, was not blocked because both values were already above the NTZ boundary, has not expired, and both centered RSI and normalized slope are rising)).
Short: bearish RSI pivot divergence OR bearish slope-RSI exhaustion OR (position is flat or long AND (NTZ filtering is disabled OR absolute normalized slope is outside the NTZ) AND (last-bar filtering is disabled OR current and previous bars close below their opens) AND ((NTZ-cross entry is enabled AND centered RSI does not cross normalized slope on this bar AND (normalized slope crosses below the negative NTZ boundary OR normalized slope is below it with negative slope delta meeting the minimum magnitude) AND (short MA is not below long MA by more than the stretch limit) AND (trend confirmation is disabled OR short MA < long MA)) OR (acceleration entry is enabled AND normalized acceleration meets its entry threshold AND negative slope delta meets its minimum magnitude AND normalized slope is below the negative NTZ boundary AND (short MA is not below long MA by more than the stretch limit) AND (trend confirmation is disabled OR short MA < long MA)) OR (RSI divergence entry is enabled AND bearish RSI pivot divergence AND (slope-RSI confirmation is disabled OR bearish slope-RSI exhaustion)) OR (slope-RSI divergence entry is enabled AND bearish slope-RSI exhaustion) OR (oscillator entry is enabled AND a centered-RSI cross below normalized slope was armed outside the NTZ, was not blocked because both values were already below the negative NTZ boundary, has not expired, and both centered RSI and normalized slope are falling)).
Exit: long, (RSI-slope cross exit is enabled AND centered RSI crosses below normalized slope or did so within the crossing timeout AND both are falling AND close < open) OR (standard position AND NTZ re-entry exit is enabled AND normalized slope has crossed from above the NTZ boundary to at or below it and stayed at or below it for more than the confirmation-bar count) OR (standard trailing stop is enabled AND grace period has passed AND close < long trailing stop) OR (oscillator-entry position AND opposite oscillator short condition OR close < oscillator long trailing stop after its grace period) OR (divergence-entry position AND close < divergence long trailing stop after its grace period).
Exit: short, (RSI-slope cross exit is enabled AND centered RSI crosses above normalized slope or did so within the crossing timeout AND both are rising AND close > open) OR (standard position AND NTZ re-entry exit is enabled AND normalized slope has crossed from below the negative NTZ boundary to at or above it and stayed at or above it for more than the confirmation-bar count) OR (standard trailing stop is enabled AND grace period has passed AND close > short trailing stop) OR (oscillator-entry position AND opposite oscillator long condition OR close > oscillator short trailing stop after its grace period) OR (divergence-entry position AND close > divergence short trailing stop after its grace period).
