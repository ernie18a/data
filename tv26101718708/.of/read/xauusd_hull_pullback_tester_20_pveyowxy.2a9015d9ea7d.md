Calculation: Hull MA(n) = WMA(2*WMA(close, round(n/2)) - WMA(close, n), round(sqrt(n))); fast Hull and reference Hull use shorter lengths than slow Hull (reference Hull length is similar to or equal to fast Hull length)
Calculation: ATR = Wilder ATR on chart-timeframe bars; ATR at the signal bar is the value used for the stop and target
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line - signal line (standard MACD)
Calculation: LongArmed = state evaluated bar by bar on chart-timeframe completed bars, in this order: (1) if fast Hull <= slow Hull, clear pushed, pulled and count; (2) if fast Hull > slow Hull AND high - reference Hull >= push_mult*ATR, pushed = true; (3) if fast Hull > slow Hull AND pushed AND low - reference Hull <= tol_mult*ATR, pulled = true; (4) count = pulled ? count+1 : 0, and if count > expiry_bars, clear pushed, pulled and count; LongArmed = fast Hull > slow Hull AND pushed AND pulled. Clear pushed, pulled and count whenever the Long condition fires.
Calculation: ShortArmed = mirror of LongArmed, evaluated in the same order: (1) if fast Hull >= slow Hull, clear pushed, pulled and count; (2) if fast Hull < slow Hull AND reference Hull - low >= push_mult*ATR, pushed = true; (3) if fast Hull < slow Hull AND pushed AND reference Hull - high <= tol_mult*ATR, pulled = true; (4) same count and expiry rule; ShortArmed = fast Hull < slow Hull AND pushed AND pulled. Clear pushed, pulled and count whenever the Short condition fires.
Long: LongArmed AND (close > previous bar high OR close crosses above reference Hull (previous close <= previous reference Hull AND close > reference Hull)) AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram AND bar time is inside the allowed entry-time window AND no open position (or a short position, if flipping on the opposite signal is enabled); entry price = close of the signal bar
Short: ShortArmed AND (close < previous bar low OR close crosses below reference Hull (previous close >= previous reference Hull AND close < reference Hull)) AND MACD histogram < previous MACD histogram AND bar time is inside the allowed entry-time window AND no open position (or a long position, if flipping on the opposite signal is enabled); entry price = close of the signal bar
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop_mult*ATR(signal bar) OR high >= entry price + reward_risk*stop_mult*ATR(signal bar) OR (flip enabled AND Short condition is true)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop_mult*ATR(signal bar) OR low <= entry price - reward_risk*stop_mult*ATR(signal bar) OR (flip enabled AND Long condition is true)
