Calculation: Session = bars from the configured session start onward; optionally reset at each calendar-day boundary. The first session bar is the first bar whose opening time is at or after that day's configured start and whose preceding bar was before its own day's start. If daily reset is disabled, session state carries across days until the next first session bar.
Calculation: Delta = sum signed volume of all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, assigning positive volume when a lower-timeframe close is above its open, negative when below, and otherwise using its close versus the preceding lower-timeframe close; if unchanged, retain the preceding lower-timeframe polarity. If the result is unavailable or zero and fallback is enabled, use chart-bar volume with the sign of close minus open, or, for a doji, the sign of close minus the preceding close; use zero if both are unchanged.
Calculation: Candle quality = for positive delta, close position within the high-low range plus body/range plus half the lower wick/range; for negative delta, one minus close position plus body/range plus half the upper wick/range; for zero delta, one. Use a positive minimum price increment as the minimum range.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its chart-bar simple moving average, or one when that average is not positive. Bar pressure = absolute delta multiplied by relative volume and candle quality when their respective weighting options are enabled; floor each enabled weight at a positive minimum.
Calculation: Dominance = the difference between accumulated positive-delta and negative-delta bar pressure, divided by their sum and expressed as a percentage; use zero when the sum is zero. Reset both accumulators on the first session bar and optionally on each new calendar day; on each in-session bar decay each accumulator by the configured fraction, then add the corresponding bar pressure.
Calculation: Opening threshold = the greater of absolute delta on the first session bar and a positive floor. Reset it at each calendar-day boundary when daily reset is enabled. Opening side = the sign of that first bar's delta.
Calculation: Superior delta = in-session bar after the first session bar with absolute delta at least opening threshold times a configured factor AND candle quality at least its configured threshold AND relative volume at least its configured threshold.
Calculation: Daily zones = at the first chart bar of each new calendar day, process the completed preceding day's chart bars and their completed lower-timeframe bars; if a chart bar has no lower-timeframe bars, use that chart bar as one sample. Use the day's chart-bar high and low as the range and the sum of sample volumes as total volume. Divide the range into equal bins. Starting at the high and moving downward, sum the volume of samples whose highs lie strictly inside each bin; the first bin where cumulative volume divided by total volume exceeds the configured threshold defines supply from the day's high to that bin's lower edge. Starting at the low and moving upward, apply the same procedure to sample lows to define demand from the day's low to that bin's upper edge. A zone requires positive total volume, a nonzero daily range, and more than one chart bar in the processed day.
Calculation: Low-volume node = divide the completed day's range into profile rows; distribute each sample's volume across rows in proportion to price-range overlap, or assign all of a zero-range sample's volume to the row containing its price. Optionally multiply each row contribution by its midpoint for a money-flow profile. A zone has a low-volume node when a row midpoint lies inside it and that row's profile value divided by the maximum row value is at or below the configured threshold. Optionally create only zones with a low-volume node and only for enabled sides.
Calculation: Zone state = create each zone on the first chart bar of the new day but allow rejection signals only after that bar. After evaluating each bar's signals, optionally mark a supply zone tested when close exceeds its top or a demand zone tested when close falls below its bottom; retain that state. After adding zones, while the total exceeds the configured limit and a tested zone exists, remove the oldest tested zone. Zone visibility and nearest-zone selection do not restrict rejection signals.
Long: First session bar AND delta > 0.
Short: First session bar AND delta < 0.
Long: In session AND dominance > positive dominance threshold.
Short: In session AND dominance < negative dominance threshold.
Long: In session AND previous bar dominance < 0 AND current dominance >= positive flip threshold.
Short: In session AND previous bar dominance > 0 AND current dominance <= negative flip threshold.
Long: In session AND delta > 0 AND absolute delta >= opening threshold times the challenge factor.
Short: In session AND delta < 0 AND absolute delta >= opening threshold times the challenge factor.
Long: Superior delta conditions hold AND delta > 0.
Short: Superior delta conditions hold AND delta < 0.
Long: In session AND an enabled demand zone exists whose creation bar precedes the current bar AND, if untested-only is enabled, the zone is untested AND high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top AND either close > zone top when close-outside is required OR close > open AND lower wick > 0 when it is not required AND at least one enabled confirmation holds: delta > 0 OR dominance > positive dominance threshold OR previous dominance < 0 AND current dominance >= positive flip threshold OR delta > 0 AND absolute delta >= opening threshold times the challenge factor OR superior delta conditions hold AND delta > 0 AND, if opposite-control blocking is enabled, dominance >= negative dominance threshold AND the number of bars since the previous hybrid long signal is greater than the cooldown, or no previous hybrid long signal exists.
