Calculation: Flux Wave primary and signal = blend of three momentum components: (1) source minus its channel EMA, normalized by the channel EMA of absolute deviation; (2) source minus channel VWMA, normalized by its channel EMA of absolute deviation; and (3) candle body/range × volume × capped square root of relative volume, normalized by its channel EMA of absolute value and scaled by 0.75. Adapt each normalization denominator by 0.015 × (1 + 0.15 × ATR(14)/source); EMA-smooth each component over the average length; weight and normalize their sum; add 0.22 × EMA of the first component's one-bar change; clamp to ±160 for the primary, then signal is its simple moving average over the signal length. Source is Heikin-Ashi typical price.
Calculation: Flux momentum, VWAP momentum, and money-flow momentum = the three EMA-smoothed normalized components used in the Flux Wave blend, respectively.
Calculation: Money flow = EMA of the simple moving average of money-flow momentum, scaled by the money-flow scale divided by 8.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at confirmed price/wave pivot lows, price makes a lower low and the Flux Wave signal makes a higher low; regular bearish divergence = at confirmed pivot highs, price makes a higher high and the Flux Wave signal makes a lower high. Apply the configured pivot spacing/lookback and minimum price-move and wave-separation filters. Hidden bullish divergence = higher price low with lower wave low; hidden bearish divergence = lower price high with higher wave high, subject to the configured spacing/lookback and filters.
Calculation: Bullish engine alignment = Flux momentum, VWAP momentum, and money-flow momentum are all above zero; bearish engine alignment = all three are below zero.
Calculation: Flux Wave bullish cross = primary crosses above signal; bearish cross = primary crosses below signal.
Calculation: Bullish Flux sync = (oversold/recent-oversold/extreme-oversold wave, bullish wave reversal or cross or bullish momentum/divergence, strong bullish money-flow shift, and at least two bullish engines, together with bullish chart context) OR bullish triple reversal. Bearish Flux sync is the mirrored condition using overbought wave, bearish reversal, strong bearish money-flow shift, bearish engines, and bearish chart context or bearish triple reversal. Bullish chart context is bullish structure break, major Fibonacci support touch, bullish liquidity sweep, bullish triple reversal, or price above nearest anchored VWAP with bullish Heikin-Ashi gate; bearish chart context is the mirrored condition.
Calculation: Bullish triple reversal = major bullish Fibonacci rejection AND bullish wave roll AND bullish money-flow shift AND (elite or active bullish divergence OR oversold wave). Bearish triple reversal = major bearish Fibonacci rejection AND bearish wave roll AND bearish money-flow shift AND (elite or regular/hidden bearish divergence OR overbought wave).
Calculation: Bullish divergence diamond = regular bullish divergence (or enabled hidden bullish divergence) AND divergence confirmation AND bullish conviction score at least the minimum score, with optional configured cross and money-flow hard gates; bearish diamond is the mirrored condition. Conviction score sums the configured points for divergence type/strength, dual-line agreement, wave cross/orientation, money-flow agreement, spread reversal, fair-value agreement, primary-line fractal, extreme-zone status, wave roll, and major-leg status. Apply configured regime, cooldown, and one-bar duplicate-signal filters.
Calculation: Bottom arm = bullish diamond OR extreme-oversold exhaustion at major Fibonacci support/liquidity sweep with money flow positive OR enabled regular bullish divergence; also require the recent low is the lookback swing low and is not locked. Top arm is the mirrored condition. Bottom fires while armed when Flux Wave is bullish, at least two engines are bullish, and close exceeds the prior structure-break lookback's highest high; top fires while armed when Flux Wave is bearish, at least two engines are bearish, and close is below the prior structure-break lookback's lowest low. Invalidate an arm if price exceeds its armed extreme by 0.10 ATR or the confirmation window expires; release its swing lock after the configured ATR move.
