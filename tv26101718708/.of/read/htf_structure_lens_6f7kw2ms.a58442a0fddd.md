Long: On a higher-timeframe candle when it completes, its low < the preceding completed candle’s low AND its close > that low AND it did not also exceed the preceding candle’s high (bullish C2)
Long: On a higher-timeframe candle when it completes, the preceding candle closed below its open AND the current low >= the preceding low AND the current open <= the lower of the preceding open and close AND the current close > the higher of the preceding open and close AND the preceding candle was not a bullish C2 against the candle before it AND the current candle is not a C2 (bullish C3)
Calculation: CISD active level and bias = On the selected CISD timeframe (which may be the chart timeframe or another timeframe), classify each candle as bullish if close > open, bearish if close < open, and otherwise neutral; skip neutral candles when finding runs. Initialize bias from the current non-neutral candle, or the most recent non-neutral candle within the prior 50 bars if current is neutral; set the active level to the lowest open in the uninterrupted run of that bias, and track the current high for bullish bias or low for bearish bias. While bias is bullish, whenever a bar makes a higher tracked high, update the active level to the lowest open in the uninterrupted bullish run ending at that bar if it is bullish, or in the preceding uninterrupted bullish run if it is bearish. While bias is bearish, whenever a bar makes a lower tracked low, update the active level to the highest open in the uninterrupted bearish run ending at that bar if it is bearish, or in the preceding uninterrupted bearish run if it is bullish. After a confirmation, set bias to the new direction, active level to the lowest open in its current bullish run or highest open in its current bearish run, and tracked extreme to that bar’s high or low, respectively.
Long: On a selected CISD timeframe bar when it completes, close > CISD active level AND CISD bias is bearish
Short: On a higher-timeframe candle when it completes, its high > the preceding completed candle’s high AND its close < that high AND it did not also fall below the preceding candle’s low (bearish C2)
Short: On a higher-timeframe candle when it completes, the preceding candle closed above its open AND the current high <= the preceding high AND the current open >= the higher of the preceding open and close AND the current close < the lower of the preceding open and close AND the preceding candle was not a bearish C2 against the candle before it AND the current candle is not a C2 (bearish C3)
Short: On a selected CISD timeframe bar when it completes, close < CISD active level AND CISD bias is bullish
