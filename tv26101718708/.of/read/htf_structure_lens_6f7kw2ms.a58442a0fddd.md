Calculation: Delivery state and level = On the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, process completed bars in order. Initialize after a warmup from the current bar's direction, or the most recent non-doji direction within an initialization lookback; set the level to the extreme open of the current same-direction run if the current bar has that direction, otherwise to the current open, and set the tracked extreme to the current high for bullish state or low for bearish state. A run skips dojis and ends at the first opposite-direction non-doji. In bullish state, whenever high exceeds the tracked high, update the tracked high and set the level to the lowest open in the current bullish run if the bar is bullish, otherwise in the most recent preceding bullish run; retain the old level if no such preceding run exists. In bearish state, mirror this using a new tracked low and the highest open in the bearish run. Limit each run search to a lookback. Apply these updates before testing the close. After a close above the level in bearish state or below the level in bullish state, switch state, reset the level to the extreme open of the current run in the new direction if the current bar has that direction, otherwise to the current open, and reset the tracked extreme to the current high for bullish state or low for bearish state. For a higher or lower timeframe, use the last completed bar; sample the last completed lower-timeframe result on each chart bar.
Long: The still-forming higher-timeframe bar's low < the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar's low.
Short: The still-forming higher-timeframe bar's high > the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar's high.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe bar's low < the preceding bar's low AND its close > the preceding bar's low AND its high <= the preceding bar's high.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe bar's high > the preceding bar's high AND its close < the preceding bar's high AND its low >= the preceding bar's low.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe bar's preceding bar closes < its open AND the last completed bar's low >= that preceding bar's low AND its open <= that preceding bar's close AND its close > that preceding bar's open AND NOT (the preceding bar's low < the bar before it, AND the preceding bar's close > the bar before that's low) AND NOT (the last completed bar's high > the preceding bar's high AND its close < the preceding bar's high).
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe bar's preceding bar closes > its open AND the last completed bar's high <= that preceding bar's high AND its open >= that preceding bar's close AND its close < that preceding bar's open AND NOT (the preceding bar's high > the bar before it, AND the preceding bar's close < the bar before that's high) AND NOT (the last completed bar's low < the preceding bar's low AND its close > the preceding bar's low).
Long: Delivery state before the close test = bearish AND the completed delivery-timeframe bar's close > its updated delivery level.
Short: Delivery state before the close test = bullish AND the completed delivery-timeframe bar's close < its updated delivery level.
