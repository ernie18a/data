Calculation: Adaptive RSI = highest/lowest close range over its lookback; use the range as the change when the highest close rises (positive) or the lowest close falls (negative), otherwise use close change; apply RMA to these changes and their absolute values, then compute 50 + 50 × (RMA(changes) / RMA(absolute changes)); use 50 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: ARSI volatility trend = ATR-style RMA of absolute Adaptive RSI changes; form bands at Adaptive RSI ± volatility multiplier × this value, trail the lower band in an uptrend and the upper band in a downtrend, and switch trend when Adaptive RSI crosses the prior trend band. The comparison series is the average of this trend band and the selected smoothed Adaptive RSI.
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot high/low = high/low from the bar `swing lookback` bars ago, confirmed on the current completed chart bar when it is at least as high/low as the preceding older lookback bars and strictly higher/lower than the intervening more recent bars.
Long: On a completed chart bar, each of these three bullish events occurred within the confluence window: (1) fast RSI average crossed above slow RSI average on the prior completed chart bar (fast lookback shorter than slow); if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, prior completed higher-timeframe RMA-of-RSI fast average > slow average; if the ADX filter is enabled, prior completed chart-bar ADX >= its minimum; (2) Adaptive RSI crossed above the comparison series on the prior completed chart bar; if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, prior completed higher-timeframe Adaptive RSI >= 50; (3) current close > latest confirmed swing pivot high, and if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, prior completed higher-timeframe RSI > 50. The three events were not all within the window on the preceding chart bar.
Short: On a completed chart bar, each of these three bearish events occurred within the confluence window: (1) fast RSI average crossed below slow RSI average on the prior completed chart bar (fast lookback shorter than slow); if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, prior completed higher-timeframe RMA-of-RSI fast average < slow average; if the ADX filter is enabled, prior completed chart-bar ADX >= its minimum; (2) Adaptive RSI crossed below the comparison series on the prior completed chart bar; if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, prior completed higher-timeframe Adaptive RSI < 50; (3) current close < latest confirmed swing pivot low, and if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, prior completed higher-timeframe RSI < 50. The three events were not all within the window on the preceding chart bar.
Exit: long, Short
Exit: short, Long
