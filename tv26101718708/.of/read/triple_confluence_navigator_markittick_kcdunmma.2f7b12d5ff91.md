Calculation: RSI averages = Apply the selected method to RSI of close: RMA with shorter and longer lengths; linear regression endpoints with shorter and longer lengths; or two independent Kalman filters. For each Kalman filter initialize estimate to RSI and covariance to 1; on each bar set gain = covariance / (covariance + measurement noise), estimate = estimate + gain × (RSI − estimate), and covariance = (1 − gain) × covariance + process noise. The slower filter has measurement noise multiplied by the ratio of longer to shorter length.
Calculation: Adaptive RSI = Let upper and lower be the rolling highest and lowest closes and range = upper − lower. Use difference = range if upper increased, otherwise −range if lower decreased, otherwise close − previous close. Adaptive RSI = 50 + 50 × RMA(difference) / RMA(abs(difference)), or 50 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive RSI signal = Apply the selected filter to adaptive RSI: RMA; a Kalman filter initialized with estimate = source and covariance = 1, updating covariance by adding process noise, then gain = covariance / (covariance + measurement noise), estimate = estimate + gain × (source − estimate), covariance = (1 − gain) × covariance; or an adaptive average initialized to source, with efficiency = min(abs(source − source[length]) / (rolling highest source − rolling lowest source), 1), or zero when the range is zero, alpha = minimum alpha + efficiency × (maximum alpha − minimum alpha), and average = previous average + alpha × (source − previous average).
Calculation: Adaptive RSI merged band = Average the adaptive RSI signal and its oscillator supertrend; if the supertrend is unavailable, use the signal alone. Oscillator volatility is RMA(abs(adaptive RSI − previous adaptive RSI)); upper and lower bands are adaptive RSI plus and minus a volatility multiplier. Initialize supertrend to the upper band and trend upward. Thereafter set trend upward if adaptive RSI is above the previous supertrend, downward if below, and otherwise retain the previous trend; set supertrend to max(lower band, previous supertrend) in an upward trend and min(upper band, previous supertrend) in a downward trend.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = The high or low of a bar with an equal-length window on each side: a high is strictly above all later highs and at least as high as all earlier highs; a low is strictly below all later lows and at most as low as all earlier lows. Use the most recently confirmed swing separately for highs and lows. Each swing can trigger its corresponding break only once.
Calculation: Bullish momentum event = On the previous chart bar the shorter RSI average crossed above the longer RSI average AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, on its last completed bar the shorter RMA of RSI is above the longer RMA of RSI AND, if the ADX filter is enabled, previous chart-bar ADX is at least its threshold.
Calculation: Bearish momentum event = On the previous chart bar the shorter RSI average crossed below the longer RSI average AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, on its last completed bar the shorter RMA of RSI is below the longer RMA of RSI AND, if the ADX filter is enabled, previous chart-bar ADX is at least its threshold.
Calculation: Bullish adaptive event = On the previous chart bar adaptive RSI crossed above its merged band AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, adaptive RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is at least 50.
Calculation: Bearish adaptive event = On the previous chart bar adaptive RSI crossed below its merged band AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, adaptive RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below 50.
Calculation: Bullish structure event = Confirmed chart-bar close is above the latest confirmed swing high AND that swing has not already triggered an upward break AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is above 50.
Calculation: Bearish structure event = Confirmed chart-bar close is below the latest confirmed swing low AND that swing has not already triggered a downward break AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below 50.
Calculation: Bullish confluence = The bars elapsed since each of the bullish momentum, adaptive, and structure events are all at most the confluence window.
Calculation: Bearish confluence = The bars elapsed since each of the bearish momentum, adaptive, and structure events are all at most the confluence window.
Long: Bullish momentum event OR bullish adaptive event OR bullish structure event OR the most recent structure event is bullish OR bullish confluence is true on the confirmed chart bar and was false on the preceding bar OR the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and the shorter RMA of RSI is above the longer RMA of RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Bearish momentum event OR bearish adaptive event OR bearish structure event OR the most recent structure event is bearish OR bearish confluence is true on the confirmed chart bar and was false on the preceding bar OR the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and the shorter RMA of RSI is at most the longer RMA of RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, bearish confluence is true on the confirmed chart bar AND was false on the preceding bar.
Exit: short, bullish confluence is true on the confirmed chart bar AND was false on the preceding bar.
