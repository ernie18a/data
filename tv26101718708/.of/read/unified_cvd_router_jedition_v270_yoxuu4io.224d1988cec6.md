Calculation: Lower-timeframe volume baseline = rolling median and median absolute deviation of valid, positive-volume lower-timeframe bars preceding the bar being evaluated. Reset the history at each reset-period boundary and optionally at the first regular-session bar; when reset at the regular-session open, exclude that bar from the history. After the warmup count is reached, update both statistics only at the configured refresh interval. Use the cached values between updates.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe buy fraction = (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low; otherwise 1 if close > the preceding lower-timeframe close, 0 if close < it, and 0.5 if equal or unavailable. Treat the preceding close as unavailable at a reset-period boundary.
Long: At least one completed lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar has valid OHLC, positive volume, a warmed-up volume baseline with positive median and median absolute deviation, volume >= baseline median + volume-outlier threshold × 1.4826 × baseline median absolute deviation, and buy fraction >= one-sided volume fraction threshold.
