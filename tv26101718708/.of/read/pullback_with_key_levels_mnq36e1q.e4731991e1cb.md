Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Calculation: Previous-bar high/low = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: +CISD level = open of the first candle in the latest consecutive bearish run
Calculation: -CISD level = open of the first candle in the latest consecutive bullish run
Long: Fast EMA > Slow EMA
Short: Fast EMA < Slow EMA
Long: Close crosses above Fast EMA
Short: Close crosses below Fast EMA
Long: Fast EMA crosses above Slow EMA
Short: Fast EMA crosses below Slow EMA
Long: High > Previous-bar high AND Close < Previous-bar high
Short: Low < Previous-bar low AND Close > Previous-bar low
Long: Close > +CISD level
Short: Close < -CISD level
Long: Low > High two bars ago
Short: High < Low two bars ago
Long: Close crosses above the lower boundary of an active bearish fair-value gap
Short: Close crosses below the upper boundary of an active bullish fair-value gap
