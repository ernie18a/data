Calculation: Confirmed pivots = chart-bar pivot highs and lows, each confirmed using the same number of bars on both sides; retain the latest pivot low and high and a fixed-size rolling list of the most recent pivots of each type.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap zones = on each newly available completed higher-timeframe bar, replace the bullish zone with [high two higher-timeframe bars earlier, current low] if the former is below the latter; replace the bearish zone with [current high, low two higher-timeframe bars earlier] if the latter is above the former. Track the latest zone of each direction independently for each of several higher timeframes. After updating, delete a bullish zone if chart close > its upper bound and delete a bearish zone if chart close < its lower bound.
Calculation: Delivery opens = on each chart bar with high > low and absolute body divided by range at least a threshold, replace the stored bullish delivery open if close > open, or the stored bearish delivery open if close < open. After updating, a bullish delivery break occurs if close > the stored bearish open, and a bearish delivery break occurs if close < the stored bullish open; clear the corresponding stored open after each break.
Calculation: Sweep invalidation = a bullish sweep occurs when low < the latest confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot; a bearish sweep occurs when high > the latest confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot. On each bullish or bearish sweep, respectively, store the swept pivot and reset that direction's invalidation flag. If the optional invalidation filter is enabled, set the long flag when close < the stored swept low and the short flag when close > the stored swept high; each flag remains set until the next sweep in its direction.
Calculation: Opposing liquidity distances = distance from close to the closest retained pivot high strictly above close and distance from close to the closest retained pivot low strictly below close; either distance is unavailable if no qualifying pivot exists.
Long: Chart low <= upper bound AND chart high >= lower bound of any retained bullish higher-timeframe gap zone AND (delivery filter disabled OR bullish delivery break) AND (liquidity filter disabled OR either opposing liquidity distance unavailable OR distance down >= distance up) AND (wick filter disabled OR min(open, close) - low <= ATR multiplied by a threshold) AND long invalidation flag is false.
Short: Chart low <= upper bound AND chart high >= lower bound of any retained bearish higher-timeframe gap zone AND (delivery filter disabled OR bearish delivery break) AND (liquidity filter disabled OR either opposing liquidity distance unavailable OR distance up >= distance down) AND (wick filter disabled OR high - max(open, close) <= ATR multiplied by a threshold) AND short invalidation flag is false.
