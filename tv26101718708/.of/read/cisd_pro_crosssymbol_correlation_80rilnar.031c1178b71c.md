Calculation: Pivot = a bar whose high is a local maximum or whose low is a local minimum over equal numbers of preceding and subsequent bars; recognize it only after the subsequent bars complete.
Calculation: Stretch = for a pivot low, find the nearest bearish body at or before the chart pivot, or strictly before the higher-timeframe pivot; for a pivot high, find the nearest bullish body by the same rule. Continue backward through consecutive bodies of that direction. The level is the open of the earliest body and the end is the close of the nearest body. Require a body within the scan lookback.
Calculation: Volatility multiplier = divide the square root of a fast exponentially weighted average of squared close-to-close log returns by that of a slower average, both initialized to zero. Select a high, low, or neutral multiplier according to whether the ratio is above, below, or between its thresholds; use the neutral multiplier when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Stretch size test = the absolute difference between the stretch level and end is at least ATR times a size parameter, optionally multiplied by the chart volatility multiplier. For a chart pivot use chart ATR at pivot recognition; for a higher-timeframe pivot use ATR at the pivot on the higher timeframe.
Calculation: Purge = a bar's low falls below an unpurged previously recognized pivot low, or its high rises above an unpurged previously recognized pivot high; remove that pivot from the purge candidates. A qualifying purge occurs on or after the new pivot and within the purge tolerance, and sweeps a low for a bullish setup or a high for a bearish setup.
Calculation: Chart pending setup = a newly recognized pivot and stretch passing the optional stretch size test and optional qualifying purge. Replace any older pending setup of the same direction. Remove it when its pivot is swept or its age from recognition reaches the pending timeout. Its level stays fixed.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pending setup = on arrival of a new higher-timeframe bar, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar to recognize a strictly higher high or strictly lower low than all bars in the pivot window, and construct its stretch using completed higher-timeframe bars. Apply the optional stretch size test, replace any older pending setup of the same direction, and remove the setup when its age in higher-timeframe bars reaches the pending timeout. Its level stays fixed.
Long: A chart pending setup is created from a pivot low.
Short: A chart pending setup is created from a pivot high.
Long: A chart bullish pending setup remains active AND chart close > its stretch level.
Short: A chart bearish pending setup remains active AND chart close < its stretch level.
Long: A higher-timeframe pending setup is created from a pivot low.
Short: A higher-timeframe pending setup is created from a pivot high.
Long: A higher-timeframe bullish pending setup remains active AND the last completed higher-timeframe close > its stretch level, evaluated when a new higher-timeframe bar arrives.
Short: A higher-timeframe bearish pending setup remains active AND the last completed higher-timeframe close < its stretch level, evaluated when a new higher-timeframe bar arrives.
