Calculation: Candle pressure = body weight × (close − open) / max(EMA(abs(close − open), body normalization lookback), minimum price increment) + close-location weight × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1) + range weight × ((high − low) − EMA(high − low, range normalization lookback)) / max(EMA(high − low, range normalization lookback), minimum price increment) + optional volume weight × (volume − EMA(volume, volume normalization lookback)) / max(EMA(volume, volume normalization lookback), volume denominator floor); use zero for close location when high equals low and omit the volume term when disabled.
Calculation: Scaled pressure = clamp(z-score of EMA(candle pressure, pressure memory lookback) over the normalization lookback × pressure scale, lower oscillator bound, upper oscillator bound); the z-score is zero when its standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Channel context = EMA(clamp(((close − (channel midpoint − channel offset)) / max(2 × channel offset, minimum price increment)) × percentage scale − half the percentage scale, lower oscillator bound, upper oscillator bound), context smoothing lookback), where channel midpoint is EMA((high + low) / 2, channel lookback), and channel offset is EMA(high − low, channel lookback) × channel scale × (1 + body boost × EMA(abs(close − open), channel lookback) / max(EMA(high − low, channel lookback), minimum price increment)).
Calculation: Composite = EMA(EMA(pressure blend weight × scaled pressure + (1 − pressure blend weight) × channel context, presmoothing lookback), main smoothing lookback); signal = EMA(composite, signal lookback).
Calculation: Regime = initially neutral; on each bar set bullish if composite > upper regime threshold + hysteresis, otherwise set bearish if composite < lower regime threshold − hysteresis, otherwise set neutral if the previous regime was bullish AND composite < upper regime threshold − hysteresis OR the previous regime was bearish AND composite > lower regime threshold + hysteresis; otherwise retain the previous regime.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = a composite pivot high or low confirmed after the specified number of right-hand bars, using specified left-hand and right-hand pivot lookbacks. Compare its composite and the price high or low at the pivot bar with those at the immediately preceding confirmed oscillator pivot of the same kind. Pivot spacing is the number of bars between their confirmation bars minus one.
Long: composite >= zero.
Short: composite < zero.
Long: composite >= signal.
Short: composite < signal.
Long: regime = bullish.
Short: regime = bearish.
Long: composite crosses above signal.
Short: composite crosses below signal.
Long: regime = bullish AND composite > upper extreme threshold AND abs(composite − previous composite) > expansion threshold.
Short: regime = bearish AND composite < lower extreme threshold AND abs(composite − previous composite) > expansion threshold.
Long: a low oscillator pivot is confirmed AND pivot spacing >= minimum spacing AND pivot spacing <= maximum spacing AND ((pivot low price < previous pivot low price AND pivot composite > previous pivot composite) OR (pivot low price > previous pivot low price AND pivot composite < previous pivot composite)).
Short: a high oscillator pivot is confirmed AND pivot spacing >= minimum spacing AND pivot spacing <= maximum spacing AND ((pivot high price > previous pivot high price AND pivot composite < previous pivot composite) OR (pivot high price < previous pivot high price AND pivot composite > previous pivot composite)).
