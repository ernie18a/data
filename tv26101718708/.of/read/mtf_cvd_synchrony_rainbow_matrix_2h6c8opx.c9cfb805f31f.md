Calculation: Smoothed flow = EMA(volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low)); use zero for the signed contribution when high equals low.
Calculation: Flow score = midpoint of the 0–100 scale plus half the scale multiplied by smoothed flow divided by the rolling maximum absolute smoothed flow; use the midpoint when that maximum is zero and clamp the result to the scale. Calculate independently on five ordered timeframes from shortest to longest, with separately configurable smoothing and normalization lengths. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Composite score = weighted average of the five flow scores, renormalizing weights over available scores; use the scale midpoint if none are available. The two middle-longer timeframes have equal, largest weights, the second-shortest has the next-largest weight, and the shortest and longest have equal, smallest weights.
Calculation: Composite smoothed flow = weighted average of the five timeframe smoothed flows, renormalizing weights over available values; use zero if none are available.
Calculation: Adaptive bands = EMA of the rolling highest composite score and EMA of its rolling lowest; their midpoint and respective distances from it define upper and lower bands by multiplying each distance by a configurable band multiplier and adding or subtracting it from the midpoint, clamped to the score scale. The extreme-band multiplier is greater than the exhaustion-band multiplier. The extreme bands may alternatively be fixed upper and lower thresholds.
Calculation: Confirmed flow pivot = a high or low of either a timeframe's smoothed flow or the chart-timeframe composite score, confirmed after an equal configurable number of bars on each side; pair each pivot with the price high or low on the pivot bar. For timeframe flow pivots, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe intrabar within the chart bar.
Long: Composite score >= score-scale midpoint OR any timeframe smoothed flow >= 0 OR composite smoothed flow >= 0.
Short: Composite score < score-scale midpoint OR any timeframe smoothed flow < 0 OR composite smoothed flow < 0.
Long: Any timeframe flow score OR the composite score > its previous chart-bar value plus a configurable deadband.
Short: Any timeframe flow score OR the composite score < its previous chart-bar value minus the same deadband.
Long: The composite score crosses above the upper exhaustion band OR crosses above the upper extreme band.
Short: The composite score crosses below the lower exhaustion band OR crosses below the lower extreme band.
Long: On confirmation of a flow pivot low, its paired price low < the preceding flow pivot low's paired price low AND its flow value > the preceding flow pivot low's flow value.
Short: On confirmation of a flow pivot high, its paired price high > the preceding flow pivot high's paired price high AND its flow value < the preceding flow pivot high's flow value.
Long: The shortest-timeframe flow score <= a lower threshold AND the longest-timeframe flow score >= an upper threshold.
Short: The shortest-timeframe flow score >= an upper threshold AND the longest-timeframe flow score <= a lower threshold.
