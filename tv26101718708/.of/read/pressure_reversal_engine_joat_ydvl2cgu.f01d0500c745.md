Calculation: Candle buy share = clamp(0.16 + 0.44 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) + 0.22 × abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) + 0.18 × (1 if close ≥ open else 0), 0.05, 0.95); sell share = 1 − buy share; pressure = buy share − sell share
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / 20-bar simple moving average of volume (use 1 if the average is zero)
Calculation: Demand absorption = pressure below negative pressure threshold AND close > open AND lower wick > upper wick AND abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) < 0.38 AND volume ratio > 1.08; supply absorption = pressure above pressure threshold AND close < open AND upper wick > lower wick AND abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) < 0.38 AND volume ratio > 1.08
Calculation: Pressure profile = over a rolling lookback, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into equal rows; assign each bar’s volume to the row containing its HLC3 and split it by that bar’s buy/sell shares. POC = midpoint of the row with greatest volume. Value area = contiguous rows expanded from the POC, adding the higher-volume adjacent row each step until the configured fraction of total volume is covered; VAL and VAH are the lower and upper boundaries. Profile skew = (total estimated buy volume − total estimated sell volume) / total volume.
Calculation: Swing high/low = most recent confirmed pivot high/low, requiring the configured numbers of chart bars on both sides of the pivot
Calculation: Reclaim high = close crosses above the most recent swing high from at or below it AND pressure is above threshold; reject high = high exceeds the most recent swing high AND close is below it AND pressure is below negative threshold; reclaim low = close crosses below the most recent swing low from at or above it AND pressure is below negative threshold; reject low = low falls below the most recent swing low AND close is above it AND pressure is above threshold
Calculation: Bull mismatch = close > open AND pressure below negative threshold; bear mismatch = close < open AND pressure above threshold
Long: confirmed chart bar AND (fast EMA > slow EMA when trend filter is enabled) AND close > POC AND close > VAL AND (demand absorption OR reject low OR reclaim high) AND profile skew > −0.20 AND (no prior signal OR chart bars since prior signal > cooldown)
Long: demand absorption OR bear mismatch
Short: confirmed chart bar AND (fast EMA < slow EMA when trend filter is enabled) AND close < POC AND close < VAH AND (supply absorption OR reject high OR reclaim low) AND profile skew < 0.20 AND (no prior signal OR chart bars since prior signal > cooldown)
Short: supply absorption OR bull mismatch
Exit: long, confirmed chart bar AND (supply absorption OR bear mismatch OR close < fast EMA OR reject high)
Exit: short, confirmed chart bar AND (demand absorption OR bull mismatch OR close > fast EMA OR reject low)
