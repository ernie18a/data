Calculation: Session, weekly, and monthly anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low) / 2 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, reset at each respective period boundary and including current chart bars.
Calculation: Selected VWAP = the session, weekly, or monthly anchored VWAP selected by the regime mode; for Any or All modes, use all three.
Calculation: Ratcheting arc trail = ATR × trail-distance multiplier defines the band distance. Initialize direction as bullish when close ≥ selected VWAP, otherwise bearish; initialize the trail to low minus the band distance when bullish or high plus it when bearish, and velocity to 1. Each bar, calculate extension as clamp(abs(close − selected VWAP) / ATR / 4, 0, 1); acceleration as base acceleration × [1 + (VWAP speed boost − 1) × extension] × [1 + 0.12 × min(run bars / run window, 2)]; velocity as clamp(previous velocity × velocity decay + acceleration, 0, velocity cap); and chase fraction as clamp(minimum chase + 0.03 × velocity + 0.08 × extension, minimum chase, maximum chase). The bullish target is low minus band distance, ratcheted to the greater of that target and the previous trail; move the previous trail toward this ratcheted target by the chase fraction, then cap it at low minus one minimum price increment. The bearish target is high plus band distance, ratcheted to the lesser of that target and the previous trail; move the previous trail toward it by the chase fraction, then floor it at high plus one minimum price increment. On a confirmed direction flip, reset the trail to the new direction’s unratcheted target, velocity to 1, and run bars to 0; otherwise increment run bars. The trail uses current chart bars.
Long: On a confirmed bar, prior direction is bearish AND close > previous bar’s trail AND bullish VWAP gate passes AND bars since prior flip ≥ minimum flip gap. Bullish VWAP gate: close ≥ the selected session, weekly, or monthly VWAP; for Any mode, close ≥ at least one of the three; for All mode, close ≥ all three.
Short: On a confirmed bar, prior direction is bullish AND close < previous bar’s trail AND bearish VWAP gate passes AND bars since prior flip ≥ minimum flip gap. Bearish VWAP gate: close < the selected session, weekly, or monthly VWAP; for Any mode, close < at least one of the three; for All mode, close < all three.
