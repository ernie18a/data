Calculation: VWAP = daily-reset cumulative sum(hlc3*volume)/cumulative sum(volume)
Calculation: Buffer = small fraction of ATR
Calculation: BreakUp = close crosses above (L + Buffer) for ANY level L in {highest high of the previous N bars excluding the current bar, highest high of the current day's premarket session so far, previous day's high, highest high of the current day's opening-range window (first minutes of the regular session) so far}; a level that does not exist yet is skipped; crossing means close > L+Buffer on this bar and previous close <= previous bar's L+Buffer
Calculation: BreakDown = close crosses below (L - Buffer) for ANY level L in {lowest low of the previous N bars excluding the current bar, current day's premarket low so far, previous day's low, current day's opening-range low so far}; a level that does not exist yet is skipped; crossing means close < L-Buffer on this bar and previous close >= previous bar's L-Buffer
Calculation: HTFBull = on bars of a higher timeframe than the chart (last completed higher-timeframe bar): close > VWAP (computed on higher-timeframe bars) AND fast EMA > mid EMA AND RSI > its midpoint AND MACD line > MACD signal line
Calculation: HTFBear = mirror of HTFBull: close < VWAP AND fast EMA < mid EMA AND RSI < its midpoint AND MACD line < MACD signal line
Calculation: BullScore = count of true among: close > VWAP; (fast EMA > mid EMA AND close > fast EMA); RSI > its midpoint; (MACD line > MACD signal AND MACD histogram > previous bar's MACD histogram); volume >= multiplier * SMA(volume); close > slow EMA; BreakUp. EMA periods: fast < mid < slow
Calculation: BearScore = count of true among: close < VWAP; (fast EMA < mid EMA AND close < fast EMA); RSI < its midpoint; (MACD line < MACD signal AND MACD histogram < previous bar's MACD histogram); volume >= multiplier * SMA(volume); close < slow EMA; BreakDown
Long: bar is inside the regular trading session AND BullScore >= minimum score AND HTFBull, and this same condition was false on the previous bar (enter only on the first bar where it becomes true)
Short: bar is inside the regular trading session AND BearScore >= minimum score AND HTFBear, and this same condition was false on the previous bar (enter only on the first bar where it becomes true)
