Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low accumulated during a configured America/Chicago session; reset all session ranges at the Asia session start. London compares against the completed Asia range, New York against the completed London range, and Asia against the completed New York range. The reset removes the New York range at the Asia start.
Calculation: Pivot bias = on the requested timeframe, use the most recent confirmed pivot high and low with equal left and right lookbacks. Bias is bullish if close > pivot high; otherwise it is upper-rejection if high > pivot high AND (upper wick >= body multiplied by a parameter OR upper wick >= range multiplied by a parameter); otherwise it is bearish if close < pivot low; otherwise it is lower-rejection if low < pivot low AND (lower wick >= body multiplied by a parameter OR lower wick >= range multiplied by a parameter); otherwise it is neutral. Body is abs(close - open), wick lengths are measured outside the body, and range is max(high - low, a positive floor).
Calculation: Pressure = SMA of volume multiplied by (2 × close - high - low) / max(high - low, a positive floor), or SMA of close - open when current volume is unavailable or cumulative volume is not positive. Pressure average = a shorter-lookback SMA of pressure.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = volume / SMA(volume) > a high-volume threshold AND body < range × a narrow-body threshold AND lower wick > upper wick × a wick-ratio parameter AND lower wick > body. Bearish rejection reverses the wick comparisons. If current volume is unavailable or cumulative volume is not positive, use relative volume equal to one. Here range is max(high - low, a positive floor).
Calculation: Bullish wide spread = relative volume > the high-volume threshold AND body > range × a wide-body threshold AND close >= open. Bearish wide spread = relative volume > the high-volume threshold AND body > range × the wide-body threshold AND close < open.
Calculation: Exhaustion boundaries = EMA(close) ± ATR multiplied by a parameter.
Calculation: Session signal state = initially zero and reset to zero at each corresponding session start; once a breakout or fade condition occurs, retain its direction and type until reset. Evaluate the upper-boundary branch first: if high > range high, do not evaluate the lower-boundary branch even if its conditions hold. Within either branch, evaluate breakout before fade. New York signals are blocked during a configured news window. A signal requires the corresponding session to be enabled and both reference boundaries to exist.
Long: Session signal state = zero AND high > reference range high AND lower-timeframe pivot bias = bullish AND pressure > 0 AND pressure > pressure average AND bullish wide spread = false. Use the lower timeframe's last completed bar on historical chart bars and its forming bar in realtime.
Short: Session signal state = zero AND high > reference range high AND bullish breakout condition = false AND (lower-timeframe pivot bias = bearish OR bearish rejection = true OR high > upper exhaustion boundary).
Short: Session signal state = zero AND high <= reference range high AND low < reference range low AND lower-timeframe pivot bias = bearish AND pressure < 0 AND pressure < pressure average AND bearish wide spread = false.
Long: Session signal state = zero AND high <= reference range high AND low < reference range low AND bearish breakout condition = false AND (lower-timeframe pivot bias = bullish OR bullish rejection = true OR low < lower exhaustion boundary).
Calculation: Alignment biases = pivot biases with three different lookbacks on the same higher timeframe, using its last completed bar on historical chart bars and its forming bar in realtime; the lookbacks are short, medium, and long. Only bullish and bearish biases count as directional.
Long: Long-lookback alignment bias = bullish AND medium-lookback alignment bias = bullish OR medium-lookback alignment bias = bullish AND short-lookback alignment bias = bullish.
Short: Long-lookback alignment bias = bearish AND medium-lookback alignment bias = bearish OR medium-lookback alignment bias = bearish AND short-lookback alignment bias = bearish.
Calculation: Stop = for a breakout, either the current session midpoint or its opposite extreme, according to the selected stop mode; the London short breakout's opposite extreme is the Asia high. For a fade short, stop = reference high + (reference high - reference midpoint); for a fade long, stop = reference low - (reference midpoint - reference low). For London fades the midpoint is the developing London midpoint; for New York and Asia fades it is the midpoint of the completed reference range. Recalculate the stop each bar. The entry and stop are unavailable on the signal bar because they are computed before the signal state changes.
Calculation: Stop-hit state = retained separately for each session; reset it only when that session has a nonzero signal state on its first bar. Once hit, retain it until such a reset. A stop can trigger only while the session signal is nonzero, the stop-hit state is false, and the session has not been closed.
Exit: long, stop-hit state = false AND session closed state = false AND low < stop.
Exit: short, stop-hit state = false AND session closed state = false AND high > stop.
Exit: long, session closed state = false AND session signal state != zero AND America/Chicago time >= the London end for a London signal OR America/Chicago time >= the New York end for a New York signal OR America/Chicago time >= the Asia end AND < the Asia start for an Asia signal.
Exit: short, session closed state = false AND session signal state != zero AND America/Chicago time >= the London end for a London signal OR America/Chicago time >= the New York end for a New York signal OR America/Chicago time >= the Asia end AND < the Asia start for an Asia signal.
