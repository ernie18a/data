Calculation: Lead K and D = select either stochastic RSI or price stochastic on the chart timeframe. For stochastic RSI, calculate RSI of close, apply the stochastic formula to RSI over its lookback, and smooth with an SMA to obtain K. For price stochastic, apply the stochastic formula to close, high, and low, and smooth with an SMA to obtain K. D is an SMA of K. The slower stochastic RSI variant uses longer RSI, stochastic, K-smoothing, and D-smoothing periods than the faster variant.
Calculation: Divergence oscillator = SMA of lead K over a separate smoothing period.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low or high = a low or high of the divergence oscillator confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars using configurable left-hand and right-hand pivot lookbacks. Compare the oscillator and the price low or high at the pivot bar with those at the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind. The number of bars since that preceding pivot's confirmation through the bar before the current confirmation must be within the configured minimum and maximum divergence ranges, inclusive.
Calculation: Raw long = lead K crosses above lead D AND lead K <= oversold threshold + zone tolerance AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close > EMA of close.
Calculation: Raw short = lead K crosses below lead D AND lead K >= overbought threshold - zone tolerance AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close < EMA of close.
Long: Raw long AND the number of bars since the most recent prior raw long > cooldown OR raw long AND no prior raw long exists OR raw long AND cooldown = 0.
Short: Raw short AND the number of bars since the most recent prior raw short > cooldown OR raw short AND no prior raw short exists OR raw short AND cooldown = 0.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its oscillator value > the preceding pivot low's oscillator value AND its price low < the preceding pivot low's price low.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its oscillator value < the preceding pivot low's oscillator value AND its price low > the preceding pivot low's price low.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its oscillator value < the preceding pivot high's oscillator value AND its price high > the preceding pivot high's price high.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its oscillator value > the preceding pivot high's oscillator value AND its price high < the preceding pivot high's price high.
Long: Lead K <= oversold threshold OR lead K > lead D AND lead K > oscillator midpoint OR lead K > oversold threshold AND lead K < overbought threshold AND lead K > lead D AND lead K - previous lead K > momentum threshold.
Short: Lead K >= overbought threshold OR lead K < lead D AND lead K < oscillator midpoint OR lead K > oversold threshold AND lead K < overbought threshold AND lead K < lead D AND lead K - previous lead K < negative momentum threshold.
Long: RSI of close - previous RSI of close > RSI momentum threshold OR RSI of close <= RSI oversold threshold.
Short: RSI of close - previous RSI of close < negative RSI momentum threshold OR RSI of close >= RSI overbought threshold.
