Calculation: Pivot high and pivot low = retain the latest pivot of each kind; on a bearish chart bar after the most recent non-doji bar was bullish, set pivot high to the greater high of the current and immediately preceding bars; on a bullish bar after the most recent non-doji bar was bearish, set pivot low to the lesser low of those bars. Record the bar of the extreme, choosing the preceding bar on a tie. Initialize the most recent non-doji direction as bullish.
Calculation: Ranges = in higher-timeframe mode, at the start of a new higher-timeframe bar, create a range from the high and low of the higher-timeframe bar preceding the last completed one if the last completed higher-timeframe close is within those extremes, inclusive. In session mode, assign each chart bar to the first matching configured session or off-hours; at a change to a different non-off-hours session, create a range from the high and low accumulated since the previous such change if the last accumulated close is within those extremes, inclusive. Off-hours bars continue updating the preceding session's accumulation. On creation, mark each normal range extreme already touched by the current bar as mitigated without creating a pending signal. Thereafter, a high is mitigated on its first high >= range high and a low on its first low <= range low. Process ranges newest first and, after mitigation processing, remove oldest ranges exceeding the configured maximum.
Calculation: Special ranges = if enabled, when a normal range high is newly mitigated and pivot high < range high, create a low-only range at that pivot high on the next bar; when a normal range low is newly mitigated and pivot low > range low, create a high-only range at that pivot low on the next bar. Remove a special range without a signal if its extreme is touched on its creation bar or the next bar; otherwise process its first mitigation like a normal range. Special ranges do not create further special ranges.
Calculation: Pending signals = on a newly mitigated range low, if a pivot high exists, replace the pending bullish signal with that pivot high, its pivot bar, and the mitigated level; on a newly mitigated range high, if a pivot low exists, replace the pending bearish signal with that pivot low, its pivot bar, and the mitigated level. After processing mitigations, if high > a pending signal's mitigated level AND low < that level, update its confirmation level and pivot bar to the current pivot high if bullish AND pivot high > mitigated level, or to the current pivot low if bearish AND pivot low < mitigated level. On the first close beyond the confirmation level, clear the pending signal whether or not the optional maximum pivot-bar distance filter passes.
Calculation: Trade levels = at each entry signal, set the stop to the current pivot low for a long or pivot high for a short; set the target to signal close + risk-reward ratio × (signal close − stop) for a long or signal close − risk-reward ratio × (stop − signal close) for a short. Retain these levels for the trade; the target is disabled when stop-loss-only mode is enabled.
Long: Pending bullish signal exists AND close > its confirmation level AND (maximum pivot-bar distance filter is disabled OR bars since its recorded pivot bar <= maximum distance).
Long: Reverse-on-stop is enabled AND previous position side = short AND current position side is not short AND high >= tracked short stop AND current pivot low exists.
Short: Pending bearish signal exists AND close < its confirmation level AND (maximum pivot-bar distance filter is disabled OR bars since its recorded pivot bar <= maximum distance).
Short: Reverse-on-stop is enabled AND previous position side = long AND current position side is not long AND low <= tracked long stop AND current pivot high exists.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR (target enabled AND high >= long target) OR short pending signal confirms within the optional maximum pivot-bar distance.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR (target enabled AND low <= short target) OR long pending signal confirms within the optional maximum pivot-bar distance.
