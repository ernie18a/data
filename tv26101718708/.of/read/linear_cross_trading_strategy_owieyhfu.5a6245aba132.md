Calculation: Volume-regression estimate = intercept + slope × current volume, where intercept and slope are the ordinary least-squares coefficients from regressing close on volume over the same lookback
Calculation: MACD line = short-term EMA of the volume-regression estimate minus long-term EMA of the volume-regression estimate
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of the MACD line
Long: Volume-regression estimate crosses above its WMA AND MACD line is above its signal line AND MACD line is higher than each of its two preceding values
Short: MACD line is below its signal line AND MACD line is lower than its preceding value AND current low is lower than each of its two preceding values
