Calculation: Setup = Maintain at most one setup. After the warmup period, create a setup when none exists, or when the previous setup is invalidated or expired and its restart cooldown has elapsed, AND the previous lookback's highest high is greater than its lowest low, AND their difference divided by current ATR is at most the maximum formation width, AND close is between those extrema extended by the trigger buffer times ATR, AND the selected direction passes its trend filter. The long trend filter is close >= long-term EMA OR short-term EMA > long-term EMA; the short filter is close <= long-term EMA OR short-term EMA < long-term EMA. Direction is configurable as long, short, or automatic; automatic selects long when short-term EMA >= long-term EMA AND close >= long-term EMA, otherwise short. At creation, fix the long trigger at the previous highest high plus trigger buffer times ATR and its invalidation at the previous lowest low minus invalidation buffer times ATR; for short, fix the trigger at the previous lowest low minus trigger buffer times ATR and invalidation at the previous highest high plus invalidation buffer times ATR. On each bar, process invalidation, first trigger, then expiry in that priority order. Invalidate a nonterminal long setup when close < its invalidation, or a nonterminal short setup when close > its invalidation. A setup can trigger only once: depending on the selected trigger model, require either a structure break, a trend reclaim, or an impulse release. A long structure break is close > trigger; a short structure break is close < trigger. A long trend reclaim requires close > short-term EMA AND previous close <= previous short-term EMA AND close > trigger minus a configurable ATR buffer; reverse all inequalities and the buffer sign for short. An impulse release requires a structure break AND absolute candle body / ATR >= the configured minimum AND a directional candle AND its close location measured from the opposite extreme / candle range >= the configured minimum. Expire a nonterminal setup when its age reaches the untriggered expiry limit without a trigger, its age since trigger reaches the post-trigger limit, or its total age reaches the hard expiry limit. A first trigger can reactivate a terminal setup before it is cleared. Clear a terminal setup after its terminal retention period, or clear any setup after its maximum total retention age. Evaluate on completed chart bars.
Long: A setup exists AND its fixed direction = long.
Short: A setup exists AND its fixed direction = short.
