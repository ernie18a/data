Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the setup lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: ATR = average true range of chart bars
Calculation: Fast and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with the fast EMA using a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Long: setup side is forced long OR (setup side is automatic AND fast EMA >= slow EMA AND close >= slow EMA); prior range high > prior range low; (prior range high - prior range low) / ATR <= maximum formation width; close <= prior range high + ATR × trigger buffer AND close >= prior range low - ATR × trigger buffer; close >= slow EMA OR fast EMA > slow EMA; sufficient history is available; the bar is confirmed when close-confirmed mode is selected; and no setup is active, or a terminal setup's restart cooldown has elapsed
Short: setup side is forced short OR (setup side is automatic AND NOT (fast EMA >= slow EMA AND close >= slow EMA)); prior range high > prior range low; (prior range high - prior range low) / ATR <= maximum formation width; close <= prior range high + ATR × trigger buffer AND close >= prior range low - ATR × trigger buffer; close <= slow EMA OR fast EMA < slow EMA; sufficient history is available; the bar is confirmed when close-confirmed mode is selected; and no setup is active, or a terminal setup's restart cooldown has elapsed
