Calculation: Horizon state = on a selected source timeframe, calculate a selected EMA, SMA, WMA, or VWMA of close, using EMA if VWMA is unavailable. Calculate log(close / MA) and log(MA / MA N source bars ago) / N, where N is a rounded fraction of the MA length subject to a minimum. Standardize each series using its mean and population standard deviation over an adaptive lookback equal to the smaller of the maximum lookback and the number of valid observations; require a minimum sample and nonzero deviation. Optionally multiply the standardized slope by an efficiency factor: a floor plus one minus that floor times the absolute MA change over N bars divided by the sum of absolute one-bar MA changes over those bars, clipped to the unit interval; use a factor of one when the ratio is unavailable. Clip both standardized components to symmetric limits, take their normalized weighted average, and optionally smooth it with an EMA. Use the chart timeframe, a higher timeframe, or a lower timeframe. For another timeframe, use either the last completed source bar or the source bar still forming; for a lower timeframe, sample the last source bar in each chart bar, with the completed-bar option using its predecessor.
Calculation: Group state = weighted average of available horizon states in a selected shorter-term, middle-term, longer-term, near-term, or all-horizon group, normalized by available weights; the near-term group contains the shorter- and middle-term horizons.
Calculation: Direction shift event = on a confirmed chart bar, the selected horizon state crosses a positive shift threshold after reaching or falling below a negative reset level since the previous bullish direction shift, or crosses the negative shift threshold after reaching or exceeding the positive reset level since the previous bearish direction shift. The reset level is the smaller of a cap and a fraction of the shift threshold; each direction starts unarmed and is disarmed after its event.
Calculation: Extreme recovery event = on a confirmed chart bar, the selected horizon state crosses above a negative extreme threshold after reaching or falling below a greater-magnitude negative arming level since the previous bullish recovery, or crosses below the positive extreme threshold after reaching or exceeding a greater positive arming level since the previous bearish recovery. Each direction starts unarmed and is disarmed after its event.
Long: Any displayed horizon state, including the chart-timeframe state, or any displayed group state > 0.
Short: Any displayed horizon state, including the chart-timeframe state, or any displayed group state < 0.
Long: Efficiency-adjusted standardized MA slope for any displayed horizon >= positive momentum threshold.
Short: Efficiency-adjusted standardized MA slope for any displayed horizon <= negative momentum threshold.
Long: Bullish direction shift event OR bullish extreme recovery event.
Short: Bearish direction shift event OR bearish extreme recovery event.
