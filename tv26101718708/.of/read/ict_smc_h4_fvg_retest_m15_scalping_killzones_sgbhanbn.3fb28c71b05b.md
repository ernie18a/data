Calculation: Fair value gap = for three consecutive bars, a bullish gap has bottom = first high and top = third low when third low > first high; a bearish gap has bottom = third high and top = first low when third high < first low. Higher-timeframe gaps use the last completed higher-timeframe bar as the third bar and become available on the next chart bar. Chart-timeframe gaps use the current chart bar as the third bar. An optional minimum chart-gap size requires top - bottom >= the minimum.
Calculation: Live gaps = the most recently confirmed higher-timeframe gaps up to the tracking limit; optionally invalidate a bullish gap when a chart bar closes below its bottom or a bearish gap when a chart bar closes above its top. A gap used by a setup is unavailable to standard setups until an unfilled limit expires or an eligible stopped retest re-arms it; instant taps can use any live gap. Re-arm a stopped retest only when enabled, its re-entry limit is not reached, and the stop bar closes at or above the bullish gap bottom or at or below the bearish gap top; reset its touch and tag state.
Calculation: Touch buffer = when near-touch is enabled, the greater of a minimum price distance and a fraction of chart-timeframe ATR; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Accepted bullish touch = chart high >= gap bottom AND chart low <= gap top, OR near-touch is enabled AND gap top < chart low <= gap top + touch buffer; optionally also require previous chart close >= gap top AND current open >= gap top - touch buffer. Accepted bearish touch = chart high >= gap bottom AND chart low <= gap top, OR near-touch is enabled AND gap bottom - touch buffer <= chart high < gap bottom; optionally also require previous chart close <= gap bottom AND current open <= gap bottom + touch buffer. A new touch requires an accepted touch on the current bar and no accepted touch on the preceding bar, tracked separately for standard and instant touches.
Calculation: Departure = since gap confirmation, bullish chart close > gap top or bearish chart close < gap bottom; in tolerant mode substitute chart high > bullish gap top or chart low < bearish gap bottom. A retest tag is recorded whenever an accepted touch occurs after departure or when direct taps are allowed; it remains fresh through the configured number of chart bars after the most recent such touch.
Calculation: Standard gap selection = among available live gaps, prefer a new touch with a ready retest, then any ready retest, then a ready continuation, then an eligible current touch, then a fresh tag, then all others. Within a rank choose the smallest distance from close to the gap, except for a new touch with a ready retest choose the smallest touch-to-gap distance; break ties in favor of the newest gap. A ready retest or continuation satisfies its corresponding Long or Short conditions before the trade-availability and bar-close filters.
Calculation: Entry availability = if blocking while a trade or limit order is active is enabled, no tracked trade or pending order exists; otherwise entry is available. Optional killzone filters require chart-bar time to be inside an enabled market-local session window. Optional close confirmation requires the chart bar to have closed.
Calculation: Planned entry = chart close for a market setup, including instant taps; for a limit setup use the chart-gap top for a bullish gap or bottom for a bearish gap when a qualifying chart gap exists, otherwise the higher-timeframe gap top for a long or bottom for a short. A continuation limit always uses its chart-gap edge. A pending long fills when low <= planned entry; a pending short fills when high >= planned entry. Check fills beginning on the setup bar; cancel an unfilled order when its age in chart bars > the expiration limit and make its still-valid source gap available again.
Calculation: Planned stop = for a continuation long, chart-gap bottom minus a price buffer; for a continuation short, chart-gap top plus the buffer. For a retest long, use the lower of higher-timeframe gap bottom and the lowest recorded tagged-bar low including the setup bar, minus the buffer; for a retest short, use the higher of higher-timeframe gap top and the highest recorded tagged-bar high including the setup bar, plus the buffer. In the optional tight mode use the qualifying chart-gap bottom or top instead, falling back to the respective tagged-bar extreme. If the configured minimum stop distance is positive, widen a long stop to at most entry - minimum distance or a short stop to at least entry + minimum distance. Risk is entry minus stop for a long and stop minus entry for a short.
Calculation: Planned target = optionally choose the nearest eligible liquidity high above the reference for a long or liquidity low below it for a short, at least minimum reward-to-risk times risk from entry. Candidates are stored completed market-session highs or lows, confirmed higher-timeframe pivot highs or lows, optionally the last completed higher-timeframe daily high or low, and currently forming market-session highs or lows; use configured storage limits and market-local session windows. Optionally exclude a completed level once a chart high reaches a high level or a chart low reaches a low level. The reference is max(entry, gap top) for a retest long, min(entry, gap bottom) for a retest short, setup-bar high for a continuation long, or setup-bar low for a continuation short. If no eligible target exists or liquidity targeting is disabled, use max(entry + retest reward-to-risk times risk, gap top + buffer) for a retest long, min(entry - retest reward-to-risk times risk, gap bottom - buffer) for a retest short, max(entry + continuation reward-to-risk times risk, setup-bar high + buffer) for a continuation long, or min(entry - continuation reward-to-risk times risk, setup-bar low - buffer) for a continuation short.
Long: A new accepted bullish touch occurs on an available live gap AND the retest module is enabled AND direct mode is selected OR departure has occurred OR direct taps are allowed.
Short: A new accepted bearish touch occurs on an available live gap AND the retest module is enabled AND direct mode is selected OR departure has occurred OR direct taps are allowed.
Long: Instant taps are enabled AND a new accepted bullish touch occurs on a live gap AND its tap count is below the configured limit or the limit is unlimited AND entry is available; if multiple gaps have new touches, use the gap whose midpoint is closest to chart close.
Short: Instant taps are enabled AND a new accepted bearish touch occurs on a live gap AND its tap count is below the configured limit or the limit is unlimited AND entry is available; if multiple gaps have new touches, use the gap whose midpoint is closest to chart close.
Long: The selected available gap is bullish AND the retest module is enabled AND the optional retest killzone and close-confirmation filters pass AND entry is available AND ((direct mode is selected AND an accepted touch occurs AND, if required, a qualifying bullish chart gap exists) OR (direct mode is not selected AND the retest tag is fresh AND (wick trigger is selected OR rejection trigger is selected AND close > gap top OR gap trigger is selected AND a qualifying bullish chart gap exists))).
Short: The selected available gap is bearish AND the retest module is enabled AND the optional retest killzone and close-confirmation filters pass AND entry is available AND ((direct mode is selected AND an accepted touch occurs AND, if required, a qualifying bearish chart gap exists) OR (direct mode is not selected AND the retest tag is fresh AND (wick trigger is selected OR rejection trigger is selected AND close < gap bottom OR gap trigger is selected AND a qualifying bearish chart gap exists))).
Long: The selected available gap is bullish AND bullish continuation is enabled AND close > gap top AND close - gap top <= a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR AND gap age <= the continuation age limit AND a qualifying bullish chart gap exists AND the optional continuation killzone filter passes AND, if structure confirmation is enabled, close > the highest high over the preceding structure lookback AND the optional close-confirmation filter passes AND entry is available AND no retest or instant-tap setup takes priority.
Short: The selected available gap is bearish AND bearish continuation is enabled AND close < gap bottom AND gap bottom - close <= a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR AND gap age <= the continuation age limit AND a qualifying bearish chart gap exists AND the optional continuation killzone filter passes AND, if structure confirmation is enabled, close < the lowest low over the preceding structure lookback AND the optional close-confirmation filter passes AND entry is available AND no retest or instant-tap setup takes priority.
Exit: long, low <= planned stop OR high >= planned target; if both occur on the same bar, the stop takes priority.
Exit: short, high >= planned stop OR low <= planned target; if both occur on the same bar, the stop takes priority.
