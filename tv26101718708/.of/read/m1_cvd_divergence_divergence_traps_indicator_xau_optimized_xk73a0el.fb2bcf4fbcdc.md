Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero when close = open
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low below the lows of the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookahead bars; confirm it after the right-lookahead bars complete. Confirmed pivot high is the inverse.
Calculation: Active bullish divergence level = most recent bullish-divergence pivot low and its pivot bar; replace it on each bullish divergence, clear it on a bearish divergence, after close falls below the level, or when current bar index minus pivot bar exceeds the trap window.
Calculation: Active bearish divergence level = most recent bearish-divergence pivot high and its pivot bar; replace it on each bearish divergence, clear it on a bullish divergence, after close rises above the level, or when current bar index minus pivot bar exceeds the trap window.
Long: Confirmed pivot low AND ((current pivot low < previous pivot low AND CVD at current pivot > CVD at previous pivot) OR (current pivot low > previous pivot low AND CVD at current pivot < CVD at previous pivot)) OR active bearish divergence level exists AND current bar index minus its pivot bar <= trap window AND close > its level
Short: Confirmed pivot high AND ((current pivot high > previous pivot high AND CVD at current pivot < CVD at previous pivot) OR (current pivot high < previous pivot high AND CVD at current pivot > CVD at previous pivot)) OR active bullish divergence level exists AND current bar index minus its pivot bar <= trap window AND close < its level
