Calculation: Chart candles = either regular OHLC or Heikin Ashi OHLC derived from regular bars, as selected; all unqualified bar references are chart-timeframe bars. Index [0] is the decision bar, [1] the preceding bar, and so on.
Calculation: Directional higher-timeframe alignment = the last two completed bars of the longer higher timeframe both have close >= open for bullish alignment or close < open for bearish alignment; an optional stricter tier also requires the last completed bar of the shorter higher timeframe to have the same direction. Both timeframes use either regular or Heikin Ashi candles as selected.
Calculation: Four stochastic lines = SMAs of four price stochastic %K series with progressively longer lookbacks; each has its own smoothing length. The fourth is the slowest.
Calculation: Paired stochastic lines = a price stochastic %K smoothed by an SMA and then another SMA, and a second price stochastic %K with a longer lookback smoothed by an SMA. Their comparison RSI is an independently parameterized RSI of the selected price source.
Calculation: Wave oscillator = EMA of (typical price minus its channel-length EMA) divided by a scale parameter times the channel-length EMA of the absolute deviation; its signal is an SMA of that oscillator.
Calculation: RSI bands = SMA of RSI over a band lookback plus or minus a deviation multiplier times the RSI standard deviation over that lookback; the signal is an SMA of that RSI over a separate lookback. RSI uses regular or Heikin Ashi close as selected.
Calculation: Repeat eligibility = for each signal type and direction separately, track entries at the real open of the decision bar; optionally require every prior entry of that type and direction to have reached the first profit target or its stop. Grade existing entries before evaluating a new signal, starting on the bar after entry; if stop and target are both reached in one bar, stop takes precedence.
