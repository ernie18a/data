Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range session, using the configured session timezone and start offset; lock the levels on the first bar outside that session and reset them at each new chart trading day.
Calculation: First upward or downward break = first chart bar of the trading day after the opening range is locked and within the configured trading session whose selected source is above the opening-range high or below the opening-range low, respectively; the selected source is either close for both directions or high for upward breaks and low for downward breaks.
Long: Opening range is locked AND bar opening time is within the configured trading session AND no long signal has occurred this trading day AND ((retest confirmation is disabled AND current bar is the first upward break) OR (retest confirmation is enabled AND first upward break has occurred AND bars since that break are within the configured retest window AND low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high)).
Short: Opening range is locked AND bar opening time is within the configured trading session AND no short signal has occurred this trading day AND ((retest confirmation is disabled AND current bar is the first downward break) OR (retest confirmation is enabled AND first downward break has occurred AND bars since that break are within the configured retest window AND high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low)).
