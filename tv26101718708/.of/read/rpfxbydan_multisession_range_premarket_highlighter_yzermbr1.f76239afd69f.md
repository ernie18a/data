Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the opening-range window; fixed when the window ends
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low during the opening-range window; fixed when the window ends
Long: Opening range is locked AND current time is within the trading session AND (retest is not required AND (close > opening-range high OR high > opening-range high, according to the configured trigger source) OR retest is required AND an upper break occurred within the configured retest window AND low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high) AND (one-signal-per-side-per-day cap is disabled OR no long signal has occurred today)
Short: Opening range is locked AND current time is within the trading session AND (retest is not required AND (close < opening-range low OR low < opening-range low, according to the configured trigger source) OR retest is required AND a lower break occurred within the configured retest window AND high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low) AND (one-signal-per-side-per-day cap is disabled OR no short signal has occurred today)
