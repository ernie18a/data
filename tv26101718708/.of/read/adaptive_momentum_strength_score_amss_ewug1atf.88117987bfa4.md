Calculation: Momentum score = SMA of the weighted average of three components, or zero when the sum of weights is zero. Each component is a ratio capped at a normalization ceiling and scaled to the score scale: absolute close minus open divided by ATR; volume divided by its SMA; and Bollinger Band width divided by its SMA. Bollinger Band width is (upper band minus lower band) divided by the middle band; its SMA uses the Bollinger Band lookback. Use zero for a ratio when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: Active threshold = either a fixed threshold or the SMA of momentum score plus a multiplier times its standard deviation over the same lookback, clamped between lower and upper bounds; substitute the fixed threshold before clamping when the adaptive calculation is unavailable.
Calculation: Directional pressure = average over a lookback of (close minus open) divided by the greater of ATR and a positive price-increment floor, substituting zero for unavailable ATR.
Calculation: Score range = highest momentum score minus lowest momentum score over the same lookback.
Long: Close > directional EMA AND directional pressure > positive pressure threshold.
Short: Close < directional EMA AND directional pressure < negative pressure threshold.
Long: Momentum score > active threshold AND previous momentum score <= previous active threshold AND close > directional EMA AND directional pressure > positive pressure threshold.
Short: Momentum score > active threshold AND previous momentum score <= previous active threshold AND close < directional EMA AND directional pressure < negative pressure threshold.
Long: Momentum score > active threshold AND previous momentum score <= previous active threshold AND close > directional EMA AND directional pressure > positive pressure threshold AND, when enabled, close > chart-timeframe filter EMA AND, when enabled, close > higher-timeframe filter EMA using either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND, when enabled, score range >= compression threshold or score range > expansion threshold according to the selected regime filter AND, when enabled, bars since the last confirmed long or short signal >= cooldown or no previous confirmed signal exists.
Short: Momentum score > active threshold AND previous momentum score <= previous active threshold AND close < directional EMA AND directional pressure < negative pressure threshold AND, when enabled, close < chart-timeframe filter EMA AND, when enabled, close < higher-timeframe filter EMA using either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND, when enabled, score range >= compression threshold or score range > expansion threshold according to the selected regime filter AND, when enabled, bars since the last confirmed long or short signal >= cooldown or no previous confirmed signal exists.
