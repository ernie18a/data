Calculation: Same-time-slot volume buzz = current 5-minute bar volume divided by the average volume of the corresponding slot on preceding lookback days, multiplied by 100; use the last completed bar from a lower timeframe when the chart timeframe is higher, and the completed bar when it is the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Premarket volume buzz = current premarket cumulative volume divided by the average premarket cumulative volume on preceding lookback days, multiplied by 100; use the still-forming bar from a lower timeframe when the chart timeframe is higher, and the still-forming bar when it is the chart timeframe.
Calculation: After-hours volume buzz = current after-hours cumulative volume divided by the average after-hours cumulative volume on preceding lookback days, multiplied by 100; use the still-forming bar from a lower timeframe when the chart timeframe is higher, and the still-forming bar when it is the chart timeframe.
