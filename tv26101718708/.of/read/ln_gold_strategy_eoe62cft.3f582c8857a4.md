Calculation: Swing state = initially top; on each chart bar, set it to top if close N bars ago > highest close of the latest N bars, otherwise set it to bottom if close N bars ago < lowest close of the latest N bars, otherwise retain the prior state. A new top or bottom occurs when the state changes. Its price is respectively the high or low N bars ago.
Calculation: Resistance zone = latest new top price ± a fixed price thickness; support zone = latest new bottom price ± the same thickness. A zone is touched when low <= its upper bound AND high >= its lower bound. Reset its break state when a new zone of the same type forms.
Calculation: Bullish gap = when low > high two bars ago, store a zone from that earlier high to current low. Bearish gap = when high < low two bars ago, store a zone from current high to that earlier low. Retain only the most recently created configured number of gaps.
Calculation: Gap state = initially waiting for retest. On a later bar, a waiting gap becomes retested if touched, its candle body has not crossed the far boundary, its age is within the optional maximum, and its penetration from the near boundary divided by gap height is within the optional maximum. A retested gap becomes used when its corresponding entry condition is met. After evaluating entries on each bar, invalidate any gap whose candle body crosses its far boundary or whose age exceeds the optional maximum. Used and invalid gaps cannot generate further entries.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe confirmation = use the last completed lower-timeframe bar selected within each chart bar, with short- and long-period close EMAs calculated on the lower timeframe. An upward break means its close > its short EMA AND its previous close <= its previous short EMA; a downward break reverses both inequalities.
Calculation: Risk distance = configured fixed price distance. Target distance = risk distance multiplied by a configured reward-to-risk ratio. Lock-profit distance and activation distance are separately configured fractions of target distance. Lock profit is armed after the bar high reaches entry plus activation distance for a long, or the bar low reaches entry minus activation distance for a short, and resets when the position becomes flat.
Long: New bottom AND its price >= the preceding bottom price.
Short: New bottom AND (its price < the preceding bottom price OR no preceding bottom exists).
Long: New top AND (its price > the preceding top price OR no preceding top exists).
Short: New top AND its price <= the preceding top price.
Long: Resistance zone exists AND close > its upper bound AND that zone has not previously been broken upward.
Short: Support zone exists AND close < its lower bound AND that zone has not previously been broken downward.
Long: Position is flat AND a bullish gap is retested on an earlier bar and remains retested AND lower-timeframe close > both lower-timeframe EMAs AND (upward lower-timeframe break OR continuation is enabled) AND chart short-period close EMA > chart long-period close EMA AND (ADX >= threshold OR trend filter is disabled) AND (close > open AND absolute candle body / candle range >= minimum body fraction OR impulse filter is disabled) AND chart bar is in an enabled session AND (resistance filter is disabled OR resistance zone is not touched OR resistance was broken upward on an earlier bar within the configured retest window AND resistance zone is touched AND close > its lower bound).
Short: Position is flat AND a bearish gap is retested on an earlier bar and remains retested AND lower-timeframe close < both lower-timeframe EMAs AND (downward lower-timeframe break OR continuation is enabled) AND chart short-period close EMA < chart long-period close EMA AND (ADX >= threshold OR trend filter is disabled) AND (close < open AND absolute candle body / candle range >= minimum body fraction OR impulse filter is disabled) AND chart bar is in an enabled session AND (support filter is disabled OR support zone is not touched OR support was broken downward on an earlier bar within the configured retest window AND support zone is touched AND close < its upper bound).
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target distance OR low <= entry price - risk distance while lock profit is unarmed OR low <= entry price + lock-profit distance while lock profit is armed.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - target distance OR high >= entry price + risk distance while lock profit is unarmed OR high >= entry price - lock-profit distance while lock profit is armed.
