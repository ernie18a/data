Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two chart bars earlier; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low].
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two chart bars earlier; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier].
Calculation: Valid bullish FVG retest = on a bar after creation, its range intersects the zone, its candle body low is at or above the zone bottom, its age is within the configured maximum, and max(0, zone top − bar low) / zone height is within the configured maximum retest depth.
Calculation: Valid bearish FVG retest = on a bar after creation, its range intersects the zone, its candle body high is at or below the zone top, its age is within the configured maximum, and max(0, bar high − zone bottom) / zone height is within the configured maximum retest depth.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation EMA cross = on the lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, close crosses above the short-term EMA; bearish confirmation EMA cross = close crosses below the short-term EMA.
Calculation: Previous support zone = configured thickness around the low of the latest confirmed swing low, where the close N chart bars earlier is below the lowest close of the current N chart bars; previous resistance zone = configured thickness around the high of the latest confirmed swing high, where the close N chart bars earlier is above the highest close of the current N chart bars.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price − configured stop distance; long target = entry price + configured stop distance × configured reward multiple; long locked-profit stop = entry price + (long target − entry price) × configured lock-profit fraction; long activation price = entry price + (long target − entry price) × configured activation fraction.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price + configured stop distance; short target = entry price − configured stop distance × configured reward multiple; short locked-profit stop = entry price − (entry price − short target) × configured lock-profit fraction; short activation price = entry price − (entry price − short target) × configured activation fraction.
Long: flat position AND a bullish FVG has a valid retest and is confirmed on a later chart bar AND (lower-timeframe close is above both EMAs AND (bullish confirmation EMA cross OR continuation-after-cross is enabled)) AND chart-timeframe short-term EMA > long-term EMA AND ADX ≥ configured threshold when the trend filter is enabled AND (close > open AND candle body / candle range ≥ configured minimum when the impulse filter is enabled) AND current time is in an enabled session AND (the bar does not intersect the previous resistance zone OR resistance was broken upward and this bar retests that zone within the configured bar limit with close > its bottom).
Short: flat position AND a bearish FVG has a valid retest and is confirmed on a later chart bar AND (lower-timeframe close is below both EMAs AND (bearish confirmation EMA cross OR continuation-after-cross is enabled)) AND chart-timeframe short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND ADX ≥ configured threshold when the trend filter is enabled AND (close < open AND candle body / candle range ≥ configured minimum when the impulse filter is enabled) AND current time is in an enabled session AND (the bar does not intersect the previous support zone OR support was broken downward and this bar retests that zone within the configured bar limit with close < its top).
Exit: long position, high ≥ long target OR (locked-profit stop is armed AND low ≤ long locked-profit stop) OR (locked-profit stop is not armed AND low ≤ long stop)
Exit: short position, low ≤ short target OR (locked-profit stop is armed AND high ≥ short locked-profit stop) OR (locked-profit stop is not armed AND high ≥ short stop)
