Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured daily opening session in America/New_York; midpoint = (high + low) / 2. Reset the range and entry state on a change of day of week; the range is complete on the first bar outside the session.
Calculation: Pending retest = after a completed opening range, record the first close crossover above its high or crossunder below its low when no retest is pending. Starting on the next bar, cancel a pending upward retest if close <= range high, or a pending downward retest if close >= range low; otherwise cancel after the configured timeout in chart bars, checking for a valid retest before cancellation. A new breakout can be recorded on a cancellation bar.
Calculation: Stop = opening-range midpoint, opposite opening-range boundary, or entry price minus a fixed distance for a long and plus that distance for a short, according to the chosen stop method. Fix the stop at the signal bar. Risk = absolute difference between signal-bar close and stop; each of three independently configured profit targets = signal-bar close plus risk multiplied by its reward-to-risk parameter for a long, or minus that amount for a short. Targets are defined only when risk > 0.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing structure = for each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other, map higher-timeframe highs and lows onto chart bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time; identify confirmed pivot highs and lows on each mapped chart-bar series using the same number of chart bars on each side and the same lookback for both timeframes. Retain the latest and preceding confirmed high and low pivots separately.
Long: No opening-range signal has fired since the day reset AND the opening range is complete AND breakout mode is selected AND close crosses above the opening-range high.
Short: No opening-range signal has fired since the day reset AND the opening range is complete AND breakout mode is selected AND close crosses below the opening-range low.
Long: No opening-range signal has fired since the day reset AND a pending upward breakout exists AND the current bar is later than its breakout bar AND low <= the opening-range high in zone-retest mode or the opening-range midpoint in midpoint-retest mode AND close > opening-range high.
Short: No opening-range signal has fired since the day reset AND a pending downward breakout exists AND the current bar is later than its breakout bar AND high >= the opening-range low in zone-retest mode or the opening-range midpoint in midpoint-retest mode AND close < opening-range low.
Long: For either higher-timeframe swing structure, latest pivot high > preceding pivot high AND latest pivot low > preceding pivot low.
Short: For either higher-timeframe swing structure, latest pivot high < preceding pivot high AND latest pivot low < preceding pivot low.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop OR high >= an active profit target; each target exits its configured quantity, and the third target exits the remaining quantity when its configured quantity is zero.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop OR low <= an active profit target; each target exits its configured quantity, and the third target exits the remaining quantity when its configured quantity is zero.
