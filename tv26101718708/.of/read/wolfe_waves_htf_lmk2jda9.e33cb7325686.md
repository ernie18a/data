Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = on higher-timeframe bars, a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across equal lookbacks on both sides; confirm after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish Wolfe pattern = five chronological confirmed pivots with P1, P3, P5 as pivot lows and P2, P4 as pivot highs; P3 < P1, P4 < P2, P4 > P3, and P5 is below the line through P1 and P3 at P5’s time; P2 is the highest pivot high between P1 and P3, and P4 the highest pivot high between P3 and P5; the P1-to-P3 duration is within the symmetry tolerance of the P3-to-P5 duration.
Calculation: Bearish Wolfe pattern = five chronological confirmed pivots with P1, P3, P5 as pivot highs and P2, P4 as pivot lows; P3 > P1, P4 > P2, P4 < P3, and P5 is above the line through P1 and P3 at P5’s time; P2 is the lowest pivot low between P1 and P3, and P4 the lowest pivot low between P3 and P5; the P1-to-P3 duration is within the symmetry tolerance of the P3-to-P5 duration.
Calculation: EPA = the line through P2 and P4, projected to the current chart bar.
Long: Bullish Wolfe pattern exists on any enabled higher timeframe.
Short: Bearish Wolfe pattern exists on any enabled higher timeframe.
Exit: Long, chart close >= bullish EPA OR chart close < bullish P5 price.
Exit: Short, chart close <= bearish EPA OR chart close > bearish P5 price.
