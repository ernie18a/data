Calculation: Gap-timeframe bars = bars on each enabled timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe; evaluate at the first chart bar of each new gap-timeframe bar using the last completed gap-timeframe bar and its history.
Calculation: Volume profile = for the current and a configurable number of preceding daily sessions, including the current chart bar, distribute each chart bar's positive volume equally among all price rows from floor(low / row size) through floor(high / row size), inclusive. Sum volume by row. Fix row size when first available: use a configured size or the last completed daily ATR divided by a configurable factor, rounded to a configured price increment and floored at that increment. Daily bars may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Gap relative volume = average profile volume across the gap's price rows divided by total profile volume divided by the number of occupied profile rows. Exclude both edge rows if the gap spans at least three rows; count missing rows as zero. The result is unavailable if the profile is empty, its total volume is zero, or the gap midpoint row lies outside the range of occupied rows.
Calculation: Confirmed gap = volume-profile confirmation is disabled OR gap relative volume is available and at or below the configured threshold.
Calculation: Stored gaps = maintain separate, ordered lists for each gap timeframe. Before adding new gaps, process each existing gap using the last completed bar of its own timeframe: a bullish gap becomes resistance when close is below its bottom; a bearish gap becomes support when close is above its top. Retain an inverted gap only if inversion display is enabled and either inversion from all gaps is enabled or the original gap was confirmed; otherwise remove it. Remove inverted resistance when close is above its top and inverted support when close is below its bottom. Store each new gap if it is confirmed or showing unconfirmed gaps is enabled, recording its confirmation at creation. After additions, remove oldest gaps until the configured per-timeframe limit is met.
Long: On a gap-timeframe update, the last completed bar's low is above the high two gap-timeframe bars earlier AND the difference is at least a configured multiple of the last completed gap-timeframe ATR AND (the gap is confirmed OR showing unconfirmed gaps is enabled).
Short: On a gap-timeframe update, the last completed bar's high is below the low two gap-timeframe bars earlier AND the difference is at least a configured multiple of the last completed gap-timeframe ATR AND (the gap is confirmed OR showing unconfirmed gaps is enabled).
Long: On a gap-timeframe update, the last completed gap-timeframe close is above the top of a stored, uninverted bearish gap AND inversion display is enabled AND (inversion from all gaps is enabled OR the original gap was confirmed).
Short: On a gap-timeframe update, the last completed gap-timeframe close is below the bottom of a stored, uninverted bullish gap AND inversion display is enabled AND (inversion from all gaps is enabled OR the original gap was confirmed).
