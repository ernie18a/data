Calculation: Anchored VWAP bands = anchored VWAP of a configurable OHLC-derived price source plus or minus its anchored volume-weighted standard deviation multiplied by a configurable positive multiplier; the outer bands use a larger multiplier than the inner bands. Reset the VWAP and standard deviation at each configurable calendar anchor boundary.
Calculation: Excursion state = maintain separate upper and lower states, initially zero and reset to zero at each anchor boundary or when VWAP is unavailable. Outside those bars, when the upper state is zero and close crosses above the inner upper band, set it to 2 if close is above the outer upper band, otherwise 1; while it is positive, set it to 2 whenever close is above the outer upper band. When the lower state is zero and close crosses below the inner lower band, set it to 2 if close is below the outer lower band, otherwise 1; while it is positive, set it to 2 whenever close is below the outer lower band. After a corresponding reversal signal, reset that side's state to zero. Crosses require a strict comparison on the current bar and the opposite or equal comparison on the previous bar.
Long: Lower excursion state = 1 AND close crosses above the inner lower band OR lower excursion state = 2 AND close crosses above the outer lower band.
Short: Upper excursion state = 1 AND close crosses below the inner upper band OR upper excursion state = 2 AND close crosses below the outer upper band.
Long: (open + close) / 2 < outer lower band.
Short: (open + close) / 2 > outer upper band.
Exit: long, high >= the anchored VWAP fixed at a lower-band reversal signal AND current bar is after that signal bar AND no later than its configurable expiry bar.
Exit: short, low <= the anchored VWAP fixed at an upper-band reversal signal AND current bar is after that signal bar AND no later than its configurable expiry bar.
