Calculation: MACD = fast moving average of close − slow moving average of close; signal line = selected moving average of MACD; histogram = MACD − signal line. Fast and slow lengths are shorter and longer, respectively.
Calculation: Momentum quality score = capped weighted score of directional MACD/histogram alignment, absolute histogram strength vs. its EMA baseline, histogram expansion, MACD slope, and MACD acceleration; bullish components require positive direction and bearish components negative direction.
Calculation: Market regime score = capped weighted score of ADX, price efficiency (absolute close change over the lookback divided by the average absolute one-bar close change over that lookback), separation of shorter- and longer-lookback close EMAs divided by ATR, and ATR divided by its longer-lookback average.
Calculation: Entry qualification score = capped weighted score of directional candle body/range and close location, absolute histogram strength vs. its EMA baseline, duration of directional MACD/histogram alignment, directional one-bar close change divided by ATR, and presence of an eligible setup event.
Calculation: Absolute histogram strength = |histogram| / ATR × 100.
Calculation: Stop and target prices = signal-bar close offset by configured stop and target distances: either ATR-based stop distance with targets at configured risk multiples, or configured fixed price distances; targets are ordered outward from entry. ATR uses chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Confirmed bar AND (bullish MACD cross while MACD < 0 OR bullish MACD cross while MACD ≥ 0 OR MACD crosses above 0 while MACD > signal line and histogram > 0 OR histogram > 0 and histogram > previous histogram and previous histogram ≤ histogram two bars ago and MACD > signal line and MACD slope > 0 and no bullish MACD or zero-line cross on this bar OR, in momentum-confirmed mode, an armed bullish setup is followed within its confirmation window by a confirmed bar with MACD > signal line, histogram > 0, histogram > previous histogram, and MACD slope > 0) AND bullish zero-line filter passes AND normalized absolute histogram strength meets its minimum when enabled AND bullish momentum quality score meets its selected minimum when enabled AND bullish trend bias passes when enabled (chart close above chart EMA; for higher-timeframe bias, last completed higher-timeframe close above its EMA; combined mode requires both; optional slope and ATR-distance requirements apply) AND market regime score meets its selected minimum when enabled AND bullish entry qualification score meets its selected minimum when enabled AND close > open AND absolute histogram strength / its EMA baseline meets its minimum AND bullish MACD/histogram alignment persistence meets its minimum AND cooldown has elapsed AND trade slot is available.
Short: Confirmed bar AND (bearish MACD cross while MACD > 0 OR bearish MACD cross while MACD ≤ 0 OR MACD crosses below 0 while MACD < signal line and histogram < 0 OR histogram < 0 and histogram < previous histogram and previous histogram ≥ histogram two bars ago and MACD < signal line and MACD slope < 0 and no bearish MACD or zero-line cross on this bar OR, in momentum-confirmed mode, an armed bearish setup is followed within its confirmation window by a confirmed bar with MACD < signal line, histogram < 0, histogram < previous histogram, and MACD slope < 0) AND bearish zero-line filter passes AND normalized absolute histogram strength meets its minimum when enabled AND bearish momentum quality score meets its selected minimum when enabled AND bearish trend bias passes when enabled (chart close below chart EMA; for higher-timeframe bias, last completed higher-timeframe close below its EMA; combined mode requires both; optional slope and ATR-distance requirements apply) AND market regime score meets its selected minimum when enabled AND bearish entry qualification score meets its selected minimum when enabled AND close < open AND absolute histogram strength / its EMA baseline meets its minimum AND bearish MACD/histogram alignment persistence meets its minimum AND cooldown has elapsed AND trade slot is available.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ TP3 price; begin checking after the entry bar, and resolve a bar touching both stop and targets using the configured priority.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ TP3 price; begin checking after the entry bar, and resolve a bar touching both stop and targets using the configured priority.
