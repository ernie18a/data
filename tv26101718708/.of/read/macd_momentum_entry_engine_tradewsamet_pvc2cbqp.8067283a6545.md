Calculation: Direction d = +1 for long and -1 for short; x[1] and x[2] are the preceding chart-bar values. Evaluate chart-bar decisions at bar close.
Calculation: MACD = selected moving average of the selected OHLC price source with a shorter lookback minus the same type of moving average with a longer lookback; signal = selected moving average of MACD; histogram H = MACD - signal. Moving-average types are EMA, SMA, RMA, or WMA.
Calculation: Directional momentum = d × H > 0 AND d × MACD > d × signal. Histogram expansion = d × H > 0 AND d × H > d × H[1]. MACD slope = MACD - MACD[1]; acceleration = slope - slope[1].
Calculation: Relative score C(x, baseline, target, weight) = weight × clamp(x / max(baseline × target, small positive floor), 0, 1); target score T(x, target, weight) = weight × clamp(max(x, 0) / max(target, small positive floor), 0, 1). Missing inputs to these scores are zero.
Calculation: Momentum quality = alignment weight when directional momentum is true, plus C(abs(H), EMA(abs(H)), strength target, strength weight) when d × H > 0, plus C(abs(H - H[1]), EMA(abs(H - H[1])), expansion target, expansion weight) when histogram expansion is true, plus C(abs(slope), EMA(abs(slope)), slope target, slope weight) when d × slope > 0, plus C(abs(acceleration), EMA(abs(acceleration)), acceleration target, acceleration weight) when d × acceleration > 0. These baseline EMAs use the same lookback.
Calculation: Price efficiency = abs(close - close[lookback]) / sum(abs(close - close[1]), lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero. Regime score = T(ADX, ADX target, ADX weight) + T(price efficiency, efficiency target, efficiency weight) + T(abs(shorter EMA(close) - longer EMA(close)) / ATR, separation target, separation weight) + T(ATR / SMA(ATR), volatility target, volatility weight).
Calculation: Directional persistence = consecutive bars of directional momentum, or zero when alignment fails; if no prior alignment failure is available, use the persistence score target. Candle range = max(high - low, minimum price increment). Directional body = max(d × (close - open), 0) / candle range; directional close location = (close - low) / candle range for long and (high - close) / candle range for short.
Calculation: Entry qualification = T(directional body, body target, body weight) + T(directional close location, location target, location weight) + C(abs(H), EMA(abs(H)), entry separation target, separation weight) when d × H > 0 + T(directional persistence, persistence target, persistence weight) + T(max(d × (close - close[1]), 0) / ATR, impulse target, impulse weight) + trigger weight when an entry candidate exists. The histogram-strength EMA is the same one used in momentum quality.
Calculation: Raw setup = an enabled MACD crossing of the signal in direction d with MACD on the opposite side of zero for reversal or on the directional side including zero for continuation; OR an enabled MACD crossing of zero in direction d with directional momentum; OR an enabled expansion turn with directional momentum, d × slope > 0, histogram expansion, d × H[1] <= d × H[2], and no same-direction MACD/signal or MACD/zero crossing on the current bar. A crossing requires the preceding values to be equal or on the opposite side.
Calculation: Entry candidate = raw setup in early mode; in confirmation mode, a raw setup arms its direction, replaces any previous armed setup, and expires after a configurable window; a later bar within that window is a candidate when directional momentum, histogram expansion, and d × slope > 0. Any accepted signal clears both armed directions.
Calculation: Trend bias = for each selected chart or higher-timeframe layer, d × (layer close - layer EMA(close)) > 0 AND d × (layer close - layer EMA(close)) >= layer ATR × buffer AND, if slope confirmation is enabled, d × (layer EMA - earlier layer EMA) > 0. The chart layer uses the current chart bar and a configurable chart-bar slope lookback. The higher-timeframe layer uses the last completed higher-timeframe close, EMA and ATR, and compares that EMA with the preceding completed higher-timeframe EMA. Combined mode requires both layers; off mode imposes no restriction.
Calculation: Trade levels = at the signal-bar close, freeze stop distance as either ATR × stop multiplier or a fixed price distance, floored at the minimum price increment. Freeze each target distance as either stop distance × its reward/risk multiplier or an independent fixed price distance; floor the first at the minimum increment and each later distance at the preceding distance plus that increment. Stop = entry price - d × stop distance; each target = entry price + d × its target distance. Target distances increase from first to third.
Long: H >= 0.
Short: H < 0.
Long: long entry candidate AND close > open AND directional persistence >= minimum persistence AND abs(H) / max(EMA(abs(H)), small positive floor) >= separation threshold AND (quality filter off OR long momentum quality >= quality threshold) AND (regime filter off OR regime score >= regime threshold) AND (qualification filter off OR long entry qualification >= qualification threshold) AND (trend bias off OR long trend bias passes) AND (zero-line filter off OR MACD > 0) AND (absolute-strength filter off OR abs(H) / max(ATR, minimum price increment) × normalization scale >= strength threshold) AND bars since either accepted signal >= cooldown, or no prior signal AND (trade engine off OR no active trade and no trade closed on this bar).
Short: short entry candidate AND close < open AND directional persistence >= minimum persistence AND abs(H) / max(EMA(abs(H)), small positive floor) >= separation threshold AND (quality filter off OR short momentum quality >= quality threshold) AND (regime filter off OR regime score >= regime threshold) AND (qualification filter off OR short entry qualification >= qualification threshold) AND (trend bias off OR short trend bias passes) AND (zero-line filter off OR MACD < 0) AND (absolute-strength filter off OR abs(H) / max(ATR, minimum price increment) × normalization scale >= strength threshold) AND bars since either accepted signal >= cooldown, or no prior signal AND (trade engine off OR no active trade and no trade closed on this bar).
Exit: long, trade engine enabled AND bar after entry AND low <= stop OR trade engine enabled AND bar after entry AND high >= third target.
Exit: short, trade engine enabled AND bar after entry AND high >= stop OR trade engine enabled AND bar after entry AND low <= third target.
Exit: long, trade engine enabled AND bar after entry AND high >= an unreached first or second target AND (stop-first mode off OR low > stop); exit one equal portion per newly reached target.
Exit: short, trade engine enabled AND bar after entry AND low <= an unreached first or second target AND (stop-first mode off OR high < stop); exit one equal portion per newly reached target.
Calculation: Same-bar resolution = in stop-first mode, a touched stop closes all remaining portions before any targets; in targets-first mode, process reached targets in order, close all remaining portions at the third target if reached, otherwise close remaining portions at the stop if touched.
