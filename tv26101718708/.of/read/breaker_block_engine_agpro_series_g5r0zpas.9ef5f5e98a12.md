Calculation: Pending bearish source = when a pivot high is confirmed, search backward from the pivot bar through the source-candle lookback and select the first bearish candle; store its high and low. Keep the previous pending source if none is found. Replace it when a later qualifying pivot supplies a source; clear it when a bullish breaker is created. A pivot high has the configured number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Pending bullish source = when a pivot low is confirmed, search backward from the pivot bar through the source-candle lookback and select the first bullish candle; store its high and low. Keep the previous pending source if none is found. Replace it when a later qualifying pivot supplies a source; clear it when a bearish breaker is created. A pivot low has the configured number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Breaker = a zone bounded by its source candle's high and low, created by the corresponding new-breaker condition. On creation, retire every active breaker of either side whose overlap with the new zone divided by the smaller zone height meets the overlap threshold; then retain at most the configured number of breakers on the new breaker's side, removing the oldest first. Process bullish creation before bearish creation. A breaker remains active until retired, pruned, or invalidated. Bullish invalidation occurs when close < zone low − ATR × invalidation buffer; bearish invalidation occurs when close > zone high + ATR × invalidation buffer. Use current-bar ATR. For a zero-height zone, floor the overlap denominator at the price increment.
Calculation: Counted retest = an active breaker that was not invalidated on the current bar AND low <= zone high AND high >= zone low AND bars since its creation or last counted retest >= the retest spacing. Update this spacing separately for each breaker.
Long: Close > pending bearish source high + ATR × displacement multiplier × sensitivity factor AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume SMA).
Short: Close < pending bullish source low − ATR × displacement multiplier × sensitivity factor AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume SMA).
Long: A bullish breaker has a counted retest.
Short: A bearish breaker has a counted retest.
