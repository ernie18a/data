Calculation: Break threshold = ATR × displacement multiplier
Calculation: Bearish source candle = the most recent bearish candle found from the confirmed swing high back through the source search window; zone = its high to low
Calculation: Bullish source candle = the most recent bullish candle found from the confirmed swing low back through the source search window; zone = its high to low
Long: On a confirmed bar, a bearish source candle exists and close > its high + break threshold, and (confirmation mode is not Close + Volume OR volume > volume moving average)
Short: On a confirmed bar, a bullish source candle exists and close < its low − break threshold, and (confirmation mode is not Close + Volume OR volume > volume moving average)
Long: An active bullish breaker is retested on a confirmed bar after the minimum retest spacing, low ≤ breaker top, high ≥ breaker bottom, and close > breaker bottom
Short: An active bearish breaker is retested on a confirmed bar after the minimum retest spacing, low ≤ breaker top, high ≥ breaker bottom, and close < breaker top
