Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute difference between close and close at the trend lookback, divided by highest high minus lowest low over that lookback including the current bar; use zero if the range is zero.
Calculation: Volume profile = divide the range from the lowest low to highest high over the profile lookback, including the current bar, into equal rows. Allocate each bar's volume to rows in proportion to the overlap with its high-low range; allocate zero for a zero-range bar. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume row, choosing the lowest row on a tie. Starting at POC, add the adjacent row with greater volume until the accumulated volume reaches the configured fraction of total profile volume; choose the upper row on a tie. VAH and VAL are the midpoints of the highest and lowest included rows. If the profile range is zero, retain the previous profile.
Calculation: Near a profile level = the level is positive AND absolute value of (close minus level) divided by close is less than the tolerance fraction.
Calculation: Estimated buy volume = volume multiplied by (close minus low) divided by (high minus low), or zero when high equals low; estimated sell volume = volume multiplied by (high minus close) divided by (high minus low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Buy threshold = the mean of estimated buy volume over the whale lookback ending at the previous bar plus a configured multiplier times its population standard deviation over the same bars; sell threshold is calculated likewise from estimated sell volume. The entry threshold multiplier is at least as large as the marking threshold multiplier.
Calculation: Long stop = entry-bar low minus entry-bar ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier; long target = entry price plus the reward-to-risk multiplier times (entry price minus long stop). Short stop = entry-bar high plus entry-bar ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier; short target = entry price minus the reward-to-risk multiplier times (short stop minus entry price). These levels remain fixed after entry.
Long: Estimated buy volume > buy marking threshold.
Short: Estimated sell volume > sell marking threshold.
Long: Confirmation is disabled AND position is flat AND bar time is within the configured Chicago session AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown AND estimated buy volume > buy entry threshold AND ((trend efficiency > trend threshold AND close > close at trend lookback AND close is near POC, VAH, or VAL) OR (trend efficiency <= trend threshold AND close is near VAL or POC) OR (close < VAL AND close is near VAL)).
Short: Confirmation is disabled AND position is flat AND bar time is within the configured Chicago session AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown AND estimated sell volume > sell entry threshold AND ((trend efficiency > trend threshold AND close < close at trend lookback AND close is near POC, VAH, or VAL) OR (trend efficiency <= trend threshold AND close is near VAH or POC) OR (close > VAH AND close is near VAH)).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR bar time is outside the configured Chicago session.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR bar time is outside the configured Chicago session.
