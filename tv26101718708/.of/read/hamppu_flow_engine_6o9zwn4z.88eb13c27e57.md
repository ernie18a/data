Calculation: Fast RSI = RSI of close using the shortest RSI lookback; mid RSI = RSI of (high + low + close) / 3 using the middle RSI lookback; slow RSI = RSI of EMA(close) using the longest RSI lookback. Fast and slow RSI averages are their respective SMAs, with the slow average using the longer smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Bull regime = slow RSI > RSI midpoint AND slow RSI > slow RSI average. Bear regime = slow RSI < RSI midpoint AND slow RSI < slow RSI average.
Calculation: Compression = absolute(fast RSI - mid RSI) < compression threshold AND absolute(mid RSI - slow RSI) < compression threshold. Release = compression on the previous bar AND no compression on the current bar. Up expansion = release AND fast RSI > mid RSI AND mid RSI > slow RSI. Down expansion = release AND fast RSI < mid RSI AND mid RSI < slow RSI.
Calculation: WaveTrend main = EMA((typical price - channel EMA) / (scaling factor × EMA(absolute(typical price - channel EMA)))), where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3 and both channel EMAs use the same lookback. Replace a zero deviation EMA with a small positive constant. WaveTrend signal = SMA(WaveTrend main). Bull WaveTrend event = the lowest main value over the preceding stretch lookback < lower stretch threshold AND main crosses above signal AND fast RSI on the previous bar < its average on the previous bar AND fast RSI > its average. Bear WaveTrend event = the highest main value over the preceding stretch lookback > upper stretch threshold AND main crosses below signal AND fast RSI on the previous bar > its average on the previous bar AND fast RSI < its average.
Calculation: Bull RSI event = bull regime AND mid RSI > bullish hold threshold AND (fast RSI crosses above its average OR fast RSI on the previous bar < lower extreme threshold AND fast RSI > higher recovery threshold). Bear RSI event = bear regime AND mid RSI < bearish hold threshold AND (fast RSI crosses below its average OR fast RSI on the previous bar > upper extreme threshold AND fast RSI < lower recovery threshold).
Calculation: Sweep range = maximum of high - low and a positive minimum price increment; body = maximum of absolute(close - open) and that increment; lower wick = minimum(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - maximum(open, close); close position = (close - low) / sweep range. Low-sweep score counts the true conditions: sweep range >= ATR × range threshold; lower wick / sweep range >= wick-range threshold; lower wick / body >= wick-body threshold; close position >= reclaim threshold. High-sweep score uses upper wick and close position <= 1 - reclaim threshold. A low sweep is low < the lowest low of the preceding liquidity lookback AND close > that lowest low AND either quality filtering is disabled or low-sweep score >= minimum score. A high sweep is high > the highest high of the preceding liquidity lookback AND close < that highest high AND either quality filtering is disabled or high-sweep score >= minimum score.
Calculation: For each selected flow-stack timeframe, compute RSI of close using the same lookback, using either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar consistently with the selected setting. A slot can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a lower timeframe use its last available intrabar within the chart bar. A slot is bullish when RSI > upper flow threshold and bearish when RSI < lower flow threshold. Bull and bear scores count the respective slots. Slot curl = current slot RSI - slot RSI a curl lookback earlier in that slot's timeframe.
Calculation: Early bull event = the fast profile is selected AND bull regime is false AND (either of the first two flow-stack slots is bullish OR bull score >= minimum bull count AND bear score <= maximum bear count) AND a price reclaim began within the recent reclaim window AND mid RSI > bullish hold threshold AND fast RSI > its average AND mid RSI > its previous value AND slow RSI >= its previous value AND this entire condition was false on the previous bar. A price reclaim begins when (close first exceeds the high of the latest low-sweep bar while that sweep is within the flow window) OR (close crosses above its price EMA AND close > previous high) OR (close > its price EMA AND close first exceeds the highest high of a short preceding lookback).
Calculation: Bull reclaim event = bull RSI event OR bull WaveTrend event OR early bull event. Bear reject event = bear WaveTrend event OR (bear RSI event AND, only in the fast profile, down expansion). A reclaim or reject is allowed when no earlier same-side reclaim or reject has been accepted, or the number of bars since the last accepted one is > its side-specific cooldown. Update that last bar on every allowed event, independently of higher-timeframe classification.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bull and bear bias compare, respectively with > and <, one selected pair: higher-timeframe close and its EMA; higher-timeframe typical price and its EMA; or shorter and longer higher-timeframe close EMAs. Use either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar according to the selected setting. Classification passes when higher-timeframe bias is disabled OR either bull or bear bias is true.
Calculation: Bull flow raw = classification passes AND a low sweep occurred within the flow window AND close > the high of the most recent low-sweep bar AND (up expansion OR bull reclaim event OR a release occurred within the flow window AND fast RSI > mid RSI AND mid RSI > slow RSI). Bear flow raw = classification passes AND a high sweep occurred within the flow window AND close < the low of the most recent high-sweep bar AND (down expansion OR bear reject event OR a release occurred within the flow window AND fast RSI < mid RSI AND mid RSI < slow RSI). A flow event is allowed when its raw condition is true and was false on the preceding bar AND no earlier same-side flow event was accepted or bars since that event > the flow cooldown. Update the last accepted bar separately for each side.
