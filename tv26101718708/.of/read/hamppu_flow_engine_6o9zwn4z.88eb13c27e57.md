Calculation: Fast RSI = RSI of close using a shorter lookback; Mid RSI = RSI of HLC3 using a medium lookback; Slow RSI = RSI of the 13-period EMA of close using a longer lookback
Calculation: Fast RSI average = SMA of Fast RSI; Slow RSI average = SMA of Slow RSI
Calculation: WT1 = EMA of ((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel lookback)), channel lookback))) using the WT average lookback; WT2 = SMA of WT1 using the WT signal lookback
Calculation: Sweep low quality score = sum of four tests: candle range ≥ ATR × minimum range/ATR; lower wick / candle range ≥ minimum wick fraction; lower wick / max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment) ≥ minimum wick/body ratio; (close − low) / candle range ≥ minimum close reclaim fraction
Calculation: Sweep high quality score = sum of four tests: candle range ≥ ATR × minimum range/ATR; upper wick / candle range ≥ minimum wick fraction; upper wick / max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment) ≥ minimum wick/body ratio; (close − low) / candle range ≤ 1 − minimum close reclaim fraction
Calculation: HTF bullish bias = selected higher-timeframe mode compares close, HLC3, or the shorter EMA of close against the longer EMA of close on the higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar when confirmed mode is active, otherwise the bar still forming
Calculation: HTF bearish bias = selected higher-timeframe mode compares close, HLC3, or the shorter EMA of close against the longer EMA of close on the higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar when confirmed mode is active, otherwise the bar still forming
