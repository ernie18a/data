Calculation: Prior CRT range = high and low of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = [high two bars earlier, current low] when current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG zone = [current high, low two bars earlier] when current high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high (low) = a bar high (low) greater (less) than the highs (lows) of the configured number of bars immediately before and after it.
Long: Intraday AND current low < prior CRT low AND current close > prior CRT low
Long: Current low > high two bars earlier AND current low − high two bars earlier ≥ configured minimum gap
Long: Active bearish FVG AND current close > its top
Long: At a confirmed pivot low, the latest chart-symbol pivot low < its preceding pivot low AND the latest correlated-symbol pivot low > its preceding pivot low AND the latest correlated-symbol pivot is no more than the configured maximum bars old
Short: Intraday AND current high > prior CRT high AND current close < prior CRT high
Short: Current high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier − current high ≥ configured minimum gap
Short: Active bullish FVG AND current close < its bottom
Short: At a confirmed pivot high, the latest chart-symbol pivot high > its preceding pivot high AND the latest correlated-symbol pivot high < its preceding pivot high AND the latest correlated-symbol pivot is no more than the configured maximum bars old
