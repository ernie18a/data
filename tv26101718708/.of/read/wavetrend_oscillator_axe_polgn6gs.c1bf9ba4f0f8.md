Calculation: WaveTrend signal = apply an improved EMA of length n2, then a 2-pole Ehlers SuperSmoother of length smoothing, to (log(close) − improved EMA(log(close), n1)) / improved EMA(abs(log(close) − improved EMA(log(close), n1)), n1); use 0 when the denominator is 0. The improved EMA uses alpha = 2/(length+1) and seeds its first value with the input.
Calculation: WaveTrend average = simple moving average of the WaveTrend signal over maperiod bars.
Calculation: Bottom turn = WaveTrend average < overbought level AND signal crosses above average AND the sum of signal over the five bars ending one bar ago is less than the corresponding sum of average.
Calculation: Top turn = WaveTrend average > oversold level AND signal crosses below average AND the sum of signal over the five bars ending one bar ago is greater than the corresponding sum of average.
Long: Bottom turn OR (Bottom turn AND a prior bottom turn exists AND current average > average at the prior bottom turn AND current lowest low over five bars < lowest low at the prior bottom turn) OR (Bottom turn AND a prior bottom turn exists AND current average < average at the prior bottom turn AND current lowest low over five bars > lowest low at the prior bottom turn) OR (signal > average on the chart timeframe and on both higher timeframes)
Short: Top turn OR (Top turn AND a prior top turn exists AND current average < average at the prior top turn AND current highest high over five bars > highest high at the prior top turn) OR (Top turn AND a prior top turn exists AND current average > average at the prior top turn AND current highest high over five bars < highest high at the prior top turn) OR (signal <= average on the chart timeframe and on both higher timeframes)
Long: Signal > average on each displayed timeframe; higher-timeframe calculations use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Signal <= average on each displayed timeframe; higher-timeframe calculations use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
