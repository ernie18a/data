Calculation: Reference levels = the high and low of each of three successively longer completed calendar periods, aggregated from chart bars; the shortest period may instead reset at the instrument session boundary. Reset the longer periods when their calendar period changes. The upper and lower volatility levels equal the current shortest-period opening price plus and minus a multiple of ATR from the last completed bar of a configurable higher timeframe, sampled at the shortest-period reset and held fixed until the next reset.
Calculation: Confirmation distance = a configurable nonnegative multiple of ATR from the last completed bar of a configurable timeframe that may be the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. If that ATR is unavailable, the distance test passes.
Calculation: Armed level = maintain independent state for each reference level. At its period reset, clear pending and locked state. When unlocked and not pending, arm an upper level if high > level, or a lower level if low < level; record the bar index. A pending setup can confirm only on a later completed chart bar within the configurable confirmation window. A qualifying enabled sweep clears pending; a qualifying enabled breakout clears pending and locks the level. Unlock an upper level when close < level and a lower level when close > level, before checking for a new breach on that bar. If the window expires, clear pending without locking; the next breach can arm it. Evaluate breach before expiry on each bar.
Long: Any armed lower level is within its confirmation window AND close > level AND close > open AND close - level >= confirmation distance.
Long: Any armed upper level is within its confirmation window AND close > level AND close > open AND close - level >= confirmation distance.
Short: Any armed upper level is within its confirmation window AND close < level AND close < open AND level - close >= confirmation distance.
Short: Any armed lower level is within its confirmation window AND close < level AND close < open AND level - close >= confirmation distance.
Long: Any reference level <= close.
Short: Any reference level > close.
