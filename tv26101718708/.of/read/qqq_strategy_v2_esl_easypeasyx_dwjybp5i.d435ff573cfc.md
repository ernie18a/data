Calculation: Main, long-trend, and short-trend moving averages = selected moving average of close; the three lookbacks are independently configurable
Calculation: EMA50 and EMA200 = exponential moving averages of close with shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively
Calculation: Fast, slow, and filter Ultimate RSI = for each respective lookback, let U = highest(source), L = lowest(source), R = U − L, and D = R if U > prior U, −R if L < prior L, otherwise source − prior source; RSI = selected moving average(D) / selected moving average(abs(D)) × 50 + 50. Their lookbacks are ordered fast < slow < filter.
Calculation: Ultimate RSI signal = selected smoothing average of its corresponding Ultimate RSI; the same smoothing lookback applies to fast, slow, and filter RSI
Calculation: Fast and slow signed standard deviation = sign(change(SMA(close, lookback))) × standard deviation(close, lookback); signal = EMA of the corresponding signed standard deviation. The slow lookback and signal lookback are longer than the fast ones.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator = EMA(hl2, shorter lookback) − EMA(hl2, longer lookback)
Calculation: Selected stop level = selected long/short stop method: trailing percentage of close, ratcheted upward for long and downward for short; rolling highest/lowest of close adjusted by the stop percentage, ratcheted in the same direction; ratcheted highest EMA(close) for long or lowest of 2×close − EMA(close) for short; ratcheted close −/+ ATR multiple for long/short; or ratcheted lowest low / highest high over the stop lookback for long/short
Long: bar close is confirmed AND (close > main moving average AND the main moving average rose over three bars OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal AND fast RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit OR slow Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal AND slow RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI AND slow RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit OR filter RSI crosses above its signal AND filter RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI AND fast RSI and slow RSI are above their lower limits AND filter RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit OR Awesome Oscillator crosses above zero OR fast signed standard deviation crosses above its signal OR slow signed standard deviation crosses above its signal) AND (long trend filter is disabled OR selected long filter condition is true)
Short: bar close is confirmed AND (close < main moving average AND the main moving average fell over three bars OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below its signal AND fast RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit OR slow Ultimate RSI crosses below its signal AND slow RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI AND slow RSI is below its lower limit AND above its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit OR filter RSI crosses below its signal AND filter RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI AND fast RSI is below its upper limit AND slow RSI is below its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit OR Awesome Oscillator crosses below zero OR fast signed standard deviation crosses below its signal OR slow signed standard deviation crosses below its signal OR close crosses below EMA50 OR close crosses below EMA200) AND (short trend filter is disabled OR selected short filter condition is true)
Exit: Long, low < selected stop level OR take profit is enabled AND (close > short-trend moving average OR fast signed standard deviation > its signal OR filter Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI when the long trend filter is set to fast-and-slow Ultimate RSI)
Exit: Short, high > selected stop level OR take profit is enabled AND (close > short-trend moving average OR fast signed standard deviation > its signal OR filter Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI when the long trend filter is set to fast-and-slow Ultimate RSI)
