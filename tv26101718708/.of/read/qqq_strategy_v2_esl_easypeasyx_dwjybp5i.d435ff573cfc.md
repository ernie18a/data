Calculation: Ultimate RSI = for a chosen source and lookback, set upper and lower to the rolling highest and lowest source, and range to upper minus lower. Set change to range if upper increased from the preceding bar, otherwise to negative range if lower decreased, otherwise to the source's one-bar change. Ultimate RSI is 50 + 50 × smoothing(change) / smoothing(abs(change)), using the lookback as smoothing length; its signal is a separately smoothed Ultimate RSI. Use fast, slow, and very slow lookbacks in increasing order; smoothing methods are selectable EMA, SMA, RMA, or double SMA.
Calculation: Signed deviation = sign of the one-bar change in SMA(close) × population standard deviation of close over the same lookback; its signal is an EMA of signed deviation. Use separate shorter and longer lookbacks and signal lengths.
Calculation: Modified Awesome Oscillator = shorter EMA of (high + low) / 2 minus longer EMA of (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Stop level = select a long and short candidate pair: close × (1 − percentage) and close × (1 + percentage); rolling lowest low and rolling highest high; rolling highest EMA(close) and rolling lowest of 2 × close − EMA(close); or close − ATR × multiplier and close + ATR × multiplier. For each side independently on every bar, let previous be the preceding bar's recursively updated candidate, replacing an unavailable previous value with zero. Update the long candidate to the current candidate if it exceeds previous OR previous close is below previous; otherwise use previous. Update the short candidate to the current candidate if it is below previous OR previous close is above previous; otherwise use previous. The long stop is the maximum of its updated candidate and previous; the short stop is the minimum of its updated candidate and previous. Calculate these series continuously, including outside positions.
Long: On a completed chart bar, datetime is at or after the configured start AND long trading is enabled AND (holding long entries until flat is disabled OR position is flat) AND the selected long trend filter passes AND any enabled long trigger passes. The long trend filter passes if its selection is none, OR close > the long trend MA, OR shorter-lookback signed deviation > its signal, OR very slow Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal, OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI, respectively for the selected filter. Long triggers are: close > main MA AND main MA > its value a configurable number of bars ago; OR fast or slow Ultimate RSI crosses above its own signal AND that RSI > its lower limit AND < its upper limit AND very slow Ultimate RSI < its upper limit; OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI AND slow Ultimate RSI > its lower limit AND < its upper limit AND very slow Ultimate RSI > its lower limit; OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI AND fast and slow Ultimate RSI each > their lower limits AND very slow Ultimate RSI > its lower limit AND < its upper limit; OR very slow Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal AND > its lower limit AND < its upper limit; OR modified Awesome Oscillator crosses above zero; OR shorter- or longer-lookback signed deviation crosses above its own signal. Main and trend MAs have independently selectable source, length, and SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, VWMA, or HMA type.
Short: On a completed chart bar, datetime is at or after the configured start AND short trading is enabled AND (holding short entries until flat is disabled OR position is flat) AND the selected short trend filter passes when the long trend filter selection is not none AND any enabled short trigger passes. The short trend filter passes if its selection is none, OR close < the short trend MA, OR shorter-lookback signed deviation < its signal, OR very slow Ultimate RSI crosses below its signal, respectively for the selected filter; the fast-versus-slow selection passes when the long filter selection is fast-versus-slow AND fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI. Short triggers are: close < main MA AND main MA < its value a configurable number of bars ago; OR fast or slow Ultimate RSI crosses below its own signal AND that RSI < its upper limit AND > its lower limit AND very slow Ultimate RSI > its lower limit; OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI AND slow Ultimate RSI < its lower limit AND > its upper limit AND very slow Ultimate RSI < its upper limit; OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI AND fast and slow Ultimate RSI each < their upper limits AND very slow Ultimate RSI < its upper limit AND > its lower limit; OR very slow Ultimate RSI crosses below its signal AND < its upper limit AND > its lower limit; OR modified Awesome Oscillator crosses below zero; OR shorter- or longer-lookback signed deviation crosses below its own signal; OR close crosses below a shorter or longer EMA of close.
Long: Close > any of the long trend MA, short trend MA, main MA, shorter EMA of close, or longer EMA of close; OR any of those averages > its preceding value; OR modified Awesome Oscillator > its preceding value OR > zero; OR fast, slow, or very slow Ultimate RSI > its own signal OR > its midpoint; OR shorter- or longer-lookback signed deviation > its own signal OR > zero.
Short: Close < any of the long trend MA, short trend MA, main MA, shorter EMA of close, or longer EMA of close; OR any of those averages < its preceding value; OR modified Awesome Oscillator < its preceding value OR < zero; OR fast, slow, or very slow Ultimate RSI < its own signal OR < its midpoint; OR shorter- or longer-lookback signed deviation < its own signal OR < zero.
Exit: long, on a completed chart bar, datetime is at or after the configured start AND low < long stop level.
Exit: short, on a completed chart bar, datetime is at or after the configured start AND high > short stop level.
Exit: long OR short, on a completed chart bar, take profit is enabled AND the selected short trend filter has the corresponding condition: close > short trend MA, OR shorter-lookback signed deviation > its signal, OR very slow Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal, OR, when the long trend filter selection is fast-versus-slow, fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI. The none selection has no take-profit condition.
Exit: long, an enabled short entry condition is met AND holding short entries until flat is disabled.
Exit: short, an enabled long entry condition is met AND holding long entries until flat is disabled.
