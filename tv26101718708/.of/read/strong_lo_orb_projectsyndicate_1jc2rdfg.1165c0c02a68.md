Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range window in the configured timezone; freeze both when the window ends. Range width = highest high minus lowest low; range midpoint = their average; session open = open of the first bar in the window. Reset the entry count to zero at each opening-range start.
Calculation: Average daily range = SMA of daily high minus low, using the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar; capture it at the opening-range start. Range percentage = opening-range width divided by captured average daily range; it is unavailable when the captured average is not positive.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Directional strength score = clamp the rounded sum of the following components to the score bounds. For each component, use configurable thresholds and points: candle body fraction abs(close - open) / (high - low), with a fallback when the range is zero, scored in descending threshold tiers; candle range / ATR, with zero when ATR is not positive, scored in descending tiers; volume / volume SMA, with a fallback when the SMA is not positive, scored in descending tiers; directional momentum scored differently when both, exactly one, or neither of close minus close at a shorter and a longer lookback agree with the direction; directional penetration beyond the opening-range edge / ATR, with zero when ATR is not positive, scored in descending tiers; RSI beyond a direction-specific threshold; higher-timeframe alignment passing, including when its filter is disabled; close beyond the session open in the direction of the trade; entry count equal to zero; and opening-range width / captured average daily range within an inclusive interval, using zero when the average is not positive. Round the sum to a configurable precision before clamping.
Calculation: Entry risk = min(max(abs(entry price - raw stop), minimum ATR multiple), maximum ATR multiple), using ATR at entry. Select raw stop as entry price minus the ATR stop distance for a long or plus it for a short, opening-range midpoint minus the ATR buffer for a long or plus it for a short, or opening-range low minus the ATR buffer for a long or opening-range high plus it for a short. Fix risk at entry.
Long: Opening range is formed AND current bar opens within the trade window in the configured timezone AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high AND (break buffer disabled OR close - opening-range high >= buffer ATR multiple) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe trend) AND (range filter disabled OR range percentage >= minimum AND range percentage <= maximum) AND directional strength score >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR directional strength score >= strong threshold) AND entry count < maximum entries per opening range AND no position is active AND no position exited on this bar AND ATR is available.
Short: Opening range is formed AND current bar opens within the trade window in the configured timezone AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low AND (break buffer disabled OR opening-range low - close >= buffer ATR multiple) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe trend) AND (range filter disabled OR range percentage >= minimum AND range percentage <= maximum) AND directional strength score >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR directional strength score >= strong threshold) AND entry count < maximum entries per opening range AND no position is active AND no position exited on this bar AND ATR is available.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= entry price - entry risk OR high >= entry price + entry risk × final target multiple OR previous bar opened within the trade window AND current bar opens outside it).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= entry price + entry risk OR low <= entry price - entry risk × final target multiple OR previous bar opened within the trade window AND current bar opens outside it).
