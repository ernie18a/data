Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during the configurable opening window; freeze these levels when the window ends.
Calculation: Prior-day ADR = average high-minus-low over a configurable lookback of completed daily bars (higher timeframe than the chart timeframe).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a configurable higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Directional breakout strength score = add 1.5/1.1/0.6/0.2 for body-to-range ratio >=0.85/0.70/0.55/otherwise; add 1.5/1.0/0.6/0.2 for candle-range-to-ATR ratio >=2.0/1.4/1.0/otherwise; add 1.5/1.0/0.5/0 for volume-to-20-bar-average ratio >=2.0/1.5/1.2/otherwise; add 1.5 if both 3-bar and 5-bar close momentum agree with the direction, 0.7 if one agrees, or -0.4 if neither; add 1.0/0.6/0.3/0 for breakout penetration in ATRs >=0.5/0.25/0.1/otherwise; add 0.5 if RSI(14)>52 for long or <48 for short; add 1.0 if price is on the directionally aligned side of the higher-timeframe EMA; add 0.5 if close is above/below the opening-window first bar's open for long/short; add 1.0 for the first trade of the day; add 0.5 if opening-range width / prior-day ADR is between 0.12 and 0.45. Round to 0.1 and clamp to 0–10.
Calculation: Stop and target levels = entry is signal-bar close; derive the raw stop from the opposite opening-range edge plus/minus an ATR buffer, the opening-range midpoint plus/minus an ATR buffer, or a configurable ATR distance; clamp entry-to-stop distance between configurable ATR floor and cap. Targets are entry plus/minus that risk distance multiplied by configurable target R multiples, for the position direction.
Long: Opening range is complete AND current time is inside the configurable trade window AND close > opening-range high AND prior close <= opening-range high AND (break buffer disabled OR close - opening-range high >= configurable ATR fraction) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range width / prior-day ADR is within its configurable minimum and maximum) AND directional breakout strength score >= configurable minimum (and, when enabled, >= configurable strong threshold) AND daily trade count < configurable maximum AND no position is active AND no exit occurred on this bar AND ATR is available.
Short: Opening range is complete AND current time is inside the configurable trade window AND close < opening-range low AND prior close >= opening-range low AND (break buffer disabled OR opening-range low - close >= configurable ATR fraction) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range width / prior-day ADR is within its configurable minimum and maximum) AND directional breakout strength score >= configurable minimum (and, when enabled, >= configurable strong threshold) AND daily trade count < configurable maximum AND no position is active AND no exit occurred on this bar AND ATR is available AND no long signal occurs on this bar.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= third target OR trade window ends; if stop and a target are touched on the same bar, treat the stop as the exit, otherwise a stop touch after the first or second target was previously reached exits at the highest reached target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= third target OR trade window ends; if stop and a target are touched on the same bar, treat the stop as the exit, otherwise a stop touch after the first or second target was previously reached exits at the highest reached target.
