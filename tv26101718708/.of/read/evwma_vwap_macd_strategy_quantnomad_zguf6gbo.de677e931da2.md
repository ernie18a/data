Calculation: EVWMA = ((sum of volume over the lookback − current volume) × previous EVWMA + current volume × close) ÷ sum of volume over the lookback; use close if no previous EVWMA exists
Calculation: MACD = shorter-lookback EMA of (VWAP − EVWMA)
Calculation: Signal = longer-lookback EMA of (VWAP − EVWMA)
Long: MACD crosses above Signal
Short: MACD crosses below Signal
