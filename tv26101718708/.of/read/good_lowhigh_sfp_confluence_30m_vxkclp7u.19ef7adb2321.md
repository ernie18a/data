Calculation: Tracked low and low count = On each confirmed pivot low with equal left and right lookbacks, replace the tracked low with the pivot price, set the count to 1, and clear its good and bad flags. On each later bar without a newly confirmed pivot low, while the good flag is clear, increment the count if low <= tracked low + ATR multiplied by a tolerance parameter. Set the good flag when the good-low condition occurs and the bad flag when the bad-low condition occurs; a bad flag does not prevent a later good-low condition.
Calculation: Tracked high and high count = On each confirmed pivot high with equal left and right lookbacks, replace the tracked high with the pivot price, set the count to 1, and clear its good and bad flags. On each later bar without a newly confirmed pivot high, while the good flag is clear, increment the count if high >= tracked high - ATR multiplied by a tolerance parameter. Set the good flag when the good-high condition occurs and the bad flag when the bad-high condition occurs; a bad flag does not prevent a later good-high condition.
