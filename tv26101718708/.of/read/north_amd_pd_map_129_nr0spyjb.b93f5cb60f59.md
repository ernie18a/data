Calculation: Zone bounds = the candle high and low when wick mode is selected; otherwise the larger and smaller of its open and close.
Calculation: Pending bearish block = on a source bar whose high exceeds the highest high of a preceding lookback, store that bar if it is bullish; otherwise store the most recent bullish bar within a separate lookback, or the sweep bar if none exists. Replace the pending block on every subsequent high sweep.
Calculation: Pending bullish block = on a source bar whose low is below the lowest low of a preceding lookback, store that bar if it is bearish; otherwise store the most recent bearish bar within a separate lookback, or the sweep bar if none exists. Replace the pending block on every subsequent low sweep.
Calculation: Source bars = chart bars or bars from a higher or lower timeframe. The sweep-based block calculation uses completed source bars. The other block calculation uses the previous observed source bar. The gap calculation uses the newly observed source bar and two earlier observed source bars; higher-timeframe values are last completed bars in historical data and can be still forming in real time, while lower-timeframe requests supply only the last available lower-timeframe bar per chart bar.
Long: Source-bar low exceeds the high two source bars earlier AND the gap is positive AND its size is at least the minimum gap size.
Short: Source-bar high is below the low two source bars earlier AND the gap is positive AND its size is at least the minimum gap size.
Long: Chart close exceeds the upper boundary of a previously created valid bearish gap AND the gap has not already flipped; validity requires the middle source bar's high minus low to be at least the minimum displacement.
Short: Chart close is below the lower boundary of a previously created valid bullish gap AND the gap has not already flipped; validity requires the middle source bar's high minus low to be at least the minimum displacement.
Long: Completed source-bar close is above its open AND above the upper bound of the pending bullish block AND the block has positive height at least the minimum block size AND that block candle has not already confirmed; clear the pending block after confirmation.
Short: Completed source-bar close is below its open AND below the lower bound of the pending bearish block AND the block has positive height at least the minimum block size AND that block candle has not already confirmed; clear the pending block after confirmation.
Long: Previous observed source-bar close is above its open AND above the upper bound of the most recent bearish observed source candle AND that candle's bounds have positive height at least the minimum block size AND that candle has not already confirmed in this calculation.
Short: Previous observed source-bar close is below its open AND below the lower bound of the most recent bullish observed source candle AND that candle's bounds have positive height at least the minimum block size AND that candle has not already confirmed in this calculation.
