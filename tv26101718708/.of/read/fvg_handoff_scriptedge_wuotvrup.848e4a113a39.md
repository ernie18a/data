Calculation: Higher-timeframe setup = On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe candle, use the three immediately preceding completed higher-timeframe candles, ordered first to third. A bullish setup has third low > first high AND second close > second open; its near edge is third low, far edge is first high, and target is the greater of second and third highs. A bearish setup has third high < first low AND second close < second open; its near edge is third high, far edge is first low, and target is the lesser of second and third lows. The target bar is the chart bar making the target extreme, choosing the third candle when the extremes are equal. The setup expires at the next higher-timeframe boundary.
Calculation: Active setup = On confirmed chart bars during the forming higher-timeframe candle immediately following the setup candles, a bullish setup becomes active when low <= near edge; a bearish setup becomes active when high >= near edge. Before activation, discard the setup if its target is reached, including on the same chart bar as the first touch. Once active, it remains active until expiry, even if the target is subsequently reached.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe gap = A bullish gap has current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open; its lower edge is the high two bars ago. A bearish gap has current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open; its upper edge is the low two bars ago. Keep the latest gap of each direction, replacing the previous gap of that direction. After evaluating confirmations on each bar, discard a bullish gap if close < its lower edge and a bearish gap if close > its upper edge.
Calculation: Swings = Confirm a swing high or low using the immediately preceding and following chart bars. Maintain alternating swings; replace consecutive swings of the same type only when the new one is more extreme. If a bar is both a swing high and low, add the type opposite the last swing first, then the other type; if there is no last swing, add the low first.
Long: The three latest completed higher-timeframe candles form a bullish setup.
Short: The three latest completed higher-timeframe candles form a bearish setup.
Long: A bullish setup becomes active on the current confirmed chart bar.
Short: A bearish setup becomes active on the current confirmed chart bar.
Long: A bullish setup is active AND a bullish chart-timeframe gap forms AND no bullish gap confirmation has previously fired for this setup.
Short: A bearish setup is active AND a bearish chart-timeframe gap forms AND no bearish gap confirmation has previously fired for this setup.
Long: A bullish setup is active AND the latest retained bearish chart-timeframe gap formed on or after the setup target bar AND close > that gap's upper edge AND no inverted-gap confirmation has previously fired for this setup.
Short: A bearish setup is active AND the latest retained bullish chart-timeframe gap formed on or after the setup target bar AND close < that gap's lower edge AND no inverted-gap confirmation has previously fired for this setup.
Long: A bullish setup is active AND the latest three confirmed swings are low, high, low AND the middle swing high < target AND a chart bar from the later of the third swing bar and activation bar through the current bar has close > the middle swing high AND no structure-shift confirmation has previously fired for this setup.
Short: A bearish setup is active AND the latest three confirmed swings are high, low, high AND the middle swing low > target AND a chart bar from the later of the third swing bar and activation bar through the current bar has close < the middle swing low AND no structure-shift confirmation has previously fired for this setup.
