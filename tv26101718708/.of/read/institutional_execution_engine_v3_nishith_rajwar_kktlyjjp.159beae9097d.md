Calculation: Efficiency = absolute change in close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; use zero when the sum is zero or insufficient bars are available.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume since the configured session reset; use typical price when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Pivot zone = a confirmed pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side. Confirm a supply zone when the pivot high minus the lowest low over the pivot-confirmation lookback exceeds current ATR; its upper boundary is the pivot high and its lower boundary is the pivot high minus an ATR multiple. Confirm a demand zone when the highest high over that lookback minus the pivot low exceeds current ATR; its lower boundary is the pivot low and its upper boundary is the pivot low plus an ATR multiple. Fix both boundaries at confirmation.
Calculation: Zone score = the sum of tiered points for pivot-bar volume divided by its volume SMA, absolute distance between the pivot price and pivot-bar session VWAP divided by confirmation-bar ATR, confirmation-bar efficiency using the pivot lookback, and zone age; add sweep points when a supply pivot high is at least the highest high over a separate lookback ending before the pivot bar, or a demand pivot low is at most the lowest low over that lookback. For volume, VWAP distance and efficiency, assign more points above the higher threshold, fewer above the lower threshold and none otherwise; for age, assign more points below the shorter threshold, fewer below the longer threshold and none otherwise. At confirmation use age zero. Thereafter use bars since the pivot bar as age. If decay is enabled, multiply the score by the greater of a decay floor and exp(-bars since last touch / decay parameter); initialize last touch to the pivot bar and update it after scoring each zone interaction.
Calculation: Eligible zone = a zone whose confirmation score meets the minimum score. Recalculate its score on each bar; deactivate it when its score falls below a separate minimum, its age exceeds a maximum, a supply-zone close exceeds its upper boundary, or a demand-zone close falls below its lower boundary. On the bar when the score or age deactivates a zone, still evaluate its interaction. A supply interaction requires high above the lower boundary and close below the upper boundary; a demand interaction requires low below the upper boundary and close above the lower boundary. Among interacting zones, select the highest-scoring zone, breaking ties by creation order.
Calculation: ATR percentile = current ATR's position between the lowest and highest ATR over a lookback, scaled to a percentage; use the midpoint when the extremes are equal.
Calculation: Session profit = realized profit from trades closed since the configured session reset, using the strategy's equity-based position sizing.
Calculation: Entry stop and target = for a long, the selected demand zone's lower boundary minus an entry-bar ATR multiple and entry price plus a risk multiple of entry price minus that stop; for a short, the selected supply zone's upper boundary plus an entry-bar ATR multiple and entry price minus a risk multiple of that stop minus entry price. Fix both levels at entry.
Long: A demand pivot zone is confirmed AND its confirmation score meets the minimum score.
Short: A supply pivot zone is confirmed AND its confirmation score meets the minimum score.
Long: The selected eligible zone is demand AND its score meets the minimum score AND ATR percentile is below the lower regime threshold OR above the upper regime threshold AND the bar is in the configured entry session AND session profit is at least the loss limit AND position side is flat AND close is above open AND (zone upper boundary minus low) divided by zone depth is below the penetration limit AND close is above the entry stop.
Short: The selected eligible zone is supply AND its score meets the minimum score AND ATR percentile is below the lower regime threshold OR above the upper regime threshold AND the bar is in the configured entry session AND session profit is at least the loss limit AND position side is flat AND close is below open AND (high minus zone lower boundary) divided by zone depth is below the penetration limit AND close is below the entry stop.
Exit: long, low is at or below the entry stop OR high is at or above the entry target.
Exit: short, high is at or above the entry stop OR low is at or below the entry target.
