Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low, confirmed only after the configured number of right-side bars; use the latest confirmed pivot on the chart timeframe or higher timeframe, as applicable.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure bias = direction of the most recent completed higher-timeframe close crossing above its latest confirmed swing high or below its latest confirmed swing low; neutral if neither has occurred.
Calculation: Structural origin = the latest confirmed opposite swing whose pivot bar is after the broken swing's pivot bar and before the current bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that swing high AND that swing has not already been broken AND a structural-origin swing low exists below the current close AND higher-timeframe structure bias is bullish AND (structure-only bias mode OR (structure-plus-EMA bias mode AND completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND that EMA > its prior completed-bar value)).
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that swing low AND that swing has not already been broken AND a structural-origin swing high exists above the current close AND higher-timeframe structure bias is bearish AND (structure-only bias mode OR (structure-plus-EMA bias mode AND completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND that EMA < its prior completed-bar value)).
