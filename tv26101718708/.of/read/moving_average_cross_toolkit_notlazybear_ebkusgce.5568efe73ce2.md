Calculation: Moving averages = independently configured moving averages of chart-bar price sources; the ordered averages need not have ordered lengths.
Calculation: Cross quality = for each adjacent moving-average pair, track every raw cross independently of the filter. A cycle starts at the cross bar's close and ends at the next opposite raw cross, including both bars' highs and lows. Up-cycle net = (cycle high − start close − drawdown weight × (start close − cycle low)) / start close. Down-cycle net = (start close − cycle low − drawdown weight × (cycle high − start close)) / start close. At a new cross, score the latest completed same-direction cycle as high, medium, or zero according as its net is above a positive cutoff, above zero, or otherwise; score the latest completed opposite-direction cycle as high, medium, or zero according as its net is below the negative cutoff, below zero, or otherwise. Use a configurable medium or zero score for a missing cycle. Cross quality is the mean of these two scores.
Calculation: Bullish trend = in hierarchy mode, the first average is above the second and each available subsequent average is below its preceding average; in custom-pairs mode, every enabled pair with available averages satisfies its configured greater-than or less-than comparison; in weighted mode, the sum of pair scores is at least the positive threshold, where each enabled pair with available averages contributes a positive point if its first average is above its second and a negative point otherwise, and other pairs contribute zero.
Calculation: Bearish trend = in hierarchy mode, the first average is below the second and each available subsequent average is above its preceding average; in custom-pairs mode, every enabled pair with available averages satisfies the reverse of its configured comparison; in weighted mode, the same pair-score sum is at most the negative threshold.
Long: For any adjacent pair, the first moving average is above the second.
Short: For any adjacent pair, the first moving average is at or below the second.
Long: For any adjacent pair, the first moving average crosses above the second AND (the cross-quality filter is disabled OR cross quality is at least its threshold).
Short: For any adjacent pair, the first moving average crosses below the second AND (the cross-quality filter is disabled OR cross quality is at least its threshold).
Long: Bullish trend is true.
Short: Bearish trend is true AND bullish trend is false.
Exit: long, the first moving average crosses below the second with the cross-quality filter satisfied OR bullish trend is false.
