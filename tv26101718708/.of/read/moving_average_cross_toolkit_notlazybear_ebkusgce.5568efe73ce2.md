Calculation: Moving average = the configured moving average of a configured price source, using its configured lookback; compare all configured adjacent lines on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Prior-cycle net result = (favorable price move from the cross-bar close to the cycle extreme − drawdown from that close × drawdown weight) / cross-bar close; for an upward cycle use the high as the favorable extreme and low as drawdown, and for a downward cycle use the low as favorable extreme and high as drawdown.
Calculation: Cross quality score = the average of (a) a score for the most recently completed cycle in the cross direction and (b) a score for the most recently completed opposite-direction cycle, where larger positive net results score higher and negative net results score higher for the opposite-direction cycle; use the configured allowance when either cycle history is unavailable.
Long: Any configured adjacent moving-average pair crosses upward (current faster line > slower line AND previous faster line ≤ slower line) AND (smart cross filter is disabled OR cross quality score ≥ configured minimum quality) OR bullish trend state; bullish trend state means, in strict hierarchy mode, each enabled line is above the next; in custom-pairs mode, every enabled pair satisfies its configured comparison; or, in weighted-score mode, the sum of enabled pair signs (faster > slower: +1, otherwise −1) ≥ configured threshold.
Short: Any configured adjacent moving-average pair crosses downward (current faster line < slower line AND previous faster line ≥ slower line) AND (smart cross filter is disabled OR cross quality score ≥ configured minimum quality) OR bearish trend state; bearish trend state means, in strict hierarchy mode, each enabled line is below the next; in custom-pairs mode, every enabled pair satisfies the inverse of its configured comparison; or, in weighted-score mode, the sum of enabled pair signs ≤ −configured threshold.
Exit: long, any configured adjacent moving-average pair crosses downward OR bullish trend state is false.
