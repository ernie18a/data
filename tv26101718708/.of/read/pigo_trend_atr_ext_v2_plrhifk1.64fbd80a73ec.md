Calculation: Impulse oscillator = zero-lag EMA of typical price minus the smoothed moving average of high when the zero-lag EMA is above that average, minus the smoothed moving average of low when it is below that average, and zero otherwise. The zero-lag EMA is twice the EMA of typical price minus the EMA of that EMA; all three averages use the same lookback.
Calculation: Impulse histogram = impulse oscillator minus its shorter-lookback SMA.
Long: (Current primary EMA of close > previous primary EMA of close when using the single-EMA method OR current primary EMA of close > current secondary EMA of close when using the two-EMA method) AND (flat detection is disabled OR (current impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)). Apply on the chart timeframe or on a selected higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar for historical decisions; on a realtime higher timeframe, use the bar still forming.
Short: (Current primary EMA of close < previous primary EMA of close when using the single-EMA method OR current primary EMA of close < current secondary EMA of close when using the two-EMA method) AND (flat detection is disabled OR (current impulse histogram != 0 AND previous impulse histogram != 0)). Apply on the chart timeframe or on a selected higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar for historical decisions; on a realtime higher timeframe, use the bar still forming.
