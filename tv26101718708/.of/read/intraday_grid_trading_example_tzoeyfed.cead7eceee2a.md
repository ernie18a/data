Calculation: Session reference price = close of the most recent earlier bar in the selected session
Calculation: Seven grid levels = the session reference price and three configured percentage offsets below it and three above it; the offsets increase in size from the first to the third
Long: Close crosses above any of the seven grid levels
Short: Close crosses below any of the seven grid levels
Exit: Long, close crosses below any of the seven grid levels
Exit: Short, close crosses above any of the seven grid levels
