Calculation: Directional energy = the smoothed, signed motion of the selected price series, optionally multiplied by relative volume or true-range effort, normalized by recent absolute raw motion, then scaled by a quality factor. Motion is displacement over a lookback or linear-regression slope over a lookback, divided by a volatility baseline (the greater of standard deviation of price changes and a floor-scaled average absolute price change); displacement and regression modes use their corresponding length adjustments. Quality is a blend of squared price/time correlation and efficiency ratio over the active motion length, optionally normalized against its recent average; below the quality gate it is zero. Apply the quality weight and smoothing, then smooth energy.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net price change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute price changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Short-term direction = current Hull moving average of the selected price series minus its prior-bar value.
Long: (energy crosses above the positive signal threshold AND quality is at least its gate AND energy is below its positive saturation level AND long is armed AND the previous signal was not long AND (short-term direction is positive OR short-term direction becomes positive within 3 bars while energy remains above the signal threshold and quality and saturation conditions still hold)) OR (at a confirmed price low, price is lower than at the prior confirmed price low, energy is greater than at that low and greater than a positive minimum, quality meets the pivot quality threshold if required, and the two lows are no more than 8 pivot lookbacks apart). Long rearms when energy is at or below the negative rearm threshold; a long signal disarms it.
Short: (energy crosses below the negative signal threshold AND quality is at least its gate AND energy is above its negative saturation level AND short is armed AND the previous signal was not short AND (short-term direction is negative OR short-term direction becomes negative within 3 bars while energy remains below the negative signal threshold and quality and saturation conditions still hold)) OR (at a confirmed price high, price is higher than at the prior confirmed price high, energy is lower than at that high and greater than a positive minimum, quality meets the pivot quality threshold if required, and the two highs are no more than 8 pivot lookbacks apart). Short rearms when energy is at or above the positive rearm threshold; a short signal disarms it.
