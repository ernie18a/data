Calculation: Source = a selected price series derived from the chart bar's OHLC fields.
Calculation: Velocity = either (source minus source at the motion lookback) divided by (volatility baseline times square root of the motion lookback), or the linear-regression slope of source divided by (volatility baseline divided by square root of the regression lookback). The volatility baseline is the greater of the standard deviation of source changes and a positive floor multiplier times the SMA of absolute source changes, both over the volatility lookback. Use zero if the denominator is not positive. Smooth velocity with an EMA.
Calculation: Effort multiplier = 1 if effort is disabled; otherwise max(minimum multiplier, 1 + effort weight times (normalized effort minus 1)). Normalized effort is the lesser of the effort cap and current effort divided by its SMA over the longer reference lookback, or 1 if that SMA is not positive. Effort is selected as volume, true range, or volume when positive and available and true range otherwise.
Calculation: Selected quality = clamp to [0,1] of R² raised to the quality mix times the Kaufman efficiency ratio raised to (1 minus the quality mix), using the motion lookback for displacement mode or the regression lookback for slope mode. Treat undefined correlation as zero. In normalized mode, instead use clamp to [0,1] of this quality blend divided by the greater of its SMA over the volatility lookback and a positive denominator floor, then multiplied by a normalization center.
Calculation: Energy = RMA of core energy times quality factor. Core energy is signed absolute smoothed velocity raised to a power, multiplied by the effort multiplier, then divided by the greater of its absolute-value SMA over the volatility lookback and a floor multiplier times its absolute-value SMA over the longer reference lookback; leave it undivided if that greater value is not positive. Quality factor is 1 when quality filtering is disabled; otherwise it is an EMA of 1 minus quality weight times (1 minus selected quality raised to a power), using zero for selected quality when it is below the quality gate.
Calculation: Signal state = both directions start armed and the last signal direction starts neutral. Before evaluating each bar, arm long when energy is at or below the negative rearm threshold and arm short when energy is at or above the positive rearm threshold. A candidate requires its direction to be armed, the last signal not to have been in that direction, selected quality at or above the signal quality threshold, and energy inside the corresponding saturation limit. A long candidate occurs when energy crosses above the positive signal threshold; a short candidate occurs when energy crosses below the negative signal threshold. If the candidate's source HMA slope has the required sign, signal immediately; otherwise start or reset that direction's retry counter. While the counter is positive, signal if the same armed, alternation, quality and saturation conditions hold, energy remains beyond the corresponding signal threshold, and the HMA slope has the required sign. Decrement positive counters after evaluating retries, including on the candidate bar. On a signal, disarm its direction, clear its counter, and record its direction as the last signal.
Calculation: Qualifying price pivots = confirmed close pivots with equal left and right lookbacks; evaluate them on the confirmation bar. A high pivot qualifies when energy at the pivot is above a positive divergence threshold and, if the pivot quality filter is enabled, selected quality at the pivot is at or above its threshold. A low pivot qualifies when energy at the pivot is below the negative divergence threshold and passes the same optional quality filter. Store every qualifying pivot and its energy, even when it does not produce divergence; compare each qualifying pivot only with the preceding qualifying pivot of the same type.
Long: The long signal state produces a signal.
Short: The short signal state produces a signal.
Long: A qualifying low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding qualifying low pivot is at most the maximum divergence gap AND its close is lower AND its energy is higher.
Short: A qualifying high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding qualifying high pivot is at most the maximum divergence gap AND its close is higher AND its energy is lower.
Long: Energy is greater than the dead-zone threshold.
Short: Energy is less than the negative dead-zone threshold.
