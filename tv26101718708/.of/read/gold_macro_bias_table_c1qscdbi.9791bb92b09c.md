Calculation: Estimated bar delta = 0 if high = low; otherwise volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Session delta = sum of estimated bar delta over chart bars since the current trading day began.
Calculation: Average session delta = arithmetic mean of session deltas from up to the configured lookback of prior completed trading days, subject to the history limit.
Long: Combined TradingView Technical Rating >= positive rating threshold OR session delta > 0 OR average session delta > 0 OR estimated bar delta > 0 on any configured higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar OR simple moving average of estimated bar delta > 0 on any configured higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar OR previous close != 0 AND close > previous close on the configured higher or lower price-alert timeframe using its last completed bar.
Short: Combined TradingView Technical Rating <= negative rating threshold OR session delta < 0 OR average session delta < 0 OR estimated bar delta < 0 on any configured higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar OR simple moving average of estimated bar delta < 0 on any configured higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar OR previous close != 0 AND close < previous close on the configured higher or lower price-alert timeframe using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Combined TradingView Technical Rating = the standard combined moving-average and oscillator technical rating, calculated on the configured higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar.
