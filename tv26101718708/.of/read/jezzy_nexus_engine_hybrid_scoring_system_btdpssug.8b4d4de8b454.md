Calculation: Session eligibility = the chart timeframe equals the session-exempt timeframe OR the bar's UTC opening time is within either enabled session, including its opening time and excluding its closing time.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low minus high two bars ago is at least ATR times the minimum gap multiplier AND close is above the lowest low of the preceding swing lookback. Its top is current low and its bottom is high two bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish gap = low two bars ago minus current high is at least ATR times the minimum gap multiplier AND close is below the highest high of the preceding swing lookback. Its top is low two bars ago and its bottom is current high.
Calculation: Inverse gaps = retain bullish and bearish gaps in separate oldest-first queues, each limited to a gap capacity. Move a bullish gap to the bearish inverse-gap queue when close falls below its midpoint; move a bearish gap to the bullish inverse-gap queue when close rises above its midpoint. Each inverse-gap queue has a separate capacity; discard its oldest item when exceeded. Remove a bearish inverse gap when close is above its top and a bullish inverse gap when close is below its bottom. After these updates, an inverse-gap touch exists for a side if any retained inverse gap for that side overlaps the high-low range of any bar in the touch lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of one point for each of: MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD line > signal line; at least a required count of slow, moderate and fast Parabolic SAR values < close; every close in the EMA confirmation lookback > its corresponding short EMA AND short EMA > long EMA; chart timeframe > the trend-check cutoff OR the short EMA > long EMA on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar; bullish gap exists; bullish inverse-gap touch exists; close > linear regression of lows AND that regression > its previous value. The higher-timeframe EMAs use the same respective lengths as the chart EMAs.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of one point for each of: MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD line < signal line; at least a required count of slow, moderate and fast Parabolic SAR values > close; every close in the EMA confirmation lookback < its corresponding short EMA AND short EMA < long EMA; chart timeframe > the trend-check cutoff OR the short EMA < long EMA on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar; bearish gap exists; bearish inverse-gap touch exists; close < linear regression of highs AND that regression < its previous value. The higher-timeframe EMAs use the same respective lengths as the chart EMAs.
Calculation: Lower rejection = min(open, close) - low >= absolute(close - open) times the wick-to-body multiplier AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR times the minimum-body multiplier AND min(open, close) - low >= ATR times the minimum-wick multiplier.
Calculation: Upper rejection = high - max(open, close) >= absolute(close - open) times the wick-to-body multiplier AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR times the minimum-body multiplier AND high - max(open, close) >= ATR times the minimum-wick multiplier.
Calculation: Opposing engulfing count = number of bars in the engulfing lookback, including the current bar, whose body strictly engulfs the preceding bar's oppositely directed body and whose body size is >= current-bar ATR times the engulfing multiplier; bullish and bearish counts are calculated separately.
Long: Session eligibility AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < upper threshold) AND volume >= its simple moving average times the volume multiplier AND (chart timeframe = session-exempt timeframe OR bearish engulfing count = 0) AND (lower rejection OR bullish gap exists) AND bullish score >= minimum score AND (no previous scored long signal OR bars since the previous scored long signal >= cooldown).
Short: Session eligibility AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > lower threshold) AND volume >= its simple moving average times the volume multiplier AND (chart timeframe = session-exempt timeframe OR bullish engulfing count = 0) AND (upper rejection OR bearish gap exists) AND bearish score >= minimum score AND (no previous scored short signal OR bars since the previous scored short signal >= cooldown).
Long: Session eligibility AND short EMA > long EMA AND previous low <= previous short EMA AND previous close > previous short EMA AND close > open.
Short: Session eligibility AND short EMA < long EMA AND previous high >= previous short EMA AND previous close < previous short EMA AND close < open.
