Calculation: P = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: R1 = 2 × P − low of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: R2 = P + (high − low) of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: S1 = 2 × P − high of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: S2 = P − (high − low) of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Long: close crosses above R1 OR close crosses above R2
Short: close crosses below S1 OR close crosses below S2
