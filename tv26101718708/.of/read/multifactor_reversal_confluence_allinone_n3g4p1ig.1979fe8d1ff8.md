Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Distribution-shift percentile = percentile rank of the mean absolute gap between the sorted recent log returns and the sorted preceding, equally sized log-return window, divided by the recent-window return standard deviation.
Calculation: Reversal drift = EMA of log returns; the long reversal direction opposes positive drift and the short reversal direction opposes negative drift.
Calculation: Changepoint strength and direction = Student-t Bayesian online changepoint probability crossing its threshold, or a mean CUSUM crossing its threshold, or a variance CUSUM crossing its threshold; direction opposes the slow reversal drift unless the shorter drift has the same sign and exceeds the configured multiple of the slow drift, in which case there is no direction.
Calculation: Exhaustion = a slow efficiency ratio above its floor, a fast efficiency ratio below its value three bars earlier, and the percentile rank of absolute close displacement over the configured lookback divided by ATR above its threshold. Upward displacement indicates a short reversal; downward displacement indicates a long reversal.
Calculation: Climax = volume z-score and high-low range z-score above their thresholds, with the close-location value beyond the rejection threshold in the reversal direction, and the composite above its adaptive upper quantile. A long climax is confirmed by a later retest near the climax low, with lower volume, that does not breach the invalidation level; a short climax uses the mirrored high retest.
Calculation: Structure confirmation = sweep below the latest confirmed swing low followed within the configured window by a close above the latest confirmed swing high with one-bar displacement above its ATR threshold (long); or sweep above the latest confirmed swing high followed within the window by a close below the latest confirmed swing low with displacement above the threshold (short). Swing pivots are confirmed after the configured number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Mean-reversion gate = the average of the positive part of the negative rolling correlation between prior close deviation from its rolling mean and the next log return, and a score that is positive only when the rolling variance ratio is below 1. If enabled, the Dickey-Fuller statistic must also be at or below its critical value.
Calculation: Turbulence gate = 1 minus the configured veto strength times the excess of the Fano-based burst score over its threshold, divided by the remaining range to 1; apply this reduction only when the burst score is above threshold and is not falling.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe signed flow = (volume on lower-timeframe bars closing above their open minus volume on bars closing below their open) divided by the sum of those volumes; use lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Ride trend direction = the selected basis: fast EMA or DEMA above a non-falling slow EMA or DEMA (long), mirrored for short; or ordered, fanning Williams Alligator lines; or chart-timeframe DEMA above (long) or below (short) at least the configured number of higher-timeframe DEMAs, including the nearest higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Ride pullback = price retraces from the recent high (long) or low (short) by at least the configured ATR multiple. Ride resumption = close crosses back above (long) or below (short) the fast trend average, or closes above the prior high and above its open (long) / below the prior low and below its open (short), according to the selected timing mode; the cross-on-trend-change mode instead triggers when ride trend direction changes to that side.
Long: Reversal candidate is long and reaches the configured actionable lifecycle tier (ARMED, EXTREME after a confirmed long climax, or CONFIRMED after long structure confirmation); at least the configured minimum number of the five directional reversal strengths meet their respective thresholds; mean-reversion and turbulence gates meet their blocking floors; and any enabled session, higher-timeframe alignment, lower-timeframe flow, grade, cooldown, and options filters pass.
Short: Reversal candidate is short and reaches the configured actionable lifecycle tier (ARMED, EXTREME after a confirmed short climax, or CONFIRMED after short structure confirmation); at least the configured minimum number of the five directional reversal strengths meet their respective thresholds; mean-reversion and turbulence gates meet their blocking floors; and any enabled session, higher-timeframe alignment, lower-timeframe flow, grade, cooldown, and options filters pass.
Long: Trend-ride mode is enabled, ride trend direction is long, the selected entry timing condition is met (and pullback depth is met unless using cross-on-trend-change timing), required lower-timeframe signed flow is positive, and the turbulence gate passes its floor.
Short: Trend-ride mode is enabled, ride trend direction is short, the selected entry timing condition is met (and pullback depth is met unless using cross-on-trend-change timing), required lower-timeframe signed flow is negative, and the turbulence gate passes its floor.
