Calculation: Return = log(close / previous close), or zero if either close is nonpositive.
Calculation: Instability score = percentile rank of the volatility-normalized Wasserstein-1 distance between the most recent return window and the preceding equal-length return window, signed opposite the EMA of returns.
Calculation: Changepoint score = signed, decaying strength of the latest Student-t Bayesian online changepoint or mean/variance CUSUM break. Standardize and winsorize returns; update the run-length posterior and CUSUMs each bar. A break has direction opposite the slower return EMA unless the faster return EMA has the same sign and exceeds the slower EMA in magnitude by the selected acceleration limit. Strength combines normalized changepoint probability, absolute standardized return, and the post-reset mean CUSUM; retain it for a limited number of bars with exponential decay.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = signed, decaying strength of an up or down leg with slow efficiency ratio above its trend floor, falling fast efficiency ratio, and high percentile-ranked absolute close displacement divided by ATR. Sign it opposite the leg. Strength increases with displacement percentile and, optionally, relative volume divided by ATR-normalized progress and a high-ATR, large-move panic condition. Retain it for a limited number of bars with exponential decay.
Calculation: Climax score = signed strength of the latest qualifying volume-and-range climax while its secondary-test state remains valid. A bottom requires volume z-score and range z-score above their thresholds and normalized close location ((close-low)-(high-close))/(high-low) above its rejection threshold; a top requires the opposite close-location comparison. Optionally require its composite intensity to exceed an adaptively updated upper quantile. Intensity combines the excess volume z-score, range z-score, and absolute close location, optionally boosted by direction-aligned rolling volume delta. Volume delta uses either close location, the normal CDF of standardized return, or the triangular bar-geometry distribution of open and close. On a later bar, invalidate the state if price exceeds the climax extreme by the allowed ATR tolerance or the test window expires; increase strength once if price retests within the ATR tolerance on volume no greater than a fraction of climax volume. A new climax replaces the prior state.
Calculation: Structure score = signed strength of a confirmed swing-pivot sweep followed within a limited window by a displacement break. A bottom sweeps below the latest confirmed pivot low and closes above it, then closes above the latest confirmed pivot high; a top sweeps above the latest confirmed pivot high and closes below it, then closes below the latest confirmed pivot low. The breaking close-to-close move divided by ATR must reach its threshold. A new sweep replaces the old one. Retain the signed break strength for a limited number of bars.
Calculation: Reversal lifecycle = count positive and negative detector scores that meet their respective thresholds. When idle, arm the direction with at least the required count; positive wins a tie. Expire an unconfirmed candidate after the allowed bars. Promote armed to extreme when the climax score meets its threshold in the candidate direction; promote armed or extreme directly to confirmed when the structure score meets its threshold in that direction. Promotions can occur on the arming bar. Reset after a confirmed state persists into the next bar.
Calculation: Mean-reversion gate = the average of max(0, minus the rolling correlation of the prior close deviation from its moving average with current return) and a clipped linear score that decreases from one to zero as the variance ratio rises toward one. The variance ratio is variance of rolling multi-bar return sums divided by their aggregation length and the variance of single-bar returns. Optionally set the gate to zero unless the Dickey-Fuller t-statistic for regression of close change on prior close is at or below its critical value; when disabled, the gate is one.
Calculation: Turbulence gate = one unless enabled and the burst proxy is above its threshold and at least its previous value. A shock is an absolute return above a multiple of return standard deviation; the burst proxy is the clipped value 1 - 1/sqrt(max(variance/mean of rolling shock counts, 1)). During a cascade, decrease the gate linearly with the proxy's excess over its threshold, using the selected veto strength.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe flow = (buy volume - sell volume)/(buy volume + sell volume), summed over completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; classify a bar's volume as buy when close > open, sell when close < open, and ignore unchanged bars.
Calculation: Options filter = pass when disabled, or when expected move is at least its selected fixed or ATR-scaled threshold AND at least the required number of these conditions hold: Rogers-Satchell realized-volatility percentile is below its high threshold, realized volatility is rising over its slope lookback, expected move reaches its threshold, and the bar is outside the configured late-session and expiry caution periods. Expected move is close times realized volatility times the square root of the forecast horizon.
Calculation: Trend direction = select one basis: fast EMA or DEMA above slow EMA or DEMA with the slow average nondecreasing, or the symmetric bearish condition; nondisplaced short, medium, and long smoothed moving averages of median price ordered bullishly or bearishly, with the shortest moving in that direction and its distance from the longest at least an ATR multiple; or chart-timeframe DEMA above or below the nearest higher-timeframe DEMA and above or below at least the required number of higher-timeframe DEMAs. Higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed bar.
Long: Reversal lifecycle enters extreme from a different prior state AND mean-reversion gate and turbulence gate each meet their enabled floors.
Short: Reversal lifecycle enters extreme from a different prior state in the negative direction AND mean-reversion gate and turbulence gate each meet their enabled floors.
Long: Reversal lifecycle enters the selected actionable stage in the positive direction AND mean-reversion gate and turbulence gate each meet their enabled floors AND the enabled session, multi-timeframe alignment, lower-timeframe flow, grade, cooldown, and options filters pass.
Short: Reversal lifecycle enters the selected actionable stage in the negative direction AND mean-reversion gate and turbulence gate each meet their enabled floors AND the enabled session, multi-timeframe alignment, lower-timeframe flow, grade, cooldown, and options filters pass.
Long: Continuation is enabled AND trend direction is positive AND turbulence gate meets its enabled floor AND lower-timeframe flow is positive when required AND options filter passes AND continuation cooldown has elapsed AND no actionable reversal is displayed on the bar AND ((trend direction has just changed to positive with cross timing selected) OR (recent highest high minus current low is at least the selected ATR multiple AND ((close crosses above the fast trend average with recross timing selected) OR (close > previous high AND close > open with early timing selected)))).
Short: Continuation is enabled AND trend direction is negative AND turbulence gate meets its enabled floor AND lower-timeframe flow is negative when required AND options filter passes AND continuation cooldown has elapsed AND no actionable reversal is displayed on the bar AND ((trend direction has just changed to negative with cross timing selected) OR (current high minus recent lowest low is at least the selected ATR multiple AND ((close crosses below the fast trend average with recross timing selected) OR (close < previous low AND close < open with early timing selected)))).
