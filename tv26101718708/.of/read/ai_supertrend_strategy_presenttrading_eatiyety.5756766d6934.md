Calculation: SuperTrend and direction = Let basis be the selected moving average of close×volume divided by the selected moving average of volume, and ATR be average true range. Raw bands are basis ± factor×ATR. Ratchet the lower band to the greater of its raw value and its prior value, except use the raw value when prior close is below the prior lower band. Ratchet the upper band to the lesser of its raw value and its prior value, except use the raw value when prior close is above the prior upper band. Initialize direction to +1 until prior ATR exists; thereafter, if prior SuperTrend equals the prior upper band, direction is -1 when close > upper band and +1 otherwise; else direction is +1 when close < lower band and -1 otherwise. SuperTrend is the lower band when direction is -1, otherwise the upper band.
Calculation: KNN score = For each of the n most recent SuperTrend values, label it 1 if the short-lookback WMA of close is greater than the long-lookback WMA of SuperTrend, otherwise 0. Find the k values nearest the current SuperTrend by absolute distance; score is the sum of their labels weighted by 1/(distance+1e-6), divided by the sum of those weights.
Long: (KNN score = 1 AND prior KNN score != 1) OR (direction = -1 AND prior direction = 1 AND KNN score = 1)
Short: (KNN score = 0 AND prior KNN score != 0) OR (direction = 1 AND prior direction = -1 AND KNN score = 0)
Exit: Long, NOT ((direction = -1 AND KNN score = 1) OR (direction = 1 AND KNN score = 0)) AND low <= SuperTrend - factor×ATR
Exit: Short, NOT ((direction = -1 AND KNN score = 1) OR (direction = 1 AND KNN score = 0)) AND high >= SuperTrend + factor×ATR
