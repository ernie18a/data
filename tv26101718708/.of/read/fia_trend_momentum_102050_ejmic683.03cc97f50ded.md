Calculation: HTFBull = EMA(close, short period) > EMA(close, medium period) > EMA(close, long period), all computed on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming at the current chart bar. HTFBear = the same with the inequalities reversed (short < medium < long).
Calculation: LongScore = 2*(chart EMA short > EMA medium > EMA long) + (RSI > its midline) + (MACD line > MACD signal line) + (MACD histogram > previous bar's histogram) + HTFBull. ShortScore = 2*(chart EMA short < EMA medium < EMA long) + (RSI < its midline) + (MACD line < MACD signal line) + (MACD histogram < previous bar's histogram) + HTFBear.
Long: LongScore >= minimum score AND HTFBull AND (pullback OR MACD line crossed above signal line on this bar) AND the same full condition was not true on the previous bar. Pullback = chart EMA short > EMA medium > EMA long AND low <= EMA short AND close > EMA short AND RSI > previous bar's RSI AND RSI > a level slightly below the RSI midline AND MACD line > signal line.
Short: ShortScore >= minimum score AND HTFBear AND (pullback OR MACD line crossed below signal line on this bar) AND the same full condition was not true on the previous bar. Pullback = chart EMA short < EMA medium < EMA long AND high >= EMA short AND close < EMA short AND RSI < previous bar's RSI AND RSI < a level slightly above the RSI midline AND MACD line < signal line.
