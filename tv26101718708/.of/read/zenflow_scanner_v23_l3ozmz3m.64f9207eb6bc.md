Calculation: All calculations use chart bars. A prior-bar reference means the immediately preceding chart bar. A breakout above or below a lookback range compares close with the highest high or lowest low, respectively, over the specified number of bars ending at the prior bar. Continuation, pullback, reversal, trigger, and trap lookbacks are independently parameterized.
Calculation: Fast EMA and slow EMA are EMAs of close, with the fast EMA using the shorter period. VWAP is the session-anchored volume-weighted average of close. ATR is the average true range.
Calculation: High volume = volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier. Near EMA = distance from close to either EMA <= ATR × EMA proximity multiplier. Near VWAP = distance from close to VWAP <= ATR × VWAP proximity multiplier. Session pass = session filtering disabled OR datetime within the selected session.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment). Body = abs(close − open). Upper wick = high − max(open, close). Lower wick = min(open, close) − low. Delta = volume × (2 × close location − 1). Delta strength = delta / EMA(abs(delta)), or zero when that EMA is zero. Cumulative delta = cumulative sum of delta.
Calculation: Bullish order-flow impulse = delta strength >= positive impulse threshold AND cumulative delta >= its EMA AND close location >= upper impulse-location threshold. Bearish order-flow impulse = delta strength <= negative impulse threshold AND cumulative delta <= its EMA AND close location <= lower impulse-location threshold.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = high volume AND lower wick > body AND close location >= upper absorption-location threshold. Bearish absorption = high volume AND upper wick > body AND close location <= lower absorption-location threshold. Bullish exhaustion = high volume AND upper wick > body × exhaustion multiplier AND close location < upper exhaustion-location threshold AND close > open. Bearish exhaustion = high volume AND lower wick > body × exhaustion multiplier AND close location > lower exhaustion-location threshold AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish trap = low < prior trap-lookback lowest low AND close > that low AND delta strength < 0 AND close location >= upper trap-location threshold. Bearish trap = high > prior trap-lookback highest high AND close < that high AND delta strength > 0 AND close location <= lower trap-location threshold. Bullish impulse tag = body >= EMA(body) × impulse-body multiplier AND bullish order-flow impulse AND close > prior high. Bearish impulse tag = body >= EMA(body) × impulse-body multiplier AND bearish order-flow impulse AND close < prior low.
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > slow EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA. Bearish trend = close < slow EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA. Bullish EMA cross = fast EMA > slow EMA AND prior fast EMA <= prior slow EMA. Bearish EMA cross = fast EMA < slow EMA AND prior fast EMA >= prior slow EMA.
Calculation: Long watch = bullish trend AND session pass AND (near EMA OR near VWAP). Short watch = bearish trend AND session pass AND (near EMA OR near VWAP). Long pullback watch = bullish trend AND close > VWAP AND near EMA AND session pass. Short pullback watch = bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND near EMA AND session pass. Long reversal watch = close < VWAP AND bullish EMA cross AND session pass. Short reversal watch = close > VWAP AND bearish EMA cross AND session pass.
Calculation: Long trigger = session pass AND (long watch OR long pullback watch OR long reversal watch) AND ((lower wick >= body × rejection multiplier AND close location >= upper rejection-location threshold AND close > open) OR breakout above trigger lookback OR bullish trap OR bullish impulse tag). Short trigger = session pass AND (short watch OR short pullback watch OR short reversal watch) AND ((upper wick >= body × rejection multiplier AND close location <= lower rejection-location threshold AND close < open) OR breakout below trigger lookback OR bearish trap OR bearish impulse tag).
Calculation: Long reset = bullish trend AND close > VWAP AND (bullish order-flow impulse OR breakout above trigger lookback OR breakout above continuation lookback). Short reset = bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR breakout below trigger lookback OR breakout below continuation lookback).
Calculation: Long invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP/EMA invalidation enabled AND close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA) OR (order-flow invalidation enabled AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR bearish absorption OR bearish trap))) AND long reset = false. Short invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP/EMA invalidation enabled AND close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA) OR (order-flow invalidation enabled AND (bullish order-flow impulse OR bullish absorption OR bullish trap))) AND short reset = false.
Calculation: Long order-flow gate = order-flow filter disabled OR order-flow scoring disabled OR bullish order-flow impulse OR bullish absorption OR bullish trap. Short order-flow gate = order-flow filter disabled OR order-flow scoring disabled OR bearish order-flow impulse OR bearish absorption OR bearish trap. Long trigger gate = trigger requirement disabled OR long trigger. Short trigger gate = trigger requirement disabled OR short trigger.
Calculation: Long continuation = continuation engine enabled AND bullish trend AND (VWAP filter disabled OR (close > VWAP AND prior close <= prior VWAP)) AND (volume filter disabled OR high volume) AND breakout above continuation lookback AND long order-flow gate AND session pass AND long trigger gate AND long invalidation = false. Short continuation = continuation engine enabled AND bearish trend AND (VWAP filter disabled OR (close < VWAP AND prior close >= prior VWAP)) AND (volume filter disabled OR high volume) AND breakout below continuation lookback AND short order-flow gate AND session pass AND short trigger gate AND short invalidation = false.
Calculation: Long execution base = execution mode enabled AND long pullback watch AND breakout above trigger lookback AND long invalidation = false. Short execution base = execution mode enabled AND short pullback watch AND breakout below trigger lookback AND short invalidation = false. Long pullback base = pullback engine enabled AND ((long pullback watch AND breakout above pullback lookback AND (pullback volume requirement disabled OR high volume) AND long order-flow gate AND long trigger gate AND long invalidation = false) OR long execution base). Short pullback base = pullback engine enabled AND ((short pullback watch AND breakout below pullback lookback AND (pullback volume requirement disabled OR high volume) AND short order-flow gate AND short trigger gate AND short invalidation = false) OR short execution base).
Calculation: Long reversal = reversal engine enabled AND close < VWAP AND close > VWAP AND prior close <= prior VWAP AND bullish EMA cross AND breakout above reversal lookback AND (reversal volume requirement disabled OR high volume) AND long order-flow gate AND session pass AND long trigger gate AND long invalidation = false. Short reversal = reversal engine enabled AND close > VWAP AND close < VWAP AND prior close >= prior VWAP AND bearish EMA cross AND breakout below reversal lookback AND (reversal volume requirement disabled OR high volume) AND short order-flow gate AND session pass AND short trigger gate AND short invalidation = false.
Calculation: Long momentum watch = momentum enabled AND bullish trend AND close > VWAP AND session pass AND (bullish order-flow impulse OR breakout above continuation lookback OR breakout above trigger lookback) AND long invalidation = false. Short momentum watch = momentum enabled AND bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND session pass AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR breakout below continuation lookback OR breakout below trigger lookback) AND short invalidation = false.
Calculation: Directional order-flow score = zero if order-flow scoring is disabled; otherwise use the first matching score for that direction's impulse, absorption, exhaustion, or none, then add the trap score when its trap is true and the impulse-tag score when its impulse tag is true. Exhaustion contributes a negative score; the other matching components contribute positive scores.
Calculation: For each direction, continuation score = sum of the corresponding weights for trend, close on the directional side of VWAP, high volume, continuation breakout, near EMA, and session pass, plus directional order-flow score. Pullback score = sum of the corresponding weights for trend, close on the directional side of VWAP, near EMA, pullback breakout, and the optional pullback-volume condition passing, plus directional order-flow score. Reversal score = sum of the corresponding weights for close on the opposite side of VWAP, VWAP crossing in the direction, EMA crossing in the direction, reversal breakout, and the optional reversal-volume condition passing, plus directional order-flow score. Each listed condition has its own score weight; a false condition contributes zero.
Calculation: Preliminary long context = long continuation OR long pullback base OR long reversal OR long trigger OR long watch OR long pullback watch OR long reversal watch OR long momentum watch OR long continuation score > short continuation score. Preliminary short context is the corresponding short expression, including short continuation score > long continuation score.
Calculation: Entry confidence = the maximum of the six directional continuation, pullback, and reversal scores, less penalties, clamped to the permitted confidence range. Apply the trend-conflict penalty once if preliminary long context AND bearish trend OR preliminary short context AND bullish trend; the location-conflict penalty once if preliminary long context AND close < VWAP OR preliminary short context AND close > VWAP; and the order-flow-conflict penalty once if preliminary long context AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR bearish absorption OR bearish trap OR bearish impulse tag) OR preliminary short context AND (bullish order-flow impulse OR bullish absorption OR bullish trap OR bullish impulse tag). Also apply the no-modifier penalty when neither trap nor either impulse tag is present, and the no-trigger penalty when triggers are required AND neither directional trigger is present.
Calculation: Long stop = min(entry-bar low, entry price − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier), fixed at entry. Short stop = max(entry-bar high, entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier), fixed at entry.
