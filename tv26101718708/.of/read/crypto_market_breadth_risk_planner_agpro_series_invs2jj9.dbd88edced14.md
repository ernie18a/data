Calculation: Breadth %, momentum %, rising-trend %, and stress % = percentages of valid basket symbols whose breadth-timeframe close is above its EMA, whose rate of change is positive, whose EMA is above its prior-bar EMA, and whose ATR as a percentage of close is at or above the stress threshold, respectively. Use the selected breadth timeframe; when it is higher than the chart timeframe, use its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Breadth Risk Score = round(clamp(0.38 × breadth % + 0.28 × momentum % + 0.20 × rising-trend % + 0.14 × (100 − stress %), 0, 100)).
Long: (Breadth Risk Score ≥ risk-on threshold AND breadth % ≥ 60 AND momentum % ≥ 55 AND risk-on was false on the prior bar) OR (risk-on is false AND risk-off is false AND stress review is false AND Breadth Risk Score ≥ rotation threshold AND Breadth Risk Score > prior-bar score AND momentum % ≥ prior-bar momentum % AND rotation watch was false on the prior bar) OR (stress % < prior-bar stress % AND Breadth Risk Score > prior-bar score AND this cooling condition was false on the prior bar)
Short: (Breadth Risk Score ≤ risk-off threshold OR (breadth % ≤ 35 AND momentum % ≤ 40)) AND risk-off was false on the prior bar OR (risk-on is false AND stress % ≥ 55 AND Breadth Risk Score < rotation threshold AND stress review was false on the prior bar)
