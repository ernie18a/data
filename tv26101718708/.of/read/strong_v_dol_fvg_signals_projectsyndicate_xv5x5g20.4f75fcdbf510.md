Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a chart-timeframe high/low pivot with the configured number of bars on each side; each confirmed pool can be raided once.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; gap size must be at least the configured ATR fraction. An FVG is inverted when a bearish gap closes above its top or a bullish gap closes below its bottom; it is eligible only within the configured freshness window.
Calculation: Long entry zone = the nearest eligible inverted bearish FVG, or, if none qualifies and the zone policy allows it, the nearest eligible fresh bullish FVG created on or after the V extreme; zone must lie between the V extreme and reclaim close. Short entry zone = the nearest eligible inverted bullish FVG, or, if none qualifies and allowed, the nearest eligible fresh bearish FVG created on or after the V extreme; zone must lie between reclaim close and the V extreme. An inverted gap takes precedence over a fresh gap.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar compared with the EMA of that same bar; bullish when close > EMA, bearish when close < EMA.
Calculation: V-Score = 10 × weighted mean of clipped [0,1] values for sweep depth / 0.8 ATR, displacement / 1.5 ATR, sharpness / 0.4 ATR per bar, rejection-wick fraction, (volume at V extreme / its chart-timeframe volume average − 1) / (configured volume surge reference − 1), and room-to-DOL R / 5; in score-only bias mode add the configured bias adjustment when aligned and subtract it when unaligned, then clip to [0,10].
Calculation: DOL target = nearest unswept eligible liquidity level beyond entry within the configured R bounds: confirmed swing high/low from a higher timeframe, previous completed daily high/low, optional previous completed weekly high/low, and optional chart-timeframe swing high/low. A level is swept when chart high exceeds a high level or chart low falls below a low level. If none qualifies, use the configured flat-R target.
Calculation: Risk distance = distance from entry to the V extreme plus the configured ATR buffer; when risk normalization is enabled, clamp it to the configured ATR floor and ceiling. TP1 and TP2 are configured R multiples; TP3 is the DOL target when available, otherwise the configured flat-R target.
Long: A confirmed swing low pool is raided when low < pool low; record the lowest low and its rejection-wick fraction and volume ratio until reclaim or timeout. Within the configured reclaim window, close > pool low AND sweep depth = (pool low − V low) / ATR meets its minimum AND displacement = (close − V low) / ATR meets its minimum AND sharpness = displacement / max(bars from V low to reclaim, 1) meets its minimum AND that bar count is within the configured width AND risk is positive and within the configured cap when normalization is off AND V-Score meets its minimum AND any enabled higher-timeframe bias filter is bullish AND (a valid long entry zone exists or zone requirement is off). In immediate mode, signal at reclaim close. In retest mode with a zone, signal when low <= zone top while no trade is active, provided close has not fallen below the V low and the arm window has not expired; entry = min(zone top, open).
Short: A confirmed swing high pool is raided when high > pool high; record the highest high and its rejection-wick fraction and volume ratio until reclaim or timeout. Within the configured reclaim window, close < pool high AND sweep depth = (V high − pool high) / ATR meets its minimum AND displacement = (V high − close) / ATR meets its minimum AND sharpness = displacement / max(bars from V high to reclaim, 1) meets its minimum AND that bar count is within the configured width AND risk is positive and within the configured cap when normalization is off AND V-Score meets its minimum AND any enabled higher-timeframe bias filter is bearish AND (a valid short entry zone exists or zone requirement is off). In immediate mode, signal at reclaim close. In retest mode with a zone, signal when high >= zone bottom while no trade is active, provided close has not risen above the V high and the arm window has not expired; entry = max(zone bottom, open).
Exit: Long position, low <= stop OR high >= TP3; stop takes precedence if stop and any target are touched on the same bar. If stopped after TP1 or TP2 was touched on an earlier bar, record the highest target reached; otherwise record a stop loss.
Exit: Short position, high >= stop OR low <= TP3; stop takes precedence if stop and any target are touched on the same bar. If stopped after TP1 or TP2 was touched on an earlier bar, record the highest target reached; otherwise record a stop loss.
