Calculation: ATR = Wilder's average true range; when unavailable use high minus low, bounded below by the minimum price increment inferred from the data.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = the most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low for longs or pivot high for shorts, using the same configurable number of bars on each side. A new pivot replaces the previous pool and resets its consumed status. A sweep begins when low is strictly below an unconsumed low pool or high is strictly above an unconsumed high pool. Freeze the swept pool and track the lowest low or highest high and its bar from the sweep onward. Starting on the next bar, process the first close back through the pool; otherwise discard and consume the pool once bars since the sweep exceed the reclaim window. A reclaim consumes the pool whether or not it qualifies. Test reclaim before expiry, so a reclaim on the first bar beyond the window is still processed. A new pivot resets the current pool's consumed status even during a sweep.
Calculation: V geometry = on the reclaim bar, depth is the distance from the frozen pool to the tracked extreme divided by current ATR; displacement is the distance from the extreme to the reclaim close divided by current ATR; width is bars since the extreme; sharpness is displacement divided by the greater of width and one. Provisional entry is the reclaim close; provisional stop is the extreme minus an ATR buffer for a long or plus it for a short; provisional risk is the directional distance from entry to stop.
Calculation: Gap = a bullish three-bar gap has current low above high two bars earlier, with bottom at that earlier high and top at current low; a bearish gap has current high below low two bars earlier, with bottom at current high and top at that earlier low. Gap size must be at least a configurable multiple of current ATR. Keep at most a configurable number of the newest gaps. A bullish gap inverts when close falls below its bottom and a bearish gap when close rises above its top; an inverted bullish gap dies when a later close rises above its top, and an inverted bearish gap when a later close falls below its bottom.
Calculation: Entry zone = for a long, select a live inverted bearish gap inverted within the freshness window and contained between the V low and reclaim close; choose the highest top. If none is eligible and the configured policy permits a regular gap, select a live, uninverted bullish gap created on or after the extreme bar and contained in that range, choosing the highest top. For a short, use a live inverted bullish gap or, as fallback, a live uninverted bearish gap created on or after the extreme, contained between reclaim close and V high; choose the lowest bottom. The configured policy may permit only inverted gaps, only regular gaps, or inverted gaps followed by regular gaps.
Calculation: Draw on liquidity = maintain separate high and low level lists from confirmed pivot highs and lows on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, optionally previous completed higher-timeframe day and week highs and lows, and optionally confirmed chart-timeframe pivots. Add a higher-timeframe value only when it first appears or changes; do not add a level within one quarter of current ATR of any existing level in the same list, including consumed levels. Keep a bounded number of the newest levels per list. Mark a high level consumed when chart high is strictly above it and a low level consumed when chart low is strictly below it, including on the bar added. For a long, select the lowest unconsumed high above entry; for a short, select the highest unconsumed low below entry. Its distance in risk units must be between the configured maximum and the greater of the configured minimum and the second target's risk multiple plus a fixed margin. If targeting is disabled or no level qualifies, use the configured fallback final-target risk multiple.
Calculation: V score = take a weighted mean of six components, each clamped to the range zero to one: depth divided by its fixed reference, displacement divided by its fixed reference, sharpness divided by its fixed reference, extreme-bar rejection wick divided by that bar's range, extreme-bar volume surge above one divided by its configured reference surge above one, and provisional draw distance in risk units divided by its fixed reference. Volume surge is extreme-bar volume divided by its volume SMA, or one if the average is unavailable or nonpositive. Use provisional entry and risk when selecting the draw; use the fallback final-target multiple if no draw qualifies. Multiply the weighted mean by the score scale; in score-only bias mode add a fixed bonus when aligned and subtract it otherwise; clamp to the score scale. The fixed depth, displacement, sharpness and room references, score scale, and bias adjustment are constants of the scoring formula.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the close with its EMA on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe; alignment is close above EMA for a long and close below EMA for a short. Missing or equal values are unaligned.
Calculation: Qualified long setup = a low-pool reclaim with depth and displacement at least their respective thresholds, width at most its threshold, sharpness at least its threshold, positive provisional risk, provisional risk at most the maximum ATR risk when risk normalization is disabled, score at least its threshold, higher-timeframe bullish alignment if bias filtering is enabled, and an entry zone if zones are required. Qualified short setup uses the symmetric high-pool reclaim and bearish alignment. Evaluate these conditions only on the first reclaim; if any fails, discard the setup.
Calculation: Armed setup = when retest mode is enabled and a qualified setup has a zone, save its extreme and zone on the reclaim bar. Starting on the next bar, cancel it before testing a fill if the close is beyond the extreme in the adverse direction or its age exceeds the arm window. Otherwise, when no simulated trade is active, a long fills if low is at or below the zone top at the lesser of open and zone top; a short fills if high is at or above the zone bottom at the greater of open and zone bottom. At fill, recompute the buffered stop using current ATR; require positive directional risk and, if normalization is disabled, risk at most the maximum ATR risk. Discard the arm after the first fill test even if risk fails.
Calculation: Trade levels = at entry, risk is the absolute distance between entry and the extreme buffered by entry-bar ATR; if normalization is enabled, clamp it between the configured ATR risk floor and cap. Stop is entry minus risk for a long or entry plus risk for a short. The first and second targets are entry plus or minus risk times their respective configured multiples. The final target is the qualifying draw selected using this risk, or entry plus or minus risk times the fallback final-target multiple. Fix all levels at entry. A simulated trade can open only when simulation is enabled, no trade is active, ATR is available, risk is positive, and no trade closed on that bar. Check exits starting on the next bar.
Long: Long setups are enabled AND a qualified long setup is present AND (retest mode is disabled OR no entry zone exists) OR long setups are enabled AND an armed long setup fills with valid risk.
Short: Short setups are enabled AND no long signal fires on the bar AND ((a qualified short setup is present AND (retest mode is disabled OR no entry zone exists)) OR an armed short setup fills with valid risk).
Exit: long, low is at or below the fixed stop OR high is at or above the fixed final target.
Exit: short, high is at or above the fixed stop OR low is at or below the fixed final target.
