Calculation: Session-anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, starting at the first bar in a configurable anchor hour in a configurable timezone and continuing until the next such bar; calculate independently on each applicable timeframe.
Calculation: Cross event = SMA of close crosses the session-anchored VWAP in either direction, excluding the session-anchor bar. Cross-anchored VWAP uses the same cumulative formula, starting at the latest cross-event bar and continuing until the next cross event.
Calculation: Setup state = on a completed cross event, arm the setup, record the current chart bar's high and low and clear the flip restriction and flip-used flag. A new cross replaces the previous setup even during a trade. At the session reset, clear the armed setup, recorded high and low, flip restriction and flip-used flag. On a later confirmed chart bar, the first permitted breakout disarms the setup and schedules its direction for the next chart bar's open; discard the scheduled entry if a trade is still open. Process a scheduled entry before replacing the setup on that bar. On a stop, rearm the same setup for the opposite direction only if its recorded cross has not been replaced and its flip has not been used; mark the flip used. A profit exit does not rearm it. Check exits before opening a scheduled entry, and do not check a new trade's entry-bar range for exits. If stop and profit are both touched on one bar, treat the exit as a stop.
Calculation: Timeframe handling = calculate the core SMA, session-anchored VWAP, cross event, cross-anchored VWAP, close/SMA crosses and sweeps on a configurable core timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed core bar, acting only when that bar and the chart bar are closed. If the core timeframe is lower, use the last available core-bar values on the chart bar, while detecting a cross event from a change in its cumulative event count. Calculate the EMA crosses separately on the core timeframe and on each configurable higher timeframe; use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe and emit each higher-timeframe cross only once when first observed.
Long: On the chart bar after the first eligible confirmed chart bar following an armed cross, at its open, position is flat AND the permitted direction is long AND either close > core SMA AND close > cross-anchored VWAP, or, under the complete-breakout mode, low > core SMA AND low > cross-anchored VWAP, on that breakout bar.
Short: On the chart bar after the first eligible confirmed chart bar following an armed cross, at its open, position is flat AND the permitted direction is short AND either close < core SMA AND close < cross-anchored VWAP, or, under the complete-breakout mode, high < core SMA AND high < cross-anchored VWAP, on that breakout bar.
Long: Core SMA crosses above the core session-anchored VWAP AND the core bar is not the session-anchor bar.
Short: Core SMA crosses below the core session-anchored VWAP AND the core bar is not the session-anchor bar.
Long: Core close crosses above the core SMA.
Short: Core close crosses below the core SMA.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close crosses above longer-period SMA of close OR crosses above session-anchored VWAP, calculated on the same applicable timeframe.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close crosses below longer-period SMA of close OR crosses below session-anchored VWAP, calculated on the same applicable timeframe.
Long: Core low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND core close > that lowest low AND the same condition was false on the previous core bar.
Short: Core high > highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND core close < that highest high AND the same condition was false on the previous core bar.
Exit: long, chart low <= recorded setup low OR chart high >= entry price + configurable profit distance, starting after the entry bar.
Exit: short, chart high >= recorded setup high OR chart low <= entry price - configurable profit distance, starting after the entry bar.
