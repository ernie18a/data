Calculation: ORB low = lowest low during the opening-range session
Calculation: Bullish 50%, 60%, and 70% retracement levels = ORB high minus 50%, 60%, and 70% of the ORB range (ORB high − ORB low)
Calculation: Bearish 50%, 60%, and 70% retracement levels = ORB low plus 50%, 60%, and 70% of the ORB range (ORB high − ORB low)
Long: ORB session has ended AND no breakout has occurred this session AND close > ORB high
Short: ORB session has ended AND no breakout has occurred this session AND close < ORB low

Short: bearish breakout occurred this session AND no prior short setup signal this session AND high >= bearish 50% level AND low <= bearish 70% level AND (candle confirmation is disabled OR close < open) AND (60% close filter is disabled OR close < bearish 60% level)
