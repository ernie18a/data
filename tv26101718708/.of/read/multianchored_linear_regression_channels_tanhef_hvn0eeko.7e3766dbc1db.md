Calculation: Regression channel = for each selected chart-timeframe source, fit a linear regression over its selected lookback and set the upper and lower channel boundaries to the regression endpoint plus and minus a selected multiple of the regression residual deviation. The lookback may be fixed or anchored to a selected historical extreme or time boundary. Regression, deviation, and their weighting may be selected independently for each channel; calculations may use linear prices or their logarithms.
Calculation: Channel position = (transformed source − transformed lower boundary) / (transformed upper boundary − transformed lower boundary). The average channel position is the arithmetic mean across selected channels.
Calculation: Average correlation = arithmetic mean of the selected channels’ Pearson correlations between their transformed sources and their fitted regression lines.
Calculation: Deviation multiple = (2 × channel position − 1) × that channel’s deviation multiplier. Average deviation multiple and average deviation multiplier are arithmetic means across selected channels.
Long: At least one channel is selected AND every selected channel has a lookback greater than one bar AND at least one entry criterion is selected AND all selected entry criteria hold: channel position or average channel position is at or above or at or below its independently selected threshold, AND correlation or average correlation is at or above or at or below its independently selected threshold, OR its absolute value is at or above or at or below the absolute threshold. Omitted criteria impose no restriction; the zero-to-correlation criterion uses the absolute-value at-or-below comparison.
Long: Any selected channel’s channel position is at or below the lower boundary OR the average channel position is at or below the lower boundary OR any selected channel’s deviation multiple is at or below the negative of its deviation multiplier OR the average deviation multiple is at or below the negative average deviation multiplier.
Exit: long, at least one channel is selected AND every selected channel has a lookback greater than one bar AND at least one exit criterion is selected AND all selected exit criteria hold: channel position or average channel position is at or above or at or below its independently selected threshold, AND correlation or average correlation is at or above or at or below its independently selected threshold, OR its absolute value is at or above or at or below the absolute threshold. Omitted criteria impose no restriction; the zero-to-correlation criterion uses the absolute-value at-or-below comparison.
Exit: long, any selected channel’s channel position is at or above the upper boundary OR the average channel position is at or above the upper boundary OR any selected channel’s deviation multiple is at or above its deviation multiplier OR the average deviation multiple is at or above the average deviation multiplier.
