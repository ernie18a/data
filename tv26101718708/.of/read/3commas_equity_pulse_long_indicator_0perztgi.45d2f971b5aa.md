Calculation: Safety-order level n = base entry price × (1 − initial step × sum of step coefficient^i for i from 0 through n−1), with the initial step expressed as a fraction.
Calculation: Average entry price = total entry notional divided by total acquired quantity. The base order fills at the entry bar's close. Each safety order fills at its level, with notional equal to initial safety-order notional × volume coefficient^(n−1). On each confirmed bar, fill every successive unfilled level reached by the low, including on the base-entry bar. Calculate the average used for that bar's exit before processing that bar's orders.
Long: Position side = flat AND (date filter disabled OR bar datetime within the inclusive date window).
Long: Position side = long AND next unfilled safety-order number ≤ maximum safety-order count AND low ≤ its safety-order level.
Exit: long, close ≥ average entry price × (1 + minimum profit fraction) AND (RSI exit filter disabled OR RSI crosses above its threshold). RSI uses the previous bar on its configured timeframe; that timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. For a lower timeframe, use the previous lower-timeframe bar relative to the last lower-timeframe bar selected within each chart bar.
