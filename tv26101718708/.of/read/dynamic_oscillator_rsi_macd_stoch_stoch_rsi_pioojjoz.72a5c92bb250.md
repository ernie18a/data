Calculation: RSI pivot low = an RSI low confirmed after a right-hand lookback, using a left-hand lookback and a right-hand lookback.
Calculation: RSI pivot high = an RSI high confirmed after a right-hand lookback, using a left-hand lookback and a right-hand lookback.
Calculation: MACD histogram = shorter-period moving average of the selected price source minus longer-period moving average of that source, minus a moving average of their difference; each moving average is independently selectable as SMA or EMA, with the same type used for the two price averages.
Calculation: SMI Ergodic histogram = True Strength Index of close, using shorter and longer smoothing periods, minus its EMA signal line.
Long: A new RSI pivot low is confirmed AND its RSI is greater than at the immediately preceding RSI pivot low AND the price low at the new pivot is less than the price low at the preceding pivot AND the number of bars between the pivot confirmations minus one is within the inclusive minimum and maximum range.
Short: A new RSI pivot high is confirmed AND its RSI is less than at the immediately preceding RSI pivot high AND the price high at the new pivot is greater than the price high at the preceding pivot AND the number of bars between the pivot confirmations minus one is within the inclusive minimum and maximum range.
Long: MACD histogram >= zero.
Short: MACD histogram < zero.
Long: SMI Ergodic histogram >= zero.
Short: SMI Ergodic histogram < zero.
