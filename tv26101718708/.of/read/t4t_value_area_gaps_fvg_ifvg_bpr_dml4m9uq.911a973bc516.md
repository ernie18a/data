Calculation: Value levels = For each completed higher-timeframe profile period and each completed longer higher-timeframe weekly period, divide the period's high-to-low range into equal price rows. Use completed lower-timeframe bars, or chart bars when the selected resolution is not lower; optionally include only bars inside a selected session. Allocate each bar's volume to rows in proportion to its high-low overlap; allocate all volume to one row if both endpoints fall in that row. If total volume is zero, use one unit per bar. The point of control is the midpoint of the first row with maximum volume. Starting there, add the higher-volume adjacent row until the accumulated volume reaches the selected fraction of total row volume, choosing the upper row on a tie. The value-area high and low are the outer edges of the included rows. Publish the profile-period levels at the next period's start, or on the first chart bar outside the selected session; publish weekly levels at the next weekly period's start.
Calculation: Gap bars = Chart bars, or the last completed bar of a selected higher timeframe, updated only when a new higher-timeframe bar begins. Calculate ATR on the same gap timeframe and completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish gap = The current gap bar's low is greater than the high two gap bars earlier AND their difference is at least a selected multiple of gap-timeframe ATR; its bottom is that earlier high and its top is the current low. Bearish gap = The current gap bar's high is less than the low two gap bars earlier AND their difference is at least a selected multiple of gap-timeframe ATR; its bottom is the current high and its top is that earlier low.
Calculation: Zone alignment = At least one selected previous profile-period value-area high, value-area low or point of control, or previous weekly value-area high or low, is between the zone bottom minus a selected multiple of gap-timeframe ATR and the zone top plus that tolerance. Store alignment when a zone is created, inverted, or reconsidered for display; otherwise retain its stored alignment.
Calculation: Zone state = Keep gap zones and overlapping-range zones in creation order, including unaligned zones. A new gap starts with its gap direction, original direction, boundaries and age zero. On each chart bar after creation, a bullish zone is touched when low <= its top and is away when low > its top; a bearish zone is touched when high >= its bottom and is away when high < its bottom. Count a touch only when the zone was previously away; initially it is away. On each new gap bar, before creating new gaps, remove zones older than the selected maximum age. Invert a bullish gap when the gap-bar close < its bottom and a bearish gap when the close > its top; retain its boundaries, original direction and creation age, reverse its direction, and reset its touch count and away state. Remove an inverted gap or overlapping-range zone when the gap-bar close crosses its opposite edge by the same strict comparisons. Retain at most the most recently created selected maximum number of zones.
Calculation: Overlapping-range zone = When a new gap forms, search existing zones newest first within a selected maximum age in gap bars, stopping when a zone is older than that window. Select the first non-overlapping-range zone whose original direction opposes the new gap and whose price interval overlaps it strictly. The new zone has the new gap's direction and the intersection of the two intervals as its boundaries.
Long: A bullish gap forms AND either its zone is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones.
Short: A bearish gap forms AND either its zone is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones.
Long: A bearish gap inverts AND either its zone is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones.
Short: A bullish gap inverts AND either its zone is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones.
Long: A bullish overlapping-range zone forms AND either it is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones.
Short: A bearish overlapping-range zone forms AND either it is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones.
Long: An existing bullish zone not currently displayed is reconsidered when a new profile-period or weekly level is published AND either it is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones AND either fresh-only display is disabled or its touch count is zero.
Short: An existing bearish zone not currently displayed is reconsidered when a new profile-period or weekly level is published AND either it is aligned or the selected display mode includes unaligned zones AND either fresh-only display is disabled or its touch count is zero.
Long: An aligned bullish zone receives its first touch after creation or inversion.
Short: An aligned bearish zone receives its first touch after creation or inversion.
