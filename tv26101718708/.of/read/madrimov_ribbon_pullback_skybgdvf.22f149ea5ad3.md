Calculation: Structure levels = Maintain a leg initialized to 0. On each bar, set it to 0 if the high a pivot-lookback number of bars ago is greater than the highest high of the bars since then; otherwise set it to 1 if the low that many bars ago is less than the lowest low of the bars since then; otherwise retain it. When the leg changes from 0 to 1, record that past low as the current low level and mark it uncrossed. When it changes from 1 to 0, record that past high as the current high level and mark it uncrossed. A close crossing above an uncrossed high level marks it crossed; a close crossing below an uncrossed low level marks it crossed. A new level replaces the previous level of the same kind.
Calculation: Prior ribbon touch = On each bar, set a state to true if the bar's low is at or below the higher of the short and long chart-timeframe moving averages AND its high is at or above the lower of those averages; then set the state to false if either structure level is crossed on that bar. Use the previous bar's state.
Calculation: Moving averages = Use either exponential or simple moving averages of close consistently. The higher-timeframe moving averages use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, updated when that bar completes.
Long: Close crosses above the uncrossed structure high level.
Short: Close crosses below the uncrossed structure low level.
Long: Short moving average is greater than long moving average on the chart timeframe.
Short: Short moving average is less than long moving average on the chart timeframe.
Long: Short moving average is greater than long moving average on the higher timeframe.
Short: Short moving average is less than long moving average on the higher timeframe.
Long: Close crosses above the uncrossed structure high level AND prior ribbon touch is true AND chart-timeframe short moving average is greater than chart-timeframe long moving average AND the increase in the short moving average over a slope lookback is greater than ATR multiplied by a slope threshold AND the difference between the short and long moving averages is greater than ATR multiplied by a separation threshold AND higher-timeframe short moving average is greater than higher-timeframe long moving average.
Short: Close crosses below the uncrossed structure low level AND prior ribbon touch is true AND chart-timeframe short moving average is less than chart-timeframe long moving average AND the decrease in the short moving average over a slope lookback is greater than ATR multiplied by a slope threshold AND the difference between the long and short moving averages is greater than ATR multiplied by a separation threshold AND higher-timeframe short moving average is less than higher-timeframe long moving average.
