Calculation: Raw Williams %R = 100 × (source − highest high) / (highest high − lowest low) over a lookback; use −50 when the highest high equals the lowest low. The source is a selected price derived from the bar fields.
Calculation: Fast and slow %R = raw Williams %R using respectively shorter and longer lookbacks, each optionally smoothed using its own length and a selected smoothing method. Average %R = the average of the two raw Williams %R values, optionally smoothed using its own length and the selected method.
Calculation: Overbought zone = fast AND slow %R >= the upper exhaustion threshold in standard mode, or average %R >= that threshold in average mode. Oversold zone = fast AND slow %R <= the lower exhaustion threshold in standard mode, or average %R <= that threshold in average mode. The thresholds are symmetric around the Williams %R midpoint.
Calculation: Bearish reversal = the first bar outside the overbought zone after being inside it. Bullish reversal = the first bar outside the oversold zone after being inside it.
Calculation: Filtered bearish reversal = bearish reversal AND, if enabled, the preceding consecutive overbought-zone duration >= a minimum AND, if enabled, at least one new zone high exceeded the previous zone high while the fast %R at that high was lower than at the previous high by more than a divergence margin AND, if enabled, raw slow %R from the last completed higher-timeframe bar is unavailable OR below the selected higher-timeframe upper threshold or midpoint. In average mode use average %R for divergence. If price confirmation is enabled, instead signal on the first bar from the reversal through the confirmation window whose close < the lowest low of a lookback ending on the preceding bar; cancel when the overbought zone is re-entered or the window expires. A new qualifying reversal resets the pending window.
Calculation: Filtered bullish reversal = bullish reversal AND, if enabled, the preceding consecutive oversold-zone duration >= a minimum AND, if enabled, at least one new zone low was below the previous zone low while the fast %R at that low was higher than at the previous low by more than a divergence margin AND, if enabled, raw slow %R from the last completed higher-timeframe bar is unavailable OR above the selected higher-timeframe lower threshold or midpoint. In average mode use average %R for divergence. If price confirmation is enabled, instead signal on the first bar from the reversal through the confirmation window whose close > the highest high of a lookback ending on the preceding bar; cancel when the oversold zone is re-entered or the window expires. A new qualifying reversal resets the pending window.
Calculation: Long setup risk = the absolute difference between entry close and the lower of the most recent oversold-zone low and the lowest low over a lookback equal to the confirmation window plus an offset, minus an ATR buffer; apply a minimum price increment to risk. Short setup risk = the absolute difference between entry close and the higher of the most recent overbought-zone high and the highest high over the same lookback, plus an ATR buffer; apply a minimum price increment to risk. ATR and zone extremes are measured at entry.
Long: Oversold zone is entered OR oversold zone is active OR bullish reversal OR filtered bullish reversal OR, in standard mode, fast %R crosses above slow %R OR, in standard mode, fast and slow %R are both above the midpoint AND either crosses above it OR, in average mode, average %R crosses above the midpoint.
Short: Overbought zone is entered OR overbought zone is active OR bearish reversal OR filtered bearish reversal OR, in standard mode, fast %R crosses below slow %R OR, in standard mode, fast and slow %R are both below the midpoint AND either crosses below it OR, in average mode, average %R crosses below the midpoint.
Long: Overbought zone is entered OR filtered bearish reversal, when the corresponding trend-following or reversal-re-entry strategy is selected.
Short: Oversold zone is entered OR filtered bullish reversal, when the corresponding trend-following or reversal-re-entry strategy is selected.
Exit: long, selected strategy's short entry condition OR slow %R crosses below the midpoint in standard mode OR average %R crosses below the midpoint in average mode, according to the selected exit mode.
Exit: short, selected strategy's long entry condition OR slow %R crosses above the midpoint in standard mode OR average %R crosses above the midpoint in average mode, according to the selected exit mode.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= entry price − long setup risk OR high >= entry price + long setup risk × selected target multiple OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + short setup risk OR low <= entry price − short setup risk × selected target multiple OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period.
