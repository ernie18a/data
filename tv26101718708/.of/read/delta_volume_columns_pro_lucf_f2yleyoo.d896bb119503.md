Calculation: Buy volume = sum of volume from lower-timeframe bars classified up; sell volume = sum of volume from lower-timeframe bars classified down. Classify each lower-timeframe bar by close vs. open; if equal, by close vs. the previous lower-timeframe close; if still equal, use the last known movement polarity. Use completed lower-timeframe bars within each completed chart bar.
Calculation: On-bar balance = buy volume − sell volume.
Calculation: Average balance = EMA(buy volume, long lookback) − EMA(sell volume, same long lookback).
Calculation: Momentum balance = RSI((EMA of buy volume using the long lookback − SMA of buy volume using twice that lookback) − (EMA of sell volume using the long lookback − SMA of sell volume using twice that lookback)), short lookback − 50.
Calculation: Relative balance = ALMA(max(0, buy volume − buy-volume EMA) − max(0, sell volume − sell-volume EMA), lookback).
Calculation: Percent balance = ALMA(100 × on-bar balance ÷ chart-bar volume, lookback).
Calculation: Combined balance = sum of +1 for each of the five balances above zero and −1 for each below zero.
Calculation: A balance divergence up occurs when the selected balance rises and the chart close falls; a balance divergence down occurs when the selected balance falls and the chart close rises. Rising/falling means greater/less than the prior bar.
Calculation: A buy bump occurs when chart close rises, on-bar balance is positive, buy volume exceeds its EMA, and buy volume rises. A sell bump occurs when chart close falls, on-bar balance is negative, sell volume exceeds its EMA, and sell volume rises.
Calculation: Marker bias = rolling sum over a lookback of the signed occurrences of balance agreement, double bumps, divergence confirmations, and balance shifts; each up occurrence is +1 and each down occurrence is −1.
Long: [Balance Agreement enabled AND all five balances are above zero] OR [Double Bumps enabled AND buy bumps occur on each of the prior two bars AND chart close rose from two bars ago to the prior bar] OR [Divergence Confirmations enabled AND selected-balance divergence up occurred two bars ago AND chart close rose on the prior bar AND neither selected-balance divergence occurred on the prior bar] OR [Balance Shifts enabled AND selected balance crossed above zero on the prior bar] OR [Marker Bias Shifts enabled AND marker bias crossed above zero on the prior bar]; subject to the configured marker direction allowing longs.
Short: [Balance Agreement enabled AND all five balances are below zero] OR [Double Bumps enabled AND sell bumps occur on each of the prior two bars AND chart close fell from two bars ago to the prior bar] OR [Divergence Confirmations enabled AND selected-balance divergence down occurred two bars ago AND chart close fell on the prior bar AND neither selected-balance divergence occurred on the prior bar] OR [Balance Shifts enabled AND selected balance crossed below zero on the prior bar] OR [Marker Bias Shifts enabled AND marker bias crossed below zero on the prior bar]; subject to the configured marker direction allowing shorts.
