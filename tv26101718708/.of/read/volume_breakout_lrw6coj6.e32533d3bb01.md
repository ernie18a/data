Calculation: Bullish volume event = close > open AND volume = highest volume over the lookback including the current bar AND volume >= volume SMA × minimum volume multiple AND high > low AND 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body percentage AND RSI of close >= minimum RSI AND RSI of close <= maximum RSI.
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > shorter-period EMA of close AND close > longer-period EMA of close AND shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close.
Calculation: Confirmed setup = bullish volume event AND bullish trend AND (breakout filter disabled OR close > highest high over the breakout lookback excluding the current bar) AND (ATR filter disabled OR high − low <= ATR × maximum range multiple) AND (risk filter disabled OR (high > 0 AND 100 × (high − low) / high <= maximum risk percentage)) AND (no earlier confirmed setup OR bars since the previous confirmed setup >= cooldown).
Calculation: Latest setup levels = on each confirmed setup, replace the stored setup bar, entry level with its high, stop level with its low, and each of two target levels with its high + (its high − its low) × that target's risk-reward multiplier.
Long: Bullish volume event AND bullish trend.
Long: Bullish volume event AND NOT bullish trend AND highest high over the reversal lookback including the current bar > lowest low over the same lookback AND close <= lowest low over that lookback + (highest high − lowest low over that lookback) × near-low fraction.
Long: Current bar is later than the latest confirmed setup bar AND high >= latest setup entry level AND no earlier entry-level touch has occurred since that setup.
Exit: long, current bar is later than the latest confirmed setup bar AND low <= latest setup stop level AND no earlier stop-level touch has occurred since that setup.
Exit: long, current bar is later than the latest confirmed setup bar AND high >= either latest setup target level AND no earlier touch of that same target level has occurred since that setup.
