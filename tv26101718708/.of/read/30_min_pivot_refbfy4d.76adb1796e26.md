Calculation: Higher-timeframe averages = the short and long EMAs of close through the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the higher timeframe is above the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Support buffer = a multiple of ATR from the higher-timeframe bar still forming; if unavailable, use the greater of chart-timeframe ATR and a fraction of chart close.
Calculation: Support test = short higher-timeframe EMA minus support buffer <= low <= short EMA plus a fraction of the buffer OR low < short EMA minus buffer AND close >= short EMA minus buffer OR long higher-timeframe EMA minus buffer <= low <= long EMA plus a fraction of the buffer AND (low < short EMA OR short EMA <= long EMA) OR low < long EMA minus buffer AND close >= long EMA minus buffer OR lower EMA minus buffer <= close <= higher EMA plus buffer OR current session low < previous completed higher-timeframe low AND previous completed higher-timeframe low <= close <= previous low plus a fraction of the buffer.
Calculation: Resistance = the smallest enabled chart-timeframe long EMA or short, medium, or long SMA that is above the setup high or has close below it, combined with the first available of the higher-timeframe rolling short-lookback high or longer-lookback high that is above the setup high; both rolling highs include the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Setup = no active position or pending setup AND chart bar is in the trading session and before the closing bar AND short higher-timeframe EMA >= a fraction of long higher-timeframe EMA AND support test AND close > open AND (previous close < previous open OR low <= either chart-timeframe EMA <= close OR support test OR the bar is not the first session bar) AND close >= each enabled resistance moving average AND (resistance filter disabled OR resistance unavailable OR resistance - high >= a risk multiple of high - low) AND (volume filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close >= open OR higher-timeframe volume <= a multiple of its volume SMA), using the higher-timeframe bar still forming for volume and its SMA.
Calculation: Pending setup = a setup whose high and low are saved as the entry trigger and initial stop; cancel before checking a fill when bars since setup >= timeout OR open > entry trigger + a multiple of initial risk OR open <= initial stop OR low <= initial stop AND open < entry trigger OR optional end-of-session expiration is enabled and the session ends or changes. Initial risk is the greater of setup high - setup low and a minimum risk parameter.
Calculation: Targets = on setup, first target = entry trigger + a risk multiple of setup high - setup low; second target = the greater of entry trigger + a larger risk multiple and the first available higher-timeframe rolling short-lookback high above entry, longer-lookback high above entry, or entry plus a fallback risk multiple, capped by the enabled moving-average resistance if available. Both rolling highs include the higher-timeframe bar still forming. If the fill opens above the trigger, use open as entry price, recompute risk as entry price - initial stop, and recompute both targets with the same rules but without the moving-average cap.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial stop until the first target is reached; then entry price on that bar, and on subsequent bars the greatest of the previous stop, entry price, and the selected short EMA. The selected EMA is either the chart-timeframe EMA or the higher-timeframe EMA through the last completed bar.
Long: Setup AND (setup-count limit disabled OR setup count since the last eligible wave reset < maximum setup count) AND current bar is after the last exit bar; reset the setup count when no position or setup is pending and high > the higher-timeframe rolling short-lookback high including the bar still forming, or on a fresh long higher-timeframe EMA test after a specified number of bars without such a test or after a new session opens more than one buffer away from that EMA.
Long: A pending setup exists AND the current bar is after its setup bar AND no cancellation condition is met AND high >= entry trigger; entry price = the greater of open and entry trigger.
Exit: long, high >= first target; exit a partial position and update the trailing stop.
Exit: long, high >= second target; exit the remaining position, taking precedence over stop exits on the same bar.
Exit: long, second target not reached AND ((first target not reached AND (low <= initial stop OR open <= initial stop)) OR (first target reached AND ((opening-bar trail filter disabled OR bar is not the first session bar) AND close < trailing stop OR current bar is after the fill bar AND open <= the stop in effect at the start of the bar))).
Exit: long, optional end-of-session expiration enabled AND the closing session bar is reached AND neither target nor stop has closed the position.
