Calculation: Confirmed swing highs and lows = highs or lows at pivots with equal numbers of chart bars on each side; recognize each pivot only when its right-side bars have completed. Compare each newly confirmed pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type.
Calculation: Supply and demand zones = for each confirmed swing high or low respectively, find the first existing same-type zone containing the pivot and increment its rejection count; otherwise create a zone from pivot × (1 − zone buffer) to pivot × (1 + zone buffer) with count one. A zone is confirmed when its count reaches the minimum rejection threshold. A close is in a zone when it is within either boundary, inclusive.
Calculation: Upward structure break = close > second-most-recent confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same high. Downward structure break = close < second-most-recent confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same low.
Calculation: Upward retest = an upward structure break has occurred since the previous upward retest AND low <= current second-most-recent confirmed swing high × (1 + zone buffer) AND close > that high. Downward retest = a downward structure break has occurred since the previous downward retest AND high >= current second-most-recent confirmed swing low × (1 − zone buffer) AND close < that low. Set the corresponding pending-break state before evaluating the retest, including on the break bar.
Calculation: Long score = count of true conditions among latest swing high > preceding swing high, latest swing low > preceding swing low, close in confirmed demand zone, upward structure break, upward retest, close > calendar-day anchored VWAP of typical price, short EMA > long EMA, and RSI > midpoint, with the two swing comparisons counted together as one condition. Short score = count of true conditions among latest swing high < preceding swing high, latest swing low < preceding swing low, close in confirmed supply zone, downward structure break, downward retest, close < calendar-day anchored VWAP of typical price, short EMA < long EMA, and RSI < midpoint, with the two swing comparisons counted together as one condition.
Calculation: Signal state = initially neither direction is active. A full signal activates its direction; clear that direction when its score < minimum score − score-reset offset. Full signals require both directions inactive before either signal is evaluated; evaluate both signals before updating state. Each direction has its own cooldown since its last full signal, with a longer cooldown on shorter chart timeframes. Consecutive tracked stop losses lock full signals at the loss limit; a tracked target win or calendar-day change resets the count and lock. Evaluate the signal before updating tracked exits and the calendar-day reset.
Calculation: Tracked trade levels = on the bar after a full signal, if no tracked trade is active, enter at open and fix risk distance as that bar's ATR × stop multiplier. The long stop and target are entry minus risk distance and entry plus a larger target multiple of risk distance; the short stop and target are entry plus risk distance and entry minus the same target multiple of risk distance. Check the stop before the target, including on the entry bar.
Calculation: Displayed trade levels = on a full long signal, reference price = signal high and stop = signal low − signal-bar ATR × stop multiplier; on a full short signal, reference price = signal low and stop = signal high + signal-bar ATR × stop multiplier. Risk distance is the absolute difference between reference price and stop. The partial target, stop-to-entry trigger, and full target are successive increasing multiples of risk distance above the long reference price or below the short reference price. Use the shorter-chart-timeframe stop multiplier where applicable.
Long: Newly confirmed swing high > preceding swing high OR newly confirmed swing low > preceding swing low.
Short: Newly confirmed swing high <= preceding swing high OR newly confirmed swing low <= preceding swing low.
Long: Latest confirmed swing high > preceding swing high AND latest confirmed swing low > preceding swing low.
Short: Latest confirmed swing high < preceding swing high AND latest confirmed swing low < preceding swing low.
Long: Absolute candle body > body-size multiplier × SMA of absolute candle body AND close > open.
Short: Absolute candle body > body-size multiplier × SMA of absolute candle body AND close < open.
Long: Upward structure break = true.
Short: Downward structure break = true.
Long: Close is in a confirmed demand zone AND previous close was not in a confirmed demand zone as evaluated on the previous bar.
Short: Close is in a confirmed supply zone AND previous close was not in a confirmed supply zone as evaluated on the previous bar.
Long: Low < lowest low over the preceding lookback bars AND close > that lowest low.
Short: High > highest high over the preceding lookback bars AND close < that highest high.
Long: Close > calendar-day anchored VWAP of typical price OR short EMA > long EMA OR RSI > midpoint.
Short: Close <= calendar-day anchored VWAP of typical price OR short EMA <= long EMA OR RSI <= midpoint.
Long: Long score >= minimum score AND both signal states inactive AND consecutive tracked stop losses < loss limit AND bars since previous full long signal > long cooldown OR long score >= minimum score AND both signal states inactive AND consecutive tracked stop losses < loss limit AND no previous full long signal exists.
Short: Short score >= minimum score AND both signal states inactive AND consecutive tracked stop losses < loss limit AND bars since previous full short signal > short cooldown OR short score >= minimum score AND both signal states inactive AND consecutive tracked stop losses < loss limit AND no previous full short signal exists.
Exit: long, low <= tracked long stop OR high >= tracked long target.
Exit: short, high >= tracked short stop OR low <= tracked short target.
Exit: long, low <= displayed long stop OR high >= displayed long partial target OR high >= displayed long full target OR high >= displayed long stop-to-entry trigger AND low <= entry price after the stop has moved to entry.
Exit: short, high >= displayed short stop OR low <= displayed short partial target OR low <= displayed short full target OR low <= displayed short stop-to-entry trigger AND high >= entry price after the stop has moved to entry.
