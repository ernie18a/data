Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high higher than the highs of the specified number of bars before and after it; confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows of those bars
Calculation: Long pullback zone = impulse high − (impulse high − impulse low) × long retracement, through impulse high − (impulse high − impulse low) × short retracement; invalidation level = impulse high − (impulse high − impulse low) × invalidation retracement
Calculation: Short pullback zone = impulse low + (impulse high − impulse low) × short retracement, through impulse low + (impulse high − impulse low) × long retracement; invalidation level = impulse low + (impulse high − impulse low) × invalidation retracement
Calculation: Initial long stop = the nearer of the swing low and entry close minus ATR × stop multiplier, capped at entry close minus ATR × maximum stop multiplier when enabled; initial risk = entry close − initial stop; first and second targets = entry close + initial risk × first and second reward multiples
Calculation: Initial short stop = the nearer of the swing high and entry close plus ATR × stop multiplier, capped at entry close plus ATR × maximum stop multiplier when enabled; initial risk = initial stop − entry close; first and second targets = entry close − initial risk × first and second reward multiples
Long: Flat position AND cooldown elapsed AND last completed higher-timeframe close > its longer-lookback EMA AND ATR / its longer-lookback SMA baseline >= expansion threshold AND close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high (with the configured ATR buffer when enabled) AND a long setup is armed by that break AND low <= long pullback zone upper bound AND high >= long pullback zone lower bound AND close > shorter-lookback EMA AND close > previous bar high (or close > open when strong confirmation is disabled) AND close is between the shorter- and longer-lookback chart-timeframe EMAs AND close > chart-timeframe EMA when that filter is enabled AND low has not crossed below the long invalidation level
Short: Flat position AND cooldown elapsed AND last completed higher-timeframe close < its longer-lookback EMA AND ATR / its longer-lookback SMA baseline >= expansion threshold AND close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low (with the configured ATR buffer when enabled) AND a short setup is armed by that break AND high >= short pullback zone lower bound AND low <= short pullback zone upper bound AND close < shorter-lookback EMA AND close < previous bar low (or close < open when strong confirmation is disabled) AND close is between the shorter- and longer-lookback chart-timeframe EMAs AND close < chart-timeframe EMA when that filter is enabled AND high has not crossed above the short invalidation level
Exit: Long, high >= first target (close the configured partial position) OR high >= second target OR low <= stop
Exit: Short, low <= first target (close the configured partial position) OR low <= second target OR high >= stop
