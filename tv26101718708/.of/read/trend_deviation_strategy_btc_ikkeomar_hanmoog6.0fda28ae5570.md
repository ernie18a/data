Calculation: +DI and −DI = 100 × Wilder-smoothed positive and negative directional movement, respectively, divided by Wilder-smoothed true range; positive movement is the upward high change when it exceeds the downward low change and zero, otherwise; negative movement is the downward low change when it exceeds the upward high change and zero, otherwise.
Calculation: Momentum = close − close from the momentum lookback bars ago; momentum change = current momentum − previous momentum.
Calculation: MACD = EMA(close, shorter lookback) − EMA(close, longer lookback); signal line = EMA(MACD, signal lookback).
Calculation: STC = stochastic normalization of MACD over the STC lookback, smoothed recursively; apply the same stochastic normalization and recursive smoothing a second time. MACD uses a shorter fast EMA lookback and longer slow EMA lookback.
Long: datetime >= configured start date AND +DI crosses above −DI AND hlc3 < upper Bollinger Band AND (momentum > 0 AND momentum change > 0 OR MACD > signal line OR Supertrend changes to uptrend OR (STC[3] >= STC[2] AND STC[2] <= STC[1] AND STC < 60) OR STC < 25 OR Aroon lower > Aroon upper OR Aroon upper crosses above Aroon lower)
Short: datetime >= configured start date AND −DI crosses above +DI AND hlc3 > lower Bollinger Band AND (momentum < 0 AND momentum change < 0 OR MACD < signal line OR Supertrend changes to downtrend OR (STC[3] <= STC[2] AND STC[2] >= STC[1] AND STC > 40) OR STC > 75 OR Aroon lower < Aroon upper OR Aroon upper crosses below Aroon lower)
