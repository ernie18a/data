Calculation: Candle geometry = body = abs(close − open); range = max(high − low, minimum price increment); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; close location = (close − low) / range; body fraction = body / range. The minimum price increment is a configurable parameter.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = any enabled pattern satisfying: hammer: lower wick >= body × primary-wick ratio AND upper wick <= body × opposite-wick ratio AND close location >= hammer threshold; engulfing: close > open AND previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open; pin: lower wick >= range × pin-wick fraction AND close location >= pin-close threshold AND body fraction <= pin-body threshold; outside reversal: high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close > open AND close > (high + low) / 2; morning star: close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND previous body <= previous high-low range × star-body fraction AND close > open AND close >= midpoint of open and close two bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = any enabled pattern satisfying: shooting star: upper wick >= body × primary-wick ratio AND lower wick <= body × opposite-wick ratio AND close location <= shooting-star threshold; engulfing: close < open AND previous close > previous open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open; pin: upper wick >= range × pin-wick fraction AND close location <= pin-close threshold AND body fraction <= pin-body threshold; outside reversal: high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close < open AND close < (high + low) / 2; evening star: close two bars ago > open two bars ago AND previous body <= previous high-low range × star-body fraction AND close < open AND close <= midpoint of open and close two bars ago. Use the corresponding bullish and bearish geometry thresholds as complementary thresholds where applicable.
Calculation: Pattern direction = bullish if any bullish pattern is present, otherwise bearish if any bearish pattern is present, otherwise neutral. Pattern quality = clamp to the score range the weighted sum of dominant wick / range, directional close location, and 1 − body fraction. Dominant wick is lower for bullish and upper for bearish; directional close location is close location for bullish and 1 − close location for bearish. For neutral direction both directional terms are zero.
Calculation: Candidate level = low for bullish direction and high for bearish direction. Its enabled key levels are the corresponding low or high of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, the developing daily-session low or high, the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low or high, session VWAP, the nearest configured round number, and up to two configured manual levels. A pivot is confirmed only after its configured right-side bars. The nearest round number is the configured increment multiplied by close divided by that increment and rounded to the nearest integer. Key-level confluence is the count of enabled, available levels within a configured ATR distance of the candidate level; the candidate is away from key levels when that count is zero. Adjusted quality = clamp to the score range pattern quality multiplied by the configured away-from-level penalty factor when away, otherwise by one, plus a configured bonus for each nearby level beyond the first. For neutral direction the candidate is not away and confluence is zero.
Calculation: Trap score = clamp to the score range the sum of configured weights for applicable conditions: close is within the configured middle zone of its rolling high-low range; the latest confirmed pivot low is below a bullish candidate low, or pivot high above a bearish candidate high, within a configured ATR distance; close is within a configured ATR distance of the nearest round number AND bullish low is at or above it or bearish high is at or below it; relative volume, volume z-score, and true-range z-score each meet their respective thresholds; the count of opposing higher-timeframe trends meets its threshold; the candidate level has at least the configured number of prior touches; and the candidate is away from key levels. Add an extra configured weight for each opposing higher-timeframe trend beyond the first. Relative volume is volume divided by its moving average; z-scores use the moving mean and standard deviation of volume and true range respectively. Count touches by scanning prior bars from newest to oldest: a bar touches when its low <= candidate level + current ATR × touch tolerance AND its high >= candidate level − current ATR × touch tolerance; count a touch only if it is separated from the previously counted touch by at least the configured number of bars.
Calculation: Opposing higher-timeframe trend count = number of three successively longer higher timeframes whose last completed bar has fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA for a bullish candidate, or fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA for a bearish candidate. Each EMA is calculated on its own higher timeframe; fast has a shorter lookback than slow. The higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar in historical backtests.
Calculation: Candidate state = process each chart bar in order. Before changing state, record failure if an active bullish candidate has close < its stored low − current ATR × failure buffer, or an active bearish candidate has close > its stored high + current ATR × failure buffer. Deactivate an active candidate if its age exceeds the evaluation window, or bullish close > its stored high, or bearish close < its stored low. Then, if no candidate is active AND adjusted quality >= minimum quality AND either the high-risk-only option is disabled or current trap score >= probable-trap threshold, create a candidate and store its direction, bar, high, low, and trap score. This creation test also applies to neutral direction. After creation, if the previously recorded failure is true, deactivate the current candidate. Use the current candidate's stored direction and score for failure markings, including when a new candidate was created on the same bar.
Long: A bullish candidate is created.
Short: A bearish candidate is created.
Short: A bullish candidate is created AND its trap score >= warning threshold.
Long: A bearish candidate is created AND its trap score >= warning threshold.
Short: Recorded candidate failure is true AND the current stored candidate direction is bullish AND its stored trap score >= confirmed-trap threshold.
Long: Recorded candidate failure is true AND the current stored candidate direction is bearish AND its stored trap score >= confirmed-trap threshold.
Short: Recorded candidate failure is true AND the current stored candidate direction is bullish AND its stored trap score < confirmed-trap threshold AND low-score failure markings are enabled.
Long: Recorded candidate failure is true AND the current stored candidate direction is bearish AND its stored trap score < confirmed-trap threshold AND low-score failure markings are enabled.
