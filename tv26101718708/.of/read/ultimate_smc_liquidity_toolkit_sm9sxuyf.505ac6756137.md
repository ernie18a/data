Calculation: Confirmed swing high = the high of a bar that is greater than or equal to each high in an equal-length preceding window and greater than each high in the following window; it becomes available when the following window completes.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = the low of a bar that is less than or equal to each low in an equal-length preceding window and less than each low in the following window; it becomes available when the following window completes.
Calculation: Unbroken swing high or low = the latest confirmed swing of that type, replaced immediately when a new swing of that type is confirmed; it remains unbroken until the first close above that swing high or below that swing low, respectively.
Long: low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago
Long: close > latest unbroken swing high
Short: high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago
Short: close < latest unbroken swing low
