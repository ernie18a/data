Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the same pivot-length number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars have completed. Use the most recently confirmed pivot of each side.
Calculation: Break setup = when no setup is active and the cooldown since the last resolved or expired setup has elapsed, break above the most recent unused pivot high or below the most recent unused pivot low by more than a configurable multiple of current ATR. Use either close or the directional extreme, according to the confirmation setting. If both breaks occur on the same bar, select the upward break. Save the pivot level, direction, break bar and ATR at the break; mark that pivot as used for its direction. A setup resolves on its first retest or expires when its retest window is exceeded; start the cooldown on that bar.
Calculation: First retest = the first bar after the break and within the maximum retest window whose low is at or below the saved level plus the retest band AND whose high is at or above the saved level minus the retest band. The band is the larger of break-bar ATR times a configurable tolerance and a configurable positive price floor.
Calculation: Retest score = sum of the following individually rounded components, each clamped before multiplication to the interval from 0 to 1. Let direction d be +1 for an upward break and -1 for a downward break, level L be the saved pivot, A be break-bar ATR floored at a configurable positive price floor, R be high minus low floored at that price floor, E be low for an upward break and high for a downward break, and n be bars since the break. Reclaim = 25 × clamp(d × (close − L) / (0.60 × A)); rejection = 20 × clamp((directional wick / R + 0.20 if d × (close − open) > 0 else directional wick / R + 0 + 0.20 × abs(close − open) / R) / 1.20), with the body term added in both cases and directional wick equal to min(open, close) − low for an upward break or high − max(open, close) for a downward break; depth = 20 × clamp(1 − max(d × (L − E), 0) / (0.80 × A)); speed = 15 × clamp(1 − (n − 1) / max(maximum retest window − 1, 1)); volatility = 10 × clamp(1 − max(high − low − A, 0) / (2 × A)); volume = 10 when volume confirmation is disabled, otherwise 10 × clamp(volume / max(volume SMA, 1) / 1.50).
Long: An upward break setup is created.
Short: A downward break setup is created.

Short: First retest of a downward break AND (retest score >= configurable display threshold OR (retest score >= configurable confirmation threshold AND close <= saved level)).
