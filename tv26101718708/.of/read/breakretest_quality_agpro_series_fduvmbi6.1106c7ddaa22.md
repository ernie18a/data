Calculation: ATR = Wilder average of true range over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars on both sides; usable only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Retest quality score = rounded sum, capped per component: reclaim (25 × clamp(close reclaim distance / (0.60 × break-bar ATR), 0, 1)); rejection (20 × clamp((directional wick / range + 0.20 if candle closes in the break direction + 0.20 × body / range) / 1.20, 0, 1)); depth (20 × clamp(1 − adverse extreme overshoot / (0.80 × break-bar ATR), 0, 1)); speed (15 × clamp(1 − (bars since break − 1) / max(maximum retest bars − 1, 1), 0, 1)); volatility (10 × clamp(1 − max(bar range − ATR, 0) / (2 × ATR), 0, 1)); and volume (10 by default, or 10 × clamp((volume / 20-bar SMA of volume) / 1.50, 0, 1) when volume confirmation is enabled). Use chart-timeframe bars; the retest score uses the ATR captured on the break bar.
Long: no active setup, cooldown elapsed, and an unused confirmed pivot high is broken by more than the configured minimum ATR distance (close above the level if close confirmation is enabled, otherwise high above); OR the first bar within the configured post-break window whose range intersects the break level’s ATR-scaled tolerance band has a score at least the display threshold; OR that retest score meets the confirmation-grade threshold and close >= the broken level.
Short: no active setup, cooldown elapsed, and an unused confirmed pivot low is broken by more than the configured minimum ATR distance (close below the level if close confirmation is enabled, otherwise low below); OR the first bar within the configured post-break window whose range intersects the break level’s ATR-scaled tolerance band has a score at least the display threshold; OR that retest score meets the confirmation-grade threshold and close <= the broken level.
