Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled weekday session and optional pre-session window, independently record the highest high and lowest low of completed lower-timeframe bars from the window open through a configurable range duration; use chart bars when the chart timeframe is at or below that lower timeframe. Maintain a shorter and a longer range. A range is complete when a bar at or beyond its duration is observed; reset its state at the next window open. A session range is live during its session; a pre-session range is live during its pre-session window and its parent session.
Calculation: Break confirmation = choose chart high and low, chart close, or reference close. Reference close is the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close when the chart timeframe is below the reference timeframe, the last completed chart bar's close when equal, and the previous chart bar's close sampled when a new reference-timeframe bucket begins when the chart timeframe is higher. Retain the sampled close until the next bucket.
Long: A live, completed shorter or longer range has no previous break in this occurrence AND its selected break-confirmation value for the upper side > its high.
Short: A live, completed shorter or longer range has no previous break in this occurrence AND its selected break-confirmation value for the upper side <= its high AND its selected break-confirmation value for the lower side < its low.
Long: A live, completed longer range previously broke down first AND has not yet broken both sides AND its selected break-confirmation value for the upper side > its high.
Short: A live, completed longer range previously broke up first AND has not yet broken both sides AND its selected break-confirmation value for the lower side < its low.
Long: A live, completed longer range has not previously received an upper-side chart-close mark in this occurrence AND chart close > its high.
Short: A live, completed longer range has not previously received a lower-side chart-close mark in this occurrence AND chart close < its low.
Long: Chart timeframe <= reference timeframe AND a live, completed longer range has not previously received an upper-side reference-close mark in this occurrence AND reference close > its high.
Short: Chart timeframe <= reference timeframe AND a live, completed longer range has not previously received a lower-side reference-close mark in this occurrence AND reference close < its low.
