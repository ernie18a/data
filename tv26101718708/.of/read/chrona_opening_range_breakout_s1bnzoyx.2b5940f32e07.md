Calculation: Short opening range = highest high and lowest low of completed lower-timeframe bars during the session’s shorter opening interval.
Calculation: Long opening range = highest high and lowest low of completed lower-timeframe bars during the session’s longer opening interval; the interval is longer than the short opening range.
Long: An enabled session is live and the short or long opening range is complete, AND the selected confirmation price (intrabar high, chart close, or last completed 5-minute close) is above that range’s high, with no prior break of that range’s high; OR the long opening range is complete and live and the last completed 5-minute close or chart close is above its high and has not previously been marked; OR the long opening range’s first break was below its low and the selected confirmation price is now above its high.
Short: An enabled session is live and the short or long opening range is complete, AND the selected confirmation price (intrabar low, chart close, or last completed 5-minute close) is below that range’s low, with no prior break of that range’s low; OR the long opening range is complete and live and the last completed 5-minute close or chart close is below its low and has not previously been marked; OR the long opening range’s first break was above its high and the selected confirmation price is now below its low.
