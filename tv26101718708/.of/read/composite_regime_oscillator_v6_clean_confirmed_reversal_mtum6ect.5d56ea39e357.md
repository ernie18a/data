Calculation: Scale(x, lower, upper) = 100 × clamp((x − lower) / (upper − lower), 0, 1), or 50 if lower = upper; each use has its own scale bounds.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = short-term ATR / long-term ATR. Drawdown = (highest high over the drawdown lookback − close) / that highest high. Range low and range high are the lowest low and highest high over the range lookback. Rebound = (close − range low) / range low. Range position = (close − range low) / (range high − range low), or 0.5 if the range is zero. Distance = (close − EMA of close) / short-term ATR. Distance from range high = (range high − close) / range high. Relative volume = volume / SMA of volume.
Calculation: Regime stress = SMA of a weighted sum of scaled volatility ratio, scaled drawdown, and scaled rebound; use 50 when any of these inputs is unavailable.
Calculation: Location score = SMA of a weighted sum of scaled range position, scaled rebound, and 100 minus scaled distance from range high; use 50 when rebound or distance from range high is unavailable.
Calculation: Bullish overreaction = SMA of a weighted sum of scaled (100 − RSI) and scaled negative distance. Bearish overreaction = SMA of a weighted sum of scaled RSI and scaled distance. Use 50 when RSI is unavailable.
Calculation: Pressure score = SMA of a weighted sum of scaled relative volume and scaled volatility ratio; use 50 when relative volume is unavailable. All component-score SMAs use the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion = EMA of a weighted sum of (100 − location score), bullish overreaction, regime stress, and pressure score. Bearish exhaustion = EMA of a weighted sum of location score, bearish overreaction, (100 − regime stress), and pressure score. Both use the same EMA length.
Calculation: Stretch score = SMA of a weighted sum of scaled absolute distance and scaled absolute (range position − 0.5); use 50 when distance is unavailable. The SMA uses the component-score smoothing length.
Calculation: Composite oscillator = EMA of the weighted average of regime stress, stretch score, location score, bearish overreaction, and pressure score; use 50 for the weighted average when the sum of its weights is zero. Its EMA uses the exhaustion-score EMA length.
Calculation: Falling or rising over a lookback means the current value is respectively below or above every preceding value in that lookback. ADX uses the same length for directional movement and ADX smoothing.
Calculation: Bearish trend block = close < short-term trend EMA AND short-term trend EMA < long-term trend EMA AND short-term trend EMA < its value at a prior slope lookback AND ADX > trend threshold AND ((cooling-required switch = on AND ADX is not falling over the ADX lookback) OR (cooling-required switch = off AND ADX is rising over that lookback)). Bullish trend block = close > short-term trend EMA AND short-term trend EMA > long-term trend EMA AND short-term trend EMA > its value at the same prior slope lookback AND ADX > trend threshold AND ((cooling-required switch = on AND ADX is not falling over the ADX lookback) OR (cooling-required switch = off AND ADX is rising over that lookback)).
Calculation: Bullish base = bullish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND bearish trend block = false AND (trigger-confirmation switch = off OR ((close > previous high OR (close > open AND previous close < previous open) OR RSI crosses above its lower reversal threshold OR close is rising over the momentum lookback) AND (EMA-confirmation switch = off OR close crosses above the distance EMA OR close > previous high))).
Calculation: Bearish base = bearish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND bullish trend block = false AND (trigger-confirmation switch = off OR ((close < previous low OR (close < open AND previous close > previous open) OR RSI crosses below its higher reversal threshold OR close is falling over the momentum lookback) AND (EMA-confirmation switch = off OR close crosses below the distance EMA OR close < previous low))).
Long: Bullish base = true AND previous bullish base = false AND (no prior confirmed bullish signal exists OR bars since the last confirmed bullish signal > cooldown).
Short: Bearish base = true AND previous bearish base = false AND (no prior confirmed bearish signal exists OR bars since the last confirmed bearish signal > cooldown).
Long: Bullish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND bearish trend block = true.
Short: Bearish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND bullish trend block = true.
Long: Composite oscillator < extreme oversold threshold.
Short: Composite oscillator >= extreme overbought threshold.
