Calculation: Detection bars = chart-timeframe bars, or bars aggregated to an independently selected higher timeframe using the last completed bar, or lower timeframe using the last completed intrabar. Evaluate each enabled detection timeframe independently.
Calculation: Swing state = initially upper; on each detection bar, set it to upper if the high a lookback ago is greater than every high since that bar, otherwise set it to lower if the low a lookback ago is less than every low since that bar, otherwise retain it. When the state changes to upper or lower, respectively record that earlier bar's high or low as the latest swing of that type and mark it uncrossed.
Calculation: Bullish volumized zone = the low and high of the bar with the lowest low strictly between the latest upper swing bar and the current bar; on tied lows use the oldest tied bar. Bearish volumized zone = the low and high of the bar with the highest high strictly between the latest lower swing bar and the current bar; on tied highs use the oldest tied bar. If there is no intervening bar, use the previous bar's high as both bullish bounds, or its low and high as the bearish bounds.
Calculation: Bullish ICT zone = the range of the most recent bearish candle strictly between the latest upper swing bar and the current bar, optionally extended backward over its contiguous bearish candle sequence within that interval. Bearish ICT zone = the corresponding range of the most recent bullish candle and optionally its contiguous bullish sequence.
Calculation: Swing consumption = in volumized mode, mark a swing crossed when its close-break condition occurs, even if its zone fails the size filter; in ICT mode, mark it crossed when its close-break, displacement, optional gap, and opposing-candle conditions occur, even if its zone fails the size filter. A newly recorded swing is uncrossed.
Long: Detection-bar close > latest upper swing high AND upper swing is uncrossed AND ((volumized mode AND bullish volumized zone height <= ATR times a multiplier) OR (ICT mode AND absolute candle body >= ATR times a displacement multiplier AND (gap confirmation disabled OR current low > high two bars ago) AND an opposing candle exists AND bullish ICT zone height <= ATR times a zone-size multiplier)).
Short: Detection-bar close < latest lower swing low AND lower swing is uncrossed AND ((volumized mode AND bearish volumized zone height <= ATR times a multiplier) OR (ICT mode AND absolute candle body >= ATR times a displacement multiplier AND (gap confirmation disabled OR current high < low two bars ago) AND an opposing candle exists AND bearish ICT zone height <= ATR times a zone-size multiplier)).
