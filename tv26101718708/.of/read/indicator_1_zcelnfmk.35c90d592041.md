Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the same number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-hand bars have completed. Track the latest two confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= the previous bar's latest confirmed swing high, using the current swing high if the previous one is unavailable. Bearish structure break = close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= the previous bar's latest confirmed swing low, using the current swing low if the previous one is unavailable. Structure state starts neutral and changes to bullish on a bullish break or bearish on a bearish break. A bullish or bearish character change is the corresponding break when the previous state was bearish or bullish, respectively.
Calculation: Structural uptrend = latest confirmed swing high > preceding confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing low > preceding confirmed swing low. Structural downtrend = latest confirmed swing high < preceding confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing low < preceding confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the range lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Normalized entropy = (raw entropy - its rolling minimum) / (its rolling maximum - its rolling minimum + epsilon), where raw entropy = standard deviation of absolute log close returns / (SMA of absolute log close returns + epsilon). Compression is normalized entropy < compression threshold; expansion is normalized entropy > 1 - compression threshold.
Calculation: Bullish phase = at least three of close > fastest EMA, fastest EMA > second EMA, second EMA > third EMA, and third EMA > slowest EMA, AND close > second EMA. Bearish phase reverses every comparison. All EMAs use close and have progressively longer lengths.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / (volume SMA + epsilon). Volume compression = volume ratio < compression threshold. Bullish volume resonance = volume ratio > surge threshold AND volume > previous volume times a surge multiplier AND close > open AND the minimum volume ratio over a preceding lookback, excluding the current bar, < compression threshold. Bearish resonance replaces close > open with close < open.
Calculation: Bullish footprint = abs(close - open) / (high - low + epsilon) > body threshold AND close > open AND (close - low) / (high - low + epsilon) > upper close-position threshold AND volume > volume SMA. Bearish footprint uses close < open and close position < lower close-position threshold.
Calculation: Velocity = (close - close at a momentum lookback) / (ATR + epsilon). Bullish acceleration = velocity > 0 AND velocity > previous velocity AND previous velocity > velocity two bars ago. Bearish acceleration reverses the comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = lowest low over the sweep-age lookback including the current bar < lowest low over the sweep-level lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > that previous-bar sweep-level low AND close > open.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the high and low of the most recent bearish candle within the order-block lookback, at least two bars ago, whose immediately following candle is bullish, has body >= an impulse multiplier times current ATR, and has volume >= its own volume SMA. It is valid when current close > its low. Bearish order block reverses candle directions and is valid when current close < its high.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago AND close > open; its lower edge is the high two bars ago. Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish score = one point each for entropy compression, bullish phase, volume compression OR bullish volume resonance, bullish footprint, bullish acceleration, enabled bullish sweep, and enabled higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA; two points for bullish character change; and one point when a bullish fair value gap has its lower edge within a valid bullish order block. Higher-timeframe EMAs use higher-timeframe closes and, on historical bars, the last completed higher-timeframe bar; a newly completed value becomes available on its final chart bar.
Calculation: Bearish exit score = one point each for entropy expansion, bearish phase, volume compression OR bearish volume resonance, bearish footprint, and bearish acceleration.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 times log10(sum of true range over the choppiness lookback / (highest high - lowest low over that lookback + epsilon)) / log10(the lookback). Hard block = choppiness filter enabled AND choppiness > hard threshold. Soft warning = filter enabled AND choppiness > soft threshold AND hard block is false. The hard threshold is higher than the soft threshold.
Calculation: Entry score threshold = base threshold minus a character-change adjustment when bullish character change occurs without a structural uptrend, plus a soft-warning adjustment when soft warning is true. Compare both current and previous bullish scores with the current threshold.
Calculation: Initial stop = valid bullish order-block low minus ATR times a stop-buffer multiplier, fixed at the entry signal bar. Initial risk = signal-bar close - initial stop. First and second targets = signal-bar close + initial risk times their respective reward multipliers; the second multiplier is larger. Fix both targets at the entry signal bar. The breakeven reference is the signal-bar close.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Bullish fair value gap.
Short: Bearish fair value gap.
Long: Valid bullish order block.
Short: Valid bearish order block.
Short: Structural downtrend AND hard block = false.
Long: Bullish score >= current entry score threshold AND previous bullish score < current entry score threshold AND hard block = false AND structure state = bullish AND close < range midpoint AND RSI < overbought threshold AND bars since the last buy signal >= cooldown AND position side = flat AND (geometry filter disabled OR (bullish order block valid AND initial risk / ATR >= minimum risk threshold AND initial risk / ATR <= maximum risk threshold)).
Exit: long, bearish exit score >= exit threshold OR bearish character change OR (close > range midpoint AND bearish footprint).
Exit: long, before the first target is reached, low <= initial stop.
Exit: long, after high first reaches the first target, low <= breakeven reference OR high >= second target.
Exit: long, bars since entry signal >= time-stop threshold AND first target has not been reached.
