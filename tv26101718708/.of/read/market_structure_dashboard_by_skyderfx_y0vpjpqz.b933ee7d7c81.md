Calculation: Swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; confirm after the bars on the right complete.
Calculation: Structure direction = maintain the latest confirmed swing high and low and a direction state initially neutral. A close (or, if wick breaks are enabled, a high/low) above an unbroken swing high sets direction bullish; below an unbroken swing low sets it bearish. Each swing level triggers at most once, until replaced by a new pivot. Swing type is higher high/lower high or higher low/lower low versus the previous pivot of the same kind. Filtered structure direction is neutral when direction is neutral, or when mixed-swing filtering is enabled and both swing types exist but disagree; otherwise it equals structure direction.
Calculation: Timeframe inputs = apply the structure calculation separately to the chart/entry timeframe and four higher-timeframe series. For each higher-timeframe series, use its last completed bar. For the chart/entry timeframe, use the forming bar when it matches the chart timeframe, the last completed bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe, and the latest available bar when it is lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction score = sum of the four higher-timeframe filtered structure directions, with bullish = 1, bearish = -1, neutral = 0.
Long: any displayed timeframe's filtered structure direction = 1 OR higher-timeframe direction score > 0
Short: any displayed timeframe's filtered structure direction = -1 OR higher-timeframe direction score < 0
