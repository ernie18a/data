Calculation: Heikin Ashi streak = number of consecutive Heikin Ashi bars with close > open since the most recent bar with close <= open, or with close < open since the most recent bar with close >= open, according to the selected direction; undefined until the corresponding opposite or equal bar has occurred.
Short: Position side = flat AND (date filter disabled OR bar datetime >= start datetime AND bar datetime <= end datetime) AND (RSI > threshold OR RSI < threshold, according to the selected comparison) AND Heikin Ashi streak >= streak threshold. RSI and Heikin Ashi use independently selected timeframes; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar, and for the chart timeframe use the completed chart bar.
Exit: short, low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage).
