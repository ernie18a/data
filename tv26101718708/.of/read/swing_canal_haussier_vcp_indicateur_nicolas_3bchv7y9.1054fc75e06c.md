Calculation: Valid ascending parallel channel = use the latest two confirmed pivot lows and latest two confirmed pivot highs; calculate each line’s price change per bar, require both slopes to be positive and their absolute difference divided by the lower-line slope to be within a tolerance, then require the extrapolated lower line to be below the extrapolated upper line.
Calculation: Near lower channel = absolute distance from close to the extrapolated lower channel line is within either a percentage of close or a multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Volume weakening = short-lookback average volume is below long-lookback average volume AND (current volume is below prior-bar volume OR short-lookback average volume is below its prior-bar value).
Calculation: ATR decreasing = ATR is below its moving average AND below its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Contracting range = short-lookback average of high minus low is below the longer-lookback average AND current high-minus-low is below its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Relative strength = instrument close divided by comparison-market close; strong when this ratio is above its moving average AND that average is above its value five bars earlier.
Calculation: Bullish rebound = close is above open AND close is above prior-bar high.
Calculation: Score = sum 15 for market close above its moving average, 20 for a valid ascending parallel channel, 15 for near lower channel, 10 for RSI within its specified bounds, 10 for short-lookback average volume below long-lookback average volume, 5 for volume decreasing, 10 for ATR decreasing, 10 for contracting range, and 15 for strong relative strength; cap the sum at 100.
Long: capped score is at least its threshold AND market close is above its moving average AND valid ascending parallel channel AND near lower channel AND RSI is above its minimum and below its maximum AND volume weakening AND ATR decreasing AND strong relative strength AND bullish rebound AND earnings filter passes (disabled, or configured earnings date is more than the configured minimum days away, or has passed).
