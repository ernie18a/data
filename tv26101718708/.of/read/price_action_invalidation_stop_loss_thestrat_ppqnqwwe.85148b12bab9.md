Calculation: Reference timeframes = three timeframes at or above the chart timeframe: short, medium, and long, ordered from shortest to longest. Use the last completed bar for completed-bar references and the bar still forming for running highs and lows.
Calculation: Long support = the last completed short-timeframe close is greater than the last completed medium-timeframe high OR greater than the last completed long-timeframe high. Short support = the last completed short-timeframe close is less than the last completed medium-timeframe low OR less than the last completed long-timeframe low.
Calculation: Failed upward break = the forming medium- or long-timeframe high is greater than its last completed high AND the last completed short-timeframe close is less than that same completed high. Failed downward break = the forming medium- or long-timeframe low is less than its last completed low AND the last completed short-timeframe close is greater than that same completed low.
Calculation: Long stop = on entering long, the higher of the last completed medium- and long-timeframe lows; on each subsequent long bar, the higher of its previous value and those lows. If a failed upward break is active, also take the higher of the stop and the last completed short-timeframe low. Short stop = on entering short, the lower of the last completed medium- and long-timeframe highs; on each subsequent short bar, the lower of its previous value and those highs. If a failed downward break is active, also take the lower of the stop and the last completed short-timeframe high. Optionally, after these updates, if a forming short-, medium-, or long-timeframe bar has a high above and a low below its own last completed bar, replace the long stop with its running low or the short stop with its running high; choose the shortest such timeframe. Update the stop before testing exits, but do not test an exit on the bar that enters a position.
Long: position is neutral AND long support is true.
Long: position is short AND the forming short-timeframe high is greater than the short stop AND (long support is true OR neutral-state behavior is disabled).
Short: position is neutral AND long support is false AND short support is true.
Short: position is long AND the forming short-timeframe low is less than the long stop AND (short support is true OR neutral-state behavior is disabled).
Exit: long, the forming short-timeframe low is less than the long stop.
Exit: short, the forming short-timeframe high is greater than the short stop.
Long: position after the state update is long AND a medium- or long-timeframe bar has just completed with its close greater than the preceding bar's high AND directional tightening is enabled.
Short: position after the state update is short AND a medium- or long-timeframe bar has just completed with its close less than the preceding bar's low AND directional tightening is enabled.
Long: position after the state update is long AND a failed upward break is active AND it was not active on the preceding chart bar.
Short: position after the state update is short AND a failed downward break is active AND it was not active on the preceding chart bar.
