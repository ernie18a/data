Calculation: K = SMA of Stochastic RSI of close; D = SMA of K, using a separate smoothing period.
Calculation: Divergence state and reference close = initially unset; when K is above the upper extreme, set state to bearish and reference close to the current close; when K is below the lower extreme, set state to bullish and reference close to the current close. While the state is bearish, whenever K crosses below D with K between the midpoint and upper extreme inclusive, compare against the reference close and then replace it with the current close, regardless of the comparison or volume filter. While the state is bullish, whenever K crosses above D with K between the lower extreme and midpoint inclusive, compare against the reference close and then replace it with the current close, regardless of the comparison or volume filter. The state persists until an opposite extreme is reached.
Calculation: Bullish gap level = initially unset; when low minus previous high exceeds a parameterized fraction of the SMA of high minus low, replace the level with the previous high. After checking for a return on each bar, clear the level if low is at or below it.
Calculation: Bearish gap level = initially unset; when previous low minus high exceeds the same fraction of the same SMA of high minus low, replace the level with the previous low. After checking for a return on each bar, clear the level if high is at or above it.
Calculation: Upper extreme reference close = close of the most recent preceding bar with K at or above the upper extreme; lower extreme reference close = close of the most recent preceding bar with K at or below the lower extreme.
Long: Divergence state is bullish AND K is between the lower extreme and midpoint inclusive AND K crosses above D AND close is below the divergence reference close before its update AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume percentile rank is at or above its threshold).
Short: Divergence state is bearish AND K is between the midpoint and upper extreme inclusive AND K crosses below D AND close is above the divergence reference close before its update AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume percentile rank is at or above its threshold).
Long: Bullish gap level is set AND low is at or below the bullish gap level.
Short: Bearish gap level is set AND high is at or above the bearish gap level.
Long: K is at or below the lower extreme AND close is at or below the lower extreme reference close multiplied by one minus a parameterized percentage.
Short: K is at or above the upper extreme AND close is at or above the upper extreme reference close multiplied by one plus the same parameterized percentage.
