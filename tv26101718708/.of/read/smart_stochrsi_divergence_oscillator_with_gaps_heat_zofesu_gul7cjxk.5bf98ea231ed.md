Calculation: StochRSI K = moving average of the stochastic normalization of RSI(close)
Calculation: D = moving average of K
Calculation: Average range = moving average of high − low
Calculation: Volume percentile rank = percentile rank of current volume over its lookback
Long: (K is between the lower extreme and midpoint, inclusive, and K crosses above D, and close is below the stored reference close from the latest bar K was below the lower extreme or the latest qualifying bullish crossover, and the volume filter is disabled or volume percentile rank meets its threshold) OR (a bullish gap was previously detected and low <= its stored boundary) OR (K is at or below the lower extreme, a prior close was recorded while K was at or below the lower extreme, and close <= that prior close × (1 − configured heat distance))
Short: (K is between the midpoint and upper extreme, inclusive, and K crosses below D, and close is above the stored reference close from the latest bar K was above the upper extreme or the latest qualifying bearish crossover, and the volume filter is disabled or volume percentile rank meets its threshold) OR (a bearish gap was previously detected and high >= its stored boundary) OR (K is at or above the upper extreme, a prior close was recorded while K was at or above the upper extreme, and close >= that prior close × (1 + configured heat distance))
