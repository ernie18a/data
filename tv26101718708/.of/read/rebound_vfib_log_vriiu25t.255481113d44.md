Calculation: V geometry = On each chart bar, find the highest high in the lookback and the lowest low in the more recent of the bars since that high or the minimum search length. Update the stored top when the candidate top is newer and either the current low is below the stored bottom or the candidate top and bottom satisfy the minimum top age and minimum bottom age; update the stored bottom when its candidate is newer and distinct from the stored top. Reset the subsequent geometry when either anchor changes. Let B be the bottom, T the top, and L(x) the straight line through their log prices. Let D be the maximum of log(high[x]) - L(x) from T through B, inclusive. The downtrend slope is (log(T price) + D - log(B price)) / (T index - B index). The uptrend slope is the minimum log-price slope from B to any later low through the current chart bar, choosing the most recent low on ties. Convert each slope to a continuously compounded percentage per day using the chart bars per trading day.
Calculation: V-Fib(price) = (log(price) - L(current chart bar index)) / (D / 0.236). Leading gain(price) = 100 times (log(price) - the current value of the log-price line through B with the uptrend slope); use zero when the uptrend slope is zero or unavailable.
Calculation: Technical rating = TradingView Technical Rating total on the chart timeframe, the average of its moving-average and oscillator ratings.
Calculation: Support = Among log-price lines through two historical lows after or at B and within the lookback, with slope greater than the uptrend slope and no historical low below the line from its rounded intersection with the uptrend line or its earlier anchor, whichever starts later, through the preceding chart bar, select the greatest slope. Rebuild candidates when B or the low defining the uptrend slope changes; otherwise add candidates ending at the preceding bar and discard candidates outside the lookback or broken by that bar. Support price is this line's current value. Support gap is the greater of the minimum support trail and the maximum percentage drawdown from a running high to a subsequent low, scanning completed lower-timeframe bars from the selected support's first anchor through the current chart bar, updating the running high before testing each low. Support stop = support price times (1 - support gap); it exists only when close >= support price.
Calculation: Support rebound state = Reset peak, trough and armed state when either V anchor changes. Sequentially scan completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar using their highs and the current chart-bar V geometry. Before updating the peak, arm when V-Fib(high) - trough >= minimum rebound. Update the peak when V-Fib(high) exceeds it; clear the trough if the new peak exceeds the previous peak by more than the minimum rebound. After updating the peak, update the trough to the lowest V-Fib(high) observed while peak - V-Fib(high) >= peak times the minimum pullback. Record the first lower-timeframe high with armed state true and V-Fib(high) > maximum entry V-Fib. Clear the armed state and trough after a support entry.
Calculation: Trend-entry lock = Set on a trend entry; release when the previous chart bar's V-Fib(high) >= unlock threshold or when the current uptrend rate < both the keep-rate threshold and the stored entry uptrend rate plus the exit-rate difference. The entry rate is retained after exit.
Calculation: Trailing stop = At entry, set the initial stop to the greater of entry price times (1 - maximum trend trail) and B price for a trend entry, or the greater of entry price times (1 - maximum support trail) and support stop for a support entry. Fix the gap as (entry price - initial stop) / entry price. Initialize the running high to entry price; on each bar update it with the high before comparing the low with running high times (1 - gap). If a chart bar both makes a new running high and hits the resulting stop, repeat the update and comparison in chronological order on its completed lower-timeframe bars. Include the entry bar.
Long: Position is flat AND uptrend rate >= minimum uptrend rate AND downtrend rate <= maximum downtrend rate AND uptrend rate + downtrend rate >= minimum combined rate AND technical rating >= minimum entry rating AND trend-entry lock is clear AND a completed lower-timeframe bar has V-Fib(high) within the inclusive entry window AND leading gain(low) >= minimum entry leading gain; enter at the first qualifying lower-timeframe high.
Long: Position is flat AND no trend entry qualifies on the chart bar AND support entry is enabled AND technical rating >= minimum entry rating AND support stop exists AND a support rebound high was recorded on the chart bar; enter at that first recorded high.
Long: No entry occurred on the current chart bar AND support stop exists AND V-Fib(chart high) > maximum entry V-Fib AND support rebound state is armed AND technical rating >= minimum entry rating.
Exit: long, low <= trailing stop OR uptrend rate < keep-rate threshold AND uptrend rate < entry uptrend rate + exit-rate difference OR V-Fib exit is enabled AND V-Fib(chart high) >= exit threshold.
