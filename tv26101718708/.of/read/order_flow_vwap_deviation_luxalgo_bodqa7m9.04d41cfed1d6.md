Calculation: Active pivot-high levels = highs confirmed as pivot highs using equal numbers of bars on each side; retain only the most recently confirmed levels up to a configurable limit, and remove a level after any bar whose high reaches or exceeds it.
Calculation: Active pivot-low levels = lows confirmed as pivot lows using equal numbers of bars on each side; retain only the most recently confirmed levels up to a configurable limit, and remove a level after any bar whose low reaches or falls below it. Use the same lookback and limit as for pivot-high levels.
Calculation: Active bearish gap = a gap from the current high to the low two bars earlier, created when the current high is below that earlier low and the previous bar's absolute candle body exceeds a multiple of the current simple moving average of absolute candle bodies. Retain each gap until a close exceeds its upper boundary.
Calculation: Active bullish gap = a gap from the high two bars earlier to the current low, created when the current low is above that earlier high and the previous bar's absolute candle body exceeds a multiple of the current simple moving average of absolute candle bodies. Retain each gap until a close falls below its lower boundary. Use the same average lookback and multiplier as for bearish gaps.
Calculation: Anchored volume profile = since the first bar entering a configurable recurring time range in a configurable timezone, divide the range of collected closes into equal-width price bins; assign each bar's entire volume to its close's bin. For each bin, sum buyer volume as volume multiplied by (close minus low) divided by (high minus low), and seller volume as volume multiplied by (high minus close) divided by (high minus low); use zero for both when high equals low. The average bin volume is total collected volume divided by the number of bins. The point-of-control bin has the greatest volume, with ties resolved in favor of the lowest bin. A profile exists only when the range of collected closes is positive.
Long: Low reaches or falls below an active pivot-low level AND volume exceeds a multiple of its simple moving average.
Short: High reaches or exceeds an active pivot-high level AND volume exceeds the same multiple of the same volume average.
Long: Close exceeds the upper boundary of an active bearish gap.
Short: Close falls below the lower boundary of an active bullish gap.
Long: An anchored volume profile bin has positive volume AND (its volume exceeds a multiple of average bin volume OR it is the point-of-control bin) AND its buyer volume exceeds its seller volume.
Short: An anchored volume profile bin has positive volume AND (its volume exceeds the same multiple of average bin volume OR it is the point-of-control bin) AND its buyer volume is less than or equal to its seller volume.
