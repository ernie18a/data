Calculation: C1 = the previous completed higher-timeframe candle; C2 = the next candle on that higher timeframe, evaluated when completed.
Calculation: CISD level = on the lower, paired entry timeframe, the open of the first bearish candle in the current downward close-direction run for a bullish C2, or the first bullish candle in the current upward run for a bearish C2. An opposite-direction close resets that run; dojis do not. Ratchet the level to any later bar open beyond it in the direction away from the run. Capture the level at the final C2 sweep extreme.
Long: C2 low < C1 low AND C2 close > C1 low AND NOT (C2 high > C1 high) OR a bullish C2 has a captured CISD level AND (C2 close > CISD level OR a completed lower-timeframe bar in C3 or later closes > CISD level before any price trades below the C2 low) OR yesterday's completed daily close > the day-before high OR (yesterday's low < the day-before low AND yesterday's close > the day-before low AND yesterday's close < the day-before high)
Short: C2 high > C1 high AND C2 close < C1 high AND NOT (C2 low < C1 low) OR a bearish C2 has a captured CISD level AND (C2 close < CISD level OR a completed lower-timeframe bar in C3 or later closes < CISD level before any price trades above the C2 high) OR yesterday's completed daily close < the day-before low OR (yesterday's high > the day-before high AND yesterday's close > the day-before low AND yesterday's close < the day-before high)
Exit: Long, after CISD confirmation and before C3 ends, low < the C2 low
Exit: Short, after CISD confirmation and before C3 ends, high > the C2 high
