Calculation: Timeframe structure = run each timeframe pair independently, with setup timeframe higher than entry timeframe and entry timeframe equal to or higher than chart timeframe. C1 is the preceding completed setup candle, C2 the newly completed setup candle, and C3 its successor. Setup classification and entry confirmation use completed candles; developing sweeps and lane imbalances use the still-forming higher-timeframe candle. Lane aggregation may use a configured session boundary; entry engines use regular aggregation boundaries.
Calculation: Delivery anchors = on the entry timeframe, initialize the downward anchor at the first down candle's open and reset it at the first down candle following an up candle; mirror for the upward anchor. Dojis do not reset either run. After these updates, raise the downward anchor to any higher current open and lower the upward anchor to any lower current open. Preserve anchors across setup-timeframe boundaries and retain each anchor's originating time.
Calculation: Captured entry levels = during each forming setup candle, whenever an entry candle makes or equals its running lowest low below C1 low, capture the current downward anchor and its time; whenever it makes or equals its running highest high above C1 high, capture the upward anchor and its time. Later qualifying extremes replace earlier captures.
Calculation: Entry hunts = a completed bullish or bearish C2 satisfying the corresponding sweep condition below arms its captured entry level when defined, replacing any unconfirmed hunt on the same side and pair. Freeze its level, origin time and C2 extreme. Confirm immediately when C2 closes beyond the level in its direction; otherwise confirm on the first subsequent completed entry candle closing beyond it. Kill an unconfirmed bullish hunt on low < C2 low, or bearish hunt on high > C2 high. Process a completed candle's confirmation before testing breaches in the following candle. Hunts have no time expiry.
Calculation: Retained setups = confirmed hunts enter a confirmed pool. Remove confirmed setups failing inside C3. Apply a configurable shared retention budget to active hunts, confirmed setups and failed records, removing older records by level-origin time; active stacked members are exempt. Retests and subsequent setup-dependent decisions use the pool after pruning. Each confirmed setup starts waiting for separation, then permits only its first retest.
Calculation: Projection targets = at the capture establishing the final entry level, snapshot the most recently confirmed entry-timeframe swing high for a bullish setup or swing low for a bearish setup; a swing extreme strictly exceeds the corresponding extremes of its immediate neighbors. Let this swing price be origin and C2's swept extreme be sweep. Each target = origin - projection multiple × (sweep - origin).
Calculation: Completed bullish gap = newest candle low > oldest candle high AND middle candle close > middle candle open, using three consecutive completed candles; its interval is oldest high through newest low. Completed bearish gap = newest high < oldest low AND middle close < middle open; its interval is newest high through oldest low.
Calculation: Retained higher-timeframe gaps = use completed gap-timeframe candles, on a timeframe higher than chart or optionally equal to chart. Process longer timeframes first. Strict price overlap means each interval's upper edge exceeds the other's lower edge. A higher-timeframe gap suppresses or removes overlapping lower-timeframe gaps, regardless of direction. Retain gaps within separate configurable capacities per timeframe. In wick-fill mode remove a bullish gap when chart low <= lower edge, or bearish gap when chart high >= upper edge; in close-fill mode remove it only when its own last completed timeframe close is strictly beyond that edge.
Calculation: Setup-owned gaps = maintain a rolling buffer of completed entry-timeframe gaps independently of setups. For the newest retained confirmed setup on each side and pair, claim the earliest buffered or subsequently completed direction-matching gap whose newest candle starts at or after the entry level's origin time and whose far edge extends beyond that level in the setup direction. Claim only one gap per owning setup; retain it through fills until its owner changes or leaves the pool.
Calculation: Stacks = at least two retained confirmed setups on distinct entry timeframes, with the same direction and C2 completion time. Retire a stack when a member leaves or chart close passes its outermost C2 extreme; a retired stack identity cannot reform. Projection ladders advance from shorter to longer entry timeframe when chart high reaches the active bullish ladder's largest-multiple target, or chart low reaches the bearish target; advancement never reverses.
Calculation: Staircase runs = build a chart-timeframe zigzag from confirmed symmetric swing pivots. Process high before low when both confirm together; consecutive same-side pivots keep the more extreme, while an opposite pivot is accepted only when its distance from the last accepted pivot >= an ATR-scaled minimum leg depth. Limit retained zigzag history. From the newest pivot, take the maximal suffix whose every pivot strictly exceeds its same-side predecessor for a rising run, or falls below it for a falling run. Require a minimum number of low pivots for rising runs or high pivots for falling runs, regression residual root-mean-square <= an ATR-scaled scatter limit, AND absolute regression slope <= an ATR-scaled slope limit. Update an existing uncollected run with the same direction and oldest qualifying pivot; otherwise create one and retain the newest configured number. Collect a rising run when chart low <= its oldest qualifying low, or falling run when chart high >= its oldest qualifying high.
