Calculation: Session = the highest-priority enabled session containing the bar's opening time, with the last configured session taking priority; session times use a configurable UTC offset and may cross midnight.
Calculation: Accumulation = a session is active AND (highest high over the accumulation lookback - lowest low over that lookback) / lowest low over that lookback <= the maximum range fraction, provided the lowest low is positive. The lookback includes the current bar and may include bars outside the session.
Calculation: Cycle state = initially no stored boundaries, sweep, or distribution. On any session change, clear all three. On every accumulation bar, store its lookback's highest high and lowest low and its bar index, and clear the sweep; retain any distribution. On other bars, clear a sweep when the current index exceeds its start index plus the search-window length. Within an active session and no later than the stored accumulation index plus the search-window length, process an upper sweep first: if neither sweep is active and high > stored high, start an upper sweep; if an upper sweep is active and low two bars ago - high > ATR times the gap multiplier AND close < stored high, record a bearish setup, start a bearish distribution through the current index plus the distribution length, and clear the stored high and upper sweep. Then process a lower sweep: if neither sweep is active and low < stored low, start a lower sweep; if a lower sweep is active and low - high two bars ago > ATR times the gap multiplier AND close > stored low, record a bullish setup, start a bullish distribution through the current index plus the distribution length, and clear the stored low and lower sweep. A sweep may confirm on its starting bar. Retain stored boundaries and sweep state otherwise; distribution expires after its end index.
Calculation: Absorption = volume > its simple moving average including the current bar times the volume multiplier AND body-to-range ratio <= the body-ratio threshold, where body-to-range ratio is abs(close - open) / (high - low) for positive range and 1 otherwise.
Long: Bullish setup = true.
Short: Bearish setup = true.
Long: Session active AND lower sweep active AND (bearish distribution inactive OR absorption filter enabled AND absorption = true).
Short: Session active AND upper sweep active AND (bullish distribution inactive OR absorption filter enabled AND absorption = true).
Long: Session active AND bullish distribution active.
Short: Session active AND bearish distribution active.
