Calculation: Adaptive midpoint = for each independently parameterized lookback and ATR multiplier, set basic upper and lower bands to (high + low) / 2 plus or minus multiplier × ATR. Set upper to min(basic upper, previous upper) if previous close < previous upper, otherwise basic upper; set lower to max(basic lower, previous lower) if previous close > previous lower, otherwise basic lower. Set regime to 1 if close > upper, to 0 if close < lower, otherwise retain previous regime. Set stop to lower in regime 1, otherwise upper. Set maximum to max(close, previous maximum) if close crosses stop or regime = 1, otherwise stop; set minimum to min(close, previous minimum) if close crosses stop or regime = 0, otherwise stop. Adaptive midpoint = (maximum + minimum) / 2. The fast midpoint uses a shorter lookback and independently chosen multiplier than the slow midpoint.
Calculation: ATR trail = initialize the previous trail to zero. If current and previous close are both above the previous trail, trail = max(previous trail, close − multiplier × ATR); if both are below it, trail = min(previous trail, close + multiplier × ATR); otherwise trail = close − multiplier × ATR when close > previous trail, and close + multiplier × ATR otherwise. Calculate fast and slow trails with independently chosen ATR periods and multipliers, with the fast period shorter.
Calculation: Confirmations = optional independent alternatives: MACD histogram > 0 for long or < 0 for short; RSI ≤ its upper threshold for long or ≥ its lower threshold for short; fast moving average > slow moving average for long or < for short; fast adaptive midpoint > slow adaptive midpoint for long or < for short; Aroon up > Aroon down for long or < for short. If multiple confirmations are enabled, any one passing is sufficient. If none is enabled, no confirmation is required. Apply the applicable confirmation at order placement to the entry and range-exit conditions below; the moving-average and MACD decisions also have unfiltered variants.
Long: During the configured date and daily time windows, place a buy stop above the current rolling highest high; fill when a subsequent bar reaches that stop, with the stop offset by the minimum price increment AND the applicable confirmation passes at placement.
Short: During the configured date and daily time windows, place a sell stop below the current rolling lowest low; fill when a subsequent bar reaches that stop, with the stop offset by the minimum price increment AND the applicable confirmation passes at placement.
Long: During the configured date and daily time windows, close has increased for at least the configured number of consecutive bars AND the applicable confirmation passes.
Short: During the configured date and daily time windows, close has decreased for at least the configured number of consecutive bars AND the applicable confirmation passes.
Long: During the configured date and daily time windows, high − low > SMA(high − low) + multiplier × standard deviation(high − low) AND close > close at the configured lookback AND the applicable confirmation passes.
Exit: long or short, during the configured date and daily time windows, high − low > SMA(high − low) + multiplier × standard deviation(high − low) AND close < close at the configured lookback AND the applicable short confirmation passes.
Long: During the configured date and daily time windows, fast moving average crosses above slow moving average OR, in repeat mode, fast moving average > slow moving average AND the applicable confirmation passes. With RSI confirmation in non-spot mode, fast moving average > slow moving average also triggers without a crossover.
Short: During the configured date and daily time windows, fast moving average crosses below slow moving average OR, in repeat mode, fast moving average < slow moving average AND the applicable confirmation passes. With RSI confirmation in non-spot mode, fast moving average < slow moving average also triggers without a crossover.
Long: During the configured date and daily time windows, MACD histogram crosses above zero OR, in repeat mode, MACD histogram > 0 AND the applicable confirmation passes.
Short: During the configured date and daily time windows, MACD histogram crosses below zero OR, in repeat mode, MACD histogram < 0 AND the applicable confirmation passes.
Long: Fast adaptive midpoint crosses above slow adaptive midpoint OR MACD histogram crosses above zero OR fast moving average > slow moving average OR fast ATR trail > slow ATR trail OR rolling highest high > its previous value OR RSI crosses below its oversold threshold.
Short: Fast adaptive midpoint crosses below slow adaptive midpoint OR MACD histogram crosses below zero OR fast moving average < slow moving average OR fast ATR trail < slow ATR trail OR rolling lowest low < its previous value OR RSI crosses above its overbought threshold.
Exit: long, in spot mode during the configured date and daily time windows, the consecutive-down, moving-average-down, or MACD-down entry condition passes with its applicable short confirmation.
Exit: long, in spot mode during the configured date and daily time windows, a short confirmation passes while the breakout strategy is enabled; if no confirmation is enabled, a subsequently reached sell stop below the rolling lowest low by the minimum price increment triggers instead.
Exit: long, close < latest confirmed pivot low, using the pivot only from the bar after its right-hand confirmation completes.
Exit: short, close > latest confirmed pivot high, using the pivot only from the bar after its right-hand confirmation completes.
Exit: long, fast ATR trail crosses below slow ATR trail.
Exit: short, fast ATR trail crosses above slow ATR trail.
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage).
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage).
