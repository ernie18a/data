Calculation: Consecutive rising closes = the number of consecutive bars whose close is greater than the previous bar’s close; reset to zero otherwise
Calculation: Consecutive falling closes = the number of consecutive bars whose close is less than the previous bar’s close; reset to zero otherwise
Calculation: High-low range threshold = multiplier × standard deviation of high minus low over the range lookback + simple moving average of high minus low over the same lookback
Calculation: Fast and slow trend lines = for close, use an ATR-width upper and lower band around hl2; trail each band using the prior band and prior close, switch trend when close crosses a band, and take the average of the resulting running maximum and minimum. The fast line uses a shorter lookback and its own multiplier; the slow line uses a longer lookback and its own multiplier.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot with left and right lookbacks, available only after the right-side bars and one additional bar have completed; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: MACD histogram = EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback) − EMA of that difference over the signal lookback; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Aroon up/down = 100 × (lookback + highest-bars offset of high / lowest-bars offset of low, respectively) / lookback
Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = the selected moving-average type applied to close; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: RSI trend average = simple moving average of RSI
Long: close reaches or exceeds the prior bar’s highest high over the breakout lookback plus one minimum price increment OR consecutive rising closes ≥ the configured count OR (high − low > high-low range threshold AND close > close from the configured lookback ago) OR fast moving average crosses above slow moving average OR fast moving average > slow moving average in repeat mode OR MACD histogram crosses above zero OR MACD histogram > 0 in repeat mode; when a confirmation filter is enabled, require its bullish condition: MACD histogram > 0 OR RSI ≤ its upper threshold OR fast moving average > slow moving average OR Ichimoku fast trend line > slow trend line OR Aroon up > Aroon down
Long: RSI crosses above its upper threshold OR Ichimoku fast trend line crosses above slow trend line
Short: close reaches or falls below the prior bar’s lowest low over the breakout lookback minus one minimum price increment OR consecutive falling closes ≥ the configured count OR (high − low > high-low range threshold AND close < close from the configured lookback ago) OR fast moving average crosses below slow moving average OR fast moving average < slow moving average in repeat mode OR MACD histogram crosses below zero OR MACD histogram < 0 in repeat mode; when a confirmation filter is enabled, require its bearish condition: MACD histogram < 0 OR RSI ≥ its lower threshold OR fast moving average < slow moving average OR Ichimoku fast trend line < slow trend line OR Aroon up < Aroon down
Short: RSI crosses below its lower threshold OR Ichimoku fast trend line crosses below slow trend line
Exit: long, close < latest confirmed swing low OR close crosses below the fast ATR trailing stop while the fast stop is above the slow stop OR close ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR close ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR lower-timeframe close ≤ entry price in entry-cover mode OR lower-timeframe close ≤ highest high over the breakout lookback × (1 − callback percentage)
Exit: short, close > latest confirmed swing high OR close crosses above the fast ATR trailing stop while the fast stop is below the slow stop OR close ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR close ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR lower-timeframe close ≥ entry price in entry-cover mode OR lower-timeframe close ≥ lowest low over the breakout lookback × (1 + callback percentage)
