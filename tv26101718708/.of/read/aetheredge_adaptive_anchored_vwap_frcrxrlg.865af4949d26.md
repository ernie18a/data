Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = Select close, hlc3, or ohlc4 as the price source. Reset the volume-weighted sums at the first bar with no accumulated volume and whenever the selected anchor occurs: confirmation of a pivot high or low with equal left and right strengths, the start of a new calendar session, or the first bar crossing a selected manual timestamp. The pivot anchor starts on the confirmation bar. Include the current bar in the sums. VWAP is sum(source × volume) / sum(volume), or source if the volume sum is zero. Deviation is the square root of max(sum(source² × volume) / sum(volume) − VWAP², 0), floored at a small positive value.
Calculation: Follow-through probability = Maintain one logistic model shared by both directions, with all weights initially zero. For a crossing side s, where long is +1 and short is −1, calculate six features: s × close momentum over a lookback divided by ATR; s × (shorter-period EMA of close − longer-period EMA of close) divided by ATR; s × centered and normalized RSI; deviation divided by its moving average minus 1; volume divided by its moving average minus 1; and bars since the anchor divided by an age scale. Clamp each feature to its configured bounds and floor ATR and moving-average denominators at a small positive value. Probability is sigmoid of the intercept plus the weighted sum of these features, with the sigmoid input clamped to configured bounds. On every confirmed crossing, save its side, close, ATR, and features as a training setup, discarding the oldest setup if the configured capacity is exceeded. On each subsequent confirmed bar, increment each setup's age and resolve it as success if side × (close − setup close) reaches the configured multiple of setup ATR; otherwise resolve it as failure if close has crossed back to the opposite side of the current anchored VWAP or the setup reaches its horizon. Success takes precedence over failure. For each resolved setup in creation order, update the intercept and feature weights by gradient descent on logistic loss using its saved features and the configured learning rate, then remove it. Resolve existing setups before calculating the current crossing's probability; create setups regardless of warmup or probability.
Long: Close crosses above anchored VWAP AND bar index exceeds the warmup length AND long-side follow-through probability is at least its threshold.
Short: Close crosses below anchored VWAP AND bar index exceeds the warmup length AND short-side follow-through probability is at least its threshold.
