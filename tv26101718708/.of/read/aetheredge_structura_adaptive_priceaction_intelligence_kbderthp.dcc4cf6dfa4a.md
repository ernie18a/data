Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the same number of bars used on each side of the pivot; evaluate it on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Active swing high/low = the latest confirmed swing high/low, active from confirmation until its first break by a close above/below it; a new confirmed swing replaces and reactivates the corresponding level before the current bar's break is evaluated.
Calculation: Structure trend = initially undefined; set it bullish on a break above an active swing high and bearish on a break below an active swing low. If both breaks occur on the same bar, process the bullish break first and the bearish break second.
Long: close > active swing high.
Long: confirmed swing low exists on the current bar.
Long: structure trend = bullish.
Long: low > high two bars ago AND low - high two bars ago >= minimum gap size in ATR multiples × ATR.
Short: close < active swing low.
Short: confirmed swing high exists on the current bar.
Short: structure trend = bearish.
Short: high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago - high >= minimum gap size in ATR multiples × ATR.
