Calculation: Overnight profile levels = From the latest completed overnight session, divide max(high) minus min(low), floored at the minimum price increment, into a configurable number of equal price rows starting at min(low). For each bar whose high-low range is at most one minimum price increment, allocate its entire volume to the clamped row containing its low; otherwise allocate volume to each touched row in proportion to the overlap of the row and the bar's high-low range. The point of control is the midpoint of the lowest-index row with maximum volume. Starting at that row, expand the value area one adjacent row at a time toward the greater adjacent row volume, choosing the lower row on ties, until a configurable fraction of total profile volume is included or all rows are included. The value-area high is the upper edge of the highest included row; the value-area low is the lower edge of the lowest included row. Recalculate and reset all touch and break tracking when an overnight session completes.
Calculation: Level touch = high >= level - tolerance AND low <= level + tolerance, where tolerance is a configurable price distance.
Calculation: Break state = For each overnight profile level, during the trading session after a completed profile is available, set the state to above when close crosses above the level and below when close crosses below it; use the prior bar's close and prior bar's level for the crossover comparison. Each new cross replaces the previous state and resets that level's retest completion. A retest can occur only on a later bar, and only the first qualifying retest after each cross is eligible.
Long: During the trading session, after a completed profile is available, the first level touch since profile completion is of the value-area low.
Long: During the trading session, after a completed profile is available, the first level touch since profile completion is of the point of control AND close >= point of control.
Short: During the trading session, after a completed profile is available, the first level touch since profile completion is of the value-area high.
Short: During the trading session, after a completed profile is available, the first level touch since profile completion is of the point of control AND close < point of control.
Long: During the trading session, for any overnight profile level, break state = above AND the retest is eligible AND low <= level + tolerance AND (the configurable close confirmation is disabled OR close >= level).
Short: During the trading session, for any overnight profile level, break state = below AND the retest is eligible AND high >= level - tolerance AND (the configurable close confirmation is disabled OR close <= level).
Long: During the trading session, volume divided by its simple moving average including the current bar > a configurable threshold AND close >= previous close.
Short: During the trading session, volume divided by its simple moving average including the current bar > a configurable threshold AND close < previous close.
