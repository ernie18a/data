Calculation: MACD histogram = difference between the fast and slow moving averages of close, minus a moving average of that difference
Calculation: Alligator lines = smoothed moving averages of hl2, with lips using the shortest lookback, teeth an intermediate lookback, and jaw the longest lookback
Calculation: Smoothed range = an EMA of the EMA of absolute close changes, multiplied by a range factor; the second EMA uses a lookback twice the first lookback minus one
Calculation: Range filter = recursively hold its prior value unless close moves beyond it by more than the smoothed range; then set it to close minus the smoothed range when close is above it, or close plus the smoothed range when close is below it
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average price, reset at the configured anchor
Calculation: ATR trailing levels = the configured recent low minus ATR times a multiplier for the long level, and the configured recent high plus ATR times a multiplier for the short level
Long: MACD histogram > 0 AND close > lips AND open > lips AND within the configured session window AND close > VWAP AND the range filter has risen since its last decline AND high > teeth AND lips > jaw AND previous close - previous open < configured threshold OR close > VWAP AND open > VWAP AND MACD histogram > 0 AND previous low < VWAP AND close - open > configured threshold AND within the configured session window OR close > range filter AND range filter has risen since its last decline AND the prior raw range-filter direction was short OR close crosses above the ATR trailing stop OR close > teeth AND close > the higher-timeframe daily opening-range high AND the previous four closes were below that high AND within the configured session window
Short: MACD histogram < 0 AND close < lips AND open < lips AND within the configured session window AND close < VWAP AND the range filter has fallen since its last rise AND high < teeth AND lips < jaw AND previous open - previous close < configured threshold OR close < VWAP AND open < VWAP AND MACD histogram < 0 AND previous high > VWAP AND open - close > configured threshold AND within the configured session window OR close < range filter AND range filter has fallen since its last rise AND the prior raw range-filter direction was long OR close crosses below the ATR trailing stop OR close < teeth AND close < the higher-timeframe daily opening-range low AND the previous four closes were above that low AND within the configured session window
Exit: long, close < lips AND MACD histogram < negative configured threshold
Exit: short, close > lips AND MACD histogram > configured threshold
Exit: long, high > entry price + configured target distance AND open < entry price + configured target distance OR previous close < previous teeth AND previous close < entry price AND previous teeth <= previous long ATR trailing level
Exit: short, low < entry price - configured target distance AND open > entry price - configured target distance OR previous close > previous teeth AND previous close > entry price AND previous teeth <= previous long ATR trailing level
