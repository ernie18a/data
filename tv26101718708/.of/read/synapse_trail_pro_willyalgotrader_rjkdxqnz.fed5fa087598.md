Calculation: ATR trail bands = EMA(close, trail length) ± ATR(ATR length) × effective multiplier; if ratcheting is enabled, the lower band in a long direction is the greater of its raw value and its prior value, and the upper band in a short direction is the lesser of its raw value and its prior value; reset the relevant band on a direction flip.
Calculation: Effective multiplier = base multiplier × volatility adjustment; when adaptive scaling is enabled, the adjustment is 0.8 below the low ATR-percentile threshold, 1.0 between the thresholds, and 1.25 above the high threshold.
Calculation: Regime score = normalized ADX × ADX weight + clamped (100 − Choppiness Index) × choppiness weight + 100 × R² × linearity weight; Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback); R² = squared correlation of close and bar index over its lookback. The weights sum to 1.
Calculation: Break strength = min(abs(close − prior breakout band) / ATR, cap) / cap × 100.
Calculation: Signal quality score = 100 × (HTF credit + volume credit + RSI credit + regime score × regime credit weight + break strength × break credit weight) / available factor-weight total; HTF credit is maximum when the last completed higher-timeframe close agrees with its EMA, neutral when that filter is disabled or data is missing, and otherwise zero (or partial when close equals EMA); volume credit is maximum when enabled volume exceeds its moving average times the volume threshold; RSI credit is maximum when RSI is above its midpoint for long signals or below its midpoint for short signals.
Calculation: Original risk = ATR on entry × stop multiplier; initial stop = entry price − position direction × original risk; target 3 = entry price + position direction × original risk × target-3 multiple; after target 1 is reached, the active stop becomes entry price from the next bar if break-even is enabled.
Long: Previous direction is short AND close > prior upper ATR trail band AND bar is confirmed AND ATR > 0 AND no long position is active AND signal quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND NOT (choppy-skip enabled AND regime score < choppy threshold) AND (hard higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND (range limit disabled OR previous-ATR data exists AND high − low ≤ range limit × previous ATR) AND (extension limit disabled OR previous-ATR data exists AND abs(close − trail EMA) ≤ extension limit × previous ATR).
Short: Previous direction is long AND close < prior lower ATR trail band AND bar is confirmed AND ATR > 0 AND no short position is active AND signal quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND NOT (choppy-skip enabled AND regime score < choppy threshold) AND (hard higher-timeframe alignment disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND (range limit disabled OR previous-ATR data exists AND high − low ≤ range limit × previous ATR) AND (extension limit disabled OR previous-ATR data exists AND abs(close − trail EMA) ≤ extension limit × previous ATR).
Exit: long, after the entry bar, bar is confirmed AND (low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ target 3 OR Short condition).
Exit: short, after the entry bar, bar is confirmed AND (high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ target 3 OR Long condition).
