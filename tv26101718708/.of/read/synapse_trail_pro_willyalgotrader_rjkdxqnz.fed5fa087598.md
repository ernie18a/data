Calculation: Trail bands and direction = Let the raw upper and lower bands be EMA(close) ± ATR × a positive base multiplier. Optionally scale the multiplier according to whether the ATR percentile rank over a lookback is below a lower threshold, above an upper threshold, or between them, using a smaller, larger, or unchanged factor respectively. Initialize direction to neutral. After a warmup equal to the largest relevant indicator lookback, including twice the ADX lookback and the adaptive percentile lookback when enabled, plus a buffer, change neutral direction to long if close > previous upper band, or to short if close < previous lower band; change long to short if close < previous lower band, and short to long if close > previous upper band. Otherwise retain direction. When the optional ratchet is enabled, the long lower band is the greater of the raw lower band and previous lower band, and the short upper band is the lesser of the raw upper band and previous upper band; reset the relevant band to its raw value on a direction change. The other band is raw. When the ratchet is disabled both bands are raw. Update direction and bands on every bar, including when an entry is filtered out.
Calculation: Regime score = 0.40 × min(ADX / ADX scaling parameter × 100, 100) + 0.35 × clamp(100 − choppiness index, 0, 100) + 0.25 × 100 × correlation(close, bar index, regression lookback)². Choppiness index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback); use 100 if the high-low range is zero and 50 if the true-range sum is zero. Use zero for unavailable ADX or correlation.
Calculation: Directional quality score = 100 × (HTF points + volume points + RSI points + 0.20 × regime score + 0.10 × break strength) / (50 + HTF weight + volume weight). If the optional HTF scoring is enabled and data is available, HTF weight is 30 and HTF points are 30 when the last completed higher-timeframe close is on the signal side of its EMA, 15 when equal, and 0 otherwise; otherwise both are zero. If optional volume scoring is enabled and volume and its SMA are available and the SMA > 0, volume weight is 20 and volume points are 20 if volume > its SMA × a multiplier, otherwise zero; if unavailable or disabled both are zero. RSI points are 20 if RSI > its midpoint for a long or RSI < its midpoint for a short, otherwise zero; use the midpoint if RSI is unavailable. Break strength = 100 × min(abs(close − previous upper band for a long, or close − previous lower band for a short) / ATR, a cap) / cap; use zero if ATR is zero.
Calculation: Entry filters = Quality score ≥ its minimum threshold OR quality score is unavailable; AND ATR > 0; AND either the optional choppiness filter is disabled or regime score ≥ its choppy threshold; AND either the optional hard higher-timeframe filter is disabled or the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for a long or below it for a short; AND either both optional late-entry filters are disabled or previous-bar ATR > 0; AND either the optional range filter is disabled or (high − low) / previous-bar ATR ≤ its limit; AND either the optional extension filter is disabled or abs(close − trail-center EMA) / previous-bar ATR ≤ its limit.
Calculation: Risk levels = At entry, snapshot the close as entry price and a stop distance equal to entry-bar ATR × a stop multiplier, rounded to a configurable price increment and floored at that increment. Round entry price minus direction × stop distance to that increment for the original stop, where long direction is +1 and short direction is −1. Original risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. The three target distances are original risk × successively larger target multipliers, rounded to the increment; floor the first at one increment and each subsequent distance at the preceding distance plus one increment. Add direction × each distance to entry and round to the increment. If optional break-even is enabled, move the stop to entry starting on the bar after the first target is reached.
Calculation: Target touch = After the entry bar, a target is reached if the bar opens beyond that target in the favorable direction, or if its favorable extreme reaches the target and the stop is not hit on that bar. A stop is hit if the adverse extreme reaches the current stop, unless the bar opens beyond the final target in the favorable direction. Previously reached targets remain reached.
Long: Previous trail direction = short AND current trail direction = long AND position side ≠ long AND entry filters pass.
Short: Previous trail direction = long AND current trail direction = short AND position side ≠ short AND entry filters pass.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND low ≤ current stop AND open < final target.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND high ≥ current stop AND open > final target.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND final target is reached.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND final target is reached.
Exit: long, short entry condition is satisfied.
Exit: short, long entry condition is satisfied.
