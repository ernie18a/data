Calculation: HTFBull = on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the last completed bar only, close > long-lookback EMA of close AND short-lookback EMA of close > long-lookback EMA of close. HTFBear = the mirror (close < long EMA AND short EMA < long EMA).
Calculation: ScoreLong = count of true among: (1) |MACD histogram| > simple average of |MACD histogram| over a long lookback; (2) fast EMA > fast EMA from a few bars earlier; (3) HTFBull; (4) |close - slow EMA| < multiple * ATR; (5) RSI < overbought level. ScoreShort = same count, but (2) fast EMA < fast EMA from a few bars earlier, (3) HTFBear, (5) RSI > oversold level.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA on the closed bar (previous fast <= slow AND current fast > slow; fast EMA lookback shorter than slow) AND MACD histogram > 0 AND ScoreLong >= minimum score threshold
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA on the closed bar (previous fast >= slow AND current fast < slow) AND MACD histogram < 0 AND ScoreShort >= minimum score threshold
