Calculation: Daily bias = using the last two completed higher-timeframe daily bars, bearish if the last bar’s high exceeds the preceding bar’s high and its close is between the preceding bar’s low and high; otherwise bullish if its low is below the preceding bar’s low and its close is between the preceding bar’s low and high; otherwise bullish if its close exceeds the preceding bar’s high; otherwise bearish if its close is below the preceding bar’s low; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Weekly bias = apply the daily-bias rules to the last two completed higher-timeframe weekly bars.
Calculation: Structure bias = separately on three requested timeframes, ordered longest to shortest; use the current requested-timeframe bar, which may still be forming. Track the latest confirmed pivot high and low using equal left/right lookbacks. Turn bullish when the close crosses above the latest pivot high (current close > pivot high AND prior close ≤ pivot high); turn bearish when the close crosses below the latest pivot low (current close < pivot low AND prior close ≥ pivot low); otherwise retain the previous bias, initially neutral.
Long: Daily bias = bullish OR weekly bias = bullish OR any structure bias = bullish
Short: Daily bias = bearish OR weekly bias = bearish OR any structure bias = bearish
