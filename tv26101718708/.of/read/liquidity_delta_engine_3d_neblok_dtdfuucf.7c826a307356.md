Calculation: Delta = sum, over completed lower-timeframe bars within the completed chart bar, of volume for close > open minus volume for close < open; equal open and close contribute zero. Alternatively, or when no lower-timeframe bars are available, delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), or zero when high = low. Treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of delta.
Calculation: Active liquidity zones = confirmed price pivots with equal left and right strengths and pivot-bar volume >= its volume SMA × a volume threshold. At confirmation, a demand zone extends from the pivot low to that low plus the lower wick height clamped between minimum and maximum multiples of the confirmation-bar ATR; a supply zone extends from the pivot high minus the similarly clamped upper wick height to the pivot high. Reject a new zone if it strictly overlaps an active zone of the same side. Process existing zones before creating new ones; a zone becomes inactive when close, or alternatively the corresponding wick extreme, strictly passes its far edge. Limit stored zones to a configurable count, removing the oldest inactive zone first, otherwise the oldest zone.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = current bar index minus the last fired bar index for the same signal type and direction; initialize so the first qualifying signal can fire. Update it only when that signal fires.
Long: On a completed chart bar, low <= lowest low over the extreme lookback including the current bar AND delta < 0 AND −delta >= SMA of absolute delta × absorption threshold AND volume >= volume SMA × volume spike threshold AND close >= (high + low) / 2 AND absorption cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Short: On a completed chart bar, high >= highest high over the extreme lookback including the current bar AND delta > 0 AND delta >= SMA of absolute delta × absorption threshold AND volume >= volume SMA × volume spike threshold AND close <= (high + low) / 2 AND absorption cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Long: On a completed chart bar, low <= lowest low over the extreme lookback including the current bar AND absolute delta <= SMA of absolute delta × exhaustion threshold AND close >= (high + low) / 2 AND sum of delta over a prior lookback ending on the previous bar < 0 AND exhaustion cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Short: On a completed chart bar, high >= highest high over the extreme lookback including the current bar AND absolute delta <= SMA of absolute delta × exhaustion threshold AND close <= (high + low) / 2 AND sum of delta over a prior lookback ending on the previous bar > 0 AND exhaustion cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Long: On a completed chart bar, a price pivot low is confirmed AND its low < the immediately preceding confirmed pivot low AND cumulative delta at this pivot > cumulative delta at the preceding pivot AND the distance between the pivot bars <= maximum pivot separation AND divergence cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Short: On a completed chart bar, a price pivot high is confirmed AND its high > the immediately preceding confirmed pivot high AND cumulative delta at this pivot < cumulative delta at the preceding pivot AND the distance between the pivot bars <= maximum pivot separation AND divergence cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Long: On a completed chart bar, an existing active demand zone has bottom <= low <= top AND close > zone top AND delta > 0 AND rejection cooldown > cooldown threshold.
Short: On a completed chart bar, an existing active supply zone has bottom <= high <= top AND close < zone bottom AND delta < 0 AND rejection cooldown > cooldown threshold.
