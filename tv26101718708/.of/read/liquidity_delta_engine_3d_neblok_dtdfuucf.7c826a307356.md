Calculation: Bar delta = net buying volume minus net selling volume; on lower-timeframe bars completed within the chart bar, classify volume as buying when close > open, selling when close < open, and split equally when equal; if intrabar delta is unavailable or bar approximation is selected, bar delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of bar delta
Calculation: Delta baseline = moving average of absolute bar delta over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Active demand zone = zone created from a confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low whose pivot-bar volume meets a configurable multiple of average volume; its bottom is the pivot low and its top extends toward the pivot candle body by a configurable ATR-clamped height. Active supply zone is created symmetrically from a confirmed pivot high, with its top at the pivot high and bottom extending toward the body. Suppress a new zone if it overlaps an active zone on the same side. A demand zone becomes inactive when price crosses below its bottom, and a supply zone when price crosses above its top; use either close or wick for crossing, as configured.
Long: (bar delta < 0 AND −bar delta ≥ delta baseline × configurable spike factor AND volume ≥ moving average volume × configurable volume factor AND close ≥ (high + low) / 2 AND low ≤ lowest low over the configured extreme lookback) OR (low ≤ lowest low over the configured extreme lookback AND |bar delta| ≤ delta baseline × configurable exhaustion factor AND close ≥ (high + low) / 2 AND sum of bar delta over a configurable prior lookback < 0) OR (a confirmed chart-timeframe price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND cumulative delta at the newer pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot AND pivot spacing ≤ configured maximum) OR (an active demand zone is touched by the low within its bounds AND close > its top AND bar delta > 0); subject to no same-direction signal of that type within its configured cooldown
Short: (bar delta > 0 AND bar delta ≥ delta baseline × configurable spike factor AND volume ≥ moving average volume × configurable volume factor AND close ≤ (high + low) / 2 AND high ≥ highest high over the configured extreme lookback) OR (high ≥ highest high over the configured extreme lookback AND |bar delta| ≤ delta baseline × configurable exhaustion factor AND close ≤ (high + low) / 2 AND sum of bar delta over a configurable prior lookback > 0) OR (a confirmed chart-timeframe price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND cumulative delta at the newer pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot AND pivot spacing ≤ configured maximum) OR (an active supply zone is touched by the high within its bounds AND close < its bottom AND bar delta < 0); subject to no same-direction signal of that type within its configured cooldown
Exit: long, no exit decision defined
Exit: short, no exit decision defined
