Calculation: Fast and slow high/low bands = the selected moving average of bar highs (upper boundary) and lows (lower boundary), using a shorter lookback for the fast band than for the slow band; each band midpoint is the average of its boundaries.
Long: Entry signals and long signals enabled AND time since any previous entry signal meets the configured minimum spacing (or spacing is zero or there was no previous signal) AND the bands do not overlap and their separation meets the configured minimum AND fast midpoint > slow midpoint AND both midpoints are above their respective values at the configured trend lookback; AND at least one enabled, selected-band trigger: (cross: after a close below that band’s lower boundary, close above its upper boundary within the configured cross window) OR (return: on a later bar within the configured return window after that cross, low <= the band’s upper boundary and close >= its lower boundary) OR (bounce: low <= the band’s upper boundary and close > its upper boundary) OR (between bands: fast upper boundary < slow lower boundary, previous close <= previous fast upper boundary, and current close > fast upper boundary and < slow lower boundary).
Short: Entry signals and short signals enabled AND time since any previous entry signal meets the configured minimum spacing (or spacing is zero or there was no previous signal) AND the bands do not overlap and their separation meets the configured minimum AND fast midpoint < slow midpoint AND both midpoints are below their respective values at the configured trend lookback; AND at least one enabled, selected-band trigger: (cross: after a close above that band’s upper boundary, close below its lower boundary within the configured cross window) OR (return: on a later bar within the configured return window after that cross, high >= the band’s lower boundary and close <= its upper boundary) OR (bounce: high >= the band’s lower boundary and close < its lower boundary) OR (between bands: fast lower boundary > slow upper boundary, previous close >= previous fast lower boundary, and current close < fast lower boundary and > slow upper boundary).
