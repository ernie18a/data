Calculation: Band = moving averages of chart-bar high and low using the same selectable average type (EMA, SMA, SMMA, or WMA) and lookback; the fast band has a shorter lookback than the slow band. A band's midpoint is the mean of its high and low averages.
Calculation: Band-cross event = for a selected band and direction, record the most recent bar whose close is below the band low for an upward cross, or above the band high for a downward cross. An upward event occurs when close subsequently exceeds the band high within the cross lookback; a downward event occurs when close subsequently falls below the band low within that lookback. Consume the recorded bar on the event, and expire it when its age exceeds the lookback. Compute events for both bands and both directions on every bar, independently of entry filters.
Calculation: Return event = after a band-cross event, retain its bar until the first matching return event, expiry, or a newer cross event in the same direction and band. An upward return occurs on a later bar within the return lookback when low <= band high AND close >= band low; a downward return occurs when high >= band low AND close <= band high. Consume the retained cross on return or expiry, independently of entry filters.
Calculation: Entry spacing = elapsed time since the most recent emitted band-entry signal of either direction; it is unrestricted before the first signal. Evaluate all band-entry signals on a bar using the same prior signal time, then update that time if any signal occurs.
Calculation: Latest pivot high or low = most recently confirmed symmetric pivot of chart-bar high or low, with equal configurable numbers of bars on each side. Replace the stored level when a new pivot is confirmed; clear it after its corresponding structure-break signal. A crossover or crossunder compares current close with the current stored level and previous close with the previous bar's stored level.
