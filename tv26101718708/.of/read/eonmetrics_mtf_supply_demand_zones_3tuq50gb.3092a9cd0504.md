Calculation: Zone candidate = On each enabled chart or higher timeframe, use the last completed bar as the impulse. Its range must exceed ATR times an impulse threshold, and its absolute body must exceed 0.55 times its range. Scan immediately preceding bars backward up to a maximum lookback, stopping at the first bar whose range exceeds the impulse range times a base-size ratio. The number of scanned base bars must meet a minimum. The base height, highest base high minus lowest base low, must be positive and between a minimum and maximum multiple of the impulse-bar ATR, inclusive. Set the zone midpoint to the average of those extremes and its bounds to the midpoint plus or minus half the base height times a zone-size multiplier.
Calculation: Active zones = Process candidates in timeframe-slot order, once per impulse bar per slot. If overlap filtering is enabled, discard a candidate when an existing active zone of the same direction overlaps it by more than the overlap threshold, where overlap is the positive intersection height divided by the smaller zone height. Otherwise create it; if its slot exceeds its active-zone limit, remove that slot's oldest active zone. On every chart bar, invalidate demand zones when close or low, according to the chosen invalidation mode, is below the lower bound; invalidate supply zones when close or high, respectively, is above the upper bound. Invalidation precedes retests. A demand zone is armed at creation or rearmed when chart low is above its upper bound; a supply zone is armed at creation or rearmed when chart high is below its lower bound. Disarm a zone after its retest.
Long: A new active zone is created AND its impulse close > impulse open.
Short: A new active zone is created AND its impulse close < impulse open.
Long: An active demand zone is armed AND chart low <= its upper bound AND, in close retest mode, chart close <= its upper bound.
Short: An active supply zone is armed AND chart high >= its lower bound AND, in close retest mode, chart close >= its lower bound.
