Calculation: Golden Triangle setup = the 10 preceding bars each have both high and low above the simple moving average; the current bar’s high is above and low below that average; and the most recent earlier bar found by scanning backward within a lookback is more than one bar ago. In that scan, stop with no setup if open is below the simple moving average from one bar ago and high is below its own simple moving average; otherwise stop at the first bar whose low is below and close is above its simple moving average. Require no existing long position.
Calculation: Pivot ATR trend = maintain a center initialized from confirmed pivot highs or lows (pivot lookback on both sides), updating it to (2 × prior center + latest pivot) / 3 when a pivot is confirmed. Set Up = center − ATR multiplier × ATR and Dn = center + ATR multiplier × ATR. Set TUp to max(Up, prior TUp) when prior close > prior TUp, otherwise Up; set TDown to min(Dn, prior TDown) when prior close < prior TDown, otherwise Dn. Trend is bullish when close > prior TDown, bearish when close < prior TUp, otherwise retain the prior trend (initially bullish).
Long: Golden Triangle setup AND Pivot ATR trend is bullish AND close crosses above VWAP
Exit: Long, close is above average entry price AND VWAP crosses below the simple moving average
Exit: Long, close crosses below average entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
