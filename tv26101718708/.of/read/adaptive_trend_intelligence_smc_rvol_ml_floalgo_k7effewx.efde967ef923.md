Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend direction = on the chart timeframe, calculate ATR and a shorter-lookback ATR; scale the base ATR multiplier by their ratio, capped between half and 1.5 times the base multiplier. Form upper/lower bands around (high + low)/2 using scaled multiplier × ATR; trail the upper band downward and lower band upward, resetting each when the prior close crosses it. Direction flips up when close crosses above the upper band and down when close crosses below the lower band. A clean flip is a direction flip that remains in the new direction for the noise-filter bar count; with a zero count, accept the flip immediately.
Calculation: Directional volume percentile = allocate volume to buying as volume × (close − low)/(high − low), and to selling as volume × (high − close)/(high − low). For each side, compare current allocated volume with its rolling-window median, scaling the ratio by 50 and capping at 100; use buying percentile for bullish direction and selling percentile for bearish direction. Require positive volume and a full lookback when the filter is enabled.
Calculation: Market-structure pivots = on the chart timeframe, smooth the stochastic oscillator with a 3-bar average. A pivot high is the highest high during a run at or above the overbought threshold, confirmed when the oscillator falls below it; a pivot low is the lowest low during a run at or below the oversold threshold, confirmed when it rises above it. Accept alternating high/low pivots; update a repeated high only to a higher high and a repeated low only to a lower low. Label accepted highs HH if above the previous high, otherwise LH; label accepted lows HL if above the previous low, otherwise LL. Market-structure bias is bullish when the latest labels are HH and HL, bearish when LH and LL, and otherwise retains the prior bias. After bias is established, a bullish BOS is a confirmed close above the prior high during bullish bias; bearish BOS is a confirmed close below the prior low during bearish bias. A bullish CHoCH is a confirmed close above the prior LH during bearish bias; bearish CHoCH is a confirmed close below the prior HL during bullish bias. Each break level is the previous same-side swing when a swing is accepted or updated.
Calculation: ML confidence = online logistic-regression probability for the flip direction, trained separately for bullish and bearish flips on prior same-direction signals, whose outcome is resolved at the next clean flip by whether close is above (bullish) or below (bearish) that signal's entry close. Features are normalized candle body, upper/lower wick, range and volume versus their rolling averages; consecutive candle direction; close location within the candle; average signed body versus average range; ATR and volume changes; distance from SuperTrend; bars since last flip; RSI; Bollinger-band position; directional volume percentile; and market-structure bias. Confidence is available only after the minimum resolved-sample count; when unavailable, the confidence gate passes.
Long: clean bullish SuperTrend flip AND (RVOL filter disabled OR positive volume with a full lookback AND bullish directional volume percentile >= minimum) AND (ML confidence unavailable OR ML confidence >= minimum) AND (market-structure filter disabled OR market-structure bias >= neutral) OR confirmed HH label OR confirmed HL label OR bullish BOS OR bullish CHoCH
Short: clean bearish SuperTrend flip AND (RVOL filter disabled OR positive volume with a full lookback AND bearish directional volume percentile >= minimum) AND (ML confidence unavailable OR ML confidence >= minimum) AND (market-structure filter disabled OR market-structure bias <= neutral) OR confirmed LH label OR confirmed LL label OR bearish BOS OR bearish CHoCH
