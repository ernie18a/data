Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend = ATR bands around (high + low) / 2, with band width equal to ATR times a multiplier optionally scaled by the ratio of shorter-lookback ATR to longer-lookback ATR and bounded by lower and upper multiples of the base multiplier. The upper band is the raw upper band when it is below the previous upper band or the previous close is above that band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. The lower band is the raw lower band when it is above the previous lower band or the previous close is below that band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Initialize direction bullish; change bearish to bullish when close exceeds the current upper band, and bullish to bearish when close falls below the current lower band. The trend line is the lower band in bullish direction and the upper band in bearish direction.
Calculation: Confirmed trend and flip = On every change in adaptive SuperTrend direction set a counter to 1; otherwise increment it while it is positive and below the noise length. If noise length is zero, the confirmed trend is the current SuperTrend direction and a flip occurs whenever that direction changes. Otherwise, whenever the counter equals the noise length, set the confirmed trend to the current SuperTrend direction and record a flip. The counter remains at the noise length until another direction change, so a flip is recorded on every such bar.
Calculation: Directional volume score = Allocate volume to buying as volume × (close − low) / (high − low), and to selling as volume × (high − close) / (high − low); use zero allocations when the range is zero or volume is unavailable or nonpositive. For each allocation, score it as the allocation divided by its rolling median, multiplied by the midpoint of the percentage scale and capped at its maximum; use the midpoint when the median is nonpositive. Use the buying score for bullish confirmed trend and the selling score for bearish confirmed trend. The score is valid only when current volume is positive and at least the lookback number of earlier bars exists.
Calculation: Market structure = Smooth the stochastic oscillator with a simple moving average. When it leaves the overbought zone, identify a high pivot at the highest high during that uninterrupted zone visit; when it leaves the oversold zone, identify a low pivot at the lowest low during that visit. Accept the first pivot and subsequently accept a pivot only when its type differs from the last accepted or updated pivot; update a consecutive same-type high only if higher and a consecutive same-type low only if lower. Each accepted or updated high is higher-high if it exceeds the previous stored high, otherwise lower-high; each accepted or updated low is higher-low if it exceeds the previous stored low, otherwise lower-low. The first stored high defaults to higher-high and the first stored low to higher-low. Once both types exist, set structural direction bullish when their latest classifications are higher-high and higher-low, bearish when lower-high and lower-low, and otherwise retain its previous direction; initialize neutral. On an accepted or updated high, if direction is bullish, set the continuation level to the previous stored high and clear the reversal level; if bearish, set the reversal level to the previous stored high and clear the continuation level. On an accepted or updated low, if direction is bearish, set the continuation level to the previous stored low and clear the reversal level; if bullish, set the reversal level to the previous stored low and clear the continuation level. A bullish continuation occurs when bullish direction and close exceeds the continuation level; a bearish continuation occurs when bearish direction and close falls below it. Clear the continuation level after use. A bullish reversal occurs when bearish direction and close exceeds the reversal level; a bearish reversal occurs when bullish direction and close falls below it. On reversal, change structural direction accordingly and clear both levels. Process pivot updates and direction classification before level assignment and breaks on each bar.
Calculation: Model probability = Maintain separate online logistic regression models and flip histories for bullish and bearish flips, initialized with zero weights. At each confirmed flip, resolve the latest unresolved record of each side as successful if current close is above its recorded close for bullish records or below it for bearish records; otherwise resolve it as unsuccessful. Record the current flip in its side's history regardless of signal filters, discard the oldest record if the history exceeds its limit, and train that side's existing weights using the most recent resolved records in a training window. Use exponentially decaying sample weights based on each record's age in the full side-specific history, normalized by the largest resolved-record weight. Run the specified number of gradient-descent epochs on weighted logistic loss, divide the learning rate by the sum of sample weights, apply L2 regularization to feature weights but not the intercept, then clip all weights. Clamp the logistic input before applying the sigmoid. Predict only after the side has the minimum number of resolved records; otherwise probability is unavailable. Features at the flip are: body size, upper wick, lower wick, range and volume, each divided by its own same-lookback simple average minus one and clipped to a symmetric range, or zero if its average is nonpositive; signed consecutive bullish or bearish candle count scaled and clipped, resetting to zero on a doji; buying minus selling allocated volume divided by their sum, or zero; same-lookback average signed candle body divided by average range, clipped; ATR divided by its value that lookback ago minus one, clipped; average volume divided by its same-length average from that lookback ago minus one, clipped; close minus adaptive SuperTrend line divided by ATR and a scale factor, clipped; bars since the preceding confirmed flip scaled, shifted and clipped; centered and scaled RSI, clipped and using the midpoint when unavailable; close minus its simple moving average divided by a multiple of its standard deviation, clipped; centered and scaled directional volume score when valid, otherwise zero; and structural direction as positive, negative or zero. Ratios with nonpositive denominators use zero. The trend-duration feature is computed before updating the preceding flip bar.
Long: Confirmed bullish flip AND (directional volume filter disabled OR directional volume score is valid AND directional volume score >= minimum) AND (model probability unavailable OR model probability >= minimum) AND (market-structure filter disabled OR structural direction >= neutral).
Short: Confirmed bearish flip AND (directional volume filter disabled OR directional volume score is valid AND directional volume score >= minimum) AND (model probability unavailable OR model probability >= minimum) AND (market-structure filter disabled OR structural direction <= neutral).
