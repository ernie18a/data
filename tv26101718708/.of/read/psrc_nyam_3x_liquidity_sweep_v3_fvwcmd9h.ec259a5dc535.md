Calculation: Market structure = use confirmed pivot highs and lows with the shared long-term pivot lookback; bullish/bearish structure is the latest swing high/low broken by a close. A change of character occurs when that break reverses or establishes the prior trend (neutral counts as prior trend).
Calculation: Liquidity sweep count = count distinct swept pools for the current day: Asian-session high/low, London-session high/low, prior completed daily high/low, and equal-pivot high/low. A high is swept when high > pool and close < pool; a low is swept when low < pool and close > pool. Equal pivots are chart-timeframe pivots clustered within a configured ATR-based tolerance using a longer ATR lookback. Each pool counts once per day; an equal-pivot pool is cleared when swept.
Calculation: Long retracement zones = retracement levels measured from the M5 displacement-leg low to its high; short retracement zones = retracement levels measured from the M5 leg high to its low. Standard and premium zones use separate configured retracement ranges, with premium deeper than standard.
Long: (NY AM session AND bullish change of character on higher-than-chart-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming, AND bullish M5 structure AND bullish Daily/H4 bias AND buy-side liquidity sweep count >= configured minimum AND (ATR(14) >= its 20-bar average * configured multiple OR volatility filter disabled) AND (H1 bias agrees with Daily/H4 bias OR H1 gate disabled)) OR (long setup armed AND price overlaps either retracement zone AND the most recent zone tap is within the configured chart-bar confirmation window AND M1 structure has a bullish break or change of character AND M1 structure is bullish AND close < M5 displacement-leg high AND no entry has fired for this setup).
Short: (NY AM session AND bearish change of character on higher-than-chart-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming, AND bearish M5 structure AND bearish Daily/H4 bias AND sell-side liquidity sweep count >= configured minimum AND (ATR(14) >= its 20-bar average * configured multiple OR volatility filter disabled) AND (H1 bias agrees with Daily/H4 bias OR H1 gate disabled)) OR (short setup armed AND price overlaps either retracement zone AND the most recent zone tap is within the configured chart-bar confirmation window AND M1 structure has a bearish break or change of character AND M1 structure is bearish AND close > M5 displacement-leg low AND no entry has fired for this setup).
Calculation: Daily/H4 bias = bullish when both Daily and H4 structure are bullish if agreement is required, otherwise when either is bullish; bearish uses the corresponding bearish conditions. H1 confirmation uses higher-than-chart-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming. Daily and H4 use higher-than-chart-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming.
Calculation: M5 displacement leg = for a long, low is the latest M5 swing low and high is the M5 high when the setup arms; for a short, high is the latest M5 swing high and low is the M5 low when the setup arms. M5 bars are higher than the chart timeframe and use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Armed long setup = the long arming condition above, while no setup is armed; armed short setup = the short arming condition above, while no setup is armed. An armed setup ends on its entry trigger, invalidation close, configured timeout when session timeout is disabled, or NY AM session end.
