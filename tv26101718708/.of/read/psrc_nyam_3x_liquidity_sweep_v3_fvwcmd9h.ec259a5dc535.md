Calculation: Structure = on each timeframe, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, using equal numbers of bars on each side; an upward break occurs when close crosses above the retained high, and a downward break when close crosses below the retained low. Trend starts neutral, becomes bullish on an upward break and bearish on a downward break. A change of character is an upward break when the previous trend was neutral or bearish, or a downward break when it was neutral or bullish. Use a shorter pivot length on the chart timeframe than on the higher timeframes.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe and carry its values forward until the next bar completes. Use two higher timeframes for bias, a separate higher timeframe for optional confirmation, and a higher timeframe for detection.
Calculation: Bias = bullish if both bias trends are bullish, or, when agreement is disabled, if either is bullish; otherwise bearish if both are bearish, or, when agreement is disabled, if either is bearish; otherwise neutral. Bullish takes precedence if both conditions hold.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = the running high and low of each of two configurable sessions, retained after each session ends; the high and low of the previous completed higher-timeframe day bar; and equal highs and lows. An equal high is the higher of two consecutive confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs whose absolute difference is at most a multiple of a longer-period ATR; an equal low is the lower of two corresponding pivot lows. Retain each equal level until replaced or swept.
Calculation: Sweep score = separately for highs and lows, count enabled liquidity-level types swept since the start of the current day. A high is swept when high exceeds its level AND close is below it; a low is swept when low is below its level AND close is above it. Reset all sweep flags at the start of each day; clear an equal level when swept. Update session levels before testing sweeps.
Calculation: Armed setup = when idle, arm long on a bullish detection-timeframe change of character during the configured entry session AND bullish bias AND low sweep score at least its threshold AND, if enabled, chart ATR at least a multiple of its moving average AND, if enabled, bullish confirmation-timeframe trend. Arm short on the corresponding bearish change of character, bearish bias, high sweep score and bearish optional confirmation. If both qualify, long takes precedence. At arming, fix the long leg low at the detection-timeframe retained pivot low and leg high at that bar's high; for short, fix the leg high at its retained pivot high and leg low at that bar's low. Fix long invalidation at leg low minus a multiple of chart ATR, and short invalidation at leg high plus a multiple of chart ATR. Reset the setup's tap and fired flags.
Calculation: Retracement zone = for a long, the interval from leg high minus the leg range times the deeper retracement fraction to leg high minus the leg range times the shallower fraction; for a short, the interval from leg low plus the leg range times the shallower fraction to leg low plus the leg range times the deeper fraction. A tap occurs when the chart bar's low is at or below the zone's upper bound AND its high is at or above the lower bound. Retain whether any tap occurred and update the most recent tap bar on every overlapping bar, including the arming bar.
Calculation: Setup processing = evaluate taps and entry triggers before cancelling a setup on the same bar. Cancel after an entry trigger, or when long close is below its fixed invalidation or short close is above its fixed invalidation, or when the configured maximum number of chart bars since arming is exceeded if that timeout is enabled, or on the first bar outside the entry session after being inside it. Otherwise retain the armed setup. Only one entry trigger is allowed per setup.
Long: armed long AND at least one zone tap AND bars since the most recent tap at most the confirmation window AND chart-timeframe upward break AND chart-timeframe trend bullish AND close below the fixed leg high AND no prior entry trigger in this setup.
Short: armed short AND at least one zone tap AND bars since the most recent tap at most the confirmation window AND chart-timeframe downward break AND chart-timeframe trend bearish AND close above the fixed leg low AND no prior entry trigger in this setup.
Long: armed long remains active after setup processing AND the long entry trigger is false.
Short: armed short remains active after setup processing AND the short entry trigger is false.
