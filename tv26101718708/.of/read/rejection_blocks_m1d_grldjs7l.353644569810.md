Calculation: Swing = On the chart timeframe, or on an enabled higher timeframe using its last completed bar, test the candidate bar with an equal parameterized number of completed bars before and after it. A swing high is at least as high as every earlier high and strictly higher than every later high; a swing low is at most as low as every earlier low and strictly lower than every later low. Use a shorter swing lookback on higher timeframes than on the chart timeframe. Evaluate a higher-timeframe swing on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar. Keep the previous confirmed swing high and low on each timeframe, whether or not they formed blocks.
Calculation: Rejection block candidate = For a swing high, let extreme be the swing high, body edge be max(swing open, swing close), band be extreme minus the highest max(open, close) in the swing window, and midpoint be (extreme + body edge) / 2. For a swing low, let extreme be the swing low, body edge be min(swing open, swing close), band be the lowest min(open, close) in the swing window minus extreme, and midpoint be (extreme + body edge) / 2. Equal closes means at least two closes within a parameterized price tolerance of the highest close for a swing high or lowest close for a swing low, within a parameterized radius of the swing capped at the swing lookback. The candidate range extends from the extreme to the body edge, or optionally to the opposite edge of the swing candle's body. The run level is the body edge.
Calculation: Active rejection blocks = Maintain separate bullish and bearish blocks per enabled timeframe. Before detecting new blocks on each closed chart bar, remove bullish blocks when chart close < block low and bearish blocks when chart close > block high. Remove chart-timeframe blocks after a parameterized number of chart bars since creation and higher-timeframe blocks after a parameterized duration since the chart bar on which their swing extreme occurred. For overlapping same-side blocks on the same timeframe, retain the existing block if its extreme is at least as deep; otherwise replace all overlapping blocks. Optionally suppress a candidate overlapping an active same-side higher-timeframe block and remove overlapping same-side lower-timeframe blocks when a higher-timeframe block is added. Process the chart timeframe first, then higher timeframes in configured order. Retain only the newest parameterized number of blocks per side and timeframe.
Long: A confirmed swing low forms a candidate with body edge > extreme AND band > 0 AND (band >= a parameterized multiple of the swing candle's absolute body size OR equal closes are enabled and present) AND, if the directional-candle filter is enabled, swing close > swing open AND, if the sweep filter is enabled, extreme < previous confirmed swing low AND swing close >= previous confirmed swing low AND, if the midpoint-hold filter is enabled, every close after the swing through confirmation >= midpoint AND the candidate is added to active rejection blocks.
Short: A confirmed swing high forms a candidate with extreme > body edge AND band > 0 AND (band >= a parameterized multiple of the swing candle's absolute body size OR equal closes are enabled and present) AND, if the directional-candle filter is enabled, swing close < swing open AND, if the sweep filter is enabled, extreme > previous confirmed swing high AND swing close <= previous confirmed swing high AND, if the midpoint-hold filter is enabled, every close after the swing through confirmation <= midpoint AND the candidate is added to active rejection blocks.
Long: Chart low < the run level of an active bullish block AND chart close > that run level AND the chart bar is later than the block's creation bar AND no earlier bar has run that block AND the bar is inside an enabled alert window OR all alert windows are disabled.
Short: Chart high > the run level of an active bearish block AND chart close < that run level AND the chart bar is later than the block's creation bar AND no earlier bar has run that block AND the bar is inside an enabled alert window OR all alert windows are disabled.
