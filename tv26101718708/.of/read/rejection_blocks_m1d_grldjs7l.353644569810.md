Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low exceeds the highs/lows of the same lookback on both sides; confirm after the right-side bars close.
Calculation: Wick band = swing high minus the highest candle body top in the swing window (bearish), or the lowest body bottom minus the swing low (bullish).
Calculation: Equal-body condition = at least two closes within a tolerance of the highest close near a swing high, or the lowest close near a swing low; the closes must be within a shorter lookback than the swing lookback.
Calculation: Wick midpoint = midpoint between the swing wick extreme and the swing candle's body edge.
Long: Confirmed swing low AND positive wick band AND (wick band ≥ swing candle body size × threshold OR equal-body condition) AND (midpoint hold is enabled only if every later close through confirmation is ≥ wick midpoint) AND (prior-swing sweep is enabled only if the low is below the previous swing low and the swing candle closes ≥ that previous low) AND (direction filter is enabled only if the swing candle closes above its open)
Short: Confirmed swing high AND positive wick band AND (wick band ≥ swing candle body size × threshold OR equal-body condition) AND (midpoint hold is enabled only if every later close through confirmation is ≤ wick midpoint) AND (prior-swing sweep is enabled only if the high is above the previous swing high and the swing candle closes ≤ that previous high) AND (direction filter is enabled only if the swing candle closes below its open)
