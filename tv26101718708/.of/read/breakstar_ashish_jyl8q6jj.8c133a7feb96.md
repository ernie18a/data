Calculation: Index [k] denotes k chart bars ago; all averages, ATR, standard deviations, and extrema include the current bar unless shifted. Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Volume surge = volume / SMA(volume) when SMA(volume) > 0, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Morning star = close[2] < open[2] AND body[2] >= first-body multiplier × SMA(body) AND body[1] <= star-body multiplier × body[2] AND close > open AND close[2] < close[2 + star-trend lookback] AND close >= open[2] - (open[2] - close[2]) × penetration fraction AND (star volume filter enabled = false OR volume surge >= volume multiplier).
Calculation: Evening star = close[2] > open[2] AND body[2] >= first-body multiplier × SMA(body) AND body[1] <= star-body multiplier × body[2] AND close < open AND close[2] > close[2 + star-trend lookback] AND close <= open[2] + (close[2] - open[2]) × (1 - penetration fraction) AND (star volume filter enabled = false OR volume surge >= volume multiplier).
Calculation: Upper band = Hull moving average of close + band multiplier × standard deviation of close; lower band = Hull moving average of close - band multiplier × standard deviation of close.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = (star detection enabled = true AND morning star) OR (lower wick >= wick multiplier × body AND upper wick <= body AND body > 0 AND close[1] < close[micro-trend lookback] AND low <= lower band AND low <= lowest low over extreme lookback AND (high >= high[1] OR low <= low[1])) OR (close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close > open[1] AND open < close[1] AND low <= lower band AND SMA(range) > 0 AND range >= engulf multiplier × SMA(range)).
Calculation: Bearish reversal = (star detection enabled = true AND evening star) OR (upper wick >= wick multiplier × body AND lower wick <= body AND body > 0 AND close[1] > close[micro-trend lookback] AND high >= upper band AND high >= highest high over extreme lookback AND (high >= high[1] OR low <= low[1])) OR (close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close < open[1] AND open > close[1] AND high >= upper band AND SMA(range) > 0 AND range >= engulf multiplier × SMA(range)). The range SMA and band standard deviation use the same lookback.
Calculation: Signal counter = 999 initially; set to 0 on an entry signal, otherwise increment by 1 after evaluating entry signals. Exits do not reset it. Evaluate long before short when both qualify.
Calculation: Long stop = on a long entry, the entry bar low, or the lowest low of the three star bars when the entry qualifies as a morning star, minus initial buffer multiplier × entry-bar ATR. On each subsequent bar without an entry signal, if trailing is enabled, set the stop to the maximum of its previous value and the highest high since entry minus trail multiplier × current ATR; also include entry price in that maximum if profit locking is enabled and (close - entry price) / ATR >= breakeven threshold but < tight threshold, or previous close if profit locking is enabled and (close - entry price) / ATR >= tight threshold. The tight threshold is higher than the breakeven threshold.
Calculation: Short stop = on a short entry, the entry bar high, or the highest high of the three star bars when the entry qualifies as an evening star, plus initial buffer multiplier × entry-bar ATR. On each subsequent bar without an entry signal, if trailing is enabled, set the stop to the minimum of its previous value and the lowest low since entry plus trail multiplier × current ATR; also include entry price in that minimum if profit locking is enabled and (entry price - close) / ATR >= breakeven threshold but < tight threshold, or previous close if profit locking is enabled and (entry price - close) / ATR >= tight threshold. The tight threshold is higher than the breakeven threshold.
Long: position side != long AND signal counter >= cooldown AND ((star detection enabled = true AND morning star) OR (close > highest close over the breakout lookback ending at the previous bar AND (breakout volume filter enabled = false OR volume surge >= volume multiplier))).
Short: position side != short AND signal counter >= cooldown AND long condition = false AND ((star detection enabled = true AND evening star) OR (close < lowest close over the breakout lookback ending at the previous bar AND (breakout volume filter enabled = false OR volume surge >= volume multiplier))).
Exit: long, long condition = false AND short condition = false AND ((stop flag enabled = true AND close < long stop) OR (pattern exit enabled = true AND (close - entry price) / ATR >= pattern-exit profit threshold AND bearish reversal)).
Exit: short, long condition = false AND short condition = false AND ((stop flag enabled = true AND close > short stop) OR (pattern exit enabled = true AND (entry price - close) / ATR >= pattern-exit profit threshold AND bullish reversal)).
