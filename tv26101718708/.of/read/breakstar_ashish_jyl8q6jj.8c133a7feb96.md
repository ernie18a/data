Calculation: Volume surge = volume ≥ volume multiplier × SMA(volume, volume lookback)
Calculation: Morning Star = the candle two bars ago is bearish and its body ≥ body-size multiplier × SMA(abs(close − open), star lookback); the prior candle’s body ≤ star-size multiplier × first candle’s body; current candle is bullish; close[2] < close[2 + star lookback]; and current close ≥ open[2] − (open[2] − close[2]) × penetration fraction
Calculation: Evening Star = the candle two bars ago is bullish and its body ≥ body-size multiplier × SMA(abs(close − open), star lookback); the prior candle’s body ≤ star-size multiplier × first candle’s body; current candle is bearish; close[2] > close[2 + star lookback]; and current close ≤ open[2] + (close[2] − open[2]) × (1 − penetration fraction)
Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = Morning Star OR Hammer OR Bullish Engulfing
Calculation: Bearish reversal pattern = Evening Star OR Shooting Star OR Bearish Engulfing
Calculation: Hammer = lower wick ≥ wick/body multiplier × body; upper wick ≤ body; body > 0; close[1] < close[trend lookback]; low ≤ HMA(close, structure lookback) − band multiplier × stdev(close, dispersion lookback); low ≥ lowest(low, extreme lookback); and current bar is not inside the prior bar
Calculation: Shooting Star = upper wick ≥ wick/body multiplier × body; lower wick ≤ body; body > 0; close[1] > close[trend lookback]; high ≥ HMA(close, structure lookback) + band multiplier × stdev(close, dispersion lookback); high ≥ highest(high, extreme lookback); and current bar is not inside the prior bar
Calculation: Bullish Engulfing = current candle is bullish; prior candle is bearish; close > open[1]; open < close[1]; low ≤ HMA(close, structure lookback) − band multiplier × stdev(close, dispersion lookback); and current range ≥ engulf-size multiplier × SMA(high − low, dispersion lookback)
Calculation: Bearish Engulfing = current candle is bearish; prior candle is bullish; close < open[1]; open > close[1]; high ≥ HMA(close, structure lookback) + band multiplier × stdev(close, dispersion lookback); and current range ≥ engulf-size multiplier × SMA(high − low, dispersion lookback)
Calculation: Long stop level = initialize to signal candle low minus initial buffer × ATR for a breakout, or the lowest low of the three star candles minus initial buffer × ATR for a Morning Star. While long, update the highest high since entry; candidate = highest high since entry − chandelier multiple × ATR; if close is at least tight-trail profit threshold × ATR above entry price, candidate = max(candidate, prior close); otherwise, if close is at least breakeven profit threshold × ATR above entry price, candidate = max(candidate, entry price); stop level = max(previous stop level, candidate).
Calculation: Short stop level = initialize to signal candle high plus initial buffer × ATR for a breakout, or the highest high of the three star candles plus initial buffer × ATR for an Evening Star. While short, update the lowest low since entry; candidate = lowest low since entry + chandelier multiple × ATR; if entry price is at least tight-trail profit threshold × ATR above close, candidate = min(candidate, prior close); otherwise, if entry price is at least breakeven profit threshold × ATR above close, candidate = min(candidate, entry price); stop level = min(previous stop level, candidate).
Long: position side is not long AND bars since last entry or flip ≥ cooldown AND (Morning Star OR close > highest(close, breakout lookback)[1] AND Volume surge) AND NOT (position side is long AND bars since last entry or flip ≥ cooldown AND (Evening Star OR close < lowest(close, breakout lookback)[1] AND Volume surge))
Short: position side is not short AND bars since last entry or flip ≥ cooldown AND (Evening Star OR close < lowest(close, breakout lookback)[1] AND Volume surge) AND NOT (position side is not long AND bars since last entry or flip ≥ cooldown AND (Morning Star OR close > highest(close, breakout lookback)[1] AND Volume surge))
Exit: long, no entry flip this bar AND (close < Long stop level OR (close is at least pattern-exit profit threshold × ATR above entry price AND Bearish reversal pattern))
Exit: short, no entry flip this bar AND (close > Short stop level OR (entry price is at least pattern-exit profit threshold × ATR above close AND Bullish reversal pattern))
