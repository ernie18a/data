Calculation: Optimized Trend Tracker (OTT) = On the selected timeframe, calculate an adaptive moving average of the selected price source: initialize it to the source, then update it as previous average + (2 / (length + 1)) × (absolute CMO of the source over its own lookback / 100) × (source − previous average). Set raw lower and upper stops to the average multiplied by (1 − percentage) and (1 + percentage). Initialize each previous stop to its raw stop when unavailable. If the average is above the previous lower stop, the lower stop is the greater of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is its raw value. If the average is below the previous upper stop, the upper stop is the lesser of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is its raw value. Initialize direction to up; change down to up when the average exceeds the previous upper stop, and up to down when it falls below the previous lower stop. Select the lower stop in the up direction and the upper stop in the down direction. OTT is the selected stop multiplied by (1 + percentage / 2) when the average is above the stop, otherwise by (1 − percentage / 2). The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; historical bars use the last completed selected-timeframe bar, while a bar still forming may be used in real time.
Calculation: Active premarket box = Reset the box on each new date in the configured session time zone. During the configured weekday premarket session, replace it with the high and low of the newest selected-timeframe bar satisfying the configured OTT interaction: low ≤ OTT AND high ≥ OTT, OR min(open, close) ≤ OTT AND max(open, close) ≥ OTT, OR close crosses OTT. Optionally require the selected-timeframe bar to have closed. While that same bar continues to satisfy the interaction, update the box high and low from it; otherwise retain the last recorded values. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; historical bars use the last completed selected-timeframe bar, while a bar still forming may be used in real time.
Long: OTT > OTT on the previous chart bar.
Short: OTT < OTT on the previous chart bar.
Long: Active premarket box exists AND close > box high.
Short: Active premarket box exists AND close < box low.
