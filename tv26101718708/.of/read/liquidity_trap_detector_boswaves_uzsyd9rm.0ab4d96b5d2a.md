Calculation: Trend baseline = DEMA of HLC3, optionally smoothed by an EMA.
Calculation: Trend state = initialize bullish when close >= trend baseline, otherwise bearish; thereafter change from bearish to bullish when close > trend baseline + trend ATR × flip multiplier AND trend baseline > previous trend baseline, or from bullish to bearish when close < trend baseline - trend ATR × flip multiplier AND trend baseline < previous trend baseline; otherwise retain the previous state. The trend ATR uses a longer lookback than the breakout ATR.
Calculation: Trap zones = confirm swing highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side; track the newest limited number of levels per side, including broken levels, and discard an unbroken level when its age from the pivot bar exceeds the age limit unless it breaks on that bar. A high level breaks when close > level + breakout ATR × buffer; a low level breaks when close < level - breakout ATR × buffer. Starting on the following bar, update the excursion extreme and confirm a trap if close returns strictly below a broken high or strictly above a broken low, checking for confirmation before discarding an unconfirmed breakout whose age reaches the trap window. A confirmed high trap zone spans the level to the highest high from breakout through confirmation; a confirmed low trap zone spans the lowest low from breakout through confirmation to the level. Insert new zones newest first; optionally remove the newest existing same-side zone with strictly overlapping bounds, then discard the oldest zones exceeding the per-side limit. Process levels oldest first, high levels before low levels. On each bar, including the confirmation bar, remove a high trap zone if close exceeds its upper edge, or a low trap zone if close falls below its lower edge; optionally use the zone midpoint instead of the far edge. For each surviving high trap zone, arm it when high < its lower edge; for each surviving low trap zone, arm it when low > its upper edge. A later bar retests an armed high trap zone when high >= its lower edge, or an armed low trap zone when low <= its upper edge; disarm it on that retest.
Long: trend state = bullish.
Short: trend state = bearish.
Long: a low trap is confirmed.
Short: a high trap is confirmed.
Long: a surviving armed low trap zone is retested AND trend state = bullish.
Short: a surviving armed high trap zone is retested AND trend state = bearish.
