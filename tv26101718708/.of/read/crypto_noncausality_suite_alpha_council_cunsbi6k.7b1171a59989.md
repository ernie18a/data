Calculation: Lower-timeframe signed volume = volume of the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar if its close is above its open, negative volume if its close is below its open, and zero otherwise; cumulative signed volume is the running sum sampled once per chart bar.
Calculation: Hurst proxy = 1 − log10(sum of true range over the Hurst lookback / highest high minus lowest low over that lookback) / log10(Hurst lookback), with the denominator of the range ratio floored at a small positive value.
Calculation: Regime = a persistent state initially neutral. Set it bullish when the Hurst proxy and ADX exceed their trend thresholds, RSI exceeds its midpoint, positive directional index exceeds negative directional index, EMA of changes in cumulative signed volume is nonnegative, and close exceeds its regime EMA. Set it bearish under the opposite directional comparisons. When the trend test fails and either the Hurst proxy or ADX is below its neutral threshold, set it neutral. Also set a bullish state neutral when close falls below the regime EMA, and a bearish state neutral when close rises above it. Otherwise retain the state. The displayed regime is neutral on a state-change bar and equals the state on other bars; in the optional binary display mode, omit the neutral-threshold and close-based resets from the visual state.
Calculation: Boundaries = asymmetric Whittaker smooths of highs and lows over a rolling topology window, using upper and lower asymmetry parameters respectively. For each boundary, initialize all weights to one; repeatedly solve (W + λDᵀD)z = Wy, where D is the second-difference matrix, then set each weight to p when its input exceeds its fitted value and to 1 − p otherwise. The boundary midpoint is the mean of the two fitted values. The source evaluates the whole window again on each latest chart bar, including earlier bars in that window.
Calculation: Polynomial channel = least-squares polynomial fitted to the selected price source over a shorter rolling window. Its upper and lower boundaries are the fitted values plus and minus a width equal to a multiplier times the highest moving average of high minus low over the channel lookback. Use the latest chart bar's width for every fitted bar in the window; refit the whole window on each latest chart bar.
Calculation: Rejection setup = a boundary touch within the touch lookback, a shockwave, or a liquidity sweep. An upper touch requires high at or above the upper Whittaker boundary or, when enabled, the upper polynomial boundary; a lower touch requires low at or below the corresponding lower boundary. Upper touches are blocked when the displayed true regime is bullish; lower touches are blocked when it is bearish. A shockwave requires range above a multiple of ATR, volume above a multiple of its moving average, the relevant wick above a fraction of range, and the close's deviation from its EMA divided by close standard deviation above a positive threshold for an upper rejection or below its negative for a lower rejection. A sweep requires the current high above the latest confirmed pivot high, close below that pivot and below open, and volume above its moving average for an upper rejection; reverse the price comparisons for a lower rejection. A pivot is confirmed one bar after its candidate bar when its high is strictly above, or its low strictly below, the specified number of preceding bars and the confirmation bar. Shockwaves and sweeps bypass the regime gate.
Calculation: Absorption = volume percentile rank above its threshold AND either absolute candle body below a fraction of range OR the relevant wick above a fraction of range; use the upper wick for a short and lower wick for a long.
Calculation: Anomaly-anchor mean = the average of volume-weighted typical prices anchored at the most recent limited set of bars where volume's moving z-score exceeds its threshold. Each anchor includes its anomaly bar through the current bar. If no anchor exists, use close. Reset the short clearance and its star and execution locks when close is below this mean; reset the corresponding long states when close is above it. Process stars before these resets and execution triggers after them.
Calculation: A short star sets short clearance and its star lock when the upper rejection setup and short absorption hold, close is at or above the boundary midpoint, and the short star lock is clear. A long star does the symmetric lower setup and absorption, with close at or below the midpoint. Each execution trigger has a separate per-side lock, set when that trigger fires and cleared by the corresponding clearance reset. Re-evaluate the topology window from oldest to newest on each latest chart bar, retaining previously emitted star and execution marks by bar index even if later refits no longer satisfy their original conditions.

Short: The visual displayed regime equals bearish.

Short: Short clearance is set AND the short vacuum trigger lock is clear AND the previous chart bar's volume percentile rank exceeds its high threshold AND the current rank is below its low threshold.
