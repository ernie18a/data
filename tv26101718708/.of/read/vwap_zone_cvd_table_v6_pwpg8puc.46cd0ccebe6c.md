Calculation: Zones = Reset cumulative volume-weighted sums at each selected anchor boundary. The central zone center is cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, where typical price is (high + low + close) / 3. Its weighted standard deviation is the square root of the maximum of zero and (cumulative sum of typical price squared times volume divided by cumulative volume minus the center squared). The other four zone centers are the central center plus or minus each of two independently configured standard-deviation multipliers times the weighted standard deviation. Each zone extends above and below its center by its configured ATR multiplier times chart-timeframe ATR; the two zones using the same standard-deviation multiplier share an ATR multiplier.
Calculation: Zone buy percentage = Maintain separate cumulative buy and sell volumes for each zone, initialized to zero and optionally reset at each selected anchor boundary or CVD-timeframe day boundary. On each chart bar, if close is within the zone inclusive and chart volume is at least the minimum volume threshold, add the CVD-timeframe volume to buy volume if CVD-timeframe volume times (2 × close − high − low) / max(high − low, a small positive denominator floor) is positive, or to sell volume if it is negative. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use its last available intrabar within the chart bar. Calculate 100 × buy volume / (buy volume + sell volume), or 50 if their sum is zero.
Long: Zone buy percentage >= bullish threshold for any zone.
Short: Zone buy percentage < bullish threshold AND zone buy percentage <= 100 − bullish threshold for any zone.
