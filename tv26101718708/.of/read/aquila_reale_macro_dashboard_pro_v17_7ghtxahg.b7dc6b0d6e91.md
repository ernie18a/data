Calculation: Percent change = (current close / previous close - 1) × 100 for each source series, using its current forming aggregated bar and preceding bar; the dashboard uses three configured timeframes for core series and its slow timeframe for other series.
Calculation: Macro bias score = sum of 18 ternary contributions: inverse series contribute -1 when change > 0.3%, +1 when change < -0.3%, otherwise 0; direct series contribute +1 when change > 0.3%, -1 when change < -0.3%, otherwise 0. Core contributions are DXY (zero when slow-timeframe VIX ≥ 30), 10Y yield, USDJPY, USDCHF, VIX, and silver on each of the three timeframes; slow-timeframe contributions are 2Y, 20Y, 30Y yields, inflation, S&P breadth, Nikkei, Hang Seng, world equities, USDCNH, EURUSD, Bitcoin, and WTI. Equity series contribute -1 when change > 0.3%, +1 when change < -0.3% and slow-timeframe VIX > 22, otherwise 0. WTI contributes -1 when level > 100 and change > 0.3%, +1 when level ≤ 100 and change > 0.3%, +1 when level > 100 and change < -0.3%, -1 when level ≤ 100 and change < -0.3%, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Scenario = stagflationary when slow-timeframe WTI > 100 and gold change < -0.5%; gold-hedge-active when WTI > 100 and gold change > 0.5%; risk-off when WTI < 100, gold change > 0.5%, and 10Y yield > 4.3%; reflationary when WTI < 100, gold change > 0.5%, and 10Y yield < 4.3%; risk-on when WTI < 100 and gold change < -0.5%; otherwise mixed. Use slow-timeframe values.
Long: Scenario is gold-hedge-active OR reflationary OR risk-off OR (mixed AND macro bias score ≥ 3)
Short: (mixed AND macro bias score ≤ -8) OR (macro bias score on each of the three core timeframes ≤ -3)
Exit: Long, false
Exit: Short, false
