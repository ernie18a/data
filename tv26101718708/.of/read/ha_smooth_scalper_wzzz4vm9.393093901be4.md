Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi = EMA-smooth each OHLC field with a configurable pre-smoothing length. Calculate HA close as the average of the four smoothed fields; HA open as the average of the preceding HA open and close, initialized to the average of the smoothed open and close; HA high and low as the maximum and minimum of the smoothed high or low, HA open, and HA close. EMA-smooth each HA field with a configurable post-smoothing length.
Calculation: Raw direction = bullish when smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= open; bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Structure direction = initialize to raw direction. Detect confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal configurable left and right strengths on either raw highs and lows or smoothed Heikin-Ashi highs and lows. Remember the latest confirmed pivot of each kind. While bullish, replace the protected low with each newly confirmed pivot low, or initialize it from the latest pivot low when missing. While bearish, do the equivalent for the protected high. Calculate both break levels before processing breaks: protected low minus a configurable multiple of ATR, and protected high plus that multiple of ATR. If bullish and close, or low in wick mode, is below the low break level, switch to bearish, set the protected high to the latest pivot high, and clear the protected low. Then, if bearish and close, or high in wick mode, is above the previously calculated high break level, switch to bullish, set the protected low to the latest pivot low, and clear the protected high. Missing levels cannot break.
Calculation: Active direction = structure direction when structure lock is enabled; otherwise raw direction.
Calculation: Timeframe direction = raw direction computed on each enabled configurable timeframe, which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. Select either its current bar or its preceding bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last selected intrabar mapped to the chart bar. For a higher timeframe, the current bar may still be forming and the preceding bar is completed. If the preceding direction is unavailable, use the current direction.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe confirmation = on a configurable higher timeframe, select either the current, possibly forming bar or the preceding completed bar, falling back to the current bar if the preceding direction is unavailable. In Heikin-Ashi mode use raw direction; in EMA mode it is bullish when close > EMA and EMA >= its preceding value, bearish when close < EMA and EMA <= its preceding value, and neutral otherwise.
