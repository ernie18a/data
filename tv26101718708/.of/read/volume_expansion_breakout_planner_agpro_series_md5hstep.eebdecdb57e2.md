Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / moving average of volume; smoothed relative volume = 3-bar moving average of relative volume
Calculation: Breakout quality score = rounded 0–100 clamp of volume score + candle-body efficiency × 18 + directional close location × 17 + breakout distance score + trend score + range-fit score; volume score = clamp((relative volume − 0.85) / max(0.10, full-volume ratio − 0.85) × 25, 0, 25); breakout distance score = clamp(close's directional distance beyond the reference / max(0.10, 4 × breakout-buffer ATR) × 15, 0, 15); trend score = 15 when fast EMA and close align directionally with the slower EMA, 8 when either condition holds, otherwise 2; range-fit score = 10, reduced proportionally when range height / ATR is below its minimum or above its maximum
Calculation: Bullish/bearish breakout = close > prior range high + ATR buffer / close < prior range low − ATR buffer, respectively, and previous close was not beyond that same buffered reference
Calculation: Acceptance band = broken reference ± ATR-scaled band; invalidation rail = broken reference − direction × ATR-scaled distance
Calculation: Active path = most recent eligible bullish or bearish breakout path; its direction, reference, ATR, start bar, failed state, and accepted state persist until replaced
Long: bullish breakout with quality score at least the weak-path floor and no same-direction path within the cooldown OR close < prior range high AND distance to it ≤ 0.65 ATR AND relative volume ≥ sustained-volume threshold AND close > open AND close > fast EMA OR active bullish path AND bars since start > 0 AND within post-break window AND relative volume ≥ expansion threshold AND close > open AND close ≥ previous close AND close above acceptance band AND path not failed OR active bullish path AND not accepted or failed AND bars since start ≥ minimum acceptance bars AND close above acceptance band AND smoothed relative volume ≥ sustained-volume threshold
Short: bearish breakout with quality score at least the weak-path floor and no same-direction path within the cooldown OR close > prior range low AND distance to it ≤ 0.65 ATR AND relative volume ≥ sustained-volume threshold AND close < open AND close < fast EMA OR active bearish path AND bars since start > 0 AND within post-break window AND relative volume ≥ expansion threshold AND close < open AND close ≤ previous close AND close below acceptance band AND path not failed OR active bearish path AND not accepted or failed AND bars since start ≥ minimum acceptance bars AND close below acceptance band AND smoothed relative volume ≥ sustained-volume threshold
Exit: long, active bullish path AND bars since start > 0 AND (close below invalidation rail OR (close below broken reference AND smoothed relative volume ≤ weak-volume threshold) OR (bars since start ≥ minimum acceptance bars AND close not above acceptance band AND smoothed relative volume below sustained-volume threshold AND relative volume ≤ weak-volume threshold))
Exit: short, active bearish path AND bars since start > 0 AND (close above invalidation rail OR (close above broken reference AND smoothed relative volume ≤ weak-volume threshold) OR (bars since start ≥ minimum acceptance bars AND close not below acceptance band AND smoothed relative volume below sustained-volume threshold AND relative volume ≤ weak-volume threshold))
