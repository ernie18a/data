Calculation: Normalized score = clamp((x − weak) / (strong − weak), 0, 1); for a lower-is-better score use 1 minus this score. A peak score rises from zero at its lower bound to one at its ideal and falls to zero at its upper bound.
Calculation: Source range = highest high and lowest low over the preceding range lookback; width is their difference. Range maturity is a weighted sum of: normalized consecutive preceding bars with both ATR-normalized range and body share below their respective limits; a peak score of source-range width / ATR; and a lower-is-better score of source-range width / its moving-average baseline.
Calculation: Expansion candle score = weighted sum of normalized close distance beyond the broken edge / ATR, directional close location within the bar, body size / bar range, peak-scored bar range / ATR, and normalized volume / moving-average volume. Use a neutral volume score if volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Expansion plan = when close exceeds the preceding source-range high by at least a breakout threshold times ATR, set direction long; when close is below the preceding source-range low by at least that distance, set direction short. Start a new plan only if no plan exists, its direction differs, its age exceeds the maximum, or it has failed or reached its target. Freeze the source range, its broken edge, maturity score, expansion candle score and start bar. A plan remains present until replaced, including after expiry, failure or target reach.
Calculation: Failure rail = frozen broken edge minus direction × current ATR × failure-buffer multiplier. Failure occurs when direction × (close − failure rail) < 0 and remains recorded until a new plan starts.
Calculation: Target edge = the nearer in the plan direction of the measured target, frozen edge + direction × frozen range width × target multiplier, and the preceding obstacle-lookback highest high for a long plan or lowest low for a short plan, provided that obstacle lies at least an ATR-scaled buffer beyond the edge; otherwise use the measured target. Recalculate the obstacle and target edge each bar. Target is reached when high >= target edge for a long plan or low <= target edge for a short plan, and remains recorded until a new plan starts.
Calculation: Accepted retest = after the plan's starting bar, low <= edge + ATR × retest-band multiplier for a long plan or high >= edge − ATR × retest-band multiplier for a short plan, AND direction × (close − edge) >= 0 AND the current bar has not crossed the failure rail. A retest is recent if an accepted retest occurred within the retest lookback, including one from an earlier plan.
Calculation: Room-risk score = weighted sum of normalized max(direction × (target edge − close), 0) / ATR and a peak score of max(direction × (close − failure rail), 0) / ATR. Planner score = weighted sum of frozen maturity, frozen expansion-candle score, normalized max(direction × (close − edge), 0) / ATR, retest quality and room-risk score. Retest quality takes, in order: the failed-plan score if failure is recorded; the accepted-retest score if accepted now; the recent-retest score if recent; the inside-range score if direction × (close − edge) < 0; the early-plan score if plan age is within the retest lookback; otherwise the late-plan score. Cap the planner score at the applicable limit, in order, for recorded failure, close inside the range, recorded target reach, or room-risk score below its low-room threshold; then round it.
Calculation: Clean expansion = failure and target reach are not recorded AND direction × (close − edge) >= 0 AND target room / ATR >= minimum room threshold AND planner score >= clean threshold AND (accepted retest OR recent retest OR both close distance beyond edge / ATR >= confirmation threshold and directional close location >= its threshold). Expansion review = the same failure, target, inside-range and minimum-room conditions AND clean expansion is false AND planner score >= lower review threshold.
Long: A new long expansion plan starts OR a long plan enters clean expansion OR a long plan enters expansion review OR a long plan's accepted retest changes from false to true AND planner score >= review threshold.
Short: A new short expansion plan starts OR a short plan enters clean expansion OR a short plan enters expansion review OR a short plan's accepted retest changes from false to true AND planner score >= review threshold.
Exit: long, a long plan's close moves inside its frozen edge OR below its failure rail OR its high reaches its target edge.
Exit: short, a short plan's close moves inside its frozen edge OR above its failure rail OR its low reaches its target edge.
