Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = smoothed stochastic of RSI(close); Stochastic RSI D = smoothed average of K
Long: position is flat AND close > each of the three higher-timeframe EMAs calculated from the last completed bar AND K crosses above D
Short: position is flat AND close < each of the three higher-timeframe EMAs calculated from the last completed bar AND K crosses below D
Exit: long, close <= entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR close >= entry price + ATR × target multiplier
Exit: short, close >= entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR close <= entry price − ATR × target multiplier
