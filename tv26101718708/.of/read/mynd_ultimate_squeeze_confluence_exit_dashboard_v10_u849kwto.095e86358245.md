Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands with an SMA basis and standard-deviation width are strictly inside Keltner Channels with the same SMA basis and an SMA of true range for width; use the same lookback for both. Compression tier is the deepest of three nested Keltner widths containing the bands, or zero if none does.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear-regression endpoint of close minus the average of (highest high plus lowest low)/2 and SMA(close), using the squeeze lookback for all components. Lean is its sign.
Calculation: Timeframe readings = calculate squeeze, tier, lean and squeeze release on each enabled timeframe. A release occurs when squeeze was on in the preceding timeframe bar and is off now. Use the preceding bar's readings on the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar on higher timeframes; on lower timeframes use the preceding lower-timeframe bar associated with the first intrabar of the chart bar. Carry higher-timeframe readings across chart bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe vote for a given timeframe = sum of lean times (compression tier + 1) over enabled timeframes with a strictly longer duration. The chart higher-timeframe vote uses enabled timeframes longer than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: CMF pivot divergence = at confirmation of a price pivot, compare it with the immediately preceding price pivot of the same type, provided their pivot bars are within the maximum separation. Compare CMF pivots confirmed on those same two confirmation bars; both CMF pivots must exist. Pivot lookbacks to the left and right are configurable, and CMF uses its own lookback.
Calculation: Chart fire = chart-timeframe squeeze release; its direction is the chart squeeze momentum sign on that bar. Remember the most recent chart fire direction and bars since that fire, including fires with zero direction.
Calculation: Acceleration z-score = z-score of EMA of the change in EMA of the change in EMA of RSI(close); each EMA has its own configurable smoothing length, and the z-score uses a configurable rolling lookback. Use zero when the rolling standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Exit score = 100 times the weighted average of five terms, or zero if their total weight is zero or the latest chart fire direction is zero. The deceleration term is min(1, abs(acceleration z-score)/(2*abs(negative deceleration threshold))) when a bullish fire has acceleration z-score at or below the negative threshold or a bearish fire has acceleration z-score at or above its positive mirror, otherwise zero. The climax term is min(1, volume z-score/(2*positive volume threshold)) when volume z-score is at least that threshold, high-low is at least a configurable multiple of positive ATR, and rejection holds, otherwise zero. Bullish-fire rejection is (close-low)/(high-low) <= 1-rejection threshold OR (high-max(open,close))/(high-low) >= rejection threshold; bearish-fire rejection is (close-low)/(high-low) >= rejection threshold OR (min(open,close)-low)/(high-low) >= rejection threshold. For zero range, use close position 0.5 and wick ratios zero; for zero volume standard deviation, use volume z-score zero. The recompression term is 1 once a squeeze occurs within the configurable post-fire window starting one bar after the fire, and remains 1 until the next chart fire; otherwise it is zero. The higher-timeframe conflict term is 1 when the chart higher-timeframe vote has the opposite sign to the latest nonzero chart fire direction, otherwise zero. The staleness term is min(1, (bars since chart fire minus typical hold)/typical hold) when bars since chart fire exceed the configurable typical hold, otherwise zero.
Long: Squeeze momentum > 0 on any enabled timeframe.
Short: Squeeze momentum < 0 on any enabled timeframe.
Long: Number of enabled timeframes with positive lean > number with negative lean.
Short: Number of enabled timeframes with negative lean > number with positive lean.
Long: Sum of lean times (compression tier + 1) across enabled timeframes > 0.
Short: Sum of lean times (compression tier + 1) across enabled timeframes < 0.
Long: Positive-lean releases across enabled timeframes >= configurable minimum AND positive-lean releases > negative-lean releases AND (higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled OR no positive-lean release has a negative higher-timeframe vote).
Short: Negative-lean releases across enabled timeframes >= configurable minimum AND negative-lean releases > positive-lean releases AND (higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled OR no negative-lean release has a positive higher-timeframe vote).
Short: Any enabled timeframe has a squeeze release AND its squeeze momentum = 0.
Long: Confirmed price pivot low < preceding price pivot low AND corresponding confirmed CMF pivot low > preceding corresponding CMF pivot low AND pivot separation <= configurable maximum.
Short: Confirmed price pivot high > preceding price pivot high AND corresponding confirmed CMF pivot high < preceding corresponding CMF pivot high AND pivot separation <= configurable maximum.
Long: Bars since bullish CMF pivot divergence <= configurable persistence window AND bars since bearish CMF pivot divergence > that window.
Short: Bars since bearish CMF pivot divergence <= configurable persistence window AND bars since bullish CMF pivot divergence > that window.
Exit: long, latest chart fire direction > 0 AND exit score >= exit threshold AND previous exit state != EXIT.
Exit: short, latest chart fire direction < 0 AND exit score >= exit threshold AND previous exit state != EXIT.
Exit: long, latest chart fire direction > 0 AND exit score >= caution threshold AND exit score < exit threshold AND previous exit state = HOLD.
Exit: short, latest chart fire direction < 0 AND exit score >= caution threshold AND exit score < exit threshold AND previous exit state = HOLD.
Exit: long, latest chart fire direction > 0 AND recompression term = 1 AND previous recompression term = 0.
Exit: short, latest chart fire direction < 0 AND recompression term = 1 AND previous recompression term = 0.
