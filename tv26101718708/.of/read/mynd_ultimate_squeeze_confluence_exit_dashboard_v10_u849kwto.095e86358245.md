Calculation: Squeeze state = Bollinger Bands (simple moving average of close ± a standard-deviation multiple) are inside the loosest Keltner Channel (simple moving average of close ± a range multiple times the simple moving average of true range); squeeze release = squeezed on the preceding bar and not squeezed now.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum direction = linear regression, at zero offset, of close minus the average of (highest high and lowest low)/2 and the simple moving average of close, over the squeeze lookback; positive is bullish and negative is bearish.
Calculation: Squeeze tier = 3 if the Bollinger Bands are inside the tightest Keltner Channel, else 2 if inside the middle channel, else 1 if inside the loosest channel, else 0.
Calculation: For every enabled configured timeframe, calculate squeeze state, release, momentum direction, and tier on its last completed bar. This timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe net direction = sign of the sum across enabled configured timeframes higher than the timeframe being evaluated, assigning each timeframe's momentum direction × (squeeze tier + 1) as its vote; ignore zero directions.
Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = sum over its lookback of volume × (((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low)) divided by sum of volume; use zero for the per-bar multiplier when high equals low.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low in low is below the preceding pivot low, while the corresponding confirmed pivot low in Chaikin Money Flow is above its preceding pivot low, and the pivot lows are within the maximum pivot gap. Bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high in high is above the preceding pivot high, while the corresponding confirmed pivot high in Chaikin Money Flow is below its preceding pivot high, within the maximum pivot gap.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe squeeze, momentum direction, momentum deceleration, volatility climax, re-compression, and staleness use chart bars including the bar still forming. For deceleration, smooth RSI(close) with an EMA, take and smooth its one-bar change with an EMA to get velocity, take and smooth the velocity's one-bar change with an EMA to get acceleration, then calculate the acceleration z-score over its lookback.
Calculation: Volatility climax = volume z-score is at least its threshold AND high − low is at least its ATR multiple AND (for a long, close position (close − low)/(high − low) is at most 1 − the close-position threshold OR upper-wick ratio (high − max(close, open))/(high − low) is at least the threshold; for a short, close position is at least the threshold OR lower-wick ratio (min(close, open) − low)/(high − low) is at least the threshold). For zero range, use close position 0.5 and wick ratios 0.
Calculation: Squeeze exit score = 100 × weighted average of: deceleration term, climax term, re-compression term, higher-timeframe conflict term, and staleness term. Deceleration term is zero unless acceleration z-score is at or below the negative threshold for a long or at or above its positive mirror for a short; otherwise it is min(1, absolute z-score / (2 × absolute threshold)). Climax term is zero unless volatility climax is true; otherwise min(1, volume z-score / (2 × volume z-score threshold)). Re-compression term is 1 if a squeeze reforms within the configured post-fire window, else 0. Higher-timeframe conflict term is 1 if at least one enabled configured timeframe higher than the chart has a nonzero net direction opposite the active fired direction, else 0. Staleness term is zero until bars since chart-timeframe fire exceed the typical hold length, then min(1, (bars since fire − typical hold length)/typical hold length). Use zero score with no active chart-timeframe fire or when the weights sum to zero.
Long: At least one enabled configured timeframe has positive squeeze momentum direction OR bullish group fire (bullish releases ≥ the configured minimum number of enabled timeframes AND bullish releases exceed bearish releases AND, if the higher-timeframe alignment filter is enabled, no releasing bullish timeframe conflicts with its higher-timeframe net direction) OR bullish divergence.
Short: At least one enabled configured timeframe has negative squeeze momentum direction OR bearish group fire (bearish releases ≥ the configured minimum number of enabled timeframes AND bearish releases exceed bullish releases AND, if the higher-timeframe alignment filter is enabled, no releasing bearish timeframe conflicts with its higher-timeframe net direction) OR bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, active chart-timeframe fired direction is long AND (exit score crosses from below the caution level to at least the caution level OR a re-compression is first detected within the post-fire window).
Exit: Short, active chart-timeframe fired direction is short AND (exit score crosses from below the caution level to at least the caution level OR a re-compression is first detected within the post-fire window).
Exit: Long, active chart-timeframe fired direction is long AND exit score crosses to at least the exit level.
Exit: Short, active chart-timeframe fired direction is short AND exit score crosses to at least the exit level.
