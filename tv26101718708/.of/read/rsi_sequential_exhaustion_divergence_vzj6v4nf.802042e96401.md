Calculation: Long confluence = 1 if higher-timeframe close > its EMA, plus 1 if ADX is below its threshold, plus 1 if a bullish RSI divergence was confirmed within the configured recent-divergence window, plus 1 if volume exceeds its 20-bar average and is usable, plus 1 if RSI > prior RSI; otherwise each item adds 0.
Calculation: Short confluence = 1 if higher-timeframe close < its EMA, plus 1 if ADX is below its threshold, plus 1 if a bearish RSI divergence was confirmed within the configured recent-divergence window, plus 1 if volume exceeds its 20-bar average and is usable, plus 1 if RSI < prior RSI; otherwise each item adds 0.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = at a confirmed RSI pivot low, price makes a lower low and RSI makes a higher low by at least the configured RSI gap (regular), OR price makes a higher low and RSI makes a lower low by at least that gap (hidden); compare with the prior RSI pivot low, which must be separated by the configured minimum and maximum bar gaps, and enforce the configured minimum bars between same-direction divergence markings.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = at a confirmed RSI pivot high, price makes a higher high and RSI makes a lower high by at least the configured RSI gap (regular), OR price makes a lower high and RSI makes a higher high by at least that gap (hidden); compare with the prior RSI pivot high, which must be separated by the configured minimum and maximum bar gaps, and enforce the configured minimum bars between same-direction divergence markings.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close > its EMA (bullish), otherwise bearish; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = count of bullish EMA biases across three higher timeframes, respectively about 3×, 5×, and 15× the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar of each.
Calculation: Volatility-cluster ratio = exponentially decaying intensity of returns whose absolute value exceeds the configured multiple of the 20-bar return standard deviation, divided by the 100-bar average of those shock occurrences; intensity decays by the configured factor each bar and rises by the configured excitation amount on a shock.
Long: (RSI < prior RSI < RSI two bars ago < RSI three bars ago AND RSI three bars ago < oversold level AND RSI four bars ago > oversold level OR bullish RSI divergence) AND (higher-timeframe filter off OR higher-timeframe bias bullish) AND (ADX regime filter off OR ADX < configured trend threshold) AND (session filter off OR configured initial session bars have elapsed) AND long confluence >= configured minimum confluence plus the rounded volatility-cluster lift when that filter is on and the ratio exceeds its warning threshold AND (multi-timeframe gate off OR higher-timeframe agreement >= configured minimum agreement).
Short: (RSI > prior RSI > RSI two bars ago > RSI three bars ago AND RSI three bars ago > overbought level AND RSI four bars ago < overbought level OR bearish RSI divergence) AND (higher-timeframe filter off OR higher-timeframe bias bearish) AND (ADX regime filter off OR ADX < configured trend threshold) AND (session filter off OR configured initial session bars have elapsed) AND short confluence >= configured minimum confluence plus the rounded volatility-cluster lift when that filter is on and the ratio exceeds its warning threshold AND (multi-timeframe gate off OR bearish higher-timeframe agreement >= configured minimum agreement).
