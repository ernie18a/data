Calculation: RSI pivot = an RSI high or low confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars against configurable numbers of left- and right-hand bars; pair each confirmed pivot with the chart high or low at the pivot bar. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding pivot of the same type, even if that preceding pivot produced no divergence. Evaluate divergence on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = consecutive RSI low pivots separated by at least a minimum and at most a maximum number of bars AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND the newer RSI is greater than the older RSI plus a minimum gap AND the newer paired low is lower) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND the newer RSI is less than the older RSI minus the minimum gap AND the newer paired low is higher)).
Calculation: Bearish divergence = consecutive RSI high pivots separated by at least a minimum and at most a maximum number of bars AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND the newer RSI is less than the older RSI minus a minimum gap AND the newer paired high is higher) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND the newer RSI is greater than the older RSI plus the minimum gap AND the newer paired high is lower)).
Calculation: Accepted divergence = a same-direction divergence whose pivot bar is at least a configurable cooldown number of bars after the pivot bar of the previous accepted same-direction divergence. Track bullish and bearish cooldowns separately.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = +1 if the last completed higher-timeframe close is greater than its EMA, otherwise -1. Use a configurable higher timeframe for the trend filter and confluence; use three progressively higher timeframes and a separately parameterized EMA for agreement.
Calculation: Directional confluence = the sum of one point each for higher-timeframe bias matching the direction, ADX below its trend threshold, an accepted same-direction divergence whose pivot bar is within a configurable number of chart bars of the current bar, usable volume above its SMA, and RSI rising for long or falling for short. Volume usability is configurable or automatically determined from the cumulative fraction of bars with positive volume; unavailable volume contributes zero.
Calculation: Volatility lift = zero unless the volatility filter is enabled and intensity divided by baseline exceeds a warning threshold. Log return is log(close / max(previous close, a positive floor)); a shock occurs when its absolute value exceeds a configurable multiple of its rolling standard deviation. Baseline is the longer-lookback SMA of the shock indicator. Intensity starts at zero and updates on each completed bar as previous intensity multiplied by exp(-decay) plus excitation when a shock occurs. The ratio is zero when baseline is not positive. When above the warning threshold, lift is the rounded value of maximum lift multiplied by min(1, (ratio - warning threshold) / scaling span).
Long: Bullish divergence is detected.
Short: Bearish divergence is detected.
Long: Current RSI < RSI one bar ago AND RSI one bar ago < RSI two bars ago AND RSI two bars ago < RSI three bars ago AND RSI three bars ago < oversold threshold AND RSI four bars ago > oversold threshold AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias = +1) AND (regime filter is disabled OR ADX < trend threshold) AND (session filter is disabled OR chart timeframe is not intraday OR bars since the calendar-day session began > session skip count) AND long confluence >= minimum confluence + volatility lift AND (agreement filter is disabled OR count of the three higher-timeframe biases equal to +1 >= minimum agreement).
Short: Current RSI > RSI one bar ago AND RSI one bar ago > RSI two bars ago AND RSI two bars ago > RSI three bars ago AND RSI three bars ago > overbought threshold AND RSI four bars ago < overbought threshold AND (higher-timeframe trend filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias = -1) AND (regime filter is disabled OR ADX < trend threshold) AND (session filter is disabled OR chart timeframe is not intraday OR bars since the calendar-day session began > session skip count) AND short confluence >= minimum confluence + volatility lift AND (agreement filter is disabled OR count of the three higher-timeframe biases equal to -1 >= minimum agreement).
