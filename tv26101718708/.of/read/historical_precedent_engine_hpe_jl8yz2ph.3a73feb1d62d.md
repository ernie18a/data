Calculation: Candle fingerprint = for each candle in a sequence, record upper wick, lower wick, and absolute body as percentages of max(high − low, minimum price tick), direction as close >= open, and volume divided by its volume SMA, or 1 when that SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Sequence momentum = percentage change from the first close to the last close in the sequence, or 0 when the first close is not positive.
Calculation: Historical matches = on each completed chart bar, first resolve eligible stored sequences, then compare the current sequence with the bounded, oldest-first rolling library before storing the current sequence. A stored sequence is eligible when its horizon outcome close is nonzero and its starting close is positive. Its final candle direction must equal the current final candle direction; optionally require equal directions for every corresponding candle. For every corresponding candle, absolute upper-wick and lower-wick differences must each be <= the live wick tolerance, and absolute body difference must be <= the live body tolerance. Optionally require every absolute volume-ratio difference <= its tolerance, absolute sequence-momentum difference <= its tolerance, and the same UTC session. Sessions are 00:00–08:00, 08:00–13:00, 13:00–21:00, and 21:00–24:00 UTC. Each match's outcome is the percentage change from its stored close to its close at the projection horizon. The resolution pointer advances through records by array index and is not adjusted when the oldest record is removed.
Calculation: Live tolerances = initialize wick and body tolerances from their respective inputs. Optionally, after at least five resolved projections have fit scores, on each bar increase both tolerances by the tuning step when the mean of the latest fit-score window is below the lower threshold, or decrease both when it is above the higher threshold; clamp each tolerance between one quarter and four times its input. A projection's fit score is 100 times clamp(1 − abs(projected percentage change − realized percentage change) / max(abs(realized percentage change), 0.001), 0, 1). Store a projection for the current sequence only when the number of historical matches meets the minimum; its projected percentage change is the configured weighted percentile of all match outcomes. Sort outcomes ascending and select the first whose cumulative weight reaches the percentile of total weight. A match's weight is 1 when weighting is disabled; otherwise it is its fit score divided by 100 when positive, 0.5 when its stored projection was unavailable, and 0.1 otherwise.
Long: Number of historical matches >= minimum required AND number of matches with outcome > 0 > number of matches with outcome <= 0.
Short: Number of historical matches >= minimum required AND number of matches with outcome > 0 < number of matches with outcome <= 0.
