Calculation: Active resistance and support levels = On each bar, first remove levels whose age since their pivot bar exceeds a maximum age. Confirm pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side. A pivot high qualifies when its high minus the higher high of its two immediate neighbors is at least a strength multiplier times current ATR; a pivot low qualifies when the lower low of its two immediate neighbors minus its low is at least that amount. If the strength multiplier is zero, skip this strength test. Add each qualifying pivot as a resistance or support level, respectively, if that level type is enabled, fewer than the maximum number of levels of that type are active, and its distance from every active level of the same type is at least a merge multiplier times current ATR. Use a configurable positive fallback value when ATR is unavailable or zero. After adding levels, evaluate break decisions and remove each broken level.
Long: Close > any active resistance level + a break sensitivity multiplier times current ATR, using the same fallback for unavailable or zero ATR.
Short: Close < any active support level - a break sensitivity multiplier times current ATR, using the same fallback for unavailable or zero ATR.
