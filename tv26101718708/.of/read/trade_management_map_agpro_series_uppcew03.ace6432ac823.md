Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR when available, otherwise current true range, floored at a positive minimum.
Calculation: Long trend = short-period EMA of close > long-period EMA of close AND change in long-period EMA over a lookback >= -Safe ATR × slope tolerance.
Calculation: Short trend = short-period EMA of close < long-period EMA of close AND change in long-period EMA over the same lookback <= Safe ATR × slope tolerance.
Calculation: Preferred side = configured side in fixed-side mode; in automatic mode, long if only Long trend holds, short if only Short trend holds, otherwise long if close >= long-period EMA of close and short if close < long-period EMA of close.
Calculation: Long invalidation = the lower of the lowest low over the invalidation lookback including the activation bar and the activation close minus Safe ATR at activation × minimum-risk multiplier; fix this value at activation.
Calculation: Short invalidation = the higher of the highest high over the invalidation lookback including the activation bar and the activation close plus Safe ATR at activation × minimum-risk multiplier; fix this value at activation.
Long: Chart bar is complete AND more than the activation lookback of bars have elapsed AND close > highest high over the activation lookback ending at the previous bar AND preferred side is long AND the configured side permits long AND (confirmation mode is close only OR (Long trend AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR its moving average is nonpositive OR volume >= its moving average × volume multiplier))) AND (no map exists OR the previous map is inactive OR bars since its activation >= map cooldown).
Short: Chart bar is complete AND more than the activation lookback of bars have elapsed AND close < lowest low over the activation lookback ending at the previous bar AND preferred side is short AND the configured side permits short AND (confirmation mode is close only OR (Short trend AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume is unavailable or nonpositive OR its moving average is nonpositive OR volume >= its moving average × volume multiplier))) AND (no map exists OR the previous map is inactive OR bars since its activation >= map cooldown).
Exit: long, chart bar is complete AND the active map side is long AND close < Long invalidation.
Exit: short, chart bar is complete AND the active map side is short AND close > Short invalidation.
