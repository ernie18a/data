Calculation: Formation = for each enabled higher timeframe, evaluate the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the two bars preceding it when the next higher-timeframe bar completes; call these three bars newest, middle, and oldest.
Calculation: Gap = newest low minus oldest high for a bullish formation, or oldest low minus newest high for a bearish formation. Minimum gap = either a parameter times higher-timeframe ATR at the newest bar, or a parameter times the newest close.
Calculation: Estimated buy volume for a bar = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or volume / 2 when high = low. Formation volume = sum of the three formation-bar volumes. Formation delta = twice the sum of their estimated buy volumes minus formation volume. Formation absorption = sum over the three bars of volume × ((high − max(open, close)) + (min(open, close) − low)) / (high − low) / 2, using zero for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Formation strength = 10 times the weighted average of six components, each clamped to [0, 1]: gap / (1.5 × higher-timeframe ATR); directional formation delta / formation volume; formation volume / (6 × higher-timeframe volume SMA); absolute formation delta / max(formation volume, 1); absolute middle-bar body / middle-bar range; and twice formation absorption / max(formation volume, 1). Directional delta is formation delta for bullish formations and its negative for bearish formations. Use zero for a component whose relevant volume, SMA, or range denominator is zero. ATR and volume SMA are measured at the newest formation bar.
Calculation: Active zones = retain each new bullish or bearish formation whose strength is at least a threshold. Its initial bounds are the gap bounds, optionally replaced by a band centered on their midpoint with height equal to a parameter times chart ATR or chart close. Optionally repeatedly merge overlapping same-direction active zones within a parameter times chart ATR: retain the earlier zone, center it at the formation-volume-weighted mean of the two zone midpoints using max(volume, 1) as each weight, preserve the earlier zone's height unless normalization is enabled, in which case use the current normalized height, sum formation volumes and deltas, and retain the union of their timeframe memberships. Keep only the newest configured maximum number of zones, counting filled zones retained as history. A zone becomes inactive when chart low reaches its upper bound, midpoint, or lower bound for bullish zones, or chart high reaches its lower bound, midpoint, or upper bound for bearish zones, according to the selected touch, half-fill, or full-fill rule. Apply zone creation, merging, and the maximum count before mitigation on each confirmed chart bar.
Calculation: Timeframe zone delta = sum of formation deltas of active zones containing that higher timeframe. Aggregate zone delta = sum of formation deltas of all active zones. Chart delta = twice the chart bar's estimated buy volume minus chart volume. Smoothed chart delta = EMA of chart delta divided by max(EMA of chart volume using the same lookback, 1).
Long: A bullish formation has newest low > oldest high AND gap >= minimum gap AND its formation time differs from the last detected bullish formation time for that higher timeframe.
Short: A bearish formation has newest high < oldest low AND gap >= minimum gap AND its formation time differs from the last detected bearish formation time for that higher timeframe.
Long: At least one active zone contains the higher timeframe AND timeframe zone delta > 0.
Short: At least one active zone contains the higher timeframe AND timeframe zone delta < 0.
Long: Aggregate zone delta > 0.
Short: Aggregate zone delta < 0.
Long: Chart delta >= 0.
Short: Chart delta < 0.
Long: Smoothed chart delta >= 0.
Short: Smoothed chart delta < 0.
