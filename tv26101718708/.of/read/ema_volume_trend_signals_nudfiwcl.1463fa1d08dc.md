Calculation: Chart EMA structure = a short-term EMA and two longer core EMAs of close; core upper and lower are their maximum and minimum. Use the same core EMA lengths on the higher timeframe, using its last completed bar for close, EMAs and SuperTrend.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), or zero when the SMA is not positive. ATR, ADX, DI and RSI use chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the low and open of the most recent bearish candle within a lookback, recorded when close breaks above the preceding lookback's highest high and close minus open is at least an ATR displacement threshold. Its age is measured from that bearish candle. A retest occurs while its age is within a maximum and low is at most its open plus an ATR buffer and close is at least its low minus that buffer; a reclaim additionally requires close above its open and above open. Order-block calculations are optional.
Calculation: OBV divergence = compare consecutive confirmed simultaneous price and OBV pivots, with equal left and right pivot lengths. Bullish divergence requires a lower price low and higher OBV low; bearish divergence requires a higher price high and lower OBV high. Age is measured from the pivot bar, although divergence becomes known only upon confirmation. Each direction is optional; a divergence is recent through its maximum age.
Calculation: Cycle state = buy stage, sell stage, last buy and sell bars, last buy price, weighted average buy price, peak high since entry, core-confirmed flag and long-cycle flag. Initialize stages to zero and flags false. Core-confirmed becomes true when the core breakout condition or stable core condition occurs. If the higher-timeframe filter fails while outside a long cycle, reset both stages and core-confirmed. On a buy, update the average using the configured buy-percentage weight, update the peak with the current high, record the bar and close, set the long-cycle flag, increment buy stage, and optionally reset sell stage when buy stage exceeds one. On a partial sell, record the bar, increment sell stage and decrement buy stage to a minimum of zero. On a full exit, reset both stages, average entry, peak and long-cycle flag. Update peak with each bar's high while in a long cycle and buy stage is positive. Evaluate conditions before applying the current bar's signal updates; use the current bar's updated peak for stop conditions. Last buy and sell bars and last buy price persist across full exits.
Calculation: Stable core = the minimum of close minus core upper over a confirmation lookback. Held fast EMA = the minimum of close minus the short-term EMA over a hold lookback. Recent touch = the minimum of low minus either the short-term EMA or core upper over a pullback lookback. Recent drop = the highest high over that pullback lookback minus close. Setup range = highest high minus lowest low over a setup lookback. Each minimum uses the corresponding historical EMA on each bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish value = the last completed higher-timeframe close minus its core upper, or its SuperTrend direction. The filter passes if disabled, or that close is above that core upper, or that SuperTrend is bullish.
Calculation: Raw core risk = current and previous closes both below their respective core lower. Raw weak risk = bullish SuperTrend AND structure-risk filter passes AND (optional confirmed fast-EMA reduction OR optional momentum weakness OR optional weak rally), where structure risk passes when disabled, raw core risk is true, or close is below bullish SuperTrend. Confirmed fast-EMA reduction requires the highest close-minus-fast-EMA over its confirmation lookback to be below negative current ATR times its buffer. Momentum weakness requires close below fast EMA minus its ATR buffer, DI+ falling over its weakness lookback, and, if required, DI- above DI+. Weak rally requires close above previous close but below fast EMA, volume ratio at or below its weak-volume threshold, and DI+ falling over its weakness lookback.
Calculation: Recent risk age = bars since raw weak risk, raw core risk or bearish SuperTrend; post-sell risk age = bars since raw weak risk or raw core risk. Missing ages pass their respective age limits.
Calculation: Bear pressure = a bearish candle with volume ratio at least its pressure threshold AND (close below fast EMA OR open minus close at least an ATR body threshold). The optional volume-price filter passes if no bear pressure occurred within its lookback, or close is above open and previous high and volume ratio is at least its reclaim threshold.
Calculation: Add trend health = bullish SuperTrend below close by at least an ATR distance, ADX at or above its threshold, optionally ADX at least its previous value, and optionally DI+ above DI-. Reclaim trend health uses the same conditions without the ADX-rising requirement. Setup trend health requires bullish SuperTrend below close and, optionally, DI+ above DI-.
Calculation: Momentum exhaustion = optional RSI at or above its overbought threshold OR optional DI+ falling over its exhaustion lookback OR (optional high within an ATR buffer of the highest high over a cycle lookback AND (optional RSI overbought OR optional DI+ exhaustion OR recent bearish OBV divergence)).
Calculation: Core breakout = higher-timeframe filter passes AND bullish SuperTrend AND close above core upper AND (volume ratio at least its breakout threshold OR stable core above zero).
Calculation: Reference buy = higher-timeframe filter passes AND bullish SuperTrend AND held fast EMA above zero AND close above fast EMA and open AND recent risk age exceeds its limit AND no recent bearish OBV divergence AND (order-block reclaim OR recent bullish OBV divergence).
