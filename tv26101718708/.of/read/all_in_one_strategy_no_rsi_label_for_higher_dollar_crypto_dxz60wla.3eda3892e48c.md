Calculation: True dip = over the lookback bars, close is below the first bar’s open, the highest high is above that open, the lowest low is below it, the highest high is above the previous bar’s open, the lowest low is below it, and the previous bar’s close is below its open
Calculation: True peak = over the lookback bars, close is above the first bar’s open, the highest high is above that open, the lowest low is above it, the highest high is above the previous bar’s open, the lowest low is below it, and the previous bar’s close is above its open
Calculation: RSI high = average RSI of bars in the lookback whose RSI is above the minimum threshold
Calculation: RSI low = average RSI of bars in the lookback whose RSI is below the minimum threshold
Calculation: RSI of OHLC4 = RSI calculated from each bar’s average of open, high, low, and close
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI of OHLC4, using the RSI length
Calculation: Fast RSI EMA = double EMA of RSI of OHLC4, using the shorter EMA length
Calculation: Slow RSI EMA = double EMA of RSI of OHLC4, using the longer EMA length
Calculation: MACD = difference between the fast and slow EMAs of close; signal line = EMA of MACD using the RSI length
Calculation: K = configured moving average of the stochastic of RSI using RSI high and RSI low; D = SMA of K
Calculation: Connors moving averages = SMAs of close using three lookbacks, with the first shorter than the second and the second shorter than the third
Calculation: Overall change = percentage change from the open at the start of the lookback to the current close
Calculation: To high = percentage change from the open at the start of the lookback to the highest high in the lookback
Calculation: To low = percentage change from the open at the start of the lookback to the lowest low in the lookback
Calculation: Previous to high = percentage change from the previous bar’s open to the highest high in the lookback
Calculation: Previous to low = percentage change from the previous bar’s open to the lowest low in the lookback
Calculation: Previous bar change = percentage change from the previous bar’s open to its close

Short: Crossover(Fast RSI EMA, Slow RSI EMA) AND datetime is within the configured trading window OR MACD < signal line AND RSI > RSI high AND Overall change > 0 AND datetime is within the configured trading window OR first Connors moving average > second Connors moving average AND open > third Connors moving average AND RSI >= overbought threshold AND True peak AND datetime is within the configured trading window OR crossunder(K, D) AND datetime is within the configured trading window OR RSI crosses below overbought threshold AND Overall change < 0 AND datetime is within the configured trading window OR RSI > overbought threshold AND True peak AND datetime is within the configured trading window OR flat position AND short trades enabled AND Smoothed RSI < RSI AND D < K AND the selected entry signal is the reverse-RSI, MACD, Connors, stochastic, or RSI signal, with its corresponding condition above
Exit: long, low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction) OR long position AND long trades enabled AND Smoothed RSI > RSI AND D > K AND the selected exit signal is the reverse-RSI, MACD, Connors, or RSI signal, with its corresponding short signal condition above
