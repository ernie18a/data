Calculation: Upper Bollinger Band = EMA(close, lookback) + multiplier × standard deviation(close, same lookback)
Calculation: Lower Bollinger Band = EMA(close, lookback) − multiplier × standard deviation(close, same lookback)
Calculation: Average volume = SMA(volume, lookback)
Long: close > upper Bollinger Band AND previous close ≤ previous upper Bollinger Band AND volume > average volume × volume threshold
Short: close < lower Bollinger Band AND previous close ≥ previous lower Bollinger Band AND volume > average volume × volume threshold
