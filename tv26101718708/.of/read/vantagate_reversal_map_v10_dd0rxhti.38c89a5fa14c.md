Calculation: Lower zone event = low reaches or crosses below the regression center minus its ATR-scaled entry distance AND (low breaks the prior-bars liquidity low and close recovers above it OR close is above open and its close location within the candle range meets the rejection threshold)
Calculation: Upper zone event = high reaches or crosses above the regression center plus its ATR-scaled entry distance AND (high breaks the prior-bars liquidity high and close falls below it OR close is below open and its close location within the candle range meets the rejection threshold)
Calculation: Zone ready = the corresponding zone event occurred on the current bar or within the zone-memory lookback
Calculation: VantaField = a short-lookback EMA of a weighted sum of normalized stretch, normalized velocity, velocity minus its short-lookback EMA, and upper-wick fraction minus lower-wick fraction scaled by volume surge; stretch is (hlc3 minus its longer-lookback EMA) divided by ATR; velocity is the change in hlc3 over a shorter lookback divided by ATR times the square root of that lookback; volume surge is volume divided by its longer-lookback EMA, minus one, clamped between zero and its upper bound
Calculation: VantaSpring = VantaNatural released toward 50 as the consecutive-bar age of an upper or lower VantaNatural extreme increases; VantaNatural is 50 plus 50 times VantaField divided by (1 plus the absolute value of VantaField)
Calculation: VantaGravity = recursive adaptive-alpha smoothing of VantaSpring, initialized to the first VantaSpring; alpha decreases as the absolute distance of VantaSpring from 50 increases
Long: Lower zone event OR (VantaSpring crosses above VantaGravity AND the lowest VantaSpring in its lookback is at or below its lower extreme threshold AND VantaSpring is below 50 AND VantaField is greater than its prior-bar value AND lower zone ready)
Short: Upper zone event OR (VantaSpring crosses below VantaGravity AND the highest VantaSpring in its lookback is at or above its upper extreme threshold AND VantaSpring is above 50 AND VantaField is less than its prior-bar value AND upper zone ready)
