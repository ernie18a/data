Calculation: Volume profile levels = For each chart bar, after at least one profile lookback of prior bars is available, divide the highest high to lowest low of the current rolling lookback into equal price rows. Allocate each bar's selected volume-source value equally across all rows touched by its low-to-high range, clamping row indices to the valid range and treating missing volume-source values as zero. Select the first lowest-index row with the greatest volume as the point of control; if no row has positive volume, select the lowest row. Starting there, expand the value area one row at a time toward the larger adjacent row volume, choosing the upper row on ties, until the selected fraction of total volume is reached or no rows remain. The point of control is its row center; the value-area high and low are the outer edges of the selected rows. Carry the last levels forward when the price range is zero.
Calculation: Smoothed levels = EMA of each volume profile level, using the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Trailing bands = Raw lower band is smoothed value-area low minus ATR multiplied by an ATR buffer; raw upper band is smoothed value-area high plus ATR multiplied by the same buffer. If the previous close is above the previous lower band, the lower band is the maximum of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is the raw value. If the previous close is below the previous upper band, the upper band is the minimum of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is the raw value. Substitute the current raw band when its previous value is unavailable.
Calculation: Trend = Initially bullish. If the previous trend is bearish and the selected bullish test price exceeds the previous upper band, switch to bullish; otherwise, if the previous trend is bullish and the selected bearish test price is below the previous lower band, switch to bearish; otherwise retain the previous trend. The test prices are both close in close mode, or high for the bullish test and low for the bearish test in wick mode. Substitute the current band when the previous band is unavailable.
Calculation: Trend strength = Use ATR if positive; otherwise use an SMA of absolute close-to-previous-close changes as the volatility denominator. Distance and slope are zero if the smoothed point of control is unavailable or the denominator is zero; otherwise distance is the absolute difference between close and smoothed point of control divided by the denominator, and slope is the absolute change in smoothed point of control over the smoothing length divided by the denominator, substituting the current point of control when the earlier value is unavailable. Strength is min(100, 40 × min(distance, 3) / 3 + 30 × min(slope, 2) / 2 + position score), where position score is 30 if close is above the smoothed value-area high or below the smoothed value-area low, 10 if strictly between them, and 20 otherwise.
Long: Trend equals bullish.
Short: Trend equals bearish.
Long: Previous trend equals bearish AND current trend equals bullish.
Short: Previous trend equals bullish AND current trend equals bearish.
Long: Previous trend equals bearish AND current trend equals bullish AND trend strength is greater than or equal to the minimum strength threshold.
Short: Previous trend equals bullish AND current trend equals bearish AND trend strength is greater than or equal to the minimum strength threshold.
Long: Close is above the smoothed value-area high.
Short: Close is below the smoothed value-area low.
