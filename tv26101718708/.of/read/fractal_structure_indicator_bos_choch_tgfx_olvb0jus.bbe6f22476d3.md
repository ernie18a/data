Calculation: Fractal high = bar whose high is the highest among N bars on each side (Williams pivot); fractal low = bar whose low is the lowest among N bars on each side. Confirmed only after N further bars close. Computed on the chart timeframe in two layers: swing (larger N) and internal (smaller N).
Calculation: Active FH (per layer) = most recent confirmed fractal high of that layer that no close has yet exceeded; a newly confirmed fractal high replaces it and re-arms it. Active FL (per layer) = most recent confirmed fractal low of that layer that no close has yet fallen below; a newly confirmed fractal low replaces it and re-arms it. Each active level triggers only once, on the first close beyond it.
Long: current bar close > Active FH of the swing layer OR current bar close > Active FH of the internal layer (covers both continuation and reversal breaks)
Short: current bar close < Active FL of the swing layer OR current bar close < Active FL of the internal layer (covers both continuation and reversal breaks)
