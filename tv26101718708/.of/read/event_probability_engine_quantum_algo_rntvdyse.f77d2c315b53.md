Calculation: Event states = each weekday Monday through Friday; RSI below its lower adaptive percentile threshold or above its upper adaptive percentile threshold, with fixed thresholds used when adaptation is disabled or unavailable; volume z-score above its threshold; consecutive falling closes or consecutive rising closes at least as numerous as the streak threshold; close in the lower or upper portion of the rolling high-low range; ATR percentile rank above its upper threshold or below its lower threshold; enabled full-moon or new-moon phase from bar datetime; and close within an ATR-multiple distance of the latest confirmed pivot low or pivot high. Volume z-score is (volume − rolling mean volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume, or zero when that deviation is zero. Range position is (close − rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low), or 0.5 when the range is zero. Pivots require equal numbers of bars on both sides for confirmation. Lunar phase is abs((datetime in Unix milliseconds / 86400000 + 2440587.5 − 2451550.1) modulo 29.530588853) / 29.530588853; full and new moons use intervals around 0.5 and 0 respectively, with the new-moon interval wrapping at 1.
Calculation: Event statistics = for each event and each of three horizons, retain a capped FIFO history of close-to-close returns realized after the event, tagged by whether the event-bar close was above its EMA. The horizons are one bar, a longer horizon, and a still longer horizon. At each bar, use samples matching the current EMA regime if their count reaches the regime minimum; otherwise use all samples. A win has return > 0. Effective sample size is max(selected sample count / horizon, 1); shrunk win rate is (win fraction × effective sample size + 0.5 × shrinkage strength) / (effective sample size + shrinkage strength). With no samples, use 0.5. Mean return is the selected returns' arithmetic mean, or zero with no samples. Profit factor is the sum of positive returns divided by the absolute sum of nonpositive returns; if the denominator is zero, use a value above 1 when the positive sum is positive and 1 otherwise.
Calculation: Composite probability = for every currently active event whose selected sample count at the longest horizon reaches the minimum, calculate quality from that horizon: 100 × abs(shrunk win rate − 0.5)^0.6 × 2^0.6 × min(sample count / quality sample scale, 1) × max(1 − 2 × abs(recent win fraction − shrunk win rate), consistency floor). The recent win fraction uses the most recent samples from the event's full, unfiltered longest-horizon history. Weight is max(quality, weight floor) / 100. For each horizon, clamp each included event's shrunk win rate to an interior probability interval, take its log-odds, and calculate the weighted mean log-odds. Multiply by min(sqrt(max(number of included events, 1)), agreement cap), then apply the logistic function. If no event qualifies, probability is 0.5. Composite mean return is the weighted mean of included events' mean returns, or zero if none qualify.
Long: Close > chart-timeframe EMA.
Short: Close <= chart-timeframe EMA.
Long: Close > EMA of closes from the separate timeframe; use its last completed bar on historical bars and its still-forming bar on realtime bars. The separate timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: Close <= EMA of closes from the separate timeframe; use its last completed bar on historical bars and its still-forming bar on realtime bars. The separate timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.

Short: RSI > upper threshold AND previous-bar RSI <= its previous-bar upper threshold.

Short: Any enabled event's shrunk win rate at any horizon <= 0.5.

Short: Any enabled event's longest-horizon shrunk win rate < lower bias threshold.
