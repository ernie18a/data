Calculation: Ribbon = a shortest-lookback Hull moving average, a longer-lookback EMA, and three progressively longer-lookback Kaufman adaptive moving averages, all applied to the selected chart price source.
Calculation: Direction = 1 when the Hull moving average is above the longest Kaufman average, -1 when below it, and 0 when equal.
Calculation: Adaptive volatility stop = for close, smooth the Kaufman efficiency ratio with an EMA; square its interpolation between the fast and slow EMA smoothing constants, and use the reciprocal, clamped to the fast and slow ATR-period bounds, as the period of a recursively smoothed true range. Multiply that range by a base multiplier reduced above the upper efficiency threshold and increased below the lower threshold. Initialize the stop below close and its direction up. While up, trail the stop upward at close minus the distance; while down, trail it downward at close plus the distance. If close falls below the up stop, change direction to down and reset the stop to close plus the distance; if close rises above the down stop, change direction to up and reset it to close minus the distance.
Calculation: Flow = within each chart bar, sum the signs of successive lower-timeframe completed-bar close changes weighted by the later sub-bar's volume, or by one in time-at-price mode; divide by the sum of those weights. If the weight sum is zero, use the sign of the chart close change, weighted by chart volume or one. Standardize the resulting ratio by its rolling mean and standard deviation, using zero when the deviation is zero. Flow agreement is the chart Direction times this standardized ratio, divided by a scaling parameter and clamped to [0,1]. Flow toxicity is the rolling mean of the absolute unstandardized flow ratio.
Calculation: Effort versus result = normalize each of volume, absolute candle body, and absolute close change by its own rolling mean using the ordered ratio-to-score steps. Give body and close-change scores their respective signs. Average the signed body score, signed close-change score, close location within the current range, body divided by current range, close location within the combined current and previous ranges, and close change divided by that combined range; scale consistently and clamp to [-1,1]. Divide the absolute result by normalized volume. Assign quality according to the ordered high-price-and-high-volume, high-ratio, low-ratio, and remaining categories. The component is absolute result times quality when its sign equals Direction, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Period profile = at each selected calendar-period boundary, create equally sized price bins over a fixed ATR multiple above and below the boundary bar's close. For every bar, allocate its volume equally across all bins touched by its high-low range, or add one to each touched bin in time-at-price mode. The point of control is the center of the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest bin on ties. Starting there, add the larger adjacent bin, choosing the upper bin on ties, until the configured fraction of total profile volume is included; the outside edges are the value-area high and low.
Calculation: Location component = weighted average of four components: (1) Direction times the selected-period VWAP slope divided by ATR and multiplied by a gain, clamped to [0,1]; (2) the average of an indicator that close is on Direction's side of swing-anchored VWAP and Direction times that VWAP's slope divided by ATR and multiplied by the gain, clamped to [0,1]; (3) a high score when close is beyond the value area in Direction, an intermediate score inside it or when unavailable, and zero beyond it against Direction; (4) a prior-period reference score. Re-anchor swing VWAP on the confirmation bar of either a pivot high or pivot low. At each period boundary retain the previous period's final VWAP and profile point of control. Give the prior-reference score its acceptance value if close crosses either retained level in Direction, its neutral value if Direction is zero or no retained level lies ahead, otherwise the value associated with the nearest level ahead in ATR-distance bands. Use the previous close against the current retained level for crossing tests.
Calculation: Conviction = clamp to [0,1] the weighted average of flow agreement, effort versus result, close-based Kaufman efficiency ratio, and location component, multiplied by an optional flow-toxicity penalty and an optional higher-timeframe agreement multiplier. The penalty decreases linearly above its toxicity threshold. For agreement, calculate adaptive volatility-stop direction independently on three progressively higher timeframes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; count those agreeing with Direction and apply the corresponding full-agreement bonus, neutral multiplier, or partial- or no-agreement penalty. If Direction is zero, use a neutral agreement multiplier. Use chart volume when usable and otherwise time-at-price weights; the volume-availability mode may also be selected explicitly.
Long: The Hull moving average crosses above any selected longer ribbon average AND conviction is at least its threshold AND either the session filter is disabled or the configured initial bars of the calendar period have passed AND either the clearance filter is disabled or close is above the longest-lookback selected average crossed on this bar by more than the configured ATR multiple.
Short: The Hull moving average crosses below any selected longer ribbon average AND conviction is at least its threshold AND either the session filter is disabled or the configured initial bars of the calendar period have passed AND either the clearance filter is disabled or close is below the longest-lookback selected average crossed on this bar by more than the configured ATR multiple.
Long: The Hull moving average crosses above the longest ribbon average AND conviction is at least its threshold AND either the session filter is disabled or the configured initial bars of the calendar period have passed.
Short: The Hull moving average crosses below the longest ribbon average AND conviction is at least its threshold AND either the session filter is disabled or the configured initial bars of the calendar period have passed.
Long: Close moves from not above to above the period-profile value-area high.
Short: Close moves from not below to below the period-profile value-area low.
Long: Close crosses above the prior-period VWAP OR the prior-period point of control.
Short: Close crosses below the prior-period VWAP OR the prior-period point of control.
Long: The adaptive volatility stop changes from down to up.
Short: The adaptive volatility stop changes from up to down.
Exit: long, close falls below the up-direction adaptive volatility stop.
Exit: short, close rises above the down-direction adaptive volatility stop.
Long: The shorter of two adjacent ribbon averages is above the longer.
Short: The shorter of two adjacent ribbon averages is below the longer.
Long: All ribbon averages are ordered from highest to lowest in increasing lookback order.
Short: All ribbon averages are ordered from lowest to highest in increasing lookback order.
Long: The selected-period VWAP slope is nonnegative.
Short: The selected-period VWAP slope is negative.
Long: Close is above swing-anchored VWAP.
Short: Close is at or below swing-anchored VWAP.
Long: The adaptive volatility-stop direction is up on the chart timeframe OR on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar.
Short: The adaptive volatility-stop direction is down on the chart timeframe OR on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar.
