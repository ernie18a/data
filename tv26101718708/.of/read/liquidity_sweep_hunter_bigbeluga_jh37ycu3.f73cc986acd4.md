Calculation: Volume change % = 100 × (bar volume − previous bar volume) / bar volume
Calculation: Support level = low at a confirmed pivot low, where the pivot low is lower than the lows across an equal-length lookback on both sides, and volume change % at the pivot bar exceeds the filter threshold
Calculation: Resistance level = high at a confirmed pivot high, where the pivot high is higher than the highs across an equal-length lookback on both sides, and volume change % at the pivot bar exceeds the filter threshold
Long: On a completed bar, low < an active support level AND close > that level
Short: On a completed bar, high > an active resistance level AND close < that level
