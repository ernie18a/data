Calculation: Higher-timeframe range = high minus low of a completed higher-timeframe bar. Average range = mean of available completed higher-timeframe ranges up to a lookback length.
Calculation: Completed higher-timeframe bar classification = consolidation if its range divided by the average range of preceding completed higher-timeframe bars is at or below a lower threshold, or if that average is unavailable or zero; expansion if the ratio is at or above a higher threshold; otherwise reversal. Check expansion before consolidation.
Calculation: Session high and low = maximum high and minimum low, respectively, of chart bars immediately preceding bars inside the configured session since the current higher-timeframe bar began; reset both at the start of each higher-timeframe bar. Update them before evaluating the session sweep.
Calculation: Range zone = current forming higher-timeframe bar open plus or minus its average range multiplied by a zone multiplier. Calculate two zones with a smaller and a larger multiplier using completed higher-timeframe ranges, including the most recently completed range.
Long: All of the specified number of most recent completed higher-timeframe bars are classified as consolidation AND current forming higher-timeframe bar open < midpoint of the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar high and low.
Long: All of the specified number of most recent completed higher-timeframe bars are classified as reversal AND current forming higher-timeframe bar open < most recently completed higher-timeframe bar close.
Short: All of the specified number of most recent completed higher-timeframe bars are classified as consolidation AND current forming higher-timeframe bar open >= midpoint of the most recently completed higher-timeframe bar high and low.
Short: All of the specified number of most recent completed higher-timeframe bars are classified as reversal AND current forming higher-timeframe bar open >= most recently completed higher-timeframe bar close.
Long: Previous chart bar low < most recently completed higher-timeframe bar low AND previous chart bar close > that low.
Short: Previous chart bar high > most recently completed higher-timeframe bar high AND previous chart bar close < that high.
Long: Previous chart bar low < a configured session low AND previous chart bar close > that session low AND absolute previous chart bar body divided by its high-low range >= body threshold AND previous chart bar high > previous chart bar low.
Short: Previous chart bar high > a configured session high AND previous chart bar close < that session high AND absolute previous chart bar body divided by its high-low range >= body threshold AND previous chart bar high > previous chart bar low.
Long: Previous chart bar low > high three chart bars ago AND either the previous chart bar low or high is within either range zone.
Short: Previous chart bar high < low three chart bars ago AND either the previous chart bar low or high is within either range zone.
