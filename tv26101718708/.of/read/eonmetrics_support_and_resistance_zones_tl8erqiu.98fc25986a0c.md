Calculation: Median range = median of high − low over the 200 most recent chart bars, floored at the minimum price tick.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured number of chart bars on each side; available on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Active zones = create a resistance zone at each valid confirmed swing high and a support zone at each valid confirmed swing low, initially extending by the configured fraction of the current median range on either side, with a minimum width of one tick. Reject a candidate if a close between pivot and confirmation breached its proposed edge plus its creation-time break margin. Merge a candidate into the nearest active zone of the same role within the configured median-range tolerance; widen merged boundaries to include the pivot, never shrink them. Store each zone's creation-time break margin. Count the founding pivot as one touch; thereafter count one touch when price range re-enters after being outside. A close beyond the zone edge plus its stored margin breaks it, flips its role, and freezes its touches; do not count a break bar as a touch. Remove active zones after the configured consecutive bars without overlap, retain broken zones for the configured period, and enforce the configured zone cap by removing the least-touched zone, oldest first on a tie.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, price range overlaps an active support zone with touches at or above the configured visibility threshold, the preceding bar's range did not overlap that zone, and the close does not break it.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, price range overlaps an active resistance zone with touches at or above the configured visibility threshold, the preceding bar's range did not overlap that zone, and the close does not break it.
Exit: long, an active support zone with touches at or above the configured visibility threshold is broken by a confirmed close below its lower boundary minus its stored break margin.
Exit: short, an active resistance zone with touches at or above the configured visibility threshold is broken by a confirmed close above its upper boundary plus its stored break margin.
