Calculation: Strength = on the signal bar, calculate a weighted average of five components, then multiply by 10; if the sum of weights is zero, use zero. Clamp each component to [0,1]: trend = (1 + side × (shorter-period EMA − longer-period EMA) / A) / 2; RSI conviction = 1.6 × abs(RSI − 50) / 50; calm = 1 − ATR percentile rank / 100; proximity = 1 − abs(close − shorter-period EMA) / (2 × A); momentum = (1 + side × change in shorter-period EMA over a lookback / A) / 2. Side is +1 for long and −1 for short. A is ATR when positive, otherwise the minimum price increment parameter. ATR percentile rank uses a separate lookback.
Calculation: Strength tier = the number of ascending strength thresholds met, from zero through four.
Calculation: Risk = on the entry bar, take the absolute difference between entry close and the selected raw stop, then clamp it between the ATR-based minimum and maximum risk. The raw stop is entry close minus an ATR multiple for a long or plus it for a short, OR the lowest low over a lookback minus an ATR buffer for a long or the highest high over that lookback plus the buffer for a short. Freeze risk at entry.
Calculation: Stop = entry price − risk for a long, or entry price + risk for a short.
Calculation: Final target = entry price + risk × final target multiple for a long, or entry price − risk × final target multiple for a short.
Long: RSI crosses above the selected midline or oversold threshold AND the trend filter is disabled OR the preceding conditions hold AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND the trend filter is enabled; in either case, strength tier >= minimum tier AND ATR is available AND no position is active AND no position exited on this bar AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown, or there has been no previous entry.
Short: RSI crosses below the selected midline or overbought threshold AND the trend filter is disabled OR the preceding conditions hold AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND the trend filter is enabled; in either case, strength tier >= minimum tier AND ATR is available AND no position is active AND no position exited on this bar AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown, or there has been no previous entry.
Long: close > shorter-period EMA.
Short: close <= shorter-period EMA.
Long: for either configured higher timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar available as of the previous chart bar has close > its shorter-period EMA.
Short: for either configured higher timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar available as of the previous chart bar has close <= its shorter-period EMA.
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target OR bars since entry >= time-stop limit).
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target OR bars since entry >= time-stop limit).
