Calculation: Chart swing pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of chart bars on each side; retain a bounded number of the newest pools on each side and remove a high pool when high exceeds it or a low pool when low falls below it.
Calculation: Pending reversal = when a chart bar breaches a swing pool and closes strictly back inside it, store the pool level and that bar's extreme; a new sweep on the same side replaces the pending reversal. Starting on the next bar, cancel it if close crosses strictly beyond the pool in the original breach direction; otherwise update the extreme with any deeper wick, test displacement, then expire it if its age exceeds the displacement window. If several pools on one side are swept on the same bar, use the oldest swept pool. Remove the pending reversal after displacement fires.
Calculation: Displacement threshold = a fixed price distance or a multiple of the previous completed day bar's high minus low, falling back to the fixed distance when that range is unavailable or nonpositive. The day bar is higher than the chart timeframe when the chart is intraday.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing = a completed higher-timeframe bar whose high is strictly above both adjacent bars' highs, or whose low is strictly below both adjacent bars' lows.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe true swing low = a higher-timeframe swing low where either the swing bar closes above its open and the preceding bar closes below its open, or the swing bar closes below its open and the following bar closes above its open. A true swing high reverses these candle directions. All three bars are completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe average range = the mean high minus low over a bounded lookback of completed bars of the same higher timeframe.
Long: A pending low-pool reversal exists AND close is at or above its pool level AND close minus its updated lowest extreme is at least the displacement threshold.
Short: A pending high-pool reversal exists AND close is at or below its pool level AND its updated highest extreme minus close is at least the displacement threshold.
Long: The last completed bar of a higher timeframe has low below a prior higher-timeframe swing low AND close above that swing low AND no intervening higher-timeframe bar has low below that level; select the nearest qualifying swing within a bounded lookback.
Short: The last completed bar of a higher timeframe has high above a prior higher-timeframe swing high AND close below that swing high AND no intervening higher-timeframe bar has high above that level; select the nearest qualifying swing within a bounded lookback.
Long: A completed higher-timeframe bar closes above a prior higher-timeframe true swing high for the first time after the swing's following bar, with both the swing and break within a bounded lookback.
Short: A completed higher-timeframe bar closes below a prior higher-timeframe true swing low for the first time after the swing's following bar, with both the swing and break within a bounded lookback.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe bar's low exceeds the high two higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the gap is at least a multiple of the higher-timeframe average range AND the intervening bar closes above its open with body size at least another multiple of that average range.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe bar's high is below the low two higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the gap is at least a multiple of the higher-timeframe average range AND the intervening bar closes below its open with body size at least another multiple of that average range.
Long: Three consecutive completed higher-timeframe bars each close above their opens AND each of the latter two opens above the preceding close AND the last open minus the first close is at least a multiple of the higher-timeframe average range AND both time gaps between bar opens are within a specified multiple of the higher timeframe's duration.
Short: Three consecutive completed higher-timeframe bars each close below their opens AND each of the latter two opens below the preceding close AND the first close minus the last open is at least a multiple of the higher-timeframe average range AND both time gaps between bar opens are within a specified multiple of the higher timeframe's duration.
Long: Close is at or above the forming day bar's open; the day bar is higher than the chart timeframe when the chart is intraday.
Short: Close is below the forming day bar's open; the day bar is higher than the chart timeframe when the chart is intraday.
