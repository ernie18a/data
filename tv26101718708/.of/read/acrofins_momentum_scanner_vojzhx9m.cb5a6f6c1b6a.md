Calculation: Bull score = 2 × (close > highest high of the prior breakout lookback bars) + 1 each for (volume > its lookback average × spike factor AND close > open), (current-chart RSI > bull threshold), (higher-timeframe RSI > midpoint, using the last completed bar), (bullish engulfing: prior close < prior open AND current close > current open AND current open ≤ prior close AND current close ≥ prior open), (close > open AND absolute candle body > its longer-lookback average body × body factor), (positive directional index > negative directional index), (ADX ≥ strong-trend threshold), (close > session VWAP), and (close within one ATR of either Camarilla L3 or L4).
Calculation: Bear score = 2 × (close < lowest low of the prior breakout lookback bars) + 1 each for (volume > its lookback average × spike factor AND close < open), (current-chart RSI < bear threshold), (higher-timeframe RSI < midpoint, using the last completed bar), (bearish engulfing: prior close > prior open AND current close < current open AND current open ≥ prior close AND current close ≤ prior open), (close < open AND absolute candle body > its longer-lookback average body × body factor), (negative directional index > positive directional index), (ADX ≥ strong-trend threshold), (close < session VWAP), and (close within one ATR of either Camarilla H3 or H4).
Calculation: Bollinger Band width = upper band minus lower band; squeeze = width < its 50-bar average × squeeze factor.
Calculation: Camarilla levels use the previous completed higher-timeframe daily bar's high, low, and close: H3 = close + (high − low) × H3 factor; H4 = close + (high − low) × H4 factor; L3 = close − (high − low) × H3 factor; L4 = close − (high − low) × H4 factor.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier; long target = entry price + ATR at entry × target multiplier. Short stop = entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier; short target = entry price − ATR at entry × target multiplier.
Calculation: For a long, favorable extreme is the highest high since entry; for a short, it is the lowest low since entry. Once the favorable extreme moves at least current ATR × trail-activation multiplier beyond entry, long trailing stop is the greater of its prior value (initially the long stop) and favorable extreme − current ATR × trail multiplier; short trailing stop is the lesser of its prior value (initially the short stop) and favorable extreme + current ATR × trail multiplier.
Long: bull score ≥ minimum score AND ADX ≥ minimum-trend threshold AND NOT squeeze AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > long-term EMA) AND flat position AND cooldown elapsed.
Short: bear score ≥ minimum score AND ADX ≥ minimum-trend threshold AND NOT squeeze AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA) AND flat position AND cooldown elapsed AND no long entry on this bar.
Exit: Long, close ≤ long stop OR close ≥ long target OR (trailing stop active AND close ≤ trailing stop).
Exit: Short, close ≥ short stop OR close ≤ short target OR (trailing stop active AND close ≥ trailing stop).
