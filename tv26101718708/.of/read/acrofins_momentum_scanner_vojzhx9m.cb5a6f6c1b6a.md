Calculation: Bull score = 2 if close > the highest high over the preceding breakout lookback, plus 1 for each true condition: volume > its moving average × volume multiplier AND close > open; chart-timeframe RSI > bullish RSI threshold; higher-timeframe RSI from the last completed bar > its midpoint; previous close < previous open AND close > open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open; close > open AND absolute candle body > its moving average × body multiplier; positive directional index > negative directional index; ADX >= strong-trend threshold; close > session VWAP of typical price; close is within the current ATR of either Camarilla support level. The body average includes the current bar.
Calculation: Bear score = 2 if close < the lowest low over the preceding breakout lookback, plus 1 for each true condition: volume > its moving average × volume multiplier AND close < open; chart-timeframe RSI < bearish RSI threshold; higher-timeframe RSI from the last completed bar < its midpoint; previous close > previous open AND close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open; close < open AND absolute candle body > its moving average × body multiplier; negative directional index > positive directional index; ADX >= strong-trend threshold; close < session VWAP of typical price; close is within the current ATR of either Camarilla resistance level. The body average includes the current bar.
Calculation: Camarilla levels = previous completed higher-timeframe bar's close plus or minus its high-low range multiplied by either a smaller or a larger positive coefficient; subtraction gives the two support levels and addition gives the two resistance levels.
Calculation: Bollinger width = upper Bollinger band minus lower Bollinger band. The squeeze threshold is a multiplier of the width's moving average over a longer lookback than the Bollinger band lookback; if that average is unavailable, the squeeze condition is false.
Calculation: Trade state = initially flat with zero cooldown. At the start of each bar decrement cooldown toward zero. Process an existing position's trailing update and exit before evaluating entries; an exit sets the position flat and resets cooldown to its configured length. Evaluate both entry conditions using that resulting state; each triggered entry uses the close as entry price. If both entries trigger, both are marked and the short position is retained.
Calculation: Long stop and target = entry price minus and plus, respectively, separate ATR multiples fixed using entry-bar ATR. Short stop and target = entry price plus and minus, respectively, separate ATR multiples fixed using entry-bar ATR.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initially unavailable; initialize best price to entry price and, on each subsequent open-position bar, update it to the maximum of itself and the current high. When best price >= entry price + current ATR × activation multiplier, update the trailing stop to the maximum of its previous value, or the fixed stop if unavailable, and best price - current ATR × trailing multiplier. Otherwise retain its previous value. Short trailing stop = initially unavailable; initialize best price to entry price and update it to the minimum of itself and the current low. When best price <= entry price - current ATR × activation multiplier, update the trailing stop to the minimum of its previous value, or the fixed stop if unavailable, and best price + current ATR × trailing multiplier. Otherwise retain its previous value. Update the trailing stop before testing exits on each bar.
Long: Bull score >= score threshold AND ADX >= minimum trend threshold AND Bollinger width is not below its squeeze threshold AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > slow EMA) AND position is flat AND cooldown = 0.
Short: Bear score >= score threshold AND ADX >= minimum trend threshold AND Bollinger width is not below its squeeze threshold AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < slow EMA) AND position is flat AND cooldown = 0.
Long: Close > slow EMA.
Short: Close <= slow EMA.
Long: Close > session VWAP of typical price.
Short: Close <= session VWAP of typical price.
Long: Chart-timeframe RSI > bullish RSI threshold.
Short: Chart-timeframe RSI < bearish RSI threshold AND chart-timeframe RSI <= bullish RSI threshold.
Long: Bull score >= score threshold.
Short: Bear score >= score threshold AND bull score < score threshold.
Exit: long, close <= fixed stop OR close >= fixed target OR close <= available trailing stop.
Exit: short, close >= fixed stop OR close <= fixed target OR close >= available trailing stop.
