Calculation: Structure pivots = Confirmed symmetric pivot highs and lows, with separate shorter and longer lookbacks for small and large structure; wait an additional maturity period before using an anchor pivot. Store each anchor's ATR at its pivot bar, or at its confirmation bar if unavailable. For each scale, maintain an alternating high/low zigzag: replace the latest same-side pivot only with a more extreme one; accept an opposite-side pivot only if its distance from the latest pivot exceeds that pivot's ATR times the scale's threshold. The large-scale threshold is larger. Process matured large highs, large lows, small highs, then small lows.
Calculation: Active trendlines = When a pivot joins its zigzag, pair it in chronological order with each eligible recent same-side zigzag pivot within the scale's scan depth and anchor lookback. A resistance line joins a higher first high to a lower second high; a support line joins a lower first low to a higher second low. Extend the straight line from the anchors. Its absolute slope must satisfy the enabled minimum and maximum bounds expressed as fractions of the anchors' average ATR. From the first anchor through the formation bar, excluding the second anchor, no candle body may extend above resistance or below support. Count both anchors and intervening confirmed same-side touch pivots within average anchor ATR times the touch tolerance; small lines use touch pivots with a shorter lookback than their anchor pivots, while large lines use the small anchor pivot lookback. Require the minimum touch count. The last eligible break bar is the second anchor bar plus the anchor spacing times min(2 + 0.5 × (touch count − 2), 3.5), truncated to an integer; formation must occur before that bar. When both scales are enabled, reject a small candidate if both anchors lie within the touch band and anchor span of an active same-side large line; an admitted large line retires such small lines. For same-side, same-scale lines whose anchor spans overlap, treat them as overlapping if overlap divided by either line's anchor spacing exceeds the overlap threshold. Reject a candidate if it does not outrank every overlapping active line; otherwise retire those lines. Rank by more touches, then more recent second anchor, then longer anchor spacing, then larger second-anchor pivot scale, then steeper absolute slope. Cap active lines per side and scale by retiring the oldest. On each confirmed bar, process existing lines for breaks before retiring unbroken lines at their last eligible bar, and form new lines afterward.
Calculation: Break = For an active line, count consecutive closes strictly beyond it and reset the count and accumulated volume flag on any other close. Accumulate the volume flag if any bar in the current run has volume greater than a multiplier times its volume SMA. A break occurs when the count reaches the confirmation length, the optional volume flag is set, the optional distance beyond the line is at least an ATR multiple, and the close is on the breakout side of the optional selected close moving average. Retire each broken line immediately. If multiple lines break in the same direction on a bar, retain for negation the line nearest the close: the highest broken resistance or lowest broken support.
Long: A resistance line has an upward break.
Short: A support line has a downward break.
Exit: long, close is below the projected line retained for an upward break within the negation window after that break.
Exit: short, close is above the projected line retained for a downward break within the negation window after that break.
