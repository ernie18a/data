Calculation: Support = lowest low over the lookback, including the current bar; resistance = highest high over the same lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Trading time = the bar's UTC opening hour is within the inclusive configured start and end hours converted to UTC, using OR across midnight; optional weekday-only and weekend-only restrictions use the exchange-timezone day of week, and the optional lunch restriction excludes the configured timezone's lunch hour. If the time filter is disabled, trading time is true.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after the first bar in the position where (close - entry price) / ATR reaches the activation multiplier, retain the maximum of close - ATR × trailing-distance multiplier over bars since entry that meet the activation condition. Short trailing stop = after the first bar in the position where (entry price - close) / ATR reaches the activation multiplier, retain the minimum of close + ATR × trailing-distance multiplier over bars since entry that meet the activation condition. Reset on entry or position closure; the stop remains active after activation even when subsequent bars do not meet the activation condition.
Long: close > support - ATR × support-buffer multiplier AND low <= support AND volume > SMA of volume with the same period as ATR AND (trend filter disabled OR close > selected EMA or SMA of close) AND trading time = true.
Short: close < resistance + ATR × resistance-buffer multiplier AND high >= resistance AND volume > SMA of volume with the same period as ATR AND (trend filter disabled OR close < selected EMA or SMA of close) AND trading time = true.
Exit: long, low <= the stop order level submitted on the previous bar, where the level is the long trailing stop if trailing is enabled and active, otherwise support - ATR × stop multiplier, calculated on that previous bar.
Exit: short, high >= the stop order level submitted on the previous bar, where the level is the short trailing stop if trailing is enabled and active, otherwise resistance + ATR × stop multiplier, calculated on that previous bar.
Exit: long, partial close enabled AND first target not previously hit in this position AND position quantity >= partial-close quantity AND high >= support + ATR × reward-to-risk multiplier × first-target multiplier; close the configured partial quantity and mark the first target hit.
Exit: long, (partial close enabled AND first target hit, including on the current bar, AND high >= support + ATR × reward-to-risk multiplier × second-target multiplier) OR (partial close disabled AND high >= support + ATR × reward-to-risk multiplier × first-target multiplier); close the remaining position.
Exit: short, partial close enabled AND first target not previously hit in this position AND position quantity >= partial-close quantity AND low <= resistance - ATR × reward-to-risk multiplier × first-target multiplier; close the configured partial quantity and mark the first target hit.
Exit: short, (partial close enabled AND first target hit, including on the current bar, AND low <= resistance - ATR × reward-to-risk multiplier × second-target multiplier) OR (partial close disabled AND low <= resistance - ATR × reward-to-risk multiplier × first-target multiplier); close the remaining position.
