Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over equal left and right pivot lookbacks; becomes available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Sweep volume filter = current volume > median volume over the shorter of the liquidity-pool lookback and 200 bars.
Calculation: Rolling volume profile = distribute each bar’s volume equally across the price bins touched by its high-low range over the profile lookback; POC is the highest-volume bin, and VAH/VAL bound the value area expanded from POC toward the greater volume of the next two bins on either side until the target value-area volume is reached.
Calculation: A-grade confluence score = 1 for proximity of sweep extreme to the matching value-area boundary, 1 for proximity to any HVN, 2 for proximity to any active naked POC, and 1 if the path from the close pivot-lookback bars ago to the sweep extreme intersects an LVN; sum applicable points. HVN/LVN are contiguous profile bins at or above/below their respective volume percentiles. A naked POC is a rolling POC snapshot taken at the start of each higher-timeframe anchor period, active until wick-tested or expired.
