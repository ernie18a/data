Calculation: Rolling volume profile = Over a lookback of chart bars including the current bar, divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high into equal price bins. For each bar with positive volume and high > low, distribute its volume equally among all bins from floor((low - range low) / bin width) through floor((high - range low) / bin width), clamped to the bin range. The POC is the midpoint of the lowest-index bin with the greatest volume. Starting at the POC bin, expand the value area until its accumulated volume reaches the selected fraction of total profile volume: compare the sums of up to two bins immediately above and below the area, add the next upper bin if its sum is at least the lower sum and an upper bin exists; otherwise add the next lower bin if available, else the next upper bin. VAH is the upper edge of the highest included bin and VAL is the lower edge of the lowest included bin. Sort bin volumes ascending; high-volume bins have volume at least the selected upper-percentile order statistic, and low-volume bins have volume at most the selected lower-percentile order statistic, using floor(bin count × percentile fraction) as the zero-based index, clamped to the bin range. Merge adjacent qualifying bins into high-volume and low-volume zones. Recalculate each chart bar; retain the preceding profile if the price range is zero.
Calculation: Active naked POCs = At the first chart bar of each new higher-timeframe period, snapshot the current chart-bar rolling POC, including that bar, and retain at most a selected number of snapshots, removing the oldest first. Remove snapshots older than the selected number of higher-timeframe periods. For wick-test consumption, remove a snapshot whenever low <= snapshot <= high, including on its creation bar; alternatively, for close-through consumption, remove it after its creation bar when consecutive closes are strictly on opposite sides of it. Evaluate confluence after these removals.
Calculation: Sweep levels = The latest confirmed pivot high and low, each requiring the same selected number of bars on each side for confirmation. Replace a level when a newer pivot of its kind is confirmed; discard it after its corresponding sweep. Its age is measured from the pivot bar, not its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Confluence score = For a bullish sweep use the current low and VAL; for a bearish sweep use the current high and VAH. Add one if the absolute difference between that extreme and the selected value-area boundary divided by the extreme is at most the selected proximity fraction; add one if the extreme is within any high-volume zone expanded on both sides by close × proximity fraction; add two if its absolute distance from any active naked POC divided by the extreme is at most the proximity fraction; add one if the interval between the close from the pivot-length number of bars ago and the current extreme intersects any current low-volume zone.
Long: The latest confirmed pivot low exists AND its age is at most the liquidity-pool lookback AND low < pivot low AND close > pivot low AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume > median volume over a lookback equal to the smaller of the liquidity-pool lookback and a selected maximum).
Short: The latest confirmed pivot high exists AND its age is at most the liquidity-pool lookback AND high > pivot high AND close < pivot high AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume > median volume over the same lookback).
Long: The bullish sweep conditions hold AND the bullish confluence score >= the selected threshold.
Short: The bearish sweep conditions hold AND the bearish confluence score >= the selected threshold.
Long: Close > current VAH.
Short: Close < current VAL.
Long: Close > current VAH AND previous close <= previous VAH.
Short: Close < current VAL AND previous close >= previous VAL.
