Calculation: Direction = positive when close minus close from the fuel lookback exceeds 0.22 ATR; negative when it is below −0.22 ATR; otherwise positive if the shorter trend EMA exceeds the longer trend EMA, negative if it is below, and zero if equal
Calculation: Fuel score = clamp(0.32 × persistence + 0.24 × participation + 0.18 × range expansion + 0.26 × efficiency, 0, 100), where persistence is the percentage of fuel-lookback bars whose close moves in the Direction; participation is current volume divided by its fuel-lookback SMA × 50; range expansion is current high-low range divided by its fuel-lookback SMA × 50; efficiency is absolute close change over the fuel lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback × 100; clamp each component to 0–100
Calculation: Exhaustion risk = clamp(0.35 × extension + 0.25 × (100 − fuel score) + 0.24 × wick pressure + 0.16 × participation fade, 0, 100), where extension is absolute distance from the longer trend EMA divided by ATR × 18; wick pressure is upper wick as a percentage of the bar range for positive Direction, lower wick as a percentage for negative Direction, and 45 when Direction is zero; participation fade is the positive drop in participation from the previous bar plus 22, or zero if participation did not drop; clamp extension and wick pressure to 0–100
Long: Direction > 0 AND fuel score ≥ fuel fade threshold AND exhaustion risk < exhaustion threshold
Short: Direction < 0 AND fuel score ≥ fuel fade threshold AND exhaustion risk < exhaustion threshold
