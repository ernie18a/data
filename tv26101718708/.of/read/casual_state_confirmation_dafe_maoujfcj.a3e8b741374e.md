Long: A bullish pattern is selected (first matching pattern within its category, then the category with the highest pattern score after category weighting) and its score ≥ minimum score; at least the signal cooldown has passed; structural proximity is satisfied if required; RCM integrity ≥ threshold if required; RCM permits long; and the bar is realtime or confirmed. Bullish patterns are: three consecutive rising closes with volume above its average; a compressed 20-bar range versus the 40-bar range with close above the 20-bar average and bullish candle; a low at or below the 20-bar low followed by a higher close; lows separated by 10 bars within 0.5 ATR and close above its close 5 bars earlier; wavelet alignment bullish with close above the second approximation; an energy surge with bullish candle; bullish absorption (body <35% of range, lower wick >2 bodies, volume >1.3× average, bullish candle); exhaustion wick (upper wick >3 bodies, high at the 20-bar high, close below range midpoint); bullish pin bar (lower wick >2 bodies, upper wick <0.5 body, bullish candle); bullish engulfing candle; bullish marubozu (body >85% of range, range >1.2× volatility-adjusted ATR, bullish candle); doji at support (body <15% of range, at structural level, close not above its close 5 bars earlier); bullish BOS (close above latest swing high and prior close, volume above average); bullish CHoCH (close above latest swing high and prior swing low above its previous value); bullish liquidity sweep (low below latest swing low, close above it and bullish candle); bullish order-block reclaim (at value-area low or support corridor, bullish candle, lower wick greater than body); no-supply pattern (narrow range, volume <0.7× average, bearish candle, close above 20-bar average); selling climax (volume >2.5× its longer average, close above range midpoint, low at or below 20-bar low); or bullish stopping volume (volume >1.8× average, range >1.5× volatility-adjusted ATR, bearish candle, low at/below 10-bar low, close above range midpoint). Pattern labels also mark long when a bullish pattern is selected, its score ≥ minimum score, and at least six bars have passed since the prior pattern label.
Short: A bearish pattern is selected (first matching pattern within its category, then the category with the highest pattern score after category weighting) and its score ≥ minimum score; at least the signal cooldown has passed; structural proximity is satisfied if required; RCM integrity ≥ threshold if required; RCM permits short; and the bar is realtime or confirmed. Bearish patterns are: three consecutive falling closes with volume above its average; a compressed 20-bar range versus the 40-bar range with close below the 20-bar average and bearish candle; a high at or above the 20-bar high followed by a lower close; highs separated by 10 bars within 0.5 ATR and close below its close 5 bars earlier; wavelet alignment bearish with close below the second approximation; an energy surge with bearish candle; bearish absorption (body <35% of range, upper wick >2 bodies, volume >1.3× average, bearish candle); exhaustion wick (lower wick >3 bodies, low at the 20-bar low, close above range midpoint); bearish pin bar (upper wick >2 bodies, lower wick <0.5 body, bearish candle); bearish engulfing candle; bearish marubozu (body >85% of range, range >1.2× volatility-adjusted ATR, bearish candle); doji at resistance (body <15% of range, at structural level, close above its close 5 bars earlier); bearish BOS (close below latest swing low and prior close, volume above average); bearish CHoCH (close below latest swing low and latest swing high below its previous value); bearish liquidity sweep (high above latest swing high, close below it and bearish candle); bearish order-block reclaim (at value-area high or resistance corridor, bearish candle, upper wick greater than body); no-demand pattern (narrow range, volume <0.7× average, bullish candle, close below 20-bar average); buying climax (volume >2.5× its longer average, close below range midpoint, high at/above 20-bar high); or bearish stopping volume (volume >1.8× average, range >1.5× volatility-adjusted ATR, bullish candle, high at/above 10-bar high, close below range midpoint). Pattern labels also mark short when a bearish pattern is selected, its score ≥ minimum score, and at least six bars have passed since the prior pattern label.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − initial risk distance OR high ≥ entry price + 2× initial risk distance.
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + initial risk distance OR low ≤ entry price − 2× initial risk distance.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = ATR × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Volatility-adjusted ATR = ATR × clamp(ATR / longer-lookback ATR, 0.5, 2.0).
Calculation: Wavelet alignment = bullish when the four smoothed approximation bands descend from shortest to longest scale; bearish when they ascend.
Calculation: Energy surge = sum of squared normalized wavelet detail bands > 1.55 × its moving average.
Calculation: Structural proximity = within 1 ATR of a point of control or latest swing, within 0.7 ATR of value-area high/low, or near a support/resistance corridor.
Calculation: Continuation score = count of: close above (bullish) or below (bearish) the 50-bar average; wavelet strength positive (bullish) or negative (bearish); volume above its 20-bar average; Lorentzian prediction direction agrees when confidence exceeds its threshold; RCM permits the direction; price is at a structural level; volume delta ratio >0.1 (bullish) or <−0.1 (bearish); MACD line above (bullish) or below (bearish) its signal line.
Calculation: Reversal score = count of the same conditions as continuation score, replacing the 50-bar average test with RSI below 40 (bullish) or above 60 (bearish).
Calculation: Volume delta ratio = (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), calculated on each of two lower timeframes and combined as 0.6 × the shorter-timeframe value + 0.4 × the longer-timeframe value; use the lower-timeframe values available for the current chart bar.
