Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on the configured analysis timeframe; use its current bar as returned without lookahead (the timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe; a higher-timeframe bar may still be forming)
Calculation: Relative volume = current analysis-timeframe volume divided by its simple moving average; use 1 if the average is unavailable or zero
Calculation: Liquidity levels and weights = confirmed swing highs form upper levels and confirmed swing lows form lower levels, using equal left and right lookbacks. Start each level at weight 1, optionally adding a capped relative-volume contribution. Merge levels within an ATR-scaled distance using a weighted-average price and increase their weight. Increase weight after a rejection near the level; otherwise decay it slightly. Select the nearest upper level above close or nearest lower level below close, with its current weight.
Calculation: Upper sweep rejection strength = (high − close) / max(high − (upper level − zone height), minimum tick), where zone height is ATR-scaled
Calculation: Lower sweep reclaim strength = (close − low) / max((lower level + zone height) − low, minimum tick), where zone height is ATR-scaled
Long: A lower liquidity level exists below close AND low is below the level minus zone height AND close reclaims above the level (or, in wick-reclaim mode, above the lower zone boundary) AND lower wick size is at least the ATR-scaled minimum AND lower sweep reclaim strength meets its minimum AND lower level weight meets its minimum AND optional relative-volume, session, and strict-trend filters pass AND the lower-sweep cooldown has elapsed; in follow-through mode, the next qualifying bar must have a low no lower than the sweep bar's low, close above the level, and close above the sweep bar's close
Short: A upper liquidity level exists above close AND high is above the level plus zone height AND close rejects below the level (or, in wick-reclaim mode, below the upper zone boundary) AND upper wick size is at least the ATR-scaled minimum AND upper sweep rejection strength meets its minimum AND upper level weight meets its minimum AND optional relative-volume, session, and strict-trend filters pass AND the upper-sweep cooldown has elapsed; in follow-through mode, the next qualifying bar must have a high no higher than the sweep bar's high, close below the level, and close below the sweep bar's close
Exit: Long, an upper sweep base event occurs AND upper level weight meets the structural-exit minimum AND close < open AND close < upper level − 0.05 × ATR
Exit: Short, a lower sweep base event occurs AND lower level weight meets the structural-exit minimum AND close > open AND close > lower level + 0.05 × ATR
