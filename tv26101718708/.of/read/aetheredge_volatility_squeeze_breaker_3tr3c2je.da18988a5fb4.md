Calculation: Squeeze = the Bollinger Band lower boundary is above and its upper boundary is below the corresponding boundaries of at least one of three Keltner channels. Each channel uses the SMA of close as its center and an SMA of either true range or high minus low as its width basis; the channels share a lookback and use wide, medium, and tight width multipliers.
Calculation: Momentum = the current endpoint of a linear regression of close minus the average of (1) the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low and (2) the SMA of close, all using the same lookback.
Long: Squeeze is false AND squeeze was true on at least one of the preceding bars within the release lookback AND momentum > 0 AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by the volume surge multiplier) AND close > previous bar's upper Bollinger Band.
Short: Squeeze is false AND squeeze was true on at least one of the preceding bars within the release lookback AND momentum <= 0 AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by the volume surge multiplier) AND close < previous bar's lower Bollinger Band.
