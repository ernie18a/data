Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the most recent occurrence of a configured session, reset at the start of each occurrence and retained until the next occurrence; determine session membership from bar datetime and the configured session timezone.
Calculation: Body percentage = 100 * abs(close - open) / (high - low) if high > low, otherwise 0.
Long: close > EMA(close).
Short: close <= EMA(close).
Long: At least one enabled session has a defined low AND the current bar is outside that session AND ((wick-sweep mode AND low < session low AND close > session low) OR (close-break mode AND close < session low)) AND abs(session low - low) >= ATR * minimum sweep multiplier AND (close-back-inside filter disabled OR close > session low) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close)) AND (outside-all-sessions filter disabled OR current bar is outside every configured session) AND (strong-reversal filter disabled OR (close > open AND close - low >= ATR * displacement multiplier AND body percentage >= minimum body percentage)) AND (no previous liquidity-grab long signal OR bars since previous liquidity-grab long signal >= cooldown).
Short: At least one enabled session has a defined high AND the current bar is outside that session AND ((wick-sweep mode AND high > session high AND close < session high) OR (close-break mode AND close > session high)) AND abs(high - session high) >= ATR * minimum sweep multiplier AND (close-back-inside filter disabled OR close < session high) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close)) AND (outside-all-sessions filter disabled OR current bar is outside every configured session) AND (strong-reversal filter disabled OR (close < open AND high - close >= ATR * displacement multiplier AND body percentage >= minimum body percentage)) AND (no previous liquidity-grab short signal OR bars since previous liquidity-grab short signal >= cooldown).
