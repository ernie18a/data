Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = with p = floor(fractal lookback / 2), the high/low p bars ago is the highest/lowest over the lookback; for a swing high, highs rose during the p bars before it and fell during the p bars after it, and for a swing low, lows fell before it and rose after it. Confirm only after the p following bars close. Use separate lookbacks for chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close > the most recent unbroken confirmed swing high and previous close ≤ that level OR, on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, close > the most recent unbroken confirmed swing high and previous close ≤ that level.
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close < the most recent unbroken confirmed swing low and previous close ≥ that level OR, on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, close < the most recent unbroken confirmed swing low and previous close ≥ that level.
