Calculation: Trend slope = SMA(EMA(close) minus its value a parameterized number of chart bars ago). The trend state starts bullish, becomes bullish when the slope exceeds a positive threshold proportional to SMA(ATR), becomes bearish when the slope is below the negative threshold, and otherwise retains its prior state.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe levels = the high and low of the last completed bar on each of three higher timeframes; also use the high and low of the last completed bar on a further higher timeframe representing the previous period.
Calculation: Support proximity = the minimum absolute distance from either chart-bar low or close to any higher-timeframe low. Resistance proximity = the minimum absolute distance from either chart-bar high or close to any higher-timeframe high.
Calculation: Stochastic K = SMA of 100 × (close − rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low), using a neutral parameter if the denominator is zero. Stochastic D = SMA of K.
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close − open); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; range = high − low; safe body = max(body, a positive price tolerance); average ATR = SMA(ATR). Subscripts indicate bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = (close > open AND close₁ < open₁ AND close >= open₁ AND open <= close₁ AND body > body₁) OR (lower wick >= safe body × wick multiplier AND upper wick <= safe body × small-wick multiplier AND range > average ATR × range multiplier) OR (close > open AND upper wick >= safe body × wick multiplier AND lower wick <= safe body × small-wick multiplier AND range > average ATR × range multiplier) OR (body <= average ATR × small-body multiplier AND range > average ATR × range multiplier AND low <= the minimum of the three higher-timeframe lows plus proximity tolerance) OR (close₂ < open₂ AND body₁ <= body₂ × star-body multiplier AND close > open AND close >= (open₁ + close₁) / 2 + body₂ × midpoint multiplier AND body >= average ATR × large-body multiplier) OR (close > open AND close₁ < open₁ AND open > close₁ AND close < open₁ AND body < body₁ × harami multiplier) OR (abs(low − low₁) <= price tolerance AND close₁ < open₁ AND close > open AND range > average ATR × range multiplier).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = (close < open AND close₁ > open₁ AND close <= open₁ AND open >= close₁ AND body > body₁) OR (upper wick >= safe body × wick multiplier AND lower wick <= safe body × small-wick multiplier AND range > average ATR × range multiplier) OR (close < open AND lower wick >= safe body × wick multiplier AND upper wick <= safe body × small-wick multiplier AND range > average ATR × range multiplier) OR (body <= average ATR × small-body multiplier AND range > average ATR × range multiplier AND high >= the maximum of the three higher-timeframe highs minus proximity tolerance) OR (close₂ > open₂ AND body₁ <= body₂ × star-body multiplier AND close < open AND close <= (open₁ + close₁) / 2 − body₂ × midpoint multiplier AND body >= average ATR × large-body multiplier) OR (close < open AND close₁ > open₁ AND open < close₁ AND close > open₁ AND body < body₁ × harami multiplier) OR (abs(high − high₁) <= price tolerance AND close₁ > open₁ AND close < open AND range > average ATR × range multiplier).
Calculation: Signal cooldown = chart bars since the previous signal of the same direction, initialized as bars since the first chart bar. Bars since a bullish or bearish trend flip are likewise initialized as bars since the first chart bar and reset on the corresponding flip.
Long: Trend state = bullish.
Short: Trend state = bearish.
Long: Support proximity <= proximity tolerance AND RSI < lower threshold AND stochastic K crosses above stochastic D AND bullish pattern = true AND signal cooldown >= cooldown parameter AND (trend state = bullish OR bars since bullish trend flip <= flip-window parameter).
Short: Resistance proximity <= proximity tolerance AND RSI > upper threshold AND stochastic K crosses below stochastic D AND bearish pattern = true AND signal cooldown >= cooldown parameter AND (trend state = bearish OR bars since bearish trend flip <= flip-window parameter).
Long: abs(low − last completed low on the shortest of the three higher timeframes) <= zone tolerance AND close > open.
Short: abs(high − last completed high on the shortest of the three higher timeframes) <= zone tolerance AND close < open.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × profit multiplier OR low <= entry price − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier; evaluate only within a parameterized number of bars after the simulated entry, with profit taking precedence if both are reached on the same bar.
Exit: short, low <= entry price − entry-bar ATR × profit multiplier OR high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier; evaluate only within a parameterized number of bars after the simulated entry, with profit taking precedence if both are reached on the same bar.
