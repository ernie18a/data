Calculation: Higher-timeframe zones = On each enabled higher timeframe, using the last completed bar, confirm symmetric pivot highs and lows. For a pivot high, scan backward from the pivot for the nearest bullish candle whose body meets the ATR-based minimum; supply extends from that candle's low to the greater of its high and the pivot high. For a pivot low, scan for the nearest qualifying bearish candle; demand extends from the lesser of its low and the pivot low to that candle's high. Optionally fall back to a zone extending an ATR-based distance inward from the pivot. Reject a new zone if its midpoint is within a zone-width-based deduplication distance of an existing zone; retain a limited number per timeframe and side. Deactivate supply when chart close exceeds its top plus an ATR buffer, and demand when chart close falls below its bottom minus an ATR buffer. A zone ceases to be fresh after a chart bar overlaps it more than one bar after its creation. A proximity hit requires chart high >= zone bottom minus the proximity buffer AND chart low <= zone top plus the buffer. Select the first matching zone on the longest enabled timeframe, then the next longest, and the oldest matching zone within that timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe directional bias = Count bullish and bearish comparisons separately across several higher timeframes, using their last completed bars: close above or below an EMA; on the longest timeframe also require that EMA above or below a longer EMA, respectively.
Calculation: Reversal = For long, a demand proximity hit AND low <= demand top AND close > demand bottom AND close > open AND lower wick > body multiplied by a threshold; for short, a supply proximity hit AND high >= supply bottom AND close < supply top AND close < open AND upper wick > body multiplied by a threshold.
Calculation: Oscillator extreme = For long, smoothed RSI below its threshold OR stochastic RSI below its threshold OR RSI below its threshold; for short, the corresponding values above their respective thresholds.
Calculation: Confluence = Count five independently satisfied pillars: (1) a directional engulfing candle, directional pin bar, ATR-sized doji, OR directional reversal; (2) smoothed RSI moving in the trade direction while within its bound, OR stochastic RSI crossing its signal in the trade direction while within its bound, OR the directional oscillator extreme; (3) volume above a multiple of its moving average; (4) adaptive fast EMA on the trade side of adaptive slower EMA, OR the unadjusted fast EMA crossing the unadjusted slower EMA in the trade direction, OR close on the trade side of a still slower adaptive EMA; (5) at least one matching higher-timeframe bias, OR matching SuperTrend direction, OR that EMA cross, OR MACD histogram on the trade side of zero. A bullish engulfing candle closes above its open and the previous high, opens below the previous close, and has a body above an ATR fraction; the bearish form is symmetric. A directional pin requires a same-direction body and a relevant wick exceeding both a body multiple and an ATR fraction. A doji requires body below one ATR fraction and range above another.
Calculation: Relaxed reaction = At the matching zone, a relevant wick reaches the near zone edge and exceeds both a body multiple and an ATR fraction, OR a directional engulfing candle reaches that edge, OR the previous bar's wick reaches the zone and its close remains beyond the far edge AND the current directional candle closes beyond the near edge, OR the current body is smaller than an ATR fraction AND its close is inside the zone and moves in the trade direction from the previous close.
Calculation: Structural gate = When enabled, require the close's normalized position between the selected demand bottom and supply top to be at or below a long threshold or at or above a short threshold. If one side is absent, construct the missing boundary at an ATR-based distance from the available zone; if both are absent, use ATR-based boundaries around close. When polarity is enabled, require distance from close to the nearer demand edge divided by the sum of distances to the nearer demand and supply edges to be at or below a long threshold or at or above a short threshold; if the ratio is unavailable, require a matching zone hit and no opposite zone hit.
Calculation: Regime gate = Combine the difference between bullish and bearish higher-timeframe bias counts with ATR-normalized slopes of a medium adaptive EMA and a longer adaptive EMA, using a longer slope lookback for the longer EMA. Classify an extreme directional regime only when the directional score and the recent high-low range divided by ATR times lookback meet their thresholds. Block the opposite direction unless both smoothed RSI and stochastic RSI are simultaneously at their corresponding extreme thresholds. The regime gate is optional.
Calculation: Armed state = On a matching zone hit with no opposite zone hit, matching higher-timeframe bias count at or above its threshold, optional freshness, signal and post-loss cooldowns satisfied, optional minimum ADX and matching SuperTrend, regime gate satisfied, and structural gate satisfied, arm that side when neither side is armed. If re-arming is enabled, a matching new hit after a required number of bars outside the zone may reset an existing same-side arm when the re-arm gap and per-zone cap permit. Save the zone edges, close and bar index at arming. Expire when the watch window is exceeded, close breaks the far edge by an ATR buffer, close runs away from the saved close by an ATR multiple without the bar reaching the near edge, or the opposite zone is hit without a matching zone hit. Strong directional momentum, defined by volume above a moving-average multiple, ADX above a threshold, matching higher-timeframe bias and a matching candle direction, optionally exempts break and runaway expiration and starts a limited late-trigger window. Clear the arm on expiration or an entry signal; a late-trigger window can permit a trigger on an expiration bar before the arm is cleared.
Calculation: Entry suppression = Optionally latch a veto after a minimum number of closed trades when the rolling win rate is below its threshold or the active asset classification is hostile; clear it on forced override, sufficient idle bars, insufficient trades, or recovery of rolling win rate above a higher threshold while the classification is not hostile. Optionally latch a separate suppression when the low-volatility asset classification has sufficient trades and rolling profit factor below its threshold; clear it when that classification ends or profit factor recovers above a higher threshold. Apply the previous bar's latch states to entries.
Long: Matching armed state is created.
Short: Matching armed state is created.
Long: Entry suppression is inactive AND structural gate and regime gate pass AND matching higher-timeframe bias count is sufficient AND ((armed mode is enabled AND long is armed AND the arm has not expired OR its late-trigger window is active) AND (long reversal OR long confluence count >= the armed threshold with the oscillator filter satisfied when enabled OR enabled relaxed long reaction) OR (armed mode is disabled AND demand proximity hit AND no supply proximity hit AND long confluence count >= its threshold AND optional freshness, cooldown, ADX, reversal, oscillator and SuperTrend filters pass)).
Short: Entry suppression is inactive AND structural gate and regime gate pass AND matching higher-timeframe bias count is sufficient AND no simultaneous long trigger AND ((armed mode is enabled AND short is armed AND the arm has not expired OR its late-trigger window is active) AND (short reversal OR short confluence count >= the armed threshold with the oscillator filter satisfied when enabled OR enabled relaxed short reaction) OR (armed mode is disabled AND supply proximity hit AND no demand proximity hit AND short confluence count >= its threshold AND optional freshness, cooldown, ADX, reversal, oscillator and SuperTrend filters pass)).
Calculation: Targets and stop = At each entry, replace the tracked position with the signal side at chart close. Fix three targets from entry using either increasing percentage distances, increasing entry-ATR multiples, or a near ATR target, the midpoint to the opposite zone edge, and that edge; if the opposite edge is unavailable, use an ATR-based substitute. Set the stop at entry plus or minus an ATR multiple, or beyond the matching zone's far edge by an ATR buffer, with an ATR-based fallback if the zone is unavailable. When trailing is enabled and full exit at the first target is disabled, after the first target move the stop at least to entry; after the second target also trail it by a current-ATR multiple from close. Update the trailing stop before checking the current bar's targets. Target hits can occur on the entry bar.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR full exit at first target is enabled AND high >= first target OR full exit at first target is disabled AND first target has been hit AND the unadjusted fast EMA crosses below the unadjusted slower EMA OR high >= third target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR full exit at first target is enabled AND low <= first target OR full exit at first target is disabled AND first target has been hit AND the unadjusted fast EMA crosses above the unadjusted slower EMA OR low <= third target.
