Calculation: Demand/supply zones = on each requested timeframe, confirm swing-low/high pivots; use the last opposing candle within the lookback before the pivot as the zone, including its full high-low range and extending it to the pivot wick; if none exists and fallback is enabled, use the pivot wick with an ATR-based buffer. Track zones until close breaks beyond the far edge plus an ATR buffer; a zone is fresh until first touched. Use completed bars on timeframes higher than the chart and the latest available bar on timeframes lower than the chart.
Calculation: HTF bias score = count of bullish/bearish requested-timeframe biases; bullish means close above the medium EMA (daily bias also requires medium EMA above long EMA), bearish is the inverse. Use completed bars on higher timeframes and the latest available bar on lower timeframes.
Calculation: ADX = directional-movement index measuring trend strength; ATR = average true range; EMA = exponential moving average; RSI = relative strength index; Stochastic RSI = stochastic oscillator applied to RSI; MACD histogram = MACD line minus signal line.
Calculation: Long/short confluence score = count of satisfied pillars: (1) matching engulfing, pin-bar, doji, or zone-reversal candle; (2) matching RSI/Stochastic RSI turn or extreme; (3) volume exceeds its moving average times a threshold; (4) fast EMA is on the matching side of the next EMA, crosses it in the matching direction, or close is on the matching side of the medium EMA; (5) matching HTF bias, SuperTrend direction, EMA crossover, or MACD histogram sign. Fast EMA lookback is shorter than the next EMA lookback.
Calculation: Zone polarity ratio = distance from close to the nearest demand-zone edge divided by the sum of that distance and distance to the nearest supply-zone edge. Range position = close's position, clamped to 0–1, between the active demand floor and supply ceiling; if either is absent, extend the other boundary by an ATR-based amount.
Long: A long arm is inactive, no short arm is active, and price enters/taps an active HTF demand zone within the ATR-based proximity buffer; the zone is not simultaneously an HTF supply zone; bullish HTF score meets its threshold; freshness or re-arm rule passes; cooldowns after a signal and a loss have elapsed; ADX meets its threshold when enabled; regime permits longs or RSI and Stochastic RSI are both extremely oversold; required SuperTrend is bullish; polarity ratio and range position pass their long thresholds. After arming, while the arm has not expired, enter long when a bullish reversal candle, the required confluence score (and required oscillator extreme), or an enabled relaxed bullish zone rejection occurs, with bullish HTF score, regime, polarity, and range-position gates still passing. An arm expires after its bar window, a close breaks below the zone by the ATR buffer, price runs away from the arm price by the ATR threshold without retapping, or an opposite supply zone is tapped. Strong bullish momentum exempts break/runaway expiry and may permit a trigger within its late-fire window. When the arm engine is disabled, enter long directly on demand-zone tap if the same long gates pass, confluence meets its threshold, and required reversal, oscillator, and SuperTrend conditions pass.
Short: A short arm is inactive, no long arm is active, and price enters/taps an active HTF supply zone within the ATR-based proximity buffer; the zone is not simultaneously an HTF demand zone; bearish HTF score meets its threshold; freshness or re-arm rule passes; cooldowns after a signal and a loss have elapsed; ADX meets its threshold when enabled; regime permits shorts or RSI and Stochastic RSI are both extremely overbought; required SuperTrend is bearish; polarity ratio and range position pass their short thresholds. After arming, while the arm has not expired, enter short when a bearish reversal candle, the required confluence score (and required oscillator extreme), or an enabled relaxed bearish zone rejection occurs, with bearish HTF score, regime, polarity, and range-position gates still passing. An arm expires after its bar window, a close breaks above the zone by the ATR buffer, price runs away from the arm price by the ATR threshold without retapping, or an opposite demand zone is tapped. Strong bearish momentum exempts break/runaway expiry and may permit a trigger within its late-fire window. When the arm engine is disabled, enter short directly on supply-zone tap if the same short gates pass, confluence meets its threshold, and required reversal, oscillator, and SuperTrend conditions pass.
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR (profile exits at first target and high >= first target) OR (profile trails beyond first target and fast EMA crosses below the next EMA) OR high >= third target.
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR (profile exits at first target and low <= first target) OR (profile trails beyond first target and fast EMA crosses above the next EMA) OR low <= third target.
