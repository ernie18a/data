Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear-regression endpoint of close minus the average of (the midpoint of the lookback highest high and lowest low) and the lookback SMA of close, using the same lookback.
Calculation: Squeeze percentage = for Bollinger Bands strictly inside Keltner Channels, the mean of (upper Keltner minus upper Bollinger) / upper Keltner and (lower Bollinger minus lower Keltner) / lower Bollinger; for Bollinger Bands strictly outside Keltner Channels, the negative mean of (upper Bollinger minus upper Keltner) / upper Bollinger and (lower Keltner minus lower Bollinger) / lower Keltner; otherwise undefined. Bollinger Bands use SMA and standard deviation of close; Keltner Channels use SMA of close and SMA of true range or high minus low.
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe trend = initialize from the fast and slow EMAs of the higher-timeframe bar still forming; subsequently retain the previous trend until a new higher-timeframe bar begins and the fast EMA has been strictly above, or strictly below, the slow EMA on each bar in the confirmation lookback of completed higher-timeframe bars, then set the trend accordingly. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Recent volume point of control = divide the chart-bar high-low range over a profile lookback into equal price rows. For each row, sum volume times max(overlap of the bar's high-low interval with the row, 1) / max(row height times number of rows, 1), for bars from the current bar through the profile-lookback offset inclusive. Select the first row with the greatest positive sum and return the weighted mean of the overlap midpoints using those weights. The range uses the current bar and the preceding profile-lookback-minus-one bars.
Calculation: Support = the greatest recent volume point of control strictly below the current low among the current and preceding support-lookback bars, or the current low if none exists. Resistance = the least recent volume point of control strictly above the current high among those bars, subject to being strictly below the highest recent volume point of control in the current support-lookback bars; use the current high if none qualifies.
Calculation: Reference level = support when the confirmed higher-timeframe trend is up; otherwise resistance.
Calculation: Entry stop fraction = for a long with close above the reference level, use the fixed stop fraction if the fixed stop price is above the reference level; otherwise use 1 minus reference level divided by close when close / reference level is at least 1 plus the minimum stop fraction, and use the minimum stop fraction otherwise. For a short with close below the reference level, use the fixed stop fraction if the fixed stop price is below the reference level; otherwise use reference level / close minus 1 when that ratio is at least 1 plus the minimum stop fraction, and use the minimum stop fraction otherwise. When dynamic stops are disabled, use the fixed stop fraction. Freeze the fraction at entry.
Calculation: Bounce target = fast chart-timeframe EMA plus a configurable fraction of the difference between slow and fast chart-timeframe EMAs. For a long exit, replace it with the reference level when that level is above close and below the target; for a short exit, replace it with the reference level when that level is below close and above the target.
Calculation: Trailing trigger and offset = freeze both at entry. In static mode use configured fractions. In dynamic non-strict mode, for a long take an EMA of the rolling maximum of percentage rises from the rolling lowest low to high; for a short take an EMA of the rolling maximum of percentage falls from the rolling highest high to low; multiply by a configured fraction and convert to a price fraction. In dynamic strict mode, let R = configured reward-risk ratio times (1 + entry stop fraction) times (1 + configured offset fraction / 100); the long trigger fraction is (100R + fee percentage) / (100 - fee percentage) - 1, and the short trigger fraction is 1 - (100 / R - fee percentage) / (100 + fee percentage). The dynamic offset fraction equals the trigger fraction times the configured offset fraction.
Long: (confirmed higher-timeframe trend is up OR EMA filter is disabled) AND ((squeeze momentum <= 0 AND squeeze momentum >= previous squeeze momentum) OR (squeeze momentum > 0 AND squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum) OR (strict mode is disabled AND fast chart-timeframe EMA > slow chart-timeframe EMA AND (max(open, close) < slow EMA + proximity fraction times (fast EMA - slow EMA) OR (support filter is enabled AND max(open, close) > support)))) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (ADX slope filter is disabled OR arctangent of the linear-regression slope of prior-bar ADX over its lookback > angle threshold) AND (squeeze-percentage filter is disabled OR absolute squeeze percentage > threshold) AND (dynamic stop is disabled OR (close > reference level AND (strict mode is disabled OR (fixed long stop price <= reference level AND (close / reference level < 1 + minimum stop fraction OR entry stop fraction < maximum stop fraction))))) AND elapsed time since the last bar with an open position > minimum interval AND long exit condition is false.
Short: (confirmed higher-timeframe trend is down OR EMA filter is disabled) AND ((squeeze momentum > 0 AND squeeze momentum <= previous squeeze momentum) OR (squeeze momentum <= 0 AND squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum) OR (strict mode is disabled AND fast chart-timeframe EMA <= slow chart-timeframe EMA AND (min(open, close) > slow EMA - proximity fraction times (slow EMA - fast EMA) OR (resistance filter is enabled AND min(open, close) < resistance)))) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (ADX slope filter is disabled OR arctangent of the linear-regression slope of prior-bar ADX over its lookback > angle threshold) AND (squeeze-percentage filter is disabled OR absolute squeeze percentage > threshold) AND (dynamic stop is disabled OR (close < reference level AND (strict mode is disabled OR (fixed short stop price >= reference level AND (reference level / close < 1 + minimum stop fraction OR entry stop fraction < maximum stop fraction))))) AND elapsed time since the last bar with an open position > minimum interval AND short exit condition is false AND long entry condition is false.
Exit: long, (plus mode is disabled AND ((squeeze momentum > 0 AND squeeze momentum <= previous squeeze momentum) OR ((squeeze momentum <= 0 AND squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (ADX slope filter is disabled OR arctangent of the linear-regression slope of prior-bar ADX over its lookback > angle threshold) AND (squeeze-percentage filter is disabled OR absolute squeeze percentage > threshold)))) OR (exit EMA filter is enabled AND confirmed higher-timeframe trend is down) OR (strict mode is disabled AND fast chart-timeframe EMA <= slow chart-timeframe EMA AND close > bounce target) OR (old-position exit is enabled AND position age >= age threshold AND close < entry price times (1 - old-position loss fraction)) OR low <= entry price times (1 - entry stop fraction) OR (fixed take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price times (1 + take-profit fraction)) OR (trailing exit is enabled AND high since entry >= entry price times (1 + trailing trigger fraction) AND low <= highest high since trailing activation minus entry price times (1 + trailing trigger fraction) times trailing offset fraction).
Exit: short, (plus mode is disabled AND ((squeeze momentum <= 0 AND squeeze momentum >= previous squeeze momentum) OR ((squeeze momentum > 0 AND squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (ADX slope filter is disabled OR arctangent of the linear-regression slope of prior-bar ADX over its lookback > angle threshold) AND (squeeze-percentage filter is disabled OR absolute squeeze percentage > threshold)))) OR (exit EMA filter is enabled AND confirmed higher-timeframe trend is up) OR (strict mode is disabled AND fast chart-timeframe EMA > slow chart-timeframe EMA AND close < bounce target) OR (old-position exit is enabled AND position age >= age threshold AND close > entry price times (1 + old-position loss fraction)) OR high >= entry price times (1 + entry stop fraction) OR (fixed take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price times (1 - take-profit fraction)) OR (trailing exit is enabled AND low since entry <= entry price times (1 - trailing trigger fraction) AND high >= lowest low since trailing activation plus entry price times (1 - trailing trigger fraction) times trailing offset fraction).
