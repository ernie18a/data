Calculation: Resampled close = chart close, or the close returned from another timeframe on each chart bar: for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar. Calculate MACD histogram and RSI on the resulting chart-bar series.
Calculation: Trend pair = either short-period EMA and long-period EMA, close and long-period EMA, or close and short-period EMA. A cross above occurs when the first value is greater than the second and its previous value was less than or equal to the previous second value; a cross below reverses the inequalities.
Calculation: Loss counter = increment on a newly closed losing trade; otherwise reset on a newly closed winning trade, when the counter equals its threshold and bars since the last loss exceed the pause length, or on either trend-pair cross, in that priority order.
Calculation: Trading pause passes = if loss-only mode is off, no trade has closed or bars since the last closed trade are at least the pause length; otherwise, the pause length is zero or the loss counter is below its threshold, AND no losing trade has closed or bars since the last losing trade are at least the pause length.
Calculation: Pullback bar count = the imported library's getPullbackBarCount result for the selected lookback and candle direction; its implementation is not included in READ.
Long: Flat position AND bar datetime is within the inclusive trading dates AND trading pause passes AND the trend pair's first value is at least its second in EMA-cross mode, or greater than its second in either close-versus-EMA mode AND bars since its cross above are at most the trend lookback AND MACD histogram is negative AND its absolute value exceeds the minimum AND (its current absolute value is the highest over the peak lookback OR its previous absolute value was the lowest over the peak lookback ending on the previous bar) AND (histogram-average filter is off OR its absolute value is at least the multiplier times the mean absolute value of negative histogram observations over the average lookback) AND (RSI filter is off OR RSI is within the inclusive long range AND (direction filter is off OR RSI is greater than every RSI value in the preceding direction lookback)) AND (pullback filter is off OR the library's bearish pullback bar count is at least the threshold).
Short: Flat position AND bar datetime is within the inclusive trading dates AND trading pause passes AND the trend pair's first value is less than its second AND bars since its cross below are at most the trend lookback AND MACD histogram exceeds the minimum AND (the current histogram is the highest over the peak lookback OR the previous histogram was the highest over the peak lookback ending on the previous bar) AND (histogram-average filter is off OR the histogram is at least the multiplier times the mean of positive histogram observations over the average lookback) AND (RSI filter is off OR RSI is within the inclusive short range AND (direction filter is off OR RSI is less than every RSI value in the preceding direction lookback)) AND (pullback filter is off OR the library's bullish pullback bar count is at least the threshold).
Exit: Long, high is at least entry price multiplied by one plus the long profit fraction OR low is at most entry price multiplied by one minus the long stop fraction.
Exit: Short, low is at most entry price multiplied by one minus the short profit fraction OR high is at least entry price multiplied by one plus the short stop fraction.
