Calculation: Range filter = recursively update the prior filter value by moving it toward close by the smoothed average absolute close change times a sensitivity factor, without crossing the prior filter value; the average absolute close change uses an EMA, then a second EMA with a longer lookback.
Calculation: Wave oscillator = EMA of (hlc3 − EMA(hlc3)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(hlc3 − EMA(hlc3)))); use the same fast lookback for both inner EMAs and a slower lookback for the outer EMA. Wave signal = 3-bar SMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Volume node = hlc3 of the most recent bar whose volume equals the highest volume over a lookback.
Long: (flat and no open trades and close > long-term EMA and medium-term EMA > long-term EMA and close > Range filter and Wave oscillator crosses above Wave signal and RSI > 50 and volume > volume SMA and close > VWAP and MACD > MACD signal and close > short-term EMA and (volume node is undefined or close > volume node or abs(close − volume node) / close < tolerance) and close > prior bar high) OR (long position and after 0 prior additions and close <= initial entry price × (1 − add interval)) OR (long position and after 1 prior addition and close <= initial entry price × (1 − 2 × add interval)) OR (long position and after 2 prior additions and close <= initial entry price × (1 − 3 × add interval)) OR (long position and after 3 prior additions and close <= initial entry price × (1 − 4 × add interval))
Short: (flat and no open trades and close < long-term EMA and medium-term EMA < long-term EMA and close < Range filter and Wave oscillator crosses below Wave signal and RSI < 50 and volume > volume SMA and close < VWAP and MACD < MACD signal and close < short-term EMA and (volume node is undefined or close < volume node or abs(close − volume node) / close < tolerance) and close < prior bar low) OR (short position and after 0 prior additions and close >= initial entry price × (1 + add interval)) OR (short position and after 1 prior addition and close >= initial entry price × (1 + 2 × add interval)) OR (short position and after 2 prior additions and close >= initial entry price × (1 + 3 × add interval)) OR (short position and after 3 prior additions and close >= initial entry price × (1 + 4 × add interval))
Exit: long, close >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR close <= average entry price × (1 − drawdown-stop percentage)
Exit: short, close <= average entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR close >= average entry price × (1 + drawdown-stop percentage)
