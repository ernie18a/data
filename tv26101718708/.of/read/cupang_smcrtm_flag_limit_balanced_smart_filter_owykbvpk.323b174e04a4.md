Calculation: Candle range = max(high - low, minimum positive price increment); body fraction = abs(close - open) / candle range.
Calculation: A bullish setup occurs when the previous bar's range <= ATR times a base-range limit AND close > open AND close > previous high AND body fraction >= a minimum fraction AND bullish quality score >= a minimum score. A bearish setup occurs when the previous bar's range <= ATR times the same base-range limit AND close < open AND close < previous low AND body fraction >= the same minimum fraction AND bearish quality score >= the same minimum score.
Calculation: Quality score = base-pattern weight plus a body-fraction weight if body fraction >= a higher fraction than the minimum, plus an equal range-expansion weight if current range >= previous range times an expansion factor, plus a smaller trend weight if the trend filter is disabled or, for bullish setups, close > EMA, or, for bearish setups, close < EMA. The base-pattern weight is greater than either equal weight.
Calculation: Each new setup replaces the active setup. Store its bar index, previous bar's high and low, and current ATR. For a bullish setup, entry level = stored high, stop = stored low - stored ATR times a buffer factor, and target = entry level + reward-to-risk factor times max(entry level - stop, stored ATR times a minimum-risk factor). For a bearish setup, entry level = stored low, stop = stored high + stored ATR times the buffer factor, and target = entry level - reward-to-risk factor times max(stop - entry level, stored ATR times the minimum-risk factor). Deactivate the setup when its corresponding exit condition occurs; a new setup replaces it before that bar's exit check.
Long: A bullish setup occurs.
Long: An active bullish setup exists AND the current bar is after its setup bar AND low <= its entry level.
Short: A bearish setup occurs.
Short: An active bearish setup exists AND the current bar is after its setup bar AND high >= its entry level.
Exit: long, an active bullish setup exists AND the current bar is at least two bars after its setup bar AND (close < its stop OR high >= its target).
Exit: short, an active bearish setup exists AND the current bar is at least two bars after its setup bar AND (close > its stop OR low <= its target).
