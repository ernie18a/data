Calculation: Directional strength = For each bar, upward movement is max(change in high, 0) and downward movement is max(-change in low, 0). Smooth each with Wilder's moving average and divide by the Wilder moving average of true range, multiplying by 100; use zero for both when the smoothed true range is zero or unavailable. Take 100 times the Wilder moving average of the absolute difference of the two results divided by their sum; use zero when their sum is zero. The movement and strength smoothing periods are equal.
Calculation: Candle state = Evaluate these cases in order and take the first match: 10 if Williams %R crosses below its range midpoint; 11 if it crosses above its midpoint; 0 if %R is below its midpoint, directional strength is at least its previous value, and %R is above its previous value; 1 if %R is above its midpoint and below an upper threshold, strength is at least its previous value, and %R is above its previous value; 2 if %R is above the upper threshold, strength is at least its previous value, and %R is above its previous value; 3 if %R is above its midpoint, strength is at most its previous value, and %R is below its previous value; 4 if %R is below its midpoint, strength is at most its previous value, and %R is below its previous value; 5 if %R is above its midpoint, strength is at least its previous value, and %R is below its previous value; 6 if %R is below its midpoint and above a lower threshold, strength is at least its previous value, and %R is below its previous value; 7 if %R is below the lower threshold, strength is at least its previous value, and %R is below its previous value; 8 if %R is below its midpoint, strength is at most its previous value, and %R is above its previous value; 9 if %R is above its midpoint, strength is at most its previous value, and %R is above its previous value. Otherwise use 0. The lower threshold is below the midpoint and the upper threshold is above it.
Calculation: Directional movements = The upward and downward movement values used in directional strength, each smoothed with the same Wilder moving average period and divided by smoothed true range; use zero for both when smoothed true range is zero or unavailable.
Long: Candle state equals 0, 1, 2, 3, 9, or 11.
Short: Candle state equals 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, or 10.
Long: Smoothed upward directional movement is greater than or equal to smoothed downward directional movement.
Short: Smoothed upward directional movement is less than smoothed downward directional movement.
Long: Williams %R is above its range midpoint on each of three increasingly longer selected timeframes AND this was not true on the previous chart bar. Each selected timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe; on historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, and on a live bar use the bar still forming.
Short: Williams %R is below its range midpoint on each of three increasingly longer selected timeframes AND this was not true on the previous chart bar. Each selected timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe; on historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, and on a live bar use the bar still forming.
