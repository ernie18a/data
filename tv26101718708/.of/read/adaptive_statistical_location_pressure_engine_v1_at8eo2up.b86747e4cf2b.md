Calculation: Session upper/lower location percentile = empirical percentile rank of the current close’s percentage displacement above/below the active session open among matched completed-session upper/lower percentage excursions; freeze the distributions at session start and relax matching when samples are sparse.
Calculation: Session range percentile = empirical percentile rank of the active session’s high-low range among matched completed-session ranges.
Calculation: Efficiency percentile = empirical percentile rank of abs(session close − session open) / (session high − session low) among matched completed sessions.
Calculation: Directed-close percentile = empirical percentile rank of the close’s position in the session range, adjusted so higher values mean closer to the extreme in the session’s direction, among matched completed sessions.
Calculation: Weakening pressure = at a completed non-overlapping block, efficiency has declined from the prior block boundary, price has retreated from the session’s directional extreme, and either marginal-progress percentile is at or below its lower-quartile threshold or signed block progress is negative; direction must remain consistent across the block.
Calculation: Marginal-progress percentile = empirical percentile rank, among historical blocks from the same session and elapsed-age bucket, of signed close progress divided by the greater of incremental session range and a floor proportional to frozen session range median.
