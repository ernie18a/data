Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range window; reset at the first bar entering that window and fix the levels when the window ends.
Calculation: Value area high and low = divide the opening range into rows of height max(one tick, round(range / configured row count / tick size) ticks), capped at a configured maximum row count. Allocate each opening-range bar's volume proportionally to its high-low overlap with each row; allocate a zero-range bar's entire volume to its containing row. Start at the highest-volume row, choosing the lowest row on a tie. Repeatedly select the higher-volume adjacent row; on a tie select the row nearer the starting row, or the upper row if equally near. Include the selected row only if its inclusion does not exceed the configured fraction of total volume; otherwise stop. The value area high is the upper edge of the highest included row and the value area low is the lower edge of the lowest included row. The profile is valid only if the opening range has positive width and total allocated volume is positive.
Calculation: Setup state = reset to waiting for breakout at the first bar entering each opening-range window. After the window, on successive chart bars through the entry cutoff inclusive, record the first close above the opening range high or below the opening range low as the breakout direction. On a later bar, advance to confirmation when close is strictly between the value area low and high. Before confirmation or entry, invalidate the setup if a long breakout is followed by a close below the opening range low or a short breakout by a close above the opening range high. After an entry, exit, invalidation, or passage of the entry cutoff, do not allow another entry until the next reset.
Calculation: Stop = opening range low fixed at entry for a long position, or opening range high fixed at entry for a short position.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus the configured positive risk multiple times the absolute difference between entry price and stop for a long position, or entry price minus that amount for a short position.
Long: Profile is valid AND bar opening time is at or before the entry cutoff AND setup state is waiting for confirmation after a long breakout and a subsequent value-area retracement AND close > value area high AND close >= opening range low.
Short: Profile is valid AND bar opening time is at or before the entry cutoff AND setup state is waiting for confirmation after a short breakout and a subsequent value-area retracement AND close < value area low AND close <= opening range high.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= stop OR high >= profit target OR bar opening time >= configured exit time).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= stop OR low <= profit target OR bar opening time >= configured exit time).
