Calculation: Structure bias = Compare the two latest confirmed pivot highs and the two latest confirmed pivot lows on the longer of two liquidity timeframes. Bullish means both latest pivots are higher than their predecessors; bearish means both are lower; otherwise neutral. A pivot has the same number of bars on each side. Use the requested timeframe's forming bar when confirming pivots and update only when a new requested-timeframe bar is observed.
Calculation: Fair value gap = On either of two gap timeframes, when a new requested-timeframe bar is observed, compare the last completed bar with the bar two bars before it. A bullish gap has the completed bar's low above the earlier bar's high; its top is that low and its bottom is that high. A bearish gap has the completed bar's high below the earlier bar's low; its top is that earlier low and its bottom is that high. Retain only gaps at least a configurable price size, formed within the configured session if the session filter is enabled, aligned with or neutral to structure bias if the permissive bias filter is enabled, strictly aligned if the strict bias filter is enabled, and with midpoint at or below the previous completed higher-timeframe period's high-low midpoint for bullish gaps or at or above it for bearish gaps if the midpoint filter is enabled. Retain at most a configurable number of gaps, discarding the oldest first. On every chart bar, including the creation bar, delete a bullish gap when low reaches its top, midpoint, or bottom according to the selected mitigation mode; delete a bearish gap when high reaches its bottom, midpoint, or top respectively. In the bottom-or-top mitigation mode, shrink a touched bullish gap's top to low when low is strictly inside it, and shrink a touched bearish gap's bottom to high when high is strictly inside it. Evaluate touches before deletion or shrinking. The two gap timeframes and the two liquidity timeframes may each be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, depending on the chart.
Long: Structure bias is bullish.
Short: Structure bias is bearish.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is created OR low <= the top of an active bullish fair value gap.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is created OR high >= the bottom of an active bearish fair value gap.
