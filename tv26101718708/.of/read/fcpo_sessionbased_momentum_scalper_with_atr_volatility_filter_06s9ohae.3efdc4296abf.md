Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volatility = simple moving average of max(high - low, abs(high - previous close), abs(low - previous close)).
Calculation: Upper trendline = line through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs, evaluated at the current bar's opening time; each pivot uses the same number of bars on each side. Lower trendline is defined analogously using pivot lows. A line is unavailable until two distinct pivots are confirmed.
Calculation: Monthly realized result = sum of closed-trade price changes in the current calendar month, using exit price minus entry price for longs and entry price minus exit price for shorts; reset at the start of each month.
Calculation: Pause deadline = the closing bar's opening time plus a fixed pause duration whenever the specified number of consecutive closed trades are losses; reset the consecutive-loss count when that deadline is set or a trade closes without a loss.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = bar opening time is within either configured trading session in the session timezone AND weekday is enabled AND bar opening time is at or after the pause deadline AND bar opening time is outside each enabled exclusion window in the session timezone, including the Friday-only window AND (monthly profit limit is disabled OR monthly realized result < profit limit) AND (monthly loss limit is disabled OR monthly realized result > loss limit) AND (daily trade limit is disabled OR number of trades opened since the start of the day < daily limit).
Long: Entry eligibility AND long direction is enabled AND volatility >= volatility floor AND volatility <= volatility ceiling AND close > open AND (trendline filter is disabled OR upper trendline is unavailable OR close > upper trendline) AND ((body ratio >= body-ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) >= body-size threshold) OR (body ratio >= lower early-warning ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) >= lower early-warning body-size threshold AND (body ratio < body-ratio threshold OR abs(close - open) < body-size threshold))).
Short: Entry eligibility AND short direction is enabled AND volatility >= volatility floor AND volatility <= volatility ceiling AND close < open AND (trendline filter is disabled OR lower trendline is unavailable OR close < lower trendline) AND ((body ratio >= body-ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) >= body-size threshold) OR (body ratio >= lower early-warning ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) >= lower early-warning body-size threshold AND (body ratio < body-ratio threshold OR abs(close - open) < body-size threshold))).
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance.
Exit: long OR short, time exit is enabled AND bars elapsed since entry >= time-exit limit.
Exit: long OR short, session exit is enabled AND ((session-transition mode is selected AND previous bar is in a trading session AND current bar is outside that same session) OR (two-session exit mode is selected AND bar opening time in the exchange timezone equals either configured session-exit time) OR (end-of-day-only mode is selected AND bar opening time in the exchange timezone equals the configured end-of-day exit time)).
