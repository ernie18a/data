Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Tracked liquidity levels = latest confirmed swing high/low; equal high/low formed by averaging the two latest confirmed swing highs/lows when their difference is within an ATR-based tolerance; previous-day and previous-week highs/lows from the last completed higher-timeframe daily and weekly bars; and current calendar-day running high/low from the prior completed chart bar.
Calculation: Selected upper/lower sweep level = eligible tracked level with the highest rank, where sweep depth is current high minus level (upper) or level minus current low (lower), must exceed an ATR-based sweep buffer and not exceed a maximum ATR depth, and rank is source importance minus a depth penalty.
Calculation: Pending raid = the selected upper/lower level and its sweep bar, extreme, source importance, maximum rejection-wick ratio, and maximum relative volume are stored when a sweep occurs; a later higher-importance sweep replaces the pending raid.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average.
Calculation: Directional flow = exponentially smoothed buy pressure minus sell pressure, divided by exponentially smoothed volume; buy pressure is volume times (close−low)/(high−low), and sell pressure is volume times (high−close)/(high−low).
Calculation: Recovery score = clamped weighted sum of normalized source importance, sweep depth in ATR, reclaim speed within the allowed reclaim window, maximum rejection-wick ratio, maximum relative volume, direction-aligned flow, close-back distance in ATR, and a bonus for a nearby active same-direction fair value gap; reduce the depth weight in high volatility and the speed weight in low volatility. High/low volatility means ATR is above/below a longer-lookback simple moving average of ATR by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Active bullish/bearish fair value gap = low > high two bars earlier / high < low two bars earlier, with the middle bar's absolute open-close displacement meeting an ATR-based minimum; retain the latest gap while within its age limit and not invalidated, and treat it as nearby when its zone overlaps the sweep level within an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Optional lower-timeframe reclaim ratio = fraction of lower-timeframe bars within the completed chart bar whose closes are above the pending lower-sweep level (long) or below the pending upper-sweep level (short); apply only when confirmation is enabled and the selected timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: A pending lower-level sweep is reclaimed within the allowed reclaim window and close > pending level + ATR-based reclaim buffer, and recovery score >= minimum score, and (lower-timeframe confirmation is disabled/invalid or its reclaim ratio >= minimum ratio).
Short: A pending upper-level sweep is reclaimed within the allowed reclaim window and close < pending level − ATR-based reclaim buffer, and recovery score >= minimum score, and (lower-timeframe confirmation is disabled/invalid or its reclaim ratio >= minimum ratio).
Long: A pending upper-level sweep has lasted at least the acceptance delay and close > pending level + ATR-based acceptance buffer.
Short: A pending lower-level sweep has lasted at least the acceptance delay and close < pending level − ATR-based acceptance buffer.
