Calculation: Liquidity levels = enabled sources for each side: latest confirmed symmetric pivot high or low; mean of the two latest confirmed same-side pivots when their difference is at most an ATR-scaled tolerance; high or low of each of two higher timeframes, using the last completed bar; and the developing session high or low through the previous chart bar. Rank source importance from highest to lowest: longer higher timeframe, shorter higher timeframe, equal pivots, developing session, single pivot.
Calculation: Sweep candidate = a high-side level with sweep depth high minus level, or a low-side level with sweep depth level minus low, where depth is greater than the ATR-scaled sweep buffer and at most the ATR-scaled maximum depth. For each side select the candidate with the highest source-importance-weighted rank minus ATR-normalized depth; resolve ties in source order: single pivot, equal pivots, shorter higher timeframe, longer higher timeframe, developing session.
Calculation: Pending sweep = maintain independent high-side and low-side states. At each bar first expire a state if its age exceeds the current session-adjusted reclaim window. Then create a state from the selected same-side candidate if no state exists or the candidate has greater source importance; store its level, importance and bar. From creation through resolution maintain the most extreme high or low, maximum same-side rejection-wick-to-range ratio, and maximum relative volume. Clear the state after a qualifying reclaim, even if its score is below the signal threshold, or after acceptance. A newly created state can resolve on the same bar. The reclaim window is the rounded base window multiplied by the current UTC session multiplier, with a minimum of one bar; the multipliers differ by session.
Calculation: Recovery score = clamp to the score range the weighted sum of: normalized source importance; normalized sweep depth using the current ATR and the most extreme price since the sweep; remaining fraction of the current reclaim window; maximum rejection-wick ratio normalized by its target; maximum relative volume normalized by its target; direction-aligned flow normalized by its target; ATR-normalized distance of close back through the swept level; and a fair-value-gap confluence bonus. Clamp each normalized component to the unit interval. Use a lower depth weight in a high-volatility regime and a lower speed weight in a low-volatility regime; determine regimes by comparing ATR with a longer simple moving average of ATR multiplied by separate upper and lower thresholds.
Calculation: Directional flow = EMA of volume times ((close minus low) minus (high minus close)) divided by bar range, divided by the greater of EMA of volume and a positive floor. Use the same smoothing length for both EMAs. Relative volume = volume divided by its simple moving average, or a neutral value when that average is not positive. Use a positive price-resolution floor for bar range.
Calculation: Fair-value-gap confluence = when enabled, retain the most recently formed gap on each side. A bullish gap forms when low exceeds the high two bars earlier; a bearish gap forms when high is below the low two bars earlier. In both cases the absolute body of the intervening bar must be at least an ATR-scaled impulse threshold. The gap is active when its age is at most the maximum age and current low remains above its lower boundary for a bullish gap, or current high remains below its upper boundary for a bearish gap. Confluence requires the pending level to lie within the active same-direction gap expanded on both sides by an ATR-scaled tolerance.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe confirmation = when enabled and the specified timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, the fraction of completed lower-timeframe bars within the current completed chart bar whose closes are above the pending low-side level or below the pending high-side level, respectively; require at least one lower-timeframe bar. Otherwise confirmation passes.
Calculation: Yearly VWAP = cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume since the start of the current year, using a neutral volume for missing volume and typical price when cumulative volume is not positive.
Long: A selected low-side sweep candidate exists AND no low-side pending sweep exists OR a selected low-side sweep candidate exists AND its importance exceeds the existing low-side pending sweep importance.
Short: A selected high-side sweep candidate exists AND no high-side pending sweep exists OR a selected high-side sweep candidate exists AND its importance exceeds the existing high-side pending sweep importance.
Long: A low-side pending sweep exists AND its age is at most the current session-adjusted reclaim window AND close is above its level plus the ATR-scaled reclaim buffer AND lower-timeframe confirmation passes AND recovery score is at least the minimum recovery score.
Short: A high-side pending sweep exists AND its age is at most the current session-adjusted reclaim window AND close is below its level minus the ATR-scaled reclaim buffer AND lower-timeframe confirmation passes AND recovery score is at least the minimum recovery score.
Long: A high-side pending sweep exists AND its age is at least the acceptance delay AND close is above its level plus the ATR-scaled acceptance buffer.
Short: A low-side pending sweep exists AND its age is at least the acceptance delay AND close is below its level minus the ATR-scaled acceptance buffer.
Long: Directional flow is greater than zero.
Short: Directional flow is less than zero.
Long: EMA of close is above yearly VWAP AND close is above EMA of close AND close is above yearly VWAP.
Short: EMA of close is below yearly VWAP AND close is below EMA of close AND close is below yearly VWAP.
