Calculation: Camarilla levels = From the last completed higher-timeframe bar, set range to the greater of high minus low and a minimum range parameter. Set S3 and R3 to close minus and plus a range multiplier times range; set S4 and R4 likewise using twice that multiplier.
Long: The current bar is not the first bar of a higher-timeframe period AND low <= S3 AND close > S3 AND close > open.
Long: The current bar is not the first bar of a higher-timeframe period AND close > R4 AND close > open AND (previous close <= current R4 OR open <= R4).
Short: The current bar is not the first bar of a higher-timeframe period AND high >= R3 AND close < R3 AND close < open.
Short: The current bar is not the first bar of a higher-timeframe period AND close < S4 AND close < open AND (previous close >= current S4 OR open >= S4).
