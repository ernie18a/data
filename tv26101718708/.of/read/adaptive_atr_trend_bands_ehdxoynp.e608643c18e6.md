Calculation: Basis = selected EMA, SMA, or VWMA of close over a lookback
Calculation: Raw upper band = basis + ATR × band multiplier
Calculation: Raw lower band = basis − ATR × band multiplier
Calculation: Final upper band = raw upper band on initialization; thereafter raw upper band if it is below the previous final upper band or the previous close is above the previous final upper band, otherwise the previous final upper band
Calculation: Final lower band = raw lower band on initialization; thereafter raw lower band if it is above the previous final lower band or the previous close is below the previous final lower band, otherwise the previous final lower band
Calculation: Trend state = initialize bullish when close ≥ basis, otherwise bearish; thereafter switch from bearish to bullish on a close above the previous final upper band when long filters pass, or from bullish to bearish on a close below the previous final lower band when short filters pass; otherwise retain the prior state
Calculation: Adaptive slope strength = clamp(0.60 × EMA of absolute basis change over a chart-timeframe-selected lag divided by ATR, smoothed over a chart-timeframe-selected lookback, 0.05, 0.30)
Calculation: Adaptive ATR expansion threshold = clamp(0.85 × EMA of ATR divided by its chart-timeframe-selected EMA, smoothed over the same chart-timeframe-selected lookback, 0.70, 1.10)
Long: Previous trend state is bearish AND close > previous final upper band AND basis change over the chart-timeframe-selected lag > ATR × adaptive slope strength AND ATR / its chart-timeframe-selected EMA ≥ adaptive ATR expansion threshold AND (RSI filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe RSI ≤ its lower threshold) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe close > its previous completed EMA) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is confirmed) AND last signal direction is not long AND bars since last signal ≥ minimum spacing
Short: Previous trend state is bullish AND close < previous final lower band AND basis change over the chart-timeframe-selected lag < −ATR × adaptive slope strength AND ATR / its chart-timeframe-selected EMA ≥ adaptive ATR expansion threshold AND (RSI filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe RSI ≥ its upper threshold) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe close < its previous completed EMA) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is confirmed) AND last signal direction is not short AND bars since last signal ≥ minimum spacing
