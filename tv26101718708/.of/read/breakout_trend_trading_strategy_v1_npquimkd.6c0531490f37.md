Calculation: Breakout direction = bullish when close > previous close and either (Supertrend is bullish AND absolute close change > ATR × trend price multiplier AND volume > SMA(volume) × trend volume multiplier), (Supertrend is bearish AND absolute close change > ATR × reverse price multiplier AND volume > SMA(volume) × reverse volume multiplier), volume > SMA(volume) × high-volume multiplier, or absolute close change > ATR × high-breakout multiplier; bearish when close < previous close and the same conditions hold with bullish and bearish Supertrend roles reversed; otherwise neutral. Missing volume satisfies the trend and reverse volume comparisons, but not the high-volume comparison.
Calculation: Breakout levels = on a bullish breakout, set the upper target to high plus true range multiplied by the trend markup if Supertrend is bullish, otherwise the reverse markup, and set the upper stop to low minus ATR × stop factor. On a bearish breakout, set the lower target to low minus true range multiplied by the trend markup if Supertrend is bearish, otherwise the reverse markup, and set the lower stop to high plus ATR × stop factor. Carry each level forward until its next update.
Calculation: Trailing levels = on each bar, if the breakout is bullish OR the position is long, propose lower = close − ATR × stop multiplier and upper = close + ATR × compound multiplier; retain each previous level when its proposal is below its previous value OR close is below the previous upper level. Otherwise, if the breakout is bearish OR the position is short, propose lower = close − ATR × compound multiplier and upper = close + ATR × stop multiplier; retain each previous level when its proposal is above its previous value OR close is above the previous lower level. Otherwise set lower = close − ATR × stop multiplier and upper = close + ATR × stop multiplier. Use the proposed level when its previous level is unavailable.
Long: Breakout direction = bullish.
Short: Breakout direction = bearish.
Exit: Long, low <= previous bar's lower trailing level.
Exit: Short, high >= previous bar's upper trailing level.
Exit: Long, datetime >= backtest cutoff AND breakout direction != bullish AND (Supertrend-dependent source crosses below previous lower target OR Supertrend-dependent source crosses below previous upper stop), where the source is close when Supertrend is bullish and low otherwise.
Exit: Short, datetime >= backtest cutoff AND breakout direction != bearish AND (Supertrend-dependent source crosses above previous upper target OR Supertrend-dependent source crosses above previous lower stop), where the source is close when Supertrend is bearish and high otherwise.
