Calculation: True range = max(high − low, |high − previous close|, |low − previous close|); ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Trend breakout = |close − previous close| > ATR × trend threshold OR (high − low > 2 × ATR × trend threshold when the range-breakout option is enabled). Reverse breakout uses a separate threshold. High breakout uses a threshold greater than either of those thresholds.
Calculation: Trend volume breakout = volume > volume SMA × (1 + trend markup); reverse volume breakout uses a larger markup. High volume breakout = volume > volume SMA × high-volume multiplier. Missing volume passes the trend and reverse volume checks but not the high-volume check.
Calculation: Breakout state = +1 when close > previous close and (bearish trend direction with trend price and volume breakouts OR bullish trend direction with reverse price and volume breakouts OR high breakout OR high-volume breakout); −1 when close < previous close and (bullish trend direction with trend price and volume breakouts OR bearish trend direction with reverse price and volume breakouts OR high breakout OR high-volume breakout); otherwise 0. Trend direction is the Supertrend direction.
Calculation: Breakout range = max(high − low, |high − previous close|, |low − previous close|).
Calculation: Up target = high + breakout range × direction-dependent markup when breakout state is +1; down target = low − breakout range × direction-dependent markup when breakout state is −1. Retain each target's last non-missing value as its target line.
Calculation: Up stop target = low − ATR × stop factor when breakout state is +1; down stop target = high + ATR × stop factor when breakout state is −1. Retain each stop target's last non-missing value as its stop line.
Calculation: New high = highest high over the shorter lookback equals highest high over the longer lookback. New low = lowest low over the shorter lookback equals lowest low over the longer lookback.
Calculation: MA alignment = a score from the ordering of close and a sequence of increasingly long SMAs; sum the per-bar score over the same lookback used for the OHLC4 percentile rank. MA-high passes when the sum exceeds the alignment threshold; MA-low passes when it is below the negative threshold. The percentile rank is of OHLC4 over that lookback.
Calculation: Yearly high filter = latest completed 12-month high exceeds each available high from the prior two years OR close exceeds the latest high and the lesser of the prior two highs OR the yearly filter is disabled. Yearly low filter = latest completed 12-month low is below each available low from the prior two years OR close is below the latest low and the greater of the prior two lows OR the yearly filter is disabled. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; compare with the corresponding bars one and two years earlier.
Long: (crossover of low in bullish trend direction or high in bearish trend direction above the prior up target line AND (last breakout state > 0 OR reverse-breakout filtering is disabled) AND close > prior down target line) OR (crossover of that same price above the prior down stop line AND (last breakout state < 0 OR reverse-breakout filtering is disabled)) OR (breakout state = +1 AND that same price > prior up target line); AND yearly high filter; AND breakout state ≠ −1; AND ((new high AND MA-high AND (MA Supertrend direction is bullish OR that filter is disabled) AND OHLC4 percentile rank exceeds its upper threshold) OR strategy net profit ≥ 0); AND compound condition passes.
Short: (crossunder of low in bearish trend direction or high in bullish trend direction below the prior down target line AND (last breakout state < 0 OR reverse-breakout filtering is disabled) AND close < prior up target line) OR (crossunder of that same price below the prior up stop line AND (last breakout state > 0 OR reverse-breakout filtering is disabled)) OR (breakout state = −1 AND that same price < prior down target line); AND yearly low filter; AND breakout state ≠ +1; AND ((new low AND MA-low AND (MA Supertrend direction is bearish OR that filter is disabled) AND OHLC4 percentile rank is below its lower threshold) OR strategy net profit ≥ 0); AND compound condition passes.
Calculation: Compound condition = allow-immediate-compound is enabled OR (long position and close > sell stop) OR (short position and close < buy stop) OR no position.
Calculation: Buy stop = close − ATR × stop multiplier; sell stop = close + ATR × stop multiplier. While long, buy stop cannot decrease and sell stop cannot decrease; while short, sell stop cannot increase and buy stop cannot increase. Apply the source's prior-stop and close-crossing hold rules when updating these stops.
Exit: Long, low ≤ buy stop OR (exit-on-signal is enabled AND (crossunder of close in bearish trend direction or low in bullish trend direction below the prior down target line OR below the prior up stop line)).
Exit: Short, high ≥ sell stop OR (exit-on-signal is enabled AND (crossover of close in bullish trend direction or high in bearish trend direction above the prior up target line OR above the prior down stop line)).
