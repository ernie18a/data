Calculation: Regime = trending if ADX exceeds its trending threshold AND the absolute difference between the short-period and long-period close EMAs exceeds ATR times a threshold; otherwise ranging if ADX is below its ranging threshold AND (highest high minus lowest low over a lookback) / close is below a threshold; otherwise volatile if Bollinger Band width exceeds its longer-lookback SMA times a threshold AND ATR exceeds its longer-lookback SMA times a threshold; otherwise quiet if both corresponding values are below their respective thresholds; otherwise other. Bollinger Band width = (upper band - lower band) / basis.
Calculation: Bullish pattern score = sum of configurable weights for each true pattern: bullish engulfing, hammer, morning star, piercing, and double bottom. Use a different hammer weight in the ranging regime and a different double-bottom weight in the volatile regime. Each pattern additionally requires low <= lowest low over a lookback times a support factor AND volume > its SMA times a volume factor. Bullish engulfing: previous close < previous open AND close > open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close. Hammer: high - low > a body-size factor times abs(open - close) AND (close - low) / (high - low + a small positive constant) > a threshold. Morning star: close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND previous absolute body < a factor times the previous range AND close > open AND close > the midpoint of the open and close two bars ago. Piercing: previous close < previous open AND close > open AND close > the midpoint of the previous open and close AND open < previous close. Double bottom: low < previous low AND previous low > low two bars ago AND low two bars ago < low three bars ago AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish pattern score = sum of configurable weights for each true pattern: bearish engulfing, shooting star, evening star, dark cloud, and double top. Use a different shooting-star weight in the ranging regime and a different double-top weight in the volatile regime. Each pattern additionally requires high >= highest high over a lookback times a resistance factor AND volume > its SMA times a volume factor. Bearish engulfing: previous close > previous open AND close < open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close. Shooting star: high - low > a body-size factor times abs(open - close) AND (high - close) / (high - low + a small positive constant) > a threshold. Evening star: close two bars ago > open two bars ago AND previous absolute body < a factor times the previous range AND close < open AND close < the midpoint of the open and close two bars ago. Dark cloud: previous close > previous open AND close < open AND close < the midpoint of the previous open and close AND open > previous close. Double top: high > previous high AND previous high < high two bars ago AND high two bars ago > high three bars ago AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish score = bullish pattern score plus configurable weights for each true condition: short-period close EMA > long-period close EMA; RSI < its lower threshold; MACD line > MACD signal line; ATR > its longer-lookback SMA times a factor.
Calculation: Bearish score = bearish pattern score plus configurable weights for each true condition: short-period close EMA < long-period close EMA; RSI > its upper threshold; MACD line < MACD signal line; ATR > its longer-lookback SMA times a factor.
Calculation: Long initial stop = lowest low over a swing lookback at the signal bar minus ATR at the signal bar times a multiplier. Long target = signal-bar close plus a reward multiplier times (signal-bar close minus long initial stop).
Calculation: Short initial stop = highest high over a swing lookback at the signal bar plus ATR at the signal bar times a multiplier. Short target = signal-bar close minus a reward multiplier times (short initial stop minus signal-bar close).
Long: bullish score >= score threshold.
Short: bearish score >= score threshold.
Long: short-period close EMA > long-period close EMA + ATR times a trend-strength threshold.
Short: short-period close EMA < long-period close EMA - ATR times a trend-strength threshold.
Exit: long, low <= long initial stop OR high >= long target OR bearish score >= score threshold.
Exit: short, high >= short initial stop OR low <= short target OR bullish score >= score threshold.
