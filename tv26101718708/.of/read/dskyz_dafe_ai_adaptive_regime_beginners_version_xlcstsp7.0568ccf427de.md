Calculation: Regime = Trending if ADX is above its threshold and the fast/slow EMA gap exceeds an ATR multiple; otherwise Range if ADX is below its threshold and the 20-bar high-low range divided by close is below its threshold; otherwise Volatile if Bollinger Band width exceeds its long-term average by a multiplier and ATR exceeds its long-term average by a multiplier; otherwise Quiet if both are below their respective average-based thresholds; otherwise Other. Use this priority order.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume exceeds its moving average by a multiplier.
Calculation: Near support = low is within a threshold percentage above the lowest low over the pattern lookback. Near resistance = high is within a threshold percentage below the highest high over the pattern lookback.
Calculation: Bullish pattern score = weighted sum of bullish engulfing, hammer, morning star, piercing pattern, and double bottom, each requiring near support and a volume spike. Double bottom is low below prior low, prior low above the low two bars ago, low two bars ago below the low three bars ago, and a bullish candle. Hammer and double-bottom weights vary by regime.
Calculation: Bearish pattern score = weighted sum of bearish engulfing, shooting star, evening star, dark cloud cover, and double top, each requiring near resistance and a volume spike. Double top is high above prior high, prior high below the high two bars ago, high two bars ago above the high three bars ago, and a bearish candle. Shooting-star and double-top weights vary by regime.
Calculation: Bull score = bullish pattern score plus weighted bullish trend (fast EMA > slow EMA), oversold RSI, bullish MACD (MACD line > signal line), and volatility expansion (ATR exceeds its moving average by a multiplier).
Calculation: Bear score = bearish pattern score plus weighted bearish trend (fast EMA < slow EMA), overbought RSI, bearish MACD (MACD line < signal line), and volatility expansion (ATR exceeds its moving average by a multiplier).
Long: Bull score >= threshold AND drawdown from peak equity <= maximum allowed drawdown.
Short: Bear score >= threshold AND drawdown from peak equity <= maximum allowed drawdown.
Exit: Long, low <= the greater of the initial stop and trailing stop OR high >= the profit target. Initial stop = lowest low over the swing lookback minus ATR times a multiplier; profit target = signal-bar close plus twice the distance from signal-bar close to initial stop. After entry, raise the trailing stop to the greater of its prior value and close minus ATR times a multiplier when close > half the profit-target price.
Exit: Short, high >= the lesser of the initial stop and trailing stop OR low <= the profit target. Initial stop = highest high over the swing lookback plus ATR times a multiplier; profit target = signal-bar close minus twice the distance from initial stop to signal-bar close. After entry, lower the trailing stop to the lesser of its prior value and close plus ATR times a multiplier when close < half the profit-target price.
