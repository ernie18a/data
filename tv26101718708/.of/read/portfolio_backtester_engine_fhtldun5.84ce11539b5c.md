Calculation: Raw long signal = RSI of close crosses above an oversold threshold; raw short signal = RSI of close crosses below an overbought threshold. Either signal may optionally be interpreted as the opposite direction.
Calculation: Delayed signal = optionally retain the latest raw signal and emit it after a configurable number of bars; reset the waiting count when the bars-since-long and bars-since-short series cross. Count the signal bar as the first waiting bar and emit when the count exceeds the configured wait.
Calculation: Entry stop = on the entry bar, for a long use either zero, min(open, close) minus an ATR multiple, or min(open, close) reduced by a percentage; for a short use either an effectively infinite value, max(open, close) plus an ATR multiple, or max(open, close) increased by a percentage. Stop gap = absolute difference between entry price and entry stop.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize to the entry stop on the entry bar. Subsequently select a candidate: the highest high since long entry minus, or the lowest low since short entry plus, either a stop-gap multiple, a percentage of that extreme, or a fixed amount; entry price; the average of the preceding two bars' min(open, close) minus an ATR multiple for a long or max(open, close) plus an ATR multiple for a short; or the previous low for a long or previous high for a short. If the candidate is above the current high for a long or below the current low for a short, retain the previous stop. Otherwise update the stop only toward the trade direction and only when the selected activation condition is met: candidate passes the entry stop; candidate passes both the entry stop and entry price adjusted in the trade direction by a stop-gap multiple, percentage, or fixed amount; or the highest high since long entry or lowest low since short entry passes both the entry stop and entry price adjusted in the trade direction by a percentage.
Calculation: Smoothed log rate of change = EMA of 100 times the natural logarithm of the selected price source divided by its value a configurable number of bars earlier.
Long: Delayed long signal AND each enabled filter passes: MACD histogram > 0; close > open; volume > previous volume; SMA of close > every SMA value in its configured preceding lookback; close minus the currently calculated long entry-stop level < a configured percentage of close; close < previous close increased by a maximum percentage; close > previous close increased by a minimum percentage; filter RSI <= its overbought threshold; close > selected moving average of a selected price source.
Short: Delayed short signal AND each enabled filter passes: MACD histogram < 0; close < open; volume > previous volume; SMA of close < every SMA value in its configured preceding lookback; the currently calculated short entry-stop level minus close < a configured percentage of close; close > previous close reduced by a maximum percentage; close < previous close reduced by a minimum percentage; filter RSI >= its oversold threshold; close < selected moving average of a selected price source.
Long: Short position exits on a non-stop condition AND reversal is enabled.
Short: Long position exits on a non-stop condition AND reversal is enabled.
Exit: long, delayed short signal OR smoothed log rate of change was greater than every value in its preceding rising lookback on the previous bar and is no longer so OR exit RSI crosses below its upper threshold OR close > entry price plus a stop-gap multiple OR close > entry price increased by a percentage OR low <= entry stop OR low < the previous bar's trailing stop, using the current trailing stop on the entry bar.
Exit: short, delayed long signal OR smoothed log rate of change was less than every value in its preceding falling lookback on the previous bar and is no longer so OR exit RSI crosses above its lower threshold OR close < entry price minus a stop-gap multiple OR close < entry price reduced by a percentage OR high >= entry stop OR high > the previous bar's trailing stop, using the current trailing stop on the entry bar.
