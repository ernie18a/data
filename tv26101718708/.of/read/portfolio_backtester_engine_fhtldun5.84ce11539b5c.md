Long: RSI crosses above its lower entry threshold AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram > 0) AND (bar-direction filter disabled OR close > open) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > prior-bar volume) AND (moving-average-slope filter disabled OR SMA is rising over its configured lookback) AND (maximum-stop-risk filter disabled OR close − long entry stop < close × allowed-risk fraction) AND (maximum-close-change filter disabled OR close < prior close × (1 + allowed-change fraction)) AND (minimum-close-change filter disabled OR close > prior close × (1 + required-change fraction)) AND (RSI-extreme filter disabled OR RSI ≤ its upper threshold) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close > selected moving average)
Short: RSI crosses below its upper entry threshold AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram < 0) AND (bar-direction filter disabled OR close < open) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > prior-bar volume) AND (moving-average-slope filter disabled OR SMA is falling over its configured lookback) AND (maximum-stop-risk filter disabled OR short entry stop − close < close × allowed-risk fraction) AND (maximum-close-change filter disabled OR close > prior close × (1 − allowed-change fraction)) AND (minimum-close-change filter disabled OR close < prior close × (1 − required-change fraction)) AND (RSI-extreme filter disabled OR RSI ≥ its lower threshold) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close < selected moving average)
Exit: long, low ≤ active long protective stop OR an opposite short entry signal occurs OR (SROC exit enabled AND SROC was rising over its configured lookback on the prior bar and is no longer rising) OR (RSI-cross exit enabled AND RSI crosses below its configured long-exit threshold) OR (X-target exit enabled AND close > entry price + target multiple × entry-stop distance) OR (percentage-target exit enabled AND close > entry price × (1 + target fraction))
Exit: short, high ≥ active short protective stop OR an opposite long entry signal occurs OR (SROC exit enabled AND SROC was falling over its configured lookback on the prior bar and is no longer falling) OR (RSI-cross exit enabled AND RSI crosses above its configured short-exit threshold) OR (X-target exit enabled AND close < entry price − target multiple × entry-stop distance) OR (percentage-target exit enabled AND close < entry price × (1 − target fraction))
Calculation: MACD histogram = difference between the MACD line and its signal line; MACD line = short-lookback EMA of close minus long-lookback EMA of close; signal line = EMA of the MACD line
Calculation: SROC = EMA-smoothed logarithmic rate of change, 100 × ln(selected source / its value at the configured ROC lookback)
