Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi = EMA-smooth each OHLC field using a pre-smoothing length; calculate Heikin-Ashi close as their average, Heikin-Ashi open as the average of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close, initialized to the average of the smoothed open and close, and Heikin-Ashi high and low as the maximum and minimum of the smoothed high or low and the Heikin-Ashi open and close; EMA-smooth each resulting field using a post-smoothing length.
Calculation: Raw direction = bullish when smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= smoothed Heikin-Ashi open; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Structure direction and protected levels = initialize direction to raw direction. Detect pivot highs and lows in the selected raw-price or smoothed Heikin-Ashi highs and lows using equal left and right strengths; recognize each pivot only after the right-side bars have completed. Store the latest confirmed pivot high and low. While bullish, replace the protected low with each newly confirmed pivot low, or initialize it from the latest pivot low if missing; while bearish, do the symmetric operation for the protected high. On each bar, calculate both break conditions before changing direction: bearish break is selected close or low < protected low - ATR multiplied by a buffer; bullish break is selected close or high > protected high + ATR multiplied by the buffer. If locking is enabled, process a bearish break while bullish first, switching direction to bearish, setting protected high to the latest pivot high and clearing protected low; then process a bullish break while bearish using the previously calculated break condition, switching direction to bullish, setting protected low to the latest pivot low and clearing protected high. If locking is disabled, direction equals raw direction.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe direction = raw direction calculated independently on each enabled selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Use either the still-forming higher-timeframe bar or the previous higher-timeframe bar; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar or its preceding intrabar respectively. Apply the same pre- and post-smoothing lengths as on the chart.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe confirmation = on a selected higher timeframe, use either the still-forming bar or the previous completed bar. In Heikin-Ashi mode use raw direction with the chart's smoothing lengths. In EMA mode, bullish means close > EMA(close) AND EMA(close) >= its preceding value; bearish means close < EMA(close) AND EMA(close) <= its preceding value; otherwise confirmation is neutral.

Short: Raw direction = bearish.
Long: An enabled multi-timeframe direction = bullish.
Short: An enabled multi-timeframe direction = bearish.

Short: Structure locking enabled AND direction before break processing = bullish AND bearish break condition = true, OR structure locking disabled AND raw direction = bearish AND previous raw direction = bullish.
Long: Structure direction changes from bearish to bullish AND bullish enabled-timeframe count >= minimum of the required agreement count and enabled-timeframe count AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe confirmation = bullish) AND (reversal mode selected OR EMA of smoothed Heikin-Ashi close > its previous value) AND (impulse filter disabled OR absolute smoothed Heikin-Ashi body > its previous value).
Short: Structure direction changes from bullish to bearish AND bearish enabled-timeframe count >= minimum of the required agreement count and enabled-timeframe count AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe confirmation = bearish) AND (reversal mode selected OR EMA of smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < its previous value) AND (impulse filter disabled OR absolute smoothed Heikin-Ashi body > its previous value).
