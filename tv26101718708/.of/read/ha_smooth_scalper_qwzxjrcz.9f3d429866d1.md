Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi open and close = EMA-smoothed OHLC are used to calculate Heikin-Ashi close as their four-price average and Heikin-Ashi open as the average of the prior Heikin-Ashi open and close (initially the average of smoothed open and close); EMA-smooth both values again. Direction is bullish when smoothed close ≥ smoothed open, otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Protected structure direction and level = initialize direction from smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction; find confirmed pivot highs and lows in raw or smoothed Heikin-Ashi prices using equal left and right lookbacks. In bullish direction, update protected low on each confirmed pivot low; in bearish direction, update protected high on each confirmed pivot high. A bullish direction changes to bearish when close (close mode) or low (wick mode) < protected low − buffer × ATR; bearish changes to bullish when close (close mode) or high (wick mode) > protected high + buffer × ATR. After a change, protect the latest opposite-side pivot. If structure locking is disabled, direction follows smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction.
Calculation: MTF agreement = count of enabled timeframes whose smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction matches the specified side; use each timeframe’s last completed bar when confirmed candles are enabled, otherwise its bar still forming. These timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe confirmation = either bullish when close > EMA and EMA ≥ its prior value, bearish when close < EMA and EMA ≤ its prior value (EMA-slope-and-price mode), or the smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar when confirmed candles are enabled, otherwise its bar still forming.
Calculation: Slow baseline = EMA of smoothed Heikin-Ashi close, using a longer lookback than the fast baseline (smoothed Heikin-Ashi close itself).
Long: protected structure direction changes from bearish to bullish OR (chart direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (no timeframes are enabled OR bullish MTF agreement ≥ minimum required agreement) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR bullish) AND (reversal mode OR slow baseline > its prior value) AND (impulse filter is disabled OR absolute difference between smoothed Heikin-Ashi close and open > its prior value))
Short: protected structure direction changes from bullish to bearish OR (chart direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (no timeframes are enabled OR bearish MTF agreement ≥ minimum required agreement) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR bearish) AND (reversal mode OR slow baseline < its prior value) AND (impulse filter is disabled OR absolute difference between smoothed Heikin-Ashi close and open > its prior value))
