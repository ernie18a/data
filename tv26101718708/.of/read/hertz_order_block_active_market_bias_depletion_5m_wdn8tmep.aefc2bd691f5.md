Calculation: Structure high and low = highest high and lowest low over a structure lookback ending on the previous bar. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above structure high plus an ATR buffer; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below structure low minus an ATR buffer. Compare the previous close with the previous bar's corresponding buffered level.
Calculation: Directional displacement = a directional candle whose body is at least an ATR multiple, whose body-to-range ratio is at least a threshold, and whose close is within a specified fraction of its range from its directional extreme; OR a three-bar sequence with at least two directional candles, a close beyond the close two bars earlier in that direction, and a move from the sequence's opposite extreme to the current close of at least an ATR multiple. Use a positive minimum price increment as the denominator floor for candle ranges and ATR.
Calculation: Directional confirmation = a same-direction fair value gap occurred within its lookback, a same-direction candle pattern occurred within its lookback, volume divided by its current-inclusive SMA reached a threshold within its lookback, and a same-direction liquidity sweep occurred within its lookback; apply each requirement only when its corresponding filter is enabled. A bullish gap is low > high two bars earlier; a bearish gap is high < low two bars earlier. A bullish sweep is low < structure low AND close > structure low; a bearish sweep is high > structure high AND close < structure high. The bullish patterns are: bearish-to-bullish engulfing with current open <= previous close and current close >= previous open; OR a bearish candle two bars earlier, a small-bodied middle candle, and a bullish current candle closing above the first candle's body midpoint; OR three bullish candles with strictly rising closes and body-to-range ratios at least their respective thresholds. Bearish patterns reverse all directions and inequalities. Pattern body ratios and middle-candle size use their own thresholds.
Calculation: Formation quality = a base score plus separately weighted points for each recent same-direction gap, same-direction pattern, volume expansion, and same-direction sweep, whether or not their filters are enabled.
Calculation: Order block creation = on a directional break with same-direction displacement within its displacement lookback, directional confirmation, and formation quality >= its threshold, select the nearest opposite-direction candle within the origin lookback. Require its index to differ from the last successfully created same-direction block's origin. The zone is optionally the candle's full high-to-low range, its body, or its refined range: low to open for a bullish block and open to high for a bearish block. Optionally require zone height divided by creation-bar ATR to lie between minimum and maximum size thresholds. Store the creation ATR, quality, directional impulse from the current close to the opposite extreme of the latest three bars divided by ATR, boundaries, birth bar and direction. Keep at most the configured number of blocks, removing the oldest first, including invalidated blocks.
Calculation: Active block lifecycle = on each confirmed bar, first invalidate a bullish block when close < zone bottom, or low < zone bottom if wick invalidation is selected; invalidate a bearish block when close > zone top, or high > zone top if wick invalidation is selected. Otherwise permanently arm it when bullish close > zone top plus a creation-ATR departure buffer, or bearish close < zone bottom minus that buffer. A touch is low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom. Starting from fresh, an armed block becomes in-zone on a touch after the minimum age, otherwise approach when close's distance outside the zone divided by creation ATR <= the approach threshold. From approach it becomes in-zone on such a touch, otherwise fresh when that distance exceeds the threshold. From in-zone, on a later bar it becomes hold if bullish close > zone top plus a creation-ATR hold buffer or bearish close < zone bottom minus that buffer; otherwise, if no longer touching, it becomes approach or fresh according to the same distance threshold. Hold remains hold. Record the first armed, minimum-age touch as the first retest, and count each armed, minimum-age touch preceded by a non-touch as a separate test.
Calculation: Block scores = reversal power is a bounded, rounded weighted sum of formation quality, a piecewise score for zone height divided by creation ATR, and a capped directional impulse score. Context is reversal power plus a piecewise age adjustment and a first-retest adjustment, minus a progressive, capped test-count penalty applied only in hold state. For each active opposing block that overlaps the zone or lies in the expected reaction direction within the conflict distance, compute a piecewise gap-based penalty plus a penalty if its context is at least the block's context, with a larger penalty if it exceeds context by a specified margin. Conflict is the largest such penalty plus an extra penalty when at least two opponents qualify, capped at a maximum. Structural score is context minus conflict, bounded and capped by a piecewise formation-quality ceiling. Action score is structural score plus a piecewise current-ATR-normalized distance-to-zone adjustment and an approach or in-zone state bonus, bounded and capped by the same quality ceiling. Distance is zero inside the zone and otherwise the distance to its nearest edge.
Calculation: Directional dominance edge = for each active block, multiply its effective score by piecewise lifecycle-state and current-ATR-normalized distance weights, with a separate inside-zone weight. Effective score is structural score for fresh or hold, and bounded structural score plus the distance adjustment and state bonus for approach or in-zone; unlike action score, do not apply the formation-quality ceiling again. Sum weighted scores separately by direction. If their total is positive, edge is bullish share minus bearish share; otherwise edge is zero.
Long: Close crosses above the buffered structure high.
Short: Close crosses below the buffered structure low.
Long: A bullish order block is created.
Short: A bearish order block is created.
Long: An active bullish order block records its first retest.
Short: An active bearish order block records its first retest.
Long: At least one active order block exists AND directional dominance edge >= the neutral-band threshold.
Short: At least one active order block exists AND directional dominance edge <= the negative neutral-band threshold.
