Calculation: Bullish gap on a completed bar = low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > high two bars earlier AND low minus that earlier high >= ATR of the completed bar multiplied by a minimum-width parameter. Its zone top is the low and bottom is that earlier high.
Calculation: Bearish gap on a completed bar = high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier minus high >= ATR of the completed bar multiplied by a minimum-width parameter. Its zone top is that earlier low and bottom is the high.
Calculation: Micro gaps = bullish and bearish gaps detected among completed lower-timeframe bars contained in the current completed chart bar, using a separate minimum-width parameter. Only triples wholly contained in that chart bar are scanned. For each direction, the aggregate zone top is the maximum gap top and its bottom is the minimum gap bottom.
Calculation: Zone ledger = on each chart bar, process qualifying chart, higher-timeframe, and micro zones in that order, bullish before bearish within each source. Optionally reject a new zone if its interval overlaps or touches any existing zone of the same direction. Add accepted zones as newest, then discard the oldest zones exceeding the per-direction capacity. For each remaining zone, first detect inversion if enabled and not previously inverted: bullish when close < bottom, bearish when close > top. Then remove it if its age in chart bars exceeds the lifetime parameter, or according to the selected retirement rule: for a bullish zone, low <= top for touch, low <= (top + bottom)/2 for midpoint, or close <= bottom for body; for a bearish zone, high >= bottom for touch, high >= (top + bottom)/2 for midpoint, or close >= top for body. Also remove an inverted bullish zone when close > top or an inverted bearish zone when close < bottom. Count remaining zones by their original direction, including any inverted zones.
Long: A bullish gap forms on the completed chart bar AND the absolute body of the preceding chart bar >= chart ATR multiplied by an impulse parameter.
Short: A bearish gap forms on the completed chart bar AND the absolute body of the preceding chart bar >= chart ATR multiplied by an impulse parameter.
Long: A new higher-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar forms a bullish gap, using the same ATR length and minimum-width parameter as the chart gap.
Short: A new higher-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar forms a bearish gap, using the same ATR length and minimum-width parameter as the chart gap.
Long: The number of bullish micro gaps in the completed chart bar >= the micro-count threshold.
Short: The number of bearish micro gaps in the completed chart bar >= the micro-count threshold.
Long: Inversion is enabled AND a previously uninverted bearish ledger zone has close > its top on the completed chart bar.
Short: Inversion is enabled AND a previously uninverted bullish ledger zone has close < its bottom on the completed chart bar.
Long: The remaining bullish zone count > the remaining bearish zone count.
Short: The remaining bearish zone count > the remaining bullish zone count.
