Calculation: Anchor band = A trailing outer band at close minus ATR times (base offset × adaptive scale + 3) in a bullish trend, or close plus that distance in a bearish trend. Adaptive scale is the configured minimum during a Bollinger-inside-Keltner squeeze, the configured maximum when ATR's percentile rank over its lookback exceeds its threshold, and 1 otherwise. Each band ratchets only in the trend direction; a bullish trend flips bearish when close < prior anchor band, and a bearish trend flips bullish when close > prior anchor band, resetting the bands on the flip.
Calculation: Trend equilibrium = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low since the current trend began.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA of estimated bullish volume minus EMA of estimated bearish volume; estimate bullish volume as volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half the volume when high = low, and bearish volume as the remainder.
Calculation: High-volume nodes = midpoints of local peaks in a volume profile built from the current trend segment, using equal price bins across its high-low range and each bar's hlc3 to select a bin; weight volume by bullish estimated volume in a bullish trend and bearish estimated volume in a bearish trend. A node confluence holds when close or the anchor band is within 0.35 ATR of any node.
Calculation: Bull confluence score = sum of votes for bullish trend, higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA and higher-timeframe close > fast EMA, ADX >= its threshold, volume delta > 0, close < trend equilibrium, and high-volume-node confluence. Bear score uses bearish trend, higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA, ADX >= its threshold, volume delta < 0, close > trend equilibrium, and the same node confluence. Use bars from a higher timeframe; use its still-forming bar.
Long: bullish trend flip OR bullish trend AND low <= anchor band + 0.35 ATR AND close > anchor band AND bull confluence score >= configured minimum AND session filter passes when enabled AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: bearish trend flip OR bearish trend AND high >= anchor band − 0.35 ATR AND close < anchor band AND bear confluence score >= configured minimum AND session filter passes when enabled AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: long, close < prior anchor band.
Exit: short, close > prior anchor band.
