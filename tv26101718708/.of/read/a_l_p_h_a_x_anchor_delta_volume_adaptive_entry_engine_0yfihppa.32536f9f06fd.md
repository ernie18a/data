Calculation: Source = a selected price derived from the chart bar’s OHLC fields.
Calculation: Adaptive scale = when adaptive scaling is enabled, the lower scale if Bollinger Bands are strictly inside Keltner Channels, otherwise the upper scale if the ATR percentile rank exceeds its threshold, otherwise the neutral scale; when disabled, the neutral scale. Bollinger Bands use a close SMA and standard-deviation offsets; Keltner Channels use a close EMA and ATR offsets.
Calculation: Anchor trend and outer band = initially bullish. The candidate lower and upper bands are source minus and plus ATR multiplied by the adaptive-scaled base multiplier plus an outer-band offset. In a bullish trend, if source is below the previous outer band, switch to bearish and reset the band to the upper candidate; otherwise set it to the greater of the lower candidate and previous band. In a bearish trend, if source is above the previous outer band, switch to bullish and reset the band to the lower candidate; otherwise set it to the lesser of the upper candidate and previous band. If the previous band is unavailable, use the corresponding current candidate.
Calculation: Trend range = since the latest trend flip, the running highest high and lowest low; initialize the range on a flip with the current high and low extended to include the newly reset outer band. Before the first flip, accumulate from the first bar. Equilibrium is the midpoint of this range.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = the shorter-lookback EMA of close is above the longer-lookback EMA AND close is above the shorter EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Bearish bias reverses both comparisons.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA of estimated bullish volume minus EMA of estimated bearish volume, using the same lookback. Bullish volume is volume multiplied by (close minus low) divided by (high minus low), or half of volume when high equals low; bearish volume is volume minus bullish volume.
Calculation: High-volume nodes = divide the current trend range into equal price bins. For each bar since the latest flip, subject to a maximum history lookback, add its volume to the bin containing its typical price, clamped to the end bins. If directional weighting is enabled, use estimated bullish volume in a bullish trend and estimated bearish volume in a bearish trend. Nodes are the midpoints of interior bins whose volume strictly exceeds both neighboring bins; no nodes exist when node detection is disabled.
Calculation: Node proximity = the absolute distance from either close or the outer band to any high-volume node is at most an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = the sum of one point each for the matching anchor trend; the matching higher-timeframe bias or its filter being disabled; ADX at or above its threshold or its filter being disabled; volume delta having the matching sign or its filter being disabled; close being below equilibrium for bullish or above equilibrium for bearish, or the equilibrium filter being disabled; and node proximity.
Long: Previous anchor trend is bearish AND source is above the previous outer band.
Short: Previous anchor trend is bullish AND source is below the previous outer band.
Long: Anchor trend is bullish AND low is at most the outer band plus the ATR-based tolerance AND close is above the outer band AND bullish confluence score is at least its threshold AND the session filter is disabled or the bar is in the configured session AND the number of bars since the most recent entry signal is at least the cooldown or no entry signal has occurred.
Short: Anchor trend is bearish AND high is at least the outer band minus the ATR-based tolerance AND close is below the outer band AND bearish confluence score is at least its threshold AND the session filter is disabled or the bar is in the configured session AND the number of bars since the most recent entry signal is at least the cooldown or no entry signal has occurred.
Long: Anchor trend is bullish AND low is at most the outer band plus the ATR-based tolerance AND close is above the outer band AND node proximity.
Short: Anchor trend is bearish AND high is at least the outer band minus the ATR-based tolerance AND close is below the outer band AND node proximity.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish bias.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish bias.
Long: Volume delta is above zero.
Short: Volume delta is below zero.
Exit: long, previous anchor trend is bullish AND source is below the previous outer band.
Exit: short, previous anchor trend is bearish AND source is above the previous outer band.
