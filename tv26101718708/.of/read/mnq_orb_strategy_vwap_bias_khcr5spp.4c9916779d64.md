Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high from the regular-session open through the configured opening-range end
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low over the same opening-range window
Calculation: Buy level = opening-range high + configured price buffer
Calculation: Sell level = opening-range low − configured price buffer
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the regular-session open
Calculation: Daily ATR = 14-period ATR calculated from higher-timeframe daily bars, using the still-forming daily bar
Long: high > buy level AND this is the first bar to break the buy level today AND the sell level has not been broken today AND no signal has fired today AND the opening-range width is within the configured multiple of daily ATR (or the ATR filter is disabled) AND the bar is within the configured entry window AND close > session VWAP (or the VWAP filter is disabled) AND long-only or both-direction bias is selected
Short: low < sell level AND this is the first bar to break the sell level today AND the buy level has not been broken today AND no signal has fired today AND the opening-range width is within the configured multiple of daily ATR (or the ATR filter is disabled) AND the bar is within the configured entry window AND close < session VWAP (or the VWAP filter is disabled) AND short-only or both-direction bias is selected
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry price + configured profit distance OR low ≤ entry price − configured stop distance OR (VWAP-cross exit is enabled AND prior close ≥ prior session VWAP AND close < session VWAP) OR (trailing-stop mode is enabled AND the trailing stop is activated after price reaches entry price + configured activation distance AND low ≤ the trailing stop, with a hard stop at entry price − configured stop distance)
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry price − configured profit distance OR high ≥ entry price + configured stop distance OR (VWAP-cross exit is enabled AND prior close ≤ prior session VWAP AND close > session VWAP) OR (trailing-stop mode is enabled AND the trailing stop is activated after price reaches entry price − configured activation distance AND high ≥ the trailing stop, with a hard stop at entry price + configured stop distance)
