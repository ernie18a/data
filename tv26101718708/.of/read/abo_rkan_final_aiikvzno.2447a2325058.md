Calculation: Upper Donchian level = highest close over the prior lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Lower Donchian level = lowest close over the prior lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Ichimoku cloud top = greater of Span A and Span B; Span A = average of Tenkan and Kijun; Tenkan and Kijun = averages of the highest high and lowest low over their respective lookbacks; Span B = average of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its moving average
Long: volume > its moving average AND ADX > its minimum AND close > Ichimoku cloud top AND close > Upper Donchian level + ATR × buffer multiplier AND close > Supertrend AND ATR ratio > its threshold
Short: volume > its moving average AND ADX > its minimum AND close < Ichimoku cloud bottom AND close < Lower Donchian level − ATR × buffer multiplier AND close < Supertrend AND ATR ratio > its threshold
