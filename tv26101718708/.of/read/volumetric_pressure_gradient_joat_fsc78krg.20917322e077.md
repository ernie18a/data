Calculation: Rolling profile = Over the chart timeframe’s lookback bars, divide the highest high to lowest low range into 20 equal price bins; assign each bar’s volume to the bin containing its high-low midpoint. The POC is the midpoint of the bin with the greatest volume. Expand the value area from the POC bin toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until it contains the selected percentage of total volume; value area high and low are the outer bin boundaries.
Calculation: Scaled POC and value area boundaries = Map each profile price from the rolling low-high range to 0–100.
Calculation: Pressure oscillator = EMA of (close − rolling low) / (rolling high − rolling low) × 100, clipped to 0–100.
Long: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, pressure oscillator crossed above scaled POC by more than the POC break filter: previous oscillator ≤ previous scaled POC + filter AND current oscillator > current scaled POC + filter; only once until oscillator returns to or below scaled POC.
Short: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, pressure oscillator crossed below scaled POC by more than the POC break filter: previous oscillator ≥ previous scaled POC − filter AND current oscillator < current scaled POC − filter; only once until oscillator returns to or above scaled POC.
Long: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, pressure oscillator crossed above scaled value area high by more than the value area break filter: previous oscillator ≤ previous scaled value area high + filter AND current oscillator > current scaled value area high + filter; only once until oscillator returns to or below scaled value area high.
Short: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, pressure oscillator crossed below scaled value area low by more than the value area break filter: previous oscillator ≥ previous scaled value area low − filter AND current oscillator < current scaled value area low − filter; only once until oscillator returns to or above scaled value area low.
Long: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, pressure oscillator is above scaled POC for the required consecutive number of bars AND was below that consecutive-bar threshold on the previous bar.
Short: On a completed chart-timeframe bar, pressure oscillator is below scaled POC for the required consecutive number of bars AND was below that consecutive-bar threshold on the previous bar.
