Calculation: Regime score = average of capped normalized ADX and inverse normalized Choppiness Index.
Calculation: Regime = classify as volatile when ATR divided by its moving-average baseline exceeds the volatility threshold; otherwise classify as trending when the regime score exceeds the trending threshold, ranging when it is below the ranging threshold, or uncertain otherwise. Smooth by the most frequent classification over a lookback; ties prioritize volatile, then trending, then ranging, then uncertain.
Calculation: Active ATR multiplier = initialize to the current regime’s multiplier, then on each bar move the previous multiplier toward the current regime’s multiplier by adding their difference divided by the transition length.
Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend bands = form basic upper and lower bands around the bar midpoint (high + low)/2, offset by ATR times the active multiplier. Ratchet the upper band to the basic upper band if it is lower than the previous upper band or the previous close was above that band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Ratchet the lower band to the basic lower band if it is higher than the previous lower band or the previous close was below that band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Direction starts up; while up, switch down when close is below the lower band; while down, switch up when close is above the upper band.
Long: Confirmed bar AND previous direction down AND current direction up (close above the ratcheted upper band).
Short: Confirmed bar AND previous direction up AND current direction down (close below the ratcheted lower band).
