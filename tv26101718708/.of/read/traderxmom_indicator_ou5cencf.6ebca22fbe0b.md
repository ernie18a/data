Calculation: Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin Ashi open = (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2; Heikin Ashi high = max(high, Heikin Ashi open, Heikin Ashi close); Heikin Ashi low = min(low, Heikin Ashi open, Heikin Ashi close).
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) * volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting at the start of each exchange trading day.
Long: Heikin Ashi close > Heikin Ashi open AND Heikin Ashi open - Heikin Ashi low <= wick tolerance AND close > shorter-period close EMA AND shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA AND close > session VWAP AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time is within an enabled session in New York time) AND (bar weekday in exchange time is outside the weekday range OR bar opening time in New York time is outside the blocked time interval) AND (no previous signal OR bars since the most recent long or short signal > cooldown).
Short: Heikin Ashi close < Heikin Ashi open AND Heikin Ashi high - Heikin Ashi open <= wick tolerance AND close < shorter-period close EMA AND shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA AND close < session VWAP AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time is within an enabled session in New York time) AND (bar weekday in exchange time is outside the weekday range OR bar opening time in New York time is outside the blocked time interval) AND (no previous signal OR bars since the most recent long or short signal > cooldown).
