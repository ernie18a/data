Calculation: Pivot high/low = a high/low with the same configurable number of bars on each side, confirmed only after the right-side bars complete. Chart and higher-timeframe pivots use the same length.
Calculation: Liquidity low/high = the latest confirmed pivot low/high, using either chart bars or higher-timeframe bars. Higher-timeframe requests use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Structure = on each chart bar, replace each stored high/low with any newly available pivot high/low. If close is above the stored high, set trend up and replace the stored high with close; then if close is below the stored low, set trend down and replace the stored low with close. Trend starts unknown. Apply separately to chart bars and higher-timeframe close and pivots; higher-timeframe requests use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time, with state updates evaluated on chart bars.
Calculation: Gap = bullish when the previous bar's low is above the high two bars ago, or, in body mode, when the lower of the previous open and close is above that high; bearish when the previous high is below the low two bars ago, or, in body mode, when the higher of the previous open and close is below that low. Optionally require the previous bar's high minus low to be at least current ATR multiplied by a displacement parameter. Store the latest bullish gap as previous low and high two bars ago, and the latest bearish gap as previous high and low two bars ago.
Calculation: Inverted gap = once close exceeds the stored bearish gap's previous high, persist the long inverted gap with its two stored bounds; once close falls below the stored bullish gap's high two bars ago, persist the short inverted gap with its two stored bounds. Each persists until replaced by a later inversion of the same direction.
Calculation: Sweep state = a long sweep occurs when low is below liquidity low and close is above it; a short sweep occurs when high is above liquidity high and close is below it. Store the latest sweep bar and wick for each side, clear that side's target on a new sweep, and expire the state when its age exceeds a configurable chart-bar limit. After a sweep, update its target on each subsequently confirmed chart pivot high for a long or pivot low for a short. Clear that side's sweep state and target after its entry.
Calculation: Session midpoint = initialize the high and low to the current bar when either is unavailable; reset them to the current high and low whenever the configured session's bar timestamp increases from the previous bar. Otherwise, while the session timestamp is available, extend the high and low with the current bar. The midpoint is their average.
Long: close > stored chart-structure high before its break update.
Short: close < stored chart-structure low before its break update.
Long: higher-timeframe close > stored higher-timeframe structure high before its break update.
Short: higher-timeframe close < stored higher-timeframe structure low before its break update.
Long: low < liquidity low AND close > liquidity low.
Short: high > liquidity high AND close < liquidity high.
Long: close > latest stored bearish gap's previous high.
Short: close < latest stored bullish gap's high two bars ago.
Long: active long sweep AND long inverted gap exists AND high >= its stored low from two bars before the gap detection AND low <= its stored previous high AND a post-sweep pivot-high target exists AND close > open AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured session) AND (ATR filter disabled OR minimum ATR percentage <= 0 OR ATR / close * 100 >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (chart-structure filter disabled OR chart trend is up) AND (higher-timeframe structure filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is up) AND (midpoint filter disabled OR close < session midpoint).
Short: active short sweep AND short inverted gap exists AND high >= its stored high from two bars before the gap detection AND low <= its stored previous low AND a post-sweep pivot-low target exists AND close < open AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured session) AND (ATR filter disabled OR minimum ATR percentage <= 0 OR ATR / close * 100 >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (chart-structure filter disabled OR chart trend is down) AND (higher-timeframe structure filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend is down) AND (midpoint filter disabled OR close > session midpoint).
Exit: long, low <= entry sweep low minus one minimum price tick OR high >= target saved at the entry signal.
Exit: short, high >= entry sweep high plus one minimum price tick OR low <= target saved at the entry signal.
Exit: long, partial targets enabled AND high >= signal-bar close + (target saved at the entry signal - signal-bar close) * configured path fraction; exit the configured position fraction.
Exit: short, partial targets enabled AND low <= signal-bar close + (target saved at the entry signal - signal-bar close) * configured path fraction; exit the configured position fraction.
