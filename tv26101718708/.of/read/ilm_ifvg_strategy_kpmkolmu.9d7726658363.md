Calculation: Chart pivot high/low = a high/low confirmed after the configured number of chart bars on both sides; higher-timeframe pivots use the same rule on aggregated higher-timeframe bars and update from the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Liquidity levels are the latest pivot low (SSL) and pivot high (BSL), using higher-timeframe pivots when enabled, otherwise chart pivots.
Calculation: Up/down chart structure = latest chart pivot high/low. On each bar, if close exceeds the stored high/low, emit the corresponding up/down structure marking and set trend up/down; replace that threshold with close. A new confirmed pivot replaces its corresponding threshold. Higher-timeframe structure and trend use the same process with the higher-timeframe close and pivots from the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = either the middle candle's body lies wholly above/below the high/low two bars earlier, or its wick lies wholly above/below that high/low, according to the body-gap setting; when displacement is enabled, also require the preceding bar's high minus low to be at least ATR times the displacement multiplier. Bullish gap bounds are the middle candle's low and the earlier high; bearish gap bounds are the middle candle's high and the earlier low. The latest bearish gap becomes a long IFVG when close exceeds its upper bound; the latest bullish gap becomes a short IFVG when close falls below its lower bound. Each IFVG remains active and retains those bounds until replaced by a later invalidated gap.
Calculation: Active long sweep = low below SSL and close above SSL; record that bar and its low, then keep active until entry or until elapsed chart bars exceed the configured maximum. Active short sweep = high above BSL and close below BSL; record that bar and its high, then keep active until entry or timeout. The long target is the latest confirmed chart pivot high occurring after the long sweep; the short target is the latest confirmed chart pivot low occurring after the short sweep.
Calculation: Session midline = midpoint of the running high and low since the start of the configured premium/discount session; reset both at each session start. ATR percentage = chart-timeframe ATR divided by close, times 100.
Long: close > latest chart-structure high (up structure marking).
Short: close < latest chart-structure low (down structure marking).
Long: higher-timeframe close > latest higher-timeframe structure high (up structure marking).
Short: higher-timeframe close < latest higher-timeframe structure low (down structure marking).
Long: session is allowed if the session filter is enabled AND ATR percentage meets the minimum if the range filter is enabled AND chart structure trend is up if the structure filter is enabled AND higher-timeframe structure trend is up if its filter is enabled AND close is below the session midline if the premium/discount filter is enabled AND an active long sweep AND an active long IFVG retest (high at or above its lower bound AND low at or below its upper bound) AND a long target exists AND close > open.
Short: session is allowed if the session filter is enabled AND ATR percentage meets the minimum if the range filter is enabled AND chart structure trend is down if the structure filter is enabled AND higher-timeframe structure trend is down if its filter is enabled AND close is above the session midline if the premium/discount filter is enabled AND an active short sweep AND an active short IFVG retest (high at or above its lower bound AND low at or below its upper bound) AND a short target exists AND close < open.
Exit: long, price <= recorded long sweep low minus one minimum tick OR price >= the configured partial target between entry and long target OR price >= long target.
Exit: short, price >= recorded short sweep high plus one minimum tick OR price <= the configured partial target between entry and short target OR price <= short target.
