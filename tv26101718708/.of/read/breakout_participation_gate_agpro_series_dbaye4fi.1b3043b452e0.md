Calculation: Upper gate = highest high over a structure lookback ending at the previous bar; lower gate = lowest low over the same lookback.
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR, or true range when ATR is unavailable, floored at a positive price increment parameter. Bar range = high minus low, floored at the same increment. Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA, or 1 when the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Body percentage = 100 × absolute(close − open) / bar range. Close position = clamp(100 × (close − low) / bar range, 0, 100).
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(100 × relative volume / minimum relative volume, 0, 150). Body score = clamp(100 × body percentage / minimum body percentage, 0, 140). Range score = clamp(55 × bar range / safe ATR, 0, 120). For an upside breakout, location score = close position and trend score = 100 if close > EMA of close, otherwise 35. For a downside breakout, location score = 100 − close position and trend score = 100 if close < EMA of close, otherwise 35. Breakout quality = clamp(0.32 × volume score + 0.27 × body score + 0.18 × location score + 0.13 × range score + 0.10 × trend score, 0, 100).
Calculation: On every upside or downside breakout, replace the active gate with the corresponding gate, save its direction, breakout quality, bar index and shelf bounds equal to the gate ± breakout-bar safe ATR × shelf-width parameter, and clear retest, fakeout and expansion flags. The active gate is within the retest window when bars since its breakout are > 0 and ≤ the retest-window parameter. Retest quality = clamp(0.50 × saved breakout quality + 0.20 × current volume score + 0.15 × current body score + 0.15 × (100 − min(100 × bars since breakout / retest-window parameter, 100)), 0, 100). Set the retest flag after a qualifying retest. On the first bar within the window where an upside gate has close < active gate − current safe ATR × fakeout-distance parameter, or a downside gate has close > active gate + current safe ATR × fakeout-distance parameter, set the fakeout flag and clear the retest flag. Set the expansion flag after a qualifying expansion. Evaluate these flag updates after that bar's decisions, in retest, fakeout, expansion order.
