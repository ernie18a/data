Calculation: Momentum = EMA of close rate of change over a momentum lookback, optionally multiplied by min(volume / SMA of volume, volume-weight cap); use a multiplier of 1 when the volume SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Confirmed positive peak = a momentum pivot high confirmed after a right-hand lookback, with a separately parameterized left-hand lookback, whose momentum exceeds a positive strength threshold and whose pivot bar is at least a minimum distance after the preceding accepted positive pivot bar. Record its momentum and the pivot bar's high. Confirmed negative trough = the corresponding pivot low below the negative strength threshold, spaced independently from preceding accepted negative troughs; record its momentum and the pivot bar's low. Update these records on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Previous peak or trough = the latest corresponding confirmed record available at the end of the preceding chart bar. For crossover or crossunder, compare momentum and this previous-record series on both the current and preceding bars.
Calculation: Sweep distance = either an absolute distance or a fraction of the absolute value of the latest corresponding confirmed momentum level. A sweep becomes active when momentum first exceeds the positive level plus its distance or falls below the negative level minus its distance, respectively. Store the level at activation; deactivate a positive sweep when a newly confirmed positive peak exceeds its stored level, and deactivate a negative sweep when a newly confirmed negative trough is below its stored level. Apply activation before deactivation on each bar.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, or zero when the sum is zero.
Calculation: Trend EMAs = EMA of chart-timeframe close and an independently parameterized EMA of higher-timeframe close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including a higher-timeframe bar that completes on the current chart bar. Their lengths are independently selectable.
Calculation: Trade state = after a primary signal, record its bar and direction for spacing. Open a tracked trade only if the signal close is above the higher-timeframe EMA for a long or below it for a short; fix its stop at that EMA and its target at entry plus or minus a selectable multiple of the entry-to-stop distance. Evaluate exits starting on the next bar, after evaluating that bar's primary signals; a tracked trade remains live until its stop or target is reached.
Long: A positive momentum peak is confirmed.
Short: A negative momentum trough is confirmed.
Long: Momentum exceeds the latest confirmed positive peak plus its sweep distance AND the positive sweep was inactive before this bar.
Short: Momentum is below the latest confirmed negative trough minus its sweep distance AND the negative sweep was inactive before this bar.
Long: A negative momentum trough is confirmed AND its pivot-bar low is below the preceding confirmed negative trough's pivot-bar low AND its momentum is above that trough's momentum.
Short: A positive momentum peak is confirmed AND its pivot-bar high is above the preceding confirmed positive peak's pivot-bar high AND its momentum is below that peak's momentum.
Long: Momentum > 0 AND momentum crosses above the previous positive peak's momentum AND close > that peak's pivot-bar high AND chart-timeframe EMA > higher-timeframe EMA AND, if enabled, (momentum - previous positive peak momentum) / abs(previous positive peak momentum) >= expansion threshold AND, if enabled, (close - previous positive peak high) / ATR >= displacement threshold AND, if enabled, efficiency ratio >= efficiency threshold AND bars since the last primary signal >= cooldown AND bars since each of the last primary long and short signals >= direction-spacing threshold AND no tracked trade is live.
Short: Momentum < 0 AND momentum crosses below the previous negative trough's momentum AND close < that trough's pivot-bar low AND chart-timeframe EMA < higher-timeframe EMA AND, if enabled, (previous negative trough momentum - momentum) / abs(previous negative trough momentum) >= expansion threshold AND, if enabled, (previous negative trough low - close) / ATR >= displacement threshold AND, if enabled, efficiency ratio >= efficiency threshold AND bars since the last primary signal >= cooldown AND no tracked trade is live AND no primary long signal occurs on this bar.
Exit: long, a tracked trade exists AND the current bar is after its entry bar AND (low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed target).
Exit: short, a tracked trade exists AND the current bar is after its entry bar AND (high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed target).
