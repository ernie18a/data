Calculation: Higher-timeframe high and low = highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending with the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Swing high and swing low = the most recent confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and pivot low, respectively, each using the same number of bars on both sides; update them on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = chart-bar low is below and close is above either the higher-timeframe low or swing low; use the higher-timeframe low as the sweep level if both are swept, otherwise use the swept level.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = chart-bar high is above and close is below either the higher-timeframe high or swing high; use the higher-timeframe high as the sweep level if both are swept, otherwise use the swept level.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = bullish sweep AND ATR > 0 AND volume divided by its moving average >= a relative-volume threshold AND (sweep level - low) / ATR <= a penetration threshold AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= a wick threshold AND the bearish candidate's corresponding raw conditions are not all true. The volume average includes the current bar.
Calculation: Bearish candidate = bearish sweep AND ATR > 0 AND volume divided by its moving average >= the same relative-volume threshold AND (high - sweep level) / ATR <= the same penetration threshold AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= the same wick threshold AND the bullish candidate's corresponding raw conditions are not all true.
Calculation: Bullish absorption displacement = the most recent bullish candidate occurred at least one bar ago and no more than a confirmation-window lookback ago AND close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR multiplied by an absorption displacement threshold AND close > that candidate's high AND close > the highest high over a structure lookback ending on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Bearish absorption displacement = the most recent bearish candidate occurred at least one bar ago and no more than the same confirmation-window lookback ago AND close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR multiplied by the same absorption displacement threshold AND close < that candidate's low AND close < the lowest low over the same structure lookback ending on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish absorption = the preceding chart bar is the most recent bullish absorption displacement AND close > the sweep level of the candidate used by that displacement AND close >= that displacement bar's (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: Confirmed bearish absorption = the preceding chart bar is the most recent bearish absorption displacement AND close < the sweep level of the candidate used by that displacement AND close <= that displacement bar's (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: Narrative = persistent bullish or bearish state. On each chart bar, first set it bullish if the last completed higher-timeframe close > the preceding higher-timeframe bar's high, otherwise set it bearish if that close < the preceding higher-timeframe bar's low. If still uninitialized, set it bullish when chart close >= (higher-timeframe high + higher-timeframe low) / 2, otherwise bearish. Then, using that state before switches, switch bullish if it is bearish AND (close > swing high OR close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR multiplied by a range-displacement threshold AND close > the highest high over a range lookback ending on the preceding chart bar OR confirmed bullish absorption). Switch bearish if it is bullish AND (close < swing low OR close < open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR multiplied by the same range-displacement threshold AND close < the lowest low over the same range lookback ending on the preceding chart bar OR confirmed bearish absorption).
Long: Confirmed bullish absorption
Short: Confirmed bearish absorption
Long: Narrative = bullish AND (swing high > close OR higher-timeframe high is available)
Short: Narrative = bearish AND (swing low < close OR higher-timeframe low is available)
Long: A new higher-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low > the high two higher-timeframe bars before it
Short: A new higher-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high < the low two higher-timeframe bars before it
