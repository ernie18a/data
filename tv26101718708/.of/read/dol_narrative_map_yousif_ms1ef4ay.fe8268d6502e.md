Calculation: Higher-timeframe liquidity bounds = highest high and lowest low over a lookback of completed higher-timeframe bars, excluding the still-forming bar
Calculation: Confirmed chart-timeframe swing high/low = latest pivot high/low requiring equal left and right lookbacks; a pivot is available only after its right-side bars complete
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a lookback including the current bar
Calculation: Bullish sweep level = higher-timeframe low if the current low crosses below it and the current close reclaims it; otherwise the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing low if crossed below and reclaimed; otherwise undefined
Calculation: Bearish sweep level = higher-timeframe high if the current high crosses above it and the current close reclaims it; otherwise the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high if crossed above and reclaimed; otherwise undefined
Calculation: Bullish candidate = completed bar has a defined bullish sweep level, relative volume at least a threshold, (sweep level − low) / ATR no greater than a threshold, and (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) at least a threshold
Calculation: Bearish candidate = completed bar has a defined bearish sweep level, relative volume at least a threshold, (high − sweep level) / ATR no greater than a threshold, and (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick) at least a threshold
Calculation: Valid bullish candidate = bullish candidate and not bearish candidate on the same bar
Calculation: Valid bearish candidate = bearish candidate and not bullish candidate on the same bar
Calculation: Bullish confirmation displacement = within the confirmation window after the latest valid bullish candidate, a completed bullish bar whose body is at least a threshold times ATR, whose close exceeds that candidate bar's high, and whose close exceeds the highest high of the preceding short-term chart-timeframe lookback bars
Calculation: Bearish confirmation displacement = within the confirmation window after the latest valid bearish candidate, a completed bearish bar whose body is at least a threshold times ATR, whose close is below that candidate bar's low, and whose close is below the lowest low of the preceding short-term chart-timeframe lookback bars
Long: the completed bar is exactly one chart-timeframe bar after a bullish confirmation displacement, its close is above the bullish candidate's sweep level, and its close is at or above the displacement bar's open-close midpoint
Short: the completed bar is exactly one chart-timeframe bar after a bearish confirmation displacement, its close is below the bearish candidate's sweep level, and its close is at or below the displacement bar's open-close midpoint
