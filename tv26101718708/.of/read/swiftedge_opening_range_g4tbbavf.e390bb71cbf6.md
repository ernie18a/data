Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled session, the maximum high and minimum low of bars opening from the first in-session bar up to but excluding the configured range duration; range size = maximum high − minimum low; midpoint = (maximum high + minimum low) / 2. Evaluate subsequent decisions independently per session after the range is complete and while range size > 0.
Calculation: Breakout = the first qualifying close in a session above the range high + ATR × buffer or below the range low − ATR × buffer, with volume / SMA(volume) ≥ the configured threshold when the volume filter is enabled. Record its direction, bar and high or low. No later close is classified as another breakout in that session.
Calculation: Fake-out = within the configured number of bars after a breakout, close < range high after an upward breakout or close > range low after a downward breakout. Include the fake-out bar when tracking the highest high after an upward breakout or the lowest low after a downward breakout; cancel any pending retest on a fake-out.
Calculation: Entry price = close plus directional slippage for close entries; for an upward stop entry, max(open, range high + ATR × buffer) plus slippage; for a downward stop entry, min(open, range low − ATR × buffer) minus slippage; for an upward retest, min(open, range high) plus slippage; for a downward retest, max(open, range low) minus slippage.
Calculation: Initial stop = the configured opposite range edge, range midpoint, entry price minus direction × ATR multiple, entry price minus direction × range-size multiple, or entry-bar low minus one price increment for a long and entry-bar high plus one price increment for a short. For a fade, use the tracked breakout extreme plus an ATR buffer for a short or minus an ATR buffer for a long, or entry price minus direction × ATR multiple. Direction is +1 for long and −1 for short; initial risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop. An actual entry requires positive risk and the stop strictly on the loss side of entry.
Calculation: Target = entry price + direction × initial risk multiple, entry price + direction × ATR multiple, range high + range-size multiple for a long or range low − range-size multiple for a short, entry price + direction × points, entry price × (1 + direction × percentage), the opposite range edge, or the range midpoint, as configured. A target is valid only strictly beyond entry in the profitable direction. A fade requires a valid target; a breakout may have no target. A second breakout target is valid only if the first target is valid and the second is strictly farther in the profitable direction.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, optionally moved no worse than entry after a partial first-target fill or after direction × (close − entry price) ≥ configured multiple × initial risk; optionally trail it using close − direction × an ATR or range-size multiple, without loosening the stop, either always or only after the first target. Stop changes apply to subsequent bars.

Short: The first downward breakout of the session.
