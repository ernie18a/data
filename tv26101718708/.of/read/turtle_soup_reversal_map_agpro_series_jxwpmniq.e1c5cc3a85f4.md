Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over the lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Safe ATR = ATR when available and positive; otherwise a positive fallback parameter.
Calculation: Setup state = initially idle. After the warm-up period, when idle, a high above prior high starts a pending short setup; otherwise a low below prior low starts a pending long setup. Save the sweep bar's high or low as the sweep extreme and set the pending stop to that extreme plus or minus a stop-distance multiplier times safe ATR on the sweep bar. Allow only one setup at a time. A pending setup fires on its sweep bar in same-bar mode or on the following bar in next-bar mode if its confirmation condition is met. Confirmation is evaluated before invalidation. An unconfirmed pending short is invalidated when high exceeds its pending stop; an unconfirmed pending long is invalidated when low falls below its pending stop. An unconfirmed pending setup also expires after its confirmation bar. A fired setup returns to idle when its target or stop is reached; an invalidated setup returns to idle. A setup resolved or invalidated on a bar cannot be replaced until the next bar. The warm-up period is the extreme lookback plus the volume-average lookback plus a warm-up offset; detection requires the bar index to exceed this period and both prior extremes to be available.
Calculation: Fired target and stop = fixed at confirmation: for a long, confirmation close plus a target-distance multiplier times safe ATR and sweep extreme minus a stop-distance multiplier times safe ATR; for a short, confirmation close minus the target-distance multiplier times safe ATR and sweep extreme plus the stop-distance multiplier times safe ATR. Use safe ATR on the confirmation bar.
Long: Setup state starts a pending long.
Short: Setup state starts a pending short.
Long: Setup state is pending long AND the bar is its confirmation bar AND close > prior low.
Short: Setup state is pending short AND the bar is its confirmation bar AND close < prior high.
Exit: long, setup state is fired long AND (high >= fired target OR low <= fired stop).
Exit: short, setup state is fired short AND (low <= fired target OR high >= fired stop).
