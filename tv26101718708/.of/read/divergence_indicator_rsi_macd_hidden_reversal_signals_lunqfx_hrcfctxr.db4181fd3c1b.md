Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the specified number of bars before and after it; confirmed after those following bars close. Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is above the highs of the specified number of bars before and after it; confirmed after those following bars close.
Long: At a confirmed pivot low, compared with the previous confirmed pivot low within the maximum allowed bar gap, either the current low is lower and at least the minimum number of enabled engines (RSI, MACD line, OBV) are higher, or hidden divergences are enabled, the current low is higher and at least the minimum number of enabled engines are lower
Short: At a confirmed pivot high, compared with the previous confirmed pivot high within the maximum allowed bar gap, either the current high is higher and at least the minimum number of enabled engines (RSI, MACD line, OBV) are lower, or hidden divergences are enabled, the current high is lower and at least the minimum number of enabled engines are higher
