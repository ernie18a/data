Calculation: Liquidity levels = Confirmed pivot highs and lows of either wick prices or closes, using the same lookback on each side. Add levels after managing existing levels on the confirmation bar. Keep at most the configured number of levels across both sides, discarding the oldest first. A level becomes inactive when swept or closed through; swept levels may either remain in the level count or be removed, while broken levels are removed or remain in the count according to the selected settings.
Calculation: Net volume = Either the sum of signed volume of lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, using completed lower-timeframe bars and the still-forming lower-timeframe bar when present, or the candle proxy. For each lower-timeframe bar, use positive volume if close > open, negative volume if close < open; when equal, use the sign of close versus the preceding lower-timeframe close, retaining the preceding sign if unchanged. The candle proxy is volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or zero when high = low. In automatic mode use lower-timeframe delta when available, otherwise the proxy.
Calculation: Smoothed pressure = EMA of net volume.
Long: Smoothed pressure > 0.
Short: Smoothed pressure <= 0.
Long: Net volume >= 0.
Short: Net volume < 0.
Long: Low < an active pivot-low level AND close >= that level AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume >= a configured multiple of its SMA, including the sweep bar) AND (delta agreement disabled OR net volume > 0) AND (next-bar confirmation disabled OR the next bar's close > the sweep bar's high); evaluate on the sweep bar when confirmation is disabled, otherwise on the next bar.
Short: High > an active pivot-high level AND close <= that level AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume >= a configured multiple of its SMA, including the sweep bar) AND (delta agreement disabled OR net volume < 0) AND (next-bar confirmation disabled OR the next bar's close < the sweep bar's low); evaluate on the sweep bar when confirmation is disabled, otherwise on the next bar.
