Calculation: Reference close and reference ATR = close and ATR of the last completed reference-timeframe bar; the selectable reference timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or equal to it. If it is lower, use the last completed reference bar available within each chart bar. If reference ATR is unavailable, use chart-timeframe ATR; floor ATR at a positive price increment parameter.
Calculation: Pocket edges = reference close plus or minus reference ATR multiplied by a pocket-width parameter.
Calculation: Relative volume = chart-bar volume divided by its simple moving average; use 1 if the average is not positive. Volatility ratio = chart-timeframe ATR divided by its simple moving average over the same lookback as relative volume; use 1 if the average is not positive. Distance = absolute difference between close and reference close divided by reference ATR.
Calculation: Reclaim score = round to the nearest integer after clamping to [0, 100] the sum of 18, close quality, wick quality, volume quality, magnet quality, volatility fit, and confirmation bonus, less distance penalty. Clamp(x, a, b) means max(a, min(x, b)). Let range be high minus low floored at a positive price increment parameter. Above the upper pocket edge, close quality = clamp((close - upper edge) / chart ATR × 50, 0, 24) and wick quality = clamp((close - low) / range × 20, 0, 20). Below the lower edge, close quality = clamp((lower edge - close) / chart ATR × 50, 0, 24) and wick quality = clamp((high - close) / range × 20, 0, 20). Inside the pocket, close quality = 8 and wick quality = clamp(abs(close - open) / range × 12, 0, 12). Volume quality = clamp(relative volume × 14, 0, 18). Magnet quality = clamp((magnet-distance parameter - distance) / max(magnet-distance parameter, 0.01) × 16, 0, 16). Volatility fit = clamp(16 - abs(volatility ratio - 1) × 18, 0, 16). Distance penalty = clamp((distance - magnet-distance parameter) × 12, 0, 42) when distance exceeds the magnet-distance parameter, otherwise 0. Confirmation bonus = 10 when the optional wick-and-close mode is selected and either low <= upper edge AND close > upper edge AND close > open, or high >= lower edge AND close < lower edge AND close < open; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Qualified reclaim = close crosses above the upper pocket edge or below the lower pocket edge AND reclaim score >= minimum score parameter AND relative volume >= minimum relative-volume parameter AND chart bars elapsed since the reference period began <= maximum reference-age parameter. A cross compares both close and the corresponding pocket edge with their values on the preceding chart bar. Track the direction and chart-bar index of the latest qualified reclaim; replace them on each new qualified reclaim and clear them after its invalidation unless a new qualified reclaim occurs on the same bar.
Long: Close > upper pocket edge.
Short: Close < lower pocket edge.
Exit: long, latest qualified reclaim direction = long AND current chart-bar index > its chart-bar index AND close < current lower pocket edge.
Exit: short, latest qualified reclaim direction = short AND current chart-bar index > its chart-bar index AND close > current upper pocket edge.
