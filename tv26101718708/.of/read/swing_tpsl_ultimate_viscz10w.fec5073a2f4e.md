Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the same parameterized number of chart bars on each side, confirmed on the last right-hand bar; a pivot high is greater than or equal to all left-hand highs and greater than all right-hand highs, and a pivot low is less than or equal to all left-hand lows and less than all right-hand lows. Calculate three chart-timeframe pivot lengths, from shorter to longer. Optionally also calculate pivots on the chart-bar series of higher-timeframe highs and lows, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar carried forward across chart bars; the left and right pivot lengths count chart bars.
Calculation: Accepted pivot samples and latest levels = process confirmed pivots in order from shorter to longer chart-timeframe length, then higher-timeframe-derived pivots. Accept a high pivot only when its value is positive and greater than the current close; accept a low pivot only when its value is positive and less than the current close. For each accepted high, append (pivot high - close) / close to a bounded FIFO high-sample buffer and set the latest high level to that pivot. For each accepted low, append (close - pivot low) / close to a separate bounded FIFO low-sample buffer and set the latest low level to that pivot. Process multiple pivots on the same bar separately.
Calculation: Directional average = separately for high and low samples, choose the pivot average as the buffer arithmetic mean, the sample-by-sample EMA initialized with the first accepted sample, the trimmed mean, or the mean of the trimmed mean and the per-bar EMA. The trimmed mean is the arithmetic mean for fewer than four samples; otherwise sort the buffer, remove max(1, round(buffer size × trim fraction)) samples from each end and average the remainder, falling back to the untrimmed mean if none remain. Empty-buffer pivot averages are zero. The high per-bar EMA uses (high - open) / open and the low per-bar EMA uses (open - low) / open. Select the final directional average as the pivot average, the per-bar EMA, or the mean of the trimmed mean and per-bar EMA; the last choice ignores the selected pivot-average method.
Long: A chart-timeframe pivot low is confirmed at any of the three lengths OR an enabled higher-timeframe-derived pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A chart-timeframe pivot high is confirmed at any of the three lengths OR an enabled higher-timeframe-derived pivot high is confirmed.
Long: Close <= latest low level × (1 + low directional average / 2) AND RSI <= oversold threshold AND close > open AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier).
Short: Close >= latest high level × (1 - high directional average / 2) AND RSI >= overbought threshold AND close < open AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier).
