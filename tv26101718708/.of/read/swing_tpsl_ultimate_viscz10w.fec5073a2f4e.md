Calculation: Last confirmed swing high/low = the latest confirmed pivot high/low among three chart-timeframe pivot lookbacks (short, medium, and long) and a pivot on a higher timeframe; each pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks, and the higher-timeframe pivot uses completed bars only.
Calculation: Final average TP% / SL% = the configured average of positive swing-high samples (pivot high minus confirmation close, divided by confirmation close) / swing-low samples (confirmation close minus pivot low, divided by confirmation close). The selected pivot average is simple, EMA-weighted, trimmed, or blended with the per-bar EMA; per-bar TP% / SL% samples are (high − open) / open and (open − low) / open. The selected sample source is pivot samples, per-bar samples, or their blend.
Long: close ≤ last confirmed swing low × (1 + final average SL% / 200) AND RSI ≤ oversold threshold AND close > open AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > 1.2 × 20-bar volume SMA)
Short: close ≥ last confirmed swing high × (1 − final average TP% / 200) AND RSI ≥ overbought threshold AND close < open AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > 1.2 × 20-bar volume SMA)
