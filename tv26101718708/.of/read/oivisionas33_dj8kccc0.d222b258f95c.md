Calculation: Confirmed price pivot high = a bar whose high is greater than the highs of the surrounding bars on both sides.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the surrounding bars on both sides.
Calculation: Open interest at a pivot = the close of the corresponding lower-timeframe bar, using the value aligned to the pivot bar.
Long: At a newly confirmed pivot high, its price is lower than the previous confirmed pivot high AND its open interest is higher than at the previous pivot high.
Short: At a newly confirmed pivot high, its price is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high AND its open interest is lower than at the previous pivot high.
Short: At a newly confirmed pivot high, its price is lower than the previous confirmed pivot high AND its open interest is higher than at the previous pivot high.
Short: At a newly confirmed pivot low, its price is higher than the previous confirmed pivot low AND its open interest is lower than at the previous pivot low.
Short: At a newly confirmed pivot low, its price is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low AND its open interest is higher than at the previous pivot low.
