Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / SMA of volume, including the current bar.
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); range = high - low.
Long: On the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe using its current forming bar, low <= either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND close > that EMA.
Long: On the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe using its current forming bar, low > either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND low <= that EMA * (1 + tap-zone percentage) AND close > that EMA.
Long: On the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe using its current forming bar, low >= either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND low <= that EMA * (1 + pullback-zone percentage) AND close > that EMA AND range > 0 AND (close - low) / range >= minimum close-position fraction AND, if the green-candle filter is enabled, close >= open.
Long: On the chart timeframe, close > open AND previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND body >= previous body * (1 + minimum body-growth fraction) AND, if the proximity filter is enabled, low is within the configured percentage of either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND, if the volume filter is enabled, relative volume >= its minimum.
Long: On the chart timeframe, range > 0 AND body <= range * maximum body fraction AND lower wick >= body * minimum lower-wick ratio AND upper wick <= body * maximum upper-wick ratio AND, if the proximity filter is enabled, low is within the configured percentage of either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND, if the volume filter is enabled, relative volume >= its minimum.
Long: On the chart timeframe, close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND previous body <= body two bars ago * maximum middle-body fraction AND close > open AND close >= close two bars ago + body two bars ago * minimum recovery fraction AND, if the proximity filter is enabled, low is within the configured percentage of either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND, if the volume filter is enabled, relative volume >= its minimum.
Long: On the chart timeframe, previous close < previous open AND open < previous low AND close > open AND close >= previous close + previous body * minimum penetration fraction AND close < previous open AND, if the proximity filter is enabled, low is within the configured percentage of either the shorter-period or longer-period EMA AND, if the volume filter is enabled, relative volume >= its minimum.
