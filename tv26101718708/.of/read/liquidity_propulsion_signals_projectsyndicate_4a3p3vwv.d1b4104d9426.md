Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR, minimum price increment), using high minus low when ATR is unavailable; the minimum price increment is a parameter.
Calculation: Liquidity state = independently for highs and lows, retain the latest confirmed symmetric pivot and whether it has been consumed. A newly confirmed pivot replaces its level and resets its consumed flag, without resetting a pending grab. When no spring is loaded and the level is unconsumed, start a pending low grab on low < low-pivot level or a pending high grab on high > high-pivot level; record the starting bar, the extreme and the preceding coil-window high-low range divided by current safe ATR. On later pending bars update the extreme. A low grab reclaims on close > the current low-pivot level; a high grab reclaims on close < the current high-pivot level. Process reclaim before expiry; consume the level and clear the grab on reclaim or when elapsed bars reach the reclaim-window parameter. A new pivot may replace the level during a pending grab.
Calculation: Charge = 100 times the weighted mean of six components, each clamped to [0, 1]: depth divided by the depth reference; 1 minus elapsed grab bars divided by the reclaim window; rejection wick fraction; (volume surge minus 1) divided by the volume-surge reference minus 1; (coil-looseness reference minus recorded coil range in ATR) divided by the coil-looseness reference minus 1; and reclaim displacement in ATR divided by the displacement reference. Floor the last three divisors and the sum of weights at small positive parameters. Depth is the distance between the current pivot level and the pending extreme divided by safe ATR on the reclaim bar. Rejection wick fraction is (min(open, close) - low) / bar range for a low grab and (high - max(open, close)) / bar range for a high grab, with bar range floored at the minimum price increment. Volume surge is current volume divided by its current simple moving average, substituting current volume when the average is unavailable and flooring the denominator at 1. Reclaim displacement is (close - pivot level) / safe ATR for a low grab and (pivot level - close) / safe ATR for a high grab. Use safe ATR as the preceding coil range if that range is unavailable.
Calculation: Loaded spring = on a qualifying reclaim, store the reclaim-bar high as the long ignition level or low as the short ignition level, the grab extreme as invalidation, the charge and the load bar. On each bar, first disarm a long spring if close < its invalidation or a short spring if close > its invalidation; otherwise fire and disarm it if close crosses beyond its ignition level; otherwise disarm it when elapsed bars since load reach the ignition-window parameter. Evaluate this sequence on the load bar too. The two sides operate independently.
Long: A low grab reclaims AND depth >= minimum depth in ATR AND charge >= arm threshold AND (strong-only filter is disabled OR charge >= strong threshold) AND the long spring was not loaded on the previous bar.
Short: A high grab reclaims AND depth >= minimum depth in ATR AND charge >= arm threshold AND (strong-only filter is disabled OR charge >= strong threshold) AND the short spring was not loaded on the previous bar.
Long: A long spring is loaded AND close >= its invalidation AND close > its ignition level.
Short: A short spring is loaded AND close <= its invalidation AND close < its ignition level.
Calculation: Trade levels = on a discharge with no active trade, available ATR, and no trade closed on that bar, enter at close; if both directions fire, use long. Freeze risk distance as entry-bar safe ATR times the risk multiplier. The stop is entry minus risk for long or entry plus risk for short; the final target is entry plus risk times the final-target R multiplier for long or entry minus that amount for short. Evaluate exits starting on the next bar.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= final target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= final target.
