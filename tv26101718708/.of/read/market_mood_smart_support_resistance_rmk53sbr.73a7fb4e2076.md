Calculation: Normalized swings = Confirm pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of preceding and succeeding chart bars. Keep alternating pivots; replace consecutive pivots of the same type only when the new pivot is more extreme. If a bar is both a pivot high and low, select the type opposite the latest swing, or the high if no swing exists.
Calculation: Structure candidate = On a normalized-swing change, examine the latest four swings A, B, C, D. A bullish candidate has types high, low, high, low, A > C, D < B, and (C - B) / (A - B) and (C - D) / (C - B) within their respective configured inclusive retracement and extension bounds. A bearish candidate has types low, high, low, high, A < C, D > B, and (B - C) / (B - A) and (D - C) / (B - C) within those bounds. Denominators must be positive. A valid candidate replaces the previous candidate of either direction; an invalid swing change leaves the current candidate unchanged. Activate it on the confirmation bar of the swing change; expire it when the age of D exceeds the maximum pattern age, and consume it on its first breakout even if no zone is found.
Calculation: Bullish breakout = An active bullish candidate AND the selected source, either close or high, > A + current ATR × breakout buffer AND either the previous bar's selected source <= that same current threshold OR the current bar is the candidate's activation bar.
Calculation: Bearish breakout = An active bearish candidate AND the selected source, either close or low, < A - current ATR × breakout buffer AND either the previous bar's selected source >= that same current threshold OR the current bar is the candidate's activation bar. Use the same close-or-wick selection for both directions.
Calculation: Required impulse = The simple moving average of high minus low on a higher timeframe, evaluated on its last completed bar, divided by that bar's close and multiplied by an impulse factor. Express impulse moves and this threshold in the same units.
Calculation: Demand zone = On a bullish breakout, scan origin bars from the minimum streak length through the lesser of the maximum scan length and the greater of the minimum streak length and D's age. An eligible origin has close < open and is immediately followed by at least the minimum number of consecutive bars with close > open, ending at the first non-bullish bar or the breakout bar. Its impulse is (last bullish close - first bullish low) / first bullish low. Require a positive required impulse and impulse >= required impulse. Select the eligible origin with the greatest impulse, breaking ties in favor of the most recent origin; zone boundaries are its low and high. Store the invalidation level as its low minus creation-bar ATR × invalidation buffer.
Calculation: Supply zone = On a bearish breakout, use the same scan bounds. An eligible origin has close > open and is immediately followed by at least the minimum number of consecutive bars with close < open, ending at the first non-bearish bar or the breakout bar. Its impulse is (first bearish high - last bearish close) / first bearish high. Require a positive required impulse and impulse >= required impulse. Select the eligible origin with the greatest impulse, breaking ties in favor of the most recent origin; zone boundaries are its low and high. Store the invalidation level as its high plus creation-bar ATR × invalidation buffer.
Calculation: Zone lifecycle = Keep at most the configured maximum number of zones, deleting the oldest on creation of another. A demand zone becomes inactive when close < its stored invalidation level; a supply zone becomes inactive when close > its stored invalidation level. Test invalidation before retest on each bar. A zone's first retest occurs only after its creation bar, while active and not previously retested, when high >= zone low AND low <= zone high.
Long: Bullish breakout.
Long: First retest of an active demand zone.
Short: Bearish breakout.
Short: First retest of an active supply zone.
