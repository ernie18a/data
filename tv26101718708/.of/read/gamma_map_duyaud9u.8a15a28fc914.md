Calculation: Signal series = close and volume sampled on each chart bar from either of two enabled signal timeframes, one shorter than the other; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar in the chart bar. Historical offsets and moving averages of these series count chart bars.
Calculation: Below-zone visit = at least one signal-series close in a configurable number of preceding chart bars is below the configured chop low.
Calculation: Above-zone visit = at least one signal-series close in the same preceding lookback is above the configured chop high.
Calculation: Volume expansion = current signal-series volume is greater than a configurable multiple of its simple moving average, including the current chart bar.
Calculation: Volume exhaustion = previous signal-series volume is greater than a configurable multiple of its simple moving average at the previous chart bar AND current signal-series volume is less than a configurable fraction of previous signal-series volume.
Long: Configured long trigger > 0 AND shorter-timeframe signal-series close on the previous chart bar < long trigger AND current shorter-timeframe signal-series close >= long trigger.
Short: Configured short trigger > 0 AND shorter-timeframe signal-series close on the previous chart bar > short trigger AND current shorter-timeframe signal-series close <= short trigger.
Long: Configured chop low > 0 AND configured chop high > 0 AND an enabled signal-series previous close < chop low AND its current close >= chop low AND either the volume-exhaustion filter is disabled or volume exhaustion holds for that series.
Long: Configured chop low > 0 AND configured chop high > 0 AND an enabled signal-series previous close < chop high AND its current close > chop high AND below-zone visit holds for that series AND either the volume-expansion filter is disabled or volume expansion holds for that series.
Short: Configured chop low > 0 AND configured chop high > 0 AND an enabled signal-series previous close > chop high AND its current close <= chop high AND above-zone visit holds for that series AND either the volume-expansion filter is disabled or volume expansion holds for that series AND no long reclaim or continuation condition holds on either signal series.
Short: Configured chop low > 0 AND configured chop high > 0 AND an enabled signal-series previous close > chop low AND its current close < chop low AND above-zone visit holds for that series AND either the volume-expansion filter is disabled or volume expansion holds for that series AND no long reclaim or continuation condition holds on either signal series.
Long: Configured chop low > 0 AND configured chop high > 0 AND an enabled signal-series current close >= chop low AND current close <= chop high AND current close > previous close AND below-zone visit holds for that series AND either the volume-expansion filter is disabled or volume expansion holds for that series AND no earlier long preliminary signal has fired since the latest chart-bar close outside the chop zone.
Short: Configured chop low > 0 AND configured chop high > 0 AND an enabled signal-series current close >= chop low AND current close <= chop high AND current close < previous close AND above-zone visit holds for that series AND either the volume-expansion filter is disabled or volume expansion holds for that series AND no earlier short preliminary signal has fired since the latest chart-bar close outside the chop zone.
