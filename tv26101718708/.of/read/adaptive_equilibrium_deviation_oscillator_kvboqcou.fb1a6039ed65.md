Calculation: Equilibrium = linear-regression endpoint or EMA of a selected OHLC-derived price source.
Calculation: Oscillator = clamp((price source − equilibrium) / ATR, −deviation limit, deviation limit), using zero when ATR is zero; optionally standardize the clamped series using either its rolling mean and standard deviation or 0.6745 × (current value − rolling median) / rolling median of the time series of absolute differences between each value and its contemporaneous rolling median. Return zero when the chosen standardizing denominator is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR / SMA of ATR, or 1 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Upper band = mode-specific base band × volatility ratio clamped between minimum and maximum multipliers; lower band = −upper band.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = an oscillator high or low surrounded by the configured numbers of lower highs or higher lows on its left and right; evaluate it when the right-hand bars have completed, using the price high or low at the pivot bar. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding oscillator pivot of the same type, even if that preceding pivot did not produce a divergence.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed low pivot within the allowed pivot-spacing range where either oscillator exceeds the preceding low pivot by more than the minimum gap AND price low is lower, or oscillator is below the preceding low pivot by more than the minimum gap AND price low is higher. Bearish divergence = a confirmed high pivot within the allowed pivot-spacing range where either oscillator is below the preceding high pivot by more than the minimum gap AND price high is higher, or oscillator exceeds the preceding high pivot by more than the minimum gap AND price high is lower. Each type may be enabled independently.
Calculation: Recent directional divergence = a same-direction divergence whose pivot bar is within the configured recent-divergence window of the current bar and whose pivot bar is at least the cooldown distance after the preceding same-direction divergence that passed this cooldown. Update the stored pivot bar only when a divergence passes the cooldown.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA, otherwise bearish. Higher-timeframe agreement = the count of matching bullish or bearish biases across three progressively higher timeframes, using each timeframe's last completed bar and a common EMA length.
Calculation: Directional confluence = one point each for matching higher-timeframe bias, ADX below its trend threshold, recent same-direction divergence, and volatility ratio at or below 1.
Long: Oscillator < 0.
Short: Oscillator >= 0.
Long: Enabled regular bullish divergence.
Long: Enabled hidden bullish divergence AND its pivot bar is at least the cooldown distance after the preceding bullish divergence that passed the cooldown.
Short: Enabled regular bearish divergence.
Short: Enabled hidden bearish divergence AND its pivot bar is at least the cooldown distance after the preceding bearish divergence that passed the cooldown.
Long: Oscillator crosses above the lower band AND optional clearance above the lower band is satisfied AND optional higher-timeframe bullish bias is satisfied AND optional ADX trend gate is satisfied AND optional session-open skip is satisfied AND bar index is at least the maximum of equilibrium length plus one and either ATR length in raw mode or standardization window in standardized mode AND directional confluence is at least its minimum AND optional higher-timeframe agreement is at least its minimum.
Short: Oscillator crosses below the upper band AND optional clearance below the upper band is satisfied AND optional higher-timeframe bearish bias is satisfied AND optional ADX trend gate is satisfied AND optional session-open skip is satisfied AND bar index is at least the maximum of equilibrium length plus one and either ATR length in raw mode or standardization window in standardized mode AND directional confluence is at least its minimum AND optional higher-timeframe agreement is at least its minimum.
