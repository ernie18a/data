Calculation: Auction Pressure = (buy volume − sell volume), normalized by the rolling maximum absolute delta to −100…+100; use native footprint delta if available, otherwise sum lower-timeframe sub-bar estimates, otherwise use the chart bar estimate. For each OHLCV bar, buy volume = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × volume; sell volume = (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × volume. Lower-timeframe bars are lower than the chart timeframe; use their bars within the confirmed chart bar.
Calculation: Candle Body Ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Robust ATR = smoothed true range after capping each true range at a multiple of its rolling median, subject to a minimum percentage-of-price floor.
Calculation: Confluence Score and Hit Count = for each active reference level, proximity is max(0, 1 − abs(close − level) / (close × tolerance)); Confluence Score is the weighted average of these proximities, and Hit Count counts levels with positive proximity. Levels: current-session high/low; prior completed day and week high/low; nearest round-number level; and footprint POC/VAH/VAL when available, otherwise session-derived proxies (midpoint, upper reference, lower reference). Prior day/week levels use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Initial Balance Regime = compare the range of the first configured number of session bars with the median range of prior completed sessions' initial balances; classify as Trend when at least the upper ratio threshold, Balance when at most the lower ratio threshold, otherwise Neutral. Trend raises and Balance lowers the composite-score threshold; require the current initial balance to be complete and sufficient prior samples.
Calculation: Fingerprint Score = capped sum of pressure conviction (absolute Auction Pressure scaled to its bounded range), candle structure (body ratio and bar range / Robust ATR), and volume-pressure balance magnitude. Composite Score = 0.60 × Fingerprint Score + 0.40 × Confluence Score.
Calculation: Higher-Timeframe EMA Slope = EMA on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, rising when the last completed higher-timeframe EMA is greater than the preceding completed higher-timeframe EMA, falling when less.
Calculation: Sizing Unit = close × a volatility-ranked percentage band; rank current Robust ATR against its shorter rolling range, and derive the band's minimum and maximum from longer-history percentiles of Robust ATR as a percentage of price (or use configured bounds).
Calculation: Long Extension = entry price + max(reaction multiplier + minimum extension gap, base extension multiplier × regime scalar × clamped current-to-baseline Robust ATR ratio) × Sizing Unit. Short Extension is entry price minus the same distance. Long Invalidation = entry price − invalidation multiplier × Sizing Unit; short Invalidation = entry price + that distance.
Long: Auction Pressure ≥ minimum pressure AND close > open AND Candle Body Ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND Hit Count ≥ minimum confluence hits AND Composite Score ≥ regime-adjusted threshold AND higher-timeframe EMA slope is rising (when enabled) AND initial balance is ready AND no active scenario AND cooldown is inactive AND (if next-bar confirmation is enabled, the next bar closes above the signal bar's close).
Short: Auction Pressure ≤ −minimum pressure AND close < open AND Candle Body Ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND Hit Count ≥ minimum confluence hits AND Composite Score ≥ regime-adjusted threshold AND higher-timeframe EMA slope is falling (when enabled) AND initial balance is ready AND no active scenario AND cooldown is inactive AND (if next-bar confirmation is enabled, the next bar closes below the signal bar's close).
Exit: long, high ≥ Long Extension OR low ≤ Long Invalidation; if both are touched on one bar, resolve to the level closer to the prior bar's close, with ties assigned to Extension.
Exit: short, low ≤ Short Extension OR high ≥ Short Invalidation; if both are touched on one bar, resolve to the level closer to the prior bar's close, with ties assigned to Extension.
