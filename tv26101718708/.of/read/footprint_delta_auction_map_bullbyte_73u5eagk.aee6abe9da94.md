Calculation: Range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); body ratio = abs(close - open) / range.
Calculation: Robust ATR = Wilder's moving average of true range capped at a multiple of its rolling median, floored at a percentage of close.
Calculation: Pressure volumes = when lower-timeframe reconstruction is enabled and completed lower-timeframe bars exist within the completed chart bar, sum (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) * volume and (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) * volume across those bars as buy and sell volumes; otherwise calculate them from the chart bar. Raw pressure = buy volume - sell volume. Normalize each method separately by dividing raw pressure by its rolling maximum absolute raw pressure, using zero for bars when that method was inactive; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Fingerprint score = min(1, pressure weight * abs(normalized pressure) + body weight * min(1, body ratio / body scale) + range weight * min(1, range / robust ATR / range scale) + balance weight * balance). For lower-timeframe reconstruction, balance = abs(buy volume - sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume), or a neutral balance when the sum is zero; for the chart-bar proxy, balance = abs((close - low) / range - midpoint) * balance scale. The pressure weight exceeds the body, range, and balance weights; their sum equals one.
Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low since the session's first chart bar, including the current bar. Reference levels = session midpoint, session high multiplied by an upper-reference factor slightly below one, session low multiplied by a lower-reference factor slightly above one, session high, session low, optionally the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, optionally the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe weekly bar, and optionally close rounded to the nearest round-number interval. If the chart timeframe is daily or higher, the daily and weekly references are the previous completed bars of those respective periods.
Calculation: Confluence = for each available enabled reference level, proximity = max(0, 1 - abs(close - level) / (close * tolerance percentage)); hits = count of levels with proximity > 0; ladder score = sum(weight * proximity) / sum(weights). The session midpoint weight exceeds each adjusted session-reference weight; daily and weekly weights exceed the midpoint weight; each adjusted session-reference weight equals each session-high/low weight; the round-number weight is lowest.
Calculation: Initial balance = high minus low of the first configured number of chart bars in the session. Compare it with the median initial balance of a rolling sample of prior completed sessions, excluding the current session. A ratio at or above the upper threshold defines a trend regime; a ratio at or below the lower threshold defines a balance regime; otherwise, or if the historical median is not positive, the regime is neutral. The score threshold is increased in the trend regime and decreased in the balance regime. The current initial balance must be complete and the required minimum number of prior session samples must exist.
Calculation: Composite score = pressure-and-candle score weight * fingerprint score + location score weight * ladder score; the former weight is larger and the weights sum to one.
Calculation: Sizing unit = close times a percentage interpolated between a minimum and maximum band using the current robust ATR's position between its rolling lowest and highest values; use the midpoint if they are equal. The band is either manually specified or derived from lower and upper nearest-rank percentiles of robust ATR as a percentage of close over a longer lookback, with a minimum separation between the band limits; use the manual band until both percentiles are available.
Calculation: Extension multiplier = the greater of the reaction multiplier plus a positive margin and the base extension multiplier, optionally multiplied by a regime factor and a volatility factor. The regime factor is above one for a trend regime, below one for a balance regime, and one for a neutral regime. The volatility factor is robust ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average, bounded by lower and upper limits, or one when that average is not positive. At entry, freeze the sizing unit and extension multiplier; the extension is entry price plus the extension multiplier times the sizing unit for a long and minus it for a short. Invalidation is entry price minus an invalidation multiplier times the sizing unit for a long and plus it for a short.
Long: On a completed chart bar, normalized pressure > 0 AND close > open AND abs(normalized pressure) >= minimum pressure AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND confluence hits >= minimum hits AND composite score >= regime-adjusted threshold AND initial balance is ready AND sizing unit is available AND position is flat AND cooldown count = 0 AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > EMA on the preceding higher-timeframe bar) AND (next-bar confirmation disabled OR the same conditions held on the immediately preceding bar and current close > preceding close). When next-bar confirmation is enabled, the preceding bar must have been flat and outside cooldown; the current bar requires only flat position, expired cooldown, and the close comparison, not renewed qualification.
Short: On a completed chart bar, normalized pressure < 0 AND close < open AND abs(normalized pressure) >= minimum pressure AND body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND confluence hits >= minimum hits AND composite score >= regime-adjusted threshold AND initial balance is ready AND sizing unit is available AND position is flat AND cooldown count = 0 AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < EMA on the preceding higher-timeframe bar) AND (next-bar confirmation disabled OR the same conditions held on the immediately preceding bar and current close < preceding close). When next-bar confirmation is enabled, the preceding bar must have been flat and outside cooldown; the current bar requires only flat position, expired cooldown, and the close comparison, not renewed qualification.
Exit: Long, on a completed chart bar after entry, high >= frozen extension OR low <= frozen invalidation; start the configured cooldown on this bar and decrement it at the start of each subsequent completed bar.
Exit: Short, on a completed chart bar after entry, low <= frozen extension OR high >= frozen invalidation; start the configured cooldown on this bar and decrement it at the start of each subsequent completed bar.
