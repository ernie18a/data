Calculation: Resistance and support = the high or low of the most recent confirmed pivot within a lookback on the requested timeframe. A pivot high is strictly greater than the highs of the specified numbers of older and newer bars; a pivot low is strictly less than their lows. Search from the newest eligible pivot through the lookback, including the newer confirmation bars. Calculate levels separately on a longer and a shorter timeframe. With lookahead off, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last intrabar in each chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish cloud = close is above each of four close EMAs, comprising a shorter-term pair and a longer-term pair.
Calculation: Bearish cloud = close is below each of those four EMAs.
Calculation: Bullish points = the sum of one point each for bullish cloud, close above the previous completed longer-timeframe period's high, and close crossing above either resistance level.
Calculation: Bearish points = the sum of one point each for bearish cloud, close below the previous completed longer-timeframe period's low, and close crossing below either support level.
