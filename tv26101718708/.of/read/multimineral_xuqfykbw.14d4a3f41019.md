Calculation: LongSetup = (bullish engulfing (close>open AND previous close<previous open AND close>previous open AND open<previous close) OR (close > highest high of previous 2 bars AND volume > SMA(volume)) OR low > high of 2 bars ago OR (high > highest high of previous N bars, N longer than 2, AND volume > m*SMA(volume), m>1)) AND close of the higher-timeframe bar containing the current bar (data up to the current bar) > HMA(close) on that same higher timeframe [HMA(x,n)=WMA(2*WMA(x,n/2)-WMA(x,n), round(sqrt(n)))] AND ADX > threshold AND +DI > -DI AND close > EMA(close) AND RSI < overbought level AND abs(EMASlope) > EMA(close)*slope fraction [EMASlope=(EMA(close) - EMA(close) k bars ago)/k] AND (high-low) > f*ATR (f<1)
Calculation: ShortSetup = (bearish engulfing (close<open AND previous close>previous open AND close<previous open AND open>previous close) OR (close < lowest low of previous 2 bars AND volume > SMA(volume)) OR high < low of 2 bars ago OR (low < lowest low of previous N bars, N longer than 2, AND volume > m*SMA(volume), m>1)) AND close of the higher-timeframe bar containing the current bar (data up to the current bar) < HMA(close) on that same higher timeframe AND ADX > threshold AND -DI > +DI AND close < EMA(close) AND RSI > oversold level AND abs(EMASlope) > EMA(close)*slope fraction AND (high-low) > f*ATR (f<1)
Long: LongSetup AND position is flat or short (a short is exited on the same bar)
Long: LongSetup AND position is long (add-on entry) AND bars since the last entry (initial or add-on) >= cooldown bars AND on some bar after the last entry, high >= last entry close*(1+p) OR low <= last entry close*(1-q), with q < p
Short: ShortSetup AND position is flat or long (a long is exited on the same bar)
Short: ShortSetup AND position is short (add-on entry) AND bars since the last entry (initial or add-on) >= cooldown bars AND on some bar after the last entry, low <= last entry close*(1-p) OR high >= last entry close*(1+q), with q < p
Exit: long, ShortSetup OR low < initial entry bar low*(1-buffer) OR bars since initial entry >= max bars
Exit: short, LongSetup OR high > initial entry bar high*(1+buffer) OR bars since initial entry >= max bars
