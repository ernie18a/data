Calculation: For weekly and daily aggregated bars, Lead A = average of the midpoints of the highest high and lowest low over the shorter and intermediate lookbacks; Lead B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the longest lookback. The same lookbacks are used for both; shorter < intermediate < longest. Use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Weekly EMA pair = exponential moving averages of weekly close; the second has a longer lookback than the first. Use the weekly bar still forming.
Long: Weekly Lead A > Weekly Lead B OR Daily Lead A > Daily Lead B
Long: Close > both weekly EMAs
Short: Weekly Lead A < Weekly Lead B OR Daily Lead A < Daily Lead B
