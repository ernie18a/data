Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: High volume = volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier. Volume ratio = volume / volume SMA when volume SMA > 0, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA, using the same EMA length as the chart EMA; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time. The filter may be disabled.
Calculation: Bull trend = close > chart EMA AND chart EMA > chart EMA at the slope lookback AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA if that filter is enabled AND datetime is in the selected session if that filter is enabled AND abs(close - chart EMA) <= ATR × maximum EMA distance. Bear trend reverses the directional comparisons.
Calculation: Trending = ADX > threshold AND abs(chart EMA - chart EMA at the slope lookback) >= ATR × minimum slope.
Calculation: Cooldown = no previous entry OR bars since the most recent entry > cooldown length.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars ago; its bottom is that earlier high, its top is current low, and its size is their difference. Bearish gap = high < low two bars ago; its bottom is current high, its top is that earlier low, and its size is their difference. Retain the latest qualifying gap of each direction, replacing any previous one, until its age exceeds the gap lifetime or its corresponding gap setup occurs. A qualifying gap has size >= ATR × minimum gap size AND the corresponding trend AND trending.
Calculation: Gap score = weighted sum of the corresponding trend, higher-timeframe direction or disabled higher-timeframe filter, trending, gap touch, directional candle, and high volume, plus a weighted capped ratio of the current bar's corresponding new-gap size to ATR. Use zero for the current gap size when there is no new gap. Each true condition contributes its own weight, otherwise zero; compare the sum with the common setup threshold.
Calculation: Exhaustion trigger = the high of the latest bullish exhaustion bar or the low of the latest bearish exhaustion bar. A bullish exhaustion bar has close < chart EMA AND high volume AND RSI < oversold threshold AND close > open AND lower wick > body × exhaustion-wick multiplier. A bearish exhaustion bar has close > chart EMA AND high volume AND RSI > overbought threshold AND close < open AND upper wick > body × exhaustion-wick multiplier. Replace an existing trigger with each newer exhaustion bar; retain it until its age exceeds the exhaustion lifetime or its corresponding exhaustion setup occurs.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = weighted sum of close on the favorable side of the chart EMA, high volume, close beyond the exhaustion trigger, and a directional candle, plus a capped volume-ratio contribution. Add one of two weights according to whether RSI is beyond a more extreme oversold or overbought threshold, and one of two weights according to whether the corresponding wick > body. Compare the sum with the common setup threshold.
Calculation: Swing high or low = the latest confirmed pivot high or low, using equal left and right lookbacks; confirm it only after the right-hand bars have elapsed. A bullish break occurs when close > the latest swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND bull trend AND trending. A bearish break occurs when close < the latest swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND bear trend AND trending. Retain the broken level and break bar, replacing them on a newer same-direction break, until the break age exceeds the retest lifetime or its corresponding retest setup occurs.
Calculation: Retest score = weighted sum of the corresponding trend, trending, higher-timeframe direction or disabled higher-timeframe filter, price touching the retained level, close beyond that level, directional candle, and high volume. Each true condition contributes its own weight, otherwise zero; compare the sum with the common setup threshold.
Calculation: Pending order = when flat and no order is pending, save the first setup in this priority order: bullish gap, bullish exhaustion, bullish retest, bearish gap, bearish exhaustion, bearish retest. Save its close as the reference price and its bar as the start; expire it when its age > pending lifetime. For a bullish gap set the stop to min(setup low, gap bottom) - setup ATR × gap stop multiplier; for bullish exhaustion use setup low - setup ATR × exhaustion stop multiplier; for bullish retest use min(setup low, retained level) - setup ATR × retest stop multiplier. For bearish setups use the corresponding max of setup high and gap top or retained level, or setup high for exhaustion, plus the applicable ATR offset. Set the target to reference price plus the setup-specific reward-to-risk multiple of reference price minus stop for long, or reference price minus that multiple of stop minus reference price for short. Keep the saved prices fixed until execution or expiry.
Long: On a confirmed bar, cooldown AND an enabled retained bullish gap exists AND gap age > 0 AND low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom AND close > open AND close > gap bottom AND bull trend AND bullish gap score >= setup threshold.
Short: On a confirmed bar, cooldown AND an enabled retained bearish gap exists AND gap age > 0 AND high >= gap bottom AND low <= gap top AND close < open AND close < gap top AND bear trend AND bearish gap score >= setup threshold.
Long: On a confirmed bar, cooldown AND an enabled bullish exhaustion trigger exists AND trigger age > 0 AND close > trigger AND close > open AND bullish exhaustion score >= setup threshold.
Short: On a confirmed bar, cooldown AND an enabled bearish exhaustion trigger exists AND trigger age > 0 AND close < trigger AND close < open AND bearish exhaustion score >= setup threshold.
Long: On a confirmed bar, cooldown AND an enabled retained bullish break exists AND break age > 0 AND low <= retained level AND close > retained level AND close > open AND bullish retest score >= setup threshold.
Short: On a confirmed bar, cooldown AND an enabled retained bearish break exists AND break age > 0 AND high >= retained level AND close < retained level AND close < open AND bearish retest score >= setup threshold.
Long: On a confirmed bar, position is flat AND a bullish pending order exists AND its age <= pending lifetime AND abs(close - reference price) <= ATR × maximum entry distance AND close >= chart EMA × long validity factor AND reference price > saved stop.
Short: On a confirmed bar, position is flat AND a bearish pending order exists AND its age <= pending lifetime AND abs(close - reference price) <= ATR × maximum entry distance AND close <= chart EMA × short validity factor AND reference price < saved stop.
Exit: long, low <= saved stop OR high >= saved target.
Exit: short, high >= saved stop OR low <= saved target.
