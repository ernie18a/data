Calculation: Ribbon averages = shorter-period and longer-period moving averages of close, both using the same selected type: EMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or ZLEMA; the Boy Plunger preset uses HMA and the Livermore preset uses EMA.
Calculation: Structural levels = recent confirmed pivot highs and lows on the higher timeframe, using equal left and right pivot lengths. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in realtime. The nearest level is the available structural level with the smallest absolute distance from the current close; ties favor the most recent pivot high, then the most recent pivot low, alternating by age.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > SMA(volume) × spike multiplier.
Calculation: Pending pullback = when enabled and no breakout is pending, record the direction and bar of the first bar where close is within the watching distance of the nearest structural level, volume spikes, and close is above the level with close >= open or below the level with close < open. Expire it when its age exceeds the pullback window. A bullish pullback is reached when close <= shorter-period ribbon average + ATR × pullback buffer AND close >= longer-period ribbon average - ATR × pullback buffer; a bearish pullback is reached when close >= shorter-period ribbon average - ATR × pullback buffer AND close <= longer-period ribbon average + ATR × pullback buffer. Clear the pending breakout immediately when its corresponding pullback is reached; either direction of reached pullback passes the entry filter on that bar.
Calculation: Active fair value gaps = bullish gaps created when low > high two bars ago, the difference exceeds ATR multiplied by a gap-size parameter, and the previous close > that earlier high; bearish gaps use high < low two bars ago, the difference exceeds the threshold, and the previous close < that earlier low. Their initial bounds are the two nonoverlapping prices. On each bar, shrink a bullish gap's upper bound to the lowest overlapping low and a bearish gap's lower bound to the highest overlapping high; remove a gap when an overlapping bar reaches its opposite bound. Limit the combined gap list by removing the oldest when a new gap is added at capacity. The entry gate uses the previous chart bar's updated active gaps and previous close inside their remaining bounds.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry, select entry price minus ATR × stop multiplier for a long or plus it for a short, the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over a swing lookback including the entry bar, or the wider of those two stops. Risk is the absolute difference between entry price and initial stop. Targets are entry price plus risk × target multiple for a long and minus it for a short; the target multiples are ordered from first to third.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop unless trailing is enabled; initialize the trailing stop to the initial stop. Starting on the bar after the first target was reached, raise the long stop to at least entry or lower the short stop to at most entry. Starting on the bar after the second target was reached, instead raise the long stop to at least the shorter-period ribbon average minus ATR × buffer or lower the short stop to at most the shorter-period ribbon average plus ATR × buffer. Never move the trailing stop backward.

Short: Shorter-period ribbon average < longer-period ribbon average.
Long: Shorter-period ribbon average crosses above longer-period ribbon average.
Short: Shorter-period ribbon average crosses below longer-period ribbon average.
Long: Volume spike AND body ratio >= conviction threshold AND close >= open.
Short: Volume spike AND body ratio >= conviction threshold AND close < open.
Long: Close > a displayed higher-timeframe pivot-high level AND abs(close - that level) <= ATR × watching-distance multiplier AND volume spike AND body ratio >= conviction threshold.
Short: Close < a displayed higher-timeframe pivot-low level AND abs(close - that level) <= ATR × watching-distance multiplier AND volume spike AND body ratio >= conviction threshold.
Long: Close > nearest structural level AND close >= open AND, when the trend gate is required, shorter-period ribbon average > longer-period ribbon average AND, when the level gate is required, abs(close - nearest structural level) <= ATR × watching-distance multiplier AND, when the volume gate is required, volume spike AND, when the body gate is required, body ratio >= conviction threshold AND, when the fair-value-gap gate is required, the previous close was inside a previous-bar active bullish gap AND, when cooldown is enabled, bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown length or no signal has occurred AND, when maximum distance is enabled, abs(close - shorter-period ribbon average) <= ATR × distance multiplier AND, when pullback is enabled, either pending pullback was reached on this bar. The Livermore preset requires level, volume and body; Boy Plunger requires trend, level, volume and body; Ribbon requires trend; Sensitive requires level and volume; Balanced requires trend, level and volume; Fair Value Gap Entry requires trend, level, volume and the gap gate; Custom selects the first four gates independently.
Short: Close < nearest structural level AND close < open AND, when the trend gate is required, shorter-period ribbon average < longer-period ribbon average AND, when the level gate is required, abs(close - nearest structural level) <= ATR × watching-distance multiplier AND, when the volume gate is required, volume spike AND, when the body gate is required, body ratio >= conviction threshold AND, when the fair-value-gap gate is required, the previous close was inside a previous-bar active bearish gap AND, when cooldown is enabled, bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown length or no signal has occurred AND, when maximum distance is enabled, abs(close - shorter-period ribbon average) <= ATR × distance multiplier AND, when pullback is enabled, either pending pullback was reached on this bar. Gate requirements are the same as for the corresponding long preset.

Long: A price pivot low and an RSI pivot low are confirmed at the same bar AND the preceding jointly confirmed low pivots are within the maximum pivot separation AND the newer price low < the preceding price low AND the newer RSI low > the preceding RSI low. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Short: A price pivot high and an RSI pivot high are confirmed at the same bar AND the preceding jointly confirmed high pivots are within the maximum pivot separation AND the newer price high > the preceding price high AND the newer RSI high < the preceding RSI high. Evaluate on the confirmation bar.
Exit: long, the most recent long or short signal was long AND the current bar is within the failed-breakout window after that signal AND close < the structural level saved at that signal AND this failure has not already been recorded.
Exit: short, the most recent long or short signal was short AND the current bar is within the failed-breakout window after that signal AND close > the structural level saved at that signal AND this failure has not already been recorded.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND high >= any not-yet-reached target OR bar is after entry AND low <= active stop; evaluate the third target before the stop when both are reached on the same bar.
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND low <= any not-yet-reached target OR bar is after entry AND high >= active stop; evaluate the third target before the stop when both are reached on the same bar.
