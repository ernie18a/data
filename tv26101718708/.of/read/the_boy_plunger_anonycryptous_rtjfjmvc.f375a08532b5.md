Calculation: Fast and slow ribbon averages = selected moving-average type of close, using shorter and longer lookbacks respectively.
Calculation: Nearest higher-timeframe pivot = closest in absolute price distance to close among the four latest confirmed pivot highs and four latest confirmed pivot lows, using higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > moving average of volume over a configurable lookback × configurable multiplier.
Calculation: Body conviction = abs(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ configurable minimum.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = active, unmitigated gap where current low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish fair-value gap = active, unmitigated gap where current high is below the low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe reversal warning = at least two of three configured higher timeframes have either a bearish/bullish EMA crossover or RSI crossing back below 70/above 30 or above 30/below 70, respectively; use the last completed bars of those higher timeframes.
Long: close ≥ open AND close > nearest higher-timeframe pivot AND configured preset gates pass (Livermore: level proximity, volume spike, body conviction; Boy Plunger: bullish ribbon, level proximity, volume spike, body conviction; Ribbon: bullish ribbon; Sensitive: level proximity, volume spike; Balanced: bullish ribbon, level proximity, volume spike; FVG Entry: Balanced gates and close inside an active bullish fair-value gap; Custom: enabled gates) AND cooldown passes AND close is within configured ATR distance of the fast ribbon average AND configured pullback gate passes OR fast ribbon average crosses above slow ribbon average OR bullish RSI divergence (price makes a lower pivot low while RSI makes a higher pivot low within configured bar limit) OR bullish higher-timeframe reversal warning.
Short: close < open AND close < nearest higher-timeframe pivot AND configured preset gates pass (Livermore: level proximity, volume spike, body conviction; Boy Plunger: bearish ribbon, level proximity, volume spike, body conviction; Ribbon: bearish ribbon; Sensitive: level proximity, volume spike; Balanced: bearish ribbon, level proximity, volume spike; FVG Entry: Balanced gates and close inside an active bearish fair-value gap; Custom: enabled gates) AND cooldown passes AND close is within configured ATR distance of the fast ribbon average AND configured pullback gate passes OR fast ribbon average crosses below slow ribbon average OR bearish RSI divergence (price makes a higher pivot high while RSI makes a lower pivot high within configured bar limit) OR bearish higher-timeframe reversal warning.
Exit: Long, close < the entry signal's pivot level within the configured failed-breakout window OR low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ third profit target.
Exit: Short, close > the entry signal's pivot level within the configured failed-breakout window OR high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ third profit target.
