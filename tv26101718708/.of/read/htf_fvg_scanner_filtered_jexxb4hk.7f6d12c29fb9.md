Calculation: Bullish FVG = on a higher timeframe, the current bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; its gap meets the minimum absolute size, timeframe-specific floor, and ATR-ratio bounds, its age is within the maximum, and no intervening close is below its lower edge.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = on a higher timeframe, the current bar’s high is below the low two bars earlier; its gap meets the minimum absolute size, timeframe-specific floor, and ATR-ratio bounds, its age is within the maximum, and no intervening close is above its upper edge.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = bullish when close > fast EMA > slow EMA; bearish when close < fast EMA < slow EMA. The fast EMA has the shorter lookback. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: FVG score = timeframe weight + state weight (active or mitigated) + trend-alignment weight + distance-to-midpoint/ATR weight + gap/ATR weight.
Calculation: Primary score = FVG score + continuation-trend bonus + distance-to-midpoint bonus or penalty + mitigated-state bonus.
Calculation: Bullish reaction = bullish momentum (close > open and close > prior high) OR bullish rejection (inside primary FVG, lower wick >= body × wick ratio, and close > open).
Calculation: Bearish reaction = bearish momentum (close < open and close < prior low) OR bearish rejection (inside primary FVG, upper wick >= body × wick ratio, and close < open).
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the prior lower-timeframe lookback low and close above it. Bearish sweep = high above the prior lower-timeframe lookback high and close below it. Evaluate on a confirmed lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish micro FVG = on the lower timeframe, low > high two bars earlier; bearish micro FVG = high < low two bars earlier. The gap must meet the minimum gap/ATR ratio. Evaluate on a confirmed lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Micro-FVG retest = after a valid micro FVG forms, a later confirmed lower-timeframe bar has low <= its midpoint and high >= its midpoint.
Long: A valid bullish higher-timeframe FVG has score >= the display threshold OR bullish H1/H4 FVGs overlap OR the selected primary is bullish, price is inside it, its higher-timeframe trend is bullish, bullish reaction occurs, price is in the primary’s lower half, the optional sweep/BOS filter passes (bullish sweep OR close above the prior lookback high), the optional BOS confirmation passes (last completed confirmation-timeframe close above the prior lookback high), and the primary is not invalidated OR the lower-timeframe trigger completes: primary zone touched, bullish sweep, then a confirmed close above the frozen pre-sweep lookback high, then a valid bullish micro FVG within the post-BOS window, then a later retest of its midpoint; the setup has not timed out or been invalidated.
Short: A valid bearish higher-timeframe FVG has score >= the display threshold OR bearish H1/H4 FVGs overlap OR the selected primary is bearish, price is inside it, its higher-timeframe trend is bearish, bearish reaction occurs, price is in the primary’s upper half, the optional sweep/BOS filter passes (bearish sweep OR close below the prior lookback low), the optional BOS confirmation passes (last completed confirmation-timeframe close below the prior lookback low), and the primary is not invalidated OR the lower-timeframe trigger completes: primary zone touched, bearish sweep, then a confirmed close below the frozen pre-sweep lookback low, then a valid bearish micro FVG within the post-BOS window, then a later retest of its midpoint; the setup has not timed out or been invalidated.
Exit: long, close < primary FVG lower edge.
Exit: short, close > primary FVG upper edge.
