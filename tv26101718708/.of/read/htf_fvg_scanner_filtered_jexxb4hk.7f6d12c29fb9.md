Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVGs = On each of three successively higher timeframes, use the last completed bar and scan prior completed bars from most recent to oldest within the lookback and maximum age. A bullish FVG has third-bar low > first-bar high, with bounds [first-bar high, third-bar low]; a bearish FVG has third-bar high < first-bar low, with bounds [third-bar high, first-bar low]. Keep the most recent FVG per direction whose width meets the absolute and timeframe-specific minimums and whose width divided by current higher-timeframe ATR is within the timeframe-specific minimum and maximum. Reject a bullish FVG if any close from its middle bar through the current higher-timeframe bar is below its bottom; reject a bearish FVG if any such close is above its top. It is touched if any of those bars overlaps its bounds; otherwise it is untouched.
Calculation: FVG score = Add timeframe weight, untouched-or-touched weight, matching-trend weight, distance weight, and width weight. Distance is the absolute difference between higher-timeframe close and FVG midpoint divided by higher-timeframe ATR; width is FVG width divided by that ATR. Distance weight uses descending thresholds with larger weights for smaller distances; width weight uses ascending thresholds with larger weights for larger widths. Matching bullish trend is higher-timeframe close > shorter EMA > longer EMA; matching bearish trend is higher-timeframe close < shorter EMA < longer EMA. The score uses separate weights for each of the three timeframes and for each distance and width threshold.
Calculation: Cluster = The intersection of the same-direction FVGs from the two lower of the three higher timeframes, when their bounds overlap. Treat it as untouched, use the middle higher timeframe's ATR and trend, measure its midpoint distance from chart close, and add a cluster bonus to its FVG score.
Calculation: Primary = For each available FVG and enabled cluster, add to its FVG score a matching-trend bonus, a chart-price distance bonus selected from descending absolute-distance thresholds, and a touched bonus; add a further bonus for a cluster. Select the highest-scoring candidate at or above the primary threshold, breaking ties in order: lowest higher timeframe bullish, lowest higher timeframe bearish, middle bullish, middle bearish, highest bullish, highest bearish, bullish cluster, bearish cluster. If primary locking is enabled, freeze the selected candidate and its trend when chart close is inside it or, if midpoint locking is enabled, chart low is at or below a bullish midpoint or chart high is at or above a bearish midpoint. Release the lock when chart close breaks the far boundary or the chart-bar lock timeout is reached; otherwise use the current candidate.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe trigger = On the last completed lower-timeframe bar, maintain a state initialized to waiting. Reset to waiting if the primary is absent, lower-timeframe close breaks its far boundary, or the number of lower-timeframe bars since arming exceeds the setup timeout. A bar overlapping the primary arms the setup. While armed, a bullish sweep is low below the lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback and close above that low; a bearish sweep is high above the highest high of the preceding sweep lookback and close below that high. Freeze the highest high or lowest low, respectively, of the preceding BOS lookback on the sweep bar. A close above the frozen high for bullish or below the frozen low for bearish confirms BOS; sweep and BOS may occur on the same bar. On a later bar within the micro-FVG window, a bullish low > high two bars earlier or bearish high < low two bars earlier, with gap width divided by lower-timeframe ATR at or above the minimum, freezes that gap's midpoint. If the window expires first, return to armed while retaining the original setup timeout. On a later bar whose low <= midpoint AND high >= midpoint, set the state to triggered and retain it until the primary disappears or is invalidated.
