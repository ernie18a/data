Calculation: Selected moving average = a configurable SMA, EMA, DEMA, TEMA, WMA, VWMA, SMMA, Hull MA, linear regression MA, ALMA, triangular MA or Ehlers SuperSmoother; use the same type and parameters for the paired open series.
Calculation: Paired open series = selected moving averages of open and previous-bar open. Optionally calculate both on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar; otherwise calculate them on the chart timeframe. Update their stored values only when the offset chart-bar index modulo the aggregation multiplier equals zero or the next offset chart-bar index modulo the multiplier equals zero; otherwise retain the previous stored values.
Calculation: Directional movement spread = chart-timeframe EMA of the difference between higher- or lower-timeframe positive DI and negative DI, using the last completed bar of the selected timeframe.
Long: stored current-open average > stored previous-open average.
Short: stored current-open average <= stored previous-open average.
Long: stored current-open average crosses above stored previous-open average a configurable number of chart bars ago AND (the MA filter is disabled OR chart close >= selected moving average of close on a higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar) AND (the RSI filter is disabled OR RSI on a higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, >= lower RSI threshold AND RSI <= upper RSI threshold) AND (the ATR filter is disabled OR chart-timeframe ATR >= minimum ATR) AND (the first ADX filter is disabled OR ADX on its selected higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, >= its minimum AND ADX <= its maximum) AND (the first uptrend filter is disabled OR its directional movement spread > zero) AND (the second ADX filter is disabled OR ADX on its independently selected higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, >= its minimum AND ADX <= its maximum) AND (the second uptrend filter is disabled OR its directional movement spread > zero).
Exit: long, chart-timeframe selected moving average of open < entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR entry price <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR entry price >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction).
Calculation: Profit-order target = close of the long-signal bar × (1 + take-profit fraction). Submit the profit exit order when a long position exists and close > entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction), or when a long exit condition is true. If trailing is enabled, activate the submitted order when high reaches the target and trail the highest price reached since activation by target × trailing-deviation fraction; otherwise use the target as a limit price.
Exit: long, a submitted non-trailing profit order exists AND high >= profit-order target.
Exit: long, a submitted trailing profit order has activated AND price <= highest price since activation − profit-order target × trailing-deviation fraction.
