Calculation: open and close series = the selected moving average of open prices, using the current bar’s open for the close series and the prior bar’s open for the open series; calculate on a higher timeframe and use its last completed bar
Calculation: ADX uptrend = a 2-bar EMA of the difference between +DI and −DI
Long: the higher-timeframe close series crosses above the higher-timeframe open series at the configured cross offset AND (ATR is at least its configured minimum OR the ATR filter is disabled) AND (RSI on its configured timeframe is between its configured lower and upper bounds OR the RSI filter is disabled) AND (chart close is at least the selected moving average of close on its configured timeframe OR that filter is disabled) AND (ADX on its configured timeframe is within its configured minimum and maximum OR that ADX filter is disabled) AND (ADX uptrend is positive OR the uptrend filter is disabled) AND (the second ADX on its configured timeframe is within its configured minimum and maximum OR that ADX filter is disabled) AND (the second ADX uptrend is positive OR its uptrend filter is disabled)
Exit: long, the selected moving average of open is below entry price times (1 − configured stop-loss fraction) OR chart close is above entry price times (1 + configured take-profit fraction), activating the configured trailing exit (exit on a retracement by its configured deviation; if trailing is disabled, exit at the take-profit price)
