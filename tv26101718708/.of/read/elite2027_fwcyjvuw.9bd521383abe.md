Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the configured number of bars before and after it; available after the bars after it complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the configured number of bars before and after it; available after the bars after it complete.
Calculation: Rejection fraction = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) for a low sweep; (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick) for a high sweep.
Long: low < an unswept tracked confirmed swing low AND (close > that level if close-back confirmation is enabled) AND rejection fraction for the low sweep ≥ configured minimum AND that level − low ≥ configured minimum sweep depth × ATR(14) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend EMA, using the configured trend timeframe or chart timeframe; for a higher timeframe use its last completed bar) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > configured multiple × volume SMA) AND bars since the last signal ≥ configured cooldown AND (repeat blocking disabled OR last signal is not long); suppress both directions if qualifying long and short signals occur on the same bar.
Short: high > an unswept tracked confirmed swing high AND (close < that level if close-back confirmation is enabled) AND rejection fraction for the high sweep ≥ configured minimum AND high − that level ≥ configured minimum sweep depth × ATR(14) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend EMA, using the configured trend timeframe or chart timeframe; for a higher timeframe use its last completed bar) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > configured multiple × volume SMA) AND bars since the last signal ≥ configured cooldown AND (repeat blocking disabled OR last signal is not short); suppress both directions if qualifying long and short signals occur on the same bar.
