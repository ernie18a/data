Calculation: SwingHighLevels = prices of confirmed pivot highs (a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the N bars on each side, known only after N later bars have closed, N is a swing-strength lookback); only the most recent few are kept, and a level is deleted on the first bar where high > level AND close < level, whether or not the other Short filters pass
Calculation: SwingLowLevels = prices of confirmed pivot lows (a bar whose low is below the lows of the N bars on each side, known only after N later bars have closed); only the most recent few are kept, and a level is deleted on the first bar where low < level AND close > level, whether or not the other Long filters pass
Calculation: TrendEMA = EMA of close, on a timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, taken from the bar of that timeframe containing the current bar (still forming)
Calculation: ATR = ATR(14) on chart timeframe
Calculation: BarsSinceSignal = number of bars since the most recent Long or Short signal on either side
Long: some level L in SwingLowLevels has low < L AND close > L AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= rejection fraction AND (L - low) >= depth multiple * ATR AND close > TrendEMA AND BarsSinceSignal >= cooldown bars AND (optional) volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiple AND (optional) the previous signal was not Long AND the Short condition is not also true on this bar
Short: some level L in SwingHighLevels has high > L AND close < L AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= rejection fraction AND (high - L) >= depth multiple * ATR AND close < TrendEMA AND BarsSinceSignal >= cooldown bars AND (optional) volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiple AND (optional) the previous signal was not Short AND the Long condition is not also true on this bar
