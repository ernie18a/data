Calculation: Liquidity zones = At confirmation of a pivot high or low with equal lookback and lookforward lengths, create a resistance zone from the pivot high to the higher of its open and close, or a support zone from the pivot low to the lower of its open and close. If its height is below a minimum fraction of ATR at the pivot, extend the body-side boundary to that minimum. Optionally filter overlaps against unswept zones of the same type: inspect oldest first; remove each overlapping zone if the new resistance high is higher or the new support low is lower, otherwise discard the new zone and stop. Keep at most a configurable number of zones per type, removing the oldest when exceeded. A zone is eligible until removed or swept, and can signal only once. Process newly confirmed zones on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Zone delta = Divide each zone into four equal price quadrants. Starting on its confirmation bar and through its first sweep bar, add to each quadrant the bar delta multiplied by the positive price overlap of the bar and quadrant divided by the bar's high-low range. Bar delta is volume times (close - open) divided by high - low; use zero if the range is zero and use zero for missing volume. Update deltas before evaluating a signal. Outer delta is the highest quadrant's accumulated delta for resistance and the lowest quadrant's accumulated delta for support. Total absolute delta is the sum of the absolute accumulated quadrant deltas. Outer ratio is the absolute outer delta divided by total absolute delta plus a small positive constant; bar ratio is the absolute bar delta divided by volume plus that constant.
Long: An eligible support zone has total absolute delta > 0 AND (low < zone bottom OR the bar has positive overlap with the zone) AND ((low < zone bottom AND outer delta > 0 AND outer ratio > an absorption threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND outer ratio < an exhaustion threshold) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer ratio > a divergence threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND bar ratio > a rejection threshold)).
Short: An eligible resistance zone has total absolute delta > 0 AND (high > zone top OR the bar has positive overlap with the zone) AND ((high > zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer ratio > an absorption threshold) OR (high > zone top AND outer ratio < an exhaustion threshold) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND outer delta > 0 AND outer ratio > a divergence threshold) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND bar ratio > a rejection threshold)).
