Calculation: KAMA = Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average of close: efficiency ratio is absolute change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes over that lookback; square the interpolation between fast and slow EMA smoothing constants (2/(length+1)) using that ratio, then update KAMA as alpha × close + (1 − alpha) × previous KAMA. For each pair, use the faster and slower KAMA settings; calculate one pair on chart-timeframe bars and another on higher-timeframe bars, using only the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: chart-timeframe fast KAMA crosses above chart-timeframe slow KAMA OR chart-timeframe KAMA rises OR higher-timeframe KAMA rises OR chart-timeframe fast KAMA is below chart-timeframe slow KAMA OR higher-timeframe fast KAMA is below higher-timeframe slow KAMA OR chart-timeframe fast KAMA crosses above chart-timeframe slow KAMA AND higher-timeframe slow KAMA rises
Short: chart-timeframe fast KAMA crosses below chart-timeframe slow KAMA OR chart-timeframe KAMA falls OR higher-timeframe KAMA falls OR chart-timeframe fast KAMA is at or above chart-timeframe slow KAMA OR higher-timeframe fast KAMA is at or above higher-timeframe slow KAMA OR chart-timeframe fast KAMA crosses below chart-timeframe slow KAMA AND higher-timeframe slow KAMA falls
