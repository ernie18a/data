Calculation: Bullish zone = on the current chart bar, create a zone from the high three bars ago to the low one bar ago when that low is greater than that high, OR from the higher of open and close two bars ago to the lower of open and close one bar ago when the latter is greater; require the gap to be at least a parameter times chart-timeframe ATR unless ATR is unavailable. Mark the zone mitigated when a subsequent bar overlaps it, including the creation bar, and remove it when its creation bar is older than the lookback.
Calculation: Bearish zone = on the current chart bar, create a zone from the high one bar ago to the low three bars ago when that high is less than that low, OR from the higher of open and close one bar ago to the lower of open and close two bars ago when the former is less; require the gap to be at least a parameter times chart-timeframe ATR unless ATR is unavailable. Mark the zone mitigated when a subsequent bar overlaps it, including the creation bar, and remove it when its creation bar is older than the lookback.
Calculation: Structure direction = independently on each of four ordered structure timeframes, maintain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, using equal left and right pivot lookbacks. Replace a stored pivot only when the new pivot differs from it by at least a parameter times that timeframe's ATR, or when no pivot is stored; use zero as the threshold if ATR is unavailable. When close exceeds the stored high and direction is not bullish, set direction bullish and clear that high; when close falls below the stored low and direction is not bearish, set direction bearish and clear that low. Evaluate the high break before the low break. A change of character occurs on a direction change from bearish to bullish or bullish to bearish and lasts for that structure bar. Each structure timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe: use its last completed bar for historical higher-timeframe values, including the bar that completes with the chart bar, and its last available intrabar for lower-timeframe values.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish structure counts = the respective counts of bullish and bearish structure directions across all four structure timeframes. Bullish or bearish change of character = the corresponding change of character on either of the two longer structure timeframes.
Calculation: Range levels = the high and low of the last completed longer range period and the last completed shorter range period, each aggregated from chart bars; use the still-forming period's high and low until the corresponding completed period exists. The range ATR uses the longer range timeframe's last completed bar, including the bar that completes with the chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish shift = bullish change of character AND (a completed shorter range period swept the longer range low within a chart-bar lookback OR price is near the longer range low OR close is near the shorter range low). A sweep requires the completed shorter-period low below the longer-period low and its close at or above that low; record only the first sweep while the longer-period low remains active, and reset at the next longer period. Near the longer-period low means absolute distance from close to that low is at most a parameter times range ATR OR low is at or below that level and close is at least that level minus the same tolerance. Near the shorter-period low means absolute distance from close to that low is at most a separate parameter times the longer-level tolerance.
Calculation: Bearish shift = bearish change of character AND (a completed shorter range period swept the longer range high within the same chart-bar lookback OR price is near the longer range high OR close is near the shorter range high). A sweep requires the completed shorter-period high above the longer-period high and its close at or below that high; record only the first sweep while the longer-period high remains active, and reset at the next longer period. Near the longer-period high means absolute distance from close to that high is at most the same parameter times range ATR OR high is at or above that level and close is at most that level plus the same tolerance. Near the shorter-period high means absolute distance from close to that high is at most the same shorter-level parameter times the longer-level tolerance.
Calculation: Selected structure direction = select the first applicable condition in this order: bearish shift and a retained mitigated zone midpoint above close within a parameter times range ATR; bullish shift and a retained mitigated zone midpoint below close within the same distance; bearish shift; bullish shift; bearish change of character; bullish change of character; bearish structure count at least a count threshold; bullish structure count at least the same threshold; bearish count positive and greater than bullish count; bullish count positive and greater than bearish count. Assign the direction named by the selected condition; otherwise direction is neutral.
Long: bullish zone is created.
Short: bearish zone is created.
Long: range levels are available AND range ATR is positive AND selected structure direction is bullish.
Short: range levels are available AND range ATR is positive AND selected structure direction is bearish.
