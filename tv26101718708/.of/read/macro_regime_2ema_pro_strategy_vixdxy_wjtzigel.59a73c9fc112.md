Calculation: Macro moving average = selected SMA or EMA of each enabled macro series’ close; Main and Confirm use their respective selected timeframes and the last completed bar of each.
Calculation: Macro slope = (moving average − its value at the shorter slope lookback) / its value at that lookback.
Calculation: Macro distance = (close − moving average) / moving average.
Calculation: Macro timeframe state = Risk-on when distance is below the negative neutral band and slope is below the negative slope band; Risk-off when distance is above the neutral band and slope is above the slope band; otherwise Neutral.
Calculation: Per-series regime = in Main + Confirm mode, use Main state and mark Warning when Main and Confirm oppose; in Main Only or Confirm Only mode, use that timeframe’s state; in Strict Main + Confirm mode, use Risk-on or Risk-off only when both states agree, and mark Warning when they oppose.
Calculation: Macro vote = count enabled series in Risk-on, Risk-off, or Warning; majority threshold is the ceiling of half the enabled series count. Warning is true when any series is Warning or Risk-on and Risk-off votes are tied.
Calculation: EMA filters = short-lookback EMA and longer-lookback EMA of chart-timeframe close.
Long: bar is confirmed AND (condition just turned true OR every-bar entry mode) AND (position is flat OR reversals are allowed and position is short) AND long trading is enabled AND close > both EMAs AND Risk-on votes ≥ majority threshold AND Risk-on votes > Risk-off votes AND (warning trades are allowed OR Warning is false).
Short: bar is confirmed AND (condition just turned true OR every-bar entry mode) AND (position is flat OR reversals are allowed and position is long) AND short trading is enabled AND close < both EMAs AND Risk-off votes ≥ majority threshold AND Risk-off votes > Risk-on votes AND (warning trades are allowed OR Warning is false).
Calculation: Long stop = the highest of entry price × (1 − initial stop percentage), the optional break-even stop after its favorable-price trigger, and the optional trailing stop after its activation; trailing stop = highest high since entry × (1 − callback percentage).
Calculation: Short stop = the lowest of entry price × (1 + initial stop percentage), the optional break-even stop after its favorable-price trigger, and the optional trailing stop after its activation; trailing stop = lowest low since entry × (1 + callback percentage).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR optional partial take-profit target is reached (high ≥ entry price + initial risk × target R) OR optional opposite-regime exit is enabled and the short regime gate is true on a confirmed bar OR optional EMA-loss exit is enabled and close is no longer above both EMAs on a confirmed bar OR optional time exit is enabled and elapsed bars since entry ≥ time limit on a confirmed bar.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR optional partial take-profit target is reached (low ≤ entry price − initial risk × target R) OR optional opposite-regime exit is enabled and the long regime gate is true on a confirmed bar OR optional EMA-loss exit is enabled and close is no longer below both EMAs on a confirmed bar OR optional time exit is enabled and elapsed bars since entry ≥ time limit on a confirmed bar.
