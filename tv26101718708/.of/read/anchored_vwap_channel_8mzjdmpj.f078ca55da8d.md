Calculation: Anchored VWAP = mean price from the selected rolling or swing anchor to the current bar, weighted by volume (use equal weights when volume is missing or nonpositive).
Calculation: Anchored standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted mean of squared price minus the squared Anchored VWAP, over the same anchored window and with the same weights.
Calculation: VWAP slope = current Anchored VWAP compared with its value at a longer lookback.
Calculation: σ-distance = (close − Anchored VWAP) / Anchored standard deviation.
Long: close crosses above Anchored VWAP OR close crosses above Anchored VWAP and VWAP slope is up (for Confirmed signal mode) OR low ≤ Anchored VWAP − outer-band multiplier × Anchored standard deviation AND close > Anchored VWAP − outer-band multiplier × Anchored standard deviation OR a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND its σ-distance is higher than at the preceding pivot low OR VWAP slope is up AND low > Anchored VWAP − outer-band multiplier × Anchored standard deviation
Short: close crosses below Anchored VWAP OR close crosses below Anchored VWAP and VWAP slope is down (for Confirmed signal mode) OR high ≥ Anchored VWAP + outer-band multiplier × Anchored standard deviation AND close < Anchored VWAP + outer-band multiplier × Anchored standard deviation OR a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND its σ-distance is lower than at the preceding pivot high OR VWAP slope is down AND high < Anchored VWAP + outer-band multiplier × Anchored standard deviation
