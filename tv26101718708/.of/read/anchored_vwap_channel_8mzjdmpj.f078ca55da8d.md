Calculation: Anchored mean and deviation = Choose a price source derived from OHLC. Weight each bar by volume, substituting a weight of one when volume is missing or nonpositive. Use either a rolling window, the window since the latest confirmed swing low, the window since the latest confirmed swing high, or the window since the more recent of those pivots. A pivot has equal lookbacks on both sides and is available only after confirmation; include the pivot and all subsequent bars. If the selected pivot is unavailable, use the rolling window. The mean is the weighted average of the source; the deviation is the square root of max(weighted average of source squared minus mean squared, zero).
Calculation: Standardized distance = (close minus anchored mean) divided by anchored deviation when deviation is positive; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Upper and lower outer bands = anchored mean plus or minus an outer-band multiplier times anchored deviation.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = on confirmation of a swing low with equal left and right lookbacks, its low is below the previous confirmed swing low AND its standardized distance at the pivot is above the standardized distance at the previous pivot.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = on confirmation of a swing high with equal left and right lookbacks, its high is above the previous confirmed swing high AND its standardized distance at the pivot is below the standardized distance at the previous pivot. The divergence pivot lookback may differ from the anchor pivot lookback.
Calculation: Session filter = disabled OR chart timeframe is not intraday OR bars since the latest calendar-day change, counting the first bar as one, exceed the skip length.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = on each higher timeframe, using its last completed bar and the same rolling length, source, weights and band multipliers as the chart calculation, calculate a rolling weighted mean and deviation. Direction is short if high reaches or exceeds its upper outer band AND close is below that band; otherwise long if low reaches or falls below its lower outer band AND close is above that band; otherwise long if the rolling mean is above its value at a slope lookback; otherwise short.
Long: Standardized distance < zero.
Short: Standardized distance >= zero.
Long: Low <= lower outer band AND close > lower outer band.
Short: High >= upper outer band AND close < upper outer band.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Long: Close crosses above anchored mean AND session filter passes AND (sensitivity is all OR sensitivity is confirmed AND anchored mean > anchored mean at the slope lookback).
Short: Close crosses below anchored mean AND session filter passes AND (sensitivity is all OR sensitivity is confirmed AND anchored mean < anchored mean at the slope lookback).
Long: Low <= lower outer band AND close > lower outer band AND session filter passes AND (confirmation is rejection-only OR confirmation is non-trending AND NOT (standardized distance < negative inner-band multiplier AND anchored mean < anchored mean at the slope lookback) OR confirmation is confirming-tell AND bullish divergence occurred within the confirmation lookback).
Short: High >= upper outer band AND close < upper outer band AND session filter passes AND (confirmation is rejection-only OR confirmation is non-trending AND NOT (standardized distance > inner-band multiplier AND anchored mean > anchored mean at the slope lookback) OR confirmation is confirming-tell AND bearish divergence occurred within the confirmation lookback).
Long: High < upper outer band OR close >= upper outer band AND low > lower outer band OR close <= lower outer band AND anchored mean > anchored mean at the slope lookback.
Short: High < upper outer band OR close >= upper outer band AND low > lower outer band OR close <= lower outer band AND anchored mean < anchored mean at the slope lookback.
Long: Higher-timeframe direction = long.
Short: Higher-timeframe direction = short.
