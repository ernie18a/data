Calculation: Rolling swing high = highest high of the preceding N completed chart bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Rolling swing low = lowest low of the preceding N completed chart bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Bullish reversal trigger = selected method: (close > prior rolling swing high, or high > prior rolling swing high when wick confirmation is selected); OR (low < prior low AND (close > prior close AND close > open, or high > prior high when wick confirmation is selected)); OR (high > prior high AND low < prior low AND (close > open, or high > low when wick confirmation is selected))
Calculation: Bearish reversal trigger = selected method: (close < prior rolling swing low, or low < prior rolling swing low when wick confirmation is selected); OR (high > prior high AND (close < prior close AND close < open, or low < prior low when wick confirmation is selected)); OR (high > prior high AND low < prior low AND (close < open, or low < high when wick confirmation is selected))
Calculation: Confluence score = count of enabled factors that qualify: bar range >= ATR × range multiplier; volume >= its longer-lookback average × volume multiplier (or no volume data); RSI <= oversold threshold for bullish triggers or >= overbought threshold for bearish triggers; one point whenever trend context is enabled; bullish hour ruler is Venus/Jupiter or bearish hour ruler is Mars/Saturn when hour ruler is enabled; bullish weekday ruler is Venus/Jupiter or bearish weekday ruler is Mars/Saturn when weekday ruler is enabled
Long: bullish reversal trigger AND bullish confluence score >= minimum score AND prior signal direction <= 0 AND (no prior signal OR current bar index - prior signal bar index >= minimum bar gap)
Short: bearish reversal trigger AND bearish confluence score >= minimum score AND prior signal direction >= 0 AND (no prior signal OR current bar index - prior signal bar index >= minimum bar gap)
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR bearish reversal trigger
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR bullish reversal trigger
