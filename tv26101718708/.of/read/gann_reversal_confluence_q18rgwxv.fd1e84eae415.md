Calculation: Up pattern = for the selected method: swing, close or high > highest high of the preceding swing-lookback bars; key reversal, low < previous low AND either close > previous close AND close > open when close confirmation is enabled, or high > previous high otherwise; outside bar, high > previous high AND low < previous low AND, when close confirmation is enabled, close > open.
Calculation: Down pattern = for the selected method: swing, close or low < lowest low of the preceding swing-lookback bars; key reversal, high > previous high AND either close < previous close AND close < open when close confirmation is enabled, or low < previous low otherwise; outside bar, high > previous high AND low < previous low AND, when close confirmation is enabled, close < open. For swing, use close when close confirmation is enabled and the stated high or low otherwise.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = sum one point for each enabled factor that passes: bar range >= ATR times its multiplier; cumulative volume through the current bar <= 0 OR volume >= its simple moving average times its multiplier; RSI <= oversold threshold for up or >= overbought threshold for down; trend factor enabled, regardless of EMA; planetary hour ruler is Venus or Jupiter for up or Mars or Saturn for down; UTC weekday is Thursday or Friday for up or Tuesday or Saturday for down. Disabled factors contribute zero. The effective minimum is the lesser of the configured minimum and the number of enabled factors.
Calculation: Planetary hour ruler = use the bar opening datetime in UTC and configured latitude and longitude. Let jd = Unix milliseconds / 86400000 + 2440587.5, d = jd - 2451543.5, and ut = 24 times the fractional part of jd + 0.5. Reduce angles in degrees modulo 360. Set w = 282.9404 + 0.0000470935d, e = 0.016709 - 0.000000001151d, M = 356.0470 + 0.9856002585d, with M reduced and converted to radians. Set E = M + e sin(M)(1 + e cos(M)), then apply E = E - (E - e sin(E) - M)/(1 - e cos(E)) three times. Solar longitude is atan2(sqrt(1-e²) sin(E), cos(E)-e) in degrees + w, reduced modulo 360; mean longitude is M in degrees + w, reduced. Obliquity is 23.4393 - 0.0000003563d degrees. Declination = asin(sin(obliquity) sin(solar longitude)); right ascension = atan2(cos(obliquity) sin(solar longitude), cos(solar longitude)) in degrees, reduced. Equation of time in minutes = 4 times mean longitude minus right ascension wrapped to (-180, 180]. Solar noon in UTC minutes = 720 - 4 longitude - equation of time. Let c = (sin(-0.833 degrees) - sin(latitude) sin(declination))/(cos(latitude) cos(declination)); if c is outside [-1,1], sunrise = 360 and sunset = 1080 UTC minutes; otherwise sunrise and sunset = solar noon minus and plus 4 acos(c) in degrees. Divide sunrise-to-sunset and sunset-to-next-sunrise each into 12 equal hours using that day's sunrise and sunset. Before sunrise use the previous UTC weekday and measure night hours from sunset minus 1440 minutes; otherwise use the current UTC weekday. Clamp the resulting zero-based hour index to [0,23]. Starting at the weekday ruler, advance that many positions cyclically through Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon. Weekday rulers Sunday through Saturday are Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn.
Long: On a completed bar, up pattern AND directional confluence score >= effective minimum AND the last displayed signal is absent or short AND bars since the last displayed signal >= cooldown or no displayed signal exists.
Short: On a completed bar, down pattern AND directional confluence score >= effective minimum AND the last displayed signal is absent or long AND bars since the last displayed signal >= cooldown or no displayed signal exists AND the long decision did not occur on this bar.
Long: In oscillator mode, on a completed bar, up pattern AND the oscillator's last direction is absent or short AND bars since its last entry >= cooldown or no entry exists; enter at close and replace any short position.
Short: In oscillator mode, on a completed bar, down pattern AND the oscillator's last direction is absent or long AND bars since its last entry >= cooldown or no entry exists AND the oscillator long decision did not occur on this bar; enter at close and replace any long position.
Exit: long, oscillator mode AND oscillator short decision.
Exit: short, oscillator mode AND oscillator long decision.
Long: In PnL test mode, on a completed bar after processing exits, position is flat AND up pattern AND up confluence score >= the tested score threshold AND bars since this threshold's last entry >= cooldown or no entry exists; enter at close and fix the stop and target at entry price minus and plus, respectively, entry-bar ATR times their separate multipliers.
Short: In PnL test mode, on a completed bar after processing exits, position is flat AND down pattern AND down confluence score >= the tested score threshold AND bars since this threshold's last entry >= cooldown or no entry exists AND the PnL test long decision did not occur on this bar; enter at close and fix the stop and target at entry price plus and minus, respectively, entry-bar ATR times their separate multipliers.
Exit: long, PnL test mode AND low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed target OR down pattern; evaluate stop first, then target, then opposite pattern, and exit at the triggered stop or target price or otherwise at close.
Exit: short, PnL test mode AND high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed target OR up pattern; evaluate stop first, then target, then opposite pattern, and exit at the triggered stop or target price or otherwise at close.
