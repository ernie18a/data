Calculation: Momentum = EMA((close - moving average of close) / ATR, smoothing length), with the ATR and moving average using the same lookback; the moving average may be SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side, available only after the right-hand bars have closed. Use independent shorter and longer pivot lengths for structure, and a separate pivot length for equal highs and lows.
Calculation: Unbroken structure level = the latest confirmed pivot high or low at a given structure length; each new pivot replaces the corresponding level, and a level becomes broken after the first close beyond it.
Long: Chart-timeframe Supertrend changes from down to up.
Short: Chart-timeframe Supertrend changes from up to down.
Long: Momentum crosses above its value two bars earlier AND momentum is below a negative threshold.
Short: Momentum crosses below its value two bars earlier AND momentum is above a positive threshold.
Long: Chart-timeframe Supertrend is up AND an up-flip and a long momentum condition occur within a window of each other AND no earlier such condition has occurred since the latest up-flip.
Short: Chart-timeframe Supertrend is down AND a down-flip and a short momentum condition occur within a window of each other AND no earlier such condition has occurred since the latest down-flip.
Long: Close exceeds an unbroken structure pivot high, using either structure length.
Short: Close is below an unbroken structure pivot low, using either structure length.
Long: A newly confirmed pivot low differs from the preceding confirmed pivot low by less than a threshold multiplied by current ATR.
Short: A newly confirmed pivot high differs from the preceding confirmed pivot high by less than a threshold multiplied by current ATR.
Long: Low exceeds the high two bars earlier AND the previous close exceeds that high AND the gap is at least a threshold multiplied by current ATR.
Short: High is below the low two bars earlier AND the previous close is below that low AND the gap is at least a threshold multiplied by current ATR.
