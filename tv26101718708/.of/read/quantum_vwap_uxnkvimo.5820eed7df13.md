Calculation: Fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier (bullish), or current high < low two bars earlier (bearish)
Calculation: VWAP touch = bar range intersects Global VWAP or an enabled VWAP band; for trend bars, allow tolerance of max(ATR, minimum tick) scaled by its parameter
Calculation: Breaker candle = nearest opposite-direction candle within the configured recent-bar search before the gap
Calculation: Macro window = New York 10:45–11:15 or 15:45–16:15
Calculation: Daily fair value gap = bullish or bearish three-daily-bar gap, using completed daily bars; retain whichever direction was created most recently (daily is higher than an intraday chart)
Calculation: Trend bar = body at least its moving average times a parameter, volume at least its moving average, largest wick no more than body times a parameter, and range touches an enabled VWAP line within ATR/tick tolerance
Calculation: Doji = body-to-range ratio no greater than its parameter and volume at least its moving average times a parameter
Calculation: Big-trade pressure = volume split between buy and sell using the configured close-in-range, body-pressure, or their average model; optionally blend 78% of that base with 22% flow pressure, where flow is 65% normalized close change from prior bar plus 35% normalized lower-wick-minus-upper-wick
Calculation: CISD swing levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal configurable left/right strength
