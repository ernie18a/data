Calculation: Anchored VWAP and bands = volume-weighted mean of a chosen bar price since the start of a higher-timeframe anchor period, and that mean plus or minus separately parameterized multiples of the volume-weighted standard deviation of the same price; use the anchor period still forming.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier, with zone [high two bars earlier, current low]; bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier, with zone [current high, low two bars earlier].
Calculation: Estimated pressure = let range be max(high − low, minimum price increment), and clamp all fractions to [0,1]. Range buy fraction = (close − low)/range; body buy fraction = ((close − open)/range + 1)/2. Choose either fraction or their average. Optionally replace it by a weighted average with flow buy fraction, where flow buy fraction = (1 + clamp(weighted average of clamp((close − previous close)/range, −1, 1) and clamp((lower wick − upper wick)/range, −1, 1), −1, 1))/2; the price-change weight is larger than the wick weight. Estimated buy volume = volume × resulting fraction; estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume. On the first bar use its close as previous close.
Calculation: Pressure threshold = either a fixed threshold or the greater of a minimum threshold and a multiple of the simple moving average of volume. Print chunk size = the greater of a minimum chunk size and a multiple of the pressure threshold.
Calculation: Liquidity displacement = maintain confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot strengths. On each bar remove at most the most recently recorded pivot high exceeded by high and at most the most recently recorded pivot low breached by low. When no displacement is pending, a high sweep on a bullish bar starts a pending bearish displacement at the open of the first bar of its current consecutive bullish sequence; otherwise a low sweep on a bearish bar starts a pending bullish displacement at the open of the first bar of its current consecutive bearish sequence. A pending displacement ends after its first qualifying opposite-direction close or when the age of its sequence start exceeds a lookback; check the qualifying close before expiry.
