Calculation: Volatility-adjusted EMA = recursively update from close using alpha = 2 / (effective length + 1), initialized to close; effective length is max(minimum length, configured length / clamp(ATR / SMA of ATR, lower bound, upper bound)) when adjustment is enabled, otherwise configured length. Use short, medium, and long lengths.
Calculation: ATR scale = ATR, or a fraction of close when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Trend = bullish when short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA AND the short EMA's change over a lookback / ATR scale exceeds a positive threshold; bearish when short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA AND the same normalized change is below the negative threshold; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: RSI momentum = bullish when bullish trend AND RSI > bullish trend threshold AND RSI has risen over its slope lookback, OR trend is not bullish AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI has risen over that lookback; bearish when bearish trend AND RSI < bearish trend threshold AND RSI has fallen over that lookback, OR trend is not bearish AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI has fallen over that lookback. Extreme long means RSI is below a lower threshold in bearish trend and otherwise below the oversold threshold; extreme short means RSI is above a higher threshold in bullish trend and otherwise above the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Volume flow = EMA of OBV compared with a longer EMA of that smoothed OBV; bullish when the smoothed OBV exceeds its longer EMA AND has risen over its slope lookback; bearish when it is below its longer EMA AND has fallen over that lookback. Treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume since the start of the current day, or current typical price if cumulative volume is zero; include the current chart bar and reset at each day boundary.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low surrounded on both sides by the same configured number of lower highs or higher lows; retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, updating them before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high; bearish structure break = close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity grab = low < latest confirmed pivot low minus ATR scale times a depth multiplier AND close > that pivot low; bearish liquidity grab = high > latest confirmed pivot high plus ATR scale times a depth multiplier AND close < that pivot high; apply the configured bar-confirmation requirement.
Calculation: Orderblock = on a confirmed-per-configuration bullish structure break, retain the high-to-low range of the most recent bearish candle within a lookback; on a bearish structure break retain the range of the most recent bullish candle. Keep only the configured maximum number of most recently created blocks across both directions; a block remains eligible regardless of its displayed extension. Price is inside a block when low <= its high AND high >= its low, including on its creation bar.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe bias = for each selected timeframe, sum +1, -1, or 0 respectively for short EMA >, <, or = medium EMA; close >, <, or = long EMA; and RSI above an upper threshold, below a lower threshold, or otherwise. Bias is bullish when the sum >= a positive threshold and bearish when the sum <= its negative. Use standard EMAs with short, medium, and long lengths. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar within the chart bar. When disabled, all biases are neutral.
Calculation: Directional base score = sum of trend, RSI, volume flow, structure, and multi-timeframe contributions. For the long side, trend contributes a larger weight for bullish trend, otherwise a smaller weight if short EMA > medium EMA; RSI contributes a larger weight for extreme long, otherwise a smaller weight for bullish RSI momentum; volume contributes a larger weight for bullish volume flow AND close > daily VWAP, otherwise a smaller weight for close > daily VWAP; structure adds separate weights for being inside a bullish orderblock, bullish structure break, and bullish liquidity grab; multi-timeframe contribution equals the bullish bias count times its weight. Compute the short side symmetrically with bearish conditions and close < daily VWAP. Each unmatched contribution is zero.
Calculation: Directional score = base score plus a strong-volume bonus when volume > its SMA times a multiplier; award the larger bonus to the side with the greater base score, or a smaller bonus to each side when base scores are equal. Clamp each score to its configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Active setup = on a long or short score signal, record its direction, bar, close, and entry-bar ATR scale; replace any opposite active setup. Before evaluating signals on each later bar, clear a long setup if low <= entry close minus entry-bar ATR scale times the stop multiplier OR high >= entry close plus that risk distance times the first target multiplier OR elapsed bars > expiry; clear a short setup symmetrically. Track the last signal bar separately for each direction.
Long: Bullish trend.
Short: Bearish trend.
Long: Multi-timeframe bias sum >= its positive threshold.
Short: Multi-timeframe bias sum <= its negative threshold.
Long: Bullish liquidity grab.
Short: Bearish liquidity grab.
Long: Long score >= score threshold AND long score > short score AND absolute score difference >= minimum gap AND bars since last long score signal > cooldown AND the configured bar-confirmation requirement is satisfied AND active setup direction != long.
Short: Short score >= score threshold AND short score > long score AND absolute score difference >= minimum gap AND bars since last short score signal > cooldown AND the configured bar-confirmation requirement is satisfied AND active setup direction != short.
Exit: long, low <= entry close minus entry-bar ATR scale times stop multiplier OR high >= entry close plus entry-bar ATR scale times stop multiplier times first target multiplier OR bars since entry > expiry.
Exit: short, high >= entry close plus entry-bar ATR scale times stop multiplier OR low <= entry close minus entry-bar ATR scale times stop multiplier times first target multiplier OR bars since entry > expiry.
