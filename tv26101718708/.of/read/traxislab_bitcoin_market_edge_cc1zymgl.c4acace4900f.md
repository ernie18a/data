Calculation: ATR = average true range; use its value on the entry bar to set risk distance.
Calculation: Volatility-adjusted EMA = recursively smoothed close with alpha 2/(effective length+1), where effective length is the EMA length divided by clamp(ATR / 50-bar SMA of ATR, 0.5, 2); fast length < mid length < slow length.
Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA AND (fast EMA − fast EMA 3 bars ago)/ATR × 100 > 0.2; bear trend = fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA AND that slope < −0.2.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close; RSI slope = RSI − RSI 3 bars ago. Bull momentum = (bull trend AND RSI > bullish midpoint AND RSI slope > 0) OR (NOT bull trend AND NOT bear trend AND RSI < oversold level AND RSI slope > 0); bear momentum = (bear trend AND RSI < bearish midpoint AND RSI slope < 0) OR (NOT bull trend AND NOT bear trend AND RSI > overbought level AND RSI slope < 0). Long RSI extreme = RSI < (bear trend ? 25 : oversold level); short RSI extreme = RSI > (bull trend ? 75 : overbought level).
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume, adding volume when close rises and subtracting it when close falls; fast OBV = EMA(OBV, smoothing length), slow OBV = EMA(fast OBV, twice the smoothing length). Bull OBV = fast OBV > slow OBV AND fast OBV > its value 3 bars ago; bear OBV = fast OBV < slow OBV AND fast OBV < its value 3 bars ago.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = running sum of (typical price × volume) divided by running volume, reset at each new day; typical price = (high + low + close)/3. Bull volume flow = bull OBV AND (close > daily VWAP OR close crosses above it); bear volume flow = bear OBV AND (close < daily VWAP OR close crosses below it). Strong volume = volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = pivot high/low confirmed using the pivot lookback on both sides; retain the latest of each. Bull BOS = close crosses above latest pivot high; bear BOS = close crosses below latest pivot low. Bull liquidity grab = low < latest pivot low − ATR × grab-depth factor AND close > latest pivot low; bear liquidity grab = high > latest pivot high + ATR × grab-depth factor AND close < latest pivot high. These grabs require a confirmed chart bar.
Calculation: Bull/bear orderblock zone = low-to-high range of the nearest prior opposite-color candle within the previous 15 bars when the respective BOS occurs on a confirmed bar. Retain up to the configured maximum number of latest zones. Price is in a zone when current high ≥ zone low AND current low ≤ zone high; bull/bear zone status is true if this holds for any retained zone of that direction.
Calculation: For each configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), timeframe score = +1 if EMA(21) > EMA(50), −1 if EMA(21) < EMA(50), +1 if close > EMA(200), −1 if close < EMA(200), +1 if RSI(14) > 55, and −1 if RSI(14) < 45; direction is bullish at score ≥ 2, bearish at score ≤ −2, otherwise neutral. Use the request.security lookahead-off value: the last completed requested-timeframe bar historically and the currently forming requested-timeframe bar in realtime. Bull/bear timeframe count = number of configured timeframes with bullish/bearish direction.
Calculation: Long base score = trend points (18 for bull trend, else 8 if fast EMA > mid EMA, else 0) + RSI points (12 for long RSI extreme, else 8 for bull momentum, else 0) + flow points (14 for bull volume flow, else 6 if close > daily VWAP, else 0) + 10 if in bull orderblock + 8 if bull BOS + 12 if bull liquidity grab + 4 × bullish timeframe count. Short base score uses the corresponding bear conditions and close < daily VWAP. Add 6 to the higher base score when volume is strong; if base scores tie, add 3 to each. Clamp each resulting score to 0–100.
Calculation: Entry risk distance = ATR on entry bar × stop-loss ATR multiplier; long stop = entry price − risk distance, long first target = entry price + risk distance × first reward/risk multiple; short stop = entry price + risk distance, short first target = entry price − risk distance × first reward/risk multiple.
Long: bullish liquidity grab OR (long score ≥ selected threshold AND long score > short score AND long score − short score ≥ minimum gap AND bars since last long signal > cooldown AND bar is confirmed when confirmed-bars-only is enabled AND active position is not long).
Short: bearish liquidity grab OR (short score ≥ selected threshold AND short score > long score AND short score − long score ≥ minimum gap AND bars since last short signal > cooldown AND bar is confirmed when confirmed-bars-only is enabled AND active position is not short).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long first target OR bars since entry > 100.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short first target OR bars since entry > 100.
