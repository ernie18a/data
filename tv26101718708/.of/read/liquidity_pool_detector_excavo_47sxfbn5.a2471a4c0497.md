Calculation: Active high and low clusters = Maintain separate ordered lists. On each closed bar, confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side; a pivot high is greater than or equal to all earlier highs and greater than all later highs in its window, and a pivot low is less than or equal to all earlier lows and less than all later lows. Process each newly confirmed pivot before checking sweeps. Merge it into the first same-side cluster whose level is within a configurable multiple of the current bar's ATR, updating the level to the touch-count-weighted average and increasing its count; otherwise create a cluster with one touch if the same-side cluster limit has not been reached. Use a configurable multiple of close as the ATR fallback while ATR is unavailable. After checking the current bar for sweeps, remove high clusters where high > level AND close < level OR close >= level, and low clusters where low < level AND close > level OR close <= level.
Long: Low < any active low cluster level AND close > that level.
Short: High > any active high cluster level AND close < that level.
