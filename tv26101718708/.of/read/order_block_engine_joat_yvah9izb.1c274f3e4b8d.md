Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side; use the most recently confirmed pivot of each kind. A newly confirmed pivot is unbroken until its first qualifying displacement break or replacement by a newer pivot.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume divided by its simple moving average; use 1 if the average is not positive. Volume confirmation = volume filtering is disabled OR the volume average is not positive OR the volume ratio is at least the volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish displacement break = the latest swing high is unbroken AND ATR > 0 AND close > open AND close minus the swing high > displacement multiplier × ATR AND volume confirmation. Bearish displacement break = the latest swing low is unbroken AND ATR > 0 AND close < open AND the swing low minus close > displacement multiplier × ATR AND volume confirmation.
Calculation: Order block = on a displacement break, take the high and low of the most recent opposing-close candle within the search lookback, excluding the break bar; create no block if none exists. Store its direction, break bar and grade. Retain only the most recently created configurable number of blocks across both directions, including mitigated blocks. Starting after its break bar, a bullish block is mitigated when close < its low or it produces a long reaction; a bearish block is mitigated when close > its high or it produces a short reaction. Process blocks oldest to newest.
Calculation: Block grade = the sum of three configurable step scores, bounded to the grade range: score the break's distance beyond its swing divided by break-bar ATR, the break-bar volume ratio, and the break-bar absolute body divided by its range, using zero for a zero range. Each component uses its own ascending thresholds and corresponding scores; higher values receive higher scores.
Calculation: Long reaction = an unmitigated bullish block is older than the current bar AND low <= block high AND close > block low AND close > open AND block grade >= minimum grade. Short reaction = an unmitigated bearish block is older than the current bar AND high >= block low AND close < block high AND close < open AND block grade >= minimum grade. Mitigate every block that reacts, including bearish blocks when a long reaction also occurs.
Calculation: Signal block = the last block in oldest-to-newest processing order that reacts on the signal bar, including a bearish block if both directions react. At entry, freeze the stop at signal block low minus ATR × stop buffer for a long, or signal block high plus ATR × stop buffer for a short; freeze risk as the absolute difference between entry price and stop. The target is entry price plus risk × target multiple for a long, or entry price minus risk × target multiple for a short.
Long: bullish displacement break AND an opposing-close candle exists within the search lookback.
Short: bearish displacement break AND an opposing-close candle exists within the search lookback.
Long: close >= low AND close <= high of an unmitigated bullish order block.
Short: close >= low AND close <= high of an unmitigated bearish order block AND close is not inside an unmitigated bullish order block AND no long reaction occurs.
Long: at least one long reaction occurs.
Short: at least one short reaction occurs AND no long reaction occurs.
Exit: long, low <= frozen stop OR high >= frozen target.
Exit: short, high >= frozen stop OR low <= frozen target.
