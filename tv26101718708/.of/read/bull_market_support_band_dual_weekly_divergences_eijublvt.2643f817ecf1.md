Calculation: Band midpoint = (SMA of the selected source + EMA of the selected source) / 2, calculated on completed higher-timeframe bars; the EMA has a longer lookback than the SMA.
Calculation: Band distance = (selected source - band midpoint) / band midpoint.
Calculation: Band momentum = (band midpoint - band midpoint a lookback ago) / band midpoint a lookback ago.
Calculation: Band width = absolute value of (SMA - EMA) / band midpoint.
Calculation: Pivot pairs = for each of two price-pivot configurations, one with a longer left lookback and both with the same right lookback, identify low pivots and high pivots on completed higher-timeframe bars. A low pivot is less than or equal to every low in its left lookback and less than every low in its right lookback; a high pivot is greater than or equal to every high in its left lookback and greater than every high in its right lookback. Confirm a pivot only when its right lookback has completed. For each configuration, pivot side, and oscillator separately, pair the newly confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding same-side pivot for which that oscillator was defined; sample the oscillator at each pivot bar. The oscillator is RSI of the selected source, band distance, band momentum, or band width, calculated on the same completed higher-timeframe bars. The two pivot times must be separated by at least a minimum and at most a maximum number of higher-timeframe periods.
Long: A valid low pivot pair is newly confirmed AND ((newer pivot low < previous pivot low AND newer oscillator > previous oscillator) OR (newer pivot low > previous pivot low AND newer oscillator < previous oscillator)).
Short: A valid high pivot pair is newly confirmed AND ((newer pivot high > previous pivot high AND newer oscillator < previous oscillator) OR (newer pivot high < previous pivot high AND newer oscillator > previous oscillator)).
