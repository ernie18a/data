Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Stochastic = smoothed stochastic %K of close, high, and low; %D is a smoothed %K. The %K and %D smoothing methods and lookbacks are configurable.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = stochastic of RSI(close), smoothed into %K and %D.
Calculation: CCI = (hlc3 − SMA(hlc3, lookback)) / (0.015 × mean absolute deviation of hlc3 over the lookback).
Calculation: MACD = EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback); signal = SMA(MACD, signal lookback); histogram = MACD − signal. The fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Bollinger bands = selected moving average of close ± configured multiplier × standard deviation of close; the selected moving-average variant and lookback are configurable.
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average price of hlc3.
Calculation: Percent change = 100 × (close − previous close) / previous close.
Calculation: Moving averages = two selected moving-average variants of close, with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: A cross above a level means the current value is above the level and its previous value was at or below it; a cross below means the current value is below the level and its previous value was at or above it.
Calculation: An alternate-timeframe indicator uses bars aggregated from the backtest bars at its configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the latest completed bar.
Long: On a confirmed bar while flat, all active required buy detector conditions are true AND the number of true active buy detector conditions is at least the configured buy consensus threshold. Each active buy detector selects one of: RSI below/above its threshold or crossing below/above it; stochastic %K below its threshold or crossing below/above it; stochastic RSI %K below/above its threshold, crossing below/above it, crossing above %D, or crossing above %D while below its threshold; CCI below/above its threshold or crossing below/above it; MACD above/below its signal or threshold, MACD-histogram crossing above/below zero, or MACD crossing above its signal; price below/above or crossing below/above the selected Bollinger band; short moving average above long moving average or crossing above it, price above a moving average, price touching a moving average from below, price above both moving averages, or price below both; price above/below or touching VWAP from either side; percent change below its threshold.
Short: On a confirmed bar while flat, all active required sell detector conditions are true AND the number of true active sell detector conditions is at least the configured sell consensus threshold. Each active sell detector selects one of: RSI below/above its threshold or crossing below/above it; stochastic %K above its threshold or crossing below/above it; stochastic RSI %K below/above its threshold, crossing below/above it, crossing above %D, or crossing above %D while above its threshold; CCI below/above its threshold or crossing below/above it; MACD above/below its signal or threshold, MACD-histogram crossing above/below zero, or MACD crossing below its signal; price below/above or crossing below/above the selected Bollinger band; short moving average below/above long moving average or crossing below it, price above/below a moving average, price touching a moving average, price above both moving averages, or price below both; price above/below or touching VWAP from either side; percent change above its threshold.
Exit: long, sell condition changes from false to true OR low ≤ highest high since entry × (1 − trailing-stop fraction) when trailing stop is enabled OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) when stop loss is enabled OR (highest high since entry ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) AND low ≤ highest high since entry × (1 − trailing-profit fraction)) when take profit is enabled.
