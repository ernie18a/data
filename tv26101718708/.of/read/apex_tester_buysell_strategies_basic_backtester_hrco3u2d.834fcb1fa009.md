Calculation: Cross above = current left operand > current right operand AND previous left operand <= previous right operand; cross below reverses the inequalities.
Calculation: Indicator sampling = each indicator independently uses chart bars or aggregated higher- or lower-timeframe bars. On historical higher-timeframe bars, Pine v3 exposes the final value of the higher-timeframe bar from its first chart bar; on a live higher-timeframe bar it uses the value still forming. A lower-timeframe request selects the first intrabar historically. Crosses compare the resulting chart-bar series. The historical higher-timeframe behavior requires future data and cannot be reproduced causally.
Calculation: Moving-average choices = SMA, EMA, WMA, DEMA, TEMA, triangular MA, KAMA, MAMA, or T3 of close. Triangular MA is SMA(SMA(close, ceiling(length/2)), floor(length/2)+1). KAMA uses efficiency ratio abs(close-close[length])/sum(abs(close-close[1]), length), or zero when the denominator is zero, and recursively updates from zero with smoothing coefficient (efficiency ratio × (0.666-0.0645)+0.0645)². MAMA uses the standard MESA adaptive moving average with fast limit 0.5 and slow limit 0.05; its length input has no effect. T3 uses six successive EMAs and volume factor 0.7. For each MA pair, the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: Band = selected moving average of close plus or minus a selectable multiple of the standard deviation of close over the same lookback; use the lower band for long detectors and the upper band for exit detectors.
Calculation: MACD = shorter-lookback EMA(close) minus longer-lookback EMA(close); its signal is an SMA of MACD, and its histogram is MACD minus that signal.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = a selected moving average of stochastic %K calculated from close, high, and low. Stochastic RSI K = SMA of stochastic applied to RSI as its close, high, and low; D = SMA of K.
Calculation: Long detector set = independently configurable instances of: RSI below, above, crossing below, or crossing above its threshold; smoothed stochastic below, crossing below, or crossing above its threshold; stochastic RSI K crossing above D, optionally AND K below its threshold, or K below, above, crossing below, or crossing above its threshold; CCI of typical price below, above, or crossing above its threshold; MACD crossing above its signal, above or below its signal, above or below its threshold, or its histogram crossing above or below zero; close crossing above or below the lower band, low below the lower band, or high above the lower band; short MA crossing above or above long MA, high above short MA optionally AND low below short MA, high above long MA, high above both MAs, or low below both MAs; high above VWAP optionally AND low below VWAP, or low below VWAP optionally AND high above VWAP; or percentage change of consecutive closes below a threshold. The two MA, band, RSI, stochastic, stochastic RSI, and MACD instances and three CCI instances have independently selectable parameters and timeframes.
Calculation: Exit detector set = independently configurable instances of: RSI below, above, crossing below, or crossing above its threshold; smoothed stochastic above, crossing above, or crossing below its threshold; stochastic RSI K crossing above D, optionally AND K above its threshold, or K below, above, crossing below, or crossing above its threshold; CCI of typical price below, above, or crossing below its threshold; MACD crossing below its signal or its histogram crossing above or below zero; close crossing above or below the upper band, low below the upper band, or high above the upper band; short MA crossing below or below long MA, high above short MA optionally AND low below short MA, high above long MA AND low below long MA, high above both MAs, or low below both MAs; high above VWAP optionally AND low below VWAP, or low below VWAP optionally AND high above VWAP; or percentage change of consecutive closes above a threshold.
Calculation: Long consensus = count of enabled long detectors that are true. Required long detectors must all be true, except that when the second RSI detector is both enabled and required, its result replaces the required-detector result for the first RSI, first band, first stochastic, first stochastic RSI, first CCI, first MACD, VWAP, first MA, and percentage-change detectors; the remaining required detectors must still be true.
Calculation: Exit consensus = count of enabled exit detectors that are true; every enabled exit detector marked required must also be true.
Calculation: Running high = highest chart-bar high since the long signal, including the current bar. Entry price = close of the long signal bar.
Long: datetime is within the configured backtest interval AND position is flat AND no close occurred on the previous bar AND long consensus >= its threshold AND required long detectors pass.
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured backtest interval AND current bar is later than the entry bar AND ((exit consensus >= its threshold AND required exit detectors pass AND the combined exit consensus was false on the previous bar) OR (take profit is enabled AND running high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) AND low <= running high × (1 - trailing-profit fraction)) OR (stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price × (1 - stop-loss fraction)) OR (trailing stop is enabled AND low <= running high × (1 - trailing-stop fraction))); trailing-profit fraction is capped at the take-profit fraction.
