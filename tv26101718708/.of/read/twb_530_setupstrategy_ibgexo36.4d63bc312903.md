Calculation: Reference range = high and low of the candle starting at 05:30 Asia/Kolkata, taken from its last completed bar; this timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: 25% retracement = reference high - 0.25 × (reference high - reference low); 75% retracement = reference high - 0.75 × (reference high - reference low).
Calculation: Long stop = 75% retracement; long target = long entry + 2 × |long entry - long stop|; short stop = 25% retracement; short target = short entry - 2 × |short stop - short entry|.
Long: The first close outside the reference range today was above the reference high AND no trade has been taken today AND low <= 25% retracement AND close > open.
Short: The first close outside the reference range today was below the reference low AND no trade has been taken today AND high >= 75% retracement AND close < open.
Exit: long, high >= long target OR low <= long stop
Exit: short, low <= short target OR high >= short stop
