Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = a bar’s high is below the low two bars later; bearish fair value gap = a bar’s low is above the high two bars later. An inversion requires the close to cross beyond the gap boundary within the following five bars, a candle range greater than ATR times the configured impulsiveness multiplier, and a body greater than 40% of its range.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = within the last ten bars, a confirmed swing low (lower than the lows of the bars immediately before and after it) was below the previous confirmed swing low. Bearish liquidity sweep = the corresponding confirmed swing high was above the previous confirmed swing high. Each pivot is two bars before its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fair value gap = the latest available gap from higher-timeframe bars, checking daily first, then 4-hour, then 1-hour; use the current, potentially still-forming bar in each gap calculation. Price is in the bullish or bearish zone when the close is within that gap.
Calculation: In-session = within the configured core session, or within the configured extended session while ATR exceeds its 20-bar average multiplied by the configured volatility multiplier.
Calculation: Liquidity present = at least three of the previous 19 comparisons have a higher high than the following bar’s high, or at least three have a lower low than the following bar’s low.
Calculation: Directional score = 2 points for price in that direction’s higher-timeframe gap, 2 for that direction’s liquidity sweep, 1 in-session, 1 when the optional ES proximity filter is disabled or confirmed, and 1 when liquidity is present. Grade is S at score 6 or higher, A at 4 or higher, B at 3 or higher, otherwise C; it passes when it meets the configured minimum grade.
Long: bullish inversion exists AND bullish liquidity sweep AND in-session AND bullish signal has not fired since the latest new leg high or since price was outside both higher-timeframe gap zones AND bullish grade passes configured minimum.
Short: bearish inversion exists AND bearish liquidity sweep AND in-session AND bearish signal has not fired since the latest new leg low or since price was outside both higher-timeframe gap zones AND bearish grade passes configured minimum.
