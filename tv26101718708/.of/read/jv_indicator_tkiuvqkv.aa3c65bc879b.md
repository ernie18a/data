Calculation: Swing low = a low lower than the lows of both adjacent bars, confirmed after the following bar; swing high = a high higher than the highs of both adjacent bars, confirmed after the following bar. Evaluate the swing one additional chart bar after confirmation.
Calculation: Recent lower-low sweep = the latest evaluated swing low is lower than the preceding evaluated swing low and its swing bar is within a maximum age. Recent higher-high sweep = the latest evaluated swing high is higher than the preceding evaluated swing high and its swing bar is within the same maximum age.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low above the high a specified number of chart bars earlier, with bounds at those prices. Bearish gap = current high below the low the same number of chart bars earlier, with bounds at those prices. Retain gaps for a maximum age; the inversion age limit is shorter than the retention limit.
Calculation: Selected bullish and bearish higher-timeframe gaps = independently retain the most recently detected gap of each direction; on each chart bar replace it with the current gap from the longest available of three selected timeframes. Each selected timeframe can be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. The source requests the bar still forming with historical lookahead, so historical values use the completed value of that same bar.
Calculation: Liquidity count condition = within a lookback of preceding chart bars, the count of highs greater than their immediately preceding highs reaches a minimum OR the count of lows lower than their immediately preceding lows reaches the same minimum.
Calculation: Session condition = chart bar time is inside the core session OR chart bar time is inside the extended session AND ATR is greater than a multiple of its moving average. Session times use the New York timezone.
Calculation: Directional score = two points if close is inside the retained gap of the corresponding direction, two points if the corresponding sweep is recent, one point if the session condition holds, one point for the disabled external-market filter, and one point if the liquidity count condition holds. The minimum passing score is determined by the chosen minimum grade.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = each direction starts eligible and becomes ineligible when its signal fires. Before evaluating signals on each bar, restore long eligibility when high exceeds both the previous bar's high and the highest high recorded before the current bar, or when close is outside both retained directional gaps. Restore short eligibility when low is below both the previous bar's low and the lowest low recorded before the current bar, or when close is outside both retained directional gaps.
Long: A retained bearish gap is within the inversion age limit AND close > its upper bound AND high - low > ATR multiplied by an impulsiveness threshold AND absolute close - open > high - low multiplied by a body threshold AND a recent lower-low sweep exists AND the session condition holds AND long signal eligibility is true AND the bullish directional score >= the minimum passing score.
Short: A retained bullish gap is within the inversion age limit AND close < its lower bound AND high - low > ATR multiplied by the impulsiveness threshold AND absolute close - open > high - low multiplied by the body threshold AND a recent higher-high sweep exists AND the session condition holds AND short signal eligibility is true AND the bearish directional score >= the minimum passing score.
