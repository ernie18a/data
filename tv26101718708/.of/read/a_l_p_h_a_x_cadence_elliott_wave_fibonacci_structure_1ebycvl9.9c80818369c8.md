Calculation: Direction-normalized prices = For direction d = +1 for long and -1 for short, set O=d×open, C=d×close, H=high and L=low for long, and H=-low and L=-high for short. Apply all directional comparisons below separately for each d. ATR, RSI and choppiness use the original prices.
Calculation: Swings = Confirm price pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low on the same bar. Maintain a newest-first list: ignore a pivot of the same type as the newest swing; for an opposite-type pivot, replace the newest swing if their price distance is less than current ATR times the minimum-leg multiplier, otherwise prepend it and discard the oldest swing if the storage limit is exceeded.
Calculation: Structure = Initially neutral. A confirmed price pivot high or low replaces the corresponding tracked high or low and its bar index. Then, in order, if the selected upper break price exceeds the tracked high on a later bar, set structure bullish and replace the tracked high and its index with that price and the current bar; if the selected lower break price is below the tracked low on a later bar, set structure bearish and replace the tracked low and its index likewise. Select close for both break prices when close-only mode is enabled; otherwise select high and low respectively.
Calculation: Wave = From the newest three swings in chronological order, assign W0, W1, W2. A directional impulse requires their types to be low, high, low in direction-normalized prices and W1>W0; its phase is W2. Then, if the newest five swings have types low, high, low, high, low, overwrite W0 through W4 with those five prices and set the directional impulse to W3>W1 AND W1>W0. If that test passes, set phase to W4; otherwise retain the previous phase. All W prices are direction-normalized. The five-swing overwrite occurs even when its impulse test fails.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, direction-normalized short-period EMA > direction-normalized long-period EMA AND direction-normalized close > direction-normalized short-period EMA.
Calculation: Directional structure alignment = Structure is in direction d OR structure is neutral AND higher-timeframe alignment is true. The fallback applies even if higher-timeframe scoring is disabled.
Calculation: Trigger = C>O AND ((C-L)/(H-L)>0.48 OR prior C<prior O AND C>=prior O).
Calculation: Golden-zone touch for an impulse leg from A to B = L<=B-(B-A)×shallower retracement parameter AND L>=B-(B-A)×deeper retracement parameter AND C>=B-(B-A)×deeper retracement parameter AND trigger is true. The deeper retracement parameter exceeds the shallower one.
Calculation: Setup candidates = W2: directional impulse, W2 phase, directional structure alignment, golden-zone touch for W0 to W1, W2>W0, and W1-W0>=ATR×minimum-leg multiplier. W4: directional impulse, W4 phase, structure in direction d, golden-zone touch for W2 to W3, and W1-W0>=ATR×minimum-leg multiplier. Breakout: W2 or W4 phase, directional impulse, directional structure alignment, newest swing is a low and preceding swing is a high in direction-normalized prices, C>that preceding high and prior C<=that same high, and W1-W0>=ATR×minimum-leg multiplier. ABC: newest three swings are low, high, low in direction-normalized prices, structure is in direction d, L<=B-(B-A)×0.618, C>B-(B-A)×0.618, and trigger is true, where A is the oldest swing and B is the middle swing. Each candidate also requires its respective setup to be enabled.
Calculation: Confluence score = For each qualifying candidate, start at four points; add one each for higher-timeframe alignment or higher-timeframe scoring disabled, volume >= its simple moving average times the volume multiplier or volume confirmation disabled, choppiness below its threshold or chop blocking disabled, direction-appropriate RSI threshold passed or RSI filter disabled, C>direction-normalized EMA or EMA filter disabled, and directional RSI momentum or RSI filter disabled. For long, RSI threshold means RSI>=long threshold and momentum means RSI>prior RSI; for short, RSI<=short threshold and RSI<prior RSI.
Calculation: Entry signal = A candidate is true AND its confluence score>=minimum score AND choppiness is below its threshold or chop blocking is disabled AND higher-timeframe alignment is true or hard higher-timeframe alignment is disabled AND the number of bars since the last signal in either direction is at least the cooldown, or no prior signal exists. Evaluate both directions before updating the last-signal bar.
Calculation: Armed condition = Directional impulse AND either golden-zone touch for W0 to W1 or W4 phase AND golden-zone touch for W2 to W3. The latter also requires W2 and W3 to exist. Use the wave assignments and impulse state produced by the wave calculation.
Long: Long entry signal = true.
Short: Short entry signal = true.
Long: Long entry signal = false AND short entry signal = false AND long armed condition = true.
Short: Long entry signal = false AND short entry signal = false AND long armed condition = false AND short armed condition = true.
Exit: long, structure is bullish AND a price pivot high and an RSI pivot high are both confirmed on the current bar AND high at the RSI pivot lookback exceeds high at that same offset on the previous price-pivot-high confirmation bar AND RSI at that offset is below RSI at that same offset on the previous RSI-pivot-high confirmation bar.
Exit: short, structure is bearish AND a price pivot low and an RSI pivot low are both confirmed on the current bar AND low at the RSI pivot lookback is below low at that same offset on the previous price-pivot-low confirmation bar AND RSI at that offset is above RSI at that same offset on the previous RSI-pivot-low confirmation bar.
