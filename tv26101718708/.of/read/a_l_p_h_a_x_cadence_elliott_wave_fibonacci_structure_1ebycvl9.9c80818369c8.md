Calculation: Confirmed swing sequence = pivot highs/lows confirmed after the pivot length of bars on both sides; retain alternating swings, replacing the latest swing with a same-direction pivot if their distance is less than ATR × minimum leg size, otherwise prepend the pivot.
Calculation: Bull/bear market structure = state set bullish/bearish when price breaks the latest swing high/low (close if close-only mode, otherwise high/low); a break from neutral or the opposite state is a change of character, and a break in the same direction is a continuation.
Calculation: Bull W2 = latest three swings are Low–High–Low, with the middle high above the first low; assign them W0, W1, W2. Bear W2 = High–Low–High, with the middle low below the first high; assign W0, W1, W2.
Calculation: Bull W4 = latest five swings are Low–High–Low–High–Low, with W3 > W1 > W0. Bear W4 = High–Low–High–Low–High, with W3 < W1 < W0. Assign W0–W4 in sequence.
Calculation: Bull W2 zone = [W1 − (W1−W0)×upper retracement, W1 − (W1−W0)×lower retracement]. Bear W2 zone = [W1 + (W0−W1)×lower retracement, W1 + (W0−W1)×upper retracement]. Bull W4 zone uses W3 and W2 in place of W1 and W0; bear W4 zone uses W3 and W2 in the corresponding reversed direction.
Calculation: Bull/bear trigger candle = respectively (close > open AND (close−low)/(high−low) > wick threshold) OR (close > open AND prior close < prior open AND close ≥ prior open); for bear, reverse directions and use (high−close)/(high−low).
Calculation: HTF bullish/bearish bias = on a higher timeframe, fast EMA >/< slow EMA AND close >/< fast EMA; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100×log10(sum of 1-bar ATR over lookback / (highest high − lowest low over lookback)) / log10(lookback); chop passes when below its threshold or the chop filter is disabled.
Calculation: Confluence score = 1 point each for setup-specific structure alignment, setup-specific Fibonacci-zone confirmation, trigger candle, HTF alignment (or 1 neutral point if HTF bias is disabled), volume ≥ volume moving average × multiplier when volume confirmation is enabled, chop passing, enabled RSI zone (RSI ≥ long minimum or ≤ short maximum), enabled EMA alignment (close above/below EMA), enabled RSI momentum (RSI rising/falling), and 1 base point. Require score ≥ minimum. For W3, Fibonacci and trigger points are always credited; for ABC, Fibonacci is always credited.
Calculation: Bearish/bullish RSI divergence = at confirmed price pivot high/low, respectively, price makes a higher high/lower low than its prior confirmed pivot while RSI at the corresponding pivot makes a lower high/higher low; require respectively bullish/bearish market structure. RSI pivots use the divergence lookback on both sides.
Long: confirmed bar AND cooldown elapsed AND chop passes AND (HTF bullish bias if hard HTF alignment is enabled) AND confluence score meets minimum AND ((bull W2 phase AND (market structure bullish OR structure neutral with HTF bullish bias) AND bar low is within bull W2 zone AND close ≥ zone bottom AND bull trigger candle AND W2 > W0 AND W1−W0 ≥ ATR×minimum leg size) OR (bull W4 phase AND market structure bullish AND bar low is within bull W4 zone AND close ≥ zone bottom AND bull trigger candle AND W1−W0 ≥ ATR×minimum leg size) OR (bull W2 or W4 phase AND (market structure bullish OR structure neutral with HTF bullish bias) AND close crosses above the latest swing high in one bar AND W1−W0 ≥ ATR×minimum leg size) OR (three latest swings are Low–High–Low in bullish structure AND bar low ≤ 61.8% retracement of the leg from the earlier low to the middle high AND close > that retracement AND bull trigger candle))
Short: confirmed bar AND cooldown elapsed AND chop passes AND (HTF bearish bias if hard HTF alignment is enabled) AND confluence score meets minimum AND ((bear W2 phase AND (market structure bearish OR structure neutral with HTF bearish bias) AND bar high is within bear W2 zone AND close ≤ zone top AND bear trigger candle AND W2 < W0 AND W0−W1 ≥ ATR×minimum leg size) OR (bear W4 phase AND market structure bearish AND bar high is within bear W4 zone AND close ≤ zone top AND bear trigger candle AND W0−W1 ≥ ATR×minimum leg size) OR (bear W2 or W4 phase AND (market structure bearish OR structure neutral with HTF bearish bias) AND close crosses below the latest swing low in one bar AND W0−W1 ≥ ATR×minimum leg size) OR (three latest swings are High–Low–High in bearish structure AND bar high ≥ 61.8% retracement of the leg from the earlier high to the middle low AND close < that retracement AND bear trigger candle))
Exit: long, bearish RSI divergence
Exit: short, bullish RSI divergence
