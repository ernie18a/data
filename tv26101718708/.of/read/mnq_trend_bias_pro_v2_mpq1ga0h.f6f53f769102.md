Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe bias = on the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, close > EMA and session VWAP, and both EMA and session VWAP > their values on the preceding higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Bearish higher-timeframe bias = on the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, close < EMA and session VWAP, and both EMA and session VWAP < their values on the preceding higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Recent high = highest high over the breakout lookback on chart-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Recent low = lowest low over the breakout lookback on chart-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: VWAP extension = absolute difference between chart-timeframe close and session VWAP, divided by chart-timeframe ATR
Long: bullish higher-timeframe bias AND previous chart bar was not bullish; OR bullish higher-timeframe bias AND chart close > recent high AND chart close > chart EMA AND chart close > chart session VWAP AND chart close > chart open AND VWAP extension <= its maximum AND no breakout entry has occurred during the current bullish-bias cycle; OR bullish higher-timeframe bias AND a prior chart bar closed below chart EMA while bullish bias was active AND chart close > chart EMA AND previous chart close <= previous chart EMA AND chart close > chart session VWAP AND chart close > chart open AND VWAP extension <= its maximum AND no breakout entry occurs on this bar
Short: bearish higher-timeframe bias AND previous chart bar was not bearish; OR bearish higher-timeframe bias AND chart close < recent low AND chart close < chart EMA AND chart close < chart session VWAP AND chart close < chart open AND VWAP extension <= its maximum AND no breakout entry has occurred during the current bearish-bias cycle; OR bearish higher-timeframe bias AND a prior chart bar closed above chart EMA while bearish bias was active AND chart close < chart EMA AND previous chart close >= previous chart EMA AND chart close < chart session VWAP AND chart close < chart open AND VWAP extension <= its maximum AND no breakout entry occurs on this bar
