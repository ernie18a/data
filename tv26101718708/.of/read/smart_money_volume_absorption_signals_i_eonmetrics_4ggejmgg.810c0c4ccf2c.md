Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Pending setup = one direction and stage at a time, initially absent. On each confirmed bar, first discard an incomplete setup if its remaining lifetime, decremented by one, is at most zero, or if its direction is long and close < its absorption low, or its direction is short and close > its absorption high. Clear a completed setup on the bar after completion. Then, in descending stage order, advance at most one stage: stage 3 completes when the corresponding Long or Short condition holds; stage 2 advances to stage 3 when body percentile rank >= expansion threshold AND the candle points in the setup direction, saving its open as origin; stage 1 advances to stage 2 when the candle points in the setup direction. Reset remaining lifetime to the setup lifetime at each advance. If no setup remains after these steps, start stage 1 when volume > 0 AND volume percentile rank >= absorption threshold AND either close position >= 1 − close-position threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > close EMA), for a long setup, or close position <= close-position threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < close EMA), for a short setup. Save that bar's low for a long setup or high for a short setup as the absorption extreme, and initialize remaining lifetime to the setup lifetime. Both percentile ranks use the same lookback; replace missing volume with zero for its rank. A candle points long when close > open and short when close < open.
Calculation: Trade levels = entry at the pending setup's saved origin; long stop = absorption extreme − stop buffer, short stop = absorption extreme + stop buffer; risk = absolute difference between entry and stop; long target = entry + target multiple × risk; short target = entry − target multiple × risk. Keep these levels fixed for the trade. Evaluate exits starting on the bar after the signal, with the stop taking priority if both levels are reached. Process an existing trade's exit before evaluating setup completion on each bar.
Long: Pending setup stage = 3 AND setup direction = long AND low <= origin AND close > origin AND open trade count = 0.
Short: Pending setup stage = 3 AND setup direction = short AND high >= origin AND close < origin AND open trade count = 0.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target.
