Calculation: Pivot zone = On each confirmed pivot, compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type. Use the same number of bars on each side to confirm pivots. If the later pivot occurs within the zone maximum age of the earlier pivot and its wick is within the earlier pivot candle's body, create a zone: for pivot highs, top = max of the two highs and bottom = min of the two body tops; for pivot lows, top = max of the two body bottoms and bottom = min of the two lows. Require top > bottom. A zone is active from its confirmation bar through the earlier pivot bar plus the zone maximum age, inclusive. Remove it after the first bar whose close is above a pivot-high zone's top or below a pivot-low zone's bottom, even if that breakout does not qualify; otherwise remove it after its expiry bar.
Calculation: Breakout targets = For each qualified breakout, set the reference price to the breakout close and target distances to one-third, two-thirds, and all of the broken zone's height. Add the distances for an upward breakout and subtract them for a downward breakout. Start checking targets on the next bar. The initial expiry is the breakout bar plus the target expiry period, inclusive. When a previously unreached first or second target is reached, reset expiry to the hit bar plus the target expiry period. A wick can reach multiple targets on the same bar. Stop tracking after the third target is reached or expiry passes.
Long: A pivot-low zone is created.
Short: A pivot-high zone is created.
Long: An active pivot-high zone exists AND close > zone top AND high > low AND max(0, high - max(low, zone top)) / (high - low) >= minimum breakout fraction.
Short: An active pivot-low zone exists AND close < zone bottom AND high > low AND max(0, min(high, zone bottom) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum breakout fraction.
Exit: long, a target from an upward breakout is active AND high >= that previously unreached target.
Exit: short, a target from a downward breakout is active AND low <= that previously unreached target.
