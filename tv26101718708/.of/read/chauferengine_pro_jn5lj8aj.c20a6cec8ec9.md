Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each daily anchor; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Range = session opening-range high and low accumulated from the session's first bar until the configured opening-range duration ends, or afternoon-range high and low accumulated throughout the configured afternoon window. A range becomes locked on the first bar after its accumulation window. The opening range is active while locked and in session until the enabled afternoon range locks; thereafter the afternoon range is active while in session. Maintain separate breakout and reversion state for each range across sessions.
Calculation: Bullish confluence = the enabled tests among close > session VWAP, volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier, RSI < overbought threshold AND RSI > previous RSI, and ADX >= threshold. Bearish confluence substitutes close < session VWAP and RSI > oversold threshold AND RSI <= previous RSI; volume and ADX tests are unchanged. Full confluence means every enabled test passes; partial confluence means at most one enabled test fails.
Calculation: Range breakout state = on an active range's close crossing above its high or below its low, arm that direction, cancel the opposite direction, and store the crossing bar's high or low and index. Cancel an armed upward breakout when close < range high, or an armed downward breakout when close > range low, unless it confirms on that bar. On a later qualifying confirmation with required confluence and cooldown satisfied, consume the armed state even if the grade filter suppresses the signal. The cooldown is shared by both ranges and both directions and counts from the last emitted breakout.
Calculation: Range reversion state = for each active range, a bearish setup is a bearish candle whose high is within a configurable fraction of the range width on either side of the range high; a bullish setup is a bullish candle whose low is within the same-sized band around the range low. Store the latest qualifying candle's low or high and its bar index; both directions share the latest setup bar index. After a signal, prevent another setup in that direction until close > range high for bearish reversions or close < range low for bullish reversions. Pending setups persist until signaled and are overwritten by later qualifying setups.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest against configurable numbers of bars to its left and right, recognized only after the right-side bars complete. Retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind.
Calculation: EMA continuation state = use short-, medium-, and long-length EMAs. A bullish trend requires short EMA > medium EMA AND close > long EMA, optionally also medium EMA > long EMA; a bearish trend reverses these comparisons. During a bullish trend, each bar with low <= short EMA and an available pivot high arms a long and replaces its level with the latest confirmed pivot high; during a bearish trend, high >= short EMA similarly arms a short at the latest confirmed pivot low. Disarm when the corresponding trend ends or its signal fires. Arming and signaling may occur on the same bar.
Calculation: VWAP reversal state = maintain independent bullish and bearish stages and counters on every bar. Band = ATR × proximity multiplier. First invalidate the bullish setup if close < VWAP - band, or the bearish setup if close > VWAP + band. Increment an active stage's counter and reset it if the counter exceeds the scope. In reversal stage, a bullish candle closing above the stored bullish reference, or a bearish candle closing below the bearish reference, creates a raw signal if its optional volume and RSI filters pass; reset that stage. Otherwise, a candle of the setup direction updates the reference to the more extreme of its close and the existing reference and resets the counter, even if a filter blocked confirmation. In touched stage, a candle of the setup direction starts reversal stage with its close as reference and resets the counter. Finally, an idle bullish setup is touched when low <= VWAP + band AND close > VWAP - band; an idle bearish setup is touched when high >= VWAP - band AND close < VWAP + band. A touch candle of the setup direction immediately starts reversal stage. These steps occur in this order, allowing a raw signal to be followed by a new touch on the same bar. A raw signal consumes its setup even if the session or same-direction signal-spacing filter subsequently suppresses it.
Calculation: Sweep levels = previous completed higher-timeframe daily high, low and close; premarket high and low accumulated through the current bar and retained after the premarket window; locked opening- and afternoon-range edges; weekly high and low from the last completed higher-timeframe weekly bar on Monday and otherwise from the higher-timeframe weekly request with lookahead off, using the last completed weekly bar on historical chart bars until the weekly bar closes; and equal-high or equal-low pools. An equal-high pool is the higher of two consecutive confirmed pivot highs whose difference is <= an ATR-based or close-percentage tolerance measured at confirmation; an equal-low pool is the lower of two such pivot lows. Retain each pool until replaced or close crosses through it; clear it before evaluating sweeps on that bar.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe momentum = for each enabled selected timeframe, calculate RSI and MACD with a shorter fast EMA than slow EMA, plus MACD signal EMA and ATR. Histogram = MACD line - signal line. Bullish bias is RSI >= midpoint + momentum band AND histogram > 0; bearish bias is RSI <= midpoint - momentum band AND histogram < 0. Bullish quality additionally requires MACD line > 0 AND absolute MACD line / ATR >= strength threshold AND histogram > previous histogram; bearish quality requires MACD line < 0 AND absolute MACD line / ATR >= strength threshold AND histogram < previous histogram. For a selected timeframe higher than the chart, use its last completed bar with lookahead off on historical bars; for a lower timeframe, use the last available lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar. A selected timeframe may also equal the chart timeframe.
Long: Active range AND an upward breakout is armed AND current bar is later than the crossing bar AND close > open AND, if enabled, close > crossing bar high AND required full confluence passes if enabled AND shared breakout cooldown passes if enabled AND partial confluence passes if grade-C suppression is enabled.
Short: Active range AND a downward breakout is armed AND current bar is later than the crossing bar AND close < open AND, if enabled, close < crossing bar low AND required full confluence passes if enabled AND shared breakout cooldown passes if enabled AND partial confluence passes if grade-C suppression is enabled.
Long: Active range AND bullish reversion is pending AND current bar is later than the shared latest reversion setup bar AND close > open AND close > stored bullish setup high AND neither breakout direction signals for that range on this bar.
Short: Active range AND bearish reversion is pending AND current bar is later than the shared latest reversion setup bar AND close < open AND close < stored bearish setup low AND neither breakout direction signals for that range on this bar.
Long: Bullish EMA continuation is armed AND close > its stored pivot high AND, if enabled, close > session VWAP AND, if enabled, current bar is in session.
Short: Bearish EMA continuation is armed AND close < its stored pivot low AND, if enabled, close < session VWAP AND, if enabled, current bar is in session.
Long: Bullish VWAP reversal raw signal AND, if enabled, current bar is in session AND bars since the last emitted bullish VWAP reversal signal > configured minimum spacing. The optional raw-signal filters are volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier and RSI < overbought threshold AND RSI > previous RSI.
Short: Bearish VWAP reversal raw signal AND, if enabled, current bar is in session AND bars since the last emitted bearish VWAP reversal signal > configured minimum spacing. The optional raw-signal filters are volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier and RSI > oversold threshold AND RSI <= previous RSI.
Long: If enabled, current bar is in session AND low < any enabled available sweep low or previous daily close AND min(open, close) > that level; range edges require their ranges to be locked and afternoon edges additionally require the afternoon range to be enabled.
Short: If enabled, current bar is in session AND high > any enabled available sweep high or previous daily close AND max(open, close) < that level; range edges require their ranges to be locked and afternoon edges additionally require the afternoon range to be enabled.
Long: Selected trigger timeframe has bullish quality AND the count of enabled timeframes with bullish bias > the count with bearish bias AND the bullish count >= the alignment threshold AND current bar is in session.
Short: Selected trigger timeframe has bearish quality AND the count of enabled timeframes with bearish bias > the count with bullish bias AND the bearish count >= the alignment threshold AND current bar is in session.
Long: Any enabled selected timeframe has bullish bias.
Short: Any enabled selected timeframe has bearish bias.
Long: Any enabled selected timeframe's MACD histogram > 0.
Short: Any enabled selected timeframe's MACD histogram <= 0.
Long: The count of enabled timeframes with bullish bias > the count with bearish bias.
Short: The count of enabled timeframes with bearish bias > the count with bullish bias.
