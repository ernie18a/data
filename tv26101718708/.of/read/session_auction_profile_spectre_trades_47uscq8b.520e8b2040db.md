Calculation: Developing session profile = During each selected session, retain the most recent configured number of chart bars, while tracking the high and low of the entire session. Divide the range from the session low into a configured number of equal rows, using the greater of the session range and the configured minimum row height times the row count. Allocate each retained bar's volume to rows in proportion to the overlap of its high-low range with each row. If its range is at most one tenth of the configured minimum price increment, allocate all its volume to the row containing its midpoint, clamped to the profile. The point of control is the midpoint of the row with the most volume, choosing the lowest row on ties. Starting at that row, add the adjacent row with more volume until the configured fraction of total volume is included, choosing the upper row on ties. Value-area high and low are the upper and lower edges of the included rows; value-area midpoint is their average. Recalculate on each session bar and retain the last calculated values outside the session; reset them at the next session start.
Long: Inside the selected session AND developing point of control > its value at the configured number of chart bars earlier + the configured minimum price increment, OR inside the selected session AND developing value-area midpoint > its value at the configured number of chart bars earlier + the configured minimum price increment, OR inside the selected session AND close > developing value-area midpoint; use the selected migration calculation.
Short: Inside the selected session AND developing point of control < its value at the configured number of chart bars earlier - the configured minimum price increment, OR inside the selected session AND developing value-area midpoint < its value at the configured number of chart bars earlier - the configured minimum price increment, OR inside the selected session AND close < developing value-area midpoint; use the selected migration calculation.
