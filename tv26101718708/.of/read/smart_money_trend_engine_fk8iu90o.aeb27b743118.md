Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = the high or low of a bar whose pivot is confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side; use independent lookbacks for market structure and order blocks.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = a newly confirmed market-structure pivot high is greater than the preceding confirmed market-structure pivot high. Bearish structure break = a newly confirmed market-structure pivot low is less than the preceding confirmed market-structure pivot low.
Calculation: Structural direction = bearish initially; set to bullish on a bullish structure break and to bearish on a bearish structure break, with bearish taking precedence if both occur on the same bar.
Long: Structural direction = bullish OR a new order-block pivot low is confirmed OR (current low > high two bars ago AND (current low - high two bars ago) / current close >= minimum gap fraction) OR (previous low < lowest low over the lookback ending on the previous bar AND current close > that lowest low AND previous close < that lowest low)
Short: Structural direction = bearish OR a new order-block pivot high is confirmed OR (current high < low two bars ago AND (low two bars ago - current high) / current close >= minimum gap fraction) OR (previous high > highest high over the lookback ending on the previous bar AND current close < that highest high AND previous close > that highest high)
