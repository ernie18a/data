Calculation: Moving averages 1, 2, and 5 = independently selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, HMA, VWMA, DEMA, TEMA, TMA, LSMA, ALMA, or rolling median of an independently selected OHLC-derived source and lookback. Their default lookbacks increase from 1 to 2 to 5, but are independently configurable. Each uses the chart timeframe or an independently selected higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar in historical data and the forming bar in live data; optionally use the preceding higher-timeframe bar instead. For a lower timeframe, use the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. The optional preceding-bar setting also shifts chart- and lower-timeframe calculations by one bar of their respective timeframe.
Long: Close crosses above moving average 1 OR close crosses above moving average 2 OR moving average 1 crosses above moving average 2 OR moving average 2 crosses above moving average 5.
Short: Close crosses below moving average 1 OR close crosses below moving average 2 OR moving average 1 crosses below moving average 2 OR moving average 2 crosses below moving average 5.
