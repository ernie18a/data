Calculation: Dashboard SMA state = compare the shorter-lookback SMA with the longer-lookback SMA of the selected price source on each selected timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use either the forming bar or the last completed bar, according to the selected mode; for a lower timeframe, use the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar in forming mode or its preceding bar in completed mode. For the chart timeframe, use the current bar in forming mode or the preceding bar in completed mode.
Calculation: Bull count and bear count = counts of selected timeframes whose shorter-lookback SMA is respectively above or below their longer-lookback SMA.
Calculation: Bollinger bands = selected moving average of the selected price source plus or minus a multiplier times the population standard deviation of that source over the same lookback.
Long: Dashboard SMA state has shorter-lookback SMA > longer-lookback SMA on any selected timeframe.
Short: Dashboard SMA state has shorter-lookback SMA < longer-lookback SMA on any selected timeframe.
Long: Bull count > bear count.
Short: Bear count > bull count.
Long: Low < previous low AND previous low - low >= minimum sweep-depth multiplier × ATR AND close > max(previous open, previous close) AND high - low >= minimum range multiplier × ATR AND high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction AND min(open, close) - low >= minimum wick-to-body ratio × abs(close - open) AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= minimum close-location fraction AND (direction filter disabled OR close > open) AND (trend filter disabled OR with-trend mode AND close > SMA(close) OR counter-trend mode AND close < SMA(close)) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR low < lower Bollinger band).
Short: High > previous high AND high - previous high >= minimum sweep-depth multiplier × ATR AND close < min(previous open, previous close) AND high - low >= minimum range multiplier × ATR AND high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction AND high - max(open, close) >= minimum wick-to-body ratio × abs(close - open) AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= minimum close-location fraction AND (direction filter disabled OR close < open) AND (trend filter disabled OR with-trend mode AND close < SMA(close) OR counter-trend mode AND close > SMA(close)) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR high > upper Bollinger band).
Long: The preceding bar satisfies the long liquidity-sweep condition above AND low > high two bars ago.
Short: The preceding bar satisfies the short liquidity-sweep condition above AND high < low two bars ago.
