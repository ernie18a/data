Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at the entry bar close multiplied by the stop-loss ATR multiplier; keep this distance fixed until the next Supertrend flip.
Long: Supertrend changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Supertrend changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - risk distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + risk distance.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + risk distance multiplied by any of the three independently configurable take-profit multiples AND that target has not previously been hit since the latest Supertrend flip AND the stop loss has not been hit on an earlier bar since that flip.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - risk distance multiplied by any of the three independently configurable take-profit multiples AND that target has not previously been hit since the latest Supertrend flip AND the stop loss has not been hit on an earlier bar since that flip.
