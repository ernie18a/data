Calculation: Leg pivots = For each lookback, initialize the leg as bearish. If the high that many bars ago exceeds the highest high of all subsequent bars through the current bar, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low that many bars ago is below the lowest low of those subsequent bars, set it bullish. A change to bearish confirms a high pivot at the earlier bar; a change to bullish confirms a low pivot there. Track the latest high and low separately and reset their crossed flags when replaced. Use a shorter lookback for internal pivots and a longer lookback for swing pivots.
Calculation: Equal-level pivots = Apply the leg-pivot procedure with an equal-level lookback, retaining the previous and latest pivot of each side.
Calculation: Gap threshold = A multiplier times the cumulative sum, through the current chart bar, of the absolute value of (previous close minus previous open) divided by previous open and a scaling constant, divided by the current zero-based bar index.
Calculation: Large bullish candle = Close > open AND absolute candle body > its moving average AND both upper and lower wick lengths < a fraction of the body. A large bearish candle uses the same body and wick tests AND close < open.
Calculation: Zigzag = Confirm a pivot high or low one bar after the candidate, using a longer left-side pivot lookback. Keep alternating high and low pivots; replace the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same side. For structure decisions use the most recent high and low excluding the latest zigzag pivot. Initialize structure direction to neutral; set it bullish or bearish on the corresponding structure shift. Track the level of the latest shift and the latest continuation break for each direction.
Calculation: Order-block pivots = Apply the leg-pivot procedure with a separate lookback, retaining the latest high and low and whether each has been crossed.
Calculation: Order blocks = On the first close above an uncrossed order-block high pivot, create a bullish block from the candle with the lowest body bottom between the pivot and current bar, excluding both endpoints; its bounds are that candle's body bottom and top. On the first close below an uncrossed low pivot, create a bearish block from the candle with the highest body top in the same interval. Mark the pivot crossed. A bullish block becomes a breaker when the lower of open and close falls below its bottom, and is removed on a later bar when close exceeds its top. A bearish block becomes a breaker when the higher of open and close exceeds its top, and is removed on a later bar when close falls below its bottom. Retain blocks newest first.
Long: Close crosses above the latest uncrossed swing high pivot.
Short: Close crosses below the latest uncrossed swing low pivot.
Long: Close crosses above the latest uncrossed internal high pivot AND that pivot differs from the latest swing high pivot.
Short: Close crosses below the latest uncrossed internal low pivot AND that pivot differs from the latest swing low pivot.
Long: A new equal-level low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding equal-level low pivot < a fraction of ATR.
Short: A new equal-level high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding equal-level high pivot < a fraction of ATR.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND (previous close - previous open) / (previous open times the scaling constant) > gap threshold.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND (previous open - previous close) / (previous open times the scaling constant) > gap threshold.
Long: The previous candle is a large bullish candle AND low > high two bars ago for a fair value gap OR low < high two bars ago for an implied fair value gap.
Short: The previous candle is a large bearish candle AND high < low two bars ago for a fair value gap OR high > low two bars ago for an implied fair value gap.
Long: Zigzag structure direction is neutral or bearish AND close > the selected zigzag high.
Short: Zigzag structure direction is neutral or bullish AND close < the selected zigzag low AND the bullish structure-shift condition is false.
Long: Zigzag structure direction is bullish AND close > the selected zigzag high AND that high differs from both the latest bullish shift level and latest bullish continuation-break level.
Short: Zigzag structure direction is bearish AND close < the selected zigzag low AND that low differs from both the latest bearish shift level and latest bearish continuation-break level.
Long: Close > the latest uncrossed order-block high pivot.
Short: Close < the latest uncrossed order-block low pivot.
Short: The newest bullish order block is a breaker AND close <= its top AND the latest order-block high pivot is strictly inside its bounds AND this condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: The newest bearish order block is a breaker AND close >= its bottom AND the latest order-block low pivot is strictly inside its bounds AND this condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: Close > short-lookback EMA of close OR close > long-lookback EMA of close.
Short: Close <= short-lookback EMA of close OR close <= long-lookback EMA of close.
