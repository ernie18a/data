Calculation: Long-lookback pivot high/low = a high/low N bars ago that is higher/lower than every high/low in the following N bars; use a longer lookback for swing pivots and a shorter one for internal pivots.
Calculation: Short-lookback confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that exceeds/is below the highs/lows on the specified left and right bars; the right-side confirmation bar must be complete.
Calculation: Large bullish/bearish body = candle body exceeds the recent mean body, occupies all but a small fraction of the candle range, and closes above/below its open.
Long: Close crosses above a swing pivot high OR close crosses above an internal pivot high different from the swing pivot high OR close is above the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot high while structure direction is bearish (MSS) OR structure direction is bullish AND close is above the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot high (BOS) OR close crosses above an un-crossed long-lookback pivot high and a bullish order block is formed OR the previous bar is a large bullish body and current low > high two bars ago OR the previous completed bar on the chart timeframe is bullish, its close > high two bars ago, current low > high two bars ago, and its percentage change exceeds the adaptive gap threshold OR a bearish order block has been broken and its breaker confirmation becomes true.
Short: Close crosses below a swing pivot low OR close crosses below an internal pivot low different from the swing pivot low OR close is below the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot low while structure direction is bullish (MSS) OR structure direction is bearish AND close is below the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot low (BOS) OR close crosses below an un-crossed long-lookback pivot low and a bearish order block is formed OR the previous bar is a large bearish body and current high < low two bars ago OR the previous completed bar on the chart timeframe is bearish, its close < low two bars ago, current high < low two bars ago, and its percentage change magnitude exceeds the adaptive gap threshold OR a bullish order block has been broken and its breaker confirmation becomes true.
