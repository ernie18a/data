Long: high < previous high AND low > previous low AND close >= open
Short: high < previous high AND low > previous low AND close < open
Long: at least one bullish inside bar occurs from the current bar through the configured lookback bar inclusive AND the configured breakout source > the high of the bar at that lookback AND the configured EMA source > its EMA AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > its threshold) AND (start date filter is disabled OR bar datetime > start datetime) AND (end date filter is disabled OR bar datetime < end datetime) AND long trading is enabled AND position side is not long
Short: at least one bearish inside bar occurs from the current bar through the configured lookback bar inclusive AND the configured breakout source < the low of the bar at that lookback AND the configured EMA source < its EMA AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX > its threshold) AND (start date filter is disabled OR bar datetime > start datetime) AND (end date filter is disabled OR bar datetime < end datetime) AND short trading is enabled AND position side is not short
Exit: long, close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) AND (start date filter is disabled OR bar datetime > start datetime) AND (end date filter is disabled OR bar datetime < end datetime)
Exit: short, close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) AND (start date filter is disabled OR bar datetime > start datetime) AND (end date filter is disabled OR bar datetime < end datetime)
Exit: long, high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage); apply each of four independently configured, progressively higher targets to its configured portion of the position, with the last target closing the remainder
Exit: short, low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage); apply each of four independently configured, progressively higher targets to its configured portion of the position, with the last target closing the remainder
