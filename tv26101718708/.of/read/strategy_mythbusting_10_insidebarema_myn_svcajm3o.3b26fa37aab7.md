Calculation: Inside bar = current bar high < previous bar high AND current bar low > previous bar low
Long: A bullish inside bar occurred on the current bar or one of the previous N bars AND close > high N bars ago AND close > EMA AND ADX > threshold when the ADX filter is enabled
Short: A bearish inside bar occurred on the current bar or one of the previous N bars AND close < low N bars ago AND close < EMA AND ADX > threshold when the ADX filter is enabled
Exit: long, close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + any take-profit target percentage)
Exit: short, close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − any take-profit target percentage)
