Calculation: Swing high / swing low = most recent confirmed pivot high / pivot low on chart-timeframe bars (same number of bars required on the left and the right; confirmed only after the right-side bars complete)
Calculation: Displacement up = close > open AND (close - open) >= ATR * multiplier AND close > highest high of the previous K chart bars (excluding the current bar). Displacement down = close < open AND (open - close) >= ATR * multiplier AND close < lowest low of the previous K chart bars (excluding the current bar)
Calculation: Narrative = persistent bull/bear state, initially unset, recomputed on every chart bar in this order. (1) Using the higher-timeframe bar last completed (Hc) and the completed higher-timeframe bar before it (Hp): if Hc close > Hp high then bull; else if Hc close < Hp low then bear. (2) If still unset: bull if chart close >= midpoint of (highest high and lowest low over the last N completed higher-timeframe bars), else bear. (3) Using the state after step 2, switch to bull if (state is bear AND close > latest swing high) OR (state is bear AND displacement up); switch to bear if (state is bull AND close < latest swing low) OR (state is bull AND displacement down)
Long: Narrative is bull after the update on the current bar
Short: Narrative is bear after the update on the current bar
