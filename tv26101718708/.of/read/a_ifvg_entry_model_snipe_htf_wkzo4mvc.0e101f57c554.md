Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low and close > that swing low; bearish liquidity sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high and close < that swing high. Swing highs/lows use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open and |close − open| > ATR × displacement multiplier; bearish displacement = close < open and |close − open| > ATR × displacement multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low[1] > high[2]; bearish FVG = high[1] < low[2]. Bullish IFVG = bullish FVG OR close > high[1]; bearish IFVG = bearish FVG OR close < low[1].
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish sweep, displacement, or IFVG = the latest occurrence was within the setup window.
Calculation: Protected low/high = the lowest low/highest high since the latest same-direction sweep while it remains within the setup window. Long stop = protected low − ATR × stop buffer; short stop = protected high + ATR × stop buffer. If no protected level exists, use current low minus/current high plus the same ATR buffer.
Calculation: Normal long/short target = highest high/lowest low over the shorter target lookback; runner target = highest high/lowest low over the longer target lookback.
Calculation: Bullish HTF bias = higher-timeframe close > its 50-bar EMA; bearish HTF bias = higher-timeframe close < its 50-bar EMA. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: bullish liquidity sweep OR (recent bullish sweep AND recent bullish displacement AND recent bullish IFVG AND close > open AND close > high[1] AND (snipe conditions OR runner conditions)), where snipe conditions require positive long risk, normal long target − close > long risk × minimum reward-to-risk, and no snipe long within the cooldown; runner conditions require bullish HTF bias, positive long risk, runner target − close > long risk × minimum reward-to-risk, and no runner long within the cooldown.
Short: bearish liquidity sweep OR (recent bearish sweep AND recent bearish displacement AND recent bearish IFVG AND close < open AND close < low[1] AND (snipe conditions OR runner conditions)), where snipe conditions require positive short risk, close − normal short target > short risk × minimum reward-to-risk, and no snipe short within the cooldown; runner conditions require bearish HTF bias, positive short risk, close − runner target > short risk × minimum reward-to-risk, and no runner short within the cooldown.
