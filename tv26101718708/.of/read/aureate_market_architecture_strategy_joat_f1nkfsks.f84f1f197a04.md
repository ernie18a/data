Calculation: Regime state = ATR / SMA(ATR) and (highest high − lowest low) / (ATR × balance lookback): candidate accumulation if volatility ratio < accumulation threshold AND balance ratio < 1.25; else candidate expansion if volatility ratio > expansion threshold AND absolute pressure signal > 0.65 × pressure volatility × pressure threshold multiplier; otherwise candidate exhaustion. Change the state to a different candidate only after that candidate persists for the configured number of confirmed bars; initialize state as accumulation. Pressure volatility = standard deviation of pressure signal over a lookback; pressure signal = EMA of the equal-weight average of four ROC values, using successively longer lookbacks (fast, medium, slow, macro). Pressure upper threshold = pressure volatility × threshold multiplier; pressure lower threshold = its negative.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish after a confirmed close above the latest confirmed medium-term pivot high; bearish after a confirmed close below the latest confirmed medium-term pivot low; otherwise retain prior bias, initially neutral. Pivots are confirmed using bars on both sides of the pivot.
Calculation: Liquidity anchors = within the liquidity lookback, upper anchor is the lowest high among bars whose high is at or above the highest body high; lower anchor is the highest low among bars whose low is at or below the lowest body low. Upper touches count bars with high >= upper anchor; lower touches count bars with low <= lower anchor. Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume).
Calculation: Bullish liquidity = lower touches + 1 >= upper touches AND (a confirmed bar sweeps below lower anchor by more than sweep-depth ATR and closes above the anchor OR pressure signal > 0.25 × pressure lower threshold OR close > fast EMA) AND relative volume >= volume floor. Bearish liquidity = upper touches + 1 >= lower touches AND (a confirmed bar sweeps above upper anchor by more than sweep-depth ATR and closes below the anchor OR pressure signal < 0.25 × pressure upper threshold OR close < fast EMA) AND relative volume >= volume floor.
Calculation: Initial long stop = the lower of (latest confirmed short-term pivot low − 0.25 × ATR) and (entry price − stop ATR multiple × ATR); if no short-term pivot low exists, use the latter. Initial short stop = the higher of (latest confirmed short-term pivot high + 0.25 × ATR) and (entry price + stop ATR multiple × ATR); if no short-term pivot high exists, use the latter. Risk distance = distance from entry price to initial stop. Long target = entry price + risk distance × target R multiple; short target = entry price − risk distance × target R multiple.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = maximum of its initial value (entry price − trail ATR multiple × ATR) and each subsequent close − trail ATR multiple × ATR. Short trailing stop = minimum of its initial value (entry price + trail ATR multiple × ATR) and each subsequent close + trail ATR multiple × ATR.
Calculation: Optional higher-timeframe bias = compare open and close of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe: bullish if close > open, bearish if close < open, neutral if equal.
Long: confirmed bar AND regime state != exhaustion AND bullish structure bias AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias >= neutral) AND pressure signal > 0.60 × pressure upper threshold AND bullish liquidity AND flat position AND daily drawdown < daily loss limit AND cooldown = 0.
Short: confirmed bar AND regime state != exhaustion AND bearish structure bias AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias <= neutral) AND pressure signal < 0.60 × pressure lower threshold AND bearish liquidity AND flat position AND daily drawdown < daily loss limit AND cooldown = 0.
Exit: long, low <= initial stop OR high >= target OR (confirmed bar AND (close < long trailing stop OR regime state = exhaustion OR bearish structure break)).
Exit: short, high >= initial stop OR low <= target OR (confirmed bar AND (close > short trailing stop OR regime state = exhaustion OR bullish structure break)).
