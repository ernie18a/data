Calculation: ATR = Wilder-smoothed true range; use the same ATR throughout.
Calculation: Pressure = EMA of the mean of close ROC over four lookbacks ordered fast, medium, slow, and macro. Pressure threshold = a positive multiplier times the standard deviation of pressure over its own lookback, floored at a positive price increment.
Calculation: Regime = persistent candidate state, initialized to accumulation. On each completed chart bar, retain the current state until a different candidate occurs for the required consecutive bars, then adopt it. Candidate is accumulation if ATR / max(SMA of ATR, positive price increment) is below the accumulation threshold AND (highest high minus lowest low over the balance lookback) / max(ATR times the balance lookback, positive price increment) is below the balance threshold; otherwise it is expansion if the ATR ratio is above the expansion threshold AND absolute pressure is above a positive fraction of the pressure threshold; otherwise it is exhaustion.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low identified using equal numbers of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. Structure bias starts neutral and becomes bullish whenever close is above the latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot high, or bearish whenever close is below the latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot low; retain the last bias otherwise, with bearish taking precedence if both occur. Short-lookback pivots use a shorter lookback than medium-lookback pivots.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish if the last completed higher-timeframe bar closes above its open, bearish if it closes below its open, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Bias score = positive or negative EMA weight according to whether fast EMA is above slow EMA, plus bullish, bearish, or zero structure weight according to structure bias, plus positive or negative price weight according to whether close is above fast EMA; when the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled, add bullish, bearish, or zero higher-timeframe weight according to higher-timeframe bias. Bullish and bearish weights have equal magnitudes and opposite signs; EMA weight is greater than structure weight, which is greater than price weight, and higher-timeframe weight is between structure and price weights.
Calculation: Upper liquidity anchor = the lowest high in the liquidity window that is at least the highest candle-body high in that window. Lower liquidity anchor = the highest low in that window that is at most the lowest candle-body low in that window. Upper touches count highs at or above the upper anchor; lower touches count lows at or below the lower anchor. The window includes the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity = lower touches plus one >= upper touches AND (low < lower anchor minus ATR times sweep multiplier AND close > lower anchor OR pressure > negative pressure threshold times a positive fraction OR close > fast EMA) AND volume / max(SMA of volume, positive volume floor) >= relative-volume threshold. Bearish liquidity = upper touches plus one >= lower touches AND (high > upper anchor plus ATR times sweep multiplier AND close < upper anchor OR pressure < pressure threshold times a positive fraction OR close < fast EMA) AND volume / max(SMA of volume, positive volume floor) >= relative-volume threshold.
Calculation: At a long entry signal, initial stop = min(latest confirmed short-lookback pivot low minus an ATR fraction, signal close minus ATR times stop multiplier), using the ATR stop if no pivot exists; target = signal close plus a target multiplier times max(signal close minus initial stop, positive price increment); trail starts at signal close minus ATR times trail multiplier and thereafter is the maximum of its prior value and close minus ATR times trail multiplier. At a short entry signal, initial stop = max(latest confirmed short-lookback pivot high plus an ATR fraction, signal close plus ATR times stop multiplier), using the ATR stop if no pivot exists; target = signal close minus a target multiplier times max(initial stop minus signal close, positive price increment); trail starts at signal close plus ATR times trail multiplier and thereafter is the minimum of its prior value and close plus ATR times trail multiplier.
Calculation: Cooldown starts after a dynamic exit signal and decreases by one on each subsequent completed chart bar until zero; stop or target exits do not start it.
Long: On a completed chart bar, position is flat AND cooldown = 0 AND regime is accumulation or expansion AND structure bias is bullish AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bullish or neutral) AND pressure > pressure threshold times a positive entry fraction AND bullish liquidity.
Short: On a completed chart bar, position is flat AND cooldown = 0 AND regime is accumulation or expansion AND structure bias is bearish AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bearish or neutral) AND pressure < negative pressure threshold times a positive entry fraction AND bearish liquidity.
Long: On a completed chart bar, structure bias is bullish AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bullish or neutral).
Short: On a completed chart bar, structure bias is bearish AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is bearish or neutral).
Long: Pressure >= 0.
Short: Pressure < 0.
Long: Bullish liquidity.
Short: Bearish liquidity AND bullish liquidity is false.
Long: Bias score > 0.
Short: Bias score < 0.
Exit: long, low <= initial stop OR high >= target OR on a completed chart bar (close < updated trail OR regime is exhaustion OR close < latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot low).
Exit: short, high >= initial stop OR low <= target OR on a completed chart bar (close > updated trail OR regime is exhaustion OR close > latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot high).
