Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive basis = KAMA of close using its own efficiency lookback and shorter and longer smoothing lengths; initialize to close and update by previous basis + (efficiency ratio × (short smoothing constant − long smoothing constant) + long smoothing constant)² × (close − previous basis), where each smoothing constant is 2 / (length + 1).
Calculation: Channel half-width = ATR × (base width + noise expansion × (1 − efficiency ratio clamped to [0, 1])); upper and lower rails are the adaptive basis plus and minus this width. Normalized basis slope = (basis − basis at the slope lookback) / ATR, or zero when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Bullish raw structure = close > adaptive basis AND normalized basis slope >= positive slope threshold AND efficiency ratio >= efficiency threshold. Bearish raw structure = close < adaptive basis AND normalized basis slope <= negative slope threshold AND efficiency ratio >= efficiency threshold.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral; from bullish, remain bullish until close is below the current lower rail for the required consecutive bars, then become bearish if bearish raw structure is true, otherwise neutral; from bearish, remain bearish until close is above the current upper rail for the required consecutive bars, then become bullish if bullish raw structure is true, otherwise neutral; from neutral, become bullish if bullish raw structure is true, otherwise bearish if bearish raw structure is true, otherwise remain neutral. Structure age counts consecutive bars in the current non-neutral state, starting at one.
Calculation: Pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using left and right lookbacks; store the three most recently confirmed pivots of each kind and their actual pivot bar indices. H1, H2, H3 and L1, L2, L3 denote newest through oldest highs and lows.
Calculation: Wedge boundaries = lines through H2 and H1 and through L2 and L1, projected to each bar. Wedge start is the later of the H2 and L2 bar indices; wedge age is current index minus start index; wedge width is upper boundary minus lower boundary. Ascending wedge = age <= maximum age AND H1 > H2 AND L1 > L2 AND upper slope > 0 AND lower slope > upper slope AND current width > minimum positive width AND start width > 0 AND current width <= start width × compression factor. Descending wedge = age <= maximum age AND H1 < H2 AND L1 < L2 AND lower slope < 0 AND upper slope < lower slope AND current width > minimum positive width AND start width > 0 AND current width <= start width × compression factor.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders candidate = H3, H2 and H1 occur in chronological order AND absolute (H3 − H1) / ATR <= shoulder tolerance AND H2 > H3 + ATR × head dominance AND H2 > H1 + ATR × head dominance AND structure state is bullish. Inverse candidate = L3, L2 and L1 occur in chronological order AND absolute (L3 − L1) / ATR <= shoulder tolerance AND L2 < L3 − ATR × head dominance AND L2 < L1 − ATR × head dominance AND structure state is bearish. Their respective necklines are (L1 + L2) / 2 and (H1 + H2) / 2.
Calculation: Flag range = highest high and lowest low over the flag lookback ending on the previous bar; pole base = lowest low over the pole lookback ending immediately before that flag window; pole = flag-range high − pole base. Bull flag candidate = bullish structure AND structure age >= trend confirmation bars AND pole >= ATR × pole threshold AND flag-range high − flag-range low <= pole × maximum retracement AND linear-regression slope of close over the flag lookback <= 0 AND close >= flag-range low AND close <= flag-range high.
Calculation: Cup rim = highest high over the cup lookback ending on the previous bar; cup base = lowest low over that lookback including the current bar; cup depth = rim − base. Handle high = highest high over the shorter handle lookback ending on the previous bar; handle low = lowest low over that lookback including the current bar. Cup-and-handle candidate = structure state is neutral or bullish AND cup depth >= ATR × minimum depth AND cup depth > 0 AND close >= rim − cup depth × rim tolerance AND (rim − handle low) / cup depth >= 0 AND (rim − handle low) / cup depth <= maximum handle fraction AND handle high >= rim − cup depth × rim tolerance.
Calculation: Pattern maturity = the number of consecutive bars for which the respective candidate is true. Each confirmed pattern break has its own cooldown measured from its last confirmed break; wedge breaks additionally require the current wedge start to be later than that break, and head-and-shoulders breaks require a different current head pivot from the last confirmed break of that type.
Long: Structure state is bullish.
Short: Structure state is bearish.
Long: Previous structure state is bearish AND close has been above the current upper rail for the required consecutive bars.
Short: Previous structure state is bullish AND close has been below the current lower rail for the required consecutive bars.
Long: Descending wedge candidate is true OR inverse head-and-shoulders candidate is true OR bull flag candidate is true OR cup-and-handle candidate is true.
Short: Ascending wedge candidate is true OR head-and-shoulders candidate is true.
Long: Descending wedge maturity on the previous bar >= required maturity AND close > current upper wedge boundary AND its cooldown has elapsed AND its wedge start is later than its previous confirmed break, if any.
Short: Ascending wedge maturity on the previous bar >= required maturity AND close < current lower wedge boundary AND its cooldown has elapsed AND its wedge start is later than its previous confirmed break, if any.
Long: Inverse head-and-shoulders maturity on the previous bar >= required maturity AND close crosses above the inverse neckline AND its cooldown has elapsed AND its head pivot differs from that of its previous confirmed break, if any.
Short: Head-and-shoulders maturity on the previous bar >= required maturity AND close crosses below the neckline AND its cooldown has elapsed AND its head pivot differs from that of its previous confirmed break, if any.
Long: Bull flag maturity on the previous bar >= required maturity AND close > current flag-range high AND its cooldown has elapsed.
Long: Cup-and-handle maturity on the previous bar >= required maturity AND close > current handle high AND its cooldown has elapsed.
