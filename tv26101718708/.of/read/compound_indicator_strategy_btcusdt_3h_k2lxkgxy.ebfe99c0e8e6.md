Calculation: Compound score = A cumulative score initialized at zero and updated on every chart bar. Use four ordered lookbacks from shortest to longest for each indicator family. For each of Chande momentum oscillator, smoothed stochastic %K, RSI, money flow index, relative volatility index, smoothed balance of power, SMA, and EMA, add one if any shorter-lookback member crosses above a longer-lookback member and subtract one if any crosses below; apply both adjustments if both occur. Use close as the default source, including for money flow index. For each lookback of RSI, money flow index, relative volatility index, and smoothed balance of power, add one if it crosses above any level in its lookback-specific grid and subtract one if it crosses below any level in that grid; for the shortest lookback, a crossing in either direction of either endpoint of the oscillator scale triggers both adjustments. The grids are evenly spaced and narrow around the scale midpoint as lookback increases. For Chande momentum oscillators, add one if any lookback crosses above any level in its positive lookback-specific grid and subtract one if any crosses below a level in its negative lookback-specific grid; the shortest lookback uses positive levels for both directions, and grid ranges narrow as lookback increases. Each of these two momentum adjustments occurs at most once per bar. Compute the volume oscillator as 100 times the difference between short and long volume averages divided by the long average, using either SMA or EMA for both. If it crosses a level in an ascending positive grid, add one for an upward cross on an up candle or a downward cross on a down candle, and subtract one for the opposite combinations; count at most one crossing per bar. For the three shortest stochastic lookbacks, compare each smoothed %K with an SMA of the separately selected stochastic %K, using the corresponding signal smoothing length: add one if any comparison is greater than or equal, and subtract one if any is false. Add one if parabolic SAR is at or below close, otherwise subtract one when it is above close. Add one for an upward supertrend, otherwise subtract one. If close is above the Bollinger middle band, add floor(close / upper band) plus one; if close is at or below the middle band, subtract floor(close / lower band) plus one. Add one if center of gravity of close is above its SMA, otherwise subtract one when it is at or below its SMA.
Calculation: Relative volatility index = 100 times the moving average of rolling standard deviation on bars where the source rises, divided by the sum of that average and the moving average of rolling standard deviation on bars where the source does not rise. Use zero for the excluded bars and the same lookback for standard deviation and either EMA or SMA smoothing.
Calculation: Smoothed balance of power = EMA or SMA of 100 times (close - open) / (high - low), plus the oscillator midpoint.
Calculation: Price covariance = Rounded population covariance of the compound score and (open + close) / 2 over a covariance lookback, divided by a scaling factor.
Calculation: High covariance = Rounded population covariance over the covariance lookback of the compound score and the rolling highest compound score divided by a scaling factor.
Calculation: Low covariance = Rounded population covariance over the covariance lookback of the compound score and the rolling lowest compound score divided by the same scaling factor.
Calculation: Covariance extreme = The rolling highest value of the corresponding high or low covariance when its current value is positive, otherwise its rolling lowest value; use a common extreme lookback.
Calculation: Falling price = The SMA of close is lower than every one of its values over the preceding trend lookback; rising price is defined symmetrically.
Calculation: Sell condition = The separately parameterized supertrend changes from upward to downward, OR it is downward AND rising price AND either (previous price covariance equals its previous rolling maximum AND current price covariance is below its current rolling maximum AND current price covariance is at least a positive cutoff) OR (previous high covariance equals its previous covariance extreme AND current high covariance is below its current covariance extreme AND current high covariance is at least a positive cutoff).
Long: Position side is flat AND falling price AND previous price covariance equals its previous rolling maximum AND current price covariance is below its current rolling maximum AND current price covariance is at least a positive cutoff AND sell condition is false.
Long: Position side is long AND SMA of close is below the average entry price by a specified fraction AND sell condition is false AND falling price AND ((previous price covariance equals its previous rolling maximum AND current price covariance is below its current rolling maximum AND current price covariance is at least a positive cutoff) OR (previous low covariance equals its previous covariance extreme AND ((current low covariance is below its current covariance extreme AND current low covariance is at least a positive cutoff) OR (current low covariance is above its current covariance extreme AND current low covariance is at most a negative cutoff))) OR (previous high covariance equals its previous covariance extreme AND current high covariance is below its current covariance extreme AND current high covariance is at least a positive cutoff)).
Exit: long, sell condition is true AND SMA of close is above the average entry price by the full-exit profit fraction.
Exit: long, sell condition is true AND SMA of close is above the most recent open entry price by the intermediate profit fraction AND SMA of close is below the average entry price by a specified fraction.
