Calculation: Moving-average ordering = compare close with the first enabled moving average, then each enabled moving average with the next in configured order; only the consecutive enabled averages starting with the first are used. Each average has independently chosen type, source, and length.
Calculation: Supertrend = calculate true range from the selected ordinary or Heikin Ashi high, low, and previous close; smooth it with either an SMA or Wilder average. Form lower and upper bands as the selected source minus and plus a multiple of the smoothed true range. Carry the lower band forward as the greater of its current value and its previous value when the previous close exceeds the previous lower band; otherwise use its current value. Carry the upper band forward as the lesser of its current value and its previous value when the previous close is below the previous upper band; otherwise use its current value. Initialize trend upward; switch downward when the selected close is below the previous lower band, and upward when it is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the trend.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K and D = K is an SMA of the stochastic oscillator of RSI; D is an SMA of K, with independently selected RSI, stochastic, K-smoothing, and D-smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Directional indicators and ADX = calculate true range and positive and negative directional movement from ordinary high, low, and previous close, substituting zero for unavailable previous values. Smooth each by current smoothed value = previous smoothed value minus previous smoothed value divided by the lookback plus current raw value, starting from zero. Positive and negative DI are their respective smoothed directional movements divided by smoothed true range. ADX is the SMA of the absolute DI difference divided by their sum, expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Signal-bar stop = for a long, signal close minus a configured percentage of signal close or a fixed price distance, signal low minus a multiple of ATR, or the latest confirmed pivot low; for a short, signal close plus the corresponding percentage or distance, signal high plus a multiple of ATR, or the latest confirmed pivot high. A pivot is confirmed when the high or low at the configured historical offset is the highest or lowest in the configured surrounding window. Capture the stop on the signal bar.
Calculation: Signal-bar target = for a long, signal close plus a configured percentage of signal close, a fixed price distance, or a multiple of the distance from signal close to the signal-bar stop; alternatively, signal high plus a multiple of ATR. For a short, subtract the corresponding percentage, distance, or stop-distance multiple from signal close, or use signal low minus a multiple of ATR. Capture the target on the signal bar.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize at the signal-bar stop; while a long is open and the combined long condition is false, raise it to the greater of its previous value and close minus the original signal-close-to-stop distance. While a short is open and the combined short condition is false, lower it to the lesser of its previous value and close plus the original stop-to-signal-close distance.
Long: Close and the consecutive configured moving averages are strictly descending in that order AND this ordering was false on the preceding bar.
Short: Close and the consecutive configured moving averages are strictly ascending in that order AND this ordering was false on the preceding bar.
Long: Supertrend switches from downward to upward.
Short: Supertrend switches from upward to downward.
Long: RSI is above its lower threshold AND previous RSI was below that threshold.
Short: RSI is below its upper threshold AND previous RSI was above that threshold.
Long: Stochastic RSI K crosses above D AND, if the optional restriction is enabled, K is at or below the lower threshold.
Short: Stochastic RSI K crosses below D AND, if the optional restriction is enabled, K is at or above the upper threshold.
Long: Positive DI is greater than negative DI.
Short: Positive DI is less than negative DI.
Long: Close is greater than or equal to parabolic SAR.
Short: Close is less than or equal to parabolic SAR.
Long: At least one indicator is enabled AND every enabled indicator configured as an entry satisfies its long entry condition AND every enabled indicator configured as a filter satisfies its long filter condition AND datetime is within the enabled start and end bounds, selected session, and selected weekdays AND position is flat or short with reversal enabled. The long entry conditions are the moving-average ordering transition, upward Supertrend switch, RSI lower-threshold recovery, restricted or unrestricted Stochastic RSI upward crossover, positive DI becoming greater than negative DI with optional ADX above threshold, and close becoming greater than or equal to parabolic SAR. The respective long filters are the current descending moving-average ordering, upward Supertrend, RSI below its lower threshold, K below its lower threshold AND D below its upper threshold, positive DI greater than negative DI with optional ADX above threshold or ADX above threshold alone, and close greater than or equal to parabolic SAR. A DI entry configured as ADX-threshold-only cannot trigger.
Short: At least one indicator is enabled AND every enabled indicator configured as an entry satisfies its short entry condition AND every enabled indicator configured as a filter satisfies its short filter condition AND datetime is within the enabled start and end bounds, selected session, and selected weekdays AND position is flat or long with reversal enabled. The short entry conditions are the moving-average ordering transition, downward Supertrend switch, RSI upper-threshold decline, restricted or unrestricted Stochastic RSI downward crossover, negative DI becoming greater than positive DI with optional ADX above threshold, and close becoming less than or equal to parabolic SAR. The respective short filters are the current ascending moving-average ordering, downward Supertrend, RSI above its upper threshold, K above its lower threshold AND D above its upper threshold, negative DI greater than positive DI with optional ADX above threshold or ADX above threshold alone, and close less than or equal to parabolic SAR. A DI entry configured as ADX-threshold-only cannot trigger.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below the enabled signal-bar stop or trailing stop OR high reaches or exceeds the enabled signal-bar target OR the combined short entry condition is satisfied with reversal enabled.
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds the enabled signal-bar stop or trailing stop OR low reaches or falls below the enabled signal-bar target OR the combined long entry condition is satisfied with reversal enabled.
