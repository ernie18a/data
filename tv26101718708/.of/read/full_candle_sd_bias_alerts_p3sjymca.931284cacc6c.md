Calculation: Smoothed stochastic K = Stochastic %K, smoothed with a moving average; smoothed stochastic D = smoothed stochastic K, smoothed with a moving average
Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low, requiring the configured number of bars on both sides
Calculation: Demand zone = from the bearish candle with the lowest low among the configured prior bars when price closes across the latest swing high from at or below it, with the close-to-swing distance exceeding a configured ATR multiple; zone bottom is that candle's low and zone top is its open in body mode or high otherwise
Calculation: Supply zone = from the bullish candle with the highest high among the configured prior bars when price closes across the latest swing low from at or above it, with the close-to-swing distance exceeding a configured ATR multiple; zone top is that candle's high and zone bottom is its open in body mode or low otherwise
Calculation: Directional bias on each of three requested timeframes = longer-period EMA of close above shorter-period EMA of close for bullish, below for bearish; use the last completed bar when the requested timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Bullish count = number of bullish timeframe biases plus one when smoothed stochastic K is above smoothed stochastic D; bearish count = number of bearish timeframe biases plus one when smoothed stochastic K is below smoothed stochastic D
Calculation: Demand rejection = bar overlaps demand zone, closes above its bottom, and closes above its open; strong demand rejection additionally closes above the zone top
Calculation: Supply rejection = bar overlaps supply zone, closes below its top, and closes below its open; strong supply rejection additionally closes below the zone bottom
Calculation: Recent large drop/rise = a bearish/bullish candle whose body exceeds a configured ATR multiple, on this bar or either of the prior two bars
Calculation: Safe to buy = smoothed stochastic K is not above the configured overbought level, is not falling while above the configured threshold, and there was no recent large rise; safe to sell = smoothed stochastic K is not below the configured oversold level, is not rising while below the configured threshold, and there was no recent large drop
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND (strong demand rejection AND all three timeframe biases are bullish AND smoothed stochastic K is above smoothed stochastic D AND safe to buy OR demand rejection AND bullish count equals three AND safe to buy OR demand rejection AND bearish count is at least three AND smoothed stochastic K is below the configured oversold level AND (smoothed stochastic K is rising while below the configured threshold OR smoothed stochastic K crosses above smoothed stochastic D))
Short: chart bar is confirmed AND (strong supply rejection AND all three timeframe biases are bearish AND smoothed stochastic K is below smoothed stochastic D AND safe to sell OR supply rejection AND bearish count equals three AND safe to sell OR supply rejection AND bullish count is at least three AND smoothed stochastic K is above the configured overbought level AND (smoothed stochastic K is falling while above the configured threshold OR smoothed stochastic K crosses below smoothed stochastic D))
