Calculation: Swing levels = the most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each requiring the same number of chart bars on both sides; update each level when its pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish break = close > latest swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND close - latest swing high > ATR × minimum break-strength multiplier. Bearish break = close < latest swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND latest swing low - close > ATR × minimum break-strength multiplier.
Calculation: Demand zone = on a bullish break, select the bearish candle with the lowest low in the preceding zone lookback, resolving equal lows in favor of the most recent candle; set the zone bottom to its low and its top to either its open or high. Supply zone = on a bearish break, select the bullish candle with the highest high in the preceding zone lookback, resolving equal highs in favor of the most recent candle; set the zone top to its high and its bottom to either its open or low. Retain the latest zone of each type until replaced; optionally remove demand when close < its bottom and supply when close > its top. If no qualifying candle exists, retain the previous zone.
Calculation: Stochastic K = SMA of the stochastic oscillator; stochastic D = SMA of K, with independently adjustable oscillator and smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = compare shorter-period and longer-period close EMAs on each of three distinct, ordered timeframes. Each timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe: on historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the chart bar itself for an equal timeframe, or the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; on a forming realtime chart bar use the latest available value, including the still-forming requested bar.
Calculation: Bullish count = number of the three timeframe biases with shorter EMA > longer EMA plus 1 if stochastic K > D. Bearish count = number of the three timeframe biases with shorter EMA < longer EMA plus 1 if stochastic K < D.
Calculation: Recent large rise = at least one of the current chart bar and the two preceding chart bars has close > open AND close - open > that bar's ATR × large-move multiplier. Recent large fall = at least one of those bars has close < open AND open - close > that bar's ATR × large-move multiplier.
Long: Shorter EMA > longer EMA on any one of the three bias timeframes.
Short: Shorter EMA < longer EMA on any one of the three bias timeframes.
Long: Stochastic K > D.
Short: Stochastic K < D.
Long: Bullish break AND at least one bearish candle exists in the preceding zone lookback.
Short: Bearish break AND at least one bullish candle exists in the preceding zone lookback.
Long: Low <= demand-zone top AND high >= demand-zone bottom AND close > open AND close > demand-zone top AND bullish count = 4 AND stochastic K <= overbought threshold AND (K >= previous K OR K <= upper turning threshold) AND recent large rise = false.
Long: Low <= demand-zone top AND high >= demand-zone bottom AND close > open AND close > demand-zone bottom AND bullish count = 3 AND stochastic K <= overbought threshold AND (K >= previous K OR K <= upper turning threshold) AND recent large rise = false.
Long: Low <= demand-zone top AND high >= demand-zone bottom AND close > open AND close > demand-zone bottom AND bearish count >= 3 AND stochastic K < oversold threshold AND ((K > previous K AND K < lower turning threshold) OR (K > D AND previous K <= previous D)).
Short: High >= supply-zone bottom AND low <= supply-zone top AND close < open AND close < supply-zone bottom AND bearish count = 4 AND stochastic K >= oversold threshold AND (K <= previous K OR K >= lower turning threshold) AND recent large fall = false.
Short: High >= supply-zone bottom AND low <= supply-zone top AND close < open AND close < supply-zone top AND bearish count = 3 AND stochastic K >= oversold threshold AND (K <= previous K OR K >= lower turning threshold) AND recent large fall = false.
Short: High >= supply-zone bottom AND low <= supply-zone top AND close < open AND close < supply-zone top AND bullish count >= 3 AND stochastic K > overbought threshold AND ((K < previous K AND K > upper turning threshold) OR (K < D AND previous K >= previous D)).
