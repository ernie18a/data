Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier AND previous bar close > previous bar open
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier AND previous bar close < previous bar open
Long: Bullish FVG
Long: (Bullish FVG OR (MACD line crosses above signal line AND RSI is below its low threshold)) AND volume > its moving average AND ADX > its threshold
Short: Bearish FVG
Short: (Bearish FVG OR (MACD line crosses below signal line AND RSI is above its high threshold)) AND volume > its moving average AND ADX > its threshold
Exit: Long, price falls to the stop-loss distance below entry price OR the trailing stop is activated and price falls to its trailing stop
Exit: Short, price rises to the stop-loss distance above entry price OR the trailing stop is activated and price rises to its trailing stop
