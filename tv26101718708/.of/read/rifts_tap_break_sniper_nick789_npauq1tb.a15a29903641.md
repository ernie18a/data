Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Zones = On each completed bar, create enabled bullish gaps [high two bars ago, current low] when low > high two bars ago, close > open, and body percentage >= the zone body threshold; bearish gaps [current high, low two bars ago] when high < low two bars ago, close < open, and body percentage >= the threshold; bullish reaction blocks [previous low, previous high] when close > previous high, close > open, previous close < previous open, and body percentage >= the threshold; bearish reaction blocks [previous low, previous high] when close < previous low, close < open, previous close > previous open, and body percentage >= the threshold. Reject a new zone if an active zone of the same direction and type overlaps it by more than 70% of the smaller zone's height. Insert gaps before reaction blocks and discard oldest zones when the storage limit is exceeded. A zone is tapped permanently once high >= its bottom AND low <= its top, including on its creation bar. Invalidate a bullish zone when close < its bottom and a bearish zone when close > its top. A tapped, active, unused zone reacts only once: a bullish zone when close > open AND close > its top, or a bearish zone when close < open AND close < its bottom, with body percentage >= the rejection threshold AND either the rejection range threshold is disabled or high − low >= ATR × that threshold. A reaction is recent through the configured number of bars after its reaction bar. For each direction, select the newest active unused zone satisfying the required tap, recent reaction, or new reaction. Mark the selected zone used after its sniper signal; used zones remain active for duplicate checks and invalidation but cannot produce another sniper signal.
Calculation: Structure breaks = Maintain independent classic, responsive, and major structures. Classic uses the latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and low, each with its own configurable pivot length; a bullish break requires the selected bullish source to cross above the latest pivot high and that pivot's price to differ from the last broken high price. A bearish break requires the selected bearish source to cross below the latest pivot low and that pivot's price to differ from the last broken low price. Responsive and major each use their own configurable lookback: if the high that many bars ago is strictly greater than every subsequent high through the current bar, set the leg to down; otherwise, if the low that many bars ago is strictly lower than every subsequent low through the current bar, set the leg to up. Only a leg change installs the corresponding old high or low as a new, unbroken level. Break that level on the first source crossover above a high or crossunder below a low. Major uses a longer lookback than responsive. The bullish source is selectable as close or high; the bearish source is selectable as close or low. A crossover compares both the current and previous source to the current level. Each structure maintains its own bias, initially neutral; a break against its previous bias is CHoCH, otherwise BOS, then the bias becomes the break direction. A selected structure break passes sniper confirmation when its BOS/CHoCH type is enabled and, if displacement is required, its candle has the break direction, body percentage >= the break body threshold, and high − low >= ATR × the break range threshold.
Calculation: Candle trend = Start bullish. The upper and lower bands are (high + low) / 2 plus and minus a configurable multiple of the SMA of high − low. While bullish, the stop is the greater of the lower band and the previous stop; while bearish, it is the lesser of the upper band and the previous stop. If close > previous stop AND previous close <= previous stop, set trend bullish and stop to the lower band; otherwise, if close < previous stop AND previous close >= previous stop, set trend bearish and stop to the upper band. Initialize a missing previous stop to the applicable current band.
Calculation: Sniper score = For each direction, add 35 for any recent reaction, otherwise 20 for any tapped active unused zone; add 30 for a passing selected structure break; add 20 for any new reaction, otherwise 10 for any recent reaction; add 10 when the volume filter is disabled or volume >= its SMA × the volume multiplier with a positive available SMA; add 5 when the EMA filter is disabled or close is on the direction's permitted side of the EMA. Cap the total at 100. The bullish EMA comparison is close >= EMA; the bearish comparison is close <= EMA.
Calculation: Sniper candidate = In reaction-only mode require a new reaction; in tap-and-structure mode require a tapped active unused zone AND a passing selected structure break; in reaction-and-structure mode require a recent reaction AND a passing selected structure break. Also require the directional score >= the selected normal or higher score threshold, the volume and directional EMA filters to pass when enabled, and a selected zone to exist. If opposite-zone blocking is enabled, reject the candidate when the current bar overlaps an active unused opposite zone of an enabled guard type AND, for a bullish candidate, close <= the opposite zone top + ATR × the clearance multiplier, or, for a bearish candidate, close >= the opposite zone bottom − ATR × the clearance multiplier. Evaluate both candidates before choosing a direction; if both qualify, choose the higher score, with ties going bullish.
Long: A bullish reaction block is created OR a bullish gap is created.
Short: A bearish reaction block is created OR a bearish gap is created.
Long: A bullish break occurs in any displayed classic, responsive, or major structure.
Short: A bearish break occurs in any displayed classic, responsive, or major structure.
Long: Candle trend = bullish when candle trend coloring is enabled.
Short: Candle trend = bearish when candle trend coloring is enabled.
Long: The bullish sniper candidate qualifies AND the bearish candidate does not qualify OR the bullish sniper candidate qualifies AND bullish score >= bearish score.
Short: The bearish sniper candidate qualifies AND the bullish candidate does not qualify OR the bearish sniper candidate qualifies AND bearish score > bullish score.
