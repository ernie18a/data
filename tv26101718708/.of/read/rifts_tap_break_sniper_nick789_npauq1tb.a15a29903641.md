Calculation: Bullish reaction block = a bearish prior bar followed by a bullish bar whose close is above the prior high and whose body is at least the configured percentage of its range; zone is the prior bar’s high-to-low range
Calculation: Bearish reaction block = a bullish prior bar followed by a bearish bar whose close is below the prior low and whose body is at least the configured percentage of its range; zone is the prior bar’s high-to-low range
Calculation: Bullish imbalance gap = current low > high two bars earlier, current close > open, and current body meets the configured body percentage; zone is from that earlier high to current low
Calculation: Bearish imbalance gap = current high < low two bars earlier, current close < open, and current body meets the configured body percentage; zone is from current high to that earlier low
Calculation: Zone tap = an active, unused zone’s range overlaps the current bar’s high-to-low range; a zone is invalidated if a bar closes beyond its far edge
Calculation: Bullish zone reaction = a tapped bullish zone, a bullish bar closing above the zone top, and body percentage and range relative to ATR meeting their configured minimums; bearish zone reaction is the inverse, closing below the zone bottom
Calculation: Recent reaction = a zone reaction within the configured number of bars; each zone can signal only once
Calculation: Bullish structure break = price crosses above an unbroken confirmed swing high; bearish structure break = price crosses below an unbroken confirmed swing low. Use the configured break source (close or wick). A break is CHoCH when opposite the prior break bias, otherwise BOS. The signal layer is selected from classic confirmed pivots, responsive pivots, or major pivots; responsive and major pivots occur when a bar’s high exceeds, or low falls below, the preceding bars over that layer’s lookback, with a pivot registered when the structure leg changes
Calculation: Bullish Sniper score = 35 for a recent bullish reaction, otherwise 20 for a bullish zone tap; plus 30 for a qualifying bullish structure break; plus 20 for a new bullish reaction, otherwise 10 for a recent bullish reaction; plus 10 if volume is at least its moving average times the configured multiplier; plus 5 if close is at or above the configured EMA. Cap at 100. Bearish score is the inverse, with close at or below the EMA
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish Sniper setup: selected mode is Reaction Only and a new bullish zone reaction occurs, or Tap + Structure and a bullish zone is tapped with a qualifying bullish structure break, or Reaction + Structure and a recent bullish reaction coincides with a qualifying bullish structure break; the bullish score meets the configured threshold, volume and EMA filters pass when enabled, the signal is confirmed if required, and when both sides qualify the bullish score is at least the bearish score
Short: bearish structure break OR bearish Sniper setup: selected mode is Reaction Only and a new bearish zone reaction occurs, or Tap + Structure and a bearish zone is tapped with a qualifying bearish structure break, or Reaction + Structure and a recent bearish reaction coincides with a qualifying bearish structure break; the bearish score meets the configured threshold, volume and EMA filters pass when enabled, the signal is confirmed if required, and when both sides qualify the bearish score exceeds the bullish score
