Calculation: Higher-timeframe close series = the last completed higher-timeframe close, repeated on each chart bar until the next higher-timeframe bar completes.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe close EMAs = short- and long-lookback EMAs calculated on the repeated higher-timeframe close series at the chart timeframe.
Calculation: RSI signal = SMA of chart-timeframe close RSI.
Calculation: Trading minute = minutes between a configured daily reference time and the chart bar's closing time.
Calculation: Entry reference price = the chart bar's open when the entry condition is met.
Calculation: Entry permissions = initially enabled; an entry disables the permission for its direction and the shared permission. After the configured cooldown measured from a direction's most recent entry, re-enable that direction's permission and the shared permission. On each bar, apply these resets after evaluating entries, then disable the shared permission during the configured opening and closing time windows.
Calculation: Exit orders = when an exit-order condition is met, place a persistent stop and limit order at the stated prices; evaluate their fills on subsequent bars using bar high and low, cancelling the other order when one fills. A close-based stop or the scheduled close also closes the position and cancels its orders.
Long: Bar time >= configured start time AND position is flat AND long and shared entry permissions are enabled AND short-lookback close EMA > long-lookback close EMA AND long-lookback higher-timeframe close EMA > short-lookback higher-timeframe close EMA AND RSI signal < configured threshold AND trading minute is in an enabled time window.
Short: Bar time >= configured start time AND position is flat AND short and shared entry permissions are enabled AND short-lookback close EMA < long-lookback close EMA AND long-lookback higher-timeframe close EMA < short-lookback higher-timeframe close EMA AND RSI signal > the configured threshold for the current enabled trading-minute window.
Long: Bar time >= configured start time AND position is flat or short AND long pin-bar trading is enabled AND high = open AND open >= close AND close - low >= configured multiple of open - close AND (high - low) / high >= configured minimum range fraction.
Short: Bar time >= configured start time AND position is flat or long AND short pin-bar trading is enabled AND close = low AND open <= close AND high - close >= configured multiple of close - open AND (high - low) / high >= configured minimum range fraction.
Exit: long, close < entry reference price * (1 - stop-loss fraction), using the updated stop price if it has been raised.
Exit: short, close > entry reference price * (1 + stop-loss fraction).
Exit: long, close >= entry reference price * (1 + configured larger profit fraction) AND (ADX <= configured threshold OR close < close at the configured lookback) AND no prior larger-profit trigger for this position; raise the close-based stop to entry reference price * (1 + take-profit fraction) and place stop and limit orders at that same price.
Exit: long, higher-timeframe close series < long-lookback higher-timeframe close EMA AND trading minute - entry trading minute > configured delay AND (ADX <= configured threshold OR close < close at the configured lookback) AND neither earlier long exit condition applies; place stop and limit orders at the current close-based stop and entry reference price * (1 + take-profit fraction).
Exit: short, close <= entry reference price * (1 - configured larger profit fraction) AND (ADX <= configured threshold OR close > close at the configured lookback) AND no prior larger-profit trigger for this position; place stop and limit orders at entry reference price * (1 + stop-loss fraction) and entry reference price * (1 - take-profit fraction), respectively.
Exit: short, higher-timeframe close series > long-lookback higher-timeframe close EMA AND trading minute - entry trading minute > configured delay AND (ADX <= configured threshold OR close > close at the configured lookback) AND neither earlier short exit condition applies; place stop and limit orders at entry reference price * (1 + stop-loss fraction) and entry reference price * (1 - take-profit fraction), respectively.
Exit: long, chart bar time equals the configured daily closing time.
Exit: short, chart bar time equals the configured daily closing time.
Calculation: ADX = EMA of DX, where DX = 100 * abs(plus DI - minus DI) / (plus DI + minus DI), and each DI is 100 times the EMA of its directional movement divided by the EMA of true range, all using the same lookback.
