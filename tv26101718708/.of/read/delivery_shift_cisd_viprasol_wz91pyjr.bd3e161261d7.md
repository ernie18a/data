Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low using equal left and right lookbacks; it becomes known after the right lookback has completed.
Calculation: Swing liquidity = retain confirmed swing levels until mitigated, expired, or pruned by the per-side capacity. A high level is mitigated when high >= level; a low level when low <= level. Expiry is measured from the pivot bar; expired levels cannot be mitigated. Process levels oldest first and retain the last mitigated level on each bar. Prune the oldest levels after processing mitigations.
Calculation: Delivery shift = maintain separate newest-first candidate lists. Add the current open to the bearish list when the previous candle is down and the current candle is up; add it to the bullish list when the previous candle is up and the current candle is down. Retain only the newest candidates up to the per-side capacity. On each bar, scan the bearish list first: while the newest candidate has close < candidate open, calculate its ratio as (maximum of zero and closes from the candidate bar through the current bar, subject to the leg lookback cap - candidate open) / (run extreme - candidate open). The run extreme is the open of the oldest consecutive down candle immediately preceding the candidate, subject to the walk cap, or zero if there is none. If the ratio > noise threshold, record a bearish shift and its ratio and clear that candidate list; otherwise discard that candidate and continue. Stop scanning if close >= the newest candidate open. Then scan the bullish list similarly, requiring close > candidate open and using (candidate open - lowest close from the candidate bar through the current bar, subject to the leg lookback cap) / (candidate open - run extreme), where the run extreme is the open of the oldest consecutive up candle immediately preceding the candidate, subject to the walk cap, or zero if there is none. A bullish shift clears its list and overrides a bearish shift on the same bar. An undefined ratio does not pass the threshold.
Calculation: Directional sweep = for a bearish shift, bars since the latest high-liquidity mitigation <= liquidity lookback AND close < the last mitigated high level; for a bullish shift, bars since the latest low-liquidity mitigation <= liquidity lookback AND close > the last mitigated low level.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes with lookahead off: use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars until the higher-timeframe bar closes, and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time; substitute the chart close when the EMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum one point each for shift ratio >= noise threshold + (1 - noise threshold) / 2, directional sweep, close on the shift's side of the higher-timeframe EMA, and volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier.
Long: confirmed swing low.
Short: confirmed swing high.
Long: bullish delivery shift.
Short: bearish delivery shift.
Long: bullish delivery shift AND directional sweep.
Short: bearish delivery shift AND directional sweep.
Long: bullish delivery shift AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND bars since the last qualified shift >= cooldown AND confluence score >= minimum score.
Short: bearish delivery shift AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND bars since the last qualified shift >= cooldown AND confluence score >= minimum score.
Long: the most recent delivery shift is bullish.
Short: no delivery shift has occurred OR the most recent delivery shift is bearish.
Long: higher-timeframe filter enabled AND close > higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: higher-timeframe filter enabled AND close <= higher-timeframe EMA.
