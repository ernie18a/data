Calculation: Indicators = RSI; MACD line; MACD histogram; Momentum; CCI; OBV; MFI; Stoch = SMA(Stochastic %K, smoothing shorter than the %K lookback); DIOSC = 100*RMA(change(high)+change(low), n)/RMA(TrueRange, n); VWMACD = VWMA(close, short) - VWMA(close, long); CMF = SMA(((close-low)-(high-close))/(high-low)*volume, n)/SMA(volume, n)
Calculation: PH = most recent confirmed pivot high (bar whose high is the highest within a left window and a right window of bars; confirmed once the right window completes); PL = same for pivot low with lows; k = number of bars from that pivot bar to the current bar; Line(a,b) = straight line over those k bars from value a at the pivot bar to value b at the current bar
Calculation: CurHigh = current bar high is the highest among the bars of the left window only (no right window); CurLow = current bar low is the lowest among the bars of the left window only
Long: CurLow AND all closes between PL and the current bar are >= Line(low at PL, current low) AND count of Indicators satisfying [indicator at PL bar < indicator now AND every indicator value between is >= Line(indicator at PL bar, indicator now)] >= minimum count AND current low < low at PL; OR (hidden form) CurLow AND all closes between PL and the current bar are >= Line(low at PL, current low) AND count of Indicators satisfying [indicator at PL bar > indicator now AND every indicator value between is >= Line(indicator at PL bar, indicator now)] >= minimum count AND current low > low at PL
Short: CurHigh AND all closes between PH and the current bar are <= Line(high at PH, current high) AND count of Indicators satisfying [indicator at PH bar > indicator now AND every indicator value between is <= Line(indicator at PH bar, indicator now)] >= minimum count AND current high > high at PH; OR (hidden form) CurHigh AND all closes between PH and the current bar are <= Line(high at PH, current high) AND count of Indicators satisfying [indicator at PH bar < indicator now AND every indicator value between is <= Line(indicator at PH bar, indicator now)] >= minimum count AND current high < high at PH
