Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low
Long: (volume >= configured multiple of its moving average AND close position >= configured threshold) OR (session filter disabled or current bar is in the configured session AND bar is confirmed AND cooldown elapsed AND no active long AND (Spike-Only Mode AND volume >= configured multiple of its moving average AND close position >= configured threshold OR NOT Spike-Only Mode AND persistent bias is long AND close crosses above the EMA of close))
Short: (volume >= configured multiple of its moving average AND close position <= 1 - configured threshold) OR (session filter disabled or current bar is in the configured session AND bar is confirmed AND cooldown elapsed AND no active short AND (Spike-Only Mode AND volume >= configured multiple of its moving average AND close position <= 1 - configured threshold OR NOT Spike-Only Mode AND persistent bias is short AND close crosses below the EMA of close))
