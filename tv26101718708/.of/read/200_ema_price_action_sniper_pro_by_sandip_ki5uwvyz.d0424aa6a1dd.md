Calculation: ADX = Wilder-smoothed average of the directional index, where the directional index is 100 × |+DI − −DI| / (+DI + −DI), and +DI/−DI are Wilder-smoothed directional movements divided by Wilder-smoothed true range
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative(close × volume) / cumulative(volume), reset each session
Long: Close > long-term EMA AND long-term EMA > its previous-bar value AND short-term EMA crosses above medium-term EMA AND Close > Open AND Close > previous-bar high AND ADX > its threshold AND ATR > its simple moving average with the same lookback AND absolute(short-term EMA − medium-term EMA) ≥ 0.25 × ATR AND Close > session VWAP
