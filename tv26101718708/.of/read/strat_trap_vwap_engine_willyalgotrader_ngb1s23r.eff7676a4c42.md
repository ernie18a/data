Calculation: Bar type = inside if high <= previous high AND low >= previous low; outside if high > previous high AND low < previous low; up if high > previous high AND low >= previous low; down if low < previous low AND high <= previous high.
Calculation: Directional setup = classify consecutive completed bars oldest first. Bullish sequences are (outside, up), (down, up), (up, up), (outside, down, up), (outside, inside, up), (down, inside, up), (up, inside, up), and (inside, down, up); optional extended sequences are (up, up, up) and (up, up, down, up). Bearish sequences exchange up and down. An optional extended bullish setup is an outside bar with close > previous high; its bearish counterpart has close < previous low. Select the longest matching sequence, falling back to the single-bar setup, and retain its final bar's high, low, and direction.
Calculation: Pending setup = the most recent directional setup, replacing any previous pending setup after resolving it on the current bar. Starting on the next bar, invalidate a bullish setup if close < its low or a bearish setup if close > its high; otherwise trigger it if high > its high for bullish or low < its low for bearish; otherwise expire it when its age reaches the expiry parameter. Invalidation precedes triggering, and triggering precedes expiry.
Calculation: Regime = initially neutral; basis is a selectable EMA, DEMA, HMA, or KAMA of close. Outer bands are basis plus or minus a regime ATR multiplied by an outer-band parameter; inner bands use the same basis and ATR with a nonnegative inner multiplier smaller than the outer multiplier. On each bar, change bullish to neutral when close crosses below the lower inner band and bearish to neutral when close crosses above the upper inner band; then set bullish when close crosses above the upper outer band or bearish when close crosses below the lower outer band. A cross compares both the current and previous close with their respective band values.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a trap entry, use the entry bar's low for long or high for short as anchor; for a trigger entry, select the setup's broken extreme, its opposite extreme, or the entry bar's adverse extreme as anchor. The long stop is the lesser of anchor minus a risk-ATR buffer and entry close minus a minimum risk-ATR distance; the short stop is the greater of anchor plus the buffer and entry close plus the minimum distance. Alternatively, for trigger entries only, use entry close minus or plus a risk-ATR multiple for long or short. Risk ATR is measured on the entry bar and may have a different lookback from regime ATR. Risk is the absolute distance from entry close to initial stop.
Calculation: Targets and active stop = the first and final targets are entry price plus risk multiples for long or minus risk multiples for short; the final target multiple is greater than the first. The active stop starts at the initial stop and optionally moves to entry after the first target is first touched; a newly moved stop applies starting on the next bar. Evaluate touches only after the entry bar.
