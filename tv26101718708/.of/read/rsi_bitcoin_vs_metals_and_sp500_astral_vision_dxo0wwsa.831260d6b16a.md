Calculation: Selected RSI = RSI of one selected benchmark-relative price ratio, or the arithmetic mean of the three benchmark-relative RSI values, calculated from higher-timeframe bars using the last completed bar
Calculation: Trend signal = EMA of the selected RSI, calculated on chart-timeframe bars
Long: In Extremes mode, selected RSI < oversold threshold OR in Trend mode, Trend signal >= 50
Short: In Extremes mode, selected RSI > overbought threshold OR in Trend mode, Trend signal < 50
