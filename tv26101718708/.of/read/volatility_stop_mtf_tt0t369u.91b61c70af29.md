Calculation: Volatility stop = On the selected source series, initialize the running maximum and minimum to the source, the trend to up, and the stop to zero. On each bar, update the running maximum and minimum with the source. Set the volatility distance to ATR multiplied by a factor, or true range when that product is unavailable. In an uptrend, set the stop to the greater of the previous stop and running maximum minus the volatility distance; in a downtrend, set it to the lesser of the previous stop and running minimum plus the volatility distance. Set the trend to up if the source is greater than or equal to the stop, and down otherwise. If the trend changes, reset the running maximum and minimum to the source and reset the stop to the source minus the volatility distance for an uptrend or plus it for a downtrend. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Early breach = A higher timeframe is used AND chart close crosses the higher-timeframe stop in either direction AND no previous early breach has occurred since the last higher-timeframe trend change AND the higher-timeframe trend is unchanged on the current chart bar.
Long: Volatility stop trend is up OR early breach AND volatility stop trend is down.
Short: Volatility stop trend is down OR early breach AND volatility stop trend is up.
Exit: long, volatility stop trend changes from up to down OR early breach AND volatility stop trend is up.
Exit: short, volatility stop trend changes from down to up OR early breach AND volatility stop trend is down.
