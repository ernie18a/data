Calculation: Higher-timeframe Volatility Stop = ATR-based trailing stop and up/down trend state calculated on higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe Volatility Stop trend is up OR close crosses above its downtrend stop
Short: Higher-timeframe Volatility Stop trend is down OR close crosses below its uptrend stop
Exit: long, close crosses below the higher-timeframe uptrend stop while its trend remains up OR its trend changes from up to down
Exit: short, close crosses above the higher-timeframe downtrend stop while its trend remains down OR its trend changes from down to up
