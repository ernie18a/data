Calculation: Directional score = count of true conditions for the direction: close versus session VWAP of typical price; main RSI versus its midpoint; MACD line versus signal line; short-term EMA versus long-term EMA; ADX above its threshold AND close versus short-term EMA; volume above its SMA AND close versus open; lower-timeframe RSI versus its midpoint. Use above for long and below for short. Use the last completed lower-timeframe bar. The external-instrument factor and instrument-recognition gate must be disabled because their data are unavailable.
Calculation: Range usage = current daily high minus current daily low, divided by the previous chart bar's value of the daily SMA of high minus low; use the last completed higher-timeframe daily value historically and the forming daily value in realtime. If the denominator is not positive, use zero.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at the entry bar multiplied by a stop multiplier. Evaluate stops and targets starting on the entry bar. For each trade, check its existing stop before targets; if the first target is reached, enable the entry-price stop on the next bar when breakeven is enabled. The final target is a larger multiple of risk distance than the first target.
Long: Short-term EMA crosses above long-term EMA AND directional score is at least the rescaled minimum for the available factors AND (session filter is disabled OR chart timeframe is daily or higher OR bar time is within either configured session) AND (range filter is disabled OR chart timeframe is daily or higher OR range usage is below its threshold) AND (no prior entry exists OR bars since the most recent entry are at least the spacing threshold).
Short: Short-term EMA crosses below long-term EMA AND directional score is at least the rescaled minimum for the available factors AND (session filter is disabled OR chart timeframe is daily or higher OR bar time is within either configured session) AND (range filter is disabled OR chart timeframe is daily or higher OR range usage is below its threshold) AND (no prior entry exists OR bars since the most recent entry are at least the spacing threshold).
Long: Close > session VWAP of typical price OR main RSI > its midpoint OR MACD line > signal line OR short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator.
Short: Close <= session VWAP of typical price OR main RSI <= its midpoint OR MACD line <= signal line OR short-term EMA <= long-term EMA OR positive directional indicator <= negative directional indicator.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus risk distance while breakeven is inactive OR low <= entry price while breakeven is active OR high >= entry price plus the final target multiple of risk distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus risk distance while breakeven is inactive OR high >= entry price while breakeven is active OR low <= entry price minus the final target multiple of risk distance.
