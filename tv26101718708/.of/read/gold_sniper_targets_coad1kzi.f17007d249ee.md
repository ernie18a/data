Calculation: Bull confluence score = count of these conditions that are true: close > session VWAP; chart-timeframe RSI > 50; MACD line > signal line; fast EMA > slow EMA; ADX > configured threshold AND close > fast EMA; volume > its moving average AND close > open; lower-timeframe RSI > 50 using the latest available lower-timeframe bar; and, when enabled, dollar-index close < its EMA AND the chart-timeframe correlation between close and dollar-index close is at or below its configured negative threshold.
Calculation: Bear confluence score = count of these conditions that are true: close < session VWAP; chart-timeframe RSI < 50; MACD line < signal line; fast EMA < slow EMA; ADX > configured threshold AND close < fast EMA; volume > its moving average AND close < open; lower-timeframe RSI < 50 using the latest available lower-timeframe bar; and, when enabled, dollar-index close > its EMA AND the chart-timeframe correlation between close and dollar-index close is at or below its configured negative threshold.
Calculation: Minimum confluence score = configured minimum score scaled by enabled factor count / 8 and rounded; enabled factor count is 8 with the dollar-index factor, otherwise 7.
Calculation: Risk distance = chart-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long targets = entry price + risk distance, + 2 × risk distance, and + 3 × risk distance; initial stop = entry price − risk distance. After the first target is reached, the active stop becomes entry price if breakeven management is enabled.
Calculation: Short targets = entry price − risk distance, − 2 × risk distance, and − 3 × risk distance; initial stop = entry price + risk distance. After the first target is reached, the active stop becomes entry price if breakeven management is enabled.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND bull confluence score >= minimum confluence score AND, when enabled for intraday bars, datetime is within a configured London or New York session AND, when enabled below daily timeframe, current daily high − current daily low < configured percentage of the average high − low of configured completed daily bars AND bars since the previous trade opened >= configured minimum spacing.
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND bear confluence score >= minimum confluence score AND, when enabled for intraday bars, datetime is within a configured London or New York session AND, when enabled below daily timeframe, current daily high − current daily low < configured percentage of the average high − low of configured completed daily bars AND bars since the previous trade opened >= configured minimum spacing.
Long: fast EMA > slow EMA
Short: fast EMA < slow EMA
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= third target after first and second targets are reached; if both stop and target conditions occur on the same bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= third target after first and second targets are reached; if both stop and target conditions occur on the same bar, exit at the stop.
