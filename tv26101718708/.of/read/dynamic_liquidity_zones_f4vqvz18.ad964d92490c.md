Calculation: Pivot high = a bar whose high is the highest among a longer left lookback of bars and a shorter right lookback of bars; it is confirmed only on the bar that completes the right lookback, and its price is that bar's high.
Calculation: Pivot low = a bar whose low is the lowest among a longer left lookback of bars and a shorter right lookback of bars; it is confirmed only on the bar that completes the right lookback, and its price is that bar's low.
Long: a pivot low is confirmed on the current bar AND, for at least one of the recent earlier confirmed pivot lows (limited count, no condition on whether price has since broken below it), abs(new pivot low - earlier pivot low) / earlier pivot low <= a small percentage threshold.
Short: a pivot high is confirmed on the current bar AND, for at least one of the recent earlier confirmed pivot highs (limited count, no condition on whether price has since broken above it), abs(new pivot high - earlier pivot high) / earlier pivot high <= a small percentage threshold.
