Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing state = On each update of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, compare the high and low of the bar one lookback before it with the highest high and lowest low, respectively, of the intervening bars through the completed bar. Set state to high if the candidate high is greater; otherwise set it to low if the candidate low is lower; otherwise retain the prior state. Initialize state to high. On a change to high or low, record the corresponding candidate price and reset its crossed flag.
Calculation: Order block = On the first completed higher-timeframe close beyond a recorded swing high or low, set its crossed flag and create a bullish or bearish block, respectively. For a bullish block, choose the bar with the lowest low between the swing bar and the breakout bar, excluding both; for a bearish block, choose the bar with the highest high over the same interval. The block bounds are that bar's high and low, or optionally its body high and low; use the same choice when selecting the extreme. Resolve equal extremes in favor of the most recent bar. If the interval is empty, use the bar immediately before the breakout. A bullish block becomes a bearish breaker when a completed higher-timeframe bar's body low is below its bottom; a bearish block becomes a bullish breaker when its body high is above its top. Remove a bearish breaker when a later completed higher-timeframe close is above its top, and remove a bullish breaker when a later completed higher-timeframe close is below its bottom.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > recorded swing high AND that swing high has not previously been crossed.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe body high > top of an existing bearish order block AND that block is not already a breaker.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close < recorded swing low AND that swing low has not previously been crossed.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe body low < bottom of an existing bullish order block AND that block is not already a breaker.
