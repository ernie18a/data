Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a symmetric pivot using equal numbers of bars on each side; confirm it only after the right-side bars close. Retain the most recently confirmed high and low separately.
Calculation: Structure breaks = a bullish break occurs when the selected source, close or high, is above the latest confirmed swing high and that high has not already been broken; a bearish break occurs when the selected source, close or low, is below the latest confirmed swing low and that low has not already been broken. Suppress both if both raw breaks occur on the same bar. Require the bar index to be at least the greater of twice the swing lookback and a minimum warmup. A newly confirmed swing resets its broken status before checking that bar. Initialize structure direction as neutral; after each unsuppressed break set it to that break's direction. A break is a continuation if the previous direction is neutral or matches it, and a reversal otherwise.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR on the entry bar multiplied by a stop-distance multiplier. Entry is at the signal bar's close. Fix the initial stop at entry minus risk for a long or entry plus risk for a short; fix the first and final targets at entry plus risk times their respective target multipliers for a long or entry minus those amounts for a short.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until break-even is enabled and, on a bar after entry, the first target is touched without the active stop also being touched; thereafter it is the entry price, effective starting on the following bar.
Long: A swing high is confirmed AND it is greater than or equal to the preceding confirmed swing high OR there is no preceding confirmed swing high.
Long: A swing low is confirmed AND it is greater than the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A swing high is confirmed AND it is less than the preceding confirmed swing high.
Short: A swing low is confirmed AND it is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed swing low OR there is no preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: A bullish structure break occurs.
Short: A bearish structure break occurs.
Long: Position is flat AND entry risk > 0 AND a bullish structure break occurs AND the selected continuation-or-reversal mode permits that break AND the higher-timeframe filter is disabled or chart close >= the EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close, substituting chart close if the EMA is unavailable.
Short: Position is flat AND entry risk > 0 AND a bearish structure break occurs AND the selected continuation-or-reversal mode permits that break AND the higher-timeframe filter is disabled or chart close <= the EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close, substituting chart close if the EMA is unavailable.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= active stop OR high >= final target).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= active stop OR low <= final target).
