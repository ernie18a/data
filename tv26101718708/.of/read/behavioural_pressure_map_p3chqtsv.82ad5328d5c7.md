Calculation: Cohorts = for each bar age j from 0 through min(cohort lookback, number of preceding bars) − 1, take cost = (high + low + close)/3 and weight = volume × 0.5^(j / recency half-life); include only cohorts with positive cost and weight, treating missing volume as zero. Calculate only when the cohort count exceeds 5.
Calculation: Net pressure = 100 × (demand − supply)/(demand + supply), where demand is the sum of weight × ((close − cost)/cost)^gain curvature for cohorts with cost < current close, and supply is the sum of weight × loss aversion × ((cost − close)/cost)^loss curvature for cohorts with cost > current close; undefined when demand + supply is zero.
Calculation: Support and resistance nodes = divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over the cohort lookback, including the current bar, into equal-width price bins. Assign each cohort's weight to its cost bin, clamping to the first or last bin when necessary. Support is the midpoint of the highest-weight bin strictly below current close; resistance is the midpoint of the highest-weight bin strictly above current close. Require positive bin weight and a nonzero price range; break weight ties using the lower-priced bin.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = the number of bars since the most recent long or short signal, with no prior signal satisfying the cooldown.
Long: Support exists AND low <= support + proximity multiplier × ATR AND close > support AND net pressure > pressure threshold AND signal cooldown >= minimum signal spacing.
Short: Resistance exists AND high >= resistance − proximity multiplier × ATR AND close < resistance AND net pressure < −pressure threshold AND signal cooldown >= minimum signal spacing.
