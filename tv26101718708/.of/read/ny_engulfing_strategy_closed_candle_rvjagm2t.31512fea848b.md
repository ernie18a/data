Calculation: Body high = max(open, close); body low = min(open, close)
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = on the previous completed chart bar, close > open and either its body contains the preceding completed bar’s body, or (if two-bar engulfing is enabled) its body combined with that preceding bar’s body contains the body of the bar before them
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = on the previous completed chart bar, close < open and either its body contains the preceding completed bar’s body, or (if two-bar engulfing is enabled) its body combined with that preceding bar’s body contains the body of the bar before them
Calculation: Long entry level = body high of the preceding bar if the one-bar pattern applies; otherwise body high of the bar before the two engulfing bars
Calculation: Short entry level = body low of the preceding bar if the one-bar pattern applies; otherwise body low of the bar before the two engulfing bars
Long: bullish engulfing AND previous completed chart bar close > moving average AND long entry level > moving average AND (slope filter disabled OR moving average > its value on the prior chart bar) AND (proximity filter disabled OR absolute difference between long entry level and moving average <= proximity limit) AND (session filter disabled OR previous completed chart bar timestamp is in the allowed session) AND fewer than the maximum trades have occurred today AND (stop-after-first-win disabled OR no winning trade has occurred today) AND not already in a position
Short: bearish engulfing AND previous completed chart bar close < moving average AND short entry level < moving average AND (slope filter disabled OR moving average < its value on the prior chart bar) AND (proximity filter disabled OR absolute difference between short entry level and moving average <= proximity limit) AND (session filter disabled OR previous completed chart bar timestamp is in the allowed session) AND fewer than the maximum trades have occurred today AND (stop-after-first-win disabled OR no winning trade has occurred today) AND not already in a position
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance OR scheduled close enabled AND current bar timestamp >= 16:55 America/New_York AND previous bar timestamp < 16:55 America/New_York
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance OR scheduled close enabled AND current bar timestamp >= 16:55 America/New_York AND previous bar timestamp < 16:55 America/New_York
