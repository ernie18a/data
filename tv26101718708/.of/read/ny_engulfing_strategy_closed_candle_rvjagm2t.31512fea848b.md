Calculation: Body high and body low = max(open, close) and min(open, close), respectively.
Calculation: Long entry price = body high of the bar before the engulfing bar if the engulfing bar alone engulfs that body; otherwise body high of the bar two bars before the engulfing bar. Short entry price = body low of the corresponding bar. Record this price when the entry decision occurs.
Calculation: A winning exit = take profit reached without stop loss being reached on the same bar, OR a forced-time exit with neither stop nor target reached and close >= entry price for a long or close <= entry price for a short. If both stop and target are reached, the exit is a loss. Count entries and winning exits by the current bar's calendar day in the configured session timezone; reset both counts at each new day.
Long: At least three preceding bars exist AND no position is open AND entries today < daily limit AND (stop-after-first-win disabled OR winning exits today = 0) AND (session filter disabled OR previous bar's opening time is in the configured session and timezone) AND previous close > previous open AND ((previous open <= body low two bars ago AND previous close >= body high two bars ago) OR (two-bar engulf enabled AND min(body low of previous bar, body low two bars ago) <= body low three bars ago AND max(body high of previous bar, body high two bars ago) >= body high three bars ago)) AND previous close > previous moving average AND long entry price > previous moving average AND (slope filter disabled OR previous moving average > moving average two bars ago) AND (proximity filter disabled OR abs(long entry price - previous moving average) <= proximity limit). Evaluate at the current bar's open; the moving average is a selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, RMA, or HMA of close.
Short: At least three preceding bars exist AND no position is open AND entries today < daily limit AND (stop-after-first-win disabled OR winning exits today = 0) AND (session filter disabled OR previous bar's opening time is in the configured session and timezone) AND previous close < previous open AND ((previous open >= body high two bars ago AND previous close <= body low two bars ago) OR (two-bar engulf enabled AND max(body high of previous bar, body high two bars ago) >= body high three bars ago AND min(body low of previous bar, body low two bars ago) <= body low three bars ago)) AND previous close < previous moving average AND short entry price < previous moving average AND (slope filter disabled OR previous moving average < moving average two bars ago) AND (proximity filter disabled OR abs(short entry price - previous moving average) <= proximity limit). Evaluate at the current bar's open; the moving average is a selectable SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, RMA, or HMA of close.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance OR (forced-time exit enabled AND current bar's opening timestamp >= configured daily exit timestamp in New York time AND previous bar's opening timestamp < that timestamp). Check on the entry bar and every subsequent bar; a stop takes precedence if stop and target are both reached.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance OR (forced-time exit enabled AND current bar's opening timestamp >= configured daily exit timestamp in New York time AND previous bar's opening timestamp < that timestamp). Check on the entry bar and every subsequent bar; a stop takes precedence if stop and target are both reached.
