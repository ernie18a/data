Calculation: Adaptive trend = ATR and a smoothed factor equal to the SMA of base factor + volatility weight × ATR / close + price-change weight × abs(close - previous close) / previous close. Upper and lower bands are (high + low) / 2 plus or minus factor × ATR. Initialize the trend line to the first available upper band and direction to bullish. On each bar, compare close with the prior trend line, or the initialized line on the first available bar: if close > line, direction is bullish and the new line is max(lower band, line); otherwise direction is bearish and the new line is min(upper band, line). Apply the same calculation to the last completed higher-timeframe bar for higher-timeframe direction.
Calculation: Liquidity sweeps = Confirm pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side. Maintain separate bounded, oldest-first lists of the most recently confirmed pivot highs and lows; add newly confirmed pivots before checking the current bar. Each pivot can be swept once and is removed when displaced by a newer pivot. A low sweep occurs when low < any unswept pivot low AND close > that pivot low; a high sweep occurs when high > any unswept pivot high AND close < that pivot high. Mark all matching pivots swept. Bars since a sweep is zero on the sweep bar and increments on each subsequent bar.
Long: Adaptive trend direction = bullish.
Short: Adaptive trend direction = bearish.
Long: Low sweep occurs.
Short: High sweep occurs.
Long: Adaptive trend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe direction = bullish) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > midpoint) AND (body filter disabled OR abs(close - open) >= ATR × multiplier) AND bars since low sweep <= sweep-memory length.
Short: Adaptive trend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe direction = bearish) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < midpoint) AND (body filter disabled OR abs(close - open) >= ATR × multiplier) AND bars since high sweep <= sweep-memory length.
Exit: Long, adaptive trend direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Short, adaptive trend direction changes from bearish to bullish.
