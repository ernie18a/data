Calculation: Strong bullish candle = close > open AND (close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= body-ratio threshold AND (close - open) / ATR >= body-size threshold
Calculation: Strong bearish candle = close < open AND (open - close) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= body-ratio threshold AND (open - close) / ATR >= body-size threshold
Calculation: Directional evidence = weighted sum of (positive DI - negative DI) / (positive DI + negative DI), the sign of the short-term EMA versus the long-term EMA, EMA of close changes divided by ATR and normalized to a capped range, and (close - EMA of hlc3) divided by ATR and normalized to a capped range
Calculation: Acceptance = EMA of (EMA of hlc3 + directional evidence × ATR × scale)
Calculation: Prior-session profile levels = volume-at-price levels built from bars in the current session through the previous completed chart bar: distribute each bar's volume across the price rows it overlaps in proportion to overlap; the control level is the highest-volume row's midpoint; expand from that row toward the higher-volume adjacent row until the target volume fraction is included to obtain core and outer boundaries; derive discount and premium levels from the midpoint and width of the core boundaries
Calculation: Qualified bullish source = (low < prior outer lower boundary AND strong bullish candle AND close > prior outer lower boundary OR low <= prior discount level AND low <= prior control level AND strong bullish candle AND close > prior discount level AND close > prior control level OR strong bullish candle AND min(open, close) > acceptance AND acceptance > its prior value AND directional evidence >= bullish threshold OR strong bullish candle AND prior close <= prior core upper boundary AND close > prior core upper boundary + ATR buffer AND close > prior control level AND positive DI > negative DI AND ADX >= strength threshold) AND directional evidence >= bullish threshold AND positive DI > negative DI AND ADX >= strength threshold AND close > acceptance AND close > current-session control level
Calculation: Qualified bearish source = (high > prior outer upper boundary AND strong bearish candle AND close < prior outer upper boundary OR high >= prior premium level AND high >= prior control level AND strong bearish candle AND close < prior premium level AND close < prior control level OR strong bearish candle AND max(open, close) < acceptance AND acceptance < its prior value AND directional evidence <= negative threshold OR strong bearish candle AND prior close >= prior core lower boundary AND close < prior core lower boundary - ATR buffer AND close < prior control level AND negative DI > positive DI AND ADX >= strength threshold) AND directional evidence <= negative threshold AND negative DI > positive DI AND ADX >= strength threshold AND close < acceptance AND close < current-session control level
Long: Qualified bullish source AND ((no prior BUY or SALE signal AND NOT qualified bearish source) OR (last signal was SALE AND at least the minimum signal gap has elapsed))
Short: Qualified bearish source AND ((no prior BUY or SALE signal AND NOT qualified bullish source) OR (last signal was BUY AND at least the minimum signal gap has elapsed))
