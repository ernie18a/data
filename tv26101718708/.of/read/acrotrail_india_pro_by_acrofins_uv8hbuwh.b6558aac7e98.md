Calculation: Adaptive bands = EMA of close plus or minus ATR times a base multiplier. Optionally adjust the multiplier down or up when the ATR percentile rank is below a lower or above an upper threshold. Initialize direction as neutral; after a warmup based on the longest ATR, EMA and regression lookbacks plus a buffer, set direction long when close exceeds the previous upper band, short when close is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retain the prior direction. If ratcheting is enabled, while direction remains long set the lower band to the greater of its raw value and its prior value; while direction remains short set the upper band to the lesser of its raw value and its prior value. Reset the ratcheted band to its raw value when direction changes; the opposite band always equals its raw value.
Calculation: Regime score = weighted sum of capped ADX, inverted Choppiness Index and squared correlation of close with bar index, each scaled to a common score range. Calculate Choppiness Index from the sum of true ranges divided by the highest high minus lowest low over its lookback; use the maximum choppiness when that range is zero. Treat unavailable ADX and correlation as zero.
Calculation: Directional quality = weighted sum of higher-timeframe alignment, volume confirmation, RSI gradient, regime score and breakout strength. Higher-timeframe alignment uses the last completed higher-timeframe close and its EMA: full credit when aligned with the candidate direction, zero when opposed, and partial credit when equal, unavailable or disabled. Volume receives full credit when its filter is disabled, volume is unavailable, or current volume exceeds a multiple of its SMA; otherwise it receives zero. The long RSI gradient assigns descending fractions as RSI passes below an upper, middle and lower threshold; the short gradient mirrors it with ascending thresholds. Breakout strength is the absolute distance from close to the previous upper band for a long candidate or previous lower band for a short candidate, divided by ATR and capped and normalized by a breakout limit; use zero if ATR is zero. Use configurable component weights; alternatively adjust base weights for the opening window, expiry window, high ATR percentile, choppy regime and trending regime, clamp them nonnegative and normalize their sum. The opening adjustment increases higher-timeframe weight and decreases breakout and RSI weights; expiry increases regime and higher-timeframe weights and decreases breakout and RSI weights; high volatility increases regime weight and decreases RSI weight; choppiness increases RSI weight and decreases breakout weight; trending increases breakout weight and decreases RSI weight. Session windows and expiry weekdays are configurable.
Calculation: Risk levels = at each accepted entry, store entry close and entry-bar ATR; set risk distance to that ATR times a stop multiplier, the initial stop one risk distance against the position, and the first and final targets configurable risk multiples in the position's favor. If break-even is enabled, move the stop to entry after the first target is first reached on a bar that does not also hit the existing stop; the new stop applies starting with the next bar. Ignore stop and target touches on the entry bar. Process an accepted opposite signal before checking the old position's stop or target on the same bar.
Long: Adaptive-band direction changes from short to long AND the chart bar is completed AND (session gate is disabled OR chart timeframe is at least daily OR bar time is within the configured session) AND directional quality for long is at least its threshold AND (choppy-regime filter is disabled OR regime score is at least its choppy threshold).
Short: Adaptive-band direction changes from long to short AND the chart bar is completed AND (session gate is disabled OR chart timeframe is at least daily OR bar time is within the configured session) AND directional quality for short is at least its threshold AND (choppy-regime filter is disabled OR regime score is at least its choppy threshold).
Long: Adaptive-band direction equals long.
Short: Adaptive-band direction equals short.
Long: Higher-timeframe filter is enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe filter is enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close is below its EMA.
Long: RSI is above its upper directional threshold.
Short: RSI is below its lower directional threshold.
Exit: long, accepted short signal OR (at least one bar since entry AND (low is at or below the active stop OR high is at or above the final target)).
Exit: short, accepted long signal OR (at least one bar since entry AND (high is at or above the active stop OR low is at or below the final target)).
