Calculation: Scheduled session opens = open of the lower-timeframe bar at each of the three daily anchor times
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar high above the highs of the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes available after the bars on the right complete. Confirmed swing low is the inverse.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low of a completed higher-timeframe bar above the high two bars earlier; gap range is from that earlier high to the completed bar's low. Bearish fair value gap = high of a completed higher-timeframe bar below the low two bars earlier; gap range is from the completed bar's high to that earlier low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = exponential moving average of higher-timeframe closes; use the last completed bar for historical decisions.
Long: At the New York open, Globex open < midnight open AND midnight open < New York open OR upward break of any available session open (current high > open AND previous close <= open in Wick/Touch mode, or current close > open AND previous close <= open in Close mode) AND close above the higher-timeframe EMA OR upward break of the most recent unconsumed confirmed swing high (close above it in Close mode, or high above it in Wick/Touch mode) AND a bullish higher-timeframe fair value gap formed before the current bar has its top at or below the broken level and within the configured maximum distance below it, and is within the configured maximum age; if the untouched-gap filter is enabled, it has not been touched by an earlier chart bar; if the EMA-side filter is enabled, the break price is above the last completed higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: At the New York open, Globex open > midnight open AND midnight open > New York open OR downward break of any available session open (current low < open AND previous close >= open in Wick/Touch mode, or current close < open AND previous close >= open in Close mode) AND close below the higher-timeframe EMA OR downward break of the most recent unconsumed confirmed swing low (close below it in Close mode, or low below it in Wick/Touch mode) AND a bearish higher-timeframe fair value gap formed before the current bar has its bottom at or above the broken level and within the configured maximum distance above it, and is within the configured maximum age; if the untouched-gap filter is enabled, it has not been touched by an earlier chart bar; if the EMA-side filter is enabled, the break price is below the last completed higher-timeframe EMA.
