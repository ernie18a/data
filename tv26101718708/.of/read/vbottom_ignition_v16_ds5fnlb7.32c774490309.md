Calculation: A lookback includes the current bar unless stated otherwise; a prior-bar lookback ends at the previous bar. A score is the number of its listed conditions that are true; an OR group counts as one condition.
Calculation: Fast EMA and slow EMA are EMAs of close, with the fast EMA using the shorter period. Trend fast EMA and trend slow EMA are either those same EMAs or a separate pair with the trend fast period shorter than the trend slow period.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume); use zero when the volume average is not positive. Volume expansion = relative volume >= its threshold. ATR compression = ATR / SMA(ATR) <= its threshold, using the same period for ATR and its average. Bollinger width compression = (Bollinger band width / middle band) / SMA(Bollinger band width / middle band) <= its threshold, using the band period for the average.
Calculation: For each base lookback, base range = (highest high - lowest low) / lowest low. For each shelf lookback, resistance = highest prior-bar high and resistance variation = (highest prior-bar high - lowest prior-bar high) / highest prior-bar high; support = lowest prior-bar low and support variation = (highest prior-bar low - lowest prior-bar low) / lowest prior-bar low.
Calculation: Divide each base lookback into three consecutive windows of floor(base lookback / 3) bars, minimum one bar, ending at the current bar. The windows are ordered oldest, middle, newest.
Calculation: Long VCP score counts: close > fast EMA AND, if strict trend is enabled, fast EMA > slow EMA; resistance variation <= its threshold; base range <= its threshold; oldest window's lowest low < middle window's lowest low AND middle window's lowest low <= newest window's lowest low; ATR compression; Bollinger width compression; volume expansion; RSI >= its long threshold.
Calculation: Short VCP score counts: close < fast EMA AND, if strict trend is enabled, fast EMA < slow EMA; support variation <= its threshold; base range <= its threshold; oldest window's highest high > middle window's highest high AND middle window's highest high >= newest window's highest high; ATR compression; Bollinger width compression; volume expansion; RSI <= its short threshold.
Calculation: Long continuation score counts: close > fast EMA; continuation resistance variation <= its threshold; continuation base range <= its threshold; oldest continuation window's lowest low <= middle window's lowest low AND middle window's lowest low <= newest window's lowest low; volume expansion; RSI >= its long threshold; Bollinger width compression OR ATR compression. Short continuation score counts the corresponding close < fast EMA, support variation, base range, oldest >= middle >= newest window highs, volume expansion, RSI <= its short threshold, and compression OR conditions. Continuation base and shelf lookbacks have their own parameters.
Calculation: Impulse high and impulse low are the highest high and lowest low over the impulse lookback; recent high and recent low are the highest high and lowest low over the pullback lookback.
Calculation: Long context score counts: (impulse high - impulse low) / impulse low >= impulse threshold; (impulse high - recent low) / (impulse high - impulse low) <= pullback threshold; close > fast EMA; close > slow EMA; fast EMA > slow EMA; RSI >= its midpoint; relative volume >= lower context volume threshold; relative volume >= higher context volume threshold; close >= impulse high * near-high fraction. Short context score counts: the same impulse condition; (recent high - impulse low) / (impulse high - impulse low) <= pullback threshold; close < fast EMA; close < slow EMA; fast EMA < slow EMA; RSI <= its midpoint; the same two relative volume conditions; close <= impulse low * (2 - near-high fraction).
Calculation: Bottom resistance is the highest prior-bar high over the bottom base lookback; bottom support is the lowest low over that lookback; major low is the lowest low over a separate major-low lookback. Bottom score counts: close <= major low * (1 + near-low fraction); (bottom resistance - bottom support) / bottom support <= bottom range threshold; close > bottom resistance * (1 + breakout buffer) AND close > open; close > fast EMA; fast EMA > previous fast EMA; RSI >= bottom RSI threshold; RSI crosses above its midpoint; volume requirement disabled OR volume average <= 0 OR volume <= 0 OR volume expansion.
Calculation: Normal long market gate = market trend filter disabled OR close > trend slow EMA AND trend fast EMA > trend slow EMA. Normal short market gate = market trend filter disabled OR close < trend slow EMA AND trend fast EMA < trend slow EMA. Consolidation gate = consolidation filter disabled OR ADX >= its threshold AND absolute percentage change in fast EMA over the slope lookback >= its threshold.
Calculation: Long VCP candidate = close > prior-bar resistance * (1 + breakout buffer) AND close > open AND long VCP score >= its threshold AND (close - fast EMA) / fast EMA <= extension threshold. Short VCP candidate = close < prior-bar support * (1 - breakout buffer) AND close < open AND short VCP score >= its threshold AND (fast EMA - close) / fast EMA <= extension threshold.
Calculation: Long continuation candidate = continuation scanner enabled AND close > prior-bar continuation resistance * (1 + breakout buffer) AND close > open AND (close - fast EMA) / fast EMA <= extension threshold AND (long continuation score >= its threshold OR context continuation enabled AND long context score >= its threshold). Short continuation candidate = continuation scanner enabled AND close < prior-bar continuation support * (1 - breakout buffer) AND close < open AND (fast EMA - close) / fast EMA <= extension threshold AND (short continuation score >= its threshold OR context continuation enabled AND short context score >= its threshold).
Calculation: Bottom candidate = bottom ignition enabled AND close > bottom resistance * (1 + breakout buffer) AND close > open AND bottom score >= its threshold AND (close - fast EMA) / fast EMA <= extension threshold.
Calculation: An entry signal is eligible when no earlier entry signal exists OR bars since the last long or short entry signal >= cooldown. A same-side entry is eligible when flat OR continuation adds are enabled; an opposite-side position blocks entry. On each entry or add, set entry price to close and reset the side's stop to min(close - ATR * stop multiplier, low) for long or max(close + ATR * stop multiplier, high) for short. Then on every bar, including the entry bar, raise the long stop to the maximum of its current value and any newly confirmed swing low, or lower the short stop to the minimum of its current value and any newly confirmed swing high. A swing is confirmed after its configured right-side bars, using configured left-side and right-side pivot lookbacks. Evaluate exits after these updates.

Short: close < trend slow EMA AND trend fast EMA < trend slow EMA

Long: long direction enabled AND entry signal eligible AND position side != short AND same-side entry eligible AND ((long VCP candidate OR long continuation candidate) AND normal long market gate AND consolidation gate OR bottom candidate AND (bottom trend bypass enabled OR normal long market gate) AND (bottom consolidation bypass enabled OR consolidation gate))
Short: short direction enabled AND entry signal eligible AND position side != long AND same-side entry eligible AND normal short market gate AND consolidation gate AND (short VCP candidate OR short continuation candidate)
Exit: long, close < long stop when close-only exit is enabled OR low < long stop when close-only exit is disabled
Exit: short, close > short stop when close-only exit is enabled OR high > short stop when close-only exit is disabled
