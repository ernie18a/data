Calculation: Fast EMA, slow EMA = exponential moving averages with shorter and longer lookbacks
Calculation: Market trend fast EMA, market trend slow EMA = selected shorter and longer EMA pair (either the market-filter pair or the entry pair)
Calculation: Market uptrend = close > market trend slow EMA AND market trend fast EMA > market trend slow EMA
Calculation: Market downtrend = close < market trend slow EMA AND market trend fast EMA < market trend slow EMA
Calculation: EMA slope % = absolute percentage change in the fast EMA over the slope lookback
Calculation: Not consolidating = ADX >= minimum ADX AND EMA slope % >= minimum slope %
Calculation: ATR compression = ATR / its moving average <= compression threshold
Calculation: Bollinger width compression = Bollinger Band width / its moving average <= compression threshold
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average
Calculation: Base range % = (highest high - lowest low) / lowest low * 100 over the base lookback
Calculation: Flat resistance = (highest prior-bar high - lowest prior-bar high) / highest prior-bar high * 100 over the shelf lookback <= shelf-variation limit
Calculation: Flat support = (highest prior-bar low - lowest prior-bar low) / lowest prior-bar low * 100 over the shelf lookback <= shelf-variation limit
Calculation: Higher lows = lowest lows in the oldest, middle, and newest thirds of the base lookback are strictly increasing, except the middle may equal the newest
Calculation: Lower highs = highest highs in the oldest, middle, and newest thirds of the base lookback are strictly decreasing, except the middle may equal the newest
Calculation: Long VCP score = count of: close > fast EMA (or, under strict trend, close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA); flat resistance; base range % <= base-range limit; higher lows; ATR compression; Bollinger width compression; relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold; RSI >= long RSI threshold
Calculation: Short VCP score = count of: close < fast EMA (or, under strict trend, close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA); flat support; base range % <= base-range limit; lower highs; ATR compression; Bollinger width compression; relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold; RSI <= short RSI threshold
Calculation: Long breakout = close > highest high of prior bars over the shelf lookback * (1 + breakout buffer %) AND close > open
Calculation: Short breakdown = close < lowest low of prior bars over the shelf lookback * (1 - breakout buffer %) AND close < open
Calculation: Continuation range % = (highest high - lowest low) / lowest low * 100 over the continuation lookback
Calculation: Continuation flat resistance and flat support = corresponding shelf variation over the continuation shelf lookback <= continuation shelf-variation limit
Calculation: Continuation higher lows and lower highs = lows or highs in the oldest, middle, and newest thirds of the continuation lookback are nondecreasing or nonincreasing, respectively
Calculation: Long continuation score = count of: close > fast EMA; flat continuation resistance; continuation range % <= continuation-range limit; continuation higher lows; relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold; RSI >= long RSI threshold; ATR or Bollinger width compression
Calculation: Short continuation score = count of: close < fast EMA; flat continuation support; continuation range % <= continuation-range limit; continuation lower highs; relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold; RSI <= short RSI threshold; ATR or Bollinger width compression
Calculation: Prior impulse % = (highest high - lowest low) / lowest low * 100 over the impulse lookback
Calculation: Long pullback depth % = (impulse high - lowest low over pullback lookback) / (impulse high - impulse low) * 100
Calculation: Short pullback depth % = (highest high over pullback lookback - impulse low) / (impulse high - impulse low) * 100
Calculation: Long context score = count of: prior impulse % >= minimum; long pullback depth % <= maximum; close > fast EMA; close > slow EMA; fast EMA > slow EMA; RSI >= 50; relative volume >= context minimum; relative volume >= context strong threshold; close >= impulse high * near-high percentage
Calculation: Short context score = count of: prior impulse % >= minimum; short pullback depth % <= maximum; close < fast EMA; close < slow EMA; fast EMA < slow EMA; RSI <= 50; relative volume >= context minimum; relative volume >= context strong threshold; close <= impulse low * (2 - near-high percentage)
Calculation: Bottom base range % = (highest high of prior bars - lowest low) / lowest low * 100 over the bottom-base lookback
Calculation: Bottom score = count of: close <= lowest low over bottom lookback * (1 + near-low percentage); bottom base range % <= maximum; bottom breakout; close > fast EMA; fast EMA > prior fast EMA; RSI >= bottom minimum; RSI crosses above 50; volume requirement satisfied (disabled, or volume unavailable, or relative volume >= breakout-volume threshold)
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initially min(entry price - ATR * initial-stop multiple, entry-bar low); thereafter, on each confirmed swing low (pivot with configured left and right bars), set to the greater of the existing stop and the pivot low
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initially max(entry price + ATR * initial-stop multiple, entry-bar high); thereafter, on each confirmed swing high (pivot with configured left and right bars), set to the lesser of the existing stop and the pivot high
Long: long trading enabled AND cooldown elapsed since last signal AND not in a short position AND (not in a long position OR pyramiding enabled) AND ((long breakout AND long VCP score >= minimum VCP score AND close is no more than maximum extension above fast EMA OR continuation breakout AND (continuation score >= minimum continuation score AND close is no more than maximum extension above fast EMA OR long context score >= minimum context score AND close is no more than maximum extension above fast EMA)) AND (market-trend filter disabled OR market uptrend) AND (consolidation filter disabled OR not consolidating OR bottom ignition bypasses it) OR bottom ignition enabled AND bottom breakout AND bottom score >= minimum bottom score AND close is no more than maximum extension above fast EMA AND (bottom trend bypass enabled OR market-trend filter disabled OR market uptrend) AND (bottom consolidation bypass enabled OR consolidation filter disabled OR not consolidating)
Short: short trading enabled AND market-trend filter disabled OR market downtrend, AND consolidation filter disabled OR not consolidating, AND short trading enabled AND cooldown elapsed since last signal AND not in a long position AND (not in a short position OR pyramiding enabled) AND (short breakdown AND short VCP score >= minimum VCP score AND close is no more than maximum extension below fast EMA OR continuation breakdown AND (continuation score >= minimum continuation score AND close is no more than maximum extension below fast EMA OR short context score >= minimum context score AND close is no more than maximum extension below fast EMA)
Exit: Long, long position AND trailing stop exists AND (close < trailing stop if close-only exit is enabled, otherwise low < trailing stop)
Exit: Short, short position AND trailing stop exists AND (close > trailing stop if close-only exit is enabled, otherwise high > trailing stop)
