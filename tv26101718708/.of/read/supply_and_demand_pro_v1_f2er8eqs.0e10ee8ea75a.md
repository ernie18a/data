Calculation: Fast, mid, and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = close > the highest high over the preceding lookback bars AND previous close <= that same high; bearish BOS = close < the lowest low over the preceding lookback bars AND previous close >= that same low.
Calculation: Strong bullish momentum = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA AND RSI > its midline; strong bearish momentum = fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA AND RSI < its midline.
Calculation: Early bullish momentum = (fast EMA > mid EMA OR close > fast EMA) AND RSI > its midline; early bearish momentum = (fast EMA < mid EMA OR close < fast EMA) AND RSI < its midline.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish BOS = the corresponding BOS occurred within the signal lookback window, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish pullback = strong bullish momentum AND close <= fast EMA times an upper tolerance AND close >= mid EMA times a lower tolerance; bearish pullback = strong bearish momentum AND close >= fast EMA times a lower tolerance AND close <= mid EMA times an upper tolerance.
Calculation: In session = current bar's datetime is within the configured stock or futures session, or session filtering is disabled.
Long: In session AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the previous long signal AND ((early bullish momentum AND RSI > its midline plus a threshold AND NOT strong bearish momentum AND bullish BOS AND the long cooldown is clear) OR (strong bullish momentum AND recent bullish BOS AND NOT strong bearish momentum AND NOT the aggressive long condition AND more than the configured continuation delay since the previous aggressive long signal, or none exists)).
Short: In session AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the previous short signal AND ((early bearish momentum AND RSI < its midline minus a threshold AND NOT strong bullish momentum AND bearish BOS AND the short cooldown is clear) OR (strong bearish momentum AND recent bearish BOS AND NOT strong bullish momentum AND NOT the aggressive short condition AND more than the configured continuation delay since the previous aggressive short signal, or none exists)).
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the signal bar's low and prior bar's low, reduced by a configured percentage buffer; short stop = the higher of the signal bar's high and prior bar's high, increased by a configured percentage buffer. Risk distance = absolute distance from entry price to stop. Long targets = entry price plus risk distance times configured target multiples; short targets = entry price minus risk distance times configured target multiples. These levels are set on aggressive signals and retained for continuation signals.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR (high >= a long target AND prior bar's low < that target).
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR (low <= a short target AND prior bar's high > that target).
