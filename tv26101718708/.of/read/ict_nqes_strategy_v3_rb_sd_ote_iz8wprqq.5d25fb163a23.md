Calculation: Opening-range high and low = high and low of the chart-timeframe bar beginning at 9:30 New York time; opening-range midpoint = their average
Calculation: Prior lookback high/low = highest high/lowest low over the chosen lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = a bullish/bearish candle whose body exceeds ATR times a displacement threshold; bullish/bearish order block = bullish/bearish displacement with the preceding candle bearish/bullish
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier AND the intervening candle closes above its open; bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier AND the intervening candle opens above its close
Calculation: Bullish rejection block = lower wick / candle range ≥ a threshold AND lower wick > 2 × upper wick; bearish rejection block = upper wick / candle range ≥ a threshold AND upper wick > 2 × lower wick
Calculation: OTE zone = the 62%–79% retracement zone of the current lookback high-low range, measured up from the low when close is above the range midpoint, otherwise down from the high
Calculation: Standard-deviation levels = the 9:30 bar open ± rolling standard deviation of close × a multiplier
Long: bullish rejection block OR bullish order block OR bullish fair value gap OR (New York regular-trading-hours session AND (low < prior lookback low AND close > prior lookback low OR low < opening-range low AND close > opening-range low) AND (bullish order block on the preceding bar OR bullish fair value gap on the preceding bar OR bullish rejection block on the preceding bar OR |close − opening-range midpoint| < ATR × threshold OR |close − opening-range low| < ATR × threshold OR close within the OTE zone OR |close − lower 1σ level| < ATR × threshold) AND close > opening-range low)
Short: bearish rejection block OR bearish order block OR bearish fair value gap OR (New York regular-trading-hours session AND (high > prior lookback high AND close < prior lookback high OR high > opening-range high AND close < opening-range high) AND (bearish order block on the preceding bar OR bearish fair value gap on the preceding bar OR bearish rejection block on the preceding bar OR |close − opening-range midpoint| < ATR × threshold OR |close − opening-range high| < ATR × threshold OR close within the OTE zone OR |close − upper 1σ level| < ATR × threshold) AND close < opening-range high)
