Calculation: Supertrend direction = -1 when TradingView Supertrend is in an uptrend, and +1 when it is in a downtrend.
Calculation: Bollinger Band Width = (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / SMA of close.
Long: Williams %R crosses above an oversold threshold AND, if the MA filter is enabled, ((close > selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or HMA of close AND previous close > its corresponding MA) OR (close < selected MA AND previous close < its corresponding MA)) AND, if enabled, Choppiness Index < threshold AND, if enabled, ADX > threshold AND, if enabled, volume > SMA of volume AND, if enabled, Bollinger Band Width > its SMA AND, if enabled, close > Supertrend direction.
Short: Williams %R crosses below an overbought threshold AND, if the MA filter is enabled, ((close > selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or HMA of close AND previous close > its corresponding MA) OR (close < selected MA AND previous close < its corresponding MA)) AND, if enabled, Choppiness Index < threshold AND, if enabled, ADX > threshold AND, if enabled, volume > SMA of volume AND, if enabled, Bollinger Band Width > its SMA AND, if enabled, close < Supertrend line.
Exit: long, if ATR exits are enabled, low <= entry price - stop multiplier × ATR at the long signal OR high >= entry price + target multiplier × ATR at the long signal.
Exit: short, if ATR exits are enabled, high >= entry price + stop multiplier × ATR at the short signal OR low <= entry price - target multiplier × ATR at the short signal.
