Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by average volume over a longer lookback, on the selected screener timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); use its current forming bar
Calculation: Relative strength vs. benchmark = RSI of the stock close divided by benchmark close, using both on the selected screener timeframe and its current forming bar
Calculation: Reference high/low = high/low when wick mode is enabled; otherwise max(open, close)/min(open, close)
Long: Relative strength vs. benchmark > its configured minimum AND ((short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > longer-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND close is within the configured distance of the long-lookback highest reference high AND relative volume >= its high threshold AND close crosses above the highest reference high of the prior shorter lookback) OR (short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > longer-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND reference low >= medium-term EMA times its lower proximity threshold AND reference high <= short-term EMA times its upper proximity threshold AND relative volume < its low threshold) OR (short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > longer-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND reference low is within the configured proximity band around medium-term EMA AND MFI > its midpoint) OR (close crosses above long-term EMA AND relative volume >= its ignition threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP)))
