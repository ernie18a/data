Calculation: Baseline = selected moving average of close; apply the same selected type and lookback to high and low for the baseline channel and continuation channel, using the baseline and shorter continuation lookbacks respectively.
Calculation: ATR = selected moving average of true range.
Calculation: C1 bullish/bearish = selected option: Accelerator Oscillator rising/falling; Vortex positive/negative line greater than the other and above its threshold; QQE smoothed RSI minus 50 above its upper band/below its lower band; Aroon upper above/below lower; SSL upper line above/below lower line; DeMarker crosses above/below zero; Squeeze Momentum crosses above/below zero; PMO crosses above/below its signal; Anchored Momentum crosses above/below its signal; WaveTrend crosses above/below zero; or close crosses above/below its ATR-based trailing stop.
Calculation: C2 bullish/bearish = selected option: QQE smoothed RSI minus 50 above its threshold/below its negative threshold; three moving averages in descending/ascending order of lookback and corresponding value (shortest above longest / shortest below longest); Absolute Strength smoothed bull/bear component falling; SuperTrend bullish/bearish state; KDJ J above/below the average of K and D; SSL upper line above/below lower line; Kuskus Starlight above/below zero; Ergodic CCI crosses above/below its signal; Coppock curve above/below zero; close above/below Trend Magic; or Williams %R below/above -50.
Calculation: Damiani volatility filter = compute the Damiani volatility value on chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe bars, using the still-forming bar; block entries when the value is below zero. Waddah Attar Explosion filter blocks entries when its Bollinger-band width is at most 3.7 times the 100-bar RMA of true range, or its MACD change is zero.
Long: (baseline filter disabled OR close > Baseline) AND (baseline filter disabled OR Baseline > close - ATR × baseline-distance parameter) AND (C1 disabled OR selected C1 bullish) AND (C2 disabled OR selected C2 bullish) AND volume filter passes when enabled AND (directional-candle filter disabled OR close > open) AND (one-candle rule disabled OR this is the second consecutive bar with close > Baseline) AND (bridge filter disabled OR C1/C2 bullish confirmation has lasted no more than seven consecutive bars); OR, for continuation, baseline filter enabled AND close > Baseline AND close > continuation-channel lower/upper state line AND C1/C2 confirmations pass AND (continuation directional-candle filter disabled OR close > open) AND baseline-above condition has lasted longer than the exit counter. Emit only on the first qualifying initial-entry bar or first two qualifying continuation bars; entries require flat position.
Short: (baseline filter disabled OR close < Baseline) AND (baseline filter disabled OR Baseline < close + ATR × baseline-distance parameter) AND (C1 disabled OR selected C1 bearish) AND (C2 disabled OR selected C2 bearish) AND volume filter passes when enabled AND (directional-candle filter disabled OR close < open) AND (one-candle rule disabled OR this is the second consecutive bar with close < Baseline) AND (bridge filter disabled OR C1/C2 bearish confirmation has lasted no more than seven consecutive bars); OR, for continuation, baseline filter enabled AND close < Baseline AND close < continuation-channel state line AND C1/C2 confirmations pass AND (continuation directional-candle filter disabled OR close < open) AND baseline-below condition has lasted longer than the exit counter. Emit only on the first qualifying initial-entry bar or first two qualifying continuation bars; entries require flat position.
Exit: long, exit filter enabled AND (selected exit condition OR (baseline filter enabled AND Baseline crosses above close) OR low <= entry price - entry ATR × stop parameter OR high >= entry price + entry ATR × target parameter). Selected exit condition is: for SSL exit, SSL exit line crosses above close while close is above Baseline (or baseline filter disabled); for C1/C2 exit, selected C1/C2 bullish condition is false.
Exit: short, exit filter enabled AND (selected exit condition OR (baseline filter enabled AND close crosses above Baseline) OR high >= entry price + entry ATR × stop parameter OR low <= entry price - entry ATR × target parameter). Selected exit condition is: for SSL exit, close crosses above SSL exit line while close is below Baseline (or baseline filter disabled); for C1/C2 exit, selected C1/C2 bearish condition is false.
