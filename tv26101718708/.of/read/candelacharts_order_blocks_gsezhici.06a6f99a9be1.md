Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low at least as extreme as the surrounding equal numbers of preceding and following chart bars; confirm it after the following bars have elapsed. If a pivot high and pivot low confirm on the same bar, add only the pivot high to the structure queues, but update the latest confirmed pivot time for both.
Calculation: Structure queues = retain confirmed pivot highs and lows in separate newest-first queues. Also retain the full history of each pivot type for counting. After an upward break, clear the high queue; after a downward break, clear the low queue. A cross uses the current and previous bar's respective queue-head levels.
Calculation: Bullish block bottom = the lowest requested-timeframe low over the chart bars from the current bar back to, but excluding, the latest confirmed pivot-high bar. Bearish block top = the highest requested-timeframe high over the chart bars from the current bar back to, but excluding, the latest confirmed pivot-low bar. The window must contain more than one chart bar. The requested timeframe may be the chart timeframe, a higher timeframe using the last completed bar with values carried forward, or a lower timeframe using the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar.
Long: Close crosses above the newest queued pivot high AND at least two pivot lows have been confirmed AND the bullish block window contains more than one chart bar AND the current close is at or above the bullish block bottom in close mitigation mode, OR close crosses above the newest queued pivot high AND at least two pivot lows have been confirmed AND the bullish block window contains more than one chart bar AND the current low is at or above the bullish block bottom in wick mitigation mode.
Short: Close crosses below the newest queued pivot low AND at least two pivot highs have been confirmed AND the bearish block window contains more than one chart bar AND the current close is at or below the bearish block top in close mitigation mode, OR close crosses below the newest queued pivot low AND at least two pivot highs have been confirmed AND the bearish block window contains more than one chart bar AND the current high is at or below the bearish block top in wick mitigation mode.
