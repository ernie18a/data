Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the configured number of bars immediately before and after it; evaluate after the bars after it have completed
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the configured number of bars immediately before and after it; evaluate after the bars after it have completed
Long: On a newly confirmed pivot low, its low is below the compared earlier pivot low, the pivot distance is within the configured minimum and maximum, and at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators (RSI, MACD histogram, MFI) have higher values at the new pivot than at the earlier pivot; compare with the immediately preceding pivot by default, or any stored pivot in range if that mode is selected
Short: On a newly confirmed pivot high, its high is above the compared earlier pivot high, the pivot distance is within the configured minimum and maximum, and at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators (RSI, MACD histogram, MFI) have lower values at the new pivot than at the earlier pivot; compare with the immediately preceding pivot by default, or any stored pivot in range if that mode is selected
