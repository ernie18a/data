Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the session-selected lookback
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: Trend regime = efficiency ratio of close displacement over summed absolute close changes; classify direction by current close versus its lookback close, and classify trending when the ratio exceeds its volatility-adjusted threshold
Calculation: Higher-timeframe vote = weighted sum of votes from higher-timeframe EMA ordering, close versus the midpoint of the recent high-low range, and close versus the short EMA; use the latest available higher-timeframe bar (last completed historically, forming in real time)
Calculation: Daily pivots = pivot levels calculated from the previous completed daily bar
Calculation: Overextension score = weighted sum of directional distance from EMA and VWAP in ATR units, swing-structure exhaustion, MFI exhaustion, volume and wick signals, distance from the latest high-volume-zone midpoint, Bollinger-band exhaustion, VWAP-band position, and intermarket divergence; compare with a context-adjusted threshold
Calculation: Confidence score = count of directional confirmations from overextension depth, mean-reverting Hurst regime, divergence, MFI exhaustion, volume climax, wick or outside-band bar, band position, momentum reversal, and structure or intermarket signals
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the midpoint of the recent highest high, lowest low, and average close; squeeze fires when Bollinger bands move from inside to outside Keltner channels
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed lower price pivot low with a higher RSI pivot low; bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed higher price pivot high with a lower RSI pivot high
Calculation: Bullish CHoCH = bearish swing structure and close above the latest swing high; bearish CHoCH = bullish swing structure and close below the latest swing low
Calculation: VRZ retest = price first moves away from the stored high-volume bar zones, then tests a zone low from above and closes above it (support), or tests a zone high from below and closes below it (resistance)
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price exceeds the prior lookback high or low and closes back across it, with wick, relative-volume, range-expansion, and wick-significance filters
Calculation: Protective stop = beyond the recent swing extreme by a context-adjusted ATR buffer, subject to session-range and ATR distance caps
Calculation: Profit target = select valid mean-reversion levels on the profitable side of price from EMA, VWAP, pivots, session midpoints, and volume-profile levels; confidence selects the nearer, intermediate, or farther level, then scale its distance by context
Long: bullish RSI divergence OR bullish CHoCH OR bullish squeeze fire OR bullish momentum flip with sufficient magnitude and relative volume outside excluded sessions OR bullish VRZ support retest with overextension and confidence filters OR sweep of the prior low with overextension and confidence filters OR an executed long entry path
Short: bearish RSI divergence OR bearish CHoCH OR bearish squeeze fire OR bearish momentum flip with sufficient magnitude and relative volume outside excluded sessions OR bearish VRZ resistance retest with overextension and confidence filters OR sweep of the prior high with overextension and confidence filters OR an executed short entry path
Long: downtrend regime AND long overextension threshold met AND minimum liquidity and ADX conditions AND confidence and higher-timeframe veto pass AND at least one enabled entry path confirms AND target distance meets minimum profit and risk-reward conditions; entry paths are fade, divergence, sweep trap, aggressive flow, or VRZ retest, each requiring its path-specific confirmation threshold; exclude doji bars and persistent-Hurst conditions for fade entries
Short: uptrend regime AND short overextension threshold met AND minimum liquidity and ADX conditions AND confidence and higher-timeframe veto pass AND at least one enabled entry path confirms AND target distance meets minimum profit and risk-reward conditions; entry paths are fade, divergence, sweep trap, aggressive flow, or VRZ retest, each requiring its path-specific confirmation threshold; exclude hidden bullish divergence when its blocking conditions apply and exclude doji bars and persistent-Hurst conditions for fade entries
Exit: long, low <= protective stop OR high >= profit target
Exit: short, high >= protective stop OR low <= profit target
