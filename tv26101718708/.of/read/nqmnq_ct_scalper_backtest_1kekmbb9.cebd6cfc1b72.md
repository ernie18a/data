Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest in a symmetric lookback window; it becomes available only after the right-hand window has completed.
Calculation: Squeeze = the upper Bollinger Band is below the upper Keltner Channel AND the lower Bollinger Band is above the lower Keltner Channel; the Keltner Channel uses an EMA of close plus or minus an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = the linear-regression endpoint of close minus the average of the rolling high-low midpoint and the SMA of close.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its rolling SMA; use a fallback value when the SMA is zero.
Long: A low-price pivot is confirmed AND its price is lower than the preceding confirmed low-price pivot AND the RSI low pivot confirmed on the same bar is higher than the RSI low pivot confirmed with the preceding low-price pivot.
Short: A high-price pivot is confirmed AND its price is higher than the preceding confirmed high-price pivot AND the RSI high pivot confirmed on the same bar is lower than the RSI high pivot confirmed with the preceding high-price pivot.
Long: The latest two confirmed swing highs are decreasing AND the latest two confirmed swing lows are decreasing AND close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high in the sense that this complete condition was false on the previous bar AND relative volume >= its threshold AND datetime is outside the configured Asian session.
Short: The latest two confirmed swing highs are increasing AND the latest two confirmed swing lows are increasing AND close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low in the sense that this complete condition was false on the previous bar AND relative volume >= its threshold AND datetime is outside the configured Asian session.
Long: The preceding bar is in a squeeze AND the current bar is not in a squeeze AND squeeze momentum > 0.
Short: The preceding bar is in a squeeze AND the current bar is not in a squeeze AND squeeze momentum < 0.
Long: Squeeze momentum > 0 AND previous squeeze momentum <= 0 AND absolute squeeze momentum > its threshold AND relative volume >= its threshold AND datetime is outside the configured Asian session.
Short: Squeeze momentum < 0 AND previous squeeze momentum >= 0 AND absolute squeeze momentum > its threshold AND relative volume >= its threshold AND datetime is outside the configured Asian session.
