Calculation: A crossover means the first series is above the second on the current bar and was at or below it on the preceding bar; a crossunder is the reverse.
Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is a high or low extreme over its left and right lookbacks, recognized after the right-lookback bars have closed. Use the latest confirmed pivot value.
Calculation: Bullish swing failure for a given pivot configuration occurs when low is below the latest pivot low and below every low in the preceding right-lookback bars, while open and close are above that pivot low. Bearish swing failure reverses these comparisons using highs and the pivot high.
Calculation: A swing failure remains valid until close breaks its extreme or its extreme is broken by either of the two preceding closes. A newer opposite swing failure also ends its validity. A confirmed bullish swing failure occurs within a confirmation window after a valid bullish swing failure, when there is no bearish swing failure on the current bar, close exceeds the failure bar's high and exceeds the preceding high by a minimum percentage, and the preceding confirmation state was not bullish. Confirmed bearish swing failure reverses these comparisons. The confirmation state changes to bullish or bearish on confirmation and resets on either new swing failure.
Calculation: Full body means absolute close minus open is at least a threshold fraction of high minus low. Bullish candle confirmation means a full bullish body OR a sufficiently large lower wick with a sufficiently small upper wick OR a lower wick exceeding a larger fraction of the range. Bearish candle confirmation reverses the wick and body directions.
Calculation: Long entry filter means upper wick is at most its range-fraction threshold AND there is no current valid bearish swing failure for either the equal-left-and-right pivot configuration or the longer-left, shorter-right pivot configuration. Short entry filter reverses wick and swing-failure directions.
Calculation: Volume spike means volume exceeds both preceding volume and its moving average. Strong volume spike means volume exceeds a multiplier times its moving average.
Calculation: A volatility breakdown means low is below open minus a multiplier times ATR. Recent volatility breakdown means this condition occurred within a lookback ending at the current bar.
Calculation: The short-lookback turtle bullish event is close crossing above the preceding bars' highest high AND close exceeding the preceding close; the bearish event reverses these comparisons. The long-lookback turtle events use a longer extreme lookback. Turtle state is the direction of the latest such event, with long-lookback events taking precedence over short-lookback events on the same bar.
Calculation: Turtle long marker means a short-lookback bullish event AND either the preceding short-lookback bullish event's high exceeds the current high OR the preceding turtle state is bearish AND the most recent of a short-lookback bullish event and a long-lookback bearish event before this bar was bearish. Turtle short marker reverses these comparisons and event directions.
Calculation: Volume-weighted ATR is the simple moving average of true range multiplied on each bar by volume divided by its own same-length moving average; use a weight of one when that volume average is not positive.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio is absolute close change over the short-side ATR lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, with a neutral fallback if the sum is zero. Regime scale is an affine function of this ratio.
Calculation: The linear-regression reference is the fitted close from a rolling linear regression evaluated one bar before its endpoint. For each side, choose a stop mode: linear-regression reference minus for longs or plus for shorts a side-specific multiplier times ordinary ATR; the same formula using volume-weighted ATR; that volume-weighted formula additionally multiplied by regime scale and ratcheted; or high minus for longs or low plus for shorts a multiplier times volume-weighted ATR and ratcheted. For a ratcheted long stop, reset to its current raw value when the previous stop is unavailable or the previous close is at or below it; otherwise use the greater of its previous and current raw values. Reverse the comparisons for shorts. In the high/low stop mode, use a separate initial multiplier on reset. Stop calculations run on every bar, independently of position.
Calculation: Bars since entry means bars since the most recent open entry, including a pyramid entry. Open return is the position-side-adjusted change from the first open entry price to close, divided by that entry price. Entry-bar high and low are taken from the first entry bar when exactly one entry is open.
Long: Shorter close EMA crosses above longer close EMA OR a separate longer-period EMA pair crosses upward, AND volume spike AND close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high AND long entry filter AND position is flat or short.
Short: Shorter close EMA crosses below longer close EMA AND volume spike AND recent volatility breakdown AND short entry filter AND position is flat or long.
Long: Close exceeds the highest high of a preceding breakout lookback AND close exceeds preceding close AND a shorter trend EMA is at least its longer trend EMA AND bullish candle confirmation AND strong volume spike AND position is flat or short.
Short: A volatility breakdown with strong volume occurred within a lookback ending at the current bar AND close is below the lowest low of a preceding breakout lookback AND close is below preceding close AND preceding turtle state is bullish AND bearish candle confirmation AND short entry filter AND position is flat or long.
Long: Confirmed bullish swing failure with equal left and right pivot lookbacks AND full body AND long entry filter AND position is flat or short.
Long: Confirmed bullish swing failure with the same left pivot lookback and a shorter right lookback AND bullish candle confirmation AND strong volume spike AND long entry filter AND position is flat or short.
Short: Confirmed bearish swing failure with equal left and right pivot lookbacks AND full body AND short entry filter AND position is flat or long.
Short: Confirmed bearish swing failure with a longer left and shorter right pivot lookback AND bearish candle confirmation AND short entry filter AND position is flat or long.
Long: Bullish swing failure for either the equal-lookback or shorter-right-lookback pivot configuration.
Short: Bearish swing failure for either the equal-lookback or longer-left, shorter-right-lookback pivot configuration.
Short: Confirmed bearish swing failure for either the equal-lookback or longer-left, shorter-right-lookback pivot configuration.

Short: Turtle short marker.
Long: Position is long AND bars since entry is at most the pyramid window AND close exceeds the highest high of a preceding pyramid lookback AND full body AND long entry filter.
Short: Position is short AND bars since entry is at most the pyramid window AND close is below the lowest low of a preceding pyramid lookback AND full body AND short entry filter.
Exit: long, bars since entry is at least the minimum holding period AND close crosses under the long stop OR bars since entry is at least the minimum holding period AND the preceding bar had a long-stop crossunder after the minimum holding period AND close is below the long stop.
Exit: short, bars since entry is at least the minimum holding period AND close crosses above the short stop OR bars since entry is at least the minimum holding period AND the preceding bar had a short-stop crossover after the minimum holding period AND close is above the short stop.
Exit: long, exactly one entry is open AND open return is at or below the smart-stop threshold AND either close is below the entry-bar low OR bars since entry is at least the minimum holding period AND close crosses under the long stop.
Exit: short, open return is at or below the smart-stop threshold AND either close is above the entry-bar high OR bars since entry is at least the minimum holding period AND close crosses above the short stop.
Exit: long, open return is below the failsafe threshold.
Exit: short, open return is below the failsafe threshold.
