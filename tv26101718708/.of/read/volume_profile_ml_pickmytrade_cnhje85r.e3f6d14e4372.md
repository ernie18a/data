Calculation: Rolling volume profile = distribute each completed chart bar’s volume across fixed price rows spanning the rolling lookback’s low to high, weighted triangularly around typical price (high + low + close)/3; point of control is the fullest row, value area expands from it by repeatedly adding the fuller adjacent row until the target volume share is reached; high-volume and low-volume nodes are nonempty rows at or above the high-volume percentile and at or below the low-volume percentile, respectively
Calculation: Node arrival = on a confirmed chart bar, the close enters a high-volume or low-volume row different from the previous bar’s row, and that row has not registered an arrival within the re-arm interval; approach direction is from below when the close three bars earlier is below the row midpoint, otherwise from above
Calculation: Arrival features = (1) row volume / total profile volume × row count, capped at 8; (2) fraction of nonempty rows with lower volume; (3) volume in the row and up to two adjacent rows on each side / total profile volume × row count / 5, capped at 8; (4) absolute change from close three bars earlier / ATR, capped at 3; (5) (point of control − row midpoint) / profile range × approach direction; (6) row midpoint’s position in profile range; (7) current volume / 20-bar average volume, capped at 4; (8) point-of-control volume / total profile volume × row count, capped at 8; (9) row midpoint’s position within the value area, clamped to −2 through 3
Calculation: Historical arrival outcome = starting after the arrival bar, a break occurs when a close passes the node by the break threshold times ATR frozen at arrival; a hold occurs when price reaches the rejection threshold times that ATR away from the node without a break; check break first, and discard arrivals reaching neither outcome before the timeout
Calculation: Analogue hold probability = among the K nearest resolved arrivals by the sum of log(1 + absolute feature difference) across the nine arrival features, weight each outcome by 1/(1 + distance), then shrink the weighted hold fraction toward the library’s overall hold fraction using the shrinkage weight; mean neighbour distance is the average of those K distances
