Calculation: Confidence = cap the sum of eight scores at a maximum: trend alignment scores a positive weight if short-, medium-, and long-period close EMAs are strictly ordered in either direction, otherwise zero; ADX scores a positive weight proportional to ADX up to a cap; RSI scores its higher weight when it lies between the neutral and overbought thresholds in an upward EMA trend or between the oversold and neutral thresholds in a downward EMA trend, otherwise its lower weight; MACD scores its higher weight when its line is above its signal in an upward EMA trend or below it in a downward EMA trend, otherwise its lower weight; relative volume scores one of four increasing weights according to three increasing thresholds; MFI and CCI each score their higher weight when respectively above their neutral threshold in an upward EMA trend or below it in a downward EMA trend, otherwise their lower weight, with a separate neutral-trend CCI weight; momentum scores its higher weight when above its shorter-period EMA in an upward EMA trend or below it in a downward EMA trend, otherwise its lower weight. The EMA trend is upward when the short-period EMA exceeds the medium-period EMA, downward when it is below, and neutral when equal. Relative volume is volume divided by its SMA, with a neutral fallback when unavailable.
Calculation: Trail = initialize to close; on each bar, if close is greater than the previous trail, set the trail to the greater of the previous trail and close minus an ATR multiple and set direction upward; otherwise set it to the lesser of the previous trail and close plus that ATR multiple and set direction downward.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe counts = count the upward and downward trends independently across four configured timeframes; on each, compare the short-period and medium-period close EMAs using the same lengths as on the chart. Each configured timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the still-forming bar for a higher timeframe and the last available lower-timeframe bar for a lower timeframe. A disabled multi-timeframe calculation contributes zero counts.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a value that is a pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side; evaluate it only on its confirmation bar. Consecutive pivots are consecutive confirmed pivots of the same kind.
