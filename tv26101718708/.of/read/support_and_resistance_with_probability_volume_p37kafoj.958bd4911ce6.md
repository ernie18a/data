Calculation: Level banks = Maintain separate banks on two source timeframes, one shorter than the other; either may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last available lower-timeframe bar, with no lookahead. On each new source bar, first update existing levels and then add confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows. Add each pivot to the last existing level within a percentage distance of that level by averaging their prices; otherwise create a level with zero accumulated buyer and seller volume. Retain each level's original creation time when merging. Remove levels older than the bank's configured time window and, when adding a level exceeds the bank's capacity, remove the oldest level.
Calculation: Level touch volumes = For each level, on each new source bar with available ATR, resolve any pending touch before detecting another touch. A pending touch expires without an outcome after its configured source-bar window; before expiry it ends when price breaks or bounces through the thresholds fixed at the touch. For a support-side touch, the break threshold is level minus an ATR multiple and the bounce threshold is level plus another ATR multiple; a break occurs when low is below the break threshold and a bounce when high is at or above the bounce threshold. For a resistance-side touch, the break threshold is level plus an ATR multiple and the bounce threshold is level minus another ATR multiple; a break occurs when high is above the break threshold and a bounce when low is at or below the bounce threshold. Give a break priority if both occur. If no touch remains pending and the source-bar cooldown since the last touch has elapsed, a touch occurs when source close is at or above the level AND source low is at or below the level plus an ATR tolerance, OR source close is below the level AND source high is at or above the level minus an ATR tolerance. On each touch, add volume times buy share to the level's buyer volume and volume times one minus buy share to its seller volume. In the up/down candle method, buy share is one when close is at or above open and zero otherwise. In the body-weighted method, buy share is (1 + clamp((close - open) / (high - low), -1, 1)) / 2; use a zero body ratio when high equals low. Start a pending touch with its side determined by the source close relative to the level and its thresholds fixed using the current ATR.
Calculation: Displayed level volumes = Optionally consolidate levels separately within each bank in insertion order: merge each level into the last previously formed group whose current price is within a configured absolute distance. Set the group price to the average of the two prices weighted by their touch counts, using a minimum weight of one for each; sum their touch counts and accumulated buyer and seller volumes. Without consolidation, each level is its own group. Buy fraction is group buyer volume divided by total buyer and seller volume, or one half when total volume is zero.
Long: Any displayed level's buy fraction is at or above the dominance threshold.
Short: Any displayed level's buy fraction is at or below one minus the dominance threshold AND below the dominance threshold.
