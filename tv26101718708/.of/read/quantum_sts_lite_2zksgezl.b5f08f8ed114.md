Calculation: Session entry levels = after a configured session ends, set the long level to that session's low minus the next configured session's long offset and the short level to its high plus that next session's short offset; clear both levels whenever any configured session starts, before setting levels for sessions ending on that same bar.
Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled session, take the highest high and lowest low of chart bars starting with its first bar and continuing while elapsed time from that bar's opening time is less than the configured range duration; finalize on the first bar outside that duration. Reset the range and both sides' signal and retest states at each enabled session start and each new day; invalidate the range and pending retests when its owning session ends.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) * volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting before including the current bar whenever the selected session, day, week, or month anchor starts; a session anchor resets at the start of any configured session.
Long: Close crosses the session long level in either direction AND no previous long-level cross has occurred since that level was last cleared.
Short: Close crosses the session short level in either direction AND no previous short-level cross has occurred since that level was last cleared.
Long: On a confirmed bar with a finalized opening range, close > opening-range high AND no prior long opening-range signal has occurred for that range AND either retest is disabled OR a prior confirmed bar closed above the opening-range high and the current low <= opening-range high.
Short: On a confirmed bar with a finalized opening range, close < opening-range low AND no prior short opening-range signal has occurred for that range AND either retest is disabled OR a prior confirmed bar closed below the opening-range low and the current high >= opening-range low.
Long: Close crosses above anchored VWAP.
Short: Close crosses below anchored VWAP.
Long: Shorter-period close EMA >= longer-period close EMA.
Short: Shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA.
