Calculation: Long Level = low of the preceding completed session in the sequence Asia → London → NY Pre-Open → NY AM → NY PM → Asia, minus the configured long offset; valid until any session starts.
Calculation: Short Level = high of the preceding completed session in that sequence, plus the configured short offset; valid until any session starts.
Calculation: Opening Range High/Low = highest high / lowest low from an enabled session’s opening bar through the configured opening-range duration; use only after the range is finalized and before its owning session ends.
Long: Long Level is enabled and active AND the close crosses the Long Level; OR the Opening Range is finalized and active, long signals are enabled, and (retest mode is off AND close > Opening Range High AND no long signal has fired for this range; OR retest mode is on AND a prior bar closed above Opening Range High AND low <= Opening Range High AND close > Opening Range High AND no long signal has fired for this range).
Short: Short Level is enabled and active AND the close crosses the Short Level; OR the Opening Range is finalized and active, short signals are enabled, and (retest mode is off AND close < Opening Range Low AND no short signal has fired for this range; OR retest mode is on AND a prior bar closed below Opening Range Low AND high >= Opening Range Low AND close < Opening Range Low AND no short signal has fired for this range).
