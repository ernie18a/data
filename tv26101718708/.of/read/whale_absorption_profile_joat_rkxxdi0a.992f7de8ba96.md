Calculation: Buy and sell volume per chart bar = sum volume from lower-timeframe bars, classified as buy when the close is at least the previous lower-timeframe close and sell otherwise; compare the first lower-timeframe close with the previous chart bar’s close. Use completed lower-timeframe bars in completed chart bars.
Calculation: Profile bins = divide the price range across the lookback chart bars into equal-width bins, using each bar’s HL2 as its price and assigning its reconstructed buy and sell volume to the bin containing that HL2. The range uses HL2 extrema when configured, otherwise high and low extrema.
Calculation: Bin volume percentile cutoff = value at floor(p × (N − 1)) in the ascending list of positive bin volumes; use the configured percentile p and the positive-volume bins in the current profile.
Calculation: Bin absorption score = min(bin buy volume, bin sell volume) / max(minimum price increment × 4, |bin buy volume − bin sell volume|); absorption percentile cutoff uses this score across positive-volume bins, selected by the same percentile rule.
Long: There is a profile bin with total volume at least the configured whale-volume percentile cutoff AND (buy volume − sell volume) / total volume at least the configured whale-delta threshold AND buy volume > sell volume; OR max(buy volume, sell volume) is at least the configured imbalance ratio × max(0.000001, min(buy volume, sell volume)) AND buy volume > sell volume.
Short: There is a profile bin with total volume at least the configured whale-volume percentile cutoff AND |buy volume − sell volume| / total volume at least the configured whale-delta threshold AND sell volume > buy volume; OR max(buy volume, sell volume) is at least the configured imbalance ratio × max(0.000001, min(buy volume, sell volume)) AND sell volume > buy volume.
