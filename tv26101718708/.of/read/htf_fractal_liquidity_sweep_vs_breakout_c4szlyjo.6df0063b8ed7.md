Calculation: BSL = untouched higher-timeframe fractal high: a higher-timeframe bar whose high is the highest of a 5-bar window (2 bars each side) and whose high is not reached (high >= level) by either of the 2 following higher-timeframe bars. BSL levels within a small tolerance of each other that are still untouched merge into the higher one. A BSL stays active until a chart bar's high >= level (touch).
Calculation: SSL = untouched higher-timeframe fractal low: a higher-timeframe bar whose low is the lowest of a 5-bar window (2 bars each side) and whose low is not reached (low <= level) by either of the 2 following higher-timeframe bars. SSL levels within a small tolerance of each other that are still untouched merge into the lower one. An SSL stays active until a chart bar's low <= level (touch).
Calculation: Resolution = after a touch, wait until the higher-timeframe bar containing the touch is complete. Decide on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar, using the close of that completed higher-timeframe bar (the chart timeframe is lower than the higher timeframe). Each level is resolved once and then removed.
Long: (a BSL was touched AND, at Resolution, the completed higher-timeframe bar's close > BSL level) OR (an SSL was touched AND, at Resolution, the completed higher-timeframe bar's close >= SSL level)
Short: (an SSL was touched AND, at Resolution, the completed higher-timeframe bar's close < SSL level) OR (a BSL was touched AND, at Resolution, the completed higher-timeframe bar's close <= BSL level)
