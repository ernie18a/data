Calculation: swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side of the pivot; last swing high/low = most recent confirmed one
Calculation: bull sweep (bar) = low < last swing low AND close > last swing low; bear sweep (bar) = high > last swing high AND close < last swing high; a sweep stays valid for a limited number of bars after it occurs and is cleared when a BOS in the same direction occurs
Calculation: bull BOS = valid bull sweep AND close > last swing high AND previous close <= last swing high; bear BOS = valid bear sweep AND close < last swing low AND previous close >= last swing low
Calculation: structure state = long after a bull BOS, short after a bear BOS; it persists until the opposite BOS
Calculation: bull OB zone = high-low range of the most recent bearish candle (close < open) before the bull BOS bar, searched within a limited lookback; bear OB zone = high-low range of the most recent bullish candle before the bear BOS bar; a zone is discarded when close goes through its far side (bull zone: close < zone low; bear zone: close > zone high); wick-only breaches do not discard it
Calculation: bull breaker zone = while structure state is long, when the 3 previous candles are all bearish and close > highest open of those 3, zone = [lowest low, highest high] of those 3 candles; bear breaker zone = while structure state is short, when the 3 previous candles are all bullish and close < lowest open of those 3, zone = [lowest low, highest high] of those 3; discarded when close goes through the far side (bull: close < zone low; bear: close > zone high)
Calculation: bull FVG zone = while structure state is long, when high 2 bars ago < current low and the gap (low - high 2 bars ago) >= a minimum size, zone = [high 2 bars ago, current low]; bear FVG zone = while structure state is short, when low 2 bars ago > current high and the gap >= minimum size, zone = [current high, low 2 bars ago]; the newest FVG replaces the previous one
Calculation: a zone (OB, FVG, breaker) is consumed after it triggers an entry and cannot trigger again; zone tap = close inside the zone (between zone low and zone high inclusive)
Calculation: HTF bias = sign of (close - EMA of close) computed on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming; +1 if close > EMA, -1 if close < EMA
Calculation: stop reference zone = the zone that was tapped at entry, priority OB, then FVG, then breaker; long stop = reference zone low - small buffer; short stop = reference zone high + small buffer; long target = entry + (entry - stop) * R; short target = entry - (stop - entry) * R, with R a fixed reward-to-risk ratio (>= 1)
Long: no open position AND bar time is inside the allowed trading session AND structure state is long AND HTF bias = +1 AND close taps an active bull OB zone OR bull FVG zone OR bull breaker zone; entry price = close
Short: no open position AND bar time is inside the allowed trading session AND structure state is short AND HTF bias = -1 AND close taps an active bear OB zone OR bear FVG zone OR bear breaker zone; entry price = close
Exit: long, high >= long target OR low <= long stop
Exit: short, low <= short target OR high >= short stop
