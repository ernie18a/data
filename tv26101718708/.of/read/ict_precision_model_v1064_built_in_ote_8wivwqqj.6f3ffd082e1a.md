Calculation: Session eligibility = compare the bar's New York local time with parameterized trading windows and cutoff; also classify it as a high-priority window, a later window, and a continuation window. Reset the signal count at each new trading day.
Calculation: Running midpoint = midpoint of the current trading day's highest high and lowest low, including the current bar. The source's daily and session midpoints are identical.
Calculation: Swing levels = latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and low, each confirmed after its parameterized number of bars on either side. An equal-high or equal-low level is the midpoint of the latest two corresponding pivots if their difference is within a price tolerance; retain it until replaced and clear it at the start of each trading day. Apply the same calculation on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; repeated higher-timeframe pivot values are processed on every chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap = for each of three successively higher timeframes, using the still-forming bar, a bullish gap is current low > high two bars earlier and spans those prices; a bearish gap is current high < low two bars earlier and spans those prices. For each direction retain the latest gap selected on a new higher-timeframe bar, preferring the highest timeframe when several qualify. A chart bar taps a gap when its high-low range overlaps it. After checking the tap, clear a bullish gap if close < its bottom and a bearish gap if close > its top.
Calculation: Liquidity = a bullish sweep occurs when low is below a level by more than a price buffer AND close > that level; a bearish sweep occurs when high is above a level by more than the buffer AND close < that level. Levels are the previous completed higher-timeframe daily and weekly lows or highs, the latest chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe swing and equal levels, and the prior chart bar's running daily low or high. Bullish or bearish liquidity also includes a tap of the corresponding higher-timeframe gap. Major liquidity includes the previous daily or weekly level, higher-timeframe swing or equal level, or higher-timeframe gap. Equal liquidity includes either equal level.
Calculation: Body = absolute close minus open; average body = its moving average. Strong body = body > average body multiplied by a parameter. Bull trend = shorter EMA of close > longer EMA AND close > shorter EMA; bear trend reverses both comparisons. Bull structure = close > highest high of the preceding swing lookback AND low > lowest low of that lookback; bear structure = close < preceding lowest low AND high < preceding highest high. Range expansion = highest high minus lowest low over a separate lookback > ATR multiplied by a parameter. Bull or bear impulse = corresponding trend AND structure AND strong body AND range expansion. Bull or bear correction = corresponding trend AND no corresponding impulse AND close remains beyond the longer EMA in the trend direction AND body < average body.
Calculation: Consolidation = highest high minus lowest low over a lookback < average bar range over a longer lookback multiplied by a parameter AND ATR < its moving average multiplied by a parameter AND absolute close change over a separate lookback < ATR multiplied by a parameter AND body < average body multiplied by a parameter. Deep chop = consolidation AND neither impulse nor correction; light chop = consolidation AND an impulse or correction.
Calculation: Signal permission = eligible trading time AND signals today < daily limit AND no deep chop. An opposite-direction signal is blocked within a parameterized number of bars after the last signal unless its close breaks the last signal bar's high for a long or low for a short, its body exceeds average body by a parameterized multiplier, and its candle direction matches the new signal. Each emitted signal updates the count, direction, bar, high, and low. Evaluate candidates in this order and emit only the first: long pivot, short pivot, long reversal, short reversal, long setup confirmation, short setup confirmation, long continuation, short continuation.
Calculation: Setup state = start neutral. Before processing each bar, calculate bullish and bearish manipulation candidates from signal permission, liquidity, a shared manipulation cooldown, and the state at the start of the bar: bullish requires state <= neutral and bearish requires state >= neutral. Expire a non-neutral state when bars since its manipulation exceed a limit. Process bullish then bearish candidates; each sets its signed manipulation state, records the bar's high and low and start bar, starts the cooldown, and clears its stored reference, displacement, gap, touches, and retest count. Thus a simultaneous bearish candidate overrides a bullish candidate. In bullish manipulation state, retain the open of the latest bearish candle whose low >= manipulation low AND high <= manipulation high multiplied by a parameter; advance when a bullish candle closes above that open. Mirror this for bearish state, retaining a bullish candle's open when high <= manipulation high AND low >= manipulation low multiplied by a parameter, and advance when a bearish candle closes below it. Then advance on a same-direction strong candle closing beyond the preceding swing-lookback high or low, recording its high and low as displacement. Then advance when a chart-timeframe bullish gap has low > high two bars earlier or a bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier; store that gap. All stages can advance on the same bar. After a setup signal, reset to neutral; pivot and continuation signals do not reset the setup.
Calculation: Setup retracements = for a bullish setup measure from manipulation low to displacement high and subtract parameterized reversal, continuation, and upper and lower optimal-entry fractions of that range from displacement high; for a bearish setup measure from displacement low to manipulation high and add the corresponding fractions to displacement low. The reversal fraction is smaller than the continuation fraction, which is smaller than the optimal-entry fractions. Mark a retracement touched once a bar overlaps the optimal-entry interval or crosses either single retracement level; retain that flag until reset. The setup midpoint is the midpoint of the manipulation-to-displacement range. Count each transition from outside to overlapping the stored gap as a retest, including the bar on which the gap is stored. A retest qualifies after a parameterized minimum count, or at a lower count during the later window if no signal has occurred that day.
Calculation: Rejection = bar range > zero AND body <= a parameterized fraction of range AND the directional wick >= body multiplied by a parameter AND a bullish candle closes > prior high for a long, or a bearish candle closes < prior low for a short. Micro confirmation = a bullish candle closes > bullish setup midpoint, or a bearish candle closes < bearish setup midpoint.
Calculation: Setup score = sum parameterized weights for current-bar directional liquidity, major liquidity, equal liquidity, time-window class, completed setup state, current-bar retracement overlap with priority optimal-entry then continuation then reversal, rejection OR micro confirmation, trend, impulse or correction state, chart-timeframe class, and chop penalty. Opposite impulse has a negative weight; deep chop has a larger penalty than light chop. Compare the total with a minimum score.
Calculation: Pivot score = sum parameterized weights for directional liquidity, major liquidity, equal liquidity, time-window class, directional trend, and chart-timeframe class; compare with a minimum score.
Calculation: Continuation zone = on each permitted continuation-window bar with a directional impulse, matching candle direction, body > average body multiplied by a parameter larger than the strong-body multiplier, and close on the trend side of the running midpoint, replace that direction's stored zone. For a bullish zone use low to open; for a bearish zone use open to high. Store its midpoint and creation bar. A retest requires a later bar overlapping the zone. Clear the corresponding zone after its continuation signal.
Long: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND setup state = neutral AND bullish liquidity AND low < running midpoint AND close > open AND close > previous high AND lower wick >= body multiplied by a parameter AND pivot score >= minimum.
Short: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND setup state = neutral AND bearish liquidity AND high > running midpoint AND close < open AND close < previous low AND upper wick >= body multiplied by a parameter AND pivot score >= minimum.
Long: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND bullish setup complete AND manipulation low < running midpoint AND retracement previously or currently touched AND bullish rejection AND current bullish liquidity AND close > preceding swing-lookback high AND strong body AND setup score >= minimum.
Short: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND bearish setup complete AND manipulation high > running midpoint AND retracement previously or currently touched AND bearish rejection AND current bearish liquidity AND close < preceding swing-lookback low AND strong body AND setup score >= minimum.
Long: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND bullish setup complete AND manipulation low < running midpoint AND qualifying gap retest count AND bullish micro confirmation AND setup score >= minimum.
Short: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND bearish setup complete AND manipulation high > running midpoint AND qualifying gap retest count AND bearish micro confirmation AND setup score >= minimum.
Long: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND setup state = neutral AND no correction AND bullish impulse AND bullish continuation-zone retest AND close > open AND close > continuation-zone midpoint AND close > running midpoint.
Short: signal permission AND opposite-direction block cleared AND setup state = neutral AND no correction AND bearish impulse AND bearish continuation-zone retest AND close < open AND close < continuation-zone midpoint AND close < running midpoint.
