Calculation: Pivot stream = On each confirmed chart bar, confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks. If both occur, retain the one farther from the last accepted pivot, choosing the high on a tie; if none exists, choose the high. Replace the last pivot with a more extreme same-type pivot; append an opposite-type pivot only when its absolute percentage change from the last pivot is at least the deviation threshold multiplied by (1 + volatility multiplier × ATR / close). Evaluate deviation on the confirmation bar. Process a pivot as new only when its pivot bar differs from the last processed pivot bar.
Calculation: Wave state = Track bullish and bearish sequences independently using the pivot stream. For bullish sequences, start at a low P0, replace P0 with a lower low while awaiting P1, then accept a later high P1 > P0, a later low P0 < P2 < P1, a later high P3 > P1 with P3 − P2 >= minimum wave-length ratio × (P1 − P0), a later low P1 < P4 < P3, and a later high P5 > P3 with P3 − P2 >= min(P1 − P0, P5 − P4). For bearish sequences reverse high and low and all price inequalities. A failed P2 or P4 test resets the sequence; a P3 beyond P1 that fails its length test, or a P5 beyond P3 that fails its length test, also resets it. Other nonqualifying pivots leave the sequence unchanged. Before processing pivots, reset an unfinished sequence when bars since its latest accepted wave pivot exceed the per-wave limit. A completed sequence remains at P5 until its exit fires; then reset its state. Record RSI at the P3 and P5 pivot bars and volume-spike status on the P3 confirmation bar.
Calculation: Wave scores = Let R(a,b,c) = abs(c − b) / abs(b − a), or zero if the denominator is zero, and let each near-target test mean absolute distance <= the Fibonacci tolerance. Wave 2 score is the score assigned to the first matching ordered retracement target, otherwise the score assigned to an inclusive fallback retracement range, otherwise zero. Wave 4 score is the weighted sum of a wave 4 retracement score, a wave 3 strength score, and a volume score: calculate the retracement score from the first matching ordered near-target test, then an inclusive fallback range, otherwise zero; calculate wave 3 strength from the first matching ordered near-target test on abs(P3 − P2) / abs(P1 − P0), then an inclusive fallback range, then a lower-bound test, otherwise zero. The volume score uses its high value when the P3 confirmation-bar volume exceeds its volume-SMA multiplier or volume filtering is disabled; otherwise it uses its low value. Clamp scores to their allowed range.
Calculation: Wave 5 score = Form a Fibonacci score by summing separate scores for R(P0,P1,P2), abs(P3 − P2) / abs(P1 − P0), R(P2,P3,P4), and abs(P5 − P4) / abs(P1 − P0); each component uses the first matching ordered near-target test, then an inclusive fallback range, otherwise zero. Use zero for a ratio with a zero denominator. Add a wave-ratio score with separate points for abs(P3 − P2) >= min(abs(P1 − P0), abs(P5 − P4)), R(P0,P1,P2) within an inclusive range, abs(P4 − P3) < abs(P2 − P1), and abs(P5 − P4) <= an extension multiplier × abs(P3 − P2). The time-symmetry score is the maximum of zero and full score minus the median absolute relative deviation of the durations of waves 1, 3, and 5 from their mean, scaled to the score range; use zero if the mean is zero. Combine the three scores using normalized configurable weights, using fallback weights when their sum is zero, add a positive adjustment if the corresponding regime passes or a negative adjustment otherwise, and clamp to the score range. Calculate and store this score when P5 is accepted.
Calculation: Confidence threshold = The wave-specific base threshold, optionally increased by a capped multiple of ATR / close and capped at a maximum. Recalculate it on each decision bar.
Calculation: Regime = When enabled, require the chart-timeframe shorter EMA of close to be above the longer EMA for bullish or below it for bearish, and chart-timeframe ADX above its threshold. When higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled and a higher timeframe is available, also require its shorter EMA above its longer EMA for bullish or below it for bearish, and its ADX above the threshold; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Otherwise use the chart-timeframe EMA relationship for confirmation. When the regime filter is disabled, the regime passes.
Calculation: Signal state = Evaluate on confirmed chart bars after a warmup equal to the greater of the longer chart EMA lookback and a minimum bar count. A wave 2 or wave 4 entry can fire only once while its corresponding wave stage remains active; rearm it when the stage falls below that stage. Entry signals share a cooldown measured from the most recent entry or exit signal, including signals of the opposite direction. Evaluate all signals before updating their fired flags and the shared signal bar.
Long: Bullish wave state = P2 AND wave 2 score >= wave 2 confidence threshold AND bullish regime passes AND volume SMA > minimum liquidity threshold OR liquidity filtering is disabled, AND chart ADX >= choppiness multiplier × ADX threshold AND shared cooldown has elapsed AND the bullish wave 2 entry has not fired in this stage.
Long: Bullish wave state = P4 AND wave 4 score >= wave 4 confidence threshold AND bullish regime passes AND volume SMA > minimum liquidity threshold OR liquidity filtering is disabled, AND chart ADX >= choppiness multiplier × ADX threshold AND shared cooldown has elapsed AND the bullish wave 4 entry has not fired in this stage AND wave 4 entry is enabled AND P3 confirmation-bar volume > its volume-SMA multiplier OR volume filtering is disabled.
Short: Bearish wave state = P2 AND wave 2 score >= wave 2 confidence threshold AND bearish regime passes AND volume SMA > minimum liquidity threshold OR liquidity filtering is disabled, AND chart ADX >= choppiness multiplier × ADX threshold AND shared cooldown has elapsed AND the bearish wave 2 entry has not fired in this stage.
Short: Bearish wave state = P4 AND wave 4 score >= wave 4 confidence threshold AND bearish regime passes AND volume SMA > minimum liquidity threshold OR liquidity filtering is disabled, AND chart ADX >= choppiness multiplier × ADX threshold AND shared cooldown has elapsed AND the bearish wave 4 entry has not fired in this stage AND wave 4 entry is enabled AND P3 confirmation-bar volume > its volume-SMA multiplier OR volume filtering is disabled.
Exit: long, bullish wave state = P5 AND stored wave 5 score >= current wave 5 confidence threshold AND (RSI divergence filtering is disabled OR P3 or P5 RSI is unavailable OR P5 > P3 AND RSI at P5 < RSI at P3) AND the bullish wave 5 exit has not fired in this stage.
Exit: short, bearish wave state = P5 AND stored wave 5 score >= current wave 5 confidence threshold AND (RSI divergence filtering is disabled OR P3 or P5 RSI is unavailable OR P5 < P3 AND RSI at P5 > RSI at P3) AND the bearish wave 5 exit has not fired in this stage.
