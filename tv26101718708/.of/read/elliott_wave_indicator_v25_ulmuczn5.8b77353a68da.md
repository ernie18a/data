Calculation: Confirmed pivots = A pivot high/low is confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on both sides; process it only on a confirmed chart bar.
Calculation: Zigzag pivots = Keep alternating pivot types. Replace the last pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type; append an opposite-type pivot only when its percentage distance from the last pivot is at least the configurable deviation multiplied by (1 + 10 × ATR(14)/close). If both pivot types confirm together, process the one farther from the last zigzag price (high on a tie); with no prior pivot, process the high.
Calculation: Five-wave structure = Build from five successive alternating zigzag pivots after an initial pivot. Bullish: low W0, high W1 above W0, low W2 strictly between W0 and W1, high W3 above W1 with W3−W2 at least 0.9×(W1−W0), low W4 strictly between W1 and W3, then high W5 above W3 with W3−W2 at least the smaller of W1−W0 and W5−W4. Bearish structure is the price-reversed equivalent. A failed required inequality resets that structure; each wave must complete within a configurable maximum number of chart bars.
Calculation: Wave 2 confidence = 100 for retracement within tolerance of 0.618; else 85 near 0.500, 75 near 0.786, 60 near 0.382, 40 if between 0.236 and 0.886 inclusive, otherwise 0. Retracement is distance from W1 to W2 divided by W0-to-W1 move size.
Calculation: Wave 4 confidence = 0.5×W4 Fibonacci score + 0.3×W3 strength score + 0.2×W3 volume score. W4 Fibonacci score: 100 near 0.382, else 85 near 0.236, 70 near 0.500, 40 for retracement 0.146–0.618 inclusive, otherwise 0. W3 strength: 100 when W3/W1 length is near 1.618, else 85 near 2.618, 60 for ratio 1.0–4.236, 30 for ratio at least 0.9, otherwise 0. Volume score is 100 if W3 has a volume spike, otherwise 30 when volume filtering is enabled and 70 when disabled. “Near” means absolute difference no greater than the configurable Fibonacci tolerance. Retracement uses the relevant wave length as denominator.
Calculation: Wave 5 confidence = 0.5×Fibonacci score + 0.3×wave-ratio score + 0.2×time-symmetry score, with configurable weights normalized to sum to 1 when combining these three scores. Fibonacci score sums: W2 gives 25 near 0.618, else 20 near 0.500, 15 near 0.786, 5 for 0.382–0.886; W3 gives 35 near 1.618, else 25 near 2.618, 15 near 1.000, 5 for 1.0–4.236; W4 gives 20 near 0.382, else 15 near 0.236, 10 near 0.500, 5 for 0.146–0.618; W5/W1 gives 20 near 1.000, else 15 near 0.618, 10 near 1.618, 5 for 0.382–1.618. Each clause is evaluated in order and contributes zero if none applies. Wave-ratio score adds 40 if W3 is at least the smaller of W1 and W5, 30 if W2/W1 is 0.382–0.886, 15 if W4 length is less than W2, and 15 if W5 is no more than 3×W3 (or 2×W3 when extensions are disabled). Time-symmetry score is 100 minus 100 times the median of the three absolute relative deviations of W1, W3, and W5 durations from their mean, floored at 0. Add 5 if the current regime is valid for the structure, otherwise subtract 5; clamp confidence to 0–100. Fibonacci “near” uses the configurable tolerance.
Calculation: Dynamic confidence threshold = base confidence threshold plus min(15, 200×ATR(14)/close) when volatility adjustment is enabled; otherwise the base threshold.
Calculation: Valid trend regime = EMA(fast) above EMA(slow) for bullish, below for bearish, and chart-timeframe ADX above the configurable threshold; also require higher-timeframe EMA(20) above EMA(50) for bullish or below for bearish and higher-timeframe ADX above that threshold. The higher timeframe is the next mapped timeframe above the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar. If no mapped higher timeframe is available, require only the chart-timeframe EMA relationship. Choppy means chart-timeframe ADX below 0.8×the threshold.
Calculation: Volume and liquidity filters = Volume spike means current volume > SMA(volume, 20)×the configurable multiplier. Liquid means SMA(volume, 20) exceeds the configurable minimum average volume. Adaptive settings may disable either filter; when disabled that filter passes.
Calculation: Bullish W5 divergence = W5 price pivot is higher than W3 price pivot and RSI at W5 pivot is lower than RSI at W3 pivot. Bearish W5 divergence = W5 price pivot is lower than W3 price pivot and RSI at W5 pivot is higher than RSI at W3 pivot. RSI uses the configurable length.
Long: Confirmed chart bar and warmed up (bar index ≥ max(slow EMA length, 100)) AND bullish structure is at completed W2 or W4 AND its corresponding confidence is at least its dynamic threshold AND valid bullish trend regime AND liquid AND not choppy AND allowed long direction AND cooldown elapsed; for W4 also require W4 entries enabled, valid W3 length and (if enabled) W3 volume spike, and valid bullish projection (W4 confidence meets its threshold and regime is valid). Permit only one signal per structure stage.
Short: Confirmed chart bar and warmed up (bar index ≥ max(slow EMA length, 100)) AND bearish structure is at completed W2 or W4 AND its corresponding confidence is at least its dynamic threshold AND valid bearish trend regime AND liquid AND not choppy AND allowed short direction AND cooldown elapsed; for W4 also require W4 entries enabled, valid W3 length and (if enabled) W3 volume spike, and valid bearish projection (W4 confidence meets its threshold and regime is valid). Permit only one signal per structure stage.
Exit: Long, confirmed chart bar and warmed up AND bullish structure completes W5 AND W5 confidence is at least its dynamic threshold AND (if RSI divergence filtering is enabled) bullish W5 divergence.
Exit: Short, confirmed chart bar and warmed up AND bearish structure completes W5 AND W5 confidence is at least its dynamic threshold AND (if RSI divergence filtering is enabled) bearish W5 divergence.
