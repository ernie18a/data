Calculation: Adaptive MACD histogram = fast-length EMA of price minus longer-length EMA, smoothed by a signal EMA, then normalized as a rolling z-score; use the selected fixed, volatility-driven, or cycle-driven length mode.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar price changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Regime = Trend Bull when ADX and efficiency ratio exceed their trend thresholds and close is above its EMA; Trend Bear when both exceed their thresholds and close is below its EMA; otherwise Volatile when the volatility-clustering z-score exceeds its threshold, else Range.
Calculation: MACD direction = sign of the fast-length EMA minus the longer-length EMA, less its signal EMA; use this calculation on each of the four higher timeframes and the separate higher timeframe bias, using the available higher-timeframe value (which may still be forming).
Calculation: MTF agreement = count of the four higher-timeframe MACD directions matching the candidate direction.
Calculation: CVD direction = sign of the five-chart-bar change in cumulative signed volume, summing lower-timeframe bar volume as positive when its close is at or above its open and negative otherwise; use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, or chart-bar signed volume if unavailable.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed price swing low below the preceding confirmed swing low while the normalized MACD histogram at the newer pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot; regular bearish divergence is the corresponding higher swing high in price with a lower histogram value.
Calculation: Outer volatility bands = rolling volume-weighted price mean plus or minus two weighted standard deviations; use unit weights when volume is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Fade direction = long when low reaches or falls below the lower outer band and close finishes above it, unless regime is Trend Bear; short when high reaches or exceeds the upper outer band and close finishes below it, unless regime is Trend Bull. Fade remains active for five bars after its signal.
Calculation: Conviction = absolute value of the weighted mean of normalized MACD histogram score (z-score divided by 2 and clamped to [-1,1]), MTF net agreement, CVD direction, regime score (+0.6 Trend Bull, -0.6 Trend Bear, otherwise 0), and active fade direction; convert to a 0–100 score. Direction is the sign of that weighted mean.
Long: MACD line crosses above its signal line OR regular bullish divergence OR long fade OR (conviction direction is positive AND conviction meets its minimum AND regime is not Trend Bear AND MTF agreement meets its minimum AND CVD does not oppose the direction AND absolute normalized MACD histogram is at least 0.5 AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND bar is confirmed AND position is not long).
Short: MACD line crosses below its signal line OR regular bearish divergence OR short fade OR (conviction direction is negative AND conviction meets its minimum AND regime is not Trend Bull AND MTF agreement meets its minimum AND CVD does not oppose the direction AND absolute normalized MACD histogram is at least 0.5 AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish AND bar is confirmed AND position is not short).
Exit: long, high reaches entry price plus twice the initial ATR-based stop distance OR low reaches the stop (initially entry minus stop distance, moved to entry after high reaches entry plus one stop distance).
Exit: short, low reaches entry price minus twice the initial ATR-based stop distance OR high reaches the stop (initially entry plus stop distance, moved to entry after low reaches entry minus one stop distance).
