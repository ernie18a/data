Calculation: Adaptive MACD = subtract a slower adaptive EMA from a faster adaptive EMA of the selected OHLC price source, optionally prefiltered by a volume-weighted moving average; its signal is an adaptive EMA of that difference. An adaptive EMA updates as previous EMA + 2/(current length + 1) × (source − previous EMA), initialized to its source. Lengths are fixed, derived from the efficiency ratio, or derived from an Ehlers homodyne dominant-cycle estimate of (high + low)/2; in adaptive modes the slow and signal lengths are rounded multiples of the fast length, and the fast length is bounded. For efficiency-ratio adaptation, fast length decreases linearly from its maximum to its minimum as efficiency ratio increases from zero to one. For cycle adaptation, fast length is the bounded rounded half-cycle estimate.
Calculation: Normalized histogram = rolling z-score of adaptive MACD minus its signal; use zero when the rolling standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Trend regime = ADX exceeds its threshold AND efficiency ratio of the selected price source exceeds its threshold; bullish trend additionally requires close > its regime EMA, otherwise the trend is bearish.
Calculation: Outer volume-weighted bands = rolling volume-weighted mean of the selected price source ± a standard-deviation multiplier times the square root of the rolling volume-weighted mean of squared source minus the squared rolling volume-weighted mean; replace nonpositive or missing volume with unit weight.
Calculation: Directional volume = sum over a configurable chart-bar lookback of lower-timeframe volume signed positive when that lower-timeframe bar closes at or above its open and negative otherwise; use the chart bar's similarly signed volume when lower-timeframe bars are unavailable. Use completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar, including the still-forming lower-timeframe bar for an intrabar calculation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe MACD direction = sign of the ordinary close-based MACD histogram, using a faster EMA, a slower EMA and a signal EMA. For each higher timeframe use the last completed bar in historical calculations and the still-forming bar in live intrabar calculations.
Calculation: Active fade direction = direction of the most recent outer-band rejection within a configurable number of chart bars, or zero if none; if both rejections occur on the same bar, use the downward direction.
Calculation: Combined score = weighted average of the available components: normalized histogram divided by a scale and clamped to [−1, 1]; optionally, the number of higher-timeframe positive MACD directions minus the number of negative directions, divided by the number of higher timeframes; sign of directional volume when volume is available; a positive constant in a bullish trend, its negative in a bearish trend, and zero otherwise; and active fade direction when fade is enabled and active. Include a component's weight in the denominator only when that component is available or enabled as specified. Treat unavailable external reference, volatility-index, open-interest and cross-asset feeds as absent. Conviction is the rounded absolute combined score multiplied by a percentage scale.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = ATR at the entry bar multiplied by a configurable stop multiplier; retain this distance and entry close until exit or reversal. Evaluate a new entry or reversal before exits on the same bar. For a long, move the stop from entry minus initial risk distance to entry when high reaches entry plus initial risk distance; for a short, move the stop from entry plus initial risk distance to entry when low reaches entry minus initial risk distance. Evaluate the target before the stop, including on the entry bar.
Long: Adaptive MACD crosses above its signal.
Short: Adaptive MACD crosses below its signal.
Long: Normalized histogram >= 0.
Short: Normalized histogram < 0.
Long: Trend regime AND close > its regime EMA.
Short: Trend regime AND close <= its regime EMA.
Long: A newly confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low AND the normalized histogram at the new pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot.
Short: A newly confirmed price pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the normalized histogram at the new pivot is below its value at the preceding pivot.
Long: Low <= lower outer volume-weighted band AND close > lower outer volume-weighted band AND the regime is not a bearish trend.
Short: High >= upper outer volume-weighted band AND close < upper outer volume-weighted band AND the regime is not a bullish trend.
Long: Combined score > 0 AND conviction >= its minimum AND the regime is not a bearish trend AND normalized histogram magnitude >= its minimum AND directional volume is nonnegative when volume is available AND, if higher-timeframe agreement is enabled, the count of positive higher-timeframe MACD directions >= its minimum AND, if the higher-timeframe bias gate is enabled, its higher-timeframe MACD direction >= 0 AND position side is flat or short.
Short: Combined score < 0 AND conviction >= its minimum AND the regime is not a bullish trend AND normalized histogram magnitude >= its minimum AND directional volume is nonpositive when volume is available AND, if higher-timeframe agreement is enabled, the count of negative higher-timeframe MACD directions >= its minimum AND, if the higher-timeframe bias gate is enabled, its higher-timeframe MACD direction <= 0 AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target multiple × initial risk distance OR low <= the current long stop.
Exit: short, low <= entry price − target multiple × initial risk distance OR high >= the current short stop.
