Calculation: Baseline = selected HMA, ALMA, KAMA, T3, VWMA, or EMA of the selected price source; use EMA instead of VWMA when current volume is not positive, and use the source when the selected average is unavailable.
Calculation: Adaptive offset = selected volatility multiplied by the trail multiplier and by (1 − adaptivity × 0.4 × min(abs(EMA-smoothed RSI of the selected price source − 50) / 50, 1)); selected volatility is ATR, standard deviation of close, or their mean, using the same lookback for both.
Calculation: Trail and regime = initially bullish with trail = baseline − adaptive offset when close >= baseline, otherwise bearish with trail = baseline + adaptive offset. On each subsequent bar, in a bullish regime set trail to max(previous trail, baseline − adaptive offset), then switch to bearish and reset trail to baseline + adaptive offset if close < that trail; in a bearish regime set trail to min(previous trail, baseline + adaptive offset), then switch to bullish and reset trail to baseline − adaptive offset if close > that trail. Calculate from the first bar, including during warm-up.
Calculation: Warm-up = bar index >= max(2 × baseline lookback, RSI lookback + RSI smoothing lookback, minimum warm-up parameter), with the first bar indexed zero.
Calculation: Entry filter = volume filter disabled OR current volume <= 0 OR (volume SMA > 0 AND current volume > volume SMA × volume threshold).
Calculation: Initial stop = for a long, either entry price − stop multiplier × entry-bar risk ATR or min(entry-bar low − wick buffer × entry-bar risk ATR, entry price − minimum-distance multiplier × entry-bar risk ATR); for a short, either entry price + stop multiplier × entry-bar risk ATR or max(entry-bar high + wick buffer × entry-bar risk ATR, entry price + minimum-distance multiplier × entry-bar risk ATR). Risk is the absolute difference between entry price and initial stop.
Calculation: First target and final target = entry price plus risk times the respective target multiplier for a long, or entry price minus risk times the respective target multiplier for a short; the final target multiplier is greater than the first target multiplier. Keep these levels fixed at entry.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until the first target has been touched on a bar after entry; if break-even is enabled, use entry price starting on the bar after that first touch. A stop touch on the first target's first-touch bar does not close the trade. Check target and stop touches before processing reversal and entry signals.
Long: Warm-up AND bullish regime.
Short: Warm-up AND bearish regime.
Long: Higher-timeframe filter enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe filter enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA.
Long: EMA-smoothed RSI > upper bullish threshold.
Short: EMA-smoothed RSI < lower bearish threshold.
Long: Warm-up AND previous regime = bearish AND current regime = bullish.
Short: Warm-up AND previous regime = bullish AND current regime = bearish.
Long: Warm-up AND previous regime = bearish AND current regime = bullish AND entry filter AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND position is flat after stop, target, and reversal processing AND entry-bar risk ATR × stop multiplier > 0 AND entry price − initial stop > 0.
Short: Warm-up AND previous regime = bullish AND current regime = bearish AND entry filter AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND position is flat after stop, target, and reversal processing AND entry-bar risk ATR × stop multiplier > 0 AND initial stop − entry price > 0.
Exit: long, bar index > entry bar index AND (high >= final target OR (low <= active stop AND first target was touched on an earlier bar OR low <= active stop AND high < first target)).
Exit: short, bar index > entry bar index AND (low <= final target OR (high >= active stop AND first target was touched on an earlier bar OR high >= active stop AND low > first target)).
Exit: long, warm-up AND previous regime = bullish AND current regime = bearish AND entry filter AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA).
Exit: short, warm-up AND previous regime = bearish AND current regime = bullish AND entry filter AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA).
