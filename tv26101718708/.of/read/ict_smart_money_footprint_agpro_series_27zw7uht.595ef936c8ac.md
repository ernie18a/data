Calculation: Swing high and swing low = the most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and pivot low, each requiring the same number of bars on both sides; update the level on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < swing low − ATR × sweep buffer AND close > swing low AND close > open. Bearish sweep = high > swing high + ATR × sweep buffer AND close < swing high AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND high − low > ATR × displacement multiplier AND close > previous high AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= close-strength threshold. Bearish displacement = close < open AND high − low > ATR × displacement multiplier AND close < previous low AND (high − close) / (high − low) >= close-strength threshold.
Calculation: Bullish lost level = the swing low when previous close >= that swing low AND close < that swing low; bearish lost level = the swing high when previous close <= that swing high AND close > that swing high. Replace each stored level and its bar whenever another loss occurs. Bullish reclaim = close crosses above the stored bullish lost level on a later bar within the reclaim window; bearish reclaim = close crosses below the stored bearish lost level on a later bar within the reclaim window. Clear the corresponding stored level after a reclaim.
Calculation: A bullish sequence occurs when bullish displacement follows the latest bullish sweep within the sequence window, including the same bar, AND max(min(sweep open, sweep close), min(current open, current close)) > min(sweep low, current low) AND the sequence score >= its threshold. A bearish sequence occurs when bearish displacement follows the latest bearish sweep within the sequence window, including the same bar, AND max(sweep high, current high) > min(max(sweep open, sweep close), max(current open, current close)) AND the sequence score >= its threshold. Replace the stored same-direction sweep on each new sweep; clear it after a qualifying sequence or expiration. The score is round(clamp(45 + 25 × clamp((range / ATR − displacement multiplier) / (1.4 × displacement multiplier), 0, 1) + 20 × directional close position, 0, 100)), where range = high − low and directional close position is (close − low) / range for bullish and (high − close) / range for bearish. The score filter may be disabled.
Calculation: Track the latest sequence direction and bar, replacing the previous sequence when a new one occurs. A sequence confirmation occurs once when a same-direction reclaim occurs within the reclaim window after that sequence, including its sequence bar. Expire the tracked sequence after its lifetime.
Calculation: Daily bias score = zero at the start of each day, then on every chart bar add 1 if any bullish sweep, displacement, or reclaim occurs; subtract 1 if any bearish sweep, displacement, or reclaim occurs; add 2 if a bullish sequence or bullish sequence confirmation occurs; subtract 2 if a bearish sequence or bearish sequence confirmation occurs.
Long: bullish sweep OR bullish displacement OR bullish reclaim OR daily bias score >= bullish bias threshold.
Short: bearish sweep OR bearish displacement OR bearish reclaim OR daily bias score <= bearish bias threshold.
