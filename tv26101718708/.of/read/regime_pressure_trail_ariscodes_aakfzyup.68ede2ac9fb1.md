Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR / SMA(ATR, regime lookback), or 1 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive; the regime is low below the lower threshold, high above the higher threshold, and medium otherwise. Select the ATR multiplier for that regime.
Calculation: Trend score = 100 × (RSI-slope weight × min(abs(RSI(source) − RSI(source) at the slope lookback) / RSI-slope scale, 1) + volume weight × clamp((volume / SMA(volume, trend-volume lookback) − 1) / volume scale, 0, 1) + ATR weight × clamp((ATR / SMA(ATR, short ATR lookback) − 1) / ATR scale, 0, 1)); use a zero RSI slope when its past value is unavailable and a ratio of 1 when either average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × (delta weight × abs(signed volume / volume) + volume weight × clamp((volume / SMA(volume, pressure-volume lookback) − 1) / volume scale, 0, 1) + body weight × min(abs(close − open) / (high − low), 1)); signed volume is volume when close >= open and −volume otherwise; use zero for the delta component when volume is nonpositive, zero for the body component when high <= low, and a volume ratio of 1 when its average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: ATR trail = initialize direction as up; on each bar set the lower candidate to source − ATR × regime multiplier and the upper candidate to source + ATR × regime multiplier. If the previous trail exists, flip up when the previous direction is down and close > previous trail, or flip down when the previous direction is up and close < previous trail. In the resulting up direction use max(lower candidate, previous trail) if the previous direction was up, otherwise the lower candidate; in the resulting down direction use min(upper candidate, previous trail) if the previous direction was down, otherwise the upper candidate. When the previous trail is unavailable, use the lower candidate.
Calculation: Cumulative signed volume = running sum of signed volume, initialized at zero.
Calculation: Trail long candidate = previous trail direction is down AND close > previous trail AND trend score >= minimum trend score AND trail engine is enabled AND (regime arming is disabled OR volatility regime is medium or high).
Calculation: Trail short candidate = previous trail direction is up AND close < previous trail AND trend score >= minimum trend score AND trail engine is enabled AND (regime arming is disabled OR volatility regime is medium or high).
Calculation: Pressure long candidate = cumulative signed volume crosses above its SMA AND pressure score >= minimum pressure score AND RSI(close) < overbought threshold AND close >= open AND pressure engine is enabled AND (regime arming is disabled OR volatility regime is low or medium).
Calculation: Pressure short candidate = cumulative signed volume crosses below its SMA AND pressure score >= minimum pressure score AND RSI(close) > oversold threshold AND close < open AND pressure engine is enabled AND (regime arming is disabled OR volatility regime is low or medium).
Calculation: Entry routing = trail-only mode enables the trail engine; pressure-only mode enables the pressure engine; hybrid mode enables both. Regime arming applies only in hybrid regime-arms mode. Hybrid confluence mode requires both same-direction candidates; all other modes accept either candidate. When both candidates trigger in a non-confluence mode, select trail; when opposite-direction candidates occur, select long. For a confluence entry, select trail trade management when trend score >= pressure score, otherwise select pressure trade management.
Calculation: Entry levels = fix levels at the signal bar close. For trail management, the long target is entry price + entry-bar ATR × target multiplier × score boost and the short target is entry price − that amount; the score boost uses the higher boost when entry-bar trend score >= strong-score threshold, otherwise the base boost. The stop is the entry-bar ATR trail, or entry price adjusted against the position by the trail stop percentage if the trail is unavailable. For pressure management, the target and stop are entry price adjusted in favor of and against the position respectively by their pressure percentages.
Long: Position is flat at the start of the bar AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown AND ((confluence mode is enabled AND trail long candidate AND pressure long candidate) OR (confluence mode is disabled AND (trail long candidate OR pressure long candidate))).
Short: Position is flat at the start of the bar AND bars since the last entry >= cooldown AND ((confluence mode is enabled AND trail short candidate AND pressure short candidate) OR (confluence mode is disabled AND (trail short candidate OR pressure short candidate) AND trail long candidate is false AND pressure long candidate is false)).
Exit: long, high >= fixed target OR low <= fixed stop OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period OR (trail-flip exit is enabled AND previous trail direction is up AND close < previous trail).
Exit: short, low <= fixed target OR high >= fixed stop OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period OR (trail-flip exit is enabled AND previous trail direction is down AND close > previous trail).
Long: close >= open AND pressure score >= bullish pressure threshold.
Short: close < open AND pressure score >= bearish pressure threshold.
Long: ATR trail direction is up.
Short: ATR trail direction is down.
Long: signed volume >= 0.
Short: signed volume < 0.
Long: close > session VWAP of typical price.
Short: close <= session VWAP of typical price.
Long: RSI(close) < oversold threshold.
Short: RSI(close) > overbought threshold.
