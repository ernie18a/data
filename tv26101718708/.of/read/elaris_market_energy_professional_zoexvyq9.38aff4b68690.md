Calculation: Market Energy = an EMA-smoothed weighted sum of six components: price impulse (price change over a lookback, normalized by ATR and clamped); candle conviction (signed body ratio, close location within the range, and lower-versus-upper wick balance); relative volume participation (candle conviction scaled by volume above its longer-term average); range expansion (range above its longer-term average, signed by candle conviction and price impulse); trend alignment (price distance from a trend EMA and the EMA slope over a lookback, both normalized by ATR); and momentum (RSI centered on 50 combined with signed net movement divided by total movement over a lookback). Add a directional DMI boost scaled by ADX strength, then clamp the result. All lookbacks, smoothing lengths, weights, and thresholds are configurable.
Calculation: Energy signal line = a more slowly smoothed Market Energy.
Calculation: Energy acceleration = a short EMA of the change in Market Energy over a lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its longer-term average volume.
Calculation: Candle body ratio = absolute close-open divided by high-low.
Calculation: Trend alignment = the average of normalized price distance from the trend EMA and normalized trend EMA slope.
Calculation: Recent compression = the minimum absolute Market Energy over a lookback is at or below the compression threshold.
Calculation: Recent bullish extreme = the highest Market Energy over a lookback is at or above the positive extreme threshold.
Calculation: Recent bearish extreme = the lowest Market Energy over a lookback is at or below the negative extreme threshold.
Long: Market Energy crosses above the positive entry threshold AND Market Energy is above the energy signal line AND energy acceleration meets the positive minimum AND trend alignment is positive AND positive DMI exceeds negative DMI AND close is above open AND candle body ratio meets its minimum; OR recent compression AND Market Energy crosses above the active threshold AND energy acceleration exceeds the positive minimum AND trend alignment is positive; OR Market Energy crosses above zero.
Short: Market Energy crosses below the negative entry threshold AND Market Energy is below the energy signal line AND energy acceleration meets the negative minimum AND trend alignment is negative AND negative DMI exceeds positive DMI AND close is below open AND candle body ratio meets its minimum; OR recent compression AND Market Energy crosses below the negative active threshold AND energy acceleration is below the negative minimum AND trend alignment is negative; OR Market Energy crosses below zero.
Exit: Long, recent bullish extreme AND the recent highest Market Energy exceeds current Market Energy by at least the exhaustion drop AND energy acceleration is negative AND (close is below open OR close is below the previous close).
Exit: Short, recent bearish extreme AND current Market Energy exceeds the recent lowest Market Energy by at least the exhaustion drop AND energy acceleration is positive AND (close is above open OR close is above the previous close).
