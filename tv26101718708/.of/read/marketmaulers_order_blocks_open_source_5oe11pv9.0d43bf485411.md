Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a local high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides; track the latest confirmed level until a bar closes beyond it.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = for the selected opposing down candle or contiguous cluster of down candles within the configured scan, top is the highest body high and bottom is the lowest low; nearest and extreme single-candle selection modes are also supported.
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = for the selected opposing up candle or contiguous cluster of up candles within the configured scan, top is the highest high and bottom is the lowest body low; nearest and extreme single-candle selection modes are also supported.
Long: On a completed chart bar, close > latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND a qualifying opposing down candle or cluster exists AND (displacement filter disabled OR abs(close - origin open) >= configured ATR multiple) AND (FVG filter disabled OR a bullish fair value gap forms within the configured number of bars after the origin) AND (kill-zone filter disabled OR origin bar is within an enabled kill zone) AND zone bottom < zone top
Short: On a completed chart bar, close < latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND a qualifying opposing up candle or cluster exists AND (displacement filter disabled OR abs(close - origin open) >= configured ATR multiple) AND (FVG filter disabled OR a bearish fair value gap forms within the configured number of bars after the origin) AND (kill-zone filter disabled OR origin bar is within an enabled kill zone) AND zone bottom < zone top
Exit: long, close < bottom of an active bullish order-block zone
Exit: short, close > top of an active bearish order-block zone
