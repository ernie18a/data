Calculation: Unbroken swing high/low = the most recently confirmed pivot high/low, using equal numbers of bars on each side; replace it when a newer pivot confirms and consume it when a close first breaks it, even if no order block qualifies.
Calculation: Order-block origin and zone = search backward from the break bar, inclusive, through a bounded lookback for an opposing candle: close < open for bullish, close > open for bearish. Select either the nearest candle, the candle with the lowest low for bullish or highest high for bearish, or the contiguous run of opposing candles starting at the nearest. For a run, the origin is its nearest candle. Bullish zone top = highest body high and bottom = lowest low among selected candles; bearish zone top = highest high and bottom = lowest body low among selected candles.
Calculation: Chart order-block qualification = an origin exists AND zone bottom < zone top AND, if the displacement filter is enabled, absolute value of break-bar close minus origin open >= an ATR multiple AND, if the fair-value-gap filter is enabled, at least one directional gap exists at an offset from max(0, origin offset minus gap-distance lookback) through the origin offset inclusive, where bullish gap means low at that offset > high two bars earlier and bearish gap means high at that offset < low two bars earlier AND, if the session filter is enabled, the origin datetime falls in an enabled session in the America/New_York timezone.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe order-block qualification = an origin exists AND zone bottom < zone top AND, if the displacement filter is enabled, absolute value of break-bar close minus origin open >= an ATR multiple; calculate the swing, origin, zone, ATR and break using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, and emit each qualifying break once per higher-timeframe bar and direction.
Long: chart-bar close > unbroken swing high.
Short: chart-bar close < unbroken swing low.
Long: chart-bar close > unbroken swing high AND bullish chart order-block qualification.
Short: chart-bar close < unbroken swing low AND bearish chart order-block qualification.
Long: last completed higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe unbroken swing high AND bullish higher-timeframe order-block qualification.
Short: last completed higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe unbroken swing low AND bearish higher-timeframe order-block qualification.
