Calculation: Gap zones = On each confirmed bar with ATR available, detect upward and downward candidates using the selected detection mode. Classic candidates have open minus previous close at least a positive minimum gap size for upward gaps or at most its negative for downward gaps. If enabled and no same-direction classic candidate exists, wick candidates require current low minus previous high, or previous low minus current high, respectively, to be at least the minimum gap size. If enabled and neither same-direction classic nor wick candidate exists, body candidates require current body low minus previous body high, or previous body low minus current body high, respectively, to be at least the minimum gap size. If enabled and no preceding same-direction candidate exists, displacement candidates require close above previous high and close minus previous close at least the displacement threshold, or close below previous low and previous close minus close at least that threshold. Minimum gap size is ATR times its multiplier; displacement threshold is ATR times the greater of four times that multiplier and a separate multiplier. Require the configured minimum spacing since the last created zone and a different bar from that zone. If both directions qualify, choose upward. Use the first qualifying candidate in classic, wick, body, displacement order. Classic boundaries are current open and previous close; upward wick boundaries are current low and previous high, downward wick boundaries are previous low and current high; upward body boundaries are current body low and previous body high, downward body boundaries are previous body low and current body high. For displacement, use previous close as the lower upward or upper downward boundary and extend the other boundary by the greater of minimum gap size and the lesser of a fraction of the absolute close change and an ATR multiple. Keep only the configured number of most recently created zones.
Calculation: Zone state = Initialize each zone as fresh with zero maximum fill. From the bar after creation through its configured lifetime, update on each confirmed bar. Upward fill is the maximum to date of the proportion of zone height between its top and current low; downward fill is the maximum to date of the proportion between its bottom and current high. Clamp each proportion to zero through one. In priority order, set state to accepted if an upward zone closes at or below its bottom or a downward zone closes at or above its top; otherwise rejected if maximum fill is at least the partial-fill threshold and below the full-fill threshold and close is above the upward zone top or below the downward zone bottom; otherwise full if maximum fill is at least the full-fill threshold; otherwise partial if maximum fill is at least the partial-fill threshold; otherwise fresh. Preserve rejected when it would change to partial, and preserve accepted permanently. After the lifetime ends, retain the last state.
Calculation: Readiness score = Round the sum of size, fill, reaction, participation and recency scores, clamped to zero through 100. Size is 20 times zone height divided by current ATR and a size scale, clamped to zero through one. Fill is 5 below the partial-fill threshold; otherwise clamp 20 minus 0.25 times the absolute difference between maximum fill percentage and 58 to zero through 20. Reaction is 30 for rejected state, otherwise 18 if the current bar overlaps the zone, otherwise 14 for partial state, otherwise 6. Participation is 15 times the sum of 0.60 times current bar range divided by ATR and a range scale, clamped to zero through one, and 0.40 times the volume factor. Volume factor is current volume divided by its moving average, clamped to zero through two and divided by two; use 0.50 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive. Recency is 15 times one minus zone age divided by zone lifetime, clamped to zero through one.
Calculation: Active zone = Among retained zones within their lifetimes whose states are fresh, partial or rejected, select the highest readiness score, breaking ties in favor of the newest zone.
Long: A new upward gap zone is created OR the active zone is upward OR a downward zone is accepted OR an upward zone changes state to rejected AND its readiness score is at least the readiness threshold.
Short: A new downward gap zone is created OR the active zone is downward OR an upward zone is accepted OR a downward zone changes state to rejected AND its readiness score is at least the readiness threshold.
