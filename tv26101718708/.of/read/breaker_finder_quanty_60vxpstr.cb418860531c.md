Calculation: Confirmed swing = a bar whose high equals the highest high, or whose low equals the lowest low, over a symmetric window of bars around it; confirm it only after the bars on its right have closed. Liquidity swings use a longer window than structure swings.
Calculation: Breaker setup = maintain independent upward and downward setups. On each closed bar, update confirmed swings first. For an upward setup, a low below the latest liquidity swing low starts or replaces the setup if that swing has not already started one; record the low as the extreme and the latest confirmed structure swing high as the level. While the setup is active, each lower low replaces the extreme and resets the level to the latest confirmed structure swing high. Otherwise, replace the level with a newly confirmed structure swing high if its bar is later than the current level bar and no later than the extreme bar. A close above the level ends the setup, whether or not displacement passes. A downward setup is symmetric, using a high above a liquidity swing high, higher extremes, structure swing lows, and a close below the level. Each liquidity swing can start only one setup of its direction.
Calculation: Breaker zone = on a qualifying shift, search backward from the level bar through a bounded number of bars for the nearest candle closing in the shift direction, subject to a maximum age relative to the shift bar; if none is found, use the level bar if it is within that age. If no eligible candle exists, create no zone. The zone is the selected candle's body, with top = max(open, close) and bottom = min(open, close). Retain a bounded number of zones per direction, discarding the oldest when exceeded. On each closed bar, process existing zones before creating new ones: deactivate an upward zone if close < bottom, otherwise mark its first return if low <= top; deactivate a downward zone if close > top, otherwise mark its first return if high >= bottom. A zone can return only once.
Long: An upward setup closes above its level AND displacement is disabled OR an upward setup closes above its level AND (close - open >= ATR OR low > high two bars earlier) AND an eligible breaker-zone candle exists.
Long: An active upward zone has its first return AND close >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top.
Short: A downward setup closes below its level AND displacement is disabled OR a downward setup closes below its level AND (open - close >= ATR OR high < low two bars earlier) AND an eligible breaker-zone candle exists.
Short: An active downward zone has its first return AND close <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom.
