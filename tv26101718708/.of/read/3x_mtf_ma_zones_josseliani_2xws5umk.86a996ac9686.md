Calculation: Selected MA pair = the two enabled MAs if exactly two are enabled; if all three are enabled, exclude the MA with the shortest length, breaking ties by excluding the lowest-numbered MA. If fewer than two are enabled, no pair exists. Each MA independently uses a configurable source and EMA, SMA, WMA, or SMMA. Use chart-timeframe bars unless its selected timeframe is higher; for a higher timeframe, use either the last completed bar or the bar still forming, according to the shared confirmation setting. Within the pair, the MA with the shorter length is the short MA; if lengths are equal, the lower-numbered MA is the short MA.
Calculation: MA cross state = initially 0; on a completed chart bar, set to 1 when the short MA is above the long MA and was at or below it on the previous chart bar; set to -1 when the short MA is below the long MA and was at or above it on the previous chart bar; otherwise retain the previous state.
Long: MA cross state = 1
Short: MA cross state = -1
