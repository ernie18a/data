Calculation: Opening range = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured box window, reset on each New York calendar day; finalize on the first subsequent bar in the wait or active window. Midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2.
Calculation: Breakout = the first confirmed bar after the range is finalized and outside the box window whose close is above the range high or below the range low AND whose volume >= a multiplier of the simple moving average of volume including the current bar; retain its direction and bar until the New York calendar day changes.
Calculation: Active window = bar opening time >= configured active start when waiting is enabled, otherwise >= box end, AND < configured session end. Convert configured fixed-offset clock times to New York clock times using the current bar's New York UTC offset; compare minutes of day without overnight wrapping.
Calculation: Retest eligibility = no retest entry has fired or expired since the breakout AND the current bar is after the breakout bar. Expire when bars since the reference bar > the timeout; with waiting disabled the reference is the breakout bar, and with waiting enabled it is the active window's first bar only if the breakout preceded that bar. Otherwise there is no timeout reference.
Calculation: Stop = opening range low minus entry-bar ATR times a buffer multiplier for a long, or opening range high plus entry-bar ATR times the buffer multiplier for a short. Entry price = entry-bar close. Final target = entry price plus the highest enabled reward multiple times (entry price minus stop) for a long, or entry price minus the highest enabled reward multiple times (stop minus entry price) for a short; no target exists if none is enabled. Freeze stop and target at entry and clear them on exit or a new New York calendar day.
Long: The current confirmed bar is the upward breakout bar.
Short: The current confirmed bar is the downward breakout bar.
Long: Active window AND upward breakout recorded AND confirmed bar AND ((immediate mode AND (current bar is the breakout bar OR first bar entering the active window)) OR (midpoint retest mode AND retest eligibility AND low <= midpoint AND close > midpoint) OR (breakout retest mode AND retest eligibility AND low <= range high AND high >= range high AND close > range high)).
Short: Active window AND downward breakout recorded AND confirmed bar AND ((immediate mode AND (current bar is the breakout bar OR first bar entering the active window)) OR (midpoint retest mode AND retest eligibility AND high >= midpoint AND close < midpoint) OR (breakout retest mode AND retest eligibility AND low <= range low AND high >= range low AND close < range low)).
Exit: long, an entry remains active AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target); evaluate from the entry bar onward.
Exit: short, an entry remains active AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target); evaluate from the entry bar onward.
