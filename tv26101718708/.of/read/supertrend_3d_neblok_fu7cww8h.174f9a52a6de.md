Calculation: Chart SuperTrend = Smooth true range with the selected RMA, SMA, EMA, or WMA method. Optionally multiply the ATR multiplier by a linear interpolation between configurable low- and high-volatility scales using the percentile rank of ATR over its lookback; use the midpoint rank when unavailable. Set basic lower and upper bands to the selected price source minus and plus the resulting multiplier times ATR. On each bar, set the lower band to max(basic lower band, previous lower band) if the previous close is above the previous lower band, otherwise to the basic lower band; set the upper band to min(basic upper band, previous upper band) if the previous close is below the previous upper band, otherwise to the basic upper band. Substitute the current basic band when its previous band is unavailable. Initialize direction bullish. Change bearish direction to bullish when the selected upper trigger exceeds the previous upper band; change bullish direction to bearish when the selected lower trigger is below the previous lower band. Otherwise retain direction. The triggers are both close in close mode, or high and low respectively in wick mode. The active band is the lower band when bullish and the upper band when bearish.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction = Apply the same band and direction recurrence on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, using RMA ATR, the unadjusted multiplier, and close as both triggers.
Calculation: Directional quality score = Count enabled checks that pass: ADX is at least its threshold; volume divided by its SMA is at least its threshold, using a ratio of one when the average is not positive; bullish conviction (close minus previous upper band) divided by ATR, or bearish conviction (previous lower band minus close) divided by ATR, is at least its threshold, using zero when ATR is not positive; higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction agrees with the signal. The required score is the lesser of the minimum score parameter and the number of enabled checks.
Calculation: Flip target = At each SuperTrend flip, record the close and the active band on that bar. For each positive target multiple, add the multiple times the absolute distance between those recorded values to the recorded close for a bullish flip, or subtract it for a bearish flip. Only create targets when that distance is positive; replace them at the next flip.
Long: Chart SuperTrend direction is bullish.
Long: Previous chart SuperTrend direction is bearish AND upper trigger is greater than the previous upper band.
Long: Previous chart SuperTrend direction is bearish AND upper trigger is greater than the previous upper band AND bullish directional quality score is at least the required score.
Short: Chart SuperTrend direction is bearish.
Short: Previous chart SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lower trigger is less than the previous lower band.
Short: Previous chart SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lower trigger is less than the previous lower band AND bearish directional quality score is at least the required score.
Exit: long, previous chart SuperTrend direction is bullish AND lower trigger is less than the previous lower band.
Exit: short, previous chart SuperTrend direction is bearish AND upper trigger is greater than the previous upper band.
Exit: long, chart SuperTrend direction is bullish AND no flip occurs on the current bar AND high is at least an unhit bullish flip target.
Exit: short, chart SuperTrend direction is bearish AND no flip occurs on the current bar AND low is at most an unhit bearish flip target.
