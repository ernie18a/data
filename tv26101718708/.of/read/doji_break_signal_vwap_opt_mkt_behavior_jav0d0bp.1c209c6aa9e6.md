Calculation: EMA1, EMA2, EMA3, EMA4 = EMAs of close with successively longer lookbacks. ATR = average true range. Normalized distance D = (close - EMA4) / ATR when ATR > 0, otherwise 0. Bull stack = EMA1 > EMA2 AND EMA2 > EMA3 AND EMA3 > EMA4. Bear stack = EMA1 < EMA2 AND EMA2 < EMA3 AND EMA3 < EMA4.
Calculation: Reversal score = min(round(stack score + stretch score + fading score + limit score), maximum score). Stack score is the highest stack weight for a bull or bear stack, an intermediate weight when EMA1 > EMA3 AND EMA2 > EMA4 OR EMA1 < EMA3 AND EMA2 < EMA4, and the lowest weight otherwise. Stretch score = min(abs(D) / stretch scale, 1) × stretch weight. Fading score is its weight when abs(EMA1 - EMA2) < its previous-bar value, otherwise 0. Limit score = min(max(abs(D) / abs(long distance limit), abs(D) / abs(short distance limit)), 1) × limit weight; a term with a zero limit is 0.
Calculation: Cumulative VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume from the start of the data; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3. If cumulative volume is zero, VWAP = close. VWAP deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative sum of typical price squared × volume / cumulative volume - VWAP squared), or 0 if cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: A doji has high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) <= a body-to-range threshold. Retain the latest doji high, low, and bar; replace them whenever another doji occurs. Track long and short doji signals separately as unused after each new doji or new day and used after their respective signal.
Calculation: Bull breakout = close > open AND abs(close - open) >= a breakout ATR multiple × ATR AND low <= max(EMA1, EMA2, EMA3, EMA4) AND close > that maximum. Bear breakout = close < open AND abs(close - open) >= a breakout ATR multiple × ATR AND high >= min(EMA1, EMA2, EMA3, EMA4) AND close < that minimum.
Calculation: Failed bull breakout = a bull breakout occurred within the preceding breakout lookback AND no subsequent bull breakout or failed bull breakout has occurred AND close < max(EMA1, EMA2, EMA3, EMA4). Failed bear breakout = a bear breakout occurred within the preceding breakout lookback AND no subsequent bear breakout or failed bear breakout has occurred AND close > min(EMA1, EMA2, EMA3, EMA4).
Calculation: Positive reversion = D < 0 AND abs(D) >= a reversion stretch threshold AND reversal score >= its threshold AND, if confirmation is enabled, close > open AND close > previous close AND, if EMA reclaim is enabled, close > EMA1. Negative reversion = D > 0 AND abs(D) >= the reversion stretch threshold AND reversal score >= its threshold AND, if confirmation is enabled, close < open AND close < previous close AND, if EMA reclaim is enabled, close < EMA1. Each direction requires at least its cooldown number of bars since its previous reversion signal.
Long: The latest doji is at least two bars old AND its long signal is unused AND high > latest doji high AND close > previous close AND D < the positive long distance limit AND, if the VWAP filter is enabled with an above-only or below-only setting, close is respectively > or < cumulative VWAP.
Short: The latest doji is at least two bars old AND its short signal is unused AND low < latest doji low AND close < previous close AND D > the negative short distance limit AND, if the VWAP filter is enabled with an above-only or below-only setting, close is respectively > or < cumulative VWAP.
Long: Close > open AND previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND high < min(EMA3, EMA4) AND min(abs(close - EMA1), abs(close - EMA2)) <= a proximity multiple × ATR.
Short: Close < open AND previous close > previous open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND low > max(EMA3, EMA4) AND min(abs(close - EMA1), abs(close - EMA2)) <= a proximity multiple × ATR.
Long: Bull breakout.
Short: Bear breakout.
Long: Bull stack AND (low <= EMA2 AND high >= EMA2 OR low <= EMA3 AND high >= EMA3) AND abs(close - open) <= a pullback body multiple × ATR.
Short: Bear stack AND (low <= EMA2 AND high >= EMA2 OR low <= EMA3 AND high >= EMA3) AND abs(close - open) <= a pullback body multiple × ATR.
Long: Positive reversion.
Short: Negative reversion.
Long: Previous close < current cumulative VWAP AND high >= cumulative VWAP - touch multiple × ATR AND high <= cumulative VWAP + touch multiple × ATR AND, if bounce confirmation is enabled, close > open AND close > cumulative VWAP AND at least the VWAP cooldown number of bars has passed since the previous long VWAP signal.
Short: Previous close > current cumulative VWAP AND low <= cumulative VWAP + touch multiple × ATR AND low >= cumulative VWAP - touch multiple × ATR AND, if bounce confirmation is enabled, close < open AND close < cumulative VWAP AND at least the VWAP cooldown number of bars has passed since the previous short VWAP signal.
Long: Close < cumulative VWAP - reversion deviation multiple × VWAP deviation AND close > open AND close > previous close AND close > cumulative VWAP AND at least the VWAP cooldown number of bars has passed since the previous long VWAP signal.
Short: Close > cumulative VWAP + reversion deviation multiple × VWAP deviation AND close < open AND close < previous close AND close < cumulative VWAP AND at least the VWAP cooldown number of bars has passed since the previous short VWAP signal.
Long: Bull stack AND (abs(close - EMA2) / ATR <= an add proximity threshold OR abs(close - EMA3) / ATR <= that threshold), with each normalized distance treated as 0 when ATR <= 0.
Short: Bear stack AND (abs(close - EMA2) / ATR <= an add proximity threshold OR abs(close - EMA3) / ATR <= that threshold), with each normalized distance treated as 0 when ATR <= 0.
Exit: long, on two consecutive bars after a doji long entry, each bar satisfies close < entry price OR low < previous close - exit offset OR low <= entry price - stop distance; the qualifying condition may differ between bars.
Exit: short, on two consecutive bars after a doji short entry, each bar satisfies close > entry price OR high > previous close + exit offset OR high >= entry price + stop distance; the qualifying condition may differ between bars.
Exit: long, failed bull breakout OR negative reversion OR abs(D) >= an exit stretch threshold OR close > cumulative VWAP + exit deviation multiple × VWAP deviation OR close < cumulative VWAP - exit deviation multiple × VWAP deviation.
Exit: short, failed bear breakout OR positive reversion OR abs(D) >= an exit stretch threshold OR close > cumulative VWAP + exit deviation multiple × VWAP deviation OR close < cumulative VWAP - exit deviation multiple × VWAP deviation.
