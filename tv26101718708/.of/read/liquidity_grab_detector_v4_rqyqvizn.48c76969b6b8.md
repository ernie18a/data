Calculation: Liquidity pools = Maintain separate upper and lower pools from confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs and lows, confirmed swing highs and lows from two higher timeframes using their last completed bars, the previous completed daily and weekly highs and lows, and newly detected pairs of approximately equal chart-timeframe swing highs or lows. Use configurable source toggles. A swing is confirmed only after the configured number of bars on its right. On registration, merge a level into the nearest unswept or swept pool within an ATR-scaled merge distance; update its midpoint as the hit-count-weighted mean, increment its hits, expand its ATR-scaled half-width with hits, and retain the highest source weight. Otherwise create a pool with one hit, full health and ATR-scaled boundaries. Use ATR at the pivot for chart swings and current ATR for other sources. Enforce minimum widths and merge distances as configurable price increments and discard the oldest pool when the per-side limit is exceeded. On each eligible bar, discard expired pools; increment hits on spaced touches of the zone followed by a close on the inside of the midpoint; reduce health when close is inside the zone in proportion to volume divided by its moving average. Delete an upper pool after two consecutive closes above its top and a lower pool after two consecutive closes below its bottom. Swept pools remain available for merging and channel calculations but cannot produce another sweep.
Calculation: Sweep score = A weighted mean of normalized penetration beyond the pool boundary, reclaim within the bar range, directional wick divided by true range, directional body, pool hits, health and source weight, absolute impulse-anchored VWAP stretch, higher-timeframe trend agreement, relative volume, recent directional micro-trend change, distance beyond the prior-bar breakout level in ATR units, and position between the nearest lower pool below close and upper pool above close. Use configurable normalization bounds, component weights and fallbacks for unavailable values. The penetration component peaks at a configurable target and decreases with distance from it; the channel component rewards either extreme. A micro-trend changes direction when close breaks the most recently confirmed short-lookback swing in the opposite direction. Select weights by ADX and Bollinger-band-width percentile regime when enabled; apply the configured session multiplier and compression-regime bonus, then an increasing, capped pool-age multiplier. Higher-timeframe trend agreement compares the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA.
Calculation: Reversal class = In priority order, absorption if volume z-score, directional wick fraction and reclaim exceed their respective thresholds; otherwise rejection if wick fraction and reclaim exceed their thresholds AND the candle body points in the reversal direction; otherwise divergence if volume z-score and reclaim exceed their thresholds AND wick fraction is below its threshold; otherwise exhaustion. Each class and the grind path has its own minimum sweep score. With delayed confirmation, classify the confirmation bar using its volume z-score, wick fraction and body and a fixed configurable reclaim value; compare that class's minimum with the score saved on the sweep bar.
Calculation: Impulse stretch = (close minus VWAP of typical price weighted by volume since the latest bar whose high exceeds the preceding anchor-lookback high OR whose low falls below the preceding anchor-lookback low) divided by long-lookback ATR, with a configurable minimum denominator.
Calculation: Exhaustion percentile = Percentile rank over a configurable lookback of a bounded hyperbolic-tangent transform of the weighted sum of: an EMA of impulse stretch, optionally daily anchored VWAP stretch, upper-minus-lower wick divided by true range, and positive volume z-score multiplied by the sign of impulse stretch; and the negative difference between the rate of change of a close EMA and the previous bar's shorter EMA of that rate of change. Daily VWAP resets at the start of each trading day; its stretch uses the same long-lookback ATR denominator as impulse stretch. All weights, smoothing lengths and rank thresholds are configurable.
Calculation: Armed state = For each side, when its exhaustion percentile crosses its configured extreme threshold and either its same-direction sweep occurs or impulse stretch exceeds its same-direction extreme AND positive volume z-score exceeds its threshold, set an armed counter to its configured lifetime if the counter and that side's cooldown are zero. Otherwise decrement the counter to a minimum of zero. Decrement side cooldowns each bar; a same-direction breakout trigger clears the counter and starts that side's cooldown. Both breakout directions share a separate minimum interval between triggers.
Long: An unswept lower pool is eligible AND session gating passes AND ((low < pool bottom AND close > pool midpoint AND (pool bottom - low) / ATR > minimum penetration AND (close - low) / (high - low) > reclaim threshold) OR (grind is enabled AND a recent close < pool bottom minus an ATR margin AND current close > pool bottom AND current close < pool top plus an ATR margin AND close > open)) AND ((confirmation window is zero AND sweep score >= the applicable reversal-class or grind minimum AND optional score filter passes AND shared sweep cooldown has elapsed AND score exceeds the best immediate sweep score already accepted on this bar) OR (a pending sweep was recorded within the confirmation window AND close > pool top AND the pending sweep has not expired or previously been invalidated by close < previous low AND saved sweep score >= the confirmation-bar reversal-class minimum AND optional score filter passes AND shared sweep cooldown has elapsed)).
Short: An unswept upper pool is eligible AND session gating passes AND ((high > pool top AND close < pool midpoint AND (high - pool top) / ATR > minimum penetration AND (high - close) / (high - low) > reclaim threshold) OR (grind is enabled AND a recent close > pool top plus an ATR margin AND current close < pool top AND current close > pool bottom minus an ATR margin AND close < open)) AND ((confirmation window is zero AND sweep score >= the applicable reversal-class or grind minimum AND optional score filter passes AND shared sweep cooldown has elapsed AND score exceeds the best immediate sweep score already accepted on this bar) OR (a pending sweep was recorded within the confirmation window AND close < pool bottom AND the pending sweep has not expired or previously been invalidated by close > previous high AND saved sweep score >= the confirmation-bar reversal-class minimum AND optional score filter passes AND shared sweep cooldown has elapsed)).
Long: Long armed counter > zero.
Short: Long armed counter = zero AND short armed counter > zero.
Long: Long armed counter > zero AND close > highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND shared trigger interval has elapsed AND session gating passes AND breakout triggers are enabled.
Short: Short armed counter > zero AND close < lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND shared trigger interval has elapsed AND session gating passes AND breakout triggers are enabled.
