Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs/lows, prior-day highs/lows, and equal swing highs/lows; optionally include confirmed swing highs/lows from higher timeframes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Merge nearby active pools; each pool is centered on its level with an ATR-scaled zone. Track touches, health, age, and source weight; discard expired, invalidated, or consumed pools.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = price penetrates below an active buy-pool zone and closes above its midpoint, or was below the zone in a recent bar and now closes back inside it with a bullish candle. Bearish sweep = price penetrates above an active sell-pool zone and closes below its midpoint, or was above the zone in a recent bar and now closes back inside it with a bearish candle. Apply the configured penetration, reclaim, pool-state, session, and cooldown rules.
Calculation: Sweep score = age-adjusted, session-adjusted weighted mean of normalized penetration, reclaim, wick, candle-body, pool significance (touches, health, and source weight), impulse-AVWAP stretch, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, volume relative to its moving average, micro-structure change, market-structure displacement, and position within the nearest buy/sell-pool channel. Adapt component weights by trend/range/compression regime. Classify a sweep as absorption, rejection, divergence, or exhaustion from volume z-score, wick ratio, reclaim, and candle direction; apply the configured score and type thresholds. If confirmation is enabled, require a close beyond the far pool-zone boundary within the configured window.
Calculation: Impulse AVWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price, reset whenever price makes a new high or low over its configured lookback. Daily AVWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price since the start of the current day.
Calculation: SFP percentile rank = current exhaustion score's percentile rank over its lookback; exhaustion score combines ATR-normalized distance from impulse AVWAP and, when enabled, daily AVWAP, wick skew, positive volume z-score signed by impulse-AVWAP stretch, and a curvature term from smoothed close rate of change.
Calculation: SFP armed state = a bearish liquidity condition (upward impulse-AVWAP stretch with elevated volume, or bearish sweep) coinciding with a high SFP percentile rank, or a bullish liquidity condition (downward impulse-AVWAP stretch with elevated volume, or bullish sweep) coinciding with a low SFP percentile rank; retain the corresponding state for its configured lifetime.
Calculation: Market-structure boundaries = prior-bar lowest low and highest high over the configured short lookback.
Long: Qualified bullish sweep OR enabled SFP trigger while bullish state is armed, close is above the prior market-structure high, the armed state is live, cooldown has elapsed, and session gate passes.
Short: Qualified bearish sweep OR enabled SFP trigger while bearish state is armed, close is below the prior market-structure low, the armed state is live, cooldown has elapsed, and session gate passes.
