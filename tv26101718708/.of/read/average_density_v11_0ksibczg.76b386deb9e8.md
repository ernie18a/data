Calculation: Moving-average squeeze = Let each center be the average of the EMA and SMA for that lookback. The shortest lookback is shorter than the intermediate lookback, which is shorter than the longest. The squeeze is true when the shortest pair’s EMA-to-SMA absolute difference divided by its center is at most its tightness threshold, and the absolute distance from the shortest center to each of the intermediate and longest centers, each divided by the shortest center, is at most its respective distance threshold.
Calculation: Upper moving-average boundary = Maximum of the EMA and SMA at the shortest, intermediate, and longest lookbacks.
Calculation: Lower moving-average boundary = Minimum of the EMA and SMA at the shortest, intermediate, and longest lookbacks.
Calculation: Prior-bar average volume = SMA of volume over the lookback ending one completed bar before the current bar.
Calculation: Candle body size = Absolute difference between close and open.
Calculation: Upper shadow = High minus the greater of close and open.
Calculation: Lower shadow = The lesser of close and open minus low.
Long: Most recent squeeze bar index is not the no-squeeze sentinel AND current bar index minus most recent squeeze bar index is at most the monitoring window AND current volume is at least prior-bar average volume times the volume multiplier AND current volume is at least the minimum volume AND close is above the upper moving-average boundary AND close is above open AND candle body size is at least upper shadow times the body-to-shadow ratio AND current bar index minus last long-signal bar index is greater than the same-direction suppression interval.
Short: Most recent squeeze bar index is not the no-squeeze sentinel AND current bar index minus most recent squeeze bar index is at most the monitoring window AND current volume is at least prior-bar average volume times the volume multiplier AND current volume is at least the minimum volume AND close is below the lower moving-average boundary AND close is below open AND candle body size is at least lower shadow times the body-to-shadow ratio AND current bar index minus last short-signal bar index is greater than the same-direction suppression interval.
