Calculation: Hull = choose HMA, exponential Hull, or triangular Hull. For length n, HMA = WMA(2 × WMA(source, n/2) − WMA(source, n), round(sqrt(n))); exponential Hull substitutes EMA for every WMA; triangular Hull = WMA(3 × WMA(source, m/3) − WMA(source, m/2) − WMA(source, m), m), where m = n/2. Use the chart timeframe or the last completed bar of a higher timeframe; the two-bar lag below is measured in chart bars.
Long: datetime > enabled start bound AND datetime < enabled end bound AND trend coloring enabled AND Hull <= Hull two chart bars ago AND LSMA crosses above Hull AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND position side is not long
Short: datetime > enabled start bound AND datetime < enabled end bound AND trend coloring enabled AND Hull > Hull two chart bars ago AND LSMA crosses below Hull AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND position side is not short
Exit: long, datetime > enabled start bound AND datetime < enabled end bound AND close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: short, datetime > enabled start bound AND datetime < enabled end bound AND close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
Exit: long, datetime > enabled start bound AND datetime < enabled end bound AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) for each of four independently configured targets, with the first three closing their configured position percentages and the fourth closing the remainder
Exit: short, datetime > enabled start bound AND datetime < enabled end bound AND low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) for each of four independently configured targets, with the first three closing their configured position percentages and the fourth closing the remainder
