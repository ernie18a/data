Calculation: Chart FVG = bullish [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago; bearish [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago. Store the middle bar's index, open, and close. Keep the most recent configurable number of FVGs. A bullish FVG becomes inverted when close < its lower bound; a bearish FVG becomes inverted when close > its upper bound. Inversion remains latched.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVG = the same three-bar FVG calculation using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it. Add it when a new higher-timeframe bar starts; keep the most recent configurable number. A bullish gap is invalidated when chart close < its lower bound; a bearish gap is invalidated when chart close > its upper bound. Otherwise, a chart bar taps it when high >= its lower bound AND low <= its upper bound; retain its latest tap bar.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = enabled confirmed pivot highs and lows, the last completed higher-timeframe daily high and low added at the start of a new day, and highs and lows of enabled completed intraday sessions in New York time. A pivot uses equal configurable numbers of chart bars on each side. If enabled, a pivot within an ATR-based tolerance of the preceding same-side pivot is an equal-high or equal-low level; otherwise add it only if swing levels are enabled. Keep the most recent configurable number of levels. For an active high level, close > level marks it broken; otherwise high > level marks it swept. For an active low level, close < level marks it broken; otherwise low < level marks it swept. Record the sweep bar; broken and swept levels are no longer active.
Calculation: Context for a direction and zone = an uninvalidated same-direction higher-timeframe FVG tapped within a configurable chart-bar lookback AND zone upper bound >= FVG lower bound minus an ATR-based buffer AND zone lower bound <= FVG upper bound plus that buffer; OR, if sweep context is enabled, a retained low level for a long or high level for a short was swept within a configurable chart-bar lookback. Update higher-timeframe gaps and liquidity levels before evaluating context.
Calculation: Reclaimed fraction = (current close - displacement close) / (displacement open - displacement close), where displacement is the FVG's middle bar; use 1 when its body is zero.
Calculation: Balanced price range = overlap of a new chart FVG and an opposite-direction inverted FVG whose middle bar is within a configurable lookback. Overlap upper bound = minimum of their upper bounds; lower bound = maximum of their lower bounds. Consider eligible unused inverted FVGs newest first; require upper bound > lower bound AND overlap size >= an ATR-based minimum. Consume the first match even if it is hidden because its context fails; each inverted FVG can be consumed once. Create and invert existing FVGs before matching the new FVG.
Long: An existing bearish chart FVG has not previously fired an entry AND current close > its upper bound AND previous close <= its upper bound AND bars since its middle bar <= the FVG lookback AND reclaimed fraction >= a configurable threshold AND (clean-tip filter disabled OR previous close < previous open) AND (context gate disabled OR long context for the FVG) AND (clear-path filter disabled OR no active low liquidity level is < current low AND >= current low minus an ATR-based buffer).
Short: An existing bullish chart FVG has not previously fired an entry AND current close < its lower bound AND previous close >= its lower bound AND bars since its middle bar <= the FVG lookback AND reclaimed fraction >= a configurable threshold AND (clean-tip filter disabled OR previous close > previous open) AND (context gate disabled OR short context for the FVG) AND (clear-path filter disabled OR no active high liquidity level is > current high AND <= current high plus an ATR-based buffer).
Long: A bullish balanced price range is created AND (context gate disabled OR hiding failed ranges disabled OR long context for its overlap).
Short: A bearish balanced price range is created AND (context gate disabled OR hiding failed ranges disabled OR short context for its overlap).
