Calculation: Buy-side sweep = current high > latest confirmed swing high AND current close < that swing high AND (current high − swing high) / ATR <= maximum sweep depth AND (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= minimum rejection ratio.
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = current low < latest confirmed swing low AND current close > that swing low AND (swing low − current low) / ATR <= maximum sweep depth AND (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= minimum rejection ratio.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier AND current low − that earlier high >= minimum FVG size in ATR units; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier AND that earlier low − current high >= minimum FVG size in ATR units.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish FVG = a bullish/bearish FVG occurred on the current bar or within the configured recent-bar lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = close >/< open AND abs(close − open) >= minimum displacement body in ATR units AND abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= minimum body-to-range ratio.
