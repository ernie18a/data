Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = the most recent pivot high or low confirmed using the same number of bars on each side; update it on the confirmation bar before evaluating signals.
Calculation: Candle range = max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Entry risk = max(ATR on the entry bar × stop multiplier, minimum price increment), fixed at entry.
Long: RSI-independent liquidity model is flat AND its previous accepted signal is not long AND low < confirmed swing low AND close > confirmed swing low AND ATR > 0 AND (confirmed swing low − low) / ATR <= maximum sweep depth AND (min(open, close) − low) / candle range >= minimum rejection ratio AND every filter in the selected combination is satisfied: trend = shorter EMA > longer EMA; bullish FVG = at least one bar within the inclusive recency lookback has low > high two bars earlier AND the gap >= that bar's ATR × minimum gap multiplier; volume = volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier; RSI = RSI <= lower threshold; displacement = close > open AND abs(close − open) >= ATR × body multiplier AND abs(close − open) / candle range >= minimum body ratio. Allowed filter combinations are none, each filter alone, trend+FVG, trend+volume, trend+RSI, trend+displacement, FVG+volume, FVG+RSI, FVG+displacement, volume+RSI, trend+FVG+volume, and all five. If both directions qualify, long takes precedence.
Short: Liquidity model is flat AND its previous accepted signal is not short AND high > confirmed swing high AND close < confirmed swing high AND ATR > 0 AND (high − confirmed swing high) / ATR <= maximum sweep depth AND (high − max(open, close)) / candle range >= minimum rejection ratio AND every filter in the selected combination is satisfied: trend = shorter EMA < longer EMA; bearish FVG = at least one bar within the inclusive recency lookback has high < low two bars earlier AND the gap >= that bar's ATR × minimum gap multiplier; volume = volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier; RSI = RSI >= upper threshold; displacement = close < open AND abs(close − open) >= ATR × body multiplier AND abs(close − open) / candle range >= minimum body ratio. Use the same allowed filter combinations as for long.
Long: ADX-style trend strength >= trend threshold AND shorter EMA > longer EMA AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator.
Short: ADX-style trend strength >= trend threshold AND shorter EMA < longer EMA AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + entry risk × final target multiple OR low <= entry price − entry risk OR, for a background model, bars since entry >= maximum trade duration.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= entry price − entry risk × final target multiple OR high >= entry price + entry risk OR, for a background model, bars since entry >= maximum trade duration.
Calculation: Model state = track each filter combination separately, enter at signal-bar close, and retain the last accepted signal direction after exit. The selected chart model cannot enter while its chart trade is active; its chart trade closes on the first bar after entry that touches its final target or stop, and the lock prevents a new signal on that closing bar. Background models can enter again on their exit bar.
