Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a local high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides; retain only pivots within the configured lookback that have not already triggered a signal
Calculation: Swept high = the lowest retained swing high with high >= its level and close < its level; swept low = the highest retained swing low with low <= its level and close > its level
Calculation: Sweep-side wick ratio = upper wick / (high - low) for a high sweep; lower wick / (high - low) for a low sweep
Calculation: Reclaim distance in ATR = (swept high - close) / ATR for a high sweep; (close - swept low) / ATR for a low sweep
Calculation: SFP score = round(min(100, wick score + reclaim score + volume score + age score)); wick score = clamp((wick ratio - 0.30) / 0.65 × 40, 0, 40); reclaim score = clamp(reclaim distance in ATR × 30, 0, 30); volume score = 15 when volume confirmation passes, otherwise 0; age score = min(15, swing age in bars / 50 × 15)
Long: A swept low exists AND low <= swept low AND close > swept low AND lower wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND reclaim distance in ATR >= minimum reclaim distance AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND SFP score >= minimum score AND the chart bar is complete
