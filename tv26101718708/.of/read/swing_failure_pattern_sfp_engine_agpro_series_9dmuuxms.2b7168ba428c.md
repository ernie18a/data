Calculation: Active swings = confirmed pivot highs and lows with the same number of left and right bars, retained until their pivot-bar age exceeds the swing lookback or they are selected by a confirmed SFP; add newly confirmed pivots before testing the current bar. For a bearish SFP select the lowest active swing high with high >= swing and close < swing; for a bullish SFP select the highest active swing low with low <= swing and close > swing. Break equal-price ties by earliest pivot. Remove only the selected swing when its SFP passes all filters.
Calculation: Safe ATR = maximum of ATR, or true range when ATR is unavailable, and a minimum positive price increment parameter. Candle range = maximum of high - low and that increment.
Calculation: SFP score = round(min(100, clamp((wick ratio - 0.30) / 0.65 * 40, 0, 40) + clamp(reclaim distance / safe ATR * 30, 0, 30) + volume component + min(15, swing age in bars / 50 * 15))); volume component = 15 when volume confirmation is disabled or volume > volume SMA * volume multiplier, otherwise 0.
Long: At bar close, a selected swing low exists AND (min(open, close) - low) / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND (close - selected swing low) / safe ATR >= minimum reclaim distance AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume SMA * volume multiplier) AND SFP score >= minimum score.
Short: At bar close, a selected swing high exists AND (high - max(open, close)) / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND (selected swing high - close) / safe ATR >= minimum reclaim distance AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume SMA * volume multiplier) AND SFP score >= minimum score.
Long: After confirmed SFP swings are removed, an active swing low exists with low <= swing low AND close > swing low AND (min(open, close) - low) / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND (close - swing low) / safe ATR >= minimum reclaim distance.
Short: After confirmed SFP swings are removed, no active swing low satisfies the pending-long conditions AND an active swing high exists with high >= swing high AND close < swing high AND (high - max(open, close)) / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND (swing high - close) / safe ATR >= minimum reclaim distance.
