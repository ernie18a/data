Calculation: Candle body high = max(open, close); candle body low = min(open, close).
Calculation: Bullish gap = [high two bars ago, current low] when at least three prior chart bars exist and high two bars ago < current low; bearish gap = [current high, low two bars ago] when at least three prior chart bars exist and low two bars ago > current high.
Calculation: Balanced range = overlap of the current gap and an opposite-direction gap whose ending bar is within a lookback. Examine every chart bar after the older gap's ending bar and before the current gap's starting bar, in chronological order. For each bar with high > range low and low < range high: if low <= range low and high < range high, raise range low to that high; otherwise, if high >= range high and low > range low, lower range high to that low; otherwise invalidate the range. A range is valid only if its high remains greater than its low. Compare against the retained recent gaps up to the gap-storage capacity.
Calculation: Session opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall from the first chart bar in the active session up to, but excluding, the opening-range end time. Lock the range on the first chart bar opening at or after that end time; discard it when the active session changes. Select among the configured sessions in priority order when they overlap.
Calculation: Breakout state = reset both armed directions, both traded-beyond flags, and the measured-gap count at each session start. After the opening range locks, set the upper flag when high > range high and the lower flag when low < range low. On each bar, after updating these flags, an upper flag AND low <= range high AND close > range high arms long, disarms short, and resets the measured-gap count; a lower flag AND high >= range low AND close < range low arms short, disarms long, and resets the count. Process the long retest before the short retest. After evaluating breakout gaps, increment the count once if either direction has a valid breakout gap and the count is below the measurement limit; disarm both directions when the count reaches that limit.
Long: Previous candle body high < current candle body low.
Short: Previous candle body low > current candle body high.
Long: A bullish gap exists.
Short: A bearish gap exists.
Long: A bullish gap forms a valid balanced range with an earlier bearish gap.
Short: A bearish gap forms a valid balanced range with an earlier bullish gap.
Long: The session opening range is locked AND at least two prior chart bars exist AND the long breakout state is armed AND current low > high two bars ago AND current low + price tolerance >= session opening range high.
Short: The session opening range is locked AND at least two prior chart bars exist AND the short breakout state is armed AND current high < low two bars ago AND current high - price tolerance <= session opening range low.
