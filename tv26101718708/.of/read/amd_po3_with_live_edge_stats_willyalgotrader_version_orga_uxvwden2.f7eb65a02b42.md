Calculation: Accumulation range = On confirmed chart bars, while idle and after a cooldown from the last cycle end, detect a Donchian width whose percentile rank over a longer statistics lookback is at or below a compression threshold and whose width is at least a minimum fraction of close. Starting with the Donchian lookback, optionally remove the oldest bar repeatedly while removing it reduces the window width by more than a specified fraction and the remaining window exceeds the minimum range maturity. Set the range start to the oldest remaining bar. In absolute mode, set the boundaries to the window's highest high and lowest low. In pivot mode, use the highest confirmed pivot high and lowest confirmed pivot low whose pivot bars are in the window, falling back independently to the absolute boundary when no corresponding pivot exists. Reject the range if its resulting width is less than the minimum fraction of close. Record ATR from the bar immediately before the range start. The range age is the number of bars since its start.
Calculation: Range lifecycle = Beginning on the bar after range creation, expire the range when bars since its expiry anchor exceed the maximum range age; initially the anchor is the range start. Reset to idle if both boundaries are breached on the same bar or if either boundary is breached before minimum maturity. A high breach is high > upper boundary + tolerance fraction × range width; a low breach is low < lower boundary - tolerance fraction × range width. When neither boundary is breached, absolute mode expands each boundary to include the current high or low within its tolerance; pivot mode expands a boundary only to a newly confirmed corresponding pivot inside its tolerance whose pivot bar is not before the range start. Recalculate width after expansion. After an expired, reset, failed, breakout, or completed cycle, begin the cooldown before detecting another range.
Calculation: Eligible sweep = On a mature, unexpired accumulation range, exactly one boundary is breached and, if enabled, the sweep bar is within an enabled time window AND the excursion beyond the boundary is at most the chosen nearest-rank percentile of true range over the statistics lookback AND at least the required count of confirmed same-side pivots within the liquidity lookback lie within a specified fraction of range width of the breached boundary. Each optional filter applies only when enabled; the depth filter is disabled at its maximum percentile. After re-arming, a sweep of the first sweep's side is ineligible. If an ineligible breach closes outside the range, reset to idle; otherwise retain the range. After an eligible breach, freeze the boundaries and track the most extreme high or low from the sweep bar through the return bar. A close within both boundaries on the sweep bar or within the maximum return bars confirms the sweep; if the deadline is exceeded first, end the cycle as a breakout.
Calculation: Confluence score = Sum the enabled true components: the sweep extreme crosses the previous higher-timeframe completed daily low for a long or high for a short and the corresponding range boundary is within an ATR-based tolerance of that level; the sweep extreme crosses the low for a long or high for a short of the last completed reference session and the corresponding range boundary is within that tolerance; the confirmation close is above the open of the still-forming higher-timeframe daily bar for a long or below it for a short; and the confirmation close is above the current session VWAP for a long or below it for a short. The tolerance is a multiple of the ATR recorded before the range start, falling back to current ATR if unavailable. Session VWAP is the cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume from the session start through the current bar, and is unavailable outside that session or when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Confirmation eligibility = The optional higher-timeframe bias filter passes when disabled or when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for a long or below it for a short; the bias also passes if its context is disabled. The optional confluence filter passes when disabled or when the confluence score reaches its minimum. If either filter rejects a confirmed sweep, optionally re-arm once with the same range, set the expiry anchor to the rejection bar, and await an eligible sweep of the opposite side; otherwise end the cycle. The range may continue expanding after re-arming.
Calculation: Reference levels = At confirmation, freeze the sweep extreme, range boundaries and pre-range ATR. For a long, stop = sweep extreme - ATR buffer × pre-range ATR and target = upper boundary + (extension factor - 1) × (upper boundary - sweep extreme). For a short, stop = sweep extreme + ATR buffer × pre-range ATR and target = lower boundary - (extension factor - 1) × (sweep extreme - lower boundary). Entry price is the confirmation close.
Long: An eligible low-side sweep is confirmed AND confirmation eligibility passes.
Short: An eligible high-side sweep is confirmed AND confirmation eligibility passes.
Exit: long, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (low <= stop OR high >= target OR bars since entry > distribution timeout).
Exit: short, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (high >= stop OR low <= target OR bars since entry > distribution timeout).
