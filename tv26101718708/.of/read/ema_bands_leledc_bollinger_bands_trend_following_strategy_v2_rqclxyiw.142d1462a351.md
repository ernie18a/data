Calculation: Middle bands = EMA(close) + multiplier × (EMA(high or low) − EMA(close)), using the same EMA length for close, high, and low and separate upper and lower multipliers.
Calculation: Buy and sell zones = WMA of the corresponding raw zone. Raw inner and outer sell zones are EMA(close) + (EMA(high) − EMA(close)) × upper middle-band multiplier × φ × π or × (φ + 4), respectively; raw buy zones substitute EMA(low) and the lower multiplier. Alternatively, raw zones are EMA(close) ± RMA(high − low) × inner or outer multiplier, with a larger outer multiplier. All EMA and RMA calculations use the middle-band length; all zone WMAs use the same smoothing length; φ = (1 + √5) / 2.
Calculation: Upward crossing of level L = (open < L AND close > L) OR (open > L AND close > L AND previous close <= previous L). Downward crossing = (open > L AND close < L) OR (open < L AND close < L AND previous close >= previous L).
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion = RSI(source) < oversold threshold AND previous source < current lower Bollinger Band AND current source > current lower Bollinger Band. Bearish exhaustion = RSI(source) > overbought threshold AND previous source > current upper Bollinger Band AND current source < current upper Bollinger Band. Both Bollinger Band and RSI calculations use the selected source.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish Leledc = maintain separate up and down counters, each initialized to zero and copied from a configurable number of bars ago; increment the up counter when close > close at a configurable lookback and the down counter when close < that close. Bullish Leledc occurs when the down counter exceeds its threshold AND close > open AND low <= lowest low over the Leledc lookback; reset the down counter. Bearish Leledc occurs when the up counter exceeds its threshold AND close < open AND high >= highest high over the same lookback; reset the up counter. If the optional volume filter is enabled, require volume > SMA(volume) × volume multiplier for either event and perform resets only when that requirement is met.
Calculation: Squeeze = upper Bollinger Band <= upper Keltner band AND lower Bollinger Band >= lower Keltner band, or optionally either comparison. The Bollinger Bands use SMA and standard deviation of close; the Keltner bands are EMA(close) ± multiplier × EMA(high − low), with a shared Keltner length. On a higher timeframe, use historical last-completed aggregated bars with gaps between updates; on a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar in each chart bar. Compute the bands on the resulting chart-bar series, ignoring missing observations.
Long: Bullish Leledc OR bullish exhaustion OR close > upper middle band.
Short: Bearish Leledc OR bearish exhaustion OR close < lower middle band.
Long: Upward crossing of the selected lower buy zone AND NOT (previous bar has open < previous zone AND previous close > previous zone), OR open < lower buy zone AND close > lower buy zone.
Short: Downward crossing of the selected upper sell zone AND NOT (previous bar has open > previous zone AND previous close < previous zone), OR open > upper sell zone AND close < upper sell zone.
Long: Upward crossing of upper middle band AND optional squeeze filter is false AND, if the EMA filter is enabled, close > filter EMA AND open < upper middle band.
Short: Downward crossing of lower middle band AND optional squeeze filter is false AND, if the EMA filter is enabled, close < filter EMA AND open > lower middle band.
Long: Position is flat AND open > upper middle band AND close > upper middle band AND low < upper middle band AND optional squeeze filter is false AND, if the EMA filter is enabled, close > filter EMA.
Short: Position is flat AND open < lower middle band AND close < lower middle band AND high > lower middle band AND optional squeeze filter is false AND, if the EMA filter is enabled, close < filter EMA.
Long: Position is flat AND optional squeeze filter is false AND ((bullish Leledc AND low < selected lower buy zone) OR bullish exhaustion).
Short: Position is flat AND optional squeeze filter is false AND ((bearish Leledc AND high > selected upper sell zone) OR bearish exhaustion).
Long: Short position AND selected bullish exhaustion or bullish Leledc count since position opened reaches its configured occurrence within the most recent 201 bars AND, respectively, open < selected lower buy zone or low < selected lower buy zone.
Short: Long position AND selected bearish exhaustion or bearish Leledc count since position opened reaches its configured occurrence within the most recent 201 bars AND, respectively, open > selected upper sell zone or high > selected upper sell zone.
Long: Position is flat AND default-long mode is enabled AND default-short mode is disabled AND, if the EMA filter is enabled, close > filter EMA.
Short: Position is flat AND default-short mode is enabled AND default-long mode is disabled AND, if the EMA filter is enabled, close < filter EMA.
