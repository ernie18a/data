Calculation: Base EMA = EMA of close over the base lookback
Calculation: Top middle band = Base EMA + (EMA of high over the base lookback − Base EMA) × top-band multiplier
Calculation: Bottom middle band = Base EMA + (EMA of low over the base lookback − Base EMA) × bottom-band multiplier
Calculation: Bollinger exhaustion = overbought when RSI(source, RSI lookback) > overbought threshold AND the previous source > previous upper band AND the current source < current upper band; oversold when RSI(source, RSI lookback) < oversold threshold AND the previous source < previous lower band AND the current source > current lower band; bands use SMA(source, band lookback) ± standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation(source, band lookback)
Calculation: Leledc exhaustion = bearish when a counter incremented if close > close at the configured comparison offset exceeds its threshold AND close < open AND high is the highest over the configured lookback; bullish when a counter incremented if close < close at that offset exceeds its threshold AND close > open AND low is the lowest over that lookback. Each counter starts from its value at the configured offset and resets to zero on its corresponding signal. If volume filtering is enabled, require volume > SMA(volume, 100) × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Inner buy/sell zones = 8-bar WMA of the corresponding inner zone; outer buy/sell zones = 8-bar WMA of the corresponding outer zone. Without Keltner zones, inner sell = EMA(close) + (EMA(high) − EMA(close)) × φ × top-band multiplier × π; inner buy = EMA(close) − (EMA(close) − EMA(low)) × φ × bottom-band multiplier × π; outer sell = EMA(close) + (EMA(high) − EMA(close)) × φ × top-band multiplier × (φ + 4); outer buy = EMA(close) − (EMA(close) − EMA(low)) × φ × bottom-band multiplier × (φ + 4). With Keltner zones, these are EMA(close) plus/minus the corresponding inner or outer multiplier times RMA(high − low, base lookback).
Long: (open < top middle band AND close > top middle band OR open > top middle band AND close > top middle band AND previous close ≤ previous top middle band) AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close > slow EMA AND open < top middle band) OR open > top middle band AND close > top middle band AND low < top middle band AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close > slow EMA) AND (squeeze filter disabled OR no BB-Keltner squeeze) OR flat position AND no squeeze (if filtered) AND (bullish Leledc exhaustion AND low < selected inner or outer buy zone OR oversold Bollinger exhaustion) OR flat position AND no squeeze (if filtered) AND open < top middle band AND close > top middle band AND low < top middle band AND close > slow EMA (when slow EMA filter is enabled)
Short: (open > bottom middle band AND close < bottom middle band OR open < bottom middle band AND close < bottom middle band AND previous close ≥ previous bottom middle band) AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close < slow EMA AND open > bottom middle band) OR open < bottom middle band AND close < bottom middle band AND high > bottom middle band AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close < slow EMA) AND (squeeze filter disabled OR no BB-Keltner squeeze) OR flat position AND no squeeze (if filtered) AND (bearish Leledc exhaustion AND high > selected inner or outer sell zone OR overbought Bollinger exhaustion) OR flat position AND open < selected inner or outer sell zone AND overbought Bollinger exhaustion
Exit: Long, close < selected nearest or opposite middle band on a downward crossing (open > band AND close < band OR open < band AND close < band AND previous close ≥ previous band) OR selected delayed-inside condition (close below bottom middle band on a downward crossing OR no middle-band breakout over the configured delay window AND current open and close are inside both middle bands AND the open and close of the bar at the delay offset crossed down through the top middle band) OR selected buy/sell-zone loss (downward crossing of the top inner or outer sell zone) OR selected Nth bearish Leledc exhaustion since entry OR selected Nth overbought Bollinger exhaustion since entry
Exit: Short, close > selected nearest or opposite middle band on an upward crossing (open < band AND close > band OR open > band AND close > band AND previous close ≤ previous band) OR selected delayed-inside condition (close above top middle band on an upward crossing OR no middle-band breakout over the configured delay window AND current open and close are inside both middle bands AND the open and close of the bar at the delay offset crossed up through the bottom middle band) OR selected buy/sell-zone loss (upward crossing of the bottom inner or outer buy zone) OR selected Nth bullish Leledc exhaustion since entry OR selected Nth oversold Bollinger exhaustion since entry
Exit: Counter-trend long, close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR selected close condition: upward crossing of the selected buy/sell zone or middle band, bearish Leledc exhaustion, overbought Bollinger exhaustion, or price touching the selected take-profit band
Exit: Counter-trend short, close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR selected close condition: downward crossing of the selected buy/sell zone or middle band, bullish Leledc exhaustion, oversold Bollinger exhaustion, or price touching the selected take-profit band
