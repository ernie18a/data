Calculation: Directional profile = Divide the high-to-low range of a trailing chart-bar window into equal price rows. For each chart bar, calculate +DI, -DI, and ADX with the same lookback; optionally use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for these values. Let the weight be 1, or, when volume weighting is enabled, the positive volume of the same timeframe used for DMI, falling back to 1 if unavailable or nonpositive. Multiply each DMI value by the weight and by 1 + abs(+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI), using zero for the fraction when its denominator is zero. Divide each result equally among the price rows touched by the chart bar's low-to-high range. Sum the allocated +DI, -DI, and ADX separately for each row; divide the ADX sum by the number of chart bars touching that row to obtain its average ADX. Normalize each row's +DI and -DI sums separately by the maximum row sum for that component.
Long: Any row's average ADX >= threshold AND its +DI sum > its -DI sum multiplied by a dominance factor greater than 1 AND its normalized +DI sum >= noise threshold.
Short: Any row's average ADX >= threshold AND its -DI sum > its +DI sum multiplied by a dominance factor greater than 1 AND its normalized -DI sum >= noise threshold.
