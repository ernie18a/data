Calculation: Event schedules = parameterized UTC timestamp lists for new moons, full moons, and total and partial lunar and solar eclipses. First-quarter timestamps are midpoints from each new moon to the next full moon; last-quarter timestamps are midpoints from each full moon to the next new moon. An event occurs on the first chart bar whose opening datetime is at or after its timestamp, including the first loaded bar when a prior timestamp exists.
Calculation: Moon window = a window beginning on each new-moon, first-quarter, full-moon or last-quarter event bar, or the first bar on which the integer day since the latest new moon plus one equals a designated lunation day. A new event replaces any previous window. The window lasts through a parameterized number of subsequent chart bars and permits only the first long or short decision within it.
Calculation: Previous-cycle pocket = for each enabled new-moon, full-moon, total-eclipse or partial-eclipse schedule, take the highest high H and lowest low L from the previous event bar inclusive to the latest event bar exclusive. If H > L, the pocket boundaries are H - (H - L) times each of two retracement parameters; the upper boundary uses the smaller parameter.
Calculation: New-moon anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) times volume divided by cumulative volume since the latest new-moon event bar, inclusive. It is unavailable when disabled, cumulative volume is zero, or the segment exceeds the configured lookback limit.
Calculation: Numerology match = convert the absolute close to an integer by repeatedly multiplying by 10 until it is at least 1000 or a fixed maximum number of multiplications is reached, then round. Its root is obtained by repeatedly summing decimal digits until reaching a single digit or 11, 22 or 33. A repeating integer has at least three identical nonzero trailing digits. Calculate the date root the same way from the sum of the bar opening date's digits in the chart's exchange timezone. Calculate the lunation root the same way from floor((bar opening time - reference full-moon timestamp) / synodic period) + 1. A match requires the price root to equal the date root or lunation root, or be 11, 22 or 33, or the price integer to repeat; additionally, the reduced price root plus the reduced date root must not equal 10 unless the price root is 11, 22 or 33 or the price integer repeats. Reduced roots replace 11, 22 and 33 with 2, 4 and 6 respectively.
Long: Moon window is open AND close is inside at least one enabled previous-cycle pocket, inclusive AND numerology match is true AND absolute close minus open is at least ATR times a body-size parameter AND close > open AND close > previous close AND (new-moon anchored VWAP is unavailable OR close > new-moon anchored VWAP).
Short: Moon window is open AND close is inside at least one enabled previous-cycle pocket, inclusive AND numerology match is true AND absolute close minus open is at least ATR times a body-size parameter AND close < open AND close < previous close AND (new-moon anchored VWAP is unavailable OR close < new-moon anchored VWAP).
