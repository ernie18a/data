Calculation: Active bearish gap = a three-bar gap where the current high is below the low two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; it remains active until a bar closes above its upper boundary.
Calculation: Active bullish gap = a three-bar gap where the current low is above the high two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; it remains active until a bar closes below its lower boundary.
Long: an active bearish gap exists AND close > its upper boundary
Short: an active bullish gap exists AND close < its lower boundary
