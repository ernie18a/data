Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the most recently completed occurrence of the specified configurable session, using the configured session timezone.
Calculation: Low sweep = during London, sweep the completed Asia low; OR during New York, sweep the completed London low; OR, if enabled, during New York, sweep the completed Asia low. A sweep requires either low < session low AND close > session low, or low <= session low, according to the selected confirmation mode. Each session low can be swept only once per occurrence of the sweeping session.
Calculation: High sweep = during London, sweep the completed Asia high; OR during New York, sweep the completed London high; OR, if enabled, during New York, sweep the completed Asia high. A sweep requires either high > session high AND close < session high, or high >= session high, according to the selected confirmation mode. Each session high can be swept only once per occurrence of the sweeping session.
Long: Low sweep.
Short: High sweep.
Long: Bars since the most recent low sweep <= expansion window AND no earlier bullish expansion since that sweep AND high - low >= ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the volume threshold) AND close > open AND close > the highest high over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Short: Bars since the most recent high sweep <= expansion window AND no earlier bearish expansion since that sweep AND high - low >= ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the volume threshold) AND close < open AND close < the lowest low over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
