Calculation: Completed Asia high/low = highest high/lowest low among bars in the most recently completed Asia session; completed London high/low = same for the most recently completed London session. Session membership uses the configured session windows and chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = during London, price sweeps the completed Asia low; or during New York, price sweeps the completed London low or, when enabled, the completed Asia low. A close-back-inside sweep requires low < level and close > level; a wick-touch sweep requires low <= level.
Calculation: Buy-side sweep = during London, price sweeps the completed Asia high; or during New York, price sweeps the completed London high or, when enabled, the completed Asia high. A close-back-inside sweep requires high > level and close < level; a wick-touch sweep requires high >= level.
Long: A sell-side sweep occurred within the expansion window, and high - low >= ATR × displacement threshold, and close > open, and close > the highest high of the previous 3 bars; signal at most once after each sell-side sweep.
Short: A buy-side sweep occurred within the expansion window, and high - low >= ATR × displacement threshold, and close < open, and close < the lowest low of the previous 3 bars; signal at most once after each buy-side sweep.
