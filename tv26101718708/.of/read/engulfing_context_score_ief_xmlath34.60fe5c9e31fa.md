Calculation: Body = abs(close − open); previous body is its value on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Candidate = previous body >= previous-bar ATR × minimum body-to-ATR parameter AND current body >= previous body × minimum body-ratio parameter.
Calculation: Score = round(100 × weighted sum of expansion, close location, rejection wick, participation, volatility and regime components / sum of their nonnegative weights). Clamp each component to [0,1]. Expansion = current body / max(previous body, positive price-floor parameter) − 1. Range = max(high − low, positive price-floor parameter). For long, close location = (close − low) / range and rejection = 2 × (min(open, close) − low) / range; for short, close location = (high − close) / range and rejection = 2 × (high − max(open, close)) / range. Participation = (volume / SMA(volume) − lower relative-volume parameter) / relative-volume span parameter; use 0.5 if volume or its SMA is unavailable or the SMA is not positive. Relative ATR = ATR / SMA(ATR), or 1 if the ATR SMA is unavailable or zero. Volatility = (relative ATR − lower volatility parameter) / (peak volatility parameter − lower volatility parameter) when relative ATR <= peak volatility parameter; otherwise 1 − (relative ATR − peak volatility parameter) / (upper volatility parameter − peak volatility parameter). Regime = 1 for long when the shorter EMA of close > the longer EMA, or for short when the shorter EMA < the longer EMA; otherwise 0. Regime EMAs use the preceding chart bar when the context timeframe equals the chart timeframe, or the last completed higher-timeframe bar when it is higher.
Calculation: Cooldown passes = no previous qualifying signal exists OR bars since the most recent qualifying long or short signal > cooldown parameter. A qualifying signal is a candidate meeting its direction's pattern conditions, score threshold and cooldown; qualifying signals in either direction reset the shared cooldown.
Long: Previous close < previous open AND close > open AND candidate AND (full-body requirement disabled OR open <= previous close AND close >= previous open) AND (close-break requirement disabled OR close > previous high) AND ((score >= score threshold AND cooldown passes) OR (below-threshold markers enabled AND score < score threshold)).
Short: Previous close > previous open AND close < open AND candidate AND (full-body requirement disabled OR open >= previous close AND close <= previous open) AND (close-break requirement disabled OR close < previous low) AND ((score >= score threshold AND cooldown passes) OR (below-threshold markers enabled AND score < score threshold)).
