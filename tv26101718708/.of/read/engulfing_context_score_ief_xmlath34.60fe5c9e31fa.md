Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close − open
Calculation: Body expansion score = clamp(current candle body / previous candle body − 1, 0, 1)
Calculation: Bullish close-location score = clamp((close − low) / candle range, 0, 1); bearish close-location score = clamp((high − close) / candle range, 0, 1)
Calculation: Bullish rejection-wick score = clamp(2 × lower-wick length / candle range, 0, 1); bearish rejection-wick score = clamp(2 × upper-wick length / candle range, 0, 1)
Calculation: Participation score = clamp((current volume / its simple-average baseline − 0.75) / 1.25, 0, 1); use a neutral score of 0.5 when volume or its positive baseline is unavailable
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp((ATR / its longer-lookback simple-average baseline − 0.60) / 0.90, 0, 1) when the ratio is at most 1.5; otherwise clamp(1 − (ratio − 1.5) / 1.5, 0, 1). Use a ratio of 1 when the baseline is unavailable or zero
Calculation: Bullish regime score = 1 when the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, otherwise 0; bearish regime score = 1 when the fast EMA is below the slow EMA, otherwise 0. Use EMAs from the last completed bar of an equal-or-higher timeframe
Calculation: Bullish score = round(100 × weighted average of body expansion, bullish close-location, bullish rejection-wick, participation, volatility, and bullish regime scores)
Calculation: Bearish score = round(100 × weighted average of body expansion, bearish close-location, bearish rejection-wick, participation, volatility, and bearish regime scores)
Long: chart bar is closed AND previous candle closes below its open AND current candle closes above its open AND previous candle body ≥ previous-bar ATR × minimum body/ATR parameter AND current candle body ≥ previous candle body × minimum body-ratio parameter AND (full body engulfment is not required OR current open ≤ previous close AND current close ≥ previous open) AND (close break is not required OR current close > previous high) AND bullish score ≥ minimum score AND no qualifying signal occurred within the cooldown period
Short: chart bar is closed AND previous candle closes above its open AND current candle closes below its open AND previous candle body ≥ previous-bar ATR × minimum body/ATR parameter AND current candle body ≥ previous candle body × minimum body-ratio parameter AND (full body engulfment is not required OR current open ≥ previous close AND current close ≤ previous open) AND (close break is not required OR current close < previous low) AND bearish score ≥ minimum score AND no qualifying signal occurred within the cooldown period
