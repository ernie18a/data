Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side; recognize it only when the right-side bars have completed. Process a simultaneous high before a low. Initialize the anchor with the first confirmed pivot. Replace it only with an opposite-type pivot whose distance from the anchor is at least a minimum number of bars and whose absolute price change is at least a configurable multiple of ATR at confirmation; otherwise retain the anchor.
Calculation: Active segment direction = up when the accepted anchor is a low and down when it is a high. Its age is the number of bars since the anchor.
Calculation: Active channel = a line from the accepted anchor to either the previous bar's close or the current bar's close, according to the non-repaint setting, projected to the current bar. Its half-width is the greater of a configurable ATR multiple and a configurable multiple of the largest high-above-line or line-above-low deviation sampled from the anchor through the endpoint. Sample every max(1, ceiling(segment bar count / 500)) bars, excluding bars outside the available historical buffer. The upper and lower boundaries are the projected line plus and minus this half-width; use current ATR for the width floor.
Calculation: Regression channel = ordinary least-squares regression of close against bar age over a configurable lookback, evaluated at the current bar, plus and minus a configurable multiple of residual standard error sqrt(sum of squared residuals / (lookback - 2)). Its fit is the squared correlation between close and bar age. Its forward slope is the negative of the fitted slope against bar age, and its angle is atan(forward slope / ATR) in degrees, or zero when ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Trend bias = retain a configurable maximum number of accepted completed segments and use up to a configurable number of the most recent. Weight each by its bar distance multiplied by max(abs(atan(segment slope / ATR at confirmation) in degrees), 1); add the active segment with its current age and current ATR-normalized angle in the same way. The up ratio is up-segment weight divided by total weight; the net move is current close minus the oldest included segment's starting price, or the active anchor price if there are no completed segments, divided by current ATR. When total weight is positive, classify in this order: strong bullish if net move exceeds a positive strong threshold AND up ratio is at least a lower override threshold; strong bearish if net move is below the negative strong threshold AND up ratio is at most the corresponding upper override threshold; strong bullish if up ratio is at least a high threshold; bullish if up ratio is at least a lower bullish threshold OR net move exceeds a smaller positive threshold AND up ratio is at least a lower bullish override threshold; strong bearish if up ratio is at most a low threshold; bearish if up ratio is at most a higher bearish threshold OR net move is below the negative smaller threshold AND up ratio is at most a higher bearish override threshold; otherwise mixed.
Long: An accepted pivot is a low.
Short: An accepted pivot is a high.
Long: Active segment direction is up.
Short: Active segment direction is down.
Long: Regression channel angle exceeds a positive direction threshold.
Short: Regression channel angle is below the negative direction threshold.
Long: Trend bias is bullish OR strong bullish.
Short: Trend bias is bearish OR strong bearish.
Long: Active segment direction is down AND close crosses above the active upper boundary.
Short: Active segment direction is up AND close crosses below the active lower boundary.
Long: Active segment direction is up AND close is at or above the active lower boundary AND close is at or below that boundary plus a configurable ATR multiple AND the same complete condition was false on the previous bar.
Short: Active segment direction is down AND close is at or below the active upper boundary AND close is at or above that boundary minus a configurable ATR multiple AND the same complete condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: Active segment direction is up AND segment age is at least a minimum and at most a maximum AND regression channel fit is at least its threshold AND close crosses above the regression channel upper boundary.
Short: Active segment direction is down AND segment age is at least a minimum and at most a maximum AND regression channel fit is at least its threshold AND close crosses below the regression channel lower boundary.
