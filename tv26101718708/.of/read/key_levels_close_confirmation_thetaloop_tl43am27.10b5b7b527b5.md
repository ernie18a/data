Calculation: Level = each enabled, available previous-day high, low or close; previous-week high or low; current-week or current-month open; opening-range high or low; or premarket high or low. Previous-period levels use the last completed calendar-period bar; current-period opens use the bar still forming. These period timeframes are higher than or equal to the chart timeframe. Previous-day levels are available on chart timeframes no higher than a day; weekly and monthly levels on chart timeframes below a week. Opening-range and premarket levels are available only on intraday charts: the opening range is the high or low of chart bars starting from the first regular-session bar and before the configured range end, available after that end; premarket levels are the high or low of premarket chart bars in the current session, available after premarket ends. Current-week and current-month opens are unavailable before their new periods begin.
Calculation: Counted confirmation close = the close of the last completed confirmation bar, evaluated on the first chart bar of the next confirmation period. The confirmation timeframe is higher than or equal to the chart timeframe; a configured lower timeframe falls back to the chart timeframe. If the regular-session filter is enabled and both timeframes are intraday, count only confirmation bars whose final chart bar is in the regular session; otherwise count every confirmation bar.
Long: A new counted confirmation close > the previous chart bar's level AND the preceding counted confirmation close <= that same level AND, if the first-event filter is enabled, no earlier upward confirmed break of that level has occurred in the current session.
Short: A new counted confirmation close < the previous chart bar's level AND the preceding counted confirmation close >= that same level AND, if the first-event filter is enabled, no earlier downward confirmed break of that level has occurred in the current session.
Long: Chart-bar low < level AND open >= level AND close >= level AND, if the regular-session filter is enabled on an intraday chart, the bar is in the regular session AND, if the first-event filter is enabled, no earlier probe from above of that level has occurred in the current session.
Short: Chart-bar high > level AND open <= level AND close <= level AND, if the regular-session filter is enabled on an intraday chart, the bar is in the regular session AND, if the first-event filter is enabled, no earlier probe from below of that level has occurred in the current session.
