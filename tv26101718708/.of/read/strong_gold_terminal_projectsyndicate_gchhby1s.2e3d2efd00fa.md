Calculation: Composite pressure score = 100 × the weighted mean across five configured timeframes, ordered from shortest to longest; each timeframe’s score is the mean of six scores: Supertrend direction (+1 up, −1 down); (number of 20/50/200 EMAs below close − 1.5)/1.5; clamp((RSI − 50)/25, −1, 1); MACD histogram score (+1 if histogram and its change are positive, +0.5 if histogram is positive and its change is nonpositive, −1 if both are negative, −0.5 otherwise); ADX directional score = sign(DI+ − DI−) × min(ADX/40, 1); and (position in the 20-bar high-low range − 50)/50. Give longer timeframes at least as much weight as shorter ones. Use each timeframe’s current bar, including while it is forming; the configured timeframes may be lower or higher than the chart timeframe.
Long: close crosses above the previous day’s high OR close crosses above the previous week’s high OR chart-timeframe Supertrend direction flips from down to up OR composite pressure crosses above +40
Short: close crosses below the previous day’s low OR close crosses below the previous week’s low OR chart-timeframe Supertrend direction flips from up to down OR composite pressure crosses below −40
