Calculation: MACD histograms = MACD histograms of the selected price source using the same fast, slow, and signal lengths on five independently selected timeframes. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar; for the chart timeframe, use the current completed bar. Compare prior histogram values on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish count = number of the five histograms greater than zero; bearish count = number less than zero.
Calculation: Long stop = entry signal close minus the stop distance. If trailing is enabled with the stop, update it to the greater of the previous stop and the highest high reached while in the position minus the trailing distance. If breakeven is enabled and has not yet been reached in the trade, set the stop to entry signal close plus the breakeven offset when the previous bar had a long position and close minus entry signal close reaches the breakeven trigger distance. Apply the breakeven update after the trailing update. Reset the highest high to the current high on the bar after any entry signal.
Calculation: Short stop = entry signal close plus the stop distance. If trailing is enabled with the stop, update it to the lesser of the previous stop and the lowest low reached while in the position plus the trailing distance. If breakeven is enabled and has not yet been reached in the trade, set the stop to entry signal close minus the breakeven offset when the previous bar had a short position and entry signal close minus close reaches the breakeven trigger distance. Apply the breakeven update after the trailing update. Reset the lowest low to the current low on the bar after any entry signal.
Long: Any one of the five histograms is greater than zero AND its value on the previous chart bar was less than zero.
Short: Any one of the five histograms is less than zero AND its value on the previous chart bar was greater than zero.
Long: Bullish count equals five AND previous bullish count is less than five AND position side is flat or short, evaluated at chart bar close.
Short: Bearish count equals five AND previous bearish count is less than five AND position side is flat or long, evaluated at chart bar close.
Exit: long, previous bar position side is long AND stop loss is enabled AND low is less than or equal to the long stop.
Exit: short, previous bar position side is short AND stop loss is enabled AND high is greater than or equal to the short stop.
Exit: long, previous bar position side is long AND the stop condition is false AND high reaches entry signal close plus the first take-profit distance; close the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: long, previous bar position side is long AND the stop condition is false AND high reaches entry signal close plus the second take-profit distance; close the remaining position.
Exit: short, previous bar position side is short AND the stop condition is false AND low reaches entry signal close minus the first take-profit distance; close the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: short, previous bar position side is short AND the stop condition is false AND low reaches entry signal close minus the second take-profit distance; close the remaining position.
Exit: long, previous bar position side is long AND close-on-opposite is enabled AND previous bullish count equals five AND bullish count is less than five, evaluated at chart bar close.
Exit: short, previous bar position side is short AND close-on-opposite is enabled AND previous bearish count equals five AND bearish count is less than five, evaluated at chart bar close.
Exit: long, bearish count equals five AND previous bearish count is less than five, evaluated at chart bar close.
Exit: short, bullish count equals five AND previous bullish count is less than five, evaluated at chart bar close.
