Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = the previous bar's high/low is a pivot against a configurable number of preceding bars and the current bar; retain the latest confirmed value of each until replaced or broken.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > retained pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high; bearish structure break = close < retained pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low. Clear the corresponding retained pivot after a break. Structure direction starts neutral and becomes bullish or bearish on the corresponding break; a change of character is a break opposite the preceding structure direction.
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); body ratio = body / (high - low), or zero when high = low. Upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low. Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close; bearish engulfing reverses all inequalities. Bullish hammer = close > open AND lower wick > body times a wick multiplier AND upper wick < body times a smaller wick multiplier; bearish shooting star reverses candle direction and exchanges upper and lower wicks.
Calculation: Swing position = (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) over a lookback longer than the pivot lookback; it is undefined when the range is zero.
Calculation: Long score = the sum of configurable points, capped at a configurable maximum: trend points for short EMA > medium EMA and close > long EMA when the trend filter is enabled, otherwise a fixed trend contribution; RSI points for RSI below an oversold threshold, otherwise fewer points below a higher threshold; stochastic %K points below a low threshold, otherwise fewer points below a higher threshold; structure points for bullish change of character, otherwise fewer points for bullish structure break; candle points for bullish engulfing, otherwise for bullish hammer, otherwise for close > open AND body ratio > a threshold; volume points when volume > its SMA times a multiplier if the volume filter is enabled, otherwise a fixed volume contribution; support points when swing position < a threshold; ADX points when ADX > a threshold if the ADX filter is enabled, otherwise a fixed ADX contribution.
Calculation: Short score = the sum of configurable points, capped at the same maximum: trend points for short EMA < medium EMA and close < long EMA when the trend filter is enabled, otherwise a fixed trend contribution; RSI points for RSI above an overbought threshold, otherwise fewer points above a lower threshold; stochastic %K points above a high threshold, otherwise fewer points above a lower threshold; structure points for bearish change of character, otherwise fewer points for bearish structure break; candle points for bearish engulfing, otherwise for bearish shooting star, otherwise for close < open AND body ratio > a threshold; the same volume and ADX contributions as the long score; resistance points when swing position > a threshold higher than the support threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed long/short signal = the corresponding score >= signal threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed signal of that same side > cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= ADX threshold). Track each side's cooldown independently.
Calculation: Smart trail = maintain a direction and level, initially unset. In order on each bar: on a confirmed long signal or bullish direction with close > trail, set the trail to close minus ATR times a multiplier, or the greater of that value and the existing trail if already bullish, and set direction bullish; on a confirmed short signal or bearish direction with close < trail, set the trail to close plus ATR times the multiplier, or the lesser of that value and the existing trail if already bearish, and set direction bearish. Then, if bullish and close < trail, set trail to close plus the ATR distance and direction bearish; if bearish and close > trail, set trail to close minus the ATR distance and direction bullish. Use close times a fallback fraction when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR at a confirmed signal times a stop multiplier; use close times a fallback fraction when ATR is unavailable. Store entry close and risk for that signal.
Long: Long score >= signal threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed long signal > cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= ADX threshold).
Short: Short score >= signal threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed short signal > cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= ADX threshold).
Long: Long score >= rounded pending fraction of signal threshold AND long score < signal threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed long signal > cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= ADX threshold).
Short: Short score >= rounded pending fraction of signal threshold AND short score < signal threshold AND bars since the previous confirmed short signal > cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= ADX threshold).
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Confirmed pivot low exists on the current bar.
Short: Confirmed pivot high exists on the current bar.
Long: Smart trail direction = bullish AND previous smart trail direction = bearish.
Short: Smart trail direction = bearish AND previous smart trail direction = bullish.
Long: Long score > short score.
Short: Long score <= short score.
Exit: long, bars since entry > minimum tracking delay AND (low <= entry price - entry risk OR high >= entry price + entry risk times first reward multiplier OR bars since entry > maximum tracking age).
Exit: short, bars since entry > minimum tracking delay AND (high >= entry price + entry risk OR low <= entry price - entry risk times first reward multiplier OR bars since entry > maximum tracking age).
