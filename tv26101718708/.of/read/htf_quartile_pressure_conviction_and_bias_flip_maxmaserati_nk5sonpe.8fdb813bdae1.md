Calculation: Lower quartile of a bar = low + 0.25 × (high − low).
Calculation: Upper quartile of a bar = low + 0.75 × (high − low).
Calculation: Bearish violation level = the lower quartile of the most recent higher-timeframe completed bar that closed below its open and whose immediately following higher-timeframe completed bar closed above that quartile; retain the level until a newer qualifying bar replaces it.
Calculation: Bullish violation level = the upper quartile of the most recent higher-timeframe completed bar that closed at or above its open and whose immediately following higher-timeframe completed bar closed below that quartile; retain the level until a newer qualifying bar replaces it.
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe bar close >= open AND close >= its upper quartile.

Short: Last completed higher-timeframe bar close < open AND close <= its lower quartile.
