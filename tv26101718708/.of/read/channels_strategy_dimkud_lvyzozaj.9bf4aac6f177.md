Calculation: Channel = selected Bollinger Bands of close or Keltner Channel of close; the extended stop band uses the same channel type and length with an independently selected width multiplier.
Calculation: ATR filter values = true range smoothed with the same selected RMA, SMA, EMA, or WMA method over two independently selected lengths.
Calculation: Each requested-timeframe RSI of close, MFI of (high + low + close) / 3, or CCI of close uses the chart timeframe or an independently selected higher or lower timeframe. On historical bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe value, including a higher bar completing at the chart bar close; for a lower timeframe use its last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Long stop = selected entry price multiplied by (1 minus stop fraction), previous bar low for a wick-out or close-out entry mode or current bar low for a return-in entry mode, current extended lower band, or current close minus ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier. Short stop = selected entry price multiplied by (1 plus stop fraction), previous bar high for a wick-out or close-out entry mode or current bar high for a return-in entry mode, current extended upper band, or current close plus ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = selected entry price multiplied by (1 plus target fraction), current upper channel band, current channel middle line, or current close plus ATR multiplied by the target multiplier. Short target = selected entry price multiplied by (1 minus target fraction), current lower channel band, current channel middle line, or current close minus ATR multiplied by the target multiplier. Stop and target orders are recalculated after each bar and after fills; their most recently submitted levels apply to subsequent price movement.
Long: Longs enabled AND (selected wick-out mode AND low < current lower channel band OR selected wick-return mode AND previous low < current lower channel band AND close > current lower channel band OR selected close-out mode AND close < current lower channel band OR selected close-return mode AND previous close < current lower channel band AND close > current lower channel band) AND (ATR filter disabled OR selected comparison of first ATR filter value < or > second ATR filter value is true) AND (first RSI filter pair disabled OR long filter enabled AND first RSI >= long lower bound AND first RSI <= long upper bound) AND (second RSI filter pair disabled OR long filter enabled AND second RSI >= long lower bound AND second RSI <= long upper bound OR long filter disabled AND short filter enabled AND second RSI is unavailable) AND (first MFI filter pair disabled OR long filter enabled AND first MFI > long lower bound AND first MFI < long upper bound) AND (second MFI filter pair disabled OR long filter enabled AND second MFI >= long lower bound AND second MFI <= long upper bound) AND (CCI filter pair disabled OR long filter enabled AND CCI >= long lower bound AND CCI <= long upper bound) AND datetime >= test start AND datetime < test end AND (datetime < excluded-period start OR datetime > excluded-period end) AND ATR > 0.
Short: Shorts enabled AND (selected wick-out mode AND high > current upper channel band OR selected wick-return mode AND previous high > current upper channel band AND close < current upper channel band OR selected close-out mode AND close > current upper channel band OR selected close-return mode AND previous close > current upper channel band AND close < current upper channel band) AND (ATR filter disabled OR selected comparison of first ATR filter value < or > second ATR filter value is true) AND (first RSI filter pair disabled OR short filter enabled AND first RSI >= short lower bound AND first RSI <= short upper bound) AND (second RSI filter pair disabled OR short filter enabled AND second RSI >= short lower bound AND second RSI <= short upper bound OR long filter disabled AND short filter enabled AND second RSI is unavailable) AND (first MFI filter pair disabled OR short filter enabled AND first MFI > short lower bound AND first MFI < short upper bound) AND (second MFI filter pair disabled OR short filter enabled AND second MFI >= short lower bound AND second MFI <= short upper bound) AND (CCI filter pair disabled OR short filter enabled AND CCI >= short lower bound AND CCI <= short upper bound) AND datetime >= test start AND datetime < test end AND (datetime < excluded-period start OR datetime > excluded-period end) AND ATR > 0.
Long: The long condition above AND position size = 0 AND (delay disabled OR bar close time minus selected delay >= time of most recent position-size change) AND current bar index != most recently closed trade's entry bar index AND current bar index != its exit bar index, unless no trade has closed.
Short: The short condition above AND position size = 0 AND (delay disabled OR bar close time minus selected delay >= time of most recent position-size change) AND current bar index != most recently closed trade's entry bar index AND current bar index != its exit bar index, unless no trade has closed AND the long entry condition is false.
Exit: long, low <= submitted long stop OR high >= submitted long target.
Exit: short, high >= submitted short stop OR low <= submitted short target.
