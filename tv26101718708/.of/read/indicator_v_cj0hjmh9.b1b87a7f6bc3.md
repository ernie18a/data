Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume >= a multiple of the chart-timeframe simple moving average of volume, including the current bar.
Calculation: Prior high and prior low = respectively the highest high and lowest low over a chart-timeframe lookback ending on the preceding bar.
Calculation: High sweep = preceding bar's high >= current prior high AND close < current prior high. Low sweep = preceding bar's low <= current prior low AND close > current prior low.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND close > open AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= an upper close-location threshold AND close > preceding open AND open < preceding close AND volume spike. Bearish displacement = absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND close < open AND (close - low) / (high - low) <= a lower close-location threshold AND close < preceding open AND open > preceding close AND volume spike. If high equals low, use a neutral close location.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = low > high two chart bars earlier AND the difference >= a fraction of current close. Bearish fair-value gap = high < low two chart bars earlier AND the difference >= a fraction of current close. Retain the latest gap of each direction and its bar index; replace it on a new gap and clear it after its corresponding fair-value-gap entry. A bullish gap spans the earlier high to current low; a bearish gap spans current high to the earlier low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap midpoint = the rounded midpoint of the latest qualifying gap of the relevant direction on either of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other. A bullish gap qualifies when the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low > the high of the preceding higher-timeframe bar AND their difference >= a fraction of current chart close; a bearish gap qualifies when the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high < the low of the preceding higher-timeframe bar AND their difference >= that fraction of current chart close. Retain each midpoint until replaced by a later qualifying gap of the same direction and timeframe.
Calculation: Long structure proximity = absolute distance from close to the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low, either of two forming higher-timeframe bars' opens, or either bullish higher-timeframe gap midpoint <= the respective ATR multiple. Short structure proximity = the same comparison using the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high, the same two forming higher-timeframe opens, or either bearish gap midpoint. The timeframe of the completed high or low is the same as the shorter open timeframe; the other open timeframe is higher. The gap timeframes are the same as the completed high or low timeframe and a shorter higher timeframe.
Calculation: Range boundaries = when the highest high minus lowest low over a chart-timeframe lookback first becomes <= an ATR multiple, activate a range and set its boundaries to those extremes. While the range remains active and that comparison remains true, expand the boundaries to include the current high and low before testing for a break. Deactivate the range after either boundary break, regardless of volume.
Calculation: Long break-of-structure level = on each low sweep, replace the pending level with the highest high over a chart-timeframe lookback ending on the preceding bar; clear it after close exceeds it or after long invalidation. Short break-of-structure level = on each high sweep, replace the pending level with the lowest low over the same lookback ending on the preceding bar; clear it after close falls below it or after short invalidation.
Calculation: Long invalidation level = on each low sweep, set the level to prior low minus an ATR multiple and activate it; deactivate the short invalidation level. Short invalidation level = on each high sweep, set the level to prior high plus an ATR multiple and activate it; deactivate the long invalidation level. If both sweeps occur, process the low sweep first. Deactivate a level after its invalidation.
Long: Low sweep.
Short: High sweep.
Long: Close > the pending long break-of-structure level.
Short: Close < the pending short break-of-structure level.
Long: Bullish displacement AND long structure proximity.
Short: Bearish displacement AND short structure proximity.
Long: Bullish displacement AND close >= the latest bullish fair-value gap's lower boundary AND close <= its upper boundary AND bars since the gap <= an age limit.
Short: Bearish displacement AND close >= the latest bearish fair-value gap's lower boundary AND close <= its upper boundary AND bars since the gap <= an age limit.
Long: Range active AND close > range upper boundary AND open < range upper boundary AND absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND volume spike.
Short: Range active AND close < range lower boundary AND open > range lower boundary AND absolute candle body >= an ATR multiple AND volume spike.
Exit: long, long invalidation level active AND close < long invalidation level.
Exit: short, short invalidation level active AND close > short invalidation level.
