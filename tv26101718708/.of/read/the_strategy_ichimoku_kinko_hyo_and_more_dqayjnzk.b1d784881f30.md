Calculation: Index [n] denotes n bars ago. Crosses above means current value > comparison value AND previous value <= its previous comparison value; crosses below is symmetric. A newly rising or falling condition is true now and was false on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Ichimoku conversion and base lines are midpoints of rolling highest high and lowest low, with the conversion lookback shorter than the base lookback. Leading span A is their midpoint; leading span B is the rolling high-low midpoint over a longer lookback than the base line. Cloud upper and lower boundaries are the maximum and minimum of the two spans shifted backward by the Ichimoku displacement minus one bar. Lagged price and cloud comparisons use values displaced backward by the same amount.
Calculation: TRIX slope input = change in the triple EMA of log(close), multiplied by a scale factor.
Calculation: Grid level = previous grid level, initially zero; replace it with close whenever high > previous level + grid gap OR low < previous level - grid gap.
Calculation: A confirmed high fractal is a pivot high with equal left and right confirmation lengths; a low fractal is symmetric. Use the most recently confirmed fractal price unless an older occurrence is explicitly selected.
Calculation: Segment-up state = low > low[1] AND, scanning backward at most a segment lookback for each successive segment, low[1] > low[i+2] for the first matching i, then low[1+c1] > low[i+2] for the first matching i starting at c1, then low[1+c1+c2] > low[i+2+c1] for some i starting at c2; c1 and c2 are the respective counts of bars scanned. Segment-down state uses high < high[1] and reverses all three inequalities.
Calculation: A reverse low fractal = a current pivot low with a specified left lookback AND close > open AND close[1] < open[1], OR a pivot low confirmed one bar later with a longer left lookback AND either the same candle conditions OR low > low[1] AND close < open AND close[1] < open[1]. A reverse high fractal swaps high for low and reverses the inequalities.
Calculation: Fractal trend-line level = the latest confirmed fractal price plus its difference from the preceding confirmed fractal price, divided by the separation of the two fractals, multiplied by the number of bars since the latest fractal; use high fractals for the upper line and low fractals for the lower line.
Calculation: Ichimoku confluence score = sum of eleven persistent binary components. Each component becomes 1 on its first comparison, 0 on its second, and otherwise retains its previous value: conversion > cloud upper / conversion < cloud lower; close > displaced high / close < displaced low; close > displaced cloud upper / close < displaced cloud lower; high > conversion / low < conversion; high > base / low < base; conversion > base / conversion < base; span A > span B / span A < span B; high > cloud upper / low < cloud lower; close > displaced base / close < displaced base; close > displaced conversion / close < displaced conversion; base > cloud upper / base < cloud upper. If both comparisons hold, use the first.
Calculation: Broad confluence score = sum of persistent binary components for short SMA > long SMA, short EMA > long EMA, high > base line versus low < base line, conversion > base line, strong Ichimoku long versus strong Ichimoku short, reduced strong Ichimoku long versus reduced strong Ichimoku short, high >= Parabolic SAR versus low <= Parabolic SAR, high > latest high fractal versus low < latest low fractal, MACD line > signal line, RSI > midpoint, positive DI > negative DI, smoothed signed-volume oscillator > zero, positive vortex > negative vortex, high > SuperTrend versus low < SuperTrend, high > conversion versus low < conversion, short EMA > short SMA, and MACD line rising. Binary components retain their previous value when neither comparison holds; the first comparison takes precedence. Add two further components worth a positive weight when HMA or ALMA is rising and a negative weight otherwise. Signed volume is volume when the change in typical price is nonnegative and negative volume otherwise; its oscillator is the EMA of the difference between a shorter and a longer EMA of signed volume.

Calculation: Each entry signal may be combined with up to two independently selected entry filters, both of which must pass. Filter choices are the corresponding directional state comparisons above, ADX > threshold, MACD line above or below its signal line, or rising versus not rising TRIX, HMA, ALMA, or MACD line. A stochastic RSI long filter requires both smoothed K and D above the lower threshold AND NOT (K < D AND both below the upper threshold); the short filter requires both below the upper threshold AND NOT (K > D AND both above the upper threshold). The second short DMI filter uses positive DI > negative DI. The selected same-side exit filter must also be false for an entry.
Calculation: Eligible entry signals and filters may use the chart timeframe or independently selected higher timeframes. Higher-timeframe calculations use either the still-forming bar or the last completed bar; in the latter mode chart-timeframe signals use the previous chart bar. Grid, segment, fractal trend-line, and confluence calculations use chart bars. Either of two selected entry signals can trigger when the second is enabled; entry filters apply to both.
