Calculation: New-high percentage = new 52-week highs / total issues × 100
Calculation: New-low percentage = new 52-week lows / total issues × 100
Calculation: McClellan Oscillator = shorter-lookback EMA of 1000 × (advancing issues − declining issues) / (advancing issues + declining issues) − longer-lookback EMA of the same series
