Calculation: Volume oscillator = either cumulative sum of sign(change in close) × volume, or volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), with the latter equal to 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Pivot pair = the newly confirmed pivot and the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right-hand bars; use the same left and right pivot lengths for price and oscillator pivots. For a price-pivot pair, use high at high pivots or low at low pivots and the oscillator on those pivot bars. For an oscillator-pivot pair, use the oscillator pivot values and high at high pivots or low at low pivots. The pivot bars must be distinct and their separation must not exceed the maximum lookback.
Calculation: Movement filter = for each pivot pair, the absolute price change divided by the absolute previous price, expressed as a percentage, must be at least the minimum price change; the absolute oscillator change divided by the absolute previous oscillator value, expressed as a percentage, must be at least the minimum oscillator change. A nonpositive minimum disables its filter. If the previous price is zero, the enabled price filter fails. If the previous oscillator value is zero, the enabled oscillator filter passes only if the oscillator changed.
Long: A price-low or oscillator-low pivot pair is confirmed AND the movement filter passes AND the newer low < the previous low AND the newer oscillator value > the previous oscillator value.
Long: A price-low or oscillator-low pivot pair is confirmed AND the movement filter passes AND the newer low > the previous low AND the newer oscillator value < the previous oscillator value.
Short: A price-high or oscillator-high pivot pair is confirmed AND the movement filter passes AND the newer high > the previous high AND the newer oscillator value < the previous oscillator value.
