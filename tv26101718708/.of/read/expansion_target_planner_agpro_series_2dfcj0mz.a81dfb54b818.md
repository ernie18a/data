Calculation: Prior range high and low = highest high and lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Expansion plan = on a confirmed expansion, lock its side, prior range edge, ATR, target band and invalidation shelf. The target distance is the average of the prior range width, floored at ATR, times the range projection multiplier and ATR times the ATR projection multiplier, clamped between the minimum and maximum target distances in ATR units. The target midpoint is the prior range high plus the target distance for a long plan, or the prior range low minus the target distance for a short plan. The band extends half the ATR-based band width above and below the midpoint. The shelf is the lowest low for a long plan or highest high for a short plan over the shelf lookback, excluding the current bar. A plan resolves when its target or invalidation condition is met, or when its age exceeds the expiration lookback; invalidation takes precedence over target, and target over expiration. Clear a resolved plan when its resolved age exceeds the resolved display lookback. A new confirmed expansion replaces a plan only when the plan cooldown has elapsed and there is no active plan, the plan is resolved, or the expansion is in the opposite direction. Evaluate replacement before resolution on each bar.
Long: No active plan AND (close > prior range high + ATR times the confirmation buffer OR (close >= prior range low - ATR times the confirmation buffer AND close >= slow EMA)).
Long: Close > prior range high + ATR times the confirmation buffer AND the plan cooldown has elapsed AND (no active plan OR the active plan is resolved OR the active plan is short).
Short: No active plan AND (close < prior range low - ATR times the confirmation buffer OR (close <= prior range high + ATR times the confirmation buffer AND close < slow EMA)).
Short: Close < prior range low - ATR times the confirmation buffer AND the plan cooldown has elapsed AND (no active plan OR the active plan is resolved OR the active plan is long).
Exit: long, active unresolved long plan AND (close < locked shelf - locked ATR times the violation buffer OR close >= locked target band bottom).
Exit: short, active unresolved short plan AND (close > locked shelf + locked ATR times the violation buffer OR close <= locked target band top).
