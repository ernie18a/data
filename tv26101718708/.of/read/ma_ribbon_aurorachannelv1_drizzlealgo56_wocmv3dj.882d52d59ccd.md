Calculation: Ribbon midline = mean of enabled moving averages; each has a selectable OHLC-derived source, type and length. If none is enabled, use close.
Calculation: Smoothed width = EMA of the maximum absolute value of 2 × (source − moving average) across enabled ribbon averages, treating disabled or unavailable values as zero.
Calculation: Volume boost = 1 + sensitivity × max(0, volume / SMA(volume) − 1); use a volume ratio of 1 when the volume SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Upper and lower asymmetry = 1 + asymmetry strength × RMA of the respective upper or lower candle wick divided by max(ATR, minimum price increment). Upper wick = max(0, high − max(open, close)); lower wick = max(0, min(open, close) − low). The wick RMAs and ATR use the same lookback; the minimum price increment is a parameter.
Calculation: Upper and lower envelope = EMA of the respective smoothed core boundary plus or minus smoothed width × envelope multiplier × volume boost × respective asymmetry / 2. The upper and lower core boundaries are EMAs of ribbon midline plus or minus smoothed width × core multiplier × volume boost × respective asymmetry / 2. All boundary EMAs use the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Up and down touch counts = independent counters initially zero. On each bar, increment the respective counter once if its crossover or retest condition is true, and reset its bars-since-touch counter to zero; otherwise increment its bars-since-touch counter. After updating, reset the touch count to zero if bars since touch exceeds a lookback parameter. The retest condition uses the touch count from the previous bar.
Long: close > upper envelope AND previous close <= previous upper envelope OR close > upper envelope AND previous close <= current upper envelope AND previous up touch count >= 1
Short: close < lower envelope AND previous close >= previous lower envelope OR close < lower envelope AND previous close >= current lower envelope AND previous down touch count >= 1
