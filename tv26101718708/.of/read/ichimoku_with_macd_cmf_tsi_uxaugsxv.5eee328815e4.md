Calculation: Tenkan-sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback
Calculation: Kijun-sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its longer lookback
Calculation: Senkou Span A = average of Tenkan-sen and Kijun-sen
Calculation: Senkou Span B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback
Calculation: Cloud top and bottom = maximum and minimum of Senkou Spans A and B, respectively, from the prior cloud-displacement bar
Long: Tenkan-sen > Kijun-sen AND close > close from the prior Chikou-offset bar AND close > cloud top AND MACD histogram > 0 AND Chaikin Money Flow > 0.1 AND True Strength Index > 0
Short: Tenkan-sen < Kijun-sen AND close < close from the prior Chikou-offset bar AND close < cloud bottom AND MACD histogram < 0 AND Chaikin Money Flow < -0.1 AND True Strength Index < 0
Exit: Long, short condition
Exit: Short, long condition
