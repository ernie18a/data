Calculation: Structure pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side, on the chart timeframe or an optionally selected higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar historically and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar. Record a new pivot only when the returned value is present and differs from the preceding returned value or the preceding value is absent. Retain the latest two highs and lows and the estimated chart-bar location of each latest pivot, obtained by subtracting the pivot lookback multiplied by the structure-to-chart timeframe duration ratio from the detection bar index.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish when the latest pivot high and low are both higher than their respective preceding pivots; bearish when both are lower; otherwise neutral. Recalculate each bar once two highs and two lows exist. If invalidation is enabled, reset bullish bias to neutral when close is below the lower of the latest high and low, and bearish bias to neutral when close is above their higher value.
Calculation: Retracement zone = use the higher and lower of the latest pivot high and low as the endpoints. The leg is upward if the latest high's estimated location is later than the latest low's; otherwise it is downward. For Fibonacci mode, subtract each of two retracement fractions times the endpoint range from the higher endpoint for an upward leg, or add them to the lower endpoint for a downward leg; sort the two results into zone bounds. For ATR mode, use the midpoint of those fractions to calculate the center in the same way, then set the bounds to the center plus and minus a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation candle = (close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close) OR (high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= rejection threshold AND close > open).
Calculation: Bearish confirmation candle = (close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close) OR (high > low AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= rejection threshold AND close < open).
Calculation: Signed volume sum = sum over a lookback of volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Gap zones = when gap tracking is enabled, create a bullish zone from high two bars ago to current low if their difference exceeds a multiple of current chart-timeframe ATR; create a bearish zone from current high to low two bars ago if their difference exceeds the same threshold. Keep only the newest configurable maximum number of zones. A fresh bullish zone permanently becomes bearish and inverted when close < its lower bound; a fresh bearish zone permanently becomes bullish and inverted when close > its upper bound. Count each transition of close from outside to inside the inclusive bounds as a touch, including when the zone is inactive; remove the zone after a configurable number of touches. A fresh zone is active when fresh zones are enabled, and an inverted zone is active when inverted zones are enabled.
Long: Structure bias = bullish AND close >= retracement zone lower bound AND close <= retracement zone upper bound AND (higher-timeframe alignment is not required OR higher-timeframe shorter EMA of close > higher-timeframe longer EMA of close) AND (volume alignment is not required OR (signed volume sum > 0 AND volume > a multiple of its moving average)) AND (confirmation is disabled OR bullish confirmation candle).
Short: Structure bias = bearish AND close >= retracement zone lower bound AND close <= retracement zone upper bound AND (higher-timeframe alignment is not required OR higher-timeframe shorter EMA of close <= higher-timeframe longer EMA of close) AND (volume alignment is not required OR (signed volume sum < 0 AND volume > a multiple of its moving average)) AND (confirmation is disabled OR bearish confirmation candle).
Long: Structure bias = bullish.
