Calculation: Swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the configured number of bars on each side; use the structure timeframe, lower than the chart timeframe, and its last completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish retracement zone = the 61.8%–78.6% retracement of the latest swing leg, measured down from its high for an upswing and up from its low for a downswing.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation candle = a bullish engulfing candle OR a bullish candle whose close is at least the configured fraction of its high-low range above its low.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation candle = a bearish engulfing candle OR a bearish candle whose close is at least the configured fraction of its high-low range below its high.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA above slow EMA for bullish trend, otherwise bearish trend; use the higher timeframe's bar still forming.
Calculation: Volume alignment = volume exceeds its moving average times the configured threshold AND the rolling sum of signed volume is positive for bullish alignment or negative for bearish alignment; signed volume is volume on up-close bars, negative volume on down-close bars, and zero on unchanged bars.
Long: latest two swing highs and latest two swing lows are each rising OR (close is within the bullish retracement zone AND latest two swing highs and lows are each rising AND close is not below the latest swing leg's low AND (bullish confirmation candle if required) AND (higher-timeframe trend is bullish if required) AND (volume alignment is bullish if required))
Short: latest two swing highs and latest two swing lows are each falling OR (close is within the bearish retracement zone AND latest two swing highs and lows are each falling AND close is not above the latest swing leg's high AND (bearish confirmation candle if required) AND (higher-timeframe trend is bearish if required) AND (volume alignment is bearish if required))
