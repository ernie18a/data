Calculation: Structure = confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks; retain the latest pivot of each kind and reset its broken flag on confirmation. A bullish break occurs when close exceeds the latest unbroken pivot high; a bearish break occurs when close falls below the latest unbroken pivot low. Mark each broken pivot as broken and set the structure regime to the break direction, initially neutral. Process bullish before bearish breaks.
Calculation: Zones = optionally create order blocks and fair value gaps. On a structure break whose candle body is at least an ATR multiple, an order block spans the body or full range, as configured, of the most recent opposite-direction candle within the origin lookback; require its height to exceed the minimum price increment. Its mass is the breaking body divided by ATR times (0.5 + the breaking bar's volume percentile / 100). A bullish fair value gap spans the high two bars ago to the current low; a bearish gap spans the current high to the low two bars ago. Require gap height to be at least an ATR multiple and, optionally, the preceding candle body to be at least another current-ATR multiple. Its mass is gap height divided by current ATR times (0.5 + the preceding bar's volume percentile / 100). A zone's birth is its creation bar. Create order blocks before gaps and cap each side by discarding the oldest after each creation. Before evaluating signals, remove zones older than the maximum age, bullish zones when close is below their bottom, and bearish zones when close is above their top. A surviving zone is first touched when born before the current bar and low <= its top for a bullish zone or high >= its bottom for a bearish zone. Touching or using a zone does not remove it.
Calculation: Gravity bias = for each surviving zone, effective mass is birth mass times 0.5 raised to (age / half-life); pull is effective mass divided by ((absolute distance from close to zone midpoint / ATR)^2 + softening^2). Sum pulls separately by side; bias is 100 times (bullish sum - bearish sum) / (bullish sum + bearish sum), or zero when the total is zero. Use a positive minimum price increment as the ATR floor.
Calculation: Trend state = compute signed efficiency ratio as (close - close at the efficiency lookback) / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback, or zero if the denominator is zero. Compute Hull slope as (current Hull moving average - its value at the slope lookback) / (ATR times slope lookback), clamped to [-1, 1]. Trend score is 100 times the weighted mean of structure regime, signed efficiency ratio, and Hull slope. Initially neutral, switch to long when state <= 0 and score > the positive entry threshold; otherwise switch to short when state >= 0 and score < the negative entry threshold; otherwise switch to neutral when state is nonzero and absolute score < the exit threshold. Retain state otherwise.
Calculation: Trigger zone = among surviving unused zones born before the current bar, select the greatest effective-mass bullish zone with low <= its top AND close >= its top, or bearish zone with high >= its bottom AND close <= its bottom; resolve equal masses in favor of the newest zone. A selected zone is fresh when its birth is after the most recent primary or add entry bar.
Calculation: Signal quality = 100 * (0.35 * trend alignment + 0.25 * gravity alignment + 0.20 * mass rank + 0.20 * rejection). Trend alignment is 1 for the corresponding trend state, 0.4 for neutral, and 0 otherwise. Gravity alignment is the corresponding signed gravity bias / 100 clamped to [0, 1]. Mass rank is the trigger zone's effective mass divided by the greatest effective mass among all surviving zones of that side, clamped to [0, 1], or zero if that maximum is zero. Rejection is (close - low) / bar range for long and (high - close) / bar range for short; floor bar range at the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Signal leg = initially flat. A primary signal sets its direction, resets its add count, and records the signal and entry bar; an add increments the count and records both bars. Mark the selected zone used on either signal. An exit sets the leg flat and resets the add count. Evaluate long signals, then short signals using the updated leg, then exits; calculate the cooldown once before both signal evaluations. Exits do not update the signal or entry bar.
Long: Newly confirmed pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high OR newly confirmed pivot low > previous confirmed pivot low.
Short: Newly confirmed pivot high <= previous confirmed pivot high OR newly confirmed pivot low <= previous confirmed pivot low.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Bullish zone created OR surviving bullish zone first touched.
Short: Bearish zone created OR surviving bearish zone first touched.
Long: Trend state = long OR gravity bias > positive dashboard threshold.
Short: Trend state = short OR gravity bias < negative dashboard threshold.
Long: Bullish trigger zone exists AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND (gravity filter disabled OR gravity bias > 0) AND ((signal leg <= flat AND trend state = long AND long quality >= primary threshold) OR (signal leg = long AND trend state >= neutral AND add count < maximum AND trigger zone is fresh AND long quality >= add threshold)).
Short: Bearish trigger zone exists AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND (gravity filter disabled OR gravity bias < 0) AND ((signal leg >= flat AND trend state = short AND short quality >= primary threshold) OR (signal leg = short AND trend state <= neutral AND add count < maximum AND trigger zone is fresh AND short quality >= add threshold)).
Exit: long, trend state = short OR bearish structure break.
Exit: short, trend state = long OR bullish structure break.
