Calculation: Flow and momentum = On each chart bar, divide close change by ATR, using true range if ATR is unavailable and close × 0.001 if true range is also unavailable; floor the divisor at 1e-8. Multiply by volume divided by its moving average, floored at 1e-8; use relative volume 1 when unavailable and impulse 0 when unavailable. Smooth the impulse with separate EMAs for flow and momentum. For each EMA, divide by 2.5 times its own rolling standard deviation floored at 1e-8, clamp to [-1, 1], and multiply by 50. The volume average and flow EMA share a length; momentum has a separate length. Both standard deviations share a lookback.
Calculation: Fade edge = Maintain wait and fade Q-values for each combination of flow regime and momentum slope. Flow regime is above the positive overflow threshold, below its negative, or between them; momentum slope is rising when momentum exceeds its previous value, otherwise not rising. Initialize wait values to 0 and fade values to 0.5. On each closed bar, update the previous bar's greedy action in its state: Q ← Q + learning rate × (reward + discount × maximum Q in current state − Q). Wait reward is 0; fade reward is negative ATR-normalized close change if the previous state had positive overflow, positive ATR-normalized close change if it had negative overflow, and negative penalty × absolute ATR-normalized close change otherwise. Use 0 for unavailable close change. After updating, fade edge is current-state fade Q minus wait Q; choose fade for the next update when edge > 0, otherwise wait.
Calculation: Divergence reliability = Confirm flow pivot lows and highs using left and right lookbacks; compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, using the price low or high at each pivot. Maintain four Beta buckets by bullish or bearish divergence and whether the new pivot flow is beyond the corresponding overflow threshold. Initialize each bucket's successes and failures to 1; reliability is successes divided by their sum. Enqueue every divergence, including those below the reliability threshold, with its pivot close and pivot bar. On each closed bar, after detecting divergences and reading their reliability, evaluate pending divergences whose age from the pivot is at least the outcome horizon: add a success if current close is above the pivot close for bullish divergence or below it for bearish divergence, otherwise add a failure, then remove it. If both divergence types occur on the same bar, process bullish first and bearish second, and use the bearish bucket's pre-update reliability for both decisions.
Long: Previous flow < negative overflow threshold AND current flow > previous flow AND fade edge > 0 AND bar index > warmup.
Short: Previous flow > positive overflow threshold AND current flow < previous flow AND fade edge > 0 AND bar index > warmup.
Long: A flow pivot low is confirmed AND its flow > the preceding flow pivot low AND its price low < the price low at the preceding flow pivot low AND divergence reliability >= reliability threshold.
Short: A flow pivot high is confirmed AND its flow < the preceding flow pivot high AND its price high > the price high at the preceding flow pivot high AND divergence reliability >= reliability threshold.
