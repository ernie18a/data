Calculation: Reference candle = each completed analysis-timeframe candle; the analysis timeframe is the chart timeframe or the configured higher timeframe. Each reference candle can confirm at most one model.
Calculation: Confirmation candle = an analysis-timeframe candle evaluated only after it closes; when the analysis timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, use its last completed candle.
Calculation: ATR = average true range using the configured lookback, calculated on the analysis timeframe for manipulation and close thresholds, and on the chart timeframe for the optional gap filter.
Calculation: Manipulation threshold = ATR × configured minimum manipulation percentage.
Calculation: Close threshold = ATR × configured minimum close-beyond-reference percentage.
Calculation: M2 long levels = target 1 at confirmation high, target 2 at reference high, stop at confirmation low; M2 short levels = target 1 at confirmation low, target 2 at reference low, stop at confirmation high.
Calculation: M3 long levels = target at confirmation high, stop at confirmation low; M3 short levels = target at confirmation low, stop at confirmation high.
Calculation: M3+ long levels = target at confirmation high, stop at reference low; M3+ short levels = target at confirmation low, stop at reference high.
Long: An unconsumed reference candle's low is swept by the current chart bar before its high is swept; OR a confirmation candle sweeps both reference extremes, closes above the reference high by at least the close threshold, and closes above its open; OR it sweeps only the reference low by at least the manipulation threshold and closes below the reference low by at least the close threshold; OR it sweeps only the reference high by at least the manipulation threshold and closes at or above the reference low but not above the reference high; AND (session filter is off OR the bar is within the configured session) AND (gap filter is off OR abs(current chart open − previous chart close) < configured gap percentage × chart-timeframe ATR).
Short: An unconsumed reference candle's high is swept by the current chart bar before its low is swept; OR a confirmation candle sweeps both reference extremes, closes below the reference low by at least the close threshold, and closes below its open; OR it sweeps only the reference high by at least the manipulation threshold and closes above the reference high by at least the close threshold; OR it sweeps only the reference low by at least the manipulation threshold and closes at or below the reference high but not below the reference low; OR it sweeps both reference extremes, closes within the reference range, and closes at or below its open; AND (session filter is off OR the bar is within the configured session) AND (gap filter is off OR abs(current chart open − previous chart close) < configured gap percentage × chart-timeframe ATR).
Exit: Long, price reaches the applicable stop or final target; for M2, reaching target 1 advances the active target to target 2, and reaching target 2 or the stop exits.
Exit: Short, price reaches the applicable stop or final target; for M2, reaching target 1 advances the active target to target 2, and reaching target 2 or the stop exits.
