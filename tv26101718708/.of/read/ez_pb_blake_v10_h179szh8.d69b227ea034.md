Calculation: Bullish FVG = a three-bar gap where the current bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; zone is between those prices. Bearish FVG = current high is below the low two bars earlier; zone is between those prices. An FVG remains active until a bar closes through its far edge.
Calculation: HTF bias = on each of four higher timeframes, classify the latest bullish and bearish FVGs: a gap that has been touched and not invalidated contributes evidence for its direction; a gap invalidated by a close through its far edge contributes evidence for the opposite direction. Each of the two longest timeframes has weight 3, the next has weight 2, and the shortest has weight 1. Sum bullish evidence positively and bearish evidence negatively; bias is bullish or bearish when the score reaches the configured threshold in that direction, otherwise neutral. Use the currently available higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish key zones = active FVGs; ITL/ITH levels formed by a confirmed pivot low/high inside the corresponding active FVG, then swept and reclaimed by a bar’s low/high and close; CISD levels formed when price trades into the aligned FVG or ITL/ITH and a later close crosses the open of the oldest consecutive opposing candle in the configured series length; and rejection blocks formed when a bullish/bearish candle wicks into an aligned FVG or ITL/ITH zone and closes in its direction. Expand ITL/ITH and CISD levels by the configured ATR-based width. Deactivate each level when a close crosses its invalidation boundary. On each enabled key timeframe, choose the nearest eligible zone in the bias direction; across timeframes choose the zone with the smallest distance from close, breaking ties in favor of the higher timeframe. Key timeframes may be above or below the chart timeframe; use the currently available aggregated bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Manipulation leg = from the chart bar with the highest high (bullish setup) or lowest low (bearish setup) in the configured lookback before key touch through the touch bar. Select the highest-timeframe enabled opposing FVG among the lower execution timeframes whose creation time is within that leg; if configured, require that gap to remain uninverted at key touch. Confirm entries using the selected timeframe’s last completed bar.
Long: HTF bias is bullish AND the selected bullish key zone is touched AND a bearish FVG in the manipulation leg is selected AND its last completed lower-timeframe bar closes above the FVG’s upper edge AND the setup key has not been invalidated by a close below its lower edge AND the bar is within the configured session AND the session signal count is below its configured maximum.
Short: HTF bias is bearish AND the selected bearish key zone is touched AND a bullish FVG in the manipulation leg is selected AND its last completed lower-timeframe bar closes below the FVG’s lower edge AND the setup key has not been invalidated by a close above its upper edge AND the bar is within the configured session AND the session signal count is below its configured maximum.
