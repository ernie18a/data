Calculation: Candle buy share = clamp(0.17 + 0.43 × (close − low) / range + 0.20 × abs(close − open) / range + 0.20 × indicator(close >= open), 0.05, 0.95), where range = max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Developing profile = divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high of the most recent min(session bar count, profile lookback) chart bars into equal rows, with range floored at the minimum price increment. During the active session, assign each bar’s volume and estimated buy volume, volume × candle buy share, to the row containing its typical price; sell volume is the remainder. Outside the session, row volumes are zero, while the range uses the same rolling bar count as at session end. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume row, choosing the lowest row on a tie. Starting at the POC row, add the larger-volume adjacent row, choosing the lower row on a tie, until the selected volume reaches the value-area fraction of total volume or all rows are included. VAL and VAH are the lower and upper edges of the selected rows.
Calculation: Session VWAP and deviation = volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of typical price since the active session began; retain them outside the session. Outer bands = session VWAP plus or minus deviation multiplied by the outer-band parameter.
Calculation: Shelf powers = for each profile row, signed power = 2 × (buy volume / row volume − 0.5) × row volume / maximum row volume, using zero for an empty row. Bid shelf power is the greatest positive signed power or zero; ask shelf power is the greatest negated signed power or zero. Profile skew = (total buy volume − total sell volume) / total volume, or zero when total volume is zero.
Calculation: Estimated CVD = cumulative volume × (2 × candle buy share − 1), reset to the current bar’s delta at the start of each session and accumulated on all other bars. CVD impulse = (shorter EMA of CVD − longer EMA of CVD) / max(abs(longer EMA of CVD), current volume), or zero when the longer EMA is zero.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed low pivot in price and a confirmed low pivot in CVD on the same pivot bar, with price lower and CVD higher than their previous jointly confirmed low pivots. Bearish divergence = a confirmed high pivot in price and a confirmed high pivot in CVD on the same pivot bar, with price higher and CVD lower than their previous jointly confirmed high pivots. Evaluate on the confirmation bar; compare each pair only with the preceding jointly confirmed pair of the same type.
Calculation: Bid quality = clamp(32 + bid location score + clamp(34 × bid shelf power, 0, 18) + clamp(85 × (CVD impulse − impulse threshold), 0, 20) + 14 × indicator(bullish divergence) + compression score + 18 × max(profile skew, 0), 0, 100). Ask quality = clamp(32 + ask location score + clamp(34 × ask shelf power, 0, 18) + clamp(85 × (−CVD impulse − impulse threshold), 0, 20) + 14 × indicator(bearish divergence) + compression score + 18 × max(−profile skew, 0), 0, 100). Bid location score is 18 if close > session VWAP, otherwise 10 if close > POC, otherwise 0; ask location score is 18 if close < session VWAP, otherwise 10 if close < POC, otherwise 0. Compression score is 8 if (VAH − VAL) / ATR <= 2.6, otherwise 4 if <= 4, otherwise 0; use zero for the ratio when ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Auction biases = bid bias clamp(50 + 55 × profile skew + 8 × VWAP distance, 0, 100) and ask bias clamp(50 − 55 × profile skew − 8 × VWAP distance, 0, 100), where VWAP distance is clamp((close − session VWAP) / ATR, −2, 2), or zero when unavailable.
Long: Active session AND close > session VWAP AND close >= VAL AND close <= upper outer VWAP band AND shorter CVD EMA > longer CVD EMA AND CVD impulse > impulse threshold AND bid quality >= quality gate AND bid quality > ask quality AND (close > current POC AND previous close <= current POC OR low <= VAL + max(ATR × tolerance multiplier, minimum price increment × tick tolerance) AND close > VAL AND close > open OR bullish divergence OR bid shelf power > shelf gate) AND bars since the last such long or short signal > cooldown, or no prior signal exists.
Short: Active session AND close < session VWAP AND close <= VAH AND close >= lower outer VWAP band AND shorter CVD EMA < longer CVD EMA AND CVD impulse < −impulse threshold AND ask quality >= quality gate AND ask quality > bid quality AND (close < current POC AND previous close >= current POC OR high >= VAH − max(ATR × tolerance multiplier, minimum price increment × tick tolerance) AND close < VAH AND close < open OR bearish divergence OR ask shelf power > shelf gate) AND bars since the last such long or short signal > cooldown, or no prior signal exists.
Long: Bullish divergence OR bid auction bias > ask auction bias OR bid quality > ask quality OR CVD impulse > 0 OR bid shelf power > ask shelf power.
Short: Bearish divergence OR ask auction bias > bid auction bias OR bid quality <= ask quality OR CVD impulse <= 0 OR bid shelf power <= ask shelf power.
Long: Bid auction bias >= candle-bias threshold AND no current long or short absorption signal.
Short: Ask auction bias >= candle-bias threshold AND bid auction bias < candle-bias threshold AND no current long or short absorption signal.
Exit: long, bearish divergence OR close < session VWAP OR close < POC.
Exit: short, bullish divergence OR close > session VWAP OR close > POC.
