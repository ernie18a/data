Calculation: Previous-day VAH = previous completed daily bar high − 15% × (high − low); use the higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Previous-day VAL = previous completed daily bar low + 15% × (high − low); use the higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Macro VAH = highest close in the most recent up to 5,000 chart-timeframe bars − 20% × (highest close − lowest close) over those bars.
Calculation: Macro VAL = lowest close in the most recent up to 5,000 chart-timeframe bars + 20% × (highest close − lowest close) over those bars.
Long: close > Macro VAH AND close > Previous-day VAH
Short: close < Macro VAL AND close < Previous-day VAL
