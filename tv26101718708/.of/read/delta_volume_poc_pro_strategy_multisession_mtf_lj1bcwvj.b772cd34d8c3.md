Calculation: Bullish SFP = on the signal-analysis timeframe (same as, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe), the current bar’s low is below the lowest low of the prior 10 bars AND its close is above that lowest low; use the current signal-analysis bar and act only when the chart bar is confirmed
Calculation: Bearish SFP = on the signal-analysis timeframe (same as, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe), the current bar’s high is above the highest high of the prior 10 bars AND its close is below that highest high; use the current signal-analysis bar and act only when the chart bar is confirmed
Long: Bullish SFP AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(200)) AND (session filter disabled OR current time is within an enabled trading session)
Short: Bearish SFP AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(200)) AND (session filter disabled OR current time is within an enabled trading session)
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR (trailing stop enabled AND high has reached entry price × (1 + trailing activation percentage) AND low <= highest high since activation − entry price × trailing offset percentage)
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR (trailing stop enabled AND low has reached entry price × (1 − trailing activation percentage) AND high >= lowest low since activation + entry price × trailing offset percentage)
