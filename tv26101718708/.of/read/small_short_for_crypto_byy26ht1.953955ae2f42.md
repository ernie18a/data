Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the source over the channel length; EMA of its absolute deviation from that EMA over the same length; divide the deviation by the deviation EMA times a scaling factor, then apply an EMA over the average length. The slow WaveTrend is an SMA of that result over a separate smoothing length.
Calculation: Signal close = close of the bar on which the short entry condition was met.
Short: Fast WaveTrend crosses below slow WaveTrend AND slow WaveTrend is at or above its bearish threshold AND the Heikin-Ashi close is at or below the Heikin-Ashi open on each of two higher timeframes, using their last completed bars, with one timeframe longer than the other AND ATR is available AND position is flat AND the current chart bar is complete AND chart close is below the last completed higher-timeframe SMA of close OR the previous chart close was below its corresponding higher-timeframe SMA AND the external market's close is below its SMA OR its previous close was below its SMA.
Exit: short, chart close is above the last completed higher-timeframe SMA of close OR the previous chart close was above its corresponding higher-timeframe SMA, if the SMA exit is enabled.
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds signal close multiplied by one plus the stop-loss percentage OR low reaches or falls below signal close multiplied by one minus the take-profit percentage.
Calculation: The external market series required by the short condition is not present in the specified backtest data, so the short decisions cannot be reproduced from those fields alone.
