Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low with the same number of bars of left and right context; it becomes available after the right-context bars have completed. Use the pivot bar’s volume and volume SMA for pivot-volume comparisons.
Calculation: Qualified downtrend = the latest confirmed pivot high is below the preceding pivot high AND the latest confirmed pivot low is below the preceding pivot low AND the rolling highest high minus current low exceeds an ATR multiple AND the rolling high exceeds the rolling low AND (current low minus rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high minus rolling lowest low) is below a threshold. Use the same lookback for the rolling high and low.
Calculation: Accumulation candidates = maintain a selling-climax low, its preceding selling-climax low as preliminary support, and an automatic-rally high. Clear all candidates when the age from the first climax pivot exceeds the candidate-age limit OR close exceeds the climax low by a percentage threshold. While the downtrend is qualified, replace the climax with a newly confirmed lower pivot low whose pivot-bar volume exceeds an elevated-volume SMA multiple OR a climactic-volume SMA multiple; move the previous climax to preliminary support and clear the rally. After a climax exists, retain the highest confirmed pivot high whose pivot bar follows the climax pivot and whose price is below the climax price times a maximum-distance multiplier. Candidate age starts at the first climax pivot and is not restarted when the climax is replaced. Update candidates even while a schematic is locked.
Calculation: Bullish structural break = no schematic is locked AND both climax and rally candidates exist AND close is above the current rally candidate AND previous close is at or below that same candidate AND close is below the candidate times an upper-bound multiplier. On the break, lock the candidate prices and pivot bars, set range height to rally minus climax, clear the candidates, and clear the post-break event states.
Calculation: Schematic lifetime = process post-break events while bars since the structural break are at most the timeout. After processing events on each bar, unlock and clear the schematic if the timeout is exceeded before a sign of strength OR close is below the climax before a sign of strength OR, after a sign of strength, the timeout is exceeded AND close is above the rally plus a multiple of range height. Suppress event markings on a bar that unlocks the schematic.
Calculation: Secondary test = the first confirmed pivot low within the schematic lifetime whose pivot bar follows the structural break, whose price is at least the climax times a lower-bound multiplier and at most the climax plus a fraction of range height, and whose pivot-bar volume is below a weak-volume SMA multiple OR is not above the climactic-volume SMA multiple.
Calculation: Spring = the first bar within the schematic lifetime whose low is below the climax AND close is above the climax AND whose distance in bars from the structural break exceeds the greater of a minimum delay and the rounded product of the pivot lookback and a delay fraction.
Calculation: Last point of support = the first confirmed pivot low within the schematic lifetime whose pivot bar follows the spring and whose price is above the secondary-test price if one exists, otherwise above the climax.
Calculation: Sign of strength = the first bar within the schematic lifetime after a last point of support exists on which close is above the rally AND volume is above the climactic-volume SMA multiple. A newly confirmed last point of support can satisfy this condition on the same bar.
Calculation: Spring follow-through levels = on a spring, if no earlier spring follow-through is open, set the target to the locked rally and the stop to the spring low minus an ATR multiple. Check the stop before the target on each bar, including the spring bar; close the follow-through when either is reached or its age exceeds the timeout. ATR uses true range as a fallback when unavailable and a positive minimum floor.
Short: Bullish structural break AND a climax candidate exists; evaluate on the break bar, using the climax pivot’s price.
Short: Bullish structural break AND a preliminary-support candidate exists; evaluate on the break bar, using the preliminary-support pivot’s price.
Long: Bullish structural break.
Long: Spring AND the schematic remains locked after the bar’s expiry check.
Long: Last point of support AND the schematic remains locked after the bar’s expiry check; evaluate on the pivot-confirmation bar.
Long: Sign of strength AND the schematic remains locked after the bar’s expiry check.
Exit: long, spring follow-through is open AND close is below its stop.
Exit: long, spring follow-through is open AND close is at or above its stop AND high is at or above its target.
