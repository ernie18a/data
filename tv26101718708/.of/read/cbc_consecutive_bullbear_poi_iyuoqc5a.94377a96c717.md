Long: On a chart bar close, for either the chart timeframe or independently for each of two higher timeframes, the last completed bar and the preceding bar both have close > open AND the last completed bar has close > preceding close AND low >= preceding low AND high > preceding high. For a higher timeframe, evaluate only when its last completed bar changes.
