Calculation: SessionVWAP = within each day, cumulative sum of ((high+low+close)/3 * volume) divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each day
Calculation: TrendEMA = EMA of close; Slope = TrendEMA now minus TrendEMA a few bars ago
Calculation: SlowEMA = EMA of close with a longer period than TrendEMA
Calculation: Near(bar) = |close - SessionVWAP| / close <= pullback threshold, evaluated on that bar
Long: no open position AND bar time inside the session window AND entries so far today < daily cap AND close > SessionVWAP AND Slope > 0 AND Slope > small fixed fraction of ATR AND close > SlowEMA AND Near is true on the current bar or any of the previous few bars AND close > previous bar high AND (optional filter: (min(open,close) - low)/(high - low) >= wick fraction AND |close - open|/(high - low) >= body fraction AND close > open) AND (optional filter: ADX >= floor)
Short: no open position AND bar time inside the session window AND entries so far today < daily cap AND close < SessionVWAP AND Slope < 0 AND -Slope > small fixed fraction of ATR AND close < SlowEMA AND Near is true on the current bar or any of the previous few bars AND close < previous bar low AND (optional filter: (high - max(open,close))/(high - low) >= wick fraction AND |close - open|/(high - low) >= body fraction AND close < open) AND (optional filter: ADX >= floor)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop multiple * ATR(at entry) OR high >= entry price + target multiple * ATR(at entry), where target multiple > stop multiple; optionally, once high >= entry price + breakeven multiple * ATR, the stop level is raised to the entry price
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop multiple * ATR(at entry) OR low <= entry price - target multiple * ATR(at entry), where target multiple > stop multiple; optionally, once low <= entry price - breakeven multiple * ATR, the stop level is lowered to the entry price
Exit: long or short, bar time is the first bar at or after the session end time
