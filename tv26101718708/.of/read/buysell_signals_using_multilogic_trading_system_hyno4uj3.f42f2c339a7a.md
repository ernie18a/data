Calculation: Prior range = highest high and lowest low over a range lookback ending on the previous bar; width = high minus low, and width percentage = width / low × 100.
Calculation: Prior swing high and low = highest high and lowest low over a separate swing lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions: close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; RSI between its bullish trend bounds; MACD line > signal line AND histogram > 0; close > Bollinger Band basis; usable volume AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier AND OBV > its SMA; close > open AND close > prior swing high. Bearish score = count of true conditions: close < short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA; RSI between its bearish trend bounds; MACD line < signal line AND histogram < 0; close < Bollinger Band basis; usable volume AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier AND OBV < its SMA; close < open AND close < prior swing low. Usable volume requires current volume and its SMA to exist and the SMA > 0. The bearish RSI bounds are lower than the corresponding bullish bounds.
Calculation: Raw long setup = (trend engine enabled AND close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND bullish score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > EMA of higher-timeframe closes) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP)) OR (momentum engine enabled AND close > short EMA AND RSI > bullish momentum threshold AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND OBV > its SMA AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > EMA of higher-timeframe closes) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP)) OR (breakout engine enabled AND prior range width percentage <= maximum AND close > prior range high + ATR × breakout buffer AND previous close <= previous bar's prior range high AND high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= minimum breakout close position AND RSI > breakout midpoint AND MACD line > signal line AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (breakout volume requirement disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP)) OR (mean-reversion engine enabled AND prior range width percentage <= maximum AND NOT(close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA) AND NOT(close < short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA) AND prior range width >= ATR × minimum range multiplier AND low <= prior range low + prior range width × zone fraction AND close > prior range low + prior range width × zone fraction AND close > open AND high > low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum wick ratio AND RSI < bullish reversion threshold AND (RSI-turn filter disabled OR RSI > previous RSI) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier)). Higher-timeframe EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Session VWAP uses typical price and resets each session.
Calculation: Raw short setup = (trend engine enabled AND close < short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA AND bearish score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < EMA of higher-timeframe closes) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP)) OR (momentum engine enabled AND close < short EMA AND RSI < bearish momentum threshold AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND OBV < its SMA AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < EMA of higher-timeframe closes) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP)) OR (breakout engine enabled AND prior range width percentage <= maximum AND close < prior range low - ATR × breakout buffer AND previous close >= previous bar's prior range low AND high > low AND (close - low) / (high - low) <= 1 - minimum breakout close position AND RSI < breakout midpoint AND MACD line < signal line AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (breakout volume requirement disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP)) OR (mean-reversion engine enabled AND prior range width percentage <= maximum AND NOT(close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA) AND NOT(close < short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA) AND prior range width >= ATR × minimum range multiplier AND high >= prior range high - prior range width × zone fraction AND close < prior range high - prior range width × zone fraction AND close < open AND high > low AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum wick ratio AND RSI > bearish reversion threshold AND (RSI-turn filter disabled OR RSI < previous RSI) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unusable OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier)). The bearish momentum threshold is below the breakout midpoint, the bullish momentum threshold is above it, and the bullish and bearish reversion thresholds are respectively below and above their corresponding momentum thresholds.
Calculation: Entry candidates = the corresponding raw setup AND (fresh-only disabled OR previous raw setup is false) AND bar index >= max(long EMA length, range lookback + 2, Bollinger length, MACD slow length + signal length) AND (no previous signal OR bars since previous signal > cooldown) AND (wait-for-exit enabled AND no active trade awaiting a stop or target touch OR wait-for-exit disabled AND (opposite signals allowed OR position is flat or already on the candidate's side)). Evaluate stop and target touches before candidates; a touch clears the waiting state. If level clearing is enabled, a touch also clears the position; otherwise the position remains until replaced by a signal. Each accepted signal enters at its closing price and replaces the active trade.
Calculation: Stop and target = fixed at the signal bar. For a long, choose stop as prior swing low - entry-bar ATR × structure buffer, entry - entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier, or entry × (1 - stop percentage); for a short, choose prior swing high + entry-bar ATR × structure buffer, entry + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier, or entry × (1 + stop percentage). If the chosen stop is unavailable or on the wrong side of entry, use the ATR stop. Choose target as entry plus for a long or minus for a short: entry-bar ATR × target multiplier, entry × target percentage, or risk × reward ratio, where risk is the greater of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and a minimum price increment parameter.
Long: long entry candidate AND (short entry candidate is false OR bullish score > bearish score).
Short: short entry candidate AND (long entry candidate is false OR bearish score > bullish score).

Long: higher-timeframe filter enabled AND close > EMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: higher-timeframe filter enabled AND close < EMA of higher-timeframe closes using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: VWAP filter enabled AND session VWAP exists AND close > session VWAP.
Short: VWAP filter enabled AND session VWAP exists AND close <= session VWAP.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
