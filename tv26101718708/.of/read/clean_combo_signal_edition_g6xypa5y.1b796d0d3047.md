Calculation: EMA S, EMA M, and EMA L are close EMAs with short, medium, and long lengths, respectively; RSI, ADX, ATR, and volume SMA use their own lengths. A crossover means current first value > second value AND previous first value <= second value; a crossunder reverses the inequalities.
Calculation: Demand and supply zones = zones created when a pivot low or high is confirmed after an equal number of bars on each side AND pivot-bar volume > its volume SMA times a zone-volume multiplier. Demand bounds are pivot low minus a lower-width ATR multiple and pivot low plus an upper-width ATR multiple; supply bounds are pivot high minus the same upper-width multiple and pivot high plus the same lower-width multiple. Use pivot-bar ATR, with lower width < upper width. Retain at most a configured number of the newest zones per side. Before evaluating signals, delete demand zones when close < lower bound minus current ATR times an invalidation multiplier, and supply zones when close > upper bound plus the same multiple. A zone is near when close is between its bounds expanded on both sides by current ATR times a distance multiplier. Zones can be disabled.
Calculation: POC and value-area high = divide the lookback's highest-high to lowest-low range into equal price buckets; distribute each bar's volume among overlapping buckets in proportion to overlap divided by that bar's high-low range. POC is the midpoint of the first highest-volume bucket. Starting at that bucket, add buckets upward until a configured share of total bucket volume is reached or the top is reached, then add buckets downward if needed; value-area high is the upper edge of the highest included bucket. Use a configurable positive minimum price increment for zero ranges. The POC filter can be disabled.
Calculation: Regime measure = 1 minus log10(sum of true range over its lookback divided by the lookback's highest high minus lowest low) divided by log10(lookback length); floor the price range at a configurable positive minimum price increment. Regime passes when the regime filter is disabled OR regime measure >= its threshold OR ADX >= its regime threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish confirmation = short EMA > medium EMA AND RSI > its midpoint on the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe bearish confirmation reverses both comparisons. Each confirmation also passes when the higher-timeframe filter is disabled.
