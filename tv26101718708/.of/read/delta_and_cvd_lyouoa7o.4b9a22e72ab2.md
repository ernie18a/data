Calculation: Delta = chart bar volume, treating missing volume as zero, multiplied by (2 × buy fraction − 1). In intrabar mode, buy fraction is estimated buy volume divided by total volume across the available lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the bar still forming. Treat missing lower-timeframe volume as zero. For each chart bar, initialize the previous intrabar direction to zero; assign direction +1 when intrabar close > open, −1 when close < open, and otherwise the previous intrabar direction. Add all volume of a +1 intrabar and half the volume of a zero-direction intrabar to estimated buy volume; add no volume for a −1 intrabar. If total intrabar volume is zero, or in candle geometry mode, buy fraction = (close − low) / (high − low) when high > low, otherwise 0.5.
Long: Delta >= 0
Short: Delta < 0
