Calculation: Pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using configurable left and right bar counts; retain separate chronological buffers of the most recent qualifying pivots of each type. Accept only positive pivot prices when current ATR is positive. Store pivot price, bar index, volume (substitute 1 when missing or nonpositive), and upper wick for highs or lower wick for lows.
Calculation: Zones = on confirmation of either pivot type, rebuild resistance zones from high pivots and support zones from low pivots using current ATR and current volume SMA. For each side, scan pivots oldest first; each unused pivot seeds a cluster, then scan later unused pivots in order and add those within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the seed and at least the minimum bar spacing from every existing member. Mark added pivots used even if the cluster ultimately has too few members. Keep clusters with at least the minimum touches. A pivot's weight is (1 + volume-weight coefficient × pivot volume / current volume SMA + wick-weight coefficient × pivot wick / current ATR) / (1 + max(1, pivot age in bars) / decay period); use volume ratio 1 if the current volume SMA is not positive. The center is the weighted mean pivot price, strength is the sum of weights, and boundaries are center plus and minus an ATR-scaled padding. Retain the strongest configured number per side, breaking ties in cluster creation order.
Calculation: Zone state = before rebuilding on each bar, mark each existing active resistance broken when close exceeds its top and each active support broken when close falls below its bottom; record the break bar and whether the previous close was respectively at or below the resistance top or at or above the support bottom. Archive broken zones even when that previous-close test fails. On a rebuild, archive previously active unbroken zones with no same-side new center within the ATR-scaled cluster tolerance as dropped; discard new zones near an archived broken zone of the same side, using the smaller archive tolerance. Archive entries are deduplicated by side and archive tolerance: a new broken zone upgrades a matching dropped entry's broken status, break bar, and previous-close test while retaining its original boundaries; otherwise keep the existing entry. Retain a bounded chronological archive, dropping the oldest entry when full. Active zones used by signals have positive, ordered boundaries and at least the minimum touches.
Calculation: Bounce cooldown = retain a bounded chronological list of bounce signal centers and bars shared by both sides. A zone passes when every recorded center within the current ATR-scaled cluster tolerance has a signal age at least the cooldown length. Check support bounces first, then resistance bounces, scanning active zones in strength order; record only the first qualifying zone per side and discard the oldest record when full.
Long: An active support zone has low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom AND close >= open AND passes bounce cooldown.
Short: An active resistance zone has high >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND close <= open AND passes bounce cooldown.
Long: An archived broken resistance zone has break age > 0 AND break age <= retest window AND low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom AND close >= open.
Short: An archived broken support zone has break age > 0 AND break age <= retest window AND high >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND close <= open.
Long: An active support zone has low <= zone top AND close > zone bottom.
Short: An active resistance zone has high >= zone bottom AND close < zone top.
Long: A resistance zone is newly archived as broken, or upgrades a matching dropped archive entry to broken, AND close > the resistance top AND previous close <= the resistance top.
Short: A support zone is newly archived as broken, or upgrades a matching dropped archive entry to broken, AND close < the support bottom AND previous close >= the support bottom.
