Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over equal left and right lookbacks; available only after the right-side bars complete. Internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots.
Calculation: ATR = average true range. Displacement = true range ranks at or above its recent percentile threshold with body/range ≥ 0.5, OR the long-lookback efficiency ratio was low five bars ago and the short-lookback efficiency ratio is now above its value three bars ago.
Calculation: Validated bullish/bearish break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low, respectively; displacement condition holds; close is in the upper/lower portion of the bar, respectively; and pivot quality meets its threshold. Pivot quality = average of (1 − distance between the latest and prior same-side pivot / 1% of close, clamped to [0,1]) and (1 − pivot age / 200 bars, clamped to [0,1]). A bullish reversal break additionally closes above the protected swing high; a bearish reversal break closes below the protected swing low. Protected swing high/low is the latest swing high/low when a lower low/higher high pivot forms, respectively.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = high exceeds an equal-high pool and source close falls below it, OR high exceeds the latest swing high while source close falls below it and close < open; bearish-side sweep is symmetric at lows, with close > open for the swing-low case. Equal-high/low pools form when consecutive same-side swing pivots differ by no more than the ATR-scaled tolerance.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago / current high < low two bars ago, respectively, and gap size is at least the ATR-scaled minimum.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the last opposing candle within the scan lookback before a validated bullish/bearish break, respectively, whose quality meets its threshold. Quality = rounded 100 × (0.34×min(displacement ATR,2)/2 + 0.20×min(body ratio,0.8)/0.8 + 0.20×clamp((volume z-score+0.5)/2.5,0,1) + 0.26×recent same-side liquidity sweep indicator).
Long: validated bullish break OR internal close crossing above its latest confirmed pivot high OR bullish-side liquidity sweep OR bullish fair value gap OR bullish order block forms OR an untagged bullish order block is selected as the highest-scoring eligible setup with score ≥ 55.
Short: validated bearish break OR internal close crossing below its latest confirmed pivot low OR bearish-side liquidity sweep OR bearish fair value gap OR bearish order block forms OR an untagged bearish order block is selected as the highest-scoring eligible setup with score ≥ 55.
Calculation: Eligible setup order block = untagged order block with structure bias aligned to its side and close in the corresponding half of the latest swing range (bullish below midpoint, bearish above midpoint). Score = clamp(quality×0.5 + 7×count of reference levels within ATR-scaled cluster tolerance + 10 if at a high-volume node (−6 if low-volume node) + 10 if aligned with premium/discount + 8 if higher-timeframe structure bias is aligned (−4 otherwise) + 6 if chart structure bias is aligned (−4 otherwise) + 6 if reversal confirmation + 4 if aligned trending regime − 6 if volatile regime − age decay capped at 18 − 8 if tagged + analog adjustment, 0, 100). The higher-timeframe structure bias uses confirmed pivots on a higher timeframe and its last completed bar. Reference levels are the rolling volume point of control, nearest untested session points of control, equal-high/low pools, range midpoint, and latest swing high/low. Reversal confirmation is bullish divergence or RSI ≤ 30 for longs; bearish divergence or RSI ≥ 70 for shorts. Divergence compares consecutive confirmed price and RSI pivots with equal lookbacks. Analog adjustment, when at least the configured minimum same-direction resolved zones exist, is 24×(weighted historical respect rate−0.5), with weights 1/(1+distance²); distance is the sum across six context features of log(1+absolute feature difference). The selected setup is the eligible order block with the highest score.
