Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over equal numbers of preceding and subsequent bars; it becomes available after the subsequent bars have closed. Swing pivots use a longer lookback than internal pivots.
Calculation: Swing bias = initially neutral; update to bullish or bearish respectively on each accepted upward or downward swing structure break and otherwise retain its previous value. The protected high is the latest swing high when a newly confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding swing low; the protected low is the latest swing low when a newly confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding swing high.
Calculation: Swing structure break = close crosses the latest confirmed swing high upward or swing low downward, comparing both the current and previous close with the current pivot level. Optionally require displacement, a clean close, and pivot quality. If the break reverses swing bias, optionally require close beyond the corresponding protected level when that level exists. Process upward before downward breaks.
Calculation: Displacement = true range divided by ATR has a rolling percentile rank at or above a threshold AND absolute candle body divided by high minus low is at or above a threshold, OR the longer-lookback efficiency ratio a specified number of bars ago is at or below a threshold AND the shorter-lookback efficiency ratio exceeds its value a specified number of bars ago. Alternatively replace the percentile test with true range at or above a multiple of ATR. Efficiency ratio is absolute net close change divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over its lookback.
Calculation: Clean close = for an upward break, (close minus low) divided by (high minus low) is at or above a threshold; for a downward break, (high minus close) divided by (high minus low) is at or above a threshold. Use the neutral close-position value when high equals low.
Calculation: Pivot quality = the average of recency and tightness, each clamped between zero and one. Recency is one minus pivot age divided by a lookback. Tightness is one minus the absolute difference between the latest and preceding same-side swing pivots divided by a fraction of current close. If the preceding pivot is unavailable, use a neutral quality.
Calculation: Internal bias = initially neutral; on a close crossing the latest confirmed internal pivot high upward set it bullish, and on a close crossing the latest confirmed internal pivot low downward set it bearish; otherwise retain it. Compare the previous close with the current pivot level and test reversals against the previous bar's internal bias.
Calculation: Equal-high level = the higher of the latest two confirmed swing highs when their difference is at most an ATR multiple; equal-low level = the lower of the latest two confirmed swing lows under the symmetric condition. Retain each latest qualifying level until another qualifies.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low is below the equal-low level AND close is above it, OR low is below the latest swing low AND close is above that low AND close is above open. Bearish sweep = high is above the equal-high level AND close is below it, OR high is above the latest swing high AND close is below that high AND close is below open.
Calculation: Order block = after an accepted swing break, take the most recent opposite-direction candle within an origin lookback, using either its high-to-low range or its body as the zone. Its direction is the break direction. Its formation quality is a weighted sum of capped normalized break penetration in ATR units, origin body-to-range ratio, origin volume z-score, and an indicator of a recent same-direction sweep. Retain it only if quality reaches a threshold. Optionally merge overlapping same-direction blocks, taking their union, maximum quality, and latest formation time while retaining their existing tagged state. When capacity is exceeded, remove the lowest-quality block. Mark a block tagged on its first overlap with a bar; remove a bullish block when close is below its bottom and a bearish block when close is above its top.
Calculation: Setup score = for each untagged order block whose direction agrees with swing bias or neutral bias and whose direction agrees with current close being respectively below or above the midpoint of the latest swing high and low, score its formation quality, nearby reference-level count, volume-profile node, midpoint alignment, higher-timeframe bias alignment, swing-bias alignment, momentum reversal confirmation, trend alignment, and volatility clustering using adjustable weights and penalties. Reference levels are the rolling volume-profile point of control, nearest untested session points of control above and below close, retained equal-high and equal-low levels, swing-range midpoint, and latest swing high and low; count each within an ATR-multiple distance of the block midpoint. The volume profile allocates each bar's volume to the price bin containing its (high + low + close) / 3 over a rolling window; its point of control is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, and high- and low-volume nodes are determined by percentiles of nonzero bin volumes. Optionally subtract age and tagged-state decay, clamp the score, and add an analog-probability adjustment when enough same-direction resolved zones exist. Select the qualifying block with the greatest positive score, breaking ties by retained-block order.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar's confirmed pivot highs and lows; on each chart bar set bias bullish when chart close exceeds the latest higher-timeframe pivot high, bearish when it falls below the latest higher-timeframe pivot low, and otherwise retain the prior bias. Neutral bias counts as aligned with either direction.
Calculation: Momentum reversal confirmation = bullish when the latest two confirmed price pivot lows make a lower low while the latest two independently confirmed RSI pivot lows make a higher low, OR RSI is at or below its lower threshold; bearish symmetrically uses a higher price high and lower RSI high, OR RSI at or above its upper threshold.
Calculation: Trend alignment = ADX and efficiency ratio both exceed their thresholds, volatility clustering is below its threshold, and swing bias agrees with the block direction or is neutral. Volatility clustering is the rolling z-score of an exponentially decaying accumulation of absolute log close returns.
Calculation: Analog probability = for each first-tagged zone, record formation quality, swing-bias alignment, regime, efficiency ratio, regime-change score, and volume z-score. After a fixed horizon, classify it successful when close has moved from the zone midpoint in its direction by at least an ATR multiple, using ATR at evaluation. For retained resolved same-direction zones, average outcomes with weights equal to the reciprocal of one plus the square of the sum of log(1 + absolute feature differences). The regime-change score is a capped weighted sum of recent changes among trend-up, trend-down, volatile, and range states. Apply the probability adjustment only after a minimum number of analogs.
Calculation: Untested session point of control = bin each session's volume by typical price using fixed bins spanning an ATR-scaled range centered on the session-opening close. At the next session, retain the preceding session's point of control if no bar in a specified recent lookback straddled it; remove retained levels when a later bar straddles them or their age exceeds a limit.
