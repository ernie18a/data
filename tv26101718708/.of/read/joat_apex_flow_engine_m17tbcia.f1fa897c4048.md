Calculation: Efficiency = absolute change in close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; use zero when the sum is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive baseline = initialize to close whenever the previous baseline is unavailable; otherwise add to the previous baseline the product of (close minus previous baseline) and the lesser of one and the acceleration parameter times the square of (efficiency times the difference between fast and slow EMA smoothing constants plus the slow smoothing constant). The fast EMA length is shorter than the slow EMA length.
Calculation: Flow state = initially neutral; change to bullish when the previous state is neutral or bearish and close has exceeded the adaptive baseline plus an ATR multiple for the required number of consecutive bars; change to bearish when the previous state is neutral or bullish and close has been below the adaptive baseline minus the same ATR multiple for the required number of consecutive bars; otherwise retain the previous state. Update the state even when quality fails its filter.
Calculation: Directional quality = momentum points plus volume points plus body points plus efficiency points. Award momentum points when close minus close at the momentum lookback is positive for bullish quality or negative for bearish quality, otherwise zero. Volume ratio is volume divided by its SMA, or one when the SMA is not positive; award the high, middle, or zero volume points according to whether the ratio meets the higher threshold, only the lower threshold, or neither. Body ratio is absolute close minus open divided by the greater of high minus low and the minimum price increment; award high, middle, or low body points according to the corresponding higher and lower thresholds. Award high, middle, or low efficiency points using the corresponding higher and lower efficiency thresholds. Compare each threshold inclusively.
Calculation: Entry risk distance = at the entry bar, either an ATR multiple or a fraction of entry price; retain this distance for the trade. The selected take-profit multiple is that of the highest-numbered enabled target; if no target is enabled, no take-profit exit applies.
Long: Flow state = bullish.
Short: Flow state = bearish.
Long: Flow state = bullish AND previous flow state != bullish AND (quality filter disabled OR bullish quality >= minimum quality).
Short: Flow state = bearish AND previous flow state != bearish AND (quality filter disabled OR bearish quality >= minimum quality).
Exit: long, bar index > entry bar index AND (low <= entry price - entry risk distance OR high >= entry price + entry risk distance × selected take-profit multiple).
Exit: short, bar index > entry bar index AND (high >= entry price + entry risk distance OR low <= entry price - entry risk distance × selected take-profit multiple).
