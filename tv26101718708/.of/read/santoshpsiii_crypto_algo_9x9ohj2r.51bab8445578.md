Calculation: Bollinger Band Width = (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / Bollinger basis
Calculation: Bollinger Band Width moving average = simple moving average of Bollinger Band Width
Long: Bollinger Band Width > its moving average AND SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish
Short: Bollinger Band Width > its moving average AND SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish
Exit: long, SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish
Exit: short, SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish
