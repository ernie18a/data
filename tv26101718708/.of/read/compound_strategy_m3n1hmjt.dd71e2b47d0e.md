Calculation: Retained low = the most recent preceding rolling minimum of hlc3 observed when that minimum increased and the rolling maximum of high did not decrease, using the same lookback for both; update from the previous bar and then the current bar, retaining the last qualifying value.
Calculation: Composite = for each of RSI, MFI, CMO, momentum, center of gravity, ADX, balance of power, stochastic, symmetrically weighted moving average, true strength index, Williams %R, accumulation/distribution, intraday intensity index, negative volume index, positive volume index, on-balance volume, price-volume trend, and true range, form four shorter-period series and one longer-period series. Use the indicators directly for RSI, MFI, CMO, momentum, and center of gravity; for the others use EMAs of the indicator. For each series subtract the SMA of its longer-period series to form gaps. Similarly form price gaps from EMAs of close and volume gaps from EMAs of volume. For each indicator, sum the products of the standard deviations and correlation of its gap and the corresponding price gap across the four shorter periods, weighting shorter periods more heavily; repeat using volume gaps. Multiply these two sums by their rolling correlation. Composite is the sum of these products across all indicators.
Long: Short-period EMA of open crosses above short-period smoothed moving average of open AND long-period smoothed moving average of open <= intermediate-period smoothed moving average of open.
Long: Position is flat AND datetime is within the optional trading window AND low < retained low AND (short-period EMA of open crosses above intermediate-period smoothed moving average of open OR the fourth difference of composite crosses zero OR the fifth difference of composite crosses zero).
