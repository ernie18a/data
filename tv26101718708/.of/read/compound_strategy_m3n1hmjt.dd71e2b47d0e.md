Calculation: Composite graph = sum across true range, price-volume trend, on-balance volume, positive and negative volume indices, intraday intensity, accumulation/distribution, Williams %R, TSI, stochastic, balance of power, ADX, center of gravity, momentum, CMO, MFI, RSI, and symmetrically weighted moving average of (price-correlation score × volume-correlation score × correlation between the two scores). For each indicator, its price- and volume-correlation scores sum four terms: standard deviation of the indicator’s gap from its 300-period Bollinger basis × standard deviation of the corresponding price or volume gap × correlation between those gaps × weight; use the indicator’s 10-, 50-, 100-, and 200-period EMA (shortest to longest) with weights 30, 6, 3, and 1.5. The price gaps use EMA of close at the same four lookbacks minus the 300-period EMA-of-close Bollinger basis; volume gaps use EMA of volume likewise. Gap standard deviations and correlations use the same lookback. Each indicator uses its standard formula, computed from chart-timeframe bars; Bollinger basis uses a 5-period SMA. For ADX, first calculate DMI with lengths 2 and 1, then EMA the ADX. For balance of power use (close − open)/(high − low); stochastic uses close, high, and low with length 1; TSI uses lengths 1 and 2; Williams %R uses length 1; SWMA uses its fixed standard weighting.
Calculation: Signal graph = standard deviation of (composite graph − its 5-period Bollinger basis) × standard deviation of (positive-signal graph − its 5-period Bollinger basis) × correlation between those standard deviations. The standard deviations and correlation use the same lookback. Positive-signal graph = positive-signal price score × positive-signal volume score × correlation between the scores; each score is standard deviation of changes in positive value × standard deviation of the 10-period price or volume gap × correlation between these series. Positive value changes on crossovers/crossunders of the short and longer EMAs of RSI, MFI, CMO, momentum, center of gravity, ADX, balance of power, stochastic, SWMA, TSI, Williams %R, accumulation/distribution, intraday intensity, NVI, PVI, OBV, PVT, and true range; add a positive weight on crossover and subtract it on crossunder. For each pair, use the first applicable pair in this order: 10/50, 10/100, 10/200, 10/300, 50/100, 50/200, 50/300, 100/200, 100/300, 200/300; weights are 180, 90, 45, 30, 18, 9, 6, 4.5, 3, 1.5. The price and volume gaps are EMA(close) and EMA(volume) at the 10-period lookback minus the corresponding 300-period EMA Bollinger basis. All standard deviations and correlations use the same lookback.
Calculation: Signal change = signal graph − its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Composite graph change = composite graph − its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Composite graph acceleration = composite graph change − its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Composite graph third, fourth, and fifth differences = prior-bar difference of the preceding difference, successively.
Calculation: Selected average = the configured moving-average method of low using its configured lookback.
Calculation: Trend averages = recursively smoothed moving averages of open with short, medium, and long lookbacks; short < medium < long. The short trend average used in crossovers is an EMA of open using the short lookback.
Calculation: Supertrend direction = standard Supertrend direction using its configured factor and ATR lookback.
Calculation: Buy signal 1 = signal change crosses zero AND selected average has fallen on each of the last six bars.
Calculation: Buy signals 2–6 = composite graph change, acceleration, third difference, fourth difference, and fifth difference, respectively, crosses zero in either direction.
Long: Short trend EMA crosses above short-lookback SMMA AND long-lookback SMMA ≤ medium-lookback SMMA.
Long: Position is flat AND (buy signal 5 OR buy signal 6 OR short trend EMA crosses above medium-lookback SMMA) AND within the configured date window AND configured lower-point source < prior-bar low point.
Long: Long position AND (buy signal 1 OR buy signal 3 OR buy signal 5 OR buy signal 6) AND selected average < 99% of average entry price AND open trades ≤ 75% of maximum trade count AND within the configured date window AND configured lower-point source < prior-bar low point.
Long: Long position AND buy signal 1 AND selected average < average entry price AND within the configured date window AND open trades > 75% of maximum trade count AND open trades ≤ maximum trade count AND configured lower-point source < prior-bar low point.
Exit: Long, configured moving average of high > lowest recorded entry price × (1 + configured swing-profit percentage) AND intermediate selling is enabled and no open-trade count change occurred since the prior bar AND prior-bar high point and current low point exist AND average entry price > selected average.
Exit: Long, configured price source has risen for its configured lookback AND configured moving average of low < current selected high entry price × 100/(100 + configured downtrend threshold) AND downtrend selling is enabled AND selected average < 99% of average entry price.
Exit: Long, (Supertrend direction changed from up to down OR Supertrend direction is down and any buy signal 1–6 is true) AND profit line < selected average AND prior-bar high point exists AND (buy signal 2 OR buy signal 3 OR buy signal 4 OR buy signal 6) AND short trend EMA < medium-lookback SMMA, OR (short trend EMA crossed below short-lookback SMMA AND profit line < selected average AND short-lookback SMMA > current high point).
Calculation: High and low points = the prior value of the rolling highest configured high source or rolling lowest configured low source, respectively, when that rolling extreme reverses direction.
Calculation: Current selected high entry price = entry price selected from recorded open-entry prices sorted descending, indexed by the count of prior partial exits.
Calculation: Profit line = on initial entry, average entry price × (1 + configured profit percentage); on each added entry, add its corresponding target amount. When loss adjustment is enabled, retain this line after partial exits; otherwise use average entry price × (1 + configured profit percentage).
