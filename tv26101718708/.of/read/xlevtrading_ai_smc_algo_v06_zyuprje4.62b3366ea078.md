Calculation: Structure pivots and bias = Maintain separate short-lookback internal and longer-lookback swing structures on chart bars. For each lookback, a high pivot occurs when the high that many bars ago exceeds every subsequent high in the lookback and the preceding leg was bullish; a low pivot occurs when the low that many bars ago is below every subsequent low in the lookback and the preceding leg was bearish. Test the high first if both occur. Start with a bearish leg. Store the latest pivot of each kind and reset its crossed flag. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest uncrossed high pivot; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest uncrossed low pivot. Mark that pivot crossed and set that structure's bias to the break direction. For internal breaks also require the corresponding internal pivot to differ from the swing pivot; optionally require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for bullish breaks, or < for bearish breaks. A break against the preceding bias is a change of character; all other breaks are breaks of structure.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing bias = On a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar. Maintain the latest confirmed symmetric pivot high and low using the swing lookback on both sides; set bias bullish when close exceeds the latest pivot high, otherwise set it bearish when close is below the latest pivot low, and otherwise retain its previous value.
Calculation: Accepted change of character = Keep the most recent internal or swing change of character; a later one replaces any pending event. Accept it on its event bar if its bar is in the selected session, its directional wick divided by range is not above the wick threshold simultaneously with absolute body divided by range below the body threshold, its range is at least the current ATR times the minimum-range factor, and optional directional displacement passes. Either protection can be disabled. If immediate acceptance fails, optionally accept it on a later bar within the follow-through lookback when that bar is in the selected session, its close exceeds the broken level plus a current-ATR buffer and is at least the event close for a bullish event, or is below the broken level minus the buffer and at most the event close for a bearish event, and optional directional displacement passes on the confirming bar. Directional displacement requires a directional candle, range at least ATR times a factor, directional body divided by range at least a threshold, and distance from close to the directional extreme at most a fraction of range. The accepted event keeps the original direction and is dated on the acceptance bar.
Calculation: Fair value gap = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, evaluate at the start of a new source bar using the still-forming source bar's high and low, the last completed source bar's open and close, and the high and low of the source bar before that. Delta is (last completed close - open) / (open × percent divisor). The optional threshold is a factor times the cumulative absolute delta sampled at source-bar changes divided by the chart bar index; otherwise it is zero. A bullish gap requires forming low > the earlier high, last close > the earlier high, and delta > threshold. A bearish gap requires forming high < the earlier low, last close < the earlier low, and -delta > threshold. Optionally require the last completed source bar's directional body, combined with the previous chart bar's high and low and current chart ATR, to meet the range, body-ratio and close-to-extreme displacement thresholds. Reject a new gap if a retained same-direction gap has both boundaries within an ATR-based tolerance. Remove a bullish gap when chart low falls below its lower boundary and a bearish gap when chart high rises above its upper boundary, subject to the optional retention period and storage limit.
Long: A bullish internal or swing structure break occurs.
Short: A bearish internal or swing structure break occurs.
Long: A bullish change of character is accepted on the current bar.
Short: A bearish change of character is accepted on the current bar.
Long: No accepted change of character is fresh AND swing bias is bearish AND internal bias is bearish, OR no accepted change of character is fresh AND swing bias is bullish AND internal bias differs from swing bias. Optionally replace chart swing bias with higher-timeframe swing bias; freshness lasts through a specified number of chart bars after acceptance.
Short: No accepted change of character is fresh AND swing bias is bullish AND internal bias is bullish, OR no accepted change of character is fresh AND swing bias is bearish AND internal bias differs from swing bias. Optionally replace chart swing bias with higher-timeframe swing bias; freshness lasts through a specified number of chart bars after acceptance.
Long: Shorter-lookback moving average > intermediate-lookback moving average AND intermediate-lookback moving average > longer-lookback moving average.
Short: Shorter-lookback moving average < intermediate-lookback moving average AND intermediate-lookback moving average < longer-lookback moving average.
Long: Moving average > its preceding value; evaluate on chart bars or on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Short: Moving average < its preceding value; evaluate on chart bars or on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is created.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is created.
