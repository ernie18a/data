Calculation: MACD line = shorter-period EMA(close) minus longer-period EMA(close)
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of the MACD line
Long: RSI crosses above 50 OR MACD line crosses above MACD signal line AND MACD line > 0 OR MACD line crosses above MACD signal line AND MACD line < 0 OR shorter-period SMA crosses above longer-period SMA
Short: RSI crosses below 50 OR MACD line crosses below MACD signal line AND MACD line > 0 OR MACD line crosses below MACD signal line AND MACD line < 0 OR shorter-period SMA crosses below longer-period SMA
