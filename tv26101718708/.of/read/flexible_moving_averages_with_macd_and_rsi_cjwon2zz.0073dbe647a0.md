Calculation: RSI price level = close + (RSI length - 1) × (target RS × Wilder-smoothed loss - Wilder-smoothed gain), where target RS = target RSI / (100 - target RSI), and gains and losses are calculated from close changes using the RSI length.
Long: RSI of close crosses above its threshold.
Short: RSI of close crosses below its threshold.
Long: MACD line crosses above its signal line AND MACD line is above or below zero.
Short: MACD line crosses below its signal line AND MACD line is above or below zero.
Long: Shorter-lookback SMA of close crosses above longer-lookback SMA of close.
Short: Shorter-lookback SMA of close crosses below longer-lookback SMA of close.
Long: RSI of close is at or above its threshold, calculated separately on each selected timeframe. A higher timeframe uses its forming bar; a lower timeframe uses its last completed bar within the chart bar.
Short: RSI of close is below its threshold, calculated separately on each selected timeframe. A higher timeframe uses its forming bar; a lower timeframe uses its last completed bar within the chart bar.
Long: RSI of close is above its previous value on the same selected timeframe. A higher timeframe uses its forming bar; a lower timeframe uses its last completed bar within the chart bar.
Short: RSI of close is below its previous value on the same selected timeframe. A higher timeframe uses its forming bar; a lower timeframe uses its last completed bar within the chart bar.
Long: Shorter-lookback EMA of close is above longer-lookback EMA of close.
Short: Shorter-lookback EMA of close is below longer-lookback EMA of close.
Long: Close is at or above the RSI price level.
Short: Close is below the RSI price level.
