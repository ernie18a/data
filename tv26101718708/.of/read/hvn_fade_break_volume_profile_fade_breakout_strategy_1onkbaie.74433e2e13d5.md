Calculation: Rolling volume profile = At the start of each higher-timeframe period, use the chart bars in a configurable number of the most recently completed periods and hold the result fixed until the next period. Divide their combined high-low range into a configurable number of equal price rows. Distribute each chart bar's volume equally among every row from its low row through its high row, inclusive, clamping row indices to the profile bounds. The point of control is the midpoint of the highest-volume row; resolve ties toward the lowest row. Starting at that row, add the larger-volume adjacent row until a configurable fraction of total volume is included, resolving ties toward the lower-price row. Value area high is the upper edge of the lowest included row; value area low is the lower edge of the highest included row.
Calculation: High-volume-node zones = In the rolling volume profile, select rows whose volume is at least a configurable fraction of the maximum row volume and is a local maximum within a configurable row window on each side; resolve equal-volume peaks toward the lower-price row. For each peak, scan each direction until a strictly higher row or the profile boundary; prominence is peak volume minus the greater of the two directional minimum volumes. Keep peaks with prominence at least a configurable fraction of peak volume. Expand each retained peak in both directions across contiguous rows with volume at least peak volume minus a configurable fraction of prominence. The zone spans the outer edges of those rows. Retain a configurable maximum number of zones in descending peak-volume order, resolving ties toward the lower-price row.
Calculation: Untouched period points of control = At the start of each higher-timeframe period, calculate the point of control of the last completed period using the same row construction as the rolling volume profile. If tracking is enabled, add it to a list capped at 30, discarding the oldest first. Before evaluating targets on each chart bar, remove every listed point between that bar's low and high inclusive.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low or high = The most recent chart-bar pivot low or high confirmed by a configurable equal number of chart bars on each side.
Calculation: Long stop = On a long decision bar, if the breakout condition is true and breakout trading is enabled, the lesser of value area high minus ATR times a configurable multiplier and the confirmed swing low; otherwise, the lesser of low minus half that ATR distance and the confirmed swing low. If the swing low is unavailable, use the ATR-based value. Freeze the stop when submitting the entry.
Calculation: Short stop = On a short decision bar, if the breakout condition is true and breakout trading is enabled, the greater of value area low plus ATR times a configurable multiplier and the confirmed swing high; otherwise, the greater of high plus half that ATR distance and the confirmed swing high. If the swing high is unavailable, use the ATR-based value. Freeze the stop when submitting the entry.
Calculation: Long target = On a long entry decision bar, use the rolling point of control for a fade. For a breakout, use the nearest price strictly above close among retained high-volume-node zone midpoints and, if enabled, untouched period points of control; if neither exists, use close plus a configurable multiple of close minus the long stop. Freeze the target when submitting the entry.
Calculation: Short target = On a short entry decision bar, use the rolling point of control for a fade. For a breakout, use the nearest price strictly below close among retained high-volume-node zone midpoints and, if enabled, untouched period points of control; if neither exists, use close minus a configurable multiple of the short stop minus close. Freeze the target when submitting the entry.
Long: Rolling profile available AND optional flat-position restriction satisfied AND ((fade enabled AND previous low < current value area low AND close > value area low AND close > previous close AND RSI <= configurable oversold threshold) OR (breakout enabled AND close > value area high AND previous close <= previous value area high AND volume > volume SMA times a configurable multiplier)).
Short: Rolling profile available AND optional flat-position restriction satisfied AND ((fade enabled AND previous high > current value area high AND close < value area high AND close < previous close AND RSI >= configurable overbought threshold) OR (breakout enabled AND close < value area low AND previous close >= previous value area low AND volume > volume SMA times a configurable multiplier)).
Calculation: Executable long entry = Long decision AND close > long stop; submit a market entry with the frozen long stop and target. If both fade and breakout conditions hold, use breakout stop and target.
Calculation: Executable short entry = Short decision AND close < short stop; submit a market entry with the frozen short stop and target. If both fade and breakout conditions hold, use breakout stop and target.
Exit: long, low <= frozen long stop OR high >= frozen long target OR executable short entry.
Exit: short, high >= frozen short stop OR low <= frozen short target OR executable long entry.
