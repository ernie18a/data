Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar's low is lower than the lows of the preceding and following pivot-lookback bars; confirmed only after the following bars complete. Confirmed swing high = a bar's high is higher than the highs of the preceding and following pivot-lookback bars; confirmed only after the following bars complete. Both use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current-bar low > high two chart-timeframe bars earlier. Bearish gap = current-bar high < low two chart-timeframe bars earlier. Current-timeframe gaps are detected on a completed chart-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe bullish gap = low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar > high of the third-last completed higher-timeframe bar; bearish gap = high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar < low of the third-last completed higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe gaps are checked when a new higher-timeframe bar begins.
Calculation: Gap size = upper boundary - lower boundary; qualifying gap size is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple, using chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Liquidity level strength = pivot-bar volume, bars since the pivot, or touch count, according to the selected metric. Touch count increases when min(abs(bar high - level price), abs(bar low - level price)) <= chart-timeframe ATR × configured touch-distance multiple. A sell-side level is swept when high > level price (wick mode) or close > level price (close mode); a buy-side level is swept when low < level price (wick mode) or close < level price (close mode).
Calculation: Buy weight = sum of max(level strength, 1) for unswept buy-side levels formed within the configured bias lookback. Sell weight = the corresponding sum for unswept sell-side levels. Bias percentage = (sell weight - buy weight) / (sell weight + buy weight) × 100; if the total weight is zero, bias percentage = 0.
Long: A confirmed swing low forms and, if session filtering is enabled, the confirmation bar is within the configured session OR a qualifying bullish gap forms on the chart timeframe or enabled higher timeframe OR an unexpired bearish gap is inverted when close > its upper boundary OR bias percentage < -configured bias threshold.
Short: A confirmed swing high forms and, if session filtering is enabled, the confirmation bar is within the configured session OR a qualifying bearish gap forms on the chart timeframe or enabled higher timeframe OR an unexpired bullish gap is inverted when close < its lower boundary OR bias percentage > configured bias threshold.
