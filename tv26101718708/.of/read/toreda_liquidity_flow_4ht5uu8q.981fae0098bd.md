Calculation: Liquidity levels = confirmed pivot lows and highs, using equal left and right pivot lookbacks; record the pivot price, pivot-bar volume and origin bar when the pivot is confirmed. Optionally require the confirmation bar to be within the selected session. A low is a buy-side level and a high is a sell-side level. On each bar, update every unswept level before checking its sweep: strength is pivot-bar volume, bars since origin, or touches, according to the selected metric. Touches start at 1 and increase by 1 whenever min(abs(high - level price), abs(low - level price)) <= ATR multiplied by the touch-distance parameter, including on the confirmation bar. A buy-side level is swept when low < its price in wick mode or close < its price in close mode; a sell-side level is swept when high > its price in wick mode or close > its price in close mode. Swept levels do not contribute to subsequent decisions. On the latest bar, retain at most the selected maximum number of unswept levels per side, removing the oldest excess levels.
Calculation: Liquidity bias = 100 * (sell-side weight - buy-side weight) / total weight, or 0 when total weight is zero. Each unswept level whose age is within the bias lookback contributes max(strength, 1) to its side's weight, regardless of the minimum display strength.
Calculation: Liquidity histogram bands = divide the price range from the lowest to the highest unswept level into the selected number of equal bands. Assign each unswept level to its band, including the highest level in the last band, and sum max(strength, 1) in each band. A band qualifies when its strength is positive and its strength divided by the maximum band strength, multiplied by the selected maximum width and rounded to an integer, is at least 1. Bands exist only when the highest level price exceeds the lowest.
Calculation: Fair value gap = on a confirmed chart bar, a bullish gap has bottom = high two bars earlier and top = current low; a bearish gap has bottom = current high and top = low two bars earlier. Alternatively, on a change of higher-timeframe bar, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the higher-timeframe bar two bars before it in place of the current and earlier bars. A gap is created only when its top > bottom and its size >= chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by the gap-size parameter. Chart and higher-timeframe gaps can be enabled independently. Chart gaps originate two chart bars before detection; higher-timeframe gaps originate on the detection bar. Remove a gap when its chart-bar age exceeds the maximum age. An uninverted bullish gap becomes inverted when chart close < its bottom; an uninverted bearish gap becomes inverted when chart close > its top. If inversion is disabled, remove the broken gap instead. Remove an inverted bullish gap when chart close >= its bottom, and an inverted bearish gap when chart close <= its top.
Long: An unswept buy-side liquidity level has strength >= the minimum display strength.
Short: An unswept sell-side liquidity level has strength >= the minimum display strength.
Long: Liquidity bias < negative bias threshold.
Short: Liquidity bias > positive bias threshold.
Long: A qualifying liquidity histogram band's midpoint < close.
Short: A qualifying liquidity histogram band's midpoint >= close.
Long: An active uninverted bullish fair value gap exists OR an active inverted bearish fair value gap exists.
Short: An active uninverted bearish fair value gap exists OR an active inverted bullish fair value gap exists.
