Calculation: Swing state and levels = Initialize swing state to high. On each bar, if the high one lookback ago exceeds the highest high of the intervening lookback bars, set swing state to high; otherwise, if the low one lookback ago is below the lowest low of those bars, set it to low; otherwise retain the prior state. On a change to high or low, respectively, record that historical high or low as the latest swing level and mark that level uncrossed.
Calculation: Structure and sweep levels = Initialize structure to down. When close exceeds an uncrossed latest swing high, set structure to up and mark that high crossed; then, when close is below an uncrossed latest swing low, set structure to down and mark that low crossed. On a net structure change, reset both running extremes to the current high and low. Before updating the extremes with the current bar, a high sweep occurs when structure is up AND high exceeds the running high AND close is below it AND more than one bar has elapsed since that high; a low sweep occurs when structure is down AND low is below the running low AND close exceeds it AND more than one bar has elapsed since that low. Then update the running extremes and their bar indices whenever a new extreme occurs. Save the swept level as the latest level for that sweep side, replacing any previous one.
Calculation: Pending runover = If the optional session filter is disabled OR the bar is in the configured session, a close above the saved high-sweep level sets a pending long trigger to that bar's high and its stop to either the lowest low over the swing lookback ending on that bar or the lesser of that bar's low and the swept level. Clear the saved high-sweep level and pending short trigger. A close below the saved low-sweep level similarly sets a pending short trigger to that bar's low and its stop to either the highest high over the same lookback or the greater of that bar's high and the swept level; clear the saved low-sweep level and pending long trigger. Process the long setup before the short setup. Before confirmation on every bar, cancel a pending long if close is below its stop, or if low is below it when wick execution is selected; cancel a pending short if close is above its stop, or if high is above it when wick execution is selected. Confirmation clears the corresponding pending trigger and sets the active stop to its pending stop.
Long: Low sweep occurs.
Short: High sweep occurs.
Long: Pending long trigger exists AND close is above it.
Short: Pending short trigger exists AND close is below it.
Exit: long, close is below the active stop OR low is below the active stop when wick execution is selected.
Exit: short, close is above the active stop OR high is above the active stop when wick execution is selected.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = Apply the same swing, sweep, pending runover, confirmation and stop rules to higher-timeframe bars. The trend becomes long or short on the corresponding confirmation and remains so until a confirmation in the opposite direction or its active stop is hit; a stop changes it to neutral. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe trend is long.
Short: Higher-timeframe trend is short.
