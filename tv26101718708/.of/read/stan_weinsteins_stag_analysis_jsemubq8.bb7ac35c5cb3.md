Calculation: Weinstein stage = Initialize at Stage 1; retain the current stage unless a transition applies. From Stage 1, enter Stage 2 when long-term weighted moving average percentage change over a longer-term lookback exceeds the positive flat threshold and close is above the average; otherwise enter Stage 4 when the change is below the negative threshold and close is below the average. From Stage 2, enter Stage 3 when the change is within the flat threshold or is below the negative threshold while close is above the average; otherwise enter Stage 4 when the change is below the negative threshold and close is below the average. From Stage 3, enter Stage 4 when the change is below the negative threshold and close is below the average; otherwise enter Stage 2 when the change exceeds the positive threshold and close is above the average. From Stage 4, enter Stage 1 when the change is within the flat threshold or exceeds the positive threshold while close is below the average; otherwise enter Stage 2 when the change exceeds the positive threshold and close is above the average.
Calculation: Percentage change = (current weighted moving average − weighted moving average at the longer-term lookback) / weighted moving average at the longer-term lookback × 100
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over a lookback longer than the slope lookback
Calculation: Trailing stop = while in Stage 2, the highest value of (close − ATR × multiplier) since entering Stage 2; reset outside Stage 2
Long: (Weinstein stage = Stage 2 AND previous bar's stage ≠ Stage 2 AND volume > volume average × multiplier AND (macro index percentage change > positive flat threshold OR macro index filter disabled) AND (sector index percentage change > positive flat threshold OR sector filter disabled)) OR (Weinstein stage = Stage 1 AND low < lowest low over a shorter lookback of prior completed bars AND close > that lowest low)
Short: Weinstein stage = Stage 3 AND high > highest high over a shorter lookback of prior completed bars AND close < that highest high
Exit: long, Weinstein stage = Stage 2 AND close < trailing stop
