Calculation: Lunar bias = −cos(2π × lunar age / synodic month), where lunar age is the elapsed time since a known new moon modulo one synodic month, derived from bar datetime
Calculation: Bull momentum score = 24 points if close > long-term EMA, short-term EMA > medium-term EMA, and short-term EMA is rising; 8 if close > medium-term EMA; 16 if RSI ≥ configurable bullish threshold; 5 if RSI > that threshold + 4; 16 if MACD histogram > 0 and ≥ its prior-bar value; 10 if close > session VWAP; 11 if relative volume ≥ its threshold, close > open, close is in the upper 40% of the bar, and body size ≥ 0.22 ATR; 10 if the optional higher-timeframe filter is off or its close > long-term EMA and short-term EMA > medium-term EMA. Sum and cap at 100. The EMAs use progressively longer lookbacks; relative volume is volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Bear momentum score = 24 points if close < long-term EMA, short-term EMA < medium-term EMA, and short-term EMA is falling; 8 if close < medium-term EMA; 16 if RSI ≤ configurable bearish threshold; 5 if RSI < that threshold − 4; 16 if MACD histogram < 0 and ≤ its prior-bar value; 10 if close < session VWAP; 11 if relative volume ≥ its threshold, close < open, close is in the lower 40% of the bar, and body size ≥ 0.22 ATR; 10 if the optional higher-timeframe filter is off or its close < long-term EMA and short-term EMA < medium-term EMA. Sum and cap at 100. The EMAs use progressively longer lookbacks; relative volume is volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Bull confluence = 0.74 × bull momentum score + 0.26 × max(lunar bias, 0) × 100 + 6 if the moon phase is full; cap at 100
Calculation: Bear confluence = 0.74 × bear momentum score + 0.26 × max(−lunar bias, 0) × 100 + 6 if the moon phase is new; cap at 100
Calculation: Long trigger = (close > highest high of the prior breakout lookback bars AND close > open) OR (lowest low of the pullback lookback bars ≤ short-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND close > open AND close > prior close)
Calculation: Short trigger = (close < lowest low of the prior breakout lookback bars AND close < open) OR (highest high of the pullback lookback bars ≥ short-term EMA AND close < short-term EMA AND close < open AND close < prior close)
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the swing lookback − ATR × stop buffer; long risk = max(entry price − long stop, 10 minimum price increments); long target 2 = entry price + long risk × target 2 multiple
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over the swing lookback + ATR × stop buffer; short risk = max(short stop − entry price, 10 minimum price increments); short target 2 = entry price − short risk × target 2 multiple
Long: bar closes AND long direction is allowed AND bars since the prior long signal > cooldown period AND lunar bias > configurable threshold AND bull momentum score ≥ configurable minimum AND bull confluence ≥ configurable minimum AND long trigger AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe close > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA; use the current higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off)
Short: bar closes AND short direction is allowed AND bars since the prior short signal > cooldown period AND lunar bias < −configurable threshold AND bear momentum score ≥ configurable minimum AND bear confluence ≥ configurable minimum AND short trigger AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA; use the current higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off)
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target 2 OR short decision
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target 2 OR long decision
