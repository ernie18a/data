Calculation: Lunar bias = -cos(2π × phase), where phase is the fraction of a synodic month elapsed since the reference new moon. Compute Julian day from the bar's opening datetime in UTC, using Julian day 2440587.5 at the Unix epoch; phase = ((Julian day - 2451550.1) modulo 29.530588853) / 29.530588853, with a nonnegative modulo.
Calculation: Moon phase bucket = floor((phase + one-sixteenth) × 8) modulo 8. A new-moon event occurs when the bucket changes to 0; a full-moon event occurs when it changes to 4.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish state = higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA. Higher-timeframe bearish state = higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe trend EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA. All use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; EMAs use close and the same respective lengths as the chart-timeframe EMAs.
Calculation: Bullish momentum score = the sum of configurable weights for each true condition: close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > its previous value; close > slow EMA; RSI >= bullish RSI threshold; RSI > bullish RSI threshold plus an offset; MACD histogram > 0 AND >= its previous value; close > session VWAP of typical price; relative volume >= its threshold AND close > open AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= a close-location threshold AND abs(close - open) >= ATR × a body-size multiplier; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish state. Clamp the sum to the score range. Relative volume = volume / max(SMA of volume, a volume floor). Fast, slow, and trend EMAs have respectively increasing lengths. RSI and chart-timeframe EMAs use a selectable price source; MACD uses the same source with a shorter EMA, a longer EMA, and an EMA signal line.
Calculation: Bearish momentum score = the sum of the corresponding bullish-score weights for each true condition: close < trend EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < its previous value; close < slow EMA; RSI <= bearish RSI threshold; RSI < bearish RSI threshold minus the same offset; MACD histogram < 0 AND <= its previous value; close < session VWAP of typical price; relative volume >= its threshold AND close < open AND (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= the close-location threshold AND abs(close - open) >= ATR × the body-size multiplier; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish state. Clamp the sum to the same score range.
Calculation: Long confluence = clamp(bullish momentum score × momentum weight + max(lunar bias, 0) × score scale × lunar weight + full-moon event bonus, score range). Short confluence = clamp(bearish momentum score × momentum weight + max(-lunar bias, 0) × score scale × lunar weight + new-moon event bonus, score range). Each event bonus applies only on its event bar.
Calculation: Long signal = long direction enabled AND no prior long signal OR the number of bars since the last long signal > cooldown, with the cooldown alternatives grouped together; AND lunar bias > lunar threshold AND bullish momentum score >= minimum momentum score AND long confluence >= minimum confluence score AND higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish state, with the filter alternatives grouped together; AND either (close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback bars AND close > open) OR (lowest low over the pullback lookback including the current bar <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND close > previous close).
Calculation: Short signal = short direction enabled AND no prior short signal OR the number of bars since the last short signal > cooldown, with the cooldown alternatives grouped together; AND lunar bias < negative lunar threshold AND bearish momentum score >= minimum momentum score AND short confluence >= minimum confluence score AND higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish state, with the filter alternatives grouped together; AND either (close < lowest low over the preceding breakout lookback bars AND close < open) OR (highest high over the pullback lookback including the current bar >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND close < previous close).
Calculation: On each long signal, set the active side to long, entry to close, stop to the lowest low over the swing lookback including the signal bar minus ATR × stop buffer, and target to entry plus the target risk multiple × max(entry - stop, minimum risk). On each short signal, set the active side to short, entry to close, stop to the highest high over the swing lookback including the signal bar plus ATR × stop buffer, and target to entry minus the target risk multiple × max(stop - entry, minimum risk). Minimum risk is a configurable multiple of the minimum price increment. Update these values on every signal, including repeated same-side signals, before evaluating exits on that bar; clear the active state after an exit.
