Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of volume × OHLC4 divided by cumulative volume, resetting at each instrument trading-day boundary.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of volume × OHLC4 divided by cumulative volume while any configured session is active; reset at each session open, including when sessions overlap, and clear outside all sessions.
Calculation: Value-area bounds = divide a fixed price range into equal rows at the anchor bar. Center the range on that bar's high-low midpoint; set its half-width to a parameter times the greater of its high-low range and a minimum price-range parameter. For each eligible bar, add volume to each overlapping row in proportion to overlap divided by the greater of the bar's high-low range and a minimum price-increment parameter. Starting at the lowest row with maximum accumulated volume, expand one adjacent row at a time toward the greater-volume neighbor, choosing the upper neighbor on ties, until the configured fraction of total volume is reached or the rows are exhausted. The bounds are the outer edges of the selected rows. Reset the profile at each anchor; include the anchor bar and current bar.
Calculation: Daily value-area bounds = value-area bounds anchored at the configured daily boundary, using every bar.
Calculation: Session value-area bounds = value-area bounds reset at each configured session open, including overlapping opens, using bars while any configured session is active.
Calculation: Initial-balance bounds = highest high and lowest low during the configured initial-balance window; update during that window and retain the completed bounds until the next window opens.
Calculation: First initial-balance touch = after the initial-balance window closes, wait until a close is within the bounds, inclusive; from that bar onward, record the first bar whose high reaches or exceeds the upper bound or whose low reaches or falls below the lower bound, giving the upper bound priority if both are touched. Retain the recorded side until the configured market close or the next initial-balance window opens.
