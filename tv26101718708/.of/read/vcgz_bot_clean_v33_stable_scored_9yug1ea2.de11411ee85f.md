Calculation: BullBias = on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar containing the current chart bar: close > EMA(close) AND EMA(close) > EMA(close) of the previous higher-timeframe bar AND RSI(close) >= a level above 50
Calculation: BearBias = same higher-timeframe values: close < EMA(close) AND EMA(close) < EMA(close) of the previous higher-timeframe bar AND RSI(close) <= a level below 50
Calculation: LongZone = armed on a bar when flat AND BullBias AND no LongZone is active AND (highest high - lowest low over impulse lookback) >= ATR * impulse multiplier AND the lowest low of that lookback is more recent than its highest high. On arming, freeze H = lookback highest high and L = lookback lowest low. ZoneTop = H - (H-L)*fibLow, ZoneBot = H - (H-L)*fibHigh (fibLow < fibHigh, both between 0.5 and 1), ZoneMid = (ZoneTop+ZoneBot)/2. TouchBar = first bar from arming onward with low <= ZoneTop AND high >= ZoneBot. LongZone is cleared on any bar where not flat or BullBias is false, when bars since TouchBar > confirmation window, or when a long is entered.
Calculation: ShortZone = armed on a bar when flat AND BearBias AND no ShortZone is active AND (highest high - lowest low over impulse lookback) >= ATR * impulse multiplier AND the highest high of that lookback is more recent than its lowest low. On arming, freeze H and L as above. ZoneBot = L + (H-L)*fibLow, ZoneTop = L + (H-L)*fibHigh (same fibLow < fibHigh), ZoneMid = (ZoneTop+ZoneBot)/2. TouchBar = first bar from arming onward with high >= ZoneBot AND low <= ZoneTop. ShortZone is cleared on any bar where not flat or BearBias is false, when bars since TouchBar > confirmation window, or when a short is entered.
Calculation: LongScore = 3 + [(H-L) of LongZone >= ATR * strong-impulse multiplier (larger than impulse multiplier)] + [volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier AND close > open]
Calculation: ShortScore = 3 + [(H-L) of ShortZone >= ATR * strong-impulse multiplier] + [volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier AND close < open]
Calculation: StopLong = lowest low of the last few bars including the entry bar (a short window, much shorter than impulse lookback) - ATR * stop buffer, fixed at entry
Calculation: TargetLong = entry + R * (entry - StopLong), R fixed at entry
Calculation: StopShort = highest high of the last few bars including the entry bar - ATR * stop buffer, fixed at entry
Calculation: TargetShort = entry - R * (StopShort - entry), R fixed at entry
Long: flat AND LongZone active with TouchBar at or before the current bar AND low <= ZoneTop AND high >= ZoneBot AND close > ZoneMid AND close > open AND close >= low + k*(high-low) (k between 0.5 and 1) AND |close-open| >= ATR * min body multiplier AND (low < lowest low of the previous few bars excluding the current bar OR (min(open,close) - low) > f*|close-open| with f < 1) AND close > EMA(close) AND EMA(close) > EMA(close) one bar earlier AND LongScore >= minimum score AND close > StopLong; enter at the signal bar close
Short: flat AND ShortZone active with TouchBar at or before the current bar AND high >= ZoneBot AND low <= ZoneTop AND close < ZoneMid AND close < open AND close <= high - k*(high-low) AND |close-open| >= ATR * min body multiplier AND (high > highest high of the previous few bars excluding the current bar OR (high - max(open,close)) > f*|close-open|) AND close < EMA(close) AND EMA(close) < EMA(close) one bar earlier AND ShortScore >= minimum score AND StopShort > close; enter at the signal bar close
Exit: long, low <= StopLong OR high >= TargetLong
Exit: short, high >= StopShort OR low <= TargetShort
