Calculation: Trend age = bars since the chart-timeframe Supertrend was last in the opposite direction.
Calculation: Selected low bar = start with the current low; scan preceding bars from nearest to oldest through the number of bars since the chart-timeframe Supertrend was last in an uptrend, scanning the preceding bar even when that number is zero. Replace the selected bar whenever its low is strictly lower than the running minimum. A selected bar exists only if a replacement occurs.
Calculation: Selected high bar = start with the current high; scan preceding bars from nearest to oldest through the number of bars since the chart-timeframe Supertrend was last in a downtrend, scanning the preceding bar even when that number is zero. Replace the selected bar whenever its high is strictly higher than the running maximum. A selected bar exists only if a replacement occurs.
Calculation: Stop = chart-timeframe Supertrend value on the entry signal bar, held fixed for the position.
Long: Chart-timeframe Supertrend is in an uptrend AND higher-timeframe Supertrend on the last completed bar is in an uptrend AND trend age is greater than a minimum and less than a maximum AND close > SMA of close AND selected low bar exists AND its close < its open AND close > its open AND close > open.
Short: Chart-timeframe Supertrend is in a downtrend AND higher-timeframe Supertrend on the last completed bar is in a downtrend AND trend age is greater than a minimum and less than a maximum AND close < SMA of close AND selected high bar exists AND its close > its open AND close < its open AND close < open.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= entry price + (entry price - stop) * risk-reward ratio.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= entry price - (stop - entry price) * risk-reward ratio.
