Calculation: Swing high = the higher high of two consecutive bars where the first closes above its open and the second closes below its open. Swing low = the lower low of two consecutive bars where the first closes below its open and the second closes above its open.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = swing highs and lows from the last completed bars of each enabled higher or equal timeframe, and highs and lows of each completed enabled session on the chart timeframe. A session high or low is either its absolute high or low, or respectively its highest swing high or lowest swing low formed within the session. Retain the configured number of most recent levels per timeframe and per session type, counting highs and lows together. A level is available until swept or removed from the retained history.
Calculation: Sweep = for an available high level, high > level in wick mode, or close > level followed on a later bar within the configured window by close < level in close mode; for an available low level, low < level in wick mode, or close < level followed on a later bar within the configured window by close > level in close mode. Both mode accepts either condition. The first close beyond starts the window; after expiry the sequence resets. A swept level becomes unavailable.
Calculation: Pending setup = on a low-level sweep, store a bullish setup with the sweep bar's low and the latest chart-timeframe swing high; on a high-level sweep, store a bearish setup with the sweep bar's high and the latest chart-timeframe swing low. A setup requires the corresponding swing to exist. Process levels in configured timeframe order from shorter to longer, then session order; each qualifying sweep replaces the previous setup. A setup remains pending through the configured bar window unless replaced, confirmed, or, if invalidation is enabled, the close falls below its stored low for a bullish setup or rises above its stored high for a bearish setup. Check invalidation and expiry before confirmation, including on the sweep bar.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = for each of two configured bias timeframes, update the latest swing high and swing low using its last completed bar; set bias bullish when that completed bar's close > latest swing high and bearish when its close < latest swing low, with the bearish update taking precedence if both occur. Otherwise retain the previous bias, initially neutral. Either bias timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a lower timeframe use the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar and its preceding completed bar. The longer bias timeframe takes precedence in the combined bias whenever it is nonneutral; otherwise use the shorter timeframe's bias.
Long: Low-level sweep AND latest chart-timeframe swing high exists.
Short: High-level sweep AND latest chart-timeframe swing low exists.
Long: Pending bullish setup AND close > stored swing high.
Short: Pending bearish setup AND close < stored swing low.
Long: Last completed bias-timeframe close > latest swing high.
Short: Last completed bias-timeframe close < latest swing low.
