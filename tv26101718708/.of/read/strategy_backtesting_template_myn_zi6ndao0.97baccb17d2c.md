Long: RSI(shorter lookback) crosses above 80 AND (EMA filter disabled OR selected price source > its EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND long trading is enabled AND no long position is open AND datetime is within the configured date range and session window
Short: RSI(shorter lookback) crosses below 20 AND (EMA filter disabled OR selected price source < its EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND short trading is enabled AND no short position is open AND datetime is within the configured date range and session window
Exit: Long, close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR RSI(longer lookback) crosses below 40 OR price reaches any configured take-profit target above entry price; evaluate only within the configured date range and session window
Exit: Short, close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR RSI(longer lookback) crosses above 60 OR price reaches any configured take-profit target below entry price; evaluate only within the configured date range and session window
