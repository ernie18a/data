Calculation: Alternative ADX = 100 times the simple moving average of abs(plus DI - minus DI) / (plus DI + minus DI), using the DI lookback; plus and minus DI are 100 times their respective smoothed directional movements divided by smoothed true range. For each series, smoothed value = previous smoothed value - previous smoothed value / lookback + current value, starting from zero. True range is the maximum of high - low, abs(high - previous close), and abs(low - previous close); replace missing previous prices with zero. Plus directional movement is max(high - previous high, 0) when high - previous high > previous low - low, otherwise zero; minus directional movement is max(previous low - low, 0) when previous low - low > high - previous high, otherwise zero.
Long: Shorter-lookback RSI of close crosses above its upper entry threshold AND position side is not long AND long trading is enabled AND (EMA filter is disabled OR selected price source > EMA of that source) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR selected standard or alternative ADX > ADX threshold) AND (start-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime >= start datetime) AND (end-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime <= end datetime) AND (time-window filter is disabled OR bar opening time is within the selected daily session in the selected time zone).
Short: Shorter-lookback RSI of close crosses below its lower entry threshold AND position side is not short AND short trading is enabled AND (EMA filter is disabled OR selected price source < EMA of that source) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR selected standard or alternative ADX > ADX threshold) AND (start-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime >= start datetime) AND (end-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime <= end datetime) AND (time-window filter is disabled OR bar opening time is within the selected daily session in the selected time zone).
Exit: long, (longer-lookback RSI of close crosses below its lower exit threshold OR close < entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction)) AND (start-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime >= start datetime) AND (end-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime <= end datetime) AND (time-window filter is disabled OR bar opening time is within the selected daily session in the selected time zone).
Exit: short, (longer-lookback RSI of close crosses above its upper exit threshold OR close > entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction)) AND (start-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime >= start datetime) AND (end-date filter is disabled OR bar datetime <= end datetime) AND (time-window filter is disabled OR bar opening time is within the selected daily session in the selected time zone).
Exit: long, high >= entry price * (1 + a take-profit fraction); apply each of four independently configured targets to its configured position fraction, with the fourth applying to the remaining position, after its limit order has been submitted within the enabled date and time window.
Exit: short, low <= entry price * (1 - a take-profit fraction); apply each of four independently configured targets to its configured position fraction, with the fourth applying to the remaining position, after its limit order has been submitted within the enabled date and time window.
