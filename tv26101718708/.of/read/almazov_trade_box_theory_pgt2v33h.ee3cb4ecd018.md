Calculation: Previous-day high/low = maximum high / minimum low of the preceding completed daily period, aggregated from chart-timeframe bars (higher timeframe); exclude the initial partial daily period.
Calculation: Lower/upper zone = [previous-day low, previous-day low + range × zone fraction] / [previous-day high − range × zone fraction, previous-day high], where range = previous-day high − previous-day low.
Calculation: Candle body = |close − open|; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; effective body = max(candle body, minimum price tick); candle range = high − low.
Calculation: Long stop = previous-day low − stop buffer; short stop = previous-day high + stop buffer; initial risk = distance from entry price to stop; target = entry price ± initial risk × target multiple, added for long and subtracted for short.
Long: candle range > 0 AND lower wick ≥ effective body × minimum main-wick/body ratio AND upper wick ≤ effective body × maximum opposite-wick/body ratio AND candle body / candle range ≤ maximum body fraction AND close > open AND low ≤ lower-zone top AND high ≥ previous-day low AND entry price − long stop > minimum price tick AND no bar in the current daily period has high > previous-day high or low < previous-day low AND no setup is active AND no setup exited on this bar AND bar closes; if both directions qualify, choose long.
Short: candle range > 0 AND upper wick ≥ effective body × minimum main-wick/body ratio AND lower wick ≤ effective body × maximum opposite-wick/body ratio AND candle body / candle range ≤ maximum body fraction AND close < open AND high ≥ upper-zone bottom AND low ≤ previous-day high AND short stop − entry price > minimum price tick AND no bar in the current daily period has high > previous-day high or low < previous-day low AND no setup is active AND no setup exited on this bar AND no long qualifies AND bar closes.
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry that closes, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target; if both are true, exit at the stop.
Exit: Short, on a bar after entry that closes, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target; if both are true, exit at the stop.
