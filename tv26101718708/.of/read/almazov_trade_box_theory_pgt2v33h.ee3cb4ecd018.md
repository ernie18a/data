Calculation: Reference high and low = high and low aggregated from chart bars in the last completed higher-timeframe trading-day session; exclude the initial session if the loaded history starts after its session boundary.
Calculation: Reference range = reference high − reference low; lower zone top = reference low + reference range × zone-width fraction; upper zone bottom = reference high − reference range × zone-width fraction.
Calculation: Session high and low = highest high and lowest low from the start of the current higher-timeframe trading-day session through the current completed chart bar.
Calculation: Body = abs(close − open); effective body = max(body, minimum price increment); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Long stop = reference low at entry − stop-buffer distance; short stop = reference high at entry + stop-buffer distance. Fix the stop at entry; entry price = entry-bar close. Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × target multiple; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × target multiple. Fix the target at entry.
Long: position side = flat AND no exit occurred on the current bar AND reference range > 0 AND session high <= reference high AND session low >= reference low AND low <= lower zone top AND high >= reference low AND high > low AND lower wick >= effective body × minimum main-wick ratio AND upper wick <= effective body × maximum opposite-wick ratio AND body <= (high − low) × maximum body fraction AND (direction filter disabled OR close > open) AND close − long stop > minimum price increment.
Short: position side = flat AND no exit occurred on the current bar AND reference range > 0 AND session high <= reference high AND session low >= reference low AND high >= upper zone bottom AND low <= reference high AND high > low AND upper wick >= effective body × minimum main-wick ratio AND lower wick <= effective body × maximum opposite-wick ratio AND body <= (high − low) × maximum body fraction AND (direction filter disabled OR close < open) AND short stop − close > minimum price increment. If both entry conditions hold, enter long; the short signal is also marked.
Exit: long, current bar is after entry bar AND (low <= long stop OR high >= long target).
Exit: short, current bar is after entry bar AND (high >= short stop OR low <= short target).
