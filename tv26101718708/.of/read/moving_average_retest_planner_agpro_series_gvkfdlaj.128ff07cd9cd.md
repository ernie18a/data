Calculation: Reference = selected moving average of a selected price source; slope = reference minus its value at the slope lookback; band = ATR multiplied by the sensitivity-dependent band multiplier. The prior-distance multiplier also depends on sensitivity, and the confirmation multiplier depends on confirmation mode.
Calculation: Bullish retest = close > reference AND slope > ATR times the slope threshold AND the highest close minus reference over the retest lookback, including the current bar, > current ATR times the prior-distance multiplier AND low <= reference + band AND close >= reference - band AND close >= reference + band times the confirmation multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish retest = close < reference AND slope < negative ATR times the slope threshold AND the highest reference minus close over the retest lookback, including the current bar, > current ATR times the prior-distance multiplier AND high >= reference - band AND close <= reference + band AND close <= reference - band times the confirmation multiplier.
Calculation: Reaction score = clamp(0.22 times slope score + 0.24 times depth score + 0.18 times wick score + 0.20 times close score + 0.16 times volume score, 0, 100). Clamp limits a value to the stated bounds. Slope score = clamp(85 times absolute slope / ATR, 0, 100). For a bullish retest use low as the depth price and max(reference - low, 0) as penetration; for a bearish retest use high and max(high - reference, 0). Depth score = clamp(100 - 42 times absolute(depth price - reference) / band - 18 times penetration / band, 0, 100). Wick score = clamp(160 times the lower wick / bar range, 0, 100) for a bullish retest, or the upper wick for a bearish retest. Close location = clamp((close - low) / bar range, 0, 1); close score = clamp(100 times close location + 10, 0, 100) for a bullish retest, or clamp(100 times (1 - close location) + 10, 0, 100) for a bearish retest. Volume score = clamp(100 times (relative volume - 0.65) / 1.05, 0, 100), where relative volume is volume divided by its simple moving average, or 1 if that average is unavailable or nonpositive. Use a small positive price increment as the minimum ATR and bar range.
Calculation: Active plan = the most recent bullish or bearish retest whose reaction score >= max(minimum ready score - 14, 1), provided the cooldown since the preceding such plan has elapsed. Freeze its side, trigger close, start bar, and invalidation level at creation; invalidation is reference - 1.25 times band for a bullish plan and reference + 1.25 times band for a bearish plan. A new plan replaces the previous plan before failure or follow-through is evaluated. A plan remains active through the retest lookback after its start and is cleared after its first failure or follow-through, or on expiry.
Long: close > reference AND slope > ATR times the slope threshold.
Short: close < reference AND slope < negative ATR times the slope threshold.
Long: bullish retest AND reaction score >= max(minimum ready score - 14, 1) AND cooldown since the preceding active-plan creation has elapsed.
Short: bearish retest AND reaction score >= max(minimum ready score - 14, 1) AND cooldown since the preceding active-plan creation has elapsed.
Long: active plan is bullish AND close >= its invalidation AND close - its trigger close >= current ATR times the follow-through multiplier.
Short: active plan is bearish AND close <= its invalidation AND its trigger close - close >= current ATR times the follow-through multiplier.
Exit: long, active plan is bullish AND close < its invalidation.
Exit: short, active plan is bearish AND close > its invalidation.
