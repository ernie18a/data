Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side, available only after the right-hand bars have completed; retain the latest two confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Bullish trend = ((shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA AND both EMAs > their previous-bar values AND latest swing high > preceding swing high AND latest swing low > preceding swing low) OR (two swing highs exist AND close > latest swing high + ATR × breakout buffer)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR shorter-period higher-timeframe close EMA > longer-period higher-timeframe close EMA). Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bearish trend = ((shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA AND both EMAs < their previous-bar values AND latest swing high < preceding swing high AND latest swing low < preceding swing low) OR (two swing lows exist AND close < latest swing low − ATR × breakout buffer)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR shorter-period higher-timeframe close EMA < longer-period higher-timeframe close EMA). Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Entry arms = maintain a trend state initially neutral and separate long and short arms initially false. On each bar, if bullish trend is true and state is not bullish, set state bullish, arm long and disarm short; otherwise, if bearish trend is true and state is not bearish, set state bearish, arm short and disarm long; otherwise, if neither trend is true, set state neutral. After evaluating entries, disarm each side whose entry fired.
Long: Bullish trend = true.
Short: Bullish trend = false AND bearish trend = true.
Long: Long entries enabled AND previous bar's bullish trend = true AND previous low <= max(previous shorter-period EMA, previous longer-period EMA) + previous ATR × pullback multiplier AND previous close >= previous longer-period EMA AND close > shorter-period EMA AND close > previous high AND close > open AND (close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body fraction AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND (level filter disabled OR ((close >= last completed shorter higher-timeframe high OR last completed shorter higher-timeframe high − close > ATR × minimum room multiplier) AND (close >= last completed longer higher-timeframe high OR last completed longer higher-timeframe high − close > ATR × minimum room multiplier))) AND (first-entry-only filter disabled OR long arm = true).
Short: Short entries enabled AND previous bar's bearish trend = true AND previous high >= min(previous shorter-period EMA, previous longer-period EMA) − previous ATR × pullback multiplier AND previous close <= previous longer-period EMA AND close < shorter-period EMA AND close < previous low AND open > close AND (open − close) / (high − low) >= minimum body fraction AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND (level filter disabled OR ((close <= last completed shorter higher-timeframe low OR close − last completed shorter higher-timeframe low > ATR × minimum room multiplier) AND (close <= last completed longer higher-timeframe low OR close − last completed longer higher-timeframe low > ATR × minimum room multiplier))) AND (first-entry-only filter disabled OR short arm = true).
