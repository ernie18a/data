Calculation: Pivot = a high strictly greater than all highs, or a low strictly less than all lows, within equal-length windows on both sides; confirm it only after the right-hand window completes.
Calculation: Active fan = on each completed chart bar, consider the four most recent confirmed low pivots, then the four most recent confirmed high pivots. For each set, accept it if the oldest pivot is within the maximum age and the absolute percentage change from the oldest to the second pivot is at least the minimum leg percentage. Replace the active fan if it has the same pivot type, or if the candidate's newest pivot is later than the active fan's newest pivot. Retain the previous fan if neither candidate replaces it. The low-pivot fan is bullish and the high-pivot fan is bearish. Its trigger line at any bar index is the straight line through its oldest and newest pivots, extrapolated to that index. Use the active fan selected on the current bar to evaluate both prior closes.
Calculation: Long signal = bullish active fan AND previous close > trigger line at the previous bar AND close two bars ago <= trigger line two bars ago AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume > current volume SMA multiplied by the volume threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR previous close > last completed higher-timeframe close).
Calculation: Short signal = bearish active fan AND previous close < trigger line at the previous bar AND close two bars ago >= trigger line two bars ago AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume > current volume SMA multiplied by the volume threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR previous close < last completed higher-timeframe close).
Calculation: Targets = on each signal, replace the stored targets using the previous chart bar's close as entry and its ATR. For a long signal, stop = max(minimum of the active fan's two newest pivot prices minus an ATR buffer, entry minus a maximum ATR distance), and each target = entry + (entry - stop) multiplied by its target risk multiple. For a short signal, stop = min(maximum of those pivot prices plus an ATR buffer, entry plus a maximum ATR distance), and each target = entry - (stop - entry) multiplied by its target risk multiple. Store three independently parameterized targets and the signal side until the next signal.
Long: Long signal.
Short: Short signal.
Exit: long, Short signal OR (latest signal side is long AND current high >= any stored target).
Exit: short, Long signal OR (latest signal side is short AND current low <= any stored target).
