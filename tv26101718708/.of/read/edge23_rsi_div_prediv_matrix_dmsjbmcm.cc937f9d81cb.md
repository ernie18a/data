Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close; calculated on each scanned timeframe using its current, possibly still-forming bar.
Calculation: Bearish raw divergence = RSI is overbought, current high exceeds the stored comparison high, and current RSI is below its stored comparison RSI. Initialize the stored pair on the first overbought bar; replace it when RSI exceeds the stored comparison RSI; reset it after the lookback expires. Bearish confirmed divergence = a bearish raw divergence with at least the configured number of bearish raw divergences in the lookback window.
Calculation: Bullish raw divergence = RSI is oversold, current low is below the stored comparison low, and current RSI is above its stored comparison RSI. Initialize the stored pair on the first oversold bar; replace it when RSI falls below the stored comparison RSI; reset it after the lookback expires. Bullish confirmed divergence = a bullish raw divergence with at least the configured number of bullish raw divergences in the lookback window.
Calculation: Bearish and bullish exhaustion scores = sum the configured points for each applicable condition: respectively, price made a higher high or lower low than the pre-divergence lookback ago and the recent average candle body shrank versus the earlier average; price made that higher high or lower low while volume fell; RSI is elevated or depressed and its latest change weakened versus the preceding change; MACD histogram fell or rose; price made that higher high or lower low while ATR did not rise; price is beyond the configured ATR-normalized distance from its EMA and made a positive but sub-ATR move in the respective direction; relative volume is below its threshold after exceeding its spike threshold in the lookback; or a sufficiently large prior close-to-close impulse was followed by failure to exceed the intervening high or low.
Long: bullish confirmed divergence OR (bullish score meets the strong threshold and exceeds the bearish score AND no bearish overbought confirmed-divergence signal) OR (bullish score meets the watch threshold and exceeds the bearish score AND neither score meets the strong threshold AND neither side has confirmed divergence) OR (RSI is oversold AND neither score meets the watch threshold with its side leading AND neither side has confirmed divergence)
Short: bearish confirmed divergence OR (bearish score meets the strong threshold and exceeds the bullish score AND no bullish oversold confirmed-divergence signal) OR (bearish score meets the watch threshold and exceeds the bullish score AND neither score meets the strong threshold AND neither side has confirmed divergence) OR (RSI is overbought AND neither score meets the watch threshold with its side leading AND neither side has confirmed divergence)
