Calculation: Evaluation bars = apply the following calculations independently to each selected timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Divergence anchors = for each side, store the price extreme, RSI, and bar index of the first bar in its RSI extreme zone. On each later bar in that zone, replace the bearish anchor when RSI is higher than its stored RSI, or the bullish anchor when RSI is lower than its stored RSI. After evaluating the current bar and updating the anchor, clear it if its age exceeds the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = RSI above the overbought threshold AND high above the bearish anchor high AND RSI below the bearish anchor RSI, with at least a threshold number of such raw events in the divergence lookback including the current bar. Evaluate the raw event before updating or clearing the anchor.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = RSI below the oversold threshold AND low below the bullish anchor low AND RSI above the bullish anchor RSI, with at least the same threshold number of such raw events in the divergence lookback including the current bar. Evaluate the raw event before updating or clearing the anchor.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its SMA when that SMA is positive; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum weights for true conditions: a smaller weight for high above its pre-divergence-lookback value AND SMA of absolute candle body below that SMA at the lookback; a larger weight for high above its lookback value AND volume below its lookback value; a larger weight for RSI above an upper weakening threshold AND its current one-bar change below its previous one-bar change; a larger weight for MACD histogram below its previous value; a smaller weight for high above its lookback value AND ATR at or below its previous value; a smaller weight for close above EMA AND absolute distance from EMA divided by positive ATR above a distance threshold AND high minus its lookback value positive and below ATR; a smaller weight for the highest relative volume in the pre-divergence lookback above a spike threshold AND current relative volume below a fade threshold; a smaller weight for close at the lookback above close at twice the lookback by more than an impulse multiple of ATR at the lookback AND current high at or below the highest high of the preceding pre-divergence-lookback bars. All smaller weights are equal and all larger weights are equal.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum the same respective weights for true conditions: low below its pre-divergence-lookback value AND SMA of absolute candle body below that SMA at the lookback; low below its lookback value AND volume below its lookback value; RSI below a lower weakening threshold AND its current one-bar change above its previous one-bar change; MACD histogram above its previous value; low below its lookback value AND ATR at or below its previous value; close below EMA AND absolute distance from EMA divided by positive ATR above the distance threshold AND low at the lookback minus current low positive and below ATR; the highest relative volume in the pre-divergence lookback above the spike threshold AND current relative volume below the fade threshold; close at the lookback below close at twice the lookback by more than the impulse multiple of ATR at the lookback AND current low at or above the lowest low of the preceding pre-divergence-lookback bars.
Calculation: Bullish pre-divergence = bullish score at or above either the strong-score threshold or the watch-score threshold AND bullish score above bearish score.
Calculation: Bearish pre-divergence = bearish score at or above either the strong-score threshold or the watch-score threshold AND bearish score above bullish score.
Long: Bullish divergence OR (bearish divergence is false AND (bullish pre-divergence OR (bearish pre-divergence is false AND RSI below the oversold threshold))).
Short: Bullish divergence is false AND (bearish divergence OR bearish pre-divergence OR (bullish pre-divergence is false AND bearish pre-divergence is false AND RSI at or above the oversold threshold AND RSI above the overbought threshold)).
