Calculation: Rolling VWAP and deviation = volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of HLC3 over the selected rolling chart-bar window, including the current bar; optionally restrict contributing bars to a configured session, or reset at a configured anchor. Optionally apply the same successive EMA smoothing passes to each series.
Calculation: VWAP slope = current rolling VWAP minus its value a configured number of chart bars ago; rising means slope > a configured price threshold, falling means slope < the negative threshold, and turning up or down means rising or falling respectively OR slope has changed in that direction over the same lookback.
Calculation: Estimated delta = sum signed volume of completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; sign is positive when close > open, negative when close < open, otherwise determined by the lower-timeframe close versus its preceding close, or zero if equal. If lower-timeframe bars are unavailable, use chart-bar volume signed by close versus open, or zero if equal.
Calculation: Print statistics = on a rolling chart-bar baseline including the current bar, calculate the median delta, median absolute deviation of delta from its contemporaneous rolling median, median absolute delta, median absolute delta divided by volume, median range, median body, and medians of volume divided by range and by body; floor range and body at a configured minimum price increment. Modified delta deviation is the absolute value of a configured scale factor times (delta minus median delta) divided by its median absolute deviation. Relative volume is volume divided by the prior same-session-time-slot volume EMA when sufficiently warmed up, otherwise divided by rolling mean volume. Relative absolute delta is absolute delta divided by the prior same-slot absolute-delta EMA. Update slot EMAs after evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Aggression score = a configured weighted sum of absolute delta divided by its rolling median, relative absolute delta when its time slot is ready otherwise modified delta deviation, and (absolute delta divided by volume) divided by its rolling median; unavailable components contribute zero.
Calculation: Aggression print = modified delta deviation >= its threshold AND relative volume >= its threshold AND absolute delta >= its threshold. Absorption print = relative volume >= its threshold AND (volume-per-range divided by its rolling median >= its threshold AND range divided by its rolling median <= its threshold OR volume-per-body divided by its rolling median >= its threshold AND body divided by its rolling median <= its threshold); use the configured range, body, or either test. Both require available print statistics and a bar in the configured session. If both qualify, classify as absorption only when the largest qualifying volume-per-range or volume-per-body ratio exceeds the aggression score; otherwise classify as aggression. Optionally accept only a print whose score is at least the prior accepted score of its own type, resetting the two records at each configured session; the absorption score is its largest qualifying effort ratio.
Calculation: Value-area profile = for the configured number of higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming, distribute each bar's volume evenly across a configured maximum number of price bins sampled across its high-low range. The point of control is the highest-volume bin. Starting there, add the heavier adjacent occupied bin, choosing the upper bin on ties, until a configured fraction of volume is reached; optionally stop before an addition would exceed the fraction. The lowest and highest included bin prices are the value-area edges. Alternatively use the last completed higher-timeframe bar's profile, built by binning completed lower-timeframe bars' volume at their closes; if unavailable, bin chart-bar volume at its close.
Calculation: Bias votes = compare close with each enabled raw moving average: one on chart bars and two on different higher timeframes, one longer than the other. Higher-timeframe averages use either the last completed bar or the bar still forming, as configured. A vote is up above its average and down below it; optionally require the average's change over its own configured lookback to have the same sign. Majority requires more than half of enabled averages; unanimous requires all enabled averages.
Calculation: Reversal context = an accepted print whose high reaches the upper VWAP deviation edge without its low reaching the lower edge, or whose low reaches the lower edge without its high reaching the upper edge; its direction is toward VWAP. Delta must point toward the reached edge for absorption or toward VWAP for aggression. Its score is the sum of the edge test, the delta test, and whether the close's fraction of the bar range is at least a configured threshold in the reversal direction; use the midpoint for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Continuation context = an accepted aggression print that is not reversal context, with nonzero delta and close on delta's side of VWAP. Its direction is the delta sign; its score is the sum of the VWAP-side test, VWAP slope aligned with delta, and whether the close's fraction of the bar range is at least a configured threshold in the delta direction; use the midpoint for a zero-range bar.
Long: Close > rolling VWAP OR VWAP slope is rising OR close > an upper VWAP deviation band.
Short: Close < rolling VWAP OR VWAP slope is falling OR close < a lower VWAP deviation band.
Long: Close > an enabled raw moving average OR the average's change over its configured lookback > 0 OR up bias votes form a majority.
Short: Close < an enabled raw moving average OR the average's change over its configured lookback < 0 OR down bias votes form a majority.
Long: Close > the value-area upper edge OR close > the point of control when inside the value area and the configured inside-value lean is positive; alternatively, with the fixed-width profile band, close > point of control plus a configured fraction of the reference range.
Short: Close < the value-area lower edge OR close < the point of control when inside the value area and the configured inside-value lean is positive; alternatively, with the fixed-width profile band, close < point of control minus a configured fraction of the reference range.
Long: An accepted print has estimated delta > 0.
Short: An accepted print has estimated delta < 0.
Long: Reversal context or continuation context points long AND its score >= the configured context threshold.
Short: Reversal context or continuation context points short AND its score >= the configured context threshold.
Long: The most recent armed long reversal context has score >= the setup threshold AND optionally is absorption AND elapsed bars <= the reclaim window AND no close since arming is below the print low AND close > the current lower VWAP deviation edge AND the configured slope filter is off or turning up or rising as selected AND the configured bias filter is off or has the required up majority or unanimity. A new qualifying reversal context replaces the previously armed reversal; evaluate the print bar itself.
Short: The most recent armed short reversal context has score >= the setup threshold AND optionally is absorption AND elapsed bars <= the reclaim window AND no close since arming is above the print high AND close < the current upper VWAP deviation edge AND the configured slope filter is off or turning down or falling as selected AND the configured bias filter is off or has the required down majority or unanimity. A new qualifying reversal context replaces the previously armed reversal; evaluate the print bar itself.
Long: A long continuation context with score >= the setup threshold occurs while VWAP is rising AND the configured bias filter is off or has the required up majority or unanimity AND either the configured hold count is zero or each of the required subsequent consecutive closes is > VWAP with VWAP not falling. A new qualifying continuation context replaces the previously armed trend; cancel on the first failed hold.
Short: A short continuation context with score >= the setup threshold occurs while VWAP is falling AND the configured bias filter is off or has the required down majority or unanimity AND either the configured hold count is zero or each of the required subsequent consecutive closes is < VWAP with VWAP not rising. A new qualifying continuation context replaces the previously armed trend; cancel on the first failed hold.
