Calculation: Opening range = running high and low of chart bars in the configured opening session; it is established after the session ends and remains established until the next session begins.
Calculation: Structure = initially bullish with no break recorded; on confirmation of a higher pivot high than the preceding pivot high, set structure bullish and record a break; on confirmation of a lower pivot low than the preceding pivot low, set structure bearish and record a break. A recorded break persists, and a lower pivot low takes precedence if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish order block = respectively, a bearish then bullish candle or a bullish then bearish candle, with current range greater than a multiple of ATR and volume greater than a multiple of average volume. Retain the latest block of each side indefinitely. Its strength is the sum of volume and range tiers, each tier increasing at two respective thresholds; a strong block has a strength at or above the configured tier threshold.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low greater than high two bars earlier, with gap size as a percentage of that earlier high at or above a threshold. Bearish gap = high less than low two bars earlier, with gap size as a percentage of that earlier low at or above a threshold. Retain the latest gap of each side indefinitely, including its creation bar. A bullish retest has low at or below the gap top and close at or above its bottom; a bearish retest has high at or above the gap bottom and close at or below its top.
Calculation: Value range = highest high and lowest low over a lookback, including the current bar; equilibrium is their midpoint. Discount is close below the level halfway from equilibrium to the range low; premium is close above the level halfway from equilibrium to the range high.
Calculation: Golden zone = determine the highest high and lowest low over the primary swing lookback, choosing the oldest occurrence of each on ties. If the low occurred before the high, start at the low and end at the high; otherwise start at the high and end at the low. Compute a retracement as start plus a configured fraction of end minus start, and a second level as that retracement times a configured multiplier. Close is in the zone when it is between those two levels inclusive. When zone display or Fibonacci calculation is disabled, retain its last computed state.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed price pivot low and RSI pivot low occur at the same pivot bar, with price lower and RSI higher than at the preceding simultaneous pair. Bearish divergence uses simultaneous pivot highs, with price higher and RSI lower. Compare only consecutive simultaneous pairs and signal on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Liquidity below = three consecutive equal lows OR a confirmed pivot low within a configured percentage of the preceding confirmed pivot low. Liquidity above = three consecutive equal highs OR a confirmed pivot high within a configured percentage of the preceding confirmed pivot high. Pivot comparisons are updated only while liquidity detection is enabled.
Calculation: Strong bullish hammer = close above open AND lower wick greater than a multiple of body AND upper wick less than a multiple of body AND body divided by range below a threshold AND volume greater than a multiple of average volume. Strong bearish shooting star reverses candle direction and exchanges upper and lower wicks. Body is absolute close minus open; upper wick is high minus the greater of open and close; lower wick is the lesser of open and close minus low; body divided by range is zero when range is zero.
Calculation: Strong bullish engulfing = close above open AND previous close below previous open AND open below previous close AND close above previous open AND volume greater than a multiple of previous volume. Strong bearish engulfing reverses every price inequality.
Calculation: Morning star = close two bars earlier below its open AND previous body below a fraction of previous range AND close above open AND close above the midpoint of the high and low two bars earlier. Evening star reverses the candle-direction and midpoint inequalities.
Calculation: Long score = sum the following enabled contributions: double-weight for an established opening-range upward close crossover, a recorded bullish structure break when structure confirmation is enabled, a bullish gap retest, short-lookback RSI below its oversold threshold with close above the trend EMA, and bullish RSI divergence; single-weight for an existing bullish order block, a strong existing bullish order block, discount, the golden zone, and liquidity below. Also add single-weight for volume above a spike multiple of its average and for RSI rising over a momentum lookback while MACD histogram is positive; add double-weight for a strong bullish hammer, strong bullish engulfing, or morning star. Count the three patterns together only once.
Calculation: Short score = sum the following enabled contributions: double-weight for an established opening-range downward close crossunder, a recorded bearish structure break when structure confirmation is enabled, a bearish gap retest, short-lookback RSI above its overbought threshold with close below the trend EMA, and bearish RSI divergence; single-weight for an existing bearish order block, a strong existing bearish order block, premium, the golden zone, and liquidity above. Also add single-weight for volume above a spike multiple of its average and for RSI falling over a momentum lookback while MACD histogram is negative; add double-weight for a strong bearish shooting star, strong bearish engulfing, or evening star. Count the three patterns together only once.
Calculation: Entry stop = opening-range low for a long or opening-range high for a short when the opening-range feature is enabled and the range is established; otherwise signal-bar low minus an ATR multiple for a long or signal-bar high plus an ATR multiple for a short. Fix the stop and signal-bar close at entry; entry risk is the absolute difference between them. The target is signal-bar close plus entry risk times the reward multiple for a long, or minus that amount for a short. If adaptive targets are enabled and signal-bar ATR exceeds a multiple of its moving average, multiply the reward multiple by the high-volatility adjustment.
Long: Long score at or above the selected score threshold AND position flat AND daily entry count below its limit AND volume above a multiple of average volume AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar in a configured trading session) AND (regime filter disabled OR choppy avoidance disabled OR ATR at or below a multiple of its moving average) AND (partial setups allowed OR short EMA above medium EMA above long EMA OR close above trend EMA AND short EMA above medium EMA) AND signal-bar close above entry stop.
Short: Short score at or above the selected score threshold AND position flat AND daily entry count below its limit AND volume above a multiple of average volume AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar in a configured trading session) AND (regime filter disabled OR choppy avoidance disabled OR ATR at or below a multiple of its moving average) AND (partial setups allowed OR short EMA below medium EMA below long EMA OR close below trend EMA AND short EMA below medium EMA) AND signal-bar close below entry stop.
Long: Established opening range AND close above opening-range high AND previous close at or below opening-range high.
Short: Established opening range AND close below opening-range low AND previous close at or above opening-range low.
Long: Bullish gap retest OR recorded bullish structure break OR bullish RSI divergence OR strong bullish hammer OR strong bullish engulfing OR morning star.
Short: Bearish gap retest OR recorded bearish structure break OR bearish RSI divergence OR strong bearish shooting star OR strong bearish engulfing OR evening star.
Long: Liquidity below AND liquidity detection enabled.
Short: Liquidity above AND liquidity detection enabled.
Long: RSI below its oversold threshold OR close in discount.
Short: RSI above its overbought threshold OR close in premium.
Long: Close above open AND no strong bearish shooting star AND no strong bearish engulfing AND no evening star.
Short: Close at or below open AND no strong bullish hammer AND no strong bullish engulfing AND no morning star AND close outside the golden zone.
Exit: long, low at or below the fixed entry stop OR high at or above the fixed target OR time exit enabled AND chart bar in the configured end-of-day session.
Exit: short, high at or above the fixed entry stop OR low at or below the fixed target OR time exit enabled AND chart bar in the configured end-of-day session.
