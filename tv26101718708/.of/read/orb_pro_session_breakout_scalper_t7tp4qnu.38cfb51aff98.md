Calculation: Session = bars whose opening time is at or after the configured start timestamp for that bar's local calendar date and before the configured end timestamp for that date; add one day to the end timestamp when the end time is at or before the start time.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of bars in the session whose opening time is before the session start plus the opening-range duration; reset at the first such bar after a bar outside that window or a change in local calendar day, and lock on the first subsequent session bar outside the window.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume over session bars, resetting at the start of the opening range; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Long stop = opening-range low when the range stop is selected, otherwise close minus ATR times the stop multiplier. Long risk = close minus long stop. Long target = close plus long risk times the target multiplier. Long partial target = close plus long risk times the partial-target multiplier.
Long: Opening range is locked AND bar is in session AND number of entries since the opening-range reset is below the trade cap AND close > opening-range high AND (range-size filter is disabled OR opening-range high minus opening-range low is between the minimum and maximum ATR multiples, inclusive) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= volume SMA times the volume multiplier) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (confirmation filter is disabled OR the number of bars since close <= the corresponding bar's opening-range high is at least the confirmation length).
Exit: Long, partial exit has not occurred AND high >= current long partial target; exit the configured fraction and mark the partial exit complete.
Exit: Long, low <= the preceding bar's active long stop OR high >= the preceding bar's long target; the active long stop is the close of the bar on which the long position first became open if breakeven is enabled and the partial exit has occurred, otherwise the long stop.
Exit: Long, current bar is outside the session AND preceding bar was in the session.
Exit: Short, current bar is outside the session AND preceding bar was in the session.
Exit: Long, failed-breakout markers are enabled AND opening range is locked AND preceding close > current opening-range high AND close < opening-range high.
Exit: Short, failed-breakout markers are enabled AND opening range is locked AND preceding close < current opening-range low AND close > opening-range low.
