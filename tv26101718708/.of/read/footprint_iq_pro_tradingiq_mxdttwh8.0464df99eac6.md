Calculation: Price-level delta ratio = Divide each lower-timeframe completed bar's volume equally among the price-grid levels from the greatest level at or below its low through the greatest level at or below its high. Add the allocated volume to each level's delta when the lower-timeframe close is greater than or equal to its open; subtract it otherwise. Add the absolute allocated volume to each level's total volume. The ratio is level delta divided by level total volume; it is undefined when total volume is zero. The grid is anchored at the open of the chart bar where it resets, with spacing set at reset from that chart bar's ATR or a configured price distance. Reset level volumes on each chart bar, or, in cumulative mode, at each configured reset-period boundary. Evaluate levels from the greatest grid level at or below the chart bar's low through the greatest grid level at or below its high.
Long: Any evaluated price-level delta ratio > 0 AND its absolute value >= the imbalance threshold.
Short: Any evaluated price-level delta ratio <= 0 AND its absolute value >= the imbalance threshold.
