Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low.
Calculation: Trend = close compared with the EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; bullish when close is greater, otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Structure score = round(min(100, aligned base + body ratio × aligned multiplier)) when trend is bullish and close >= open OR trend is bearish and close < open; otherwise round(min(100, misaligned base + body ratio × misaligned multiplier)).
Calculation: Proximity score = clamp(round(100 - distance multiplier × nearest distance), 0, 100), where nearest distance is the smaller absolute distance from close to the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high or pivot low divided by max(ATR, minimum ATR parameter). Use whichever pivot exists if only one exists; use a neutral proximity score if neither exists. Pivot left and right lookbacks are equal.
Calculation: Session score = configured score for the bar's opening hour in the America/New_York timezone and selected session mode; the continuous-session mode uses a constant score.
Calculation: Momentum score = round(body ratio × 100).
Calculation: Volatility score = 100 when ATR / SMA of ATR is between the lower and upper volatility bounds inclusive; round(100 × ratio / lower bound) below the lower bound; otherwise max(0, round(100 - (ratio - upper bound) × volatility penalty)). Use ratio 1 when the ATR SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. The ATR SMA uses a longer lookback than the ATR.
Calculation: Entry score = round(weighted average of structure, proximity, session, momentum, and volatility scores, with weights normalized by their sum).
Long: Entry score >= entry threshold AND close > the EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Entry score >= entry threshold AND close <= the EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
