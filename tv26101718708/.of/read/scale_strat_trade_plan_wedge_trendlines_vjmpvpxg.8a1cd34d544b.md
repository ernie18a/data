Calculation: Bar types = inside when high <= previous high AND low >= previous low; outside when high > previous high AND low < previous low; up when high > previous high AND low >= previous low; down when low < previous low AND high <= previous high.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = current bar is up AND (previous bar is down OR (previous bar is inside AND the bar before it is down or outside)). Bearish reversal = current bar is down AND (previous bar is up OR (previous bar is inside AND the bar before it is up or outside)).
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = the latest pivot high or low confirmed with equal left and right lookbacks. Update it on the confirmation bar before evaluating that bar's decisions.
Calculation: Pivot levels = from the last completed bar of a configurable anchor timeframe, pivot = (high + low + close) / 3, resistance 1 = 2 × pivot - low, resistance 2 = pivot + high - low, support 1 = 2 × pivot - high, support 2 = pivot - high + low. The anchor may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a lower timeframe use the last available completed bar.
Calculation: Near support = absolute distance from close to any of support 1, support 2, latest confirmed swing low, prior completed bar low of a higher timeframe, or pivot <= close × proximity parameter. Near resistance = the same comparison with resistance 1, resistance 2, latest confirmed swing high, prior completed bar high of that same higher timeframe, or pivot.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high. Bearish structure break = close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low. Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low. Bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high.
Calculation: Bullish gap zone = the interval from high two bars ago to current low when current low > high two bars ago; retain the latest such interval until replaced. Bearish gap zone = the interval from current high to low two bars ago when current high < low two bars ago; retain the latest such interval until replaced. Price is in a zone when close is between its endpoints, inclusive.
Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close - open; range = high - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low. Doji = body <= range × doji fraction. Hammer = lower wick >= body × wick ratio AND upper wick <= body. Shooting star = upper wick >= body × wick ratio AND lower wick <= body. Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close. Bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA confirmation for long = a shorter EMA of close crossed above a longer EMA within a lookback of bars of that higher timeframe OR chart close > that longer EMA AND chart low <= that EMA × (1 + proximity parameter). Short confirmation = the shorter EMA crossed below the longer EMA within the same lookback OR chart close < that longer EMA AND chart high >= that EMA × (1 - proximity parameter). Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Long score = sum of one point each for (near support OR near resistance), (doji OR hammer OR bullish engulfing), (bullish reversal OR hammer OR all three successively higher-timeframe closes > their opens), (bullish sweep OR bullish structure break OR close in bullish gap zone), and long higher-timeframe EMA confirmation. Short score = sum of one point each for (near support OR near resistance), (doji OR shooting star OR bearish engulfing), (bearish reversal OR shooting star OR all three successively higher-timeframe closes <= their opens), (bearish sweep OR bearish structure break OR close in bearish gap zone), and short higher-timeframe EMA confirmation. Use the bars still forming for higher-timeframe comparisons.
Calculation: Long noise filter = ATR / close <= volatility limit AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND close > open AND high - close <= range × maximum upper-wick fraction AND close - open >= range × minimum body fraction. Short noise filter = ATR / close <= volatility limit AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND close < open AND close - low <= range × maximum lower-wick fraction AND open - close >= range × minimum body fraction.
Calculation: Long confirmation = shorter EMA > longer EMA OR close > longer EMA on a configurable other timeframe; short confirmation = shorter EMA <= longer EMA AND close <= longer EMA on that timeframe. Use its bar still forming if it is higher than the chart timeframe, or its last available bar if lower.
Calculation: Long plan stop = max(trigger-bar low - stop-buffer multiplier × trigger-bar ATR, trigger-bar high - stop-cap multiplier × trigger-bar ATR); long target = trigger-bar high + target multiplier × trigger-bar ATR. Short plan stop = min(trigger-bar high + stop-buffer multiplier × trigger-bar ATR, trigger-bar low + stop-cap multiplier × trigger-bar ATR); short target = trigger-bar low - target multiplier × trigger-bar ATR. Freeze these levels when a full-plan trigger occurs; if locking is enabled, replace a side's levels only when that side is unlocked. Unlock long when close <= long stop OR close - trigger-bar high > extended-target multiplier × current ATR; unlock short when close >= short stop OR trigger-bar low - close > extended-target multiplier × current ATR. The extended-target multiplier is greater than the target multiplier.
Long: Bullish reversal.
Short: Bearish reversal.

Short: (reversal mode enabled AND bearish reversal OR reversal mode disabled AND short score >= full-plan threshold AND previous short score < full-plan threshold) AND short score >= full-plan threshold AND (noise filter disabled OR short noise filter) AND (level filter disabled OR near resistance OR bearish structure break AND NOT near support) AND (confirmation filter disabled OR short confirmation) AND (late-entry filter disabled OR local session hour < cutoff hour) AND high - low >= minimum-range multiplier × ATR.
Long: Bullish reversal AND long score >= watch threshold AND long score < full-plan threshold AND (noise filter disabled OR long noise filter) AND (level filter disabled OR near support OR bullish structure break AND NOT near resistance).
Short: Bearish reversal AND short score >= watch threshold AND short score < full-plan threshold AND (noise filter disabled OR short noise filter) AND (level filter disabled OR near resistance OR bearish structure break AND NOT near support).
Long: Shorter chart-timeframe EMA > longer chart-timeframe EMA AND close > longer chart-timeframe EMA.
Short: Shorter chart-timeframe EMA < longer chart-timeframe EMA AND close < longer chart-timeframe EMA.
Long: Close > open on any displayed other timeframe, using its forming bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available bar when lower.
Short: Close <= open on any displayed other timeframe, using its forming bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available bar when lower.
Long: Shorter EMA > longer EMA on any displayed other timeframe, using its forming bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available bar when lower.
Short: Shorter EMA <= longer EMA on any displayed other timeframe, using its forming bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available bar when lower.
Long: Shorter EMA > longer EMA OR close > longer EMA on any displayed confirmation timeframe, using its forming bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available bar when lower.
Short: Shorter EMA <= longer EMA AND close <= longer EMA on any displayed confirmation timeframe, using its forming bar when higher than the chart timeframe and its last available bar when lower.
Exit: long, high >= frozen long target AND previous high < frozen long target OR low <= frozen long stop AND previous low > frozen long stop.
Exit: short, low <= frozen short target AND previous low > frozen short target OR high >= frozen short stop AND previous high < frozen short stop.
