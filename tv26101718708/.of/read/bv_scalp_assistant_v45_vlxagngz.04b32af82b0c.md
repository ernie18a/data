Calculation: Use chart-bar OHLC and ATR throughout. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the same length as the chart EMA; with lookahead off, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars, updating on its closing chart bar, and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Build range = highest high minus lowest low over the build lookback; build close position = (close minus build-range low) / build range, or 0.5 when the range is zero. EMA near build = EMA within the build-range boundaries extended by an ATR-based margin.
Calculation: Upper barrier = nearest level strictly above close among the two most recently confirmed symmetric pivot highs, the highest high over the barrier lookback ending before the build window, and, if enabled, the next round-number level. Lower barrier = nearest corresponding pivot low, prior lowest low, or round-number level strictly below close. Round-number spacing and price-unit conversion are parameters. Barrier near = its distance from the corresponding build-range boundary is at most a price-distance threshold. Room = rounded distance from close to the corresponding barrier meets a minimum, or that barrier is near; room is sufficient if no corresponding barrier exists.
Calculation: Weighted overlap = weighted mean, over adjacent pairs in the build window, of their high-low intersection divided by the larger bar range, using zero when both ranges are zero; weights decrease linearly with age.
Calculation: Strong compression = three successive build-window segments have strictly decreasing high-low ranges, OR the newer half has a smaller range than the older half AND mean bar range in the build window is less than in the preceding build-length window. Weak compression = no strong compression AND at least one of: the regression slope of lows is positive, the regression slope of highs is negative, the newer half-range is smaller, or the build range is smaller than in the preceding build-length window. Segment lengths and offsets follow the build lookback, with minimum segment lengths as parameters.
Calculation: Build score = sum of configurable weights for: build range at most an ATR multiple; weighted overlap at least its threshold; count of bars whose range is below their contemporaneous ATR multiple at least build lookback minus allowed noise count; EMA near build; strong compression, otherwise weak compression at a smaller weight; either barrier near; and noise quality. Noise quality is always satisfied because every build-window bar lies inside the build range extended by the nonnegative noise margin. Strong compression and overlap have equal weights; narrow range, small bars, and EMA proximity have equal smaller weights; barrier proximity and noise quality have equal still smaller weights.
Calculation: Long direction score = weighted sum of: chart EMA change over a slope lookback at least the negative ATR-based slope threshold; close at or above EMA OR EMA near build; positive low-regression slope OR newer half-range smaller than older half-range; build close position at least its long score threshold; sum of lower wicks at least sum of upper wicks in the build window; and upper room AND mean bearish body size at most a fraction of mean absolute body size, with the last body condition satisfied when mean body size is zero. Subtract a configurable penalty, floored at zero, when the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and higher-timeframe EMA is at or above chart EMA. The slope and compression components have equal weights; EMA position and close position have equal smaller weights; wick and room components have equal still smaller weights.
Calculation: Short direction score = the long score mirrored: EMA change at most the positive slope threshold; close at or below EMA OR EMA near build; negative high-regression slope OR newer half-range smaller; build close position at most its short score threshold; upper-wick sum at least lower-wick sum; and lower room AND mean bullish body size at most the specified fraction of mean absolute body size, with the body condition satisfied when mean body size is zero. Apply the same weights and subtract the penalty when enabled and higher-timeframe EMA is at or below chart EMA. The long score's close-position threshold is below the short score's close-position threshold.
Calculation: Range market = range filter enabled AND high-low range over its lookback at most an ATR multiple AND absolute chart EMA change over that lookback at most an ATR multiple AND the number of close/EMA crosses over that lookback at least a threshold. Range position = close's fraction of that high-low range, or 0.5 when the range is zero.
Calculation: A strong bullish bar has close above open, high-low range above its contemporaneous ATR multiple, and absolute body divided by range at least a threshold; a strong bearish bar has the same tests with close below open. A zero-range bar is not strong.
Long: Build score at least its threshold AND long direction score at least its threshold and greater than short direction score by at least the normal gap AND, in a range market, the score gap meets the range gap and range position is at least the upper-zone threshold AND (EMA near build OR upper barrier near) AND build close position at least its long entry threshold AND upper room is sufficient AND absolute close-to-EMA distance at most an ATR multiple AND close minus the lowest low over the surge lookback at most an ATR multiple AND no strong bullish bar in the power-bar lookback AND, if the session filter is enabled, the chart-bar opening time is in either configured session AND more than the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the previous long decision.
Short: Build score at least its threshold AND short direction score at least its threshold and greater than long direction score by at least the normal gap AND, in a range market, the score gap meets the range gap and range position is at most the lower-zone threshold AND (EMA near build OR lower barrier near) AND build close position at most its short entry threshold AND lower room is sufficient AND absolute close-to-EMA distance at most an ATR multiple AND highest high over the surge lookback minus close at most an ATR multiple AND no strong bearish bar in the power-bar lookback AND, if the session filter is enabled, the chart-bar opening time is in either configured session AND more than the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since the previous short decision.
