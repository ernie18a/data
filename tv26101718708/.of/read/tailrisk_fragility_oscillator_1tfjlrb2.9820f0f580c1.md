Calculation: Returns = log(price / previous price) or price minus previous price; realized mode uses lower-timeframe closes and the last completed lower-timeframe bar, otherwise use the selected chart-timeframe price.
Calculation: Return skewness and excess kurtosis = for each bar, subtract the rolling mean return from its return; take rolling means of the squared, cubed, and fourth-power residual series as m2, m3, and m4. Skewness = m3 / m2^(3/2) and excess kurtosis = m4 / m2^2 - 3; use zero when the respective denominator is zero. In realized mode, calculate these on the lower timeframe and use the last completed lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Standardized skewness = either the skewness z-score using rolling mean and standard deviation, or (skewness - rolling median skewness) / (1.4826 times the rolling median of the series of absolute differences between skewness and its contemporaneous rolling median); use zero when the denominator is zero or negligible. Optionally use the median of three such scores with shorter, medium, and longer normalization windows, where the shorter window is half the medium window, subject to a minimum length, and the longer window is twice the medium window.
Calculation: Skew wave = 100 times tanh(0.33 times standardized skewness), with the tanh input bounded to prevent overflow.
Calculation: Fat tails = percentile rank of excess kurtosis over its chart-timeframe lookback exceeds its threshold.
Calculation: Rising variance = correlation of rolling variance of chart-timeframe returns with bar index over a separate trend lookback exceeds its threshold; use the moment lookback for the variance.
Long: Fat tails AND skew wave >= 0.
Short: Fat tails AND skew wave < 0.
Long: Fat tails AND skew wave > positive extreme threshold AND previous bar did not have both fat tails and skew wave > positive extreme threshold AND (variance confirmation disabled OR rising variance).
Short: Fat tails AND skew wave < negative extreme threshold AND previous bar did not have both fat tails and skew wave < negative extreme threshold AND (variance confirmation disabled OR rising variance).
