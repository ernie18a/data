Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low over either a configured session window or the first configured number of chart bars of each exchange day; lock it on the first bar outside that range-building period. In automatic mode, choose the method according to the chart timeframe. Reset breakout and retest state when a new opening range begins.
Calculation: Tracking eligibility = the opening range is locked, its high minus its low exceeds five minimum price increments, and the current bar is outside the range-building period and within the configured tracking session; in first-bars-of-day mode, tracking instead starts after those bars and continues for the rest of the day. Session windows use a configured timezone.
Calculation: Safe ATR = the greater of the current ATR and one minimum price increment; substitute current true range when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Breakout state = record only the first eligible breakout after each opening-range reset, its direction, bar index, and broken boundary. A retest remains unresolved until a ready, invalidation, or expiration event; evaluate all three events before updating this state. Invalidation requires an eligible bar after the breakout with close below the broken high minus the configured invalidation buffer times safe ATR for an upward breakout, or above the broken low plus that buffer for a downward breakout. Expiration requires an eligible bar whose number of bars since breakout exceeds the configured retest window.
Calculation: Retest score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: 18 for a pocket touch; 24 for a touch with a close holding the broken boundary, otherwise 10 for a touch; 18 × clamp(1 − bars since breakout / retest window, 0, 1); 14 × clamp(relative volume / 1.6, 0, 1), or 11 when volume scoring is disabled; 12 × clamp(1 − abs(opening range size / safe ATR − 1) / 1.5, 0, 1); and 14 × clamp(target room / 1.2, 0, 1). Relative volume is current volume divided by its simple moving average, or 1 when that average is unavailable or nonpositive. The pocket is the broken boundary plus or minus the configured tolerance times current safe ATR; a touch requires high at or above its bottom and low at or below its top. Target room is the distance from close to the first target in the breakout direction divided by current safe ATR; the first target is the broken boundary plus or minus the opening range size times a configured target multiplier. The required score is the configured minimum adjusted by the configured confirmation mode and clamped to 1–100.
Long: Tracking eligibility is true AND no breakout has yet been recorded for the current opening range AND close > opening-range high + configured breakout buffer × safe ATR.
Short: Tracking eligibility is true AND no breakout has yet been recorded for the current opening range AND close < opening-range low − configured breakout buffer × safe ATR.
Long: Tracking eligibility is true AND the recorded breakout is upward AND the retest was unresolved before this bar AND bar index > breakout bar index AND low <= broken boundary + configured tolerance × safe ATR AND high >= broken boundary − configured tolerance × safe ATR AND close >= broken boundary AND retest score >= required score.
Short: Tracking eligibility is true AND the recorded breakout is downward AND the retest was unresolved before this bar AND bar index > breakout bar index AND low <= broken boundary + configured tolerance × safe ATR AND high >= broken boundary − configured tolerance × safe ATR AND close <= broken boundary AND retest score >= required score.
