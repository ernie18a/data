Calculation: Main swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding and following bars over the main swing lookback
Calculation: Main swing low = a low less than the lows of the preceding and following bars over the main swing lookback
Calculation: Internal swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding and following bars over a shorter lookback than the main swing lookback
Calculation: Internal swing low = a low less than the lows of the preceding and following bars over a shorter lookback than the main swing lookback
Long: A new main swing high is higher than the previous main swing high AND structure is bullish OR a new main swing low is higher than the previous main swing low AND structure is bullish OR a new main swing high is higher than the previous main swing high AND structure is bearish OR a new internal swing high is higher than the previous internal swing high AND structure is bullish OR RSI crosses above 50 AND MACD histogram is positive
Short: A new main swing high is lower than the previous main swing high AND structure is bearish OR a new main swing low is lower than the previous main swing low AND structure is bearish OR a new main swing low is lower than the previous main swing low AND structure is bullish OR a new internal swing low is lower than the previous internal swing low AND structure is bearish OR RSI crosses below 50 AND MACD histogram is negative
