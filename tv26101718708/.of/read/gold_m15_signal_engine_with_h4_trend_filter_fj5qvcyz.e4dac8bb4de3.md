Calculation: Support and resistance levels = Confirm pivot lows and highs using equal left and right lookbacks. On confirmation, add a pivot low as support only if it is below the current close, or a pivot high as resistance only if it is above the current close. Reject a new level if its absolute distance from any existing level on the same side is less than close times a gap percentage. Keep separate capped lists, removing the oldest level when adding beyond the cap. Update levels before evaluating signals.
Calculation: Signal paths = Evaluate the EMA pullback, support/resistance reaction, and breakout paths in that order. Within the reaction path evaluate long before short; within the other paths evaluate long before short. Select only the first qualifying path, including its directional filters. A new signal replaces any active opposite-side signal. Evaluate exits before entries on each bar.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = Bars since the last emitted signal are at least the cooldown parameter AND no active signal has the candidate side. The higher-timeframe filter, when enabled, requires the last completed higher-timeframe close to be above its EMA for a long or below its EMA for a short.
Calculation: Initial stop = At entry, for a long subtract the smaller of the entry-bar range times a stop-range multiplier and entry price times a maximum-stop percentage from the signal base; for a short add it. The base is the entry-bar low for a long pullback or breakout, the entry-bar high for a short pullback or breakout, or the selected level for a support/resistance reaction.
Calculation: Targets = Fix all targets at entry. Target 1 is entry price plus or minus ATR times its multiplier, adding for long and subtracting for short. Target 2 is the nearest resistance strictly above target 1 for a long or nearest support strictly below target 1 for a short; if none exists, use entry price plus or minus ATR times a larger fallback multiplier. Target 3 is entry price plus or minus ATR times a still larger multiplier; if it is not beyond target 2, set it beyond target 2 by a fraction of the absolute distance from entry to target 2.
Calculation: Current stop = Initially the initial stop. After target 1 is reached, set it to entry price; after target 2 is reached, set it to target 1. Check the existing stop before targets on each bar, starting after the entry bar. If multiple targets are reached on a bar, process the furthest target reached and all earlier targets. Book one third of the original position at each of targets 1 and 2, and the remaining position at target 3, the stop, or expiry.
Long: Signal eligibility AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND ((shortest-period EMA > intermediate-period EMA AND close > longest-period EMA AND low <= shortest-period EMA AND close > shortest-period EMA AND high > low AND close - low >= candle-strength fraction times (high - low) AND RSI >= lower pullback bound AND RSI <= upper pullback bound) OR (an eligible support level < close AND low <= that level + close times reaction tolerance AND low >= that level - twice close times reaction tolerance AND close - low >= candle-strength fraction times (high - low) AND RSI < long reaction bound) OR (sufficient prior bars exist AND close > highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND high > low AND close - low >= breakout-strength fraction times (high - low) AND RSI < long breakout bound)) AND the long path is the first qualifying signal path.
Short: Signal eligibility AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND ((shortest-period EMA < intermediate-period EMA AND close < longest-period EMA AND high >= shortest-period EMA AND close < shortest-period EMA AND high > low AND high - close >= candle-strength fraction times (high - low) AND RSI >= lower pullback bound AND RSI <= upper pullback bound) OR (an eligible resistance level > close AND high >= that level - close times reaction tolerance AND high <= that level + twice close times reaction tolerance AND high - close >= candle-strength fraction times (high - low) AND RSI > short reaction bound) OR (sufficient prior bars exist AND close < lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars AND high > low AND high - close >= breakout-strength fraction times (high - low) AND RSI > short breakout bound)) AND the short path is the first qualifying signal path.
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND (low <= current stop OR high >= an unreached target) OR expiry enabled AND bars since entry >= expiry lookback.
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND (high >= current stop OR low <= an unreached target) OR expiry enabled AND bars since entry >= expiry lookback.
