Calculation: ATR percentile = (ATR - lowest ATR over its calibration lookback) / (highest ATR over that lookback - lowest ATR over that lookback), or a neutral value if the range is zero.
Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum RSI = RSI constructed from separate fast and slow EMAs of positive and negative close changes weighted by sqrt(volume / volume SMA). Blend the fast and slow EMAs using ATR percentile as the fast weight, or equal weights when adaptation is disabled. If blended negative momentum is zero, use a relative strength of zero.
Calculation: Directional divergence = on confirmation of a price pivot, compare it with the previous confirmed pivot of the same type. Bullish divergence requires a lower pivot low and higher values at the pivots for at least a parameterized number of volume-weighted momentum RSI, CCI, and stochastic oscillators. Bearish divergence requires a higher pivot high and lower oscillator values. Keep divergence active for a parameterized number of bars after confirmation.
Calculation: Directional confluence = a normalized weighted average of directional efficiency, ATR-normalized close momentum, ATR percentile, smoothed signed volume flow, and Fisher-transformed volume-weighted momentum RSI, each scaled and clamped to a common score range; add separate bonuses when momentum and signed volume flow agree with the direction and when matching divergence is active, then cap the result. Directional efficiency is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback. Signed volume flow is SMA(sign(close - open) × volume × abs(close - open) / (high - low + minimum price increment)), normalized by the highest absolute value of that SMA over a longer lookback. Fisher-transform the momentum RSI after mapping it to a bounded range around zero.
Calculation: Directional sweep rank = for bullish direction, compare the current low and close with the lowest low of a lookback ending on the previous bar; for bearish direction, compare the current high and close with the corresponding highest high. If price pierces the level and closes back across it, rank the penetration divided by current ATR against increasing thresholds; otherwise rank it zero. Override with the highest rank if both of the preceding two lows are below the current prior-low level and the current close is above it, or, for bearish direction, both preceding highs are above the current prior-high level and the current close is below it.
Calculation: Displacement quality = weighted sum of clamped body / ATR, body / bar range, presence of a directional fair value gap, volume / volume SMA, and directional sweep penetration / ATR. A bullish fair value gap is current low > high two bars ago; a bearish gap is current high < low two bars ago. Map the quality to ordered grades using increasing thresholds.
Calculation: Multitimeframe gate score = count of directional agreements among two close-versus-Hull-moving-average comparisons and three close-versus-earlier-close comparisons, each calculated on its own configured timeframe. The second Hull timeframe is higher than the first; the three return timeframes increase in length. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including one completed at the chart bar close, and the last available completed lower-timeframe bar. A close equal to its Hull moving average counts as bearish; an unchanged return agrees with neither direction.
Calculation: Displacement thresholds = configured ATR-body and body/range thresholds, or, when automatic calibration is enabled, bounded functions respectively of the SMA of total wick / body and of ATR percentile. Use a neutral wick/body value for zero-body bars.
Calculation: Qualified order block = the high and low of the most recent opposite-colored candle within a search lookback preceding a qualifying break bar. A bullish break bar has close > open, body >= ATR × its displacement threshold, body/range >= its displacement threshold, close > the latest confirmed pivot high, and close > a long-term close EMA; reverse these inequalities for bearish. Require the displacement quality grade to meet its minimum. When enabled, also require directional sweep rank and directional confluence to meet their respective minimums, multitimeframe gate score to meet its minimum, short-lookback volume SMA < long-lookback volume SMA × a threshold with the long SMA positive, and the recent highest-high minus lowest-low divided by ATR < a compression threshold. All filters are evaluated on the break bar. Pivots use equal left and right confirmation lengths.
Calculation: Active order blocks = qualified blocks that have not been mitigated, have not exceeded their age limit measured from the opposite candle, and have not been invalidated by two consecutive closes below the bullish block low or above the bearish block high. Process newest blocks first; after mitigation and invalidation processing, discard the oldest unmitigated blocks until their count is at most the configured maximum. Test mitigation before expiry and invalidation, including on the creation bar.
Calculation: Entry level = for a bullish block, block high minus a parameterized fraction of its height; for a bearish block, block low plus that fraction of its height.
Calculation: Stop = block low minus entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier for a long, or block high plus that amount for a short. Optionally scale the multiplier by a bounded linear function of entry-bar ATR percentile. Risk = abs(entry level - stop). Target levels = entry level plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, increasing parameterized multiples of risk. Fix these levels at entry.
Long: bullish qualified order block is created.
Short: bearish qualified order block is created.
Long: no trade is open AND an active bullish order block exists AND low <= its entry level AND close > its low.
Short: no trade is open AND an active bearish order block exists AND high >= its entry level AND close < its high.
Long: volume-weighted momentum RSI > its neutral level.
Short: volume-weighted momentum RSI <= its neutral level.
Long: bullish directional divergence is active.
Short: bearish directional divergence is active AND bullish directional divergence is not active.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop OR high >= a fixed target level not previously reached; if stop and targets are reached on the same bar, process the stop first. The position remains open after intermediate targets and closes at the final target or stop.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop OR low <= a fixed target level not previously reached; if stop and targets are reached on the same bar, process the stop first. The position remains open after intermediate targets and closes at the final target or stop.
