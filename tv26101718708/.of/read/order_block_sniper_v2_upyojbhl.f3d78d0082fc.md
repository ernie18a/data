Calculation: Bullish BOS = close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high, after an up candle whose body is at least the minimum ATR multiple and minimum fraction of its high-low range; bearish BOS is the inverse using the latest confirmed swing low and a down candle.
Calculation: Order block = the high-low range of the nearest opposite-color candle within the lookback before the BOS.
Calculation: Sweep = bullish when low pierces the prior 20-bar low and close recovers above it; bearish when high pierces the prior 20-bar high and close falls below it. Classify by wick penetration in ATR units as minor, major, or flush; classify as grind when at least two preceding bars also pierced the level and the current close recovers beyond it.
Calculation: Directional confluence = normalized weighted average of directional efficiency (absolute net close change divided by the sum of absolute close changes), ATR-scaled momentum, ATR percentile within its lookback, normalized signed volume-weighted candle flow, and volume-weighted momentum score; add directional bonuses for momentum, flow, and an active matching pivot divergence, capped at 100.
Calculation: Order-block grade = score from body size relative to ATR (30%), body-to-range ratio (25%), presence of a three-bar gap (20%), volume relative to its average (15%), and sweep depth in ATR units (10%); grades are A+ at 85+, A at 70+, B at 55+, otherwise C.
Long: bullish BOS AND close > 200-bar EMA AND bullish multi-timeframe gate score >= minimum AND bullish sweep type >= minimum AND recent average volume < longer volume average * vacuum threshold AND recent high-low range / ATR < compression limit AND bullish directional confluence >= minimum AND order-block grade >= minimum AND price touches the long entry level (OB top - OB height * entry fraction) with close > OB bottom AND no trade is open; use the nearest eligible, unexpired, non-invalidated bullish order block, subject to the active-order-block limit. The multi-timeframe gate scores two configured HMA comparisons and three configured five-bar close-change signs against the trade direction; use aggregate bars from configured higher or lower timeframes relative to the chart, using the latest available bar (forming in real time, completed historically).
Short: bearish BOS AND close < 200-bar EMA AND bearish multi-timeframe gate score >= minimum AND bearish sweep type >= minimum AND recent average volume < longer volume average * vacuum threshold AND recent high-low range / ATR < compression limit AND bearish directional confluence >= minimum AND order-block grade >= minimum AND price touches the short entry level (OB bottom + OB height * entry fraction) with close < OB top AND no trade is open; use the nearest eligible, unexpired, non-invalidated bearish order block, subject to the active-order-block limit. The multi-timeframe gate scores two configured HMA comparisons and three configured five-bar close-change signs against the trade direction; use aggregate bars from configured higher or lower timeframes relative to the chart, using the latest available bar (forming in real time, completed historically).
Exit: long, low <= stop (OB bottom - ATR * effective stop multiplier) OR high >= third profit target.
Exit: short, high >= stop (OB top + ATR * effective stop multiplier) OR low <= third profit target.
