Calculation: Enabled MA bundle = the configured moving averages of the selected OHLC price source; faster and slower averages are ordered by configured horizon rank or, when ordering by length, shorter and longer lookbacks. Bundle high/low = highest/lowest enabled MA; centroid = their mean. Fast/slow centroids = means of the configured fast and slow cohorts.
Calculation: Pair-order score = weighted mean of eligible fast-minus-slow MA differences after configured normalization; classify each difference as positive, negative, or tied using the configured separation threshold. Slope coherence = 100 × sum of normalized MA changes over the configured slope lookback / sum of their absolute values. Cohort spread = normalized fast-centroid minus slow-centroid. Bias score = 0.45 × pair-order score + 0.30 × slope coherence + 0.25 × clamp(100 × cohort spread / configured cohort scale, −100, 100). Bias is positive when the score is positive and at least the configured bias threshold, negative when it is negative and at most the negative threshold, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Structure is ready only with valid distinct fast and slow cohorts, eligible MA pairs, complete MA and slope data, valid normalization, sufficient configured warm-up and history, and available width-rank and reorder-rate values. Directional order and slope hold when pair-order score and slope coherence respectively meet their configured direction-aligned thresholds. Compression requires ready structure, width rank and slope energy at or below configured maxima, absolute pair-order score at or below its configured maximum, and reorder rate below its configured maximum. Release requires non-neutral bias, direction-aligned pair order above its configured minimum, width expansion over the configured motion lookback (including its geometric component when required), aligned centroid acceleration, and compression within the configured recent-origin window. Pullback requires non-neutral bias, close inside the bundle, direction-aligned pair order and slow-cohort slope coherence above their configured hold floors, and no release. Expansion requires non-neutral bias, directional order and slope, close on the bias side of the centroid, and neither release nor pullback.
Calculation: Enabled higher-timeframe bias = the same bias calculation on the preceding completed bar of each enabled strictly higher timeframe. Full alignment = ready local structure with non-neutral bias matching every enabled higher-timeframe bias.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, structure enters release with positive bias and a known state entry; OR structure enters expansion with positive bias and a known state entry; OR an active positive directional cycle first touches the prior completed bundle while bias remains positive; OR, after that touch on an earlier bar, close crosses upward through the reclaim boundary, positive bias remains, and pair-order score meets its positive pullback hold floor; OR full alignment becomes positive; OR close first reaches the configured positive ATR distance above the fast-cohort centroid in positive bias direction.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, structure enters release with negative bias and a known state entry; OR structure enters expansion with negative bias and a known state entry; OR an active negative directional cycle first touches the prior completed bundle while bias remains negative; OR, after that touch on an earlier bar, close crosses downward through the reclaim boundary, negative bias remains, and pair-order score meets its negative pullback hold floor; OR full alignment becomes negative; OR close first reaches the configured positive ATR distance below the fast-cohort centroid in negative bias direction.
Exit: Long, an active positive directional cycle has opposite (negative) bias OR close is below the prior completed slow-cohort centroid and pair-order score is below the configured failure floor or slow-cohort slope coherence meets its negative failure threshold.
Exit: Short, an active negative directional cycle has opposite (positive) bias OR close is above the prior completed slow-cohort centroid and pair-order score is above the negative failure floor or slow-cohort slope coherence meets its positive failure threshold.
