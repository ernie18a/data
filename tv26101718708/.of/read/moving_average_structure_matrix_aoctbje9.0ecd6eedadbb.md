Calculation: MA ensemble = unique enabled moving averages of a selectable OHLC-derived source, each with a selectable type, length and analytical horizon rank; divide them into fast and slow cohorts by rank or length. Bundle high and low are the highest and lowest MA; centroid and cohort centroids are their respective arithmetic means.
Calculation: Normalized distance = distance divided by ATR or rolling source standard deviation, or 100 times distance divided by the absolute ensemble centroid, according to the selected normalization.
Calculation: Pair order = for each pair with unequal horizon rank, or unequal length when ordered by length, assign +1, -1 or 0 according as normalized faster-minus-slower MA distance is above, below the negative of, or within the separation threshold. Weight each vote by horizon distance raised to a selectable power, where horizon distance is the absolute rank difference or 1 plus the absolute log length ratio. Order coherence is 100 times weighted vote sum divided by total pair weight. A reorder occurs when a pair's current nonzero vote differs from its last nonzero vote; reorder rate is 100 times reorders over the lookback divided by eligible pair count and lookback length.
Calculation: Slope = normalized change in an MA over the slope lookback. Slope coherence is 100 times the sum of slopes divided by the sum of their absolute values, or zero when the denominator is zero; slow-cohort slope coherence uses only slow-cohort MAs. Slope energy is the mean absolute slope divided by its scale and multiplied by 100, capped at 100. Centroid acceleration is the normalized centroid change over the slope lookback minus that change at the deceleration lookback.
Calculation: Bias composite = 0.45 times order coherence + 0.30 times slope coherence + 0.25 times the normalized fast-minus-slow cohort centroid spread divided by its scale and multiplied by 100, capped between -100 and 100. Bias is +1 when the composite is positive and at least the bias threshold, -1 when negative and at most the negative bias threshold, and otherwise zero.
Calculation: Width = normalized bundle-high-minus-bundle-low distance; width rank is its rolling percentile rank. Width change is current width minus width at the motion lookback. Geometric width change is the change in unnormalized bundle width over that lookback normalized using the current denominator.
Calculation: Compression = width rank at or below its threshold AND slope energy at or below its threshold AND absolute order coherence at or below its threshold AND reorder rate below its threshold. Release = nonzero bias AND compression on an earlier bar within the origin lookback AND order coherence times bias at or above the release-order threshold AND width change at or above the release-width threshold AND, if the geometric-width guard is enabled, geometric width change at or above that threshold AND centroid acceleration times bias at or above its threshold.
Calculation: Pullback = nonzero bias AND close inside the bundle inclusive AND order coherence times bias at or above the pullback-order threshold AND slow-cohort slope coherence times bias at or above its hold threshold AND no release. Deceleration = nonzero bias AND order coherence times bias at or above the pullback-order threshold AND either slope energy or absolute slope coherence has fallen by its respective threshold since the deceleration lookback, OR width change is at or below the negative contraction threshold with geometric width change also meeting that condition if the geometric-width guard is enabled; AND no release AND no pullback. Expansion = nonzero bias AND order coherence times bias at or above the directional-order threshold AND slope coherence times bias at or above the directional-slope threshold AND (close minus centroid) times bias at or above zero AND no release AND no pullback AND no deceleration. Resolve phase in priority order: compression, release, pullback, deceleration, expansion, transition.
Calculation: State entry = the current bias and phase have remained unchanged for the configured state-entry bar count after a known change from a previous ready state. Evaluate structure only when all unique MAs, slopes, normalization and required lookback history are available, both cohorts and at least one eligible pair exist, and the configured warm-up is complete.
Calculation: Directional cycle = start or replace a cycle with the current nonzero bias on a release state entry; on an expansion state entry start one only if no cycle exists, its direction differs, or its reclaim has occurred. Before starting a new cycle, end the old cycle on a later bar if its age exceeds the cycle limit or its bias reverses or both (close minus the previous bar's slow-cohort centroid) times cycle direction is negative and either order coherence times cycle direction is below the failure-order floor or slow-cohort slope coherence times cycle direction is at or below the negative failure-slope threshold. Reset the cycle if structure becomes unavailable.
Calculation: First touch = the first bar after cycle start on which low is at or below the previous bar's bundle high AND high is at or above the previous bar's bundle low AND bias equals cycle direction; save that previous bundle. Reclaim checkpoint = the first price tick strictly beyond the saved bundle high plus the configured tick clearance for an upward cycle, or strictly beyond the saved bundle low minus that clearance for a downward cycle; alternatively use the immediately previous bar's bundle in place of the saved bundle. Tick size and clearance are parameters. A reclaim occurs at most once per cycle, on a bar after first touch, when bias equals cycle direction AND order coherence times cycle direction is at or above the pullback-order threshold AND close crosses the current checkpoint in the cycle direction, using the previous close and current checkpoint for the crossing comparison.

Short: Bias composite < 0 AND bias composite <= negative bias threshold.
Long: Release or expansion state entry AND bias = +1.
Short: Release or expansion state entry AND bias = -1.
Long: First touch or reclaim AND cycle direction = +1.
Short: First touch or reclaim AND cycle direction = -1.

Short: An upward directional cycle ends because bias reverses or because close is below the previous slow-cohort centroid AND (order coherence is below the failure-order floor OR slow-cohort slope coherence is at or below the negative failure-slope threshold).
