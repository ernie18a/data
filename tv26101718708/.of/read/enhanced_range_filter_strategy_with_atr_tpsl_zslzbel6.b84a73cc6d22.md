Calculation: Range filter = recursively retain the prior filter when the source is within the smoothed range; otherwise set it to source minus the smoothed range when source is above the prior filter, or source plus the smoothed range when source is below it. Smoothed range = EMA of EMA of absolute source changes, using the sampling period for the first EMA and twice that period minus one for the second, multiplied by a range multiplier.
Calculation: Upward and downward counters = upward counter increments when the range filter rises and resets to zero when it falls; downward counter increments when the range filter falls and resets to zero when it rises. Either counter carries forward when the filter is unchanged.
Calculation: Prior breakout direction = on each bar, set to long when close is above the range filter and the upward counter is positive; otherwise set to short when close is below the range filter and the downward counter is positive; otherwise carry forward the prior direction.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by (highest high over the lookback minus lowest low over the lookback, divided by the lookback).
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times the stop multiplier; long target = signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times the target multiplier times the reward-to-risk multiplier. Short stop = signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times the stop multiplier; short target = signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times the target multiplier times the reward-to-risk multiplier.
Long: close above range filter AND upward counter positive AND (close above prior close OR close below prior close) AND prior breakout direction short AND volume at least its moving average times the volume multiplier AND RSI below its upper threshold AND shorter-period EMA above longer-period EMA AND ATR ratio at least the ranging threshold
Short: close below range filter AND downward counter positive AND (close below prior close OR close above prior close) AND prior breakout direction long AND volume at least its moving average times the volume multiplier AND RSI above its lower threshold AND shorter-period EMA below longer-period EMA AND ATR ratio at least the ranging threshold
Exit: long, low at or below long stop OR high at or above long target
Exit: short, high at or above short stop OR low at or below short target
