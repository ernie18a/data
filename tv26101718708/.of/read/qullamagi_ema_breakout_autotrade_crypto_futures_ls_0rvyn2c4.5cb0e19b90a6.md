Calculation: Fast EMA, slow EMA, and medium SMA = moving averages with shorter lookbacks in that order; strict mode also uses two longer SMAs, in increasing lookback order.
Calculation: Box high/low = highest high / lowest low over the box lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Tight box = box high minus box low is no greater than ATR times the configured multiplier.
Calculation: Pullback line = one of the fast EMA, slow EMA, or medium SMA.
Calculation: Pullback proximity = low (long) or high (short) is within the configured ATR-scaled tolerance of the pullback line.
Calculation: Initial stop = trailing moving average at entry, offset below it for long or above it for short by the configured percentage.
Calculation: Effective stop = trailing moving average offset below it for long or above it for short; after breakeven activation, use the tighter of this stop and the entry price adjusted by the configured breakeven offset; if the hard stop is enabled, use the tighter of that result and the entry price adjusted by the configured maximum-loss percentage.
Calculation: Initial risk = entry price minus initial stop for long; initial stop minus entry price for short.

Exit: Long, partial take-profit enabled AND not already scaled out AND initial risk > 0 AND close >= entry price + initial risk × configured reward multiple.
Exit: Short, partial take-profit enabled AND not already scaled out AND initial risk > 0 AND close <= entry price − initial risk × configured reward multiple.
Exit: Long, close < effective stop OR time exit enabled AND bars in trade >= configured maximum AND close < entry price.
Exit: Short, close > effective stop OR time exit enabled AND bars in trade >= configured maximum AND close > entry price.
