Calculation: Trend averages = two EMAs followed by three SMAs of close, with progressively longer lookbacks; call them A through E from shortest to longest.
Calculation: Box high and box low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over the same lookback ending on the previous chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe close series = higher-timeframe close requested without lookahead and carried forward across chart bars; historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while realtime bars use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. Its SMA is calculated over chart bars of this carried-forward series.
Calculation: Pullback average = a selectable one of A, B or C; proximity bounds = pullback average × (1 ± proximity multiplier × ATR / close).
Calculation: Initial stop = the selected trailing EMA or SMA of close on the first bar with an open position, multiplied by (1 − stop buffer percentage) for a long or (1 + stop buffer percentage) for a short. Initial risk = entry price − initial stop for a long, or initial stop − entry price for a short.
Calculation: Effective stop = current trailing EMA or SMA of close multiplied by (1 − stop buffer percentage) for a long or (1 + stop buffer percentage) for a short; if breakeven is enabled and the high since entry has reached entry price + initial risk × activation multiple for a long, or the low since entry has reached entry price − initial risk × activation multiple for a short, take the maximum of that stop and entry price × (1 + breakeven offset percentage) for a long, or the minimum of that stop and entry price × (1 − breakeven offset percentage) for a short; if the hard stop is enabled, also take the maximum with entry price × (1 − hard-stop percentage) for a long, or the minimum with entry price × (1 + hard-stop percentage) for a short.
Long: Long trading enabled AND (current position is flat OR previous bar's position was flat) AND datetime is within the enabled date limits AND bars since the most recent transition to flat > cooldown, or no such transition exists or cooldown is disabled AND (ADX filter disabled OR long minimum ADX ≤ ADX ≤ maximum ADX) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < long RSI limit) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close series > its SMA) AND (wide-candle filter disabled OR high − low ≤ ATR × range multiplier) AND ((strict mode AND (perfect-order filter disabled OR close > A > B > C > D > E) AND (slope filter disabled OR each of A, B, C, D and E > its previous value) AND (box filter disabled OR (box high − box low ≤ ATR × box multiplier AND (hold filter disabled OR box low > previous C))) AND ((close-confirmation enabled AND close > box high AND previous close ≤ previous box high) OR (close-confirmation disabled AND high > box high AND previous close ≤ current box high)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × strict breakout multiplier)) OR (loose mode AND close > A > B > C AND high > box high AND previous close ≤ current box high AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × loose breakout multiplier)) OR (pullback enabled AND ((strict mode AND (perfect-order filter disabled OR A > B > C > D > E)) OR (loose mode AND A > B > C)) AND low ≥ lower proximity bound AND low ≤ upper proximity bound AND close > pullback average AND (bounce filter disabled OR close > open) AND highest high over the pullback lookback including the current bar > current box high AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × pullback multiplier))).
Short: Short trading enabled AND (current position is flat OR previous bar's position was flat) AND datetime is within the enabled date limits AND bars since the most recent transition to flat > cooldown, or no such transition exists or cooldown is disabled AND (ADX filter disabled OR short minimum ADX ≤ ADX ≤ maximum ADX) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > short RSI limit) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close series < its SMA) AND (short higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close series < its SMA) AND (longest-average filter disabled OR close < E) AND each of the specified number of immediately preceding bars has close < open AND (wide-candle filter disabled OR high − low ≤ ATR × range multiplier) AND ((strict mode AND (perfect-order filter disabled OR close < A < B < C < D < E) AND (slope filter disabled OR each of A, B, C, D and E < its previous value) AND (box filter disabled OR (box high − box low ≤ ATR × box multiplier AND (hold filter disabled OR box low > previous C))) AND ((close-confirmation enabled AND close < box low AND previous close ≥ previous box low) OR (close-confirmation disabled AND low < box low AND previous close ≥ current box low)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × strict breakout multiplier)) OR (loose mode AND close < A < B < C AND low < box low AND previous close ≥ current box low AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × loose breakout multiplier)) OR (pullback enabled AND ((strict mode AND (perfect-order filter disabled OR A < B < C < D < E)) OR (loose mode AND A < B < C)) AND high ≥ lower proximity bound AND high ≤ upper proximity bound AND close < pullback average AND (bounce filter disabled OR close < open) AND lowest low over the pullback lookback including the current bar < current box low AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × pullback multiplier))).
Exit: long, partial take profit enabled AND no previous partial take profit in this position AND initial risk > 0 AND close ≥ entry price + initial risk × take-profit multiple; exit the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: short, partial take profit enabled AND no previous partial take profit in this position AND initial risk > 0 AND close ≤ entry price − initial risk × take-profit multiple; exit the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: long, close < effective stop OR (time exit enabled AND bars in position ≥ maximum trade duration AND close < entry price).
Exit: short, close > effective stop OR (time exit enabled AND bars in position ≥ maximum trade duration AND close > entry price).
