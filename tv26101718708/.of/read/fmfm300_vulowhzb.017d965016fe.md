Calculation: Pivot high = a confirmed local high, using equal left and right lookbacks; pivot low = a confirmed local low, using equal left and right lookbacks. Major pivots use a longer lookback than temporary pivots.
Calculation: Bullish order-block level = the lowest low from the latest recorded swing high through the bar that closes above that swing high; bearish order-block level = the highest high from the latest recorded swing low through the bar that closes below that swing low. A swing is recorded when the zigzag trend reverses, using the zigzag lookback.
Long: Supertrend changes from bearish to bullish
Long: A confirmed major pivot low occurs
Long: A confirmed temporary pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed temporary pivot low
Long: Close is at or above an active bullish order-block level and at or below that level plus ATR(14)
Short: Supertrend changes from bullish to bearish
Short: A confirmed major pivot high occurs
Short: A confirmed temporary pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed temporary pivot high
Short: Close is at or below an active bearish order-block level and at or above that level minus ATR(14)
