Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a pivot high or low identified using specified numbers of bars to its left and right; evaluate it on the bar that completes the right-hand confirmation window.
Calculation: RSI signal = RSI of the selected price source, either raw or smoothed with the selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA; if smoothing is disabled, use SMA when the smoothed signal is selected.
Calculation: Rounded pivot price = pivot price rounded to the nearest price tick.
Long: Price Supertrend is in an uptrend.
Short: Price Supertrend is in a downtrend.
Long: Price Supertrend changes from a downtrend to an uptrend.
Short: Price Supertrend changes from an uptrend to a downtrend.
Long: Price Supertrend is in an uptrend AND RSI signal crosses above the selected level.
Short: Price Supertrend is in a downtrend AND RSI signal crosses below the selected level.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low AND the price low at the current RSI pivot is lower than the price low at the preceding RSI pivot AND the number of bars since the bar immediately after the preceding pivot confirmation is within the inclusive selected range.
Short: A confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot high AND the price high at the current RSI pivot is higher than the price high at the preceding RSI pivot AND the number of bars since the bar immediately after the preceding pivot confirmation is within the inclusive selected range.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low has no preceding confirmed price pivot low OR its rounded price is greater than or equal to the preceding rounded pivot low when equal lows are not counted as breaks, or greater than the preceding rounded pivot low when equal lows are counted as breaks.
Long: A confirmed price pivot high has a preceding confirmed price pivot high AND its rounded price is greater than the preceding rounded pivot high, or greater than or equal to it when equal highs are counted as breaks.
Short: A confirmed price pivot low has a preceding confirmed price pivot low AND its rounded price is lower than the preceding rounded pivot low, or lower than or equal to it when equal lows are counted as breaks.
Short: A confirmed price pivot high has no preceding confirmed price pivot high OR its rounded price is lower than or equal to the preceding rounded pivot high when equal highs are not counted as breaks, or lower than the preceding rounded pivot high when equal highs are counted as breaks.
