Calculation: Source bars = chart-timeframe bars or bars from a higher timeframe; use each source bar only after it completes. Run independent zone inventories for multiple source timeframes.
Calculation: FVG zone = for three consecutive completed source bars, a bullish zone has lower boundary = first bar high and upper boundary = third bar low when third bar low > first bar high; a bearish zone has lower boundary = third bar high and upper boundary = first bar low when third bar high < first bar low. Retain only zones whose width >= the source-specific minimum. Store each zone independently and remove it when its formation time is outside its source-specific calendar-day or trading-session lookback.
Calculation: IFVG state = in confirmed mode, an existing bearish FVG becomes a bullish IFVG after a threshold number of consecutive completed source closes > its upper boundary; an existing bullish FVG becomes a bearish IFVG after a threshold number of consecutive completed source closes < its lower boundary. Update existing zones before creating new FVGs. Remove a bullish IFVG after a threshold number of consecutive subsequent source closes < its lower boundary, and a bearish IFVG after a threshold number of consecutive subsequent source closes > its upper boundary; invalidation can be disabled where configurable. In persistent mode, classify an original bearish FVG as bullish IFVG when chart close > upper boundary and as bearish FVG when chart close < lower boundary; classify an original bullish FVG as bearish IFVG when chart close < lower boundary and as bullish FVG when chart close > upper boundary. At or inside the boundaries retain the last classification. Persistent zones are removed only by lookback. Some source inventories contain only FVGs.
Calculation: First retest = the first chart bar whose low <= a bullish zone's upper boundary or whose high >= a bearish zone's lower boundary after that zone is created or changes classification. Reset the retest state on each classification change. On a source-timeframe chart, exclude the creation or classification bar; on a lower chart, a zone created or converted from the last completed higher-timeframe bar can be retested on the first chart bar of the new source period. A persistent-mode classification change excludes its current chart bar.
Long: A bullish FVG forms OR a zone becomes a bullish IFVG OR the first retest of an active bullish FVG or bullish IFVG occurs.
Short: A bearish FVG forms OR a zone becomes a bearish IFVG OR the first retest of an active bearish FVG or bearish IFVG occurs.
