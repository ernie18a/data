Calculation: FVG zone = for three consecutive completed source-timeframe bars, bullish [first bar high, third bar low] when third bar low > first bar high; bearish [third bar high, first bar low] when third bar high < first bar low. Source timeframe is equal to or higher than the chart timeframe; use the last completed source bar when it is higher.
Long: A bullish FVG forms with gap size at least the configured minimum and within the selected lookback OR a bearish FVG zone is converted to bullish IFVG after two consecutive confirmed source-timeframe closes above its upper boundary OR, in persistent classification mode, the current close is above the upper boundary of an original bearish FVG zone OR the current chart low reaches the upper boundary of an active bullish FVG or IFVG zone for the first time after its formation or classification.
Short: A bearish FVG forms with gap size at least the configured minimum and within the selected lookback OR a bullish FVG zone is converted to bearish IFVG after two consecutive confirmed source-timeframe closes below its lower boundary OR, in persistent classification mode, the current close is below the lower boundary of an original bullish FVG zone OR the current chart high reaches the lower boundary of an active bearish FVG or IFVG zone for the first time after its formation or classification.
