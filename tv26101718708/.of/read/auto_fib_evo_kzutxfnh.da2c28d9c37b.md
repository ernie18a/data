Calculation: Structural direction = Track the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed after an equal configurable number of bars on both sides. When a newly confirmed high occurs after the latest pivot low and their price difference is at least a configurable multiple of ATR at the high pivot, set direction to up. When a newly confirmed low occurs after the latest pivot high and their price difference is at least the same ATR multiple at the low pivot, set direction to down. Process a newly confirmed high before a newly confirmed low if both occur together. Otherwise retain the previous direction. Optionally restrict accepted directions to up or down: on the chart timeframe, ignore disallowed legs before updating direction; on a higher timeframe, calculate both directions and retain the latest accepted higher-timeframe direction. For a higher-timeframe calculation, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Structural direction = up
Short: Structural direction = down
