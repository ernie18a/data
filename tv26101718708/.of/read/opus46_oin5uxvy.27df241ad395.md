Calculation: Ribbon = shorter-period SMMA of the selected price source and longer-period SMMA of the same source; the default source is (high + low) / 2. Each SMMA starts with an SMA and thereafter equals (previous SMMA × (length − 1) + source) / length. Bearish regime means shorter SMMA < longer SMMA; bullish regime means shorter SMMA >= longer SMMA.
Calculation: Normalized spread = (shorter SMMA − longer SMMA) / ATR, or zero when ATR is zero. Spread z-score = (normalized spread − its rolling SMA) / its rolling standard deviation, or zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Penetration bands = prior-bar Donchian low minus ATR × lower multiplier, and prior-bar Donchian high plus ATR × upper multiplier. Maintain separate rolling collections of the most recent positive penetrations: (prior Donchian low − current low) / current ATR and (current high − prior Donchian high) / current ATR. Add the current penetration before calculating the current bands. Once a collection has the minimum sample count, its multiplier is its mean × sensitivity, clamped between minimum and maximum multipliers; otherwise use the initial multiplier. Higher-timeframe bands use the same construction on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Pressure z-score = rolling z-score of pressure, or zero when its standard deviation is zero. Pressure = percentage difference between close and cost basis, multiplied by volume / volume SMA, or by one when the volume SMA is zero. Cost basis is a weighted average of longer- and shorter-lookback rolling volume-weighted averages of (high + low + close) / 3; the shorter average has the greater weight. Use the typical price when a rolling volume sum is zero.
Calculation: Regime maturity = current regime age >= minimum age OR the maximum absolute normalized spread observed in the current regime >= minimum depth. Regime age starts at one on the first bar of a regime.
Calculation: Structural gate = for each side independently, an enabled mode selects chart-band touch, higher-timeframe-band touch, either touch, or both touches; the disabled mode passes. A lower touch is current low <= the selected lower band; an upper touch is current high >= the selected upper band.
Calculation: Cycle state = count executed buys and sells since the last cycle reset. Before processing orders, reset both counts and the remembered buy and sell prices and extremes when a raw buy trigger occurs after at least one cycle sell while a long position remains, or when the position is zero after a cycle sell. Evaluate the current bar's signal filters before this reset, but evaluate the buy-count limit as zero when the raw buy trigger will reset the cycle. A buy records its close and low and clears the remembered sell close and high; a sell records its close and high and clears the remembered buy low. Buy and sell bar timestamps persist across resets.
Calculation: Buy breaker = when enabled, halt buys once (average long entry price − close) / average long entry price >= halt threshold and resume once it <= the lower resume threshold; use zero drawdown without a long position. Reset the breaker on a raw-buy cycle reset.
Calculation: Sell throttle = rounded base cooldown × (1 + min(maximum multiplier − 1, max(0, bullish-regime spread z-score) × scale)); use zero in place of the spread z-score outside the bullish regime.
Long: On a completed chart bar, ((low <= chart lower penetration band AND shorter SMMA < longer SMMA AND (maturity gate disabled OR regime maturity) AND (pressure gate disabled OR minimum pressure z-score in the recent window <= negative capitulation threshold)) OR (z-score override enabled AND spread z-score <= buy threshold)) AND lower structural gate passes AND cycle buy count < buy limit AND buy breaker permits buying AND (buy cooldown disabled OR no prior buy bar OR bars since prior buy >= buy cooldown) AND (lower-low filter disabled OR no remembered buy low OR low < remembered buy low) AND (buy ATR separation disabled OR no remembered buy close OR absolute difference between close and remembered buy close >= buy separation multiplier × ATR).
Exit: long, on a completed chart bar, ((high >= chart upper penetration band AND shorter SMMA >= longer SMMA AND (higher-high filter disabled OR no remembered sell high OR high > remembered sell high) AND (sell ATR separation disabled OR no remembered sell close OR close − remembered sell close >= sell separation multiplier × ATR) AND (maturity gate disabled OR regime maturity) AND (fixed sell cooldown disabled OR no prior sell bar OR bars since prior sell >= fixed sell cooldown) AND (pressure gate disabled OR maximum pressure z-score in the recent window >= euphoria threshold)) OR (z-score override enabled AND spread z-score >= sell threshold)) AND upper structural gate passes AND cycle sell count < sell limit AND a long position exists AND (sell throttle disabled OR cycle sell count = 0 OR no prior sell bar OR bars since prior sell >= sell throttle).
Long: A price pivot low is confirmed AND its low < the preceding confirmed pivot low AND (normalized spread at that pivot > normalized spread at the preceding pivot low OR RSI of close at that pivot > RSI at the preceding pivot low).
Short: A price pivot high is confirmed AND its high > the preceding confirmed pivot high AND (normalized spread at that pivot < normalized spread at the preceding pivot high OR RSI of close at that pivot < RSI at the preceding pivot high).
