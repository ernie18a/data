Calculation: ATR-normalized spread = (short-lookback SMMA of HL2 − long-lookback SMMA of HL2) / ATR; the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: Spread z-score = rolling z-score of the ATR-normalized spread
Calculation: Pressure z-score = rolling z-score of ((close − composite rolling VWAP) / composite rolling VWAP × volume / rolling volume average); the composite VWAP weights a shorter lookback more heavily than a longer lookback
Calculation: Learned lower and upper bands = prior-bar Donchian low and high, respectively, extended outward by ATR times separate learned multipliers; each multiplier is a sensitivity-scaled rolling average of same-side wick penetration depths, clamped to bounds, with a fallback multiplier until enough samples exist
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed swing low below the prior swing low while either the ATR-normalized spread or close-based RSI at that pivot is above its value at the prior pivot
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a confirmed swing high above the prior swing high while either the ATR-normalized spread or close-based RSI at that pivot is below its value at the prior pivot
Long: Bullish divergence
Short: Bearish divergence
Long: ((low ≤ learned lower band AND short-lookback SMMA of HL2 < long-lookback SMMA of HL2 AND bearish-regime age ≥ minimum age OR bearish-regime peak absolute ATR-normalized spread ≥ minimum depth AND pressure z-score reached the negative capitulation threshold within the recent lookback) OR spread z-score ≤ negative buy override threshold) AND bar is confirmed AND (if enabled, low ≤ lower band calculated on the higher timeframe from its last completed bar) AND buy cooldown elapsed AND (if enabled, low is below the low at the prior buy) AND (if enabled, absolute distance from close to prior buy close ≥ ATR-based separation) AND buy capacity remains AND (if enabled, drawdown breaker is inactive)
Exit: long, total position quantity > 0 AND (sell trigger) AND sell capacity remains AND (first sell of cycle OR sell throttle interval elapsed), where sell trigger is ((high ≥ learned upper band AND short-lookback SMMA of HL2 ≥ long-lookback SMMA of HL2 AND (bullish-regime age ≥ minimum age OR bullish-regime peak absolute ATR-normalized spread ≥ minimum depth) AND pressure z-score reached the positive euphoria threshold within the recent lookback AND (if enabled, high exceeds the high at the prior sell) AND (if enabled, close minus prior sell close ≥ ATR-based separation) AND sell cooldown elapsed) OR spread z-score ≥ sell override threshold) AND (if enabled, high ≥ upper band calculated on the higher timeframe from its last completed bar); sell throttle interval increases with positive spread z-score in a bullish regime
