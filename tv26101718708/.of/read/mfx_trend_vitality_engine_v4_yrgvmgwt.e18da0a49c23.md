Calculation: Higher-timeframe inputs = OHLCV bars aggregated to a higher timeframe than the chart; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with each newly completed value made available on its closing chart bar.
Calculation: Trend oscillator = EMA((A − E) / (scale × D), smoothing length), where A is the SMA of (high + low + close) / 3, E is the EMA of A, and D is the EMA of abs(A − E), using the same EMA length for E and D; use zero when D is zero.
Calculation: Buyer/seller pressure = the average of Chaikin Money Flow and Money Flow Index, each independently normalized over a lookback as 100 × (value − lowest) / (highest − lowest) − 50; use zero for a zero range and zero for Chaikin Money Flow when its volume sum is zero.
Calculation: Wave history = for each positive or negative trend-oscillator wave, record its maximum absolute oscillator value, the sum of absolute oscillator values, the close at that maximum, and the pressure at that maximum; use absolute pressure for negative waves. A wave completes when the oscillator crosses zero into the opposite side.
Calculation: Side vitality = round and clamp to 0–100 a weighted sum of structural vitality and live vitality. Structural vitality is the clamped weighted sum of wave score and 100 minus the side's penalty. Once at least three same-side waves exist, wave score is the clamped weighted sum of the latest wave's peak oscillator divided by a scale, its area divided by the preceding wave's area, and its pressure divided by the preceding wave's pressure; clamp each ratio to its respective bounds and floor denominators. Before three waves exist, wave score is 100. Live vitality is a base plus weighted, bounded positive parts of directional oscillator and directional pressure after adding their respective offsets; directional values are positive for buyers and negated for sellers.
Calculation: Side penalty = initialize at zero and clamp to 0–100 each higher-timeframe bar. With at least three same-side waves, form an aging impulse from weighted comparisons of the latest wave against the third-latest: price advances in the side's direction AND peak oscillator decreases; area decreases; pressure decreases; and all three comparisons occur together. Add another weighted impulse when a newly completed wave's absolute efficiency divided by the preceding completed wave's absolute efficiency is below a threshold. Efficiency is the side-directed percentage change in the peak-oscillator close between the third-latest and latest waves divided by the floored sum of their and the intervening wave's areas. On a same-side wave completion add the impulse multiplied by a damage factor to the penalty; on other bars add the impulse multiplied by a leak factor and subtract a recovery amount.
Long: Higher-timeframe buyer/seller pressure >= 0
Short: Higher-timeframe buyer/seller pressure < 0
Long: Higher-timeframe trend oscillator crosses above its SMA AND trend oscillator < 0 AND seller vitality <= a threshold
Short: Higher-timeframe trend oscillator crosses below its SMA AND trend oscillator > 0 AND buyer vitality <= a threshold
