Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high greater than or equal to all highs in an equally sized left window and greater than all highs in an equally sized right window; confirm on the last bar of the right window.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low less than or equal to all lows in an equally sized left window and less than all lows in an equally sized right window; confirm on the last bar of the right window.
Calculation: Market structure = on confirmation, compare the new pivot with the preceding pivot of the same kind; retain higher high or lower high for a high, and higher low or lower low for a low. Equal highs count as lower highs and equal lows as lower lows. If both kinds confirm on the same bar, retain the high comparison.
Long: Confirmed pivot low.
Short: Confirmed pivot high.
Long: Market structure = higher high OR market structure = higher low.
Short: Market structure = lower high OR market structure = lower low.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR at entry * stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry * stop multiplier * reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry * stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR at entry * stop multiplier * reward-to-risk ratio.
