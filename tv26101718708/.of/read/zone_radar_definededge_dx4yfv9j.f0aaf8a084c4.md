Calculation: Active zone = On each completed bar with positive ATR, if no zone is active, the bar index is greater than the zone lookback, and the bar index is at least the last breakout bar index plus the cooldown, form a zone when the highest high minus the lowest low over the zone lookback is at most a width multiplier times current ATR AND the absolute difference between current close and the close one zone lookback ago is at most a drift multiplier times that range. Initially there is no zone and the cooldown boundary is zero. Set the zone top and bottom to that highest high and lowest low, and lock the current ATR. Starting on the next bar, test for a breakout before updating the zone. After a breakout, deactivate the zone and start the cooldown. Otherwise, expand the top to the maximum of its current value and the bar high and the bottom to the minimum of its current value and the bar low only if the expanded range is at most the width multiplier times the locked ATR; otherwise leave both edges unchanged.
Long: A zone is active AND ATR is positive AND close > zone top + breakout buffer multiplier times locked ATR.
Short: A zone is active AND ATR is positive AND close < zone bottom - breakout buffer multiplier times locked ATR.
