Calculation: Active zone = after the post-break cooldown, form when the highest high minus the lowest low over a lookback is at most a width multiple of ATR and the absolute difference between the current close and the close one lookback ago is at most a drift fraction of that range; set zone top and bottom to that window’s highest high and lowest low and lock ATR at formation. While active, test for a break before updating the zone; if there is no break, expand the top and bottom to include the bar’s high and low only if the resulting width is at most the width multiple of locked ATR. After a break, clear the zone and start the cooldown.
Long: Active zone AND close > zone top + break buffer × locked ATR
Short: Active zone AND close < zone bottom − break buffer × locked ATR
