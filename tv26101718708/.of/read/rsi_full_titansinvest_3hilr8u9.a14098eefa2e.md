Calculation: RSI moving average = SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or volume-weighted MA of RSI; the Bollinger Bands option uses SMA.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot = an RSI pivot high or low with equal left and right lookbacks, known only after the right lookback has elapsed. Compare consecutive confirmed pivots of the same type; their spacing is the number of bars between their confirmations minus one.
Long: For the independently selected buy conditions, combine with a chosen AND or OR: RSI > or < upper threshold, lower threshold, RSI midpoint, or RSI moving average; RSI moving average > or < upper or lower threshold; RSI crosses above or below upper threshold, lower threshold, RSI midpoint, or RSI moving average; RSI moving average crosses above or below upper or lower threshold; or either divergence condition below. Optionally treat each selected condition as true if it occurred within the selected validity lookback, including the current bar. With no selected conditions, AND is true and OR is false.
Short: For the independently selected sell conditions, combine with a chosen AND or OR: RSI > or < upper threshold, lower threshold, RSI midpoint, or RSI moving average; RSI moving average > or < upper or lower threshold; RSI crosses above or below upper threshold, lower threshold, RSI midpoint, or RSI moving average; RSI moving average crosses above or below upper or lower threshold; or either divergence condition below. Optionally treat each selected condition as true if it occurred within the selected validity lookback, including the current bar. With no selected conditions, AND is true and OR is false.
Long: A new RSI pivot low is confirmed AND its RSI is higher than the preceding RSI pivot low AND its corresponding price low is lower than the price low at that preceding pivot AND pivot spacing is within the inclusive minimum and maximum limits.
Short: A new RSI pivot high is confirmed AND its RSI is lower than the preceding RSI pivot high AND its corresponding price high is higher than the price high at that preceding pivot AND pivot spacing is within the inclusive minimum and maximum limits.
