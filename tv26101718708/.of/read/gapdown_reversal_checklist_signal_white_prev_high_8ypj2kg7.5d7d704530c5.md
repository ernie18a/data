Calculation: Setup score = count of these seven true conditions: close > VWAP; close > previous regular-session close; close > opening-range high after the opening-range window is complete; close >= previous regular-session high × (1 − near-high threshold); constructive moving-average interaction; RSI < RSI limit; RSI moving average >= its previous value (true when RSI moving average is disabled or its previous value is unavailable)
Calculation: Constructive moving-average interaction = close within the near threshold of the 50-day SMA or 200-day SMA, or close crossing above either SMA from at or below it, or close above either SMA
Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the opening-range window of the current day
Calculation: Previous regular-session high = highest high during the preceding regular session
Calculation: RSI moving average = configured moving average of RSI
Long: In regular session AND setup score >= 4
