Calculation: Paired score = sum of the specified contributions; each contribution adds its positive weight when its bullish condition is true, otherwise subtracts that weight when its bearish condition is true, otherwise adds zero. A crossover requires the first series to be greater than the second now and less than or equal to it on the previous bar; a crossunder is the reverse. Unavailable conditions contribute zero.
Calculation: Momentum score = paired score of: weight 2 for short EMA > longer EMA AND close > medium EMA, or the reversed comparisons; weight 1 for close above or below the longest EMA; weight 2 for MACD line above or below its signal; weight 1 for MACD line crossing above or below its signal; weight 1 for RSI above an upper bias threshold or below a lower bias threshold; weight 1 for ADX >= its trend threshold AND positive DI > negative DI, or negative DI > positive DI; weight 1 for volume > its SMA AND close > open, or volume > its SMA AND close < open; optionally weight 1 for higher-timeframe close above or below its EMA. The higher-timeframe calculation uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars, and the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Oscillator score = paired score of: weight 2 for RSI >= upper bias threshold or <= lower bias threshold; weight 1 for RSI crossing above its oversold threshold or below its overbought threshold; weight 2 for smoothed stochastic %K > %D AND %K > midpoint, or %K < %D AND %K < midpoint; weight 1 for %K crossing above %D while %K < overbought threshold, or crossing below %D while %K > oversold threshold; weight 1 for %K crossing above its oversold threshold or below its overbought threshold; weight 2 for a fast-minus-slow moving-average oscillator above or below its signal; weight 1 for that oscillator crossing above or below its signal; optionally weight 1 for short EMA > longer EMA AND close > trend EMA, or the reversed comparisons; optionally weight 1 for volume > its SMA AND close > open, or volume > its SMA AND close < open. The oscillator and its signal each independently use either SMA or EMA.
Calculation: Momentum long signal = according to the selected mode, momentum score first reaches its positive entry threshold, oscillator score first reaches its positive entry threshold, either first reaches its threshold, or both are at or above their respective thresholds and at least one was below its threshold on the previous bar. Momentum short signal uses the corresponding negative thresholds and reversed comparisons.
Calculation: Forecast drift = the normalized weighted average of the linear-regression slope of price over a lookback, the average of the momentum and oscillator scores divided by their respective maximum possible scores multiplied by ATR and a scale factor, and the price change over that lookback divided by its length. Replace unavailable components with zero.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = an oscillator low or high surrounded by the specified numbers of bars on its left and right with no lower or higher oscillator value respectively; identify it only after its right-hand bars have completed. The oscillator is RSI, MACD histogram, or smoothed stochastic %K. Compare price low or high at the pivot with price at the immediately preceding oscillator pivot of the same type; require the number of bars between their confirmations, minus one, to be within the configured range.
Long: close < mean EMA - ATR multiplied by stretch factor AND short-period RSI < oversold threshold AND optional trend alignment requires close > longest EMA AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and its EMA is available, higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA AND optional date, session, weekday, and daily entry-count limits pass.
Short: close > mean EMA + ATR multiplied by stretch factor AND short-period RSI > overbought threshold AND shorts are enabled AND optional trend alignment requires close < longest EMA AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and its EMA is available, higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA AND optional date, session, weekday, and daily entry-count limits pass.
Long: momentum long signal AND optional trend alignment requires close > longest EMA AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and its EMA is available, higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA AND optional ADX >= trend threshold AND optional close - short EMA <= ATR multiplied by maximum extension AND RSI < long guard threshold AND optional date, session, weekday, and daily entry-count limits pass.
Short: momentum short signal AND shorts are enabled AND optional trend alignment requires close < longest EMA AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled and its EMA is available, higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA AND optional ADX >= trend threshold AND optional short EMA - close <= ATR multiplied by maximum extension AND RSI > short guard threshold AND optional date, session, weekday, and daily entry-count limits pass.
Long: momentum score >= positive entry threshold AND previous momentum score < positive entry threshold.
Short: momentum score <= negative entry threshold AND previous momentum score > negative entry threshold.
Long: either score >= its positive early-warning threshold AND < its positive entry threshold AND its previous value < its positive early-warning threshold.
Short: either score <= its negative early-warning threshold AND > its negative entry threshold AND its previous value > its negative early-warning threshold.
Long: momentum score >= its positive strong threshold AND oscillator score >= its positive strong threshold OR momentum score >= its positive weaker threshold OR oscillator score >= its positive weaker threshold, provided the scores do not both meet their negative strong thresholds.
Short: momentum score <= its negative strong threshold AND oscillator score <= its negative strong threshold OR momentum score <= its negative weaker threshold OR oscillator score <= its negative weaker threshold, provided the scores do not both meet their positive strong thresholds and neither score meets its positive weaker threshold.
Long: forecast drift > 0 OR close > previous close.
Short: forecast drift < 0 OR close < previous close.

Short: confirmed oscillator high pivot AND current pivot oscillator < previous pivot oscillator AND current pivot price high > previous pivot price high OR current pivot oscillator > previous pivot oscillator AND current pivot price high < previous pivot price high.

Exit: short, high >= entry-bar close + entry-bar ATR multiplied by the selected engine's stop factor OR low <= momentum target at entry-bar close - entry-bar ATR multiplied by stop factor and reward-to-risk factor OR low <= current mean EMA for mean reversion.

Calculation: Adjusted long stop = initial long stop, raised to at least entry-bar close if breakeven is enabled and high reaches the first target; if trailing is enabled and high reaches the first target, also raise it to at least high - current ATR multiplied by trailing factor. Preserve the highest stop reached. Adjusted short stop reverses these comparisons, using low + current ATR multiplied by trailing factor and preserving the lowest stop reached.
Exit: long, optional ATR exits enabled AND low <= adjusted long stop.
Exit: short, optional ATR exits enabled AND high >= adjusted short stop.
Exit: long OR short, optional time stop enabled AND bars since the most recent entry signal >= holding limit, using the greater of the configured holding limit and the mean-reversion minimum in mean-reversion mode.
Exit: long OR short, optional session flatten enabled AND datetime is within the flatten window.
Exit: long, momentum mode AND optional opposite-signal exit enabled AND momentum short signal OR momentum mode AND optional neutral exit enabled AND momentum score = 0 AND oscillator score = 0.
Exit: short, momentum mode AND optional opposite-signal exit enabled AND momentum long signal OR momentum mode AND optional neutral exit enabled AND momentum score = 0 AND oscillator score = 0.
