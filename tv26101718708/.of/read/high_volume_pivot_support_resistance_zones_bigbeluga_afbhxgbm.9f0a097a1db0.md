Calculation: Resistance zone = the latest confirmed pivot high whose pivot-bar volume exceeds the pivot-bar SMA of volume multiplied by a threshold; its bottom is the greater of the pivot-bar open and close, and its top is that bottom plus the pivot-bar ATR. Confirm the pivot using equal numbers of bars before and after it. Replace the zone on each new qualifying pivot high.
Calculation: Support zone = the latest confirmed pivot low whose pivot-bar volume exceeds the pivot-bar SMA of volume multiplied by a threshold; its top is the lesser of the pivot-bar open and close, and its bottom is that top minus the pivot-bar ATR. Confirm the pivot using equal numbers of bars before and after it. Replace the zone on each new qualifying pivot low. The support and resistance pivot lookbacks may differ.
Calculation: Zone break state = false when its zone is created; set it to true on the first subsequent bar whose close exceeds the resistance-zone top or falls below the support-zone bottom, respectively, and keep it true until the zone is replaced.
Long: A resistance zone exists AND it was not created on the current bar AND close > resistance-zone top AND its break state was false before the current bar.
Long: A support zone exists AND it was not created on the current bar AND close > support-zone bottom AND previous high is not used AND previous low <= support-zone top AND low > support-zone top.
Long: A resistance zone exists AND it was not created on the current bar AND close > resistance-zone top AND previous low <= resistance-zone top AND low > resistance-zone top AND its break state changed neither on the current bar nor on the previous bar.
Short: A support zone exists AND it was not created on the current bar AND close < support-zone bottom AND its break state was false before the current bar.
Short: A resistance zone exists AND it was not created on the current bar AND close < resistance-zone top AND previous high >= resistance-zone bottom AND high < resistance-zone bottom.
