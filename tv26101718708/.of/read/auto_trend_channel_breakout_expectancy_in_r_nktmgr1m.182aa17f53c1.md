Calculation: Hull boundaries = Maintain separate rolling sets of confirmed pivot highs and lows, using equal numbers of bars on each side of each pivot. Construct the upper convex hull of the highs and the lower convex hull of the lows in chronological order, removing the middle point of each non-clockwise turn for the upper hull and each non-counterclockwise turn for the lower hull. Extend the last edge of each hull to the current bar. Validate a resistance edge when its slope divided by current ATR is within the configured negative slope bounds, and a support edge when its normalized slope is within the configured positive slope bounds. Recalculate and validate each edge only when a new pivot of its kind confirms. Mark an edge unbroken when its endpoints change; mark it broken on its first qualifying break.
Calculation: Up break = An unbroken valid resistance edge exists AND close exceeds the edge plus an ATR buffer AND high minus low is at least a displacement multiple of ATR AND absolute close minus open divided by high minus low is at least the body threshold AND close minus low divided by high minus low is at least the close-position threshold. Mark the edge broken immediately, regardless of any other filters.
Calculation: Down break = An unbroken valid support edge exists AND close is below the edge minus an ATR buffer AND high minus low is at least a displacement multiple of ATR AND absolute close minus open divided by high minus low is at least the body threshold AND high minus close divided by high minus low is at least the close-position threshold. Mark the edge broken immediately, regardless of any other filters.
Calculation: Channel width = At the break, take the maximum perpendicular price-axis distance from the broken resistance edge to confirmed pivot lows, or from the broken support edge to confirmed pivot highs, considering only pivots at or after the edge's first endpoint; floor the result at zero. If no qualifying opposite pivot exists, use an ATR-multiple fallback.
Calculation: Trade barriers = At entry, set the long stop to the lower of the current broken resistance line or entry-bar low, as selected, and entry price minus a minimum ATR distance; set the short stop to the higher of the current broken support line or entry-bar high, as selected, and entry price plus that distance. Set the target distance to either the channel width saved at the break or an entry-bar ATR multiple, as selected, capped at another entry-bar ATR multiple. Add the distance to the long entry price or subtract it from the short entry price. Keep the barriers fixed after entry.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = Choose rolling volume-weighted typical price, session-reset volume-weighted typical price, or volume-weighted typical price reset when a cumulative-delta pivot confirms. For the latter, accumulate volume times z divided by sqrt(1 + z squared), where z is the close change divided by the rolling standard deviation of close changes, or zero if that deviation is zero; reset the VWAP on confirmation of a pivot high or low in that cumulative series, using equal pivot lengths on each side. Use the chart timeframe or the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Long: Up break.
Short: Down break.
Long: After an up break, within the configured arm window, low reaches or falls below the current resistance line plus an ATR tolerance AND close is above the current resistance line AND no earlier close since the break was below that line AND the break's volume percentile belongs to an enabled tier AND, if VWAP agreement is required, the break-time close on the selected timeframe is above its anchored VWAP.
Short: After a down break, within the configured arm window, high reaches or exceeds the current support line minus an ATR tolerance AND close is below the current support line AND no earlier close since the break was above that line AND the break's volume percentile belongs to an enabled tier AND, if VWAP agreement is required, the break-time close on the selected timeframe is below its anchored VWAP.
Long: Close is above anchored VWAP.
Short: Close is below anchored VWAP.
Long: The current fitted value of a linear regression of close is greater than the preceding fitted value from the same regression window, on the chart timeframe, the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, or the last completed intrabar of a lower timeframe.
Short: The current fitted value of a linear regression of close is less than the preceding fitted value from the same regression window, on the chart timeframe, the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, or the last completed intrabar of a lower timeframe.
Exit: long, high is at least the fixed target OR low is at most the fixed stop.
Exit: short, low is at most the fixed target OR high is at least the fixed stop.
