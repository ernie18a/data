Calculation: Chandelier long stop = highest close over the ATR lookback minus the ATR multiplier times ATR; ratchet it upward to the greater of its raw value and prior stop when prior close was above prior stop, otherwise use the raw value.
Calculation: Chandelier short stop = lowest close over the ATR lookback plus the ATR multiplier times ATR; ratchet it downward to the lesser of its raw value and prior stop when prior close was below prior stop, otherwise use the raw value.
Calculation: Chandelier direction = bullish if close is above the prior short stop; bearish if close is below the prior long stop; otherwise retain the prior direction (initially bullish).
Calculation: HTF bias = bullish when the short-lookback EMA exceeds the long-lookback EMA on both daily and weekly bars; bearish when it is below on both. These are higher-timeframe series when above the chart timeframe and use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: HTF bias allowed = HTF bias is disabled, aggressive mode is selected, or HTF bias matches the direction being evaluated.
Calculation: Long score = 25 if fast EMA > slow EMA, plus 25 if Chandelier direction is bullish, plus 20 if the smoothed RSI is above 50 and rising, plus 20 if HTF bias is allowed for long, plus volume points: 10 if volume / volume SMA >= 1, else 7 if >= 0.75, else 4 if >= 0.50, else 0.
Calculation: Short score = 25 if fast EMA < slow EMA, plus 25 if Chandelier direction is bearish, plus 20 if the smoothed RSI is below 50 and falling, plus 20 if HTF bias is allowed for short, plus the same volume points as the long score.
Calculation: Confidence = the greater of the long and short scores, or 0 if they are equal.
Calculation: Direction = long if long score > short score; short if short score > long score; otherwise neutral.
Long: (confidence >= 90 AND direction is long) OR (direction is long AND HTF bias is allowed for long AND confidence >= 80 AND (confidence >= 90 OR (low <= fast EMA OR close > prior high)) AND cooldown has elapsed AND (one-signal-per-direction is disabled OR the last signal was not long))
Short: (confidence >= 90 AND direction is short) OR (direction is short AND HTF bias is allowed for short AND confidence >= 80 AND (confidence >= 90 OR (high >= fast EMA OR close < prior low)) AND cooldown has elapsed AND (one-signal-per-direction is disabled OR the last signal was not short))
