Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the simple moving average of volume, provided that average is greater than zero.
Calculation: Target hit = for a long setup, current high >= a configured target AND previous high < that target; for a short setup, current low <= a configured target AND previous low > that target.
Calculation: Invalidation = for a long setup, close < the configured invalidation level OR close < the configured hard invalidation level; for a short setup, close > the configured invalidation level OR close > the configured hard invalidation level.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = the number of bars since the most recent trigger signal, confirmation signal, invalidation, or target hit, counting invalidations and target hits only during the configured trading session; if none has occurred, the cooldown is satisfied.
Long: Long setup AND within the configured trading session AND bar closed AND signal cooldown >= the configured minimum AND (close crosses above the configured long trigger OR close crosses above the higher confirmation level) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > shorter EMA of close AND shorter EMA >= longer EMA of close) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= its configured minimum).
Short: Short setup AND within the configured trading session AND bar closed AND signal cooldown >= the configured minimum AND (close crosses below the configured short trigger OR close crosses below the lower confirmation level) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < shorter EMA of close AND shorter EMA <= longer EMA of close) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= its configured minimum).
Long: Long setup AND close >= the configured long trigger AND NOT (within the configured trading session AND (invalidation OR target hit)).
Short: Short setup AND close <= the configured short trigger AND NOT (within the configured trading session AND (invalidation OR target hit)).
Exit: long, within the configured trading session AND (bar closed AND invalidation OR target hit).
Exit: short, within the configured trading session AND (bar closed AND invalidation OR target hit).
