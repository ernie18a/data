Calculation: Smoothed moving average (SMMA) = initialize with a simple moving average; thereafter, (previous SMMA × (length − 1) + current hl2) / length.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a low/high that is no greater/less than the lows/highs in the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes available after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Clean divergence path = at every intervening bar, both price and the indicator stay on or above the straight line between pivot values for pivot lows, or on or below that line for pivot highs.
Long: Current bar meets all of: close is at least the configured gap percentage above the short-term SMMA of hl2 shifted forward by its configured offset; that shifted SMMA is above the long-term SMMA of hl2 shifted forward by its configured offset; either the VWAP filter is disabled or close > VWAP; either the long-term SMA filter is disabled or close > that SMA; and the same conditions were not all true on the previous bar. OR At a newly confirmed pivot low, at least the configured minimum number of enabled indicators show bullish regular divergence (price makes a lower low and indicator a higher low) or bullish hidden divergence (price makes a higher low and indicator a lower low) versus the same prior pivot low, with the clean divergence path; the prior pivot is more than five bars and within the configured maximum lookback away. Indicators: MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Momentum, OBV, VW MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, Money Flow Index, and configured external source.
Short: Current bar meets all of: close is at least the configured gap percentage below the short-term SMMA of hl2 shifted forward by its configured offset; the long-term SMMA of hl2 shifted forward by its configured offset is above that short-term SMMA; either the VWAP filter is disabled or close < VWAP; either the long-term SMA filter is disabled or close < that SMA; and the same conditions were not all true on the previous bar. OR At a newly confirmed pivot high, at least the configured minimum number of enabled indicators show bearish regular divergence (price makes a higher high and indicator a lower high) or bearish hidden divergence (price makes a lower high and indicator a higher high) versus the same prior pivot high, with the clean divergence path; the prior pivot is more than five bars and within the configured maximum lookback away. Indicators: MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Momentum, OBV, VW MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, Money Flow Index, and configured external source.
