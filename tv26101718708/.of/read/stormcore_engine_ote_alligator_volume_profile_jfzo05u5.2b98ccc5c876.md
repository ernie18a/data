Calculation: Displaced smoothed averages = smoothed moving averages of (high + low) / 2, initialized with a simple moving average; use the short-lookback average and the long-lookback average from their respective configurable numbers of bars ago.
Calculation: Divergence candidates = confirmed price pivots using equal left and right lookbacks, based on either close for both pivot types or low for lows and high for highs. Compare the new pivot with retained earlier pivots, newest first, within a maximum separation and beyond a minimum separation. Retain a configurable maximum number of pivots of each type. For each enabled indicator, select the first earlier pivot satisfying an enabled divergence type and its clean-line test. Compare close at the new pivot when pivots use close; otherwise compare (high + low) / 2 at the new pivot. Compare that value with the earlier pivot's stored close, low, or high, according to its pivot source. Compare indicator values at the two pivot bars. For a low-pivot clean-line test, every intermediate price source and indicator value must be at or above its respective straight line between the endpoints; for a high-pivot test, both must be at or below their respective lines. Intermediate price source is close or (high + low) / 2, matching the new pivot's comparison source. Count indicators selecting the same earlier pivot and divergence type. Evaluate on the pivot confirmation bar; optionally disable divergence calculations for intraday charts and select the pivot lookback by chart timeframe.
Calculation: Divergence indicators = MACD line; MACD histogram; RSI of close; SMA of stochastic calculated from close, high, and low; CCI of close; close momentum; cumulative signed volume using the sign of close change; short-lookback VWMA of close minus long-lookback VWMA of close; SMA of volume times ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), divided by SMA of volume, replacing a zero high-low denominator with 1; MFI using close as its source; and a selectable source derived from the backtest data. MACD and volume-weighted averages use a shorter and a longer lookback; MACD histogram also uses a signal-line lookback.
Long: The displaced short-lookback smoothed average > the displaced long-lookback smoothed average AND (close - displaced short-lookback smoothed average) / close >= a minimum gap fraction AND (the VWAP filter is disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (the SMA filter is disabled OR close > SMA of close) AND the complete condition was false on the preceding bar.
Short: The displaced long-lookback smoothed average > the displaced short-lookback smoothed average AND (displaced short-lookback smoothed average - close) / close >= a minimum gap fraction AND (the VWAP filter is disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND (the SMA filter is disabled OR close < SMA of close) AND the complete condition was false on the preceding bar.
Long: The middle bar's low <= every other low in a configurable odd-length window ending on the current bar.
Short: The middle bar's high >= every other high in a configurable odd-length window ending on the current bar.
Long: A confirmed low-pivot divergence candidate has at least the minimum number of indicators sharing its earlier pivot and type AND ((the new price comparison value < the earlier pivot price AND each counted indicator is higher at the new pivot) OR (the new price comparison value > the earlier pivot price AND each counted indicator is lower at the new pivot)).
Short: A confirmed high-pivot divergence candidate has at least the minimum number of indicators sharing its earlier pivot and type AND ((the new price comparison value > the earlier pivot price AND each counted indicator is lower at the new pivot) OR (the new price comparison value < the earlier pivot price AND each counted indicator is higher at the new pivot)).
