Calculation: Opening range high and low = running maximum high and minimum low during the configured session, reset at each new day. Use the configured aggregation timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; with historical lookahead enabled, a higher timeframe supplies the completed bar's final values from its start, and a lower timeframe supplies the first intrabar's values.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars ago.
Calculation: Internal bullish gap = bullish fair value gap AND high two bars ago > opening range low AND high two bars ago < opening range high; internal bearish gap = bearish fair value gap AND low two bars ago > opening range low AND low two bars ago < opening range high. Record each occurrence until the next day.
Calculation: External bullish gap = bullish fair value gap AND low > opening range high; external bearish gap = bearish fair value gap AND high < opening range low. Record each occurrence until the next day.
Calculation: Retracement met at a configured fraction = since the start of the day, on any bar with close > opening range high, the running maximum high of such bars exceeds opening range high AND low <= running maximum high - fraction * (running maximum high - opening range high); OR on any bar with close < opening range low, the running minimum low of such bars is below opening range low AND high >= running minimum low + fraction * (opening range low - running minimum low). Each fraction remains met until the next day, regardless of direction.
Calculation: Signal state = neutral initially and after the new-day reset; set to long or short when the corresponding entry signal occurs. EMA exits do not change this state. Apply the new-day reset after processing that bar's signals and exits.
Long: Bullish fair value gap.
Short: Bearish fair value gap.
Long: Close > (opening range high + opening range low) / 2.
Short: Close <= (opening range high + opening range low) / 2.
Long: Time is within the configured date range AND close > opening range high AND ((signal state = neutral AND the opening-range session has ended) OR signal state = short) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA * multiplier OR volume > previous volume) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > threshold) AND (fair value gap filter disabled OR bullish fair value gap) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram > 0) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (EMA filter disabled OR shorter EMA > longer EMA) AND (internal gap filter disabled OR an internal bullish gap has occurred today) AND (external gap filter disabled OR an external bullish gap has occurred today) AND (each enabled retracement fraction has been met).
Short: Time is within the configured date range AND close < opening range low AND ((signal state = neutral AND the opening-range session has ended) OR signal state = long) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA * multiplier OR volume > previous volume) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < 100 - threshold) AND (fair value gap filter disabled OR bearish fair value gap) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD histogram < 0) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (EMA filter disabled OR shorter EMA < longer EMA) AND (internal gap filter disabled OR an internal bearish gap has occurred today) AND (external gap filter disabled OR an external bearish gap has occurred today) AND (each enabled retracement fraction has been met).
Exit: Long, after the entry signal bar AND before the first EMA exit for that signal AND signal state = long AND shorter EMA crosses below longer EMA.
Exit: Short, after the entry signal bar AND before the first EMA exit for that signal AND signal state = short AND shorter EMA crosses above longer EMA.
Exit: Long, simulated performance trade is active AND time is within the configured date range AND (high >= entry price + (entry price - opening range low fixed at entry) OR low <= opening range low fixed at entry).
Exit: Short, simulated performance trade is active AND time is within the configured date range AND (low <= entry price - (opening range high fixed at entry - entry price) OR high >= opening range high fixed at entry).
