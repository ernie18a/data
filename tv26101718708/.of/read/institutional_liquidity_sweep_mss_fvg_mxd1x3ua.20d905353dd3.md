Calculation: Session high/low = highest high / lowest low observed in the respective Asia, London, or New York session so far
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar on a higher timeframe
Calculation: Four-hour high/low = high/low of the last completed four-hour bar on a higher timeframe
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier
Long: (current low < Asia session low AND current close > Asia session low OR current low < London session low AND current close > London session low OR current low < New York session low AND current close > New York session low OR current low < previous-day low AND current close > previous-day low OR current low < four-hour low AND current close > four-hour low) AND (previous bar had a long sweep AND current close > previous bar high) AND Bullish FVG AND current close > current open AND current close - current open > a parameterized fraction of ATR AND current close > EMA AND current volume > its moving average
Short: (current high > Asia session high AND current close < Asia session high OR current high > London session high AND current close < London session high OR current high > New York session high AND current close < New York session high OR current high > previous-day high AND current close < previous-day high OR current high > four-hour high AND current close < four-hour high) AND (previous bar had a short sweep AND current close < previous bar low) AND Bearish FVG AND current close < current open AND current open - current close > a parameterized fraction of ATR AND current close < EMA AND current volume > its moving average
