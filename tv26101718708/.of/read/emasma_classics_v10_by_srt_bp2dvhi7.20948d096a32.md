Calculation: MA stack = at least two enabled close-based EMA or SMA series, each with its own configurable type and length, ordered by their configured sequence from shorter to longer.
Calculation: Ladder score = number of strict increases between successive rolling lowest lows, ordered from longest to shortest lookback, minus number of strict decreases between successive rolling highest highs, ordered from longest to shortest lookback; use the same ordered lookbacks for both counts.
Calculation: Previous-bar bullish engulfing = previous bar range > 0 AND previous-bar ATR > 0 AND previous bar range >= previous-bar ATR × engulfing range threshold AND previous close > previous open AND previous body low < body low two bars ago AND previous body high > body high two bars ago AND previous body size / previous bar range >= engulfing body threshold.
Calculation: Previous-bar bearish engulfing = previous bar range > 0 AND previous-bar ATR > 0 AND previous bar range >= previous-bar ATR × engulfing range threshold AND previous close < previous open AND previous body low < body low two bars ago AND previous body high > body high two bars ago AND previous body size / previous bar range >= engulfing body threshold.
Calculation: Previous-bar bullish long tail = previous bar range > 0 AND previous-bar ATR > 0 AND previous bar range >= previous-bar ATR × long-tail range threshold AND previous lower wick / previous bar range >= tail threshold AND previous body low >= previous low + body-position threshold × previous bar range AND previous close > previous open AND, if the optional body filter is enabled, previous body size / previous bar range >= long-tail body threshold.
Calculation: Previous-bar bearish long tail = previous bar range > 0 AND previous-bar ATR > 0 AND previous bar range >= previous-bar ATR × long-tail range threshold AND previous upper wick / previous bar range >= tail threshold AND previous body high <= previous high - body-position threshold × previous bar range AND previous close < previous open AND, if the optional body filter is enabled, previous body size / previous bar range >= long-tail body threshold.
Long: Every successive pair in the MA stack is strictly decreasing.
Short: Every successive pair in the MA stack is strictly increasing.
Long: Ladder score > 0.
Short: Ladder score < 0.
Long: Previous-bar bullish engulfing = true OR previous-bar bullish long tail = true.
Short: Previous-bar bearish engulfing = true OR previous-bar bearish long tail = true.
Long: Previous RSI < upper breakout threshold AND current RSI >= upper breakout threshold.
Short: Previous RSI > lower breakout threshold AND current RSI <= lower breakout threshold.
Long: Lowest RSI over the retracement lookback, including the current bar, < lower breakout threshold AND current RSI >= lower breakout threshold AND current SMA of RSI <= lower RSI-MA threshold AND current RSI > current SMA of RSI AND previous RSI <= previous SMA of RSI AND bars since the last emitted bullish RSI retracement > cooldown, or no bullish RSI retracement has been emitted.
Short: Highest RSI over the retracement lookback, including the current bar, > upper breakout threshold AND current RSI <= upper breakout threshold AND current SMA of RSI >= upper RSI-MA threshold AND current RSI < current SMA of RSI AND previous RSI >= previous SMA of RSI AND bars since the last emitted bearish RSI retracement > cooldown, or no bearish RSI retracement has been emitted.
