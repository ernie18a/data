Calculation: MA1–MA4 = each configured EMA or SMA of chart close, using its own lookback
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = on the previous completed chart bar, close > open, its body strictly contains the body of the bar before it, body/range ≥ the configured minimum, and range ≥ the prior bar’s ATR × the configured threshold
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = on the previous completed chart bar, close < open, its body strictly contains the body of the bar before it, body/range ≥ the configured minimum, and range ≥ the prior bar’s ATR × the configured threshold
Calculation: Bullish long-tail bar = on the previous completed chart bar, lower wick/range ≥ the configured minimum, body is in the top portion of the range, close > open, range ≥ the prior bar’s ATR × the configured threshold, and, if enabled, body/range ≥ the configured minimum
Calculation: Bearish long-tail bar = on the previous completed chart bar, upper wick/range ≥ the configured minimum, body is in the bottom portion of the range, close < open, range ≥ the prior bar’s ATR × the configured threshold, and, if enabled, body/range ≥ the configured minimum
Calculation: Bullish RSI retracement = RSI touched below its lower extreme within the configured lookback, current RSI ≥ that extreme, RSI moving average ≤ its configured lower threshold, and RSI crossed above its moving average on this completed chart bar; no same-side retracement signal occurred within the configured cooldown
Calculation: Bearish RSI retracement = RSI touched above its upper extreme within the configured lookback, current RSI ≤ that extreme, RSI moving average ≥ its configured upper threshold, and RSI crossed below its moving average on this completed chart bar; no same-side retracement signal occurred within the configured cooldown
Calculation: Bullish ladder bias = count of true comparisons among lowest lows over successively shorter lookbacks rising from the longest to shortest; minus count of true comparisons among highest highs over successively shorter lookbacks falling from longest to shortest, is positive
Calculation: Bearish ladder bias = the same ladder net count is negative
Long: Bullish engulfing OR Bullish long-tail bar OR RSI crossed from below its configured upper extreme to at or above it on this completed chart bar OR Bullish RSI retracement OR at least two visible moving averages are strictly stacked in configured order from shorter to longer lookback AND each of MA1–MA2, MA2–MA3, and MA1–MA3 distances is not below ATR × the configured spacing threshold OR at least two visible moving averages are strictly stacked in configured order from shorter to longer lookback AND Bullish ladder bias
Short: Bearish engulfing OR Bearish long-tail bar OR RSI crossed from above its configured lower extreme to at or below it on this completed chart bar OR Bearish RSI retracement OR at least two visible moving averages are strictly stacked in configured order from shorter to longer lookback AND each of MA1–MA2, MA2–MA3, and MA1–MA3 distances is not below ATR × the configured spacing threshold OR at least two visible moving averages are strictly stacked in configured order from shorter to longer lookback AND Bearish ladder bias
