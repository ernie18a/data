Calculation: Trend = +1/-1 supertrend variant. V = SMA of (high - low) over a lookback; upper = hl2 + mult*V, lower = hl2 - mult*V. Lower is ratcheted up (lower = max(lower, previous lower) unless previous close < previous lower). Upper is ratcheted down (upper = min(upper, previous upper) unless previous close > previous upper). If V is unavailable on the previous bar, Trend = +1. If the previous trailing stop was the upper band, Trend = +1 when close > upper, else -1. Otherwise Trend = -1 when close < lower, else +1. Trend change = Trend differs from previous bar's Trend.
Calculation: Pivot with strength k (same k on both sides) = low (or high) of the bar k bars ago is the lowest low (or highest high) over k bars before and k bars after it. It is confirmed on the current bar.
Calculation: Zone = a single active zone (top, bottom, side, buyShare). Update each bar, in this order. (1) If Trend = +1 and a pivot low is confirmed on this bar: candidate side = bull, top = min(open, close) of the pivot bar, bottom = top - V (current V). (2) If Trend = -1 and a pivot high is confirmed on this bar: candidate side = bear, bottom = max(open, close) of the pivot bar, top = bottom + V (current V). (3) A candidate replaces the active Zone unless its range intersects the active Zone's range; if none is active it is always accepted. (4) On acceptance, buyShare = sum of volume over the current bar and the previous k bars where close >= open, divided by total volume over those bars (0.5 if total is 0). sellShare = 1 - buyShare. (5) The Zone is cleared (none active) when an Exit condition below fires.
Long: Zone active AND low crosses above Zone top (previous low <= top, current low > top) AND no pivot low confirmed on this bar AND buyShare >= minimum buy share AND no Trend change on this bar
Short: Zone active AND high crosses below Zone bottom (previous high >= bottom, current high < bottom) AND no pivot high confirmed on this bar AND sellShare >= minimum sell share AND no Trend change on this bar
Exit: long, Zone side is bull AND high < Zone bottom
Exit: short, Zone side is bear AND low > Zone top
