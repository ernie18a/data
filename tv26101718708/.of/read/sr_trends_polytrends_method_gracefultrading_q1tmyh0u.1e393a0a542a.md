Calculation: Analysis bars = chart bars or last completed higher-timeframe bars; process each higher-timeframe bar once. For potential reversals, use the still-forming higher-timeframe high and low when a higher timeframe is selected, otherwise use the chart bar high and low.
Calculation: Swings = initialize direction upward if the first analysis close > open, otherwise downward; initialize running high and low to that bar's extremes. On each subsequent analysis bar, update both running extremes and their originating bars when exceeded. In an upward leg, confirm the running high when high < previous analysis high OR close < previous analysis low OR low < previous confirmed swing low OR (close < open AND the current bar made the running high AND close < max(open, close) of that high's originating bar). In a downward leg, confirm the running low when low > previous analysis low OR close > previous analysis high OR high > previous confirmed swing high OR (close > open AND the current bar made the running low AND close > min(open, close) of that low's originating bar). After a high confirmation, start a downward leg with running low equal to the current low; after a low confirmation, start an upward leg with running high equal to the current high. Retain the most recent confirmed high and low and their preceding same-type swings.
Calculation: Regime = initially neutral; before processing each analysis bar's swing confirmation, set bullish if its close > the latest confirmed swing high, otherwise set bearish if its close < the latest confirmed swing low; otherwise retain the prior regime.
Calculation: Levels = for each confirmed swing, save its originating bar's body top for a high or body bottom for a low, or optionally its wick extreme; commit it when the next opposite swing is confirmed. Initialize each committed level as unbroken and untested. On each processed analysis bar after its origin, until tested, mark a high level broken when max(open, close) > level + offset, or a low level broken when min(open, close) < level - offset. Using the broken state from before that bar, mark a broken high level tested when min(open, close) < level - offset, or a broken low level tested when max(open, close) > level + offset. Offset is a configurable price buffer, optionally zero or proportional to the level.
Calculation: Selected levels = within a configurable chart-bar history, include all levels when containment is enabled; otherwise keep the alternating sequence of non-containment swing nodes, replacing a same-type node when a more extreme same-type swing appears. Optionally include tested containment or absorbed levels. Partition untested levels into above and below groups: a high level belongs above if level >= chart close; a low level belongs above if broken AND level >= chart close; all others belong below. Select the nearest configurable number from each group, and include each selected high's next low or each selected low's preceding high, optionally excluding tested partners. Optionally select tested levels separately on each side, nearest first, choosing the largest preceding connector swing within a configurable price separation and suppressing neighboring candidates within that separation; include each selected tested level's partner. Partners are the next low after a high or the preceding high before a low, restricted to retained nodes when containment is disabled.
Long: A swing low is confirmed AND its low >= the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A swing high is confirmed AND its high <= the preceding confirmed swing high.
Long: A selected low level is unbroken OR a selected broken high or low level has chart close > level.
Short: A selected broken high level has chart close <= level OR a selected broken low level has chart close < level.
Long: The current live high > latest confirmed swing high AND regime is not bullish.
Short: The current live high <= latest confirmed swing high OR regime is bullish; AND the current live low < latest confirmed swing low AND regime is not bearish.
Long: Regime is bullish AND neither potential upward nor potential downward reversal condition holds.
Short: Regime is bearish AND neither potential upward nor potential downward reversal condition holds.
