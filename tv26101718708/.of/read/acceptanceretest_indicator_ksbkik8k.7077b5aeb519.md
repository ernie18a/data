Calculation: PDH/PDL = high/low of the previous completed regular session; ONH/ONL = high/low of the current overnight session while it is forming, otherwise the previous completed overnight session
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback of chart bars
Calculation: Level range = PDH − PDL for PDH/PDL; ONH − ONL for ONH/ONL
Calculation: Eligible levels = all available levels, excluding the lower of PDH/ONH when their distance is within a configurable percentage of close, and excluding the higher of PDL/ONL when their distance is within that tolerance
Calculation: Stop buffer = close × configurable buffer percentage
Calculation: Measured-move target = level + direction × level range, adjusted to the nearest eligible opposing level strictly between the breakout level and the measured-move target, if one exists
Long: For an eligible level L, in a configured entry window, previous chart-bar close ≤ L AND current chart-bar close > L; the next configured number of chart bars all close > L; before the configured watch limit expires, no watch-phase chart bar closes ≤ L AND (current close > the recovery high since the lowest watch-phase low OR (the prior watch-range high − prior watch-range low ≤ ATR × configured base multiplier AND prior base-bar count ≥ configured minimum AND current close > prior watch-range high)); entry = current close AND structural stop = (lowest watch-phase low − stop buffer) for a retest or (prior watch-range low − stop buffer) for a base AND entry − stop > 0 AND entry − stop ≤ max(level range, ATR × configured ATR cap multiplier) ÷ configured stop-cap divisor AND (target − entry) ÷ (entry − stop) ≥ configured minimum reward-to-risk AND current bar is in a configured entry window
Short: For an eligible level L, in a configured entry window, previous chart-bar close ≥ L AND current chart-bar close < L; the next configured number of chart bars all close < L; before the configured watch limit expires, no watch-phase chart bar closes ≥ L AND (current close < the recovery low since the highest watch-phase high OR (prior watch-range high − prior watch-range low ≤ ATR × configured base multiplier AND prior base-bar count ≥ configured minimum AND current close < prior watch-range low)); entry = current close AND structural stop = (highest watch-phase high + stop buffer) for a retest or (prior watch-range high + stop buffer) for a base AND stop − entry > 0 AND stop − entry ≤ max(level range, ATR × configured ATR cap multiplier) ÷ configured stop-cap divisor AND (entry − target) ÷ (stop − entry) ≥ configured minimum reward-to-risk AND current bar is in a configured entry window
Exit: long, low ≤ structural stop OR (low > structural stop AND high ≥ target) OR (low > structural stop AND high < target AND current time ≥ configured cutoff)
Exit: short, high ≥ structural stop OR (high < structural stop AND low ≤ target) OR (high < structural stop AND low > target AND current time ≥ configured cutoff)
