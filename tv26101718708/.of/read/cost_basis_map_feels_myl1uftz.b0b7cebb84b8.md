Calculation: Position ledger = Starting empty at the beginning of the engine window, process each confirmed chart bar. Use typical price = (high + low + close) / 3 and volume = raw volume, or raw volume times typical price for money weighting; replace missing or nonpositive raw volume with 1. Divide prices into logarithmic bins with configurable percentage width; a bin's representative price is the exponential of its logarithmic midpoint. Before adding the current bar, multiply every existing bin weight by max(1 - current volume / (volume SMA × position-memory parameter), 0) when the ledger total and volume SMA are positive; otherwise use a multiplier of 1. Add current volume to the bin containing current typical price. Periodically remove bins whose weight is below a configurable fraction of total ledger weight when the number of bins exceeds a configurable minimum. The volume SMA uses the longer volume lookback.
Calculation: Cost basis = Sum of each ledger bin's representative price times its weight, divided by total ledger weight.
Calculation: Underwater share = Sum of weights in bins whose representative price exceeds current close, divided by total ledger weight.
Calculation: Realized loss proxy = The ledger's total weight before current-bar decay times (1 - current-bar decay multiplier) times the previous bar's underwater share; use zero when the prior ledger or prior underwater share is unavailable.
Long: Underwater share <= a healthy-uptrend threshold.
Long: Realized loss proxy > its SMA plus a sensitivity parameter times its standard deviation AND its standard deviation > 0 AND close < cost basis AND bars since the previous capitulation signal >= the marker cooldown.
Short: Underwater share <= a euphoria threshold AND volume > its SMA times a turnover multiplier AND bars since the previous euphoria signal >= the marker cooldown.
Short: Underwater share <= a euphoria threshold.
Calculation: The euphoria threshold is lower than the healthy-uptrend threshold. The realized-loss SMA and standard deviation use the same lookback as the longer volume SMA; the turnover volume SMA uses a shorter lookback. Capitulation and euphoria cooldowns are tracked independently.
