Calculation: Gap size = open of the current, still-forming higher-timeframe period minus close of the last completed higher-timeframe period; a gap qualifies when its absolute size exceeds the minimum-gap parameter times chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Gap-fill probability = α/(α+β), using separate counts for up and down gaps. Initialize α and β to prior strength; after each completed higher-timeframe period with that gap direction, update α = prior strength + decay × (α − prior strength) + success and β = prior strength + decay × (β − prior strength) + failure. Success means price reached the prior close during the period: low ≤ prior close for a down gap, high ≥ prior close for an up gap; failure means it did not.
Calculation: Warm-up observations = the sum of (α + β − 2 × prior strength) for prior-high taps after a down prior period, prior-low taps after a down prior period, and prior-low taps after an up prior period. A tap succeeds when the period high ≥ the prior completed higher-timeframe period's high or the period low ≤ its low, respectively. Update these event counts with the same prior-strength and decay rules as the gap-fill counts.
Long: Chart bar is confirmed AND warm-up observations ≥ minimum periods AND current higher-timeframe gap size < −minimum-gap parameter × chart-timeframe ATR AND current period low > prior completed higher-timeframe close AND gap-fill probability for down gaps > probability threshold AND no long or short signal has been issued in the current higher-timeframe period.
Short: Chart bar is confirmed AND warm-up observations ≥ minimum periods AND current higher-timeframe gap size > minimum-gap parameter × chart-timeframe ATR AND current period high < prior completed higher-timeframe close AND gap-fill probability for up gaps > probability threshold AND no long or short signal has been issued in the current higher-timeframe period.
