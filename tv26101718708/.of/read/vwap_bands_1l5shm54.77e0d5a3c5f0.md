Calculation: Event-anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of a selected OHLC-derived source from the start bar or the latest reset bar, inclusive. After the selected start time or from the first bar, reset on the selected event: the number of bars processed since the start, before processing the current bar, is divisible by the lookback; the rolling highest high increases; the rolling lowest low decreases; the rounded value of (source minus its rolling minimum) divided by (its rolling maximum minus its rolling minimum) changes; the source crosses a selected OHLC-derived series; or that series is nonzero. A start-only option never resets.
Long: EMA of close crosses above event-anchored VWAP.
Short: EMA of close crosses below event-anchored VWAP.
Exit: long, (highest high since the most recent long entry, including the entry bar, minus entry price) / entry price >= activation profit percentage AND close <= highest high since that entry * (1 - trailing distance percentage).
Exit: short, (entry price - lowest low since the most recent short entry, including the entry bar) / entry price >= activation profit percentage AND close >= lowest low since that entry * (1 + trailing distance percentage).
