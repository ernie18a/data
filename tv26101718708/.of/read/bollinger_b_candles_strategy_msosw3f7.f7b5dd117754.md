Calculation: Bollinger %B candle = for each OHLC price series, 100 × (price − lower Bollinger Band of that series) ÷ (upper − lower); use the same configurable band parameters for all four series.
Calculation: Transformed candle = either each Bollinger %B OHLC series or its configurable moving average, according to the moving-average switch.
Calculation: Transformed-candle ATR = configurable moving average of (max(transformed high, prior transformed close) − min(transformed low, prior transformed close)), multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Direction = initialize to +1; thereafter retain the prior direction except flip from −1 to +1 when the selected transformed high (current high if wick mode, otherwise current close) exceeds the prior short stop, or from +1 to −1 when the selected transformed low (current low if wick mode, otherwise current close) falls below the prior long stop. The long stop starts at transformed close − transformed-candle ATR and ratchets upward to the greater of its current value and prior stop when the selected prior low (prior low if wick mode, otherwise prior close) is above the prior stop; otherwise reset to its current value. The short stop starts at transformed close + transformed-candle ATR and ratchets downward to the lesser of its current value and prior stop when the selected prior high (prior high if wick mode, otherwise prior close) is below the prior stop; otherwise reset to its current value.
Calculation: Yearly high filter = true when disabled; otherwise true if the prior completed 12-month bar's high is greater than the highs sampled 365 and 730 chart bars earlier (when available), or if close exceeds a trend threshold. The threshold is the latest high if the 365-bar-ago value is unavailable, the greater of the latest and 365-bar-ago highs if the 730-bar-ago value is unavailable, or max(latest high, min(365-bar-ago high, 730-bar-ago high)). Missing latest high makes the filter true.
Calculation: Yearly low filter = true when disabled; otherwise true if the prior completed 12-month bar's low is lower than the lows sampled 365 and 730 chart bars earlier (when available), or if close is below a trend threshold. The threshold is the latest low if the 365-bar-ago value is unavailable, the lesser of the latest and 365-bar-ago lows if the 730-bar-ago value is unavailable, or min(latest low, max(365-bar-ago low, 730-bar-ago low)). Missing latest low makes the filter true.
Long: Direction is +1 on each of the last configured signal-delay bars AND (new-long-term-high filter disabled OR highest high over the shorter lookback = highest high over the longer lookback) AND Yearly high filter AND datetime is on or after the configured backtest start date.
Short: Direction is −1 on each of the last configured signal-delay bars AND (new-long-term-low filter disabled OR lowest low over the shorter lookback = lowest low over the longer lookback) AND Yearly low filter AND datetime is on or after the configured backtest start date.
Exit: Long, Direction is −1 on any of the last configured signal-delay bars.
Exit: Short, Direction is +1 on each of the last configured signal-delay bars.
