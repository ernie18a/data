Calculation: Moving averages = Four independently configurable moving averages of configurable bar-price sources, ordered from shortest to longest lookback; each can use SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA. The first two form the fast pair and the last two form the slow pair. A pair is available only when both averages are enabled and defined; its upper and lower bounds are their maximum and minimum.
Calculation: Rising or falling = Current value is respectively greater or less than every preceding value in the configured lookback. The fast and slow pairs have separately configured lookbacks.
Calculation: Fast bullish condition = Fast pair available AND shortest average > second average AND both averages rising AND close > both averages.
Calculation: Fast bearish condition = Fast pair available AND shortest average < second average AND both averages falling AND close < both averages.
Calculation: Slow bullish condition = Slow pair available AND third average > longest average AND both averages rising AND close > at least one slow average.
Calculation: Slow bearish condition = Slow pair available AND third average < longest average AND both averages falling AND close < at least one slow average.
Calculation: Aligned condition = Fast bullish condition AND slow bullish condition AND close > fast upper bound.
Calculation: Wick condition = Upper wick divided by max(high - low, configured minimum range) <= configured maximum, unless this filter is disabled.
Calculation: Demand condition = Count of bars with close > open > count of bars with close < open over the configured lookback, unless this filter is disabled.
Calculation: Pullback touch = Lowest low over the configured preceding pullback window <= previous bar's fast upper bound.
Calculation: Pivot low or high = A low or high confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars, with the configured number of left-hand bars; ties follow TradingView pivot semantics. Confirmation occurs on the current bar, not the pivot bar.
Calculation: Standard continuation = Fast bullish condition AND close > both slow averages AND breakout-ready condition AND optional pullback touch AND demand condition. For breakout-ready, track the most recently confirmed anchor pivot low; on its confirmation set the segment length to its right-hand confirmation length plus one and increment it on each subsequent bar. Count lower-low events among prior bars in that segment, capped at the configured maximum segment inspection length; an event compares each low with the immediately preceding low and optionally permits equality. The count must reach its configured minimum AND current high, or optionally current close, must exceed the highest high of the preceding segment AND optionally close > open AND wick condition. After any breakout-ready event, block another until a newly confirmed cooldown pivot low exceeds the preceding confirmed cooldown pivot low; update this block even when the other continuation filters fail.
Calculation: Rotational continuation = Fast and slow pairs available AND shortest average > second average AND close > fast lower bound AND close > slow upper bound AND fast bearish condition is false AND pullback touch AND high > highest high over the configured preceding breakout window AND optionally close > open AND wick condition AND demand condition AND bars since the previous rotational continuation > configured cooldown, or no previous rotational continuation exists.
Calculation: Recent bearish phase = Slow bearish condition occurred within a configured lookback; turnaround and ignition use separately configured lookbacks.
Calculation: Turnaround = Recent bearish phase AND fast lower bound <= slow upper bound AND both fast averages > their preceding values AND fast upper bound >= slow lower bound AND close > open AND close is within the configured upper portion of the bar range AND close > fast upper bound AND wick condition AND close > the latest confirmed pivot high recorded while the recent bearish phase and fast lower bound <= slow upper bound held AND no turnaround or ignition has occurred since the most recent slow bearish bar.
Calculation: Ignition = Recent bearish phase AND no turnaround or ignition has occurred since the most recent slow bearish bar AND bars since prior ignition > configured cooldown, or no prior ignition exists AND high > highest high over the configured preceding breakout window AND close > open AND close >= low + configured fraction of max(high - low, configured minimum range) AND max(high - low, configured minimum range) > its configured multiple of the SMA of high - low AND wick condition AND, when enabled, either close crosses above the shortest average or close > fast upper bound AND, when enabled, shortest average > its prior value and second average >= its prior value AND, when enabled, close >= slow lower bound AND turnaround is false. Reset the shared turnaround-used flag on every slow bearish bar, before evaluating turnaround and ignition.
Calculation: Re-acceleration = Fast pair available AND fast bearish condition is false AND close > fast upper bound AND the count of lower-low events in the configured preceding pullback window >= its configured minimum, with equality permitted when configured AND high > highest high over the configured preceding higher-high window AND close > open AND the number of bars since the previous bar's most recent higher-high event >= configured pause, or no such event exists AND wick condition AND bars since the previous re-acceleration > configured cooldown, or none exists AND bars since the preceding turnaround or ignition > configured suppression interval, or none exists AND the trend lock does not suppress it AND the re-acceleration reset gate is open. Initially the gate is open; when its reset requirement is enabled, close < open OR low <= shortest average opens it, and a re-acceleration closes it.
Calculation: Compression = At least a configured number of preceding bars in a configured window have (high - low) / close < a configured multiple of that bar's SMA of (high - low) / close.
Calculation: Strong re-acceleration = Re-acceleration AND (compression OR volume / SMA(volume) >= configured threshold) AND bars since the previous strong re-acceleration > its configured cooldown, or none exists AND (the compression-reset requirement is disabled OR a bar without compression has occurred since the previous strong re-acceleration OR both compression and the volume threshold hold). Initially the compression-reset condition is satisfied; clear it on strong re-acceleration and restore it on any bar without compression.
Calculation: Trend lock = Initially unlocked. Before evaluating re-acceleration, unlock when the decline from the highest close in the configured reset window to current low, divided by that highest close, >= configured percentage OR that decline divided by ATR >= configured multiple OR, when enabled, close < second average or fast bearish condition holds OR, when enabled, close < slow upper bound. Lock after a strong re-acceleration and after a turnaround or ignition when the respective options are enabled. A locked state suppresses re-acceleration when locking is enabled AND no turnaround or ignition has occurred OR the configured maturity interval has elapsed since the last turnaround or ignition. Evaluate re-acceleration before the current bar's turnaround and ignition.
Calculation: Efficiency = Absolute change in close over the configured lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the sum is zero.
Calculation: Extension = (close - shortest average) / ATR, or zero when ATR is not positive or the fast pair is unavailable.
Calculation: Maturity = Weighted sum of capped duration, extension, and efficiency components. Duration is bars since the last turnaround or ignition divided by its full-duration parameter, capped at one, or zero when no turn exists. Extension is max(extension, zero) divided by its full-extension parameter, capped at one. Efficiency is efficiency divided by the greater of its high-efficiency threshold and a configured positive floor, capped at one. Duration and extension have equal weights, each twice the efficiency weight, and the weights sum to one.
Calculation: Exhaustion = Extension >= exhaustion threshold OR maturity >= exhaustion maturity threshold AND extension >= extended threshold OR efficiency <= low-efficiency threshold AND aligned condition AND (extension >= extended threshold OR maturity >= mature threshold) OR shortest-average slope over the configured slope lookback > zero AND its change from the preceding equal-length slope < zero AND aligned condition AND (extension >= extended threshold OR maturity >= mature threshold) AND extension >= extended threshold.
Calculation: Trend stage = With no previous turnaround or ignition, no active turn; otherwise choose the first true condition in this order: exhaustion; recent turn AND positive change in shortest-average slope AND current fast-pair width > SMA of that width; aligned condition AND (extension >= extended threshold OR maturity >= mature threshold); aligned condition AND bars since turn >= structural-duration threshold AND close > slow upper bound AND maturity >= structural-maturity threshold; aligned condition AND efficiency >= high-efficiency threshold AND extension < extended threshold; aligned condition; otherwise transition. The corresponding stages are exhaustion, expansion, mature, structural, persistent, developing, and transition. If stage persistence is enabled, retain the previous stage until the new candidate has remained unchanged for the configured number of bars; initialize immediately.
Calculation: Chase risk = Aligned condition AND extension >= chase threshold.
Calculation: Optimal pullback = Aligned condition AND low <= fast upper bound AND close >= fast lower bound AND extension <= configured pullback extension threshold AND exhaustion is false.
Calculation: Core hold = Persisted stage is structural or persistent AND chase risk is false AND exhaustion is false.
Calculation: Opportunity = Choose the first true condition in this order: exhaustion OR persisted stage is mature AND chase risk; current turnaround or ignition; current re-acceleration AND chase risk is false; core hold AND optimal pullback; aligned condition AND efficiency <= compression-efficiency threshold AND absolute extension <= near-ribbon threshold AND extension < pullback extension threshold; optimal pullback; chase risk; core hold; aligned condition; persisted stage is transition; both pairs available; otherwise no edge. The corresponding states are reduce, early entry, re-acceleration, add on pullback, compression, optimal pullback, chase risk, core hold, hold or wait, watchlist, neutral, and no edge.
Calculation: Risk = High when exhaustion OR extension >= high-risk threshold OR opportunity is reduce; otherwise elevated when chase risk OR extension >= elevated-risk threshold OR persisted stage is mature; otherwise low when aligned condition AND extension <= low-risk threshold AND efficiency >= low-efficiency threshold; otherwise normal when aligned condition, and unclear otherwise.
Calculation: Ideal entry state = Aligned condition AND fast lower bound > slow upper bound AND maturity is between the configured inclusive minimum and maximum AND efficiency >= configured minimum AND extension <= configured maximum AND opportunity is compression, optimal pullback, add on pullback, or re-acceleration, or optionally core hold AND risk is low or optionally normal AND exhaustion is false AND chase risk is false AND, when required, current re-acceleration or continuation is present. Without the fresh-signal requirement, the ideal entry event occurs only on transition into this state; with it, the event occurs on every qualifying bar.
Calculation: Structural deterioration = Fast bearish condition OR close < fast lower bound OR close < slow upper bound when the slow pair is available OR efficiency <= low-efficiency threshold AND close < fast upper bound.
Calculation: Healthy trend = Aligned condition AND close > fast lower bound AND fast bearish condition is false AND, when the slow pair is available, close > slow lower bound AND efficiency >= low-efficiency threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive extension limit = The ordinary extended threshold when adaptation is disabled; otherwise select the high-efficiency, middle-efficiency, or low-efficiency extension limit according to whether efficiency is above the high threshold, above the lower middle threshold, or neither. The allowed extension decreases across those three groups.
Calculation: Build condition = Ideal entry state OR aligned condition AND current re-acceleration or continuation AND maturity within its configured inclusive build range AND efficiency >= build minimum AND extension <= build maximum AND risk is not high AND chase risk is false AND opportunity is neither reduce nor chase risk.
Calculation: Extended condition = Healthy trend AND build condition is false AND structural deterioration is false AND (extension >= adaptive extension limit AND maturity >= extended maturity threshold OR chase risk OR risk is high OR opportunity is reduce OR maturity >= extended maturity threshold AND extension >= separate extended threshold). It also holds after such a bar for the configured persistence window while healthy trend holds AND maturity > configured persistence threshold AND build condition is false.
Calculation: Extended-hold condition = Healthy trend AND build condition is false AND the raw extended condition is false AND maturity >= extended maturity threshold AND (opportunity is core hold or hold or wait OR risk is normal or low).
Calculation: Small-entry condition = Aligned condition AND current re-acceleration or continuation AND build and extended conditions are false AND risk is not high AND (risk is elevated OR opportunity is chase risk OR maturity > configured small-entry threshold OR extension > configured small-entry threshold).
Calculation: Raw trim condition = Opportunity is reduce AND structural deterioration OR maturity >= extended maturity threshold AND extension >= adaptive extension limit AND structural deterioration OR maturity >= extended maturity threshold AND extension >= adaptive extension limit AND persisted stage is exhaustion AND (upper-wick fraction > configured maximum OR close < open OR close < fast upper bound) OR risk is high AND structural deterioration. Trim is confirmed after this condition remains true for the configured consecutive confirmation bars.
Calculation: Review condition = (Persisted stage is exhaustion OR fast bearish condition AND close < fast upper bound OR slow pair available AND close < slow upper bound AND close < third average OR aligned condition is false AND persisted stage is transition AND close < fast lower bound AND efficiency <= low-efficiency threshold) AND at least the configured review interval has elapsed since the last bar satisfying aligned condition AND close > fast lower bound AND fast bearish condition is false. Review is confirmed after the condition remains true for the configured consecutive confirmation bars, provided trim is not confirmed and extended-hold condition is false.
Calculation: Portfolio event = A portfolio condition is true after being false on the preceding bar AND more than the configured cooldown has elapsed since any portfolio event, or none exists. Update the shared cooldown when any build, small-entry, extended, extended-hold, hold, trim, review, neutral, or watchlist condition has such an event, including conditions without an entry or exit marking. Hold requires alignment or a healthy rotational trend and excludes build, small, extended, extended-hold, trim, and review; neutral and watchlist follow their opportunity and trend-state classifications.
Calculation: Scanner regime = Review condition gives deteriorating regime; otherwise a healthy aligned trend with close > fast upper bound and efficiency >= low-efficiency threshold gives structural, developing, or transition regime according to descending configured maturity and efficiency thresholds; otherwise neutral. Scanner risk is elevated for review, high when extension >= the scanner high-risk threshold, elevated at the lower scanner extension threshold, low when regime is at least transition AND efficiency >= scanner efficiency threshold, and normal otherwise.
Long: Fast bullish condition OR slow bullish condition.
Short: Fast bearish condition OR slow bearish condition.
Long: Turnaround OR ignition, AND confirmed trim is false AND confirmed review is false.
Long: Re-acceleration AND structural deterioration is false AND risk is not high AND confirmed trim is false AND confirmed review is false.
Long: Standard continuation OR rotational continuation, AND confirmed trim is false AND confirmed review is false.
Long: Ideal entry event.
Long: Portfolio event for build condition OR portfolio event for small-entry condition.
Long: Scanner regime is at least transition AND scanner risk is low or normal AND extension < configured scanner entry threshold AND efficiency >= configured scanner minimum, subject to scanner-state priority: high risk, core hold, early trend, fresh money, watchlist, then neutral.
Exit: long, Opportunity is reduce.
Exit: long, Portfolio event for confirmed trim.
