Short: The short conditions are true now and were false on the prior bar AND prior close is between the prior lower band minus 0.25 standard deviations and the prior lower band plus 0.25 standard deviations AND current dollar volume is at least the minimum dollar-volume threshold AND developing POC is between those same prior lower-band offsets AND developing value-area low is between the prior lower band minus 0.5 standard deviations and the prior lower band.
