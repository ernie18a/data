Calculation: WaveTrend = Let source be a selectable price series, defaulting to (high + low + close) / 3. Let center = EMA(source, channel length), deviation = EMA(abs(source - center), channel length), fast = EMA((source - center) / (0.015 * deviation), averaging length), and slow = SMA(fast, smoothing length).
Calculation: Money flow = SMA(multiplier * (close - open) / (high - low), lookback) - offset.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = SMA(stochastic of RSI of either the selected source or its logarithm, stochastic lookback), K smoothing length); optionally replace this value with its average with its SMA over a D smoothing length.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = An oscillator value two bars ago strictly greater than, for a top, or strictly less than, for a bottom, each of the oscillator values on the two bars before and the two bars after it. A threshold-limited top must also be at or above its upper threshold; a threshold-limited bottom must also be at or below its lower threshold. Compare its oscillator value and the high or low of its bar with those of the most recent qualifying pivot whose confirmation occurred at least two bars before the current bar.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = A confirmed oscillator bottom AND its bar's low < the previous qualifying bottom's bar low AND its oscillator value > the previous qualifying bottom's oscillator value.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = A confirmed oscillator top AND its bar's high > the previous qualifying top's bar high AND its oscillator value < the previous qualifying top's oscillator value.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = A confirmed oscillator bottom AND its bar's low > the previous qualifying bottom's bar low AND its oscillator value < the previous qualifying bottom's oscillator value.
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence = A confirmed oscillator top AND its bar's high < the previous qualifying top's bar high AND its oscillator value > the previous qualifying top's oscillator value.
Long: WaveTrend fast crosses above or equals WaveTrend slow AND WaveTrend slow <= oversold threshold.
Short: WaveTrend fast crosses below or equals WaveTrend slow AND WaveTrend slow >= overbought threshold.
Long: Regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend slow using either of two bottom thresholds, or in RSI using a bottom threshold, or in Stochastic RSI without a threshold.
Short: Regular bearish divergence in WaveTrend slow using either of two top thresholds, or in RSI using a top threshold, or in Stochastic RSI without a threshold.
Long: Hidden bullish divergence in WaveTrend slow or RSI, using either its threshold-limited pivots or its unrestricted pivots, or in Stochastic RSI using unrestricted pivots.
Short: Hidden bearish divergence in WaveTrend slow or RSI, using either its threshold-limited pivots or its unrestricted pivots, or in Stochastic RSI using unrestricted pivots.
Long: Regular bullish divergence in threshold-limited WaveTrend slow or RSI AND the previous qualifying WaveTrend bottom <= deep oversold threshold AND current WaveTrend slow > deep oversold threshold AND current WaveTrend slow - previous qualifying WaveTrend bottom >= minimum rise AND RSI at the previous qualifying WaveTrend bottom < RSI threshold.
Long: Money flow > bullish threshold AND WaveTrend slow < bullish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses above or equals WaveTrend slow AND higher-timeframe WaveTrend fast - slow > bullish threshold; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Money flow < bearish threshold AND WaveTrend slow > bearish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses below or equals WaveTrend slow AND higher-timeframe WaveTrend fast - slow < bearish threshold; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: WaveTrend slow <= bullish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses above or equals WaveTrend slow AND Heikin-Ashi close > open on each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other; use each timeframe's last completed bar.
Short: WaveTrend slow >= bearish threshold AND WaveTrend fast crosses below or equals WaveTrend slow AND Heikin-Ashi close <= open on each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other; use each timeframe's last completed bar.
Exit: long, high >= entry price * (1 + target percentage) OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop percentage).
Exit: long, close < higher-timeframe SMA of close OR previous close < the previous chart bar's higher-timeframe SMA of close; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
