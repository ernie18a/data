Calculation: Trend Thrust Indicator (TTI) = (short-term VWMA of close × (short-term VWMA of close / long-term VWMA of close)²) − (long-term VWMA of close / (short-term VWMA of close / long-term VWMA of close)²); its signal line is the short-term SMA of TTI
Calculation: Volume Price Confirmation Indicator (VPCI) = (long-term VWMA of close − long-term SMA of close) × (short-term VWMA of close / short-term SMA of close) × (short-term SMA of volume / long-term SMA of volume)
Long: ADX > its threshold AND TTI > its signal line AND VPCI > 0
Exit: long, VPCI < 0
