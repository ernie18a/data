Long: Shorter-lookback SMA crosses above longer-lookback SMA OR (close > any of the progressively longer-lookback SMAs for three consecutive bars AND the close three bars ago < that SMA three bars ago), AND VIX close from the higher-timeframe bar still forming < threshold
Calculation: Ratchet stop = While long, advance the stop stage from 0 to 1 when close profit from entry first reaches the stage-1 trigger, saving that close as the stage-1 reference; advance to stage 2 when close profit reaches the stage-2 trigger. Stage-0 stop = entry price × (1 − initial stop distance); stage-1 stop = saved reference close × (1 − stage-1 stop distance); stage-2 stop = highest high since entry × (1 − trailing distance). Active stop is the greater of the prior active stop and the current stage stop; reset stage, reference close, highest high, and active stop when flat.
Exit: Long, low <= ratchet stop OR shorter-lookback SMA crosses below longer-lookback SMA OR VIX close from the higher-timeframe bar still forming > threshold
