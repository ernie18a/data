Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when the current bar's low exceeds the high two bars earlier, with lower and upper boundaries at those two prices; bearish when the current bar's high is below the low two bars earlier, with lower and upper boundaries at those two prices.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe zone = separately for bullish and bearish gaps, retain the most recently detected gap among three higher timeframes, giving the longest timeframe priority when gaps exist simultaneously. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it. Retain each zone until another gap of the same direction replaces it.
Calculation: Recent bullish sweep = the current low is at or below the latest confirmed swing low and the current close is above it, or that condition occurred within a lookback. A confirmed swing low is a low below both its immediately adjacent lows, identified two chart bars after the swing bar. Recent bearish sweep is the symmetric condition using a swing high, high at or above it, and close below it.
Calculation: Liquidity condition = within a lookback of prior chart bars, the count of highs greater than their immediately preceding highs reaches a threshold OR the count of lows lower than their immediately preceding lows reaches a threshold.
Calculation: Session condition = chart bar is in the core session OR chart bar is in the extended session AND ATR exceeds a multiple of its SMA. Session times use the New York timezone.
Calculation: Directional score = 2 points if close is inside the corresponding retained higher-timeframe zone, plus 2 points for the corresponding recent sweep, plus 1 point each for the session condition, the liquidity condition, and the optional cross-instrument confirmation being disabled. Cross-instrument confirmation cannot be evaluated from the specified data; use the disabled setting.
Calculation: Signal availability = separately for each direction, initially available; after a qualifying signal in that direction, unavailable until close is outside both retained higher-timeframe zones OR, for long signals, high exceeds both the previous bar's high and the highest high seen before the current bar, OR, for short signals, low is below both the previous bar's low and the lowest low seen before the current bar. Apply resets before evaluating the current bar's signals.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is present on any of the selected higher timeframes, using its still-forming bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars earlier.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is present on any of the selected higher timeframes, using its still-forming bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars earlier.
Long: A bearish chart-timeframe fair value gap formed within the maximum age selected for the chart timeframe AND close is above its upper boundary AND high minus low exceeds a multiple of ATR AND absolute close minus open exceeds a fraction of high minus low AND long signal availability is true AND bullish directional score reaches the selected minimum grade's score threshold.
Short: A bullish chart-timeframe fair value gap formed within the maximum age selected for the chart timeframe AND close is below its lower boundary AND high minus low exceeds a multiple of ATR AND absolute close minus open exceeds a fraction of high minus low AND short signal availability is true AND bearish directional score reaches the selected minimum grade's score threshold.
