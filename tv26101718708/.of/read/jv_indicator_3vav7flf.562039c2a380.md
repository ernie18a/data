Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = the gap where the low of the current chart bar is above the high two chart bars earlier; zone bounds are those two prices.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = the gap where the high of the current chart bar is below the low two chart bars earlier; zone bounds are those two prices.
Calculation: Impulsive candle = current bar range > ATR × configured impulsive multiplier AND body size > configured fraction of current bar range.
Calculation: Bullish HTF FVG zone = latest available bullish FVG among higher-timeframe daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour bars, preferring daily, then 4-hour, then 1-hour; use the still-forming bar for detection.
Calculation: Bearish HTF FVG zone = latest available bearish FVG among higher-timeframe daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour bars, preferring daily, then 4-hour, then 1-hour; use the still-forming bar for detection.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = price reaches or falls below the latest confirmed swing low and closes above it; swing low has a lower low than the adjacent chart bars on each side. Valid for the configured recent-bar window.
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = price reaches or rises above the latest confirmed swing high and closes below it; swing high has a higher high than the adjacent chart bars on each side. Valid for the configured recent-bar window.
Calculation: Trendline/equal-high-low liquidity present = either the count of higher highs across the configured recent lookback or the count of lower lows across that lookback meets its configured threshold.
Calculation: Session eligibility = within the configured core session OR within the configured extended session while ATR exceeds its moving average multiplied by the configured volatility multiplier.
Calculation: ES key-level proximity = ES close is within the configured percentage of either prior-day high or prior-day low; use the same chart timeframe for ES close and the prior completed daily bar for prior-day levels.
Calculation: Direction score = 2 points for price inside the matching HTF FVG zone, 2 for a matching recent liquidity sweep, 1 for session eligibility, 1 for SMT confirmation (or SMT filter disabled), and 1 for trendline/equal-high-low liquidity. Convert score to grade using increasing score thresholds; require grade at or above the configured minimum.
Long: close > the top of a bearish FVG formed within the configured inversion age AND current candle is impulsive AND no long signal has fired in the current manipulation leg AND bullish grade passes the configured minimum.
Short: close < the bottom of a bullish FVG formed within the configured inversion age AND current candle is impulsive AND no short signal has fired in the current manipulation leg AND bearish grade passes the configured minimum.
