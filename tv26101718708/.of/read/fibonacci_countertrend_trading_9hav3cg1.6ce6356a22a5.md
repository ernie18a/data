Calculation: Upper/lower Fibonacci level = volume-weighted moving average of source price ± selected ratio (0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.764, or 1) × standard deviation of source price × multiplier; both calculations use the same lookback
Long: Source price crosses below the selected lower Fibonacci level
Short: Source price crosses above the selected upper Fibonacci level
Exit: Long, source price crosses the volume-weighted moving average OR the short signal has occurred and low <= selected lower Fibonacci level
Exit: Short, source price crosses the volume-weighted moving average OR the long signal has occurred and high >= selected upper Fibonacci level
