Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars starting at the configured session anchor and spanning the configured opening-range duration, expanded to at least one chart bar; lock the range on the first subsequent bar. Reset session state at each new anchored session. Range width = range high minus range low; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Breakout state = after the range locks, record the first close above the range high and first close below the range low separately. Count a long retest when a later bar closes above the range high and its low is at most the range high plus an ATR tolerance; count another only after the low has left that tolerance. Mirror this for short retests. Count consecutive closes beyond each edge. Record the first high above the range high that closes at or below it and the first low below the range low that closes at or above it, including their extremes and bar indices.
Calculation: Trend score = weighted average of fast/middle/slow EMA alignment, directional ADX, higher-timeframe trend and fast-EMA slope normalized by ATR. Assign bullish alignment a high score, bearish alignment a low score and mixed alignment a neutral score; orient ADX by the sign of positive DI minus negative DI; use the last completed higher-timeframe close versus its EMA, or neutral if unavailable; clamp normalized slope around a neutral midpoint. Fast, middle and slow EMAs use increasing lookbacks.
Calculation: Momentum score = weighted average of clamped scores for RSI and its change over a lookback, MACD histogram normalized by ATR with an adjustment when its absolute value expands, close change over a lookback normalized by ATR, and candle body normalized by ATR. Each directional component is centered on a neutral midpoint.
Calculation: Directional volume score = weighted combination of relative volume, volume percentile rank, a relative-volume spike indicator and whether OBV is above its EMA for long or below it for short; use neutral values when volume is unavailable. Relative volume is volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Volatility score = weighted average of clamped ratios of ATR to its moving average, Bollinger Band width to its moving average, and current high-low range to its moving average.
Calculation: Directional structure score = weighted average of session VWAP alignment, ATR-normalized distance to the nearest resistance above close or support below close, close beyond the latest confirmed swing high or low, and session-opening gap alignment. Resistance candidates are previous-day high, latest confirmed swing high and premarket high; support candidates are the corresponding lows. Gap is session open minus previous session close divided by current ATR, with a stronger score when the open is beyond the previous day's corresponding extreme. A swing is confirmed only after the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Directional breakout-quality score = neutral unless close is beyond the corresponding locked range edge; otherwise use a weighted average of a stepped score for breakout distance in ATRs, absolute candle body divided by range, consecutive closes beyond the edge, elapsed time since range lock and whether at least one corresponding retest has occurred. Distance scores favor a moderate positive breakout over a small or extended one.
Calculation: Directional liquidity score = a clamped neutral score increased by a prior sweep of the opposite range edge and by the recent rolling extreme being within an ATR tolerance of the corresponding previous-day extreme, and decreased by a prior sweep of the same range edge.
Calculation: Regime = classify in priority order using ADX and ATR relative to its average as strong trend, ADX as trend, choppiness as choppy, ATR relative to its average as high or low volatility, ADX as weak trend, otherwise range. Choppiness is the logarithm of summed true range divided by the rolling high-low span, normalized by the logarithm of its lookback. A narrow opening range relative to the average of recent session ranges adds a breakout-day adjustment.
Calculation: Directional confidence = clamped regime-weighted average of trend, momentum, directional volume, volatility, directional structure, directional breakout quality and directional liquidity scores; invert trend and momentum for shorts. Trend regimes increase the trend weight and reduce breakout-quality and liquidity weights; range or choppy regimes do the reverse. Effective threshold = base threshold plus regime, breakout-day and risk-mode adjustments, clamped to the score range.
Calculation: Directional veto count = number of comparisons true among an excessive opposing wick fraction, MACD histogram weakening for consecutive bars, close excessively extended from the fast EMA in ATR units, and nearest resistance or support closer than an ATR multiple. The allowed count depends on risk mode.
Calculation: Qualified long = range locked AND elapsed time since session start at most the cutoff AND enabled ATR-percent, relative-volume, trend, VWAP and wide-range filters pass AND (close above range high OR, in touch mode, high above range high OR, when enabled, a prior low sweep within the reversal window AND close above midpoint) AND conservative confirmation passes AND long retest count reaches the required count AND long confidence reaches the effective threshold AND long veto count is at most its risk-mode limit. Conservative confirmation requires consecutive closes above the range high or at least one long retest. The wide-range filter compares range width with both ATR and the average of recent session range widths; the latter includes the current range.
Calculation: Qualified short = range locked AND elapsed time since session start at most the cutoff AND enabled ATR-percent, relative-volume, trend, VWAP and wide-range filters pass AND (close below range low OR, in touch mode, low below range low OR, when enabled, a prior high sweep within the reversal window AND close below midpoint) AND conservative confirmation passes AND short retest count reaches the required count AND short confidence reaches the effective threshold AND short veto count is at most its risk-mode limit. Conservative confirmation requires consecutive closes below the range low or at least one short retest.
Calculation: KNN vote = fraction of wins among the nearest stored resolved qualified-breakout episodes, using the sum of log(1 + absolute feature difference) over direction-oriented trend, momentum, volume, volatility, structure and breakout-quality scores. Log one candidate per episode, including candidates blocked by trading permission or the KNN gate; unlock logging only after both the corresponding close-beyond streak and qualification end. From the next bar, record a loss if the candidate stop is touched, otherwise a win if its one-risk-unit target is touched; discard an unresolved candidate after the outcome window. Check stops first and retain only the configured number of latest resolved outcomes. If both directions qualify on a bar, use the short-oriented fingerprint for both.
Calculation: Entry and stop = entry is the broken range edge for a touch-mode breakout and otherwise the close. The initial stop is the swept extreme for a sweep reversal; otherwise select entry minus or plus an ATR multiple, range midpoint, opposite range edge, or entry-bar low or high. Constrain the stop to remain at least a minimum ATR-based distance from entry. Risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance.
Calculation: Targets = for the risk ladder, entry plus or minus successively larger risk multiples; for range projection, the corresponding range edge plus or minus successively larger range-width multiples, constrained to at least corresponding risk-based distances from entry. For the dynamic method, successive targets are the closes satisfying the dynamic EMA exit conditions. After the first target, optionally move the stop to entry.
Long: Range locked AND first close of the session above range high.
Short: Range locked AND first close of the session below range low.
Long: Range locked AND a new long retest episode is confirmed.
Short: Range locked AND a new short retest episode is confirmed.
Long: Range locked AND long direction unused AND elapsed time since session start at most the cutoff AND long confidence at least the effective threshold minus the watch margin.
Short: Range locked AND short direction unused AND elapsed time since session start at most the cutoff AND short confidence at least the effective threshold minus the watch margin.
Long: Trend score at least the bullish trend threshold.
Short: Trend score at most the bearish trend threshold.
Long: Range locked AND long confidence exceeds short confidence by more than the bias margin.
Short: Range locked AND short confidence exceeds long confidence by more than the bias margin.
Long: Qualified long AND session signal count below its limit AND long direction unused AND outside the blackout AND manual stand-aside disabled AND first-break-direction restriction passes AND (KNN gate inactive OR KNN vote at least its threshold). A used direction may be rearmed after its false breakout; the first-break restriction blocks a direction only when the opposite close-break occurred earlier. The KNN gate becomes active only after its library, warm-up and neighbor-count minima are all reached.
Short: Qualified short AND session signal count below its limit AND short direction unused AND outside the blackout AND manual stand-aside disabled AND first-break-direction restriction passes AND (KNN gate inactive OR KNN vote at least its threshold). A used direction may be rearmed after its false breakout; the first-break restriction blocks a direction only when the opposite close-break occurred earlier. The KNN gate becomes active only after its library, warm-up and neighbor-count minima are all reached.
Exit: long, a later bar's low reaches the active stop OR the corresponding entry-bar stop is reached in touch mode; for a breakout-candle stop on the touch entry bar use close instead of low.
Exit: short, a later bar's high reaches the active stop OR the corresponding entry-bar stop is reached in touch mode; for a breakout-candle stop on the touch entry bar use close instead of high.
Exit: long, high reaches an unhit risk-ladder or range-projection target in sequence OR, for the first target on a touch entry bar, high reaches that target. Resolve a simultaneous stop and target using the configured priority; when target-first is selected, suppress the stop on a bar that records a target.
Exit: short, low reaches an unhit risk-ladder or range-projection target in sequence OR, for the first target on a touch entry bar, low reaches that target. Resolve a simultaneous stop and target using the configured priority; when target-first is selected, suppress the stop on a bar that records a target.
Exit: long, dynamic method selected AND fast EMA exceeds entry plus an ATR offset for the first target or the preceding target close plus a smaller ATR offset for a subsequent target AND close is below the fast EMA AND the corresponding target has not already been hit.
Exit: short, dynamic method selected AND fast EMA is below entry minus an ATR offset for the first target or the preceding target close minus a smaller ATR offset for a subsequent target AND close is above the fast EMA AND the corresponding target has not already been hit.
Exit: long, before the first target and within the false-breakout window after a breakout entry, close falls below range high AND (previous close was at or above range high OR the touch-entry bar closed below range high).
Exit: short, before the first target and within the false-breakout window after a breakout entry, close rises above range low AND (previous close was at or below range low OR the touch-entry bar closed above range low).
Exit: long, a short entry occurs OR a new anchored session starts.
Exit: short, a long entry occurs OR a new anchored session starts.
