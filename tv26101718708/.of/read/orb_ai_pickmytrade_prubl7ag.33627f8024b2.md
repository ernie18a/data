Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured session-opening window; lock both when the window ends and reset at the next session.
Calculation: Long/short confidence = weighted composite of trend, momentum, volume, volatility, structure, breakout quality, and liquidity scores; use direction-oriented scores for each side, with regime-dependent weights and threshold adjustment.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = last completed bar's close compared with its EMA on a higher timeframe; ignore when the chart timeframe is not lower.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average; ATR percentage = ATR divided by close; wide opening range = opening-range width exceeds its recent-session average or ATR-based limit.
Long: opening range is locked AND (close > opening-range high OR low < opening-range low AND close > opening-range midpoint within the false-breakout window after a sweep below the range) AND long confidence >= effective threshold AND all enabled filters pass AND veto count <= risk-mode limit AND required retests and confirmation pass AND session signal limits, blackout, news, and first-break-direction restrictions pass AND (KNN gate is inactive OR winning-neighbour share >= minimum vote); evaluate on confirmed bar close. Also mark a long-direction breakout when close > opening-range high, and a long-direction retest when, after such a close, low returns within the ATR tolerance of the range high and close remains above it.
Short: opening range is locked AND (close < opening-range low OR high > opening-range high AND close < opening-range midpoint within the false-breakout window after a sweep above the range) AND short confidence >= effective threshold AND all enabled filters pass AND veto count <= risk-mode limit AND required retests and confirmation pass AND session signal limits, blackout, news, and first-break-direction restrictions pass AND (KNN gate is inactive OR winning-neighbour share >= minimum vote); evaluate on confirmed bar close. Also mark a short-direction breakout when close < opening-range low, and a short-direction retest when, after such a close, high returns within the ATR tolerance of the range low and close remains below it.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= an active target OR (dynamic EMA target mode and close < fast EMA while its ATR-scaled profit condition is met) OR close returns inside the opening range within the false-breakout window after entry OR session rolls over OR a short signal fires.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= an active target OR (dynamic EMA target mode and close > fast EMA while its ATR-scaled profit condition is met) OR close returns inside the opening range within the false-breakout window after entry OR session rolls over OR a long signal fires.
