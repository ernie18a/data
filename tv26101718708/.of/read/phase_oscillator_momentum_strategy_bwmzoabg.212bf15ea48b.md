Calculation: Phase oscillator = EMA of ((close − EMA(close, longer lookback)) ÷ (ATR over a lookback × volatility scale) × scale), with a shorter lookback for the smoothing EMA than for the close EMA, using chart-timeframe bars
Long: Phase oscillator crosses above the selected upper signal level AND time is within the configured entry window AND time is within the configured date range AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR |close − session VWAP| ≤ configured ATR-distance limit × ATR)
Short: Phase oscillator crosses below the selected lower signal level AND time is within the configured entry window AND time is within the configured date range AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR |close − session VWAP| ≤ configured ATR-distance limit × ATR)
Exit: Long, Phase oscillator crosses below the selected exit level OR close ≤ entry price × (1 − configured hard-stop percentage) OR price reaches the trailing stop after rising by the configured trail distance OR configured flat time is reached OR time is outside the regular session
Exit: Short, Phase oscillator crosses above the selected exit level OR close ≥ entry price × (1 + configured hard-stop percentage) OR price reaches the trailing stop after falling by the configured trail distance OR configured flat time is reached OR time is outside the regular session
