Calculation: Swing low and swing high = respectively the lowest low and highest high over the swing lookback ending at the previous chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < swing low AND close > swing low; bearish sweep = high > swing high AND close < swing high.
Calculation: Bullish order block formation = each of the preceding impulse-lookback bars has close > open AND the bar immediately before them has close < open; bearish order block formation = each of the preceding impulse-lookback bars has close < open AND the bar immediately before them has close > open.
Calculation: Active order blocks = for each direction retain the high and low of the candle immediately before the most recent corresponding impulse; first remove an existing bullish block when close < its low or an existing bearish block when close > its high, then replace it if a new block of that direction forms on the current bar.
Calculation: Sweep score = compute the following on every chart bar, using a positive tick-size parameter: bullish depth = swing low - low + tick size; bearish depth = high - swing high + tick size; wick factor = min(1, (close - low) / bullish depth) on a bullish sweep, otherwise min(1, (high - close) / bearish depth) on a bearish sweep, otherwise (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0.5; volume factor = volume; delta = volume * (2 * close - low - high) / (high - low + tick size); recovery factor = min(2, (close - swing low) / bullish depth) on a bullish sweep, otherwise min(2, (swing high - close) / bearish depth) on a bearish sweep, otherwise 0. For each factor, calculate its rolling z-score over the same normalization lookback including the current bar, using population standard deviation; use zero when the standard deviation is zero or unavailable. Negate the delta z-score on a bearish sweep unless there is also a bullish sweep; use its absolute value when neither sweep occurs. Transform each z-score to max(0, min(1, (z + 3) / 6)). Session factor = 1 during the first configured session, otherwise 0.85 during the second configured session, otherwise 0.45; compare the bar's opening hour in the configured timezone with each session's start hour inclusive and start hour plus window width exclusive, without wrapping across midnight. Score = min(100, 100 * (0.28 * transformed wick + 0.20 * transformed volume + 0.25 * transformed delta + 0.17 * transformed recovery + 0.10 * session factor) + order-block bonus). Apply the bonus once when a bullish sweep's swing low lies inclusively within the active bullish block OR a bearish sweep's swing high lies inclusively within the active bearish block; otherwise the bonus is zero.
Calculation: Qualified long = bullish sweep AND sweep score >= score threshold AND close > EMA of close; qualified short = bearish sweep AND sweep score >= score threshold AND close < EMA of close. A qualified long or short entry occurs when its qualification is true on the current bar and false on the previous bar.
Long: bullish sweep OR bullish order block formation.
Short: bearish sweep OR bearish order block formation.
Exit: long, qualified short entry = true.
Exit: short, qualified long entry = true.
