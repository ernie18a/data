Calculation: Prior range = highest high and lowest low over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar; fall back to the current high or low when unavailable.
Calculation: Trend = bullish when the shorter-period close EMA > the longer-period close EMA AND the longer EMA's change over its slope lookback >= 0 AND close >= the shorter EMA; bearish when the shorter EMA < the longer EMA AND its change <= 0 AND close <= the shorter EMA.
Calculation: Invalidation candidate = the lowest prior low over the invalidation lookback minus ATR times an ATR buffer for long, or the highest prior high over that lookback plus ATR times the buffer for short; exclude the current bar and fall back to its low or high when unavailable. Risk in ATR units = absolute difference between close and the candidate divided by ATR. Use true range as the ATR fallback and a positive minimum for zero denominators.
Calculation: Activation score = weighted sum of trend, candle, risk-fit and volume scores. Trend score is the high level when the corresponding trend holds, an intermediate level when close is beyond the longer EMA in the candidate direction, and a low level otherwise. Candle score is a weighted sum of absolute candle body divided by high minus low and directional close location; directional close location is (close minus low) divided by high minus low for long and its complement for short, clamped to the unit interval. Risk-fit score rises linearly from zero to a lower score at the minimum permitted risk, rises to the maximum score at an ideal risk clamped between the permitted bounds, falls linearly to a higher score at the maximum permitted risk, and is zero above the maximum; clamp each segment to its endpoint scores. Volume score is a linear normalization of volume divided by its volume-lookback SMA between lower and upper reference ratios, clamped to the score range; use a neutral score if volume or its SMA is unavailable or nonpositive. Use a positive minimum for zero denominators.
Calculation: Active cycle = begin at an activation with entry equal to that bar's close and stop equal to its invalidation candidate; end after output on the first subsequent bar whose low touches or falls below the long stop, whose high touches or rises above the short stop, or whose age reaches the maximum tracking bars. Do not activate another cycle on the bar that ends a cycle.
Long: No active cycle AND bar index > structure lookback AND long side is enabled AND (range-break mode is enabled AND close > prior range high AND close > open OR trend-pulse mode is enabled AND bullish trend AND close > shorter EMA AND low <= shorter EMA AND directional close location >= its threshold) AND long invalidation candidate < close AND long risk in ATR units is within the permitted minimum and maximum inclusive AND long activation score >= its threshold AND (short candidate is invalid OR long activation score >= short activation score).
Short: No active cycle AND bar index > structure lookback AND short side is enabled AND (range-break mode is enabled AND close < prior range low AND close < open OR trend-pulse mode is enabled AND bearish trend AND close < shorter EMA AND high >= shorter EMA AND directional close location >= its threshold) AND short invalidation candidate > close AND short risk in ATR units is within the permitted minimum and maximum inclusive AND short activation score >= its threshold AND (long candidate is invalid OR short activation score > long activation score).
Exit: long, active cycle age > 0 AND low <= the cycle's long invalidation candidate.
Exit: short, active cycle age > 0 AND high >= the cycle's short invalidation candidate.
