Calculation: Selected-timeframe values = moving averages of a configurable price source, with the slower average using twice the faster average's lookback, and ATR; use the chart timeframe or, for a higher timeframe, its last completed bar, or, for a lower timeframe, its last completed bar within each chart bar.
Calculation: Trend state = bullish when the faster selected-timeframe moving average is greater than the slower average; otherwise bearish. A trend change occurs when this Boolean state differs from its previous selected-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Adjusted average = faster selected-timeframe moving average minus selected-timeframe ATR in a bullish trend, or plus ATR in a bearish trend.
Calculation: Smoothed bands = Bollinger upper and lower bands of chart-timeframe typical price, each smoothed by a normalized one-sided Gaussian weighted average of current and past band values. If repaint smoothing is enabled, both decision bands equal zero.
Calculation: Retest memory = initialize the last retest bar index to zero and the signal-in-trend flag to false; reset the flag when the selected-timeframe trend-change value is true, before evaluating signals. After evaluating signals, set the flag to true and update the last retest bar index when either retest signal occurs.
Calculation: Cooldown = a configurable base number of chart bars, or, if adaptive cooldown is enabled, the rounded base multiplied by chart-timeframe long-lookback ATR divided by short-lookback ATR, subject to a configurable minimum; use a ratio of one if either ATR is unavailable or the short ATR is zero.
Long: Selected-timeframe low crosses above the adjusted average AND trend is bullish AND the selected-timeframe trend-change value is false AND chart bars since the last retest signal exceed cooldown AND (smart filter is disabled OR signal-in-trend is false) AND (volatility filter is disabled OR chart-timeframe short-lookback ATR exceeds a configurable multiple of medium-lookback ATR) AND (band filter is disabled OR close is above the smoothed lower band) AND the chart bar is confirmed.
Short: Selected-timeframe high crosses below the adjusted average AND trend is bearish AND the selected-timeframe trend-change value is false AND chart bars since the last retest signal exceed cooldown AND (smart filter is disabled OR signal-in-trend is false) AND (volatility filter is disabled OR chart-timeframe short-lookback ATR exceeds a configurable multiple of medium-lookback ATR) AND (band filter is disabled OR close is below the smoothed upper band) AND the chart bar is confirmed.
Long: Mean-reversion signals are enabled AND close crosses above the smoothed lower band AND signal-in-trend is false.
Short: Mean-reversion signals are enabled AND close crosses below the smoothed upper band AND signal-in-trend is false.
