Calculation: AvgVolProfile = over the current forming bar-period of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe (reset at each new period start, includes the current bar), build a price grid; each chart bar covers the grid levels from its rounded low to its rounded high and gives each covered level an equal share of the bar volume (volume / number of covered levels); for each level, AvgVol = sum of shares / number of bars that covered it
Calculation: HAV = price of the level with the highest AvgVol (if tied, the middle of the first and last tied levels)
Calculation: VAH, VAL = start at the HAV level and repeatedly add the adjacent level above or below, whichever has the larger AvgVol (above if equal; only the existing side if the other is exhausted), until the summed AvgVol of included levels >= value-area fraction * sum of AvgVol of all levels; VAH = the top included level, VAL = the bottom included level
Long: close crosses above HAV OR close crosses above VAH OR close crosses above VAL (close > level on this bar AND close <= the same level on the previous bar)
Short: close crosses below HAV OR close crosses below VAH OR close crosses below VAL (close < level on this bar AND close >= the same level on the previous bar)
