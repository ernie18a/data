Calculation: Estimated delta = selected candle-volume estimate: OHLC proportional to close position within high-low range; all volume positive when close >= open and negative otherwise; or volume weighted by lower wick plus bullish body versus upper wick plus bearish body. If high = low, use the close-versus-open sign rule. Optionally smooth with an EMA.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of estimated delta, optionally reset daily or weekly.
Calculation: Cumulative-delta EMA = EMA of cumulative delta.
Calculation: Buy/sell volume ratio = estimated buy volume divided by estimated sell volume; buy volume uses close position within the range for OHLC mode, all volume when close >= open for close/open mode, and half the volume for wick mode; sell volume is remaining volume.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish cumulative-delta divergence = confirmed pivot high in price exceeds the previous pivot high while the corresponding cumulative-delta pivot high is lower; or confirmed pivot low in price is below the previous pivot low while the corresponding cumulative-delta pivot low is higher. Use the same configurable pivot length for price and cumulative delta.
Calculation: Momentum change = cumulative delta change over a shorter lookback; momentum acceleration = change in that momentum change over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Aggression index = (estimated delta / volume * 100) * (volume / volume average).
Calculation: Weighted delta score = 0.4 * (estimated delta / volume * 100) + 0.3 * ((volume / volume average - 1) * 30) + 0.3 * (100 * (close - low) / (high - low) - 50); when the range is zero, close position is 50.
Calculation: Overall bias score = sum of +1 or -1 for positive or negative cumulative-delta change over a short lookback, positive or negative cumulative-delta change over a longer lookback, confirmed candle direction matching positive or negative delta, buy/sell volume ratio above 1.3 or below 0.77, and positive or negative daily cumulative delta; otherwise each contribution is 0. The longer lookback exceeds the shorter one.
Calculation: Consecutive delta count = number of consecutive bars with delta of the same sign, resetting on a zero or opposite-sign delta.
Long: bullish divergence OR high volume (volume >= volume average * high-volume multiplier) and delta > 0 OR absorption (volume >= volume average * high-volume multiplier and |delta / volume * 100| < absorption threshold) and close > open OR climax (volume >= volume average * climax multiplier and |delta / volume * 100| > 60) and delta > 0 OR cumulative delta crosses above its EMA OR positive-delta consecutive count >= its threshold OR buy/sell volume ratio > 1.3 OR aggression index > 15 OR momentum change > 0 OR weighted delta score > 10 OR overall bias score >= 2 OR close > EMA(short) and EMA(short) > EMA(medium) OR cumulative delta > its EMA OR daily cumulative delta >= 0 OR close position > 60% of bar range.
Short: bearish divergence OR high volume (volume >= volume average * high-volume multiplier) and delta < 0 OR absorption (volume >= volume average * high-volume multiplier and |delta / volume * 100| < absorption threshold) and close < open OR climax (volume >= volume average * climax multiplier and |delta / volume * 100| > 60) and delta < 0 OR cumulative delta crosses below its EMA OR negative-delta consecutive count >= its threshold OR buy/sell volume ratio < 0.77 OR aggression index < -15 OR momentum change < 0 OR weighted delta score < -10 OR overall bias score <= -2 OR close < EMA(short) and EMA(short) < EMA(medium) OR cumulative delta <= its EMA OR daily cumulative delta < 0 OR close position < 40% of bar range.
