Calculation: Zigzag = Confirm a pivot high or low after the same configurable number of bars on each side. Process highs before lows. Append an opposite-type pivot only if its distance from the latest pivot is at least a configurable multiple of current ATR; replace a same-type pivot only if it is at least as extreme. Retain a configurable maximum number of pivots. A finer zigzag uses a shorter pivot strength and a smaller ATR swing threshold.
Calculation: Wave count = On each new or replaced coarse pivot, examine the newest consecutive pivots in this priority: completed impulse plus A-B-C, plus A-B, plus A, completed impulse, completed diagonal, waves 1-4, diagonal waves 1-4, waves 1-2. Use the first valid candidate; otherwise the stage is zero. Direction d is +1 when the first pivot is a low and -1 when it is a high. Number the impulse pivots p0 through p5 and correction pivots pA, pB, pC. Require (p1-p0)*d > 0, (p2-p1)*d < 0, (p3-p2)*d > 0, (p3-p1)*d > 0, (p4-p3)*d < 0 and (p5-p4)*d > 0 wherever those pivots exist. Unless truncation is enabled, also require (p5-p3)*d > 0. For A-B-C require successive legs opposite, with and opposite d, and B still on the correction side of p5. At A-B stage apply the corresponding first two requirements; at A stage require A opposite d. Require wave 2 to remain strictly beyond p0 in direction d when its rule is enabled. Require wave 4 to remain strictly beyond p1 in direction d when its rule is enabled, except for diagonals. For a diagonal require wave 4 strictly inside wave 1 territory but strictly beyond p2 in direction d, wave 3 length no greater than wave 1 length times a configurable wedge tolerance, and, when present, wave 5 length no greater than wave 3 length times that tolerance. For wave-length rules use either absolute price differences or absolute log price ratios. When wave 5 exists, wave 3 must be at least as long as the shorter of waves 1 and 5, or at least as long as both in strict mode. Before wave 5 exists, strict mode requires wave 3 at least as long as wave 1; otherwise this rule passes. Preserve the count until the next zigzag update.
Calculation: Live invalidation = The count exists AND invalidation is enabled AND (the wave-2 rule is enabled AND close is strictly beyond p0 against d OR stage is at least 4 AND a nondiagnonal count has the wave-4 rule enabled AND close is strictly beyond p1 against d OR stage is at least 4 AND a diagonal count has close strictly beyond p2 against d).
Calculation: Retracement depth = Absolute corrective-leg price change divided by the absolute preceding impulse-leg price change. The wave-2 and wave-4 zones interpolate between the preceding impulse endpoint and start using their respective lower and upper configurable retracement fractions.
Calculation: Guideline score = Count the following comparisons when the count is rebuilt, treating unavailable values as false: wave 5 and wave 1 lengths differ by no more than the equality tolerance relative to wave 1; wave 2 and wave 4 have different correction types for an impulse or the same type for a diagonal; pC is within the larger of a configurable fraction of wave 4 length and current ATR of p4; the relative difference between waves 1 and 5, between waves 1 and 3, between waves 3 and 5, or between wave 5 and a configurable fraction of wave 1 is within the equality tolerance, with each difference normalized by the earlier wave in the pair; average volume of wave 5 is less than that of wave 3. A correction is sharp if its retracement depth is at least a configurable threshold OR its duration divided by its preceding impulse duration is at most another threshold; otherwise it is flat. Wave-5 volume runs from p4 through p5, or through the current bar if p5 is absent; average each leg over its inclusive bars, subject to a configurable maximum length.
Calculation: Zone gap = A directional three-bar fair value gap whose size is at least a configurable multiple of current ATR, whose price interval overlaps the relevant retracement zone, and whose first bar is at or after the preceding impulse endpoint. Retain a configurable number of recent gaps; do not remove filled gaps. A bullish gap has current low above the high two bars earlier; a bearish gap has current high below the low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Trigger = Within a configurable maximum number of bars after the corrective pivot and strictly after that pivot, use one selected mode: the current bar overlaps the relevant zone; OR, in direction d, close crosses the most recently confirmed minor pivot high for d = +1 or pivot low for d = -1, using that same current pivot level for both closes; OR close exceeds the previous high AND open for d = +1, or is below the previous low AND open for d = -1. Minor pivots use a shorter confirmation strength than coarse pivots.
Calculation: Trade levels = Entry is close, except for a zone-tap wave entry where it is the zone price at the configured first retracement fraction. Select a stop reference as the corrective pivot, the rule-invalidation pivot, or entry minus trade direction times an ATR distance. For a wave-3 trade the corrective and invalidation pivots are p2 and p0; for wave 5 they are p4 and p1; for wave C both are pB. The long stop is the lower of the reference and current low minus an ATR buffer; the short stop is the higher of the reference and current high plus that buffer. First target is p2 plus a configurable extension fraction of p1-p0 for wave 3, p4 plus p1-p0 for wave 5, and p4 for wave C. Risk is the absolute difference between entry and stop; reward/risk is the absolute difference between target and entry divided by risk. Freeze entry, stop and target when the trade fires.
Calculation: Wave-entry setup = No trade is live AND no live invalidation exists AND the guideline score meets its configurable minimum AND either gaps are optional OR a same-direction zone gap exists AND the trigger passes AND risk > 0 AND reward/risk meets its configurable minimum. For wave 3 additionally require stage 2, wave-2 depth between a configurable fraction of the smaller zone retracement and a configurable maximum, wave 2 strictly beyond p0 in direction d, and no earlier wave-3 trigger attempt at the same p2 bar. For wave 5 additionally require stage 4, wave-4 depth between configurable multiples of the smaller and larger zone retracements, all three wave rules passing, and no earlier wave-5 trigger attempt at the same p4 bar. Record an attempt even when reward/risk rejects it.
Calculation: Wave-C setup = Stage 7 AND no trade is live AND no live invalidation exists AND pB and p4 exist AND the current bar is strictly after pB and within a configurable maximum age AND risk > 0 AND reward/risk meets its configurable minimum.
Calculation: Break-even stop = After entry, if enabled and a bar reaches entry plus trade direction times a configurable multiple of initial risk, use entry as the stop from that bar onward; otherwise use the frozen initial stop. Do not evaluate target or stop on the entry bar; on later bars evaluate the target before the stop, including the bar that activates break-even.
Long: Wave-entry setup AND d = +1.
Short: Wave-entry setup AND d = -1.
Long: Wave-C setup AND d = -1 AND close > previous high AND close > open.
Short: Wave-C setup AND d = +1 AND close < previous low AND close < open.
Long: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces stage 2 AND d = +1 AND wave-2 depth lies within its zone retracement fractions AND the current bar is the corrective pivot's confirmation bar.
Short: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces stage 2 AND d = -1 AND wave-2 depth lies within its zone retracement fractions AND the current bar is the corrective pivot's confirmation bar.
Long: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces stage 4 AND d = +1 AND wave-4 depth lies within its zone retracement fractions AND the current bar is the corrective pivot's confirmation bar.
Short: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces stage 4 AND d = -1 AND wave-4 depth lies within its zone retracement fractions AND the current bar is the corrective pivot's confirmation bar.
Long: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces a diagonal count with stage at least 4 AND d = -1.
Short: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces a diagonal count with stage at least 4 AND d = +1.
Long: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces a count with p5 at or short of p3 in direction d AND d = -1.
Short: A newly confirmed or replaced pivot produces a count with p5 at or short of p3 in direction d AND d = +1.
Long: Stage 4 AND d = -1 AND close is strictly beyond, in direction d, the line through p3 parallel to the line connecting p2 and p4.
Short: Stage 4 AND d = +1 AND close is strictly beyond, in direction d, the line through p3 parallel to the line connecting p2 and p4.
Exit: long, live invalidation exists OR high >= frozen first target OR low <= break-even stop.
Exit: short, live invalidation exists OR low <= frozen first target OR high >= break-even stop.
