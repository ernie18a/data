Calculation: Stochastic RSI = SMA of stochastic applied to RSI; its signal line is an SMA of that result.
Calculation: Bull Bear Power = high + low − 2 × EMA of close.
Calculation: Ichimoku cloud bounds = maximum and minimum of the two leading spans calculated a base-period number of bars ago; the first span is the average of the conversion and base lines, and the second is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback than the base line. Each conversion and base line is the midpoint of its lookback's highest high and lowest low; the conversion lookback is shorter than the base lookback.
Calculation: Accepted event = a raw event for which bars since the previous accepted occurrence of that same raw event are greater than the cooldown; initialize each event's previous occurrence sufficiently far in the past.
Long: An accepted event occurs AND (RSI moves from at or above its lower threshold to below it OR stochastic %K crosses above %D while %K is below its lower threshold OR CCI crosses above its lower threshold OR +DI crosses above −DI OR Awesome Oscillator crosses above zero OR momentum crosses above zero OR MACD crosses above its signal line OR Stochastic RSI crosses above its signal line while Stochastic RSI is below its lower threshold OR Williams %R crosses above its lower threshold OR Bull Bear Power crosses above zero OR Ultimate Oscillator crosses above its lower threshold OR close crosses above its EMA OR close crosses above either a shorter-lookback or a longer-lookback SMA OR the shorter-lookback SMA crosses above the longer-lookback SMA OR close crosses above the upper Ichimoku cloud bound OR Hull moving average rises after being flat or falling on the previous bar OR close crosses above its VWMA OR close moves from at or above the lower Bollinger Band to below it OR close becomes less than or equal to the lowest close over a lookback including the current bar after the preceding bar did not satisfy that condition OR open / previous close − 1 is at least the gap threshold OR a selectable backtest data field moves from at or below zero to above zero).
Short: An accepted event occurs AND (RSI moves from at or below its upper threshold to above it OR stochastic %K crosses below %D while %K is above its upper threshold OR CCI crosses below its upper threshold OR −DI crosses above +DI OR Awesome Oscillator crosses below zero OR momentum crosses below zero OR MACD crosses below its signal line OR Stochastic RSI crosses below its signal line while Stochastic RSI is above its upper threshold OR Williams %R crosses below its upper threshold OR Bull Bear Power crosses below zero OR Ultimate Oscillator crosses below its upper threshold OR close crosses below its EMA OR close crosses below either a shorter-lookback or a longer-lookback SMA OR the shorter-lookback SMA crosses below the longer-lookback SMA OR close crosses below the lower Ichimoku cloud bound OR Hull moving average falls after being flat or rising on the previous bar OR close crosses below its VWMA OR close moves from at or below the upper Bollinger Band to above it OR close becomes greater than or equal to the highest close over a lookback including the current bar after the preceding bar did not satisfy that condition OR 1 − open / previous close is at least the gap threshold).
