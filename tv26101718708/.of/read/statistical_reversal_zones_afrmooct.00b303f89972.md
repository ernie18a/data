Calculation: Zone boundaries = for each independently parameterized resistance zone, lower = current forming higher-timeframe bar's open × (1 + center offset − half-width) and upper = open × (1 + center offset + half-width); for each independently parameterized support zone, lower = open × (1 − center offset − half-width) and upper = open × (1 − center offset + half-width). Offsets increase from the nearest to the farthest zone; the half-width is shared.
Calculation: Zone overlap = chart bar high >= zone lower AND chart bar low <= zone upper, using each bar's own zone boundaries.
Calculation: Zone state = independently for each zone, reset entered and rejection count at the start of each higher-timeframe bar; set entered on a fresh overlap; on a confirmed chart bar, clear entered and increment the rejection count when a resistance close is below its lower boundary or a support close is above its upper boundary. A fresh overlap can set entered and be rejected on the same bar.
Long: For any support zone, current bar overlaps the zone AND previous bar does not overlap that zone AND that zone's rejection count before processing the current bar = 0.
Long: For any support zone, entered = true after processing the current bar's fresh overlap AND confirmed chart bar close > zone upper boundary.
Short: For any resistance zone, current bar overlaps the zone AND previous bar does not overlap that zone AND that zone's rejection count before processing the current bar = 0.
Short: For any resistance zone, entered = true after processing the current bar's fresh overlap AND confirmed chart bar close < zone lower boundary.
