Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by mean past volume, or zero if the mean is zero. In cumulative mode, current volume is the volume accumulated through the current chart bar in the higher timeframe bar still forming, and past volume is each preceding higher timeframe bar's accumulated volume through the same elapsed-time offset. In point-to-point mode, current volume is the current chart bar's volume and past volume is the volume of chart bars at the same elapsed-time offset in preceding completed higher timeframe bars; omit missing matching bars.
Calculation: Directional relative volume = relative volume when close > open OR close = open AND close > previous close; otherwise, negative relative volume.
Calculation: Relative volume average = Hull moving average of relative volume.
Calculation: Directional relative volume average = Hull moving average of directional relative volume, using the same period as the relative volume average.
Calculation: Upper and lower thresholds = either the same fixed threshold; or the simple moving average of relative volume plus and minus a factor times its standard deviation, with the lower threshold floored at zero; or the highest and lowest relative volume over a lookback starting at the current higher timeframe bar, the beginning of the past-volume lookback, or a fixed number of chart bars. Use the previous chart bar's thresholds in decisions.
Long: Directional relative volume average > 0.
Short: Directional relative volume average <= 0.
Long: At least one of the following conditions is selected AND every selected condition holds: close > open OR close = open AND close > previous close; relative volume > 1; relative volume > relative volume average; relative volume > previous upper threshold; directional relative volume average > 0; directional relative volume average > previous upper threshold; relative volume average > 1; relative volume average > previous upper threshold. AND, if the high-volatility filter is selected, shorter-period ATR > longer-period ATR; if the low-volatility filter is selected, shorter-period ATR <= longer-period ATR.
Short: At least one of the following conditions is selected AND every selected condition holds: close < open OR close = open AND close < previous close; relative volume < 1; relative volume < relative volume average; relative volume < previous lower threshold; directional relative volume average < 0; directional relative volume average < negative previous upper threshold; relative volume average < 1; relative volume average < previous lower threshold. AND, if the high-volatility filter is selected, shorter-period ATR > longer-period ATR; if the low-volatility filter is selected, shorter-period ATR <= longer-period ATR.
