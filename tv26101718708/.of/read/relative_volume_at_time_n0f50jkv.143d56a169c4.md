Calculation: Relative volume = (volume accumulated from the start of the current higher timeframe unit through the current chart bar) / (the average accumulated volume through the same elapsed offset in prior higher timeframe units); alternatively, use current-bar volume divided by the average volume of bars at the same offset in prior higher timeframe units. Use the selected mode.
Calculation: Relative-volume average = Hull moving average of relative volume.
Calculation: Directional relative-volume average = Hull moving average of relative volume made positive on up bars and negative on down bars; an up bar has close > open or close = open and close > prior close, and a down bar has close < open or close = open and close < prior close.
Calculation: Relative-volume threshold high/low = selected fixed threshold; or relative-volume simple moving average plus/minus its standard deviation times a factor (low is floored at zero); or the highest/lowest relative volume over the selected lookback. For the high/low modes, the lookback is respectively the current higher-timeframe unit, the bars from the start of the past-volume lookback, or a fixed lookback.
Calculation: Bullish state = every enabled coloring condition is bullish: up bar; relative volume > 1; relative volume > relative-volume average; relative volume > prior-bar threshold high; directional relative-volume average > 0; directional relative-volume average > prior-bar threshold high; relative-volume average > 1; or relative-volume average > prior-bar threshold high. Bearish state = every enabled coloring condition is bearish: down bar; relative volume < 1; relative volume < relative-volume average; relative volume < prior-bar threshold low; directional relative-volume average < 0; directional relative-volume average < negative prior-bar threshold high; relative-volume average < 1; or relative-volume average < prior-bar threshold low. At least one condition must be enabled. Apply the selected volatility filter: all bars, fast ATR > slow ATR, or fast ATR <= slow ATR.
Long: bullish state and prior bar was not bullish state
Short: bearish state and prior bar was not bearish state
