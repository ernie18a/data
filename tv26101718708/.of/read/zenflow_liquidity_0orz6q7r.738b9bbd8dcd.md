Calculation: Short EMA and long EMA = EMAs of close, with the short EMA using the shorter lookback.
Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of close.
Calculation: Bull trend = close > long EMA AND short EMA > long EMA; bear trend = close < long EMA AND short EMA < long EMA.
Calculation: Near EMA = absolute difference between close and either EMA <= ATR multiplied by an EMA proximity parameter; near VWAP = absolute difference between close and VWAP <= ATR multiplied by a VWAP proximity parameter.
Calculation: Session pass = session filter disabled OR bar time within the selected permitted session.
Calculation: High volume = volume > its SMA multiplied by a volume threshold parameter.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment); body = absolute difference between close and open; lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close).
Calculation: Delta proxy = volume * (2 * close location - 1); delta strength = delta proxy / EMA(abs(delta proxy)), or zero when that EMA is zero; cumulative delta = cumulative sum of delta proxy.
Calculation: Bull impulse = delta strength >= positive threshold AND cumulative delta >= its EMA AND close location >= upper location threshold; bear impulse = delta strength <= negative threshold AND cumulative delta <= its EMA AND close location <= lower location threshold.
Calculation: Bull absorption = high volume AND lower wick > body AND close location >= absorption threshold; bear absorption = high volume AND upper wick > body AND close location <= complementary absorption threshold.
Calculation: Bull trap = low < lowest low of the preceding trap lookback AND close > that low AND delta strength < 0 AND close location >= absorption threshold; bear trap = high > highest high of the preceding trap lookback AND close < that high AND delta strength > 0 AND close location <= complementary absorption threshold.
Calculation: Bull regime reset = bull trend AND close > VWAP AND (bull impulse OR close > highest high of the preceding trigger lookback OR close > highest high of the preceding continuation lookback); bear regime reset = bear trend AND close < VWAP AND (bear impulse OR close < lowest low of the preceding trigger lookback OR close < lowest low of the preceding continuation lookback).
Calculation: Long invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP-flip invalidation enabled AND close < VWAP AND short EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA) OR (order-flow invalidation enabled AND (bear impulse OR bear absorption OR bear trap))) AND bull regime reset = false; short invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP-flip invalidation enabled AND close > VWAP AND short EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA) OR (order-flow invalidation enabled AND (bull impulse OR bull absorption OR bull trap))) AND bear regime reset = false.
Calculation: Long watch = session pass AND ((bull trend AND (near EMA OR near VWAP)) OR (close < VWAP AND short EMA crosses above long EMA)); short watch = session pass AND ((bear trend AND (near EMA OR near VWAP)) OR (close > VWAP AND short EMA crosses below long EMA)).
Calculation: Long trigger = long watch AND ((lower wick >= body multiplied by a rejection parameter AND close location >= upper location threshold AND close > open) OR close > highest high of the preceding trigger lookback OR bull trap OR (body >= EMA(body) multiplied by an impulse-body parameter AND bull impulse AND close > previous high)); short trigger = short watch AND ((upper wick >= body multiplied by a rejection parameter AND close location <= lower location threshold AND close < open) OR close < lowest low of the preceding trigger lookback OR bear trap OR (body >= EMA(body) multiplied by an impulse-body parameter AND bear impulse AND close < previous low)).
Calculation: Long continuation = continuation enabled AND bull trend AND (VWAP filter disabled OR (close > VWAP AND previous close <= previous VWAP)) AND (volume filter disabled OR high volume) AND close > highest high of the preceding continuation lookback AND (order-flow filter disabled OR order-flow scoring disabled OR bull impulse OR bull absorption OR bull trap) AND session pass AND (trigger requirement disabled OR long trigger) AND long invalidation = false; short continuation = continuation enabled AND bear trend AND (VWAP filter disabled OR (close < VWAP AND previous close >= previous VWAP)) AND (volume filter disabled OR high volume) AND close < lowest low of the preceding continuation lookback AND (order-flow filter disabled OR order-flow scoring disabled OR bear impulse OR bear absorption OR bear trap) AND session pass AND (trigger requirement disabled OR short trigger) AND short invalidation = false.
Calculation: Long stop = minimum of the entry bar's low and entry price minus entry-bar ATR multiplied by a stop parameter; short stop = maximum of the entry bar's high and entry price plus entry-bar ATR multiplied by a stop parameter. Hold each stop fixed from entry.
Long: Long watch = true OR long continuation = true OR bull absorption = true OR bull trap = true OR bull impulse = true OR (momentum enabled AND bull trend AND close > VWAP AND session pass AND (bull impulse OR close > highest high of the preceding continuation lookback OR close > highest high of the preceding trigger lookback) AND long invalidation = false) OR (liquidity overlay enabled AND ((low < last completed higher-timeframe low AND close > that low) OR (low < lowest low of the preceding liquidity lookback AND close > that low))).
Short: Short watch = true OR short continuation = true OR bear absorption = true OR bear trap = true OR bear impulse = true OR (momentum enabled AND bear trend AND close < VWAP AND session pass AND (bear impulse OR close < lowest low of the preceding continuation lookback OR close < lowest low of the preceding trigger lookback) AND short invalidation = false) OR (liquidity overlay enabled AND ((high > last completed higher-timeframe high AND close < that high) OR (high > highest high of the preceding liquidity lookback AND close < that high))).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR long invalidation = true.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR short invalidation = true.
