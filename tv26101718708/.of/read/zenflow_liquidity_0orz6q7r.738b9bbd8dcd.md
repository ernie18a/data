Calculation: Bull trend = close > longer-lookback EMA AND shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA; bear trend is the inverse.
Calculation: High volume = volume > moving average of volume × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Volume pass = high volume when the volume filter is enabled; otherwise true.
Calculation: VWAP reclaim = close > VWAP AND previous close <= previous VWAP; VWAP reject = close < VWAP AND previous close >= previous VWAP.
Calculation: Swing break up/down = close above the previous completed bars’ highest high / below their lowest low over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Near EMA = absolute distance from close to either EMA <= ATR × proximity multiplier; near VWAP is defined the same way using VWAP.
Calculation: Bull rejection = lower wick >= candle body × wick-ratio threshold AND close is in the upper 40% of the bar AND close > open; bear rejection is the inverse using the upper wick and lower 40%.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick); delta proxy = volume × (2 × close location − 1); delta strength = delta proxy / EMA of absolute delta proxy; cumulative delta = cumulative sum of delta proxy.
Calculation: Bull order-flow impulse = delta strength >= threshold AND cumulative delta >= its EMA AND close location >= 0.60; bear impulse uses the inverse inequalities and close location <= 0.40.
Calculation: Bull absorption = high volume AND lower wick > candle body AND close location >= 0.55; bear absorption = high volume AND upper wick > candle body AND close location <= 0.45.
Calculation: Failed low break = low below the previous bars’ lowest low AND close above that low; failed high break is the inverse. Long trap = failed low break AND delta strength < 0 AND close location >= 0.55; short trap = failed high break AND delta strength > 0 AND close location <= 0.45.
Calculation: Bull impulse tag = candle body >= its EMA × impulse multiplier AND bull order-flow impulse AND close > previous high; bear impulse tag is the inverse using previous low.
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = low below previous-day low or previous bars’ lowest low AND close back above that level; buy-side sweep = high above previous-day high or previous bars’ highest high AND close back below that level. Previous-day levels use the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Session pass = true unless session filtering is enabled, in which case the bar must be in the selected allowed session.
Calculation: Long watch = bull trend AND session pass AND (near EMA OR (session pass AND near VWAP)); short watch is the inverse.
Calculation: Pullback long watch = bull trend AND close > VWAP AND near EMA AND session pass; short watch is the inverse.
Calculation: Reversal long watch = close < VWAP AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND session pass; short watch is the inverse.
Calculation: Long trigger = session pass AND (long watch OR pullback long watch OR reversal long watch) AND (bull rejection OR upward swing break OR long trap OR bull impulse tag); short trigger is the inverse.
Calculation: Long invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP-flip invalidation enabled AND close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA) OR (opposite-flow invalidation enabled AND (bear order-flow impulse OR bear absorption OR short trap))) AND NOT (bull trend AND close > VWAP AND (bull order-flow impulse OR upward trigger break OR configured upward swing break)); short invalidation is the inverse.
Calculation: Bull/bear score = 25 for matching trend + 20 for close on matching VWAP side + 15 for high volume + 15 for matching configured swing break + 10 for near EMA + 10 for session pass + matching order-flow score + matching sweep score. Order-flow score is 15 for matching impulse, else 12 for matching absorption, else −8 for matching exhaustion, plus configured trap and impulse-tag boosts; sweep score is the configured boost for a recent matching-side sweep. Pullback scores use 25 trend + 20 matching VWAP side + 20 near EMA + 20 matching pullback break + 10 volume pass + the same order-flow and sweep scores. Reversal scores use 20 opposite VWAP side + 25 matching VWAP cross + 20 matching EMA cross + 20 matching reversal break + 10 volume pass + the same order-flow and sweep scores.
Calculation: Entry confidence = clamp to 0–100 the maximum of the six bull, bear, pullback, and reversal scores minus penalties: 15 for directional trend conflict, 10 for directional VWAP-side conflict, 15 for opposing order-flow conflict, 5 when neither trap nor impulse tag is present, and 8 when trigger confirmation is required but absent.
Long: (continuation enabled AND bull trend AND (VWAP filter disabled OR VWAP reclaim) AND volume pass AND configured upward swing break AND order-flow gate passes AND session pass AND (trigger not required OR long trigger) AND NOT long invalidation) OR (pullback enabled AND (bull trend AND close > VWAP AND near EMA AND upward pullback break AND (volume expansion not required OR high volume) AND order-flow gate passes AND session pass AND (trigger not required OR long trigger) AND NOT long invalidation OR lower-timeframe execution enabled AND pullback long watch AND bull trend AND close > VWAP AND near EMA AND upward trigger break AND NOT long invalidation AND entry confidence >= configured minimum)) OR (reversal enabled AND close < VWAP AND VWAP reclaim AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND upward reversal break AND (volume expansion not required OR high volume) AND order-flow gate passes AND session pass AND (trigger not required OR long trigger) AND NOT long invalidation) OR (momentum state enabled AND bull trend AND close > VWAP AND session pass AND (bull order-flow impulse OR configured upward swing break OR upward trigger break) AND NOT long invalidation AND entry confidence >= configured minimum) OR long trigger OR long watch OR pullback long watch OR reversal long watch OR momentum long watch OR impulse watch long (bull order-flow impulse AND close > VWAP AND NOT bear trend) OR sell-side sweep.
Short: Invert the directional long conditions: use bear trend, close < VWAP, VWAP reject, downward breaks, bear order-flow gate, short trigger, short-side watches, short momentum conditions, and NOT short invalidation; include buy-side sweep.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR long invalidation. At entry the stop is min(entry-bar low, entry price − ATR × stop multiplier); targets are entry price + initial risk × configured reward multiples and are tracked, but do not themselves close the position.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR short invalidation. At entry the stop is max(entry-bar high, entry price + ATR × stop multiplier); targets are entry price − initial risk × configured reward multiples and are tracked, but do not themselves close the position.
