Calculation: EMA = recursively smoothed close, seeded with the first close, using alpha = 2 / (length + 1); use short, longer, and longest lengths for the fast, slow, and macro EMAs.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = the high/low strictly greater/less than every other high/low in a symmetric lookback window; evaluate the window ending at the previous bar. Retain the two most recently confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Structure break direction = initially neutral; an upward break occurs when the previous close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high and the previous break direction is neutral or down; a downward break occurs when the previous close is below the latest confirmed pivot low and the previous break direction is neutral or up. Update the direction on a break, processing the upward break before the downward break if both occur.
Calculation: Directional trend score = sum of 40 for fast EMA above/below slow EMA, 30 for close above/below macro EMA, 20 for fast EMA above/below its value at the slope lookback, and 10 for slow EMA above/below its value at the same lookback; use above for bullish and below for bearish.
Calculation: Directional momentum score = sum of 35 for RSI above/below its midline, 35 for MACD line above/below its signal line, and 30 for MACD histogram above/below its previous value; use above for bullish and below for bearish. MACD uses a shorter fast length and a longer slow length.
Calculation: Directional volume score = sum of 60 when volume ratio is at least the spike threshold and the candle closes above/below its open, and 40 when volume exceeds previous volume and the candle closes above/below its open; use above for bullish and below for bearish. Volume ratio is volume divided by its simple moving average when that average is positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Directional structure score = sum of 60 when both latest confirmed pivot highs and lows are higher/lower than their respective preceding pivots, and 40 on an upward/downward structure break; use higher and upward for bullish, lower and downward for bearish.
Calculation: Bull/bear score = weighted sum of the corresponding directional trend, momentum, volume, and structure scores divided by the sum of their configurable weights; use 1 as the divisor if the weight sum is zero.
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR divided by close times 100 when close is positive, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Take-profit level = entry price plus/minus ATR at the most recent long/short signal multiplied by the stop-distance multiplier and a take-profit risk-reward multiplier; use three independently specified risk-reward multipliers.
Long: Bull score >= medium threshold AND bull score - bear score >= minimum score gap AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its slow-length EMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (no previous long signal OR bars since previous long signal >= minimum signal gap).
Short: Bear score >= medium threshold AND bear score - bull score >= minimum score gap AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its slow-length EMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (no previous short signal OR bars since previous short signal >= minimum signal gap).
Long: Bull score > bear score AND bull score >= medium threshold.
Short: Bear score > bull score AND bear score >= medium threshold.
Long: Bull score > 0.
Short: Bear score > 0.
Exit: long, bear score >= medium threshold AND bear score - bull score >= minimum score gap AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its slow-length EMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (no previous short signal OR bars since previous short signal >= minimum signal gap).
Exit: short, bull score >= medium threshold AND bull score - bear score >= minimum score gap AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its slow-length EMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percentage) AND (no previous long signal OR bars since previous long signal >= minimum signal gap).
Exit: long, most recent signal direction = long AND high >= any long take-profit level.
Exit: short, most recent signal direction = short AND low <= any short take-profit level.
