Calculation: Chandelier Exit = highest high (or close, if using closes for extrema) over the lookback minus ATR times the multiplier for the long line, and lowest low (or close) plus ATR times the multiplier for the short line; each line only moves in the favorable direction. Bullish reversal = close > previous short line; bearish reversal = close < previous long line.
Calculation: ZLSMA = linear regression of close over the lookback plus the difference between that regression and a linear regression of the first regression over the same lookback.
Calculation: Heikin Ashi open = previous Heikin Ashi open and close averaged; on the first bar, average of real open and close. Heikin Ashi close = average of real open, high, low, and close; high = maximum of real high, Heikin Ashi open, and Heikin Ashi close; low = minimum of real low, Heikin Ashi open, and Heikin Ashi close.
Calculation: Swing stop = lowest low over the lookback for a long, or highest high over the lookback for a short, including the signal bar; place it at least the configured minimum stop distance from entry. The Chandelier-stop strategy uses the corresponding Chandelier line, also at least the configured minimum stop distance from entry.
Calculation: Risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop. Target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the configured risk multiple times risk.
Long: bullish Chandelier reversal OR (bullish Chandelier reversal AND close > ZLSMA) OR (after a bullish Chandelier reversal, while Chandelier direction remains bullish and before the wait expires, close > ZLSMA) OR (after a bullish Chandelier reversal, while Chandelier direction remains bullish and before the wait expires, close > ZLSMA AND previous close <= previous ZLSMA AND close > open).
Short: bearish Chandelier reversal OR (bearish Chandelier reversal AND close < ZLSMA) OR (after a bearish Chandelier reversal, while Chandelier direction remains bearish and before the wait expires, close < ZLSMA) OR (after a bearish Chandelier reversal, while Chandelier direction remains bearish and before the wait expires, close < ZLSMA AND previous close >= previous ZLSMA AND close < open).
Exit: long, swing-stop position AND low <= swing stop OR Chandelier-stop position AND low <= Chandelier long line OR close < ZLSMA OR high >= target OR bearish Chandelier reversal OR (close < ZLSMA AND close > entry price).
Exit: short, swing-stop position AND high >= swing stop OR Chandelier-stop position AND high >= Chandelier short line OR close > ZLSMA OR low <= target OR bullish Chandelier reversal OR (close > ZLSMA AND close < entry price).
