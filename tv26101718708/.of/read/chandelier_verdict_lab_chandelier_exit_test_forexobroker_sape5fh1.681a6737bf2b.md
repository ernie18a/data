Calculation: Signal candles = either standard OHLC or Heikin Ashi OHLC. Heikin Ashi close is (open + high + low + close) / 4; its open is the average of the previous Heikin Ashi open and close, initialized to (open + close) / 2; its high is the maximum of standard high, Heikin Ashi open and close; its low is the corresponding minimum.
Calculation: Measurement candles = standard OHLC or, when signal candles are Heikin Ashi, optionally Heikin Ashi OHLC. All calculations use completed chart bars.
Calculation: Chandelier lines and direction = on signal candles, calculate true range and its Wilder moving average over the Chandelier lookback. The raw long line is the highest signal close or high over that lookback minus an ATR multiple; the raw short line is the lowest signal close or low plus that multiple. If the previous signal close is above the previous long line, the current long line is the greater of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is raw. If the previous signal close is below the previous short line, the current short line is the lesser of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is raw. Initialize direction long; set it long if signal close exceeds the previous short line, otherwise set it short if signal close is below the previous long line, otherwise retain it. Initialize missing previous lines to their current raw values.
Calculation: Zero-lag least-squares moving average = twice the linear regression endpoint of signal close minus the linear regression endpoint of that first regression series, both over the same lookback.
Calculation: Entry stop and target = at entry, choose the current side's Chandelier line, the zero-lag average, or the measurement-candle swing low for a long or swing high for a short, including the entry bar. Set the minimum distance to the greater of a multiple of standard-candle ATR and a fixed price increment allowance. The long stop is the lesser of the chosen level and entry price minus minimum distance; the short stop is the greater of the chosen level and entry price plus minimum distance. Risk is the absolute distance from entry to stop; target is entry price plus position sign times a risk multiple. Fix stop and target at entry.
Long: Chandelier direction changes from short to long.
Short: Chandelier direction changes from long to short.
Long: No open position after processing exits AND the selected entry rule is satisfied: Chandelier direction changes from short to long; OR that change occurs AND signal close is above the zero-lag average; OR that change occurs AND the zero-lag average is above its previous value; OR, after that change and before the wait limit is reached, direction remains long AND signal close is above the zero-lag average. The wait rule includes the signal bar.
Short: Short entries are enabled AND no open position after processing exits AND the selected entry rule is satisfied: Chandelier direction changes from long to short; OR that change occurs AND signal close is below the zero-lag average; OR that change occurs AND the zero-lag average is below its previous value; OR, after that change and before the wait limit is reached, direction remains short AND signal close is below the zero-lag average. The wait rule includes the signal bar.
Long: No open position after processing exits AND the crossing entry rule is selected AND signal close crosses above the zero-lag average AND signal close is above signal open AND either Chandelier direction changes from short to long on this bar OR that change occurred within the wait limit, signal close was below the zero-lag average on the signal bar, and direction has remained long. A cross requires the previous close to be at or below the previous average.
Short: Short entries are enabled AND no open position after processing exits AND the crossing entry rule is selected AND signal close crosses below the zero-lag average AND signal close is below signal open AND either Chandelier direction changes from long to short on this bar OR that change occurred within the wait limit, signal close was above the zero-lag average on the signal bar, and direction has remained short. A cross requires the previous close to be at or above the previous average.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND measurement low is at or below the fixed stop.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND measurement high is at or above the fixed stop.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND stop is not hit AND the selected exit rule is satisfied: target or target-or-opposite mode AND measurement high is at or above target; OR opposite or target-or-opposite mode AND Chandelier direction changes from long to short; OR average-close mode AND signal close is below the zero-lag average; OR profit-or-opposite mode AND ((signal close is below the zero-lag average AND measurement close is above entry price) OR Chandelier direction changes from long to short).
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND stop is not hit AND the selected exit rule is satisfied: target or target-or-opposite mode AND measurement low is at or below target; OR opposite or target-or-opposite mode AND Chandelier direction changes from short to long; OR average-close mode AND signal close is above the zero-lag average; OR profit-or-opposite mode AND ((signal close is above the zero-lag average AND measurement close is below entry price) OR Chandelier direction changes from short to long).
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND stop is not hit AND half-target mode is selected AND measurement high has reached target on this or an earlier post-entry bar AND signal close is below the zero-lag average; half the position is credited at target and the remainder exits at measurement close.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND stop is not hit AND half-target mode is selected AND measurement low has reached target on this or an earlier post-entry bar AND signal close is above the zero-lag average; half the position is credited at target and the remainder exits at measurement close.
