Calculation: HTF = a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; every HTF value is taken from completed HTF bars only. C = the last completed HTF bar, with C-1 and C-2 the two HTF bars before it.
Calculation: Pivot level = the high (or low) of an HTF bar that is the highest high (or lowest low) over a left window of earlier HTF bars and a right window of later HTF bars. It is confirmed once the full right window has completed.
Calculation: Pending level = a confirmed pivot level that has not yet matched a gap and has not been discarded. Discard it when the range of C-1 contains the level (low of C-1 <= level <= high of C-1) and the level did not match on this same evaluation, or when it is older than a maximum age in HTF bars.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low of C > high of C-2, with gap range (high of C-2, low of C). Bearish gap = high of C < low of C-2, with gap range (high of C, low of C-2).
Long: evaluated once per new completed HTF bar, for each pending pivot-high level. A bullish gap exists AND high of C-2 < level < low of C. The level then becomes an active support level, and the long decision applies from the next HTF bar onward.
Short: evaluated once per new completed HTF bar, for each pending pivot-low level. A bearish gap exists AND high of C < level < low of C-2. The level then becomes an active resistance level, and the short decision applies from the next HTF bar onward.
