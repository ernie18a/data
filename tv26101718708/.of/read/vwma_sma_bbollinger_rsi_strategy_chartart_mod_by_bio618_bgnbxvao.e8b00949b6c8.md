Calculation: Bollinger Bands = SMA of close ± standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of close, using the same lookback for both.
Calculation: Oversold RSI threshold = 0 + configurable RSI range; overbought RSI threshold = 100 − configurable RSI range.
Long: RSI crosses above its oversold threshold AND close crosses above the lower Bollinger Band
Short: RSI crosses below its overbought threshold AND close crosses below the upper Bollinger Band
