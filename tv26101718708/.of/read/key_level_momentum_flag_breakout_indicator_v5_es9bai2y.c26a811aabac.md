Calculation: Premarket high and low = highest high and lowest low accumulated during the configured premarket session, reset at the start of each premarket session and retained afterward until the next reset.
Calculation: Previous-day high and low = high and low of the last completed daily bar, aggregated from higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative sum of close multiplied by volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each trading-day boundary.
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal EMA; the MACD line is the shorter-period close EMA minus the longer-period close EMA.
Long: Position side is not long AND the bar is in the configured signal session if the session filter is enabled AND close > VWAP AND short EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA AND, within the configured setup window, close crossed above the current premarket high or previous-day high, with the preceding close <= that same current level AND lowest low over the flag lookback including the current bar > long EMA AND close > highest high over the preceding flag-lookback bars AND close > close at the impulse lookback + ATR multiplied by the impulse threshold AND close > open AND, if enabled, MACD histogram > 0 AND > its preceding value AND, if enabled, volume > volume SMA multiplied by the breakout volume threshold.
Short: Position side is not short AND the bar is in the configured signal session if the session filter is enabled AND close < VWAP AND short EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA AND, within the configured setup window, close crossed below the current premarket low or previous-day low, with the preceding close >= that same current level AND highest high over the flag lookback including the current bar < long EMA AND close < lowest low over the preceding flag-lookback bars AND close < close at the impulse lookback - ATR multiplied by the impulse threshold AND close < open AND, if enabled, MACD histogram < 0 AND < its preceding value AND, if enabled, volume > volume SMA multiplied by the breakout volume threshold.
Long: Position side is not long AND the bar is in the configured signal session if the session filter is enabled AND close > VWAP AND short EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA AND, if the pullback filter is enabled, the minimum over the pullback lookback including the current bar of the absolute difference between each bar's low and its short EMA, long EMA, or VWAP <= current ATR multiplied by the rejection distance AND, if the rejection filter is enabled, close > open AND low <= short EMA AND close > preceding close AND close > highest high over the preceding microstructure-lookback bars AND close > open AND, if enabled, MACD histogram > 0 AND >= its preceding value AND, if enabled, volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the continuation volume threshold.
Short: Position side is not short AND the bar is in the configured signal session if the session filter is enabled AND close < VWAP AND short EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA AND, if the pullback filter is enabled, the minimum over the pullback lookback including the current bar of the absolute difference between each bar's high and its short EMA, long EMA, or VWAP <= current ATR multiplied by the rejection distance AND, if the rejection filter is enabled, close < open AND high >= short EMA AND close < preceding close AND close < lowest low over the preceding microstructure-lookback bars AND close < open AND, if enabled, MACD histogram < 0 AND <= its preceding value AND, if enabled, volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the continuation volume threshold.
Long: Position side is not long AND the bar is in the configured signal session if the session filter is enabled AND close > VWAP AND short EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA AND close > highest high over the preceding ignition-lookback bars AND close > open AND absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by the body threshold AND, if enabled, volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the continuation volume threshold AND, if enabled, MACD histogram > its preceding value.
Short: Position side is not short AND the bar is in the configured signal session if the session filter is enabled AND close < VWAP AND short EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA AND close < lowest low over the preceding ignition-lookback bars AND close < open AND absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by the body threshold AND, if enabled, volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the continuation volume threshold AND, if enabled, MACD histogram < its preceding value.
Exit: long, close < short EMA OR close < VWAP OR (MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram < its preceding value) OR any Short entry condition holds with its position-side restriction removed; each exit alternative is independently configurable.
Exit: short, close > short EMA OR close > VWAP OR (MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram > its preceding value) OR any Long entry condition holds with its position-side restriction removed; each exit alternative is independently configurable.
Long: close > VWAP AND short EMA > long EMA AND MACD histogram > 0.
Short: close < VWAP AND short EMA < long EMA AND MACD histogram < 0.
Long: short EMA > long EMA.
Short: short EMA <= long EMA.
Long: MACD histogram > 0.
Short: MACD histogram <= 0.
