Calculation: Pending high and low levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows, respectively, using configurable left and right lookbacks. Retain each level until its pivot age exceeds a lifetime or it is broken. Optionally reject a new level if its absolute distance from any pending level of the same type is less than the new level times a distance fraction.
Calculation: Active high level = a pending high level after an upward breakout. Remove it on the first bar after the breakout and within a confirmation window where close < level OR low <= level, or after its first eligible retest. Active low level = a pending low level after a downward breakout. Remove it on the first bar after the breakout and within the confirmation window where close > level OR high >= level, or after its first eligible retest. Check invalidation before retest.
Calculation: Tracked maximum close and MFI = on each bar where MFI is the highest in its configurable chart-timeframe lookback, reset both to current close and MFI; otherwise retain their previous values, initializing missing values to current close and MFI. Then separately raise each to the current close or MFI if greater. Tracked minimum close and MFI are calculated symmetrically using the lowest MFI and lowering each value if the current value is less.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic = simple moving average of the stochastic oscillator calculated from close, high and low.
Long: Close > pending high level AND previous close <= that level AND close > open AND high > low AND (close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction.
Long: Bars since upward breakout > retest delay AND low <= active high level * (1 + retest buffer fraction).
Long: Previous tracked minimum close < its value two bars ago AND previous tracked minimum MFI < previous MFI AND current MFI >= previous MFI AND (previous smoothed stochastic < oversold threshold OR current smoothed stochastic < oversold threshold).
Long: Current tracked minimum MFI = its value two bars ago AND its value two bars ago != its value three bars ago AND (previous smoothed stochastic < oversold threshold OR current smoothed stochastic < oversold threshold).
Short: Close < pending low level AND previous close >= that level AND close < open AND high > low AND (open - close) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction.
Short: Bars since downward breakout > retest delay AND high >= active low level * (1 - retest buffer fraction).
Short: Previous tracked maximum close > its value two bars ago AND previous tracked maximum MFI > previous MFI AND current MFI <= previous MFI AND (previous smoothed stochastic > overbought threshold OR current smoothed stochastic > overbought threshold).
Short: Current tracked maximum MFI = its value two bars ago AND its value two bars ago != its value three bars ago AND (previous smoothed stochastic > overbought threshold OR current smoothed stochastic > overbought threshold).
