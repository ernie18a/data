Calculation: Log return = natural logarithm of current close divided by previous close.
Calculation: Signed sigma = (current log return − reference center) / reference scale; selectable reference uses either the mean and population standard deviation of prior returns, an EWMA center and standard deviation based only on prior returns, or the prior-return median and 1.4826 × median absolute deviation.
Calculation: Estimated delta pressure = weighted moving average of signed volume divided by simple moving average of volume; signed volume uses candle direction, or—if selected—directional volume estimated from lower-timeframe bars (lower than the chart timeframe, including any bar still forming).
Calculation: RTH flow = cumulative estimated directional volume from the regular-session open, divided by cumulative volume; use 1-minute bars (lower than charts above 1 minute, same timeframe on a 1-minute chart) through the latest available minute, including a forming minute in live data. RTH price change = latest RTH close minus RTH open.
Long: Sigma mode AND signed sigma ≥ positive extreme threshold OR Delta mode AND estimated delta pressure > 0 OR RTH price change ≤ −minimum tick AND RTH flow ≥ minimum flow threshold.
Short: Sigma mode AND signed sigma ≤ −positive extreme threshold OR Delta mode AND estimated delta pressure < 0 OR RTH price change ≥ minimum tick AND RTH flow ≤ −minimum flow threshold.
