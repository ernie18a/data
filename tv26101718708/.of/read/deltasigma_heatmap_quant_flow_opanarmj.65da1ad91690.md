Calculation: Log return = ln(close / previous close), when both closes are positive.
Calculation: Signed sigma = (current log return − reference center) / reference scale, using one selected model and only prior chart-bar returns. Rolling: center is the mean and scale is the population standard deviation over a complete lookback. Robust: center is the median and scale is 1.482602218505602 times the median absolute deviation from that median over a complete lookback. EWMA: seed the mean with the first valid return and variance with zero; for each subsequent return, alpha = 1 − exp(ln(0.5) / half-life), deviation = return − previous mean, variance = (1 − alpha) × (previous variance + alpha × deviation²), and mean = previous mean + alpha × deviation. Reset on an invalid return; require at least the greater of the rolling lookback and one plus five half-lives of prior valid returns. Require a positive reference scale.
Calculation: Directional volume = either chart-bar volume multiplied by +1 when close >= open and −1 otherwise, or the sum of signed volume on lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the lower-timeframe bar still forming. For each lower-timeframe bar, direction is +1 if close > open, −1 if close < open, otherwise +1 if close > previous close, −1 if close < previous close, otherwise the previous direction. Require valid lower-timeframe volumes and their sum to match chart-bar volume within a coverage tolerance.
Calculation: Delta pressure = weighted moving average of directional volume divided by simple moving average of its corresponding volume, using the same lookback; require every bar in the lookback to have valid directional volume and the average volume to be positive.
Calculation: Session flow disagreement = session price change and cumulative signed-volume percentage from the regular-session open through the last completed lower-timeframe bar, or through the chart bar when the session calculation timeframe equals the chart timeframe. Sign each bar's volume using the lower-timeframe direction rule above, with direction carried across session boundaries. Session price change is the last session bar's close minus the session open; cumulative signed-volume percentage is 100 × sum of signed volume / sum of volume. Require the exact session opening bar, continuous valid bars through the last session bar, and positive total volume; after the session ends, retain the last session values until the next session anchor.
Long: Signed sigma >= positive sigma threshold.
Short: Signed sigma <= negative sigma threshold.
Long: Delta pressure > 0.
Short: Delta pressure < 0.
Long: Delta pressure >= positive saturation threshold AND previous delta pressure < positive saturation threshold.
Short: Delta pressure <= negative saturation threshold AND previous delta pressure > negative saturation threshold.
Long: Session price change <= negative minimum price movement AND cumulative signed-volume percentage >= positive flow threshold AND (the preceding chart bar did not satisfy these two comparisons OR the regular session changed).
Short: Session price change >= positive minimum price movement AND cumulative signed-volume percentage <= negative flow threshold AND (the preceding chart bar did not satisfy these two comparisons OR the regular session changed).
