Calculation: Normalized Williams oscillator = 100 × (close − lowest low over the oscillator lookback) / (highest high over that lookback − lowest low over that lookback); use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Flow = normalized Williams oscillator smoothed with the selected none, EMA, RMA, WMA, HMA, or zero-lag EMA method. Zero-lag EMA applies EMA to source + (source − source lagged by max(1, floor((length − 1) / 2)) bars), substituting the current source when the lagged value is unavailable.
Calculation: Signal line = flow smoothed with the selected EMA, RMA, WMA, HMA, or zero-lag EMA method, using its own smoothing length.
Calculation: Selected oscillator = flow and signal line calculated either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Optionally apply a separate chart-timeframe EMA to each higher-timeframe series after mapping it to chart bars. The display oscillator and the entry oscillator can select these sources independently; disabling multi-timeframe mode forces both to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Cross above = current first series > current second series AND previous first series <= previous second series; cross below = current first series < current second series AND previous first series >= previous second series.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = entry flow crosses above the oversold level OR entry flow was below the oversold level within the memory lookback AND entry flow crosses above its signal line AND entry flow > the oversold level. Bearish reversal = entry flow crosses below the overbought level OR entry flow was above the overbought level within the memory lookback AND entry flow crosses below its signal line AND entry flow < the overbought level.
Calculation: Bullish continuation = entry flow crosses above the midline OR entry flow > the midline AND entry flow crosses above its signal line. Bearish continuation = entry flow crosses below the midline OR entry flow < the midline AND entry flow crosses below its signal line.
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > chart-timeframe trend EMA AND that EMA >= its value at the trend-slope lookback. Bearish trend = close < that EMA AND that EMA <= its value at the trend-slope lookback.
Calculation: Bullish slope = entry flow > its previous value AND entry signal line >= its previous value. Bearish slope = entry flow < its previous value AND entry signal line <= its previous value.
Calculation: Bullish impulse = close > open AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) >= the minimum body ratio. Bearish impulse = close < open AND the same body ratio >= the minimum; use zero for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = on a higher timeframe, EMA direction is bullish when close > its EMA AND EMA >= its previous value, bearish when close < its EMA AND EMA <= its previous value, otherwise neutral; oscillator direction is bullish when flow > signal line AND flow > midline, bearish when flow < signal line AND flow < midline, otherwise neutral. Select EMA direction, oscillator direction, or their common direction when they agree, with disagreement neutral. Use either the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Directional score = weighted mean of extreme, trend, slope, ADX, candle, and cross components, scaled to a percentage; use zero when the sum of weights is zero. For the bullish side, the extreme component is 1 for bullish reversal, otherwise 0.65 when entry flow <= extreme-low level, otherwise 0.55 when entry flow < oversold level, otherwise 0.20. The trend component is 1 for bullish trend, otherwise 0.70 when the trend filter is disabled, otherwise 0.45 when close > trend EMA, otherwise 0. The slope component is 1 for bullish slope, otherwise 0.50 when entry flow rises, otherwise 0. The ADX component is clamp((ADX − an ADX baseline) / an ADX scaling range, 0, 1). The candle component is 1 for bullish impulse, otherwise 0.50 when close > open, otherwise 0. The cross component is 1 for an entry-flow cross above its signal line, otherwise 0.55 when entry flow > its signal line, otherwise 0. For the bearish side, reverse all directional comparisons, use bearish reversal, extreme-high and overbought levels, bearish trend, bearish slope, bearish impulse, and a cross below; use the same ADX component.
Calculation: Raw directional setup = the selected reversal, continuation, or either condition AND, in balanced mode, the corresponding slope and optionally entry flow on the directional side of its signal line AND, in sniper mode, those balanced conditions and optionally the corresponding trend and optionally entry flow on the directional side of the midline AND, in any mode, optionally the corresponding impulse AND directional score >= score threshold. Aggressive mode adds no balanced or sniper restrictions.
Long: Raw bullish setup AND previous bar's raw bullish setup is false AND higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR, with the preceding conditions, higher-timeframe direction is bullish OR neutral is allowed and higher-timeframe direction is neutral, AND no previous long signal exists or bars since the previous long signal >= cooldown.
Short: Raw bearish setup AND previous bar's raw bearish setup is false AND higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR, with the preceding conditions, higher-timeframe direction is bearish OR neutral is allowed and higher-timeframe direction is neutral, AND no previous short signal exists or bars since the previous short signal >= cooldown.
Long: Display flow >= display signal line.
Short: Display flow < display signal line.
Long: Display flow < oversold level OR display flow <= overbought level AND display flow >= oversold level AND display flow >= midline.
Short: Display flow > overbought level OR display flow <= overbought level AND display flow >= oversold level AND display flow < midline.
Long: Higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled AND higher-timeframe direction is bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled AND higher-timeframe direction is bearish.
Long: Bullish directional score >= score threshold.
Short: Bearish directional score >= score threshold.
