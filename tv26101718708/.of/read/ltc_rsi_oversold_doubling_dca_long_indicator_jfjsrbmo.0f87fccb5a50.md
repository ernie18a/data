Calculation: Average entry price = total quote currency spent on the base entry and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of each order's quote currency size divided by its fill close.
Long: Position is flat AND the optional active date window is disabled OR enabled and the bar datetime is within its inclusive bounds, AND RSI is below its threshold AND close is below the price ceiling. Calculate RSI on the selected timeframe: if higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed bar; if lower, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; if equal, use the chart bar.
Long: Position is long AND the optional active date window is disabled OR enabled and the bar datetime is within its inclusive bounds, AND fewer than four averaging orders have filled AND close <= base entry price × (1 − the next averaging order's deviation). Fill only the next order, at most one per bar, using its configured quote currency size; deviations and sizes are separately configurable for each successive order.
Exit: Long, the optional active date window is disabled OR enabled and the bar datetime is within its inclusive bounds, AND close >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage). Evaluate after any averaging order fill on the same bar.
