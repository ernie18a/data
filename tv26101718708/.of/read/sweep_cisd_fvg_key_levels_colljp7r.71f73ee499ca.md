Calculation: Higher-timeframe bars A, B, and C = the last completed, preceding, and second preceding completed higher-timeframe bars; evaluate their conditions on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Log midpoint of a bar = take logarithms of OHLC; if the larger wick exceeds the body in log space, use the midpoint of the larger wick, choosing the lower wick on a tie; otherwise use the midpoint of the log high and low; exponentiate the result.
Calculation: Outside rejection of A relative to B = A.high > B.high AND A.low < B.low AND A.close > B.low AND A.close < B.high.
Calculation: Bullish wick-lick setup = A.close > log midpoint of A AND outside rejection of A relative to B is false AND ((A.low < B.low AND A.close > B.low) OR (B.low < C.low AND A.close > min(B.open, B.close) AND (B.close <= log midpoint of B OR B.close <= C.low OR outside rejection of B relative to C)) OR (A.low < log midpoint of B AND A.low > B.open AND A.close > B.high)).
Calculation: Bearish wick-lick setup = A.close < log midpoint of A AND outside rejection of A relative to B is false AND ((A.high > B.high AND A.close < B.high) OR (B.high > C.high AND A.close < max(B.open, B.close) AND (B.close >= log midpoint of B OR B.close >= C.high OR outside rejection of B relative to C)) OR (A.high > log midpoint of B AND A.high < B.open AND A.close < B.low)).
Calculation: Wick-lick state = retain the latest setup's direction and A.close as its touch level. A new setup replaces the previous one and clears its touch state. A bullish touch occurs when (chart low < touch level OR chart open < touch level) AND chart close > touch level; a bearish touch occurs when (chart high > touch level OR chart open > touch level) AND chart close < touch level. Record the latest touch bar, including repeated touches, until a confirmation clears the touch state.
Calculation: Confirmation pivot = the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high or low, using a configurable number of bars on each side. For body confirmation, a pivot high exists when open is a pivot high OR close and high are both pivot highs, and its level is max(open, close) on the pivot bar; a pivot low exists when open is a pivot low OR close and low are both pivot lows, and its level is min(open, close) on the pivot bar. For wick confirmation, use the high or low pivot and its price.
Calculation: CISD level = for a standard wick-lick use the most recent chart bar within a configurable lookback whose high or low equals A's swept extreme; for an expansive wick-lick use B's swept extreme. Starting with that bar, include a configurable maximum number of immediately preceding consecutive non-bullish chart candles for a bullish setup or bullish chart candles for a bearish setup. The bullish level is the maximum body top and the bearish level is the minimum body bottom of the series; alternatively use wick extremes, with the starting bar represented by the swept extreme. Search from the extreme bar through the setup bar for the first close beyond the level; recognize the break on the setup bar if already present, otherwise do not extend the search.
Long: A.close > B.high OR (A.close >= B.low AND A.close <= B.high AND A.high <= B.high AND A.low < B.low AND A.close > B.low) OR (A.close = B.high AND A.high > B.high AND A.low < B.low AND lower wick of A < upper wick of A).
Short: A.close < B.low OR (A.close >= B.low AND A.close <= B.high AND A.high > B.high AND A.close < B.high) OR (A.close = B.low AND A.high > B.high AND A.low < B.low AND lower wick of A > upper wick of A).

Short: A bearish wick-lick setup occurs.

Short: A bearish wick-lick touch is active AND the latest confirmed pivot low's bar precedes the latest touch bar AND pivot low < touch level AND chart open > pivot low AND chart close < pivot low; clear the touch state after confirmation.
Long: A standard or expansive bullish wick-lick setup is selected AND a CISD close above its bullish level occurred between its extreme bar and the setup bar.
Short: A standard or expansive bearish wick-lick setup is selected AND a CISD close below its bearish level occurred between its extreme bar and the setup bar.

Short: On a selected other timeframe, the last completed bar's high < the low of the bar two bars earlier; evaluate when the requested completed OHLC changes. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
