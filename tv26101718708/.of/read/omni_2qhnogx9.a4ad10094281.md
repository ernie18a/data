Calculation: Confirmed swing highs/lows = pivot highs/lows using configured left/right bars; accept a new high when the prior accepted swing was not a high and the high is at least the configured ATR multiple above the last accepted low, and accept a new low symmetrically; same-side more extreme pivots update the current extreme without confirming a new swing. Use chart-timeframe bars and ATR.
Calculation: Bullish structure = latest accepted swing high > previous accepted swing high AND latest accepted swing low > previous accepted swing low; bearish structure = both latest swing points are lower than their respective previous points.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = third of three consecutive completed bars on the configured FVG timeframe has low > first bar's high, with gap size at least the configured ATR multiple of that timeframe's ATR; bearish FVG = third bar's high < first bar's low with the same size filter. Use last completed bars; the FVG timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
