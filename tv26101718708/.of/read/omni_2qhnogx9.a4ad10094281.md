Calculation: Confirmed swings = A pivot high or low is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars when its high or low is respectively the highest or lowest over the configured left-hand and right-hand bars. Maintain the last accepted top and bottom and their preceding accepted values. Accept a pivot of the opposite type if there is no accepted opposite swing or its distance from that swing is at least a configured multiple of the current chart-bar ATR. If a pivot has the same type as the last accepted swing, replace that swing's price only if the pivot is more extreme; do not update its preceding value or emit another swing decision. Process pivot highs before pivot lows on the same bar.
Calculation: Displayed EMA = For each configured EMA, if smoothing is enabled and a timeframe is specified, calculate the chart-timeframe close EMA with its length multiplied by the rounded ratio of specified timeframe duration to chart timeframe duration, with the ratio at least one and the resulting length capped at an implementation limit. Otherwise calculate the close EMA on the specified timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time. For a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar available within the chart bar. The two EMA lengths are short-term and longer-term, with independently specified timeframes.
Calculation: Fair value gap = On each newly available completed bar of the configured higher or lower timeframe, compare its low and high with the high and low of the bar two bars earlier on that timeframe. An upward gap has bottom equal to the earlier high and top equal to the latest low; a downward gap has bottom equal to the latest high and top equal to the earlier low. Keep a gap only if its width is at least a configured multiple of ATR on the latest completed bar of that timeframe. Retain at most a configured number of the most recent gaps. For an upward gap, first change it to a downward inverse gap if inversion is enabled and chart close is below its bottom; otherwise remove it if filled-gap removal is enabled and chart low is at or below its bottom. For a downward gap, first change it to an upward inverse gap if inversion is enabled and chart close is above its top; otherwise remove it if filled-gap removal is enabled and chart high is at or above its top. Remove an upward inverse gap when chart close is below its bottom and a downward inverse gap when chart close is above its top.
Calculation: Bias votes = Count separately the six bullish and six bearish comparisons: short-term displayed EMA above or below longer-term displayed EMA; chart close above or below a higher-timeframe close EMA; chart close above or below session VWAP; RSI above or below its midpoint; latest accepted top and bottom both above or both below their respective preceding accepted values; and chart close above or below the last completed higher-timeframe previous-day close. For the higher-timeframe EMA, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time. Equality contributes no vote.
Long: A pivot low is accepted as a confirmed swing.
Short: A pivot high is accepted as a confirmed swing.
Long: The latest completed gap-timeframe low is above the high two gap-timeframe bars earlier AND the upward gap width is at least the configured ATR multiple.
Short: The latest completed gap-timeframe high is below the low two gap-timeframe bars earlier AND the downward gap width is at least the configured ATR multiple.
Long: Inversion is enabled AND an active downward gap exists AND chart close is above its top.
Short: Inversion is enabled AND an active upward gap exists AND chart close is below its bottom.
Long: Short-term displayed EMA > longer-term displayed EMA.
Short: Short-term displayed EMA < longer-term displayed EMA.
Long: Chart close > higher-timeframe close EMA.
Short: Chart close < higher-timeframe close EMA.
Long: Chart close > session VWAP.
Short: Chart close <= session VWAP.
Long: RSI > its midpoint.
Short: RSI <= its midpoint.
Long: Latest accepted swing top > preceding accepted swing top AND latest accepted swing bottom > preceding accepted swing bottom.
Short: Latest accepted swing top < preceding accepted swing top AND latest accepted swing bottom < preceding accepted swing bottom.
Long: Chart close > last completed higher-timeframe previous-day close.
Short: Chart close <= last completed higher-timeframe previous-day close.
Long: Bullish bias votes > bearish bias votes.
Short: Bearish bias votes > bullish bias votes.
