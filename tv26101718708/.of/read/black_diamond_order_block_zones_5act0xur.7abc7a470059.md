Calculation: Body strength = absolute value of close minus open; average body strength = simple moving average of body strength, floored at a positive price increment; average volume = simple moving average of volume, floored at a positive volume threshold.
Calculation: Qualifying strength = body strength > average body strength × weak body multiplier OR (body strength > average body strength × medium body multiplier AND volume > average volume × medium volume multiplier) OR (body strength > average body strength × strong body multiplier AND volume > average volume × strong volume multiplier); the strong body and volume multipliers are greater than the corresponding medium multipliers, and the medium body multiplier is greater than the weak body multiplier.
Long: Close > highest high over the preceding lookback bars AND at least one preceding bar within the opposite-candle search lookback has close < open AND qualifying strength is true AND no long or short decision has occurred within the minimum zone-spacing period.
Short: Close < lowest low over the preceding lookback bars AND at least one preceding bar within the opposite-candle search lookback has close > open AND qualifying strength is true AND no long or short decision has occurred within the minimum zone-spacing period.
