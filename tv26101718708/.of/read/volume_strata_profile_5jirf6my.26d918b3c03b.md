Calculation: Profile row volumes = divide the lookback chart bars into chronological strata; partition the lookback high–low range into equal price rows and allocate each bar’s volume across overlapped rows in proportion to price-range overlap. Use lower-timeframe bars when available, otherwise chart bars; include the still-forming bar and its available intrabars.
Calculation: Stratum POC = midpoint of the price row with the greatest volume in that stratum; composite POC = midpoint of the row with the greatest total volume across all strata.
Calculation: Value Area High (VAH) = upper boundary of the contiguous range grown outward from the composite POC until it contains the configured percentage of total profile volume, adding the higher-volume adjacent row first (ties go upward).
Calculation: Recent stratum half POCs = midpoints of the greatest-volume rows in the older and newer halves of the newest stratum, respectively.
Calculation: Upward POC rotation = newest stratum POC row minus the immediately preceding stratum POC row exceeds the configured row tolerance; persistent upward rotation = the preceding stratum POC row minus the second preceding stratum POC row also exceeds that tolerance.
Calculation: New value acceptance = newer-half POC row is at least the older-half POC row minus the configured row tolerance.
Calculation: Migration quality = signed net change from oldest to newest stratum POC divided by the sum of absolute changes between consecutive stratum POCs, multiplied by the smaller of 1 and the absolute net change divided by the configured migration-size scale.
Calculation: Newest activity ratio = newest-stratum volume per chart bar divided by total profile volume per chart bar.
Calculation: Intrabar quality gate = pass when lower-timeframe data is inactive, or when the square root of (coverage of chart bars in the newest three strata × density adequacy) meets the configured quality threshold; density adequacy is average intrabars per covered bar divided by the maximum observed intrabars per bar, capped at 1.
Long: Upward POC rotation AND New value acceptance AND Migration quality > 0 OR upward POC rotation AND persistent upward rotation AND New value acceptance AND Migration quality meets the configured minimum with recent migration upward AND Newest activity ratio meets its configured minimum AND close >= VAH OR Intrabar quality gate passes AND at least the configured number of these six conditions pass: upward POC rotation, persistent upward rotation, New value acceptance, Migration quality meets its configured minimum with recent migration upward, close >= composite POC, close >= VAH - ATR × configured distance.
