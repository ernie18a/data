Calculation: Selected session = a configurable intraday time window on configurable weekdays in a configurable timezone. A chart bar is selected when its open is in the session, its last intrabar is in the session, all its intrabars are in the session, or any intrabar or its open is in the session, according to the selected mode. Intrabars use a lower timeframe aligned with the session boundaries and chart timeframe, or the chart timeframe if it is no longer than the chosen intrabar timeframe; use completed intrabars. Optionally invert the selection. Only intraday chart bars can be selected.
Long: Chart bar is selected AND close > open, OR chart bar is selected AND close > previous close when comparison with previous close is enabled; use open if previous close is unavailable.
Short: Chart bar is selected AND close < open, OR chart bar is selected AND close < previous close when comparison with previous close is enabled; use open if previous close is unavailable.
