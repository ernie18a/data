Calculation: Timeframe sampling = evaluate execution indicators on the still-forming execution-timeframe bar and merge their latest available values onto each chart bar; calculate any subsequent EMA, rolling window, crossover, or bars-since operation on the resulting chart-bar series. The execution timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Calculate zone extremes and ATR on the still-forming zone-timeframe bar, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. For each of the ordered trend timeframes, which may individually be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, use either its last completed bar and preceding bar or its still-forming bar and last completed bar, according to the closed-bar setting.
Calculation: Trend direction = bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND slow EMA > its preceding value; bearish when all three inequalities are reversed; otherwise neutral. Use the same fast and slow EMA lengths across trend and execution timeframes. Count bullish and bearish directions over the four ordered core trend timeframes. Trend agreement for a side requires its count >= the required count AND its count > the opposite count.
Calculation: Fractal direction = bullish when the chart-bar fast EMA of merged execution RSI > its slower EMA AND > the RSI midpoint AND (execution close > the latest confirmed execution pivot high OR execution close > execution slow EMA); bearish when the inequalities are reversed using the latest confirmed pivot low. Pivots require the configured numbers of bars on both sides. The oscillator EMA lengths are separate from the price EMA lengths.
Calculation: Zones = the zone-timeframe rolling lowest low through the current bar to that low plus zone ATR times a thickness multiplier for support, and the rolling highest high minus the same thickness to that high for resistance. A touch occurs when execution high and low overlap the corresponding zone. Recent touch means the latest touch is within the configured chart-bar window. A bullish zone exit requires a recent support touch as of the preceding chart bar AND execution close > support-zone upper edge AND close > open; bearish uses resistance, close < its lower edge, and close < open. A bullish breakout is a cross of execution close above the moving support-zone upper edge; bearish is a cross below the moving resistance-zone lower edge. A bullish retest requires that the latest bullish breakout occurred more than zero and at most the configured number of chart bars ago AND execution low <= support upper edge + execution ATR times retest tolerance AND close > support upper edge AND close > open; bearish reverses the comparisons around the resistance lower edge. Zone permission is the corresponding exit OR retest, unless the zone-exit filter is disabled.
Calculation: Body confirmation = execution close > open AND absolute body >= execution ATR times the body threshold for bullish; reverse the close/open inequality for bearish. Structure clearance = execution close > the previous execution-bar rolling highest high OR that high minus close >= execution ATR times the clearance multiplier for bullish; execution close < the previous execution-bar rolling lowest low OR close minus that low >= the same clearance for bearish. Depth = execution rolling highest high minus rolling lowest low, including the current execution bar.
Calculation: Confidence score = count one point for each satisfied item in this ordered bullish list: bullish trend agreement; bullish count > bearish count; bullish direction on the shortest core trend timeframe; bullish direction on the second-shortest core timeframe; bullish direction on either of the two longer core timeframes; execution close > fast EMA; fast EMA > slow EMA; close > slow EMA; slow EMA rising; middle EMA > long EMA; close > middle EMA; Hull MA rising; close > Hull MA; RSI > midpoint; RSI rising; MACD line > signal; MACD histogram > zero; CCI > zero; stochastic K > D; stochastic K > midpoint; MFI > midpoint; Williams %R > midpoint; ADX > its strength threshold AND positive DI > negative DI; parabolic SAR < close; OBV rising; VWMA rising; close > VWMA; close > Bollinger basis; close > midpoint of Bollinger basis and upper band; close > midpoint of previous execution-bar Donchian high and low; close > previous execution-bar structure highest high; bullish body confirmation; absolute body >= the body-confirmation ATR threshold; lower wick > upper wick; bullish fractal direction; bullish structure clearance. The bearish list reverses directional comparisons, uses the lower Bollinger band, the previous structure lowest low, bearish body and fractal conditions, upper wick > lower wick, and negative DI > positive DI; ADX remains above its threshold. Use shorter and longer EMA, MACD, stochastic smoothing, and Bollinger/Donchian lookbacks as appropriate. Confidence is the rounded percentage of satisfied items; rank is the same count rescaled and rounded to the rank scale.
Calculation: Pattern = evaluate execution-bar conditions on chart bars and retain the last matching pattern in this priority order, from lowest to highest: bullish structure break, bearish structure break, support doji else resistance doji else high-confidence doji, support hammer, resistance shooting star, double bottom, double top, bullish flag, bearish flag, bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing, bullish marubozu, bearish marubozu, bear trap, bull trap, bullish retest, bearish retest. A structure break is close beyond the previous execution-bar structure extreme AND corresponding body confirmation. A doji has body/range <= its threshold; the high-confidence variant is neutral. A hammer has lower wick >= body times its long-wick multiplier AND upper wick <= body times its short-wick multiplier AND close above its range-position threshold; shooting star mirrors it. A double bottom requires low within an ATR tolerance of the previous structure low AND bullish candle AND close > fast EMA AND recent support touch; double top mirrors it. A bullish flag requires close above its value a flag-lookback number of chart bars ago by more than an ATR multiple AND close > the preceding chart-bar rolling highest execution high over that lookback AND close > fast EMA > slow EMA AND bullish body confirmation; bearish mirrors it. Engulfing requires opposite-colored consecutive candles and the current body containing the previous body. Marubozu requires a directional candle, body/range >= its threshold, and each wick/range <= its threshold. A bear trap requires a prior bearish close crossing below the support-zone lower edge with bearish body confirmation, followed within the trap window, excluding that breakout bar, by close > support upper edge AND close > open; bull trap mirrors it at resistance. Use the selected pattern's direction and its source-defined score scale: structure break, ordinary engulfing, double patterns, flags, marubozu, traps, zone doji, hammer/shooting star, and retests have distinct configurable scores; upgrade double patterns on a structure break, flags and engulfing on a retest, engulfing on a recent zone touch, and marubozu on a retest or corresponding trend agreement plus fractal direction. A pattern is compatible with a side when the compatibility filter is disabled OR its direction is neutral or that side OR its score is below the strong-opposite-pattern threshold.
Calculation: Core setup = corresponding trend agreement AND confidence >= minimum confidence AND rank >= minimum rank AND depth >= execution ATR times depth multiplier AND corresponding structure clearance AND (body filter disabled OR corresponding body confirmation) AND zone permission AND pattern compatibility AND (fractal filter disabled OR corresponding fractal direction).
Calculation: Early setup = core setup is false AND the two shortest core trend directions match the side AND the side count >= its early minimum AND opposite count <= its early maximum AND confidence >= early confidence threshold AND rank >= minimum rank AND corresponding fractal direction AND the same body, zone, and pattern permissions as the core setup. Strong setup = core setup AND confidence >= strong threshold AND rank >= strong threshold AND corresponding fractal direction AND (strong-retest filter disabled OR corresponding retest). Normal setup = core setup AND strong setup is false. Each setup category is available only when its own enable setting is on; strong enablement affects whether normal is suppressed.
Calculation: Signal candidate = an enabled early, normal, or strong setup that is true now and was false on the preceding chart bar. Rank these strengths from early to normal to strong. Track the last printed signal's side and strength and the last printed chart-bar index for each side. An early candidate passes when the one-signal-per-trend filter is disabled OR the last side differs OR last strength is below early, AND its side's cooldown has elapsed or no prior signal exists. A normal or strong candidate passes when the filter is disabled OR the last side differs OR last strength is lower than the candidate, AND either its side's cooldown has elapsed or the last strength is lower than the candidate. If both sides pass, select the greater strength, breaking ties using greater confidence and choosing long on equal confidence. Update the selected side, strength, and its last signal bar; after that update, reset last strength below early whenever neither bullish nor bearish pressure ribbon is active. Pressure ribbon direction is determined by fast and slow chart-bar EMAs of bullish confidence minus bearish confidence: bullish requires fast > slow AND fast > a positive pressure threshold AND bullish fractal direction; bearish requires fast < slow AND fast < the negative threshold AND bearish fractal direction.
Calculation: Sentiment score = for each side, add configurable weights for its zone exit or retest; resistance breakout or support breakdown or corresponding flag or selected retest pattern; retest; selected matching pattern above the label-score threshold; selected reversal pattern; selected continuation pattern; confidence > opposite confidence; trend count > opposite count; corresponding pressure ribbon; corresponding fractal direction; and any corresponding core or early setup. A reversal pattern is a selected doji, hammer, shooting star, double top or bottom, or trap; other directional patterns are continuations. A breakout beyond the opposite zone requires a cross of close beyond that zone's outer edge AND body confirmation. Sentiment direction is the side whose score >= its minimum AND exceeds the opposite score by more than its margin, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Projection score = for each side, sum configurable weights for matching sentiment; zone exit or retest; zone exit; retest; breakout event; breakout retest; selected matching pattern with a larger weight above the strong-pattern threshold; reversal pattern; continuation pattern; trend agreement; trend count > opposite count; confidence > opposite confidence; fractal direction; pressure ribbon; and the highest available strong, core, or early setup. Breakout retest is the selected matching retest pattern or a retest coinciding with a matching breakout event. The projected direction is bullish when bullish minus bearish projection score >= the projection threshold and bearish when the difference <= its negative.
Calculation: Forecast direction = the side of the core or early setup, with bullish taking precedence; otherwise sentiment direction; otherwise selected pattern direction if its score >= the forecast-pattern threshold; otherwise the side with greater confidence, or neutral on a tie.
Long: selected signal candidate side = long
Short: selected signal candidate side = short
Long: selected pattern direction = bullish AND selected pattern score >= pattern-label threshold
Short: selected pattern direction = bearish AND selected pattern score >= pattern-label threshold
Long: bullish sentiment score >= sentiment minimum AND bullish sentiment score > bearish sentiment score + sentiment margin
Short: bearish sentiment score >= sentiment minimum AND bearish sentiment score > bullish sentiment score + sentiment margin
Long: bullish projection score - bearish projection score >= projection threshold
Short: bullish projection score - bearish projection score <= negative projection threshold
Long: bullish pressure ribbon = true
Short: bearish pressure ribbon = true
Long: forecast direction = bullish
Short: forecast direction = bearish
Long: trend direction on any displayed trend timeframe = bullish
Short: trend direction on any displayed trend timeframe = bearish
