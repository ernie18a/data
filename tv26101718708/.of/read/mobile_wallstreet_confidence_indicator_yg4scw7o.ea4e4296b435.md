Calculation: Execution-bar data = OHLCV and indicators calculated on the execution timeframe’s current bar; relative to the chart timeframe, it may be higher or lower, and may still be forming.
Calculation: MTF direction = bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA, close > slow EMA, and current slow EMA > prior slow EMA; bearish when all inequalities are reversed; otherwise neutral. Evaluate each requested timeframe on its last completed bar when closed-timeframe mode is enabled, otherwise on its forming bar.
Calculation: Bullish MTF count / bearish MTF count = number of bullish / bearish directions among the four signal timeframes. MTF bullish agreement = bullish count >= required count AND bullish count > bearish count; bearish agreement = bearish count >= required count AND bearish count > bullish count.
Calculation: Hull MA = Hull moving average of execution-timeframe close; RSI = 14-period RSI; ATR = average true range. Fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA; middle EMA uses a longer lookback than slow EMA; long EMA uses a longer lookback than middle EMA.
Calculation: Fractal ribbon bullish = fast EMA of execution-timeframe RSI > its slower EMA AND fast EMA > 50 AND (execution close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high OR exceeds slow EMA). Bearish = fast EMA < slower EMA AND fast EMA < 50 AND (execution close is below the latest confirmed pivot low OR below slow EMA). The RSI EMAs use a shorter and longer lookback, respectively; pivots use left/right bar lookbacks.
Calculation: Bull confidence raw score = count of these 36 true conditions: bullish MTF agreement; bullish count > bearish count; M15 bullish; H1 bullish; H4 or D1 bullish; close > fast EMA; fast EMA > slow EMA; close > slow EMA; slow EMA > its prior value; middle EMA > long EMA; close > middle EMA; Hull MA > its prior value; close > Hull MA; RSI > 50; RSI > prior RSI; MACD line > signal line; MACD histogram > 0; CCI > 0; stochastic K > D; stochastic K > 50; MFI > 50; Williams %R > -50; ADX > 15 AND +DI > -DI; parabolic SAR < close; OBV > prior OBV; VWMA > prior VWMA; close > VWMA; close > Bollinger basis; close > midpoint of basis and upper band; close > midpoint of prior Donchian high and low; close > prior structure high; bullish candle body AND body >= 0.12 ATR; body >= 0.12 ATR; lower wick > upper wick; bullish fractal ribbon; bullish structure clearance. Bear confidence raw score counts the corresponding reversed conditions (including ADX > 15 AND -DI > +DI, SAR > close, upper wick > lower wick, and bearish fractal ribbon/structure clearance). Confidence = round(raw score / 36 * 100); rank = round(raw score / 3.6).
Calculation: Structure high/low = highest high / lowest low over the structure lookback, excluding the current execution bar. Bullish structure clearance = close > structure high OR structure high - close >= ATR * clearance multiplier; bearish clearance = close < structure low OR close - structure low >= ATR * clearance multiplier.
Calculation: Depth condition = highest high minus lowest low over the recent 10 execution bars >= ATR * minimum-depth multiplier.
Calculation: Buy/sell zones = lowest low / highest high over the zone-timeframe lookback, with thickness equal to zone-timeframe ATR * zone-thickness multiplier. The zone timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; values use the available current zone-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Buy zone exit = a buy-zone touch occurred within the allowed bars before the current bar AND close > buy-zone high AND close > open. Sell zone exit = a sell-zone touch occurred within the allowed bars before the current bar AND close < sell-zone low AND close < open. Buy post-retest = a prior close crossed above buy-zone high within the post-retest lookback AND current low <= buy-zone high + ATR * retest tolerance AND close > buy-zone high AND close > open. Sell post-retest is the reverse across sell-zone low. Zone filter passes when disabled or the corresponding zone exit or post-retest condition is true.
Calculation: Candle confirmation = bullish close > open with body >= 0.12 ATR; bearish close < open with body >= 0.12 ATR.
Calculation: Pattern direction and score = assign the matching pattern’s direction and score in this priority order, with later matches overriding earlier ones: bullish/bearish structure break (close beyond prior structure high/low with candle confirmation, score 6); doji at recently touched buy/sell zone (score 5); doji with either confidence >= 70 (neutral, score 4); hammer at buy zone / shooting star at sell zone (score 8); double bottom/top (execution low/high within ATR tolerance of prior structure low/high, bullish/bearish candle, close above/below fast EMA, and recent zone touch; score 7, or 9 if close also exceeds prior structure high / falls below prior structure low); bull/bear flag (directional impulse over flag lookback > 1.5 ATR followed by breakout of prior pullback high/low, close on the correct side of fast EMA, fast EMA above/below slow EMA, and candle confirmation; score 8, or 9 on post-retest); bullish/bearish engulfing (opposite-colored prior candle and current body contains prior body; score 6, or 8 at a recently touched matching zone, or 9 on post-retest); bullish/bearish marubozu (directional body >= 75% of range and each wick <= 12% of range; score 7, or 9 on post-retest, or 10 when matching MTF agreement and fractal ribbon hold); bear/bull trap reversal (within trap lookback after the corresponding resistance/support break, close back beyond the opposite zone boundary with reversed candle color; score 9); buy/sell breakout retest (post-retest condition; score 10). Pattern compatibility passes when disabled, pattern direction is not opposite, or pattern score < 7.
Calculation: Buy core = bullish MTF agreement AND bullish confidence >= minimum confidence AND bullish rank >= minimum rank AND depth condition AND bullish structure clearance AND bullish candle confirmation when required AND buy zone filter AND pattern compatibility AND bullish fractal ribbon when its gate is enabled. Sell core is the mirrored bearish condition.
Long: (Buy core AND NOT strong buy) OR (bullish MTF count >= 2 AND M15 bullish AND H1 bullish AND bearish count <= 1 AND bullish confidence >= early-confidence threshold AND bullish rank >= minimum rank AND bullish fractal ribbon AND bullish candle confirmation when required AND buy zone filter AND pattern compatibility AND NOT buy core) OR (pattern direction = bullish AND pattern score >= pattern-label threshold).
Short: (Sell core AND NOT strong sell) OR (bearish MTF count >= 2 AND M15 bearish AND H1 bearish AND bullish count <= 1 AND bearish confidence >= early-confidence threshold AND bearish rank >= minimum rank AND bearish fractal ribbon AND bearish candle confirmation when required AND sell zone filter AND pattern compatibility AND NOT sell core) OR (pattern direction = bearish AND pattern score >= pattern-label threshold).
Calculation: Strong buy = buy core AND bullish confidence >= strong-confidence threshold AND bullish rank >= strong-rank threshold AND bullish count >= required MTF count AND bullish fractal ribbon AND (retest requirement disabled OR buy post-retest). Strong sell is the mirrored bearish condition.
Long: Strong buy OR buy core, only on its false-to-true transition and when repeat-control rules allow it: if one-signal-per-trend is enabled, the previous signal is not an equal-or-stronger buy; cooldown has elapsed unless signal strength upgrades. Strength order: early < normal < strong.
Short: Strong sell OR sell core, only on its false-to-true transition and when repeat-control rules allow it: if one-signal-per-trend is enabled, the previous signal is not an equal-or-stronger sell; cooldown has elapsed unless signal strength upgrades. Strength order: early < normal < strong.
Calculation: Active sentiment score = for bullish: +2 buy-zone exit/post-retest; +2 bullish resistance break, flag breakout, or breakout retest; +1 buy post-retest; +2 bullish pattern at/above label threshold; +1 bullish reversal pattern; +1 bullish continuation pattern; +1 bullish confidence > bearish confidence; +1 bullish MTF count > bearish count; +1 bullish ribbon; +1 bullish fractal ribbon; +2 any buy core/early/strong condition. Bearish score is the mirrored sum. Active bullish sentiment = bullish score >= 4 AND bullish score > bearish score + 1; bearish sentiment is the reverse.
Long: Active bullish sentiment AND prior bar was not active bullish sentiment.
Short: Active bearish sentiment AND prior bar was not active bearish sentiment.
