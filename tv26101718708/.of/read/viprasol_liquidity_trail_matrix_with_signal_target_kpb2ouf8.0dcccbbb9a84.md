Calculation: Trail bands and trend = Use a selectable price source and a trail ATR. Initialize trend bullish. For each of four bands, use an increasing ATR multiplier equal to a base multiplier times one plus the band index offset times a spacing parameter. In a bullish trend, each band is the greater of source minus its ATR offset and its preceding value; in a bearish trend, it is the lesser of source plus its ATR offset and its preceding value. If the source falls below the preceding value of a selected outer band in a bullish trend, flip bearish and reset all bands to source plus their offsets. If the source rises above that band in a bearish trend, flip bullish and reset all bands to source minus their offsets. Trend age is bars since the most recent flip, initially bars since the first bar.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI regime = Bullish when the selectable regime price source is above its SMA and RSI of a selectable source is within the bullish range, both continuously for a confirmation period. Bearish when the regime price source is below its SMA and RSI is within the bearish range continuously for that period. Otherwise neutral. The bullish range is higher than the bearish range and the ranges may overlap.
Calculation: Retest state = On each bar, decrement each directional pending counter to a minimum of zero and clear its stored depth if the counter reaches zero; clear both counters and depths on a trend flip. In a bullish trend, a low at or below a trail band touches that band; in a bearish trend, a high at or above a trail band touches it. Record the deepest touched band, reset the corresponding counter to the retest window, and retain the greatest depth since the counter was last cleared. A confirmed retest clears its counter and depth. The last confirmed retest bar is shared by both directions for cooldown.
Calculation: Retest score = Sum depth, candle, trend-age, volume and bias points, optionally adding regime points and renormalizing by the maximum possible total. Depth points rank the second band highest, then the third, first and fourth. Candle points use the close's fraction of the high-low range measured from the favorable end, with higher points for a closer favorable close; use the midpoint for a zero-range bar. Trend-age points are highest within a preferred age interval, intermediate below it and lowest above it. Volume points are highest when volume exceeds a multiple greater than one of the preceding bar's volume SMA, intermediate when it exceeds that SMA, and lowest otherwise; use neutral points when cumulative volume is zero. Bias points are highest when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for long or at or below it for short, neutral when unavailable, and zero when opposed; alternatively use the preceding chart bar when the chart timeframe is selected. Regime points are highest for an aligned confirmed RSI regime, intermediate for neutral and zero for an opposed regime. Round the renormalized score to the nearest integer.
Calculation: Trade levels = At entry close, set the initial stop either at entry minus or plus a risk-ATR multiple, or, for a long, the lesser of entry-bar low minus a risk-ATR buffer and entry minus a minimum risk-ATR distance, and for a short the greater of entry-bar high plus the buffer and entry plus the minimum distance. Risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Set three profit targets at entry plus risk multiples for a long or minus them for a short; the target multiples increase from first to third. After the first target is touched without the stop being touched on that bar, optionally move the stop to entry starting on the next bar.
Long: Bullish trend = true.
Short: Bearish trend = true.
Long: Confirmed bullish RSI regime = true.
Short: Confirmed bearish RSI regime = true.
Long: Trend flips bullish AND chart bar index >= warmup threshold, where the threshold is the greater of a multiple of the trail ATR lookback and a minimum bar count.
Short: Trend flips bearish AND chart bar index >= warmup threshold, where the threshold is the greater of a multiple of the trail ATR lookback and a minimum bar count.
Long: Bullish trend AND long pending counter > 0 AND close > innermost trail band AND close > open AND chart bar index >= warmup threshold AND bars since the last confirmed retest >= cooldown AND long retest score >= minimum score AND (regime filtering is disabled OR (confirmed bullish RSI regime AND (ADX filtering is disabled OR ADX >= minimum))).
Short: Bearish trend AND short pending counter > 0 AND close < innermost trail band AND close < open AND chart bar index >= warmup threshold AND bars since the last confirmed retest >= cooldown AND short retest score >= minimum score AND (regime filtering is disabled OR (confirmed bearish RSI regime AND (ADX filtering is disabled OR ADX >= minimum))).
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= active stop OR high >= third profit target OR (timeout enabled AND bars since entry >= timeout)).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= active stop OR low <= third profit target OR (timeout enabled AND bars since entry >= timeout)).
Exit: long, reversal enabled AND (confirmed short retest OR (bearish trend flip after warmup AND (regime filtering disabled OR (confirmed bearish RSI regime AND (ADX filtering disabled OR ADX >= minimum))))).
Exit: short, reversal enabled AND (confirmed long retest OR (bullish trend flip after warmup AND (regime filtering disabled OR (confirmed bullish RSI regime AND (ADX filtering disabled OR ADX >= minimum))))).
