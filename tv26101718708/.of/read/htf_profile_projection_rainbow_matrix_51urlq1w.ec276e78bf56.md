Calculation: Forming higher-timeframe profile = Divide the still-forming higher-timeframe bar's high-to-low range into equal price buckets; choose the bucket count by rounding the range divided by chart-timeframe ATR and clamping it to the minimum and maximum bucket counts. For every chart bar since the higher-timeframe bar opened, assign its volume, or signed volume in delta mode, equally to every bucket its low-to-high range spans. Signed volume is positive when close > open, negative when close < open, and zero otherwise. Sum contributions in each bucket. The imbalance threshold is a fraction of the mean absolute bucket value.
Long: Still-forming higher-timeframe close >= still-forming higher-timeframe open
Short: Still-forming higher-timeframe close < still-forming higher-timeframe open
Long: Maximum absolute profile bucket value > 0 AND still-forming higher-timeframe high > still-forming higher-timeframe low AND bucket value >= 0 AND (imbalance marking is disabled OR absolute bucket value > imbalance threshold)
Short: Maximum absolute profile bucket value > 0 AND still-forming higher-timeframe high > still-forming higher-timeframe low AND bucket value < 0 AND (imbalance marking is disabled OR absolute bucket value > imbalance threshold)
