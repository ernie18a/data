Calculation: Swing high = latest higher-timeframe pivot high; if stale beyond the configured age, use the rolling high over a longer higher-timeframe lookback. Pivot uses a higher-timeframe bar still forming; fallback uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Swing low = latest higher-timeframe pivot low; if stale beyond the configured age, use the rolling low over a longer higher-timeframe lookback. Pivot uses a higher-timeframe bar still forming; fallback uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish fib zone = chart close is within an ATR-scaled width of any configured retracement level measured down from swing high across the swing range.
Calculation: Bearish fib zone = chart close is within an ATR-scaled width of any configured retracement level measured up from swing low across the swing range.
Calculation: Bullish candle = close > open AND (body > 0.4 × full range OR open − low > 1.5 × body OR (body > previous body AND previous close < previous open)).
Calculation: Bearish candle = close < open AND (body > 0.4 × full range OR high − open > 1.5 × body OR (body > previous body AND previous close > previous open)).
Calculation: Initial risk distance = chart ATR × configured stop multiplier.
Long: swing high > swing low AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA (EMA uses a higher-timeframe bar still forming) AND close is in the bullish fib zone AND bullish candle AND close > chart EMA when EMA confirmation is enabled AND RSI is below its overbought threshold and above its lower bound AND within the configured session and date range AND position is flat.
Short: swing high > swing low AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA (EMA uses a higher-timeframe bar still forming) AND close is in the bearish fib zone AND bearish candle AND close < chart EMA when EMA confirmation is enabled AND RSI is above its oversold threshold and below its upper bound AND within the configured session and date range AND position is flat.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − initial risk distance OR high >= entry price + initial risk distance × first target multiple OR high >= entry price + initial risk distance × second target multiple OR configured end-of-day close time.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + initial risk distance OR low <= entry price − initial risk distance × first target multiple OR low <= entry price − initial risk distance × second target multiple OR configured end-of-day close time.
