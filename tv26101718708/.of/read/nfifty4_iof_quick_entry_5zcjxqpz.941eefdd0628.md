Calculation: Sampled bar = the chart bar when the selected timeframe equals the chart timeframe; for a higher timeframe, use the last completed aggregated bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. Process a sample only when its sampled opening time changes. The three independently enabled zone timeframes are ordered shorter, intermediate, and longer.
Calculation: Base and impulse = for each sampled bar, body fraction is abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low; wick fraction is 1 - body fraction when high exceeds low, otherwise zero. A base has wick fraction >= its timeframe's threshold. In strict mode an impulse has body fraction >= its timeframe's threshold; otherwise an impulse is any non-base bar.
Calculation: Zone = after an impulse on the preceding chart bar, start a cluster at the next sampled base; accumulate the minimum and maximum body endpoints and the minimum low and maximum high of subsequent sampled bases. Discard the cluster if its base count exceeds the maximum. A sampled non-base impulse following a nonempty cluster creates a demand zone if its close > open, otherwise a supply zone. A sampled bar that is neither base nor impulse leaves the cluster unchanged. On each confirmed chart bar, deactivate a demand zone if min(open, close) < its cluster low, or a supply zone if max(open, close) > its cluster high.
Calculation: Zone levels = demand entry is the cluster's minimum body endpoint, demand height is maximum body endpoint minus cluster low, and demand stop is cluster low minus a configurable price buffer; supply entry is the maximum body endpoint, supply height is cluster high minus minimum body endpoint, and supply stop is cluster high plus the buffer. Target number k is entry plus k times a configurable target step times zone height for demand, and entry minus that distance for supply.
Calculation: Armed trade = after processing invalidations and existing trades on each confirmed chart bar, arm an active zone when its height > 0, abs(close - zone entry) <= a configurable proximity multiple of height, close > entry for demand or close < entry for supply, chart bars since the zone was last armed >= the cooldown, the zone has no armed or active trade, the optional block-after-loss condition is satisfied, and the risk budget permits at least one contract at the zone's stop distance. An armed trade persists even if its zone is subsequently invalidated; it can fill starting on the next chart bar. If blocking after loss is enabled, a zone that has produced a loss remains blocked.
Long: A demand zone is created.
Short: A supply zone is created.
Long: An active demand zone has height > 0 AND close > zone entry AND abs(close - zone entry) <= the proximity multiple of height AND its entry is among the configured number of nearest eligible zone entries on its timeframe.
Short: An active supply zone has height > 0 AND close < zone entry AND abs(close - zone entry) <= the proximity multiple of height AND its entry is among the configured number of nearest eligible zone entries on its timeframe.
Long: A demand-zone trade is armed AND low <= its entry.
Short: A supply-zone trade is armed AND high >= its entry.
Exit: long, min(open, close) < the trade's demand stop OR the configured final target number is at most three AND high >= that target.
Exit: short, max(open, close) > the trade's supply stop OR the configured final target number is at most three AND low <= that target.
