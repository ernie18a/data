Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a bar's high or low confirmed as a pivot using equal numbers of bars before and after it; emit it on the confirmation bar. Each structure, zone, and liquidity calculation has its own independent pivot lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot stream = confirmed pivots calculated on the higher timeframe using its last completed bar and merged onto chart bars with lookahead off and forward-filled values. Treat every nonmissing merged value as a new pivot, even when it repeats.
Calculation: Major trend = initially neutral; update the previous and latest higher-timeframe pivot highs and lows on every chart bar. Set a lower-high flag when the latest pivot high is below its predecessor; a subsequent lower low sets trend bearish and clears both flags. Set a higher-low flag when the latest pivot low is above its predecessor; a subsequent higher high sets trend bullish and clears both flags. Process lower high, lower low, higher low, and higher high in that order. Otherwise retain the trend.
Calculation: Structure break = chart close crosses above the latest previously recorded pivot high, or crosses below the latest previously recorded pivot low; compare both the current and preceding closes with that same level. Check the break against the pivot recorded before processing the current bar's pivot. Apply this independently to chart-timeframe pivots and the higher-timeframe pivot stream.
Calculation: Chart trend and change of character = chart trend starts neutral; an upward chart structure break is a bullish change of character if the prior trend was bearish, then sets trend bullish. A downward chart structure break is a bearish change of character if the prior trend was bullish, then sets trend bearish. Process upward before downward breaks.
Calculation: Supply or demand zone = at a chart-timeframe pivot high or low, respectively, start with the pivot bar's high and low as the zone bounds. For each of the two preceding bars, include its high and low in the bounds if its high is below the pivot high for supply, or its low is above the pivot low for demand. Retain only the most recent configured number of supply and demand zones combined, inserting supply before demand when both are created on one bar. A supply zone becomes invalid when close is above its upper bound; a demand zone becomes invalid when close is below its lower bound. Invalidation occurs before testing zone membership.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = retain the most recent configured number of chart-timeframe pivot high and low levels combined, inserting highs before lows when both are confirmed on one bar. A retained unswept high level is swept when high exceeds it; a retained unswept low level is swept when low falls below it. Mark each level swept only once. Insert new levels before checking sweeps.
Calculation: Recent event = the current bar index minus the most recent event bar index is no greater than the configured event lookback; require the difference to be greater than zero for a change of character. Initialize each most recent change-of-character and sweep bar index to zero.
Long: A chart-timeframe pivot high is confirmed AND its value is greater than the preceding confirmed pivot high.
Long: A chart-timeframe pivot low is confirmed AND its value is greater than the preceding confirmed pivot low.
Short: A chart-timeframe pivot high is confirmed AND its value is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed pivot high OR no preceding pivot high exists.
Short: A chart-timeframe pivot low is confirmed AND its value is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed pivot low OR no preceding pivot low exists.
Long: Chart timeframe is longer than the configured entry timeframe limit AND a higher-timeframe pivot high or low is present AND its value is greater than the preceding same-kind pivot in the merged stream.
Short: Chart timeframe is longer than the configured entry timeframe limit AND a higher-timeframe pivot high or low is present AND its value is less than or equal to the preceding same-kind pivot in the merged stream OR no preceding same-kind pivot exists.
Long: An upward chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe structure break occurs.
Short: A downward chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe structure break occurs.
Long: Major trend is bullish.
Short: Major trend is bearish.
Long: A chart-timeframe pivot low for a demand zone is confirmed.
Short: A chart-timeframe pivot high for a supply zone is confirmed.
Long: Chart timeframe is no longer than the configured entry timeframe limit AND major trend is bullish AND a bullish chart change of character is recent AND close is within the bounds of a valid demand zone, inclusive AND a low-level liquidity sweep is recent.
Short: Chart timeframe is no longer than the configured entry timeframe limit AND major trend is bearish AND a bearish chart change of character is recent AND close is within the bounds of a valid supply zone, inclusive AND a high-level liquidity sweep is recent.
