Calculation: Chart swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding and following swing-lookback bars; it is confirmed after the following bars complete. Chart swing low = a low less than the lows of those bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing high and swing low = the same pivot definitions calculated from aggregated bars of a higher timeframe, using confirmed bars.
Calculation: Latest and previous swing highs/lows = the most recently confirmed pivot and the pivot confirmed immediately before it, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest swing high (current close > level and previous close ≤ level). Bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest swing low (current close < level and previous close ≥ level).
Calculation: Major trend = bearish after a lower high followed by a lower low; bullish after a higher low followed by a higher high. Each sequence may span multiple bars.
Calculation: Bullish minor change of character = a bullish structure break while the prior minor trend is bearish. Bearish minor change of character = a bearish structure break while the prior minor trend is bullish. A minor trend changes to bullish or bearish on each respective structure break.
Calculation: Demand zone = the range from the low to the high of a confirmed chart swing-low candle, expanded to include either of the two preceding candles whose low is above the swing-low candle's low. Supply zone = the corresponding range around a confirmed chart swing-high candle, including either of the two preceding candles whose high is below the swing-high candle's high. A demand zone is invalidated by a close below its bottom; a supply zone by a close above its top. Only the newest configured maximum number of zones are retained.
Calculation: Sellside sweep = a bar's low falls below an unswept confirmed chart swing-low level. Buyside sweep = a bar's high rises above an unswept confirmed chart swing-high level. A sweep is recent when it occurred within the configured lookback bars before the current bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe swing high is confirmed and exceeds the preceding higher-timeframe swing high OR higher-timeframe swing low is confirmed and exceeds the preceding higher-timeframe swing low OR bullish structure break of a higher-timeframe swing level OR bullish structure break of a chart swing level OR chart timeframe is within the indicator's sniper-eligible range AND major trend is bullish AND a bullish minor change of character occurred within the configured lookback bars, excluding its event bar AND close is within a valid demand zone AND a sellside sweep occurred within the configured lookback bars.
Short: Higher-timeframe swing high is confirmed and is below the preceding higher-timeframe swing high OR higher-timeframe swing low is confirmed and is below the preceding higher-timeframe swing low OR bearish structure break of a higher-timeframe swing level OR bearish structure break of a chart swing level OR chart timeframe is within the indicator's sniper-eligible range AND major trend is bearish AND a bearish minor change of character occurred within the configured lookback bars, excluding its event bar AND close is within a valid supply zone AND a buyside sweep occurred within the configured lookback bars.
