Calculation: Composite value area = Over the chart-bar lookback including the current bar, divide the price axis into bins whose size is either a parameterized fraction of the current close, a fixed price increment, or the lookback high-low range divided by a target bin count; round the size to a price-increment parameter and enforce that increment as the minimum. For each bar, divide its volume equally among bins from its low bin through its high bin, capped at a parameterized maximum number of consecutive bins starting at the low bin. Sum volume by bin. The point of control is the highest-volume bin, breaking ties by first insertion while processing bars newest to oldest and bins low to high. Starting there, expand toward the larger-volume adjacent bin until a parameterized fraction of total profile volume is included; choose the upper bin on a tie and stop if both adjacent bins have zero volume. The value area high is the upper edge of the highest included bin; the value area low is the lower edge of the lowest included bin.
Calculation: Completed-session points of control = For each completed daily session, distribute chart-bar volume into price bins from low to high as in the composite profile, processing bars oldest to newest. Use a bin size fixed at the start of the session: either a fixed price increment or a parameterized fraction of ATR from the last completed daily bar, falling back to a parameterized fraction of the session's first close when ATR is unavailable; round to a price-increment parameter and enforce that increment as the minimum. The point of control is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, breaking ties by first insertion. Daily bars are higher than an intraday chart timeframe and the same timeframe as a daily chart.
Calculation: Point-of-control drift span = The larger of the absolute differences between the latest and previous completed-session points of control and between the previous and preceding completed-session points of control. Compare this span with a parameterized multiple of the current session's fixed bin size.
Long: Close > composite value area high
Short: Close < composite value area low
Long: Point-of-control drift span > its threshold AND latest completed-session point of control > preceding completed-session point of control
Short: Point-of-control drift span > its threshold AND latest completed-session point of control <= preceding completed-session point of control
Long: Close > current composite value area high AND previous close <= current composite value area high
Short: Close < current composite value area low AND previous close >= current composite value area low
