Calculation: Price source = a selected series derived from chart-bar OHLC fields.
Calculation: Session gap = the elapsed time since the preceding bar exceeds a multiple of the rolling median elapsed time between bars. When session guards are enabled, reset the session bar count to zero at a session gap; otherwise increment it each bar.
Calculation: OU fit = regress the current price source on its one-bar lag over the OU window, obtaining intercept a and slope b. Mean = a / (1 - b); half-life = ln(2) / -ln(b) when 0 < b < 1. For each pair in the window, calculate the reverse residual as lagged price minus (a + b × current price); equilibrium deviation = sqrt(sum of squared reverse residuals / (window length - 2)) / sqrt(1 - b²). OU z-score = (current price source - mean) / equilibrium deviation.
Calculation: Hurst estimate = ln(R / S) / ln(n), where n is the OU window length minus one, R is the range of the chronological cumulative sum of demeaned one-bar log returns, and S is their sample standard deviation.
Calculation: ADF statistic = the t-statistic of the lagged-price coefficient in an intercept-inclusive regression of one-bar price changes on lagged price over the OU window, using residual variance divided by window length minus two.
Calculation: Variance ratio at lag q = the sample variance of overlapping q-bar price changes divided by q times the sample variance of one-bar price changes, both calculated over the OU window. Use shorter, intermediate, and longer q for the short, intermediate, and long horizons respectively.
Calculation: Statistical direction = +1 when the OU z-score is below its negative entry threshold, -1 when it is above its positive entry threshold, and 0 otherwise; set it to 0 unless the OU half-life is positive and at most its maximum, the Hurst estimate is below its ceiling, the optional ADF statistic is below its cutoff, the optional intermediate-lag variance ratio is below its cutoff, and the OU window contains no session gap when gap invalidation is enabled. Statistical strength = min(abs(OU z-score) / a larger OU z-score scale, strength cap) when those validity conditions hold, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP z-score = the z-score, over its lookback, of close minus the chart's daily-anchored VWAP of typical price. Anchored VWAP = cumulative typical price times nonnegative volume divided by cumulative nonnegative volume, reset at each detected session gap when session guards are enabled. Anchored deviation = the square root of the cumulative sum of nonnegative volume times (typical price minus the anchored VWAP as calculated on that bar) squared, divided by cumulative nonnegative volume. Anchored VWAP z-score = (close - anchored VWAP) / anchored deviation, after more than two accumulated bars.
Calculation: Each VWAP direction = +1 below its negative z-score threshold, -1 above its positive threshold, and 0 otherwise; its strength = min(abs(z-score) / its threshold, strength cap). If the anchored z-score is unavailable or anchoring is disabled, use the rolling direction and strength. Otherwise, when both directions agree, use that direction and their weighted-average strength; when exactly one direction is nonzero, use it and its weighted strength; when they oppose, use direction and strength 0.
Calculation: Flow-adjusted VWAP strength = combined VWAP strength multiplied by the volume-spike factor when volume exceeds a multiple of its moving average, by the flow-alignment factor when the combined direction opposes order-flow imbalance, and by the toxicity penalty when the toxicity z-score exceeds its threshold. Estimate buy fraction as 1 / (1 + exp(-sensitivity × close change / standard deviation of close changes)), or one-half when that deviation is zero; buy and sell volume are volume times buy fraction and its complement. Order-flow imbalance is the rolling sum of buy minus sell volume divided by the greater of rolling volume and a positive floor. Toxicity is the rolling sum of absolute buy-minus-sell volume divided by rolling volume; its z-score uses a longer rolling mean and standard deviation, replacing unavailable toxicity with zero in those rolling statistics.
Calculation: Composite = the weighted sum of statistical direction times statistical strength and combined VWAP direction times flow-adjusted VWAP strength, multiplied by a clamped product of enabled multipliers. With external feeds unavailable, the volatility-regime multiplier is its unknown-regime value when enabled and 1 otherwise; the constituent, volatility-premium, and cross-asset multipliers are 1. For optional expiry pinning, at a detected session gap on the configured expiry weekday, set the pin to close rounded to the configured strike increment; clear it on other weekdays. After the configured hour, multiply by the alignment boost if the pin direction agrees with the sign of the unmultiplied sum, or the opposition penalty if it disagrees; otherwise use 1.
Calculation: Confluence count = the number of nonzero statistical and VWAP directions equal to the composite sign. Strict agreement holds when each enabled statistical and VWAP direction is either zero or equal to the nonzero composite sign.
Calculation: Tentative horizon = in weighted mode, select the longest qualifying horizon in order: long if absolute composite reaches its long threshold, OU half-life is unavailable or exceeds its longer cutoff, and the optional longer-lag variance ratio is below its horizon cutoff; otherwise intermediate if absolute composite reaches its intermediate threshold, OU half-life is unavailable or exceeds its shorter cutoff, and the optional intermediate-lag variance ratio is below its horizon cutoff; otherwise short if absolute composite reaches its short threshold and the optional shorter-lag variance ratio is below its horizon cutoff. The long threshold exceeds the intermediate threshold, which exceeds the short threshold. In confluence mode, select a horizon from the confluence count when it reaches the minimum, with higher counts selecting longer horizons. In strict mode, select the long horizon when strict agreement holds. Otherwise select no horizon.
Calculation: Required edge = round-trip cost multiplied by the edge multiplier and a horizon multiplier that increases from short to intermediate to long. Expected edge = abs(close - OU mean) / close, expressed in the same units as cost.
Long: Composite > 0 AND tentative horizon exists AND (cost filter disabled OR expected edge > required edge) AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is at least the configured offset after session open AND at least the configured offset before session close AND (lunch exclusion disabled OR bar hour differs from the excluded hour)) AND (session guards disabled OR session bar count >= session warmup bars).
Short: Composite < 0 AND tentative horizon exists AND (cost filter disabled OR expected edge > required edge) AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is at least the configured offset after session open AND at least the configured offset before session close AND (lunch exclusion disabled OR bar hour differs from the excluded hour)) AND (session guards disabled OR session bar count >= session warmup bars).
