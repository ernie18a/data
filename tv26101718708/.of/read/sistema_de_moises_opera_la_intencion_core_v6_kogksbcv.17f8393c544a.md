Calculation: A confirmed swing high or low is the high or low of a bar with the specified number of bars on each side; it becomes available after the right-hand bars close. Use the latest confirmed swing of each kind.
Calculation: Upper levels are the latest confirmed swing high and the highs of the last completed bars of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other. Lower levels are the corresponding swing low and completed higher-timeframe lows.
Calculation: An upper sweep occurs when high exceeds any available upper level and close is below that same level. A lower sweep occurs when low is below any available lower level and close is above that same level. A sweep is recent when its latest occurrence is within the sweep lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish intent occurs when close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high OR when close > open AND the absolute candle body divided by high minus low is at least the body threshold AND high minus low is at least an ATR multiple AND close exceeds the previous high. Bearish intent occurs when close is below the latest confirmed swing low OR when close < open AND the absolute candle body divided by high minus low is at least the body threshold AND high minus low is at least the same ATR multiple AND close is below the previous low. Use a minimum price increment as the minimum candle range. Intent is recent when its latest occurrence is within the intent lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish post-sweep intent means a recent lower sweep AND recent bullish intent AND the latest bullish intent occurred after the latest lower sweep. Bearish post-sweep intent means a recent upper sweep AND recent bearish intent AND the latest bearish intent occurred after the latest upper sweep.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish context means close > short-period EMA AND short-period EMA > long-period EMA on a higher timeframe. Bearish context reverses both comparisons. Calculate this separately on a primary higher timeframe and a longer macro higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Range position is (close minus the lowest low) divided by (highest high minus lowest low) over the range lookback including the current bar, with a minimum price increment as the denominator floor. Discount means range position <= a lower threshold; premium means range position >= a higher threshold; middle means neither.
Calculation: An upper target exists when close is below any available upper level; a lower target exists when close is above any available lower level. An upper or lower level is near when the absolute distance from close to any available level on that side is at most an ATR multiple.
Calculation: The long score adds a primary-context weight for bullish primary context, a smaller macro-context weight for bullish macro context, a sweep weight for a recent lower sweep, a target weight for an upper target, a post-sweep weight for bullish post-sweep intent or otherwise an intent weight for recent bullish intent, a location weight for discount or otherwise a smaller middle-location weight for middle AND bullish primary context, and a proximity weight for a near lower level. The short score mirrors these conditions. Sweep and post-sweep weights are equal and greater than the equal primary-context, target, intent, and location weights; those are greater than the equal macro-context, middle-location, and proximity weights.
Long: Bullish primary higher-timeframe context.
Short: Bearish primary higher-timeframe context.
Long: Recent lower sweep.
Short: Recent upper sweep AND no recent lower sweep.
Long: Bullish post-sweep intent OR no bullish post-sweep intent AND no bearish post-sweep intent AND recent bullish intent AND no recent bearish intent.
Short: Bearish post-sweep intent AND no bullish post-sweep intent OR no bullish post-sweep intent AND no bearish post-sweep intent AND recent bearish intent AND no recent bullish intent.
Long: Range position <= lower discount threshold.
Short: Range position >= higher premium threshold.
Long: Long score exceeds short score by more than the mixed-bias threshold.
Short: Short score exceeds long score by more than the mixed-bias threshold.
