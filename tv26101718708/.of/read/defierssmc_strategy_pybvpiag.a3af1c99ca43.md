Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot whose high or low is extreme over equal configurable numbers of preceding and subsequent chart bars; recognize it only after the subsequent bars close. Retain a configurable number of pivots. The latest higher low is the most recent retained swing low greater than the preceding retained swing low; the latest lower high is defined symmetrically.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral; on each completed chart bar, calculate all breaks using the previous state and the latest confirmed pivots. An upward break occurs when close > latest swing high AND previous state <= neutral, or previous state is bearish AND close > latest lower high. A downward break occurs when close < latest swing low AND previous state >= neutral, or previous state is bullish AND close < latest higher low. Set state bullish on an upward break and bearish on a downward break; process the upward high break, downward low break, upward lower-high break, and downward higher-low break in that order. Record the latest break bar.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = for each newly confirmed swing, compare it with earlier retained swings of the same kind in chronological order. At the first swing within the greater of a configurable percentage of current close and a configurable multiple of current ATR, create a level at their average unless a same-kind level already lies within that tolerance. Retain a configurable maximum number of levels, deleting the oldest first. A high level is swept when high > level AND close < level; a low level is swept when low < level AND close > level. A swept level cannot be swept again. Record the direction and bar of the last sweep, processing levels from newest to oldest.
Calculation: Directional order block = after an upward high break, select the nearest preceding bearish candle within a configurable lookback whose volume / its volume SMA is at least the detection threshold; its high and low bound a bullish block. Define bearish blocks symmetrically after a downward low break. Store the volume ratio; expire blocks after a configurable age measured from the selected candle and retain a configurable maximum number, deleting the oldest first. Mark a block mitigated when a completed bar closes inside its bounds, including on its creation bar. Detection and retention depend on order-block detection being enabled.
Calculation: Directional fair value gap = a bullish gap has current low > high two bars ago AND gap size >= a configurable multiple of current ATR; its bounds are those two prices. A bearish gap is symmetric. Expire gaps after a configurable age measured from the middle candle and retain a configurable maximum number, deleting the oldest first. Track maximum penetration of each gap by subsequent bar ranges, including the creation bar; it is fully filled when penetration reaches the far edge, or when an overlapping bar closes beyond that edge. Gap detection depends on being enabled.
Calculation: Retracement zone = the interval between two configurable retracement fractions of the range bounded by the latest confirmed swing high and low, measured down from the range top when structure is neutral or bullish and up from the range bottom when bearish.
Calculation: Directional point-of-interest strength = the largest applicable strength: full strength if the bar range overlaps an unmitigated same-direction order block whose stored volume ratio meets the effective volume threshold; intermediate strength if it overlaps a same-direction gap that remains less than fully filled after the current bar's update; lower strength if it overlaps the retracement zone; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Volatility adjustment = compare ATR / SMA of ATR with configurable upper and lower thresholds; use a positive adjustment above the upper threshold, a negative adjustment below the lower threshold, and zero otherwise. If adjustment is disabled, use zero. Multiply the order-block volume threshold and gap-size threshold by one plus this adjustment, and add a configurable multiple of the adjustment to the entry-score threshold, rounding the result. When the parallel tuner is enabled, use its lower detection volume threshold to create blocks, but use the adjusted volume threshold to score them.
Calculation: Directional factors = higher-timeframe factor is one when the last completed higher-timeframe close is respectively above or below its EMA; structure factor is one when structure state matches the direction AND the latest break is within a configurable number of chart bars; sweep factor is one when the latest sweep is respectively a low-level or high-level sweep AND is within a configurable number of chart bars; point-of-interest factor is the directional point-of-interest strength; range factor is one when close is respectively <= or >= the midpoint of the latest swing-high and swing-low prices. Otherwise each factor is zero.
Calculation: Directional score = a configurable weighted sum of the five directional factors. Optionally reduce the higher-timeframe factor's weight outside configurable sessions identified using datetime and a configurable timezone. Active-factor count is the number of nonzero factors, counting the point-of-interest factor when it reaches its lower strength.
Calculation: Entry risk and targets = at the signal bar, the long initial stop is the latest swing low minus an ATR buffer, or close minus a configurable ATR multiple if no swing low exists; the short stop is symmetric. Risk distance is the greater of the directional close-to-stop distance and a configurable ATR floor. First target is close plus or minus a configurable risk multiple. Second target is the nearest unswept opposite liquidity level beyond the first target if liquidity targeting is enabled and such a level exists; otherwise it is close plus or minus a separate configurable risk multiple. Potential reward-to-risk is the directional distance from close to the second target divided by risk distance.
Calculation: Managed stop = initialize at the entry bar's initial stop. If early break-even is enabled and the first target has not been filled, move the stop to entry when the current bar's favorable extreme reaches a configurable multiple of the remaining entry-to-stop distance and that distance is positive. After a partial first-target fill, move the stop to entry regardless of the trailing setting. If trailing is enabled after that fill, raise the long stop to the latest higher low minus the current ATR buffer when higher than the existing stop; lower the short stop symmetrically using the latest lower high plus the buffer. Preserve the first and second target prices fixed at entry.
Long: Close > latest swing high AND previous structure state <= neutral
Long: Close > latest lower high AND previous structure state = bearish
Short: Close < latest swing low AND previous structure state >= neutral
Short: Close < latest higher low AND previous structure state = bullish
Long: Low < an unswept low liquidity level AND close > that level
Short: High > an unswept high liquidity level AND close < that level
Long: An upward high break occurs AND a qualifying bullish order block is found
Short: A downward low break occurs AND a qualifying bearish order block is found
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND gap size >= adjusted ATR gap threshold
Short: High < low two bars ago AND gap size >= adjusted ATR gap threshold
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA
Long: Shorter-period EMA > intermediate-period EMA AND intermediate-period EMA > longer-period EMA, calculated on each dashboard timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar
Short: Shorter-period EMA < intermediate-period EMA AND intermediate-period EMA < longer-period EMA, calculated on each dashboard timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar
Long: Long score > short score
Short: Short score > long score
Long: Position is flat AND long active-factor count >= preview threshold AND higher-timeframe bearish bias is absent when the bias filter is enabled AND long potential reward-to-risk >= first-target risk multiple
Short: Position is flat AND long preview condition is false AND short active-factor count >= preview threshold AND higher-timeframe bullish bias is absent when the bias filter is enabled AND short potential reward-to-risk >= first-target risk multiple

Short: Position is flat AND short score >= adjusted entry threshold AND short active-factor count >= required count AND every enabled required factor is active AND higher-timeframe bullish bias is absent when the bias filter is enabled AND datetime is inside an allowed session when the hard session filter is enabled AND short potential reward-to-risk >= first-target risk multiple AND short risk distance <= configurable maximum fraction of close
Exit: long, low <= managed stop OR high >= first target before its partial fill OR high >= second target
Exit: short, high >= managed stop OR low <= first target before its partial fill OR low <= second target
