Calculation: Smoothed MFI = Money Flow Index of a configurable source and lookback, optionally smoothed by SMA, EMA, RMA, or WMA over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = Relative Strength Index of a configurable source and lookback, optionally smoothed by SMA, EMA, RMA, or WMA over a configurable lookback.
Long: Smoothed MFI <= its oversold threshold OR Smoothed RSI <= its oversold threshold
Short: Smoothed MFI >= its overbought threshold OR Smoothed RSI >= its overbought threshold
