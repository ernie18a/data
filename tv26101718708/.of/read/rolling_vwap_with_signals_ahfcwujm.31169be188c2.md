Calculation: Rolling VWAP and deviation = On the selected calculation timeframe, take HLC3 and volume over the trailing time window, including at least a minimum number of bars. The rolling VWAP is sum(HLC3 × volume) / sum(volume); deviation is sqrt(sum(volume × (HLC3 − rolling VWAP)²) / sum(volume)). Optionally smooth the VWAP and deviation separately with the same selectable moving-average type and length. On a higher timeframe, historical chart bars use the last completed calculation bar and the realtime chart bar uses the still-forming calculation bar; on a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar in each chart bar; on the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: Upper band = rolling VWAP + multiplier × deviation; lower band = rolling VWAP − multiplier × deviation.
Calculation: Trend comparison lag = the calculation-timeframe duration divided by the chart-timeframe duration, rounded to the nearest integer and clamped to at least one chart bar.
Calculation: Relative-volume baseline = mean chart-bar volume over a trailing time window, including at least a minimum number of bars.
Calculation: Directional volume means = separately average volume for chart bars with close > open and close < open over the preceding flow lookback, excluding the current bar and requiring a minimum number of qualifying bars for each mean.
Calculation: Band-width ratio = (upper band − lower band) / rolling VWAP; its baseline is the mean band-width ratio over the same trailing time window and minimum bar count as the relative-volume baseline.
Long: rolling VWAP > rolling VWAP at the trend comparison lag AND close > upper band.
Short: rolling VWAP < rolling VWAP at the trend comparison lag AND close < lower band.
Long: close > upper band AND previous close <= previous upper band AND (narrow-band filter disabled OR previous band-width ratio <= multiplier × previous band-width baseline) AND (ATR filter disabled OR current SWMA-smoothed ATR > previous SWMA-smoothed ATR) AND (trend filter disabled OR rolling VWAP > rolling VWAP at the trend comparison lag AND close > upper band) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > multiplier × relative-volume baseline) AND (directional-volume filter disabled OR preceding up-bar volume mean > preceding down-bar volume mean).
Short: close < lower band AND previous close >= previous lower band AND (narrow-band filter disabled OR previous band-width ratio <= multiplier × previous band-width baseline) AND (ATR filter disabled OR current SWMA-smoothed ATR > previous SWMA-smoothed ATR) AND (trend filter disabled OR rolling VWAP < rolling VWAP at the trend comparison lag AND close < lower band) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > multiplier × relative-volume baseline) AND (directional-volume filter disabled OR preceding down-bar volume mean > preceding up-bar volume mean).
