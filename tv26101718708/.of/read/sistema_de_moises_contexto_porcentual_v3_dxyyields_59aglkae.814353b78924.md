Calculation: Bull and bear scores = add the listed points for each true condition, then clamp each total to 0–100. Macro (higher than chart timeframe; use the still-forming bar): bull +6 close > fast EMA, +6 fast EMA > slow EMA, +5 normalized fast-EMA slope > 0.25 (or +3 if > 0), +5 bullish swing break, +4 bullish fair value gap, +4 close above latest confirmed swing high; bear uses the mirrored conditions and negative slope thresholds (−0.25, 0) with +6, +6, +5, +5, +4, +4 points.
Calculation: HTF score contribution = on the higher-than-chart HTF still-forming bar, bull +5 close > fast EMA, +5 fast EMA > slow EMA, +4 normalized fast-EMA slope > 0.20 (or +2 if > 0), +5 bullish swing break, +3 bullish impulse, +3 bullish fair value gap; bear uses mirrored conditions and negative slope thresholds (−0.20, 0) with the same points.
Calculation: Intraday score contribution = on the configured intraday bar, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar: bull +4 close > fast EMA, +4 fast EMA > slow EMA, +5 bullish swing break, +4 bullish impulse, +4 bullish fair value gap, +4 exploring higher; bear uses mirrored conditions and points.
Calculation: Normalized fast-EMA slope = (fast EMA − its value five bars earlier) / ATR. Swing break = close above the latest confirmed pivot high (bullish) or below the latest confirmed pivot low (bearish). Impulse = candle direction matches the side, body/range ≥ minimum body fraction, range ≥ ATR × impulse threshold, and close exceeds the previous bar's high (bullish) or falls below its low (bearish). Fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier by more than ATR × gap threshold (bullish), or current high < low two bars earlier by more than ATR × gap threshold (bearish).
Calculation: Liquidity/intention score contribution = bull +6 recent sweep of a pivot/prior-day/prior-week low, +6 bullish intent after that sweep, +4 close below any available pivot/prior-day/prior-week high, +2 bullish day bias, +2 current-day sweep of prior-day low; bear mirrors these conditions at highs/lows. Subtract 6 from bull for recent bearish imbalance and 6 from bear for recent bullish imbalance. Recent means within the configured memory bars. Intent is impulse or swing break or fair value gap; imbalance is impulse or fair value gap. Intent after sweep means the latest such intent is no older than the latest sweep and both are within memory. Bullish day bias = current day open above prior-day midpoint and current-day high ≤ prior-day high; bearish is the mirror. Day sweep means current-day high/low exceeds prior-day high/low.
Calculation: External score contribution = when external pressure is enabled, bull +4 for each of DXY, US10Y, and US02Y with close < its EMA and ROC < 0; bear +4 for each with close > its EMA and ROC > 0. These use the configured external timeframe's still-forming bars, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish percentage = round(100 × bull score / max(bull score + bear score, 1)); bearish percentage is the same formula using bear score.
Long: bullish percentage > bearish percentage AND their difference ≥ 12
Short: bearish percentage > bullish percentage AND their difference ≥ 12
