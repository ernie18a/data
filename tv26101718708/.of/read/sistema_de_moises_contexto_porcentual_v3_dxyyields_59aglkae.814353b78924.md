Calculation: Timeframe values = Evaluate each calculation on its configured aggregated timeframe. Relative to the chart timeframe, that timeframe may be higher or lower. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last available lower-timeframe bar; on a live chart bar, higher-timeframe values may use the bar still forming. Use the same short EMA length and long EMA length across the three analysis timeframes, with the macro timeframe longer than the higher timeframe, and the higher timeframe longer than the intraday timeframe.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = A high or low exceeding the corresponding highs or lows on both sides over equal pivot lookbacks; use the latest pivot only after its right-hand lookback has completed.
Calculation: Bullish impulse = close > open AND absolute candle body / candle range >= minimum body fraction AND candle range >= ATR multiplied by an impulse threshold AND close > previous high. Bearish impulse uses close < open and close < previous low with the same body and range tests.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high from two bars earlier AND their difference > ATR multiplied by a gap threshold. Bearish gap = high < low from two bars earlier AND their difference > ATR multiplied by the same gap threshold.
Calculation: Bullish break = close > latest confirmed pivot high. Bearish break = close < latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: High sweep = high > any of the latest confirmed pivot high, previous day high, or previous week high AND close < that same level. Low sweep = low < any of the latest confirmed pivot low, previous day low, or previous week low AND close > that same level. Recent means the event occurred within the configured memory lookback, including the current intraday bar.
Calculation: Bullish intent = bullish impulse OR bullish break OR bullish gap. Bearish intent = bearish impulse OR bearish break OR bearish gap. Bullish imbalance = bullish impulse OR bullish gap. Bearish imbalance = bearish impulse OR bearish gap.
Calculation: Exploring higher = (bullish break OR bullish impulse) AND recent bullish imbalance AND no recent high sweep. Exploring lower = (bearish break OR bearish impulse) AND recent bearish imbalance AND no recent low sweep. Evaluate these on the intraday timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish after sweep = recent low sweep AND recent bullish intent AND the latest bullish intent occurred on or after the latest low sweep. Bearish after sweep = recent high sweep AND recent bearish intent AND the latest bearish intent occurred on or after the latest high sweep.
Calculation: Bullish day bias = current day open > midpoint of previous day high and low AND current day high so far <= previous day high. Bearish day bias = current day open < that midpoint AND current day low so far >= previous day low. Compute day values from intraday bars.
Calculation: Bull and bear context scores = With external-data pressure disabled, sum configurable weights for each true condition below, subtract the specified imbalance penalty, and clamp each score to a configurable lower and upper bound. Use separate weights for the listed contributions, including both macro pivot-break contributions. Bull conditions: macro close > short EMA, macro short EMA > long EMA, positive macro short-EMA change over a lookback divided by ATR, that normalized change > a positive macro threshold, macro bullish break twice, macro bullish gap; higher-timeframe close > short EMA, short EMA > long EMA, positive normalized short-EMA change, that change > a positive higher-timeframe threshold, bullish break, bullish impulse, bullish gap; intraday close > short EMA, short EMA > long EMA, bullish break, bullish impulse, bullish gap, exploring higher; recent low sweep, bullish after sweep, close below at least one of the latest pivot high, previous day high or previous week high, bullish day bias, current day high so far > previous day high. Subtract a weight when bearish imbalance is recent. For each normalized-change pair, award the threshold weight when the threshold is exceeded, otherwise award the positive-change weight when the change is positive. Bear conditions mirror all price comparisons and bullish events: use negative normalized changes and negative thresholds, bearish breaks, impulses and gaps, exploring lower, recent high sweep, bearish after sweep, close above at least one of the latest pivot low, previous day low or previous week low, bearish day bias, and current day low so far < previous day low; subtract a weight when bullish imbalance is recent. Calculate normalized changes and their ATR on their respective timeframes, and all liquidity and day conditions on the intraday timeframe.
Calculation: Bull and bear percentages = Round each clamped score divided by the greater of their sum and a positive minimum denominator, multiplied by a percentage scale. The score-based decisions apply when external-data pressure is disabled.
Calculation: Range position = (intraday close - lowest intraday low over a lookback) / (highest intraday high over the same lookback - lowest intraday low over that lookback).
Long: Macro close > macro short EMA
Short: Macro close < macro short EMA
Long: Higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe short EMA
Short: Higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe short EMA
Long: NOT exploring lower AND (exploring higher OR intraday close > intraday short EMA)
Short: exploring lower OR (NOT exploring higher AND intraday close < intraday short EMA)
Long: NOT recent high sweep AND recent low sweep
Short: NOT recent low sweep AND recent high sweep
Long: recent bullish imbalance AND NOT recent bearish imbalance
Short: recent bearish imbalance AND NOT recent bullish imbalance
Long: range position <= discount threshold
Short: range position >= premium threshold
Long: bull percentage > bear percentage
Short: bear percentage > bull percentage
