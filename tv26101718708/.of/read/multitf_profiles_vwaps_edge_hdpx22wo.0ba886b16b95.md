Calculation: Index and direction = index 0 is the current chart bar and index k is k bars ago. Direction d is +1 for long and -1 for short. For a directional price x, use d*x; directional high is high for long and -low for short; directional low is low for long and -high for short. Apply every directional comparison and calculation independently for each side.
Calculation: Volume profile = for each chart bar in a higher-timeframe period, round its low and high to a configurable price-bin grid and distribute its volume equally among every bin between them, inclusive; if there is at most one bin, assign all volume to the bin containing typical price. POC is the lowest-price bin tied for maximum volume. Starting at POC, add the larger-volume adjacent occupied bin, choosing the upper bin on a tie, until a configurable fraction of total volume is included; the highest and lowest included bin prices are VAH and VAL. Maintain profiles for three successively longer higher timeframes, including configurable numbers of completed periods. A completed profile is computed at period rollover. In the source's historical execution, a forming profile has no computed levels until the last chart bar; on that bar it is computed only when its profile display is enabled, or, for the two shorter periods, when the bias display is enabled. The shortest and middle forming profiles are also computed by the bias display after cluster construction. Use current-period levels as they exist on the decision bar even when testing a previous bar.
Calculation: Period VWAP and deviation = volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of chart-bar typical price, reset at the start of each of two successively longer higher-timeframe periods or at the start of a configurable intraday session. Use the still-forming period through the current chart bar; the session calculation is unavailable outside its session.
Calculation: Initial balance = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in a configurable intraday window, available after the window ends and retained until the next window begins.
Calculation: Level set = POC, VAH and VAL of the forming profiles of the three higher timeframes, their retained completed profiles when their respective prior-profile and profile-display settings are enabled, enabled period and session VWAPs, and enabled completed initial-balance levels. A bar touches a level when its high is at least the level minus a configurable tolerance AND its low is at most the level plus that tolerance. Current profile levels are eligible irrespective of profile-display settings when available. Early higher-timeframe-zone testing instead includes all retained current and completed profile levels irrespective of those display settings. A level cluster is a run of sorted profile levels whose successive gaps do not exceed a configurable width and which contains at least a configurable minimum number of levels; cluster membership means close lies between its endpoints. In historical execution clusters are constructed only on the last chart bar, before the bias display recomputes profiles.
Calculation: Level priority = the highest applicable priority for the tested bar: highest for an enabled VWAP or the shortest forming profile's POC; intermediate for that profile's VAH or VAL, the middle forming profile's POC, or an enabled completed initial-balance level; lowest for any other touched level; zero if none. Test touches against levels available on the decision bar.
Calculation: Range-weighted cumulative volume delta = accumulate volume times ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), using zero for zero-range bars, and reset at each shortest higher-timeframe period. Delta direction is the sign of the difference between the current cumulative value and its value a configurable number of chart bars earlier, substituting zero for a missing earlier value.
Calculation: Prior-value bias = for the most recently completed shortest higher-timeframe profile, assign successively positive scores when close is above POC and above VAH, successively negative scores when close is below POC and below VAL, and zero at POC or when the profile is unavailable; evaluate the outside-value comparisons first. Directional bias is positive when this score agrees with d, or when it is zero and cumulative-volume-delta direction agrees with d.
Calculation: Directional context = let P, U and L be the forming shortest-period POC, VAH and VAL; let W be the forming middle-period VAH for long or VAL for short. Acceptance means directional close is beyond directional U for long or directional L for short by a configurable fraction of the level tolerance AND the previous directional close is beyond that boundary minus the tolerance. Prior outside value means each of the two preceding directional closes is below directional L for long or below directional U for short. Failed breakout means current directional close is above directional L for long or below directional U for short AND either of the two preceding directional closes was on the opposite side of that same boundary. A sweep means the previous low is below any of the forming shortest-period VAL or POC or middle-period VAL by a configurable fraction of the tolerance for long, or the previous high is above the corresponding VAH or POC levels by that fraction for short. A commit means d*(close-open) > 0 AND body/range meets a configurable minimum AND directional close exceeds the previous directional high.
Calculation: Directional bias score = sum configurable weights for prior-value bias aligned with the direction, using a larger weight outside value than inside value; aligned cumulative-volume-delta direction; directional close beyond W; directional close beyond P by a configurable tolerance fraction; failed breakout; and acceptance. Calculate the opposing score independently. Countertrend means the opposing score exceeds the directional score by a configurable margin AND meets a configurable minimum.
Calculation: Pattern scores = each score is zero unless its stated prerequisite is true; otherwise start with its configurable base weight and add the stated configurable weights for true conditions. A pattern qualifies only when its score reaches its configurable minimum; the breakout and continuation minima are at least one common floor, and the reversal, mean-reversion, failed-breakout and sweep minima are at least a higher common floor. The selected pattern is the enabled qualifying pattern with the highest score, breaking ties in this order: breakout, continuation, reversal, mean reversion, failed breakout, sweep, candle displacement, gap reaction, acceptance, rejection.
Calculation: Breakout score = prerequisite directional close beyond the forming shortest-period value boundary in the trade direction by a configurable tolerance fraction; add weights for acceptance, body/range above a threshold, body/ATR above a threshold, volume/SMA(volume) above a threshold, aligned delta direction, and directional close beyond W; subtract a weight for opposing directional bias.
Calculation: Continuation score = prerequisite directional bias AND previous-bar level touch; add weights for commit, otherwise a smaller weight for a directional candle closing sufficiently far toward its directional high; sweep; close within a configurable tolerance distance inside the forming shortest-period value boundary opposite the trade direction; non-opposing delta; volume/SMA(volume) above a threshold; membership in a configurable intraday time window; and cluster membership.
Calculation: Reversal score = prerequisite prior outside value AND previous-bar level touch; add weights for sweep, commit, directional close back inside the opposite value boundary, aligned delta, membership in a configurable intraday time window, and cluster membership; subtract a weight for opposing directional bias.
Calculation: Mean-reversion score = prerequisite directional close sufficiently far below directional P in ATR units OR sufficiently far below directional shortest-period VWAP in its weighted standard-deviation units; add weights for a directional candle closing sufficiently far toward its directional high, commit, volume/SMA(volume) above a threshold, aligned delta, and a more extreme VWAP deviation.
Calculation: Failed-breakout score = prerequisite failed breakout; add weights for sweep, commit, body/range above a threshold, volume/SMA(volume) above a threshold, aligned delta, and membership in a configurable intraday time window.
Calculation: Sweep score = prerequisite sweep AND previous-bar level touch; add weights for commit, otherwise a smaller weight for a directional candle closing sufficiently far toward its directional high, non-opposing delta, and volume/SMA(volume) above a threshold.
Calculation: Candle-displacement score = prerequisite previous candle against the trade direction AND directional close beyond its directional midpoint; use a higher base weight if directional close also exceeds the previous directional open; add weights for previous-bar level touch and aligned delta.
Calculation: Gap-reaction score = retain the most recent same-direction three-bar fair-value gap, replacing it whenever directional low exceeds directional high two bars earlier. Its lower and upper directional boundaries are that earlier directional high and the current directional low. Prerequisite: gap age is within a configurable maximum AND the previous bar's entire range lies inside the retained gap. Add weights for directional close above the upper boundary and aligned delta.
Calculation: Acceptance score = prerequisite acceptance; add weights for the directional close two bars earlier being beyond the relevant value boundary minus the tolerance, and for aligned delta.
Calculation: Rejection score = prerequisite previous-bar level touch AND previous directional close's distance above previous directional low divided by previous range meets a configurable minimum AND the current candle points in the trade direction; add weights for current directional close's position in its range meeting a threshold and for aligned delta.
Calculation: Confirmation count = sum one for each of: qualifying sweep pattern with sweep; commit; candle displacement meeting its midpoint prerequisite, regardless of its pattern score; qualifying gap-reaction pattern; qualifying acceptance pattern; qualifying rejection pattern; and failed breakout.
Calculation: Candidate grade = a weighted sum of directional bias score, previous-bar level priority, confirmation count, and structural-stop quality, each capped at its configurable component maximum; stop quality is maximal at or below a preferred stop distance, decreases linearly to zero at the maximum distance, and is zero outside the valid distance range. Convert the sum into ordered grade bands. Required confirmations are the maximum of a configurable minimum and the minimum associated with the candidate's grade band, plus an additional configurable count when countertrend.
Calculation: Structural stop distance = directional close minus the lowest directional low among a configurable number of preceding chart bars. Entry extension is the positive part of directional close minus the directional forming shortest-period opposite value boundary when a sweep occurred and that boundary exists, otherwise minus the previous directional high. Reward/risk is the distance from close to the nearest eligible level in the trade direction beyond a configurable price increment, divided by structural stop distance. Eligible reward levels are enabled VWAPs, all retained current and completed profiles of the two shorter higher timeframes irrespective of their display settings, and enabled completed initial-balance levels.
Calculation: Valid location = previous-bar level touch OR sweep OR close within a configurable ATR distance of the shortest-period VWAP OR directional close beyond the forming shortest-period value boundary opposite the trade direction OR directional close sufficiently far below directional P in ATR units. Middle of value means close is inside the forming shortest-period value area AND farther than a configurable ATR distance from its VAH, VAL and POC AND the previous bar touched no level.
Calculation: Confirmed-signal latch = keep a separate latch and last-signal bar for each direction; clear both directional latches when neither the current nor previous bar touches any level, using the decision bar's levels. Set a direction's latch and update its last-signal bar when its confirmed signal fires.
Calculation: Early grade = sum configurable weights for current-bar level priority, direction-adjusted prior-value bias, volume/SMA(volume) band, direction-adjusted cumulative-volume-delta direction, membership in a configurable intraday time window, and cluster membership. The volume contribution is positive above its upper threshold, negative below its lower threshold, and zero otherwise. Early latches are separate by direction and from confirmed latches; reset them using the same two-bar level-touch rule and set the corresponding latch when an early signal fires.
Long: The selected long pattern exists AND its score meets the configurable setup-score threshold AND long confirmation count meets its required count AND the enabled valid-location and minimum-level-priority filters pass AND the enabled middle-of-value filter passes or the selected pattern is mean reversion AND the long confirmed latch is clear AND the elapsed bars since the previous confirmed long signal meet the cooldown AND structural stop distance is positive and at most its maximum AND entry extension is at most its maximum AND the enabled reward/risk filter meets its minimum.
Short: The selected short pattern exists AND its score meets the configurable setup-score threshold AND short confirmation count meets its required count AND the enabled valid-location and minimum-level-priority filters pass AND the enabled middle-of-value filter passes or the selected pattern is mean reversion AND the short confirmed latch is clear AND the elapsed bars since the previous confirmed short signal meet the cooldown AND structural stop distance is positive and at most its maximum AND entry extension is at most its maximum AND the enabled reward/risk filter meets its minimum.
Long: The current bar touches an eligible early zone AND (close-low)/(high-low) meets a configurable minimum, substituting the neutral fraction for zero range AND the enabled sweep, midpoint-reclaim and directional-body/ATR filters pass AND the configured sensitivity's prior-value-bias, delta-direction and intraday-window requirements pass AND early grade meets the configured minimum AND the early long latch is clear AND the confirmed long signal is false. Aggressive sensitivity imposes no bias or delta requirement; normal requires nonnegative prior-value bias and delta direction; conservative requires positive prior-value bias, nonnegative delta direction and membership in the configured intraday window.
Short: The current bar touches an eligible early zone AND (high-close)/(high-low) meets a configurable minimum, substituting the neutral fraction for zero range AND the enabled sweep, midpoint-reclaim and directional-body/ATR filters pass AND the configured sensitivity's prior-value-bias, delta-direction and intraday-window requirements pass AND early grade meets the configured minimum AND the early short latch is clear AND the confirmed short signal is false. Aggressive sensitivity imposes no bias or delta requirement; normal requires nonpositive prior-value bias and delta direction; conservative requires negative prior-value bias, nonpositive delta direction and membership in the configured intraday window.
