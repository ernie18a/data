Calculation: Source bars = bars on a configurable timeframe equal to, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe. For a different timeframe, use the last completed source bar; on a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar. Evaluate zone creation only when the returned source-bar time changes. Let B0 be the returned bar, B1 the preceding source bar, and B2 the bar before B1.
Calculation: Zones = retain bullish and bearish FVG and VI zones separately for each source timeframe and type, removing the oldest zone of that type and timeframe when the configurable capacity is exceeded. A bullish FVG has top B0.low and bottom B2.high; a bearish FVG has top B2.low and bottom B0.high. A bullish VI has top B0.open and bottom B1.close; a bearish VI has top B1.close and bottom B0.open. Track first touch and first fill independently for each retained zone, including on its creation bar. For FVG rebalancing, after evaluating touch and fill on each chart bar, reduce an unmitigated bullish zone's top to the chart low when bottom < low < top, or raise an unmitigated bearish zone's bottom to the chart high when bottom < high < top; mark it mitigated when low <= bottom for bullish zones or high >= top for bearish zones. Alternatively, mark zones mitigated on close < bottom for bullish zones or close > top for bearish zones, or on low < top for bullish zones or high > bottom for bearish zones. Mitigated zones remain eligible for first-touch and first-fill events until removed by the capacity limit.
Long: Source-bar time changes AND B0.low > B2.high AND B1.low <= B2.high AND B1.high >= B0.low
Long: Source-bar time changes AND B0.open > B1.close AND B0.low <= B1.high AND B0.close > B0.open AND B1.close > B1.open
Long: Chart low <= retained bullish zone top AND that zone has not previously been touched
Long: Chart low <= retained bullish zone bottom AND that zone has not previously been filled
Short: Source-bar time changes AND B0.high < B2.low AND B0.high >= B1.low AND B1.high >= B2.low
Short: Source-bar time changes AND B0.open < B1.close AND B0.high >= B1.low AND B0.close < B0.open AND B1.close < B1.open
Short: Chart high >= retained bearish zone bottom AND that zone has not previously been touched
Short: Chart high >= retained bearish zone top AND that zone has not previously been filled
