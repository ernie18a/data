Calculation: MACD oscillator = 10000 × (shorter EMA(close) − longer EMA(close)) / longer EMA(close); for volume-weighted MACD, replace each EMA(close) with EMA(volume × close) / EMA(volume), using the same length in each ratio. Its basis is an EMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Directional range momentum = EMA(50 + 50 × RMA(force) / RMA(abs(force))); use 50 before the EMA when the denominator is zero. Force is the current rolling highest close minus rolling lowest close if the rolling highest close increased; otherwise, its negative if the rolling lowest close decreased; otherwise, close minus previous close. Both RMAs use the range lookback.
Calculation: Non-MACD oscillator = EMA of RSI(close), MFI(close), CCI(typical price), or directional range momentum; alternatively, SMA of stochastic %K of price or stochastic %K of RSI(close). For directional range momentum, do not apply an additional EMA. Its basis is an EMA of the oscillator, except for either stochastic oscillator, whose basis is an SMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Oscillator midpoint = 0 for MACD, volume-weighted MACD, or CCI; otherwise 50. Oversold = 2 × midpoint − overbought; extreme oversold = oversold − extreme offset; extreme overbought = overbought + extreme offset.
Calculation: Trend = session VWAP of close when VWAP trend is selected; otherwise EMA(close).
Calculation: A cross above requires the current first value > the current second value AND the previous first value <= the previous second value; a cross below requires the current first value < the current second value AND the previous first value >= the previous second value.
Calculation: Fast cooldown is shorter than slow cooldown. For each direction independently, a fast signal requires at least the fast cooldown bars since the last fast signal, and resets both fast and slow timers; a slow signal requires at least the slow cooldown bars since the last fast or slow signal. An uninitialized timer permits its signal. Evaluate these timers on every bar, including when the trend setting is off.
Calculation: A confirmed oscillator pivot low or high is an oscillator minimum or maximum over a window with equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot; it becomes known only after the bars following the pivot. Compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind, and use the price low or high at each oscillator pivot.
Long: MACD or volume-weighted MACD crosses above its basis AND typical price > trend AND (current MACD oscillator < 0 OR previous MACD oscillator < 0).
Short: MACD or volume-weighted MACD crosses below its basis AND typical price < trend AND (current MACD oscillator > 0 OR previous MACD oscillator > 0).
Long: A non-MACD oscillator is selected AND the trend setting is not off AND (the trend setting is range OR (high + low) / 2 > trend AND close > trend) AND (((oscillator crosses above its basis AND oscillator < oversold) OR oscillator crosses above extreme oversold) AND fast cooldown permits a signal OR oscillator crosses above oversold AND slow cooldown permits a signal).
Short: A non-MACD oscillator is selected AND the trend setting is not off AND (the trend setting is range OR (high + low) / 2 < trend AND close < trend) AND (((oscillator crosses below its basis AND oscillator > overbought) OR oscillator crosses below extreme overbought) AND fast cooldown permits a signal OR oscillator crosses below overbought AND slow cooldown permits a signal).
Long: Divergence is enabled AND a confirmed oscillator pivot low > the previous oscillator pivot low AND price low at the current pivot < price low at the previous pivot.
Short: Divergence is enabled AND a confirmed oscillator pivot high < the previous oscillator pivot high AND price high at the current pivot > price high at the previous pivot.
Long: Alternate oscillator colors are enabled AND oscillator > midpoint.
Short: Alternate oscillator colors are enabled AND oscillator <= midpoint.
Long: MACD or volume-weighted MACD histogram > 0.
Short: MACD or volume-weighted MACD histogram <= 0.
Long: Overbought/oversold fill is enabled AND a non-MACD oscillator > overbought.
Short: Overbought/oversold fill is enabled AND a non-MACD oscillator < oversold.
