Calculation: Internal and swing pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively higher/lower than the highs/lows of N bars before and after it; confirm after the N bars. Internal N is shorter than swing N.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest pivot high / below the latest pivot low, with at least two opposite-side pivots recorded. It is BOS when the prior trend is not bearish / bullish, and CHoCH when it is. CHoCH+ additionally requires the latest opposite-side pivot to be higher / lower than the preceding one.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the price zone from the lowest-low / highest-high bar between the relevant pivot and structure break to that bar’s open or midpoint, with the selected positioning method applied. Keep it active until close falls below its lower boundary / rises above its upper boundary; the order-block filter may restrict creation to BOS, CHoCH, or CHoCH+.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap =, on the selected gap timeframe, a gap where the current low is above the high two bars earlier / current high is below the low two bars earlier. The indicator also supports its volume-imbalance and opening-gap definitions.
Calculation: Accumulation/distribution zone = alternating internal pivot lows and highs (fast: four pivots; slow: six) where successive lows rise and successive highs fall / successive highs fall and successive lows rise, respectively.
Long: bullish BOS OR bullish CHoCH OR bullish CHoCH+ OR bullish order-block creation OR current low crosses below the upper boundary of an active bullish order block OR bullish fair value gap forms OR accumulation zone forms
Short: bearish BOS OR bearish CHoCH OR bearish CHoCH+ OR bearish order-block creation OR current high crosses above the lower boundary of an active bearish order block OR bearish fair value gap forms OR distribution zone forms
