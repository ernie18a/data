Calculation: Pivot streams = Confirm a high or low pivot using equal left and right lookbacks. Maintain separate shorter-lookback and longer-lookback streams. For structure, process simultaneous confirmations in this priority: shorter high, shorter low, longer low, longer high. Store all confirmed pivot prices by side; separately retain the latest unbroken high and low, replacing each when a new pivot of that side confirms.
Calculation: Structure break = For either lookback, an upward break occurs when close crosses above the latest unbroken pivot high and at least two pivot lows have been confirmed; a downward break occurs when close crosses below the latest unbroken pivot low and at least two pivot highs have been confirmed. After a break, clear that side's unbroken pivot and set that lookback's trend to the break direction until its next break. Process upward before downward breaks. The longer lookback exceeds the shorter lookback. In dynamic mode, calculate the z-score of the shorter-lookback percentage change in close using the same lookback; when its absolute value reaches the first threshold, replace the shorter lookback with its corresponding fixed value, otherwise retain it.
Calculation: Structure trend = The direction of the most recent structure break for a given lookback, initially neutral. For the multi-timeframe trend markings, calculate the shorter-lookback structure on each selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Alternating pivot sequence = Add each confirmed shorter-lookback high or low pivot in confirmation order, adding both if both occur. Clear the sequence if its two newest pivots have the same type, and clear it after a qualifying accumulation or distribution pattern.
Calculation: Gap series = On the selected gap timeframe, which may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe, obtain open, high, low and close and the high and low two gap bars earlier. Higher-timeframe requests use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; lower-timeframe requests use the last available lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. A previous value of the obtained current-bar series means its value on the previous chart bar. Evaluate gap conditions only when a new selected-timeframe period begins, and emit the decision on the following chart bar.
Calculation: Order block = At a structure break, examine chart bars from the breaking bar back to but excluding the broken pivot bar. For a bullish block, its bottom is the minimum low in that interval and its top is selected as the high, OHLC average, or high-low midpoint of the bar one bar before the most recent minimum-low bar. For a bearish block, its top is the maximum high in the interval and its bottom is selected as the low, OHLC average, or high-low midpoint of the bar one bar before the most recent maximum-high bar. In the precise midpoint option, halve the bullish block from its top if its midpoint is below the lower of that selected bar's open and close and its top is above that bar's high-low-close-close average; halve the bearish block from its bottom if its midpoint is above the selected bearish bar's open when the selected bullish bar is bearish, otherwise above the selected bearish bar's close, and its bottom is below the selected bearish bar's high-low-close-close average. Optionally discard either the new or previous same-side block if the new bullish bottom is below the previous bullish top or the new bearish top is above the previous bearish bottom. Remove a bullish block when close is below its bottom or midpoint, and a bearish block when close is above its top or midpoint, according to the selected mitigation method. Keep separate internal and swing blocks.
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken shorter-lookback or longer-lookback pivot high AND at least two corresponding pivot lows have been confirmed.
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken shorter-lookback or longer-lookback pivot low AND at least two corresponding pivot highs have been confirmed.
Long: Shorter-lookback structure trend equals bullish on the chart timeframe or on any selected higher or lower timeframe.
Short: Shorter-lookback structure trend equals bearish on the chart timeframe or on any selected higher or lower timeframe.
Long: The newest alternating pivot sequence ends with a low AND its successive lows are strictly higher AND its successive highs are strictly lower, using either the shorter or longer selected sequence length.
Short: The newest alternating pivot sequence ends with a high AND its successive highs are strictly lower AND its successive lows are strictly higher, using either the shorter or longer selected sequence length.
Long: A newly confirmed low pivot and the preceding confirmed low pivot differ by less than a selected fraction of ATR; process a simultaneously confirmed high pivot instead of the low.
Short: A newly confirmed high pivot and the preceding confirmed high pivot differ by less than a selected fraction of ATR.
Long: On the previous chart bar, a new gap-timeframe period began AND the selected gap-timeframe low was above the high two gap bars earlier, OR the selected gap-timeframe low was above the previous chart bar's aligned gap-timeframe high, OR the aligned gap-timeframe open was above its previous chart-bar close AND the previous aligned high was above the aligned low AND the aligned close was above its previous value AND the aligned open was above its previous value AND the previous aligned high was below both aligned open and close.
Short: On the previous chart bar, a new gap-timeframe period began AND the selected gap-timeframe high was below the low two gap bars earlier, OR the selected gap-timeframe high was below the previous chart bar's aligned gap-timeframe low, OR the aligned gap-timeframe open was below its previous chart-bar close AND the previous aligned low was below the aligned high AND the aligned close was below its previous value AND the aligned open was below its previous value AND the previous aligned low was above both aligned open and close.
Long: Low crosses below the top of the latest surviving bullish order block.
Short: High crosses above the bottom of the latest surviving bearish order block.
