Calculation: Anchor reset = the first bar, or high > the highest high of the preceding structure lookback bars, or low < the lowest low of the preceding structure lookback bars.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume divided by cumulative volume, starting on the latest anchor-reset bar; undefined when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: ATR baseline = SMA of ATR over a longer lookback, or ATR when that SMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Band center = (high + low + close) / 3 on an anchor-reset bar; otherwise anchored VWAP. Upper and lower inner bands = band center ± inner multiplier × ATR baseline; upper and lower outer bands = band center ± outer multiplier × ATR baseline. The outer multiplier is greater than the inner multiplier.
Calculation: Outer-touch tolerance and exit tolerance = their respective multipliers × ATR baseline.
Calculation: Lower outer touch = low <= lower outer band + outer-touch tolerance. Upper outer touch = high >= upper outer band - outer-touch tolerance. VWAP touch = low <= anchored VWAP AND high >= anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Armed direction = initially neutral; on each bar, set to long on a lower outer touch, otherwise set to short on an upper outer touch; then set to neutral on a VWAP touch or anchor reset. Preserve it otherwise, including while a position is open; set it to neutral after an entry.
Calculation: ATR z-score = (ATR - its longer-lookback SMA) / its standard deviation over the same longer lookback; undefined if the SMA or standard deviation is unavailable or the standard deviation is zero.
Long: position side = flat AND armed direction = long AND close > lower inner band AND (no previous entry OR bars since previous entry >= cooldown) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR z-score undefined OR ATR z-score >= low-volatility threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Short: position side = flat AND armed direction = short AND close < upper inner band AND (no previous entry OR bars since previous entry >= cooldown) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR z-score undefined OR ATR z-score >= low-volatility threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA of close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Exit: long, position was long at the previous bar close AND ((wick exit enabled AND VWAP touch) OR (close exit enabled AND abs(close - anchored VWAP) <= exit tolerance) OR lower outer touch OR anchor reset).
Exit: short, position was short at the previous bar close AND ((wick exit enabled AND VWAP touch) OR (close exit enabled AND abs(close - anchored VWAP) <= exit tolerance) OR upper outer touch OR anchor reset).
