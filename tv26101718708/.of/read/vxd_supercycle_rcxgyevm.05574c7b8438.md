Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is the high or low of a bar with the specified number of bars on each side satisfying the pivot-high or pivot-low test; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. The latest pivot high and latest pivot low retain their last confirmed values.
Calculation: Trend line = rolling volume-weighted average of the selected price source over a time window with a minimum number of chart bars, or EMA of close when rolling VWAP is disabled or cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Regression trend = EMA of the linear-regression endpoint of close; its directional comparison uses its value a fixed number of chart bars earlier.
Calculation: Smoothed candle open = EMA of (open + previous close) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish zone = short EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend direction is bullish AND close > regression trend AND smoothed candle open > its previous value. Bearish zone = short EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend direction is bearish AND close < regression trend AND smoothed candle open <= its previous value.
Calculation: Filtered bullish zone = short EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend direction is bullish AND regression trend > its earlier value AND smoothed candle open > its previous value. Filtered bearish zone = short EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend direction is bearish AND regression trend < its earlier value AND smoothed candle open <= its previous value.
Calculation: Bullish action = selected bullish zone AND close > latest pivot high AND datetime is inside the selected backtest interval AND ((RSI moving average crosses above its midpoint AND RSI > RSI moving average AND RSI condition is enabled) OR (close > Supertrend AND position is flat or short)). Select the filtered zone when the trend filter is enabled, otherwise select the bullish zone.
Calculation: Bearish action = selected bearish zone AND close < latest pivot low AND datetime is inside the selected backtest interval AND ((RSI moving average crosses below its midpoint AND RSI < RSI moving average AND RSI condition is enabled) OR (close < Supertrend AND position is flat or long)). Select the filtered zone when the trend filter is enabled, otherwise select the bearish zone.
Calculation: On either change of bullish action, store the lowest latest pivot low over the sizing lookback as the stop; on either change of bearish action, store the highest latest pivot high over that lookback as the stop. Store the corresponding entry price and calculate the target as entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, the selected reward-to-risk multiple of the distance between entry price and stop. A change includes both false-to-true and true-to-false transitions.
Long: Bullish action changes AND the previous one-trade state is not long. When the take-profit percentage equals the whole position, the one-trade state becomes long on either change of bullish action and short on either change of bearish action; otherwise it is neutral.
Short: Bearish action changes AND the previous one-trade state is not short. When the take-profit percentage equals the whole position, the one-trade state becomes long on either change of bullish action and short on either change of bearish action; otherwise it is neutral.
Long: Bullish zone.
Short: Bearish zone.
Long: Regression trend > its earlier value.
Short: Regression trend < its earlier value.
Long: Previous bar is bearish AND current bar is bullish AND current close > previous high AND (minimum of current and previous low < current latest pivot low AND current close > current latest pivot low OR minimum of current and previous low < previous latest pivot low AND current close > previous latest pivot low).
Long: Previous bar is bearish AND current bar is bullish AND current close > previous open AND current close <= previous high AND (minimum of current and previous low < current latest pivot low AND current close > current latest pivot low OR minimum of current and previous low < previous latest pivot low AND current close > previous latest pivot low).
Short: Previous bar is bullish AND current bar is bearish AND current close < previous low AND (maximum of current and previous high > current latest pivot high AND current close < current latest pivot high OR maximum of current and previous high > previous latest pivot high AND current close < previous latest pivot high).
Short: Previous bar is bullish AND current bar is bearish AND current close < previous open AND current close >= previous low AND (maximum of current and previous high > current latest pivot high AND current close < current latest pivot high OR maximum of current and previous high > previous latest pivot high AND current close < previous latest pivot high).
Long: Previous bar is bullish AND bar before previous is bearish AND previous close > high two bars ago AND (previous low > close two bars ago minus a selected fraction of the distance from that close to low two bars ago OR previous low < low two bars ago).
Short: Previous bar is bearish AND bar before previous is bullish AND previous close < low two bars ago AND (previous high < close two bars ago plus a selected fraction of the distance from that close to high two bars ago OR previous high > high two bars ago).
Long: Close > open AND volume > a selected multiple of SMA of volume.
Short: Close < open AND volume > a selected multiple of SMA of volume.
Exit: long, short EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend direction is bearish AND close < latest pivot low AND close < regression trend.
Exit: short, short EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend direction is bullish AND close > latest pivot high AND close > regression trend.
Exit: long, take-profit and stop are enabled AND the preceding bar's low < entry price AND the active stop order is reached; OR take-profit and stop are enabled AND the preceding bar's low > entry price AND the active limit target is reached, for the selected fraction of the position.
Exit: short, take-profit and stop are enabled AND the preceding bar's high > entry price AND the active stop order is reached; OR take-profit and stop are enabled AND the preceding bar's high < entry price AND the active limit target is reached, for the selected fraction of the position.
