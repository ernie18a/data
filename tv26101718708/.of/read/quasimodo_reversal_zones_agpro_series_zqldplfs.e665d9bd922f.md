Calculation: Swing sequence = Confirm pivot highs and lows after the same configurable number of bars on each side. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low on the same bar. Keep alternating pivot types; replace the latest pivot with a same-type pivot only if the new high is higher or the new low is lower. Let P1 through P4 be the last four recorded pivots, with bar gaps G1, G2, and G3 between consecutive pivots. Evaluate a sequence only when a pivot is added or replaced.
Calculation: Normalized ATR = ATR at the decision bar, floored at a configurable minimum price increment; before ATR is available, use the greater of the current high minus low and that increment.
Calculation: Pattern score = Round and clamp to 0–100 the sum of: 25 times clamp(head extension / (2 times minimum head extension times normalized ATR), 0, 1); 25 times clamp(sequence break / (2 times minimum sequence break times normalized ATR), 0, 1); 15 times clamp(minimum of G1, G2, G3 / (2 times minimum pivot spacing), 0, 1); 15 times clamp(mean of the three absolute consecutive pivot price changes / (1.25 times normalized ATR), 0, 1); 10 times (1 minus clamp(abs(G1 − G2) / max(1, mean of G1, G2, G3), 0, 1)); and either 10 times clamp(current volume / its moving average / 1.35, 0, 1) when volume scoring is enabled, or 10 otherwise. Use relative volume of 1 when the volume average is unavailable or nonpositive, and treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: Accepted pattern = A swing sequence whose three gaps each meet the minimum pivot spacing, head extension is at least the minimum head extension times normalized ATR, sequence break is at least the minimum sequence break times normalized ATR, score is at least the minimum pattern score, P4 is not at the same bar as the last accepted P4, and the current bar is at least the cooldown distance after the last accepted pattern; the last two restrictions pass when there is no earlier accepted pattern. Head extension is abs(P3 − P1) and sequence break is abs(P4 − P2).
Calculation: Active zone = For the latest accepted pattern, set the level to P1, the upper and lower boundaries to P1 plus and minus the greater of normalized ATR at acceptance times the zone-width parameter and twice the minimum price increment, and the invalidation level to P3 minus the invalidation-buffer parameter times acceptance-bar normalized ATR for a bullish pattern, or P3 plus that amount for a bearish pattern. Replace the active zone whenever a new pattern is accepted. A zone is touched once current high is at least its lower boundary and current low is at most its upper boundary; retain the touched state until replacement.
Long: A newly accepted pattern has pivot types low, high, low, high in order AND P3 < P1 AND P4 > P2.
Short: A newly accepted pattern has pivot types high, low, high, low in order AND P3 > P1 AND P4 < P2.
Long: The active pattern is bullish AND its zone has been touched on this or an earlier bar AND close > the zone's upper boundary plus current normalized ATR times the reaction-distance parameter AND this is the first such reaction since the pattern was accepted.
Short: The active pattern is bearish AND its zone has been touched on this or an earlier bar AND close < the zone's lower boundary minus current normalized ATR times the reaction-distance parameter AND this is the first such reaction since the pattern was accepted.
Exit: long, the active pattern is bullish AND low <= its invalidation level AND this is the first invalidation since the pattern was accepted.
Exit: short, the active pattern is bearish AND high >= its invalidation level AND this is the first invalidation since the pattern was accepted.
