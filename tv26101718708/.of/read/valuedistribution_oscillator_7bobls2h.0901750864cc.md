Calculation: Value-location oscillator = On each confirmed chart-timeframe bar, distribute volume from the rolling lookback across a price grid spanning that lookback’s high–low range with a configurable pad; weight the open–close body more heavily than the wicks. Decay existing grid volume geometrically each bar, and rebuild the grid from the lookback when the current price leaves its range. Find the highest-volume grid level (POC), then expand the value area outward from the POC toward the more heavily traded adjacent level until it contains the configured share of total volume. Map the close to a bounded scale: POC = 0, value-area edges = ±25, lookback extremes = ±50, with linear interpolation between levels and clipping at ±50. Smooth this reading with an EMA; use the unsmoothed reading when smoothing is disabled.
Calculation: Turning value-location = 3-bar EMA of the value-location oscillator.
