Calculation: Decaying volume profile = Maintain a grid of equally sized price bins. Initialize or rebuild it when no grid exists or close is outside the grid: set its bounds to the rolling highest high and lowest low, padded above and below by a configurable fraction of those respective prices, and clear the bins. Seed it with the available bars within the profile lookback, including the current bar, weighting each bar's volume by exp(-age / profile lookback). Otherwise multiply every bin by exp(-1 / profile lookback) and add the current bar's volume. For each added bar, clamp the low and high bin indices to the grid; distribute the non-body fraction of volume equally across all bins from the low bin through the high bin inclusive. Distribute the body fraction to those bins in proportion to their overlap with the open-close body, divided by the greater of bin width and body length. Ignore nonpositive or unavailable volume.
Calculation: Value-distribution oscillator = Find the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest-index bin on a tie; its midpoint is the point of control. Starting at that bin, expand the value area one adjacent bin at a time toward the larger adjacent volume, choosing the upper bin on a tie, until the accumulated volume reaches the configured fraction of total volume. The lower edge of its lowest bin and upper edge of its highest bin are the value-area boundaries. Define the upper extreme as the greater of the rolling highest high over the profile lookback and the upper boundary, and the lower extreme as the lesser of the rolling lowest low over that lookback and the lower boundary. Map close piecewise linearly from the lower extreme, lower boundary, point of control, upper boundary, and upper extreme to -50, -25, 0, 25, and 50 respectively; clamp the result to [-50, 50]. If an applicable segment has zero width, use 0 for an inner segment and the corresponding value-area boundary reading for an outer segment. Optionally smooth the result with an EMA.
Calculation: Turn = EMA of the value-distribution oscillator using a separate smoothing length.
Long: Value-distribution oscillator <= negative stretch threshold AND Turn > previous bar's Turn
Short: Value-distribution oscillator >= positive stretch threshold AND Turn < previous bar's Turn
