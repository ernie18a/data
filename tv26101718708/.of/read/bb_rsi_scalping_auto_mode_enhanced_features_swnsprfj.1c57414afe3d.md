Calculation: Bands = Bollinger Bands of close; width = upper band − lower band.
Calculation: Oversold threshold = lowest RSI over its lookback + buffer; overbought threshold = highest RSI over its lookback − buffer.
Calculation: Recent bullish MACD turn = MACD histogram > previous histogram AND previous histogram < histogram two bars ago on any bar within the confirmation window, including the current bar. Recent bearish MACD turn reverses both inequalities.
Calculation: Recent strong volume = volume > its SMA × volume multiplier on any bar within the confirmation window, including the current bar; optionally also require that bar's volume > its previous bar's volume.
Calculation: Entry mode = selected conservative, balanced, or aggressive mode; in automatic mode, aggressive when band width > its SMA AND ATR > its SMA AND volume > its SMA × automatic volume multiplier; conservative when band width <= its SMA AND volume <= its SMA × automatic volume multiplier; balanced otherwise.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND its RSI is higher AND the pivots are at most the divergence lookback apart; pivots use equal left and right confirmation windows. Bearish divergence uses a higher pivot high and lower RSI. Evaluate pivot price, bands, RSI, and RSI threshold at the pivot bar; evaluate recent MACD turn and volume at the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Long continuation score = number of true conditions among low <= band basis, close > basis, close > open, RSI > RSI midpoint, MACD histogram > previous histogram, MACD line >= signal line, and recent strong volume. Short continuation score = number of true conditions among high >= basis, close < basis, close < open, RSI < RSI midpoint, MACD histogram < previous histogram, MACD line <= signal line, and recent strong volume.
Calculation: Initial long stop = lowest low over the initial stop lookback at entry − price buffer; initial short stop = highest high over that lookback at entry + price buffer. Dynamic long stop starts at lowest low over the dynamic stop lookback at entry − price buffer; dynamic short stop starts at highest high over that lookback at entry + price buffer. When dynamic updating is enabled, update each bar to the greater of the previous stop and current lookback low − buffer for longs, or the lesser of the previous stop and current lookback high + buffer for shorts. Test the updated stop even when updating is disabled.
Calculation: Long trailing stop activates when high >= entry price + current ATR × activation multiplier, then equals the highest high − current ATR × trailing multiplier observed on any bar since activation, including the current bar. Short trailing stop activates when low <= entry price − current ATR × activation multiplier, then equals the lowest low + current ATR × trailing multiplier observed since activation. Break-even activation uses the same respective high or low comparison with its own ATR multiplier and remains active thereafter.
Calculation: Long profit targets = entry price + entry-bar ATR × each target multiplier; short targets = entry price − entry-bar ATR × each target multiplier. Track each target as hit when the current high reaches it for a long or the current low reaches it for a short, updating all hit states before evaluating exits.
Long: Position is flat AND session filter is satisfied when enabled AND entry mode is conservative AND ((low < lower band AND RSI <= oversold threshold AND recent bullish MACD turn AND recent strong volume AND close > lower band AND close > previous close) OR (bullish divergence AND pivot low < lower band at pivot AND RSI at pivot <= oversold threshold at pivot AND recent bullish MACD turn AND recent strong volume)).
Short: Position is flat AND session filter is satisfied when enabled AND entry mode is conservative AND ((high > upper band AND RSI >= overbought threshold AND recent bearish MACD turn AND recent strong volume AND close < upper band AND close < previous close) OR (bearish divergence AND pivot high > upper band at pivot AND RSI at pivot >= overbought threshold at pivot AND recent bearish MACD turn AND recent strong volume)).
Long: Position is flat AND session filter is satisfied when enabled AND entry mode is balanced AND low <= lower band + width × near-band fraction AND RSI <= oversold threshold + near-RSI buffer AND close > open.
Short: Position is flat AND session filter is satisfied when enabled AND entry mode is balanced AND high >= upper band − width × near-band fraction AND RSI >= overbought threshold − near-RSI buffer AND close < open.
Long: Position is flat AND session filter is satisfied when enabled AND entry mode is aggressive AND low <= lower band + width × near-band fraction AND RSI <= oversold threshold + near-RSI buffer.
Short: Position is flat AND session filter is satisfied when enabled AND entry mode is aggressive AND high >= upper band − width × near-band fraction AND RSI >= overbought threshold − near-RSI buffer.
Long: Continuation mode is enabled AND low <= basis AND close > basis AND close > open AND RSI > RSI midpoint AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND MACD line >= signal line AND recent strong volume.
Short: Continuation mode is enabled AND high >= basis AND close < basis AND close < open AND RSI < RSI midpoint AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND MACD line <= signal line AND recent strong volume.
Long: Near-entry warnings are enabled AND position is flat AND low <= lower band + width × near-band fraction AND RSI <= oversold threshold + near-RSI buffer AND bars since the previous long near-entry warning >= its cooldown, or no previous warning exists.
Short: Near-entry warnings are enabled AND position is flat AND high >= upper band − width × near-band fraction AND RSI >= overbought threshold − near-RSI buffer AND bars since the previous short near-entry warning >= its cooldown, or no previous warning exists.
Long: Continuation warnings and continuation mode are enabled AND position is flat AND long continuation score < maximum score AND long continuation score >= warning threshold AND bars since the previous long continuation warning >= its cooldown, or no previous warning exists.
Short: Continuation warnings and continuation mode are enabled AND position is flat AND short continuation score < maximum score AND short continuation score >= warning threshold AND bars since the previous short continuation warning >= its cooldown, or no previous warning exists.
Exit: long, reversal trade AND selected reversal exit mode is first retrace AND close < open.
Exit: short, reversal trade AND selected reversal exit mode is first retrace AND close > open.
Exit: long, reversal trade AND selected reversal exit mode is retrace with MACD weakness AND close < open AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: short, reversal trade AND selected reversal exit mode is retrace with MACD weakness AND close > open AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
Exit: long, continuation trade AND selected continuation exit mode is basis break with MACD weakness AND close < basis AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: short, continuation trade AND selected continuation exit mode is basis break with MACD weakness AND close > basis AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
Exit: long, selected exit mode for the trade type is momentum reversal AND ((histogram exit enabled AND histogram < previous histogram AND previous histogram > histogram two bars ago) OR (MACD cross exit enabled AND MACD line crosses below signal line) OR (RSI exit enabled AND RSI crosses below its midpoint)).
Exit: short, selected exit mode for the trade type is momentum reversal AND ((histogram exit enabled AND histogram > previous histogram AND previous histogram < histogram two bars ago) OR (MACD cross exit enabled AND MACD line crosses above signal line) OR (RSI exit enabled AND RSI crosses above its midpoint)).
Exit: long, continuation trade AND selected continuation exit mode is MACD cross only AND MACD line crosses below signal line.
Exit: short, continuation trade AND selected continuation exit mode is MACD cross only AND MACD line crosses above signal line.
Exit: long, low <= initial long stop OR low <= dynamic long stop OR (trailing stop enabled AND activated AND low <= long trailing stop) OR (break-even stop enabled AND activated AND low <= entry price) OR (maximum holding time enabled AND bar time >= entry time + maximum holding time).
Exit: short, high >= initial short stop OR high >= dynamic short stop OR (trailing stop enabled AND activated AND high >= short trailing stop) OR (break-even stop enabled AND activated AND high >= entry price) OR (maximum holding time enabled AND bar time >= entry time + maximum holding time).
Exit: long, profit targets enabled AND ((third target newly hit AND second and first targets are hit) OR (third target newly hit AND first target is not hit) OR (second target newly hit AND first target is hit)).
Exit: short, profit targets enabled AND ((third target newly hit AND second and first targets are hit) OR (third target newly hit AND first target is not hit) OR (second target newly hit AND first target is hit)).
Exit: long, exit-soon warnings enabled AND no full exit condition AND no partial profit-target exit condition AND MACD histogram < previous histogram.
Exit: short, exit-soon warnings enabled AND no full exit condition AND no partial profit-target exit condition AND MACD histogram > previous histogram.
Calculation: A partial profit-target exit condition holds when profit targets are enabled AND no full exit condition holds AND ((first target is newly hit AND second target is not hit) OR (second target is newly hit AND first target is hit AND third target is not hit)); target hit states are updated first. A continuation trade is recorded when its side's continuation condition holds at entry; otherwise it is a reversal trade. Actual entries require a flat position and the enabled session filter; if both entry sides occur together, the short entry takes precedence. Entry and exit conditions are evaluated on the same bar, with stops updated before exits.
