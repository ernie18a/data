Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low less than or equal to each low in the preceding pivot-lookback bars and less than each low in the following pivot-lookback bars; it is confirmed on the last of those following bars. A confirmed pivot high uses the corresponding greater-than-or-equal and greater-than comparisons on highs.
Calculation: Bullish shift = on the previous bar, the shorter-period close EMA crossed above the longer-period close EMA AND their absolute difference was at least a configurable multiple of ATR, using a configurable minimum price increment if ATR was unavailable AND volume was greater than a configurable multiple of its SMA, with a zero threshold if the SMA was unavailable or zero AND, on the current bar, the number of bars since a pivot low was confirmed is at most twice the pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bearish shift = on the previous bar, the shorter-period close EMA crossed below the longer-period close EMA AND their absolute difference was at least a configurable multiple of ATR, using a configurable minimum price increment if ATR was unavailable AND volume was greater than a configurable multiple of its SMA, with a zero threshold if the SMA was unavailable or zero AND, on the current bar, the number of bars since a pivot high was confirmed is at most twice the pivot lookback.
Long: Shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA OR bullish shift occurred on the current bar or either of the preceding two bars.
Short: Shorter-period close EMA <= longer-period close EMA OR bearish shift occurred on the current bar or either of the preceding two bars AND no bullish shift occurred on the current bar or either of the preceding two bars.
