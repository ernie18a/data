Calculation: Prior-period levels = the high and low of each of several progressively longer higher timeframes, using the last completed bar of each timeframe.
Calculation: Prior-session levels = the high and low of the last completed occurrence of each of several configurable sessions, calculated from chart bars whose timestamps fall within that session.
Calculation: Level touch = low <= level + close * touch-tolerance percentage AND high >= level - close * touch-tolerance percentage.
Calculation: Level proximity = absolute value of (close - level) / close < proximity percentage.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of the configured weights for all touched prior-period lows for long or highs for short where close is respectively above or below the level and respectively above or below open; add the corresponding configured weights for qualifying prior-session lows or highs. Longer prior-period levels have greater weights; session weights increase in session order. Add a higher-timeframe proximity weight if any prior-period high or low other than the shortest-period levels is near, a session proximity weight if any prior-session high or low is near, and a volume-spike weight if there is a volume spike. Proximity and volume weights apply even without a rejection.
Calculation: Projected resistance zone = confirm pivot highs using equal numbers of chart bars on each side. Starting at a configurable older pivot depth, take a configurable number of older pivot highs; average only positive differences between the close at the latest confirmed pivot high and those older highs. Add that average to the latest pivot's close for the upper boundary, and add a configurable smaller fraction of it for the lower boundary.
Calculation: Projected support zone = confirm pivot lows using equal numbers of chart bars on each side. Starting at a configurable older pivot depth, take a configurable number of older pivot lows; average only positive differences between those older lows and the close at the latest confirmed pivot low. Subtract that average from the latest pivot's close for the lower boundary, and subtract a configurable smaller fraction of it for the upper boundary.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 from the anchor bar through the current chart bar. Anchor to the enabled touched prior-period or prior-session high or low closest to close, resolving equal distances in prior-period order from shortest to longest, high before low, then in session order, high before low. A new anchor requires its distance from the existing anchor divided by close to exceed the touch-tolerance percentage. In extend mode, or auto mode with re-anchoring enabled, switch only when inactive or after a configurable minimum number of bars; in auto mode with hold enabled, switch only when inactive. In auto mode deactivate after a configurable maximum number of bars or when absolute value of (close - anchor) / close exceeds a configurable percentage. Include the anchor bar in the accumulation and bar count.
Long: Long directional score >= score threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > shorter EMA(close) AND shorter EMA(close) > longer EMA(close)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume spike) AND bars since the previous long-score signal > cooldown, or no previous long-score signal exists.
Short: Short directional score >= score threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < shorter EMA(close) AND shorter EMA(close) < longer EMA(close)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume spike) AND bars since the previous short-score signal > cooldown, or no previous short-score signal exists.
Long: close > shortest prior-period high AND previous close <= current shortest prior-period high AND volume spike.
Short: close < shortest prior-period low AND previous close >= current shortest prior-period low AND volume spike.
Long: close >= projected support zone lower boundary AND close <= projected support zone upper boundary AND (zone trend filter disabled OR close > shorter EMA(close) AND shorter EMA(close) > longer EMA(close) OR close < shorter EMA(close) AND shorter EMA(close) < longer EMA(close)).
Short: close >= projected resistance zone lower boundary AND close <= projected resistance zone upper boundary AND (zone trend filter disabled OR close > shorter EMA(close) AND shorter EMA(close) > longer EMA(close) OR close < shorter EMA(close) AND shorter EMA(close) < longer EMA(close)).
Long: Anchored VWAP is active AND anchored VWAP > anchor.
Short: Anchored VWAP is active AND anchored VWAP <= anchor.
