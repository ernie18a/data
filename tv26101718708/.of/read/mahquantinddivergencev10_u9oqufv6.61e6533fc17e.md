Calculation: Indicator set = selected MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI of close, SMA-smoothed stochastic of close, CCI of close, momentum of close, OBV, short-lookback VWMA of close minus long-lookback VWMA of close, Chaikin money flow, and MFI calculated from close; MACD uses a shorter fast EMA than slow EMA.
Calculation: Pivot = a confirmed high or low of the selected price source, using equal numbers of bars on either side; the source is either close for both sides or high for highs and low for lows.
Calculation: Divergence candidate = for each selected indicator, direction and selected regular or hidden type, search the most recent permitted number of confirmed pivots of the corresponding side, newest first, within the maximum lookback and beyond the minimum separation; use the first pivot satisfying the corresponding price and indicator comparisons and the line-of-sight test. The endpoint is the current bar without confirmation, otherwise the preceding bar. In confirmation mode, bullish candidates additionally require current indicator > previous indicator OR current close > previous close; bearish candidates require current indicator < previous indicator OR current close < previous close. For bullish candidates, every intermediate indicator value and close must be at or above its respective straight line joining the pivot and endpoint values; for bearish candidates, both must be at or below their respective lines.
Calculation: Bullish regular candidate = endpoint low-source price < pivot low-source price AND endpoint indicator > pivot indicator. Bullish hidden candidate = endpoint low-source price > pivot low-source price AND endpoint indicator < pivot indicator. Bearish regular candidate = endpoint high-source price > pivot high-source price AND endpoint indicator < pivot indicator. Bearish hidden candidate = endpoint high-source price < pivot high-source price AND endpoint indicator > pivot indicator.
Calculation: Accepted divergence = a divergence candidate AND the total number of candidates across all selected indicators, sides and types >= the minimum divergence count AND the active same-side filter passes. The confluence filter requires at least one regular and one hidden candidate; the multiple-regular filter requires regular candidates with at least two distinct pivot distances; the multiple-hidden filter requires hidden candidates with at least two distinct pivot distances. If multiple filters are enabled, multiple-hidden overrides multiple-regular, which overrides confluence.
Calculation: Entry risk levels = on the signal bar, use close as entry price and the pivot associated with the first accepted divergence in indicator order, with regular bullish, regular bearish, hidden bullish, then hidden bearish checked within each indicator. For a long, raw stop = min(signal-bar low, pivot-bar low) - ATR times its multiplier; for a short, raw stop = max(signal-bar high, pivot-bar high) + ATR times its multiplier. Cap the directional distance from entry to stop at a configurable maximum price distance. Risk = absolute difference between entry and stop; long targets = entry + risk and entry + twice risk; short targets = entry - risk and entry - twice risk.
Long: At least one accepted bullish divergence exists.
Short: At least one accepted bearish divergence exists.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= first long target for half the position OR high >= second long target for the remaining position.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= first short target for half the position OR low <= second short target for the remaining position.
