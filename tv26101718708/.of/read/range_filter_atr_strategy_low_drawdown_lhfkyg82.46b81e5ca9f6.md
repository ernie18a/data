Calculation: Selected OHLC = either chart OHLC or Heikin Ashi OHLC, optionally smoothed componentwise by an SMA. Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; initial Heikin Ashi open = (open + close) / 2, subsequently (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close) / 2; Heikin Ashi high = max(high, Heikin Ashi open, Heikin Ashi close); Heikin Ashi low = min(low, Heikin Ashi open, Heikin Ashi close).
Calculation: Range center and deviation = SMA(selected close) and a multiplier times its standard deviation, using the same lookback. Depending on the selected lock method, use current values or retain both values from the latest update: update on selected close moving more than an ATR multiple from the last locked close or a time limit passing; on selected close crossing the current SMA; on volume exceeding an average-volume multiple or, after a timeout, volume falling to or below average; or periodically. Initialize the locked values on the first available update. ATR uses chart OHLC.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = range center plus and minus range deviation. Upper entry level = upper band + band-width multiplier × (upper band − lower band); lower entry level = lower band − band-width multiplier × (upper band − lower band). Upper and lower midpoint levels = range center × (1 plus or minus a midpoint buffer fraction).
Calculation: Volume confirmation = either volume > SMA(volume) × an average-volume multiplier, or volume > each of the preceding two volumes AND volume > SMA(volume), or volume > SMA(volume) × a surge multiplier, or volume > SMA(volume) × an average-volume multiplier AND abs(selected close − selected open) / selected open > a movement threshold; each alternative also requires volume > a minimum. The surge multiplier is larger than the average-volume multiplier.
Calculation: Directional volume ratio = SMA(buy volume) / (SMA(buy volume) + SMA(sell volume)), or one half if the denominator is not positive. With the tick-direction method, for an up bar buy volume = volume × (selected close − selected open) / (selected high − selected low), for a down bar sell volume = volume × (selected open − selected close) / (selected high − selected low), and the other side is the remainder; split volume equally on a flat bar. With the price-action method, assign all volume to buying or selling according to whether selected close rose or fell from the preceding bar, splitting equally if unchanged. With the volume-weighted method, buy volume = volume × (selected close − selected low) / (selected high − selected low) and sell volume is the remainder; split equally if high equals low.
Calculation: Long swing stop = min(lowest selected low over the swing lookback minus a buffer, selected close minus a minimum ATR distance); short swing stop = max(highest selected high over the same lookback plus the buffer, selected close plus the minimum ATR distance). Long risk = selected close − long swing stop; short risk = short swing stop − selected close. If the swing stop is unavailable, use an ATR-multiple risk.
Calculation: Entry filters = the selected trade direction permits the side AND the bar is in an enabled session when the session filter is enabled AND, for the swing-based exit method, the side's risk > the minimum ATR distance. Each enabled entry method optionally requires volume confirmation and optionally requires directional volume ratio >= its threshold for long or <= one minus its threshold for short.
Long: Entry filters AND an enabled entry method satisfies one of: selected close crosses above range center; OR selected close crosses above upper entry level AND selected close − upper band >= an ATR distance; OR selected close is between the midpoint levels AND abs(selected close − previous selected close) >= an ATR distance AND selected close > previous selected close AND selected close > selected open AND selected close > range center.
Short: Entry filters AND an enabled entry method satisfies one of: selected close crosses below range center; OR selected close crosses below lower entry level AND lower band − selected close >= an ATR distance; OR selected close is between the midpoint levels AND abs(selected close − previous selected close) >= an ATR distance AND selected close < previous selected close AND selected close < selected open AND selected close < range center.
Long: Selected close > range center AND RSI(selected close) < an upper threshold.
Short: Selected close < range center AND RSI(selected close) > a lower threshold.
Long: Directional volume filter is enabled AND directional volume ratio > its threshold.
Short: Directional volume filter is enabled AND directional volume ratio < one minus its threshold.
Calculation: Swing targets and stop = on each qualifying swing-method entry signal, save the selected close, swing stop, and risk from that signal bar. The quick, first, and final targets are the saved close plus the respective risk multiples for long, and minus them for short; the first target is at one risk. Reset the target-hit flags on each such signal. If the breakeven option is enabled and selected high reaches the first long target or selected low reaches the first short target, set the corresponding flag and change the displayed current stop to entry price plus or minus the swing buffer respectively.
Calculation: Trailing stop = after price moves favorably from entry by the submitted activation distance, trail the highest price for a long or lowest price for a short by the submitted offset. Both distances are the signal-bar ATR multiplied by the selected trailing multiplier and expressed in minimum price ticks.
Exit: long, an enabled opposite-signal exit AND a short entry condition.
Exit: short, an enabled opposite-signal exit AND a long entry condition.
Exit: long, fixed-ATR method AND price >= position average price plus the signal-bar ATR profit multiple OR price <= position average price minus the signal-bar ATR stop multiple.
Exit: short, fixed-ATR method AND price <= position average price minus the signal-bar ATR profit multiple OR price >= position average price plus the signal-bar ATR stop multiple.
Exit: long, fixed-points method AND price >= position average price plus the first point target OR price <= position average price minus the point stop.
Exit: short, fixed-points method AND price <= position average price minus the first point target OR price >= position average price plus the point stop.
Exit: long, swing-based method AND trailing from entry is enabled AND price <= trailing stop.
Exit: short, swing-based method AND trailing from entry is enabled AND price >= trailing stop.
Exit: long, swing-based method AND trailing from entry is disabled AND price <= saved swing stop OR price >= saved final target; with partial exits enabled, also exit a partial position at the first target, and at the quick target when quick profit is enabled.
Exit: short, swing-based method AND trailing from entry is disabled AND price >= saved swing stop OR price <= saved final target; with partial exits enabled, also exit a partial position at the first target, and at the quick target when quick profit is enabled.
Exit: long, swing-based method AND time exit is enabled AND bars since the latest qualifying long signal >= maximum holding bars.
Exit: short, swing-based method AND time exit is enabled AND bars since the latest qualifying short signal >= maximum holding bars.
Exit: long, multiple-point-target method AND price reaches any of three increasing point targets above position average price OR price <= position average price minus the point stop; each target exits its configured portion.
Exit: short, multiple-point-target method AND price reaches any of three increasing point distances below position average price OR price >= position average price plus the point stop; each target exits its configured portion.
Exit: long, multiple-ATR-target method AND price reaches any of three increasing signal-bar ATR targets above position average price OR price <= position average price minus the signal-bar ATR stop multiple; each target exits its configured portion.
Exit: short, multiple-ATR-target method AND price reaches any of three increasing signal-bar ATR distances below position average price OR price >= position average price plus the signal-bar ATR stop multiple; each target exits its configured portion.
Exit: long, trailing-only or hybrid method AND price <= trailing stop.
Exit: short, trailing-only or hybrid method AND price >= trailing stop.
Exit: long, momentum or hybrid method AND price <= position average price minus the point stop.
Exit: short, momentum or hybrid method AND price >= position average price plus the point stop.
Exit: long, hybrid method AND price reaches either of two increasing point targets above position average price; each target exits a partial position.
Exit: short, hybrid method AND price reaches either of two increasing point distances below position average price; each target exits a partial position.
Exit: long, abs(momentum of selected close) < abs(SMA(momentum)) × a weakening threshold AND momentum < 0 OR RSI(selected close) > an overbought threshold OR volume-decline exit is enabled AND volume / SMA(volume) < a decline threshold. If the volume average is not positive, use one as the ratio.
Exit: short, abs(momentum of selected close) < abs(SMA(momentum)) × a weakening threshold AND momentum > 0 OR RSI(selected close) < an oversold threshold OR volume-decline exit is enabled AND volume / SMA(volume) < a decline threshold. If the volume average is not positive, use one as the ratio.
Exit: long, swing-based method AND quick-profit option is enabled AND selected high has reached the saved quick target since the latest qualifying long signal.
Exit: short, swing-based method AND quick-profit option is enabled AND selected low has reached the saved quick target since the latest qualifying short signal.
Exit: long, swing-based method AND breakeven option is enabled AND selected high has reached the saved first target since the latest qualifying long signal.
Exit: short, swing-based method AND breakeven option is enabled AND selected low has reached the saved first target since the latest qualifying short signal.
