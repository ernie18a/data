Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening period, reset each local day; it is complete after that period ends.
Calculation: Signal price = close of the bar that generated the entry order. Risk distance = signal price multiplied by the configured risk fraction. Trailing distance = ATR on the entry-signal bar multiplied by the configured trailing multiplier.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after price reaches signal price plus the configured activation multiple of risk distance, the highest price reached since activation minus trailing distance. Short trailing stop = after price reaches signal price minus the configured activation multiple of risk distance, the lowest price reached since activation plus trailing distance.
Long: Bar opening time is within the entry window AND opening range is complete AND position is flat AND entry count since the daily or opening-range reset is below its limit AND close > opening-range high AND (previous close <= current opening-range high if close confirmation is enabled, otherwise previous close <= previous opening-range high) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX > threshold AND (rising-ADX filter disabled OR ADX > previous ADX))) AND (longer-period EMA filter disabled OR close > longer-period EMA) AND (shorter-period EMA filter disabled OR close > shorter-period EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < upper threshold).
Short: Bar opening time is within the entry window AND opening range is complete AND position is flat AND entry count since the daily or opening-range reset is below its limit AND close < opening-range low AND (previous close >= current opening-range low if close confirmation is enabled, otherwise previous close >= previous opening-range low) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX > threshold AND (rising-ADX filter disabled OR ADX > previous ADX))) AND (longer-period EMA filter disabled OR close < longer-period EMA) AND (shorter-period EMA filter disabled OR close < shorter-period EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > lower threshold).
Long: Close > session VWAP of typical price.
Short: Close <= session VWAP of typical price.
Exit: Long, low <= signal price minus risk distance OR high >= signal price plus risk distance multiplied by the reward-to-risk ratio OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND price <= long trailing stop) OR bar opening time is within the session-exit window.
Exit: Short, high >= signal price plus risk distance OR low <= signal price minus risk distance multiplied by the reward-to-risk ratio OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop activated AND price >= short trailing stop) OR bar opening time is within the session-exit window.
