Calculation: Long setup = close crosses either direction through the 0% Fibonacci level AND MACD minus its signal line > 0 AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI > its value 1 bar ago AND RSI 1 bar ago > its value 2 bars ago
Calculation: Short setup = close crosses either direction through the 100% Fibonacci level AND MACD minus its signal line < 0 AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI < its value 1 bar ago AND RSI 1 bar ago < its value 2 bars ago
Calculation: Fibonacci level p = latest confirmed pivot low + p × (latest confirmed pivot high − latest confirmed pivot low); levels used are p = 0%, 21%, 30%, 50%, 62%, 70%, and 100%
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = highest high/lowest low over the pivot lookback, confirmed when the deviation of that rolling highest/lowest series over the same lookback is zero; retain the most recent confirmed pivot
Calculation: MACD histogram = (short-lookback EMA of close − long-lookback EMA of close) − EMA of that difference; the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: A signal timestamp = current bar time when its setup is true, otherwise its previous value; latest signal timestamp is retained
