Calculation: Pivot high = the most recent rolling highest high that stayed unchanged for a full pivot lookback; pivot low = the most recent rolling lowest low that stayed unchanged for the same lookback.
Calculation: Retracement level at fraction f = pivot low + f × (pivot high − pivot low). The low endpoint has fraction 0 and the high endpoint has fraction 1; the four intermediate fractions used below are strictly increasing, with the middle level between the second and third.
Calculation: MACD histogram = short-period EMA of close minus long-period EMA of close, minus an EMA of that difference.
Calculation: Raw long event = close crosses the configurable retracement level in either direction AND MACD histogram > 0 AND RSI of close < upper RSI threshold AND RSI > previous RSI AND previous RSI > RSI two bars ago. Raw short event = close crosses the high endpoint in either direction AND MACD histogram < 0 AND RSI of close > lower RSI threshold AND RSI < previous RSI AND previous RSI < RSI two bars ago. The lower RSI threshold is below the upper RSI threshold. If reverse orders is enabled, swap the raw long and short events before all subsequent calculations.
Calculation: Event time = datetime of the most recent occurrence of an event, initialized to zero. A timestamp crosses above another when it is greater now and was less than or equal to it on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Initial long signal = raw long event time crosses above raw short event time. Initial short signal = raw short event time crosses above raw long event time.
Calculation: Long stage 1 event = close crosses the lowest intermediate retracement level in either direction AND MACD histogram > 0 AND RSI < upper RSI threshold AND RSI > previous RSI AND previous RSI > RSI two bars ago AND previous bar's initial long signal time > current initial short signal time. Short stage 1 event = close crosses the highest intermediate retracement level in either direction AND MACD histogram < 0 AND RSI > lower RSI threshold AND RSI < previous RSI AND previous RSI < RSI two bars ago AND previous bar's initial short signal time > current initial long signal time. Each stage 1 signal occurs when its event time crosses above its same-side initial signal time.
Calculation: Long stage 2 event = close crosses the second intermediate retracement level in either direction AND MACD histogram > 0 AND RSI < upper RSI threshold AND RSI > previous RSI AND previous RSI > RSI two bars ago AND long stage 1 signal time > previous bar's initial long signal time AND long stage 1 signal is false AND previous bar's initial long signal time > current initial short signal time. Short stage 2 event uses the third intermediate retracement level, MACD histogram < 0, RSI > lower RSI threshold, RSI < previous RSI, previous RSI < RSI two bars ago, and the corresponding short-side signal-time comparisons. Each stage 2 signal occurs when its event time crosses above its same-side stage 1 signal time AND its same-side stage 1 signal is false.
Calculation: Each stage 3 event uses the middle retracement level and otherwise has the same conditions as its same-side stage 2 event. Each stage 3 signal occurs when its event time crosses above its same-side stage 2 signal time AND its same-side stage 2 signal is false.
Calculation: Each same-side stage 4 through stage 9 event = its raw event AND its stage 1 signal time > previous bar's initial signal time AND its stage 1 signal is false AND previous bar's initial signal time > current opposite-side initial signal time. Each stage signal occurs when its event time crosses above its immediately preceding same-side stage signal time, subject to these same-side exclusions: stage 4 requires stages 2 and 3 false; stage 5 requires stages 3 and 4 false; stage 6 requires stages 2, 4 and 5 false; each of stages 7 through 9 requires stage 2 and every stage from 4 through its immediately preceding stage false.
Long: Initial long signal OR any long stage 1 through stage 9 signal.
Short: Initial short signal OR any short stage 1 through stage 9 signal.
Exit: short, low <= most recent raw short event's open − profit target AND previous bar's raw short event is false OR high >= most recent raw short event's open + stop distance AND previous bar's raw short event is false; apply to the initial short entry and short stages 4 through 9.
Exit: short, low <= most recent short stage 1 event's open − profit target AND previous bar's short stage 1 event is false OR high >= most recent short stage 1 event's open + stop distance AND previous bar's short stage 1 event is false; apply to the stage 1 short entry.
Exit: short, low <= most recent short stage 2 event's open − profit target AND previous bar's short stage 2 event is false OR high >= most recent short stage 2 event's open + stop distance AND previous bar's short stage 2 event is false; apply to the stage 2 short entry.
Exit: short, low <= most recent short stage 3 event's open − profit target AND previous bar's short stage 3 event is false OR high >= most recent short stage 3 event's open + stop distance AND previous bar's short stage 3 event is false; apply to the stage 3 short entry.
