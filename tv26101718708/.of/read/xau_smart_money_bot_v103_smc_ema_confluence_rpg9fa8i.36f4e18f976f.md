Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = values calculated on an independently selected timeframe that may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. For historical bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar available at the chart bar close; for a lower timeframe, use its last completed intrabar within the chart bar. While a higher-timeframe bar is forming in real time, use its current values. The bias timeframe is shorter than the timeframe used for the gap and order-block calculations.
Calculation: Bullish gap = three consecutive higher-timeframe bullish candles AND current higher-timeframe low > its high two bars earlier. Bearish gap = three consecutive higher-timeframe bearish candles AND current higher-timeframe high < its low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Bullish order block = previous higher-timeframe close < previous higher-timeframe open AND current higher-timeframe close > previous higher-timeframe high. Bearish order block = previous higher-timeframe close > previous higher-timeframe open AND current higher-timeframe close < previous higher-timeframe low.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions: shorter EMA > longer EMA; low < lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback AND close > midpoint of current high and low; close > open AND close - open > ATR multiplied by the strong-candle threshold; bullish gap; bullish order block; OBV > previous OBV; ATR > SMA of ATR; absolute distance from close to the bullish retracement level < distance threshold; low <= shorter EMA AND close > shorter EMA; high > previous high AND previous high > high two bars ago AND close > longer EMA; absolute difference between current and previous high < equality threshold AND close > low; close < midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the longer range lookback; close > open AND close - open > ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold. The strong-candle threshold is greater than the displacement threshold. For the liquidity condition, use the absolute difference between current and previous low, not high.
Calculation: Bearish score = count of true conditions: shorter EMA < longer EMA; high > highest high over the preceding sweep lookback AND close < midpoint of current high and low; close < open AND open - close > ATR multiplied by the strong-candle threshold; bearish gap; bearish order block; OBV < previous OBV; ATR > SMA of ATR; absolute distance from close to the bearish retracement level < distance threshold; high >= shorter EMA AND close < shorter EMA; low < previous low AND previous low < low two bars ago AND close < longer EMA; absolute difference between current and previous high < equality threshold AND close < high; close > midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the longer range lookback; close < open AND open - close > ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold.
Calculation: Bullish retracement level = highest high over the retracement lookback minus a retracement fraction of the difference between that highest high and the lowest low over the same lookback. Bearish retracement level = that lowest low plus the same fraction of the difference. The sweep lookback is shorter than the retracement lookback, which is shorter than the range lookback. All three ranges include the current bar except the sweep range, which ends at the previous bar.
Calculation: Bullish setup = bullish score >= score threshold AND chart bar opening time in the Budapest time zone is outside the excluded session AND shorter EMA > longer EMA on the bias timeframe. Bearish setup = bearish score >= the same score threshold AND chart bar opening time in the Budapest time zone is outside the excluded session AND shorter EMA < longer EMA on the bias timeframe. The shorter and longer EMA lengths match those used on the chart timeframe.
Long: Bullish setup is true AND bullish setup was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: Bearish setup is true AND bearish setup was false on the previous chart bar.
