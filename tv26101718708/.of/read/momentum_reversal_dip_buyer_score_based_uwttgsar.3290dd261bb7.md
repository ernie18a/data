Calculation: VWMO = percentage change in volume-weighted moving average of close between its current value and its value one lookback-length earlier.
Calculation: Cumulative Delta = cumulative sum of volume signed positive when close rises from the prior bar, negative when it falls, and zero when unchanged.
Calculation: Momentum Score = sum of point scores for close relative to the simple moving average, exponential moving average, and session VWAP (highest when above all, lowest when below all, intermediate otherwise); RSI, VWMO, ADX, stochastic %K, CCI, MFI, ATR relative to its longer simple moving average, and Cumulative Delta direction. For each oscillator, assign higher points to stronger or higher readings and lower points to weaker or lower readings, with intermediate points for middle ranges.
Long: Momentum Score < low-score threshold AND CCI crosses upward through its oversold threshold AND daily trade count < daily limit AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in the configured session)
Short: Momentum Score > high-score threshold AND Momentum Score < upper-score threshold AND CCI crosses downward through its overbought threshold AND daily trade count < daily limit AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in the configured session)
Exit: Long, low <= the greatest of entry price minus the configured percentage stop, entry price plus the configured breakeven offset when close - entry price >= main ATR × breakeven trigger (if enabled), and close minus trailing ATR × trailing distance when close - entry price >= trailing ATR × trailing trigger (if enabled) OR high >= entry price + main ATR × target multiple OR force-flat is enabled and the configured session has just ended
Exit: Short, high >= the least of entry price plus the configured percentage stop, entry price minus the configured breakeven offset when entry price - close >= main ATR × breakeven trigger (if enabled), and close plus trailing ATR × trailing distance when entry price - close >= trailing ATR × trailing trigger (if enabled) OR low <= entry price - main ATR × target multiple OR force-flat is enabled and the configured session has just ended
