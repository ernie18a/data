Calculation: Triangle boundaries = lines through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs and the two most recent confirmed pivot lows, projected to the current bar; also project both lines to the prior bar. The pattern starts at the earlier of the first pivot-high and first pivot-low anchors.
Calculation: Valid triangle = both boundary pairs exist and each has increasing anchor bars; pattern age is within its minimum and maximum; starting and current widths are positive; starting width / ATR meets its minimum; current width / ATR is at most its maximum; (starting width − current width) / starting width meets its minimum; upper slope < lower slope; upper boundary > lower boundary; and the projected line intersection is from 5 bars behind through the maximum allowed bars ahead.
Calculation: Breakout quality score = compression score + apex score + breakout-distance score + candle-body score + close-position score + volume score. Compression score is clamp((compression% − minimum compression%) / (80 − minimum compression%), 0, 1) × 22. Apex score is (0.45 × clamp(1 − |bars-to-apex − 18| / maximum-forward-apex-bars, 0, 1) + 0.55 × clamp(1 − current-width-in-ATR / maximum-current-width-ATR, 0, 1)) × 22. Breakout-distance score = clamp(distance beyond boundary / (1.25 × ATR), 0, 1) × 22. Candle-body score = clamp(|close − open| / (high − low), 0, 1) × 16. Close-position score = clamp((close − low) / (high − low), 0, 1) × 8 for bullish breakouts, or clamp((high − close) / (high − low), 0, 1) × 8 for bearish breakouts. Volume score = clamp((volume / average volume − 1) / (full-score volume ratio − 1), 0, 1) × 10 when volume participation is enabled; otherwise 7.
Calculation: Breakout event = bullish or bearish boundary break, and either the triangle structure is new or the prior breakout is older than the cooldown, and rounded breakout quality score meets the minimum label score.
Calculation: Retest band = projected broken boundary ± ATR × retest tolerance. Retest window runs from the minimum through maximum bars after the breakout. A retest touches the band when the bar's high and low overlap it.
Long: Valid triangle AND close > current upper boundary + ATR × minimum break distance AND prior close ≤ prior upper boundary OR active bullish breakout AND retest age is within the retest window AND bar touches the retest band AND close > projected broken upper boundary AND low ≥ projected broken upper boundary − ATR × retest tolerance; breakout condition must also satisfy Breakout event.
Short: Valid triangle AND close < current lower boundary − ATR × minimum break distance AND prior close ≥ prior lower boundary OR active bearish breakout AND retest age is within the retest window AND bar touches the retest band AND close < projected broken lower boundary AND high ≤ projected broken lower boundary + ATR × retest tolerance; breakout condition must also satisfy Breakout event.
Exit: long, active bullish breakout AND bars since breakout > 0 AND close < projected broken upper boundary − ATR × retest tolerance.
Exit: short, active bearish breakout AND bars since breakout > 0 AND close > projected broken lower boundary + ATR × retest tolerance.
