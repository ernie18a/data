Calculation: DOM buyer percentage = 100 × estimated buy volume summed over the DOM lookback / estimated buy and sell volume summed over the DOM lookback; per bar, estimated buy volume is volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half the volume if high = low, and estimated sell volume is the remainder. Exclude bars outside the configured session when session filtering is enabled.
Calculation: Aggressive buyer share = 100 × aggressive buy volume summed over the tape lookback / (aggressive buy volume + aggressive sell volume summed over the tape lookback); per bar, close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 if high = low; aggressive buy volume = volume × 2 × max(close position − 0.5, 0), and aggressive sell volume = volume × 2 × max(0.5 − close position, 0). Exclude bars outside the configured session when session filtering is enabled.
Long: within the configured session when session filtering is enabled, DOM buyer percentage > 50% OR DOM buyer percentage > the configured DOM alert threshold OR aggressive buyer share > 50% OR aggressive buyer share > the configured tape alert threshold OR (DOM buyer percentage > 50% AND aggressive buyer share > 50%) OR (volume ≥ the configured big-contract threshold AND close > open). For big-contract detection, evaluate each completed bar on the chart timeframe or, when selected, each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: within the configured session when session filtering is enabled, DOM buyer percentage < 50% OR DOM buyer percentage < (100% − configured DOM alert threshold) OR aggressive buyer share < 50% OR aggressive buyer share < (100% − configured tape alert threshold) OR (DOM buyer percentage < 50% AND aggressive buyer share < 50%) OR (volume ≥ the configured big-contract threshold AND close < open). For big-contract detection, evaluate each completed bar on the chart timeframe or, when selected, each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
