Calculation: Structure = On the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, confirm swing highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks. Replace each protected high or low with the latest confirmed corresponding swing and mark it unbroken. On each completed structure bar, a high break occurs when the selected close or high is strictly above the unbroken protected high; a low break occurs when the selected close or low is strictly below the unbroken protected low. Initially the state is neutral. In neutral state, a high break takes priority over a simultaneous low break and sets the state bullish; otherwise a low break sets it bearish. In bullish state, a low break takes priority, sets the state bearish and produces a bearish event; otherwise a high break produces a bullish event. In bearish state, a high break takes priority, sets the state bullish and produces a bullish event; otherwise a low break produces a bearish event. Mark the broken level as broken until replaced by a newly confirmed swing. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar returned within each chart bar. An event is new when it is nonzero and the previous chart bar had no event or its event's swing time differs from the previous chart bar's.
Long: Close >= chart-timeframe EMA of the selected OHLC-derived source
Short: Close < chart-timeframe EMA of the selected OHLC-derived source
Long: Close >= EMA of the selected OHLC-derived source on any selected higher timeframe, using its last completed bar
Short: Close < EMA of the selected OHLC-derived source on any selected higher timeframe, using its last completed bar
Long: Supertrend direction is bullish on the chart timeframe or on any selected higher or lower timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe intrabar within the chart bar
Short: Supertrend direction is bearish on the chart timeframe or on any selected higher or lower timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe intrabar within the chart bar
Long: Structure state is bullish OR a new bullish structure event occurs
Short: Structure state is bearish OR a new bearish structure event occurs
