Calculation: Each of five preset averaging-order prices = base entry reduced by that order’s fixed percentage deviation; deviations increase rung by rung
Long: In a long position AND fewer than five averaging orders have filled AND close is at or below the next averaging-order price
Exit: Long, close is at or above average entry price increased by the fixed take-profit percentage
