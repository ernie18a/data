Calculation: Bias level = highest high over a lookback of higher-timeframe bars minus 0.618 times the difference between that highest high and the lowest low over the same lookback; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, and require the difference to be greater than zero.
Calculation: ATR extreme levels = SMA of close plus or minus a multiplier times ATR, calculated on the chart timeframe or on the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe.
Long: Close > bias level.
Short: Close < bias level.
Long: Close crosses below the lower ATR extreme level, with previous close >= the previous lower ATR extreme level.
Short: Close crosses above the upper ATR extreme level, with previous close <= the previous upper ATR extreme level.
