Calculation: Bollinger-inside-Keltner squeeze = upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel AND lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel; Bollinger Bands use a simple moving average of close ± a standard-deviation multiple, and Keltner Channels use a simple moving average of close ± an ATR multiple
Calculation: KNN upward percentage = percentage of the K nearest stored completed squeezes whose close at the end of the outcome window is above the close at release; nearest neighbors use Euclidean distance across normalized squeeze duration, maximum compression depth, average volume-to-20-bar-average-volume ratio, momentum divided by ATR, and ATR divided by its baseline average. Normalize these features respectively as min(1, duration/40), clamp(compression, 0, 1), min(1, average volume ratio/3), clamp(momentum/ATR, -1, 1), and min(1, ATR/baseline ATR average/3). Momentum is the current 20-bar linear-regression value of close minus its value one bar earlier. Store a squeeze outcome after the outcome window; a close equal to the release close counts as down. Use up to K nearest stored squeezes.
Long: During a squeeze, stored squeeze count ≥ minimum training count AND KNN upward percentage ≥ 50 OR on squeeze release, squeeze duration ≥ minimum duration AND stored squeeze count ≥ minimum training count AND KNN upward percentage ≥ 50
Short: During a squeeze, stored squeeze count ≥ minimum training count AND KNN upward percentage < 50 OR on squeeze release, squeeze duration ≥ minimum duration AND stored squeeze count ≥ minimum training count AND KNN upward percentage < 50
