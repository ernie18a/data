Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band AND Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band, using close SMA for each center, close standard deviation for Bollinger width, and ATR for Keltner width.
Calculation: Squeeze features = duration capped after division by a duration scale; maximum during-squeeze compression depth clamped to [0,1], where compression depth is 1 minus Bollinger width divided by Keltner width; average during-squeeze volume divided by its SMA, divided by a volume scale and capped at 1; current linear-regression slope of close divided by current ATR and clamped to [-1,1]; current ATR divided by its SMA and a volatility scale, capped at 1. If the volume SMA is not positive, use 1 for that bar's volume ratio. At release, duration is the number of squeeze bars and momentum and volatility use the release bar. During a squeeze, duration is the number of bars since its first bar. Outside a squeeze, live duration is 0, live average volume ratio is 1, and compression is the maximum from the most recently tracked squeeze.
Calculation: Training samples = on each qualifying squeeze release, if no earlier release is awaiting an outcome, save its release features and close. After the outcome window, label the sample upward if that bar's close > release close, otherwise downward. Retain at most the maximum training sample count, removing oldest samples first. Process a release before grading an outcome on the same bar.
Calculation: Upward vote fraction = among the available training samples with the smallest Euclidean distances from the current feature vector, up to the neighbor count, the fraction labeled upward. Break equal-distance ties using the source selection-sort order over samples from oldest to newest. Before the minimum training sample count is reached, use one-half.
Long: A squeeze has just ended AND its duration >= minimum duration AND no earlier release is awaiting an outcome AND release upward vote fraction >= one-half.
Short: A squeeze has just ended AND its duration >= minimum duration AND no earlier release is awaiting an outcome AND release upward vote fraction < one-half.
Long: Training sample count >= minimum training sample count AND live upward vote fraction >= one-half.
Short: Training sample count >= minimum training sample count AND live upward vote fraction < one-half.
