Long: close > open AND high >= previous bar high AND low <= previous bar low AND close > previous bar open AND volume > 1.3 × previous bar volume AND close > EMA(close, lookback) AND (bar time is 09:30–16:00 or 20:00–03:00 America/New_York) AND at least the cooldown lookback bars have elapsed since the last non-flat bar (initially satisfied) AND bar is confirmed AND position is flat
Short: close < open AND high >= previous bar high AND low <= previous bar low AND close < previous bar open AND volume > 1.3 × previous bar volume AND close < EMA(close, lookback) AND (bar time is 09:30–16:00 or 20:00–03:00 America/New_York) AND at least the cooldown lookback bars have elapsed since the last non-flat bar (initially satisfied) AND bar is confirmed AND position is flat
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop distance OR high >= entry price + first target distance OR high >= entry price + second target distance; after close >= entry price + stop distance, exit remaining quantity if low <= entry price
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price − first target distance OR low <= entry price − second target distance; after close <= entry price − stop distance, exit remaining quantity if high >= entry price
