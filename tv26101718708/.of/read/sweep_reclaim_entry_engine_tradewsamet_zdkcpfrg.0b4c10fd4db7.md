Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low at a candidate bar that is respectively no lower or no higher than the selected number of bars to its left and strictly higher or lower than the selected number to its right; it becomes available only after the right-side bars close.
Calculation: Level memory = maintain separate chronological lists of confirmed pivot highs and lows. On confirmation, add a pivot unless its distance from any retained level on the same side is at most the merge tolerance times current ATR; then remove the oldest levels exceeding the per-side capacity. A level starts fresh with zero attempts. Remove it when its age from the pivot bar exceeds the maximum age, when consumed, or when its reclaimed hold period expires. A fresh level becomes armed when the current high is at least the high level minus the arming distance times ATR, or the current low is at most the low level plus that distance; both fresh and armed levels are eligible for sweeps. Remove an unswept high level when close exceeds it by more than the breakout distance times ATR, and an unswept low level when close falls below it by more than that distance. A swept level remains reserved until its sweep age exceeds the greater of the sweep timeout and reclaim window; then remove it if first-penetration-only is enabled, otherwise rearm it and clear its sweep metadata. After a successful reclaim, retain the level until its reclaim age exceeds the hold period. Remove a filtered selected reclaim when filtered-reclaim consumption is enabled.
Calculation: Sweep candidate = on a closed bar, a fresh or armed high level has high > level AND previous high <= level, or a fresh or armed low level has low < level AND previous low >= level. Depth is respectively (high - level) / ATR or (level - low) / ATR and must be between the minimum and maximum depth, inclusive; use the greater of the configured maximum and minimum as the effective maximum. A high sweep is body type when max(open, close) > level, otherwise wick type; a low sweep is body type when min(open, close) < level, otherwise wick type. The type must match the selected wick-only, body-only, or either mode, and attempts must be zero when first-penetration-only is enabled. Before updating levels, select the valid candidate with smallest depth on each side; optionally block both sides if both have candidates. Process all valid levels or only the selected level according to the multi-level setting. Increment attempts on every new penetration, including unselected or blocked ones; remove a level after an unaccepted penetration when first-penetration-only is enabled. An accepted sweep stores its bar, depth, extreme, and type and changes the level to swept. Apply age and other level removals before accepting the sweep.
Calculation: Sweep importance = clamp to its maximum the sum of an age component increasing linearly to its cap over a reference based on the combined left and right pivot lengths; a depth component decreasing linearly with distance from an interior preferred depth within the allowed depth range; an interaction component that is larger for the first attempt; a pivot-structure component that increases with pivot mode or combined custom pivot lengths; and a type component that is larger for a wick than a body sweep. The component caps, preferred depth, age reference, and weights are parameters.
Calculation: Reclaim quality = round and clamp the sum of these parameter-weighted components: sweep-depth quality peaks at an interior preferred normalized depth and falls linearly with distance from it; directional close-location quality increases linearly between lower and upper close-location bounds; wick quality is the current directional wick ratio scaled and capped for a same-bar reclaim, or the greater of a type-dependent stored-sweep baseline and a scaled current wick quality for a later reclaim; freshness is a power of one minus capped level age divided by maximum level age; volume quality is capped relative volume above a baseline, with a neutral value for unavailable volume and a full value when volume scoring is disabled; displacement combines capped body size / ATR and capped absolute reclaim distance / ATR; market quality combines a peaked candle-range / ATR score and a capped ATR / SMA(ATR) regime score; cleanliness decreases in steps with the number of penetration attempts. Relative volume is volume / SMA(volume). Directional close location is (close - low) / range for long and (high - close) / range for short; directional wick ratio is (min(open, close) - low) / range for long and (high - max(open, close)) / range for short. Range and ATR are floored by a configurable minimum price increment.
Calculation: Qualified reclaim candidate = a swept level whose sweep age is between zero and the reclaim window inclusive AND, for a low level, close >= level + ATR times the reclaim buffer AND (close - low) / range >= the close-location threshold; for a high level, close <= level - ATR times the reclaim buffer AND (high - close) / range >= the close-location threshold; AND absolute candle body / ATR >= the body threshold AND, if directional body is required, close > open for a low level or close < open for a high level AND, if same-bar wick confirmation is required and sweep age is zero, the directional wick ratio >= its threshold AND reclaim quality >= the minimum quality. Select one candidate per side by highest quality, breaking ties by smaller absolute close-to-level distance, or select the nearest level; remaining ties retain the oldest stored level.
Calculation: Market efficiency = absolute change in close over the efficiency lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, capped at one; use zero if the denominator is zero. Volatility ratio = ATR / SMA(ATR), or one if the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Stop and target = at a qualifying trade-model entry, store entry at the signal-bar close and ATR from that bar. For a long, stop is either entry minus ATR times the stop multiplier or the selected sweep-bar low minus ATR times the stop buffer; risk is entry minus stop and target is entry plus risk times the reward-to-risk multiplier. For a short, stop is either entry plus ATR times the stop multiplier or the selected sweep-bar high plus ATR times the stop buffer; risk is stop minus entry and target is entry minus risk times the reward-to-risk multiplier. Open the model only if risk exceeds the configurable minimum price increment, no model is active, and reopening on the exit bar is allowed or no model closed on that bar.
Long: A confirmed pivot low becomes available.
Short: A confirmed pivot high becomes available.
Long: An accepted low-level sweep occurs AND, if sweep importance filtering is selected, sweep importance >= its threshold.
Short: An accepted high-level sweep occurs AND, if sweep importance filtering is selected, sweep importance >= its threshold.
Long: A qualified low-level reclaim candidate is selected AND long direction is permitted AND, if chart EMA bias is enabled, close > chart EMA AND, if slope agreement is enabled, chart EMA > its previous chart-bar value AND, if higher-timeframe EMA bias is enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND, if slope agreement is enabled, that EMA > its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar AND, if volatility filtering is enabled, the volatility ratio is within its configured bounds AND, if efficiency filtering is enabled, market efficiency >= its threshold AND, if active-trade blocking is enabled, no trade model remains active after this bar's exit check AND, if exit-bar signals are disabled, no trade model closed on this bar AND, if cooldown is enabled, bars since the last applicable any-direction or same-direction signal > the cooldown AND, if clustering is enabled and a previous long signal exists, abs(close - previous long signal close) / ATR >= the distance threshold AND, if duplicate blocking is enabled, the last signal bar differs from the current bar AND, if a short reclaim candidate also exists, dual-side reclaim blocking is disabled and conflict resolution selects long by priority or by strictly higher quality.
Short: A qualified high-level reclaim candidate is selected AND short direction is permitted AND, if chart EMA bias is enabled, close < chart EMA AND, if slope agreement is enabled, chart EMA < its previous chart-bar value AND, if higher-timeframe EMA bias is enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND, if slope agreement is enabled, that EMA < its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar AND, if volatility filtering is enabled, the volatility ratio is within its configured bounds AND, if efficiency filtering is enabled, market efficiency >= its threshold AND, if active-trade blocking is enabled, no trade model remains active after this bar's exit check AND, if exit-bar signals are disabled, no trade model closed on this bar AND, if cooldown is enabled, bars since the last applicable any-direction or same-direction signal > the cooldown AND, if clustering is enabled and a previous short signal exists, abs(close - previous short signal close) / ATR >= the distance threshold AND, if duplicate blocking is enabled, the last signal bar differs from the current bar AND, if a long reclaim candidate also exists, dual-side reclaim blocking is disabled and conflict resolution selects short by priority or by strictly higher quality.
Exit: long, bar index > entry bar index AND (low <= stored stop OR high >= stored target).
Exit: short, bar index > entry bar index AND (high >= stored stop OR low <= stored target).
