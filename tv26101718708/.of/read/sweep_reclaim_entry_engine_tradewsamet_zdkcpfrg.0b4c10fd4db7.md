Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured left and right pivot lookback; confirm only after all right-side bars close, and store its price as a level.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Valid high sweep = on a confirmed bar, high > stored swing high and previous high <= that level, with (high − level) / ATR within configured minimum and maximum depth; valid low sweep = low < stored swing low and previous low >= that level, with (level − low) / ATR within those bounds. Apply configured wick/body penetration mode and first-interaction rule; select the configured valid level, and reject simultaneous high- and low-side sweeps when dual-side blocking is enabled.
Calculation: Bull close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick); bear close location = (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick). Body strength = abs(close − open) / ATR; lower-wick ratio = (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick); upper-wick ratio = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Reclaim quality score = rounded 0–100 sum of: sweep-depth score (15 points, highest at 35% of the configured min-to-max depth interval, declining linearly to zero at either end); directional close score (20 × clamp((directional close location − 0.45)/0.55, 0, 1)); wick score (15 × clamp(wick ratio/0.45, 0, 1), using the current rejection wick on the sweep bar, or the greater of 0.72 for wick sweeps / 0.42 for body sweeps and 0.65 × current wick quality on later bars); freshness score (10 × (1 − clamp(level age / maximum age, 0, 1))^0.65); relative-volume score (10 × clamp((volume / its longer-lookback average − 0.55)/1.25, 0, 1), or neutral 5 when volume is unavailable; 10 if excluded); displacement score (15 × (0.65 × clamp(body strength/0.55, 0, 1) + 0.35 × clamp(abs(close − level)/ATR/0.45, 0, 1))); market score (10 × (0.60 × range quality + 0.40 × volatility-regime quality), where range quality is clamp(range/ATR/1.20, 0, 1) up to 1.20 ATR then declines linearly to zero at 4 ATR, and volatility-regime quality is clamp((ATR / its longer-lookback average − 0.65)/0.85, 0, 1)); cleanliness score = 5, 3, or 1 for at most 1, exactly 2, or more than 2 level interactions.
Calculation: EMA bias = close above/below the chart-timeframe EMA, optionally requiring its slope up/down; higher-timeframe bias uses the last completed higher-timeframe close and EMA, optionally requiring the EMA slope up/down. Combined mode requires both biases to agree.
Calculation: Market filter = configured volatility condition (ATR / its longer-lookback average within configured bounds) and/or efficiency condition (abs(close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute close changes over N bars >= configured minimum), as selected.
Calculation: Long stop/target = configured ATR-distance below entry or below the stored sweep low extreme with an ATR buffer; target = entry + (entry − stop) × configured reward/risk. Short stop/target = configured ATR-distance above entry or above the stored sweep high extreme with an ATR buffer; target = entry − (stop − entry) × configured reward/risk.
Long: A stored, eligible swing low has a valid sweep; within the configured reclaim window from its sweep bar, close >= level + ATR × configured reclaim buffer AND bull close location >= configured threshold AND body strength >= configured minimum AND (directional candle requirement is off OR close > open) AND (not a same-bar reclaim OR same-bar wick requirement is off OR lower-wick ratio >= configured threshold); reclaim quality score >= configured minimum; configured direction, EMA bias, market filter, cooldown, signal-distance cluster, duplicate-bar, active-trade, exit-bar, and opposite-signal-conflict checks pass.
Short: A stored, eligible swing high has a valid sweep; within the configured reclaim window from its sweep bar, close <= level − ATR × configured reclaim buffer AND bear close location >= configured threshold AND body strength >= configured minimum AND (directional candle requirement is off OR close < open) AND (not a same-bar reclaim OR same-bar wick requirement is off OR upper-wick ratio >= configured threshold); reclaim quality score >= configured minimum; configured direction, EMA bias, market filter, cooldown, signal-distance cluster, duplicate-bar, active-trade, exit-bar, and opposite-signal-conflict checks pass.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= target; if both occur on one bar, apply configured stop-first or target-first priority.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= target; if both occur on one bar, apply configured stop-first or target-first priority.
