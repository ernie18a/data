Calculation: Active liquidity levels = confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs (buyside) and swing lows (sellside), each confirmed after the pivot lookback bars to its right; merge same-side levels within an ATR-based tolerance, and deactivate levels when swept or broken
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / its simple moving average over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Sweep-bar confluence score = count of enabled filters met: bullish EMA stack (fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA) for long setups or bearish EMA stack (fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA) for short setups; relative volume at or above its threshold
Long: On a confirmed bar, low < an active sellside level − minimum pierce distance (ATR × configured threshold) AND close > that level; then, within the confirmation window on a later confirmed bar, close > the sweep bar high AND no intervening confirmed close < the sweep bar low AND the sweep-bar confluence score meets the configured minimum
Short: On a confirmed bar, high > an active buyside level + minimum pierce distance (ATR × configured threshold) AND close < that level; then, within the confirmation window on a later confirmed bar, close < the sweep bar low AND no intervening confirmed close > the sweep bar high AND the sweep-bar confluence score meets the configured minimum
