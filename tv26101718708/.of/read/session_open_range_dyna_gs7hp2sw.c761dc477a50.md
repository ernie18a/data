Calculation: Opening range high and low = the highest high and lowest low of the current session's first chart bar and all subsequent chart bars whose opening timestamps are less than the configured opening-range duration after the first session bar's opening timestamp.
Long: The completed chart bar is in the session AND its opening timestamp is at least the opening-range duration after the first session bar's opening timestamp AND close > opening range high AND no earlier completed bar after the opening range formed in this session closed > opening range high.
Short: The completed chart bar is in the session AND its opening timestamp is at least the opening-range duration after the first session bar's opening timestamp AND close < opening range low AND no earlier completed bar after the opening range formed in this session closed < opening range low.
