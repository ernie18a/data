Calculation: Range box = a range formed when the long-period weighted moving average of absolute candle bodies crosses below the long-period exponential moving average of absolute candle bodies; its boundaries are the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, with each pivot using equal left and right lookbacks. A box is created only when the close is within its boundaries and overlap rules permit it; it remains active until a close breaks above or below it.
Calculation: Box context = a same-direction box break within the configured recent-break window, or a close inside an active box; when box context is required, at least one must hold.
Calculation: Box location = for box top T and bottom B, equilibrium is B + 0.50(T−B), premium boundary is B + 0.75(T−B), and discount boundary is B + 0.25(T−B). Long no-chase location is close ≤ B + 0.75(T−B); short no-chase location is close ≥ B + 0.25(T−B). Use the most recent broken box, or the active box if no broken-box levels are available.
Calculation: Prior swing high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Local impulse = for long, close minus the lowest low over the configured lookback; for short, the highest high over that lookback minus close. Primary candle quality = local impulse ≥ its configured minimum AND adverse wick ≤ its configured maximum AND, if required, candle body points in the signal direction.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range interval. Pulse direction = sign of the last close during the configured initial pulse interval relative to the session's first open. Session high/low = highest high / lowest low since that open.
Calculation: Base = highest high and lowest low over the configured base lookback, excluding the current bar; base range = base high minus base low.
Calculation: Long continuation = active direction is long AND the high-to-rolling-low impulse meets its configured minimum AND the pullback from the rolling high is within its configured minimum and maximum. Short continuation = active direction is short AND the rolling-high-to-low impulse meets its configured minimum AND the pullback from the rolling low is within its configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Long base breakout = long continuation AND base range is within its configured maximum AND close > base high + entry buffer. Short base breakdown = short continuation AND base range is within its configured maximum AND close < base low − entry buffer.
Calculation: Long pullback rejection = long continuation AND low ≤ prior low AND close ≥ low + max(minimum rejection distance, one quarter of the adverse wick). Short pullback rejection = short continuation AND high ≥ prior high AND close ≤ high − max(minimum rejection distance, one quarter of the adverse wick).
Calculation: Fair-value gap = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier; bearish when current high < low two bars earlier. Its midpoint is the average of those two gap boundaries. A gap is armed only in the active direction during the configured session interval; it expires after the configured retest window.
Calculation: Long gap retest = an armed bullish gap is retested when low ≤ its midpoint and close ≥ its midpoint. Short gap retest = an armed bearish gap is retested when high ≥ its midpoint and close ≤ its midpoint.
Calculation: PD long trigger = low ≤ box equilibrium AND close > prior swing high using the configured micro-break lookback; PD short trigger = high ≥ box equilibrium AND close < prior swing low using that lookback. If reclaim/reject is disabled, omit the equilibrium touch condition.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = within the configured entry session AND below the daily marker limit AND outside the configured spacing from the last marker and last same-side marker.
Calculation: Box filters = require box context when configured; require a box-edge touch/retest when configured; apply the box-location no-chase filter when configured. For long, an active-box edge touch means low ≤ active box bottom + buffer; a broken-box retest means low ≤ broken box top + buffer. For short, these mean high ≥ active box top − buffer and high ≥ broken box bottom − buffer.
Long: Entry eligibility AND box filters AND (PD long trigger AND (a same-direction box break occurred within the configured PD window AND close ≤ premium boundary OR inside an active box AND close ≤ equilibrium) AND per-box long marker count is below its limit AND (primary candle quality if required) OR bullish gap retest AND (primary candle quality OR lower-tier entries are enabled and the session is early or a direction flip is recent) AND configured mode permits this family and tier OR long base breakout OR (long continuation AND pullback rejection AND primary candle quality), with continuation unused AND the configured mode permitting this family and tier OR a late long flip: active direction is short, session range meets the configured flip-move minimum, flip cooldown has elapsed, a low extreme occurred within the recent-extreme window, close > prior swing high + entry buffer, close ≥ open, session elapsed time meets the configured minimum, and close has risen from the leg low by the configured minimum; then configured mode permits the primary flip entry OR context markers are enabled and entry eligibility holds.
Short: Entry eligibility AND box filters AND (PD short trigger AND (a same-direction box break occurred within the configured PD window AND close ≥ discount boundary OR inside an active box AND close ≥ equilibrium) AND per-box short marker count is below its limit AND (primary candle quality if required) OR bearish gap retest AND (primary candle quality OR lower-tier entries are enabled and the session is early or a direction flip is recent) AND configured mode permits this family and tier OR short base breakdown OR (short continuation AND pullback rejection AND primary candle quality), with continuation unused AND the configured mode permitting this family and tier OR a late short flip: active direction is long, session range meets the configured flip-move minimum, flip cooldown has elapsed, a high extreme occurred within the recent-extreme window, close < prior swing low − entry buffer, close ≤ open, session elapsed time meets the configured minimum, and the leg high has fallen by the configured minimum; then configured mode permits the primary flip entry OR context markers are enabled and entry eligibility holds.
Long: During the configured initial post-open interval, before any A or B event, session low ≤ opening range low AND close > prior swing high + entry buffer AND pulse direction is short AND context markers are enabled AND box context holds AND entry eligibility holds.
Short: During the configured initial post-open interval, before any A or B event, session high ≥ opening range high AND close < prior swing low − entry buffer AND pulse direction is long AND context markers are enabled AND box context holds AND entry eligibility holds.
