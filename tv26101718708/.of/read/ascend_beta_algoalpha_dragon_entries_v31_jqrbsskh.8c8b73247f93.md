Calculation: Box = When the weighted moving average of absolute candle body crosses below its exponential moving average using the same lookback, take the most recently confirmed symmetric pivot high or low. If it is a high, set the box top to that pivot and the bottom to the lowest low after the pivot through the current bar; if it is a low, set the bottom to that pivot and the top to the highest high after the pivot through the current bar. Create the box if close is within its bounds and, when overlap prevention is enabled, the pivot is later than the right edge of the most recently created box. Extend active boxes until close breaks either boundary, then remove them. Process simultaneous breaks from oldest to newest, retaining the last processed box and direction as the latest break. Reset latest-break context daily, but retain active boxes.
Calculation: Box context = For each side, context passes when its filter is disabled, or the latest break was in that direction within its lookback, or close is inside the newest active box. The optional edge filter passes when disabled, or the newest active box exists and low is within a buffer above its bottom for longs or high is within a buffer below its top for shorts, or a same-side recent break exists and low is within a buffer above the context top for longs or high is within a buffer below the context bottom for shorts. Context bounds are the latest broken box bounds if available, otherwise the newest active box bounds. The optional no-chase filter passes when disabled, context bounds are unavailable or invalid, or close is at or below the upper box fraction for longs or at or above the lower box fraction for shorts. The upper fraction exceeds the lower fraction.
Calculation: Mark eligibility = The entry window is open, the daily mark limit is not reached, and the minimum bar intervals since the last mark of either side and since the last mark of this side have elapsed. Every emitted PD, A, C, D, X or E mark updates these counts and times, including context and lower-confidence marks. Reset them daily. Evaluate marks in source order: PD long, PD short, A, D, C, X short, X long, E.
Calculation: Session state = Reset daily. From the first eligible bar at or after the session reference time, record its open, update session high and low on eligible bars, record the opening-range high and low during the opening-range interval, and record the last close during the shorter pulse interval. After the pulse interval, initialize direction long if pulse close >= session open, otherwise short; initialize leg high and low to the current high and low and continuation extreme to the directional high or low. While the window is open, update leg high and low. At each A reversal, B confirmation or D reversal, set direction and switch direction to that event's side, record its bar as the switch and last flip, reset leg high and low and continuation extreme to the current bar, and clear continuation-used. A and B can each occur at most once daily; A prevents B, while B does not prevent a later A. A occurs after the opening range and before its deadline when the opposite pulse direction and the corresponding A price conditions hold. B occurs after the opening range and before its deadline when pulse direction matches the side, close breaks the corresponding opening-range boundary by the entry buffer, and the candle body agrees with the side. A and B update state even if no mark is emitted. For long direction, a high at least a reset distance above the continuation extreme resets that extreme to high, resets leg high and low to the current bar, and clears continuation-used; mirror for shorts using low. A C or X mark sets continuation-used; an E mark clears it. Process state changes in the order A, B, D, continuation reset, gap arming, C, X, E.
Calculation: Primary quality = For a long, close minus the lowest low over the local lookback is at least the impulse threshold and close minus low is at most the adverse-wick threshold; for a short, the highest high over that lookback minus close is at least the impulse threshold and high minus close is at most the adverse-wick threshold. If the optional body filter is enabled, close >= open for a long and close <= open for a short.
Calculation: Gap retest = During the allowed session interval, a long gap is armed when direction is long and low > high two bars earlier; its midpoint is the average of those two prices. A short gap is armed when direction is short and high < low two bars earlier; its midpoint is their average. A newer same-side gap replaces the previous one. Within the retest limit, a long retest occurs when low <= midpoint and close >= midpoint; a short retest occurs when high >= midpoint and close <= midpoint. Disarm on the first qualifying retest even if no mark is emitted; reset daily.
Calculation: Trend and base = Long trend requires long direction and current high minus the lowest low over the impulse lookback >= the impulse threshold; short trend requires short direction and the highest high over that lookback minus current low >= the impulse threshold. The base is the highest high and lowest low over the base lookback excluding the current bar. A long base break requires long trend, base range <= its limit, and close > base high plus the entry buffer; a short base break requires short trend, base range <= its limit, and close < base low minus the entry buffer. A long pullback rejection requires long trend, the impulse-lookback highest high minus current low within the pullback bounds, low <= previous low, and close - low >= the larger of a fixed minimum rejection distance and a fraction of close - low. A short pullback rejection mirrors this using current high minus the impulse-lookback lowest low, high >= previous high, and high - close.
Calculation: D reversal = A long reversal requires short direction, session high minus session low >= the flip-move threshold, sufficient bars since the last flip, the lowest low in the longer recent-extreme lookback occurring within the shorter recent-extreme interval, close > the prior-bar highest high over the flip-break lookback plus the entry buffer, close >= open, elapsed session time >= the flip minimum, and close minus leg low >= the leg-move threshold. A short reversal mirrors these conditions using long direction, the highest high, close below the prior lowest low minus the buffer, close <= open, and leg high minus close. These reversals update session state even when no mark is emitted.
Long: Close > an active box top.
Short: Close < an active box bottom.
