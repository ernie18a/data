Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured session; retain the completed range until the next session starts.
Calculation: Asia bias = direction of the first confirmed close above the completed Asia session high or below its low after the session ends, before or at the signal-window end, and, if configured, at or after the signal-window start; require the Asia range size to be within the optional inclusive minimum and maximum, expressed either in price units or as percentages of the current close. Reset at the start of each Asia session.
Calculation: Bias alignment = at the bias bar, for each enabled higher timeframe, compare the last completed bar's close with its EMA using the same EMA length; long alignment requires close > EMA and short alignment requires close <= EMA. The two higher timeframes have different durations, one longer than the other; retain the result until the next Asia session.
Calculation: Initial stop = for an Asia-break entry, the opposite Asia range edge or entry close minus ATR times a multiplier for a long, plus ATR times a multiplier for a short; for a London-continuation entry, the London opening range low minus a buffer for a long or high plus a buffer for a short. Initial risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus initial risk times the selected target multiple for a long, or minus it for a short; single-target and three scaled-target multiples are independently configurable.
Calculation: Managed stop = initial stop; in single-target mode optionally replace it with entry price after a long high reaches entry price plus initial risk or a short low reaches entry price minus initial risk. In scaled mode, after each of the first two targets is reached by the high for a long or low for a short, optionally tighten the stop to entry price or the reached first target, or after the second target to the second target; never loosen it.
Calculation: Runner stop = after the configured first or second target is reached, the greater of the managed stop and the highest trailing candidate since activation for a long, or the lesser of the managed stop and the lowest trailing candidate since activation for a short. A trailing candidate is current high minus trailing distance for a long or current low plus trailing distance for a short; trailing distance is initial risk times a multiplier or current ATR times a multiplier. Before activation, use the managed stop.
Long: Asia bias first becomes long on this bar.
Short: Asia bias first becomes short on this bar.
Long: Current low minus high two bars ago > configured minimum gap, expressed in price units or as a percentage of current close.
Short: Low two bars ago minus current high > configured minimum gap, expressed in price units or as a percentage of current close.
Long: Asia-break mode enabled AND Asia bias first becomes long on this bar AND bar opening time is at or before the break-window end AND entry date, optional weekday and optional entry-time filters pass AND position is flat AND optional one-trade-per-Asia-session limit passes AND long entries are enabled AND initial risk > 0.
Short: Asia-break mode enabled AND Asia bias first becomes short on this bar AND bar opening time is at or before the break-window end AND entry date, optional weekday and optional entry-time filters pass AND position is flat AND optional one-trade-per-Asia-session limit passes AND short entries are enabled AND initial risk > 0.
Long: London-continuation mode enabled AND London opening range is complete AND bars since its last bar <= configured maximum AND bar opening time is before the configured exit hour AND Asia bias is long AND bias alignment passes AND close > London opening range high AND entry date, optional weekday and optional entry-time filters pass AND position is flat AND optional one-trade-per-Asia-session limit passes AND long entries are enabled AND no London-continuation entry has fired since the Asia session began AND no eligible Asia-break entry takes priority AND initial risk > 0.
Short: London-continuation mode enabled AND London opening range is complete AND bars since its last bar <= configured maximum AND bar opening time is before the configured exit hour AND Asia bias is short AND bias alignment passes AND close < London opening range low AND entry date, optional weekday and optional entry-time filters pass AND position is flat AND optional one-trade-per-Asia-session limit passes AND short entries are enabled AND no London-continuation entry has fired since the Asia session began AND no eligible Asia-break entry takes priority AND initial risk > 0.
Exit: long, low <= previously submitted managed stop OR high >= previously submitted single profit target in single-target mode.
Exit: short, high >= previously submitted managed stop OR low <= previously submitted single profit target in single-target mode.
Exit: long, in scaled mode, low <= previously submitted managed stop OR high >= previously submitted first or second profit target, for each corresponding configured position portion.
Exit: short, in scaled mode, high >= previously submitted managed stop OR low <= previously submitted first or second profit target, for each corresponding configured position portion.
Exit: long, in scaled mode, for the remaining runner portion, low <= previously submitted managed stop OR high >= previously submitted third target in fixed-target mode, OR low <= previously submitted runner stop in trailing mode.
Exit: short, in scaled mode, for the remaining runner portion, high >= previously submitted managed stop OR low <= previously submitted third target in fixed-target mode, OR high >= previously submitted runner stop in trailing mode.
Exit: long, optional time exit enabled AND current bar's opening hour equals the configured exit hour AND previous bar's opening hour differs, in the configured session timezone; OR optional backtest end enabled AND bar opening time > end date.
Exit: short, optional time exit enabled AND current bar's opening hour equals the configured exit hour AND previous bar's opening hour differs, in the configured session timezone; OR optional backtest end enabled AND bar opening time > end date.
