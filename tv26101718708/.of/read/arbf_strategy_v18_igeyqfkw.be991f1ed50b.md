Calculation: Asia range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured Asia session; complete when that session ends
Calculation: London opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured London opening-range interval; complete when that interval ends
Calculation: Initial risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop; initial stop is the opposite Asia range edge or an ATR-based distance for a range-break entry, and the opposite London range edge plus a configured buffer for a London-continuation entry
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) initial risk multiplied by the configured target multiples
Calculation: Trailing stop = highest high since trailing starts minus the configured trailing distance for a long, or lowest low plus that distance for a short; distance is configured as an initial-risk multiple or chart-timeframe ATR multiple
Long: (configured range-break mode is enabled AND Asia range is complete AND close > Asia range high on the first qualifying post-Asia breakout bar within the configured signal and entry windows) OR (configured London-continuation mode is enabled AND Asia breakout bias is long AND close > London opening range high after that range completes and within the configured bar limit and entry window AND enabled higher-timeframe EMA filters align at the bias bar using the last completed higher-timeframe bars); AND configured date, weekday, entry-time, and Asia-range-size filters pass; AND long entries are enabled; AND position is flat; AND daily trade limit permits entry
Short: (configured range-break mode is enabled AND Asia range is complete AND close < Asia range low on the first qualifying post-Asia breakout bar within the configured signal and entry windows) OR (configured London-continuation mode is enabled AND Asia breakout bias is short AND close < London opening range low after that range completes and within the configured bar limit and entry window AND enabled higher-timeframe EMA filters align at the bias bar using the last completed higher-timeframe bars); AND configured date, weekday, entry-time, and Asia-range-size filters pass; AND short entries are enabled; AND position is flat; AND daily trade limit permits entry
Exit: long, low <= current stop price OR (single-target mode AND high >= single profit target) OR (scaled-target mode AND high >= TP1 OR high >= TP2 OR (fixed TP3 mode AND high >= TP3) OR (trailing-runner mode AND low <= trailing stop)) OR configured time exit occurs OR configured backtest end date is passed
Exit: short, high >= current stop price OR (single-target mode AND low <= single profit target) OR (scaled-target mode AND low <= TP1 OR low <= TP2 OR (fixed TP3 mode AND low <= TP3) OR (trailing-runner mode AND high >= trailing stop)) OR configured time exit occurs OR configured backtest end date is passed
