Calculation: Normalization = H(x,a,b) = clamp(100 × (x − a) / (b − a), 0, 100), or 0 when a = b; L(x,a,b) = H(b − x, 0, b − a); P(x,a,b,c) = H(x,a,b) when x ≤ b, otherwise L(x,b,c).
Calculation: Impulse measurements = range = max(high − low, positive price floor); ATR = ATR of chart bars, falling back to current true range when unavailable and bounded below by the price floor; size = range / ATR; body efficiency = abs(close − open) / range; bullish edge = (close − low) / range; bearish edge = 1 − bullish edge; participation = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 if the average is unavailable or nonpositive. The short EMA and long EMA are EMAs of close; slope = long EMA minus its value a specified number of bars earlier, or 0 if unavailable.
Calculation: Impulse quality for direction d = +1 for bullish and −1 for bearish. Size score = P(size, a fraction of the minimum impulse size, minimum impulse size plus an offset, oversized impulse threshold); body score = H(body efficiency, a fraction of minimum body efficiency, body saturation threshold); edge score = H(directional edge, a fraction of minimum edge, edge saturation threshold); participation score = P(participation, low participation threshold, ideal participation threshold, climax threshold). Trend score = a higher base if d × (close − long EMA) ≥ 0, otherwise a lower base, plus a stack weight if d × (short EMA − long EMA) ≥ 0, plus a slope weight × H(abs(slope) / ATR, low slope threshold, high slope threshold) if d × slope ≥ 0; clamp to 0–100. Impulse quality is the 0–100-clamped weighted sum of size, body, edge, participation and trend scores.
Calculation: Impulse event = a qualifying directional impulse whose bar is at least a cooldown number of bars after the previous impulse event, or the first such event. A qualifying bullish impulse has close > open, size ≥ minimum impulse size, body efficiency ≥ minimum body efficiency, bullish edge ≥ minimum edge and bullish impulse quality ≥ minimum quality; a bearish impulse uses close < open and bearish edge and quality with the same other tests. Optional side selection can exclude either direction. Each event replaces the active impulse and records its direction, bar index, high, low, close, range, ATR, impulse quality and participation score. Its initial best price is its high for bullish or low for bearish; its initial worst pullback and follow-through close count are zero.
Calculation: Active follow-through = for bars after the active impulse through the tracking limit, until failure or target is reached, update best price to the highest high for bullish or lowest low for bearish; worst pullback to the maximum of its previous value and max(impulse close − low, 0) / impulse range for bullish or max(high − impulse close, 0) / impulse range for bearish; and best participation score to the maximum observed. During the follow-through window, count each close strictly beyond the impulse close in the impulse direction. Failure rail = impulse low − impulse ATR × buffer for bullish or impulse high + impulse ATR × buffer for bearish; target = impulse close + direction × impulse range × target multiple. Failure occurs when close reaches or crosses the rail; otherwise target is reached when high reaches the bullish target or low reaches the bearish target. Failure takes priority when both occur on the same bar. Freeze these measurements and the resolution close after either outcome. The active window includes the impulse bar through the tracking limit.
Calculation: Follow-through quality = progress = max(direction × (best price − impulse close), 0) / impulse ATR; target room = direction × (target − evaluation close) / impulse ATR, where evaluation close is the frozen resolution close after failure or target and current close otherwise. Pullback score = L(worst pullback, good pullback threshold, maximum pullback); progress score = H(progress, a lower fraction of required progress, a higher multiple of required progress); close-count score = H(follow-through close count, lower close-count threshold, required close count); follow-through score = the 0–100-clamped weighted sum of progress, close-count and pullback scores. Target-room score = H(target room, low room threshold, high room threshold). Overall quality = the rounded, 0–100-clamped weighted sum of impulse quality, follow-through score, best participation score, pullback score and target-room score; cap it at a failure ceiling after failure, or at a weak-follow-through ceiling when the follow-through window has elapsed and the follow-through score is below its weak threshold.
Calculation: Follow-through state = outside the active window, inactive; otherwise, in priority order: failed if failure occurred; impulse on the impulse bar; target review if target was reached; pullback risk if worst pullback ≥ maximum pullback; valid if overall quality ≥ valid threshold AND follow-through score ≥ valid follow-through threshold AND follow-through close count ≥ required close count; watch if overall quality ≥ watch threshold; weak if the follow-through window has elapsed; otherwise wait. An event occurs when the current state differs from the previous bar's state and equals the named state.
