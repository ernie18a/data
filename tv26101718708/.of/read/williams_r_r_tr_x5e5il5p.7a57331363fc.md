Calculation: Williams %R = (highest high over lookback - close) / (highest high over lookback - lowest low over lookback) * -100, on chart-timeframe bars, using the current bar
Calculation: Threshold = one of two options: (a) the midpoint of the %R scale (-50), or (b) a moving average of %R whose lookback is much longer than the %R lookback
Long: %R crosses above Threshold (previous bar %R <= previous bar Threshold AND current bar %R > current bar Threshold)
Short: %R crosses below Threshold (previous bar %R >= previous bar Threshold AND current bar %R < current bar Threshold)
