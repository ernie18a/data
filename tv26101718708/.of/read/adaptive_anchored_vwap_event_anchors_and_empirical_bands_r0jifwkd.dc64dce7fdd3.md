Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 since the selected calendar, event, date, pivot, volume, or gap anchor
Calculation: Outer bands = AVWAP adjusted by the outer percentiles of prior percentage deviations from AVWAP since the anchor; use standard-deviation bands until the minimum sample count is reached
Long: AVWAP and previous AVWAP are defined AND not the anchor bar AND bars since anchor ≥ minimum AND ((close > AVWAP AND previous close ≤ previous AVWAP AND volume > volume moving average × confirmation multiplier) OR (previous close > previous AVWAP AND low ≤ AVWAP AND close > AVWAP) OR (previous close < previous lower outer band AND close ≥ lower outer band))
