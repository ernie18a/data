Calculation: Anchor event and anchor bar = Select one mode. Calendar mode: the first available bar and the first bar of each selected calendar period; anchor at that bar. Custom-date mode: the first bar at or after the selected date; anchor at that bar. Pivot-low or pivot-high mode: when a pivot with selected left and right confirmation lengths is confirmed, anchor at the pivot bar. Latest-pivot mode: use either confirmed pivot, giving the low priority if both confirm together. Volume-high mode: anchor at the current bar when volume > 0 AND volume equals its highest value over the selected lookback including the current bar. Volume-spike mode: anchor at the current bar when the z-score of log(max(volume, 1)) against the mean and population standard deviation of that quantity over the preceding lookback is >= the selected threshold; use z-score 0 when that standard deviation is 0 or unavailable. Gap mode: anchor at the current bar when open > previous high × (1 + selected gap percentage) OR open < previous low × (1 - selected gap percentage). Corporate-event modes cannot be computed from the available fields.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = Sum(source × volume) / sum(volume) from the latest anchor bar through the current bar, using a selected price source derived from the available fields. On pivot confirmation, include all bars from the pivot through confirmation; earlier plotted values are not revised. A new anchor resets the sums.
Calculation: Outer bands = Select empirical, standard-deviation, or percentage mode. For empirical mode, retain up to a selected cap of the most recent source deviations (source - anchored VWAP) / anchored VWAP since the anchor, excluding the current bar; include deviations reconstructed from pivot to confirmation. Once the selected minimum sample count is available, multiply current anchored VWAP by 1 plus the linearly interpolated selected upper percentile of deviations for the upper band, and by 1 plus its complementary lower percentile for the lower band. Otherwise use standard-deviation bands. Standard-deviation bands are anchored VWAP plus or minus a selected multiplier times the volume-weighted population standard deviation of source since the anchor. Percentage bands are anchored VWAP multiplied by 1 plus or minus a selected percentage.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = Current and previous anchored VWAP are available AND the current bar is not an anchor event AND bars elapsed since the anchor bar >= the selected minimum.
Long: Signal eligibility AND close > anchored VWAP AND previous close <= previous anchored VWAP AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > selected multiplier × volume SMA including the current bar).
Long: Signal eligibility AND previous close > previous anchored VWAP AND low <= anchored VWAP AND close > anchored VWAP.
Long: Signal eligibility AND previous close < previous lower outer band AND close >= lower outer band.
Short: Signal eligibility AND close < anchored VWAP AND previous close >= previous anchored VWAP AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > selected multiplier × volume SMA including the current bar).
Short: Signal eligibility AND previous close < previous anchored VWAP AND high >= anchored VWAP AND close < anchored VWAP.
Short: Signal eligibility AND previous close > previous upper outer band AND close <= upper outer band.
Long: Pivot-low mode OR latest-pivot mode, AND a pivot low is confirmed.
Short: Pivot-high mode AND a pivot high is confirmed OR latest-pivot mode AND a pivot high is confirmed AND no pivot low is confirmed.
Long: Volume-high mode OR volume-spike mode, AND an anchor event occurs AND close >= open.
Short: Volume-high mode OR volume-spike mode, AND an anchor event occurs AND close < open.
Long: Gap mode AND open > previous high × (1 + selected gap percentage).
Short: Gap mode AND open < previous low × (1 - selected gap percentage).
