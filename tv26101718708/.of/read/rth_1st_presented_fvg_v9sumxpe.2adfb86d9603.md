Calculation: Gap bars = chart-timeframe bars or completed lower-timeframe bars; evaluate each lower-timeframe bar in chronological order.
Calculation: Bullish gap = gap-bar low > high two gap bars earlier AND their difference >= minimum gap size. Bearish gap = gap-bar high < low two gap bars earlier AND their difference >= minimum gap size.
Calculation: Gap bounds = for a bullish gap, initially [high two bars earlier, current low]; replace the lower bound with the earlier bar's body high if it is below the middle bar's body low, and replace the upper bound with the current bar's body low if it is above the middle bar's body high. For a bearish gap, initially [current high, low two bars earlier]; replace the upper bound with the earlier bar's body low if it is above the middle bar's body high, and replace the lower bound with the current bar's body high if it is below the middle bar's body low.
Calculation: Eligible session gap = bullish or bearish gap whose current bar is in the configured session AND whose middle bar starts at least a configured interval after the session start.
Calculation: Displacement gap = eligible session gap AND middle-bar absolute body size >= minimum body size AND, for a bullish gap, middle-bar close or high > highest high over the configured lookback ending three gap bars before the current bar, or, for a bearish gap, middle-bar close or low < lowest low over that lookback. Select close or wick comparison by parameter; use only bars from the current calendar day.
Calculation: Reference gap = first eligible session gap, replaced whenever a displacement gap occurs. Reset the reference at the start of each session; each replacement resets the reflection count.
Calculation: Overlapping gap = bullish or bearish gap whose adjusted bounds have width >= a separate minimum overlap size AND overlap the bounds of an earlier stored first session, displacement, or reflection gap. Optionally require the earlier gap to be within the selected history, the overlap to be its first since the session reset, and the earlier edge of the new gap to fall within a configured time window. On lower-timeframe bars, the gap must also be an eligible session gap after the first session gap; on chart-timeframe bars, it need not be in the session.
Long: First eligible session gap is bullish OR displacement gap is bullish AND (first displacement gap of the session OR additional displacement gaps are allowed) AND (no reflection has occurred OR displacement after reflection is allowed) OR first eligible session gap after the reference gap with direction opposite to the reference is bullish OR overlapping gap is bullish.
Short: First eligible session gap is bearish OR displacement gap is bearish AND (first displacement gap of the session OR additional displacement gaps are allowed) AND (no reflection has occurred OR displacement after reflection is allowed) OR first eligible session gap after the reference gap with direction opposite to the reference is bearish OR overlapping gap is bearish.
