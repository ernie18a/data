Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening session, accumulated until the session ends; reset when the exchange day of month changes.
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2.
Calculation: Range filter failed for the day = the minimum-range filter is enabled AND, on any bar after the opening session since the daily reset, opening range high - opening range low <= ATR × minimum-range multiplier; retain this state until the daily reset.
Calculation: Eligible bar = opening range exists AND bar is outside the opening session AND bar is within the configured entry window in the configured session timezone AND no entry has been made since the daily reset AND the session has not been cancelled AND range filter failed for the day is false AND (the minimum-range filter is disabled OR opening range high - opening range low > ATR × minimum-range multiplier).
Calculation: Breakout direction = on the first eligible bar since the daily reset whose close is above the opening range high or below the opening range low, record the corresponding direction and bar; retain it until the daily reset. On a subsequent eligible bar, cancel the session until the daily reset if an upward breakout is followed by close < opening range low or a downward breakout is followed by close > opening range high.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe VWAP = session-anchored VWAP calculated on higher-timeframe bars; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Initial risk = entry price - opening range midpoint for a long position, or opening range midpoint - entry price for a short position, using the opening range midpoint at entry.
Long: Eligible bar AND breakout direction is upward AND current bar is later than the breakout bar AND position size = 0 AND low < opening range high AND min(open, close) > opening range high AND minimum wick fraction <= (opening range high - low) / (opening range high - opening range midpoint) <= maximum wick fraction AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > ADX threshold) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe VWAP).
Short: Eligible bar AND breakout direction is downward AND current bar is later than the breakout bar AND position size = 0 AND high > opening range low AND max(open, close) < opening range low AND minimum wick fraction <= (high - opening range low) / (opening range midpoint - opening range low) <= maximum wick fraction AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > ADX threshold) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe VWAP).
Exit: long, low <= opening range midpoint at entry OR high >= entry price + initial risk × full profit multiplier; if partial profit and stop adjustment are enabled and the partial exit has occurred, replace the midpoint stop with the opening range high at entry.
Exit: short, high >= opening range midpoint at entry OR low <= entry price - initial risk × full profit multiplier; if partial profit and stop adjustment are enabled and the partial exit has occurred, replace the midpoint stop with the opening range low at entry.
Exit: long, partial profit enabled AND partial exit has not occurred AND close >= entry price + initial risk × partial profit multiplier; exit the configured fraction of the position.
Exit: short, partial profit enabled AND partial exit has not occurred AND close <= entry price - initial risk × partial profit multiplier; exit the configured fraction of the position.
