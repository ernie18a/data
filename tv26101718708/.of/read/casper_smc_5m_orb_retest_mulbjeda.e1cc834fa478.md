Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the opening-range session
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low during the opening-range session
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2
Calculation: Wick depth percentage = 100 × distance from the range boundary to the retest wick extreme / distance from that boundary to the opening-range midpoint
Calculation: Higher-timeframe VWAP = VWAP calculated from bars of a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming
Long: opening range is set AND current bar is after the opening-range session and within the entry window AND no trade has been taken that day AND session is not cancelled AND (range-size filter is disabled OR opening-range high − opening-range low > chart-timeframe ATR × configured multiplier) AND a prior bar after the opening range closed above the opening-range high AND no later bar closed below the opening-range low AND current bar low < opening-range high AND min(open, close) > opening-range high AND configured minimum wick percentage ≤ wick depth percentage ≤ configured maximum wick percentage AND (ADX filter is disabled OR chart-timeframe ADX > configured threshold) AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR close > higher-timeframe VWAP)
Short: opening range is set AND current bar is after the opening-range session and within the entry window AND no trade has been taken that day AND session is not cancelled AND (range-size filter is disabled OR opening-range high − opening-range low > chart-timeframe ATR × configured multiplier) AND a prior bar after the opening range closed below the opening-range low AND no later bar closed above the opening-range high AND current bar high > opening-range low AND max(open, close) < opening-range low AND configured minimum wick percentage ≤ wick depth percentage ≤ configured maximum wick percentage AND (ADX filter is disabled OR chart-timeframe ADX > configured threshold) AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter is disabled OR close < higher-timeframe VWAP)
Exit: Long, low ≤ opening-range midpoint OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − opening-range midpoint) × configured risk-reward ratio OR (partial take-profit is enabled AND close ≥ entry price + (entry price − opening-range midpoint) × configured partial risk-reward ratio)
Exit: Short, high ≥ opening-range midpoint OR low ≤ entry price − (opening-range midpoint − entry price) × configured risk-reward ratio OR (partial take-profit is enabled AND close ≤ entry price − (opening-range midpoint − entry price) × configured partial risk-reward ratio)
Exit: Long, partial take-profit has been reached and trailing stop is enabled AND low ≤ opening-range high
Exit: Short, partial take-profit has been reached and trailing stop is enabled AND high ≥ opening-range low
