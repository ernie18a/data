Calculation: Confirmed support/resistance trendlines = lines through pairs of confirmed swing lows/highs, respectively; evaluate pivot pairs from short-, medium-, and long-lookback windows, keep valid lines, and rank lines on each side by touch clusters, fit, violations, candle respect, wick rejection, recency, span, EMA/VWAP proximity, and higher-timeframe bias. Higher-timeframe bias uses the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR = a blend of shorter- and longer-lookback ATR with smoothed Parkinson volatility.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = adaptive ATR × a parameter; retest tolerance and rejection buffer = adaptive ATR × their respective parameters.
Calculation: Target levels = on a breakout, entry is the signal close; risk is the greater of an ATR-based risk distance and the distance from entry to the broken line plus an ATR-based structure buffer. Long stop = entry − risk and target = entry + risk × reward/risk; short stop = entry + risk and target = entry − risk × reward/risk.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a qualifying resistance trendline is crossed upward: close > current line + breakout buffer AND previous close ≤ previous line + breakout buffer AND candle body low > current line + 0.2 × breakout buffer AND close > EMA(short) AND EMA(short) ≥ EMA(medium) AND smoothed RSI ≥ 50 AND MACD histogram ≥ 0.
Long: A qualifying resistance trendline had an upward breakout within the retest window AND the current bar is after that breakout and within the window AND low ≤ current line + retest tolerance AND close > current line AND close > EMA(short) AND EMA(short) ≥ EMA(medium) AND smoothed RSI ≥ 50 AND MACD histogram ≥ 0.
Long: A qualifying support trendline is rejected: low ≤ current line + retest tolerance AND close > current line + rejection buffer AND close > open AND close > EMA(short) AND EMA(short) ≥ EMA(medium) AND smoothed RSI ≥ 50 AND MACD histogram ≥ 0.
Long: After a qualifying resistance trendline's downward-crossing breakout, while within its retest window, close < current line − 0.5 × breakout buffer.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a qualifying support trendline is crossed downward: close < current line − breakout buffer AND previous close ≥ previous line − breakout buffer AND candle body high < current line − 0.2 × breakout buffer AND close < EMA(short) AND EMA(short) ≤ EMA(medium) AND smoothed RSI ≤ 50 AND MACD histogram ≤ 0.
Short: A qualifying support trendline had a downward breakout within the retest window AND the current bar is after that breakout and within the window AND high ≥ current line − retest tolerance AND close < current line AND close < EMA(short) AND EMA(short) ≤ EMA(medium) AND smoothed RSI ≤ 50 AND MACD histogram ≤ 0.
Short: A qualifying resistance trendline is rejected: high ≥ current line − retest tolerance AND close < current line − rejection buffer AND close < open AND close < EMA(short) AND EMA(short) ≤ EMA(medium) AND smoothed RSI ≤ 50 AND MACD histogram ≤ 0.
Short: After a qualifying support trendline's upward-crossing breakout, while within its retest window, close > current line + 0.5 × breakout buffer.
Exit: Long, high ≥ target OR close ≤ stop.
Exit: Short, low ≤ target OR close ≥ stop.
