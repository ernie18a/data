Calculation: Breakout levels = highest high and lowest low over their respective lookbacks, excluding the current bar. An upward breakout occurs when close crosses above the highest-high level; a downward breakout occurs when close crosses below the lowest-low level.
Calculation: Virtual trailing stop = maintain a virtual position, initially flat. When flat, enter long on an upward breakout or short on a downward breakout, giving the upward breakout priority. On the same bar and subsequent bars, reset a virtual long to flat on a downward breakout or when low <= max(previous fixed stop, previous long trailing stop); reset a virtual short to flat on an upward breakout or when high >= min(previous fixed stop, previous short trailing stop). A comparison involving an unavailable fixed stop is false. Do not re-enter on a bar after resetting. The fixed stop while in a virtual position is the virtual entry bar's close minus, for long, or plus, for short, that bar's fixed-stop ATR multiplied by its multiplier. After updating the virtual position, the long trailing stop is max(close - trailing-stop ATR multiplied by its multiplier, previous long trailing stop) while virtually long and zero otherwise; the short trailing stop is min(close + trailing-stop ATR multiplied by its multiplier, previous short trailing stop) while virtually short and a large initial ceiling otherwise. The virtual trailing stop is the corresponding updated trailing stop while in a virtual position and unavailable otherwise.
Calculation: Smoothed directional movement = upward movement is change in high; downward movement is minus change in low. Positive directional movement is upward movement when it is positive and exceeds downward movement, otherwise zero; negative directional movement is downward movement when it is positive and exceeds upward movement, otherwise zero. Divide the Wilder moving average of each directional movement by the Wilder moving average of true range, using the same directional-movement lookback. Smooth each quotient by applying the following operation three consecutive times with the same smoothing length: 1.5 times its EMA minus 0.5 times the EMA of that EMA.
Long: Virtual trailing stop < lowest-low breakout level AND close > previous close AND smoothed positive directional movement > smoothed negative directional movement AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND datetime within the enabled date boundaries AND long trading enabled AND position side is not long.
Short: Virtual trailing stop > highest-high breakout level AND close < previous close AND smoothed positive directional movement < smoothed negative directional movement AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND datetime within the enabled date boundaries AND short trading enabled AND position side is not short.
Exit: long, high >= entry price multiplied by (1 + a take-profit percentage); apply each of four independently configured targets and their position quantities, with the final target closing the remainder.
Exit: short, low <= entry price multiplied by (1 - a take-profit percentage); apply each of four independently configured targets and their position quantities, with the final target closing the remainder.
Exit: long, close < entry price multiplied by (1 - stop-loss percentage) AND datetime within the enabled date boundaries.
Exit: short, close > entry price multiplied by (1 + stop-loss percentage) AND datetime within the enabled date boundaries.
Exit: long, virtual trailing stop > highest-high breakout level AND close < previous close AND smoothed positive directional movement < smoothed negative directional movement AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND datetime within the enabled date boundaries AND short trading enabled.
Exit: short, virtual trailing stop < lowest-low breakout level AND close > previous close AND smoothed positive directional movement > smoothed negative directional movement AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND datetime within the enabled date boundaries AND long trading enabled.
