Calculation: Normalized price or volume slope = apply a configurable SMA or EMA to the selected OHLC price source or to volume; calculate its percentage change over a shared slope lookback, using zero if the earlier average is unavailable or zero; divide that change by the EMA of its absolute value over a shared normalization lookback, using zero if the denominator is unavailable or zero; clamp the result to [-3, 3] and divide by 3. The price and volume averages may have different types and lengths.
Calculation: Price efficiency = absolute normalized price slope divided by (absolute normalized volume slope + 0.20).
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low pivot confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars, using the configured number of left-hand bars. Compare each confirmed pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type that has available pivot volume, even if that preceding pivot did not produce a signal. Pivot volume is either the pivot bar's volume or the SMA of volume ending on the pivot bar. Evaluate signals on the confirmation bar.
Long: Normalized price slope >= strong-move threshold AND normalized volume slope >= high-volume threshold.
Long: Normalized price slope > flat-price threshold AND normalized volume slope < 0 AND price efficiency >= efficiency threshold.
Long: A confirmed low pivot is below the preceding low pivot AND its percentage decrease >= minimum price difference AND its pivot volume differs from the preceding pivot volume by at least the minimum volume difference in percent AND its pivot bar is more than 0 and at most the maximum pivot distance after the preceding pivot bar.
Short: Normalized price slope <= negative strong-move threshold AND normalized volume slope >= high-volume threshold.
Short: A confirmed high pivot is above the preceding high pivot AND its percentage increase >= minimum price difference AND its pivot volume differs from the preceding pivot volume by at least the minimum volume difference in percent AND its pivot bar is more than 0 and at most the maximum pivot distance after the preceding pivot bar.
