Calculation: FirstMove = close of the first bar completing at or after (news time + delay) minus open of the bar containing news time
Calculation: Score = weighted sum of direction-aligned checks, where dir = sign(FirstMove): (1) min(|FirstMove|/ATR, 1); (2) ATR > its SMA; (3) close > (dir long) or < (dir short) the close of the first bar completing at or after (news time + delay); (4) close on the dir side of VWAP(hlc3); (5) close on the dir side of the SMA of close (Bollinger basis), plus an extra weight if Bollinger bandwidth > its SMA; (6) trend of a higher timeframe than the base bars, using the bar still forming, equals dir, where trend is up if EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback), else down; (7) the same trend test on a timeframe higher than (6), with longer lookbacks than in (6)
Long: bar is at or after (news time + delay) AND FirstMove > 0 AND Score >= threshold
Short: bar is at or after (news time + delay) AND FirstMove < 0 AND Score >= threshold
