Calculation: Detrended A/D = cumulative sum of volume times close-location value, minus its EMA; close-location value is ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Normalized A/D = either (detrended A/D - rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation, with zero returned when the standard deviation is zero, or (detrended A/D - rolling median) / (1.4826 times rolling median absolute deviation). Use either one normalization window or the median of normalized values from shorter, medium, and longer windows, with the shorter window half the medium window subject to a minimum length and the longer window twice the medium window.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low surrounded by an equal number of earlier and later bars; a high is at least all earlier highs and greater than all later highs, and a low is at most all earlier lows and less than all later lows. Evaluate the pivot when its later bars have completed.
Long: Normalized A/D >= 0.
Short: Normalized A/D < 0.
Long: A low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding confirmed low pivot is at most the maximum pivot separation AND ((its low < the preceding pivot low AND its normalized A/D > normalized A/D at the preceding pivot) OR (hidden divergences enabled AND its low > the preceding pivot low AND its normalized A/D < normalized A/D at the preceding pivot)).
Short: A high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding confirmed high pivot is at most the maximum pivot separation AND ((its high > the preceding pivot high AND its normalized A/D < normalized A/D at the preceding pivot) OR (hidden divergences enabled AND its high < the preceding pivot high AND its normalized A/D > normalized A/D at the preceding pivot)).
