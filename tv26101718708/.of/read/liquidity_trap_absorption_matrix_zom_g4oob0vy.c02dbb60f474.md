Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low that is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on each side; available only after the bars on both sides have completed.
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening session; available after that session ends.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick share = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), and (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), respectively.
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = VWAP ± standard deviation of close − VWAP over the configured lookback, multiplied by the configured deviation multiplier.
Calculation: Delta pressure = 100 × EMA of (volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) / max(volume moving average, 1)); its smoothing lookback is configured.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish delta flip = delta pressure is rising/falling versus the prior bar and is above the configured negative limit/below the configured positive limit, respectively.
Calculation: Trend = short-lookback EMA of close compared with a longer-lookback EMA of close; up when the short EMA is higher, down when lower, otherwise balanced.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep depth = clamp((swept level − low) / ATR, 0, 1.5) / 1.5 and clamp((high − swept level) / ATR, 0, 1.5) / 1.5, respectively; use the opening-range level if its corresponding sweep occurs, otherwise the latest confirmed swing low/high.
Calculation: Volume score = clamp((relative volume − 1) / 1.75, 0, 1).
Calculation: Bullish VWAP score = clamp((VWAP − low) / VWAP deviation, 0, 1) when close < VWAP, otherwise 0.35. Bearish VWAP score = clamp((high − VWAP) / VWAP deviation, 0, 1) when close > VWAP, otherwise 0.35. Use minimum price increment in the denominator if VWAP deviation is smaller.
Calculation: Bullish trend score = 0.75 in an uptrend, 0.35 in a downtrend, otherwise 0.55. Bearish trend score = 0.75 in a downtrend, 0.35 in an uptrend, otherwise 0.55.
Calculation: Bull trap score = 22 × bullish sweep depth + 24 × lower-wick share + 18 × volume score + 16 × bullish VWAP score + 12 if bullish delta flip, otherwise 0 + 8 × bullish trend score. Bear trap score uses bearish sweep depth, upper-wick share, bearish VWAP score, bearish delta flip, and bearish trend score with the same weights.
Long: (low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low OR opening-range low is swept below and close reclaims above it) AND lower-wick share ≥ configured minimum AND relative volume ≥ configured minimum AND (VWAP context disabled OR low ≤ lower VWAP band OR close > lower VWAP band OR close < VWAP) AND bull trap score ≥ configured score threshold AND no bull or bear trap has occurred within the configured cooldown bars.
Short: (high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high OR opening-range high is swept above and close rejects below it) AND upper-wick share ≥ configured minimum AND relative volume ≥ configured minimum AND (VWAP context disabled OR high ≥ upper VWAP band OR close < upper VWAP band OR close > VWAP) AND bear trap score ≥ configured score threshold AND no bull or bear trap has occurred within the configured cooldown bars.
