Calculation: Division by zero = 0; clamp limits a value to the stated bounds. Treat all named thresholds, lengths, multipliers, learning rates, initial values and coefficients as parameters.
Calculation: Trend score = clamp(weighted sum of EMA-stack score, pivot score and directional-movement score, -score limit, +score limit). The EMA-stack score is a score unit times the number of adjacent ascending pairs minus the number of adjacent descending pairs among five close EMAs ordered from shortest to longest. Confirm pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side; the pivot score adds a score unit for each of the latest confirmed pivot high and low above its preceding pivot of the same kind and subtracts a score unit for each below it. The directional-movement score is zero below the lower ADX threshold; above it, its magnitude is the lower score magnitude until ADX exceeds the higher threshold, then the higher magnitude; its sign is positive if +DI > -DI and negative otherwise.
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(weighted sum of (EMA-smoothed RSI minus RSI midpoint) times a scale factor and MACD histogram divided by ATR times a scale factor, -score limit, +score limit). Clamp the normalized histogram to the same bounds before combining it. The MACD fast EMA is shorter than its slow EMA.
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp(candle-direction vote plus normalized slope, -score limit, +score limit). The vote is candle direction times a vote magnitude if Bollinger Band width exceeds its longer SMA times an expansion multiplier, ATR divided by its longer SMA is strictly between lower and upper volatility thresholds, and absolute candle body divided by high minus low exceeds a body threshold; otherwise it is zero. Candle direction is the sign of close minus open. Normalized slope is close minus an earlier close, divided by ATR and multiplied by a scale factor, clamped to symmetric slope limits. Bollinger Band width is upper minus lower band divided by the basis, multiplied by a scale factor.
Calculation: Regime = trend if ADX is at least its regime threshold and at least a required number of adjacent EMA pairs in the trend stack ascend or descend; otherwise volatile if ATR divided by its SMA is at least the high regime threshold; otherwise quiet if Bollinger Band width is below its SMA times a squeeze multiplier or the ATR ratio is at most the low regime threshold; otherwise range.
Calculation: Axis weights = zero for a disabled axis; otherwise its reliability weight times its regime multiplier. If adaptive weighting is disabled, reliability weight is one; otherwise linearly map axis reliability from its bounded range to the configured minimum and maximum weights. Initialize each reliability to the same value. On each confirmed bar, define each axis direction as positive or negative when its score strictly exceeds the corresponding symmetric direction threshold, and zero otherwise. Record a sample at the current close when trend or momentum direction changes to a nonzero direction, and on every bar with nonzero volatility direction. Age new and existing samples on that bar. At the outcome horizon, process samples newest first: outcome is one if direction times the change from sample close to current close is at least a required multiple of current ATR, otherwise zero; update that axis reliability by an EWMA toward the outcome and clamp it to its bounds. If regime weighting is disabled, every regime multiplier is one. Otherwise use separate axis multipliers for each regime: trend favors trend over momentum over volatility, volatile favors volatility over momentum over trend, quiet gives all axes the same reduced multiplier, and range favors momentum over volatility over trend.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe verdict = +1 if the shorter close EMA exceeds the longer close EMA and RSI exceeds its midpoint; -1 if both comparisons are reversed; otherwise zero. Calculate independently on two higher timeframes, one longer than the other, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Final score = clamp(weighted average of enabled axis scores plus the higher-timeframe adjustment, -score limit, +score limit). If higher-timeframe alignment is enabled, each nonzero higher-timeframe verdict adds an adjustment when it agrees with the sign of the weighted average and subtracts that adjustment otherwise; a zero verdict contributes zero. If alignment is disabled, the adjustment is zero.
Calculation: Active threshold = fixed midpoint of a configured threshold range when adaptation is disabled. Otherwise initialize equally spaced threshold candidates and equal candidate values; on each confirmed bar choose the candidate with the highest value, breaking ties toward the lowest index, unless the fractional part of sin(bar index times a frequency plus a phase) times a scale is below the exploration probability, in which case use the floor of that fraction times a candidate-index scale modulo the number of candidates. Record the chosen candidate, direction and close on each strong signal. Age new and existing samples on that bar; at the same outcome horizon used for axis reliability, process samples newest first and update the recorded candidate value by an EWMA toward the same ATR-based binary outcome, clamped to its bounds. Select the threshold before resolving samples on the current bar.
Calculation: Leaning axes = enabled axes whose absolute score exceeds the lean threshold. Up and down counts are the numbers of positive and negative leaning axes. Their spread is the maximum minus minimum leaning score, or zero when fewer than two axes lean.
Calculation: Strong-signal state = initialize neutral; on each confirmed bar set it to long if the strong-long conditions hold, otherwise short if the strong-short conditions hold; reset it to neutral if neither holds and absolute final score is below the active threshold minus a reset margin. Evaluate a new strong signal against the state before updating it.
Long: Final score >= active threshold AND trend score > lean threshold AND up count > down count AND up count >= 1 AND leaning-axis spread <= tolerance limit AND prior strong-signal state != long.
Short: Final score <= negative active threshold AND trend score < negative lean threshold AND down count > up count AND down count >= 1 AND leaning-axis spread <= tolerance limit AND prior strong-signal state != short.
Long: Final score >= active threshold times a weaker-signal fraction.
Short: Final score <= negative active threshold times a weaker-signal fraction.
Long: Any axis score > its display-direction threshold.
Short: Any axis score < negative display-direction threshold.
Long: Either higher-timeframe verdict > 0 AND higher-timeframe alignment enabled.
Short: Either higher-timeframe verdict < 0 AND higher-timeframe alignment enabled.
Long: The shortest EMA > the next-shortest EMA OR the next-shortest EMA > the middle EMA OR the middle EMA > the longest EMA.
Short: The shortest EMA <= the next-shortest EMA OR the next-shortest EMA <= the middle EMA OR the middle EMA <= the longest EMA.
