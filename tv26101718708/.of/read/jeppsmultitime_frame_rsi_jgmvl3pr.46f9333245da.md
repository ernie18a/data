Calculation: Timeframe RSI = RSI of a selected price source on the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe; for historical bars, higher-timeframe values update on the higher-timeframe bar's close and otherwise retain the last completed value, while lower-timeframe values use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot = an RSI pivot high or low confirmed after a right-side lookback equal to its left-side lookback; compare the price high or low at the RSI pivot bar, and evaluate the decision on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Zone count = the number of enabled timeframe RSIs, out of three independently configured chart, higher, or lower timeframes, at or beyond their respective overbought or oversold thresholds.
Long: A timeframe RSI crosses below its oversold threshold AND no previous oversold crossing has fired within the current bar of that timeframe.
Short: A timeframe RSI crosses above its overbought threshold AND no previous overbought crossing has fired within the current bar of that timeframe.
Long: Oversold zone count equals two AND its previous chart-bar value was less than two, OR oversold zone count equals three AND its previous chart-bar value was less than three.
Short: Overbought zone count equals two AND its previous chart-bar value was less than two, OR overbought zone count equals three AND its previous chart-bar value was less than three.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low AND the price low at the current pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot AND the number of timeframe bars between their confirmation bars minus one is within the inclusive divergence range.
Short: A confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot high AND the price high at the current pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot AND the number of timeframe bars between their confirmation bars minus one is within the inclusive divergence range.
