Calculation: Price source = a selected OHLC-derived price series. All calculations use chart bars unless another timeframe is specified.
Calculation: Adaptive band length = either a fixed lookback or a bounded, scaled Ehlers dominant-cycle period calculated from HL2 using the smoothed in-phase and quadrature Hilbert-transform components, their smoothed complex product, and the arctangent of its imaginary-to-real ratio.
Calculation: Band basis = the adaptive-length SMA, recursive EMA with smoothing factor 2/(length+1), rolling volume-weighted average, or median of the price source. When rolling volume is zero, use the SMA.
Calculation: Band deviations = select population standard deviation about the basis; median absolute deviation about the rolling median multiplied by its normal-consistency factor; separate square roots of the mean squared positive and negative deviations about the basis multiplied by the square root of two; square root of the exponentially weighted variance of one-bar price-source changes; or distances from the basis to the upper and lower rolling price quantiles divided by the main-band multiplier. Multiply each deviation by an optional bounded kurtosis adjustment and an optional containment-controller multiplier. The controller starts at one and, after each completed bar with sufficient history, adds gain times (target containment minus rolling fraction of closes within the main bands), bounded above and below; its updated value applies on the next bar.
Calculation: Inner, main, and outer bands = basis plus or minus the corresponding smaller, middle, or larger multiplier times the upper or lower deviation. Band z = (price source minus basis) divided by the upper deviation above the basis or the lower deviation below it, with zero when that deviation is zero. Percent B = (price source minus lower main band)/(upper main band minus lower main band), or the midpoint when the band range is zero.
Calculation: Trend = ADX above its threshold AND efficiency ratio above its threshold. Efficiency ratio = absolute price-source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar price-source changes over that lookback. Bull trend = trend AND close above the EMA of close; bear trend = trend AND close at or below that EMA. Volatility clustering = exponentially decayed cumulative absolute log-close change, standardized using its rolling mean and standard deviation, above its threshold. Range = trend is false AND volatility clustering is false.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = confirmation is disabled OR cumulative volume has never been positive OR current volume exceeds its rolling SMA times a threshold.
Calculation: Upper or lower band walk = consecutive closes respectively above the upper main band or below the lower main band reach a streak threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = sign of higher-timeframe close minus its SMA, or zero when the corresponding standard-deviation band has zero width or insufficient data. On historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with lookahead disabled.
Calculation: Fade long = fade enabled AND range AND low at or below the lower main band AND close above that band AND (previous percent B at or below its lower boundary OR previous band z at or below the negative main-band multiplier) AND volume confirmation. Fade short = fade enabled AND range AND high at or above the upper main band AND close below that band AND (previous percent B at or above its upper boundary OR previous band z at or above the main-band multiplier) AND volume confirmation.
Calculation: Ride long = ride enabled AND bull trend AND (upper band walk OR close above the upper inner band AND close above the previous close) AND volume confirmation AND (higher-timeframe gate disabled OR higher-timeframe bias nonnegative). Ride short = ride enabled AND bear trend AND (lower band walk OR close below the lower inner band AND close below the previous close) AND volume confirmation AND (higher-timeframe gate disabled OR higher-timeframe bias nonpositive).
Calculation: Squeeze = rolling percentile rank of bandwidth at or below its threshold, where bandwidth is main-band width divided by basis, or zero when basis is zero. Squeeze expansion = squeeze detection enabled AND squeeze is false AND bars since the most recent squeeze are within an expansion window AND bandwidth is greater than its previous value.
Calculation: Confirmed percent-B pivot = a pivot high or low using selected left and right lookbacks, detected on the bar completing the right lookback. Compare the two most recent pivots of the same kind and the closes at their pivot bars.
Calculation: Trade direction = long when fade long or ride long is true; otherwise short when fade short or ride short is true; otherwise zero. A simultaneous long and short resolves to long. Signal mode is fade whenever either fade condition is true, otherwise ride when either ride condition is true.
Calculation: Mean-reversion half-life = minus log(2) divided by log(beta), where beta is rolling covariance of price-source-minus-basis with its one-bar lag divided by rolling variance of that lag; undefined unless beta is strictly between zero and one. Mean-reverting variance ratio = rolling variance of a multi-bar close change divided by that change's bar count times rolling variance of one-bar close change, below one.
Calculation: Timeframe agreement = count of selected higher or lower timeframes whose close is respectively above or below its same-timeframe SMA, ignoring zero-width bands and unavailable values. For higher timeframes use the last completed bar; for lower timeframes use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, with lookahead disabled. The higher-timeframe bias gate uses its own higher timeframe.
Calculation: Flow sign = sign of the change in cumulative signed volume over a lookback. Signed volume is the sum of lower-timeframe completed intrabar volumes, positive when intrabar close is at least open and negative otherwise; if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar volume signed by chart-bar close versus open.
Calculation: Calibrated edge by signal mode = zero until a minimum number of mode samples has resolved; thereafter bounded recency-decayed, uniqueness-weighted win rate minus the arithmetic mean of the samples' direction-specific regime base rates. Create samples on confirmed fade or ride signals, subject to a minimum gap between samples of the same mode; record close, ATR, direction and regime, and weight each sample by the reciprocal of one plus the number of pending samples when uniqueness weighting is enabled, otherwise one. Resolve samples from their entry bar onward when an ATR-scaled stop, ATR-scaled profit barrier, or horizon is reached; stop takes precedence if both barriers are hit, and a horizon resolution wins only if directional close movement reaches the profit barrier. Decay the mode's accumulated sample weight and winning weight on each resolution before adding the resolved weight. The base rate is measured separately by entry regime and direction from every confirmed bar with positive ATR: at the same horizon, count whether directional close movement reaches the ATR-scaled profit barrier; use the base rate available when the sample resolves, or one half when no base observations exist. Resolve pending samples after calculating the current bar's conviction and before updating the current bar's matured base observations.
Calculation: Conviction = rounded, bounded weighted average of stretch, regime fit, timeframe agreement, half-life quality, calibrated edge, volume thrust and flow agreement. Fade stretch is absolute band z divided by the outer-band multiplier, bounded to [0,1]; ride stretch is the larger band-walk streak divided by twice the walk threshold, bounded to [0,1]. Regime fit is one for the matching range or trend and a smaller fallback otherwise, reduced by a factor when the optional variance-ratio test disagrees with the mode. Fade timeframe agreement is one minus the absolute difference between bullish and bearish timeframe counts divided by their total count; ride agreement is the fraction aligned with direction, or one half when timeframe agreement is disabled. Fade half-life quality is a bounded decreasing function of half-life relative to its estimation window, with a low fallback when undefined; ride quality is high when half-life is undefined and lower otherwise. Calibrated-edge quality is one half plus the mode's bounded calibrated edge. Volume quality is high when current volume exceeds its rolling average times the thrust threshold or no positive volume has been observed, and lower otherwise. Flow quality is high when flow sign equals trade direction and lower otherwise. Use configurable nonnegative weights and the selected thresholds and fallback factors.
Calculation: Approved long or short = corresponding trade direction AND conviction at or above its minimum AND, for ride signals, timeframe agreement disabled OR the count aligned with direction at or above its minimum. External-symbol volatility and cross-asset filters cannot be evaluated from the specified data and are disabled.
Calculation: Initial stop and targets = freeze them at an approved entry close. For a long, choose lower outer band minus ATR padding, entry minus ATR plus ATR padding, or entry minus the absolute price-source-to-basis stretch minus ATR padding; in the last choice substitute an ATR multiple for stretch when half-life is undefined. Mirror for a short. Initial risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Fade first and final targets are the entry-bar basis and opposite inner band; ride first and final targets are entry plus or minus a smaller and a larger multiple of initial risk. If the first target is reached without the stop or final target being reached on that bar, move a still-adverse stop to entry for subsequent bars.
Long: Fade long OR ride long.
Short: Fade short OR ride short.
Long: Squeeze expansion AND close above basis.
Short: Squeeze expansion AND close at or below basis.
Long: A percent-B pivot low is confirmed AND its pivot-bar close is below the preceding pivot-low close AND its percent B is above the preceding pivot-low percent B.
Short: A percent-B pivot high is confirmed AND its pivot-bar close is above the preceding pivot-high close AND its percent B is below the preceding pivot-high percent B.
Exit: long, low at or below the current frozen or breakeven stop OR high at or above the frozen final target.
Exit: short, high at or above the current frozen or breakeven stop OR low at or below the frozen final target.
