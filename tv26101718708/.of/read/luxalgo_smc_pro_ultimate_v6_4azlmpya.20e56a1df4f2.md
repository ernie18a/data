Calculation: Internal and swing pivots = highs/lows that are the highest/lowest in a centered window, with the pivot bar respectively a shorter/longer lookback behind the current bar; use the latest recorded pivot of the selected structure mode(s).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = current low > high two bars ago and prior close > that high / current high < low two bars ago and prior close < that low.
Calculation: Premium/discount midpoint = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback.
Calculation: Initial long stop = max(pivot below close, else lowest low over the fallback lookback, minus ATR buffer; close minus maximum stop-distance cap); if not below close, use close minus 1.5 ATR. Initial short stop = min(pivot above close, else highest high over the fallback lookback, plus ATR buffer; close plus maximum stop-distance cap); if not above close, use close plus 1.5 ATR. ATR buffer uses an ATR-based multiplier adjusted by the recent closed-trade win rate when self-optimization is enabled.
Calculation: TP1 = entry price plus/minus initial risk for long/short. TP2 = the selected structural target pivot when structural mode is selected and it is beyond TP1; otherwise entry price plus/minus 2.5 times initial risk.
Calculation: Current stop = initial stop, moved to entry price after TP1; for a long, when high exceeds prior high, raise it to at least high minus 3 ATR; for a short, when low is below prior low, lower it to at most low plus 3 ATR.
Long: Flat position AND long direction is enabled AND (close crosses above the latest internal pivot high OR close crosses above the latest swing pivot high, according to the selected structure mode) AND (discount filter disabled OR close < premium/discount midpoint) AND (FVG filter disabled OR bullish FVG now or on the prior bar) AND (divergence filter disabled OR RSI rises while low falls) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close > Bollinger middle band).
Short: Flat position AND short direction is enabled AND (close crosses below the latest internal pivot low OR close crosses below the latest swing pivot low, according to the selected structure mode) AND (premium filter disabled OR close > premium/discount midpoint) AND (FVG filter disabled OR bearish FVG now or on the prior bar) AND (divergence filter disabled OR RSI falls while high rises) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close < Bollinger middle band).
Exit: Long, high >= TP1 OR high >= TP2 OR low <= current stop.
Exit: Short, low <= TP1 OR low <= TP2 OR high >= current stop.
