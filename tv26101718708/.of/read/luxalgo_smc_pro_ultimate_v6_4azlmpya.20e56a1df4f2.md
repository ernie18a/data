Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = the high or low N bars ago when it equals the highest high or lowest low over the current bar and the preceding 2N bars; retain the latest confirmed value separately for a shorter and a longer pivot lookback. A crossover of close above a retained pivot high requires current close > current retained value AND previous close <= previous retained value; a crossunder of close below a retained pivot low requires current close < current retained value AND previous close >= previous retained value.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago; bearish gap = high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago.
Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low over the range lookback) / 2.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR multiplier = the configured ATR multiplier adjusted by a configurable factor according to whether the rolling fraction of profitable closed trades is below a lower threshold or above a higher threshold; use the unadjusted multiplier when optimization is disabled. Record a trade as profitable when the change in net profit on its closing bar is positive; initialize the performance score neutrally.
Calculation: Initial stop = on the signal bar, for a long, the greater of (latest shorter-lookback pivot low if below close, otherwise lowest low over a fallback lookback) minus ATR times the adaptive ATR multiplier, and close reduced by a maximum stop percentage; if the result is at or above close, use close minus ATR times a fallback multiplier. For a short, use the lesser of (latest shorter-lookback pivot high if above close, otherwise highest high over the fallback lookback) plus ATR times the adaptive ATR multiplier, and close increased by the maximum stop percentage; if the result is at or below close, use close plus ATR times the fallback multiplier.
Calculation: Targets = calculated on the signal bar and fixed for the trade. Risk is the absolute difference between signal close and initial stop. The first target is signal close plus risk for a long or minus risk for a short. The second target is the latest longer-lookback pivot high for a long if structural targets are enabled and that pivot is above the first target, or the latest longer-lookback pivot low for a short if structural targets are enabled and that pivot is below the first target; otherwise it is signal close plus or minus risk times a configurable target multiplier.
Calculation: Active stop = initially the initial stop. After the first target is reached, reset it to the actual position entry price. On each subsequent calculation, including that same bar, if a long's high exceeds the previous high, raise the stop to the greater of its current value and high minus ATR times a trailing multiplier; if a short's low is below the previous low, lower the stop to the lesser of its current value and low plus ATR times the trailing multiplier. Submit the updated stop for execution from the next available tick.
Long: Position is flat AND long trading is enabled AND close crosses above the latest pivot high of the configured shorter lookback, longer lookback, or either lookback AND (range filter disabled OR close < range midpoint) AND (gap filter disabled OR bullish gap on the current or previous bar) AND (divergence filter disabled OR RSI > previous RSI AND low < previous low) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close > Bollinger Bands middle line).
Short: Position is flat AND short trading is enabled AND close crosses below the latest pivot low of the configured shorter lookback, longer lookback, or either lookback AND (range filter disabled OR close > range midpoint) AND (gap filter disabled OR bearish gap on the current or previous bar) AND (divergence filter disabled OR RSI < previous RSI AND high > previous high) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close < Bollinger Bands middle line).
Exit: Long, first target has not previously been reached AND high >= first target; mark the target reached, reset the stop, and submit a limit exit for a configured portion of the original position at the first target from the next available tick.
Exit: Long, second target has not previously been reached AND high >= second target; mark the target reached and submit a limit exit for a configured portion of the original position at the second target from the next available tick.
Exit: Long, price <= active stop.
Exit: Short, first target has not previously been reached AND low <= first target; mark the target reached, reset the stop, and submit a limit exit for a configured portion of the original position at the first target from the next available tick.
Exit: Short, second target has not previously been reached AND low <= second target; mark the target reached and submit a limit exit for a configured portion of the original position at the second target from the next available tick.
Exit: Short, price >= active stop.
