Calculation: ATR = average true range of chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the strict extreme of a lookback window; confirm after the shorter right-side window, and accept only if its distance from the previous same-side swing exceeds an ATR-based threshold.
Calculation: Chart structure = bullish when the latest two confirmed swing highs and lows are both higher than their predecessors; bearish when both are lower; otherwise mixed.
Calculation: Context bias = bullish when the close of the higher-timeframe bar still forming exceeds its close from a longer lookback by more than an ATR-based tolerance; bearish when it is below by more than that tolerance; otherwise retain the prior bias, initially neutral.
Calculation: Structural break = a chart-timeframe close crossing a confirmed swing reference from the other side. In the bias direction, crossing the latest same-side swing is BOS; against bias, crossing the latest opposite-side swing is CHoCH; crossing the second-latest opposite-side swing against bias with displacement is MSS. Displacement means candle body greater than an ATR-based threshold.
Calculation: Swing-range percentile = 100 × (close − latest confirmed swing low) / (latest confirmed swing high − latest confirmed swing low).
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price wicks beyond an unswept pool made from confirmed swing highs/lows, closes back across it, and travels at least an ATR-based minimum distance from the pool origin. A sweep below a low is bullish; a sweep above a high is bearish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish zone = an order block formed from the last opposite-color candle in the recent lookback before a structural event; a fair-value gap formed when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier (bullish) or current high is below the low two bars earlier (bearish); an inverted gap is a filled gap with its effective direction reversed; a breaker block is an order block swept and confirmed by an opposite-direction CHoCH, then retested with a direction-matching close; a balanced price range is overlapping active opposite-direction fair-value gaps.
Long: A confirmed swing low forms OR a bullish BOS, CHoCH, or MSS fires OR a bullish liquidity sweep fires OR bullish SMT divergence fires at a confirmed swing low OR a bullish order block, fair-value gap, inverted fair-value gap, breaker block, or balanced price range forms OR price enters the swing-range percentile's 21–38% zone OR the Continuation or Reversal verdict displays BULL with a non-INACTIVE tier.
Short: A confirmed swing high forms OR a bearish BOS, CHoCH, or MSS fires OR a bearish liquidity sweep fires OR bearish SMT divergence fires at a confirmed swing high OR a bearish order block, fair-value gap, inverted fair-value gap, breaker block, or balanced price range forms OR price enters the swing-range percentile's 62–79% zone OR the Continuation or Reversal verdict displays BEAR with a non-INACTIVE tier.
