Calculation: Pivot high = high n bars ago strictly greater than every other high at offsets 1 through 2n; pivot low = low n bars ago strictly less than every other low at offsets 1 through 2n. The current bar is excluded.
Calculation: Pivot feed = pivot calculated on the chart timeframe or a selected higher timeframe, using the previous completed calculation bar. A separate higher-timeframe feed uses its own pivot lookback and the previous completed calculation bar.
Calculation: Active resistance and support levels = respectively, pivot highs and lows from a pivot feed, added whenever its pivot value is available. For the separate higher-timeframe feed, add a level only when its value differs from the last added pivot of the same kind. Keep the most recent configured number of levels per side and feed. Remove a level after a qualifying break; optionally remove chart-feed levels when their age, measured from the chart bar on which they were added minus the configured right offset, exceeds the configured decay period. Test for a break before expiration.
Long: A chart-feed pivot high is greater than the previous available chart-feed pivot high OR a chart-feed pivot low is greater than or equal to the previous available chart-feed pivot low.
Short: A chart-feed pivot high is less than or equal to the previous available chart-feed pivot high OR a chart-feed pivot low is less than the previous available chart-feed pivot low.
Long: A newly added higher-timeframe pivot high is greater than the previous added higher-timeframe pivot high OR a newly added higher-timeframe pivot low is greater than or equal to the previous added higher-timeframe pivot low.
Short: A newly added higher-timeframe pivot high is less than or equal to the previous added higher-timeframe pivot high OR a newly added higher-timeframe pivot low is less than the previous added higher-timeframe pivot low.
Long: Close is above an active chart-feed resistance level AND previous close is at or below that level AND, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume is above its simple moving average.
Short: Close is below an active chart-feed support level AND previous close is at or above that level AND, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume is above its simple moving average.
Long: Close is above an active higher-timeframe resistance level AND previous close is at or below that level.
Short: Close is below an active higher-timeframe support level AND previous close is at or above that level.
Long: Low is above high two bars ago AND the previous bar's close is above its open AND low minus high two bars ago is at least the minimum gap size.
Short: High is below low two bars ago AND the previous bar's close is below its open AND low two bars ago minus high is at least the minimum gap size.
Long: On a higher timeframe using the still-forming bar, low is above high two higher-timeframe bars ago AND the previous higher-timeframe bar's close is above its open AND low minus high two higher-timeframe bars ago is at least the minimum gap size AND no gap has already been recorded for this higher-timeframe bar.
Short: On a higher timeframe using the still-forming bar, high is below low two higher-timeframe bars ago AND the previous higher-timeframe bar's close is below its open AND low two higher-timeframe bars ago minus high is at least the minimum gap size AND no gap has already been recorded for this higher-timeframe bar.
