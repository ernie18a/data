Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar’s high is strictly higher than the highs of the same parameterized number of bars before and after it; confirmed pivot low = a bar’s low is strictly lower than the lows of that same number of bars before and after it. Use the configured pivot timeframe; when it is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Active resistance levels = the most recent confirmed pivot highs, up to the configured level limit, removed when broken and, if time decay is enabled, when their age exceeds the configured limit. Active support levels are defined the same way from confirmed pivot lows.
Long: Low > high two bars earlier AND close one bar earlier > open one bar earlier AND low − high two bars earlier ≥ the configured minimum gap size; OR close > any active resistance level AND previous close ≤ that level AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > its configured-length simple moving average).
Short: High < low two bars earlier AND close one bar earlier < open one bar earlier AND low two bars earlier − high ≥ the configured minimum gap size; OR close < any active support level AND previous close ≥ that level AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > its configured-length simple moving average).
