Calculation: Bull score = 100 × the lookback sum of true range × bullish close-location fraction, divided by the lookback sum of true range; bullish close-location fraction is (close − low) / (high − low) when close > prior close, otherwise 0. Apply linearly increasing weights from oldest to newest when weighting is enabled.
Calculation: Bear score = 100 × the lookback sum of true range × bearish close-location fraction, divided by the lookback sum of true range; bearish close-location fraction is (high − close) / (high − low) when close < prior close, otherwise 0. Apply linearly increasing weights from oldest to newest when weighting is enabled.
Calculation: Upper and lower noise bands = mean ± standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of the midpoint of bull and bear scores at recent confirmed crossovers.
Long: Bull score crosses above bear score AND the crossover midpoint is outside the noise bands AND sufficient crossover history exists AND no long position is active AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average); OR a bullish crossover midpoint is inside the noise bands, bull score remains above bear score for the confirmation lookahead, and close > open AND sufficient crossover history exists AND no long position is active AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average); OR bull score re-enters above the upper noise band after a sustained period below it, or bull score is above the upper band while flat, AND sufficient crossover history exists AND no long position is active AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average); OR a ghost long is active on the first new day and open < close AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average).
Short: Bear score crosses above bull score AND the crossover midpoint is outside the noise bands AND sufficient crossover history exists AND no short position is active AND short trading is enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average); OR a bearish crossover midpoint is inside the noise bands, bear score remains above bull score for the confirmation lookahead, and close < open AND sufficient crossover history exists AND no short position is active AND short trading is enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average); OR bear score re-enters above the upper noise band after a sustained period below it, or bear score is above the upper band while flat, AND sufficient crossover history exists AND no short position is active AND short trading is enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average); OR a ghost short is active on the first new day and open > close AND short trading is enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average).
Exit: long, low < trailing stop buffer OR confirmed close < trailing stop level OR close reaches entry price plus the initial stop distance (partial exit) OR square-off time occurs in a lower-timeframe bar.
Exit: short, high > trailing stop buffer OR confirmed close > trailing stop level OR close reaches entry price minus the initial stop distance (partial exit) OR square-off time occurs in a lower-timeframe bar.
