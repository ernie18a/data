Calculation: Bull and bear scores = 100 times the respective weighted sum divided by the weighted sum of true range, or zero if that denominator is zero. Use the current bar and up to a lookback of preceding completed bars. A bar's bull contribution is true range times (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low and close > previous close, otherwise zero. Its bear contribution is true range times (high - close) / (high - low) when high > low and close < previous close, otherwise zero. Use either equal weights or linearly increasing weights from oldest to newest; apply the same weights to the denominator.
Calculation: Cross bands = mean plus and minus a multiplier times the population standard deviation of the most recent specified number of confirmed bull-score and bear-score cross levels, where each cross level is their average on the crossing bar. Include the current cross before evaluating it. The system is ready when that many cross levels have been collected.
Calculation: Trend filter = an optional close-based SMA, EMA, or DEMA. A long passes when the filter is disabled or close > trend filter; a short passes when short trading is enabled AND the filter is disabled or close < trend filter.
Calculation: Pending confirmation = an in-band score crossover sets the pending direction and resets its count. On each confirmed bar, cancel it if the corresponding score is not greater than the other score; otherwise increment the count, including on the crossover bar. Once the count reaches the confirmation lookback, reset the count if the candle is not in the pending direction; if the candle is in that direction but readiness, trend filter, or internal direction prevents the signal, retain the count and pending direction. Clear the pending direction when its signal triggers. A new in-band crossover replaces any pending direction.
Calculation: Raw signals = evaluate confirmed bars in this order: bull crossover; otherwise bull breakout; otherwise bear crossover; otherwise bear breakout. An in-band crossover creates pending confirmation; an out-of-band crossover signals its direction if the system is ready, its trend filter passes, and the internal direction differs. A breakout signals its direction if the system is ready and its trend filter passes AND either the score has just moved above the upper band after at least a specified number of bars without exceeding it and the internal direction differs, or the internal direction is flat and the score is above the upper band. Evaluate pending confirmation after this sequence. A qualifying pending confirmation also signals its direction. Internal direction starts flat, changes to the signaled direction on entry, and returns to flat on a stop.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially entry-bar low minus ATR times the long multiplier for a long, or entry-bar high plus ATR times the short multiplier for a short. After each confirmed bar that does not stop out, update it to the greater of its previous value and close minus ATR times the long multiplier for a long, or the lesser of its previous value and close plus ATR times the short multiplier for a short, except on a same-direction signal. The live hard threshold is the trailing stop minus a longer-period ATR for a long, or plus that ATR for a short. The separately submitted hard-stop order remains at the initial hard threshold.
Calculation: Warning = while a stop is active, an opposite raw signal is suppressed and records its bar as a warning. On a stop, an opposite entry is triggered if the most recent warning is within the reversal lookback and the opposite trend filter passes.
Long: bull score > upper cross band.
Short: bull score <= upper cross band AND bear score > upper cross band.
Long: confirmed long raw signal AND no active short stop.
Short: confirmed short raw signal AND no active long stop.
Long: active short stop AND confirmed long raw signal.
Short: active long stop AND confirmed short raw signal.
Long: short stop triggered AND bars since most recent warning <= reversal lookback AND long trend filter passes.
Short: long stop triggered AND bars since most recent warning <= reversal lookback AND short trend filter passes.
Exit: long, low < live hard threshold OR confirmed close < trailing stop OR low <= initial hard-stop order.
Exit: short, high > live hard threshold OR confirmed close > trailing stop OR high >= initial hard-stop order.
Exit: long, partial exit enabled AND high >= entry-bar close + absolute difference between entry-bar close and initial trailing stop; exit the specified fraction.
Exit: short, partial exit enabled AND low <= entry-bar close - absolute difference between entry-bar close and initial trailing stop; exit the specified fraction.
Exit: long, intraday mode enabled AND a lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar falls in the square-off time window AND the stop is active; retain the direction and stop as a ghost position.
Exit: short, intraday mode enabled AND a lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar falls in the square-off time window AND the stop is active; retain the direction and stop as a ghost position.
Long: ghost long is active AND a new day has started since square-off AND the stop remains active AND confirmed close > open AND long trend filter passes.
Short: ghost short is active AND a new day has started since square-off AND the stop remains active AND confirmed close < open AND short trend filter passes.
