Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low pivot confirmed after the specified number of right-side bars, using the specified left-side and right-side pivot lookbacks. Retain the most recent 40 confirmed pivots of each type.
Calculation: Pattern candidates = for each newly confirmed pivot, examine retained earlier pivots of the same type from newest to oldest. For each pair, let P1 and P2 be their prices, A the ATR at P2, T = A × the price-tolerance multiplier, and N the lowest low strictly between high pivots or the highest high strictly between low pivots. For a top, height H = max(P1, P2) − N; for a bottom, H = N − min(P1, P2). A candidate is valid when its pivot separation is within the minimum and maximum inclusive, |P1 − P2| ≤ T, at least one bar lies between the pivots, H ≥ A × the minimum-height multiplier, and its quality score ≥ the score threshold. A top also requires N < min(P1, P2) and every intervening high ≤ max(P1, P2) + T × 0.5; a bottom requires N > max(P1, P2) and every intervening low ≥ min(P1, P2) − T × 0.5. Select only the newest valid P1 for each P2. Store the resulting pattern at P2's confirmation bar; retain at most 30 patterns, removing the oldest after processing the bar.
Calculation: Pattern quality score = round to one decimal the sum of: 25 × max(0, 1 − |P1 − P2| / max(T, 0.000001)); an RSI component equal to min(25, 10 + |RSI at P1 − RSI at P2|) if RSI at P2 is lower for a top or higher for a bottom, otherwise 0; a volume component equal to min(25, 10 + 15 × min((volume at P1 − volume at P2) / max(volume at P1, 1), 1)) if volume at P2 < volume at P1, otherwise 0; and a height component equal to min(25, 10 + 15 × min(max((H / max(A, 0.000001) − minimum-height multiplier) / (2 × minimum-height multiplier), 0), 1)) if H / max(A, 0.000001) ≥ minimum-height multiplier, otherwise 0.
Long: A valid bottom pattern is newly confirmed.
Short: A valid top pattern is newly confirmed.

Mark that pattern broken after this signal.
Short: An unbroken, unexpired top pattern exists AND no bar from its confirmation through the current bar has high > max(P1, P2) + current-bar ATR × stop-buffer multiplier or bar index − P2 bar index > the breakout-wait limit AND close < N AND (previous close ≥ N OR the current bar is P2's confirmation bar) AND (the trend filter is disabled OR close < EMA of close). Mark that pattern broken after this signal.
Calculation: Breakout levels = fixed at the breakout bar: for a bottom, stop = min(P1, P2) − breakout-bar ATR × stop-buffer multiplier, first target = N + H, and second target = N + 1.618 × H; for a top, stop = max(P1, P2) + breakout-bar ATR × stop-buffer multiplier, first target = N − H, and second target = N − 1.618 × H. Monitor each broken pattern starting on the bar after breakout until its stop or second target is hit, or until processing the first bar more than 100 bars after breakout; the first target can be hit only once per pattern. Check all target and stop conditions on a bar even if more than one is hit.
Exit: long, an actively monitored bottom pattern has high ≥ its first target for the first time OR high ≥ its second target OR low ≤ its stop.
Exit: short, an actively monitored top pattern has low ≤ its first target for the first time OR low ≤ its second target OR high ≥ its stop.
