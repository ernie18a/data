Calculation: Pattern quality score = Price match score + RSI divergence score + volume exhaustion score + height score. Price match score = max(0, 1 − absolute difference between the two pivot prices / maximum allowed price difference) × 25. For double tops, RSI divergence score is 0 unless the second pivot RSI is lower; otherwise it is min(25, 10 + (RSI decrease / 15) × 15). For double bottoms, use the RSI increase and require it to be positive. Volume exhaustion score is 0 unless second-pivot volume is lower; otherwise it is min(25, 10 + min((first volume − second volume) / max(first volume, 1), 1) × 15). Height score is 0 if pattern height / ATR is below the minimum height multiple; otherwise it is min(25, 10 + min(max((pattern height / ATR − minimum height multiple) / (2 × minimum height multiple), 0), 1) × 15). Round the total to one decimal place.
Calculation: Maximum allowed price difference = ATR at the second pivot × peak tolerance multiple.
Calculation: Short stop = higher of the two top pivots + breakout-bar ATR × stop buffer multiple; short target = neckline − pattern height × 1.618.
Calculation: Long stop = lower of the two bottom pivots − breakout-bar ATR × stop buffer multiple; long target = neckline + pattern height × 1.618.
Long: A confirmed second pivot low has an earlier pivot low whose separation is within the minimum and maximum bar distances, whose price difference is no greater than the maximum allowed price difference, and which is the nearest earlier pivot satisfying the pattern conditions; the highest high between the pivots is above both pivot lows, no intervening low is below the lower pivot low minus half the maximum allowed price difference, pattern height is at least ATR at the second pivot × minimum height multiple, and pattern quality score ≥ minimum score threshold OR (a valid such pattern is awaiting breakout, is not invalidated by a low below the lower pivot low minus current ATR × stop buffer multiple, has not exceeded the maximum breakout wait, and the close crosses above its neckline from a prior close at or below it or closes above it on the second pivot's confirmation bar, with close above the EMA when the optional trend filter is enabled).
Short: A confirmed second pivot high has an earlier pivot high whose separation is within the minimum and maximum bar distances, whose price difference is no greater than the maximum allowed price difference, and which is the nearest earlier pivot satisfying the pattern conditions; the lowest low between the pivots is below both pivot highs, no intervening high is above the higher pivot high plus half the maximum allowed price difference, pattern height is at least ATR at the second pivot × minimum height multiple, and pattern quality score ≥ minimum score threshold OR (a valid such pattern is awaiting breakout, is not invalidated by a high above the higher pivot high plus current ATR × stop buffer multiple, has not exceeded the maximum breakout wait, and the close crosses below its neckline from a prior close at or above it or closes below it on the second pivot's confirmation bar, with close below the EMA when the optional trend filter is enabled).
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
