Calculation: LongScore = count of true among: (1) EMA(close, short period) > EMA(close, medium period) > EMA(close, long period); (2) RSI(close) > 50 AND RSI < overbought level (above 50); (3) close > session-anchored VWAP of hlc3 (resets each session); (4) volume > multiplier (>1) * SMA(volume); (5) low > low 2 bars ago AND RSI < RSI 2 bars ago AND RSI < 55; (6) high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) > 0.5
Calculation: ShortScore = count of true among: (1) EMA(close, short period) < EMA(close, medium period) < EMA(close, long period); (2) RSI(close) < 50 AND RSI > oversold level (below 50); (3) close < session-anchored VWAP of hlc3; (4) volume > multiplier (>1) * SMA(volume); (5) high < high 2 bars ago AND RSI > RSI 2 bars ago AND RSI > 45; (6) high > low AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) > 0.5
Long: LongScore >= minimum score AND LongScore > previous bar's LongScore AND more than 3 bars have passed since the last Long signal (entry at the signal bar's close)
Short: ShortScore >= minimum score AND ShortScore > previous bar's ShortScore AND more than 3 bars have passed since the last Short signal (entry at the signal bar's close)
Exit: long, high >= entry price + TP multiple * ATR OR low <= entry price - SL multiple * ATR (ATR taken at the entry bar; TP multiple > SL multiple)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - TP multiple * ATR OR high >= entry price + SL multiple * ATR (ATR taken at the entry bar; TP multiple > SL multiple)
