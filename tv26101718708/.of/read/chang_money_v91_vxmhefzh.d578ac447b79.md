Calculation: High-volume bar = a non-doji bar whose volume oscillator is positive and at least a threshold fraction of its highest value over a lookback; the oscillator is volume divided by its highest value over that lookback, multiplied by a scale factor, minus an EMA of that scaled ratio.
Calculation: Sweep levels = on a higher timeframe, use the bar still forming and the preceding completed bars. Start the high-sweep level at the immediately preceding high. Replace it successively with an older high within a finite lookback when the forming high exceeds that high, both the forming open and close are at or below it, it exceeds the current level, and the forming high is at least every intervening high. A high sweep exists when the forming high exceeds the selected level and its open and close are at or below the level, or its open is above the immediately preceding high and its close is below it; cancel it if the close exceeds the selected level. Define the low-sweep level and low sweep symmetrically, reversing highs, lows, and inequalities.
Calculation: Sweep-zone qualification = reset the high-zone count, maximum body, and high-volume flag whenever the forming higher-timeframe high changes. Count chart bars whose close is between the high-sweep level and the forming high, inclusive, provided the high exceeds the level; track their largest absolute body. Set the high-volume flag when a high-volume chart bar overlaps the interval from the high-sweep level to the forming high. The high zone qualifies when its count is at least a threshold, its flag is set, its maximum body is positive, and its height is at most a threshold multiple of that body. Define the low zone symmetrically, resetting when the forming higher-timeframe low changes.
Calculation: Confirmation levels = use a second higher timeframe, shorter than the sweep timeframe, with its bar still forming and its completed bars. When a chart bar makes the forming sweep-timeframe high and exceeds the preceding sweep-timeframe high, reset bearish confirmation. If the preceding confirmation-timeframe bar is bearish or a doji and the forming bar is bullish, set the bearish level to the forming bar's open and its timestamp to that bar's start. Otherwise, search completed confirmation bars backward within a finite lookback, stopping if an examined high exceeds the forming confirmation bar's high, for the most recent bullish bar immediately preceded by a bearish bar or doji. Set the level to the lowest open among bullish completed bars from that bar through the latest completed bar. If that level exceeds the forming bar's open, replace it with the forming open when the forming bar is non-bearish, or with the forming low when it is bearish; timestamp either replacement at the current chart bar. Retain the previous level if no candidate is found. Define the bullish level symmetrically: reset at a new sweep-timeframe low below the preceding low; seek a bearish bar preceded by a bullish bar or doji, use the highest bearish open, stop at an examined low below the forming low, and replace a level below the forming open with the forming open when non-bullish or the forming high when bullish.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation = at the close of a confirmation-timeframe bar, its close exceeds the bullish level, the chart-bar timestamp is later than the level timestamp, bullish confirmation is inactive, and the low sweep zone qualifies. Activate it and save the level; reset it when the chart low makes a new sweep-timeframe low below the preceding sweep-timeframe low or falls below the low recorded at the most recent such reset. After activation, set the bullish level to an unreachable upper sentinel until a subsequent level update. Bearish confirmation is symmetric, using a close below the bearish level, the high zone, new highs, and an unreachable lower sentinel.
Calculation: Compression = the displaced shorter-lookback Ichimoku span B lies inclusively between a shorter and a longer close EMA for a consecutive number of chart bars, and a bullish or bearish confirmation activation occurred within a lookback. The span uses chart bars and its displacement is the corresponding Ichimoku forward displacement. Track the latest compression bar and the highest high and lowest low of all compression bars since the previous breakout or compression timeout; include the current bar's high and low when evaluating a breakout.
Calculation: Active volume wall = for each of three successively longer wall timeframes, retain the high and low of its most recent high-volume bar, using the bar still forming when higher than the chart timeframe and the last intrabar when lower. If the retained ranges overlap, use their intersection. Otherwise use the range of the latest qualifying session bar: in each configured session retain the latest bar whose volume equals the running session maximum and exceeds a threshold multiple of a recursively smoothed volume average; publish its range when that session ends. If no qualifying range exists, the wall is absent.
Calculation: Alignment = on each of three successively longer radar timeframes, positive directional movement exceeds negative directional movement and MACD exceeds its signal for bullish alignment; reverse both comparisons for bearish alignment. Use the still-forming bar for a radar timeframe higher than the chart timeframe and the last intrabar for one lower than the chart timeframe; use the chart bar when equal.
Calculation: Cloud bounds = the minimum and maximum of the displaced Ichimoku spans A and B calculated on chart bars. Use two clouds with corresponding longer lookbacks and a longer displacement for the second cloud.
Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = two bars ago is bearish, the previous bar is bullish, the previous body top exceeds the body top two bars ago, and previous volume is at most volume two bars ago. Bearish reversal pattern = two bars ago is bullish, the previous bar is bearish, its body bottom is below the body bottom two bars ago, and previous volume is at most volume two bars ago.
Long: Bullish confirmation changes from inactive to active AND the sweep timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe AND the confirmation timeframe is at least the chart timeframe.
Short: Bearish confirmation changes from inactive to active AND the sweep timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe AND the confirmation timeframe is at least the chart timeframe.
Long: A fixed number of chart bars have elapsed since bullish confirmation activation AND bullish confirmation remains active AND its saved level equals its value at activation.
Short: A fixed number of chart bars have elapsed since bearish confirmation activation AND bearish confirmation remains active AND its saved level equals its value at activation.
Long: Bullish alignment becomes true AND the latest compression is within a lookback AND close exceeds both cloud upper bounds AND a confirmation activation is within a shorter lookback AND (the active volume wall is absent OR the compression range high exceeds the wall top AND the wall does not lie wholly inside the compression range).
Short: Bearish alignment becomes true AND the latest compression is within a lookback AND close is below both cloud lower bounds AND a confirmation activation is within a shorter lookback AND (the active volume wall is absent OR the compression range low is below the wall bottom AND the wall does not lie wholly inside the compression range).
Long: The latest breakout was long and occurred within a lookback AND the bullish reversal pattern is true AND close is outside both clouds AND no cloud-exit event has occurred since the latest compression or breakout AND the latest compression is within a shorter lookback.
Short: The latest breakout was short and occurred within a lookback AND the bearish reversal pattern is true AND close is outside both clouds AND no cloud-exit event has occurred since the latest compression or breakout AND the latest compression is within a shorter lookback.
Exit: long OR short, the latest breakout occurred within a lookback AND either reversal pattern is true AND close is inside either cloud, inclusive AND no earlier cloud-exit event has occurred since the latest compression or breakout.
