Calculation: Bull CISD = a qualifying low sweep of one of the four most recent completed higher-timeframe bars, followed by a close above the bullish CISD level on a completed middle-timeframe bar; the sweep zone must contain a high-volume bar, at least the configured minimum number of chart bars, and have height no greater than 1.6 times the largest candle body in that zone. The bullish CISD level is selected from the middle-timeframe candle sequence around the sweep low as the code specifies.
Calculation: Bear CISD = a qualifying high sweep of one of the four most recent completed higher-timeframe bars, followed by a close below the bearish CISD level on a completed middle-timeframe bar; the sweep zone must contain a high-volume bar, at least the configured minimum number of chart bars, and have height no greater than 1.6 times the largest candle body in that zone. The bearish CISD level is selected from the middle-timeframe candle sequence around the sweep high as the code specifies.
Calculation: Bullish CISD confirmation = bullish CISD first became active exactly three chart bars ago AND remains active AND its level is unchanged since then.
Calculation: Bearish CISD confirmation = bearish CISD first became active exactly three chart bars ago AND remains active AND its level is unchanged since then.
Calculation: Bullish multi-timeframe momentum = on each of the three requested lower-timeframe series, +DI > −DI AND MACD line > MACD signal line.
Calculation: Bearish multi-timeframe momentum = on each of the three requested lower-timeframe series, −DI > +DI AND MACD line < MACD signal line.
Calculation: Kumo cloud boundaries = Ichimoku span A and span B, using shorter and longer lookbacks; use the values displaced back by their plotted offsets.
Calculation: EMA squeeze = the shorter cloud span lies between the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs for three consecutive chart bars, and a bullish or bearish CISD first became active within the preceding 52 chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish squeeze breakout = bullish multi-timeframe momentum first becomes true on this bar AND an EMA squeeze occurred within the preceding 52 chart bars AND close is above both cloud tops AND a CISD event occurred within the preceding 26 chart bars AND the current box high exceeds the active volume-wall top or no wall exists, with no wall overlapping the box.
Calculation: Bearish squeeze breakout = bearish multi-timeframe momentum first becomes true on this bar AND an EMA squeeze occurred within the preceding 52 chart bars AND close is below both cloud bottoms AND a CISD event occurred within the preceding 26 chart bars AND the current box low is below the active volume-wall bottom or no wall exists, with no wall overlapping the box.
Calculation: Up SK pattern = the candle two bars ago is bearish, the prior candle is bullish, the prior candle body high exceeds the two-bars-ago body high, and prior volume is no greater than volume two bars ago.
Calculation: Down SK pattern = the candle two bars ago is bullish, the prior candle is bearish, the two-bars-ago body low exceeds the prior candle body low, and prior volume is no greater than volume two bars ago.
Long: bullish CISD confirmation.
Long: bullish squeeze breakout.
Long: a bullish squeeze breakout occurred within the preceding 52 chart bars AND the up SK pattern is true AND close is outside both clouds AND no cloud-trap exit has occurred since that breakout AND the bar is within 17 bars of the latest EMA squeeze.
Short: bearish CISD confirmation.
Short: bearish squeeze breakout.
Short: a bearish squeeze breakout occurred within the preceding 52 chart bars AND the down SK pattern is true AND close is outside both clouds AND no cloud-trap exit has occurred since that breakout AND the bar is within 17 bars of the latest EMA squeeze.
Exit: long or short, a squeeze breakout occurred within the preceding 52 chart bars AND either SK pattern is true AND close is inside either cloud AND no cloud-trap exit has occurred since that breakout.
