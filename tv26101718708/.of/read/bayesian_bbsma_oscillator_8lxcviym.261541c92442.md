Calculation: For each of the upper Bollinger Band, Bollinger basis, and close SMA, the up probability = count of closes above it over the lookback divided by the total count of closes above or below it; the down probability = count below divided by that same total. Closes equal to the level are excluded.
Calculation: Down oscillator = zero when undefined; otherwise (((upper up probability × basis up probability × SMA up probability) / upper up probability) × basis up probability × SMA up probability) + ((1 − upper up probability) × (1 − basis up probability) × (1 − SMA up probability)).
Calculation: Up oscillator = zero when undefined; otherwise (((upper down probability × basis down probability × SMA down probability) / upper down probability) × basis down probability × SMA down probability) + ((1 − upper down probability) × (1 − basis down probability) × (1 − SMA down probability)).
Calculation: Prime probability = zero when undefined; otherwise (((down oscillator × up oscillator) / down oscillator) × up oscillator) + ((1 − down oscillator) × (1 − up oscillator) × (1 − up oscillator)).
Long: (prime probability > threshold AND previous bar's prime probability = 0 OR up oscillator < 1 AND previous bar's up oscillator = 1) AND (confirmation disabled OR (AC is rising AND AO is rising AND open > Alligator jaw AND close > Alligator jaw))
Short: (prime probability = 0 AND previous bar's prime probability > threshold OR down oscillator < 1 AND previous bar's down oscillator = 1) AND (confirmation disabled OR (AC is not rising AND open < Alligator jaw AND close < Alligator jaw))
