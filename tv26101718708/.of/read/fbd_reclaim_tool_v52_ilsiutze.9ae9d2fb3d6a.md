Calculation: Major support and resistance levels = separately configured sets of price levels.
Calculation: Long setup = when no long position is open, select the lowest major support whose chart-bar low is at least a flush-depth parameter below it, or at or below it if that level was previously armed on the same New York calendar day. Arm a level when first selected after a flush-depth breach; clear armed levels at the start of each new New York calendar day. While a setup is pending, replace it only with a lower qualifying support. Start or restart the setup at the first completed lower-timeframe bar in the chart bar whose low reaches the applicable threshold; if none exists, use the chart-bar opening time. Expire the setup when the chart-bar opening time exceeds its start time by more than the validity-window parameter.
Calculation: Short setup = when no short position is open, select the highest major resistance whose chart-bar high is at least a flush-depth parameter above it, or at or above it if that level was previously armed on the same New York calendar day. Arm a level when first selected after a flush-depth breach; clear armed levels at the start of each new New York calendar day. While a setup is pending, replace it only with a higher qualifying resistance. Start or restart the setup at the first completed lower-timeframe bar in the chart bar whose high reaches the applicable threshold; if none exists, use the chart-bar opening time. Expire the setup when the chart-bar opening time exceeds its start time by more than the validity-window parameter.
Calculation: Acceptance time = from the setup start, accumulate elapsed time represented by completed lower-timeframe bars whose close is at or beyond the selected level in the reclaim direction. Consecutive time on the other side does not add to acceptance; reset accumulated acceptance to zero when that consecutive time reaches the dip-buffer parameter. Reset the consecutive time on the other side to zero whenever a bar closes at or beyond the level in the reclaim direction. Evaluate acceptance after processing all lower-timeframe bars in the chart bar.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the stop-distance parameter, or, if the structural-stop option is enabled and the nearest major support below the selected support is lower, that support. Long target = entry price plus the target-distance parameter, or, if the structural-target option is enabled and the nearest major resistance above entry is at least that distance from entry, that resistance.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the stop-distance parameter, or, if the structural-stop option is enabled and the nearest major resistance above the selected resistance is higher, that resistance. Short target = entry price minus the target-distance parameter, or, if the structural-target option is enabled and the nearest major support below entry is at least that distance from entry, that support.
Long: Long setup is active AND long acceptance time is at least the long acceptance-time parameter; entry price equals selected support plus an entry-offset parameter.
Short: Short setup is active AND short acceptance time is at least the short acceptance-time parameter; entry price equals selected resistance minus an entry-offset parameter.
Exit: long, chart-bar low is at or below the long stop OR chart-bar high is at or above the long target; stop takes precedence if both are reached.
Exit: short, chart-bar high is at or above the short stop OR chart-bar low is at or below the short target; stop takes precedence if both are reached.
