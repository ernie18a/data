Calculation: Long acceptance time = accumulated duration of lower-timeframe bars whose close is at or above the support, starting at the flush event; reset the accumulation after consecutive time below support reaches the dip-reset duration. Use completed lower-timeframe bars historically and include the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Short acceptance time = accumulated duration of lower-timeframe bars whose close is at or below the resistance, starting at the sweep event; reset the accumulation after consecutive time above resistance reaches the pop-reset duration. Use completed lower-timeframe bars historically and include the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the configured stop distance, or the next lower major support if structural stops are enabled and that support is farther away.
Calculation: Long target = the nearest major resistance above entry if structural targets are enabled and it is at least the configured minimum target distance away; otherwise entry price plus the configured target distance.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the configured stop distance, or the next higher major resistance if structural stops are enabled and that resistance is farther away.
Calculation: Short target = the nearest major support below entry if structural targets are enabled and it is at least the configured minimum target distance away; otherwise entry price minus the configured target distance.
Long: A major support is swept when low is at or below support minus the configured flush depth, or a previously swept support is revisited when low is at or below support; the sweep remains valid until its configured timeout, and long acceptance time reaches its configured duration.
Short: A major resistance is swept when high is at or above resistance plus the configured flush depth, or a previously swept resistance is revisited when high is at or above resistance; the sweep remains valid until its configured timeout, and short acceptance time reaches its configured duration.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR (low > long stop AND high >= long target)
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR (high < short stop AND low <= short target)
