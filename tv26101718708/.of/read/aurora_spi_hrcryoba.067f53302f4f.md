Calculation: Analysis bars = completed lower-timeframe bars within each closed chart bar when a lower analysis timeframe is selected; otherwise use the closed chart bar. Process analysis bars in order, and reset session history and takeover zones at each configured session boundary.
Calculation: Estimated buy fraction = choose one model: candle direction gives 1 when close > open, 0 when close < open, otherwise 0. Intrabar direction gives 1 or 0 according to the most recent nonzero close-to-close change within contiguous bars of the same session, otherwise 0. The volatility model applies the standard normal CDF to the current close-to-close change divided by the population standard deviation of preceding changes over a rolling lookback, with the standardized change capped symmetrically; use 0.5 until the configured warmup is met or volatility is unavailable. Calculate volatility before adding the current change; retain its rolling history across sessions, but exclude changes across session boundaries or gaps exceeding 1.5 analysis-bar durations.
Calculation: Takeover row statistics = at each analysis bar, divide the session high-to-low range so far into equal price rows. Allocate each analysis bar's nonnegative volume to rows in proportion to overlap with its high-to-low range; allocate a zero-range bar's volume to its containing row. Estimated buy volume is allocated volume multiplied by the bar's estimated buy fraction. For each row calculate total and estimated buy volume over two consecutive equal-length windows, the current window ending at the analysis bar, and separately for each of a configurable number of most recent analysis bars. The confirmation count may differ from the window length. The peak is the maximum current-window row volume.
Calculation: Accepted takeover = rank qualifying rows separately for each direction by current-window volume, highest first. Starting at the highest remaining row, add adjacent qualifying rows up to a maximum width, choosing the higher-volume neighbor first and the lower row on ties. Remove the selected rows and their immediate neighbors from consideration and repeat up to a maximum number of zones per direction. A candidate is accepted only if it does not overlap an unbroken existing zone of the same direction and the direction's retained-zone limit has room or a broken zone can be replaced. A zone becomes broken on a later analysis bar when its close is below a buy zone's bottom or above a sell zone's top; accepting an overlapping opposite-direction zone also breaks it. Commit accepted takeovers at the close of the containing chart bar.
Long: A buy takeover is accepted AND its row's current-window and previous-window volumes are each at least a configured fraction of the current-window peak AND its current-window buy fraction is at least the dominance threshold AND its previous-window buy fraction is at most one minus that threshold AND every confirmation bar has positive volume in that row and a buy fraction at least the dominance threshold AND the session contains at least the configured minimum number of analysis bars, two complete windows, and the confirmation count.
Short: A sell takeover is accepted AND its row's current-window and previous-window volumes are each at least a configured fraction of the current-window peak AND its current-window buy fraction is at most one minus the dominance threshold AND its previous-window buy fraction is at least that threshold AND every confirmation bar has positive volume in that row and a buy fraction at most one minus the dominance threshold AND the session contains at least the configured minimum number of analysis bars, two complete windows, and the confirmation count.
