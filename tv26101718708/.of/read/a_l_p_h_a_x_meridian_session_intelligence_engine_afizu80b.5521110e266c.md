Calculation: Active session = the first enabled session containing the bar's opening hour in the configured timezone, in the order Asian, London, New York; otherwise OFF. A session with start hour before end hour contains hours from start inclusive to end exclusive; otherwise it contains hours at or after start or before end.
Calculation: Session state = on a change to an active session, reset session high and low to the current high and low, session VWAP to typical price, and opening-range high and low to the current high and low. On subsequent active bars, extend session high and low and set the session midpoint to their average; retain the previous midpoint on a session's first bar. Session VWAP is cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume since the session start; retain its previous value when cumulative volume is zero. Typical price is (high + low + close) / 3. On a direct change between active sessions, save the preceding session high and low as prior-session high and low; do not update them when entering a session from OFF. Retain session state while OFF.
Calculation: Opening range = in the second or third session, extend the opening-range high and low through bars whose opening time is within the configured duration from the configured session start, inclusive; lock the range on the first subsequent bar. Reset it on every active-session change and retain it while OFF.
Calculation: Bull rejection = close > open AND close - low > (high - low) / 2.
Calculation: Bear rejection = close < open AND high - close > (high - low) / 2.
Calculation: Bull retest = after close > prior-session high AND previous close <= current prior-session high, retain a pending state through the configured number of bars, including the break bar; reset its start on another such break and clear it on the first bull retest or after expiry. Bull retest requires that pending state AND low <= current prior-session high + ATR times tolerance multiplier AND close > current prior-session high AND bull rejection. Pending state persists across session changes.
Calculation: Bear retest = after close < prior-session low AND previous close >= current prior-session low, retain a pending state through the configured number of bars, including the break bar; reset its start on another such break and clear it on the first bear retest or after expiry. Bear retest requires that pending state AND high >= current prior-session low - ATR times tolerance multiplier AND close < current prior-session low AND bear rejection. Pending state persists across session changes.
Calculation: Bull opening breakout = opening range locked AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= current opening-range high AND bull rejection.
Calculation: Bear opening breakout = opening range locked AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= current opening-range low AND bear rejection.
Calculation: Bull midpoint bounce = close > session VWAP AND close > session midpoint AND low <= session midpoint + ATR times tolerance multiplier AND bull rejection.
Calculation: Bear midpoint bounce = close < session VWAP AND close < session midpoint AND high >= session midpoint - ATR times tolerance multiplier AND bear rejection.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bull alignment = the shorter-period EMA of close > the longer-period EMA of close AND close > the shorter-period EMA, calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bear alignment = the shorter-period EMA of close < the longer-period EMA of close AND close < the shorter-period EMA, calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bull score = sum one point each for close > session VWAP AND close > session midpoint; close > session VWAP; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bull alignment; active session enabled for trading; volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA times its multiplier; bull rejection; bull retest OR bull opening breakout OR bull midpoint bounce. Count setup points regardless of setup enable settings.
Calculation: Bear score = sum one point each for close < session VWAP AND close < session midpoint; close < session VWAP; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bear alignment; active session enabled for trading; volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA times its multiplier; bear rejection; bear retest OR bear opening breakout OR bear midpoint bounce. Count setup points regardless of setup enable settings.
Long: close > prior-session high AND previous close <= current prior-session high.
Short: close < prior-session low AND previous close >= current prior-session low.
Long: bull opening breakout.
Short: bear opening breakout.
Long: close > session VWAP.
Short: close < session VWAP.
Long: higher-timeframe bull alignment.
Short: higher-timeframe bear alignment.
Long: bull score >= minimum confluence score.
Short: bear score >= minimum confluence score.
Long: ((bull retest enabled AND bull retest) OR (opening breakout enabled AND bull opening breakout) OR (midpoint bounce enabled AND bull midpoint bounce)) AND bull score >= minimum confluence score AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA times its multiplier) AND bars since the most recent long or short entry signal >= cooldown, or no previous entry signal.
Short: ((bear retest enabled AND bear retest) OR (opening breakout enabled AND bear opening breakout) OR (midpoint bounce enabled AND bear midpoint bounce)) AND bear score >= minimum confluence score AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA times its multiplier) AND bars since the most recent long or short entry signal >= cooldown, or no previous entry signal.
