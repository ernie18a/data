Calculation: Bullish confluence count = Sum one for each true condition: volume price trend > its simple moving average; on-balance volume > its simple moving average; accumulation/distribution line > its simple moving average; Williams %R > a midpoint threshold; short-, medium-, and long-lookback close rate of change are all > 0; close-based CCI > 0 AND < an upper threshold; positive directional indicator > a strength threshold AND negative directional indicator > ADX; close > parabolic SAR.
Calculation: Bearish confluence count = Sum one for each true condition: volume price trend <= its simple moving average; on-balance volume <= its simple moving average; accumulation/distribution line <= its simple moving average; Williams %R <= a midpoint threshold; short-, medium-, and long-lookback close rate of change are all < 0; close-based CCI < 0 AND > a lower threshold; positive directional indicator > a strength threshold AND ADX > negative directional indicator; close <= parabolic SAR. For the four comparisons using <=, also count the bearish condition when the corresponding bullish comparison is unavailable.
Calculation: Long entry trigger = According to the selected method: fast close EMA crosses above medium close EMA; OR MACD line crosses above its signal line AND RSI is above a middle threshold AND below an upper threshold; OR close rises on every bar over a specified consecutive lookback AND close > fast close EMA AND RSI is between lower and upper momentum thresholds; OR, for the hybrid method, fast close EMA crosses above medium close EMA OR MACD line > signal line AND RSI > a hybrid threshold OR close > fast close EMA AND close > previous close AND previous close > close two bars ago. A crossover requires the current first value > the second and the previous first value <= the second.
Calculation: Short entry trigger = According to the selected method: fast close EMA crosses below medium close EMA; OR MACD line crosses below its signal line AND RSI is below a middle threshold AND above a lower threshold; OR close falls on every bar over a specified consecutive lookback AND close < fast close EMA AND RSI is between lower and upper momentum thresholds; OR, for the hybrid method, fast close EMA crosses below medium close EMA OR MACD line < signal line AND RSI < a hybrid threshold OR close < fast close EMA AND close < previous close AND previous close < close two bars ago. A crossunder requires the current first value < the second and the previous first value >= the second.
