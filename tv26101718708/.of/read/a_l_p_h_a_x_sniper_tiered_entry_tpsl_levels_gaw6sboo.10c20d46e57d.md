Calculation: Trend score = sum of four contributions: +1 for fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, -1 for fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA, otherwise 0; +1 for fast EMA > its previous value > its value two bars ago, -1 for the reverse, otherwise 0; +1 for medium EMA > its previous value, -1 for medium EMA < its previous value, otherwise 0; +1 for close > anchor EMA, -1 for close < anchor EMA, otherwise 0. EMA lookbacks increase from fast to medium to slow to anchor.
Calculation: Stochastic K = stochastic of close, high and low; stochastic D = SMA of K. An upward stochastic cross is K crossing above D AND K < the overbought threshold; a downward stochastic cross is K crossing below D AND K > the oversold threshold. An oversold recovery is previous K < the oversold threshold AND current K > it; an overbought recovery is previous K > the overbought threshold AND current K < it.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / volume SMA when the SMA > 0, otherwise a neutral ratio. Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0. Volatility ratio = ATR / close. London is the UTC hour from the London open inclusive to the New York close exclusive; New York is from the New York open inclusive to the New York close exclusive; Asia is from the Asia open inclusive to the Asia close exclusive.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = clamp to the score range the sum of the following, using the first matching case within each component: a strong, moderate, or mild trend award according to whether signed trend score reaches the respective descending thresholds; an RSI recovery award when RSI crosses its directional pullback threshold toward the direction, otherwise a smaller pullback award when RSI is inside the directional pullback zone and between the lower and upper RSI bounds; a largest stochastic award when the directional stochastic cross AND directional extreme-zone recovery both occur, otherwise a smaller award for the cross, otherwise a still smaller award for the recovery; the full MACD award when the MACD filter is disabled, otherwise the full award when MACD is aligned with the direction AND its histogram moves in that direction, otherwise a smaller award when MACD is aligned; a largest volume award when volume ratio > the spike threshold, otherwise a smaller award when it > the above-average threshold, otherwise a still smaller award when the optional volume filter passes; a larger candle award when close is directional AND body ratio >= the minimum, otherwise a smaller award when close is directional; an award when the directional fast bias EMA is on the directional side of the slow bias EMA; an award for London OR New York; minus an RSI penalty for the more extreme opposing RSI threshold, otherwise a smaller penalty for the less extreme opposing threshold; minus a low-volatility penalty when the enabled volatility filter fails; minus an Asia penalty during Asia; minus the larger of two extension penalties when abs(close - anchor EMA) / anchor EMA exceeds the higher extension threshold, otherwise the smaller penalty when it exceeds the lower threshold; minus the larger of two age penalties when either consecutive bullish or bearish ribbon count exceeds the longer age threshold, otherwise the smaller penalty when either count exceeds the shorter threshold. Bullish and bearish ribbons require fast, medium and slow EMAs to be strictly ordered in the respective direction; their consecutive counts reset to zero when their respective ordering fails. Bias EMAs use the chart timeframe and lookbacks obtained by multiplying the fast and slow trend EMA lookbacks by the same factor. For the bullish pullback zone RSI <= the bullish pullback threshold AND RSI > its lower bound; for the bearish zone RSI >= the bearish pullback threshold AND RSI < its upper bound. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA lookback and a signal EMA lookback.
Long: (RSI crosses above the bullish pullback threshold OR upward stochastic cross OR oversold recovery) AND close > open AND trend score >= the mild bullish threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio >= its minimum) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility ratio >= its minimum) AND (body filter disabled OR body ratio >= its minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR London OR New York) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line > signal line) AND bullish confluence score >= the minimum signal score AND bars since the last long signal >= the cooldown.
Short: (RSI crosses below the bearish pullback threshold OR downward stochastic cross OR overbought recovery) AND close < open AND trend score <= the mild bearish threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio >= its minimum) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility ratio >= its minimum) AND (body filter disabled OR body ratio >= its minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR London OR New York) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line < signal line) AND bearish confluence score >= the minimum signal score AND bars since the last short signal >= the cooldown.
Long: fast EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > slow EMA.
Short: fast EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < slow EMA.
Long: trend score >= the mild bullish threshold.
Short: trend score <= the mild bearish threshold.
Long: MACD line > signal line.
Short: MACD line < signal line.
Long: fast bias EMA > slow bias EMA.
Short: fast bias EMA < slow bias EMA.
Long: RSI < the dashboard bullish RSI threshold OR stochastic K < the oversold threshold.
Short: RSI > the dashboard bearish RSI threshold OR stochastic K > the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Fixed stop and target levels = entry price minus or plus entry-bar ATR multiplied by the respective stop or target multiplier; use minus for a long stop and short targets, plus for a short stop and long targets. There are three independently tracked targets with increasing multipliers. Each new entry signal replaces the active entry, levels and target-hit flags, and a stop hit or trailing-stop breach clears the active side. Evaluate target hits before the fixed stop, including on the entry bar.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on each active long bar, the maximum of its previous value and close minus current ATR times the trail multiplier; on each active short bar, the minimum of its previous value and close plus current ATR times the trail multiplier. If no previous trail exists, use the current candidate. Clear it when the active side is cleared; a new signal does not itself clear an existing trail. Evaluate it after the fixed stop and update it before testing the current bar.
Exit: long, high >= any fixed target AND that target has not already been hit for the active entry.
Exit: short, low <= any fixed target AND that target has not already been hit for the active entry.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop OR low < trailing stop.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop OR high > trailing stop.
