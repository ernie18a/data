Calculation: RSI source = RSI of close on a configurable timeframe; use the chart bar if timeframes match, the last completed higher-timeframe bar if higher, or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar if lower.
Calculation: Average entry price = total quote-currency order size divided by the sum of each filled order's quote-currency size divided by its fill close. Reset on exit; include the base entry and each filled averaging order.
Long: Active window filter is disabled OR chart bar datetime is within the inclusive active window; AND position is flat AND RSI source < RSI threshold. Fill the base order at close.
Long: (Active window filter is disabled OR chart bar datetime is within the inclusive active window) AND position is long AND an averaging order remains unfilled AND close <= base entry price × (1 − the next averaging order's deviation). Fill only the next averaging order at close, in configured order, at most one per bar; each order has its own deviation and quote-currency size.
Exit: long, (active window filter is disabled OR chart bar datetime is within the inclusive active window) AND close >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction). Evaluate after any entry and averaging-order fill on the same bar.
