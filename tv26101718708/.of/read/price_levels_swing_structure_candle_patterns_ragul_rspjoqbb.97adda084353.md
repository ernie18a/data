Calculation: Swing high = a bar whose high is the highest among itself and the N bars before and after it; confirm N bars later.
Calculation: Swing low = a bar whose low is the lowest among itself and the N bars before and after it; confirm N bars later.
Calculation: Bullish hammer = range > 0 AND lower wick >= 2 × body AND upper wick <= 0.1 × range
Calculation: Inverted hammer = range > 0 AND upper wick >= 2 × body AND lower wick <= 0.1 × range
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle closes above its open AND previous candle closes below its open AND current close > previous open AND current open < previous close
Calculation: Hanging man = range > 0 AND lower wick >= 2 × body AND upper wick <= 0.1 × range AND close < open
Calculation: Shooting star = range > 0 AND upper wick >= 2 × body AND lower wick <= 0.1 × range
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle closes below its open AND previous candle closes above its open AND current close < previous open AND current open > previous close
Long: At swing high confirmation, pivot high > previous pivot high OR no previous pivot high; OR at swing low confirmation, pivot low > previous pivot low OR no previous pivot low; OR a confirmed swing high or swing low has a bullish hammer, inverted hammer, or bullish engulfing pattern on the pivot bar
Short: At swing high confirmation, pivot high <= previous pivot high; OR at swing low confirmation, pivot low <= previous pivot low; OR a confirmed swing high or swing low has a hanging man, shooting star, or bearish engulfing pattern on the pivot bar
