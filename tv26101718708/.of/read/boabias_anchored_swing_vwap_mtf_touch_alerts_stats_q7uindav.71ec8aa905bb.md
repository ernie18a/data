Calculation: Swing-anchored VWAP = For each confirmed swing high or low, divide cumulative typical price times volume by cumulative volume from the pivot bar through the current bar, using (high + low + close) / 3 as typical price and a small positive floor for volume. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks. Keep separate active lines for swing highs and lows. Starting only when the number of bars since the pivot is greater than the pivot lookback plus two, deactivate a high-anchored line after the configured number of consecutive closes above its VWAP, or a low-anchored line after the configured number of consecutive closes below its VWAP; the configured number may be one. Evaluate chart-timeframe touches before deactivation on the current bar. For higher-timeframe lines, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar with no lookahead, carry its values forward between completions, and exclude lines deactivated on that completed bar. Maintain the source's separate capacity limits for chart and higher-timeframe active lines.
Long: Chart low <= any active chart-timeframe swing-low-anchored VWAP AND chart high >= the same VWAP.
Short: Chart low <= any active chart-timeframe swing-high-anchored VWAP AND chart high >= the same VWAP.
