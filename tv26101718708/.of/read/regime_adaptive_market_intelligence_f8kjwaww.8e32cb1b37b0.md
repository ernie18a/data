Calculation: Normalized EMA separation = (short-lookback EMA of close − long-lookback EMA of close) / ATR; both use completed chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: Normalized EMA slope = (long-lookback EMA of close − its value several completed chart-timeframe bars earlier) / ATR
Calculation: Bull regime = Bull candidate confirmed for the trend-confirmation count of consecutive completed chart bars, or prior Bull state retained while ADX ≥ trend-exit threshold and normalized EMA separation and slope are both positive; otherwise retain a confirmed candidate state, or Transition if the prior state cannot be retained
Calculation: Bear regime = Bear candidate confirmed for the trend-confirmation count of consecutive completed chart bars, or prior Bear state retained while ADX ≥ trend-exit threshold and normalized EMA separation and slope are both negative; otherwise retain a confirmed candidate state, or Transition if the prior state cannot be retained
Long: ADX ≥ trend-entry threshold AND normalized EMA separation ≥ positive trend-entry threshold AND normalized EMA slope ≥ positive trend-entry threshold for the trend-confirmation count of consecutive completed chart-timeframe bars, OR Bull regime
Short: ADX ≥ trend-entry threshold AND normalized EMA separation ≤ negative trend-entry threshold AND normalized EMA slope ≤ negative trend-entry threshold for the trend-confirmation count of consecutive completed chart-timeframe bars, OR Bear regime
