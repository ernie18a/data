Calculation: Adaptive centric average = Let smoothed price, high, and low be SMAs of close, high, and low using the same lookback N. Let H and L be the highest smoothed high and lowest smoothed low over N bars. Let stochastic = 100 × (smoothed price − lowest L over N bars) / (highest H over N bars − lowest L over N bars). Let alpha = (abs(stochastic − 50) / 100 / attenuation)^power, midpoint = (H + L) / 2, and adjusted price = midpoint + (smoothed price − midpoint) / attenuation. The average is the previous average, or zero if unavailable, plus alpha × (adjusted price − previous average).
Calculation: Structure levels = A bullish break is close > previous high; otherwise a bearish break is close < previous low. On a bearish break immediately following a bullish break, calculate a candidate high as the highest high over the bars since the most recent bearish break plus a longer offset; if that equals the previous corrected high, use a shorter offset instead. On a bullish break immediately following a bearish break, calculate a candidate low symmetrically using the most recent bullish break. Each corrected value is its current candidate when available, otherwise the previous bar's candidate. Track the bar of each candidate. Initialize the major high and low to zero. Replace the major high with the corrected high if the major high is zero, the corrected high is higher, or the corrected high is lower and the previous bar had a bearish structure break at or after the latest candidate-high bar. Replace the major low symmetrically if zero, the corrected low is lower, or it is higher and the previous bar had a bullish structure break at or after the latest candidate-low bar. Otherwise retain the major level. A bullish structure break occurs when close > current major high AND previous close <= current major high; otherwise a bearish structure break occurs when close < current major low AND previous close >= current major low. For each side, the order-block reference bar is the bar immediately before the latest change in its corrected low for demand or corrected high for supply. Initialize each last-used reference to zero.
Long: close > SMA of close with the shortest lookback
Short: close <= SMA of close with the shortest lookback
Long: SMA of close with an intermediate lookback > its previous value
Short: SMA of close with an intermediate lookback <= its previous value
Long: intermediate-lookback SMA of close > longer-lookback SMA of close AND abs(close − longer-lookback SMA) / longer-lookback SMA >= distance threshold
Short: intermediate-lookback SMA of close < longer-lookback SMA of close AND abs(close − longer-lookback SMA) / longer-lookback SMA >= distance threshold
Long: high − low > ATR × range multiplier AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > body threshold AND close > open
Short: high − low > ATR × range multiplier AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > body threshold AND close < open
Long: low > high two bars ago
Short: high < low two bars ago
Long: close > EMA of close on a higher timeframe AND previous close <= the higher-timeframe EMA value mapped to the previous chart bar; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the current higher-timeframe bar when it completes
Short: close < EMA of close on a higher timeframe AND previous close >= the higher-timeframe EMA value mapped to the previous chart bar; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the current higher-timeframe bar when it completes
Long: bullish structure break AND demand order-block reference bar differs from the last-used demand reference; update the last-used demand reference to that bar
Short: bearish structure break AND supply order-block reference bar differs from the last-used supply reference; update the last-used supply reference to that bar
Long: SMA of close used in the adaptive centric average > adaptive centric average
Short: SMA of close used in the adaptive centric average <= adaptive centric average
