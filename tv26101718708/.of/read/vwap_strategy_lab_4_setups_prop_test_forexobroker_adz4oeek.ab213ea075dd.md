Calculation: Session VWAP and bands = volume-weighted average of hlc3 since the start of the current trading day, and that VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiple of the volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3 over the same period.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 from the first bar of the previous trading day's highest high or lowest low, respectively, through the current bar. At the start of a new session, limit the initial accumulation to the current bar and a configurable maximum number of preceding bars.
Calculation: First qualifying touch = the first bar in a continuous run of closes strictly on the indicated side of the relevant VWAP that meets the stated preceding-run and touch conditions; reset when the close is no longer strictly on that side or a new session starts. Mark the touch as used even if no trade is entered.
Calculation: Valid break = after the required consecutive session closes on one side of the session VWAP, a close strictly on the opposite side; it remains valid through a configurable number of subsequent bars, unless a close moves strictly back across the VWAP or the first qualifying retest occurs. Reset at a new session; consume a qualifying retest even if no trade is entered.
Calculation: Band excursion = consecutive processed closes strictly outside the same session VWAP band, beginning no earlier than the configured session age; retain their highest high for an upper excursion or lowest low for a lower excursion. End the excursion at the first processed close no longer outside that band or at a new session.
Calculation: Entry risk = maximum of a configurable ATR multiple and the distance from entry close to the proposed stop. For first-touch and retest entries, the proposed stop is the entry-bar low minus an ATR buffer for a long, or high plus that buffer for a short. For band entries, it is the lower of the excursion low and entry-bar low minus the buffer for a long, or the higher of the excursion high and entry-bar high plus the buffer for a short. Fix the stop at entry price minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short. Fix the target at entry price plus a configurable risk multiple for a long or minus it for a short; for band entries, fix the target at the session VWAP at entry instead.
Long: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND at least the configured number of immediately preceding session closes are above session VWAP AND low <= session VWAP AND close > session VWAP AND this is the first qualifying touch.
Short: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND at least the configured number of immediately preceding session closes are below session VWAP AND high >= session VWAP AND close < session VWAP AND this is the first qualifying touch.
Long: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND an upward valid break exists from a prior bar AND low <= session VWAP AND close > session VWAP.
Short: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND a downward valid break exists from a prior bar AND high >= session VWAP AND close < session VWAP.
Long: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND session age in bars after its first bar >= the configured minimum AND a lower band excursion existed on the preceding bar AND close >= lower band AND close < session VWAP.
Short: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND session age in bars after its first bar >= the configured minimum AND an upper band excursion existed on the preceding bar AND close <= upper band AND close > session VWAP.
Long: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND at least the configured number of immediately preceding session closes are above the previous-low anchored VWAP AND low <= that VWAP AND close > that VWAP AND this is the first qualifying touch.
Short: Current bar is not the first bar of the session AND current volume > 0 AND ATR is available AND the corresponding setup has no open position AND at least the configured number of immediately preceding session closes are below the previous-high anchored VWAP AND high >= that VWAP AND close < that VWAP AND this is the first qualifying touch.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed target; execute at open if open <= stop, otherwise at stop if low <= stop, otherwise at target.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed target; execute at open if open >= stop, otherwise at stop if high >= stop, otherwise at target.
Exit: long OR short, session-end exit is enabled AND the current bar is the first bar of a new session AND the position was entered in an earlier session; execute at open before checking stops or targets.
