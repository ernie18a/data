Calculation: Move score = (close - previous close) / population standard deviation of close-to-close changes over the statistics lookback including the current bar; use 0 if the standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low) if high > low; otherwise 0.5.
Long: Move score >= positive threshold AND close location >= close-location threshold AND high - low > simple moving average of high - low over the same statistics lookback including the current bar × range factor AND (volume filter disabled OR (volume > 0 AND simple moving average of volume including the current bar > 0 AND volume > that volume average × volume factor)) AND (structure filter disabled OR high > highest high over the structure lookback ending at the previous bar).
Short: Move score <= negative of the positive threshold AND close location <= 1 - close-location threshold AND high - low > simple moving average of high - low over the same statistics lookback including the current bar × range factor AND (volume filter disabled OR (volume > 0 AND simple moving average of volume including the current bar > 0 AND volume > that volume average × volume factor)) AND (structure filter disabled OR low < lowest low over the structure lookback ending at the previous bar).
