Calculation: Range = highest high minus lowest low over a lookback of N bars
Calculation: Compressed = Range <= the low-percentile (nearest-rank) value of Range over a longer rolling history window, OR Range / ATR < a tightness multiple
Calculation: Buffer = a fraction of ATR
Calculation: Box = a consolidation box created on a bar when no box is active AND Compressed AND the cooldown has elapsed. At creation, top = highest high and bottom = lowest low over the N-bar lookback, and BarsInBox = N. On each later bar BarsInBox increases by 1. On a bar where close is within [bottom - Buffer, top + Buffer], top = max(top, high) and bottom = min(bottom, low). The box closes on a Break in either direction, and a cooldown (a fixed number of bars) then blocks new boxes.
Long: Box active (from the bar after creation) AND BarsInBox >= minimum bars AND close > top + Buffer for a required number of consecutive bars (at least 1) AND (optional filter) volume > multiple * simple average of volume
Short: Box active (from the bar after creation) AND BarsInBox >= minimum bars AND close < bottom - Buffer for a required number of consecutive bars (at least 1) AND (optional filter) volume > multiple * simple average of volume
Long: Box active AND an earlier bar in the same box (probe, using the top/bottom as of the bar before it) had low < bottom AND close >= bottom AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= wick ratio AND this is the first later bar with close > previous close (the probe stays armed until it triggers, an opposite-side probe occurs, or the box closes)
Short: Box active AND an earlier bar in the same box (probe, using the top/bottom as of the bar before it) had high > top AND close <= top AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= wick ratio AND this is the first later bar with close < previous close (the probe stays armed until it triggers, an opposite-side probe occurs, or the box closes)
