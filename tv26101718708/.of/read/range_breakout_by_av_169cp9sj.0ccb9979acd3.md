Calculation: Compression = rolling high-low range is at or below its nearest-rank percentile over a longer history window (adaptive mode), or its range-to-ATR ratio is below a configurable threshold (ATR mode).
Calculation: Range state = when compression qualifies and cooldown is zero, activate a range with top and bottom equal to the lookback highest high and lowest low; while active, count bars and extend top/bottom to include each bar's high/low when its close is within top + buffer and bottom - buffer; deactivate on breakout and apply cooldown.
Calculation: Break buffer = ATR over a configurable lookback multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Volume filter = pass when disabled, or when volume exceeds a configurable multiple of its shorter-lookback average.
Calculation: Wick reversal setup = within an active range, arm a short reversal when high exceeds top, close is at or below top, and upper wick divided by bar range meets a configurable minimum; arm a long reversal when low is below bottom, close is at or above bottom, and lower wick divided by bar range meets that minimum. Confirm an armed short when a later close is below the preceding close, or an armed long when a later close is above the preceding close; an opposite-side probe replaces the armed setup.
Long: Active range AND bars in range >= configurable minimum AND configurable consecutive closes > top + buffer AND volume filter passes OR confirmed long wick reversal.
Short: Active range AND bars in range >= configurable minimum AND configurable consecutive closes < bottom - buffer AND volume filter passes OR confirmed short wick reversal.
