Calculation: Signal bars = chart-timeframe bars, or bars aggregated to a configurable higher or lower timeframe; use either the last completed signal bar or the still-forming signal bar. For a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar. Calculate signal-bar OHLC, EMA, RSI and Stochastic RSI on that timeframe.
Calculation: Trend bars = bars aggregated to a configurable timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar, or the last available completed intrabar if lower. Trend slope = (short-period EMA on that bar minus the same EMA a configurable number of trend bars earlier) / the earlier EMA. The trend EMA period equals the shorter signal EMA period.
Calculation: Proximity = (chart close - nearest support) / (nearest resistance - nearest support). On the last chart bar, scan a configurable number of chart bars from newest to oldest for strictly greater pivot highs and strictly lower pivot lows than all bars within a configurable lookback on both sides. Cluster highs and lows separately: add each pivot to the first existing cluster whose positive mean is within a configurable relative tolerance, updating its mean and touch count; otherwise start a cluster. Score each cluster as min(touches × touch weight, touch-score cap) + confluence count × confluence weight + base score. Confluence count is the number of two chart-timeframe SMAs and the upper, middle and lower Bollinger Bands whose relative distance from the cluster mean is below a tolerance, plus one if the mean is a round number. The SMAs have shorter and longer periods; the Bollinger period equals the shorter SMA period. A round number is an integer at or above a configurable price threshold, or, below that threshold, a price which rounds to a configurable decimal scale as a multiple of a configurable increment. Nearest resistance is the lowest qualifying cluster mean above chart close; nearest support is the highest qualifying cluster mean below chart close. A cluster qualifies when its score meets a minimum. Proximity is unavailable if either level is absent.
Long: Shorter signal EMA > longer signal EMA AND signal close > signal open AND (signal open < shorter signal EMA OR signal open < longer signal EMA) AND signal close > shorter signal EMA AND signal close > longer signal EMA AND NOT (last completed trend close < trend EMA AND trend slope < negative slope threshold) AND abs(signal close - signal open) / signal close >= minimum body ratio AND abs(shorter signal EMA - longer signal EMA) / longer signal EMA >= minimum spread ratio AND the selected confluence mode is satisfied: neither, RSI > midpoint, Stochastic RSI < midpoint, either of those comparisons, or both of those comparisons AND (S/R filter disabled OR (last chart bar AND proximity <= long-zone maximum)) AND counter mode disabled.
Short: Shorter signal EMA < longer signal EMA AND signal close < signal open AND (signal open > shorter signal EMA OR signal open > longer signal EMA) AND signal close < shorter signal EMA AND signal close < longer signal EMA AND NOT (last completed trend close > trend EMA AND trend slope > positive slope threshold) AND abs(signal close - signal open) / signal close >= minimum body ratio AND abs(shorter signal EMA - longer signal EMA) / longer signal EMA >= minimum spread ratio AND the selected confluence mode is satisfied: neither, RSI < midpoint, Stochastic RSI > midpoint, either of those comparisons, or both of those comparisons AND (S/R filter disabled OR (last chart bar AND proximity >= short-zone minimum)) AND counter mode disabled.
Short: The preceding long reclaim conditions, including their long-side confluence and S/R filter, are satisfied AND counter mode enabled.
Long: The preceding short reclaim conditions, including their short-side confluence and S/R filter, are satisfied AND counter mode enabled.
Long: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA.
Short: Chart-timeframe shorter EMA <= longer EMA.
Long: Last completed trend close > trend EMA AND trend slope > positive slope threshold.
Short: Last completed trend close < trend EMA AND trend slope < negative slope threshold.
Long: Last completed close on a second, longer trend timeframe > its short-period EMA AND its slope > positive slope threshold.
Short: Last completed close on a second, longer trend timeframe < its short-period EMA AND its slope < negative slope threshold.
Long: Last chart bar AND proximity <= long-zone maximum.
Short: Last chart bar AND proximity >= short-zone minimum.
