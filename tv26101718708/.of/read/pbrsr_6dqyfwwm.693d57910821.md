Calculation: RSI = 14-period relative strength index
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = 3-period simple moving average of the 14-period stochastic of the 14-period RSI
Calculation: Candle body ratio = absolute(close − open) / close
Calculation: EMA spread ratio = absolute(13-period EMA − 21-period EMA) / 21-period EMA
Calculation: Signal-bar values = OHLC, 13- and 21-period EMAs, RSI, and Stochastic RSI from the selected signal timeframe; use the forming bar unless close confirmation is enabled, then use the last completed bar. If this timeframe differs from the chart timeframe, it may be higher or lower.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with its 13-period EMA, and compare that EMA with its value three bars earlier; bullish when close is above the EMA and EMA change exceeds a positive threshold, bearish when close is below the EMA and EMA change exceeds a negative threshold, otherwise neutral.
Long: (counter mode off AND 13-period EMA > 21-period EMA AND close > open AND (open < 13-period EMA OR open < 21-period EMA) AND close > 13-period EMA AND close > 21-period EMA AND higher-timeframe trend is not bearish AND candle body ratio exceeds its minimum AND EMA spread ratio exceeds its minimum AND selected RSI/Stochastic RSI confluence passes AND (S/R filter off OR price is in the support-side zone)) OR (counter mode on AND the corresponding raw short conditions hold)
Short: (counter mode off AND 13-period EMA < 21-period EMA AND close < open AND (open > 13-period EMA OR open > 21-period EMA) AND close < 13-period EMA AND close < 21-period EMA AND higher-timeframe trend is not bullish AND candle body ratio exceeds its minimum AND EMA spread ratio exceeds its minimum AND selected RSI/Stochastic RSI confluence passes AND (S/R filter off OR price is in the resistance-side zone)) OR (counter mode on AND the corresponding raw long conditions hold)
