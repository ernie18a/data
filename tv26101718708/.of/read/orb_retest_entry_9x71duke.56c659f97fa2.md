Calculation: Opening range = chart bars from the first bar of the exchange session through the bar before the first subsequent boundary of the configured range timeframe; that timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and its current boundary is used rather than waiting for a completed bar. Upper level = highest high of those chart bars × (1 + buffer); lower level = lowest low × (1 - buffer); half-range = (upper level - lower level) / 2. Levels remain fixed until the next range is completed.
Calculation: Upper sequence = after range completion, high > upper level, followed on the same or a later bar by low <= upper level. Lower sequence = after range completion, low < lower level, followed on the same or a later bar by high >= lower level. Neither sequence updates on the first bar after range completion. Both sequences reset at the first bar of each session; each also resets after its own entry and can then form again.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = session entry count < maximum entries AND the same-side signal flag is false. Reset the count and both flags at the first bar of each session; increment the count and set the corresponding flag on entry. Clear that flag on its stop loss, but not on its take profit. If both entries occur on the same bar, process the long first.
Long: Upper sequence is complete AND high > upper level AND bar opening time in exchange time <= entry cutoff time AND entry eligibility is true. Entry price = upper level.
Short: Lower sequence is complete AND low < lower level AND bar opening time in exchange time <= entry cutoff time AND entry eligibility is true. Entry price = lower level.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - half-range at entry OR high >= entry price + half-range at entry × risk-reward ratio. Check the stop first if both are hit; exits can occur on the entry bar.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + half-range at entry OR low <= entry price - half-range at entry × risk-reward ratio. Check the stop first if both are hit; exits can occur on the entry bar.
