Calculation: Market trend score = weighted sum of the dominant cluster's trend bias and trend slope. Trend bias is (short EMA of close - long EMA of close) / close; trend slope is (long EMA of close - its value a configured number of bars earlier) / close. Cluster these two features and volume / SMA(volume) over the market-state lookback after independently standardizing each feature by its population standard deviation, using 1 when the deviation is zero. Initialize the first center at the observation nearest the standardized origin and each subsequent center at the observation farthest from its nearest existing center. Assign observations to their nearest center and update centers to their assigned means until the configured iteration limit or squared-center-shift tolerance is reached; replace an empty center with the observation at its evenly spaced index in the newest-first sample. Reassign observations to final centers and select the most frequent cluster over the recent dominance window, breaking ties by lowest center index. Convert its center back to the original feature scales. The trend threshold is a configured multiple of SMA(ATR / close) over the market-state lookback.
Calculation: Nearest zone = on each configured refresh bar, collect confirmed pivot highs and lows from the zone lookback, using the pivot high when both are confirmed on the same bar, and add highs and lows sampled at a configured bar interval. If there are fewer observations than clusters, add closes from the lookback. Initialize one-dimensional K-means centers at evenly spaced indices of the sorted observations, then assign observations to their nearest center and update centers to their means until the iteration limit or squared-center-shift tolerance is reached; replace empty centers with their initial sorted quantiles. Sort the final centers and select the center closest to the refresh bar's close. Hold this raw zone between refreshes. On every bar, retain the prior target unless the raw zone is closer to close by more than a configured ATR-based margin; initialize the target with the first available raw zone. The zone used by price action is the previous bar's stabilized target: choose raw target, exponential smoothing toward target, or a locked target that changes only when its distance from the prior stabilized value reaches a configured ATR-based threshold. Initialize with the first available target. Use a configured minimum price increment as the floor for the switching and locking distances.
Calculation: Candle ratios = body, upper wick, and lower wick divided by high - low, where body = abs(close - open), upper wick = high - max(open, close), and lower wick = min(open, close) - low; ratios are undefined when high = low.
Calculation: Zone proximity threshold = the greater of a configured minimum distance and a configured multiple of the previous bar's ATR / close, substituting the configured minimum when that ATR ratio is unavailable.
Long: Market trend score > trend threshold.
Short: Market trend score < -trend threshold.
Long: On a refresh bar, close > open AND body ratio > configured breakout threshold AND open < nearest zone AND close > nearest zone.
Short: On a refresh bar, close < open AND body ratio > configured breakout threshold AND open > nearest zone AND close < nearest zone.
Long: On a refresh bar, close > open AND lower wick ratio > configured rejection threshold AND lower wick ratio > upper wick ratio AND lower wick ratio > body ratio multiplied by a configured factor AND (abs(low - nearest zone) / close <= zone proximity threshold OR (low <= nearest zone AND close > nearest zone)).
Short: On a refresh bar, close < open AND upper wick ratio > configured rejection threshold AND upper wick ratio > lower wick ratio AND upper wick ratio > body ratio multiplied by a configured factor AND (abs(high - nearest zone) / close <= zone proximity threshold OR (high >= nearest zone AND close < nearest zone)).
Long: On a refresh bar, close > open AND body ratio > configured momentum threshold AND close > previous high.
Short: On a refresh bar, close < open AND body ratio > configured momentum threshold AND close < previous low.
