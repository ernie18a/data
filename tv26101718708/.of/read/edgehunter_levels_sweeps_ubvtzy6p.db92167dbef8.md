Calculation: Previous daily high and low = high and low of the last completed daily bar, from a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Previous hourly high and low = high and low of the last completed hourly bar, from a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Previous four-hour high and low = high and low of the last completed four-hour bar, from a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Asia and London session highs and lows = highest high and lowest low of the completed 1-minute bars in the respective session, from a lower timeframe than the chart.
Long: A tracked low level has been swept (a 1-minute low < the level) AND its last completed higher-timeframe close is > the level AND its prior close status was not inside.
Short: A tracked high level has been swept (a 1-minute high > the level) AND its last completed higher-timeframe close is < the level AND its prior close status was not inside.
