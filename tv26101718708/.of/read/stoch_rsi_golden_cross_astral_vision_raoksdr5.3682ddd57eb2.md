Calculation: K = simple moving average of the stochastic of RSI(close), using a longer lookback for the stochastic range than for the RSI; calculate on completed bars of a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: D = simple moving average of K, with a shorter lookback than K's smoothing
Long: K > D AND previous chart bar's K < previous chart bar's D AND K < oversold threshold
Short: K < D AND previous chart bar's K > previous chart bar's D AND K > overbought threshold
