Calculation: ATR trail and direction = Initialize direction bullish and trail at close minus ATR multiplied by a configurable factor. On each subsequent bar, if the previous direction is bullish, set the trail to the larger of the previous trail and close minus the ATR distance when close is above the previous trail; otherwise retain the previous trail. If close is below this trail, switch direction to bearish and reset the trail to close plus the ATR distance. If the previous direction is bearish, set the trail to the smaller of the previous trail and close plus the ATR distance when close is below the previous trail; otherwise retain the previous trail. If close is above this trail, switch direction to bullish and reset the trail to close minus the ATR distance.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = Absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Regime = Initially ranging. Switch to trending after a configurable number of consecutive bars with ADX above its threshold AND efficiency ratio above its threshold. Switch to ranging after the same number of consecutive bars on which either comparison fails. Otherwise retain the previous regime. ADX uses the same period for directional movement and smoothing.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = From the selected session, calendar-period, or custom-date anchor through the current bar, calculate the volume-weighted mean of the selected price source and the square root of the nonnegative volume-weighted variance of that source. For calendar anchors start at the first available bar if it precedes the first reset; for a custom anchor start at the first bar at or after its timestamp. Both are unavailable before the anchor or when accumulated volume is zero.
Calculation: Mean-reversion target and stop = At a mean-reversion entry, fix the target and stop at entry close plus or minus independently configured ATR multiples, respectively above and below entry for a long and below and above entry for a short. If the optional VWAP target is enabled and VWAP is available, use current VWAP instead of the fixed target. Trend entries have no mean-reversion target or stop.
Long: ATR trail direction = bullish.
Short: ATR trail direction = bearish.
Long: RSI > long momentum threshold.
Short: RSI <= long momentum threshold AND RSI < short momentum threshold.
Long: EMA of close > its value at the configured slope lookback.
Short: EMA of close <= its value at the configured slope lookback.
Long: Position side is flat or short AND datetime is at or after the optional start date AND ATR trail direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (regime filter is disabled OR regime = trending) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI > long momentum threshold) AND (VWAP side filter is disabled OR close > anchored VWAP) AND (VWAP extension guard is disabled OR VWAP deviation is unavailable or zero OR absolute distance from close to VWAP divided by VWAP deviation <= extension threshold).
Short: Position side is flat or long AND short entries are enabled AND datetime is at or after the optional start date AND ATR trail direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (regime filter is disabled OR regime = trending) AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI < short momentum threshold) AND (VWAP side filter is disabled OR close < anchored VWAP) AND (VWAP extension guard is disabled OR VWAP deviation is unavailable or zero OR absolute distance from close to VWAP divided by VWAP deviation <= extension threshold).
Long: Position side is flat or short AND datetime is at or after the optional start date AND regime filter is enabled AND regime = ranging AND mean-reversion mode is enabled AND (RSI < oversold threshold if VWAP band mode is off; close <= VWAP minus its deviation multiplied by the mean-reversion band factor if band mode replaces RSI; both comparisons if band mode confirms RSI).
Short: Position side is flat or long AND short entries are enabled AND datetime is at or after the optional start date AND regime filter is enabled AND regime = ranging AND mean-reversion mode is enabled AND (RSI > overbought threshold if VWAP band mode is off; close >= VWAP plus its deviation multiplied by the mean-reversion band factor if band mode replaces RSI; both comparisons if band mode confirms RSI).
Exit: long, (entry was a trend entry AND ATR trail direction changes from bullish to bearish) OR (EMA exit is enabled AND bars held >= grace period AND EMA of close < its value at the configured slope lookback) OR (time exit is enabled AND bars held >= maximum holding period) OR (regime filter is enabled AND regime = ranging AND mean-reversion mode is enabled AND (high >= mean-reversion target OR low <= mean-reversion stop OR RSI > RSI midpoint)) OR short entry condition is met.
Exit: short, (entry was a trend entry AND ATR trail direction changes from bearish to bullish) OR (EMA exit is enabled AND bars held >= grace period AND EMA of close > its value at the configured slope lookback) OR (time exit is enabled AND bars held >= maximum holding period) OR (regime filter is enabled AND regime = ranging AND mean-reversion mode is enabled AND (low <= mean-reversion target OR high >= mean-reversion stop OR RSI < RSI midpoint)) OR long entry condition is met.
