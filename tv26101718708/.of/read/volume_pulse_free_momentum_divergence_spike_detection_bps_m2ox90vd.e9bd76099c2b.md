Calculation: Volume momentum = let signed volume be the cumulative sum of sign(close minus previous close) times volume; subtract its simple moving average and divide by the standard deviation of that difference over a volatility lookback, using zero when that standard deviation is zero; multiply by the standard deviation of high minus low over the same volatility lookback. Add the result to high if it is positive, otherwise add it to low. Smooth with an EMA, apply the selected fast moving average, and subtract a longer-lookback EMA of close. The selected fast average may be EMA, DEMA, TEMA, HMA, WMA, ZLEMA, zero-lag DEMA, zero-lag TEMA, or double ZLEMA. For each zero-lag average, apply its underlying average to x + (x minus x at the rounded half-length lag), replacing an unavailable lagged value with zero; double ZLEMA is twice ZLEMA(x) minus ZLEMA(ZLEMA(x)).
Calculation: Chart regression = the fitted value at the newest observation of a rolling linear regression of chart-timeframe volume momentum, plus its slope; replace unavailable momentum observations in the regression window with zero.
Calculation: Trend band = initialize at zero; on each bar calculate the cumulative sum of absolute differences between chart regression and the preceding trend band, divided by the number of elapsed bars before the current bar and multiplied by a sensitivity parameter, using zero on the first bar. Set the band to chart regression when it is above the preceding band plus this deviation or below the preceding band minus this deviation; otherwise retain the preceding band. Trend direction is the sign of the latest nonzero band change, initially zero.
Calculation: Rolling momentum high and low = respectively the highest and lowest chart regression observed since the latest bar on which chart regression equaled its highest or lowest value over a pivot lookback; initialize each to chart regression.
Calculation: Timeframe momentum = the standard rolling linear regression endpoint of volume momentum, with a regression lookback of at least two bars. Calculate it separately on each of five selected timeframes; for a timeframe higher than the chart use the last completed aggregated bar, and for a timeframe lower than the chart use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. The four timeframes used for flow are a subset of these five, ordered from longer to shorter.
Long: Trend direction equals positive.
Short: Trend direction equals negative.
Long: Trend direction changes from a lower value to positive.
Short: Trend direction changes from a higher value to negative.
Long: Rolling momentum low equals its value two bars ago AND its value two bars ago differs from its value three bars ago.
Short: Rolling momentum high equals its value two bars ago AND its value two bars ago differs from its value three bars ago.
Long: Rolling momentum low equals its value two bars ago AND its value two bars ago differs from its value three bars ago AND chart regression is below the preceding rolling momentum low AND trend direction equals negative.
Short: Rolling momentum high equals its value two bars ago AND its value two bars ago differs from its value three bars ago AND chart regression is above the preceding rolling momentum high AND trend direction equals positive.
Long: Volume exceeds a multiple of its simple moving average AND trend direction equals positive AND the bearish divergence condition is false.
Short: Volume exceeds a multiple of its simple moving average AND trend direction equals negative AND the bullish divergence condition is false.
Long: A majority of the four flow timeframes have timeframe momentum above zero AND neither divergence condition is true AND volume does not exceed the volume-spike threshold when trend direction is nonzero.
Short: A majority of the four flow timeframes have timeframe momentum at or below zero AND neither divergence condition is true AND volume does not exceed the volume-spike threshold when trend direction is nonzero.
Long: Timeframe momentum is above zero on any of the five selected timeframes.
Short: Timeframe momentum is at or below zero on any of the five selected timeframes.
