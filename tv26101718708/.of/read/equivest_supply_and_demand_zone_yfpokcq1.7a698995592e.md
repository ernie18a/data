Calculation: swing pivot high/low = the latest confirmed swing high/low, identified when price turns from a rising leg to a falling leg or vice versa; the swing lookback is longer than the internal-structure lookback.
Calculation: internal pivot high/low = the latest confirmed pivot high/low using the shorter internal-structure lookback.
Calculation: bullish order block = the high/low range of the bar with the lowest parsed low between a pivot low and its bullish structure break; bearish order block = the range of the bar with the highest parsed high between a pivot high and its bearish structure break. On bars whose range is at least twice ATR, parsed high is the bar low and parsed low is the bar high; otherwise they are the bar high and low.
Calculation: fair value gap = on the selected timeframe (chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe), use the last completed bar and the still-forming bar; bar change percent = (last completed close − last completed open) / (last completed open × 100); threshold = twice the cumulative mean of absolute bar change percent.
Long: close crosses above an unbroken latest swing pivot high OR close crosses above an unbroken latest internal pivot high OR price breaks above a bearish order block high OR the still-forming selected-timeframe low is above the high two selected-timeframe bars back AND the last completed selected-timeframe close is above that high AND bar change percent exceeds the threshold AND a new selected-timeframe bar begins.
Short: close crosses below an unbroken latest swing pivot low OR close crosses below an unbroken latest internal pivot low OR price breaks below a bullish order block low OR the still-forming selected-timeframe high is below the low two selected-timeframe bars back AND the last completed selected-timeframe close is below that low AND negative bar change percent exceeds the threshold AND a new selected-timeframe bar begins.
