Calculation: Pivot levels = For each independent lookback, maintain a leg initially bearish. On each bar, if the high that many bars ago is greater than every high since, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low that many bars ago is less than every low since, set the leg bullish. A change to bearish confirms a pivot high at the earlier high; a change to bullish confirms a pivot low at the earlier low. Retain the latest and previous confirmed highs and lows. Use a shorter lookback for internal structure than for swing structure; use an independent lookback for equal highs and lows.
Calculation: Structure bias = Independently for internal and swing structure, initialize bias to neutral; set it bullish on an eligible upward structure break and bearish on an eligible downward structure break. A pivot can be broken only once until replaced. An upward break occurs when close crosses above the latest pivot high; a downward break occurs when close crosses below the latest pivot low. For internal breaks, the relevant internal pivot must differ from the corresponding swing pivot. If the optional internal confluence filter is enabled, an upward break additionally requires high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low), and a downward break requires high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low).
Calculation: Order blocks = For each eligible structure break, select the earliest bar with the minimum parsed low for an upward break or maximum parsed high for a downward break, from the broken pivot bar inclusive to the break bar exclusive. A parsed bar swaps its high and low when its range is at least twice the selected volatility measure; otherwise it retains them. The volatility measure is either ATR or cumulative true range divided by the zero-based chart bar index. Store its parsed high and low as a bullish block after an upward break or bearish block after a downward break. Keep separate internal and swing block lists, discarding the oldest when the list reaches its capacity. Remove a bullish block when its selected mitigation source is below its parsed low and a bearish block when its selected mitigation source is above its parsed high. The mitigation source is either close for both sides or low for bullish blocks and high for bearish blocks.
Calculation: Fair value gap inputs = Use the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, use that still-forming bar's high and low and the last completed and second-last completed higher-timeframe bars. For the chart timeframe, use the current bar and the preceding two bars. The prior-bar change is (prior close - prior open) / (prior open * 100). The optional threshold is twice the cumulative sum of the absolute prior-bar change on new timeframe bars divided by the zero-based chart bar index; otherwise it is zero. The source's historical higher-timeframe request uses lookahead and exposes the current higher-timeframe bar's final high and low, so reproducing those historical markings requires future data.
Long: Close crosses above an unbroken swing pivot high.
Short: Close crosses below an unbroken swing pivot low.
Long: Close crosses above an unbroken internal pivot high AND the internal pivot high differs from the swing pivot high AND, if the internal confluence filter is enabled, high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low).
Short: Close crosses below an unbroken internal pivot low AND the internal pivot low differs from the swing pivot low AND, if the internal confluence filter is enabled, high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low).
Long: Internal structure bias is bullish.
Short: Internal structure bias is bearish OR neutral.
Long: A swing pivot low is confirmed AND its low is below OR at or above the previous swing pivot low.
Short: A swing pivot high is confirmed AND its high is above OR at or below the previous swing pivot high.
Long: A low is confirmed using the equal-level lookback AND the absolute difference from the previous such pivot low is less than the threshold multiplier times ATR.
Short: A high is confirmed using the equal-level lookback AND the absolute difference from the previous such pivot high is less than the threshold multiplier times ATR.
Long: The selected bearish-block mitigation source is greater than an active bearish block's parsed high.
Short: The selected bullish-block mitigation source is less than an active bullish block's parsed low.
Long: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its low is above the second-last completed bar's high AND the last completed bar's close is above that high AND prior-bar change is greater than the gap threshold.
Short: A new gap-timeframe bar begins AND its high is below the second-last completed bar's low AND the last completed bar's close is below that low AND negative prior-bar change is greater than the gap threshold.
