Calculation: ATR = average true range; relative volume = volume / its longer-lookback average volume; close location = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); normalized price movement = absolute close change over 10 bars / ATR.
Calculation: Compression score = weighted mean of (100 − historical percentile rank of ATR/close, Bollinger Band width, rolling high-low range/close, realized volatility, and smoothed candle-body/range), weighted 24%, 24%, 20%, 18%, and 8%; add 6% when Bollinger Bands are inside Keltner Channels; clamp to 0–100. Use the compression lookback for bands, ranges, realized volatility, and smoothing; use the longer adaptive lookback for percentile ranks.
Calculation: Accumulation score = weighted mean of historical percentile ranks of smoothed (close location × relative volume), OBV regression slope normalized by average volume, positive close change/ATR × relative volume, capped bullish absorption, and up-volume share; weights 24%, 20%, 20%, 20%, and 16%. Bullish absorption on down closes = relative volume × ((1 + close location)/2) / (absolute close change/ATR + 0.25), capped at 5. Distribution score uses the complementary flow and OBV ranks, negative close change/ATR × relative volume, capped bearish absorption on up closes = relative volume × ((1 − close location)/2) / (close change/ATR + 0.25), and down-volume share, with the same weights. Use the flow lookback for smoothing, OBV regression, and directional-volume averages; the longer adaptive lookback for ranks; the volume baseline for relative volume and directional-volume averages.
Calculation: Relative strength score = weighted mean of historical percentile ranks of the asset/benchmark ratio’s shorter-lookback rate of change (30%), asset/sector ratio’s shorter-lookback rate of change (20%), mean asset-minus-benchmark return on benchmark-down bars (20%), mean asset-minus-benchmark return on benchmark-up bars (15%), and smoothed beta-adjusted asset return (15%). The shorter lookback is half the relative-strength lookback. Relative weakness score = 100 − relative strength score. Use the relative-strength lookback for conditional mean returns, volatility, correlation, and beta; the longer adaptive lookback for percentile ranks.
Calculation: Bullish structure = weighted mean (25% each) of resistance proximity pressure, bullish moving-average alignment, higher-low score, bullish trend efficiency, and seller exhaustion weighted 15% instead of 25% (full weights: 25%, 25%, 20%, 15%, 15%). Bearish structure is the inverse: support proximity pressure, bearish moving-average alignment, lower-high score, bearish trend efficiency, and buyer exhaustion with those same weights. Proximity pressure = clamp(100 − distance from close to prior-window resistance/support in ATRs × 20, 0, 100). Bullish alignment adds 20 for close > EMA20, 30 for EMA20 > EMA50, 30 for EMA50 > EMA200, and 20 for close > EMA200; bearish alignment uses the corresponding < comparisons. Higher-low/lower-high scores are historical percentile ranks of the regression slope of lows / negative regression slope of highs, normalized by ATR. Trend efficiency = absolute net close change over the structure lookback / summed absolute close changes over that lookback × 100 when movement is in the corresponding direction, otherwise 0. Near resistance/support means the bar high/low is within 0.35 ATR of the prior-window resistance/support. Seller/buyer exhaustion = clamp(50 + (40-bar conditional mean upper/lower wick share − 10-bar conditional mean upper/lower wick share) × 200, 0, 100); use 50 when the baseline is zero. Use the resistance/support lookback for levels, structure lookback for regressions and efficiency, 10 and 40 bars for rejection averages, and the longer adaptive lookback for percentile ranks.
Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe trend score = 0.40 × primary higher-timeframe trend + 0.60 × macro higher-timeframe trend. Each trend is the sum of four +0.25 tests: prior completed higher-timeframe close > EMA20, EMA20 > EMA50, EMA50 > EMA200, and close > EMA200, minus the corresponding four bearish tests. Use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe. Bullish MTF score = (combined trend + 1) × 50; bearish MTF score = 100 − bullish MTF score.
Calculation: Bull pressure = 18% compression + 24% accumulation + 22% relative strength + 28% bullish structure + 8% bullish MTF; bear pressure = 18% compression + 24% distribution + 22% relative weakness + 28% bearish structure + 8% bearish MTF. Clamp scores to 0–100. Bullish dominant means bull pressure > bear pressure. Bullish/bearish expansion = 75% corresponding pressure + 15% historical percentile rank of directional close change/ATR × relative volume × favorable close location + 10% corresponding resistance/support proximity pressure.
Calculation: Prior-window resistance/support = highest high / lowest low over the resistance/support lookback, excluding the current bar. Bull/bear breakout level = that resistance/support on the breakout bar.
Calculation: Bull exhaustion = a bullish breakout within the last 12 bars, RSI(14) > 70, and bullish pressure falling for 3 bars. Bear exhaustion = a bearish breakout within the last 12 bars, RSI(14) < 30, and bearish pressure falling for 3 bars. Bull failure = a bullish breakout within the failure window followed by close below its breakout level; bear failure = a bearish breakout within the failure window followed by close above its breakout level.
Long: confirmed bar AND close > prior-window resistance AND previous close <= previous bar's prior-window resistance AND bull pressure > bear pressure

Short: bearish expansion >= extreme-pressure threshold AND bear pressure >= bull pressure

Short: distribution score >= 65 AND bear pressure >= bull pressure
