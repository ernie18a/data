Calculation: RSI signal = the selected SMA, EMA, RMA, or WMA of RSI.
Calculation: Bull structure score = sum of four points: one if its EMA component is disabled OR close >= price EMA AND price EMA >= its previous value; one if its VWAP component is disabled OR close >= session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3; one if its swing component is disabled OR low > the lowest low over the preceding lookback OR low > previous low; and one if its reversal component is disabled OR close > open AND close >= (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Bear structure score = sum of four points: one if its EMA component is disabled OR close <= price EMA AND price EMA <= its previous value; one if its VWAP component is disabled OR close <= session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3; one if its swing component is disabled OR high < the highest high over the preceding lookback OR high < previous high; and one if its reversal component is disabled OR close < open AND close <= (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Bull divergence filters = (trend EMA filter disabled OR close >= trend EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND (structure filter disabled OR bull structure score >= minimum structure score) AND (trend lock disabled OR RSI has not remained >= the bullish trend-lock threshold for the required consecutive bars) AND (gap protection disabled OR no open < previous close - current ATR multiplied by the gap threshold occurred within the protection window, including the current bar) AND (no previously emitted regular or hidden divergence OR bars since the last emitted divergence > divergence cooldown). Evaluate these filters on the pivot confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bear divergence filters = (trend EMA filter disabled OR close <= trend EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND (structure filter disabled OR bear structure score >= minimum structure score) AND (trend lock disabled OR RSI has not remained <= the bearish trend-lock threshold for the required consecutive bars) AND (gap protection disabled OR no open > previous close + current ATR multiplied by the gap threshold occurred within the protection window, including the current bar) AND (no previously emitted regular or hidden divergence OR bars since the last emitted divergence > divergence cooldown). Evaluate these filters on the pivot confirmation bar.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot = an RSI high or low with the configured numbers of bars on its left and right; evaluate it only when the right-hand bars have completed. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind, whether or not that earlier pivot produced a divergence. Price at a low pivot is that bar's low; price at a high pivot is that bar's high.
Calculation: Bull regular grade passes = weak-grade hiding disabled OR (ADX >= normal-grade threshold AND bull structure score >= normal-grade threshold AND (direction penalty disabled OR minus DI <= plus DI OR ADX <= previous ADX)).
Calculation: Bear regular grade passes = weak-grade hiding disabled OR (ADX >= normal-grade threshold AND bear structure score >= normal-grade threshold AND (direction penalty disabled OR plus DI <= minus DI OR ADX <= previous ADX)).
