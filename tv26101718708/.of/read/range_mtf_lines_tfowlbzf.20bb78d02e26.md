Calculation: RangeDir = direction state of a close-break range sequence, computed on bars of a timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, using only its last completed bar (not the forming bar). The first bar sets range high = its high, range low = its low, RangeDir = 0. On each later bar: if close > range high, then RangeDir = +1, and range high/low are replaced by this bar's high/low; else if close < range low, then RangeDir = -1, and range high/low are replaced by this bar's high/low; otherwise (close inside the range, wicks ignored) nothing changes and RangeDir persists. RangeDir is evaluated independently per timeframe, and several timeframes may be read in parallel, each one giving its own decision.
Long: RangeDir == +1 on that timeframe
Short: RangeDir == -1 on that timeframe
