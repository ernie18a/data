Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = initially neutral; using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the two preceding higher-timeframe bars, set bullish if the middle low is below both neighboring lows; otherwise set bearish if the middle high is above both neighboring highs; otherwise retain the previous bias. Bullish takes precedence if both patterns occur.
Calculation: Trade levels = at the entry signal, retain the signal close and the preceding chart bar's low for a long or high for a short. The initial stop is that low minus a price buffer for a long, or that high plus a price buffer for a short. Risk is the distance from the signal close to the initial stop. The first and second targets are the signal close plus the respective risk multiples for a long, or minus them for a short; the second target uses the larger multiple.
Long: Previous low < low two bars ago AND current low > previous low AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar hour >= session start hour AND chart bar hour < session end hour) AND (bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias = bullish) AND long entries signaled on the current day < per-direction daily limit AND position = flat.
Short: Previous high > high two bars ago AND current high < previous high AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar hour >= session start hour AND chart bar hour < session end hour) AND (bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias = bearish) AND short entries signaled on the current day < per-direction daily limit AND position = flat.
Exit: Long, before the first target is marked hit, price <= initial stop OR price >= first target; exit the configured first fraction of the position. Mark the first target hit when a chart bar high >= first target while a long position is open.
Exit: Long, after the first target is marked hit and before the second target is marked hit, price <= signal close OR price >= second target; exit the configured second fraction of the remaining position. Mark the second target hit when a chart bar high >= second target while a long position is open and the first target is marked hit; both targets can be marked hit on the same bar.
Exit: Long, after both targets are marked hit, price <= signal close OR price <= the activated trailing stop; the trailing stop activates when price reaches the high of the chart bar on which its order was placed and trails the highest price reached after activation by a configured price distance. Exit the remaining position.
Exit: Short, before the first target is marked hit, price >= initial stop OR price <= first target; exit the configured first fraction of the position. Mark the first target hit when a chart bar low <= first target while a short position is open.
Exit: Short, after the first target is marked hit and before the second target is marked hit, price >= signal close OR price <= second target; exit the configured second fraction of the remaining position. Mark the second target hit when a chart bar low <= second target while a short position is open and the first target is marked hit; both targets can be marked hit on the same bar.
Exit: Short, after both targets are marked hit, price >= signal close OR price >= the activated trailing stop; the trailing stop activates when price reaches the low of the chart bar on which its order was placed and trails the lowest price reached after activation by a configured price distance. Exit the remaining position.
