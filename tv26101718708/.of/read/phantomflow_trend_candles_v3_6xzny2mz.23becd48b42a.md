Calculation: Confirmed swing highs and lows = pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars before and after each pivot; a pivot becomes available on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bull trend = ((shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA AND both EMAs > their previous-bar values AND the latest confirmed swing high > the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed swing low > the preceding confirmed swing low) OR (close > latest confirmed swing high + ATR × structure-break multiplier AND two confirmed swing highs exist)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe shorter-period close EMA > higher-timeframe longer-period close EMA). Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bear trend = ((shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA AND both EMAs < their previous-bar values AND the latest confirmed swing high < the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed swing low < the preceding confirmed swing low) OR (close < latest confirmed swing low - ATR × structure-break multiplier AND two confirmed swing lows exist)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe shorter-period close EMA < higher-timeframe longer-period close EMA). Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Trend state and entry arms = initialize state to neutral and both arms to false. On each bar, before evaluating entries: if bull trend is true and state is not bullish, set state bullish, arm long and disarm short; otherwise, if bear trend is true and state is not bearish, set state bearish, arm short and disarm long; otherwise, if neither trend is true, set state neutral without changing the arms. After evaluating entries, disarm each side whose entry occurred.
Long: Previous bar's bull trend is true AND previous low <= previous maximum of shorter- and longer-period close EMAs + previous ATR × pullback multiplier AND previous close >= previous longer-period close EMA AND close > shorter-period close EMA AND close > previous high AND close > open AND (close - open) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND (first-entry-only filter disabled OR long arm is true).
Short: Previous bar's bear trend is true AND previous high >= previous minimum of shorter- and longer-period close EMAs - previous ATR × pullback multiplier AND previous close <= previous longer-period close EMA AND close < shorter-period close EMA AND close < previous low AND open > close AND (open - close) / (high - low) >= minimum body fraction AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND (first-entry-only filter disabled OR short arm is true).
