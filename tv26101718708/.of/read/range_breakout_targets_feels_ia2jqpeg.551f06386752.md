Calculation: Quiet bar = bar index exceeds the longer lookback AND (highest high over the shorter lookback − lowest low over the shorter lookback) / close is at or below the nearest-rank percentile of that ratio over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Pending range = on the first quiet bar, start at the later of the beginning of the shorter lookback and the bar after the last range ended; initialize its upper and lower edges to the highest high and lowest low from that start through the current bar. While consecutive bars are quiet, extend the edges with each bar's high and low. On the first non-quiet bar, retain the range only if the number of bars from its start through the preceding bar meets the minimum range length. If it overlaps the most recently created pending range, replace that pending range's edges with the combined extremes and reset its waiting age; otherwise add a pending range. Process this before checking breakouts. Remove a pending range when its waiting age reaches the maximum, unless it breaks on that bar. On a breakout, remove the pending range, record the breakout bar as the last range end, and discard any range currently forming. When a pending range expires, update the last range end to the later of its current value and the last bar through which that range was extended.
Calculation: Breakout target = the broken range edge plus, for an upward breakout, or minus, for a downward breakout, a positive multiplier times the range height. Track each target from its breakout bar until reached, failed, or expired. Check only subsequent bars; on each bar process target reach before failure, and failure before expiration. A target expires when its age reaches the target lifetime. Failure monitoring is optional.
Long: Close > upper edge of a pending range.
Short: Close < lower edge of a pending range.
Exit: long, high >= the associated active upward breakout target OR failure monitoring is enabled AND close < the associated breakout range's lower edge.
Exit: short, low <= the associated active downward breakout target OR failure monitoring is enabled AND close > the associated breakout range's upper edge.
