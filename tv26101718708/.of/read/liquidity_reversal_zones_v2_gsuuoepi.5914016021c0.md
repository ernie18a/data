Calculation: Volume delta = sum of volume-weighted pressure over the lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; use either 2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1 or the sign of close − open for each lower-timeframe bar. Use completed lower-timeframe bars; if coverage is insufficient, blend the sum toward chart-bar volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1) according to coverage. Use the chart-bar estimate alone when lower-timeframe data is unavailable.
Calculation: CVD = either a rolling sum or an EMA of volume delta.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low that is the corresponding extreme over equally many preceding and subsequent chart bars; identify it on the bar that completes the subsequent window.
Calculation: Consolidation box = the highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous chart bar.
Long: A newly confirmed price-low pivot is lower than the preceding price-low pivot AND CVD at the new pivot is higher than CVD at the preceding pivot.
Short: A newly confirmed price-high pivot is higher than the preceding price-high pivot AND CVD at the new pivot is lower than CVD at the preceding pivot.
Long: Consolidation-box height <= an ATR multiple AND efficiency ratio over the box lookback < a threshold AND low < the box low AND close > the box low.
Short: Consolidation-box height <= an ATR multiple AND efficiency ratio over the box lookback < a threshold AND high > the box high AND close < the box high.
Long: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, low < the preceding higher-timeframe bar's low AND close > that preceding low AND lower-wick length / bar range >= a threshold.
Short: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, high > the preceding higher-timeframe bar's high AND close < that preceding high AND upper-wick length / bar range >= a threshold.
