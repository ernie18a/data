Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = price makes a lower low at a confirmed pivot low while cumulative volume delta makes a higher low; bearish CVD divergence = price makes a higher high at a confirmed pivot high while cumulative volume delta makes a lower high. Compare the two latest pivots using the same pivot width; recognize each divergence on the confirming pivot bar.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = latest confirmed pivot highs/lows, rolling highs/lows excluding the current bar, previous completed day/week/month highs/lows, and previous completed day's volume-profile point of control.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = price pierces a liquidity level below it by at least a minimum ATR-scaled depth within a reclaim window, then closes back above the level; bearish liquidity sweep = price pierces a level above it by the minimum depth within the window, then closes back below. Fire on the first reclaim close; do not repeat for a consumed level until price pierces beyond it again by the minimum depth.
Calculation: Reversal direction = normalized weighted combination of oversold/overbought extremity, reversal flow, CVD divergence, sweep direction, candle rejection, higher-timeframe extremity, optional funding and spot-perpetual basis, and optional matching RSI-and-OBV divergence; apply countertrend penalties for strong chart-timeframe trends, opposing higher-timeframe trend, and opposing hidden divergence. Positive means long; negative means short.
Calculation: Composite reversal score = smooth-saturate(reversal direction × event gate × average of the enabled volume, open-interest, volatility-regime, VWAP/session-context, and chop multipliers × score scale). The event gate combines a base floor with aligned sweep, absorption, rejection, overextension, climax, session, Wyckoff, and squeeze-stress contributions.
Calculation: Medium-score condition = absolute composite reversal score meets the medium threshold: either its rolling percentile rank meets the medium percentile and it meets the absolute floor, or it meets the fixed medium threshold.
Calculation: Directional confluence = count of enabled, available sources agreeing with reversal direction: extremity, either flow or CVD divergence, sweep, rejection, open-interest quadrant, higher-timeframe extremity, funding, and basis. Require at least the configured minimum, capped by the number of enabled and available sources.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe extremity = bounded composite of RSI, MFI, and price position relative to its moving average and standard deviation; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar when closed-bars mode is enabled, otherwise the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe sweep/rejection = on a higher-timeframe bar, low pierces the previous higher-timeframe low and closes above it with a lower-wick fraction of range meeting the rejection threshold (long); high pierces the previous higher-timeframe high and closes below it with an upper-wick fraction meeting the threshold (short). Evaluate and create the projected zone when the next higher-timeframe bar begins, using the just-completed bar.
Calculation: Zone retest = after a zone's first touch, a later bar re-enters it after the retest cooldown, has the zone-side rejection wick at least the configured fraction of its range, and closes back beyond the zone's far boundary (long: above bottom; short: below top).
Long: (bullish liquidity sweep AND reversal direction > 0 AND medium-score condition AND bullish reversal-candle condition if that filter is enabled AND directional confluence meets its minimum AND chop filter does not block) OR bullish CVD divergence OR higher-timeframe sweep/rejection long OR (long-zone retest); apply the configured cooldown; in strict confirmation mode require the prior bar to meet the setup conditions and current close > prior close; trigger only on a confirmed chart bar.
Short: (bearish liquidity sweep AND reversal direction < 0 AND medium-score condition AND bearish reversal-candle condition if that filter is enabled AND directional confluence meets its minimum AND chop filter does not block) OR bearish CVD divergence OR higher-timeframe sweep/rejection short OR (short-zone retest); apply the configured cooldown; in strict confirmation mode require the prior bar to meet the setup conditions and current close < prior close; trigger only on a confirmed chart bar.
