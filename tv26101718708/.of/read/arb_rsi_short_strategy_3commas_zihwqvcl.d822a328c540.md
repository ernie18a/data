Calculation: Averaging level k = initial short entry price × (1 + cumulative adverse spacing through rung k)
Short: no open position AND RSI crosses downward through its threshold (previous RSI ≥ threshold AND current RSI < threshold) OR short position AND an averaging rung remains AND close ≥ the next averaging level
Exit: short, close ≤ average entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction)
