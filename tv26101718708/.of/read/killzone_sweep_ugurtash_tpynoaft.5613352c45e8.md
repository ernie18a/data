Calculation: Liquidity pools = enabled previous-day high and low from the last completed higher-timeframe day and enabled completed-session highs and lows; add each pair when its day or session ends, retain the newest configured number of levels, and retire each level after a reclaim or after a breach exceeds the reclaim window without a reclaim.
Calculation: Sweep = a live low pool is breached by low < pool, or a live high pool by high > pool; track the most extreme price from breach onward. On each bar, first test close back across the pool; if reclaimed, emit the sweep even if the reclaim window has just expired; otherwise retire the pool when elapsed time > the reclaim window. If multiple pools reclaim on one bar, emit only the newest pool's sweep, but retire all reclaimed pools. Require positive ATR and an available reference open.
Calculation: Reference open = selected active session's first bar open, midnight's first available bar open, or current day's first bar open; the active-session choice falls back to midnight open and then daily open. The current day is a forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Sweep depths = penetration = abs(sweep extreme − pool) / ATR; extension = max(0, direction × (reference open − sweep extreme)) / ATR, with direction positive for a low sweep and negative for a high sweep. ATR is chart-timeframe ATR or ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe day or week, falling back to chart ATR when unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Depth buckets = intervals separated by ordered configured edges, or edges calibrated from configured quantiles of the first configured number of sweeps; assign equality to the higher interval. Automatic calibration completes on the sweep that fills the sample; that sweep is eligible. Enforce positive, strictly increasing automatic edges.
Calculation: Signal gate = selected direction, weekday and extension and penetration buckets are allowed AND, if enabled, the bar is inside an enabled session AND the same-side sweep count since the most recent enabled session start does not exceed its limit AND no eligible setup is armed or open AND both same-side depth buckets meet their minimum historical sample and t-statistic. The adaptive statistics use all registered events resolved before the current bar, including unsignalled events; unresolved events contribute zero R and count toward the sample. For each bucket, t = mean net R / sqrt((mean squared net R − mean net R squared) / sample count), requiring positive variance. Net R subtracts configured round-trip percentage of entry price divided by original stop distance; unresolved R is zero. Optional bias, range-position and session-progress filters also pass: bias is neutral or agrees with direction, using the last completed higher-timeframe close versus its EMA or open, or chart close versus midnight open; range position is (close − range low) / (range high − range low), using the last completed higher-timeframe day, forming higher-timeframe day, or running active session, and must be at or below the long threshold or at or above the short threshold; session elapsed fraction must be at or below its threshold. Missing bias, range, or session progress passes its filter.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > that earlier high AND previous body change (previous close − previous open) / (previous open × 100) > threshold AND gap height >= configured multiple of chart ATR; bearish when high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < that earlier low AND the negative of previous body change > threshold AND gap height >= that multiple. The threshold is zero or a configured multiple of the cumulative mean absolute body change. A bullish gap spans the earlier high to current low; a bearish gap spans current high to the earlier low. A gap is mitigated when a later or current low < its lower edge for bullish, or high > its upper edge for bearish; retain the newest configured number of gaps.
Calculation: Confirmed entry = a gated sweep enters at reclaim close, OR on a later bar at close beyond the reclaim bar's high for a long or low for a short, OR after that displacement at a configured depth into the newest unmitigated same-direction gap formed no earlier than the sweep, when a later low reaches the bullish gap entry or high reaches the bearish gap entry. The gap entry is measured from the near edge toward the far edge. A displacement without a qualifying gap optionally enters at displacement close. Before displacement or gap fill, cancel if price exceeds the sweep extreme or the corresponding deadline passes; on the same bar, confirmation or fill takes precedence over cancellation. At entry require the entry to be beyond the stop in the trade direction and the stop distance to meet the optional ATR minimum.
Calculation: Initial stop = sweep extreme minus direction × configured ATR multiple, using ATR at entry. Initial risk = absolute entry-to-stop distance. First target = entry plus direction × configured risk multiple, or the nearest unbreached opposite-side pool beyond entry whose distance is at least the configured risk multiple; fall back to the fixed target if no pool qualifies. The optional second target is the next qualifying pool or a configured risk multiple, adjusted beyond the first target if necessary.
Calculation: Managed stop = initial stop, optionally moved toward entry after the configured favorable risk multiple, or after the first target when a partial exit is enabled; optionally trail by a configured percentage of the best price through the previous bar after the configured favorable risk multiple. Test the standing stop and target before updating the stop or best price. After a partial exit, replace the first target with the second target.
Long: A low-pool sweep is reclaimed.
Short: A high-pool sweep is reclaimed.
Long: Bullish fair value gap conditions hold.
Short: Bearish fair value gap conditions hold.
Long: A low-pool sweep passes the signal gate AND its selected confirmed-entry condition holds.
Short: A high-pool sweep passes the signal gate AND its selected confirmed-entry condition holds.
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry low <= managed stop OR high >= active target OR elapsed time > resolution horizon; if stop and target are both touched, apply the configured stop-first or target-first rule; if partial exits are enabled, the first target exits the configured fraction and the second target exits the remainder.
Exit: Short, on a bar after entry high >= managed stop OR low <= active target OR elapsed time > resolution horizon; if stop and target are both touched, apply the configured stop-first or target-first rule; if partial exits are enabled, the first target exits the configured fraction and the second target exits the remainder.
