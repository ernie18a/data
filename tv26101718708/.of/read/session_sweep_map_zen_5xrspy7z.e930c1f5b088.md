Calculation: Session = three consecutive, non-overlapping time-of-day windows (Asia, then London, then New York, by UTC hour), with a gap after New York until Asia starts. A bar belongs to a session if its hour is inside that window, otherwise to no session. On chart timeframes above 1H, use the completed lower-timeframe (1H) bars inside each chart bar, evaluated in time order; otherwise use chart bars. Only completed bars are used.
Calculation: Pool = the high and low of a completed session, tracked separately. It becomes active on the first bar after that session ends and replaces any older pool of the same session type. A high pool is consumed by the first bar after activation whose high > pool high. A low pool is consumed by the first bar after activation whose low < pool low. The consuming bar is either a bar in a different session type than the pool's, or a bar in no session. A bar in the same session type as the pool never consumes it. A pool is consumed only once. A bar in no session that consumes a pool produces no signal.
Short: the bar is in session X, and it consumes an active high pool of a different session Y, AND close <= that pool high (wick above, close back at or below).
Long: the bar is in session X, and it consumes an active low pool of a different session Y, AND close >= that pool low (wick below, close back at or above).
