Calculation: Gap candidate = three consecutive bars numbered oldest to newest; a bullish candidate has bar 3 low > bar 1 high, with bottom = bar 1 high and top = bar 3 low; a bearish candidate has bar 3 high < bar 1 low, with bottom = bar 3 high and top = bar 1 low.
Calculation: Gap size filter = optionally require top - bottom >= a multiple of ATR on the candidate's timeframe; alternatively require (top - bottom) / bar 3 close > the mean of that ratio for all earlier candidates on the same timeframe, using zero when there are no earlier candidates. Candidates include those that fail the size filter but satisfy the selected displacement requirement.
Calculation: Active gap = a candidate that passes the selected filters, tracked from its creation bar with its original direction and an uninverted status. Before detecting new gaps on each chart bar, update existing gaps: if the applicable close is strictly beyond the far edge, reverse its direction and mark it inverted if inversion tracking is enabled and it has not previously been inverted; otherwise retire it. If that close condition does not occur, retire it when the selected source reaches the selected mitigation level. For a bullish gap, near edge = top, far edge = bottom, and reach means <=; for a bearish gap, near edge = bottom, far edge = top, and reach means >=. The mitigation level is optionally the near edge, midpoint, or far edge; the source is optionally the chart bar's low for bullish gaps and high for bearish gaps, or the applicable close. For chart-timeframe gaps the applicable close is the chart close on every bar; for higher-timeframe gaps it is the last completed higher-timeframe close and is available only on the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period. Wick mitigation uses chart bars for both gap timeframes.
Long: A bullish chart-timeframe gap candidate passes the gap size filter AND optionally bar 2 close > bar 1 high.
Short: A bearish chart-timeframe gap candidate passes the gap size filter AND optionally bar 2 close < bar 1 low.
Long: On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period, the last completed higher-timeframe bar is bar 3 of a bullish gap candidate that passes the gap size filter AND optionally bar 2 close > bar 1 high.
Short: On the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period, the last completed higher-timeframe bar is bar 3 of a bearish gap candidate that passes the gap size filter AND optionally bar 2 close < bar 1 low.
Long: Inversion tracking is enabled AND an active uninverted bearish gap's applicable close > its far edge.
Short: Inversion tracking is enabled AND an active uninverted bullish gap's applicable close < its far edge.
