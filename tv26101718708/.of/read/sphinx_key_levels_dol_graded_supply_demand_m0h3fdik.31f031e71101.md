Calculation: Liquidity levels = enabled prior completed higher-timeframe period highs and lows and prior completed session highs and lows; session boundaries are configurable in New York time. Track each level independently, resetting its setup when its period rolls or its session ends. Suppress an overnight level while it differs from an enabled corresponding session level by no more than a configurable price tolerance; disabling a level clears its setup and active zone.
Calculation: Zone setup = for each enabled, unconsumed liquidity level, start a pending sweep when high > a high-type level or low < a low-type level, and record the most extreme high or low until the setup ends. If the pending sweep exceeds the sweep-to-reclaim window, clear it; if close remains beyond the level in the sweep direction on that bar, mark the level consumed until it resets. Reclaim occurs when close is strictly back across the level within the window, the setup is not already awaiting displacement, and no zone for that level is active. At reclaim, find the most recent bar with the highest high for a high-type level or lowest low for a low-type level from the sweep start through the reclaim bar. From that bar backward toward the sweep start, within a configurable search limit, select the first up-close bar for a high-type level or down-close bar for a low-type level; if none exists, select the extreme bar. The zone is that bar's high-low range, optionally extended to the recorded sweep extreme. If displacement is required, retain the setup until the displacement window expires and accept a bearish gap when high < low two chart bars earlier or a bullish gap when low > high two chart bars earlier, including on the reclaim bar. On zone formation clear the pending sweep and displacement state. An active zone is removed when close crosses strictly beyond its far side, optionally on the first subsequent entry after price has been outside it, and optionally when its source level resets. An entry into a zone requires high >= zone low AND low <= zone high AND the previous evaluated bar was outside; initialize the zone as inside on formation.
Long: A low-type liquidity level is reclaimed AND displacement is not required OR a low-type reclaimed setup is awaiting displacement AND low > high two chart bars earlier within the displacement window.
Short: A high-type liquidity level is reclaimed AND displacement is not required OR a high-type reclaimed setup is awaiting displacement AND high < low two chart bars earlier within the displacement window.
