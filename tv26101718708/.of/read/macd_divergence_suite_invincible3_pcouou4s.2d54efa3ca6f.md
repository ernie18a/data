Calculation: Normalized MACD line = MACD divided by the rolling highest absolute MACD over its normalization lookback, multiplied by 100
Calculation: Normalized MACD histogram oscillator = (MACD minus its signal line) divided by the rolling highest absolute MACD histogram over its normalization lookback, multiplied by 100
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a local low/high with the same number of bars on each side; it is confirmed after those bars complete
Long: Normalized MACD line crosses above its signal line AND (normalized MACD filter is disabled OR normalized MACD line is at or below the bullish threshold)
Short: Normalized MACD line crosses below its signal line AND (normalized MACD filter is disabled OR normalized MACD line is at or above the bearish threshold)
Long: Normalized MACD histogram oscillator crosses above zero
Short: Normalized MACD histogram oscillator crosses below zero
Long: At a newly confirmed pivot low, its price is below the preceding pivot low AND its normalized MACD histogram oscillator is above the oscillator at the preceding pivot low AND (divergence zone filter is disabled OR either pivot oscillator is at or below the oversold threshold)
Short: At a newly confirmed pivot high, its price is above the preceding pivot high AND its normalized MACD histogram oscillator is below the oscillator at the preceding pivot high AND (divergence zone filter is disabled OR either pivot oscillator is at or above the overbought threshold)
