Calculation: MACD = fast moving average of close minus slow moving average of close; each may use EMA or SMA, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA or SMA of MACD
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: Signal MA = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of close; alternatively, DEMA = 2 × EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close)), or TEMA = 3 × (EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close))) + EMA(EMA(EMA(close)))
Calculation: Zone high MA = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of high; alternatively, DEMA or TEMA calculated from high
Calculation: A confirmed MACD pivot low/high is a pivot with the selected left and right lookbacks; it is confirmed after the right-lookback bars complete
Calculation: Regular bullish MACD divergence = current confirmed MACD pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed MACD pivot low, its price low is lower than the previous pivot low, the MACD pivot is below zero, the prior pivot is within the selected minimum and maximum pivot-distance range, and the highest MACD value over the selected lookback window is below zero
Calculation: Regular bearish MACD divergence = current confirmed MACD pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed MACD pivot high, its price high is higher than the previous pivot high, the MACD pivot is above zero, the prior pivot is within the selected minimum and maximum pivot-distance range, and the lowest MACD value over the selected lookback window is above zero
Long: datetime ≥ start time AND datetime < end time AND (MACD crosses above its signal line OR (MACD crosses above its signal line AND MACD < overbought level AND MACD signal line < overbought level) OR (MACD crosses above its signal line AND at least one of the current or previous 3 closes is above its corresponding Signal MA, DEMA, or TEMA) OR (MACD crosses above its signal line AND at least one of the current or previous 3 closes is above its corresponding Zone high MA, DEMA, or TEMA) OR (MACD crosses above its signal line AND RSI > entry level AND RSI < no-entry level) OR regular bullish MACD divergence)
Short: regular bearish MACD divergence
Exit: long, MACD crosses below its signal line OR (MACD crosses below its signal line AND at least one of the current or previous 3 closes is below its corresponding Signal MA, DEMA, or TEMA, or the current close is above its corresponding Signal MA) OR (MACD crosses below its signal line AND at least one of the current or previous 3 closes is below its corresponding Zone high MA, DEMA, or TEMA) OR (MACD crosses below its signal line AND RSI < entry level) OR RSI crosses below its no-entry level now or within the previous 6 bars OR datetime < start time OR datetime ≥ end time
