Calculation: ATR = average true range over a shorter lookback; ATR baseline = moving average of ATR over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on each of two higher timeframes, last completed bar close > short-term EMA > long-term EMA for bullish trend; reverse all comparisons for bearish trend. The second timeframe is higher than the first.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = confirmed bar low < lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback AND close > that low; bearish liquidity sweep = confirmed bar high > highest high of the preceding sweep lookback AND close < that high.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish structure break = confirmed close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. A pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks. A break against the prior structure direction is a change of character; otherwise it is a break of structure.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish sweep or structure = the corresponding event occurred within the event lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = the open-to-close range of the first qualifying bearish candle found within the lookback before a bullish change of character; bearish zone is the mirror using a bullish candle before a bearish change of character. A qualifying candle body exceeds a fraction of ATR. A bullish zone is touched when low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom and is invalidated when close < zone bottom; bearish conditions are mirrored. Keep the most recent zones up to a configured limit.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = a confirmed three-bar gap where the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier by at least a fraction of ATR; bearish gap is the mirror. A bullish gap is touched when low <= gap top AND close >= gap bottom and is removed when low < gap bottom; bearish conditions are mirrored. Keep the most recent gaps up to a configured limit.
Calculation: Discount zone = close is in the lowest quarter of the high-low range over a lookback; premium zone = close is in the highest quarter.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confluence score = capped weighted sum of: current sweep or recent sweep; both higher-timeframe trends aligned or only the first aligned; current structure break, recent structure break, or structure direction aligned; kill zone or London/New York session; matching order-block touch; matching fair-value-gap touch; discount for bullish or premium for bearish; ATR above its baseline. Subtract a volatility penalty when ATR exceeds a multiple of its baseline; cap the result to the score range.
Calculation: Dominant bullish momentum = net close change over a lookback divided by ATR times that lookback exceeds a threshold OR bullish candles make up at least a fraction of that lookback. Dominant bearish momentum is the mirror.
Calculation: Stop loss = for long, entry price minus a configured distance, or the latest bullish sweep low (fall back to the lowest low over a short lookback) minus an ATR buffer; for short, entry price plus a configured distance, or the latest bearish sweep high (fall back to the highest high over a short lookback) plus an ATR buffer. The configured distance may be ATR-based or fixed-price-unit based.
Calculation: Take-profit levels = entry price plus configured ATR-based or fixed-price-unit distances for long; entry price minus those distances for short. The furthest level is TP3.
Long: (sensitivity = Strict AND recent bullish sweep AND recent bullish structure break AND (bullish order-block touch OR bullish fair-value-gap touch) AND bullish score >= threshold OR sensitivity = Standard AND recent bullish sweep AND recent bullish structure break AND bullish score >= threshold OR sensitivity = Aggressive AND bullish score crosses above threshold AND (both higher-timeframe trends bullish OR first higher-timeframe trend bullish) OR sensitivity = Maximum AND bullish score >= threshold AND (both higher-timeframe trends bullish OR first higher-timeframe trend bullish)) AND confirmed bar AND (momentum filter disabled OR NOT dominant bearish momentum) AND more than the cooldown interval since the last signal; OR bullish liquidity sweep; OR bullish structure break.
Short: (sensitivity = Strict AND recent bearish sweep AND recent bearish structure break AND (bearish order-block touch OR bearish fair-value-gap touch) AND bearish score >= threshold OR sensitivity = Standard AND recent bearish sweep AND recent bearish structure break AND bearish score >= threshold OR sensitivity = Aggressive AND bearish score crosses above threshold AND (both higher-timeframe trends bearish OR first higher-timeframe trend bearish) OR sensitivity = Maximum AND bearish score >= threshold AND (both higher-timeframe trends bearish OR first higher-timeframe trend bearish)) AND confirmed bar AND (momentum filter disabled OR NOT dominant bullish momentum) AND more than the cooldown interval since the last signal; OR bearish liquidity sweep; OR bearish structure break.
Exit: Long, low <= stop loss OR high >= TP3 OR bars since entry > maximum holding interval.
Exit: Short, high >= stop loss OR low <= TP3 OR bars since entry > maximum holding interval.
