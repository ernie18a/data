Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over equal lookbacks on both sides; it becomes available only after the right-side lookback has completed.
Calculation: Live structure level = the latest confirmed pivot high or low at a given lookback, retained until a newer pivot of the same type replaces it or price breaks it; use both a shorter internal lookback and a longer swing lookback.
Long: Close or high, according to the selected break-price rule, > a live structure pivot high.
Short: Close or low, according to the selected break-price rule, < a live structure pivot low.
