Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted mean of typical price, accumulated from either the beginning of each selected higher-or-equal-timeframe period or the first chart bar at or after an anchor date; ignore bars with nonpositive volume.
Calculation: Band = anchored VWAP plus or minus a configurable positive ratio times a configurable multiplier times the volume-weighted population standard deviation of typical price since the anchor. Use five increasing ratios for breakout bands and the fixed ratio 0.236 for reaction cores. Bands require positive standard deviation.
Calculation: Reaction zone = either 0.236 reaction core with top and bottom equal to core plus and minus a half-width; half-width is the greater of a minimum price increment and either ATR times a multiplier or a configured price distance.
Calculation: Reaction state = maintain separate state for each zone. Clear it on an anchor reset or when reaction signals are unavailable, including while active-trade gating blocks signals. A close above the current top with the previous close at or below the previous top arms a short reclaim and starts an upward acceptance count at one; a close below the current bottom with the previous close at or above the previous bottom arms a long reclaim and starts a downward acceptance count at one. A new break replaces both prior setups. On subsequent bars, increase the acceptance count while closes remain strictly beyond the corresponding current boundary; otherwise clear the acceptance setup. A reclaim is valid only after its break bar and within the configured bar window. A selected reclaim clears both setups; a selected acceptance clears all state for that zone. Reclaim validity on a bar uses the setup from before any new break on that bar.
Calculation: Reaction candidate = for each zone, choose the first enabled qualifying condition in this order: reclaim, rejection, hold, acceptance. If both zones have candidates, choose the higher-priority candidate, choosing the upper zone on a tie. Accept it only when more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the last accepted reaction. Cancel all entry candidates on a bar if both long and short candidates qualify. Consume reaction state and update cooldown only for an accepted reaction.
Calculation: Moving-average cloud = independently selected fast and slow moving averages of a selected price source, with their own lengths and types. Available types are EMA, SMA, WMA, HMA, RMA, T3 and HEMA; HEMA is EMA of Heikin Ashi close, and T3 is three successive generalized double-EMA stages, each equal to (1 + factor) times EMA of its input minus factor times EMA of that EMA. Each average may use the chart timeframe, the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, or the last available completed intrabar of a lower timeframe.
Calculation: Gann HiLo = SMA of highs and SMA of lows with separately configurable lengths; initialize direction upward, set it upward when close exceeds the previous high SMA, set it downward when close is below the previous low SMA, and otherwise retain direction. The level is the current low SMA in the upward direction and the current high SMA in the downward direction. Use the chart timeframe, the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, or the last available completed intrabar of a lower timeframe.
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted mean of a selected price source during a configured clock session and timezone, reset at each session start and retained after the session until the next session begins.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on a new managed entry, round entry close minus side times distance to the minimum price increment, with side positive for long and negative for short. Distance is the greater of the minimum price increment and either ATR times a multiplier or a configured price distance. On each later bar, first compare close with the previous stop; if no exit occurs, round close minus side times current distance to the minimum price increment and retain the greater of it and the previous long stop, or the lesser of it and the previous short stop. Same-side signals do not reset the stop; an opposite-side signal replaces the position and stop when active-trade gating is disabled.
Long: Fast moving average > slow moving average, when cloud candle coloring is enabled.
Short: Fast moving average < slow moving average, when cloud candle coloring is enabled.
Long: An enabled VWAP or band level is crossed upward: close > current level AND previous close <= previous level AND no anchor reset AND optional cloud filter passes with fast average > slow average AND close > both averages AND optional Gann filter passes with upward direction AND close > Gann level AND optional session filter passes with close > all or any selected session VWAPs according to the selected rule.
Short: An enabled VWAP or band level is crossed downward: close < current level AND previous close >= previous level AND no anchor reset AND optional cloud filter passes with fast average < slow average AND close < both averages AND optional Gann filter passes with downward direction AND close < Gann level AND optional session filter passes with close < all or any selected session VWAPs according to the selected rule.
Long: The selected reaction candidate is a valid long reclaim AND close > current core AND previous close <= previous core.
Short: The selected reaction candidate is a valid short reclaim AND close < current core AND previous close >= previous core.
Long: The selected reaction candidate is a rejection AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND previous close > previous zone top AND close > core AND close > open.
Short: The selected reaction candidate is a rejection AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND previous close < previous zone bottom AND close < core AND close < open.
Long: The selected reaction candidate is a hold AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND the short hold condition is false AND either conservative mode has previous close > previous zone top AND close >= core, or balanced mode has previous close > previous zone top AND close >= zone bottom, or aggressive mode has previous close >= previous core AND close >= zone bottom.
Short: The selected reaction candidate is a hold AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND the long hold condition is false AND either conservative mode has previous close < previous zone bottom AND close <= core, or balanced mode has previous close < previous zone bottom AND close <= zone top, or aggressive mode has previous close <= previous core AND close <= zone top.
Long: The selected reaction candidate is upward acceptance AND the number of consecutive closes above the current zone top since a fresh upward boundary break >= the configured acceptance count.
Short: The selected reaction candidate is downward acceptance AND the number of consecutive closes below the current zone bottom since a fresh downward boundary break >= the configured acceptance count.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry close < previous trailing stop.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry close > previous trailing stop.
Calculation: Signal gating = breakout and reaction entries require their respective signal switches and available calculations; reaction entries additionally require no anchor reset and previous zone values. When risk management is enabled, entries require an available positive stop distance; when active-trade gating is enabled, an active trail blocks entries. A stop-exit bar cannot produce an entry. Breakout entries use confirmed chart-bar closes; reaction entries use confirmed closes when reaction confirmation or risk management is enabled. With neither enabled, provisional intrabar reactions cannot be reproduced from completed bars. All entry families participate in the same simultaneous-opposite-signal cancellation.
