Calculation: Base MA = selectable ALMA, EMA, VWMA, or Hull MA of close, or EMA of close multiplied by (1 + TSI of close); the TSI uses longer and shorter smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Slope = base MA minus its value a configurable number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Exit history = bar index of the most recently observed closed trade and slope on the preceding bar; initially both are zero. Update when the closed-trade count changes.
Calculation: Stop fraction = selectable fixed fraction or ATR multiplied by a coefficient and divided by close.
Calculation: Trailing start and distance fractions = selectable fixed fractions, optionally multiplied by current ATR divided by an SMA of ATR when both are positive and by 1 otherwise, or separately selectable ATR multiples divided by close. Automatic ATR fractions override the fixed and volatility-adjusted fractions.
Calculation: Long dynamic stop = entry price times (1 - stop fraction) on the entry bar; thereafter the greater of its previous value and the highest high since entry times (1 - current stop fraction). Short dynamic stop = entry price times (1 + stop fraction) on the entry bar; thereafter the lesser of its previous value and the lowest low since entry times (1 + current stop fraction).
Calculation: Trailing stop = after price reaches entry price times (1 + trailing start fraction) for a long or (1 - trailing start fraction) for a short, trail the highest price for a long or lowest price for a short by entry price times the trailing distance fraction, rounded to the nearest price increment with a minimum of one increment.
Long: Slope > 0.
Short: Slope <= 0.
Long: Position side is flat or long AND no condition exit is submitted on this bar AND bars since the last recorded exit >= cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold AND (ADX rising filter disabled OR ADX > previous ADX)) AND ((slope entry enabled AND slope crosses above zero) OR (price-cross entry enabled AND close crosses above base MA) OR ((slope entry enabled OR price-cross entry enabled) AND mid-trend re-entry enabled AND slope > 0 AND slope > slope recorded at last exit AND slope > previous slope AND (acceleration filter disabled OR slope has risen for the required consecutive bars))).
Short: Position side is flat or short AND no condition exit is submitted on this bar AND bars since the last recorded exit >= cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold AND (ADX rising filter disabled OR ADX > previous ADX)) AND ((slope entry enabled AND slope crosses below zero) OR (price-cross entry enabled AND close crosses below base MA) OR ((slope entry enabled OR price-cross entry enabled) AND mid-trend re-entry enabled AND slope < 0 AND slope < slope recorded at last exit AND slope < previous slope AND (acceleration filter disabled OR slope has fallen for the required consecutive bars))).
Exit: long, (base MA exit enabled AND close crosses below base MA) OR (VWMA exit enabled AND close crosses below exit VWMA) OR (slope deceleration exit enabled AND slope > 0 AND slope < previous slope for the required consecutive bars) OR (slope-cross exit enabled AND slope crosses below zero).
Exit: short, (base MA exit enabled AND close crosses above base MA) OR (VWMA exit enabled AND close crosses above exit VWMA) OR (slope deceleration exit enabled AND slope < 0 AND slope > previous slope for the required consecutive bars) OR (slope-cross exit enabled AND slope crosses above zero).
Exit: long, (stop enabled AND price <= long dynamic stop) OR (take profit enabled AND price >= entry price times (1 + take profit fraction)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND price <= activated long trailing stop).
Exit: short, (stop enabled AND price >= short dynamic stop) OR (take profit enabled AND price <= entry price times (1 - take profit fraction)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND price >= activated short trailing stop).
