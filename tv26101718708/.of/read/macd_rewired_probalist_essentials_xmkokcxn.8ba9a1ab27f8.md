Calculation: MACD = EMA of the selected price source over a shorter period minus EMA of the same source over a longer period; signal = EMA of MACD; histogram = MACD minus signal.
Calculation: Confirmed histogram pivot = a histogram high or low with the same parameterized number of bars on each side; confirm it only when the right-hand bars have completed. For equal values, the most recent extreme is the pivot. Pair each histogram low pivot with the low of its pivot bar and each histogram high pivot with the high of its pivot bar.
Long: MACD crosses above signal, meaning MACD > signal AND previous MACD <= previous signal.
Short: MACD crosses below signal, meaning MACD < signal AND previous MACD >= previous signal.
Long: MACD crosses above zero, meaning MACD > 0 AND previous MACD <= 0.
Short: MACD crosses below zero, meaning MACD < 0 AND previous MACD >= 0.
Long: Histogram >= 0 AND histogram > previous histogram.
Long: Histogram >= 0 AND histogram <= previous histogram.
Short: Histogram < 0 AND histogram > previous histogram.
Short: Histogram < 0 AND histogram <= previous histogram.
Long: A histogram low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the immediately preceding histogram low pivot is > 0 AND <= the divergence search lookback AND its paired price low < the preceding paired price low AND its histogram value > the preceding histogram value.
Long: A histogram low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the immediately preceding histogram low pivot is > 0 AND <= the divergence search lookback AND its paired price low > the preceding paired price low AND its histogram value < the preceding histogram value.
Short: A histogram high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the immediately preceding histogram high pivot is > 0 AND <= the divergence search lookback AND its paired price high > the preceding paired price high AND its histogram value < the preceding histogram value.
Short: A histogram high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the immediately preceding histogram high pivot is > 0 AND <= the divergence search lookback AND its paired price high < the preceding paired price high AND its histogram value > the preceding histogram value.
Long: No MACD-signal cross has occurred within the parameterized signal horizon AND MACD > 0 AND MACD > signal.
Short: No MACD-signal cross has occurred within the parameterized signal horizon AND MACD < 0 AND MACD < signal.
