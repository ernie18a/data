Calculation: body = |close-open|; range = high-low; upper = high-max(open,close); lower = min(open,close)-low; prev = the bar before a given bar
Calculation: per-bar flags: doji = body/range < a; spin = body/range < b AND body/range >= a (b > a); star = upper/body > c AND lower < body; hang = lower/body > d AND upper < body; bearEng = close < prev open AND open > prev close AND body/prev body > e; maru = body/range > f (f is high); hammer = lower/body > g AND upper < body; bullEng = close > prev open AND open < prev close AND body/prev body > h; soldiers = close>open AND prev close>prev open AND prev2 close>prev2 open AND close>prev close AND prev close>prev2 close
Calculation: score_p for each flag p = (previous value of score_p + number of bars among the previous L completed bars, excluding the current bar, where flag p is true) / L; carried across bars, initial 0
Calculation: CEI = (indecision + fear + greed)/3; indecision = mean(score_doji, score_spin); fear = mean(score_star, score_hang, score_bearEng); greed = mean(score_maru, score_hammer, score_bullEng, score_soldiers)
Calculation: entry price = close of the bar where the entry signal occurred; held = number of bars in position, with the first bar after the entry signal counted as 1; avgVol = simple moving average of volume over L bars including the current bar (L = same lookback type as the score window)
Long: CEI crosses above a lower level (previous CEI <= level AND current CEI > level) AND position is flat AND the cooldown of N bars since the last entry or exit has elapsed
Short: CEI crosses above a higher level (higher than the Long level) AND position is flat AND the cooldown of N bars since the last entry or exit has elapsed AND the Long condition is not true on the same bar
Exit: long, (close > open AND volume > multiplier * avgVol AND close > entry price) OR (held >= maxHold AND close >= entry price) OR (held >= lossHold AND close < entry price * (1 - lossPct)); maxHold > lossHold
Exit: short, (close < open AND volume > multiplier * avgVol AND close < entry price) OR (held >= maxHold AND close <= entry price) OR (held >= lossHold AND close > entry price * (1 + lossPct)); maxHold > lossHold
