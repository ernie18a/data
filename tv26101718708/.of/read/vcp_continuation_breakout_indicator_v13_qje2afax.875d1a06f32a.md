Calculation: Base statistics = for each of the VCP and continuation lookbacks, including the current bar, calculate range percentage as 100 × (highest high − lowest low) / lowest low; divide the most recent 3 × max(floor(lookback / 3), 1) bars into three equal consecutive blocks and calculate each block's lowest low and highest high, ordered oldest to newest.
Calculation: Shelf statistics = over the preceding shelf-lookback bars, excluding the current bar, resistance is the highest high and support is the lowest low; resistance variation is 100 × (resistance − lowest high) / resistance, and support variation is 100 × (highest low − support) / support. VCP and continuation use separately parameterized shelves.
Calculation: Compression = ATR divided by its SMA using the same lookback; Bollinger Band width is (upper band − lower band) / middle band, and band compression is width divided by its SMA using the band lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / SMA of volume including the current bar; use zero when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: VCP long score = count the true conditions among: close > fast EMA AND, if strict trend filtering is enabled, fast EMA > slow EMA; resistance variation <= threshold; base range percentage <= threshold; oldest block low < middle block low AND middle block low <= newest block low; ATR compression <= threshold; band compression <= threshold; relative volume >= threshold; RSI >= long threshold.
Calculation: VCP short score = count the true conditions among: close < fast EMA AND, if strict trend filtering is enabled, fast EMA < slow EMA; support variation <= threshold; base range percentage <= threshold; oldest block high > middle block high AND middle block high >= newest block high; ATR compression <= threshold; band compression <= threshold; relative volume >= threshold; RSI <= short threshold.
Calculation: Continuation long score = count the true conditions among: close > fast EMA; continuation resistance variation <= threshold; continuation range percentage <= threshold; oldest block low <= middle block low AND middle block low <= newest block low; relative volume >= threshold; RSI >= long threshold; band compression <= threshold OR ATR compression <= threshold. The final OR contributes at most one point.
Calculation: Continuation short score = count the true conditions among: close < fast EMA; continuation support variation <= threshold; continuation range percentage <= threshold; oldest block high >= middle block high AND middle block high >= newest block high; relative volume >= threshold; RSI <= short threshold; band compression <= threshold OR ATR compression <= threshold. The final OR contributes at most one point.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on a long entry set it to the lower of low and close − ATR × stop multiplier; on a short entry set it to the higher of high and close + ATR × stop multiplier. On a same-side add, retain the higher of the existing and new long stops or the lower of the existing and new short stops. While in a position, update the long stop to the higher of itself and each newly confirmed pivot low, or the short stop to the lower of itself and each newly confirmed pivot high. Confirm pivots after the configured number of right-hand bars using the configured left-hand bars. Apply entry and pivot updates before evaluating exits on the same bar; clear the stop on exit.
Long: Close > VCP resistance × (1 + breakout buffer percentage / 100) AND close > open AND VCP long score >= its threshold AND 100 × (close − fast EMA) / fast EMA <= extension threshold AND, if the market trend filter is enabled, close > market slow EMA AND market fast EMA > market slow EMA AND the configured cooldown since the last entry signal has elapsed AND position is flat OR same-side adds are enabled and position is long; trade direction must allow longs.
Long: Continuation scanner enabled AND close > continuation resistance × (1 + breakout buffer percentage / 100) AND close > open AND continuation long score >= its threshold AND 100 × (close − fast EMA) / fast EMA <= extension threshold AND, if the market trend filter is enabled, close > market slow EMA AND market fast EMA > market slow EMA AND the configured cooldown since the last entry signal has elapsed AND position is flat OR same-side adds are enabled and position is long; trade direction must allow longs.
Short: Close < VCP support × (1 − breakout buffer percentage / 100) AND close < open AND VCP short score >= its threshold AND 100 × (fast EMA − close) / fast EMA <= extension threshold AND, if the market trend filter is enabled, close < market slow EMA AND market fast EMA < market slow EMA AND the configured cooldown since the last entry signal has elapsed AND position is flat OR same-side adds are enabled and position is short; trade direction must allow shorts.
Short: Continuation scanner enabled AND close < continuation support × (1 − breakout buffer percentage / 100) AND close < open AND continuation short score >= its threshold AND 100 × (fast EMA − close) / fast EMA <= extension threshold AND, if the market trend filter is enabled, close < market slow EMA AND market fast EMA < market slow EMA AND the configured cooldown since the last entry signal has elapsed AND position is flat OR same-side adds are enabled and position is short; trade direction must allow shorts.
Long: Close > market slow EMA AND market fast EMA > market slow EMA.
Short: Close < market slow EMA AND market fast EMA < market slow EMA.
Long: RSI >= bullish dashboard threshold.
Short: RSI <= bearish dashboard threshold.
Exit: long, close < trailing stop when close-only exits are enabled OR low < trailing stop when close-only exits are disabled.
Exit: short, close > trailing stop when close-only exits are enabled OR high > trailing stop when close-only exits are disabled.
