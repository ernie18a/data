Calculation: Previous-session value area high and low = Build a profile from bars in the configured session window over the configured trailing number of sessions. Split the profile’s lowest low to highest high into the configured number of equal bins. Allocate each bar’s max(volume, 1) to each bin in proportion to the overlap of the bar’s high-low range with that bin; for a zero-range bar, allocate its full weight to the bin containing its price. Starting at the highest-volume bin, expand the value area one adjacent bin at a time, choosing the side with greater volume (choose the upper side on a tie), until it contains the configured percentage of total volume. The high and low are the upper and lower edges of the included bins. At each new session, use the levels from the last completed in-session bar and hold them fixed for that session.
Long: Current bar is confirmed and in session AND no long breakout has fired this session AND previous close <= previous-session value area high AND close > previous-session value area high
Short: Current bar is confirmed and in session AND no short breakout has fired this session AND previous close >= previous-session value area low AND close < previous-session value area low
