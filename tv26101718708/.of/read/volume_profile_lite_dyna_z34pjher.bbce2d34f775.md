Calculation: Profile value-area bounds = On each completed chart bar inside the configured session, maintain bars from the configured number of most recent sessions. If their highest high minus lowest low exceeds the minimum price increment, divide that range into equal price bins. Give each bar weight max(volume, 1), treating missing volume as zero; distribute its weight among bins in proportion to the overlap with its low-to-high range. For a zero-range bar, assign all its weight to its containing bin, clamped to the first or last bin. Start the value area at the highest-weight bin, choosing the lowest bin on a tie. Add the adjacent bin with greater weight until the included weight reaches the configured fraction of total weight; choose the upper bin on a tie and use the available side at an edge. The bounds are the bottom of the lowest included bin and the top of the highest included bin. If the range is too small, retain the last calculated bounds. At each new session, before adding its first bar or removing old sessions, lock the last calculated bounds as the previous-session bounds.
Long: Current completed chart bar is inside the session AND the current profile range exceeds the minimum price increment AND previous close <= previous-session upper bound AND close > previous-session upper bound AND no earlier qualifying upper-bound cross has occurred in the current session.
Short: Current completed chart bar is inside the session AND the current profile range exceeds the minimum price increment AND previous close >= previous-session lower bound AND close < previous-session lower bound AND no earlier qualifying lower-bound cross has occurred in the current session.
