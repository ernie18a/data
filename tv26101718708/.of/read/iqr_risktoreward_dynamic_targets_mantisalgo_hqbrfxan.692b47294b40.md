Calculation: Two-pole smoother(x, length) = let a = exp(-1.414 * 3.14159 / length), b = 2 * a * cos(1.414 * 3.14159 / length); output x when the previous output is unavailable, otherwise (1 - b + a²) * (x + previous x, replacing an unavailable previous x with 0) / 2 + b * previous output - a² * output two bars ago, replacing an unavailable output with 0.
Calculation: Wave = two-pole smoother of (high + low + close) / 3; its smoothing length is longer than the IQR smoothing length.
Calculation: Smoothed IQR = two-pole smoother of the difference between the rolling 75th and 25th percentiles of close, calculated by linear interpolation.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = wave plus and minus, respectively, a band multiplier times smoothed IQR.
Calculation: Upper and lower fences = wave plus and minus, respectively, a fence multiplier times smoothed IQR; the fence multiplier is greater than the band multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = maximum of the current upper fence and the highest high over a liquidity lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Short target = minimum of the current lower fence and the lowest low over a liquidity lookback ending on the previous bar.
Long: Wave > previous wave.
Short: Wave < previous wave.
Long: (At least one low in the preceding sweep lookback <= its corresponding lower band OR current low <= current lower fence) AND close > wave AND previous close <= previous wave AND volume >= a multiplier times its simple moving average AND bars since the last qualified long or short setup >= cooldown, with the last setup index initially set to a fixed negative offset AND close > the lowest low of the current bar and preceding sweep lookback AND long target > close AND (long target - close) / (close - lowest low of the current bar and preceding sweep lookback) >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Short: (At least one high in the preceding sweep lookback >= its corresponding upper band OR current high >= current upper fence) AND close < wave AND previous close >= previous wave AND volume >= a multiplier times its simple moving average AND bars since the last qualified long or short setup >= cooldown, with the last setup index initially set to a fixed negative offset AND close < the highest high of the current bar and preceding sweep lookback AND short target < close AND (close - short target) / (highest high of the current bar and preceding sweep lookback - close) >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
