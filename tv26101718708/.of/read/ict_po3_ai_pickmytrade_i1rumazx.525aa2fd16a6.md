Calculation: All decisions use completed chart bars. Treat each distinct threshold, weight, lookback, session boundary, timeout, capacity and price increment below as a parameter; use the same parameter wherever the same name recurs. Clamp(x) limits x to [0,1]; rising(x,a,b)=clamp((x-a)/(b-a)); falling(x,a,b)=clamp((b-x)/(b-a)); band(x,a,b,c,d)=0 outside (a,d), rises linearly from 0 to 1 between a and b, equals 1 between b and c, and falls linearly to 0 between c and d.
Calculation: Volatility regime = classify the percentile rank of ATR over a long lookback and ATR divided by its long moving average as dormant, low, normal or high using ordered thresholds. Structure regime = classify ADX and the ratio of a shorter to a longer moving average of true range as trending, compressing, expanding or ranging, in that order. Use live volatility regime for compression and raid eligibility; freeze both regimes when a range locks and use the frozen regimes for subsequent regime-dependent thresholds and context.
Calculation: Compression score = weighted sum of falling ramps of range width divided by ATR, close standard deviation divided by ATR, absolute change in a short close SMA over the compression lookback divided by lookback times ATR, range width divided by the range over a longer lookback, and maximum true range divided by range width. The width and relative-range ramp thresholds depend on live volatility regime. A bar is compressed when this score meets its threshold AND maximum true range divided by range width does not exceed its veto threshold.
Calculation: Accumulation range = while idle, start on a compressed bar; extend the running high and low through compressed bars and a limited consecutive allowance of uncompressed bars, resetting that allowance on each compressed bar. Abandon the formation when the allowance or maximum formation age is exceeded. On a compressed bar after the minimum formation length, lock the running high and low if their width divided by the exponentially smoothed range of completed calendar days is within its allowed band, or that daily average is unavailable, AND accumulation quality meets its floor. Exclude the first observed partial day when seeding the daily average. Freeze the range, its width, ATR, regimes and session at lock. Abandon a locked range if its age exceeds its limit, close breaks either boundary by more than the regime-dependent invalidation distance, or both boundaries are penetrated by more than the regime-dependent minimum penetration on the same bar.
Calculation: Accumulation quality = weighted sum of a falling ramp of compression-window range divided by longer-window range, a band score of locked width divided by lock ATR, a rising ramp of the smaller boundary-touch count, the average equality score of the two boundaries, and a band score of formation length. Count a boundary touch only when the bar reaches within an ATR-scaled tolerance of that boundary, its body rejects back inside by the specified tolerance, and it is sufficiently separated from the previous counted touch. Each equality score is the clamped inverse normalized difference between the two most extreme counted touches of that boundary, or zero with fewer than two touches.
Calculation: Liquidity references = highs and lows of the last completed calendar day and week, the most recently latched completed Asian session, and the latest confirmed pivot high and low with specified left and right confirmation lookbacks. Build day, week and session extremes from chart bars in the configured timezone. The Asian-session values are latched at the start of the next Asian session. Previous day and week are higher-timeframe completed periods; the session is a completed interval of chart bars. A pivot is available only after its right-hand confirmation bars have completed.
Calculation: Pool strength = weighted sum of the highest tier assigned to a same-side liquidity reference within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the locked boundary, a rising ramp of the count of same-side references within a wider ATR-scaled cluster tolerance, and a rising ramp of that boundary's touch count. The tier order is previous week, previous day, Asian session, confirmed pivot, then an unmatched boundary. Apply the specified price-increment minimums to distance tolerances.
Calculation: Raid and reclaim = after the lock bar, a one-sided penetration of the lower boundary sets direction long; a one-sided penetration of the upper boundary sets direction short. Require live volatility regime not dormant, pool strength at least its floor, and the boundary to be fresh relative to the last raided boundary by either elapsed bars or ATR-scaled distance. Record the most extreme price and its bar. From the raid bar onward, count excursion bars, closes beyond the boundary, consecutive closes beyond it, and the fraction of each bar's range beyond it. Acceptance volume is the sum of volume times that fraction divided by current volume SMA times excursion-bar count; if volume SMA is unavailable or nonpositive, use the fraction of excursion bars with any penetration. Acceptance is a weighted sum of clamped acceptance volume, consecutive closes beyond divided by its scale, and closes beyond divided by excursion bars. A reclaim closes back inside the raided boundary by at least the greater of a lock-ATR-scaled buffer and a price-increment buffer. On a reclaim, require manipulation quality to meet its floor; otherwise abandon the setup. Before a reclaim, abandon it when consecutive closes beyond or acceptance volume exceeds its limit, penetration divided by lock ATR or range width exceeds its limit, or excursion duration reaches its regime-dependent limit. Test reclaim before these invalidations.
Calculation: Manipulation quality = weighted sum of a regime-dependent band score of maximum penetration divided by lock ATR, a falling score of acceptance, a rising ramp of reclaim overshoot divided by lock ATR, a rising ramp of the rejection-wick fraction of the bar containing the excursion extreme, pool strength, and a band score of excursion duration.
Calculation: Expansion = from reclaim onward, track the largest absolute candle body and its body-to-range ratio, and the first directional three-bar imbalance whose gap exceeds a lock-ATR-scaled minimum: current low above the high two bars earlier for long, or current high below the low two bars earlier for short. Abandon the setup if close crosses beyond the raid extreme against the direction, or if the opposite range boundary is penetrated by the minimum penetration and close returns inside it; increment the opposing-raid penalty on the latter. Otherwise require a directional displacement candle whose absolute body exceeds a regime-dependent multiple of lock ATR and whose body-to-range ratio meets its minimum. Abandon if displacement takes longer than its limit. After displacement, require close above the locked upper boundary for long or below the locked lower boundary for short; abandon if that shift takes longer than its regime-dependent limit. Check invalidations before displacement and before the shift. Reclaim, displacement and shift may occur on the same bar, subject to a maximum of three state transitions per bar.
Calculation: Distribution quality = weighted sum of a rising ramp of the absolute shift close minus raided boundary divided by lock ATR, a base-plus-rising score for the first imbalance's size divided by lock ATR or zero if absent, a base-plus-rising score for shift clearance beyond the opposite locked boundary divided by lock ATR, a rising ramp of the largest-body candle's body-to-range ratio, and a falling ramp of bars since reclaim.
Calculation: Trade levels = entry is the signal-bar close. Set the raw stop beyond the raid extreme by a lock-ATR-scaled buffer; if its distance from entry is less than the greater of a lock-ATR-scaled minimum and a price-increment minimum, move it to that minimum distance. Reject the candidate if this unrounded stop distance exceeds the maximum multiple of lock ATR. Round the stop away from entry to the price increment; risk is the greater of rounded stop distance and one price increment. Target the nearest opposite-side liquidity reference beyond the opposite locked range boundary and at least the required risk multiple from entry; if none qualifies, use the fallback risk multiple. Round the target toward entry to the price increment.
Calculation: Structural score = require accumulation, manipulation and distribution qualities each to meet their respective floors; take their weighted geometric mean after scaling to [0,1], and multiply by a penalty based on the capped count of recent opposite-boundary raids. Reset that count on a calendar-day change or when its last occurrence is older than the range-age limit. Multiply the resulting chain by an affine function of context and clamp to [0,1]. Context is a weighted sum of higher-timeframe alignment, optional session fit, frozen-regime fit, target-room score and directional position within the range between the raid extreme and the opposite locked boundary; renormalize the non-session weights when session context is disabled. Higher-timeframe alignment uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar's shorter and longer close EMAs and its rolling high and low, combined with current chart close's position in that range. Session fit weights the sessions at range lock, reclaim and signal. Target room is a band score of target distance divided by current ATR when a liquidity target exists, otherwise a fixed parameter. Directional position favors the lower part for long and the upper part for short.
Calculation: Adaptive gate = maintain separate bounded first-in-first-out libraries of resolved long and short signals. For each signal store entry, stop, target, risk and an eight-component fingerprint: normalized accumulation, manipulation and distribution qualities, current ATR percentile, normalized frozen structure regime, higher-timeframe alignment, pool strength and target-room score. From the next bar, resolve on target or stop touch, treating a bar touching both as a loss; otherwise resolve at the outcome horizon as a win only if maximum favorable excursion in risk units meets its threshold and exceeds maximum adverse excursion. Keep bounded pending and resolved queues. For a candidate with enough same-direction records, select the nearest records by the sum over fingerprint components of log(1+absolute difference); weight each by 1/(1+distance), and shrink their weighted win rate toward the same-direction library win rate using a prior weight. In optional Gate mode, block the candidate when the same-direction library has at least its gate sample minimum AND this estimate is below its gate threshold.
Long: a lower-boundary raid reclaims successfully AND manipulation quality meets its floor.
Short: an upper-boundary raid reclaims successfully AND manipulation quality meets its floor.
Long: a lower-boundary raid and reclaim are followed by valid bullish expansion and a close above the locked upper boundary AND the stop-distance limit, all three quality floors and structural-score threshold are satisfied AND the adaptive gate does not block the candidate.
Short: an upper-boundary raid and reclaim are followed by valid bearish expansion and a close below the locked lower boundary AND the stop-distance limit, all three quality floors and structural-score threshold are satisfied AND the adaptive gate does not block the candidate.
Exit: long, high >= the signal's target OR low <= the signal's stop OR bars since signal >= the outcome horizon.
Exit: short, low <= the signal's target OR high >= the signal's stop OR bars since signal >= the outcome horizon.
