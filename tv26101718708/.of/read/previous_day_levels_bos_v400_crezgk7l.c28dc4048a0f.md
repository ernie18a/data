Calculation: Previous-day high = highest high of the last completed calendar-day bar, aggregated at a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Previous-day low = lowest low of the last completed calendar-day bar, aggregated at a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding N and following N chart bars; it is confirmed after the following N bars
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a low less than the lows of the preceding N and following N chart bars; it is confirmed after the following N bars
Long: low <= previous-day low AND low > previous-day low on a prior bar since the last such break OR, after a break above the previous-day high or below the previous-day low in the current calendar day, close[1] <= latest confirmed swing high AND close > latest confirmed swing high, where that swing high is between the previous-day low and previous-day high and was confirmed after the break
Short: high >= previous-day high AND high < previous-day high on a prior bar since the last such break OR, after a break above the previous-day high or below the previous-day low in the current calendar day, close[1] >= latest confirmed swing low AND close < latest confirmed swing low, where that swing low is between the previous-day low and previous-day high and was confirmed after the break
