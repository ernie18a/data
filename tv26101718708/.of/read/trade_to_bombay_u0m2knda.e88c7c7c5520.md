Calculation: Chart Supertrend direction = +1 when chart close > prior upper trailing band, -1 when chart close < prior lower trailing band, otherwise its prior value (initially +1); lower and upper trailing bands are hl2 − multiplier × ATR(lookback) and hl2 + multiplier × ATR(lookback), respectively, each replaced by the maximum or minimum of its current basic band and prior trailing band when the prior close is beyond that prior band. Supertrend line = lower trailing band when direction is +1, otherwise upper trailing band.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Supertrend direction = the same calculation using higher-timeframe OHLC bars and the same ATR lookback and multiplier as the chart calculation; it uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: (close > Supertrend line AND prior close <= prior Supertrend line) OR (Chart Supertrend direction = +1 AND prior Chart Supertrend direction = -1 AND Higher-timeframe Supertrend direction = +1)
Short: (close < Supertrend line AND prior close >= prior Supertrend line) OR (Chart Supertrend direction = -1 AND prior Chart Supertrend direction = +1 AND Higher-timeframe Supertrend direction = -1)
