Calculation: Main stochastic = configured choice among four stochastic %D lines, with candidate lookbacks ordered shortest to longest; each %D is the moving average of stochastic %K computed from close, high, and low.
Calculation: Continuation stochastic = independently configured choice among the same four stochastic %D lines.
Calculation: Oversold-visited state = becomes true when main stochastic is at or below the configured oversold level; resets on a qualifying long signal or when main stochastic reaches the configured overbought level while this state is true.
Calculation: Overbought-visited state = becomes true when main stochastic is at or above the configured overbought level; resets on a qualifying short signal or when main stochastic reaches the configured oversold level while this state is true.
Calculation: Strong-trend-seen state = becomes true when main stochastic is at either configured extreme while ADX is at or above its configured threshold; resets when either visited state triggers its opposite-extreme reset, or on a qualifying long or short signal.
Calculation: Bull mode = turns on after a qualifying long signal and off after a qualifying short signal; bear mode = turns on after a qualifying short signal and off after a qualifying long signal.
Long: Main stochastic crosses above 50 AND oversold-visited state is true AND (ADX filter disabled OR (strong-trend-seen state is true AND current ADX < previous-bar ADX)) AND (candle filter disabled OR close > open) AND (price-structure filter disabled OR current low > the lowest low of the configured lookback ending on the previous bar) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its configured moving average) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > a configured fraction of its longer-term moving average)
Long: Bull mode is true AND continuation stochastic crosses above 50
Short: Main stochastic crosses below 50 AND overbought-visited state is true AND (ADX filter disabled OR (strong-trend-seen state is true AND current ADX < previous-bar ADX)) AND (candle filter disabled OR close < open) AND (price-structure filter disabled OR current high < the highest high of the configured lookback ending on the previous bar) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its configured moving average) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > a configured fraction of its longer-term moving average)
Short: Bear mode is true AND continuation stochastic crosses below 50
