Calculation: Higher-timeframe ATR = the selected RMA, SMA, or EMA of true range over a lookback, using higher-timeframe bars; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar’s ATR
Calculation: Upper level = last completed higher-timeframe close + upper multiplier × higher-timeframe ATR
Calculation: Lower level = last completed higher-timeframe close − lower multiplier × higher-timeframe ATR
Long: chart close ≤ Lower level
Short: chart close ≥ Upper level
