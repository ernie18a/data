Calculation: Bullish order-block candidate = on the first chart bar of a new same-or-higher-timeframe bar, the preceding completed bar closed below its open AND the last completed bar closed above the preceding bar's high AND the last completed bar's high minus low is at least a threshold multiple of the preceding bar's high minus low; its top and bottom are the preceding bar's high and low.
Calculation: Bearish order-block candidate = on the first chart bar of a new same-or-higher-timeframe bar, the preceding completed bar closed above its open AND the last completed bar closed below the preceding bar's low AND the last completed bar's high minus low is at least a threshold multiple of the preceding bar's high minus low; its top and bottom are the preceding bar's high and low.
Calculation: Breaker candidates = retain the most recent limited number of order-block candidates per same-or-higher timeframe, including candidates already broken; add new candidates before checking breaks and remove the oldest excess candidates after checking breaks. A candidate becomes broken on its first qualifying break.
Long: A bullish order-block candidate exists AND its top is greater than its bottom AND its top differs from the top of the last marked order block for that timeframe, or no order block has previously been marked for that timeframe.
Short: A bearish order-block candidate exists AND its top is greater than its bottom AND its top differs from the top of the last marked order block for that timeframe, or no order block has previously been marked for that timeframe.
Long: On the first chart bar of a new same-or-higher-timeframe bar, the last completed bar's close is above the top of a retained, unbroken bearish order-block candidate.
Short: On the first chart bar of a new same-or-higher-timeframe bar, the last completed bar's close is below the bottom of a retained, unbroken bullish order-block candidate.
