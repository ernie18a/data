Calculation: Smoothed momentum = EMA of the difference between the shorter-lookback and longer-lookback simple moving averages of bar midpoint (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Noise threshold = standard deviation of smoothed momentum over a longer lookback, scaled by a factor.
Long: Bullish momentum state begins (smoothed momentum > 0 and either smoothed momentum is rising or its absolute value is below the noise threshold) AND the prior bar was not in a bullish momentum state; OR aggressive mode is enabled AND smoothed momentum > 0 and rising AND the prior bar was not in that strong bullish state; OR early entry is enabled AND smoothed momentum < 0 and rising AND its prior value was not rising AND the applicable regular long signal is false; OR bottom reversal is enabled AND current momentum state is not bullish AND prior close < prior lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close > open AND the lowest RSI of the last three bars is below the oversold threshold AND the applicable regular and early long signals are false. If the ADX filter is enabled, ADX must exceed its threshold.
Short: Bearish momentum state begins (smoothed momentum < 0 and either smoothed momentum is falling or its absolute value is below the noise threshold) AND the prior bar was not in a bearish momentum state; OR aggressive mode is enabled AND smoothed momentum < 0 and falling AND the prior bar was not in that strong bearish state. If the ADX filter is enabled, ADX must exceed its threshold.
Exit: Long, smoothed momentum < 0 AND falling AND early entry or bottom reversal is enabled; OR an opposite short signal closes/reverses the long; OR price reaches the configured long stop (entry price less a configured percentage, or entry price less a configured ATR multiple).
Exit: Short, an opposite long signal closes/reverses the short; OR price reaches the configured short stop (entry price plus a configured percentage, or entry price plus a configured ATR multiple).
