Calculation: Williams VIX Fix (WVF) = 100 × (highest close over a lookback, including the current bar − current low) / highest close over that lookback.
Calculation: WVF spike = WVF >= its upper Bollinger Band OR WVF >= a multiplier × the highest WVF over a lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: WVF standardized deviation = (WVF − SMA of WVF) / standard deviation of WVF, or zero if that standard deviation is zero; use the Bollinger Band lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = SMA of higher-timeframe closes, evaluated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar and on the completed higher-timeframe bar before it.
Calculation: Signal target = close immediately before the latest confirmed long or short signal, plus for a long signal or minus for a short signal the ATR on that signal bar × a stop-distance multiplier × a target risk multiple. Maintain three independently parameterized targets, replacing them whenever either signal occurs.
Long: On a completed bar, (WVF spike on the current or previous bar) AND close > open AND close > previous close AND (spike-strength filter disabled OR WVF standardized deviation >= Bollinger Band multiplier + spike-strength parameter / 10) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR >= SMA of ATR using the ATR lookback × volatility multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR (volume >= SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND volume > previous volume)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR (last completed higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend AND higher-timeframe trend > its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar)) AND (chart-trend filter disabled OR (close > SMA of close AND SMA of close > its value a specified number of chart bars ago)) AND (cooldown disabled OR no previous long signal exists OR bars since the previous long signal >= cooldown parameter).
Short: On a completed bar, WVF < SMA of WVF AND previous WVF >= previous SMA of WVF, using the Bollinger Band lookback, AND a WVF spike occurred within a specified number of chart bars including the current bar AND close < open AND close < previous close AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR >= SMA of ATR using the ATR lookback × volatility multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR (volume >= SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND volume > previous volume)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR (last completed higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe trend AND higher-timeframe trend < its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar)) AND (chart-trend filter disabled OR (close < SMA of close AND SMA of close < its value a specified number of chart bars ago)) AND (cooldown disabled OR no previous short signal exists OR bars since the previous short signal >= cooldown parameter).
Long: Last completed higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend AND higher-timeframe trend > its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close <= higher-timeframe trend OR higher-timeframe trend <= its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Close > chart-timeframe trend SMA of close.
Short: Close <= chart-timeframe trend SMA of close.
Exit: long, confirmed short-signal entry condition is true.
Exit: short, confirmed long-signal entry condition is true.
Exit: long, latest confirmed signal is long AND high >= any of its three signal targets.
Exit: short, latest confirmed signal is short AND low <= any of its three signal targets.
