Calculation: cRSI high/low event = a local cRSI peak/trough, using the configured peak detector lookback; a peak requires consecutive rises then a fall, and a trough consecutive falls then a rise. The longer lookback requires one additional rise or fall.
Calculation: bullish divergence = at a cRSI low event, cRSI is higher than at the preceding cRSI low event while close is lower (regular), or cRSI is lower while close is higher (hidden).
Calculation: bearish divergence = at a cRSI high event, cRSI is lower than at the preceding cRSI high event while close is higher (regular), or cRSI is higher while close is lower (hidden).
Calculation: corrective wave sequence = wave A is a cRSI trough whose preceding bar is below the lower cRSI band, excluding troughs that complete ABC or ABCDE; wave B is a cRSI peak whose preceding cRSI is below the cRSI WMA or 50; wave C is a cRSI trough whose preceding cRSI is below the lower band or cRSI WMA. Each next wave in A-B-C-D-E-F-G alternates B/C, requires the corresponding wave event, and must follow the preceding sequence within 50 bars without cRSI rising above its WMA. Wave C is ABC, wave E is ABCDE, and wave G is ABCDEFG.
Long: cRSI crosses above its WMA OR (wave A OR wave C) AND regular bullish divergence OR wave E with the longer peak detector lookback OR wave G with the shorter peak detector lookback; suppress if a filtered long signal occurred within the preceding five bars; buy size after lower-band weighting and rounding is greater than zero.
Long: regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence.
Short: cRSI crosses below its WMA.
Short: (a strong impulse peak above the upper cRSI band OR cRSI is above the upper band now, one bar ago, or two bars ago, and is falling now) AND the upper cRSI band exceeds its configured high threshold; suppress if a filtered short signal occurred within the preceding five bars; sell size after upper-band weighting and rounding is greater than zero.
Short: regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence.
