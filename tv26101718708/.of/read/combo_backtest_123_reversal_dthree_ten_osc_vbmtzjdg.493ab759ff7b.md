Calculation: Fast line = short-lookback EMA of daily-bar HL2 minus longer-lookback EMA of daily-bar HL2; use the higher-timeframe daily bar while it is forming.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-lookback SMA of the fast line.
Long: The latest reversal trigger is bullish (close two bars ago < prior close AND current close > prior close AND fast stochastic < slow stochastic AND fast stochastic > level) AND fast line > slow line
Short: The latest reversal trigger is bearish (close two bars ago > prior close AND current close < prior close AND fast stochastic > slow stochastic AND fast stochastic < level) AND slow line > fast line
Exit: Long, NOT (the latest reversal trigger is bullish AND fast line > slow line)
Exit: Short, NOT (the latest reversal trigger is bearish AND slow line > fast line)
