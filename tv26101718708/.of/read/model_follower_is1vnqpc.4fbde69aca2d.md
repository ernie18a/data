Calculation: Active structure = Initialize its high and low from the first bar, with direction unset. A bearish pullback starts on a bar with close < open AND close < prior low; track its lowest low, updating it whenever a later low is lower. A bullish pullback starts on a bar with close > open AND close > prior high; track its highest high, updating it whenever a later high is higher. A pullback qualifies only if its latest tracked bar is later than the structure origin. On an upward break, set direction up, structure high to the breaking bar’s high, structure low to the qualifying bearish pullback’s lowest low or the prior structure low, and origin to that pullback’s bar or prior origin; reset bearish pullback tracking. While direction is up, extend structure high to each higher bar high. On a downward break, apply the symmetric rules using the qualifying bullish pullback’s highest high, set direction down, and reset bullish pullback tracking. While direction is down, extend structure low to each lower bar low.
Long: Close > active structure high AND (structure direction is unset OR a bearish pullback qualifies)
Short: Close < active structure low AND (structure direction is unset OR a bullish pullback qualifies)
