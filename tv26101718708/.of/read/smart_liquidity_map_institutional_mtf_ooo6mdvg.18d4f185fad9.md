Calculation: Active liquidity levels = Maintain separate pivot-high and pivot-low level lists from enabled chart or higher timeframes; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time. A pivot is an extreme confirmed by equal numbers of bars before and after it. Process the timeframes from shortest to longest, pivot high before pivot low. Add a pivot only when its reported value is nonmissing and differs from the preceding chart bar's reported value, or that preceding value is missing. For each new pivot, first search active opposite-side levels in insertion order, then active same-side levels, for the first price within a tolerance based on chart-timeframe ATR. If found, retain that level's price and side and do not add a new level; otherwise add the new price to its side's list. Limit each list to its most recently added levels. After adding pivots on each chart bar, evaluate all unswept levels: a pivot-high level is swept when high > level AND either close-back is disabled OR close <= level; a pivot-low level is swept when low < level AND either close-back is disabled OR close >= level. A swept level cannot fire again. When close-back and clearing broken levels are enabled, remove an unswept pivot-high level if close > level and an unswept pivot-low level if close < level.
Long: low < an unswept pivot-low level AND (close-back is disabled OR close <= level is false)
Short: high > an unswept pivot-high level AND (close-back is disabled OR close > level is false)
