Calculation: Structure swings = For each of two independently selected timeframes equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, use confirmed pivot highs and lows from the last completed timeframe bar. Process newly confirmed pivots in pivot-time order. Merge consecutive pivots of the same type by retaining only a more extreme pivot; otherwise alternate types. Track the latest high and low, their chart-bar confirmation times, and whether each has been broken. A newly accepted or replaced pivot is immediately marked broken if the chart close is already beyond it; record its direction as a retro break. Initialize each timeframe's trend as up.
Calculation: Structure break = An unbroken latest swing high or low is broken when a later chart bar's close, or optionally its high or low, respectively exceeds the high or falls below the low. Mark it broken and set the timeframe trend to the break direction. If both break on the same bar, process the low first when close >= open and the high first otherwise. If neither regular break occurs, an enabled retro break sets trend to its recorded direction. A retro break is recorded only on the chart bar that accepts or replaces the pivot.
Calculation: Equilibrium = The midpoint of the latest high and low structure swings of the first structure timeframe. A bullish zone is on the correct side when its midpoint <= equilibrium; a bearish zone is on the correct side when its midpoint >= equilibrium. If equilibrium is unavailable, either side is correct. Optionally disable this test.
Calculation: Fair value gap = A bullish gap has current low > high two chart bars earlier and that earlier bar's close > open; its bounds are that earlier high and current low. A bearish gap has current high < low two chart bars earlier and that earlier bar's close < open; its bounds are current high and that earlier low. Optionally require gap size >= a minimum absolute size, >= ATR times a multiplier, or both; an unavailable ATR passes the ATR test. Create at most one gap per origin bar. Optionally reject zones on the wrong side of equilibrium.
Calculation: Order block = In structure-break mode, after a break in an enabled structure timeframe, use the high and low of the most recent opposite-direction chart candle in a lookback before the break; optionally, if none exists, use the lowest-low candle for a bullish block or highest-high candle for a bearish block. In displacement mode, a bullish block is the high-low range of an earlier bearish candle when current low > that candle's high; a bearish block is the range of an earlier bullish candle when current high < that candle's low. Create at most one block per origin bar and direction. Optionally reject zones on the wrong side of equilibrium.
Calculation: Active zone = A created fair value gap or order block remains active until its selected mitigation threshold is reached on a later chart bar or it is removed by the configured per-type capacity. Bullish penetration is zone top minus low; bearish penetration is high minus zone bottom. Mitigation is penetration > 0 for touch, or penetration / zone height >= the selected partial-fill or full-fill threshold. A zone is touched on the first later bar with low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom, provided it was on the correct side of equilibrium at creation. Optionally, at a confirmed chart-timeframe pivot, retain only the configured number of most extreme order blocks accumulated since the previous corresponding pivot.
Calculation: Liquidity level = The latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high or low, using the sweep pivot lookbacks. After a high sweep replace the high level with that bar's high; after a low sweep replace the low level with that bar's low. A new confirmed pivot replaces the corresponding level before testing the current bar. Minimum penetration is ATR times a multiplier, or zero if ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Retracement zone = Whenever the later pivot time of the first structure timeframe's latest swing-high and swing-low pair changes and their price distance is positive, use its current trend as the zone direction. For an uptrend, subtract shallow and deep retracement fractions of the absolute swing distance from the swing high; for a downtrend, add them to the swing low. The two results are the zone bounds. Retain the zone and its direction until the next qualifying update. Record same-direction active order blocks and gaps that were on the correct side of equilibrium at creation and overlap the new zone by a positive amount; retain their overlap counts until the next update. The proposed entry is the intermediate retracement level, optionally replaced by the midpoint of the largest overlap with an order block, or with a gap if no block overlaps. The stop is the opposite swing beyond a buffer, and the first target is the anchor swing. These levels remain fixed until the next update.
Calculation: Confluence score = A weighted average, scaled to a percentage, of these factors: recency of the latest first-timeframe structure event in the current bias direction, fading linearly from one to zero over a lifespan; whether the second timeframe trend matches the bias; retained order-block and gap overlap counts each divided by a saturation count and capped at one; distance of close from equilibrium toward the correct swing extreme divided by the corresponding half-range and clamped to zero through one; similarly decayed recency of the supporting liquidity sweep; one when close is inside the retained retracement zone, otherwise a linearly decreasing partial credit within an ATR-based distance of it; the retained proposed entry's absolute target distance divided by its absolute stop distance and a reward-risk target, capped at one; and the count of active, untouched, correct-side same-direction order blocks divided by a saturation count and capped at one. Unavailable factors contribute zero. Bias is the retained retracement-zone direction, or the first timeframe trend before a zone exists. If total weight is zero, score is zero.
Long: A bullish structure break occurs on either structure timeframe.
Short: A bearish structure break occurs on either structure timeframe.
Long: A bullish fair value gap or order block is created.
Short: A bearish fair value gap or order block is created.
Long: A previously untouched, active bullish fair value gap or order block is touched.
Short: A previously untouched, active bearish fair value gap or order block is touched.
Long: Low < the latest liquidity low minus minimum penetration AND the bar is later than that pivot AND, if the close-back filter is enabled, close > the liquidity low.
Short: High > the latest liquidity high plus minimum penetration AND the bar is later than that pivot AND, if the close-back filter is enabled, close < the liquidity high.
Long: A new bullish retracement zone is created.
Short: A new bearish retracement zone is created.
Long: The retained retracement zone is bullish AND low <= its top AND high >= its bottom AND this is the first such bar since its creation.
Short: The retained retracement zone is bearish AND low <= its top AND high >= its bottom AND this is the first such bar since its creation.
Long: Bias is up AND confluence score >= its threshold AND the previous score, or zero if unavailable, < the threshold.
Short: Bias is down AND confluence score >= its threshold AND the previous score, or zero if unavailable, < the threshold.
