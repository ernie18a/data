Calculation: VFI = sum of capped signed volume over a lookback ÷ prior-bar average volume over that lookback; cap each bar’s volume at average volume × a cap factor, and assign it positive when the change in HLC3 exceeds a volatility cutoff, negative when it is below the negative cutoff, and zero otherwise. The cutoff is a factor × the standard deviation of log HLC3 changes over a volatility lookback × close.
Calculation: VFI signal = EMA of VFI over a shorter signal lookback.
Calculation: lower band = if close exceeds a threshold, SMA(close, lookback) minus a fixed offset; otherwise, SMA(close, lookback) × a multiplier.
Long: (current forming lower-timeframe bar is within the configured trade session AND close three chart bars ago > upper band AND close > SMA(close, lookback) AND VFI three chart bars ago > 0 AND VFI > 0 AND VFI > VFI five chart bars ago AND more than five chart bars have elapsed since the last bar satisfying an exit condition) OR close > open × a configured rise factor.
Exit: long, close < lower band OR close > entry price × a configured profit factor OR close < entry price − a configured loss amount OR VFI < a configured negative threshold OR VFI signal < a configured negative threshold OR close < open × a configured drop factor.
