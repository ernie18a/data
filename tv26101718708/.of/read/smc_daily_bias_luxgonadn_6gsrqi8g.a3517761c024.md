Calculation: Prior-day candle direction = prior completed higher-timeframe bar closes above or below its open.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the corresponding values on the configured number of bars before and after it; use confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Higher-high = newest confirmed pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high; lower-high = it is below. Higher-low = newest confirmed pivot low is above the preceding confirmed pivot low; lower-low = it is below.
Calculation: Combined score = sum of the three votes: prior-day candle direction, structure direction, and chart close relative to the current higher-timeframe bar's open; bullish = +1, bearish = -1, neutral = 0.
Long: selected method is prior-day candle and prior-day candle direction is bullish OR selected method is structure and the latest pivots form a higher-high and higher-low OR selected method is opening range and chart close is above the current higher-timeframe bar's open OR selected method is combined and combined score is at least +2.
Short: selected method is prior-day candle and prior-day candle direction is bearish OR selected method is structure and the latest pivots form a lower-high and lower-low OR selected method is opening range and chart close is below the current higher-timeframe bar's open OR selected method is combined and combined score is at most -2.
