Calculation: Feature vector = (price structure, liquidity, sentiment), with each component clipped to [-1, 1]; use chart-timeframe bars throughout and treat lookbacks, coefficients, thresholds, and denominator floors as parameters.
Calculation: Price structure = mean of tanh(a × (short EMA(close) − medium EMA(close)) / medium EMA(close)), tanh((close − short EMA(close)) / (b × ATR)), normalized RSI, and tanh(c × (medium EMA(close) − long EMA(close)) / long EMA(close)); short, medium, and long EMA lookbacks increase in that order; normalized RSI = (RSI − midpoint) / midpoint.
Calculation: Liquidity = mean of 2 × SMA(close position) − 1, tanh(d × (lower wick − upper wick)), tanh((close − session VWAP) / (e × ATR)), and sweep score; close position = (close − low) / bar range, upper wick = (high − max(open, close)) / bar range, lower wick = (min(open, close) − low) / bar range; floor bar range and ATR at a small positive value; sweep score is positive for low below the preceding lookback low AND close above that low, negative for high above the preceding lookback high AND close below that high, and zero otherwise, with the low sweep taking precedence; session VWAP is the volume-weighted average of typical price since the daily session began.
Calculation: Sentiment = mean of tanh(clipped volume z-score × sign(close − open) / f), tanh(g × sign(close − close at a short lookback) × (volume − short SMA(volume)) / long SMA(volume)), tanh(h × (SMA(close position × volume) / long SMA(volume) − 0.5)), and tanh(abs(clipped volume z-score) / i) × sign(close − previous close); volume z-score = (volume − long SMA(volume)) / standard deviation of volume over the same lookback, or zero if that deviation is zero; clip it symmetrically; use zero for terms divided by long SMA(volume) when it is nonpositive.
Calculation: Quantum probabilities = for each class up, down, side, sum complex amplitudes across the three features in order: amplitude = exp(−(feature − class target)² / (2 × width²)) × exp(i × phase), with class targets +1, −1, 0; phases are respectively feature × phase intensity × π/2 + feature offset, −feature × phase intensity × π/2 + feature offset, and abs(feature) × phase intensity × π + feature offset; feature offsets increase from zero in equal steps. Square each sum's magnitude, normalize across classes or use equal probabilities if their sum is zero, then mix each probability with the equal-probability distribution using the decoherence parameter.
Calculation: Historical labels = after an initial warm-up, on each confirmed bar classify the percentage return from the forecast-horizon bar ago to the current close as up above a positive move threshold, down below its negative, and side otherwise; associate that label with the feature vector from the forecast-horizon bar ago and retain the most recent training-window records.
Calculation: Neighbor probabilities = equal probabilities until the historical record count exceeds a minimum; thereafter select the specified number of records with smallest Euclidean distance to the current feature vector, breaking ties by earliest retained record, and normalize the sums of 1 / (1 + distance) by historical label.
Calculation: Neural probabilities = three-class softmax of class-specific linear functions of the current feature vector. Initialize up weights positive, down weights to their negatives, side weights and all biases to zero, with the first feature weighted more heavily than the other two. On each confirmed bar after the same warm-up and before current prediction, train on the forecast-horizon-old feature vector and its historical label: for each class, gradient = predicted probability − one-hot label; update each weight to decay × weight − learning rate × gradient × corresponding feature, and each bias to decay × bias − learning rate × gradient. Compute softmax after subtracting the largest logit and clipping differences symmetrically.
Calculation: Ensemble probabilities = weighted average of quantum, neighbor, and neural probabilities for each class, normalized across classes. Dominant class is the largest ensemble probability, resolving ties in order up, down, side. Confidence = 1 + sum(p × ln(max(p, small positive floor))) / ln(3), over ensemble class probabilities.
