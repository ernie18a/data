Calculation: Long confluence score = weighted sum of evidence for bullish trend (fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA), close at/above VWAP, ATR as a percentage of close above its minimum, ADX above its threshold with +DI > -DI, RSI above 50 and nondecreasing, volume expansion, positive pressure delta (more points above its stronger threshold), rising CVD above its EMA, bullish VWAP discount reclaim, bullish wick rejection, downside sweep, bullish compression breakout with volume expansion, and bullish regime continuation or flat-regime breakout probability.
Calculation: Short confluence score = weighted sum of the corresponding bearish evidence: bearish trend (fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA), close at/below VWAP, ATR percentage above its minimum, ADX above its threshold with -DI > +DI, RSI below 50 and nonincreasing, volume expansion, negative pressure delta (more points below its stronger negative threshold), falling CVD below its EMA, bearish VWAP premium rejection, bearish wick rejection, upside sweep, bearish compression breakout with volume expansion, and bearish regime continuation or flat-regime breakout probability. The EMA used for CVD is shorter than the CVD accumulation history; the trend EMA is shorter than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume exceeds its moving-average baseline multiplied by a factor OR its volume z-score exceeds a threshold.
Calculation: Pressure delta = rolling sum of estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume, divided by rolling sum of total estimated volume, expressed as a percentage. Estimate buy share from close position in the bar range, candle direction, and body size; estimated buy and sell volumes are volume times buy share and one minus buy share.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume.
Calculation: Bullish VWAP discount reclaim = close is below VWAP by more than an ATR-scaled band AND close > open. Bearish VWAP premium rejection = close is above VWAP by more than the band AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish wick rejection = lower wick > body (or minimum tick) times a factor AND close > open AND close is in the upper portion of the bar range. Bearish wick rejection = upper wick > body (or minimum tick) times a factor AND close < open AND close is in the lower portion of the bar range.
Calculation: Downside sweep = low below the lowest low of the preceding structure lookback AND close above that prior low AND close > open. Upside sweep = high above the highest high of the preceding structure lookback AND close below that prior high AND close < open.
Calculation: Compression = recent high-low range as a percentage of close is below ATR percentage times a factor AND the fast/slow EMA spread is below ATR times a factor. Bullish compression breakout = prior bar was compressed AND close exceeds the highest high of the preceding breakout lookback AND volume expansion. Bearish compression breakout = prior bar was compressed AND close falls below the lowest low of that lookback AND volume expansion.
Calculation: Bullish regime = bullish trend AND close at/above VWAP; bearish regime = bearish trend AND close at/below VWAP; otherwise flat. Regime continuation probabilities are the historical same-regime count divided by same-regime plus opposite-regime transitions; flat breakout probability is the larger of flat-to-bullish and flat-to-bearish counts divided by their sum.
