Calculation: Bull trend = short-term close EMA > long-term close EMA AND close > short-term close EMA; bear trend = short-term close EMA < long-term close EMA AND close < short-term close EMA.
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR / close, or zero when close is zero. Sufficient range = ATR percentage >= minimum ATR percentage.
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume > volume SMA multiplied by a threshold OR (volume − volume SMA) / volume standard deviation > a threshold; use zero for the standardized value when standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Buy share = clamp(weighted sum of (close − low) / range, candle direction, and absolute candle body / range multiplied by (candle direction − neutral direction), plus an offset, to the interval from zero to one); candle direction is bullish, bearish, or neutral according to close versus open; range is the greater of high − low and a minimum price increment.
Calculation: Pressure = rolling sum of volume × (2 × buy share − 1) divided by rolling sum of volume, or zero when the denominator is zero. Cumulative delta = cumulative sum of volume × (2 × buy share − 1); its signal is its EMA.
Calculation: Discount reclaim = (close − session VWAP of typical price) / ATR < negative deviation threshold AND close > open; premium reject = that distance > positive deviation threshold AND close < open. Use zero for the distance when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Bull rejection = lower wick > a multiple of the greater of absolute candle body and minimum price increment AND close > open AND close > low + a fraction of range. Bear rejection = upper wick > the same multiple of the greater of absolute candle body and minimum price increment AND close < open AND close < low + the complementary fraction of range. Range is the greater of high − low and the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Downside sweep = low < lowest low of preceding sweep-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND close > open. Upside sweep = high > highest high of preceding sweep-lookback bars AND close < that highest high AND close < open.
Calculation: Compression = (highest high − lowest low over the compression lookback) / close < a multiple of ATR percentage AND absolute difference between short-term and long-term close EMAs < a multiple of ATR; use zero for the first ratio when close is zero. Bull expansion = previous bar was compressed AND close > highest high of preceding breakout-lookback bars AND volume expansion. Bear expansion = previous bar was compressed AND close < lowest low of preceding breakout-lookback bars AND volume expansion.
Calculation: Regime = bullish when bull trend AND close >= session VWAP, bearish when bear trend AND close <= session VWAP, otherwise neutral. Initialize each bullish-hold, bullish-turn, bearish-hold, bearish-turn, neutral-to-bullish and neutral-to-bearish count to the same positive prior. On every completed bar increment the corresponding count for the previous and current regimes; transitions to neutral do not increment a count. Bullish continuation = bullish-hold / (bullish-hold + bullish-turn); bearish continuation = bearish-hold / (bearish-hold + bearish-turn); neutral breakout = maximum of the two neutral transition counts / their sum.
Calculation: Long score = capped weighted sum of bull trend, close >= session VWAP, sufficient range, ADX above its threshold AND positive DI > negative DI, RSI above its midpoint AND RSI >= previous RSI, volume expansion, positive pressure above a stronger threshold or otherwise above zero, cumulative delta > its signal AND signal >= previous signal, discount reclaim, bull rejection, downside sweep, bull expansion, and bullish continuation above its threshold or otherwise neutral breakout above its threshold AND bullish regime. Short score uses the corresponding opposite directional conditions, with the same weights and symmetric thresholds. The stronger and weaker pressure contributions have different weights; the continuation and neutral-breakout contributions have different weights. Cap both scores at the same maximum.
Long: Long score >= setup threshold AND long score > short score + score margin AND sufficient range AND (trend filter disabled OR bull trend) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND (no prior setup signal OR bars since the last long or short setup signal > cooldown) AND (completed-bar filter disabled OR current bar completed).
Short: Short score >= setup threshold AND short score > long score + score margin AND sufficient range AND (trend filter disabled OR bear trend) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND (no prior setup signal OR bars since the last long or short setup signal > cooldown) AND (completed-bar filter disabled OR current bar completed).
Long: Long score > short score + score margin.
Short: Short score > long score + score margin.
Long: (downside sweep OR bull expansion OR discount reclaim) AND (completed-bar filter disabled OR current bar completed).
Short: (upside sweep OR bear expansion OR premium reject) AND (completed-bar filter disabled OR current bar completed).
Long: Pressure >= zero.
Short: Pressure < zero.
Long: Pressure > positive expansion threshold AND (completed-bar filter disabled OR current bar completed).
Short: Pressure < negative expansion threshold AND (completed-bar filter disabled OR current bar completed).
Long: Cumulative delta > its signal AND signal >= previous signal.
Short: Cumulative delta < its signal AND signal <= previous signal.
Long: Close >= session VWAP.
Short: Close < session VWAP.
