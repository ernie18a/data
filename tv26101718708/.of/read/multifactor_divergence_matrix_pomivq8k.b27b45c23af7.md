Calculation: Composite = mean of the active lenses, each standardized as (value − rolling mean) / rolling standard deviation; the lenses are RSI, KST, smoothed directional momentum, fractional difference of log price, signed efficiency ratio, signed SAR distance in ATR units, signed fast-minus-slow efficiency ratio, VWAP deviation, ATR-scaled linear-regression deviation, signed range, implied-minus-realized volatility spread, cumulative signed-volume deviation from its EMA, MFI, price-minus-reference-price, and inverse volatility-index z-score.
Calculation: Family extreme = at least one active aspect in that family is at or beyond the extreme z-score threshold; group aspects into momentum, trend/efficiency, location, volatility, flow, and cross-asset families.
Calculation: Required families = the smaller of active family count and the threshold selected by chart timeframe (4 for up to 1 minute, 3 up to 5 minutes, otherwise 2).
Calculation: Pivot divergence = compare successive confirmed price pivots and composite values, with a maximum gap; composite gap must exceed a threshold scaled by its rolling standard deviation. For pivot detection, use the chart timeframe and wait for the right-side confirmation bars.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe signed volume = sum volume for lower-timeframe bars that close above their open, minus volume for bars that close below their open; if unavailable, estimate signed volume from the chart bar's close location within its range.
