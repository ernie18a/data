Calculation: Z(x) = (x − rolling SMA(x)) / rolling standard deviation(x), or 0 when the standard deviation is 0; use the same normalization lookback for all lenses.
Calculation: Low-lag strength = clamp((U − D) / (U + D), −1, 1), or 0 when the denominator is negligible; U and D are the two-pole filtered positive and negative price changes. For input x and period P, the filter is y[t] = (1 − c1)x[t] + (2c1 − c2)x[t−1] − (c1 + c3)x[t−2] + c2 y[t−1] + c3 y[t−2], initialized to x while either preceding output is unavailable; a = exp(−sqrt(2)π/P), c2 = 2a cos(sqrt(2)π/P), c3 = −a², c1 = (1 + c2 − c3)/4.
Calculation: Fractional difference = sum of w[k] × log(price[k]) over a fixed width, with w[0] = 1 and w[k] = −w[k−1] × (d − k + 1) / k; first available only after the width has elapsed.
Calculation: Signed efficiency = (price − price N bars ago) / sum of absolute one-bar price changes over N bars, or 0 if the denominator is 0. Efficiency gap = absolute short-lookback efficiency − absolute long-lookback efficiency.
Calculation: Signed volume delta = sum, over completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, of volume multiplied by sign(close − open); if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar volume × (2 × clamp((close − low)/(high − low), 0, 1) − 1), with the fraction set to 0.5 when high equals low.
Calculation: Available lenses = enabled, nonmissing values from Z(RSI); Z(Know Sure Thing); Z(low-lag strength); Z(fractional difference); Z(signed efficiency); sign(price − Parabolic SAR) × Z(clamp(SAR extension / ATR, 0, upper cap)), where extension is high − SAR above SAR, SAR − low below SAR, and 0 at SAR; sign(price − price at the shorter efficiency lookback) × Z(efficiency gap), using 1 when that sign is 0; Z(price − session VWAP); Z((price − linear regression value) / ATR); Z(high − low) × sign(price − price at the range-direction lookback), using 1 when that sign is 0; Z(cumulative signed volume delta − its EMA); and Z(Money Flow Index). Use 0 for the ATR-normalized values when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Volume-based lenses require cumulative volume greater than 0. The external-reference and volatility-index lenses are unavailable from the permitted data fields and are omitted. Know Sure Thing is the weighted sum of smoothed rates of change at four increasing lookbacks, with increasing weights and their respective smoothing lookbacks.
Calculation: Composite = arithmetic mean of available lenses, or 0 if none are available. Composite overbought and extreme gates = their respective multipliers × rolling standard deviation of the composite; use their respective fixed fallback thresholds when that standard deviation is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Aspects = the mean of available RSI and Know Sure Thing lenses as one aspect, plus each other available lens as its own aspect. Families = momentum (oscillatory, low-lag, fractional difference), trend (signed efficiency, SAR extension, efficiency gap), location (VWAP, regression deviation), volatility (directional range), and flow (signed-volume delta, Money Flow Index). Family net = mean of its available aspects. A family is split when at least one aspect is at or above the positive lean threshold AND at least one is at or below its negative. The required family count is the configured timeframe-dependent or manual minimum, capped at the number of active families and floored at 1.
Calculation: Pivot comparison = compare each confirmed price pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind, provided their distance is at most the maximum pivot gap. Price pivot highs use high and lows use low. At each pivot, the composite comparison value is either its value at the price pivot or the highest composite for a high pivot / lowest composite for a low pivot in the full symmetric price-pivot confirmation window. The minimum composite difference is a multiplier of the composite rolling standard deviation at the confirmation bar. Equal price extremes have an absolute difference no greater than an ATR multiple at the confirmation bar. Evaluate the composite gate at the confirmation bar.
Long: Composite <= negative composite overbought gate.
Short: Composite >= composite overbought gate.
Long: An active family net > 0.
Short: An active family net < 0.
Long: The number of active families with at least one aspect <= the negative extreme threshold >= required family count.
Short: The number of active families with at least one aspect >= the positive extreme threshold >= required family count.

Short: A price-high pivot comparison has current high > previous high OR equal highs AND current composite comparison value < previous value AND their difference >= minimum composite difference AND, if the extreme filter is enabled, current composite comparison value >= composite overbought gate.

Short: A price-high pivot comparison has current high < previous high AND current composite comparison value > previous value AND their difference >= minimum composite difference.
