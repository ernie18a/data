Calculation: Session opening range = for each configured Asia, London, and New York session, the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening-range window, measured from the session start in America/New_York time. Reset the range at its session start; also reset the New York range at each new day in America/New_York time.
Calculation: Session eligibility = the opening range has finished and contains at least one bar AND the bar opening time is within that session. For New York, the bar opening time must also be before its configured signal cutoff.
Calculation: High breakout = session eligibility AND close >= opening-range high + breakout distance AND previous close < opening-range high + breakout distance AND (first-only mode disabled OR no earlier high breakout in this session). If previous close is unavailable, substitute the opening-range high.
Calculation: Low breakout = session eligibility AND close <= opening-range low - breakout distance AND previous close > opening-range low - breakout distance AND (first-only mode disabled OR no earlier low breakout in this session). If previous close is unavailable, substitute the opening-range low.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3 * volume) divided by cumulative volume, resetting at each new day in America/New_York time; undefined when cumulative volume is zero.
Long: For any eligible session, high breakout AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > daily VWAP).
Long: For any eligible session, high breakout = false AND low breakout = false AND low <= opening-range low AND close >= opening-range low + rejection buffer AND close <= opening-range high AND (wick filter disabled OR close - low >= minimum wick) AND (first-only mode disabled OR no earlier low rejection in this session) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > daily VWAP).
Short: For any eligible session, low breakout AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < daily VWAP).
Short: For any eligible session, high breakout = false AND low breakout = false AND high >= opening-range high AND close <= opening-range high - rejection buffer AND close >= opening-range low AND (wick filter disabled OR high - close >= minimum wick) AND (first-only mode disabled OR no earlier high rejection in this session) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < daily VWAP).
Calculation: First-only state = track high breakouts, low breakouts, high rejections, and low rejections separately for each session; mark each occurrence before applying the VWAP filter, reset at that session's start, and also reset the New York state at each new day in America/New_York time.
