Calculation: Lower-timeframe source = a configurable price source computed on each completed lower-timeframe bar within the completed chart bar.
Calculation: Signed lower-timeframe volume = lower-timeframe volume if its source is greater than or equal to the preceding lower-timeframe source; otherwise, negative lower-timeframe volume. Missing volume is zero.
Calculation: Volume point of control = source of the first lower-timeframe bar with the highest volume within the chart bar.
Calculation: Time point of control = midpoint of the first most-populated equal-width price bin in the chart bar's high-low range, counting lower-timeframe sources. The first bin includes sources below its upper boundary; subsequent bins include sources above their lower boundary and at or below their upper boundary. A source exactly at the first boundary is excluded.
Long: The highest signed lower-timeframe volume is greater than the absolute value of the lowest signed lower-timeframe volume AND chart-bar volume is greater than zero.
Long: The highest signed lower-timeframe volume is less than or equal to the absolute value of the lowest signed lower-timeframe volume AND at least one lower-timeframe source exists.
Short: The highest signed lower-timeframe volume is less than or equal to the absolute value of the lowest signed lower-timeframe volume AND chart-bar volume is greater than zero.
Short: The highest signed lower-timeframe volume is greater than the absolute value of the lowest signed lower-timeframe volume AND at least one lower-timeframe source exists.
Long: Volume point of control is below the chart-bar midpoint AND (close is above the previous chart-bar low AND volume point of control is below the previous chart-bar low OR close is below the previous chart-bar low AND volume point of control is below close AND absolute distance from close to volume point of control is at least a specified fraction of the chart-bar range).
Long: Time point of control is below the chart-bar midpoint AND (close is above the previous chart-bar low AND time point of control is below the previous chart-bar low OR close is below the previous chart-bar low AND time point of control is below close AND absolute distance from close to time point of control is at least a specified fraction of the chart-bar range).
Short: Volume point of control is above the chart-bar midpoint AND (close is below the previous chart-bar high AND volume point of control is above the previous chart-bar high OR close is above the previous chart-bar high AND volume point of control is above close AND absolute distance from close to volume point of control is at least a specified fraction of the chart-bar range).
Short: Time point of control is above the chart-bar midpoint AND (close is below the previous chart-bar high AND time point of control is above the previous chart-bar high OR close is above the previous chart-bar high AND time point of control is above close AND absolute distance from close to time point of control is at least a specified fraction of the chart-bar range).
Long: Close is above the previous chart-bar high AND time point of control is below the chart-bar midpoint AND volume point of control is above the chart-bar midpoint AND chart-bar range is greater than a specified multiple of the previous chart-bar range AND absolute distance between the time and volume points of control is at least a specified fraction of the chart-bar range.
Short: Close is below the previous chart-bar low AND volume point of control is below the chart-bar midpoint AND time point of control is above the chart-bar midpoint AND chart-bar range is greater than a specified multiple of the previous chart-bar range AND absolute distance between the time and volume points of control is at least a specified fraction of the chart-bar range.
