Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a pivot high or low confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars, using the configured left-hand bars. Store a limited number of the most recent confirmed pivots on each side.
Calculation: Pattern = on confirmation of a new pivot, examine stored same-side pivots from newest to oldest. Stop examining when their separation exceeds the maximum span. For each pivot within the minimum span and within the ATR-scaled price tolerance, set the neckline to the lowest low between top pivots or the highest high between bottom pivots, and the extreme to the higher top or lower bottom. Stop examining if an intervening high exceeds the top extreme or an intervening low falls below the bottom extreme. Otherwise, select the first candidate whose absolute distance from extreme to neckline is at least the minimum ATR-scaled height. Use ATR on the confirmation bar. If low-quality patterns are hidden, retain the selected pattern only if its score meets the minimum; do not examine further candidates. A retained pattern replaces any still-forming pattern on the same side.
Calculation: Pattern score = round the sum of weighted peak match, RSI divergence, volume divergence, height, symmetry, and prior trend components. Peak match is max(0, 1 − absolute pivot-price difference / ATR-scaled tolerance). RSI divergence is 1 when the second pivot's RSI is lower for tops or higher for bottoms, otherwise 0. Pivot volume is the sum of volume over a window centered on the pivot; volume divergence is 1 when both pivot volumes are positive and the second is lower, 0 when both are positive otherwise, and one-half when either is not positive. Height is min(1, pattern height / an ATR-scaled height benchmark). Symmetry is the shorter pivot-to-neckline bar distance divided by the longer. Prior trend is min(1, prior move / pattern height), where prior move is the nonnegative distance from the lowest low before the first top, or highest high before the first bottom, over the prior-trend lookback to that first pivot. Each component has its own weight.
Calculation: Forming pattern validity = retain a pattern through each bar until its neckline breaks, it is replaced, or it is invalidated. Invalidation takes precedence over a break: invalidate a top when high exceeds the higher pivot plus an ATR-scaled stop buffer, or a bottom when low falls below the lower pivot minus that buffer. Also invalidate when bars since the second pivot exceed the expiry and the current close has not broken the neckline. The buffer uses ATR at pattern confirmation.
Calculation: Targets and stop = for a bottom, add separately parameterized pattern-height multiples to the neckline for the first and second targets and subtract the ATR-scaled buffer from the lower pivot for the initial stop; for a top, subtract the multiples from the neckline and add the buffer to the higher pivot. After the first target is hit, optionally replace the stop with the neckline-break close. Evaluate targets and stop starting on the bar after the break; stop takes precedence over both targets, and the second target takes precedence over the first. Stop tracking after the second target, a stop hit, or the active-pattern expiry; a first-target hit alone keeps tracking active.
Long: A valid forming double-bottom pattern exists AND close > its neckline.
Short: A valid forming double-top pattern exists AND close < its neckline.
Exit: long, low <= the active stop OR high >= the first target OR high >= the second target.
Exit: short, high >= the active stop OR low <= the first target OR low <= the second target.
