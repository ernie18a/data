Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the same structure lookback on both sides, available only after the right-side bars complete; retain the latest high and low separately. A new pivot replaces the previous pivot of its type and resets its broken and ready flags.
Calculation: Structure break = close above the latest unbroken swing high or below the latest unbroken swing low; prioritize the high break if both occur. Mark the broken pivot as broken even if no filtered signal follows. The structure direction starts neutral and becomes the direction of each break. A break against the previous structure direction is a reversal; any other break is a continuation.
Calculation: Filter agreement for direction d, where d is +1 for long and -1 for short = every enabled condition holds: d × (close − close at the momentum lookback) / ATR >= momentum threshold; higher-timeframe last completed close is above its EMA for long or below it for short; lower-timeframe last completed close is above its EMA for long or below it for short; volume / SMA(volume) >= volume threshold, using zero when the average is zero; close is above the highest high of the preceding breakout lookback for long or below the lowest low for short; d × order-flow change > 0; d × ribbon score > −0.2; and the bar is within the configured session. Use zero momentum when ATR is zero. For ready decisions, ignore the momentum and breakout conditions.
Calculation: Order-flow change = change over the flow lookback in cumulative chart-bar delta. For each chart bar, delta is the sum of volume on completed lower-timeframe bars with close >= open minus volume on completed lower-timeframe bars with close < open. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, delta is volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), or zero if high equals low.
Calculation: Ribbon score = EMA of clamp(0.6 × (adaptive baseline − adaptive baseline at the slope lookback) / ATR + 0.4 × (close − adaptive baseline) / (2 × ATR), −1, 1); use zero when ATR is zero. The adaptive baseline starts at close and updates as baseline + smoothing constant × (close − baseline). The smoothing constant is (efficiency ratio × (fast smoothing constant − slow smoothing constant) + slow smoothing constant) squared. The efficiency ratio is the absolute close change over the adaptive lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero if that sum is zero. Each smoothing constant is 2 / (its length + 1); the fast length is shorter than the slow length.
Calculation: Trade risk = fixed ATR multiple at entry, or, for a structure stop, absolute difference between entry and the latest opposite swing plus an ATR buffer, clamped between minimum and maximum ATR multiples. If the opposite swing is unavailable, use the fixed ATR multiple before clamping. Stop = entry − direction × risk. Each enabled target = entry + direction × target distance, where distance is its configured risk multiple, entry-bar ATR multiple, or point distance. Retain entry-time levels until the plan closes or a new signal replaces it.
Long: An upward structure break occurs AND its reversal/continuation type is enabled AND long filter agreement holds AND, if one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last filtered signal was not long.
Short: A downward structure break occurs AND its reversal/continuation type is enabled AND short filter agreement holds AND, if one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last filtered signal was not short.
Long: Close < latest unbroken swing high AND close >= that high − ready distance × ATR AND no long ready decision has occurred for that pivot AND long ready filter agreement holds AND, if one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last filtered signal was not long.
Short: Close > latest unbroken swing low AND close <= that low + ready distance × ATR AND no short ready decision has occurred for that pivot AND short ready filter agreement holds AND, if one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last filtered signal was not short.
Long: A confirmed swing high > the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low >= the preceding confirmed swing low.
Short: A confirmed swing high <= the preceding confirmed swing high OR a confirmed swing low < the preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: Ribbon score > 0.
Short: Ribbon score < 0.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA.
Exit: long, a bar after entry has low <= stop OR, if the stop was not touched on that bar, high >= an enabled target not previously reached; process the stop before targets.
Exit: short, a bar after entry has high >= stop OR, if the stop was not touched on that bar, low <= an enabled target not previously reached; process the stop before targets.
Exit: long OR short, a new filtered long or short signal occurs while the previous plan is active.
