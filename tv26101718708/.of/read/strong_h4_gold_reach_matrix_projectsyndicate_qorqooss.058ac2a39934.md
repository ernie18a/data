Calculation: Neural direction forecast = Apply fixed training-set standardization to the previous completed chart bar's features, then pass them through a fully connected neural network with three tanh hidden layers and a linear output layer; reverse the training-set standardization of the direction output. The features are: SMA of close-to-close returns; stochastic position of close within the rolling high-low range; RSI; SMA of (high − low) / close; SMA of (close − open) / open; shorter-return SMA minus longer-return SMA; short ATR / close; short ATR / long ATR; (close − EMA of close) / short ATR; and volume / SMA of volume − 1. Use the same short ATR for the last three ATR-based features, and the same longer-return SMA for momentum and acceleration. Network weights, biases, standardization statistics, and lookbacks are parameters. Use stochastic position 0.5 when its range is zero, ATR ratio 1 when long ATR is zero, EMA distance 0 when short ATR is zero, and volume ratio 0 when average volume is zero.
Calculation: Directional bias = Clamp to [−1, 1] the weighted average of: clamp((short EMA − long EMA) / ATR, −1, 1); clamp((RSI − RSI midpoint) / RSI midpoint, −1, 1); clamp((close − close at velocity lookback) / (velocity lookback × ATR), −1, 1); and, when the neural network is enabled and ready, clamp(neural direction forecast × previous close / previous ATR, −1, 1). Exclude the neural term and its weight when the network is disabled or unready.
Long: Short EMA > long EMA
Short: Short EMA <= long EMA
Long: Directional tilt enabled AND directional bias > positive threshold
Short: Directional tilt enabled AND directional bias < negative threshold
Long: Neural network enabled AND neural network ready AND neural direction forecast >= 0
Short: Neural network enabled AND neural network ready AND neural direction forecast < 0
