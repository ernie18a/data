Calculation: Pivots = On each closed chart bar, test the high and low a pivot-lookback number of bars ago against every other high or low in the preceding and following pivot-lookback bars, excluding the current bar. A pivot high must be strictly greater than all tested highs; a pivot low must be strictly less than all tested lows. Store each confirmed price with index equal to the current bar index minus the separately configured pivot-index offset. Keep the most recent configured number of highs and lows separately.
Calculation: Near(a,b,t) = abs(a-b) <= (a+b)*t/2, including when a and b are slopes.
Calculation: Boundary = The straight line through the specified pivot coordinates, extrapolated to the requested bar; if their indices coincide, use the first price. U and L are the lines through the two latest pivot highs and two latest pivot lows, respectively; su and sl are their slopes. H1 and L1 denote the latest pivot high and low; H2, H3, L2 and L3 denote progressively older pivots of the same kind. Indices are compared using their stored pivot indices.
Calculation: Breakout(B,d) = Search past bars from the oldest to the newest within a lookback equal to max(configured scan lookback, pivot-index offset) plus a configured buffer, excluding the current bar. Select the first bar whose high exceeds B plus its margin for d=up, or whose low is below B minus its margin for d=down. Optionally substitute close for high or low. The margin is either that bar's ATR times a multiplier or B times a percentage. If full-body confirmation is enabled and confirmation is not ignored, both open and close must also exceed the upper threshold or both must be below the lower threshold. If consecutive confirmation is enabled and confirmation is not ignored, the requested number of subsequent closes must also exceed or fall below their respective projected thresholds; these subsequent bars may include the current bar. The selected bar need not follow the pivots. Entry reference E is its open.
Calculation: Valid setup = A breakout exists AND, unless confirmation is ignored, optional breakout-bar volume exceeds its volume SMA times a multiplier AND risk is nonzero AND max(0, abs(target-E) - slippage cost - E*commission percentage*2)/abs(E-stop) >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio. Slippage cost is a configurable price amount. Volume SMA uses available history up to its configured length. Unless otherwise stated, stop is E minus a configured fraction of abs(target-E) for an upward setup or E plus that fraction for a downward setup; alternatively, stop is the specified stop anchor minus current ATR times a buffer for an upward setup or plus that buffer for a downward setup.
Calculation: Valid size(h) = h > max(current close times a configured percentage, current ATR times a configured multiplier).
Calculation: Double setup = For a top, H2.index < L1.index < H1.index AND Near(H1.price,H2.price,level tolerance) AND Valid size((H1.price+H2.price)/2-L1.price); break downward through the horizontal L1.price; target = L1.price minus the size; stop anchor = max(H1.price,H2.price). For a bottom, L2.index < H1.index < L1.index AND Near(L1.price,L2.price,level tolerance) AND Valid size(H1.price-(L1.price+L2.price)/2); break upward through horizontal H1.price; target = H1.price plus the size; stop anchor = min(L1.price,L2.price).
Calculation: Triple setup = For a top, H3.index < L2.index < H2.index < L1.index < H1.index AND Near(H1.price,H2.price,level tolerance) AND Near(H2.price,H3.price,level tolerance) AND Valid size(max(H1.price,H2.price,H3.price)-min(L1.price,L2.price)); break downward through L; target = L at the breakout minus the size; stop anchor = max(H1.price,H2.price,H3.price). For a bottom, L3.index < H2.index < L2.index < H1.index < L1.index AND Near(L1.price,L2.price,level tolerance) AND Near(L2.price,L3.price,level tolerance) AND Valid size(max(H1.price,H2.price)-min(L1.price,L2.price,L3.price)); break upward through horizontal max(H1.price,H2.price); target = that horizontal level plus the size; stop anchor = min(L1.price,L2.price,L3.price).
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders setup = For a top, H3.index < L2.index < H2.index < L1.index < H1.index AND H2.price > H1.price AND H2.price > H3.price AND Near(H1.price,H3.price,symmetry tolerance) AND Valid size(H2.price-(L1.price+L2.price)/2); break downward through L; target = L at the breakout minus the size; stop anchor = H1.price. For a bottom, L3.index < H2.index < L2.index < H1.index < L1.index AND L2.price < L1.price AND L2.price < L3.price AND Near(L1.price,L3.price,symmetry tolerance) AND Valid size((H1.price+H2.price)/2-L2.price); break upward through U; target = U at the breakout plus the size; stop anchor = L1.price.
Calculation: Flag setup = Pole range is the highest high minus lowest low from the earlier of H2.index and L2.index through the current bar, capped at a configured maximum lookback. Pole range > current ATR times a multiplier AND either su < 0 AND sl < 0 for an upward setup, or su > 0 AND sl > 0 for a downward setup. Break upward through U or downward through L respectively; target = breakout boundary plus or minus pole range respectively; stop anchor = opposite boundary at breakout. Classify as flag if Near(su,sl,parallel tolerance), otherwise pennant; the corresponding classification must be enabled.
Calculation: Wedge setup = U at the current bar > L at the current bar AND either sl < su < 0 for an upward setup or sl > su > 0 for a downward setup. Break upward through U or downward through L respectively; target = H2.price or L2.price respectively; stop anchor = opposite boundary at breakout.
Calculation: Triangle setup = Base height is abs(U-L) at the earlier of H2.index and L2.index. U at the current bar > L at the current bar AND Valid size(base height) AND either (su < 0 AND sl > 0) OR (abs(su) < current close times a flatness factor AND sl > 0) for an upward setup, or (su < 0 AND sl > 0) OR (su < 0 AND abs(sl) < current close times a flatness factor) for a downward setup. Break upward through U or downward through L respectively; target = breakout boundary plus or minus base height respectively; stop anchor = opposite boundary at breakout.
Calculation: Rectangle setup = abs(su) and abs(sl) are each less than current ATR times a flatness factor AND Near(su,sl,slope tolerance) AND Valid size((H1.price+H2.price-L1.price-L2.price)/2). Break upward through horizontal (H1.price+H2.price)/2 or downward through horizontal (L1.price+L2.price)/2; target = breakout boundary plus or minus the size respectively; stop anchor = the opposite horizontal boundary.
Calculation: Cup setup = For an upward setup, H2.index < L2.index < H1.index < L1.index AND Near(H1.price,H2.price,symmetry tolerance) AND L2.price < L1.price < H1.price AND Valid size(H1.price-L2.price); break upward through horizontal H1.price; target = U at the breakout plus the size; stop anchor = L1.price. For a downward setup, L2.index < H2.index < L1.index < H1.index AND Near(L1.price,L2.price,symmetry tolerance) AND L1.price < H1.price < H2.price AND Valid size(H2.price-L1.price); break downward through horizontal L1.price; target = L at the breakout minus the size; stop anchor = H1.price.
Calculation: Selected setup = Evaluate enabled setups with their required pivots and Valid setup. Within double, triple, head-and-shoulders, triangle and rectangle, choose the top or upward branch first as implemented: top first for double, triple and head-and-shoulders; upward first for triangle and rectangle. For cup, choose upward first. Select the first valid result in this order: double, triple, head-and-shoulders, flag or pennant, wedge, triangle, rectangle, cup. Require at least two stored highs and two stored lows and a current bar index above the configured history threshold. Emit a new setup only when its start index differs from the last emitted setup's start index by more than the configured cooldown, or no setup has yet been emitted. The start index is H2.index for flag, wedge, triangle and rectangle; the oldest same-side pivot for double, triple and head-and-shoulders; and H2.index for an upward cup or L2.index for a downward cup.
Long: Selected setup is upward.
Short: Selected setup is downward.
