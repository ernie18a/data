Calculation: Pivot high/low = a high/low that is strictly greater/less than every other high/low in the surrounding pivot lookback window; confirm after the right-side bars complete, and anchor it at the configured right-side offset.
Calculation: Pattern boundary = straight line through the two most recent relevant pivots; a flat boundary is the average of its two pivot prices. Project each boundary to the breakout bar.
Calculation: Breakout = price exceeds the pattern boundary by the configured margin (ATR-based or percentage-based); use high/low unless close-only is enabled, require the whole candle body beyond the margin if body-only is enabled, and require consecutive confirming closes when configured.
Calculation: Valid pattern size = pattern height exceeds both the configured minimum price percentage and ATR multiple.
Calculation: Valid risk/reward = adjusted target reward divided by entry-to-stop risk meets the configured minimum; adjusted reward subtracts slippage and round-trip commission. If volume filtering is enabled, breakout-bar volume must exceed its volume moving average times the configured multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = any enabled structure satisfying its geometry and valid-size and risk/reward filters: double bottom (two lows aligned within level tolerance, with an intervening high; break above that high); triple bottom (three lows aligned within level tolerance, with intervening highs; break above the higher intervening high); inverse head and shoulders (three ordered lows, middle low below both outer lows, outer lows aligned within symmetry tolerance; break above the line between the two intervening highs); cup and handle (two highs aligned within symmetry tolerance, intervening bottom, then a higher handle low below the rim; break above the rim); bullish flag or pennant (upper and lower pivot boundaries both slope down, prior pole range exceeds 3 ATR; break above upper boundary; parallel boundaries define a flag, otherwise a pennant); falling wedge (both boundaries slope down, lower boundary falls faster, current projected height is positive; break above upper boundary); ascending or symmetrical triangle (boundaries converge, upper boundary slopes down or is flat, lower slopes up; break above upper boundary); rectangle (both boundaries are flat, slopes aligned within tolerance; break above average upper pivots).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = any enabled structure satisfying its geometry and valid-size and risk/reward filters: double top (two highs aligned within level tolerance, with an intervening low; break below that low); triple top (three highs aligned within level tolerance, with intervening lows; break below the lower intervening low); head and shoulders (three ordered highs, middle high above both outer highs, outer highs aligned within symmetry tolerance; break below the line between the two intervening lows); inverted cup and handle (two lows aligned within symmetry tolerance, intervening top, then a lower handle high above the rim; break below the rim); bearish flag or pennant (upper and lower pivot boundaries both slope up, prior pole range exceeds 3 ATR; break below lower boundary; parallel boundaries define a flag, otherwise a pennant); rising wedge (both boundaries slope up, lower boundary rises faster, current projected height is positive; break below lower boundary); descending or symmetrical triangle (boundaries converge, upper boundary slopes down, lower slopes up or is flat; break below lower boundary); rectangle (both boundaries are flat, slopes aligned within tolerance; break below average lower pivots).
Long: confirmed bar has a valid bullish pattern and its breakout is confirmed; if volume filtering is enabled, breakout volume exceeds its moving average times the configured multiplier.
Short: confirmed bar has a valid bearish pattern and its breakout is confirmed; if volume filtering is enabled, breakout volume exceeds its moving average times the configured multiplier.
