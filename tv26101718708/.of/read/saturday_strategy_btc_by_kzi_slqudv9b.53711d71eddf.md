Calculation: Previous completed daily candle is bullish = its close > its open (daily is higher than the chart timeframe; use the last completed daily bar)
Long: Current chart bar is Saturday AND previous completed daily candle is bullish
Short: Current chart bar is Saturday AND previous completed daily candle close <= its open
Exit: Long, chart close >= entry price × (1 + long take-profit rate) OR current chart bar is not Saturday
Exit: Short, chart close <= entry price × short exit multiplier OR current chart bar is not Saturday
