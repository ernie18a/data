Calculation: RSI cross-down = RSI of closes on the selected timeframe is below its threshold and the preceding selected-timeframe bar’s RSI was at or above the threshold. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: Averaging threshold for order k = base entry price × (1 + first deviation × sum of deviation multiplier raised to powers from 0 through k − 1), with deviations expressed as fractions.
Calculation: Average entry = total quote-currency size of the base entry and filled averaging orders divided by the sum of each order’s quote-currency size divided by its fill close. The size of averaging order k is the first averaging-order size × order-size multiplier raised to k − 1.
Calculation: Trailing low = lowest close since the short entry among bars on which the active-window condition holds and close is at or below the then-current average entry × (1 − take-profit fraction). Reset it on entry and exit.
Short: Active-window filter is disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive active window, AND position is flat AND RSI cross-down occurs.
Short: Active-window filter is disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive active window, AND position is short AND filled averaging-order count is below its limit AND close is at or above the threshold for the next averaging order. Fill at close, at most one averaging order per bar, before evaluating exits.
Exit: short, active-window filter is disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive active window, AND close is at or above average entry × (1 + stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, active-window filter is disabled OR bar time is within the inclusive active window, AND close is below average entry × (1 + stop-loss fraction) AND trailing low exists AND close is at or above trailing low × (1 + trailing fraction).
