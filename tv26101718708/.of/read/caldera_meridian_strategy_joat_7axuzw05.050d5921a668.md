Calculation: Bull regime = short EMA(close) > intermediate EMA(close) AND intermediate EMA(close) > long EMA(close) AND close > intermediate EMA(close); bear regime = short EMA(close) < intermediate EMA(close) AND intermediate EMA(close) < long EMA(close) AND close < intermediate EMA(close); otherwise regime is neutral.
Calculation: Continuation probability = the fraction of transitions during a rolling lookback whose previous regime equals the current regime and whose new regime also equals the current regime; use a configurable fallback when there are no such previous regimes. Expansion = percentile rank of ATR/close over a separate lookback > a threshold; use a configurable fallback rank when unavailable. Probability pass = continuation probability >= a threshold OR expansion.
Calculation: Spread = max(high - low, minimum price increment). Signed body volume = (close - open) / spread × volume. Pressure = clamp(EMA(signed body volume) / EMA(abs(signed body volume)) × 100, -100, 100), using zero when the denominator is effectively zero. Volume impulse = volume / SMA(volume), using a configurable fallback when the denominator is effectively zero.
Calculation: Auction center = rolling sum(typical price × volume) / rolling sum(volume), using typical price when the denominator is effectively zero. Upper and lower value boundaries = auction center ± a multiplier × rolling standard deviation of typical price over the same lookback. Discount = close < lower boundary AND close > low + a fraction of spread. Premium = close > upper boundary AND close < high - the same fraction of spread. Reclaim = close > lower boundary AND previous close <= previous lower boundary. Reject = close < upper boundary AND previous close >= previous upper boundary.
Calculation: A confirmed pivot high or low is a high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side satisfying the pivot condition; it becomes available only after the right-side bars have completed. Retain the most recently confirmed pivot high and low. Displacement = abs(close - open) > ATR × a multiplier AND volume impulse > a threshold. Upward break = close > latest pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high AND displacement. Downward break = close < latest pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low AND displacement. Structure bias starts neutral and changes to bullish on an upward break or bearish on a downward break, with the upward break taking precedence if both occur. Bull structure = bullish structure bias OR (low < latest pivot low AND close > latest pivot low) OR (low > high two bars ago AND close > open). Bear structure = bearish structure bias OR (high > latest pivot high AND close < latest pivot high) OR (high < low two bars ago AND close < open).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pass for long = higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > EMA of close on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe pass for short = filter disabled OR close < that EMA.
Calculation: Long score = clamp to the score range the sum of configurable weights for each true condition: bull regime; MACD histogram > zero AND RSI > a bullish threshold AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator; close > session VWAP of typical price AND session VWAP > its previous value; pressure > a positive threshold AND volume impulse > a threshold; low < previous low AND close > open AND pressure > previous pressure AND volume impulse > a separate threshold; bull structure; discount OR reclaim; higher-timeframe pass for long; probability pass; plus a capped contribution proportional to ADX. Short score uses corresponding weights for bear regime; MACD histogram < zero AND RSI < a bearish threshold AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator; close < session VWAP AND session VWAP < its previous value; pressure < a negative threshold AND volume impulse > the pressure threshold; high > previous high AND close < open AND pressure < previous pressure AND volume impulse > the absorption threshold; bear structure; premium OR reject; higher-timeframe pass for short; probability pass; plus the same capped ADX contribution. MACD uses shorter and longer EMA lookbacks and a signal lookback.
Calculation: At long entry, stop = min(entry close - ATR × stop multiplier, latest confirmed pivot low), or the ATR stop if no pivot low exists. At short entry, stop = max(entry close + ATR × stop multiplier, latest confirmed pivot high), or the ATR stop if no pivot high exists. Fix the stop at entry. Risk = max(abs(entry close - stop), ATR at entry × a minimum-risk multiplier). Fix three targets at entry close plus risk × each target multiplier for long, or entry close minus risk × each target multiplier for short; assign each target a configurable fraction of the original position.
Long: Long score > short score.
Short: Short score > long score.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND position is flat AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured trading session) AND long score >= execution threshold AND long score > short score + entry margin AND higher-timeframe pass for long AND premium is false.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND position is flat AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured trading session) AND short score >= execution threshold AND short score > long score + entry margin AND higher-timeframe pass for short AND discount is false.
Exit: Long, low <= fixed long stop OR high >= a fixed long target for its assigned position fraction.
Exit: Short, high >= fixed short stop OR low <= a fixed short target for its assigned position fraction.
Exit: Long, confirmed chart bar AND ((regime exit enabled AND bear regime) OR (pressure exit enabled AND pressure < a negative risk-off threshold) OR short score > long score + risk-off margin).
Exit: Short, confirmed chart bar AND ((regime exit enabled AND bull regime) OR (pressure exit enabled AND pressure > a positive risk-off threshold) OR long score > short score + risk-off margin).
