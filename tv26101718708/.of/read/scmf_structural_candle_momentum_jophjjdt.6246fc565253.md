Calculation: Close Position = 2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1; for a doji, use 0.30 × sign(open − previous close).
Calculation: Wick Absorption = clamp(((lower wick − upper wick) / range) × (1 + body size / range) / 2, −1, 1); for a doji, use 0.30 × (lower wick − upper wick) / total wick length, clamped to [−1, 1].
Calculation: Net Volume Delta = volume-weighted buy aggression minus sell aggression, divided by total adjusted volume. Per lower-timeframe bar, buy aggression is the mean of close position within that bar’s range and the tick direction versus the previous lower-timeframe close (use the prior chart-bar close for the first intrabar); use 0.5 for an unchanged tick. Weight each bar’s volume by the inverse of 1 plus its range excess over the mean intrabar range. Multiply the result by 0.70 when its sign conflicts with chart-bar close versus intrabar volume-weighted typical price. Use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, only after they are completed.
Calculation: Temporal Volume Distribution = clamp(weighted phase volume / total phase volume × direction, −1, 1), using lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, only after they are completed. The phases are the first two bars, next five bars, and remaining bars; their weights are respectively 0, 0.50, 1 normally, and 0.80, 0.45, 0.35 on an event day. Direction is the sign of the last close versus the third-from-last close in the final phase; if equal, use the sign of Close Position.
Calculation: Price Path Topology = clamp(final direction × fraction of lower-timeframe close-to-close steps matching final direction + 0.30 × (late normalized price slope − early normalized price slope), −1, 1). Normalize slopes by chart-bar range (or ATR if range is zero); split the lower-timeframe closes at the midpoint. Use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, only after they are completed.
Calculation: Composite Momentum Score = weighted average of Close Position, Wick Absorption, Net Volume Delta, Temporal Volume Distribution, and Price Path Topology, dropping unavailable dimensions and renormalizing the weights. Regime-dependent weights may be used, with greater Net Volume Delta and Temporal Volume Distribution weights when trending and greater Close Position and Wick Absorption weights when ranging. Suppress the score unless the chart timeframe is daily or weekly, chart-bar volume is positive, the liquidity concentration has not exceeded its abort threshold, and the product of validity, intrabar coverage/concentration, event, liquidity, doji, and regime quality factors is at least its minimum threshold.
Long: Composite Momentum Score > 0.10 OR (Composite Momentum Score is not suppressed AND Close Position < −0.35 AND Net Volume Delta > 0.25 AND Wick Absorption > 0.15)
Short: Composite Momentum Score < −0.10 OR (Composite Momentum Score is not suppressed AND Close Position > 0.35 AND Net Volume Delta < −0.25 AND Temporal Volume Distribution < −0.15)
