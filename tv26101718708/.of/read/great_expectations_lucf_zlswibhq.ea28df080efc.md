Calculation: Directional indicators and ADX = compute true range and positive/negative directional movement, substituting zero for missing prior OHLC values; recursively smooth each as previous smoothed value minus previous smoothed value divided by the directional-indicator length plus the current value, starting from zero. Positive and negative directional indicators are their respective smoothed movements divided by smoothed true range times 100. ADX is the simple moving average, over a separate smoothing length, of 100 times the absolute difference between the directional indicators divided by their sum.
Calculation: Entry stop = on the entry bar, use its low for a long or high for a short as the base; subtract for a long or add for a short either an ATR multiple, a percentage of the base, or a fixed amount, and floor the result at zero. ATR is measured on the entry bar.
Calculation: Active stop = initialize to the entry stop. On each later bar, calculate a candidate as either the average of the previous two bars' min(open, close) for a long or max(open, close) for a short, minus or plus the current ATR times a multiplier respectively; or the highest high for a long or lowest low for a short since entry, including the current bar and initialized to entry price, multiplied by one minus or plus a chosen deviation respectively. The deviation is either a fixed percentage or a multiple of entry risk divided by entry price; entry risk is the absolute difference between entry price and entry stop plus entry fees divided by 100. Floor the candidate at zero and retain the higher of it and the previous stop for a long, or the lower for a short. Use the entry-stop ATR length for the in-trade ATR.
Long: (close SMA is greater than every one of its preceding values over the entry lookback OR positive directional indicator crosses above negative directional indicator AND ADX is above its threshold) AND, if enabled, close is greater than open AND, if enabled, volume is greater than previous volume AND, if enabled, a separately parameterized close SMA is greater than every one of its preceding values over the filter lookback AND, if enabled, the selected data-field source equals 1 AND, if enabled, datetime is within the selected date range.
Short: (close SMA is less than every one of its preceding values over the entry lookback OR negative directional indicator crosses above positive directional indicator AND ADX is above its threshold) AND, if enabled, close is less than open AND, if enabled, volume is greater than previous volume AND, if enabled, a separately parameterized close SMA is less than every one of its preceding values over the filter lookback AND, if enabled, the selected data-field source equals 0 AND, if enabled, datetime is within the selected date range AND no long entry condition is simultaneously true.
Exit: long, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND close is below the previous bar's active stop.
Exit: short, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND close is above the previous bar's active stop.
