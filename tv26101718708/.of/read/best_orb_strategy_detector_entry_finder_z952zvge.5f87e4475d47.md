Calculation: Opening Range High/Low = highest high and lowest low during the selected opening window from session start; range = high − low
Calculation: Signal bar = closed bar of the selected opening-range timeframe when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe; otherwise, the closed chart bar
Calculation: Stop = opposite Opening Range boundary, extended outward by a configurable fraction of the Opening Range width
Calculation: Profit target at each enabled reward multiple = the nearer in the trade direction of (entry ± Opening Range width × multiple) and (entry ± stop distance × multiple × price-tier adjustment)
Long: Opening Range is complete AND (signal bar close crosses from at-or-below to above Opening Range High plus breakout buffer OR signal bar high crosses from at-or-below to above that level when wick confirmation is selected) AND breakout filters pass AND breakout entry mode is enabled; OR a prior upward breakout traveled at least the minimum retest distance, remained outside for at least the minimum number of bars, then closed back inside the Opening Range within retest tolerance, and a later closed signal bar closes above Opening Range High plus retest tolerance AND retest entry mode is enabled
Short: Opening Range is complete AND (signal bar close crosses from at-or-above to below Opening Range Low minus breakout buffer OR signal bar low crosses from at-or-above to below that level when wick confirmation is selected) AND breakout filters pass AND breakout entry mode is enabled; OR a prior downward breakout traveled at least the minimum retest distance, remained outside for at least the minimum number of bars, then closed back inside the Opening Range within retest tolerance, and a later closed signal bar closes below Opening Range Low minus retest tolerance AND retest entry mode is enabled
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ highest enabled profit target
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ lowest enabled profit target
