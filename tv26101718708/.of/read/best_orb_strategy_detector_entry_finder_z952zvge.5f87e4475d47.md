Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars starting in the selected session's opening-range window, measured from the first available session bar. Reset the range and breakout state at each session start or calendar-day change. Complete the range when a bar starts within one minute of the window's end, or on the first bar starting after the window; a bar starting exactly at the window's end neither updates nor completes it.
Calculation: Signal bar = the last completed opening-range-duration bar and its preceding bar when that duration is higher than the chart timeframe; evaluate it only on the first confirmed chart bar of a new opening-range-duration bar. Otherwise use the current and previous confirmed chart bars.
Calculation: Retained gap = a bullish gap between the high two chart bars ago and the current low, or a bearish gap between the current high and the low two chart bars ago. Retain the most recent configurable number of gaps detected after the opening range completes; retain filled gaps as well. A gap is near a level when the level lies between its bottom minus a configurable multiple of its width and its top plus the same multiple. Gap detection must be enabled for gaps to be retained.
Calculation: Breakout state = set separately for each direction on its qualifying breakout. Clear it at reset, on that direction's stop, on a failed break, or on a qualifying retest in cycle-tracking mode. A failed break occurs when, within a configurable number of chart bars after the breakout, the close returns inside the retest-buffered range before the required consecutive outside-bar count is reached.
Calculation: Retest state = after a directional breakout, count consecutive subsequent chart closes outside the corresponding unbuffered range boundary, resetting the count to zero whenever a close is not outside. Remember whether any subsequent close exceeded the boundary by the minimum retest distance; reset that memory on the next breakout in that direction. The retest-buffered range extends both boundaries by a configurable percentage of the range high.
Calculation: Stop = opening-range low minus a configurable fraction of range width for a long, or opening-range high plus that fraction for a short, fixed at entry.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus for a long, or minus for a short, a configurable target multiple times the smaller of opening-range width and adjusted risk. Adjusted risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance multiplied by a configurable factor selected from tiers of entry price. Fix the target at entry; use the largest enabled target multiple for the final exit.
Long: During the selected session after the range completes, the directional breakout state is clear AND the signal bar close crosses above the range high plus its percentage buffer, OR its high crosses above that level in wick mode AND the optional volume filter passes AND the optional bullish-gap filter passes. For the volume filter require current chart volume greater than previous chart volume, or at least the volume SMA times a configurable multiplier, or both, according to the selected mode; the average-volume alternative also passes at a separate strong-volume multiplier. For the gap filter require a retained bullish gap near the range high.
Short: During the selected session after the range completes, the directional breakout state is clear AND the signal bar close crosses below the range low minus its percentage buffer, OR its low crosses below that level in wick mode AND the same optional volume filter passes AND the optional bearish-gap filter passes at the range low.
Long: After an upward breakout, the minimum retest distance has been reached AND the current close is inside the retest-buffered range AND the current consecutive outside-bar count is at least its configured minimum AND no retest has occurred since that breakout AND the applicable retest cycle limit has not been reached.
Short: After a downward breakout, the minimum retest distance has been reached AND the current close is inside the retest-buffered range AND the current consecutive outside-bar count is at least its configured minimum AND no retest has occurred since that breakout AND the applicable retest cycle limit has not been reached.
Long: A qualifying upward retest has occurred AND a signal-bar close exceeds the range high plus its own configurable percentage retest buffer before the rejection timeout expires.
Short: A qualifying downward retest has occurred AND a signal-bar close falls below the range low minus its own configurable percentage retest buffer before the rejection timeout expires.
Exit: long, chart-bar low <= stop OR chart-bar high >= highest enabled target.
Exit: short, chart-bar high >= stop OR chart-bar low <= highest enabled target.
