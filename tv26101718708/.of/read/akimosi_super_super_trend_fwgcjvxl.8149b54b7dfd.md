Calculation: Chart-timeframe SuperTrend direction and line = Calculate ATR-based bands from the chart bars: lower band = (high + low) / 2 − multiplier × ATR; upper band = (high + low) / 2 + multiplier × ATR. Each bar’s lower trend band is the greater of its lower band and the prior lower trend band when the prior close is above the prior lower trend band; otherwise it is its lower band. Each bar’s upper trend band is the lesser of its upper band and the prior upper trend band when the prior close is below the prior upper trend band; otherwise it is its upper band. Direction is bullish when close exceeds the prior upper trend band, bearish when close is below the prior lower trend band, and otherwise carries forward the prior direction (initially bullish). The line is the lower trend band when bullish and the upper trend band when bearish.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction = Calculate the same bands and direction using bars from a higher timeframe, using the same ATR lookback and multiplier as the chart-timeframe SuperTrend. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: (close > current chart-timeframe SuperTrend line AND prior close <= prior chart-timeframe SuperTrend line) OR (chart-timeframe SuperTrend direction is bullish AND prior chart-timeframe SuperTrend direction is bearish AND higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction is bullish)
Short: (close < current chart-timeframe SuperTrend line AND prior close >= prior chart-timeframe SuperTrend line) OR (chart-timeframe SuperTrend direction is bearish AND prior chart-timeframe SuperTrend direction is bullish AND higher-timeframe SuperTrend direction is bearish)
