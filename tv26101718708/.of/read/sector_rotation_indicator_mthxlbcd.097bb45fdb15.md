Calculation: Kernel deviation = mean of (close - kernel estimate) / kernel estimate for three normalized weighted averages of closes at offsets i = 0 through bandwidth; their respective weights are 0.75 × (1 - (i / bandwidth)²), 1 / (exp((i / bandwidth)²) + 2 + exp(-(i / bandwidth)²)), and cos(π × i / (2 × bandwidth)) × (1 - i / bandwidth). The kernel midpoint is an SMA of the kernel deviation over a longer lookback. The calculation can use chart bars or bars from another timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last available completed bar. When adapting to intraday chart bars, scale the bandwidth and midpoint lookback by estimated chart bars per higher-timeframe period; for sufficiently short chart bars, sample at an evenly spaced stride and use an EMA for the midpoint if its SMA is unavailable.
Long: Kernel deviation > kernel midpoint
Short: Kernel deviation <= kernel midpoint
Long: Low > upper Bollinger Band AND no earlier low > upper Bollinger Band since close was last <= the Bollinger basis; calculate the bands on chart bars or on another timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or last available completed lower-timeframe bar.
Short: High < lower Bollinger Band AND no earlier high < lower Bollinger Band since close was last >= the Bollinger basis; calculate the bands on chart bars or on another timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or last available completed lower-timeframe bar.
