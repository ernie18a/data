Calculation: Donchian midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over its lookback. Use a short lookback for conversion, a longer lookback for base, and a still longer lookback for span B. In classic mode use the fixed lookbacks. In adaptive mode, normalize ATR within its rolling highest-to-lowest range, using one-half if the range is zero; multiply each base lookback by 1 + adaptation strength * (1 - 2 * normalized ATR), round, and clamp to the rounded base lookback times 1 minus and 1 plus adaptation strength, with the lower bound at least the minimum lookback.
Calculation: Cloud = span A, the average of conversion and base, and span B, the longest-lookback Donchian midpoint. Current cloud top and bottom are the maximum and minimum of these spans displaced forward by displacement minus one bars. Cloud thickness is top minus bottom; thin means thickness < its moving average times a thickness factor.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume <= 0 OR volume > volume SMA times a confirmation multiplier. Strong volume = volume <= 0 OR volume > the same volume SMA times a breakout multiplier.
Calculation: Momentum = EMA of the clipped weighted sum of normalized conversion-minus-base spread, its change over an acceleration lookback, and the difference between span A and span B changes over a cloud-slope lookback. Normalize both spread and cloud-slope difference by ATR, substituting zero when ATR is zero; clip the sum to symmetric positive and negative limits.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of configurable points for price outside the cloud in the direction or fewer points for price inside it; conversion on the directional side of base; source on the directional side of its value displacement bars ago; span A change over a slope lookback greater than span B change for long or less than or equal for short; cloud not thin or fewer points if thin; volume confirmation; momentum beyond a directional threshold or fewer points when merely on the directional side of zero; and momentum on the directional side of zero and changing further in that direction. Cap the sum at the score maximum.
Calculation: Warmup = bar index >= the greater of longest base lookback plus displacement and a minimum warmup length.
Calculation: Entry stop = at the entry bar, either the cloud bottom for long or top for short adjusted outward by an ATR buffer, the base line adjusted outward by an ATR buffer, or entry price adjusted outward by an ATR multiple. Hold the chosen stop fixed until a new signal. Entry target = entry price plus for long or minus for short a target multiple of the greater of absolute entry-to-stop distance and an ATR-based minimum risk, all fixed at entry. Each new entry signal resets these levels at its close; process long before short if both occur, then check exits on that same bar.
Long: Warmup AND conversion crosses above base AND (thickness filter disabled OR cloud thickness >= its moving average times the thickness factor) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume confirmation) AND (source confirmation filter disabled OR source > source displacement bars ago) AND long directional score >= minimum score.
Short: Warmup AND conversion crosses below base AND (thickness filter disabled OR cloud thickness >= its moving average times the thickness factor) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume confirmation) AND (source confirmation filter disabled OR source < source displacement bars ago) AND short directional score >= minimum score.
Long: Warmup AND close > cloud top AND previous close <= previous cloud top AND strong volume.
Short: Warmup AND close < cloud bottom AND previous close >= previous cloud bottom AND strong volume.
Long: Warmup AND bars since the most recent upward cloud breakout > minimum retest delay AND bars since that breakout <= maximum retest lookback AND low <= cloud top AND close > cloud top.
Short: Warmup AND bars since the most recent downward cloud breakout > minimum retest delay AND bars since that breakout <= maximum retest lookback AND high >= cloud bottom AND close < cloud bottom.
Long: Close > cloud top AND conversion > base.
Short: Close < cloud bottom AND conversion < base.
Long: Span A > span B.
Short: Span A <= span B.
Long: Momentum > 0.
Short: Momentum < 0.
Long: On another timeframe, close > maximum of undisplaced span A and span B AND classic conversion > classic base. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the bar immediately preceding the first lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: On another timeframe, close < minimum of undisplaced span A and span B AND classic conversion < classic base. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the bar immediately preceding the first lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Exit: long, low <= entry stop OR high >= entry target.
Exit: short, high >= entry stop OR low <= entry target.
