Calculation: Fast and mid-term ATR trails = For each trail, let distance = its ATR multiplied by its multiplier and previous trail = the prior trail, or zero if unavailable. If current and previous close are both above previous trail, trail = max(previous trail, close − distance); otherwise, if both are below previous trail, trail = min(previous trail, close + distance); otherwise, trail = close − distance if close > previous trail, else close + distance. The fast trail has a shorter ATR period and a smaller multiplier than the mid-term trail.
Long: datetime is within the configured backtest range AND close has increased from the previous close for at least the configured long-entry consecutive-bar count AND close > long-term moving average AND fast moving average > long-term moving average.
Short: spot mode is disabled AND datetime is within the configured backtest range AND close has decreased from the previous close for at least the configured short-entry consecutive-bar count AND close < long-term moving average AND fast moving average < long-term moving average.
Exit: long, automatic exit is enabled AND datetime is within the configured backtest range AND ((close has decreased from the previous close for at least the configured long-exit consecutive-bar count AND close > long-term moving average AND current close and each of the previous two closes < current mid-term moving average) OR fast moving average crosses below mid-term moving average).
Exit: short, automatic exit is enabled AND datetime is within the configured backtest range AND ((close has increased from the previous close for at least the long-entry consecutive-bar count AND close < long-term moving average AND current close and each of the previous two closes > current mid-term moving average) OR fast moving average crosses above mid-term moving average).
Exit: long, ATR trailing exit is enabled AND fast ATR trail crosses below mid-term ATR trail.
Exit: short, ATR trailing exit is enabled AND fast ATR trail crosses above mid-term ATR trail.
Exit: long, percentage exits are enabled AND long take-profit percentage > 0 AND high >= entry price × (1 + long take-profit percentage).
Exit: short, percentage exits are enabled AND short take-profit percentage > 0 AND low <= entry price × (1 − short take-profit percentage).
Exit: long, percentage exits are enabled AND stop-loss percentage > 0 AND ((spot mode is disabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)) OR (spot mode is enabled AND low <= entry price − entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) × minimum price tick)).
Exit: short, percentage exits are enabled AND stop-loss percentage > 0 AND high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage).
Exit: long, breakout trailing exit is enabled AND close < highest high over the configured lookback including the current bar × (1 − callback percentage).
Exit: short, breakout trailing exit is enabled AND close > lowest low over the configured lookback including the current bar × (1 + callback percentage).
