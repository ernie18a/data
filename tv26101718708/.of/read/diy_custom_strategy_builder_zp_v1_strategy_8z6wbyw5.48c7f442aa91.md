Long: Selected leading indicator is bullish AND every enabled long confirmation is bullish AND the leading bullish condition has been true for no more than the signal-expiry lookback AND (alternation is disabled OR the previous qualifying state was short)
Short: Selected leading indicator is bearish AND every enabled short confirmation is bearish AND the leading bearish condition has been true for no more than the signal-expiry lookback AND (alternation is disabled OR the previous qualifying state was long)
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confirmations = enabled filters, respectively: close above/below the selected EMA; fast EMA above/below slow EMA, or fast, medium, and slow EMAs ordered bullish/bearish; bullish/bearish direction of the selected Range Filter, Rational Quadratic Kernel, Supertrend, Half Trend, Donchian Trend Ribbon, ROC, TSI, TDFI, Trendline Breakout, Range Detector, Heiken-Ashi Oscillator, Chandelier Exit, Stochastic, RSI, VWAP, CCI, B-Xtrender, Bull Bear Power Trend, DPO, BB Oscillator, DMI, PSAR, MACD, SSL Channel, Waddah Attar Explosion, Chaikin Money Flow, Vortex Index, STC, Awesome Oscillator, Wolfpack ID, QQE Mod, or Hull Suite; plus enabled volume, Choppiness Index, and Damiani Volatility conditions as defined by their selected modes. Range Detector confirmation requires close outside its active range; Choppiness Index requires its value below its threshold; Damiani Volatility requires volatility above its selected threshold condition.
Long: A confirmed up-fractal is marked when its center low is below the preceding lows and is no lower than the following lows; a Supertrend buy marker occurs when its trend changes bearish to bullish; an STC buy marker occurs when its value is at or above its upper band
Short: A confirmed down-fractal is marked when its center high is above the preceding highs and is no lower than the following highs; a Supertrend sell marker occurs when its trend changes bullish to bearish; an STC sell marker occurs when its value is at or below its lower band
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price
Calculation: Stop price = latest confirmed swing low below close for longs or swing high above close for shorts, preferring chart-timeframe pivots over pivots from the configured swing timeframe; if unavailable, use close minus/plus the selected ATR-based fallback distance, or close multiplied by the fallback percentage
Calculation: Target price = entry price plus/minus the selected reward-to-risk multiple of the entry-to-stop distance
