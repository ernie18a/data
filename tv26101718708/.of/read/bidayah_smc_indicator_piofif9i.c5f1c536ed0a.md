Calculation: Structure pivots = On each bar, identify a new pivot high when the high from the lookback bars ago exceeds the highs of the intervening bars and the leg changes to bearish; identify a new pivot low when the low from the lookback bars ago is below the intervening lows and the leg changes to bullish. Swing pivots use a longer lookback than internal pivots.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = On the configured gap timeframe, the current bar's low and the previous completed bar's close are above the high from two bars ago, the previous completed bar's percentage change from open exceeds the adaptive threshold, and a new timeframe bar has begun. The adaptive threshold is twice the cumulative mean of absolute percentage changes on new timeframe bars, divided by the chart bar index.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = On the configured gap timeframe, the current bar's high and the previous completed bar's close are below the low from two bars ago, the magnitude of the previous completed bar's percentage change from open exceeds the adaptive threshold, and a new timeframe bar has begun. The adaptive threshold is twice the cumulative mean of absolute percentage changes on new timeframe bars, divided by the chart bar index.
Long: Close crosses above the most recent swing pivot high OR close crosses above the most recent internal pivot high, subject to the internal confluence filter when enabled OR a bullish fair value gap forms.
Short: Close crosses below the most recent swing pivot low OR close crosses below the most recent internal pivot low, subject to the internal confluence filter when enabled OR a bearish fair value gap forms.
