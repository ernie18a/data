Calculation: Structure pivots = Maintain independent leg states for a shorter internal lookback, a longer swing lookback, and an equal-level lookback, initially bearish. At each bar, if the high lookback bars ago is greater than every high in the subsequent lookback bars, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low lookback bars ago is less than every low in the subsequent lookback bars, set it bullish. A change to bearish confirms a high pivot at the earlier bar; a change to bullish confirms a low pivot there. Store the latest and preceding pivot of each type and lookback. Each new pivot starts uncrossed.
Calculation: Structure break = For each of the internal and swing lookbacks, a bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest high pivot and that pivot is uncrossed; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest low pivot and that pivot is uncrossed. Mark the pivot crossed after a break. For internal breaks only, also require the internal pivot level to differ from the corresponding swing pivot level; if the optional confluence filter is enabled, require high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low) for a bullish break or high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low) for a bearish break. Maintain separate internal and swing biases, initially neutral, and set each to the direction of its latest break.
Calculation: Order block = On a structure break, select the first bar with the minimum parsed low for a bullish break or maximum parsed high for a bearish break, from the broken pivot bar inclusive to the break bar exclusive. Parsed high and low equal the bar's high and low, respectively, except that they are swapped when high - low is at least twice the selected volatility measure. The volatility measure is either ATR or cumulative true range divided by the zero-based chart bar index. Store the selected parsed high and low as the block boundaries; retain up to the most recent storage-limit blocks separately for internal and swing structures. Remove a bullish block when the selected mitigation source, either low or close, is below its parsed low; remove a bearish block when the selected source, either high or close, is above its parsed high.
Calculation: Gap values = On the first chart bar of a new selected timeframe period, use the previous selected-timeframe bar's open and close, the high and low two selected-timeframe bars back, and the current selected-timeframe bar's high and low. The selected timeframe may be higher, equal, or lower than the chart timeframe. The source uses lookahead: on historical bars the current higher-timeframe high and low are the final values of that still-forming period, exposed at its start; for a lower timeframe it selects the first intrabar. Gap delta = (previous close - previous open) / (previous open * 100). The optional gap threshold is twice the cumulative sum of absolute gap delta on selected-timeframe changes divided by the zero-based chart bar index; otherwise it is zero.
Long: Bullish internal or swing structure break.
Short: Bearish internal or swing structure break.
Long: Internal structure bias is bullish.
Short: Internal structure bias is neutral or bearish.
Long: A swing high pivot is confirmed AND its price is greater than the preceding swing high pivot.
Short: A swing high pivot is confirmed AND its price is less than or equal to the preceding swing high pivot.
Long: A swing low pivot is confirmed AND its price is greater than or equal to the preceding swing low pivot.
Short: A swing low pivot is confirmed AND its price is less than the preceding swing low pivot.
Long: An equal-level low pivot is confirmed AND the absolute difference from the preceding equal-level low pivot is less than a threshold times ATR.
Short: An equal-level high pivot is confirmed AND the absolute difference from the preceding equal-level high pivot is less than a threshold times ATR.
Long: The selected mitigation source is above the parsed high of an active bearish internal or swing order block.
Short: The selected mitigation source is below the parsed low of an active bullish internal or swing order block.
Long: A new selected-timeframe period begins AND its current low is above the high two selected-timeframe bars back AND the previous selected-timeframe close is above that high AND gap delta is above the gap threshold.
Short: A new selected-timeframe period begins AND its current high is below the low two selected-timeframe bars back AND the previous selected-timeframe close is below that low AND negative gap delta is above the gap threshold.
