Calculation: Naive Bayes fill probability = for each gap, use F1 = z-score of the change in cumulative volume delta over the CVD momentum lookback, where each bar’s delta is volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), or 0 when high = low; F2 = gap size / ATR; F3 = fraction of up to 30 preceding closes inside the gap. Estimate each feature’s Gaussian likelihood for fill and no-fill from online class samples, multiply the three likelihoods by the empirical class prior, and normalize to get P(fill). Initialize each feature’s statistics with n=1, sum=0, sum of squares=1, and each class count at 0. A gap is a fill sample on the first later bar whose range overlaps its zone; it is a no-fill sample when it expires unfilled. Drop the oldest active gap without training if the active-gap cap is exceeded. Show/use probability only after both class counts reach the warmup minimum.
