Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line.
Calculation: Volume-weighted MACD = shorter-lookback volume-weighted moving average of close minus longer-lookback volume-weighted moving average of close.
Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = the longer-lookback average of volume multiplied by the close's location within each bar, divided by the average volume over the same lookback.
Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta = cumulative up-volume minus down-volume within each anchor period, estimated from lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Up/down volume delta = up-volume minus down-volume, estimated from lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Positive divergence = for any calculated indicator series (MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, smoothed Stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, Money Flow Index, cumulative volume delta, or optional external series), compare the current endpoint with a prior confirmed pivot low within the configured pivot and bar search limits: regular if the indicator is higher and the selected price source is lower; hidden if the indicator is lower and the selected price source is higher. The endpoint is the preceding chart bar when confirmation is required, otherwise the current chart bar. The price source is close when close pivots are selected, otherwise low. Reject a candidate if any intervening indicator or close value falls below its straight line between endpoints.
Calculation: Negative divergence = for any calculated indicator series above, compare the current endpoint with a prior confirmed pivot high within the configured pivot and bar search limits: regular if the indicator is lower and the selected price source is higher; hidden if the indicator is higher and the selected price source is lower. The endpoint is the preceding chart bar when confirmation is required, otherwise the current chart bar. The price source is close when close pivots are selected, otherwise high. Reject a candidate if any intervening indicator or close value rises above its straight line between endpoints.
Long: At least the configured minimum number of divergences exists across all calculated series, AND at least one positive regular or positive hidden divergence exists among MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, smoothed Stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, Money Flow Index, or cumulative volume delta, AND (confirmation is disabled OR the divergence's indicator rises from the preceding bar OR close rises from the preceding bar).
Short: At least the configured minimum number of divergences exists across all calculated series, AND at least one negative regular or negative hidden divergence exists among MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, smoothed Stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, Money Flow Index, or cumulative volume delta, AND (confirmation is disabled OR the divergence's indicator falls from the preceding bar OR close falls from the preceding bar).
