Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low in the selected price source with the same configurable number of bars on each side; evaluate it when the right-hand bars have completed. The source is either close or low.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high in the selected price source with the same configurable number of bars on each side; evaluate it when the right-hand bars have completed. The source is either close or high.
Calculation: Divergence indicators = enabled MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI of close, smoothed stochastic of close using high and low, CCI of close, momentum of close, OBV, short-period VWMA of close minus long-period VWMA of close, Chaikin money flow, MFI of close, anchored cumulative volume delta, and per-bar volume delta. MACD uses a shorter fast EMA and a longer slow EMA. Cumulative volume delta and volume delta classify each completed lower-timeframe bar's volume as positive when its close exceeds its open, negative when its close is below its open, and otherwise by its close versus the preceding lower-timeframe close, carrying the preceding classification on a tie. Sum signed volume within the chart bar for volume delta; accumulate it from the start of the current higher-timeframe anchor period for cumulative volume delta, including the still-forming anchor period.
Calculation: Divergence candidates = for each enabled indicator and enabled regular or hidden type, compare an endpoint with each of the most recent configurable number of confirmed price pivots within a configurable maximum age and beyond a configurable minimum age; retain the first qualifying pivot for each indicator and type. The endpoint is the current chart bar when confirmation is disabled, otherwise the preceding chart bar. For a low-pivot candidate, every intermediate indicator value and close must be at or above its respective straight line between pivot and endpoint; for a high-pivot candidate, both must be at or below their respective lines. With confirmation enabled, a low-pivot candidate additionally requires current indicator > previous indicator OR current close > previous close; a high-pivot candidate requires current indicator < previous indicator OR current close < previous close.
Calculation: Divergence count = the number of qualifying indicator-and-type candidates across both directions, including candidates calculated from per-bar volume delta and an enabled data-derived external source.
Long: Confirmed pivot low.
Short: Confirmed pivot high.
Long: Divergence count >= configurable minimum AND at least one qualifying candidate among the indicators through cumulative volume delta has either endpoint low-source price < pivot low AND endpoint indicator > pivot indicator for regular divergence OR endpoint low-source price > pivot low AND endpoint indicator < pivot indicator for hidden divergence.
Short: Divergence count >= configurable minimum AND at least one qualifying candidate among the indicators through cumulative volume delta has either endpoint high-source price > pivot high AND endpoint indicator < pivot indicator for regular divergence OR endpoint high-source price < pivot high AND endpoint indicator > pivot indicator for hidden divergence.
