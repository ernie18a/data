Calculation: ALMA-based regime = Compute ALMA(close) and rolling standard deviation of close; form upper/lower bands at ALMA ± multiplier × standard deviation. Ratchet the upper band to the lower of its raw value and prior ratcheted value unless prior close exceeded the prior upper band; ratchet the lower band to the higher of its raw value and prior ratcheted value unless prior close fell below the prior lower band. Start bearish. From bearish, turn bullish when close > upper band; otherwise stay bearish. From bullish, turn bearish when close < lower band; otherwise stay bullish. Calculate on each signal’s timeframe; use the last completed bar for higher-timeframe signals and the current bar for same-timeframe signals.
Calculation: Current regime-run length = consecutive bars in the current regime; average regime-run length = mean lengths of completed runs of that same regime over its rolling lookback. Calculate on each included timeframe’s native bars; use the last completed bar for higher-timeframe values and the current bar for same-timeframe values.
Long: On a signal timeframe, close > upper band when the prior regime is bearish OR close >= lower band when the prior regime is bullish; OR at least the configured minimum number of included timeframes are bullish and their current run lengths exceed their respective configured multipliers × average bullish-run lengths. For the overheat condition, use the prior bar’s timeframe values and require a bearish-to-bullish chart-regime flip if reversal-only mode is enabled.
Short: On a signal timeframe, close < lower band when the prior regime is bullish OR close <= upper band when the prior regime is bearish; OR at least the configured minimum number of included timeframes are bearish and their current run lengths exceed their respective configured multipliers × average bearish-run lengths. For the overheat condition, use the prior bar’s timeframe values and require a bullish-to-bearish chart-regime flip if reversal-only mode is enabled.
