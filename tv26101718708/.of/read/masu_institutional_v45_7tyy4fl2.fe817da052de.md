Calculation: Ribbon up count = number of adjacent pairs among moving averages of close with successively longer lookbacks for which the shorter-lookback average is greater; use the same selectable EMA, SMA, or HMA type for all pairs. Ribbon down count is defined similarly with less than.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of volume times (high + low + close) / 3 divided by cumulative volume, reset at each new day; use close when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bars where close > open minus volume on bars where close < open.
Calculation: Buy-volume ratio = volume on the current bar if close > open, otherwise zero, divided by the greater of a unit volume and volume on the current bar if close < open, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a high or low that is the respective extreme over equal numbers of preceding and subsequent bars; it becomes available only when the subsequent bars have completed. Use the most recently confirmed swing value.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = count of the following satisfied conditions: the corresponding higher-timeframe filter is disabled or satisfied; the corresponding ribbon filter is disabled or satisfied; order flow is disabled or close is on the corresponding side of session VWAP; smart money is disabled or the corresponding order-block, fair-value-gap, liquidity-sweep, or structure-break condition below is satisfied; ADX > its threshold.
Long: (ADX > threshold AND short-lookback EMA of close crosses above longer-lookback EMA of close AND close > a still longer-lookback EMA of close AND volume > its SMA times a multiplier) OR (close > the highest high over a lookback ending on the previous bar AND volume > its SMA times a multiplier AND close > the long-lookback EMA of close) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above that threshold); AND the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR the higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA > its longer-lookback EMA AND higher-timeframe close > the short-lookback EMA; AND the ribbon filter is disabled OR ribbon up count >= alignment threshold minus one; AND order flow is disabled OR close > session VWAP; AND smart money is disabled OR any of the bullish order-block, fair-value-gap, low-liquidity-sweep, or bullish structure-break conditions below is satisfied; AND the session filter is disabled OR the bar time is within the configured session. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar for realtime bars.
Short: (ADX > threshold AND short-lookback EMA of close crosses below longer-lookback EMA of close AND close < a still longer-lookback EMA of close AND volume > its SMA times a multiplier) OR (close < the lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous bar AND volume > its SMA times a multiplier AND close < the long-lookback EMA of close) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below that threshold); AND the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR the higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA < its longer-lookback EMA AND higher-timeframe close < the short-lookback EMA; AND the ribbon filter is disabled OR ribbon down count >= alignment threshold minus one; AND order flow is disabled OR close < session VWAP; AND smart money is disabled OR any of the bearish order-block, fair-value-gap, high-liquidity-sweep, or bearish structure-break conditions below is satisfied; AND the session filter is disabled OR the bar time is within the configured session. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar for realtime bars.
Long: close > open AND previous close < previous open AND high - close < a fraction of high - low.
Short: close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close - low < a fraction of high - low.
Long: close - open > ATR times an impulse multiplier AND close > open AND previous close < previous open.
Short: open - close > ATR times an impulse multiplier AND close < open AND previous close > previous open.
Long: low > high two bars ago AND low - high two bars ago > ATR times a gap multiplier.
Short: high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago - high > ATR times a gap multiplier.
Long: low < the lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > open AND close > that lowest low.
Short: high > the highest high over a lookback ending on the previous bar AND close < open AND close < that highest high.
Long: close > the latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high.
Short: close < the latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low.
Long: ribbon up count >= alignment threshold minus one.
Short: ribbon up count < alignment threshold minus one AND ribbon down count >= alignment threshold minus one.
Long: order flow is enabled AND (cumulative volume delta > its SMA AND close < previous close AND cumulative volume delta > its previous value OR buy-volume ratio > one plus a threshold) AND close > session VWAP.
Short: order flow is enabled AND the preceding order-flow long condition is false AND (cumulative volume delta < its SMA AND close > previous close AND cumulative volume delta < its previous value OR buy-volume ratio < one minus a threshold) AND close < session VWAP.
Long: bullish directional confluence score > bearish directional confluence score.
Short: bearish directional confluence score > bullish directional confluence score.
Long: RSI > an upper threshold AND close > open.
Short: RSI < a lower threshold AND close < open.
Long: the sum of volume on up-close bars over a lookback divided by the sum of volume on up-close and down-close bars over that lookback > a threshold.
Short: the sum of volume on up-close bars over a lookback divided by the sum of volume on up-close and down-close bars over that lookback < a lower threshold.
Long: on a selected other timeframe, the absolute difference between the short- and long-lookback EMAs of close >= ATR times a multiplier AND the short-lookback EMA > the long-lookback EMA. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in realtime, and for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar available within each chart bar.
Short: on a selected other timeframe, the absolute difference between the short- and long-lookback EMAs of close >= ATR times a multiplier AND the short-lookback EMA <= the long-lookback EMA. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in realtime, and for a lower timeframe use the last intrabar available within each chart bar.
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times the stop multiplier OR trailing is disabled AND high >= signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times the profit multiplier OR trailing is enabled AND high since entry >= entry price plus the signal-bar ATR trailing distance expressed in ticks AND low <= the highest price since trailing activation minus that same distance expressed in ticks.
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times the stop multiplier OR trailing is disabled AND low <= signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times the profit multiplier OR trailing is enabled AND low since entry <= entry price minus the signal-bar ATR trailing distance expressed in ticks AND high >= the lowest price since trailing activation plus that same distance expressed in ticks.
