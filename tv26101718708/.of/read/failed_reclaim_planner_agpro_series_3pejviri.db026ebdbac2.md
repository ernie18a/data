Calculation: Candle geometry = range is max(high - low, a positive price-resolution parameter); close location is (close - low) / range; body share is abs(close - open) / range; upper wick share is (high - max(open, close)) / range; lower wick share is (min(open, close) - low) / range.
Calculation: ATR = average true range, using high - low until available and a positive price-resolution parameter as its minimum. Shelf half-width is max(ATR multiplied by a width parameter, the price-resolution parameter). Break padding and failure padding are ATR multiplied by separate parameters; failure padding may be negative.
Calculation: Active shelf = maintain the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low using configurable left and right bars. On each bar, first clear an active bearish shelf if close > shelf + current shelf half-width, or an active bullish shelf if close < shelf - current shelf half-width; clear either shelf if its age exceeds the retest window. Then activate a bearish shelf at the latest pivot low if close < pivot low - break padding AND close location <= a lower-half threshold AND the active shelf is not bearish. Activate a bullish shelf at the latest pivot high if close > pivot high + break padding AND close location >= a corresponding upper-half threshold AND the active shelf is not bullish. The pivot bar must precede the current bar. A new shelf replaces the previous shelf; if both activate on the same bar, choose bearish. Shelf age is bars since activation.
Calculation: Failure score = clamp each component and the final weighted sum to the score range. Depth is the fraction of the full current shelf width penetrated by the retest, multiplied by the score range. Close rejection is a baseline plus the distance from close to shelf in the continuation direction divided by ATR and multiplied by a coefficient, plus a bonus when close location is beyond a more extreme threshold in the continuation direction. Wick quality is the relevant wick share multiplied by a coefficient. Volume participation is a baseline plus a coefficient multiplied by (volume / max(volume SMA, a positive volume floor) - 1); use current volume when the SMA is unavailable, and use a ratio of one when volume is zero. Follow-through is a weighted sum of directional close location, body share, and a bonus if close moves in the continuation direction from the previous close. Room is the distance from close to the lowest prior low for bearish setups or highest prior high for bullish setups over a lookback excluding the current bar, provided that extreme is in the continuation direction; otherwise use an ATR-multiple fallback distance. Room score is room divided by the fallback distance, multiplied by the score range. Combine depth, close rejection, wick quality, volume participation, room score, and follow-through with configurable weights: close rejection has the largest weight, followed by wick quality, depth, volume participation, and equal smaller weights for room and follow-through.
