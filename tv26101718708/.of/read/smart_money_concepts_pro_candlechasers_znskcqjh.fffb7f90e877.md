Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest over equal configurable numbers of bars on each side; recognize it only after the right-side bars close. Run independent structure engines with a longer swing lookback and a shorter internal lookback, and with the swing lookback on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar. Apply higher-timeframe events on the first following chart bar close.
Calculation: Structure engine = retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, their broken flags, directional bias and protected opposite pivot. Before incorporating pivots confirmed on the current bar, an upward break occurs when close exceeds the latest unbroken pivot high, or, while bearish, exceeds the protected high; a downward break occurs when close is below the latest unbroken pivot low, or, while bullish, below the protected low. Process an upward break first if both occur. An opposite-bias break is a change of character; another break is a continuation. On an upward break set bullish bias, mark the high broken and protect the latest confirmed low; on a downward break set bearish bias, mark the low broken and protect the latest confirmed high. Then incorporate newly confirmed pivots, marking a new high broken if close exceeds it and a new low broken if close is below it; if the relevant protected level is absent, initialize it from the newly confirmed opposite pivot.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = retain confirmed swing pivot highs and lows only if the confirmation bar has not breached them. Optionally add completed selected-session highs and lows after their completion bar, provided that bar has not breached the level; session extrema use observed chart bars fully contained in the selected timezone-aware session. Merge a new pool with the newest same-side, same-source pool within a configurable multiple of current ATR, retaining the higher high or lower low and resetting its age. Remove the oldest pool when capacity is exceeded. On each chart bar, before adding new pools, remove any pool whose level is breached by the wick or whose age reaches its configurable limit.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below an existing low pool AND close above that pool. Bearish sweep = high above an existing high pool AND close below that pool.
Calculation: Displacement = absolute candle body at least a configurable multiple of prior-bar ATR AND body divided by candle range at least a configurable threshold; require a positive range. Direction is bullish when close > open and bearish when close < open.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars earlier AND the gap width is at least a configurable multiple of prior-bar ATR AND the intervening candle closes above its open; its zone extends from that earlier high to current low. Bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars earlier AND the gap width is at least the same configurable multiple of prior-bar ATR AND the intervening candle closes below its open; its zone extends from current high to that earlier low. Detect gaps on chart bars and optionally on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, importing them at the first following chart bar close.
Calculation: Order block = on a swing structure break with same-direction displacement, optionally require a same-direction fair value gap within a configurable recent window and after the origin candle. Find the nearest opposite-direction candle within a configurable search window; its wick range or body range, as configured, is the zone. Require positive zone width. Walking subsequent bars up to but excluding the break, reject the zone if any wick crosses its distal edge or if any bar overlaps it after a prior close beyond its near edge. On the break bar, reject it if it overlaps after an earlier departure; require the close beyond the near edge and the wick not beyond the distal edge. Detect on chart bars and optionally on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, importing at the first following chart bar close.
Calculation: Active zones = enabled fair value gaps and order blocks, deduplicated by origin, kind, direction and source timeframe. Add newly detected zones after evaluating existing zones on the chart bar; retire the oldest on exceeding configurable capacity. On each later chart bar count a distinct encounter when overlap starts after a non-overlapping bar, and allow at most one setup per encounter. Before qualification exclude expired zones and invalid order blocks: invalidation is optionally a distal-edge wick breach or a close beyond the distal edge; for higher-timeframe order blocks, close invalidation uses only the last completed higher-timeframe close when a new higher-timeframe bar is imported, while wick invalidation uses chart bars. After qualification, update maximum penetration from chart-bar wicks and retire zones at the configured first touch, midpoint fill or full fill, or on invalidation or expiry. Chart-zone age is measured in chart bars and higher-timeframe-zone age in completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = an active bullish zone predates the current chart bar AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND close > zone top AND close > open AND low >= zone bottom AND the zone is not invalid or expired AND this encounter has not signaled AND its encounter count is within the configured first-only or multiple-attempt limit. Bearish rejection = an active bearish zone predates the current chart bar AND low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND close < open AND high <= zone top AND the zone is not invalid or expired AND this encounter has not signaled AND its encounter count is within the configured first-only or multiple-attempt limit.
Calculation: Directional sequence = a same-direction sweep followed on a later bar by a same-direction break from the selected swing or internal structure engine, optionally restricted to a change of character, followed on that break bar or later by same-direction displacement. Reset on an opposite sweep or opposite selected-engine break, expiry after a configurable number of chart bars from the sweep, or a qualified setup; a new same-direction sweep restarts the sequence unless an opposite sweep also occurs. The setup bar must follow the displacement bar and its zone detection time must be at or after the break time.
