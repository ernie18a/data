Calculation: Candle centroid = low + (high - low) × (0.5 + clamp(0.3 × (2 × close position - 1) + 0.15 × wick asymmetry + 0.05 × candle direction, -0.45, 0.45)), where close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low; wick asymmetry = (lower wick - upper wick) / (lower wick + upper wick), or 0 when their sum is zero; and candle direction = (close - open) / max(abs(close - open), 0.00000001).
Calculation: Dynamic Gaussian average = the weighted mean of a series over the available lookback, with newest observation at index 0, weight exp(-(i - m)² / (2s²)), m = (1 - offset) × (length - 1), and s = length / sigma. Effective length is limited by available observations.
Calculation: Statistical baseline and deviation = the dynamic Gaussian average and corresponding population standard deviation of candle centroid, additionally weighting each observation by its volume when cumulative volume is positive. Use volume 1 for missing volume; if the volume-weight sum is zero, use the current centroid as baseline. For the deviation, divide the weighted squared deviations by max(weighted volume sum, 1) when volume is available, otherwise by the Gaussian weight sum. If the deviation is zero or unavailable, use the ordinary population standard deviation of centroid over the effective lookback. Use the previous bar's normalized volatility for offset = 0.5 + 0.5 × normalized volatility and sigma = 1 + 2 × normalized volatility, initializing normalized volatility to 0.5. Calculate a long-lookback baseline and deviation and a shorter-lookback baseline, with the shorter lookback equal to the rounded square root of the long lookback, subject to a minimum lookback.
Calculation: Normalized volatility = sigmoid((combined volatility - volatility mean) / volatility standard deviation), using zero for the quotient when the standard deviation is unavailable or zero. Combined volatility = 100 × long-lookback deviation / long-lookback baseline, multiplied by current volume / max(Gaussian-weighted mean volume, 1) when volume is available; use zero when the baseline is unavailable or zero. The volatility standard deviation is the population standard deviation of combined volatility over a fixed lookback. Once that lookback is available, volatility mean is its Gaussian-weighted mean, with Gaussian center determined by half the long baseline lookback; before then, use the dynamic Gaussian average of all available centroids.
Calculation: Adaptive reversal threshold and backstep = long-lookback deviation × threshold multiplier, and rounded maximum backstep - probability × (maximum backstep - minimum backstep), respectively, clamped to at least one bar. Probability = sigmoid(a × (normalized volatility - b)); a is a bounded linear function of normalized volatility × min(1, abs(volatility skew)), and b is a bounded decreasing linear function of volatility skew. Volatility skew is the mean cubed difference of combined volatility from volatility mean, divided by volatility standard deviation cubed; it is zero before the volatility lookback is available and when autotuning is disabled. With autotuning enabled, the threshold multiplier is probability and the long baseline lookback is proportional to maximum backstep; otherwise use the configured threshold multiplier and baseline lookback. When the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, scale the baseline lookback and both backstep bounds by the higher-to-chart timeframe duration ratio.
Calculation: Pivot state = initialize pivot price to the first close, pivot bar to the first bar, and direction to zero. On each bar use the pivot price and bar from before updating the state. If direction is zero, start an up leg at the current high when high > pivot price + reversal threshold and bars since pivot >= backstep and the optional higher-timeframe up filter passes; otherwise start a down leg at the current low when low < pivot price - reversal threshold and bars since pivot >= backstep and the optional higher-timeframe down filter passes. During an up leg, first replace the pivot with the current high and bar if high > pivot price; otherwise start a down leg under the same down condition. During a down leg, first replace the pivot with the current low and bar if low < pivot price; otherwise start an up leg under the same up condition. A new leg saves the preceding pivot; an extension replaces the current pivot. For calculations on the current bar, use the pivot price and bar captured before this update and the preceding pivot after the update.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe up filter = shorter-lookback dynamic Gaussian average of higher-timeframe candle centroid > longer-lookback dynamic Gaussian average of higher-timeframe candle centroid. The down filter reverses the comparison. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Buyer percentage = round(100 × accumulated buyer power / max(0.001, accumulated buyer power + accumulated seller power)); seller percentage = 100 - buyer percentage. For each bar, previous close defaults to open; effective high = max(high, previous close), effective low = max(min(low, previous close), 0.00000001), overnight variance = (100 × ln(open / previous close))², Garman-Klass variance = 0.5 × (100 × ln(effective high / effective low))² - (2 × ln(2) - 1) × (100 × ln(close / open))², and Rogers-Satchell variance = (100 × ln(effective high / close)) × (100 × ln(effective high / open)) + (100 × ln(effective low / close)) × (100 × ln(effective low / open)). When open is nonpositive, the close/open and effective-low/open log terms are zero. Hybrid volatility = max(0.01, sqrt(max(0, 0.5 × overnight variance + 0.25 × (max(Garman-Klass variance, 0) + max(Rogers-Satchell variance, 0))))). Direction weight = standard normal CDF of 100 × ln(close / ((open + previous close) / 2)) / hybrid volatility. Buyer force = close position × direction weight divided by the sum of that quantity and (1 - close position) × (1 - direction weight), or 0.5 if the sum is zero. Leg speed = absolute percentage change from the preceding pivot to the captured current pivot divided by max(1, their bar distance), with percentage change zero when the preceding pivot price is zero or unavailable. Candle weight = 10 × hybrid volatility × max(0.1, 1 + leg speed / max(0.0000000001, dynamic Gaussian average of centroid over a separate lookback)) × clamp(current volume / max(1, ALMA of volume over a normalization lookback), 0.2, 5); use 1 for the volume ratio when cumulative volume is not positive. Add buyer force × candle weight and (1 - buyer force) × candle weight to accumulated buyer and seller power respectively; reset both accumulators to those current-bar values when a new leg starts or the captured pivot bar is the current bar.
Long: Buyer percentage > 50 AND shorter-lookback baseline > its previous-bar value AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe up filter passes).
Short: Seller percentage > 50 AND shorter-lookback baseline < its previous-bar value AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe down filter passes).
