Calculation: opening-range high = highest high during the configured opening-range interval for the current day
Calculation: opening-range low = lowest low during the configured opening-range interval for the current day
Calculation: buffer = configured buffer distance in price units
Long: opening-range interval has ended AND close > opening-range high AND close <= opening-range high + buffer
Short: opening-range interval has ended AND close < opening-range low AND close >= opening-range low - buffer
