Calculation: Higher-timeframe levels = the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on each chart bar use the levels available on that bar.
Calculation: Break state = reset at the start of each higher-timeframe period; a close crossing above the higher-timeframe high activates bullish break state, and a close crossing below the higher-timeframe low activates bearish break state. Either break replaces the previous state and clears any active gap and touch. A same-direction gap invalidation or confirmed entry clears the break state. Evaluate the break before gap formation on each bar.
Calculation: Active gap = after a break, retain the first same-direction gap formed on a later chart bar while no gap is active. A bullish gap has top equal to its formation bar's low and bottom equal to the high two chart bars earlier; a bearish gap has top equal to the low two chart bars earlier and bottom equal to its formation bar's high. Reset the gap at a new higher-timeframe period or any new break. Invalidate a bullish gap when close < its bottom, and a bearish gap when close > its top; invalidation also clears its touch and break state. Evaluate invalidation before touch and entry.
Calculation: Gap touch = the first bar after gap formation with low <= gap top AND high >= gap bottom AND body-to-range ratio < threshold AND, for a bullish gap, close <= gap top OR, for a bearish gap, close >= gap bottom. Body-to-range ratio is abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 1 when high = low. Retain the touch close until the gap is cleared; a confirmed entry clears the gap, touch and break state.
Long: close crosses above the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high.
Short: close crosses below the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low.
Long: bullish break state is active AND the current chart bar is later than the break bar AND low > high two chart bars earlier.
Short: bearish break state is active AND the current chart bar is later than the break bar AND high < low two chart bars earlier.
Long: a bullish gap and its touch are active AND the current chart bar is later than the touch bar AND close >= touch close + confirmation price offset.
Short: a bearish gap and its touch are active AND the current chart bar is later than the touch bar AND close <= touch close - confirmation price offset.
