Calculation: Trend bias = sign(EMA of close with shorter lookback minus EMA of close with longer lookback).
Calculation: Pivot = a high or low confirmed only after an equal number of bars on each side; retain the latest pivots in confirmation order, appending a high before a low if both are confirmed on the same bar. Use the latest required pivots even if their types do not alternate.
Calculation: Pattern score = weighted mean of shape, symmetry, volume, time and trend scores. Volume score is a neutral constant for patterns shorter than a minimum duration; otherwise it is the clipped linear decreasing function of average volume in the late part divided by average volume in the early part, splitting the bars from the first pivot inclusive to the last pivot exclusive into halves. Time score is piecewise linear: low and constant below a first duration, rising to a peak at a second duration, falling slightly to a third duration, then falling to a floor. Trend score depends on trend bias: for head-and-shoulders and double-top use the bullish trend score; for their inverse forms use the bearish trend score; for other patterns use the neutral-pattern score. A directional trend score is highest when bias agrees, intermediate when neutral, and lowest when opposed; the neutral-pattern score is higher for nonzero bias than for zero bias.
Calculation: Pattern candidate = evaluate on a confirmed bar with a new pivot and more than a minimum number of bars since the last accepted candidate. Try enabled patterns in this order and accept only the first with score at least the threshold: head-and-shoulders, inverse head-and-shoulders, double top, double bottom, triangle or wedge or rectangle, flag. ATR and all scores use the detection bar. The configured minimum pivot count and maximum pattern duration do not restrict detection.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders candidate = latest five pivots high-low-high-low-high; the middle high exceeds both outer highs by an ATR-based margin, and the absolute differences between outer highs and between the two lows are each below an ATR-based tolerance. Shape and symmetry scores are respectively one minus each difference divided by its tolerance, floored at zero. Direction is short, neckline is the average of the two lows, and stop is the middle high plus an ATR-based margin. Inverse head-and-shoulders reverses highs, lows and inequalities; its direction is long and stop is the middle low minus an ATR-based margin.
Calculation: Double-top candidate = latest three pivots high-low-high, with outer-high difference below an ATR-based tolerance and pivot span at least a minimum duration. Shape score is one minus that difference divided by its tolerance, floored at zero; symmetry score is constant. Direction is short, neckline is the middle low, and stop is the higher high plus an ATR-based margin. Double bottom reverses highs and lows; its direction is long and stop is the lower low minus an ATR-based margin.
Calculation: Triangle, wedge or rectangle candidate = latest four pivots alternate high-low-high-low or low-high-low-high. Calculate the slopes connecting the two highs and the two lows, each divided by detection-bar ATR. In order: a falling high slope and rising low slope beyond a flatness threshold gives a symmetric triangle, directed long when trend bias is nonnegative and short otherwise; a flat high slope and rising low slope gives a long ascending triangle; a falling high slope and flat low slope gives a short descending triangle; two rising slopes with the low slope greater than a factor times the high slope give a short rising wedge; two falling slopes with the high slope less than a factor times the low slope give a long falling wedge; two flat slopes give a rectangle directed long when trend bias is nonnegative and short otherwise. Flat means absolute slope below the flatness threshold; rising and falling mean above the positive threshold and below its negative respectively. Extrapolate both pivot lines to the detection bar. The neckline is the upper line for long and lower line for short; the stop is the opposite line minus an ATR-based margin for long or plus one for short. Convergence is one minus the extrapolated line separation divided by the range of the four pivot prices. Shape score is constant for a rectangle and otherwise convergence plus a constant, clipped to a floor and ceiling. Symmetry score is one minus the absolute difference between the absolute normalized slopes, clipped to zero and one; if either slope is zero, use a constant instead.
Calculation: Flag candidate = use the latest four pivots. Pole start is the close a lookback before the first pivot, bounded by the start of the data; pole end is the average price of the first two pivots. Absolute pole length divided by ATR must exceed a threshold; the range of the four pivot prices divided by ATR must be less than a factor times normalized pole length; and the pivot span must not exceed a duration limit. Shape score is one minus that range divided by the allowed range, clipped to a floor and ceiling; symmetry score is constant. Direction is long when pole end exceeds pole start, otherwise short. Neckline is the highest pivot for long and lowest pivot for short; stop is the lowest pivot minus an ATR-based margin for long and the highest pivot plus an ATR-based margin for short.
Calculation: Active pattern = an accepted candidate retained in the most recent bounded pattern buffer, until its breakout or breakdown condition is met or, otherwise, close crosses its fixed stop. Evaluate the candidate on its detection bar as well; test breakout or breakdown before failure. Freeze neckline and stop at detection, but use current-bar ATR for breakout confirmation.
Long: Trend bias > 0.
Short: Trend bias < 0.
Long: RSI of close <= oversold threshold.
Short: RSI of close >= overbought threshold.
Long: An accepted pattern candidate has long direction.
Short: An accepted pattern candidate has short direction.
Long: An active long-direction pattern AND close > its neckline + current ATR times the confirmation factor.
Short: An active short-direction pattern AND close < its neckline - current ATR times the confirmation factor.
