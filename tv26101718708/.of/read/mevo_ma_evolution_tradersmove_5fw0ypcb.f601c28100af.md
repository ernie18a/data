Calculation: Moving average = selectable EMA, SMA, RMA, DEMA, TMA, WMA, VWMA, ZLEMA, TEMA, HMA, EHMA, THMA, T3, or Heikin Ashi EMA of close; Heikin Ashi EMA uses Heikin Ashi open instead of close. EHMA(n) = EMA(2 × EMA(close, n/2) − EMA(close, n), round(sqrt(n))); THMA(n) = WMA(3 × WMA(close, n/3) − WMA(close, n/2) − WMA(close, n), n); Heikin Ashi EMA = EMA of Heikin Ashi open, where Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close)/4 and Heikin Ashi open = (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open + close)/2.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe line = either a chart-timeframe moving average with its length scaled by the higher-to-chart timeframe ratio, or the moving average of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, optionally smoothed by a chart-timeframe EMA whose length is the timeframe ratio. For scaled EMA, RMA, TEMA, DEMA, ZLEMA, Heikin Ashi EMA, and T3, use the alpha-equivalent length round((r + 1)/(1 − r)), where r = ((n − 1)/(n + 1))^(1/ratio); for other types use round(n × ratio).
Calculation: Directional source = a short-lookback or long-lookback cloud moving average, a momentum or longer-lookback trend moving average, a higher-timeframe copy of a cloud or momentum moving average, or session VWAP of (high + low + close)/3. Momentum and trend may use the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. Higher-timeframe momentum and trend slope compares moving averages on the last completed higher-timeframe bar and its predecessor, regardless of the higher-timeframe line mode; other line slopes compare consecutive chart-bar line values.
Calculation: Cloud = the short-lookback and long-lookback moving averages, either both on the chart timeframe or both using the higher-timeframe line calculation.
Long: Close > open.
Short: Close < open.
Long: A directional source > its previous value OR close > that source.
Short: A directional source < its previous value OR close < that source.
Long: Both cloud lines > their respective previous values OR the short-lookback cloud line > the long-lookback cloud line OR close > both cloud lines.
Short: Both cloud lines < their respective previous values OR the short-lookback cloud line < the long-lookback cloud line OR close < both cloud lines.
Long: At least one selected directional source exists AND every selected directional source satisfies its individually selected condition: source > its previous value OR close > source.
Short: At least one selected directional source exists AND every selected directional source satisfies its individually selected condition: source < its previous value OR close < source.
