Calculation: Lower-timeframe effort = maximum of absolute body, low-to-close, and high-to-close distances on a completed lower-timeframe bar; ties select body, then low-to-close, then high-to-close. Body effort is bullish when close exceeds open, bearish when it is lower; for a doji use the close versus the previous lower-timeframe close. Low-to-close effort is bullish; high-to-close effort is bearish.
Calculation: Weighted-average volume = sum of prior lower-timeframe volume × effort divided by sum of prior effort. Compare against prior lower-timeframe bars over a rolling lookback.
Calculation: Effort zone = for bullish body effort, [open, open + effort/2]; for bullish low-to-close effort, [low, max(open, low + effort/2)]; for bearish body effort, [open − effort/2, open]; for bearish high-to-close effort, [min(open, high − effort/2), high].
Calculation: Cumulative volume-weighted delta = rolling sum of (volume × effort) for bullish effort and its negative for bearish effort, using completed lower-timeframe bars.
Long: Cumulative volume-weighted delta > 0 OR a bullish bubble occurs when volume > prior maximum volume AND effort > prior maximum effort, OR effort > prior maximum effort AND volume > weighted-average volume × threshold, OR volume > weighted-average volume × threshold AND effort >= prior maximum effort × threshold, OR (volume > prior maximum volume OR volume >= weighted-average volume × threshold) AND effort <= prior mean effort × threshold AND (a prior ranked event exists OR volume > prior maximum volume × threshold) OR an active bearish effort zone is trapped.
Short: Cumulative volume-weighted delta <= 0 OR a bearish bubble occurs under the same volume and effort ranking conditions as a bullish bubble, with bearish effort, OR an active bullish effort zone is trapped.
