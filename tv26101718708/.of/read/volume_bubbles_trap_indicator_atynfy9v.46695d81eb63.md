Calculation: Lower-timeframe effort record = For each completed lower-timeframe bar in chronological order, let body = abs(open - close), lower range = abs(low - close), upper range = abs(high - close), and effort = max(body, lower range, upper range). Process the bar only if close != open or close != previous lower-timeframe close. Resolve equal maxima in body, lower range, upper range order. A body record is bullish if close > open or if close = open and close > previous close; otherwise it is bearish. A lower-range record is bullish and an upper-range record is bearish. For a bullish body, zone bottom = open and zone top = open + effort × zone fraction; for a bearish body, zone top = open and zone bottom = open - effort × zone fraction. For a lower-range record, zone bottom = low and zone top = max(open, low + effort × zone fraction). For an upper-range record, zone top = high and zone bottom = min(open, high - effort × zone fraction).
Calculation: Effort history = A rolling window of processed lower-timeframe effort records, initially filled with zero volume and zero effort. Evaluate each new record against the preceding window before inserting it. Weighted volume = sum(volume × effort × volume scale) / sum(effort). Maximum volume, maximum effort, and average effort are taken over the preceding window. Begin classifying records after the window has filled.
Calculation: Effort classification = Evaluate these cases in order and take the first match: major if volume > maximum volume AND effort > maximum effort; moderate if effort > maximum effort AND volume > weighted volume × impact volume factor; minor if volume > weighted volume × impact volume factor AND effort >= maximum effort × moderate effort fraction; absorption if (volume > maximum volume OR volume >= weighted volume × absorption volume factor) AND effort <= average effort × low effort fraction AND (the preceding processed record was major, moderate, or minor OR volume > maximum volume × extreme volume factor); exhaustion if volume < weighted volume × exhaustion volume fraction AND effort > average effort. Otherwise the record is unclassified. The preceding-record state is reset by absorption, exhaustion, and unclassified records.
Calculation: Active zone = A zone is created on the chart bar containing a major, moderate, or minor record, or an absorption record with volume >= maximum volume × extreme volume factor. Its direction, boundaries, and effort are those of that record. It is eligible for a first trap after its creation bar through the end of a configured chart-bar window. Once trapped, it remains trapped; while still within that window, it can also mark a recovery. Retain only the configured maximum number of most recently created zones.
Calculation: Net volume effort = Sum of volume × effort × volume scale over the current effort-history window, positive for bullish records and negative for bearish records.
Long: A bullish lower-timeframe effort record is classified major, moderate, or minor on the current completed chart bar.
Short: A bearish lower-timeframe effort record is classified major, moderate, or minor on the current completed chart bar.
Long: Net volume effort > 0.
Short: Net volume effort <= 0.
Long: An eligible bearish zone is not yet trapped AND chart close >= zone top + zone effort.
Short: An eligible bullish zone is not yet trapped AND chart close <= zone bottom - zone effort.
Long: An eligible bullish zone is already trapped AND chart close > zone top + zone effort.
Short: An eligible bearish zone is already trapped AND chart close < zone bottom - zone effort.
