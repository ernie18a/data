Calculation: Timeframe EMA = EMA of close on each configured timeframe; use the last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, and the last intrabar within the chart bar when it is lower.
Calculation: Composite signal = simple average of the four timeframe EMAs in Simple mode; their weighted average in Weighted mode; in Consensus mode, their simple average when at least three EMA slopes agree in sign, otherwise their weighted average. EMA slopes compare each EMA with its previous bar on its own timeframe.
Calculation: Brick size = ATR on the configured brick-size timeframe multiplied by a factor, or a fixed price amount.
Calculation: Renko level and direction = initialize the level to the composite signal and direction to 0. Thereafter, if signal minus level is at least two brick sizes, raise the level by the whole-number count of brick sizes in that difference and set direction to +1; if the difference is at most minus one brick size, lower the level by the whole-number count of brick sizes in its absolute value and set direction to -1; otherwise retain both. A direction change to +1 or -1 is a signal.
Calculation: Long stop = close on the first long entry signal multiplied by (1 − stop-loss fraction); short stop = close on the first short entry signal multiplied by (1 + stop-loss fraction). These stops remain fixed until reset.
Calculation: Long trailing level = highest high since entering the long position multiplied by (1 − trailing offset); short trailing level = lowest low since entering the short position multiplied by (1 + trailing offset).
Long: Renko direction changes to +1 AND position is flat or long.
Short: Renko direction changes to -1 AND position is flat or short.
Exit: Long, Renko direction changes to -1 OR close <= long stop OR trailing is enabled and activated and close <= long trailing level OR trailing is disabled and close >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction).
Exit: Short, Renko direction changes to +1 OR close >= short stop OR trailing is enabled and activated and close >= short trailing level OR trailing is disabled and close <= average entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction).
