Calculation: Stochastic RSI %K = 100 × (chart-timeframe RSI − lowest chart-timeframe RSI over the lookback) ÷ (highest chart-timeframe RSI over the lookback − lowest chart-timeframe RSI over the lookback)
Calculation: Stochastic RSI %D = short-term simple moving average of %K
Long: chart close > EMA on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar AND RSI on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar < pullback threshold AND (%K crosses above %D AND %K < oversold threshold) OR (long position AND chart low <= average entry price − ATR × first DCA distance) OR (long position AND chart low <= average entry price − ATR × second DCA distance)
Short: chart close < EMA on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar AND RSI on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar > rally threshold AND (%K crosses below %D AND %K > overbought threshold) OR (short position AND chart high >= average entry price + ATR × first DCA distance) OR (short position AND chart high >= average entry price + ATR × second DCA distance)
Exit: long, chart low <= average entry price − ATR × stop distance OR chart high >= average entry price + ATR × partial profit distance OR chart low <= ATR-based trailing stop following the highest price since entry
Exit: short, chart high >= average entry price + ATR × stop distance OR chart low <= average entry price − ATR × partial profit distance OR chart high >= ATR-based trailing stop following the lowest price since entry
