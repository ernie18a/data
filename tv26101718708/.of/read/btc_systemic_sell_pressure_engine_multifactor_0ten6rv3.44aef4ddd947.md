Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Synthetic delta = volume × (close position × bullish-candle multiplier − (1 − close position) × bearish-candle multiplier); apply each multiplier only to its corresponding candle direction and otherwise use 1.
Calculation: High effort = volume z-score ≥ anomaly threshold OR close × volume z-score ≥ anomaly threshold OR z-score of max(−synthetic delta, 0) ≥ anomaly threshold.
Calculation: Band width = (upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band, or zero when the middle band is zero.
Short: High effort AND ((absolute close-to-previous-close change / ATR ≤ weak-progress threshold AND (close < open OR close position < lower-half threshold) AND close ≥ current rolling highest high × near-high fraction) OR ((high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ upper-wick threshold AND close position < upper-close threshold) OR (high ≥ previous rolling highest high AND close < high − (high − low) × rejection fraction) OR (high > previous rolling highest high over a sweep lookback AND close < that previous rolling highest high AND close position < lower-half threshold)).
Short: Previous bar's band width ≤ the previous bar's preceding rolling minimum band width × compression factor AND close < previous rolling lowest low AND close < open AND ATR / SMA of ATR ≥ expansion threshold; the ATR baseline uses a longer lookback than the ATR.
