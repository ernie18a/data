Calculation: Delta line = For each chart bar, sum the volume of its completed lower-timeframe bars, adding volume when close > open and subtracting it when close < open; for a flat bar, use the preceding lower-timeframe bar's direction, initially zero. If the selected timeframe is not lower than the chart timeframe, use the completed chart bar itself. Cumulatively sum these values. Over a fit window of chart bars, regress close on cumulative delta; use the fitted close at the current cumulative delta if the regression slope is positive, otherwise use the window's mean close. Apply an EMA twice with the same smoothing period. The line is available only after consecutive chart bars with lower-timeframe data cover the fit window plus four smoothing periods, and the fit-window volume sum is positive.
Long: close > delta line
Short: close < delta line
