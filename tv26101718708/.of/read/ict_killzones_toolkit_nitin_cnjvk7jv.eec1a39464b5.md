Calculation: Active session = the bar's opening datetime falls within any enabled session in the configured fixed-offset timezone AND the chart timeframe multiplier is at or below the configured limit; a new active session starts when this condition changes from false to true.
Calculation: Swing high and swing low = maintain a state initialized to high. On each bar, set it to high if the high a swing-lookback number of bars ago exceeds the highest high of the intervening bars including the current bar; otherwise set it to low if the corresponding low is below the lowest low of those bars; otherwise retain the state. On a change to high or low, respectively record that historical high or low as the latest swing, with its bar index and an unused flag.
Calculation: Order block = on its creation, select the lowest-low bar for a bullish block or highest-high bar for a bearish block between the relevant swing bar and the current bar, excluding the swing and current bars; ties select the earliest bar. Its bounds are that bar's high and low, or its body high and low when body detection is selected. A new active session clears existing blocks when extension is enabled; otherwise blocks are cleared outside active sessions. Each block can become a breaker once. Use the selected closing-price or wick mitigation mode.
Calculation: Structure pivot = a confirmed pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side; retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind and mark it unused. Outside active sessions reset the high level to a large positive sentinel, the low level to zero, and the shift state to neutral. Each newly confirmed pivot replaces its respective level and resets its unused flag.
Calculation: Gap threshold = ATR multiplied by a configurable factor, with unavailable ATR replaced by zero.
Long: Active session AND previous close > latest unused swing high AND that swing's bar is at or after the start of the current active session; mark the swing used and create a bullish order block.
Short: Active session AND previous close < latest unused swing low AND the latest swing high's bar is at or after the start of the current active session; mark the swing low used and create a bearish order block.
Long: An existing unconverted bullish order block is being monitored AND min(previous open, previous close in closing-price mode OR previous low in wick mode) < its bottom; convert it to a bullish breaker.
Short: An existing unconverted bearish order block is being monitored AND max(previous open, previous close in closing-price mode OR previous high in wick mode) > its top; convert it to a bearish breaker.
Long: Active session AND previous close > latest unused structure pivot high AND shift state is neutral or bearish; mark the pivot used and set shift state to bullish. A break also marks the pivot used and sets the shift state to bullish when the prior state is bullish, without a new decision.
Short: Active session AND previous close < latest unused structure pivot low AND shift state is neutral or bullish; mark the pivot used and set shift state to bearish. A break also marks the pivot used and sets the shift state to bearish when the prior state is bearish, without a new decision.
Long: Active session AND current low - high two bars ago > gap threshold AND current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND current low <= previous high AND previous low <= high two bars ago.
Short: Active session AND low two bars ago - current high > gap threshold AND current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND current high >= previous low AND previous high >= low two bars ago.
