Calculation: Trail and trail direction = EMA of close minus or plus an ATR multiple. Initialize the direction bullish when close is above the EMA, bearish otherwise, and initialize the trail on the corresponding side. In the bullish direction, flip bearish when close is below the previous trail AND the shorter EMA of close is below the longer EMA, if the EMA gate is enabled; set the trail to EMA plus ATR multiple. Otherwise retain the previous trail when the close is below it, or set the trail to the greater of the previous trail and EMA minus ATR multiple. Reverse the comparisons and use the lesser trail for the bearish direction.
Calculation: Basis direction = direction of change of a double EMA of typical price; retain the previous direction when unchanged. The higher-timeframe basis and direction use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Pivot-break zone = confirm a symmetric pivot high or low after the same number of bars on either side. A high or low can be broken once, until replaced by a newly confirmed pivot of that type. A bullish break occurs when close, or optionally high, exceeds the latest unbroken pivot high; a bearish break occurs when close, or optionally low, falls below the latest unbroken pivot low. Remember the direction of every break. A zone event occurs when a break reverses the preceding break direction AND, if the quality filter is enabled, the break bar's range is at least an ATR multiple AND its close is no more than an ATR multiple above the recent lowest low for a bullish break or below the recent highest high for a bearish break AND, if higher-timeframe agreement is required, the last completed higher-timeframe basis direction agrees. The active zone is the high and low of the latest zone-event bar.
Calculation: Structure pivots = separately for a shorter internal and longer swing lookback, set the leg bearish when the high at the lookback is greater than every subsequent high through the current bar, or bullish when the low at the lookback is lower than every subsequent low through the current bar; otherwise retain its direction. When the leg changes to bearish, record that lookback bar's high as the latest high pivot; when it changes to bullish, record its low as the latest low pivot. Each pivot can be crossed once until replaced.
Calculation: Directional score = count the enabled conditions that agree with the direction: long-term Hull is above its value two chart bars ago for long or below for short; shorter-term Hull is similarly aligned; trail direction agrees; active zone direction agrees; active zone direction agrees AND close is within a configured multiple of the zone range from either zone boundary; close minus its value at the momentum lookback, divided by trail ATR, is above a positive threshold for long or below its negative for short; basis direction agrees OR close is above a long-term EMA for long or below it for short; volume is at least a multiple of its moving average. The long-term Hull may instead use a higher timeframe's still-forming bar, with its resulting series compared two chart bars apart.
Calculation: Directional qualification = trail direction agrees AND directional score is at least its threshold AND either the basis direction agrees or close is above a long-term EMA for long or below it for short, unless this gate is disabled AND either the directional score reaches a higher override threshold with matching trail direction or all enabled basis filters pass. The basis filters require the selected chart-timeframe direction, higher-timeframe direction, or both to agree; close above each enabled chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe basis for long or below for short; and, for each enabled cooldown, either the corresponding direction agrees or more than the configured number of chart bars has elapsed since that direction changed. A missing higher-timeframe basis passes its price filter.
Calculation: Trade stop = at entry use the current trail and initial risk is the absolute distance between entry close and trail. Targets are entry plus, for long, or minus, for short, increasing configured multiples of initial risk. Before the first target, use the current trail as stop. After the first target, use the more protective of the trail and entry adjusted by a breakeven buffer. After the second, use the more protective of the trail and either the first target or buffered entry. After the third, use the more protective of the current trail and previous stop. Check the stop and opposite trail flip before targets on each bar; then advance through every target reached on that bar in order.
Long: Trail direction changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Trail direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: A bullish pivot-break zone event occurs.
Short: A bearish pivot-break zone event occurs.
Long: RSI is above its bullish threshold AND below its upper exhaustion threshold AND an EMA of close rises AND the same three conditions were not all true on the previous bar.
Short: RSI is below its bearish threshold AND above its lower exhaustion threshold AND an EMA of close falls AND the same three conditions were not all true on the previous bar.
Long: Close crosses above an unbroken internal or swing high pivot AND, for an internal pivot, that pivot differs from the swing high pivot AND, if the internal confluence filter is enabled, the upper wick is longer than the lower wick.
Short: Close crosses below an unbroken internal or swing low pivot AND, for an internal pivot, that pivot differs from the swing low pivot AND, if the internal confluence filter is enabled, the upper wick is shorter than the lower wick.
Long: The current bar is later than the latest bullish zone event and within the retest window AND low is at or below that zone's high AND close is above its low AND directional qualification for long passes.
Short: The current bar is later than the latest bearish zone event and within the retest window AND high is at or above that zone's low AND close is below its high AND directional qualification for short passes.
Exit: long, close is at or below the trade stop, or low is at or below the trade stop when wick-based stops are selected, OR trail direction changes from bullish to bearish OR a bearish pivot-break zone event occurs.
Exit: short, close is at or above the trade stop, or high is at or above the trade stop when wick-based stops are selected, OR trail direction changes from bearish to bullish OR a bullish pivot-break zone event occurs.
