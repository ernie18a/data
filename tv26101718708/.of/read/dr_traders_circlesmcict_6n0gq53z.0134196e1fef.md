Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); body top = max(open, close); body bottom = min(open, close).
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND body > SMA(body) AND high - body top < body × wick fraction AND body bottom - low < body × wick fraction; bearish displacement uses close < open with the same remaining conditions.
Calculation: Structure pivots = highs or lows confirmed by a longer lookback to the left and a shorter lookback to the right. Maintain alternating pivot highs and lows, replacing the latest pivot with a more extreme same-side pivot. The structure high or low is the most recent corresponding pivot excluding the latest pivot in the sequence.
Calculation: Structure direction = initially neutral; set bullish when close > structure high while direction is not bullish; set bearish when close < structure low while direction is not bearish. Remember the price of each direction-changing break and the latest subsequent continuation break in that direction.
Calculation: Bias high and low = higher-timeframe high and low mapped onto chart bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical evaluation; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime. Compare each mapped value with the highest mapped high or lowest mapped low over preceding chart bars. A higher high is high > preceding highest high; a higher low is low > preceding lowest low; a lower low is low < preceding lowest low; a lower high is high < preceding highest high.
Calculation: Bias direction = initially neutral. A higher high AND higher low sets bullish and increments the bullish count and resets the bearish count; a lower low AND lower high sets bearish and increments the bearish count and resets the bullish count. Otherwise, a higher high increments the bullish count and sets bullish and resets the bearish count when the bullish count reaches its threshold; otherwise, a lower low increments the bearish count and sets bearish and resets the bullish count when the bearish count reaches its threshold. Retain direction and counts otherwise.
Calculation: Order-block swings = maintain a state initially high-swing. Switch to high-swing when the high at the swing lookback exceeds the highest high of the bars since that bar; otherwise switch to low-swing when the low at the swing lookback is below the lowest low of those bars. Record the corresponding lookback bar as a new swing only when the state changes. Each swing can be broken once.
Calculation: Bullish order block = when close first exceeds the latest high swing, select the lowest body bottom or low, according to the selected price basis, among bars after that swing and before the current bar; its paired body top or high is the block top. A bearish order block is created when close first falls below the latest low swing, using the highest body top or high over the corresponding bars and its paired body bottom or low as the block bottom. A bullish block becomes a breaker when body bottom < block bottom and is removed after becoming a breaker if close > block top. A bearish block becomes a breaker when body top > block top and is removed after becoming a breaker if close < block bottom. Retain blocks in newest-first order, including breakers.
Calculation: Bullish breaker confirmation = at least one of the retained newest bullish order blocks is a breaker AND the latest high swing is strictly between its bottom and top; bearish breaker confirmation = at least one of the retained newest bearish order blocks is a breaker AND the latest low swing is strictly between its bottom and top.
Calculation: Bullish gap = previous bar is a bullish displacement AND current low > high two bars ago for fair value gaps OR current low < high two bars ago for implied fair value gaps. Bearish gap = previous bar is a bearish displacement AND current high < low two bars ago for fair value gaps OR current high > low two bars ago for implied fair value gaps. Gap bounds are the two compared prices, ordered from bottom to top. Consecutive same-direction gaps update the latest gap; otherwise create a new gap.
Calculation: Bullish balance range = latest bullish gap bottom < latest bearish gap top AND latest bearish gap bottom < latest bullish gap bottom. Bearish balance range = latest bearish gap bottom < latest bullish gap top AND latest bullish gap bottom < latest bearish gap bottom. A new range is identified by a change in the earlier starting bar of the two gaps.
Long: bullish displacement.
Short: bearish displacement.
Long: bias direction = bullish.
Short: bias direction = bearish.
Long: close > structure high AND structure direction before the current bar is not bullish.
Short: close < structure low AND structure direction before the current bar is not bearish.
Long: structure direction is bullish AND close > structure high AND structure high differs from both the latest bullish direction-changing break price and the latest bullish continuation break price, if one exists.
Short: structure direction is bearish AND close < structure low AND structure low differs from both the latest bearish direction-changing break price and the latest bearish continuation break price, if one exists.
Long: close first exceeds the latest unbroken high swing.
Short: close first falls below the latest unbroken low swing.
Long: bearish breaker confirmation is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Short: bullish breaker confirmation is true AND was false on the previous bar.
Long: bullish gap.
Short: bearish gap.
Long: bullish balance range is identified as a new range.
Short: bearish balance range is identified as a new range.
Long: open > previous close AND previous high > low AND close > previous close AND open > previous open AND previous high < body bottom.
Short: open < previous close AND previous low < high AND close < previous close AND open < previous open AND previous low > body top.
