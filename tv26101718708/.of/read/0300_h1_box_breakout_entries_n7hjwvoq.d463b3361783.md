Calculation: BoxHigh and BoxLow = high and low of the selected-hour H1 bar, using its last completed bar; H1 is higher than or equal to the chart timeframe.
Long: The latest completed H1 close is above BoxHigh and the active breakout direction is bullish, with no prior long since the current box was created or the latest signal is not long; AND ((body > 0 AND lower wick > body × the pin-bar ratio AND upper wick < body AND low <= BoxHigh AND close > BoxHigh) OR (previous chart bar closes below its open AND current chart bar closes above its open AND current close >= previous open AND current open <= previous close AND (previous low <= BoxHigh OR current low <= BoxHigh) AND current close > BoxHigh)); evaluate on a confirmed chart bar.
Short: The latest completed H1 close is below BoxLow and the active breakout direction is bearish, with no prior short since the current box was created or the latest signal is not short; AND ((body > 0 AND upper wick > body × the pin-bar ratio AND lower wick < body AND high >= BoxLow AND close < BoxLow) OR (previous chart bar closes above its open AND current chart bar closes below its open AND current close <= previous open AND current open >= previous close AND (previous high >= BoxLow OR current high >= BoxLow) AND current close < BoxLow)); evaluate on a confirmed chart bar.
