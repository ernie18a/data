Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks; retain the most recent pivot highs and lows up to the pivot storage limit. A pivot is available only after its right-hand bars have completed.
Calculation: Triangle candidate = any two retained pivot highs and two retained pivot lows. Connect the highs with an upper line and the lows with a lower line; let start and end be the earliest and latest pivot bar indices. The high-pair span and low-pair span have a maximum that is at least twice the pivot lookback and at most the formation lookback. The four pivots alternate high and low in chronological order. The upper line is above the lower line at both start and end, their slopes differ by more than a small numerical tolerance, and their intersection is after end. Formation height is the upper-line value minus the lower-line value at start. A line's normalized angle is atan(abs(price change) / formation height / (bar distance between its pivots / (end - start))) in degrees.
Calculation: Chart triangle candidate = a triangle candidate for which every bar from start through end has high at or below the upper line and low at or above the lower line.
Calculation: Other-timeframe triangle candidate = a triangle candidate using the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, or the last available intrabar of a lower timeframe. Use a shorter retained-pivot limit and require all four pivots to lie within a recent-bar limit. From start through end, check containment at intervals equal to the rounded formation span divided by a sampling parameter, with a minimum interval of one bar; each checked high must be at or below the upper line and each checked low at or above the lower line.
Long: A chart triangle candidate OR other-timeframe triangle candidate exists AND its upper-line normalized angle is at most the flat-angle tolerance AND its lower-line slope is positive AND its lower-line normalized angle exceeds twice the flat-angle tolerance plus an angle offset.
Short: A chart triangle candidate OR other-timeframe triangle candidate exists AND its lower-line normalized angle is at most the flat-angle tolerance AND its upper-line slope is negative AND its upper-line normalized angle exceeds twice the flat-angle tolerance plus an angle offset.
