Calculation: Fast, slow, and trend EMAs = EMAs of close with progressively longer lengths; MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line; VWAP = session-anchored VWAP of close.
Calculation: Trend strength = absolute difference between fast and slow EMAs divided by ATR, with a minimum positive price-increment denominator.
Calculation: Smoothed positive directional indicator = weighted moving average of the positive directional indicator from DMI; its DMI and smoothing lengths are parameters.
Calculation: Session eligibility = session filtering is disabled OR the bar opens in either configured trading session AND the opening time of the current forming higher-timeframe daily bar is outside the configured lunch period AND (news avoidance is disabled OR that daily opening time is outside the configured news period).
Calculation: Time-quality score = the highest configured time-quality weight if the bar opens in the primary session and the current daily bar's opening time is outside both lunch and news periods; otherwise the middle weight if it opens in the secondary session and the daily opening time is outside lunch; otherwise the lowest weight.
Calculation: Range condition = range avoidance is enabled AND the absolute fast-to-slow EMA difference is below an ATR-multiple threshold AND the absolute close-to-VWAP difference is below another ATR-multiple threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = chart close above the shorter higher-timeframe EMA and that EMA above the longer higher-timeframe EMA for bullish alignment, or both comparisons reversed for bearish alignment. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time. If the selected timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, use its last intrabar in the chart bar.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of independently parameterized weights: a three-tier score for trend strength using two increasing thresholds; a three-tier score for volume divided by its SMA using two increasing thresholds; a three-tier score for MACD histogram improvement in the trade direction, with the highest tier also requiring the histogram to have that direction's sign; a three-tier score for ATR divided by close using two increasing thresholds; the time-quality score; an alignment bonus when higher-timeframe alignment matches the trade direction; and a bonus when absolute distance from close to its longer-lookback SMA is below an ATR-multiple threshold. Within each three-tier score, higher tiers have higher weights.
Calculation: Support = lowest low over a lookback including the current bar; prior structure low and prior structure high = lowest low and highest high over a shorter lookback ending on the previous bar.
Long: Position side is flat AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend EMA.
Short: Position side is flat AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend EMA.
Long: Chart close > shorter selected-timeframe EMA AND shorter selected-timeframe EMA > longer selected-timeframe EMA, using the same bar-selection rules as higher-timeframe alignment.
Short: Chart close < shorter selected-timeframe EMA AND shorter selected-timeframe EMA < longer selected-timeframe EMA, using the same bar-selection rules as higher-timeframe alignment.
Long: Position side is flat or short AND bars since the last entry signal >= cooldown parameter AND session eligibility = true AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend EMA AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (MACD confirmation disabled OR MACD line > signal line AND histogram >= 0) AND trend strength >= threshold AND (directional-indicator filter disabled OR smoothed positive directional indicator > threshold) AND range condition = false AND absolute distance from close to its shorter-lookback SMA <= ATR-multiple threshold AND confluence score >= threshold AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume / volume SMA > threshold) AND (close crosses above fast EMA OR close crosses above VWAP OR close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > open AND close > previous close OR low <= VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > open OR low <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA OR low <= support multiplied by a proximity factor AND close > support AND (high > prior structure high OR low > prior structure low)).
Short: Position side is flat or long AND bars since the last entry signal >= cooldown parameter AND session eligibility = true AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend EMA AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (MACD confirmation disabled OR MACD line < signal line AND histogram <= 0) AND trend strength >= threshold AND (directional-indicator filter disabled OR smoothed positive directional indicator > threshold) AND range condition = false AND absolute distance from close to its shorter-lookback SMA <= ATR-multiple threshold AND confluence score >= threshold AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume / volume SMA > threshold) AND (close crosses below fast EMA OR close crosses below VWAP OR close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < open AND close < previous close OR high >= VWAP AND close < VWAP AND close < open OR high >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA).
Exit: long, close < VWAP AND close < slow EMA OR close < fast EMA AND close < open OR low <= entry price minus entry-bar ATR multiplied by stop parameter OR bars since entry > time-limit parameter.
Exit: short, close > VWAP AND close > slow EMA OR close > fast EMA AND close > open OR high >= entry price plus entry-bar ATR multiplied by stop parameter OR bars since entry > time-limit parameter.
Exit: long, high >= entry price plus entry-bar ATR multiplied by first-target parameter AND first target has not previously been reached during this trade; exit a configured fraction once.
Exit: short, low <= entry price minus entry-bar ATR multiplied by first-target parameter AND first target has not previously been reached during this trade; exit a configured fraction once.
