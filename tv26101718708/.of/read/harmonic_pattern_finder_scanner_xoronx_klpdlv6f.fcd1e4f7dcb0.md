Calculation: ATR ZigZag pivots = alternating swing highs and lows selected by an ATR-based ZigZag; use only pivot sequences that pass the configured leg-length and leg-bar filters.
Calculation: Harmonic pattern = five alternating pivots X-A-B-C-D, starting at a trough for bullish patterns and a peak for bearish patterns. Let XA=|A−X|, AB=|A−B|, BC=|B−C|, XC=|C−X|, CD=|D−C|, and AD=|D−A|. Ratios must be within their stated ranges, expanded by the configured Fibonacci slack: Bat: AB/XA 0.382–0.5, BC/AB 0.382–0.886, CD/BC 1.618–2.618, AD/XA 0.886. Butterfly: AB/XA 0.786, BC/AB 0.382–0.886, CD/BC 1.618–2.618, AD/XA 1.272. Shark: BC/AB 1.13–1.618, CD/BC 1.618–2.24, CD/XC 0.886–1.13. Cypher: AB/XA 0.382–0.618, CD/XC 0.786, XC/XA 1.13–1.414. Crab: AB/XA 0.382–0.618, BC/AB 0.382–0.886, CD/BC 2.24–3.618, AD/XA 1.618. Deep Crab: AB/XA 0.886, BC/AB 0.382–0.886, CD/BC 2–3.618, AD/XA 1.618. Gartley: AB/XA 0.618, BC/AB 0.382–0.886, CD/BC 1.272–1.618, AD/XA 0.786. For a completed pattern, D must lie within the PRZ formed by the applicable D projections for that pattern. Apply configured PRZ-purity and early/late timing filters when enabled.
Long: a valid bullish completed pattern with D in its PRZ OR a valid bullish emerging projection whose latest C is the latest peak and whose projected D is not reached.
Short: a valid bearish completed pattern with D in its PRZ OR a valid bearish emerging projection whose latest C is the latest trough and whose projected D is not reached.
