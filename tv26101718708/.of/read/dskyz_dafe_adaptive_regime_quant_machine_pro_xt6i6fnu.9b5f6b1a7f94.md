Calculation: Index 0 denotes the current chart bar; positive indices denote preceding chart bars. Each distinct threshold, multiplier, weight, and lookback is a parameter unless a relationship is stated.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe RSI and MACD = the higher-timeframe RSI and MACD values mapped to chart bars with historical completed-bar mapping, then shifted back by one chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish trend = higher-timeframe MACD line > its signal line AND higher-timeframe RSI > a midpoint threshold. Higher-timeframe bearish trend = higher-timeframe MACD line < its signal line AND higher-timeframe RSI < the same midpoint threshold.
Calculation: Trending regime = ADX > its trend threshold AND absolute difference between shorter and longer EMA > ATR multiplied by a spread threshold AND (higher-timeframe bullish trend OR higher-timeframe bearish trend). Range regime = ADX < its range threshold AND (highest high − lowest low) over a lookback divided by close < a range threshold AND higher-timeframe bullish trend is false AND higher-timeframe bearish trend is false AND trending regime is false. Volatile regime = Bollinger Band width divided by its middle band > its moving average multiplied by a volatility threshold AND ATR > its moving average multiplied by an expansion threshold AND (higher-timeframe RSI > an upper threshold OR higher-timeframe RSI < a lower threshold) AND trending regime is false AND range regime is false.
Calculation: Bullish pattern filter = low <= lowest low over a lookback including the current bar multiplied by a support multiplier AND volume > its moving average multiplied by a volume threshold. Bearish pattern filter = high >= highest high over the same lookback multiplied by a resistance multiplier AND volume > its moving average multiplied by the same volume threshold.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND close > open[1] AND open < close[1] AND bullish pattern filter. Hammer = high − low > a body multiplier multiplied by absolute(open − close) AND (close − low) / (a positive offset + high − low) > a wick threshold AND bullish pattern filter. Morning star = close[2] < open[2] AND absolute(close[1] − open[1]) < a small-body multiplier multiplied by (high[1] − low[1]) AND close > open AND close > (open[2] + close[2]) / 2 AND bullish pattern filter. Piercing = close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND close > (open[1] + close[1]) / 2 AND open < close[1] AND bullish pattern filter. Double bottom = low < low[1] AND low[1] > low[2] AND low[2] < low[3] AND close > open AND bullish pattern filter.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = close[1] > open[1] AND close < open AND close < open[1] AND open > close[1] AND bearish pattern filter. Shooting star = high − low > the hammer body multiplier multiplied by absolute(open − close) AND (high − close) / (the hammer offset + high − low) > the hammer wick threshold AND bearish pattern filter. Evening star = close[2] > open[2] AND absolute(close[1] − open[1]) < the morning-star small-body multiplier multiplied by (high[1] − low[1]) AND close < open AND close < (open[2] + close[2]) / 2 AND bearish pattern filter. Dark cloud = close[1] > open[1] AND close < open AND close < (open[1] + close[1]) / 2 AND open > close[1] AND bearish pattern filter. Double top = high > high[1] AND high[1] < high[2] AND high[2] > high[3] AND close < open AND bearish pattern filter.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of weights for each true condition: bullish engulfing; hammer, with a larger weight in the range regime; morning star; piercing; double bottom, with a larger weight in the volatile regime; shorter EMA > longer EMA; RSI < a lower threshold; MACD line > signal line; ATR > its moving average multiplied by the expansion threshold; and higher-timeframe bullish trend AND higher-timeframe RSI < an upper threshold. Bearish score uses corresponding bearish patterns, shorter EMA < longer EMA, RSI > an upper threshold, MACD line < signal line, the same ATR expansion condition, and higher-timeframe bearish trend AND higher-timeframe RSI > a lower threshold. Corresponding bullish and bearish factors have equal weights; morning star and piercing have equal weights, as do evening star and dark cloud.
Calculation: Initial long stop = lowest low over a shorter swing lookback at the long signal minus either a fixed distance or ATR at the signal multiplied by a stop multiplier. Initial short stop = highest high over the same swing lookback at the short signal plus the same selected distance. Long target = signal-bar close + a target multiplier multiplied by (signal-bar close − initial long stop). Short target = signal-bar close − the same target multiplier multiplied by (initial short stop − signal-bar close).
Calculation: Long trailing stop starts at the initial long stop; whenever close > long target multiplied by an activation fraction, update it to the maximum of its previous value and close − ATR multiplied by a trailing multiplier. Short trailing stop starts at the initial short stop; whenever close < short target multiplied by the same activation fraction, update it to the minimum of its previous value and close + ATR multiplied by the same trailing multiplier.
Long: Bullish score >= a score threshold.
Short: Bearish score >= the same score threshold.
Long: Shorter EMA > longer EMA + ATR multiplied by a trend-strength threshold.
Short: Shorter EMA < longer EMA − ATR multiplied by the same trend-strength threshold.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish trend.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish trend.
Exit: long, low <= initial long stop OR high >= long target OR low <= long trailing stop OR close < initial long stop.
Exit: short, high >= initial short stop OR low <= short target OR high >= short trailing stop OR close > initial short stop.
