Calculation: High/low bands = Moving averages of high and low, using the same selectable SMA, EMA, SMMA or WMA type. The fast band uses a shorter lookback than the slow band; each band midpoint is the mean of its high and low averages.
Calculation: Band-cross state = For each band and direction, record the latest bar whose close is below the band low for a long cross or above the band high for a short cross. A raw long cross occurs when close is above that band's high within the configured cross lookback; a raw short cross occurs when close is below that band's low within that lookback. Clear the corresponding recorded bar on a raw cross or when it expires. Record each raw cross as the latest breakout for that band and direction, regardless of entry filters.
Calculation: Band-return state = For each band and direction, retain the latest raw-cross breakout until the first subsequent raw return or until the configured return lookback expires. A raw long return has low <= band high AND close >= band low; a raw short return has high >= band low AND close <= band high. Clear the breakout on that first raw return, regardless of entry filters.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = A high/low identified as a pivot using equal numbers of bars on each side. Retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind, replacing it when a newer one is confirmed and clearing it after its corresponding structure break.
Calculation: Gap source high/low = Either the bar's high/low or the greater/lesser of its open and close, using the same choice for both bars.
Long: On any selected higher timeframe, the last completed bar's shorter-lookback moving average of close > its longer-lookback moving average of close; use the same selectable SMA, EMA, SMMA or WMA type for both.
Short: On any selected higher timeframe, the last completed bar's shorter-lookback moving average of close < its longer-lookback moving average of close; use the same selectable SMA, EMA, SMMA or WMA type for both.
Long: On any selected higher timeframe, the last completed bar's close > the preceding higher-timeframe bar's high.
Short: On any selected higher timeframe, the last completed bar's close < the preceding higher-timeframe bar's low.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; the current close must be above the current pivot level AND the preceding close must be at or below the preceding bar's pivot level.
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low; the current close must be below the current pivot level AND the preceding close must be at or above the preceding bar's pivot level.
Long: Current gap source low > gap source high two bars earlier AND their difference >= the minimum fair value gap size.
Short: Current gap source high < gap source low two bars earlier AND their difference >= the minimum fair value gap size.
Long: Open > previous close AND open minus previous close >= the minimum gap size.
Short: Open < previous close AND previous close minus open >= the minimum gap size.
Long: Fast band midpoint > slow band midpoint AND both midpoints > their respective values at the trend lookback AND fast band low > slow band high AND fast band low minus slow band high >= the minimum band gap AND elapsed time since the latest band entry signal of either direction >= the minimum spacing, or no such signal exists AND, for an enabled signal type and selected band, (a raw long cross occurs OR a raw long return occurs OR low <= band high AND close > band high).
Short: Fast band midpoint < slow band midpoint AND both midpoints < their respective values at the trend lookback AND slow band low > fast band high AND slow band low minus fast band high >= the minimum band gap AND elapsed time since the latest band entry signal of either direction >= the minimum spacing, or no such signal exists AND, for an enabled signal type and selected band, (a raw short cross occurs OR a raw short return occurs OR high >= band low AND close < band low).
