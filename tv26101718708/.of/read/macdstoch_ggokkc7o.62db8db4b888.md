Calculation: MACD histogram = fast moving average of close minus slow moving average of close, minus a moving average of that difference.
Calculation: Stochastic %D = moving average of %K from the shortest stochastic lookback, with %K smoothed first.
Calculation: Weighted stochastic = moving average of the weighted average of four smoothed stochastic %K series, using weights 1, 3, 9, and 18; their lookbacks and smoothing lengths increase from shortest to longest.
Long: prior MACD histogram < 0 AND current MACD histogram ≥ its values at six progressively shorter lag intervals (from longest to shortest) AND Stochastic %D is below the oversold threshold AND volume > prior-bar volume AND Weighted stochastic > prior-bar Weighted stochastic AND Weighted stochastic < the overbought threshold AND long entry is armed (arms when all six histogram comparisons are ≤ 0; disarms on a long entry or when all six are ≥ 0).
Short: prior MACD histogram > 0 AND current MACD histogram ≤ its values at six progressively shorter lag intervals (from longest to shortest) AND Stochastic %D is above the overbought threshold AND volume > prior-bar volume AND Weighted stochastic < prior-bar Weighted stochastic AND Weighted stochastic > the oversold threshold AND short entry is armed (arms when all six histogram comparisons are ≤ 0; disarms on a short entry or when all six are ≥ 0).
Exit: long, Stochastic %D > the overbought threshold OR close < entry price reduced by the stop-loss percentage OR Weighted stochastic < the oversold threshold AND prior-bar Weighted stochastic > the oversold threshold OR close > entry price increased by the take-profit percentage.
Exit: short, Stochastic %D < the oversold threshold OR close > entry price increased by the stop-loss percentage OR Weighted stochastic > the overbought threshold AND prior-bar Weighted stochastic < the overbought threshold OR close < entry price reduced by the take-profit percentage.
