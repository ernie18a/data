Calculation: High levels = the high of the last completed bar of each of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other; the running highs of each of three separately parameterized intraday sessions, reset at each session start and retained outside the session; and the latest confirmed pivot high. Higher-timeframe values use historical bar merging with lookahead off: the previous higher-timeframe bar's value updates on the final chart bar of the current higher-timeframe bar. A pivot high is a high with a parameterized number of lower or equal highs on each side and becomes available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Low levels = the corresponding last completed higher-timeframe lows, running session lows, and latest confirmed pivot low, defined symmetrically to high levels.
Calculation: Initial stop = the high two chart bars before entry minus a nonnegative price buffer for a long, or the low two chart bars before entry plus the buffer for a short. Initial risk = the absolute difference between entry close and initial stop. Entry price is entry-bar close.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus a parameterized multiple of initial risk for a long, or entry price minus that multiple for a short.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial stop until close profit divided by initial risk first reaches a parameterized trigger. Collect the high two bars earlier for every bar since entry, including the entry bar, whose low exceeds that earlier high for a long; collect the low two bars earlier for every bar since entry whose high is below that earlier low for a short. On the trigger bar, including its newly collected level, set the stop to the collected level nearest the trigger price that is at or below it for a long, or at or above it for a short; if none qualifies, use the most recently collected level. Trigger price is entry price plus the trigger multiple of initial risk for a long, or minus it for a short. From the trigger bar onward, replace the stop with a newly collected level whenever that level is higher for a long or lower for a short. Update the stop before testing exits, including on the entry bar.
Long: No position at the start of the bar AND low > high two bars earlier AND close > high two bars earlier AND close crosses above any high level.
Short: No position at the start of the bar AND high < low two bars earlier AND close < low two bars earlier AND close crosses below any low level.
Exit: Long, close < trailing stop OR low <= trailing stop OR high >= target.
Exit: Short, close > trailing stop OR high >= trailing stop OR low <= target.
