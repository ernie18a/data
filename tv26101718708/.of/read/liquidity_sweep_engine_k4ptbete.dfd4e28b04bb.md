Calculation: Liquidity levels = confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows; merge a new pivot into the most recently registered unpierced level of the same side within an ATR tolerance, retaining its price and incrementing its touches only if the pivot is newer. Add the last completed higher-timeframe period highs and lows, completed session highs and lows, and confirmed pivot highs and lows from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; add a higher-timeframe pivot only when the current high is below a new pivot high or the current low is above a new pivot low. Replace each prior-period or session level when its source changes. Assign each level an origin weight and birth bar; limit the collection size by removing the oldest minimum-weight level, or the oldest level if none has minimum weight.
Calculation: Volume-profile levels = divide each completed chart-timeframe bar's volume equally among fixed-width price bins intersecting its low-to-high range. At each higher-timeframe anchor boundary, select the highest-volume bin as the point of control, breaking ties toward lower bins; expand from it toward the higher-volume adjacent bin, breaking ties upward, until a specified share of total volume is included. Replace the previous value-area high and low with the upper and lower edges of the included bins and add the point of control as a neutral liquidity level. Retire a point of control when a later bar's range contains it. Fix bin width from the first positive close, using a configurable price increment.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier by at least an ATR threshold; bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier by at least an ATR threshold. Retain each gap until a later bar reaches its far edge or its age exceeds a limit; limit the collection by removing the oldest gap.
Calculation: Sweep candidate = for each non-neutral liquidity level after its birth bar, record the first bar whose high exceeds a high-side level or whose low falls below a low-side level, then track the most extreme price since that bar. Divide penetration beyond the level by current ATR. Remove the level if penetration exceeds its maximum or if the reclaim deadline passes without a close back across the level. On a close below a pierced high-side level or above a pierced low-side level, remove the level and create a short or long candidate respectively if penetration meets its minimum; otherwise count a touch, move the level to the extreme and reset its penetration. If several candidates occur together, retain the highest-quality one, breaking ties in favor of the most recently registered level.
Calculation: Level quality = capped weighted sum of origin weight, capped extra touches, capped age, capped ATR-normalized distance from the recent opposite price extreme, and a bonus for proximity to the nearest fixed round-number grid. Fix the grid spacing from the first positive close using a configurable price scale and preferred round-number increments.
Calculation: Sweep setup = the latest eligible sweep candidate with quality at least a threshold, accepted while the sweep trade is flat and its cooldown has elapsed. A new eligible candidate replaces any existing setup. Save its level, extreme, penetration bar, quality, the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot low for a short or pivot high for a long, the maximum relative volume over the sweep lookback, and the sign of the sum of signed lower-timeframe volumes over the current and preceding chart bars. Signed volume is positive for a lower-timeframe bar closing above its open, negative below, and zero at equality; use completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar. Invalidate the setup if a later high exceeds a short setup's saved extreme or a later low falls below a long setup's saved extreme, or if its confirmation age exceeds a limit.
Calculation: Confirmation and score = while a setup remains valid, accumulate whether a direction-matching displacement candle, close beyond the saved pivot in the setup direction, and direction-matching fair value gap have occurred; count the gap only if its middle bar is no earlier than the penetration bar. A displacement candle has a direction-matching body at least an ATR threshold and closes within a specified fraction of its range from its directional extreme. Score one point each for elevated saved relative volume, matching saved signed volume, accumulated displacement, accumulated pivot break, accumulated gap, matching bias from shorter versus longer EMAs of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, swept level on the appropriate side of the midpoint of a rolling high-low range, ATR percentile rank above its threshold, swept extreme on the appropriate side of anchored VWAP, and, for intraday charts, a configured active-hours window. Divide by the number of applicable checks. Anchor VWAP at a configurable higher-timeframe boundary and use its still-forming anchor period.
Calculation: Target risk multiple = collect unpierced opposing or neutral liquidity levels and opposite-direction unfilled gap edges beyond entry whose distance after an ATR offset is at least one risk unit. Major pools are levels with sufficient origin weight or touches. For a sweep trade, select the nearest pool at ordinary score, the nearest major pool at higher score, or the second-nearest major pool at the highest score; if no major pool exists, use all pools. If no pool exists, use the first fixed round-number grid level beyond one risk unit plus the offset, or one risk unit if the grid is unavailable. For a front entry, select the nearest pool or one risk unit if none exists. Divide the selected offset-adjusted distance by entry risk, floor it to a whole number and clamp it to configured minimum and maximum multiples.
Long: A valid long sweep setup exists AND accumulated bullish displacement or bullish pivot break has occurred AND score is at least its threshold AND the bar is outside configured blocked news windows AND ATR times the stop multiplier is positive AND the absolute distance from close to the saved sweep low minus an ATR buffer is no greater than that risk.
Short: A valid short sweep setup exists AND accumulated bearish displacement or bearish pivot break has occurred AND score is at least its threshold AND the bar is outside configured blocked news windows AND ATR times the stop multiplier is positive AND the absolute distance from close to the saved sweep high plus an ATR buffer is no greater than that risk.
Long: Front entries are enabled AND no front entry or sweep trade is open AND ATR is available AND the shorter EMA exceeds the longer EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar OR front entries are enabled AND no front entry or sweep trade is open AND ATR is available AND higher-timeframe EMA bias is unavailable AND close is at or above anchored VWAP.
Short: Front entries are enabled AND no front entry or sweep trade is open AND ATR is available AND the shorter EMA is at or below the longer EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar OR front entries are enabled AND no front entry or sweep trade is open AND ATR is available AND higher-timeframe EMA bias is unavailable AND close is below anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Entry risk and holding limit = at entry close, freeze ATR times the stop multiplier as risk and freeze the target risk multiple. The holding limit is a configured number of chart bars multiplied by the target risk multiple capped at a configured maximum. Sweep trades and front entries are tracked independently; a newly opened sweep trade cancels an existing front entry after its normal exit checks.
Exit: long, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND low is at or below entry minus frozen risk OR at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND high is at or above entry plus frozen risk times target multiple OR bars since entry are at least the holding limit OR a sweep trade opens while a front entry is open.
Exit: short, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND high is at or above entry plus frozen risk OR at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND low is at or below entry minus frozen risk times target multiple OR bars since entry are at least the holding limit OR a sweep trade opens while a front entry is open.
