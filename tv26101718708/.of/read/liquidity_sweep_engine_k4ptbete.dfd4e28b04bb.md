Calculation: Eligible sweep levels = unpierced chart-timeframe swing highs/lows (merge levels within an ATR-based tolerance as equal highs/lows); previous completed day, week, and month highs/lows; completed session highs/lows; and higher-timeframe swing highs/lows confirmed from the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Assign each level its source direction and quality weight; exclude a level after it is used or price accepts beyond it.
Calculation: Sweep = price pierces a level, then closes back inside within a limited number of chart bars; penetration measured in ATR must be within configured minimum and maximum bounds. A level swept too shallowly is updated as a touch; a level swept too deeply or not reclaimed in time is discarded. A qualifying level can signal only once.
Calculation: Level quality = score from the level's source timeframe, repeated touches, age, distance reached beyond it, and proximity to a round number.
Calculation: Internal structure = latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and low, using the same left and right pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = a candle in that direction whose body is large relative to ATR and whose close is near its respective extreme.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = a three-bar gap in that direction whose size meets an ATR-based minimum; retain only unfilled gaps within a maximum age.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = fast EMA versus slower EMA, both calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; bullish when fast is above slow, bearish when below.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe delta = sum intrabar volume signed positive for up intrabars, negative for down intrabars, and zero otherwise; compare the current chart bar's delta plus the previous chart bar's delta. Use bars from a lower timeframe.
Calculation: Confluence score = fraction of available checks that pass: relative volume exceeds its baseline; lower-timeframe delta agrees with direction; displacement, structure shift, and fair value gap agree; higher-timeframe bias agrees; sweep level is in premium for shorts or discount for longs; ATR rank meets its minimum; swept wick is on the appropriate side of anchored VWAP; chart time is in an active session; and, when a partner market is available, SMT divergence agrees. Structure shift is a close beyond the latest internal pivot in the trade direction. Premium/discount compares the swept level with the midpoint of the recent chart-timeframe range. SMT divergence is a new chart-timeframe extreme over the recent sweep window that the partner market does not make, with highs used for bearish and lows for bullish divergence. Compare the score with the configured minimum.
Calculation: Stop distance = configured ATR multiple for the trader profile times chart-timeframe ATR; the stop is that distance from entry. The swept wick plus an ATR-based buffer must fit within the stop distance.
Calculation: Target = a whole-number multiple of stop risk, at least one risk unit, aimed at an opposing unpierced liquidity pool ahead of entry. Use the nearest pool at standard confluence, the nearest major pool at high confluence, and the next major pool at very high confluence; if unavailable, use the next round number, otherwise one risk unit. Major pools have higher source weight or repeated touches. Round-number targets and pool targets include an ATR-based offset.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a downside sweep qualifies by minimum level quality; no sweep trade is open and the post-exit cooldown has elapsed; before the confirmation deadline price has not broken below the sweep extreme and either bullish displacement or a close above the stored internal pivot low occurs; the confluence score meets its configured minimum; the bar is outside blocked news minutes; and the sweep wick plus buffer fits inside the stop distance. Enter at that bar's close.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an upside sweep qualifies by minimum level quality; no sweep trade is open and the post-exit cooldown has elapsed; before the confirmation deadline price has not broken above the sweep extreme and either bearish displacement or a close below the stored internal pivot high occurs; the confluence score meets its configured minimum; the bar is outside blocked news minutes; and the sweep wick plus buffer fits inside the stop distance. Enter at that bar's close.
Long: When the optional front-of-market entry is enabled, no front entry or sweep trade is open, and ATR is available, enter at the confirmed bar's close if higher-timeframe bias is bullish, or if bias is neutral and close is at or above anchored VWAP.
Short: When the optional front-of-market entry is enabled, no front entry or sweep trade is open, and ATR is available, enter at the confirmed bar's close if higher-timeframe bias is bearish, or if bias is neutral and close is below anchored VWAP.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= fixed stop OR (if stop is not hit) high >= target OR the configured holding limit is reached; at the holding limit exit at close.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= fixed stop OR (if stop is not hit) low <= target OR the configured holding limit is reached; at the holding limit exit at close. A stop takes priority when stop and target are both touched on the same bar.
Exit: long, for a front-of-market entry, after the entry bar, low <= fixed stop OR high >= target OR the configured holding limit is reached.
Exit: short, for a front-of-market entry, after the entry bar, high >= fixed stop OR low <= target OR the configured holding limit is reached.
