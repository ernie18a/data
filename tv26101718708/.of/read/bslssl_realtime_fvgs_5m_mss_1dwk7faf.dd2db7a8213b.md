Calculation: ActiveSweepZone = price range derived from swing pivots. Pivot high/low = local extreme over many bars on the left and one bar on the right. Keep an alternating high/low zigzag, where a same-type pivot replaces the stored one only if it is more extreme. tol = ATR(shorter lookback)/m. When a new pivot high P confirms, scan stored zigzag highs from newest to oldest and stop at the first high > P+tol. Cluster = scanned highs within (P-tol, P+tol). If cluster size >= 3, a pending buyside zone is created with level = price of the oldest cluster high, band top = mean(cluster max, cluster min)+tol and band bottom = mean(cluster max, cluster min)-tol. Pivot lows are mirrored: stop at the first low < P-tol, giving a pending sellside zone. A pending buyside zone is swept when high > band top; a pending sellside zone is swept when low < band bottom. On the sweep bar the zone becomes active. Buyside active range = [level, min(level+w*ATR(shorter lookback), high)]. Sellside active range = [max(level-w*ATR(shorter lookback), low), level]. (w is a constant multiple.) On each later bar the zone stays active only if low > level-w*ATR(shorter lookback) AND high < level+w*ATR(shorter lookback). While active, the buyside upper edge extends to the running max high and the sellside lower edge extends to the running min low. The first bar that fails the condition makes the zone permanently inactive. Only the most recent few zones per side are tracked.
Calculation: HTF pivot = pivot high/low with equal left and right bar counts, computed on completed bars of a fixed timeframe that is a different timeframe from the chart (intended to be higher than the chart timeframe). Track the latest unbroken HTF pivot high and the latest unbroken HTF pivot low. A new pivot overwrites the stored one. A stored pivot is cleared after it triggers a signal, so each pivot signals once.
Long: low > high 2 bars ago AND (low - high 2 bars ago) > k*ATR(longer lookback) AND the gap range [high 2 bars ago, low] does not overlap any ActiveSweepZone, buyside or sellside, evaluated after zone updates on the same bar
Long: close > latest unbroken HTF pivot high
Short: high < low 2 bars ago AND (low 2 bars ago - high) > k*ATR(longer lookback) AND the gap range [high, low 2 bars ago] does not overlap any ActiveSweepZone, buyside or sellside, evaluated after zone updates on the same bar
Short: close < latest unbroken HTF pivot low
