Calculation: Timeframe score = For each timeframe, calculate the fast and slow close EMAs, with fast shorter than slow; ATR; fast EMA change from a configurable number of timeframe bars ago; RSI; MACD histogram with fast length shorter than slow length; Supertrend direction; and ADX. Assign each of six components +1, -1, or 0 respectively for: fast EMA above, below, or equal to slow EMA; close above, below, or equal to fast EMA; EMA change greater than ATR times a configurable nonnegative multiplier, less than its negative, or otherwise; RSI above an upper threshold, below a lower threshold, or otherwise; MACD histogram above, below, or equal to zero; and bullish, bearish, or unavailable Supertrend direction. Multiply each component by its configurable nonnegative weight and sum. Add a configurable nonnegative alignment weight times the common direction if the EMA structure, price, EMA change, MACD, and Supertrend components all have the same nonzero direction. Multiply the result by clamp(0.65 + 0.45 × min(ADX, ADX reference) / ADX reference, 0.65, 1.10), then clamp to [-100, 100].
Calculation: Aggregate score = Weighted mean of available timeframe scores with positive weights, or zero if none are available. Include each distinct configured timeframe only once, retaining its first weight; exclude timeframes lower than the chart timeframe. For the chart timeframe use the current bar. For higher timeframes use either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the configurable confirmation mode.
Long: Chart-timeframe score >= positive bias threshold OR any configured non-lower-timeframe score >= positive bias threshold OR aggregate score >= positive bias threshold OR aggregate score >= positive strong-bias threshold
Short: Chart-timeframe score <= negative bias threshold OR any configured non-lower-timeframe score <= negative bias threshold OR aggregate score <= negative bias threshold OR aggregate score <= negative strong-bias threshold
