Calculation: Reference edges = highest high and lowest low over a lookback of prior bars, excluding the current bar; the reference is valid when the upper edge exceeds the lower edge.
Calculation: Safe ATR = max(ATR, minimum positive price increment); before ATR is available, use the current high minus low in place of ATR. Use a configurable positive floor when the price increment is unavailable.
Calculation: Breakout sequence = on a valid-reference close above the upper edge plus a buffer times safe ATR, start a bullish sequence at the upper edge; on a close below the lower edge minus that buffer times safe ATR, start a bearish sequence at the lower edge. Start only if no sequence is active, the active sequence is older than its expiry, or the new break is opposite to the active sequence. Store the edge and start bar; reset both counters. On each completed bar, including the start bar, increment the beyond-close counter if the close is strictly beyond the stored edge in the sequence direction; increment the retest counter if the bar is after the start bar, its range intersects the stored edge plus or minus the current safe ATR times the band-width parameter, and its close is strictly beyond the edge. End the sequence after evaluating the current bar when its age exceeds the expiry or its failure shelf is broken. The failure shelf is the stored edge minus the sequence direction multiplied by the shelf-distance parameter and current safe ATR; direction is +1 for bullish and -1 for bearish.
Calculation: Normalization N(x,a,b) = 0 if a equals b; otherwise clamp 100 × (x-a)/(b-a) to the range 0–100.
Calculation: Acceptance score = 0.30 × persistence score + 0.25 × retest score + 0.20 × distance score + 0.15 × candle score + 0.10 × volume score. Persistence score = clamp(0.72 × N(beyond-close count, 1, minimum beyond-close count) + 28 × clamp(beyond-close count / max(min(sequence age + 1, expiry), 1), 0, 1), 0, 100). Retest score = 100 if the retest count is positive, otherwise 78 if the current bar is a retest hold, otherwise 56 if the close is beyond the edge, otherwise 20. Distance score = N((close - stored edge) × direction / safe ATR, breakout buffer, max(breakout buffer + 0.75, 0.85)). Candle score = clamp(62 × directional body + 38 × directional close location, 0, 100), where candle range is max(high - low, minimum positive price increment), body efficiency is abs(close - open) / candle range, directional body is body efficiency when the candle closes in the sequence direction or is flat and otherwise 0.35 × body efficiency, and directional close location is (close - low) / candle range for bullish sequences or (high - close) / candle range for bearish sequences. Volume score = N(relative volume, 0.75, strong-volume threshold) when volume support is enabled, otherwise 70; relative volume is volume divided by its SMA, or 1 when that SMA is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Failure risk = 100 when the shelf is broken; otherwise clamp(0.42 × (100 - N((close - failure shelf) × direction / safe ATR, 0.10, 1.50)) + 0.38 × (100 - persistence score) + 0.20 × (100 - candle score), 0, 100).
Long: A bullish breakout sequence starts.
Short: A bearish breakout sequence starts.
Long: A bullish sequence is active AND its age is at least the minimum acceptance age AND its beyond-close count is at least the minimum count AND its acceptance score is at least the acceptance threshold AND close is at or above the failure shelf.
Short: A bearish sequence is active AND its age is at least the minimum acceptance age AND its beyond-close count is at least the minimum count AND its acceptance score is at least the acceptance threshold AND close is at or below the failure shelf.
Long: A bullish sequence is active AND its age is greater than zero AND high is at least the stored edge minus the band-width parameter times safe ATR AND low is at most the stored edge plus the band-width parameter times safe ATR AND close is above the stored edge.
Short: A bearish sequence is active AND its age is greater than zero AND high is at least the stored edge minus the band-width parameter times safe ATR AND low is at most the stored edge plus the band-width parameter times safe ATR AND close is below the stored edge.
Long: A bullish sequence is active AND close is above the stored edge AND acceptance score is at least the watch threshold AND the sequence is not accepted AND the current bar is not a retest hold AND failure risk is below the risk threshold AND either sequence age is below the tag-review age OR beyond-close count is at least the minimum count.
Short: A bearish sequence is active AND close is below the stored edge AND acceptance score is at least the watch threshold AND the sequence is not accepted AND the current bar is not a retest hold AND failure risk is below the risk threshold AND either sequence age is below the tag-review age OR beyond-close count is at least the minimum count.
Exit: long, a bullish sequence is active AND close is below its failure shelf.
Exit: short, a bearish sequence is active AND close is above its failure shelf.
