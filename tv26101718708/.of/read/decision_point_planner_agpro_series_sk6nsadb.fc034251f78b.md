Calculation: Planning side = user-selected long or short; in automatic mode, long when short-term EMA(close) >= long-term EMA(close) AND close >= long-term EMA(close), short when short-term EMA(close) <= long-term EMA(close) AND close <= long-term EMA(close), otherwise the previous planning side; initialize to long. Evaluate the long condition first when both hold.
Calculation: Decision level = the closest to close of the latest confirmed symmetric pivot low for long or pivot high for short, the corresponding balance edge, and the long-term EMA(close); break distance ties in that order. Before a pivot is confirmed, use the lookback low or high respectively. The balance edge is lookback low + 0.382 × range span for long, or lookback high − 0.382 × range span for short; range span = max(lookback high − lookback low, ATR).
Calculation: Zone = decision level ± zone-width parameter × ATR; touched means low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom; near means touched OR absolute distance from close to decision level / ATR <= reach parameter. Invalidation level = zone bottom − invalidation offset × ATR for long, or zone top + invalidation offset × ATR for short. Risk distance = max((close − invalidation level) / ATR, 0) for long, or max((invalidation level − close) / ATR, 0) for short. Room = max((max(lookback high, latest confirmed pivot high) − close) / ATR, 0) for long, or max((close − min(lookback low, latest confirmed pivot low)) / ATR, 0) for short; use the corresponding lookback extreme before a pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Accepted reaction = touched AND close > decision level AND close > open AND abs(close − open) / candle range >= body-efficiency parameter AND (min(open, close) − low) / ATR >= zone-width parameter × wick factor for long; reverse the price inequalities and use (high − max(open, close)) / ATR for short. Candle range and ATR have a positive minimum precision parameter. Soft reaction = touched AND close is above the decision level for long or below it for short AND close has not crossed the invalidation level.
Calculation: Decision score = round the weighted sum of location, reaction, volume, trend, room, risk and volatility scores, with weights 0.25, 0.23, 0.13, 0.14, 0.14, 0.07 and 0.04 respectively. Cap the sum at 55 if not near, 70 if room is below minimum room, 64 if risk distance is below minimum risk, 62 if above maximum risk, and 28 if close is beyond invalidation; clamp to 0–100 before rounding. Normalize high-good(x,a,b) = clamp(100 × (x − a) / (b − a), 0, 100), or 0 if a = b; low-good(x,a,b) = clamp(100 × (b − x) / (b − a), 0, 100), or 0 if a = b. Location = 100 if touched, otherwise low-good(distance / ATR, 0, 1.6 × reach). Reaction = 100 if accepted and volume ratio >= volume threshold, 84 if accepted, 68 if soft, 48 if near, otherwise 18. Volume = high-good(volume ratio, 0.6, max(1.45 × volume threshold, 1.1)); volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive. Trend = 100 if short-term EMA >= long-term EMA AND close >= long-term EMA for long, or short-term EMA <= long-term EMA AND close <= long-term EMA for short; otherwise 70 if close is on the favorable side of the long-term EMA, including equality, otherwise 38. Room score = high-good(room, 0.35 × minimum room, strong room). Risk score = high-good(risk distance, minimum risk, ideal risk) at or below ideal risk, otherwise low-good(risk distance, ideal risk, maximum risk). Volatility score = 72, 100, 62 or 34 as ATR / longer-lookback SMA(ATR) is <= 0.70, <= 1.35, <= 1.85 or above 1.85, respectively; use ratio 1 when the baseline is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Review state = decision score >= review threshold AND accepted reaction AND room >= minimum room AND close has not crossed the invalidation level. A review event occurs when review state is true and it was false on the preceding confirmed bar.
Long: planning side = long AND review event = true.
Short: planning side = short AND review event = true.
