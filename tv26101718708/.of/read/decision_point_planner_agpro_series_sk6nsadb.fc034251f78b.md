Calculation: Planning side = forced long/short if selected; otherwise bullish when fast EMA >= slow EMA and close >= slow EMA, bearish when fast EMA <= slow EMA and close <= slow EMA, else prior planning side (initially long if close >= range midpoint, short otherwise)
Calculation: Decision level = nearest by absolute close distance among the latest confirmed pivot low (long side) or pivot high (short side), the balance edge (long: range low + 0.382 × range span; short: range high − 0.382 × range span), and slow EMA; ties favor the structure shelf, then balance edge
Calculation: ATR = average true range; safe ATR = max(ATR, current true range if ATR is unavailable, minimum price tick)
Calculation: Decision zone = decision level ± safe ATR × zone half-width; touched when bar range intersects the zone; near when touched or absolute close-to-level distance / safe ATR <= decision reach
Calculation: Invalidation level = long: decision level − safe ATR × (zone half-width + 0.18); short: decision level + safe ATR × (zone half-width + 0.18); invalidated when long close < level or short close > level
Calculation: Long reaction = long side AND touched AND close > decision level AND close > open AND abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= minimum body efficiency AND (min(open, close) − low) / safe ATR >= 0.35 × zone half-width
Calculation: Short reaction = short side AND touched AND close < decision level AND close < open AND abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= minimum body efficiency AND (high − max(open, close)) / safe ATR >= 0.35 × zone half-width
Calculation: Reaction accepted = not invalidated AND (long reaction OR short reaction)
Calculation: Volume supported = volume / average volume over the volume-support lookback >= minimum volume ratio; if average volume is unavailable or zero, ratio is 1
Calculation: Target room = max(0, long: max(range high, latest pivot high) − close; short: close − min(range low, latest pivot low)) / safe ATR; room open when target room >= minimum room
Calculation: Risk distance = max(0, long: close − invalidation level; short: invalidation level − close) / safe ATR
Calculation: Decision score = round(clamp(0.25 × location score + 0.23 × reaction score + 0.13 × volume score + 0.14 × trend score + 0.14 × room score + 0.07 × risk score + 0.04 × volatility score, 0, 100)), then apply these upper caps: 55 if not near; 70 if room is not open; 64 if risk distance < minimum risk; 62 if risk distance > maximum risk; 28 if invalidated. Location score = 100 if touched, else clamp(100 × (1 − distance / (1.6 × decision reach)), 0, 100), where distance = abs(close − decision level) / safe ATR. Reaction score = 100 if reaction accepted and volume supported, 84 if reaction accepted, 68 if not invalidated and touched with close on the planned side of decision level, 48 if near, else 18. Volume score = clamp(100 × (volume ratio − 0.60) / (max(1.45 × minimum volume ratio, 1.10) − 0.60), 0, 100). Trend score = 100 when trend agrees with planning side, 70 when close is on the planned side of slow EMA, else 38. Room score = clamp(100 × (target room − 0.35 × minimum room) / (strong room − 0.35 × minimum room), 0, 100). Risk score rises linearly from 0 at minimum risk to 100 at ideal risk, then falls linearly to 0 at maximum risk, clamped to 0–100. Volatility score = 72 if ATR / its longer baseline <= 0.70; 100 if <= 1.35; 62 if <= 1.85; else 34
Calculation: Action ready = decision score >= action threshold AND reaction accepted AND room open AND not invalidated
Long: planning side is long AND action ready AND previous bar was not in the review state
Short: planning side is short AND action ready AND previous bar was not in the review state
Exit: long, close < invalidation level
Exit: short, close > invalidation level
