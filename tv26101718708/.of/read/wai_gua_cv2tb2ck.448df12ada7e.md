Calculation: Fast ribbon = two moving averages of close using the same selected type, one with a shorter lookback and one with a longer lookback; fast ribbon midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Slow ribbon = two moving averages of close using the same selected type, with longer lookbacks than the corresponding fast ribbon averages; slow ribbon midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Effective high and low = high and low, or max(open, close) and min(open, close) when the Renko option is enabled.
Calculation: Running high and running low = highest effective high and lowest effective low, respectively, since the current trade state began, including the current bar.
Long: Close > EMA of close.
Short: Close <= EMA of close.
Long: Fast ribbon shorter-lookback moving average > fast ribbon longer-lookback moving average AND their difference increases on each of the current and previous bars.
Short: Fast ribbon shorter-lookback moving average < fast ribbon longer-lookback moving average AND their difference decreases on each of the current and previous bars.
Long: Slow ribbon shorter-lookback moving average > slow ribbon longer-lookback moving average AND their difference increases on each of the current and previous bars.
Short: Slow ribbon shorter-lookback moving average < slow ribbon longer-lookback moving average AND their difference decreases on each of the current and previous bars.
Long: Trade state is flat AND the fast ribbon midpoint exceeds the slow ribbon midpoint with their difference increasing on each of the current and previous bars AND both ribbon long conditions hold AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar AND long orders are enabled AND the bar is within the configured test period. If flip mode is enabled, this condition instead enters short.
Short: Trade state is flat AND the fast ribbon midpoint is below the slow ribbon midpoint with their difference decreasing on each of the current and previous bars AND both ribbon short conditions hold AND this combined condition was false on the previous bar AND short orders are enabled AND the bar is within the configured test period. If flip mode is enabled, this condition instead enters long.
Exit: Long trade state, no long entry condition on the current bar AND long orders are enabled AND the bar is within the configured test period AND ((trailing stop is enabled AND effective low <= running high × (1 − trailing-stop fraction)) OR (take profit is enabled AND running high >= entry signal close × (1 + profit fraction) AND effective low <= running high × (1 − trailing-profit fraction)) OR (stop loss is enabled AND effective low <= entry signal close × (1 − stop-loss fraction)) OR ((opposite-trade close is enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled) AND previous-bar fast ribbon midpoint < previous-bar slow ribbon midpoint AND current open < previous-bar slow ribbon midpoint AND this last two-comparison condition was false on the previous bar)).
Exit: Short trade state, no short entry condition on the current bar AND short orders are enabled AND the bar is within the configured test period AND ((trailing stop is enabled AND effective high >= running low × (1 + trailing-stop fraction)) OR (take profit is enabled AND running low <= entry signal close × (1 − profit fraction) AND effective high >= running low × (1 + trailing-profit fraction)) OR (stop loss is enabled AND effective high >= entry signal close × (1 + stop-loss fraction)) OR ((opposite-trade close is enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled) AND previous-bar fast ribbon midpoint > previous-bar slow ribbon midpoint AND current open > previous-bar slow ribbon midpoint AND this last two-comparison condition was false on the previous bar)).
