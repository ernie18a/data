Calculation: Zigzag pivots = on each chart bar, identify whether its high is the highest high or its low is the lowest low over a lookback including the current bar. If only one is true, set direction to high or low respectively; otherwise retain the previous direction. On a direction change, prepend the corresponding extreme and its bar time to the pivot list. Without a direction change, replace the newest pivot only when exactly one extreme is present and it is more extreme in the current direction. The last confirmed leg runs from the third-newest pivot to the second-newest pivot; the newest pivot may still change.
Calculation: Gauge levels = choose the confirmed leg's end as the initial anchor and its start as the final anchor, or reverse them when inversion is enabled. For each ordered entry or target ratio, round initial anchor + ratio × (final anchor − initial anchor) to a whole number. Entry ratios comprise three increasing ratios between the anchors and one at the final anchor; target ratios are increasing extensions beyond the final anchor.
Calculation: First reach = for each gauge level and confirmed leg, scan bars after the leg-end pivot in chronological order. A level is reached when high >= level if the final anchor > initial anchor, or low <= level if the final anchor < initial anchor. Count only the first reach of each level per leg. In non-repainting mode, use the previous chart bar's confirmed leg and anchors, and retire levels reached before the current bar without issuing a decision.
Long: first reach of an entry gauge level.
Exit: long, first reach of a target gauge level.
