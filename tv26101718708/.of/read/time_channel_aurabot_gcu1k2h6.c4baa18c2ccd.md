Calculation: Resistance line = Least-squares linear fit to the highs in the analysis window, repeatedly refit using only highs at or above the current fit minus an ATR-based tolerance; freeze the fit when the window closes and apply the configured ATR-based channel width and resistance offset.
Calculation: Support line = Least-squares linear fit to the lows in the analysis window, repeatedly refit using only lows at or below the current fit plus an ATR-based tolerance; freeze the fit when the window closes and apply the configured ATR-based channel width and support offset.
Long: After the analysis window closes and before any qualifying break that day, a confirmed close is above the resistance line and either the breakout size filter is off or the close is at least the configured distance beyond the line OR, after an upward break, the average close of the subsequent read-window bars is above the resistance line OR, after an upward break, that average is at or below the resistance line.
Short: After the analysis window closes and before any qualifying break that day, a confirmed close is below the support line and either the breakout size filter is off or the close is at least the configured distance beyond the line OR, after a downward break, the average close of the subsequent read-window bars is below the support line OR, after a downward break, that average is at or above the support line.
