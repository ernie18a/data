Calculation: Channel = At the end of each analysis session containing at least three bars, fit separate least-squares lines to session highs and lows against bar index. For each refinement pass, refit the high line using all highs at or above the previous line minus the fit-bar ATR times a tolerance, and refit the low line using all lows at or below the previous line plus that tolerance; retain the previous fit if no points qualify. A one-point fit has zero slope. Freeze both fits until the next session. At each subsequent bar, resistance is the projected high fit plus current ATR times the sum of channel-width and resistance-offset parameters; support is the projected low fit minus current ATR times the sum of channel-width and support-offset parameters.
Calculation: Break read = Before the configured projection-end time, record the first post-session close above resistance or, otherwise, below support, independently of the breakout-size filter and line visibility. On the bar completing a configured number of subsequent chart bars, compare their average close with the corresponding channel line at that bar. Convert the configured read duration to chart bars by rounding duration divided by chart-bar duration, with a minimum of one bar. Reset at the next analysis session.
Long: After the analysis session, the first qualifying breakout since that session has close > resistance AND, if enabled, close - resistance >= a fixed distance or a multiple of current ATR.
Short: After the analysis session, the first qualifying breakout since that session has close < support AND, if enabled, support - close >= a fixed distance or a multiple of current ATR AND no upward breakout qualifies on the same bar.
Long: An upward break read completes AND average close > current resistance.
Short: A downward break read completes AND average close < current support.
Short: An upward break read completes AND average close <= current resistance.
Long: A downward break read completes AND average close >= current support.
Long: Following a held upward break, the first bar within the configured interval measured from the break has low > high two bars earlier.
Short: Following a held downward break, the first bar within the configured interval measured from the break has high < low two bars earlier.
Long: At session-end fitting, a session bar's low is within the fit-bar ATR times the tolerance of the unadjusted fitted low line at that bar; this marks the historical session bar.
Short: At session-end fitting, a session bar's high is within the fit-bar ATR times the tolerance of the unadjusted fitted high line at that bar; this marks the historical session bar.
