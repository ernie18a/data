Calculation: Liquidity pools = Enabled highs and lows of completed named sessions; highs and lows of the last completed higher-timeframe day and week, with the week longer than the day; and equal pivot highs or lows. Equal pivots are consecutive confirmed display pivots whose prices differ by at most an ATR-scaled tolerance; their pool is the higher high or lower low. Add pools in session, day, week, then pivot order; reject a new pool if an unswept pool on the same side is within an ATR-scaled deduplication distance. Retain the newest bounded number of pools, including swept pools. A high pool is swept when high exceeds it by more than the ATR-scaled penetration; a low pool is swept when low falls below it by more than that penetration. Mark all crossed pools swept on that bar.
Calculation: Structure break = A confirmed pivot high or low uses equal numbers of bars on both sides. Track the latest structure pivot on each side and mark it untaken on confirmation. An upward break occurs once per high pivot when close exceeds it, or on its confirmation bar when the highest close over the pivot confirmation window exceeds it. A downward break is symmetric, using close below the low pivot or the lowest close over the confirmation window. Display pivots and structure pivots have independently configurable lengths.
Calculation: Bullish gap = Current low exceeds high two bars earlier; its top is current low and its bottom is that earlier high. Bearish gap = Current high is below low two bars earlier; its top is that earlier low and its bottom is current high.
Calculation: Setup = On a pool sweep, reset any earlier setup. Sweeps of both sides cancel the setup and start a cooldown; an opposite-side sweep within a configurable window also cancels it and starts a cooldown when the two-sided filter is enabled. Track the most recent sweep side and bar even when no setup is armed. Otherwise arm a long setup on a low-pool sweep or a short setup on a high-pool sweep if the optional ADX filter passes and the cooldown has expired. Track the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short from the sweep through the structure-break bar. Cancel if the matching structure break does not occur within the sweep-to-break limit. After the break, bind the latest matching gap formed on or after the sweep, including one formed before the break; if none exists, bind the first matching gap that forms afterward. Cancel when the break-to-entry limit is exceeded, or when close falls below the long gap bottom or rises above the short gap top; before a gap is bound, use the tracked sweep extreme for that invalidation. A new sweep replaces or cancels the setup before these checks. After the first qualifying gap tap, clear the setup even if another entry filter fails.
Calculation: Entry level = The bound gap midpoint, or its near edge: top for a long and bottom for a short. Stop = The tracked sweep extreme minus a configurable price buffer for a long or plus it for a short; the buffer may be the greater of a fixed price buffer and an ATR-scaled buffer, according to the profile. Risk = Absolute difference between the entry level and stop.
Calculation: Confluence score = Sum one point each for a swept day, week, or equal-pivot pool; a sweep-bar close back inside the last swept pool of that side; alignment of current close with an EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, above for long and at or below for short; current close below the open of the first chart bar of the current session-timezone day for long or above it for short; current bar in the configured entry session; a confirmed structure break; the break bar having a nonzero range and an absolute body greater than half its range; a bound gap; and a nearest unswept opposing pool whose reward from the entry level is at least a configurable multiple of positive directional risk. Cross-instrument divergence is excluded because it cannot be calculated from the supplied data.
Calculation: Target = At entry, choose the nearest unswept opposing pool beyond the entry level if its reward is at least risk; otherwise choose the latest confirmed display pivot on the opposing side if its reward is at least risk; otherwise use the entry level plus risk times a configurable reward multiple for a long or minus it for a short. Freeze the stop and target at the signal.
Long: Current low > high two bars earlier.
Short: Current high < low two bars earlier.
Long: Upward structure break.
Short: Downward structure break.
Long: Close > EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Close <= EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Active long setup AND gap is bound AND current bar is later than the gap bar AND low <= entry level AND close > gap bottom AND optional entry-session filter passes AND optional news-time and start-date filters pass AND confluence score >= threshold AND position is flat AND long trading is enabled AND risk > 0.
Short: Active short setup AND gap is bound AND current bar is later than the gap bar AND high >= entry level AND close < gap top AND optional entry-session filter passes AND optional news-time and start-date filters pass AND confluence score >= threshold AND position is flat AND short trading is enabled AND risk > 0.
Exit: long, low <= frozen stop OR high >= frozen target.
Exit: short, high >= frozen stop OR low <= frozen target.
