Calculation: CCI = ((high + low) / 2 - SMA((high + low) / 2)) / (0.015 × mean absolute deviation of (high + low) / 2 over the same lookback). Use separate short-lookback and longer-lookback CCIs, each with its own EMA smoothing period.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe close and its displaced close = the closes of the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar a displacement minus one higher-timeframe bars before it. Lower-timeframe close and its displaced close = the closes of the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar and the bar a displacement minus one lower-timeframe bars before it. Use the same displacement for chart, higher, and lower timeframes.
Calculation: Stop level = the close of the most recent long signal minus its ATR times a multiplier, or the close of the most recent short signal plus its ATR times a multiplier; use the long signal if both occur on the same bar. Reset the level and its age on every long or short signal.
Long: For either CCI independently, its raw CCI crosses above a negative threshold OR, when the optional EMA-cross trigger is enabled, its raw CCI crosses above its own EMA; AND close > close a displacement minus one chart bars ago; AND, if higher-timeframe alignment is enabled, higher-timeframe close > displaced higher-timeframe close; AND, if the lower-timeframe filter is enabled, lower-timeframe close > displaced lower-timeframe close; AND, if the EMA trend filter is enabled, the shorter-lookback close EMA > the longer-lookback close EMA OR that CCI's EMA <= its negative override threshold; AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, ATR > SMA of ATR; AND, if the volume filter is enabled, volume > SMA of volume; AND, if the depth filter is enabled, that CCI's EMA <= the negative depth threshold; AND this complete conjunction for the same CCI was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: For either CCI independently, its raw CCI crosses below a positive threshold OR, when the optional EMA-cross trigger is enabled, its raw CCI crosses below its own EMA; AND close < close a displacement minus one chart bars ago; AND, if higher-timeframe alignment is enabled, higher-timeframe close < displaced higher-timeframe close; AND, if the lower-timeframe filter is enabled, lower-timeframe close < displaced lower-timeframe close; AND, if the EMA trend filter is enabled, the shorter-lookback close EMA < the longer-lookback close EMA OR that CCI's EMA >= its positive override threshold; AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, ATR > SMA of ATR; AND, if the volume filter is enabled, volume > SMA of volume; AND, if the depth filter is enabled, that CCI's EMA >= the positive depth threshold; AND this complete conjunction for the same CCI was false on the previous chart bar.
Exit: long, stop level is enabled AND its age is greater than zero AND its age is at most the stop duration in chart bars AND low <= stop level.
Exit: short, stop level is enabled AND its age is greater than zero AND its age is at most the stop duration in chart bars AND high >= stop level.
