Calculation: Bar range = high − low; previous midpoint = (previous high + previous low) / 2; previous upper and lower bands = previous midpoint ± previous bar range / band divisor.
Calculation: Average range and average volume = simple moving averages over a long lookback, falling back separately to a shorter lookback when the long average is unavailable; missing volume is treated as zero for the volume average.
Calculation: Neutral classification = (bar range > average range AND close > previous lower band AND close < previous upper band AND volume > average volume AND volume < average volume × upper volume multiplier AND volume > previous volume) OR (bar range < average range / range divisor AND volume < average volume / volume divisor) OR (close > previous lower band AND close < previous upper band).
Calculation: Buying classification = NOT neutral classification AND (close > previous midpoint OR (bar range > average range AND close > previous upper band AND volume > average volume) OR (high > previous high AND bar range < average range / range divisor AND volume < average volume) OR (low < previous low AND bar range < average range / range divisor AND volume > average volume)).
Calculation: Selling classification = NOT neutral classification AND NOT buying classification AND ((bar range > average range AND close < previous lower band AND volume > average volume) OR close < previous midpoint).
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / normal volume when current volume and normal volume are positive, otherwise zero. Normal volume is the median volume over a lookback ending on the previous bar; on intraday charts, use the median of the most recent same-time-of-day session volumes when at least a minimum number are available, otherwise use the bar-based median. Include only completed bars with positive volume in the same-time-of-day history, and treat missing volume as zero in the bar-based median.
Calculation: Prior range high and low = highest high and lowest low over the breakout lookback ending on the previous bar. Breakout buffer = a multiplier times the previous bar's ATR, substituting zero when that ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Major levels = clusters of confirmed pivot highs and lows within a long lookback. A pivot has the specified number of bars on each side. Rebuild the clusters only when a pivot is added or an old pivot expires. For each pivot, gather all pivots within a tolerance equal to a multiplier times the previous bar's ATR, substituting zero for unavailable ATR; if the count meets the minimum, form a candidate at their mean price with their earliest pivot bar as its first touch. Process candidates by descending touch count and retain each only if its mean is farther than the tolerance from every retained level. A level is eligible once its first touch meets the minimum age. Previous resistance and support are respectively the nearest eligible level strictly above and below the previous close, as calculated on the previous bar.
Calculation: Up breakout = (close > prior range high + breakout buffer AND previous close ≤ the previous bar's prior range high + its breakout buffer) OR close > previous resistance + breakout buffer. Down breakout = (close < prior range low − breakout buffer AND previous close ≥ the previous bar's prior range low − its breakout buffer) OR close < previous support − breakout buffer. The broken level is the previous resistance or support when its respective major breakout condition holds; otherwise it is the prior range high or low.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low), or one half when high equals low. Base filter passes when disabled, when previous ATR is unavailable or nonpositive, or when (prior range high − prior range low) / previous ATR ≤ the maximum base width. Trend filter passes when disabled, when its configured timeframe is not higher than the chart timeframe, or when the EMA is unavailable; otherwise the last completed higher-timeframe close must be above its EMA for an up breakout or below its EMA for a down breakout.
Calculation: Up qualification = buying classification AND close location ≥ the close-location threshold AND base filter passes AND up trend filter passes. Down qualification = selling classification AND close location ≤ 1 − the close-location threshold AND base filter passes AND down trend filter passes.
Calculation: Pending breakout = an up or down breakout with its matching qualification, positive volume, and relative volume below the immediate-volume threshold, started only when no pending breakout remains after processing the current bar; if both directions start together, choose up. Store its direction, broken level and initial relative volume. On each subsequent bar add relative volume and cancel the pending breakout if the close is below the stored level for up or above it for down. Otherwise, at the end of the confirmation window, confirm if the mean relative volume across the breakout bar and all window bars meets the sustained-volume threshold; then clear the pending breakout. The immediate and sustained-volume thresholds are separately parameterized.
Long: Buying classification.
Short: Selling classification.
Long: Up breakout AND up qualification AND relative volume ≥ the immediate-volume threshold.
Short: Down breakout AND down qualification AND relative volume ≥ the immediate-volume threshold.
Long: An up pending breakout completes its confirmation window without cancellation AND its mean relative volume ≥ the sustained-volume threshold.
Short: A down pending breakout completes its confirmation window without cancellation AND its mean relative volume ≥ the sustained-volume threshold.
