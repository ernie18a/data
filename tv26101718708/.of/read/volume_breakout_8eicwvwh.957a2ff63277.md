Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by normal volume; normal volume is the median of prior bars, or for intraday bars the median of prior sessions’ same-time bars when enough samples exist.
Calculation: ATR buffer = prior completed chart bar’s ATR multiplied by a configurable buffer fraction.
Calculation: Bullish HawkEye bar = not neutral and (close > previous bar midpoint, or range > average range and close > previous midpoint + previous range / divisor and volume > average volume, or high > previous high and range < average range / divisor and volume < average volume, or low < previous low and range < average range / divisor and volume > average volume). Neutral bars take precedence and are those where (range > average range and close lies between previous midpoint ± previous range / divisor and average volume < volume < 1.5 × average volume and volume > previous volume), or (range < average range / divisor and volume < average volume / divisor), or (close lies between previous midpoint ± previous range / divisor).
Calculation: Bearish HawkEye bar = not neutral, not bullish HawkEye, and (range > average range and close < previous midpoint − previous range / divisor and volume > average volume, or close < previous bar midpoint).
Calculation: Major resistance/support = average price of a cluster of swing highs/lows within an ATR-based tolerance; a swing high has lower highs on both sides and a swing low has higher lows on both sides. A cluster must meet configurable minimum touch count and age criteria, and only swings within a configurable lookback are included.
Long: (close > prior lookback highest high + ATR buffer AND previous close <= prior lookback highest high + prior ATR buffer OR close > prior bar’s nearest qualifying major resistance + ATR buffer) AND bullish HawkEye bar AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= configurable close-strength threshold AND range between prior lookback highest high and lowest low / prior ATR <= configurable maximum, when enabled AND (higher timeframe last completed close > its EMA OR higher-timeframe filter inactive) AND (relative volume >= configurable threshold OR breakout passed all other checks with relative volume below that threshold, closes stay above the broken level through the confirmation window, and average relative volume across the breakout and following bars >= configurable sustained-volume threshold).
Short: (close < prior lookback lowest low − ATR buffer AND previous close >= prior lookback lowest low − prior ATR buffer OR close < prior bar’s nearest qualifying major support − ATR buffer) AND bearish HawkEye bar AND (close − low) / (high − low) <= 1 − configurable close-strength threshold AND range between prior lookback highest high and lowest low / prior ATR <= configurable maximum, when enabled AND (higher timeframe last completed close < its EMA OR higher-timeframe filter inactive) AND (relative volume >= configurable threshold OR breakout passed all other checks with relative volume below that threshold, closes stay below the broken level through the confirmation window, and average relative volume across the breakout and following bars >= configurable sustained-volume threshold).
