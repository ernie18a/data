Calculation: Bullish structure = the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing high is above the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing low is above the preceding confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bearish structure = the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing high is below the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing low is below the preceding confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = the close is at or below the low of the latest completed higher-timeframe bar and at or above the high from two completed higher-timeframe bars earlier, when the former is above the latter.
Calculation: Bearish FVG zone = the close is at or above the high of the latest completed higher-timeframe bar and at or below the low from two completed higher-timeframe bars earlier, when the former is below the latter.
Long: latest completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND bullish structure AND (bullish FVG zone OR close is within the configured proportional price tolerance of the latest confirmed higher-timeframe higher low) AND the chart-timeframe fast EMA crosses above the chart-timeframe slow EMA (fast EMA has the shorter lookback)
Short: latest completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND bearish structure AND (bearish FVG zone OR close is within the configured proportional price tolerance of the latest confirmed higher-timeframe lower high) AND the chart-timeframe fast EMA crosses below the chart-timeframe slow EMA (fast EMA has the shorter lookback)
