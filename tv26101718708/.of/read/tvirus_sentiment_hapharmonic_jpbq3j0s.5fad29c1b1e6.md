Calculation: Rising(x, lookback) = x is greater than every value of x in the preceding lookback bars; falling is defined analogously with less than.
Calculation: RSI score = first matching rank: 5 if RSI < extreme oversold threshold; 4 if RSI < oversold threshold; 1 if RSI > extreme overbought threshold; 2 if RSI > overbought threshold; otherwise 3. The extreme thresholds lie farther from the center than the ordinary thresholds.
Calculation: EMA score = first matching rank: 1 if six close EMAs are strictly increasing from shortest to longest period; 2 if shortest EMA > second-shortest EMA AND second-shortest EMA < third-shortest EMA; 4 if the four shortest EMAs are strictly decreasing from shortest to longest period AND the six EMAs are not strictly decreasing from shortest to longest period; 5 if all six EMAs are strictly decreasing from shortest to longest period; otherwise 3.
Calculation: MACD score = first matching rank: 1 if MACD < 0 AND histogram < 0 AND histogram is falling; 2 if MACD < 0 AND (histogram >= 0 OR histogram is rising); 3 if MACD crosses above its signal AND MACD <= 0, OR MACD crosses below its signal AND MACD >= 0; 5 if MACD > 0 AND histogram > 0 AND MACD and histogram are rising; 4 if MACD > 0 AND histogram > 0 AND (MACD > signal OR histogram is rising); otherwise 3. MACD is the difference between a shorter-period and a longer-period moving average of close; each moving average and the signal average may be SMA or EMA. The MACD rising lookback is longer than the histogram rising lookback.
Calculation: ADX score = first matching rank: 5 if ADX > upper trend threshold AND +DI > -DI; 4 if ADX >= trend threshold AND ADX <= upper trend threshold AND +DI > -DI; 3 if ADX >= lower trend threshold AND ADX < trend threshold AND abs(+DI - -DI) <= equality tolerance; 2 if ADX >= lower trend threshold AND ADX < trend threshold AND -DI > +DI; 1 if ADX < lower trend threshold AND -DI > +DI; otherwise 3. The lower trend threshold is less than the trend threshold, which is less than the upper trend threshold.
Calculation: Ichimoku score = first matching rank: 1 if conversion line < base line AND close < both displaced cloud spans AND close < close at the displacement lookback; 2 if conversion line < base line AND (close is inside the displaced cloud OR close >= close at the displacement lookback); 5 if conversion line > base line AND close > both displaced cloud spans AND close > close at the displacement lookback AND current leading span A > current leading span B AND their difference is greater than on the previous bar; 4 if conversion line > base line AND close > both displaced cloud spans AND close > close at the displacement lookback; otherwise 3. Conversion and base lines are midpoints of the highest high and lowest low over shorter and longer lookbacks respectively; leading span A is their average, and leading span B is the midpoint over a still longer lookback. The displaced cloud uses the spans from the displacement lookback; its boundaries count as inside.
Calculation: Bollinger score = first matching rank: 1 if close < lower band OR (close <= lower band AND (lower band is falling OR basis is falling)); 5 if close > upper band OR (close >= upper band AND (upper band is rising OR basis is rising)); 2 if close >= lower band AND close < basis; 4 if close > basis AND close <= upper band; otherwise 3. The basis may be SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of a selected price source; the bands add or subtract a multiple of that source's standard deviation.
Calculation: OBV score = first matching rank: 1 if OBV < its moving average AND OBV is falling over a short lookback; 2 if OBV < its moving average AND (OBV is rising over a short lookback OR OBV >= its moving average multiplied by (1 - near tolerance)); 3 if the absolute difference between OBV and its moving average <= near tolerance multiplied by the absolute moving average, or <= near tolerance when the moving average is zero; 4 if OBV > its moving average AND OBV is rising over a short lookback; 5 if OBV > its moving average AND OBV is rising over a longer lookback AND its current change > a spike multiplier times the moving average of its absolute changes; otherwise 3. The OBV moving average may be SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA.
Calculation: Overall score = round(100 × (average of the seven indicator scores - 1) / 4).
Calculation: Buy pressure = sum of estimated buy volume divided by sum of volume over a fixed lookback including the current bar; estimated buy volume is volume × (close - low) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise half of volume. Use one half when the volume sum is zero.
Long: RSI score >= 4 OR EMA score >= 4 OR MACD score >= 4 OR ADX score >= 4 OR Ichimoku score >= 4 OR Bollinger score >= 4 OR OBV score >= 4.
Long: Overall score > sentiment midpoint threshold AND buy pressure > pressure midpoint threshold.
Short: RSI score <= 2 OR EMA score <= 2 OR MACD score <= 2 OR ADX score <= 2 OR Ichimoku score <= 2 OR Bollinger score <= 2 OR OBV score <= 2.
