Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 0 when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high equals low.
Calculation: Bullish EMA condition = close > long-term EMA of close AND short-term EMA of close > long-term EMA of close AND short-term EMA of close > its previous value. Bearish EMA condition = close < long-term EMA of close AND short-term EMA of close < long-term EMA of close AND short-term EMA of close < its previous value.
Calculation: Bullish RSI condition = RSI >= bullish lower threshold AND RSI <= bullish upper threshold AND RSI > previous RSI. Bearish RSI condition = RSI >= bearish lower threshold AND RSI <= bearish upper threshold AND RSI < previous RSI.
Calculation: Bullish momentum RSI condition = RSI > bullish momentum threshold AND RSI > previous RSI. Bearish momentum RSI condition = RSI < bearish momentum threshold AND RSI < previous RSI.
Calculation: Bullish MACD condition = MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram >= previous histogram. Bearish MACD condition = MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram <= previous histogram.
Calculation: Bullish ADX condition = ADX >= threshold AND positive DI > negative DI. Bearish ADX condition = ADX >= threshold AND negative DI > positive DI.
Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe condition = last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND that EMA > the preceding higher-timeframe bar's EMA. Bearish higher-timeframe condition = last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND that EMA < the preceding higher-timeframe bar's EMA. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the same length as the chart's long-term EMA.
Calculation: Bullish candle condition = close > open AND body fraction >= threshold. Bearish candle condition = close < open AND body fraction >= threshold.
Calculation: Bullish primary score = count of true conditions among bullish EMA, bullish RSI, bullish MACD, bullish ADX, relative volume >= primary volume threshold, bullish higher-timeframe, and bullish candle. Bearish primary score = count of the corresponding bearish conditions with the same volume condition.
Calculation: Bullish breakout score = count of true conditions among bullish EMA, bullish momentum RSI, bullish MACD, bullish ADX, bullish higher-timeframe, and bullish candle. Bearish breakout score = count of the corresponding bearish conditions.
Calculation: Last confirmed direction = direction of the most recent confirmed long or short signal, initially neither; if both occur on the same bar, update it to short.
Long: Chart bar is complete AND last confirmed direction is not long AND ((Supertrend is bullish AND bars since its most recent flip from bearish to bullish <= confirmation window AND bullish primary score >= primary score threshold) OR (close > highest high of the preceding breakout lookback bars AND relative volume >= breakout volume threshold AND bullish breakout score >= breakout score threshold)).
Short: Chart bar is complete AND last confirmed direction is not short AND ((Supertrend is bearish AND bars since its most recent flip from bullish to bearish <= confirmation window AND bearish primary score >= primary score threshold) OR (close < lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback bars AND relative volume >= breakout volume threshold AND bearish breakout score >= breakout score threshold)).
