Calculation: Zero-centered RSI = RSI of the selected price source minus 50. Optionally smooth it recursively as (previous smoothed value + current zero-centered RSI) / 2, initializing with the first available zero-centered RSI.
Calculation: HARSI candle = Calculate zero-centered RSI separately for close, high, and low. Set RSI open to the previous close RSI, or current close RSI if unavailable; RSI high and low are the maximum and minimum of high RSI and low RSI. HARSI close is the average of RSI open, high, low, and close. HARSI open is (previous HARSI open × open-smoothing parameter + previous HARSI close) / (open-smoothing parameter + 1); until the HARSI open that many bars ago exists, use (RSI open + close RSI) / 2. HARSI high is the maximum of RSI high, HARSI open, and HARSI close; HARSI low is their minimum using RSI low.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the selected price source using the channel lookback; calculate an EMA of its absolute deviation from that EMA using the same lookback. Normalize the deviation from the source EMA by dividing it by 0.015 times the deviation EMA. The first WaveTrend line is an EMA of the normalized value using the averaging lookback; the second is an SMA of the first line using the signal lookback.
Calculation: WaveTrend fractal = A top occurs when the second WaveTrend line two bars ago is strictly greater than its values on each of the two preceding and two subsequent bars; a bottom is strictly less than all four. Confirm it on the second subsequent bar. For a limited fractal, its WaveTrend value must also be at or beyond the corresponding overbought or oversold divergence threshold. Compare each confirmed fractal with the most recent same-type qualifying fractal whose confirmation occurred at least two bars earlier, using the price high or low at each fractal bar.
Calculation: Structure pivots = Confirm HARSI-high and HARSI-low pivots after the configured right-hand bars, using the configured left-hand bars. If both confirm together, select the high. Process confirmed pivots chronologically, comparing each candidate to the most recent previously retained pivot: discard a same-side low if higher, a same-side high if lower, a low following a high if higher, and a high following a low if lower. For a retained candidate, set A to its value and B, C, D to the most recent earlier retained opposite-side, same-side, and opposite-side pivots found sequentially within the configured search window; use zero for any value not found.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe HARSI aggregate = For the higher timeframe, use the bar still forming: its open is the chart-timeframe HARSI open on the first chart bar of the interval and its close is the current chart-timeframe HARSI close. For a completed higher-timeframe interval, use that first open and the HARSI close on its last chart bar.
Long: Two bars ago the first WaveTrend line crossed above the second OR crossed it with the two lines equal at the end of that bar, AND the second line was at or below the oversold threshold then, AND the gate zero-centered RSI on the previous bar was below the negative gate threshold.
Short: Two bars ago the first WaveTrend line crossed below the second OR crossed it with the two lines equal at the end of that bar, AND the second line was at or above the overbought threshold then, AND the gate zero-centered RSI on the previous bar was above the positive gate threshold.
Long: A limited WaveTrend bottom fractal is confirmed AND its price low is below the preceding qualifying bottom's price low AND its WaveTrend value is above that bottom's WaveTrend value.
Long: A limited WaveTrend bottom fractal is confirmed AND its price low is above the preceding qualifying bottom's price low AND its WaveTrend value is below that bottom's WaveTrend value.
Short: A limited WaveTrend top fractal is confirmed AND its price high is above the preceding qualifying top's price high AND its WaveTrend value is below that top's WaveTrend value.
Short: A limited WaveTrend top fractal is confirmed AND its price high is below the preceding qualifying top's price high AND its WaveTrend value is above that top's WaveTrend value.
Long: A retained structure high pivot is confirmed AND A > B AND A > C AND C > B AND C > D.
Short: A retained structure low pivot is confirmed AND A < B AND A < C AND C < B AND C < D.
Long: HARSI close > HARSI open.
Short: HARSI close <= HARSI open.
Long: Higher-timeframe HARSI aggregate close > its open.
Short: Higher-timeframe HARSI aggregate close <= its open.
Long: Display zero-centered RSI < negative oversold threshold.
Short: Display zero-centered RSI > positive overbought threshold.
