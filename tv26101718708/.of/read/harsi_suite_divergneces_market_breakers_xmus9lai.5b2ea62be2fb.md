Calculation: WaveTrend = Let ESA be the EMA of HLC3 over the channel lookback, deviation the EMA of |HLC3 − ESA| over the same lookback, and CI = (HLC3 − ESA) / (0.015 × deviation). WT1 is the EMA of CI over the average lookback; WT2 is the SMA of WT1 over the shorter signal lookback.
Calculation: Gate RSI = RSI(source, lookback) − 50, recursively smoothed as the average of the current zero-centered RSI and the prior smoothed value.
Calculation: WT divergence = A confirmed WT2 top/bottom fractal occurs when the center of five consecutive bars (two bars before confirmation) is strictly above/below the other four. Compare its WT2 value and the price high/low at that center with those at the prior qualifying fractal of the same kind. Regular bearish: price high higher and WT2 lower; regular bullish: price low lower and WT2 higher. Hidden bearish: price high lower and WT2 higher; hidden bullish: price low higher and WT2 lower. Regular divergences qualify in either the primary or the more permissive extra divergence zone; hidden divergences qualify in the primary zone or without a zone limit according to the hidden-divergence setting.
Long: Gate RSI[1] is below the negative gate threshold AND WT1 crossed above WT2 in the oversold zone (WT2 at or below its oversold boundary) two bars earlier.
Short: Gate RSI[1] is above the positive gate threshold AND WT1 crossed below WT2 in the overbought zone (WT2 at or above its overbought boundary) two bars earlier.
Long: A confirmed WT2 bottom fractal has a regular or hidden bullish divergence.
Short: A confirmed WT2 top fractal has a regular or hidden bearish divergence.
