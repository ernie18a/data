Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, the current bar's low is greater than the high two bars earlier AND the previous bar's close is greater than that earlier high; its bounds are that earlier high and the current low.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, the current bar's high is less than the low two bars earlier AND the previous bar's close is less than that earlier low; its bounds are the current high and that earlier low.
Calculation: Bullish balanced price range = a bullish fair value gap overlaps an earlier bearish fair value gap within a maximum lookback, starting at least two bars earlier; choose the most recent qualifying earlier gap. The lower bound is the maximum of the gaps' lower bounds and the upper bound is the minimum of their upper bounds. Its start time is the time two bars before the earlier gap. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Bearish balanced price range = a bearish fair value gap overlaps an earlier bullish fair value gap within a maximum lookback, starting at least two bars earlier; choose the most recent qualifying earlier gap. The lower bound is the maximum of the gaps' lower bounds and the upper bound is the minimum of their upper bounds. Its start time is the time two bars before the earlier gap. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Active balanced price range = a newly detected range remains active until its age from start time exceeds a maximum measured in chart-bar durations, it is replaced by a newer range of the same direction with the same start time, or it is mitigated. A bullish range is mitigated when the selected timeframe's close is below its lower bound; a bearish range is mitigated when that close is above its upper bound. Evaluate mitigation on confirmed chart bars, using either the last completed higher-timeframe close or the still-forming higher-timeframe close.
Long: A new bullish balanced price range is detected.
Long: Chart close is greater than or equal to an active bullish balanced price range's lower bound AND less than or equal to its upper bound.
Short: A new bearish balanced price range is detected.
Short: Chart close is greater than or equal to an active bearish balanced price range's lower bound AND less than or equal to its upper bound.
