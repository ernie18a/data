Calculation: Technical rating = mean of the moving-average score and oscillator score. Each score is the sum of its component votes divided by its number of components; each vote is +1 for its bullish condition, -1 for its bearish condition, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Moving-average votes = for each of several progressively longer lookbacks, separately vote +1 when close is above its SMA or EMA and -1 when below; also apply this rule to a volume-weighted moving average and a Hull moving average. Add an Ichimoku vote: +1 when close is above both displaced cloud spans and conversion line is above base line; -1 when close is below both spans and conversion line is below base line. The conversion line uses a shorter high-low midpoint lookback than the base line; the first span is their mean, the second span is a longer-lookback high-low midpoint, and both spans use the same backward displacement.
Calculation: Oscillator votes = RSI: +1 below its lower threshold and rising, -1 above its upper threshold and falling; smoothed stochastic and smoothed stochastic RSI: +1 when both main and signal lines are below the lower threshold and main is above signal, -1 when both are above the upper threshold and main is below signal; CCI: +1 below its lower threshold and rising, -1 above its upper threshold and falling; ADX: +1 above its threshold, rising, and positive DI above negative DI, -1 above its threshold, rising, and negative DI above positive DI; Awesome Oscillator: +1 above zero and rising, -1 below zero and falling; momentum: +1 when rising, -1 when falling; MACD: +1 when its main line is above its signal line, -1 when below; Williams %R: +1 below its lower threshold and rising, -1 above its upper threshold and falling; bull-bear power, defined as high plus low minus twice the close EMA: +1 above zero and rising, -1 below zero and falling; Ultimate Oscillator: +1 above its upper threshold, -1 below its lower threshold.
Calculation: Magnet levels = on each calculation timeframe, maintain separate capped, oldest-first lists of confirmed pivot highs and lows and estimated upper and lower liquidation levels. On each bar first remove upper levels reached by the high and lower levels reached by the low, then add newly confirmed symmetric-lookback pivots. When volume exceeds its SMA times a multiplier, add one upper and one lower liquidation level per configured leverage: close × (1 + 1 / leverage - maintenance-margin fraction) and close × (1 - 1 / leverage + maintenance-margin fraction), respectively. Optionally add the previous day's high if the current day's running high is below it, and its low if the current day's running low is above it. Use only levels strictly above close for the upper side and strictly below close for the lower side; pivots and previous-day levels have equal higher weight and liquidation levels have lower weight.
Calculation: Magnet strength and pull = for each side, use each eligible level within the maximum fractional distance from close as a cluster center; include every same-side level within the cluster-width fraction of that center, including levels beyond the maximum distance. Cluster strength is the sum of weights, cluster price is the weighted mean of levels, and pull is strength divided by the greater of the cluster's percentage distance from close and a positive distance floor. Select the cluster with greatest pull, retaining the first on a tie. With no cluster, strength and pull are zero and magnet price is unavailable.
Calculation: Agreement = sum of enabled timeframe votes divided by the number of enabled vote slots. In technicals-only mode each enabled timeframe contributes one slot; in combined mode each enabled pull, strength, and technicals component contributes one slot per enabled timeframe. A technical vote is +1 at or above its positive rating threshold, -1 at or below its negative threshold, otherwise 0. A pull vote is +1 if upper pull exceeds lower pull, -1 if lower exceeds upper, otherwise 0. A strength vote is +1 if the upper magnet exists and the lower does not or upper strength exceeds lower strength; -1 for the converse; otherwise 0. Missing technicals vote 0. Agreement is unavailable when there are no enabled slots.
Calculation: Signal inputs = calculate chart-timeframe values on the closing chart bar. For each selected higher timeframe use the immediately preceding completed higher-timeframe bar; for each selected lower timeframe use the immediately preceding lower-timeframe bar relative to the last lower bar in the chart bar. Chart-only mode uses chart technical rating regardless of enabled timeframe settings; the other modes use agreement. Track the last emitted signal, initially neither.
Long: At chart-bar close, chart-only mode AND chart technical rating >= positive rating threshold AND last signal is not long OR an all-timeframes mode AND agreement >= positive agreement threshold AND last signal is not long.
Short: At chart-bar close, chart-only mode AND chart technical rating <= negative rating threshold AND last signal is not short OR an all-timeframes mode AND agreement <= negative agreement threshold AND last signal is not short.
Exit: long, short signal occurs.
Exit: short, long signal occurs.
Calculation: Displayed timeframe values = chart-timeframe calculations use the current chart bar; higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, and lower-timeframe calculations use the last lower-timeframe bar in the chart bar.
Long: Enabled displayed timeframe AND upper pull >= lower pull.
Short: Enabled displayed timeframe AND lower pull > upper pull.
Long: Enabled displayed timeframe AND upper magnet exists AND (lower magnet does not exist OR upper strength >= lower strength).
Short: Enabled displayed timeframe AND lower magnet exists AND (upper magnet does not exist OR lower strength > upper strength).
Long: Enabled displayed timeframe AND technical rating > positive display threshold.
Short: Enabled displayed timeframe AND technical rating < negative display threshold.
