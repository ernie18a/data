Calculation: Mature range = Track one active range using either detection method. For candle detection, a compressed bar has abs(close - open) < body-fraction threshold × (high - low) AND high - low < ATR; form a candidate after a minimum consecutive run of compressed bars, with boundaries equal to the highest high and lowest low of the run. For visual detection, seed fixed boundaries from the highest high and lowest low over a seed lookback; on each completed bar outside either boundary, reset the seed using the latest seed-lookback bars, including that bar. A candidate starts at the first seed bar. A candidate may start only when its detection start is later than the last release bar. While developing, update its boundaries from the detection run and discard it if the detection start changes or the run ends. On a completed bar, mature the candidate when its age in bars reaches the selected method's confirmation threshold; freeze its boundaries. The candle candidate minimum and visual seed lookback are separate from their respective confirmation thresholds. A mature range remains active until released, then is cleared.
Long: A mature range is active AND ((close-break method AND completed bar close > frozen range high) OR (wick-break method AND high > frozen range high)).
Short: A mature range is active AND ((close-break method AND completed bar close < frozen range low) OR (wick-break method AND low < frozen range low AND high <= frozen range high)).
