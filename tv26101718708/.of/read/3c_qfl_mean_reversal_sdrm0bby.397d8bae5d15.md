Calculation: Upper base = the most recent high[3] for which high[3] > high[4] > high[5], high[3] > high[2] > high[1], and volume[3] > SMA(volume)[3]; carry the value forward, initially zero. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Lower base = the most recent low[3] for which low[3] < low[4] < low[5], low[3] < low[2] < low[1], and volume[3] > SMA(volume)[3]; carry the value forward, initially zero. Calculate on the chart timeframe or the same higher timeframe as the upper base, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Base age = number of chart bars since the upper base last changed; undefined before its first change.
Calculation: Safety deviation for order n = the sum, for i from 1 through n, of the initial safety deviation multiplied by the safety step scale raised to the power i - 1; the deviation for zero orders is zero.
Calculation: Average entry price = quantity-weighted average fill price of the initial entry and filled safety orders; initial quantity is initial order value divided by its fill price, and safety order n quantity is initial safety order value multiplied by the safety volume scale raised to n - 1, divided by its limit price.
Long: Position is flat AND datetime satisfies the enabled date range, weekday, and session restrictions AND close < lower base × (1 - entry deviation) AND (maximum base age = 0 OR base age < maximum base age) AND (consecutive signals enabled OR previous chart bar did not satisfy the close and base-age conditions OR lower base changed from the previous chart bar).
Short: Position is flat AND datetime satisfies the enabled date range, weekday, and session restrictions AND close > upper base × (1 + entry deviation) AND (maximum base age = 0 OR base age < maximum base age) AND (consecutive signals enabled OR previous chart bar did not satisfy the close and base-age conditions OR upper base changed from the previous chart bar).
Long: Position is long AND safety order n is unfilled AND n <= maximum safety order count AND low <= initial entry price × (1 - safety deviation for order n).
Short: Position is short AND safety order n is unfilled AND n <= maximum safety order count AND high >= initial entry price × (1 + safety deviation for order n).
Exit: long, take profit based on total volume is selected AND high >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage).
Exit: short, take profit based on total volume is selected AND low <= average entry price × (1 - take-profit percentage).
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND stop-loss percentage > safety deviation for the maximum safety order count AND low <= initial entry price × (1 - stop-loss percentage).
Exit: short, stop loss enabled AND stop-loss percentage > safety deviation for the maximum safety order count AND high >= initial entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage).
