Calculation: Master POC and value-area edges = On each completed chart-timeframe bar, use a rolling master window longer than the local profile lookback. Split the window’s low-to-high range into equal price bins; assign each bar’s volume to the bin containing its HLC3. The fullest bin is the POC. Starting there, add the heavier adjacent bin repeatedly until the configured share of total volume is included; the outer bin boundaries are the value-area edges.
Long: Confirmed close > previous bar high AND close > open (if the flip candle direction filter is enabled) AND current bar range >= configured minimum multiple of ATR (or no range filter if the minimum is zero) AND bullish control was not already active AND (close > value-area high AND extension beyond that edge <= configured maximum percentage of the edge-to-POC distance (or no extension limit if zero) OR close <= POC OR POC < close <= value-area high) AND this signal class and direction differ from the previous displayed signal.
Short: Confirmed close < previous bar low AND close < open (if the flip candle direction filter is enabled) AND current bar range >= configured minimum multiple of ATR (or no range filter if the minimum is zero) AND bearish control was not already active AND (close < value-area low AND extension beyond that edge <= configured maximum percentage of the POC-to-edge distance (or no extension limit if zero) OR close >= POC OR value-area low <= close < POC) AND this signal class and direction differ from the previous displayed signal.
