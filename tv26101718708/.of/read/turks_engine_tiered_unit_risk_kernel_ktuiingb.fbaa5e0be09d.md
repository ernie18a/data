Calculation: Swing high/low = a high/low confirmed as the corresponding extremum over equal numbers of preceding and following bars; retain the latest confirmed high and low separately and mark each unused until first broken.
Calculation: Trend quality = a weighted average of efficiency ratio, normalized volume, price location, and directional persistence. Efficiency ratio is absolute source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes. Normalize the volume z-score to a bounded range, or normalize ATR divided by its longer SMA when no positive volume has yet appeared. Price location is the absolute distance of close from the midpoint of the lookback high-low range, normalized by half that range. Directional persistence is the fraction of rising closes when the lookback close change is positive, the fraction of falling closes when negative, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Regime score = a weighted average of normalized ADX, inverted choppiness index, and squared correlation of close with bar index. Choppiness index is the logarithm of the sum of true ranges divided by the lookback high-low range, normalized by the logarithm of the lookback length.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR = ATR at a length interpolated between longer and shorter lengths as regime score increases, multiplied by a bounded ATR-to-longer-SMA-of-ATR ratio and bounded to the permitted length range; linearly interpolate between the two precomputed ATR lengths surrounding that length.
Calculation: Trend bands = source plus or minus adaptive ATR times separately smoothed active and passive multipliers. Derive the symmetric multiplier from trend quality using a configurable power and scaling, then decrease the active multiplier and increase the passive multiplier in proportion to trend quality. Smooth both recursively. In a long trend use the active multiplier below and passive multiplier above; in a short trend reverse them. The lower band is the greater of its raw value and its previous value when the previous close exceeded the previous lower band, otherwise its raw value; the upper band is the lesser of its raw value and its previous value when the previous close was below the previous upper band, otherwise its raw value.
Calculation: Trend direction = initially long; change from short to long when close exceeds the previous upper trend band, and from long to short when close falls below the previous lower trend band; otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Graded exposure = for the ensemble, average the clamped zero-to-one ramps (close minus each SMA) divided by ATR times a width proportional to the square root of that SMA's lookback divided by the longest SMA lookback; the SMAs have successively longer lookbacks. For the single-mean variant, use the same clamped ramp with one SMA and a configurable ATR width. Treat unavailable ensemble means as zero contributions and unavailable or nonpositive ATR as zero exposure. Once the trend SMA is available, optionally quantize: initialize held exposure to zero and replace it with the raw exposure rounded to the nearest step only when its distance from held exposure reaches the hysteresis fraction of a step; otherwise retain it. With quantization disabled use raw exposure.
Calculation: Binary exposure = a gate initialized off that turns on after a configurable consecutive run of closes strictly above the trend SMA and turns off after the same number of consecutive closes at or below it; exposure is one only when the gate is on AND the optional rising-SMA requirement is satisfied, otherwise zero. The rising requirement compares the trend SMA with its value a configurable number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Kernel envelope = a one-sided Gaussian weighted average of the selected price source over a bandwidth-dependent truncated lookback, with weight exp(-lag squared divided by twice bandwidth squared); upper and lower bands are that average plus and minus separately configurable multiples of the SMA of absolute source-minus-average residuals.
Calculation: Envelope capacity = one minus sold rungs divided by total rungs. Initialize sold rungs to zero; increment by one when the source crosses above the upper envelope and sold rungs are below the total, and decrement by one when the source crosses below the lower envelope and sold rungs are above zero. A cross requires the previous source to be on or beyond the opposite side of the previous band.
Calculation: Effective exposure = selectable as graded or binary exposure alone, envelope capacity alone, the lesser of exposure and envelope capacity, or gated exposure. For gated exposure, initialize held exposure to zero; when the kernel average is unavailable or source is at or above it, set held exposure to the selected graded or binary exposure, otherwise set it to the greater of its previous value and that exposure. Disabling the envelope selects exposure alone.
Calculation: Target weight = zero until the trend SMA is available; thereafter cap at one the sum of a configurable floor and effective exposure multiplied by a configurable scale and by one minus the floor.
Long: Position side is flat AND effective exposure > 0 at the confirmed close.
Exit: long, effective exposure <= 0 at the confirmed close.
Long: Graded exposure is selected AND the trend SMA is available AND target weight exceeds its previous value by more than the fill threshold.
Exit: long, graded exposure is selected AND the trend SMA is available AND target weight is below its previous value by more than the fill threshold.
Long: After the structural warm-up, close > the latest unused confirmed swing high.
Short: After the structural warm-up, close < the latest unused confirmed swing low.
Long: Trend direction = long.
Short: Trend direction = short.
