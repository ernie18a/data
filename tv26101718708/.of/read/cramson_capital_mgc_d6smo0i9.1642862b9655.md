Calculation: Opening-range value-area bounds = For each session, collect chart bars whose open times are within the configured opening range. If their highest high is not above their lowest low, or their total distributed volume is zero, the bounds are unavailable. Divide the range upward from its lowest low into rows of height max(1, round(range / configured row count / minimum price increment)) × minimum price increment, with the row count equal to ceil(range / row height), capped at a configured maximum. Distribute each bar's volume across its overlapping rows in proportion to price overlap; assign a zero-range bar's volume to the row containing its price. Select the lowest-index row with maximum volume as the point-of-control row. Starting there, repeatedly add the adjacent row with greater volume; on a tie select the row closer to the point of control, selecting the upper row if distances also tie. Stop before adding a row that would make accumulated volume exceed the configured fraction of total volume. The lower and upper edges of the included rows are the value-area low and high. Freeze these bounds for the session.
Calculation: Post-range open = The open of the first chart bar after the opening range.
Calculation: Entry levels = For a reversion entry, target the far value-area boundary and set the stop the same distance from entry on the opposite side. For a breakout entry, set the stop at the opposite value-area boundary and the target the same distance from entry on the opposite side. Entry price is the signal bar's close; freeze the levels at entry.
Long: The first bar after the opening range has valid value-area bounds AND its open < value-area low.
Short: The first bar after the opening range has valid value-area bounds AND its open > value-area high.
Long: Valid value-area bounds AND no entry yet in the current session AND bar open time is after the opening range and no later than the entry cutoff AND ((post-range open < value-area low AND value-area low < close AND close < value-area high) OR (value-area low < post-range open AND post-range open < value-area high AND close > value-area high)).
Short: Valid value-area bounds AND no entry yet in the current session AND bar open time is after the opening range and no later than the entry cutoff AND ((post-range open > value-area high AND value-area low < close AND close < value-area high) OR (value-area low < post-range open AND post-range open < value-area high AND close < value-area low)).
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= target OR low <= stop OR bar open time >= time-close).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= target OR high >= stop OR bar open time >= time-close).
