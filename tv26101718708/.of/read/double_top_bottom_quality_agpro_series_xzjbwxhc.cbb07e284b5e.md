Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the pivot bar and the configured number of bars on each side; confirmed on the later bar.
Calculation: Double-top neckline = lowest low over a window as long as the distance between the two latest confirmed pivot highs, ending on the bar the second pivot is confirmed. Double-bottom neckline = highest high over the corresponding window for the two latest confirmed pivot lows.
Calculation: Pattern depth = for a double top, the lower pivot high minus its neckline; for a double bottom, its neckline minus the higher pivot low.
Calculation: Pattern height = pattern depth. Double-top target = neckline − pattern height; double-bottom target = neckline + pattern height.
Calculation: Double-pattern quality score = sum of four rounded scores (25 points each): equality points = max(0, 1 − distance between pivot prices / (equality tolerance × ATR)) × 25; break-volume points = clamp((break-bar volume / 20-bar average volume − 1) / (volume multiplier − 1 + 0.0001), 0, 1) × 25; symmetry points = shorter leg / max(longer leg, 1) × 25; depth points = min(1, pattern depth / ATR / 3) × 25. For symmetry, the neckline bar is the second pivot bar minus the pivot length; legs run from first pivot to neckline and neckline to second pivot. Use safe ATR = max(ATR, minimum tick), with missing ATR replaced by minimum tick.
Long: A double-bottom candidate from the two latest confirmed pivot lows has pivot spacing within the configured minimum and maximum, pivot-price difference ≤ equality tolerance × safe ATR, and pattern depth ≥ minimum depth × safe ATR; it is not invalidated (low has not fallen below the lower pivot by more than 0.1 × safe ATR), has not expired (more than twice the maximum spacing since the second pivot), passes cooldown and same-region suppression, and the current close > neckline with quality score ≥ minimum score.
Short: A double-top candidate from the two latest confirmed pivot highs has pivot spacing within the configured minimum and maximum, pivot-price difference ≤ equality tolerance × safe ATR, and pattern depth ≥ minimum depth × safe ATR; it is not invalidated (high has not risen above the higher pivot by more than 0.1 × safe ATR), has not expired (more than twice the maximum spacing since the second pivot), passes cooldown and same-region suppression, and the current close < neckline with quality score ≥ minimum score.
Exit: long, high ≥ double-bottom target.
Exit: short, low ≤ double-top target.
