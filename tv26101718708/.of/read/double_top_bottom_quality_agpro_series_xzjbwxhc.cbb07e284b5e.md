Calculation: ATR floor = max(ATR, price-increment floor); use a configurable positive price-increment floor.
Calculation: Candidate = on confirmation of a pivot high or low, pair it with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind. A pivot has equal configurable numbers of bars on each side. Let distance be the number of bars between the pivots, and let the neckline be the lowest low for a top or highest high for a bottom over the most recent distance bars, including the current bar. Freeze the pivot prices and neckline when the candidate is created. Top depth = lower pivot price minus neckline; bottom depth = neckline minus higher pivot price. Create a candidate only if distance is within the configured inclusive minimum and maximum, pivot price difference <= equality tolerance × current ATR floor, depth >= minimum depth × current ATR floor, and no pending or active confirmed candidate of the same type has its second pivot within 2 × current ATR floor of the new second pivot. Also require no previous confirmation of that type, or bars since its latest confirmation > cooldown, or the distance between the new second pivot and that confirmation's neckline > 2 × current ATR floor. Candidate creation requires the pending-pattern setting to be enabled.
Calculation: Candidate state = process candidates newest first, including candidates created on the current bar. Remove a pending top if high > its higher pivot + 0.1 × current ATR floor, or a pending bottom if low < its lower pivot − 0.1 × current ATR floor, before checking its break. On the first close beyond its neckline, confirm it if quality score >= minimum score; otherwise remove it. After the break check, remove a still-pending candidate if bars since its second pivot > 2 × maximum pivot distance. For a confirmed candidate, beginning on the bar after confirmation, mark it inactive on a target hit; otherwise mark it inactive if the smaller of the absolute close-to-neckline and close-to-target distances > stale-distance multiplier × current ATR floor. After processing all candidates, if active confirmed candidates exceed the configured maximum, mark the oldest excess candidates inactive.
Calculation: Quality score = round(25 × max(0, 1 − pivot price difference / max(equality tolerance × current ATR floor, price-increment floor))) + round(25 × clamp((volume / max(volume SMA, 1) − 1) / (volume multiplier − 1 + 0.0001), 0, 1)) + round(25 × min(1, depth / current ATR floor / 3)). Use 1 for the volume SMA before it is available. The symmetry component is zero because the neckline bar is assigned to the second pivot bar.
Calculation: Target = neckline + depth for a confirmed bottom, or neckline − depth for a confirmed top; freeze it at confirmation.
Long: A bottom candidate is created AND current low >= lower pivot − 0.1 × current ATR floor AND the candidate remains pending after the current bar's break and expiry checks.
Short: A top candidate is created AND current high <= higher pivot + 0.1 × current ATR floor AND the candidate remains pending after the current bar's break and expiry checks.
Long: A pending bottom survives invalidation AND close > its neckline AND quality score >= minimum score.
Short: A pending top survives invalidation AND close < its neckline AND quality score >= minimum score.
Exit: long, an active confirmed bottom exists AND high >= its target.
Exit: short, an active confirmed top exists AND low <= its target.
