Calculation: Pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using specified left and right bar counts; record the latest five in confirmation order as X, A, B, C, D, with their prices, types, and pivot bar indices. If a high and low confirm together, record only the high. Repeated pivot types are retained.
Calculation: Percentage difference(a, b) = 100 × abs(a − b) / max(abs(b), minimum positive price increment).
Calculation: Normalized value = 100 × (value − rolling minimum) / (rolling maximum − rolling minimum), or the midpoint if the extrema are equal.
Calculation: Trend = EMA of the midpoint-scaled correlation between close changes and volume changes; replace an unavailable correlation with zero. Direction = EMA of normalized close rate of change. Impulse = EMA of normalized (ATR / max(EMA of ATR, minimum positive price increment) × volume / max(EMA of volume, minimum volume denominator)). Trend, direction, and impulse use the same smoothing length; direction and impulse use the same normalization lookback; ATR and close rate of change use the same length; the ATR baseline and volume baseline use the same EMA length.
Calculation: Pattern quality = weighted sum of structure score, impulse, and trend. For a head-and-shoulders structure, structure score is a weighted sum of shoulder similarity, neckline similarity, width symmetry, and head depth. Similarity is the clipped percentage score obtained by subtracting the percentage difference divided by its tolerance from one; neckline tolerance is a multiple of shoulder tolerance. Width symmetry is one minus the absolute difference between X-to-B and B-to-D bar widths divided by the greater width, bounded by a positive denominator. Head depth is the distance from B to the nearer shoulder divided by ATR, scaled and clipped. For a cup structure, structure score is the clipped sum of scaled depth in ATR units divided by the minimum depth, a contribution when the extreme is in the middle of the lookback, and a contribution when the close is on the required side of the cup EMA. Scores are scaled to the normalization range and clipped to that range. Evaluate head-and-shoulders quality on the pivot confirmation bar and cup quality on the current bar.
Calculation: Active structure = the latest qualified inverse head-and-shoulders or head-and-shoulders formation; a new qualified formation replaces the active structure. Its neckline is respectively max(A, C) or min(A, C), fixed at formation confirmation. Invalidate an active inverse formation after its breakout check if close is below the current B pivot price, and invalidate an active regular formation after its breakout check if close is above the current B pivot price. A qualifying neckline cross consumes the structure.
Calculation: Pending retest = after a qualifying neckline cross when the retest option is enabled, retain its direction, neckline, and breakout bar; a new qualifying neckline cross replaces any pending retest. Evaluate the retest on the breakout bar and subsequent bars. Cancel it after a successful retest, after the maximum waiting period, or when close moves beyond the neckline against the breakout by more than the ATR tolerance; check for success before cancellation.
