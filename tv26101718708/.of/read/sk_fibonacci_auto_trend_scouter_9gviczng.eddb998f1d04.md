Calculation: Swing records = For each of two chart-timeframe lookbacks, one shorter and one longer, use either the selected price source for both extremes or high for highs and low for lows. A high candidate is the current bar’s lookback high; a low candidate is the current bar’s lookback low. If exactly one candidate exists, set the direction to high or low accordingly; otherwise retain the prior direction. On a direction change, prepend a record containing the candidate value, its bar time, and the selected price source at that bar. Without a direction change, replace the newest record only when the candidate is more extreme in the current direction. Retain the seven newest records, numbered newest first; calculations requiring records are available only when all seven exist.
Calculation: Selected coordinate = For a chosen lookback and pair, select records 1 and 2, 3 and 4, or 5 and 6 for the front, middle, or back pair respectively. Use each record’s candidate value when extremes are based on high and low, otherwise its stored source value. Select the higher value for a top coordinate or the lower value for a bottom coordinate; on a tie select the first record.
Calculation: Trend midpoint = The average of the values of independently selected top and bottom coordinates from the same lookback. Select a from midpoint and a to midpoint independently.
Long: For either lookback, to trend midpoint > from trend midpoint.
Short: For either lookback, to trend midpoint <= from trend midpoint.
Long: For a custom top or bottom line, the to selected coordinate value > the from selected coordinate value, with each endpoint’s lookback and pair selected independently AND the longer-lookback swing records available.
Short: For a custom top or bottom line, the to selected coordinate value <= the from selected coordinate value, with each endpoint’s lookback and pair selected independently AND the longer-lookback swing records available.
Long: Close > the average stored source value of the shorter-lookback records 3 and 4 OR close > the average stored source value of the longer-lookback records 5 and 6.
Short: Close <= the average stored source value of the shorter-lookback records 3 and 4 OR close <= the average stored source value of the longer-lookback records 5 and 6.
