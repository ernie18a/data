Calculation: Distance from moving average (%) = (daily close − daily moving average) ÷ daily moving average × 100, using the still-forming daily bar from the higher timeframe and the configured SMA or EMA.
Calculation: Signal line = SMA of distance from moving average (%) over chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Distance from moving average (%) crosses above 0 OR distance from moving average (%) crosses above the signal line.
Short: Distance from moving average (%) crosses below 0 OR distance from moving average (%) crosses below the signal line.
