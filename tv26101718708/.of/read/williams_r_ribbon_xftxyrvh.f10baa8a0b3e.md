Calculation: Williams %R = 100 × (selected price source − highest high over the lookback) / (highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback); use the midpoint of the Williams %R range when the range is zero. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on an optionally higher or lower timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within each chart bar.
Calculation: Signal = selected moving average of Williams %R, calculated on the same timeframe as Williams %R; for a volume-weighted moving average use that timeframe's volume.
Calculation: Displayed Williams %R and signal = Williams %R and signal separately, optionally smoothed by a selected moving average on the chart timeframe.
Long: Displayed Williams %R > displayed signal AND their absolute difference >= the neutral spread threshold.
Short: Displayed Williams %R < displayed signal AND their absolute difference >= the neutral spread threshold.
Long: Displayed Williams %R <= any oversold extension threshold.
Short: Displayed Williams %R >= any overbought extension threshold.
Long: Unsmoothed Williams %R crosses above the unsmoothed signal OR crosses above the oversold level OR crosses below any oversold extension threshold.
Short: Unsmoothed Williams %R crosses below the unsmoothed signal OR crosses below the overbought level OR crosses above any overbought extension threshold.
