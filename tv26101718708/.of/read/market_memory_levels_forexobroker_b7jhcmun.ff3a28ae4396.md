Calculation: Previous-period levels = high, low, and close of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, mapped to the chart with updates at the close of each higher-timeframe interval; use a longer higher timeframe for the second set of high and low levels.
Calculation: Round levels = floor(close / round-number spacing) * round-number spacing plus each integer offset within a configurable symmetric range multiplied by the spacing.
Calculation: Support proximity = (the shorter higher-timeframe levels are enabled AND absolute(close - previous low) < ATR * proximity multiplier) OR (the longer higher-timeframe levels are enabled AND absolute(close - previous low) < ATR * proximity multiplier) OR (the shorter higher-timeframe levels are enabled AND absolute(close - previous close) < ATR * proximity multiplier AND close > previous close) OR (round levels are enabled AND any round level satisfies absolute(close - round level) < ATR * proximity multiplier AND close >= round level).
Calculation: Resistance proximity = (the shorter higher-timeframe levels are enabled AND absolute(close - previous high) < ATR * proximity multiplier) OR (the longer higher-timeframe levels are enabled AND absolute(close - previous high) < ATR * proximity multiplier) OR (the shorter higher-timeframe levels are enabled AND absolute(close - previous close) < ATR * proximity multiplier AND close < previous close) OR (round levels are enabled AND any round level satisfies absolute(close - round level) < ATR * proximity multiplier AND close <= round level).
Calculation: Bars since last filtered signal = current zero-based bar index minus the bar index of the most recent volume-confirmed long or short bounce signal; initialize the last signal index to zero.
Long: Support proximity AND close > open AND close - open > (high - low) * body fraction.
Short: Resistance proximity AND open - close > (high - low) * body fraction.
Long: Support proximity AND close > open AND close - open > (high - low) * body fraction AND volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier AND bars since last filtered signal >= cooldown.
Short: Resistance proximity AND open - close > (high - low) * body fraction AND volume > SMA(volume) * volume multiplier AND bars since last filtered signal >= cooldown.
Long: close > previous shorter higher-timeframe high OR close > previous shorter higher-timeframe close.
Short: close <= previous shorter higher-timeframe high AND close <= previous shorter higher-timeframe close AND (close < previous shorter higher-timeframe low OR close < previous shorter higher-timeframe close).
