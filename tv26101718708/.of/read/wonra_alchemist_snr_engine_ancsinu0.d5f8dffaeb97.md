Calculation: Confirmed swings = ATR-threshold zigzag of chart-bar highs and lows: track the current leg's extreme; a reversal exceeding the current swing ATR threshold makes that extreme a candidate; cancel the candidate if price exceeds it before confirmation; confirm it after a parameterized number of bars measured from its extreme. Keep alternating confirmed swings, replacing a same-direction last swing only with a more extreme one.
Calculation: Support and resistance levels = At each confirmed swing, search a parameterized window around the swing for consecutive opposite-direction candles: bearish then bullish for support, bullish then bearish for resistance. Use the lowest qualifying first close for support or highest for resistance, with the band between that close and the next open; fall back to the swing price when no qualifying body junction exists. Also create support or resistance at the previous close when the current open gaps above or below it by at least an ATR-scaled threshold; its band spans those two prices. Merge same-side levels within an ATR-scaled tolerance, adding a touch. Reject a newly created level if an intervening close already broke its price by an ATR-scaled tolerance. Limit stored levels per type. A close beyond the far band edge by an ATR-scaled tolerance flips an unflipped level's side, or deletes an already flipped level. A band intersection within an ATR-scaled tolerance counts as a touch only after sufficient departure and a touch cooldown; any such intersection makes the level unfresh. A confirmed swing turning inside an ATR-scaled miss window beyond the touch tolerance counts as a miss after sufficient departure and cooldown. Proof is touches plus misses; a new level starts with one touch.
Calculation: Reaction = The bar has positive range AND, according to the selected confirmation mode, either its low reaches the bullish level or its high reaches the bearish level within an ATR-scaled tolerance; OR the relevant rejection wick divided by range exceeds a threshold and close is beyond the level in the reaction direction; OR open and close are beyond the level in that direction, close exceeds an ATR-scaled distance from it, and the candle points in that direction or its relevant wick exceeds a parameterized fraction of the opposite wick; OR the candle points and closes beyond the level in that direction with an ATR-scaled minimum body and minimum body-to-range ratio.
Calculation: Quasimodo setup = On each confirmed chart bar examine the last five consecutive confirmed swings. For bullish setups they are low, high, shoulder low, neck high, head low; for bearish setups reverse all sides. The head must exceed the shoulder in the head direction, the earlier opposite swing must exceed the neck away from the head when the context filter is enabled, and the distance from that earlier swing to the head must meet an ATR-scaled minimum at the head. No intervening swing between shoulder and head may exceed the shoulder or neck in its respective direction. After the head and within a parameterized maximum number of bars, a close must break the neck in the setup direction by an ATR-scaled minimum; use the first such break. At creation the close must be beyond the shoulder in the setup direction. Optionally reject recently used same-side shoulder prices within an ATR-scaled tolerance. Reject a same-side shoulder within an ATR-scaled tolerance of any stored setup. Require the quality score to meet its threshold. Store the shoulder as the level, the neck as the break level, the head as the invalidation level, and an ATR-scaled zone extending from the shoulder toward the head. A new setup is fresh. Remove fresh setups exceeding their age or ATR-scaled distance limits. After a parameterized delay from the break, a fresh unflipped setup closing through its shoulder against its direction by an ATR-scaled tolerance flips direction once and resets its head to the shoulder. A fresh setup becomes unfresh on its first post-delay intersection with an ATR-scaled band around its shoulder or on invalidation; remove it if invalidated or if configured to remove unreplied retests, unless it produced a reaction on that bar. Invalidation is selectable between wick beyond head, close beyond head, wick beyond head plus an ATR buffer, or close beyond the far zone edge. Keep a parameterized maximum number of setups, removing oldest unfresh setups first.
Calculation: Quality score = Rounded, capped weighted sum of direct head sweep quality, nearby previously established same-side liquidity-pool sweep quality, first neck-break quality, unmitigated same-direction three-bar gap quality near the shoulder after the head, break-candle directional displacement, head and break volume relative to current volume average and break body-to-range ratio, shoulder-to-head RSI divergence, and higher-timeframe EMA alignment, multiplied by an ADX and ATR-regime factor. Sweep quality measures ATR-normalized depth and reclaim; pool quality additionally measures clustered pivot count. Break quality combines close-break status, ATR-normalized depth and time from head. Gap quality combines ATR-normalized gap size and freshness. The higher-timeframe alignment uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar and compares shorter-period and longer-period EMAs. Disabled quality components use their neutral values. The effective score threshold depends on the selected strictness preset.
Calculation: Active trendline = A rising support line joins two consecutive confirmed pivot lows with the newer anchor higher and above the earlier pivot's close; a falling resistance line joins two consecutive confirmed pivot highs with the newer anchor lower and below the earlier pivot's close. Pivots use body bottoms and tops respectively, or wick lows and highs when configured, with equal left and right lookbacks. Extend each line linearly until close breaks it against its support or resistance direction. For confluence use the most recently iterated active line of the relevant side, retaining its last computed value when no line remains.
Long: A bullish engulfing candle has a larger body than the preceding bearish candle AND its body is at least an ATR-scaled minimum AND its body covers the preceding body, or its entire range covers the preceding range when full-wick engulfing is selected.
Short: A bearish engulfing candle has a larger body than the preceding bullish candle AND its body is at least an ATR-scaled minimum AND its body covers the preceding body, or its entire range covers the preceding range when full-wick engulfing is selected.
Long: A fresh bullish Quasimodo setup survives its age and distance limits AND the bar is beyond the post-break delay AND its range intersects the ATR-scaled band around the shoulder AND bullish Reaction is true.
Short: A fresh bearish Quasimodo setup survives its age and distance limits AND the bar is beyond the post-break delay AND its range intersects the ATR-scaled band around the shoulder AND bearish Reaction is true.
Long: A confirmed swing low formed after the break of a fresh bullish Quasimodo setup is above its shoulder and no higher than the shoulder plus a parameterized fraction of the shoulder-to-neck distance plus an ATR-scaled tolerance AND a support level within an ATR-scaled tolerance of that swing has at least the required proof.
Short: A confirmed swing high formed after the break of a fresh bearish Quasimodo setup is below its shoulder and no lower than the shoulder minus a parameterized fraction of the shoulder-to-neck distance minus an ATR-scaled tolerance AND a resistance level within an ATR-scaled tolerance of that swing has at least the required proof.
Long: A support-level touch that qualifies for counting has proof at least the required minimum AND bullish Reaction occurs within the confirmation window AND the elapsed bars since the previous bullish level-reaction signal exceed the directional cooldown.
Short: A resistance-level touch that qualifies for counting has proof at least the required minimum AND bearish Reaction occurs within the confirmation window AND the elapsed bars since the previous bearish level-reaction signal exceed the directional cooldown.
Long: A fresh bullish Quasimodo shoulder OR a fresh support level with at least the required proof is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the active rising trendline AND the bar's range intersects that level within the same tolerance.
Short: A fresh bearish Quasimodo shoulder OR a fresh resistance level with at least the required proof is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the active falling trendline AND the bar's range intersects that level within the same tolerance.
