Calculation: Active liquidity levels = Confirmed pivot highs and lows on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Process a pivot only when its returned value changes from unavailable to available. Store high and low levels separately in insertion order. For each new pivot, first add or update its ordinary level; if it is within the equality tolerance of the previous pivot of the same side, also add or update an equal level at the higher of the two highs or lower of the two lows. An update uses the most recently stored same-side level within the equality tolerance, moves its price to the higher high or lower low, retains equal status once acquired, sets its base score to the greater of its current base score and the incoming level's base score, then adds the repeat bonus, and retains the greater volume ratio. A new level starts with the ordinary base score plus the equal bonus if applicable. Limit the number of stored levels by removing the oldest when a new one exceeds the limit. After adding levels on each chart bar, remove high levels when high >= level + sweep buffer and low levels when low <= level - sweep buffer.
Calculation: Pivot volume ratio = Pivot bar volume divided by the higher-timeframe volume SMA at pivot confirmation, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; use 1 if either value is unavailable or the SMA is zero.
Calculation: Level score = Stored base score plus an optional volume score and, when distance from close to level <= danger distance, a danger bonus. The volume score is the high-volume score if the stored volume ratio >= the upper threshold, the low-volume score if it <= the lower threshold, and otherwise the normal-volume score; it is zero when volume scoring is disabled.
Calculation: Nearest lower level = Highest active low level strictly below close.
Calculation: Nearest upper level = Lowest active high level strictly above close.
Long: Nearest lower level is unavailable OR its distance from close > danger distance OR its score < medium threshold OR its score < major threshold AND treating medium scores as trade allowed is enabled.
Short: Nearest upper level is unavailable OR its distance from close > danger distance OR its score < medium threshold OR its score < major threshold AND treating medium scores as trade allowed is enabled.
