Calculation: Candle pressure = Let range = max(true range, minimum price increment), body = close − open, upper wick = high − max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) − low, and candle range = max(high − low, minimum price increment). Buy strength = (close − low) / range × close-position weight + lower wick / candle range × wick weight + max(body / range, 0) × body weight. Sell strength = (1 − (close − low) / range) × close-position weight + upper wick / candle range × wick weight + max(−body / range, 0) × body weight. Delta = volume × (buy strength − sell strength) / (buy strength + sell strength), or zero if the denominator is not positive. Treat missing volume as zero.
Calculation: SmartFlow = Let volume Z-score = clamp((volume − SMA(volume, volume-mean lookback)) / standard deviation(volume, volume-deviation lookback), −Z-score limit, Z-score limit), or zero if the standard deviation is not positive. Weighted delta = candle pressure × (1 + Z-score × Z-score weight). Acceleration = weighted delta − previous weighted delta, using zero for a missing previous value. Flow power = weighted delta / (SMA(abs(weighted delta), flow lookback) + small positive constant). Boost = min(abs(flow power), power limit) raised to the boost exponent. Agreement weight is the matching-sign weight if weighted delta and candle pressure have equal signs, otherwise the differing-sign weight. Acceleration weight is the matching-sign weight if acceleration and weighted delta have equal signs, otherwise the differing-sign weight. SmartFlow = weighted delta × (1 + boost) × agreement weight + candle pressure × delta weight + acceleration × acceleration coefficient × acceleration weight.
Calculation: Pressure distribution = At each bar, calculate a Bollinger basis as the SMA of the selected price source and deviation as its standard deviation times the band multiplier. If unavailable, use the selected price source for the basis and max(high − low, minimum price increment) times the band multiplier for the deviation. Set channel bounds to basis ± deviation; if their difference is at most the minimum price increment, use basis ± a minimum-range multiplier times the minimum price increment. Divide max(upper bound − lower bound, minimum price increment × layer count) into equal layers. For each bar in the pressure scan, calculate its layer index using that historical bar's channel bounds and step max((upper bound − lower bound) / layer count, minimum price increment), clamping indices to the first and last layer. Allocate its SmartFlow, using zero if unavailable, either to the layer containing the selected allocation price or equally to all layers from the low's layer through the high's layer. Add a neighboring-layer fraction of each allocation to each existing adjacent layer. Sum allocations by layer index across the scan. For each resulting layer, take the absolute value of its sum and locate its midpoint using the current bar's channel and layer step. Bull pressure is the sum for layer midpoints below the current close; bear pressure is the sum for layer midpoints at or above the current close.
Long: Bull pressure > bear pressure
Short: Bear pressure > bull pressure
