Calculation: Higher-timeframe levels = highs and lows of the last completed bars of two higher timeframes, one longer than the other.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < lowest low of the preceding sweep-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low, OR low < either higher-timeframe low AND close > that low. Bearish sweep = high > highest high of the preceding sweep-lookback bars AND close < that highest high, OR high > either higher-timeframe high AND close < that high.
Calculation: Sweep direction = direction of the most recent sweep within the setup window; a new sweep clears the opposite direction, and a bearish sweep takes precedence if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Directional displacement = a candle in the sweep direction whose absolute body is at least the minimum body size AND whose body divided by its high-low range is at least the minimum body fraction; a zero-range candle has body fraction zero. Record its bar only if it occurs after a sweep of the same direction while that sweep is within the setup window. A sweep clears the recorded displacement of the opposite direction.
Calculation: Directional fair value gap = for bullish direction, current low > high two bars earlier, with bottom equal to that earlier high and top equal to current low; for bearish direction, current high < low two bars earlier, with bottom equal to current high and top equal to that earlier low. Record the latest gap of each direction only when it occurs after a sweep of that direction while that sweep is within the setup window. A sweep clears the recorded gap of the opposite direction. A gap is valid when recorded, its direction is the current sweep direction, and its age is at most the gap validity window.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of one point for each true condition: bar opening time in America/New_York falls within any configured inclusive session window; current low <= any higher-timeframe level plus a proximity buffer AND current high >= that level minus the buffer; a current sweep direction exists; recorded displacement in the current sweep direction is within the setup window; a valid gap exists in the current sweep direction; directional retracement condition; directional gap pullback condition; directional lower-timeframe structure break. For the retracement condition, use the highest high and lowest low over a lookback including the current bar, require their range > 0, and require close between the two configured retracement fractions of that range measured down from the high for bullish direction or up from the low for bearish direction; also require close < open for bullish direction or close > open for bearish direction. For the gap pullback condition, require a valid directional gap AND either current low <= gap top AND current high >= gap bottom, or for bullish direction previous close < gap bottom AND current close >= gap bottom, or for bearish direction previous close > gap top AND current close <= gap top. For the structure break, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar selected for the current chart bar and the corresponding selected lower-timeframe values on the preceding chart bar: bullish requires selected close > preceding chart bar's selected high AND selected close > selected open; bearish requires selected close < preceding chart bar's selected low AND selected close < selected open.
Long: Current chart bar is complete AND sweep direction is bullish AND confluence score >= minimum score.
Short: Current chart bar is complete AND sweep direction is bearish AND confluence score >= minimum score.
