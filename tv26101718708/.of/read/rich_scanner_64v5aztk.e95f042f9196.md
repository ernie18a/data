Calculation: Bullish sweep = current low is below the lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback, with current close above that low, OR current low is below the previous completed daily low or previous completed weekly low, with current close above that level; a new bullish sweep resets the bearish sweep state.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = current high is above the highest high of the preceding sweep lookback, with current close below that high, OR current high is above the previous completed daily high or previous completed weekly high, with current close below that level; a new bearish sweep resets the bullish sweep state.
Calculation: Recent sweep = the most recent sweep direction is still within the setup context window.
Calculation: Killzone = chart-bar time in New York is 20:00–23:59, 02:00–05:00, 07:00–10:00, or 13:30–16:00.
Calculation: HTF level touch = current bar's high-low range is within the level proximity buffer of a previous completed daily or weekly high or low.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = a bullish candle whose body is at least the minimum body percentage of its range and at least the minimum body size; bearish displacement is the corresponding bearish candle.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = the current low is above the high two chart bars earlier; bearish FVG = the current high is below the low two chart bars earlier. A gap is valid while its direction's sweep is recent and it is within the FVG validity window; a new opposite-direction sweep clears it.
Calculation: OTE zone = use the highest high and lowest low over the fib leg lookback. For bullish direction, the zone spans the high minus the high-end retracement fraction of the range through the high minus the low-end retracement fraction; for bearish direction, it spans the low plus the low-end retracement fraction through the low plus the high-end retracement fraction.
Calculation: FVG pullback = price range overlaps a valid same-direction FVG, OR a bullish close crosses from below the bullish FVG bottom to at or above it, OR a bearish close crosses from above the bearish FVG top to at or below it.
Calculation: 1-minute bullish structure break = the 1-minute close is above the preceding 1-minute high and above its open; bearish structure break = the 1-minute close is below the preceding 1-minute low and below its open. Use the lower timeframe's latest available bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of eight true/false conditions: killzone; HTF level touch; recent sweep; same-direction displacement after the latest sweep and within the setup context window; valid same-direction FVG; same-direction close inside its OTE zone and against the candle direction; same-direction FVG pullback; same-direction 1-minute structure break.
Long: recent sweep is bullish AND confluence score is at least the minimum score AND the chart bar is confirmed
Short: recent sweep is bearish AND confluence score is at least the minimum score AND the chart bar is confirmed
