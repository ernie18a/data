Calculation: Rail = highest high and lowest low over a chart-timeframe lookback. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the previous bar's upper rail; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the previous bar's lower rail. For a cross, compare the previous close with the previous value of the same shifted rail. Rail trend starts neutral, becomes bullish on a bullish break, bearish on a bearish break, and otherwise retains its previous state. Apply the same calculation independently to each selected higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar and its own lookback.
Calculation: Equilibrium = let range be upper rail minus lower rail and position be clamp(((high + low + close) / 3 - lower rail) / range, 0, 1), or the midpoint fraction when range is no greater than a price increment. Average the EMA and VWMA of position over the same lookback, replacing VWMA with WMA when unavailable. Shift this average toward the midpoint by a configurable factor, clamp it between configurable symmetric inner boundaries, and map it back into the rail range. When the range is no greater than a price increment, use the rail midpoint.
Calculation: Candle measures = body percentage is absolute close minus open divided by high minus low; close position is close minus low divided by high minus low; upper and lower wick percentages are their respective wick lengths divided by high minus low. Express each as a percentage. On a zero-range bar use zero body and wick percentages and midpoint close position. Channel position is clamp((close - lower rail) / rail range, 0, 1), expressed as a percentage; use the midpoint when rail range is no greater than a price increment.
Calculation: Reversal pressure = a weighted sum of directional close position, directional rejection wick percentage, directional body percentage, participation score, directional equilibrium score, and directional channel score. Directional close position is close position for bullish pressure and its complement for bearish pressure. Directional body is full body percentage for a candle in the pressure direction and a smaller configurable fraction otherwise. Participation uses volume divided by its SMA; if unavailable use a neutral score; otherwise normalize and clamp it using one divisor and bounds when relative volume is high with a small body OR relative range is high, and another divisor and bounds otherwise. Relative range is candle range divided by its SMA over the same baseline lookback. Directional equilibrium score is a configurable ordered step function of equilibrium change divided by rail range, with the highest score when equilibrium has moved in the pressure direction for two consecutive bars; use zero normalized change when rail range is no greater than a price increment. Directional channel score is a configurable linear transformation of channel position, clamped to the score range, increasing toward the lower rail for bullish pressure and the upper rail for bearish pressure.
Calculation: Pending retest = on a permitted bullish or bearish break with a previously non-neutral rail trend, store the previous upper or lower rail and its bar, replacing any existing pending retest. Permit continuation, trend-change, and direction according to the selected configuration. Do not arm on a bar when an active plan hits its stop or target, or when an active plan already has the breakout direction. Before arming and testing each bar, cancel the pending retest if its age exceeds the expiry, its rail trend changes direction, or close moves below equilibrium for a bullish setup or above equilibrium for a bearish setup. Clear it after an entry.
Calculation: Plan risk = at retest entry, store close as entry price. Select either a structure stop beyond the entry bar's low for a long or high for a short by an ATR buffer, rounded outward to a price increment; an ATR-multiple stop distance rounded to the nearest price increment and bounded below by one increment; or a fixed price-distance stop. Structure stop distance is the entry-to-stop distance bounded below by one increment. The target distance is either a fixed price distance or the selected stop distance multiplied by a reward-to-risk parameter, rounded to the nearest price increment and bounded below by one increment. Freeze stop and target at entry. Structure entries require their stop distance to be no greater than a configurable ATR multiple. A price increment and fixed price distances are parameters.
Long: Rail trend = bullish.
Short: Rail trend = bearish.
Long: Previous rail trend is non-neutral AND close crosses above the previous upper rail.
Short: Previous rail trend is non-neutral AND close crosses below the previous lower rail.
Long: Rail trend = bearish AND no trend change on this bar AND close <= equilibrium AND channel position <= lower-zone threshold AND (equilibrium rises for two consecutive bars OR normalized equilibrium change >= bullish momentum threshold OR lower wick percentage >= rejection threshold OR (close > open AND body percentage >= body threshold) OR close position >= bullish close threshold) AND bullish reversal pressure >= pressure threshold AND bars since the last bullish or bearish warning >= cooldown, or no prior warning exists.
Short: Rail trend = bullish AND no trend change on this bar AND close >= equilibrium AND channel position >= upper-zone threshold AND (equilibrium falls for two consecutive bars OR normalized equilibrium change <= bearish momentum threshold OR upper wick percentage >= rejection threshold OR (close < open AND body percentage >= body threshold) OR close position <= bearish close threshold) AND bearish reversal pressure >= pressure threshold AND bars since the last bullish or bearish warning >= cooldown, or no prior warning exists.
Long: Pending bullish retest exists AND its age > 0 AND rail trend = bullish AND no active long plan exists AND no plan stop or target was hit on this bar AND low <= stored break level AND close > stored break level AND close > open AND close position >= bullish rejection threshold AND close > equilibrium AND stored break level > equilibrium AND (slope filter disabled OR equilibrium > previous equilibrium) AND structure stop meets its maximum-distance condition when structure risk is selected AND (rail-width filter disabled OR rail range >= long stop distance multiplied by a minimum-width parameter).
Short: Pending bearish retest exists AND its age > 0 AND rail trend = bearish AND no active short plan exists AND no plan stop or target was hit on this bar AND high >= stored break level AND close < stored break level AND close < open AND close position <= bearish rejection threshold AND close < equilibrium AND stored break level < equilibrium AND (slope filter disabled OR equilibrium < previous equilibrium) AND structure stop meets its maximum-distance condition when structure risk is selected AND (rail-width filter disabled OR rail range >= short stop distance multiplied by a minimum-width parameter).
Exit: long, low <= frozen long stop OR high >= frozen long target OR a valid bearish retest entry occurs; evaluate stop before target and evaluate both before a replacement entry, starting on the bar after entry.
Exit: short, high >= frozen short stop OR low <= frozen short target OR a valid bullish retest entry occurs; evaluate stop before target and evaluate both before a replacement entry, starting on the bar after entry.
