Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low at a bar with the specified number of bars on each side, confirmed only after the right-hand bars have completed. Use the same pivot length for trendlines and breakout confirmation; use a separate shorter length for short-term structure and a longer length for long-term structure.
Calculation: Structure direction = at each confirmed structure pivot high, set direction bullish if it exceeds the preceding pivot high or bearish if it is lower; at each confirmed structure pivot low, set direction bullish if it exceeds the preceding pivot low or bearish if it is lower. Equal pivots leave direction unchanged. Update the stored pivot even when equal; process a high before a low if both confirm on the same bar. Maintain independent directions for the short-term and long-term lengths.
Calculation: Trendline breaks = maintain separate newest-first buffers of confirmed pivot highs and lows, active resistance and support lines, and one shared broken flag initially false. Process highs before lows on each bar. On a pivot, insert it into its buffer. A resistance candidate uses the newest two highs when the newest is lower; a support candidate uses the newest two lows when the newest is higher. The candidate is eligible if the shared broken flag is true, or if the buffer has at least four pivots and both newest highs are below both preceding highs for resistance, or both newest lows are above both preceding lows for support. For an eligible candidate, create a line through the two newest pivots. Validate it by starting with its endpoint at the newest pivot and, for each chart bar from the current bar backward through the older pivot bar inclusive, moving the endpoint to that bar and subtracting the line's current slope from its endpoint price; reject resistance if that bar's low exceeds the resulting price, or support if its high is below the resulting price. On rejection clear that direction's active line and pivot buffer and set the shared broken flag true. Otherwise replace that direction's active line with the original candidate, clear its buffer, and set the shared broken flag false. After processing each direction's pivot, test its active line at its stored endpoint: an upward break occurs when current low is above the resistance endpoint; a downward break occurs when current high is below the support endpoint. On a break clear that line and its pivot buffer and set the shared broken flag true. Otherwise move its endpoint to the current bar and add its current slope to its endpoint price. At an upward break save the resistance endpoint minus padding times min(long-lookback ATR times a parameter, close times a parameter); at a downward break save the support endpoint plus that padding amount.
Calculation: Estimated delta = volume times (2 times (close minus low) divided by (high minus low) minus 1); use zero when high equals low. Delta ratio is estimated delta divided by volume, or zero when volume is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed breakout = a trendline break arms a pending breakout of its direction, replacing any earlier pending breakout of that direction. Expire it when its age exceeds a waiting-period parameter. A pending upward breakout confirms when close exceeds the latest confirmed trendline-length pivot high; a pending downward breakout confirms when close is below the latest confirmed trendline-length pivot low. Confirmation also requires the enabled displacement and delta filters. Clear the pending state when confirmation passes, even if a session or cooldown filter later prevents a tracked signal. Update confirmed pivots and arm or expire pending states before testing confirmation. Pending states are maintained even when raw-break mode is selected.
Calculation: Displacement filter = when enabled, high minus low is at least a parameter times shorter-lookback ATR, that ATR is positive, absolute close minus open divided by high minus low is at least a body-ratio parameter, with the ratio zero for a zero-range bar, and, when the directional-close option is enabled, close is above open for upward confirmation or below open for downward confirmation.
Calculation: Delta filter = when enabled, delta ratio is at least a nonnegative threshold for an upward confirmation or at most its negative for a downward confirmation; if the volume option is enabled, volume is at least a multiple of its current-bar-inclusive volume SMA and that SMA is positive; if cumulative agreement is enabled, the sum of estimated delta over the most recent min(max(bars since the latest same-direction break, 1), maximum lookback) bars is positive for upward confirmation or negative for downward confirmation. This sum includes the current bar and generally excludes the break bar.
Calculation: Tracked signal = the selected raw trendline break with enabled displacement and delta filters, or the confirmed breakout when confirmation mode is selected, AND, if the session gate is enabled, the bar opens within either configured trading window in the configured timezone, AND the number of bars since the preceding tracked signal in the same direction is at least the cooldown parameter, or no such preceding signal exists. A tracked signal records its bar for that direction and replaces the currently tracked trade; process upward before downward signals on the same bar.
Calculation: Tracked trade levels = entry is the tracked signal bar's close. Choose as the raw stop reference either the saved same-direction break level, falling back to the structure extreme if unavailable, or the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over a lookback ending on the bar before entry. Subtract a price-buffer parameter from the long reference or add it to the short reference; if the resulting stop is at or beyond entry on the wrong side, place it one price-increment parameter below long entry or above short entry. Set target at entry plus the absolute entry-to-stop distance times the selected stop-method-specific reward-to-risk parameter for a long, or entry minus that distance times the parameter for a short.
Long: An upward trendline break occurs.
Short: A downward trendline break occurs.
Long: A short-term confirmed pivot high exceeds the preceding short-term pivot high OR a short-term confirmed pivot low exceeds the preceding short-term pivot low.
Short: A short-term confirmed pivot high is below the preceding short-term pivot high OR a short-term confirmed pivot low is below the preceding short-term pivot low.
Long: Short-term structure direction is bullish OR long-term structure direction is bullish.
Short: Short-term structure direction is bearish OR long-term structure direction is bearish.
Long: A tracked upward signal occurs.
Short: A tracked downward signal occurs.
Exit: long, a tracked long trade exists AND the current bar is after its entry bar AND (low is at or below its stop OR high is at or above its target).
Exit: short, a tracked short trade exists AND the current bar is after its entry bar AND (high is at or above its stop OR low is at or below its target).
