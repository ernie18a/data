Calculation: Combined RSI = Compute RSI with the same period on each of four selected timeframes, take their normalized weighted average, then apply two successive Wilder moving averages on the chart timeframe, with the first smoothing length longer than the second. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar; for the chart timeframe, use the chart bar.
Calculation: Smoothed percentile = Wilder moving average of the percentile rank of combined RSI over a chart-bar lookback. Convert the configured lookback duration to bars using the shortest of the four selected timeframes, then clamp the resulting integer to the configured minimum and maximum lookback lengths. Percentile rank is the percentage of lookback values less than or equal to the current combined RSI.
Calculation: Regime feature = A weighted sum of smoothed percentile, the absolute change in smoothed percentile over a slope lookback, the standard deviation of combined RSI over a compression lookback, the absolute distance of combined RSI from the RSI midpoint, and the standard deviation of smoothed percentile over the same compression lookback. Substitute zero for unavailable standard deviations and midpoint distance.
Calculation: Regime = Keep a rolling window of valid regime features. Until the window contains a minimum number of observations, use the middle regime. Otherwise initialize three cluster centers to the window minimum, midpoint of minimum and maximum, and maximum. For a configured number of iterations, assign each feature to its nearest center, breaking ties toward the lower-index center, and replace each nonempty center with its assigned mean. Sort the centers and assign the current feature to its nearest center, breaking ties toward the lower center; the result is the low, middle, or high regime.
Calculation: Adaptive lower threshold = The base lower percentile threshold minus a low-regime offset in the low regime, unchanged in the middle regime, or plus a high-regime offset in the high regime, clamped to its configured lower and upper bounds.
Calculation: Adaptive upper threshold = The base upper percentile threshold plus a low-regime offset in the low regime, unchanged in the middle regime, or minus a high-regime offset in the high regime, clamped to its configured lower and upper bounds. The corresponding low-regime offsets are equal, and the corresponding high-regime offsets are equal.
Long: Smoothed percentile <= adaptive lower threshold AND combined RSI > combined RSI at the slope lookback.
Exit: long, smoothed percentile >= adaptive upper threshold AND combined RSI < combined RSI at the slope lookback.
