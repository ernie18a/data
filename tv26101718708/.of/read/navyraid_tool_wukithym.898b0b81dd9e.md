Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current bar low > high two bars earlier AND previous bar close > high two bars earlier; evaluated on chart bars and the higher timeframe’s still-forming bars
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current bar high < low two bars earlier AND previous bar close < low two bars earlier; evaluated on chart bars and the higher timeframe’s still-forming bars
Calculation: Bullish SMT divergence = exactly one of the chart instrument and comparison symbol makes a lower low than its corresponding reference period
Calculation: Bearish SMT divergence = exactly one of the chart instrument and comparison symbol makes a higher high than its corresponding reference period
Long: Bullish fair value gap OR a bearish fair value gap closes above its upper boundary on a later bar OR bullish SMT divergence between current and previous weekly periods OR bullish SMT divergence between current and previous daily cycles OR, during the New York session, exactly one instrument makes a lower low than the Asia session low OR exactly one instrument makes a lower low than the London session low (reference session extreme no more than one day old)
Short: Bearish fair value gap OR a bullish fair value gap closes below its lower boundary on a later bar OR bearish SMT divergence between current and previous weekly periods OR bearish SMT divergence between current and previous daily cycles OR, during the New York session, exactly one instrument makes a higher high than the Asia session high OR exactly one instrument makes a higher high than the London session high (reference session extreme no more than one day old)
