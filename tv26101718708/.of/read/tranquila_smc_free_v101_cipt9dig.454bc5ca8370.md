Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = high of a bar whose high is highest among that bar and an equal number of preceding and following chart bars; it becomes available after the following bars complete. Confirmed pivot low is defined analogously using lows and the lowest low. Retain the latest confirmed pivot of each kind and reset its crossed flag when a new pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Fair value gap bars = chart bars, or bars from a selected higher or lower timeframe. For historical higher-timeframe requests, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar as returned at each chart bar; for lower-timeframe requests, use the last completed intrabar within each chart bar. On a forming higher-timeframe bar in real time, use its current values. Compare the selected bar with the bar two selected-timeframe bars earlier.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high AND that pivot has not previously been crossed since confirmation.
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low AND that pivot has not previously been crossed since confirmation.
Long: A new selected-timeframe period starts AND the selected bar's low > the high two selected bars earlier AND their difference >= chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by a size threshold.
Short: A new selected-timeframe period starts AND the selected bar's high < the low two selected bars earlier AND their difference >= chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by a size threshold.
