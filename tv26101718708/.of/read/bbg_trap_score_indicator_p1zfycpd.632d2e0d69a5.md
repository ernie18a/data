Calculation: Reference high and low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over a chart-timeframe lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Delta = volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low. Average absolute delta is the SMA of absolute delta over the same lookback as average volume.
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; range = high − low. Positive delta = max(delta, 0); negative delta = max(−delta, 0).
Calculation: Proximity = the minimum of |high − reference high|, |low − reference low|, |high − previous daily high|, |low − previous daily low|, |high − previous weekly high|, |low − previous weekly low|, |high − previous higher-timeframe high|, and |low − previous higher-timeframe low|. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bars for their highs, lows, and ATR; use the last completed daily and weekly bars for their highs and lows, respectively.
Calculation: VWAP bands = session VWAP of typical price plus or minus the standard deviation of close over a chart-timeframe lookback.
Calculation: Common score = 10 if volume ≥ average volume × volume-expansion multiplier, plus 10 if proximity ≤ higher-timeframe ATR × proximity multiplier, plus 5 if volume < previous volume AND previous volume < volume two bars ago AND range < chart-timeframe ATR × narrow-range multiplier; each unmet condition contributes zero.
Calculation: Bullish score = common score plus: 15 if low < reference low AND close > reference low AND reference low − low ≤ ATR × sweep-distance multiplier AND range > 0 AND lower wick / range ≥ minimum wick ratio; 15 if bars since low < its contemporaneous reference low ≤ reclaim window AND close > current reference low; 10 if negative delta ≥ average absolute delta × delta multiplier; 15 if that delta condition AND volume ≥ average volume × volume-expansion multiplier AND max(0, open − close) ≤ ATR × progress multiplier; 10 if delta ≤ lowest delta over the divergence lookback ending on the previous bar AND close > lowest close over that same lookback; 10 if volume ≥ average volume × large-volume multiplier AND max(0, open − close) ≤ ATR × progress multiplier AND lower wick ≥ range × large-wick multiplier; 5 if (low ≤ session VWAP AND close > session VWAP) OR (low ≤ lower VWAP band AND close > lower VWAP band); 5 if close > previous high OR (close > reference low AND delta ≥ 0). Each unmet condition contributes zero. Subtract 5 if both the sweep and reclaim conditions hold AND neither the absorption nor divergence condition holds; clamp the result to the score range.
Calculation: Bearish score = common score plus: 15 if high > reference high AND close < reference high AND high − reference high ≤ ATR × sweep-distance multiplier AND range > 0 AND upper wick / range ≥ minimum wick ratio; 15 if bars since high > its contemporaneous reference high ≤ reclaim window AND close < current reference high; 10 if positive delta ≥ average absolute delta × delta multiplier; 15 if that delta condition AND volume ≥ average volume × volume-expansion multiplier AND max(0, close − open) ≤ ATR × progress multiplier; 10 if delta ≥ highest delta over the divergence lookback ending on the previous bar AND close < highest close over that same lookback; 10 if volume ≥ average volume × large-volume multiplier AND max(0, close − open) ≤ ATR × progress multiplier AND upper wick ≥ range × large-wick multiplier; 5 if (high ≥ session VWAP AND close < session VWAP) OR (high ≥ upper VWAP band AND close < upper VWAP band); 5 if close < previous low OR (close < reference high AND delta ≤ 0). Each unmet condition contributes zero. Subtract 5 if both the sweep and reclaim conditions hold AND neither the absorption nor divergence condition holds; clamp the result to the score range.
Long: Bullish score ≥ entry-score threshold.
Short: Bearish score ≥ entry-score threshold.
