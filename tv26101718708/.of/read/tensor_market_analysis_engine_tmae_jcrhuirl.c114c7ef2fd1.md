Calculation: Volatility distance = For each configured volatility dimension, calculate its current volatility measure and standardize it against the mean and standard deviation of its prior values over the primary lookback; take the square root of the mean squared standardized values. Dimensions are selected from return volatility, normalized-volume volatility, high-low-range volatility, price-volume-correlation volatility, and intrabar-position volatility.
Calculation: Volatility jump = Volatility distance exceeds the configured jump threshold scaled by sensitivity, and more than the configured cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous jump.
Calculation: Jump intensity = 1 on a volatility-jump bar; otherwise, the previous intensity multiplied by the configured decay factor.
Calculation: Volatility component = Volatility jump OR jump intensity exceeds its sensitivity-scaled activation threshold.
Calculation: Smoothed Hurst regime = Calculate Hurst as log(range of cumulative deviations from mean log return / standard deviation of log returns) divided by log of the lookback, then smooth it with an EMA. Set regime to trending when smoothed Hurst exceeds the trending threshold plus a buffer, or reverting when it falls below the mean-reversion threshold minus a buffer; otherwise retain the prior regime until the neutral interval persists beyond its configured duration, then set it to random.
Calculation: Regime component = In a trending regime, close is above the simple moving average and prior close; in a reverting regime, close is above the upper Bollinger Band or below the lower Bollinger Band; in a random regime, Bollinger Band width relative to its middle band is below the squeeze threshold and close is above the prior upper band or below the prior lower band.
Calculation: Information-flow component = True when either volume leads price or price leads volume, based on the difference between their smoothed directional flow scores exceeding the configured significance threshold after sensitivity scaling, OR volume exceeds its moving average by the sensitivity-scaled volume-spike threshold. Each flow score is binary entropy of the fraction of lagged source/target pairs whose values fall in the same bin among equal-width bins spanning each series' lookback range.
Calculation: Active component count = Number of true components among the volatility component, regime component, and information-flow component. Required count is one in aggressive mode, two in confluence mode, and three in conservative mode.
Long: Flat position AND active component count meets the configured mode's required count AND RSI is above its midpoint AND close is above its EMA AND more than the configured signal-spacing interval has elapsed since the previous long or short signal.
Short: Flat position AND active component count meets the configured mode's required count AND RSI is below its midpoint AND close is below its EMA AND more than the configured signal-spacing interval has elapsed since the previous long or short signal.
Exit: Long, bars held exceed the configured maximum holding period OR (close - entry price) / entry price exceeds the configured profit threshold OR falls below the configured loss threshold.
Exit: Short, bars held exceed the configured maximum holding period OR (entry price - close) / entry price exceeds the configured profit threshold OR falls below the configured loss threshold.
