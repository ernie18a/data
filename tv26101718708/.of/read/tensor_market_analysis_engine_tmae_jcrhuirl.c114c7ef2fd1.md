Calculation: Volatility distance = square root of the mean squared standardized deviations of the selected dimensions; each dimension is standardized against its mean and standard deviation over the preceding primary lookback, and dimensions with zero standard deviation contribute zero. The ordered dimensions are the primary-lookback standard deviations of close-to-close returns, volume divided by its primary-lookback SMA minus one, (high-low)/close, the primary-lookback close-volume correlation, and (close-(high+low)/2)/(high-low+epsilon). Use a configurable prefix of the dimensions.
Calculation: Jump = volatility distance > jump threshold × sensitivity AND bars since the previous jump > jump cooldown; initialize the previous jump bar to the first bar. Jump intensity is one on a jump and otherwise its previous value × decay, initialized to zero.
Calculation: Smoothed rescaled range = EMA of log(R/S)/log(primary lookback), where R is the range of cumulative deviations from the mean of the current and preceding log returns, ordered newest first, and S is their population standard deviation; use a neutral value when R or S is zero.
Calculation: Regime = trending when smoothed rescaled range > upper regime threshold plus hysteresis margin, reverting when it < lower regime threshold minus hysteresis margin, otherwise retain the previous regime until the consecutive bars in this middle region exceed the regime persistence period, then set it to random; initialize to random.
Calculation: Binned flow from X to Y = binary entropy of the fraction of matching bins among pairs X[i-lag] and Y[i], with arrays ordered newest first over the primary lookback. Bin each series independently by linearly scaling its own window minimum and maximum to a fixed number of bins and truncating to an integer. Return zero before primary lookback plus lag bars, when either range is zero, or when the matching fraction is zero or one. Net flow = EMA of binned flow from log(volume+1) to close-to-close returns minus EMA of binned flow in the reverse direction, using the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Active component count = sum of three Boolean components: (jump OR jump intensity > intensity threshold × sensitivity); (regime is trending AND close > close SMA AND close > previous close) OR (regime is reverting AND (close > upper Bollinger Band OR close < lower Bollinger Band)) OR (regime is random AND Bollinger Band width divided by its middle band < squeeze threshold AND (close > previous upper band OR close < previous lower band)); and (absolute net flow > flow threshold × sensitivity OR volume > volume SMA × (one + sensitivity × volume multiplier)).
Calculation: Signal gap = bars since the most recent signal of either momentum direction; initialize the previous signal bar to the first bar and update it whenever either momentum direction passes the component count and signal gap conditions.
Long: Active component count >= configured minimum component count AND RSI > midpoint AND close > close EMA AND signal gap > signal cooldown
Short: Active component count >= configured minimum component count AND RSI < midpoint AND close < close EMA AND signal gap > signal cooldown
Long: Active component count >= configured minimum component count AND RSI < midpoint AND close < close EMA AND signal gap > signal cooldown
Short: Active component count >= configured minimum component count AND RSI > midpoint AND close > close EMA AND signal gap > signal cooldown
Long: Regime = trending
Short: Regime = reverting
Long: ATR > zero AND lower wick of a prior bar > its absolute candle body × wick threshold AND current bar index > prior bar offset, for each offset up to the configured pressure lookback
Short: ATR > zero AND upper wick of a prior bar > its absolute candle body × wick threshold AND current bar index > prior bar offset, for each offset up to the configured pressure lookback
Exit: long, bars since entry > holding period OR (close - entry price)/entry price > profit threshold OR (close - entry price)/entry price < -loss threshold
Exit: short, bars since entry > holding period OR (entry price - close)/entry price > profit threshold OR (entry price - close)/entry price < -loss threshold
