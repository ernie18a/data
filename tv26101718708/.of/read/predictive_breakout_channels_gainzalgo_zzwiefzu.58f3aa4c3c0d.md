Calculation: Anchored regression channel = Start with anchor bar index 0, sample count and sums zero. On a confirmed pivot low, set the anchor to its bar and price; on a confirmed pivot high, do the same, taking precedence if both confirm together. At an anchor reset, set the regression sample to the single point (0, pivot price), omitting the intervening confirmation bars. On each other bar append (current bar index minus anchor bar index, close). Fit ordinary least squares to these points; use slope zero when its denominator is zero. Evaluate the line at the current bar's distance from the anchor. The residual is current close minus this value. The upper and lower boundaries are this value plus or minus a channel-width multiplier times the population standard deviation of the historical residual series over the current sample count, with a minimum lookback of two bars. Each historical residual retains the regression value calculated on its own bar.
Calculation: ANOVA Z-score = For the prior-bar close, low and high series, calculate each group's mean and population variance over the same lookback n. Let the grand mean be the average of the three group means, between-group mean square be n times the sum of squared deviations of the group means from the grand mean divided by 2, and within-group mean square be the sum of the three variances times (n - 1), divided by (3n - 6). The statistic is their ratio, or zero when the within-group mean square is zero. Standardize the statistic using its SMA and population standard deviation over the same lookback n.
