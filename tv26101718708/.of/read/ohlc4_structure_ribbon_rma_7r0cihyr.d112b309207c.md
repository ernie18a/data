Calculation: Ribbon averages = Wilder RMAs of OHLC4 at six strictly increasing lookbacks, R1 through R6; R7 and R8 are separate Wilder RMAs of OHLC4 with successively longer lookbacks than R6.
Calculation: Structural core = For R1 through R6, regress their values on their Wilder lags, lookback minus one; multiply the regression slope by the negative span of those lags to obtain signed body B. Divide B by ATR to obtain raw strength X. Maintain a decaying peak P = max(abs(B), prior P times peak-decay factor), initialized to abs(B). Order efficiency is abs(R1 - R6) divided by the sum of the absolute differences of adjacent ribbon averages. Guard eligibility requires X and prior X to have the same nonzero sign, abs(B)/P and order efficiency to meet their respective minimums, and abs(X) to meet a minimum. Release the guard if eligibility fails or abs(B) decreases. Otherwise, trigger it when abs(X) decreases, abs(B) does not decrease, and ATR increases. On a trigger, set the guard floor to max(abs(X), prior floor times floor-decay factor), using prior abs(X) as the floor seed if no floor exists. Set guard strength to the maximum of its decayed prior value and a capped product of normalized peak confidence, normalized order confidence, and a linear function of capped normalized percentage decline in abs(X). While eligible and not triggered, decay an existing floor and strength in the same way, keeping the floor at least abs(X); without an existing floor, strength is zero. On release, clear the floor and set strength to zero. Guarded strength is sign(X) times (abs(X) plus guard strength times max(floor - abs(X), zero)). Map its magnitude through a bounded, increasing piecewise-linear function with ordered input and output anchors, then restore its sign. Calculate independently on the chart timeframe and on the higher timeframe using its bar still forming.
Calculation: Accepted trend state = Maintain a directional lineage and an active direction, initially neutral. A bullish stack has R1 > R2 > R3 > R4 > R5 > R6; a bearish stack has all these inequalities reversed. Accept a bullish stack when the prior active direction is bullish OR prior lineage is bullish AND chart structural core is at least the positive reentry threshold OR chart structural core is at least the higher positive transfer threshold. Accept a bearish stack symmetrically using negative thresholds. An accepted stack sets both lineage and active direction to its direction; otherwise retain lineage and clear the active direction if its corresponding strict stack is no longer intact.
Calculation: Price-side ownership = For each of R1 through R8 independently, set ownership bullish when close is above the average and bearish when close is below it; retain prior ownership on equality, starting neutral.
