Calculation: Prior range high and low = highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Expansion direction = a configurable forced long or short direction, or, in automatic mode, long if the ATR-normalized close above the prior range high meets the breakout threshold and the ATR-normalized close below the prior range low does not; short if the converse holds. Otherwise, long if close and the shorter EMA are both at or above the longer EMA and the corresponding short trend does not hold; short if close and the shorter EMA are both at or below the longer EMA and the corresponding long trend does not hold; otherwise long if close is at or above the longer EMA, and short if it is below.
Calculation: Range break = the nonnegative distance from close beyond the prior range edge in the expansion direction, divided by ATR, meets or exceeds the breakout threshold.
Calculation: Close efficiency = a weighted sum of the close's fractional location within the bar in the expansion direction and the absolute candle body divided by the bar range, clamped to the score range.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average, or a neutral ratio when that average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Cooldown = there is no range break, the number of bars since the most recent range break is within the cooldown limit, and the ATR ratio is at or above its cooldown threshold.
Calculation: Noise = ATR ratio exceeds its hot-volatility threshold AND close efficiency is below its efficiency threshold.
Calculation: Displayed direction = on a range-break bar that follows a bar without a range break or whose expansion direction differs from the previous bar, save the expansion direction. While a range break, cooldown, or noise condition holds and a saved direction exists, use the saved direction; otherwise use the current expansion direction.
Long: Displayed direction equals long.
Short: Displayed direction equals short.
