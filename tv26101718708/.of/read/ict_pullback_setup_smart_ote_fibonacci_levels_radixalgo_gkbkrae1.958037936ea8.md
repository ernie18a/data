Calculation: Structural candidate = for each side, store the latest confirmed pivot low or high, its bar, and the longer-lookback ATR on its confirmation bar; until another pivot is confirmed, replace its price and bar whenever a lower low or higher high occurs, retaining the stored ATR. A pivot is confirmed after a parameterized number of bars on each side.
Calculation: Pending setup = when the corresponding setup is inactive or in stop-loss status and the candidate is at least a parameterized number of bars old and directional displacement from candidate to close divided by candidate age is at least a parameterized multiple of its stored ATR, set the origin to the candidate and the destination to the highest high for a long or lowest low for a short from one bar before the origin through the setup bar. Extend the destination on subsequent bars while the setup is active. Deactivate the setup when its origin reaches a parameterized age; a stop-loss also permits a new setup. A new setup replaces any pending setup of the same side. Its entry remains pending until touched, blocked, expired, or replaced, even if the setup becomes inactive.
Calculation: Pending entry = destination + retracement parameter × (origin − destination). Start its expiration clock on the setup bar and reset it on each destination extension. Do not test for a touch on the setup bar. On later bars, test for a touch before expiring the entry when its clock reaches the expiration parameter. For historical bars, optionally scan completed lower-timeframe bars in order: on each, calculate the entry using the previous destination and using any destination extended by that lower-timeframe bar; if the bar extends the destination and touches the new entry, use the new entry; otherwise, if it touches the old entry, use the old entry; otherwise retain any extension and continue. Without lower-timeframe data or with the check disabled, use the chart bar's updated destination if extended, otherwise its previous destination, and test the chart bar's high or low.
Calculation: Stop = origin minus a parameterized multiple of the shorter-lookback ATR at entry for a long, or origin plus that multiple for a short. Target = entry price plus that side's risk multiplied by a reward-to-risk parameter for a long, or entry price minus that amount for a short; risk is the absolute distance between entry price and stop. Fix stop and target at entry.
Long: A new long pending setup is created.
Short: A new short pending setup is created.
Long: A pending long entry is touched by a low AND the absolute distance from the resolved entry to its stop is at most a parameterized multiple of the shorter-lookback ATR AND (same-side blocking is disabled OR no long position is active).
Short: A pending short entry is touched by a high AND the absolute distance from the resolved entry to its stop is at most a parameterized multiple of the shorter-lookback ATR AND (same-side blocking is disabled OR no short position is active).
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR position age >= expiration parameter OR (opposite-entry closing is enabled AND a short entry triggers AND the long was active before the current bar's entries).
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR position age >= expiration parameter OR (opposite-entry closing is enabled AND a long entry triggers AND the short was active before the current bar's entries).
