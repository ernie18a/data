Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low below the lows of the preceding longer window and following shorter window; confirmed pivot high = a high above the highs of those windows. Use chart-timeframe bars; a pivot is available only after the following window completes.
Calculation: Displacement ATR = Average True Range with a longer lookback than the stop ATR.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = (current close − latest tracked pivot-low price) / bars since that low; bearish displacement = (latest tracked pivot-high price − current close) / bars since that high.
Calculation: Bullish origin = latest tracked pivot low; bullish destination = highest high since that origin. Bearish origin = latest tracked pivot high; bearish destination = lowest low since that origin. Update the destination whenever price extends it.
Calculation: Selected retracement level = destination + (origin − destination) × selected Fibonacci coefficient.
Calculation: Stop = origin minus (stop ATR × stop buffer) for longs; origin plus (stop ATR × stop buffer) for shorts. Stop ATR uses a shorter lookback than displacement ATR.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus (entry-to-stop distance × reward/risk multiple) for longs; entry price minus that distance multiplied by the reward/risk multiple for shorts.
Long: bars since latest tracked pivot low meet the minimum and bullish displacement meets the ATR-scaled threshold, and the long setup is inactive or previously lost; then, while its setup is active and entry is pending, low reaches or falls below the selected retracement level, the stop distance is within its ATR-scaled limit, and (same-side position blocking is disabled or no long position is open). If intrabar entry checking is enabled, resolve the touch in order using lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar; otherwise use chart-bar low. Expire the pending entry after its configured limit since the latest destination extension.
Short: bars since latest tracked pivot high meet the minimum and bearish displacement meets the ATR-scaled threshold, and the short setup is inactive or previously lost; then, while its setup is active and entry is pending, high reaches or exceeds the selected retracement level, the stop distance is within its ATR-scaled limit, and (same-side position blocking is disabled or no short position is open). If intrabar entry checking is enabled, resolve the touch in order using lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar; otherwise use chart-bar high. Expire the pending entry after its configured limit since the latest destination extension.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR position age reaches its configured limit OR an opposite short entry triggers while opposite-position closing is enabled.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR position age reaches its configured limit OR an opposite long entry triggers while opposite-position closing is enabled.
