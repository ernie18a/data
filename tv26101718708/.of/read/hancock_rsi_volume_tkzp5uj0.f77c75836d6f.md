Calculation: Volume RSI = RSI of the configured source, with positive source changes weighted by estimated buy volume and negative changes by estimated sell volume; smooth both with Wilder RMA over the same lookback. Estimate buy/sell volume from lower-timeframe bars aggregated within each chart bar: with wick weighting, allocate volume in proportion to (upper wick + body + lower wick) for the candle’s direction and (upper wick + lower wick) against it, using denominator 2×upper wick + body + 2×lower wick; without wick weighting, assign all volume to buy when close ≥ open and to sell when close < open. Use lower-timeframe bars as they form.
Calculation: RSI trend = +1 if Volume RSI > upper threshold; otherwise +1 if Volume RSI > lower threshold and prior RSI trend > 0; otherwise −1 if Volume RSI < lower threshold; otherwise −1 if Volume RSI < upper threshold and prior RSI trend < 0; otherwise carry forward the prior RSI trend. Upper and lower thresholds are symmetric around 50.
Long: RSI trend = +1
Short: RSI trend = −1
