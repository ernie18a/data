Calculation: Selected average = a selectable ALMA, EMA, VWMA, or Hull MA of close, or price-mapped TSI = EMA(close) × (1 + TSI(close, shorter smoothing length, longer smoothing length)).
Calculation: Slope = selected average minus its value a lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: KSI = true range squared divided by (volume × EMA(volume)), multiplied by a scaling parameter; use zero when the denominator is not positive. KSI signal = EMA(KSI).
Calculation: Last exit slope = slope on the bar before the bar where a closed trade is detected; initialize to zero. Last exit bar = the bar where a closed trade is detected; initialize to the first bar.
Calculation: Stop-loss fraction = a selectable fixed fraction or ATR × stop-loss multiplier / close.
Calculation: Trailing activation fraction and trailing distance fraction = selectable fixed fractions, optionally each multiplied by ATR / SMA(ATR), using a multiplier of one if either ATR or its SMA is not positive; alternatively, each fraction is its respective ATR multiplier × ATR / close. The automatic alternative overrides the fixed and volatility-adjusted alternatives.
Calculation: Long dynamic stop = entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) on the first bar with a long position; thereafter the greater of its previous value and highest high since entry × (1 − current stop-loss fraction). Short dynamic stop = entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) on the first bar with a short position; thereafter the lesser of its previous value and lowest low since entry × (1 + current stop-loss fraction).
Calculation: Trailing offset = entry price × current trailing distance fraction, rounded to the nearest price increment and floored at one price increment. Once activated, the long trailing stop is the highest price reached since activation minus the offset; the short trailing stop is the lowest price reached since activation plus the offset.
Long: Slope > 0.
Short: Slope <= 0.
Long: Position side is flat or long AND no condition exit is issued on this bar AND bars since last exit bar >= cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX >= threshold AND (rising-ADX filter disabled OR ADX > previous ADX))) AND (KSI filter off OR (avoid-spike mode AND (spike threshold <= 0 OR KSI <= spike threshold)) OR (avoid-squeeze mode AND (KSI >= squeeze threshold OR KSI signal >= squeeze threshold)) OR (strong-momentum mode AND KSI > KSI signal) OR (avoid-weakness mode AND KSI >= KSI signal)) AND ((slope-entry mode enabled AND slope crosses above zero) OR (price-cross-entry mode enabled AND close crosses above selected average) OR ((slope-entry mode enabled OR price-cross-entry mode enabled) AND mid-trend re-entry enabled AND slope > 0 AND slope > last exit slope AND slope > previous slope AND (acceleration filter disabled OR slope has increased on each of the required consecutive bars))).
Short: Position side is flat or short AND no condition exit is issued on this bar AND bars since last exit bar >= cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX >= threshold AND (rising-ADX filter disabled OR ADX > previous ADX))) AND (KSI filter off OR (avoid-spike mode AND (spike threshold <= 0 OR KSI <= spike threshold)) OR (avoid-squeeze mode AND (KSI >= squeeze threshold OR KSI signal >= squeeze threshold)) OR (strong-momentum mode AND KSI > KSI signal) OR (avoid-weakness mode AND KSI >= KSI signal)) AND ((slope-entry mode enabled AND slope crosses below zero) OR (price-cross-entry mode enabled AND close crosses below selected average) OR ((slope-entry mode enabled OR price-cross-entry mode enabled) AND mid-trend re-entry enabled AND slope < 0 AND slope < last exit slope AND slope < previous slope AND (acceleration filter disabled OR slope has decreased on each of the required consecutive bars))).
Exit: long, (reverse-average exit enabled AND close crosses below selected average) OR (reverse-VWMA exit enabled AND close crosses below exit VWMA) OR (slope exit enabled AND (slope crosses below zero OR slope > 0 AND slope has decreased on each of the required consecutive bars)) OR (KSI spike exit enabled AND spike threshold > 0 AND KSI > spike threshold).
Exit: short, (reverse-average exit enabled AND close crosses above selected average) OR (reverse-VWMA exit enabled AND close crosses above exit VWMA) OR (slope exit enabled AND (slope crosses above zero OR slope < 0 AND slope has increased on each of the required consecutive bars)) OR (KSI spike exit enabled AND spike threshold > 0 AND KSI > spike threshold).
Exit: long, (stop-loss enabled AND low <= long dynamic stop) OR (take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND price has reached entry price × (1 + trailing activation fraction) AND subsequent price <= long trailing stop).
Exit: short, (stop-loss enabled AND high >= short dynamic stop) OR (take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction)) OR (trailing stop enabled AND price has reached entry price × (1 − trailing activation fraction) AND subsequent price >= short trailing stop).
