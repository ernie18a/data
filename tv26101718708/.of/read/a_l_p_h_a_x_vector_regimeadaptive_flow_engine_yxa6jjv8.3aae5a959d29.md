Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: KAMA = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close
Calculation: Balance bands = moving average of close ± a configurable multiple of close standard deviation, using the same lookback
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA of volume weighted by close position within the bar’s range minus EMA of the remaining volume; use the same smoothing lookback
Calculation: Flow Vector = sum of signed component scores for EMA alignment (fast, intermediate, and slow EMAs, with partial alignment based on close vs. intermediate EMA), KAMA slope, OBV slope, volume delta, higher-timeframe EMA bias, and close vs. KAMA; cap the result to its configured range. OBV slope compares OBV now with OBV at a configurable earlier bar.
Calculation: Market regime = Chop if Choppiness Index is at or above its configured ceiling, or if Efficiency Ratio is at or below its configured chop ceiling and ADX is below its configured trend minimum; otherwise Trend Bull if ADX and Efficiency Ratio meet their configured trend minima, Flow Vector is positive beyond its directional threshold, and close is above KAMA; Trend Bear is the mirrored condition; otherwise Balance. Efficiency Ratio is absolute net close change divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over its lookback; Choppiness Index is 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over its lookback ÷ high-low range over that lookback) ÷ log10(lookback).
Calculation: Bull pin bar = close > open, lower wick ≥ configured fraction of bar range, upper wick ≤ configured fraction, body ≥ configured fraction of ATR, and close in the configured upper portion of the bar range. Bear pin bar is the mirror condition.
Calculation: HTF bias = bullish when higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA and higher-timeframe close > fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Confluence score = 2 points for a trend-pullback or impulse setup, 1 point for a fade or vector-shift setup, plus 1 each for flow accelerating in the signal direction over two consecutive comparisons, aligned HTF bias, volume meeting its configured average-volume requirement, non-Chop regime, and RSI being in the signal’s acceptable zone.
Long: (Trend Bull regime AND Flow Vector ≥ configured strong-bull threshold AND Flow Vector rising on two consecutive comparisons AND (low ≤ EMA(8) + ATR-scaled tolerance AND close > EMA(8) OR low ≤ KAMA + ATR-scaled tolerance AND close > KAMA) AND bull pin bar AND lowest low over the recent pullback lookback ≤ min(EMA(8), KAMA) + ATR-scaled tolerance AND close − KAMA ≤ configured ATR-scaled extension AND RSI is within the configured long pullback range) OR (Trend Bull or Balance regime AND close > highest high of the preceding micro-swing lookback AND prior close ≤ that high AND body ≥ configured ATR-scaled impulse minimum AND Flow Vector > configured positive threshold AND volume delta > 0 AND bull pin bar AND HTF bias bullish when the HTF filter is enabled AND close − KAMA ≤ configured ATR-scaled extension) OR (Balance regime AND low ≤ lower balance band + ATR-scaled tolerance AND (Flow Vector ≤ configured extreme-bear threshold OR Flow Vector rising after recently being below a configured negative threshold) AND bull pin bar AND RSI < configured fade threshold) OR (not Chop AND Flow Vector crosses above zero AND bull pin bar with body ≥ configured ATR-scaled shift minimum AND volume delta > 0 AND HTF bias bullish when the HTF filter is enabled); AND confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND session filter passes when enabled AND volume requirement passes when enabled AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: (Trend Bear regime AND Flow Vector ≤ negative configured strong-bear threshold AND Flow Vector falling on two consecutive comparisons AND (high ≥ EMA(8) − ATR-scaled tolerance AND close < EMA(8) OR high ≥ KAMA − ATR-scaled tolerance AND close < KAMA) AND bear pin bar AND highest high over the recent pullback lookback ≥ max(EMA(8), KAMA) − ATR-scaled tolerance AND KAMA − close ≤ configured ATR-scaled extension AND RSI is within the configured short pullback range) OR (Trend Bear or Balance regime AND close < lowest low of the preceding micro-swing lookback AND prior close ≥ that low AND body ≥ configured ATR-scaled impulse minimum AND Flow Vector < negative configured threshold AND volume delta < 0 AND bear pin bar AND HTF bias bearish when the HTF filter is enabled AND KAMA − close ≤ configured ATR-scaled extension) OR (Balance regime AND high ≥ upper balance band − ATR-scaled tolerance AND (Flow Vector ≥ configured extreme-bull threshold OR Flow Vector falling after recently being above a configured positive threshold) AND bear pin bar AND RSI > configured fade threshold) OR (not Chop AND Flow Vector crosses below zero AND bear pin bar with body ≥ configured ATR-scaled shift minimum AND volume delta < 0 AND HTF bias bearish when the HTF filter is enabled); AND confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND session filter passes when enabled AND volume requirement passes when enabled AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
