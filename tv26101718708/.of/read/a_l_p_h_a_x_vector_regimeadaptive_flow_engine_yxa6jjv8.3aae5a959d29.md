Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Choppiness index = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar true ranges over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback); use a neutral fallback if the range is zero.
Calculation: Adaptive line = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close, using its own efficiency-ratio lookback and faster and slower smoothing periods; initialize with close.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA(volume × (close − low) / (high − low)) − EMA(volume − volume × (close − low) / (high − low)), with the same smoothing length; use half of volume for each component when the range is zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close > fast EMA; bearish bias reverses both comparisons. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical decisions; the higher-timeframe bar still forming can be used in real time.
Calculation: Flow score = sum of six signed components: short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA gives the strongest positive stack weight, the reversed stack gives its negative, otherwise close above or below the medium EMA gives a smaller positive or negative weight; adaptive line rising or falling gives a positive or negative slope weight; OBV above or below its value a configured lookback ago gives a positive or negative OBV weight; volume delta above or below zero gives a positive or negative delta weight; higher-timeframe bullish or bearish bias gives a positive or negative higher-timeframe weight; close above or below the adaptive line gives a positive or negative price weight. A neutral component contributes zero. The full-stack weight is greatest; the slope and higher-timeframe weights are equal and next; the OBV and price weights are equal and next; the delta weight is next; the partial-stack weight is smallest. Clamp the sum to symmetric score limits.
Calculation: Chop = choppiness index >= its maximum OR (efficiency ratio <= its chop threshold AND ADX < its trend threshold). Bull trend = chop is false AND ADX >= its trend threshold AND efficiency ratio >= its higher trend threshold AND flow score > a positive trend threshold AND close > adaptive line. Bear trend reverses the flow and price comparisons. Balance = chop, bull trend, and bear trend are all false.
Calculation: Bull candle = close > open AND close − low >= a minimum fraction of high − low AND high − close <= a maximum fraction of high − low AND absolute close − open >= a minimum ATR multiple AND close >= high − a maximum fraction of high − low. Bear candle = close < open AND high − close >= the minimum fraction of high − low AND close − low <= the maximum fraction of high − low AND absolute close − open >= the minimum ATR multiple AND close <= low + the maximum fraction of high − low.
Calculation: Bull acceleration = flow score > previous flow score AND previous flow score >= flow score two bars ago. Bear acceleration reverses both comparisons.
Calculation: Bull pullback = bull trend AND flow score >= positive strong threshold AND bull acceleration AND ((low <= short EMA + touch tolerance × ATR AND close > short EMA) OR (low <= adaptive line + touch tolerance × ATR AND close > adaptive line)) AND bull candle AND lowest low over the recent dip lookback <= min(short EMA, adaptive line) + dip tolerance × ATR AND close − adaptive line <= extension limit × ATR AND RSI > long lower bound AND RSI < long upper bound. Bear pullback reverses directions, uses the recent highest high and max(short EMA, adaptive line), and uses the short RSI bounds.
Calculation: Bull impulse = (bull trend OR balance) AND close > highest high over the preceding swing lookback AND previous close <= that same highest high AND candle body >= impulse body threshold × ATR × impulse discount AND flow score > strong threshold × impulse flow fraction AND volume delta > zero AND bull candle AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish bias) AND close − adaptive line <= extension limit × ATR. Bear impulse reverses directions and uses the preceding lowest low.
Calculation: Bull fade = balance AND low <= lower Bollinger band + band tolerance × ATR AND (flow score <= negative extreme threshold OR (flow score > previous flow score AND previous flow score < negative extreme threshold + fade offset)) AND bull candle AND RSI < lower fade threshold. Bear fade = balance AND high >= upper Bollinger band − band tolerance × ATR AND (flow score >= positive extreme threshold OR (flow score < previous flow score AND previous flow score > positive extreme threshold − fade offset)) AND bear candle AND RSI > higher fade threshold.
Calculation: Bull shift = chop is false AND flow score > zero AND previous flow score <= zero AND bull candle AND candle body >= shift body threshold × ATR AND volume delta > zero AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish bias). Bear shift reverses these directional comparisons.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = two units if that direction's enabled pullback or impulse is true, otherwise one unit if its enabled fade or shift is true, otherwise zero; add one unit each for same-direction acceleration, the higher-timeframe filter being disabled or aligned, the volume filter being disabled or volume >= its SMA × volume multiplier, chop being false, and RSI strictly between that direction's bounds.
Long: Any enabled bull pullback, impulse, fade, or shift is true AND bull confluence score >= minimum AND chop is false AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within the configured session) AND bars since the last signal of either direction >= cooldown, or no previous signal exists.
Short: Any enabled bear pullback, impulse, fade, or shift is true AND bear confluence score >= minimum AND chop is false AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within the configured session) AND bars since the last signal of either direction >= cooldown, or no previous signal exists.
Long: Flow score > zero.
Short: Flow score < zero.
Long: Volume delta > zero.
Short: Volume delta <= zero.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish bias is true.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish bias is true.
Long: Bull trend is true.
Short: Bear trend is true.
Long: Bull confluence score >= minimum AND chop is false.
Short: Bear confluence score >= minimum AND chop is false.
Exit: long, chop changes from false to true.
Exit: short, chop changes from false to true.
