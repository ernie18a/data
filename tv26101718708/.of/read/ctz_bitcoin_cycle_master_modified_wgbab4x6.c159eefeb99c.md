Calculation: StochRSI %K = SMA of the stochastic oscillator of RSI of close; %D = SMA of %K.
Calculation: Bullish StochRSI event = %K crosses above %D AND the lowest %K over a lookback is below the oversold threshold. Bearish StochRSI event = %K crosses below %D AND the highest %K over the same lookback is above the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Cycle-low confirmation = for each of a shorter and a longer symmetric pivot-low lookback, maintain a separate candidate. On pivot confirmation, replace the candidate if none exists, the pivot is at least that tier's minimum bar gap after the preceding candidate, or its low is below the preceding candidate's low. Reset two flags on replacement; starting on that same bar, latch one flag when close > confirmation EMA and the other when a bullish StochRSI event occurs. Confirm once when both flags are set. The longer pivot lookback uses the larger minimum gap. Retain the longer-tier confirmed pivot's bar, time and low.
Calculation: Cycle-high confirmation = apply the same two-tier candidate and flag procedure to symmetric pivot highs: replace when the minimum gap is met or the new high is above the preceding candidate's high; latch close < confirmation EMA and a bearish StochRSI event. Confirm once when both flags are set. Use the same respective pivot lookbacks and gaps as the cycle-low confirmations.
Calculation: Calendar low = track the lowest low and its bar and time in each calendar year, replacing it only on a strictly lower low. On the first bar of a new year, finalize the preceding year's low unless its time is within the minimum spacing after the previous finalized low; in that case replace the previous finalized low only if the preceding year's low is lower. Start tracking the new year's low on that bar. Once per year, after the tracked low's bar, latch confirmation when close > confirmation EMA AND a bullish StochRSI event occurs; do not reset this latch if the tracked low subsequently changes.
Calculation: Long-cycle low = retain confirmed longer-tier cycle lows whose pivot times are on or after an anchor date. Starting after the anchor date plus a settlement delay, select the retained low nearest the anchor; if any retained low lies within the snap interval around the anchor, select the lowest of those instead. Set its time as the last long-cycle low and initialize the target duration from a parameter. After last low time + target duration + settlement delay, select the retained low nearest that target whose time is later than the last low; if the lowest retained low within the snap interval of that selection is later than the last low, select it instead. Set the window end to target + snap interval and set the target duration to the rounded average of elapsed durations between successive selected lows. While the window end exists and current time <= window end, recompute the target from the current last-low time and updated target duration; replace the selection if the lowest retained low within the snap interval of that target is lower than the selected low and has a different time, and revise the most recent duration in the average. The window end is never reset.
Calculation: Provisional long-cycle low = after the first long-cycle low is selected and before the long-cycle window end exists, track the lowest chart-bar low in the interval from the current target minus the snap interval through the current target plus the settlement delay; initialize on the first bar in the interval and replace only on a strictly lower low.
Calculation: Cycle translation = after a longer-tier cycle low confirms, track the highest high from the confirmation bar onward, resetting on each new confirmation. Divide the number of bars from the confirmed pivot-low bar to the tracked highest-high bar by the expected cycle length in bars; cycle age is the number of bars since that pivot-low bar.
Long: A shorter-tier OR longer-tier cycle low confirms.
Long: The first chart bar of a calendar year occurs OR the current low is below the previously tracked calendar-year low OR the calendar-year low's confirmation latch first becomes set OR the preceding year's calendar low is finalized or replaces a higher finalized low.
Long: A long-cycle low is selected or replaced OR the provisional long-cycle low is initialized or decreases.
Long: Close > confirmation EMA.
Long: Shorter trend EMA > longer trend EMA.
Long: Cycle age >= a fraction of expected cycle length AND cycle translation >= a higher fraction.
Short: A shorter-tier OR longer-tier cycle high confirms.
Short: Close <= confirmation EMA.
Short: Shorter trend EMA <= longer trend EMA.
Short: Cycle age >= a fraction of expected cycle length AND cycle translation <= a lower fraction.
