Calculation: Profile = the running chart-bar high and low within a configurable daily session in a configurable time zone; the profile is ready from the first bar after that session until the next session starts.
Calculation: Support lower and upper edges = profile low minus and plus ATR multiplied by a zone-width parameter; resistance lower and upper edges = profile high minus and plus the same amount. Recalculate edges on every chart bar.
Calculation: Recent delta = sum of chart-bar deltas over a configurable lookback. Each chart-bar delta is the sum of signed volume of its completed lower-timeframe bars: assign positive volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open; if close = open, compare close with the previous lower-timeframe close, and if still equal, use the previous lower-timeframe bar's assigned sign. Assign zero if no sign is available.
Calculation: Pending states = maintain separate states for CVD support bounce, support bounce, support rejection, CVD resistance rejection, resistance rejection, and resistance bounce. On a confirmed chart bar with a ready profile, arm the relevant states when the bar overlaps the zone and the previous close is above the current upper edge for a bounce or below the current lower edge for a rejection. Reset that state's age to zero whenever it is armed, then increment the age on the same bar and each subsequent bar. Before evaluating signals, clear a state if its age exceeds the pending timeout, or if close is below the current lower edge for a bounce or above the current upper edge for a rejection. Clear each state after its own signal. Preserve pending states across profile starts. Each signal type has its own cooldown: allow it if it has never occurred or more than the cooldown number of bars have elapsed since its last occurrence.
Calculation: Breakout state = reset at each profile start. On each confirmed chart bar with a ready profile, set the state to up and record the current bar when close > resistance upper edge AND (close - resistance upper edge) / resistance upper edge >= breakout threshold; set it to down and record the current bar when close < support lower edge AND (support lower edge - close) / support lower edge >= breakout threshold. Repeated qualifying bars replace the recorded bar.
Long: CVD support bounce pending AND close > support upper edge AND recent delta >= positive delta threshold AND its cooldown permits.
Short: CVD resistance rejection pending AND close < resistance lower edge AND recent delta <= negative delta threshold AND its cooldown permits.
Long: Support bounce pending AND close crosses above support upper edge AND its cooldown permits.
Short: Support rejection pending AND close crosses below support lower edge AND its cooldown permits.
Long: Resistance bounce pending AND close crosses above resistance upper edge AND its cooldown permits.
Short: Resistance rejection pending AND close crosses below resistance lower edge AND its cooldown permits.
Long: Profile ready AND close > resistance upper edge AND (close - resistance upper edge) / resistance upper edge >= breakout threshold.
Short: Profile ready AND close < support lower edge AND (support lower edge - close) / support lower edge >= breakout threshold.
Long: Breakout state = up AND current bar is later than the recorded breakout bar AND high >= resistance upper edge AND low <= resistance upper edge AND close > resistance upper edge.
Short: Breakout state = down AND current bar is later than the recorded breakout bar AND high >= support lower edge AND low <= support lower edge AND close < support lower edge.
Exit: long, high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction) OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit fraction) OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction).
