Calculation: Reference MA = a selected EMA, SMA, WMA, VWMA, or Hull MA of close; for Hull MA, use WMA(2 × WMA(close, max(minimum period, half the MA period)) − WMA(close, MA period), max(minimum period, floor(sqrt(MA period)))).
Calculation: Candidate distance = a candidate fixed price distance, or a candidate multiplier times current ATR, or a candidate multiplier times current close standard deviation. Standard deviation uses a lookback equal to the greater of the reference MA period and a minimum period. Candidates are ordered by increasing distance or multiplier.
Calculation: Directional event statistics = maintain separate histories for each direction and candidate. On a confirmed bar with a defined positive candidate distance, if no event for that direction and candidate is active and the directional distance from the reference MA is at least the candidate distance, start an event at close and increment occurrences. On each subsequent confirmed bar with a defined positive candidate distance, update maximum adverse and favorable close excursions from the event entry, floored at zero. A long event succeeds when close >= reference MA; a short event succeeds when close <= reference MA. On success, increment returns, add its adverse and favorable excursions to their respective sums, and end the event. Otherwise end it without adding excursions when its elapsed bars reach the timeout. An event cannot restart on the bar it ends. Occurrences include active and timed-out events. Win rate = returns / occurrences; average adverse excursion = successful-event adverse excursion sum / occurrences; average favorable excursion = successful-event favorable excursion sum / returns; use zero for a zero denominator. Expectancy = win rate × average favorable excursion − (1 − win rate) × average adverse excursion.
Calculation: Optimal directional distance = current candidate distance for the first candidate, in ascending candidate order, having the highest expectancy among candidates with occurrences >= minimum occurrences, win rate >= minimum win rate, and expectancy > minimum expectancy; undefined if no candidate qualifies. Update event statistics before selecting the candidate on each confirmed bar.
Calculation: Common filter = (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume) AND (session filter disabled OR chart bar's first lower-timeframe bar opens within the configured session in the configured time zone) AND (minimum ATR filter disabled OR ATR >= minimum ATR).
Long: Confirmed chart bar count >= minimum history AND optimal long distance is defined AND common filter AND (trend filter disabled OR EMA of close − its value a configured number of bars ago >= −EMA of close × configured slope fraction) AND reference MA − close >= optimal long distance.
Short: Confirmed chart bar count >= minimum history AND optimal short distance is defined AND common filter AND (trend filter disabled OR EMA of close − its value a configured number of bars ago <= EMA of close × configured slope fraction) AND close − reference MA >= optimal short distance.
