Calculation: Historical threshold statistics = For each of 15 ascending distance thresholds and each side, record an occurrence when a confirmed close is at least that far below (long) or above (short) the selected moving average. From the occurrence close, track the largest adverse and favorable close-based excursions until a confirmed close reaches the moving average or the configured timeout expires. Count a return only if the moving average is reached before timeout; occurrences that time out count as failures. Calculate win rate as returns divided by occurrences, average MAE as summed returned-event MAE divided by all occurrences, and average MFE as summed returned-event MFE divided by returns. Expectancy = win rate × average MFE − (1 − win rate) × average MAE.
Calculation: Optimal long/short threshold = For each side, choose the threshold with the highest expectancy among those meeting the configured minimum occurrence count, minimum win rate, and minimum expectancy; ties select the smallest threshold. Candidate distances use the configured mode: fixed price points, ATR multiples, or standard-deviation multiples.
Long: Confirmed close is at or below selected moving average − optimal long threshold AND minimum history is met AND an optimal long threshold exists AND, if enabled, volume exceeds its configured average AND the bar is in the configured US regular session AND ATR meets its configured minimum AND EMA50 slope is not below its configured negative trend limit AND the same long condition was false on the prior bar.
Short: Confirmed close is at or above selected moving average + optimal short threshold AND minimum history is met AND an optimal short threshold exists AND, if enabled, volume exceeds its configured average AND the bar is in the configured US regular session AND ATR meets its configured minimum AND EMA50 slope is not above its configured positive trend limit AND the same short condition was false on the prior bar.
