Long: Short-period EMA of close crosses above long-period EMA of close.
Long: High exceeds the highest high of the preceding Donchian lookback.
Short: Short-period EMA of close crosses below long-period EMA of close.
Short: Low falls below the lowest low of the preceding Donchian lookback.
Exit: Long, low is at or below entry price minus an ATR multiple calculated at entry OR high is at or above entry price plus a target multiple of that ATR stop distance.
Exit: Short, high is at or above entry price plus an ATR multiple calculated at entry OR low is at or below entry price minus a target multiple of that ATR stop distance.
