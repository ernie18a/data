Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 0 when SMA(volume) <= 0; the SMA includes the current bar.
Calculation: Prior high = highest high over a lookback ending at the previous bar.
Calculation: Gap state = on the first chart bar of each new exchange-calendar day, compare that bar's open with the previous chart bar's close. If their absolute percentage difference relative to the previous close meets the minimum gap threshold, activate the gap, set its top and bottom to the higher and lower of those prices, record its direction, and reset partial fill to false; otherwise deactivate the gap. On every bar with an active gap, calculate fill depth as (gap top - low) / (gap top - gap bottom) for a gap up, or (high - gap bottom) / (gap top - gap bottom) for a gap down. Set partial fill to true when fill depth reaches the partial-fill threshold, and deactivate the gap when fill depth reaches the full-fill threshold, before evaluating signals. Partial fill remains true until the next qualifying gap.
