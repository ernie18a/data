Calculation: Volume z-score = (current volume − rolling mean volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume; use 0 when the standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Real-surge probability = sigmoid of the weighted sum of bias 1, volume z-score clamped to [−4, 4], absolute candle body / ATR clamped to [−4, 4], (2 × body/range ratio − 1) clamped to [−1, 1], and one-bar close return / ATR clamped to [−4, 4]. Initialize all weights to 0. For each raw surge, buffer its features, candle direction, and close; after the follow-through horizon, label it 1 if its close move in the buffered candle direction is at least the required ATR multiple, otherwise 0, using ATR at the labeling bar. Update each weight by learning rate × (label − old probability) × its buffered feature. Calculate the current probability using the weights before that bar’s updates.
Long: Volume z-score ≥ surge threshold AND body/range ratio ≥ minimum ratio AND close > open AND (AI classifier disabled OR real-surge probability ≥ probability threshold)
Short: Volume z-score ≥ surge threshold AND body/range ratio ≥ minimum ratio AND close < open AND (AI classifier disabled OR real-surge probability ≥ probability threshold)
