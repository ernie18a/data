Calculation: Supertrend direction = For each ATR length and multiplier, set basic upper and lower bands to (high + low) / 2 plus and minus multiplier × ATR. Set the upper band to the basic upper band if the previous upper band is unavailable, the basic upper band is lower than the previous upper band, or the previous close is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Set the lower band to the basic lower band if the previous lower band is unavailable, the basic lower band is higher than the previous lower band, or the previous close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Initialize direction bullish; change bearish to bullish when close > upper band, and bullish to bearish when close < lower band. Grid ATR is Wilder-smoothed true range initialized with the first true range; manual ATR is standard ATR.
Calculation: Grid selection = Calculate Supertrend directions for a rectangular grid of increasing ATR lengths and increasing multipliers. Within a learning window ending at the final dataset bar, initialize each grid cell's simulated entry at the first window close. On every subsequent direction change, close the previous-direction trade at close, record its percentage return, and enter the new direction at close. Count zero returns as losses. Score each cell only after a minimum number of closed trades, using a selected metric: win rate, net profit, profit factor, trade count, average return, average win divided by average loss, gross profit, win count, or win count divided by loss count. Gross profit is the sum of positive returns and gross loss is the absolute sum of nonpositive returns. For a ratio with a zero denominator, use the script's fixed positive fallback when its numerator is positive, otherwise zero. Select the first highest-scoring cell as best, defaulting to the first grid cell when none qualifies. Select the first interior cell with the highest mean score across its complete immediate neighboring square including itself as stable; if no such square has scores for every cell, use best. The active direction is the selected best or stable cell's current direction, or the separately calculated manual Supertrend direction. Re-select on each bar; previous active direction means the active direction recorded on the previous bar, even if the selected cell changed.
Calculation: Agreement = percentage of grid Supertrend directions equal to the current active direction.
Calculation: Long momentum score = count of true conditions: close > fast momentum EMA, fast momentum EMA > slow momentum EMA, RSI >= long threshold, close > previous close, and volume confirmation disabled OR volume >= volume SMA. The fast momentum EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow momentum EMA.
Calculation: Short momentum score = count of true conditions: close < fast momentum EMA, fast momentum EMA < slow momentum EMA, RSI <= short threshold, close < previous close, and volume confirmation disabled OR volume >= volume SMA.
Calculation: Volatility percentage = 100 × manual-length ATR / close, or zero when close is zero.
Long: Active direction = bullish AND at least one grid cell has a valid score AND agreement >= minimum agreement AND volatility percentage >= minimum AND volatility percentage <= maximum AND (momentum filter disabled OR long momentum score >= minimum score).
Short: Active direction = bearish AND at least one grid cell has a valid score AND agreement >= minimum agreement AND volatility percentage >= minimum AND volatility percentage <= maximum AND (momentum filter disabled OR short momentum score >= minimum score).
Long: ((Active direction = bullish AND previous active direction = bearish) OR (pullback entries enabled AND active direction = bullish AND previous active direction = bullish AND close crosses above fast momentum EMA AND close > slow momentum EMA)) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend EMA) AND (momentum filter disabled OR long momentum score >= minimum score) AND at least one grid cell has a valid score AND agreement >= minimum agreement AND volatility percentage >= minimum AND volatility percentage <= maximum AND (no prior long or short signal OR bars since the most recent such signal > cooldown).
Short: ((Active direction = bearish AND previous active direction = bullish) OR (pullback entries enabled AND active direction = bearish AND previous active direction = bearish AND close crosses below fast momentum EMA AND close < slow momentum EMA)) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend EMA) AND (momentum filter disabled OR short momentum score >= minimum score) AND at least one grid cell has a valid score AND agreement >= minimum agreement AND volatility percentage >= minimum AND volatility percentage <= maximum AND (no prior long or short signal OR bars since the most recent such signal > cooldown).
