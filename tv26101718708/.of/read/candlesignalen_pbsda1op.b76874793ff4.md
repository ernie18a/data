Calculation: Order blocks = On each chart bar, first remove bullish blocks when close is below their bottom and bearish blocks when close is above their top; optionally use low and high respectively instead of close. Then create a bullish block spanning the previous bar's low to high when its close < open AND the current close > previous high AND, if displacement is enabled, current close − open > ATR × displacement multiplier. Create a bearish block spanning the previous bar's low to high when its close > open AND current close < previous low AND, if displacement is enabled, current open − close > ATR × displacement multiplier. Retain at most a configured number of the newest blocks per side. Blocks are disabled when order-block detection is disabled.
Calculation: Near a block = price >= block bottom − current ATR × proximity multiplier AND price <= block top + current ATR × proximity multiplier.
Calculation: Touching a block = bar high >= block bottom − that bar's ATR × proximity multiplier AND bar low <= block top + that bar's ATR × proximity multiplier, using blocks active on that bar.
Calculation: Pattern state = On the first confirmed bar, set C1 to that bar. On the next confirmed bar, set a long setup if low < C1 low AND high <= C1 high AND close >= C1 low AND close <= C1 high; set a short setup if high > C1 high AND low >= C1 low AND close >= C1 low AND close <= C1 high. If neither setup occurs, replace C1 with that bar and repeat. Test the setup only on the following bar. After that bar, reset to the initial state if its breakout and moving-average or order-block confirmation conditions hold, regardless of position or warning suppression; otherwise replace C1 with that bar and repeat. Remember whether C1 or the setup bar touched a matching block at the time of that bar.
Calculation: Trend moving average = SMA of a selected bar-price source on the chart timeframe or a configurable other timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar on historical chart bars; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Trade levels = On a long signal, store C1 high as the reference entry and C1 low − signal-bar ATR × stop multiplier as the stop; on a short signal, store C1 low as the reference entry and C1 high + signal-bar ATR × stop multiplier as the stop. The target is reference entry plus, for long, or minus, for short, the absolute reference-entry-to-stop distance × reward-to-risk multiplier. A new same-side signal replaces the stored levels. Start testing them on the next bar.
Long: Long setup AND current high >= C1 high + current ATR × breakout multiplier AND position is flat or long AND (moving-average filter is disabled OR close > trend moving average OR C1 high is near an active bullish block OR order-block bypass is enabled AND C1 or the setup bar touched a bullish block) AND (opposite-block suppression is disabled OR C1 high is not near an active bearish block).
Short: Short setup AND current low <= C1 low − current ATR × breakout multiplier AND position is flat or short AND (moving-average filter is disabled OR close < trend moving average OR C1 low is near an active bearish block OR order-block bypass is enabled AND C1 or the setup bar touched a bearish block) AND (opposite-block suppression is disabled OR C1 low is not near an active bullish block).
Exit: long, high >= stored target OR low <= stored stop.
Exit: short, low <= stored target OR high >= stored stop.
