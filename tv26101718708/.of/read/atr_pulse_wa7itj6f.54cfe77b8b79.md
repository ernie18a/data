Calculation: ATR trailing stop = Let distance = ATR multiplied by a sensitivity parameter, and previous stop = the prior bar's ATR trailing stop, or 0 if unavailable. If close and prior close are both above previous stop, use max(previous stop, close - distance); if both are below previous stop, use min(previous stop, close + distance); otherwise use close - distance if close > previous stop, or close + distance otherwise.
Long: Close > ATR trailing stop.
Short: Close < ATR trailing stop.
Long: Close crosses above ATR trailing stop AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX >= minimum ADX AND +DI > -DI)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI of close > RSI direction level) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume or its simple moving average is unavailable OR its simple moving average <= 0 OR volume >= its simple moving average multiplied by a volume parameter) AND (candle filter disabled OR (close > open AND body fraction >= minimum body fraction)) AND (no previous accepted crossover signal exists OR bars since the last accepted long or short crossover signal > minimum bars between signals).
Short: Close crosses below ATR trailing stop AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX >= minimum ADX AND -DI > +DI)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI of close < RSI direction level) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume or its simple moving average is unavailable OR its simple moving average <= 0 OR volume >= its simple moving average multiplied by a volume parameter) AND (candle filter disabled OR (close < open AND body fraction >= minimum body fraction)) AND (no previous accepted crossover signal exists OR bars since the last accepted long or short crossover signal > minimum bars between signals).
Calculation: Body fraction = abs(close - open) / (high - low) if high > low, otherwise 0.
