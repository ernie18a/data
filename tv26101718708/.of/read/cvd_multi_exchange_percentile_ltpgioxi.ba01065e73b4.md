Calculation: Intrabar delta = volume × (2 × buy ratio − 1), where buy ratio is 0.5 if high = low; otherwise it is 0.5 + 0.5 × (close − low) / (high − low) if close >= open, and 0.5 × (close − low) / (high − low) if close < open. Use lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar, including the still-forming lower-timeframe bar when evaluating a forming chart bar.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of intrabar delta over available lower-timeframe bars, optionally reset to zero at the start of each new daily session before adding that chart bar's delta.
Calculation: Bull peak percentile = 100 × the fraction of stored positive session CVD peaks less than or equal to the current session's maximum CVD, including zero as the initial maximum. Store only positive peaks from completed sessions, retaining the most recent peaks up to the percentile lookback. Return zero if the current peak is not positive or fewer than five peaks are stored.
Calculation: Bear peak percentile = 100 × the fraction of stored negative session CVD peaks greater than or equal to the current session's minimum CVD, including zero as the initial minimum. Store only negative peaks from completed sessions, retaining the most recent peaks up to the same percentile lookback. Return zero if the current peak is not negative or fewer than five peaks are stored.
Long: The lowest close over the current swing lookback < the lowest close over the immediately preceding swing lookback AND the lowest CVD over the current swing lookback > the lowest CVD over the immediately preceding swing lookback AND bull peak percentile >= percentile threshold AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these three conditions AND (trend filter disabled OR close > SMA of close).
Short: The highest close over the current swing lookback > the highest close over the immediately preceding swing lookback AND the highest CVD over the current swing lookback < the highest CVD over the immediately preceding swing lookback AND bear peak percentile >= percentile threshold AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these three conditions AND (trend filter disabled OR close < SMA of close).
Long: Close < previous close AND CVD > previous CVD AND volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier AND bull peak percentile >= percentile threshold AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these four conditions.
