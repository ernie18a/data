Calculation: Major levels = Confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks, calculated on a separate timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the still-forming confirmation bar with lookahead: historical results expose its final pivot result from the start of that bar. For a lower timeframe, use the first intrabar result within each chart bar. Add each pivot once by pivot time, checking the most recent stored levels for duplicates; process highs before lows. Store newest first. A high level is swept when high > level; a low level is swept when low < level. Swept levels remain eligible for touches on their sweep bar only and are removed after a retention period. Remove unswept levels beyond the maximum stored-array index; retained swept levels also count toward that index.
Calculation: Rejected low touch = chart low <= an eligible major low level AND close > that level. Rejected high touch = chart high >= an eligible major high level AND close < that level.
Calculation: Valid bullish candle = close > open AND abs(close - open) > a body-to-range threshold × (high - low). Valid bearish candle = close < open AND abs(close - open) > the same threshold × (high - low).
Calculation: Structure levels = most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and pivot low, each with equal left and right lookbacks. Each newly confirmed pivot replaces the corresponding level and resets its broken state. The structure lookback and major-level lookback are independent parameters. An upward break occurs once per high level when close > level, or high > level in wick mode. A downward break occurs once per low level when close < level, or low < level in wick mode.
Calculation: Last touched side = low when low <= an eligible major low level, high when high >= an eligible major high level; if both occur on the same bar, high takes precedence. Retain this side until another touch or a matching structure-break signal resets it. Process touches before breaks and upward breaks before downward breaks.
Long: Upward break.
Short: Downward break.
Long: Valid bullish candle AND rejected low touch on this bar OR valid bullish candle AND the most recent rejected low touch occurred within the signal lookback AND no valid bullish candle occurred from that touch bar through the previous bar.
Short: Valid bearish candle AND rejected high touch on this bar OR valid bearish candle AND the most recent rejected high touch occurred within the signal lookback AND no valid bearish candle occurred from that touch bar through the previous bar.
Long: Upward break AND last touched side = low.
Short: Downward break AND last touched side = high.
