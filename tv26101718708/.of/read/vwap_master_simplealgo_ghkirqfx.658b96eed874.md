Calculation: Value and deviation = Use typical price (high + low + close) / 3. Select either a rolling window or an anchored calendar period. Value is the volume-weighted mean of typical price; variance is the volume-weighted mean of its square minus value squared. Until cumulative volume becomes positive, use equal weights instead. For an anchored period, reset sums at its start; if total weight is zero, use the current typical price as value and zero variance. Raw deviation is the maximum of the square root of nonnegative variance and a fraction of ATR, treating unavailable variance or ATR as zero. In rolling mode, deviation is an EMA of raw deviation; in anchored mode, it is raw deviation. Distance = (close - value) / deviation.
Calculation: Confirmed side = Initialize candidate side and confirmed side to neutral. Set candidate side to above when distance is greater than a positive buffer, to below when distance is less than the negative buffer, and otherwise retain it. Count consecutive bars with the same candidate side, including bars within the buffer. When the count reaches a confirmation length, set confirmed side to the candidate side. Reset confirmed side to neutral at the start of an anchored period.
Calculation: Band visit = Track upper and lower visits separately. Start an upper visit when distance reaches or exceeds the positive inner-band threshold, and a lower visit when distance reaches or falls below its negative. During each visit, track the maximum absolute distance reached on bars in that band's zone and the most recent such bar. End an upper visit after evaluating its signals when distance is at or below a positive reset threshold smaller than the inner-band threshold; end a lower visit when distance is at or above the negative reset threshold. Reset visits at the start of an anchored period. On each new visit clear its recorded last bounce depth and stretched-event flag; retain the last bounce bar separately for each side across visits and period resets.
