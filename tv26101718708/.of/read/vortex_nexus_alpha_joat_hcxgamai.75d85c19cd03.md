Calculation: ATR = average true range; all calculations use chart bars. Bracketed offsets refer to prior bars.
Calculation: Ribbon = EMAs of close at a fast, an intermediate, and a slow length; the intermediate length is the truncated fourth equally spaced step from fast toward slow using the configured layer count. Ribbon direction is bullish when fast > slow and bearish otherwise. Spread = abs(fast − slow); alignment = (close > intermediate AND fast > slow) OR (close < intermediate AND fast <= slow). Tight = spread / close < SMA(spread / close) × tightness factor; expansion = spread > spread[1]; ribbon power = spread / ATR.
Calculation: Buy pressure = volume × (close − open + upper wick × wick factor) / (high − low) when close > open; volume × (upper wick + abs(close − open) × body factor) / (high − low) when close < open; otherwise volume / 2. Upper wick = high − max(open, close). Pressure delta = 2 × buy pressure − volume; pressure index = SMA(pressure delta) / SMA(volume, same length) × 100; cumulative pressure = running sum of pressure delta from zero.
Calculation: Price efficiency = abs(close − close[lookback]) / sum(abs(close − close[1]), lookback), or zero when the denominator is zero. Chaos = 100 × log10(sum(true range, ATR lookback) / (highest high − lowest low over the ATR lookback)) / log10(ATR lookback). Volatility rank = percentile rank of 100 × ATR / close over its lookback. Directional power = (100 × price efficiency + 100 − chaos + an ADX bonus when ADX > its threshold) / a normalization factor; equilibrium power = (100 − volatility rank + 100 − 100 × price efficiency + the same ADX bonus when ADX < its threshold) / the same normalization factor; chaos power = (chaos + 100 − 100 × price efficiency) / another normalization factor. Directional score = 100 × directional power / sum of the three powers, or a fallback value when their sum is not positive. Directional regime = directional score > its threshold.
Calculation: Fractal efficiency = clamp((1 + log(sum(max(high, previous close) − min(low, previous close), lookback) / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log(lookback)) / 2, 0, 1); use a zero log ratio when the high-low span is not positive, and zero for an unavailable previous close.
Calculation: Gaussian close = alpha^4 × close + 4 × (1 − alpha) × Gaussian close[1] − 6 × (1 − alpha)^2 × Gaussian close[2] + 4 × (1 − alpha)^3 × Gaussian close[3] − (1 − alpha)^4 × Gaussian close[4], with unavailable prior values zero. Alpha = −beta + sqrt(beta^2 + 2 × beta); beta = (1 − cos(2 × pi / smoothing length)) / (sqrt(2) − 1).
Calculation: Adaptive Laguerre = set gamma to a weighted interpolation between a higher and a lower gamma using fractal efficiency; L0 = (1 − gamma) × Gaussian close + gamma × L0[1], L1 = −gamma × L0 + L0[1] + gamma × L1[1], L2 = −gamma × L1 + L1[1] + gamma × L2[1], L3 = −gamma × L2 + L2[1] + gamma × L3[1], with unavailable prior values zero. Up = sum(max(Li − L(i+1), 0)) and down = sum(max(L(i+1) − Li, 0)) for i = 0, 1, 2. Laguerre = 100 × up / (up + down), or the midpoint when the denominator is zero. Fractal momentum = clamp(EMA((Laguerre − midpoint) × (1 + fractal efficiency)), −100, 100).
Calculation: Price flow = EMA(close, short flow length) − EMA(close, longer flow length). Flow is bullish when price flow > 0 and bearish otherwise; flow acceleration = price flow − price flow[acceleration lookback]; flow magnitude = abs(price flow) / ATR. Net flow = EMA(volume when close > open, otherwise 0, minus volume when close < open, otherwise 0). Temporal force = price flow / ATR × (volume / EMA(volume) − 1) × flow magnitude. Smoothed velocity = EMA((close − close[velocity lookback]) / velocity lookback).
Calculation: Volume surge = volume > SMA(volume) × surge factor. Volume absorption = volume / SMA(volume) > its threshold AND (high − low) / SMA(high − low, same lookback) < its threshold. Bullish order block = close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND volume > SMA(volume) × order-block factor; bearish order block = close[1] > open[1] AND close < open AND volume > SMA(volume) × order-block factor. Up gap = low > high[2] AND low − high[2] > ATR × gap factor; down gap = high < low[2] AND low[2] − high > ATR × gap factor. Strong bullish candle = close > open AND close − open > ATR × candle factor; strong bearish candle = close < open AND open − close > ATR × candle factor.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low uses equal numbers of bars on each side. Record the latest confirmed high and low. Structure direction starts neutral and changes to bullish only when a confirmed higher pivot high occurs while bearish, or bearish only when a confirmed lower pivot low occurs while bullish. A bullish break is a confirmed higher pivot high while bullish; a bearish break is a confirmed lower pivot low while bearish. A bullish shift is a confirmed higher pivot high while bearish; a bearish shift is a confirmed lower pivot low while bullish. With the stated neutral initialization and these transition rules, breaks and shifts never occur.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of six Boolean components: (1) any of Laguerre < oversold threshold AND bullish flow AND close > fast ribbon; pressure index > positive reversal threshold AND flow changed from bearish to bullish; fractal momentum > positive momentum threshold AND volume surge AND close > intermediate ribbon; strong bullish candle AND bullish ribbon AND pressure index > a lower positive threshold; fractal momentum > 0 AND flow acceleration > 0 AND alignment; (2) any of bullish ribbon AND close > slow ribbon AND directional regime AND +DI > −DI; bullish break AND fractal momentum > positive momentum threshold; bullish order block AND bullish flow AND ADX > lower strength threshold; up gap AND pressure index > positive gap threshold AND alignment; close > prior bar's highest high over the breakout lookback AND volume surge AND cumulative pressure > 0 AND flow magnitude > threshold; (3) any of fractal momentum > higher positive threshold AND bullish ribbon AND ADX > higher strength threshold; Laguerre < oversold threshold AND bullish flow AND volume surge; fractal momentum > positive extreme threshold AND expansion; pressure index > extreme positive threshold AND flow acceleration > 0 AND directional regime AND +DI > −DI; +DI > −DI AND +DI > strength threshold AND alignment; (4) any of bullish shift AND volume surge; bullish order block AND close > latest confirmed pivot low AND fractal momentum > positive momentum threshold; up gap AND bullish flow AND bullish ribbon; bullish break AND pressure index > positive structure threshold; volume absorption AND pressure delta > 0 AND close > intermediate ribbon; (5) tight AND expansion AND bullish ribbon AND volume surge; (6) net flow > 0 AND temporal force > positive force threshold AND directional regime AND +DI > −DI.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of six Boolean components: (1) any of Laguerre > overbought threshold AND bearish flow AND close < fast ribbon; pressure index < negative reversal threshold AND flow changed from bullish to bearish; fractal momentum < negative momentum threshold AND volume surge AND close < intermediate ribbon; strong bearish candle AND bearish ribbon AND pressure index < a lower-magnitude negative threshold; fractal momentum < 0 AND flow acceleration < 0 AND alignment; (2) any of bearish ribbon AND close < slow ribbon AND directional regime AND −DI > +DI; bearish break AND fractal momentum < negative momentum threshold; bearish order block AND bearish flow AND ADX > lower strength threshold; down gap AND pressure index < negative gap threshold AND alignment; close < prior bar's lowest low over the breakout lookback AND volume surge AND cumulative pressure < 0 AND flow magnitude > threshold; (3) any of fractal momentum < higher-magnitude negative threshold AND bearish ribbon AND ADX > higher strength threshold; Laguerre > overbought threshold AND bearish flow AND volume surge; fractal momentum < negative extreme threshold AND expansion; pressure index < extreme negative threshold AND flow acceleration < 0 AND directional regime AND −DI > +DI; −DI > +DI AND −DI > strength threshold AND alignment; (4) any of bearish shift AND volume surge; bearish order block AND close < latest confirmed pivot high AND fractal momentum < negative momentum threshold; down gap AND bearish flow AND bearish ribbon; bearish break AND pressure index < negative structure threshold; volume absorption AND pressure delta < 0 AND close < intermediate ribbon; (5) tight AND expansion AND bearish ribbon AND volume surge; (6) net flow < 0 AND temporal force < negative force threshold AND directional regime AND −DI > +DI.
