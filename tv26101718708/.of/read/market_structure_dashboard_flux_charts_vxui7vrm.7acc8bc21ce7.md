Calculation: Analysis bars = chart bars or bars aggregated to a higher timeframe; for higher timeframes use the bar still forming. Swing levels and zone detection use analysis bars; comparisons of chart close with retrieved levels use chart bars.
Calculation: Swing high or low = a pivot confirmed after the same configurable number of analysis bars on each side. The latest and previous levels are the two most recently confirmed pivots of the same type. A newly confirmed pivot replaces its corresponding level before current-bar comparisons.
Calculation: Swing position = (chart close - latest swing low) / (latest swing high - latest swing low).
Calculation: Swing reclaim = after a new swing low or high is confirmed, record whether an analysis-bar low has fallen below that low or an analysis-bar high has risen above that high; retain each record until the corresponding new pivot. A low reclaim requires that record and analysis close > swing low; a high reclaim requires that record and analysis close < swing high.
Calculation: Structure types = the latest swing high and low each have type +1 if greater than their preceding same-type pivot, otherwise -1; override high type to +1 when analysis close > previous swing high and low type to -1 when analysis close < previous swing low. Structure bias is +1 when both types are +1, -1 when both are -1, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Order blocks = on an analysis close crossing above the latest unused swing high or below the latest unused swing low, consume that pivot and create respectively a bullish or bearish zone spanning the low to high of the lowest-low or highest-high candle between the pivot and the current bar, including the current bar, limited to a configurable maximum lookback. Do not add a zone overlapping an existing same-direction order block. Keep a configurable number of newest zones; remove bullish zones when analysis close < zone bottom and bearish zones when analysis close > zone top.
Calculation: Fair value gaps = on analysis bars, a bullish gap exists when low[1] > high[3] AND (close[2] > open[2] OR close[2] > close[3]) AND low[1] < high[2] AND low[2] < high[3]; its bottom is high[3] and top is low[1]. A bearish gap exists when high[1] < low[3] AND (close[2] < open[2] OR close[2] < close[3]) AND high[1] > low[2] AND high[2] > low[3]; its bottom is high[1] and top is low[3]. Keep a configurable number of newest gaps; remove bullish gaps when analysis low < bottom and bearish gaps when analysis high > top.
Calculation: Nearest zone direction = among unmitigated zones of the same kind, compare absolute distances from analysis close to each bullish zone top and each bearish zone bottom; select the smallest distance, resolving a bullish-versus-bearish tie as bearish. Return its direction, or 0 if no eligible zone exists.
Calculation: Trend bias = for each enabled chart or higher timeframe, sum enabled factor scores and multiply by its configurable weight; divide the sum across timeframes by the sum of weights multiplied by the number of enabled factors. Structure and nearest order-block and gap factors equal their respective directions; EMA factor is +1 when analysis close > its EMA and -1 otherwise; swing factor is -1 when swing position < 0, +1 when swing position > 1 or is below a lower interior threshold, -1 when it is above a higher interior threshold, and 0 otherwise. Higher-timeframe factors use the forming bar, except swing position uses chart close against retrieved swing levels.
Calculation: Previous-period levels = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Reset each period's high-break and low-break records when a new higher-timeframe bar starts; set them when chart high > previous-period high or chart low < previous-period low, respectively.
Long: Analysis close > EMA of analysis close.
Short: Analysis close <= EMA of analysis close.
Long: Swing position > 1 OR swing position >= 0 AND swing position < a midpoint AND low reclaim is false AND high reclaim is false OR swing position >= 0 AND swing position <= 1 AND low reclaim is true.
Short: Swing position < 0 OR swing position >= a midpoint AND swing position <= 1 AND low reclaim is false AND high reclaim is false OR swing position >= 0 AND swing position <= 1 AND low reclaim is false AND high reclaim is true.
Long: A newly confirmed swing high or low is greater than or equal to its previous same-type pivot, or has no previous same-type pivot.
Short: A newly confirmed swing high or low < its previous same-type pivot.
Long: Structure bias = +1 OR a displayed structure label is a higher high or higher low, including a real-time higher-high override.
Short: Structure bias = -1 OR a displayed structure label is a lower high or lower low, including a real-time lower-low override.
Long: A bullish order block is created OR nearest order block direction = +1.
Short: A bearish order block is created OR nearest order block direction = -1.
Long: A bullish fair value gap is created OR nearest fair value gap direction = +1.
Short: A bearish fair value gap is created OR nearest fair value gap direction = -1.
Long: Trend bias > a positive threshold.
Short: Trend bias < the corresponding negative threshold.
Long: Chart close > previous-period high OR chart close is in the lower half of the previous-period range with neither reclaim active OR chart close is within that range AND the low-break record is set AND chart close > previous-period low.
Short: Chart close < previous-period low OR chart close is in the upper half of the previous-period range with neither reclaim active OR chart close is within that range AND the low reclaim is inactive AND the high-break record is set AND chart close < previous-period high.
Long: Chart close crosses above previous-period high OR the previous-period low-break record is set AND chart close > previous-period low AND that reclaim was false on the preceding chart bar.
Short: Chart close crosses below previous-period low OR the previous-period high-break record is set AND chart close < previous-period high AND that reclaim was false on the preceding chart bar.
