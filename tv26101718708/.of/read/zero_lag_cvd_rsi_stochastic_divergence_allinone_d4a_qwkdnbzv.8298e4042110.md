Calculation: Volume delta = EMA(buying volume) − EMA(selling volume), using the same length; buying volume = volume × (max(close − open, 0) + (high − max(open, close) + min(open, close) − low) / 2) / (high − low); selling volume = volume × (max(open − close, 0) + (high − max(open, close) + min(open, close) − low) / 2) / (high − low). A zero high-low range produces an undefined value.
Calculation: Stochastic = SMA of stochastic %K calculated from close, high and low.
Long: For either the shorter or longer divergence lookback and any of volume delta, RSI of close, or stochastic: close > open AND previous close < previous open AND previous low <= lowest low from 2 bars ago through the lookback endpoint AND previous oscillator > lowest oscillator from 2 bars ago through the same endpoint.
Short: For either the shorter or longer divergence lookback and any of volume delta, RSI of close, or stochastic: close < open AND previous close > previous open AND previous high >= highest high from 2 bars ago through the lookback endpoint AND previous oscillator < highest oscillator from 2 bars ago through the same endpoint.
