Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = EMA(buying volume, lookback) − EMA(selling volume, lookback), where each bar’s volume is allocated by candle body and wicks: the body is assigned to buying volume when close > open and to selling volume when close < open; half the wick volume is assigned to each side.
Calculation: Short and long divergence lookbacks = two lookback lengths, with the long lookback longer than the short lookback.
Long: For either lookback, current close > open AND previous close < previous open AND previous low ≤ every low from 2 bars ago through the lookback AND, for the selected oscillator (CVD, RSI, or Stochastic), its value 1 bar ago > every value from 2 bars ago through the lookback; in Agreement mode, at least the configured number of CVD, RSI, and Stochastic bullish-divergence conditions for that lookback are true (RSI additionally ≤ its oversold level and Stochastic additionally ≤ its oversold level when their respective filters are enabled).
Short: For either lookback, current close < open AND previous close > previous open AND previous high ≥ every high from 2 bars ago through the lookback AND, for the selected oscillator (CVD, RSI, or Stochastic), its value 1 bar ago < every value from 2 bars ago through the lookback; in Agreement mode, at least the configured number of CVD, RSI, and Stochastic bearish-divergence conditions for that lookback are true (RSI additionally ≥ its overbought level and Stochastic additionally ≥ its overbought level when their respective filters are enabled).
