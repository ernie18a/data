Calculation: Relative volume = chart-bar volume / max(SMA of chart-bar volume, 1).
Calculation: Prior mapped higher-timeframe high and low = the high and low mapped to the previous chart bar by a higher-timeframe request with lookahead on; historically these are the final values of the higher-timeframe bar containing that chart bar, even if that bar was still forming at the time.
Calculation: Stop and target = at entry, freeze the chart-bar close as entry price; for a long, stop = min(entry-bar low, prior mapped higher-timeframe low) - ATR × buffer and target = entry price + (entry price - stop) × reward multiple; for a short, stop = max(entry-bar high, prior mapped higher-timeframe high) + ATR × buffer and target = entry price - (stop - entry price) × reward multiple.
Long: The close of each of two higher-timeframe bars still forming is above both its open and its EMA of close; the two EMAs use the same lookback, and one timeframe is higher than the other.
Short: The close of each of two higher-timeframe bars still forming is below both its open and its EMA of close; the two EMAs use the same lookback, and one timeframe is higher than the other.
Long: Close > prior mapped higher-timeframe high AND no sweep or trend entry condition is true.
Short: Close < prior mapped higher-timeframe low AND close <= prior mapped higher-timeframe high AND no sweep or trend entry condition is true.
Long: Session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is in the configured New York session, AND both higher-timeframe trend bars still forming have close > open AND close > their EMA of close, AND ((low < prior mapped higher-timeframe low AND close > prior mapped higher-timeframe low AND relative volume > sweep threshold) OR (close > session VWAP of close AND close > EMA of close on a still-forming higher timeframe below both trend timeframes AND relative volume > trend threshold AND close > open)).
Short: Session filter is disabled OR chart-bar time is in the configured New York session, AND both higher-timeframe trend bars still forming have close < open AND close < their EMA of close, AND ((high > prior mapped higher-timeframe high AND close < prior mapped higher-timeframe high AND relative volume > sweep threshold) OR (close < session VWAP of close AND close < EMA of close on a still-forming higher timeframe below both trend timeframes AND relative volume > trend threshold AND close < open)).
Exit: long, (high >= target AND target > previous chart-bar close) OR (low <= target AND target < previous chart-bar close) OR (low <= stop AND stop < previous chart-bar close) OR (high >= stop AND stop > previous chart-bar close).
Exit: short, (high >= target AND target > previous chart-bar close) OR (low <= target AND target < previous chart-bar close) OR (low <= stop AND stop < previous chart-bar close) OR (high >= stop AND stop > previous chart-bar close).
