Calculation: Scan RSI = RSI of close on the selected scan timeframe. On historical chart bars, use the last completed scan bar; if the scan timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, use the last scan bar within the chart bar. On a live chart bar, use the scan bar still forming when applicable.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot = an RSI high greater than or equal to each RSI value in an equal-length preceding window and greater than each value in the following window, or an RSI low less than or equal to each value in the preceding window and less than each value in the following window. Confirm the pivot after the following window closes; record the price high or low on the pivot bar. When a new pivot of a given type is confirmed, discard stored pivots of that type whose pivot bars are more than the search range before the current scan bar. Compare the newest pivot with either the immediately preceding stored pivot or any preceding stored pivot, according to the selected comparison mode.
Long: Scan RSI <= oversold threshold
Short: Scan RSI >= overbought threshold
Long: newest confirmed RSI low pivot is no older than the maximum age measured from confirmation AND its price low < the selected preceding RSI low pivot's price low AND its RSI > that preceding pivot's RSI
Short: newest confirmed RSI high pivot is no older than the maximum age measured from confirmation AND its price high > the selected preceding RSI high pivot's price high AND its RSI < that preceding pivot's RSI
