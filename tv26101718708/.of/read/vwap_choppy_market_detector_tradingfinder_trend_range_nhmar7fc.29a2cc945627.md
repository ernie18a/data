Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of bar open, reset at the selected anchor period
Calculation: Full VWAP bands = anchored VWAP ± volume-weighted standard deviation of bar open times the band-width multiplier
Calculation: Inner VWAP bands = anchored VWAP ± volume-weighted standard deviation of bar open times the range multiplier
Calculation: ATR = average true range of chart-timeframe bars
Long: Full VWAP bands differ AND close > inner upper VWAP band AND full VWAP band width >= ATR times a threshold
Short: Full VWAP bands differ AND close < inner lower VWAP band AND full VWAP band width >= ATR times a threshold
