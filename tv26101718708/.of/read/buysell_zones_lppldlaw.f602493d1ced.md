Calculation: Confirmed swing levels = symmetric pivot highs and lows using the same configurable strength on each side; a pivot becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. The latest confirmed pivot replaces the previous level of its kind and is initially unbroken.
Calculation: Structure breaks = an unbroken swing high is broken when close, or optionally high, is strictly above it; an unbroken swing low is broken when close, or optionally low, is strictly below it. Mark each level broken on its first break, even if the zone filters fail. Track the direction of every break, initially neutral; process an upward break before a downward break if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Active range = the high and low of the latest accepted zone's signal bar, extended above its high and below its low by the same fixed multiple of that bar's high-low range. A new accepted zone replaces the active range and resets both excursion flags.
Long: An upward structure break follows a downward structure break AND, when enabled, close > session VWAP AND, when enabled, close > EMA of close AND, when enabled, volume >= SMA of volume multiplied by the minimum relative-volume parameter AND, when enabled, high - low is between the lower and upper ATR multiples inclusive AND, when enabled, the bar is confirmed AND the number of bars since the last accepted zone is at least the cooldown parameter or no zone has yet been accepted.
Short: A downward structure break follows an upward structure break AND, when enabled, close < session VWAP AND, when enabled, close < EMA of close AND, when enabled, volume >= SMA of volume multiplied by the minimum relative-volume parameter AND, when enabled, high - low is between the lower and upper ATR multiples inclusive AND, when enabled, the bar is confirmed AND the number of bars since the last accepted zone is at least the cooldown parameter or no zone has yet been accepted.
Long: An active range exists AND low has been below its lower boundary since the latest accepted zone or the previous bullish return signal AND close is strictly between its boundaries; include the current bar when checking the excursion and reset the lower excursion flag after the signal.
Short: An active range exists AND high has been above its upper boundary since the latest accepted zone or the previous bearish return signal AND close is strictly between its boundaries; include the current bar when checking the excursion and reset the upper excursion flag after the signal.
