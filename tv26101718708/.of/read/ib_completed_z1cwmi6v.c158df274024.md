Calculation: Initial balance = the highest high, lowest low, first open and last close of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening window; reset each local calendar day and finalize after the window.
Calculation: Location levels = previous completed session-week high and low, with the week resetting on the first observed bar in the configured Sunday evening window; previous completed session-day high and low, resetting when the local hour crosses the configured session-start hour; highest high and lowest low within the configured pre-market window, reset at each daily boundary; and lowest low since the exact configured evening start, updated during the overnight window. Use available values even before their display windows.
Calculation: Location averages = close SMAs on several higher timeframes, including a shorter-lookback SMA on two timeframes and two longer-lookback SMAs on each of four timeframes; the second long lookback exceeds the first. On historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the current higher-timeframe bar only when it completes.
Calculation: Initial stop = at the entry signal, for a long use the latest confirmed pivot low whose pivot bar follows the latest confirmed pivot high above the initial balance high; for a short use the latest confirmed pivot high whose pivot bar follows the latest confirmed pivot low below the initial balance low. Reset the candidate when a new qualifying preceding pivot is confirmed. If no candidate exists, use the lowest low since the long retest or highest high since the short retest, including the entry bar. Subtract a configurable fixed or percentage offset for a long and add it for a short. Pivots require configurable numbers of bars on both sides and are usable only when confirmed.
Calculation: Risk distance = signal-bar close minus initial stop for a long, or initial stop minus signal-bar close for a short. Target levels use the signal-bar close plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, successive configured multiples of this distance.
Long: Initial balance completes AND its last close > its first open.
Short: Initial balance completes AND its last close <= its first open.
Long: Initial balance complete AND no prior entry signal this local day AND after a bar with high > initial balance high a later bar has low <= initial balance high AND close < chart-timeframe trigger SMA AND a still later bar has close > trigger SMA AND close > initial balance high AND, if opposite-boundary invalidation is enabled, no bar from the breakout through the entry has low < initial balance low AND local time < entry cutoff AND local time < flatten time AND weekday is allowed AND, if the size filter is enabled and the range in ticks is available, initial balance range in ticks <= maximum AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, close > higher-timeframe close SMA, EMA, WMA or VWMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the current bar only when it completes AND close > each enabled, available location level and location average.
Short: Initial balance complete AND no prior entry signal this local day AND after a bar with low < initial balance low a later bar has high >= initial balance low AND close > chart-timeframe trigger SMA AND a still later bar has close < trigger SMA AND close < initial balance low AND, if opposite-boundary invalidation is enabled, no bar from the breakout through the entry has high > initial balance high AND local time < entry cutoff AND local time < flatten time AND weekday is allowed AND, if the size filter is enabled and the range in ticks is available, initial balance range in ticks <= maximum AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, close < higher-timeframe close SMA, EMA, WMA or VWMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the current bar only when it completes AND close < each enabled, available location level and location average.
Exit: long, low <= initial stop OR high >= an enabled target level for its allocated portion OR break-even is enabled AND risk distance > 0 AND a previous bar while the position was open had high >= actual entry price + configured break-even multiple * risk distance AND low <= actual entry price OR local time >= flatten time.
Exit: short, high >= initial stop OR low <= an enabled target level for its allocated portion OR break-even is enabled AND risk distance > 0 AND a previous bar while the position was open had low <= actual entry price - configured break-even multiple * risk distance AND high >= actual entry price OR local time >= flatten time.
