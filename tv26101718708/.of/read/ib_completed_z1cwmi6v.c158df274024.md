Calculation: Initial Balance High/Low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening window; use these levels after that window completes
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar high/low confirmed as a local extreme by the configured number of bars on both sides
Calculation: Long stop = configured offset below the latest confirmed pivot low that follows a pivot high above Initial Balance High; if none exists, use the lowest low from the retest phase. Short stop = configured offset above the latest confirmed pivot high that follows a pivot low below Initial Balance Low; if none exists, use the highest high from the retest phase
Calculation: Risk distance (1R) = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop price
Long: After Initial Balance completes, a bar's high > Initial Balance High; on a later bar, low <= Initial Balance High AND close < chart-timeframe SMA; on a subsequent bar, close > Initial Balance High AND close > chart-timeframe SMA AND close > enabled higher-timeframe moving average(s) AND close > each enabled location/trend level AND before the entry cutoff and flat time AND the day is not excluded AND any enabled Initial Balance size limit is satisfied AND no trade has been taken in the session
Short: After Initial Balance completes, a bar's low < Initial Balance Low; on a later bar, high >= Initial Balance Low AND close > chart-timeframe SMA; on a subsequent bar, close < Initial Balance Low AND close < chart-timeframe SMA AND close < enabled higher-timeframe moving average(s) AND close < each enabled location/trend level AND before the entry cutoff and flat time AND the day is not excluded AND any enabled Initial Balance size limit is satisfied AND no trade has been taken in the session
Exit: Long, low <= initial stop or active break-even stop OR high >= entry price + configured target multiple × 1R OR time >= configured flat time
Exit: Short, high >= initial stop or active break-even stop OR low <= entry price - configured target multiple × 1R OR time >= configured flat time
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + configured break-even trigger multiple × 1R; move stop to entry price
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - configured break-even trigger multiple × 1R; move stop to entry price
