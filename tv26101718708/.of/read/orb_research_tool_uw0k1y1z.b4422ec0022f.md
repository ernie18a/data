Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of lower-timeframe bars from the session open until the configured opening-range end; use completed bars.
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of lower-timeframe bars from the session open until the configured opening-range end; use completed bars.
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening-range high − opening-range low
Long: Flat AND no trade taken today AND before the configured force-flat time AND enabled weekday/month filters pass AND (opening-range size filter disabled OR opening-range size ≤ configured maximum) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR chart close > session VWAP) AND opening range complete AND (first eligible lower-timeframe close > opening-range high OR, after an earlier lower-timeframe close > opening-range high, lower-timeframe low ≤ opening-range high + configured retest buffer AND lower-timeframe close ≥ opening-range high OR chart close > opening-range high)
Short: Flat AND no trade taken today AND before the configured force-flat time AND enabled weekday/month filters pass AND (opening-range size filter disabled OR opening-range size ≤ configured maximum) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR chart close < session VWAP) AND opening range complete AND (first eligible lower-timeframe close < opening-range low OR, after an earlier lower-timeframe close < opening-range low, lower-timeframe high ≥ opening-range low − configured retest buffer AND lower-timeframe close ≤ opening-range low OR chart close < opening-range low)
Exit: Long, bar low ≤ opening-range low − configured stop buffer OR bar high ≥ entry price + (entry price − stop price) OR datetime ≥ configured force-flat time
Exit: Short, bar high ≥ opening-range high + configured stop buffer OR bar low ≤ entry price − (stop price − entry price) OR datetime ≥ configured force-flat time
