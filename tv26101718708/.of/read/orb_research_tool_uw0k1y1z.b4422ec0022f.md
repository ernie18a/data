Calculation: OR_High = highest high, and OR_Low = lowest low, of the bars of a timeframe lower than the chart timeframe, from the session open until the opening-range duration has elapsed. Both values are then frozen for the rest of the day and reset at the next session open.
Calculation: OR_locked = current bar time >= session open + opening-range duration.
Calculation: VWAP = session volume-weighted average of (high+low+close)/3, reset each session.
Long: (no open position AND no trade entered yet today AND time of day < force-flat time AND [if enabled: month in allowed months AND weekday in allowed weekdays] AND [if enabled: OR_High - OR_Low <= max OR size] AND [if enabled: chart close > VWAP]) AND (A OR B OR C). A: OR_locked AND lower-TF close > OR_High, and this is the first lower-TF bar today meeting it (use the last completed lower-TF bar at the chart bar close). B (retest): OR_locked AND some lower-TF close > OR_High has occurred on this bar or an earlier bar today since lock AND lower-TF low <= OR_High + retest buffer AND lower-TF close >= OR_High, and this is the first such retest today. C: OR_locked AND chart-TF close > OR_High.
Short: (no open position AND no trade entered yet today AND time of day < force-flat time AND [if enabled: month in allowed months AND weekday in allowed weekdays] AND [if enabled: OR_High - OR_Low <= max OR size] AND [if enabled: chart close < VWAP]) AND (A OR B OR C). A: OR_locked AND lower-TF close < OR_Low, and this is the first lower-TF bar today meeting it. B (retest): OR_locked AND some lower-TF close < OR_Low has occurred on this bar or an earlier bar today since lock AND lower-TF high >= OR_Low - retest buffer AND lower-TF close <= OR_Low, and this is the first such retest today. C: OR_locked AND chart-TF close < OR_Low.
Exit: long, low <= OR_Low - stop buffer OR high >= entry + (entry - (OR_Low - stop buffer))
Exit: short, high >= OR_High + stop buffer OR low <= entry - ((OR_High + stop buffer) - entry)
Exit: long or short, time of day >= force-flat time
