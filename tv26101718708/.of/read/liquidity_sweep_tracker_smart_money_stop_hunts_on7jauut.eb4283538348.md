Calculation: Swings = confirmed pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks; retain a parameterized maximum number per side, discarding the oldest. Each starts unswept with one touch; on each subsequent processing bar increment its touches when the corresponding high or low is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the level. A swept level remains unavailable even if its setup fails.
Calculation: Structure = initially neutral; change to bullish when close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high and structure is not already bullish, or bearish when close falls below the latest confirmed swing low and structure is not already bearish. The change itself is a break of structure. Process bullish before bearish.
Calculation: Pending sweep = when no sweep is pending on that side, select the most recently confirmed unswept swing high exceeded by high plus an ATR-scaled buffer, or swing low breached by low minus that buffer. Mark it swept and record its level, touch count, sweep-bar high and low, and whether sweep-bar volume exceeds its volume SMA times a multiplier. Track the highest high for a high sweep or lowest low for a low sweep through confirmation. Check for rejection on the sweep bar and each following bar within the confirmation window; expire after the parameterized number of non-rejection bars. The first rejection ends the pending sweep whether or not its filters pass.
Calculation: Rejection = close below the pending high-sweep level or close above the pending low-sweep level.
Calculation: Trigger = if no confirmation trigger is enabled, pass; otherwise at least one enabled trigger must pass. For a low sweep, bullish engulfing means close > open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND close - open >= absolute previous candle body; bullish displacement means close > open AND close - open >= ATR times a multiplier; sweep-candle break means close > sweep-bar high. For a high sweep, bearish engulfing means close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND open - close >= absolute previous candle body; bearish displacement means close < open AND open - close >= ATR times a multiplier; sweep-candle break means close < sweep-bar low.
Calculation: Confidence = weighted average of depth, touch, structure, volatility-regime, displacement, volume and higher-timeframe proximity scores. Depth is sweep distance beyond the level divided by current ATR; its score rises linearly from 0 to 100 below an ideal minimum, stays at 100 through an ideal maximum, then falls linearly to 0 at twice that maximum. Touch score is min(100, recorded touches / touch cap * 100). Structure score is 100 for matching structure, 55 for neutral and 25 for opposing structure. Volatility-regime score is clamp((ATR / its longer-period SMA - lower reference) / (upper reference - lower reference) * 100, 0, 100), using a ratio of 1 when the SMA is not positive. Displacement score is min(100, distance from the tracked sweep extreme to current close / (ATR * displacement reference) * 100). Volume score is 50 when its filter is disabled; otherwise clamp((current volume / volume SMA - 1) / volume multiplier * 100, 0, 100), using a volume ratio of 1 when the SMA is not positive. Higher-timeframe score is 50 when its filter is disabled or no corresponding pivot exists; otherwise max(0, 100 * (1 - absolute distance from swept level to latest corresponding higher-timeframe pivot / (ATR * proximity reference))). Use the latest confirmed pivot from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with equal left and right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a low sweep, max(tracked lowest low - ATR * stop-buffer multiplier * clamped volatility-regime ratio, close - ATR * maximum-stop multiplier); for a high sweep, min(tracked highest high + ATR * stop-buffer multiplier * clamped volatility-regime ratio, close + ATR * maximum-stop multiplier). Clamp the volatility-regime ratio between its configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Target = nearest unswept opposing swing beyond the confirmation close; if none exists and the real-opposing-swing requirement is disabled, use close plus an ATR-scaled distance for a low sweep or close minus that distance for a high sweep. Otherwise the target is unavailable. The interim target equals this target because each newly detected fair value gap is marked filled on its creation bar.
Calculation: Entry filters = confidence >= minimum AND target is available AND, if the volume filter is enabled, sweep-bar volume exceeded its volume SMA times the volume multiplier AND, if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled, the corresponding higher-timeframe pivot exists and its distance from the swept level is <= current ATR times the proximity reference AND trigger passes AND, if the reward-to-risk filter is enabled, initial stop is on the risk side of close and absolute target distance / initial stop distance >= minimum reward-to-risk.
Calculation: Trailing stop = start with the stored initial stop. For a long, once a confirmed swing low whose pivot bar is after the entry signal bar exists, retain the highest value of that swing low minus current ATR times a trail-buffer multiplier; use the greater of this value and the initial stop. For a short, similarly retain the lowest value of a post-entry swing high plus current ATR times the multiplier and use the lesser of it and the initial stop. If the ATR backstop is enabled, additionally use the greater of the long stop and close minus ATR times its multiplier, or the lesser of the short stop and close plus ATR times its multiplier.
Long: Pending low sweep AND rejection AND entry filters pass.
Short: Pending high sweep AND rejection AND entry filters pass.
Exit: long, short signal occurs AND current position is long.
Exit: short, long signal occurs AND current position is short.
Exit: long, opposing-structure exit enabled AND bearish break of structure occurs.
Exit: short, opposing-structure exit enabled AND bullish break of structure occurs.
Exit: long, no opposing-structure exit on the current calculation AND a live exit order's stop is reached by price OR no opposing-structure exit on the current calculation AND its target is reached by price; exit the configured partial percentage.
Exit: short, no opposing-structure exit on the current calculation AND a live exit order's stop is reached by price OR no opposing-structure exit on the current calculation AND its target is reached by price; exit the configured partial percentage.
Exit: long, time exit enabled AND bars since entry signal >= time limit AND no bullish break of structure has occurred while in the position AND close <= entry price.
Exit: short, time exit enabled AND bars since entry signal >= time limit AND no bearish break of structure has occurred while in the position AND close >= entry price.
