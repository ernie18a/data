Calculation: Features (all on chart timeframe, computed on each closed bar) = F1 Choppiness Index; F2 = v + 0.5*a where v = close - previous close and a = v - previous v; F3 = (close - session-anchored VWAP) / stdev of close; F4 = RSI of close; F5 = (ATR - SMA of ATR) / stdev of ATR (SMA and stdev over the same window); F6 = volume / SMA of volume. Each feature is min-max normalized over a rolling lookback window including the current bar: (x - min) / (max - min), or 0 if max equals min.
Calculation: Memory = on every closed bar, store the normalized feature vector from 4 bars earlier together with a label from change = close - close 4 bars ago and noise = a fraction (below 1) of ATR: label +1 if change > noise, -1 if change < -noise, otherwise 0. Keep only the most recent lookback-many entries.
Calculation: Prob = if Memory holds fewer than K entries, 50. Otherwise take the K Memory entries nearest to the current normalized feature vector, with distance = sum over the 6 features of ln(1 + |current - stored|). Each neighbor weight = exp(-distance^2 / bandwidth). bull = sum of weights of neighbors labeled +1, bear = sum of weights of neighbors labeled -1 (label 0 ignored). Prob = 100 * bull / (bull + bear), or 50 if bull + bear = 0.
Calculation: P = weighted moving average of Prob over a very short window (linearly increasing weights, latest bar heaviest).
Calculation: Fast EMA and Slow EMA = EMA of close on chart timeframe, with Fast EMA length shorter than Slow EMA length.
Long: (on a closed bar) P >= bull threshold (above 50) AND P <= upper extreme level (above the bull threshold) AND Fast EMA crosses above Slow EMA AND the most recent previously fired Long/Short entry signal was not Long (Long and Short signals must alternate; the first signal is allowed).
Short: (on a closed bar) P <= bear threshold (below 50) AND P >= lower extreme level (below the bear threshold, mirror of the upper extreme level around 50) AND Fast EMA crosses below Slow EMA AND the most recent previously fired Long/Short entry signal was not Short.
Exit: long, P crosses above the upper extreme level on a closed bar
Exit: short, P crosses below the lower extreme level on a closed bar
