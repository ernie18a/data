Calculation: Qualified pivot = a confirmed pivot high or low using independent left and right lookbacks, whose pivot-bar volume divided by its volume SMA is at least the relative-volume threshold, whose rejection wick divided by its high-low range is at least the wick threshold, and whose distance from the extreme to the close divided by that range is at least the close-distance threshold. The rejection wick is high minus max(open, close) for a high and min(open, close) minus low for a low. The close distance is high minus close for a high and close minus low for a low. A zero-range bar or a bar with a nonpositive or unavailable volume SMA cannot qualify.
Calculation: Active zones = retain only the latest qualified pivot high as resistance and the latest qualified pivot low as support; replace each immediately when a new qualified pivot of that type is confirmed. A zone expires when its age in bars from the pivot bar exceeds the maximum age. Resistance has pivot price P and upper boundary P + ATR at the pivot bar times the zone-width multiplier; support has pivot price P and lower boundary P - ATR at the pivot bar times the zone-width multiplier. On each bar, create or replace zones before evaluating them. An unbroken resistance breaks when close > its upper boundary; an unbroken support breaks when close < its lower boundary. A broken zone remains broken until replaced or expired. Track the break bar and whether each zone has already produced a hold or flip; reset these on replacement.
Long: A qualified pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A qualified pivot high is confirmed.
Long: Active unbroken support is past its confirmation bar AND it has not previously held AND low <= its pivot price AND close > its pivot price.
Short: Active unbroken resistance is past its confirmation bar AND it has not previously held AND high >= its pivot price AND close < its pivot price.
Long: Active unbroken resistance has close > its upper boundary.
Short: Active unbroken support has close < its lower boundary.
Long: Active broken resistance is past its break bar AND it has not previously flipped AND low <= its upper boundary AND close > its upper boundary.
Short: Active broken support is past its break bar AND it has not previously flipped AND high >= its lower boundary AND close < its lower boundary.
