Calculation: Price = a selected price series derived from the input bars.
Calculation: Centre = selected EMA or rolling median of price, or session VWAP of typical price using bar volume; in VWAP mode, use the rolling median when volume is missing or its moving average is not positive.
Calculation: Deviation = price minus centre. Local scale = when normalization is enabled, the greater of the previous bar's EMA of absolute deviation and an ATR-based minimum width, using the current EMA if the previous value is unavailable; otherwise, local scale = 1. A nonpositive or unavailable scale makes the normalized score unavailable. Score = absolute deviation divided by local scale.
Calculation: Conformal bands = seed inner and outer score quantiles from a full rolling calibration window of valid scores, using the sorted score at the rounded coverage-times-last-index position; the outer quantile is at least the inner quantile. On each subsequent confirmed bar with a valid score, first calculate each band's half-width as the greater of its prior quantile times current local scale and the ATR-based minimum width, and set its upper and lower boundaries to centre plus and minus that half-width. Then update both quantiles for the next bar: for each coverage target, accumulate target minus the indicator that score is strictly below its prior quantile; add learning rate times scale factor times decay factor times that same difference, plus an optional integrator gain times scale factor times a bounded arctangent transform of the accumulated error. The scale factor is 1 when normalized, otherwise the maximum of the current EMA of absolute deviation, minimum width, and a positive floor. The optional decay factor decreases with the number of updates and has a positive floor. Floor the updated inner quantile at zero and the updated outer quantile at the updated inner quantile. Append each valid score after updating; seed only after the window fills, so bands first exist on the following bar.
Calculation: Divergence oscillator = RSI of price, or deviation divided by local scale, substituting 1 when local scale is unavailable. Oscillator pivots use selected left and right confirmation lookbacks; compare consecutive confirmed pivots of the same type and price at their pivot bars. Evaluate divergence on the confirmation bar.
Long: Price < lower outer band, when close-back confirmation is disabled.
Long: Previous price < previous lower outer band AND current price >= current lower outer band, when close-back confirmation is enabled.
Long: A new oscillator pivot low is confirmed AND its value > the preceding oscillator pivot low AND price at the new pivot < price at the preceding pivot.
Long: Price >= centre.
Short: Price > upper outer band, when close-back confirmation is disabled.
Short: Previous price > previous upper outer band AND current price <= current upper outer band, when close-back confirmation is enabled.
Short: A new oscillator pivot high is confirmed AND its value < the preceding oscillator pivot high AND price at the new pivot > price at the preceding pivot.
Short: Price < centre.
