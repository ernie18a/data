Calculation: Forecast path and drift = Select one of: EMA of the price source and its change over a drift lookback divided by that lookback; linear-regression fitted endpoint and slope over a lookback; anchored VWAP of the price source and its change over the drift lookback divided by that lookback, using volume as weight when positive and otherwise using unit weight; an alpha-beta tracker whose prediction is prior level plus prior velocity, residual is source minus prediction, updated level is prediction plus level gain times residual, and updated velocity is prior velocity plus velocity gain times residual; or an ensemble of all four paths and drifts. Initialize the tracker level to the first source and velocity to zero. For the ensemble, each component's one-step forecast is its prior path plus prior drift; update an exponential average of its absolute forecast error on each completed bar, then weight its current path and drift by the reciprocal of that average plus a small positive constant and normalize the weights. Reset anchored VWAP at each selected calendar anchor.
Calculation: Prediction bands = The current one-step forecast is the prior bar's selected path plus prior drift. Store each completed bar's source minus its one-step forecast, optionally divided by the ATR known on the prior bar when positive; otherwise use a scale of one. Before storing the current error, calculate each band as the forecast plus that same scale times the linearly interpolated lower and upper empirical quantiles of the most recent stored errors. The quantile probabilities are half the effective miscoverage and one minus half the effective miscoverage. Maintain separate bands with lower and higher nominal coverage, initially using their nominal miscoverages. After scoring each completed bar against its bands, optionally update each effective miscoverage by adding the calibration rate times nominal miscoverage minus the outside-band indicator, then clamp it to configured bounds. The rate is either fixed or the weighted average of several different expert rates: for each bar, compute the fraction of recent stored errors strictly below the current normalized error and define depth as twice the smaller of that fraction and its complement; each expert's error indicator is depth below its own effective miscoverage, its error estimate is an exponential average of that indicator, and its effective miscoverage is updated using its rate and the nominal miscoverage of the lower-coverage band, then clamped. Multiply each expert weight by an exponential penalty based on the squared difference between its error estimate and that nominal miscoverage, enforce a positive weight floor, normalize, and mix with uniform weights. Use this blended rate for both bands. Limit the stored error history and require a minimum sample count before band decisions.
Calculation: Anomaly thresholds = The upper threshold is the higher-coverage upper band plus a sensitivity multiplier times the greater of the gap between the higher- and lower-coverage upper bands and an ATR multiple. The lower threshold is the higher-coverage lower band minus the sensitivity multiplier times the greater of the gap between the lower- and higher-coverage lower bands and an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Band position = (price source minus lower edge of the lower-coverage band) divided by that band's width, when the width is positive.
