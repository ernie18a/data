Calculation: Timeframes = the chart timeframe, a higher signal timeframe, a still higher alignment timeframe, and three successively higher bias timeframes. Use the last completed bar for every higher-timeframe calculation; forward-fill its value onto chart bars.
Calculation: Structure bias = retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low, each requiring the same configurable number of bars on both sides. Set persistent direction to bullish when close exceeds the latest pivot high and bearish when close falls below the latest pivot low, with bearish taking precedence if both occur; otherwise retain the direction. Optionally require close to be above the bias EMA for bullish or below it for bearish. Alternatively, replace this structure method with bullish when the shorter EMA exceeds the longer EMA and close exceeds the shorter EMA, or bearish when the shorter EMA is below the longer EMA and close is below the shorter EMA; otherwise the bias is neutral.
Calculation: Cluster = the absolute difference between a long-term EMA of close and session VWAP is at most a configurable ATR multiple or fixed distance. For higher-timeframe clusters use the ATR multiple. VWAP is cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset each trading session.
Calculation: Candle trigger = on the preceding bar, body is absolute close minus open, upper wick is high minus the greater of open and close, lower wick is the lesser of open and close minus low, and range is high minus low. A bullish trigger is an enabled bullish engulfing candle, or an enabled small-body candle with lower wick at least a configurable fraction of range and upper wick no greater than body, or an enabled doji with lower wick at least that fraction of range; current close must exceed the preceding high. Bullish engulfing requires a bullish preceding candle, a bearish candle before it, preceding close at least the earlier open, and preceding open at most the earlier close. A bearish trigger reverses these comparisons and wick roles, with current close below the preceding low. Small body and doji use separately configurable maximum fractions of range.
Calculation: Non-chop = a close crossed above the latest confirmed pivot high or below the latest confirmed pivot low within a configurable number of bars, and either both pivots are not available or their absolute difference is at least a configurable ATR multiple. For a cross above, the previous close is at or below the current pivot high; for a cross below, it is at or above the current pivot low.
Calculation: Signal-timeframe EMA state = its shorter EMA of close is above its longer EMA for bullish, or at or below its longer EMA for bearish. A recent cross means the corresponding EMA crossover or crossunder occurred within a configurable number of bars: chart bars for the chart signal and signal-timeframe bars for the mirrored signal.
Calculation: Base signal = at least two of the three bias timeframes have the indicated structure bias AND the alignment timeframe has that bias AND, optionally, the chart, signal and alignment timeframes all have that bias AND, optionally, the signal-timeframe EMA state agrees and its corresponding cross is recent when recency is enabled AND, optionally, the chart cluster holds and close is beyond both its EMA and VWAP in the indicated direction AND, optionally, the chart candle trigger agrees AND, optionally, the chart is non-chop. The mirrored base signal substitutes signal-timeframe candle trigger, non-chop and cluster for the chart versions, always requires signal and alignment structure biases to agree, and uses the signal-timeframe cross recency.
Calculation: Qualified signal = the applicable base signal AND, if the higher minimum tier is selected, all three bias timeframes agree and volume exceeds its moving average on the signal's own timeframe AND, if the highest minimum tier is selected, the cluster holds and close is beyond both EMA and VWAP in the indicated direction on the chart, signal and alignment timeframes for the chart signal, or on the signal and alignment timeframes for the mirrored signal. The chart cluster uses its selected tolerance; higher-timeframe clusters use ATR tolerance.
Long: Structure bias is bullish on the chart or any higher timeframe.
Short: Structure bias is bearish on the chart or any higher timeframe.
Long: Chart close is above both the chart long-term EMA and session VWAP.
Short: Chart close is below both the chart long-term EMA and session VWAP.
Long: The signal-timeframe shorter EMA crosses above its longer EMA, and this mapped cross was not present on the previous chart bar.
Short: The signal-timeframe shorter EMA crosses below its longer EMA, and this mapped cross was not present on the previous chart bar.
Long: Signal-timeframe EMA state is bullish.
Short: Signal-timeframe EMA state is bearish.
Long: The chart bullish candle trigger holds.
Short: The chart bearish candle trigger holds.
Long: Longs are enabled AND the chart qualified signal holds AND it did not hold on the preceding chart bar.
Short: Shorts are enabled AND the chart qualified signal holds AND it did not hold on the preceding chart bar.
Long: Longs are enabled AND the mirrored qualified signal holds AND it did not hold on the preceding chart bar.
Short: Shorts are enabled AND the mirrored qualified signal holds AND it did not hold on the preceding chart bar.
