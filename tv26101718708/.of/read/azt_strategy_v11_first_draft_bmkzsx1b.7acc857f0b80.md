Calculation: Structure bias = on each timeframe, retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low; set bullish when close breaks above the latest pivot high, bearish when close breaks below the latest pivot low, and otherwise retain the prior bias. Use the same pivot-strength parameter on all timeframes. Higher-timeframe readings use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Rejection candle trigger = bullish if the previous candle is bullish and engulfs the candle before it, or has a small body with a long lower wick and upper wick no larger than its body, or has a doji-sized body with a long lower wick; bearish uses the mirrored conditions. A trigger confirms when the current close exceeds the previous high for bullish, or falls below the previous low for bearish.
Calculation: EMA/VWAP cluster = the distance between the EMA and VWAP is no greater than an ATR-based tolerance; bullish requires close above both, bearish requires close below both. Use the same EMA lookback and ATR length on the chart and higher timeframes.
Calculation: Structure regime is valid = a structural break occurred within the recency window and the distance between the latest pivot high and low is at least the ATR-based range threshold.
Long: At least two of the three longest higher-timeframe structure biases are bullish AND the next higher-timeframe structure bias is bullish AND chart, shorter higher-timeframe, and next higher-timeframe structure biases are all bullish AND the shorter higher-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA is above its longer-lookback EMA with an upward cross within the recency window AND chart EMA/VWAP cluster is bullish AND a bullish rejection candle trigger confirms AND structure regime is valid
Long: Separately, the mirrored higher-timeframe signal: at least two of the three longest higher-timeframe structure biases are bullish AND the next higher-timeframe structure bias is bullish AND shorter and next higher-timeframe structure biases are bullish AND shorter higher-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA is above its longer-lookback EMA with an upward cross within the recency window AND shorter higher-timeframe EMA/VWAP cluster is bullish AND a bullish rejection candle trigger confirms on that higher timeframe AND its structure regime is valid
Short: At least two of the three longest higher-timeframe structure biases are bearish AND the next higher-timeframe structure bias is bearish AND chart, shorter higher-timeframe, and next higher-timeframe structure biases are all bearish AND the shorter higher-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA is below its longer-lookback EMA with a downward cross within the recency window AND chart EMA/VWAP cluster is bearish AND a bearish rejection candle trigger confirms AND structure regime is valid
Short: Separately, the mirrored higher-timeframe signal: at least two of the three longest higher-timeframe structure biases are bearish AND the next higher-timeframe structure bias is bearish AND shorter and next higher-timeframe structure biases are bearish AND shorter higher-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA is below its longer-lookback EMA with a downward cross within the recency window AND shorter higher-timeframe EMA/VWAP cluster is bearish AND a bearish rejection candle trigger confirms on that higher timeframe AND its structure regime is valid
