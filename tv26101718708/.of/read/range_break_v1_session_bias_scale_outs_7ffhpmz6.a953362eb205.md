Calculation: Session ranges = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in each configured Asia, London, and opening-range session; reset all session and breakout state at each Asia session start. A selected range is ready after its session ends. London move = close of the first chart bar after the London session minus open of its first chart bar. London range and Asia range = their respective session highs minus lows.
Calculation: Volatility score = the number of true conditions among: Asia range divided by the chart-bar SMA of the most recently completed Asia range is at most its threshold; absolute London move divided by Asia range is at least its threshold; London range divided by Asia range is at most its threshold. The completed Asia range is held constant between session ends, so the SMA samples it on every chart bar. An unavailable ratio contributes zero.
Calculation: Bullish bias = session bias filtering disabled OR (completed Asia and London data available AND London move > 0 AND London move > Asia range multiplied by the dominance threshold AND (outside-close filtering disabled OR the close of the first bar after London > Asia high) AND (volatility filtering disabled OR volatility score >= its threshold)). Bearish bias = the corresponding negative London move with its absolute value > Asia range multiplied by the dominance threshold and, when required, the close of the first bar after London < Asia low. Neutral permission for a side applies only when bias filtering is enabled and neither bias is true.
Calculation: Breakout state = while the trade session is open and the selected completed range passes its optional inclusive size bounds, record whether high > range high plus breakout buffer, low < range low minus breakout buffer, low < range low, and high > range high. After recording breaks on each bar, record a long retest if a long break has occurred and low <= range high plus retest tolerance, and a short retest if a short break has occurred and high >= range low minus retest tolerance. All states persist until the next Asia session start, and the break, retest, or sweep may occur on the entry bar.
Calculation: Base stop = the selected range's opposite boundary, its midpoint, the opposite London extreme, the opposite Asia extreme, or entry price offset adversely by a fixed distance. Risk = entry price minus base stop for a long, or base stop minus entry price for a short; risk is valid only if positive. Each of three configured targets = entry price offset favorably by either its own fixed distance or its own risk multiple. The active stop becomes entry price after the first target has been touched if break-even is enabled; the first target touch is tracked even if scale-outs are disabled.
Long: trade session open AND selected range ready AND (range-size filter disabled OR range size >= minimum AND range size <= maximum) AND (bullish bias OR neutral long permission enabled) AND high > range high plus breakout buffer AND (direct mode OR retest mode AND long retest recorded OR sweep mode AND low < range low has been recorded) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND position is flat AND (one-trade-per-day disabled OR no entry signal since the latest Asia session start).
Short: trade session open AND selected range ready AND (range-size filter disabled OR range size >= minimum AND range size <= maximum) AND (bearish bias OR neutral short permission enabled) AND low < range low minus breakout buffer AND (direct mode OR retest mode AND short retest recorded OR sweep mode AND high > range high has been recorded) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND position is flat AND (one-trade-per-day disabled OR no entry signal since the latest Asia session start).
Exit: long, scale-outs enabled AND risk > 0 AND (stop enabled AND low <= active stop OR high >= any unfilled target); exit the configured fraction assigned to each affected target.
Exit: short, scale-outs enabled AND risk > 0 AND (stop enabled AND high >= active stop OR low <= any unfilled target); exit the configured fraction assigned to each affected target.
Exit: long OR short, forced exit enabled AND bar hour equals the configured hour AND bar minute equals the configured minute.
