Calculation: Active fair value gaps = On each of three higher timeframes, ordered from shortest to longest, use the last completed bar and the bar two bars before it. A bullish gap has last completed low > earlier high, with bottom = earlier high and top = last completed low. A bearish gap has last completed high < earlier low, with bottom = last completed high and top = earlier low. Require gap height >= either a minimum gap size or a multiple of that timeframe's ATR on its last completed bar. Retain only the latest qualifying gap per direction and timeframe; clear a bullish gap when a last completed close < its bottom, or a bearish gap when that close > its top.
Calculation: Gap strength = When strength rating is enabled, assign a score according to ascending thresholds of gap height divided by ATR, using ascending scores; use a fallback score when ATR <= 0. When disabled, use a fixed score.
Calculation: Merged gap = For each direction, start with the active gap from the shortest higher timeframe that has one. Process the remaining timeframes in ascending order; add a gap if the absolute difference between its midpoint and the current merged midpoint <= a multiple of the shortest higher timeframe's ATR on its last completed bar. On addition, set top to the maximum top, bottom to the minimum bottom, strength to the maximum component strength, and formation time to the latest component formation time. Count included timeframes. Merged strength is the maximum component strength plus a bonus per additional timeframe, capped at a maximum score.
Calculation: Stored zones = Store each accepted merged gap by direction, optionally normalizing its height around its midpoint to either a multiple of the shortest higher timeframe's last completed ATR or a percentage of its last completed close. After each chart bar's decision, remove bullish zones when the shortest higher timeframe's last completed low <= the zone top, bottom, or midpoint for touch, full-fill, or midpoint-fill mode respectively; remove bearish zones when its last completed high >= the zone bottom, top, or midpoint respectively. If mitigated zones are retained, keep them in the stored set. Limit each direction's stored zones by removing the oldest when the configured maximum is exceeded.
Long: On a completed chart bar, the bullish merged gap's timeframe count >= the minimum confluence count AND its merged strength >= the minimum strength AND its formation time > the formation time of the last accepted bullish merged gap, or no bullish merged gap has been accepted yet, AND the absolute distance from its midpoint to every stored bullish zone midpoint >= a multiple of the shortest higher timeframe's last completed ATR.
Short: On a completed chart bar, the bearish merged gap's timeframe count >= the minimum confluence count AND its merged strength >= the minimum strength AND its formation time > the formation time of the last accepted bearish merged gap, or no bearish merged gap has been accepted yet, AND the absolute distance from its midpoint to every stored bearish zone midpoint >= a multiple of the shortest higher timeframe's last completed ATR.
