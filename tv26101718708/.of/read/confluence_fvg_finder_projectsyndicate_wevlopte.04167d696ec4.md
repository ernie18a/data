Calculation: Bullish FVG = On a higher timeframe, low of the last completed bar > high three bars earlier; zone = [high three bars earlier, low of last completed bar]. Keep the latest zone until the close of a completed bar is below its bottom. Accept it when its gap is at least ATR × the minimum-gap multiplier if the ATR filter is enabled, otherwise at least the minimum gap size.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = On a higher timeframe, high of the last completed bar < low three bars earlier; zone = [high of last completed bar, low three bars earlier]. Keep the latest zone until the close of a completed bar is above its top. Apply the same minimum-gap filter as for a bullish FVG.
Calculation: FVG strength = If strength rating is disabled, 5; otherwise, for gap size / ATR, 1.5 if ratio ≥ 1.5, 6 if ≥ 1, 4.5 if ≥ 0.75, 3 if ≥ 0.5, and 1.5 otherwise; use 3 when ATR ≤ 0.
Calculation: Merged zone = Starting with the lowest of the three higher timeframes, add each available same-direction FVG from the next higher timeframe when the absolute difference between its midpoint and the current merged-zone midpoint ≤ anchor-timeframe ATR × proximity multiplier. Merge bounds by taking the highest top and lowest bottom. Merged strength = min(10, highest member strength + (number of members − 1) × confluence bonus); merged time = latest member formation time.
Long: Chart bar is confirmed AND a merged bullish zone has at least the minimum timeframe count AND merged strength ≥ minimum strength AND merged time is later than the last emitted bullish zone time AND no existing bullish zone midpoint is within 0.5 × anchor-timeframe ATR of the merged-zone midpoint.
Short: Chart bar is confirmed AND a merged bearish zone has at least the minimum timeframe count AND merged strength ≥ minimum strength AND merged time is later than the last emitted bearish zone time AND no existing bearish zone midpoint is within 0.5 × anchor-timeframe ATR of the merged-zone midpoint.
