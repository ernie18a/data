Calculation: Delta = Sum each lower-timeframe bar's volume when its close is above the previous lower-timeframe close, minus its volume when below; use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including a still-forming bar in realtime.
Calculation: Gom momentum = Current Delta plus previous Gom momentum when both are nonnegative or both are nonpositive; otherwise, current Delta alone.
Long: Gom momentum >= 0
Short: Gom momentum < 0
