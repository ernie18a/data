Calculation: Candle body = absolute difference between close and open
Long: Long is armed AND close > medium-term EMA AND close > longer-term EMA AND (close crosses above medium-term EMA OR close crosses above longer-term EMA OR close crosses above longest-term EMA) AND candle body > an ATR-scaled threshold
Short: Short is armed AND close < longer-term EMA AND (close crosses below medium-term EMA OR close crosses below longer-term EMA OR close crosses below longest-term EMA) AND candle body > an ATR-scaled threshold
Exit: Long, close crosses below short-term EMA
Exit: Short, close crosses above short-term EMA
