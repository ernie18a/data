Calculation: MACD histogram = (EMA of close with the shorter lookback − EMA of close with the longer lookback) − EMA of that difference
Calculation: Projected histogram = current MACD histogram + (current linear-regression estimate of the histogram − its estimate offset one bar backward) × projection horizon
Calculation: Low sweep active = set when low < the lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback; age is 0 on the sweep bar and increments on each later bar; remains active through the maximum allowed age, then expires; cleared by a long signal. High sweep active is defined symmetrically using high > the highest high and is cleared by a short signal.
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low + a small positive constant)
Long: Low sweep active AND (candle body ratio > displacement threshold AND close > open OR volume > its moving average × volume multiplier AND close > open) AND (MACD histogram < zero tolerance AND projected histogram > 0 OR MACD histogram crosses above 0) AND MACD histogram < maximum entry distance AND MACD histogram > previous-bar histogram AND NOT (all preceding long conditions were true on the previous bar) AND, when close confirmation is enabled, bar is confirmed
Short: High sweep active AND (candle body ratio > displacement threshold AND close < open OR volume > its moving average × volume multiplier AND close < open) AND (MACD histogram > −zero tolerance AND projected histogram < 0 OR MACD histogram crosses below 0) AND MACD histogram > −maximum entry distance AND MACD histogram < previous-bar histogram AND NOT (all preceding short conditions were true on the previous bar) AND, when close confirmation is enabled, bar is confirmed
