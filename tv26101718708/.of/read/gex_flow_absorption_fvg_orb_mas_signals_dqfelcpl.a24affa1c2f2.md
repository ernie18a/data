Calculation: Delta ratio = 100 times the sum of signed volume of all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, divided by chart-bar volume; sign is positive when lower-timeframe close > open, negative when close < open, and zero otherwise. If no lower-timeframe bars exist, use chart-bar volume when close > open and negative chart-bar volume otherwise. If chart-bar volume is zero, the ratio is zero.
Calculation: Bullish gap = a bar whose low > the high two bars earlier and whose difference is at least the minimum gap size; its bounds are that earlier high and current low. Bearish gap = a bar whose high < the low two bars earlier and whose difference is at least the minimum gap size; its bounds are current high and that earlier low. Track the most recent configured number of chart-timeframe gaps together and the most recent configured number separately for each higher timeframe. Remove a bullish gap when a chart-bar close < its lower bound and a bearish gap when a chart-bar close > its upper bound. Check existing gaps for removal and retest before adding new gaps. Higher-timeframe gaps use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it, and are added on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = volume > its simple moving average times a multiplier AND delta ratio <= negative delta threshold AND close >= (high + low) / 2. Bearish absorption = volume > its simple moving average times a multiplier AND delta ratio >= delta threshold AND close <= (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish chart-gap retest = an active bullish chart-timeframe gap remains after removal AND low <= its upper bound AND, if first-retest-only is enabled, it has not been touched since creation. Bearish chart-gap retest = an active bearish chart-timeframe gap remains after removal AND high >= its lower bound AND, if first-retest-only is enabled, it has not been touched since creation. Mark a gap touched whenever its respective price condition is met.
Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe-gap retest = an active bullish higher-timeframe gap remains after removal AND low <= its upper bound AND it has not been touched since creation. Bearish higher-timeframe-gap retest = an active bearish higher-timeframe gap remains after removal AND high >= its lower bound AND it has not been touched since creation. Mark a gap touched whenever its respective price condition is met.
Calculation: Bullish bias for each of two selected timeframes = that timeframe's close > its EMA AND its EMA > its previous-bar EMA. Bearish bias = its close < its EMA AND its EMA < its previous-bar EMA. The two timeframes use the same EMA length; one timeframe is shorter than the other. Either may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Use the last completed bar or the still-forming bar according to the confirmation setting; when a selected timeframe is lower, use its last available intrabar.
Calculation: Bullish trigger count = the number of enabled conditions met among bullish absorption, bullish chart-gap retest, bullish higher-timeframe-gap retest, and low <= signal moving average AND close > signal moving average AND, if the slope filter is enabled, signal moving average > its value at the slope lookback. Bearish trigger count = the number of enabled conditions met among bearish absorption, bearish chart-gap retest, bearish higher-timeframe-gap retest, and high >= signal moving average AND close < signal moving average AND, if the slope filter is enabled, signal moving average < its value at the slope lookback. The signal moving average is a selected moving average of chart-bar close.
Calculation: Bullish score = bullish trigger count + 1 if close > anchored VWAP + 1 if both timeframe biases are bullish. Bearish score = bearish trigger count + 1 if close < anchored VWAP + 1 if both timeframe biases are bearish. Anchored VWAP uses chart-bar typical price and volume, resetting at the selected New York daily, New York session-start, or weekly anchor. Score ranks are ordered by thresholds: below the lower threshold, at least the lower threshold, at least the middle threshold, and at least the upper threshold.

Short: A bearish chart-timeframe gap forms OR a bearish gap forms on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.

Short: Bearish bias on either selected timeframe OR bearish bias on both selected timeframes.

Short: The count of chart bars with both timeframe biases bearish since the New York day began > the corresponding count with both biases bullish.
Long: Close > the open of the first chart bar of the New York day.
Short: Close < the open of the first chart bar of the New York day.
Long: Bullish trigger count >= minimum trigger count AND bullish trigger count > bearish trigger count AND bullish score rank >= minimum rank AND ATR is available AND bars since the last buy or sell signal > cooldown AND, if enabled, the chart bar is inside the selected New York signal window AND, if required, close > anchored VWAP AND, if required, both timeframe biases are bullish.
