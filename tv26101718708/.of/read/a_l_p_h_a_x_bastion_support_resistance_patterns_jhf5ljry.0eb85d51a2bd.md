Calculation: Higher-timeframe series = On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on realtime chart bars, use the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Forward-fill the latest available value between higher-timeframe updates.
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = Maintain separate newest-first lists from chart-timeframe confirmed pivot lows and highs, respectively. Optionally also process confirmed pivot lows and highs from a higher timeframe, using its forward-filled series on every chart bar, including repeated values. Process chart pivots before higher-timeframe pivots. A pivot is confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on each side. For each pivot, merge into the first same-side zone whose midpoint is within the merge-distance multiple of current chart ATR: replace its midpoint with the touch-count-weighted mean including the new pivot, expand its boundaries to include the pivot plus and minus the zone-half-height multiple of current ATR, increment touches, and update last-touch bar. Do not change its original higher-timeframe flag. Otherwise create a zone centered on the pivot with those boundaries, one touch, the current birth and last-touch bar, and the pivot's higher-timeframe flag; insert it first and remove the oldest list entry if the side's maximum count is exceeded. After processing pivots, remove zones whose bars since last touch exceed the decay limit.
Calculation: Zone strength = Touch count plus a higher-timeframe bonus if the zone was originally created from a higher-timeframe pivot, plus a recency bonus if bars since birth are below the recency limit.
Calculation: Near(price, zone) = Absolute distance from price to zone midpoint is at most the proximity multiple of chart ATR OR price is between the zone boundaries inclusive.
Calculation: Range = Maximum of high minus low and the minimum price increment parameter; lower wick = minimum of open and close minus low; upper wick = high minus maximum of open and close.
Calculation: Choppiness = If the highest high minus lowest low over the lookback is positive, 100 times the base-10 logarithm of the sum of true ranges over that lookback divided by that high-low difference, divided by the base-10 logarithm of the lookback; otherwise the neutral choppiness value.
Calculation: Long patterns for a support zone = In priority order, select the first enabled matching pattern: rejection when lower wick divided by range is at least the wick threshold AND close is above minimum(open, close) plus a range fraction AND close is above zone bottom; sweep when low is below zone bottom minus the sweep-depth multiple of ATR AND close is above zone midpoint AND close is above open; retest when any prior close within the retest lookback is below the current zone bottom AND low is at most zone top AND close is above zone midpoint AND close is above open; compression when each true range in the compression lookback is at most a multiple of current ATR AND Near(close, zone) AND close is above zone midpoint.
Calculation: Short patterns for a resistance zone = In priority order, select the first enabled matching pattern: rejection when upper wick divided by range is at least the wick threshold AND close is below maximum(open, close) minus a range fraction AND close is below zone top; sweep when high is above zone top plus the sweep-depth multiple of ATR AND close is below zone midpoint AND close is below open; retest when any prior close within the retest lookback is above the current zone top AND high is at least zone bottom AND close is below zone midpoint AND close is below open; compression when each true range in the compression lookback is at most a multiple of current ATR AND Near(close, zone) AND close is below zone midpoint.
Calculation: Candidate score = Sum one point each for zone strength at least its minimum; higher-timeframe bias disabled OR aligned with the candidate; volume filter disabled OR volume at least its moving average times the volume multiplier; choppiness filter disabled OR choppiness below its threshold; selected pattern is sweep or retest; range at least its ATR multiple; session filter disabled OR the bar is in the active session. Higher-timeframe bias is aligned long when the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA AND higher-timeframe close is above the shorter EMA; aligned short when both comparisons are reversed. Use the higher-timeframe series for close and EMAs.
Calculation: Selected candidate = After the warmup, consider each support zone for a long candidate when its strength is at least the minimum AND Near(low, zone) AND a long pattern matches; then consider each resistance zone for a short candidate when its strength is at least the minimum AND Near(high, zone) AND a short pattern matches. Require the candidate score to be at least its minimum AND the higher-timeframe bias to be disabled or aligned. Select the candidate with the highest score, retaining the first candidate on ties; traverse each zone list newest first and support before resistance.
Long: Selected candidate is long AND choppiness filter is disabled OR choppiness is below its threshold AND session filter is disabled OR the bar is in the active session AND bars since the last emitted signal are at least the cooldown or no signal has been emitted, with the OR conditions grouped individually.
Short: Selected candidate is short AND choppiness filter is disabled OR choppiness is below its threshold AND session filter is disabled OR the bar is in the active session AND bars since the last emitted signal are at least the cooldown or no signal has been emitted, with the OR conditions grouped individually.
Long: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA > longer EMA AND higher-timeframe close > shorter EMA.
Short: Higher-timeframe shorter EMA < longer EMA AND higher-timeframe close < shorter EMA.
