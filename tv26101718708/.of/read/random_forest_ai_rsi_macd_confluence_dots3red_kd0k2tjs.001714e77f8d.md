Calculation: Features = RSI of close; MACD histogram of close using a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA; ATR ratio = ATR / max(SMA of ATR, epsilon); volume ratio = volume / SMA of volume when that SMA > 0, otherwise 1; DI difference = +DI - -DI; price displacement = (close - SMA of close) / max(ATR, epsilon).
Calculation: Votes = For each of the following paired conditions, assign +1 if the first condition holds, otherwise -1 if the second holds, otherwise 0. Each threshold is a parameter; positive and negative counterparts have equal magnitude, RSI extreme thresholds are symmetric around the RSI midpoint, and the wider RSI extreme thresholds are farther from the midpoint than the narrower ones. The higher volume-expansion threshold exceeds the lower one; the higher ATR-ratio ceiling exceeds the lower one; the larger displacement and DI thresholds exceed their respective smaller thresholds. (1) RSI < lower extreme / RSI > upper extreme; (2) MACD histogram > 0 / MACD histogram < 0; (3) RSI < lower narrow threshold AND MACD histogram > 0 / RSI > upper narrow threshold AND MACD histogram < 0; (4) DI difference > larger positive threshold / DI difference < corresponding negative threshold; (5) price displacement > smaller positive threshold / price displacement < corresponding negative threshold; (6) MACD histogram > 0 AND volume ratio > lower expansion threshold / MACD histogram < 0 AND volume ratio > lower expansion threshold; (7) RSI > midpoint AND RSI > previous RSI / RSI < midpoint AND RSI < previous RSI; (8) ATR ratio < higher ceiling AND DI difference > smaller positive threshold / ATR ratio < higher ceiling AND DI difference < corresponding negative threshold; (9) price displacement < larger negative threshold AND RSI < lower reversal threshold / price displacement > corresponding positive threshold AND RSI > upper reversal threshold; (10) volume ratio > higher expansion threshold AND MACD histogram > 0 / volume ratio > higher expansion threshold AND MACD histogram < 0; (11) RSI > midpoint AND RSI < upper extreme / RSI < midpoint AND RSI > lower extreme; (12) DI difference > 0 AND volume ratio > unity / DI difference < 0 AND volume ratio > unity; (13) MACD histogram > 0 AND price displacement > 0 / MACD histogram < 0 AND price displacement < 0; (14) ATR ratio < lower ceiling AND RSI < lower extreme / ATR ratio < lower ceiling AND RSI > upper extreme; (15) DI difference > 0 AND MACD histogram > 0 AND price displacement > 0 / DI difference < 0 AND MACD histogram < 0 AND price displacement < 0.
Calculation: Bullish share = For each vote, cumulatively grade every nonzero vote after the outcome lookback: it is correct if its sign matches the sign of the close change over that lookback; an unchanged close is incorrect. Include the grade completed on the current closed bar. The vote's weight is cumulative correct grades divided by cumulative graded votes once the minimum sample count is reached, otherwise a neutral half weight. Divide the sum of weights of current positive votes by the sum of weights of all current nonzero votes; use a neutral half share when that denominator is zero.
Calculation: Verdict = bullish if bullish share >= bullish threshold; otherwise bearish if bullish share <= bearish threshold; otherwise neutral. The bullish threshold is at least the neutral half share and the bearish threshold is at most the neutral half share.
Calculation: Marker eligibility = The current verdict and RSI extreme agree in direction, that agreement was false on the previous bar, no marker has fired since the latest verdict change, and either no previous marker of the same direction exists or at least the cooldown number of bars has elapsed since it. Reset the shared fired flag whenever the verdict changes; set it and record the bar for that direction when a marker fires.
