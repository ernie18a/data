Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close over a configurable lookback
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line, using configurable fast, slow, and signal lookbacks
Calculation: ATR ratio = current 14-bar ATR divided by its configurable-lookback simple moving average
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume divided by its configurable-lookback simple moving average of volume
Calculation: DI difference = positive directional index minus negative directional index, using the same configurable DMI lookback for both
Calculation: Price extension = (close − configurable-lookback simple moving average of close) divided by 14-bar ATR
Calculation: Tree votes = 15 ternary votes: (1) bullish at oversold RSI, bearish at overbought RSI; (2) bullish when MACD histogram is positive, bearish when negative; (3) bullish when RSI is low and MACD histogram positive, bearish when RSI is high and MACD histogram negative; (4) bullish when DI difference exceeds a positive threshold, bearish when below its negative; (5) bullish when price extension exceeds a positive threshold, bearish when below its negative; (6) bullish when MACD histogram is positive and volume ratio exceeds a threshold, bearish when MACD histogram is negative and volume ratio exceeds it; (7) bullish when RSI is above its midpoint and rising, bearish when below its midpoint and falling; (8) bullish when ATR ratio is below a threshold and DI difference exceeds a positive threshold, bearish when ATR ratio is below it and DI difference is below its negative; (9) bearish when price extension is high and RSI high, bullish when price extension is low and RSI low; (10) bearish when volume ratio is high and MACD histogram negative, bullish when volume ratio is high and MACD histogram positive; (11) bullish when RSI is between its midpoint and overbought level, bearish when between oversold level and midpoint; (12) bullish when DI difference is positive and volume ratio exceeds its baseline, bearish when DI difference is negative and volume ratio exceeds its baseline; (13) bullish when MACD histogram is positive and price extension positive, bearish when both are negative; (14) bullish when ATR ratio is low and RSI oversold, bearish when ATR ratio is low and RSI overbought; (15) bullish when DI difference, MACD histogram, and price extension are all positive, bearish when all are negative; otherwise each vote is neutral
Calculation: Vote weight = for each tree, its fraction of correct non-neutral votes over the configurable minimum sample count; use 0.5 until that count is reached. Grade each vote after the configurable outcome window by comparing the sign of the vote with the sign of the change in close over that window.
Calculation: Bullish vote share = sum of weights for bullish votes divided by sum of weights for all non-neutral votes; use 50% if there are no non-neutral votes
Calculation: Bearish vote share = 100% − bullish vote share
Long: bullish vote share ≥ configurable bullish threshold OR (bullish vote share ≥ bullish threshold AND RSI oversold AND current bar confirmed AND bullish signal condition is newly true AND no bullish signal has fired in the current verdict run AND bullish cooldown elapsed)
Short: bearish vote share ≥ configurable bearish threshold OR (bearish vote share ≥ bearish threshold AND RSI overbought AND current bar confirmed AND bearish signal condition is newly true AND no signal has fired in the current verdict run AND bearish cooldown elapsed)
