Calculation: Cross above = current first value > current second value AND previous first value <= previous second value; cross below reverses the inequalities.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot with the same number of chart bars on each side, available only after the right-hand bars have completed. The latest and previous swings are updated on confirmation.
Calculation: Structure = initially neutral; set bullish when close crosses above the current latest swing high, and bearish when close crosses below the current latest swing low. A change of character is a break against the immediately preceding structure. A new pivot may change the reference level used for both current and previous closes in the break test.
Calculation: Upward displacement = low > high two bars ago AND close > open AND volume > a multiple of current volume SMA. Downward displacement = high < low two bars ago AND close < open AND volume > a multiple of current volume SMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = higher-timeframe close above its shorter EMA AND shorter EMA above longer EMA for bullish; reverse both comparisons for bearish. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars; a still-forming higher-timeframe bar can be used in real time.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe vote = count of a fixed set of progressively longer timeframes whose shorter close EMA > longer close EMA. Each timeframe may be lower or higher than the chart timeframe: use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar.
Calculation: Trend filter = choose one: close relative to a filter EMA, Supertrend direction, multi-timeframe vote relative to its majority threshold, or no filter. Bullish and bearish EMA conditions use strict > and < respectively; bullish and bearish Supertrend conditions use its up and down directions; a majority of bullish votes is bullish and all other vote counts are bearish.
Calculation: Momentum long trigger = smoothed stochastic K crosses above its signal D OR RSI crosses above its buy threshold OR MACD histogram crosses above zero OR close crosses above the shorter close EMA. Momentum short trigger reverses the crosses, using the RSI sell threshold. The stochastic D is an SMA of the smoothed K; MACD uses shorter and longer EMAs and a signal EMA.
Calculation: Momentum signal = the corresponding momentum trigger AND corresponding trend filter AND sufficient chart history AND optional session membership AND bars since the last momentum signal >= cooldown. The cooldown is shared by both directions; the long signal takes precedence if both qualify on the same confirmed bar.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = the maximum of two consecutive confirmed swing highs whose difference is <= an ATR multiple, or the minimum of two consecutive confirmed swing lows whose difference is <= an ATR multiple. A pool is active until high >= the high pool or low <= the low pool; retain a bounded pool history. A sweep arms the opposite-direction setup at the swept level, replacing any previously pending sweep.
Calculation: Zone = bullish order block when upward displacement occurs AND the candle two bars earlier is bearish AND its volume > a multiple of the current volume SMA; its bounds are that earlier candle's high and low. A bearish order block reverses the directions. Also record upward gaps from high two bars ago to current low and downward gaps from current high to low two bars ago. An upward gap becomes a bearish inverted zone when close falls below its lower bound; a downward gap becomes a bullish inverted zone when close rises above its upper bound; require gap height >= an ATR multiple at inversion. Retain bounded histories of gaps and zones. From the bar after creation, a bullish zone is tapped when low <= its upper bound and a bearish zone when high >= its lower bound; deactivate the tapped zone and arm its direction and bounds, replacing any earlier pending zone.
Calculation: Zone confirmation = a pending zone tap no older than the confirmation window AND no active trade AND the corresponding candle direction AND close at or beyond the zone midpoint in that direction. Optionally require matching structure; optionally require close below the latest swing midpoint for a higher-timeframe bullish long or above it for a higher-timeframe bearish short; optionally require volume > a multiple of its SMA OR corresponding displacement. Enable the higher-timeframe-aligned setup class when its trend agrees and the local setup class otherwise. If both swings are unavailable, use the current bar's typical price as the midpoint. The pending zone is cleared on confirmation, even if the subsequent trade cooldown, history, or session filter prevents entry.
Calculation: Sweep confirmation = a pending opposite-side liquidity-pool sweep no older than the confirmation window AND no active trade AND a change of character in the setup direction AND a candle in that direction. Optionally require volume > a multiple of its SMA OR corresponding displacement. Enable the higher-timeframe-aligned setup class when its trend agrees and the local setup class otherwise. A qualifying zone confirmation takes precedence; the pending sweep is cleared on confirmation even if the subsequent trade cooldown, history, or session filter prevents entry.
Calculation: Trade entry = a zone confirmation OR sweep confirmation OR momentum signal, in that priority order, AND no active trade AND bars since the last trade entry >= trade cooldown AND sufficient chart history AND optional session membership. Entry price is the confirmation bar's close. Momentum arrows have their own independent cooldown and do not require a flat trade.
Calculation: Entry zone bounds = tapped zone bounds for a zone entry; for a long sweep entry, the lower bound is the minimum of current low and swept level and the upper bound is close; for a short sweep entry, the upper bound is the maximum of current high and swept level and the lower bound is close. For a momentum entry, use the lowest low of its stop lookback and close for a long, or close and the highest high of that lookback for a short.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry-zone lower bound minus an ATR buffer for a long, or upper bound plus an ATR buffer for a short. Risk = maximum of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and an ATR floor. After the first target is touched, immediately move the stop to entry, including when both are touched on the same bar.
Calculation: Targets = at entry, collect latest and previous confirmed swing highs and lows, active unswept liquidity pools, rolling high-low midpoint levels from two distinct timeframes, and previous completed-period highs and lows from multiple timeframes. For each other-timeframe midpoint use its last completed bar on historical chart bars and its still-forming bar in real time; each such timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using the last available intrabar when lower. For previous-period levels use the last completed period. Keep candidate duplicates, sort by distance in the trade direction, and select the first three beyond an ATR buffer from entry. If a target is missing, set the first to entry plus or minus risk and each subsequent missing target to the preceding target plus or minus risk. Hold targets fixed after entry.
Long: A confirmed swing high > the previous confirmed swing high OR no previous swing high exists.
Short: A confirmed swing high <= the previous confirmed swing high.
Long: A confirmed swing low >= the previous confirmed swing low AND a previous swing low exists.
Short: A confirmed swing low < the previous confirmed swing low OR no previous swing low exists.
Long: Close crosses above the latest swing high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest swing low.
Long: Momentum long signal OR long trade entry.
Short: Momentum short signal OR short trade entry.
Exit: long, no momentum signal on this bar AND momentum state is long AND (MACD histogram crosses below zero OR close crosses below the shorter EMA OR the selected trend filter flips bearish). Momentum state becomes long or short on the corresponding momentum signal and becomes neutral on this exit; signals take precedence over exits.
Exit: short, no momentum signal on this bar AND momentum state is short AND (MACD histogram crosses above zero OR close crosses above the shorter EMA OR the selected trend filter flips bullish). Momentum state becomes long or short on the corresponding momentum signal and becomes neutral on this exit; signals take precedence over exits.
Exit: long, active trade AND bar after entry AND (low <= current stop OR high >= third target OR bars since entry > class-specific holding limit OR bearish change of character OR optional opposite momentum short signal OR optional smart exit: MACD histogram crosses below zero OR downward displacement OR close > entry AND close < open AND current and previous volume are each below the current volume SMA).
Exit: short, active trade AND bar after entry AND (high >= current stop OR low <= third target OR bars since entry > class-specific holding limit OR bullish change of character OR optional opposite momentum long signal OR optional smart exit: MACD histogram crosses above zero OR upward displacement OR close < entry AND close > open AND current and previous volume are each below the current volume SMA).
