Calculation: Basis = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3; its lookback is between those of the fast and slow close EMAs.
Calculation: Safe ATR = maximum of ATR and the minimum price increment, with unavailable ATR replaced by zero.
Calculation: Channels = basis plus or minus an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Regime = trend when choppiness index is below its threshold AND absolute basis change over a slope lookback / safe ATR exceeds a threshold AND the fraction of closes above basis OR the fraction below basis over that lookback exceeds a persistence threshold; otherwise expansion when ATR / SMA(ATR) exceeds its threshold AND choppiness is below a second, higher threshold; otherwise quiet when choppiness is at least its threshold AND ATR / SMA(ATR) is below 1; otherwise noisy. Use the same lookback for the choppiness index and SMA(ATR); substitute 50 for undefined choppiness and 1 for an undefined ATR ratio.
Calculation: Directional quality score = a weighted sum of clipped, linearly scaled relative volume, directional channel stretch, directional close rate of change, absolute candle body / safe ATR, directional fast-minus-slow EMA gap / safe ATR, and regime value. Channel stretch is (close − upper channel) / safe ATR for long and (lower channel − close) / safe ATR for short; negate rate of change and the EMA gap for short. Clip each scaled component to [0, 1]. The first three component weights are equal; the body weight is smaller, the EMA-gap weight is smaller still, and the regime weight is largest. Assign the highest regime value to trend, followed by expansion, noisy, and quiet. Use the same scaling bounds and weights for both directions.
Calculation: Planned risk = absolute difference between the signal-bar close and the initial stop, where the initial long stop is min(signal-bar low, signal-bar basis) − an ATR multiple and the initial short stop is max(signal-bar high, signal-bar basis) + the same ATR multiple; use signal-bar safe ATR. Retain the planned risk from the latest corresponding entry signal.
Long: Fast close EMA > slow close EMA AND close > basis.
Short: Fast close EMA < slow close EMA AND close < basis.
Long: Long directional quality score > short directional quality score.
Short: Short directional quality score > long directional quality score.
Long: Close > short-context close EMA.
Short: Close < short-context close EMA.
Long: Close > session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3.
Short: Close < session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3.
Long: Datetime is within the configured trading window AND bars since the last entry signal > cooldown AND fast close EMA > slow close EMA AND close > basis AND (liquidity filter disabled OR SMA(close × volume) >= liquidity threshold) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= its threshold) AND long directional quality score >= its threshold AND ((breakout enabled AND close > upper channel AND close > previous high) OR (continuation enabled AND lowest low over the pullback lookback <= fast EMA + safe ATR × pullback-zone multiplier OR continuation enabled AND lowest low over the pullback lookback <= basis + safe ATR × pullback-zone multiplier) AND (pullback-hold filter disabled OR lowest low over a shorter hold lookback > basis − safe ATR × hold-zone multiplier) AND previous close >= previous low AND previous close <= previous high AND high > previous high AND close > previous high AND close > fast EMA).
Short: Datetime is within the configured trading window AND bars since the last entry signal > cooldown AND fast close EMA < slow close EMA AND close < basis AND (liquidity filter disabled OR SMA(close × volume) >= liquidity threshold) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= its threshold) AND short directional quality score >= its threshold AND ((breakout enabled AND close < lower channel AND close < previous low) OR (continuation enabled AND highest high over the pullback lookback >= fast EMA − safe ATR × pullback-zone multiplier OR continuation enabled AND highest high over the pullback lookback >= basis − safe ATR × pullback-zone multiplier) AND (pullback-hold filter disabled OR highest high over a shorter hold lookback < basis + safe ATR × hold-zone multiplier) AND previous close >= previous low AND previous close <= previous high AND low < previous low AND close < previous low AND close < fast EMA).
Exit: Long, short entry signal occurs.
Exit: Short, long entry signal occurs.
Exit: Long, low reaches entry price − planned risk OR high reaches entry price + planned risk × reward/risk multiplier.
Exit: Short, high reaches entry price + planned risk OR low reaches entry price − planned risk × reward/risk multiplier.
Exit: Long, break-even enabled AND the stop submitted after a bar whose high >= entry price + planned risk is reached at max(entry price − planned risk, entry price × (1 + break-even buffer)).
Exit: Short, break-even enabled AND the stop submitted after a bar whose low <= entry price − planned risk is reached at min(entry price + planned risk, entry price × (1 − break-even buffer)).
Exit: Long, enabled time stop AND bars since position entry >= time-stop threshold.
Exit: Short, enabled time stop AND bars since position entry >= time-stop threshold.
