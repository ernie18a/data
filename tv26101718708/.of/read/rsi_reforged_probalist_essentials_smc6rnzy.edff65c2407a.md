Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of a selectable price source, optionally EMA-smoothed.
Calculation: Adaptive bands = rolling mean of RSI plus or minus a multiple of its rolling standard deviation; cap the upper band and floor the lower band.
Calculation: Divergence pivot pairs = On confirmation of an RSI pivot high or low, select the most recent earlier same-type RSI pivot among the retained pivots whose distance is within the maximum divergence lookback and at least the sum of the left and right pivot widths plus one bar. For the current RSI pivot, use the latest confirmed same-type price pivot only if its index is within the right pivot width plus a matching allowance of the RSI pivot index. For the prior RSI pivot, use the most recent confirmed same-type price pivot within that same matching distance. Price pivot highs use high; price pivot lows use low. Retain a bounded number of the most recent pivots of each type. Evaluate divergence on the RSI pivot's confirmation bar.
Long: RSI was above the lower adaptive band and its preceding value was at or below the preceding lower band, a configurable confirmation delay ago.
Short: RSI was below the upper adaptive band and its preceding value was at or above the preceding upper band, a configurable confirmation delay ago.
Long: A divergence pivot low pair exists AND ((current price pivot low < prior price pivot low AND current RSI pivot low > prior RSI pivot low) OR (current price pivot low > prior price pivot low AND current RSI pivot low < prior RSI pivot low)).
Short: A divergence pivot high pair exists AND ((current price pivot high > prior price pivot high AND current RSI pivot high < prior RSI pivot high) OR (current price pivot high < prior price pivot high AND current RSI pivot high > prior RSI pivot high)).
