Calculation: Channel index = (price source − anchor) / standard deviation of price source over the channel lookback × scale; use zero when the standard deviation is zero. The anchor is either VWMA or EMA of the price source over the same lookback.
Calculation: Oscillator = recursive scalar Kalman smoothing of the channel index. Initialize the estimate to the first available channel index and error variance to one. On each subsequent bar, predicted variance = previous error variance + process noise; gain = predicted variance / (predicted variance + measurement noise); estimate = previous estimate + gain × (channel index − previous estimate); error variance = (1 − gain) × predicted variance. Optionally multiply process noise by the ratio of ATR to its longer-lookback SMA, clamped between lower and upper bounds. The signal line is an EMA of the oscillator.
Calculation: Oscillator rank = percentage of oscillator values in its rolling lookback that are less than or equal to the current oscillator value.
Calculation: Smoothed Hurst proxy = EMA of log base 2 of the ratio of the long-lookback standard deviation of the price source to its shorter, half-length lookback standard deviation. Use a ratio of one if the shorter standard deviation is zero, a proxy of one-half if the ratio is nonpositive, and one-half before the long lookback is available.
Calculation: Smoothed entropy = EMA of normalized Shannon entropy of consecutive price-source changes over a rolling lookback. Divide the range from the minimum to maximum change into equal-width bins, including the maximum in the last bin; entropy is −sum(p × ln(p)) / ln(number of bins), where p is each bin's fraction of observations. Substitute one-half before enough observations are available or when the range is zero.
Long: Oscillator crosses above its signal line AND oscillator rank < lower central rank threshold AND (entropy gate disabled OR smoothed entropy <= entropy ceiling) AND (Hurst gate disabled OR smoothed Hurst proxy >= one-half − Hurst band OR oscillator rank < lower extreme rank threshold OR oscillator rank > upper extreme rank threshold).
Short: Oscillator crosses below its signal line AND oscillator rank > upper central rank threshold AND (entropy gate disabled OR smoothed entropy <= entropy ceiling) AND (Hurst gate disabled OR smoothed Hurst proxy >= one-half − Hurst band OR oscillator rank < lower extreme rank threshold OR oscillator rank > upper extreme rank threshold).
