Calculation: Bar notation = [i] denotes i bars ago; unindexed values refer to the current bar. Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low. Average body is the simple moving average of body; trend average is the simple moving average of close.
Calculation: Bullish patterns = Engulfing: close[1] < open[1] AND close > open AND open <= close[1] AND close >= open[1]; Hammer: body <= average body * small-body multiplier AND lower wick >= body * wick multiplier AND upper wick <= body * 0.5 AND close[1] < trend average[1]; Inverted Hammer: body <= average body * small-body multiplier AND upper wick >= body * wick multiplier AND lower wick <= body * 0.5 AND close[1] < trend average[1]; Harami: max(open, close) <= max(open[1], close[1]) AND min(open, close) >= min(open[1], close[1]) AND body <= body[1] * 0.6 AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[2] < trend average[2]; Morning Star: close[2] < open[2] AND body[2] >= average body[2] * large-body multiplier AND body[1] <= body[2] * 0.5 AND close > open AND close > (open[2] + close[2]) / 2 AND close[3] < trend average[3]; Piercing Line: close[1] < open[1] AND body[1] >= average body[1] * large-body multiplier AND close > open AND open < close[1] AND close > (open[1] + close[1]) / 2 AND close < open[1] AND close[2] < trend average[2]; Three White Soldiers: close > open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[2] > open[2] AND close > close[1] AND close[1] > close[2] AND open > open[1] AND open < close[1] AND open[1] > open[2] AND open[1] < close[2] AND body >= range * 0.5 AND body[1] >= range[1] * 0.5 AND body[2] >= range[2] * 0.5.
Calculation: Bearish patterns = Engulfing: close[1] > open[1] AND close < open AND open >= close[1] AND close <= open[1]; Hanging Man: body <= average body * small-body multiplier AND lower wick >= body * wick multiplier AND upper wick <= body * 0.5 AND close[1] > trend average[1]; Shooting Star: body <= average body * small-body multiplier AND upper wick >= body * wick multiplier AND lower wick <= body * 0.5 AND close[1] > trend average[1]; Harami: max(open, close) <= max(open[1], close[1]) AND min(open, close) >= min(open[1], close[1]) AND body <= body[1] * 0.6 AND close[1] > open[1] AND close[2] > trend average[2]; Evening Star: close[2] > open[2] AND body[2] >= average body[2] * large-body multiplier AND body[1] <= body[2] * 0.5 AND close < open AND close < (open[2] + close[2]) / 2 AND close[3] > trend average[3]; Dark Cloud Cover: close[1] > open[1] AND body[1] >= average body[1] * large-body multiplier AND close < open AND open > close[1] AND close < (open[1] + close[1]) / 2 AND close > open[1] AND close[2] > trend average[2]; Three Black Crows: close < open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close[2] < open[2] AND close < close[1] AND close[1] < close[2] AND open < open[1] AND open > close[1] AND open[1] < open[2] AND open[1] > close[2] AND body >= range * 0.5 AND body[1] >= range[1] * 0.5 AND body[2] >= range[2] * 0.5.
Calculation: Pattern reliability = independently for each raw chart-timeframe pattern occurrence, grade it after a configurable number of bars: a bullish occurrence succeeds if its occurrence close > 0 AND the grading close > occurrence close; a bearish occurrence succeeds if its occurrence close > 0 AND the grading close < occurrence close; otherwise it fails. Reliability is the fraction of successes among that pattern's most recent configurable number of graded occurrences, including grades completed on the current bar. A pattern is reliable if its graded occurrence count < the minimum count OR its reliability is unavailable OR its reliability >= the minimum reliability threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement count = the number of enabled higher timeframes whose last completed bar has a raw pattern in the direction of the first reliable chart-timeframe pattern, using this priority: bullish Engulfing, Hammer, Inverted Hammer, Harami, Morning Star, Piercing Line, Three White Soldiers, then bearish Engulfing, Hanging Man, Shooting Star, Harami, Evening Star, Dark Cloud Cover, Three Black Crows. On each higher timeframe, select the first raw pattern in the same priority order before comparing direction; use that timeframe's own aggregated bars for all pattern calculations.
Long: At least one bullish pattern occurs AND that same pattern is reliable AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume >= simple moving average of volume * volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe gating is disabled OR no higher timeframe is enabled OR higher-timeframe agreement count >= minimum agreement count).
Short: At least one bearish pattern occurs AND that same pattern is reliable AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume >= simple moving average of volume * volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe gating is disabled OR no higher timeframe is enabled OR higher-timeframe agreement count >= minimum agreement count).
