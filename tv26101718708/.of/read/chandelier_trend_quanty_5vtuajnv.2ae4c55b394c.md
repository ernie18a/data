Calculation: Trend and trailing level = Use ATR multiplied by a configurable multiplier as the span. Choose either highs and lows or closes as the extreme sources. When ATR first becomes available, initialize the trend as up, the extreme as the current high source, and the level as extreme minus span. On each subsequent bar in an up trend, update the extreme to the maximum of its previous value and the current high source, then update the level to the maximum of its previous value and extreme minus span. If close is below this updated level, change the trend to down, reset the extreme to the current low source, and reset the level to extreme plus span. On each subsequent bar in a down trend, update the extreme to the minimum of its previous value and the current low source, then update the level to the minimum of its previous value and extreme plus span. If close is above this updated level, change the trend to up, reset the extreme to the current high source, and reset the level to extreme minus span. Evaluate on closed bars.
Long: Trend = up
Short: Trend = down
