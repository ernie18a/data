Calculation: CRSI = average of RSI(close), RSI(signed consecutive up/down streak), and percentile rank of one-bar close change; calculate on the signal timeframe and the higher timeframe, using each timeframe’s bars
Calculation: Keltner bands = EMA(close) ± multiplier × ATR; calculate on the signal timeframe, using the current signal-timeframe bar
Long: signal-timeframe bar boundary AND ( (higher-timeframe CRSI is within the middle trend range AND signal-timeframe low < lower Keltner band AND signal-timeframe CRSI ≤ oversold threshold AND signal-timeframe close > open AND bullish body ≥ a fraction of candle range) OR (higher-timeframe CRSI > upper trend threshold AND signal-timeframe CRSI ≤ oversold threshold AND signal-timeframe close ≤ lower Keltner band × pullback factor) OR (higher-timeframe CRSI > upper trend threshold AND signal-timeframe CRSI ≤ oversold threshold AND signal-timeframe high > prior upper Keltner band AND signal-timeframe close < current upper Keltner band) ) AND no prior trade OR minimum signal-bar spacing elapsed since prior trade
Short: signal-timeframe bar boundary AND ( (higher-timeframe CRSI is within the middle trend range AND signal-timeframe high > upper Keltner band AND signal-timeframe CRSI ≥ overbought threshold AND signal-timeframe close < open AND bearish body ≥ a fraction of candle range) OR (higher-timeframe CRSI < lower trend threshold AND signal-timeframe CRSI ≥ overbought threshold AND signal-timeframe close ≥ upper Keltner band × pullback factor) OR (higher-timeframe CRSI < lower trend threshold AND signal-timeframe CRSI ≥ overbought threshold AND signal-timeframe low < prior lower Keltner band AND signal-timeframe close > current lower Keltner band) ) AND no prior trade OR minimum signal-bar spacing elapsed since prior trade
Exit: Long, signal-timeframe bar boundary AND minimum holding period elapsed AND raw short signal OR low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + target distance OR, after favorable movement reaches the trailing activation distance, price retraces by the trailing offset
Exit: Short, signal-timeframe bar boundary AND minimum holding period elapsed AND raw long signal OR high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − target distance OR, after favorable movement reaches the trailing activation distance, price retraces by the trailing offset
