Calculation: Delta = sum the signed volumes of all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; use chart bars themselves when the selected timeframe equals the chart timeframe. Sign each volume positive if its bar closes above its open, negative if it closes below its open; for a doji, sign it according to whether its close is above or below the preceding bar's close, or zero if equal. Treat missing volume and missing preceding close as zero. If no intrabar data is available, use volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Absorption = absolute delta is at least its nearest-rank percentile over a lookback including the current bar AND (absolute candle body is at most a threshold times ATR OR delta and candle body do not have the same nonzero sign) AND (positive delta AND high is the highest high over a lookback including the current bar AND upper wick divided by candle range is at least a threshold OR negative delta AND low is the lowest low over that lookback AND lower wick divided by candle range is at least a threshold) AND more than a spacing parameter's number of bars have elapsed since the last absorption, or there has been no previous absorption. Use candle range in place of unavailable ATR; use zero for the wick ratio when the range is zero. Absorption sensitivity jointly sets the percentile, body threshold, extreme lookback and wick threshold; higher sensitivity uses a lower percentile, larger body threshold, shorter lookback and smaller wick threshold.
Calculation: Bubble = absolute delta is at least its nearest-rank percentile over a lookback including the current bar AND delta and candle body have the same nonzero sign AND there is no absorption on the current bar AND (absolute delta is at least a higher nearest-rank percentile over the same lookback AND absolute delta divided by volume is at least a dominance threshold OR more than a spacing parameter's number of bars have elapsed since the last bubble, or there has been no previous bubble). Use zero for the dominance ratio when volume is zero. The bubble's price is its close.
Long: Bubble AND delta > 0
Short: Bubble AND delta < 0
Long: Absorption AND delta < 0
Short: Absorption AND delta > 0
Long: A previous negative-delta bubble has not yet been trapped AND the current bar is after that bubble and within its trapping window AND close > that bubble's price + a buffer times ATR at the bubble bar
Short: A previous positive-delta bubble has not yet been trapped AND the current bar is after that bubble and within its trapping window AND close < that bubble's price − a buffer times ATR at the bubble bar
