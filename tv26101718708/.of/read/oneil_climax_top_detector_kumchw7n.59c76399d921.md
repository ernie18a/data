Calculation: percentage distance from SMA = (source − simple moving average of source / simple moving average of source) × 100
Calculation: lower extension band = fixed lower-band threshold, or the mean percentage distance plus a standard-deviation multiple over a lookback
Calculation: runup percentage = (source − source from the runup lookback bars ago) / source from the runup lookback bars ago × 100
Short: percentage distance from SMA ≥ lower extension band AND runup percentage ≥ minimum runup threshold AND volume ≥ highest volume over the volume-record lookback
