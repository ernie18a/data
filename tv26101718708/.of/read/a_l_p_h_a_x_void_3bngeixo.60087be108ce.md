Calculation: Bull gap = on a confirmed bar, low > high two bars ago AND absolute body of the preceding bar >= current SMA of absolute candle bodies times a displacement multiplier AND low minus high two bars ago >= current ATR times a gap multiplier; top = current low, bottom = high two bars ago.
Calculation: Bear gap = on a confirmed bar, high < low two bars ago AND absolute body of the preceding bar >= current SMA of absolute candle bodies times the same displacement multiplier AND low two bars ago minus high >= current ATR times the same gap multiplier; top = low two bars ago, bottom = current high.
Calculation: Gap inventory = append each new gap with its direction and first candle's bar index; immediately before each append, discard oldest gaps until the inventory size is at most the configured maximum. Before scanning each bar, permanently mark a bull gap filled when low <= its bottom and a bear gap filled when high >= its top, including on the formation bar.
Calculation: Bull retest = among unfilled bull gaps whose age from the first candle's bar index <= maximum age, select the most recent gap satisfying low <= top AND high >= bottom AND close > open AND close >= midpoint if midpoint rejection is enabled, otherwise close >= bottom.
Calculation: Bear retest = among unfilled bear gaps whose age from the first candle's bar index <= maximum age, select the most recent gap satisfying low <= top AND high >= bottom AND close < open AND close <= midpoint if midpoint rejection is enabled, otherwise close <= top.
Calculation: Momentum = current endpoint of linear regression of close minus the average of the recent high-low midpoint and SMA of close, all using the momentum lookback.
Calculation: Volume pressure = EMA of estimated bull volume minus EMA of estimated bear volume, using the same lookback; estimated bull volume = volume times (close - low) / (high - low), or half of volume when high = low; estimated bear volume = volume minus estimated bull volume. Smoothed volume pressure = EMA of volume pressure using its own lookback. OBV change = current OBV minus OBV at the volume slope lookback.
Calculation: Bull votes = count of true conditions: shortest EMA > intermediate EMA AND intermediate EMA > longest EMA AND close > shortest EMA; momentum > 0 AND momentum > preceding momentum; volume pressure > 0 AND smoothed volume pressure > 0 AND OBV change > 0; VWAP filter disabled OR close > session-anchored VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3; ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold AND +DI > -DI.
Calculation: Bear votes = count of true conditions: shortest EMA < intermediate EMA AND intermediate EMA < longest EMA AND close < shortest EMA; momentum < 0 AND momentum < preceding momentum; volume pressure < 0 AND smoothed volume pressure < 0 AND OBV change < 0; VWAP filter disabled OR close < session-anchored VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3; ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold AND -DI > +DI.
Calculation: Bull setup = bull retest exists AND bull votes >= minimum votes AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar time within configured session) AND bars since the last bull entry signal >= cooldown AND (trend alignment disabled OR shortest EMA > intermediate EMA). Bear setup = bear retest exists AND bear votes >= minimum votes AND the same ADX and session conditions AND bars since the last bear entry signal >= cooldown AND (trend alignment disabled OR shortest EMA < intermediate EMA). Evaluate each setup using the prior stored signal times before updating them.
Calculation: Position state = each bull entry signal sets the tracked position long and each bear entry signal sets it short, even if a position is already open; if both occur on the same bar, process bull first and bear second. Entry price is signal-bar close. A full stop or second target resets the position; evaluate exits after entries.
Calculation: Exit levels = at each entry, freeze ATR and set the stop base to the selected retested gap's far edge if gap anchoring is enabled, otherwise the entry bar's low for long or high for short. Long stop = base minus stop multiplier times entry ATR; short stop = base plus stop multiplier times entry ATR. First and second targets = entry price plus their respective multipliers times entry ATR for long, minus for short; the second target is farther from entry. Initialize long trail to base minus trail multiplier times entry ATR and short trail to base plus trail multiplier times entry ATR. On each subsequent bar with trailing enabled, update long trail to the maximum of its previous value and current low minus trail multiplier times current ATR; update short trail to the minimum of its previous value and current high plus trail multiplier times current ATR, before testing exits.

Long: Shortest EMA > intermediate EMA AND intermediate EMA > longest EMA AND close > shortest EMA.
Short: Shortest EMA < intermediate EMA AND intermediate EMA < longest EMA AND close < shortest EMA.
Long: Bollinger upper band >= Keltner upper band OR Bollinger lower band <= Keltner lower band, AND momentum > 0 AND momentum > preceding momentum.
Short: Bollinger upper band >= Keltner upper band OR Bollinger lower band <= Keltner lower band, AND momentum < 0 AND momentum < preceding momentum.
Long: Volume pressure > 0 AND smoothed volume pressure > 0 AND OBV change > 0.
Short: Volume pressure < 0 AND smoothed volume pressure < 0 AND OBV change < 0.
Long: VWAP filter disabled OR close > session-anchored VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3.
Short: VWAP filter enabled AND close < session-anchored VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3.
Long: ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold AND +DI > -DI.
Short: ADX filter enabled AND ADX >= threshold AND -DI > +DI.
Long: Bull setup is true AND preceding bar's bull setup is false.
Short: Bear setup is true AND preceding bar's bear setup is false.
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND high >= first target AND high < second target, for half the position.
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND low <= first target AND low > second target, for half the position.
Exit: long, bars since entry > 0 AND (low <= fixed stop OR high >= second target) OR trailing enabled AND bars since entry > 1 AND low <= updated trail.
Exit: short, bars since entry > 0 AND (high >= fixed stop OR low <= second target) OR trailing enabled AND bars since entry > 1 AND high >= updated trail.
