Calculation: Confirmed pivots = a high/low that remains the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars on both sides; use only after the right-side bars complete. Build alternating pivot sequences in chronological order.
Calculation: Pattern ratios = with consecutive pivots X,A,B,C,D, XA=|A−X|, AB=|B−A|, BC=|C−B|, CD=|D−C|, XC=|C−X|; XB=AB/XA, BC ratio=BC/AB, CD ratio=CD/BC, XD=|D−X|/XA, XC ratio=XC/XA, D-to-XC ratio=|C−D|/XC, and AB=CD ratio=CD/AB. Compare target ratios within configured tolerance and range ratios inclusively.
Calculation: PRZ = range from the minimum to maximum of three pattern-specific projected price levels, padded on both sides by the configured ATR padding. ATR uses the chart timeframe. D is near the PRZ when its distance from the zone is no greater than the configured ATR distance.
Calculation: Qualifying pattern = confirmed pivot structure, minimum XA size (or specified structural size), enabled pattern type, pattern-specific ratio/shape conditions, any required higher-timeframe alignment (compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA), required PRZ proximity, and score at least the configured minimum. Harmonic-pattern score = 62% ratio score + 22% PRZ tightness score + 8% pivot-time symmetry score + 8% context score. Ratio score averages target/range scores for the pattern's ratios; target score is 100 minus the ratio error as a percentage of its tolerance, floored at 0; range score is 0 outside the range and otherwise 100 minus 40 times distance from its midpoint as a fraction of its half-width. PRZ tightness score = clamp(100−45×zone-width/max(ATR, 2.5% of XA), 0, 100). Symmetry score = 100×shorter/longer duration of the compared legs. Context score starts at 50 and adds/subtracts: candle +14/−6 when close>open and above/below the bar midpoint for the pattern direction; RSI +12/−4 when rising and above 38 for bullish or falling and below 62 for bearish; volume +10/−3 when volume exceeds its moving average times the configured multiplier; optional higher-timeframe alignment +10/−8; optional close-versus-VWAP alignment +6/−4; ATR regime +5/−5 when ATR is at least 85% of its moving average. Clamp context score to 0–100. Structural patterns use their respective shape score and configured minimum score.
Calculation: Pattern structures = bullish XABCD pivots are low-high-low-high-low; bearish are high-low-high-low-high. Gartley: XB≈0.618, BC ratio 0.382–0.886, CD ratio 1.13–1.618, XD≈0.786, D between X and A. Bat: XB 0.382–0.500, BC 0.382–0.886, CD 1.618–2.618, XD≈0.886, D between X and A. Alternate Bat: XB 0.382–0.500, BC 0.382–0.886, CD 2.000–3.618, XD≈1.130, D beyond X. Butterfly: XB≈0.786, BC 0.382–0.886, CD 1.618–2.240, XD 1.270–1.618, D beyond X. Crab: XB 0.382–0.618, BC 0.382–0.886, CD 2.240–3.618, XD≈1.618, D beyond X. Deep Crab: XB≈0.886, BC 0.382–0.886, CD 2.240–3.618, XD≈1.618, D beyond X. Cypher: XB 0.382–0.618, XC 1.272–1.414, D-to-XC≈0.786, and D lies between X and C. 5-0: XB 1.130–1.618, BC 1.618–2.240, CD≈0.500. AB=CD: BC 0.382–0.886 and AB=CD ratio≈1.000, 1.272, or 1.618. Three Drives: six alternating pivots forming three same-direction legs; successive leg lengths each approximately equal within 25%, both intervening retracements 0.500–0.886, and first leg meets minimum size. Double/triple top or bottom: alternating pivots, respective tops or bottoms within configured relative tolerance, intervening move at least 0.5 ATR. Head-and-shoulders/inverse: seven alternating pivots, middle head beyond both shoulders in the reversal direction, shoulders within configured relative tolerance, neckline pivots within 0.75 ATR. Triangle: six alternating pivots with successively lower highs and higher lows, final range under 85% of initial range. Falling wedge: successively lower highs and lows with converging range. Rising wedge: successively higher highs and lows with converging range. Triangle direction is bullish when close exceeds the midpoint of its final high-low pair; wedge direction is bullish for a falling wedge and bearish for a rising wedge. Structural patterns must meet the configured minimum score and any required higher-timeframe alignment.
Calculation: Pattern entry, stop, and targets = entry is signal-bar close (or pivot D when that model is selected). Stop is beyond the PRZ by configured ATR padding, with a fallback risk distance if it would be on the wrong side of entry. Structure targets project the configured successive multiples of |C−D| from entry in the pattern direction; the third target uses C if beyond target two, otherwise the configured third multiple. Alternatively, configured risk-multiple targets use entry-to-stop risk. Ensure targets progress away from entry.
Long: A new qualifying bullish pattern is detected on a confirmed chart bar and its initial status is not failed, expired, or at/above target two.
Short: A new qualifying bearish pattern is detected on a confirmed chart bar and its initial status is not failed, expired, or at/above target two.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the stop (or close is at or below it when close-trigger mode is selected), or high reaches a projected target; stop takes precedence, then target three, target two, target one. Exit: Short, high is at or above the stop (or close is at or above it in close-trigger mode), or low reaches a projected target; stop takes precedence, then target three, target two, target one. An active pattern also expires after its configured age if it has not reached reaction status.
