Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs or lows with the configured number of bars on each side; process confirmed highs before lows. Replace the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type; append an opposite-type pivot only when its distance from the latest pivot is at least a configured multiple of current ATR. Reject opposite-type pivots at the same bar and retain a bounded pivot history.
Calculation: Candidate patterns = evaluate on a confirmed bar when the pivot history changes and contains at least five pivots. Let X,A,B,C,D be its latest five pivots, oldest first; require X to be within the configured maximum age. Bullish harmonic candidates have alternating low,high,low,high,low pivots; bearish candidates have the opposite sequence. Require |A-X| >= a configured ATR multiple. Define XB=|B-A|/|A-X|, BC=|C-B|/|B-A|, CD=|D-C|/|C-B|, XD=|D-X|/|A-X|, XC=|C-X|/|A-X|, DXC=|D-C|/|C-X|, and ABCD=|D-C|/|B-A|, with denominators floored by a small positive parameter. For each enabled harmonic family, apply its configured inclusive ratio ranges and relative-tolerance target bands: Gartley and Bat use XB, BC, CD and XD with D between X and A; Alternate Bat, Butterfly, Crab and Deep Crab use those ratios with D beyond X away from A; Cypher uses XB, XC and DXC with D between X and C; 5-0 uses XB, BC and CD; each AB=CD variant uses BC and an ABCD target band with its own tighter tolerance.
Calculation: Other candidates = use the same pivot-change and age requirements. Three Drives uses the sixth-latest pivot P and A,B,C,D: P and D are lows and A,C highs for bullish, or the reverse for bearish; compare |C-B|/|A-P| and |D-C|/|C-B| to configured target bands, |B-A|/|A-P| and |D-C|/|C-B| to configured ranges, and |A-P| to a configured ATR multiple. Double top or bottom requires the last three alternating pivots to end at a high or low respectively, the relative difference between the first and last extreme to be within a configured tolerance, and the intervening swing to exceed a configured ATR multiple. Triple top or bottom applies the same depth filter to the first swing of the last five alternating pivots and compares the range of their three highs or lows, divided by the absolute highest of those extremes, with the tolerance. Head and shoulders or its inverse uses the first six of the latest seven alternating pivots: the middle of the three highs or lows must be more extreme than both shoulders, shoulder relative difference must be within tolerance, and the difference between the two intervening pivots divided by ATR must be within a configured limit. For six alternating pivots, define successive highs H1,H2,H3 and lows L1,L2,L3; require both H1-L1 and H3-L3 positive and the latter less than a configured fraction of the former. Lower highs and higher lows form a triangle; lower highs and lower lows form a bullish falling wedge; higher highs and higher lows form a bearish rising wedge. A triangle is bullish when close > (H3+L3)/2 and bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Pattern zone = for each harmonic family, form three family-specific projections from X,A,B,C using configurable leg multipliers: an XA retracement or extension, an XC retracement, a BC projection, or an AB projection as applicable. Extend the lowest and highest projections by configured ATR padding. AB=CD variants use their respective AB projection and BC projection; the extended variants use an additional XA projection based on XD. Three Drives, double, triple, head-and-shoulders and triangle/wedge zones are centered on D and padded by their respective configured ATR multiples. Zone tightness is a clamped decreasing function of zone width divided by max(ATR, a configured fraction of the relevant pattern size).
Calculation: Pattern score = a clamped weighted combination of ratio fit, zone tightness, swing-duration symmetry and context; use separate configurable weights for standard harmonic, AB=CD and Three Drives patterns. Target-ratio fit decreases linearly with normalized distance from its target; range fit is zero outside the range and otherwise decreases from the midpoint toward its boundaries. Swing-duration symmetry is the shorter divided by the longer of the two specified leg durations. Double, triple, head-and-shoulders and triangle/wedge scores are separate clamped affine functions of their respective relative-extreme difference, neckline difference or range contraction, plus context. Context is a clamped sum of optional contributions for a directional reversal candle, RSI turning in the pattern direction past its configured threshold, volume exceeding a multiple of its moving average, close relative to VWAP, ATR relative to its moving average, and close relative to EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Each contribution has configured pass and fail weights.
Calculation: Eligible candidate = an enabled candidate whose score meets its configured minimum and, when higher-timeframe alignment is required, whose last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA for bullish or below it for bearish. Standard harmonic candidates also require minimum zone tightness; standard harmonic and AB=CD candidates optionally require D to be within a configured ATR distance of the zone. Evaluate double, triple, head-and-shoulders and triangle/wedge candidates only if the number of earlier matching candidates is below the configured limit plus its allowance. Rank all matches by descending score with original evaluation order breaking ties, keep the configured maximum per changed pivot, and suppress previously seen combinations of D bar, pattern type and side using a bounded history.
Calculation: Pattern risk levels = freeze the zone and ATR on detection. The reference entry is either detection-bar close or D. Set the bullish stop below the zone and the bearish stop above it by configured ATR padding; if it is on the wrong side of the reference entry, use a configured minimum ATR distance from entry. Targets are successive increasing multiples of either entry-to-stop risk or |C-D| in the favorable direction; for the structural model use C as the third target when it lies beyond the second, otherwise use the configured third multiple. Enforce a small positive minimum separation between entry and consecutive targets.
Calculation: Pattern lifecycle = retain a bounded number of detected patterns. For a D-reference entry, optionally replay bars after D through the detection bar using the frozen stop and targets, with stop taking priority over the furthest hit target. For subsequent confirmed bars, stop takes priority, followed by expiration before reaction, then the furthest target, reaction and zone touch. A long stop is hit when low or close, according to the configured trigger, is <= stop; a short stop uses high or close >= stop. A long target is hit when high >= target; a short target when low <= target. A zone touch is high >= zone low AND low <= zone high. A bullish reaction is a zone touch AND close > open AND close > (high+low)/2 AND, if required, (min(open,close)-low)/(high-low) >= the configured wick ratio; alternatively, a previous zone touch AND close > reference entry AND close > previous high. Reverse these comparisons and use the upper wick for bearish reaction. A signal-close pattern cannot evaluate stop, target or reaction on its detection bar. Expire a pattern after the configured age only if it has not reached reaction; stop, expiration and the third target terminate it.
Long: an eligible bullish candidate is selected and its D-bar, pattern-type and side combination has not already been seen.
Short: an eligible bearish candidate is selected and its D-bar, pattern-type and side combination has not already been seen.
Long: an active bullish pattern advances to reaction.
Short: an active bearish pattern advances to reaction.
Exit: long, the associated active bullish pattern hits its stop OR reaches a configured target.
Exit: short, the associated active bearish pattern hits its stop OR reaches a configured target.
