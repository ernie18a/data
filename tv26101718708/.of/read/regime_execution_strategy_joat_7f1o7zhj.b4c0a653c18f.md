Calculation: Adaptive forecast mean = Recursive linear forecast of close, rebased every configured interval: initialize intercept to close and slope to 0; each bar forecast intercept + slope × max(0, bars since rebase / regression horizon), then update intercept and slope by recursive least squares using the forecast error and forgetting factor.
Calculation: Forecast bands = Adaptive forecast mean ± max(minimum price increment, band multiplier × square root of the exponentially weighted mean squared forecast error + ATR blend × 14-bar ATR).
Calculation: Adaptive channel = Upper and lower cores start at the first bar’s high and low. A new or equal high/low resets that core to the bar’s extreme and its age to zero; otherwise increment its age, and when it reaches the reset length, move the core toward the current high/low by the reset alpha and reset the age. Channel midpoint = (upper core + lower core) / 2; channel range = max(minimum price increment, upper core − lower core).
Calculation: Pressure bias = tanh((tanh(ROC(close, pressure lookback) / max(standard deviation of that ROC over the same lookback, minimum price increment)) + 2 × (close − lowest low over the pressure lookback) / max(highest high − lowest low over that lookback, minimum price increment) − 1) / 2).
Calculation: Forecast bias = tanh((close − adaptive forecast mean) / forecast band spread).
Calculation: Channel bias = 1 if channel state is bullish, −1 if bearish; otherwise tanh((close − channel midpoint) / channel range). Channel state becomes bullish on a new low when the confirmed bar closes above the midpoint and above its open; it becomes bearish on a new high when the confirmed bar closes below the midpoint and below its open. A new high otherwise sets it bullish; a new low otherwise sets it bearish.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / max(20-bar simple moving average of volume, 1).
Calculation: Regime score = 0.40 × forecast bias + 0.30 × channel bias + 0.20 × pressure bias + 0.10 × EMA structure, where EMA structure is 1 when the shorter-lookback close EMA is at least the longer-lookback close EMA, otherwise −1.
Calculation: Bull votes = count of (forecast bias > 0), (channel bias > 0), and (pressure bias > pressure threshold). Bear votes = count of (forecast bias < 0), (channel bias < 0), and (pressure bias < −pressure threshold).
Calculation: Bull regime = regime score > 0.12 AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume. Bear regime = regime score < −0.12 AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume.
Long: Confirmed bar AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND bull votes ≥ vote threshold AND bull regime AND ((close crosses above adaptive forecast mean AND close > channel midpoint AND pressure bias > pressure threshold) OR (close crosses above lower forecast band AND channel bias ≥ 0 AND pressure bias > 0)).
Short: Confirmed bar AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND bear votes ≥ vote threshold AND bear regime AND ((close crosses below adaptive forecast mean AND close < channel midpoint AND pressure bias < −pressure threshold) OR (close crosses below upper forecast band AND channel bias ≤ 0 AND pressure bias < 0)).
Calculation: Long stop = min(max(entry price − ATR × stop multiplier, lower channel core − minimum price increment), entry price − minimum price increment). Long target = entry price + ATR × target multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop = max(min(entry price + ATR × stop multiplier, upper channel core + minimum price increment), entry price + minimum price increment). Short target = entry price − ATR × target multiplier.
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR (confirmed bar AND bear votes ≥ vote threshold AND regime score < −0.10).
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR (confirmed bar AND bull votes ≥ vote threshold AND regime score > 0.10).
