Calculation: Forecast mean and bands = Use a configurable price source from the bar fields. Initialize intercept to the source, slope and error variance to zero, covariance diagonal to a large positive parameter, covariance off-diagonal to zero, and origin to the first bar. At each configurable rebase interval, reset these values and origin to the current bar before calculating the forecast. On each bar set x = bars since origin / regression horizon, mean = intercept + slope × x, and error = source − mean. Update error variance = forgetting factor × previous error variance + (1 − forgetting factor) × error². With covariance entries p00, p01, p11, set q0 = p00 + p01 × x, q1 = p01 + p11 × x, and d = forgetting factor + q0 + x × q1. If d ≠ 0, set k0 = q0 / d and k1 = q1 / d; update intercept += k0 × error, slope += k1 × error, p00 = (old p00 − k0 × q0) / forgetting factor, p01 = (old p01 − k0 × q1) / forgetting factor, and p11 = (old p11 − k1 × q1) / forgetting factor. Spread = max(minimum price increment, band multiplier × sqrt(max(error variance, 0)) + ATR multiplier × ATR); upper and lower bands = mean ± spread. The mean used on the current bar is calculated before the coefficient update.
Calculation: Channel = Initialize upper core to high, lower core to low, both ages to zero, and state to zero. On each bar, if high ≥ upper core, set upper core = high, upper age = 0, and state = +1; otherwise increment upper age and, when it reaches the reset length, replace upper core with (1 − reset fraction) × upper core + reset fraction × high and reset its age. Then, if low ≤ lower core, set lower core = low, lower age = 0, and state = −1; otherwise increment lower age and apply the equivalent reset using low. Midpoint = (upper core + lower core) / 2; range = max(minimum price increment, upper core − lower core). After updating both cores, set state = +1 if the bar made a new lower extreme and close > midpoint and close > open; then set state = −1 if the bar made a new upper extreme and close < midpoint and close < open.
Calculation: Pressure = tanh((normalized momentum + pullback bias) / 2), where momentum is close rate of change over the pressure lookback, normalized momentum = tanh(momentum / max(standard deviation of momentum over the same lookback, minimum price increment)), and pullback bias = 2 × (close − lowest low over the pressure lookback) / max(minimum price increment, highest high − lowest low over that lookback) − 1. Use 1 for unavailable momentum standard deviation.
Calculation: Forecast bias = tanh((close − forecast mean) / forecast spread).
Calculation: Channel bias = +1 if channel state = +1, −1 if channel state = −1, otherwise tanh((close − channel midpoint) / channel range).
Calculation: Structure bias = +1 if the shorter-lookback close EMA ≥ the longer-lookback close EMA; otherwise −1.
Calculation: Regime score = weighted sum of forecast bias, channel bias, pressure, and structure bias, with positive weights in decreasing order.
Calculation: Bull votes = count of forecast bias > 0, channel bias > 0, and pressure > positive pressure threshold. Bear votes = count of forecast bias < 0, channel bias < 0, and pressure < negative pressure threshold of equal magnitude.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / max(volume SMA, 1); use current volume when the SMA is unavailable.
Long: On a completed bar, relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND bull votes ≥ minimum votes AND regime score > positive entry score threshold AND ((close crosses above forecast mean AND close > channel midpoint AND pressure > positive pressure threshold) OR (close crosses above lower forecast band AND channel bias ≥ 0 AND pressure > 0)).
Short: On a completed bar, relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND bear votes ≥ minimum votes AND regime score < negative entry score threshold of equal magnitude AND ((close crosses below forecast mean AND close < channel midpoint AND pressure < negative pressure threshold) OR (close crosses below upper forecast band AND channel bias ≤ 0 AND pressure < 0)).
Long: On a completed bar, relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND regime score > positive entry score threshold.
Short: On a completed bar, relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND regime score < negative entry score threshold of equal magnitude.
Long: Regime score ≥ 0.
Short: Regime score < 0.
Long: Pressure ≥ 0.
Short: Pressure < 0.
Long: Bull votes ≥ bear votes.
Short: Bull votes < bear votes.
Exit: long, low ≤ min(max(entry price − stop multiplier × ATR, lower channel core − minimum price increment), entry price − minimum price increment) OR high ≥ entry price + target multiplier × ATR; use stop and target calculated on the preceding completed bar while the long position was open.
Exit: short, high ≥ max(min(entry price + stop multiplier × ATR, upper channel core + minimum price increment), entry price + minimum price increment) OR low ≤ entry price − target multiplier × ATR; use stop and target calculated on the preceding completed bar while the short position was open.
Exit: long, on a completed bar bear votes ≥ minimum votes AND regime score < negative exit score threshold.
Exit: short, on a completed bar bull votes ≥ minimum votes AND regime score > positive exit score threshold of equal magnitude.
