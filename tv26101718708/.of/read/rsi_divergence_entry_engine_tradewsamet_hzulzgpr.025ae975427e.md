Calculation: RSI pivot pair = the newly confirmed RSI pivot and the immediately preceding confirmed RSI pivot of the same type. RSI uses close; each pivot requires a left lookback and a right confirmation lookback. Compare the RSI and price low or high at the pivot bars on the confirmation bar. Pivot separation must be within an inclusive minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR on the entry bar multiplied by a stop-distance parameter; retain this value for the trade. Entry price is the confirmation-bar close.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot-low pair has separation within the allowed range AND the newer pivot RSI > the preceding pivot RSI AND the newer pivot-bar low < the preceding pivot-bar low.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot-low pair has separation within the allowed range AND the newer pivot RSI < the preceding pivot RSI AND the newer pivot-bar low > the preceding pivot-bar low.
Short: A confirmed RSI pivot-high pair has separation within the allowed range AND the newer pivot RSI < the preceding pivot RSI AND the newer pivot-bar high > the preceding pivot-bar high.
Short: A confirmed RSI pivot-high pair has separation within the allowed range AND the newer pivot RSI > the preceding pivot RSI AND the newer pivot-bar high < the preceding pivot-bar high.
Long: The regular bullish divergence condition is true AND the regular bearish divergence condition is false AND no trade is active AND no trade closed on the current bar AND ATR > 0 AND (trend filter is off OR the selected chart EMA filter has close > EMA OR the selected Supertrend filter is bullish OR the selected higher-timeframe EMA filter has higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA). The higher-timeframe comparison uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime; the chart and higher-timeframe EMAs use the same length.
Short: The regular bearish divergence condition is true AND the regular bullish divergence condition is false AND no trade is active AND no trade closed on the current bar AND ATR > 0 AND (trend filter is off OR the selected chart EMA filter has close < EMA OR the selected Supertrend filter is bearish OR the selected higher-timeframe EMA filter has higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA). The higher-timeframe comparison uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in realtime; the chart and higher-timeframe EMAs use the same length.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= entry price - risk distance OR high >= entry price + risk distance × target reward-to-risk parameter); if both are touched, the stop takes priority.
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= entry price + risk distance OR low <= entry price - risk distance × target reward-to-risk parameter); if both are touched, the stop takes priority.
