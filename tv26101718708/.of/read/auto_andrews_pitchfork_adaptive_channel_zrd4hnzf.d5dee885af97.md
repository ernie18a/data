Calculation: Pitchfork = Maintain confirmed alternating pivot highs and lows. Replace the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type; accept an opposite pivot only when its distance from the latest pivot, divided by current ATR, meets a prominence threshold. Build from the latest three accepted pivots. For the standard variant, use the first pivot as origin; for Schiff, use its bar and the average price of the first two pivots; for modified Schiff, use the average bar and average price of the first two pivots. The median passes through the origin and the midpoint of the second and third pivots. The upper and lower tines are parallel to the median through the second and third pivots. Select the configured variant or the variant with the smallest mean absolute close-to-median distance from the first pivot through the current bar, subject to a lookback cap. Retain the current fork until a changed pivot permits a new fork.
Calculation: Trending = ADX exceeds its threshold AND efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold; efficiency ratio is the absolute price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar price changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Riding direction = For each bar, normalize price minus the pitchfork median by half the distance between the tines. A break occurs when this normalized position remains above one or below minus one for at least a configured consecutive-bar count. On a break, score one point each for break distance beyond the corresponding tine divided by ATR meeting its threshold; volume meeting a multiple of its moving average; true range exceeding a multiple of ATR while ATR is rising; close position within the bar toward the break meeting its threshold; and a longer-lookback efficiency ratio being at or below its compression threshold a configured number of bars ago while a shorter-lookback efficiency ratio exceeds its value a configured number of bars ago. A riding direction exists when a break occurs AND Trending is true AND the score meets its threshold; its direction is the break direction.
Calculation: Pitchfork re-anchoring = If enabled, when a break first becomes non-riding and a minimum interval since the previous break-triggered reset has elapsed, clear the pivot buffer and seed it with the current high for an upward break or current low for a downward break. Until a new fork forms, accept only pivots at or after that reset using a shorter pivot lookback and a reduced prominence threshold; retain the previous fork. Resume the standard pivot rules once a new fork forms.
Long: Pitchfork median slope >= 0 OR (Trending AND close > EMA of close) OR riding direction is upward OR (low >= pitchfork lower tine AND price > pitchfork lower tine) OR (low <= pitchfork lower tine AND price > pitchfork lower tine)
Short: Pitchfork median slope < 0 OR (Trending AND close <= EMA of close) OR riding direction is downward OR (high >= pitchfork upper tine AND price < pitchfork upper tine)
