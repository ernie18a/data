Calculation: Longer-horizon moving average = simple moving average of closes on completed bars of a higher timeframe; shorter-horizon moving average = simple moving average of closes on completed bars of a different, shorter higher timeframe.
Calculation: Weekly swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over a lookback of prior completed bars on the shorter higher timeframe.
Calculation: Weekly Fibonacci retracement band = the band between two configured retracement levels of the directional impulse from the prior swing low to swing high when the shorter higher-timeframe close is above its moving average, or from swing high to swing low when it is below its moving average; midpoint = the configured halfway retracement of that impulse.
Calculation: Weekly Fibonacci extension target = the directional impulse endpoint extended beyond that endpoint by a configured extension ratio.
Calculation: Bullish order block = on an upward close breakout above the highest high of prior completed bars on a higher timeframe, the high-low range of the nearest preceding down-close bar on that higher timeframe; retain it until a higher-timeframe close falls below its midpoint.
Calculation: Bearish order block = on a downward close breakout below the lowest low of prior completed bars on a higher timeframe, the high-low range of the nearest preceding up-close bar on that higher timeframe; retain it until a higher-timeframe close rises above its midpoint.
Calculation: Squeeze release = Bollinger bands extend outside the Keltner channels.
Calculation: Momentum = linear regression of close minus its simple moving average, using a lookback on chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Chart timeframe is the execution timeframe AND longer-horizon close > its moving average AND shorter-horizon close > its moving average AND close is within the weekly Fibonacci retracement band AND close is below the weekly Fibonacci midpoint AND close is within the bullish order block AND squeeze release AND momentum > 0 AND momentum > prior-bar momentum AND (killzone filter disabled OR time is in the configured London or New York killzone).
Short: Chart timeframe is the execution timeframe AND longer-horizon close < its moving average AND shorter-horizon close < its moving average AND close is within the weekly Fibonacci retracement band AND close is above the weekly Fibonacci midpoint AND close is within the bearish order block AND squeeze release AND momentum < 0 AND momentum < prior-bar momentum AND (killzone filter disabled OR time is in the configured London or New York killzone).
Exit: Long, chart timeframe is the execution timeframe AND (momentum < prior-bar momentum AND prior-bar momentum > 0 OR close >= weekly Fibonacci extension target OR bearish order block exists AND close >= its low).
Exit: Short, chart timeframe is the execution timeframe AND (momentum > prior-bar momentum AND prior-bar momentum < 0 OR close <= weekly Fibonacci extension target OR bullish order block exists AND close <= its high).
