Calculation: Per-market trend signal = +1 when the shorter-lookback moving average of close exceeds the longer-lookback moving average by more than a margin times ATR; -1 when it falls below by more than that amount; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Per-market volatility signal = +1 when close is at or above an adaptive trailing stop, otherwise -1; the stop trails the running high in an uptrend and the running low in a downtrend by a volatility multiple, resetting its reference when direction changes.
Calculation: Per-market signal = the selected trend signal, volatility signal, or their sum; trend and volatility modes require their respective calculations to be ready, and combined mode requires both.
Calculation: Regime scores = count each valid bullish or bearish market signal toward the four regimes according to its growth/inflation classification; bullish contributes to its assigned regime(s), bearish to the opposite regime(s). Divide each regime's count by the total contributions and optionally smooth each percentage with a simple moving average.
Calculation: Dominant regime = the regime with the highest smoothed score; on ties retain the previous regime if it remains tied for highest, otherwise choose Goldilocks, then Reflation, then Inflation, then Deflation.
Calculation: Target allocation = the assigned asset for the dominant regime when its price and prior-bar price are available; if no custom regime assets are assigned, use the chart asset for Goldilocks and Reflation and cash for Inflation and Deflation; if any custom asset is assigned, unassigned regimes are cash.
Long: The target allocation changes from cash to an assigned asset OR changes from one assigned asset to another.
Exit: long, the target allocation changes to cash OR changes to a different assigned asset.
