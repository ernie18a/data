Calculation: Supply zone = On a closed bar, after a run of consecutive bearish candles, find the most recent bullish candle within the base lookback. Set the zone bottom to that candle's low and its top to that low plus the current ATR multiplied by the zone-height parameter. Its volume is the absolute value of the sum of signed volumes from the current bar back to, but excluding, that candle: bearish bars contribute negative volume and all other bars contribute positive volume.
Calculation: Demand zone = On a closed bar, after a run of consecutive bullish candles, find the most recent bearish candle within the base lookback. Set the zone top to that candle's high and its bottom to that high minus the current ATR multiplied by the zone-height parameter. Its volume is the absolute value of the sum of signed volumes from the current bar back to, but excluding, that candle: bullish bars contribute positive volume and all other bars contribute negative volume.
Calculation: Active zones = For each side, create a candidate zone only when the previous bar's volume exceeds its volume SMA, the side's cooldown has expired, and the candidate does not overlap an existing active zone of the same side. Start that side's cooldown when a zone is created; decrement it once on each subsequent closed bar. After creation, remove supply zones whose top is below the close and demand zones whose bottom is above the close. Then retain only the most recently created zones up to the per-side limit. Total supply or demand volume is the sum of the volumes of the corresponding active zones.
Long: A demand zone is created.
Long: Total active demand volume > total active supply volume multiplied by the bias threshold.
Short: A supply zone is created.
Short: Total active supply volume > total active demand volume multiplied by the bias threshold.
