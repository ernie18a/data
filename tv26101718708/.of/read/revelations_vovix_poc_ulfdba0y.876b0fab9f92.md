Calculation: Volatility spike = (short-period ATR - long-period ATR) / (population standard deviation of short-period ATR over its normalization window + 0.000001).
Calculation: Regime count = number of bars in the regime window for which the absolute volatility spike on the preceding bar exceeds the base spike threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe spike = volatility spike calculated on higher-timeframe bars, mapped to chart bars with no lookahead and sampled on the previous chart bar; historical bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while a real-time bar can use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Confirmed decay = if the decay confirmation period is at most one bar, the regime count two bars ago is at least the regime threshold AND the regime count one bar ago is at most the regime threshold minus the decay buffer; otherwise, the regime count one bar ago is below the regime threshold minus the decay buffer AND at least the decay confirmation period has elapsed since the regime count one bar ago was above that buffered threshold.
Long: Position is flat AND the previous bar's volatility spike is greater than the base spike threshold AND its absolute value equals the highest absolute volatility spike in the local window ending on that bar AND its absolute value is greater than the super-spike multiplier times the SMA of absolute volatility spike over a window four times the local window, evaluated on that bar AND the previous bar's regime count is at least the regime threshold AND the previous close equals the lowest close in the local window ending on that bar AND the higher-timeframe spike is greater than the base spike threshold.
Short: Position is flat AND the previous bar's volatility spike is less than the negative base spike threshold AND its absolute value equals the highest absolute volatility spike in the local window ending on that bar AND its absolute value is greater than the super-spike multiplier times the SMA of absolute volatility spike over a window four times the local window, evaluated on that bar AND the previous bar's regime count is at least the regime threshold AND the previous close equals the highest close in the local window ending on that bar AND the higher-timeframe spike is less than the negative base spike threshold.
Exit: long, high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage / 100) OR low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage / 100) OR confirmed decay.
Exit: short, low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage / 100) OR high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100) OR confirmed decay.
