Calculation: Raw bullish reversal = Supertrend direction changes from down to up, or, when a data-derived external source is selected, that source was above the previous close and is below the current close.
Calculation: Late bullish entry = no raw bullish reversal on the current bar AND bars since the latest raw bullish reversal <= entry lookback AND current chart high and all checked lower-timeframe highs since that reversal <= reversal close + 2 strike steps AND chart Supertrend direction is up, or selected external source is below chart close, AND an available lower-timeframe close <= reversal close × (1 + entry tolerance). Check lower-timeframe bars in order, including a bar still forming; invalidate before checking entry if the chart high exceeds the limit.
Calculation: Stop = initially entry price × (1 - initial stop percentage); on a different day of the month from entry, raise it to at least entry price × (1 - half the initial stop percentage). Once an available lower-timeframe high or close, according to the selected trigger, exceeds the nearest strike to entry price + 4 strike steps, set it to that strike minus 2 strike steps plus 0.3 strike steps. Thereafter, let steps = min(floor((current chart high - that strike) / strike step), maximum trail levels); when steps >= 1, raise the stop to at least current chart high minus 2 strike steps if steps = 1, 1.5 strike steps if steps = 2, or 1 strike step if steps > 2. Lower-timeframe trigger bars may still be forming. Evaluate the exit before updating the stop for an adjustment or trailing on the current chart bar.
Long: Supertrend direction is up when the external source is disabled.
Short: Supertrend direction is down when the external source is disabled.
Long: (Raw bullish reversal OR late bullish entry) AND position is flat AND current time is before midnight on the day preceding the next configured expiry AND no entry has occurred for that expiry.
Exit: long, chart low < stop OR chart bar time or an available lower-timeframe bar time >= the configured square-off time on the active expiry date; the lower-timeframe bar may still be forming.
