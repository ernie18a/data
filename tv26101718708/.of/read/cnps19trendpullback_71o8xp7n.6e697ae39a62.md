Calculation: Long setup = on a flat-position bar in an entry window, arm or replace the setup if close < open, short-period EMA > long-period EMA, and the optional upward EMA-slope filter passes; store that bar's index and low. Before evaluating each later bar, disarm if its age exceeds the setup lifetime, short-period EMA <= long-period EMA, close < stored low - invalidation buffer, or the session-end bar is reached. Disarm after an entry. Evaluate entries before arming a new setup.
Calculation: Short setup = on a flat-position bar in an entry window, arm or replace the setup if close > open, short-period EMA < long-period EMA, and the optional downward EMA-slope filter passes; store that bar's index and high. Before evaluating each later bar, disarm if its age exceeds the setup lifetime, short-period EMA >= long-period EMA, close > stored high + invalidation buffer, or the session-end bar is reached. Disarm after an entry. Evaluate entries before arming a new setup.
Calculation: Confirmation body percentage = 100 × abs(close - open) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Entry window = bar opening time in the configured timezone is within either configured entry interval, inclusive.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price - stop distance; if optional break-even is enabled and a high since entry >= entry price + break-even trigger distance, change the stop to the greater of its previous value and entry price + break-even offset.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price + stop distance; if optional break-even is enabled and a low since entry <= entry price - break-even trigger distance, change the stop to the lesser of its previous value and entry price - break-even offset.
Long: Long setup is armed AND its age > 0 AND its age <= setup lifetime AND short-period EMA > long-period EMA AND (slope filter disabled OR short-period EMA > its value at the slope lookback) AND close > open AND confirmation body percentage >= minimum AND (previous-bar break filter disabled OR close > previous high) AND (EMA reclaim filter disabled OR close > short-period EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND position is flat AND bar is confirmed AND opening time is in entry window AND long trading is enabled AND entries in the current trading day < daily maximum AND (no previous entry OR bars since previous entry > cooldown).
Short: Short setup is armed AND its age > 0 AND its age <= setup lifetime AND short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND (slope filter disabled OR short-period EMA < its value at the slope lookback) AND close < open AND confirmation body percentage >= minimum AND (previous-bar break filter disabled OR close < previous low) AND (EMA reclaim filter disabled OR close < short-period EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND position is flat AND bar is confirmed AND opening time is in entry window AND short trading is enabled AND entries in the current trading day < daily maximum AND (no previous entry OR bars since previous entry > cooldown).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + profit-target distance OR (bar opening time < session end AND bar closing time >= session end).
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price - profit-target distance OR (bar opening time < session end AND bar closing time >= session end).
