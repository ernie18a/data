Calculation: Range high and range low = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured range-building window in America/New_York; lock both at the first bar of the following trading window and retain them until the next lock.
Calculation: Range midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2.
Calculation: Valid range = range high and range low exist AND range high - range low is between the configured minimum and maximum, inclusive.
Calculation: Trading window = chart-bar opening time in America/New_York is at or after the range-building end and at or before the configured session end. Reset the session entry count at the first bar of each trading window.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long stop = minimum of range low minus stop buffer and signal-bar close minus ATR multiplied by the ATR stop multiplier; freeze at the entry signal.
Calculation: Short stop = maximum of range high plus stop buffer and signal-bar close plus ATR multiplied by the ATR stop multiplier; freeze at the entry signal.
Calculation: Long target = range midpoint if the long fade condition is true on the entry signal bar; otherwise signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - long stop) multiplied by the reward-to-risk multiplier; freeze at the entry signal.
Calculation: Short target = range midpoint if the short fade condition is true on the entry signal bar; otherwise signal-bar close - (short stop - signal-bar close) multiplied by the reward-to-risk multiplier; freeze at the entry signal.
Long: Trading window AND valid range AND low <= range low + fade entry buffer AND close > range low AND previous close <= range low AND RSI < oversold threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by fade volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA).
Long: Trading window AND session entry count < maximum AND position is flat AND valid range AND close > range high + breakout buffer AND previous close <= range high + breakout buffer AND RSI > long breakout threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by breakout volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA).
Short: Trading window AND valid range AND high >= range high - fade entry buffer AND close < range high AND previous close >= range high AND RSI > overbought threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by fade volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA).
Short: Trading window AND session entry count < maximum AND position is flat AND valid range AND close < range low - breakout buffer AND previous close >= range low - breakout buffer AND RSI < short breakout threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA multiplied by breakout volume multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR current bar is outside both the range-building and trading windows AND previous bar was inside either window.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR current bar is outside both the range-building and trading windows AND previous bar was inside either window.
