Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the configured range-building window
Calculation: Opening range low = lowest low during the configured range-building window
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2
Calculation: Valid range = opening range high − opening range low is within the configured minimum and maximum
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = exponential moving average of close on a higher timeframe, using the value from the higher-timeframe bar available at the chart bar's close (last completed bar historically; may be still forming in real time)
Calculation: Long stop = min(opening range low − configured stop buffer, entry-bar close − entry-bar ATR × configured ATR multiplier)
Calculation: Short stop = max(opening range high + configured stop buffer, entry-bar close + entry-bar ATR × configured ATR multiplier)
Calculation: Long target = opening range midpoint for a fade; otherwise entry-bar close + (entry-bar close − long stop) × configured reward-to-risk ratio
Calculation: Short target = opening range midpoint for a fade; otherwise entry-bar close − (short stop − entry-bar close) × configured reward-to-risk ratio
Long: Valid range AND during the configured Asian session after range building AND ((close > opening range high + configured breakout buffer AND prior close ≤ opening range high + configured breakout buffer AND RSI > configured breakout threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > average volume × configured breakout multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND session trades below configured limit AND flat) OR (high ≥ opening range high − configured reversion buffer AND close < opening range high AND prior close ≥ opening range high AND RSI > configured overbought threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > average volume × configured fade multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA)))
Short: Valid range AND during the configured Asian session after range building AND ((close < opening range low − configured breakout buffer AND prior close ≥ opening range low − configured breakout buffer AND RSI < configured breakout threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > average volume × configured breakout multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND session trades below configured limit AND flat) OR (low ≤ opening range low + configured reversion buffer AND close > opening range low AND prior close ≤ opening range low AND RSI < configured oversold threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > average volume × configured fade multiplier) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA)))
Exit: Long, low ≤ stored long stop OR high ≥ stored long target OR the session ends
Exit: Short, high ≥ stored short stop OR low ≤ stored short target OR the session ends
