Calculation: TPO value area = Divide the profile high-to-low range into an adjustable number of equal price rows. For each profile bar, add one touch to every row intersected by its low-to-high range, with row indices clamped to the profile. Start at the lowest row with the maximum touch count and expand one adjacent row at a time until the accumulated touches reach an adjustable fraction of total touches; choose the side with more touches, choosing the upper side on a tie. The value-area high is the upper edge of the highest included row; the value-area low is the lower edge of the lowest included row.
Calculation: Market phase and buffered value area = Calculate independently on each of three requested timeframes, ordered from shorter to longer. Each may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Initialize in balance with the first bar as the profile anchor and its high and low as the profile range. In balance, expand the range for each new high or low, rebuild the TPO profile from the anchor when the range expands, and otherwise add each confirmed bar. Add an adjustable multiple of ATR to the value-area high and subtract it from the value-area low. Save these buffered boundaries on each balance bar; if its close is outside them, enter out-of-balance and record the breakout time. While out of balance, retain the profile and saved boundaries. If close returns inside or on both saved boundaries, return to balance, expand the profile range to include all bars since the breakout, and rebuild from the existing anchor. Otherwise, on each confirmed bar, examine a window whose length is the lesser of an adjustable maximum and the elapsed timeframe intervals since breakout. Once this length reaches an adjustable minimum, build a TPO value area from that many most recent bars. If its included rows are between adjustable minimum and maximum fractions of all rows, inclusive, return to balance, replace the profile range with that window's high and low, set the anchor to its oldest bar, and rebuild the profile. Rebuilds use available bars from the anchor, limited to the most recent adjustable maximum history. Recalculate buffered boundaries after rebuilding. For each requested timeframe, use the phase and buffered boundaries of its previous bar as delivered by a non-lookahead security request: for a higher timeframe, update on the last chart bar of the current higher-timeframe interval; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar result.
Long: Any requested timeframe's returned phase is out-of-balance AND chart close > its returned buffered value-area high.
Short: Any requested timeframe's returned phase is out-of-balance AND chart close < its returned buffered value-area low.
