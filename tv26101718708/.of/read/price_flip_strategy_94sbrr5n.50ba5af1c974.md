Calculation: Inverted price = highest high over its lookback, including the current bar, plus lowest low over its independently chosen lookback, including the current bar, minus current close.
Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages use the same selected type and respectively shorter and longer lengths. Their source is close, except for the price-specific types. Supported types are SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, VWMA, HMA, TEMA, DEMA, ALMA and linear-regression moving average; SMMA uses RMA, VAMA uses VWMA, EPMA uses linear regression, and JMA uses an EMA of an EMA with the same length. GMA uses ALMA with midpoint offset and the script's fixed sigma. ZLEMA is an EMA of close + (close - close at floor(length/2) bars ago). High, low, median-price, typical-price and weighted-close moving averages are EMAs of the named price. KAMA uses efficiency ratio = absolute close change over length / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over length, or zero when the denominator is zero; its smoothing factor is the square of the interpolation between 2/(2+1) and 2/(efficiency-period+1) by that ratio, and its recursive value starts at zero. FRAMA uses current and preceding nonoverlapping length-bar close ranges divided by length as n1 and n2; d = log((n1+n2)/(n1-n2 if both are positive, otherwise 1))/log(2/(slow-constant+1)); alpha = exp(-4.6*(d-1)) clamped to [0.01,1]; its recursive value is alpha*close + (1-alpha)*previous value, initialized at zero.
Long: Previous close > current inverted price AND fast moving average crosses above slow moving average AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close > slow moving average AND bar datetime is within the configured inclusive date range.
Short: Previous close < current inverted price AND fast moving average crosses below slow moving average AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close < slow moving average AND bar datetime is within the configured inclusive date range.
Long: Fast moving average > slow moving average.
Short: Fast moving average <= slow moving average.
Long: Close <= open.
Short: Close > open.
Exit: short, previous close > current inverted price AND fast moving average crosses above slow moving average AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close > slow moving average AND bar datetime is within the configured inclusive date range.
Exit: long, previous close < current inverted price AND fast moving average crosses below slow moving average AND, if the trend filter is enabled, close < slow moving average AND bar datetime is within the configured inclusive date range.
