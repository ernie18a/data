Calculation: A pivot high or low is confirmed after its specified number of right-hand bars, using the specified left-hand bars; all decisions occur on the confirmation bar. Index [n] denotes n chart bars ago. Parameters and thresholds are configurable.
Calculation: Momentum index = EMA of ((Laguerre RSI − 0.5) × 60 + Fisher transform × 15 + TSI × 0.6 + (MFI − 50) × 0.3 + volume contribution). Laguerre RSI uses L0 = (1 − gamma) × close + gamma × L0[1], L1 = −gamma × L0 + L0[1] + gamma × L1[1], L2 = −gamma × L1 + L1[1] + gamma × L2[1], and L3 = −gamma × L2 + L2[1] + gamma × L3[1], initialized to zero; it is the sum of positive adjacent differences divided by the sum of their absolute differences, or 0.5 when that sum is zero. For Fisher transform, normalize the midpoint of high and low to −1 through 1 within its rolling range; recursively smooth it as clamp(0.66 × normalized value + 0.67 × previous smoothed value, −0.999, 0.999), then calculate 0.5 × log((1 + smoothed value) / (1 − smoothed value)) + 0.5 × previous Fisher value; initialize both recursive values to zero. When current volume is positive, volume contribution = 10 × SMA(sign(close − close[1]) × volume) / max(volume SMA, 1) + 5 × OBV change over a specified short lookback / max(standard deviation of one-bar OBV changes over a longer lookback, 1) + 5 × accumulation/distribution change over the same short lookback / max(standard deviation of its one-bar changes over the same longer lookback, 1) + 30 × CMF; otherwise it is zero. MFI uses typical price; TSI has a shorter and a longer smoothing period.
Calculation: Trend score = +1 if Hull MA > Hull MA two bars ago, otherwise −1; +1 for bullish SuperTrend, otherwise −1; +1 if fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA, −1 if fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA, otherwise 0; and +0.5 or −0.5 for each of two SMAs according as close is above it or not. The first SMA has a shorter period than the second. Trend state = 2 when score ≥ strong-trend score threshold AND ADX > strength threshold; otherwise 1 when score ≥ bullish score threshold; otherwise −2 when score ≤ negative strong-trend score threshold AND ADX > strength threshold; otherwise −1 when score ≤ negative bullish score threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish countertrend condition = trend state < 0 AND momentum index > momentum index[1] > momentum index[2] AND close > Hull MA AND close[1] ≤ Hull MA[1]. Bearish countertrend condition = trend state > 0 AND momentum index < momentum index[1] < momentum index[2] AND close < Hull MA AND close[1] ≥ Hull MA[1].
Calculation: Supply and demand zones are created on confirmation of a high or low pivot when current volume is missing or nonpositive OR pivot-bar volume > its volume SMA. A supply zone has top = pivot high and bottom = max(pivot-bar min(open, close), pivot high − current ATR). A demand zone has bottom = pivot low and top = min(pivot-bar max(open, close), pivot low + current ATR). Before adding new zones, remove demand when low < bottom, close < bottom, or close < midpoint, according to the selected wick, close, or average mitigation mode; remove supply when high > top, close > top, or close > midpoint, respectively. Keep only the specified maximum number of most recent zones of each type.
Calculation: A bullish gap has bottom = high[2] and top = low when low > high[2] AND close[1] > open[1]. A bearish gap has bottom = high and top = low[2] when high < low[2] AND close[1] < open[1]. Before adding new gaps, remove any gap when low < its bottom AND high > its top; retain only the specified maximum number of most recent gaps. Both gap directions share one ordered collection.
Calculation: Liquidity levels are confirmed pivot highs and lows, retained separately up to a specified maximum count. Before adding newly confirmed levels, remove every high level crossed by high > level and every low level crossed by low < level. A bearish sweep occurs when a removed high level also has close < level; a bullish sweep occurs when a removed low level also has close > level.
Calculation: Wave momentum = ALMA of (close − close over its momentum lookback) divided by ATR and multiplied by 10. Calculate five EMAs of wave momentum with successively longer periods; each of four wave bases is the average of an adjacent EMA pair. Mean wave basis is their average, and wave deviation is its rolling standard deviation.
Calculation: For each newly confirmed price pivot, compare it with the immediately preceding pivot of the same type if their separation is between the minimum and maximum lookbacks inclusive. Compare RSI, MACD line, TSI, and smoothed stochastic at the two pivot bars. Regular bullish divergence requires a lower price low and a specified minimum count of higher oscillator values; hidden bullish divergence requires a higher price low and that count of lower oscillator values. Regular bearish divergence requires a higher price high and that count of lower oscillator values; hidden bearish divergence requires a lower price high and that count of higher oscillator values. Update the preceding pivot even when its separation is outside the allowed range. A regular divergence remains active from its confirmation bar through the specified number of bars equal to the pivot right-hand length plus an additional lookback.
Calculation: Squeeze = upper Bollinger band < upper Keltner band AND lower Bollinger band > lower Keltner band. Bollinger bands use close SMA and close standard deviation; Keltner bands use close EMA and ATR, with their respective multipliers. An upward release requires squeeze[1] AND no squeeze now AND close > Keltner EMA; a downward release substitutes close < Keltner EMA.
Calculation: Flow index = EMA of (0.4 × 100 × CMF + 0.4 × (typical-price MFI − 50) + 10 if OBV > its EMA, otherwise −10, + 10 if accumulation/distribution > its EMA, otherwise −10). Flow phase = 2 above the strong positive threshold, 1 above the weaker positive threshold, −2 below the strong negative threshold, −1 below the weaker negative threshold, and 0 otherwise. Volume surge = volume > volume SMA × surge multiplier. Buy pressure = CMF > 0 AND MFI > 50 AND OBV > its EMA AND volume surge; sell pressure = CMF < 0 AND MFI < 50 AND OBV ≤ its EMA AND volume surge.
Calculation: Range position = (close − rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low). Bullish votes count one each for: momentum index > 0 AND rising; close inside an active demand zone OR close above the top of the most recent active gap; wave momentum above all four bases OR above mean wave basis plus deviation × multiplier; bullish sweep; trend state > 0 OR bullish countertrend condition; active regular bullish divergence; upward squeeze release; flow phase > 0 OR buy pressure; range position below the discount threshold AND trend state ≥ 0; volume surge AND close > open. Bearish votes count one each for the mirrored conditions: momentum index < 0 AND falling; close inside supply OR below the bottom of the most recent active gap; wave momentum below all four bases OR below mean wave basis minus deviation × multiplier; bearish sweep; trend state < 0 OR bearish countertrend condition; active regular bearish divergence; downward squeeze release; flow phase < 0 OR sell pressure; range position above the premium threshold AND trend state ≤ 0; volume surge AND close < open.
Calculation: Raw long condition = bullish votes ≥ minimum vote count AND bullish votes − bearish votes ≥ minimum vote margin AND close > Hull MA. Raw short condition = bearish votes ≥ minimum vote count AND bearish votes − bullish votes ≥ minimum vote margin AND close < Hull MA. A long signal requires the raw long condition now and its absence on the preceding bar; a short signal uses the equivalent raw short transition. Either signal additionally requires the elapsed bars since the most recent long or short signal to be greater than the signal gap; initialize the last-signal bar sufficiently far in the past and update it only after a signal.
Calculation: Sentiment = 0.2 × (100 − percentile rank of volatility index) + 0.3 × clamp(50 + momentum index, 0, 100) + 0.2 × clamp(50 + 5 × (close − longer SMA) / ATR, 0, 100) + 0.1 × volume sentiment + 0.2 × RSI. Volatility index = 0.4 × ATR / close × 100 + 0.3 × Bollinger band width / close × 100 + 0.003 × historical volatility, where historical volatility is the standard deviation of log(close / close[1]) multiplied by the square root of the annualization factor and by 100. Volume sentiment = clamp(50 + 50 × (volume / max(volume SMA, 1) − 1) × (1 if close > open, otherwise −1), 0, 100) when volume is positive, otherwise 50.
