Calculation: Bullish vote count = sum of these 10 Boolean votes (true = 1): Momentum Index > 0 and rising; close inside an active demand zone OR above the newest active fair value gap; wave momentum above all four wave bases OR more than 2 standard deviations above their average; bullish sell-side liquidity sweep; bullish trend state OR bullish counter-trend setup; regular bullish divergence within the pivot confirmation window; bullish squeeze release; positive flow phase OR buying pressure; discount value with non-bearish trend state; volume surge on an up-close bar.
Calculation: Bearish vote count = sum of these 10 Boolean votes: Momentum Index < 0 and falling; close inside an active supply zone OR below the newest active fair value gap; wave momentum below all four wave bases OR more than 2 standard deviations below their average; bearish buy-side liquidity sweep; bearish trend state OR bearish counter-trend setup; regular bearish divergence within the pivot confirmation window; bearish squeeze release; negative flow phase OR selling pressure; premium value with non-bullish trend state; volume surge on a down-close bar.
Calculation: Momentum Index = smoothed blend of Laguerre RSI, Fisher Transform, TSI, MFI, signed-volume flow, OBV slope, A/D slope, and Chaikin Money Flow; rising/falling means greater/less than its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Trend state = combine Hull MA slope, SuperTrend direction, short/medium/long EMA alignment, price relative to short- and long-lookback SMAs, and whether ADX exceeds its strength threshold; positive is bullish, negative bearish, zero neutral. Bullish/bearish counter-trend setup = trend state is opposite, Momentum Index moves in that direction for two consecutive bars, and close crosses the Hull MA in that direction.
Calculation: Wave bases = four pairwise averages of adjacent short-to-long EMAs of ALMA-smoothed, ATR-normalized price momentum; wave momentum is the ALMA-smoothed momentum.
Calculation: Bullish (bearish) divergence = at a confirmed price pivot low (high), price makes a lower low (higher high) than its previous pivot and at least the configured minimum number of RSI, MACD, TSI, and Stochastic values make higher lows (lower highs); hidden bullish (bearish) divergence uses a higher low (lower high) in price and lower lows (higher highs) in at least that many oscillators. Compare pivots only when their separation is within the configured minimum and maximum lookback.
Calculation: Discount value = close is below 40% of the range from the lowest low to highest high over the value lookback; premium value = above 60% of that range.
Calculation: Volume surge = volume exceeds its moving average multiplied by the surge factor. Buying pressure = positive Chaikin Money Flow, MFI above its midpoint, OBV above its EMA, and volume surge; selling pressure is the converse. Flow phase = sign and strength category of the smoothed Flow Index, which combines Chaikin Money Flow, MFI, and whether OBV and A/D are above their EMAs.
Calculation: Bullish squeeze release = prior bar was in a Bollinger-inside-Keltner squeeze, current bar is not, and close is above the Keltner basis; bearish squeeze release uses close below the basis.
Calculation: Bullish (bearish) liquidity sweep = price wicks above an active prior swing high (below an active prior swing low) and closes back below (above) that level.
Long: bullish vote count >= minimum confluence threshold AND bullish vote count - bearish vote count >= minimum vote margin AND close > Hull MA AND the same raw long condition was false on the prior bar AND bars since the last signal > minimum signal gap.
Long: trend state < 0 AND Momentum Index > its prior-bar value AND its prior value > its value two bars ago AND close > Hull MA AND prior close <= prior Hull MA.
Long: regular or hidden bullish divergence at a confirmed pivot low.
Short: bearish vote count >= minimum confluence threshold AND bearish vote count - bullish vote count >= minimum vote margin AND close < Hull MA AND the same raw short condition was false on the prior bar AND bars since the last signal > minimum signal gap.
Short: trend state > 0 AND Momentum Index < its prior-bar value AND its prior value < its value two bars ago AND close < Hull MA AND prior close >= prior Hull MA.
Short: regular or hidden bearish divergence at a confirmed pivot high.
Exit: Long, a short signal OR flow phase becomes negative after being nonnegative.
Exit: Short, a long signal OR flow phase becomes positive after being nonpositive.
