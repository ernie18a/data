Calculation: Klinger Oscillator = EMA(volume if close >= previous close, otherwise -volume, shorter lookback) minus EMA(the same signed volume, longer lookback)
Calculation: Smoothed Klinger Oscillator = selected moving average of the Klinger Oscillator
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving average of the Smoothed Klinger Oscillator
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot low/high = respectively, a low/high with the specified number of lower/higher oscillator values on both sides; compare the pivot value and corresponding price low/high with the previous same-kind pivot, which must be within the specified bar range
Long: (Smoothed Klinger Oscillator crosses above Signal line when smoothing is selected, otherwise Klinger Oscillator crosses above Smoothed Klinger Oscillator) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, the oscillator pivot is higher and the corresponding price low is lower than at the previous pivot low) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, the oscillator pivot is lower and the corresponding price low is higher than at the previous pivot low)
Short: (Smoothed Klinger Oscillator crosses below Signal line when smoothing is selected, otherwise Klinger Oscillator crosses below Smoothed Klinger Oscillator) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, the oscillator pivot is lower and the corresponding price high is higher than at the previous pivot high) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, the oscillator pivot is higher and the corresponding price high is lower than at the previous pivot high)
