Calculation: True range = maximum of high minus low, absolute value of high minus previous close, and absolute value of low minus previous close; use high minus low when the previous close is unavailable.
Calculation: Activity percentiles = when the chart timeframe is at least the main timeframe, calculate the percent ranks over the lookback of chart-bar volume and of the sum of true ranges of completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar; if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use the chart-timeframe average true range with length equal to the main-to-lower timeframe ratio in place of that sum. If the selected lower timeframe exceeds the chart timeframe, use the chart timeframe instead. When the chart timeframe is lower than the main timeframe, calculate the percent ranks of rolling sums of chart-bar volume and true range, each over the main-to-chart timeframe bar ratio, capped at the platform history limit; use a rank lookback equal to the original lookback multiplied by that capped ratio, also capped at the platform history limit.
Calculation: Reference open = the current chart-bar open when the chart timeframe is at least the main timeframe; otherwise, the open of the chart bar the capped main-to-chart timeframe bar ratio bars ago.
Long: Volume percentile > volume threshold AND true-range percentile > volatility threshold AND close > reference open
Short: Volume percentile > volume threshold AND true-range percentile > volatility threshold AND close <= reference open
