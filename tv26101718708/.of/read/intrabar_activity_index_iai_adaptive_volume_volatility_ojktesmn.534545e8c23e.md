Calculation: Volume percentile = percentile rank of volume over the lookback; on charts below the main timeframe, use the rolling sum of chart-bar volume over one main-timeframe span and a proportionally expanded lookback. Use the current value, including the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Volatility percentile = percentile rank of true range over the lookback; on charts below the main timeframe, use the rolling sum of chart-bar true range over one main-timeframe span and a proportionally expanded lookback. Otherwise, sum lower-timeframe true range within the current main-timeframe bar and rank it over the lookback. Use the current value, including the still-forming bar; the lower timeframe is below the chart timeframe.
Long: Volume percentile > volume threshold AND volatility percentile > volatility threshold AND (chart timeframe is at least the main timeframe AND current chart bar close > open OR chart timeframe is below the main timeframe AND current close > open one main-timeframe-to-chart-timeframe ratio bars ago)
Short: Volume percentile > volume threshold AND volatility percentile > volatility threshold AND (chart timeframe is at least the main timeframe AND current chart bar close <= open OR chart timeframe is below the main timeframe AND current close <= open one main-timeframe-to-chart-timeframe ratio bars ago)
