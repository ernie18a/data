Calculation: Developing volume profile = At each configurable calendar-session boundary, reset a fixed 500-bin array, session volume, and base price. Set the bin width to a configurable price increment. On every bar, round high and low to the nearest bin width; initialize the base price to the rounded low. If the rounded low is below the base, shift existing bins upward by the corresponding number of bins and set the base to the rounded low only if the shift is less than the array size. For bars eligible under the optional trading-hours volume filter, add volume to session volume and distribute it equally over all rounded price bins from low through high inclusive; discard allocations outside the array. The developing point of control is the price stored in the first bin with maximum volume. Starting at that bin, expand upward or downward toward the adjacent bin with greater volume, choosing upward on a tie, until the accumulated volume reaches a configurable fraction of session volume or both array ends are reached. If both adjacent volumes are zero, advance both available indices without changing the boundaries. The last selected upper and lower bin prices are the developing value-area high and low; initialize both to the point of control. Recalculate after each chart bar, including the current bar.
Calculation: Previous point of control = The developing point of control saved immediately before the latest calendar-session reset.
Calculation: Near a level = High >= level minus a configurable price tolerance AND low <= level plus that tolerance.
Calculation: Eligible signal = The optional trading-hours signal filter passes AND the number of bars since the last eligible signal of the same type is at least a configurable minimum. Upward and downward value-area breakouts share one cooldown; point-of-control, value-area-high, value-area-low, and previous-point-of-control touches each have separate cooldowns. Update cooldowns whenever their conditions pass, regardless of marking settings.
