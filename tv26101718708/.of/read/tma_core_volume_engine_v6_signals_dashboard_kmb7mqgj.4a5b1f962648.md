Calculation: Current-session volume profile = Round each bar’s high and low to the configured price-bin size; distribute its volume equally across every bin in that range and accumulate by bin for the session. Include only bars in the configured session window when session filtering is enabled.
Calculation: Developing point of control (dPOC) = Price of the current-session bin with the greatest accumulated volume.
Calculation: Value Area High (VAH) and Value Area Low (VAL) = Starting at the dPOC, expand to adjacent bins by adding the higher-volume side (choose the higher-price side on ties) until the configured share of current-session volume is included; VAH and VAL are the outer prices reached.
Calculation: Previous-session POC = The completed session’s dPOC; this is the reference POC for the following session and is naked until price reaches it.
Long: A new touch of VAL OR close crosses above VAH OR a new dPOC touch with close >= dPOC OR a new touch of the naked previous-session POC with close >= previous-session POC; a touch means high >= level - configured touch distance AND low <= level + configured touch distance, and is new when the preceding bar was not a touch; apply the configured signal-session filter and same-signal cooldown.
Short: A new touch of VAH OR close crosses below VAL OR a new dPOC touch with close < dPOC OR a new touch of the naked previous-session POC with close < previous-session POC; a touch means high >= level - configured touch distance AND low <= level + configured touch distance, and is new when the preceding bar was not a touch; apply the configured signal-session filter and same-signal cooldown.
