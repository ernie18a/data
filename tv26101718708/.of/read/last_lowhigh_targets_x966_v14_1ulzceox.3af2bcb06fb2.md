Calculation: Pivot low = a low lower than the lows of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; it is confirmed after the bars after it complete
Calculation: Pivot high = a high higher than the highs of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; it is confirmed after the bars after it complete
Calculation: Long break level = highest high strictly between the preceding pivot low and the current, higher pivot low
Calculation: Short break level = lowest low strictly between the preceding pivot high and the current, lower pivot high
Calculation: Quality score = 25 for an active setup + 25 if a breakout has been seen + 20 for displacement (or 20 if disabled) + 15 for volume confirmation (or 15 if disabled) + 15 for higher-timeframe bias confirmation (or 15 if disabled); displacement requires the close to exceed the break level in the trade direction by the configured ATR multiple, volume confirmation requires volume to be at least its configured SMA multiple, and higher-timeframe bias compares the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA from that same completed bar
Long: a current pivot low is higher than its preceding pivot low, the setup has not been invalidated (close below the current pivot low in close mode, or low below it in wick mode), and (the configured entry mode includes breakout and the close crosses above the long break level, or late-breakout capture is enabled and the close is above that level after a crossing since the current pivot low, or the configured entry mode includes retest and a later bar has low at or below the level and closes above it); apply the configured score gate if enabled; evaluate each configured pivot lookback and select the setup using the configured priority rule; apply the configured direction mode, and if both sides are enabled and active, select the side with the higher quality score, breaking ties in favor of the more recent pivot
Short: a current pivot high is lower than its preceding pivot high, the setup has not been invalidated (close above the current pivot high in close mode, or high above it in wick mode), and (the configured entry mode includes breakout and the close crosses below the short break level, or late-breakout capture is enabled and the close is below that level after a crossing since the current pivot high, or the configured entry mode includes retest and a later bar has high at or above the level and closes below it); apply the configured score gate if enabled; evaluate each configured pivot lookback and select the setup using the configured priority rule; apply the configured direction mode, and if both sides are enabled and active, select the side with the higher quality score, breaking ties in favor of the more recent pivot
