Calculation: percent of average daily volume completed = cumulative chart-bar volume since the current day began ÷ average volume of prior completed daily bars × 100 (daily bars are higher timeframe)
Calculation: price change from daily open = (current close − current day’s first open) ÷ current day’s first open × 100
Calculation: average daily turnover = average daily close × volume over prior completed daily bars (daily bars are higher timeframe)
Calculation: volume threshold = configured manual threshold, or an IST-time-dependent threshold when auto mode is enabled
Long: percent of average daily volume completed ≥ volume threshold AND absolute price change from daily open ≥ minimum price move AND average daily turnover ≥ minimum turnover
