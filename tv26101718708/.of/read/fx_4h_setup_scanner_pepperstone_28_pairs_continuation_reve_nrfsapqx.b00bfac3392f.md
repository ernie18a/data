Calculation: Scan bars = bars on the selected scan timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; evaluate the last completed scan bar and its preceding scan bars.
Calculation: Body = absolute value of close minus open; previous body is the same calculation on the preceding scan bar.
Calculation: Similar bodies = previous body > 0 AND body > 0 AND absolute value of (body minus previous body) divided by previous body <= body tolerance.
Calculation: Bullish signal candle = close > open AND ((high > previous high AND low < previous low) OR (previous close < previous open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND body > previous body) OR (previous close < previous open AND similar bodies AND absolute value of (open minus previous close) <= price tolerance AND absolute value of (close minus previous open) <= price tolerance)).
Calculation: Bearish signal candle = close < open AND ((high > previous high AND low < previous low) OR (previous close > previous open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND body > previous body) OR (previous close > previous open AND similar bodies AND absolute value of (open minus previous close) <= price tolerance AND absolute value of (close minus previous open) <= price tolerance)).
Calculation: Band touch count = number of the upper, middle, and lower Bollinger Bands whose current values are between the current low and high, inclusive.
Long: high > low AND bullish signal candle AND band touch count < 2 AND close >= low + (high - low) * close fraction AND (close > middle Bollinger Band + price buffer OR (high < middle Bollinger Band AND at least one scan bar from the signal bar through the reversal lookback has low <= its corresponding lower Bollinger Band)).
Short: high > low AND bearish signal candle AND band touch count < 2 AND close <= high - (high - low) * close fraction AND (close < middle Bollinger Band - price buffer OR (low > middle Bollinger Band AND at least one scan bar from the signal bar through the reversal lookback has high >= its corresponding upper Bollinger Band)).
