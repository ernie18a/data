Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: Session VWAP = running volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset each session

Calculation: Higher low = a confirmed pivot low above the previous confirmed pivot low
Calculation: Lower high = a confirmed pivot high below the previous confirmed pivot high
Calculation: Lower low = a confirmed pivot low below the previous confirmed pivot low
