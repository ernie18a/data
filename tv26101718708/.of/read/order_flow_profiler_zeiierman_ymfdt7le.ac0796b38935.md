Calculation: Buy and sell volume per price cell = For each bar in the rolling chart-bar window, estimate buy share as 0.5 when high=low; otherwise clamp(0.5 + 0.43 × [0.48 × (2 × clamp((close−low)/(high−low), 0, 1)−1) + 0.32 × clamp((close−open)/(high−low), −1, 1) + 0.20 × (lower wick−upper wick)/(high−low)], 0.025, 0.975). Allocate volume × buy share and remaining volume among equal cells spanning the window’s low-to-high range. For each bar, allocate each side across touched cells in proportion to overlapped bar-range fraction × [1 + 2.6 × (1−min(abs(cell midpoint−side’s center)/(high−low), 1))^concentration], normalized across touched cells separately for each side; buy center = clamp(0.50×close + 0.32×high + 0.18×open, low, high), sell center = clamp(0.50×close + 0.32×low + 0.18×open, low, high). Include the current chart bar according to the live-bar setting.
Calculation: Control cell = price cell with the greatest combined buy and sell volume.
Long: The nearest cell above or below the control cell has buy volume greater than sell volume in the cell immediately below it multiplied by the configured pressure ratio, and its combined volume is at least the configured minimum percentage of the greatest combined cell volume; if that same cell also qualifies as sell pressure, buy volume divided by the sell volume below is greater than or equal to sell volume divided by the buy volume above.
Short: The nearest cell above or below the control cell has sell volume greater than buy volume in the cell immediately above it multiplied by the configured pressure ratio, and its combined volume is at least the configured minimum percentage of the greatest combined cell volume; if that same cell also qualifies as buy pressure, sell volume divided by the buy volume above is greater than buy volume divided by the sell volume below.
