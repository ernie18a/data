Calculation: Profile buy and sell volumes = Over a configurable chart-bar lookback, optionally excluding the current bar, use bars with complete OHLCV and positive volume. Divide their overall low-to-high range into equal ascending price cells. For each bar with range r = high - low > 0, calculate close position p = clamp((close - low) / r, 0, 1), body b = clamp((close - open) / r, -1, 1), upper wick u = max(high - max(open, close), 0) / r, lower wick l = max(min(open, close) - low, 0) / r, and buy share q = clamp(0.5 + 0.43 * (0.48 * (2 * p - 1) + 0.32 * b + 0.20 * (l - u)), 0.025, 0.975). For r = 0, q = 0.5. Buy volume is volume * q and sell volume is volume * (1 - q). For r > 0, distribute each side's volume separately over cells touched by the bar, proportionally to weight = overlap / r * (1 + 2.6 * (1 - min(abs(cell midpoint - anchor) / r, 1)) ^ concentration), where the buy anchor is clamp(0.50 * close + 0.32 * high + 0.18 * open, low, high), the sell anchor is clamp(0.50 * close + 0.32 * low + 0.18 * open, low, high), and concentration is configurable. For r = 0, assign both volumes to the cell containing close. Sum contributions per cell; clamp cell indices to the profile bounds. If the entire profile range is zero, there is no directional decision.
Calculation: Control cell = The lowest-index cell with the maximum sum of profile buy and sell volumes.
Calculation: Segment volumes = Divide the ascending profile cells into a configurable number of segments. Segment i contains cells from floor(i * cell count / segment count) through max(floor((i + 1) * cell count / segment count) - 1, floor(i * cell count / segment count)); sum buy and sell volumes separately over those cells.
Calculation: Pressure side = For each cell, a buy hit requires cell buy plus sell volume >= the maximum combined cell volume * a configurable minimum activity fraction, a lower neighboring cell with sell volume > 0, and cell buy volume > lower neighbor sell volume * a configurable pressure ratio. A sell hit requires the same activity condition, an upper neighboring cell with buy volume > 0, and cell sell volume > upper neighbor buy volume * the same pressure ratio. If both hit, select buy when its buy-to-lower-sell ratio >= the sell-to-upper-buy ratio; otherwise select the hit side. If neither hits, the cell has no pressure side.
Long: Any profile cell buy volume > its sell volume OR any segment buy volume > its sell volume OR the nearest cell above or below the control cell with a pressure side has buy pressure side.
Short: Any profile cell sell volume > its buy volume OR any segment sell volume > its buy volume OR the nearest cell above or below the control cell with a pressure side has sell pressure side.
