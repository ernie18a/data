Calculation: Evaluate all decisions on completed chart bars. A pivot high or low is confirmed after the specified equal numbers of bars to its left and right have formed; compare it with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind. Internal pivots use a shorter lookback than major pivots. Process major highs before major lows when both are confirmed on the same bar.
Calculation: Bull trend = fast close EMA > slow close EMA AND close > fast EMA; bear trend = fast close EMA < slow close EMA AND close < fast EMA. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA. Higher-timeframe bull and bear trends use the same comparisons and EMA lengths on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; when higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled, use chart-timeframe trends instead.
Calculation: Mean = session VWAP of typical price on intraday charts when available, otherwise a close EMA. Volume is available when current volume and its simple moving average are available and the average is positive.
Calculation: Bollinger bandwidth = twice the Bollinger standard-deviation multiple divided by its close SMA, or zero when that SMA is zero. Compression = bandwidth < its longer-lookback SMA multiplied by the compression threshold. EMA separation = absolute difference between fast and slow EMAs divided by ATR, or zero when ATR is not positive. Range = compression OR (ADX < range threshold AND EMA separation < minimum separation). Directional regime = NOT range AND (ADX >= trend threshold OR EMA separation >= structure activation threshold OR ATR > its longer-lookback SMA multiplied by the expansion threshold).
Calculation: Major structure direction starts neutral. On a directional-regime bar, a bullish break occurs when close > latest confirmed major pivot high + ATR multiplied by the break buffer and that pivot has not previously broken; a bearish break occurs when close < latest confirmed major pivot low - ATR multiplied by the break buffer and that pivot has not previously broken. A new pivot resets its own broken flag. Each break sets direction to its side; process a bullish break before a bearish break on the same bar.
Calculation: For a confirmed major pivot, pivot ATR is ATR at the pivot bar, falling back to current ATR; reaction is max(pivot high - current close, 0) / pivot ATR for a high or max(current close - pivot low, 0) / pivot ATR for a low, or zero if pivot ATR is not positive. Leg is the absolute distance to the latest opposite major pivot divided by pivot ATR, or 1 if unavailable or pivot ATR is not positive. Volume ratio is pivot-bar volume / its volume SMA when that SMA is positive and available, otherwise 1. Pivot strength = round(min(100, 15 + 30 × clamp(reaction, 0, 2) / 2 + 25 × clamp(leg, 0, 4) / 4 + 20 × clamp(volume ratio, 0, 2) / 2 + 10 if the last completed higher-timeframe trend is bearish for a high or bullish for a low)); the final term is zero otherwise. When higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled, use the chart trend for this alignment.
Calculation: A bullish major sequence is a higher major high and higher major low; a bearish sequence is a lower-or-equal major high and lower-or-equal major low. At each newly confirmed major pivot, compare its type with the latest opposite pivot type. Sequence strength is the rounded average of the new and latest opposite pivot strengths. The new pivot's leg is measured as defined for pivot strength.
Calculation: Maintain bullish and bearish major-pair counts starting at zero. On a higher high reset the bearish count; on a lower-or-equal high reset the bullish count. On a higher low with latest high higher, increment the bullish count and reset the bearish count; on a lower-or-equal low with latest high lower-or-equal, increment the bearish count and reset the bullish count. Otherwise, a lower-or-equal low resets the bullish count and a higher low resets the bearish count.
Calculation: Bull score = 15 for bull trend + 20 for higher-timeframe bull trend + 20 for bullish major structure direction + 10 for RSI above its bullish threshold + 5 for MACD histogram > 0 + 10 for close > mean + volume points + 10 for ADX >= trend threshold AND +DI > -DI. Bear score uses the opposite comparisons and bearish RSI threshold. Volume points are 5 for each side if volume use is disabled or volume is unavailable; otherwise they are 10 for a side when volume > volume SMA multiplied by the general volume threshold AND close is respectively above or below the previous close, and zero otherwise. Score difference = bull score - bear score.
Calculation: A confirmed bullish trend requires directional regime AND bull trend AND close > mean AND bull score >= minimum signal score AND score difference >= minimum advantage; additionally require higher-timeframe bull trend if both higher-timeframe use and its signal filter are enabled, bullish major structure direction if its filter is enabled, ADX >= trend threshold AND +DI > -DI if its filter is enabled, and volume >= volume SMA multiplied by the signal volume threshold AND close > open if participation is required, volume use is enabled, and volume is available. A confirmed bearish trend uses the opposite directional comparisons, bear score and negative score difference, with the same optional filters.
Calculation: Signal trend state starts neutral. From neutral, a confirmed bullish trend sets it bullish and a confirmed bearish trend sets it bearish, with bullish priority. From an active state, an opposite confirmed trend immediately changes the state; otherwise count consecutive bars failing the active side's health condition and reset the count on a healthy bar. Bullish health = bull trend AND NOT range AND score difference > 0; bearish health = bear trend AND NOT range AND score difference < 0. Reset state to neutral when failures reach the reset-bar threshold; do not start a new trend on that reset bar.
Calculation: Each bullish break stores its level and bar, enables one bullish retest, and cancels any bearish retest; each bearish break does the converse. A retest must occur after its break and within the retest window, before another break cancels it, and consumes that retest opportunity.
Long: Internal pivot high > previous internal pivot high.
Short: Internal pivot high <= previous internal pivot high.
Long: Internal pivot low > previous internal pivot low.
Short: Internal pivot low <= previous internal pivot low.
Long: Newly confirmed major pivot forms a bullish major sequence AND sequence strength >= minimum band strength AND leg >= minimum band leg.
Short: Newly confirmed major pivot forms a bearish major sequence AND sequence strength >= minimum band strength AND leg >= minimum band leg.
Long: Bullish major structure break.
Short: Bearish major structure break.
Long: Bullish retest opportunity is active AND NOT range AND low <= bullish break level + ATR multiplied by retest tolerance AND low >= bullish break level - ATR multiplied by maximum penetration AND close > bullish break level AND close > open.
Short: Bearish retest opportunity is active AND NOT range AND high >= bearish break level - ATR multiplied by retest tolerance AND high <= bearish break level + ATR multiplied by maximum penetration AND close < bearish break level AND close < open.
Long: Confirmed bullish trend AND prior signal trend state is neutral or bearish.
Short: Confirmed bearish trend AND prior signal trend state is neutral or bullish.
Long: Bull trend.
Short: Bear trend.
Long: Higher-timeframe bull trend.
Short: Higher-timeframe bear trend.
Long: Score difference >= bullish bias threshold.
Short: Score difference <= negative bearish bias threshold.
Long: Bullish major-pair count > 0.
Short: Bearish major-pair count > 0.
Long: Latest major high > latest major low AND close < midpoint of those pivots - their range multiplied by the equilibrium tolerance.
Short: Latest major high > latest major low AND close > midpoint of those pivots + their range multiplied by the equilibrium tolerance.
