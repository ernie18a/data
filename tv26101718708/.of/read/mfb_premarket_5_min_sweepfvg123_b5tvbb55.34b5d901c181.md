Calculation: Session extremes = For each configured intraday session in the specified time zone, maintain the highest high and lowest low seen in that session, reset at each new day, and retain them until the next daily reset. Include the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar and the last completed, longer higher-timeframe weekly bar. The upper extreme is the maximum of these highs, substituting zero for unavailable highs; the lower extreme is the minimum of these lows, substituting a fixed large fallback for unavailable lows.
Calculation: Level touches = A high touch occurs when high >= upper extreme; a low touch occurs when low <= lower extreme. Each touch remains active through the configured lookback, inclusive, and both are cleared after any FVG entry. A new touch reactivates its side.
Calculation: Intention = Retain the most recent crossing of close above any individual high level or below any individual low level, comparing each close with its contemporaneous level. A crossing above sets bullish intention; otherwise a crossing below sets bearish intention. Bullish takes precedence if both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: FVG = A bullish FVG forms when low > high two bars earlier, with top = current low and bottom = that earlier high. A bearish FVG forms when high < low two bars earlier, with top = that earlier low and bottom = current high. At creation, record whether an active high touch and bottom >= upper extreme coincide, and whether an active low touch and top <= lower extreme coincide. Retain only the most recently created configured maximum number of FVGs.
Calculation: FVG processing = Process active FVGs newest first on each bar, including their creation bar. Before other processing, an eligible inversion can signal and deactivate the FVG. Otherwise deactivate a bullish FVG when low <= bottom or a bearish FVG when high >= top. For an FVG still active and not done, after its creation bar record the current bar as its latest touch whenever high >= bottom AND low <= top AND, for a bullish FVG, close >= bottom, or, for a bearish FVG, close <= top. Mark it done if, after creation, a bearish FVG closes above its top or a bullish FVG closes below its bottom. A standard FVG setup requires a recorded touch on an earlier bar and an FVG that is not done; after its first qualifying close beyond the opposite boundary, mark it done even if the entry filters fail. At most one FVG entry may occur per bar; after one occurs, later FVGs on that bar cannot enter.
Calculation: EMA retrace state = Use a shorter-period and a longer-period EMA of close. A crossover of the shorter EMA above the longer arms the long state and clears the short state; a crossunder arms the short state and clears the long state. If requiring a crossover is disabled and neither crossover occurs, arm an inactive long state when the shorter EMA > the longer EMA, and arm an inactive short state when the shorter EMA < the longer EMA. An armed long state advances when low <= the shorter EMA; an armed short state advances when high >= the shorter EMA. Advancement and entry can occur on the same bar. Reset the relevant state after its entry.
Long: The long EMA retrace state has advanced AND close > the shorter EMA.
Short: The short EMA retrace state has advanced AND close < the shorter EMA.
Long: An active bearish FVG was created beyond the lower extreme AND a low touch is active AND close > its top AND the FVG inversion setup is enabled AND no FVG entry has occurred on this bar.
Short: An active bullish FVG was created beyond the upper extreme AND a high touch is active AND close < its bottom AND the FVG inversion setup is enabled AND no FVG entry has occurred on this bar.
Long: A standard bullish FVG setup has close > its top AND bearish intention AND (an active low touch OR low <= lower extreme) AND FVG age <= the configured maximum age AND no FVG entry has occurred on this bar.
Short: A standard bearish FVG setup has close < its bottom AND bullish intention AND (an active high touch OR high >= upper extreme) AND FVG age <= the configured maximum age AND no FVG entry has occurred on this bar.
