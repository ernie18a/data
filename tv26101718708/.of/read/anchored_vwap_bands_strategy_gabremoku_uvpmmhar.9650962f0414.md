Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of OHLC4 from the configured anchor bar through the current bar
Calculation: Deviation = either the volume-weighted standard deviation of OHLC4 from the configured anchor bar through the current bar, or the rolling standard deviation of OHLC4 over a configured lookback
Calculation: Lower Band 1 = Anchored VWAP − Deviation × configured Band 1 multiplier
Calculation: Upper Band 1 = Anchored VWAP + Deviation × configured Band 1 multiplier
Calculation: AVWAP slope = current Anchored VWAP − Anchored VWAP at a shorter configured lookback
Long: configured strategy side allows long AND anchor is active AND ((configured signal mode is Retest AVWAP AND low ≤ Anchored VWAP + retest tolerance AND close > Anchored VWAP AND previous close ≤ previous Anchored VWAP) OR (configured signal mode is Band Reversal AND low ≤ Lower Band 1 AND close > Lower Band 1) OR (configured signal mode is Hybrid AND ((low ≤ Anchored VWAP + retest tolerance AND close > Anchored VWAP AND previous close ≤ previous Anchored VWAP) OR (low ≤ Lower Band 1 AND close > Lower Band 1)))) AND (close confirmation is disabled OR close > open) AND (slope filter is disabled OR AVWAP slope > 0)
Long: configured strategy side allows long AND anchor is active AND low ≤ Lower Band 1 + lower-band retest tolerance AND (close > open OR close ≥ low + 0.30 × (high − low))
Short: configured strategy side allows short AND anchor is active AND ((configured signal mode is Retest AVWAP AND high ≥ Anchored VWAP − retest tolerance AND close < Anchored VWAP AND previous close ≥ previous Anchored VWAP) OR (configured signal mode is Band Reversal AND high ≥ Upper Band 1 AND close < Upper Band 1) OR (configured signal mode is Hybrid AND ((high ≥ Anchored VWAP − retest tolerance AND close < Anchored VWAP AND previous close ≥ previous Anchored VWAP) OR (high ≥ Upper Band 1 AND close < Upper Band 1)))) AND (close confirmation is disabled OR close < open) AND (slope filter is disabled OR AVWAP slope < 0)
