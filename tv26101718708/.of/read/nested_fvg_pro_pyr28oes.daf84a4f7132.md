Calculation: Bullish gap = [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago; bearish gap = [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago. Gap size is upper bound minus lower bound.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe zones = gaps detected at the start of a new higher-timeframe bar using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it. Create a zone only inside the configured session and when its gap size is at least the higher-timeframe minimum, expressed either in points or as a fraction of the lower gap boundary. On each chart bar, before testing nesting, optionally invalidate bullish zones when low <= upper bound, low <= midpoint, or close < lower bound, according to the selected mitigation mode; invalidate bearish zones when high >= lower bound, high >= midpoint, or close > upper bound, respectively. On first invalidation, if inverted zones are enabled and the zone has not exceeded its maximum age, reverse its direction and mark it inverted; otherwise retire it. Retire zones exceeding the configured age or inverted-zone age, and retain only the newest configured maximum number of active zones per direction. Clear zones on the configured trade-date rollover.
Calculation: Eligible zone = active higher-timeframe zone whose direction matches the chart-timeframe gap and whose virgin or inverted status matches the selected source; the chart-timeframe gap's lower bound >= zone lower bound minus proximity tolerance AND its upper bound <= zone upper bound plus proximity tolerance.
Calculation: Signal gate = chart bar is in the configured session AND trading windows are disabled or the bar is in an enabled window AND the daily loss limit is disabled or realized daily net P&L > negative daily loss limit AND bars since the last signal >= cooldown AND no open or pending trade exists. Reset daily P&L and signal cooldown at the configured trade-date rollover. Daily net P&L includes realized exit P&L and configured commissions, with entry and exit fills booked at their plan levels or trigger-bar closes plus adverse slippage, as configured.
Calculation: Plan anchor = signal-bar close when immediate entry and entry-fill anchoring are selected; otherwise the bullish gap's lower bound for a long or the bearish gap's upper bound for a short. Stop and partial, main, extended, and optional runner targets are the anchor minus or plus their respective configured point distances, with the stop adverse and targets favorable to the position. A pending pullback entry uses the same gap boundary as its entry level for every pullback setting.
Long: A bullish higher-timeframe zone is created.
Short: A bearish higher-timeframe zone is created.
Long: A bullish chart-timeframe gap has size >= the chart-timeframe minimum AND an eligible bullish zone exists AND the signal gate holds.
Short: A bearish chart-timeframe gap has size >= the chart-timeframe minimum AND an eligible bearish zone exists AND the signal gate holds.
Long: A pending long entry exists from an earlier signal AND low <= its entry level; otherwise cancel the pending entry if cancellation is enabled AND high >= its partial target.
Short: A pending short entry exists from an earlier signal AND high >= its entry level; otherwise cancel the pending entry if cancellation is enabled AND low <= its partial target.
Long: Entry laddering is enabled AND a long position is before its partial exit AND an unfilled enabled ladder level at the signal gap midpoint or lower bound has low <= that level.
Short: Entry laddering is enabled AND a short position is before its partial exit AND an unfilled enabled ladder level at the signal gap midpoint or upper bound has high >= that level.
Exit: long, emergency loss limit is enabled AND (close - weighted average entry fill) × remaining quantity × point value < negative loss limit.
Exit: short, emergency loss limit is enabled AND (weighted average entry fill - close) × remaining quantity × point value < negative loss limit.
Exit: long, before the partial exit, low <= stop; otherwise high >= partial target exits the configured partial quantity; otherwise high >= main target exits all remaining quantity. After a partial, high >= the next unhit target exits its configured quantity, in main, extended, then runner order, with each stage eligible on the same bar; otherwise low <= stop exits the remainder, except on the bar the stop was moved to break-even. On a partial, optionally move the stop to the weighted average entry fill. Cap each exit quantity at the remaining quantity.
Exit: short, before the partial exit, high >= stop; otherwise low <= partial target exits the configured partial quantity; otherwise low <= main target exits all remaining quantity. After a partial, low <= the next unhit target exits its configured quantity, in main, extended, then runner order, with each stage eligible on the same bar; otherwise high >= stop exits the remainder, except on the bar the stop was moved to break-even. On a partial, optionally move the stop to the weighted average entry fill. Cap each exit quantity at the remaining quantity.
