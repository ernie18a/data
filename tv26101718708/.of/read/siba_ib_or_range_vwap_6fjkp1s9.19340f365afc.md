Calculation: Initial balance high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars overlapping the configured initial-balance interval, starting with the bar containing its start; freeze both when a bar reaches the interval end.
Calculation: Locked ATR = ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar when the initial balance completes; retain it until the next initial-balance start.
Calculation: Previous value area = at each initial-balance start, build a volume profile from the preceding interval between initial-balance starts, optionally including only chart bars fully within a configured session. Use completed lower-timeframe bars within each included chart bar. Divide each lower-timeframe bar's volume equally among every price bin from floor(low/bin size) through floor(high/bin size), inclusive. At the preceding start, fix the bin size using either a price parameter, a tick-size parameter times a multiplier, or the greater of a tick-size parameter and the last completed higher-timeframe daily ATR divided by a parameter; use the price parameter if that ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Center a fixed-size array of bins on floor(start-bar close/bin size), discard volume outside it, and select the lowest-index bin with maximum volume as the point of control. Expand from that bin toward the adjacent bin with more volume, choosing the lower bin on a tie, until a configured fraction of total retained volume is included. Point of control is the center of its bin; value-area high and low are the upper and lower edges of the included bins. If no volume is retained, keep the preceding available value area.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = a multiplier times locked ATR, or the same multiplier times initial balance high minus low when locked ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Upward breakout = initial balance completed AND close > initial balance high AND the upward breakout flag was false. Set that flag on the breakout; reset it when close < initial balance high minus breakout buffer, and reset it at the next initial-balance start.
Calculation: Downward breakout = initial balance completed AND close < initial balance low AND the downward breakout flag was false. Set that flag on the breakout; reset it when close > initial balance low plus breakout buffer, and reset it at the next initial-balance start.
Calculation: Upper and lower extension = initial balance high plus, and initial balance low minus, a multiplier times the initial balance range.
Long: Upward breakout.
Short: Downward breakout.
Long: Initial balance completed AND a downward breakout has occurred since the initial-balance start or the last failed downward breakout AND previous close <= initial balance low AND close > initial balance low.
Short: Initial balance completed AND an upward breakout has occurred since the initial-balance start or the last failed upward breakout AND previous close >= initial balance high AND close < initial balance high.
Long: Initial balance completed AND low <= previous value-area low AND close > previous value-area low AND close < previous point of control AND this is the first such long fade condition since the initial-balance start.
Short: Initial balance completed AND high >= previous value-area high AND close < previous value-area high AND close > previous point of control AND this is the first such short fade condition since the initial-balance start.
Long: Initial balance completed AND high >= upper extension AND no earlier bar since completion has reached either extension AND low > lower extension.
Short: Initial balance completed AND low <= lower extension AND no earlier bar since completion has reached either extension AND high < upper extension.
Calculation: Breakout long stop = entry price minus the smaller of entry price minus initial balance low and locked ATR times a stop multiplier; use initial balance low if locked ATR is unavailable. Breakout short stop = entry price plus the smaller of initial balance high minus entry price and locked ATR times the same stop multiplier; use initial balance high if locked ATR is unavailable. Freeze stops and extension targets at entry.
Calculation: Fade long stop = previous value-area low minus locked ATR times a fade stop multiplier; fade short stop = previous value-area high plus locked ATR times the same fade stop multiplier. If locked ATR is unavailable, replace the ATR buffer with a fraction of previous value-area width, or zero if that width is unavailable. Freeze stops and previous point-of-control targets at entry.
Exit: Long breakout position, low <= breakout long stop OR high >= upper extension target.
Exit: Short breakout position, high >= breakout short stop OR low <= lower extension target.
Exit: Long fade position, low <= fade long stop OR high >= previous point-of-control target.
Exit: Short fade position, high >= fade short stop OR low <= previous point-of-control target.
