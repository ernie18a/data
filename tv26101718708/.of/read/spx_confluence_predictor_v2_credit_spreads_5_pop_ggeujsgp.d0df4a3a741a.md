Calculation: Cumulative delta = sum signed volume since the start of the current higher-timeframe period, including its still-forming bar; signed volume is volume when close > open, -volume when close < open, and 0 otherwise or when volume is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Z-score = (value - its moving average) / its standard deviation over the same lookback; use 0 when the standard deviation is unavailable or zero.
Calculation: Component scores = clamp each score to [-1, 1]: VWAP score = location weight × sign(close - VWAP) + slope weight × sign(VWAP - previous VWAP), using VWAP reset at each higher-timeframe period boundary and including the still-forming period; delta score = Z-score of the change in cumulative delta over a lookback, divided by a scale; MACD score = MACD histogram divided by ATR times a scale; MFI score = (MFI - midpoint) divided by a scale; RSI score = strong positive score below a lower extreme threshold, strong negative score above an upper extreme threshold, weaker positive score below a higher lower threshold, weaker negative score above a lower upper threshold, and 0 otherwise. Evaluate the RSI cases in that order.
Calculation: Confluence score = weighted average of available component scores, with the external-data components disabled. Include VWAP when available, delta when current volume is positive, MACD when its histogram is available, MFI when current volume is positive and MFI is available, and RSI with the external volatility filter disabled. Replace unavailable component values with 0; return 0 if the sum of included weights is not positive.
Calculation: Spread probabilities = standard normal CDF of (close + drift - put strike) / expected move for the put, and of (call strike - close - drift) / expected move for the call. Drift = confluence score × drift factor × expected move. Expected move is a positive configured value or ATR × square root of a configured bar horizon, floored at a positive minimum. Put strike = close minus a configured fixed or ATR-based distance, rounded down to a strike increment; call strike = close plus that distance, rounded up to the same increment.
Long: VWAP score > component threshold OR delta score > component threshold OR MACD score > component threshold OR MFI score > component threshold OR RSI score > component threshold, with the respective component available.
Short: VWAP score < -component threshold OR delta score < -component threshold OR MACD score < -component threshold OR MFI score < -component threshold OR RSI score < -component threshold, with the respective component available.
Long: Confluence score > signal threshold OR confluence score > -signal threshold AND put spread probability >= minimum probability.
Short: Confluence score <= -signal threshold OR confluence score < signal threshold AND call spread probability >= minimum probability.
Long: Confluence score crosses above signal threshold AND put spread probability >= minimum probability.
Short: Confluence score crosses below -signal threshold AND call spread probability >= minimum probability.
