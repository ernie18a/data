Calculation: MACD = shorter-lookback EMA(close) − longer-lookback EMA(close)
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA(volume) − longer-lookback EMA(volume)) / longer-lookback EMA(volume)
Long: MACD crosses above 0 (current MACD > 0 AND previous MACD ≤ 0) AND volume oscillator > 0 AND volume > previous bar volume / 2
Short: MACD crosses below 0 (current MACD < 0 AND previous MACD ≥ 0) AND volume oscillator > 0 AND volume < previous bar volume / 2
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + 1.1 × stop distance
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − 1.1 × stop distance
