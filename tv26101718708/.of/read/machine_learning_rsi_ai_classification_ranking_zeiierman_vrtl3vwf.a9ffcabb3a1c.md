Calculation: RSI features = base RSI divided by 100; its change over a step lookback; the change in that change over the same step; absolute distance of base RSI from its midpoint divided by the midpoint; base RSI percentile rank divided by 100; standard deviation of base RSI; fast RSI minus slow RSI; and EMA of base RSI minus its midpoint. Min-max normalize each feature except the first, fourth and fifth over a feature lookback, returning 0.5 when the window maximum equals its minimum. The fast RSI uses a shorter lookback than the base RSI, and the slow RSI uses a longer lookback.
Calculation: Labeled memory = on each completed bar, prepend the feature snapshot from an outcome-horizon lookback and its outcome, retaining at most the memory-depth number of rows. Outcome is +3, +2 or +1 when the price-source change over the horizon is respectively greater than twice the outcome band, greater than the outcome band, or greater than zero; it is -3, -2 or -1 when the change is respectively less than minus twice the band, less than minus the band, or less than zero; otherwise it is zero. The outcome band is an ATR multiplier times ATR at the snapshot bar.
Calculation: Feature weights = either independent configurable weights or automatically learned weights initialized to one. For each feature, when memory has at least the minimum row count and each nonzero outcome class has more than two rows, calculate squared difference of the positive- and negative-class feature means divided by the sum of their population variances plus an epsilon; divide each feature's result by the maximum result across features, using one if that maximum is zero, multiply by the weight scale and apply a minimum weight floor. Otherwise use one for each raw weight. On each completed bar, update each automatic weight by adding the adaptation rate times the difference between its raw and previous weights.
Calculation: Analog classification = after updating memory and weights, scan every spacing-interval row starting with the newest row; discard rows with undefined distance. Distance is the sum across features of weight times log(1 + absolute feature difference). Retain up to the analog-count smallest distances, replacing the first most-distant retained row only when a candidate is strictly closer. Each retained row votes its signed outcome with weight 1 / (1 + distance). Analog score is the weighted mean outcome, or zero with no votes. Bias is bullish when score exceeds the positive bias threshold, bearish when below its negative, and neutral otherwise. Agreement is the fraction of total vote weight belonging to outcomes with the bias's sign, or zero for neutral bias or no votes. Tightness is clamp(1 - mean retained distance / (weight sum times a distance scale plus epsilon), 0, 1), using zero as mean distance when no neighbors exist.
Calculation: Market context = ATR percentile rank over a volatility lookback; healthy volatility means that rank is between its lower and upper thresholds inclusive. Trend force is absolute difference between a faster and a slower price-source EMA divided by ATR, or zero when ATR is not positive; raw chop means trend force is below its threshold, and active chop means raw chop with the chop filter enabled. RSI slope is up when base RSI exceeds its value at the step lookback. Slope fits bullish bias when up and bearish bias when not up. RSI is stretched when it exceeds an upper threshold with bullish bias or falls below a lower threshold with bearish bias. RSI regime is an EMA of base RSI; RSI smoothness is up when a shorter EMA of base RSI exceeds its preceding value.
Calculation: Adaptive trailing trend = smooth clamp(analog score / conviction scale, -1, 1) with an EMA. Drive is clamp(weighted sum of absolute smoothed conviction, tightness and agreement, 0, 1), multiplied by a chop reduction factor when active chop is present. Band multiplier is the base multiplier times (1 + adaptivity times (1 - drive)). Raw lower and upper bands are the trailing-trend price source minus and plus this multiplier times trailing-trend ATR. The lower band is the maximum of its raw value and previous lower band when the previous close exceeds the previous lower band, otherwise its raw value; the upper band is the minimum of its raw value and previous upper band when the previous close is below the previous upper band, otherwise its raw value. Initialize each band to its raw value and trend to bullish. Change bearish trend to bullish when close exceeds the previous upper band; change bullish trend to bearish when close falls below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous trend.
Calculation: Stance = initialize neutral. On each bar set stance to the non-neutral analog bias if the enabled trend gate agrees with the trailing trend, the enabled volatility gate is healthy, and active chop is false; otherwise retain the previous stance. Reset stance age to zero on a stance change and increment it otherwise. An early flip is a stance change with another stance change within the preceding early-flip lookback.
Calculation: Rank = for non-neutral bias, clamp the sum of agreement times its rank weight, tightness times its rank weight, a slope-fit award, a non-stretched award, a trailing-trend alignment award, a volatility award selected for healthy, below-lower-bound, or above-upper-bound volatility, an RSI-regime award selected for bias-aligned, neutral-range, or opposing regime, an RSI-smoothness alignment award, and stance age capped at its hold award, minus a capped penalty comprising raw-chop, stretched, early-flip and insufficient-neighbor penalties. The insufficient-neighbor penalty is proportional to (requested analog count - retained count) / requested analog count. Rank is zero for neutral bias.
Calculation: Confidence = for non-neutral bias, clamp the sum of agreement times its confidence weight, tightness times its confidence weight, capped normalized stance age times its confidence weight, and a slope-fit award, minus early-flip and insufficient-neighbor penalties; the latter is proportional to (requested analog count - retained count) / requested analog count. Confidence is zero for neutral bias.
Calculation: Signal cooldown = bars since the most recent qualifying long or short stance-change signal, initialized with no previous signal; update it on either qualifying signal, independently of trailing-trend markings.
Long: Trailing trend is bullish.
Short: Trailing trend is bearish.
Long: Stance changes to bullish AND rank is at least its threshold AND confidence is at least its threshold AND signal cooldown is at least its threshold or no previous qualifying signal exists.
Short: Stance changes to bearish AND rank is at least its threshold AND confidence is at least its threshold AND signal cooldown is at least its threshold or no previous qualifying signal exists.
Exit: long, trailing trend changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: short, trailing trend changes from bearish to bullish.
