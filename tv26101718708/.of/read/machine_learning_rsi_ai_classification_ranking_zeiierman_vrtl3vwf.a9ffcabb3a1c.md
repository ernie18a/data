Calculation: RSI feature vector = [RSI / 100, normalized RSI change over a lookback, normalized change in that RSI change, |RSI − 50| / 50, RSI percentile rank, normalized rolling RSI standard deviation, normalized fast RSI minus slow RSI, normalized EMA(RSI) − 50]; normalize each series to its rolling min–max range (use 0.5 when range is zero). Fast RSI uses a shorter length and slow RSI a longer length than the base RSI.
Calculation: Labeled analog = a stored feature vector labeled by the selected price source’s movement over a forward horizon: +3/+2/+1 for positive movement greater than two ATR thresholds / one ATR threshold / zero; −3/−2/−1 for negative movement beyond two thresholds / one threshold / zero; otherwise zero. The threshold is ATR at the sample bar times a sensitivity parameter. Store a sample only after its forward outcome is known.
Calculation: Analog score = distance-weighted mean label of the k nearest stored samples, using weighted distance equal to the sum across features of feature weight × log(1 + absolute feature difference), and each vote weight equal to 1 / (1 + distance). Consider only every configured spacing of stored samples. Bias is bullish when score exceeds a positive threshold, bearish when below its negative counterpart, otherwise neutral. Feature weights are either selected weights or adaptive weights: Fisher separation of positive-label and negative-label samples per feature, scaled to a shared range and smoothed over time.
Calculation: ML Supertrend direction = adaptive ATR trailing bands around the selected price source. Band width is base ATR multiplier × (1 + adaptivity × (1 − conviction drive)); conviction drive combines smoothed absolute analog conviction, neighbor-distance tightness, and analog agreement, and is reduced during chop. Trail the lower band upward while prior close is above its prior value and the upper band downward while prior close is below its prior value. Flip bearish to bullish when close exceeds the prior upper band; flip bullish to bearish when close falls below the prior lower band.
Calculation: Setup rank = clamp(25 × directional analog agreement + 15 × distance tightness + 10 if directional RSI slope fits + 5 if not stretched + 10 if Supertrend aligns + volatility points + regime points + 5 if smoothed RSI slope fits + min(5, stance age) − min(20, penalty), 0, 100). Directional RSI slope fits when bullish bias has rising RSI or bearish bias has non-rising RSI. Stretched means bullish bias with RSI above its upper threshold or bearish bias with RSI below its lower threshold. Volatility points are 10 inside the healthy ATR percentile band, 5 below it, otherwise 3. Regime points are 10 when EMA(RSI) is above its bullish threshold or below its bearish threshold, 4 when between neutral thresholds, otherwise 6. Penalty sums chop, stretch, early stance flip, and insufficient-neighbor penalties.
Calculation: Setup confidence = clamp(40 × directional analog agreement + 25 × distance tightness + 15 × min(1, stance age / persistence period) + 10 if directional RSI slope fits − early-flip penalty − insufficient-neighbor penalty, 0, 100).
Long: On a confirmed bar, stance changes to bullish, where stance is bullish when analog bias is bullish and all enabled gates pass (Supertrend bullish, ATR percentile inside its healthy band, and not choppy); setup rank and confidence meet their minimums; and the minimum interval since the prior signal has elapsed.
Short: On a confirmed bar, stance changes to bearish, where stance is bearish when analog bias is bearish and all enabled gates pass (Supertrend bearish, ATR percentile inside its healthy band, and not choppy); setup rank and confidence meet their minimums; and the minimum interval since the prior signal has elapsed.
Exit: long, close < prior lower Supertrend trailing band
Exit: short, close > prior upper Supertrend trailing band
