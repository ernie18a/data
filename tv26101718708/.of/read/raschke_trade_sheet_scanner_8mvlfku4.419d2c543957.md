Calculation: 3/10 fast line = 3-bar simple moving average of close minus 10-bar simple moving average of close, on the selected scan timeframe; if this differs from the chart timeframe, use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: 3/10 slow line = 16-bar simple moving average of the 3/10 fast line, on the selected scan timeframe; if this differs from the chart timeframe, use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: 2-bar rate of change = close minus close from 2 bars earlier, on the selected scan timeframe; if this differs from the chart timeframe, use the still-forming bar.
Long: 3/10 fast line > its prior value AND 3/10 slow line > its prior value AND 2-bar rate of change > its prior value OR close > 5-bar simple moving average AND at least 7 prior consecutive closes were not above their corresponding 5-bar simple moving averages OR 2-bar rate of change >= its highest value over the 30-bar lookback OR high >= its highest value over the 20-bar lookback
