Calculation: TRF = Average two ranges, each equal to EMA(EMA(abs(close − previous close), lookback), 2 × lookback − 1) × multiplier, using a longer lookback and multiplier for the slow range than for the fast range. Range-filter close recursively: if close is above the prior filter, use max(prior filter, close − average range); otherwise use min(prior filter, close + average range). Recursively maintain upper and lower bands at filter ± average range: upper band becomes filter + average range when that value is below its prior value or prior close is above its prior value; otherwise it carries forward. Lower band becomes filter − average range when that value is above its prior value or prior close is below its prior value; otherwise it carries forward. TRF is the upper band while the prior TRF equals the prior upper band and close is at or below the upper band; it switches to the lower band when close rises above the upper band. While the prior TRF equals the prior lower band, TRF is the lower band when close is at or above it, and switches to the upper band when close falls below it.
Calculation: Opening-range extensions = opening-range low + opening-range range × extension ratio (upper), and opening-range low + opening-range range × (1 − extension ratio) (lower); use only after the first 15 minutes of the session are complete.
Calculation: Previous-day extensions = previous-day low + previous-day range × extension ratio (upper), and previous-day low + previous-day range × (1 − extension ratio) (lower); use the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Upper extension = the greater of the previous-day upper extension and opening-range upper extension when the opening range is complete; otherwise use the previous-day upper extension. Lower extension = the lesser of the previous-day lower extension and opening-range lower extension when the opening range is complete; otherwise use the previous-day lower extension.
Long: Flat AND current chart bar is confirmed AND current close crosses above current TRF (prior close at or below prior TRF) AND VIX close is below its TRF on a higher-timeframe bar still forming AND instrument close is above its TRF on the previous completed higher-timeframe bar AND time is 9:30–11:59 ET.
Short: Flat AND current chart bar is confirmed AND current close crosses below current TRF (prior close at or above prior TRF) AND VIX close is above its TRF on a higher-timeframe bar still forming AND time is 9:30–11:59 ET.
Exit: Long, low ≤ current stop OR high ≥ take-profit OR time is 12:00 ET or later OR a new day begins; initial stop = entry price − 2.2 × current chart ATR, take-profit = entry price + 2 × initial stop distance, and after close ≥ upper extension set stop to entry price.
Exit: Short, high ≥ current stop OR low ≤ take-profit OR VIX close ≤ its TRF on a higher-timeframe bar still forming OR time is 12:00 ET or later OR a new day begins; initial stop = entry price + 2.2 × current chart ATR, take-profit = entry price − 2 × initial stop distance, and after close ≤ lower extension set stop to entry price.
