Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot, lower band and upper band = (high + low + close) / 3, (high + low) / 2, and twice the pivot minus the lower band, respectively, using the last completed bar of each of two higher timeframes; the first timeframe is shorter than the second. The bands are not reordered.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the channel index, where source is (high + low + close) / 3, channel index is (source - EMA(source)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(source - EMA(source)))), and both inner EMAs have the same channel length. Its signal is an SMA of WaveTrend; momentum is WaveTrend minus its signal.
Calculation: Bullish oscillator divergence = on the confirmation bar of an oscillator pivot low, the low at that pivot is below the low at the preceding confirmed oscillator pivot low AND the oscillator at that pivot is above its value at the preceding pivot. Bearish divergence uses pivot highs, a higher price high and a lower oscillator value. Use equal left and right pivot lookbacks; calculate separately for RSI and WaveTrend.
Calculation: Volume delta = cumulative volume on bars where close > open minus cumulative volume on bars where close < open. High volume and climax volume mean volume exceeds its SMA multiplied by their respective thresholds, with the climax threshold greater than the high-volume threshold.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = abs(close - open) > ATR × displacement threshold AND climax volume AND close > open. Bearish displacement uses close < open.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of the following points, with disabled optional components contributing zero: optional pivot component: 10 if close > both higher-timeframe pivots, 3 if close > the shorter-timeframe upper band, 2 if (upper band - lower band) / pivot is below a narrowness threshold; optional HMA component: 5 if close > middle-length HMA and that HMA is rising, 7 if all five close HMAs decrease strictly from shortest to longest length, 3 if shorter EMA > longer EMA; optional WaveTrend component: 8 if WaveTrend crosses above its signal while below an oversold threshold, otherwise 5 if it crosses above, plus 5 for bullish WaveTrend divergence and 2 if momentum > 0 and is rising; optional divergence component: 5 for bullish RSI divergence plus 5 if bullish RSI and WaveTrend divergences occur together; ADX component: 10 if positive directional index > negative directional index and ADX > its threshold, otherwise 5 if positive directional index > negative directional index; optional volume component: 7 if high volume, close > open and volume delta > its SMA, otherwise 5 if high volume and close > open, plus 3 if climax volume and close > open; optional market-structure component: 2 if low > high two bars ago, previous close > high two bars ago and (low - high two bars ago) / high two bars ago > gap threshold, 2 if the two preceding bars both close below their opens and the current bar closes above its open on high volume, 1 if close crosses above the previous bar's low of the most recent bearish order block, 2 if low < the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars and close > that lowest low on high volume, and 3 for bullish displacement; optional multi-timeframe component: 15 if close > HMA and ADX > its threshold on each of three timeframes, otherwise 10 if close > HMA on all three, otherwise 5 if close > HMA on the middle timeframe; moving-average component: 3 if close > shorter SMA, 4 if close > longer SMA and 3 if shorter SMA > longer SMA. A bearish order block is a bar closing below its open on high volume after two bars each closing above its open. For the multi-timeframe component use three successively longer timeframes, each higher than the chart timeframe, and the bar still forming in each.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of the corresponding bearish components with the same weights and optional switches: pivot component uses close < both pivots, close < the shorter-timeframe lower band, and the same narrowness test; HMA component uses close < middle-length HMA with that HMA falling, strictly increasing HMAs from shortest to longest, and shorter EMA < longer EMA; WaveTrend component uses a cross below its signal with WaveTrend above an overbought threshold, bearish WaveTrend divergence, and momentum < 0 and falling; divergence component uses bearish RSI and WaveTrend divergences; ADX component uses negative directional index > positive directional index; volume component uses close < open and volume delta < its SMA; market-structure component uses high < low two bars ago AND previous close < that low AND (low two bars ago - high) / high > gap threshold, two preceding bullish candles followed by a bearish candle on high volume, close crossing below the previous bar's high of the most recent bullish order block, high > the highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND close < that highest high on high volume, and bearish displacement. A bullish order block is a bar closing above its open on high volume after two bars each closing below its open. The multi-timeframe component uses close <= HMA on the same three forming higher-timeframe bars, with ADX above its threshold for the strongest tier. The moving-average component uses close < each SMA and shorter SMA < longer SMA.
Calculation: Strong long = bullish score >= strong threshold AND bearish score < opposing-score limit AND the optional session filter passes. Strong short reverses the scores. The session filter passes when disabled or the bar is inside either configured session.
Calculation: Ultra long = bullish score >= ultra threshold AND bearish score < ultra opposing-score limit AND (WaveTrend crosses above its signal OR bullish RSI divergence OR bullish displacement). Ultra short reverses direction and scores. The ultra threshold exceeds the strong threshold and the ultra opposing-score limit is lower than the strong opposing-score limit.
Calculation: Dynamic long stop = minimum of close - ATR × stop multiplier and lowest low over a swing lookback - ATR × swing offset. Dynamic short stop = maximum of close + ATR × stop multiplier and highest high over a swing lookback + ATR × swing offset. The stop multiplier is the base multiplier scaled down if either ultra condition holds, unchanged if either strong condition holds and neither ultra condition holds, and scaled up otherwise.
Calculation: Initial exit levels = at the entry signal bar, save the applicable dynamic stop and the signal close as entry price; initial target is entry price plus the entry-to-stop distance × reward multiplier for a long, or entry price minus that distance × reward multiplier for a short.
Calculation: Revised long exit levels = once per position, when the optional breakeven rule is enabled and close >= entry price + (entry price - current dynamic long stop) × trigger multiplier, save a stop at entry price + (entry price - current dynamic long stop) × breakeven offset and a target at entry price + (entry price - current dynamic long stop) × reward multiplier. Revised short levels use close <= entry price - (current dynamic short stop - entry price) × trigger multiplier, a stop at entry price - (current dynamic short stop - entry price) × breakeven offset, and a target at entry price - (current dynamic short stop - entry price) × reward multiplier. The saved revised levels replace the initial levels.
Long: Position side = flat AND bullish score >= minimum score AND session filter passes AND ((bullish score >= strong threshold AND bearish score < opposing-score limit) OR (bullish score >= medium threshold AND bullish score > bearish score + score margin)).
Short: Position side = flat AND bearish score >= minimum score AND session filter passes AND ((bearish score >= strong threshold AND bullish score < opposing-score limit) OR (bearish score >= medium threshold AND bearish score > bullish score + score margin)).
Long: Ultra long condition = true.
Short: Ultra short condition = true.
Long: Bullish score >= dashboard signal threshold.
Short: Bearish score >= dashboard signal threshold.
Long: Shorter close EMA > longer close EMA.
Short: Shorter close EMA <= longer close EMA.
Exit: long, low <= active saved stop OR high >= active saved target.
Exit: short, high >= active saved stop OR low <= active saved target.
