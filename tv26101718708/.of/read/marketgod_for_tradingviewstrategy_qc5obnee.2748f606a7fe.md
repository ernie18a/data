Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2; Heikin-Ashi high = max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); Heikin-Ashi low = min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: Rise(x, n) = x is greater than every one of its preceding n values; Fall(x, n) = x is less than every one of its preceding n values.
Calculation: KDJ = stochastic of Heikin-Ashi close within the rolling Heikin-Ashi high-low range, smoothed twice with Wilder moving averages to obtain K and D; J = 3 × K − 2 × D; KDJ midpoint = (K + D) / 2. Stochastic K = an SMA of a separately parameterized stochastic of Heikin-Ashi prices.
Calculation: Smoothed momentum RSI = RSI calculated from Wilder moving averages of max(Heikin-Ashi close − EMA(Heikin-Ashi close), 0) and max(EMA(Heikin-Ashi close) − Heikin-Ashi close, 0), returning 100 when the latter average is zero.
Calculation: Wave trend = EMA of (close − EMA(close)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(close − EMA(close)))); its signal = SMA of wave trend, using its own lookback.
Calculation: Ichimoku lines = rolling high-low midpoints over short, medium, and long lookbacks, respectively conversion, base, and long midpoint; undisplaced spans are (conversion + base) / 2 and long midpoint; displaced spans are those spans from a preceding displacement number of bars. Lagged price = Heikin-Ashi close from that many bars ago.
Calculation: Ichimoku bullish movement = conversion crosses above base OR Heikin-Ashi close crosses above base OR Heikin-Ashi close crosses above the higher displaced span while the spans differ OR the first undisplaced span crosses above the second OR Heikin-Ashi close > lagged price. Bearish movement reverses all crossings and comparisons, including crossing below the lower displaced span.
Calculation: Ichimoku readiness = the interpolated intersection price of the current and previous conversion and base lines lies at or below both displaced spans on both bars, OR lies at or above both spans on both bars, OR lies neither below nor above them; apply the same test to Heikin-Ashi close and base as an alternative; alternatively, lagged price is strictly above both current displaced spans or strictly below both. Undefined intersection prices do not satisfy their test.
Calculation: Two-bar Heikin-Ashi bullish = current and previous Heikin-Ashi close > their respective Heikin-Ashi open; two-bar bearish = current Heikin-Ashi close ≤ its open AND previous Heikin-Ashi close < its open.
Calculation: Version 1 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND J > KDJ midpoint AND conversion < Heikin-Ashi close AND Rise(stochastic K, 1) AND Rise(J, 2); version 1 sell = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND conversion > Heikin-Ashi close AND Fall(J, 1) AND J < KDJ midpoint.
Calculation: Version 2 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Rise(smoothed momentum RSI, 1) AND K < J AND Rise(close, 1) AND Heikin-Ashi high > SMA(Heikin-Ashi high); version 2 sell = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Fall(smoothed momentum RSI, 1) AND K > J AND Fall(close, 1) AND Heikin-Ashi low < SMA(Heikin-Ashi low).
Calculation: Version 3 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Rise(Schaff Trend Cycle of Heikin-Ashi close, 1) AND Rise(EMA(close), 1) AND Rise(CCI of Heikin-Ashi close, 2) AND wave trend > its signal; version 3 sell reverses these rising and greater-than comparisons. The Schaff Trend Cycle uses a faster and a slower EMA.
Calculation: Version 4 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Ichimoku bullish movement AND J > KDJ midpoint AND Heikin-Ashi close > Hull moving average of close AND two-bar Heikin-Ashi bullish; version 4 sell = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Ichimoku bearish movement AND J < KDJ midpoint AND Heikin-Ashi close < Hull moving average of close AND two-bar Heikin-Ashi bearish.
Calculation: Version 5 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Rise(Hull moving average of close, 1) AND Rise(smoothed momentum RSI, 1) AND Rise(Bollinger %B of Heikin-Ashi close, 1); version 5 sell replaces each Rise with Fall.
Calculation: Version 6 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Rise(KDJ D, 1) AND Rise(Bollinger %B of Heikin-Ashi close, 1) AND Rise(smoothed momentum RSI, 1) AND high > EMA(high); version 6 sell replaces Rise with Fall and requires low < EMA(low).
Calculation: Version 7 buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND open < close AND close > SMA(Heikin-Ashi high) AND J > stochastic K AND Rise(KDJ D, 1); version 7 sell = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND open > close AND close < SMA(Heikin-Ashi low) AND J < stochastic K AND Fall(KDJ D, 1).
Calculation: Version 8 buy = version 2 buy AND version 5 buy AND version 6 buy AND version 7 buy AND close > Heikin-Ashi close; version 8 sell = version 2 sell AND version 5 sell AND version 6 sell AND version 7 sell AND close < Heikin-Ashi close.
Calculation: Squared version buy = version 7 buy AND two-bar Heikin-Ashi bullish AND wave trend > its signal; squared version sell = version 7 sell AND two-bar Heikin-Ashi bearish AND wave trend < its signal.
Calculation: Ichimoku version buy = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Ichimoku bullish movement AND Ichimoku readiness AND Rise(KDJ D, 1) AND Rise(previous J, 2) AND two-bar Heikin-Ashi bullish AND version 7 buy AND Rise(KDJ D, 2) AND high equals its rolling highest high AND close is nonzero; Ichimoku version sell = previous Heikin-Ashi close is nonzero AND Ichimoku bearish movement AND Ichimoku readiness AND Fall(KDJ D, 1) AND Fall(previous J, 2) AND two-bar Heikin-Ashi bearish AND version 7 sell AND Fall(KDJ D, 2) AND low equals its rolling lowest low AND close is nonzero.
Calculation: Selected buy and selected sell = the corresponding conditions of one selected version. Signal state starts at 0; on each bar set it to 1 if selected buy is true, otherwise to 0 if selected sell is true, otherwise retain its previous value. Buy takes precedence when both are true.
Long: Selected buy AND previous signal state = 0.
Short: Selected sell AND previous signal state = 1.
Exit: long, selected sell AND previous signal state = 1 AND datetime is within the configured trading window.
Exit: short, selected buy AND previous signal state = 0 AND datetime is within the configured trading window.
Exit: long, optional stop-loss enabled AND low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR optional take-profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction).
Exit: short, optional stop-loss enabled AND high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR optional take-profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction).
