Calculation: SRZones = price bands [bottom, top] built from pivot highs/lows (pivot = high above, or low below, the highs/lows of an equal number of bars on each side; confirmed only after those right-side bars close; only pivots within a limited lookback are kept). Band height limit = ATR x multiplier. Clustering: starting from each pivot, grow a band [bottom, top] by adding any pivot whose inclusion keeps band height <= the limit. Cluster strength = 20 x number of pivots in the band + number of lookback bars whose high or low lies inside the band. Keep clusters with strength >= a minimum; pick the strongest first, skip clusters overlapping an already-picked one, up to a maximum number of zones. Zones are recomputed only on bars where a new pivot is confirmed and otherwise persist. A zone is permanently removed (and never re-added) when the previous close was entirely above it and close < bottom, or the previous close was entirely below it and close > top.
Calculation: SupportTouch = top of an existing zone where close > top AND low <= top AND low >= bottom (price above the zone, wick into it); na if none.
Calculation: ResistTouch = bottom of an existing zone where close < bottom AND high >= bottom AND high <= top (price below the zone, wick into it); na if none.
Calculation: DXYBias(tf) = close minus SMA(close) of the dollar index on higher timeframe tf (current, still-forming bar of tf, as of the current chart bar); same SMA length for every tf. Needs valid data.
Long: SupportTouch on the previous bar is not na (call it T) AND close[1] <= T AND close > T AND DXYBias < 0 on both the longer higher timeframe and the shorter higher timeframe
Short: ResistTouch on the previous bar is not na (call it B) AND close[1] >= B AND close < B AND DXYBias > 0 on both the longer higher timeframe and the shorter higher timeframe
