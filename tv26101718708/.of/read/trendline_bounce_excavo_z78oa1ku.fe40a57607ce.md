Calculation: Confirmed swing highs/lows = chart-timeframe bars whose high/low is the extreme across a longer lookback on each side; confirm after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Fast swing highs/lows = confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs/lows using a shorter lookback than the quality pivots.
Calculation: Valid resistance/support trendlines = fit straight lines to quality swing highs/lows; choose the slope with the most pivots within an ATR-scaled tolerance, requiring at least the minimum touches. Reject lines whose slope exceeds an ATR-scaled limit, whose span is too short, whose intervening highs/lows cross the line beyond tolerance, or whose latest touch is too far from close. Retain the most recent qualifying lines per side.
Long: (the latest quality swing low touches a valid support trendline OR the latest fast swing low touches a valid support trendline OR low is within the tolerance band of a valid support trendline projected to the current bar)
Short: (the latest quality swing high touches a valid resistance trendline OR the latest fast swing high touches a valid resistance trendline OR high is within the tolerance band of a valid resistance trendline projected to the current bar)
