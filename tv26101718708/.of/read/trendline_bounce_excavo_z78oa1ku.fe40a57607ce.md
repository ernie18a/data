Calculation: Quality pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same lookback on each side; retain a rolling maximum number of pivots separately for highs and lows.
Calculation: Trendlines = recompute separately for support and resistance on each bar using current ATR and tolerance = ATR multiplied by a tolerance parameter. Visit quality pivots as anchors from newest to oldest, skipping anchors already used by an accepted line and stopping at a maximum number of lines per side. For each anchor, visit other pivots from oldest to newest and calculate the line through the pair. Reject slopes whose absolute value exceeds an ATR-based slope limit. Count pivots within tolerance of the line; require at least the minimum touch count and require every stored high pivot to be at or below the resistance line plus tolerance, or every stored low pivot to be at or above the support line minus tolerance. Choose the qualifying slope with the greatest touch count, retaining the first in a tie. Reject that chosen line if its earliest and latest touches are less than the minimum span apart, if the latest touch price is farther than an ATR-based distance limit from current close, or if a sampled high between those touches exceeds the resistance line plus tolerance or a sampled low falls below the support line minus tolerance. Sample from the earliest touch at integer intervals equal to max(1, floor(span / sample-count parameter)), stopping at the latest touch or after sample-count parameter plus one samples. Do not try the next-best slope if the chosen line fails these checks. Mark all touches of each accepted line as used before considering the next anchor.
Calculation: Fast pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same shorter lookback on each side than quality pivots.
Long: An accepted support trendline has at least the minimum touch count; the decision is marked at its latest quality-pivot touch and can be identified only when the line is computed.
Short: An accepted resistance trendline has at least the minimum touch count; the decision is marked at its latest quality-pivot touch and can be identified only when the line is computed.
Long: An accepted support trendline exists AND (its latest quality-pivot touch is on the newly confirmed quality-pivot bar OR the newly confirmed fast pivot low is within current tolerance of the line projected to that pivot bar OR current low is within current tolerance of the line projected to the current bar) AND (the optional trend filter is disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (the optional volatility filter is disabled OR ATR percentile rank over its lookback < its threshold).
Short: An accepted resistance trendline exists AND (its latest quality-pivot touch is on the newly confirmed quality-pivot bar OR the newly confirmed fast pivot high is within current tolerance of the line projected to that pivot bar OR current high is within current tolerance of the line projected to the current bar) AND (the optional trend filter is disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (the optional volatility filter is disabled OR ATR percentile rank over its lookback < its threshold).
