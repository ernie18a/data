Calculation: A bullish zone is created when the previous close < previous open AND the current close > current open AND the current absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by a body-size threshold. A bearish zone is created when the previous close > previous open AND the current close < current open AND the current absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by the same threshold. Its initial top and bottom are the previous high and low, its birth bar is the current bar, its volume score is previous volume divided by the current volume SMA, or 1 if that SMA is zero, and its trend score is 1 if a bullish zone has current close > current close EMA or a bearish zone has current close < current close EMA, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Maintain zones in descending quality order. On creation, optionally merge the new zone into the first existing zone of the same direction whose overlap is positive and at least half the smaller zone's height, provided the combined height <= current ATR multiplied by a merge-size threshold. A merge takes the union of the bounds, the maximum of each zone's volume and trend scores, resets mitigation to 0, and retains the existing birth bar. Otherwise add the new zone. Then, for every zone, if a bullish zone has low <= top, set mitigation to clamp((top - low) / height, 0, 1); if a bearish zone has high >= bottom, set mitigation to clamp((high - bottom) / height, 0, 1). Otherwise retain its previous mitigation. Remove zones with mitigation >= 1, rank the remainder by quality = height * 100 + volume score * 10 + trend score * 20 - mitigation * 50 - age in bars * 0.1, and discard the lowest-ranked zones beyond the storage limit. Evaluate touches before removing fully mitigated zones and before enforcing the storage limit.

Short: Previous close > previous open AND current close < current open AND current absolute candle body >= ATR multiplied by the same body-size threshold.
Long: Current low <= top of any stored bullish zone after creation or merging and before mitigation removal.
Short: Current high >= bottom of any stored bearish zone after creation or merging and before mitigation removal.
