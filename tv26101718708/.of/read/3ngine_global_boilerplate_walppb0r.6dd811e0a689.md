Calculation: Long run-up = highest high from the locked ladder-start bar through the current bar.
Calculation: Short run-down = lowest low from the locked ladder-start bar through the current bar.
Long: Ladder start price > fixed stop price AND bar datetime > start datetime AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session in GMT-6) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX is between its configured bounds, inclusive) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close > selected moving average of the selected source on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND position side is not short AND no trade has previously closed AND low <= the next unfilled long entry rung, whose price starts at the ladder start price and decreases by the configured percentage of that price per rung.
Short: Ladder start price <= fixed stop price AND bar datetime > start datetime AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session in GMT-6) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX is between its configured bounds, inclusive) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close > selected moving average of the selected source on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND position side is not long AND no trade has previously closed AND high >= the next unfilled short entry rung, whose price starts at the ladder start price and increases by the configured percentage of that price per rung.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop price AND long run-up <= average entry price * (1 + trailing activation percentage) OR low <= long run-up * (1 - trailing distance percentage) AND long run-up > average entry price * (1 + trailing activation percentage) OR high >= an unfilled profit rung starting at average entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage) and increasing by the configured rung spacing percentage of that initial target per rung.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop price AND short run-down >= average entry price * (1 - trailing activation percentage) OR high >= short run-down * (1 + trailing distance percentage) AND short run-down < average entry price * (1 - trailing activation percentage) OR low <= an unfilled profit rung starting at average entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) and decreasing by the configured rung spacing percentage of that initial target per rung.
