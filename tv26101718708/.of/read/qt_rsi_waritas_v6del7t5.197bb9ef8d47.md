Calculation: Conventions = Use the selected OHLC-derived source, defaulting to close. All calculations use chart-timeframe bars. Parameter symbols denote configurable values; distinct symbols may differ. Clamp missing values to the lower bound. Normalize(x, L, g) = clamp to [0,1] of sigmoid(g × (x − EMA(x,L)) / max(standard deviation(x,L), epsilon)); replace missing x with zero. P(x,p) is the linearly interpolated rolling percentile of x over the union lookback. A cross above means current value > current boundary AND previous value <= previous boundary; a cross below reverses both inequalities.
Calculation: Market features = Let r be the change in log source. Apply a scalar Kalman filter to source with a random-walk prediction, fixed process and measurement noise, initial state equal to source, and initial error variance one. Kalman lock K = 1 − Normalize(abs(Kalman innovation / source), model memory, gain). Volatility transition M = SMA of the absolute change in the binary state defined by rolling standard deviation of r > its longer-lookback rolling median. Wave energy W = Normalize((log(source) − 2 × log(source lagged by a medium interval) + log(source lagged by twice that interval))², model memory, gain). Hawkes pressure H = Normalize(h, model memory, gain), where h = decay × previous h + baseline + previous jump, and jump = abs(r) / rolling standard deviation(r) when abs(r) > a multiple of that standard deviation, otherwise zero. Spectral purity S = Normalize(maximum absolute difference between adjacent short, medium, long, and longer SMAs of r, divided by a still longer rolling standard deviation of r, model memory, gain). Long memory D = Normalize(rolling standard deviation of the residual from a rolling linear regression of cumulative (r − rolling SMA(r)), divided by rolling standard deviation of r, model memory, gain). Mass A = Normalize(abs(r) × (1 + coefficient × raw wave energy), model memory, gain). Spin B = tanh(sign(r) × min(h, cap) × coefficient). Charge C = tanh(coefficient × rolling skewness of r). Lifetime T = Normalize(1 / rolling standard deviation of r, model memory, gain). Use positive denominator floors throughout.
Calculation: Model components = Let V be a rolling standard deviation of r. Drift component d = tanh(coefficient × EMA(r, drift length) / V) × D. Kalman component k = tanh(coefficient × change in Kalman state over a short lag / (source × V)) × K × S. Wave component w = tanh(coefficient × change in log source over a short lag / V) × W × S × (1 − coefficient × H). RSI component u = tanh((RSI(source) − midpoint) / scale) × (base + coefficient × S). Spin component b = B × A × (base + coefficient × T). Charge component c = C × (1 − H) × (base + coefficient × S). Shock risk R = clamp to [0,1] of a weighted sum of H, M, and (1 − T). Confidence F = clamp to [0,1] of a weighted sum of K, S, D, A, and T minus a weighted R. Ensemble signal Q = tanh(gain × weighted mean of b, d, w, k, c, u) × (base + coefficient × F). Stack Z = tanh(gain × (b + d + w + k)). Coordination J = clamp to [0,1] of abs(b + d + w + k) / max(abs(b) + abs(d) + abs(w) + abs(k), epsilon); dispersion E = 1 − J. Quality G = clamp to [0,1] of a weighted sum of J, S, D, and T.
Calculation: Signal threshold = Either a fixed floor or the greater of that floor and a rolling percentile of abs(Q) over model memory, clamped to configured bounds; use the floor until the percentile is available.
Calculation: Direction-aligned series = Multiply each historical observation of Q, d, k, b, c, and w by the sign of Q on that observation, treating zero as positive. Denote the resulting series by Q*, d*, k*, b*, c*, and w*.
Calculation: Union alternatives = (k > P(k, high) AND d > P(d, moderately high) AND G > P(G, moderately high)) OR (d > P(d, high) AND b > P(b, upper) AND A > P(A, above median)) OR (k > P(k, upper) AND K < P(K, very low) AND b* > P(b*, low)) OR (Q* > P(Q*, median) AND K < P(K, lower) AND G > P(G, moderately high)) OR (k* > P(k*, below median) AND D > P(D, high) AND b > P(b, high)) OR (d* > P(d*, upper) AND K < P(K, lower) AND b > P(b, low)) OR (k* > P(k*, lower middle) AND D > P(D, high) AND b* > P(b*, high)) OR (Q* > P(Q*, above median) AND S > P(S, moderately high) AND R < P(R, median)) OR (G > P(G, upper) AND J > P(J, above median) AND E < P(E, below median) AND T > P(T, above median)) OR (w* > P(w*, above median) AND W > P(W, above median) AND H < P(H, median) AND k* > P(k*, above median)) OR (c* > P(c*, above median) AND D > P(D, moderately high) AND F > P(F, moderately high) AND R < P(R, below median)) OR (d* > P(d*, moderately high) AND b* > P(b*, moderately high) AND W > P(W, moderately high) AND S > P(S, moderately high)). Percentile ranks are ordered very low < lower < low < lower middle < below median < median < above median < moderately high < upper < high.
Long: Bar index > both model memory and union lookback AND F >= minimum confidence AND R <= maximum shock risk AND E <= maximum dispersion AND J >= minimum coordination AND (union filter disabled OR union alternatives) AND (Q crosses above signal threshold OR (continuation enabled AND Q > signal threshold AND Z crosses above zero)).
Short: Bar index > both model memory and union lookback AND F >= minimum confidence AND R <= maximum shock risk AND E <= maximum dispersion AND J >= minimum coordination AND (union filter disabled OR union alternatives) AND (Q crosses below negative signal threshold OR (continuation enabled AND Q < negative signal threshold AND Z crosses below zero)).
