Calculation: Rolling normalized value(x, n, g) = clamp01(sigmoid((x − EMA(x,n) / stdev(x,n)) × g))
Calculation: Log return = ln(close / prior close); replace close values used as denominators with a small positive value
Calculation: Kalman estimate = recursive scalar filter of close, process variance 0.0001, measurement variance 0.01, initial estimate=close and initial variance=1; innovation=close−prior estimate; gain=prior variance/(prior variance+0.01); estimate=prior estimate+gain×innovation; variance=(1−gain)×(prior variance+0.0001)
Calculation: Kalman lock = 1 − rolling normalized value(abs(innovation/close), model lookback, 1)
Calculation: Markov transition rate = SMA(50, abs(current volatility state−prior state)); volatility state is 1 when 100-bar log-return standard deviation exceeds its rolling 500-bar median, otherwise 0
Calculation: Wavelet energies = squared differences between consecutive log-price changes at spans 2, 4, and 8 bars, using log(close)−log(close[n]) for each span; wavelet energy = rolling normalized value of the span-4 energy using model lookback and gain 1
Calculation: Hawkes pressure = rolling normalized value of a recursive state using model lookback and gain 1; state = 0.90×prior state + 0.01 + prior jump, where jump=abs(log return)/stdev(log return,50) if abs(log return)>1.5×that standard deviation, otherwise 0
Calculation: Spectral purity = rolling normalized value of peak/stdev(log return,128), using model lookback and gain 1; peak=max(abs(SMA(log return,8)−SMA(16)), abs(SMA(16)−SMA(32)), abs(SMA(32)−SMA(64)))
Calculation: Long-memory strength = rolling normalized value of stdev(residual,64)/stdev(log return,64), using model lookback and gain 1; residual=cumulative sum of (log return−SMA(log return,64)) minus its 64-bar linear regression
Calculation: Sub-mass = rolling normalized value(abs(log return)×(1+0.5×span-4 wavelet energy), model lookback,1); sub-spin=tanh(sign(log return)×min(Hawkes state,5)×0.35); sub-lifetime=rolling normalized value(1/max(stdev(log return,20), small positive value), model lookback,1)
Calculation: Drift model = tanh(4×EMA(log return, trend length)/stdev(log return,64))×long-memory strength
Calculation: Kalman model = tanh(2×(Kalman estimate−its value 3 bars ago)/(close×stdev(log return,64)))×Kalman lock×spectral purity
Calculation: Wave model = tanh(0.125×(log(close)−log(close 4 bars ago))/stdev(log return,64))×wavelet energy×spectral purity×(1−0.35×Hawkes pressure)
Calculation: RSI model = tanh((RSI(close, RSI length)−50)/18)×(0.35+0.65×spectral purity)
Calculation: Spin model = sub-spin×sub-mass×(0.50+0.50×sub-lifetime)
Calculation: Charge model = tanh(0.25×SMA((log return−SMA(log return,50))³,50)/stdev(log return,50)³)×(1−Hawkes pressure)×(0.50+0.50×spectral purity)
Calculation: Shock risk = clamp01(0.55×Hawkes pressure+0.35×Markov transition rate+0.10×(1−sub-lifetime))
Calculation: Model confidence = clamp01(0.30×Kalman lock+0.24×spectral purity+0.22×long-memory strength+0.14×sub-mass+0.10×sub-lifetime−0.25×shock risk)
Calculation: Composite signal = tanh(2.20×weighted mean of spin, drift, wave, Kalman, charge, and RSI models)×(0.35+0.65×model confidence); weights are their respective configurable weights
Calculation: Signal threshold = max(configurable floor, rolling 78th percentile of absolute composite signal over model lookback) when adaptive threshold is enabled; otherwise the floor
Calculation: Coordination = clamp01(abs(spin+drift+wave+Kalman models)/max(sum of their absolute values, small positive value)); dispersion=1−coordination
Calculation: Manifold quality = clamp01(0.35×coordination+0.25×spectral purity+0.20×long-memory strength+0.20×sub-lifetime)
Calculation: Signal stack = tanh(0.65×(spin+drift+wave+Kalman models))
Calculation: Direction-aligned value = value×sign(composite signal), applied to the composite signal and each model; rolling percentiles below use the union lookback and include the current bar
Calculation: Directional union = OR of these 12 conditions: (Kalman model>its 80th percentile AND drift model>its 70th percentile AND manifold quality>its 70th percentile); (drift model>its 80th percentile AND spin model>its 75th percentile AND sub-mass>its 60th percentile); (Kalman model>its 75th percentile AND Kalman lock<its 5th percentile AND direction-aligned spin model>its 25th percentile); (direction-aligned composite signal>its 50th percentile AND Kalman lock<its 20th percentile AND manifold quality>its 70th percentile); (direction-aligned Kalman model>its 40th percentile AND long-memory strength>its 80th percentile AND spin model>its 80th percentile); (direction-aligned drift model>its 75th percentile AND Kalman lock<its 20th percentile AND spin model>its 25th percentile); (direction-aligned Kalman model>its 30th percentile AND long-memory strength>its 80th percentile AND direction-aligned spin model>its 80th percentile); (direction-aligned composite signal>its 60th percentile AND spectral purity>its 70th percentile AND shock risk<its 50th percentile); (manifold quality>its 75th percentile AND coordination>its 60th percentile AND dispersion<its 40th percentile AND sub-lifetime>its 60th percentile); (direction-aligned wave model>its 60th percentile AND wavelet energy>its 60th percentile AND Hawkes pressure<its 50th percentile AND direction-aligned Kalman model>its 60th percentile); (direction-aligned charge model>its 60th percentile AND long-memory strength>its 70th percentile AND model confidence>its 70th percentile AND shock risk<its 40th percentile); (direction-aligned drift model>its 70th percentile AND direction-aligned spin model>its 70th percentile AND wavelet energy>its 70th percentile AND spectral purity>its 70th percentile)
Long: bar index>max(model lookback, union lookback) AND model confidence≥minimum confidence AND shock risk≤maximum shock risk AND dispersion≤maximum dispersion AND coordination≥minimum coordination AND directional union passes (when union filter is enabled) AND (composite signal crosses above signal threshold OR (continuation enabled AND composite signal>signal threshold AND signal stack crosses above zero))
Short: bar index>max(model lookback, union lookback) AND model confidence≥minimum confidence AND shock risk≤maximum shock risk AND dispersion≤maximum dispersion AND coordination≥minimum coordination AND directional union passes (when union filter is enabled) AND (composite signal crosses below negative signal threshold OR (continuation enabled AND composite signal<negative signal threshold AND signal stack crosses below zero))
