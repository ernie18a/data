Calculation: Confirmed support pair = when a chart-timeframe low pivot is confirmed using left and right lookbacks, set the solid level to the pivot low and the dashed level to the following bar’s low, provided that low is at least the pivot low; if the levels are within the same-level proximity threshold, use the lower of the following bar’s open and close as the dashed level.
Calculation: Confirmed resistance pair = when a chart-timeframe high pivot is confirmed using left and right lookbacks, set the solid level to the pivot high and the dashed level to the following bar’s high, provided that high is at most the pivot high; if the levels are within the same-level proximity threshold, use the higher of the following bar’s open and close as the dashed level.
Calculation: Active pivot pairs = confirmed pairs not duplicated within the same-level proximity threshold of either level of an active pair of the same side; remove a support pair when close falls below its solid level and a resistance pair when close rises above its solid level. Evaluate the newest active pair, the nearest other active resistance pair, and the nearest other active support pair, with nearest measured by distance from close to the solid level.
Calculation: Pending support retest = on an evaluated support pair, low < solid level AND close > dashed level AND close <= open; retain that dashed level for the next bar only.
Calculation: Pending resistance retest = on an evaluated resistance pair, high > solid level AND close < dashed level AND close >= open; retain that dashed level for the next bar only.
Calculation: Dashed-level sweep count = for each active pair, count tests since the first test in a 10-bar window, resetting to one when there is no first test or the current bar is more than 10 bars after it; a support test is low < dashed level AND close >= dashed level, and a resistance test is high > dashed level AND close <= dashed level. A solid-level sweep takes precedence over a dashed-level test on the same bar.
Long: On any evaluated support pair, low < solid level AND close > open OR (on the next bar after a pending support retest) close > pending dashed level AND close > open OR (on the third counted dashed-level support test) abs(close - open) >= (high - low) * body-size threshold AND close > open
Short: On any evaluated resistance pair, high > solid level AND close < open OR (on the next bar after a pending resistance retest) close < pending dashed level AND close < open OR (on the third counted dashed-level resistance test) abs(close - open) >= (high - low) * body-size threshold AND close < open
