Calculation: Zone levels = At each new higher-timeframe bar, collect the log return between the last completed higher-timeframe close and its preceding close, retaining a rolling lookback. Once the minimum return count is available, anchor the levels to the last completed higher-timeframe close and hold them until the next update. In direct mode, each level is anchor × exp(the corresponding nearest-rank-by-floor percentile of returns × forecast horizon). In Monte Carlo mode, simulate price paths from the anchor for forecast horizon minus one steps, multiplying at each step by exp(mean return + population standard deviation of returns × z), where z is generated by the Box–Muller transform from independent uniform random draws over the configured interval; take the corresponding nearest-rank-by-floor percentile of terminal prices. The buy level uses the lower percentile and the sell level the upper percentile. For either percentile, sort ascending and use zero-based index floor(percentile × (count − 1)).
Calculation: ATR ratio = shorter-lookback ATR divided by longer-lookback ATR; use a neutral fallback value when the longer-lookback ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Long: close < buy level
Short: close > sell level
Long: close < buy level AND previous bar was not below its buy level
Short: close > sell level AND previous bar was not above its sell level
Long: close < buy level AND close < open of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND ATR ratio >= threshold AND New York local bar-open time is within an enabled London or New York session
Short: close > sell level AND close > open of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND ATR ratio >= threshold AND New York local bar-open time is within an enabled London or New York session
