Calculation: Band = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3 plus or minus ATR times a band-width parameter; use true range until ATR is available and apply a positive floor to ATR.
Calculation: Acceptance count for each side = consecutive closes strictly above the upper band or strictly below the lower band, respectively. The required count, failure distance, minimum body share, and minimum close location are sensitivity parameters.
Calculation: Normalize(x, weak, strong) = clamp((x - weak) / (strong - weak), 0, 1). Candle range = high - low with a positive floor; body share = abs(close - open) / candle range; close location = (close - low) / candle range; volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: For direction d, where d = 1 for long and -1 for short, prior acceptance = previous bar's lower acceptance count for long or upper acceptance count for short; failed edge = lower band for long or upper band for short; opposite edge = upper band for long or lower band for short; failure distance = d * (close - failed edge) / ATR; directional close location = close location for long or 1 - close location for short; invalidation = low - ATR times an invalidation buffer for long or high + ATR times that buffer for short; target = opposite edge if d * (opposite edge - close) > 0, otherwise close + d * ATR times a fallback distance; room = d * (target - close) / ATR; risk = d * (close - invalidation) / ATR.
Calculation: Reversal score = weighted sum of normalized prior acceptance, failure distance, body share, volume ratio, room, and risk, plus a directional candle score. Normalize prior acceptance from the required count to that count plus an acceptance-span parameter; failure distance from the required failure distance to a strong-failure parameter; body share from a fraction of the minimum body share to a strong-body parameter; volume ratio and room from their respective weak to strong parameters. Normalize risk upward from a low-risk parameter to an ideal-risk parameter and downward from ideal risk to a high-risk parameter. The directional candle score has a high value when d * (close - open) > 0, an intermediate value otherwise when d * (close - previous close) > 0, and a low value otherwise. All normalization bounds, score values, and weights are parameters.
Calculation: Watch score = weighted sum of normalized prior acceptance, normalized volume ratio, and normalized abs(close - failed edge) / ATR; normalize the distance from zero to a strong-distance parameter. Use the same acceptance and volume normalizations as the reversal score; weights and threshold are parameters.
Calculation: Active plan = the most recent reversal-ready event's direction, invalidation, and target, retained through a parameterized number of bars unless replaced by a later reversal-ready event; invalidation ends the plan after the current bar, and target review is available only until first touched.
Long: Previous lower acceptance count >= required count AND ((strict confirmation AND close > band midpoint AND volume ratio >= strict-volume threshold) OR (volume confirmation AND close > lower band + ATR times required failure distance AND volume ratio >= volume threshold) OR (other confirmation AND close > lower band + ATR times required failure distance)) AND close location >= minimum close location AND body share >= minimum body share times a body-share fraction AND long reversal score >= reversal-score threshold.
Short: Previous upper acceptance count >= required count AND ((strict confirmation AND close < band midpoint AND volume ratio >= strict-volume threshold) OR (volume confirmation AND close < upper band - ATR times required failure distance AND volume ratio >= volume threshold) OR (other confirmation AND close < upper band - ATR times required failure distance)) AND close location <= 1 - minimum close location AND body share >= minimum body share times a body-share fraction AND short reversal score >= reversal-score threshold.
Long: Lower acceptance count = required count AND long watch score >= watch-score threshold.
Short: Upper acceptance count = required count AND short watch score >= watch-score threshold.
Exit: long, active plan direction = long AND close < active invalidation.
Exit: short, active plan direction = short AND close > active invalidation.
Exit: long, active plan direction = long AND target has not previously been touched AND high >= active target.
Exit: short, active plan direction = short AND target has not previously been touched AND low <= active target.
