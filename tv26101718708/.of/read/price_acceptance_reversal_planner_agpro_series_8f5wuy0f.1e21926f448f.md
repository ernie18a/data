Calculation: Acceptance band = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3 ± ATR × band-width parameter.
Calculation: Upper/lower acceptance count = consecutive completed chart bars whose close is above the band high / below the band low; reset to zero otherwise.
Calculation: Normalized higher-is-better score = clamp((value − weak threshold) / (strong threshold − weak threshold) × 100, 0, 100).
Calculation: Reversal score = clamp(18% × acceptance-duration score + 22% × close-back distance score + 13% × candle-body score + 12% × direction score + 10% × relative-volume score + 15% × target-room score + 10% × risk-distance score, 0, 100).
Long: prior lower-acceptance count ≥ effective minimum acceptance bars AND (close > band midpoint in strict confirmation mode, otherwise close > band low + ATR × effective failure distance) AND close location within candle range ≥ effective close-location threshold AND candle body / (high − low) ≥ 70% × effective minimum body share AND (relative volume ≥ 1.0 in close-and-volume mode, ≥ 1.10 in strict mode, otherwise true) AND reversal score ≥ minimum reversal score AND bar is confirmed unless realtime confirmation mode is enabled.
Short: prior upper-acceptance count ≥ effective minimum acceptance bars AND (close < band midpoint in strict confirmation mode, otherwise close < band high − ATR × effective failure distance) AND close location within candle range ≤ 1 − effective close-location threshold AND candle body / (high − low) ≥ 70% × effective minimum body share AND (relative volume ≥ 1.0 in close-and-volume mode, ≥ 1.10 in strict mode, otherwise true) AND reversal score ≥ minimum reversal score AND bar is confirmed unless realtime confirmation mode is enabled.
Exit: long, while its reversal plan is active, close < that plan's signal-bar low − ATR × invalidation buffer.
Exit: short, while its reversal plan is active, close > that plan's signal-bar high + ATR × invalidation buffer.
