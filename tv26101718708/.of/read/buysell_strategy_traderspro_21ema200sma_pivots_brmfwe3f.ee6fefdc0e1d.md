Calculation: Shorter higher-timeframe pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe
Calculation: Longer higher-timeframe pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe
Long: close > EMA(close) AND close > SMA(close) AND close > shorter higher-timeframe pivot AND close > longer higher-timeframe pivot
Short: close < EMA(close) AND close < SMA(close) AND close < shorter higher-timeframe pivot AND close < longer higher-timeframe pivot
