Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a swing high or low with the same number of preceding and subsequent comparison bars; it becomes available only on the bar that confirms it.
Calculation: Watched swing = the latest confirmed wick pivot of its side; replace it when a newer pivot is confirmed, and expire it when its age exceeds the maximum swing age. A swing is consumed after its first qualifying break.
Calculation: Microstructure = a state updated on each completed lower-timeframe analysis bar, or each completed chart bar if the analysis timeframe equals the chart timeframe. Reset the state and pivot history after a data gap exceeding the gap limit; exclude pivots spanning that gap. Track the latest two confirmed wick highs and lows. From mixed, enter rising if both latest pivots are higher than their predecessors or close exceeds the latest high plus a price buffer; otherwise enter falling if both are lower or close is below the latest low minus the buffer. From falling, a close above the latest high plus the buffer enters reclaim up, with guard equal to the lower of the latest low and current low; from rising, a close below the latest low minus the buffer enters reclaim down, with guard equal to the higher of the latest high and current high. A reclaim expires after its maximum age or fails if close crosses its guard against the reclaim direction by more than the buffer; either event changes the state to falling for reclaim up or rising for reclaim down. During a reclaim, the first confirmed swing in the reclaim direction whose pivot bar is after the reclaim start is the continuation trigger. A subsequent confirmed opposite swing is the retest if it is beyond the guard in the reclaim direction by more than the buffer; a later wick through the retest cancels it. With a valid retest, a close beyond the trigger in the reclaim direction by more than the buffer changes reclaim up to recovery or reclaim down to decline, and sets the guard to the retest. Recovery or decline fails into falling or rising respectively when close crosses the guard against its direction by more than the buffer; otherwise, raise the recovery guard to any higher confirmed swing low or lower the decline guard to any lower confirmed swing high. Use the last completed analysis bar and require its data to be recent.

Long: Current low minus the high two bars earlier >= the larger of a minimum price gap and previous-bar ATR multiplied by a gap threshold AND, if gap continuity is required, the three bars are consecutive intraday bars on the same exchange date.
Short: The low two bars earlier minus current high >= the larger of a minimum price gap and previous-bar ATR multiplied by a gap threshold AND, if gap continuity is required, the three bars are consecutive intraday bars on the same exchange date.
Long: Close > unconsumed watched swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high.
Short: Close < unconsumed watched swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND no upward swing break occurs on the same bar.
Long: Microstructure = rising OR reclaim up OR recovery.
Short: Microstructure = falling OR reclaim down OR decline.
Long: A wick pivot is confirmed AND pivot-bar volume divided by its volume SMA ending on the pivot bar >= the elevated-volume threshold AND the pivot bar's close position within its high-low range > the midpoint plus the neutral half-band.
Short: A wick pivot is confirmed AND pivot-bar volume divided by its volume SMA ending on the pivot bar >= the elevated-volume threshold AND the pivot bar's close position within its high-low range < the midpoint minus the neutral half-band.
