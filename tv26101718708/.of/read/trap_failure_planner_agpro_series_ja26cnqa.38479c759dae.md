Calculation: Upper/lower rails = highest high / lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar; valid only when upper > lower.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; relative volume = current volume / its moving average.
Calculation: Trap thesis = valid rails and (upper: high > upper rail + minimum probe ATR × ATR, lower: low < lower rail − minimum probe ATR × ATR), with the close between the probed rail and the midpoint of the rails. If both occur, select the larger probe in ATR units; ties select upper. When no thesis is active and its side is allowed, store its side, rail edge, probe size, zone, and rail range. Keep it active through the failure window; then expire it.
Calculation: Return closes = consecutive confirmed closes beyond the stored edge by the control-return ATR buffer, in the direction opposite the trap thesis; reset to zero on any other close.
Calculation: Failure score = clamp to 0–100 the weighted sum of probe quality (18%), close recovery (22%), follow-through (18%), relative-volume spike (14%), level acceptance (18%), and return-close persistence (10%). Probe quality peaks at the ideal probe size and declines toward the minimum and large-probe limits; close recovery is normalized move beyond edge / zone height; follow-through is normalized move beyond edge / ATR; volume spike is normalized relative volume; level acceptance, only when beyond the buffered edge, is 45% directional close location within the bar, 35% return-close persistence, and 20% follow-through. Normalize close recovery from 0.05 to 1.10, follow-through from 0.05 to 0.80, volume spike from 0.80 to 1.75, and persistence from zero to the required return-close count; clamp each normalized score to 0–100.
Long: lower trap thesis OR an active upper trap thesis with a confirmed close above its stored edge plus the ATR buffer, at least the required consecutive return closes, and failure score at least the minimum.
Short: upper trap thesis OR an active lower trap thesis with a confirmed close below its stored edge minus the ATR buffer, at least the required consecutive return closes, and failure score at least the minimum.
