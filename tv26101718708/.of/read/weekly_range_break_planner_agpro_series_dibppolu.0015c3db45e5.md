Calculation: Reference range = high minus low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; use its high and low as the range boundaries. Reset the break state at the start of each new higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Break state = when no break is active and the reference range is positive and at least a minimum multiple of chart-timeframe ATR, latch the reference range, broken boundary, direction and chart-bar index on the first close above the upper boundary plus an ATR buffer or below the lower boundary minus an ATR buffer. Keep the break active until the next higher-timeframe bar. Initially it is unresolved; mark it resolved when a ready, invalidation or expiration condition occurs. Invalidation is a close beyond the broken boundary back into the range by more than an ATR buffer. Expiration is elapsed chart bars since the break greater than a maximum window. Evaluate ready, invalidation and expiration using the unresolved state at the start of the bar; the break bar has no elapsed-bar value.
Calculation: Break score = clamp to [0, 100] the sum of: 16 × clamp(reference range / (4 × current ATR), 0, 1); 24 × clamp(abs(close − broken boundary) / current ATR / 1.4, 0, 1); 22 if the current bar touches the shelf and closes on the breakout side of or at the boundary, otherwise 12 if it touches the shelf, otherwise 7; 16 × clamp(1 − elapsed bars / maximum window, 0, 1), or 0 on the break bar; either 12 × clamp(relative volume / 1.6, 0, 1) or 9 when volume scoring is disabled; and 14 × clamp(remaining room / current ATR / 2, 0, 1). The shelf spans the broken boundary plus and minus an ATR multiple; a bar touches it when its high is at least the shelf bottom and its low is at most the shelf top. Relative volume is current volume divided by its chart-timeframe simple moving average, or 1 when that average is not positive. The first target is the broken boundary plus, for an upward break, or minus, for a downward break, a multiple of the latched reference range. Remaining room is target minus close for an upward break and close minus target for a downward break. ATR and shelf depth are recalculated on each chart bar.
Long: No break is active AND the last completed higher-timeframe range is positive AND chart-timeframe ATR is positive AND the range is at least a minimum ATR multiple AND close > its high + an ATR buffer.
Short: No break is active AND the last completed higher-timeframe range is positive AND chart-timeframe ATR is positive AND the range is at least a minimum ATR multiple AND close < its low − an ATR buffer.
Long: An upward break is unresolved AND break score >= the confirmation-adjusted score threshold AND ((high >= shelf bottom AND low <= shelf top AND close >= broken boundary) OR abs(close − broken boundary) / ATR >= a minimum distance) AND close >= broken boundary − the invalidation ATR buffer.
Short: A downward break is unresolved AND break score >= the confirmation-adjusted score threshold AND ((high >= shelf bottom AND low <= shelf top AND close <= broken boundary) OR abs(close − broken boundary) / ATR >= a minimum distance) AND close <= broken boundary + the invalidation ATR buffer.
