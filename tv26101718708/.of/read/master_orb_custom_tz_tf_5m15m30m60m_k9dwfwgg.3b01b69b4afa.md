Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the selected daily opening window in the selected time zone. Alternatively, begin at the exchange session's first bar and end immediately before the next boundary of a configurable timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Update the range while the window is active; lock it on the first bar outside the window if its high exceeds its low. Reset the breakout-used flag and entry levels at each opening-window start.
Calculation: Long breakout level = locked opening-range high + entry buffer; short breakout level = locked opening-range low - entry buffer.
Calculation: Initial long stop = minimum of (opening-range low + (1 - stop fraction) × opening-range width - stop buffer) and (long breakout level - minimum price increment). Initial short stop = maximum of (opening-range high - (1 - stop fraction) × opening-range width + stop buffer) and (short breakout level + minimum price increment). Fix these values using the range and breakout level at the entry signal.
Calculation: Long target = long breakout level + reward-to-risk multiplier × absolute value of (long breakout level - initial long stop). Short target = short breakout level - reward-to-risk multiplier × absolute value of (short breakout level - initial short stop). Fix the target at the entry signal.
Calculation: Long stop = initial long stop, or, if break-even adjustment is enabled and a bar since the fill has high >= entry price + absolute value of (entry price - initial long stop), maximum of initial long stop and entry price. Short stop = initial short stop, or, if break-even adjustment is enabled and a bar since the fill has low <= entry price - absolute value of (entry price - initial short stop), minimum of initial short stop and entry price. A stop adjustment calculated at a bar's close applies to subsequent bars.
Long: Current weekday is enabled AND position is flat AND no breakout has triggered since the opening-window start AND a locked long breakout level exists AND (opening-window entry blocking is disabled OR the opening window is inactive) AND (entry-time restriction is disabled OR bar open time <= opening-window start time + entry-time limit) AND close > long breakout level AND previous close <= previous bar's long breakout level. Mark the breakout as used when triggered.
Short: Current weekday is enabled AND position is flat AND no breakout has triggered since the opening-window start AND a locked short breakout level exists AND (opening-window entry blocking is disabled OR the opening window is inactive) AND (entry-time restriction is disabled OR bar open time <= opening-window start time + entry-time limit) AND close < short breakout level AND previous close >= previous bar's short breakout level. Mark the breakout as used when triggered.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
