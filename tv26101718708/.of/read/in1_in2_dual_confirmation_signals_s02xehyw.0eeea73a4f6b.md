Calculation: StochRSI K = SMA(Stochastic(RSI(C)), smoothing), where C = close of a timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, taken from its last completed bar; Stochastic(x) = 100*(x - lowest x)/(highest x - lowest x) over a lookback; RSI length and Stochastic lookback are separate parameters
Calculation: Offset SuperTrend on K = SuperTrend with K as the input series and a constant offset instead of ATR; upper = K + offset, lower = K - offset; final upper = upper if (upper < previous final upper OR previous K > previous final upper) else previous final upper; final lower = lower if (lower > previous final lower OR previous K < previous final lower) else previous final lower; state starts bearish; if previous state bearish then state = bullish if K > final upper else bearish; if previous state bullish then state = bearish if K < final lower else bullish
Calculation: In1 bull flip = state changed to bullish on this bar AND K < midline (50 of the 0-100 scale); In1 bear flip = state changed to bearish on this bar AND K > midline (50)
Calculation: Norm(x) = r = x / SMA(x, L), mapped stepwise: r>1.5 ->1.0; r>1.2 ->0.9; r>1.0 ->0.8; r>0.8 ->0.7; r>0.6 ->0.6; r>0.4 ->0.5; r>0.2 ->0.25; else 0.1 (same lookback L for all uses)
Calculation: EVO = WMA(2*(100 - 100/(1 + dx)) - 100, smooth length), dx = WMA(bulls, n)/WMA(bears, n), bulls = max(flow,0), bears = max(-flow,0), flow = mean(c1..c6) * 100*Norm(volume)/100 (volume treated as 1 if missing), with Range = high-low, Spread = close-open, R2 = max(high, previous high) - min(low, previous low), dC = close - previous close: c1 = Range!=0 ? 2*(close-low)/Range*100-100 : 0; c2 = Range!=0 ? Spread/Range*100 : 0; c3 = sign(Spread)*100*Norm(|Spread|); c4 = R2!=0 ? 2*(close-min(low, previous low))/R2*100-100 : 0; c5 = R2!=0 ? dC/R2*100 : 0; c6 = sign(dC)*100*Norm(|dC|)
Long: EVO > previous EVO AND EVO of previous bar <= EVO of two bars ago AND (In1 bull flip on this bar OR (In1 bull flip on the previous bar or two bars ago AND K > previous K))
Short: EVO < previous EVO AND EVO of previous bar > EVO of two bars ago AND (In1 bear flip on this bar OR (In1 bear flip on the previous bar or two bars ago AND K < previous K))
