Calculation: DemandSetup = for some n in a range of offsets (origin followed by a short run of several candles, at least 2 and at most 10), close[n] < open[n] AND close[1] > open[n] AND for all j in 1..n-1 close[j] >= open[j] AND for all j in 2..n-1 close[j] < open[n]; bar[k] is k bars before the current closed bar; origin bar not already used by an earlier DemandSetup signal
Calculation: SupplySetup = mirror of DemandSetup: close[n] > open[n] AND close[1] < open[n] AND for all j in 1..n-1 close[j] <= open[j] AND for all j in 2..n-1 close[j] > open[n]; origin bar not already used by an earlier SupplySetup signal
Calculation: BullGapZ = (low - high[2]) / stdev(low - high[2], long lookback)
Calculation: BearGapZ = (low[2] - high) / stdev(low[2] - high, long lookback)
Long: DemandSetup is true on the chart bars, OR DemandSetup is true on bars aggregated to a higher timeframe than the chart, evaluated on the last completed higher-timeframe bars (applies only when the chart timeframe is lower than that higher timeframe), OR (low > high[2] AND high[1] > high[2] AND BullGapZ > minimum size threshold AND no datetime gap between consecutive bars among the last 3 exceeds 2.5 chart bar durations)
Short: SupplySetup is true on the chart bars, OR SupplySetup is true on bars aggregated to a higher timeframe than the chart, evaluated on the last completed higher-timeframe bars (applies only when the chart timeframe is lower than that higher timeframe), OR (high < low[2] AND low[1] < low[2] AND BearGapZ > minimum size threshold AND no datetime gap between consecutive bars among the last 3 exceeds 2.5 chart bar durations)
