Calculation: Bias score = For each of four ordered timeframes, compare the shorter-period EMA with the longer-period EMA, close with the shorter-period EMA, and the shorter-period EMA with its value on the previous chart bar. Score each comparison +1 if greater and -1 otherwise; average the three scores. Take a weighted average of the four scores, normalize it from [-1, 1] to [0, 100], and round. On historical bars use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe, the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe, and the chart bar for an equal timeframe.
Calculation: Session open = Open of the chart bar at the latest configured daily refresh anchor; initialize to the first available chart bar's open.
Calculation: Reference ATR = ATR of the last completed bar of the daily reference timeframe, which may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe; substitute zero when unavailable and apply a minimum price increment.
Calculation: Plan quality = Round the weighted sum of confirmation, bias, volatility, risk, and target scores. Confirmation score depends on whether close is on the bias side of the session open and, optionally, whether the candle body points in that direction. Bias score increases with the absolute distance of the bias score from its midpoint and is capped. Volatility score depends on whether projected range divided by session open is below, within, or above configured thresholds. Risk score is tiered by remaining distance to the risk line divided by the original risk-line distance. Target score is remaining distance to the target divided by a configured fraction of reference ATR, capped to its score range. The risk line is session open minus reference ATR times a distance parameter for a bullish bias, or plus that distance for a bearish bias. The target is session open plus or minus half of reference ATR times a range multiplier, in the bias direction. Remaining target distance is floored at zero.
Long: Bias score >= bullish threshold
Short: Bias score <= bearish threshold
Exit: long, close <= entry price minus chart-timeframe ATR at entry times a stop multiplier OR close >= entry price plus chart-timeframe ATR at entry times a target multiplier OR bars elapsed since entry > maximum holding period
Exit: short, close >= entry price plus chart-timeframe ATR at entry times a stop multiplier OR close <= entry price minus chart-timeframe ATR at entry times a target multiplier OR bars elapsed since entry > maximum holding period
