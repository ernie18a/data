Calculation: Pivot structure = compare the two most recent confirmed pivot highs and the two most recent confirmed pivot lows, using the same number of bars on each side of a pivot; a pivot becomes available only after its right-side bars have completed. Up structure requires both latest pivots to exceed their predecessors; down structure requires both to be below their predecessors.
Calculation: Trend level = 2 if up or down structure exists and the absolute difference between the linear regression of close at the current bar and at the start of its lookback, divided by ATR, reaches the higher threshold; 1 if structure exists and the ratio reaches the lower threshold but is below the higher threshold; otherwise 0. A nonpositive ATR gives a ratio of 0.
Calculation: Momentum levels = count bullish and bearish candles separately over a momentum lookback; a direction qualifies when its count reaches the rounded product of that lookback and a proportion parameter. Average the absolute candle body divided by its high-low range over the same lookback, assigning 0 to zero-range candles. Divide current volume by its SMA, using 1 if the SMA is nonpositive. For each qualifying direction, assign level 2 when body average and volume ratio both reach their respective higher thresholds, level 1 when both reach their respective lower thresholds and level 2 was not assigned, otherwise 0. A direction that does not qualify has level 0.
Calculation: EMA vote = 1 for a direction when the short, medium and long close EMAs are strictly stacked in that direction, -1 when stacked in the opposite direction, otherwise 0.
Calculation: VWAP slope = session VWAP of typical price minus its value a longer lookback ago, or minus its value a shorter lookback ago if the longer-lookback value is unavailable.
Calculation: VWAP vote = for long, 1 if close is above VWAP AND VWAP slope is positive, otherwise -1 if close is below VWAP OR VWAP slope is negative, otherwise 0; for short, 1 if close is below VWAP AND VWAP slope is negative, otherwise -1 if close is above VWAP OR VWAP slope is positive, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Direction score = for each direction, use trend level plus that direction's momentum level if its pivot structure exists, otherwise 0; add the sign of the sum of its EMA vote and VWAP vote, then clamp the result to the range 0 through 4.
Calculation: Display state = in live mode use current calculations on every bar. In each timed mode, use current calculations during its configurable live window, capture calculations on the first bar whose opening time falls within a configurable lock window, and retain that snapshot until the configurable reset window. Direction, momentum and VWAP markings use the snapshot; pivot-structure and EMA markings use current calculations. Timed-mode markings are available only within the mode's configurable daily time gate and while its live window is active or a snapshot is locked outside the reset window. Times use the New York timezone.
Long: Display state is available AND long direction score is greater than short direction score AND long direction score is greater than 0.
Short: Display state is available AND short direction score is greater than long direction score AND short direction score is greater than 0.
Long: Display state is available AND up pivot structure exists.
Short: Display state is available AND down pivot structure exists.
Long: Display state is available AND momentum display is enabled for the selected mode AND bullish momentum level is greater than 0.
Short: Display state is available AND momentum display is enabled for the selected mode AND bullish momentum level equals 0 AND bearish momentum level is greater than 0.
Long: Display state is available AND ((close is above session VWAP AND VWAP slope is nonzero) OR (close is below session VWAP AND VWAP slope is positive)), using the display-state values.
Short: Display state is available AND close is below session VWAP AND VWAP slope is negative, using the display-state values.
Long: Display state is available AND short-period close EMA is greater than medium-period close EMA AND medium-period close EMA is greater than long-period close EMA.
Short: Display state is available AND short-period close EMA is less than medium-period close EMA AND medium-period close EMA is less than long-period close EMA.
