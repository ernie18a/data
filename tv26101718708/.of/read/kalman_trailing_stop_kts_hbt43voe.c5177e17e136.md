Calculation: Kalman baseline and velocity = level and velocity from a 2D Kalman filter of close, using rolling close variance as measurement noise, responsiveness-scaled process variance, and volume-weighted measurement noise.
Calculation: Trailing stop = Kalman baseline ± stop-distance × EMA(true range), ratcheted in the current trend direction and adjusted by price extremes, intermediate swing levels, slope and momentum, volatility expansion, and optional sigmoid smoothing.
Calculation: Dynamic sweep threshold = sweep multiplier × sqrt(clamped excess kurtosis of close returns / 3) × EMA of squared close-minus-Kalman-baseline residuals, square-rooted.
Calculation: Intermediate swing highs/lows = confirmed pivots among non-inside bars; an intermediate high is a lower-level swing high higher than the swing highs before and after it, and an intermediate low is a lower-level swing low lower than the swing lows before and after it.
Long: initial regime and close > Kalman baseline OR bearish trend and close > trailing stop + signal offset × EMA(true range) and close > prior close and (slope confirmation disabled or Kalman velocity > 0) OR low is the lowest over the plunger lookback and close > open and (high − close) / (high − low) ≤ wick fraction and high > low and high − low ≥ ATR / 2 and low < Kalman baseline − dynamic sweep threshold OR close crosses above the latest intermediate swing high and prior pullback average volume < median volume and current volume > prior pullback average volume and active trend is long and trailing stop ≥ entry price and add cooldown is zero.
Short: initial regime and close ≤ Kalman baseline OR bullish trend and close < trailing stop − signal offset × EMA(true range) and close < prior close and (slope confirmation disabled or Kalman velocity < 0) OR high is the highest over the plunger lookback and close < open and (close − low) / (high − low) ≤ wick fraction and high > low and high − low ≥ ATR / 2 and high > Kalman baseline + dynamic sweep threshold OR close crosses below the latest intermediate swing low and prior pullback average volume < median volume and current volume > prior pullback average volume and active trend is short and trailing stop ≤ entry price and add cooldown is zero.
Exit: long, take-profit enabled and high ≥ entry price + take-profit multiple × EMA(true range) OR intraday filter enabled and bar is outside the configured session.
Exit: short, take-profit enabled and low ≤ entry price − take-profit multiple × EMA(true range) OR intraday filter enabled and bar is outside the configured session.
