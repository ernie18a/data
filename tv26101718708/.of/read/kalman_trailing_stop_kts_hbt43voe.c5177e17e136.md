Calculation: Kalman baseline and velocity = Apply a constant-velocity Kalman filter to close. Initialize level to the first available close, velocity to 0, and covariance P to the identity matrix once rolling close variance is available. On each subsequent bar, predict x = F x and P = F P Fᵀ + diag(Q, 0.1Q), where x = [level, velocity], F = [[1,1],[0,1]], Q = rolling close variance × 10^((responsiveness − 50)/10), and measurement variance R = max(rolling close variance / relative volume, 10⁻⁶) when volume weighting is enabled and max(rolling close variance, 10⁻⁶) otherwise. Relative volume is volume divided by its rolling median, defaulting to 1 when unavailable and clamped to [0.1, 3]. Update with K = P[:,0] / (P[0,0] + R), x = x + K × (close − x[0]), and P = P − K × P[0,:]. The updated level and velocity are the baseline and slope.
Calculation: Filtered oscillator = When oscillator tightening is enabled, calculate raw oscillator = (close − rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low), or 0.5 when the denominator is zero. Filter it independently with a scalar random-walk Kalman filter: initialize state to the first available raw oscillator and covariance to 1; subsequently predict covariance as covariance + Q, use measurement variance equal to its rolling variance divided by relative volume when volume weighting is enabled, otherwise its rolling variance, floored at 10⁻⁶; update state and covariance using the scalar Kalman gain. Use the same Q and variance lookback as the baseline filter.
Calculation: Swing structure = Exclude bars whose high is at most the immediately preceding bar's high AND whose low is at least that bar's low. Among each three consecutive remaining bars, confirm the middle high as a short-term swing high when it is at least the newest high AND greater than the oldest high; confirm the middle low as a short-term swing low when it is at most the newest low AND less than the oldest low. Among each three consecutive confirmed short-term highs, confirm the middle one as an intermediate high under the same comparisons; apply the analogous low comparisons to confirm an intermediate low. Retain the latest confirmed value of each type.
Calculation: Trailing stop and trend = Let A be EMA(true range) over the Kalman variance lookback, U = baseline + stop-distance multiplier × A, and L = baseline − stop-distance multiplier × A. Initialize trend to long before the stop is available. When U and L first become available, initialize trend to long and stop to L if close > baseline; otherwise initialize trend to short and stop to U. Initialize the running high and low to that bar's high and low. On later bars, test the previous stop before updating it: flip long to short if close < previous stop − offset × A AND close < previous close AND slope < 0 when slope confirmation is enabled; flip short to long if close > previous stop + offset × A AND close > previous close AND slope > 0 when slope confirmation is enabled. On a flip to short set running low to low and stop to min(U, low + stop-distance multiplier × A); on a flip to long set running high to high and stop to max(L, high − stop-distance multiplier × A); cancel any sigmoid transition. Otherwise update the running high in a long trend or running low in a short trend, then calculate the target as follows. In a long trend start at max(previous stop, L); if slope > 0 AND close > previous close, add push multiplier × A, capped at U. If oscillator tightening is enabled AND filtered oscillator > 0.9, raise target to at least close − 0.5A. Set floor to the greater of running high − stop-distance multiplier × A and latest intermediate low − A, using only the former when no intermediate low exists; if floor > target, set target to min(floor, close − 0.5A). If A > previous A, set target to min(target, max(previous stop − (A − previous A) × stop-distance multiplier × relaxation multiplier, L)). In a short trend start at min(previous stop, U); if slope < 0 AND close < previous close, subtract push multiplier × A, floored at L. If oscillator tightening is enabled AND filtered oscillator < 0.1, lower target to at most close + 0.5A. Set ceiling to the lesser of running low + stop-distance multiplier × A and latest intermediate high + A, using only the former when no intermediate high exists; if ceiling < target, set target to max(ceiling, close + 0.5A). If A > previous A, set target to max(target, min(previous stop + (A − previous A) × stop-distance multiplier × relaxation multiplier, U)). With sigmoid smoothing disabled, stop = target. With it enabled, start a transition when abs(target − previous stop) > 0.2A AND no transition is active, saving previous stop as start and setting the counter to zero. During a transition increment the counter, set t = clamp(counter / transition length, 0, 1), and interpolate from start to the current target using normalized sigmoid (sigmoid(−6 + 12t) − sigmoid(−6)) / (sigmoid(6) − sigmoid(−6)); clamp the interpolated stop to at least L in a long trend or at most U in a short trend. When the counter reaches transition length, end the transition and set stop to target.
Calculation: Active trend side = Start flat with underlying trend long. Each bar, after updating the trend and stop, clear the active side on an opposite trend flip or an enabled take-profit or session exit, then immediately set a flat active side to the current trend. A take-profit or session exit therefore does not produce a flat bar or a new entry when the trend remains unchanged. The entry price is close on the most recent bar where active side changed to its present non-flat side.
Calculation: Sweep distance = Base sweep multiplier × sqrt(clamp(kurtosis, 3, 20) / 3) × sqrt(EMA((close − baseline)²)). For kurtosis, let return be close − previous close, with unavailable return replaced by 0; let deviation be return − EMA(return); kurtosis is EMA(deviation⁴) / EMA(deviation²)², or 3 when the denominator is zero. All EMAs use the Kalman variance lookback.
Calculation: Pullback volume = For long additions, average volume from the bar that most recently confirmed a short-term swing high through the previous bar; for short additions, use the bar that most recently confirmed a short-term swing low through the previous bar. Before the first such confirmation, average from the start of the data. If the previous average is unavailable, use current volume. The shared addition cooldown starts at zero, decreases by one to a minimum of zero before each bar's addition checks, and resets to a configurable duration after either addition.
Long: Active trend side changes to long.
Short: Active trend side changes to short.
Exit: long, active trend side changes from long to short.
Exit: short, active trend side changes from short to long.
Long: low equals the lowest low over the shorter cycle-related lookback AND close > open AND high > low AND (high − close) / (high − low) <= wick fraction AND high − low >= ATR over the longer cycle-related lookback / 2 AND low < baseline − sweep distance.
Short: high equals the highest high over the shorter cycle-related lookback AND close < open AND high > low AND (close − low) / (high − low) <= wick fraction AND high − low >= ATR over the longer cycle-related lookback / 2 AND high > baseline + sweep distance.
Long: trend is short AND close > open AND true range > warning multiplier × EMA(true range) over the warning lookback.
Short: trend is long AND close < open AND true range > warning multiplier × EMA(true range) over the warning lookback.
Long: active trend side is long AND trailing stop >= entry price AND close crosses above the latest short-term swing high AND long pullback volume < dry-up multiplier × current rolling median volume AND volume > long pullback volume AND addition cooldown = 0.
Short: active trend side is short AND trailing stop <= entry price AND close crosses below the latest short-term swing low AND short pullback volume < dry-up multiplier × current rolling median volume AND volume > short pullback volume AND addition cooldown = 0.
