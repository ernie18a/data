Calculation: Lunar bias = -cos(2π × phase), where phase is the fractional part of (bar opening datetime in Julian days − reference new-moon Julian day) / synodic-month duration. Lunar phase bucket = floor(8 × (phase + 1/16)) modulo 8; a full-moon event is a change into the full-moon bucket, and a new-moon event is a change into the new-moon bucket.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish state = higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA. Higher-timeframe bearish state reverses both comparisons. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including the bar completing at the current chart-bar close. The fast, slow, and trend EMA lengths correspond to their chart-timeframe counterparts, ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > previous fast EMA. Bearish trend reverses all comparisons. EMAs and RSI use a configurable price source, defaulting to close.
Calculation: Bullish participation = volume / max(volume SMA, minimum volume denominator) >= relative-volume threshold AND close > open AND (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) >= close-location threshold AND abs(close − open) >= ATR × body-size multiplier. Bearish participation uses close < open and (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) >= close-location threshold, with the same remaining comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish momentum score = capped weighted sum of these conditions: bullish trend; close > slow EMA; RSI >= bullish RSI threshold; RSI > bullish RSI threshold + RSI offset; MACD histogram > 0 AND histogram >= previous histogram; close > session VWAP of typical price; bullish participation; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish state. Bearish momentum score uses bearish trend; close < slow EMA; RSI <= bearish RSI threshold; RSI < bearish RSI threshold − RSI offset; MACD histogram < 0 AND histogram <= previous histogram; close < session VWAP; bearish participation; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish state. Corresponding conditions use the same weights; cap each score at its maximum. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA and a signal EMA.
Calculation: Long confluence = capped sum of bullish momentum score × momentum weight, max(lunar bias, 0) × score scale × lunar weight, and a phase bonus when a full-moon event occurs. Short confluence substitutes bearish momentum score, max(−lunar bias, 0), and a new-moon event. Long and short use the same weights and bonus.
Calculation: Long signal = long direction enabled AND bars since the previous long signal > cooldown, or no previous long signal, AND lunar bias > bias threshold AND bullish momentum score >= momentum threshold AND long confluence >= confluence threshold AND (close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback AND close > open OR lowest low over the pullback lookback including the current bar <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND close > previous close) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish state). Evaluate at chart-bar close. Short signal reverses directions: short enabled, separate short cooldown, lunar bias < −bias threshold, bearish momentum and short confluence above their thresholds, and either close < lowest low over the preceding breakout lookback AND close < open, or highest high over the pullback lookback including the current bar >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND close < previous close; require the bearish higher-timeframe state when the filter is enabled.
Calculation: On each long signal, replace the active position state with long, entry = close, stop = lowest low over the swing lookback including the signal bar − ATR × stop buffer, and target = entry + target multiple × max(entry − stop, minimum risk). On each short signal, replace it with short, entry = close, stop = highest high over the swing lookback including the signal bar + ATR × stop buffer, and target = entry − target multiple × max(stop − entry, minimum risk). Minimum risk is a configurable multiple of the minimum price increment. Apply signal updates before checking exits on the same bar; clear the active state after an exit.
