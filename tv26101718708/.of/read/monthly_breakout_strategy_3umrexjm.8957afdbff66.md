Calculation: Monthly high = highest high of the higher-timeframe monthly bar still forming
Calculation: Monthly low = lowest low of the higher-timeframe monthly bar still forming
Long: selected calendar month AND entry mode is Long at High AND close > monthly high AND previous close <= previous monthly high OR selected calendar month AND entry mode is Long at Low AND close > monthly low AND previous close <= previous monthly low
Short: selected calendar month AND entry mode is Short at High AND close < monthly high AND previous close >= previous monthly high OR selected calendar month AND entry mode is Short at Low AND close < monthly low AND previous close >= previous monthly low
Exit: long or short, bars since entry >= holding period
