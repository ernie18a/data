Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low of chart-timeframe bars from the regular-session open through the configured opening-range window; levels lock when the window ends.
Calculation: Nearest resistance/support = closest level above/below close, respectively, among confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs/lows, the prior completed daily bar’s high/low/close (higher timeframe, last completed bar), and the most recently completed overnight, Asia, and London session highs/lows.
Calculation: Body percentage = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high = low.
Long: opening range locked AND (high > opening-range high in break mode OR close > opening-range high AND min(open, close) > opening-range high in close mode) AND (in close mode, if enabled, body percentage ≥ configured minimum AND volume > configured moving average of volume) AND (if enabled, nearest resistance is absent OR nearest resistance − close ≥ configured target distance) AND (configured signal-session filter is satisfied, if enabled) AND no prior long signal since close was below the opening-range high (reset each regular-session day).
Short: opening range locked AND (low < opening-range low in break mode OR close < opening-range low AND max(open, close) < opening-range low in close mode) AND (in close mode, if enabled, body percentage ≥ configured minimum AND volume > configured moving average of volume) AND (if enabled, nearest support is absent OR close − nearest support ≥ configured target distance) AND (configured signal-session filter is satisfied, if enabled) AND no prior short signal since close was above the opening-range low (reset each regular-session day).
