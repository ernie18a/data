Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening window in New York time; lock the range after that window ends during regular trading hours.
Calculation: Resistance pool = the retained recent confirmed swing highs, the high and close of the last completed daily bar, and the highs of the most recently completed overnight, Asian, and London sessions. A swing high is confirmed only after the configured equal number of chart bars on each side; retain the configured maximum number of most recent swing highs. The daily timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Support pool = the retained recent confirmed swing lows, the low and close of the last completed daily bar, and the lows of the most recently completed overnight, Asian, and London sessions. A swing low is confirmed only after the configured equal number of chart bars on each side; retain the configured maximum number of most recent swing lows. The daily timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long reset = no earlier long signal since the start of the current regular trading session OR close has been below the opening-range high since the last long signal, including on the current bar.
Calculation: Short reset = no earlier short signal since the start of the current regular trading session OR close has been above the opening-range low since the last short signal, including on the current bar.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute value of close minus open divided by high minus low, or zero when high equals low.
Long: Opening range is locked AND bar is within regular trading hours AND (session filter is disabled OR bar is within the configured signal session) AND long reset AND (resistance filter is disabled OR no resistance-pool level is above close OR the nearest resistance-pool level above close minus close >= target distance) AND (break mode AND high > opening-range high OR close mode AND open > opening-range high AND close > opening-range high AND (body filter is disabled OR body fraction >= minimum body fraction) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA)).
Short: Opening range is locked AND bar is within regular trading hours AND (session filter is disabled OR bar is within the configured signal session) AND short reset AND (support filter is disabled OR no support-pool level is below close OR close minus the nearest support-pool level below close >= target distance) AND (break mode AND low < opening-range low OR close mode AND open < opening-range low AND close < opening-range low AND (body filter is disabled OR body fraction >= minimum body fraction) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA)).
