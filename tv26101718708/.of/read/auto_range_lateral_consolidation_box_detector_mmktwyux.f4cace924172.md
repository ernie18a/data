Calculation: Prior range = for each of two lookbacks, a shorter shelf and a longer standard range, the highest high and lowest low over the lookback ending at the previous chart bar; midpoint is their average, width percent is 100 times their difference divided by the midpoint, containment is the percentage of those bars whose closes are within the bounds, and upper-half containment is the percentage whose closes are at or above the midpoint.
Calculation: Prior advance percent = 100 times the difference between the prior range high and the lowest low over a lookback ending immediately before the range, divided by that lowest low.
Calculation: Average range percent = the simple moving average of 100 times (high minus low) divided by close.
Calculation: Qualified range = a prior range whose containment is at least its threshold and whose width percent is at most the previous bar's average range percent times its width multiplier; the shelf also requires upper-half containment at least its threshold and uses a lower width multiplier than the standard range. The previous close must be above the previous long-term close SMA if that filter is enabled, and the previous short-term close EMA must be at least the previous longer-term close EMA OR the previous close must be at least that longer-term EMA. If enabled, prior advance percent must be at least its threshold; average volume over the prior range must be at most the previous bar's volume SMA times its multiplier; and previous-bar RSI must be between its minimum and maximum, inclusive. Apply each enabled filter. If both ranges qualify, select the shelf; otherwise select the qualified standard range.
Long: A qualified range exists AND close > selected range high.
Short: A qualified range exists AND close < selected range low.
Exit: long, a qualified range exists AND close < selected range low.
