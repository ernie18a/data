Calculation: Higher-timeframe bar = OHLC aggregated from chart bars; evaluate its still-forming values at each chart-bar close and use completed values after it closes.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = a bar's low is below the preceding bar's low AND its close is at or above that low. Bearish sweep = a bar's high is above the preceding bar's high AND its close is at or below that high.
Calculation: Bullish extended sweep = a bar's low is below the preceding bar's low AND its close is below that low AND the next bar's close is above both its high and its open. Bearish extended sweep = a bar's high is above the preceding bar's high AND its close is above that high AND the next bar's close is below both its low and its open.
Calculation: Confirmation origin = the open of the earliest bar in the consecutive run of bearish chart bars for a bullish sweep or bullish chart bars for a bearish sweep. Search backward from the chart bar that first made the sweep extreme, allowing a parameterized number of initial bars of other types before the run; stop at the first bar outside the run or a parameterized search limit.
Calculation: Delivery gap = the newest chart-bar gap between the confirmation origin and the sweep extreme. For a bullish sweep, a newer bar's high is below the low two bars earlier and the inversion level is that earlier low. For a bearish sweep, a newer bar's low is above the high two bars earlier and the inversion level is that earlier high.
Calculation: Confirmed sweep = a completed higher-timeframe sweep has a confirmation origin AND, within a parameterized higher-timeframe deadline, a chart close or the sweep bar's close is above the origin for a bullish sweep or below it for a bearish sweep AND, if gap confirmation is enabled and a delivery gap exists, a close has also passed its inversion level in the same direction. The origin and gap conditions may occur on different bars. Before confirmation, no chart bar after detection may exceed the sweep extreme, using either wick or close as a parameter. An ordinary sweep takes precedence over an extended sweep detected at the same higher-timeframe boundary.
Long: The still-forming or last completed higher-timeframe bar has a bullish sweep.
Short: The still-forming or last completed higher-timeframe bar has a bearish sweep.
Long: A completed higher-timeframe bar has a bullish extended sweep confirmed by the close of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Short: A completed higher-timeframe bar has a bearish extended sweep confirmed by the close of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: A bullish higher-timeframe sweep is confirmed.
Short: A bearish higher-timeframe sweep is confirmed.
Long: A bullish higher-timeframe sweep or extended sweep has been detected AND a chart bar within its sweep bar's span, optionally padded by a parameterized number of bars, has a bullish sweep or extended sweep AND, if the pivot filter is enabled, its low is no higher than the lows of a parameterized equal number of chart bars on each side. Select the qualifying chart bar nearest the sweep extreme, preferring the later bar on a tie; evaluate only after the necessary right-side bars exist.
Short: A bearish higher-timeframe sweep or extended sweep has been detected AND a chart bar within its sweep bar's span, optionally padded by a parameterized number of bars, has a bearish sweep or extended sweep AND, if the pivot filter is enabled, its high is no lower than the highs of a parameterized equal number of chart bars on each side. Select the qualifying chart bar nearest the sweep extreme, preferring the later bar on a tie; evaluate only after the necessary right-side bars exist.
Long: The low of a still-forming or completed higher-timeframe bar is above the high two higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the lower end of its body is above the upper end of that earlier bar's body.
Short: The high of a still-forming or completed higher-timeframe bar is below the low two higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the upper end of its body is below the lower end of that earlier bar's body.
Long: Chart close is above the older high of a previously detected bearish higher-timeframe gap.
Short: Chart close is below the older low of a previously detected bullish higher-timeframe gap.
Long: The still-forming or completed higher-timeframe close is at or above its open.
Short: The still-forming or completed higher-timeframe close is below its open.
