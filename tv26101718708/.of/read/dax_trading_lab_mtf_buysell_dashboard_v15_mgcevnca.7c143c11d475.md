Calculation: MACD pair = two MACD lines (fast EMA minus slow EMA) and their signal EMAs; both use the same fast and slow lookbacks, and one signal lookback is shorter than the other.
Calculation: Volume delta = volume × (2 × close − high − low) ÷ (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Long: On the chart timeframe or any displayed higher timeframe’s last completed bar, both MACD lines are above their signal lines
Long: Both chart-timeframe MACD lines are above their signal lines AND their alignment began within the shorter of the configured confirmation and synchronization windows AND close is above each enabled EMA filter AND close > previous close AND the configured time filter allows signals AND volume delta > 0 AND |volume delta| > the configured multiplier × the moving average of |volume delta| over the synchronization lookback AND more than the confirmation-window number of bars have elapsed since the previous long trigger
Short: On the chart timeframe or any displayed higher timeframe’s last completed bar, both MACD lines are below their signal lines
