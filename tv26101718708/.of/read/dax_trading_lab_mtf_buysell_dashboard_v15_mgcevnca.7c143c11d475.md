Calculation: Volume delta = 0 if high = low; otherwise volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Bullish alignment = both chart-timeframe MACD lines > their respective signal lines; bearish alignment = both chart-timeframe MACD lines < their respective signal lines. The two MACDs have independently configurable fast and slow lengths; one has a shorter signal length than the other.
Long: Either MACD line > its signal line on the chart timeframe OR on any of four successively higher timeframes, using the last completed bar of each higher timeframe.
Short: Either MACD line < its signal line on the chart timeframe OR on any of four successively higher timeframes, using the last completed bar of each higher timeframe.
Long: Bullish alignment AND bars since the most recent bar without bullish alignment <= synchronization window AND bars since bullish alignment first became true <= confirmation window AND close > previous close AND (first EMA filter disabled OR close > shorter EMA of close) AND (second EMA filter disabled OR close > longer EMA of close) AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time outside the configured blackout session in its configured timezone) AND (volume delta filter disabled OR (volume delta > 0 AND absolute volume delta > its SMA over the synchronization window × surge multiplier)) AND current bar index > previous long trigger bar index + confirmation window; initialize previous long trigger bar index to zero.
Short: Bearish alignment AND bars since the most recent bar without bearish alignment <= synchronization window AND bars since bearish alignment first became true <= confirmation window AND close < previous close AND (first EMA filter disabled OR close < shorter EMA of close) AND (second EMA filter disabled OR close < longer EMA of close) AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time outside the configured blackout session in its configured timezone) AND (volume delta filter disabled OR (volume delta < 0 AND absolute volume delta > its SMA over the synchronization window × surge multiplier)) AND current bar index > previous short trigger bar index + confirmation window; initialize previous short trigger bar index to zero.
