Calculation: Estimated delta = sum signed volume over completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar: add volume when close > open, subtract it when close < open; for a doji, use the sign of close minus the preceding lower-timeframe close, or zero if equal. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar volume with the sign of close minus open, or zero for a doji.
Calculation: Participation = rank the chart-bar volume EMA over a lookback, either by percentile or by its position in the lookback high-low range. Optionally use the higher of that score and the current raw-volume percentile rank. The baseline may instead use a separate rolling history for each configured trading session; in that mode rank both EMA and raw volume against that session's EMA history. Thin means participation <= the lower threshold; climax means participation >= the higher threshold; mid means strictly between them.
Calculation: Fuel = max(floor, bell × climax factor), where bell = 1 at or above the participation peak and otherwise exp(-(participation - peak)^2 / (2 × width^2)); the optional climax factor is max(factor floor, 1 - decay × (participation - climax threshold)) above the climax threshold and 1 otherwise. Paid means fuel >= its threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive line = initialize to close; on each bar add (close - previous line) × (minimum speed + (maximum speed - minimum speed) × fuel^exponent) + lag coefficient × fuel × (close - previous close). If this update crosses close in the direction of the close change while the previous line was on the other side of close, set the line to close. Gap = close - line; normalized gap = gap / ATR, or zero when ATR is zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe delta = sum of estimated chart-bar deltas in the higher-timeframe bar. A block contains a lookback number of consecutive higher-timeframe bars; its delta is their summed delta and its price change is last close minus first open. For the live verdict, the newest block includes the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and preceding completed bars; the two older blocks use completed bars. For the confirmed verdict all three blocks use completed bars. Block reference = mean absolute block delta over a longer history of completed, nonoverlapping blocks; until sufficient blocks exist, use mean absolute delta of recent completed higher-timeframe bars multiplied by the square root of the block length. Block price tolerance = price tolerance multiplied by the square root of the block length.
Calculation: Directional verdict = bullish absorption when newest block delta <= negative absorption multiple × block reference AND newest block price change >= negative block price tolerance; bearish absorption when newest block delta >= absorption multiple × block reference AND newest block price change <= block price tolerance. Optionally invert the absorption directions. Absorption has priority. Otherwise bullish when all three block deltas > meaningful multiple × block reference AND their summed price changes > block price tolerance; bearish when all three block deltas < negative meaningful multiple × block reference AND their summed price changes < negative block price tolerance. Require all three blocks and a positive reference. The working verdict either updates when a live verdict persists for a configured number of chart bars, or updates from the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Flip state = initially long. A provisional short flip occurs when the state is long, close < line - hysteresis × ATR, previous close >= previous line, the bar is paid, and estimated delta < 0; reverse all inequalities and directions for a provisional long flip. Set state to the provisional direction. Confirm immediately if instant confirmation is selected, the bar is mid participation, and absolute estimated delta >= conviction multiple × its chart-bar moving average. Otherwise confirm after the configured number of chart bars if close remains strictly on the new side of the line, or, in higher-timeframe-close mode, at the start of the next higher-timeframe bar if the last completed chart bar remained strictly on that side. A failed pending flip restores the previous state unless a new provisional flip occurs simultaneously; a new pending flip replaces the old one.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = sum(chart-bar typical price × volume) / sum(volume) over a chart-bar window equivalent to the block length of higher-timeframe bars, including the current chart bar.
Long: Paid AND adaptive line > previous adaptive line.
Short: Paid AND adaptive line < previous adaptive line.
Long: Estimated delta > 0; if estimated delta = 0, close > open.
Short: Estimated delta < 0; if estimated delta = 0, close < open.
Long: Still-forming higher-timeframe delta > 0.
Short: Still-forming higher-timeframe delta < 0.
Long: NOT paid AND normalized gap <= negative shading threshold.
Short: NOT paid AND normalized gap >= shading threshold.
Long: NOT paid AND participation is thin AND normalized gap <= negative fade threshold AND no fade has fired since absolute normalized gap last fell below half the fade threshold.
Short: NOT paid AND participation is thin AND normalized gap >= fade threshold AND no fade has fired since absolute normalized gap last fell below half the fade threshold.
Long: Participation is climax AND paid AND line < previous line AND close < previous close AND absolute gap > previous absolute gap AND no climax has fired since participation last left the climax band.
Short: Participation is climax AND paid AND line > previous line AND close > previous close AND absolute gap > previous absolute gap AND no climax has fired since participation last left the climax band.
Long: Provisional long flip occurs and subsequently confirms.
Short: Provisional short flip occurs and subsequently confirms.
Long: Working directional verdict becomes bullish.
Short: Working directional verdict becomes bearish.
Long: Confirmed long flip AND working directional verdict is bullish AND participation at the provisional flip was mid AND, if the VWAP filter is enabled, close > rolling VWAP.
Short: Confirmed short flip AND working directional verdict is bearish AND participation at the provisional flip was mid AND, if the VWAP filter is enabled, close < rolling VWAP.
