Calculation: Bullish gap = a zone with top equal to the third bar's low and bottom equal to the first bar's high, where the third low > the first high AND the difference >= chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by a threshold. Bearish gap = a zone with top equal to the first bar's low and bottom equal to the third bar's high, where the third high < the first low AND the difference >= chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by the same threshold. The three bars are consecutive chart-timeframe bars or consecutive higher-timeframe bars; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar as the third bar and detect it on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Tracked gaps = keep separate, limited-size, oldest-first queues for each direction and timeframe. A newly formed chart-timeframe gap may merge with the most recently formed gap in its queue if merging is enabled AND that gap is untouched AND was formed or last merged on the preceding chart bar AND the zones overlap; replace its bounds with their union and reset its formation bar. Higher-timeframe gaps do not merge. Otherwise add the gap and remove the oldest if the queue exceeds its limit. Starting after its formation bar, remove a bullish gap when close < its bottom and a bearish gap when close > its top; optionally remove it when low <= its bottom or high >= its top, respectively. Count a hold when a bullish gap is reached by low <= its top AND close > its top, or a bearish gap is reached by high >= its bottom AND close < its bottom. Count at most one hold per visit; a new visit begins after at least one bar without a reach. A full-fill removal still permits a hold on that same bar.
Long: A bullish gap forms on the chart timeframe OR a bullish gap forms on the higher timeframe OR a tracked bullish gap records its first hold.
Short: A bearish gap forms on the chart timeframe OR a bearish gap forms on the higher timeframe OR a tracked bearish gap records its first hold.
