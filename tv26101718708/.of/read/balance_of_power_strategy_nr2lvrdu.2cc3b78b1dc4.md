Calculation: Balance of Power = (close − open) / (high − low)
Calculation: Composite = average of Balance of Power and five exponential moving averages of Balance of Power, with lookbacks increasing from shortest to longest
Calculation: Projected = Composite + (Composite − previous Composite) × previous volume / sum of volume over the current and previous three bars + (previous Composite − Composite two bars ago) × volume two bars ago / that volume sum + (Composite two bars ago − Composite three bars ago) × volume three bars ago / that volume sum
Long: Projected crosses above zero
Short: Projected crosses below zero
