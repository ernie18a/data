Calculation: First daily inside bar = the first bar of each day whose high is below the previous bar's high and whose low is above the previous bar's low; its mother candle is the previous bar.
Long: On a lower timeframe than the chart timeframe, high > mother candle high
Short: On a lower timeframe than the chart timeframe, low < mother candle low
Exit: long, low <= mother candle low
Exit: short, high >= mother candle high
