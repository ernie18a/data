Calculation: ATR-deviation ZigZag pivot = the current leg’s highest high or lowest low, confirmed when price reverses from it by at least ATR × a deviation multiplier; discard reversals whose leg is smaller than ATR × a minimum-size multiplier or whose triggering bar’s corresponding wick is at least a maximum wick-to-body ratio × its body
Calculation: Trend = the price move between consecutive alternating ZigZag pivots; an uptrend runs from a low pivot to a high pivot, and a downtrend from a high pivot to a low pivot
Calculation: Countertrend swing = the next completed trend in the opposite direction; retracement ratio = countertrend swing’s price range divided by the trend’s price range
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing = an ATR-deviation ZigZag trend computed from aggregated higher-timeframe completed bars, with its own deviation and minimum-size multipliers
Long: an uptrend has a following completed countertrend swing AND the trend direction matches its containing completed higher-timeframe swing, or—if no completed higher-timeframe swing contains it—matches the direction of the developing higher-timeframe leg AND the trend and countertrend swing are both contained in the same completed higher-timeframe swing, or neither is contained in one
Short: a downtrend has a following completed countertrend swing AND the trend direction matches its containing completed higher-timeframe swing, or—if no completed higher-timeframe swing contains it—matches the direction of the developing higher-timeframe leg AND the trend and countertrend swing are both contained in the same completed higher-timeframe swing, or neither is contained in one
