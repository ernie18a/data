Calculation: Session range = the highest high and lowest low of the chart bars in the most recently completed range-building session; reset all daily setup and trade state at the next range-building session open.
Calculation: Swing and structure break = a swing high or low is a pivot confirmed by the same number of bars on each side; a downward break occurs when close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low, and an upward break when close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low and the most recent bias, initially neutral; set bearish when close is below the pivot low and bullish when close is above the pivot high, with bullish taking precedence if both comparisons hold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe zones = on each enabled higher timeframe, use the last completed bars: a bearish gap spans the current completed bar's high to the low two bars earlier when the latter exceeds the former; a bullish gap spans the high two bars earlier to the current completed bar's low when the latter exceeds the former. A bearish order block spans the preceding bar's low to high when it is bullish and the current completed close is below its low; a bullish order block spans the preceding bar's low to high when it is bearish and the current completed close is above its high. Add enabled zones at higher-timeframe bar changes, retain only the most recent configured number, and process them in creation order. A chart close beyond a zone's far edge flips an original order block or gap to the opposite direction if its corresponding breaker or inverse-gap option is enabled; otherwise remove it. Remove a previously flipped zone when breached again, and remove any zone whose midpoint is farther from chart close than a configured multiple of chart ATR. A price touches a zone when it lies inclusively between its bounds.
Calculation: Chart gaps = a bearish gap spans current high to the low two chart bars earlier when the latter is higher; a bullish gap spans the high two bars earlier to current low when the latter is higher. Require gap width at least a configured multiple of chart ATR; retain the most recent configured number. A bar taps a gap when its low is at or below the gap top and its high is at or above the gap bottom.
Calculation: Protected range side = during the range-building session, the high side is protected if the enabled zone filter finds a bearish zone containing a bar's high and the number of downward structure breaks reaches its threshold, or if the enabled strong-side filter finds a high above the latest swing high closing below it followed within a configured bar count by a downward structure break, with the swept swing in the configured upper portion of the developing range. The low side is the symmetric bullish case. The zone touch and structure breaks need not occur in a particular order.
Calculation: Sweep = after the range is complete and during the enabled reversal session, excluding configured news dates, accept only the first qualifying sweep until the next range-building session: short when high exceeds the range high and close is below it, the high side is not protected, higher-timeframe bias is bearish or neutral if enabled, and high is inside an enabled bearish zone if the zone filter is enabled; long when low is below the range low and close is above it, the low side is not protected, higher-timeframe bias is bullish or neutral if enabled, and low is inside an enabled bullish zone if the zone filter is enabled. Give the short sweep precedence if both qualify. Until the reversal entry fires, update the sweep extreme, sweep bar and its high and low whenever a more extreme high for short or low for long occurs, even outside the reversal session.
Calculation: Confirmation = after a sweep and before a reversal entry, arm structure confirmation on a structure break in the sweep direction. Arm delivery confirmation when a bearish candle closes below the opening price of the first candle of the latest bullish run, with the preceding close at or above that price, or symmetrically for a bullish candle and latest bearish run; retain the latest confirmation level and bar. A gap confirmation taps the most recent chart gap in the sweep direction formed on or after the current sweep bar, on a later bar. A delivery confirmation taps its retained level on a later bar using high at or above the level for short or low at or below it for long. A hidden-block confirmation taps the zone of the highest-high bullish candle for short, or lowest-low non-bullish candle for long, between and including the two latest same-direction chart-gap formation bars, provided their separation is within the configured lookback; the short zone runs from the candle's lower body edge to its high and the long zone from its low to its upper body edge. Choose the configured model: armed structure plus gap tap, delivery tap, gap tap, hidden-block tap, or any of these. When several taps coincide, use gap, then hidden block, then delivery for entry pricing.
Calculation: Reversal entry and stop = price a gap entry at the configured proximal edge, midpoint or distal edge; price a hidden-block entry at its lower edge for short or upper edge for long; price a delivery entry at its level. Select the stop reference from the sweep extreme, the corresponding high or low of its bar, the trigger bar extreme, or the most protective high or low from the entry reference bar through the trigger bar; the reference bar is before the gap formation by the gap's construction span, before the hidden-block candle by one bar, or before the delivery confirmation by one bar. Fall back to the trigger extreme if the protective extreme is unavailable. The short stop is the greater of reference and trigger high plus an ATR buffer; the long stop is the lesser of reference and trigger low minus the buffer. The first target is the opposite session-range boundary; risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance and reward is the absolute entry-to-target distance.
Calculation: Continuation impulse = after a sweep, the first chart close beyond the opposite session-range boundary establishes an impulse from the current sweep extreme to that bar's low for short or high for long; until continuation entry, extend the endpoint whenever a more extreme low or high occurs. The continuation entry level is the start plus a configured fraction of the distance from start to endpoint. Its stop is the start plus a separately configured fraction of that distance, plus an ATR buffer for short or minus it for long. Its target is the reversal's optional second target if available, otherwise the endpoint extended by a configured fraction of the impulse. The optional second target is the lowest prior low below the first target for short, or highest prior high above it for long, within a configured lookback at reversal entry. Mark that draw reached if price touches it outside the continuation session after the range break and before continuation entry.
Short: A short sweep is accepted, OR a new more extreme short sweep high updates its bar before the reversal entry.
Long: A long sweep is accepted, OR a new more extreme long sweep low updates its bar before the reversal entry.
Short: A short sweep exists AND the reversal session is open AND the reversal entry has not fired since the range-building session opened AND the configured confirmation occurs within the configured bar limit of the current sweep bar AND the optional gap-validation filter is disabled OR, with all preceding conditions satisfied, a same-direction chart gap formed on or after the current sweep bar has been tapped since the sweep OR the current bar taps the selected gap or hidden block; AND reversal risk is positive AND reversal reward divided by risk is at least the configured minimum.
Long: A long sweep exists AND the reversal session is open AND the reversal entry has not fired since the range-building session opened AND the configured confirmation occurs within the configured bar limit of the current sweep bar AND the optional gap-validation filter is disabled OR, with all preceding conditions satisfied, a same-direction chart gap formed on or after the current sweep bar has been tapped since the sweep OR the current bar taps the selected gap or hidden block; AND reversal risk is positive AND reversal reward divided by risk is at least the configured minimum.
Short: A short sweep exists AND the opposite range boundary has been broken by close AND the continuation session is open AND continuation has not fired since the range-building session opened AND current low is at or below the continuation entry level AND current high is at or above it AND, if enabled, the originating zone is on the designated longest zone timeframe AND, if enabled, the draw was not reached before continuation.
Long: A long sweep exists AND the opposite range boundary has been broken by close AND the continuation session is open AND continuation has not fired since the range-building session opened AND current low is at or below the continuation entry level AND current high is at or above it AND, if enabled, the originating zone is on the designated longest zone timeframe AND, if enabled, the draw was not reached before continuation.
Exit: short, on a bar after reversal entry, low is at or below the first target OR high is at or above the reversal stop; if break-even management is enabled and low has reached entry minus the configured risk multiple, including on this bar, replace the stop with entry; target takes precedence over stop.
Exit: long, on a bar after reversal entry, high is at or above the first target OR low is at or below the reversal stop; if break-even management is enabled and high has reached entry plus the configured risk multiple, including on this bar, replace the stop with entry; target takes precedence over stop.
Exit: short, on a bar after continuation entry, low is at or below the continuation target OR high is at or above the continuation stop; target takes precedence over stop.
Exit: long, on a bar after continuation entry, high is at or above the continuation target OR low is at or below the continuation stop; target takes precedence over stop.
