Calculation: Candle quantities = body = max(abs(close - open), price epsilon); range = max(high - low, price epsilon); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; close location = (close - low) / range.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 when the SMA is not positive; replace missing volume with 0.
Calculation: Range edges = highest high and lowest low over the lookback ending at the previous bar; the range is valid when both exist and their difference exceeds price epsilon.
Calculation: ATR = ATR over its lookback, falling back to current true range when unavailable, and floored at price epsilon.
Calculation: Normalization N(x, a, b) = clamp((x - a) / max(b - a, epsilon), 0, 1).
Calculation: Rejection score for each side = round(clamp(weighted sum of N(relative volume, lower volume scoring threshold, upper volume scoring threshold), N(wick / body, minimum wick ratio, upper wick-ratio scoring threshold), N(wick / ATR, minimum wick-size threshold, upper wick-size scoring threshold), 1 - N(abs(relevant range edge - wick extreme) / ATR, 0, edge-distance scoring threshold), directional close location, and N(directional room / ATR, lower room scoring threshold, upper room scoring threshold), 0, maximum score)). Directional close location is close location for bullish rejection and 1 - close location for bearish rejection; directional room is range high - close for bullish rejection and close - range low for bearish rejection. The volume scoring thresholds depend on the minimum relative-volume threshold; the wick-ratio scoring threshold depends on the minimum wick ratio; the wick-size scoring threshold depends on the minimum wick-size threshold; the edge-distance scoring threshold depends on the edge-tolerance threshold; and the upper room scoring threshold is the room-guide ATR multiple. Sensitivity and optional chart-timeframe adaptation adjust the event thresholds and score floor.
Calculation: Bullish candidate = valid range AND low <= range low + ATR × edge-tolerance multiple AND relative volume >= minimum relative volume AND lower wick / body >= minimum wick ratio AND lower wick >= ATR × minimum wick-size multiple AND close location >= minimum directional close location AND bullish rejection score >= score floor.
Calculation: Bearish candidate = valid range AND high >= range high - ATR × edge-tolerance multiple AND relative volume >= minimum relative volume AND upper wick / body >= minimum wick ratio AND upper wick >= ATR × minimum wick-size multiple AND close location <= 1 - minimum directional close location AND bearish rejection score >= score floor.
Calculation: Active event = the most recent selected candidate, replacing the previous event when the event cooldown has elapsed. Select the bullish candidate when both qualify and its score is at least the bearish score; otherwise select the qualifying bearish candidate. Store its side, bar index, close, ATR, score, and risk edge; the bullish risk edge is event low - event ATR × risk-buffer multiple, and the bearish risk edge is event high + event ATR × risk-buffer multiple. Store the follow level as event close plus the follow-through ATR distance for bullish events or minus it for bearish events. The follow-through distance depends on the confirmation mode. On each bar, including the event bar, mark the event failed if close crosses beyond its risk edge; otherwise mark it accepted when high reaches the bullish follow level or low reaches the bearish follow level. Compute expiry before acceptance on each bar: an unaccepted, unfailed event expires when its age exceeds the tracking window. Failure and expiry persist, and a later event replaces the active event.
Long: Bullish candidate AND event cooldown has elapsed AND (bearish candidate is false OR bullish rejection score >= bearish rejection score).
Short: Bearish candidate AND event cooldown has elapsed AND (bullish candidate is false OR bearish rejection score > bullish rejection score).
Long: Active event side is bullish AND active event is not failed AND active event is not expired.
Short: Active event side is bearish AND active event is not failed AND active event is not expired.
Exit: long, active event side is bullish AND close < active event risk edge.
Exit: short, active event side is bearish AND close > active event risk edge.
