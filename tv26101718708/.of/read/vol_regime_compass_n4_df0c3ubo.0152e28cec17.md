Calculation: Volatility rank = percentile rank of the ratio of the EMA of absolute log returns to its rolling median; use 1 when the ratio is unavailable.
Calculation: Coupling = population variance of volatility ranks calculated on three distinct timeframes ordered from shortest to longest. For each timeframe use the previous completed bar; each timeframe may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. Substitute a fixed fallback when the variance is unavailable.
Calculation: Coupling weight = the lesser of inverse coupling, calculated as 1 / (a small positive constant + coupling), and a rolling nearest-rank percentile of inverse coupling.
Calculation: Extremal index = the number of starts of consecutive runs of absolute log returns above their rolling nearest-rank percentile, divided by the number of such exceedances in the same lookback, capped at 1; use a fallback when the exceedance count does not exceed a minimum count.
Calculation: Trend regime = bullish when the shorter close SMA exceeds the longer close SMA AND the rolling mean log return is positive; bearish when the shorter close SMA is below the longer close SMA AND the rolling mean log return is negative; otherwise neutral. Volatility is quiet when the rolling standard deviation of log returns is below its rolling nearest-rank median, and noisy otherwise.
Calculation: Regime multiplier = an optional multiplier selected by trend regime and quiet or noisy volatility; the bullish-quiet multiplier is greater than the neutral and bearish-quiet multipliers, which are greater than the bullish-noisy multiplier, which is greater than the bearish-noisy multiplier. When disabled, use 1.
Calculation: Score = volatility sizing factor × trend factor × coupling weight × inverse realized volatility × extremal-index factor × regime multiplier. The volatility sizing factor is a decreasing sigmoid of chart-timeframe volatility rank, scaled between configurable minimum and maximum values. The trend factor is 1 plus a scaled hyperbolic tangent of a scaled log ratio of close to the longer close SMA. Inverse realized volatility is 1 / (a small positive constant + the rolling standard deviation of log returns). The extremal-index factor is the greater of the extremal index and a floor, raised to a configurable exponent.
Calculation: Conviction = rounded rolling percentile rank of score.
Long: Close > longer close SMA.
Short: Close <= longer close SMA.
Long: Extremal index >= dislocation threshold OR coupling <= dislocation threshold, AND conviction >= applicable ACT threshold. The ACT threshold is a configurable instrument-specific parameter.
