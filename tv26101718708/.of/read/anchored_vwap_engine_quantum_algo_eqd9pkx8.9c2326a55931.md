Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of chart bars on each side, recognized on the confirmation bar; retain the latest confirmed level of each kind and reset its swept flag when a new level confirms.
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > that level AND that level has not been swept since confirmation. Mark the level swept after the event.
Calculation: Buy-side sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < that level AND that level has not been swept since confirmation. Mark the level swept after the event.
Calculation: Sweep direction = the direction of the latest sweep; sell-side is bullish and buy-side is bearish. If both sweeps occur on the same bar, the direction is bullish.
Calculation: Primary anchored VWAP = volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3 from the selected anchor through the current bar. The anchor is the latest session, week, or month boundary; the actual bar of the latest confirmed swing high or swing low; the latest bar whose volume is at least the highest volume over a lookback including that bar; or the latest sweep bar. Initialize each stream at the first available bar until its first anchor event.
Calculation: Outer deviation bands = primary anchored VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiple of the square root of the volume-weighted variance of (high + low + close) / 3 over the same anchored bars.
Long: Sweep direction = bullish.
Short: Sweep direction = bearish.
Long: On a completed bar, low <= lower outer deviation band AND close > lower outer deviation band AND close < primary anchored VWAP AND bars since the last emitted band-rejection signal of either direction >= cooldown, or no prior signal exists.
Short: On a completed bar, high >= upper outer deviation band AND close < upper outer deviation band AND close > primary anchored VWAP AND bars since the last emitted band-rejection signal of either direction >= cooldown, or no prior signal exists.
