Calculation: Primary anchored VWAP = cumulative sum(hlc3 × volume) / cumulative volume from the selected anchor; reset at the selected session, week, or month open, confirmed swing high or low, highest-volume bar in its lookback, or qualifying liquidity sweep. A qualifying sweep wicks beyond the latest confirmed swing level and closes back inside it.
Calculation: Volume-weighted deviation = sqrt(max(cumulative sum(hlc3² × volume) / cumulative volume − primary anchored VWAP², 0)); outer bands = primary anchored VWAP ± outer-band multiple × volume-weighted deviation.
Long: bar is confirmed AND primary stream is active AND lower outer band is available AND low ≤ lower outer band AND close > lower outer band AND close < primary anchored VWAP AND cooldown since prior signal has elapsed
Short: bar is confirmed AND primary stream is active AND upper outer band is available AND high ≥ upper outer band AND close < upper outer band AND close > primary anchored VWAP AND cooldown since prior signal has elapsed
