Calculation: Active levels = Maintain separate resistance and support lists for each enabled timeframe. On each chart bar, first remove levels whose age since creation exceeds the maximum age in chart bars. From the last completed bar of each higher timeframe, or the last completed constituent bar of each lower timeframe, obtain ATR and confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot lookbacks; for the chart timeframe use the current bar. A pivot high qualifies when its high minus the greater high of its two immediate neighbors is at least a strength multiplier times that timeframe's ATR at confirmation. A pivot low qualifies when the lesser low of its two immediate neighbors minus its low is at least the same multiplier times that ATR. On every chart bar where a qualifying pivot value is available, add it as a resistance or support level unless an active level of the same type and timeframe is less than a merge multiplier times the current timeframe ATR away. If adding it would exceed the active-level limit for that type and timeframe, remove the oldest such level first. After additions, evaluate breakouts and remove each broken level immediately.
Long: Chart close > any active resistance level + breakout multiplier times the current ATR of that level's timeframe.
Short: Chart close < any active support level - breakout multiplier times the current ATR of that level's timeframe.
