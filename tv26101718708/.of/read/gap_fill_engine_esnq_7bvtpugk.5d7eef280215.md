Calculation: Previous session = the most recent completed configured regular trading session; its close is the close of its last chart bar, and its high and low are the highest high and lowest low of its chart bars.
Calculation: Gap = current session's first chart bar open minus previous session close.
Calculation: Gap class = classify absolute gap divided by ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe bar using three increasing thresholds; each resulting interval has an independent enabled setting.
Calculation: Stop distance = either a fixed price distance or a multiple of the ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe bar at entry; retain this distance for the position.
Long: Current bar is the first bar of the configured regular trading session AND no position is open AND gap < 0 AND absolute gap >= minimum price distance AND higher-timeframe ATR > 0 AND gap class is enabled AND (inside-range filter is disabled OR current open >= previous session low AND current open <= previous session high) AND absolute value of (previous session close minus current close) / stop distance >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Short: Current bar is the first bar of the configured regular trading session AND no position is open AND gap > 0 AND absolute gap >= minimum price distance AND higher-timeframe ATR > 0 AND gap class is enabled AND (inside-range filter is disabled OR current open >= previous session low AND current open <= previous session high) AND absolute value of (previous session close minus current close) / stop distance >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= entry price minus stop distance OR high >= previous session close at entry OR bar is outside the configured trade window).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= entry price plus stop distance OR low <= previous session close at entry OR bar is outside the configured trade window).
