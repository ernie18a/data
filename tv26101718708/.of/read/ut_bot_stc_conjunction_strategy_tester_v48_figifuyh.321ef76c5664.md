Calculation: UT trailing stop = Let loss be ATR multiplied by a sensitivity parameter. Initialize the stop to close minus loss. Thereafter, if current and previous close are both above the previous stop, use the greater of the previous stop and close minus loss; if both are below it, use the lesser of the previous stop and close plus loss; otherwise use close minus loss when close is above the previous stop, and close plus loss otherwise.
Calculation: Schaff trend cycle = Subtract the slow close EMA from the fast close EMA. Normalize that difference to its rolling minimum and maximum over the cycle lookback, multiplied by 100; if the range is zero, retain the previous normalized value, initially zero. Smooth recursively as previous smoothed value plus a smoothing factor times the difference between the normalized and previous smoothed values, initializing with the normalized value. Normalize this smoothed series over the same lookback in the same way, including the zero-range fallback, and apply the same recursive smoothing again.
Calculation: Volatility regime = The fast ATR divided by the slow ATR, or 1 if the slow ATR is zero. High volatility is a ratio at or above the high threshold; otherwise low volatility is a ratio at or below the low threshold; otherwise it is normal. Select the stop lookback, reward-to-risk target and optional price offset by regime; high volatility uses a shorter stop lookback than the normal and low regimes. The offset is zero when disabled.
Calculation: Pending setup = On a qualifying setup while flat, save its direction and the current rolling lowest low for a long or highest high for a short, using the regime-selected lookback including the current bar. Cancel an opposite pending setup. With the optional lag window enabled, retain the setup through the configured maximum number of subsequent bars, cancelling it when the session ends, a position exists, an opposite setup qualifies, or its age exceeds the limit. Without the lag window, retain it only on the setup bar. Clear it after an entry order is submitted.
Calculation: Trade levels = At entry signal, set reference price to close plus the regime offset for a long and close minus the offset for a short. Initial risk is reference price minus the saved low for a long, or saved high minus reference price for a short. Save the signal-bar close as the breakeven reference, the saved swing as the stop, and reference price plus or minus initial risk times the regime-selected reward-to-risk target as the full target. Set the optional partial target similarly using its own risk multiple. After entry, if the optional breakeven condition is met, replace the stop with the saved signal-bar close: the highest high since the signal bar minus that close must be at least the breakeven multiple of initial risk for a long; for a short, that close minus the lowest low since the signal bar must be at least that amount.
Long: Flat AND a pending long setup exists AND Schaff trend cycle is greater than its previous value AND close plus current regime offset minus the saved swing low is at least the minimum stop distance. A qualifying long setup requires close to cross above the UT trailing stop AND Schaff trend cycle to be at or below the lower zone AND the optional session filter to pass AND, when enabled, high minus low to be at least the minimum ATR multiple AND, when enabled, high minus low to be at most the maximum ATR multiple AND, when enabled, fast volume EMA to exceed slow volume EMA AND, when enabled, the upper wick divided by candle range to be at most the wick threshold, with a zero ratio for a zero-range candle AND, when enabled, the close EMA change over the velocity lookback to be at least the negative slope threshold.
Short: Flat AND a pending short setup exists AND Schaff trend cycle is less than its previous value AND the saved swing high minus close plus current regime offset is at least the minimum stop distance. A qualifying short setup requires close to cross below the UT trailing stop AND Schaff trend cycle to be at or above the upper zone AND the optional session filter to pass AND, when enabled, high minus low to be at least the minimum ATR multiple AND, when enabled, high minus low to be at most the maximum ATR multiple AND, when enabled, fast volume EMA to exceed slow volume EMA AND, when enabled, the lower wick divided by candle range to be at most the wick threshold, with a zero ratio for a zero-range candle AND, when enabled, the close EMA change over the velocity lookback to be at most the positive slope threshold.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the active stop OR high is at or above the full target OR the optional partial exit is enabled AND not yet executed AND high is at or above the partial target; the partial exit closes half the position.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the active stop OR low is at or below the full target OR the optional partial exit is enabled AND not yet executed AND low is at or below the partial target; the partial exit closes half the position.
