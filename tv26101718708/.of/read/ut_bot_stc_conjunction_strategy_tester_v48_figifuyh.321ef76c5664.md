Calculation: UT Bot trailing stop = initialize at close − key × ATR; thereafter, if current and prior closes are both above the prior stop, take the maximum of the prior stop and close − key × ATR; if both are below, take the minimum of the prior stop and close + key × ATR; otherwise set it to close − key × ATR when close > prior stop, else close + key × ATR. UT long trigger = prior close < prior stop AND close > current stop; UT short trigger = prior close > prior stop AND close < current stop.
Calculation: Schaff Trend Cycle = normalize the fast EMA minus the slower EMA over a lookback to 0–100; smooth it recursively with a factor; normalize that smoothed series over the same lookback to 0–100; smooth again with the same factor. The fast EMA length is shorter than the slow EMA length.
Calculation: Candle range = high − low; ATR range baseline uses a longer lookback.
Calculation: Volume intensity = fast EMA(volume) > slow EMA(volume); the fast EMA length is shorter.
Calculation: Long rejection wick percentage = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) × 100; short rejection wick percentage = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) × 100.
Calculation: Trend velocity = EMA(close) − its value several bars earlier.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = shorter-lookback ATR / longer-lookback ATR.
Calculation: Swing stop = lowest low over the active swing lookback for longs; highest high over it for shorts. Use a shorter swing lookback in the high-volatility regime than in other regimes.
Calculation: Entry offset = volatility-regime-dependent offset; calculated long entry = close + offset; calculated short entry = close − offset.
Calculation: Initial risk = calculated long entry − long swing stop; for shorts, short swing stop − calculated short entry. Profit target = calculated entry plus (long) or minus (short) initial risk × regime-dependent reward multiple. Breakeven stop = entry price after the favorable extreme since entry moves at least initial risk × the breakeven trigger multiple.
Long: UT long trigger AND Schaff Trend Cycle <= its lower-zone threshold AND Schaff Trend Cycle > its prior value within the allowed confirmation window after the qualifying setup AND candle range >= its minimum ATR proportion AND candle range <= its maximum ATR proportion AND fast EMA(volume) > slow EMA(volume) AND long rejection wick percentage <= its maximum AND trend velocity >= negative maximum adverse slope AND current time is within the configured trading session AND weekly circuit breaker is not tripped AND initial risk >= minimum stop distance.
Short: UT short trigger AND Schaff Trend Cycle >= its upper-zone threshold AND Schaff Trend Cycle < its prior value within the allowed confirmation window after the qualifying setup AND candle range >= its minimum ATR proportion AND candle range <= its maximum ATR proportion AND fast EMA(volume) > slow EMA(volume) AND short rejection wick percentage <= its maximum AND trend velocity <= maximum adverse slope AND current time is within the configured trading session AND weekly circuit breaker is not tripped AND initial risk >= minimum stop distance.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop level OR high >= full profit target OR (partial take profit enabled AND high >= partial profit target)
Exit: Short, high >= active stop level OR low <= full profit target OR (partial take profit enabled AND low <= partial profit target)
