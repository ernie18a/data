Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average over a shorter lookback, or divided by the average volume of preceding completed chart bars with the same hour and minute.
Calculation: Z-score(x) = (x − moving average of x over a lookback) / standard deviation of x over that lookback.
Calculation: Buy volume = sum of volume in lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar whose close exceeds their open; sell volume = sum for bars whose close is below their open. Include any lower-timeframe bar still forming within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Volume delta = buy volume − sell volume.
