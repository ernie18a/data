Calculation: Estimated delta = choose one method: directional, candle anatomy, or hybrid. Directional buy volume is volume if close > open OR close > previous close, otherwise half volume; directional sell volume is volume if close < open OR close < previous close, otherwise half volume; directional delta is buy volume minus sell volume. For candle anatomy, let body = abs(close - open), wicks = high - max(open, close) + min(open, close) - low, and range = wicks + body. The rate is (wicks + 2 * body) / (2 * range) when range > 0, otherwise 0.5; replace a zero rate with 0.5. Anatomy delta is volume * rate if close > open, -volume * rate if close < open, and 0 otherwise. Hybrid delta is a weighted sum of directional and anatomy delta, with more weight on anatomy.
Calculation: Participation histogram = EMA-smooth estimated delta; divide it by an EMA of its absolute value, using zero if the denominator is zero, and clamp the result symmetrically. Calculate normalized true strength index as EMA of EMA of close change divided by EMA of EMA of absolute close change, using zero if the denominator is zero; use the shorter smoothing period first and the longer second. Acceleration is an EMA of the change in normalized true strength index multiplied by an acceleration factor. Multiply normalized delta by a base weight. When normalized delta and acceleration have the same nonzero sign, add abs(normalized delta) * abs(acceleration) * boost weight * sign(normalized delta). When their signs oppose, first multiply the base component by max(penalty floor, 1 - abs(acceleration) * penalty factor). Multiply the resulting sum by a histogram scaling factor. If both abs(normalized delta) and abs(acceleration) are below the chop threshold, multiply by the chop compression factor. If the previous compressed value's magnitude is below the ignition threshold, its current magnitude is greater than its previous magnitude, and normalized delta and acceleration agree in sign, multiply by 1 + ignition boost. EMA-smooth this value to obtain the preliminary histogram. If the preliminary histogram and its previous value have the same nonzero sign, acceleration agrees with normalized delta, abs(preliminary histogram) exceeds the sustain threshold, abs(normalized delta) exceeds the chop threshold, and histogram magnitude is not falling, multiply by the sustain factor. If the preliminary histogram and its previous value have the same nonzero sign and the magnitudes of the preliminary histogram, acceleration, and normalized delta are all falling, multiply by max(decay floor, 1 - decay sensitivity). If the adjusted histogram magnitude is below the tail threshold, acceleration opposes normalized delta, and normalized delta magnitude is falling, multiply by the tail decay factor. If the resulting magnitude is below the reset threshold and normalized delta magnitude is below the chop threshold, multiply by the reset factor. Clamp the result to the same symmetric limits as normalized delta.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe histogram = participation histogram calculated independently on each of three selected timeframes, ordered longest to shortest. A selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe: for historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including one completing on the current chart bar, or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; on a live bar use the still-forming selected-timeframe bar.
Long: participation histogram > 0
Short: participation histogram < 0
Long: participation histogram >= bullish threshold
Short: participation histogram <= bearish threshold
Long: participation histogram >= bullish threshold AND participation histogram > previous participation histogram
Short: participation histogram <= bearish threshold AND participation histogram < previous participation histogram
Long: any multi-timeframe histogram >= bullish threshold
Short: any multi-timeframe histogram <= bearish threshold
Long: at least two multi-timeframe histograms >= bullish threshold AND no multi-timeframe histogram <= bearish threshold

Long: at least two multi-timeframe histograms >= bullish threshold AND at least one multi-timeframe histogram <= bearish threshold
