Calculation: Structure = The latest confirmed pivot high and low, each formed by a high or low with the same configurable number of bars on either side; optionally increase the pivot lookback as the chart timeframe increases. Each newly confirmed pivot activates its level. A level becomes inactive on its first break, even if no trade is entered. In stop-entry mode a high/low reaching the level is a break; in close-entry mode the close must strictly exceed the high or fall below the low. Record the direction of every break, initially neutral; process a high break before a low break. Optionally reset pivots, active levels, and break direction at the start of each calendar day.
Calculation: Break type = An upward break is continuation if the preceding break direction is not downward; a downward break is continuation if the preceding break direction is not upward. Otherwise it is a change of character. Select continuation only, change of character only, or both.
Calculation: Trend filter = Optionally require one selected condition: all three SMA slopes have the trade direction; at least two of the three have the trade direction; the short- and medium-lookback SMA slopes have the trade direction; the short-lookback SMA slope has the trade direction; or close is above all three SMAs for long and below all three for short. SMA slope is current SMA minus its previous-bar value. Independently, optionally require short-, medium-, and long-lookback SMAs to be strictly ordered descending for long or ascending for short, and optionally require their slopes to be strictly ordered in the same respective direction.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = Cumulative volume-weighted mean of (open + high + low + close) / 4, reset on the first bar at or after a configurable daily anchor time in a configurable timezone. Two independently configured anchored VWAPs may each optionally filter entries.
Calculation: Entry reference = The active pivot level for stop-entry mode, or the signal bar close for close-entry mode. The stop-entry order is placed at the active pivot while its filters pass and is canceled when they fail; its trigger is the high reaching the pivot for long or the low reaching it for short. Close-entry signals place a market order for the next bar open.
Calculation: Initial stop = For long, select the latest confirmed pivot low, the signal bar low, or the entry reference reduced by a configurable percentage; subtract a configurable absolute buffer and a configurable percentage of the entry reference. For short, select the latest confirmed pivot high, the signal bar high, or the entry reference increased by a configurable percentage; add the same kinds of buffers. Calculate and save the stop when the entry order is placed.
Calculation: Target = At order placement, add to the entry reference for long, or subtract for short, the absolute distance between the entry reference and initial stop multiplied by a configurable reward-to-risk ratio; the ratio may be shared or side-specific.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = The active pivot must differ from the last pivot used for an order on that side by more than a configurable price tolerance, or no such pivot has been used that day. Record the pivot when the order is placed, including an unfilled stop order, and reset this record daily. The number of positions opened from flat that day must be below an optional daily limit. A long requires a flat or short position; a short requires a flat or long position.
Long: Entry eligibility AND upward break type matches the selected type AND trend filter passes for long AND close > each enabled anchored VWAP AND initial stop < entry reference AND ((stop-entry mode AND active pivot high is reached by high) OR (close-entry mode AND close > active pivot high AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close > open AND absolute(close - open) >= (high - low - absolute(close - open)) × a configurable body-to-wick factor)))).
Short: Entry eligibility AND downward break type matches the selected type AND trend filter passes for short AND close < each enabled anchored VWAP AND initial stop > entry reference AND ((stop-entry mode AND active pivot low is reached by low) OR (close-entry mode AND close < active pivot low AND (strong-candle filter disabled OR (close < open AND absolute(close - open) >= (high - low - absolute(close - open)) × a configurable body-to-wick factor)))).
Exit: long, low <= saved initial stop OR high >= saved target OR (daily close enabled AND a new calendar day begins) OR short entry condition is met.
Exit: short, high >= saved initial stop OR low <= saved target OR (daily close enabled AND a new calendar day begins) OR long entry condition is met.
