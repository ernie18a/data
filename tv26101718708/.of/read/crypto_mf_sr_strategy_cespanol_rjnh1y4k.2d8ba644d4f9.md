Calculation: Active levels = Maintain a fixed-capacity ordered array of price levels, initially empty. On each bar, identify a candidate when the close a pivot lookback ago equals the highest or lowest close in the symmetric window centered on that bar. Reject the candidate if any active level has either (a) a swing failure on the pivot bar or the preceding bar: open and close strictly on one side of the level and the high or low strictly beyond the other side, or (b) a near test on the pivot bar or the following bar: open and close strictly on one side and the corresponding high or low is within an ATR-based percentage of the level. The near-test percentage is a configurable fraction of current ATR divided by current close. Insert an accepted candidate into the first empty slot. Then increment each level's break count when open and close are strictly on opposite sides of it. Remove a level and reset its count when its break count reaches the maximum or its absolute percentage distance from current close reaches a configurable multiple of the near-test percentage. Perform tests and breaks using the remaining levels, in array order; the last matching level determines the result for each kind of event.
Calculation: Level test = For each active level in array order, assign -1 if open < level AND high > level minus a configurable price tolerance AND close < level; assign 1 if open > level AND low < level plus that tolerance AND close > level. The result is the last assigned value, or 0 if none.
Calculation: Level break = For each active level in array order, assign 1 if open < level AND close > level; assign -1 if open > level AND close < level. The result is the last assigned value, or 0 if none.
Calculation: Technical rating = Average the available moving-average rating and oscillator rating. The moving-average rating is the average of votes for several increasing lookbacks of SMA and EMA of close, plus HMA and VWMA: each votes 1 when close is above it, -1 when below, otherwise 0. Include one additional Ichimoku vote: 1 when the first leading span > the second, close > first span, close < base line, previous close < conversion line, and close > conversion line; -1 when the second span > the first, close < second span, close > base line, previous close > conversion line, and close < conversion line; otherwise 0. Use unshifted spans, with increasing conversion, base, and second-span lookbacks. The oscillator rating is the average of available votes below; each votes 1 for its first condition, -1 for its second, otherwise 0. RSI: below lower threshold and rising; above upper threshold and falling. Stochastic: K and D below lower threshold and K crosses strictly above D; both above upper threshold and K crosses strictly below D. CCI: below lower threshold and rising; above upper threshold and falling. DMI: ADX above threshold and +DI crosses strictly above -DI; ADX above threshold and +DI crosses strictly below -DI. Awesome Oscillator: crosses above zero, or is positive on both current and previous bars and turns up from a previous-bar trough; crosses below zero, or is negative on both bars and turns down from a previous-bar peak. Momentum: rising; falling. MACD: above signal; below signal. Stochastic RSI: close below trend EMA, K and D below lower threshold, and K crosses strictly above D; close above trend EMA, K and D above upper threshold, and K crosses strictly below D. Williams %R: below lower threshold and rising; above upper threshold and falling. Bull/bear power: close above trend EMA and low minus a separate EMA is negative and rising; close below trend EMA and high minus that EMA is positive and falling. Ultimate oscillator: above upper threshold; below lower threshold. Strict crosses require the previous values to be strictly ordered oppositely, except zero crosses allow equality on the previous bar. Use the source's ultimate oscillator: weighted average of sums of close minus min(low, previous close) divided by sums of true range over short, medium, and long lookbacks, with weights long/short, long/medium, and 1, multiplied by 100.
