Calculation: Lower-timeframe inputs = close, high, low, volume, EMA of close, ATR, RSI, and SMA of volume from the last completed lower-timeframe bar; calculate indicators on lower-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Zone score = divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over a lower-timeframe lookback into five equal zones; assign a separate score to the zone containing the lower-timeframe close, with scores decreasing from the top zone to the bottom zone. Include the lower boundary in each zone except the bottom zone.
Calculation: Volume ratio = lower-timeframe volume / (lower-timeframe volume SMA + a positive denominator offset).
Calculation: Normalized price displacement = (lower-timeframe close - lower-timeframe close EMA) / (lower-timeframe ATR + the denominator offset).
Calculation: Liquidity gap score = use the first matching case: volume ratio above an upper threshold AND normalized price displacement above an upper positive threshold: strongest positive score; volume ratio above a lower threshold AND displacement above a lower positive threshold: weaker positive score; volume ratio above a balance threshold AND absolute displacement below the lower positive threshold: zero; volume ratio above the lower threshold AND displacement below the negative of the lower positive threshold: negative drain score; volume ratio above the upper threshold AND displacement below the negative of the upper positive threshold: strongest negative score; otherwise: negative low-liquidity score. The upper volume threshold exceeds the lower threshold, which exceeds the balance threshold. The upper displacement threshold exceeds the lower threshold.
Calculation: Liquidity trend score = clamp the weighted sum of (lower-timeframe volume change over a lookback / (lower-timeframe volume SMA over the same lookback + the denominator offset)) and (lower-timeframe close change over the same lookback / (lower-timeframe ATR + the denominator offset)) to symmetric score limits. The two components have equal weights. Assign scores in descending order using four descending thresholds: strongest positive, weaker positive, zero, negative, and strongest negative.
Calculation: Reversal score = sum three positive weights when their respective conditions hold: lower-timeframe RSI is above an upper extreme or below a lower extreme; lower-timeframe close is at or beyond either outer zone boundary; liquidity gap score is above the weaker positive score or below the negative drain score. Assign a reversal score in descending order using three descending thresholds: highest, medium, low, and lowest.
Calculation: Final score = clamp the mean of the zone score, liquidity gap score, and liquidity trend score minus a positive multiple of the reversal score to symmetric score limits.
Long: Final score > positive bias threshold.
Short: Final score <= negative bias threshold.
