Calculation: Reference levels = highs and lows of the last completed bars of two reference timeframes, one longer than the other, and the last completed ranges of three configurable sessions. Each reference timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Reset both sides' sweep and invalidation states when either reference timeframe starts a new bar or any completed session high changes. After resetting, invalidate the high side if close is above any available reference high, and invalidate the low side if close is below any available reference low; invalidation persists until the next reset. Mark each side as swept after its first sweep and keep it marked until the next reset.
Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low with the same configurable number of chart bars on each side, available only after the right-side bars have closed. Maintain the most recently confirmed high and low independently.
Calculation: Delivery setup = maintain a pending setup independently for each side. A bullish setup starts at the open of a down-close bar whose low is below the latest confirmed swing low, or below the previous bar's low when the swing filter is disabled. A bearish setup starts at the open of an up-close bar whose high is above the latest confirmed swing high, or above the previous bar's high when the swing filter is disabled. Consecutive same-direction bars extend the setup without changing its starting bar or level. A later qualifying sweep that is not consecutive replaces the pending setup. Expire a setup when its age from its starting bar exceeds its configurable lifetime. A confirmation clears both pending setups; evaluate bearish confirmation before bullish confirmation on each bar.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish zone from the high two bars ago to the current low when current low is above that high, or a bearish zone from the current high to the low two bars ago when current high is below that low. Retain a configurable maximum number of zones, removing the oldest first. A bullish zone inverts to bearish when close is below its bottom; a bearish zone inverts to bullish when close is above its top. After inversion, remove it on the first later bar that overlaps the zone, or when close passes its opposite boundary before that overlap.
Calculation: Order block = on the first close above an unused latest confirmed swing high, create a bullish zone spanning the high and low of the most recent earlier down-close bar, provided that bar is within a configurable lookback. On the first close below an unused latest confirmed swing low, create a bearish zone spanning the most recent earlier up-close bar under the same lookback rule. Mark the swing used even if no source bar qualifies; a new confirmed swing resets its side's used state. Retain a configurable maximum number of blocks, removing the oldest first. With breaker conversion enabled, a bullish block becomes bearish when close is below its bottom, and a bearish block becomes bullish when close is above its top. Remove a breaker on a later bar overlapping its zone or on a close beyond its invalidation boundary while unmitigated. With conversion disabled, remove an invalidated order block.
Long: The low side is neither invalidated nor already swept AND open is above any reference low AND low is below that same reference low AND close is above it.
Short: The high side is neither invalidated nor already swept AND open is below any reference high AND high is above that same reference high AND close is below it.
Long: A bullish delivery setup is pending AND its age is within its lifetime AND close is above its level.
Short: A bearish delivery setup is pending AND its age is within its lifetime AND close is below its level.
Long: Low is above the high two bars ago.
Short: High is below the low two bars ago.
Long: A retained bearish fair value gap has not inverted AND close is above its top.
Short: A retained bullish fair value gap has not inverted AND close is below its bottom.
Long: Close is above the latest unused confirmed swing high AND the most recent earlier down-close bar is within the order-block source lookback.
Short: Close is below the latest unused confirmed swing low AND the most recent earlier up-close bar is within the order-block source lookback.
Long: Breaker conversion is enabled AND a retained bearish order block has not become a breaker AND close is above its top.
Short: Breaker conversion is enabled AND a retained bullish order block has not become a breaker AND close is below its bottom.
