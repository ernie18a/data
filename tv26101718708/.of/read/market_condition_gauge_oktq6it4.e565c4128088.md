Calculation: Signed normalization(x, maximum) = clamp(50 + 50 × x / maximum, 0, 100) if maximum > 0, otherwise 50.
Calculation: Composite score = EMA of the weighted mean of RSI, signed normalization of MACD histogram by its highest absolute value over a lookback, signed normalization of 100 × (close − EMA(close)) / EMA(close) by its highest absolute value over the same lookback, clamp(50 × volume / SMA(volume), 0, 100), and clamp(50 × ATR / SMA(ATR), 0, 100). Use volume ratio or ATR ratio of 1 when its denominator is not positive; use trend percentage of 0 when its EMA denominator is 0; use a raw weighted mean of 50 when total weight is not positive. The MACD fast EMA length is shorter than its slow EMA length; the ATR average uses the ATR length.
Calculation: Score slope = composite score − previous bar's composite score.
Calculation: Trend stretch = 100 × absolute trend percentage / highest absolute trend percentage over the normalization lookback, or 0 when that maximum is not positive.
Calculation: Chop = ADX < chop threshold AND composite score > bear-bias threshold AND composite score < bull-bias threshold AND absolute score slope <= flat-slope threshold.
Calculation: Bull stretch = composite score >= strong-bull threshold AND score slope <= 0 AND RSI >= bullish stretch RSI threshold AND trend stretch >= stretch threshold.
Calculation: Bear stretch = composite score <= strong-bear threshold AND score slope >= 0 AND RSI <= bearish stretch RSI threshold AND trend stretch >= stretch threshold.
Long: composite score >= strong-bull threshold OR composite score > bull-bias threshold OR bull stretch OR (composite score >= 50 AND chop = false AND bull stretch = false AND bear stretch = false).
Short: (composite score < strong-bull threshold AND composite score <= bull-bias threshold AND (composite score <= strong-bear threshold OR composite score < bear-bias threshold)) OR bear stretch OR (composite score < 50 AND chop = false AND bull stretch = false AND bear stretch = false).
