Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured left and right lookback; use confirmed bars on each enabled source timeframe (higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe).
Calculation: Zone band = pivot level ± a configurable half-width.
Calculation: Zone grade = weighted blend of source-timeframe rank, number of distinct timeframes contributing to the zone, rejection-wick size, and pivot prominence; merge overlapping same-side zones within a configurable tolerance.
Long: Price touches a strong or elite support zone and rejects upward on the first touch.
Long: Price closes above a resistance zone, then retests and holds it as support.
Short: Price touches a strong or elite resistance zone and rejects downward on the first touch.
Short: Price closes below a support zone, then retests and holds it as resistance.
Exit: Long, price closes below the entry support zone's lower edge OR reaches the nearest opposing resistance zone.
Exit: Short, price closes above the entry resistance zone's upper edge OR reaches the nearest opposing support zone.
