Calculation: Previous session high and low = high and low of the last completed configured session, updated when the next configured session begins; the session is a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Current day high and low = high and low so far in the still-forming higher-timeframe day.
Calculation: Sweep state = set the low-sweep state when chart-bar low < previous session low and the high-sweep state when chart-bar high > previous session high; reset both at the start of each day and reset each separately after its corresponding entry signal.
Calculation: Tracked trade state = set the corresponding side and entry price on each entry signal; clear it on a stop exit or at the start of a new day. Same-side signals replace the tracked entry and stop.
Calculation: Long stop = current day low at the entry signal minus a price buffer; initial risk = entry price minus this stop. If trailing is enabled, on each bar whose high >= entry price + initial risk × activation multiple and which confirms a pivot low, raise the stop to the maximum of its previous value and the confirmed pivot low minus ATR × buffer multiplier. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand chart bars and is lower than the configured surrounding left and right bars.
Calculation: Short stop = current day high at the entry signal plus a price buffer; initial risk = this stop minus entry price. If trailing is enabled, on each bar whose low <= entry price - initial risk × activation multiple and which confirms a pivot high, lower the stop to the minimum of its previous value and the confirmed pivot high plus ATR × buffer multiplier. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand chart bars and is higher than the configured surrounding left and right bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, low-sweep state is set AND close > previous session low AND previous chart-bar close <= current previous session low AND (rejection filter disabled OR close > (high + low) / 2) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured trading session) AND tracked trade side is not short AND (one-long-per-day filter disabled OR no long signal has occurred this day) AND close - (current day low - price buffer) > minimum price increment.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, high-sweep state is set AND close < previous session high AND previous chart-bar close >= current previous session high AND (rejection filter disabled OR close < (high + low) / 2) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured trading session) AND tracked trade side is not long AND (one-short-per-day filter disabled OR no short signal has occurred this day) AND (current day high + price buffer) - close > minimum price increment.
Exit: Long, tracked trade side is long AND low <= long stop after any update on the current bar.
Exit: Short, tracked trade side is short AND high >= short stop after any update on the current bar.
