Calculation: Source bars = chart bars or bars aggregated to a selected higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe, evaluate its last completed bar once when it becomes available on the chart. Apply an optional session filter using each evaluated bar's opening time in the selected New York session.
Calculation: Pivot levels = the high and low of the latest confirmed pivot-low candle and the latest confirmed pivot-high candle, each pivot using equal left and right lookbacks. Replace each stored candle when a newer pivot of that type is confirmed; clear the pivot-low candle after a source close crosses above its high and clear the pivot-high candle after a source close crosses below its low, provided the session filter passes. A crossover requires the previous close to be at or below the previous stored level; a crossunder requires it to be at or above the previous stored level.
Calculation: Swing sequence = source pivot highs and lows using a separate left lookback and one right bar, confirmed in chronological order. Keep alternating swing directions; replace the latest swing of the same direction only if the new high is higher or the new low is lower. For each direction, D is the swing of that direction at index 2 if it has that direction, otherwise index 1, counting the latest swing as index 0; C is the preceding older swing and E the following newer swing. Maintain a direction state initially neutral, set to bullish whenever an in-session close exceeds bullish D while the state is not bullish, and set to bearish whenever an in-session close falls below bearish D while the state is not bearish, even if the remaining pattern conditions fail.
Calculation: Breaker zone = in pivot mode, the full high-to-low range of the corresponding stored pivot candle; in swing-run mode, the range of the latest consecutive run of bullish candles for a bullish zone or bearish candles for a bearish zone between D and C, scanned backward from the more recent endpoint within a search-depth limit. Optionally use candle bodies instead of full ranges for swing-run zones. A run must exist. Create a zone at a corresponding breaker event unless optional overlap prevention rejects it because its range overlaps an active same-direction zone. Keep at most a configurable number of active zones per direction, removing the oldest by origin time first. Remove a bullish zone when chart low falls below its bottom in pivot mode or chart close falls below its bottom in swing-run mode; remove a bearish zone when chart high rises above its top in pivot mode or chart close rises above its top in swing-run mode. Process creation before retest and invalidation on each chart bar.
Calculation: Retest eligibility = an active breaker zone has not previously produced a retest signal or been cancelled. Before invalidation, cancel an eligible bullish zone when chart close is strictly between its bottom and midpoint, or a bearish zone when chart close is strictly between its midpoint and top. A retest signal takes precedence over cancellation on the same bar and makes that zone ineligible for further retest signals.
Calculation: Chart FVG rank = maintain separate rolling samples by time-of-day bucket: chart volume for every bar and positive three-candle gap heights for confirmed gap bars, inserting the current observation before testing. For the middle candle's time bucket, calculate the population mean and standard deviation of each sample. Gap Z is the current gap height minus that bucket's gap mean, divided by its gap standard deviation; volume Z is the middle candle's volume minus that bucket's volume mean, divided by its volume standard deviation. Use zero for Z when the standard deviation is zero. Each enabled rank filter passes when its Z is at least its selected threshold and its tested gap height or volume is positive; a disabled filter passes unconditionally.
Long: Pivot mode AND source session filter passes AND source close crosses above the stored pivot-low candle high.
Short: Pivot mode AND source session filter passes AND source close crosses below the stored pivot-high candle low.
Long: Swing-run mode AND source session filter passes AND source close exceeds bullish D AND the prior direction state is not bullish AND bullish E is below bullish C AND C and D have different times AND a bullish candle run exists between D and C within the search-depth limit.
Short: Swing-run mode AND source session filter passes AND source close is below bearish D AND the prior direction state is not bearish AND bearish E is above bearish C AND C and D have different times AND a bearish candle run exists between D and C within the search-depth limit.
Long: Source session filter passes AND source close is below the stored pivot-low candle high AND at or above that high minus a selected multiple of source ATR AND this complete condition was false on the preceding source bar.
Short: Source session filter passes AND source close is above the stored pivot-high candle low AND at or below that low plus a selected multiple of source ATR AND this complete condition was false on the preceding source bar.
Long: Source session filter passes AND source low is above source high two bars earlier AND, when source and chart timeframes match and ranking is enabled, both enabled chart FVG rank filters pass.
Short: Source session filter passes AND source high is below source low two bars earlier AND, when source and chart timeframes match and ranking is enabled, both enabled chart FVG rank filters pass.
Long: Source and chart timeframes differ AND chart session filter passes AND chart low is above chart high two bars earlier AND, if ranking is enabled, both enabled chart FVG rank filters pass.
Short: Source and chart timeframes differ AND chart session filter passes AND chart high is below chart low two bars earlier AND, if ranking is enabled, both enabled chart FVG rank filters pass.
Long: An eligible bullish breaker zone exists AND chart open is strictly above its midpoint and below its top AND chart close is above its top.
Short: An eligible bearish breaker zone exists AND chart open is strictly below its midpoint and above its bottom AND chart close is below its bottom.
