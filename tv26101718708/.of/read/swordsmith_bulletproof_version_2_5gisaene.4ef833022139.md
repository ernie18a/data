Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low within the configured New York opening window, reset each day and retained after the window closes; its midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high for bullish, or below the latest confirmed pivot low for bearish; pivots use equal left and right lookbacks, and the previous close is compared with the current latest pivot level.
Calculation: Order block = on a structure break, use the high and low of the most recent opposite-colored candle within a configured preceding lookback; retain the previous block if none is found. Deactivate a bullish block when close falls below its low plus a configured fraction of its range, or a bearish block when close rises above its high minus that fraction of its range.
Calculation: Fair value gap = on low > high two bars earlier, store those prices as the bullish upper and lower bounds; on high < low two bars earlier, store those prices as the bearish lower and upper bounds. Replace the previous gap of the same direction when a new one forms; its age is bars since formation.
Calculation: Smart trail = initialize to close and direction to neutral. On each bar, if close > previous trail, set trail to max(previous trail, close minus an ATR multiple) and direction bullish; if close < previous trail, set trail to min(previous trail, close plus the ATR multiple) and direction bearish; otherwise retain both.
Calculation: Divergence = at each confirmed RSI pivot, store that RSI pivot and the price pivot confirmed on the same bar, including an unavailable price pivot. Bearish divergence requires the latest two stored price highs to rise and their RSI highs to fall; bullish divergence requires the latest two stored price lows to fall and their RSI lows to rise. RSI and price pivots use the structure-pivot lookback.
Calculation: Directional score = sum the enabled components: one point for the higher-timeframe trend; a configured weight for the corresponding structure break; one point each for the corresponding liquidity sweep, active order-block interaction, recent fair-value-gap interaction, volume flow, smart-trail direction, momentum, and enabled session on an enabled weekday. Bullish order-block interaction requires low <= block high AND close >= block low; bearish interaction requires high >= block low AND close <= block high. Gap interaction requires close within the latest same-direction gap AND gap age <= its configured maximum. Bullish volume flow requires volume > its SMA AND close > open AND, if enabled, OBV > OBV at a configured prior bar; bearish reverses the price and OBV comparisons. Bullish momentum requires RSI >= its threshold AND, if divergence filtering is enabled, no bearish divergence; bearish momentum requires RSI <= its threshold AND, if enabled, no bullish divergence. Session and weekday tests use New York time.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = shorter EMA > longer EMA AND chart close > shorter EMA for bullish, with both comparisons reversed for bearish. Calculate EMAs on the higher timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the still-forming bar in realtime.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = at the entry signal close, select an ATR multiple, distance to opening-range midpoint, distance to the opposite opening-range boundary, or a fixed distance; floor the selected distance at the minimum price increment and substitute the ATR distance if unavailable. Target prices are entry signal close plus or minus the initial stop distance multiplied by the respective configured target multiples; the second target uses the longer multiple.
Calculation: Active stop = initially entry signal close minus initial stop distance for long or plus it for short. Once a long bar's high reaches the first target, use entry signal close plus a configured offset, optionally raised to the smart trail if higher; once a short bar's low reaches the first target, use entry signal close minus the offset, optionally lowered to the smart trail if lower.
Long: close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that same pivot high.
Short: close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that same pivot low.
Long: low < lowest low of the preceding liquidity lookback AND close > that lowest low.
Short: high > highest high of the preceding liquidity lookback AND close < that highest high.
Long: low > high two bars earlier.
Short: high < low two bars earlier.
Long: smart-trail direction = bullish.
Short: smart-trail direction != bullish.
Long: higher-timeframe trend = bullish.
Short: higher-timeframe trend = bearish.
Long: bullish directional score >= configured minimum.
Short: bearish directional score >= configured minimum.
Long: position side = flat AND long trading is enabled AND opening range is set AND close > opening-range high AND New York time < cutoff AND current weekday and session are enabled AND bullish directional score >= configured minimum AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend = bullish) AND (retest mode is disabled OR low <= opening-range high + configured fraction of opening-range width).
Short: position side = flat AND short trading is enabled AND opening range is set AND close < opening-range low AND New York time < cutoff AND current weekday and session are enabled AND bearish directional score >= configured minimum AND (higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe trend = bearish) AND (retest mode is disabled OR high >= opening-range low - configured fraction of opening-range width).
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= first target for the configured first-target quantity OR high >= second target for the remaining quantity OR New York time >= cutoff.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= first target for the configured first-target quantity OR low <= second target for the remaining quantity OR New York time >= cutoff.
