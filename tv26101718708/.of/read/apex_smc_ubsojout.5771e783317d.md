Calculation: Previous higher-timeframe high/low = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Major/minor swing pivots = confirmed pivot highs/lows using equal left and right lookbacks; the major lookback is longer than the minor lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = a bullish/bearish candle whose body exceeds an ATR-based threshold; this uses a longer ATR lookback than the short ATR lookback used by the trailing-stop direction.
Calculation: Trailing-stop direction = bullish when close > prior short stop, bearish when close < prior long stop, otherwise the prior direction; the long stop trails highs and the short stop trails lows by a short-lookback ATR offset, resetting when the prior close crosses its prior stop.
Long: For any displayed timeframe, close > EMA AND that timeframe's bar is the current bar (still forming on a higher timeframe; use the sampled bar on a lower timeframe)
Short: For any displayed timeframe, close < EMA AND that timeframe's bar is the current bar (still forming on a higher timeframe; use the sampled bar on a lower timeframe)
Long: (low <= lowest prior low over the short sweep lookback within the recent lookback AND close > open AND MACD histogram > prior MACD histogram AND (trailing-stop direction is bullish OR bullish displacement) AND time since prior bullish scalp trigger > scalp cooldown, with an opposite bearish scalp trigger resetting that timer) OR (low <= previous higher-timeframe low OR low <= a confirmed major swing low within the recent lookback AND close > open AND MACD histogram > prior MACD histogram AND (trailing-stop direction is bullish OR bullish displacement) AND time since prior bullish swing trigger > swing cooldown)
Short: (high >= highest prior high over the short sweep lookback within the recent lookback AND close < open AND MACD histogram < prior MACD histogram AND (trailing-stop direction is bearish OR bearish displacement) AND time since prior bearish scalp trigger > scalp cooldown, with an opposite bullish scalp trigger resetting that timer) OR (high >= previous higher-timeframe high OR high >= a confirmed major swing high within the recent lookback AND close < open AND MACD histogram < prior MACD histogram AND (trailing-stop direction is bearish OR bearish displacement) AND time since prior bearish swing trigger > swing cooldown)
