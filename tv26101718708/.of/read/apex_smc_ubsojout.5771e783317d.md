Calculation: Trailing direction = initialize direction to long. On each bar, set the long stop to max(high − ATR with a single-bar lookback × multiplier, previous long stop), using current low when the previous stop is unavailable; instead set it to high − ATR with a single-bar lookback × multiplier if previous close is below the previous long stop. Set the short stop to min(low + ATR with a single-bar lookback × multiplier, previous short stop), using current high when the previous stop is unavailable; instead set it to low + ATR with a single-bar lookback × multiplier if previous close is above the previous short stop. Set direction to long if close is above the previous short stop, otherwise short if close is below the previous long stop, otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND close − open > ATR × multiplier.
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND open − close > ATR × multiplier.
Calculation: Scalp long trigger = low has been at or below the lowest low of the preceding lookback within the recent-event window AND close > open AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram AND (trailing direction = long OR bullish displacement) AND bars since the last scalp long trigger > scalp cooldown. Initialize both scalp trigger timestamps to a negative bar-index sentinel. After a scalp long trigger, record its bar index and reset the scalp short timestamp to the sentinel; after a scalp short trigger, record its bar index and reset the scalp long timestamp to the sentinel. Evaluate both triggers before updating either timestamp.
Calculation: Scalp short trigger = high has been at or above the highest high of the preceding lookback within the recent-event window AND close < open AND MACD histogram < previous MACD histogram AND (trailing direction = short OR bearish displacement) AND bars since the last scalp short trigger > scalp cooldown.
Calculation: Swing long trigger = within the swing recent-event window, low has been at or below either the low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar or a major pivot low confirmed on that same chart bar AND close > open AND MACD histogram > previous MACD histogram AND (trailing direction = long OR bullish displacement) AND bars since the last swing long trigger > swing cooldown. Initialize the swing trigger timestamp to a negative bar-index sentinel and update it on each swing long trigger. The swing cooldown and swing recent-event window are longer than their scalp counterparts. Major pivots use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Swing short trigger = within the swing recent-event window, high has been at or above either the high of the last completed higher-timeframe bar or a major pivot high confirmed on that same chart bar AND close < open AND MACD histogram < previous MACD histogram AND (trailing direction = short OR bearish displacement) AND bars since the last swing short trigger > swing cooldown. Initialize and update its timestamp independently of the swing long timestamp.
Long: Scalp long trigger OR swing long trigger.
Short: Scalp short trigger OR swing short trigger.
Long: low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > previous open.
Short: high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < previous open.
Long: Close > EMA of close on any configured higher or lower timeframe. For a higher timeframe, historical bars use the last completed bar and the live bar uses the bar still forming; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar.
Short: Close <= EMA of close on any configured higher or lower timeframe, using the same bar-selection rules.
