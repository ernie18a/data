Calculation: Reference range = high and low of a selected last completed higher-timeframe bar: the previous trading day, a longer intraday period with either a session anchor or exchange anchor, or a shorter intraday period; alternatively, high and low accumulated over a specified New York morning window, retained until the trading-day reset. Reset a CRT setup whenever either reference boundary changes.
Calculation: CRT sweep state = for each reference range and direction, record the most extreme low below the range or high above it. At each new extreme, record the open of the first bearish or bullish candle, respectively, in its current same-direction candle run; if unavailable, use the corresponding range boundary. Cancel that direction for the current reference range when the sweep depth exceeds a configurable fraction of the range. Each direction can signal once per reference range, even if a later filter rejects it.
Calculation: Strict CRT state = when a reference range changes, arm a previously swept, uncancelled direction if the preceding chart bar closed strictly inside the old range. Retain the old range and sweep extreme. Disarm a long if a subsequent low breaks the saved low, or a short if a subsequent high breaks the saved high; disarm after its first signal. The morning-window setup also resets on a new trading day, and its reference range is cleared at a specified later New York time.
Calculation: Opening range = high and low accumulated over a configurable New York opening window, retained until the trading-day reset. Each direction can signal once per day, even if a later filter rejects it.
Calculation: Regime = either disabled, chart-timeframe ribbon direction, or ADX and three EMAs on the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Ribbon up means the shorter EMA exceeds the longer EMA; ribbon down includes equality. ADX regime up requires ADX at least its threshold and shortest EMA > middle EMA > longest EMA; down requires ADX at least its threshold and shortest EMA < middle EMA < longest EMA. Otherwise the ADX regime is neutral.
Calculation: Candidate selection = evaluate enabled CRT models in order from the daily range to the longer intraday range, morning-window range, and shorter intraday range, followed by the opening range. Within each model prefer long over short when both signal. Reject CRT candidates against the regime and require opening-range candidates to agree with a non-disabled regime; select the first remaining candidate before applying the other entry filters. A candidate consumed by a signal is not restored if it is rejected or another candidate wins selection.
Calculation: Entry risk = for CRT, the greater of the minimum stop distance and the distance from close to the sweep extreme plus a configurable buffer beyond that extreme; for the opening range, the greater of the minimum stop distance and the absolute distance from close to the opening-range midpoint. Entry is at close and the initial stop is entry minus risk for a long or entry plus risk for a short.
Calculation: Targets = for CRT, midpoint, opposite range boundary, and a configurable range-width extension beyond that boundary. If the midpoint is positive but less than a configurable risk multiple away and the shift option is enabled, use the opposite boundary and then one and two extension increments beyond it; if the extension parameter is zero, use a configurable positive fallback extension for the shifted targets. For the opening range, use three configurable risk multiples in the position direction. Save risk, stop and targets at entry.
Calculation: Trade management = start on the bar after entry. Check the existing stop first, fixed third target second, and time exit third. Otherwise, on reaching the first target close one third, then on reaching the second target close another third, including both on the same bar. Optionally move the stop to entry after the first target; after the second target, optionally move it to the first target, otherwise move it no worse than entry if breakeven management is enabled. In trailing mode after the second target, update the stop each bar to the more protective of its existing value and close minus ATR times a configurable multiplier for a long, or close plus that amount for a short; the updated stop applies from the next bar. A fixed third-target hit closes the entire remaining position and credits all three targets even if earlier targets were not recorded as hit. Count a loss when the realized, fraction-weighted return after configurable trading costs is negative; reset daily trade and loss counts on a new trading day.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = chart bar is confirmed, New York weekday and time are within the configurable trading window and outside the optional news window, no position is open or closed on this bar, and daily trade and loss counts are below their limits. Also require candle high minus low <= a configurable multiple of the preceding chart-bar ATR, optional current ATR <= a configurable multiple of its longer-lookback SMA, risk <= maximum stop distance, and risk-based position size >= one unit. If enabled and the previous completed daily midpoint exists, require long close <= that midpoint or short close >= it. For CRT additionally require the midpoint to be profitable, reward to the opposite range boundary divided by risk >= its minimum, and, when the skip option is enabled, midpoint reward divided by risk >= its minimum.
Long: Entry eligibility AND selected CRT candidate has a low sweep not cancelled AND either (fast mode AND close > range low AND close > open) OR (CISD mode AND close > max(range low, saved bearish-run open) AND close > open) OR (strict mode AND long state is armed AND close > saved range low AND close < saved range midpoint).
Short: Entry eligibility AND selected CRT candidate has a high sweep not cancelled AND either (fast mode AND close < range high AND close < open) OR (CISD mode AND close < min(range high, saved bullish-run open) AND close < open) OR (strict mode AND short state is armed AND close < saved range high AND close > saved range midpoint).
Long: Entry eligibility AND selected opening-range candidate AND close > opening-range high.
Short: Entry eligibility AND selected opening-range candidate AND close < opening-range low.
Long: Shorter chart-timeframe EMA > longer chart-timeframe EMA.
Short: Shorter chart-timeframe EMA <= longer chart-timeframe EMA.
Long: Close <= midpoint of the last completed higher-timeframe daily range.
Short: Close > midpoint of the last completed higher-timeframe daily range.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND low <= current stop OR fixed-target mode AND high >= third target OR New York time is within the configured end-of-day exit window OR a new trading day begins.
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND high >= current stop OR fixed-target mode AND low <= third target OR New York time is within the configured end-of-day exit window OR a new trading day begins.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND low > current stop AND fixed third target is not hit AND time exit is not due AND high >= the next unhit first or second target; exit one third per target reached.
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND high < current stop AND fixed third target is not hit AND time exit is not due AND low <= the next unhit first or second target; exit one third per target reached.
