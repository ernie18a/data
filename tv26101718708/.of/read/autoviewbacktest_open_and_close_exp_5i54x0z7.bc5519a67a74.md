Calculation: Open and close averages = equal-length simple moving averages of chart-timeframe open and close. If alternate resolution is enabled, sample each average at the end of each higher-timeframe bar and propagate that final value to every chart bar within it, including while the higher-timeframe bar is still forming in a historical backtest.
Calculation: Signal state = initially flat; when flat, set to long or short on the first bar of the corresponding enabled, in-period crossover signal, provided neither close signal was active on the previous bar. Reset to flat after a close signal. If trade inversion is enabled, exchange the resulting long and short entry decisions without changing the signal state.
Calculation: Position high and position low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, since the signal state entered its current direction, including the current bar. If Heikin-Ashi is enabled, use Heikin-Ashi high and low; if Renko mode is enabled, use the maximum and minimum of the selected candle open and close instead.
Long: Signal state changes from flat to long AND trade inversion is disabled AND long trading is enabled AND bar time is within the test period; OR signal state changes from flat to short AND trade inversion is enabled AND long trading is enabled AND bar time is within the test period.
Short: Signal state changes from flat to short AND trade inversion is disabled AND short trading is enabled AND bar time is within the test period; OR signal state changes from flat to long AND trade inversion is enabled AND short trading is enabled AND bar time is within the test period.
Exit: long, long trading is enabled AND signal state is long AND no long entry decision occurs on this bar AND bar time is within the test period AND (take profit is enabled AND position high >= entry-signal close * (1 + take-profit fraction) AND selected low <= position high * (1 - trailing-profit fraction); OR stop loss is enabled AND selected low <= entry-signal close * (1 - stop-loss fraction); OR trailing stop is enabled AND selected low <= position high * (1 - trailing-stop fraction); OR trailing stop and stop loss are both disabled AND open average crosses above close average). The trailing-profit fraction is capped at the take-profit fraction.
Exit: short, short trading is enabled AND signal state is short AND no short entry decision occurs on this bar AND bar time is within the test period AND (take profit is enabled AND position low <= entry-signal close * (1 - take-profit fraction) AND selected high >= position low * (1 + trailing-profit fraction); OR stop loss is enabled AND selected high >= entry-signal close * (1 + stop-loss fraction); OR trailing stop is enabled AND selected high >= position low * (1 + trailing-stop fraction); OR trailing stop and stop loss are both disabled AND open average crosses below close average). The trailing-profit fraction is capped at the take-profit fraction.
Calculation: Long crossover signal = open average crosses below close average AND bar time is within the test period. Short crossover signal = open average crosses above close average AND bar time is within the test period.
