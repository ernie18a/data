Calculation: Setup score = count of: close > 50-bar SMA > 150-bar SMA > 200-bar SMA; RSI(14) between its minimum and maximum; 5-bar average volume / 20-bar average volume >= its minimum and 20-bar average volume > 0; ATR(14) / close × 100 between its minimum and maximum; and close's percentage distance from the highest high of the last 252 bars between its minimum and maximum. Calculate on the selected scan timeframe, using its last completed bar; that timeframe's relation to the chart timeframe is configurable.
Long: Setup score >= its minimum AND the broad-market close > its 200-bar SMA when that filter is enabled AND the volatility index close < its threshold when that filter is enabled. Use the last completed bar on the selected regime timeframe, whose relation to the chart timeframe is configurable.
