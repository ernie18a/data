Calculation: Bollinger bandwidth = (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / Bollinger basis; use zero when the basis is zero.
Calculation: Squeeze = upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel AND lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel, where the Keltner basis is an EMA of close and its bands are that basis plus or minus an ATR multiple. Squeeze duration is the number of consecutive squeeze bars. A release occurs when the preceding bar is in a squeeze and the current bar is not. Post-squeeze starts on a release, lasts for a configured number of bars, and restarts on a new release.
Calculation: Upper-band failure = at least one close above its upper Bollinger Band within the head-fake lookback AND current close < upper Bollinger Band AND current close > Bollinger basis. Lower-band failure = at least one close below its lower Bollinger Band within that lookback AND current close > lower Bollinger Band AND current close < Bollinger basis.
Calculation: Directional candle quality = absolute close-minus-open divided by high-minus-low is at least a body threshold AND, for bullish quality, close > open and the close's fraction of the range measured from low is at least one minus a close-zone threshold; for bearish quality, close < open and that fraction is at most the close-zone threshold. For a zero-range bar, use zero for the body ratio and one-half for the close fraction.
Calculation: Volume expansion = current volume > previous volume AND previous volume > volume two bars ago times a volume persistence factor. Bandwidth expansion = current Bollinger bandwidth > previous bandwidth AND previous bandwidth >= bandwidth two bars ago times a bandwidth persistence factor. ATR expansion = ATR > its SMA times an ATR expansion factor.
Calculation: Directional score = clamp the sum of these components between zero and a score cap, using the same component weights for both directions: candle-quality points, otherwise smaller points if the candle closes in the direction; RSI points for exceeding the stronger directional threshold, otherwise smaller points for exceeding the midpoint; RSI acceleration points if RSI moves in the direction on each of the last two changes, otherwise smaller points if it moves in the direction on the latest change; volume-spike points if volume > its SMA times a spike factor, otherwise smaller points if volume > its SMA times a lower factor; volume-expansion points; OBV-slope points if OBV has moved in the direction over its slope lookback, otherwise smaller points if OBV is on the directional side of its SMA; MACD-acceleration points if the MACD histogram moves in the direction on each of the last two changes, otherwise smaller points if the MACD line is on the directional side of its signal line and the histogram is on the same side of zero; bandwidth-expansion points; ATR-expansion points; trend points if both current and previous closes are on the directional side of their Bollinger bases; post-squeeze points; and squeeze-duration points if the preceding bar's squeeze duration exceeds a high threshold, otherwise smaller points if it exceeds a lower threshold. Subtract wick-rejection points when the optional wick filter is enabled and the previous bar's opposing wick divided by its range exceeds a threshold, provided that range is positive; subtract head-fake points for an upper-band failure in the bullish score or lower-band failure in the bearish score. Directional side means above for bullish and below for bearish. The MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA plus a signal EMA.
Calculation: Money Flow Index = compute typical price as (high + low + close) / 3 and raw money flow as typical price times volume; sum raw money flow separately over the lookback for bars whose typical price rose and fell. Divide the positive sum by the negative sum, substituting a finite fallback ratio when the negative sum is zero, then transform the ratio with the standard Money Flow Index formula.
Long: close > upper Bollinger Band OR bullish directional score >= bearish directional score OR RSI > its midpoint OR OBV > its SMA OR Money Flow Index > its midpoint OR accumulation/distribution line > its SMA OR (ADX > its threshold AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator) OR (MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram >= zero) OR (shorter EMA > longer EMA AND close > shorter EMA) OR lower-band failure.
Short: close < lower Bollinger Band OR bearish directional score > bullish directional score OR RSI < its midpoint OR OBV < its SMA OR Money Flow Index < its midpoint OR accumulation/distribution line < its SMA OR (ADX > its threshold AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator) OR (MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram <= zero) OR (shorter EMA < longer EMA AND close < shorter EMA) OR upper-band failure.
