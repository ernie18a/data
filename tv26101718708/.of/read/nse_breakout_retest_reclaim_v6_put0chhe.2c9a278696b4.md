Calculation: VWAP = day-anchored (resets each day) volume-weighted average of (high+low+close)/3; VWAPUp = VWAP > previous bar's VWAP
Calculation: ATR = Wilder average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: BarRVOL = volume / simple average of volume over the last N bars including the current bar
Calculation: DayPaceRVOL = cumulative volume since the day's first bar / mean of cumulative volume at the same bar-of-day count on each of the previous several days (use the days available)
Calculation: Gap = (today's open - previous day's close) / previous day's close, using the daily timeframe (higher than chart), last completed daily bar for the previous day
Calculation: Levels = {PDH = previous day's high (higher-timeframe daily, last completed bar); ORshort = running max of high over the first n1 bars of the day, fixed after n1 bars; ORlong = same over the first n2 bars, n2 > n1; HighMultiDay = highest daily high of the previous M completed days, excluding today}
Calculation: Setup (bar s, level L) = no setup currently active AND bar-of-day count > k skipped opening bars AND Gap within [lower bound, upper bound] AND close > VWAP AND VWAPUp AND BarRVOL >= T_break AND DayPaceRVOL >= T_pace AND (high - low) <= c*ATR AND close > L AND previous close <= L for some L in Levels (if several cross, pick in priority order PDH, ORshort, ORlong, HighMultiDay). On Setup, set Zone = [L - a*ATR, L + a*ATR] using ATR at bar s (fixed afterwards); the setup stays active until entry, cancellation or day end
Calculation: Retest (bar r) = setup active AND no retest yet for this setup AND low <= Zone upper AND high >= Zone lower AND close >= Zone lower AND close >= VWAP (may be the setup bar itself; only the first qualifying bar counts); RetestHigh = high of bar r
Calculation: Cancel (clears the active setup, retest and Zone; day change also clears) = [bars since s > maxSetupBars AND no retest yet] OR [retest exists AND bars since r > maxRetestBars] OR [close < Zone lower] OR [close < VWAP]; evaluated each bar after the Long check
Long: setup active AND retest exists AND 1 <= bars since r <= maxRetestBars AND close > Zone upper AND close > RetestHigh AND BarRVOL >= T_reclaim (T_reclaim <= T_break) AND close > VWAP
