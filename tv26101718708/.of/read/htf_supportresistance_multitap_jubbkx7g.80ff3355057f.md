Calculation: Higher-timeframe resistance/support pivots = confirmed pivot highs/lows from bars higher than the chart timeframe, using left and right lookbacks; use only pivots confirmed by completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Support/resistance shelves = group same-side higher-timeframe pivots whose prices are within an ATR-based tolerance; retain shelves meeting the minimum touch count and use the selected active shelves. A shelf price is the midpoint of its upper and lower bounds.
Calculation: Prior-day high/low and prior-week high/low = high/low of the last completed daily or weekly bar, respectively.
Calculation: Asia/London high/low = highest high/lowest low during the respective session, usable for sweep entries only after that session ends.
Calculation: Synthetic bar delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1); use zero when volume is zero and use a close position of 0.5 when high equals low.
Calculation: Bar delta ratio = synthetic bar delta / volume, or zero when volume is zero.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = sum of synthetic bar delta from the latest qualifying support/resistance tap or sweep through the current bar, capped at a maximum lookback.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of chart-timeframe volume over a longer lookback than one bar.
Calculation: Displacement = current bar range / chart-timeframe ATR(14), current bar body / range, and close direction relative to open.
Calculation: HTF trend = close relative to an exponential moving average on a higher timeframe; use the latest available higher-timeframe values.
Long: A selected support shelf is tapped (bar range intersects its midpoint within a threshold, but the previous bar did not), or a qualifying support level is swept (low below prior-day low, prior-week low, or completed Asia/London low and close back above it); within the allowed bars after the latest such event, chart-timeframe close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high plus a buffer; current bar is confirmed; close is within a configured trading session; bullish displacement passes (range at least a configured ATR multiple, body/range at least a configured minimum, and close above open when directional close is required); bullish delta passes (delta ratio at least a configured minimum, volume at least a configured multiple of average volume when required, and cumulative delta positive when required); optional HTF bias filter passes (HTF close above its EMA); and the prior long signal is outside its cooldown.
Short: A selected resistance shelf is tapped (bar range intersects its midpoint within a threshold, but the previous bar did not), or a qualifying resistance level is swept (high above prior-day high, prior-week high, or completed Asia/London high and close back below it); within the allowed bars after the latest such event, chart-timeframe close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low minus a buffer; current bar is confirmed; close is within a configured trading session; bearish displacement passes (range at least a configured ATR multiple, body/range at least a configured minimum, and close below open when directional close is required); bearish delta passes (delta ratio at most the negative configured minimum, volume at least a configured multiple of average volume when required, and cumulative delta negative when required); optional HTF bias filter passes (HTF close below its EMA); and the prior short signal is outside its cooldown.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop price OR high >= target price; stop is the configured structure lookback low or latest confirmed pivot low, adjusted below entry if needed, and target is entry plus risk multiplied by the configured reward/risk ratio.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop price OR low <= target price; stop is the configured structure lookback high or latest confirmed pivot high, adjusted above entry if needed, and target is entry minus risk multiplied by the configured reward/risk ratio.
