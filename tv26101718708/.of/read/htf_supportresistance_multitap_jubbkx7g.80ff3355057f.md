Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low more extreme than its specified numbers of preceding and following bars; it becomes available on the confirming bar. Structure-tag pivots use equal left and right lengths, independently of the equal-length breakout pivots. Shelf pivots use independently specified left and right lengths.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe inputs = primary and optionally secondary higher-timeframe bars; the secondary timeframe is shorter than the primary. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on realtime chart bars, use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. A shelf pivot's volume and ATR are taken from its pivot bar. Bias uses the primary higher-timeframe close and EMA.
Calculation: Shelves = maintain separate support and resistance shelves from confirmed higher-timeframe pivot lows and highs. For each new pivot, set merge distance to the lesser of its pivot-bar ATR multiplied by a tolerance and a maximum price distance; if ATR is unavailable or nonpositive, use the maximum distance. The maximum distance is at least one minimum price increment. Merge into the nearest same-side shelf whose midpoint is within that distance and whose last merged touch is sufficiently many chart bars earlier; expand its bounds to include pivot price plus and minus half the merge distance, increment touches, retain the maximum pivot volume, and combine timeframe membership. Otherwise create a shelf with those bounds and one touch. Discard oldest shelves beyond the storage limit. On each completed chart bar, change resistance to support when close exceeds its midpoint and support to resistance when close falls below its midpoint; retain a flipped flag.
Calculation: Active shelves = also maintain a bounded oldest-first list of higher-timeframe pivot prices, flipping their sides when chart close crosses them. On each chart bar, independently select a configurable candidate count of nearest distinct pivot prices above and below close, merging nearby prices and preferring a primary-timeframe price as anchor. For each selected pivot, use the nearest stored shelf with enough touches, midpoint on the same side of close, and within the merge distance; otherwise use a one-touch, zero-width shelf at the pivot. Assign its selected pivot side, combine timeframe membership and flip flags, and retain the greater pivot volume. Rank candidates per side either by proximity alone or by a score combining capped touches, primary/secondary timeframe membership, pivot volume relative to the maximum candidate volume, flip status, and ATR-normalized proximity. Select a configurable number of distinct highest-ranked shelves per side; ties retain the first encountered candidate. The score weights and caps are configurable parameters, with combined-timeframe membership weighted above primary-only membership and primary-only above secondary-only membership.
Calculation: Support or resistance arm = during an allowed New York time session, record the current bar when an active same-side shelf midpoint is within a configurable distance of the bar's high-low range but was outside that distance of the preceding bar's high-low range, using the current shelf midpoint for both tests. Alternatively, if enabled, arm support when low is below and close is above an unswept previous completed higher-period low or completed configured-session low; arm resistance when high is above and close is below the corresponding high. The higher periods are the previous day and, optionally, previous week; session extremes are from the most recently completed configured session. Each extreme can arm once until its value changes for previous-period levels or its session starts again for session levels. Sweeps are checked on completed bars and only during allowed trading sessions. A new arm replaces the prior same-side arm; a sweep takes precedence over a shelf tap on the same bar. Expire an arm after a configurable number of chart bars and clear it after a same-side entry.
Calculation: Synthetic delta = volume multiplied by twice the close's fractional position in the high-low range minus volume; use zero when the range is zero. Delta ratio is delta divided by volume, or zero when volume is nonpositive. Cumulative delta sums the most recent min(max(bars since the relevant arm, one bar), configurable cap) chart bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Breakout level = the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high plus a configurable price buffer for longs, or pivot low minus that buffer for shorts. A crossover requires current close above the current level and previous close at or below the previous level; a crossunder uses the opposite inequalities.
Calculation: Long stop = at entry, select either the lowest low over a configurable lookback of completed bars before entry or the latest confirmed breakout pivot low; if unavailable, fall back to the pivot low, then the entry-bar low. If the selected level is not below entry price, use the lesser of entry-bar low and entry price minus one minimum price increment. Subtract a configurable buffer. Long target = entry price plus a configurable reward-to-risk multiple of entry price minus stop. Short stop and target mirror this using the highest prior high or latest pivot high, an entry-bar high fallback, an upward buffer, and a target below entry.
Long: Current confirmed structure pivot high > previous confirmed structure pivot high.
Long: Current confirmed structure pivot low > previous confirmed structure pivot low.
Short: Current confirmed structure pivot high < previous confirmed structure pivot high.
Short: Current confirmed structure pivot low < previous confirmed structure pivot low.
Long: Primary higher-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: Primary higher-timeframe close < its EMA.
Long: Synthetic delta >= 0.
Short: Synthetic delta < 0.
Long: On a completed bar in an allowed trading session, close crosses above the breakout level AND a support arm is active AND the same-side entry cooldown has elapsed AND, if enabled, primary higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND, if displacement is enabled, ATR > 0 AND high - low >= an ATR multiple AND absolute body / range >= a body-ratio threshold AND, if directional close is enabled, close > open AND, if delta confirmation is enabled, delta ratio >= its threshold AND, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume >= a multiple of its positive moving average AND, if cumulative delta confirmation is enabled, cumulative delta > 0. Use body / range = 0 for a zero-range bar.
Short: On a completed bar in an allowed trading session, close crosses below the breakout level AND a resistance arm is active AND the same-side entry cooldown has elapsed AND, if enabled, primary higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND, if displacement is enabled, ATR > 0 AND high - low >= an ATR multiple AND absolute body / range >= a body-ratio threshold AND, if directional close is enabled, close < open AND, if delta confirmation is enabled, delta ratio <= minus its threshold AND, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume >= a multiple of its positive moving average AND, if cumulative delta confirmation is enabled, cumulative delta < 0. Use body / range = 0 for a zero-range bar.
Exit: Long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= long stop OR high >= long target).
Exit: Short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= short stop OR low <= short target).
