Calculation: Pivot high/low = bar whose high (low) is the highest (lowest) among a left-bar count of bars before and a right-bar count of bars after it; confirmed only after the right-bar count of bars has passed
Calculation: EQH level = when a newly confirmed pivot high is within a tolerance of the previous confirmed pivot high (absolute difference <= tolerance, tolerance = a fraction of ATR or a fixed price distance), the level is the higher of the two pivot highs. It is active from confirmation until swept, and only a limited number of the most recent active levels per side are kept
Calculation: EQL level = same as EQH level but with pivot lows, and the level is the lower of the two pivot lows
Long: (on a completed chart bar, for any active EQL level: low < EQL level AND close > EQL level; the level is then deactivated) OR (on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar: low < lowest low of the previous N higher-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar, AND close > that lowest low, AND NOT the short condition on the same higher-timeframe bar)
Short: (on a completed chart bar, for any active EQH level: high > EQH level AND close < EQH level; the level is then deactivated) OR (on a higher timeframe than the chart, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar: high > highest high of the previous N higher-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar, AND close < that highest high)
