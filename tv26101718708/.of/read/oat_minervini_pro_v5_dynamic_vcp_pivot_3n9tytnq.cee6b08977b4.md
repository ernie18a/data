Calculation: Relative strength = chart close / benchmark close, using the benchmark’s bar on the same chart timeframe.
Calculation: Trend score = count of 8 true conditions: close > medium-term EMA and long-term EMA; medium-term EMA > long-term EMA; long-term EMA > its value at a configurable lookback; short-term EMA > both medium- and long-term EMAs; close is at least a configurable percentage above the lowest low in the configurable 52-week lookback; close is within a configurable percentage of the highest high in that lookback; relative-strength filter passes; close > short-term EMA. The three EMA lookbacks are short-, medium-, and long-term, respectively. The relative-strength filter is selected from: relative strength > its value at the shorter lookback; that condition AND relative strength > its value at the longer lookback AND relative strength > its fast EMA; or those conditions AND fast relative-strength EMA > slow relative-strength EMA AND fast relative-strength EMA > its value at the configured slope lookback. The longer relative-strength lookback exceeds the shorter one; the slow EMA lookback exceeds the fast one.
Calculation: Relative-strength new high = relative strength > its highest value over the prior 52-week lookback; stock new high = current high > its highest high over that prior lookback.
Calculation: Setup score = count of: base forming; 40-bar range percentage > 20-bar range percentage > 10-bar range percentage; short-smoothed average ATR as a percentage of close < long-smoothed average ATR percentage × a configurable ratio; 10-bar range percentage <= a configurable maximum; short-smoothed volume average < long-smoothed volume average × a configurable ratio; close is between 0 and a configurable percentage below the active pivot; relative-strength score >= a configurable threshold. The range and smoothing lookbacks are long-, medium-, and short-term, respectively.
Calculation: Relative-strength score = count of: relative strength > its value at the shorter lookback; relative strength > its value at the longer lookback; relative strength > fast relative-strength EMA; fast relative-strength EMA > slow relative-strength EMA; fast relative-strength EMA > its value at a configurable lookback; relative strength is within a configurable percentage of its prior 52-week high; relative strength makes a new high while the stock does not.
Calculation: Active pivot = most recent confirmed swing high whose pivot age is within both the base lookback and maximum pivot age; confirm a swing high using configurable numbers of chart bars on its left and right. If enabled and no valid swing pivot exists, use the highest high over a configurable lookback of prior bars.
Long: Trend score changes from < 8 on the prior bar to 8; OR relative strength makes a new high while stock high does not, and this condition was false on the prior bar; OR VCP-ready condition changes from false on the prior bar to true, where trend score >= configurable minimum, setup score >= configurable minimum, a valid pivot exists or fallback is enabled, close > short-term EMA, and close is no more than a configurable percentage above short-term EMA; OR close > active pivot AND prior close <= active pivot AND volume >= long-smoothed volume average × configurable breakout ratio; OR benchmark close <= its lowest value over the prior 60 bars AND current low > stock's lowest low over the prior 60 bars AND relative strength > its value at the shorter lookback AND relative strength > fast relative-strength EMA, and this condition was false on the prior bar; OR close is above the active pivot and no more than a configurable percentage above it.
