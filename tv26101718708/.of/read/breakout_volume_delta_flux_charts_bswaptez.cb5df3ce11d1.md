Calculation: Swing levels = On the first chart bar of each new swing period, use completed chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe swing bars. A swing high is strictly higher, and a swing low strictly lower, than every bar in its respective configurable left and right windows. Add each newly confirmed swing price to its respective high or low level list, retaining only the most recent configurable number of levels per list, including broken levels. Each new level is unbroken. On each chart bar, test every unbroken level, including newly added levels; once price breaches it, mark it broken if the optional volume filter passes, otherwise remove it. Broken levels cannot trigger again.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish volume dominance = Sum volume separately for lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar whose close is respectively above or below their open; use the available intrabars of the chart bar still forming. Divide each sum by their combined sum; if the combined sum is zero, set each dominance to 0.5.
Long: Current high, in wick mode, or current close, in close mode, > any unbroken swing high level AND either the volume filter is disabled OR bullish volume dominance > the configured threshold.
Short: Current low, in wick mode, or current close, in close mode, < any unbroken swing low level AND either the volume filter is disabled OR bearish volume dominance > the configured threshold.
