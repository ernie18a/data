Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap = 100 × abs(shorter-period EMA of close − longer-period EMA of close) / longer-period EMA of close when the longer-period EMA is positive; otherwise 0. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. For gap expansion, compare the absolute EMA difference on that bar with the absolute difference a configurable number of higher-timeframe bars earlier.
Long: On the longer higher timeframe, the shorter-period EMA of close > the longer-period EMA of close AND close > the longer-period EMA of close, using the last completed bar.
Short: On the longer higher timeframe, the shorter-period EMA of close < the longer-period EMA of close AND close < the shorter-period EMA of close, using the last completed bar.
Long: Chart close > chart base-period EMA of close AND, if the optional strict long filter is enabled, chart fast-period EMA > chart medium-period EMA AND chart medium-period EMA > chart base-period EMA. The fast period is shorter than the medium period, which is shorter than the base period.
Short: Chart close < chart base-period EMA of close AND, if the optional strict short filter is enabled, chart fast-period EMA < chart medium-period EMA AND chart medium-period EMA < chart base-period EMA. The fast period is shorter than the medium period, which is shorter than the base period.
Long: In the single higher-timeframe mode, the shorter-period EMA of close > the longer-period EMA of close AND close > the longer-period EMA AND the shorter-period EMA > its value a configurable number of higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the absolute difference between the EMAs > its value a configurable number of higher-timeframe bars earlier. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: In the single higher-timeframe mode, the shorter-period EMA of close < the longer-period EMA of close AND close < the shorter-period EMA AND the shorter-period EMA < its value a configurable number of higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the absolute difference between the EMAs > its value a configurable number of higher-timeframe bars earlier. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
