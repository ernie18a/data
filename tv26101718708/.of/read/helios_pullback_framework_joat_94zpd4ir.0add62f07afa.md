Calculation: Trend bias = bullish when chart-timeframe fast ALMA > slow ALMA and normalized linear-regression slope > 0 and (HTF bias disabled or fast ALMA > slow ALMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar); bearish when all inequalities reverse; otherwise neutral. The fast ALMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow ALMA.
Calculation: Pullback depth = in bullish bias, (fast ALMA − low) / ATR; in bearish bias, (high − fast ALMA) / ATR; otherwise zero, bounded below by zero.
Calculation: Stretch = absolute distance between close and fast ALMA / ATR.
Calculation: Regression envelope = linear regression of close over its lookback, plus or minus 1.35 times the greater of regression-residual standard deviation and 0.55 ATR.
Calculation: Long score = capped weighted sum of bullish bias, bullish HTF bias, pullback depth, confirmed sweep below the latest confirmed swing low, lower-wick proportion, bullish impulse recovery, volume Z-score, compression, positive fast-ALMA slope, bullish close recovery within the bar, and session-gate status. A bullish impulse recovery means bullish bias, close above fast ALMA and open, and positive change in smoothed RSI phase.
Calculation: Short score = capped weighted sum of bearish bias, bearish HTF bias, pullback depth, confirmed sweep above the latest confirmed swing high, upper-wick proportion, bearish impulse rejection, volume Z-score, compression, negative fast-ALMA slope, bearish close recovery within the bar, and session-gate status. A bearish impulse rejection means bearish bias, close below fast ALMA and open, and negative change in smoothed RSI phase.
Long: On a confirmed bar, long score ≥ signal threshold AND stretch ≤ maximum stretch AND close > regression envelope lower boundary AND bullish trend bias AND long state was false on the prior bar AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the last long or short event.
Short: On a confirmed bar, short score ≥ signal threshold AND stretch ≤ maximum stretch AND close < regression envelope upper boundary AND bearish trend bias AND short state was false on the prior bar AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the last long or short event.
