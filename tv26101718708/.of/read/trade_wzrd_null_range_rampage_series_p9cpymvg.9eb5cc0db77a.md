Calculation: Range profile = over a lookback including the current bar, divide the range from lowest low to highest high into a fixed number of equal price bins; distribute each bar's volume across overlapping bins in proportion to the overlap with that bar's high-low range, excluding bars with missing volume or high equal to low. If total distributed volume is positive, equilibrium is the midpoint of the first bin whose cumulative volume reaches half the total, and deviation is the volume-weighted standard deviation of bin midpoints about their volume-weighted mean. Otherwise equilibrium is the range midpoint and deviation is a fixed fraction of the range. The range is valid when highest high > lowest low.
Calculation: Upper wall = equilibrium + deviation multiplied by the wall-width parameter; lower wall = equilibrium - deviation multiplied by the same parameter. Balance = (equilibrium - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low); when profile volume is zero, balance = one half.
Calculation: Lower trap = close > current lower wall AND previous close > current lower wall AND low < current lower wall AND the preceding run of consecutive bars with low below their respective lower walls <= trap-window parameter, OR close > current lower wall AND previous close < current lower wall AND the same preceding run <= trap-window parameter. Upper trap = close < current upper wall AND previous close < current upper wall AND high > current upper wall AND the preceding run of consecutive bars with high above their respective upper walls <= trap-window parameter, OR close < current upper wall AND previous close > current upper wall AND the same preceding run <= trap-window parameter.
Calculation: Long reclaim = close > equilibrium AND previous close <= previous equilibrium AND close - equilibrium > ATR multiplied by the reclaim-distance parameter. Short reclaim = close < equilibrium AND previous close >= previous equilibrium AND equilibrium - close > ATR multiplied by the same parameter.
Calculation: Trap depth = (lower wall - low) / ATR for a lower trap, or (high - upper wall) / ATR for an upper trap. Each raw trap belongs to a depth bucket defined by ordered depth thresholds; each raw reclaim belongs to an aligned or counter-aligned bucket according to whether balance is below one half for a long or above one half for a short.
Calculation: Bucket statistics = counts of completed tracked trades and target hits, maintained separately by side and by trap-depth or reclaim-alignment bucket; win rate is the rounded percentage of target hits. A bucket passes when its count is below the minimum-sample parameter OR its win rate >= minimum-win-rate parameter. Evaluate statistics before recording the current bar's exit. Every new signal replaces the tracked trade without recording an outcome; when a signal satisfies both trap and reclaim, track it as a trap. If stop and target are both hit on the same bar, record a stop.
Calculation: Long signal = valid range AND the selected signal model permits a lower trap or long reclaim that is present AND volume >= its SMA multiplied by the volume-gate parameter, with the gate passing when volume or its SMA is unavailable or the parameter <= zero AND bars since the last long or short signal > cooldown parameter, with the first signal passing AND the lower-trap bucket passes and trap depth <= maximum-depth parameter whenever a lower trap is present AND the reclaim bucket passes whenever either raw reclaim is present AND balance < long-alignment threshold when the alignment filter is enabled. If both raw reclaims occur, use the long reclaim bucket for the reclaim filter.
Calculation: Short signal = valid range AND the selected signal model permits an upper trap or short reclaim that is present AND the same volume gate and cooldown pass AND the upper-trap bucket passes and trap depth <= maximum-depth parameter whenever an upper trap is present AND the same reclaim filter passes whenever either raw reclaim is present AND balance > short-alignment threshold when the alignment filter is enabled AND long signal is false. The short-alignment threshold is lower than the long-alignment threshold.
Calculation: Entry stop and target = fix both at the signal bar. For a long, stop is the signal low or lower wall minus an ATR buffer; for a short, stop is the signal high or upper wall plus the same ATR buffer. Round to the price increment; if the long stop >= entry close or the short stop <= entry close, replace it with entry close minus or plus ATR respectively, rounded to the price increment. The selected target is equilibrium or the midpoint between equilibrium and the relevant wall if it is beyond entry by more than the reclaim-distance ATR parameter; otherwise, or when the risk-multiple target is selected, use entry plus the selected risk multiple of entry-to-stop distance for a long or entry minus that distance for a short. Round the target to the price increment.
Calculation: Normalized fuel = volume multiplied by absolute close-to-previous-close change, normalized to its lookback minimum and maximum; use zero when the value is unavailable or the maximum <= minimum.
Calculation: Buy pressure = SMA of volume on bars with close >= open and zero otherwise; sell pressure = SMA of volume on bars with close < open and zero otherwise, using the same lookback.
Long: long signal = true
Short: short signal = true
Long: valid range AND volume is available AND close >= equilibrium AND normalized fuel >= spike threshold
Short: valid range AND volume is available AND close < equilibrium AND normalized fuel >= spike threshold
Long: valid range AND volume is available AND normalized fuel >= spike threshold AND low < lower wall
Short: valid range AND volume is available AND normalized fuel >= spike threshold AND high > upper wall
Long: close < close at the pressure-shift lookback AND buy pressure > sell pressure AND buy pressure > buy pressure at the pressure-shift lookback
Short: close > close at the pressure-shift lookback AND sell pressure > buy pressure AND sell pressure > sell pressure at the pressure-shift lookback
Exit: long, short signal = true
Exit: short, long signal = true
Exit: long, bar index > entry bar index AND low <= entry stop OR bar index > entry bar index AND high >= entry target
Exit: short, bar index > entry bar index AND high >= entry stop OR bar index > entry bar index AND low <= entry target
