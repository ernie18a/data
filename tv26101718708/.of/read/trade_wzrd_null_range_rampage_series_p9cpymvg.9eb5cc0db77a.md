Calculation: Dealing range = highest high and lowest low over a lookback of chart bars; divide the range into 100 equal bins. Allocate each bar’s volume to each bin in proportion to the overlap between the bar’s high-low range and the bin. If close ≥ open, count that allocated volume as buy volume; otherwise count it as sell volume.
Calculation: Volume center = midpoint of the first bin where cumulative allocated volume reaches half of total volume; if volume is unavailable, use the dealing range midpoint. Center position = (volume center − range low) / (range high − range low) × 100.
Calculation: Volume deviation = square root of the volume-weighted variance of bin midpoints around their volume-weighted mean; if volume is unavailable, use one quarter of the dealing range width.
Calculation: Outer walls = volume center ± volume deviation × outer-wall multiplier.
Calculation: ATR = average true range. Average volume = simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Long trap extension = (lower wall − signal-bar low) / ATR. Short trap extension = (signal-bar high − upper wall) / ATR.
Calculation: Trap win probability = historical win percentage for the same direction and extension-depth bucket; reclaim win probability = historical win percentage for the same direction and whether the volume center is aligned (long: center position < 50%; short: center position > 50%). Buckets use configurable extension thresholds. A bucket with fewer than the required samples passes the probability filter; otherwise its win percentage must meet the minimum.
Calculation: Entry price = signal-bar close. Stop = configurable ATR buffer beyond either the signal-bar extreme or the outer wall; if that stop is on the wrong side of the close, use one ATR beyond the close. Target = configurable equilibrium, half-sigma, or fixed reward-to-risk target; use the selected target only when it is more than 0.2 ATR toward the target, otherwise use the fixed reward-to-risk target.
Long: (close > lower wall AND ((low < lower wall AND previous close > previous lower wall AND previous consecutive lower-wall breaches ≤ trap window) OR (previous close < previous lower wall AND previous consecutive lower-wall breaches ≤ trap window)) OR (close > volume center AND previous close ≤ previous volume center AND close − volume center > 0.2 ATR)) AND volume is unavailable, its average is unavailable, the volume gate is disabled, or volume ≥ average volume × volume gate AND bars since prior signal > cooldown AND applicable trap/reclaim win-probability filters pass AND (not requiring balance alignment OR center position < 55%) AND bar is confirmed.
Short: (close < upper wall AND ((high > upper wall AND previous close < previous upper wall AND previous consecutive upper-wall breaches ≤ trap window) OR (previous close > upper wall AND previous consecutive upper-wall breaches ≤ trap window)) OR (close < volume center AND previous close ≥ previous volume center AND volume center − close > 0.2 ATR)) AND volume is unavailable, its average is unavailable, the volume gate is disabled, or volume ≥ average volume × volume gate AND bars since prior signal > cooldown AND applicable trap/reclaim win-probability filters pass AND (not requiring balance alignment OR center position > 45%) AND bar is confirmed AND no long signal occurs on the same bar.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ target; if both are reached on the same bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ target; if both are reached on the same bar, exit at the stop.
