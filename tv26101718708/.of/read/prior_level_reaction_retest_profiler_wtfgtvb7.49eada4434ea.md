Calculation: Prior level = the high or low of the last completed bar of either a shorter or longer higher timeframe; track each timeframe and high/low separately, resetting its state at the start of each new source period after observing a source-period change.
Calculation: Frozen ATR = ATR of the last completed bar of the shorter higher timeframe, recorded at the first qualifying touch of a prior level in its current source period. A touch qualifies when ATR > 0 AND high >= a prior high or low <= a prior low AND, if the close-through filter is enabled, close > the prior high or close < the prior low.
Calculation: Historical breakout and reaction counts = for each prior-level series, evaluate each first touch starting on the following chart bar until the evaluation horizon or source-period end. For a prior high, breakout excursion = high - frozen level and reaction excursion = frozen level - low; for a prior low, breakout excursion = frozen level - low and reaction excursion = high - frozen level. Count a breakout or reaction only when its respective ATR-multiple threshold is reached before the other; count neither if both thresholds are reached on the same bar or neither is reached before expiry. Process the current event before registering a new touch.
Calculation: Pending retest = after a qualifying first touch, on that bar or while its event remains active, start one pending retest per level and source period when historical breakout count > historical reaction count AND close > a prior high or close < a prior low. Freeze the touched level and ATR. If volatility conditioning is enabled, require that the last completed shorter higher-timeframe ATR and its longer-lookback moving average are both available and positive. Cancel the pending retest at the next source-period boundary or when close < frozen high level - tolerance × frozen ATR or close > frozen low level + tolerance × frozen ATR. A zone touch occurs when high >= frozen level - tolerance × frozen ATR AND low <= frozen level + tolerance × frozen ATR. Record a zone touch only after the breakout bar. Cancel for expiry when bars since breakout > retest window, after checking cancellation by close and confirmation on that bar.
Long: A pending retest of a prior high exists AND the chart bar is after the breakout bar AND close >= frozen level - tolerance × frozen ATR AND (the current bar touches the zone OR an earlier bar after the breakout touched the zone) AND close > frozen level AND (the directional-candle filter is disabled OR close > open).
Short: A pending retest of a prior low exists AND the chart bar is after the breakout bar AND close <= frozen level + tolerance × frozen ATR AND (the current bar touches the zone OR an earlier bar after the breakout touched the zone) AND close < frozen level AND (the directional-candle filter is disabled OR close < open).
Exit: long, after the confirmation bar and before the next source-period boundary, high >= frozen level + breakout threshold × frozen ATR OR close < frozen level - tolerance × frozen ATR.
Exit: short, after the confirmation bar and before the next source-period boundary, low <= frozen level - breakout threshold × frozen ATR OR close > frozen level + tolerance × frozen ATR.
