Calculation: Lower wick = min(open, close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); body = abs(close − open); range = high − low; wick percentage = wick / range when range > 0, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = swing high or low with the same lookback on each side, available only after the right-hand bars have completed. Pattern pivots, failure-entry pivots, and zone pivots have independently configurable lookbacks. Use the latest confirmed pivot of the relevant type, including one confirmed on the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish price failure = low < latest pattern pivot low AND close > that pivot AND previous low >= that same pivot. Bearish price failure = high > latest pattern pivot high AND close < that pivot AND previous high <= that same pivot.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = close > open AND previous open > previous close AND close > previous open AND open < previous close. Bearish absorption = close < open AND previous open < previous close AND close < previous open AND open > previous close.
Calculation: Bullish wick trap = lower wick > body × wick-to-body threshold AND lower wick > ATR × wick-to-ATR threshold. Bearish wick trap = upper wick > body × wick-to-body threshold AND upper wick > ATR × wick-to-ATR threshold.
Calculation: Broken high = latest pattern pivot high saved when close crosses above that pivot; clear it after a bullish retest or when close < broken high. Broken low = latest pattern pivot low saved when close crosses below that pivot; clear it after a bearish retest or when close > broken low. A crossover compares both the current close and pivot with their previous-bar values.
Calculation: Zone score = weighted sum of pivot-bar body / range, capped pivot-bar volume / its volume SMA, and capped pivot-bar range / its ATR; use zero for body / range when range is zero and one for either ratio when its denominator is not positive; clamp the result to its allowed score range.
Calculation: Zones = on confirmation of a zone pivot, create a demand zone from the pivot low to pivot low + confirmation-bar ATR × zone-width multiplier, or a supply zone from pivot high − confirmation-bar ATR × zone-width multiplier to the pivot high, if the pivot-bar zone score > zero. Compare the new midpoint with existing same-side zones from newest to oldest; at the first midpoint distance < current ATR × separation multiplier, reject the new zone if the existing score >= its score, otherwise remove that existing zone. Add an accepted zone as fresh with zero tests; if the same-side creation limit is exceeded, remove the oldest zone. Process demand zones before supply zones, each newest first. On each bar increment a fresh demand zone's tests when low is within its boundaries, or a fresh supply zone's tests when high is within its boundaries. Its hold percentage is the rounded percentage (tests − 1) / tests when tests > zero, otherwise the full percentage. A confirmed sweep makes the zone non-fresh. If flipping is enabled, a fresh demand zone with close < its lower boundary becomes fresh supply, and a fresh supply zone with close > its upper boundary becomes fresh demand; retain its boundaries, score, and tests. Move flipped demand zones to the supply list before processing supply zones; move flipped supply zones to the demand list afterward. If a zone did not flip, remove it when the fresh-only setting is enabled and it is non-fresh, or when a demand zone's low > its upper boundary + ATR × distance multiplier, or a supply zone's high < its lower boundary − ATR × distance multiplier. Flipped zones are not subject to the creation limit when moved.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND previous close > previous open.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND previous close < previous open.

Short: Bearish absorption = true.

Short: Bearish price failure = true.
Long: Previous broken high exists AND close does not cross above the current pattern pivot high AND low <= broken high AND close >= broken high.
Short: Previous broken low exists AND close does not cross below the current pattern pivot low AND high >= broken low AND close <= broken low.
Long: Bullish wick trap = true.
Short: Bearish wick trap = true.
Long: Failure-entry mode = off AND a fresh demand zone exists AND (zone tests = zero OR zone hold percentage >= minimum hold percentage) AND low < zone lower boundary AND (zone lower boundary − low) / zone lower boundary >= minimum penetration when the boundary > zero, otherwise zero >= minimum penetration AND close > zone lower boundary AND (volume filter = off OR volume >= volume SMA × sweep volume multiplier) AND (wick filter = off OR lower wick percentage >= minimum sweep rejection) AND bars since the last demand sweep >= sweep cooldown AND (failure gate = off OR bullish price failure = true) AND (wick-trap gate = off OR bullish wick trap = true) AND (absorption gate = off OR bullish absorption = true).
Short: Failure-entry mode = off AND a fresh supply zone exists AND (zone tests = zero OR zone hold percentage >= minimum hold percentage) AND high > zone upper boundary AND (high − zone upper boundary) / zone upper boundary >= minimum penetration when the boundary > zero, otherwise zero >= minimum penetration AND close < zone upper boundary AND (volume filter = off OR volume >= volume SMA × sweep volume multiplier) AND (wick filter = off OR upper wick percentage >= minimum sweep rejection) AND bars since the last supply sweep >= sweep cooldown AND (failure gate = off OR bearish price failure = true) AND (wick-trap gate = off OR bearish wick trap = true) AND (absorption gate = off OR bearish absorption = true).
Long: Failure-entry mode = on AND low < latest failure-entry pivot low AND close > that pivot AND previous low >= that same pivot AND lower wick >= ATR × failure wick multiplier AND lower wick percentage >= minimum failure rejection AND (failure volume filter = off OR volume >= volume SMA × failure volume multiplier) AND bars since the last bullish failure entry >= failure cooldown.
Short: Failure-entry mode = on AND high > latest failure-entry pivot high AND close < that pivot AND previous high <= that same pivot AND upper wick >= ATR × failure wick multiplier AND upper wick percentage >= minimum failure rejection AND (failure volume filter = off OR volume >= volume SMA × failure volume multiplier) AND bars since the last bearish failure entry >= failure cooldown.
Calculation: Sweep backtest stop = entry close minus ATR × (zone-width multiplier + stop buffer) for a long or plus that amount for a short in zone stop mode; otherwise entry-bar low minus ATR × stop buffer for a long or entry-bar high plus ATR × stop buffer for a short. Failure backtest stop = entry-bar low or latest failure pivot low, according to stop mode, minus entry-bar ATR × failure stop buffer for a long; entry-bar high or latest failure pivot high plus that buffer for a short. Each target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, abs(entry price − stop) × the corresponding reward-to-risk multiplier. Freeze stop and target at entry.
Exit: long, high >= the applicable target OR low <= the applicable stop.
Exit: short, low <= the applicable target OR high >= the applicable stop.
