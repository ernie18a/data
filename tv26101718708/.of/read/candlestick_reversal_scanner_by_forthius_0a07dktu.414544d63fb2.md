Calculation: Direction-normalized bars = for long decisions use the original OHLC bars; for short decisions use open=-original open, high=-original low, low=-original high, close=-original close. Volume is unchanged. Apply all following calculations separately to each direction; index 0 is the current bar and index k is k bars ago.
Calculation: Candle measures = body B[i]=abs(close[i]-open[i]); range R[i]=high[i]-low[i]; lower wick L[i]=min(open[i],close[i])-low[i]; upper wick U[i]=high[i]-max(open[i],close[i]); body fraction F[i]=B[i]/R[i] if R[i]>0, otherwise 0. A bullish candle has close>open; a bearish candle has close<open. M=current close times a minimum-body percentage; T=ATR times a wick-tolerance percentage. All M and T references use their current-bar values.
Calculation: Down context = close < short-period EMA of close AND short-period EMA < long-period EMA of close AND (short-period EMA - its value a slope-lookback number of bars ago)/(ATR times slope lookback) < a negative slope threshold; OR the optional relaxed-context setting is enabled AND neither this downtrend nor its direction-reversed uptrend is present.
Calculation: Dragonfly = F[0] <= a doji threshold AND L[0] >= an ATR wick multiplier times ATR AND U[0] <= T AND R[0] >= an ATR range multiplier times ATR.
Calculation: Hammer shape = F[0] <= a body-fraction threshold AND L[0] >= the ATR wick multiplier times ATR AND U[0] <= max(a body-wick fraction times B[0], T) AND R[0] >= the ATR range multiplier times ATR. Inverted-hammer shape uses the same conditions with L and U exchanged.
Calculation: Engulfing = bearish candle[1] AND bullish candle[0] AND close[0] >= open[1] AND open[0] <= close[1] AND B[0] > an engulfing multiplier times B[1] AND B[0] > M AND B[1] > M.
Calculation: Morning star = bearish candle[2] AND B[2] > a large-body multiplier times M AND B[1]/max(R[1], epsilon) < a star-body threshold AND bullish candle[0] AND B[0] > the large-body multiplier times M AND close[0] > (open[2]+close[2])/2 AND, if true gaps are required, high[1] < low[2], otherwise open[1] < close[2].
Calculation: Three soldiers = bullish candles[0], [1], and [2] AND close[0] > close[1] > close[2] AND open[1] <= open[0] <= close[1] AND open[2] <= open[1] <= close[2] AND each of B[0], B[1], and B[2] > M.
Calculation: Piercing = bearish candle[1] AND B[1] > M AND bullish candle[0] AND, if true gaps are required, open[0] < low[1], otherwise open[0] < close[1] AND close[0] > (open[1]+close[1])/2 AND close[0] < open[1] AND B[0] > M.
Calculation: Harami = bearish candle[1] AND B[1] > the large-body multiplier times M AND bullish candle[0] AND max(open[0],close[0]) <= max(open[1],close[1]) AND min(open[0],close[0]) >= min(open[1],close[1]) AND B[0] < a harami-body fraction times B[1].
Calculation: Tweezer = bearish candle[1] AND bullish candle[0] AND abs(low[0]-low[1]) <= an ATR tolerance times ATR AND L[0] >= B[0].
Calculation: Eligible pattern = the first true item in this priority order: engulfing AND down context[1]; morning star AND down context[2]; three soldiers AND down context[3]; dragonfly AND down context[0]; hammer shape AND down context[0] AND NOT dragonfly; piercing AND down context[1]; harami AND down context[1]; tweezer AND down context[0]; inverted-hammer shape AND down context[0].
Calculation: Confluence = sum of one point each for volume > its SMA times a volume multiplier; current low within an ATR tolerance of the latest confirmed pivot low, whose left and right confirmation lookbacks are equal; RSI below a threshold; ADX above a threshold; and current low below the lowest low of the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND current RSI above the lowest RSI of those preceding bars. A missing volume average or pivot contributes zero. Compute RSI on the direction-normalized close; use ADX and ATR on the direction-normalized bars.
Calculation: Tier(x; lower, higher) = 2 if x >= higher, 1 if x >= lower, otherwise 0. Reverse-tier(x; lower, higher) = 2 if x <= lower, 1 if x <= higher, otherwise 0. Each named tier has its own ordered thresholds unless explicitly shared.
Calculation: Pattern quality = for the eligible pattern, capped at a maximum score: engulfing, Tier(B[0]/max(B[1],epsilon)) plus Tier((close[0]-low[0])/R[0], or 0 if R[0]<=0); morning star, reverse-tier(B[1]/max(R[1],epsilon)) plus Tier((close[0]-close[2])/max(open[2]-close[2],epsilon)); three soldiers, Tier(mean of F[0..2]) plus one point if mean of (close[i]-low[i])/R[i] over i=0..2, using 0 when R[i]<=0, is at least a threshold; dragonfly, Tier(L[0]/max(ATR,epsilon)) plus one point if U[0]<=T; hammer, the same wick tier plus one point if (min(open[0],close[0])-low[0])/R[0], or 0 if R[0]<=0, is at least a threshold, plus one point if U[0]<=max(a body fraction times B[0], an ATR fraction times ATR); piercing, a three-level score increasing with (close[0]-close[1])/max(open[1]-close[1],epsilon); harami, reverse-tier(B[0]/max(B[1],epsilon)) plus one point if B[1]/max(ATR,epsilon) >= a threshold; tweezer, reverse-tier(abs(low[0]-low[1])/max(ATR,epsilon)) plus one point if L[0]/max(B[0],epsilon) >= a threshold; inverted hammer, Tier(U[0]/max(ATR,epsilon)) plus one point if (high[0]-max(open[0],close[0]))/R[0], or 0 if R[0]<=0, is at least a threshold. The inverted-hammer cap is lower than the shared cap of the other pattern scores. The dragonfly, hammer, and inverted-hammer wick tiers share thresholds.
Calculation: Pattern volume score = Tier(volume[0]/max(reference volume,epsilon)) with shared volume-tier thresholds; reference volume is the current volume SMA for dragonfly, hammer, and inverted hammer; volume[1] for engulfing, piercing, harami, and tweezer; and mean(volume[1],volume[2]) for morning star and three soldiers. Use the same volume SMA as in confluence.
Calculation: Strength = pattern quality + confluence + pattern volume score. A separate last-signal bar is maintained for each direction, initially far enough in the past to allow a signal; update it only when that direction's full strength-filtered signal fires.
Long: Dragonfly AND down context[0] OR eligible pattern exists AND strength >= the selected minimum strength AND current bar index - last long-signal bar > the same-direction cooldown.
Short: Direction-normalized dragonfly AND direction-normalized down context[0] OR direction-normalized eligible pattern exists AND direction-normalized strength >= the selected minimum strength AND current bar index - last short-signal bar > the same-direction cooldown.
