Calculation: Intrabar buy share = for each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, choose close location (close-low)/(high-low), body direction (1 if close>open, 0 if close<open, otherwise 0.5), or a weighted blend of the two; clamp the result to [0,1]. Use 0 for close location when high=low.
Calculation: Bar buy volume = sum of lower-timeframe volume multiplied by buy share. Bar sell volume = sum of lower-timeframe volume multiplied by (1-buy share). Bar volume = bar buy volume + bar sell volume. Bar delta = bar buy volume - bar sell volume. Cumulative delta = running sum of bar delta from the first available chart bar.
Calculation: Footprint = divide the chart bar's low-to-high range into a configurable number of equal rows. For each completed lower-timeframe bar, allocate its volume, buy volume and sell volume to each touched row in proportion to the overlap of its high-low range with that row; allocate all of it to the row if only one row is touched. Number rows from zero at the low. Row volume is allocated total volume; row buy and sell are allocated buy and sell volume. The POC index is the lowest index with maximum row volume; POC location = (POC index + 0.5)/row count.
Calculation: Buy imbalance at row i = row buy at i > 0 AND row buy at i > imbalance ratio × max(row sell at i-1, 0.000000001), for i>0. Sell imbalance at row i = row sell at i > 0 AND row sell at i > imbalance ratio × max(row buy at i+1, 0.000000001), for i<row count-1.
Calculation: Longest imbalance run = scan rows from low to high, counting consecutive buy imbalances or consecutive sell imbalances; a buy imbalance takes precedence if both occur at a row. Retain the first run to reach the greatest length, its direction and its ending row index.
Calculation: Auction shape = B if the first and last rows with volume at least 0.70 × maximum row volume are at least three indices apart and at least two rows between them have volume below 0.35 × maximum row volume; otherwise P if POC location >= 0.62, b if POC location <= 0.38, and D otherwise.
Calculation: Candidate long = low < lowest low of the preceding extreme-lookback bars AND (mean-extension filter disabled OR low <= EMA(close, mean lookback) - extension multiplier × ATR) AND lower wick >= upper wick. Candidate short = high > highest high of the preceding extreme-lookback bars AND (mean-extension filter disabled OR high >= EMA(close, mean lookback) + extension multiplier × ATR) AND upper wick >= lower wick. If both candidates occur, select short.
Calculation: Conviction score = clamp(1.6 × wick + 1.8 × effort + 1.8 × trap + 1.4 × excess + 1.4 × shape + 1.4 × divergence + 0.6 × imbalance, 0, 10), multiplied by 0.55 before clamping when auction shape is B. Let range = high-low, body = abs(close-open), upper wick = high-max(open,close), lower wick = min(open,close)-low, midpoint = (high+low)/2, relative volume = bar volume / SMA(chart volume, volume lookback), and delta dominance = abs(bar delta)/bar volume. Effort = clamp((relative volume-1)/1.5,0,1) × clamp(1-body/range,0,1). For a short candidate, wick = clamp(upper wick/range/0.5,0,1); trap = clamp(delta dominance/0.5,0,1) if bar delta>0 AND close<midpoint, otherwise 0; excess = clamp(1-average volume of the highest two rows/average row volume,0,1); shape = clamp((POC location-0.5)/0.35,0,1) if auction shape is P, otherwise 0; divergence = 1 if cumulative delta < its highest value over the preceding divergence-lookback bars, otherwise 0; imbalance = clamp(longest imbalance run length/4,0,1) if its direction is sell AND its ending index >= floor(0.6 × row count), otherwise 0. For a long candidate, use lower wick; trap requires bar delta<0 AND close>midpoint; excess uses the lowest two rows; shape = clamp((0.5-POC location)/0.35,0,1) if auction shape is b, otherwise 0; divergence = 1 if cumulative delta > its lowest value over the preceding divergence-lookback bars, otherwise 0; imbalance requires a buy run ending at an index <= floor(0.4 × row count). If the volume average is unavailable, use bar volume for its denominator; division by zero returns 0.
Long: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND candidate long AND NOT candidate short AND conviction score >= score threshold AND auction shape != B AND bars since the last gated long or short signal >= cooldown.
Short: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND candidate short AND conviction score >= score threshold AND auction shape != B AND bars since the last gated long or short signal >= cooldown.
Long: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND buy imbalance at any row.
Short: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND sell imbalance at any row AND no buy imbalance at that same row.
Long: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND row buy - row sell >= 0 for any row.
Short: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND row buy - row sell < 0 for any row.
Long: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND close >= open.
Short: completed lower-timeframe data exists AND high>low AND bar volume>0 AND close < open.
Long: bar delta >= 0 OR cumulative delta >= 0.
Short: bar delta < 0 OR cumulative delta < 0.
