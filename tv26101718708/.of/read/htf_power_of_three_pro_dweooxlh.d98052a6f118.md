Calculation: Effective higher-timeframe open = the higher-timeframe bar's open, or, when the New York midnight option applies, the most recent chart-bar open at New York midnight as of the start of that higher-timeframe bar.
Long: The close of the forming or last completed higher-timeframe bar >= its effective open.
Short: The close of the forming or last completed higher-timeframe bar < its effective open.
Long: Chart-bar close < (forming higher-timeframe high + forming higher-timeframe low) / 2.
Short: Chart-bar close > (forming higher-timeframe high + forming higher-timeframe low) / 2.
