Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted mean of the selected bar price from the active anchor; rolling VWAP uses the selected-price and volume totals in its rolling window.
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted mean squared deviation of the selected bar price from VWAP; σ bands = VWAP ± k × standard deviation, using successively larger k values for bands 1, 2, and 3.
Calculation: Bar delta = buying volume − selling volume, estimated by summing lower-timeframe bar volume as positive when that bar closes above its open, negative when it closes below its open, and otherwise by its close versus the previous close; use completed lower-timeframe bars. Higher-timeframe delta = sum of bar delta within the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Same-time-of-day relative volume = current bar volume divided by average volume at that time over recent sessions.
Calculation: EMA trend references = 233-period chart-timeframe EMA and 20-period EMA of higher-timeframe weekly closes; use the latest available weekly value.
Long: After price touches the lower band 2, within the setup window close crosses above band 1 from at or below its prior value, with close below VWAP; if the delta gate is enabled, delta share is positive and meets its threshold; the long slot is free and any direction, session, anchor-validity, and signal-enable filters pass.
Short: After price touches the upper band 2, within the setup window close crosses below band 1 from at or above its prior value, with close above VWAP; if the delta gate is enabled, delta share is negative and meets its threshold; the short slot is free and any direction, session, anchor-validity, and signal-enable filters pass.
Long: After a close beyond lower band 3, within the setup window close crosses back above lower band 2, with close below VWAP; apply the delta gate and entry filters above.
Short: After a close beyond upper band 3, within the setup window close crosses back below upper band 2, with close above VWAP; apply the delta gate and entry filters above.
Long: Previous close is at or below prior lower band 1 and current close is above lower band 1 but below upper band 1; the long slot is free and direction, session, and signal-enable filters pass.
Short: Previous close is at or above prior upper band 1 and current close is below upper band 1 but above lower band 1; the short slot is free and direction, session, and signal-enable filters pass.
Long: Close crosses upward through the VWAP or an enabled σ band; for a band break, use the outermost crossed band. The long grade meets the required gate count and any confirmation closes remain above the crossed level; apply direction, session, anchor-validity, and signal-enable filters.
Short: Close crosses downward through the VWAP or an enabled σ band; for a band break, use the outermost crossed band. The short grade meets the required gate count and any confirmation closes remain below the crossed level; apply direction, session, anchor-validity, and signal-enable filters.
Long: Below VWAP, close crosses upward back inside an enabled lower σ band, choosing the outermost reclaimed band; the long grade meets the required gate count and any confirmation closes remain above the reclaimed level; apply direction, session, anchor-validity, and signal-enable filters.
Short: Above VWAP, close crosses downward back inside an enabled upper σ band, choosing the outermost reclaimed band; the short grade meets the required gate count and any confirmation closes remain below the reclaimed level; apply direction, session, anchor-validity, and signal-enable filters.
Calculation: Break/reclaim grade gates = acceptance (close beyond the level by a threshold and in the outer portion of the candle range), size (relative volume meets a threshold), flow (bar delta share points with the signal and meets a threshold), pressure (current still-forming higher-timeframe delta share points with the signal and meets a threshold), and trend (VWAP slope over a lookback points with the signal, or price is above/below most enabled EMA references). Count only enabled gates.
Exit: Long, low reaches the stop; otherwise high reaches the target; otherwise the trade reaches its time limit, using the closing price for the exit.
Exit: Short, high reaches the stop; otherwise low reaches the target; otherwise the trade reaches its time limit, using the closing price for the exit.
Exit: Long, close falls below band 3; otherwise high reaches the target; otherwise, for the stretch setup, price reaches band 1 and closes back below it; otherwise the trade reaches its time limit.
Exit: Short, close rises above band 3; otherwise low reaches the target; otherwise, for the stretch setup, price reaches band 1 and closes back above it; otherwise the trade reaches its time limit.
Exit: Long, close falls below band 2; otherwise high reaches the lesser of the fixed-risk target and upper band 1; otherwise the trade reaches its time limit.
Exit: Short, close rises above band 2; otherwise low reaches the greater of the fixed-risk target and lower band 1; otherwise the trade reaches its time limit.
