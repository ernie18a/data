Calculation: VWAP bands = For a chosen rolling or calendar-anchored window of chart bars through the current bar, calculate the volume-weighted mean and volume-weighted population standard deviation of a chosen price source: close, HLC3, or OHLC4. Upper and lower bands equal the mean plus and minus a standard-deviation multiple; the inner, middle, and outer bands use successively larger multiples. Rolling windows use either elapsed time or a chart-bar count. Require positive standard deviation and a minimum number of bars.
Calculation: Signed volume = For each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, assign positive volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, and otherwise use the sign of close minus the preceding lower-timeframe close; sum these values. Alternatively apply the same rule to the chart bar. Buying and selling volume are the sums of positive and absolute negative signed volumes. The higher-timeframe pressure is the sum of chart-bar signed volume divided by the sum of buying and selling volume in the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = Current buying plus selling volume, or chart volume if that sum is zero, divided by the prior exponential average of volume for the same session-time slot; before the slot has sufficient history, divide by the chart-bar simple moving average of volume. The average is updated after evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Directional gate count = Count the enabled gates that pass for direction d and crossed level L: acceptance, where d × (close − L) is at least a price-offset parameter and the close is at least a specified fraction of the bar range toward d; size, where relative volume is at least its threshold; flow, where signed volume has sign d and its absolute share of buying plus selling volume is at least its threshold; pressure, where higher-timeframe pressure has sign d and its absolute value is at least its threshold; and trend, where either d × the VWAP change over a lookback is positive or the sum of votes from close versus a smoothed chart-timeframe EMA and a higher-timeframe EMA is positive in direction d. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Reversion setup = A middle-band touch arms the corresponding side; each subsequent touch refreshes its age and extends the recorded high or low. Expire it when its age exceeds the setup lifetime and clear it after its trigger. An outer-band close beyond the band arms the alternative stretch setup in the same manner.
Calculation: Break or reclaim candidate = On a completed chart bar, compare the previous close with the previous value of an enabled VWAP or band and the current close with its current value. An outward cross is a break; an inward cross of a band with close still on that band's side of VWAP is a reclaim. Give breaks priority over reclaims and the outermost crossed band priority within each type. The target is the next band outward for a break or inward for a reclaim; beyond the outer band use an additional inner-band distance. The stop is beyond the signal-bar extreme for a break or beyond the recent-window extreme for a reclaim. Require the target and stop to be on opposite sides of the signal close, a sufficient directional gate count, sufficient bars in the VWAP window, no anchor change on the signal bar, and an allowed session. Save the crossed level, target and stop. A later candidate replaces any pending candidate; cancel a pending candidate if close fails to remain on its directional side of the saved level. Confirm after the specified number of additional eligible bars, including zero on the signal bar.
Calculation: Opening range = High and low of chart bars in the selected opening session window. After the window ends, track whether each side has already been exceeded and the greatest distance reached beyond each side as a multiple of the range height.
Calculation: Opening-window delta = Sum signed volumes of completed lower-timeframe bars in the opening window, using positive volume for close > open, negative volume for close < open, and zero otherwise; use chart bars if lower-timeframe bars are unavailable. Divide by opening-window volume. Opening-window price move is its last close minus its first open, divided by its high minus its low.
Long: An armed lower middle-band touch is no older than the setup lifetime AND previous close ≤ previous lower inner band AND close > current lower inner band AND the VWAP window has sufficient bars AND the anchor has not changed on this bar; clear the setup after the signal.
Short: An armed upper middle-band touch is no older than the setup lifetime AND previous close ≥ previous upper inner band AND close < current upper inner band AND the VWAP window has sufficient bars AND the anchor has not changed on this bar; clear the setup after the signal.

Short: An armed upper middle-band touch is no older than the setup lifetime AND previous close ≥ previous upper inner band AND VWAP < close < upper inner band AND the original-reversion trade slot is free AND the session is allowed AND, if enabled, signed volume is negative and its absolute share of buying plus selling volume meets the flow threshold AND the stop above the recorded setup high and the entry-time VWAP or opposite inner-band target bracket the close.

Short: An armed close above the upper outer band is no older than the setup lifetime AND previous close ≥ previous upper middle band AND VWAP < close < upper middle band AND the stretch trade slot is free AND the session is allowed AND, if enabled, the negative flow threshold is met AND the entry-time upper outer band is above close.

Short: Previous close ≥ previous upper inner band AND current lower inner band < close < current upper inner band AND the inner-band-cross trade slot is free AND the session is allowed AND the current upper middle band > close.

Exit: short, after an inner-band-cross entry bar, close > current upper middle band OR low ≤ the greater of the current lower inner band and entry price minus the configured reward multiple of entry-time risk OR bars since entry ≥ timeout.
