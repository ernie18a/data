Calculation: Session data = On a session-calculation timeframe equal to or lower than the chart timeframe, use the last completed bar. At each regular-session end, save the preceding bar's close and the high and low since the regular-session start; during postmarket use the preceding saved regular-session record, otherwise use the latest. At each extended-session start, save the preceding bar's close and the high and low since the preceding extended-session start. Keep the previous saved close for each session type. Track the current premarket high and low while premarket bars form, and at each extended-session start save the high and low since the preceding postmarket start. Session boundaries are determined from datetime and configurable session schedules.
Calculation: Pivot source = Select regular or extended session data using a configurable mode. In automatic mode select extended data when either the greatest of the saved regular high, saved postmarket high, and current premarket high exceeds the saved regular high by more than a configurable fraction of the ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, or the smallest of the corresponding lows is below the saved regular low by more than that fraction of ATR. Otherwise select regular data. Use the selected session's saved close as C and high as H. Use its saved low as L, except on the first bar after switching from regular to extended data, use the regular low.
Calculation: Pivot levels = R3 = C + (H - L) × a; S3 = C - (H - L) × a; R4 = C + (H - L) × b; S4 = C - (H - L) × b, where b is twice a.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its simple moving average, or a neutral fallback value when that average is not positive.
Long: low crosses below S3 AND close > S3
Long: close crosses above R4 AND close > open AND (the volume filter is disabled OR relative volume >= its threshold)
Short: high crosses above R3 AND close < R3
Short: close crosses below S4 AND close < open AND (the volume filter is disabled OR relative volume >= its threshold)
