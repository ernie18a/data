Calculation: Swing candidates = For each enabled swing lookback, update the stored high and its bar when the current high is the rolling highest high, and update the stored low and its bar when the current low is the rolling lowest low. Direction is +1 if the stored high is more recent than the stored low, otherwise -1; the candidate anchor is the stored low for +1 and the stored high for -1. A turn occurs when a previously nonzero direction changes. A candidate is valid when its anchor exists and its age is within the historical limit.
Calculation: Candidate score = Replace nonpositive or missing volume with 1. Let true range and its running moving average, volume and its running moving average, and ATR and its running moving average use their respective lookbacks. For each valid candidate, sum true range, average true range, volume, and average volume from its anchor through the current bar. Let directional movement be max(direction × (close − anchor price), 0), efficiency be directional movement divided by summed true range, and distance be directional movement divided by ATR at the anchor times a scale factor. Let trend strength be the square root of clip(efficiency divided by its scale factor) × clip(distance); volatility participation be clip((summed true range / summed average true range − offset) / scale); volume participation be clip((summed volume / summed average volume − offset) / scale). Here clip limits a value to [0, 1], and denominators are bounded below by a positive minimum. Move strength is the geometric mean of those three strengths multiplied by an affine function of their minimum. Range strength is clip(abs(stored high − stored low) / current ATR / range scale). Agreement is the mean over all valid candidates, including itself, of zero for an opposite direction and otherwise the larger of exponential decay in anchor-price distance measured in current ATR and exponential decay in anchor-bar distance. Score is a weighted sum of agreement, range strength, and move strength.
Calculation: Active anchor and structure = On each completed chart bar, evaluate anchor selection when an enabled scale turns, or when no anchor exists and the shortest configured lookback has elapsed. Select the highest-scoring valid candidate meeting the minimum score and minimum move strength. Accept it if the minimum bars since the previous acceptance have elapsed, it is not the same direction with both anchor-price distance within an ATR-scaled tolerance and anchor-bar distance within a bar tolerance, and either no anchor exists, its direction differs, the active anchor's current score is unavailable, or its score exceeds that current score by the required margin. Refresh the active score from a valid candidate with the same anchor bar and direction when available. On acceptance, set structural direction and level to the candidate direction and anchor price, and initialize the opposite extreme to the highest high since a bullish anchor or lowest low since a bearish anchor, including the anchor and current bars. Otherwise, update that opposite extreme with the current high in bullish structure or current low in bearish structure. In bearish structure, close >= structural level flips structure bullish and makes the tracked lowest low the new structural level; in bullish structure, close <= structural level flips structure bearish and makes the tracked highest high the new structural level. After a flip, initialize the opposite extreme over the bars from the new structural-level bar through the current bar. In the segment-preserving mode, a flip also replaces the active anchor with the new structural level and bar, resets its score to the minimum score, and resets the anchor age; in the recoloring mode it does not. Anchor acceptance takes precedence over a flip on the same bar.
Calculation: Adaptive anchored VWAP = On anchor acceptance, initialize price-volume to anchor price × anchor volume and volume accumulator to anchor volume, then process every bar from the anchor through the current bar inclusive. On later bars process the current bar once, including on structure flips. For each processed bar, replace nonpositive or missing volume with 1; set tracking length to the rounded, bounded base length, optionally divided by (ATR / its running moving average) raised to a volatility-adjustment exponent; set alpha to 1 − exp(−ln(2) / tracking length); update each accumulator to (1 − alpha) × its previous value + alpha × the bar's volume times, for the price-volume accumulator only, (high + low + close) / 3. VWAP is price-volume accumulator / volume accumulator. Use each historical bar's own tracking length during reconstruction.
Calculation: Retest arm = Reset to neutral on anchor acceptance, structure flip, missing active anchor, or missing VWAP. Otherwise, after evaluating the current bar's retest against the previous arm, reset to neutral if a retest occurs; else set bullish if close > VWAP + setup distance × ATR, set bearish if close < VWAP − setup distance × ATR, and otherwise retain the previous arm.
Long: A new active anchor is accepted AND its direction is bullish.
Short: A new active anchor is accepted AND its direction is bearish.
Long: Bearish structure AND close >= structural level AND no new anchor is accepted.
Short: Bullish structure AND close <= structural level AND no new anchor is accepted.
Long: Active anchor exists AND no new anchor or structure flip occurs AND close > adaptive anchored VWAP AND previous close <= previous adaptive anchored VWAP.
Short: Active anchor exists AND no new anchor or structure flip occurs AND close < adaptive anchored VWAP AND previous close >= previous adaptive anchored VWAP.
Long: No new anchor or structure flip occurs AND previous retest arm is bullish AND structure is bullish AND low <= VWAP + touch distance × ATR AND high >= VWAP − touch distance × ATR AND close > VWAP AND close >= open.
Short: No new anchor or structure flip occurs AND previous retest arm is bearish AND structure is bearish AND low <= VWAP + touch distance × ATR AND high >= VWAP − touch distance × ATR AND close < VWAP AND close <= open.
