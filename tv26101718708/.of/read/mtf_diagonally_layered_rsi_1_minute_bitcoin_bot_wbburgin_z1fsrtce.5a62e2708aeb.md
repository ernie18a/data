Long: Current-timeframe RSI crosses above oversold AND both higher-timeframe RSIs are below oversold, OR the shorter higher-timeframe RSI crosses above oversold AND the longer higher-timeframe RSI and current-timeframe RSI are above oversold, OR the longer higher-timeframe RSI crosses above oversold AND the shorter higher-timeframe RSI and current-timeframe RSI are above oversold; AND the EMA filter is disabled or close is above the EMA
Short: Current-timeframe RSI crosses below overbought AND both higher-timeframe RSIs are above overbought, OR the shorter higher-timeframe RSI crosses below overbought AND the longer higher-timeframe RSI and current-timeframe RSI are below overbought, OR the longer higher-timeframe RSI crosses below overbought AND the shorter higher-timeframe RSI and current-timeframe RSI are below overbought; AND the EMA filter is disabled or close is below the EMA, AND short trading is enabled
Exit: long, current-timeframe RSI crosses below overbought OR close crosses below the long stop-loss level when stop loss is enabled
Exit: short, current-timeframe RSI crosses above oversold OR close crosses above the short stop-loss level when stop loss is enabled
The two higher-timeframe RSI calculations use the same lookback parameter; one uses a shorter higher timeframe and one a longer higher timeframe. Both use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
