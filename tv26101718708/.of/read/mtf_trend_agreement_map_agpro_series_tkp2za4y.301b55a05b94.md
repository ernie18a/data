Calculation: Timeframe votes = For each of the four selected timeframes and, optionally, the chart timeframe when it does not duplicate a selected timeframe, count bullish and bearish votes from each enabled method. The EMA method votes bullish when the shorter EMA of close > the longer EMA of close AND close > the shorter EMA, bearish when the shorter EMA < the longer EMA AND close < the shorter EMA, and neutral otherwise. The structure method votes bullish when the latest confirmed pivot high > the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the latest confirmed pivot low > the preceding confirmed pivot low, bearish when both comparisons are <, and neutral otherwise; each pivot requires the same number of bars on both sides for confirmation. The momentum method votes bullish when the linear-regression slope of close divided by ATR > a positive threshold, bearish when it < the negative of that threshold, and neutral otherwise; use the same lookback for regression and ATR, and set normalized slope to zero when ATR is not positive. For higher timeframes use the last completed bar in historical backtests; the bar still forming can update in real time. For lower timeframes use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar.
Calculation: Agreement = The larger of total bullish votes and total bearish votes across included timeframes divided by the number of enabled method slots across those timeframes; neutral votes remain in the denominator. If there are no enabled slots, agreement is zero.
Calculation: Dominant side = Bullish when total bullish votes > total bearish votes, bearish when total bearish votes > total bullish votes, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Conflict = Dominant side is neutral OR agreement < the conflict threshold.
Calculation: Locked = Agreement >= the strong-alignment threshold AND conflict is false.
Long: Bullish votes > bearish votes in any included timeframe.
Short: Bearish votes > bullish votes in any included timeframe.
Long: Dominant side is bullish AND conflict is false.
Short: Dominant side is bearish AND conflict is false.
Long: Locked is true AND locked was false on the previous chart bar AND dominant side is bullish.
Short: Locked is true AND locked was false on the previous chart bar AND dominant side is bearish.
Long: Dominant side is bullish AND dominant side differed on the previous chart bar.
Short: Dominant side is bearish AND dominant side differed on the previous chart bar.
