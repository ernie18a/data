Calculation: Recent volatility = standard deviation of close over a lookback divided by the square root of that lookback
Calculation: Fast channel center = EMA(close, short lookback); fast upper/lower bands = center × (1 ± margin/1000) ±/− recent volatility × volatility factor
Calculation: Slow channel center = EMA(EMA(close, long lookback), long lookback); slow upper/lower bands = center × (1 ± margin/2000) ±/− recent volatility × volatility factor
Calculation: Trendiness = (standard deviation of close over the trend lookback / close) × (1 − restrictiveness/100); long-term trend = (close − close from the longer trend lookback) / close
Calculation: Adjusted stop percentage = base stop percentage × (1 + (standard deviation of close over the trend lookback × (1 + stop flexibility/5) / close))
Long: position is not short AND (close − close from the trend lookback) / close > 0 AND absolute value of Trendiness × 100 < trend sensitivity + long-term trend × long-term trend importance AND close < close[1] AND fast HMA < fast upper band AND fast lower band < slow lower band AND EMA(volume, lookback) < EMA(previous bar's EMA(volume, lookback) × (1 − volume cutoff/100), 5) AND absolute value of EMA(ROC(close, lookback), longer lookback) < ROC cutoff AND correlation(close, OBV, lookback) > OBV correlation threshold
Short: position is not long AND (close − close from the trend lookback) / close < 0 AND absolute value of Trendiness × 100 < trend sensitivity + long-term trend × long-term trend importance AND close > close[1] AND fast HMA > fast upper band AND fast upper band > slow upper band AND EMA(volume, lookback) < EMA(previous bar's EMA(volume, lookback) × (1 − volume cutoff/100), 5) AND absolute value of EMA(ROC(close, lookback), longer lookback) < ROC cutoff AND correlation(close, OBV, lookback) > OBV correlation threshold
Exit: long, fast HMA > fast upper band AND fast lower band > slow upper band OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − adjusted stop percentage/100)
Exit: short, fast HMA < fast lower band AND fast upper band < slow lower band OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + adjusted stop percentage/100)
