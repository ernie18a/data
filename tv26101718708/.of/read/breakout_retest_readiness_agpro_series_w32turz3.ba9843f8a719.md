Calculation: Normalized rising score = clamp((value - lower threshold) / (upper threshold - lower threshold), 0, 1); normalized falling score = 1 - normalized rising score; peaked score = normalized rising score below the ideal and normalized falling score above it.
Calculation: Breakout plan = on each confirmed breakout, replace the previous plan with its direction, bar index, broken level, breakout ATR, pocket half-width equal to breakout ATR times a pocket-width parameter, risk shelf equal to the broken level minus direction times breakout ATR times a shelf parameter, target equal to the broken level plus direction times the prior structure range times a target multiplier, breakout relative volume, and breakout quality. Direction is positive for an upward breakout and negative for a downward breakout. Clear the touched and locked flags. Track the greatest favorable extension from the broken level since breakout, divided by breakout ATR, initialized to the breakout close's distance beyond the level divided by breakout ATR. On a later bar within the retest window, set touched when its high-low range intersects the pocket. After touched, track the greatest adverse excursion beyond the broken level, divided by breakout ATR. Lock the plan on the first accepted, rejected, risk-edge, or expired condition; acceptance takes precedence over rejection and risk edge. Rejection requires touched and a close beyond the adverse side of the level by a fraction of pocket half-width; risk edge requires a close beyond the risk shelf; expiration requires no touch before the retest window ends.
Calculation: Breakout quality = a clamped weighted sum of normalized breakout distance beyond the level, breakout candle quality, breakout relative volume, directional trend agreement, and peaked prior structure range divided by ATR. Breakout candle quality is a weighted sum of the close's position within the candle toward the breakout direction, candle body divided by range, and a falling score of the opposite wick divided by range. Relative volume is volume divided by its moving average, with a neutral fallback when unavailable. Directional trend agreement is a weighted sum of whether close is on the breakout side of a slow EMA, a faster EMA is on that side of the slow EMA, and the slow EMA's change over a lookback is in the breakout direction; use separate positive contributions for agreement and disagreement. Freeze breakout quality at the breakout.
Calculation: Acceptance score = a clamped weighted sum of frozen breakout quality, peaked deepest retest depth, rising score of the current candle's wick on the retest side divided by candle range, peaked current relative volume divided by breakout relative volume, current directional trend agreement, rising score of greatest favorable extension, and rising score of remaining target room. Remaining target room is the positive distance from close to target in the breakout direction divided by breakout ATR. Before a touch, use neutral values for depth, wick, and retest-volume scores; use a neutral volume ratio if breakout relative volume is unavailable or nonpositive. Each normalization, peak, weight, and neutral value is a parameter; the depth peak's ideal is a fraction of the pocket-width parameter.
