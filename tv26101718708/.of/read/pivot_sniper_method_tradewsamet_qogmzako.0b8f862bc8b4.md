Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = the low/high of the bar a configurable number of completed bars before the current bar, respectively no greater/no less than every low/high in its independently configurable left window and strictly lower/higher than every low/high in its independently configurable right window. Evaluate on the current bar close.
Calculation: Directional strength = round(min(100, pivot-distance score + wick score + candle score + volume score + trend score)). For a long/short candidate, pivot-distance score = 20 × clamp((close − pivot low)/(ATR × 2), 0, 1) / 20 × 20 or 20 × clamp((pivot high − close)/(ATR × 2), 0, 1), respectively; use zero if ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Wick score = 20 × clamp(relevant pivot-bar wick / pivot-bar range, 0, 1), or zero if the range is zero; the relevant wick is min(open, close) − low for a long and high − max(open, close) for a short. Candle score = min(20, 15 × abs(close − open)/(high − low) + 5 if the current candle closes in the candidate direction, otherwise the body term alone); use a zero body ratio when the range is zero. Volume score = 20 × clamp(volume/(1.5 × SMA(volume)), 0, 1), or 10 if volume or its SMA is unavailable or the SMA is nonpositive. Trend score adds 10 for close above/below the close EMA and 10 for the EMA rising/falling versus its prior value, for long/short respectively.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry, select entry minus/plus max(ATR × multiplier, minimum price increment), confirmed pivot low/high, that pivot minus/plus ATR × buffer, or the signal-bar low/high, for long/short respectively. If the selected stop is unavailable or is not strictly more than one minimum price increment below/above entry, use the ATR stop. Risk = max(abs(entry − initial stop), minimum price increment). Each of three independently parameterized targets = entry plus/minus risk × its target multiple for long/short. The first and second targets are intermediate; the third is final.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until break-even is enabled and the selected first or second target has been touched on a post-entry bar without the active stop or final target being touched on that bar; from the following bar, active stop = entry plus/minus a configurable nonnegative price offset for long/short. Break-even can instead be disabled.
Long: Confirmed pivot low exists AND confirmed pivot high does not exist AND long bias is permitted AND the bar opening time is within the selected session or session filtering is disabled AND the strength filter is disabled OR the preceding conditions hold AND directional strength is at least the minimum threshold; additionally, no earlier accepted signal exists OR the number of bars since the last accepted long or short signal is greater than the cooldown.
Short: Confirmed pivot high exists AND confirmed pivot low does not exist AND short bias is permitted AND the bar opening time is within the selected session or session filtering is disabled AND the strength filter is disabled OR the preceding conditions hold AND directional strength is at least the minimum threshold; additionally, no earlier accepted signal exists OR the number of bars since the last accepted long or short signal is greater than the cooldown.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= active stop OR high >= final target; if both occur, the stop takes priority.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= active stop OR low <= final target; if both occur, the stop takes priority.
Exit: long OR short, a new long or short signal occurs AND ATR is available; exit at the signal bar close unless the existing position has already exited at its stop or final target on that bar, then open the new position at the close.
