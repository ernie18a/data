Calculation: ATR = Average True Range (short fixed lookback on chart timeframe)
Calculation: GapSize = for bullish: low - high[2]; for bearish: low[2] - high (high[2]/low[2] = values 2 bars before the current bar)
Calculation: Strength(side) = 100 * score / maxScore, where score = count of true components and maxScore = number of enabled components. Always-on components: (1) GapSize >= small multiple of ATR; (2) GapSize >= large multiple of ATR (large multiple > small multiple); (3) previous bar's (high - low) >= displacement multiple of ATR. Optional components, each adds 1 to maxScore only if enabled: (4) volume trend: previous bar volume > multiplier (>1) * SMA of volume as of previous bar; (5) EMA trend: bullish = close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA, bearish = close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA (fast EMA has shorter period than slow EMA); (6) structure break: bullish = close > highest high of the prior N bars (excluding current bar), bearish = close < lowest low of the prior N bars (excluding current bar); (7) liquidity sweep: bullish = previous bar low < lowest low of the N bars ending 2 bars ago AND previous bar close > previous bar open, bearish = previous bar high > highest high of the N bars ending 2 bars ago AND previous bar close < previous bar open
Long: low > high[2] (bullish fair value gap) AND Strength(bullish) >= minimum strength threshold
Short: high < low[2] (bearish fair value gap) AND Strength(bearish) >= minimum strength threshold
