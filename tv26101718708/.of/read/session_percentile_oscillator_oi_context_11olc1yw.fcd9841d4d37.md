Calculation: Session percentile = For each enabled session, track its running high and low and, after it ends, store its range divided by the last completed higher-timeframe ATR when the trading day changes, together with the preceding trading day's weekday. During an active session, divide its running range by the last completed higher-timeframe ATR and calculate the percentage of stored normalized ranges for that session whose weekday is enabled and whose value is less than or equal to the current normalized range. Define sessions and trading days using configurable New York local times; if sessions overlap, use the last active session in the configured session order.
Calculation: Smoothed session percentile = Apply the selected smoothing method: none, SMA, EMA, RMA, or rational quadratic. For rational quadratic smoothing, take the weighted average of the current and preceding lookback bars, replacing missing inputs with zero; the weight at lag i is (1 + i² / (2 × lookback² × relative weight)) raised to the negative relative weight.
Long: Close is below the lowest close of the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND smoothed session percentile is above its lowest value over the preceding divergence-lookback bars.
Short: Close is above the highest close of the preceding divergence-lookback bars AND smoothed session percentile is below its highest value over the preceding divergence-lookback bars.
