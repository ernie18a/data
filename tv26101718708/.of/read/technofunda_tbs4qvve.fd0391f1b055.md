Calculation: Volatility-stop trend = Initialize trend as up, running maximum and minimum as close, and stop as undefined. On each bar, update the running maximum and minimum with close; set the band to ATR times a multiplier, or true range if ATR is unavailable. If the prior trend is up, set the stop to the greater of its prior value and running maximum minus band; otherwise set it to the lesser of its prior value and running minimum plus band. If the stop is undefined, use close. Set the trend to up if close is at least the stop, otherwise down. If the trend changes, reset both running extrema to close and reset the stop to close minus band for an uptrend or close plus band for a downtrend.
Calculation: Swing low = On the selected timeframe, confirm pivot highs and lows after their respective right-side confirmation bars, using left-side and right-side lookbacks. In most-recent mode, retain the latest confirmed pivot low. In Dow mode, retain the latest confirmed pivot high, a running leg high, and a queue of pending pivot lows. On each bar, remove pending lows from the end of the queue while the current low is below them; append a newly confirmed pivot low only if the queue is empty or it exceeds the last pending low. If the current high exceeds the prior leg high or the latest confirmed pivot high, set the swing low to the first pending low and remove it if the queue is nonempty, clear the confirmed pivot high, and reset the leg high to the current high. Otherwise initialize the leg high to the current high if undefined, or update it to the greater of itself and the current high. Retain the swing low until replaced. For a higher timeframe use its last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Volatility-stop trend is up.
Short: Volatility-stop trend is down.
Long: High is at least the highest high over the lookback including the current bar.
Short: Low is at most the lowest low over the lookback including the current bar.
Long: Close is above the short-term moving average OR close is above the long-term moving average; use SMAs on the shorter chart timeframe and EMAs on the higher chart timeframe.
Short: Close is at or below the short-term moving average OR close is at or below the long-term moving average; use SMAs on the shorter chart timeframe and EMAs on the higher chart timeframe.
Exit: Long, close is below the swing low.
