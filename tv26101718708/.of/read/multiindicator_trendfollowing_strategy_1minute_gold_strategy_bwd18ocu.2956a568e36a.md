Calculation: All indicators use chart bars unless stated otherwise. Higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the higher timeframe used for the trend filter is longer than the higher timeframe used for MACD, reversal confirmation, and automatic strictness. The two higher timeframes used for EMA trend markings have shorter and longer durations.
Calculation: Short, medium, and long EMAs = EMAs of close with increasing lookback lengths. Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe close using the medium EMA lookback.
Calculation: Uptrend = short EMA > long EMA AND close > short EMA. Downtrend = short EMA < long EMA AND close < short EMA. Ranging = uptrend is false AND downtrend is false. Higher-timeframe uptrend and downtrend use the same definitions calculated entirely on the longer higher timeframe.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of volume multiplied by sign of close minus previous close. Bullish OBV = OBV > SMA of OBV AND OBV > OBV at the longer OBV slope lookback AND OBV SMA > OBV SMA at the shorter OBV slope lookback AND close > close at the shorter OBV slope lookback. Bearish OBV reverses all four inequalities.
Calculation: OBV reversal component = min(abs(OBV minus EMA of OBV) / Wilder-smoothed rolling range of that difference, 1), or 0 if the smoothed range is not positive. Replace a zero rolling range with 1 before smoothing.
Calculation: Bullish reversal = reversal RSI <= oversold threshold AND reversal MACD line crosses above its signal line. Bearish reversal = reversal RSI >= overbought threshold AND reversal MACD line crosses below its signal line. A cross requires the prior line to be on or beyond the opposite side of its signal line.
Calculation: Reversal strength = min((mean of the RSI extreme indicator, MACD cross indicator, and OBV reversal component + optional higher-timeframe confirmation weight) / normalization factor, 1). The RSI extreme indicator is 1 when reversal RSI is oversold or overbought; the MACD cross indicator is 1 on either reversal MACD cross. Add the confirmation weight only when confirmation is enabled and the higher-timeframe reversal RSI is in the matching extreme with its MACD line on the matching side of its signal line. Use the enabled-confirmation normalization factor whenever confirmation is enabled and 1 otherwise.
Calculation: Reversal cooldown = a counter initially 0 and a last confirmed direction initially neutral. On a bullish or bearish reversal whose strength meets the reversal-strength threshold, reset the counter to the cooldown length only if its direction differs from the last confirmed direction, and update that direction. On every bar, including a reset bar, decrement a positive counter by 1. Cooldown is active when the resulting counter > 0.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > SMA of volume times the spike multiplier.
Calculation: Confidence = weighted sum of four indicators: either chart-timeframe trend is valid, volume spike, either OBV direction is valid, and higher-timeframe MACD line differs from its signal line. Each indicator contributes its own weight when true and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Directional trend-validation score = ADX tier score + 1 if chart MACD histogram has the direction's sign + 1 if RSI exceeds the bullish threshold or is below the bearish threshold, respectively + 2 if the short EMA is on the direction's side of the long EMA + 1 if volume > its SMA. The ADX tier score uses descending ADX thresholds, assigning the highest satisfied tier's score. A direction is trend-valid when this score >= its threshold AND the short EMA is on the direction's side of the long EMA.
Calculation: Upper ATR band = previous close + ATR times an ATR multiplier; lower ATR band = previous close - ATR times the same multiplier.
Calculation: Strictness = the user-selected level unless automatic strictness is enabled. With automatic strictness, use high when higher-timeframe ATR > its higher-timeframe SMA times the high-volatility multiplier, medium when ATR > that SMA but does not meet the high condition, and low otherwise.
Calculation: Raw long condition = high strictness AND bullish trend-validation AND not ranging AND higher-timeframe MACD histogram > 0 AND close > previous high AND close > open AND close > upper ATR band; OR medium strictness AND bullish trend-validation AND (higher-timeframe MACD histogram > 0 OR chart MACD histogram > 0) AND (close > previous high OR close > short EMA) AND (close > open OR RSI > its midpoint) AND close > upper ATR band times the long band factor; OR low strictness AND ((bullish trend-validation AND (higher-timeframe MACD histogram > 0 OR chart MACD histogram > 0) AND (close > open OR RSI > its midpoint) AND close > upper ATR band times the long band factor) OR (ranging AND chart MACD histogram > 0 AND RSI > the bullish threshold AND volume spike AND close > short EMA AND close > upper ATR band)).
Calculation: Raw short condition = high strictness AND bearish trend-validation AND not ranging AND higher-timeframe MACD histogram < 0 AND close < previous low AND close < open AND close < lower ATR band; OR medium strictness AND bearish trend-validation AND (higher-timeframe MACD histogram < 0 OR chart MACD histogram < 0) AND (close < previous low OR close < short EMA) AND (close < open OR RSI < its midpoint) AND close < lower ATR band times the short band factor; OR low strictness AND ((bearish trend-validation AND (higher-timeframe MACD histogram < 0 OR chart MACD histogram < 0) AND (close < open OR RSI < its midpoint) AND close < lower ATR band times the short band factor) OR (ranging AND chart MACD histogram < 0 AND RSI < the bearish threshold AND volume spike AND close < short EMA AND close < lower ATR band)). The long band factor is below 1 and the short band factor is above 1.
Calculation: Eligible long = raw long condition AND confidence >= minimum trade confidence. Eligible short = raw short condition AND confidence >= minimum trade confidence. A new eligible direction also requires a previous eligible bar of that direction and close != close on the immediately preceding eligible bar of that direction.
Calculation: Executable long = new eligible long AND higher-timeframe uptrend. Executable short = new eligible short AND higher-timeframe downtrend.
Calculation: Directional dashboard score = ADX tier score + 1 if higher-timeframe MACD histogram has the direction's sign + 1 if RSI exceeds the bullish threshold or is below the bearish threshold, respectively + 2 if short EMA is on the direction's side of long EMA + 1 if volume > its SMA.
Calculation: Dashboard buy candidate = chart uptrend AND confidence >= strong-signal threshold AND volume spike AND higher-timeframe MACD histogram > 0 AND bullish dashboard score > score threshold AND close > highest high over the preceding breakout lookback. Dashboard sell candidate reverses the trend, MACD, score direction, and breakout inequalities. Maintain one shared last candidate price, initially undefined; a candidate is new if that price is undefined or differs from close. Update it to close for each new candidate even when confidence blocks the dashboard signal; process the buy candidate before the sell candidate.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band.
Calculation: Precision long = executable long AND close > open AND (close - open) / (high - low) > candle-body threshold AND Bollinger width > width threshold AND ADX > trend threshold AND confidence >= precision-confidence threshold. Precision short replaces the candle conditions with close < open AND (open - close) / (high - low) > candle-body threshold.
Calculation: Base bracket prices are fixed when an executable entry is signaled: long limit = signal close + signal ATR times (base target multiplier + confidence times confidence adjustment); short limit subtracts the same amount. Trailing activation distance = signal ATR times trailing multiplier, and trailing offset = activation distance times offset multiplier, both interpreted as tick counts and converted to price using a tick-size parameter. Activate the long trail when high reaches entry price plus activation distance, then trail the highest price reached by the offset; activate the short trail when low reaches entry price minus activation distance, then trail the lowest price reached by the offset.
Calculation: Precision bracket prices are fixed on the precision signal bar. Long stop = max(signal close - signal ATR times stop multiplier, lowest low over the structure lookback); long target = signal close + (signal close - long stop) times reward-to-risk multiplier. Short stop = min(signal close + signal ATR times stop multiplier, highest high over the structure lookback); short target = signal close - (short stop - signal close) times reward-to-risk multiplier.
Long: Reversal RSI <= oversold threshold AND reversal MACD line > its signal line AND reversal cooldown is inactive.
Short: Reversal RSI >= overbought threshold AND reversal MACD line < its signal line AND reversal cooldown is inactive.
Long: Shorter higher-timeframe EMA > chart-timeframe long EMA.
Short: Shorter higher-timeframe EMA < chart-timeframe long EMA.
Long: Higher-timeframe MACD line > its signal line.
Short: Higher-timeframe MACD line < its signal line.
Long: Confidence >= minimum trade confidence AND dashboard buy candidate is new.
Short: Confidence >= minimum trade confidence AND dashboard buy candidate is not new AND dashboard sell candidate is new.
Long: Executable long.
Short: Executable short.
Long: Precision long.
Short: Precision short.
Exit: Long, higher-timeframe downtrend.
Exit: Short, higher-timeframe uptrend.
Exit: Long base entry, high >= base long limit OR activated trailing stop AND low <= long trailing stop.
Exit: Short base entry, low <= base short limit OR activated trailing stop AND high >= short trailing stop.
Exit: Long precision entry, low <= precision long stop OR high >= precision long target.
Exit: Short precision entry, high >= precision short stop OR low <= precision short target.
