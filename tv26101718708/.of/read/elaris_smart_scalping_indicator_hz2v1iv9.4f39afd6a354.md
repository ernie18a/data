Calculation: ATR% = ATR / close * 100
Long: (close > fast EMA of close AND fast EMA > slow EMA of close, where fast EMA length < slow EMA length) AND RSI > bullish level AND MACD histogram > 0 AND volume > volume SMA * multiplier (multiplier > 1) AND ATR% >= minimum threshold AND close of the higher-timeframe bar still forming (as of the current chart bar) > EMA of close on higher-timeframe bars (that EMA longer than the chart-timeframe fast EMA) AND, if used, bar time is within a trading session; trigger only on the bar where this full condition becomes true, i.e. it was not true on the previous bar. Optional trend variants: looser = close > fast EMA only; stricter = also fast EMA and slow EMA both higher than their previous-bar values.
Short: (close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA) AND RSI < bearish level (bearish level < bullish level) AND MACD histogram < 0 AND volume > volume SMA * multiplier AND ATR% >= minimum threshold AND close of the higher-timeframe bar still forming (as of the current chart bar) < EMA of close on higher-timeframe bars AND, if used, bar time is within a trading session; trigger only on the bar where this full condition becomes true, i.e. it was not true on the previous bar. Optional trend variants: looser = close < fast EMA only; stricter = also fast EMA and slow EMA both lower than their previous-bar values.
