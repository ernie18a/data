Calculation: Confirmed liquidity levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot lengths; retain the newest configured number of each, including pivots confirmed on the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = ATR is available AND high > low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= minimum wick ratio AND low < any retained pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND close > open. Bearish sweep = ATR is available AND high > low AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= minimum wick ratio AND high > any retained pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars ago AND gap size = low - high two bars ago >= current ATR × minimum gap multiplier AND, if displacement is required, previous close > previous open AND previous candle body >= current ATR × displacement multiplier; its top is current low and bottom is the high two bars ago. Bearish gap = high < low two bars ago AND gap size = low two bars ago - high >= current ATR × minimum gap multiplier AND, if displacement is required, previous close < previous open AND previous candle body >= current ATR × displacement multiplier; its top is the low two bars ago and bottom is current high.
Calculation: Retained gaps = add new gaps before evaluating signals and retain only the newest configured number across both directions, including gaps that have already become inactive. A gap becomes permanently inactive when its age in bars > maximum age, or its close < bottom for a bullish gap, or its close > top for a bearish gap. Age is zero on creation.
Calculation: Live gap score = round(clamp(clamp((top - bottom) / current ATR × 3.2, 0, 3) + clamp(current ATR / current ATR × 2, 0, 3) + clamp(3 - age / 40, 0, 2) + clamp((current volume / current volume SMA) × 1.2, 0, 2), 0, 10)); division by zero or an unavailable denominator returns zero.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: New bullish gap.
Short: New bearish gap.
Long: Bars since the most recent bullish sweep >= sweep confirmation delay AND <= sweep window AND any retained live bullish gap has low <= its top AND high >= its bottom AND live gap score >= minimum score AND close > open AND bars since the previous long magnet signal > sweep window, or no previous long magnet signal exists.
Short: Bars since the most recent bearish sweep >= sweep confirmation delay AND <= sweep window AND any retained live bearish gap has low <= its top AND high >= its bottom AND live gap score >= minimum score AND close < open AND bars since the previous short magnet signal > sweep window, or no previous short magnet signal exists.
Long: Number of retained live bullish gaps > number of retained live bearish gaps + 1.
Short: Number of retained live bearish gaps > number of retained live bullish gaps + 1.
