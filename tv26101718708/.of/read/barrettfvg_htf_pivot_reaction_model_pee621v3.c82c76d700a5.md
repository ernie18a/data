Calculation: Pivot = a high or low confirmed after the same configurable number of chart or source-timeframe bars on each side; use separate lengths for source levels, reclaim structure, BOS, stops, and trendlines.
Calculation: Reference ATR = ATR of the first enabled source timeframe, using its last completed higher-timeframe bar; use chart-timeframe ATR until it is available. Source pivots likewise use last completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Levels = register confirmed pivot highs as resistance and lows as support from each enabled source timeframe. Process source timeframes in input order, highs before lows. Merge a pivot into the first resistance-array level within a configurable price tolerance, otherwise the first support-array level within tolerance; retain its polarity, age, and reactions, add the source timeframe, and replace its price only if the new pivot is from a higher timeframe than its current anchor. Otherwise create a level with zero reactions. Store levels in their original arrays even after polarity flips. Per array, evict the level with fewest reactions when capacity is exceeded, breaking ties by oldest creation time; remove levels older than the configured elapsed time.
Calculation: Level visit = a chart bar's high reaches level minus touch tolerance AND its low reaches level plus touch tolerance. At the start of each visit record whether the previous close, or current close if unavailable, is above the level. While visiting, track the maximum of level minus low for resistance or high minus level for support, floored at zero. Count one reaction and end the visit when that maximum reaches the configurable reference-ATR distance; otherwise end it when the elapsed reaction window is exceeded. A new visit can start on the next touching bar. A level qualifies when its reaction count and number of distinct source timeframes both reach their respective thresholds.
Calculation: Level interaction = on each confirmed bar, update reactions, then mark a resistance broken when close exceeds level plus touch tolerance plus configurable reference-ATR overshoot, or a support broken when close is below level minus those distances. A broken resistance gains far-side structure when a newly confirmed chart pivot low exceeds level plus touch tolerance; a broken support gains it when a newly confirmed chart pivot high is below level minus touch tolerance. Expire the break and structure after the configured elapsed break-to-retest window. A qualified level touched in an allowed session produces a reclaim interaction if broken with far-side structure and reclaim is enabled; reset its break and structure and flip its polarity immediately. Otherwise, if unbroken and tap is enabled, it produces a tap interaction when resistance high is at most level plus touch tolerance plus overshoot or support low is at least level minus touch tolerance minus overshoot. Direction is long if the recorded visit approach was above the level, short otherwise. Select the closest interaction to close separately for taps and reclaims, breaking equal-distance ties in favor of the resistance array and, within an array, the most recently stored level. All qualifying reclaim levels selected while scanning are reset and flipped, even if a closer one is subsequently found.
Calculation: Pending BOS = independently for each direction, an interaction arms or replaces a pending setup if its direction's elapsed arm cooldown has passed; prefer the reclaim when both selected interactions have that direction. Expire it after the configured elapsed BOS window and clear it when an entry of that direction occurs. BOS up is close crossing above the latest confirmed chart pivot high plus a configurable price buffer; BOS down is close crossing below the latest confirmed chart pivot low minus that buffer. A crossing compares current and preceding closes with their respective current and preceding pivot-based thresholds.
Calculation: Accumulation range = every selected tap or reclaim interaction replaces any existing range with the interaction bar's high and low, regardless of interaction direction or arm cooldown. On subsequent confirmed bars, expand the range with each high and low until the configurable minimum elapsed formation time is reached, then freeze it. Discard it if its width exceeds the current configurable reference-ATR limit or its total elapsed time exceeds the breakout window. After freezing, the first close above its high or below its low ends the range, even if volume or another entry filter fails. A new interaction replaces the range before breakout evaluation.
Calculation: Directional entry = a pending BOS setup with the corresponding BOS crossing OR an enabled accumulation breakout in that direction with volume at least a configurable multiple of its current chart-timeframe volume SMA when the volume filter is enabled, the SMA being positive. Require an allowed session and the configurable same-direction bar cooldown since the previous directional entry; evaluate on confirmed bars. Directional entries update their own cooldown and clear their own pending BOS setup.
Calculation: Trendline break = build descending channels from confirmed chart pivot highs and ascending channels from confirmed chart pivot lows. For each side retain successive pivots until a valid pair forms: the latest two highs must descend, or lows ascend; unless a shared prior-break flag is set, each of those two pivots must also be below both preceding highs, or above both preceding lows. Connect the latest pair and create a parallel edge displaced by configurable padding times the smaller of a longer-lookback chart ATR fraction and a configurable fraction of close. Before accepting, sweep from the current bar back through the earlier pivot: starting at the later pivot endpoint, subtract the line slope once per inspected bar and reject a descending channel if that bar's low is above the resulting base value, or an ascending channel if its high is below it. Rejection clears that side's pivots and active channel and sets the shared prior-break flag; acceptance replaces that side's active channel, clears its pivots, and clears the flag. On each bar test close against the active base endpoint before advancing both endpoints by one slope increment: close above a descending base is an upward break, and close below an ascending base is a downward break. A break removes that channel, clears its pivot list, and sets the shared prior-break flag. Process descending before ascending.
Calculation: Major-level reaction = the latest chart bar whose range touches a configurable tolerance around an enabled previous completed higher-timeframe day or week high or low; if several are touched on that bar, choose day high, day low, week high, then week low. It remains recent for the configured chart-bar window.
Calculation: Confluence = a directional entry with a corresponding trendline break within the configured preceding chart-bar window, including the current bar, OR a directional entry with an enabled recent major-level reaction and close above that level for long or below it for short, OR a corresponding trendline break within the configured forward chart-bar window after an otherwise unconfirmed directional entry. A newer waiting entry replaces the older one; clear the wait on confluence. Evaluate on confirmed bars.
Calculation: Confluence stop and target = on a confluence bar use its close as entry. For a long, use the latest confirmed chart pivot low if below entry, otherwise the accumulation range low if an accumulation long entry occurs on this bar, otherwise the current long entry's selected level if available, otherwise the bar low; stop is the smaller of that reference and bar low minus a configurable price buffer. For a short use the latest confirmed pivot high if above entry, otherwise the accumulation range high if an accumulation short entry occurs on this bar, otherwise the current short entry's selected level if available, otherwise the bar high; stop is the larger of that reference and bar high plus the buffer. Target is entry plus or minus a configurable multiple of the entry-to-stop distance. Create these levels only if enabled and risk is positive; each new confluence with valid levels replaces the previous tracked levels. If both directions create levels on one bar, the short levels replace the long levels.
Long: Directional entry is long OR confluence is long.
Short: Directional entry is short OR confluence is short.
Exit: long, a tracked long confluence stop and target exist from an earlier bar AND low is at or below the stop OR high is at or above the target.
Exit: short, a tracked short confluence stop and target exist from an earlier bar AND high is at or above the stop OR low is at or below the target.
