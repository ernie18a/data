Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); candle range = high - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; body fraction = candle body / candle range when the range is positive, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Qualified volume = volume >= its SMA multiplied by a volume multiplier AND, if the expanding-volume filter is enabled, volume > previous volume AND previous volume > volume two bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish EMA stack = shorter EMA > middle EMA AND middle EMA > longer EMA; bearish EMA stack reverses both comparisons. Higher-timeframe stacks use the corresponding EMAs on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Calculation: Support levels = previous completed daily low and close, previous completed weekly low, current daily low through the current bar, current pre-session low through the current bar, current opening-range low through the current bar, middle EMA, session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3, and latest confirmed pivot low. Resistance levels substitute highs for lows, retaining the previous daily close, middle EMA, and VWAP. Daily and weekly bars are higher than the chart timeframe; current-session levels include the still-forming bar. A pivot is confirmed after equal numbers of bars on each side.
Calculation: Spring = after low falls below the lowest low of a lookback excluding the current bar, retain that level and the sweep bar until the first qualifying spring or expiry; within a confirmation window including the sweep bar, close > retained level AND close > open AND qualified volume. Ignore subsequent low sweeps while one is pending. Upthrust reverses the comparisons using the highest preceding high, high, and close < open, with independent pending state.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = enabled spring OR enabled bullish engulfing (previous close < previous open AND close > open AND close > previous open AND open <= previous close AND candle body > previous candle body) OR enabled hammer (lower wick > body times a multiplier AND upper wick < body times a smaller multiplier AND body fraction < a threshold AND close >= open) OR enabled breakout (close > highest high of preceding lookback AND close > open AND body fraction > a threshold) OR enabled bounce (low within a percentage of any support level AND close > open AND body fraction > a threshold AND close > (high + low) / 2).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = enabled upthrust OR enabled bearish engulfing (previous close > previous open AND close < open AND close < previous open AND open >= previous close AND candle body > previous candle body) OR enabled shooting star (upper wick > body times a multiplier AND lower wick < body times a smaller multiplier AND body fraction < a threshold AND close <= open) OR enabled breakdown (close < lowest low of preceding lookback AND close < open AND body fraction > a threshold) OR enabled rejection (high within a percentage of any resistance level AND close < open AND body fraction > a threshold AND close < (high + low) / 2).
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of one point each for bullish EMA stack; close > VWAP AND VWAP > its value a specified number of chart bars ago; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR bullish higher-timeframe EMA stack; RSI within its bullish inclusive bounds; MACD line > signal line AND histogram > previous histogram; qualified volume. Bearish score uses the bearish EMA stack, close < VWAP AND falling VWAP, disabled filter OR bearish higher-timeframe stack, bearish RSI bounds, MACD line < signal line AND histogram < previous histogram, and qualified volume.
Calculation: Bullish trend count = number of true conditions among bullish EMA stack, close > VWAP, and higher-timeframe filter disabled OR bullish higher-timeframe EMA stack. Bearish trend count uses the corresponding bearish comparisons.
Calculation: Entry signals use the chart close as entry price. Track long and short positions independently; a new same-side signal does not reset an existing position. For each position, initial stop is entry price minus ATR at entry times a multiplier for long, or plus that amount for short; alternatively it is a percentage below the minimum of entry-bar low, previous low and entry-bar middle EMA for long, or a percentage above the maximum of the corresponding highs and EMA for short. Risk is the distance from entry to initial stop. The first and second targets are entry plus risk times a target multiplier for long, or entry minus risk times that multiplier for short; the second multiplier is larger. After the first target is reached, move the stop to entry before evaluating the full exit on the same bar. Count elapsed chart bars from zero on the entry bar.
