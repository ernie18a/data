Calculation: Range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); ATR scale = max(ATR, minimum price increment); body = close - open; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; bullish close location = (close - low) / Range; bearish close location = (high - close) / Range. Clamp confines a value to the interval from zero to one. Normalize(x) = Clamp(z * (A + z²) / (A + B * z²)), where z = max(x, zero) and A and B are fixed coefficients.
Calculation: Direction = sign of the short-horizon EMA of body / ATR scale. Relative volume = volume / volume EMA when volume and its EMA are available and the EMA is positive; otherwise use the neutral relative-volume treatment. Wick bias = Clamp(midpoint + midpoint * (lower wick - upper wick) / Range). Bullish pressure = Clamp(weighted sum of bullish close location, Clamp(midpoint + body / (two * Range)), and wick bias). Bearish pressure = Clamp(weighted sum of bearish close location, Clamp(midpoint - body / (two * Range)), and one minus wick bias). Effort = Clamp(weighted sum of Normalize(true range / ATR scale / range scale), Normalize(relative volume / volume scale), absolute body / Range, and bullish pressure if Direction is nonnegative or bearish pressure otherwise); substitute a neutral constant for the volume component when relative volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Structural result = the greater of normalized positive directional breakout beyond the preceding structural-lookback highest high or lowest low, and the clamped directional distance of close from the midpoint of the current structural-lookback high-low range; both are zero when Direction is zero. Follow-through = Clamp(weighted sum of an indicator that the last two close changes have the same sign as Direction, and Normalize(directional two-bar close change / ATR scale / progress scale)); directional two-bar close change is zero when Direction is zero. Result = Clamp(weighted sum of Normalize(absolute one-bar close change / ATR scale / displacement scale), bullish close location if Direction is nonnegative or bearish close location otherwise, Follow-through, and Structural result).
Calculation: Signed evidence = Direction * square root of max(Effort * Result, zero). Smooth it with short-, medium-, and long-horizon EMAs, respectively. Agreement is the full-agreement coefficient when all three nonzero EMA signs agree, the partial-agreement coefficient when only the short- and medium-horizon signs agree, and the remaining coefficient otherwise. Magnitude = Clamp(weighted sum of the three absolute EMA values); persistence = Clamp(weighted sum of the medium- and long-horizon absolute EMA values). Commitment = Clamp(Magnitude * (baseline coefficient + agreement coefficient * Agreement) + persistence coefficient * persistence). Commitment direction = sign of the weighted sum of the three signed evidence EMAs.
Calculation: Stress = Clamp(weighted sum of Clamp(medium-horizon EMA of Effort minus medium-horizon EMA of Result), Clamp(negative scaled change in the Result EMA over a fixed lookback), and the adverse wick fraction); adverse wick fraction is Clamp(upper wick / Range) for positive Commitment direction, Clamp(lower wick / Range) for negative Commitment direction, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Lifecycle starts dormant with episode direction, persistence count, weakness count, confirmed age, and established flag all zero. On each completed chart bar, first update persistence count: if Commitment is at least the emerging threshold and Commitment direction is nonzero, increment it when episode direction is zero or agrees with Commitment direction, otherwise set it to one; otherwise set it to zero. Increment weakness count when Commitment is below the hold threshold; otherwise reduce it by one, bounded below by zero. If the prior lifecycle is dormant, released, or failed, reset confirmed age and established flag, then set lifecycle to emerging and episode direction to Commitment direction if Commitment is at least the emerging threshold and Commitment direction is nonzero. Otherwise increment confirmed age if the prior lifecycle is confirmed, established, mature, or stressed, and apply the first matching transition: failed if Commitment direction is nonzero and opposes episode direction, Commitment is at least the confirmation threshold, and persistence count is at least the confirmation persistence; released if weakness count is at least the release persistence and Commitment is below the release threshold; stressed if the established flag is set and Stress is at least the stress threshold; mature if the established flag is set, confirmed age is at least the maturity age, and Commitment is at least the hold threshold; established, setting the established flag, if Commitment direction agrees with episode direction and either Commitment is at least the establishment threshold and persistence count exceeds the confirmation persistence, or the prior lifecycle is confirmed, confirmed age is at least the establishment age, Commitment is at least the hold threshold, and Structural result is at least its threshold; confirmed if Commitment is at least the confirmation threshold, persistence count is at least the confirmation persistence, and Commitment direction agrees with episode direction; otherwise, if the prior lifecycle is confirmed, established, mature, or stressed, Commitment is at least the hold threshold, and Commitment direction agrees with episode direction, set lifecycle to established when the established flag is set and confirmed otherwise; emerging if Commitment is at least the emerging threshold and Commitment direction agrees with episode direction. If no transition matches, retain the prior lifecycle.
Long: Lifecycle is confirmed, established, mature, or stressed AND episode direction is positive.
Short: Lifecycle is confirmed, established, mature, or stressed AND episode direction is negative.
