Calculation: Relative-strength score = weighted average of percentile ranks for long-term return excluding the most recent short-term period, intermediate-term return, shorter-term return, proximity to the long-term high, and change in the price-to-benchmark ratio; give the long-term return the greatest weight, then the intermediate-term return, then the shorter-term return, with smaller weights for the high proximity and ratio change. Use higher-timeframe bars when applicable, including the latest bar still forming.
Calculation: Trend score = weighted average of normalized close-to-long-term-average distance, medium-term average relative to long-term average, and drawdown from a recent high relative to an ATR-based drawdown limit; weight the close-to-average component most, with the other two components weighted equally. Use higher-timeframe bars when applicable, including the latest bar still forming.
Calculation: Overall score = weighted average of available relative-strength, earnings-quality, and trend scores, renormalized over available components; earnings quality is unavailable from the specified OHLCV data, so omit it. Use higher-timeframe bars when applicable, including the latest bar still forming.
Long: Overall score is at or above the buy threshold for two consecutive bars AND history is sufficient AND data coverage and confidence meet their buy thresholds AND the required benchmark is available AND (the safety filter is disabled OR close is above the long-term average AND drawdown from the recent high meets its limit AND, for equities, average price-times-volume meets its minimum; for ETFs, if volume is available, average price-times-volume meets its minimum) AND ((these good-buy conditions were false on the previous bar AND the strong-buy conditions are false) OR (strong-buy conditions are true now and were false on the previous bar; relative-strength score meets its strong threshold AND trend score meets its strong threshold AND data coverage and confidence meet their strong thresholds AND drawdown meets the stricter of its normal limit and a tighter limit AND, for equities, earnings quality meets its strong threshold))
