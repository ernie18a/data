Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting at the start of each trading day; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Volume-weighted delta = EMA of candle pressure × volume ratio; candle pressure = ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), or zero when high = low; volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), capped at a threshold, or one when volume is unavailable or its SMA is zero.
Calculation: Velocity = EMA of (close - close a velocity-lookback number of bars ago) / ATR, using the same smoothing length as volume-weighted delta.
Calculation: Acceleration = velocity - velocity a velocity-lookback number of bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish count = number of conditions true among volume-weighted delta > positive delta threshold, close > session VWAP, and velocity > positive velocity threshold. Bearish count = number of conditions true among volume-weighted delta < negative delta threshold, close < session VWAP, and velocity < negative velocity threshold; the negative thresholds are the negatives of the corresponding positive thresholds.
Calculation: Signal stop distance = ATR on the signal bar × stop multiplier; retain this distance and the signal-bar close until the next signal.
Long: Bullish count > bearish count.
Short: Bearish count > bullish count.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias filter enabled AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe session VWAP, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar on realtime chart bars.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias filter enabled AND higher-timeframe close <= higher-timeframe session VWAP, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar on realtime chart bars.
Long: (Bullish count >= bearish count AND volume-weighted delta > positive delta threshold) OR (bullish count < bearish count AND volume-weighted delta < negative delta threshold).
Short: (Bullish count >= bearish count AND volume-weighted delta <= positive delta threshold) OR (bullish count < bearish count AND volume-weighted delta >= negative delta threshold).
Long: (Bullish count >= bearish count AND close > session VWAP) OR (bullish count < bearish count AND close < session VWAP).
Short: (Bullish count >= bearish count AND close <= session VWAP) OR (bullish count < bearish count AND close >= session VWAP).
Long: (Bullish count >= bearish count AND velocity > positive velocity threshold) OR (bullish count < bearish count AND velocity < negative velocity threshold).
Short: (Bullish count >= bearish count AND velocity <= positive velocity threshold) OR (bullish count < bearish count AND velocity >= negative velocity threshold).
Long: ((close <= session VWAP - ATR × band multiplier + ATR × reversal-zone multiplier AND volume-weighted delta > its previous-bar value AND acceleration > 0) OR (close crosses above session VWAP) OR (close > session VWAP AND volume-weighted delta crosses above a positive momentum threshold) OR (close > session VWAP - ATR × band multiplier AND close < session VWAP + ATR × bounce-zone multiplier AND volume-weighted delta > its previous-bar value AND its previous-bar value > its value two bars ago AND volume-weighted delta > a positive bounce threshold)) AND bullish count >= minimum count AND (session filter disabled OR bar opens within either configured UTC weekday session) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe session VWAP) AND bars since either signal >= cooldown; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar on realtime chart bars.
Short: ((close >= session VWAP + ATR × band multiplier - ATR × reversal-zone multiplier AND volume-weighted delta < its previous-bar value AND acceleration < 0) OR (close crosses below session VWAP) OR (close < session VWAP AND volume-weighted delta crosses below the negative of the momentum threshold) OR (close < session VWAP + ATR × band multiplier AND close > session VWAP - ATR × bounce-zone multiplier AND volume-weighted delta < its previous-bar value AND its previous-bar value < its value two bars ago AND volume-weighted delta < the negative of the bounce threshold)) AND bearish count >= minimum count AND (session filter disabled OR bar opens within either configured UTC weekday session) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe session VWAP) AND bars since either signal >= cooldown; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming higher-timeframe bar on realtime chart bars.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - signal stop distance OR high >= entry price + signal stop distance × final target multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + signal stop distance OR low <= entry price - signal stop distance × final target multiplier.
