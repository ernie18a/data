Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low with the specified number of bars on each side; it becomes available only after the right-hand bars complete.
Calculation: Sweep = for each confirmed swing, track whether its level has been broken by a close and whether a wick sweep has already occurred. Before a break, a high-swing wick sweep requires high > swing high AND close < swing high; a low-swing wick sweep requires low < swing low AND close > swing low. After a close above a swing high, a bullish breakout retest requires low < that level AND close > that level; after a close below a swing low, a bearish breakout retest requires high > that level AND close < that level. Remove a broken high after close < its level, a broken low after close > its level, and either swing after its breakout retest or maximum age. A wick sweep can occur only once per swing. Wick and breakout-retest modes can be enabled independently.
Calculation: Sweep zone = for a bearish sweep, the interval from the swept level to the sweep bar's high; for a bullish sweep, the interval from the sweep bar's low to the swept level. A zone remains active until its maximum age, close < its bottom for a bullish zone, or close > its top for a bearish zone. Evaluate the first top touch before invalidation, including on the creation bar.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a bullish gap has low > high two bars ago and size low minus that high > ATR times a multiplier; a bearish gap has high < low two bars ago and size that low minus high > ATR times a multiplier. Store its top and bottom until it completes inversion or exceeds its maximum age.
Calculation: Change in state = arm bullish at a bullish sweep and store that bar's high; arm bearish at a bearish sweep and store that bar's low. The sweep requirement and the requirement that the sweep close be beyond the swept level in the sweep direction can each be disabled. A new arm replaces the opposite arm and resets its own level and age. Trigger once when a later close exceeds the stored high for bullish or falls below the stored low for bearish, within the validity window.
Calculation: Order block = a bullish block forms when the previous bar is bearish and its low is below the low two bars ago, the current bar is bullish with body >= ATR times a displacement multiplier, low > high two bars ago with gap size >= ATR times a gap multiplier, and close > the latest confirmed swing high. A bearish block reverses these inequalities. Its initial bounds are the previous bar's high and low; merge overlapping same-side blocks into their combined bounds, remove merged blocks, and keep at most the configured number of most recent blocks per side. A block is tapped on its first bar with low <= top AND high >= bottom, including its creation bar. After testing for a tap, invalidate a bullish block when low < bottom or, under the alternative mitigation rule, close < bottom; invalidate a bearish block when high > top or, under that alternative, close > top.
Calculation: Dynamic support and resistance zones = create a zone around each confirmed swing at pivot price plus and minus the ATR at the pivot times a width multiplier. On confirmation, merge it into the oldest active zone whose bounds overlap or whose midpoint is within current ATR times a merge multiplier, expanding the bounds; otherwise create a new zone. On every bar a zone is support if close >= its midpoint and resistance otherwise. After processing touches, retain at most the configured number of newest zones of each side. A support rejection is high >= bottom AND low <= top AND close > top; a resistance rejection is high >= bottom AND low <= top AND close < bottom.
Calculation: Zero-lag trend = on each selected other timeframe, compute EMA of close + (close - close at floor((length - 1) / 2) bars ago), and a band width equal to a multiplier times the highest ATR over a longer lookback than the EMA and ATR length. Initialize trend to neutral; set it bullish when close crosses above basis plus width and bearish when close crosses below basis minus width; otherwise retain its previous state. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar historically and the forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar.
Calculation: Directional bias score = sum the latest unexpired change-in-state, completed inverse-gap, order-block, support/resistance, and sweep directions, each bullish = +1, bearish = -1, absent = 0. Each event remains current for its configured validity window and is replaced by the next event in its category. The order-block category records creation and strong-block taps. The support/resistance category uses the latest unexpired rejection, otherwise the currently selected zone's side; select the nearest visible chart-timeframe zone, then consider higher-timeframe zones within the configured ATR distance, with designated major zones taking priority in processing order. Higher-timeframe zones use the latest confirmed pivot and ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the forming bar in real time; their side is determined by close relative to the zone midpoint.
Long: close >= any one of the short-, medium-, or long-lookback SMAs or EMAs.
Short: close < any one of the short-, medium-, or long-lookback SMAs or EMAs.
Long: close > previous close OR RSI > an upper threshold.
Short: close <= previous close OR RSI < a lower threshold.
Long: zero-lag trend = bullish on any selected higher or lower timeframe.
Short: zero-lag trend = bearish OR neutral on any selected higher or lower timeframe.
Long: bullish sweep occurs.
Short: bearish sweep occurs.
Long: an active bullish sweep zone has not previously been touched AND high >= its top.
Short: an active bearish sweep zone has not previously been touched AND high >= its top.
Long: an active bearish fair value gap has high > its bottom AND close <= its top, OR close > its top.
Short: an active bullish fair value gap has low < its top AND close >= its bottom, OR close < its bottom.
Long: bullish change in state triggers.
Short: bearish change in state triggers.
Long: a bullish order block forms OR an active bullish order block is tapped.
Short: a bearish order block forms OR an active bearish order block is tapped.
Long: a dynamic support zone is rejected.
Short: a dynamic resistance zone is rejected.
Long: the selected chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe support/resistance zone has close >= its midpoint.
Short: the selected chart-timeframe or higher-timeframe support/resistance zone has close < its midpoint.
Long: directional bias score >= the positive bias threshold.
Short: directional bias score <= the negative bias threshold.

Short: on a selected other timeframe, high < low two bars earlier AND gap size >= that timeframe's ATR times a multiplier; use the last completed bar for a higher timeframe historically, the forming bar in real time, or the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe.
