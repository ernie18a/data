Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the specified number of right-hand bars, using separate left- and right-hand lookbacks; retain the latest confirmed value of each, updating it before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low. A crossover compares the current close and current swing level and the previous close and previous swing level. Trend starts neutral and becomes bullish on a bullish break and bearish on a bearish break. A bullish/bearish change of character is the corresponding break while the preceding trend is bearish/bullish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = high and low of the nearest bearish/bullish candle within a specified prior-bar lookback when the corresponding structure break occurs. Retain the previous block if no qualifying candle is found.
Calculation: Confidence = fraction of successful outcomes among the specified number of nearest stored signal features by Lorentzian distance, or zero if fewer stored outcomes exist. Features are RSI divided by its scale, volume divided by its moving average plus a small constant then capped and scaled, and ADX capped and scaled. Distance is the sum over features of log(1 + absolute feature difference); resolve equal distances in storage order. Store features only for qualifying long or short signals. After the specified outcome delay, evaluate only that bar: a long succeeds if its high reaches the signal bar's low plus ATR times a stop multiplier times a reward-to-risk multiplier; a short succeeds if its low reaches the signal bar's high minus the same ATR-based distance. Otherwise the outcome is failure, including when neither target nor stop is reached. Add resolved outcomes before evaluating new signals, processing simultaneously due signals newest first, and discard the oldest stored outcomes beyond the history limit. History starts empty.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low.
Long: Low exceeds the high of a prior bar at a specified lag AND the difference exceeds ATR times a gap threshold.
Short: High is below the low of a prior bar at a specified lag AND the difference exceeds ATR times a gap threshold.
Long: (Bullish change of character OR (low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low)) AND low <= bullish order-block high AND close >= bullish order-block low minus ATR times a tolerance multiplier AND confidence >= confidence threshold.
Short: (Bearish change of character OR (high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high)) AND high >= bearish order-block low AND close <= bearish order-block high plus ATR times a tolerance multiplier AND confidence >= confidence threshold.
