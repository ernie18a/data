Calculation: Resistance = latest pivot high, confirmed using left and right lookback bars, delayed by one additional chart bar, and carried forward
Calculation: Support = latest pivot low, confirmed using left and right lookback bars, delayed by one additional chart bar, and carried forward
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) ÷ longer-lookback EMA of volume
Long: Close crosses above Resistance AND (Volume oscillator > volume threshold OR Open − Low > Close − Open)
Short: Close crosses below Support AND (Volume oscillator > volume threshold OR Open − Close < High − Open)
