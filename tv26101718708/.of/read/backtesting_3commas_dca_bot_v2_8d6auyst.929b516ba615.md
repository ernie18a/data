Calculation: Indicator bars = chart bars, or bars aggregated to a selected higher timeframe using the last completed bar on historical data and the still-forming bar in real time, or bars aggregated to a selected lower timeframe using the last intrabar in each chart bar. Indicator values and the base-order limit price use the same selected indicator bar.
Calculation: Technical rating = mean of the available moving-average rating and oscillator rating. Each component rating is the mean of its available votes; each vote is +1 for its bullish condition, -1 for its bearish condition, and 0 otherwise, with the bullish condition taking precedence if both hold.
Calculation: Moving-average rating = votes comparing indicator-bar close with each of several SMAs and EMAs at corresponding progressively longer lookbacks, a Hull moving average, and a volume-weighted moving average: +1 above, -1 below, 0 equal; plus an Ichimoku vote. The Ichimoku conversion line and base line are midpoints of the highest high and lowest low over shorter and longer lookbacks; leading span A is their mean and leading span B is the midpoint over a still longer lookback, without displacement. Its bullish condition is span A > span B AND close > span A AND close < base line AND previous close < current conversion line AND close > conversion line; its bearish condition is span B > span A AND close < span B AND close > base line AND previous close > current conversion line AND close < conversion line.
Calculation: Oscillator rating = mean of available votes for the following bullish/bearish condition pairs. RSI: RSI below its lower threshold and rising / above its upper threshold and falling. Stochastic: both smoothed K and D below their lower threshold and K crosses strictly above D / both above their upper threshold and K crosses strictly below D. CCI: below its lower threshold and rising / above its upper threshold and falling. DMI: ADX above its threshold and positive DI crosses strictly above negative DI / ADX above its threshold and positive DI crosses strictly below negative DI. Awesome Oscillator: crosses above zero, or is positive for the current and previous bars and rises after falling / crosses below zero, or is negative for the current and previous bars and falls after rising; it is the shorter SMA of midpoint price minus the longer SMA. Momentum: rising / falling. MACD: MACD line above / below signal line. Stochastic RSI: close below its trend EMA, both smoothed K and D below their lower threshold, and K crosses strictly above D / close above its trend EMA, both above their upper threshold, and K crosses strictly below D. Williams %R: below its lower threshold and rising / above its upper threshold and falling. Bull-bear power: close above its trend EMA and low minus the power EMA is negative and rising / close below its trend EMA and high minus the power EMA is positive and falling. Ultimate Oscillator: above its upper threshold / below its lower threshold. Strict crosses require the previous value to be strictly on the opposite side, except crosses of zero include equality on the previous bar. Each threshold and lookback is independently configurable, with lower thresholds below upper thresholds and the usual short, medium, and long relationships for the named indicators.
Calculation: Safety-order level at index i = preceding order level multiplied by (1 minus deviation multiplied by step scale to the power i minus 1) for a long, or by (1 plus that quantity) for a short; the preceding level for the first safety order is the base-order limit price. Maximum deviation = sum of deviation multiplied by step scale to the power i minus 1 across all configured safety-order indices.
Calculation: Profit target = base-order limit price or position-average entry price, according to the selected target basis, multiplied by (1 plus profit fraction) for a long or (1 minus profit fraction) for a short. Position-average entry price is the quantity-weighted average of filled base and safety orders; base and safety order quantities equal their configured order values divided by their respective limit prices, with successive safety-order values scaled geometrically and order values optionally based on equity.
Calculation: Trailing extremum = the maximum qualifying high since the position opened for a long, initialized to zero; for a short it is the minimum qualifying low since the position opened, initialized to zero. A high or low qualifies only when it reaches the current profit target. Trailing remains active once a qualifying bar occurs until the position closes.
Long: Bot direction is long AND no open trade AND bar time is within the configured start and end times inclusive AND close > 0 AND equity > 0 AND (start-immediately mode is selected OR RSI mode is selected AND indicator-bar RSI < threshold OR technical-rating mode is selected AND technical rating > selected strength threshold); place a base-order buy limit at chart close in start-immediately mode, otherwise at indicator-bar close, filled when price reaches the limit.
Short: Bot direction is short AND no open trade AND bar time is within the configured start and end times inclusive AND close > 0 AND equity > 0 AND (start-immediately mode is selected OR RSI mode is selected AND indicator-bar RSI > threshold OR technical-rating mode is selected AND technical rating < negative selected strength threshold); place a base-order sell limit at chart close in start-immediately mode, otherwise at indicator-bar close, filled when price reaches the limit.
Long: A long deal has been started AND the configured safety-order index is within the maximum count AND price reaches its previously placed safety-order buy limit.
Short: A short deal has been started AND the configured safety-order index is within the maximum count AND price reaches its previously placed safety-order sell limit.
Exit: long, stop-loss fraction > maximum deviation AND trailing is inactive AND low < base-order limit price multiplied by (1 minus stop-loss fraction); place a stop at that level.
Exit: short, stop-loss fraction > maximum deviation AND trailing is inactive AND high > base-order limit price multiplied by (1 plus stop-loss fraction); place a stop at that level.
Exit: long, no stop-loss condition on the current bar AND trailing fraction = 0 AND price reaches the profit-target sell limit.
Exit: short, no stop-loss condition on the current bar AND trailing fraction = 0 AND price reaches the profit-target buy limit.
Exit: long, no stop-loss condition on the current bar AND trailing fraction > 0 AND trailing is active AND price reaches the stop at trailing extremum multiplied by (1 minus trailing fraction).
Exit: short, no stop-loss condition on the current bar AND trailing fraction > 0 AND trailing is active AND price reaches the stop at trailing extremum multiplied by (1 plus trailing fraction).
