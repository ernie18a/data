Calculation: VWAP bands = VWAP of a selected price source, reset by the selected session, day, or week, plus or minus a multiplier times the rolling standard deviation of that source.
Calculation: Long confirmation = RSI of close below its long threshold, close above its EMA, and MACD line above its signal line with positive histogram; combine enabled comparisons using the selected AND or OR mode, with no enabled comparisons passing automatically. The MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA lookback.
Calculation: Short confirmation = RSI of close above its short threshold, close below its EMA, and MACD line below its signal line with negative histogram; combine enabled comparisons using the selected AND or OR mode, with no enabled comparisons passing automatically. The MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA lookback.
Long: Price-cross entry enabled AND long direction enabled AND position flat AND bar is in the selected entry session AND weekday is enabled AND long confirmation passes AND ((selected entry is VWAP crossover AND close crosses above VWAP) OR (selected entry is upper-band breakout AND close crosses above the upper VWAP band) OR (selected entry is upper-band reversal AND close crosses below the upper VWAP band)).
Short: Price-cross entry enabled AND short direction enabled AND position flat AND bar is in the selected entry session AND weekday is enabled AND short confirmation passes AND ((selected entry is VWAP crossunder AND close crosses below VWAP) OR (selected entry is lower-band breakout AND close crosses below the lower VWAP band) OR (selected entry is lower-band reversal AND close crosses above the lower VWAP band)).
Exit: long, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is VWAP cross AND close crosses below VWAP.
Exit: short, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is VWAP cross AND close crosses above VWAP.
Exit: long OR short, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is upper-band cross AND close crosses below the upper VWAP band.
Exit: long OR short, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is lower-band cross AND close crosses above the lower VWAP band.
Exit: long, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is fixed-distance AND (high reaches entry price plus the profit distance OR low reaches entry price minus the stop distance).
Exit: short, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is fixed-distance AND (low reaches entry price minus the profit distance OR high reaches entry price plus the stop distance).
Exit: long, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is ATR-based AND (high reaches entry price plus the ATR distance OR low reaches entry price minus the ATR distance), where the ATR distance is the latest completed bar's ATR times a multiplier and, for capped ATR, is limited to a selected percentage of entry price.
Exit: short, exit strategy enabled AND selected exit is ATR-based AND (low reaches entry price minus the ATR distance OR high reaches entry price plus the ATR distance), where the ATR distance is the latest completed bar's ATR times a multiplier and, for capped ATR, is limited to a selected percentage of entry price.
Exit: long OR short, automatic close enabled AND bar closing time equals the selected close time.
