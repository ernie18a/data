Calculation: Smoothed OBV = Hull moving average of cumulative signed volume, adding volume when close rises, subtracting it when close falls, and adding zero when close is unchanged.
Calculation: Slope Z = Z-score of smoothed OBV minus its value at a slope lookback, using a rolling mean and population standard deviation over a separate normalization window; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low with configurable left and right bar counts, confirmed after the right bars have elapsed; for a high, left values are less than or equal to the pivot and right values are strictly less, with inequalities reversed for a low. Use the same left and right counts for price and smoothed OBV pivots.
Calculation: OBV structure = compare the latest two confirmed OBV highs and the latest two confirmed OBV lows. In priority order: higher high AND higher low is uptrend; lower high AND lower low is downtrend; higher high AND lower low is expanding; lower high AND higher low is contracting; otherwise, classify the latest high as higher high or lower high, or the latest low as higher low or lower low. Equal or unavailable comparisons do not qualify. A high is the latest pivot only if its bar is later than the latest low pivot bar or no low pivot exists.
Long: A smoothed OBV low pivot is confirmed.
Short: A smoothed OBV high pivot is confirmed.
Long: Slope Z > positive slope threshold.
Short: Slope Z < negative slope threshold.
Long: OBV structure = uptrend OR higher high OR higher low.
Short: OBV structure = downtrend OR lower high OR lower low.
Long: Smoothed OBV > its rolling mean over the slope Z normalization window.
Short: Smoothed OBV <= its rolling mean over the slope Z normalization window.
Long: A price low pivot confirms AND the latest price low is lower than the preceding price low AND the latest OBV low is higher than the preceding OBV low AND the price low pivots are separated by at least a minimum and at most a larger maximum number of bars AND the latest price and OBV low pivot bars are at most twice the pivot right count apart AND either the same-side slope filter is disabled OR slope Z > negative slope threshold AND either no prior bullish divergence exists OR more than pivot right count plus one bars have elapsed since it.
Short: A price high pivot confirms AND the latest price high is higher than the preceding price high AND the latest OBV high is lower than the preceding OBV high AND the price high pivots are separated by at least a minimum and at most a larger maximum number of bars AND the latest price and OBV high pivot bars are at most twice the pivot right count apart AND either the same-side slope filter is disabled OR slope Z < positive slope threshold AND either no prior bearish divergence exists OR more than pivot right count plus one bars have elapsed since it.
