Calculation: Baseline = selected SMA, EMA or HMA of close, or session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3; session VWAP resets at each new trading day.
Calculation: Entry width = selected ATR, standard deviation of close using the baseline lookback, or session volume-weighted standard deviation of (high + low + close) / 3, resetting each trading day.
Calculation: Lower band = baseline - entry width × entry-gap multiplier; upper band = baseline + entry width × entry-gap multiplier.
Calculation: Last exit close = close of the bar on which a closed trade is detected; clear it on an initial entry, or when (reversal mode AND ((last exit close < current baseline AND close > current lower band) OR (last exit close > current baseline AND close < current upper band))) OR (trending mode AND ((last exit close > current baseline AND close < current upper band) OR (last exit close < current baseline AND close > current lower band))).
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = on the first bar with high >= average entry price + ATR × profit multiplier, max(average entry price, high - ATR × profit multiplier); on subsequent bars, max(previous long trailing stop, high - ATR × profit multiplier), until flat.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = on the first bar with low <= average entry price - ATR × profit multiplier, min(average entry price, low + ATR × profit multiplier); on subsequent bars, min(previous short trailing stop, low + ATR × profit multiplier), until flat.
Long: Position is flat AND long entries are enabled AND (session restriction is disabled OR bar opening time in America/New_York is inside the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window) AND ((reversal mode AND close <= lower band for at least the configured consecutive bar count) OR (trending mode AND close >= upper band for at least the configured consecutive bar count)) AND (last exit close is unavailable OR (reversal mode AND last exit close > current lower band) OR (trending mode AND last exit close < current upper band) OR close <= last exit close - ATR × scale-step multiplier).
Short: Position is flat AND short entries are enabled AND (session restriction is disabled OR bar opening time in America/New_York is inside the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window) AND ((reversal mode AND close >= upper band for at least the configured consecutive bar count) OR (trending mode AND close <= lower band for at least the configured consecutive bar count)) AND (last exit close is unavailable OR (reversal mode AND last exit close < current upper band) OR (trending mode AND last exit close > current lower band) OR close >= last exit close + ATR × scale-step multiplier).
Long: Position is long AND (session restriction is disabled OR bar opening time in America/New_York is inside the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window) AND close <= last entry signal close - ATR × scale-step multiplier AND (Heikin-Ashi confirmation is disabled OR (Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open AND previous Heikin-Ashi close < previous Heikin-Ashi open)).
Short: Position is short AND (session restriction is disabled OR bar opening time in America/New_York is inside the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window) AND close >= last entry signal close + ATR × scale-step multiplier AND (Heikin-Ashi confirmation is disabled OR (Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open AND previous Heikin-Ashi close > previous Heikin-Ashi open)).
Exit: long, session restriction is enabled AND bar opening time in America/New_York is outside the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window.
Exit: short, session restriction is enabled AND bar opening time in America/New_York is outside the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window.
Exit: long, trailing is disabled AND (high >= average entry price + ATR × profit multiplier OR low <= initial entry signal close - ATR × stop multiplier).
Exit: short, trailing is disabled AND (low <= average entry price - ATR × profit multiplier OR high >= initial entry signal close + ATR × stop multiplier).
Exit: long, trailing is enabled AND ((long trailing stop is unavailable AND low <= initial entry signal close - ATR × stop multiplier) OR (long trailing stop is available AND low <= long trailing stop)).
Exit: short, trailing is enabled AND ((short trailing stop is unavailable AND high >= initial entry signal close + ATR × stop multiplier) OR (short trailing stop is available AND high >= short trailing stop)).
