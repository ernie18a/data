Calculation: A bullish sweep occurs when the close is below the lows of at least floor(sweep lookback × sweep fraction) bars among the current bar and the preceding sweep lookback minus one bars. A bearish sweep occurs when the close is above the highs of at least that many bars in the same range.
Calculation: For each direction independently, maintain an active sweep, its high and low, its age, and a fired flag. On each new sweep of that direction, replace the stored high and low, activate it, reset its age to zero, and reset its fired flag. Then increment the age if active. Deactivate a bullish sweep if the close is below its stored low, or a bearish sweep if the close is above its stored high. After evaluating the corresponding reversal decision, set its fired flag if that decision is true. Do not deactivate a sweep merely because it has aged beyond the confirmation window.
Calculation: The reversal evaluation bar is the previous chart bar when delayed mode is enabled and the current chart bar otherwise. Sweep state, age, fired flag, close, volume, volume SMA, and session membership are taken from the evaluation bar. Trend is evaluated on the current chart bar. Historical higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off; on a live bar they use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: Bullish sweep is active on the evaluation bar AND evaluation-bar close > stored sweep high AND fired flag is false on the evaluation bar AND sweep age <= confirmation window + 1 AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe close EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR evaluation-bar volume > evaluation-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR evaluation-bar opening time is within the configured UTC session).
Short: Bearish sweep is active on the evaluation bar AND evaluation-bar close < stored sweep low AND fired flag is false on the evaluation bar AND sweep age <= confirmation window + 1 AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe close EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR evaluation-bar volume > evaluation-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR evaluation-bar opening time is within the configured UTC session).
Long: Trend filter enabled AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe close EMA.
Short: Trend filter enabled AND higher-timeframe close <= higher-timeframe close EMA.
