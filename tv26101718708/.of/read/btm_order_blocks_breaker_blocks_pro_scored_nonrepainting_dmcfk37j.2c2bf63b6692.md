Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low with equal numbers of bars on each side; register it when the right-hand bars complete. Keep the latest swing of each type as unbroken until it is broken or replaced by a newer confirmed swing.
Calculation: Structure break = the selected close or high exceeds the latest unbroken swing high for an upward break, or the selected close or low falls below the latest unbroken swing low for a downward break. Mark that swing broken immediately, even if no order block is created.
Calculation: Candidate order block = on a structure break, search prior bars back to the broken swing, limited by a maximum lookback, for the lowest low on an upward break or highest high on a downward break; ties select the most recent bar. Select that extreme bar, or, if the opposite-candle option is selected and the extreme bar is not bearish for an upward break or bullish for a downward break, select the first opposite-colored candle among the three bars immediately preceding the extreme bar; if none exists, retain the extreme bar. The body-range option uses the selected candle's open and close as zone boundaries. The wick-range option uses its high and low, extended on the lower side to the leg low for an upward break or on the upper side to the leg high for a downward break.
Calculation: Order block quality = the sum of four Boolean points: at least one candle after the selected candle through the break bar has an absolute body at least a displacement multiple of the break bar's ATR; at least one three-candle bullish gap with the newer low above the older high for an upward break, or bearish gap with the newer high below the older low for a downward break, occurs entirely after the selected candle through the break bar; the leg low is below the latest retained confirmed swing low whose pivot bar precedes the extreme bar for an upward break, or the leg high is above the corresponding swing high for a downward break; and the selected candle's volume is at least a volume multiple of its volume SMA, provided both are available and the SMA is positive. Retain only the most recent fixed-capacity history of confirmed swings of each type.
Calculation: Valid candidate = every close after the selected candle through the break bar is at or above the lower boundary for an upward break, or at or below the upper boundary for a downward break; each enabled mandatory quality point is present, except that mandatory volume passes when volume or its SMA is unavailable or the SMA is nonpositive; quality is at least the minimum score; zone height is at most an ATR multiple using the break bar's ATR; and, when overlap filtering is enabled, the zone does not overlap an active same-direction order block.
Calculation: Active zones = add each valid candidate as a support order block after an upward break or a resistance order block after a downward break. Starting on the bar after creation, a support zone fails when the selected close or low is below its lower boundary; a resistance zone fails when the selected close or high is above its upper boundary. Remove a failed zone; if it was an order block, breakers are enabled, and the enabled break-displacement filter passes, replace it immediately with a breaker of opposite direction and the same boundaries. The break-displacement filter requires the failure bar's absolute body to be at least the displacement multiple of its ATR. Starting on the bar after creation or conversion, a nonfailed support zone is retested when low is at or below its upper boundary and the previous low was above that boundary; a nonfailed resistance zone is retested when high is at or above its lower boundary and the previous high was below that boundary. If first-retest removal is enabled, remove an order block on its first retest. After zone management, retain only the newest configured maximum number of zones for each combination of direction and order-block or breaker type.
Long: Upward structure break.
Short: Downward structure break.
Long: Upward structure break AND valid candidate.
Short: Downward structure break AND valid candidate.
Long: Active resistance order block fails AND breakers are enabled AND the enabled break-displacement filter passes.
Short: Active support order block fails AND breakers are enabled AND the enabled break-displacement filter passes.
Long: Active support order block or support breaker is retested AND it does not fail on the same bar.
Short: Active resistance order block or resistance breaker is retested AND it does not fail on the same bar.
