Calculation: ATR ratio = shorter-lookback ATR divided by longer-lookback ATR
Long: ATR ratio is between its configured lower and upper bounds AND bar time is from 15:00 through 18:59 or 20:00 through 23:59 Taiwan time AND no earlier long or short signal occurred that day AND the still-forming higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and that EMA is above its value on the previous completed higher-timeframe bar AND close is above the highest high over a lookback ending on the previous chart bar AND close is above open and the absolute difference between close and open exceeds a configured fraction of the shorter-lookback ATR
Short: ATR ratio is between its configured lower and upper bounds AND bar time is from 15:00 through 18:59 or 20:00 through 23:59 Taiwan time AND no earlier long or short signal occurred that day AND the still-forming higher-timeframe close is below its EMA and that EMA is below its value on the previous completed higher-timeframe bar AND close is below the lowest low over a lookback ending on the previous chart bar AND close is below open and the absolute difference between close and open exceeds a configured fraction of the shorter-lookback ATR
