Calculation: Regression = fit a quadratic a + bx + cx² to closes in a lookback window, with x increasing from 1 at the oldest bar to the window length at the current bar. Use equal weights, or weights 1 + weight-strength × (x − 1) / window length when weighting is enabled. Calculate R² as 1 − sum of squared residuals / sum of squared deviations from the unweighted mean close, clipped to [0, 1]; use 0 when the denominator is zero. If adaptive lookback is enabled, fit five evenly spaced, rounded candidate lengths between the minimum and maximum lookbacks and select the first with the highest R²; otherwise use the configured lookback.
Calculation: Projected close at step i = evaluate the regression at window length + i. If damping is enabled, blend this value toward the tangent at the end of the fitted window, extended i bars, using damping-strength × i / projection-length as the tangent weight. If volume adjustment is enabled, multiply by 1 + volume-strength × i / projection-length × volume-impact, where volume-strength = 2 × (sum of volume on bars with close > open / total volume over the volume lookback − 0.5). If the EMA filter is enabled and the projection is below current close while close > EMA, or above current close while close ≤ EMA, multiply its distance from close by 1 − counter-trend-reduction. If the RSI filter is enabled, multiply its distance from close by 0.3 when RSI is above its upper threshold and the projection is above close, or RSI is below its lower threshold and the projection is below close. If support/resistance capping is enabled and both a confirmed pivot high and pivot low exist on the current bar, replace a projection above the pivot high with 0.98 × pivot high when close is below it, and replace a projection below the pivot low with 1.02 × pivot low when close is above it; both pivots use the same number of bars on each side and are calculated from high. If confidence scaling is enabled, multiply the projection's distance from close by R². The projected open at step 1 is current close; at each later step it is the preceding step's projected close.
Long: For each projection step, projected close ≥ projected open AND regression exists AND (confidence display is disabled OR R² ≥ confidence threshold).
Short: For each projection step, projected close < projected open AND regression exists AND (confidence display is disabled OR R² ≥ confidence threshold).
