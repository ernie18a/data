Calculation: Forecast close = Fit a weighted quadratic regression to recent closes, assigning linearly greater weights to newer bars; if adaptive lookback is enabled, use the tested lookback with highest R². Extrapolate each future step. When enabled, blend farther steps toward the fitted curve’s tangent at the end of the lookback; adjust by signed up-bar versus down-bar volume, reduce moves against the close-versus-EMA trend, soften moves further in the RSI overbought/oversold direction, cap moves beyond the nearest confirmed pivot resistance/support, and scale the move from current close by R². The first step starts from current close; each later step starts from the previous forecast close.
Long: Forecast close >= preceding forecast close AND projection is not hidden by the R² confidence threshold.
Short: Forecast close < preceding forecast close AND projection is not hidden by the R² confidence threshold.
