Calculation: Volume Zone Oscillator = Let relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, VZO length). Let base momentum = EMA(EMA(close − prior close, smoothing length) × EMA(relative volume, smoothing length), smoothing length). Let positive momentum = EMA(max(base momentum, 0), VZO length), and negative momentum = EMA(abs(min(base momentum, 0)), VZO length). Let ratio = positive momentum / negative momentum when negative momentum > 0.00001; otherwise 100 when positive momentum > 0.00001, else 1. Raw oscillator = 100 × (ratio − 1) / (ratio + 1). Fourier-smoothed oscillator = weighted average of the raw oscillator over the current and prior Fourier-window bars, with weight exp(−i / (0.3 × Fourier window)) for bar i bars ago. Trend multiplier = 1 + clamp(0.2 × (SMA(raw oscillator, max(1, floor(VZO length / 3))) − SMA(raw oscillator, VZO length)) / standard deviation(raw oscillator, VZO length), −0.1, 0.1); use 1 when standard deviation is zero. Volume Zone Oscillator = clamp((raw oscillator × (1 − Fourier blend) + Fourier-smoothed oscillator × Fourier blend) × trend multiplier, −100, 100). The smoothing length is shorter than the VZO length; the short SMA lookback in the trend multiplier is approximately one third of its long lookback.
Calculation: Signal = simple moving average of Volume Zone Oscillator over the signal length.
Long: Volume Zone Oscillator > 20 OR (Volume Zone Oscillator is above Signal on this bar and was at or below Signal on the prior bar, with this bar confirmed) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, its price low is below the prior confirmed oscillator pivot low's price low and its oscillator value is above that prior pivot's oscillator value) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, its price low is above the prior confirmed oscillator pivot low's price low and its oscillator value is below that prior pivot's oscillator value). Pivot confirmation uses the configured number of chart bars on both sides; compare each pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type.
Short: Volume Zone Oscillator < −20 OR (Volume Zone Oscillator is below Signal on this bar and was at or above Signal on the prior bar, with this bar confirmed) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, its price high is above the prior confirmed oscillator pivot high's price high and its oscillator value is below that prior pivot's oscillator value) OR (at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, its price high is below the prior confirmed oscillator pivot high's price high and its oscillator value is above that prior pivot's oscillator value). Pivot confirmation uses the configured number of chart bars on both sides; compare each pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type.
