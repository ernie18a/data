Calculation: Raw oscillator = let relative volume = volume / max(SMA(volume, primary lookback), 0.0001); momentum = EMA(EMA(close - previous close, smoothing lookback) * EMA(relative volume, smoothing lookback), smoothing lookback); positive = EMA(max(momentum, 0), primary lookback); negative = EMA(abs(min(momentum, 0)), primary lookback); ratio = positive / negative if negative > 0.00001, otherwise 100 if positive > 0.00001, otherwise 1; return 100 * (ratio - 1) / (ratio + 1).
Calculation: Oscillator = let window = min(Fourier lookback, number of available chart bars); weighted oscillator = sum over i = 0 to window - 1 of (raw oscillator i bars ago, replacing unavailable values with 0) * exp(-i / (window * 0.3)), divided by the sum of those weights; trend = (SMA(raw oscillator, max(1, primary lookback / 3)) - SMA(raw oscillator, primary lookback)) / standard deviation(raw oscillator, primary lookback), or 0 if that standard deviation is 0; multiplier = 1 + clamp(trend * 0.2, -0.1, 0.1); return clamp(((1 - blend weight) * raw oscillator + blend weight * weighted oscillator) * multiplier, -100, 100).
Calculation: Signal = SMA(oscillator, signal lookback).
Calculation: Flow = (oscillator - EMA(oscillator, flow lookback)) * 0.5.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot low = an oscillator value at a past pivot bar with the specified number of preceding oscillator values greater than or equal to it and following oscillator values strictly greater than it; confirm it on the last required following bar. A confirmed oscillator pivot high uses preceding values less than or equal to the pivot and following values strictly less than it. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed oscillator pivot of the same type, using the low or high at each oscillator pivot bar.
Long: Oscillator crosses above signal on a completed chart bar.
Short: Oscillator crosses below signal on a completed chart bar.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its price low < the previous pivot low's price low AND its oscillator > the previous pivot low's oscillator.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed AND its price low > the previous pivot low's price low AND its oscillator < the previous pivot low's oscillator.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its price high > the previous pivot high's price high AND its oscillator < the previous pivot high's oscillator.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed AND its price high < the previous pivot high's price high AND its oscillator > the previous pivot high's oscillator.
Long: Oscillator >= 0 OR signal > 0 OR flow >= 0 OR oscillator > signal OR oscillator > positive regime threshold.
Short: Oscillator <= 0 OR signal <= 0 OR flow < 0 OR oscillator <= signal OR oscillator < negative regime threshold.
