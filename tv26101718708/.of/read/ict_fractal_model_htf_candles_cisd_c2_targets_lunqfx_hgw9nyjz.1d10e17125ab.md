Calculation: Model state = Evaluate on each closed chart bar using a higher timeframe. Track one model at a time. When searching, except on the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe candle, compare the forming higher-timeframe candle C2 with the last completed higher-timeframe candle C1. Start a bullish model if C2 low < C1 low AND C2 high <= C1 high; start a bearish model if C2 high > C1 high AND C2 low >= C1 low. Store C1 and the sweep extreme. While C2 forms, update the extreme on each deeper sweep. At the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe candle, confirm the model if completed C2 has not exceeded both C1 boundaries AND either C2 close is strictly inside C1 or the configurable inside-close requirement is disabled; otherwise discard it. A confirmed model ends on invalidation, the opposite C1 boundary being reached, or the first chart bar after C3 completes. Check invalidation before targets; do not check targets on the confirmation bar. After a model ends, resume searching on the same chart bar if it is not the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe candle.
Calculation: Delivery level = At a sweep or deeper sweep, store the open of the first chart bar in the most recent consecutive run of down-closing bars for a bullish model, or up-closing bars for a bearish model; a doji or opposite-direction bar ends a run. On each subsequent chart bar, before updating the sweep or confirming C2, signal the first close above the stored level for a bullish model or below it for a bearish model. A deeper sweep updates the level but does not reset an already signaled delivery change.
Calculation: Target levels = C1 midpoint and the opposite C1 boundary. At confirmation, mark the midpoint as reached if completed C2 close is at or beyond it in the model direction. Subsequently, check the midpoint and opposite boundary in order, allowing both to be reached on the same bar. Invalidation is the final C2 sweep extreme.
Long: A bullish model starts OR a bullish model has its first close above its delivery level OR a bullish model is confirmed.
Short: A bearish model starts OR a bearish model has its first close below its delivery level OR a bearish model is confirmed.
Exit: long, a confirmed bullish model has chart-bar low < its invalidation level OR chart-bar high >= its next unreached target level OR C3 has completed.
Exit: short, a confirmed bearish model has chart-bar high > its invalidation level OR chart-bar low <= its next unreached target level OR C3 has completed.
