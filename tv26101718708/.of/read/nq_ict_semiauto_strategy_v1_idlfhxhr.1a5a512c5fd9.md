Calculation: Previous-day high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Latest swing high and low = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each identified using the same number of chart bars on both sides of the pivot.
Calculation: Upper sweep reference = maximum of the previous-day high and latest swing high, or the previous-day high if no swing high exists.
Calculation: Lower sweep reference = minimum of the previous-day low and latest swing low, or the previous-day low if no swing low exists.
Long: low < lower sweep reference AND close > lower sweep reference
Long: close > latest swing high
Short: high > upper sweep reference AND close < upper sweep reference
Short: close < latest swing low
