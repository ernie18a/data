Calculation: RSI and its signal line = Wilder RSI of the selected price source and an SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or volume-weighted moving average of that RSI. Calculate both on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe, select either the last completed bar or the bar still forming. SMA with Bollinger Bands uses the SMA as the signal line.
Calculation: Bull regime = the lowest RSI over the regime lookback is at or above the bull floor AND the highest RSI over that lookback is above the bear ceiling. Bear regime = the highest RSI over the same lookback is at or below the bear ceiling AND the lowest RSI over that lookback is below the bull floor.
Calculation: Bull score = the number of these conditions satisfied: RSI is above its midpoint; RSI is above its signal line; the signal line is above the RSI midpoint; RSI minus its value a slope-lookback number of chart bars ago is above the slope threshold; bull regime is true.
Calculation: RSI pivot pair = the current confirmed RSI pivot low or high and the immediately preceding confirmed RSI pivot of the same type. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of right-side chart bars, using the specified left- and right-side pivot lookbacks. Compare RSI and chart-bar low or high at the pivot bars; require the distance between the pivot bars to be within the specified minimum and maximum, inclusive.
Long: RSI is at or above its midpoint.
Short: RSI is below its midpoint.
Long: RSI is above its signal line.
Short: RSI is at or below its signal line.
Long: Bull regime is true.
Short: Bear regime is true.
Long: Bull regime is true AND RSI is below its signal line.
Short: Bear regime is true AND RSI is above its signal line.
Long: Bull score is 4 or 5 AND the bull-regime pullback and bear-regime bounce conditions are both false.
Short: Bull score is 0 or 1 AND the bull-regime pullback and bear-regime bounce conditions are both false.
Long: RSI crosses above its signal line.
Short: RSI crosses below its signal line.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot-low pair has a lower price low AND a higher RSI low.
Long: A confirmed RSI pivot-low pair has a higher price low AND a lower RSI low.
Short: A confirmed RSI pivot-high pair has a higher price high AND a lower RSI high.
Short: A confirmed RSI pivot-high pair has a lower price high AND a higher RSI high.
Exit: long, RSI crosses below the overbought level.
Exit: short, RSI crosses above the oversold level.
