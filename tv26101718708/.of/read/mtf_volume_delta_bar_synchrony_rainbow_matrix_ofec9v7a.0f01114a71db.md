Calculation: Volume percentile rank = current volume’s percentile rank among recent volume bars on the same timeframe; calculate separately for each configured timeframe. For bars from a higher timeframe, use its last completed bar; for bars from a lower timeframe, use its last completed bar. The forming bar may update live.
Calculation: Master volume percentile = weighted average of the five volume percentile ranks, ordered from fastest to slowest timeframe, with the greatest weights on the middle two.
Calculation: Confirmed volume percentile pivot high/low = a local high/low in the volume percentile series, confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides; pair each pivot with that bar’s price high/low and retain the two most recent pivots.
Long: At a newly confirmed volume percentile pivot low, its price low is below the prior pivot’s price low AND its volume percentile is above the prior pivot’s percentile (on any configured timeframe or on the chart timeframe using the Master volume percentile) OR the fastest timeframe’s volume percentile is at or below the lower strong-divergence threshold AND the slowest timeframe’s percentile is at or above the upper strong-divergence threshold OR the fastest percentile is at or below the lower moderate-divergence threshold AND the slowest percentile is at or above the upper moderate-divergence threshold AND the strong-divergence condition is false OR close > open.
Short: At a newly confirmed volume percentile pivot high, its price high is above the prior pivot’s price high AND its volume percentile is below the prior pivot’s percentile (on any configured timeframe or on the chart timeframe using the Master volume percentile) OR the fastest timeframe’s volume percentile is at or above the upper strong-divergence threshold AND the slowest timeframe’s percentile is at or below the lower strong-divergence threshold OR the fastest percentile is at or above the upper moderate-divergence threshold AND the slowest percentile is at or below the lower moderate-divergence threshold AND the strong-divergence condition is false OR close < open.
