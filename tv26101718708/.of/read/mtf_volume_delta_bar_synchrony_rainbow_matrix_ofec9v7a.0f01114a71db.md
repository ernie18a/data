Calculation: Timeframe volume percentile = percentile rank of volume over a configurable lookback on each of five increasingly long timeframes. On a higher-than-chart timeframe, use the last completed bar; on a lower-than-chart timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. Use the chart bar when the timeframes match.
Calculation: Composite volume percentile = weighted average of the available timeframe volume percentiles, with equal highest weights on the two middle-long timeframes, a lower weight on the second-shortest timeframe, and equal lowest weights on the shortest and longest timeframes; normalize by the weights of available values, use the percentile-scale midpoint if none are available, and clamp to the percentile scale.
Calculation: Volume pivot = a high or low of a volume percentile series with the same configurable number of bars on each side; evaluate it only when the right-side bars have completed. Record the price high or low on the pivot bar. Apply this calculation independently to each timeframe's volume percentile and corresponding price bars, and to the composite volume percentile and chart price bars. For other timeframes, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Chart close > chart open.
Long: Close > open on any of the five timeframes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: A volume pivot low is confirmed AND its price low < the price low at the preceding volume pivot low AND its volume percentile > the preceding volume pivot low.
Short: Chart close < chart open.
Short: Close < open on any of the five timeframes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Short: A volume pivot high is confirmed AND its price high > the price high at the preceding volume pivot high AND its volume percentile < the preceding volume pivot high.
Long: Shortest-timeframe volume percentile <= a low threshold AND longest-timeframe volume percentile >= a high threshold.
Short: Shortest-timeframe volume percentile >= a high threshold AND longest-timeframe volume percentile <= a low threshold.
