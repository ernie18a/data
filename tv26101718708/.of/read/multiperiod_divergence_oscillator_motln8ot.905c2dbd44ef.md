Calculation: Buying-pressure oscillator = 100 × (4 × short-window ratio + 2 × medium-window ratio + long-window ratio) / 7, where each ratio is the window sum of buying pressure divided by the window sum of true range, buying pressure = close − min(low, previous close), and true range = max(high, previous close) − min(low, previous close).
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low with the same parameterized number of chart bars on each side; evaluate it on the bar that confirms it. Compare its price and oscillator value at the pivot bar with those at the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = forward-fill the last completed higher-timeframe high, low, and oscillator onto chart bars; identify high and low pivots in these forwarded series using equal left and right chart-bar lengths. Set the bias bullish when a newly confirmed low pivot has a lower low and higher oscillator than the preceding low pivot; set it bearish when a newly confirmed high pivot has a higher high and lower oscillator than the preceding high pivot. Retain the latest bias until the opposite condition occurs; initially neither bias is set.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over the regime lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Long: Buying-pressure oscillator >= midpoint OR buying-pressure oscillator < oversold threshold OR higher-timeframe bias = bullish.
Short: Buying-pressure oscillator < midpoint OR buying-pressure oscillator > overbought threshold OR higher-timeframe bias = bearish.
Long: A low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding low pivot <= maximum pivot separation AND (its low < preceding low AND its oscillator > preceding oscillator OR hidden divergences enabled AND its low > preceding low AND its oscillator < preceding oscillator) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe bias = bullish) AND (regime gate disabled OR gate mode = trending only AND efficiency ratio > trend threshold OR gate mode = ranging only AND efficiency ratio <= trend threshold OR gate mode = match type AND (regular divergence AND efficiency ratio <= trend threshold OR hidden divergence AND efficiency ratio > trend threshold)).
Short: A high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding high pivot <= maximum pivot separation AND (its high > preceding high AND its oscillator < preceding oscillator OR hidden divergences enabled AND its high < preceding high AND its oscillator > preceding oscillator) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe bias = bearish) AND (regime gate disabled OR gate mode = trending only AND efficiency ratio > trend threshold OR gate mode = ranging only AND efficiency ratio <= trend threshold OR gate mode = match type AND (regular divergence AND efficiency ratio <= trend threshold OR hidden divergence AND efficiency ratio > trend threshold)).
Long: Higher-timeframe confirmation enabled AND a low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding low pivot <= maximum pivot separation AND (its low < preceding low AND its oscillator > preceding oscillator OR hidden divergences enabled AND its low > preceding low AND its oscillator < preceding oscillator) AND neither confirmed long nor confirmed short divergence is present.
Short: Higher-timeframe confirmation enabled AND a high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding high pivot <= maximum pivot separation AND (its high > preceding high AND its oscillator < preceding oscillator OR hidden divergences enabled AND its high < preceding high AND its oscillator > preceding oscillator) AND neither confirmed long nor confirmed short divergence is present AND no raw bullish divergence is present.
