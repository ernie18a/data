Calculation: Directional indicators = Wilder-smoothed positive and negative directional movement divided by Wilder-smoothed true range, multiplied by 100; positive movement is max(high − previous high, 0), negative movement is max(previous low − low, 0), and only the larger is retained, with both zero on a tie. ADX is the Wilder average of 100 × absolute difference of the directional indicators divided by their sum; use zero when a denominator is zero.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR divided by its SMA over three times the ATR lookback, or 1 if that SMA is not positive. Trending means ADX ≥ trend threshold; volatile means volatility ratio ≥ expansion threshold AND ADX < trend threshold; ranging means volatility ratio ≤ contraction threshold AND ADX < trend threshold AND volatility ratio < expansion threshold.
Calculation: Directional bias score = for long, 30 if fast close EMA > slow close EMA > trend close EMA, plus 20 if close > both fast and slow EMAs, plus 20 if close > trend EMA, plus 30 if positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator; for short, reverse all inequalities. Fast, slow, and trend EMAs use progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Directional momentum score = for long, 1 if RSI > bullish threshold, plus 1 if RSI > RSI three bars earlier, plus 1 if MACD histogram > 0 AND > its previous value; for short, 1 if RSI < bearish threshold, plus 1 if RSI < RSI three bars earlier, plus 1 if MACD histogram < 0 AND < its previous value.
Calculation: Swing levels = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, using independently configurable numbers of left and right bars. Before a pivot low exists, substitute current low for structure alignment; before a pivot high exists, substitute current high. A low sweep requires an existing pivot low AND low < pivot low AND close > pivot low; a high sweep requires an existing pivot high AND high > pivot high AND close < pivot high.
Calculation: Directional structure score = for long, 1 if close > latest swing low or its fallback, plus 1 if close > fast EMA; for short, 1 if close < latest swing high or its fallback, plus 1 if close < fast EMA.
Calculation: Smoothed pressure = EMA of an EMA of (close − low) / (high − low), substituting 0.5 when high = low. Pressure momentum = smoothed pressure minus its value a configured number of bars earlier. Bullish pressure is enabled AND (smoothed pressure ≥ extreme-buy threshold OR smoothed pressure > 0.5 + pressure threshold OR pressure momentum > pressure threshold); bearish pressure is enabled AND (smoothed pressure ≤ extreme-sell threshold OR smoothed pressure < 0.5 − pressure threshold OR pressure momentum < −pressure threshold).
Calculation: Squeeze release = squeeze enabled AND the previous bar's Bollinger Bands were strictly inside the Keltner Channel AND the current bar's bands are not strictly inside it. Bollinger Bands use an SMA of close and standard deviation; the Keltner Channel uses an EMA of close and ATR, each with its own lookback and multiplier.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = the count of MACD histograms above zero for long or below zero for short across three progressively higher timeframes ≥ a configured count, provided multi-timeframe analysis is enabled. On historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including a higher-timeframe bar completing on the current chart bar.
Calculation: Directional qualification score = 3 if directional bias score ≥ bias threshold, plus directional momentum score, plus directional structure score, plus 2 if trending, plus 1 if matching directional pressure, plus 1 if matching low sweep for long or high sweep for short, plus 1 if squeeze release, plus 1 if matching higher-timeframe alignment.
Calculation: Directional risk = for long, close minus the lesser of latest confirmed swing low and close − ATR × adaptive stop multiplier; for short, the greater of latest confirmed swing high and close + ATR × adaptive stop multiplier minus close. Substitute the ATR stop when the respective swing does not exist. The adaptive stop multiplier equals the base multiplier, optionally multiplied by the volatile-regime factor, the ranging-regime factor, or the trending-regime factor according to the current regime.
Calculation: Directional trailing stop = initialize to the corresponding structural/ATR stop on the entry signal bar. While long, if close − signal-bar entry price ≥ current long risk × trailing activation multiplier, raise the stop to the greater of its previous value and close − current long risk × trailing offset multiplier. While short, if signal-bar entry price − close ≥ current short risk × trailing activation multiplier, lower the stop to the lesser of its previous value and close + current short risk × trailing offset multiplier.
Calculation: Directional target = current close plus, for long, or minus, for short, current directional risk × target risk-reward multiplier × adaptive target factor. The factor uses the expansion factor when volatility ratio ≥ expansion threshold, the contraction factor when volatility ratio ≤ contraction threshold, and otherwise 1. The second target uses a larger risk-reward multiplier than the first. Stop and target orders submitted on a bar apply to subsequent price movement and are recalculated on each chart bar.
Long: Long qualification score ≥ minimum score AND (close crosses above fast EMA OR MACD histogram crosses above zero OR (RSI > RSI three bars earlier AND RSI > midpoint) OR (squeeze release AND bullish pressure) OR low sweep) AND (volatile-regime avoidance disabled OR volatile regime = false) AND (structure filter disabled OR long structure score ≥ 1) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND bars since last executed entry ≥ cooldown AND (daily trade limit disabled OR entries today < daily limit) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR long higher-timeframe alignment).
Short: Short qualification score ≥ minimum score AND (close crosses below fast EMA OR MACD histogram crosses below zero OR (RSI < RSI three bars earlier AND RSI < midpoint) OR (squeeze release AND bearish pressure) OR high sweep) AND (volatile-regime avoidance disabled OR volatile regime = false) AND (structure filter disabled OR short structure score ≥ 1) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND bars since last executed entry ≥ cooldown AND (daily trade limit disabled OR entries today < daily limit) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR short higher-timeframe alignment).
Exit: long, low ≤ active trailing stop OR high ≥ active second target OR (partial scaling enabled AND high ≥ active first target) OR (flip enabled AND short entry condition = true).
Exit: short, high ≥ active trailing stop OR low ≤ active second target OR (partial scaling enabled AND low ≤ active first target) OR (flip enabled AND long entry condition = true).
