Calculation: Oscillator = selected RSI or rate of change of a selectable data-field source, smoothed stochastic %K of close, MACD histogram of close, CCI or MFI of (high + low + close) / 3, Williams %R, or OBV. Stochastic %K uses a simple moving average; MACD uses shorter and longer EMA lengths and a signal EMA length. An external oscillator is usable only if it can be calculated from the available data.
Calculation: Pivot pair = a newly confirmed price pivot P2 and one of the most recent stored same-side pivots P1. A price pivot low or high is confirmed after its configurable number of right-hand bars, using configurable left- and right-hand pivot widths. Store its price, oscillator and oscillator percentile rank at the pivot bar; store only pivots with available oscillator and ATR values. Scan prior pivots before storing P2, retaining a configurable maximum number per side. If a pivot's percentile rank is unavailable, use the midpoint percentile.
Calculation: Pair metrics = span is the number of bars from P1 to P2; oscillator difference D is the absolute difference between their oscillator values; price difference P is the absolute difference between their prices; range R is the oscillator's highest minus lowest value over the normalization lookback ending on the confirmation bar, floored at a small positive constant; A is ATR at P2 floored at the instrument's minimum price increment. For low pivots, swing S is the highest intervening high minus the higher pivot price; for high pivots, S is the lower pivot price minus the lowest intervening low. Percentile ranks use the normalization lookback ending at each respective pivot.
Calculation: Line-of-sight margin = the minimum, across bars strictly between P1 and P2 and across both price and oscillator, of observed value minus the linearly interpolated P1-to-P2 value for low pivots, or interpolated value minus observed value for high pivots. Use lows for low pivots and highs for high pivots.
Calculation: Quality score = 100 times a weighted sum of oscillator, swing, price and, for regular divergence, zone components. Oscillator component is min(D / (0.25 × R), 1); swing component is min(S / (2 × A), 1); price component is min(P / (2 × A), 1). For regular low-pivot divergence, zone component is max(0, (25 − P1 percentile rank) / 25); for regular high-pivot divergence it is max(0, (P1 percentile rank − 75) / 25). Regular weights for oscillator, zone, swing and price are 0.40, 0.25, 0.20 and 0.15; hidden weights for oscillator, swing and price are 0.50, 0.30 and 0.20.
