Calculation: Composite moving-average rating = equal-weight mean of +1 when price is above, -1 when below, and 0 when equal to each of six SMAs with progressively longer lookbacks, six EMAs with progressively longer lookbacks, a short-lookback HMA, a medium-lookback VWMA, and an Ichimoku rating; Ichimoku gives +1 when the cloud and conversion/base-line conditions are bullish, -1 when the corresponding conditions are bearish, and 0 otherwise. Use the last completed bar of a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Long-term EMA trend = close above a long-lookback EMA. Use the last completed bar of a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Daily RSI and MFI = RSI and MFI calculated from daily bars of an equity-index futures series. On an intraday chart, use the daily bar still forming.
Calculation: Daily breadth trend = average of the latest three changes in the SMA of the daily percentage of stocks above their 100-day average; each change is current SMA minus prior SMA. Use the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Daily band position = (value − lower band) / (upper band − lower band), where the bands are an SMA plus or minus two standard deviations over a configurable lookback. Use separate configurable lookbacks for the volatility-of-volatility index, volatility-linked fund, and skew index; use the last completed daily bar.
Long: MA-rating mode AND no MA-rating sell condition AND, for each of four equity-index futures series, ((prior composite rating > its applicable threshold AND current composite rating > its applicable threshold) OR current composite rating >= 0); OR EMA-trend mode AND every series' close > its long-term EMA.
Short: MA-rating mode AND (any of four equity-index futures series' composite rating <= its applicable threshold OR (daily front-volatility-futures roll yield <= its threshold AND daily breadth trend < 0 AND (daily volatility-of-volatility band position >= its threshold OR daily volatility-linked-fund band position >= its threshold) AND neither the current nor prior daily skew band position > its threshold)); OR EMA-trend mode AND any series' close <= its long-term EMA.
Exit: long, Short condition
Exit: short, Long condition
