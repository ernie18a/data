Calculation: Bar indexing = [0] is the current chart bar; [k] is k chart bars ago.
Calculation: Bull zone = the low and high of the bar with the lowest low among [0] through [reaction lookback]; on ties choose the most recent bar. Bear zone = the low and high of the bar with the highest high over the same range; on ties choose the most recent bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes with lookahead disabled; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars, including the higher-timeframe bar completing on the current chart bar, and the still-forming bar in real time. If unavailable, use the current chart close.
Calculation: Bull confluence = one point each for: bull zone low < lowest low over the structure lookback ending at [reaction lookback + 1]; low[0] > high[2] OR low[1] > high[3]; bull zone midpoint <= (bull zone low + high[0]) / 2; close[0] > higher-timeframe EMA. Bear confluence = one point each for: bear zone high > highest high over the structure lookback ending at [reaction lookback + 1]; high[0] < low[2] OR high[1] < low[3]; bear zone midpoint >= (bear zone high + low[0]) / 2; close[0] < higher-timeframe EMA.
Calculation: Zone lifecycle = retain at most the configured number of most recently created zones per side, including invalidated zones; remove the oldest after creating a zone beyond the limit. Before creating zones on each bar, mark an existing valid zone mitigated on its first bar with low <= zone high AND high >= zone low; then invalidate a bull zone if close < zone low or a bear zone if close > zone high. A newly created zone cannot be mitigated on its creation bar. Invalidated or removed zones cannot subsequently be mitigated.
Long: close[0] > highest high over the structure lookback ending at [1] AND close[1] <= highest high over the structure lookback ending at [2] AND each enabled bull confluence filter is true AND bull confluence score >= minimum score AND bars since the last bull zone creation >= cooldown, or no bull zone has previously been created.
Short: close[0] < lowest low over the structure lookback ending at [1] AND close[1] >= lowest low over the structure lookback ending at [2] AND each enabled bear confluence filter is true AND bear confluence score >= minimum score AND bars since the last bear zone creation >= cooldown, or no bear zone has previously been created.
Long: an existing valid, unmitigated bull zone has low[0] <= zone high AND high[0] >= zone low.
Short: an existing valid, unmitigated bear zone has low[0] <= zone high AND high[0] >= zone low.
