Calculation: Anchored VWAP bands = Select a price source from open, high, low, close, HL/2, HLC/3, or OHLC/4. From the first available bar or the latest anchor reset through the current bar, calculate volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of the source, using max(volume, a small positive constant) as each bar's weight. Upper and lower bands equal the mean plus and minus a selectable positive standard-deviation multiplier. Select a daily session, regular-hours session, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly anchor. Daily and regular-hours anchors reset when bar opening time crosses their respective configured Eastern Time reset times; a manual reset time, if selected, replaces the anchor's reset rule. Weekly resets on a Sunday crossing of the configured session reset time or on the first Monday bar following a Friday or Saturday bar. Monthly resets on the last calendar day's crossing of the configured session reset time, or on the first bar of a new month if the previous bar was not on the last calendar day. Quarterly and yearly anchors reset on the first bar whose calendar quarter or year differs from the preceding bar. All bars contribute to the calculation, including outside regular hours.
Long: Close > upper anchored VWAP band AND (anchor is not regular-hours OR bar opening time is within the configured regular-hours window in Eastern Time)
Short: Close < lower anchored VWAP band AND (anchor is not regular-hours OR bar opening time is within the configured regular-hours window in Eastern Time)
