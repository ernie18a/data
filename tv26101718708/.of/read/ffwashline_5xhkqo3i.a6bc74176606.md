Calculation: Swing levels = Confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks on chart bars. Add each pivot on its confirmation bar, retaining at most a parameterized number per side, oldest first. Before scanning each bar, identify the newest pivot on each side. Remove levels older than a parameterized age or closed through, and otherwise remove every level poked on that bar. For each remaining level, increment its touch count when the bar extreme reaches within a parameterized ATR distance of it. Process newly confirmed pivots before scanning.
Calculation: Poke = For a tracked high, close <= level AND high - level > a parameterized multiple of current ATR AND upper tail / range >= a parameterized threshold. For a tracked low, close >= level AND level - low > the same ATR multiple AND lower tail / range >= the same threshold. Upper tail = high - max(open, close); lower tail = min(open, close) - low; either ratio is zero when range is zero.
Calculation: Wash lines = On a poke, create one line per side at the highest poked high or lowest poked low; record the poke bar, its extreme, ATR, tail ratio, number of same-side levels poked, selected pivot age, prior touch count, and whether that pivot was the newest on its side before scanning. When the active-line limit is reached, remove the oldest line before adding another; add high-side lines before low-side lines. Starting on the next bar, count each close beyond the line as a breach; remove the line when the close, or optionally the wick, passes the poke extreme plus a current-ATR buffer away from the line, when breaches reach a threshold, or when age exceeds a threshold. For a surviving high-side line, a touch requires a prior departure, high >= line - current-ATR tolerance, and close <= line; for a low-side line, it requires a prior departure, low <= line + current-ATR tolerance, and close >= line. Count each qualifying bar as a touch. After checking the touch, update maximum departure as the greatest line - low for a high-side line or high - line for a low-side line since the poke; departure becomes true when this maximum >= a multiple of ATR at the poke. A line can trigger only after its poke bar.
Calculation: Wash-line score = A weighted average of six components, scaled to a percentage, multiplied by a linearly declining age factor and by a parameterized breach factor raised to the cumulative breach count. Clamp each normalized component to [0,1]: poke tail quality is (poke tail ratio - minimum poke-tail ratio) / (full-score tail ratio - minimum poke-tail ratio); pivot obviousness is a weighted sum of prior pivot touches normalized to a cap, pivot freshness relative to maximum pivot age, and an indicator that it was newest; cluster quality uses parameterized scores for one, two, or at least three poked levels; departure quality is maximum departure divided by a multiple of poke-bar ATR; touch quality uses parameterized scores for zero, one, or at least two touches; room quality is room in risk units divided by a target-risk multiple. Risk is the absolute distance from the line to the poke extreme plus an ATR-at-poke stop buffer, floored by a small positive price increment. Room is the greatest positive distance from the line to any currently tracked opposing pivot, or zero if none, divided by risk. Calculate score after updating the line on the current bar.
Calculation: Selected wash line = Among surviving lines, maximize score multiplied by clamp(1 - abs(close - line) / (current ATR times a proximity parameter), 0, 1); break equal scores by smaller ATR-normalized distance, and otherwise retain the first line encountered when scanning newest to oldest.
