Calculation: Confirmed swing levels = pivot highs and lows with equal left and right lookbacks, confirmed after the right-hand bars close. Keep the latest level of each kind and mark it unbroken when confirmed; mark it broken on the first subsequent or current bar whose close exceeds the swing high or falls below the swing low, even if no zone is created.
Calculation: Origin candle = the nearest preceding candle within the impulse lookback whose close is below its open for a bullish zone or above its open for a bearish zone. Zone top and bottom are either that candle's high and low or the maximum and minimum of its open and close, according to the selected range method.
Calculation: Volume strength = origin-candle volume divided by the simple moving average of volume at the origin candle; use zero when that average is not positive.
Calculation: Active zones = accepted zones that have not been invalidated, expired or removed by the storage cap. Process new zones before updating existing zones on each closed bar. Reject a proposed zone if its range overlaps an active zone of the same direction, including at an edge. Invalidate a bullish zone when the selected source, low or close, is below its bottom; invalidate a bearish zone when the selected source, high or close, is above its top. Remove zones older than the age limit. Retain invalidated zones as breakers or historic zones when enabled; a breaker becomes historic or is removed when the selected high or close exceeds a broken bullish zone's top or the selected low or close falls below a broken bearish zone's bottom. After lifecycle updates, remove the oldest stored zones exceeding the per-side storage cap; the cap is larger when breakers or historic zones are retained.
Long: Close > latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND a bullish origin candle exists AND the proposed zone does not overlap an active bullish zone AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume strength >= its threshold) AND (the size filter is disabled OR zone height <= current ATR multiplied by its threshold) AND the selected low or close >= zone bottom AND origin-candle age <= the age limit.
Short: Close < latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND a bearish origin candle exists AND the proposed zone does not overlap an active bearish zone AND (the volume filter is disabled OR volume strength >= its threshold) AND (the size filter is disabled OR zone height <= current ATR multiplied by its threshold) AND the selected high or close <= zone top AND origin-candle age <= the age limit.
