Calculation: Green = close > open; Red = close < open
Calculation: Body = |close − open|; Range = high − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low
Calculation: TrendUp = EMA(close, trend length) − its value at the configured slope lookback > ATR × configured slope threshold
Calculation: TrendDown = EMA(close, trend length) − its value at the configured slope lookback < −ATR × configured slope threshold
Calculation: NotSideways = TrendUp OR TrendDown
Calculation: Significant lower wicks = both current and previous lower wicks are at least the configured fraction of their respective ranges and at least the configured ATR multiple
Calculation: Significant upper wicks = both current and previous upper wicks are at least the configured fraction of their respective ranges and at least the configured ATR multiple
Calculation: Bullish GUSS setup = a bar makes a higher high than the preceding lookback bars, is at least the configured ATR multiple above EMA21, and is green; then, within the configured wait period, a later bar is not green, has low above EMA21, and low at or below EMA50. EMA50 uses a longer lookback than EMA21. A green bar, a low at or below EMA21, or expiry cancels the pending setup; on a bar meeting multiple conditions, check green, then EMA21, then EMA50 touch, then expiry.
Calculation: Bearish GUSS setup = a bar makes a lower low than the preceding lookback bars, is at least the configured ATR multiple below EMA21, and is red; then, within the configured wait period, a later bar is not red, has high below EMA21, and high at or above EMA50. EMA50 uses a longer lookback than EMA21. A red bar, a high at or above EMA21, or expiry cancels the pending setup; on a bar meeting multiple conditions, check red, then EMA21, then EMA50 touch, then expiry.
