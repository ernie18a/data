Calculation: ATR = Wilder average true range of the chart timeframe
Calculation: SupplyBlock = zone with top = H and bottom = H - h, where H is the high of a source bar and h = |H - max(open, close)| of that bar clamped between a lower and an upper multiple of ATR at that bar (lower multiple < upper multiple). Source bar is either (a) a swing high bar (its high is the highest among an equal number of bars on each side; confirmed only after those bars on the right have completed) whose upper wick share (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= a minimum wick share, or (b) the current bar when its high > the latest confirmed swing high AND its close < that swing high AND its upper wick share >= the minimum wick share. The block is created on the confirmation bar for (a) and on the current bar for (b). It is dead once any close > its top (from its creation bar on), and it also expires after a maximum age in bars since creation. When the combined count of supply and demand blocks exceeds a maximum, the oldest block is dropped.
Calculation: DemandBlock = mirror of SupplyBlock: bottom = L, top = L + h, where L is the low of the source bar and h = |L - min(open, close)| clamped the same way. Source is a swing low bar with lower wick share (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= the minimum wick share, or the current bar when its low < the latest confirmed swing low AND its close > that swing low AND its lower wick share >= the minimum wick share. It is dead once any close < its bottom. Expiry and the oldest-dropped rule are the same as for SupplyBlock.
Long: there exists a live DemandBlock created on an earlier bar with high >= its bottom AND low <= its top AND close > its top AND close > open
Short: there exists a live SupplyBlock created on an earlier bar with high >= its bottom AND low <= its top AND close < its bottom AND close < open
