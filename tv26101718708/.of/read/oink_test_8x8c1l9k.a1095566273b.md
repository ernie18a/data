Calculation: Active midpoint levels = At each change in the time of the higher-timeframe bar, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it. If the older high < the newer low, create a bullish level at their average; if the older low > the newer high, create a bearish level at their average. Reject a new level if its distance from any stored midpoint < a configurable tick-distance threshold; keep at most a configurable number of levels, newest first. Before evaluating a chart bar's setup, remove bullish levels if its low <= the older high and bearish levels if its high >= the older low, except the level currently used by a setup in its K2 or K3 stage. Unblock all levels when the higher-timeframe bar time changes.
Calculation: Setup = When flat and trading is permitted, select the newest unblocked active midpoint within the chart bar's low-to-high range. If the bar opens above the midpoint, start a long setup; if it opens below, start a short setup. Call this bar K1. On each immediately following chart bar, process K2, then K3 if needed, then K4 if needed. For a long setup, invalidate and block the level if K2, K3, or K4 closes below the midpoint; confirm at K2 if its close > K1 high, otherwise at K3 if its close > K2 high, otherwise at K4 if its close > K3 high. For a short setup, invalidate and block the level if K2, K3, or K4 closes above the midpoint; confirm at K2 if its close < K1 low, otherwise at K3 if its close < K2 low, otherwise at K4 if its close < K3 low. If K4 does not confirm, block the level and end the setup. Once confirmed, retain the confirmation until a flat, permitted trading bar; then evaluate the entry and end the setup. Block the level if an entry is placed.
Calculation: Trading permitted = The chart bar's opening datetime is inside the configurable trading session if that filter is enabled AND outside all configurable excluded sessions if that filter is enabled; interpret the sessions in the specified session timezone.
Calculation: Initial stop = For a long setup, the lowest low from K1 through the confirmation bar; for a short setup, the highest high over those bars.
Calculation: Risk multiple = Round the absolute distance between the confirmation-entry bar's close and the initial stop in configurable tick units. Test the configurable distance thresholds in their stated order and select the risk multiple associated with the first threshold that is >= the rounded distance; no multiple exists if none matches.
Calculation: Target = The confirmation-entry bar's close plus the absolute distance from that close to the initial stop multiplied by the selected risk multiple for a long, or minus that distance multiplied by the selected risk multiple for a short.
Calculation: Active stop = Initially the initial stop. For a trade whose risk multiple > one, after a completed chart bar reaches the midpoint between the confirmation-entry bar's close and the target, replace the stop with that close for subsequent bars.
Long: A long setup starts at K1 OR a long setup reaches K2, K3, or K4.
Short: A short setup starts at K1 OR a short setup reaches K2, K3, or K4.
Long: A long setup is confirmed AND the position is flat AND trading is permitted AND a risk multiple exists.
Short: A short setup is confirmed AND the position is flat AND trading is permitted AND a risk multiple exists.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= target.
