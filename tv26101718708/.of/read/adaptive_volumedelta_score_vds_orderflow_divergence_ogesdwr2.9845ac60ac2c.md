Calculation: Bar delta = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high = low.
Calculation: Percent rank = 100 × the fraction of values in the lookback window, including the current value, that are less than or equal to the current value.
Calculation: Observed volume score and observed delta score = when the chart timeframe is at least the reference timeframe, use the current chart bar's volume and sum the bar deltas of its lower-timeframe bars, falling back to the chart bar delta if no lower-timeframe bars are available; use the chart timeframe for delta if the configured lower timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe. Rank chart-bar volume and the absolute delta sum separately over the same lookback. When the chart timeframe is lower than the reference timeframe, let N be the integer reference-to-chart timeframe ratio capped at 4900; sum chart-bar volume and bar delta over the latest N chart bars including the current bar, rank the volume sum over the lookback multiplied by N and capped at 4900, and rank the absolute delta sum over the unscaled lookback. In either case, observed volume score = 0.09 × volume percent rank − 4.5, and observed delta score = 0.045 × absolute delta percent rank × sign of delta sum.
Calculation: Comparison open = the current chart bar's open when the chart timeframe is at least the reference timeframe; otherwise, the open N chart bars before the current bar, where N is the integer reference-to-chart timeframe ratio capped at 4900.
Long: observed volume score >= low-volume threshold AND observed delta score > 0.
Long: observed volume score > high-volume threshold AND observed delta score > dominance threshold.
Long: observed volume score > high-volume threshold AND observed delta score > 0 AND comparison open >= close.
Short: observed volume score >= low-volume threshold AND observed delta score < 0.
Short: observed volume score > high-volume threshold AND observed delta score < −dominance threshold.
Short: observed volume score > high-volume threshold AND observed delta score < 0 AND comparison open < close.
