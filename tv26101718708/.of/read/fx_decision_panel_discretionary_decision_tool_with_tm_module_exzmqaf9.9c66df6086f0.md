Calculation: Regime = trend when chart ADX >= threshold AND ATR / SMA of ATR >= minimum ratio; otherwise range when the absolute change in the long-period chart EMA over a slope lookback < ATR × flatness multiplier; otherwise neither. Use the same ADX threshold and ADX length on both timeframes.
Calculation: Bias = in trend regime, long when the last completed higher-timeframe ADX >= threshold AND its close > its long-period EMA, short when ADX >= threshold AND close < EMA; in range regime, long when chart close <= the lower nearest-rank percentile of closes over the zone lookback, otherwise short when close >= the upper percentile; otherwise flat. Use the same long EMA length on both timeframes and a shorter EMA for pullbacks.
Calculation: Reference stop = for long bias, max(short EMA − ATR × maximum-stop multiplier, min(lower close percentile − ATR × structural buffer, short EMA − ATR × stop multiplier)); for short bias, min(short EMA + ATR × maximum-stop multiplier, max(upper close percentile + ATR × structural buffer, short EMA + ATR × stop multiplier)). Reference target = short EMA plus, for long bias, or minus, for short bias, the absolute distance from short EMA to reference stop × target multiplier.
Calculation: Invalidation = reset both sides when bias changes. For long bias, set invalidated when close <= current reference stop and clear it when close >= current reference stop + ATR × recovery multiplier; for short bias, set invalidated when close >= current reference stop and clear it when close <= current reference stop − ATR × recovery multiplier. Clear a side when its bias is absent. Evaluate setting before recovery on each bar.
Calculation: Pullback state = maintain separate long and short active states, signal prices, and bar counters. A long touch is long bias AND low <= short EMA + ATR × pullback multiplier AND close > short EMA; a short touch is short bias AND high >= short EMA − ATR × pullback multiplier AND close < short EMA. A touch activates its side, stores that bar's high for long or low for short, and resets its counter. While active, each subsequent bar increments the counter and refreshes the signal price and resets the counter whenever the corresponding price-touch conditions hold, even if bias has changed. Deactivate when the counter > memory length. After these updates, a long trigger is active long state AND close > stored high; a short trigger is active short state AND close < stored low. Each trigger deactivates its own state, even if invalidated. Process long and short independently.
Calculation: Trade levels = on an automatic entry at close, freeze the current reference stop and reference target; initial risk = absolute difference between entry price and frozen stop. On each active bar, including the entry bar, arm the trail permanently when directional close profit / initial risk >= arm threshold, treating the ratio as zero when initial risk is zero. Once armed, the long trail is the running maximum since arming of the highest high over the trailing lookback − ATR × trailing multiplier; the short trail is the running minimum since arming of the lowest low over that lookback + ATR × trailing multiplier. Effective stop is the frozen stop until armed, then the maximum of frozen stop and trail for long or minimum for short.
Long: Bias = long.
Short: Bias = short.
Long: Long pullback state = active.
Short: Short pullback state = active.
Long: Long trigger = true AND invalidation = false.
Short: Short trigger = true AND invalidation = false.
Exit: long, close <= effective stop OR close >= frozen target OR bias = flat OR bias = short.
Exit: short, close >= effective stop OR close <= frozen target OR bias = flat OR bias = long.
