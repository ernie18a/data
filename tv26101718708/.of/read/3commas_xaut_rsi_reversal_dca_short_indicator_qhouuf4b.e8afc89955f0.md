Calculation: Active window = all bars if the date filter is disabled; otherwise bars whose datetime is between the configured start and end times, inclusive.
Calculation: RSI crossunder = RSI of close on the selected timeframe is below the threshold and its previous value on that timeframe is greater than or equal to the threshold. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar and carry its crossunder result across chart bars until the next higher-timeframe bar completes; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within each chart bar; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: Averaging ladder = for rung k, cumulative deviation is the sum of the first k terms of a geometric sequence starting at the first deviation and multiplied by the deviation step multiplier at each rung; rung k has quote size equal to the first averaging-order size multiplied by the order size multiplier to the power k − 1. Fill at most the next unfilled rung per chart bar, at close, before evaluating exits.
Calculation: Average entry = sum of the base and filled averaging-order quote sizes divided by the sum of each fill's quote size divided by its close price. The base entry is the close of the initial short-entry bar.
Calculation: Trailing minimum = unset at entry; while the position is open and within the active window, whenever close is at or below the current average entry multiplied by one minus the take-profit fraction, set it to close if unset, otherwise to the smaller of its existing value and close. Retain it until exit, including when close is above the take-profit level.
Short: datetime is within the active window AND position is flat AND RSI crossunder is true.
Short: datetime is within the active window AND position is short AND filled averaging-order count is less than the configured maximum AND close is greater than or equal to base entry multiplied by one plus the next rung's cumulative deviation fraction.
Exit: short, datetime is within the active window AND close is greater than or equal to average entry multiplied by one plus the stop-loss fraction.
Exit: short, datetime is within the active window AND close is less than the stop-loss level AND trailing minimum is set AND close is greater than or equal to trailing minimum multiplied by one plus the trailing fraction.
