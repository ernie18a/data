Calculation: On-Balance Volume = cumulative volume added when close rises from the previous bar, subtracted when close falls, and unchanged when close is equal.
Calculation: Price slope = current linear-regression value of close over a lookback minus its value one bar earlier; OBV slope = the same calculation on On-Balance Volume, using the same lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = price slope < 0 AND OBV slope > 0 AND close > the simple moving average of close; bearish divergence = price slope > 0 AND OBV slope < 0 AND close < the simple moving average of close.
Calculation: Z-score = (close − simple moving average of close) / standard deviation of close, using the same lookback for both; if standard deviation is 0, Z-score = 0.
Calculation: ATR percentile threshold = the selected percentile of the recent ATR values; ATR uses a shorter lookback than the percentile window.
Calculation: Cooldown = the base bar interval after a winning or non-losing trade; after a losing trade, double the prior cooldown up to a cap. A signal is cooldown-eligible if no prior signal exists or the elapsed chart bars since it meet the current cooldown.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend average = simple moving average of close on a higher timeframe, using the current higher-timeframe bar while it is forming.
Long: bullish divergence AND bullish divergence on the previous chart bar AND cooldown-eligible
Short: bearish divergence AND bearish divergence on the previous chart bar AND cooldown-eligible
Long: bullish divergence AND bullish divergence on the previous chart bar AND cooldown-eligible AND current ATR > ATR percentile threshold AND (session filter disabled OR time is within 09:30–16:00 America/New_York) AND daily-loss kill switch inactive AND rolling-drawdown kill switch inactive AND (Z-score filter disabled OR absolute Z-score < threshold) AND close > higher-timeframe trend average AND last direction is not long AND no long position is open
Short: bearish divergence AND bearish divergence on the previous chart bar AND cooldown-eligible AND current ATR > ATR percentile threshold AND (session filter disabled OR time is within 09:30–16:00 America/New_York) AND daily-loss kill switch inactive AND rolling-drawdown kill switch inactive AND (Z-score filter disabled OR absolute Z-score < threshold) AND close < higher-timeframe trend average AND last direction is not short AND no short position is open
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR-based stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + ATR-based profit distance OR bars since entry ≥ maximum holding bars OR current ATR ≤ ATR percentile threshold OR daily-loss kill switch active OR rolling-drawdown kill switch active
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR-based stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − ATR-based profit distance OR bars since entry ≥ maximum holding bars OR current ATR ≤ ATR percentile threshold OR daily-loss kill switch active OR rolling-drawdown kill switch active
