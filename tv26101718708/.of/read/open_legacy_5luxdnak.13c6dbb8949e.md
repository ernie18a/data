Calculation: Bullish gap setup = Before updating gaps on each bar, test the most recently stored bullish gap: its age is greater than zero and at most the gap lifetime, low <= gap top, close > open, and close > gap bottom. If true, remove that gap even if an entry filter fails. Then, if low > high two bars earlier AND their difference >= the minimum gap size times ATR, store a new bullish gap with bottom = that earlier high, top = current low, and age = zero, replacing any stored bullish gap.
Calculation: Bearish gap setup = Before updating gaps on each bar, test the most recently stored bearish gap: its age is greater than zero and at most the gap lifetime, high >= gap bottom, close < open, and close < gap top. If true, remove that gap even if an entry filter fails. Then, if high < low two bars earlier AND their difference >= the minimum gap size times ATR, store a new bearish gap with top = that earlier low, bottom = current high, and age = zero, replacing any stored bearish gap.
Calculation: Bullish trend setup = Shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND close > shorter-period EMA AND RSI > its midpoint AND this conjunction was false on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Bearish trend setup = Shorter-period EMA of close < longer-period EMA of close AND close < shorter-period EMA AND RSI < its midpoint AND this conjunction was false on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Bullish sensitivity condition = Aggressive sensitivity is selected OR close > trend EMA AND (Balanced sensitivity is selected OR Conservative sensitivity is selected AND the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND ADX >= its threshold).
Calculation: Bearish sensitivity condition = Aggressive sensitivity is selected OR close < trend EMA AND (Balanced sensitivity is selected OR Conservative sensitivity is selected AND the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND ADX >= its threshold).
Calculation: Time condition = No trading session is enabled OR the bar opening datetime is within an enabled session's time window and selected days; AND the bar closing datetime is outside the inclusive pause window around every enabled news event.
Calculation: Qualified bullish setup = (Gap method is selected AND bullish gap setup OR trend method is selected AND bullish trend setup) AND bullish sensitivity condition.
Calculation: Qualified bearish setup = (Gap method is selected AND bearish gap setup OR trend method is selected AND bearish trend setup) AND bearish sensitivity condition AND qualified bullish setup is false.
Long: Qualified bullish setup AND time condition AND (position is flat OR position side is short OR the existing position's target or stop is reached on this bar).
Short: Qualified bearish setup AND time condition AND (position is flat OR position side is long OR the existing position's target or stop is reached on this bar).
Exit: long, a later bar's high >= entry price + target distance OR a later bar's low <= entry price - stop distance OR a short entry occurs.
Exit: short, a later bar's low <= entry price - target distance OR a later bar's high >= entry price + stop distance OR a long entry occurs.
