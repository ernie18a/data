Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above high two bars earlier by at least the configured minimum fraction of ATR(14); zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish FVG = current high is below low two bars earlier by at least the configured minimum fraction of ATR(14); zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]. Minimum gap fraction increases from Aggressive to Balanced to Conservative; lifetime also increases in that order.
Calculation: Entry EMAs = fast and slow EMAs, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback (Aggressive lengths are shorter than Balanced, which are shorter than Conservative).
Calculation: Take-profit and stop-loss prices = entry price plus/minus fixed configured distances, with take-profit above entry and stop-loss below entry for longs; reverse for shorts.
Long: (Entry Method is FVG Pullback AND a bullish FVG formed on an earlier bar within its configured lifetime AND current low <= FVG zone top AND current close > current open AND current close > FVG zone bottom) OR (Entry Method is EMA Trend AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND current close > fast EMA AND RSI(14) > 50 AND the preceding bar did not meet all three conditions); AND (Aggressive sensitivity OR current close > EMA(50) [Balanced or Conservative] AND, for Conservative, previous completed bar on the 4x higher timeframe closed above its EMA(50) AND ADX(14) >= 20); AND (flat or currently short); AND within any enabled trading session and outside any enabled news pause.
Short: (Entry Method is FVG Pullback AND a bearish FVG formed on an earlier bar within its configured lifetime AND current high >= FVG zone bottom AND current close < current open AND current close < FVG zone top) OR (Entry Method is EMA Trend AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND current close < fast EMA AND RSI(14) < 50 AND the preceding bar did not meet all three conditions); AND (Aggressive sensitivity OR current close < EMA(50) [Balanced or Conservative] AND, for Conservative, previous completed bar on the 4x higher timeframe closed below its EMA(50) AND ADX(14) >= 20); AND (flat or currently long); AND within any enabled trading session and outside any enabled news pause.
Exit: long, current high >= take-profit price OR current low <= stop-loss price OR a short entry signal occurs.
Exit: short, current low <= take-profit price OR current high >= stop-loss price OR a long entry signal occurs.
