Calculation: Swing high and swing low = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using the same number of bars on both sides; record each pivot's bar index when confirmed.
Calculation: Golden zone = with range = swing high − swing low, calculate the 50% and 61.8% retracement levels as swing high − range × ratio when the swing low occurred before the swing high, otherwise swing low + range × ratio; the zone bottom and top are the smaller and larger levels, respectively.
Calculation: Bars since last signal = current zero-based bar index minus the bar index of the most recent long or short signal, initialized to zero before the first signal.
Long: Swing high − swing low > 0 AND swing low bar index < swing high bar index AND low <= golden zone top AND high >= golden zone bottom AND close > open AND close > (golden zone top + golden zone bottom) / 2 AND volume > SMA of volume AND close > EMA of close AND bars since last signal >= cooldown length.
Short: Swing high − swing low > 0 AND swing low bar index >= swing high bar index AND low <= golden zone top AND high >= golden zone bottom AND close < open AND close < (golden zone top + golden zone bottom) / 2 AND volume > SMA of volume AND close < EMA of close AND bars since last signal >= cooldown length.
