Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the chart-timeframe bars spanning the specified number of bars before and after it; it becomes available after those later bars complete.
Calculation: Line 1 = price of the second-most-recent confirmed pivot; Line 2 = price of the third-most-recent confirmed pivot.
Long: Close crosses above Line 1 OR, when available, Line 2 (current close > level AND previous close ≤ level).
Short: Close crosses below Line 1 OR, when available, Line 2 (current close < level AND previous close ≥ level).
