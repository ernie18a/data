Calculation: Opening range high and low = On intraday chart bars, starting at a configurable local daily opening time, take the maximum high and minimum low, or alternatively the maximum of open and close and minimum of open and close, over bars whose opening times fall within a configurable opening-range duration. The duration may be selected from a configurable stepwise mapping based on chart timeframe. Reset the range at each local calendar day change or new opening-range start, and lock it when a bar first falls outside the opening range after a bar inside it.
Long: Chart timeframe is intraday AND bar opening time is at or after the opening-range end AND the opening range is locked AND opening range high exists AND ((close > opening range high AND previous close <= current opening range high) OR (high > opening range high AND previous high <= current opening range high)), using either the close pair or the high pair according to a configurable breakout mode AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA × configurable multiplier) AND (one-signal-per-side filter is disabled OR no earlier long signal has occurred since the latest local calendar day change or opening-range start).
Short: Chart timeframe is intraday AND bar opening time is at or after the opening-range end AND the opening range is locked AND opening range low exists AND ((close < opening range low AND previous close >= current opening range low) OR (low < opening range low AND previous low >= current opening range low)), using either the close pair or the low pair according to the same configurable breakout mode AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > volume SMA × configurable multiplier) AND (one-signal-per-side filter is disabled OR no earlier short signal has occurred since the latest local calendar day change or opening-range start).
