Calculation: Selected regression = For each candidate window from a minimum to a maximum in fixed steps, fit ordinary least-squares regression to closes, indexing the oldest close as 0. Discard windows with zero close variance or R-squared below a quality threshold. For each remaining window, calculate residual sigma as sqrt(sum of squared residuals / (window length - 2)) and score as R-squared * ln(window length) + slope-weight * abs(slope) / ATR - sigma-weight * sigma / ATR; use zero for both ATR-normalized terms when ATR is not positive. Select the highest score, keeping the first candidate on a tie. No model is selected if no candidate qualifies.
Calculation: Channel = The selected regression's fitted value at the current bar is the center. Sigma unit is the greater of residual sigma and a configurable minimum price increment. Core, outer, and stress bands are the center plus or minus their respective sigma multipliers times sigma unit; the outer multiplier is greater than the core multiplier, and the stress multiplier is greater than the outer multiplier. Recalculate the model and bands independently on each bar.
Calculation: Residual z = (close - channel center) / sigma unit.
