Calculation: Origin = search backward from the preceding bar through the origin lookback for an opposing candle: close < open for support, close > open for resistance. With sweep disabled, select the first opposing candle. With search-back sweep, select the first opposing candle whose immediately following bar has a lower low for support or a higher high for resistance. With first-only sweep, select the first opposing candle only if that same sweep condition holds; otherwise no origin exists.
Calculation: Zone = the origin candle's high-to-low range when sweep is disabled; otherwise the combined high-to-low range of the origin and immediately following bar. At creation, expand the range symmetrically around its midpoint if its height is less than a chosen fraction of current ATR. Keep the resulting boundaries fixed.
Calculation: Zone state = maintain separate capacity-limited, newest-first pools for original support, original resistance, flipped support, and flipped resistance. On each closed bar, first remove flipped support zones when close < zone bottom and flipped resistance zones when close > zone top. Next, move each original support zone with close < zone bottom to flipped resistance, and each original resistance zone with close > zone top to flipped support; trim each flipped pool by removing its oldest zones. Finally create new zones and trim each original pool by removing its oldest zones. Remember the most recently created origin separately for support and resistance, even after its zone is removed.
Long: Every close in the streak lookback >= its preceding close AND current volume / its inclusive volume SMA >= a threshold AND the volume SMA > 0 AND a support origin exists AND its bar differs from the most recently created support origin.
Short: Every close in the streak lookback <= its preceding close AND current volume / its inclusive volume SMA >= a threshold AND the volume SMA > 0 AND a resistance origin exists AND its bar differs from the most recently created resistance origin.
Long: Close > the top of an existing original resistance zone.
Short: Close < the bottom of an existing original support zone.
