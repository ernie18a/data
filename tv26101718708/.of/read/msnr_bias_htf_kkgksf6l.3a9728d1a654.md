Calculation: Bearish line-chart breakout = the still-forming lower-timeframe close crosses below the lowest close of the previous 5 completed lower-timeframe bars
Calculation: Weekly storyline = bullish on a bullish breakout and bearish on a bearish breakout from daily bars; otherwise retain its previous state
Calculation: Daily storyline = bullish on a bullish breakout and bearish on a bearish breakout from lower-timeframe bars; otherwise retain its previous state

Short: Weekly storyline = bearish AND Daily storyline = bearish
