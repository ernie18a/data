Calculation: Middle band = selected moving average of the selected price source over the selected lookback, calculated on the selected candle type and timeframe; that timeframe may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe, using the latest available bar without lookahead (a higher-timeframe bar is its last completed bar historically and may still be forming in real time
Calculation: Deviation = selected lookback standard deviation of the selected price source or selected-period ATR, on the same candle type and timeframe as the middle band
Calculation: Upper band = selected moving average of (middle band + deviation × upper multiplier), using its selected smoothing type and lookback
Calculation: Lower band = selected moving average of (middle band − deviation × lower multiplier), using its selected smoothing type and lookback
Calculation: Band width = (unsmoothed upper band − unsmoothed lower band) / middle band
Calculation: Percent B = (selected-timeframe close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band)
Long: Within the configured date window AND the selected long-open conditions meet the configured requirement (all, any, or minimum count): close crosses above, crosses below, is above, or is below any of the upper, middle, or lower bands; band width crosses above, crosses below, is above, or is below either configured threshold; or Percent B crosses above or below either configured threshold; AND no long-close signal is present
Exit: Long, the selected long-close conditions meet the configured requirement (all, any, or minimum count): close crosses above, crosses below, is above, or is below any of the upper, middle, or lower bands; band width crosses above, crosses below, is above, or is below either configured threshold; or Percent B crosses above or below either configured threshold; OR high ≥ entry price + entry-signal close × configured take-profit percentage / 100; OR low ≤ entry price − entry-signal close × configured stop-loss percentage / 100; OR datetime is outside the configured date window
