Calculation: Session opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low from the start of an enabled session through its first 15 minutes; retain these levels afterward.
Calculation: External levels = previous completed daily, weekly, and monthly bar highs/lows, plus the running highs/lows of the Asia and London sessions. Daily, weekly, and monthly bars are higher than the chart timeframe and use the last completed bar; session highs/lows use chart-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Nearest external level and distance = the external level with the smallest absolute price distance from the relevant opening-range boundary, and that distance.
Calculation: Bullish FVG-123 = a bar has low > high two bars earlier and the intervening bar closes above its open; after that gap forms, a bar's low reaches or falls below the gap's upper boundary, followed by a close above that boundary.
Calculation: Bearish FVG-123 = a bar has high < low two bars earlier and the intervening bar closes below its open; after that gap forms, a bar's high reaches or exceeds the gap's lower boundary, followed by a close below that boundary.
Long: During an active enabled session's signal window AND bullish FVG-123 AND ((the opening-range low is within the confluence distance of its nearest external level AND price has previously traded below both that opening-range low and its nearest external level AND close > opening-range low) OR (the opening-range high is farther than the confluence distance from its nearest external level AND close > opening-range high))
Short: During an active enabled session's signal window AND bearish FVG-123 AND ((the opening-range high is within the confluence distance of its nearest external level AND price has previously traded above both that opening-range high and its nearest external level AND close < opening-range high) OR (the opening-range low is farther than the confluence distance from its nearest external level AND close < opening-range low))
