Calculation: RSI rollover = RSI of closes is below its threshold and RSI on the preceding RSI-timeframe bar was greater than or equal to that threshold. For a higher RSI timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower RSI timeframe, use the last available intrabar. The RSI timeframe may also equal the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Averaging threshold = base entry price multiplied by one plus the cumulative deviation, where the cumulative deviation for the next unfilled averaging order is the sum of the initial deviation and its successive products by the deviation-step multiplier, up to that order's index. Fill at most one averaging order per chart bar, in order, up to the configured maximum.
Calculation: Average entry price = quantity-weighted average of the base and filled averaging entries; each order's quantity is its configured cash size divided by the signal-bar close, and each successive averaging order's cash size is the preceding averaging order's cash size multiplied by the size multiplier.
Calculation: Trailing minimum = the lowest close since entry among bars where close is at or below the current average entry price multiplied by one minus the take-profit fraction. Retain this minimum until the position closes.
Short: Position is flat AND datetime is within the configured date window if the date filter is enabled AND RSI rollover is true.
Short: Position is short AND datetime is within the configured date window if the date filter is enabled AND filled averaging orders are fewer than the configured maximum AND close is greater than or equal to the averaging threshold.
Exit: short, close is greater than or equal to average entry price multiplied by one plus the stop-loss fraction.
Exit: short, trailing minimum exists AND close is greater than or equal to trailing minimum multiplied by one plus the trailing fraction.
