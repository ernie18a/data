Calculation: Tracked liquidity levels = Enabled session highs and lows, added on the first chart bar outside each configurable session in the New York timezone; enabled highs and lows of the last completed higher-timeframe bars, added only when the corresponding value changes; and enabled chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows, added when confirmed using equal numbers of bars on each side. Classify each pivot as equal if it is within a configurable ATR multiple of any of a fixed number of most recent prior same-side pivots; otherwise classify it as a swing. Retain a configurable number of the most recently added levels per previous-period and pivot type. Expire session levels by elapsed time since session start. Skip levels older than a configurable number of chart bars from their anchor. Add and trim levels before evaluating sweeps; expire session levels after evaluating sweeps on the current bar.
Calculation: Sweep state = For each tracked level, record the first bar after tracking begins on which high exceeds a high level or low falls below a low level. On that bar, confirm a sweep if close is back through the level. On subsequent bars, mark the level broken if the number of bars since the pierce exceeds the confirmation window; otherwise confirm a sweep on the first close back through the level. A confirmed or broken level cannot signal again.
Long: A tracked low level has a newly confirmed sweep AND close > the level.
Short: A tracked high level has a newly confirmed sweep AND close < the level.
