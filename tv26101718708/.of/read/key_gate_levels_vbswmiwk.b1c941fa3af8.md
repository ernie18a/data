Calculation: Gate clusters = On the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, retain the requested timeframe's close, high, low and ATR series. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed intrabar available within each chart bar. Recalculate clusters on the last chart bar. Scan candidate bars from the bar immediately before the forward window back through the lookback, excluding the most recent eligible candidate bar. For each candidate, compare the highest high and lowest low in its subsequent forward window with its close. Create a bullish candidate if the high minus the close exceeds the acceleration threshold times the candidate's ATR; create a bearish candidate if the close minus the low exceeds the same threshold times that ATR. A candidate's price is its close and its force is its directional move divided by its ATR. Process candidates from newest to oldest, bullish before bearish for the same bar. Merge each candidate into the first existing cluster whose running mean price is within the cluster-width multiplier times current requested-timeframe ATR; otherwise create a cluster. Each cluster's price is the mean candidate price, its force is the sum of candidate forces, its signed force is bullish force minus bearish force, and its hits are the candidate count. Rank clusters by descending force, preserving creation order for ties, and select up to the maximum number of clusters before applying the minimum-hit filter. Use the minimum-hit parameter selected for the requested timeframe group. Replace an unavailable ATR with a small positive fallback.
Long: Selected cluster hits >= its minimum-hit threshold AND cluster signed force / cluster force > bias threshold
Short: Selected cluster hits >= its minimum-hit threshold AND cluster signed force / cluster force < negative bias threshold
