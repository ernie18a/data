Calculation: Synthetic delta ratio = Clamp estimated delta divided by the moving average of volume to [-1, 1], or zero when that average is not positive. Estimated delta is volume multiplied by a clamped weighted sum of close location within the bar, candle direction, wick pressure, and the previous delta ratio; multiply that sum by a volume z-score adjustment and an ATR-relative range adjustment before clamping. Close location is scaled from -1 at the low to +1 at the high, with zero for a zero-range bar. Candle direction is the sign of close minus open. Wick pressure is negative when the upper wick exceeds both a multiple of the lower wick and a fraction of the body, positive under the mirrored condition, and otherwise zero. Clamp the previous-ratio contribution, volume adjustment, and final estimated-delta fraction; use separate higher and lower range thresholds and multipliers. Initialize the previous ratio to zero.
Calculation: First latent component = tanh(weighted sum of centered, normalized RSI; ATR-normalized momentum; normalized rate of change; and synthetic delta ratio), with each momentum and rate-of-change input clamped to [-1, 1]. The RSI weight exceeds the momentum weight, which exceeds the rate-of-change weight, which exceeds the delta weight.
