Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over a sweep lookback ending with the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Absorption = (volume / volume SMA, or 1 if the SMA is not positive) / max(max(abs(close - open), price increment) / ATR, or 1 if ATR is not positive, minimum relative displacement).
Calculation: Bar delta = sum over the completed lower-timeframe bars within the completed chart bar of volume × (2 × buy fraction - 1). For each lower-timeframe bar, its price change is its close minus the preceding lower-timeframe close within the chart bar, or minus its own open for the first bar. Buy fraction is the Student-t CDF, or optionally the normal CDF, of that change divided by its scale. Scale is the sample standard deviation of those changes, floored by a fraction of fallback scale and by the price increment; with fewer than two changes use fallback scale. Fallback scale is the standard deviation of chart-bar close changes over a lookback, divided by the square root of the lower-timeframe divisor and floored by the price increment; use the price increment if that standard deviation is unavailable.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low that is an extremum against its configured numbers of bars to the left and right. Reference high and reference low are the most recent respective pivots whose confirmation bars fall within a pivot search lookback ending with the preceding chart bar.
Long: On a completed chart bar, low < prior low AND high <= prior high AND ((absorption >= absorption threshold AND close > prior low) OR ((min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, price increment) >= wick threshold AND close > open) OR bar delta > 0 OR volume < sweep volume SMA × exhaustion multiplier).
Short: On a completed chart bar, high > prior high AND low >= prior low AND ((absorption >= absorption threshold AND close < prior high) OR ((high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, price increment) >= wick threshold AND close < open) OR bar delta < 0 OR volume < sweep volume SMA × exhaustion multiplier).
Long: On a completed chart bar, volume > liquidity volume SMA × spike multiplier AND low < reference low AND close > reference low AND close - low >= (reference low - low) × (1 + reclaim fraction).
Short: On a completed chart bar, volume > liquidity volume SMA × spike multiplier AND high > reference high AND close < reference high AND high - close >= (high - reference high) × (1 + reclaim fraction) AND NOT (low < reference low AND close > reference low AND close - low >= (reference low - low) × (1 + reclaim fraction)).
