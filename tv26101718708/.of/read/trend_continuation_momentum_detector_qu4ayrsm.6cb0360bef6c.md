Calculation: Heikin Ashi (HA) close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; HA open = (previous HA open + previous HA close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2; HA high = max(high, HA open, HA close); HA low = min(low, HA open, HA close). HA body = abs(HA close - HA open); upper wick = HA high - max(HA open, HA close); lower wick = min(HA open, HA close) - HA low. Average body = SMA of preceding HA bodies, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Momentum score = weighted average of body, wick, slope, volume, and distance scores, clamped to [0, 100]; use zero if the sum of weights is zero. Body score = min(50 * HA body / average body, 100), or zero if average body is not positive. Opposing wick = lower wick for bullish HA, upper wick for bearish HA, otherwise the mean of both wicks; wick ratio = opposing wick / HA body, or 1 if body is zero; wick score = max(100 - 100 * wick ratio, 0). Slope = (EMA of HA close - its value at the slope lookback) / slope lookback; slope score = min(abs(100 * slope / ATR) * sensitivity, 100), using zero when ATR is zero. Volume score = min(50 * volume / SMA(volume), 100), using a volume ratio of 1 if the average is not positive. Distance z = (HA close - EMA of HA close) / standard deviation of HA close, or zero if the standard deviation is zero. For x = abs(distance z), distance score is 120*x if x <= 0.5; 60 + (x - 0.5)*40/1.5 if x <= 2; 100 - (x - 2)*40 if x <= 3; otherwise max(60 - (x - 3)*30, 0).
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3 since the trading-session reset. Session VWAP standard deviation = square root of max(cumulative volume-weighted mean of squared typical price minus the square of its cumulative volume-weighted mean, 0). VWAP z = (close - session VWAP) / session VWAP standard deviation, or zero if the deviation is zero.
Calculation: Delta = sum of volume of completed lower-timeframe bars inside the chart bar whose close > open minus sum of volume of those whose close < open; if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use 1, -1, or 0 according as the chart close is greater than, less than, or equal to its open.
Calculation: On each completed chart bar, classify state in priority order as strong long, strong short, long bias, short bias, or neutral. Strong long requires momentum score > strong threshold, HA close > HA open, lower wick <= HA body * wick tolerance, HA body > average body, HA close > EMA of HA close, and optionally close > session VWAP and delta > 0. Strong short mirrors the inequalities and uses upper wick. Long bias requires HA close > HA open, HA close > EMA of HA close, momentum score >= bias threshold, and optionally close > session VWAP. Short bias mirrors these inequalities. The strong threshold is greater than or equal to the bias threshold.
Calculation: Each side's readiness starts true. On each completed bar, save readiness before updating it; set long readiness true when low <= EMA of HA close and short readiness true when high >= EMA of HA close, then set a side's readiness false whenever its strong state holds and its updated readiness is true.
Long: state = strong long OR state = long bias.
Short: state = strong short OR state = short bias.
Long: state = strong long AND previous state != strong long AND long readiness before the current bar's update = true.
Short: state = strong short AND previous state != strong short AND short readiness before the current bar's update = true.
Long: bars since session reset >= minimum session bars AND close > 0 AND session VWAP standard deviation / close >= minimum deviation fraction AND momentum score < mean-reversion maximum AND (fading filter disabled OR momentum score < previous momentum score) AND VWAP z <= negative deviation threshold AND HA body < average body AND previous HA close < previous HA open AND high >= max(previous HA open, previous HA close) AND low <= min(previous HA open, previous HA close).
Short: bars since session reset >= minimum session bars AND close > 0 AND session VWAP standard deviation / close >= minimum deviation fraction AND momentum score < mean-reversion maximum AND (fading filter disabled OR momentum score < previous momentum score) AND VWAP z >= deviation threshold AND HA body < average body AND previous HA close > previous HA open AND high >= max(previous HA open, previous HA close) AND low <= min(previous HA open, previous HA close).
Long: slope of EMA of HA close >= 0.
Short: slope of EMA of HA close < 0.
Long: delta > 0.
Short: delta < 0.
Long: close > session VWAP.
Short: close <= session VWAP.
Exit: long, previous state = strong long AND (state = neutral OR state = short bias).
Exit: short, previous state = strong short AND (state = neutral OR state = long bias).
Exit: long, close < active long stop, where the stop is the next bar's open after the latest fresh strong-long signal minus a stop multiplier times that signal bar's HA high-low range; activate it on that next bar, clear it on a fresh strong-long signal or after a stop hit.
Exit: short, close > active short stop, where the stop is the next bar's open after the latest fresh strong-short signal plus a stop multiplier times that signal bar's HA high-low range; activate it on that next bar, clear it on a fresh strong-short signal or after a stop hit.
