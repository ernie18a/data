Calculation: VWAP = session VWAP of typical price (high+low+close)/3 weighted by volume, cumulated from session start and reset each day
Calculation: SD = sqrt(max(cumulative(typical price^2 * volume)/cumulative(volume) - VWAP^2, 0)), cumulated over the same session window as VWAP
Long: flat AND SD > 0 AND bar time is inside the entry window (at or after an entry start time that is later than session start, and before the session cutoff time) AND not within the cooldown period after a stop-loss exit earlier in the same session AND low <= VWAP - 3*SD + tolerance (fixed point offset toward VWAP; fill at that level) AND stop level < that entry level < long target level (stop and target defined in the Exit line)
Short: flat AND SD > 0 AND bar time is inside the entry window (at or after an entry start time that is later than session start, and before the session cutoff time) AND not within the cooldown period after a stop-loss exit earlier in the same session AND high >= VWAP + 3*SD - tolerance (fixed point offset toward VWAP; fill at that level) AND short target level < that entry level < stop level (stop and target defined in the Exit line)
Exit: long, high >= VWAP - tolerance (current-bar VWAP, target) OR low <= VWAP - m*SD (stop, m greater than 3, VWAP and SD frozen at entry time) OR bar time reaches the session cutoff time OR the position was opened on a prior day
Exit: short, low <= VWAP + tolerance (current-bar VWAP, target) OR high >= VWAP + m*SD (stop, m greater than 3, VWAP and SD frozen at entry time) OR bar time reaches the session cutoff time OR the position was opened on a prior day
