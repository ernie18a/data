Calculation: Williams %R = 100 × (weekly close − highest weekly close over lookback) / (highest weekly close − lowest weekly close), using the current, still-forming weekly bar on a higher timeframe than the chart
Calculation: Smoothed Williams %R = simple moving average of Williams %R over a shorter lookback, using the current, still-forming weekly bar on a higher timeframe than the chart
Long: Smoothed Williams %R > bull threshold AND previous weekly Smoothed Williams %R ≤ bull threshold
Short: Smoothed Williams %R < bear threshold AND previous weekly Smoothed Williams %R ≥ bear threshold
