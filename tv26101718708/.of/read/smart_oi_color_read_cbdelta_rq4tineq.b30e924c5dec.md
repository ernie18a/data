Calculation: Aggregated open interest = Sum the open interest of the enabled exchanges, converted to the selected units; use its bar open and close
Calculation: CVD = Cumulative volume delta from bars lower than the chart timeframe, including data for the chart bar still forming
Calculation: Bullish flow score = 2 if close > previous close, plus 1 if CVD’s smoothed rate of change exceeds the slope threshold, plus 1 if OBV’s smoothed rate of change exceeds the slope threshold
Calculation: Bearish flow score = 2 if close < previous close, plus 1 if CVD’s smoothed rate of change is below the negative slope threshold, plus 1 if OBV’s smoothed rate of change is below the negative slope threshold
Long: (Aggregated open interest close − open > 0 AND bullish flow score > bearish flow score) OR (Aggregated open interest close − open < 0 AND bullish flow score > bearish flow score) OR (mixed override enabled AND no system classification applies AND close > previous close AND aggregated open interest close − open > 0) OR (mixed override enabled AND no system classification applies AND close > previous close AND aggregated open interest close − open < 0)
