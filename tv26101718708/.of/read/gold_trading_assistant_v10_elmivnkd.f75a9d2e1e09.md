Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = enabled bullish engulfing OR enabled hammer. Bullish engulfing requires close > open, previous close < previous open, close >= previous open, open <= previous close, and body >= previous body × body-ratio parameter. Hammer requires lower wick >= body × wick-ratio parameter, upper wick <= body × opposite-wick parameter, and body > 0.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = enabled bearish engulfing OR enabled shooting star. Bearish engulfing requires close < open, previous close > previous open, close <= previous open, open >= previous close, and body >= previous body × body-ratio parameter. Shooting star requires upper wick >= body × wick-ratio parameter, lower wick <= body × opposite-wick parameter, and body > 0.
Calculation: Bullish alignment count = the number of these conditions satisfied: last completed higher-timeframe close > its short EMA > its long EMA; chart close > its short EMA > its long EMA; last completed lower-timeframe close > its short EMA. Bearish alignment count reverses each inequality. Corresponding EMAs use the same lengths across timeframes; when multi-timeframe calculations are disabled, the higher- and lower-timeframe conditions are false.
Calculation: Structure levels = enabled last completed higher-timeframe highs and lows from two different higher timeframes, the two most recent confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot lengths, and the nearest price-step multiples above and below close. A multiple equal to close is excluded.
Calculation: Long risk = close - min(lowest low over the stop lookback - ATR × stop-buffer parameter, close - ATR × stop-distance parameter). Short risk = max(highest high over the stop lookback + ATR × stop-buffer parameter, close + ATR × stop-distance parameter) - close.
Calculation: Long first target = close + long risk × target reward-to-risk parameter, unless hybrid targets are enabled and an enabled high structure level lies above close and at or below that target, in which case it is the nearest such level minus a price buffer. Short first target = close - short risk × target reward-to-risk parameter, unless hybrid targets are enabled and an enabled low structure level lies below close and at or above that target, in which case it is the nearest such level plus a price buffer.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = pattern base score + alignment score + volume score + structure score + trend score + news-clear score. Alignment score assigns increasing scores to one, two, or three aligned timeframes and zero to none. Volume score uses a higher score when volume >= its SMA × strong-volume multiplier, a lower score when the optional volume filter passes, and zero otherwise; the strong-volume multiplier exceeds the filter multiplier. Structure score is positive when an enabled low structure level is below close and at least close - ATR for long, or an enabled high structure level is above close and at most close + ATR for short. Trend score assigns increasing scores when the change in chart short EMA over a lookback divided by ATR exceeds increasing positive thresholds for long, or is below their negatives for short. Otherwise each component is zero. With only backtest data available, external macro contributions are zero; news-clear score is a configurable constant for news enabled with no scheduled events, otherwise zero.
Long: Chart close > chart short EMA AND chart short EMA > chart long EMA.
Short: Chart close < chart short EMA AND chart short EMA < chart long EMA.
Long: Multi-timeframe calculations enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe short EMA AND higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA.
Short: Multi-timeframe calculations enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe short EMA AND higher-timeframe short EMA < higher-timeframe long EMA.
Long: Multi-timeframe calculations enabled AND last completed lower-timeframe close > lower-timeframe short EMA.
Short: Multi-timeframe calculations enabled AND last completed lower-timeframe close < lower-timeframe short EMA.
Long: Long confluence score >= short confluence score.
Short: Short confluence score > long confluence score.
Long: Bullish pattern AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × filter multiplier) AND body <= ATR × maximum-body parameter AND highest high minus lowest low over the recent-move lookback <= ATR × maximum-move parameter AND bars since the last long or short signal >= spacing parameter AND (multi-timeframe alignment not required OR bullish alignment count >= required count) AND (confluence filter disabled OR long confluence score >= minimum score) AND (long risk > 0 AND (long first target - close) / long risk >= minimum reward-to-risk parameter OR long risk <= 0 AND zero >= minimum reward-to-risk parameter).
Short: Bearish pattern AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume SMA × filter multiplier) AND body <= ATR × maximum-body parameter AND highest high minus lowest low over the recent-move lookback <= ATR × maximum-move parameter AND bars since the last long or short signal >= spacing parameter AND (multi-timeframe alignment not required OR bearish alignment count >= required count) AND (confluence filter disabled OR short confluence score >= minimum score) AND (short risk > 0 AND (close - short first target) / short risk >= minimum reward-to-risk parameter OR short risk <= 0 AND zero >= minimum reward-to-risk parameter).
