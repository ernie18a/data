Calculation: FVG channel = On each completed chart bar, add high from two bars ago to the bullish references if low > high from two bars ago AND the previous close > that high; add low from two bars ago to the bearish references if high < low from two bars ago AND the previous close < that low. Cap each reference list independently by removing its oldest item immediately after an addition exceeds the storage limit. Then remove bullish references above the current close and bearish references below the current close. For each list, use its average, or the SMA of close over the smoothing length when the list is empty. Apply two consecutive SMAs of the same smoothing length to each resulting series. The upper boundary is the maximum and the lower boundary the minimum of the two smoothed series.
Calculation: Super classification = For a long signal, max((lower boundary - low) / abs(lower boundary), 0) >= the wick-extension threshold; for a short signal, max((high - upper boundary) / abs(upper boundary), 0) >= the same threshold. If the relevant boundary is zero, the extension is zero. Otherwise the signal is standard.
Calculation: Outcome levels = At the signal close, select the standard or Super target and stop percentages according to the signal classification. In percentage target mode, the long target is entry price * (1 + target percentage) and the short target is entry price * (1 - target percentage). In internal-level target mode, the target is the selected entry-bar value of lower boundary + (upper boundary - lower boundary) * selected proportion; the three selectable proportions are ordered from lower to higher. The long stop is entry price * (1 - stop percentage) and the short stop is entry price * (1 + stop percentage). Fix these levels at entry. Start an outcome only when tracking is enabled, no prior outcome remains active after evaluating the current bar, and the target is above the entry price for a long or below it for a short. If both directions signal on the same bar, consider the long first; a rejected long target does not initiate a short outcome on that bar.
Long: Close > lower boundary AND previous close <= previous lower boundary AND each of at least the required consecutive bars immediately before the current bar closed below its respective lower boundary AND either no previous long signal exists OR bars since the previous long signal >= cooldown AND either volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier.
Short: Close < upper boundary AND previous close >= previous upper boundary AND each of at least the required consecutive bars immediately before the current bar closed above its respective upper boundary AND either no previous short signal exists OR bars since the previous short signal >= cooldown AND either volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) * volume multiplier.
Exit: long, an outcome is active AND the current bar is later than the entry bar AND (high >= fixed target OR low <= fixed stop).
Exit: short, an outcome is active AND the current bar is later than the entry bar AND (low <= fixed target OR high >= fixed stop).
