Calculation: ATR = Wilder's average true range, falling back to current true range when unavailable and floored at the minimum price increment.
Calculation: Candle range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); body ratio = abs(close - open) / candle range; bullish close location = (close - low) / candle range; bearish close location = (high - close) / candle range.
Calculation: Bullish trend filter = close > longer-period EMA for early sensitivity; shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND close > shorter-period EMA for balanced sensitivity; the balanced filter AND shorter-period EMA > its value a specified number of bars earlier for strict sensitivity. Bearish trend filter reverses all comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish raw activation = close > highest high of the preceding structure-lookback bars + ATR × impulse threshold × sensitivity factor AND close > open AND body ratio >= sensitivity-dependent body threshold AND bullish close location >= sensitivity-dependent close-location threshold AND bullish trend filter. Bearish raw activation = close < lowest low of the preceding structure-lookback bars - ATR × impulse threshold × sensitivity factor AND close < open AND body ratio >= sensitivity-dependent body threshold AND bearish close location >= sensitivity-dependent close-location threshold AND bearish trend filter. Early sensitivity has a lower impulse factor and lower body and close-location thresholds than balanced sensitivity; strict sensitivity has higher values.
Calculation: Bullish invalidation candidate = latest confirmed pivot low minus ATR × shelf buffer, or the lowest low over the structure lookback including the current bar minus that buffer if no pivot exists. Bearish invalidation candidate = latest confirmed pivot high plus ATR × shelf buffer, or the highest high over that lookback including the current bar plus that buffer if no pivot exists. A pivot is confirmed only after the specified equal numbers of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Context = on activation, record the side, close as reference, invalidation candidate as shelf, absolute reference-to-shelf distance as risk, and activation bar. Current R = (close - reference) / risk for bullish context and (reference - close) / risk for bearish context. A context is invalid when its shelf is touched; it is stale when its age exceeds the maximum age AND current R < the review threshold × a stale-progress fraction. Invalid and stale states persist until a new context starts.
Long: Bullish raw activation AND close - bullish invalidation candidate > max(ATR × minimum risk factor, minimum price increment × minimum tick-distance factor) AND (no context exists OR the previous context is invalid or stale OR its shelf is touched on this bar OR its age exceeds the maximum age AND its current R < the review threshold × the stale-progress fraction OR the previous context is bearish).
Short: Bearish raw activation AND bearish invalidation candidate - close > max(ATR × minimum risk factor, minimum price increment × minimum tick-distance factor) AND (no context exists OR the previous context is invalid or stale OR its shelf is touched on this bar OR its age exceeds the maximum age AND its current R < the review threshold × the stale-progress fraction OR the previous context is bullish).
Exit: long, active bullish context AND no new context starts on this bar AND context is not already invalid or stale AND low <= its shelf.
Exit: short, active bearish context AND no new context starts on this bar AND context is not already invalid or stale AND high >= its shelf.
