Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed with equal left and right lookbacks; retain the most recent configured maximum number of each. Index 1 denotes the most recent pivot, index 2 the preceding pivot, and so on. Two prices are near when their absolute difference is at most their mean times the symmetry tolerance.
Calculation: Projected upper and lower boundaries = lines through the two most recent high pivots and two most recent low pivots, respectively, evaluated at the current bar. Their slopes are the price differences divided by the bar-index differences.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = the shorter EMA of higher-timeframe close exceeds the longer EMA AND higher-timeframe close exceeds the shorter EMA. Bearish bias reverses both comparisons. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for historical chart bars; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Pattern candidates = calculate each enabled family independently using the most recent confirmed pivots. In each family, select the first matching alternative in the order stated. Each candidate has direction, boundary B, height H, target T, stop S, completion C, and start pivot index. E is the current bar's open; A is ATR; proximity means abs(close − B) <= A times the armed-zone multiplier; breakout means close > B + A times the break multiplier for a bullish candidate, or close < B − A times the break multiplier for a bearish candidate. Phase is FIRED if breakout, otherwise ARMED if proximity, otherwise FORMING.
Calculation: Double candidate = bearish if high 1 is later than low 1, low 1 is later than high 2, highs 1 and 2 are near, and H = mean(high 1, high 2) − low 1 > A; B = low 1. Otherwise bullish if low 1 is later than high 1, high 1 is later than low 2, lows 1 and 2 are near, and H = high 1 − mean(low 1, low 2) > A; B = high 1. Start at pivot 2 of the two matching extremes. T = B − H for bearish or B + H for bullish; S = E plus or minus, respectively, abs(T − E) times the stop fraction. C = 70 plus 15 if close is below B for bearish or above B for bullish, plus 15 if proximity.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders candidate = bearish if high 2 exceeds highs 1 and 3, highs 1 and 3 are near, and H = high 2 − mean(low 1, low 2) > A; B is the line through lows 2 and 1 projected to the current bar. Otherwise bullish if low 2 is below lows 1 and 3, lows 1 and 3 are near, and H = mean(high 1, high 2) − low 2 > A; B is the line through highs 2 and 1 projected to the current bar. Start at pivot 3 of the three matching extremes. T and S follow the double candidate formulas. C = 65 plus 12 if close is below B for bearish or above B for bullish, plus 18 if proximity. No additional chronological ordering of these pivots is required.
Calculation: Triangle candidate = projected upper boundary U exceeds projected lower boundary L AND H = abs(upper boundary projected to low 2's bar − low 2) > A. Let upward breakout be close > U + A times the break multiplier, downward breakout be close < L − A times the break multiplier, ascending mean abs(upper slope) < close times the flat-slope threshold AND lower slope > 0, and descending mean upper slope < 0 AND abs(lower slope) < close times the flat-slope threshold. Select bullish if upward breakout OR (no downward breakout AND (ascending OR upper slope < 0)); otherwise select bearish if downward breakout OR descending. For bullish, B = U, S = L, T = U + H; for bearish, B = L, S = U, T = L − H. Start at low 2. C = 60 plus 15 if H / A exceeds the large-base threshold, otherwise 5, plus 15 if proximity.
Calculation: Flag candidate = span from the earlier of high 2 and low 2 through the current bar exceeds the minimum span AND H, the highest high minus the lowest low over that span capped at the maximum pole lookback, exceeds A times the pole multiplier. Select bullish if both boundary slopes are negative, otherwise bearish if both are positive. For bullish, B = projected upper boundary, S = projected lower boundary, T = B + H; for bearish, B = projected lower boundary, S = projected upper boundary, T = B − H. Start at the earlier of high 2 and low 2. C = 62 plus 20 if proximity.
Calculation: Wedge candidate = projected upper boundary U > projected lower boundary L. Select bullish if both slopes are negative AND lower slope < upper slope; otherwise bearish if both slopes are positive AND lower slope > upper slope. H = U − L. For bullish, B = U, S = L, T = high 2; for bearish, B = L, S = U, T = low 2. Start at low 2. C = 58 + min(20, 5 × H / A).
Calculation: Candidate reward-to-risk = abs(T − E) / abs(E − S), or zero if E = S. Candidate confluence = 2 if C >= the high-completion threshold, otherwise 1 if C >= the minimum completion, otherwise 0; plus 2 for matching higher-timeframe bias when that layer is enabled, or 1 when disabled; plus 1 if the chop gate passes; plus 2 for FIRED or 1 for ARMED; plus 1 if volume-coil scoring is enabled AND volume SMA > 0 AND volume <= volume SMA times the coil multiplier; plus 1 if reward-to-risk >= its minimum; plus 1 if H >= A times the size multiplier. Cap the total at 10. The chop gate passes when disabled OR Choppiness Index < its threshold.
Calculation: Selected candidate = the candidate with the greatest confluence + 0.05 × C + phase bonus, where the phase bonus is 3 for FIRED, 2 for ARMED, and 0 for FORMING; ties favor double, head-and-shoulders, triangle, flag, then wedge. Select before applying the minimum completion, confluence, reward-to-risk, and higher-timeframe gates; do not fall back to another candidate if the winner fails. Candidate scanning requires the chart bar index to exceed the warm-up threshold, the chop gate to pass, and at least two confirmed highs and two confirmed lows.
Calculation: Eligible event = selected candidate exists AND C >= minimum completion AND confluence >= minimum confluence AND (phase is not FIRED OR reward-to-risk >= its minimum) AND (phase is not FIRED OR the hard higher-timeframe gate is disabled OR direction matches higher-timeframe bias) AND the confirmed bar's pattern name, phase, and start index differ from the last eligible event's key AND no previous FIRED event exists OR the configured cooldown has elapsed since the last FIRED event. Apply the final cooldown alternative as a single parenthesized condition; update the key on every eligible event and the last FIRED bar only on a FIRED event.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish bias.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish bias.
Long: Eligible event AND selected candidate is bullish AND phase is ARMED or FIRED.
Short: Eligible event AND selected candidate is bearish AND phase is ARMED or FIRED.
