Calculation: Reference band = The high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar. In extend mode, use the highest high and lowest low over successively longer windows of completed higher-timeframe bars, stopping at the first window whose (high - low) / low reaches the range threshold or at the shorter of the configured maximum lookback and the implementation's five-bar limit. In off and skip modes, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The midpoint is (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = On confirmation of a price-low pivot, its low is below the preceding confirmed price-low pivot and RSI at the new pivot is above RSI at the preceding pivot. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars and is lower than the configured number of left-hand bars and no higher than the right-hand bars.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = On confirmation of a price-high pivot, its high is above the preceding confirmed price-high pivot and RSI at the new pivot is below RSI at the preceding pivot. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars and is higher than the configured number of left-hand bars and no lower than the right-hand bars.
Calculation: Entry state = For an initial signal, set first entry and worst entry to the initial limit price, and set the leg count to one; the limit is reference low × (1 + zone / 2) for a long and reference high × (1 - zone / 2) for a short. An add updates worst entry to its limit and increments the leg count. Reset these values after the position closes. Count completed cycles per day and reset that count on a new day.
Calculation: Breakeven state = If enabled, arm for the rest of the position when close reaches the configured favorable percentage from first entry; reset after the position closes.
Calculation: Long stop = Worst entry × (1 - stop percentage), or, if breakeven is armed, the greater of that value and first entry × (1 + breakeven buffer).
Calculation: Short stop = Worst entry × (1 + stop percentage), or, if breakeven is armed, the lesser of that value and first entry × (1 - breakeven buffer).
Calculation: Long target = Midpoint × (1 - midpoint offset), except in hybrid mode after an add, when it is first entry × (1 + post-add target percentage).
Calculation: Short target = Midpoint × (1 + midpoint offset), except in hybrid mode after an add, when it is first entry × (1 - post-add target percentage).
Long: Bullish RSI divergence is confirmed AND divergence markings are enabled.
Short: Bearish RSI divergence is confirmed AND divergence markings are enabled.
Long: Position is flat AND completed cycles today are below the configured maximum AND (end-of-day flattening is disabled OR UTC hour is before the configured close hour) AND low <= reference low × (1 + zone) AND low > reference low × (1 - penetration limit) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= volume multiplier × SMA of volume) AND (range mode is not skip OR reference band range / reference low >= range threshold) AND (divergence filter is disabled OR bars since bullish RSI divergence confirmation <= freshness limit).
Short: Position is flat AND completed cycles today are below the configured maximum AND (end-of-day flattening is disabled OR UTC hour is before the configured close hour) AND high >= reference high × (1 - zone) AND high < reference high × (1 + penetration limit) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume >= volume multiplier × SMA of volume) AND (range mode is not skip OR reference band range / reference low >= range threshold) AND (divergence filter is disabled OR bars since bearish RSI divergence confirmation <= freshness limit).
Long: Position is long AND leg count is below the configured maximum AND low <= worst entry × (1 - add spacing); place the add at that limit.
Short: Position is short AND leg count is below the configured maximum AND high >= worst entry × (1 + add spacing); place the add at that limit.
Exit: long, high >= long target OR low <= long stop; apply the target and stop orders to the first, second, and third entry legs only.
Exit: short, low <= short target OR high >= short stop; apply the target and stop orders to the first, second, and third entry legs only.
Exit: long, end-of-day flattening is enabled AND UTC hour >= configured close hour AND the position was not held at the close hour today AND (holding is disabled OR the position was held on an earlier day OR (close - first entry) / first entry < minimum favorable percentage for holding).
Exit: short, end-of-day flattening is enabled AND UTC hour >= configured close hour AND the position was not held at the close hour today AND (holding is disabled OR the position was held on an earlier day OR (first entry - close) / first entry < minimum favorable percentage for holding).
