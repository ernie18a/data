Calculation: Volume-clock moving average = Set target volume to a window parameter times the average volume over a separate lookback ending at the previous bar. Starting with the current bar, accumulate volume backward up to a maximum number of bars equal to the window parameter times a cap parameter. Weight each bar's selected price source by its included volume, using only the volume needed from the oldest bar if the target is reached. Divide the sum of weighted prices by the included volume. If the target volume is not positive or the included volume is zero, the average is unavailable.
Long: Close > volume-clock moving average AND the previous bar's close was not above its volume-clock moving average.
Short: Close < volume-clock moving average AND the previous bar's close was not below its volume-clock moving average.
