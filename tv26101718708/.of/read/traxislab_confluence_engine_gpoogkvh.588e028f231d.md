Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1), or zero when high equals low; cumulative volume delta is its running sum, initialized to zero; pressure is an EMA of estimated delta.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish divergence = close is below its close at a divergence lookback AND cumulative volume delta is above its value at that lookback for at least a confirmation count of consecutive bars AND volume is above its SMA. Confirmed bearish divergence reverses both slope comparisons. Use the same volume SMA in all calculations below.
Calculation: Structure high and low = latest confirmed pivot high and low, respectively, each requiring a configurable number of bars to its left and one bar to its right. Replace each level when a new pivot of its kind is confirmed. Bullish structure break = close > structure high AND previous close <= that same high; bearish structure break = close < structure low AND previous close >= that same low. Clear the respective level after its break. Structure direction becomes bullish or bearish on the respective break, including an unconfirmed break; initialize it as neutral. Bullish change of character = bullish break when the previous structure direction was bearish; bearish change of character is the reverse.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish structure break = bullish structure break AND cumulative volume delta > its value at a confirmation lookback AND volume > its SMA × a confirmation multiplier. Confirmed bearish structure break reverses the cumulative volume delta comparison. Use a separate confirmation lookback from divergence.
Calculation: Effort = volume / volume SMA, or one when the SMA is not positive. Result = (high − low) / SMA(high − low), or one when that SMA is not positive. Use the same lookback for both SMAs. Absorption = effort >= an effort threshold AND result <= an absorption threshold. Climax = effort >= the same effort threshold AND result >= a climax threshold. Bullish absorption = absorption AND close < EMA(close); bearish absorption = absorption AND close > EMA(close). Bullish climax = climax AND close < open AND close < EMA(close); bearish climax = climax AND close > open AND close > EMA(close).
Calculation: Sweep high and low = latest confirmed pivot high and low using a sweep lookback and one bar to the right. Bullish sweep = low < sweep low AND close > sweep low; bearish sweep = high > sweep high AND close < sweep high. Clear the respective level after a sweep, including one without volume confirmation. Volume-confirmed sweep = sweep AND volume > volume SMA × a sweep multiplier.
Calculation: Equilibrium = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a range lookback, captured on each confirmed structure break and held until the next one. Discount = close < equilibrium; premium = close > equilibrium.
Calculation: A bullish fair value gap has low > high two bars earlier, with top = low and bottom = that earlier high. A bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier, with top = that earlier low and bottom = high. Gap score starts at a base score; add a larger or smaller size weight when gap size exceeds the respective larger or smaller ATR multiple, a volume weight when previous volume exceeds current volume SMA × a multiplier, a location weight when the bullish bottom is below equilibrium or the bearish top is above equilibrium, and a structure weight when the corresponding confirmed structure break occurs or the corresponding structure pivot was confirmed within a proximity lookback AND structure direction matches the gap. ATR falls back to close × a fallback multiplier when unavailable. Keep gaps scoring at least a minimum; remove a bullish gap when low <= its bottom, a bearish gap when high >= its top, or either when its age exceeds an age limit; then remove oldest gaps until their count is within a maximum. Near bullish or bearish gap = absolute distance from close to the midpoint of a retained gap of that direction < ATR × a proximity multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = capped sum of independent weights for confirmed bullish divergence, confirmed bullish structure break, bullish change of character, bullish absorption, volume-confirmed bullish sweep, near bullish gap, discount, bullish climax, and pressure > 0. Bearish confluence score uses the corresponding bearish conditions, premium, and pressure < 0. A change of character contributes independently of break confirmation.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish label cooldowns are tracked separately from the most recent same-direction confluence label. A label updates its cooldown only when its score reaches the label threshold, its cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is within the configured final-bar window of the dataset. The alert threshold and cooldown use the same scores and label cooldowns, but alerts do not update cooldowns.
Long: Confirmed bullish divergence.
Short: Confirmed bearish divergence.
Long: Confirmed bullish structure break OR bullish change of character.
Short: Confirmed bearish structure break OR bearish change of character.
Long: Bullish absorption OR bullish climax OR bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish absorption OR bearish climax OR bearish sweep.
Long: Bullish gap score >= minimum gap score OR near bullish gap.
Short: Bearish gap score >= minimum gap score OR near bearish gap.
Long: Structure direction = bullish OR pressure > 0 OR discount.
Short: Structure direction = bearish OR pressure < 0 OR premium.
Long: Bullish confluence score >= label threshold AND bullish label cooldown elapsed AND bar is within the configured final-bar window of the dataset.
Short: Bearish confluence score >= label threshold AND bearish label cooldown elapsed AND bar is within the configured final-bar window of the dataset.
Long: Bullish confluence score >= alert threshold AND bullish label cooldown elapsed.
Short: Bearish confluence score >= alert threshold AND bearish label cooldown elapsed.
