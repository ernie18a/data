Calculation: Candle parts = range = high - low; body = abs(close - open); floored body = max(body, tick size); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Small body = range > 0 AND body <= maximum body fraction × range.
Calculation: Hammer = small body AND lower wick >= minimum dominant-wick multiple × floored body AND lower wick >= minimum dominant-wick fraction × range AND upper wick <= maximum opposite-wick multiple × floored body.
Calculation: Star = small body AND upper wick >= minimum dominant-wick multiple × floored body AND upper wick >= minimum dominant-wick fraction × range AND lower wick <= maximum opposite-wick multiple × floored body.
Calculation: Spinning top = small body AND upper wick >= minimum spinning-wick multiple × floored body AND lower wick >= minimum spinning-wick multiple × floored body.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close.
Calculation: Long shape = (enabled hammer OR enabled spinning top OR enabled bullish engulfing) AND (bullish-close requirement disabled OR close >= open).
Calculation: Short shape = (enabled star OR enabled spinning top OR enabled bearish engulfing) AND (bearish-close requirement disabled OR close <= open).
Calculation: Down context = trend filter disabled OR, according to the selected mode, (close < trend EMA AND trend EMA < trend EMA at the slope lookback), low <= lowest low over the swing lookback including the current bar, OR either of these conditions.
Calculation: Up context = trend filter disabled OR, according to the selected mode, (close > trend EMA AND trend EMA > trend EMA at the slope lookback), high >= highest high over the swing lookback including the current bar, OR either of these conditions.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = short-period EMA > long-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar for bullish; short-period EMA <= long-period EMA on that bar for bearish.
Calculation: Stretch ATR = ATR on the last completed daily bar; the daily timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe according to their relative durations.
Calculation: Long entry level = high of the reversal bar or its preceding bar, according to the selected reference; short entry level = low of the corresponding bar.
Calculation: Long stop = reversal-bar low minus a tick buffer OR long entry level minus a fixed tick distance; short stop = reversal-bar high plus a tick buffer OR short entry level plus a fixed tick distance. Long risk = long entry level - long stop; short risk = short stop - short entry level. Long target = long entry level + either a risk multiple or the reversal-bar range; short target = short entry level - either a risk multiple or the reversal-bar range.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = no pending order or open position AND filled trades on the current day below the enabled daily limit AND bars since the last exit >= the enabled cooldown AND current time is inside the enabled entry window AND outside the enabled force-flat window AND no stop or target exit occurred on this bar.
Calculation: Long setup = long side enabled AND long shape AND down context AND short shape is false AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe direction agrees with the selected trade-direction or prior-trend mode) AND (structure filter disabled OR long entry level > highest high over the structure lookback ending at the bar offset by the excluded-bar count) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI <= oversold threshold) AND (stretch filter disabled OR close <= selected SMA or EMA - stretch multiple × stretch ATR).
Calculation: Short setup = short side enabled AND short shape AND up context AND long shape is false AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe direction agrees with the selected trade-direction or prior-trend mode) AND (structure filter disabled OR short entry level < lowest low over the structure lookback ending at the bar offset by the excluded-bar count) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI >= overbought threshold) AND (stretch filter disabled OR close >= selected SMA or EMA + stretch multiple × stretch ATR).
Long: Entry eligibility AND long setup AND long risk > 0 AND (post-win filter disabled OR no prior winning long exists OR bars since the latest winning trade >= the no-chase window OR the latest winning trade was short OR long entry level < its exit price), followed on a later bar by high >= the stored long entry level while the order remains pending. Cancel an unfilled order after checking fills when bars since setup > the validity limit OR a new day starts OR the force-flat window is entered.
Short: Entry eligibility AND short setup AND short risk > 0 AND (post-win filter disabled OR no prior winning short exists OR bars since the latest winning trade >= the no-chase window OR the latest winning trade was long OR short entry level > its exit price), followed on a later bar by low <= the stored short entry level while the order remains pending. Cancel an unfilled order after checking fills when bars since setup > the validity limit OR a new day starts OR the force-flat window is entered.
Exit: Long, low <= stored long stop OR high >= stored long target OR current time is inside the enabled force-flat window OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period.
Exit: Short, high >= stored short stop OR low <= stored short target OR current time is inside the enabled force-flat window OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period.
Calculation: Fill and stop/target evaluation = process completed lower-timeframe bars chronologically, using the shortest available configured lower timeframe and otherwise the chart bar; check the fill before stop and target on each bar, including the fill bar. If stop and target are both touched in the same evaluation bar, select the configured stop-first or target-first outcome. Check force-flat and holding-period exits after stop and target. A winning trade has directional exit-price profit minus round-trip tick slippage > 0.
