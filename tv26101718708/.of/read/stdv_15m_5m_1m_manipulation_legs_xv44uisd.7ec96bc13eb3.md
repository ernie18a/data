Calculation: Confirmed directional leg = Run an independent state machine for each source timeframe, using completed bars: the chart timeframe, each higher timeframe's last completed bar, and each completed lower-timeframe bar in chronological order. Reset pivots and candidates at a configurable time boundary or data gap. Confirm pivot highs and lows with configurable left and right windows entirely within the current context; retain the latest of each and mark it unused. For direction d, where d=1 is bullish and d=-1 is bearish, a sweep occurs when the bar penetrates the latest unused low for d=1 or high for d=-1 by at least a configurable price distance; mark each swept pivot used, and reject new candidates if both sides are swept on the same bar. A candidate requires the latest opposite pivot as origin A, earlier than the sweep bar and within a configurable maximum leg length, with d×(A−swept pivot)>0. Set extreme B to the sweep bar's low for d=1 or high for d=-1, store the swept pivot, sweep-bar ATR and initial sweep-bar index, and set the break reference to A. Replace an existing candidate when the new origin is more recent; otherwise update its swept pivot and B only if the new sweep bar reaches or exceeds its existing extreme. On each subsequent source bar, expire the candidate when its age from the initial sweep bar exceeds a configurable maximum. Update B on a new directional extreme and reset the consecutive-confirmation count and displacement flag. Replace A with a more recent confirmed opposite pivot strictly between A and B when it is on the correct side of B and within the maximum leg length; reset the break reference and confirmation state. Reject the candidate if A-to-B length in bars exceeds that maximum. Optionally replace the break reference with the latest confirmed opposite pivot after B, resetting the confirmation state. A bar is eligible when |A−B| is at least the larger of a configurable minimum price distance and, if enabled, sweep-bar ATR times a configurable multiplier; d×(A−swept pivot)>0; d×(swept pivot−B) is at least the sweep distance; d×(close−swept pivot)>0; d×(close−break reference) exceeds a configurable break buffer; and the bar is later than B. Displacement requires d×(close−open) to be at least a configurable multiple of sweep-bar ATR, or a configurable minimum price distance when ATR normalization is disabled, AND |close−open| divided by max(high−low, minimum price distance) to be at least a configurable fraction. Reset the consecutive-eligible count and accumulated displacement flag on an ineligible bar; otherwise increment the count and accumulate whether any eligible bar was displaced. Confirm on an eligible bar with either displacement, a configurable number of consecutive eligible bars including at least one displaced bar, or no additional displacement requirement, according to the selected confirmation mode. Reject both candidates if they confirm on the same bar; otherwise confirmation clears both candidates.
