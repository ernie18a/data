Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low of the selected source confirmed after a configurable number of subsequent bars, using configurable preceding and confirmation lookbacks; the source is close or high/low. Update pivot history on confirmation, regardless of volume. If high and low are confirmed together, process the high first and the low second; the low supplies the single pivot label and volume-validation decision for that bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = pivot-bar volume divided by either its rolling SMA of volume, including the pivot bar, or the average positive volume at the same bar offset in prior daily sessions within a configurable session lookback. Use zero when the denominator is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Validated pivot = confirmed pivot whose pivot-bar relative volume is at least a threshold. Its zone price is the pivot source price, or the volume-weighted average of lower-timeframe sub-bar (high + low + close) / 3 within the completed pivot bar; if sub-bar volume is zero or unavailable, use the pivot bar's (high + low + close) / 3. Use completed lower-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Active zones = retain validated pivots in insertion order up to a configurable pool size, removing the oldest excess and removing pivots whose age and distance from current close both exceed their respective thresholds, with distance measured in ATR multiples. When the pool changes, rebuild clusters in insertion order: assign each pivot to the nearest existing centroid strictly within an ATR-multiple radius, or create a cluster; update its centroid as the relative-volume-weighted average and its weight as the sum of pivot relative volumes. Retain zones within an ATR-multiple activation radius of close, sort by descending weight, and keep up to a configurable maximum. The nearest zone strictly above close is resistance and the nearest strictly below close is support.
Calculation: Density above and below a zone = divide the interval centered on its centroid with an ATR-multiple radius into equal bins. For each bar in a configurable lookback, distribute its volume multiplied by a configurable recency-decay factor raised to its age equally across the bins intersected by its high-low range. Divide each bin by the maximum bin volume; average bins strictly above and strictly below the centroid bin separately. A density is unavailable if its zone, positive radius, positive maximum bin volume, or relevant bins are unavailable.
Calculation: Density bias = support density below minus resistance density above when both zones have both densities; otherwise support density below minus support density above when available, otherwise resistance density below minus resistance density above when available. The table's bullish threshold is positive and its bearish threshold is the corresponding negative value, with equality included in the directional regions.
Calculation: Reaction slope = either (close minus the nearest active zone centroid) / ATR, choosing support on equal distance, or the slope of a linear regression of close divided by ATR. Acceleration is the change in reaction slope from the previous bar, replacing an unavailable previous slope with zero, optionally EMA-smoothed. Rejection is (close - low) / (high - low), or one-half for a zero-range bar, only when the nearest zone is within a configurable fraction of the activation radius. Reaction slope and acceleration are unavailable when reaction metrics are disabled; rejection is also unavailable when rejection computation is disabled.
Calculation: Comprehensive gradient score = weighted average of density bias clamped to [-1, 1], reaction slope clamped to [-1, 1], acceleration multiplied by a configurable scale and clamped to [-1, 1], and (rejection - one-half) multiplied by two. Replace unavailable components with zero; use zero when the sum of weights is zero. Optionally smooth the result with an SMA.
Calculation: Pivot-anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of chart-bar (high + low + close) / 3 times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting both sums on the confirmation bar of each validated pivot and including that bar; use current (high + low + close) / 3 if cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Large fair value gap = a positive gap between current low and the high two bars ago, or between the low two bars ago and current high, whose size is at least the selected percentile of the most recent configurable number of positive gaps, including the current gap. The percentile is the sorted gap at floor(count × percentile), capped at the last index; it is unavailable until a minimum gap count is reached.
