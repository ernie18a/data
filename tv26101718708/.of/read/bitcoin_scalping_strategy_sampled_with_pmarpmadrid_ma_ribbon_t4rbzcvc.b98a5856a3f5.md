Calculation: Price-to-moving-average percentile = 100 times the fraction of prior price-to-moving-average ratios in the percentile lookback that are less than or equal to the current ratio; each ratio is the absolute value of the selected price source divided by its selected moving average. Use the available prior bars until the lookback is full; the percentile is unavailable before the moving-average lookback is reached.
Long: Every one of the 18 shorter-period close moving averages is above the longest-period close moving average AND each is greater than or equal to its previous value AND previous close <= previous open AND RSI of close > the RSI threshold AND position side is not long.
Short: Every one of the 18 shorter-period close moving averages except the 11th when ordered from shortest to longest is below the longest-period close moving average AND each of those moving averages is below its previous value AND position side is not short.
Exit: long, price-to-moving-average percentile >= upper percentile threshold AND every one of the 18 shorter-period close moving averages except the 11th when ordered from shortest to longest is below the longest-period close moving average AND each of those moving averages is below its previous value.
Exit: short, price-to-moving-average percentile <= lower percentile threshold.
Exit: long, high >= entry price multiplied by one plus the take-profit fraction OR low <= entry price multiplied by one minus the stop-loss fraction.
Exit: short, low <= entry price multiplied by one minus the take-profit fraction OR high >= entry price multiplied by one plus the stop-loss fraction.
