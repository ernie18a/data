Calculation: Structure and delivery levels = Maintain top and bottom, initially zero, and two independently active pullbacks. At each bar, if the previous bar is bullish and the bearish pullback is inactive, activate it and set its level and index to the previous bar's open and index. If the previous bar is bearish and the bullish pullback is inactive, activate it and set its level and index to the previous bar's open and index. While the bullish pullback is active, set its level and index to the current open and index if open is below its level, then also do so if the current bar is bearish and open is above its level. While the bearish pullback is active, set its level and index to the current open and index if open is above its level, then also do so if the current bar is bullish and open is below its level. Next, if low is below bottom, set bottom to low; if the bearish pullback is active and its index differs from the current index, set top to the greater high at its index and the preceding index, deactivate it, and create a positive delivery level; otherwise, if the previous bar is bullish and the current bar is bearish, set top to the previous high, deactivate that pullback, and create a positive delivery level. Then, if high is above top, set top to high; if the bullish pullback is active and its index differs from the current index, set bottom to the lesser low at its index and the preceding index, deactivate it, and create a negative delivery level; otherwise, if the previous bar is bearish and the current bar is bullish, set bottom to the previous low, deactivate that pullback, and create a negative delivery level.
Long: Chart timeframe is at least the configurable minimum AND a close pivot high is confirmed AND its value is greater than the preceding confirmed close pivot high.
Long: Chart timeframe is at least the configurable minimum AND a close pivot low is confirmed AND its value is greater than the preceding confirmed close pivot low.
Long: A positive delivery level is created.
Long: Low is greater than the high two bars ago.
Short: Chart timeframe is at least the configurable minimum AND a close pivot high is confirmed AND its value is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed close pivot high.
Short: Chart timeframe is at least the configurable minimum AND a close pivot low is confirmed AND its value is less than or equal to the preceding confirmed close pivot low.
Short: A negative delivery level is created.
Short: High is less than the low two bars ago.
