Calculation: Candle location = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Pulse score = clamp(34 × the z-score of the smoothed raw pressure, −100, 100), where raw pressure = candle location × relative volume × 100; smooth raw pressure with an EMA using a shorter lookback than the EMA used for the bias rail, then calculate the z-score against its moving mean and standard deviation over the pressure normalization lookback
Calculation: Bias rail = EMA of pulse score using a longer lookback than the pulse smoothing EMA
Calculation: Pulse acceleration = current pulse score − previous pulse score
Calculation: Body share = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Upper wick share = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Lower wick share = (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick size)
Calculation: Progress in ATR = abs(close − previous close) / max(ATR, minimum tick size)
