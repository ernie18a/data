Calculation: Bar offsets are relative to the decision bar; offset 0 is the current bar. A bullish bar has close > open; a bearish bar has close < open. Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close).
Calculation: Equal high or low = a pivot high or low confirmed on the specified bar, with another same-kind pivot confirmed within the preceding equal-level lookback and absolute pivot-price difference <= the specified bar's close times the equal-level tolerance. Both pivots use the same left and right pivot strength.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = range > 0 AND lower wick / range >= sweep wick threshold AND close > (high + low) / 2 AND volume > volume SMA times sweep volume multiplier. Bearish sweep = range > 0 AND upper wick / range >= sweep wick threshold AND close < (high + low) / 2 AND volume > volume SMA times sweep volume multiplier. The volume SMA includes the specified bar.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current close > current open AND current open < previous close AND current close > previous open AND current body >= previous body times engulfing multiplier. Bearish engulfing = current close < current open AND current open > previous close AND current close < previous open AND current body >= previous body times engulfing multiplier.
Calculation: Session passes when the bar is in an enabled session; if no session filter is enabled, every bar passes. Session windows are interpreted in New York time.
Calculation: Signed volume = volume if close > open, -volume if close < open, otherwise 0. Its absolute-value SMA includes the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish zone confirmation = the first bar of a configurable confirmation sequence is bearish AND every subsequent bar in the sequence is bullish AND the count of bearish bars in the eight bars immediately preceding the first bar >= the zone trend threshold AND, if the zone volume filter is enabled, volume on either of the first two sequence bars > current volume SMA times zone volume multiplier AND first-bar range >= current ATR times zone range multiplier AND either no previous bullish zone exists or abs(current close - previous bullish zone confirmation close) > current close times zone proximity threshold. Bearish zone confirmation reverses bullish and bearish bars and uses the previous bearish zone. Update the respective previous zone confirmation close whenever a zone confirms, including when its extra wick or engulfing condition fails.
Calculation: Bullish three-bar reversal = bar 2 is bearish AND bar 1 low < bar 2 low AND bar 1 high < bar 2 high AND bar 1 is bearish AND bar 0 is bullish AND bar 0 high > bar 2 high. Bearish three-bar reversal = bar 2 is bullish AND bar 1 high > bar 2 high AND bar 1 low > bar 2 low AND bar 1 is bullish AND bar 0 is bearish AND bar 0 low < bar 2 low. For the enhanced selection additionally require bullish close > bar 2 high or bearish close < bar 2 low; for the normal selection require bullish close <= bar 2 high or bearish close >= bar 2 low; the all selection adds no condition.
Calculation: A bullish reversal support process starts or resets on each bullish three-bar reversal, storing that pattern's bar 2 high and the minimum of its bars 1 and 0 lows. On each bar, including the start bar, it confirms if previous close > stored high; it ends if confirmed, previous close < stored minimum low, or a bearish three-bar reversal occurs. A bearish reversal resistance process starts or resets on each bearish three-bar reversal, storing that pattern's bar 2 low and the maximum of its bars 1 and 0 highs. On each bar, including the start bar, it confirms if previous close < stored low; it ends if confirmed, previous close > stored maximum high, or a bullish three-bar reversal occurs. Confirmation is evaluated before cancellation.
Long: Bullish sweep on the current bar AND session passes AND, if the equal-level filter is enabled, equal low on the current bar.
Short: Bearish sweep on the current bar AND session passes AND, if the equal-level filter is enabled, equal high on the current bar.
Long: Bullish sweep on bar 1 AND bullish engulfing on bar 0 AND session passes on bar 0 AND, if the equal-level filter is enabled, equal low on bar 1.
Short: Bearish sweep on bar 1 AND bearish engulfing on bar 0 AND session passes on bar 0 AND, if the equal-level filter is enabled, equal high on bar 1.
Long: Session passes AND current low <= lowest low over the extreme lookback including the current bar AND signed volume > 0 AND close > (high + low) / 2 AND abs(signed volume) > its SMA times the divergence volume multiplier AND, if the trend filter is enabled, NOT (shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA AND close > shorter-period EMA).
Short: Session passes AND current high >= highest high over the extreme lookback including the current bar AND signed volume < 0 AND (body = 0 OR (close - open) / range < the strong-body threshold) AND abs(signed volume) > its SMA times the divergence volume multiplier AND, if the trend filter is enabled, NOT (shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA AND close < shorter-period EMA).
Long: Bullish zone confirmation.
Short: Bearish zone confirmation.
Long: Bullish three-bar reversal.
Short: Bearish three-bar reversal.
Long: Bullish reversal support process confirms.
Short: Bearish reversal resistance process confirms.
Long: A swing pivot low is confirmed using the same left and right pivot strength.
Short: A swing pivot high is confirmed using the same left and right pivot strength.
Long: A swing pivot high is confirmed AND its pivot bar is bullish AND the bar before the pivot is bearish AND pivot close > preceding open AND pivot open < preceding close AND no simultaneously confirmed pivot low has either lower wick > body AND upper wick < body, or upper wick > body AND lower wick < body.
Short: A swing pivot low is confirmed AND its pivot bar is bearish AND the bar before the pivot is bullish AND pivot close < preceding open AND pivot open > preceding close AND the pivot bar satisfies neither lower wick > body AND upper wick < body nor upper wick > body AND lower wick < body.
