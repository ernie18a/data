Calculation: DMI readings = +DI and -DI calculated independently on each of three progressively longer timeframes. Each timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Long: +DI > -DI on any one of the three timeframes.
Short: +DI < -DI on any one of the three timeframes.
Long: +DI crosses above -DI on any one of the three timeframes.
Short: +DI crosses below -DI on any one of the three timeframes.
