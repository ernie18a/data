Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of either the first configured number of chart bars in each day or the chart bars in a configured opening session, using the configured session timezone. Lock it after the last opening-range bar; reset all probe and per-side failure state when a new range starts.
Calculation: Tracking = the range is locked, the current bar is outside the opening-range period and within the configured tracking session if session mode is used, the number of bars since the final range bar is at most the watch limit, the chart timeframe is intraday and within the configured supported bounds, and the bar index exceeds the warmup length. Warmup length is the larger of the ATR-baseline and volume-average lookbacks plus the ATR lookback plus a warmup offset.
Calculation: ATR buffer = ATR multiplied by its configured break or reclaim multiplier. Floor ATR and opening-range span at a positive price increment. Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average, or 1 if the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Probe state = while tracking and no probe is active, start an upside probe if high minus range high exceeds the break buffer, no upside failure has been confirmed for this range, and that upside depth is at least range low minus low; otherwise start a downside probe if range low minus low exceeds the break buffer and no downside failure has been confirmed for this range. Store its start bar, running extreme and number of closes beyond its boundary, including the start bar. On each tracking bar update the extreme and count. A reclaim is a close below range high minus the reclaim buffer for an upside probe, or above range low plus the reclaim buffer for a downside probe. Process a confirmed reclaim first, even if the probe is past its reclaim window or exceeds the maximum depth; then clear the probe whether or not its quality passes. Otherwise clear it when its depth exceeds the current ATR times the maximum-depth multiplier or its age exceeds the reclaim window. A probe persists without updates while tracking is inactive.
Calculation: Failure quality = clamp(x) confines x to 0–100; depth is the distance from the probe extreme to its range boundary divided by current ATR; wick is the confirmation bar's upper wick for an upside probe or lower wick for a downside probe, divided by its high-low range floored at a positive price increment; inside distance is boundary minus close for an upside probe or close minus boundary for a downside probe. Base quality = 0.20 × clamp(100 − abs(depth − 0.42) × 85) + 0.25 × clamp(wick × 145) + 0.25 × clamp(inside distance / max(range span × 0.35, positive price increment) × 100) + 0.20 × clamp(100 − probe age in bars × 8) + 0.10 × clamp(100 − outside-close count × 18). If volume scoring is enabled, quality = 0.86 × base quality + 0.14 × clamp(relative volume / 1.35 × 100); otherwise quality = base quality.
Calculation: Probe-marker eligibility = probe markers are enabled AND the number of bars since the last printed probe marker is at least the probe-marker cooldown or no probe marker has yet printed AND the previous printed probe-marker direction differs from the new probe direction. Reset the previous marker direction and marker time when a new opening range starts.
Long: A downside probe starts AND probe-marker eligibility is satisfied.
Short: An upside probe starts AND probe-marker eligibility is satisfied.
Long: Tracking is active AND an active downside probe reclaims on the confirmed bar AND failure quality is at least the minimum quality; mark the downside failure as confirmed for the current range.
Short: Tracking is active AND an active upside probe reclaims on the confirmed bar AND failure quality is at least the minimum quality; mark the upside failure as confirmed for the current range.
