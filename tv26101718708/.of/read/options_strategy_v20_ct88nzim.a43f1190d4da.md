Calculation: BodyRatio = abs(close - open) / (high - low + tiny constant)
Calculation: Reversal = (last closed trade was long AND short-trend EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback) AND RSI <= short-side RSI threshold) OR (last closed trade was short AND EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND RSI >= long-side RSI threshold)
Calculation: Cooldown = NOT Reversal AND current bar is within a fixed number of bars after the exit bar of the last closed trade
Long: position is flat AND bar time is inside regular trading session AND bar time is outside the no-trade time window AND NOT Cooldown AND EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND abs(EMA short - EMA long) >= minimum price distance AND RSI >= long RSI threshold AND RSI <= long RSI upper cap (cap above threshold) AND volume >= volume multiplier * SMA(volume, long lookback) AND BodyRatio >= minimum body ratio AND (optional filter) close > session VWAP; enter at the signal bar close
Short: position is flat AND bar time is inside regular trading session AND bar time is outside the no-trade time window AND NOT Cooldown AND EMA(close, short lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback) AND abs(EMA short - EMA long) >= minimum price distance AND RSI <= short RSI threshold AND RSI >= short RSI lower floor (floor below threshold) AND volume >= volume multiplier * SMA(volume, long lookback) AND BodyRatio >= minimum body ratio AND (optional filter) close < session VWAP; enter at the signal bar close
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop multiplier * ATR(at entry bar, fixed) OR high >= entry price + take-profit/stop ratio * stop multiplier * ATR(at entry bar, fixed)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop multiplier * ATR(at entry bar, fixed) OR low <= entry price - take-profit/stop ratio * stop multiplier * ATR(at entry bar, fixed)
Exit: long or short, bar time equals the end-of-day close time (close position at that bar's close)
