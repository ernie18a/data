Calculation: Sensor = for each of four ordered timeframes, compute OBV as the cumulative sum of volume multiplied by the sign of the close change; compute the difference between the current and one-bar-offset linear regressions of OBV, divide by the rolling standard deviation of OBV floored at 1e-10, and clip to [-3, 3]. Sensor change is the sensor minus its previous bar’s sensor, calculated within that timeframe. The timeframes may be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe. Select either the previous completed bar of each timeframe or its developing bar; when a timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, use the last available intrabar. Use the same regression and standard-deviation lookbacks across the four timeframes.
Calculation: Consensus = weighted mean of the four sensors, with nonnegative weights normalized to sum to one; if all weights are zero, use the fixed fallback weight ratio 1:2:4:3 in timeframe order.
Calculation: Concentration = 1 - clip(weighted standard deviation of the four sensors around consensus / concentration scale, 0, 1).
Calculation: Bullish strength = 100 times the cube root of concentration multiplied by the sum of weights whose sensor is positive multiplied by the weighted sum of clip(sensor change / acceleration scale, 0, 1).
Calculation: Bearish strength = 100 times the cube root of concentration multiplied by the sum of weights whose sensor is negative multiplied by the weighted sum of clip(-sensor change / acceleration scale, 0, 1).
Long: Bullish strength >= strength threshold AND bullish strength > bearish strength
Short: Bearish strength >= strength threshold AND bearish strength > bullish strength
