Calculation: Per-timeframe OBV z = clamp((current linear-regression value of OBV minus its value one bar earlier) / rolling standard deviation of OBV, -3, 3); OBV cumulatively adds volume when close rises, subtracts it when close falls, and is unchanged otherwise. Use a shorter regression lookback than the normalization lookback. Calculate on the chart timeframe and three progressively higher timeframes, using each higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: OBV z change = current OBV z minus its value on the preceding bar of that timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish strength = 100 × cube root of concentration × bullish side agreement × bullish acceleration agreement; bearish strength uses bearish side agreement and bearish acceleration agreement. Concentration = 1 − clamp(weighted standard deviation of the four OBV z values / concentration scale, 0, 1). Side agreement = the normalized-weight sum of sensors whose OBV z is positive (bullish) or negative (bearish). Acceleration agreement = the normalized-weight sum of clamp(the OBV z change in the relevant direction / acceleration scale, 0, 1). Normalize the four attention weights to sum to 1.
Long: bullish strength ≥ strength threshold AND bullish strength > bearish strength
Short: bearish strength ≥ strength threshold AND bearish strength > bullish strength
