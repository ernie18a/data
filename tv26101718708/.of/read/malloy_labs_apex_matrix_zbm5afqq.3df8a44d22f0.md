Calculation: EMA strength = absolute difference between short-term and long-term chart-timeframe EMAs, divided by the long-term EMA, multiplied by 100; EMA momentum = change in EMA strength over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Cloud lines = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over each lookback on a higher timeframe: conversion line uses the shortest lookback, base line an intermediate lookback, and second leading line the longest lookback; first leading line = average of conversion and base lines. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Cloud strength = absolute difference between higher-timeframe close and the average of the two leading lines, divided by higher-timeframe close, multiplied by 100
Calculation: MACD histogram = higher-timeframe MACD line minus its signal line; MACD line = short-term EMA minus long-term EMA; signal line = EMA of the MACD line. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. MACD acceleration = change in histogram over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: RSI momentum = change in higher-timeframe 14-period RSI over a configurable lookback. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bull power = sum of enabled component scores: EMA score is the lesser of 5 × EMA strength and 25 when short-term EMA > long-term EMA, EMA momentum > 0, and close > short-term EMA; cloud score is the lesser of 2.5 × cloud strength and 25 when higher-timeframe close is above both leading lines and conversion line > base line; MACD score is the lesser of 20 × absolute MACD histogram and 25 when histogram > 0 and MACD acceleration > 0; RSI score is the lesser of 0.25 × absolute RSI distance from 50 and 25 when RSI > 45 and < 75 and RSI momentum > 0. Otherwise each component score is 0.
Calculation: Bear power = sum of enabled component scores: EMA score is the lesser of 5 × EMA strength and 25 when short-term EMA < long-term EMA, EMA momentum < 0, and close < short-term EMA; cloud score is the lesser of 2.5 × cloud strength and 25 when higher-timeframe close is below both leading lines and the cloud-bull condition is false; MACD score is the lesser of 20 × absolute MACD histogram and 25 when histogram < 0 and MACD acceleration < 0; RSI score is the lesser of 0.25 × absolute RSI distance from 50 and 25 when RSI > 25 and < 55 and RSI momentum < 0. Otherwise each component score is 0.
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of bull power minus bear power, using a configurable smoothing lookback
Long: EMA bull condition OR cloud-bull condition OR (MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD acceleration > 0) OR (RSI > 45 AND RSI < 75 AND RSI momentum > 0) OR (signal line > 0 AND absolute signal line > configurable signal threshold)
Short: EMA trend is false OR cloud-bull condition is false OR (MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD acceleration < 0) OR (RSI < 45 AND RSI momentum < 0) OR (signal line < 0 AND absolute signal line > configurable signal threshold)
