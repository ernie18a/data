Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4
Calculation: Signal line = SMA or EMA of Heikin-Ashi close, or of its linear-regression value when linear regression is enabled; the linear-regression lookback is longer than the signal-average lookback.
Long: Heikin-Ashi close crosses above the signal line
Short: Heikin-Ashi close crosses below the signal line
Exit: long, Heikin-Ashi close crosses below the signal line
Exit: short, Heikin-Ashi close crosses above the signal line
