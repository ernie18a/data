Calculation: Swing = the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed after its respective left and right lookbacks; optionally replace the high with the highest high since the pivot low when the low is older, or replace the low with the lowest low since the pivot high otherwise. Retain the original confirmed pivot times for determining swing direction. The swing is valid when both pivots exist and the effective high differs from the effective low.
Calculation: Swing volume profile = divide the effective swing price range into equal bins. Include bars from the earlier effective endpoint through the later endpoint, optionally extending through the current chart bar, and trim the beginning to a maximum lookback. Distribute each bar's volume either equally across bins touched by its clipped high-low range, entirely into the typical-price bin, or partly into the close bin and the remainder equally across touched bins. Optionally substitute equal bar weights when cumulative volume is unavailable. Optionally distribute lower-timeframe bars for the current chart bar only, using all completed lower-timeframe bars within it; use chart bars for history. Rebuild when an effective swing endpoint changes and optionally on every completed chart bar; otherwise retain the previous profile.
Calculation: Value area = start at the highest-volume profile bin, choosing the lowest bin on a tie; repeatedly add the higher-volume adjacent bin, choosing the lower bin on a tie, until the accumulated volume reaches the target fraction of profile volume. Its bounds are the outer edges of the included bins.
Long: swing is valid AND confirmed pivot low time < confirmed pivot high time
Short: swing is valid AND confirmed pivot high time <= confirmed pivot low time
Long: swing volume profile total volume > 0 AND close > value area upper bound
Short: swing volume profile total volume > 0 AND close < value area lower bound
