Calculation: Price-action confirmation = any enabled pattern: (close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND absolute body of previous bar < a fraction of absolute body two bars ago AND close > open AND close > midpoint of open and close two bars ago) OR (close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close > previous open) OR (high < previous high AND low > previous low AND close > open) OR (lower wick > a multiple of absolute body AND lower wick > upper wick) OR (absolute body <= a fraction of high minus low). Body = close minus open; lower wick = min(open, close) minus low; upper wick = high minus max(open, close).
Calculation: Negative divergence state = initially false; whenever a high pivot is confirmed using equal numbers of bars on each side, set it true if that pivot's high > the preceding confirmed pivot high AND RSI at that pivot < RSI at the preceding pivot, otherwise set it false. Reset it to false when close crosses below the long-lookback SMA.
Calculation: Target levels = pivot plus range multiplied by each of five increasing multipliers. Pivot = (high + low + close) / a divisor, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; range = its high minus low, replaced by a configurable minimum price increment when zero. Recalculate levels as higher-timeframe bars complete.
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar low reduced by a configurable percentage at entry. While long, update the maximum high seen since the signal bar; sequentially raise the stop to entry price if high >= first target and stop < entry price, to first target if high >= second target and stop < first target, to second target if high >= third target and stop < second target, and to third target if high >= fourth target and stop < third target. If percentage trailing is enabled and maximum high reduced by the same configurable percentage > stop, raise stop to that value.
Long: position is flat AND short-lookback SMA > medium-lookback SMA AND medium-lookback SMA > long-lookback SMA AND low <= medium-lookback SMA + ATR multiplied by a configurable fraction AND price-action confirmation AND (negative divergence filter is disabled OR negative divergence state is false).
Exit: long, high >= fifth target OR low <= long stop.
