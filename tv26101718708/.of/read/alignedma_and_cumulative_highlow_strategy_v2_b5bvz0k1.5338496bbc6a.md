Calculation: Moving-average alignment score = +1 when close and seven moving averages with progressively longer lookbacks are strictly descending; −1 when strictly ascending; otherwise, if partial alignment is enabled, +0.5 for at least six of seven upward comparisons, +0.25 for at least four, −0.5 for at most one, and −0.25 otherwise; if disabled, 0.
Calculation: Alignment direction = direction of the rolling sum of moving-average alignment scores: +1 if the sum exceeds its value at the short lag when positive, or its value at the longer lag otherwise; −1 if it is below its value at the short lag when negative, or its value at the longer lag otherwise; otherwise retain the prior nonzero direction.
Calculation: High/low index = +1 if the current high equals the highest high over the lookback, otherwise −1 if the current low equals the lowest low over the lookback, otherwise 0.
Calculation: High/low direction = direction of the rolling sum of high/low indices, using the same signed short-lag/longer-lag comparison and prior-direction carry as alignment direction.
Calculation: Annual high filter = enabled condition: the latest completed higher-timeframe annual high exceeds both historical annual-high samples, or close exceeds the latest annual high when no historical samples are available, exceeds the greater of the latest and older sample when only one is available, or exceeds the greater of the latest and the lower of the two samples when both are available; otherwise true. Historical samples use progressively longer chart-timeframe lookbacks.
Calculation: Annual low filter = enabled condition: the latest completed higher-timeframe annual low is below both historical annual-low samples, or close is below the latest annual low when no historical samples are available, below the lesser of the latest and older sample when only one is available, or below the lesser of the latest and the higher of the two samples when both are available; otherwise true. Historical samples use progressively longer chart-timeframe lookbacks.
Calculation: Trailing stop = Supertrend; when Supertrend is beyond entry price by the configured risk-reward multiple of ATR times the Supertrend multiplier, offset Supertrend by a configurable ATR amount in the direction of the position; ratchet upward for longs and downward for shorts.
Long: alignment direction = +1 AND high/low direction = +1 AND annual high filter is true AND selected moving average is above its value at the configured lag AND Supertrend is bearish AND low > trailing stop AND (position is flat OR the long position is risk-free) AND datetime is within the selected backtest date window.
Short: alignment direction = −1 AND high/low direction = −1 AND annual low filter is true AND selected moving average is below its value at the configured lag AND Supertrend is bullish AND low < trailing stop AND (position is flat OR the short position is risk-free) AND datetime is within the selected backtest date window.
Exit: Long, Supertrend turns bearish OR low <= trailing stop.
Exit: Short, Supertrend turns bullish.
