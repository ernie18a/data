Calculation: Sampled path efficiency = absolute difference between the final sample close and opening sample price divided by the sum of absolute changes from opening sample price through each sample close, using completed lower-timeframe samples (or confirmed chart-timeframe bars when fallback applies)
Calculation: Close location = (final sample close − completed opening-range low) / opening-range width
Calculation: Expansion skew = [(completed opening-range high − opening sample price) − (opening sample price − completed opening-range low)] / opening-range width
Calculation: Late expansion share = (final opening-range width − width at the halfway checkpoint) / final opening-range width
Calculation: Early checkpoint width share = width at the configured early checkpoint / final opening-range width
Long: Complete, non-partial opening range AND ((sampled path efficiency ≥ configured drive minimum AND close location ≥ configured upper-edge threshold AND expansion skew ≥ configured directional threshold) OR (late expansion share ≥ configured minimum AND expansion skew ≥ configured directional threshold))
Short: Complete, non-partial opening range AND ((sampled path efficiency ≥ configured drive minimum AND close location ≤ 1 − configured upper-edge threshold AND expansion skew ≤ −configured directional threshold) OR (late expansion share ≥ configured minimum AND expansion skew ≤ −configured directional threshold))
Long: Complete, non-partial opening range is locked AND within the configured post-range window AND no accepted departure has occurred AND the configured number of consecutive completed lower-timeframe samples (or confirmed chart-timeframe fallback bars) have sample close above the range high plus the configured range-width buffer, or sample high above it when wick evidence is selected
