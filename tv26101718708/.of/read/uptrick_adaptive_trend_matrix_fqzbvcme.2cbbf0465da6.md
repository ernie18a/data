Calculation: Basis = EMA of an EMA of hlc3; smoothed ATR = EMA of ATR, using the same smoothing length as the outer basis EMA.
Calculation: Bull trail = basis minus smoothed ATR times a positive trail multiplier on the first available bar; subsequently, if close is below the previous bull trail, use the current basis minus smoothed ATR times the multiplier, otherwise use the greater of that candidate and the previous bull trail. Bear trail = basis plus smoothed ATR times the same multiplier on the first available bar; subsequently, if close is above the previous bear trail, use the current basis plus smoothed ATR times the multiplier, otherwise use the lesser of that candidate and the previous bear trail.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation score = count of true conditions among: shorter EMA of close > longer EMA of close; RSI of close > an upper RSI threshold; MACD line > signal line AND histogram > zero; stochastic %K > its SMA %D AND %K > a lower stochastic threshold; CCI of hlc3 > zero; +DI > -DI AND ADX >= its minimum; ROC of close > zero; MFI of hlc3 > an upper MFI threshold; OBV > its EMA; close > session VWAP of hlc3. The upper RSI and MFI thresholds are above their respective midpoints; the lower stochastic threshold is below its midpoint.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation score = count of true conditions among: shorter EMA of close < longer EMA of close; RSI of close < a lower RSI threshold; MACD line < signal line AND histogram < zero; stochastic %K < its SMA %D AND %K < an upper stochastic threshold; CCI of hlc3 < zero; -DI > +DI AND ADX >= its minimum; ROC of close < zero; MFI of hlc3 < a lower MFI threshold; OBV < its EMA; close < session VWAP of hlc3. The lower RSI and MFI thresholds are below their respective midpoints; the upper stochastic threshold is above its midpoint. Use the same indicator parameters as in the bullish score.
Calculation: Trend state = initially bullish if close >= basis, otherwise bearish, including when basis is unavailable; change to bullish on a long signal and bearish on a short signal. The most recent signal direction is initially neither and changes to the direction of each signal.
Calculation: Take-profit levels = entry signal close plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, the smoothed ATR on the entry signal bar multiplied by each of three independently specified positive multipliers. Reset all levels and their hit states on every new signal; mark each level hit on its first qualifying bar.
Long: Trend state is bearish AND most recent signal direction is not long AND close > current bear trail AND close > basis AND bullish confirmation score >= required confirmation count.
Short: Trend state is bullish AND most recent signal direction is not short AND close < current bull trail AND close < basis AND bearish confirmation score >= required confirmation count.
Exit: long, current bar is after the latest long signal AND no short signal occurs on the current bar AND high >= any long take-profit level that has not previously been hit since that signal.
Exit: short, current bar is after the latest short signal AND no long signal occurs on the current bar AND low <= any short take-profit level that has not previously been hit since that signal.
