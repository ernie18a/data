Calculation: Pair flow score = On the chart timeframe, or on a selected higher or lower timeframe using either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar, calculate ATR and: momentum = clamp((close − close at the momentum lookback) / ATR / momentum scale, −1, 1), or zero if ATR is not positive; relative volume = clamp(volume / SMA(volume), lower activity bound, upper activity bound), or 1 if volume is unavailable or its SMA is not positive; signed volume = clamp((relative volume − 1) × sign(momentum) / volume scale, −1, 1); trend = clamp((shorter EMA(close) − longer EMA(close)) / ATR / trend scale, −1, 1), or zero if ATR is not positive; ATR percentage = ATR / close × 100, or zero if close is zero; volatility expansion = ATR percentage / SMA(ATR percentage) − 1, or zero if that SMA is not positive; signed volatility = clamp(volatility expansion, −1, 1) × sign(momentum); breadth = 1 if close > shorter EMA(close), otherwise −1. Take the weighted average of momentum, signed volume, trend, signed volatility, and breadth, using a positive minimum denominator, smooth it with an EMA, and multiply by the score scale. The volume and ATR-percentage SMAs use the same lookback.
Long: Pair flow score ≥ positive signal threshold
Short: Pair flow score ≤ negative signal threshold, whose magnitude equals the positive signal threshold
