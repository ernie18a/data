Calculation: Return z-score = (log(close / previous close) - SMA of log returns) / standard deviation of log returns over the same lookback; use zero when the previous close is zero or the standard deviation is effectively zero.
Calculation: Regime = bullish when the short-term close EMA > the long-term close EMA AND close > the basis-length close EMA AND return z-score > -0.25; otherwise bearish when the short-term close EMA < the long-term close EMA AND close < the basis-length close EMA AND return z-score < 0.25; otherwise neutral. The return z-score and basis EMA use the same lookback.
Calculation: Transition probability from regime A to regime B = the rolling count of bars whose previous regime is A and current regime is B divided by the rolling count of bars whose previous regime is A, using the same transition lookback. If the denominator is effectively zero, use 0.50 for bullish-to-bullish and bearish-to-bearish, 0.25 for bullish-to-bearish and bearish-to-bullish, 0.34 for neutral-to-bullish, and 0.33 for neutral-to-bearish. Treat the previous regime on the first bar as neutral.
Calculation: Bull probability = 100 times the transition probability from the current regime to bullish; bear probability = 100 times the transition probability from the current regime to bearish. Clamp each to [0, 100].
Calculation: Efficiency = absolute change in close over the efficiency lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, clamped to [0, 1]; use zero when the denominator is effectively zero.
Calculation: Adaptive basis = previous adaptive basis + clamp(0.08 + 0.32 × efficiency, 0.05, 0.45) × (close - previous adaptive basis); initialize with the basis-length close EMA.
Calculation: Corridor width = ATR × (1.05 + volatility rank / 100) × (1.28 if regime is neutral, otherwise 0.92) × (1 + absolute difference between continuation probability and 50 divided by 140). Volatility rank is the rolling percent rank of 100 × ATR / close, using zero when close is zero and rank 50 when the rank is unavailable. Continuation probability is the current regime's transition probability to itself, multiplied by 100 and clamped to [0, 100]; for neutral-to-neutral use 0.33 when its transition denominator is effectively zero.
Calculation: Upper corridor = adaptive basis + corridor width; lower corridor = adaptive basis - corridor width.
Long: Regime = bullish.
Short: Regime = bearish.
Long: Close > close EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Close < close EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Regime = bullish AND bull probability >= continuation threshold AND absolute difference between bull probability and bear probability >= minimum spread AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > close EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND ((close > adaptive basis AND previous close <= previous adaptive basis) OR (close > upper corridor AND previous close <= previous upper corridor)).
Short: Regime = bearish AND bear probability >= continuation threshold AND absolute difference between bull probability and bear probability >= minimum spread AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < close EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND ((close < adaptive basis AND previous close >= previous adaptive basis) OR (close < lower corridor AND previous close >= previous lower corridor)).
Exit: long, regime != bullish OR close < lower corridor OR bear probability > bull probability + minimum spread.
Exit: short, regime != bearish OR close > upper corridor OR bull probability > bear probability + minimum spread.
