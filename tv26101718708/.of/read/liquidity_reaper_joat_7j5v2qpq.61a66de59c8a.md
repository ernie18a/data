Calculation: High and low pools = confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same number of bars on each side; a pivot may equal an earlier extreme but must be strictly more extreme than later bars in its confirmation window. Maintain separate oldest-first lists, adding confirmed pivots before checking raids and removing the oldest when a configurable per-side capacity is exceeded.
Calculation: Pending sweep = when high exceeds a high pool or low falls below a low pool, remove every breached pool and replace that side's pending sweep with the oldest breached pool; record its level, raid bar, raid-bar range floored at a configurable minimum price increment, and raid-bar high or low. Update the extreme through the current bar. A new raid replaces any pending sweep on that side; clear a sweep on confirmation or after its confirmation window expires.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its current simple moving average when that average is positive; otherwise 1.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR at entry multiplied by a configurable factor, or entry price multiplied by a configurable percentage. Fix it at entry. The target multiplier is that of the highest-priority enabled target, chosen in order third, second, first; if none is enabled, no target applies.
Long: A pending low sweep is at least one bar old and no older than the confirmation window AND close > swept level AND close > open AND (volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= volume multiplier) AND (swept level - lowest low since the raid, including the current bar) / raid-bar range >= minimum rejection fraction.
Short: A pending high sweep is at least one bar old and no older than the confirmation window AND close < swept level AND close < open AND (volume filter disabled OR relative volume >= volume multiplier) AND (highest high since the raid, including the current bar - swept level) / raid-bar range >= minimum rejection fraction.
Calculation: Position tracking = enter or replace the tracked position at the signal bar's close whenever target tracking is enabled; if both signals occur on the same bar, choose long. Process replacement before exit checks and do not exit on the entry bar.
Exit: long, bar after entry AND (low <= entry price - risk distance OR high >= entry price + risk distance * target multiplier).
Exit: short, bar after entry AND (high >= entry price + risk distance OR low <= entry price - risk distance * target multiplier).
