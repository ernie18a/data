Calculation: Delta = simple moving average over a short lookback of volume*((close-low)-(high-close))/max(high-low, minimum tick)
Calculation: Pivot low (pivot high) = bar whose low (high) is the lowest (highest) among N bars on each side; it is confirmed only N bars later, so evaluate on the confirmation bar using the pivot bar's own values. Previous pivot = the immediately preceding pivot of the same type, whether or not it produced a signal.
Calculation: DeltaChange% = abs(Delta at current pivot - Delta at previous pivot)/max(abs(Delta at previous pivot), 1)*100
Long: a pivot low is confirmed on the current bar AND a previous pivot low exists AND bars between the two pivots <= a maximum gap AND DeltaChange% >= a minimum threshold AND volume at the pivot bar >= a multiple of the simple moving average of volume (longer lookback than the Delta smoothing) AND ((pivot low < previous pivot low AND Delta at pivot > Delta at previous pivot) OR (pivot low > previous pivot low AND Delta at pivot < Delta at previous pivot))
Short: a pivot high is confirmed on the current bar AND a previous pivot high exists AND bars between the two pivots <= a maximum gap AND DeltaChange% >= a minimum threshold AND volume at the pivot bar >= a multiple of the simple moving average of volume (longer lookback than the Delta smoothing) AND ((pivot high > previous pivot high AND Delta at pivot < Delta at previous pivot) OR (pivot high < previous pivot high AND Delta at pivot > Delta at previous pivot))
