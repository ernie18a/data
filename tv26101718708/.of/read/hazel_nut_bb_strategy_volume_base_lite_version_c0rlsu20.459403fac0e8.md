Calculation: Bollinger basis = selected SMA, EMA, or VWMA of close; Bollinger upper/lower bands = basis ± standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of close, using the same lookback as the basis.
Calculation: Bollinger %B = (close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band), or 0.5 when band width is zero.
Calculation: Normalized bandwidth = (current bandwidth − lowest bandwidth over the squeeze lookback) / (highest bandwidth over that lookback − lowest bandwidth over that lookback), or 0 when the denominator is zero; bandwidth = (upper band − lower band) / basis.
Calculation: CVD pressure = change in the EMA of cumulative volume delta, divided by ATR; volume delta = buy volume − sell volume. Buy and sell volume use either the chart bar’s geometric estimate (volume × (close − low)/(high − low) and volume × (high − close)/(high − low), respectively; split volume equally if high = low), or up/down volume aggregated from lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including the still-forming lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Average volume = SMA of total volume over the Bollinger lookback; total volume is chart-bar volume in geometric mode and the sum of lower-timeframe up/down volume in intrabar mode.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: VCS trailing stop = for a long, the highest of its initial stop and all subsequent close − ATR × trailing-stop multiplier; for a short, the lowest of its initial stop and all subsequent close + ATR × trailing-stop multiplier. Initial stop = entry-signal close − ATR × stop multiplier for a long, or entry-signal close + ATR × stop multiplier for a short.
Long: Valid-volume-bar count > max(Bollinger lookback, squeeze lookback) AND ((close > previous bar’s upper band AND total volume > average volume × volume-spike multiplier AND (normalized bandwidth ≤ squeeze threshold on this bar or one of the prior 3 bars) AND volume delta > 0 AND CVD pressure > positive pressure threshold) OR (%B < 0.05 AND total volume > average volume × absorption volume multiplier AND ((high > low AND abs(close − open)/(high − low) < effort/result threshold) OR volume delta > 0) AND CVD pressure > −CVD confirmation threshold).
Short: Valid-volume-bar count > max(Bollinger lookback, squeeze lookback) AND ((close < previous bar’s lower band AND total volume > average volume × volume-spike multiplier AND (normalized bandwidth ≤ squeeze threshold on this bar or one of the prior 3 bars) AND volume delta < 0 AND CVD pressure < negative pressure threshold) OR (%B > 0.95 AND total volume > average volume × absorption volume multiplier AND ((high > low AND abs(close − open)/(high − low) < effort/result threshold) OR volume delta < 0) AND CVD pressure < CVD confirmation threshold).
Exit: Long, low ≤ VCS trailing stop when the active entry type is VCS.
Exit: Short, high ≥ VCS trailing stop when the active entry type is VCS.
Exit: Long, low ≤ fixed initial stop OR (dynamic target > latest entry price AND high ≥ dynamic target) when the active entry type is absorption; dynamic target is the Bollinger basis or upper band, according to the selected target setting.
Exit: Short, high ≥ fixed initial stop OR (dynamic target < latest entry price AND low ≤ dynamic target) when the active entry type is distribution; dynamic target is the Bollinger basis or lower band, according to the selected target setting.
