Calculation: Box = On each chart bar, find confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot lengths. For highs and lows separately, search pivots within the box lookback newest first; anchor on the newest pivot and select the first older pivot whose price is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the anchor and whose bar is separated from it by at least a minimum gap. Each boundary is the average of its two selected pivots. Create a box only when both boundaries exist, the upper boundary exceeds the lower boundary, box height divided by current ATR is within its minimum and maximum bounds, current close is within the boundaries extended by an ATR-scaled tolerance, and the fraction of available closes in the box lookback within the unextended boundaries is at least a minimum. Freeze the boundaries and start bar when the box is created. Keep it active until an upward breakout, downward breakdown, or age exceeds a maximum; evaluate breakout and breakdown before expiration and allow a new box starting on the next bar.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or one-half when high equals low.
Calculation: Upward breakout = An active box exists AND each close in the confirmation lookback exceeds the frozen upper boundary plus current ATR times a breakout buffer AND current close position is at least a minimum.
Calculation: Downward breakdown = An active box exists AND each close in the confirmation lookback is below the frozen lower boundary minus current ATR times a breakout buffer AND current close position is at most one minus the upward-breakout minimum.
Calculation: Initial stop = At entry, select one of: entry price minus entry ATR times a multiplier selected from four tiers by entry ATR divided by entry close; frozen box lower boundary minus entry ATR times a multiplier; frozen box lower boundary reduced by a percentage; box midpoint; or the greater of the first two stop candidates. Freeze the selected stop.
Calculation: Risk = At entry, freeze the greater of entry price minus initial stop and entry ATR times a minimum-risk multiplier.
Calculation: First target = At entry, freeze the box upper boundary plus box height times a target multiplier.
Calculation: Second target = At entry, freeze entry price plus risk times a target multiplier.
Calculation: Protection stop = Entry price plus risk times a protection multiplier.
Calculation: Runner stop = Initial stop until the first target has been touched; protection stop after the first target has been touched and before the second target has been touched; thereafter the greater of protection stop and the highest close since the position became active minus current ATR times a trailing multiplier. Update target-touch states using current high before calculating the runner stop.
Long: Upward breakout AND position is flat AND risk > 0.
Exit: long, first-target portion AND (low <= initial stop OR high >= first target).
Exit: long, second-target portion AND (low <= initial stop before the first target is touched OR low <= protection stop after the first target is touched OR high >= second target).
Exit: long, runner portion AND low <= runner stop.
