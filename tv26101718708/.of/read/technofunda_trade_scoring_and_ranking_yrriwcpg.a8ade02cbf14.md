Calculation: Score = number of the following that are true on the bar, plus the fixed fundamental points (taken as all satisfied because they are not in the data). (a) close > short-term SMA of close AND close > medium-term SMA AND close > long-term SMA. (b) short-term SMA > medium-term SMA AND medium-term SMA > long-term SMA. (c) long-term SMA > its own value some bars earlier. (d) close >= a fixed fraction (below 1) of the highest high over a long lookback (about one year). (e) volume surge = close >= previous close AND volume > the maximum volume among the previous N bars (current bar excluded) that closed below their prior close (0 if none) AND volume >= a multiple (above 1) of the SMA of volume over N bars (current bar included).
Long: Score >= buy threshold
