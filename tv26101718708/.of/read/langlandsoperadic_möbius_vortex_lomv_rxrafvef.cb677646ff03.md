Calculation: Higher-timeframe series = close, RSI, SMA of close, and ATR calculated on higher-timeframe bars; on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and carry its value forward. Calculate any SMA of higher-timeframe ATR on the resulting chart-timeframe series.
Calculation: Trend direction = sign(close − SMA of close); calculate separately for chart and higher timeframes. Before the initial warmup ends, both trend directions are zero.
Calculation: Order-flow polarity = T3 smoothing of the rolling sum of volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low + epsilon) − 1), divided by the rolling sum of volume; use zero when that sum is zero. T3 is the weighted sum of the third through sixth successive EMAs: (1 + 3v + 3v² + v³)E3 − (3v + 6v² + 3v³)E4 + (3v² + 3v³)E5 − v³E6, where v is the smoothing factor.
Calculation: Flow shift = order-flow polarity on the previous bar is strictly above both its current and two-bars-ago values, or strictly below both.
Calculation: Strategy signals = in this order, normalized RSI (RSI − midpoint) / midpoint; negative deviation of close from its SMA divided by a parameter times its standard deviation; order-flow polarity multiplied by a shift factor when a flow shift occurs; normalized ADX (ADX − reference level) / reference level; normalized higher-timeframe RSI. Select the first configurable number of signals. Each confidence is the absolute value of its signal, except flow confidence is the absolute unamplified polarity, multiplied by a confidence factor and capped at one when a flow shift occurs.
Calculation: Agreement direction = positive if the count of selected signals above a positive voting threshold meets the required count; otherwise negative if the count below the negative threshold meets it; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Composite signal = the confidence-weighted mean of selected signals, or zero if total confidence is zero, multiplied by (1 + chart trend direction × mixing weight) and (1 + higher-timeframe trend direction × mixing weight), then clipped to the interval from negative one to positive one.
Calculation: Harmonic component = hyperbolic tangent of the normalized weighted sum of percentage close-to-close returns over the available lookback. For lag n, weight the return from close n bars ago to close n−1 bars ago by the Möbius function of n divided by n raised to a configurable exponent; divide the sum by the sum of absolute weights. Use zero during the initial warmup.
Calculation: Stress component = percentile rank of chart ATR over its lookback, expressed as a fraction; negate it when ATR exceeds a multiplier times its shorter-lookback SMA, and force it negative when higher-timeframe trend direction is negative. Use zero during the initial warmup.
Calculation: Correspondence component = (1 − absolute difference between the harmonic component and the rolling correlation of close with volume) × sign(composite signal); substitute zero for unavailable correlation. Use zero correspondence error during the initial warmup.
Calculation: Score = weighted sum of harmonic, stress, composite, and correspondence components. If positive, multiply by a positive base factor, an additional calm-market factor when the stress comparison is false, an additional flow factor when polarity exceeds a positive threshold, and an additional trend factor when higher-timeframe trend is positive. If negative, multiply by the same base factor, the corresponding stress factor when the stress comparison is true, the flow factor when polarity is below the negative threshold, and the trend factor when higher-timeframe trend is negative. Clip to the interval from negative one to positive one and rescale.
Calculation: Signal validity = chart ATR exceeds a multiplier times its longer-lookback SMA AND higher-timeframe ATR exceeds a multiplier times the SMA of its chart-timeframe series AND either trend direction is nonzero.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = bullish engulfing, with the current open strictly below the prior close and current close strictly above the prior open; OR a bearish candle two bars ago, a small-bodied candle one bar ago, and a bullish current candle closing above the close two bars ago; OR three consecutive bullish candles with strictly increasing closes. Small body means absolute body less than a parameter times that candle's range.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = bearish engulfing, with the current open strictly above the prior close and current close strictly below the prior open; OR a bullish candle two bars ago, a small-bodied candle one bar ago, and a bearish current candle closing below the close two bars ago; OR three consecutive bearish candles with strictly decreasing closes.
Long: Position is flat AND bars since the last entry exceed the cooldown, initialized from the first chart bar AND ((signal validity holds AND score exceeds the long score threshold AND agreement direction is nonnegative) OR (order-flow polarity exceeds the strong-flow threshold AND composite signal exceeds the positive composite threshold) OR (bullish pattern holds AND score exceeds a reduced long score threshold AND order-flow polarity is positive) OR (higher-timeframe trend direction is positive AND flow shift holds AND order-flow polarity is positive) OR (order-flow polarity exceeds the higher extreme-flow threshold AND score is positive)).
Short: Position is flat AND bars since the last entry exceed the cooldown, initialized from the first chart bar AND ((signal validity holds AND score is below the negative short score threshold AND agreement direction is nonpositive) OR (order-flow polarity is below the negative strong-flow threshold AND composite signal is below the negative composite threshold) OR (bearish pattern holds AND score is below the negative reduced short score threshold AND order-flow polarity is negative) OR (higher-timeframe trend direction is negative AND flow shift holds AND order-flow polarity is negative) OR (order-flow polarity is below the negative higher extreme-flow threshold AND score is negative)).
Exit: long, score is below the positive continuation threshold OR signal validity does not hold OR (order-flow polarity is below the negative strong-flow threshold AND its absolute value exceeds its previous-bar absolute value).
Exit: short, score is above the negative continuation threshold OR signal validity does not hold OR (order-flow polarity exceeds the positive strong-flow threshold AND its absolute value exceeds its previous-bar absolute value).
Long: Score is greater than or equal to zero.
Short: Score is below zero.
Long: Composite signal exceeds its positive display threshold OR order-flow polarity exceeds its positive strong-flow threshold OR higher-timeframe trend direction is positive OR agreement direction is positive.
Short: Composite signal is below its negative display threshold OR order-flow polarity is below its negative strong-flow threshold OR higher-timeframe trend direction is negative OR agreement direction is negative.
Long: Chart ATR is positive AND a prior bar within the configured wick lookback has min(open, close) − low greater than a multiplier times absolute candle body.
Short: Chart ATR is positive AND a prior bar within the configured wick lookback has high − max(open, close) greater than a multiplier times absolute candle body.
