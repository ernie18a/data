Calculation: Selected moving average = ALMA, EMA, HMA, linear regression at the current bar, RMA, SMA, VWMA, WMA, or SMA of close + close - close[lag], where lag = (length - 1) / 2; use the same type for both moving averages in a crossover.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low or high = a low, high, or close that is respectively the minimum or maximum among a symmetric, parameterized number of bars before and after it; report it on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR multiplied by a factor when ATR or standard deviation is selected, or close multiplied by a fixed percentage when fixed mode is selected.
Calculation: Stop price = choose one method and initialize on entry using the selected price source and stop distance: static = source minus distance for long or plus distance for short; trailing = running maximum of source minus distance for long or running minimum of source plus distance for short; fast trailing = initial source minus or plus distance, then on a higher high for long add the positive part of the change in OHLC average, or on a lower low for short add the negative part; volatility = track the highest source since long entry or lowest source since short entry and ratchet that extreme minus or plus current distance in the favorable direction; chandelier = track the highest high since long entry or lowest low since short entry and ratchet that extreme minus or plus current distance in the favorable direction; activator = initialize at source minus or plus distance, then add sign(close minus previous stop) multiplied by entry distance divided by the distance multiplier and distance lookback, multiplied by bars since entry; moving average = initialize at source minus or plus distance, then add sign(previous close minus previous stop) multiplied by the absolute change in the selected moving average of close; SAR = initialize the extreme at entry high for long or entry low for short, the acceleration at its starting parameter, and the stop at the lesser of the previous low and entry high for long or greater of the previous high and entry low for short; thereafter advance the stop by acceleration times the difference between the extreme and stop, increase acceleration up to its maximum when a new favorable extreme occurs, and update the extreme; cap the stop at the low two bars ago for long or floor it at the high two bars ago for short. If the entry stop lies strictly inside the entry bar, set it to the entry bar low for long or high for short. Evaluate exits against the stop calculated on the previous bar.
Calculation: Take-profit price = entry price plus the risk-reward multiplier times entry price minus initial stop for long, or entry price minus the multiplier times initial stop minus entry price for short; alternatively use entry price plus or minus the entry-bar take-profit distance, calculated as ATR times its multiplier or close times a fixed percentage. The standard-deviation selection also uses ATR.
Long: A newly confirmed pivot low in low or close AND position is flat AND the position was not long a parameterized number of bars ago.
Short: A newly confirmed pivot high in high or close AND position is flat AND the position was not short a parameterized number of bars ago.
Long: The first selected moving average of close crosses below the second selected moving average of close with an independently parameterized lookback.
Short: The first selected moving average of close crosses above the second selected moving average of close with an independently parameterized lookback.
Long: MACD line crosses above its EMA signal line.
Short: MACD line crosses below its EMA signal line.
Long: RSI crosses above an oversold threshold.
Short: RSI crosses below an overbought threshold.
Exit: long, low <= previous stop price OR high >= take-profit price OR an enabled opposite short entry occurs.
Exit: short, high >= previous stop price OR low <= take-profit price OR an enabled opposite long entry occurs.
