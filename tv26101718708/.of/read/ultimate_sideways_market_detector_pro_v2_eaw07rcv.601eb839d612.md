Calculation: Resistance = the high of the most recent confirmed pivot high; a pivot high is at least as high as each of the preceding pivot-lookback highs and higher than each of the following pivot-lookback highs. It becomes available after those following bars complete.
Calculation: Support = the low of the most recent confirmed pivot low; a pivot low is at most as low as each of the preceding pivot-lookback lows and lower than each of the following pivot-lookback lows. It becomes available after those following bars complete.
Calculation: Range high = the highest high over the range lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Range low = the lowest low over the range lookback, including the current bar.
Long: Low < support AND close > support
Short: High > resistance AND close < resistance
Long: Close > range high AND close > previous bar's high AND close - range high >= ATR multiplied by the breakout-distance parameter
Short: Close < range low AND close < previous bar's low AND range low - close >= ATR multiplied by the breakout-distance parameter
