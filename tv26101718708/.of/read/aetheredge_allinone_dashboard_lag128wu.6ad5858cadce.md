Calculation: Trend score = 0.4 × EMA alignment score + 0.3 × close-versus-EMA score + 0.3 × ADX directional score; EMA periods are short, medium, and long. EMA alignment is +100 when fast > medium > slow, −100 when fast < medium < slow, otherwise 33 × sign(fast−medium) + 33 × sign(medium−slow). Close-versus-EMA score is +100 when close is above all three, −100 when below all three, otherwise +50 when above the medium EMA, −50 when below it, or 0. ADX directional score is min(100, ADX / strong-ADX threshold × 50), positive when +DI > −DI and negative otherwise.
Calculation: Momentum score = 0.35 × RSI score + 0.4 × MACD score + 0.25 × stochastic score. RSI score = 2 × (RSI − 50); stochastic score = 2 × (%K − 50). MACD score uses the MACD histogram scaled by price (histogram / close × 10,000, then ×20 and clipped to [−100, 100]); multiply by +1 when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, by −1 when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0, or otherwise by 0.25 with the histogram's sign.
Calculation: Volatility contribution = 30 when Bollinger Band width is below its rolling lookback percentile 20; otherwise −20 when ATR-percentile > 80, +10 when > 40, or +20. ATR-percentile and Bollinger Band width percentile each locate the current value within its rolling lookback minimum-to-maximum range.
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(0.35 × OBV score + 0.3 × CMF score + 0.35 × MFI score, −100, 100). OBV score is +50 when OBV > its EMA and −50 otherwise; CMF score = CMF × 200; MFI score = 2 × (MFI − 50).
Calculation: Raw composite = weighted average of enabled trend, momentum, volatility contribution, and volume scores, using their respective configurable module weights. Bull probability = clamp(50 + raw composite / 2, 0, 100); bear probability = 100 − bull probability. Alignment = max(0, 100 − population standard deviation of enabled trend, momentum, and volume scores). Signal probability = max(bull probability, bear probability) × (0.7 × alignment / 100 + 0.3).
Long: Raw composite crosses above zero AND signal probability ≥ minimum signal probability.
Short: Raw composite crosses below zero AND signal probability ≥ minimum signal probability.
