Calculation: Session ATR = ATR with a shorter lookback during the opening, morning and closing sessions, a longer lookback during premarket, London and Asia, and an intermediate lookback otherwise.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Trend threshold = the lesser of a cap and a base threshold multiplied by ATR divided by its longer-lookback SMA.
Calculation: SuperTrend = use (high + low + 2 × close) / 4 as source; initialize the trend upward. Set the lower band to source minus an ATR multiple, retaining the greater of this value and its previous value when the previous close was above the previous lower band. Set the upper band to source plus an ATR multiple, retaining the lesser of this value and its previous value when the previous close was below the previous upper band. Switch an upward trend downward when close is below the previous lower band; switch a downward trend upward when close is above the previous upper band.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe score = weighted sum of votes from three progressively higher timeframes, a still higher timeframe and the highest timeframe, all using last completed bars. For each of the first three, add a positive or negative weight according to close versus slow EMA, a larger positive or negative weight according to fast versus slow EMA, and a positive or negative weight according to close versus the midpoint of the lookback high and low. For the still higher and highest timeframes, use separately scaled and capped fast-minus-slow EMA spreads. Add a negative penalty when the shortest vote is negative and both intermediate votes are positive; add the opposite penalty when the shortest vote is positive and both intermediate votes are negative.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low that is respectively the highest or lowest over the specified number of bars on each side; recognize it only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Pivot size threshold = session ATR multiplied by a bounded sum of a base and adjustments for session, efficiency-ratio regime, ADX, absolute higher-timeframe score, ATR relative to its SMA, and volume relative to its SMA.
Long: SuperTrend direction = upward
Short: SuperTrend direction = downward
Long: volume / SMA(volume) >= minimum relative volume AND ATR >= minimum ATR AND efficiency ratio > trend threshold AND close > close at the efficiency-ratio lookback
Short: volume / SMA(volume) >= minimum relative volume AND ATR >= minimum ATR AND efficiency ratio > trend threshold AND close < close at the efficiency-ratio lookback
Long: higher-timeframe score >= positive threshold
Short: higher-timeframe score <= negative threshold
Long: a newly confirmed pivot low > the preceding pivot low AND their difference >= pivot size threshold AND the preceding pivot-low classification was not higher AND the latest confirmed pivot high > the preceding pivot high
Short: a newly confirmed pivot low <= the preceding pivot low AND their absolute difference >= pivot size threshold AND the preceding pivot-low classification was higher AND the latest confirmed pivot high < the preceding pivot high
Long: a newly confirmed pivot high > the preceding pivot high AND their difference >= pivot size threshold AND the preceding pivot-high classification was not higher AND the latest confirmed pivot low > the preceding pivot low
Short: a newly confirmed pivot high <= the preceding pivot high AND their absolute difference >= pivot size threshold AND the preceding pivot-high classification was higher AND the latest confirmed pivot low < the preceding pivot low
