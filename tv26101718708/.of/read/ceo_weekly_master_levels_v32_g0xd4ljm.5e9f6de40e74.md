Calculation: Weekly high and low = the high and low of the first regular-session trading day of the week; while that higher-timeframe day is forming, use its high and low so far, then use its last completed bar for the rest of the week. Weekly midpoint = (weekly high + weekly low) / 2.
Calculation: Cross above = current first value > current second value AND previous first value <= previous second value; cross below = current first value < current second value AND previous first value >= previous second value.
Calculation: Touch event for a level = the bar's high >= level minus a configurable fraction of the level AND its low <= level plus that fraction AND the corresponding touch is not active on the previous bar. A touch becomes active on the event and becomes inactive when the bar no longer intersects a wider, configurable release band around that level. Maintain separate touch states for each reaction and flip path; update them only during the regular session and reset them when their family is disabled or at the first regular-session bar of a new week.
Calculation: Reaction setup = a touch event of the weekly low for longs or weekly high for shorts, fresh while elapsed time since the touch <= a configurable validity period, and unconsumed until an accepted reaction signal uses it. A new touch resets its consumption and midpoint-void state. For a fresh setup, on regular-session bars after the touch, set the long midpoint void when close >= weekly midpoint and clear it when close < weekly midpoint; set the short midpoint void when close <= weekly midpoint and clear it when close > weekly midpoint. Evaluate a signal using the void state before the current bar's midpoint update. Reset reaction setups when reactions are disabled or at the first regular-session bar of a new week.
Calculation: Upward flip = a configurable number of consecutive regular-session closes > weekly high; downward flip = a configurable number of consecutive regular-session closes < weekly low. Use a larger confirmation count on lower chart timeframes than on higher chart timeframes. Reset the upward flip and its touch, confirmation time, and consumption states when a regular-session close <= weekly high; reset the downward flip and its corresponding states when close >= weekly low. Reset both when flips are disabled or at the first regular-session bar of a new week. A flip confirmation is fresh while elapsed time since first confirmation <= the validity period; its momentum signal can be accepted once per flip.
Calculation: Flip pullback setup = after the flip was already confirmed on the previous bar, a touch event of the weekly high with close > weekly high for an upward flip, or of the weekly low with close < weekly low for a downward flip. Its release-band intersection also requires close to remain on that same side of the level. The setup is fresh while elapsed time since its most recent touch <= the validity period and can be accepted once per touch.
Calculation: Family eligibility = the family is enabled AND either the current day is not the first trading day of the week OR that family is allowed on the first trading day. When a family is ineligible, discard its state. For each direction, accept at most one candidate per bar in priority order: flip momentum, flip pullback, reaction. Acceptance consumes only the selected candidate; a blocked candidate is not consumed. Each direction has a separate cooldown measured from its last accepted signal across all families; do not reset cooldown at the weekly reset.
Long: Shorter-period EMA of close crosses above longer-period EMA of close.
Short: Shorter-period EMA of close crosses below longer-period EMA of close.
Long: Regular-session bar AND reaction family eligible AND long reaction setup fresh and unconsumed AND elapsed time since the last accepted long >= configurable cooldown AND (close crosses above SMA of close AND close > weekly low OR long midpoint void was clear before the current bar AND close > SMA of close AND (close crosses above weekly low OR close > weekly low AND close > open)).
Short: Regular-session bar AND reaction family eligible AND short reaction setup fresh and unconsumed AND elapsed time since the last accepted short >= configurable cooldown AND (close crosses below SMA of close AND close < weekly high OR short midpoint void was clear before the current bar AND close < SMA of close AND (close crosses below weekly high OR close < weekly high AND close < open)).
Long: Regular-session bar AND flip family eligible AND upward flip active AND upward flip pullback setup fresh and unconsumed AND elapsed time since the last accepted long >= configurable cooldown AND close > SMA of close AND close > weekly high AND close > open.
Short: Regular-session bar AND flip family eligible AND downward flip active AND downward flip pullback setup fresh and unconsumed AND elapsed time since the last accepted short >= configurable cooldown AND close < SMA of close AND close < weekly low AND close < open.
Long: Regular-session bar AND flip family eligible AND upward flip active AND upward flip confirmation fresh and momentum unconsumed AND elapsed time since the last accepted long >= configurable cooldown AND close > SMA of close AND close > weekly high AND close > open AND close > previous close.
Short: Regular-session bar AND flip family eligible AND downward flip active AND downward flip confirmation fresh and momentum unconsumed AND elapsed time since the last accepted short >= configurable cooldown AND close < SMA of close AND close < weekly low AND close < open AND close < previous close.
