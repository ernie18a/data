Calculation: Current weekly high/low = first weekly trading day's RTH high/low, developing from chart bars during that day; thereafter use the last completed daily RTH bar's finalized first-day high/low until the next week. Midpoint = average of current weekly high and low.
Calculation: EMA bull/bear cross = shorter-lookback chart-timeframe EMA crosses above/below longer-lookback chart-timeframe EMA.
Calculation: SMA = chart-timeframe simple moving average with an unspecified lookback.
Calculation: Level touch = a bar's high-low range intersects the level's tolerance band. A new touch is counted only after price has left the wider release band; a touch is fresh for a parameterized time window. Signals require RTH bars. Suppress a same-direction signal within a parameterized cooldown after the prior accepted signal. On a bar with multiple candidates, prefer momentum, then flip pullback, then reaction.
Calculation: Reactions comprise ML and MH interactions; flips comprise confirmed level breaks and subsequent pullbacks or momentum follow-through. Reaction and flip families have separate enable and first-weekly-day gates.
Calculation: A level flip is confirmed after the required consecutive RTH closes beyond the level (more closes on chart timeframes of five minutes or less, fewer on longer chart timeframes); it is invalidated by a close back across the level. Flip pullbacks require a fresh touch of the flipped level, with the close defending the level.
Calculation: MMR void = after a fresh ML touch, a close at or above the weekly midpoint temporarily blocks long continuation until a close below the midpoint; after a fresh MH touch, a close at or below the midpoint temporarily blocks short continuation until a close above it. A new touch resets the void.
Long: EMA bull cross OR an enabled, fresh ML reaction not already used, with (close crosses above SMA and close > current weekly low OR close > SMA and ((close crosses above current weekly low) OR (new current weekly-low touch AND close > current weekly low AND close > open) OR (fresh current weekly-low touch AND close > current weekly low AND close > open))), where the latter continuation alternatives require no MMR void OR an enabled, confirmed current-week-high flip with close > SMA and ((new pullback touch AND close > current weekly high AND close > open) OR (fresh pullback touch AND close > current weekly high AND close > open) OR (close > open AND close > prior close AND close > current weekly high)). Apply RTH, family and first-weekly-day gates, freshness, unused-setup, cooldown, and candidate-priority filters.

Apply RTH, family and first-weekly-day gates, freshness, unused-setup, cooldown, and candidate-priority filters.
