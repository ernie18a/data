Calculation: Structure state = Initially neutral. Confirm swing highs and lows using equal configurable numbers of bars on each side. On confirmation, replace the corresponding latest swing level and mark it unbroken. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest unbroken swing high; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest unbroken swing low. Mark the broken level as broken and set the state bullish or bearish respectively; if both breaks occur on the same bar, bearish takes precedence. Crosses compare close and the applicable swing level on both the current and previous bars.
Calculation: Momentum = Linear regression endpoint of close minus the average of (1) the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low and (2) the SMA of close, using the same lookback for all three calculations.
Calculation: Oversold count = Number of these conditions satisfied: Bollinger %b <= its oversold threshold; RSI of close <= its oversold threshold; SMA-smoothed stochastic %K <= its oversold threshold. Bollinger %b is 0.5 when the band width is zero.
Long: Position is flat after processing exits AND close > slow EMA of close AND fast EMA of close > slow EMA of close AND slow EMA of close > its value at a configurable earlier bar AND structure state = bullish AND momentum > 0 AND momentum > previous momentum AND oversold count changed from >= required count to < required count on the current bar or within a configurable number of preceding bars AND (RSI confirmation disabled OR RSI of close > confirmation threshold AND RSI of close > previous RSI of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > minimum threshold AND +DI > -DI) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume is unavailable OR volume > SMA of volume multiplied by a configurable factor). Enter at close; the RSI used for confirmation is the same RSI used in the oversold count.
Exit: long, low <= entry price multiplied by (1 - stop-loss fraction).
Exit: long, low > entry price multiplied by (1 - stop-loss fraction) AND high >= entry price multiplied by (1 + take-profit fraction).
