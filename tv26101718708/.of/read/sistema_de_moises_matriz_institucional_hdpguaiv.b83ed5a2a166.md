Calculation: Timeframe sampling = For each configured analysis timeframe, use its last completed bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe; when it is lower, use its last available intrabar within the chart bar. The macro timeframe is longer than the intermediate timeframe, which is longer than the intraday timeframe.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = A high or low whose neighboring bars on both sides satisfy the pivot-high or pivot-low test over the same lookback. Use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish impulse = On the analysis timeframe, the candle closes respectively above or below its open, its absolute body divided by its high-low range is at least a threshold, its range is at least an ATR multiple, and its close is respectively above the previous high or below the previous low.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish fair value gap = On the analysis timeframe, the low is above the high two bars earlier or the high is below the low two bars earlier, respectively, and the gap is greater than an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish structure break = Close above the latest confirmed pivot high or below the latest confirmed pivot low, respectively.
Calculation: Recent event = The event occurred on the current intraday bar or within a configurable number of preceding intraday bars.
Calculation: Low or high sweep = On the intraday timeframe, low is below and close is above a level, or high is above and close is below a level, respectively. The eligible levels are the latest confirmed pivot and the previous completed day's and week's corresponding extremes, calculated from intraday bars.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish imbalance = A bullish or bearish impulse or fair value gap, respectively, occurred recently.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish exploration = A current bullish or bearish structure break or impulse, respectively, AND the corresponding imbalance is recent AND the opposite-side sweep is not recent.
Calculation: Layer directional points = For each direction and analysis timeframe, add configurable weights for close on that side of the short EMA, short EMA on that side of the long EMA, a structure break, an impulse, and a fair value gap. On the macro and intermediate timeframes also add a weight when the short EMA's ATR-normalized change over a lookback has the corresponding sign, using a larger weight when its magnitude exceeds a threshold. On the intraday timeframe also add a weight for corresponding exploration. The close/EMA, EMA/EMA, and structure-break weights are equal; the impulse weight is half as large on the macro and intermediate timeframes and equal on the intraday timeframe; the fair-value-gap and exploration weights are half as large. The larger slope weight equals the close/EMA weight and the smaller slope weight is half as large.
Calculation: Technical directional score = Sum weighted directional conditions across the macro, intermediate, and intraday timeframes: close versus short EMA, short versus long EMA, structure break, and fair value gap on each; signed ATR-normalized short-EMA change on macro and intermediate; impulse on intermediate and intraday; and exploration on intraday. Add weights for a recent same-direction sweep, a recent same-direction intent occurring on or after that sweep, an available target beyond close in the direction, the current day's directional opening bias, and the current day sweeping the opposite previous-day extreme. Subtract a weight for recent opposite imbalance, then clamp each directional score to its allowed range. Intent is impulse, structure break, or fair value gap. An upper or lower target exists when close is below a latest pivot high, previous-day high, or previous-week high, or above the corresponding low. Bullish opening bias requires the current day to open above the previous day's midpoint and its running high not to exceed the previous day's high; bearish bias reverses these comparisons using the running low. Normalize the two scores by their sum with a positive minimum denominator and round each resulting percentage.
Long: Any analysis layer's bullish directional points exceed its bearish directional points by at least a configurable margin.
Short: Any analysis layer's bearish directional points exceed its bullish directional points by at least a configurable margin.
Long: Bullish technical percentage > bearish technical percentage.
Short: Bearish technical percentage > bullish technical percentage.
Long: Bullish imbalance is recent AND bearish imbalance is not recent.
Short: Bearish imbalance is recent AND bullish imbalance is not recent.
Long: A low sweep is recent AND a high sweep is not recent.
Short: A high sweep is recent AND a low sweep is not recent.
Long: Intraday close's normalized position between the lowest low and highest high over a lookback is at or below the discount threshold.
Short: Intraday close's normalized position between the lowest low and highest high over a lookback is at or above the premium threshold.
