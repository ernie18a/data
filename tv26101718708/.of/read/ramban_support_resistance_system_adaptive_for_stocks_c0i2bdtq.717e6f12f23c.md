Calculation: Pivot levels = Maintain separate chronological lists of confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows from last-completed higher-timeframe bars. A pivot has configurable left and right confirmation lengths. With gaps filled, process a pivot only when its higher-timeframe pivot result changes from unavailable on the preceding chart bar to available on the current chart bar. Process highs before lows. Add a pivot only if its distance from every retained level in both lists is at least the pivot price multiplied by a configurable minimum-distance percentage. After each addition, remove the oldest level from that list if its configurable capacity is exceeded.
Calculation: Support-wick condition = Select the pivot-low level nearest to the chart bar's low among levels within the low multiplied by a configurable tolerance percentage; if none qualifies, select the nearest qualifying pivot-high level. The condition is true if a level was selected AND the low is at or below that level plus the level multiplied by the tolerance percentage.
Calculation: Resistance-wick condition = Select the pivot-high level nearest to the chart bar's high among levels within the high multiplied by the same tolerance percentage; if none qualifies, select the nearest qualifying pivot-low level. The condition is true if a level was selected AND the high is at or above that level minus the level multiplied by the tolerance percentage.
Long: Support-wick condition is true.
Short: Resistance-wick condition is true.
Long: On a completed chart bar, signals are enabled AND the bar is within the configured session when session filtering is enabled AND the support-wick condition is true AND the tracked position is flat after processing exits; enter at the bar's close.
Exit: Long, session filtering is enabled AND the current bar is outside the configured session AND the preceding bar was inside it.
Exit: Long, on a completed chart bar, low is at or below entry price multiplied by one minus a configurable stop-loss percentage.
Exit: Long, on a completed chart bar, low is above the stop-loss price AND signals are enabled AND the bar is within the configured session when session filtering is enabled AND the resistance-wick condition is true. After either completed-bar exit, evaluate the entry condition again on the same bar.
