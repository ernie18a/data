Calculation: Common operators = All calculations use chart bars unless stated otherwise. Lag [n] means n chart bars earlier. Clamp(x,a,b) limits x to [a,b]. Normalize(x,n) = (x − lowest(x,n))/(highest(x,n) − lowest(x,n)), or 0.5 when the denominator is zero. Z(x,n) = (x − SMA(x,n))/standard_deviation(x,n), or zero when the deviation is zero. Percent(x) = clamp(50 + 5x, 0, 100). OBV is cumulative signed volume, signed by the change in close. Treat the minimum price increment as a parameter wherever a denominator or price is bounded below by it. Cross above means current value is greater and previous value was less than or equal; cross below is the converse.
Calculation: Higher- or lower-timeframe mapping = The three strength timeframes are ordered from shortest to longest and are configurable relative to the chart timeframe. Calculate their strength and quality on their own bars, lag each result by one of their own bars, and map it to chart bars with gaps filled and historical lookahead off. Thus, for a higher timeframe, use the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar as exposed at the end of the current higher-timeframe bar and carry the last exposed value between updates; for a lower timeframe, use the last available lower-timeframe result within each chart bar. The block timeframe is also configurable as higher or lower than the chart timeframe: map its OHLCV with gaps filled and historical lookahead off, using the last completed higher-timeframe value or the last available lower-timeframe value, respectively. Perform subsequent block calculations on chart bars.
Calculation: Organ score = Clamp(EMA(2Z(EMA(close, trend length) − its previous value) + 1.5Z(ROC(close, momentum length)) + Z(change in OBV) + 0.5Z(change in ATR multiplied by 1 if close is above a shorter price EMA, otherwise −1), organ smoothing length) × 10/6, −10, 10). All Z terms use the same lookback.
Calculation: Band score = Let midpoint be EMA(hl2, band smoothing length), and upper and lower be EMAs of hl2 multiplied by 1 plus and minus, respectively, the product of a band-width parameter and a mirror parameter. Clamp(10 × (close − midpoint)/(upper − lower), −10, 10), bounding the denominator below by the minimum price increment.
Calculation: PI energy score = Compute raw energy as π × ATR² × signed square root of OBV ROC. Normalize raw energy over its normalization lookback, multiply by 100, and smooth with an EMA. Clamp((smoothed energy − its longer EMA)/5, −10, 10).
Calculation: Flow score = For each chart bar, sum volume over the merge lookback and sign it by close versus the open at the start of that lookback; also sign that volume by close versus previous close. Average the normalized values of the EMA of the first signed volume, the EMA of its cumulative sum, and the EMA of the cumulative second signed volume, each normalized over the same lookback. Call the result fusion. Clamp(20 × (fusion − 0.5) + 2 if fusion exceeds its EMA, otherwise −2, plus 2 if fusion crosses above the previous bar's rolling fusion high, otherwise minus 2 if it crosses below the previous bar's rolling fusion low, −10, 10).
Calculation: Power-zone score = Within a rolling high–low range, define the zone between its lower and upper Fibonacci retracement fractions. A bullish candle scores 3 if bullish with lower wick greater than twice its body, otherwise 2 if bullish with a larger body than the preceding candle, otherwise 0; a bearish candle is defined symmetrically using the upper wick. Inside the zone, take the larger candle score plus relative volume capped at 5, capped at 10, positive for bullish and negative for bearish; otherwise return zero. Relative volume is volume divided by its SMA, with denominator at least 1.
Calculation: Harmonic score = Maintain the five latest confirmed symmetric pivot highs or lows, choosing the high if both occur together. Let their prices be X,A,B,C,D and their absolute consecutive distances be XA, AB, BC and CD. Score one point for each: AB/XA near any specified retracement target; BC/AB within its specified interval; CD/BC within its specified interval; and absolute(D−X)/XA within its specified interval. Expand each target or interval by the same tolerance and bound each distance denominator below by a small positive constant. If at least two tests pass, return twice the count, positive when D is a pivot low and negative when D is a pivot high; otherwise return zero.
Calculation: Force score = Clamp(10 × ((close − rolling low)/(rolling high − rolling low) − (rolling high − close)/(rolling high − rolling low)), −10, 10), bounding the range below by the minimum price increment.
Calculation: RSI-band score = Return 6 when RSI exceeds its SMA plus its standard deviation, −6 when below its SMA minus its standard deviation, otherwise clamp((RSI − 50)/8, −10, 10). The SMA and deviation use the same lookback.
Calculation: Quality = Clamp(50 + clamp(price-versus-long-EMA contribution + short-EMA-versus-medium-EMA contribution + (RSI − 50)/5 + MACD-histogram contribution + momentum/ATR + 8 × wick imbalance + volume-candle contribution, −50, 50), 0, 100). The first contribution is 10, −10 or 0 according as close is above, below or equal to the long EMA; the second is 8, −8 or 0; the MACD contribution is 7, −7 or 0 according to histogram sign. Momentum is close minus close at the quality lookback, divided by ATR, or zero if ATR is zero. Wick imbalance is (lower wick − upper wick)/(high − low), with denominator bounded below by the minimum price increment. The volume-candle contribution is 5 for a bullish candle with volume above its quality-lookback SMA, −5 for a bearish candle with such volume, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Range power = Compute quality on each of the three strength timeframes using the mapping above. A bullish cascade requires all three values above a bullish threshold; a bearish cascade requires all three below a bearish threshold. Bullish and bearish persistence are the percentages of chart bars in a persistence lookback whose quality exceeds the bullish threshold or falls below the bearish threshold, respectively. Multitimeframe quality is 80 + 0.2 × bullish persistence for a bullish cascade, 20 − 0.2 × bearish persistence for a bearish cascade, otherwise 50 + 0.25 × (bullish persistence − bearish persistence). Range power is clamp(0.4 × chart quality + 0.6 × multitimeframe quality, 0, 100). A strong bullish or bearish range additionally requires range power above its bullish threshold or below its bearish threshold, respectively, and the corresponding cascade.
Calculation: Vertex score = Clamp to [−10,10] the sum of five terms: ATR minus ATR at the vertex lookback, close change, OBV minus its vertex-lookback EMA, close ROC, and short price EMA minus longer price EMA. Divide each term by the vertex-lookback SMA of its absolute value, using zero when that SMA is zero.
Calculation: Block direction = From mapped block OHLC, take square roots after bounding each price below by the minimum price increment. Compute true range from square-root high, low and the preceding chart bar's mapped square-root close, and smooth with an EMA. Relative volume is mapped volume divided by its chart-bar SMA, or 1 if the SMA is zero. Square-root delta is 100 × (square-root close − square-root open)/square-root open, or zero if the denominator is zero. Add weighted relative volume, square-root delta and chart-close ROC; divide by smoothed square-root true range when nonzero, smooth with an EMA, normalize over a chart-bar lookback and multiply by 100. A block buy occurs when this flow crosses above its EMA while below a buy-zone threshold. A block top occurs when flow exceeds a top-zone threshold, is below its preceding value, and that preceding value is at least its value two bars ago. Persist direction as +1 after a block buy and −1 after a block top, initially zero; a simultaneous top takes precedence.
Calculation: Timeframe strength = On each strength timeframe independently, compute organ score, band score, short-versus-long price EMA direction, RSI, and PI energy as above. Convert organ score to 50 + 5 × organ score and band score to 50 + 5 × band score, clamping both to [0,100]. Convert EMA direction to 65, 35 or 50 for above, below or equal. Convert PI energy to 65 plus its positive distance above its longer EMA capped at 25, or 35 minus its nonnegative distance below that EMA capped at 25. Strength is the weighted sum of these five components. Map the previous own-timeframe strength to chart bars as described above and combine the three strengths with their respective weights.
Calculation: Unified power = Form a weighted average of organ score, band score, clamp((RSI−50)/5,−10,10), short-versus-long EMA direction scored +4/−4/0, PI energy score, flow score, power-zone score, harmonic score, force score and RSI-band score; convert it using Percent. Combine this result, mapped timeframe strength, range power, Percent(vertex score), and Percent(harmonic score) with their respective weights. If the vertex option is disabled, replace its contribution with neutral 50. Clamp the result to [0,100]. If the block option is enabled, combine it with neutral 50 using their respective weights, add a positive or negative block-direction bonus, and clamp to [0,100]; otherwise use the unclipped combination before the block adjustment. Block direction still affects the rail calculation when the block option is disabled.
Calculation: Expanding rails = Directional power is unified power when it is at least 50, otherwise 100 minus unified power. Increase the ATR multiplier by its high-impulse factor when directional power exceeds its high threshold, block direction is nonzero, or either strong range condition holds; otherwise decrease it by its low-impulse factor when directional power is below its low threshold; otherwise leave it unchanged. For each of two linear regressions of hl2, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback, set bullish and bearish bases to regression minus and plus ATR times the adjusted multiplier. Initialize direction bullish if close is at least the regression, otherwise bearish, and initialize the rail to the corresponding base. On each completed bar, if bearish and close exceeds the previous rail, switch bullish and set the rail to the bullish base; if bullish and close is below the previous rail, switch bearish and set the rail to the bearish base. Otherwise, in bullish direction set the rail to the greater of the previous rail and bullish base; in bearish direction set it to the lesser of the previous rail and bearish base. If the previous rail is unavailable, use the applicable current base.
Long: Fast rail >= slow rail
Short: Fast rail < slow rail
Long: Fast rail >= slow rail AND close < fast rail
Short: Fast rail < slow rail AND close > fast rail
Long: Fast rail >= slow rail AND previous close < previous fast rail AND close > fast rail
Short: Fast rail < slow rail AND previous close > previous fast rail AND close < fast rail
Long: Vertex score >= bullish display threshold
Short: Vertex score <= bearish display threshold
