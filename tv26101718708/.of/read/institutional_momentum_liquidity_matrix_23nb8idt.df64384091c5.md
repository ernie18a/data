Calculation: Reverse RSI price for a target = using the previous chart bar's close and Wilder RMAs of close gains and losses, let RS = target / (100 - target), U = (RS × loss RMA - gain RMA) × (RSI length - 1), and D = (gain RMA / RS - loss RMA) × (RSI length - 1); return previous close + U if U > 0, otherwise previous close - D if D > 0, otherwise previous close. Replace unavailable previous values with zero.
Calculation: Latest profile POC = for the current configured calendar period or active custom session, or the most recently completed custom session when outside a session, use at most its latest 1,000 chart bars. Divide their high-to-low range into equal price rows. For each chart bar, sum either volume or absolute close-minus-open over its selected most recent completed lower-timeframe bars, and distribute that sum equally among all rows touched by the chart bar's low-to-high range. The POC is the midpoint of the row with the greatest sum, choosing the lowest row on a tie. If the profile high equals its low, the POC is unavailable.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR on the entry chart bar × a stop-distance multiplier; retain this value until exit. The entry price is the relevant reverse RSI price at entry.
Long: Position is flat AND the configured lower timeframe is valid AND chart bar is complete AND low <= reverse RSI price for the oversold target AND (POC proximity filter is disabled OR absolute difference between that reverse RSI price and latest profile POC <= current ATR × a proximity multiplier).
Short: Position is flat AND the long condition is false AND the configured lower timeframe is valid AND chart bar is complete AND high >= reverse RSI price for the overbought target AND (POC proximity filter is disabled OR absolute difference between that reverse RSI price and latest profile POC <= current ATR × a proximity multiplier).
Exit: Long, on a chart bar after entry, low <= entry price - entry risk OR high >= entry price + entry risk × a reward multiplier; evaluate the stop before the target.
Exit: Short, on a chart bar after entry, high >= entry price + entry risk OR low <= entry price - entry risk × a reward multiplier; evaluate the stop before the target.
