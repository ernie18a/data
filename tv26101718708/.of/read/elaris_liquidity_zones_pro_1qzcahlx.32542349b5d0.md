Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low at the center of equal-length preceding and succeeding windows, available on the bar that completes the succeeding window.
Calculation: Equal-level match = the pivot's high or low differs by no more than a tolerance multiple of ATR at the pivot bar from at least one corresponding high or low between the bar before the pivot and the equal-level lookback limit; require more history than that lookback.
Calculation: Zone strength = max(min(abs(confirmation-bar close − pivot price) / max(confirmation-bar ATR, price-increment floor), prominence cap) × prominence weight + freshness score + rejection weight × rejection / max(confirmation-bar ATR, price-increment floor) + optional volume contribution + optional equal-level bonus, strength floor). Rejection is pivot-bar high − close for a high pivot and pivot-bar close − low for a low pivot. If the volume contribution is enabled, it is min(pivot-bar volume / pivot-bar volume SMA, volume cap), or a fallback score when that SMA is not positive. Add the equal-level bonus when equal-level detection is enabled and a match exists.
Calculation: Zone = pivot price ± a padding multiple of pivot-bar ATR. On each bar, process confirmed high pivots before confirmed low pivots. Create a zone when its strength meets the minimum and no non-invalid zone on the same side overlaps it or has a midpoint within a similarity multiple of current ATR. Retain at most the configured number of zones, deleting the oldest on overflow, including invalid zones in the count. A high-pivot zone becomes invalid when close > its top; a low-pivot zone becomes invalid when close < its bottom. Update invalidation after evaluating sweeps on each bar, including the creation bar.
Long: A confirmed low pivot creates a zone.
Short: A confirmed high pivot creates a zone.
Long: A non-invalid low-pivot zone exists AND low < its bottom AND close > its bottom.
Short: A non-invalid high-pivot zone exists AND high > its top AND close < its top.
