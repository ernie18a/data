Calculation: Adaptive range filter = Apply an ATR band to the regime source: multiply ATR by the range multiplier adjusted to 1.15× when the 3-bar EMA of the efficiency ratio is below 0.25, 0.85× when above 0.70, and 1× otherwise; move the filter to source minus the band when source exceeds prior filter plus band, to source plus the band when source is below prior filter minus band, and otherwise leave it unchanged. Efficiency ratio = absolute source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar source changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Locked regime = Direction of the adaptive range filter, changed only after it differs from the prior locked regime for the required consecutive bars; required bars are base confirmation + 2 when smoothed efficiency ratio < 0.22, base + 1 when < 0.42, base when < 0.68, and max(base − 1, 1) otherwise. Update on a confirmed bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = close above the EMA with the same higher-timeframe lookback, both from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Daily pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed daily bar, using higher-timeframe data when daily is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: EMA weave bias = bullish when close > daily pivot, short-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA, and the mean of the short-, medium-, and long-lookback EMAs is above its value two chart bars ago; bearish when all three comparisons are reversed; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Geometry bias = bullish when the latest confirmed swing low is above the preceding confirmed swing low; bearish when the latest confirmed swing high is below the preceding confirmed swing high; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Reversion basis = EMA of close; reversion bands = basis ± standard deviation of close over the same lookback multiplied by a deviation factor.
Calculation: Regime quality = count of: ADX ≥ its floor, Choppiness Index ≤ its ceiling, and higher-timeframe bias equals locked regime. Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Bullish BOS = confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high and absolute close − open ≥ ATR × displacement factor when displacement is required. Bearish BOS = confirmed close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low and absolute close − open ≥ ATR × displacement factor when displacement is required.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high two chart bars ago. Bearish FVG = high < low two chart bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish divergence block = a bearish RSI divergence occurred within the block lookback: a confirmed swing high made a higher high while RSI at that pivot made a lower high than at the preceding confirmed swing high, with the pivots no farther apart than the divergence window. Bearish divergence block = the corresponding bullish divergence: a confirmed swing low made a lower low while RSI at that pivot made a higher low.
Long: position side ≤ 0 AND confirmed bar AND (locked regime = bullish OR close > adaptive range filter OR short-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA) AND (regime quality ≥ 1 OR aggressive entries enabled) AND (higher-timeframe bias = locked regime OR higher-timeframe hard gate disabled) AND at least one of: (bullish BOS AND bullish FVG now or within the prior two chart bars), (continuation entries enabled AND locked regime bullish AND close crosses above adaptive range filter AND close > daily pivot AND close > reversion basis), (aggressive entries enabled AND (locked regime bullish OR close > adaptive range filter) AND close crosses above daily pivot OR reversion basis OR short-lookback EMA) AND (reclaim not required OR low < reversion basis AND close > reversion basis OR bullish rotation OR bullish continuation) AND at least one of: EMA weave bias ≥ neutral, geometry bias ≥ neutral, close > adaptive range filter, close > daily pivot AND (divergence block disabled OR no bullish divergence block).
Short: position side ≥ 0 AND confirmed bar AND (locked regime = bearish OR close < adaptive range filter OR short-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA) AND (regime quality ≥ 1 OR aggressive entries enabled) AND (higher-timeframe bias = locked regime OR higher-timeframe hard gate disabled) AND at least one of: (bearish BOS AND bearish FVG now or within the prior two chart bars), (continuation entries enabled AND locked regime bearish AND close crosses below adaptive range filter AND close < daily pivot AND close < reversion basis), (aggressive entries enabled AND (locked regime bearish OR close < adaptive range filter) AND close crosses below daily pivot OR reversion basis OR short-lookback EMA) AND (reclaim not required OR high > reversion basis AND close < reversion basis OR bearish rotation OR bearish continuation) AND at least one of: EMA weave bias ≤ neutral, geometry bias ≤ neutral, close < adaptive range filter, close < daily pivot AND (divergence block disabled OR no bearish divergence block).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR locked regime = bearish OR EMA weave bias = bearish. Long stop = the lower of the latest confirmed swing low (or close − ATR × stop multiple if unavailable) and close − ATR × stop multiple; after high reaches entry price + live risk × trail activation multiple, use the higher of that stop and close − ATR × trail multiple. Live risk = max(entry price − long stop, minimum tick). Long target = entry price + live risk × reward multiple.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR locked regime = bullish OR EMA weave bias = bullish. Short stop = the higher of the latest confirmed swing high (or close + ATR × stop multiple if unavailable) and close + ATR × stop multiple; after low reaches entry price − live risk × trail activation multiple, use the lower of that stop and close + ATR × trail multiple. Live risk = max(short stop − entry price, minimum tick). Short target = entry price − live risk × reward multiple.
