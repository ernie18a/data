Calculation: Research series = bars aggregated to the research timeframe, which may be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. On intraday chart bars, use the last completed research bar as of the chart bar; on other chart bars, update the requested values at the close of each research bar. For a lower research timeframe, use the last available research bar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Box high, box low, box width, and box close = the highest high, lowest low, their difference, and final close over the selected number of completed research bars, excluding the research bar on which the calculation is evaluated.
Calculation: Box range percentage = box width / box close × 100.
Calculation: Range Z-score = current box range percentage minus the mean of the historical lookback of box range percentages ending one research bar earlier, divided by their population standard deviation.
Calculation: Range percentile = percentile rank of the previous research bar's box range percentage within the historical lookback of box range percentages ending at that previous bar.
Calculation: Drift ratio = absolute difference between the first and last closes in the box / box width.
Calculation: Slope percentage = absolute least-squares slope of research-bar closes in the box × box length / box close × 100.
Calculation: Range variation = population standard deviation / mean of research-bar ranges in the box, where each range is either high minus low or true range, according to the selected range metric.
Calculation: Largest range ratio = largest research-bar range in the box / box width, using the same range metric as range variation.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = either a fraction of research-timeframe ATR from the last completed research bar, a percentage of box close, a fixed price increment, or zero.
Calculation: Signal eligibility = in once-per-setup mode, no prior breakout signal in the current uninterrupted qualified-setup streak; in once-per-side-per-research-period mode, no prior breakout signal on the same side since the start of the current research period or the current qualified-setup streak, whichever is later. Reset eligibility when the setup ceases to qualify or a new qualified-setup streak begins.
Long: Research bar count exceeds the historical lookback plus box length plus research ATR length plus the warm-up allowance AND box width > 0 AND the selected percentile and/or Z-score tightness rule passes its maximum threshold AND (sideways filter is disabled OR drift ratio <= its maximum AND slope percentage <= its maximum) AND (range stability filter is disabled OR range variation <= its maximum AND largest range ratio <= its maximum) AND (minimum chart-bar range percentage filter is disabled OR (high - low) / close × 100 >= its minimum) AND (minimum chart-bar range-to-chart ATR filter is disabled OR (high - low) / chart ATR >= its minimum) AND (maximum chart-bar range-to-chart ATR filter is disabled OR (high - low) / chart ATR <= its maximum) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume / chart-bar volume SMA >= its minimum) AND signal eligibility for long is true AND close >= box high + breakout buffer.
Short: Research bar count exceeds the historical lookback plus box length plus research ATR length plus the warm-up allowance AND box width > 0 AND the selected percentile and/or Z-score tightness rule passes its maximum threshold AND (sideways filter is disabled OR drift ratio <= its maximum AND slope percentage <= its maximum) AND (range stability filter is disabled OR range variation <= its maximum AND largest range ratio <= its maximum) AND (minimum chart-bar range percentage filter is disabled OR (high - low) / close × 100 >= its minimum) AND (minimum chart-bar range-to-chart ATR filter is disabled OR (high - low) / chart ATR >= its minimum) AND (maximum chart-bar range-to-chart ATR filter is disabled OR (high - low) / chart ATR <= its maximum) AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume / chart-bar volume SMA >= its minimum) AND signal eligibility for short is true AND close <= box low - breakout buffer.
