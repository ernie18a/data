Calculation: Moving-average rating = on the chart timeframe or an optionally selected higher or lower timeframe, average the available votes from close-based SMA, EMA, ALMA, Wilder's smoothed moving average, least-squares moving average, VWMA, DEMA, HMA, KAMA, FRAMA, VIDYA, JMA, TEMA, ZLEMA, TRIMA and T3, plus the Ichimoku vote. Use five shared lookbacks ordered from shortest to longest for every average, and one additional intermediate lookback for SMA and EMA. Each available average votes +1 if close is above it, -1 if close is below it, and 0 if equal; exclude unavailable averages. Include the Ichimoku vote only when all its inputs and the previous close are available. On a higher timeframe use the last completed bar in historical evaluation and the forming bar in real time; on a lower timeframe use the last available intrabar in each chart bar.
Calculation: Ichimoku vote = +1 if leading span A > leading span B AND close > leading span A AND close < base line AND previous close < current conversion line AND close > current conversion line; otherwise -1 if leading span B > leading span A AND close < leading span B AND close > base line AND previous close > current conversion line AND close < current conversion line; otherwise 0. Conversion and base lines are midpoints of the highest high and lowest low over shorter and longer lookbacks respectively; leading span A is their midpoint and leading span B is the high-low midpoint over a still longer lookback. Use undisplaced spans.
Calculation: KAMA = previous KAMA plus squared smoothing coefficient times (close minus previous KAMA); the coefficient before squaring interpolates between fast and slow EMA coefficients using absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, with efficiency zero when the denominator is zero. Initialize the previous KAMA to zero.
Calculation: FRAMA = alpha times close plus (1 - alpha) times previous FRAMA; make the lookback even, rounding odd lengths up. Let N3 be the high-low range over that lookback divided by its length, N1 the high-low range over the most recent half-length minus one bars divided by the half-length, and N2 the high-low range over the preceding half-length bars divided by the half-length. Fractal dimension is log(N1 + N2) minus log(N3), divided by log(2), when all three ranges are positive, and zero otherwise. Alpha is exp(-4.6 times (dimension - 1)), bounded between 0.01 and 1. Initialize the previous value to 1 and use close as FRAMA through the first lookback plus one bars.
Calculation: VIDYA = close times alpha times absolute CMO plus previous VIDYA times (1 - alpha times absolute CMO), where alpha is the EMA coefficient for the lookback and CMO is the difference between sums of positive and absolute negative close changes divided by their sum over that lookback. Initialize the previous VIDYA to zero.
Calculation: JMA = recursively compute alpha = beta raised to a power, beta = 0.45 times (length - 1) divided by (0.45 times (length - 1) + 2), and phase ratio = phase / 100 + 1.5 bounded between 0.5 and 2.5. Compute A = (1 - alpha) times close + alpha times previous A; D = (close - A) times (1 - beta) + beta times previous D; B = A + phase ratio times D; E = (B - previous JMA) times (1 - alpha) squared + alpha squared times previous E; JMA = previous JMA + E. Initialize all previous recursive values to zero.
Calculation: ZLEMA = EMA of twice close minus close lagged by integer-truncated (length - 1) / 2.
Calculation: TRIMA = SMA of an SMA of close, both using the rounded half of (length + 1).
Long: Moving-average rating >= long rating threshold
Short: Moving-average rating <= negative short rating threshold
Exit: long, moving-average rating <= negative short rating threshold
Exit: short, moving-average rating >= long rating threshold
