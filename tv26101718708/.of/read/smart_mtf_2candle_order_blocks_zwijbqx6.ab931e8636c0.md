Calculation: Bullish two-candle order block = previous bar closes below its open AND current bar closes above its open AND current close is above previous high.
Calculation: Bearish two-candle order block = previous bar closes above its open AND current bar closes below its open AND current close is below previous low.
Long: Bullish two-candle order block on the chart timeframe OR on a lower timeframe.
Short: Bearish two-candle order block on the chart timeframe OR on a lower timeframe.
