Calculation: Normalize(x, weak, strong) = clamp(100 × (x − weak) / (strong − weak), 0, 100), or 0 if strong = weak. Peak(x, weak, ideal, excessive) = Normalize(x, weak, ideal) when x ≤ ideal; otherwise clamp(100 × (excessive − x) / max(excessive − ideal, epsilon), 0, 100). All thresholds, weights, multipliers, lookbacks, windows, and epsilon are parameters.
Calculation: On each chart bar, use ATR, falling back to the bar's high − low when unavailable and flooring it at epsilon. The reference high and low are the highest high and lowest low over the reference lookback excluding the current bar; the range is ready when both exist and the high exceeds the low. Candle range = max(high − low, epsilon); body share = abs(close − open) / candle range; lower wick share = (min(open, close) − low) / candle range; upper wick share = (high − max(open, close)) / candle range; close location = (close − low) / candle range. Volume ratio = volume / its simple moving average when that average is positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: For a bullish sweep, penetration = max((reference low − low) / ATR, 0), wick share = lower wick share, reference = reference low, extreme = low, and opposite = reference high. For a bearish sweep, penetration = max((high − reference high) / ATR, 0), wick share = upper wick share, reference = reference high, extreme = high, and opposite = reference low. Wick score = Normalize(wick share, wick threshold × wick-weak multiplier, wick-strong threshold); penetration score = Peak(penetration, penetration threshold, ideal penetration, excessive penetration); volume score = Normalize(volume ratio, weak volume ratio, strong volume ratio). Seed score = clamp(weighted sum of wick, penetration, and volume scores, 0, 100), with wick weight greater than penetration weight and penetration weight greater than volume weight.
Calculation: A sweep candidate requires a ready range, penetration ≥ penetration threshold, wick share ≥ wick threshold, and seed score ≥ max(minimum seed score, watch threshold − seed-score margin). Process the bullish candidate first; process the bearish candidate only if the bullish candidate does not qualify. Replace the pending sweep if its side differs, its extreme is unavailable, the candidate has a more extreme low for bullish or high for bearish, or the pending sweep is older than the confirmation window. Store the candidate's bar, side, reference, extreme, opposite, seed score, wick score, penetration score, and volume score. A replaced sweep is a new seed event. A pending sweep remains valid through the confirmation window, including its seed bar.
Calculation: For a valid pending sweep, directional reclaim distance = (close − stored reference) / ATR for bullish or (stored reference − close) / ATR for bearish. Directional close location = close location for bullish or 1 − close location for bearish. Directional follow-through = (close − previous high) / ATR for bullish or (previous low − close) / ATR for bearish. Close-back score = Normalize(directional reclaim distance, weak reclaim, strong reclaim); location score = Normalize(directional close location, close-location threshold − location margin, strong location); body score = Normalize(body share, body threshold × body-weak multiplier, strong body share); follow score = Normalize(directional follow-through, weak follow-through, strong follow-through). Directional body score = strong-body score when close > open for bullish or close < open for bearish, otherwise weak-body score. Confirmation score = clamp(weighted sum of body, follow, directional body, and location scores, 0, 100), with weights decreasing in that order.
Calculation: Target = stored opposite reference if it is above close for bullish or below close for bearish; otherwise close plus or minus an ATR fallback distance in the sweep direction. Target-room score = Normalize(abs(target − close) / ATR, weak target room, strong target room). Trend-turn score = the sum of a higher or lower score according to whether close is above the shorter EMA for bullish or below it for bearish, plus a higher or lower score according to whether the shorter EMA rises for bullish or falls for bearish, plus a higher or lower score according to whether the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA for bullish or below it for bearish, clamped to 0–100. Sweep score = clamp(weighted sum of stored wick and penetration scores, 0, 100), with wick weight greater than penetration weight. Reversal score = clamp(weighted sum of sweep, close-back, confirmation, stored volume, target-room, and trend-turn scores, 0, 100); sweep, close-back, and confirmation weights are equal and greater than target-room weight, which is greater than volume weight, which is greater than trend-turn weight.
Calculation: Confirmation requires a valid pending sweep, reversal score ≥ minimum reversal score, and at least the plan-cooldown number of bars since the previous confirmed plan, if any. Bullish confirmation also requires close ≥ stored reference + ATR × reclaim-pad multiplier, close location ≥ close-location threshold, and body share ≥ body threshold × confirmation-body multiplier. Bearish confirmation requires close ≤ stored reference − ATR × reclaim-pad multiplier, close location ≤ 1 − close-location threshold, and the same body-share condition. On confirmation, store the side, target, and invalidation level equal to the stored extreme minus an ATR buffer for bullish or plus an ATR buffer for bearish; clear the pending sweep. A new confirmed plan replaces any active plan. Otherwise clear the pending sweep when its valid bullish close is below its current-ATR-buffered extreme, its valid bearish close is above its current-ATR-buffered extreme, or its age exceeds the confirmation window.
Long: New bullish seed event AND stored seed score ≥ watch threshold AND bars since the last event label ≥ label cooldown, or no previous event label exists, AND no confirmation occurs on the same bar.
Short: New bearish seed event AND stored seed score ≥ watch threshold AND bars since the last event label ≥ label cooldown, or no previous event label exists, AND no confirmation occurs on the same bar.
Long: Bullish confirmation.
Short: Bearish confirmation.
Calculation: An active confirmed plan remains eligible for an outcome through its forward window, including the confirmation bar. Only its first outcome is recorded; invalidation takes priority if invalidation and target occur on the same bar.
Exit: long, active bullish plan is eligible AND close < stored invalidation level OR active bullish plan is eligible AND high ≥ stored target.
Exit: short, active bearish plan is eligible AND close > stored invalidation level OR active bearish plan is eligible AND low ≤ stored target.
