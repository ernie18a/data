Calculation: V-shape anchors (per selected analysis timeframe A/B/C, whether higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use that timeframe’s current bar, which may still be forming in realtime) = Long side: track the highest high within the lookback as the prior top and the lowest low since that top as the V-bottom; require the top-to-current and bottom-to-current distances to meet the minimum downtrend and uptrend bar counts. Short side: mirror this, tracking the lowest low and then the highest high since it; apply the same bar-count requirements. Between the anchors, measure the greatest high above the straight line joining the long top and bottom, or the greatest low below the short bottom and peak. Update anchors as new qualifying extrema occur.
Calculation: Long/short active-leg rate = steepest qualifying slope from the V-bottom/peak to a later low/high, expressed as percent of the anchor price per day using bars per day for that analysis timeframe. Prior-leg rate = percent-per-day slope from the prior top/bottom, adjusted by the measured greatest deviation from the anchor line. V-Trend = active-leg rate + prior-leg rate.
Calculation: Long/short V-Fib = distance of current high (long) or low (short) from the anchor line projected from the V-bottom/peak toward the prior top/bottom, divided by (greatest deviation from that line / 0.236); use the upward distance for long and downward distance for short.
Calculation: Leading gain (long/short) = distance of current low/high from the line projected from the V-bottom/peak using the active-leg slope, divided by (active-leg slope × bars per day), then multiplied by the active-leg rate.
Calculation: Long readiness per timeframe = weighted average of (V-Fib zone readiness, trend readiness, leading-gain readiness), using configured zone:trend:lead weights. Zone readiness is 0 below the minimum entry V-Fib, ramps linearly to 1 at that minimum, stays 1 through the maximum entry V-Fib, then falls linearly to 0 at the unlock V-Fib. Trend readiness is the smaller of two values: clamp(1 + (active-leg rate − minimum uptrend rate) / minimum uptrend rate, 0, 1), and clamp(1 + (V-Trend − minimum V-Trend) / minimum uptrend rate, 0, 1). Leading-gain readiness = clamp(1 + (leading gain − minimum leading gain) / minimum leading gain, 0, 1). For short, calculate the same readiness after negating the active-leg rate, V-Trend, and leading gain.
Calculation: Long/short score = sum of readiness for slots A, B, and C multiplied by their respective configured weights, normalized by the sum of those weights and multiplied by 3.
Calculation: Opposite-side gate = that side’s score under each of the configured fast and slow A:B:C weight profiles is below its configured maximum.
Long: Long active-leg rate ≥ minimum uptrend rate AND long prior-leg rate ≤ maximum downtrend rate AND long V-Trend ≥ minimum V-Trend AND long V-Fib is between the minimum and maximum entry V-Fib, inclusive AND long leading gain ≥ minimum leading gain AND short opposite-side gate AND the resulting long entry state is true now and false on the prior chart bar.
Short: Short active-leg rate ≤ −minimum uptrend rate AND short prior-leg rate ≥ −maximum downtrend rate AND short V-Trend ≤ −minimum V-Trend AND short V-Fib is between the minimum and maximum entry V-Fib, inclusive AND short leading gain ≤ −minimum leading gain AND long opposite-side gate AND the resulting short entry state is true now and false on the prior chart bar.
