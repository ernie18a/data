Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the specified number of bars on each side that satisfies the pivot-high or pivot-low test; it becomes known only after the right-hand bars close. Use a longer lookback for swing pivots and a shorter lookback for minor pivots.
Calculation: Swing-break state = initially neutral; on a confirmed bar after warm-up, set bullish when close exceeds the latest confirmed swing pivot high that has not already been broken, and set bearish when close falls below the latest confirmed swing pivot low that has not already been broken. Each pivot can be broken once; a newly confirmed pivot resets its side's broken flag.
Calculation: Trend-quality score = a weighted average, clamped to [0,1], of the efficiency ratio of the selected source, normalized volume z-score, absolute distance of close from the midpoint of its recent high-low range, and the fraction of recent close changes agreeing with the net close change. If volume has never been positive, replace the volume component with normalized ATR divided by its longer-term SMA.
Calculation: Regime score = a weighted average of normalized ADX, inverted choppiness index, and the squared correlation of close with bar index, each scaled to a common range.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR = interpolate between ATRs calculated at several increasing lookbacks; select the interpolation length by decreasing it as the regime score rises, multiplying it by the clamped ratio of ATR to its longer-term SMA, and clamping it to the supported lookback range.
Calculation: Trend-core direction = initially long. Form active and passive ATR multipliers from a base multiplier and a nonlinear function of trend-quality score; narrow the active multiplier and widen the passive multiplier as trend quality rises, then exponentially smooth each multiplier. From the selected source, subtract adaptive ATR times the active multiplier for the lower band and add adaptive ATR times the passive multiplier for the upper band when the previous direction is long; exchange the multipliers when it is short. Trail the lower band upward while the previous close is above its previous value, and trail the upper band downward while the previous close is below its previous value; otherwise reset the respective band to its current raw value. Change short to long when close exceeds the previous upper band, and long to short when close falls below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the direction.
Calculation: Binary exposure = a state initially off, switched on after a parameterized number of consecutive closes strictly above the trend SMA and switched off after the same number of consecutive closes at or below it; exposure is one only when the state is on and, if the optional slope filter is enabled, the SMA is above its value a parameterized number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Graded exposure = for the single-mean option, clamp (close minus trend SMA) divided by an ATR-scaled ramp width to [0,1]. For the ensemble option, average the same clamped expression for four SMAs with progressively longer lookbacks, using an ATR ramp width proportional to the square root of each SMA lookback divided by the longest lookback. Treat an unavailable ensemble SMA as contributing zero and a nonpositive or unavailable ATR as zero exposure. Require the trend SMA to be available. If quantization is enabled, initialize held exposure to zero and update it to the nearest quantization step only when raw exposure differs from held exposure by at least the configured fraction of a step; otherwise retain held exposure.
Calculation: Causal kernel envelope = the selected price source averaged over a finite trailing window with Gaussian weights exp(-lag squared divided by twice bandwidth squared); the window length is the smaller of the tap limit and a bandwidth-derived length. The upper band is the kernel mean plus an upper multiplier times the SMA of absolute source-minus-mean error; the lower band is the mean minus a separately parameterized lower multiplier times the same error.
Calculation: Band exposure cap = initialize sold rungs to zero; increment by one, up to the rung count, when the selected source crosses above the upper envelope band, and decrement by one, down to zero, when it crosses below the lower band. Cap equals one minus sold rungs divided by rung count.
Calculation: Effective exposure = choose binary or graded exposure. With the envelope disabled or in dial-only mode, use that exposure; in band-only mode use the band exposure cap; in combined-cut mode use the smaller of exposure and cap. In gated mode, initialize held exposure to zero and set it to the dial exposure when the kernel mean is unavailable or the selected source is at or above the mean; otherwise set it to the larger of its previous value and the dial exposure.
Calculation: Target weight = zero until the trend SMA is available; thereafter the smaller of one and the configured floor plus the remaining fraction multiplied by the risk scale and effective exposure.
Long: Confirmed bar AND internal position is flat AND effective exposure > 0.
Long: Graded exposure mode AND trend SMA is available AND target weight minus its previous value exceeds the fill threshold.
Exit: long, graded exposure mode AND trend SMA is available AND previous target weight minus target weight exceeds the fill threshold.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND effective exposure <= 0.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND target weight <= 0.
Long: Confirmed swing pivot high > previous confirmed swing pivot high AND warm-up is complete.
Short: Confirmed swing pivot high <= previous confirmed swing pivot high AND warm-up is complete.
Long: Confirmed swing pivot low >= previous confirmed swing pivot low AND warm-up is complete.
Short: Confirmed swing pivot low < previous confirmed swing pivot low AND warm-up is complete.
Long: Close > latest confirmed swing pivot high AND that pivot has not already been broken AND warm-up is complete.
Short: Close < latest confirmed swing pivot low AND that pivot has not already been broken AND warm-up is complete.
Long: Trend-core direction = long.
Short: Trend-core direction = short.
Long: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the shorter EMA of close > the longer EMA of close AND close > the longer EMA.
Short: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, the shorter EMA of close < the longer EMA of close AND close < the longer EMA.
Long: Close > close a momentum lookback earlier.
Short: Close < close a momentum lookback earlier.
