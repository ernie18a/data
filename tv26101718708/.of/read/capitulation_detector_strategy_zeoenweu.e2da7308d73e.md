Calculation: Long persistence = bars since close was strictly above the long-term EMA; short persistence = bars since close was strictly below it. The count is unavailable until the respective comparison has occurred; equality does not reset it.
Calculation: Long acceleration count = bars since close was strictly above the short-term EMA; short acceleration count = bars since close was strictly below it. The count is unavailable until the respective comparison has occurred; equality does not reset it.
Calculation: Long leg count = count of successively lower qualifying swing lows. A swing low qualifies when low[0] < low[1], low[0] < low[2], low[1] < low[2], and low[1] < low[3]. On a qualifying swing, increment the count if there is no previous qualifying swing low or its low is lower than the previous qualifying swing low; otherwise set the count to one. Store the current swing low. After processing the swing, reset the count and stored swing low when close crosses above the long-term EMA. Short leg count is the symmetric count of successively higher qualifying swing highs, using the opposite comparisons on highs, and resets when close crosses below the long-term EMA.
Calculation: Long score = the number of true conditions among: long persistence >= its threshold; long acceleration count >= its threshold AND long persistence >= its threshold; volume >= a multiplier times its SMA; absolute candle body >= a multiplier times its SMA; close < long-term EMA AND absolute distance from that EMA / ATR >= an extension threshold; long leg count >= its threshold. Short score uses short persistence, short acceleration count, close > long-term EMA, and short leg count, with the same volume and body conditions.
Long: Long score >= score threshold AND close < open AND close < long-term EMA AND (volume-spike filter disabled OR volume >= its SMA times its multiplier) AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time >= session start AND bar opening time <= session end)
Short: Short score >= score threshold AND close > open AND close > long-term EMA AND (volume-spike filter disabled OR volume >= its SMA times its multiplier) AND (time filter disabled OR bar opening time >= session start AND bar opening time <= session end)
Exit: Long, stop enabled AND price <= entry-signal close - entry-signal ATR times stop multiplier OR target enabled AND price >= entry-signal close + entry-signal ATR times target multiplier
Exit: Short, stop enabled AND price >= entry-signal close + entry-signal ATR times stop multiplier OR target enabled AND price <= entry-signal close - entry-signal ATR times target multiplier
Calculation: Trailing distance = current ATR times trailing multiplier, interpreted as a number of minimum price ticks. A long trailing stop activates when price reaches entry price plus that distance and then follows the highest price reached at that distance; a short trailing stop activates when price reaches entry price minus that distance and then follows the lowest price reached at that distance. Reissue the trailing order using the current distance on each bar while in position.
Exit: Long, trailing stop enabled AND trailing stop activated AND price <= trailing stop
Exit: Short, trailing stop enabled AND trailing stop activated AND price >= trailing stop
