Calculation: Inside-bar setup = A bar is inside when its high and low are within the preceding bar's high and low, its body is within the preceding body, or its high and low are within the preceding body, according to the selected definition; edges are either strictly inside or may be equal. Each inside bar replaces the prior setup, storing its high, low, preceding high, and bar index. Its breakout level is the stored inside-bar high or preceding high. It remains alive through the selected number of subsequent bars until close falls below its stored low or it triggers.
Calculation: Flag setup = On each bar where high is at least the highest high of the pole lookback and high minus the lowest low of that lookback is at least the selected ATR multiple or percentage of that lowest low, store that high, lowest low, bar index, and mean volume over the pole lookback, and reset the flag. On subsequent bars track the minimum low, the maximum high through the preceding bar, and mean completed-bar volume since the pole, treating missing volume as zero. The flag is alive from the next bar through the maximum flag age unless its minimum low falls below the pole high minus the selected fraction of the pole height or it triggers. Its breakout level is the pole high or the maximum flag high through the preceding bar, falling back to the pole high when unavailable. The volume filter passes when disabled, current volume is unavailable, either volume average is unavailable, or mean flag volume is below mean pole volume. Evaluate the breakout before updating the flag high and before replacing the pole on the current bar.
Calculation: Range setup = A window is tight when its highest high minus lowest low is at most the selected ATR multiple or percentage of its lowest low. When tightness changes from false to true, store the window high, low, and current bar index if no range exists or the previous range is done, and if the optional near-high filter is disabled, its reference is unavailable, or the window high is at least the selected fraction of the highest high over a longer lookback. The range remains alive until it triggers, closes below its stored low, or its age plus the tightness lookback exceeds the maximum total length. Evaluate a breakout against the stored high before applying the current bar's invalidation or creating a new range.
Calculation: Trend filter = The filter passes when disabled, the selected close SMA or EMA is unavailable, or close is above it.
Calculation: Setup grade = Count available enabled votes and count those passing: close above the trend moving average; fast close EMA minus slow close EMA above zero; volume above its SMA times a multiplier; close position within the bar's range at or above a threshold, treating a zero-range bar as its upper edge; resistance headroom at or above a threshold; and the setup floor within an ATR multiple of either a faster or slower close SMA or EMA. The volume vote is unavailable when volume is missing or the bar is forming and partial volume is excluded. The headroom vote is available only when the selected setup level exceeds the preceding low. The moving-average proximity vote is available only when the setup floor exists. Select level and floor from the pending flag, otherwise pending range, otherwise pending inside bar; use close if the selected level is unavailable. Headroom is the distance from the selected level to the nearest qualifying resistance above it divided by the level minus the preceding low; if no resistance is above it, headroom passes. Build resistance from confirmed pivot highs: merge each new pivot into the first retained level within a current-ATR multiple, incrementing its touch count and keeping the higher price; otherwise append it with one touch and discard the oldest level when the retention limit is exceeded. A level qualifies after the selected minimum touches. Grade A has all available votes passing, B has all but one passing, and C has fewer passing. The grade filter accepts the selected set of A only, A and B, or all grades.
Calculation: Risk distance = At a triggered setup, use the selected setup level, falling back to close, minus the preceding low, or an ATR multiple. Measurement positions are opened only when this distance is positive and within the selected minimum and maximum ATR multiples. From the bar after entry, track the highest high since entry and the position's age. Compare profit milestones and stop levels as selected multiples of the stored risk distance; milestones are ordered from nearest to farthest, and the stop moves from below entry to entry after the first milestone and to a selected level at or above entry after the second. Evaluate each bar's milestones before its stop.
Long: An inside-bar, flag, or range setup is alive AND the trend filter passes AND no setup triggers on the current bar.
Long: An inside-bar setup is alive AND the current bar is not inside AND setup age is at least one bar AND the selected close or high is above its breakout level AND the trend filter passes AND the selected bar-confirmation condition passes AND the grade filter passes.
Long: A flag setup is alive AND its age is at least the minimum flag length AND the selected close or high is above its breakout level AND the flag volume filter passes AND the trend filter passes AND the selected bar-confirmation condition passes AND the grade filter passes.
Long: A range setup is alive AND the selected close or high is above its stored high AND the trend filter passes AND the selected bar-confirmation condition passes AND the grade filter passes.
Exit: long, measurement position is open AND the highest high since entry reaches the first or second profit milestone; exit the corresponding fraction of the position.
Exit: long, measurement position is open AND the highest high since entry reaches the final profit milestone.
Exit: long, measurement position is open AND the final profit milestone has not been reached AND low is at or below the stop corresponding to the highest milestone reached.
Exit: long, measurement position is open AND the final profit milestone has not been reached AND low is above the applicable stop AND position age is at least the observation horizon.
