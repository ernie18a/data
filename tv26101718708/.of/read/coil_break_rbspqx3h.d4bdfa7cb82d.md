Calculation: Trend MA = selected simple or exponential moving average of close; when the trend filter is enabled, a trigger passes if close is above it or it is unavailable.
Calculation: Inside-bar setup = selected pattern comparing the current bar with the previous bar: full range inside previous range, current body inside previous body, or full range inside previous body; equality is allowed unless strict mode is enabled. Store its high, low, previous-bar high, and bar index; a close below its low invalidates it. It remains eligible through the configured breakout window, but not on a bar that is itself an inside bar.
Calculation: Inside-bar breakout = close above the selected stored level (inside-bar high or previous-bar high), or, if close confirmation is disabled, high above that level.
Calculation: Bull-flag setup = track a pole when current high minus the lowest low over the pole lookback meets the configured minimum in ATR units or percent of that low, and current high equals or exceeds the highest high over that lookback. Each qualifying new high resets or extends the pole and starts a new flag. Track flag highs and lows on subsequent bars; invalidate if the flag low falls below the pole high minus the configured retracement share of the pole, or flag age exceeds its maximum. A flag is eligible from its minimum through maximum age. If volume drying is enabled and measurable, average volume of completed flag bars must be below the volume average over the pole lookback captured at the pole bar. The breakout level is either the highest flag high recorded before the current bar (falling back to pole high if none exists) or pole high, as selected.
Calculation: Bull-flag breakout = close above the selected breakout level, or, if close confirmation is disabled, high above it.
Calculation: Tight-range setup = rolling highest high minus rolling lowest low over the tightness lookback is at most the configured ATR multiple or percent of the rolling low. Form a stored box only on the first tight bar after a non-tight bar, and only if the optional near-recent-high condition passes. Freeze its ceiling and floor; invalidate it on a close below its floor or when its age plus the tightness lookback exceeds the configured maximum total length.
Calculation: Tight-range breakout = while a stored box is active, close above its frozen ceiling, or, if close confirmation is disabled, high above it.
Long: (enabled, eligible inside-bar setup AND inside-bar breakout OR enabled, eligible bull-flag setup AND bull-flag breakout OR enabled, active tight-range setup AND tight-range breakout) AND trend filter disabled OR trend MA unavailable OR close above trend MA
