Calculation: Hann average = weighted average of the current and preceding closes over a lookback of length N, with weight 1 − cos(2πi/(N+1)) for the close i−1 bars ago, for i from 1 through N. Use three equally spaced lookbacks: short, medium, and long.
Calculation: Swing high = previous bar's high when it equals the highest high over the swing lookback ending on the previous bar AND the current high is below the highest high over the swing lookback ending on the current bar. Swing low = previous bar's low when it equals the lowest low over the swing lookback ending on the previous bar AND the current low is above the lowest low over the swing lookback ending on the current bar. Retain the latest value of each.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = current close > latest swing high AND that swing-high price differs from the price used for the previous bullish structure break. Bearish structure break = current close < latest swing low AND that swing-low price differs from the price used for the previous bearish structure break. Track the last broken price separately for each direction.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral; set bullish when a new swing high exceeds the preceding swing high, bearish when a new swing low is below the preceding swing low, bullish on a bullish structure break, and bearish on a bearish structure break. Apply these updates in that order and retain the state between updates.
Calculation: Bullish gap = after the configured initial-bar warmup, the previous close > previous open AND the high two bars ago < current low; retain the most recent such gap with lower boundary equal to the high two bars ago and upper boundary equal to current low. Bearish gap = after the same warmup, the previous close < previous open AND the low two bars ago > current high; retain the most recent such gap with lower boundary equal to current high and upper boundary equal to the low two bars ago. Retain each gap until a newer gap of the same direction replaces it.
Calculation: Entry session = a weekday AND the bar's UTC opening time falls within an enabled session's configured UTC time window.
Calculation: Long risk distance = at the entry signal bar, the greater of close minus the rolling lowest low plus an ATR buffer, and a minimum ATR multiple. Short risk distance = at the entry signal bar, the greater of rolling highest high plus an ATR buffer minus close, and a minimum ATR multiple. Fix each distance and the signal-bar close for the resulting trade.
Long: The short Hann average crosses above the medium Hann average.
Short: The short Hann average crosses below the medium Hann average.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Short Hann average > medium Hann average AND medium Hann average > long Hann average.
Short: Short Hann average < medium Hann average AND medium Hann average < long Hann average.
Short: ADX is within its inclusive configured range AND short Hann average <= medium Hann average OR ADX is within its inclusive configured range AND medium Hann average <= long Hann average.
Long: Structure state = bullish.
Short: Structure state = bearish.
Long: Bullish gap exists AND close > its lower boundary − ATR proximity buffer AND close < its upper boundary + ATR proximity buffer.
Short: Bullish gap is not near AND bearish gap exists AND close > its lower boundary − ATR proximity buffer AND close < its upper boundary + ATR proximity buffer.
Long: Position is flat AND (short Hann average crosses above medium Hann average OR (bullish structure break AND short Hann average > long Hann average)) AND structure state is bullish or neutral AND ADX is within its inclusive configured range AND (session filter is disabled OR entry session is true) AND (gap filter is disabled OR bullish gap exists AND close > its lower boundary − ATR proximity buffer AND close < its upper boundary + ATR proximity buffer).
Short: Position is flat AND (short Hann average crosses below medium Hann average OR (bearish structure break AND short Hann average < long Hann average)) AND structure state is bearish or neutral AND ADX is within its inclusive configured range AND (session filter is disabled OR entry session is true) AND (gap filter is disabled OR bearish gap exists AND close > its lower boundary − ATR proximity buffer AND close < its upper boundary + ATR proximity buffer).
Exit: long, low <= entry signal-bar close − long risk distance OR high >= entry signal-bar close + long risk distance × reward-to-risk ratio OR bearish structure break.
Exit: short, high >= entry signal-bar close + short risk distance OR low <= entry signal-bar close − short risk distance × reward-to-risk ratio OR bullish structure break.
