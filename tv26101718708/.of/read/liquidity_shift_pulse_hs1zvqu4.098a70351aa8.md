Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low using separately parameterized left and right bar counts, available only after the right bars have closed; the liquidity reference on each bar is the most recently confirmed pivot through the preceding bar.
Calculation: Candle measures = range is high minus low; body ratio is abs(close minus open) divided by range; lower wick ratio is (min(open, close) minus low) divided by range; upper wick ratio is (high minus max(open, close)) divided by range; bullish close location is (close minus low) divided by range; bearish close location is (high minus close) divided by range. For zero range, wick and body ratios are zero and close locations are 0.5. Range in ATRs is range divided by current ATR, or zero if ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest swing low minus current ATR times sweep penetration parameter AND close > latest swing low AND lower wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = high > latest swing high plus current ATR times sweep penetration parameter AND close < latest swing high AND upper wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio.
Calculation: Volume filter = disabled OR current volume or the preceding-bars volume SMA is unavailable OR preceding-bars volume SMA <= 0 OR current volume divided by that SMA >= minimum relative volume.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 times log(sum of true range over its lookback divided by highest high minus lowest low over that lookback) divided by log of the lookback; unavailable if the sum or price range is nonpositive or unavailable. Regime filter = disabled OR choppiness <= directional threshold OR (choppiness <= transition threshold AND choppiness < previous-bar choppiness).
Calculation: Bullish setup = while flat, each bullish sweep replaces the setup with its bar, low, and the highest high over the internal-structure lookback ending on the preceding bar, provided that high is available. Expire it when its age in bars > setup window or close < stored sweep low minus current ATR times invalidation parameter. Clear it on entry. Evaluate setup creation and invalidation before entry, including on the sweep bar.
Calculation: Bearish setup = while long and maximum favorable excursion divided by entry ATR >= reversal activation threshold, each bearish sweep replaces the setup with its bar, high, and the lowest low over the internal-structure lookback ending on the preceding bar, provided that low is available. Expire it when its age in bars > reversal window or close > stored sweep high plus current ATR times invalidation parameter. Clear it on entry and exit. Evaluate setup creation and invalidation before exit, including on the sweep bar.
Calculation: Protected level = on a liquidity-shift entry, the stored bullish sweep low minus entry ATR times initial protection parameter; otherwise the lowest low over the internal-structure lookback ending before entry minus entry ATR times initial protection parameter. On each subsequent bar, a newly confirmed pivot low whose pivot bar is after entry can raise the level to the pivot low minus the ATR on the pivot bar times the higher-low protection parameter, or to the pivot low if that ATR is unavailable, provided the candidate is below current close and above the existing level; if the existing level is unavailable, use the valid candidate. Update before evaluating exits.
Calculation: Maximum favorable excursion = max(highest high from entry through the current bar minus entry price, 0). Giveback percentage = 100 times (maximum favorable excursion minus max(current close minus entry price, 0)) divided by maximum favorable excursion, or zero if the maximum is zero. Entry price is the entry bar close and entry ATR is the ATR on that bar.
Long: Bullish sweep.
Short: Bearish sweep.
Long: Flat AND bullish setup is active AND setup age <= setup window AND close > open AND range in ATRs >= minimum displacement range AND body ratio >= minimum displacement body ratio AND bullish close location >= minimum displacement close location AND close > stored setup high plus current ATR times structure-break buffer AND volume filter AND regime filter.
Long: Flat AND expansion entry is enabled AND close > highest high over the expansion lookback ending on the preceding bar plus current ATR times expansion-break buffer AND close > open AND range in ATRs >= minimum expansion range AND body ratio >= minimum expansion body ratio AND bullish close location >= minimum expansion close location AND volume filter AND regime filter AND (regime filter is disabled OR choppiness < previous-bar choppiness).
Exit: Long, bar is after entry AND close < protected level.
Exit: Long, bearish setup is active AND setup age <= reversal window AND close < open AND range in ATRs >= minimum displacement range AND body ratio >= minimum displacement body ratio AND bearish close location >= minimum displacement close location AND close < stored setup low minus current ATR times structure-break buffer.
Exit: Long, large-profit protection is enabled AND maximum favorable excursion divided by entry ATR >= large-trend activation threshold AND giveback percentage >= maximum giveback threshold AND close < open AND close < previous close.
