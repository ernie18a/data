Calculation: Swing = Start with no direction. Use high and low, or close for both, optionally applying the same SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or HMA to each series. In an up or undefined swing, update the swing high and its bar only when the high series is strictly higher; in a down or undefined swing, update the swing low and its bar only when the low series is strictly lower. After updating extremes, an up reversal requires a down or undefined swing, a bar later than the swing-low bar, and high series >= swing low × (1 + reversal fraction); a down reversal requires an up or undefined swing, a bar later than the swing-high bar, and low series <= swing high × (1 - reversal fraction). If both qualify, choose up when the swing low was set more recently, or when both extremes were set on the same bar and close >= their midpoint; otherwise choose down. On an up reversal save the swing low as pivot low, set direction up, and reset swing high to the current high series; on a down reversal save the swing high as pivot high, set direction down, and reset swing low to the current low series. Update the swing even when an entry is blocked.
Calculation: Trade levels = At entry, use the signal bar close as entry price. Long stop = max(pivot low, entry price - ATR × stop multiplier); short stop = min(pivot high, entry price + ATR × stop multiplier). Risk = abs(entry price - stop). Sort the three target multipliers from smallest to largest; each long target = entry price + risk × multiplier and each short target = entry price - risk × multiplier. Fix these levels at entry.
Calculation: Valid levels = ATR is available AND stop > 0 AND all targets > 0 AND risk > minimum price increment AND, for a long, stop < entry price AND nearest target rounded to the minimum price increment > entry price rounded to the minimum price increment, or, for a short, stop > entry price AND nearest target rounded to the minimum price increment < entry price rounded to the minimum price increment.
Long: Swing direction = up.
Short: Swing direction = down.
Long: Up reversal AND bar is closed AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR previous chart bar ADX >= threshold) AND valid levels AND (signal lock disabled OR bar is not the latest bar).
Short: Down reversal AND bar is closed AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR previous chart bar ADX >= threshold) AND valid levels AND (signal lock disabled OR bar is not the latest bar).
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND high >= an unhit target; exit partially at each of the two nearest targets and fully at the farthest target.
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND low <= an unhit target; exit partially at each of the two nearest targets and fully at the farthest target.
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND farthest target has not been hit AND closed bar close < stop.
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND farthest target has not been hit AND closed bar close > stop.
Exit: long, down reversal on a closed bar AND position remains open after target and stop checks AND (signal lock disabled OR bar is not the latest bar).
Exit: short, up reversal on a closed bar AND position remains open after target and stop checks AND (signal lock disabled OR bar is not the latest bar).
