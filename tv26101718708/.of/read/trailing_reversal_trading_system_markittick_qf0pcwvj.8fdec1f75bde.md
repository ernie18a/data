Calculation: Selected high/low = bar high/low, or bar close for both, optionally smoothed by the chosen moving average
Calculation: Trailing swing reversal = track the running selected high during an up or undefined swing and running selected low during a down or undefined swing; after the extreme-setting bar, reverse down when selected low <= running high × (1 − reversal percentage), or reverse up when selected high >= running low × (1 + reversal percentage); on the first bar where both reversals qualify, choose the one whose extreme was set later, or if tied choose up when close >= the midpoint of the extremes, otherwise down
Calculation: Long stop = max(pivot low, entry close − ATR × stop multiplier); short stop = min(pivot high, entry close + ATR × stop multiplier)
Calculation: Risk = absolute difference between entry close and stop; sort the three target risk multiples from lowest to highest; long targets = entry close + risk × each sorted multiple; short targets = entry close − risk × each sorted multiple
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = previous completed higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA on that same higher timeframe
Calculation: ADX = directional movement index trend-strength measure
Long: confirmed up swing reversal AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA, with the selected timeframe no lower than the chart timeframe) AND (ADX filter disabled OR previous confirmed chart-bar ADX >= minimum) AND (lock disabled OR this is not the latest bar) AND ATR available AND stop > 0 AND all targets > 0 AND risk > one tick AND stop < entry close AND rounded first target > rounded entry close
Short: confirmed down swing reversal AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA, with the selected timeframe no lower than the chart timeframe) AND (ADX filter disabled OR previous confirmed chart-bar ADX >= minimum) AND (lock disabled OR this is not the latest bar) AND ATR available AND stop > 0 AND all targets > 0 AND risk > one tick AND stop > entry close AND rounded first target < rounded entry close
Exit: long, after entry bar, high >= first target (partial exit) OR high >= second target (partial exit) OR high >= third target (final exit)
Exit: short, after entry bar, low <= first target (partial exit) OR low <= second target (partial exit) OR low <= third target (final exit)
Exit: long, confirmed bar after entry bar, close < stop OR confirmed down swing reversal (unless the latest bar is locked)
Exit: short, confirmed bar after entry bar, close > stop OR confirmed up swing reversal (unless the latest bar is locked)
