Calculation: Directional probability = for each of a lookback of completed prior close-to-close changes, sum linearly decreasing recency weights separately for positive and negative changes; divide each sum by their combined sum, or use zero if the combined sum is zero.
Calculation: Direction and memory = direction is the sign of bullish probability minus bearish probability; momentum is their absolute difference; confidence is their maximum. On each calculation, add a signed affine function of momentum to decayed prior memory and clamp to a symmetric range. Memory direction is determined by positive and negative thresholds; preferred direction is probability direction unless that is neutral, then memory direction.
Calculation: Directional bias = the percentage of seven tests satisfied: close versus session VWAP of typical price, chart RSI versus its midpoint, MACD versus its signal, short EMA versus longer EMA, ADX above its threshold AND close versus short EMA, volume above its SMA AND candle direction, and RSI versus its midpoint on the additional timeframe. Use greater-than comparisons for bullish and less-than for bearish, except that both volume and ADX use greater-than. The additional RSI timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; with historical lookahead off, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the last available lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Trend alignment = close above the trend EMA AND that EMA rising for bullish; close below it AND that EMA falling for bearish. The additional-timeframe trend EMA uses the same length, the last completed bar when higher than the chart, and the last available bar when lower.
Calculation: Candle strength = absolute close minus open divided by the larger of high minus low and minimum price increment.
Calculation: Chop state = ADX at or below its threshold AND absolute trend EMA change at or below an ATR fraction AND the count of close/VWAP crosses over a lookback at or above its threshold. Increment a nonnegative counter on each bar satisfying all three tests; otherwise decrement it to a minimum of zero. Chop is confirmed when the counter reaches its threshold.
Calculation: Structural votes = a bullish vote occurs only on the confirmation bar of a pivot low when the pivot bar's high-low range is at most an ATR multiple, its age is within the maximum age, and the current bar overlaps that pivot bar's range; bearish uses a pivot high. A bullish gap vote requires current low above a prior high at the gap lookback AND previous close above previous open; bearish requires current high below the prior low AND previous close below previous open.
Calculation: Cluster = count and weight directional votes for probability direction, memory direction, chart trend alignment, additional-timeframe trend alignment, close versus VWAP, MACD versus signal, ADX above a threshold AND short EMA versus longer EMA, directional candle with sufficient candle strength, above-average volume AND directional candle, directional bias above its threshold and greater than opposing bias, the directional EMA-cross trigger, structural pivot touch, and gap. Each category has its own weight. Consensus is directional votes divided by all votes; direction is bullish if bullish consensus reaches its threshold, otherwise bearish if bearish consensus reaches its threshold, otherwise neutral. Strength is the larger directional weight sum divided by the fixed possible-weight denominator; conflict is the smaller directional vote count divided by total votes. Use zero for ratios when there are no votes.
Calculation: Directional EMA-cross trigger = short EMA crosses above the longer EMA AND bullish bias exceeds bearish bias and its minimum AND close is above VWAP AND MACD is above its signal AND additional-timeframe RSI is above its midpoint; reverse all directional comparisons for bearish.
Calculation: Smoothed-trend state = bullish when close is above the middle-length Wilder-smoothed close AND the shorter smoothed close is above the middle one AND the middle is above the longer one; bearish reverses all comparisons.
Calculation: Directional candle pattern = bullish when the preceding three candles are bearish AND current close exceeds the preceding open, OR current open is at or below the preceding close AND below the preceding open AND current close exceeds the preceding open. Bearish reverses the directional comparisons, including current open at or above the preceding close.
Calculation: Directional state across timeframes = maintain the open of the latest bearish non-inside bar as the bullish target and the open of the latest bullish non-inside bar as the bearish target. An inside bar has lower high AND higher low than the preceding bar. On a confirmed bar, set state bullish when close crosses above the existing bullish target, otherwise bearish when close crosses below the existing bearish target; update targets after testing crosses. Calculate independently on the chart and two additional timeframes, one longer than the other; use the last completed bar for each higher timeframe and the last available bar for each lower timeframe. Alignment requires all three states to agree; confirmed alignment requires agreement for the configured consecutive chart bars.
Calculation: ATR trailing trigger = use close or same-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close as source. Set loss distance to an ATR multiple. If current and previous source are both above the previous stop, stop is the maximum of previous stop and source minus distance; if both are below, stop is the minimum of previous stop and source plus distance; otherwise stop is source minus distance when source is above the previous stop, and source plus distance otherwise. Initialize the previous stop to source. Bullish trigger is source crossing above the stop; bearish trigger is source crossing below it.
Calculation: Directional score = add weighted points for preferred direction, probability direction, memory direction, confidence above its threshold, momentum above its threshold, enabled chart trend alignment, enabled additional-timeframe trend alignment, enabled volume above its SMA multiple, enabled ATR above its ATR-SMA multiple, enabled directional candle strength, directional bias exceeding opposing bias, strong directional bias difference, close versus VWAP, MACD versus signal, ADX above its threshold, additional-timeframe RSI versus its midpoint, enabled directional candle pattern, and cluster direction. Disabled chart trend, additional-timeframe trend, volume, ATR, and candle filters contribute their points automatically. Directional edge is that score minus the opposing score.
Calculation: Fast directional score = add points for close versus short EMA, short EMA versus longer EMA, close versus VWAP, MACD versus signal, and directional probability above its threshold; the probability test has greater weight than each other test.
Calculation: Entry timing = after an entry or full close, require the elapsed chart bars to reach the ceiling of a configured time interval divided by chart-bar duration, multiplied by a stepwise factor based on ATR divided by its SMA. The factor increases at two rising ATR-ratio thresholds. For another signal in the same direction, require close to have moved from the last recorded signal price in that direction by an ATR multiple; the fast layer uses a separate move threshold. A full close resets the recorded signal time and price but retains the last signal direction.
Calculation: Entry loss guard = after a losing full trade, block its direction through a cooldown proportional to the consecutive same-direction loss count within a lookback, capped at a maximum; double the cooldown if holding time is at most a threshold or chop is confirmed. Also block that direction during the loss lookback while close is within an ATR multiple of the losing entry. Optionally reset the loss count after a winning trade.
Calculation: Initial stop = at the signal close, choose an ATR-distance stop, a buffered most-recent confirmed swing pivot stop, or the closer valid one according to the selected mode; fall back to the ATR stop if the structure stop is unavailable or on the wrong side. Offset the selected stop farther from entry by a configured price increment. Initial risk is the larger of the distance from signal close to stop and the minimum price increment. Profit targets are signal close plus initial risk times their respective multiples for a long, and minus those amounts for a short; the final target uses a longer multiple than the first target.
Calculation: Post-first-target stop = after a partial reduction, track the highest high for a long or lowest low for a short since reduction; subtract or add the current ATR times a configured fraction, respectively. The active exit order uses this current trailing value.
Long: Flat and clean position AND directional loss guard passes AND no long entry has fired on the current bar AND either a pending long reversal exists OR the bullish raw signal exists and wins any simultaneous bullish and bearish raw-signal tie by having the higher directional score.
Short: Flat and clean position AND directional loss guard passes AND no short entry has fired on the current bar AND either a pending short reversal exists OR the bearish raw signal exists and wins any simultaneous bullish and bearish raw-signal tie by having the higher directional score.
Calculation: Raw directional signal = allowed session AND chop filter disabled or chop not confirmed AND one of: entry timing passes AND directional score and edge meet their thresholds AND directional entry gates pass; OR directional EMA-cross trigger occurs AND directional entry gates pass; OR the enabled fast layer has its own score and edge at or above thresholds AND its optional KAMA, timeframe-alignment, smoothed-trend, cooldown, and directional rearm filters pass. The fast branch uses its own rearm threshold and does not require the ordinary entry gates.
Calculation: Directional entry gates = in strict mode, each enabled gate requires directional bias or strong directional bias, cluster direction with minimum strength and maximum conflict, confirmed timeframe alignment, ATR trailing trigger, source on the directional side of KAMA with optional directional KAMA slope, and smoothed-trend alignment. In balanced mode, enabled gates require directional bias greater than opposing bias, cluster direction not opposite, timeframe alignment not opposite, ATR trailing trigger not opposite, KAMA state not opposite, and smoothed-trend state not opposite. KAMA state is directional only when source is on the corresponding side and KAMA slopes in that direction.
Exit: Long, enabled opposite-signal close AND bearish raw signal wins simultaneous-signal arbitration AND no reversal is already pending; retain a pending short entry until flat and clean.
Exit: Short, enabled opposite-signal close AND bullish raw signal wins simultaneous-signal arbitration AND no reversal is already pending; retain a pending long entry until flat and clean.
Exit: Long, low at or below the initial stop before the first partial exit OR low at or below the post-first-target stop after the partial exit.
Exit: Short, high at or above the initial stop before the first partial exit OR high at or above the post-first-target stop after the partial exit.
Exit: Long, high at or above the first profit target before its partial exit; close the configured portion, or the whole position when the configured order quantity is at or below its cutoff.
Exit: Short, low at or below the first profit target before its partial exit; close the configured portion, or the whole position when the configured order quantity is at or below its cutoff.
Exit: Long, high at or above the final profit target for the remaining position.
Exit: Short, low at or below the final profit target for the remaining position.
