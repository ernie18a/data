Calculation: Weighted K = SMA of the weighted mean of enabled components, using a separately configurable positive weight for each component. Components are: SMA-smoothed stochastic K of close using high and low; SMA-smoothed stochastic K of close RSI; SMA-smoothed stochastic K of the SMA of 100 * (close - open) / open; SMA-smoothed stochastic K of the SMA of signed volume, where signed volume is volume if close >= open and -volume otherwise; close RSI; a configurable moving average of close RSI; MFI of typical price; and a configurable moving average of MFI of typical price. For stochastic calculations on a single series, use that series as the source, high, and low. Each component has independently configurable lookbacks and smoothing lengths; moving-average choices are RMA, SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA.
Long: Weighted K < very oversold threshold
Short: Weighted K > very overbought threshold
