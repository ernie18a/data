Calculation: Absorption bar = previous chart bar if next-bar confirmation is enabled; otherwise the current chart bar.
Calculation: Safe range = maximum of the absorption bar's high minus low and a minimum price increment parameter.
Long: Absorption bar volume > its volume SMA including that bar × volume threshold AND absolute absorption bar close minus open < its high minus low × maximum body fraction AND (minimum of its open and close minus its low) / safe range >= minimum wick fraction AND (reversal-close filter disabled OR absorption bar close > open) AND (next-bar confirmation disabled OR current low >= absorption bar low minus confirmation tolerance).
Short: Absorption bar volume > its volume SMA including that bar × volume threshold AND absolute absorption bar close minus open < its high minus low × maximum body fraction AND (its high minus maximum of its open and close) / safe range >= minimum wick fraction AND (reversal-close filter disabled OR absorption bar close < open) AND (next-bar confirmation disabled OR current high <= absorption bar high plus confirmation tolerance).
