Calculation: Trend count = number of true conditions among: close > medium-term close SMA AND close > long-term close SMA; medium-term close SMA > long-term close SMA; long-term close SMA > its value a slope-lookback number of bars ago; short-term close SMA > medium-term close SMA AND short-term close SMA > long-term close SMA; close >= a minimum-low multiple of the lowest low over the range lookback; close >= a minimum-high multiple of the highest high over the same range lookback; close > short-term close SMA.
Calculation: Base depth = (highest high - lowest low) / highest high over the base lookback, expressed as a percentage; undefined when the highest high <= 0.
Calculation: Range for a lookback = (highest high - lowest low) / current close over that lookback, expressed as a percentage; undefined when close <= 0. Use short, medium and long lookbacks, in increasing order.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / longer-term volume SMA; undefined when the longer-term volume SMA <= 0.
Calculation: Volume ratio = shorter-term volume SMA / longer-term volume SMA; undefined when the longer-term volume SMA <= 0.
Calculation: Contraction score = sum of weights for each true condition: base depth is between its minimum and maximum thresholds inclusive; short range < medium range multiplied by its contraction factor; medium range < long range multiplied by its contraction factor; short-term ATR < medium-term ATR multiplied by its contraction factor; medium-term ATR < long-term ATR; relative volume < its dry-up threshold AND volume ratio < its dry-up threshold; short range < medium range multiplied by the same contraction factor as above. The two range-contraction weights are equal, the two ATR-contraction weights are equal, and the base-depth and volume-dry-up weights are equal; the ATR weight is smaller than the range weight, which is smaller than the final short-range weight, which is smaller than the base-depth weight. ATR lookbacks increase from short to medium to long.
Calculation: Pivot = highest high over the pivot lookback ending at the previous bar.
Calculation: Pivot distance = (close / pivot - 1) expressed as a percentage; undefined when pivot <= 0.
Long: Trend count equals its maximum AND contraction score >= its minimum threshold AND pivot distance > zero AND pivot distance <= maximum chase distance AND relative volume >= breakout threshold AND the same combined condition was false on the previous bar.
