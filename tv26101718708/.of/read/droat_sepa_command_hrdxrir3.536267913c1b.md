Calculation: Trend template pass = close > 150-period SMA AND close > 200-period SMA AND 150-period SMA > 200-period SMA AND 200-period SMA > its value a longer lookback ago AND 50-period SMA > 150-period SMA AND 50-period SMA > 200-period SMA AND close >= lowest low over a long-term lookback × a minimum multiplier AND close >= highest high over a long-term lookback × a minimum multiplier AND close > 50-period SMA; the 50-period SMA is shorter-term than the 150- and 200-period SMAs.
Calculation: VCP score = weighted score for base depth within a minimum-to-maximum range, short-range contraction versus medium-range contraction, medium-range contraction versus longer-range contraction, short ATR contraction versus medium ATR and medium ATR versus longer ATR, volume dry-up, and right-side tightness; short, medium, and long lookbacks increase in that order. Volume dry-up means current volume / longer-term average volume is below a threshold AND short-term average volume / longer-term average volume is below a threshold. Right-side tightness means short-range contraction versus medium-range contraction.
Calculation: Pivot = highest high over a lookback of prior completed chart-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Pivot distance = (close / pivot − 1) × 100.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / longer-term average volume.
