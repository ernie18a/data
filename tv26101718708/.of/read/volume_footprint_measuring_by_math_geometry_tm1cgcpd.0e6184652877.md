Calculation: Bar buy and sell volume = Either split volume geometrically: buy = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), sell = volume − buy, with equal halves when high = low; or sum lower-timeframe bar volumes by direction within each chart bar, including all completed lower-timeframe bars and the currently forming lower-timeframe bar: an up close versus open is buy, a down close is sell, an equal close versus open follows its change from the previous lower-timeframe close, and an unchanged close retains the previous lower-timeframe direction.
Calculation: Price-row volumes = Distribute each chart bar's buy and sell volume over price rows of a configurable width using separate normal distributions truncated to that bar's low–high range. Their centers are (low + close) / 2 and (close + high) / 2 respectively, and their shared standard deviation is the bar range divided by a concentration parameter. Integrate each distribution over each row's intersection with the bar range; put a zero-range bar's volume entirely in its price row.
Calculation: Window rows = Center a fixed-width price frame on the current close rounded to a price row. Scan backward from the current chart bar, selecting up to a configurable number of bars with positive volume whose ranges overlap the frame; sum their price-row buy and sell volumes separately. The window POC is the lowest row with the greatest positive combined volume. A window imbalance row is the nearest row above or below the POC with positive buy volume exceeding the sell volume in the row below times an imbalance ratio, or positive sell volume exceeding the buy volume in the row above times that ratio; buy takes precedence if both qualify.
Calculation: Profile = Over a configurable number of chart bars through the current bar, sum the same truncated normal distributions separately as buy and sell densities; retain zero-range bars as point masses at their closes. Sample the densities uniformly from the period's lowest low to highest high. The profile POC is the lowest sample with the greatest combined density. A profile imbalance sample is the nearest sample above or below the POC with positive buy density exceeding the sell density at the preceding sample times the imbalance ratio, or positive sell density exceeding the buy density at the following sample times that ratio; buy takes precedence if both qualify.
Calculation: Profile overlap = Integrate the pointwise minimum of the buy and sell profile densities after dividing each by its own period total volume, and add the minimum of the normalized buy and sell point masses at each shared price. Profile tilt = (period buy volume − period sell volume) / (period buy volume + period sell volume).
Long: Current bar buy volume >= current bar sell volume AND current bar total volume > 0.
Short: Current bar buy volume < current bar sell volume AND current bar total volume > 0.
Long: Window buy volume >= window sell volume.
Short: Window buy volume < window sell volume.
Long: Window POC row buy volume > window POC row sell volume.
Short: Window POC row sell volume > window POC row buy volume.
Long: Profile buy volume integrated over a price row centered on the profile POC > profile sell volume integrated over that row.
Short: Profile sell volume integrated over a price row centered on the profile POC > profile buy volume integrated over that row.
Long: A window imbalance row has positive buy volume > the sell volume in the row below × imbalance ratio.
Short: A window imbalance row has positive sell volume > the buy volume in the row above × imbalance ratio AND its buy volume is not greater than the sell volume in the row below × imbalance ratio.
Long: At a window imbalance row, a selected bar's buy row volume > 0 AND its buy row volume > that bar's sell volume in the row below × imbalance ratio, with both rows present.
Short: At a window imbalance row, a selected bar's sell row volume > 0 AND its sell row volume > that bar's buy volume in the row above × imbalance ratio, with both rows present.
Long: A profile imbalance sample has positive buy density > the sell density at the preceding sample × imbalance ratio.
Short: A profile imbalance sample has positive sell density > the buy density at the following sample × imbalance ratio AND its buy density is not greater than the sell density at the preceding sample × imbalance ratio.
Long: Profile overlap < balance threshold AND profile tilt >= tilt threshold AND period buy and sell volumes are both positive.
Short: Profile overlap < balance threshold AND profile tilt <= negative tilt threshold AND profile tilt < tilt threshold AND period buy and sell volumes are both positive.
