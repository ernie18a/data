Calculation: Bars are indexed backward from the current bar: [1] is the previous bar. All thresholds, lengths, multipliers, counts and windows are parameters. RSI and MFI use close and standard definitions; MFI returns its maximum when negative money flow sums to zero.
Calculation: For each oscillator and direction, remember the price low and oscillator value at the lowest oscillator reading below its oversold threshold, or the price high and oscillator value at the highest oscillator reading above its overbought threshold. Update the memory before testing divergence; discard it when its age exceeds the divergence lookback. Bullish divergence requires oscillator < oversold threshold AND low < remembered low AND oscillator > remembered oscillator. Bearish divergence requires oscillator > overbought threshold AND high > remembered high AND oscillator < remembered oscillator.
Calculation: Strong RSI divergence = RSI divergence on the current bar AND the count of same-direction RSI divergence bars in the divergence lookback, including the current bar, >= required count.
Calculation: An active long setup starts or is replaced on each strong bullish RSI divergence bar, storing that bar's high and low. It is active only on later bars within the signal-life window; after evaluating the current bar, clear it if close > stored high OR low < stored low. An active short setup starts or is replaced on each strong bearish RSI divergence bar, storing that bar's high and low. It is active only on later bars within the signal-life window; after evaluating the current bar, clear it if close < stored low OR high > stored high.
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; range = high - low. Average body is the simple moving average of body over its lookback.
Calculation: Bullish candle = bullish engulfing OR bullish pin bar OR morning star OR bullish inside-bar breakout OR bullish outside bar OR bullish marubozu. Bullish engulfing requires close > open AND close[1] < open[1] AND close > open[1] AND open < close[1] AND body > body[1]. Bullish pin bar requires lower wick >= body multiplied by its minimum multiplier AND upper wick <= body multiplied by its maximum multiplier AND close > low + range multiplied by its close-location fraction. Morning star requires close[2] < open[2] AND body[2] > average body[2] multiplied by its minimum multiplier AND body[1] < body[2] multiplied by its maximum multiplier AND close > open AND close > (open[2] + close[2]) / 2. Bullish inside-bar breakout requires high[1] < high[2] AND low[1] > low[2] AND close > high[1]. Bullish outside bar requires high > high[1] AND low < low[1] AND body > body[1] AND close > open. Bullish marubozu requires body / range >= its minimum fraction AND each wick <= range multiplied by its maximum fraction AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish candle = bearish engulfing OR bearish pin bar OR evening star OR bearish inside-bar breakout OR bearish outside bar OR bearish marubozu. Bearish engulfing requires close < open AND close[1] > open[1] AND close < open[1] AND open > close[1] AND body > body[1]. Bearish pin bar requires upper wick >= body multiplied by its minimum multiplier AND lower wick <= body multiplied by its maximum multiplier AND close < high - range multiplied by its close-location fraction. Evening star requires close[2] > open[2] AND body[2] > average body[2] multiplied by its minimum multiplier AND body[1] < body[2] multiplied by its maximum multiplier AND close < open AND close < (open[2] + close[2]) / 2. Bearish inside-bar breakout requires high[1] < high[2] AND low[1] > low[2] AND close < low[1]. Bearish outside bar requires high > high[1] AND low < low[1] AND body > body[1] AND close < open. Bearish marubozu requires body / range >= its minimum fraction AND each wick <= range multiplied by its maximum fraction AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation state = RSI below its oversold threshold at least once within the confirmation window AND bullish RSI divergence at least once within that window AND bullish candle at least once within that window. Bearish confirmation state uses RSI above its overbought threshold, bearish RSI divergence and bearish candle. The window includes the current bar and bars whose age is <= the window. An optional setting substitutes strong RSI divergence for ordinary RSI divergence.
Calculation: For dashboard calculations on each selected timeframe, use that timeframe's last completed bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe and its last completed intrabar within the chart bar when it is lower. Dashboard bullish oscillator divergence requires the oscillator below its oversold threshold AND low below the low of the immediately preceding oversold bar AND oscillator above its value on that preceding bar. Dashboard bearish oscillator divergence requires the oscillator above its overbought threshold AND high above the high of the immediately preceding overbought bar AND oscillator below its value on that preceding bar. Apply this separately to RSI and MFI. Dashboard strong RSI divergence counts dashboard RSI divergence events over the divergence lookback. Dashboard confirmation state uses dashboard RSI divergence, optionally dashboard strong RSI divergence, and the same confirmation-window and candle rules on that timeframe.
Calculation: Dashboard bullish and bearish factor counts each sum the corresponding RSI extreme, RSI divergence, MFI extreme, MFI divergence, candle and newly activated confirmation state. For candles and confirmation, bullish takes precedence if both directions occur. Dashboard bias is the direction of a newly activated confirmation state if present; otherwise it is the sign of bullish factor count minus bearish factor count.
Calculation: RSI extreme counts sum the chart-timeframe RSI extreme and the same extreme on each enabled selected timeframe; use the last completed bar of a higher timeframe and the last completed intrabar within the chart bar of a lower timeframe.
Long: RSI oversold extreme count > RSI overbought extreme count AND RSI oversold extreme count > 0.
Short: RSI overbought extreme count > RSI oversold extreme count AND RSI overbought extreme count > 0.
