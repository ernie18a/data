Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = while RSI is oversold, price makes a lower low and RSI a higher low than the stored oversold extreme; bearish RSI divergence = while RSI is overbought, price makes a higher high and RSI a lower high than the stored overbought extreme. Store the price and RSI at the most extreme RSI value in each state, update while that state persists, and expire the stored pair after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Repeated bullish/bearish RSI divergence = the corresponding RSI divergence occurs and its event count within the divergence lookback reaches the required count.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish MFI divergence = the corresponding RSI divergence definition using MFI instead of RSI, with overbought/oversold MFI states.
Calculation: Bullish candle signal = any of: bullish engulfing (bullish current bar engulfs prior bearish bar, with larger body); bullish pin bar (lower wick at least 2× body, upper wick at most 1.2× body, close in top 40% of range); hammer (prior close below the close five bars earlier, lower wick at least 2× body, upper wick at most 0.8× body, close in top 40% of range); morning star (large bearish bar, next body less than 55% of its body, then bullish bar closing above the midpoint of the first bar); inside-bar upside breakout (prior bar is inside the bar before it, current close above prior high); bullish outside bar (current high above prior high, low below prior low, body larger than prior body, and close above open); or bullish marubozu (body at least 75% of range, each wick at most 12% of range, and close above open). Bearish candle signal = the corresponding bearish engulfing, pin bar, shooting star, evening star, inside-bar downside breakout, bearish outside bar, or bearish marubozu conditions.
Long: bullish RSI divergence; repeated bullish RSI divergence; close above the high of the repeated bullish RSI divergence bar within the signal life, provided price has not first fallen below that bar's low; bullish MFI divergence; bullish candle signal; or a new bullish GEM condition where RSI was oversold, bullish RSI divergence (or repeated bullish RSI divergence when required), and a bullish candle signal each occurred within the confirmation window.
Short: bearish RSI divergence; repeated bearish RSI divergence; close below the low of the repeated bearish RSI divergence bar within the signal life, provided price has not first risen above that bar's high; bearish MFI divergence; bearish candle signal; or a new bearish GEM condition where RSI was overbought, bearish RSI divergence (or repeated bearish RSI divergence when required), and a bearish candle signal each occurred within the confirmation window.
