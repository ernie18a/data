Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Band width <= Keltner Channel width; the Bollinger Bands use a moving average of close ± a multiple of close's standard deviation, and the Keltner Channels use a moving average of close ± a multiple of the same moving average applied to true range
Calculation: Pressure Flow = 50 × clamp(WMA of [((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low) × (volume / moving average of volume)], −2, 2)
Calculation: Squeeze release = current bar is not in a squeeze AND the previous bar was in a squeeze
Long: Within the expansion window after a squeeze release, before another directional signal, close > open AND (close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= 0.60 AND high > previous high AND close > Bollinger basis AND Pressure Flow > minimum pressure AND close > regime EMA; OR, after a long signal, within the follow-through window, close > Bollinger basis AND Pressure Flow > strong-pressure threshold AND high > previous high
