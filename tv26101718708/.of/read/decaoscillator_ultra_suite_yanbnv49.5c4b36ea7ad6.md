Calculation: Composite = weighted-average (fixed weights) of ten oscillators each scaled to 0-100, then smoothed with a short weighted moving average. Components: RSI; MFI; Stochastic of close in the high-low range; CCI scaled over a fixed symmetric range; Williams %R + 100; ROC scaled over a fixed symmetric range; Bull/Bear Power ((high - EMA) + (low - EMA)) scaled by a symmetric multiple of ATR; Awesome Oscillator (SMA of hl2, short minus long) scaled by a symmetric multiple of the long ATR; Momentum (close minus close N bars ago) scaled by a symmetric multiple of ATR; Fisher Transform of hl2 scaled over a fixed symmetric range
Calculation: Signal = EMA of Composite, with a longer smoothing length than the Composite's weighted moving average
Calculation: HTFComposite = Composite computed on bars of a higher timeframe than the chart, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: BullVotes = count of {RSI, MFI, Stochastic, scaled CCI} above 50; BearVotes = count of the same four below 50
Calculation: Pivot low of Composite = Composite 5 bars ago is below Composite 6 and 7 bars ago and at or below Composite 4 and 3 bars ago; Pivot high is the mirror (above, and at or above)
Long: (Composite crosses above Signal AND Composite < oversold level AND HTFComposite > 50 AND BullVotes >= 3) OR (bullish divergence: Pivot low of Composite AND there exists an earlier bar, at least a minimum number of bars before the pivot bar and within a maximum lookback, where the pivot bar's low < that bar's low AND the pivot bar's Composite > that bar's Composite)
Short: (Composite crosses below Signal AND Composite > overbought level AND HTFComposite < 50 AND BearVotes >= 3) OR (bearish divergence: Pivot high of Composite AND there exists an earlier bar, at least a minimum number of bars before the pivot bar and within a maximum lookback, where the pivot bar's high > that bar's high AND the pivot bar's Composite < that bar's Composite)
