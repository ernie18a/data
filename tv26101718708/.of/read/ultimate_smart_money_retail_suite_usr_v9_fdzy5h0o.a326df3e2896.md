Calculation: Safe volume = volume if cumulative volume since the first bar is positive; otherwise a constant fallback volume.
Calculation: Volume delta = safe volume when close > open, negative safe volume when close < open, otherwise zero. Cumulative volume delta is the running sum of volume delta, reset before adding the current delta at a configurable periodic bar interval. Delta momentum = cumulative volume delta minus its value a configurable number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Spread = either high minus low or absolute close minus open, selected by a parameter. A stop-volume event occurs when cumulative volume is positive AND close < open AND safe volume > its moving average times a high-volume multiplier AND spread < its moving average times a narrow-spread multiplier. A buying-climax event occurs when cumulative volume is positive AND close > open AND safe volume > its moving average times an ultra-high-volume multiplier AND spread > its moving average times a wide-spread multiplier. Both averages use the same lookback; the ultra-high-volume multiplier exceeds the high-volume multiplier.
Calculation: Smart-money index = Hull moving average of the mean of the rolling percent ranks of MFI of close, money flow, and delta momentum, all ranked over the same normalization lookback. Money flow = rolling sum of safe volume times ((close minus low) minus (high minus close)) divided by spread, divided by the rolling sum of safe volume; protect both denominators against zero. If cumulative volume is not positive, replace MFI with RSI of close using the MFI lookback and replace money flow with RSI of close using the money-flow lookback divided by the RSI scale maximum.
Calculation: Retail index = Hull moving average of the mean of the rolling percent rank of RSI of close and the rolling percent rank of the rolling percent rank of close. The outer ranks use the smart-money normalization lookback; the Hull average uses the smart-money smoothing length. RSI and the inner price rank use the same lookback.
Calculation: Upper and lower adaptive zones for each index = its upper and lower nearest-rank percentiles over the normalization lookback.
Calculation: Fear and greed = Hull moving average of the mean of RSI of the previous close, inverted rolling percent rank of previous-bar ATR, previous-bar directional volume score, and stochastic oscillator computed from previous-bar close, high, and low. The directional volume score is the previous safe volume divided by its moving average, capped at a configurable maximum and scaled to the oscillator range when previous close > previous open; otherwise subtract that score from the oscillator maximum. Protect the volume-average denominator with a positive floor. The ATR rank uses a longer lookback than the component indicators.
Calculation: A confirmed price pivot is a close that is a pivot high or low with equal configurable left and right confirmation lengths. Compare it with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type. Pivot-index values and delta momentum are sampled at the pivot bar, while adaptive retail zones are sampled at the confirmation bar. The event count is the number of relevant VSA events in a configurable window ending at the pivot bar.
Calculation: A signal's cooldown passes if it has never occurred or if the number of bars since its last occurrence exceeds its configurable cooldown length. Each signal type has its own last-occurrence bar.
