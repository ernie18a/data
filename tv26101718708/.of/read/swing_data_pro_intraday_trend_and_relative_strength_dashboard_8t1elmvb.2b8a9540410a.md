Calculation: Daily OHLC series = OHLC from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, mapped onto each chart bar; historical offsets refer to chart bars after mapping, not previous daily bars. Evaluate candle patterns only when that daily bar is confirmed.
Calculation: Body[i] = abs(daily close[i] - daily open[i]); range[i] = daily high[i] - daily low[i]; lower wick[i] = min(daily open[i], daily close[i]) - daily low[i]; upper wick[i] = daily high[i] - max(daily open[i], daily close[i]). Index 0 is the current chart bar and positive indices are prior chart bars.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of chart-bar typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset each session.
Calculation: Relative volume = rounded percentage of current chart-bar volume divided by the average volume of preceding chart bars, or, for intraday charts, rounded percentage of cumulative session volume divided by its historical average at the same session offset.
Calculation: Up/down volume ratio = sum of chart-bar volume where close exceeds the preceding close divided by sum of chart-bar volume where close is below the preceding close, over the same lookback. If down volume is zero and up volume is positive, use a high fallback value; if both are zero, the ratio is unavailable.
Long: Close >= the open of the forming higher-timeframe daily bar AND close >= session VWAP.
Short: Close < the open of the forming higher-timeframe daily bar AND close < session VWAP.
Long: Close >= the open of the forming higher-timeframe daily bar AND close >= session VWAP AND relative volume >= bullish relative-volume threshold AND up/down volume ratio >= bullish ratio threshold, substituting zero for unavailable relative volume or ratio.
