Calculation: Jump pressure = previous jump pressure × decay + weighted rebound event score, where rebound event score is positive candle body ÷ (highest high − lowest low over a lookback) × 100, plus a tiered add-on when that ratio meets one of three ascending thresholds; score is zero below the lowest threshold.
Calculation: Small pulse oscillator = recency-weighted count of confirmed local peaks in jump pressure whose amplitude (peak minus the lowest jump pressure in a preceding lookback) meets a lower threshold, minus the longer moving average of that count. Weight newer peaks more; use only bars from the current day.
Calculation: Large pulse oscillator = the same calculation as the small pulse oscillator, using a higher amplitude threshold.
Long: After the large pulse oscillator makes a lower low than every bar in the preceding lookback, jump pressure rises versus the prior bar; this signal is eligible once per new low and remains armed until it triggers or a newer low rearms it.
Short: While tracking the highest small pulse oscillator value since the prior reset, it falls from that candidate high by at least a retracement threshold; after confirming a peak, rearm peak tracking only after the oscillator rises from its subsequent lowest value by at least that threshold.
