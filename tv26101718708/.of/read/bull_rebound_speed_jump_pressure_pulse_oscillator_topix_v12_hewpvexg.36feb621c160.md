Calculation: Rebound pressure = Initialize to zero. On each bar, calculate the positive part of close minus open as a percentage of the difference between the highest high and lowest low over a lookback including the current bar; use zero when that difference is zero. If this percentage reaches any of three ordered thresholds, add the score associated with the highest threshold reached; otherwise use zero. Multiply the previous pressure by a decay factor, add the resulting score times a weight, and clamp the result to zero or above.
Calculation: Pulse oscillator = A pulse occurs when the previous bar's rebound pressure is greater than that of the bar before it and greater than or equal to the current pressure, and the previous pressure minus the lowest pressure in a lookback ending two bars ago reaches an amplitude threshold. Require enough bars for that lookback. For a rolling window entirely within the current calendar day, sum pulse indicators weighted from the window length for the current bar down to one for the oldest bar, and divide by the average weight. The oscillator is this weighted count minus its simple moving average, with the average ignoring unavailable counts. The small-pulse oscillator uses a lower amplitude threshold than the large-pulse oscillator.
Calculation: Peak search = Initially searching. While the small-pulse oscillator is available and searching, track its highest value, replacing the candidate on equal values. When it falls below the candidate by at least a retracement threshold, confirm a peak and stop searching; initialize a subsequent valley to the current oscillator. While stopped, track the lowest oscillator value, replacing the valley on equal values. When the oscillator rises above the valley by at least the same threshold, resume searching with the current oscillator as the candidate. If the oscillator is unavailable, clear the candidate and valley and resume searching.
Calculation: Buy readiness = Initially false. Set readiness to true whenever the large-pulse oscillator is below its lowest value over a lookback of preceding bars. After a long decision, set readiness to false; a new low can set it true again on the same bar as a long decision.
Long: Buy readiness is true AND rebound pressure is greater than its previous-bar value.
Short: A peak is confirmed by the peak search on the current bar.
