Calculation: Anchor series = the last completed bar of the selected higher timeframe, or the last completed intrabar of the selected lower timeframe, mapped to each chart bar; use chart bars when the timeframes match.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = an anchor-bar high or low confirmed after a configurable number of right-hand bars against configurable numbers of bars on its left and right. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, and measure their separation in anchor bars.
Long: Anchor RSI >= momentum midpoint.
Short: Anchor RSI < momentum midpoint.
Long: EMA of anchor Money Flow Index >= money-flow midpoint.
Short: EMA of anchor Money Flow Index < money-flow midpoint.
Long: Anchor positive directional index >= anchor negative directional index.
Short: Anchor positive directional index < anchor negative directional index.
Long: A confirmed anchor price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND their separation <= maximum pivot lookback AND anchor RSI at the new pivot > anchor RSI at the preceding pivot.
Short: A confirmed anchor price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND their separation <= maximum pivot lookback AND anchor RSI at the new pivot < anchor RSI at the preceding pivot.
Long: A confirmed anchor price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND their separation <= maximum pivot lookback AND anchor ADX at the new pivot < anchor ADX one anchor bar before that pivot.
Short: A confirmed anchor price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND their separation <= maximum pivot lookback AND anchor ADX at the new pivot < anchor ADX one anchor bar before that pivot.
