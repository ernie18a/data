Calculation: Previous month/week/day high and low = high and low of the last completed bar of the corresponding higher timeframe.
Calculation: OHP = highest high and OLP = lowest low among chart bars from the start of the current higher-timeframe period through the current bar; this higher-timeframe period is still forming.
Calculation: C1 body high = max(open, close) and C1 body low = min(open, close), measured on the C1 bar.
Long: A bullish C1 occurred 2–4 bars ago, where its low <= previous month low OR previous week low OR previous day low OR OLP, and its close > open; the previous bar is a bullish C2 occurring 1–3 bars after that C1 and satisfies the selected grade: First Class, close >= C1 body low + 0.3 × (C1 body high − C1 body low) AND close <= C1 body low + 0.5 × (C1 body high − C1 body low); Second Class, open < C1 body low AND close > C1 body high; or Third Class, open < C1 body low AND close <= C1 low; current low < previous bar low AND current close > previous bar low AND manual bias is not Sell.
Short: A bearish C1 occurred 2–4 bars ago, where its high >= previous month high OR previous week high OR previous day high OR OHP, and its close < open; the previous bar is a bearish C2 occurring 1–3 bars after that C1 and satisfies the selected grade: First Class, close <= C1 body high − 0.3 × (C1 body high − C1 body low) AND close >= C1 body high − 0.5 × (C1 body high − C1 body low); Second Class, open > C1 body high AND close < C1 body low; or Third Class, open > C1 body high AND close >= C1 high; current high > previous bar high AND current close < previous bar high AND manual bias is not Buy.
