Calculation: Swing levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks; retain the most recent bounded number of each. A level becomes broken on the first bar whose close is above a high level or below a low level, respectively.
Calculation: Order block candidate = the most recent prior bearish candle within a search lookback for a bullish candidate, or bullish candle for a bearish candidate. Its top and bottom are either its high and low or its body maximum and minimum. If volume confirmation is enabled, its volume must exceed its own bar's volume SMA multiplied by a threshold.
Calculation: Active zones = accepted order block and breaker block candidates, stored separately by type in creation order. Reject a candidate if its midpoint is less than ATR multiplied by a spacing threshold from an active zone of the same type. After processing creations and violations, remove the oldest zones of each type exceeding the active cap. A bullish zone is touched when low is within its inclusive boundaries; a bearish zone is touched when high is within its inclusive boundaries. Record touches before checking violations, including on the creation bar. Remove a bullish zone when close is below its bottom, or low is below its bottom in wick mode; remove a bearish zone when close is above its top, or high is above its top in wick mode. A violated order block becomes an opposite-direction breaker candidate with the same boundaries unless cancellation of untouched zones is enabled and the order block has never been touched. Breaker candidates are subject to the same spacing filter; violated breaker blocks are removed without conversion. Process bullish order block creation, bearish order block creation, then violations and conversions in reverse creation order.
Long: Close > an unbroken confirmed swing high AND a bullish order block candidate exists and passes volume confirmation when enabled AND its midpoint is at least the spacing threshold from every active bullish order block midpoint.
Short: Close < an unbroken confirmed swing low AND a bearish order block candidate exists and passes volume confirmation when enabled AND its midpoint is at least the spacing threshold from every active bearish order block midpoint.
Long: An active bearish order block is violated AND untouched-zone cancellation is disabled OR an active bearish order block is violated AND it has been touched; AND its midpoint is at least the spacing threshold from every active bullish breaker block midpoint.
Short: An active bullish order block is violated AND untouched-zone cancellation is disabled OR an active bullish order block is violated AND it has been touched; AND its midpoint is at least the spacing threshold from every active bearish breaker block midpoint.
