Calculation: Prior liquidity low/high = lowest low/high over the liquidity lookback ending on the previous chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < prior liquidity low AND close > prior liquidity low; bearish sweep = high > prior liquidity high AND close < prior liquidity high.
Calculation: Fibonacci range = highest high minus lowest low over the Fibonacci lookback including the current bar. The bullish zone runs from highest high minus the larger retracement fraction of the range to highest high minus the smaller retracement fraction; the bearish zone runs from lowest low plus the smaller fraction to lowest low plus the larger fraction. Expand both ends by an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish momentum = close > fast EMA AND close > session VWAP of typical price AND RSI > midpoint AND MACD line > signal line AND ADX > threshold. Bearish momentum = close < fast EMA AND close < session VWAP of typical price AND RSI < midpoint AND MACD line < signal line AND ADX > threshold.
Calculation: Bullish stack = fast EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > slow EMA AND close > longer-term trend EMA. Bearish stack = fast EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < slow EMA AND close < longer-term trend EMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA. Previous-period high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe period.
Calculation: Directional quality = weighted sum of higher-timeframe trend agreement, sweep or sweep filter disabled, close in corresponding Fibonacci zone or Fibonacci filter disabled, unavailable external confirmation, corresponding EMA stack, corresponding momentum, and session filter satisfied. The respective weights decrease from trend to sweep to Fibonacci; external confirmation has the same weight as Fibonacci; stack and momentum have equal smaller weights; session has the smallest weight. External confirmation is unavailable from the supplied data and receives its full weight. Compare quality with a mode-dependent threshold; the balanced threshold is at least the base threshold and the strict threshold is at least the balanced threshold.
Calculation: Common entry filters = the bar is closed AND its UTC time is outside every enabled news-block window AND the session filter is disabled or its time is within an allowed session AND the chase filter is disabled or absolute close-to-fast-EMA distance <= an ATR multiple. News windows wrap across midnight.
Calculation: Long entry filters = common entry filters AND the trend filter is disabled or higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA AND the sweep filter is disabled or bullish sweep AND the Fibonacci filter is disabled or close is in the bullish zone AND the previous-period filter is disabled or previous-period high is unavailable or close < previous-period high minus a buffer or close > previous-period high plus a buffer. Short entry filters = common entry filters AND the trend filter is disabled or higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA AND the sweep filter is disabled or bearish sweep AND the Fibonacci filter is disabled or close is in the bearish zone AND the previous-period filter is disabled or previous-period low is unavailable or close > previous-period low plus a buffer or close < previous-period low minus a buffer.
Calculation: Long trigger = bullish sweep OR close crosses above fast EMA AND close > medium EMA OR close > highest high over a breakout lookback ending on the previous bar AND bullish momentum. Short trigger = bearish sweep OR close crosses below fast EMA AND close < medium EMA OR close < lowest low over a breakout lookback ending on the previous bar AND bearish momentum.
Calculation: At entry, store the close as entry price and set the stop to the prior liquidity low minus a buffer for a long or prior liquidity high plus a buffer for a short if swing stops are enabled and that level is available and lies beyond the entry in the loss direction; otherwise set it an adjustable fixed distance or entry-bar ATR multiple from entry in the loss direction. Set the first and final targets at independently adjustable fixed distances or entry-bar ATR multiples from entry in the profit direction; the final target is farther than the first. Keep these levels fixed for the trade.
Calculation: First target previously hit = at least one bar after entry and before the current bar had high >= the long first target or low <= the short first target. Best price = maximum of entry price and highs since entry for a long, or minimum of entry price and lows since entry for a short, excluding the entry bar and including the current bar.
Long: low < prior liquidity low AND close > prior liquidity low.
Short: high > prior liquidity high AND close < prior liquidity high.
Long: close >= bullish Fibonacci zone lower bound AND close <= bullish Fibonacci zone upper bound.
Short: close >= bearish Fibonacci zone lower bound AND close <= bearish Fibonacci zone upper bound.
Long: position side = flat AND long entry filters satisfied AND long quality >= mode-dependent threshold AND long quality > short quality AND long trigger.
Short: position side = flat AND short entry filters satisfied AND short quality >= mode-dependent threshold AND short quality > long quality AND short trigger.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (high >= final target OR low <= stop OR first target previously hit AND ((early pullback exit enabled AND close <= best price minus pullback distance) OR close < fast EMA OR RSI < midpoint OR MACD line < signal line)).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (low <= final target OR high >= stop OR first target previously hit AND ((early pullback exit enabled AND close >= best price plus pullback distance) OR close > fast EMA OR RSI > midpoint OR MACD line > signal line)).
Calculation: If final target and stop are both hit on the same bar, execute the final-target exit; otherwise stop takes priority over an early exit.
