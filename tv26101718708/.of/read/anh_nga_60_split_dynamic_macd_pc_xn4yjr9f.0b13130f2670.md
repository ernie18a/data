Calculation: WaveTrend wt1 = EMA(( hlc3 - EMA(hlc3, n) ) / (0.015 * EMA(abs(hlc3 - EMA(hlc3, n)), n)), m), with smoothing length m longer than channel length n; wt2 = SMA(wt1, p), with p short
Calculation: Basis = SMA(close, L); SD = standard deviation of close over the same L
Calculation: Stop level (long) = Basis - k*SD; Stop level (short) = Basis + k*SD, where k is larger than the zone multipliers 1.0 and 1.5 used below
Calculation: HTF MACD histogram = histogram of standard MACD(close) on bars of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; two such timeframes are used (HTF_A shorter, HTF_B longer)
Calculation: Fast MA = SMA(close) and Slow MA = SMA(close) with Fast MA lookback shorter than Slow MA lookback; ATR = standard ATR
Calculation: R = abs(entry price - stop level taken at the entry bar); target = entry price +/- R*rr (+ for long, - for short); partial target = entry price +/- 0.5*abs(target - entry price)
Long: flat AND bar datetime inside the allowed daily trading window AND bars since last exit > small wait N1 AND (wt1 crossed above wt2 with wt1 < 0 on the current bar or within the previous lookback bars, where at that cross bar the previous closed trade was not short OR bars since its exit > cooldown N2, with N2 > N1) AND NOT (HTF_A MACD histogram < 0 AND HTF_B MACD histogram < 0) AND close > Fast MA AND close > Slow MA AND close - Slow MA >= min distance AND abs(close - Fast MA) < ATR * limit AND close > Basis AND close <= Basis + 1.5*SD AND abs(close - long stop level) <= max stop distance; enter at the bar close
Short: flat AND bar datetime inside the allowed daily trading window AND bars since last exit > small wait N1 AND (wt1 crossed below wt2 with wt1 > 0 on the current bar or within the previous lookback bars, where at that cross bar the previous closed trade was not long OR bars since its exit > cooldown N2, with N2 > N1) AND NOT (HTF_A MACD histogram > 0 AND HTF_B MACD histogram > 0) AND close < Fast MA AND close < Slow MA AND Slow MA - close >= min distance AND abs(close - Fast MA) < ATR * limit AND close < Basis AND close >= Basis - 1.5*SD AND abs(close - short stop level) <= max stop distance; enter at the bar close
Exit: long, partial (closes only the partial-size portion, then the stop becomes the entry price), first time since entry that high >= partial target
Exit: short, partial (closes only the partial-size portion, then the stop becomes the entry price), first time since entry that low <= partial target
Exit: long, low <= stop (entry-time stop level, or entry price after the partial exit) OR high >= target
Exit: short, high >= stop (entry-time stop level, or entry price after the partial exit) OR low <= target
