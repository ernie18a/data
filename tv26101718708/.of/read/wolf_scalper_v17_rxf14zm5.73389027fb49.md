Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the specified number of bars on each side satisfying the pivot condition; recognize it only when the right-side bars have completed. Sweep pivots and trend pivots have independently configurable lengths.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the latest confirmed sweep pivot low AND close above that low AND (min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, a small range floor) > minimum wick fraction. Bearish sweep = high above the latest confirmed sweep pivot high AND close below that high AND (high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, a small range floor) > minimum wick fraction. Update pivots before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars ago AND their difference >= minimum gap size; its zone is [high two bars ago, low]. Bearish gap = high < low two bars ago AND their difference >= minimum gap size; its zone is [high, low two bars ago]. Keep the most recent configured maximum number of zones separately for each direction. A bullish gap becomes a bearish inverted gap when close < its lower boundary; a bearish gap becomes a bullish inverted gap when close > its upper boundary. On subsequent bars remove an inverted bullish gap when high > its upper boundary, and remove an inverted bearish gap when low < its lower boundary. A bar overlaps a retained zone when low <= its upper boundary AND high >= its lower boundary.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the previous bar's body zone when close > previous high AND close - open > short-period ATR times an order-block displacement multiplier. Bearish order block = the previous bar's body zone when close < previous low AND open - close > short-period ATR times an order-block displacement multiplier. Keep the most recent configured maximum number separately by direction. Remove a bullish block when close < lower boundary + zone height times (1 - mitigation fraction); remove a bearish block when close > upper boundary - zone height times (1 - mitigation fraction). A bar overlaps a retained block when low <= its upper boundary AND high >= its lower boundary.
Calculation: Trend direction = initially neutral; after updating confirmed trend pivots, set bullish when close > latest trend pivot high AND direction is not bullish; then set bearish when close < latest trend pivot low AND direction is not bearish.
Calculation: Bullish close-sequence event = close > previous close AND previous close > close two bars ago AND the last close-sequence event was not bullish AND the configured minimum number of bars has elapsed since that event. Bearish close-sequence event = close < previous close AND previous close < close two bars ago AND the last close-sequence event was not bearish AND the configured minimum number of bars has elapsed since that event. Either event can occur if no prior event exists.
Calculation: Discount boundary = lowest low over the range lookback + one quarter of that range; premium boundary = lowest low over the range lookback + three quarters of that range. The range includes the current bar.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish pattern = close > open AND, if the displacement filter is enabled, abs(close - open) > short-period ATR times the displacement multiplier AND ((sweep patterns enabled AND bullish sweep AND close - latest sweep pivot low <= short-period ATR times maximum entry-distance multiplier) OR (inverted-gap patterns enabled AND overlap a bullish inverted gap) OR overlap a bullish order block). Confirmed bearish pattern = close < open AND the same optional displacement condition AND ((sweep patterns enabled AND bearish sweep AND latest sweep pivot high - close <= short-period ATR times maximum entry-distance multiplier) OR (inverted-gap patterns enabled AND overlap a bearish inverted gap) OR overlap a bearish order block).
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = sum of one point each for: confirmed bullish pattern; bullish sweep on the current bar or within the sweep recency window; bullish trend direction; overlap of a bullish gap, bullish inverted gap, or bullish order block; bullish close-sequence event on the current bar; close < discount boundary; close > the most recently recorded session-open price. Bearish confluence count is the directional mirror, using close > premium boundary and close < the most recently recorded session-open price. Record the session-open price as the open of the confirmed bar whose New York local time equals the configured session-open time.
