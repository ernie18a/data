Calculation: DeltaWave = EMA(S) of [100 * (EMA(upVol, L) - EMA(downVol, L)) / EMA(volume, L)], where upVol = volume if close>open else 0, downVol = volume if close<open else 0, the same lookback L is used in all three EMAs, and the smoothing period S is shorter than L (if volume is 0 use 1 in the denominator EMA)
Calculation: WaveHighPivot (confirmed on the current bar, pivot bar = previous bar) = DeltaWave[1] > DeltaWave[2] AND DeltaWave < DeltaWave[1] AND DeltaWave[1] > UpperFilter, where UpperFilter = a positive fraction (<1) of a positive upper threshold, or 0 if the extreme-zone filter is off
Calculation: WaveLowPivot (confirmed on the current bar, pivot bar = previous bar) = DeltaWave[1] < DeltaWave[2] AND DeltaWave > DeltaWave[1] AND DeltaWave[1] < LowerFilter, where LowerFilter = the same positive fraction (<1) of a negative lower threshold, or 0 if the extreme-zone filter is off
Long: WaveLowPivot is true on the current bar AND low[1] < the low at the previous WaveLowPivot bar AND DeltaWave[1] > DeltaWave at the previous WaveLowPivot bar AND the number of bars between the two pivot bars >= a minimum spacing (the previous WaveLowPivot is the most recent earlier pivot, whether or not it produced a signal; enter on the current bar)
Short: WaveHighPivot is true on the current bar AND high[1] > the high at the previous WaveHighPivot bar AND DeltaWave[1] < DeltaWave at the previous WaveHighPivot bar AND the number of bars between the two pivot bars >= a minimum spacing (the previous WaveHighPivot is the most recent earlier pivot, whether or not it produced a signal; enter on the current bar)
