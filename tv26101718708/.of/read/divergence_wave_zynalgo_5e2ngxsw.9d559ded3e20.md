Calculation: Delta wave = EMA of 100 × (EMA(volume when close > open, long lookback; otherwise 0) − EMA(volume when close < open, long lookback; otherwise 0)) ÷ EMA(volume when volume > 0; otherwise 1, long lookback), using 1 if the denominator is not positive; then apply a shorter-lookback EMA to the result.
Calculation: Wave-pivot filter boundaries = 40% of the corresponding upper and lower exhaustion thresholds when the extreme-zone filter is enabled; otherwise both boundaries are zero.
Long: On the chart bar confirming a pivot one bar earlier, the pivot's delta wave is below the delta wave on the chart bar before it AND below the delta wave on the confirmation bar AND below the lower wave-pivot filter boundary AND its low is below the low at the preceding qualifying wave low pivot AND its delta wave is above the delta wave at that preceding pivot AND the pivot bars are at least the minimum pivot spacing apart.
Short: On the chart bar confirming a pivot one bar earlier, the pivot's delta wave is above the delta wave on the chart bar before it AND above the delta wave on the confirmation bar AND above the upper wave-pivot filter boundary AND its high is above the high at the preceding qualifying wave high pivot AND its delta wave is below the delta wave at that preceding pivot AND the pivot bars are at least the minimum pivot spacing apart.
