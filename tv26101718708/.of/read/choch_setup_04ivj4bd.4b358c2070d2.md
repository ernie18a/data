Calculation: Structure pivots = Maintain a leg initialized down. A high n bars ago greater than every high since then changes the leg to down; otherwise, a low n bars ago lower than every low since then changes it to up. A change to down confirms that earlier high; a change to up confirms that earlier low. Retain the latest confirmed high and low, each unbroken until replaced or broken.
Calculation: Structure break = On a completed bar, the selected source, close or the corresponding high/low, crosses above the latest unbroken high or below the latest unbroken low. If both breaks occur on the same bar, retain the upward break when close >= open, otherwise the downward break. The initial direction is neutral; each break sets direction to its direction. A reversal from the opposite direction is a change of character; a break in the existing direction is a continuation. The active regime is the direction of the latest change of character.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = Apply the structure-pivot calculation to the last completed higher-timeframe bar; a close crossing an unbroken pivot high sets bias bullish and a close crossing an unbroken pivot low sets it bearish. Initialize bias bullish.
Calculation: Point of control = Divide the range of chart-bar (high + low) / 2 prices within the last completed higher-timeframe bar into equal price bins; sum chart-bar volume in each bin and select the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, resolving ties toward the lowest bin.
Calculation: Change-of-character zone = For a bullish change of character, use the latest structure low, or the current low if unavailable, as the lower endpoint and the current high as the upper endpoint. For a bearish change of character, use the current low as the lower endpoint and the latest structure high, or current high if unavailable, as the upper endpoint. The bullish zone lies between a lower and a higher retracement fraction measured upward from the lower endpoint; the bearish zone lies between those fractions measured downward from the upper endpoint. Replace the zone on each change of character.
Calculation: Fair value gap = A bullish gap has current low > high two bars earlier and previous close > previous low + a threshold times previous ATR; its bounds are the earlier high and current low. A bearish gap has current high < low two bars earlier and previous close < previous high - a threshold times previous ATR; its bounds are current high and the earlier low. Register a gap one bar after its formation. Before evaluating a bounce, invalidate a bullish gap when the selected mitigation source, either min(open, close), low, or low compared with the gap midpoint, is below its lower bound or midpoint respectively; invalidate a bearish gap symmetrically using max(open, close) or high above its upper bound or midpoint. Optionally convert invalidated gaps into opposite-direction breakers, remove older overlapping same-direction gaps when a newer gap is registered, and retain only the configured maximum number of gaps. Breakers are excluded from bounces.
Calculation: Rejected block = In the active regime, confirm a symmetric pivot after the change-of-character bar. For a bullish block, the pivot is a low and its lower wick extends from the low to min(open, close); for a bearish block, the pivot is a high and its upper wick extends from max(open, close) to the high. Require positive candle range, wick / range >= a threshold, and wick >= a threshold times ATR at the pivot, unless the ATR filter is disabled. The wick is the block. Before retest, replace it with any newly qualified block. After confirmation, invalidate it on close beyond its outer edge; otherwise arm it on a later bar entirely beyond its inner edge in the regime direction, then identify the first subsequent bar overlapping the block as its retest. Reset the sequence on a new change of character.
Calculation: Sequence gap = After a rejected-block retest, record the most recent same-direction continuation break and the most recent newly registered same-direction fair value gap. Associate them when their bar indices differ by no more than a window. Starting after both event bars, invalidate the associated bullish gap if min(open, close) < its lower edge in close-mitigation mode, otherwise if low < its lower edge; reverse these inequalities and use max(open, close) or high for a bearish gap. On invalidation resume searching for an association; otherwise the first bar with low < the bullish gap upper edge or high > the bearish gap lower edge completes the sequence. Reset the sequence on a new change of character.
Calculation: Liquidity trend line = At a change of character, retain the preceding regime's lowest low and highest high and their times. For a bullish regime start at that low; for a bearish regime start at that high. Connect it to the first subsequently confirmed structure pivot whose pivot bar is after the change of character and whose low is higher than the starting low or whose high is lower than the starting high, respectively. Extend the line linearly in time; only the first break after the validation bar counts.
Long: An upward structure break occurs with previous structure direction bearish OR bullish.
Short: A downward structure break occurs with previous structure direction bullish OR bearish.
Long: A symmetric pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A symmetric pivot high is confirmed.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias = bearish.
Long: Active regime = bullish.
Short: Active regime = bearish.
Long: Close >= point of control when the point-of-control display is enabled and a point of control exists OR active regime >= neutral otherwise.
Short: Close < point of control when the point-of-control display is enabled and a point of control exists OR active regime = bearish otherwise.
Long: Active regime = bullish AND close > change-of-character range midpoint AND higher-timeframe bias = bullish AND RSI >= its midpoint.
Short: Active regime = bearish AND close < change-of-character range midpoint AND higher-timeframe bias = bearish AND RSI < its midpoint.
Long: Active regime = bullish AND close > open AND low < previous low AND low <= upper edge AND high >= lower edge of an active bullish fair value gap.
Short: Active regime = bearish AND close < open AND high > previous high AND high >= lower edge AND low <= upper edge of an active bearish fair value gap.
Long: A bullish rejected block is qualified OR its armed block is retested OR its same-direction continuation break and fair value gap are associated OR its sequence gap completes.
Short: A bearish rejected block is qualified OR its armed block is retested OR its same-direction continuation break and fair value gap are associated OR its sequence gap completes.
Long: After a bullish change-of-character bar, price overlaps its zone AND on that bar or a later bar close > the zone's upper edge AND no earlier such exit from that zone has occurred.
Short: After a bearish change-of-character bar, price overlaps its zone AND on that bar or a later bar close < the zone's lower edge AND no earlier such exit from that zone has occurred.
Long: In a bearish regime, the selected source, close or high, > the validated liquidity trend line for the first time after validation.
Short: In a bullish regime, the selected source, close or low, < the validated liquidity trend line for the first time after validation.
