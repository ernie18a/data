Calculation: Prior dealing-range high/low = high/low of the selected previous day, week, or month, using the last completed bar of that higher timeframe.
Calculation: Dealing-range midpoint = average of the prior dealing-range high and low.
Calculation: Midnight open = open of the bar beginning at 00:00 New York time on the current day.
Long: Close < dealing-range midpoint OR close > midnight open
Short: Close >= dealing-range midpoint OR close < midnight open
