Calculation: Rolling pivot pairs = For each of two chart-bar lookbacks, one shorter and one longer, clear the pivot list and set direction to neutral at the start of each forming higher-timeframe reset period. A bar is a high candidate when its high is the highest in the lookback, and a low candidate when its low is the lowest in the lookback. Set direction to high if only the high qualifies, to low if only the low qualifies, and otherwise retain it. On a direction change, prepend the corresponding candidate and its bar index to the list. Otherwise, use the high candidate if direction is high and the low candidate otherwise; if it exists, prepend it when the list is empty, or replace the newest pivot when it is more extreme in the current direction. Retain the seven newest pivots. When at least four pivots exist, select the two newest high pivots and the two newest low pivots as separate pairs.
Long: For either lookback and either pivot pair, the newer pivot's bar index differs from the older pivot's bar index AND the newer pivot's price > the older pivot's price.
Short: For either lookback and either pivot pair, the newer pivot's bar index differs from the older pivot's bar index AND the newer pivot's price <= the older pivot's price.
