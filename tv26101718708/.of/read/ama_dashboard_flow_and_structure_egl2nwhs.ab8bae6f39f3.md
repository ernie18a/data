Calculation: Adaptive moving average = Kaufman adaptive moving average of (high + low + close) / 3. Its efficiency ratio is the absolute change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar changes over that lookback, or zero when the sum is zero. Square the efficiency-ratio interpolation between the fast and slow smoothing constants; seed the average with the source and recursively update it using that squared constant. Both averages use the same fast and slow smoothing parameters; one has a longer lookback.
Calculation: Trend direction = bullish when close is above both adaptive moving averages, bearish when close is below both, and neutral otherwise. Treat the trend as neutral until both lookback windows have filled.
Calculation: Flow = the moving average of volume multiplied by (2 × close location within the high-low range − 1), divided by the moving average of volume over the same lookback. Close location is (close − low) / (high − low), or one-half when high equals low. Replace missing volume with zero. Flow is zero when average volume is zero. Volume is available once any bar has positive volume.
Calculation: Trend score = a weighted sum of strength score, momentum score, the rate-of-change bonus, and the flow score. Divide by the maximum possible weighted sum, excluding the flow component until volume is available. The strength score uses the angle in degrees of the arctangent of the change in (high + low + close) / 3 over a lookback, divided by that lookback and current ATR. It is zero when the angle opposes or equals zero on the trend side; otherwise assign ordered scores for weak, improving weak, moderate, improving moderate, strong, and very strong. Moderate and strong require the absolute angle to exceed their respective ordered thresholds; improving or very strong additionally requires the absolute angle to exceed its previous value. The momentum score uses the trend-side change in angle over a momentum lookback, its previous-bar value, and the average sign of each consecutive trend-side angle change over that lookback. Reverse the consistency sign when momentum is negative. Classify momentum as strongly strengthening, strengthening, weakly strengthening, stable, weakly weakening, weakening, or strongly weakening: positive or negative momentum is strong when its absolute value is increasing and consistency exceeds the strong-consistency threshold; otherwise it is strengthening or weakening when consistency exceeds that threshold, weakly so when consistency is positive, and stable otherwise. Assign decreasing scores in the stated order; unavailable momentum is stable. Add the rate-of-change bonus when the sign of close rate of change equals the trend direction. For flow, use zero when volume is unavailable; otherwise assign a weighted base score for flow with the trend, against the trend, or neutral. Flow is with or against the trend when flow has the corresponding sign and close is respectively above or below session VWAP. The with-trend base score is higher when close location exceeds an upper threshold for bullish flow or falls below a lower threshold for bearish flow. Add a further weighted bonus for with-trend flow, using its higher value when absolute flow multiplied by capped current volume / average volume exceeds a threshold. The maximum score uses the highest strength, momentum, rate-of-change and available flow contributions.
Calculation: Setup = the normalized trend score meets the higher grade threshold AND normalized distance from the shorter-lookback adaptive moving average is at most both a near-distance threshold times the volatility ratio and a near-distance cap; OR the normalized trend score meets the lower grade threshold AND normalized distance exceeds the near-distance threshold times the volatility ratio but is at most both a stretched-distance threshold times the volatility ratio and a stretched-distance cap. Normalized distance is the absolute difference between close and the shorter-lookback average divided by ATR. The volatility ratio is ATR divided by its moving average, using the same lookback for both. Require both adaptive-average windows filled, a nonneutral trend, a defined score, angle and its value one bar beyond the momentum lookback, ATR, and volatility ratio.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral. A confirmed swing high or low is the strictly highest high or lowest low in a symmetric window around a candidate bar, confirmed after the right-hand window completes. Track the latest confirmed high and low and whether each has been broken; a newly confirmed swing resets its corresponding broken flag. In neutral state, a close above an unbroken latest high with a latest low available changes state to bullish and protects that low; otherwise a close below an unbroken latest low with a latest high available changes state to bearish and protects that high. In bullish state, a close below the protected low with a latest high available changes state to bearish and protects that high; otherwise a close above an unbroken latest high marks that high broken and, if a latest low exists, replaces the protected low with it. In bearish state, a close above the protected high with a latest low available changes state to bullish and protects that low; otherwise a close below an unbroken latest low marks that low broken and, if a latest high exists, replaces the protected high with it. On a reversal, also mark the latest swing on the reversal side broken if its break condition is met. Process newly confirmed swings before evaluating breaks.

Short: Volume is available AND flow < 0 AND close < session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3.

Short: Trend direction = bearish AND setup = true.
