Calculation: AMA = Kaufman adaptive moving average of hlc3; efficiency ratio = abs(hlc3 − hlc3[length]) / sum(length bars of abs(hlc3 − hlc3[1])); smoothing constant = (ER × (2/(fast end+1) − 2/(slow end+1)) + 2/(slow end+1))²; seed with hlc3, then recursively add smoothing constant × (hlc3 − prior AMA). Use separate short-term and long-term lengths.
Calculation: Trend = bullish when close is above both AMAs; bearish when close is below both; neutral otherwise. Do not grade until both AMA windows are available.
Calculation: Strength = angle in degrees of ((hlc3 − hlc3[lookback]) / lookback / ATR(14)); classify its direction and magnitude relative to the trend, its change from the prior bar, and the moderate and strong angle thresholds as in the source rules.
Calculation: Momentum = change in strength angle over a lookback, signed by trend direction; classify strengthening or weakening using its consistency across the momentum lookback and comparison with the prior momentum change.
Calculation: Flow = net volume / average volume over the flow lookback, where each bar's net volume is volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1), with zero-range bars assigned 0.5 for the close location; classify buying/selling only when net flow sign agrees with close being above/below VWAP(hlc3). If no positive volume is present, omit flow from the grade.
Calculation: Grade = score divided by maximum possible score, mapped to A++ / A+ / A / B+ / B at descending thresholds. Score is the weighted sum of trend-strength points (Very Strong 3.5, Strong 2.5, Improving Moderate 2, Moderate 1.5, Improving Weak 1, Weak 0.5), momentum points (Strongly Strengthening 3.5, Strengthening 2.5, Weakly Strengthening 2, Stable 1.5, Weakly Weakening 1, Weakening 0.5), a trend-aligned ROC bonus, and flow points (with trend: 2 or 3 for strong flow, against trend: 0.5, otherwise 1; add 1 or 0.5 for flow strength when aligned). Maximum score is the sum of maximum component scores. ROC is (close − close[ROC period])/close[ROC period] × 100. Require both AMAs ready, valid strength/momentum/ATR/volatility values, and non-neutral trend.
Calculation: Location distance = abs(close − short-term AMA)/ATR(14); volatility ratio = ATR(volatility length)/SMA(ATR(volatility length), volatility length).
Calculation: Setup = at-AMA when distance ≤ at-AMA threshold × volatility ratio; stretched when distance ≤ stretched threshold × volatility ratio. Setup grade must be A++/A+/A for at-AMA, or A++/A+/A/B+ for stretched; at-AMA additionally requires distance ≤ 1 ATR, stretched requires distance ≤ 1.5 ATR.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = unique highest high/lowest low among the swing length bars before and after the pivot bar; only available after the bars on both sides complete. Track the latest confirmed swing high/low and the protected level using the structure state: bullish state protects the latest swing low; bearish state protects the latest swing high. A bullish flip occurs from neutral when close breaks the latest unbroken swing high and a latest swing low exists, or from bearish when close breaks the protected high and a latest swing low exists. A bearish flip is the mirror condition.
Long: close > short-term AMA AND close > long-term AMA OR bullish trend setup (at-AMA or stretched) OR bullish structure flip.
Short: close < short-term AMA AND close < long-term AMA OR bearish trend setup (at-AMA or stretched) OR bearish structure flip.
