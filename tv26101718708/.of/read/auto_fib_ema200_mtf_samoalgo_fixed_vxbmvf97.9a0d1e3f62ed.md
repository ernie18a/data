Calculation: Multi-timeframe EMA agreement = Count how many of four EMAs of close, all using the same length on separately configured timeframes, are below or above the chart close. Each timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe. For a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar; for the chart timeframe use the current completed chart bar.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = A high or low with the configured number of bars on each side that qualifies as a pivot high or low, respectively; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete. Track the latest confirmed high and low and their pivot bar indices.
Calculation: Swing setup = While flat, create a direction's setup only if it has no active setup, its direction is enabled, the latest confirmed pivots are ordered low then high for long or high then low for short, their high minus low is positive and exceeds the setup-bar ATR times the minimum swing multiplier, and, if swing deduplication is enabled, the relevant pivot index differs from that recorded at the last entry of that direction. The relevant pivot is the low for long and the high for short. Freeze the pivot prices, setup-bar ATR, and creation bar. Increment setup age on the creation bar and each subsequent bar; deactivate when age exceeds the expiry limit. Deactivate the corresponding setup on an entry signal. Record the latest relevant pivot index at entry, even if it changed since setup creation.
Calculation: Entry level = For a long setup, frozen pivot low plus the frozen swing size times the entry retracement fraction; for a short setup, frozen pivot high minus the frozen swing size times that fraction.
Calculation: Stop and target = At the entry signal, freeze both levels. In ATR mode, the long stop and target are close minus ATR times the stop multiplier and close plus ATR times the target multiplier; the short stop and target reverse those signs. In swing mode, the long stop is the frozen pivot low minus setup-bar ATR times the stop buffer, and the short stop is the frozen pivot high plus that buffer. The swing-mode target is either close plus the signed distance from close to stop times the risk-reward multiplier, or the frozen pivot low plus swing size times the extension fraction for long and the frozen pivot high minus swing size times the extension fraction for short.
Calculation: Entry filters = The current bar is in the configured exchange-time session if the session filter is enabled; ATR exceeds its moving average times the volatility multiplier if the volatility filter is enabled; ADX exceeds its threshold if the ADX filter is enabled; bars since the last entry signal are at least the cooldown length, or no entry signal has occurred; and the number of entry signals since the exchange calendar day changed is below the daily limit if the daily limit is enabled.
Calculation: Reward-to-risk = For long, (target minus close) divided by absolute value of close minus stop; for short, (close minus target) divided by absolute value of stop minus close.
Long: Multi-timeframe EMA filter enabled AND count of chart close above the four timeframe EMAs >= agreement threshold.
Short: Multi-timeframe EMA filter enabled AND count of chart close below the four timeframe EMAs >= agreement threshold.
Long: Active long swing setup AND close > long entry level AND flat position AND entry filters pass AND (multi-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR count of chart close above the four timeframe EMAs >= agreement threshold) AND (minimum reward-to-risk filter disabled OR long reward-to-risk >= threshold).
Short: Active short swing setup AND close < short entry level AND flat position AND entry filters pass AND (multi-timeframe EMA filter disabled OR count of chart close below the four timeframe EMAs >= agreement threshold) AND (minimum reward-to-risk filter disabled OR short reward-to-risk >= threshold).
Exit: long, low <= frozen long stop OR high >= frozen long target.
Exit: short, high >= frozen short stop OR low <= frozen short target.
