Calculation: Volume profile = Over the chart-timeframe lookback, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into price bins; distribute each bar’s volume equally across bins intersecting its high-low range, optionally weighting older bars by an exponential decay.
Calculation: Low-volume pocket = A run of bins at or below the volume percentile threshold that contains a bin lower than both neighbors and whose volume is at most the configured ratio of their average; require the configured minimum bin count and ATR-relative width.
Calculation: Pocket classification = For each pocket, compare reversal and breakthrough weighted scores. Reversal score weights normalized far-wall volume, inverse vacuum, inverse Hurst, HTF confluence, and inverse touch density; breakthrough score weights inverse normalized far-wall volume, vacuum, Hurst, and touch density. Normalize each score by the sum of its included weights; classify as reversal when its score is at least the breakthrough score, otherwise breakthrough. For pockets above price, the far wall is above and the approach wall below; reverse these below price. Normalized far-wall volume = 2/(1+exp(-0.5 × far-wall volume / mean pocket-bin volume))-1. Vacuum = 1-min(far-wall volume / approach-wall volume, 1), or 1 if the far wall is zero. Hurst = clamp(log(R/S)/log(n/2), 0.01, 0.99), where R is the range of cumulative deviations from the lookback mean and S is the lookback standard deviation. Touch density = closes mapped to pocket bins over the touch-count lookback, divided by that lookback; inverse touch density = 1-min(10 × touch density, 1). HTF confluence is 1 when the pocket midpoint is within the ATR-relative confluence window of any enabled high or low from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Nearest classified pockets = Select the nearest reversal pocket and nearest breakthrough pocket separately above and below the current close.
Calculation: Rising volume = Current volume / 10-bar simple moving average of volume > 1.2. Falling volume = Current volume / 10-bar simple moving average of volume < 0.8. Bullish momentum = momentum filter disabled OR close > EMA. Bearish momentum = momentum filter disabled OR close < EMA. Strong up = velocity filter disabled OR rate of change > 0. Strong down = velocity filter disabled OR rate of change < 0.
Long: (Close crosses above the lower boundary of the nearest reversal pocket below and (falling volume OR NOT strong down)) OR (Close crosses above the lower boundary of the nearest breakthrough pocket above and bullish momentum and (rising volume OR strong up)); when close confirmation is disabled, use low <= the crossed boundary AND close > the boundary instead of the close crossover.
Short: (Close crosses below the upper boundary of the nearest reversal pocket above and (falling volume OR NOT strong up)) OR (Close crosses below the upper boundary of the nearest breakthrough pocket below and bearish momentum and (rising volume OR strong down)); when close confirmation is disabled, use high >= the crossed boundary AND close < the boundary instead of the close crossunder.
