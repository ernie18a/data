Calculation: Volume profile = On each eligible chart bar, take up to a configurable lookback of bars including the current bar. Divide the range from their lowest low to highest high into a configurable number of equal bins; use a configurable minimum range if the observed range is smaller. For each bar with positive volume, distribute its volume equally among all bins intersected by its low-to-high range, including both endpoint bins. Optionally multiply volume by a configurable decay factor raised to the bar's age. Each bin's price is its midpoint. Eligibility requires a configurable minimum number of elapsed bars and an available, positive ATR.
Calculation: Pockets = Sort bin volumes and select the configurable percentile using the floored index in the sorted array. A candidate bin must be strictly below both immediate neighbors, have volume divided by their average no greater than a configurable ratio, and have volume no greater than the percentile threshold. Expand it in both directions through contiguous bins at or below that threshold. Retain pockets meeting configurable minimum bin count and minimum width in ATR multiples; their bounds are the midpoint prices of the first and last bins. For each pocket, depth is its mean bin volume; upper and lower walls are the mean volumes of the available bins immediately beyond each edge, over a configurable scan depth constrained to at least three bins and at most one quarter of the profile resolution. Touch density is the fraction of a configurable recent window of closes, including the current close, that map to the pocket's bins by rounding to the nearest bin midpoint; exclude closes outside the outermost bin midpoints from the count but retain them in the denominator. Limit the window to the number of elapsed bars.
Calculation: Hurst proxy = For a configurable close lookback, use the mean and population standard deviation of closes. Accumulate deviations from the mean from newest to oldest, starting at zero; let R be the maximum accumulated value minus the minimum, including the initial zero. If the standard deviation and R are positive, use log(R / standard deviation) / log(lookback / 2), clamped to [0.01, 0.99]; otherwise use 0.5. Before the lookback is available, use 0.5.
Calculation: Pocket classification = A pocket is above when its midpoint is at least the current close, otherwise below. Its far wall is the upper wall for an above pocket and the lower wall for a below pocket; its near wall is the opposite wall. Let W = 2 / (1 + exp(-0.5 * far wall / max(depth, epsilon))) - 1, V = clamp(1 - min(far wall / max(near wall, epsilon), 1), 0, 1) when the far wall is positive and 1 otherwise, T = min(touch density * 10, 1), and H = Hurst proxy. Confluence C is 1 when the pocket midpoint is within a configurable ATR multiple of any enabled high or low of the last completed bar of either of two configurable higher timeframes, otherwise 0. With configurable nonnegative weights for wall, vacuum, Hurst, confluence and touch, reversal score is wall weight * W + vacuum weight * (1 - V) + Hurst weight * (1 - H) + confluence weight * C + touch weight * (1 - T); breakthrough score is wall weight * (1 - W) + vacuum weight * V + Hurst weight * H + touch weight * T. A reversal pocket has reversal score >= breakthrough score; otherwise it is a breakthrough pocket. For each side and classification, select the pocket whose midpoint is nearest the current close, retaining the first encountered on ties.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume divided by its configurable-period SMA, or 1 if that SMA is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Above entry = For the selected above pocket, either current close > its current lower bound AND previous close <= the previous bar's selected lower bound of the same classification, or, when the configurable wick mode is enabled, current low <= its current lower bound AND current close > that bound.
Calculation: Below entry = For the selected below pocket, either current close < its current upper bound AND previous close >= the previous bar's selected upper bound of the same classification, or, when the configurable wick mode is enabled, current high >= its current upper bound AND current close < that bound.
Long: Reversal pocket below exists AND below entry AND (volume ratio < configurable falling-volume threshold OR the configurable ROC filter is enabled AND close ROC <= 0).
Long: Breakthrough pocket above exists AND above entry AND (the configurable EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA) AND (volume ratio > configurable rising-volume threshold OR the configurable ROC filter is disabled OR close ROC > 0).
Short: Reversal pocket above exists AND above entry AND (volume ratio < configurable falling-volume threshold OR the configurable ROC filter is enabled AND close ROC >= 0).
Short: Breakthrough pocket below exists AND below entry AND (the configurable EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA) AND (volume ratio > configurable rising-volume threshold OR the configurable ROC filter is disabled OR close ROC < 0).
