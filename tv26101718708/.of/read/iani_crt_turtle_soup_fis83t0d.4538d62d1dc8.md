Calculation: Swing high / swing low = a chart-timeframe bar whose high (low) is the highest (lowest) among an equal number of bars on each side of it; it is confirmed only after that many later bars have completed. Last swing high / low = the most recently confirmed one.
Long: (A) on a reference timeframe (equal to or higher than the chart timeframe), the still-forming reference bar has low < previous completed reference bar's low AND close > that previous low, evaluated on the first chart bar of this reference bar that satisfies it (this event is the 'long TS signal'; it records the previous reference bar's high as the long objective); OR (B) the most recent TS signal was long AND no CHoCH has fired since that signal AND chart close > last swing high; OR (C) the most recent TS signal was long AND chart bar low > the high of the chart bar two bars earlier (bullish fair value gap).
Short: (A) on a reference timeframe (equal to or higher than the chart timeframe), the still-forming reference bar has high > previous completed reference bar's high AND close < that previous high, evaluated on the first chart bar of this reference bar that satisfies it (this event is the 'short TS signal'; it records the previous reference bar's low as the short objective); OR (B) the most recent TS signal was short AND no CHoCH has fired since that signal AND chart close < last swing low; OR (C) the most recent TS signal was short AND chart bar high < the low of the chart bar two bars earlier (bearish fair value gap).
Exit: long, chart bar high >= the long objective recorded at the most recent long TS signal
Exit: short, chart bar low <= the short objective recorded at the most recent short TS signal
