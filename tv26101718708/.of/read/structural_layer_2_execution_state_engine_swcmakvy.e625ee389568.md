Calculation: Velocity Z-score = Z-score of absolute close minus open over a rolling lookback that includes the current bar
Calculation: Volume Z-score = Z-score of volume over the same rolling lookback, including the current bar
Long: close > open AND Velocity Z-score > 0 AND Volume Z-score > 0
Short: close < open AND Velocity Z-score > 0 AND Volume Z-score > 0
