Calculation: Liquidity levels = On each bar, register enabled major and/or minor confirmed pivot highs and lows, using equal numbers of bars on each side; the major lookback is longer than the minor lookback. Process major highs, major lows, minor highs, then minor lows. Skip a new level if an unswept level on the same side is within one price increment. If equal-level detection is enabled, compare the new level with existing unswept same-side levels from newest to oldest; if the first match is within the chosen ATR-based or close-percentage tolerance, mark both equal, increment the existing level's touch count, and assign that count to the new level. Otherwise the new level has one touch. Keep levels in insertion order, removing the oldest when the storage limit is exceeded. After each bar's sweep scan, remove levels whose pivot age exceeds the lookback limit. A level becomes swept when its sweep passes all enabled filters, even if it fails the minimum score; swept levels remain stored but cannot be swept again.
Calculation: Sweep score = Weighted average, scaled to a percentage and capped at the maximum score, of rejection distance divided by candle range, directional body divided by candle range, penetration divided by ATR, volume divided by a multiple of its SMA, absolute close-to-short-term EMA distance divided by a multiple of ATR, and touch count divided by a touch-count scale. Cap each component at its maximum; floor directional body and rejection at zero. Use zero for ATR- or volume-dependent components when their denominator is nonpositive. Use a price increment as the minimum candle range. The rejection distance is close minus low for a bullish sweep and high minus close for a bearish sweep; directional body is close minus open for bullish and open minus close for bearish.
Calculation: Sweep filters = Apply the selected preset: off requires no candle filters; normal requires positive candle range, rejection distance divided by range at least a threshold, and volume greater than its SMA times a multiplier; strict additionally requires directional body divided by range at least a threshold, positive ATR, range at least an ATR multiple, and penetration at least an ATR multiple. Strict uses a higher rejection threshold and volume multiplier than normal. If the optional trend filter is enabled, continuation requires the short-term EMA above the long-term EMA for bullish sweeps and below it for bearish sweeps; reversal requires the opposite. If the optional higher-timeframe filter is enabled, require higher-timeframe close above its EMA for bullish sweeps and below it for bearish sweeps, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. If the optional minimum-score filter is enabled, require sweep score at least its threshold.
Long: Low < an unswept pivot-low liquidity level AND close > that level AND sweep filters pass, with penetration = level minus low.
Short: High > an unswept pivot-high liquidity level AND close < that level AND sweep filters pass, with penetration = high minus level.
Long: Next-candle confirmation is enabled AND the preceding bar produced a long sweep decision AND (close > open OR close > preceding bar's high).
Short: Next-candle confirmation is enabled AND the preceding bar produced a short sweep decision AND (close < open OR close < preceding bar's low).
