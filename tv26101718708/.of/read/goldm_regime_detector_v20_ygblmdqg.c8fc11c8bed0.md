Calculation: EMA slope percent = 100 × ((EMA(close, long-term lookback) − its value at the shorter slope lookback ago) / slope lookback) / current EMA
Calculation: ATR percentile = 100 × count of prior bars in the lookback whose ATR is below current ATR / lookback
Calculation: ATR percentile jump = current ATR percentile − ATR percentile from 3 bars ago
Calculation: DTE = max(0, ceiling((expiry datetime − bar datetime) / 1 day))
Long: EMA slope percent > positive threshold AND ADX ≥ no-trend threshold AND ATR percentile ≥ comfortable minimum AND ATR percentile ≤ extreme maximum AND fast ATR / slow ATR ≤ volatility-expansion threshold AND DTE > full-size cutoff AND NOT (event flag AND ATR percentile > comfortable maximum) AND NOT (ADX crosses above no-trend threshold + offset OR ATR percentile jump > spike threshold OR Bollinger %B crosses above its upper extreme or below its lower extreme)
Short: EMA slope percent < negative threshold AND ADX ≥ no-trend threshold AND ATR percentile ≥ comfortable minimum AND ATR percentile ≤ extreme maximum AND fast ATR / slow ATR ≤ volatility-expansion threshold AND DTE > full-size cutoff AND NOT (event flag AND ATR percentile > comfortable maximum) AND NOT (ADX crosses above no-trend threshold + offset OR ATR percentile jump > spike threshold OR Bollinger %B crosses above its upper extreme or below its lower extreme)
Exit: long, ADX crosses above no-trend threshold + offset OR ATR percentile jump > spike threshold OR Bollinger %B crosses above its upper extreme or below its lower extreme OR DTE ≤ force-close threshold
Exit: short, ADX crosses above no-trend threshold + offset OR ATR percentile jump > spike threshold OR Bollinger %B crosses above its upper extreme or below its lower extreme OR DTE ≤ force-close threshold
