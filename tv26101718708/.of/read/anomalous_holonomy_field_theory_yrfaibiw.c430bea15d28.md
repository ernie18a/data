Calculation: Field score = Start at zero. If close is above both the short EMA of close and the EMA of close on a higher timeframe, add a positive base amount, increased when close is also above the longer EMA; add a scaled close rate of change when positive, and add a volume bonus when volume exceeds a multiple of its SMA. Otherwise, if close is below both the short EMA and the higher-timeframe EMA, subtract the corresponding base amount, increased in magnitude when close is also below the longer EMA; add the scaled rate of change when negative, and subtract the volume bonus when volume exceeds the same multiple of its SMA. Add the percentage distance of close from the session VWAP of typical price, divided by close and clamped to symmetric limits. If the resulting score is positive, add a larger bonus when RSI is below its lower threshold, otherwise a smaller bonus when RSI is below its higher threshold; if the score is negative, subtract a larger bonus when RSI is above its upper threshold, otherwise a smaller bonus when RSI is above its lower threshold. Finally, add a bonus when the score is positive and MACD is above its signal line, or subtract it when the score is negative and MACD is below its signal line; multiply the result by a positive amplifier. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars.
Calculation: Signal threshold = Sensitivity multiplied by a mode factor and by a low-, normal-, or high-volatility factor. Volatility is ATR divided by close; classify it as low when below its lower nearest-rank rolling percentile, high when above its upper nearest-rank rolling percentile, and normal otherwise. The low-volatility factor is smaller than the normal factor, which is smaller than the high-volatility factor; the conservative mode factor is larger than the adaptive factor, which is larger than the aggressive factor.
Calculation: Trading eligibility = The chart bar is complete, its opening time is within the configured session in the configured timezone, weekends are allowed or its opening day is a weekday, the daily closed-trade counter is below the mode-adjusted limit, and smart timing is disabled or bars since the most recent actual entry signal are at least the mode-adjusted separation. Initialize the last signal bar index to zero. The aggressive separation is shorter than the adaptive separation, which is shorter than the conservative separation; the aggressive trade limit is greater than the adaptive limit, which is greater than the conservative limit. Reset the counter when the exchange-timezone day of week changes; on other bars add twice the increase event for closed trades, except add only once when performance reset is enabled.
Calculation: Entry stops and targets = On the entry signal bar, let range be the highest high minus lowest low over a longer lookback and pivot be the mean of the preceding bar's high, low, and close. The long stop is the greater of close minus ATR times a mode-dependent multiplier and the lesser of the lowest low over a shorter lookback and pivot minus a fraction of range. The short stop is the lesser of close plus ATR times a separate mode-dependent multiplier and the greater of the highest high over the same shorter lookback and pivot plus the same fraction of range. The long target is signal-bar close plus a mode-dependent multiple of signal-bar close minus the long stop; the short target is signal-bar close minus the same multiple of the short stop minus signal-bar close. Keep these levels fixed for the trade. The aggressive target multiple is greater than the adaptive multiple, which is greater than the conservative multiple; the aggressive stop multiplier is smaller than the adaptive multiplier, which is smaller than the conservative multiplier.
Long: Field score >= signal threshold AND trading eligibility is true.
Short: Field score <= negative signal threshold AND trading eligibility is true.
Long: Field score >= a larger multiple of signal threshold AND position is flat AND (conservative mode with confirmation enabled is false OR (close > short EMA AND RSI > lower confirmation threshold AND RSI < upper confirmation threshold)).
Short: Field score <= negative of the same larger multiple of signal threshold AND position is flat AND (conservative mode with confirmation enabled is false OR (close < short EMA AND RSI > lower confirmation threshold AND RSI < upper confirmation threshold)).
Long: Close > SMA of close AND RSI > midpoint AND close momentum > zero.
Short: Close < SMA of close AND RSI < midpoint AND close momentum < zero.
Long: Close > SMA of close.
Short: Close < SMA of close.
Long: Field score > zero.
Short: Field score <= zero.
Exit: long, low <= fixed long stop OR high >= fixed long target OR bars since entry > mode-dependent holding limit.
Exit: short, high >= fixed short stop OR low <= fixed short target OR bars since entry > mode-dependent holding limit.
