Long: current H3 low < previous completed H3 low (H3 higher than chart timeframe; current H3 bar still forming)
Short: current H3 high > previous completed H3 high (H3 higher than chart timeframe; current H3 bar still forming)
Calculation: confirmed swing high = a bar high greater than the highs of the N bars before and after it
Calculation: confirmed swing low = a bar low less than the lows of the N bars before and after it
Long: not entered during the current New York day AND after a current H3 low sweep and before the next H3 bar AND close crosses above the most recent confirmed swing high AND flat position AND close > current H3 low
Short: not entered during the current New York day AND after a current H3 high sweep and before the next H3 bar AND close crosses below the most recent confirmed swing low AND flat position AND current H3 high > close
Exit: long, bar low <= the entry stop at the H3 low recorded on entry OR bar high >= entry price + (entry price - entry stop) × risk-reward
Exit: short, bar high >= the entry stop at the H3 high recorded on entry OR bar low <= entry price - (entry stop - entry price) × risk-reward
