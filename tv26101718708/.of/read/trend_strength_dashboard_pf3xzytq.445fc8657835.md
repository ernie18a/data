Calculation: Directional indicators = For each enabled selected timeframe, +DI = 100 × RMA(positive directional movement, direction lookback) / RMA(true range, direction lookback), and −DI = 100 × RMA(negative directional movement, direction lookback) / RMA(true range, direction lookback). Positive directional movement is the increase in high when it exceeds the magnitude of the decrease in low and is positive; otherwise 0. Negative directional movement is the magnitude of the decrease in low when it exceeds the increase in high and is positive; otherwise 0. Use that timeframe’s aggregated bars; for a timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe, use the lookahead-off value (latest completed historical bar or currently forming realtime bar). The direction lookback is shared across enabled timeframes.
Calculation: Trend strength = For each enabled selected timeframe, RMA(100 × abs(+DI − −DI) / ( +DI + −DI, or 1 when their sum is 0), trend lookback). Use the same directional indicators and timeframe bar/value handling above. The trend lookback is shared across enabled timeframes and is independent of the direction lookback.
Long: For every enabled selected timeframe, trend strength ≥ Strong threshold AND +DI > −DI
Short: For every enabled selected timeframe, trend strength ≥ Strong threshold AND −DI > +DI
