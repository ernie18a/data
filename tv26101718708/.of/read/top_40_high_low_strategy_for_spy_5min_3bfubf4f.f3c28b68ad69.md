Calculation: Smoothed regression slope = EMA of the change from the preceding bar in the fitted endpoint of a linear regression of close.
Calculation: Long base stop = minimum of the low preceding the entry bar minus ATR on the entry bar times an ATR multiplier, and the preceding bar's low; short base stop = maximum of the high preceding the entry bar plus ATR on the entry bar times the same multiplier, and the preceding bar's high.
Calculation: Long risk = entry price minus long base stop; short risk = short base stop minus entry price. Long stop = long base stop plus long risk times the stop adjustment; short stop = short base stop minus short risk times the stop adjustment. Long target = entry price plus long risk times the reward-to-risk parameter; short target = entry price minus short risk times the reward-to-risk parameter.
Calculation: Trailing activation = during the configured trailing session, the current high exceeds the long target OR the current low is below the short target OR trailing activation was true on the preceding bar; reset it when there is no open position. When activated, the long trailing stop is the maximum of the preceding bar's long stop and current low minus ATR times the ATR multiplier; the short trailing stop is the minimum of the preceding bar's short stop and current high plus ATR times the ATR multiplier.
Long: Absolute smoothed regression slope exceeds the trend threshold AND the preceding bar's absolute smoothed regression slope was at or below the threshold AND smoothed regression slope > 0.
Short: Absolute smoothed regression slope exceeds the trend threshold AND the preceding bar's absolute smoothed regression slope was at or below the threshold AND smoothed regression slope < 0.
Exit: Long, trailing is disabled AND the low reaches the active long stop OR the high reaches the active long target, with orders placed during the configured trading window.
Exit: Short, trailing is disabled AND the high reaches the active short stop OR the low reaches the active short target, with orders placed during the configured trading window.
Exit: Long, trailing is enabled AND the low reaches the active long stop before trailing activation OR the active long trailing stop after trailing activation, with ordinary stop orders placed during the configured trading window and trailing stop orders placed during the configured trailing session.
Exit: Short, trailing is enabled AND the high reaches the active short stop before trailing activation OR the active short trailing stop after trailing activation, with ordinary stop orders placed during the configured trading window and trailing stop orders placed during the configured trailing session.
Exit: Long, expiry exits are enabled AND the bar is in the configured end-of-day exit interval on a full-exit weekday; close half instead on a partial-exit weekday.
Exit: Short, expiry exits are enabled AND the bar is in the configured end-of-day exit interval on a full-exit weekday; close half instead on a partial-exit weekday.
