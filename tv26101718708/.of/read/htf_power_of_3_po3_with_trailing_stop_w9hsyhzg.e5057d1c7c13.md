Calculation: Accumulation boundaries = At the start of each forming higher-timeframe bar, initialize both boundaries to that bar's open. During the following configurable number of chart bars, update the upper boundary to the maximum of itself and high, and the lower boundary to the minimum of itself and low. Then hold them fixed until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Session state and trailing stop = At the start of each forming higher-timeframe bar, reset the state to accumulation and clear the stop. On the first accumulation breakout, set the state to manipulation with the breakout direction and initialize the stop to close minus ATR times a multiplier for an upward breakout, or close plus ATR times the multiplier for a downward breakout. On a manipulation stop breach, switch to distribution in the opposite direction and initialize the stop using the same directional formula. On a distribution stop breach, mark the session done and clear the stop. After checking for a breach on each bar, update an active upward stop to the maximum of its previous value and close minus ATR times the multiplier, or an active downward stop to the minimum of its previous value and close plus ATR times the multiplier.
Long: Session state is accumulation AND close > upper accumulation boundary OR session state is manipulation AND direction is downward AND close > trailing stop.
Short: Session state is accumulation AND close < lower accumulation boundary OR session state is manipulation AND direction is upward AND close < trailing stop.
Exit: long, session state is manipulation OR distribution AND direction is upward AND close < trailing stop.
Exit: short, session state is manipulation OR distribution AND direction is downward AND close > trailing stop.
