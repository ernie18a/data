Calculation: General conventions = Evaluate on each completed chart bar. All lengths, thresholds, weights, multipliers, score values, retracement ratios, tolerances, cooldowns, and enabled switches are parameters. Clamp percentages to their allowed range. Normalize a series to a percentage using its rolling minimum and maximum, returning the midpoint when they are equal. A confirmed pivot uses equal numbers of bars on each side and becomes available only on its confirmation bar. Use a positive price-floor parameter wherever a denominator would otherwise be zero.
Calculation: Trend percentage = Convert a tiered trend score to a percentage centered at neutral. In order, assign the strongest positive or negative score when close is respectively above or below the longest close EMA and the three shorter close EMAs are strictly ordered in the same direction; assign a smaller signed score when close is on the corresponding side of the third EMA and the shortest EMA is on that side of the second EMA; otherwise assign the smallest signed score according to the ordering of those two EMAs, or zero if equal. The EMA lengths increase successively.
Calculation: Multitimeframe alignment percentage = For each of several selected timeframes, calculate a linear regression of midpoint price and an ATR. Initialize direction from whether close is at or above the regression; initialize the rail at regression minus ATR times a multiplier for upward direction, or regression plus that distance for downward direction. On a completed bar, reverse upward when downward and close exceeds the preceding rail, or reverse downward when upward and close falls below the preceding rail; reset the rail to the corresponding current regression-plus-or-minus-ATR base on reversal. Otherwise trail the upward rail at the maximum of its preceding value and its current base, or the downward rail at the minimum. The percentage is the proportion of upward directions. The selected timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe: for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar within the chart bar.
Calculation: Price-volume momentum percentage = Weighted sum of three smoothed components: the correlation of close change and volume change, mapped from its signed range to a percentage and replaced by neutral when unavailable; normalized ATR divided by its EMA times volume divided by its EMA; and normalized close rate of change. Smooth each component with an EMA. The normalization lookback is longer than the correlation and smoothing lookbacks; the volume and ATR baseline EMAs share a length.
Calculation: Volume-efficiency percentage = Divide positive close change by bullish-candle volume and negative close change by bearish-candle volume, using zero volume for candles of the other side and a positive volume floor. For each result, take its rolling percentile rank, scale it and smooth it with an EMA. Compare these efficiencies and the current and preceding EMAs of bullish-candle and bearish-candle volume. Assign a bullish score when bullish efficiency is greater, bullish volume EMA is nonincreasing and bearish volume EMA is nondecreasing; assign a bearish score for the opposite conditions. Otherwise use a clamped neutral percentage plus a scaled bullish-minus-bearish efficiency difference.
Calculation: Ichimoku percentage = Use current, undisplaced conversion and base lines, their midpoint, and the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer cloud lookback. Add signed weights for close above the upper or below the lower cloud boundary, the first cloud boundary above or below the second, conversion above or below base, and close above or below a past close. Convert the clamped sum to a neutral-centered percentage. Conversion has the shortest and the cloud boundary the longest lookback; the past-close offset is the base-line length.
Calculation: RSI structure percentage = Calculate RSI, its first difference and the EMA of the second difference. Store the latest confirmed RSI pivot high and low. Assign a positive breakout score when RSI exceeds the stored high and smoothed acceleration is positive, or a negative breakout score when RSI is below the stored low and acceleration is negative; otherwise use a weighted sum of RSI's deviation from neutral and smoothed acceleration. Clamp and convert the score to a neutral-centered percentage.
Calculation: RSI price-band percentage = Form upper and lower RSI bands from an RSI SMA plus or minus its standard deviation. Map each band to price as the longer-lookback close SMA plus the band's deviation from neutral RSI times close standard deviation divided by neutral RSI. A squeeze is RSI-band width below its SMA; release is a squeeze on the preceding bar and no squeeze now. Assign the strongest bullish or bearish percentage when release coincides with close above the upper or below the lower price band, a smaller directional percentage when close is outside the respective band without release, neutral during a squeeze, and a slightly bullish percentage otherwise. The price-scale lookback is longer than the RSI-band lookback.
Calculation: Regression-flow percentage = Fit linear regression to midpoint price and evaluate the same fit at the current and two preceding offsets. Slope is the current value minus the first offset; curvature is the difference between the two successive slopes. Smooth the current regression value plus weighted slope and curvature with an EMA to obtain the flow core. Width is the greater of ATR and an EMA of absolute close-to-core distance. Flow power is an EMA of a weighted sum of normalized absolute slope, normalized absolute curvature, clamped absolute close-to-core distance divided by width, and normalized ATR. If the core rises or falls from its preceding value, add or subtract scaled power from neutral respectively; otherwise use neutral.
Calculation: Structure pivots = Maintain a bounded chronological list of confirmed high and low pivots. Append an opposite-type pivot; for consecutive pivots of the same type replace the last one only if the new high is higher or the new low is lower. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low when both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Structure percentage = Add signed scores for the latest stored high being above versus at or below the preceding stored high, the latest stored low being above versus at or below the preceding stored low, and close exceeding the latest high versus falling below the latest low. Give the close breakout the larger weight and precedence if both breakout comparisons hold. Missing comparisons contribute zero. Clamp and convert to a neutral-centered percentage.
Calculation: Impulse percentage = From the last four structure pivots, assign a bullish percentage when the final pair is low then high and both the last high and last low exceed their respective preceding same-type pivots. Assign a bearish percentage when the final pair is high then low and both are below their respective preceding same-type pivots. Otherwise use neutral.
Calculation: Power-zone scores = Store the latest confirmed pivot high as resistance and pivot low as support. Count every bar whose high or low respectively lies within a parameterized relative tolerance of the current level, excluding the pivot's own bar; retain the cumulative counters when levels change. Strong support or resistance requires the corresponding counter to reach its threshold. Define the middle retracement zone from the rolling highest high and lowest low. Bullish points are awarded independently for close inside that zone and above support, a bullish pin bar or else bullish engulfing bar, above-threshold relative volume on a bullish candle, and strong support; bearish points mirror these conditions below resistance with bearish candles and strong resistance. A pin bar requires its directional wick to exceed a body multiple, its opposite wick to be below another body multiple, and its range to exceed an ATR multiple. An engulfing bar requires opposite-colored consecutive candles, the current body strictly engulfing the preceding body, and the current body exceeding a multiple of the preceding body. Relative volume is volume divided by its SMA, with a positive floor and a cap on its score contribution.
Calculation: Power-zone percentage = If the bullish power-zone score exceeds the bearish score, convert the capped bullish score to a neutral-centered percentage; if the bearish score is greater, convert its capped negative; otherwise use neutral.
Calculation: Liquidity percentage = Compare the current bar with the highest high and lowest low of a preceding rolling window that excludes the current bar. A bullish rejection requires low below the preceding low, close above it, and close minus low exceeding an ATR multiple; bearish rejection mirrors this at the preceding high. Assign scores in priority order to bullish rejection, bearish rejection, close above the preceding high, close below the preceding low, or neither; rejection scores have greater magnitude than breakout scores. Convert to a neutral-centered percentage.
Calculation: Volume-delta percentage = For a rolling group of chart bars, compare current close with the group's first open and assign the group's total volume a positive, negative, or zero sign. Smooth this delta with an EMA and also accumulate it and smooth the cumulative series with a longer EMA. Separately accumulate the same rolling group volume signed by current close versus preceding close and smooth it with the longer EMA. Normalize each of the three series over a rolling window to the range from zero to one, using the midpoint when its range is zero; average them and map the result to a percentage.
Calculation: Harmonic percentage = With at least five structure pivots, calculate the absolute consecutive leg lengths and their successive ratios. A bullish pattern requires the last four pivot types to alternate low, high, low, high and the last leg divided by the preceding leg to lie within its parameterized range; a bearish pattern reverses the pivot types. Assign the corresponding directional percentage, otherwise neutral. Modify its magnitude in sequence when the first two ratios meet a first pattern's ranges, when they meet a second pattern's ranges, and when the first and last ratios meet a third pattern's ranges; each later match overrides the preceding magnitude, and neutral remains neutral. Undefined ratios fail their comparisons.
Calculation: Volatility percentage = Divide ATR by its EMA. Determine direction from close relative to a close EMA. Assign a stronger signed score when the ratio exceeds the expansion threshold, zero when it is below the contraction threshold, and a weaker signed score otherwise; a close equal to its EMA has zero direction. Convert the score to a neutral-centered percentage.
Calculation: Pullback percentage and bonus = Use the last two structure pivots to identify an upward low-to-high impulse or downward high-to-low impulse. Measure retracement from the final pivot toward the first, divided by impulse range. Within the configured minimum and maximum retracement, assign a positive quality score that increases across successive deeper retracement bands. A bullish reaction additionally requires an upward impulse and either close above open, close above preceding close, or positive smoothed RSI acceleration; a bearish reaction mirrors these comparisons. Use the quality score with the reaction's sign; for a retracement beyond the maximum, use a small score against the impulse direction; otherwise use zero. Convert to a neutral-centered percentage. A directional bonus requires the corresponding reaction and quality score at or above its bonus threshold. When the pullback feature is disabled, its percentage is neutral and neither bonus applies.
Calculation: Composite percentage = Weighted average of trend, multitimeframe alignment, price-volume momentum, volume efficiency, Ichimoku, RSI structure, RSI price-band, regression-flow, structure, impulse, power-zone, liquidity, volume-delta, harmonic, volatility, and optionally pullback percentages. Clamp to the percentage range. Composite edge is this percentage minus its EMA.
Calculation: Directional agreement = Count one vote per component when its percentage meets that component's bullish or bearish threshold. For multitimeframe alignment, require a directional supermajority; for pullback, use its directional bonus instead of its percentage. Calculate bullish and bearish counts independently.
Calculation: Net force = Sum each component percentage's deviation from neutral, including the pullback percentage even when its feature is disabled. Take the signed square root of the sum's absolute value. The bullish force boundary is a close EMA minus the absolute signed root times ATR and two width multipliers; the bearish boundary uses addition.
Calculation: Main signal state = Start neutral. A main long signal sets state to long and records its bar; a main short signal sets state to short and records its bar. After processing main signals on each bar, reset a long state to neutral when composite percentage is below neutral, or a short state to neutral when it is above neutral. Each direction's cooldown is measured from its own last main signal and requires strictly more than the configured number of bars.
Calculation: Power-zone event state = For each direction independently, record the bar of every power-zone event. An event requires its score to reach its threshold after being below it on the preceding bar and requires strictly more than the configured cooldown since the preceding event of that direction.
Long: Composite percentage >= bullish threshold AND bullish agreement >= minimum agreement AND bullish agreement > bearish agreement AND (edge filter disabled OR composite edge > positive edge threshold) AND (force filter disabled OR net force >= positive force threshold AND close > bullish force boundary) AND long cooldown satisfied AND main signal state is not long.
Short: Composite percentage <= bearish threshold AND bearish agreement >= minimum agreement AND bearish agreement > bullish agreement AND (edge filter disabled OR composite edge < negative edge threshold) AND (force filter disabled OR net force <= negative force threshold AND close < bearish force boundary) AND short cooldown satisfied AND main signal state is not short.
Long: Bullish power-zone score >= event threshold AND preceding bullish power-zone score < event threshold AND bullish power-zone event cooldown satisfied.
Short: Bearish power-zone score >= event threshold AND preceding bearish power-zone score < event threshold AND bearish power-zone event cooldown satisfied.
Long: Net force >= positive force threshold AND close > bullish force boundary.
Short: Net force <= negative force threshold AND close < bearish force boundary.
Long: Composite percentage >= bullish threshold.
Short: Composite percentage <= bearish threshold.
Long: Multitimeframe upward-direction count >= directional supermajority.
Short: Multitimeframe downward-direction count >= directional supermajority.
Long: Regression-flow core > preceding regression-flow core.
Short: Regression-flow core < preceding regression-flow core.
Long: Close > latest stored structure pivot high.
Short: Close < latest stored structure pivot low AND close <= latest stored structure pivot high.
Long: Low < preceding-window lowest low AND close > preceding-window lowest low.
Short: High > preceding-window highest high AND close < preceding-window highest high AND NOT (low < preceding-window lowest low AND close > preceding-window lowest low).
Long: Trend, structure-part weighted average, power-part weighted average, multitimeframe alignment, regression-flow, liquidity, pullback, or composite EMA percentage >= its displayed bullish threshold.
Short: Trend, structure-part weighted average, power-part weighted average, multitimeframe alignment, regression-flow, liquidity, pullback, or composite EMA percentage <= its displayed bearish threshold.
Long: Composite edge >= neutral edge OR net force >= neutral force.
Short: Composite edge < neutral edge OR net force < neutral force.
Exit: long, main signal state is long AND composite percentage < neutral.
Exit: short, main signal state is short AND composite percentage > neutral.
