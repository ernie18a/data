Calculation: Trend score = higher-timeframe-independent trend strength from price vs. long-term EMA and short-term vs. medium-term EMA ordering; map bullish, bearish, and weaker alignment to directional percentages.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe regime = on each of four higher timeframes, bullish when close crosses above a trailing ATR-offset linear-regression rail and bearish when it crosses below; otherwise retain regime and trail the rail. Use each timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Price-volume momentum = weighted blend of normalized EMA-smoothed price/volume-change correlation, ATR expansion times relative volume, and normalized rate of change.
Calculation: Volume-efficiency score = compare EMA-smoothed percentile ranks of upward and downward price movement per directional volume; adjust for rising or falling averages of green- and red-bar volume.
Calculation: Cloud score = combine price relative to the Ichimoku cloud, cloud direction, conversion-line vs. base-line direction, and close vs. its lagged close.
Calculation: RSI structure score = RSI breakout above its latest confirmed pivot high with positive smoothed acceleration, or below its latest confirmed pivot low with negative acceleration; otherwise use RSI distance from 50 and acceleration.
Calculation: Price-scaled RSI bands = map RSI mean and standard-deviation bands onto price using price mean and standard deviation; score price above/below the bands and whether the bands have just released from a squeeze.
Calculation: Regression-flow score = direction of a smoothed, projected linear regression of hl2, scaled by normalized regression slope, curvature, price distance, and ATR.
Calculation: Structure score = combine higher-high/lower-high and higher-low/lower-low swing-pivot sequences with close breaking the latest swing high or low. OTE score is bullish/bearish when the last four alternating pivots form the corresponding higher-high/higher-low or lower-high/lower-low impulse.
Calculation: Power-zone score = compare bullish and bearish sums of: support/resistance with price in the 38.2%–61.8% range of the recent swing, qualifying pin-bar or engulfing candle, directional volume above its average, and support/resistance tested at least twice. A power-zone event occurs when either sum first reaches its signal threshold, subject to its cooldown.
Calculation: Liquidity score = bullish for a low sweep and close back above the prior lookback low with a sufficient ATR-sized rejection; bearish for the mirrored high sweep; otherwise score a close breakout above the prior high or below the prior low.
Calculation: Volume-delta score = normalize and average smoothed merged-bar signed volume, cumulative signed volume, and OBV trend, then center the result on 50.
Calculation: Harmonic score = directional score from the latest five alternating swing pivots when the terminal-leg ratio or retracement ratios satisfy the configured harmonic ranges.
Calculation: Volatility score = combine ATR expansion/contraction relative to its EMA with close relative to the short-term trend EMA.
Calculation: Fibonacci pullback score = from the latest confirmed impulse pivots, score a retracement within the configured healthy range according to its depth and directional reaction (bar direction, close change, or RSI acceleration); score a deeper retracement against the impulse. Include only when enabled.
Calculation: Final directional score = configurable weighted average of the trend, multi-timeframe regime, price-volume momentum, volume-efficiency, cloud, RSI structure, RSI bands, regression-flow, structure, OTE, power-zone, liquidity, volume-delta, harmonic, volatility, and (when enabled) Fibonacci pullback percentages.
Calculation: Bull agreement count = number of bullish component tests met across those 16 components; bear agreement count is the corresponding bearish count. For the regime component, count strong alignment; for Fibonacci, count only a qualifying pullback reaction bonus.
Calculation: Score edge = final directional score minus its EMA. Net-force value = signed square root of the sum of the 16 component percentages centered on 50. Force threshold = EMA(close) offset by ATR times the absolute net-force value and configured multipliers, below the basis for bullish force and above it for bearish force.
Long: On a completed bar, final directional score >= bullish threshold AND bull agreement count >= minimum agreement AND bull agreement count > bear agreement count AND (edge filter disabled OR score edge > minimum edge) AND (net-force filter disabled OR net-force value >= signal threshold AND close > bullish force threshold) AND more than the configured cooldown bars since the prior long signal AND no active long signal state.
Short: On a completed bar, final directional score <= bearish threshold AND bear agreement count >= minimum agreement AND bear agreement count > bull agreement count AND (edge filter disabled OR score edge < negative minimum edge) AND (net-force filter disabled OR net-force value <= negative signal threshold AND close < bearish force threshold) AND more than the configured cooldown bars since the prior short signal AND no active short signal state.
Long: Bullish power-zone score >= its signal threshold AND was below that threshold on the prior bar AND its cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Bearish power-zone score >= its signal threshold AND was below that threshold on the prior bar AND its cooldown has elapsed.
Long: Net-force value >= signal threshold AND close > bullish force threshold.
Short: Net-force value <= negative signal threshold AND close < bearish force threshold.
Exit: Long, final directional score < 50.
Exit: Short, final directional score > 50.
