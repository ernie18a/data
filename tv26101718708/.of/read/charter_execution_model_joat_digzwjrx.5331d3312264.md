Calculation: Directional midpoint = average of the fast EMA and fast HMA of close; structural midpoint = average of the slow EMA and slow HMA of close, with both fast lookbacks shorter than both slow lookbacks
Calculation: Regime strength = clamp(0.60 × abs(directional midpoint − structural midpoint) / ATR × 100 + 0.80 × abs(heat − 50), 0, 100), where heat = (close − lowest low over the heat lookback) / (highest high over the heat lookback − lowest low over the heat lookback) × 100
Calculation: Regime persistence = consecutive bars for which directional midpoint > structural midpoint has remained unchanged
Calculation: Liquidity bias = (buy volume − sell volume) / max(buy volume + sell volume, 1), where bars over the liquidity lookback contribute their volume to a price bin based on HLC3 within the lookback's highest-high to lowest-low range; up-close bars contribute buy volume and down-close bars contribute sell volume
Calculation: Recent confirmed pivot low/high = low/high at the most recent pivot confirmed using equal left and right pivot lookbacks; use the rolling swing low/high over the swing lookback if no confirmed pivot exists
Calculation: Bull gap sigma = (current low − low two bars ago's preceding high) / max(standard deviation of (low − high two bars ago) over the gap lookback, minimum tick)
Calculation: Bear gap sigma = (low two bars ago − current high) / max(standard deviation of (low two bars ago − high) over the gap lookback, minimum tick)
Calculation: Pressure RSI = RSI of close momentum over the momentum lookback, using the RSI lookback
Calculation: Displacement = clamp(abs(fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR × 40 + abs(pressure RSI − 50), 0, 100)
Calculation: Long invalidation = min(recent confirmed pivot low or fallback swing low, current low); short invalidation = max(recent confirmed pivot high or fallback swing high, current high)
Calculation: Long initial stop = min(close − ATR × stop multiplier, long invalidation − ATR × 0.15); short initial stop = max(close + ATR × stop multiplier, short invalidation + ATR × 0.15)
Calculation: Long risk = max(close − long initial stop, 10 minimum ticks); short risk = max(short initial stop − close, 10 minimum ticks)
Calculation: Long targets = entry close + long risk × each target R multiple; short targets = entry close − short risk × each target R multiple
Calculation: Long active stop = max(stored long stop, rolling lowest low over 5 bars − ATR × 0.20) when strong-regime trailing is enabled and regime strength ≥ 60; otherwise stored long stop. Short active stop = min(stored short stop, rolling highest high over 5 bars + ATR × 0.20) under the same condition; otherwise stored short stop
Long: confirmed bar AND position size ≤ 0 AND directional midpoint > structural midpoint AND regime strength ≥ regime floor AND regime persistence ≥ persistence floor AND liquidity bias > liquidity bias floor AND close ≥ EMA(close, 55) AND close > (recent confirmed pivot low or fallback swing low) AND close > slow EMA AND (current low > high two bars ago AND high one bar ago > high two bars ago AND bull gap sigma > gap threshold OR displacement ≥ displacement threshold AND pressure RSI > 54 AND close > fast EMA AND directional midpoint > structural midpoint OR continuation enabled AND pressure RSI ≥ continuation floor AND close > directional midpoint AND current low ≤ fast EMA)
Short: confirmed bar AND position size ≥ 0 AND directional midpoint ≤ structural midpoint AND regime strength ≥ regime floor AND regime persistence ≥ persistence floor AND liquidity bias < −liquidity bias floor AND close ≤ EMA(close, 55) AND close < (recent confirmed pivot high or fallback swing high) AND close < slow EMA AND (current high < low two bars ago AND low one bar ago < low two bars ago AND bear gap sigma > gap threshold OR displacement ≥ displacement threshold AND pressure RSI < 46 AND close < fast EMA AND directional midpoint < structural midpoint OR continuation enabled AND pressure RSI ≤ 100 − continuation floor AND close < directional midpoint AND current high ≥ fast EMA)
Exit: long, low ≤ long active stop OR high ≥ either long target
Exit: short, high ≥ short active stop OR low ≤ either short target
