Calculation: Candle measures = range = high - low; body ratio = abs(close - open) / range, or 0 if range is 0; close position = (close - low) / range, or 0.5 if range is 0; range expansion = range / ATR. Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume), or 1 if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Structure break = close above the latest confirmed pivot high for long, or below the latest confirmed pivot low for short. A pivot has the configured number of bars on each side; update the latest pivot on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Coil = (highest high - lowest low over the coil lookback ending on the preceding bar) / current ATR; coil is tight when this ratio is at most its threshold.
Calculation: Directional strength = sum of parameterized, descending-threshold step scores for range expansion, body ratio, directional close position, absolute net close change over the momentum window divided by ATR, consecutive closes in the direction, and volume ratio; add parameterized scores for directional acceleration, tight coil, structure break, and trend alignment. Directional close position is close position for long and 1 - close position for short. Directional acceleration compares the current close change over an acceleration lookback with the same change one bar earlier: both must be positive and increasing for long, or negative and decreasing for short. Chart trend alignment is the close EMA rising for long or falling for short compared with its value a configured number of bars earlier. Higher-timeframe alignment is true when its filter is disabled, its EMA is unavailable, or close is above the EMA for long or below it for short; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Add the both-aligned score if chart and higher-timeframe alignment are both true, the one-aligned score if exactly one is true, otherwise zero. Round the sum to the configured precision and clamp it to the score range. Use separate ordered thresholds and scores for each step-score component.
Calculation: Risk = at entry close, select either the lowest low for long or highest high for short over the momentum window including the entry bar, or select the entry bar low for long or high for short; subtract an ATR buffer from the long anchor or add it to the short anchor. Clamp the absolute distance from entry close to this buffered anchor between the configured minimum and maximum ATR risk distances, applying the minimum first and then the maximum. Fix risk, stop, and targets at entry; stop = entry minus risk for long or entry plus risk for short; each target = entry plus its configured risk multiple for long or entry minus it for short.
Long: close > open AND range expansion >= ignition threshold AND body ratio >= body threshold AND close position >= close-position threshold AND (structure filter disabled OR long structure break) AND (coil filter disabled OR coil tight) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio >= volume threshold) AND directional strength >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR directional strength >= strong threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA unavailable OR close > last completed higher-timeframe EMA) AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior entry OR bars since prior entry >= cooldown) AND no active trade.
Short: close < open AND range expansion >= ignition threshold AND body ratio >= body threshold AND 1 - close position >= close-position threshold AND (structure filter disabled OR short structure break) AND (coil filter disabled OR coil tight) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio >= volume threshold) AND directional strength >= minimum strength AND (strong-only filter disabled OR directional strength >= strong threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA unavailable OR close < last completed higher-timeframe EMA) AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior entry OR bars since prior entry >= cooldown) AND no active trade.
Long: close > open AND range expansion >= ignition threshold AND body ratio >= body threshold AND close position >= close-position threshold AND (structure filter disabled OR long structure break) AND (coil filter disabled OR coil tight) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio >= volume threshold) AND long directional strength > 0.
Short: close < open AND range expansion >= ignition threshold AND body ratio >= body threshold AND 1 - close position >= close-position threshold AND (structure filter disabled OR short structure break) AND (coil filter disabled OR coil tight) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ratio >= volume threshold) AND short directional strength > 0.
Exit: long, bar after entry or later AND (low <= stop OR high >= third target).
Exit: short, bar after entry or later AND (high >= stop OR low <= third target).
