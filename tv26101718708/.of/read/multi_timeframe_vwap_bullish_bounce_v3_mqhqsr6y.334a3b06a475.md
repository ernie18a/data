Calculation: VWAP_tf = session-anchored VWAP = cumulative(hlc3*volume)/cumulative(volume), reset each session, computed on bars of timeframe tf aggregated from chart bars, using the still-forming tf bar at each chart bar. tf is higher than the chart timeframe. VWAP_S, VWAP_M and VWAP_L use a short, medium and long tf respectively (short < medium < long).
Long: on the current bar C, close > EMA_fast(close, chart TF) AND close > VWAP_S AND close > VWAP_M AND close > VWAP_L, AND the previous bar P (the setup bar) satisfied ALL of: (a) EMA_fast > EMA_mid > EMA_slow, all on chart-TF close, with lengths fast < mid < slow; (b) for each of VWAP_S, VWAP_M and VWAP_L: low(P) <= that VWAP at P AND the lowest close over the N bars before P > that VWAP's value at the bar before P; (c) close(P) > open(P) AND either [hammer: (min(open,close) - low) >= m*|close-open| with m > 1, AND (high - max(open,close)) <= f*|close-open| with f < 1, AND close > VWAP_S] OR [bullish engulfing: the bar before P is bearish (open > close), close(P) > open of the bar before P, open(P) < close of the bar before P, AND close(P) > VWAP_S]; (d) volume(P) >= k * SMA(volume, length) at P, with k > 1 and the average including P; (e) (RSI(P) in [lo, hi] OR RSI of the bar before P in [lo, hi]) AND RSI(P) > RSI of the bar before P, with lo < hi, both below 50; (f) at P, the forming medium-timeframe bar has close > EMA_fast of medium-timeframe closes AND VWAP_M at its current bar > VWAP_M at the previous medium-timeframe bar
