Calculation: Long trailing stop = close − ATR × trailing-stop multiplier
Calculation: Short trailing stop = close + ATR × trailing-stop multiplier
Long: close crosses above upper Bollinger Band AND ADX > entry threshold AND volume > its 20-bar SMA × volume-spike multiplier AND close > Supertrend
Short: close crosses below lower Bollinger Band AND ADX > entry threshold AND volume > its 20-bar SMA × volume-spike multiplier AND close < Supertrend
Exit: long, MACD line crosses below signal line OR close < Long trailing stop OR ADX < exit threshold
Exit: short, MACD line crosses above signal line OR close > Short trailing stop OR ADX < exit threshold
