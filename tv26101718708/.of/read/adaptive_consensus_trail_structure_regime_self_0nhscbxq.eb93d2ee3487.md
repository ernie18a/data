Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over the same lookback.
Calculation: Trend regime = ADX exceeds its threshold AND efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold AND (Hurst is not enabled OR Hurst is at least 0.5 minus its neutral buffer); Hurst uses close-to-close log returns over its lookback.
Calculation: Structural references = anchored VWAP upper/lower bands; developing-session or nearest unfilled prior-session volume point of control; latest bullish/bearish fair-value-gap midpoint; latest confirmed swing low/high using left and right pivot lookbacks; and lowest/highest price of the maximum-flow absorption bar over its lookback. For absorption, flow is estimated from volume and the standardized close-to-close move; support is the low of the highest-sell-flow bar closing at or above its open, and resistance is the high of the highest-buy-flow bar closing at or below its open.
Calculation: Reference trust = the Wilson 95% lower bound of each reference's rolling respect rate, or a neutral prior weight until its minimum sample count. For support/resistance references other than POC, count a touch as respected when price enters the ATR tolerance zone and closes clearly back beyond it; count it as failed when it closes clearly through the level. For naked POC, count a touch as respected if price first moves away by the POC ATR margin, or failed if it first moves through by that margin, within the response horizon; discard ambiguous or timed-out cases. Halve hit and total counts when the sample cap is reached.
Calculation: Structural consensus = on each side of price, choose the densest cluster of active structural references within the ATR cluster band, weighted by reference trust; its level is the weighted median of cluster levels. Side confidence combines the cluster's share of total reference weight, cluster tightness, mean trust, and regime quality, then is reduced by regime thrash. Regime quality combines trend status, efficiency ratio, ADX, inverse thrash, and Hurst persistence. Regime thrash is the capped, decay-weighted count of regime-code changes over the recent bars.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR band = ATR multiplied by a volatility-rank-scaled base multiplier, widened by inefficiency and again when the market is not trending. Use the mid-price ± this width, the recent high/low Chandelier bands adjusted by this width, or their average according to the selected backbone.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize direction long. When long, ratchet the stop upward to the greater of its prior value and the long target; when short, ratchet it downward to the lesser of its prior value and the short target. The long target is the lower of support consensus and close minus the minimum ATR stop distance when support confidence meets its threshold, otherwise the lower adaptive band. The short target is the higher of resistance consensus and close plus the minimum ATR stop distance when resistance confidence meets its threshold, otherwise the upper adaptive band. A flip requires the minimum bars since the previous flip and a close beyond the active stop by ATR multiplied by the confirmation threshold plus a thrash adjustment and an extra amount when not trending; on a flip, set direction to the new side and stop to that side's target.
Long: direction changes from short to long (previous direction = short AND current direction = long).
Short: direction changes from long to short (previous direction = long AND current direction = short).
Exit: long, close < trailing stop − ATR × (flip-confirmation threshold + thrash adjustment + non-trending adjustment).
Exit: short, close > trailing stop + ATR × (flip-confirmation threshold + thrash adjustment + non-trending adjustment).
