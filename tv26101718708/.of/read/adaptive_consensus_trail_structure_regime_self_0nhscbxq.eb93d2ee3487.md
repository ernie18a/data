Calculation: Price source = a selected OHLC-derived price; use chart-bar volume. Evaluate chart-timeframe calculations at each bar close.
Calculation: Structural references = for support and resistance respectively: the lower and upper bands of VWAP anchored to the selected session, week, or month; the nearest developing-session or unfilled prior-session point of control below or above close; the most recent bullish or bearish fair-value-gap midpoint; the latest confirmed pivot low or high; and an absorption low or high. A bullish gap occurs when low exceeds high two bars earlier; its midpoint is the average of those two prices. A bearish gap is the inverse. Keep each gap midpoint until replaced. A pivot is confirmed after its specified right-hand lookback. For absorption, select the low of the eligible up bar with the largest estimated sell volume and the high of the eligible down bar with the largest estimated buy volume over the lookback. Estimated buy fraction is the logistic function of the standardized close-to-close change; estimated sell fraction is its complement. Alternatively, select the highest-volume strictly up or down bar without splitting volume.
Calculation: Point of control = the midpoint of the price bin containing the most accumulated volume since the session began; fix the bin width at an ATR multiple at the session start. Add each completed lower-timeframe bar's source price and volume to its bin, or use the chart bar if lower-timeframe bars are unavailable. At a new session, retain the prior developing point of control until a later chart bar's high-low range touches it, subject to a maximum retained count. Select the nearest retained or developing point strictly on each side of close; test and use retained points before removing touched points.
Calculation: Reference trust = a neutral prior until enough observations exist, then the Wilson lower confidence bound of the reference type's respect rate. For each non-point-of-control reference, record a support observation when low enters its ATR-scaled touch zone from above and close finishes outside the zone: above is a respect and below is a failure. Apply the inverse to resistance. Count at most one observation per reference type per bar; a clear resistance verdict overrides a simultaneous support verdict, while either respect makes the observation a hit. For retained points of control, record a fresh touch from the appropriate side and resolve it on subsequent bars by the first touch of symmetric ATR-scaled rejection or break levels fixed at the touch; discard a simultaneous touch of both levels or a timeout. When an observation count reaches its cap, halve and truncate both its count and hit count.
Calculation: Regime = efficiency ratio is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback. Calculate ADX and, optionally, the rescaled-range Hurst exponent of chronological log returns. Calculate volatility-cluster intensity recursively as previous intensity times exponential decay plus absolute log return; a cluster exists when its rolling standardized score exceeds its threshold. Trending requires ADX and efficiency ratio above their respective thresholds and, if Hurst is enabled, Hurst at or above its mean-reversion boundary. A trend regime requires trending and no volatility cluster. Assign the regime as volatile if clustered and not trending, otherwise trend up or down according to close versus its EMA if trending, otherwise range. Thrash is the capped, normalized decay-weighted sum of recent regime changes. Regime quality is a clamped weighted sum of the trend-regime indicator, capped efficiency ratio relative to its threshold, capped ADX, inverse thrash, and capped Hurst persistence; use a neutral Hurst contribution when disabled.
Calculation: Consensus and confidence = separately for support and resistance, retain enabled, available references strictly below or above close and weight each by its reference trust. Around each candidate, sum the weights of candidates within an ATR-scaled distance; select the first candidate with the largest sum and its entire neighborhood. Consensus is either the weighted median of that neighborhood, choosing the first eligible member in reference order, or its member closest to close. Confidence is the clamped weighted sum of neighborhood weight divided by total eligible weight, one minus its capped price range divided by the ATR-scaled neighborhood distance, mean neighborhood trust, and regime quality, multiplied by one minus a fraction of thrash. Confidence is zero when no valid consensus exists.
Calculation: Trail direction and trail = initialize direction long and the trail at the long target. Scale the base ATR multiplier between calm and volatile scales using ATR's rolling percentile rank; multiply by a factor increasing as efficiency falls and by a further widening factor outside the trend regime. The adaptive lower and upper bands are the bar midpoint minus and plus this ATR distance. The alternative chandelier bands are the rolling highest high minus the distance and rolling lowest low plus the distance; a blend averages corresponding adaptive and chandelier bands. The long target is the lower band unless support confidence reaches its structure threshold and support consensus exists, in which case it is the smaller of support consensus and close minus a minimum ATR distance. The short target is the upper band unless resistance confidence reaches its structure threshold and resistance consensus exists, in which case it is the larger of resistance consensus and close plus that minimum distance. In a long direction update the trail to the greater of its previous value and the long target; after the minimum holding interval, flip short if close is below that updated trail minus ATR times the breach multiplier, and reset the trail to the short target. In a short direction update the trail to the lesser of its previous value and the short target; after the minimum holding interval, flip long if close is above that updated trail plus ATR times the breach multiplier, and reset the trail to the long target. The breach multiplier is its base plus a thrash-scaled addition and a further addition outside the trend regime. The holding interval starts at initialization and restarts at every flip.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, calculate ATR, efficiency ratio, ATR percentile rank, and midpoint bands using the same volatility scaling and efficiency adjustment as the adaptive trail, without the chart-timeframe regime widening. Initialize long with the lower band as stop. While long, raise the stop to the greater of its previous value and the lower band, then flip short and reset to the upper band if close is below the updated stop. While short, lower the stop to the lesser of its previous value and the upper band, then flip long and reset to the lower band if close is above the updated stop.
Long: Trail direction = long.
Short: Trail direction = short.
Long: Trail direction changes from short to long AND support confidence >= label confidence threshold AND (higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled OR higher-timeframe direction = long).
Short: Trail direction changes from long to short AND resistance confidence >= label confidence threshold AND (higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled OR higher-timeframe direction = short).
Long: Higher-timeframe direction = long.
Short: Higher-timeframe direction = short.
