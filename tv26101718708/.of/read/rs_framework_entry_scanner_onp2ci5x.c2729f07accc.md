Calculation: Relative strength = the asset’s percentage price change over a lookback minus the benchmark’s percentage price change over the same lookback; use a shorter and a longer lookback.
Calculation: Prior swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over the swing lookback, excluding the current bar.
Long: Count of (shorter-lookback relative strength > 0, longer-lookback relative strength > 0, close > long-term SMA, MACD histogram > 0, RSI within its long-side range) >= minimum long score AND (close within the configured percentage tolerance of the fast EMA AND close > medium-term SMA OR MACD histogram crosses above 0 AND volume > volume moving average * volume multiplier OR close > prior swing high AND volume > volume moving average * volume multiplier OR the long score crosses from below to at least the minimum long score)
Short: Count of (shorter-lookback relative strength < 0, longer-lookback relative strength < 0, close < long-term SMA, MACD histogram < 0, RSI within its short-side range) >= minimum short score AND (close within the configured percentage tolerance of the fast EMA AND close < medium-term SMA OR MACD histogram crosses below 0 AND volume > volume moving average * volume multiplier OR close < prior swing low AND volume > volume moving average * volume multiplier OR the short score crosses from below to at least the minimum short score)
