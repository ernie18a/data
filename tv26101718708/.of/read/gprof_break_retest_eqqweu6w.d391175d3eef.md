Calculation: Directional prices = For each direction d, use (open, high, low, close) for long and (-open, -low, -high, -close) for short; apply all comparisons below independently to each direction and level.
Calculation: Levels = For long, use the running premarket high, previous session high, previous regular-session close, and running trading-week high; for short, use the negatives of the corresponding lows, previous regular-session close, and running trading-week low. Aggregate chart bars using configurable session boundaries and time zone. The previous session high or low can use regular-session bars or all session bars; its roll can occur at the session boundary or regular-session open. The previous regular-session close rolls at the selected roll boundary. Premarket extremes run during the premarket and freeze at the regular-session open; weekly extremes reset at the trading-week boundary. Update levels before evaluating decisions.
Calculation: Thresholds = Each proximity, coil-height, range-height, momentum-body, and gap threshold is the greater of a configurable fraction of ATR and a configurable minimum price increment multiple. ATR uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Breakout buffer and immediate-retest tolerance are configurable price increment multiples.
Calculation: Level state = Maintain an independent state per direction and level: idle, coiling, awaiting confirmation, armed, or awaiting retest. A level is virgin until a directional close exceeds the current level; renew virginity when the level changes, at the selected roll for previous-session levels and previous close, and at the regular-session open for premarket levels. A running weekly level, or premarket level while its session is open, is mature only after its value has remained unchanged for a configurable number of chart bars; other levels are always mature. After resolving a setup, require more than the coil lookback in chart bars before starting another. Evaluate the state machine before consuming virginity on each bar.
Calculation: Coil = Over a configurable window including the current bar, no directional close exceeds the level, the highest directional high is at least level minus proximity, and the highest directional high minus the lowest directional low is at most the coil-height threshold.
Calculation: Range = Over a configurable lookback ending at the previous bar, level minus the lowest directional low is at most the range-height threshold and the highest directional high is at most level plus proximity.
Calculation: Momentum = Directional close exceeds directional open and their difference is at least the momentum-body threshold.
Calculation: Qualified gap = Directional low exceeds the directional high two chart bars earlier by at least the gap threshold.
Calculation: Setup transitions = On each completed chart bar with available ATR, process each enabled level. From idle, if cooldown, maturity and virginity hold, first check for a range break: range holds, directional close exceeds level plus buffer, and previous directional close is at most level. Otherwise, if coil detection is enabled and coil holds, enter coiling and snapshot the level. From coiling, a directional close above the snapshot plus buffer breaks it; otherwise update the snapshot to the current level if the current level is mature and coil holds, or return to idle. On a break, record its bar, snapshot level, directional high as the extreme, and an anchor: the lowest directional low in the coil window including the break bar for a coil break, or the lesser of the level and prior range-window directional low for a range break. If momentum holds on the break bar, enter armed; otherwise enter awaiting confirmation. While awaiting confirmation, update the extreme to the maximum directional high; resolve if close is below the snapshot, otherwise enter armed if close is above the snapshot and momentum or qualified gap holds, otherwise resolve when elapsed bars since break reach the confirmation window. While armed, update the extreme and reset its age on a strictly higher directional high; otherwise increment its age. Resolve if close is below the snapshot or elapsed bars exceed the setup timeout; otherwise signal an immediate retest and resolve if the bar is later than the break and directional low is at most snapshot plus retest tolerance; otherwise, when extreme age reaches the swing-confirmation window, freeze the midpoint of anchor and extreme and enter awaiting retest. While awaiting retest, signal a retest and resolve if directional low is at most the greater of the frozen midpoint and snapshot; otherwise resolve if close is below the snapshot or elapsed bars exceed the timeout. A resolved setup returns to idle and starts cooldown. Range and coil detection can each be enabled independently.
Long: Long-direction state changes to armed OR long-direction retest is signaled.
Short: Short-direction state changes to armed OR short-direction retest is signaled.
