Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR calculated from completed daily bars (a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe).
Calculation: Tracked levels = premarket high/low, previous session high/low, previous regular-session close, and running weekly high/low; previous-session levels roll at the session boundary, and premarket levels freeze at the regular-session open.
Calculation: Live-level maturity = a live level is eligible only after it has remained unchanged for the configured number of chart bars; weekly levels are always live, and premarket levels are live while the premarket session is open. Frozen levels are exempt.
Calculation: Virgin level = a level that has had no close beyond it since it was created or renewed; any close beyond it consumes it until its value changes or its session rolls.
Calculation: Proximity, coil-height, range-height, momentum-body, FVG-size, and retest tolerances = their configured thresholds, with ATR-based thresholds using the completed daily ATR and the configured tick floor where applicable.
Calculation: Tight coil at a level = over the configured short chart-bar window, no close is beyond the level, the window's high (long) or low (short) is within the proximity threshold of the level, and the window's high-low range is within the coil-height threshold.
Calculation: Range sit at a level = over the configured longer chart-bar lookback excluding the current bar, the range is within the range-height threshold of the level and its far boundary is within the proximity threshold of the level.
Calculation: Qualified bullish FVG = current low is above the high two chart bars earlier by at least the FVG-size threshold. Qualified bearish FVG = current high is below the low two chart bars earlier by at least the FVG-size threshold.
Calculation: Momentum candle = absolute close-open body is at least the momentum-body threshold, with close above open for bullish momentum and below open for bearish momentum.
Long: At an enabled upper level (premarket high, previous session high, previous-session close, or weekly high), the level is virgin and, if live, mature; AND either (a) tight coil was established and current close is above the level plus breakout buffer, or (b) current close is above the level plus breakout buffer, previous close is at or below the level, and range sit holds; AND either the breakout bar is a bullish momentum candle or, while closes remain above the level and within the configured confirmation window from the break, a bullish momentum candle or qualified bullish FVG occurs. Also mark long on a later retest while armed: before swing confirmation, after the break bar, low is at or below the level plus retest tolerance; OR after the configured swing-confirmation count of chart bars without a new extreme, low is at or below the greater of the leg midpoint and the level. Cancel the armed setup if close falls below the level or the configured setup timeout expires.
Short: At an enabled lower level (premarket low, previous session low, previous-session close, or weekly low), the level is virgin and, if live, mature; AND either (a) tight coil was established and current close is below the level minus breakout buffer, or (b) current close is below the level minus breakout buffer, previous close is at or above the level, and range sit holds; AND either the breakout bar is a bearish momentum candle or, while closes remain below the level and within the configured confirmation window from the break, a bearish momentum candle or qualified bearish FVG occurs. Also mark short on a later retest while armed: before swing confirmation, after the break bar, high is at or above the level minus retest tolerance; OR after the configured swing-confirmation count of chart bars without a new extreme, high is at or above the lesser of the leg midpoint and the level. Cancel the armed setup if close rises above the level or the configured setup timeout expires.
