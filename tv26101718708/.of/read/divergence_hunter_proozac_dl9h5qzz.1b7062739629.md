Calculation: PivotHigh = a bar whose high is the highest among the N bars before it and the M bars after it; it is only known (confirmed) M bars later, and the decision is evaluated on that confirming bar. PivotLow = the mirror, a bar whose low is the lowest among the N bars before it and the M bars after it, confirmed M bars later.
Calculation: Osc = any of RSI of close, MACD line (EMA of close with shorter length minus EMA of close with longer length), or OBV (cumulative volume, added on up-close bars and subtracted on down-close bars). Each Osc is tested independently, and the Osc value is read at the pivot bar itself, not the confirming bar.
Long: on the bar confirming a new PivotLow, for any Osc, comparing the pivot low price P and Osc value O at the pivot bar with P_prev and O_prev at the immediately previous confirmed PivotLow: (P < P_prev AND O > O_prev) [regular bullish divergence] OR (P > P_prev AND O < O_prev) [hidden bullish divergence]
Short: on the bar confirming a new PivotHigh, for any Osc, comparing the pivot high price P and Osc value O at the pivot bar with P_prev and O_prev at the immediately previous confirmed PivotHigh: (P > P_prev AND O < O_prev) [regular bearish divergence] OR (P < P_prev AND O > O_prev) [hidden bearish divergence]
