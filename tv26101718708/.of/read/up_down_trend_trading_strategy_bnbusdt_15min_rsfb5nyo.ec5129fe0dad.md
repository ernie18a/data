Calculation: Custom trend = initialize direction upward; set lower band to source minus ATR multiplier times ATR and upper band to source plus ATR multiplier times ATR. If previous close is above the previous lower band, replace the lower band with the greater of its raw value and previous value; if previous close is below the previous upper band, replace the upper band with the lesser of its raw value and previous value. Switch direction upward when previously downward and close exceeds the previous upper band; switch downward when previously upward and close is below the previous lower band. ATR is either Wilder ATR or SMA of true range; the source defaults to OHLC average.
Calculation: Retained low = initialize to the rolling lowest low; update it to the rolling lowest low only when that rolling lowest low decreases.
Calculation: Bottom reversal = on the previous bar, retained low crossed the low in either direction and retained low decreased; on the current bar, open exceeds close OR open two bars ago exceeds current close.
Calculation: Relative volatility = 100 times EMA of rolling standard deviation on source-increasing bars, zero otherwise, divided by the sum of that EMA and the EMA of rolling standard deviation on non-increasing bars, zero otherwise.
Calculation: Oscillator bucket = the lower boundary of the oscillator's parameterized equal-width interval; retain the previous bucket when the oscillator equals its maximum or is undefined. Apply separately to short-, medium-, and long-lookback RSI, relative volatility, and money flow index. RSI uses close for the short lookback and weighted close for the others; relative volatility uses typical price for the short and medium lookbacks and weighted close for the long; money flow uses open, high, and low respectively.
Calculation: Power average = SMA of 100 times (close minus open) divided by (high minus low), plus 50.
Calculation: Smoothed bullish and bearish supertrend bands = SMA of the standard supertrend value separately on bullish and bearish bars, ignoring undefined values.
