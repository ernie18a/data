Calculation: Opening range = for each enabled session, the highest high and lowest low of the first required number of chart bars beginning in that session's opening-range window; the range is available once that count is reached and remains fixed until the next opening-range window for that session.
Calculation: Prior-session range break = after the preceding range window ends and before the first session's opening-range window begins, at least one bar has high above the preceding range's highest high OR low below its lowest low; reset at the start of each preceding range window.
Calculation: Session eligibility = the session is enabled AND its opening range is available AND its entry window is active AND its entry count since the opening-range window began is below the entry limit AND no earlier stopped trade in that session reached the winner R-level threshold AND either no stop-out has occurred since the opening-range window began OR, after the latest stop-out, a close has been strictly between the opening-range low and high during the entry window. If the optional prior-session range filter is enabled, the prior-session range break must also have occurred. Reset the entry count and winner and reversion states at the start of each session's opening-range window.
Calculation: EMA filter = EMA of closes on the configured EMA timeframe; if that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; if lower, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; if equal, use the current chart bar.
Calculation: R = entry close minus entry-bar low for a long, or entry-bar high minus entry close for a short. The R level starts at zero and, at each completed bar while in a position, becomes the maximum of its previous value and the floor of (bar high minus entry price) divided by R for a long, or (entry price minus bar low) divided by R for a short.
Calculation: Stop = entry-bar low for a long or entry-bar high for a short while the R level is below the first trailing level; at the first trailing level, entry price plus an offset for a long or minus the offset for a short; at higher levels, entry price plus (R level minus one) times R for a long or minus that amount for a short. A stop calculated at a bar's close applies from the next bar; the initial stop applies after entry.
Long: Position is flat AND session eligibility is true AND close >= opening-range high plus breakout distance AND close > low AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA filter).
Short: Position is flat AND session eligibility is true AND close <= opening-range low minus breakout distance AND close < high AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA filter).
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR the optional forced close for the entry session is enabled AND the bar is within that session's close window.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR the optional forced close for the entry session is enabled AND the bar is within that session's close window.
