Calculation: Opening gap = current NY RTH open minus prior NY RTH session close
Calculation: Minimum valid gap = configured threshold in points, as a percentage of the RTH open, or as a multiple of ATR(14) from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the configured first N minutes of NY RTH, locked at the range end
Calculation: Pivot high/low = confirmed chart-timeframe pivot using the configured equal left/right lookback; compare consecutive pivots from the same RTH session, using the same lookback for comparison indices
Short: confirmed bar AND valid opening gap AND before the configured detection cutoff AND ((opening gap > 0 AND opening range locked AND no earlier same-session high sweep AND high > opening range high AND close < opening range high) OR (opening gap > 0 AND (opening range locked unless configured to allow SMT within it) AND chart pivot high > prior chart pivot high AND the configured number of usable comparison indices have pivot highs below their respective prior pivot highs AND pivot anchor is in the current RTH session))
Long: confirmed bar AND valid opening gap AND before the configured detection cutoff AND ((opening gap < 0 AND opening range locked AND no earlier same-session low sweep AND low < opening range low AND close > opening range low) OR (opening gap < 0 AND (opening range locked unless configured to allow SMT within it) AND chart pivot low < prior chart pivot low AND the configured number of usable comparison indices have pivot lows above their respective prior pivot lows AND pivot anchor is in the current RTH session))
