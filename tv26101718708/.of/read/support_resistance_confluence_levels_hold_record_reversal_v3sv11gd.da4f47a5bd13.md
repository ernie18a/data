Calculation: Confluence levels = On each confirmed chart bar with positive ATR, process enabled candidates in this order: confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high, pivot low, kernel-maximum high, kernel-minimum low, higher-timeframe pivot high and low when their returned values change, then prior-period highs and lows for each of two different period timeframes when their returned values change. The higher-timeframe pivots use the last completed higher-timeframe bar and equal left and right pivot lengths. The two prior-period calculations use the last completed bars of their respective timeframes; either period timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. A kernel candidate uses a Gaussian Nadaraya-Watson weighted average of closes over a centered historical window; when its middle value is strictly above or below both adjacent values, nominate respectively the high or low at the middle value's corresponding chart bar. For each candidate, merge into the first stored level within max(merge-tolerance parameter, twice the zone-half-width parameter) × current ATR; update its price to the mean of all candidates merged into it and retain each distinct proposing method as a voice. Otherwise create an armed level at the candidate price with one voice, birth bar equal to the current bar, and a fixed half-width equal to the greater of the zone-half-width parameter × birth-bar ATR and a minimum-price-increment parameter. After each candidate, if the level count exceeds its limit, remove the level maximizing abs(level price − close) / ATR − 10 × (voice count − 1). On every bar, add the enabled volume-node voice permanently to a level if it is within the merge tolerance of a node, and add the enabled round-number voice permanently if its distance to the nearest multiple of the round-number step is at most half the merge-tolerance parameter × ATR. A volume node is the midpoint of a price bin whose summed volume is at least 70% of the largest bin's volume; divide the rolling high-to-low range into equal bins and assign each bar's volume to its close's bin. Rebuild the nodes every tenth chart bar after the profile lookback has elapsed, provided cumulative volume is positive. A level is eligible when its voice count is at least the minimum and the current bar is later than its birth bar. The watched levels are the configured number of eligible levels with the nearest distinct prices strictly above close and the same number strictly below close.
Calculation: Level test = For each eligible level, use its current price plus or minus its fixed half-width as zone boundaries. Initially the level is armed. Rearm a disarmed level when close is above the upper boundary plus the rearm parameter × current ATR or below the lower boundary minus that distance. When armed with no open test, open a support test if low ≤ upper boundary AND high ≥ lower boundary AND previous close > upper boundary; otherwise open a resistance test if low ≤ upper boundary AND high ≥ lower boundary AND previous close < lower boundary. Resolve a support test as held when close > upper boundary or broken when close < lower boundary; resolve a resistance test as held when close < lower boundary or broken when close > upper boundary. Resolution can occur on the opening bar. After a resolution, disarm the level and close its test. If neither resolution occurs and elapsed bars since opening exceed the test timeout, close the test and disarm the level. Update candidates and voices before processing tests.
Calculation: Relative volume = Current volume divided by its simple moving average, with missing volume treated as zero and the denominator floored at a small positive parameter; use 1 when cumulative volume is zero.
Calculation: Reversal setup = A watched level's held test passes when the relative-volume threshold is disabled or relative volume ≥ that threshold. On a bar with passing holds, use the price of the last passing level in stored-level order; choose long if any passing hold is a support hold, otherwise short. Entry is close. For long, stop = min(entry-bar low, selected level price) − stop-buffer parameter × entry-bar ATR; for short, stop = max(entry-bar high, selected level price) + stop-buffer parameter × entry-bar ATR. Risk = abs(entry − stop); target = entry plus risk × target-multiple parameter for long, or entry minus that amount for short. A new positive-risk setup replaces the previous setup.
Long: A watched support test is held AND the relative-volume threshold is disabled OR relative volume ≥ the threshold, grouped as a single volume condition, AND reversal-setup risk > 0.
Short: A watched resistance test is held AND the relative-volume threshold is disabled OR relative volume ≥ the threshold, grouped as a single volume condition, AND no watched support test passes the volume condition AND reversal-setup risk > 0.
Short: A watched support test is broken.
Long: A watched resistance test is broken.
Exit: long, after the entry bar AND low ≤ the setup stop OR high ≥ the setup target OR bars elapsed since entry ≥ the grading horizon, grouped as a single exit condition.
Exit: short, after the entry bar AND high ≥ the setup stop OR low ≤ the setup target OR bars elapsed since entry ≥ the grading horizon, grouped as a single exit condition.
