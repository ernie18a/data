Calculation: Hurst exponent = For a lookback of n closes, subtract their mean from each close, accumulate the deviations from the current bar backward, and compute R as the maximum cumulative sum minus the minimum cumulative sum, including zero in both extrema. Let S be the population standard deviation of those closes. If R/S is positive, clamp log(R/S)/log(n/2) to [0,1]; otherwise use the neutral Hurst level.
Calculation: Vortex for period p = close + sin(bar index × rotation speed / p) × ATR(p) × volume factor, where bar index starts at zero and volume factor is either 1 or 1 + (volume / SMA(volume) − 1) × volume amplification coefficient.
Calculation: Trend strength = absolute value of (Hurst exponent − neutral Hurst level) × trend strength multiplier.
Long: Any of the five vortices, each with a different period, > close.
Short: Any of the five vortices, each with a different period, <= close.
Long: Bars since the previous fractal signal > cooldown, with the previous signal bar initialized to bar index zero, AND ((Hurst exponent > high Hurst threshold AND ATR > SMA of ATR × volatility expansion multiplier) OR (Hurst exponent < low Hurst threshold AND RSI < low RSI threshold) OR (trend strength > fractal strength threshold AND both shortest-period vortices > close AND RSI < upper RSI threshold)).
Short: Bars since the previous cascade signal > cooldown, with the previous signal bar initialized to bar index zero, AND ((Hurst exponent > cascade high Hurst threshold AND close > SMA of close AND RSI > cascade high RSI threshold AND both shortest-period vortices < close) OR (Hurst exponent < cascade low Hurst threshold AND close < SMA of close AND RSI < cascade low RSI threshold AND both shortest-period vortices > close) OR (ATR > SMA of ATR × volatility expansion multiplier AND trend strength > cascade strength threshold AND absolute value of (close − SMA of close) / SMA of close > distance threshold)).
Exit: long, Hurst exponent < exit Hurst threshold.
Exit: short, Hurst exponent < exit Hurst threshold.
