Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by the simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Volume spike = relative volume is at least the configured weak threshold; in graded mode, classify the spike as strong if it meets the strong threshold, medium if it meets the medium threshold but not the strong threshold, otherwise weak. In fixed mode, any spike meeting the weak threshold qualifies.
Calculation: Relative body size = absolute difference between close and open divided by the simple moving average of absolute close-open differences over the volume lookback.
Calculation: Spike concentration = relative volume divided by the greater of relative body size and 0.25.
Calculation: Active trigger range = the high and low of the first qualifying spike in a burst, replaced by a later qualifying spike only when its concentration is greater than the active trigger's concentration. Each qualifying spike resets the burst's confirmation timeout. The burst expires when the number of bars since its most recent qualifying spike exceeds the configured confirmation window.
Long: Confirmed-direction mode AND an active, untriggered burst AND current bar is after the active trigger bar AND confirmation has not expired AND (Wick mode: high > active trigger high OR Body mode: greater of open and close > active trigger high OR Close mode: close > active trigger high); Body and Close modes require the current bar to be completed.
Short: Confirmed-direction mode AND an active, untriggered burst AND current bar is after the active trigger bar AND confirmation has not expired AND (Wick mode: low < active trigger low OR Body mode: lesser of open and close < active trigger low OR Close mode: close < active trigger low); Body and Close modes require the current bar to be completed.
