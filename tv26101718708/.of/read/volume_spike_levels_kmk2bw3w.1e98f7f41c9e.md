Calculation: Volume spike = volume >= SMA(volume) multiplied by the fixed-mode threshold, or, in graded mode, volume >= SMA(volume) multiplied by any of the weak, medium, or strong thresholds. The averages of volume and absolute candle body use the same lookback.
Calculation: Concentration = (volume / SMA(volume)) / max(absolute(close - open) / SMA(absolute(close - open)), body-size floor). It is undefined when either average is not positive.
Calculation: Active burst = maintain one trigger candle and the bar of the most recent qualifying spike. On a completed bar, expire the burst before checking for confirmation if bars since its most recent spike > confirmation window. After checking for confirmation, process a completed volume spike with defined concentration: start a burst if none is active, otherwise update the most recent spike bar and replace the trigger only if the new concentration > the trigger's concentration. A bar that confirmed a burst cannot start a new burst. End the burst after a long or short confirmation; evaluate both directions before ending it.
Long: Confirmed-direction mode AND burst is active AND current bar index > trigger bar index AND (wick mode AND high > trigger high OR body mode AND bar is complete AND max(open, close) > trigger high OR close mode AND bar is complete AND close > trigger high).
Short: Confirmed-direction mode AND burst is active AND current bar index > trigger bar index AND (wick mode AND low < trigger low OR body mode AND bar is complete AND min(open, close) < trigger low OR close mode AND bar is complete AND close < trigger low).
