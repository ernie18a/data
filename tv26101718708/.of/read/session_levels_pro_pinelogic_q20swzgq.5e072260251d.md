Calculation: Previous day high = high of the last completed bar of the higher timeframe covering a day
Calculation: Previous day low = low of the last completed bar of the higher timeframe covering a day
Calculation: Previous week high = high of the last completed bar of the higher timeframe covering a week
Calculation: Previous week low = low of the last completed bar of the higher timeframe covering a week
Calculation: Opening range high = highest high from the first bar of the session through bars starting before the configured opening-range duration has elapsed from that bar
Calculation: Opening range low = lowest low from the first bar of the session through bars starting before the configured opening-range duration has elapsed from that bar
Long: close crosses above previous day high OR close crosses above previous week high OR close crosses above opening range high
Short: close crosses below previous day low OR close crosses below previous week low OR close crosses below opening range low
