Calculation: Zone book = On confirmed chart bars after a warmup based on the pivot, reaction, ATR, and volume lookbacks, confirm pivot highs and lows using equal numbers of bars on each side. For each pivot, score its volume relative to the average volume preceding the pivot and its largest reaction over the shorter of the reaction window and pivot confirmation delay, normalized by ATR at the pivot; add a baseline and cap the score. A high pivot creates resistance and a low pivot creates support. At confirmation, merge the pivot into the closest unbroken zone of the same type whose midpoint is within a parameterized multiple of current ATR; otherwise create a zone around the pivot with parameterized ATR padding. A merge expands the bounds to include the padded pivot, increments touches, and adds a capped fraction of the pivot score and a square-root touch bonus. On every bar subtract a parameterized score decay, remove zones below a minimum score, older than a maximum age, or broken longer than a multiple of the maximum retest window, then remove the lowest-scored zones until the maximum count is met. Store creation time at the pivot bar. Process pivot additions, decay, removal and pruning before breaks.
Calculation: Break candidates = Unbroken resistance zones where close > zone top + optional ATR confirmation distance AND previous close <= the current zone top, and unbroken support zones where close < zone bottom - optional ATR confirmation distance AND previous close >= the current zone bottom. For either direction, optionally require volume > 0 AND volume >= a multiple of average volume over preceding bars, optionally also average volume > 0 AND volume <= a higher multiple of that average; optionally require high - low >= a multiple of ATR. Select the highest-scored candidate across both directions, with resistance winning an equal-score cross-direction tie and the earliest stored zone winning an equal-score same-direction tie. Mark only that zone broken and add its bounds, direction, score and break bar to a bounded FIFO retest queue, evicting the oldest when full.
Calculation: Retest candidates = Scan the break queue newest first; remove entries older than the maximum retest window. A candidate must be at least the minimum and at most the maximum number of bars after its break. Optionally exclude entries already retested. Select at most the newest qualifying candidate per direction and mark it retested; without the optional exclusion it can qualify again. A bullish candidate requires low <= stored zone top AND close > stored zone top AND close - low >= a parameterized multiple of current ATR. A bearish candidate requires high >= stored zone bottom AND close < stored zone bottom AND high - close >= a parameterized multiple of current ATR. Process break candidates before retests, and preserve the selected zone bounds for risk calculations; when several signals occur on one bar, the last qualifying retest in the newest-first scan supplies those bounds.
Calculation: Initial stop and targets = At entry close, set the initial long stop to entry minus an ATR multiple and the initial short stop to entry plus that multiple. Optionally compare it with the selected zone bottom minus an ATR buffer for a long or zone top plus an ATR buffer for a short, choosing the lower or higher stop respectively for the wider setting, and the opposite for the tighter setting. Risk distance is the absolute difference between entry and initial stop. The first and final targets are entry plus risk distance times their respective multipliers for a long, or entry minus those distances for a short; the final multiplier is larger. From the bar after entry, if the first target is touched and the stop is not touched on that bar, optionally move the stop to entry for subsequent bars. Evaluate the stop before this update.
Long: The selected break candidate is resistance OR a bullish retest candidate qualifies.
Short: The selected break candidate is support OR a bearish retest candidate qualifies.
Exit: long, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (low <= current stop OR high >= final target).
Exit: short, at least one bar has elapsed since entry AND (high >= current stop OR low <= final target).
Exit: long OR short, intraday force-close is enabled AND the bar time is at or after the configured force-close time.
