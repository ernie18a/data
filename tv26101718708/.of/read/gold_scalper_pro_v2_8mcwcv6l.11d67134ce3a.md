Calculation: Bullish score = count of satisfied conditions: (1) short EMA > middle EMA > long EMA AND short EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago AND middle EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago; (2) close > middle EMA; (3) RSI > bullish lower threshold AND RSI < overbought threshold AND RSI > previous RSI; (4) MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND the MACD line crossed above its signal within the recent-cross lookback; (5) stochastic K crosses above D AND K < overbought threshold, OR K > D AND oversold threshold < K < overbought threshold; (6) close > Bollinger middle band AND previous close <= current middle band, OR low <= lower band AND close > lower band; (7) ADX >= trend threshold AND positive DI > negative DI; (8) close > open AND close - open > candle-range fraction × (high - low).
Calculation: Bearish score = count of satisfied conditions: (1) short EMA < middle EMA < long EMA AND short EMA < its value a slope-lookback ago AND middle EMA < its value a slope-lookback ago; (2) close < middle EMA; (3) RSI < bearish upper threshold AND RSI > oversold threshold AND RSI < previous RSI; (4) MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram <= previous histogram AND the MACD line crossed below its signal within the recent-cross lookback; (5) stochastic K crosses below D AND K > oversold threshold, OR K < D AND oversold threshold < K < overbought threshold; (6) close < Bollinger middle band AND previous close >= current middle band, OR high >= upper band AND close < upper band; (7) ADX >= trend threshold AND negative DI > positive DI; (8) close < open AND open - close > candle-range fraction × (high - low).
Calculation: Stochastic K = simple moving average of raw stochastic %K; stochastic D = simple moving average of K, with independently parameterized lookbacks.
Calculation: ADX and DI = positive DM is high - previous high when it exceeds both previous low - low and zero, otherwise zero; negative DM is previous low - low when it exceeds both high - previous high and zero, otherwise zero. Each DI is its DM's Wilder moving average divided by the Wilder moving average of true range, multiplied by 100. ADX is the Wilder moving average of 100 × abs(positive DI - negative DI) / (positive DI + negative DI), using the same lookback.
Calculation: Raw bullish signal = bullish score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA) AND ATR < ATR simple moving average × volatility multiplier AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume simple moving average × volume multiplier) AND bars since the last final bullish signal >= cooldown. Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time; their lookbacks match the chart's short and long EMAs. The initial cooldown is satisfied.
Calculation: Raw bearish signal = bearish score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA < higher-timeframe long EMA) AND ATR < ATR simple moving average × volatility multiplier AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume simple moving average × volume multiplier) AND bars since the last final bearish signal >= cooldown. Higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time; their lookbacks match the chart's short and long EMAs. The initial cooldown is satisfied.
Long: Raw bullish signal is true AND raw bearish signal is false.
Short: Raw bearish signal is true AND raw bullish signal is false.
Long: Short EMA > middle EMA AND middle EMA > long EMA.
Short: Short EMA < middle EMA AND middle EMA < long EMA.
Long: Higher-timeframe short EMA > higher-timeframe long EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the forming bar in real time.
Short: Higher-timeframe short EMA <= higher-timeframe long EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical backtests and the forming bar in real time.
Long: MACD line > signal line.
Short: MACD line <= signal line.
Long: Stochastic K > D.
Short: Stochastic K <= D.
Long: Short EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago OR middle EMA > its value a slope-lookback ago OR short EMA > middle EMA.
Short: Short EMA <= its value a slope-lookback ago OR middle EMA <= its value a slope-lookback ago OR short EMA < middle EMA.
