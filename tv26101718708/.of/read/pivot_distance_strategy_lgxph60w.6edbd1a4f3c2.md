Calculation: Pivot distance = 100 × (close / latest confirmed pivot high − latest confirmed pivot low / close); a pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars, using the configured left- and right-hand pivot lengths.
Calculation: Squeeze threshold and breakout threshold = respectively selected constants, ATR / close × 100 times their respective multipliers, Bollinger Band width of pivot distance times their respective ratios, or Keltner width times their respective ratios. Bollinger width is 2 × standard deviation of pivot distance × its multiplier. Keltner width is 2 × its multiplier × either ATR or the SMA of the rolling standard deviation of pivot distance. Higher-timeframe thresholds use only the constant or ATR alternatives.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on the higher timeframe, using the last completed bar and forward-filling between updates, classify pivot distance as neutral if its absolute value is at most the squeeze threshold, long if it is above the breakout threshold, short if it is below the negative breakout threshold, and otherwise neutral; alternatively classify the faster pivot-distance MA by its sign, or classify the faster MA relative to the slower MA, with equality neutral. Retain the previous bias when the required pivot distance or MA is unavailable; initialize it neutral. On a still-forming higher-timeframe bar, live calculations can use its developing values.
Calculation: Same-side cooldown = enabled AND fewer than the configured number of bars have elapsed since that side's qualifying stop exit, enabled trailing-stop exit, or enabled breakout-cross exit in breakout mode. A qualifying stop exit is a stop/limit order exit whose fill is within a configured ATR fraction of entry price minus the current ATR stop distance for a long, or plus it for a short.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after close − entry price reaches the activation ATR multiple, the running maximum of close − the trailing ATR multiple; short trailing stop = after entry price − close reaches the activation ATR multiple, the running minimum of close + the trailing ATR multiple. Reset on a new same-side entry or exit.
Long: Within the configured date range AND long trading is enabled AND same-side cooldown is inactive AND (higher-timeframe filtering is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is long) AND (in breakout mode, pivot distance > squeeze threshold OR in zero-cross mode, pivot-distance MA > 0 AND its previous value <= 0 OR in dual-MA mode, faster pivot-distance MA > slower pivot-distance MA AND its previous value <= the previous slower MA).
Short: Within the configured date range AND short trading is enabled AND same-side cooldown is inactive AND (higher-timeframe filtering is disabled OR higher-timeframe bias is short) AND (in breakout mode, pivot distance < −squeeze threshold OR in zero-cross mode, pivot-distance MA < 0 AND its previous value >= 0 OR in dual-MA mode, faster pivot-distance MA < slower pivot-distance MA AND its previous value >= the previous slower MA).
Long: Breakout mode AND absolute previous pivot distance <= current squeeze threshold AND pivot distance > current breakout threshold.
Short: Breakout mode AND absolute previous pivot distance <= current squeeze threshold AND pivot distance < −current breakout threshold.
Long: Breakout mode AND absolute pivot distance > squeeze threshold AND pivot distance >= 0; OR zero-cross mode AND pivot-distance MA does not cross below zero AND pivot distance >= 0; OR dual-MA mode AND faster pivot-distance MA does not cross below slower pivot-distance MA AND faster pivot-distance MA > slower pivot-distance MA.
Short: Breakout mode AND absolute pivot distance > squeeze threshold AND pivot distance < 0; OR zero-cross mode AND pivot-distance MA does not cross above zero AND pivot distance < 0; OR dual-MA mode AND faster pivot-distance MA does not cross above slower pivot-distance MA AND faster pivot-distance MA <= slower pivot-distance MA.
Long: Higher-timeframe filtering is enabled AND higher-timeframe bias changes to long.
Short: Higher-timeframe filtering is enabled AND higher-timeframe bias changes to short.
Exit: long, within the configured date range AND (higher-timeframe filtering is enabled AND higher-timeframe bias is not long OR both-direction trading is enabled AND the filtered short entry condition holds OR long-only mode AND (breakout mode AND pivot distance < 0 OR zero-cross mode AND pivot-distance MA crosses below zero OR dual-MA mode AND faster pivot-distance MA crosses below slower pivot-distance MA) OR breakout-cross exit is enabled AND breakout mode AND pivot distance < current breakout threshold AND previous pivot distance >= current breakout threshold OR trailing stop is enabled AND close <= long trailing stop OR no active trailing stop AND stop loss is enabled AND low reaches entry price − current ATR × stop multiplier OR no active trailing stop AND take profit is enabled AND high reaches entry price + current ATR × target multiplier).
Exit: short, within the configured date range AND (higher-timeframe filtering is enabled AND higher-timeframe bias is not short OR both-direction trading is enabled AND the filtered long entry condition holds OR short-only mode AND (breakout mode AND pivot distance > 0 OR zero-cross mode AND pivot-distance MA crosses above zero OR dual-MA mode AND faster pivot-distance MA crosses above slower pivot-distance MA) OR breakout-cross exit is enabled AND breakout mode AND pivot distance > −current breakout threshold AND previous pivot distance <= −current breakout threshold OR trailing stop is enabled AND close >= short trailing stop OR no active trailing stop AND stop loss is enabled AND high reaches entry price + current ATR × stop multiplier OR no active trailing stop AND take profit is enabled AND low reaches entry price − current ATR × target multiplier).
