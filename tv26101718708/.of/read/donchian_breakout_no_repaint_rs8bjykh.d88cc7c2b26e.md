Calculation: Donchian upper/lower band = highest high / lowest low over the respective lookback, using the last completed chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: ADR = 21-bar simple moving average of (daily high − daily low) / |daily low| × 100, using the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming.
Calculation: Support/resistance distance = (previous Donchian upper band − previous Donchian lower band) / previous Donchian upper band × 100.
Calculation: Selected stop = with the tighter initial stop enabled, use the previous tighter lower Donchian band until entry price ≤ that band; then hold that stop, raising it to the previous main lower Donchian band whenever that band is higher. Otherwise use the previous main lower Donchian band.
Long: close > previous Donchian upper band (close trigger) OR high > previous Donchian upper band (wick trigger), AND, when enabled, previous Donchian upper band > chart-timeframe moving average, AND, when enabled, previous Donchian upper band > higher-timeframe moving average (higher-timeframe bar still forming), AND, when enabled, support/resistance distance < ADR × configured percentage, AND, when enabled, higher-timeframe moving average is rising versus its prior higher-timeframe bar (higher-timeframe bar still forming), AND datetime > start date AND datetime < finish date.
Exit: long, close < selected stop (close trigger) OR low < selected stop (wick trigger).
