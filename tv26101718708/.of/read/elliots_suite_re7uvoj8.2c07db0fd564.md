Calculation: Confirmed swing high = high of a bar higher than the highs of the configured number of bars before and after it; use the same left and right lookbacks as for the confirmed swing low
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = low of a bar lower than the lows of the configured number of bars before and after it; use the same left and right lookbacks as for the confirmed swing high
Long: Close crosses above the most recent confirmed swing high
Short: Close crosses below the most recent confirmed swing low
