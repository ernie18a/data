Calculation: Reference range and volume = high, low, and mean chart-bar volume of the last completed higher-timeframe period; on the first chart bar of a new period, evaluate entry conditions using the previously stored reference range, volume, and trend, then update them to the just-completed period.
Calculation: Trend = bullish if the last completed higher-timeframe period's high and low are both greater than those of the preceding completed period; bearish if both are lower; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Volume gate = volume filtering is disabled OR the selected positive volume baseline multiplied by a volume multiplier is less than or equal to current volume; the baseline is either the reference period's mean chart-bar volume, the chart-bar volume SMA including the current bar, or the previous chart-bar volume.
Calculation: Setup = a qualifying range break arms its direction through the current higher-timeframe period; reset armed setups and per-direction signal counts at each new period before processing that bar's entry signal.
Calculation: Trailing stop = SuperTrend at entry, subsequently updated before exit checks to the maximum of the previous stop and current bullish SuperTrend for a long, or the minimum of the previous stop and current bearish SuperTrend for a short; retain the previous stop when the corresponding SuperTrend is unavailable.
Calculation: ATR targets = entry close plus for a long or minus for a short the entry-bar ATR multiplied by the selected target multiplier; freeze targets at entry. The single-target and each of the three scale-out multipliers are independently configurable.
Long: No prior long signal in the current higher-timeframe period AND (trend filtering is disabled OR trend is bearish) AND volume gate passes AND low < reference low AND SuperTrend linkage is disabled.
Long: No prior long signal in the current higher-timeframe period AND SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND a long setup is armed by low < reference low AND volume gate passes AND (trend filtering is disabled OR trend is bearish), on this bar or an earlier bar in the current period, AND SuperTrend flips from bearish to bullish on the current bar.
Short: No prior short signal in the current higher-timeframe period AND (trend filtering is disabled OR trend is bullish) AND volume gate passes AND high > reference high AND SuperTrend linkage is disabled.
Short: No prior short signal in the current higher-timeframe period AND SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND a short setup is armed by high > reference high AND volume gate passes AND (trend filtering is disabled OR trend is bullish), on this bar or an earlier bar in the current period, AND SuperTrend flips from bullish to bearish on the current bar.
Exit: long, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND low <= trailing stop; exit the entire remaining position at the stop, with priority over all other exits on the bar.
Exit: short, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND high >= trailing stop; exit the entire remaining position at the stop, with priority over all other exits on the bar.
Exit: long, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND low > trailing stop AND dynamic ATR exit is selected AND high >= single ATR target; exit the entire remaining position at the target.
Exit: short, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND high < trailing stop AND dynamic ATR exit is selected AND low <= single ATR target; exit the entire remaining position at the target.
Exit: long, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND low > trailing stop AND scale-out exit is selected AND high >= an unfilled scale-out ATR target AND its configured exit quantity > 0; exit that configured fraction of the original position, capped at the remaining position, at the target. Process the first and second targets in order, then the third target for the entire remainder only if third-target remainder exit is selected.
Exit: short, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND high < trailing stop AND scale-out exit is selected AND low <= an unfilled scale-out ATR target AND its configured exit quantity > 0; exit that configured fraction of the original position, capped at the remaining position, at the target. Process the first and second targets in order, then the third target for the entire remainder only if third-target remainder exit is selected.
Exit: long, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND low > trailing stop AND (SuperTrend exit is selected OR scale-out exit with SuperTrend-trailing remainder is selected) AND SuperTrend flips from bullish to bearish; exit any remaining position at close after processing scale-out targets.
Exit: short, SuperTrend linkage is enabled AND high < trailing stop AND (SuperTrend exit is selected OR scale-out exit with SuperTrend-trailing remainder is selected) AND SuperTrend flips from bearish to bullish; exit any remaining position at close after processing scale-out targets.
