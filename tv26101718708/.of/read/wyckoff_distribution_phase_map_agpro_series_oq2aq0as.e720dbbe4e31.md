Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding and following swing-lookback bars; confirmed swing low = a low lower than the lows of those bars. Each pivot is confirmed after the following bars complete.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / simple moving average of volume.
Short: During an uptrend (each of the two latest confirmed swing highs and lows is higher than its predecessor), track the highest confirmed swing high as BC, the latest earlier confirmed swing high below BC with elevated volume as PSY, and the lowest low after BC as AR; when close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low from at or above it, mark BC, AR, and PSY if available (PSY only if its pivot is more than one swing-lookback away from BC). After that break, in order: mark UT when a later confirmed swing high is above BC by more than 0.1%, its close is below BC, and its volume ratio is elevated or higher; mark SOW when a later bar's close is below AR, its volume ratio is elevated or higher, and it closes below its open; mark LPSY when a later confirmed swing high is below BC and its volume ratio is weak or lower.
