Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low with the same lookback on each side, recognized when the right-hand lookback has completed. Use the pivot bar's price, volume, and bar index when evaluating the pivot.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume divided by its simple moving average, or zero when the average is not positive. Elevated and weak volume mean the ratio is respectively at least an elevated threshold and at most a lower weak threshold.
Calculation: Distribution state = initially inactive with neutral trend and no candidate. On each bar, update the two most recently confirmed swing highs and lows. Set the trend to up when the latest high exceeds the preceding high and the latest low exceeds the preceding low, unless the trend is locked bearish. While the trend is up, maintain the candidate high as the highest confirmed swing high since the candidate was cleared; whenever it is replaced, clear the candidate low. After a candidate high exists, update the candidate low to the lowest bar low observed on bars after that high, including the current bar. Clear the candidates if the candidate high exceeds its maximum age while no distribution is active. On a distribution break, freeze the candidate high and low as the range high and low, activate the distribution, lock the trend bearish, and set the stage to awaiting an upthrust. Advance the stage on each subsequent short condition in order. After the incomplete-distribution timeout measured from the break, or after the completed-distribution timeout measured from the last supply pivot bar, clear the distribution and candidates and set the trend neutral. Process pivot and candidate updates, candidate expiry, the break, the stage transitions, and distribution expiry in that order on each bar.
Short: Distribution inactive AND trend up AND candidate high exists AND current bar is after the candidate high bar AND close < the preceding confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low.
Short: Stage awaiting an upthrust AND a swing high is confirmed AND its pivot bar is after the distribution break AND its high > range high multiplied by a factor above one AND its close < range high AND its volume ratio is elevated.
Short: Stage awaiting weakness AND the bar one swing lookback ago is after the upthrust pivot bar AND its close < range low AND its volume ratio is elevated AND its close < its open.
Short: Stage awaiting the last supply point AND a swing high is confirmed AND its pivot bar is after the weakness bar AND its high < range high AND its volume ratio is weak.
