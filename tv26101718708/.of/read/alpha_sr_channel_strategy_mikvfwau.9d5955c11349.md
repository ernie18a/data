Calculation: Channel = higher-timeframe EMA of the selected price source, optionally shifted by a configured number of higher-timeframe bars, plus and minus half its width; width is either chart close times a percentage, higher-timeframe ATR times a multiplier, or a fixed distance. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar, including the higher-timeframe bar that completes on the current chart bar.
Calculation: Directional bias = initially unset; set long when the condition that both the current and previous chart closes exceed the current upper channel changes from false to true; set short when the condition that both closes are below the current lower channel changes from false to true; retain the last direction otherwise.
Calculation: Engulfing minimum and maximum body sizes = chart close times respective percentages, chart-timeframe ATR times respective multipliers, or respective fixed distances. The maximum is greater than the minimum. Previous-body allowance = minimum body size times a fraction.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current close > open AND previous close < open OR previous close > open AND previous close - open <= previous-body allowance, with the previous-candle alternatives grouped together; AND current body size is between the engulfing minimum and maximum inclusive; AND, in body mode, current open <= previous close + gap allowance AND current close > previous open if the previous candle is bearish, otherwise current close > previous high; OR, in full-range mode, current low < previous low AND current high > previous high. The body-mode and full-range-mode alternatives are grouped together.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current close < open AND previous close > open OR previous close < open AND previous open - close <= previous-body allowance, with the previous-candle alternatives grouped together; AND current body size is between the engulfing minimum and maximum inclusive; AND, in body mode, current open >= previous close - gap allowance AND current close < previous open if the previous candle is bullish, otherwise current close < previous low; OR, in full-range mode, current low < previous low AND current high > previous high. The body-mode and full-range-mode alternatives are grouped together.
Calculation: Bullish pin-and-engulf = previous range >= current engulfing minimum times a fraction AND previous range > 0 AND previous absolute body / range <= body limit AND previous lower wick > previous upper wick AND previous lower wick / range >= wick limit AND previous lower wick / absolute body >= wick-to-body limit AND previous low < lowest low over the configured lookback ending two bars ago AND current close > current open AND current close > previous high AND current body size is between current engulfing minimum times the pin-range fraction and the engulfing maximum inclusive.
Calculation: Bearish pin-and-engulf = previous range >= current engulfing minimum times a fraction AND previous range > 0 AND previous absolute body / range <= body limit AND previous upper wick >= previous lower wick AND previous upper wick / range >= wick limit AND previous upper wick / absolute body >= wick-to-body limit AND previous high > highest high over the configured lookback ending two bars ago AND current close < current open AND current close < previous low AND current body size is between current engulfing minimum times the pin-range fraction and the engulfing maximum inclusive.
Calculation: Momentum = linear-regression endpoint of close minus the average of (the average of the lookback highest high and lowest low) and the lookback SMA of close, using the same lookback for all three calculations.
Calculation: Initial stop = at the signal bar, for a long, either low minus a buffer, lowest low over a lookback minus a buffer, lower channel minus a buffer, or close minus a fixed distance; for a short, either high plus a buffer, highest high over a lookback plus a buffer, upper channel plus a buffer, or close plus a fixed distance. The signal-bar close is the reference entry price for stop and target calculations.
Calculation: Target = signal-bar close plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, the signed distance from signal-bar close to initial stop multiplied by the configured reward-to-risk ratio; use separately configured ratios for the first and second entries. Partial target uses its own ratio.
