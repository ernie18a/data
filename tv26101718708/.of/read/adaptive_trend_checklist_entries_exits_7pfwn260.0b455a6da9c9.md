Calculation: Moving average = EMA or SMA of chart close; higher-timeframe comparisons always use EMA with the same length.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = compare the latest completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA and Supertrend; on a forming higher-timeframe bar, use its forming values in real time and its completed values only when the higher-timeframe bar closes in historical evaluation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe directional score = sum the weights of enabled higher-timeframe states whose close is strictly above both EMA and Supertrend for the bullish score, or strictly below both for the bearish score; weights increase by slot order. The required score is a configurable fraction of the sum of enabled weights.
Calculation: Ultra mode = ultra-aggressive mode is selected AND ADX is enabled AND ADX >= its minimum plus a strength margin; otherwise, selected ultra-aggressive mode behaves as aggressive mode.
Calculation: Long setup = close > moving average AND close != Supertrend AND (ultra mode OR close > Supertrend) AND (ADX disabled OR ADX >= its minimum) AND (no higher-timeframe filters enabled OR bullish score >= required score) AND each enabled filter in the first two higher-timeframe slots has its close either above both its EMA and Supertrend or below both AND (ultra mode AND close > previous close OR ultra mode is inactive AND close > previous high).
Calculation: Short setup = close < moving average AND close != Supertrend AND (ultra mode OR close < Supertrend) AND (ADX disabled OR ADX >= its minimum) AND (no higher-timeframe filters enabled OR bearish score >= required score) AND each enabled filter in the first two higher-timeframe slots has its close either above both its EMA and Supertrend or below both AND (ultra mode AND close < previous close OR ultra mode is inactive AND close < previous low).
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry, for a long position, the lower of the lowest low over the swing lookback including the entry bar and entry price minus a multiplier times the absolute distance between entry price and entry-bar Supertrend; for a short position, the higher of the highest high over the swing lookback including the entry bar and entry price plus that distance. The swing lookback is shortest in ultra mode, longer in aggressive mode, and longest in conservative mode.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a selected reward multiplier times the absolute distance from entry price to initial stop; the three selectable target multipliers increase from the first target to the third.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial stop updated on every bar starting with the entry bar to the maximum of its previous value and current Supertrend for a long, or the minimum for a short.
Long: close > moving average
Short: close < moving average
Long: close > Supertrend
Short: close < Supertrend
Long: an enabled higher-timeframe state's close > its EMA AND its close > its Supertrend
Short: an enabled higher-timeframe state's close < its EMA AND its close < its Supertrend
Long: higher-timeframe score display is enabled AND bullish score minus bearish score >= bullish score display threshold
Short: higher-timeframe score display is enabled AND bullish score minus bearish score <= bearish score display threshold
Long: position side = flat AND long setup
Short: position side = flat AND short setup
Exit: long, low <= initial stop OR (target exit mode selected AND high >= selected profit target) OR (target-plus-trailing mode selected AND (high >= its selected profit target OR low <= trailing stop)) OR (trailing mode selected AND low <= trailing stop) OR (trend-reversal mode selected AND (close < moving average OR close < Supertrend OR short setup))
Exit: short, high >= initial stop OR (target exit mode selected AND low <= selected profit target) OR (target-plus-trailing mode selected AND (low <= its selected profit target OR high >= trailing stop)) OR (trailing mode selected AND high >= trailing stop) OR (trend-reversal mode selected AND (close > moving average OR close > Supertrend OR long setup))
