Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend state = close above both the EMA and Supertrend is bullish; close below both is bearish. Use enabled higher timeframes, each higher than the chart timeframe, with the bar still forming.\nCalculation: Higher-timeframe confirmation = bullish (or bearish) weighted score is at least 60% of the total enabled weight; give successively higher weights to successively higher timeframes. If no higher timeframe is enabled, confirmation passes.\nCalculation: Recent swing low/high = lowest low / highest high over a short lookback in ultra-aggressive mode, a longer lookback in aggressive mode, and a still longer lookback in conservative mode.\nCalculation: Initial stop = for a long, the lower of the recent swing low and entry price minus the absolute distance from entry price to Supertrend multiplied by a volatility-based factor; for a short, the higher of the recent swing high and entry price plus that distance multiplied by the factor.\nCalculation: Profit targets = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) one, two, or three times the absolute distance between entry price and initial stop.\nCalculation: Trailing stop = for a long, the highest value reached by the greater of the initial stop and Supertrend; for a short, the lowest value reached by the lesser of the initial stop and Supertrend.\nLong: Position is flat AND (close > moving average AND close > Supertrend, or ultra-aggressive mode with strong ADX and close > moving average) AND ADX meets the mode-dependent minimum (base minimum in conservative mode, a lower threshold in aggressive mode, or a still lower threshold in ultra-aggressive mode; ultra-aggressive mode requires ADX at or above the strong-ADX threshold to use its relaxed trend condition) AND higher-timeframe confirmation passes AND no range condition: close is not equal to the moving average or Supertrend, ADX is not below its base minimum, and neither of the first two enabled higher-timeframe trend states is neutral AND (close > previous bar high, or in ultra-aggressive mode close > previous bar close) AND bar is confirmed when close-confirmation is enabled.\nShort: Position is flat AND (close < moving average AND close < Supertrend, or ultra-aggressive mode with strong ADX and close < moving average) AND ADX meets the mode-dependent minimum (base minimum in conservative mode, a lower threshold in aggressive mode, or a still lower threshold in ultra-aggressive mode; ultra-aggressive mode requires ADX at or above the strong-ADX threshold to use its relaxed trend condition) AND higher-timeframe confirmation passes AND no range condition: close is not equal to the moving average or Supertrend, ADX is not below its base minimum, and neither of the first two enabled higher-timeframe trend states is neutral AND (close < previous bar low, or in ultra-aggressive mode close < previous bar close) AND bar is confirmed when close-confirmation is enabled.\nExit: long, low <= initial stop OR (TP1 mode AND high >= target 1) OR (TP2 mode AND high >= target 2) OR (TP3 mode AND high >= target 3) OR (TP2-plus-trailing mode AND (high >= target 2 OR low <= trailing stop)) OR (trailing-supertrend mode AND low <= trailing stop) OR (trend-reversal mode AND bar is confirmed AND (close < moving average OR close < Supertrend OR short-entry condition)).\nExit: short, high >= initial stop OR (TP1 mode AND low <= target 1) OR (TP2 mode AND low <= target 2) OR (TP3 mode AND low <= target 3) OR (TP2-plus-trailing mode AND (low <= target 2 OR high >= trailing stop)) OR (trailing-supertrend mode AND high >= trailing stop) OR (trend-reversal mode AND bar is confirmed AND (close > moving average OR close > Supertrend OR long-entry condition)).
