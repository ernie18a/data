Calculation: Each selected timeframe’s close and EMAs = calculated on that timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its current forming bar by default, or its last completed bar when close-only updates are enabled.
Long: On any selected timeframe, close > EMA with shorter lookback > EMA with longer lookback
Short: On any selected timeframe, close < EMA with shorter lookback < EMA with longer lookback
Long: Count of selected timeframes satisfying the long condition ≥ required agreement count AND count of long conditions > count of short conditions
Short: Count of selected timeframes satisfying the short condition ≥ required agreement count AND count of short conditions > count of long conditions
