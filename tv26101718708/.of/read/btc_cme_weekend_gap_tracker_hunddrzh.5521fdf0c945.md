Long: current CME daily open > previous completed CME daily close AND previous close is available and nonzero AND absolute CME gap % ≥ minimum gap threshold AND elapsed time between the daily bar timestamps > 30 hours AND current CME daily open differs from its value on the prior chart bar or that prior value is unavailable
