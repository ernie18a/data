Calculation: Order-block candidate = At each change of the higher timeframe, record the last completed higher-timeframe bar if the absolute difference between its close and open is at least the minimum body size. Record its open as the level, classify it as bullish if close > open and bearish otherwise, and expire it after the candidate lifetime in chart bars. On each chart bar, inspect candidates newest first and consume at most the first one that meets its confirmation condition, even if its signal is suppressed.
Calculation: Active signals = Retain displayed signals up to the maximum count, removing the oldest when the count is exceeded. Optionally suppress a new signal when an active signal of the same direction exists. A consumed candidate is not reconsidered. Remove an active signal when its stop or target is hit if automatic removal is enabled; otherwise it remains active until displaced by the maximum count. Evaluate removal after new signals are processed, including on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Stop = For a long signal, find a chart-bar low below the candidate level that is strictly lower than each available older low and each newer low within the same pivot strength. For a short signal, find a chart-bar high above the candidate level that is strictly higher than each available older high and each newer high within the same pivot strength. Search pivot strengths from minimum to maximum and, for each strength, search from that many bars ago toward the maximum lookback; select the first qualifying pivot. If none exists, the stop and target are undefined.
Calculation: Target = Candidate level plus the risk-reward multiplier times the absolute difference between candidate level and stop for a long signal, or candidate level minus that product for a short signal.
Long: A bearish candidate has chart close >= its level AND either the previous chart close < its level OR this is the candidate creation bar AND the long signal is enabled AND the active-signal restriction permits it.
Short: A bullish candidate has chart close <= its level AND either the previous chart close > its level OR this is the candidate creation bar AND the short signal is enabled AND the active-signal restriction permits it.
Exit: long, automatic removal is enabled AND the signal stop is defined AND (low <= stop OR high >= target).
Exit: short, automatic removal is enabled AND the signal stop is defined AND (high >= stop OR low <= target).
