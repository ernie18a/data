Calculation: Volume-weighted RSI (VARSI) = Compute close change × volume (use 1 when volume weighting is off or volume is unavailable); average positive and absolute negative changes separately over the RSI lookback using the selected averaging method; return 100 if the negative average is 0, 0 if the positive average is 0, otherwise 100 − 100/(1 + positive average/negative average). Optionally smooth the current-timeframe result with a moving average. Compute the higher-timeframe VARSI using the same RSI parameters on aggregated higher-timeframe bars; use the forming higher-timeframe bar in live mode or the last completed higher-timeframe bar in closed-bar mode.
Long: Current-timeframe VARSI > 50 OR current-timeframe VARSI crosses above 50 OR higher-timeframe VARSI > 50 OR current-timeframe and higher-timeframe VARSI are both > 50 and were not both > 50 on the previous chart bar
Short: Current-timeframe VARSI ≤ 50 OR current-timeframe VARSI crosses below 50 OR higher-timeframe VARSI ≤ 50 OR current-timeframe and higher-timeframe VARSI are both ≤ 50 and were not both ≤ 50 on the previous chart bar
