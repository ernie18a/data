Calculation: Yield change = (EMA(close, smoothing lookback) − EMA(close, comparison lookback bars ago)) × 100, calculated on a higher timeframe; use its forming bar in real time and completed bars historically. The smoothing and comparison lookbacks are shared across yield series.
Calculation: Percentage change = (EMA(close, smoothing lookback) / EMA(close, comparison lookback bars ago) − 1) × 100, calculated on a higher timeframe; use its forming bar in real time and completed bars historically. The lookbacks match those used for yield change.
Calculation: Alignment score = 2 × primary yield direction + enabled context-series directions; each direction is +1 for a change at or below its negative minimum threshold, −1 for a change at or above its positive minimum threshold, and 0 otherwise. The primary yield uses yield change; the secondary yield uses the same yield change calculation; other context series use percentage change. The crude-oil direction is inverted or direct according to its configured relationship.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, primary yield change is at or below its negative minimum threshold AND alignment score is at least the configured positive minimum AND, if secondary-yield confirmation is enabled, secondary yield change is at or below its negative minimum threshold AND, if the chart EMA filter is enabled, close is above the chart EMA AND previous bar's bias was not long AND all required quotes are fresh.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, primary yield change is at or above its positive minimum threshold AND alignment score is at or below the negative configured minimum AND, if secondary-yield confirmation is enabled, secondary yield change is at or above its positive minimum threshold AND, if the chart EMA filter is enabled, close is below the chart EMA AND previous bar's bias was not short AND all required quotes are fresh.
