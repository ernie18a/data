Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA((typical price - EMA(typical price, short lookback)) / (scale × EMA(abs(typical price - EMA(typical price, short lookback)), short lookback)), longer lookback); its signal is an SMA of WaveTrend.
Calculation: Relative volatility index = 100 × EMA(upward standard deviation) / (EMA(upward standard deviation) + EMA(downward standard deviation)), where upward standard deviation is the rolling standard deviation of close when close rises and otherwise zero, and downward standard deviation is the rolling standard deviation when close does not rise and otherwise zero.
Calculation: OBV oscillator = OBV minus its EMA.
Calculation: Pivot levels = the most recent confirmed close pivot high and low with a shorter right confirmation window, plus the three most recent confirmed close pivot highs and lows with a longer right confirmation window; both use the same left lookback.
Calculation: ATR trailing direction = initialize direction to up; raw long stop is rolling highest high minus a multiple of ATR, and raw short stop is rolling lowest low plus that multiple of ATR. If previous close is above the previous long stop, long stop is the greater of its raw value and previous value; otherwise it is its raw value. If previous close is below the previous short stop, short stop is the lesser of its raw value and previous value; otherwise it is its raw value. Set direction to up if close is above the previous short stop, otherwise down if close is below the previous long stop, otherwise retain its previous value.
Calculation: Regular divergence for an oscillator = on the confirmation bar of an oscillator pivot, compare that pivot with the immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type; their confirmation bars must be separated by a specified minimum and maximum number of bars. Bullish divergence has a higher oscillator pivot low and a lower price low at the pivot bars; bearish divergence has a lower oscillator pivot high and a higher price high at the pivot bars. For MACD divergence, the current pivot and the highest MACD over a specified window must be below zero for bullish divergence; the current pivot and the lowest MACD over that window must be above zero for bearish divergence. A divergence contributes to the score on its confirmation bar and the following specified number of bars.
Calculation: Engulfing body average = EMA of abs(close - open). Bullish engulfing requires close below its trend SMA, a bullish current body larger than its body average, a bearish previous body smaller than its previous body average, close >= previous open, open <= previous close, and at least one of those last two inequalities strict. Bearish engulfing reverses all price directions and requires close above its trend SMA.
Calculation: Directional scores = sum the independently weighted true conditions in the following ordered bullish/bearish pairs; use the same weight for both conditions in each pair: close above/below the longest close EMA; shorter EMA rising while longer EMA falls / shorter EMA falling while longer EMA rises; Supertrend up/down; Supertrend changes down-to-up / up-to-down; parabolic SAR below/above close; SAR below close with previous SAR above previous close / SAR above close with previous SAR below current close; close above/below HMA, with the bullish condition also requiring previous HMA to be nonzero; HMA turns up/down; regular bullish/bearish RSI divergence; RSI below oversold / above overbought threshold; RSI above/below its midpoint with RSI on either of the preceding two bars strictly on the opposite side; regular bullish/bearish MACD divergence; MACD histogram turns up below zero / down above zero; MACD crosses strictly above its signal while the signal is below zero / strictly below while the signal is above zero; regular bullish/bearish WaveTrend divergence; WaveTrend crosses strictly above its signal below a lower threshold / strictly below its signal above an upper threshold; regular bullish/bearish divergence of SMA-smoothed stochastic %K; smoothed stochastic %K crosses strictly above/below its SMA signal; close below the lower Bollinger band / open above the upper Bollinger band; ATR trailing direction changes down-to-up / up-to-down; relative volatility index above a lower threshold with its previous value below a higher threshold / below an upper threshold with its previous value above a lower threshold; regular bullish/bearish relative volatility index divergence; shorter HMA strictly crosses above/below any pivot level with close above/below close two bars earlier; regular bullish/bearish OBV oscillator divergence; Chaikin money flow strictly crosses above/below zero; Chaikin money flow above/below zero; session VWAP of typical price strictly crosses above/below HMA; shorter close EMA above/below session VWAP; bullish/bearish engulfing. Each strict cross requires a strict inequality on both the current and previous bars.
Calculation: Alternating signal state = initialize to neutral. On each bar, if the bullish score meets its strong threshold and state is neutral or short, set state to long; then, if the bearish score meets its strong threshold and state is neutral or long, set state to short. A long or short state transition occurs when the final state differs from its previous value and equals that direction.
Long: Bullish score >= weak bullish threshold.
Short: Bearish score >= weak bearish threshold.
Long: (Bullish score >= strong bullish threshold AND alternating mode is disabled OR long state transition AND alternating mode is enabled) AND (trend filter is disabled OR trend EMA > previous trend EMA).
Short: (Bearish score >= strong bearish threshold AND alternating mode is disabled OR short state transition AND alternating mode is enabled) AND (trend filter is disabled OR trend EMA < previous trend EMA).
Long: Close > HMA OR any displayed close EMA > its previous value OR close >= any pivot level.
Short: Close <= HMA OR any displayed close EMA <= its previous value OR close < any pivot level.
Long: A nonzero-weight bullish status condition is true for EMA direction or pressure, HMA position or pivot, RSI divergence or level or midpoint condition, MACD divergence or histogram pivot or signal cross, WaveTrend divergence or signal cross, relative volatility index position or divergence, or VWAP cross or trend, using the corresponding conditions in the directional scores.
Short: A nonzero-weight bearish status condition is true for EMA direction or pressure, HMA position or pivot, RSI divergence or level or midpoint condition, MACD divergence or histogram pivot or signal cross, WaveTrend divergence or signal cross, relative volatility index position or divergence, or VWAP cross or trend, using the corresponding conditions in the directional scores.
Exit: long, short state transition OR close < entry price × (1 - stop percentage) AND previous close > previous entry price × (1 - stop percentage).
Exit: short, long state transition OR close > entry price × (1 + stop percentage) AND previous close < previous entry price × (1 + stop percentage).
