Calculation: Bull probability = 70% of the percentage of summed positive candle bodies among all summed positive and negative candle bodies in the lookback, plus a 30-point bonus when close is above the lookback SMA of close; if the summed bodies are zero, positive and negative body percentages are each 50%.
Calculation: Bear probability = 70% of the percentage of summed negative candle bodies among all summed positive and negative candle bodies in the lookback, plus a 30-point bonus when close is below the lookback SMA of close; if the summed bodies are zero, positive and negative body percentages are each 50%.
Long: Bull probability > minimum probability AND (MTF agreement disabled OR at least 3 of the local and three configured higher-timeframe biases have bull probability > minimum probability and bear probability ≤ minimum probability); create a pending setup only when the previous bias was not bullish and no setup is pending or active; enter when low ≤ the setup bar's close.
Short: Bear probability > minimum probability AND bull probability ≤ minimum probability AND (MTF agreement disabled OR at least 3 of the local and three configured higher-timeframe biases have bear probability > minimum probability and bull probability ≤ minimum probability); create a pending setup only when the previous bias was not bearish and no setup is pending or active; enter when high ≥ the setup bar's close.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the stop lookback, reduced by the stop buffer; long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward-to-risk ratio.
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over the stop lookback, increased by the stop buffer; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
