Calculation: Key levels = enabled close SMAs with shorter and longer lookbacks on each of three higher timeframes; previous day high and low; previous session high and low; Fibonacci levels; and active swing levels. Higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed bar. Track session high and low from chart bars in the configured session; finalize them when the session ends or a new day begins, reset the running values on a new day, and restart them on session entry.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = most recently confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low plus a configured retracement ratio times the difference between the most recently confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high and low. Use four configured ratios. A pivot is confirmed after equal numbers of bars on each side.
Calculation: Active swing levels = confirmed higher-timeframe pivot highs and lows with equal numbers of bars on each side, added when the confirmation bar's volume exceeds both its volume SMA and the preceding higher-timeframe bar's volume. Keep high and low levels separately; reject a new level if it is within a configured distance of an existing level of the same kind. Before evaluating signals, remove high levels when chart close exceeds them and low levels when chart close falls below them.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of chart-bar typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset each session.
Long: At least one enabled key level is positive AND close > that level AND close > open AND (low <= that level + touch buffer OR previous close < that level) AND (strong-body filter disabled OR absolute value of close minus open divided by high minus low, or zero when high equals low, >= body threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its chart-timeframe SMA) AND (VWAP bias disabled OR selected price bias AND close > session VWAP OR selected slope bias AND session VWAP > its value at the slope lookback OR selected combined bias AND close > session VWAP AND session VWAP > its value at the slope lookback).
Short: At least one enabled key level is positive AND close < that level AND close < open AND (high >= that level - touch buffer OR previous close > that level) AND (strong-body filter disabled OR absolute value of close minus open divided by high minus low, or zero when high equals low, >= body threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its chart-timeframe SMA) AND (VWAP bias disabled OR selected price bias AND close < session VWAP OR selected slope bias AND session VWAP < its value at the slope lookback OR selected combined bias AND close < session VWAP AND session VWAP < its value at the slope lookback).
