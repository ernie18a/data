Calculation: Liquidity zones = On confirmation of a pivot high or low with equal lookback and lookforward lengths, create a high zone from the pivot high down to the lower of the pivot candle's body high and pivot high minus a minimum ATR-based width, or a low zone from the pivot low up to the higher of the pivot candle's body low and pivot low plus that width. Use ATR at the pivot bar. Maintain separate newest-first lists for high and low zones, including swept zones, and discard the oldest when a list exceeds its capacity. If overlap filtering is enabled, inspect existing unswept same-side zones from oldest to newest: for each overlapping zone, remove it if the new pivot is more extreme; otherwise discard the new zone and stop checking. A zone is eligible from its confirmation bar through its first sweep, inclusive, and can signal only once.
Calculation: Zone delta = Divide each zone into four equal price quadrants. Starting on the confirmation bar, add to each quadrant on every eligible bar: bar delta multiplied by the positive overlap of the bar's high-low range with that quadrant, divided by the bar's high-low range. Bar delta = volume * (close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low. Missing volume is zero. Update quadrant deltas before evaluating the current bar. Outer delta is the highest quadrant's accumulated delta for a high zone and the lowest quadrant's accumulated delta for a low zone. Total absolute delta is the sum of the absolute accumulated deltas of all quadrants. Outer ratio = absolute outer delta / (total absolute delta + a small positive constant). Bar ratio = absolute bar delta / (volume + the same constant).
Long: An eligible low zone has not previously signaled AND total absolute delta > 0 AND (low < zone bottom OR the bar has positive price-range overlap with the zone) AND ((low < zone bottom AND outer delta > 0 AND outer ratio > absorption threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND outer ratio < exhaustion threshold) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer ratio > divergence threshold) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND bar ratio > absorption threshold)); divergence threshold > absorption threshold > exhaustion threshold.
Short: An eligible high zone has not previously signaled AND total absolute delta > 0 AND (high > zone top OR the bar has positive price-range overlap with the zone) AND ((high > zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer ratio > absorption threshold) OR (high > zone top AND outer ratio < exhaustion threshold) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND outer delta > 0 AND outer ratio > divergence threshold) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND bar ratio > absorption threshold)); divergence threshold > absorption threshold > exhaustion threshold.
