Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall in the configured opening-range window, reset on each new session-timezone calendar day; the range is ready on the first bar outside that window after a bar inside it. Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2.
Calculation: First breakout = the first bar of the day with a ready opening range, an opening time inside the trading window, a flat position, and a close strictly above the opening range high or strictly below the opening range low; record its direction even if continuation entry is disabled or its risk is invalid.
Calculation: Breakout extreme = highest high for an upward first breakout or lowest low for a downward first breakout, from the first breakout bar through the current trading-window bar, inclusive.
Calculation: Continuation stop = opening range midpoint or, according to the selected stop mode, the opposite opening range boundary. Reversal stop = breakout extreme on the reversal entry bar. Fix the applicable stop at entry. Target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a configurable reward-to-risk multiple times the absolute difference between entry price and stop.
Long: Continuation enabled AND first breakout direction = up AND close - continuation stop > minimum price increment.
Short: Continuation enabled AND first breakout direction = down AND continuation stop - close > minimum price increment.
Long: Reversal enabled AND bar opening time is inside the trading window AND first breakout direction = down AND no reversal has been taken today AND opening range low < close AND close < opening range high AND (continuation disabled OR reversal after a closed continuation allowed OR short position open) AND close - breakout extreme > minimum price increment.
Short: Reversal enabled AND bar opening time is inside the trading window AND first breakout direction = up AND no reversal has been taken today AND opening range low < close AND close < opening range high AND (continuation disabled OR reversal after a closed continuation allowed OR long position open) AND breakout extreme - close > minimum price increment.
Exit: long, low <= fixed stop OR high >= fixed target.
Exit: short, high >= fixed stop OR low <= fixed target.
Exit: long, reversal enabled AND bar opening time is inside the trading window AND first breakout direction = up AND no reversal has been taken today AND opening range low < close AND close < opening range high AND breakout extreme - close > minimum price increment.
Exit: short, reversal enabled AND bar opening time is inside the trading window AND first breakout direction = down AND no reversal has been taken today AND opening range low < close AND close < opening range high AND close - breakout extreme > minimum price increment.
Exit: long, session-end closing enabled AND previous bar opening time is inside the trading window AND current bar opening time is outside the trading window.
Exit: short, session-end closing enabled AND previous bar opening time is inside the trading window AND current bar opening time is outside the trading window.
