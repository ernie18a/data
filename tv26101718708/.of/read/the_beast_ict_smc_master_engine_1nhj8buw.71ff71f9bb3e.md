Calculation: Watched levels = Enabled highs and lows of configured sessions, the previous completed higher-timeframe daily high and low, and the most recent configured number of confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows. A pivot has the same configured number of bars on each side and becomes available on its confirmation bar. Session levels become available on the first bar after the session ends; clear the Asia, London, and opening-range levels when their next session starts, while the previous New York levels remain until replaced. Replace daily levels on a new daily period. A level cannot be swept on the bar it is set.
Calculation: Sweep state = Track bullish and bearish sweeps independently for every watched level. Start a bullish sweep when low <= level - minimum depth AND previous close >= level; start a bearish sweep when high >= level + minimum depth AND previous close <= level. Track the lowest low or highest high from the start through the current bar. A sweep ends when its depth exceeds maximum depth, its close reclaims the level, or its age reaches the allowed reclaim bars, checked in that order. Once ended, that direction cannot restart for the same level until the level is replaced. A reclaim on the expiry bar is valid.
Calculation: Sweep grade score = Sum one point each for another enabled active level with a different name or price within the configured price band, a reversal-direction candle body of at least a configured multiple of ATR, and the reclaim bar being inside an enabled configured session window. Use bullish displacement for a bullish sweep and bearish displacement for a bearish sweep.
Long: Bullish sweep is pending after processing the closed bar.
Long: Bullish sweep depth <= maximum depth AND close > level AND sweep grade score >= configured minimum score.
Short: Bearish sweep is pending after processing the closed bar.
Short: Bearish sweep depth <= maximum depth AND close < level AND sweep grade score >= configured minimum score.
