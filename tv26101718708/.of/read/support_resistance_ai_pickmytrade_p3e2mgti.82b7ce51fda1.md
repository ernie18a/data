Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = confirmed swing lows and highs, respectively; confirm a swing after the configurable number of right-side chart bars close. Give each pivot a band centered on its price with half-width equal to ATR times the zone-width parameter. Merge a new pivot into the nearest live zone of the same role when its price is within ATR times the merge-distance parameter of the zone midpoint; recenter the band using the pivot-count-weighted average. Do not recenter a zone with an open test.
Calculation: Test arrival features = freeze when an armed live zone is touched: (1) chart-timeframe close change over the approach lookback divided by ATR times the square root of that lookback, signed positive toward the zone (for resistance, use the change as-is; for support, negate it), clipped to [-4, 4]; (2) current volume divided by its chart-timeframe simple moving average over the volume-baseline lookback, capped at 4 (use 1 if volume or baseline is unavailable or baseline is nonpositive); (3) prior resolved test count for the zone, capped at 6; (4) natural log of 1 plus chart bars since zone birth, capped at 5; (5) (close − chart-timeframe EMA) / ATR, signed positive toward the zone (as for feature 1), clipped to [-5, 5]; (6) zone width / ATR, capped at 4; (7) merged pivot count, capped at 6; (8) cumulative chart bars touching the zone, capped at 8.
Calculation: Test outcome = break if price closes beyond the zone in the break direction by more than ATR times the break-buffer parameter, provided either the zone has never flipped or at least the post-flip cooldown bars have elapsed since its role flip. Otherwise, hold if price closes beyond the opposite zone edge by more than ATR times the rejection-distance parameter. Otherwise resolve at the test-timeout bar count, labeling break if a resistance test closes above the zone top or a support test closes below the zone bottom, and hold otherwise. Train the model on the frozen arrival features and each resolved label. A decisive break flips a zone's role once and resets its birth bar and test count; a second decisive break retires it. A zone is armed initially and rearms only after price has fully left it on the approach side (below a resistance, above a support).
Calculation: Hold probability = online Gaussian Naive Bayes posterior for the frozen test-arrival features, using separately learned per-feature means and sample variances (variance floor 1e-6; use mean 0 and variance 1 with fewer than two observations), class priors from resolved hold and break counts, and the product of the eight Gaussian likelihoods. Use the running hold fraction until total resolved tests reach the warmup count; use 0.5 before any test resolves.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, a live support or resistance zone is armed, price touches its band (low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom), and total resolved tests >= warmup count AND ((support AND hold probability >= conviction threshold) OR (resistance AND hold probability <= 1 − conviction threshold)).
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, a live support or resistance zone is armed, price touches its band (low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom), and total resolved tests >= warmup count AND ((resistance AND hold probability >= conviction threshold) OR (support AND hold probability <= 1 − conviction threshold)).
