Calculation: Zones = On each completed chart bar with positive ATR, process confirmed pivot highs as resistance and pivot lows as support, in that order. A pivot is confirmed after its right-side lookback; use its pivot price and the current ATR. Merge it into the first live same-role zone whose midpoint is within a parameterized ATR distance; increment its pivot count, and, unless a test is open, replace its midpoint with the pivot-count-weighted average and its width with a parameterized multiple of current ATR. Otherwise create an armed zone centered on the pivot with that width, pivot count 1, and other counts 0. Process live zones newest first. A touch is low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom; increment touches before opening a test. An armed, untested zone opens a test on a touch and becomes disarmed. A resistance decisively breaks when close > top + an ATR buffer; a support decisively breaks when close < bottom - that buffer. After a role flip, decisive breaks are disabled until a parameterized cooldown has elapsed. An open resistance test holds when close < bottom - a parameterized ATR rejection distance; an open support test holds when close > top + that distance. Resolve an open test on a decisive break, hold, or parameterized timeout, in that priority order. At timeout classify it as a break if resistance close > top or support close < bottom; otherwise classify it as a hold. Train the model on the test's frozen features and increment its test count. A decisive break, including one without an open test, flips a zone's role on its first occurrence and retires it on its second; on a flip reset age and test count, retain pivot and touch counts, and arm only if high < bottom for new resistance or low > top for new support. Otherwise rearm a disarmed zone without an open test when high < bottom for resistance or low > top for support. After processing, if live zones exceed a parameterized cap, repeatedly remove the earliest non-testing live zone with the lowest value of twice its pivot count plus touches.
Calculation: Arrival features = Freeze the following when a test opens: signed (close - close at the approach lookback) / (ATR * square root of that lookback), clipped to [-4, 4]; volume / its SMA, capped at 4, or 1 if unavailable or its SMA is nonpositive; prior zone tests capped at 6; log(1 + bars since zone creation or flip), capped at 5; signed (close - EMA of close) / ATR, clipped to [-5, 5]; zone width / ATR capped at 4; pivot count capped at 6; and touch count capped at 8. The signed features use positive sign for resistance and negative sign for support; unavailable velocity or EMA stretch is 0.
Calculation: Hold probability = Train one shared online Gaussian naive Bayes model on each resolved test's frozen features and hold/break outcome, in zone processing order. For each class and feature use its sample mean and sample variance, flooring variance at 0.000001; use mean 0 with no observations and variance 1 with fewer than two. Use class prior max(class count, 0.5) / total count and normalize the products of Gaussian likelihoods to obtain the hold posterior. If the posterior is unavailable, use the overall hold rate. Before any resolved test the hold rate is 0.5. A model is ready when total resolved tests >= a parameterized warmup count. On test opening calculate probability before resolving that test; for an already tested zone with no open test, recalculate it from its latest frozen features and the current model.
Calculation: Eligible labeled zone = A live zone within a parameterized ATR distance of close and among a parameterized number of nearest live zones on its side of close, with distance ties sharing rank, AND either all-zone labeling is enabled, its test is open, or it is the nearest live zone on its side. Its label is directional only when it has a resolved or open test, close is outside its boundaries, and the model is ready.
Long: Model ready AND a test opens AND ((support AND hold probability >= conviction threshold) OR (resistance AND hold probability <= 1 - conviction threshold)) OR eligible labeled zone AND ((support AND hold probability >= conviction threshold) OR (resistance AND hold probability <= 1 - conviction threshold)).
