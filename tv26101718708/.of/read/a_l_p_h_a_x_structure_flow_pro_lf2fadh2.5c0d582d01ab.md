Calculation: Momentum Z = (close change − SMA of close change) / standard deviation of close change; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low); use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA of volume; use one when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a high or low confirmed as a pivot using the same number of bars on each side. Track the latest confirmed high and low separately; clear the corresponding tracked level after a momentum-confirmed break, even if no order block is found.
Calculation: Bullish momentum-confirmed break = close > tracked swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND Momentum Z > positive break threshold. Bearish momentum-confirmed break = close < tracked swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND Momentum Z < negative break threshold.
Calculation: Bullish body-confirmed break = close > tracked swing high AND previous close <= that same swing high AND Body ratio > body-break threshold. Bearish body-confirmed break = close < tracked swing low AND previous close >= that same swing low AND Body ratio > body-break threshold.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback AND close > that lowest low AND close > open. Bearish sweep = high > highest high of the preceding sweep lookback AND close < that highest high AND close < open.
Calculation: High-probability order block = on a momentum-confirmed break, select the most recent opposite-colored candle within the order-block lookback and use its high and low as the zone. Its quality is the sum of weighted absolute Momentum Z, volume percentile rank, Body ratio, and zone height / ATR, clamped to the score range; the zone qualifies when quality >= its threshold. Optionally reject a new zone when either boundary lies inside an existing unmitigated zone. Retain at most the configured number of zones, removing the oldest first, including mitigated zones in that count. A bullish zone is mitigated when low < its bottom; a bearish zone is mitigated when high > its top. Update mitigation before counting nearby zones. A zone is nearby when close is between its bottom minus an ATR multiple and its top plus the same ATR multiple.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = clamp the sum to the score range: add the larger structure weight for a same-direction momentum-confirmed break, otherwise the smaller structure weight for a same-direction body-confirmed break; add a sweep weight for a same-direction sweep; add a larger or smaller zone weight for at least two or exactly one nearby same-direction high-probability order block; add one of three decreasing momentum weights when direction-adjusted Momentum Z exceeds the corresponding high, medium, or low positive threshold; add one of three decreasing volume weights when Volume ratio exceeds the corresponding high, medium, or low threshold; add a larger candle weight when the candle closes in the direction and Body ratio exceeds its strong-body threshold, otherwise a smaller candle weight when it closes in the direction; subtract a momentum penalty when direction-adjusted Momentum Z is below a negative threshold and a zone penalty when at least two opposite-direction high-probability order blocks are nearby. Direction-adjusted Momentum Z is Momentum Z for bullish and its negation for bearish. Fair value gaps contribute zero because each new gap is marked filled on its creation bar.
Long: bullish momentum-confirmed break OR bullish body-confirmed break OR bullish sweep OR (low > high two bars ago AND low − high two bars ago >= ATR × minimum gap factor).
Short: bearish momentum-confirmed break OR bearish body-confirmed break OR bearish sweep OR (high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago − high >= ATR × minimum gap factor).
Long: bullish confluence score >= minimum score AND (bullish momentum-confirmed break OR bullish sweep) AND bars since the previous bullish confluence signal >= cooldown AND (volume filter disabled OR Volume ratio >= minimum volume ratio).
Short: bearish confluence score >= minimum score AND (bearish momentum-confirmed break OR bearish sweep) AND bars since the previous bearish confluence signal >= cooldown AND (volume filter disabled OR Volume ratio >= minimum volume ratio).
Exit: long, bearish momentum-confirmed break.
Exit: short, bullish momentum-confirmed break.
