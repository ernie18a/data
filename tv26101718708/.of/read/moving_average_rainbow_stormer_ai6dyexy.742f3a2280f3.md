Calculation: Moving averages = 12 close-price moving averages of one selectable type (EMA, SMA, RMA, WMA, HMA or VWMA), indexed from shortest to longest lookback; their mean is their arithmetic average.
Calculation: Uptrend = close > moving-average mean AND moving averages 5 > 6 > 7 > 8. Downtrend = close < moving-average mean AND moving averages 5 < 6 < 7 < 8.
Calculation: Uptrend touch = while in an uptrend, the longer-lookback average of the first adjacent pair, searched from shortest to longest, for which the shorter-lookback average >= low >= the longer-lookback average; if no pair matches, undefined. Outside an uptrend, retain its previous value. Downtrend touch is analogous, using the first pair for which the shorter-lookback average <= high <= the longer-lookback average while in a downtrend, and retaining its previous value otherwise.
Calculation: Long stop = previous-bar uptrend touch, replaced when entry-bar low < previous low by the entry-bar uptrend touch if defined, otherwise by the average of the previous-bar uptrend touch and entry-bar low. Short stop = previous-bar downtrend touch, replaced when entry-bar high > previous high by the entry-bar downtrend touch if defined, otherwise by the average of the previous-bar downtrend touch and entry-bar high.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + absolute value of (entry price - long stop) multiplied by a configurable positive profit factor. Short target = entry price - absolute value of (entry price - short stop) multiplied by the same factor.
Calculation: Long reversal indication = while long, moving averages 1 < 2 < 3 < 4 AND close < moving-average mean AND highest high since entry > entry price; once met, it remains active until the position is reset. Short reversal indication = while short, moving averages 1 > 2 > 3 > 4 AND close > moving-average mean AND lowest low since entry < entry price; once met, it remains active until the position is reset.
Calculation: Active long target = long target until the optional turnover-trend setting is enabled and the long reversal indication occurs; thereafter it is the highest high since entry as of the most recent bar satisfying the indication's original conditions. Active short target = short target until the optional turnover-trend setting is enabled and the short reversal indication occurs; thereafter it is the lowest low since entry as of the most recent bar satisfying the indication's original conditions.
Long: Uptrend AND high > previous high AND previous-bar uptrend touch is defined AND long stop > 0 AND long target > 0 AND (position is flat OR position is short AND optional turnover-signal setting is enabled AND (optional profitable-exit filter is disabled OR close < entry price)).
Short: Downtrend AND low < previous low AND previous-bar downtrend touch is defined AND short stop > 0 AND short target > 0 AND (position is flat OR position is long AND optional turnover-signal setting is enabled AND (optional profitable-exit filter is disabled OR close > entry price)).
Long: Optional turnover-trend setting is enabled AND short reversal indication is active.
Short: Optional turnover-trend setting is enabled AND long reversal indication is active.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= active long target OR (optional turnover-signal setting is enabled AND downtrend AND low < previous low AND previous-bar downtrend touch is defined AND (optional profitable-exit filter is disabled OR close > entry price) AND short stop > 0 AND short target > 0).
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= active short target OR (optional turnover-signal setting is enabled AND uptrend AND high > previous high AND previous-bar uptrend touch is defined AND (optional profitable-exit filter is disabled OR close < entry price) AND long stop > 0 AND long target > 0).
