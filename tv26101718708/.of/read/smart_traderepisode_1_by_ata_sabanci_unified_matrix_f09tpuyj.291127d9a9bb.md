Calculation: Buy and sell volume = either chart-bar volume split as volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and volume × (high − close) / (high − low), respectively, with an equal split when high = low; or sums of lower-timeframe completed intrabar volumes classified by close versus open, then versus the previous intrabar close when equal, retaining the previous intrabar direction when both comparisons are equal. In intrabar mode, require data for every available chart bar in the event lookback.
Calculation: Volume events = within the event lookback ending at the current chart bar, rank buy and sell volumes separately; highest-buy and highest-sell events have the largest volumes, and lowest-buy and lowest-sell events have the smallest positive volumes. Break ties in favor of the newer bar. Use the highest-ranked event of each type for subsequent calculations. The highest-buy event price is its high; the highest-sell event price is its low.
Calculation: Event consumption = sum of opposing volume after the event through the current bar. The highest-buy remaining power percentage is the highest-buy volume less its consumption, divided by its initial volume. Cumulative delta is the sum of buy volume less sell volume after the highest-buy event through the current bar. Both sums are zero at the event. Recalculate when the ranked event changes; the highest-buy and highest-sell trackers also reset when their remaining volume equals zero.
Calculation: Depletion event = the first bar after a highest-buy or highest-sell event where its consumption reaches a configurable fraction of its initial volume; a second depletion event occurs at a higher fraction equal to the first fraction plus full initial volume. Recalculate these event locations whenever the underlying highest-volume event changes.
Calculation: Nearest event = the most recent highest-buy, highest-sell, lowest-buy, lowest-sell, or either depletion event for either highest-volume event. Resolve equal ages in that order, with the lower depletion threshold before the higher threshold. Highest-buy, lowest-buy, and highest-sell depletion events are bullish; highest-sell, lowest-sell, and highest-buy depletion events are bearish.
Calculation: Post-event momentum = count increases and decreases of close versus previous close over the most recent bars after the nearest event, capped at a separate momentum lookback. Bullish momentum means increases exceed decreases; bearish momentum means decreases exceed increases; otherwise momentum is neutral.
Calculation: Trend = divide the event lookback into equal-sized consecutive segments, assigning the remainder to the oldest segment; regress the high and its bar index at the highest high of each segment and separately the low and its bar index at the lowest low of each segment. Use the newest occurrence of tied extremes. Uptrend means both regression slopes exceed an ATR-scaled positive threshold; downtrend means both are below its negative; otherwise trend is ranging. ATR uses a separate lookback.
Calculation: Long signal = highest-buy volume > 0 AND close > at least one highest-volume event price AND ((nearest event age <= recent-event threshold AND nearest event is bullish AND post-event momentum is bullish) OR (nearest event age > recent-event threshold OR post-event momentum is neutral) AND ((trend is uptrend AND cumulative delta > 0) OR (trend is downtrend AND cumulative delta > 0 AND remaining power percentage >= minimum long-power threshold AND post-event momentum is bullish) OR (trend is ranging AND cumulative delta > 0 AND post-event momentum is bullish))).
Calculation: Short signal = highest-buy volume > 0 AND close <= at least one highest-volume event price AND ((nearest event age <= recent-event threshold AND nearest event is bearish AND post-event momentum is bearish) OR (nearest event age > recent-event threshold OR post-event momentum is neutral) AND ((trend is uptrend AND remaining power percentage < opponent-dominance threshold AND cumulative delta < 0 AND post-event momentum is bearish) OR (trend is downtrend AND cumulative delta < 0) OR (trend is ranging AND cumulative delta < 0 AND post-event momentum is bearish))).
Long: Long signal = true.
Short: Short signal = true.
Long: A bar ranks among the configured highest-buy or lowest-buy volume events in the current event lookback.
Short: A bar ranks among the configured highest-sell or lowest-sell volume events in the current event lookback.
Long: Highest-sell consumption first reaches either depletion threshold after its event.
Short: Highest-buy consumption first reaches either depletion threshold after its event.
Exit: long, Short signal = true OR low <= highest-sell event price at the most recent long entry − entry-bar close × stop buffer OR high >= highest-buy event price at the most recent long entry − entry-bar close × target buffer.
Exit: short, Long signal = true OR high >= highest-buy event price at the most recent short entry + entry-bar close × stop buffer OR low <= highest-sell event price at the most recent short entry + entry-bar close × target buffer.
