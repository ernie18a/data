Calculation: Bullish gap = when low > high two bars earlier AND low - that earlier high >= a configurable ATR multiple, store the earlier high as the lower boundary, the current low as the upper boundary, and the current bar as the creation bar. Replace any previous bullish gap when a new one forms. Before creating a new gap on each bar, consume the stored gap if its pullback condition is met, even if an entry filter fails; otherwise it expires after a configurable number of bars.
Calculation: Bearish gap = when high < low two bars earlier AND that earlier low - high >= a configurable ATR multiple, store the current high as the lower boundary, the earlier low as the upper boundary, and the current bar as the creation bar. Replace any previous bearish gap when a new one forms. Before creating a new gap on each bar, consume the stored gap if its pullback condition is met, even if an entry filter fails; otherwise it expires after a configurable number of bars.
Calculation: Bullish gap pullback = a stored bullish gap exists AND the current bar is after its creation bar AND its age is at most the configured lifetime AND low <= its upper boundary AND close > open AND close > its lower boundary.
Calculation: Bearish gap pullback = a stored bearish gap exists AND the current bar is after its creation bar AND its age is at most the configured lifetime AND high >= its lower boundary AND close < open AND close < its upper boundary.
Calculation: Bullish trend = shorter-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND close > shorter-period EMA AND RSI > its midpoint.
Calculation: Bearish trend = shorter-period EMA of close < longer-period EMA of close AND close < shorter-period EMA AND RSI < its midpoint.
Calculation: Trading time allowed = no session is enabled OR the bar opening time and weekday in the configured timezone fall inside an enabled session; session start is inclusive and end is exclusive, and overnight sessions wrap midnight. AND the bar closing time is outside the inclusive blackout interval around every enabled configured news event.
Calculation: Bullish sensitivity filter = aggressive mode OR (close > trend-filter EMA of close AND (balanced mode OR (the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA of close AND chart-timeframe ADX >= a configurable threshold))).
Calculation: Bearish sensitivity filter = aggressive mode OR (close < trend-filter EMA of close AND (balanced mode OR (the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA of close AND chart-timeframe ADX >= a configurable threshold))).
Calculation: Bullish signal = trading time allowed AND bullish sensitivity filter AND ((gap-pullback method selected AND bullish gap pullback) OR (EMA-trend method selected AND bullish trend is true AND bullish trend on the preceding bar is false)).
Calculation: Bearish signal = trading time allowed AND bearish sensitivity filter AND bullish signal is false AND ((gap-pullback method selected AND bearish gap pullback) OR (EMA-trend method selected AND bearish trend is true AND bearish trend on the preceding bar is false)).
Long: bullish signal AND position side is not long; enter at the confirmed bar's close.
Short: bearish signal AND position side is not short; enter at the confirmed bar's close.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, high >= entry price + configurable take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - configurable stop-loss distance; evaluate before signals on the confirmed bar.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, low <= entry price - configurable take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + configurable stop-loss distance; evaluate before signals on the confirmed bar.
Exit: long, bearish signal; exit at the confirmed bar's close and enter short.
Exit: short, bullish signal; exit at the confirmed bar's close and enter long.
