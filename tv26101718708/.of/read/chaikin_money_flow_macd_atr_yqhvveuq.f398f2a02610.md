Calculation: MACD = shorter-period moving average of the selected price source minus longer-period moving average of the same source; both averages use the same selectable SMA or EMA method. Its signal line is a selectable SMA or EMA of MACD.
Calculation: EMA filter = EMA of close on a selectable timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the last completed bar, and for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar in the chart bar.
Long: datetime is within the configured inclusive date range AND position is flat AND long entries are enabled AND at least one of the four filters is enabled AND (MACD crossover filter is disabled OR MACD crosses above its signal line) AND (MACD zero-line filter is disabled OR MACD > 0) AND (Chaikin Money Flow filter is disabled OR Chaikin Money Flow > 0) AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close > EMA filter).
Short: datetime is within the configured inclusive date range AND position is flat AND short entries are enabled AND at least one of the four filters is enabled AND (MACD crossover filter is disabled OR MACD crosses below its signal line) AND (MACD zero-line filter is disabled OR MACD < 0) AND (Chaikin Money Flow filter is disabled OR Chaikin Money Flow < 0) AND (EMA filter is disabled OR close < EMA filter).
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured inclusive date range AND (low <= entry price minus ATR at entry multiplied by the long stop factor OR high >= entry price plus ATR at entry multiplied by the long profit factor).
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured inclusive date range AND (high >= entry price plus ATR at entry multiplied by the short stop factor OR low <= entry price minus ATR at entry multiplied by the short profit factor).
