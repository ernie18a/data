Calculation: Higher-timeframe data = For historical bars, use the last completed bar of each higher timeframe; on a real-time bar, use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. The trend-table timeframes and the two environment timeframes are higher than the chart timeframe; the environment timeframe for the primary SMA is higher than the zone timeframe.
Calculation: Environment = Long when chart close > primary-timeframe SMA OR that SMA > its value a configured number of primary-timeframe bars ago. Short when primary-timeframe SMA > zone-timeframe SMA AND zone-timeframe SMA > chart close AND both SMAs < their respective values a configured number of their own timeframe bars ago. For the displayed direction, long takes precedence when both conditions hold.
Calculation: Zone bounds = Let H and L be the highest high and lowest low over a zone-timeframe lookback. On every change in displayed environment direction, reset running high and running low to H and L. Otherwise, when the long environment holds, update running high to the maximum of its prior value and chart high; when the short environment holds, update running low to the minimum of its prior value and chart low. Zone high is running high when the long environment holds, otherwise H; zone low is L when the long environment holds, otherwise running low when the short environment holds, otherwise L.
Calculation: Zone overlap = The long Fibonacci interval runs from zone high minus 0.618 times the zone range to zone high minus 0.5 times the zone range; the short interval runs from zone low plus 0.5 times the zone range to zone low plus 0.618 times the zone range. For the long fair-value gap, search zone-timeframe bars from newest to oldest over a configured lookback for the first bar whose low exceeds the high two zone bars earlier AND whose low is at or below the midpoint of the current zone-timeframe H and L; its interval is that earlier high to that low. For the short gap, find the first bar whose high is below the low two zone bars earlier AND whose high is at or above that midpoint; its interval is that high to the earlier low. A long or short zone overlaps when the chart bar's low-to-high interval intersects the corresponding Fibonacci or gap interval AND the corresponding environment holds.
Calculation: Pin bar = Body is abs(close - open), upper wick is high - max(open, close), and lower wick is min(open, close) - low. A bullish pin bar has close > open AND lower wick > twice body AND lower wick > upper wick AND lower wick >= chart ATR times a configured multiplier. A bearish pin bar reverses these comparisons using the upper wick.
Calculation: Engulfing bar = A bullish engulfing bar has close > open AND prior close < prior open AND close >= prior open AND open <= prior close AND body >= the pin-bar ATR times a configured multiplier. A bearish engulfing bar reverses the price comparisons.
Calculation: Statistical wick = Average the long-period SMAs of upper and lower wick lengths; take the long-period standard deviation of that average. A statistical upper or lower wick occurs when that deviation is nonzero AND (corresponding wick length - average) / deviation times a configured sensitivity divided by 20 > 1.
Calculation: Range break = Let range high and low be the highest high and lowest low over a chart-bar lookback ending on the previous bar. An upward break requires range high - range low <= chart ATR times a configured multiplier AND close > range high AND close - range high >= pin-bar ATR times a configured margin. A downward break reverses the price comparisons and uses range low - close.
Calculation: Swing break = Track the latest confirmed chart pivot high and low using configured left and right confirmation bars, updating each before evaluating the current bar. An upward swing break occurs when close > tracked pivot high AND there is no upward range break; then replace the tracked high with current high. A downward swing break occurs when close < tracked pivot low AND there is no downward range break; then replace the tracked low with current low.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI reversal = Compute RSI of close, normalize it over its stochastic lookback to 0–100, using 50 when its range is zero, and smooth with an SMA to obtain K. A bullish reversal requires K's lowest value over a configured lookback <= an oversold threshold AND K crossing above that threshold AND K > prior K. A bearish reversal requires K's highest value over that lookback >= an overbought threshold AND K crossing below it AND K < prior K.
Calculation: Directional score = Add 1 for the corresponding pin bar, plus another 1 if statistical-wick enhancement is enabled and its corresponding wick qualifies; add 1 for the corresponding engulfing bar, 2 for the corresponding range break, 2 for the corresponding swing break, 1 for the short-period close EMA crossing the longer-period close EMA in that direction, and 1 for the corresponding stochastic RSI reversal if enabled. Add 1 if the resulting score > 0 AND the corresponding zone overlaps. Crossings require the prior value to be on or beyond the opposite side of the crossed value.
Calculation: Take-profit extremes = Select EMA or SMA consistently. Subtract the longer-period close MA from the shorter-period close MA, then subtract the short-period MA of that difference to obtain a histogram. Calculate RSI separately on each close MA, with each RSI period proportional to its MA period and subject to a minimum period. A long extreme requires both RSI values > an overbought threshold; a short extreme requires both < an oversold threshold. Histogram downturn is the histogram crossing below its previous-bar value; histogram upturn is crossing above its previous-bar value.
Calculation: Trade state = A base long or short signal sets the corresponding trade side, resets that side's add-on state, and replaces the opposite side. Process a simultaneous long signal before a short signal. A full-exit condition clears the trade state after evaluating the add-on and exit conditions; a partial-exit condition does not clear it.
Calculation: Add-on reference = For a long trade, record the high and bar index of the first bar since the latest base signal where close > the highest high over a configured lookback ending on the previous bar. For a short trade, record the low and index of the first bar where close < the lowest low over that lookback. Reset the reference on each base signal or full exit; allow only one add-on per base signal.
Long: Displayed environment direction = long.
Short: Displayed environment direction = short.
Long: Higher-timeframe close > its higher-timeframe close SMA, for each displayed trend timeframe.
Short: Higher-timeframe close <= its higher-timeframe close SMA, for each displayed trend timeframe.
Long: Long environment holds AND long directional score >= configured threshold AND bars since the previous base long signal > configured cooldown OR long environment holds AND long directional score >= configured threshold AND no previous base long signal exists.
Short: Short environment holds AND short directional score >= configured threshold AND bars since the previous base short signal > configured cooldown OR short environment holds AND short directional score >= configured threshold AND no previous base short signal exists.
Long: Trade state = long AND add-on reference exists AND current bar index > reference bar index AND close > reference high AND no add-on has occurred since the latest base signal.
Short: Trade state = short AND add-on reference exists AND current bar index > reference bar index AND close < reference low AND no add-on has occurred since the latest base signal.
Exit: long, partial, high >= zone high OR short-period close EMA crosses below longer-period close EMA OR take-profit extremes are enabled AND long extreme holds AND (statistical upper wick occurs OR histogram downturn occurs).
Exit: short, partial, low <= zone low OR short-period close EMA crosses above longer-period close EMA OR take-profit extremes are enabled AND short extreme holds AND (statistical lower wick occurs OR histogram upturn occurs).
Exit: long, full, close < lowest low over the add-on lookback ending on the previous bar OR bearish pin bar OR bearish engulfing bar OR RSI of close > an overbought threshold AND close < open OR take-profit extremes are enabled AND long extreme holds AND statistical upper wick occurs AND histogram downturn occurs.
Exit: short, full, close > highest high over the add-on lookback ending on the previous bar OR bullish pin bar OR bullish engulfing bar OR RSI of close < an oversold threshold AND close > open OR take-profit extremes are enabled AND short extreme holds AND statistical lower wick occurs AND histogram upturn occurs.
