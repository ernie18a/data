Calculation: Long environment = chart close > long-term SMA from a higher timeframe OR that SMA is rising versus its prior higher-timeframe value.
Calculation: Short environment = long-term SMA from a higher timeframe > short-term SMA from a higher timeframe > chart close AND both SMAs are falling versus their prior higher-timeframe values. The long-term timeframe is higher than the short-term timeframe; both use the last completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Long zone = long environment AND chart bar overlaps either the 50%–61.8% retracement zone between the running high since the long environment began and the rolling low from a higher timeframe, or a bullish fair value gap from that higher timeframe in the lower half of its rolling range. Short zone = the mirrored condition using the running low, rolling high, upper retracement zone, and bearish fair value gap. Higher-timeframe rolling values and gaps use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = lower wick > 2 × body, lower wick > upper wick, close > open, and lower wick ≥ ATR × a threshold. Bearish pin bar = the mirrored condition. Strong wick = the current side's wick is more than a sensitivity-scaled standard deviation above the long-term standard deviation baseline of the average of the long-term mean upper and lower wicks.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle is bullish, prior candle bearish, current close ≥ prior open, current open ≤ prior close, and current body ≥ ATR × a threshold. Bearish engulfing = the mirrored condition.
Calculation: Double bottom = two confirmed pivot lows at least a minimum bar distance apart and within a tolerance of each other as a percentage of close, with close above their neckline; use the intervening pivot high as neckline when available, otherwise the highest high since the second pivot. Trigger once per pivot pair. Double top is the mirrored condition using pivot highs, an intervening pivot low or the lowest low since the second pivot, and close below the neckline.
Calculation: Range breakout = prior-bar rolling high-low width ≤ ATR × a range threshold, and close breaks above the prior rolling high (long) or below the prior rolling low (short) by at least ATR × a margin.
Calculation: BOS = close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high (long) or below the latest confirmed swing low (short), except when the corresponding range breakout is true; after a BOS, reset that swing level to the current high (long) or low (short).
Calculation: Stochastic RSI reversal = smoothed stochastic RSI has reached the oversold level within a recent lookback and crosses above that level while rising (long), or reached the overbought level and crosses below it while falling (short).
Calculation: Long structure score = bullish pin bar (2 if strong wick, otherwise 1) + bullish engulfing (1) + double bottom (2) + bullish range breakout (2) + bullish BOS (2) + fast EMA crossing above slower EMA (1) + stochastic RSI reversal (1). Short structure score uses the mirrored patterns and EMA cross below.
Calculation: Side total score = structure score + 1 when the corresponding zone is true and structure score > 0; otherwise structure score. Long and short use separate last-signal bars and cooldowns.
Calculation: Long add-on trigger = while in a long position, close exceeds the prior rolling high over the BOS lookback; record that bar's high, then trigger once on a later bar whose close exceeds that high. Short add-on trigger is the mirrored condition using rolling low and later close below the recorded low.
Calculation: Partial long exit trigger = while in a long position, high ≥ the active zone high OR fast EMA crosses below slower EMA OR long-side take-profit condition. Partial short exit trigger = while in a short position, low ≤ active zone low OR fast EMA crosses above slower EMA OR short-side take-profit condition.
Calculation: Long-side take-profit condition = both RSI values of the short-term and long-term moving averages exceed an overbought threshold AND (upper wick is statistically strong OR the moving-average MACD histogram turns down). Short-side take-profit condition = both RSI values are below an oversold threshold AND (lower wick is statistically strong OR the histogram turns up). Strong versions require both the statistically strong wick and histogram turn.
Calculation: Full long exit trigger = while in a long position, bearish BOS using the prior rolling low OR bearish pin bar OR bearish engulfing OR RSI > an overbought threshold with a bearish candle OR strong long-side take-profit condition. Full short exit trigger = while in a short position, bullish BOS using the prior rolling high OR bullish pin bar OR bullish engulfing OR RSI < an oversold threshold with a bullish candle OR strong short-side take-profit condition.
Long: Long environment AND long total score ≥ entry threshold AND (no prior long signal OR bars since the last long signal > cooldown) OR long add-on trigger.
Short: Short environment AND short total score ≥ entry threshold AND (no prior short signal OR bars since the last short signal > cooldown) OR short add-on trigger.
Exit: Long, partial long exit trigger OR full long exit trigger.
Exit: Short, partial short exit trigger OR full short exit trigger.
