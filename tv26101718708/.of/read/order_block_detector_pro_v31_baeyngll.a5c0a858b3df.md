Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low whose value is respectively the highest or lowest among an equal number of bars before and after it; it becomes available when the later bars have completed.
Calculation: Structure break level = the most recently confirmed pivot high or low for the selected structure lookback; track the last broken high and low separately for each of the shorter and longer structure lookbacks, initially undefined. Update a tracked broken level only when its corresponding structure break occurs.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed.
Long: For either structure lookback, close > the latest confirmed pivot high AND that high differs from the last broken high for that lookback.
Long: The last completed higher-timeframe close > its higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed.
Short: For either structure lookback, close < the latest confirmed pivot low AND that low differs from the last broken low for that lookback.
Short: The last completed higher-timeframe close < its higher-timeframe EMA.
