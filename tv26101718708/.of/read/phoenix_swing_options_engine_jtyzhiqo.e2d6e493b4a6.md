Calculation: Compression = 100 - 100 × (Bollinger Band width - its rolling minimum) / max(its rolling maximum - its rolling minimum, a small positive floor), where width is (upper band - lower band) / middle band.
Calculation: Momentum score = trend score + RSI score + rate-of-change score. Trend score is +2 when the short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA, -2 when short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA, otherwise +1 when short EMA > medium EMA, -1 when short EMA < medium EMA, and 0 otherwise. RSI score is +1 above an upper threshold, -1 below a lower threshold, and 0 otherwise. Rate-of-change score is +2 when rate of change > 0 AND its change over a lookback > 0, +1 when rate of change > 0 otherwise, -2 when rate of change < 0 AND its change over that lookback < 0, -1 when rate of change < 0 otherwise, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Energy buildup = ATR SMA < its value a lookback ago AND volume SMA > its value a lookback ago, using the same comparison lookback for both.
Calculation: Upper trigger = Donchian channel midpoint + ATR multiplier × ATR; lower trigger = Donchian channel midpoint - ATR multiplier × ATR.
Calculation: Armed states = initially both false, with one shared arm bar. On each bar, in order: if compression >= its threshold AND momentum score >= its positive threshold AND energy buildup is true AND long armed is false, set long armed true and the shared arm bar to the current bar; if compression >= its threshold AND momentum score <= the negative of that threshold AND energy buildup is true AND short armed is false, set short armed true and the shared arm bar to the current bar. Then clear long armed if momentum score < 0 OR bars since the shared arm bar > the arm window; clear short armed if momentum score > 0 OR bars since the shared arm bar > the arm window. Evaluate both entry triggers using these states, then clear the corresponding armed state for each trigger that fires. Armed-state history records the states after these clears.
Calculation: Position state = initially flat. On each bar, process a long entry first and then a short entry: each sets the position side, entry price to close, entry ATR to current ATR, and entry bar to the current bar, replacing any existing position. Then evaluate exits, including on the entry bar, and set the position flat if an exit fires.
Long: long armed before entry-trigger clearing is true AND close crosses above the upper trigger AND volume > volume surge multiplier × volume SMA.
Short: short armed before entry-trigger clearing is true AND close crosses below the lower trigger AND volume > volume surge multiplier × volume SMA.
Long: long armed after entry-trigger clearing is true AND long armed on the previous bar is false.
Short: short armed after entry-trigger clearing is true AND short armed on the previous bar is false.
Long: momentum score >= its positive threshold.
Short: momentum score <= the negative of its positive threshold.
Long: long armed after entry-trigger clearing is true OR (short armed after entry-trigger clearing is false AND momentum score > 0).
Short: long armed after entry-trigger clearing is false AND (short armed after entry-trigger clearing is true OR momentum score < 0).
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target multiplier × entry ATR OR low <= entry price - stop multiplier × entry ATR OR momentum score <= the negative of its positive threshold OR bars since entry >= time limit.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - target multiplier × entry ATR OR high >= entry price + stop multiplier × entry ATR OR momentum score >= its positive threshold OR bars since entry >= time limit.
