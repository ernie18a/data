Calculation: VCI = 100 minus the current Bollinger Band width’s min-max normalized value over a rolling lookback; Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band) / middle band
Calculation: DMS = trend score + RSI score + ROC score. Trend score is +2 when fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, +1 when fast EMA > medium EMA but the full stack is not bullish, −2 when fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA, −1 when fast EMA < medium EMA but the full stack is not bearish, otherwise 0. RSI score is +1 above its upper threshold, −1 below its lower threshold, otherwise 0. ROC score is +2 when ROC and its change from several bars earlier are positive, +1 when ROC is positive but that change is not, −2 when both are negative, −1 when ROC is negative but that change is not, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Energy buildup = the short-term SMA of ATR is below its value several bars earlier AND the short-term SMA of volume is above its value several bars earlier
Calculation: Bullish setup armed = becomes true when VCI meets the compression threshold, DMS meets the positive trigger threshold, and energy buildup is true; remains true until DMS turns negative, the arm window expires, or a long entry triggers
Calculation: Bearish setup armed = becomes true when VCI meets the compression threshold, DMS meets the negative trigger threshold, and energy buildup is true; remains true until DMS turns positive, the arm window expires, or a short entry triggers
Long: bullish setup armed = true AND close crosses above the Donchian midline + ATR buffer AND volume > volume moving average × surge multiplier
Short: bearish setup armed = true AND close crosses below the Donchian midline − ATR buffer AND volume > volume moving average × surge multiplier
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + target multiple × ATR at entry OR low ≤ entry price − stop multiple × ATR at entry OR DMS ≤ negative trigger threshold OR bars since entry ≥ time limit
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − target multiple × ATR at entry OR high ≥ entry price + stop multiple × ATR at entry OR DMS ≥ positive trigger threshold OR bars since entry ≥ time limit
