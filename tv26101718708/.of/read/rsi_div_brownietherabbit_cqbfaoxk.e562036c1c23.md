Calculation: RSI divergence ray = a line through two confirmed RSI pivots of the same kind, with the newer pivot within the configured lookback range; a bearish ray comes from price making a higher high while RSI makes a lower high, and a bullish ray from price making a lower low while RSI makes a higher low. Hidden divergences use price lower high with RSI higher high for bearish rays, and price higher low with RSI lower low for bullish rays.
Calculation: Decision level = the latest confirmed price swing high if it is more recent than the latest confirmed swing low, otherwise the latest confirmed swing low; use the opposite swing as the target.
Long: RSI crosses above the latest bearish divergence ray OR above the upper boundary of two valid bearish divergence rays after RSI remained between them; OR close is above the decision level by the configured buffer.
Short: RSI crosses below the latest bullish divergence ray OR below the lower boundary of two valid bullish divergence rays after RSI remained between them; OR close is below the decision level by the configured buffer.
Exit: Long, RSI crosses back below the bearish divergence ray that produced the active long signal within the configured invalidation window.
Exit: Short, RSI crosses back above the bullish divergence ray that produced the active short signal within the configured invalidation window.
