Calculation: RSI rays = On each completed chart bar, confirm RSI pivots using configurable left and right lookbacks. For consecutive same-type pivots whose confirmation bars are separated by the configured minimum to maximum range, create a ray through their RSI values when low pivots have higher RSI and lower price low, or lower RSI and higher price low, or when high pivots have lower RSI and higher price high, or higher RSI and lower price high. Extend each ray linearly by bar index. Keep the rays in creation order separately for low and high pivots; select either the latest or second-latest ray in each group.
Calculation: Ray cross = An upward cross occurs when previous RSI is strictly below the selected ray evaluated at the previous bar and current RSI is strictly above that same ray evaluated at the current bar; a downward cross reverses both inequalities.
Calculation: Strict upward cross = An upward cross of the selected high-pivot ray AND the maximum ray-minus-RSI gap over the preceding approach lookback is at least the minimum gap AND the most recent occurrence of that maximum is at least the minimum number of bars ago AND RSI is more than the approach buffer below that ray on every preceding approach bar AND current RSI minus previous RSI is at least the minimum jump. Strict downward cross reverses the ray-minus-RSI gap, the side of the ray, and the RSI change, using the selected low-pivot ray.
Calculation: Upward channel breakout = The latest two high-pivot rays have an absolute slope difference at most the slope tolerance AND their current values differ by at least the minimum channel gap AND RSI on every preceding containment bar is between their values at that bar, allowing the containment buffer outside each boundary AND RSI crosses upward through whichever ray is currently higher. Downward channel breakout uses the latest two low-pivot rays and crosses downward through whichever ray is currently lower.
Calculation: Volume pass = The volume filter is disabled OR current volume is at least its SMA multiplied by the volume threshold.
Calculation: Bull trend pass = The trend filter is disabled OR the short, medium, and long close EMAs are not strictly descending OR tiered mode is enabled AND the EMAs are strictly descending AND a strict upward cross occurs AND volume pass holds. Bear trend pass reverses the EMA ordering and uses a strict downward cross.
Calculation: Bull zone pass = The zone filter is disabled OR RSI is at most the bullish zone threshold OR RSI is strictly between the bullish and bearish zone thresholds AND neutral-zone allowance is enabled AND a strict upward cross occurs AND volume pass holds. Bear zone pass uses RSI at least the bearish zone threshold and a strict downward cross.
Calculation: Level proximity = Close is positive AND its absolute percentage distance from an enabled level is at most the proximity threshold. Levels are the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar and the running high and low of the configured premarket session on the current day, through the current chart bar.
Calculation: Recent volume event = At least one bar from the current bar through the configured lookback has volume divided by its volume SMA at least either the major or extreme threshold, with a positive SMA.
Calculation: Bull arrow = (strict-only mode is enabled AND (strict upward cross OR upward channel breakout) OR strict-only mode is disabled AND raw-cross display is enabled AND (upward cross of the selected high-pivot ray OR upward channel breakout)) AND volume pass AND bull trend pass AND bull zone pass AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR RSI of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below the bullish threshold) AND (fan-out filter is disabled OR short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA AND 100 × |short EMA − long EMA| / close is at least the spacing threshold, with positive close) AND (level proximity is not required OR level proximity holds).
Calculation: Bear arrow = (strict-only mode is enabled AND (strict downward cross OR downward channel breakout) OR strict-only mode is disabled AND raw-cross display is enabled AND (downward cross of the selected low-pivot ray OR downward channel breakout)) AND volume pass AND bear trend pass AND bear zone pass AND (higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled OR RSI of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is above the bearish threshold) AND (fan-out filter is disabled OR short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA AND 100 × |short EMA − long EMA| / close is at least the spacing threshold, with positive close) AND (level proximity is not required OR level proximity holds).
Calculation: Decision level = The price of the more recently occurring of the latest confirmed symmetric-lookback high and low price pivots, provided both exist and their pivot bars differ.
Long: Bull arrow AND (confluence-only mode is disabled OR confluence is enabled AND recent volume event AND (counter-trend requirement is disabled OR close < long EMA)).
Short: Bear arrow AND (confluence-only mode is disabled OR confluence is enabled AND recent volume event AND (counter-trend requirement is disabled OR close > long EMA)).
Long: Close > decision level × (1 + bias buffer).
Short: Close < decision level × (1 − bias buffer).
Exit: Long, close < decision level.
Exit: Short, close > decision level.
Exit: Long, invalidation is enabled AND the most recent bull or bear arrow before the current bar was bullish AND no subsequent invalidation has occurred AND bars since that arrow are within the invalidation window AND RSI crosses downward through the high-pivot ray selected on the arrow bar.
Exit: Short, invalidation is enabled AND the most recent bull or bear arrow before the current bar was bearish AND no subsequent invalidation has occurred AND bars since that arrow are within the invalidation window AND RSI crosses upward through the low-pivot ray selected on the arrow bar.
