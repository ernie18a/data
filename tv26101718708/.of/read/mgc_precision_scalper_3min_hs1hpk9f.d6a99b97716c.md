Calculation: Long quality = premium when the short SMA of close > the shorter EMA of close > the longer EMA of close AND close > the long SMA of close AND RSI > a bullish momentum threshold above its midpoint; otherwise good when (the short SMA > the shorter EMA OR the short SMA > the longer EMA) AND close > the long SMA; otherwise basic. The short SMA has a shorter lookback than both EMAs, and the long SMA has a longer lookback than both EMAs.
Calculation: Short quality = premium when the short SMA of close < the shorter EMA of close < the longer EMA of close AND close < the long SMA of close AND RSI < a bearish momentum threshold below its midpoint; otherwise good when (the short SMA < the shorter EMA OR the short SMA < the longer EMA) AND close < the long SMA; otherwise basic. The short SMA has a shorter lookback than both EMAs, and the long SMA has a longer lookback than both EMAs.
Calculation: Long armed = initially true; set true when RSI crosses below its midpoint, and set false after a long signal. Short armed = initially true; set true when RSI crosses above its midpoint, and set false after a short signal. Apply rearming before evaluating signals on each bar.
Long: RSI crosses above its long trigger AND long armed = true AND (no previous long signal exists OR bars since the previous long signal >= the per-side cooldown) AND (chop filter disabled OR abs(short SMA - longer EMA) >= chop multiplier × ATR) AND (abs(close - short SMA) < extension multiplier × ATR OR close <= short SMA) AND RSI < the upper exhaustion threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar opens within the configured session and timezone) AND long quality >= the configured minimum quality.
Short: RSI crosses below its short trigger AND short armed = true AND (no previous short signal exists OR bars since the previous short signal >= the per-side cooldown) AND (chop filter disabled OR abs(short SMA - longer EMA) >= chop multiplier × ATR) AND (abs(close - short SMA) < extension multiplier × ATR OR close >= short SMA) AND RSI > the lower exhaustion threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA of volume × volume multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar opens within the configured session and timezone) AND short quality >= the configured minimum quality.
