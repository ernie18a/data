Calculation: Liquidity levels = For each of short, medium, and long lookbacks, calculate the rolling highest high and lowest low including the current bar. Initially create each available extreme; thereafter create an upper level when its rolling high is greater than its value a specified number of bars ago and equals its previous-bar value, and create a lower level when its rolling low is less than its value the same number of bars ago and equals its previous-bar value. Round each new level to a configurable price increment. Maintain separate upper and lower level sets; merge a new level with the nearest existing level within a configurable tolerance by refreshing its birth bar without changing its price or strength, otherwise add it with zero strength. On confirmed bars, evaluate sweeps before removing upper levels at or below the selected high-or-close mitigation price and lower levels at or above the selected low-or-close mitigation price; periodically remove levels whose age exceeds the maximum age, then add newly detected levels. Sweep signals require high/low mitigation.
Calculation: Visible liquidity bands = After level updates on each confirmed bar, select the specified number of nearest unexpired upper levels strictly above close and lower levels strictly below close. On a down bar, add capped volume divided by its rolling simple average to the selected upper levels; on an up bar, add it to the selected lower levels. Use decreasing weights for levels ordered nearest to farthest and cap each level's cumulative strength; use current volume as the average when the average is unavailable or nonpositive, and use zero normalized volume when that denominator is nonpositive. At each periodic sampling bar, record each selected level's strength divided by the greatest strength among all selected upper and lower levels, or zero if that maximum is zero, and its band height as the greater of the rolling high-low range and a configurable minimum price range divided by a resolution parameter. A level is visible for sweep evaluation if it has a recorded sample no older than the sampling interval. Use its last sampled height and normalized strength. Among swept visible levels on the same side, merge overlapping price intervals centered on the levels into distinct bands; a band's strength is the maximum sampled normalized strength of its constituent levels, and sweep strength is the mean of those band strengths.
Calculation: Qualified sweep = On a confirmed bar, identify visible upper levels at or below high and visible lower levels at or above low before mitigation. A sweep qualifies when its number of distinct bands reaches a minimum and the optional strength filter is disabled or its mean band strength meets the threshold. Its upper reclaim level is the lowest swept upper level; its lower reclaim level is the highest swept lower level. Store each qualified sweep until the first qualifying reclaim close or until its age exceeds the reclaim window; the sweep bar itself is eligible.
Calculation: Trade levels = For a signal, use the next bar's open as entry. The long stop is the lowest low over a stop lookback ending on the signal bar; the short stop is the highest high over the same lookback. Create trade levels only when the stop is available and the entry-to-stop distance exceeds a configurable price increment. The final target is entry plus the risk distance times a reward-to-risk factor for a long, or entry minus that amount for a short. Track each trade from its entry bar through its expiry bar; stop or final target ends tracking.
Long: A qualified lower sweep remains pending within the reclaim window AND close > its reclaim level.
Short: A qualified upper sweep remains pending within the reclaim window AND close < its reclaim level.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long final target within the trade tracking window.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short final target within the trade tracking window.
