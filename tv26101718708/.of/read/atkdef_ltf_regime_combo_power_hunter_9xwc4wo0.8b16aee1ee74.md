Calculation: EMA direction = compare the shorter-lookback EMA with the longer-lookback EMA
Calculation: Direction score = 90 if close > shorter EMA AND shorter EMA > longer EMA AND shorter EMA is rising AND close is in the top 10% of the bar range; otherwise 75 if close > shorter EMA AND shorter EMA > longer EMA; otherwise 55 if close > shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA; otherwise 40 if close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA > longer EMA; otherwise 15 if close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA AND shorter EMA is falling AND close is in the bottom 10% of the bar range; otherwise 25 if close < shorter EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA; otherwise 50
Calculation: Momentum score = 90 if RSI >= 65 AND RSI > prior RSI AND close > prior high; otherwise 75 if RSI >= 55 AND RSI < 65 AND RSI > prior RSI; otherwise 55 if RSI is between 45 and 55 inclusive AND absolute close percent change < 0.3%; otherwise 35 if RSI >= 35 AND RSI < 45 AND RSI < prior RSI; otherwise 20 if RSI >= 20 AND RSI < 35 AND RSI < prior RSI AND close < prior low; otherwise 5 if RSI < 20 OR RSI > 80; otherwise 50
Calculation: Velocity score = compare absolute close percent change with the average absolute close percent change over the velocity lookback, and compare volume with its moving average: score 90 if change > 2.5 times average AND volume > 2 times its average; otherwise 75 if change > 1.8 times average AND volume > 1.5 times its average; otherwise 55 if change is 0.6 to 1.5 times average inclusive; otherwise 35 if change is between 0.3 and 0.6 times average; otherwise 15 if change < 0.3 times average AND volume < 0.8 times its average; otherwise 5 if change < 0.05%; otherwise 50
Calculation: Behavior score = compare absolute candle body with full range and combined shadows: score 90 if body/range > 0.8 AND shadows < 0.25 times body; otherwise 75 if body/range > 0.7 AND shadows < 0.5 times body; otherwise 55 if body/range is 0.5 to 0.7 inclusive; otherwise 35 if body/range is 0.3 to 0.5; otherwise 15 if body/range is 0.15 to 0.3; otherwise 5 if body/range < 0.15 AND shadows > 3 times body; otherwise 50
Calculation: C1 score = arithmetic mean of the direction, momentum, velocity, and behavior scores
Calculation: Total score = arithmetic mean of C1, C2, and C3 scores; each of C2 and C3 is the arithmetic mean of its four component scores
Long: C1 score >= 60 OR Total score >= 60
Short: C1 score < 40 OR Total score < 40
