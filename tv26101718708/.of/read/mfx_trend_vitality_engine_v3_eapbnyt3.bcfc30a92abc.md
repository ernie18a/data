Calculation: All calculations below use higher-timeframe bars. On historical chart bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe result; on a live chart bar, use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Centered normalization of a series = (series − rolling minimum) / (rolling maximum − rolling minimum) × scale − half scale; use zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Buyer/seller pressure = the average of centered-normalized Chaikin Money Flow and centered-normalized Money Flow Index of typical price, using the same normalization lookback.
Calculation: Trend oscillator = EMA of (SMA of typical price − EMA of that SMA) / (scale factor × EMA of the absolute difference between that SMA and its EMA)); use the same EMA lookback in the numerator and denominator, use zero when the denominator is zero, and apply a separate smoothing EMA to the ratio.
Calculation: For each side, a completed wave is a consecutive positive or negative trend-oscillator run, ending when the oscillator crosses zero. Record its peak absolute oscillator, sum of absolute oscillator values, close and buyer/seller pressure at that peak; use absolute pressure for seller waves. Compare the latest completed wave with an older completed wave of the same side to identify oscillator, area, and pressure decay. Directional price progress is the percentage change between their recorded closes, positive for an increase for buyers and a decrease for sellers. Efficiency = directional price progress / max(minimum energy, sum of wave areas over that comparison interval). At each wave close, efficiency decays if the absolute current efficiency / max(absolute previous efficiency, minimum denominator) is below a threshold.
Calculation: For each side, aging impulse = the sum of configurable weights for directional price progress with oscillator decay, area decay, pressure decay, efficiency decay, and the simultaneous presence of directional price progress and all three decay conditions. Initialize penalty to zero. At a same-side wave close add impulse × wave-damage multiplier; on other higher-timeframe bars add impulse × aging-leak multiplier and subtract recovery per bar. Clamp the penalty to its score range.
Calculation: For each side, wave score = the capped sum of weighted, capped ratios of latest peak oscillator to a reference magnitude, latest wave area to the previous same-side wave area, and latest wave pressure to the previous same-side wave pressure; floor each denominator at its minimum. Before the older comparison wave exists, use the maximum wave score. Structural vitality = weighted average of wave score and maximum score minus penalty. Live vitality = base score plus weighted, capped positive directional oscillator and pressure scores, each shifted by its own bias and divided by its own normalization scale; negate oscillator and pressure for the seller calculation. Side vitality = rounded, capped weighted average of structural and live vitality.
Long: Buyer/seller pressure >= zero.
Short: Buyer/seller pressure < zero.
Long: Buyer vitality > seller vitality OR (buyer vitality = seller vitality AND higher-timeframe close > its simple moving average).
Short: Seller vitality > buyer vitality OR (seller vitality = buyer vitality AND higher-timeframe close < its simple moving average).
Long: Trend oscillator crosses above its simple moving average AND trend oscillator < zero AND seller vitality <= a weakness threshold.
Short: Trend oscillator crosses below its simple moving average AND trend oscillator > zero AND buyer vitality <= a weakness threshold.
