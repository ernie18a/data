Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of source price times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting at the start of the selected calendar period or at the selected custom anchor bar; use source price when cumulative volume is not positive.
Calculation: VWAP bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus a band multiplier times the square root of the nonnegative volume-weighted variance of source price since the anchor. The second band's multiplier is independently adjustable relative to the first band's multiplier.
Calculation: Pending band touch = for each band and side independently, retain a touch until a subsequent bar resolves it. Resolve an upper first-band touch when first upper band minus close >= rejection-distance multiplier times ATR OR close < anchored VWAP OR close > first upper band plus touch-tolerance multiplier times ATR OR elapsed bars >= outcome window. Resolve a lower first-band touch when close minus first lower band >= rejection-distance multiplier times ATR OR close > anchored VWAP OR close < first lower band minus touch-tolerance multiplier times ATR OR elapsed bars >= outcome window. Resolve an upper second-band touch when second upper band minus close >= rejection-distance multiplier times ATR OR close < first upper band OR elapsed bars >= outcome window. Resolve a lower second-band touch when close minus second lower band >= rejection-distance multiplier times ATR OR close > first lower band OR elapsed bars >= outcome window. Resolve existing touches before detecting new touches on each bar.
Long: close > anchored VWAP
Short: close <= anchored VWAP
Long: no pending lower touch for the selected band AND low <= selected lower band plus touch-tolerance multiplier times ATR AND close > selected lower band
Short: no pending upper touch for the selected band AND high >= selected upper band minus touch-tolerance multiplier times ATR AND close < selected upper band
