Short: (EMA Crossover selected AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close < long-term regime EMA) OR (RSI Strategy selected AND RSI crosses above overbought level) OR (MACD Strategy selected AND MACD line crosses below signal line) OR (Bollinger Bands Breakout selected AND close crosses below upper band) OR (SuperTrend selected AND SuperTrend direction changes from uptrend to downtrend) OR (Custom selected AND short-term SMA crosses below longer-term SMA), within configured backtest date range
Exit: Long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR trailing stop is active and low <= highest high since entry − trailing distance
Exit: Short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR trailing stop is active and high >= lowest low since entry + trailing distance
