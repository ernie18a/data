Calculation: Confirmed pivot for lookback N = Maintain a leg state, initially bearish. On each chart bar, if the high N bars ago is greater than every high of the latest N bars, set the leg bearish; otherwise, if the low N bars ago is less than every low of the latest N bars, set it bullish. A change to bearish confirms a high pivot at the high N bars ago; a change to bullish confirms a low pivot at the low N bars ago. Maintain separate pivot and leg states for the swing, shorter internal, and optional equal-high/low lookbacks.
Calculation: Structure break = A close crosses above the latest confirmed high pivot or below the latest confirmed low pivot; each pivot can be broken only once. Internal breaks also require the internal pivot price to differ from the corresponding swing pivot price. If the optional internal confluence filter is enabled, an upward break requires high - max(close, open) > min(close, open - low), and a downward break requires high - max(close, open) < min(close, open - low).
Calculation: Swing cycle counts = Process newly confirmed swing, internal, and, when enabled, equal-high/low pivots in that order using one shared cycle state. Classify a low as lower low if below the previous low of its own pivot series, otherwise higher low; classify a high as higher high if above the previous high of its own series, otherwise lower high. Lower lows and lower highs share a bearish cycle; higher lows and higher highs share a bullish cycle. When the cycle changes, reset both counts for the new cycle and set the current classification count to one; otherwise increment that classification's count.
Calculation: Fibonacci setup = On a confirmed swing higher low, create a long setup if the shared higher-low and higher-high counts are each at least their respective minimum counts, a prior swing higher high exists, and that high exceeds the new higher low. On a confirmed swing lower high, create a short setup if the shared lower-high and lower-low counts are each at least their respective minimum counts, a prior swing lower low exists, and the new lower high exceeds that low. Store the latest qualifying swing higher high and lower low independently. A new setup replaces any existing setup and resets its entered state. For a long setup, each level equals the stored higher high minus its independently selected Fibonacci ratio times the distance to the new higher low. For a short setup, each level equals the stored lower low plus its independently selected Fibonacci ratio times the distance to the new lower high. Use separate ratios for entry, target, and stop; the target ratio is closer to the reference extreme than the entry ratio, and the stop ratio is farther from it. A setup remains active until its target or stop is touched, including on its entry bar.
Calculation: Fair-value-gap threshold = Optionally use zero; otherwise, on each chart bar cumulatively sum the absolute value of (last completed gap-timeframe close - open) / (last completed gap-timeframe open × 100) only when a new gap-timeframe bar starts, then divide by the chart bar index and multiply by a threshold multiplier. The gap timeframe may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe. Its current high and low are from the bar still forming; its previous open and close and its two-bars-ago high and low are from completed bars. The source uses lookahead for historical higher-timeframe current highs and lows, exposing the completed bar's final values at the start of that bar.
Long: A swing or internal upward structure break occurs.
Short: A swing or internal downward structure break occurs.
Long: A swing low pivot is confirmed.
Short: A swing high pivot is confirmed.
Long: An equal-low pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding equal-low pivot is less than a multiple of ATR.
Short: An equal-high pivot is confirmed AND its distance from the preceding equal-high pivot is less than a multiple of ATR.
Long: A new gap-timeframe bar starts AND its current low > the high two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the last completed gap-timeframe close > that high AND (last completed gap-timeframe close - open) / (last completed gap-timeframe open × 100) > the fair-value-gap threshold.
Short: A new gap-timeframe bar starts AND its current high < the low two gap-timeframe bars ago AND the last completed gap-timeframe close < that low AND -(last completed gap-timeframe close - open) / (last completed gap-timeframe open × 100) > the fair-value-gap threshold.
Long: A long Fibonacci setup is active AND it has not entered AND low <= its entry level.
Short: A short Fibonacci setup is active AND it has not entered AND high >= its entry level.
Exit: long, its Fibonacci setup is active and entered AND (high >= its target level OR low <= its stop level).
Exit: short, its Fibonacci setup is active and entered AND (low <= its target level OR high >= its stop level).
