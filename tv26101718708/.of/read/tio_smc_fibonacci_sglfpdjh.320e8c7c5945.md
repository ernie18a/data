Calculation: Swing pivot high/low = the high/low from the configured swing lookback bars ago when it exceeds the highest/lowest value in the intervening bars; internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure = close crosses above the latest pivot high / below the latest pivot low, respectively; each pivot can be crossed only once.
Calculation: Long entry level = latest higher high − (latest higher high − latest higher low) × entry retracement ratio, set after at least two higher highs and two higher lows in the current bullish swing cycle.
Calculation: Short entry level = latest lower low + (latest lower high − latest lower low) × entry retracement ratio, set after at least two lower lows and one lower high in the current bearish swing cycle.
Calculation: Long take-profit/stop-loss = latest higher high − (latest higher high − latest higher low) × take-profit/stop-loss retracement ratio.
Calculation: Short take-profit/stop-loss = latest lower low + (latest lower high − latest lower low) × take-profit/stop-loss retracement ratio.
Long: an active long setup has not entered and low ≤ long entry level OR close crosses above the latest swing or internal pivot high.
Short: an active short setup has not entered and high ≥ short entry level OR close crosses below the latest swing or internal pivot low.
Exit: long, active entered long setup and (high ≥ long take-profit OR low ≤ long stop-loss)
Exit: short, active entered short setup and (low ≤ short take-profit OR high ≥ short stop-loss)
