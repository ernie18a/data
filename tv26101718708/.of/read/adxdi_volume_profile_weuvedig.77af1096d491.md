Calculation: Profile rows = Divide each fixed-length session, anchored at the first chart bar, into equal price rows from its lowest low to its highest high. On each chart bar, calculate the current session through that bar and the configured preceding sessions. If a session has no price range, assign all its bars to one row. When using a higher timeframe, scale the session length by the rounded ratio of higher to chart timeframe durations, subject to a maximum session length.
Calculation: Row energy = For each session bar, calculate +DI, -DI and ADX with the same length, either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe. For historical bars, use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; while a bar is forming in real time, use the forming higher-timeframe bar. Replace missing indicator values with zero. Let imbalance = absolute value of (+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI), or zero when the denominator is zero. Multiply each indicator by (1 + imbalance) and by volume if volume weighting is enabled and volume is positive; otherwise use a weight of one. For higher-timeframe calculations, use the corresponding higher-timeframe volume. Divide each result equally among all profile rows touched by the chart bar's low-to-high range, and sum each indicator's contributions separately for each row. Row average ADX energy is its ADX energy divided by the number of chart bars touching it, or zero if none. Normalize each row's +DI and -DI energy separately by the maximum of that energy across the session's rows, using one as the denominator when the maximum is nonpositive.
Long: Any profile row's average ADX energy >= ADX threshold AND its +DI energy > its -DI energy multiplied by a dominance factor AND its normalized +DI energy >= noise threshold.
Short: Any profile row's average ADX energy >= ADX threshold AND its -DI energy > its +DI energy multiplied by the same dominance factor AND its normalized -DI energy >= noise threshold.
