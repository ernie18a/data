Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND current low − high two bars ago >= configurable multiple of ATR; its lower bound is high two bars ago and its upper bound is current low.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago − current high >= configurable multiple of ATR; its lower bound is current high and its upper bound is low two bars ago.
Calculation: Displacement = high > low AND absolute value of close − open >= 0.55 × (high − low) AND absolute value of close − open >= configurable multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Order block = on a displacement bar, search a configurable number of preceding bars for candles opposite to the displacement direction whose high − low <= 2.5 × current ATR and whose absolute body / range >= 0.25. For the volume-filtered method, also require candidate volume >= current simple moving average of volume. Select the most recent qualifying candle, or, for the extreme method, the lowest-low candidate before an upward displacement or the highest-high candidate before a downward displacement. The selected candle's low and high are the zone bounds.
Calculation: Tracked zones = add enabled fair value gaps and order blocks on their formation bar, in bullish-gap, bearish-gap, bullish-order-block, bearish-order-block order; retain at most a configurable number, removing the oldest first. Collapsed zones remain in this count until removed. Each zone stores its formation ATR, bounds, midpoint, first touch bar, and collapsed state.
Calculation: First touch = the first bar after or including formation where high >= zone lower bound − configurable ATR multiple AND low <= zone upper bound + configurable ATR multiple; alternatively, use close >= zone lower bound − configurable ATR multiple AND close <= zone upper bound + configurable ATR multiple. Use current ATR for the tolerance.
Calculation: Collapse = for an uncollapsed tracked zone, on the bar a configurable number of bars after its first touch, calculate the average close over that number of most recent bars. Collapse to support if the average > upper bound, or if the average is between the bounds inclusive AND close >= midpoint. Collapse to resistance if the average < lower bound, or if the average is between the bounds inclusive AND close < midpoint. A zone collapses only once; intervening touches or holds are not required.
Calculation: Flow = clamp((0.5 × (EMA of close − its previous value) + 0.5 × rate of change of close) / flow ATR / 1.5, −1, 1), using zero for unavailable EMA slope or rate of change and zero when flow ATR is not positive; smooth this series with an EMA, initializing its first value to the unsmoothed flow. The rate-of-change lookback is half the flow EMA lookback, subject to a minimum of two bars; the flow ATR lookback is at least as long as the flow EMA lookback, and the smoothing EMA lookback is approximately one quarter of the flow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Zone support probability = clamp(0.5 + 0.5 × clamp((normalized direction weight × direction bias + normalized flow weight × smoothed flow) × (0.4 + 0.6 × (normalized distance weight × distance pull + (1 − normalized distance weight) × 0.5)), −1, 1), 0.01, 0.99). Normalize the three configurable weights by their sum, using 1 as the denominator if their sum is zero. Direction bias = clamp((close − zone midpoint) / max(zone upper bound − zone lower bound, 1e−10) / 4, −1, 1). Distance pull = exp(−ln(2) × distance in ATR units / configurable half-life), where distance in ATR units = absolute value of close − midpoint divided by max(current ATR, half the formation ATR), or zero if that denominator is not positive. Resistance probability = 1 − support probability.
Long: Bullish fair value gap forms OR upward displacement produces an order block.
Short: Bearish fair value gap forms OR downward displacement produces an order block.
Long: An uncollapsed tracked zone has support probability >= resistance probability.
Short: An uncollapsed tracked zone has resistance probability > support probability.
Long: A tracked zone collapses to support.
Short: A tracked zone collapses to resistance.
Long: Smoothed flow > positive flow threshold.
Short: Smoothed flow < negative flow threshold.
