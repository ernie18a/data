Calculation: Percentage change = (current chart-bar close − close from the lookback period ago on 1-minute bars) / that earlier close × 100; the 1-minute timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, and the earlier close is from a completed bar.
Long: Percentage change ≤ −threshold AND position is flat or short
Short: Percentage change ≥ threshold AND position is flat or long
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
