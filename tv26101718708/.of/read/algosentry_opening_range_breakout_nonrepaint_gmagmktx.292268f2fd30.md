Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening session; freeze both when the first bar outside the session begins and reset all break, retest, and trend states at the next session start.
Calculation: Volume point of control = at opening-session end, select chart bars within the configured window from either the opening-session start or the trading-day start through the last opening-session bar, limited to a fixed maximum lookback. Divide their lowest low to highest high into equal price bins; distribute each bar's volume equally among all bins touched by its low-to-high range. Use the midpoint of the lowest bin with the greatest total volume, only if it lies within the opening range.
Calculation: Confirmation close = chart-bar close, or the close returned at chart-bar close from the last completed bar of the selected higher or lower confirmation timeframe; for another timeframe, evaluate a break only when the returned confirmation-bar opening time changes.
Long: Opening range is frozen AND bars elapsed since the last opening-session bar <= the tracking limit AND no long break has occurred this session AND (both directions are allowed OR no short break has occurred this session) AND confirmation close > opening-range high.
Short: Opening range is frozen AND bars elapsed since the last opening-session bar <= the tracking limit AND no short break has occurred this session AND (both directions are allowed OR no long break has occurred this session) AND confirmation close < opening-range low.
Long: Retest sequence is enabled AND a long break occurred on an earlier bar this session AND no long retest or long trend has occurred this session AND bars elapsed since the last opening-session bar <= the tracking limit AND ((edge retest is enabled AND low <= opening-range high) OR (percentage retest is enabled AND low <= opening-range high minus the configured fraction of opening-range height) OR (volume-point-of-control retest is enabled AND volume point of control exists AND low <= volume point of control)).
Short: Retest sequence is enabled AND a short break occurred on an earlier bar this session AND no short retest or short trend has occurred this session AND bars elapsed since the last opening-session bar <= the tracking limit AND ((edge retest is enabled AND high >= opening-range low) OR (percentage retest is enabled AND high >= opening-range low plus the configured fraction of opening-range height) OR (volume-point-of-control retest is enabled AND volume point of control exists AND high >= volume point of control)).
Long: Retest sequence is enabled AND a long retest occurred on an earlier bar this session AND no long trend has occurred this session AND bars elapsed since the last opening-session bar <= the tracking limit AND close > opening-range high.
Short: Retest sequence is enabled AND a short retest occurred on an earlier bar this session AND no short trend has occurred this session AND bars elapsed since the last opening-session bar <= the tracking limit AND close < opening-range low.
