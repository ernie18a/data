Calculation: MA_k = moving average (EMA, SMA, WMA, VWMA, RMA or HMA) of close. Each enabled MA has its own lookback and may be computed on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. A higher-timeframe MA uses the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Enabled MAs are ordered fast to slow, from shorter to longer lookback.
Calculation: KAMA = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close, computed on a higher timeframe than the chart using the bar still forming. ER = |close - close[n]| / sum over i=0..n-1 of |close[i] - close[i+1]|. sc = (ER*(2/(fast+1) - 2/(slow+1)) + 2/(slow+1))^2, with fast period < slow period. KAMA = KAMA[1] + sc*(close - KAMA[1]), seeded with close.
Calculation: HTF_VWAP = session-anchored VWAP of hlc3 (resets each session), computed on a higher timeframe than the chart using the bar still forming.
Calculation: +DI, -DI, ADX = Wilder DMI/ADX. plusDM = up-move if up-move > down-move and > 0, else 0; minusDM = down-move if down-move > up-move and > 0, else 0. +DI = 100*RMA(plusDM)/RMA(TR) and -DI = 100*RMA(minusDM)/RMA(TR). ADX = 100*RMA(|+DI - -DI| / (+DI + -DI)), with a smoothing length that may differ from the DI length.
Long: (close > every enabled MA AND each enabled MA is above the next slower enabled MA; with a single enabled MA only close > that MA) AND (optional filter) +DI > -DI AND ADX > threshold AND (optional filter) close > KAMA AND (optional filter) close > HTF_VWAP AND the most recent prior entry signal was not Long (first bar of a new Long state; a Short position is reversed to Long)
Short: (close < every enabled MA AND each enabled MA is below the next slower enabled MA; with a single enabled MA only close < that MA) AND (optional filter) -DI > +DI AND ADX > threshold AND (optional filter) close < KAMA AND (optional filter) close < HTF_VWAP AND the most recent prior entry signal was not Short (first bar of a new Short state; a Long position is reversed to Short)
