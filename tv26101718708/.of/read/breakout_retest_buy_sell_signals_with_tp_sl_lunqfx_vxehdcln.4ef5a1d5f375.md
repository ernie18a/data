Calculation: Swing level = a confirmed swing high or low, identified using configurable bars on both sides; merge a new swing into an existing waiting level if their prices are within the retest zone, otherwise create a level.
Calculation: Retest stop = for long, the lower of the retest bar low, prior bar low, and level, minus an ATR buffer; for short, the higher of those highs and the level, plus an ATR buffer. Enforce a configurable minimum stop distance in ATR; risk distance is the absolute difference between entry close and stop.
Long: A waiting level breaks upward when close > level + ATR × break buffer AND prior close ≤ level; from the second bar after that break and within the configured retest window, the break has not failed (close ≥ level − ATR × break buffer), low ≤ level + ATR × retest-zone size, close > level, and close > open; if enabled, the break must have had either an absolute candle body ≥ ATR or volume > its recent average × configured factor, and close > the long-period trend EMA; the retest stop risk distance ≤ ATR × maximum stop distance; no long position is open.
Short: A waiting level breaks downward when close < level − ATR × break buffer AND prior close ≥ level; from the second bar after that break and within the configured retest window, the break has not failed (close ≤ level + ATR × break buffer), high ≥ level − ATR × retest-zone size, close < level, and close < open; if enabled, the break must have had either an absolute candle body ≥ ATR or volume > its recent average × configured factor, and close < the long-period trend EMA; the retest stop risk distance ≤ ATR × maximum stop distance; no short position is open.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ entry + risk distance × configured TP3 multiple OR a short-entry signal occurs; check stop before TP3 when both are reached on the same bar. Evaluate from the bar after entry.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ entry − risk distance × configured TP3 multiple OR a long-entry signal occurs; check stop before TP3 when both are reached on the same bar. Evaluate from the bar after entry.
