Calculation: Level store = On each closed bar, add confirmed pivot highs and then pivot lows, using the same number of bars on each side. Merge each pivot into the first unbroken level within the current ATR-scaled retest zone; otherwise append it. Remove the oldest level whenever the store exceeds its capacity. Walk levels newest first. An unbroken level breaks upward when close > level + current ATR-scaled break buffer AND previous close <= level; it breaks downward when close < level - current ATR-scaled break buffer AND previous close >= level. Record the break bar, direction, and whether the break candle has absolute body >= ATR OR positive volume > its moving average multiplied by the strong-volume factor. Remove an unbroken level when its age from the pivot bar exceeds the maximum level age. Remove a broken-up level when close < level - current ATR-scaled break buffer, and a broken-down level when close > level + current ATR-scaled break buffer; also remove either when its age from the break exceeds the retest window. Starting on the second bar after a break, select and remove the newest remaining level whose directional retest and enabled strong-break and trend filters pass. Select at most one level per bar; remove it even if its subsequent stop-distance or position-side check fails.
Calculation: Long retest = low <= level + current ATR-scaled retest zone AND close > level AND close > open AND (strong-break filter disabled OR recorded break is strong) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend EMA).
Calculation: Short retest = high >= level - current ATR-scaled retest zone AND close < level AND close < open AND (strong-break filter disabled OR recorded break is strong) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend EMA).
Calculation: Long stop = at the selected retest, min(min(low, previous low, level) - ATR × stop-buffer factor, close - ATR × minimum-stop factor); retain this value for the trade.
Calculation: Short stop = at the selected retest, max(max(high, previous high, level) + ATR × stop-buffer factor, close + ATR × minimum-stop factor); retain this value for the trade.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus position direction × absolute(entry price - retained stop) × final-target risk multiple, where position direction is positive for long and negative for short.
Long: Selected level broke upward AND long retest AND close - long stop <= ATR × maximum-stop factor AND position side is not long.
Short: Selected level broke downward AND short retest AND short stop - close <= ATR × maximum-stop factor AND position side is not short.
Exit: long, bar is after entry AND (low <= retained long stop OR high >= target).
Exit: short, bar is after entry AND (high >= retained short stop OR low <= target).
Exit: long, short entry condition is true.
Exit: short, long entry condition is true.
