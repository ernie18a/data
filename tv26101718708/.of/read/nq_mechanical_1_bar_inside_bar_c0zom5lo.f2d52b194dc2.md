Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the first bar of each trading session; typical price is (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Continuity score = number of selected timeframes where chart close is above the current bar's open minus the number where chart close is below that open. Use the chart timeframe and selected higher timeframes; use their still-forming bars.
Calculation: Inside-bar break = on each confirmed chart bar, maintain two independent ranges. For the chart-timeframe range, if no range is active and the preceding bar's high is at most its predecessor's high and its low is at least its predecessor's low, activate the preceding bar's range. An upward or downward break occurs when close is respectively above its high or below its low; deactivate after either break, or when the current high exceeds the range high or current low falls below the range low without a close break. For the higher-timeframe range, at the start of each higher-timeframe bar, activate the last completed higher-timeframe bar's range if that bar's high is at most its predecessor's high and its low is at least its predecessor's low; otherwise deactivate it. A chart close above or below the active range produces the corresponding break once per higher-timeframe bar. Reset the one-break counter at the start of each higher-timeframe bar after evaluating that chart bar's break, and whenever the range is inactive. A break consumes its range or counter even when entry filters fail.
Calculation: Recent key-level cross = at least one cross during a parameterized number of chart bars including the current bar, where a cross occurs when chart close, high, or low moves from at or below a level to above it, or from at or above it to below it, comparing both price and level with their respective previous-bar values. Levels are the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe day, the completed highs and lows of two parameterized intraday sessions, and the open at a parameterized session start. Reset the intraday session ranges at that session start and include each range only after its session ends.
Long: confirmed chart bar AND bar time is within the parameterized entry window AND upward inside-bar break AND close > session VWAP AND continuity score > 0.
Short: confirmed chart bar AND bar time is within the parameterized entry window AND downward inside-bar break AND close > session VWAP AND recent key-level cross AND continuity score <= 0.
