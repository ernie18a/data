Calculation: A cross above means current value > the other value AND previous value <= the other value; a cross below means current value < the other value AND previous value >= the other value. Evaluate signals on the current chart bar; a confirmed pivot is recognized only after its equal-sized left and right confirmation windows have elapsed.
Calculation: Trend up = short EMA >= medium EMA, or, under the alternative trend setting, close >= long EMA. Trend down is its complement.
Calculation: Fast average = either Hull moving average of close or EMA of close + (close - close at lag floor((length - 1) / 2)); rising means current value >= previous value, falling means current value < previous value.
Calculation: Structure = maintain the latest confirmed high and low pivots and whether each has already been broken. An up break occurs once per high pivot when close, or high under the alternative break setting, > that pivot; a down break occurs once per low pivot when close, or low under the alternative break setting, < that pivot. The structure direction becomes the direction of the most recent break; process an up break before a down break on the same bar. A change of character is a break opposite to the immediately preceding structure direction. Retain the latest upward and downward broken levels separately.
Calculation: Liquidity zones = maintain confirmed high and low pivots using a separate pivot length. Discard stored high pivots when high > pivot + ATR tolerance and stored low pivots when low < pivot - ATR tolerance, or when their age exceeds the pivot lookback. A new high pivot merges into the first high zone if it is <= the zone maximum AND >= its minimum minus tolerance; otherwise create a high zone from the first stored high pivot within tolerance. Mirror for lows: merge when the pivot is >= the zone minimum AND <= its maximum plus tolerance; otherwise create a zone from the first stored low pivot within tolerance. Store each new pivot; limit the total number of zones by deleting the oldest. Before processing new pivots, remove a high zone and record a high sweep when high > its maximum; remove a low zone and record a low sweep when low < its minimum.
Calculation: Bullish zone touch = the bar overlaps an active low zone expanded by ATR tolerance, OR overlaps the latest upward broken level expanded by that tolerance, OR overlaps the long EMA expanded by that tolerance. Bearish zone touch uses a high zone, the latest downward broken level, and the long EMA.
Calculation: Buy volume and sell volume = sum completed lower-timeframe intrabar volumes within the chart bar, assigning rising intrabars to buy, falling intrabars to sell, and half of flat intrabar volume to each. Total volume is their sum; buy percentage is buy volume divided by total volume. Relative volume is total volume divided by its rolling median or, under the alternative setting, rolling mean. A buy bubble requires positive total volume, relative volume >= its threshold, buy percentage >= the dominance threshold, and either no previously accepted bubble in the suppression window or relative volume > that bubble's relative volume. A sell bubble uses sell percentage >= the dominance threshold AND buy percentage < one half. Track the last accepted bubble separately by side or jointly, according to the selected suppression setting; a stronger accepted bubble replaces the previous one. Use the chart instrument's available volume.
Calculation: Delta = buy volume - sell volume; missing delta counts as zero in sums. Cumulative delta is the running sum of delta, optionally reset at each new day or week. Bullish divergence occurs when a newly confirmed price-low pivot < the preceding confirmed price-low pivot AND cumulative delta at the new pivot > cumulative delta at the preceding pivot, both in the same cumulative-delta reset period. Bearish divergence uses a higher price-high pivot AND lower cumulative delta. Recognize divergence on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Bias = sum of trend directions across the configured set of distinct timeframes, with each direction +1 for short EMA >= medium EMA, or for bullish Supertrend, or for close >= long EMA, according to the selected method, and -1 otherwise. For higher-than-chart timeframes use the last completed bar as available at the chart bar close; for lower-than-chart timeframes use the last completed intrabar. Missing directions contribute zero.
Calculation: Sideways = the highest high minus lowest low over the preceding range lookback bars <= an ATR multiple OR the number of short/medium EMA crosses in the current range lookback >= a threshold. An upward range breakout occurs when the previous bar was sideways AND close > the highest high of the preceding range lookback; a downward breakout uses close < the lowest low. A direction passes the range filter when the filter is disabled OR the current bar is not sideways OR the matching breakout occurred within the allowed number of bars.
Calculation: Recent means the event occurred on the current bar or within the relevant configurable lookback; the pullback/sweep lookback and continuation lookback are independent. Directional delta confirmation means the sum of delta over its short lookback > 0 for long or < 0 for short, OR the matching divergence is recent.
Calculation: Pullback score = count of these eight conditions: bias >= its positive threshold; close > long EMA; structure direction is up; bullish zone touch is recent; buy bubble is recent; a bullish candle in a downtrend is recent; long delta confirmation; fast average rising. The short score mirrors each direction, using bias <= the negative threshold, close < long EMA, bearish structure and touches, sell bubble, bearish candle in an uptrend, short delta confirmation, and fast average falling.
Calculation: Reversal score = count of a recent matching bubble, recent matching divergence, recent matching change of character, fast average rising for long or falling for short, and delta sum > 0 for long or < 0 for short.
Calculation: Continuation score = count of bias meeting the directional threshold, matching structure direction, recent matching zone touch, recent matching bubble, and directional delta confirmation, using the continuation lookback.
Calculation: Structure-break score = count of bias > 0 for long or < 0 for short, recent matching bubble, directional delta confirmation, fast average rising for long or falling for short, and positive volume with directional volume percentage >= its structure-volume threshold AND relative volume >= its structure-volume threshold. Use the continuation lookback for recent events.
