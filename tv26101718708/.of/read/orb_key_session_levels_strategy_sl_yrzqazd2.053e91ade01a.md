Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured session in America/New_York; finalize on the first bar outside the session and reset on a chart bar whose exchange-timezone day of week differs from the preceding bar. Its midpoint is (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Pending breakout = a close crossing above the completed opening-range high for a long, or below its low for a short, before any opening-range entry signal that day. In a retest mode, start waiting on the breakout bar; on each later bar, first check the retest, then cancel if the long close is <= the range high or the short close is >= the range low, then cancel if bars elapsed >= the timeout. Reset at the day change. A new breakout may be recorded on a bar that cancels a pending breakout.
Calculation: Initial stop = opening-range midpoint, opposite opening-range boundary, or signal-bar close minus a fixed distance for a long and plus that distance for a short, according to the selected stop method. Risk = absolute difference between signal-bar close and initial stop. Each of three profit targets = signal-bar close plus risk times its respective reward-to-risk parameter for a long, or minus that amount for a short; targets are undefined when risk is zero. The first, second, and third targets use independently configurable reward-to-risk parameters and exit quantities.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until break-even is enabled and a bar while the position is open has high >= the first target for a long or low <= the first target for a short; thereafter it is the signal-bar close. Reset the stored stop and targets on the day change.
Calculation: Gap threshold = either the configured percentage divided by 100, or the cumulative sum from the first chart bar of (high - low) / low divided by the zero-based chart-bar index.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing structure = request high and low from each of two independently configured higher timeframes, with one longer than the other; on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while on a live chart bar use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. On each resulting chart-bar series, identify pivot highs and lows using the same number of chart bars on each side and the same lookback for both timeframes; retain the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows, including repeated pivot values when detected.
Long: Before any opening-range entry signal that day AND the opening range is complete AND the selected entry mode is breakout AND close crosses above the opening-range high.
Short: Before any opening-range entry signal that day AND the opening range is complete AND the selected entry mode is breakout AND close crosses below the opening-range low.
Long: Before any opening-range entry signal that day AND the selected entry mode is retest AND a long breakout is pending AND this bar is later than the breakout bar AND low <= the opening-range high in zone mode or the opening-range midpoint in midpoint mode AND close > the opening-range high.
Short: Before any opening-range entry signal that day AND the selected entry mode is retest AND a short breakout is pending AND this bar is later than the breakout bar AND high >= the opening-range low in zone mode or the opening-range midpoint in midpoint mode AND close < the opening-range low.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND (low - high two bars ago) / high two bars ago > gap threshold.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND (low two bars ago - high) / high > gap threshold.
Long: For either configured higher timeframe, latest confirmed pivot high > preceding confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low > preceding confirmed pivot low.
Short: For either configured higher timeframe, latest confirmed pivot high < preceding confirmed pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low < preceding confirmed pivot low.
Exit: long, low <= active stop.
Exit: short, high >= active stop.
Exit: long, high >= a defined profit target AND that target's configured exit quantity > 0, or the target is the third target and its configured quantity is zero; the first two targets close their configured quantities and the third closes its configured quantity or all remaining quantity when zero.
Exit: short, low <= a defined profit target AND that target's configured exit quantity > 0, or the target is the third target and its configured quantity is zero; the first two targets close their configured quantities and the third closes its configured quantity or all remaining quantity when zero.
