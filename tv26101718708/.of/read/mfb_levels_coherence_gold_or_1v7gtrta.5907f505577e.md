Calculation: Bullish gap = interval from the high two chart bars ago to the current low, created when the current low exceeds that high and the gap size divided by the current close meets the minimum percentage threshold.
Calculation: Bearish gap = interval from the current high to the low two chart bars ago, created when the current high is below that low and the gap size divided by the current close meets the minimum percentage threshold.
Long: A bullish gap is created.
Short: A bearish gap is created.
Long: An unfinished bullish gap exists AND a later bar touched the gap with its close at or above the lower boundary AND the current bar is after the most recent such touch AND the current close is above the upper boundary. Mark the gap finished after this decision or after a close below its lower boundary; optionally also mark it finished when the low reaches or falls below its lower boundary, before evaluating the decision. Optionally require the current bar to be within a configured session.
Short: An unfinished bearish gap exists AND a later bar touched the gap with its close at or below the upper boundary AND the current bar is after the most recent such touch AND the current close is below the lower boundary. Mark the gap finished after this decision or after a close above its upper boundary; optionally also mark it finished when the high reaches or exceeds its upper boundary, before evaluating the decision. Optionally require the current bar to be within a configured session.
