Calculation: Pivot-level pools = For each enabled source timeframe, use symmetric confirmed pivot highs and lows with the same left and right lookback. Use the chart bar when timeframes match; for a higher timeframe use its last completed bar on historical chart bars and its still-forming bar in real time; if the longest source timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, use the last available intrabar. At pivot confirmation, retain the pivot only if its bar's volume divided by the source-timeframe volume SMA at confirmation is at least the source-timeframe-group threshold; if the SMA is unavailable or nonpositive, use the fallback ratio. Add each pivot once per source-timeframe confirmation bar, retain separate high and low pools, and discard oldest levels when the source-specific pool capacity is exceeded. On each confirmed chart bar, optionally remove high levels when close > level × (1 + half the zone-height fraction), and low levels when close < level × (1 − half the zone-height fraction), before evaluating grabs.
Calculation: Displayed zone levels = Combine the retained pivot-high and pivot-low pools with optional chart-timeframe equal-high and equal-low clusters. For each cluster type, retain the most recent configured number of confirmed chart-timeframe pivots; scan oldest to newest, grouping unused later pivots within the configured percentage distance of each positive anchor, marking matched later pivots used even if the group fails the minimum count, and output the group average if its count reaches the minimum. Split all levels into above-close and at-or-below-close pools, sort each nearest first, and merge adjacent levels within the configured percentage distance relative to the first level: retain the higher source timeframe, or the first level on equal priority; cluster levels have lower priority than source-timeframe levels. Repeat at the same index after a removal, then retain the configured maximum number of levels on each side.
Long: At least one displayed zone level <= close.
Short: At least one displayed zone level > close.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, low < a retained pivot-low level × (1 − half the zone-height fraction) AND min(open, close) >= that boundary AND bars since the previous long grab > the cooldown; initialize the previous long grab bar index to zero.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, high > a retained pivot-high level × (1 + half the zone-height fraction) AND max(open, close) <= that boundary AND bars since the previous short grab > the cooldown; initialize the previous short grab bar index to zero.
