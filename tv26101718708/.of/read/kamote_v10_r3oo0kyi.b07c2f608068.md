Calculation: Higher-timeframe ATR percentile = percentile rank of higher-timeframe ATR over a longer lookback; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: ATR regime = dead, extreme, or healthy by percentile thresholds with hysteresis: enter dead below the low threshold minus its band and remain dead until above low plus band; enter extreme above high plus band and remain extreme until below high minus band; otherwise healthy.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe slope = change in linear regression of close over a lookback, divided by close; normalize its threshold by ATR/close. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Bullish if slope exceeds the positive threshold, bearish if below the negative threshold, otherwise flat.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback (0 if the denominator is 0). Blend chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe ratios, weighting the higher-timeframe ratio more heavily; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Valid if the blend meets its threshold.
Calculation: Volume percentile = percentile rank of volume over a lookback, with hysteresis: enter expanding above the high threshold plus its band and remain expanding until below high minus band; enter contracting below low minus band and remain contracting until above low plus band; otherwise flat.
Calculation: Trend Long score = eligible only outside the shortest horizon when slope is bullish and efficiency is valid; score it using the longer-horizon weight, increased when ATR is healthy and volume is expanding.
Calculation: Trend Short score = eligible only outside the shortest horizon when slope is bearish and efficiency is valid; score it using the longer-horizon weight, increased when ATR is healthy and volume is expanding.
Calculation: Pullback Long score = eligible only outside the shortest horizon when slope is bullish and efficiency is invalid; score it using the longer-horizon weight, increased when ATR is healthy and volume is not expanding.
Calculation: Pullback Short score = eligible only outside the shortest horizon when slope is bearish and efficiency is invalid; score it using the longer-horizon weight, increased when ATR is healthy and volume is not expanding.
Calculation: Momentum Long score = eligible outside the longest horizon when ATR is extreme, slope bullish, efficiency valid, and volume expanding; score it using the shortest-horizon weight.
Calculation: Momentum Short score = eligible outside the longest horizon when ATR is extreme, slope bearish, efficiency valid, and volume expanding; score it using the shortest-horizon weight.
Calculation: Breakout score = eligible when slope is flat, volume expanding, ATR is not dead, and (ATR is extreme or efficiency is invalid); score it using the horizon weight, increased when both ATR is extreme and efficiency is invalid.
Calculation: Mean Reversion score = eligible when ATR is dead, slope is flat, and volume is not expanding; score it using the horizon weight, increased when volume is flat.
Calculation: Invalid market = (ATR extreme AND efficiency invalid, except when slope is flat AND volume expanding AND ATR is not dead) OR (slope flat AND efficiency invalid AND volume flat) OR (longest horizon AND slope flat AND ATR extreme).
Calculation: Selected strategy = highest positive score among Trend Long, Trend Short, Pullback Long, Pullback Short, Momentum Long, Momentum Short, Breakout, and Mean Reversion, in that tie-breaking order; if the market is invalid, select none.
Long: Selected strategy is Trend Long OR Pullback Long OR Momentum Long.
Short: Selected strategy is Trend Short OR Pullback Short OR Momentum Short.
