Calculation: Reference levels = opens of enabled higher-timeframe bars still forming, plus the close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Touch zone = ATR × touch-distance parameter.
Calculation: Anchor events = For each reference, reset its state to untouched and its counter to zero when its reference period starts. On each confirmed chart bar, in-zone means low ≤ reference + touch zone AND high ≥ reference − touch zone. Bullish rejection means in-zone AND close > reference AND lower wick > candle body × rejection multiple AND close > low + candle range × 0.65. Bearish rejection means in-zone AND close < reference AND upper wick > candle body × rejection multiple AND close < low + candle range × 0.35. Above means close > reference + touch zone; below means close < reference − touch zone. Increment the counter when (above AND prior bias ≤ 0) OR (below AND prior bias ≥ 0); otherwise reset it to zero. Up-cross means close > reference + touch zone AND prior close ≤ reference; down-cross means close < reference − touch zone AND prior close ≥ reference. Reclaim event occurs on either cross when the counter meets the confirmation-bars parameter. Rejection event occurs on either rejection condition. On first in-zone contact, set bias positive if close ≥ reference, otherwise negative; on rejection set it positive for bullish rejection and negative for bearish rejection; on a cross set it positive for up-cross and negative for down-cross. A bullish anchor event is a reclaim or rejection event with resulting bias ≥ 0; a bearish anchor event is one with resulting bias < 0.
Calculation: Control reference = enabled reference with the highest score, where score = max(0, 1 − abs(close − reference) / ATR / fade-distance parameter) × state weight; state weights are 6 for reclaimed, 5 rejected, 4 touched, 3 tested, 2 held, and 0.5 untouched. Resolve ties in order: higher-timeframe opens, then previous close. Control bias and price come from the selected reference.
Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA(close, trend-filter length); bullish trend means close > EMA AND EMA > EMA[3], bearish trend means close < EMA AND EMA < EMA[3].
Calculation: Buy pressure = 100 × sum(buy volume − sell volume, pressure-window length) / sum(volume, pressure-window length). Per bar, buy share is clamp(0.56 × close position in range + 0.28 × body direction + 0.32 × body power × (body direction − 0.5) + 0.14, 0, 1); close position = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick), body direction = 1 if close > open, 0 if close < open, otherwise 0.5, and body power = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick). Buy volume = volume × buy share; sell volume = volume × (1 − buy share). Bullish pressure means buy pressure > 3; bearish pressure means buy pressure < −3.
Calculation: Control score = maximum enabled-reference score. Control is in range when the bar range intersects control price ± touch zone. Control distance = abs(close − control price) / ATR.
Calculation: Long quality = clamp((control score ≥ minimum-control-score ? 22 : control score × 6) + (bullish trend ? 20 : close > trend EMA ? 10 : 0) + (bullish pressure ? 18 : buy pressure > 0 ? 9 : 0) + (control bias ≥ 0 ? 16 : 0) + (control in range OR control distance ≤ 0.75 ? 12 : 0) + (close > open ? 8 : 0) + (any reclaim event ? 4 : 0), 0, 100).
Calculation: Short quality = clamp((control score ≥ minimum-control-score ? 22 : control score × 6) + (bearish trend ? 20 : close < trend EMA ? 10 : 0) + (bearish pressure ? 18 : buy pressure < 0 ? 9 : 0) + (control bias < 0 ? 16 : 0) + (control in range OR control distance ≤ 0.75 ? 12 : 0) + (close < open ? 8 : 0) + (any reclaim event ? 4 : 0), 0, 100).
Long: confirmed chart bar AND any bullish anchor event AND control score ≥ minimum-control-score AND long quality ≥ 58 AND long quality > short quality AND no setup occurred within the execution-cooldown parameter.
Short: confirmed chart bar AND any bearish anchor event AND control score ≥ minimum-control-score AND short quality ≥ 58 AND short quality > long quality AND no setup occurred within the execution-cooldown parameter.
