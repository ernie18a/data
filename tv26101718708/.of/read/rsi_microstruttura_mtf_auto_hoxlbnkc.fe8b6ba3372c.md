Calculation: Micro RSI = arithmetic mean of the RSI values from the available five standard timeframe grades immediately below the chart timeframe, using each grade’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Mean RSI moving average = arithmetic mean of the moving averages of those same lower-timeframe RSI series, using the same MA type and lookback for each.
Long: Micro RSI crosses above Mean RSI moving average
Short: Micro RSI crosses below Mean RSI moving average
