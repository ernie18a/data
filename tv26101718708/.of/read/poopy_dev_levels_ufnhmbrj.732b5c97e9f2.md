Calculation: Pivot high = a bar’s high exceeds the highs of the specified number of bars before and after it; confirmed after those following bars complete. Calculate on chart-timeframe bars and on higher-timeframe bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Pivot low = a bar’s low is below the lows of the specified number of bars before and after it; confirmed after those following bars complete. Calculate on chart-timeframe bars and on higher-timeframe bars using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: On chart timeframe OR higher timeframe, a confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous pivot low of that timeframe AND the latest pivot high occurred before that pivot low AND chart-timeframe close > that latest pivot high.
Short: On chart timeframe OR higher timeframe, a confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous pivot high of that timeframe AND the latest pivot low occurred before that pivot high AND chart-timeframe close < that latest pivot low.
