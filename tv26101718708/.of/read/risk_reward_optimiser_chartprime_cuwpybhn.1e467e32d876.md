Calculation: AO change = (short-lookback SMA of (high + low) / 2 minus long-lookback SMA of (high + low) / 2) minus its previous-bar value.
Calculation: RSI average = SMA of close RSI using the same lookback as the RSI.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA of the stochastic oscillator applied to RSI as its high, low, and close series, using the same lookback for RSI and stochastic; stochastic RSI D = SMA of stochastic RSI K.
Calculation: Entry risk = absolute value of entry close minus the selected entry-bar stop basis, where the stop basis is low minus an ATR multiple, the lowest low over a lookback, or low minus a multiple of the absolute value of (rolling sum of session VWAP of true range times current volume divided by rolling sum of volume).
Long: For each independently tracked reward-to-risk ratio, no long position is active AND the bar datetime is within the selected inclusive date range when the date filter is enabled AND every enabled internal condition holds: Aroon up crosses above Aroon down, or Aroon up is above or below a selected threshold; AO change crosses above zero, or AO change is above or below a selected threshold; close RSI crosses above RSI average, or RSI is above or below a selected threshold; MACD line crosses above its signal line, or MACD line is above or below a selected threshold; smoothed stochastic K crosses above stochastic D, or K is above or below a selected threshold; stochastic RSI K crosses above stochastic RSI D, or K is above or below a selected threshold; selected shorter-lookback moving average of close crosses above selected longer-lookback moving average of close; SuperTrend direction crosses below zero. Each indicator's crossover, above-threshold, or below-threshold condition is selected independently; disabled indicators impose no condition.
Long: For each independently tracked reward-to-risk ratio, no long position is active AND the bar datetime is within the selected inclusive date range when the date filter is enabled AND a selected backtest data series equals a selected value.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + selected reward-to-risk ratio times entry risk OR low <= entry price minus entry risk.
