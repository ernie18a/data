Calculation: Structure = For each layer, confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right pivot depths. Initialize the structural high and low from the first confirmed pivot of each kind; initialize direction to neutral. Since the last break, retain the lowest confirmed pivot low and highest confirmed pivot high as candidates. On each bar, first update the candidates. If the selected bullish break price is strictly above the structural high, then, if the previous direction was bullish and a candidate low exists, replace the structural low and its time with that candidate; set the structural high to the break price and its time to the current bar, set direction bullish, and clear both candidates. Otherwise, if the selected bearish break price is strictly below the structural low, then, if the previous direction was bearish and a candidate high exists, replace the structural high and its time with that candidate; set the structural low to the break price and its time to the current bar, set direction bearish, and clear both candidates. Select either high and low as the respective break prices or close for both. A structure is valid when direction is nonneutral, both structural endpoints and their times exist, and structural high > structural low. Calculate local and macro structures on chart bars, with a longer pivot depth for macro. Calculate bias structure on an equal or higher timeframe, using either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Golden zone = The interval between the two Fibonacci retracement levels defining the golden zone, measured from the structural high toward the structural low for a bullish structure and from the structural low toward the structural high for a bearish structure.
Long: Any valid layer has bullish direction AND chart high >= its golden zone lower boundary AND chart low <= its golden zone upper boundary.
Short: Any valid layer has bearish direction AND chart high >= its golden zone lower boundary AND chart low <= its golden zone upper boundary.
Long: All three structures are valid AND all three directions are bullish.
Short: All three structures are valid AND all three directions are bearish.
