Calculation: Kaufman adaptive moving average = recursively update the prior value toward close using a squared smoothing constant; the constant interpolates between a faster and a slower smoothing rate according to the efficiency ratio, defined as the absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Order-flow oscillator = EMA of ((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick size) × 2 − 1) × relative volume, where relative volume is volume divided by its SMA over the oscillator smoothing lookback (use 1 if volume or its SMA is not positive).
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across an equal number of chart-timeframe bars on both sides; the pivot becomes available only after the bars on its right have completed.
Long: Kaufman adaptive moving average > its prior value OR current close > the latest confirmed swing high AND prior close ≤ that swing high OR order-flow oscillator ≥ 0 OR order-flow oscillator > 0 AND its prior value ≤ 0
Short: Kaufman adaptive moving average ≤ its prior value OR current close < the latest confirmed swing low AND prior close ≥ that swing low OR order-flow oscillator < 0 OR order-flow oscillator < 0 AND its prior value ≥ 0
