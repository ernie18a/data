Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands are entirely inside Keltner Channels
Calculation: Squeeze release = previous bar was squeezed AND current bar is not squeezed; strict expansion mode additionally requires Bollinger Bands to be entirely outside Keltner Channels on the release bar
Calculation: Coil duration = consecutive squeezed bars immediately before the release; coil high/low = highest high and lowest low across those bars; coil tightness = lowest Bollinger half-width / Keltner half-width ratio during the coil
Calculation: Momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of (the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the momentum lookback) and the Keltner middle line, over that lookback
Calculation: Release strength = 0–10 score combining coil duration, coil tightness, normalized absolute momentum, normalized momentum acceleration in the signal direction, volume / its moving average, candle body / range and range / ATR, Bollinger width / its moving average, higher-timeframe trend alignment, and directional RSI agreement
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Long stop = entry close minus risk; short stop = entry close plus risk; risk = clamp(distance from entry to beyond the coil low/high by an ATR buffer, minimum ATR risk, maximum ATR risk)
Calculation: Long targets = entry close plus risk times each configured R multiple; short targets = entry close minus risk times each configured R multiple
Long: squeeze release AND prior coil duration meets the minimum AND (strict expansion mode is off OR Bollinger Bands are entirely outside Keltner Channels) AND release strength meets the minimum (and the strong-signal threshold when that filter is enabled) AND momentum > 0 OR (momentum = 0 AND prior momentum >= 0) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe value is unavailable OR close > higher-timeframe trend) AND cooldown has elapsed AND no trade is active AND ATR is available
Short: squeeze release AND prior coil duration meets the minimum AND (strict expansion mode is off OR Bollinger Bands are entirely outside Keltner Channels) AND release strength meets the minimum (and the strong-signal threshold when that filter is enabled) AND momentum < 0 OR (momentum = 0 AND prior momentum < 0) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe value is unavailable OR close < higher-timeframe trend) AND cooldown has elapsed AND no trade is active AND ATR is available
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long third target; if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, exit at the stop; if the stop is touched after an earlier target, exit at the highest target reached
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short third target; if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, exit at the stop; if the stop is touched after an earlier target, exit at the highest target reached
