Calculation: Bollinger bands = SMA of close plus or minus a parameter times the standard deviation of close. Keltner channel = SMA of close plus or minus a parameter times the SMA of either true range or high minus low; use its own lookback.
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger lower band > Keltner lower band AND Bollinger upper band < Keltner upper band. Expansion = Bollinger lower band < Keltner lower band AND Bollinger upper band > Keltner upper band. Coil length, high, low and tightness are respectively the number of consecutive compressed bars, their highest high, their lowest low, and the minimum ratio of Bollinger half-width to Keltner half-width; use a ratio of 1 when Keltner half-width is not positive.
Calculation: Momentum = the current fitted value of a linear regression, over the Keltner lookback, of close minus the average of the Keltner center and the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over that same lookback. Direction is long if momentum > 0, or if momentum = 0 and previous momentum with missing values replaced by 0 >= 0; otherwise direction is short.
Calculation: Trend alignment = true if the optional trend filter is disabled, the higher-timeframe EMA is unavailable, or close is above that EMA for long direction or below it for short direction. The EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Strength score = sum parameterized tier scores for coil length, coil tightness, normalized momentum magnitude, signed normalized momentum change, volume ratio, candle body and range, Bollinger width ratio, trend alignment, and RSI agreement; round to one decimal place and clamp to the score range. Use the completed coil's length and tightness. Normalize momentum magnitude and its direction-signed change by the highest absolute momentum over a separate lookback, using 0 when that maximum is not positive and replacing missing previous momentum with 0. Volume ratio is volume divided by its SMA, or 1 if that SMA is not positive. Body ratio is absolute close minus open divided by high minus low, or 0.5 if the range is not positive. Range ratio is high minus low divided by ATR, or 0 if ATR is not positive. Bollinger width ratio is current Bollinger width divided by its SMA, or 1 if that SMA is not positive. RSI agreement means RSI >= an upper threshold for long direction or RSI <= a lower threshold for short direction. Coil length, momentum magnitude, signed momentum change, volume ratio and Bollinger width ratio each use ordered ascending thresholds and corresponding tier scores; tightness uses ordered descending thresholds and corresponding tier scores. The candle score uses a high tier when body ratio and range ratio both exceed their respective high thresholds, a middle tier when either exceeds its respective lower threshold, and a low tier otherwise. Trend alignment and RSI agreement each add their respective score when true.
Calculation: Entry risk = absolute difference between entry price and the completed coil low minus an ATR buffer for a long, or the completed coil high plus an ATR buffer for a short, floored and capped by respective ATR multiples at entry. Entry price is the signal bar close. Stop = entry price minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short. Final target = entry price plus a target multiple of risk for a long or minus it for a short. Keep these levels fixed for the trade.
Long: Previous bar compressed AND current bar not compressed AND completed coil length >= minimum AND (strict expansion filter disabled OR current bar expanded) AND direction = long AND strength score >= minimum AND (strong-only filter disabled OR strength score >= strong threshold) AND trend alignment = true AND (cooldown disabled OR no previous signal OR bars since previous signal >= cooldown) AND position side = flat AND ATR available.
Short: Previous bar compressed AND current bar not compressed AND completed coil length >= minimum AND (strict expansion filter disabled OR current bar expanded) AND direction = short AND strength score >= minimum AND (strong-only filter disabled OR strength score >= strong threshold) AND trend alignment = true AND (cooldown disabled OR no previous signal OR bars since previous signal >= cooldown) AND position side = flat AND ATR available.
Exit: long, bar after entry or later AND (low <= stop OR high >= final target).
Exit: short, bar after entry or later AND (high >= stop OR low <= final target).
