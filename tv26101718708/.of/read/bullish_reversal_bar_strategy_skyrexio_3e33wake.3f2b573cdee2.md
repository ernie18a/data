Calculation: Alligator lines = smoothed moving averages of (high + low) / 2, initialized with a simple moving average and thereafter updated as (previous average × (length − 1) + current median price) / length; use longer, intermediate, and shorter lengths with respectively longer, intermediate, and shorter backward offsets.
Calculation: Squat bar = (high − low) / volume is less than on the preceding bar AND volume is greater than on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Fractal trend = retain the most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed by the specified number of bars on both sides. Retain separate upward and downward activation levels and initialize trend to neutral. On each bar, first detect whether high crosses above the upward activation level or low crosses below the downward activation level, comparing each with its preceding-bar value. Then, in order: if the latest pivot high is above the Alligator's intermediate line, set the upward activation level to that pivot; on an upward cross, set trend to up, clear the upward activation level, and set the downward activation level to the latest pivot low; if the latest pivot low is below the intermediate line, set the downward activation level to that pivot; on a downward cross, set trend to down, clear the downward activation level, and set the upward activation level to the latest pivot high; finally, clear the upward activation level while trend is up and clear the downward activation level while trend is down.
Calculation: Reversal bar = close > (high + low) / 2 AND low equals the lowest low over the lookback including the current bar AND high is below each Alligator line AND, if the optional Awesome Oscillator filter is enabled, the shorter-period simple moving average of median price minus the longer-period simple moving average of median price is below its preceding-bar value AND, if the optional squat filter is enabled, at least one of the current and two preceding bars is a squat bar.
Calculation: Pending entry = a reversal bar within the configured trading period places or replaces a long stop order at its high plus one minimum price increment; the order remains pending until filled or replaced. The latest reversal bar's low is retained as the invalidation level, including for reversal bars outside the trading period.
Calculation: Stop level = on a new long position, set it to the latest retained invalidation level; clear it when fractal trend changes from down to up or when no position is open.
Long: A pending entry exists AND price reaches or exceeds its stop price.
Exit: long, price reaches or falls below the stop level.
Exit: long, the stop level is cleared AND fractal trend changes from up to down.
