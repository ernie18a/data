Calculation: Active macro segment and channel = connect consecutive confirmed alternating strict swing highs and lows; a swing is confirmed after a volatility-adjusted lookback, 18 bars divided by the short-term ATR / long-baseline ATR ratio clamped to 0.70–1.60. Extend an up segment to a later confirmed higher high and a down segment to a later confirmed lower low. Skip segments shorter than the minimum bar length or price move smaller than the minimum ATR multiple. The basis is the line between its pivot prices; upper and lower widths are the configured percentile of their respective high-above-basis and basis-above-low deviations, multiplied by the width factor and floored at the minimum ATR width. Active direction is up after a low pivot and down after a high pivot.
Calculation: Micro direction and fit = recency-weighted linear regression of close over the shorter lookback, weighting each bar by decay^age, with the newest bar at age zero. Normalize its slope by ATR and convert to degrees; direction is up above 3 degrees, down below -3 degrees, otherwise flat. Fit quality is weighted R²; the micro channel is visible when R² meets its minimum.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = active macro direction on each of two automatically selected higher timeframes, using the last completed bar on each.
Calculation: Side score = alignment points (35 if macro and micro directions both match the side, otherwise 0) + channel-position points (20 × favorable position fraction, clipped to 0–20; for long use 100 minus close's percentage position between active channel bounds, for short use that percentage) + fit points (20 × micro R² clipped to 0–1 when the micro channel is visible, otherwise 0) + 12.5 for each higher-timeframe direction matching the side. If strict MTF is enabled and both higher timeframes do not agree, cap the score below the entry threshold.
Long: Confirmed bar AND active macro direction is up AND (long score crosses above the entry threshold AND entry cooldown has elapsed OR close crosses above the micro upper band AND micro channel is visible AND bars since active segment start are 3–25 AND long score meets the minimum signal score AND continuation cooldown has elapsed OR close first enters the range from active lower band through active lower band + 0.2 ATR AND active macro direction is up AND long score meets the minimum signal score AND pullback cooldown has elapsed OR close crosses above active upper band AND active macro direction is down AND long score meets the minimum signal score AND break cooldown has elapsed).
Short: Confirmed bar AND active macro direction is down AND (short score crosses above the entry threshold AND entry cooldown has elapsed OR close crosses below the micro lower band AND micro channel is visible AND bars since active segment start are 3–25 AND short score meets the minimum signal score AND continuation cooldown has elapsed OR close first enters the range from active upper band − 0.2 ATR through active upper band AND active macro direction is down AND short score meets the minimum signal score AND pullback cooldown has elapsed OR close crosses below active lower band AND active macro direction is up AND short score meets the minimum signal score AND break cooldown has elapsed).
