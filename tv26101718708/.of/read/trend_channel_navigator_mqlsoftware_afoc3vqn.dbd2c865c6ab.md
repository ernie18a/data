Calculation: Confirmed pivot = On each bar choose a fixed lookback or round a base lookback divided by the clamped ratio of short-term ATR to longer-term ATR. The candidate is that many bars back; a high pivot has a high strictly above all older highs and at least as high as all newer highs in the equally sized windows on either side; reverse the inequalities for a low pivot. Accept only pivots newer than the last accepted anchor. Process highs before lows. Initialize the anchor with the first pivot. Replace a same-type anchor only with a more extreme pivot. Accept an opposite-type pivot only if its distance from the anchor meets minimum bar and current-ATR-multiple thresholds; otherwise retain the anchor.
Calculation: Active macro direction = Up after an accepted low pivot and down after an accepted high pivot. Its age is the number of bars since that pivot.
Calculation: Active macro channel = Draw a basis from the accepted pivot to the previous bar's close, or optionally the current close, and extend it to the current bar. Sample highs and lows from the pivot through the selected endpoint at an evenly spaced stride that limits the sample count. Separately take a linearly interpolated percentile of max(high minus basis, zero) and max(basis minus low, zero). The upper and lower bands are the current extended basis plus and minus, respectively, the larger of the corresponding percentile times a width multiplier and current ATR times an ATR-floor multiplier. If the endpoint is not later than the pivot, use a horizontal basis and zero deviations before applying the floor.
Calculation: Micro channel = Fit closes over a lookback ending at the previous bar, or optionally the current bar, by weighted least squares with weight equal to a decay raised to bar age. The basis is the fitted value at the newest observation; the upper and lower bands are the basis plus and minus a multiplier of the weighted root-mean-square residual. Fit quality is weighted correlation squared. Micro direction is up or down when the arctangent of chronological regression slope divided by current ATR exceeds a positive or negative angle threshold, respectively; otherwise it is flat.
Calculation: Side score = With no active macro channel, zero. Otherwise sum a fixed alignment weight when macro and micro directions both match the side; a channel-position weight multiplied by the clamped favorable-edge proximity, where position is (close minus lower band) divided by (upper band minus lower band), proximity is one minus position for long and position for short, and an unavailable position contributes zero; a fit weight multiplied by clamped micro fit quality when fit quality meets its minimum, otherwise zero; and an equal agreement weight for each of two progressively higher timeframes whose active macro direction matches the side. Calculate each higher-timeframe direction from its last completed bar. Optionally cap the score below the entry threshold unless both higher timeframes agree; clamp the result to its allowed range.
Calculation: Trend bias = For a limited number of most recent accepted completed segments and the active segment, sum up and down weights separately, each weight being segment age in bars times the greater of its absolute ATR-normalized slope angle and an angle floor. A completed segment connects accepted opposite pivots; extend its endpoint and recompute its angle when a more extreme same-type pivot replaces that endpoint. Divide up weight by total weight. Compute net move in current ATR units from the oldest included segment's start price to current close. Classify bullish or bearish using the ordered comparisons of net move and up-weight ratio against positive and negative strong-move, ordinary-move, strong-ratio, ordinary-ratio and mixed-ratio thresholds; the strong positive move test requires the positive strong-move threshold and a minimum up ratio, the strong negative test requires the negative strong-move threshold and a maximum up ratio, and the ordinary move tests similarly require their respective ratio bounds.
Long: Active macro direction is up.
Short: Active macro direction is down.
Long: Micro direction is up AND micro fit quality is at least its minimum.
Short: Micro direction is down AND micro fit quality is at least its minimum.
Long: Trend bias is bullish or strongly bullish.
Short: Trend bias is bearish or strongly bearish.
Long: Active macro direction on either higher timeframe is up, using its last completed bar.
Short: Active macro direction on either higher timeframe is down, using its last completed bar.
Long: Active channel position is at or above its upper threshold.
Short: Active channel position is at or below its lower threshold.
Long: Long side score is greater than or equal to short side score AND an active macro channel exists.
Short: Short side score is greater than long side score AND an active macro channel exists.
Long: Active macro direction is down AND close crosses above the active macro upper band.
Short: Active macro direction is up AND close crosses below the active macro lower band.
Long: Active macro direction is up AND close is at or above the active macro lower band AND at or below that band plus an ATR multiple AND the same combined condition was false on the previous bar.
Short: Active macro direction is down AND close is at or below the active macro upper band AND at or above that band minus an ATR multiple AND the same combined condition was false on the previous bar.
Long: Active macro direction is up AND close crosses above the micro upper band AND micro fit quality is at least its minimum AND macro segment age is between its minimum and maximum continuation ages inclusive.
Short: Active macro direction is down AND close crosses below the micro lower band AND micro fit quality is at least its minimum AND macro segment age is between its minimum and maximum continuation ages inclusive.
Long: Active macro direction is up AND long side score crosses above the entry threshold AND bars since the last entry marker of either side are at least the entry cooldown.
Short: Active macro direction is down AND short side score crosses above the entry threshold AND bars since the last entry marker of either side are at least the entry cooldown.
