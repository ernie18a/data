Calculation: Opening range = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar at the end of the configured opening-range session; reset at the start of each opening-range session.
Calculation: Breakout levels = opening-range high plus the confirmed breakout buffer and opening-range low minus the confirmed breakout buffer; buffers are configurable price distances.
Calculation: Body strength = 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Trend EMA and trend close = EMA of close and close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; previous trend EMA = EMA of close of the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Stochastic K = SMA of raw stochastic %K; stochastic D = SMA of stochastic K, with independently configurable smoothing periods.
Calculation: Long trend filter = trend filter disabled OR trend close > trend EMA AND (slope filter disabled OR trend EMA > previous trend EMA).
Calculation: Short trend filter = trend filter disabled OR trend close < trend EMA AND (slope filter disabled OR trend EMA < previous trend EMA).
Calculation: Long stochastic filter = stochastic filter disabled OR stochastic K > stochastic D AND stochastic K > stochastic midpoint.
Calculation: Short stochastic filter = stochastic filter disabled OR stochastic K < stochastic D AND stochastic K < stochastic midpoint.
Calculation: Long breakout = opening range available AND current bar in entry session AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-session filter disabled OR no trade opened since opening-range session began) AND close > upper breakout level AND previous close <= upper breakout level AND close > open AND body strength >= minimum body strength AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND long trend filter.
Calculation: Short breakout = opening range available AND current bar in entry session AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-session filter disabled OR no trade opened since opening-range session began) AND close < lower breakout level AND previous close >= lower breakout level AND close < open AND body strength >= minimum body strength AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND short trend filter.
Calculation: Long confirmation = long breakout on the current closed bar when second-close confirmation is disabled OR long breakout on the previous bar AND current close > upper breakout level AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND long trend filter when second-close confirmation is enabled.
Calculation: Short confirmation = short breakout on the current closed bar when second-close confirmation is disabled OR short breakout on the previous bar AND current close < lower breakout level AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND short trend filter when second-close confirmation is enabled.
Calculation: Long retest armed = a long confirmation occurred on an earlier bar with retest enabled AND no more than the configured maximum retest bars have elapsed AND no subsequent close, including the current close, was below the opening-range high AND no subsequent short breakout occurred; reset at the next opening-range session or after a long retest signal.
Calculation: Short retest armed = a short confirmation occurred on an earlier bar with retest enabled AND no more than the configured maximum retest bars have elapsed AND no subsequent close, including the current close, was above the opening-range low AND no subsequent long breakout occurred; reset at the next opening-range session or after a short retest signal.
Long: Possible alerts enabled AND opening range available AND current bar in entry session AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-session filter disabled OR no trade opened since opening-range session began) AND no previous possible long alert since that session began AND high >= opening-range high + possible-alert buffer AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND long trend filter.
Short: Possible alerts enabled AND opening range available AND current bar in entry session AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-session filter disabled OR no trade opened since opening-range session began) AND no previous possible short alert since that session began AND low <= opening-range low − possible-alert buffer AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND short trend filter.
Long: Current bar closed AND opening range available AND current bar in entry session AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-session filter disabled OR no trade opened since opening-range session began) AND long stochastic filter AND (retest disabled AND long confirmation OR retest enabled AND long retest armed AND low <= upper breakout level AND close > upper breakout level AND close > open AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND long trend filter).
Short: Current bar closed AND opening range available AND current bar in entry session AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-session filter disabled OR no trade opened since opening-range session began) AND short stochastic filter AND (retest disabled AND short confirmation OR retest enabled AND short retest armed AND high >= lower breakout level AND close < lower breakout level AND close < open AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND short trend filter).
Long: Trend filter enabled AND trend close > trend EMA.
Short: Trend filter enabled AND trend close < trend EMA.
Long: Stochastic filter enabled AND stochastic K > stochastic D AND stochastic K > stochastic midpoint.
Short: Stochastic filter enabled AND stochastic K < stochastic D AND stochastic K < stochastic midpoint.
Calculation: Long trade = enter at signal-bar close only if close − (opening-range low − stop buffer) >= minimum price increment; freeze stop at opening-range low − stop buffer and target at entry price + reward-to-risk multiplier × (entry price − stop).
Calculation: Short trade = enter at signal-bar close only if (opening-range high + stop buffer) − close >= minimum price increment; freeze stop at opening-range high + stop buffer and target at entry price − reward-to-risk multiplier × (stop − entry price).
Exit: long, bar is later than entry bar AND management session has just ended; exit at previous close.
Exit: short, bar is later than entry bar AND management session has just ended; exit at previous close.
Exit: long, bar is later than entry bar AND management session has not just ended AND (low <= frozen stop OR high >= frozen target); exit at stop if both are hit, otherwise at the level hit.
Exit: short, bar is later than entry bar AND management session has not just ended AND (high >= frozen stop OR low <= frozen target); exit at stop if both are hit, otherwise at the level hit.
