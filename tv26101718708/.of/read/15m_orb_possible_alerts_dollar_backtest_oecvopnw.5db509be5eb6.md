Calculation: Opening-range high/low = high/low of the completed opening-range candle on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; use the last completed candle, not the forming candle.
Calculation: Possible breakout levels = opening-range high plus/minus the possible-alert buffer.
Calculation: Confirmed breakout levels = opening-range high plus the long buffer, and opening-range low minus the short buffer.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low); use 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed bar; EMA slope compares it with the EMA from the preceding completed bar.
Calculation: Long stop = opening-range low minus stop buffer; short stop = opening-range high plus stop buffer. Risk = absolute distance from entry price to stop. Long target = entry price + risk × reward-to-risk ratio; short target = entry price − risk × reward-to-risk ratio.
Long: During the entry session, after the opening range is complete, with no open trade and (if the one-trade-per-day limit is enabled) no trade already taken that day: (high ≥ possible breakout level and long VWAP filter passes and long higher-timeframe trend filter passes) OR (close > confirmed long breakout level and previous close ≤ that level and close > open and candle body percentage ≥ minimum and long VWAP filter passes and long higher-timeframe trend filter passes and the enabled confirmation mode passes). The VWAP filter passes when close > VWAP; the higher-timeframe trend filter passes when the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA and, if slope confirmation is enabled, that EMA > its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar. Confirmation mode: if two-close confirmation is enabled, the next bar must close above the confirmed long breakout level and pass the VWAP and trend filters; otherwise, if retest confirmation is enabled, after a qualifying breakout (or the second close when both options are enabled), within the allowed retest bars and before any close below the opening-range high, a later bar must have low ≤ the confirmed long breakout level, close > that level, close > open, and pass the VWAP, trend, and enabled stochastic filters; otherwise, the breakout bar must pass the enabled stochastic filter. The stochastic filter passes when smoothed K > smoothed D and smoothed K > 50. A possible-alert marking is allowed once per day and requires alerts enabled, high ≥ the possible breakout level, and the long VWAP and trend filters to pass.
Short: During the entry session, after the opening range is complete, with no open trade and (if the one-trade-per-day limit is enabled) no trade already taken that day: (low ≤ possible breakout level and short VWAP filter passes and short higher-timeframe trend filter passes) OR (close < confirmed short breakout level and previous close ≥ that level and close < open and candle body percentage ≥ minimum and short VWAP filter passes and short higher-timeframe trend filter passes and the enabled confirmation mode passes). The VWAP filter passes when close < VWAP; the higher-timeframe trend filter passes when the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA and, if slope confirmation is enabled, that EMA < its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar. Confirmation mode: if two-close confirmation is enabled, the next bar must close below the confirmed short breakout level and pass the VWAP and trend filters; otherwise, if retest confirmation is enabled, after a qualifying breakout (or the second close when both options are enabled), within the allowed retest bars and before any close above the opening-range low, a later bar must have high ≥ the confirmed short breakout level, close < that level, close < open, and pass the VWAP, trend, and enabled stochastic filters; otherwise, the breakout bar must pass the enabled stochastic filter. The stochastic filter passes when smoothed K < smoothed D and smoothed K < 50. A possible-alert marking is allowed once per day and requires alerts enabled, low ≤ the possible breakout level, and the short VWAP and trend filters to pass.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR the management session ends.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR the management session ends. If stop and target are both reached on one bar, exit at the stop.
