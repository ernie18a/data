Calculation: RSI = RSI of close.
Calculation: Bull and bear price sources = respectively low and high in wick mode, or close for both in close mode.
Calculation: Bull and bear references = independent stored price, RSI, and bar index. Before evaluating each bar, clear the bull reference if RSI > bull reset level or its age > maximum lookback; clear the bear reference if RSI < bear reset level or its age > maximum lookback. Evaluate all decisions against the remaining references before updating either reference. After evaluating decisions, replace each side's reference with its current price source, RSI, and bar index if that side's divergence signal occurred; otherwise replace it if that side's reference-point condition occurred.
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = cumulative volume-weighted mean of (high + low) / 2 since the start of the calendar day, plus or minus the respective band multiplier times the square root of max(volume-weighted mean of ((high + low) / 2)^2 minus the squared mean, 0). The bands are undefined when cumulative volume is zero.
Long: RSI < oversold level AND (bull reference is undefined OR RSI < bull reference RSI).
Short: RSI > overbought level AND (bear reference is undefined OR RSI > bear reference RSI).
Long: Bull reference exists AND bull price source < bull reference price AND (bull reference price / bull price source - 1) * 100 >= minimum price extension AND RSI - bull reference RSI >= minimum RSI difference AND (RSI turn filter disabled OR RSI >= previous bar RSI) AND (candle direction filter disabled OR close > open) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR (wick validation selected AND low <= lower VWAP deviation band) OR (close validation selected AND close <= lower VWAP deviation band)) AND (no previous long divergence signal OR bars since previous long divergence signal > cooldown) AND (close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is closed).
Short: Bear reference exists AND bear price source > bear reference price AND (bear price source / bear reference price - 1) * 100 >= minimum price extension AND bear reference RSI - RSI >= minimum RSI difference AND (RSI turn filter disabled OR RSI <= previous bar RSI) AND (candle direction filter disabled OR close < open) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR (wick validation selected AND high >= upper VWAP deviation band) OR (close validation selected AND close >= upper VWAP deviation band)) AND (no previous short divergence signal OR bars since previous short divergence signal > cooldown) AND (close confirmation disabled OR chart bar is closed).
