Calculation: Previous Asia session high/low = the highest high and lowest low of the immediately preceding Asia session
Calculation: 8:30 news high/low = the high and low of the 8:30 ET bar
Calculation: Bullish/bearish vote counts = count of bullish/bearish votes from the Asia range, VWAP, and news-level conditions; the Asia range and news-level conditions may be neutral
Long: close > previous Asia session high
Long: close > session VWAP
Long: absolute distance from close to 8:30 news low <= an ATR-scaled threshold
Long: bullish vote count > bearish vote count
Short: close < previous Asia session low
Short: close < session VWAP
Short: absolute distance from close to 8:30 news high <= an ATR-scaled threshold
Short: bearish vote count > bullish vote count
