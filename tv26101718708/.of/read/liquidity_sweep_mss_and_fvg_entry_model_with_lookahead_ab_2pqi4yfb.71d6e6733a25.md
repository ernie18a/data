Calculation: Active buy-side pool = nearest eligible level above close among the previous completed higher-timeframe daily high and confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs; swing highs use left and right pivot lookbacks and are available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Active sell-side pool = nearest eligible level below close among the previous completed higher-timeframe daily low and confirmed chart-timeframe swing lows; swing lows use left and right pivot lookbacks and are available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = the interval between high two bars ago and current low when high two bars ago < current low; bearish fair value gap = the interval between current high and low two bars ago when low two bars ago > current high.
Calculation: Displacement = absolute candle body >= ATR × configured multiplier.
Long: In an enabled kill zone, with daily loss below the configured limit and outside the flatten window, price trades below the active sell-side pool and closes above it; within the configured sweep-to-MSS bar limit, a displacement candle closes above the latest confirmed swing high; within the configured MSS-to-gap bar limit, a bullish fair value gap meets the configured minimum width and its bottom is at or above the sweep extreme; place a limit at the configured proximal edge, distal edge, or midpoint of that gap while the order remains valid, and enter when price reaches it.
Short: In an enabled kill zone, with daily loss below the configured limit and outside the flatten window, price trades above the active buy-side pool and closes below it; within the configured sweep-to-MSS bar limit, a displacement candle closes below the latest confirmed swing low; within the configured MSS-to-gap bar limit, a bearish fair value gap meets the configured minimum width and its top is at or below the sweep extreme; place a limit at the configured proximal edge, distal edge, or midpoint of that gap while the order remains valid, and enter when price reaches it.
Exit: Long, low <= sweep extreme − ATR × configured stop buffer OR high >= opposing liquidity target (when eligible) OR high >= entry price + configured R multiple × risk distance (when using R target or no opposing pool qualifies) OR time is within the configured flatten window.
Exit: Short, high >= sweep extreme + ATR × configured stop buffer OR low <= opposing liquidity target (when eligible) OR low <= entry price − configured R multiple × risk distance (when using R target or no opposing pool qualifies) OR time is within the configured flatten window.
