Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = the most recent chart-bar pivot high or low confirmed by the specified numbers of bars on its left and right; retain the preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind.
Calculation: Upper pool = the nearest enabled level strictly above the current close among the higher-timeframe high and confirmed swing high. Lower pool = the nearest enabled level strictly below the current close among the higher-timeframe low and confirmed swing low. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, or optionally the final high and low of the higher-timeframe bar still forming, including its future values on historical bars. If the equal-swing filter is enabled, exclude higher-timeframe levels and include a swing only when its distance from the preceding same-kind swing is at most the tolerance.
Calculation: Sweep setup = while flat and idle, within an enabled setup time window if required and outside the flatten window if enabled, select exactly one of: high > upper pool and upper pool differs from the last used upper pool; or low < lower pool and lower pool differs from the last used lower pool. If close-back confirmation is enabled, also require close < upper pool for the first case or close > lower pool for the second. Record the selected pool, the opposite pool at that bar, and the sweep bar's high for an upper-pool sweep or low for a lower-pool sweep. Mark the selected pool as used even if the setup later expires.
Calculation: Structure shift = on a later bar within the sweep-to-shift limit, absolute close minus open >= ATR multiplied by the displacement multiplier AND, after a lower-pool sweep, close > confirmed swing high, or after an upper-pool sweep, close < confirmed swing low. Through this bar, update the sweep extreme to the lowest low after a lower-pool sweep or the highest high after an upper-pool sweep.
Calculation: Gap = on a later bar within the shift-to-gap limit, after a lower-pool sweep, high two bars ago < current low AND current low minus high two bars ago >= minimum gap size AND high two bars ago >= sweep extreme; or after an upper-pool sweep, low two bars ago > current high AND low two bars ago minus current high >= minimum gap size AND low two bars ago <= sweep extreme. The gap boundaries are the two prices in the applicable inequality.
Calculation: Limit price = the selected proximal edge, distal edge, or midpoint of the gap; the proximal edge is the upper boundary for a long and the lower boundary for a short. Starting on the bar after the gap, while flat and until the order expires, submit or update the limit order and bracket on each bar. The long stop is sweep extreme minus current ATR multiplied by the stop buffer; the short stop is sweep extreme plus that buffer. Risk is the absolute difference between limit price and stop. The target is the opposite pool recorded at the sweep if pool targeting is selected and that pool is beyond the limit price in the trade direction; otherwise it is the limit price plus risk multiplied by the target multiplier for a long, or minus that amount for a short. Freeze the latest submitted stop and target on fill. Expire each phase when its elapsed bars exceed its respective limit, cancel an expired order, and reset the setup when a position is open.
Long: A lower-pool sweep setup occurs.
Short: An upper-pool sweep setup occurs.
Long: A lower-pool sweep setup has a structure shift.
Short: An upper-pool sweep setup has a structure shift.
Long: A lower-pool sweep setup with a structure shift has a gap.
Short: An upper-pool sweep setup with a structure shift has a gap.
Long: A lower-pool sweep setup has a valid gap AND risk > 0 AND price reaches the active long limit order before expiry.
Short: An upper-pool sweep setup has a valid gap AND risk > 0 AND price reaches the active short limit order before expiry.
Exit: Long, price reaches the frozen stop OR price reaches the frozen target OR the bar is in the enabled flatten time window.
Exit: Short, price reaches the frozen stop OR price reaches the frozen target OR the bar is in the enabled flatten time window.
