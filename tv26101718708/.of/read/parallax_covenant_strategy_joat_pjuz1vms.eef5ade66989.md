Calculation: Composite baseline = mean of the Donchian midpoints of three lookbacks: short, intermediate, and long
Calculation: Regime state = retain the prior state unless a completed bar closes above both the composite baseline and the upper regime boundary, setting state bullish, or below both the baseline and lower boundary, setting state bearish; boundaries are the composite baseline ± the average candle body over a lookback times a tolerance multiplier, clipped to a band around the SMA of bar midpoints whose offset is average bar range times a scale
Calculation: Pressure composite = EMA of (55% × normalized rolling candle-body pressure + 45% × channel position); rolling pressure sums candle direction (up = +1, down = −1, unchanged = 0) times absolute body divided by average absolute body, capped at a weight limit, then normalizes by the lookback and cap to percent; channel position maps close within a channel centered on the SMA of bar midpoints, with offset based on average range and average body
Calculation: Mapped midpoint = lowest low over a lookback + EMA(RSI(close)) × ((highest high − lowest low) / 100 × mapping stretch)
Calculation: Mapped slope = EMA of the mapped midpoint's one-bar change
Calculation: Bullish mapping = close above mapped midpoint AND mapped slope nonnegative; bearish mapping = close below mapped midpoint AND mapped slope nonpositive
Calculation: Bullish pressure = pressure composite above its threshold AND above its signal SMA; bearish pressure = pressure composite below the negative threshold AND below its signal SMA
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with the EMA from that same completed bar; bullish if above, bearish if below, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Demand zone = latest up candle with volume above average volume times an impulse multiplier; zone top is its high and bottom is the greater of its low or close minus ATR times a zone-height multiplier. Supply zone = latest down candle meeting the same volume condition; zone bottom is its low and top is the lesser of its high or close plus ATR times the multiplier. Each zone is active for a limited number of bars after formation
Calculation: Bullish structure = active confirmed swing support with close above it OR close within the active demand zone; bearish structure = active confirmed swing resistance with close below it OR close within the active supply zone
Calculation: Wave direction = sign of the sum of per-bar close direction over a lookback (or the current bar's close direction in directional mode); retain the previous nonzero direction when the result is zero. Wave strength = accumulated candle size during the current wave (absolute body, optionally plus half the bar range) divided by its moving average. Bullish/bearish wave = corresponding direction AND strength at least 1
Calculation: Compression = average bar range divided by its moving average is at or below a threshold; release = prior bar compressed AND current bar not compressed AND wave is bullish/bearish
Long: completed bar AND long entries enabled AND position flat or short AND regime state nonbearish AND at least two of bullish pressure, bullish mapping, bullish structure, and bullish wave AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias nonbearish) AND (release required ? (bullish release OR bullish wave) : (bullish wave AND not compressed)) AND (demand retest required ? close within active demand zone : (bullish structure OR close above composite baseline)) AND (pressure composite crosses above its signal SMA OR close crosses above mapped midpoint OR close crosses above composite baseline)
Short: completed bar AND short entries enabled AND position flat or long AND regime state nonbullish AND at least two of bearish pressure, bearish mapping, bearish structure, and bearish wave AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias nonbullish) AND (release required ? (bearish release OR bearish wave) : (bearish wave AND not compressed)) AND (supply retest required ? close within active supply zone : (bearish structure OR close below composite baseline)) AND (pressure composite crosses below its signal SMA OR close crosses below mapped midpoint OR close crosses below composite baseline)
Exit: long, low at or below the effective stop OR high at or above the target; initial stop = entry price − ATR × stop multiple, target = entry price + initial risk × reward/risk multiple; after high reaches entry price + initial risk × trail activation multiple, trail is the greatest of prior trail and close − ATR × trail multiple, and effective stop is the greater of initial stop and trail
Exit: short, high at or above the effective stop OR low at or below the target; initial stop = entry price + ATR × stop multiple, target = entry price − initial risk × reward/risk multiple; after low reaches entry price − initial risk × trail activation multiple, trail is the least of prior trail and close + ATR × trail multiple, and effective stop is the lesser of initial stop and trail
Exit: long, regime-failure exit enabled AND completed bar AND (regime state bearish OR pressure composite below zero OR bearish mapping)
Exit: short, regime-failure exit enabled AND completed bar AND (regime state bullish OR pressure composite above zero OR bullish mapping)
Exit: long or short, bars since entry at or above the maximum bars in trade
