Calculation: Chandelier Exit direction = set long when close > prior short stop, set short when close < prior long stop, otherwise retain prior direction; prior long stop is the previous bar’s trailing max of (highest high over the ATR lookback − ATR multiplier × ATR), and prior short stop is the previous bar’s trailing min of (lowest low over the ATR lookback + ATR multiplier × ATR). Each trailing stop starts at its raw value and ratchets only while the previous close remains beyond its previous stop.
Long: (Chandelier Exit direction changes from short to long AND chart-timeframe EMA > higher-timeframe EMA (same EMA lookback; higher-timeframe EMA uses the still-forming bar) AND close > chart-timeframe EMA) OR (chart-timeframe EMA crosses above higher-timeframe EMA AND Chandelier Exit direction is long AND close > chart-timeframe EMA) OR (close crosses above chart-timeframe EMA AND Chandelier Exit direction is long AND chart-timeframe EMA > higher-timeframe EMA)
Short: (Chandelier Exit direction changes from long to short AND chart-timeframe EMA < higher-timeframe EMA (same EMA lookback; higher-timeframe EMA uses the still-forming bar) AND close < chart-timeframe EMA) OR (chart-timeframe EMA crosses below higher-timeframe EMA AND Chandelier Exit direction is short AND close < chart-timeframe EMA) OR (close crosses below chart-timeframe EMA AND Chandelier Exit direction is short AND chart-timeframe EMA < higher-timeframe EMA)
