Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a lookback.
Calculation: Range ratio = current high-low range / simple moving average of high-low range over a lookback.
Calculation: Body ratio = absolute(close - open) / (high - low).
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low).
Calculation: Bullish spike = relative volume >= its threshold AND range ratio >= its threshold AND body ratio >= its threshold AND close > open AND close location >= 0.60.
Calculation: Bearish spike = relative volume >= its threshold AND range ratio >= its threshold AND body ratio >= its threshold AND close < open AND close location <= 0.40.
Calculation: New spike = bullish or bearish spike now AND neither was true on the previous bar. The newest spike replaces the active context; it expires when its age exceeds the maximum age.
Calculation: Spike ATR = ATR on the spike bar. Spike depth = minimum of 0.38 × spike range and Spike ATR × band-depth parameter. For a bullish spike, hold rail = close - depth + Spike ATR × hold-buffer parameter, absorb rail = close - Spike ATR × absorption-buffer parameter, failure rail = low - Spike ATR × failure-buffer parameter, and room rail = maximum of high + Spike ATR × target-room parameter and the highest high over a lookback excluding the spike bar. For a bearish spike, hold rail = close + depth - Spike ATR × hold-buffer parameter, absorb rail = close + Spike ATR × absorption-buffer parameter, failure rail = high + Spike ATR × failure-buffer parameter, and room rail = minimum of low - Spike ATR × target-room parameter and the lowest low over a lookback excluding the spike bar.
Calculation: Holding = active, unexpired, not failed, and (bullish context with close >= hold rail OR bearish context with close <= hold rail). Absorbed becomes true when bullish-context close < absorb rail or bearish-context close > absorb rail. Failed becomes true when bullish-context close < failure rail or bearish-context close > failure rail. Room reached becomes true when bullish-context high >= room rail or bearish-context low <= room rail.
Calculation: Aftershock score = rounded value clamped to 0–100 of volume score + range score + body score + hold score + participation-decay score + room score - age penalty - absorption penalty. Volume score = clamp(spike relative volume / relative-volume threshold × 28, 0, 28); range score = clamp(spike range ratio / range-ratio threshold × 20, 0, 20); body score = clamp(spike body ratio / body-ratio threshold × 16, 0, 16); hold score = bullish: clamp((close - hold rail) / max(spike high - hold rail, minimum price increment) × 18, 0, 18), bearish: clamp((hold rail - close) / max(hold rail - spike low, minimum price increment) × 18, 0, 18); decay ratio = current relative volume / spike relative volume; decay score = clamp((1 - abs(decay ratio - 0.55) / 0.55) × 12, 0, 12); room score = bullish: clamp((room rail - close) / ATR × 3, 0, 12), bearish: clamp((close - room rail) / ATR × 3, 0, 12); age penalty = clamp(spike age / maximum age × 8, 0, 8); absorption penalty = 12 if absorbed, otherwise 0. Clamp means limiting the value to the stated range.
Calculation: Ready = active, unexpired, not failed, holding, not absorbed, room not reached, and aftershock score >= ready-score threshold.
Long: New bullish spike OR (bullish context AND holding becomes true) OR (bullish context AND ready becomes true).
Short: New bearish spike OR (bearish context AND holding becomes true) OR (bearish context AND ready becomes true).
Exit: Long, (bullish context AND absorbed becomes true) OR (bullish context AND failed becomes true).
Exit: Short, (bearish context AND absorbed becomes true) OR (bearish context AND failed becomes true).
