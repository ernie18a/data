Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume); use 1 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Range ratio = max(high - low, minimum price increment) / SMA(max(high - low, minimum price increment)); use 1 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Bull spike = relative volume >= minimum relative volume AND range ratio >= minimum range ratio AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= minimum body ratio AND close > open AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= 0.60.
Calculation: Bear spike = relative volume >= minimum relative volume AND range ratio >= minimum range ratio AND abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= minimum body ratio AND close < open AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) <= 0.40.
Calculation: New spike = a bull spike or bear spike on the current bar AND neither kind of spike on the previous bar. Each new spike replaces the active spike and resets its absorbed, failed, and target-reached flags. Store its direction, bar index, close, high, low, and ATR, using true range if ATR is unavailable and flooring the result at the minimum price increment. Spike depth = min(0.38 * spike range, spike ATR * band-depth multiplier). For a bull spike, hold rail = spike close - spike depth + spike ATR * hold-buffer multiplier, absorption rail = spike close - spike ATR * absorption-buffer multiplier, failure rail = spike low - spike ATR * failure-buffer multiplier, and target rail = max(spike high + spike ATR * target multiplier, highest high over the preceding room lookback). For a bear spike, hold rail = spike close + spike depth - spike ATR * hold-buffer multiplier, absorption rail = spike close + spike ATR * absorption-buffer multiplier, failure rail = spike high + spike ATR * failure-buffer multiplier, and target rail = min(spike low - spike ATR * target multiplier, lowest low over the preceding room lookback).
Calculation: Active spike state = the latest spike is unexpired while its age in bars <= maximum spike age. Before updating the current bar's flags, holding is true when the spike is unexpired, failure was not previously flagged, and close >= the bull hold rail or close <= the bear hold rail. On each unexpired bar before failure, permanently flag absorption when close < the bull absorption rail or close > the bear absorption rail; flag failure when close < the bull failure rail or close > the bear failure rail; and flag target reached when high >= the bull target rail or low <= the bear target rail. Update all three flags on the same bar before evaluating the decisions.
Long: Bull spike AND neither a bull spike nor a bear spike on the previous bar.
Short: Bear spike AND neither a bull spike nor a bear spike on the previous bar.
Long: Active spike direction is bullish AND holding is true AND holding was false on the previous bar.
Short: Active spike direction is bearish AND holding is true AND holding was false on the previous bar.
Long: Active spike direction is bullish AND holding is true AND failure, absorption, and target reached are all unflagged.
Short: Active spike direction is bearish AND holding is true AND failure, absorption, and target reached are all unflagged.
