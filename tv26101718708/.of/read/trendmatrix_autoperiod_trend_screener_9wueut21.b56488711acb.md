Calculation: Source = close or EMA of close, according to the smoothing setting.
Calculation: Regime bands = for the same lookback, calculate the highest and lowest Source, linear-regression endpoint of Source, and ATR. Calculate z = (Source − EMA(Source)) / standard deviation of Source, using a separate extension lookback; use zero when the standard deviation is zero. On a higher timeframe, calculate the percentile rank of an EMA of the standard deviation of close, using a longer rank lookback; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time. Use the midpoint percentile when unavailable. Set the upper width to ATR × upper base multiplier × (1 − upper compression parameter / (1 + exp(−z))) × (1 + volatility percentile adjustment), and the lower width analogously with lower parameters and exp(z). The two raw bands are weighted averages of the highest Source and regression endpoint minus the upper width, and the lowest Source and regression endpoint plus the lower width. The upper band is the maximum of the raw bands and the lower band is their minimum. The centerline is an EMA of their midpoint.
Calculation: Regime state = initially bearish; change to bullish when the selected bullish trigger is true, otherwise change to bearish when the selected bearish trigger is true; otherwise retain the previous state. Bullish and bearish triggers use the same selected execution mode.
Calculation: Trend stop = highest close over the candidate lookback with the greatest absolute Pearson correlation between log(close) and time, multiplied by one minus the trend-stop fraction. Break ties in favor of the shorter candidate lookback. Calculate on the screener timeframe, which can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar on historical bars and its forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Event stop = close at the start of the rolling event window multiplied by one minus the event-stop fraction. Calculate on the screener timeframe, which can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar on historical bars and its forming bar in real time.
Long: Previous regime state is bearish AND the selected bullish trigger is true: Source crosses above the centerline in fast mode, OR Source crossed above the centerline within the configured recent number of bars AND Source > centerline AND the linear-regression endpoint > its previous value in conservative mode, OR Source crosses above the lower band in band-reversal mode.
Short: Previous regime state is bullish AND the selected bearish trigger is true: Source crosses below the centerline in fast mode, OR Source crossed below the centerline within the configured recent number of bars AND Source < centerline AND the linear-regression endpoint < its previous value in conservative mode, OR Source crosses below the upper band in band-reversal mode.
Long: Screener close crosses above the trend stop OR crosses above the event stop.
Exit: long, screener close crosses below or equals the trend stop from above OR crosses below or equals the event stop from above.
