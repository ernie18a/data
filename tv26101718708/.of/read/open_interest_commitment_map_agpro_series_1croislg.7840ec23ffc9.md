Calculation: Participation = EMA(volume, proxy length), where proxy length is approximately half the change-baseline length.
Calculation: Participation change = 100 × (participation / participation at the change lookback − 1); use current participation when the lookback value is unavailable. The change is unavailable when its denominator is zero.
Calculation: Participation z-score = (participation change − EMA(participation change, change-baseline length)) / standard deviation of participation change over the normalization lookback; use participation change when its EMA is unavailable and apply a positive floor to the standard deviation.
Calculation: Price move = (close − close at the price-move lookback) / ATR; use current close when the lookback close is unavailable and true range when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Raw long build = participation z-score > expansion threshold AND participation change > 0 AND price move > positive movement threshold AND shorter EMA of close > longer EMA of close AND close > longer EMA of close.
Calculation: Raw short build = participation z-score > expansion threshold AND participation change > 0 AND price move < negative movement threshold AND shorter EMA of close < longer EMA of close AND close < longer EMA of close.
Calculation: Raw absorption = participation z-score > expansion threshold AND participation change > 0 AND absolute price move <= absorption threshold.
Calculation: Raw unwind = participation z-score < negative contraction threshold AND participation change < 0 AND absolute price move > unwind movement threshold.
Calculation: Active persistence = maximum percentage of bars satisfying each of raw long build, raw short build, raw absorption, and raw unwind over the same persistence window.
Calculation: Commitment score = weighted sum of capped normalized absolute participation z-score, capped normalized absolute participation change, capped normalized absolute price move, active persistence, and volume percentile rank over the normalization lookback; use a neutral percentile rank when unavailable and cap the result to the score range.
Long: Raw long build AND commitment score >= minimum commitment score.
Short: Raw short build AND commitment score >= minimum commitment score.
