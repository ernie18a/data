Calculation: Swing state = Initially up; set to up when high equals the highest high over the swing lookback, then set to down when low equals the lowest low over that lookback. A flip occurs when the state changes. Track the latest confirmed swing high when the previous high was the previous rolling highest high and the current high is lower than the current rolling highest high; confirm swing lows symmetrically. A bullish flip uses the latest confirmed swing low as its pivot, and a bearish flip uses the latest confirmed swing high.
Calculation: Swing-anchored VWAP = VWAP of lows since the latest confirmed swing low when the swing state is up, otherwise VWAP of highs since the latest confirmed swing high. Calculate it with short, longer and longest swing lookbacks.
Calculation: Level proximity = Absolute distance from close to a level is less than a configurable ATR multiple. Levels are current session, week and month VWAPs; the three swing-anchored VWAPs; previous completed higher-timeframe period highs and lows; and available profile point of control and value-area boundaries. The profile is calculated only on the last available chart bar: divide the recent high-low range into equal bins, assign each bar's volume to its typical-price bin, choose the largest-volume bin as point of control, and expand from it toward the larger adjacent bin until the configured volume fraction is included. The point of control is the bin midpoint and the value-area boundaries are the outer bin edges.
Calculation: Regime = Chaos when the percentile rank of exponentially decayed true-range shock counts reaches its threshold and a true-range shock occurred within the shock lookback; a shock is true range above a multiple of its moving average. Otherwise strong trend when ADX and slow efficiency ratio meet their strong thresholds and ADX is rising, or the previous regime was strong trend and both remain above their lower hysteresis thresholds. Otherwise trend when ADX and slow efficiency ratio meet their trend thresholds. Otherwise reversion when the variance ratio is unavailable or below one, ADX is below its trend threshold, and slow efficiency ratio is below its reversion threshold; otherwise neutral. Efficiency ratio is absolute source-price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes.
Calculation: Flow = Sum over all completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar of volume multiplied by ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), using zero for a zero-range bar. If lower-timeframe flow is disabled or unavailable, use chart volume multiplied by (2 × normal CDF of close change divided by the standard deviation of close changes - 1). Cumulative flow is the running sum. Toxicity is the moving average of absolute buy-volume minus sell-volume divided by the moving average of total volume, where buy-volume is volume × clamp(0.5 + 0.5 × flow / volume, 0, 1), with a zero flow-to-volume ratio when volume is zero; sell-volume is the remainder.
Calculation: Flow alignment = Sum of three signs, each +1 above a positive deadzone, -1 below its negative, otherwise zero. The signs use chart bars and two progressively higher timeframes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bars. For each timeframe, candle pressure is volume × ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low), using unit volume when volume is zero or unavailable and zero pressure for zero range. Its score is (pressure - rolling median pressure) / (rolling median absolute deviation from the contemporaneous rolling median × a scale factor), clipped to symmetric bounds; use zero when the deviation is negligible.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = On each of two higher timeframes, using the last completed bar, +1 when close is above its EMA and the EMA is nondecreasing, -1 when close is below its EMA and the EMA is nonincreasing, otherwise zero. Sum the two biases; disabling this calculation makes both zero.
Calculation: Flip score = Sum configured step scores for level grade, pivot extension, relative volume, lower-timeframe cascade and swing velocity. Level grades descend from month VWAP, profile point of control or longest swing VWAP; to week VWAP or profile value-area boundaries; to longer swing VWAP or previous completed shorter-period high or low; to session VWAP. Pivot extension is (session VWAP at pivot - pivot low) / current ATR for a bullish flip and (pivot high - session VWAP at pivot) / current ATR for a bearish flip. Relative volume is current volume / its moving average. Swing velocity is (latest confirmed swing high - latest confirmed swing low) / bars from the earlier relevant pivot to the later relevant pivot / current ATR, capped at a configurable maximum and zero when that bar count is not positive. Cascade counts matching swing flips on two lower timeframes, using the last completed lower-timeframe bar sampled within each chart bar, seen within a configurable number of chart bars. Map the sum to ordered score tiers using configurable thresholds.
Calculation: Hull break = Confirm pivot highs and lows after equal numbers of bars on each side. For each side retain a configurable number of recent pivots, build the upper convex hull of highs or lower convex hull of lows, and extend its last edge. Accept resistance only when its ATR-normalized slope is negative and its magnitude is within configured bounds; accept support only when its slope is positive and within those bounds. Count retained pivots at or after the edge's first point within an ATR-scaled distance of the line. An unbroken resistance breaks once when close exceeds the line plus an ATR buffer, bar range exceeds an ATR multiple, body / range meets its minimum, and close position within the range meets its upper threshold. A support break is symmetric. Reset the corresponding broken flag when its hull edge changes.
Calculation: Failed break = Within a configurable number of bars after a hull break, the first close back beyond the saved break-line value by an ATR buffer; an upward break failure is short and a downward break failure is long. Use the current corresponding hull touch count. Track the highest high after an upward break or lowest low after a downward break through the failure bar for the failure stop.
Calculation: Price-leading flow = Normalize each signed-flow increment and source-price change over a rolling window by its current window standard deviation. Starting cumulative flow and price at zero, sum cumulative-flow-before-increment × price increment minus cumulative-price-before-increment × flow increment, divide by the window length, and smooth with an EMA seeded by its first available value. Price leads when the smoothed area is negative and the percentile rank of its absolute value meets a configurable significance threshold after the calibration warmup.
Calculation: Mode permission = When the optional edge gate is enabled, separately for flip and continuation, track the most recent configured number of decisive simulated outcomes of fresh signals. At signal close, store favorable and adverse distances: use recent empirical quantiles of subsequent high and low excursions normalized by the historical signal-bar ATR when enabled and sufficiently populated, otherwise use configurable regime-adjusted volatility multiples; scale the flip favorable distance by its separate multiplier. On each subsequent evaluation, including the signal bar, resolve a win if the favorable barrier is hit, including simultaneous hits, a loss if only the adverse barrier is hit, and exclude timeouts. Use a shorter timeout for flips. After the minimum sample count, mute a mode when its rolling win rate falls below the lower threshold and unmute when it reaches the higher threshold; use the previous bar's mute state for the current decision.
Calculation: Common signal gate = The current confirmed bar is at least the configured global cooldown from the last fresh directional signal. Update the last-signal bar for every accepted flip or continuation in either direction.
Calculation: Flip gate = The flip score tier meets its configured minimum AND the pivot extension meets its minimum AND (a graded level is near close OR the score reaches the high-tier threshold) AND the regime is neither chaos nor strong trend AND the configured intraday exclusion interval is not active AND ATR / its longer moving average is not above its kill threshold AND the ADX increase over its kill lookback is not above its threshold AND flip mode is permitted.
Calculation: Continuation gate = Continuation mode is permitted AND toxicity is below its threshold AND the relevant hull touch count meets its minimum AND no opposite swing flip occurred within the global cooldown lookback AND (fade mode is selected AND regime is not strong trend OR chase mode is selected AND regime is trend or strong trend AND the higher-timeframe bias sum agrees with the direction when required AND price-leading flow does not block chasing). Continuations additionally require regime not chaos and ATR percentile rank below the extreme threshold.
Calculation: Exit levels = On each fresh signal, replace the stored levels. For a flip, trigger is its pivot and stop is the pivot plus or minus an ATR multiple, or the conformal adverse distance when enabled and ready. For a chase, trigger is the break line and stop is the farther of the opposite hull edge and a regime-adjusted volatility stop from signal close; if the edge is unavailable use the volatility stop. For a fade, trigger is the saved break line and stop is the failure extreme. The second target is signal close plus or minus a configurable multiple of the favorable distance, with the flip distance separately scaled; favorable distance is the conformal favorable excursion quantile when enabled and ready, otherwise a regime-adjusted volatility multiple. For a fade with an available opposite hull edge, instead extrapolate the second target from signal close through that edge by the target multiple. Optionally snap the target to the nearest eligible structural level within an ATR-scaled tolerance. Conformal distances use interpolated quantiles of recent fixed-horizon maximum upward and downward excursions from historical closes, each normalized by its starting ATR, multiplied by current ATR. Store the resulting stop and second target until hit or replaced by a fresh signal.
Long: Bullish swing flip AND flip gate AND common signal gate AND flow alignment above the negative opposition threshold when the alignment gate is enabled AND (flow veto is disabled OR toxicity is below its threshold OR cumulative flow is at least its EMA).
Short: Bearish swing flip AND flip gate AND common signal gate AND flow alignment below the positive opposition threshold when the alignment gate is enabled AND (flow veto is disabled OR toxicity is below its threshold OR cumulative flow is below its EMA).
Long: (Downward failed break in fade mode OR upward hull break in chase mode) AND continuation gate AND common signal gate AND flow alignment above the negative opposition threshold when enabled AND no accepted flip candidate in either direction on the same bar.
Short: (Upward failed break in fade mode OR downward hull break in chase mode) AND continuation gate AND common signal gate AND flow alignment below the positive opposition threshold when enabled AND no accepted flip candidate in either direction on the same bar.
Long: Cumulative flow >= its EMA.
Short: Cumulative flow < its EMA.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias sum > 0.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias sum < 0.
Long: Flow alignment > 0.
Short: Flow alignment < 0.
Exit: long, low <= stored stop OR high >= stored second target.
Exit: short, high >= stored stop OR low <= stored second target.
