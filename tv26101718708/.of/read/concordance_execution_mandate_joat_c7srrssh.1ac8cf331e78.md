Calculation: Crossframe score = clamp(40 × (primary higher-timeframe fast EMA − slow EMA) / chart ATR + 45 × (secondary higher-timeframe fast EMA − slow EMA) / chart ATR + 12 × (chart fast EMA − slow EMA) / chart ATR, −100, 100); both higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed bar; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Auction profile = Over the lookback bars, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into equal bins; allocate each bar’s volume to bins in proportion to its range overlap. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin. Value area expands from the POC bin by repeatedly adding the adjacent bin with greater volume until captured volume reaches the target percentage.
Calculation: Auction score = clamp(55 × (short-lookback SMA(volume) / long-lookback SMA(volume) − 1) + 30 × (EMA(close, 34) − EMA(close, 89)) / chart ATR + 22 × (close − POC) / chart ATR, −100, 100); short volume lookback is shorter than long volume lookback.
Calculation: Constructive auction location = close > value-area high OR (value-area low ≤ close ≤ value-area high AND close > POC).
Calculation: Defensive auction location = close < value-area low OR (value-area low ≤ close ≤ value-area high AND close < POC).
Calculation: Structure midpoint = Initialize to SMA(close, step lookback); on each bar, if close is above prior midpoint + ATR × band multiplier or below prior midpoint − ATR × band multiplier, reset midpoint to SMA(close, step lookback), otherwise retain prior midpoint.
Calculation: Long structure = close > structure midpoint AND current midpoint − prior midpoint ≥ 0 AND (no confirmed pivot high exists OR close > latest confirmed pivot high).
Calculation: Short structure = close < structure midpoint AND current midpoint − prior midpoint ≤ 0 AND (no confirmed pivot low exists OR close < latest confirmed pivot low); pivots use left and right lookbacks and become available after the right lookback bars.
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(1.2 × (wave − wave signal) + (RSI − EMA(RSI, smoothing lookback)) + (RSI − 50) / 10 + 8 × (EMA(close, 34) − EMA(close, 89)) / chart ATR, −100, 100). Wave = EMA((hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, channel lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, channel lookback)), channel lookback)), average lookback); wave signal = SMA(wave, signal lookback).
Calculation: Momentum signal = EMA(momentum score, 8).
Calculation: Absorption range = Trigger when volume exceeds its SMA baseline times a multiplier. Start or reset the active range to the triggering bar’s high, low, hlc3, and bullish balance (0.65 if close ≥ open, else 0.35) when there is no active range, it expired, or close is outside it. Otherwise extend its high/low and update hlc3 and bullish balance as (prior value + new value) / 2. Clear it after its lifetime expires.
Calculation: Long absorption = no active absorption range OR (close ≥ range equilibrium AND bullish balance ≥ 0.50).
Calculation: Short absorption = no active absorption range OR (close ≤ range equilibrium AND bullish balance ≤ 0.50).
Calculation: ATR percentile = Percent rank of current ATR among current and prior lookback ATR values, counting values ≤ current ATR; use 50 before the lookback is available.
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp(ATR percentile − 50 + 20 × (EMA(high − low on bullish bars, direction lookback) − EMA(high − low on bearish bars, direction lookback)) / chart ATR, −100, 100); nonmatching bars contribute zero to each EMA.
Calculation: Volatility pass = minimum ATR percentile ≤ ATR percentile ≤ maximum ATR percentile.
Long: bar is confirmed AND crossframe score > threshold AND auction score > 10 AND constructive auction location AND long structure AND long absorption AND volatility pass AND volatility score > −15 AND momentum score crosses above momentum signal AND momentum score > threshold AND position size ≤ 0.
Short: bar is confirmed AND crossframe score < −threshold AND auction score < −10 AND defensive auction location AND short structure AND short absorption AND volatility pass AND volatility score < 15 AND momentum score crosses below momentum signal AND momentum score < −threshold AND position size ≥ 0.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price − max(entry price − min(entry price − ATR × stop multiplier, active absorption range low when structure anchoring is enabled), minimum tick distance). Long targets = entry price + risk × target multiples; the two target multiples are distinct, with the second larger than the first.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price + max(max(entry price + ATR × stop multiplier, active absorption range high when structure anchoring is enabled) − entry price, minimum tick distance). Short targets = entry price − risk × target multiples; the second target multiple is larger than the first.
Exit: Long, price ≤ long stop OR price ≥ first long target OR price ≥ second long target OR crossframe score < 0 OR momentum score < 0 OR auction score < 0 OR close < structure midpoint.
Exit: Short, price ≥ short stop OR price ≤ first short target OR price ≤ second short target OR crossframe score > 0 OR momentum score > 0 OR auction score > 0 OR close > structure midpoint.
