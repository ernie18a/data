Calculation: Crossframe score = clamp to [-100, 100] the sum of 40 times the primary higher-timeframe fast-minus-slow close EMA, 45 times the secondary higher-timeframe fast-minus-slow close EMA, and 12 times the chart-timeframe fast-minus-slow close EMA, each divided by the greater of chart-timeframe structure ATR and minimum price increment. Both higher timeframes use the last completed bar; the secondary timeframe is higher than the primary. On historical bars, use the higher-timeframe values mapped to the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Auction profile = over the lookback including the current bar, divide the lowest-low-to-highest-high range into equal price bins. Allocate each bar's volume across overlapping bins in proportion to overlap with its high-low range, using the minimum price increment as the minimum range and bin width. The point of control is the center of the first bin with the greatest allocated volume. Starting there, add the larger adjacent bin until the configured fraction of total bar volume is captured or all bins are included; choose the lower bin on ties. The value area is bounded by the outer edges of the included bins. Initialize the profile only after the lookback has elapsed.
Calculation: Auction score = clamp to [-100, 100] 55 times (short-lookback volume SMA / max(long-lookback volume SMA, minimum price increment) - 1), plus 30 times (short-period close EMA - long-period close EMA) / max(structure ATR, minimum price increment), plus 22 times (close - auction point of control) / max(structure ATR, minimum price increment). Use zero before the auction profile is initialized.
Calculation: Stepped midpoint = initialize to the close SMA; on each subsequent bar, replace it with the current close SMA if close is above the previous midpoint plus the current structure ATR times a multiplier or below the previous midpoint minus that amount; otherwise retain the previous midpoint.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low that is the corresponding extreme across its configured numbers of left and right bars; recognize it only after the right bars have completed and retain the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp to [-100, 100] 1.2 times (wave - wave signal), plus RSI - EMA(RSI), plus (RSI - 50) / 10, plus 8 times (short-period close EMA - long-period close EMA) / max(structure ATR, minimum price increment). Wave = EMA((typical price - EMA(typical price)) / (0.015 times EMA(abs(typical price - EMA(typical price))))); use zero for the normalized value when its denominator is zero. The first two EMAs use the same channel length; wave signal is an SMA of wave. The momentum signal is an EMA of the momentum score.
Calculation: Absorption range = when volume exceeds its SMA times a multiplier, start a range at the bar's high and low, with equilibrium equal to typical price and bullish balance equal to 0.65 if close >= open, otherwise 0.35. On a later trigger, reset it if its age exceeds its lifetime or close is outside its existing bounds; otherwise extend the bounds to include the bar and replace equilibrium and balance with the average of their previous values and the current bar's typical price and bullish balance. Clear the range when its age exceeds its lifetime; extending it does not reset its start bar.
Calculation: ATR percentile = 100 times the fraction of ATR values in the lookback, including the current value, that are <= the current ATR; use 50 until the lookback has elapsed. Volatility score = clamp to [-100, 100] (ATR percentile - 50) + 20 times (EMA of high - low on bullish candles, zero otherwise, minus EMA of high - low on bearish candles, zero otherwise) / max(volatility ATR, minimum price increment); both directional EMAs use the same length.
Calculation: Entry risk = at entry, take the greater of the volatility ATR times its stop multiplier and a minimum risk distance; if the optional structure anchor is enabled and an absorption range exists, also take the greater of that result and the distance from entry to the range bottom for a long or range top for a short. Fix the risk at entry. The second profit-target risk multiple is greater than the first.

Short: Crossframe score < 0
Long: Auction score > 0
Short: Auction score < 0

Short: Close < stepped midpoint AND stepped midpoint <= its previous value AND (no confirmed pivot low exists OR close < latest confirmed pivot low)
Long: Momentum score > 0
Short: Momentum score <= 0
Long: Absorption range exists AND bullish balance >= 0.5
Short: Absorption range exists AND bullish balance < 0.5
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND crossframe score > positive threshold AND auction score > positive threshold AND close > stepped midpoint AND stepped midpoint >= its previous value AND (no confirmed pivot high exists OR close > latest confirmed pivot high) AND (no absorption range exists OR (close >= absorption equilibrium AND bullish balance >= 0.5)) AND ATR percentile >= minimum threshold AND ATR percentile <= maximum threshold AND volatility score > negative threshold AND (close > auction value-area high OR (close >= value-area low AND close <= value-area high AND close > point of control))
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND crossframe score < negative threshold AND auction score < negative threshold AND close < stepped midpoint AND stepped midpoint <= its previous value AND (no confirmed pivot low exists OR close < latest confirmed pivot low) AND (no absorption range exists OR (close <= absorption equilibrium AND bullish balance <= 0.5)) AND ATR percentile >= minimum threshold AND ATR percentile <= maximum threshold AND volatility score < positive threshold AND (close < auction value-area low OR (close >= value-area low AND close <= value-area high AND close < point of control))

Short: All short permission conditions AND momentum score crosses below its signal AND momentum score < negative momentum threshold
