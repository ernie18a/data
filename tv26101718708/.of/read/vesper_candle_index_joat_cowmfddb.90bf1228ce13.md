Calculation: Bar anatomy = body = abs(close - open); range = max(high - low, minimum price increment); body fraction = body / range; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; close position = (close - low) / range. A bullish body has close > open; a bearish body has close < open. Subscripts denote bars ago.
Calculation: Regression field = linear regression of close evaluated at the current bar; slope = current regression value minus its previous-bar value; deviation = standard deviation of the series close minus its contemporaneous regression value. Lower field is reached when close <= regression value - inner field multiplier × deviation OR low <= regression value - outer field multiplier × deviation; upper field is reached when close >= regression value + inner field multiplier × deviation OR high >= regression value + outer field multiplier × deviation. The outer multiplier is greater than the inner multiplier.
Calculation: Trend = bullish when shorter-lookback close EMA > longer-lookback close EMA AND regression slope >= zero; bearish when shorter-lookback close EMA < longer-lookback close EMA AND regression slope <= zero; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Structure high or low = most recent confirmed pivot high or low, using separately parameterized left and right confirmation lengths; update the level on the confirmation bar before evaluating that bar. A bullish structure break occurs when close > current structure high AND previous close <= that same current structure high. A bearish structure break occurs when close < current structure low AND previous close >= that same current structure low.
Calculation: Bullish wick match = lower wick >= body × wick-to-body multiplier AND close position >= bullish wick close-position threshold. Bearish wick match = upper wick >= body × wick-to-body multiplier AND close position <= bearish wick close-position threshold.
Calculation: Bullish location match = lower field is reached OR trend is bullish AND close > shorter-lookback EMA. Bearish location match = upper field is reached OR trend is bearish AND close < shorter-lookback EMA. Bullish structure match = close > structure high; bearish structure match = close < structure low. Bullish momentum match = RSI > its neutral threshold AND RSI > previous RSI; bearish momentum match = RSI < its neutral threshold AND RSI < previous RSI. Volume match = volume / SMA(volume) >= volume expansion threshold, with the ratio set to one when the volume SMA is not positive. Strong body match = body fraction >= strong-body threshold.
Calculation: Bullish candidates = engulfing: bullish body AND previous bearish body AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND body >= previous body × engulfing body multiplier; hammer: bullish body AND lower wick >= body × hammer wick multiplier AND upper wick <= range × small-wick multiplier AND close position >= hammer close-position threshold; inverted hammer: bullish body AND upper wick >= body × inverted-hammer wick multiplier AND close position >= inverted-hammer close-position threshold AND lower field is reached; morning star: bearish body two bars ago AND previous body fraction <= star middle-body threshold AND bullish body AND close > midpoint of the body two bars ago; piercing: previous bearish body AND bullish body AND open < previous close AND close > midpoint of previous body AND close < previous open; harami: previous bearish body AND bullish body AND high <= previous high AND low >= previous low AND close > previous open; inside break: previous high < high two bars ago AND previous low > low two bars ago AND close > previous high; marubozu: bullish body AND body fraction >= marubozu body threshold AND close position >= bullish marubozu close-position threshold AND lower wick <= range × marubozu wick threshold; soldiers: bullish bodies on current and two preceding bars AND close > previous close AND previous close > close two bars ago AND body fraction >= sequence body threshold; tweezer: abs(low - previous low) <= ATR × tweezer tolerance AND previous bearish body AND bullish body AND close position >= bullish tweezer close-position threshold; rail: previous bearish body AND bullish body AND body fraction >= rail body threshold AND abs(body - previous body) <= ATR × rail tolerance AND close > previous high; reclaim: low < structure low AND close > structure low AND bullish wick match.
Calculation: Bearish candidates = engulfing: bearish body AND previous bullish body AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND body >= previous body × engulfing body multiplier; shooting star: bearish body AND upper wick >= body × hammer wick multiplier AND lower wick <= range × small-wick multiplier AND close position <= shooting-star close-position threshold; hanging man: bearish body AND lower wick >= body × inverted-hammer wick multiplier AND upper field is reached AND close position <= hanging-man close-position threshold; evening star: bullish body two bars ago AND previous body fraction <= star middle-body threshold AND bearish body AND close < midpoint of the body two bars ago; dark cloud: previous bullish body AND bearish body AND open > previous close AND close < midpoint of previous body AND close > previous open; harami: previous bullish body AND bearish body AND high <= previous high AND low >= previous low AND close < previous open; inside break: previous high < high two bars ago AND previous low > low two bars ago AND close < previous low; marubozu: bearish body AND body fraction >= marubozu body threshold AND close position <= bearish marubozu close-position threshold AND upper wick <= range × marubozu wick threshold; crows: bearish bodies on current and two preceding bars AND close < previous close AND previous close < close two bars ago AND body fraction >= sequence body threshold; tweezer: abs(high - previous high) <= ATR × tweezer tolerance AND previous bullish body AND bearish body AND close position <= bearish tweezer close-position threshold; rail: previous bullish body AND bearish body AND body fraction >= rail body threshold AND abs(body - previous body) <= ATR × rail tolerance AND close < previous low; reject: high > structure high AND close < structure high AND bearish wick match.
Calculation: Candidate score = candidate-specific base score plus the aligned-trend adjustment when trend matches its direction, the neutral-trend adjustment when trend is neutral, or the opposed-trend adjustment otherwise; plus the location award if location matches, structure award × structure weight if structure matches, momentum award if momentum matches or momentum penalty otherwise, volume award if volume matches, wick award if wick matches, and min(max(abs(regression slope) / ATR × slope scale, zero), slope cap), using zero for the slope contribution when ATR is not positive. For inside break, marubozu, soldiers, crows and rail, replace the location match with close above the shorter EMA for bullish candidates or below it for bearish candidates, and replace the wick match with strong body match. For hammer, inverted hammer, shooting star and hanging man, the wick match is always true. For reclaim and reject, location, structure and wick matches are always true. Base scores have the order reclaim/reject > morning/evening star > soldiers/crows > engulfing > rail > piercing/dark cloud > inside break > hammer/shooting star > marubozu > tweezer > inverted hammer/hanging man > harami. The aligned-trend adjustment exceeds the neutral-trend adjustment, which exceeds the opposed-trend adjustment; the momentum award exceeds the momentum penalty.
Calculation: Best candidate = the present candidate with the greatest positive score. Resolve equal scores by the first candidate in this order: bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing, hammer, inverted hammer, shooting star, hanging man, morning star, evening star, piercing, dark cloud, bullish harami, bearish harami, bullish inside break, bearish inside break, bullish marubozu, bearish marubozu, soldiers, crows, bullish tweezer, bearish tweezer, bullish rail, bearish rail, reclaim, reject. If none has a positive score, there is no best candidate.
Calculation: Accepted signal = best candidate score >= minimum score AND either no earlier accepted signal exists OR the number of bars since the most recent accepted signal > cooldown length. Update the most recent accepted-signal bar after every accepted signal, regardless of direction.

Short: best candidate direction = bearish AND best candidate score > zero
