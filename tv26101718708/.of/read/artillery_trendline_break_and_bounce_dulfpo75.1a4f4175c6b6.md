Calculation: Confirmed swing = a pivot high or low using equal numbers of chart bars on each side; it becomes available when the right-side bars complete. Retain the most recent confirmed swing high and low and a rolling history of confirmed swings of each type.
Calculation: Touch tolerance = current ATR multiplied by the touch-tolerance parameter.
Calculation: Active trendlines = maintain separate rising support and falling resistance lines. On each newly confirmed swing, first absorb it into the corresponding active line if its distance from that line at the swing bar is at most the current touch tolerance. Otherwise, if no corresponding line is active or its first anchor is older than the maximum line age, consider each retained earlier swing of the same type as a first anchor and the new swing as the second. Require a higher second low for support or a lower second high for resistance, anchor separation of at least twice the swing length, and first-anchor age at most the maximum line age. Extend each candidate line through the current bar. Every close from the first anchor through the current bar must be at least the support value minus current touch tolerance, or at most the resistance value plus current touch tolerance. Count two anchor touches plus every retained swing after the first anchor within current touch tolerance of the candidate line. Select the candidate with the most touches, breaking ties by the longest anchor span and then the earliest candidate in retained-history order. Replace the active line if a candidate exists. Retain only the configured number of latest swings of each type. Process new swings and construct lines before evaluating signals; retire a support line after a close below it or a resistance line after a close above it, after evaluating signals on that bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with the EMA of higher-timeframe closes through that same completed bar. The trend filter may be disabled.
Calculation: Long bounce condition = bounce enabled AND active support age from its first anchor >= minimum signal age AND low <= current support + touch tolerance AND close > current support AND close > open AND (trend filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA).
Calculation: Short bounce condition = bounce enabled AND active resistance age from its first anchor >= minimum signal age AND high >= current resistance - touch tolerance AND close < current resistance AND close < open AND (trend filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA).
Calculation: Long break condition = break enabled AND active resistance age from its first anchor >= minimum signal age AND close > current resistance AND latest confirmed swing low < close AND close - latest confirmed swing low <= break-distance parameter × ATR AND (trend filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA).
Calculation: Short break condition = break enabled AND active support age from its first anchor >= minimum signal age AND close < current support AND latest confirmed swing high > close AND latest confirmed swing high - close <= break-distance parameter × ATR AND (trend filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA).
Calculation: Safety line = for a bounce, initialize at the entry bar's support minus buffer × ATR for a long or resistance plus buffer × ATR for a short; retain the entry line's anchors and extend that line on subsequent bars. Before each subsequent exit check, raise a long safety line to the greater of its previous value and extended line minus current buffer × ATR, or lower a short safety line to the lesser of its previous value and extended line plus current buffer × ATR. For a break, initialize at the latest confirmed swing low for a long or swing high for a short; before each subsequent exit check, replace it with a newly confirmed swing low if that low is above the previous safety line and below close for a long, or with a newly confirmed swing high if that high is below the previous safety line and above close for a short.
Long: position flat AND long bounce condition.
Short: position flat AND short bounce condition AND NOT long bounce condition.
Long: position flat AND long break condition AND NOT long bounce condition AND NOT short bounce condition.
Short: position flat AND short break condition AND NOT long bounce condition AND NOT short bounce condition AND NOT long break condition.
Exit: long, bar after entry AND close < safety line.
Exit: short, bar after entry AND close > safety line.
