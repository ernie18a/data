Calculation: Configured indicator test = the AND of all enabled tests, with at least one test enabled. For each of RSI of close, Ultimate Oscillator, Bollinger %B of close, ADX, stochastic, MFI of typical price, and CCI of typical price, its test is the selected comparison: indicator crosses above, crosses below, is above, or is below its configured level. The moving-average test compares the shorter-lookback SMA or EMA of close with the longer-lookback average of the same type using the selected crossover, crossunder, above, or below comparison. The MACD zero-line test is MACD above or below zero; its crossover test is MACD crossing above or below its signal line. The PSAR test compares chart close with PSAR using the selected crossover, crossunder, above, or below comparison. The Heikin Ashi test requires the configured number of consecutive chart bars, including the current bar, to have Heikin Ashi close above open or below open, according to the selected direction. The screener test is true for the Buy selection on entry and the Sell selection on exit, and false for the other selections. Entry and exit configurations are independent.
Calculation: Stochastic = SMA of raw stochastic %K over the configured smoothing length. The separately selectable stochastic %K versus %D crossover test is always false because %D equals this same smoothed stochastic.
Calculation: Timeframe override = each indicator except Heikin Ashi may independently use the chart timeframe or an aggregated timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar's indicator value; for a lower timeframe, use the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar. Perform cross comparisons on the resulting chart-bar series. PSAR is compared with chart close.
