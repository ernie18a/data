Calculation: Structure = compare the latest two confirmed pivot highs and the latest two confirmed pivot lows calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; each pivot has a configurable, equal number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Prior levels = either the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar used for structure, or the high and low of the preceding execution session, frozen at the start of the current execution session.
Calculation: Pools = the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high and low, each confirmed using a configurable equal number of bars on both sides; the running high and low of each of two configurable sessions; and the prior levels. Reset each session pool at the start of its session and retain it until that session starts again. A session pool is eligible only within a configurable freshness interval after its session ends. Pool types are independently selectable.
Calculation: Raid = an eligible, unconsumed low pool with low <= pool - penetration and previous low > pool - penetration, optionally requiring close >= pool; or an eligible, unconsumed high pool with high >= pool + penetration and previous high < pool + penetration, optionally requiring close <= pool. Penetration is a configurable price increment. Consume every pool raided by the event; reset consumption for a swing when a new corresponding pivot is confirmed, for a session pool when its session starts, and for a prior-level pool when the higher-timeframe period used for structure changes.
Calculation: Setup sequence = evaluate confirmed chart bars in order, with only one active sequence or position. Start idle. An eligible raid starts the waiting-for-gap state and records its direction, bar and extreme. On each subsequent bar in that state, extend the extreme through the current bar; reset if structure changes direction, the execution session is inactive, or bars elapsed since the raid exceed a configurable limit. Otherwise, a directional gap may arm the setup: a bullish gap has low > high two bars earlier and size low - earlier high; a bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier and size earlier low - high. Gap size must be >= a configurable multiple of chart-timeframe ATR. Optionally require the middle bar to close in the gap direction and its absolute body divided by its range to be >= a configurable threshold, using zero efficiency for a zero range. The planned entry is the gap edge nearest the current price or its midpoint, rounded to a configurable price increment; the stop is the recorded extreme minus a configurable buffer for a long or plus it for a short; the target is the prior high for a long or prior low for a short. Arm only if absolute entry-to-stop risk > 0, directional entry-to-target reward > 0, and reward / risk >= a configurable minimum; otherwise continue waiting for a gap. While armed, reset if structure changes direction, the execution session ends, or the target is touched before entry. Otherwise enter only after the gap bar when the entry is touched. Reset after an exit; do not start a new sequence on the same bar as a reset. Count entries per higher-timeframe period used for structure and reset the count when that period changes.
Long: Idle AND execution session active AND entry count < configurable cap AND latest higher-timeframe pivot high > preceding pivot high AND latest higher-timeframe pivot low > preceding pivot low AND low-pool raid AND (weekly-open filter disabled OR raid low < open of a longer higher-timeframe bar still forming).
Short: Idle AND execution session active AND entry count < configurable cap AND latest higher-timeframe pivot high < preceding pivot high AND latest higher-timeframe pivot low < preceding pivot low AND high-pool raid AND (weekly-open filter disabled OR raid high > open of a longer higher-timeframe bar still forming).
Long: Long setup waiting for gap AND bullish gap meets the setup sequence conditions AND risk and reward satisfy the setup sequence conditions.
Short: Short setup waiting for gap AND bearish gap meets the setup sequence conditions AND risk and reward satisfy the setup sequence conditions.
Long: Long setup armed AND structure remains bullish AND execution session has not ended AND high < target AND bar is after the gap bar AND low <= planned entry.
Short: Short setup armed AND structure remains bearish AND execution session has not ended AND low > target AND bar is after the gap bar AND high >= planned entry.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR (session-end flattening enabled AND execution session has just ended).
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR (session-end flattening enabled AND execution session has just ended).
Calculation: Exit execution = evaluate the entry bar for the stop immediately after entry; on later bars, session-end flattening takes priority and fills at the preceding in-session close, otherwise the stop takes priority over the target when both are touched. Stops and targets fill at their recorded levels.
