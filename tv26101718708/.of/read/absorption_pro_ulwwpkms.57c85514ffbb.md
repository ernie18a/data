Calculation: Anchored VWAP and deviation = At each anchor-period boundary reset cumulative volume V, cumulative typical-price times volume P, and variance accumulator S. On each bar, add volume to V and typical price times volume to P; VWAP = P / V. Set S to zero on the first bar; otherwise add volume * (typical price - previous VWAP) * (typical price - current VWAP) to S. Deviation = sqrt(S / V). Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3. The anchor is a higher timeframe, and the current period is still forming.
Calculation: Confirmed swing = Track a high and low and a direction, initially zero. While direction is positive, update the high and its RSI when high >= tracked high; otherwise, if low < tracked high * (1 - reversal fraction), confirm the previous bar's tracked high, set direction negative, save the previous tracked low and high as the latest upward swing, and set the tracked low to the current low. While direction is nonpositive, update the low and its RSI when low <= tracked low; otherwise, if high > tracked low * (1 + reversal fraction), confirm the previous bar's tracked low, set direction positive, save the previous tracked low and high as the latest downward swing, and set the tracked high to the current high. Initialize both tracked prices to zero. Retain the two most recently confirmed highs and lows and the RSI recorded at each extreme.
Calculation: Value-area levels = For each completed higher-timeframe period, distribute each chart bar's volume equally across a fixed number of evenly spaced prices from low to high, rounding each price to the nearest price-bin increment; if high equals low, assign all volume to the rounded close. Sum volume by occupied bin. Starting at the lowest-priced bin with maximum volume, expand to the adjacent occupied bin with greater volume, choosing the lower bin on a tie, until the accumulated volume reaches the specified fraction of period volume or no bins remain. The lowest and highest included prices are value-area low and high. Calculate separate levels for a longer and a shorter higher timeframe, using the last completed period of each.
Calculation: Base score = Sum the following components, each calculated on the score's own timeframe: (1) Band score: for each successive deviation step through the maximum step, add a fixed point amount if close > EMA(close) + step * standard deviation(close), or subtract it if close < EMA(close) - step * standard deviation(close); the EMA and deviation use the same lookback. (2) RSI score: the signed number of whole RSI steps between RSI and its midpoint, multiplied by points per step. (3) Delta-streak score: approximate delta = volume * (2 * (close - low) / (high - low) - 1), or zero if high = low; on consecutive bars with delta > 0 AND close < open, use a positive starting score plus an increment for each additional bar; on consecutive bars with delta < 0 AND close > open, use the negative equivalent; otherwise use zero and reset both streaks. (4) Value-area score: subtract the shorter-period weight when open > its value-area low AND abs(close - that low) <= tolerance; add that weight when open < its value-area high AND abs(close - that high) <= tolerance; repeat for the longer period with its larger weight. (5) Absorption score: range = high - low, replaced by a minimum price increment when zero; delta proxy = volume * (close - open) / range; body percentage = abs(close - open) / close * 100; close position = (close - low) / range; intensity = floor(abs(delta proxy) / (delta threshold * intensity scale) * intensity steps), clamped between the minimum and maximum intensity. Add intensity when volume >= volume threshold AND delta proxy > delta threshold AND close > open AND body percentage < body threshold AND close position >= upper close-position threshold AND close > trend EMA AND high > VWAP + inner deviation multiplier * deviation AND low < VWAP + outer deviation multiplier * deviation. Subtract intensity when volume >= volume threshold AND delta proxy < -delta threshold AND close < open AND body percentage < body threshold AND close position <= lower close-position threshold AND close < trend EMA AND high > VWAP - outer deviation multiplier * deviation AND low < VWAP - inner deviation multiplier * deviation. (6) Stochastic score: raw stochastic = 100 * (close - rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high - rolling lowest low); average and signal are separate simple moving averages of raw stochastic. Above the upper extreme threshold use a linear score centered at an upper reference; below the lower extreme threshold use a linear score centered at a lower reference; otherwise use zero. Subtract a crossover bonus when average crosses above signal below a lower crossover threshold; add a crossunder bonus when average crosses below signal above an upper crossover threshold; clamp to the score bounds. (7) CCI score: typical-price average is its simple moving average; mean deviation is the simple moving average of abs(typical price - its contemporaneous average); CCI = (typical price - average) / (CCI scale * mean deviation). Above the positive threshold, score floor((CCI - threshold) / step) + one point, capped at the positive bound; below the negative threshold, score ceil((CCI + threshold) / step) - one point, floored at the negative bound; otherwise zero. (8) MACD score: normalize the MACD histogram by its rolling standard deviation, or use zero if that deviation is zero. When its absolute value exceeds the extreme threshold, use its sign times a linearly increasing magnitude capped at the score bound; otherwise zero. Add a bonus if close > previous close AND histogram < previous histogram AND score > 0; subtract it if close < previous close AND histogram > previous histogram AND score < 0; clamp to the score bounds. MACD uses a faster EMA, a slower EMA, and a signal EMA. (9) ADX score: when ADX > its upper threshold, use (positive directional indicator - negative directional indicator) / 100 times a linear function of ADX above that threshold, clamped to the score bounds; otherwise zero. (10) Key-level score: for each resistance level, add one point if open < level AND abs(close - level) <= tolerance; for each support level, subtract one point if open > level AND abs(close - level) <= tolerance. Resistance levels are the previous completed shorter- and longer-period highs and their standard first and second pivot resistances; supports are the corresponding lows and pivot supports. For each neutral level, add one point if open < level AND abs(close - level) <= tolerance, or subtract one if open > level AND abs(close - level) <= tolerance. Neutral levels are the two completed periods' pivot points and closes and close rounded to each of two price increments. Both periods are higher than the score timeframe. Add a positive Fibonacci-zone weight when the latest confirmed swing is downward AND open < its lower retracement boundary AND close is within its inclusive retracement zone; subtract that weight when the latest swing is upward AND open > its upper retracement boundary AND close is within its inclusive zone. The upward zone is measured downward from the swing high and the downward zone upward from the swing low, using the same two ordered retracement fractions. (11) RSI divergence score: on confirmation of a swing high, add a divergence weight if the new high > the preceding confirmed high AND its recorded RSI < the preceding high's RSI; on confirmation of a swing low, subtract that weight if the new low < the preceding confirmed low AND its recorded RSI > the preceding low's RSI; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Total score = Base score on the chart timeframe or a selected calculation timeframe, plus optional scaled base scores on additional higher timeframes. For a higher calculation timeframe use the bar still forming; for a lower calculation timeframe use its last completed bar within the chart bar. Each additional higher-timeframe score uses its bar still forming. Compute the following adjustments on the chart timeframe: slope = (trend EMA - its value a specified number of bars ago) / that number; strength = clamp(slope strength scale * abs(slope) / ATR, 0, 1). Multiply a positive score by a decreasing linear function of strength when slope > 0, or an increasing linear function when slope < 0; multiply a negative score by the increasing function when slope > 0, or the decreasing function when slope < 0. Multiply by 1 + distance scale * abs(close - smoothed moving average) / price increment when score > 0 AND close > that average, or score < 0 AND close < that average. Multiply by the high-ADX factor when ADX > its upper threshold, the low-ADX factor when ADX < its lower threshold, and otherwise the neutral factor; round to the specified score increment. The high-ADX factor is smaller than the neutral factor, which is smaller than the low-ADX factor.
Long: Total score < long threshold selected for close > trend EMA AND close > trend EMA OR total score < long threshold selected for close <= trend EMA AND close <= trend EMA.
Short: Total score > short threshold selected for close > trend EMA AND close > trend EMA OR total score > short threshold selected for close <= trend EMA AND close <= trend EMA.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + profit distance OR the short condition is true.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - profit distance OR the long condition is true.
