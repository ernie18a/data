Calculation: Normalized MACD oscillator = (fast moving average of close − slow moving average of close) / selected scale, where scale is ATR, MACD standard deviation, close/100, or 1; signal = selected moving average of oscillator; histogram = oscillator − signal. Use configurable fast/slow lengths with fast shorter than slow. Confirm oscillator pivot highs/lows after the configured right bars; compare each to configured recent same-type pivots whose gap is within configured bounds. A candidate’s divergence score = weighted mean (0–100) of: magnitude, geometric mean of clamped absolute price swing/(2 ATR) and oscillator swing/oscillator standard deviation; extremity, regular = clamped abs(pivot oscillator)/(1.5 standard deviations), hidden = clamped (1 − extremity/1.2); spacing, clamped (1 − abs(gap − midpoint of allowed gap range)/midpoint); regime fit, with-trend direction gets 0.85 for regular or clamped trend strength×1.2 for hidden, against-trend gets clamped (1 − trend strength)×0.85 for regular or 0.05 for hidden, transition gets 0.60 for regular or trend strength×0.40 for hidden; trend strength = clamped mean of close-path efficiency ratio and clamped ADX/45, direction = DMI direction confirmed by close above/below structural EMA; extension from structural EMA in ATR, signed toward trade for regular and reversed for hidden, clamped to 0–1 over 3 ATR; volume, compare 3-bar volume averages at pivots, favor lower second-push volume for regular and higher for hidden (neutral if unavailable); higher-timeframe MACD bias alignment, using a higher-timeframe bar still forming, aligned=1, neutral=0.5, opposed=0.1 (or 0.5 if filter disabled); momentum shift, 0.6 if histogram turns toward trade at confirmation plus 0.4 if oscillator is on the trade side of its signal; zero-line integrity, 1 if the oscillator segment stays on the required side of zero, 0.5 if only both pivot values are on that side, otherwise 0; structure, 0.6×clamped pivot RSI extremity (bull: (45−RSI)/20; bear: (RSI−55)/20) plus 0.4×clamped rejection wick proportion/0.5. Select the highest-scoring eligible candidate for each pivot type; require its score to meet the configured minimum. Apply configured regular/hidden enablement and zero-line mode (strict segment, soft endpoints, or off). Use the configured weights and parameters.
Long: confirmed bullish regular divergence (current pivot low < prior price low AND current oscillator pivot low > prior oscillator pivot low) OR bullish hidden divergence (current pivot low > prior price low AND current oscillator pivot low < prior oscillator pivot low), with the applicable zero-line rule (regular below zero; hidden above zero) AND eligible candidate score ≥ configured minimum.
Short: confirmed bearish regular divergence (current pivot high > prior price high AND current oscillator pivot high < prior oscillator pivot high) OR bearish hidden divergence (current pivot high < prior price high AND current oscillator pivot high > prior oscillator pivot high), with the applicable zero-line rule (regular above zero; hidden below zero) AND eligible candidate score ≥ configured minimum.
