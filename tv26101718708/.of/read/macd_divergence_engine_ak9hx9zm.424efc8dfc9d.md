Calculation: Normalized oscillator = (shorter-lookback moving average of the selected price source minus longer-lookback moving average of that source) divided by the selected scale: 1, ATR, standard deviation of the unnormalized oscillator, or price divided by a percentage-scaling constant. Replace an unavailable or nonpositive scale with 1. Use the selected moving-average type for both averages. Signal = selected moving average of the normalized oscillator; histogram = oscillator minus signal.
Calculation: Pivot pair = a newly confirmed oscillator pivot and one of the most recent configured number of earlier oscillator pivots of the same type. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars; its price is the low at an oscillator pivot low or the high at an oscillator pivot high. The pair's bar gap must be at least the greater of the configured minimum gap and the sum of the left and right pivot lengths, and at most the configured maximum gap. Evaluate the decision on the confirmation bar.
Calculation: Zero integrity = 1 if every available oscillator value between the pivots, inclusive, is strictly on the required side of zero; otherwise 0.5 if both pivot values are strictly on that side; otherwise 0. The required side is below zero for regular bullish and hidden bearish divergence, and above zero for regular bearish and hidden bullish divergence. Strict mode requires integrity = 1; soft mode requires integrity >= 0.5; off mode imposes no zero-integrity restriction.
Calculation: Confluence score = the weighted average of the following ten components, multiplied by 100; use configurable nonnegative weights and return 0 if their sum is zero. Clamp each indicated component to [0,1], replacing unavailable clamped values with 0. Current means the confirmation bar; pivot means the newly confirmed pivot; prior means its paired earlier pivot. Oscillator dispersion is the current standard deviation of the normalized oscillator over a lookback longer than its normalization lookback, floored at a small positive constant. ATR at the pivot is also floored at a small positive constant. Magnitude = square root of the product of clamped absolute pivot-to-prior price change divided by pivot ATR and a price-magnitude coefficient, and clamped absolute oscillator change divided by current oscillator dispersion and an oscillator-magnitude coefficient. Extremity = clamped absolute pivot oscillator divided by current oscillator dispersion and an extremity coefficient for regular divergence; for hidden divergence use clamped 1 minus that extremity divided by a hidden-extremity coefficient. Spacing = clamped 1 minus the absolute difference between pivot gap and the midpoint of the configured minimum and maximum gaps, divided by that midpoint. Trend power = clamped weighted blend of the current close efficiency ratio and clamped current ADX divided by an ADX scale; the efficiency ratio is absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback, or 0 when the denominator is zero. Trend direction is up when +DI > -DI AND close > its structural EMA, down when +DI <= -DI AND close <= that EMA, and transition otherwise. Regime fit uses configurable scores: for hidden divergence, clamped trend power times a coefficient when aligned with trend, a low fixed score when opposed, or trend power times a transition coefficient in transition; for regular divergence, a fixed aligned score, clamped 1 minus trend power times an opposing-trend coefficient when opposed, or a fixed transition score. Extension = clamped signed distance of pivot close from its structural EMA divided by pivot ATR and an extension coefficient for regular divergence; for hidden divergence use the clamped sum of an offset and the negative signed distance divided by a hidden-extension coefficient. Sign the distance negative for bullish and positive for bearish divergence. Volume = a neutral score if either pivot's short-lookback volume SMA is nonpositive or unavailable; otherwise use the clamped difference between a regular-volume reference and the ratio of pivot to prior volume SMA, divided by a volume scale, for regular divergence, or the clamped difference between that ratio and a hidden-volume reference divided by the same scale for hidden divergence. Higher-timeframe alignment = a neutral score when disabled or neutral, an aligned score when its bias matches the divergence direction, and an opposing score otherwise; higher-timeframe bias is positive when its shorter EMA of close minus its longer EMA is both above its EMA signal and above zero, negative when both comparisons are reversed, and neutral otherwise. Use higher-timeframe bars with lookahead off: on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, becoming available on its closing chart bar; the higher-timeframe bar still forming may be used in real time. Momentum = the sum of a histogram-turn weight when the current histogram turns toward the divergence direction relative to the preceding two bars and a line-alignment weight when the current oscillator is above its signal for bullish or below it for bearish divergence. Zero component = zero integrity. Structure = weighted sum of clamped pivot RSI positioning, using a bullish reference minus RSI for bullish divergence or RSI minus a bearish reference for bearish divergence, divided by an RSI scale, and clamped pivot rejection wick divided by pivot bar range and a wick scale; use the lower wick for bullish and upper wick for bearish divergence, and floor the range at a small positive constant.
Long: A newly confirmed oscillator pivot low has at least one eligible prior pivot low AND the corresponding price low is lower while the oscillator low is higher for enabled regular divergence, OR the price low is higher while the oscillator low is lower for enabled hidden divergence; the pair satisfies the selected zero-integrity mode AND its confluence score >= the minimum score. Select the highest-scoring eligible pair, breaking ties in favor of the most recent prior pivot.
Short: A newly confirmed oscillator pivot high has at least one eligible prior pivot high AND the corresponding price high is higher while the oscillator high is lower for enabled regular divergence, OR the price high is lower while the oscillator high is higher for enabled hidden divergence; the pair satisfies the selected zero-integrity mode AND its confluence score >= the minimum score. Select the highest-scoring eligible pair, breaking ties in favor of the most recent prior pivot.
