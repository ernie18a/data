Calculation: Index bars relative to the evaluation bar: bar 1 is the previous completed bar, bar 2 precedes it, and so on. Body = abs(close − open); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); range = high − low. Guard body, range, ATR, and average activity by replacing values below a minimum price increment parameter with that parameter; replace unavailable ATR and average activity with zero before guarding.
Calculation: Activity on each bar = volume if cumulative volume through that bar > 0, otherwise range. Activity score = 20 × clamp((activity on bar 1 / guarded SMA of activity ending on bar 1 − 1) / max(activity multiplier − 1, 0.01), 0, 1); replace unavailable activity on bar 1 with zero.
Calculation: Bullish conviction = 40 + activity score + 20 × clamp(1 − (low of bar 1 − lowest low over the swing lookback ending on bar 1) / (guarded ATR on bar 1 × swing-distance multiplier), 0, 1) + 20 × clamp((close at the prior-move lookback before bar 1 − close of bar 1) / (guarded ATR on bar 1 × prior-move multiplier), 0, 1).
Calculation: Bearish conviction = 40 + activity score + 20 × clamp(1 − (highest high over the swing lookback ending on bar 1 − high of bar 1) / (guarded ATR on bar 1 × swing-distance multiplier), 0, 1) + 20 × clamp((close of bar 1 − close at the prior-move lookback before bar 1) / (guarded ATR on bar 1 × prior-move multiplier), 0, 1).
Long: Bullish conviction >= conviction threshold AND ((lower wick of bar 1 >= wick-ratio parameter × guarded body of bar 1 AND lower wick of bar 1 > upper wick of bar 1 AND lower wick of bar 1 >= 0.5 × guarded range of bar 1) OR (close of bar 2 < open of bar 2 AND close of bar 1 > open of bar 1 AND close of bar 1 >= open of bar 2 AND open of bar 1 <= close of bar 2) OR (high of bar 2 <= high of bar 3 AND low of bar 2 >= low of bar 3 AND close of bar 1 > high of bar 2)).
Short: Bearish conviction >= conviction threshold AND ((upper wick of bar 1 >= wick-ratio parameter × guarded body of bar 1 AND upper wick of bar 1 > lower wick of bar 1 AND upper wick of bar 1 >= 0.5 × guarded range of bar 1) OR (close of bar 2 > open of bar 2 AND close of bar 1 < open of bar 1 AND close of bar 1 <= open of bar 2 AND open of bar 1 >= close of bar 2) OR (high of bar 2 <= high of bar 3 AND low of bar 2 >= low of bar 3 AND close of bar 1 < low of bar 2)).
