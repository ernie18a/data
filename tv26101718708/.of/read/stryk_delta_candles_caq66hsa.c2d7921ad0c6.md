Calculation: Signed volume = for each completed lower-than-chart-timeframe bar, volume if close > open, negative volume if close < open; if close = open, use the sign of close minus the previous lower-timeframe close, or zero if equal. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable, use chart-bar volume signed by close minus open, or zero if equal.
Calculation: Delta = sum of signed volume within a higher-than-or-equal-to-chart-timeframe bar. Use the forming bar for live delta and the last completed bar for completed delta. Evaluate completed-bar signals once, on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Average absolute delta = arithmetic mean of absolute completed deltas over the lookback, including the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Main bias score = the weighted mean of completed higher-timeframe deltas, with weights increasing from 1 for the oldest to the number of available lookback bars for the newest, plus the forming delta weighted by its elapsed fraction times one more than the number of completed bars; divide by average absolute delta. The forming-bar elapsed fraction is clamped between zero and one.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias score = the weighted mean of signed volumes of the forming higher-timeframe bar and preceding bars over that timeframe's lookback, with weights decreasing from the lookback length for the forming bar to 1 for the oldest bar, divided by their mean absolute signed volume. Sign each higher-timeframe bar's volume by its close minus open; if equal, use its close minus the previous higher-timeframe close. Apply independently to each higher-timeframe bias track.
Long: Live delta > 0.
Short: Live delta < 0.
Long: On completion of a higher-timeframe bar, its delta >= 0.
Short: On completion of a higher-timeframe bar, its delta < 0.

Short: Last completed higher-timeframe close minus open > flat-price tolerance AND last completed delta < 0 AND (flip signals disabled OR previous completed delta <= 0 OR previous completed delta is unavailable).
Long: Absolute last completed delta >= absorption multiplier * average absolute delta AND average absolute delta > 0 AND absolute last completed higher-timeframe close minus open <= flat-price tolerance AND ((absorption inversion disabled AND last completed delta < 0) OR (absorption inversion enabled AND last completed delta > 0)).
Short: Absolute last completed delta >= absorption multiplier * average absolute delta AND average absolute delta > 0 AND absolute last completed higher-timeframe close minus open <= flat-price tolerance AND ((absorption inversion disabled AND last completed delta > 0) OR (absorption inversion enabled AND last completed delta < 0)).
