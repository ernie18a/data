Calculation: EMA9 = 9-period EMA of close; EMA50 = 50-period EMA of close; Exit SMA = configured-period SMA of close
Calculation: CHoCH swing high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low, using configured left- and right-bar lookbacks
Calculation: CHoCH break level = swing high plus configured ATR buffer for an upward break, or swing low minus configured ATR buffer for a downward break
Calculation: CHoCH stop = latest opposite swing (swing low for long, swing high for short), offset outward by a configured ATR buffer; if unavailable, use entry price offset by 1 ATR. CHoCH targets = entry price plus/minus configured multiples of the entry-to-stop risk distance
Long: EMA9 crosses above EMA50; OR close crosses above the CHoCH break level; OR an armed upward CHoCH break is retested within the configured bar limit (low reaches the swing high plus an ATR buffer and close > swing high), with EMA9 > EMA50 if enabled, close > VWAP if enabled, volume > its longer-lookback average times the configured multiplier if enabled, within the configured session, and CHoCH position is flat; OR an upward EMA9/EMA50 cross is armed and EMA9 > EMA50, with enabled VWAP, volume, session, cooldown, and bar-close filters passing
Short: EMA9 crosses below EMA50; OR close crosses below the CHoCH break level; OR an armed downward CHoCH break is retested within the configured bar limit (high reaches the swing low minus an ATR buffer and close < swing low), with EMA9 < EMA50 if enabled, close < VWAP if enabled, volume > its longer-lookback average times the configured multiplier if enabled, within the configured session, and CHoCH position is flat; OR a downward EMA9/EMA50 cross is armed and EMA9 < EMA50, with enabled VWAP, volume, session, cooldown, and bar-close filters passing
Exit: long, EMA9 crosses below the Exit SMA; OR low <= the live CHoCH stop; OR high >= CHoCH TP2 when runner mode is disabled
Exit: short, EMA9 crosses above the Exit SMA; OR high >= the live CHoCH stop; OR low <= CHoCH TP2 when runner mode is disabled
