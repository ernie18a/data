Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price ((high + low + close) / 3) times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each trading-day boundary.
Calculation: Opening range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured opening session; reset each trading day and use only after that session ends.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the latest pivot high/low confirmed after the configured numbers of left and right chart bars, respectively.
Calculation: Structure break = close crosses above the current confirmed swing high plus an ATR multiple, or below the current confirmed swing low minus an ATR multiple; compare both current and previous closes with the current threshold. Use zero as the ATR distance when ATR is unavailable. A bullish break requires the previous structure direction to be neutral or bearish; a bearish break requires it to be neutral or bullish and no bullish break on the same bar. Set structure direction to the break direction; optionally reset it to neutral when close crosses back through the unbuffered broken swing level.
Calculation: Pending EMA direction = the direction of the most recent fast/slow EMA cross, cleared when its signal fires and overwritten by an opposite cross. At the first in-session bar or first bar of a new day, if no direction is pending and the active EMA signal differs from the EMA ordering, arm that ordering. Clear the active EMA signal at each new day.
Calculation: Armed structure level = the unbuffered swing level at the latest structure break; disarm the opposite direction on a break, disarm after the configured maximum number of bars, and disarm that direction after its entry. Invalidating structure direction does not disarm it.
Calculation: Structure trade levels = at entry close, set the long stop to the latest confirmed swing low minus an ATR buffer, or entry minus ATR if no swing low exists; reverse for shorts. Optionally cap the stop distance by an ATR multiple. Risk is the greater of the entry-to-stop distance and a minimum price increment. Set two targets at configured risk multiples from entry. In runner mode, before checking exits, repeatedly roll while the bar reaches the second target, up to a configured per-bar limit: move the first target to the second and extend the second by the greater of the prior target spacing and the minimum price increment; set the stop to the prior first target, fast EMA, close minus/plus an ATR trail, or leave it unchanged according to the chosen mode. Mark the first target hit on a roll. Otherwise mark it hit after exit checks when the bar reaches it. If breakeven is enabled and the first target was hit before the current exit check, use entry as the stop until the first roll; thereafter use the rolled stop.
Long: Fast close EMA crosses above slow close EMA.
Short: Fast close EMA crosses below slow close EMA.
Long: Fast close EMA >= slow close EMA.
Short: Fast close EMA < slow close EMA.
Long: Forming higher-timeframe bar close > its open.
Short: Forming higher-timeframe bar close < its open.
Long: Fast close EMA > slow close EMA AND forming bars on both configured higher timeframes have close > open.
Short: Fast close EMA < slow close EMA AND forming bars on both configured higher timeframes have close < open.
Long: Close > completed opening-range high.
Short: Close < completed opening-range low.
Long: Close > completed opening-range high AND previous close <= current opening-range high.
Short: Close < completed opening-range low AND previous close >= current opening-range low.
Long: Low > high two bars ago AND low minus that high >= configured ATR multiple, using zero if ATR is unavailable.
Short: High < low two bars ago AND that low minus high >= configured ATR multiple, using zero if ATR is unavailable.
Long: On a confirmed bar, either deferred mode is enabled AND pending EMA direction is long AND fast EMA > slow EMA, OR deferred mode is disabled AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA; AND optional session and blackout restrictions pass AND optional close > session VWAP AND optional volume > volume SMA times its multiplier AND optional bars since the last EMA signal >= cooldown.
Short: On a confirmed bar, either deferred mode is enabled AND pending EMA direction is short AND fast EMA < slow EMA, OR deferred mode is disabled AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA; AND optional session and blackout restrictions pass AND optional close < session VWAP AND optional volume > volume SMA times its multiplier AND optional bars since the last EMA signal >= cooldown.
Long: Bullish structure break.
Short: Bearish structure break.
Long: Structure position is flat AND either pullback mode is disabled AND a bullish structure break occurs, OR pullback mode is enabled AND a bullish structure level is armed AND the current bar is after the break bar AND low <= armed level + ATR buffer AND close > armed level; AND optional session and blackout restrictions pass AND optional fast EMA > slow EMA AND optional close > session VWAP AND optional volume > volume SMA times its multiplier.
Short: Structure position is flat AND either pullback mode is disabled AND a bearish structure break occurs, OR pullback mode is enabled AND a bearish structure level is armed AND the current bar is after the break bar AND high >= armed level - ATR buffer AND close < armed level; AND optional session and blackout restrictions pass AND optional fast EMA < slow EMA AND optional close < session VWAP AND optional volume > volume SMA times its multiplier.
Exit: EMA long, fast close EMA crosses below the exit close SMA OR an opposite EMA signal fires OR a new trading day starts.
Exit: EMA short, fast close EMA crosses above the exit close SMA OR an opposite EMA signal fires OR a new trading day starts.
Exit: Structure long, low <= structure trade stop OR runner mode is disabled AND high >= second structure target.
Exit: Structure short, high >= structure trade stop OR runner mode is disabled AND low <= second structure target.
