Calculation: Resistance = Select the midpoint of the most frequently hit of the top three of ten equal price-range buckets. Build the range from the current close and highs/lows of the preceding lookback bars whose closes differ from the current close by less than the configured consolidation-range percentage. Count a hit when a selected bar’s high falls within a bucket; ties keep the first bucket, and if none are hit use the range high.
Calculation: Support = Same range and bar selection as Resistance, using the bottom three buckets and counting selected bars’ lows; ties keep the first bucket, and if none are hit use the range low.
Calculation: Stop level = Resistance − 2/3 × (Resistance − Support)
Calculation: Stop distance percentage = (close − Stop level) / close × 100
Calculation: MACD = Difference between the 12-period and 26-period exponential moving averages of close; signal line = 9-period exponential moving average of MACD.
Calculation: NATR = ATR / close × 100.
