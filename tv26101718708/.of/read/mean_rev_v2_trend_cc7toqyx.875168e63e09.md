Calculation: Long ATR stop = close - (ATR × multiplier)
Calculation: Short ATR stop = close + (ATR × multiplier)
Long: smoothed stochastic %K crosses above smoothed stochastic %D AND smoothed stochastic %K is below the oversold threshold AND fast EMA is above slow SMA
Short: smoothed stochastic %K crosses below smoothed stochastic %D AND smoothed stochastic %K is above the overbought threshold AND slow SMA is above fast EMA
Exit: Long, smoothed stochastic %K crosses below smoothed stochastic %D AND smoothed stochastic %K is above the overbought close threshold OR open is below the Long ATR stop
Exit: Short, smoothed stochastic %K crosses above smoothed stochastic %D AND smoothed stochastic %K is below the oversold close threshold OR open is above the Short ATR stop
