Calculation: Composite score = clip to [-1,1] the sum of seven scores times their normalized weights: RSI, volume/OBV, VWAP, EMA trend, MACD, Bollinger Bands, and volume intelligence. Each weight starts at its configured initial value and, after the lookback plus outcome delay, is adjusted by (rolling directional accuracy − 0.5) × learning rate, clipped to [0.05,0.6]; accuracy compares each past score’s direction with the price move after the outcome delay, ignoring moves below the configured minimum. Normalize weights by their sum.
Calculation: RSI score = RSI-based score: at or below oversold, min(1,(oversold−RSI)/oversold); at or above overbought, −min(1,(RSI−overbought)/(100−overbought)); otherwise (50−RSI)/50 × mid-range multiplier. Multiply by 1.4 on an RSI cross up through oversold or down through overbought, then add 0.08 in the sign of the RSI change if its absolute change exceeds 5; clip to [-1,1].
Calculation: Volume/OBV score = determine raw score by priority: +1 if OBV is above its moving average and its 5-bar slope is positive; −1 if below and slope negative; +0.7 if volume exceeds its moving average times the spike multiplier, close rose, and volume ratio exceeds 2.5; −0.7 for the corresponding falling close. Multiply raw score by min(volume ratio/spike multiplier,1.5) during a volume spike, otherwise by 0.5; clip to [-1,1].
Calculation: VWAP score = clip(-(close−VWAP)/ (20-bar close standard deviation × deviation multiplier), [-1,1]); add 0.2 if close rose and prior close was below VWAP, or subtract 0.2 if close fell and prior close was above VWAP, clipping again. If momentum override is enabled, set score to +min(1,abs(score)×0.8) on a volume-spike VWAP cross up or strong uptrend, and to −min(1,abs(score)×0.8) on the corresponding cross down or strong downtrend. Strong uptrend/downtrend means distance from VWAP exceeds 0.8 deviation in that direction, volume ratio exceeds 1.8, and close moves in that direction.
Calculation: EMA trend score = clip(0.3 × (fast EMA−slow EMA)/14-bar ATR, [-1,1]); add 0.3 on a fast EMA cross above the slow EMA or subtract 0.3 on a cross below; add 0.08 if close is above both EMAs or subtract 0.08 if below both; clip to [-1,1]. The fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: MACD score = clip(2 × MACD histogram/14-bar ATR, [-1,1]); add 0.35 on MACD line crossing above its signal line or subtract 0.35 on a cross below; add 0.15 on MACD line crossing above zero or subtract 0.15 on a cross below; add 0.05 when the positive histogram increases or subtract 0.05 when the negative histogram decreases; clip to [-1,1]. MACD fast length is shorter than slow length.
Calculation: Bollinger score = clip((basis−close)/deviation, [-1,1]), where basis is a moving average and deviation is its lookback standard deviation times the band multiplier. Multiply by 1.3 when band width is below 0.75 times its 50-bar average, then clip. On two consecutive closes above the upper band set score to +min(1,abs(score)×0.6); below the lower band set it to −min(1,abs(score)×0.6). Add 0.25 if prior close was at/below the lower band and close rose; subtract 0.25 if prior close was at/above the upper band and close fell; clip to [-1,1].
Calculation: Volume intelligence score = 0 when disabled; otherwise clip to [-1,1] the sum of: +0.35 for accumulation regime or −0.35 for distribution; 0.25 × clipped(raw delta / (delta standard deviation × 3)); 0.20 × clipped(3-bar slope of the fast cumulative-delta EMA / (14-bar ATR × volume/1000)); +0.10 if delta z-score exceeds its positive threshold or −0.10 if below its negative threshold; +0.10 on a confirmed shift from selling or neutral to buying, or −0.10 on a shift from buying or neutral to selling. Raw delta = volume × (2 × (close−low)/(high−low)−1), using 0.5 as close position when high equals low. Accumulation/distribution is assigned when at least 3 of these 5 conditions hold: fast cumulative-delta EMA above/below its longer-lookback EMA; fast delta EMA above/below its double-length EMA; volume above its moving average with close above/below open; at least 3 consecutive positive/negative raw-delta bars; OBV above/below its moving average. Delta z-score uses its lookback mean and standard deviation. Confirm a regime shift when the regime differs from the established non-neutral regime and has persisted for 2 bars.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = configured signal threshold × clip(1.3 − (14-bar ATR / 50-bar average of 14-bar ATR) × 0.3, [0.80,1.10]) when adaptive thresholds are enabled; otherwise use the configured signal threshold.
Calculation: Chop filter passes if disabled or ADX is at least its minimum.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = hammer (body < 0.35 × range, lower wick > 2 × body, upper wick < 0.5 × body, and prior three closes descending); bullish engulfing (prior candle bearish with body > 0.3 × 14-bar average body, current bullish body > 1.1 × prior body, current open ≤ prior close, current close ≥ prior open, and close two bars ago < close three bars ago); morning star (candle two bars ago bearish with body > 14-bar average body, prior body < 0.5 × average body, current bullish body > 0.7 × average body, and current close above midpoint of candle two bars ago); three white soldiers (three bullish candles each with body > 0.5 × average body, successive opens and closes rising, and each later open ≤ previous close); double bottom (current low within 0.5 ATR of the lowest low over pattern lookback, intervening highest high exceeds that low by 1.5 ATR, the lowest low occurred more than 5 bars and fewer than pattern-lookback bars ago, and close > prior close and open); or bull flag (close 5 bars ago > close 10 bars ago by more than 2 ATR, three-bar declining highs before current bar, current 5-bar close standard deviation < 0.6 × the prior 5-bar close standard deviation, and close > prior high).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = shooting star (body < 0.35 × range, upper wick > 2 × body, lower wick < 0.5 × body, and prior three closes rising); bearish engulfing (prior candle bullish with body > 0.3 × 14-bar average body, current bearish body > 1.1 × prior body, current open ≥ prior close, current close ≤ prior open, and close two bars ago > close three bars ago); evening star (candle two bars ago bullish with body > 14-bar average body, prior body < 0.5 × average body, current bearish body > 0.7 × average body, and current close below midpoint of candle two bars ago); three black crows (three bearish candles each with body > 0.5 × average body, successive opens and closes falling, and each later open ≥ previous close); double top (current high within 0.5 ATR of the highest high over pattern lookback, intervening lowest low is more than 1.5 ATR below that high, the highest high occurred more than 5 bars and fewer than pattern-lookback bars ago, and close < prior close and open); or bear flag (close 5 bars ago < close 10 bars ago by more than 2 ATR, three-bar rising lows before current bar, current 5-bar close standard deviation < 0.6 × the prior 5-bar close standard deviation, and close < prior low).
