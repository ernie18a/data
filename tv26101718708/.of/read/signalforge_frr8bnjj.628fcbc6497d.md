Calculation: Notation = All calculations use chart bars. x[k] is x k bars ago; x[0] is current. Each named threshold, multiplier, length, offset, weight, and toggle is an independent parameter unless a relationship is stated. Clamp(x,a,b) limits x to [a,b]. A crossover requires current left > right and previous left <= previous right; a crossunder reverses the inequalities.
Calculation: Candle measures = Body = abs(close-open); upper shadow = high-max(open,close); lower shadow = min(open,close)-low; range = high-low; average body = SMA(body, body lookback), including the current bar.
Calculation: Estimated delta = volume * (2*(close-low)/(high-low)-1) when high > low, otherwise 0. Delta z-score = (estimated delta - SMA(estimated delta, delta-statistics lookback)) / standard deviation(estimated delta, delta-statistics lookback), or 0 when that standard deviation is 0. Cumulative delta is the running sum of estimated delta.
Calculation: Pressure regime = Count buying votes: EMA(cumulative delta, shorter delta-trend length) > EMA(cumulative delta, cumulative-delta lookback); EMA(estimated delta, delta-smoothing length) > EMA(estimated delta, twice that length); volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) AND close > open; consecutive positive estimated deltas >= consecutive-bar threshold; OBV > SMA(OBV, volume lookback). Count selling votes using the opposite inequalities and consecutive negative deltas. Regime is +1 if buying votes >= regime-vote threshold, otherwise -1 if selling votes >= regime-vote threshold, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Regime shift = Initialize confirmed regime to 0 and regime counter to 0. On each bar, increment the counter if the current pressure regime equals the confirmed regime; otherwise set it to 1. A shift requires counter = confirmation length AND current regime differs from confirmed regime AND current regime != 0; its direction is from the confirmed regime to the current regime. After testing for a shift, set confirmed regime to the current regime if counter >= confirmation length AND current regime != 0. The confirmation length exceeds 1; therefore the shift condition cannot occur under this update order.
Calculation: RSI score = If RSI <= oversold level, use min(1,(oversold level-RSI)/oversold level); else if RSI >= overbought level, use -min(1,(RSI-overbought level)/(100-overbought level)); else use (50-RSI)/50 * midrange multiplier. If RSI crosses above oversold or below overbought, multiply the score by a crossing multiplier and cap the result at 1 or floor it at -1 respectively. Add sign(RSI-RSI[1]) * acceleration increment when abs(RSI-RSI[1]) exceeds the acceleration threshold; otherwise add 0. Clamp to [-1,1].
Calculation: Volume score = Volume average is SMA(volume, volume lookback); volume ratio is volume / volume average; volume spike means volume > volume average * spike multiplier. Use raw score +1 if OBV > SMA(OBV, volume lookback) AND OBV > OBV[OBV-slope offset], else -1 if both inequalities reverse, else +momentum score if volume spike AND close > close[1] AND volume ratio > momentum-volume threshold, else -momentum score if volume spike AND close < close[1] AND volume ratio > momentum-volume threshold, else 0. Multiply raw score by min(volume ratio / spike multiplier, spike cap) on a volume spike, otherwise by nonspike multiplier; clamp to [-1,1].
Calculation: VWAP score = Use session VWAP of (high+low+close)/3; deviation = standard deviation(close, VWAP-deviation lookback) * deviation multiplier. Start with clamp(-(close-VWAP)/deviation,-1,1). If close > close[1] AND close[1] < current VWAP, add returning increment and cap at 1; else if close < close[1] AND close[1] > current VWAP, subtract it and floor at -1. If momentum override is enabled and either close > current VWAP AND close[1] <= current VWAP AND volume spike, or close-VWAP > deviation * trend-distance multiplier AND volume ratio > trend-volume threshold AND close > close[1], replace score with min(1,abs(score)*override multiplier). Then, if momentum override is enabled and either close < current VWAP AND close[1] >= current VWAP AND volume spike, or close-VWAP < -deviation * trend-distance multiplier AND volume ratio > trend-volume threshold AND close < close[1], replace score with max(-1,-abs(score)*override multiplier).
Calculation: EMA score = Clamp ((EMA(close, shorter length)-EMA(close, longer length))/ATR * spread multiplier) to [-1,1]. Add crossover increment and cap at 1 on a shorter-EMA crossover above the longer EMA; otherwise subtract it and floor at -1 on a crossunder. Then add price-position increment and cap at 1 if close is above both EMAs; otherwise subtract it and floor at -1 if close is below both.
Calculation: MACD score = Clamp (MACD histogram / ATR * histogram multiplier) to [-1,1]. Add signal-cross increment and cap at 1 on MACD line crossover above its signal; otherwise subtract it and floor at -1 on a crossunder. Add zero-cross increment and cap at 1 on MACD line crossover above zero; otherwise subtract it and floor at -1 on a crossunder. Add histogram-growth increment and cap at 1 if histogram > 0 AND histogram > histogram[1]; otherwise subtract it and floor at -1 if histogram < 0 AND histogram < histogram[1]. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA length and a signal EMA length.
Calculation: Bollinger score = Basis = SMA(close, band lookback); deviation = standard deviation(close, band lookback) * band multiplier; upper and lower bands = basis +/- deviation; width = (upper-lower)/basis. Start with clamp((basis-close)/deviation,-1,1). If width < SMA(width, width lookback) * squeeze multiplier, multiply score by squeeze boost and clamp to [-1,1]. If close > current upper band AND close[1] > current upper band, replace score with min(1,abs(score)*band-walk multiplier); else if close < current lower band AND close[1] < current lower band, replace it with max(-1,-abs(score)*band-walk multiplier). If close[1] <= current lower band AND close > close[1], add bounce increment and cap at 1; else if close[1] >= current upper band AND close < close[1], subtract it and floor at -1.
Calculation: Volume-intelligence score = If disabled, 0; otherwise clamp to [-1,1] the sum of regime * regime weight; clamp(estimated delta/(delta standard deviation * delta normalization multiplier),-1,1) * delta weight, or 0 if delta standard deviation is 0; clamp(((EMA(cumulative delta, shorter delta-trend length)-its value at a prior slope offset)/slope offset)/(ATR * volume / volume normalization divisor),-1,1) * slope weight, or 0 if ATR is 0; positive anomaly weight if delta z-score > anomaly threshold, negative anomaly weight if delta z-score < -anomaly threshold, otherwise 0; and positive shift weight for a shift to buying, negative shift weight for a shift to selling, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Adaptive composite = For each of the seven scores, start with its own initial weight. Retain a rolling history of scores and closes with capacity equal to accuracy lookback + outcome offset + history padding. For each historical score with a close outcome outcome-offset bars later, count it only if abs(score) > vote threshold AND abs((outcome close - historical close)/historical close) > minimum move fraction; a zero historical close has zero move. Accuracy is the fraction of counted observations whose score and price move have the same sign, or neutral accuracy if none qualify. Once bar index > accuracy lookback + outcome offset, if adaptation is enabled, update each weight every bar to clamp(previous weight + (accuracy-neutral accuracy)*learning rate, minimum weight, maximum weight). Composite is the weighted mean of the seven current scores, clamped to [-1,1].
Calculation: Signal threshold = Base threshold, or, when volatility adaptation is enabled, base threshold * clamp(volatility intercept - ATR/SMA(ATR, longer ATR-average lookback) * volatility coefficient, lower volatility bound, upper volatility bound).
Calculation: Signal history = Initialize the last gated-signal bar index to 0 and direction to 0. After evaluating both gated signals on each bar, if either fires, set the last signal bar to the current bar and direction to +1 for a gated long or -1 for a gated short. Bars since signal is current bar index minus last signal bar.
Long: Composite >= signal threshold AND count of seven scores > positive vote threshold >= minimum agreement AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND bars since signal >= minimum signal gap AND (last gated direction != -1 OR bars since signal >= reversal window multiple * minimum signal gap OR composite >= signal threshold * reversal-strength multiplier).
Short: Composite <= -signal threshold AND count of seven scores < negative vote threshold >= minimum agreement AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND bars since signal >= minimum signal gap AND (last gated direction != +1 OR bars since signal >= reversal window multiple * minimum signal gap OR composite <= -signal threshold * reversal-strength multiplier).
Long: Composite > signal threshold * bar-strength multiplier.
Short: Composite < -signal threshold * bar-strength multiplier.
Long: Any one of the seven scores > dashboard score threshold OR composite > signal threshold OR pressure regime = +1 OR estimated buying volume / volume > buying-pressure threshold, with buying pressure treated as neutral when volume <= 0.
Short: Any one of the seven scores < -dashboard score threshold OR composite < -signal threshold OR pressure regime = -1 OR estimated buying volume / volume < selling-pressure threshold, with buying pressure treated as neutral when volume <= 0.
Long: Delta z-score > anomaly threshold.
Short: Delta z-score < -anomaly threshold.

Calculation: Double-extreme references = Rolling low = lowest low over pattern lookback; low age = bars since the latest bar on which low equaled that bar's rolling low. Rolling high = highest high over pattern lookback; high age = bars since the latest bar on which high equaled that bar's rolling high. Each age is evaluated against the rolling extreme on its own historical bar.
Long: abs(low-rolling low) < ATR * extreme-tolerance multiplier AND highest high over max(1,min(low age,pattern lookback)) bars > rolling low + ATR * bounce multiplier AND low age > minimum separation AND low age < pattern lookback AND close > close[1] AND close > open.
Short: abs(high-rolling high) < ATR * extreme-tolerance multiplier AND lowest low over max(1,min(high age,pattern lookback)) bars < rolling high - ATR * bounce multiplier AND high age > minimum separation AND high age < pattern lookback AND close < close[1] AND close < open.
Long: close[shorter pole offset] > close[longer pole offset] AND close[shorter pole offset]-close[longer pole offset] > ATR * pole multiplier AND high[1] < high[2] AND high[2] < high[3] AND standard deviation(close, flag lookback) < standard deviation(close[flag lookback], flag lookback) * tightness multiplier AND close > high[1].
Short: close[shorter pole offset] < close[longer pole offset] AND close[longer pole offset]-close[shorter pole offset] > ATR * pole multiplier AND low[1] > low[2] AND low[2] > low[3] AND standard deviation(close, flag lookback) < standard deviation(close[flag lookback], flag lookback) * tightness multiplier AND close < low[1].
