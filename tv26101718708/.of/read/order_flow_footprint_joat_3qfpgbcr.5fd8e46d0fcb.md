Calculation: Bar delta = total buy volume minus total sell volume, summed across the lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar. Classify each lower-timeframe bar’s volume as buy if its close is above the previous lower-timeframe close, sell if below, or split equally if equal; for the first lower-timeframe bar, compare its close with its open. Assign volume to the equal-width price row containing the midpoint of that bar’s high and low, using the current chart bar’s high-low range. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable, estimate buy volume as chart-bar volume × (close − low) / (high − low), and sell volume as the remainder; use a 50/50 split when high equals low. The lower timeframe is below the chart timeframe; use its bars within the current chart bar, including any still-forming bar.
Calculation: Row imbalance = buy imbalance when the row’s buy volume is at least the imbalance ratio times the sell volume in the row below; if that sell volume is zero, require positive buy volume and zero sell volume in the row. Sell imbalance is the reverse comparison against buy volume in the row above, with the analogous zero-volume rule. Exclude rows below the minimum row-volume filter; if both sides qualify, assign the side with greater volume, choosing buy on a tie. Do not evaluate imbalances when using the chart-bar fallback.
Calculation: Stacked imbalance = at least the minimum stack count of consecutive rows marked as imbalanced on the same side.
Calculation: Point of Control (POC) = midpoint of the price row with the greatest total volume.
Long: Bar delta > 0 OR a buy imbalance OR a buy stacked imbalance OR buy imbalance count >= burst threshold OR bar delta changes from negative to positive after at least the minimum run length of consecutive negative-delta bars OR POC − previous chart bar’s POC >= shift threshold in ticks.
Short: Bar delta < 0 OR a sell imbalance OR a sell stacked imbalance OR sell imbalance count >= burst threshold OR bar delta changes from positive to negative after at least the minimum run length of consecutive positive-delta bars OR previous chart bar’s POC − POC >= shift threshold in ticks.
