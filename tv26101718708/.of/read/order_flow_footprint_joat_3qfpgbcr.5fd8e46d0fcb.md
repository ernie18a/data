Calculation: Reconstructed row volumes = Divide the chart bar's low-to-high range into an adjustable number of equal rows, ordered low to high. Use all available lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the bar still forming. For each lower-timeframe bar with positive volume, assign all volume to buy if its close exceeds the preceding lower-timeframe close within the chart bar, to sell if it is lower, or half to each if equal. For the first lower-timeframe bar, compare its close with its open instead. Assign that volume to the row containing the midpoint of its high and low, clamping the row index to the valid range. If the chart bar has no range, assign no row volumes.
Calculation: Bar delta = Sum of reconstructed buy volume minus sum of reconstructed sell volume. If there are no lower-timeframe bars or their assigned total volume is zero and chart volume is positive, use chart volume multiplied by (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1); use zero if high equals low.
Calculation: Row imbalance = Evaluate rows only when reconstructed total volume is positive. For each row whose buy plus sell volume is at least an adjustable minimum, a buy candidate requires buy volume at least an adjustable ratio times the sell volume in the row below; if that neighboring sell volume is zero or the row below does not exist, require positive buy volume and zero sell volume instead. A sell candidate requires sell volume at least the same ratio times the buy volume in the row above; if that neighboring buy volume is zero or the row above does not exist, require positive sell volume and zero buy volume instead. If both candidates occur, classify the row as buy when its buy volume is at least its sell volume, otherwise classify it as sell.
Calculation: Point of control = Center price of the row with the greatest reconstructed total volume, choosing the lowest row on a tie. When the chart bar has no range, use an adjustable minimum price increment as the row height.
Long: Bar delta > 0.
Short: Bar delta < 0.
Long: At least one row has a buy imbalance.
Short: At least one row has a sell imbalance.
Long: Point of control − previous bar's point of control > 0 AND point of control − previous bar's point of control >= an adjustable price-distance threshold.
Short: Point of control − previous bar's point of control <= 0 AND absolute point-of-control change >= an adjustable price-distance threshold.
