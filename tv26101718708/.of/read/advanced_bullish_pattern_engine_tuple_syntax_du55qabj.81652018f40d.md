Calculation: Confirmed pivots = highs or lows that are extrema over configurable left and right bar counts, available only after the right-side bars have completed; retain a configurable maximum number of each type, discarding the oldest.
Calculation: Percentage difference(a, b) = abs(a - b) / abs(a), with a positive denominator guard.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > volume SMA × configurable multiplier.
Calculation: Strong trend = close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; healthy trend = close > medium EMA > long EMA.
Calculation: Momentum = RSI >= configurable threshold AND rate of change > 0.
Calculation: Bullish candle = bullish engulfing candle OR bullish hammer-like candle OR strong bullish candle. A bullish engulfing candle has close > open, prior close < prior open, open <= prior close, and close >= prior open. A hammer-like candle has close > open, lower wick > body × configurable multiplier, upper wick <= body, and (close - low) / (high - low) > configurable threshold. A strong bullish candle has close > open, (close - low) / (high - low) > a configurable threshold, and body / (high - low) > a configurable threshold. Body is abs(close - open); guard zero ranges with a positive minimum.
Calculation: Breakout pattern = close > highest high over a configurable lookback ending on the prior bar × configurable breakout multiplier AND close > short EMA AND volume spike.
Calculation: Pullback pattern = strong trend AND abs(close - short EMA) <= ATR AND close > short EMA AND strong bullish candle.
Calculation: Compression expansion pattern = prior bar had close > medium EMA > long EMA, high < its prior high, low > its prior low, range < the lowest range over a configurable lookback ending on its prior bar, and volume < volume SMA; current close > prior high AND current volume spike.
Calculation: Inverse head-and-shoulders pattern = the latest three confirmed pivot lows, ordered left shoulder, head, right shoulder, have percentage difference between shoulders <= configurable tolerance, head below both shoulders, and absolute difference between the two shoulder-to-head bar intervals <= configurable tolerance; the highest confirmed pivot high between each adjacent pair of lows exists; close > the current-bar value of the line through those two highs AND volume spike AND close > short EMA.
Calculation: Cup-and-handle pattern = the latest two confirmed pivot highs are the left and right lips, with percentage difference <= configurable tolerance; the lowest confirmed pivot low between them occurs strictly between the lips; (lower lip - bottom) / lower lip is between configurable minimum and maximum depths; (right lip - lowest low from the right-lip bar through the current bar) / (right lip - bottom) is between zero and a configurable maximum retracement; close > both lips AND volume spike AND close > short EMA. Guard zero denominators with a positive minimum.
Calculation: Double-bottom pattern = percentage difference between the latest two confirmed pivot lows <= configurable tolerance AND close > the highest confirmed pivot high between those lows AND volume spike AND close > short EMA.
Calculation: Bull-flag pattern = healthy trend AND (highest high - close) / (highest high - lowest low) over a configurable lookback including the current bar > configurable minimum pullback AND <= configurable maximum pullback AND close > prior high AND volume spike; the high-low range must exceed a positive minimum.
Calculation: Ascending-triangle pattern = percentage difference between the latest two confirmed pivot highs <= configurable tolerance AND the latest confirmed pivot low > the preceding confirmed pivot low AND close > both pivot highs AND volume spike AND close > short EMA.
Calculation: Selected pattern = the first true pattern in this priority order: inverse head-and-shoulders, cup-and-handle, double bottom, bull flag, ascending triangle, compression expansion, breakout, pullback.
Calculation: Pattern stop = right shoulder low for inverse head-and-shoulders; short EMA for cup-and-handle or breakout; latest pivot low for double bottom or ascending triangle; medium EMA for bull flag or pullback; prior low for compression expansion.
Calculation: Risk pass = (close - pattern stop) / close > 0 AND <= configurable maximum risk fraction.
Calculation: Pattern score = a configurable pattern-specific base plus configurable additive weights: strong trend, momentum, and volume spike for breakout or bull flag; strong trend, momentum, and bullish candle for pullback; healthy trend and volume spike for compression expansion; healthy trend, momentum, and volume spike for inverse head-and-shoulders, cup-and-handle, double bottom, or ascending triangle. Add configurable weights for risk pass and bullish candle, then round to the nearest integer. Shared conditions use the same weight wherever they occur; the healthy-trend weight is distinct from the strong-trend weight.
Long: Inverse head-and-shoulders pattern OR cup-and-handle pattern OR double-bottom pattern OR bull-flag pattern OR ascending-triangle pattern.
Long: Selected pattern exists AND pattern score >= configurable candidate threshold.
