Calculation: Fast, middle, and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows in the configured number of bars on both sides; usable once the right-side bars have completed
Calculation: Bullish pattern = any of: (1) close exceeds the previous lookback-period high times a breakout buffer, is above the fast EMA, and volume exceeds average volume times a volume multiplier; (2) strong uptrend, close within one ATR of and above the fast EMA, and a strong bullish bar; (3) healthy uptrend and a prior inside bar whose range is the narrowest of the configured recent bars and whose volume is below average volume, followed by a close above that inside bar's high with volume above the volume threshold; (4) three successive confirmed pivot lows form shoulders with the middle low below both outer lows, outer lows are within a symmetry tolerance, the two time intervals are within a tolerance, and close breaks above the line joining the highest confirmed pivot highs between the lows, above the fast EMA and with volume above threshold; (5) two successive confirmed pivot highs are within a lip symmetry tolerance, the lowest confirmed pivot low between them forms a cup within configured depth bounds, the subsequent handle retracement is within its configured maximum, and close breaks above both lips and the fast EMA with volume above threshold; (6) two successive confirmed pivot lows are within a low symmetry tolerance and close breaks above the highest confirmed pivot high between them and the fast EMA with volume above threshold; (7) healthy uptrend, close above the middle EMA, pullback from the recent high is within configured bounds, and close exceeds the prior high with volume above threshold; (8) two successive confirmed pivot highs are within a resistance tolerance, the second of two successive confirmed pivot lows is higher than the first, and close breaks above resistance and the fast EMA with volume above threshold
Calculation: Strong bullish bar = close > open, close is in the upper portion of the bar's range, and body exceeds a configured fraction of the range
Calculation: Bullish candle pass = bullish engulfing candle OR bullish hammer-like candle OR strong bullish bar
Calculation: Strong uptrend = close > fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA
Calculation: Healthy uptrend = close > middle EMA > slow EMA
Calculation: Momentum pass = RSI is at least its configured minimum and rate of change is positive
Calculation: Volume pass = volume > average volume × configured volume multiplier
Calculation: Pattern score = winning pattern's pattern-specific score, plus configured bonuses for strong uptrend, momentum pass, and volume pass; add a risk bonus when the positive distance from close to the pattern stop, as a percentage of close, is within the configured maximum risk, and a candle bonus when bullish candle pass holds
Long: Bullish pattern OR a bullish pattern is present and the rounded pattern score is at least the candidate threshold
