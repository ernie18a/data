Calculation: Active consolidation zone = on each chart-timeframe bar, scan lookbacks from longest to shortest and select the first window whose (highest high − lowest low) / midpoint × 100 is at most the tightness threshold; its highest high, lowest low, and average volume are the zone top, bottom, and average volume. While active, test breakouts against the zone established before the current bar; if none occurs, update the zone when a qualifying window exists. After a breakout, block new zone formation for the cooldown period. If no qualifying window exists and an active zone has gone more than the maximum lookback without an update, clear it.
Long: Active zone AND close > zone top AND volume ≥ zone average volume × breakout volume multiple
Short: Active zone AND close < zone bottom AND volume ≥ zone average volume × breakout volume multiple
