Calculation: Pivot high or low = a high or low confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on each side; use separate, shorter confirmation lengths for oscillator pivots than for price pivots.
Calculation: Active bullish order block = on a confirmed price pivot low, if the current close exceeds the high at that pivot, store the low and high of the bar immediately preceding the pivot; remain active until close falls below the stored low. Active bearish order block = on a confirmed price pivot high, if close is below the low at that pivot, store the preceding bar's high and low; remain active until close exceeds the stored high. A new block replaces the previous block of its side.
Calculation: Structure state = initially neutral; on a confirmed pivot high, set bullish if it exceeds the last recorded higher high or no higher high exists, otherwise bearish; update the recorded higher high only when the new high exceeds it or none exists. Then, on a confirmed pivot low, set bearish if it is below the last recorded lower low or none exists, otherwise bullish; update the recorded lower low only when the new low is below it or none exists.
Calculation: Equilibrium = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback.
Calculation: Normalized series = (series minus its rolling minimum) divided by its rolling maximum minus its rolling minimum, scaled to 0–100; centered normalization subtracts 50.
Calculation: Volume flow input = sum over a lookback of capped volume signed positive when the change in typical price exceeds a cutoff, negative when it is below the negative cutoff, otherwise zero; divide by the previous bar's average volume and multiply by a scale. Cap volume at a multiple of that previous average; cutoff is a multiplier times close times the standard deviation of changes in log typical price.
Calculation: Laguerre momentum = recursively calculate L0 = (1 − gamma) × close + gamma × previous L0; L1 = −gamma × L0 + previous L0 + gamma × previous L1; L2 and L3 follow the same recurrence using the preceding level. Initialize previous levels to zero. Sum positive successive level differences as upward movement and negative successive level differences as downward movement, floor each sum at a small positive constant, and scale upward movement divided by total movement around zero.
Calculation: Composite momentum = EMA of a weighted sum of volume flow input, Laguerre momentum, EMA of the hyperbolic tangent of a scaled average of those two inputs, TSI, centered MFI, centered normalized OBV, centered normalized EMA of the accumulation/distribution line, and CMF; each component has its own scaling or weight.
Calculation: Wave = close minus close a momentum lookback ago; smooth it with five EMAs ordered from shortest to longest. The first four bases are averages of adjacent EMAs; the fifth is the average of the longest and shortest. Spread is first base minus fifth base. Wave upper and lower bands are an SMA of the mean of the five bases plus or minus a multiple of its standard deviation.
Calculation: All-wave bullish = wave exceeds each of the five bases; all-wave bearish = wave is less than or equal to each of the five bases. Extreme bullish = first base and wave both exceed the upper wave band AND wave exceeds its value a lookback ago; extreme bearish = first base and wave both fall below the lower wave band AND wave is below its value a lookback ago.
Calculation: Volume surge = volume exceeds a multiple of its SMA. Liquidity high and low are the most recently confirmed price pivot high and low respectively.
Calculation: Trend direction = initially bearish; set bullish when close exceeds current bar midpoint plus an ATR multiple, set bearish when close is below current midpoint minus that multiple, otherwise retain the previous direction. Rising or falling means the current value is respectively greater or less than every value in its preceding lookback.
Calculation: Divergence state = retain separate bullish and bearish flags and one shared confluence count. When price and RSI pivot lows are confirmed on the same bar, set the bullish flag if the price pivot is lower than the previous confirmed price pivot low and the RSI pivot is higher than the previous confirmed RSI pivot low; otherwise clear it. When price and RSI pivot highs are confirmed on the same bar, set the bearish flag if the price pivot is higher than the previous confirmed price pivot high and the RSI pivot is lower than the previous confirmed RSI pivot high; otherwise clear it. On either qualifying divergence, reset the shared count to the number of these comparisons satisfied: the RSI pivot comparison, current MACD line versus its value an oscillator-pivot lookback ago, TSI versus its value that many bars ago, and SMA-smoothed stochastic versus its value that many bars ago; use greater-than for bullish and less-than for bearish. Process highs after lows. Flags and count persist until updated.
Calculation: Composite oscillator = RSI minus its midpoint plus MACD line plus TSI plus SMA-smoothed stochastic minus its midpoint.
Calculation: Volatility index = average of rolling 0–100 normalizations of EMA-smoothed ATR, Bollinger Band width as a percentage of its middle band, and standard deviation of log close returns scaled by a constant. A squeeze occurs when both Bollinger Bands lie strictly inside Keltner Channels constructed from EMA close plus or minus an ATR multiple; a squeeze breakout occurs when the preceding bar was in a squeeze and the current bar is not.
Calculation: Flow index = average of scaled CMF, centered MFI, centered normalized OBV, and centered normalized EMA of the accumulation/distribution line.
Calculation: Bullish system conditions = (1) composite momentum above zero and its preceding value; (2) active bullish order block OR low above the high two bars ago, AND bullish structure state AND close below equilibrium; (3) first wave base above second base and above every value in its rising lookback AND spread above a positive threshold; (4) low below liquidity low AND volume surge; (5) rising Hull MA AND bullish trend direction AND ADX above a threshold AND short EMA above medium EMA above long EMA; (6) bullish divergence flag AND shared confluence count at least a threshold AND composite oscillator below a negative threshold; (7) squeeze breakout AND close above previous close AND volatility index below a threshold; (8) flow index above a positive threshold AND CMF above zero AND MFI above its midpoint. Parenthesize the order-block OR gap in condition 2.
Calculation: Bearish system conditions = (1) composite momentum below zero and its preceding value; (2) active bearish order block OR high below the low two bars ago, AND bearish structure state AND close above equilibrium; (3) first wave base below second base and below every value in its falling lookback AND spread below a negative threshold; (4) high above liquidity high AND volume surge; (5) falling Hull MA AND bearish trend direction AND ADX above a threshold AND short EMA below medium EMA below long EMA; (6) bearish divergence flag AND shared confluence count at least a threshold AND composite oscillator above a positive threshold; (7) squeeze breakout AND close below previous close AND volatility index below a threshold; (8) flow index below a negative threshold AND CMF below zero AND MFI below its midpoint. Parenthesize the order-block OR gap in condition 2.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish counts = number of satisfied bullish and bearish system conditions respectively, counted independently. Net system score = sum across systems of +1 for its bullish condition, otherwise −1 for its bearish condition, otherwise zero; for the wave system additionally treat all-wave bullish as bullish and all-wave bearish as bearish, with bullish precedence.
Calculation: Bullish score = bullish count plus one each for extreme bullish, all-wave bullish, maximum divergence confluence count, and volume surge with flow index above a positive bonus threshold, plus the positive part of net system score. Bearish score = bearish count plus one each for extreme bearish, all-wave bearish, maximum divergence confluence count, and volume surge with flow index below a negative bonus threshold, plus the absolute value of the negative part of net system score. The maximum-confluence bonus applies to both sides regardless of divergence direction.
Long: Bullish count >= minimum AND bullish count > bearish count, when count mode is selected.
Short: Bearish count >= minimum AND bearish count > bullish count, when count mode is selected.
Long: Bullish score >= minimum AND bullish score > bearish score, when confluence mode is selected.
Short: Bearish score >= minimum AND bearish score > bullish score, when confluence mode is selected.
Long: Bullish order block is active OR low > high two bars ago.
Short: Bearish order block is active OR high < low two bars ago.
Long: Hull MA is rising OR trend direction is bullish.
Short: Hull MA is falling OR trend direction is bearish.
Exit: long, an active entry-signal stop is reached at or below the signal bar's close minus its ATR times the stop multiplier OR its target is reached at or above that close plus the same stop distance times the reward-to-risk multiplier.
Exit: short, an active entry-signal stop is reached at or above the signal bar's close plus its ATR times the stop multiplier OR its target is reached at or below that close minus the same stop distance times the reward-to-risk multiplier.
Exit: long, trailing is enabled AND price reaches or falls below the most recently submitted trailing stop, recalculated each bar as that bar's close minus its ATR times the trailing multiplier.
Exit: short, trailing is enabled AND price reaches or exceeds the most recently submitted trailing stop, recalculated each bar as that bar's close plus its ATR times the trailing multiplier.
Exit: long, short entry condition is satisfied.
Exit: short, long entry condition is satisfied.
