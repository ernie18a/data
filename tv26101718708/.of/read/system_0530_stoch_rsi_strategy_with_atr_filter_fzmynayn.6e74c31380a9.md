Calculation: Stochastic RSI %K = SMA of 100 × (RSI of close − lowest RSI) / (highest RSI − lowest RSI) over the stochastic lookback; %D = SMA of %K. Use the same RSI, stochastic, %K smoothing, and %D smoothing parameters on both timeframes. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Entry wait state = while flat, check the previous chart bar for a %K/%D cross and its %K trigger level. An eligible long cross starts or restarts a long wait at age 1 and cancels the short wait; otherwise an eligible short cross starts or restarts a short wait at age 1 and cancels the long wait. A cross is eligible if the same-direction cooldown is disabled or enough chart bars have elapsed since the last same-direction entry. If neither trigger condition occurs, increment the age of an existing wait. If a trigger condition occurs but fails its cooldown, leave the existing wait and age unchanged. Cancel the wait when its age exceeds the wait window, upon entry, or while positioned.
Calculation: Pending take-profit state = while positioned, set the side-specific pending state when chart %K crosses against the position's direction. Clear it after a take-profit, a stop, or a new entry. A pending state can confirm on the same bar it is set.
Calculation: Take-profit quantity = 50% of the position on the first take-profit of a trade and the entire remaining position on the next; reset on a new trade. A stop exits the entire position and takes priority over take-profit on the same bar.
Long: Position is flat AND long wait age is within the wait window AND higher-timeframe %K > %D AND higher-timeframe %K < long confirmation threshold AND (same-direction cooldown is disabled OR bars since last long entry >= cooldown).
Short: Position is flat AND short wait age is within the wait window AND higher-timeframe %K < %D AND higher-timeframe %K > short confirmation threshold AND (same-direction cooldown is disabled OR bars since last short entry >= cooldown).
Exit: long, close < entry bar low.
Exit: short, close > entry bar high.
Exit: long, close >= entry bar low AND (chart %K > long extreme threshold OR higher-timeframe %K > long extreme threshold OR (pending long take-profit state = true AND higher-timeframe %K < %K of the preceding higher-timeframe bar)); exit the take-profit quantity.
Exit: short, close <= entry bar high AND (chart %K < short extreme threshold OR higher-timeframe %K < short extreme threshold OR (pending short take-profit state = true AND higher-timeframe %K > %K of the preceding higher-timeframe bar)); exit the take-profit quantity.
