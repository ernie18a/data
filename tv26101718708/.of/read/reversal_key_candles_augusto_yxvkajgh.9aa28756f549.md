Calculation: Confirmed oscillator divergence = for each enabled oscillator (RSI of a selected bar-data source, OBV, MACD histogram of close, or momentum of close), compare its newly confirmed pivot with its immediately preceding confirmed pivot of the same type. A pivot has configurable numbers of bars on its left and right; it is available only after the right-side bars have elapsed. The pivot spacing must be between configurable minimum and maximum bounds. Bullish divergence requires a higher oscillator pivot low AND a lower price low at the corresponding pivot bars. Bearish divergence requires a lower oscillator pivot high AND a higher price high at the corresponding pivot bars. All oscillators use the same pivot and spacing parameters. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA length and a signal EMA length.
Calculation: Candle quantities = body = abs(close − open), range = high − low, upper wick = high − max(open, close), lower wick = min(open, close) − low, safe range = max(range, a small positive floor). Subscripts 1 and 2 refer to the preceding and second-preceding bars. All candle proportions below use their respective bar's safe range.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = any enabled pattern: lower wick / safe range >= hammer range threshold AND lower wick >= body × wick multiplier AND upper wick <= body × small-wick multiplier; OR close >= open AND upper wick >= body × wick multiplier AND lower wick <= body × small-wick multiplier AND upper wick / safe range >= inverted-hammer threshold; OR close >= open AND close1 < open1 AND close > open1 AND open < close1 AND body > body1 × engulfing multiplier; OR lower wick / safe range >= pin-bar long-wick threshold AND upper wick / safe range <= pin-bar short-wick threshold; OR lower wick / safe range >= doji long-wick threshold AND body / safe range <= doji wick-pattern body threshold; OR body / safe range <= doji body threshold AND range > 0; OR close2 < open2 AND body2 / safe range2 >= star first-body threshold AND body1 / safe range1 <= star middle-body threshold AND close >= open AND close >= (open2 + close2) / 2; OR bullish key reversal. The inverted-hammer, pin-bar and doji wick-pattern long-wick thresholds are distinct, with the doji threshold greater than the pin-bar threshold, which is greater than the inverted-hammer threshold. The hammer range threshold is greater than the inverted-hammer threshold and less than the pin-bar threshold.
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = any enabled pattern: upper wick / safe range >= hammer range threshold AND upper wick >= body × wick multiplier AND lower wick <= body × small-wick multiplier; OR close <= open AND close1 > open1 AND close < open1 AND open > close1 AND body > body1 × engulfing multiplier; OR upper wick / safe range >= pin-bar long-wick threshold AND lower wick / safe range <= pin-bar short-wick threshold; OR upper wick / safe range >= doji long-wick threshold AND body / safe range <= doji wick-pattern body threshold; OR body / safe range <= doji body threshold AND range > 0; OR close2 > open2 AND body2 / safe range2 >= star first-body threshold AND body1 / safe range1 <= star middle-body threshold AND close <= open AND close <= (open2 + close2) / 2; OR bearish key reversal.
Calculation: Bullish key reversal = low < low1 AND close > close1 AND lower wick / safe range >= key-reversal wick threshold AND (close − low) / safe range >= key-reversal close-location threshold. Bearish key reversal = high > high1 AND close < close1 AND upper wick / safe range >= key-reversal wick threshold AND (high − close) / safe range >= key-reversal close-location threshold. The close-location threshold equals the small-wick multiplier; the wick threshold is below the inverted-hammer threshold.
Calculation: Divergence confluence score for each direction = sum of five binary points: at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators have confirmed divergence in that direction; the candle filter is disabled OR an enabled matching candle pattern occurs at an offset from the current bar between the pivot right-side length and that length plus the pattern search window; the volume filter is disabled OR volume exceeds its selected SMA, EMA, WMA or RMA on any bar from the current bar through the pivot right-side length plus two bars ago; the Fibonacci filter is disabled OR window relative volume is at least its threshold OR the pivot-bar low for bullish divergence or high for bearish divergence is within a configured fraction of pivot-bar close of a Fibonacci retracement level; window relative volume is enabled AND at least its threshold. Disabled candle, volume and Fibonacci filters each contribute one point. Window relative volume = maximum volume from the current bar through the pivot right-side length plus two bars ago divided by the selected volume average at the pivot offset, or zero if that average is not positive. Fibonacci levels are the latest independently confirmed pivot low and pivot high of price and the standard Fibonacci retracement levels between them; their pivot uses equal left and right lengths derived from a separate configurable lookback. Use the latest levels available on the divergence confirmation bar.
Long: RSI bullish divergence is confirmed AND RSI divergence is enabled; OR the number of enabled oscillators with confirmed bullish divergence >= its minimum AND bullish divergence confluence score >= its minimum; OR bullish key reversal is enabled AND bullish key reversal holds AND current volume / current selected volume average >= the standalone relative-volume threshold AND current selected volume average > 0.
Short: RSI bearish divergence is confirmed AND RSI divergence is enabled; OR the number of enabled oscillators with confirmed bearish divergence >= its minimum AND bearish divergence confluence score >= its minimum; OR bearish key reversal is enabled AND bearish key reversal holds AND current volume / current selected volume average >= the standalone relative-volume threshold AND current selected volume average > 0.
