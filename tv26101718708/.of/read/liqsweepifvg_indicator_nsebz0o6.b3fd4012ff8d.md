Calculation: Active session liquidity levels = the high and low of each completed NY, London, or Asia session; keep each level live until first touched, and retain only the configured number of newest untouched levels of each type.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two chart bars earlier, with gap size at least the minimum; its boundaries are current low (top) and that earlier high (bottom).
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two chart bars earlier, with gap size at least the minimum; its boundaries are that earlier low (top) and current high (bottom).
Calculation: Equal highs/lows = a later high/low within the configured tolerance of a one-bar-left, one-bar-right swing high/low, after the configured minimum bar gap; EQH/EQL raid levels are their outer extreme plus/minus the tolerance.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVG = a 3-bar FVG on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using only confirmed bars; it is live until a chart-timeframe close passes its far edge.
Long: low <= an active session low - raid buffer OR (equal-low raid arming enabled AND low < an active EQL raid level) AND the corresponding low raid is enabled AND, before its expiration, (selected iFVG mode AND close > the top of a live bearish FVG formed no earlier than the configured lookback before the newest applicable raid OR selected return-through-level mode AND close > the raided level) AND (higher-timeframe FVG filter disabled OR price overlapped a live higher-timeframe FVG at any time from raid through trigger) AND trigger occurs within the entry window.
Short: high >= an active session high + raid buffer OR (equal-high raid arming enabled AND high > an active EQH raid level) AND the corresponding high raid is enabled AND, before its expiration, (selected iFVG mode AND close < the bottom of a live bullish FVG formed no earlier than the configured lookback before the newest applicable raid OR selected return-through-level mode AND close < the raided level) AND (higher-timeframe FVG filter disabled OR price overlapped a live higher-timeframe FVG at any time from raid through trigger) AND trigger occurs within the entry window.
