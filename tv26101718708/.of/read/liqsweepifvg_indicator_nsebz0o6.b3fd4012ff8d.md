Calculation: Session levels = For each enabled session in America/New_York, record its high and low when it ends. Keep the configured number of most recent untouched levels for each session and side, also subject to the session-history limit. A level is removed on its first touch: high >= a high level or low <= a low level. Session levels exist only when level tracking is enabled.
Calculation: Equal-level liquidity = Confirm a swing high when the preceding bar is the most recent highest high of the current and preceding two bars; confirm a swing low analogously. For each unreferenced swing, compare the preceding bar's corresponding extreme with the swing price: consume it on a break beyond the tolerance, or on a match within the tolerance when the number of bars since confirmation is greater than the minimum gap. For matches on the same bar, use the most recent swing to create an active line from the swing to the matched extreme; extend an existing line if its endpoint is that swing. Retain active lines up to the configured capacity. A high line is swept when the preceding bar's high > the greater endpoint price + tolerance; a low line is swept when the preceding bar's low < the lesser endpoint price - tolerance. Remove a swept line and, if equal-level arming is enabled, create an arm at the greater high-line endpoint or lesser low-line endpoint on the current bar.
Calculation: Fair value gap = A bullish gap forms when current low > high two bars earlier and their difference >= minimum gap size; its top is current low and bottom is the earlier high. A bearish gap forms when current high < low two bars earlier and their difference >= minimum gap size; its top is the earlier low and bottom is current high. Keep gaps up to the configured capacity. After trigger evaluation, remove a live bullish gap when a later bar's close < its bottom, or low < its bottom if wick-fill mode is selected; remove a bearish gap when a later bar's close > its top, or high > its top in wick-fill mode. An inverted gap cannot trigger again and remains in the capacity-limited collection for its configured retention period.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gap interaction = Form gaps using the same gap definition and minimum size on the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Keep gaps up to the configured capacity. Before testing interaction, remove a bullish gap when the chart-bar close < its bottom and a bearish gap when the chart-bar close > its top. Interaction occurs when chart-bar low <= a remaining gap's top AND chart-bar high >= its bottom.
Calculation: Reversal arm = On the first touch of an enabled session level whose session permits arming, create a short arm when high >= the high level + raid buffer, or a long arm when low <= the low level - raid buffer. Also create an arm from an enabled equal-level sweep. Store its direction, level, creation bar, expiration time, and whether higher-timeframe gap interaction has occurred. Expire it when current bar time > creation time + trigger window; otherwise record any higher-timeframe gap interaction from creation through the current bar. A trigger clears all arms of its direction, including when it occurs outside the entry window.
Calculation: Eligible inversion = Among live chart-timeframe gaps formed no earlier than the newest unexpired same-direction arm's creation bar minus the gap lookback, choose the newest gap whose far edge the current close crosses: close > the top of a bearish gap for a long arm, or close < the bottom of a bullish gap for a short arm. Evaluate inversion before gap removal.
Long: An unexpired long arm exists AND (higher-timeframe filtering is disabled OR at least one unexpired long arm has recorded higher-timeframe gap interaction) AND ((inversion mode is selected AND an eligible inversion exists) OR (return mode is selected AND close > the level of any unexpired long arm)).
Short: An unexpired short arm exists AND (higher-timeframe filtering is disabled OR at least one unexpired short arm has recorded higher-timeframe gap interaction) AND ((inversion mode is selected AND an eligible inversion exists) OR (return mode is selected AND close < the level of any unexpired short arm)).
