Calculation: Bollinger Band Width = 100 × (upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band, using close.
Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum = EMA of (close rate of change × volume / max(SMA of volume, 1)).
Calculation: DCA trigger for pending level n = initial-entry signal close × (1 − level drop / 100), where n starts at 1 and level drop is either first-level drop + (n − 1) × linear increment or first-level drop × geometric multiplier^(n − 1).
Calculation: Cycle low = lowest low since the previous entry signal, excluding that signal bar; reset after each entry signal. Bars since cycle low resets to zero on a strictly lower low and otherwise increments by one.
Calculation: DCA rebound armed = true after the cycle low is at or below both the pending DCA trigger and the previous entry signal close reduced by the minimum gap percentage; remains true until the next entry signal or position closure.
Calculation: Average entry price = quantity-weighted average of actual entry fill prices; each entry's quantity is min(base amount × (1 + amount increment × level), per-entry cap) / signal close, with initial-entry level zero.
Calculation: Breakeven armed = true after the number of DCA entry signals reaches the rescue threshold and close reaches the average entry price increased by the breakeven arm percentage; remains true until position closure.
Long: No active entry cycle AND close > 0 AND close <= highest high over the swing lookback, including the current bar, reduced by the initial drop percentage AND ((Bollinger Band Width < its threshold AND ADX < its lateral threshold) OR, when permissive lateral mode is selected, (Bollinger Band Width < its threshold OR ADX < its lateral threshold)).
Long: Trend re-entry enabled AND no active entry cycle AND a position has closed since the last initial entry AND close > 0 AND Supertrend is bullish AND ADX >= its trend threshold AND +DI > −DI AND close > EMA of close AND close > close at the trend-slope lookback AND ((Bollinger Band Width >= its threshold OR ADX >= its lateral threshold) in strict lateral mode OR (Bollinger Band Width >= its threshold AND ADX >= its lateral threshold) in permissive lateral mode).
Long: Pending DCA level <= maximum DCA levels AND no entry signal has occurred on this bar AND ((rebound mode disabled AND close <= pending DCA trigger) OR (rebound mode enabled AND DCA rebound armed AND bars since cycle low >= confirmation bars AND close >= cycle low increased by the rebound percentage AND (green candle requirement disabled OR close > open))) AND ((Bollinger Band Width < its threshold AND ADX < its lateral threshold) OR, when permissive lateral mode is selected, (Bollinger Band Width < its threshold OR ADX < its lateral threshold)).
Exit: long, close > average entry price increased by the minimum profit percentage AND ((volume-weighted momentum has declined strictly for the required consecutive bars AND its value that many bars ago > momentum threshold) OR (Supertrend safety exit enabled AND Supertrend flips from bullish to bearish)).
Exit: long, breakeven armed AND close <= average entry price.
