Calculation: Common notation = All calculations use chart bars unless identified otherwise. Fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA. VWAP is session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3. Body fraction is abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when the range is zero. Crosses use the current and previous values of both operands. A new entry replaces the tracked trade, including on an exit bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = Close compared with VWAP on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe. Higher-timeframe structure = Close compared with VWAP and fast EMA compared with slow EMA on the last completed bar of a second, lower higher timeframe; bullish requires both comparisons to be above, bearish requires both below, and otherwise it is mixed.
Calculation: Opening range = The running high and low of chart bars within the configured regular-session opening window; lock both levels when that window ends and reset them on each new session day. An opening-range break requires a close crossing the locked high upward or locked low downward during regular market hours.
Calculation: Session volume profile = Divide a fixed percentage range around the regular-session opening price into equal bins. Add each regular-session chart bar's volume to the bin containing its typical price, clamping out-of-range prices to the edge bins. After the configured elapsed session time, the point of control is the center of the highest-volume bin, breaking ties toward the lower bin. Starting there, add the larger adjacent bin's volume, choosing the upper bin on ties, until the configured fraction of total volume is included; value-area high and low are the outer edges of the included bins. Reset at each regular-session open.
Calculation: Reclaim path = Close is above VWAP and the fast EMA, and the number of bars since close crossed above VWAP is less than the reclaim window. Rejection path is the mirrored condition. Pullback path = Close is above VWAP and the fast EMA, close is no more than an ATR multiple above the fast EMA, and a low was at or below the fast EMA, slow EMA, or VWAP within the pullback lookback including the current bar; mirror for short. Momentum path = Close is above VWAP and fast EMA, and fast EMA is above slow EMA; mirror for short.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = Sum configured weights for aligned higher-timeframe bias, aligned higher-timeframe structure, volume at or above a strong-volume multiple of its moving average, ADX at or above its score threshold, RSI within the side's configured interval, and close beyond the slow EMA in the trade direction. Add a weight when close is within an ATR-scaled distance above value-area low for long or below value-area high for short; subtract a weight when value-area high or point of control is within that distance above close for long, or value-area low or point of control is within that distance below close for short. These profile components require a valid session profile. Add the full path weight for an enabled reclaim or qualifying opening-range break, otherwise half that weight for an enabled pullback with the less restrictive directional body condition. Add the configured session-timing weight in the prime window and subtract it in the lunch or power window. Add a directional-volume weight when volume exceeds a separate multiple of its average and close is higher than the preceding close for long or lower for short. Clamp the result to configured lower and upper bounds.
Calculation: Raw main entry = The selected side's higher-timeframe bias is aligned, or is not opposed when strict bias is disabled; higher-timeframe structure is not opposed unless its filter is disabled; the avoid regime is absent, meaning ADX is not below its avoid threshold while structure is mixed; and enabled volume, near-VWAP chop, minimum ADX and RSI-extreme filters pass. The candle closes in the trade direction relative to both open and previous close, with body fraction at least the entry threshold. In addition, either an enabled reclaim or opening-range break passes the chart trigger, an enabled pullback passes the chart trigger, or enabled momentum passes its own trigger. The chart trigger requires close beyond the fast EMA, fast EMA beyond slow EMA, and close beyond VWAP in the trade direction; an opening-range break may optionally substitute close beyond VWAP. The separate EMA trend filter applies to reclaim and pullback and may be disabled or relaxed to allow close beyond VWAP. An opening-range break may optionally require ADX at or above its trend threshold and non-mixed higher-timeframe structure.
Calculation: Raw countertrend entry = For long, close is below VWAP by no more than an ATR multiple, ADX is below its countertrend ceiling, RSI is below its oversold threshold and above its value at a configured earlier bar, fast EMA is above its value at that earlier bar, low is above its value at another configured earlier bar, close is above open, body fraction is at least the entry threshold, volume is at least the strong-volume multiple of its average, higher-timeframe structure is not bearish, and the avoid regime is absent. Mirror all directional comparisons for short, using the overbought RSI threshold.
Calculation: Final entry = Raw main or raw countertrend entry passes the configured entry-volume filter. Apply the confluence threshold unless its gate is disabled or countertrend is the selected path. Select pullback before reclaim or opening-range break, then momentum, then countertrend when several paths qualify. If both sides have raw entries, long additionally requires positive directional movement greater than negative directional movement, and short requires the reverse; equal values permit neither.
Long: Final long entry = true
Short: Final short entry = true
Long: No final entry on either side AND no active trade AND the avoid regime is absent AND higher-timeframe bias is bullish AND close > VWAP AND enabled watch filters pass: fast EMA > slow EMA, volume >= its moving average times the watch volume multiplier, higher-timeframe structure is not bearish, and ADX >= its watch minimum AND, when the chop filter is enabled, abs(close - VWAP) > ATR times the chop multiplier
Short: No final entry on either side AND no active trade AND the avoid regime is absent AND higher-timeframe bias is bearish AND close < VWAP AND enabled watch filters pass: fast EMA < slow EMA, volume >= its moving average times the watch volume multiplier, higher-timeframe structure is not bullish, and ADX >= its watch minimum AND, when the chop filter is enabled, abs(close - VWAP) > ATR times the chop multiplier
Long: No final entry on either side AND no active trade AND the avoid regime is absent AND close < VWAP AND VWAP - close <= ATR times the countertrend distance multiplier AND ADX < the countertrend ceiling AND RSI < its watch oversold threshold AND RSI > RSI at the configured earlier bar AND fast EMA > fast EMA at that earlier bar
Short: No final entry on either side AND no active trade AND the avoid regime is absent AND close > VWAP AND close - VWAP <= ATR times the countertrend distance multiplier AND ADX < the countertrend ceiling AND RSI > its watch overbought threshold AND RSI < RSI at the configured earlier bar AND fast EMA < fast EMA at that earlier bar AND the long countertrend watch condition is false
Calculation: Signal stop = At a long entry, set stop to the entry-bar low, or to the lesser of that low and entry close minus an ATR multiple when the ATR option is enabled. At a short entry, use the entry-bar high, or the greater of that high and entry close plus the ATR multiple. After the configured delay, ratchet the long stop upward to the slow EMA or the short stop downward to the slow EMA. Chart exit markers compare against the stop before the current bar's ratchet; the trade-state exit also compares against the updated stop.
Exit: long, close <= the prior signal stop OR close <= the lowest low over the swing exit lookback including the current bar OR bars since entry >= the soft-exit delay AND at least two of close < entry-bar low, close < fast EMA, and close < VWAP; exclude the VWAP comparison and double the delay for a countertrend entry
Exit: short, close >= the prior signal stop OR close >= the highest high over the swing exit lookback including the current bar OR bars since entry >= the soft-exit delay AND at least two of close > entry-bar high, close > fast EMA, and close > VWAP; exclude the VWAP comparison and double the delay for a countertrend entry
Calculation: Mentor targets = Recalculate each bar. On a selected higher timeframe use the last completed regular-session bar's rolling swing high and low. Form three directional Fibonacci extensions of that range beyond the swing high for long or below the swing low for short. Keep distinct levels farther than the configured minimum distance from entry, ordered nearest first; use them if the nearest is within the configured ATR cap, filling missing targets with progressively larger ATR offsets from entry. Otherwise choose the three nearest distinct qualifying levels from opening-range high, low and midpoint; prior completed regular-session daily high, low, close and open; current regular-session high and low; chart-timeframe rolling swing high and low; VWAP; both EMAs; valid session-profile levels when enabled; and configured whole and optional half-price increments around entry. Fill missing levels with progressively larger ATR offsets. The minimum distance is the maximum of configured price and ATR floors and the configured level-deduplication tolerance.
Calculation: Mentor stop = Initialize from the previous completed regular-session bar on a higher timeframe when its low is below long entry or its high is above short entry; otherwise use entry minus or plus an ATR distance scaled by an ADX-dependent multiplier. When the higher-timeframe structural-stop option is enabled, ratchet its long low upward after a newly completed higher-timeframe close rises from the preceding close, or its short high downward after the close falls, provided the wick remains on the entry side. Ratchet the mentor stop toward profit using that structural stop or the current ATR fallback. Before target one is touched, constrain it to remain a configured minimum distance beyond entry. After target one is touched on a bar at least one bar after entry, ratchet the long stop to at least entry and toward the lesser of slow EMA and close minus an ATR offset; ratchet the short stop toward the greater of slow EMA and close plus an ATR offset, subject to its configured minimum price increment above entry.
Exit: long, bars since entry >= mentor buffer AND (close <= mentor stop OR (entry - close) / ATR >= the hard-failure threshold AND higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND volume > its moving average times the surge multiplier AND close < previous close OR close < opening-range high AND high > opening-range high AND close < target one OR higher-timeframe bias is not bullish AND higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND close < VWAP AND close >= target one OR volume > its moving average times the surge multiplier AND close < previous close AND high < previous high AND close >= target one OR RSI >= its extreme threshold OR close >= target three OR close >= target two AND (RSI >= its exhaustion threshold OR close > close at the divergence lookback AND RSI < RSI at that lookback OR current time is in the power window))
Exit: short, bars since entry >= mentor buffer AND (close >= mentor stop OR (close - entry) / ATR >= the hard-failure threshold AND higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND volume > its moving average times the surge multiplier AND close > previous close OR close > opening-range low AND low < opening-range low AND close > target one OR higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND close > VWAP AND close <= target one OR volume > its moving average times the surge multiplier AND close > previous close AND low > previous low AND close <= target one OR RSI <= its extreme threshold OR close <= target three OR close <= target two AND (RSI <= its exhaustion threshold OR close < close at the divergence lookback AND RSI > RSI at that lookback OR current time is in the power window))
