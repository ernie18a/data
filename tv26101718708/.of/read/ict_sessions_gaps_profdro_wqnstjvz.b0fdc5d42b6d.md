Calculation: Gap = on the first chart bar of a new gap-timeframe bar, compare the last completed gap-timeframe bar with the bar two gap-timeframe bars earlier. For a higher timeframe use completed bars; for a lower timeframe use the first intrabar available to the chart bar. A bullish gap exists when the later low exceeds the earlier high, with bottom equal to the earlier high and top equal to the later low. A bearish gap exists when the later high is below the earlier low, with bottom equal to the later high and top equal to the earlier low.
Calculation: Active gap = a formed gap retained until it is inverted, expires after its configurable lifetime, is removed by an overlapping same-direction gap on a longer designated timeframe, or is paired with an active opposite-direction inverse gap on the same timeframe whose range overlaps it. Pairing removes both gaps. Process overlap removal before inversion, inversion before expiry, and expiry before pairing.
Calculation: Active inverse gap = a gap with its original bounds and reversed direction created when an active gap is inverted. Remove it when the applicable close is below its bottom if bullish or above its top if bearish, when its configurable lifetime since inversion expires, when an overlapping same-direction inverse gap exists on a longer designated timeframe, or when it is paired with an active opposite-direction gap on the same timeframe. Process overlap removal before inverse-gap invalidation, invalidation before expiry, and expiry before pairing.
Calculation: Applicable close = the last completed gap-timeframe close when the gap timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe; otherwise the chart close.
Long: A bullish gap forms.
Short: A bearish gap forms.
Long: The applicable close is above the top of an active bearish gap.
Short: The applicable close is below the bottom of an active bullish gap.
