Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over the lookback ending at the previous chart bar.
Calculation: Upper sweep = high > prior high AND close < prior high AND (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) >= wick-ratio threshold AND high - close >= ATR × rejection threshold AND close < open; use a wick ratio of zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Lower sweep = low < prior low AND close > prior low AND (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) >= wick-ratio threshold AND close - low >= ATR × rejection threshold AND close > open; use a wick ratio of zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume - SMA(volume)) / standard deviation of volume over the same lookback; use zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Sweep probability = sigmoid(sweep weight × rejection / max(ATR, epsilon) + volume weight × max(volume z-score, 0) + impulse weight × abs(close - close at an earlier bar) / max(ATR, epsilon) + gap weight × abs(open - previous close) / max(ATR, epsilon) + bias), where sigmoid(x) = 1 / (1 + exp(-x)); rejection is high - close for an upper sweep and close - low for a lower sweep. Alternatively, use a fixed fallback probability for a sweep.
Calculation: Active voids = on each completed bar, process upper and lower sweeps in that order. For a sweep whose probability >= probability threshold AND whose volume > SMA(volume) × burst multiplier OR absolute volume z-score > z-score threshold, construct its void only when either volume condition holds: an upper sweep produces a downward void with top = min(close, prior low + ATR × upper-bound multiplier) and bottom = prior low - ATR × lower-bound multiplier; a lower sweep produces an upward void with bottom = max(close, prior high - ATR × lower-bound multiplier) and top = prior high + ATR × upper-bound multiplier. Require top - bottom >= ATR × minimum-size multiplier. Merge into the first existing unfilled void of the same direction if their overlap is positive OR the smaller absolute difference between corresponding boundaries is < ATR × merge multiplier; set the merged top to the greater top, bottom to the smaller bottom, and urgency to 1, retaining its original birth bar. Otherwise create a void with urgency 1 and birth bar equal to the current bar. After creation and merging, mark each void filled when low <= its top AND high >= its bottom; remove it when current bar index - birth bar > lifetime. Multiply the urgency of each remaining void by the decay factor on every bar, including its creation or merge bar.
Calculation: Nearest active void = the unfilled void with the smallest abs(close - midpoint) / max(ATR, epsilon) that is <= the maximum distance; break ties by the earliest void in creation order. Its midpoint is (top + bottom) / 2.
Long: Lower sweep.
Short: Upper sweep.
Long: Nearest active void is upward AND its urgency > urgency threshold.
Short: Nearest active void is downward AND its urgency > urgency threshold.
