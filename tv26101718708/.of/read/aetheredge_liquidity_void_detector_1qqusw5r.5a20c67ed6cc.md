Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − volume moving average) / volume standard deviation; use 0 when standard deviation is 0
Calculation: Void probability = sigmoid(1.8 × sweep rejection distance / ATR + 0.9 × max(Volume Z-score, 0) + 0.6 × absolute 3-bar close change / ATR + 0.4 × absolute open-to-previous-close gap / ATR − 1.2); when neural weighting is disabled, use 0.7 for a confirmed sweep and 0 otherwise
Calculation: Confirmed upward sweep = high exceeds the highest high of the preceding lookback bars, close is below that prior high, upper wick / high-to-low range meets the wick-ratio threshold, high − close meets the rejection threshold × ATR, and close < open
Calculation: Confirmed downward sweep = low is below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars, close is above that prior low, lower wick / high-to-low range meets the wick-ratio threshold, close − low meets the rejection threshold × ATR, and close > open
Calculation: Volume anomaly = Volume Z-score magnitude exceeds its threshold OR volume exceeds volume moving average × burst multiplier
Calculation: Create or update an upward-fill void after a confirmed downward sweep when its probability meets the minimum and volume anomaly is true; its bottom is prior low − ATR and its top is min(close, prior low + 0.5 × ATR). Create or update a downward-fill void after a confirmed upward sweep under the same filters; its bottom is max(close, prior high − 0.5 × ATR) and its top is prior high + ATR. Reject a zone smaller than the minimum size × ATR; merge overlapping or sufficiently near same-direction unfilled zones, retaining the greatest probability and resetting urgency to 1. New zones start with urgency 1 and become filled when low ≤ zone top and high ≥ zone bottom; remove zones older than the maximum lifetime and multiply unfilled-zone urgency by the decay factor each bar
Calculation: Nearest void = unfilled void whose midpoint has the smallest absolute distance from close, among voids within the maximum fill distance × ATR
Long: Confirmed downward sweep OR nearest void is a downward-fill void and its midpoint is within the maximum fill distance × ATR and its urgency > 0.3
Short: Confirmed upward sweep OR nearest void is an upward-fill void and its midpoint is within the maximum fill distance × ATR and its urgency > 0.3
