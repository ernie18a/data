Calculation: Fast/slow EMA trend = bullish when the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA and close is above the shorter-lookback EMA; bearish when the shorter-lookback EMA is below the longer-lookback EMA and close is below it
Calculation: Directional Flux = EMA of close change divided by ATR, with a shorter-lookback EMA signal; bullish when Flux is above its signal and zero, bearish when below its signal and zero
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint of the recent high-low range and its moving average; bullish when it is above its EMA signal and rising, bearish when below its signal and falling
Calculation: Recent squeeze = a squeeze was active within a short lookback; squeeze is active when Bollinger Bands lie inside Keltner Channels, and squeeze release is the first bar after an active squeeze
Calculation: Directional liquidity sweep = price exceeds the latest confirmed swing high/low by an ATR-scaled margin but closes back below/above it, with open also below/above that swing; range fake break = price exceeds the prior-bar rolling high/low and closes back inside, with volume above a lower fraction of its moving average
Calculation: Breakout = close exceeds the prior-bar rolling high/low, volume exceeds a lower fraction of its moving average, and either a recent squeeze occurred or the prior close was on the other side of the level
Calculation: Confirmed structure break = on a completed chart bar, close breaks the latest confirmed swing high/low not previously broken; classify as change of character when the prior structure bias was neutral or opposite, otherwise as break of structure, then set bias to the break direction
Calculation: Order-block retest = after an upward structure break, use the latest bearish candle's high-low zone; retest when low reaches its top and close is at or above its bottom. After a downward structure break, use the latest bullish candle's zone; retest when high reaches its bottom and close is at or below its top. Deactivate a zone after close passes beyond its far edge
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement zone = for upward structure, the configured retracement band between the latest structure-break high and the latest confirmed swing low; for downward structure, between the latest confirmed swing high and the structure-break low. Update the impulse extreme when it extends. In-zone means the current bar's high-low range overlaps the band
Calculation: Fibonacci confluence = the matching impulse direction is active, price is in its retracement zone, and at least one applies: opposing liquidity sweep, matching order-block retest, momentum is rising/falling in the impulse direction, or close is on the impulse side of the midpoint
Calculation: Range retest = within a limited number of bars after breakout, price revisits the broken level and closes back beyond it in the breakout direction
Calculation: Candle reversal = bullish lower wick exceeds body by a configured multiple and close > open, or bullish engulfing of the prior bearish candle; bearish is the inverse
Calculation: RSI divergence = at confirmed swing lows, price makes a lower low while RSI makes a higher low; at confirmed swing highs, price makes a higher high while RSI makes a lower high
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend state = shorter-lookback EMA above/below longer-lookback EMA and close above/below the shorter EMA; evaluate on higher-timeframe bars using the last completed bar. Count bullish and bearish states across the configured higher timeframes
Calculation: Trend quality = average of capped directional efficiency, normalized ADX, persistence of rising/falling squeeze momentum, and ATR stability relative to its longer moving average
Calculation: Adaptive trail = close minus/plus ATR times a factor that increases as trend quality falls; trail direction updates from the current and prior closes relative to the prior trail
Calculation: Long/short scores = weighted sums of: matching EMA trend, matching trail side, ADX above trend minimum, volume above its moving average (higher weight) or above a lower fraction of it (lower weight), matching higher-timeframe count, matching squeeze momentum, matching Flux, matching liquidity sweep, matching RSI divergence, matching reversal candle, matching breakout, matching range retest, matching structure score (new change of character > new break of structure > persistent matching bias), matching Fibonacci score (confluence > matching premium/discount location), matching order-block score (retest > active zone with close beyond it), and high/medium trend quality. Cap each score to its bounded range
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, position is not already long, news block is inactive, and late-long is false, and either (long score ≥ entry threshold AND long score ≥ short score + lead threshold AND bullish higher-timeframe count ≥ minimum AND entry-mode-valid long trigger), OR (early-long trigger AND long score ≥ early threshold AND long score ≥ short score + reduced lead threshold AND bearish higher-timeframe count ≤ allowed maximum), OR pullback-long, OR continuation-long. Entry-mode-valid long trigger is safe-long trigger in safe mode, aggressive-long trigger in aggressive mode, or either in hybrid mode. Safe-long = range retest OR bullish order-block retest OR bullish Fibonacci confluence OR pullback-long OR (downward liquidity sweep AND bullish reversal candle AND bearish higher-timeframe count ≤ allowed maximum). Aggressive-long = upward breakout OR upward structure break OR early-long OR continuation-long. Pullback-long = pullback entries enabled AND trend-up AND re-entry interval elapsed AND low reaches the fast EMA plus an ATR-scaled zone AND close is above the fast EMA and open AND momentum is rising AND long score ≥ early threshold AND bearish higher-timeframe count ≤ allowed maximum AND late-long is false. Continuation-long = trend-up AND re-entry interval elapsed AND close exceeds the prior rolling high AND volume exceeds its lower threshold AND long score ≥ entry threshold AND bullish higher-timeframe count ≥ minimum AND late-long is false AND trend quality ≥ weak threshold
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, position is not already short, news block is inactive, and late-short is false, and either (short score ≥ entry threshold AND short score ≥ long score + lead threshold AND bearish higher-timeframe count ≥ minimum AND entry-mode-valid short trigger), OR (early-short trigger AND short score ≥ early threshold AND short score ≥ long score + reduced lead threshold AND bullish higher-timeframe count ≤ allowed maximum), OR pullback-short, OR continuation-short. Entry-mode-valid short trigger is safe-short trigger in safe mode, aggressive-short trigger in aggressive mode, or either in hybrid mode. Safe-short = range retest OR bearish order-block retest OR bearish Fibonacci confluence OR pullback-short OR (upward liquidity sweep AND bearish reversal candle AND bullish higher-timeframe count ≤ allowed maximum). Aggressive-short = downward breakout OR downward structure break OR early-short OR continuation-short. Pullback-short = pullback entries enabled AND trend-down AND re-entry interval elapsed AND high reaches the fast EMA minus an ATR-scaled zone AND close is below the fast EMA and open AND momentum is falling AND short score ≥ early threshold AND bullish higher-timeframe count ≤ allowed maximum AND late-short is false. Continuation-short = trend-down AND re-entry interval elapsed AND close is below the prior rolling low AND volume exceeds its lower threshold AND short score ≥ entry threshold AND bearish higher-timeframe count ≥ minimum AND late-short is false AND trend quality ≥ weak threshold
Exit: long, on a confirmed chart bar with no simultaneous long signal, if close < adaptive trail OR close < slow EMA OR close ≤ stop (move stop to entry price after target one is reached, if enabled) OR short score > long score + reversal margin OR (trend-up AND (RSI is overbought OR trend quality is falling below weak threshold with falling momentum) AND close is more than an ATR-scaled distance above fast EMA AND volume does not exceed its higher threshold)
Exit: short, on a confirmed chart bar with no simultaneous short signal, if close > adaptive trail OR close > slow EMA OR close ≥ stop (move stop to entry price after target one is reached, if enabled) OR long score > short score + reversal margin OR (trend-down AND (RSI is oversold OR trend quality is falling below weak threshold with rising momentum) AND close is more than an ATR-scaled distance below fast EMA AND volume does not exceed its higher threshold)
