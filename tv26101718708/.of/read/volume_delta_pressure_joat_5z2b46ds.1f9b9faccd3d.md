Calculation: Estimated bar delta = estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume; estimate from signed lower-timeframe bars (up bars buy, down bars sell, dojis split evenly) or from chart-bar volume split by a weighted blend of close position in the range and candle direction
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of estimated bar delta, reset at the selected anchor
Calculation: Pressure = either the z-score of EMA-smoothed bar delta against its rolling mean and standard deviation, or bar delta as a percentage of estimated bar volume
Long: bullish divergence (a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND cumulative volume delta at that pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot low) OR (pressure crosses above zero AND absolute bar delta >= its rolling average multiplied by the expansion factor AND volume has been observed AND the chart bar is confirmed AND close > previous close when price confirmation is enabled AND a recent bullish divergence exists when divergence is required AND the bars since the previous long signal >= the cooldown)
Short: bearish divergence (a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND cumulative volume delta at that pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot high) OR (pressure crosses below zero AND absolute bar delta >= its rolling average multiplied by the expansion factor AND volume has been observed AND the chart bar is confirmed AND close < previous close when price confirmation is enabled AND a recent bearish divergence exists when divergence is required AND the bars since the previous short signal >= the cooldown)
