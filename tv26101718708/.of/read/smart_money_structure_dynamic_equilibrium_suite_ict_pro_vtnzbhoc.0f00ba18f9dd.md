Calculation: Fast MA = moving average of close (same type as Slow MA) with a shorter lookback than Slow MA
Calculation: Slow MA = moving average of close (same type as Fast MA) with a longer lookback than Fast MA
Long: Fast MA > Slow MA on the chart timeframe, evaluated on the current bar
Long: the low k bars ago is strictly lower than the lows of the k bars before it AND the k bars after it (swing low confirmed on the current bar, equal left and right count k)
Short: Fast MA <= Slow MA on the chart timeframe, evaluated on the current bar
Short: the high k bars ago is strictly higher than the highs of the k bars before it AND the k bars after it (swing high confirmed on the current bar, equal left and right count k)
