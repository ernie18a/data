Calculation: Selected oscillator = the configured choice of RSI, OBV, RVSI (RSI of the EMA-smoothed cumulative change in source multiplied by volume), MFI, or RSI of the triple-EMA log-price rate of change
Calculation: Oscillator EMA = EMA of the selected oscillator
Long: A regular bullish divergence exists at a confirmed oscillator pivot low: the current pivot's low is lower than the prior pivot's low, its oscillator value is higher than the prior pivot's oscillator value, and the prior pivot is within the configured bar-distance range; AND the oscillator was below the configured oversold threshold within its recent lookback if that filter is enabled, and is below its configured maximum if enabled; AND all enabled long-range filters pass (close is inside at least one enabled manual price range; close is between enabled SMA upper and lower bounds; close is between enabled anchored VWAP and deviation-band bounds; enabled auto-Fibonacci bounds pass; and the selected-source EMA is rising if the uptrend filter is enabled); AND the configured signal delays pass, if enabled
Long: A hidden bullish divergence exists at a confirmed oscillator pivot low: the current pivot's low is higher than the prior pivot's low, its oscillator value is lower than the prior pivot's oscillator value, and the prior pivot is within the configured bar-distance range; AND the same enabled oversold and long-range filters as for regular bullish divergence pass; AND the configured signal delays pass, if enabled
Long: Evaluate the divergence conditions for each configured pivot-left lookback, from shorter to longer; use only the shortest if the first-lookback-only option is enabled. A signal occurs immediately when delays are disabled; otherwise it occurs within the configured wait window after a divergence when enabled oscillator/EMA-cross, close-above-EMA, and price-bounce conditions pass
Short:
Exit: long, high reaches the configured take-profit level measured above entry price OR low reaches the configured stop-loss level measured below entry price; the stop is either a configured percentage below entry price or the lowest low over its configured lookback less the configured offset (the low-based stop is active only during the configured entry window)
