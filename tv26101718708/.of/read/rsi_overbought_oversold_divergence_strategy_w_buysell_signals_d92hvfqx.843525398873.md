Calculation: Oscillator = selected RSI of a price source, OBV, MFI of a price source, RSI of EMA of cumulative (change in price source × volume), or RSI of 10000 × change in triple EMA of log(price source); the cumulative-volume EMA and triple EMA use a secondary length independent of the RSI length.
Calculation: Fibonacci level = lowest low over its lookback + (highest high over that lookback − lowest low over that lookback) × (1 − selected fraction); the upper and lower levels use independently selected lookbacks and fractions.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP bands = anchored VWAP of a price source ± a selected multiplier times the square root of max(0, cumulative volume-weighted mean of squared source − anchored VWAP squared); reset the cumulative sums at the selected calendar anchor.
Long: For any enabled oscillator pivot-left lookback, an oscillator pivot low is confirmed after the selected right lookback AND the number of bars since the preceding pivot confirmation minus one is between the selected minimum and maximum inclusive AND ((regular divergence is enabled AND oscillator at the current pivot > oscillator at the preceding pivot AND low at the current pivot < low at the preceding pivot) OR (hidden divergence is enabled AND oscillator at the current pivot < oscillator at the preceding pivot AND low at the current pivot > low at the preceding pivot)) AND, if the oversold filter is enabled, the lowest oscillator over its lookback < the oversold threshold AND, if any manual price ranges are enabled, close is strictly inside at least one enabled range AND, if enabled, close < the upper-source SMA AND, if enabled, close > the lower-source SMA AND, if enabled, close > anchored VWAP AND, if enabled, close < anchored VWAP AND, if enabled, close > the lower anchored VWAP band AND, if enabled, close < the upper anchored VWAP band AND, if enabled, lowest low over the current and preceding bar < upper Fibonacci level AND, if enabled, highest low over the current and preceding bar > lower Fibonacci level AND, if enabled, EMA of the oscillator price source > its preceding value. Evaluate each pivot-left lookback separately, using only the shortest when the single-lookback option is enabled; evaluate all filters on the pivot confirmation bar.
Long: If any delay is enabled, a regular or hidden divergence from the preceding long line occurred within the delay window AND every enabled delay condition holds: high − lowest low over the bounce lookback > the bounce threshold; close > EMA of the oscillator price source; oscillator − its EMA > 1 AND bars since the latest divergence of either type <= the oscillator-delay lookback. For each divergence type separately, emit at elapsed age zero whenever the conditions hold; at a positive elapsed age emit only when a previous delayed signal of that same type exists and its age is at least the elapsed divergence age plus one, except hidden divergence at elapsed ages one, two, and three requires the previous delayed signal age to be at least one, two, and three respectively. The delay window has the fixed maximum age implemented by the strategy.
Exit: long, high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage).
Exit: long, if static stop is enabled, low <= entry price × (1 − stop percentage).
Exit: long, if the low-based stop is enabled, low <= the stop most recently submitted during the entry bar or the following bar while a divergence entry signal was at most one bar old; that stop is the lowest low over the stop lookback at submission minus either a fixed offset or a percentage of that lowest low. The submitted stop remains active until filled or replaced.
