Calculation: Indicator = a selected series computable from the available bar fields, optionally normalized over a rolling window including the current bar by min-max scaling, Z-score, or robust scaling; robust scaling is 0.6745 × (value − rolling median) / rolling median absolute deviation. Use 0 when the Z-score standard deviation or robust-scaling deviation is 0.
Calculation: Price high and price low = respectively high and low, or both close, according to the selected price source.
Calculation: Confirmed indicator pivot = an indicator high or low confirmed using selected left and right lookbacks; associate it with the price high or low on the pivot bar. Evaluate a pivot divergence on the confirmation bar using the two most recent confirmed pivots of the same type.
Calculation: Immediate anchor = the most recent confirmed indicator pivot of the corresponding type, with its associated price; require at least two confirmed pivots of that type. For regular bearish divergences, append the current indicator and price high as a new anchor whenever the current indicator exceeds the current regular bearish anchor. For regular bullish divergences, replace the current anchor with the current indicator and price low whenever the current indicator is below that anchor. Hidden divergences use the most recent confirmed pivot without these updates. Process confirmed pivots and these anchor updates before evaluating the current bar.
Calculation: Immediate divergence eligibility = for each divergence type, the current anchor time has not been retired and no divergence of that type is active. Activate it when its immediate divergence condition first holds. While active, do not emit another signal; retire the then-current anchor time and deactivate when the indicator-versus-anchor and price-versus-anchor comparison pair ceases to hold. Track these states separately for each divergence type.
Long: Pivot detection selected AND a confirmed indicator low pivot has price low < the preceding low pivot's price low AND indicator > the preceding low pivot's indicator.
Long: Pivot detection selected AND a confirmed indicator low pivot has price low > the preceding low pivot's price low AND indicator < the preceding low pivot's indicator.
Short: Pivot detection selected AND a confirmed indicator high pivot has price high > the preceding high pivot's price high AND indicator < the preceding high pivot's indicator.
Short: Pivot detection selected AND a confirmed indicator high pivot has price high < the preceding high pivot's price high AND indicator > the preceding high pivot's indicator.
Long: Immediate detection selected AND regular bullish divergence eligibility holds AND previous indicator > regular bullish anchor indicator AND previous price low < regular bullish anchor price low AND previous indicator < indicator two bars ago AND previous indicator < current indicator.
Long: Immediate detection selected AND hidden bullish divergence eligibility holds AND previous indicator < hidden bullish anchor indicator AND previous price low > hidden bullish anchor price low AND previous indicator < indicator two bars ago AND previous indicator < current indicator.
Short: Immediate detection selected AND regular bearish divergence eligibility holds AND previous indicator < regular bearish anchor indicator AND previous price high > regular bearish anchor price high AND previous indicator > indicator two bars ago AND previous indicator > current indicator.
Short: Immediate detection selected AND hidden bearish divergence eligibility holds AND previous indicator > hidden bearish anchor indicator AND previous price high < hidden bearish anchor price high AND previous indicator > indicator two bars ago AND previous indicator > current indicator.
Calculation: Stop and target = fixed at entry close using independently selected stop and target distances, each either its multiplier times ATR at entry or its percentage of entry price. Subtract the stop distance and add the target distance for a long; add the stop distance and subtract the target distance for a short. Round each level to the selected price tick.
Exit: long, high >= target OR low <= stop.
Exit: short, low <= target OR high >= stop.
