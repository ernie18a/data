Calculation: Divergence series = selected indicator source, optionally normalized by rolling min-max, rolling z-score, or robust scaling
Calculation: Price pivot values = high and low, or close for both when close-based detection is selected
Long: confirmed bullish regular or hidden divergence, with no open position; in pivot mode, the latest price low is lower than the preceding price low and the latest indicator pivot is higher, OR the latest price low is higher and the latest indicator pivot is lower; in immediate mode, the preceding bar's low is below the stored price pivot and its indicator value is above the stored indicator pivot, and that indicator value is lower than both the bar before it and the current bar, OR the preceding bar's low is above the stored price pivot and its indicator value is below the stored indicator pivot, and that indicator value is higher than both the bar before it and the current bar
Short: confirmed bearish regular or hidden divergence, with no open position; in pivot mode, the latest price high is higher than the preceding price high and the latest indicator pivot is lower, OR the latest price high is lower and the latest indicator pivot is higher; in immediate mode, the preceding bar's high is above the stored price pivot and its indicator value is below the stored indicator pivot, and that indicator value is higher than both the bar before it and the current bar, OR the preceding bar's high is below the stored price pivot and its indicator value is above the stored indicator pivot, and that indicator value is lower than both the bar before it and the current bar
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price
