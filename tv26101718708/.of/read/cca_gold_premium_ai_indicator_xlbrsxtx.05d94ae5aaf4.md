Calculation: Short-term EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the long-term EMA.
Calculation: Long-term EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the short-term EMA.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = count of close > open, short-term EMA > long-term EMA, close > VWAP, and close > the longer-term EMA.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the bars on both sides over the pivot lookback; confirmation occurs after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = on a close crossing above the latest confirmed pivot high, use the range of the first bearish candle among the pivot-low bar and the next five bars whose low is below 1.01 times the pivot-low bar's low; if none qualifies, use the pivot-low bar's range. Keep the zone active until a close is below its low.
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = on a close crossing below the latest confirmed pivot low, use the range of the first bullish candle among the pivot-high bar and the next five bars whose high is above 0.99 times the pivot-high bar's high; if none qualifies, use the pivot-high bar's range. Keep the zone active until a close is above its high.
Long: More than the signal cooldown interval has elapsed since the last long or short signal AND ((RSI crosses above its oversold threshold AND (short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR bullish confluence score >= 2)) OR (an active bullish order-block zone has low <= zone high AND close >= zone low AND close > open AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA)).
Short: More than the signal cooldown interval has elapsed since the last long or short signal AND ((RSI crosses below its overbought threshold AND (short-term EMA <= long-term EMA OR bullish confluence score <= 2)) OR (an active bearish order-block zone has high >= zone low AND close <= zone high AND close < open AND short-term EMA <= long-term EMA)).
