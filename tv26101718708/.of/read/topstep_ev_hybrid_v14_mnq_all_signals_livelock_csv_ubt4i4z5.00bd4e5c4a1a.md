Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low), or zero when high equals low; wick ratios are the respective wick lengths divided by the range, or zero when the range is zero; close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or one half when the range is zero.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), or one when volume or its SMA is unavailable or the SMA is zero.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = the high or low of a bar that is respectively the highest or lowest over equal numbers of preceding and subsequent chart bars; use the most recently confirmed swing, including one confirmed on the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low; bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high.
Calculation: Bullish gap = low > high two bars earlier; bearish gap = high < low two bars earlier. Bullish structure break = close > previous high; bearish structure break = close < previous low. Bullish order block = previous close < previous open AND close > previous high; bearish order block = previous close > previous open AND close < previous low.
Calculation: Bullish flow = close > open AND relative volume > flow threshold AND close > chart EMA; bearish flow = close < open AND relative volume > flow threshold AND close < chart EMA. Strong pressure uses a higher relative-volume threshold and the corresponding candle direction.
Calculation: EMA slope = EMA minus its value a specified number of bars earlier. Low volatility = ATR < its SMA multiplied by a threshold. Compression = highest high minus lowest low over a lookback < ATR multiplied by a threshold. Range expansion uses a shorter lookback and requires its range > ATR multiplied by another threshold. Bad conditions = abs(chart EMA slope) < ATR multiplied by a flatness threshold OR low volatility OR compression OR body ratio < a weak-body threshold OR both the current and previous bars have either wick ratio > an excessive-wick threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish and bearish scores = the nonnegative sum of configured weights for, respectively, trend, slope, displacement, volume flow, gap, structure break and entry-window conditions, minus configured penalties for midday, overextension and chart bad conditions. Higher-timeframe close is from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe open, EMA, ATR, EMA slope, volume flow, gap and structure break use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. Trend compares the completed close with the forming EMA; displacement compares that close with the forming open and an ATR-scaled minimum body. Slope compares the forming EMA slope with a positive or negative ATR-scaled threshold. Volume flow requires a directional forming candle and volume > its SMA, or accepts volume if unavailable or its SMA is zero. Overextension = abs(completed higher-timeframe close - forming higher-timeframe EMA) > forming higher-timeframe ATR multiplied by a threshold.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe confirmation score = the number of corresponding gap, structure-break and order-block conditions satisfied on the last lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar; in real time use the still-forming lower-timeframe bar. Confirmation requires at least a lower count when the corresponding higher-timeframe score is within its inclusive golden range and exceeds the opposite score, otherwise at least a higher count.
Calculation: Normal directional setup = corresponding higher-timeframe trend AND score >= required threshold AND (score within the inclusive golden range AND score > opposite score OR score >= a higher clean-score threshold AND score > opposite score AND higher-timeframe extension <= its normal limit AND range expansion AND body ratio >= minimum) AND, when countertrend blocking is enabled, score > opposite score + dominance buffer.
Calculation: Normal directional trigger = (close on the corresponding side of chart EMA AND EMA slope beyond the corresponding signed ATR-scaled threshold AND (corresponding flow OR strong pressure)) OR ((corresponding sweep OR gap OR structure break OR order block) AND corresponding flow) OR corresponding lower-timeframe confirmation.
Calculation: Entry quality = body ratio >= minimum AND (ATR > its SMA multiplied by a healthy-volatility threshold OR range expansion) AND bad conditions = false AND higher-timeframe extension <= normal limit.
Calculation: Breakout exception = entry time within the configured entry window AND close beyond the chart EMA in the trade direction AND EMA slope has that direction AND close beyond the highest prior highs for a long or lowest prior lows for a short over the breakout lookback AND body ratio >= breakout threshold AND bar range >= ATR multiplied by a breakout threshold AND corresponding higher-timeframe score >= breakout threshold AND (corresponding lower-timeframe confirmation score >= its minimum OR directional candle body > ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold OR corresponding strong pressure) AND higher-timeframe extension <= breakout limit AND low volatility = false AND compression = false.
Calculation: Reversal-short exception = entry time within the configured entry window AND bearish sweep AND open - close > ATR multiplied by the displacement threshold AND close < previous low AND body ratio >= reversal threshold AND bearish higher-timeframe score >= reversal threshold AND bearish lower-timeframe confirmation score >= minimum AND higher-timeframe extension <= reversal limit AND low volatility = false AND compression = false.
Calculation: Short structure stop = the nearest available high above the signal close among the previous high, highest high over the prior invalidation lookback, latest confirmed swing high and, if enabled, signal high, plus an ATR-scaled buffer; if none is available use signal high plus the buffer.
Calculation: Short objective = the highest available enabled candidate below signal close among recent individual lows, the latest confirmed swing low, the last completed higher-timeframe daily low and, when individual lows are disabled, the lowest prior low over the objective lookback. Objective risk-reward = (signal close - objective) / (structure stop - signal close).
Calculation: Structure-short exception = entry time within the configured entry window AND close < previous low AND (bearish lower-timeframe confirmation score >= minimum OR bearish flow OR strong selling pressure OR open - close > ATR multiplied by displacement threshold) AND bearish higher-timeframe score >= structure threshold AND open - close > ATR multiplied by structure-impulse threshold AND body ratio >= structure threshold AND relative volume >= structure threshold AND close location <= structure threshold AND objective exists AND low > objective AND lowest low over the configured objective-tap lookback > objective when that lookback check is enabled AND abs(close - chart EMA) / ATR <= extension threshold AND structure stop - close > zero AND structure stop - close <= risk cap AND objective risk-reward >= minimum AND low volatility = false AND compression = false.
Calculation: Long day filter = configured weekday blocks are false AND, when the weekday risk patch is enabled, the restricted weekday body ratio >= its threshold AND the restricted late weekday window is false. Short day filter = configured weekday blocks are false, except an enabled strong-body weekday exception, AND enabled weekday risk-patch blocks are false AND, on the restricted weekday, prospective short risk < its cap. Prospective short risk uses the structure stop when structure stops are enabled and the structure-short exception holds; otherwise it uses signal high + ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier.
Calculation: At entry, store entry close, entry bar, initial stop, target and break-even trigger. Long stop = signal low - ATR multiplied by stop multiplier; short stop = prospective short stop. Risk = distance from entry to stop. Target = entry plus risk multiplied by target reward multiple for a long, or entry minus that distance for a short; when the structure-short exception and structure stop are enabled, the short target is the short objective. Break-even trigger = entry plus or minus risk multiplied by the break-even trigger multiple in the trade direction.
Long: low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low.
Short: high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high.
Long: low > high two bars earlier.
Short: high < low two bars earlier.
Long: previous close < previous open AND close > previous high.
Short: previous close > previous open AND close < previous low.
Long: close > open AND relative volume > flow threshold AND close > chart EMA.
Short: close < open AND relative volume > flow threshold AND close < chart EMA.
Long: position side = flat AND long day filter = true AND (weak-long blocking disabled OR body ratio >= live-long threshold) AND ((entry time within the configured entry window AND normal bullish setup = true AND normal bullish trigger = true AND entry quality = true AND (outside opening window OR bullish higher-timeframe score >= opening threshold AND bullish score > bearish score + strong dominance buffer AND body ratio >= opening threshold AND bullish lower-timeframe confirmation score >= opening count)) OR enabled bullish breakout exception = true).
Short: position side = flat AND short day filter = true AND (negative-body-bucket blocking disabled OR body ratio < bucket lower bound OR body ratio > bucket upper bound) AND ((entry time within the configured entry window AND normal bearish setup = true AND normal bearish trigger = true AND entry quality = true AND (outside opening window OR bearish higher-timeframe score >= opening threshold AND bearish score > bullish score + strong dominance buffer AND body ratio >= opening threshold AND bearish lower-timeframe confirmation score >= opening count) AND (chart timeframe differs from the restricted timeframe OR restricted-timeframe short blocking disabled OR extreme-trend exception enabled AND bearish higher-timeframe trend = true AND bearish higher-timeframe slope passes its threshold AND bearish score >= extreme threshold AND bearish score > bullish score + strong dominance buffer AND higher-timeframe extension <= normal limit AND bad conditions = false)) OR enabled bearish breakout exception = true OR enabled reversal-short exception = true OR enabled structure-short exception = true).
Exit: long, high >= stored target OR low <= stored entry after break-even is enabled and the high has reached the stored break-even trigger on this or an earlier trade bar OR low <= stored initial stop while break-even is inactive OR bar time >= configured management cutoff OR bars since entry >= timeout.
Exit: short, low <= stored target OR high >= stored entry after break-even is enabled and the low has reached the stored break-even trigger on this or an earlier trade bar OR high >= stored initial stop while break-even is inactive OR bar time >= configured management cutoff OR bars since entry >= timeout.
Calculation: Apply management on the entry bar as well as later bars; target has priority over break-even activation and stop, and stop has priority over time exits. If both entry directions occur while flat, initialize the short after the long.
