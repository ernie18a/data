Calculation: HTF directional score = On a higher timeframe, add points for the direction’s close beyond its EMA, EMA slope beyond an ATR threshold, directional displacement, directional volume flow, directional fair value gap, directional close beyond the prior bar’s high/low, and being in the entry window; subtract points for midday, excessive distance from the EMA, and hard-bad conditions; floor at zero. Use the prior completed higher-timeframe close for close-based comparisons; other higher-timeframe values use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe confirmation score = Count bullish/bearish fair value gaps, closes beyond the prior bar’s high/low, and bullish/bearish order blocks on a lower timeframe. Use the latest available lower-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Golden directional range = Directional higher-timeframe score is within the golden score bounds and exceeds the opposing score.
Calculation: Clean high score = Directional higher-timeframe score exceeds the high-score threshold, is not overextended, range expansion is present, the current candle has a strong body, and the directional score exceeds the opposing score.
Calculation: Directional higher-timeframe setup = Directional higher-timeframe trend, score at least the required threshold, golden directional range or clean high score, and (counter-trend blocking disabled or directional score exceeds the opposing score by the dominance buffer).
Calculation: Directional lower-timeframe trigger = Either price is on the matching side of the chart-timeframe EMA with matching EMA slope and matching volume flow or strong pressure; or a matching sweep, fair value gap, CISD, or order block occurs with matching volume flow; or lower-timeframe confirmation score meets its threshold (at least one in the matching golden range, otherwise at least two).
Calculation: Hard-bad conditions = EMA slope is flat, ATR is below its longer-lookback ATR average threshold, recent range is compressed, or the candle is weak-bodied or has excessive wicks on consecutive bars.
Calculation: Long breakout exception = Entry window, confirmed bar if confirmation is required, close above the highest high over a prior-bar lookback, close above the chart-timeframe EMA with positive EMA slope, body and range meet their thresholds, bullish higher-timeframe score meets its minimum, at least one bullish lower-timeframe confirmation or bullish displacement or strong buy pressure, higher-timeframe distance is within its maximum extension, and neither low volatility nor compression.
Calculation: Short breakout exception = Entry window, confirmed bar if confirmation is required, close below the lowest low over a prior-bar lookback, close below the chart-timeframe EMA with negative EMA slope, body and range meet their thresholds, bearish higher-timeframe score meets its minimum, at least one bearish lower-timeframe confirmation or bearish displacement or strong sell pressure, higher-timeframe distance is within its maximum extension, and neither low volatility nor compression.
Calculation: Reversal short exception = Entry window, confirmed bar if confirmation is required, bearish sweep, bearish displacement and bearish CISD, body ratio at least its minimum, bearish higher-timeframe score at least its minimum, at least one bearish lower-timeframe confirmation, higher-timeframe distance within its maximum extension, and neither low volatility nor compression.
Calculation: Iceberg short objective = Closest untapped downside level below close among the recent prior-bar lows, recent swing low, and prior day low (using the closest-objective and liquidity settings).
Calculation: Iceberg short validity = Confirmed bar if confirmation is required; entry window; early breakdown and rejection context; bearish lower-timeframe confirmation, bearish flow, strong sell pressure, or bearish displacement; bearish higher-timeframe score at least its minimum; bearish impulse and body ratio at least their minima; relative volume at least its minimum; close in the lower part of the candle; objective not already tapped and still below the current low; EMA extension and structural risk within their maxima; objective-to-risk ratio at least its minimum; positive structural risk; and neither low volatility nor compression.
Long: (confirmed bar if confirmation is required AND entry window AND bullish higher-timeframe setup AND bullish lower-timeframe trigger AND strong body AND good follow-through AND NOT hard-bad conditions AND NOT higher-timeframe overextension AND opening-window filter passes) OR long breakout exception; AND not blocked by weekday rules (Tuesday weak-body long or Thursday late window) AND body ratio is not below the live-long minimum AND no active trade.
Short: ((confirmed bar if confirmation is required AND entry window AND bearish higher-timeframe setup AND bearish lower-timeframe trigger AND strong body AND good follow-through AND NOT hard-bad conditions AND NOT higher-timeframe overextension AND opening-window filter passes AND five-minute short filter passes) OR short breakout exception OR reversal short exception OR iceberg short validity); AND not blocked by weekday rules or Thursday short-risk rule AND body ratio is outside the blocked short-body interval AND no active trade.
Long: Current low is below the last confirmed swing low and current close is above that swing low; OR current close is above open, relative volume exceeds its threshold, and close is above the chart-timeframe EMA; OR the prior candle is bearish and current close exceeds its high.
Short: Current high is above the last confirmed swing high and current close is below that swing high; OR current close is below open, relative volume exceeds its threshold, and close is below the chart-timeframe EMA; OR the prior candle is bullish and current close is below its low.
Exit: long, current high >= target.
Exit: long, current low <= stop.
Exit: long, break-even is armed and current low <= entry price.
Exit: long, current hour is at or after noon OR bars in trade >= timeout.
Exit: short, current low <= target.
Exit: short, current high >= stop.
Exit: short, break-even is armed and current high >= entry price.
Exit: short, current hour is at or after noon OR bars in trade >= timeout.
