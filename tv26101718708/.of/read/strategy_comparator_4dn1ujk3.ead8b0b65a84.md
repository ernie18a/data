Long: close > lower Bollinger Band AND previous close <= previous lower Bollinger Band OR close > upper Keltner Channel OR short-period SMA >= long-period SMA OR MACD line >= signal line OR close > close N bars ago OR high > highest high over the preceding N bars OR RSI crosses above its lower threshold OR fast Random Walk Index > 0 AND slow Random Walk Index >= 0 OR current bar is outside the previous bar AND close > open OR up-close streak >= its threshold OR Supertrend direction is bullish OR combined technical rating > its long threshold OR close crosses above Parabolic SAR OR Stochastic %K crosses above %D while %K is below its oversold threshold OR cumulative normalized upper-minus-lower wick imbalance > its positive threshold
Short: close < upper Bollinger Band AND previous close >= previous upper Bollinger Band OR close < lower Keltner Channel OR short-period SMA < long-period SMA OR MACD line < signal line OR close < close N bars ago OR low < lowest low over the preceding N bars OR RSI crosses below its upper threshold OR fast Random Walk Index < 0 AND slow Random Walk Index <= 0 OR current bar is outside the previous bar AND close < open OR down-close streak >= its threshold OR Supertrend direction is bearish OR combined technical rating < its short threshold OR close crosses below Parabolic SAR OR Stochastic %K crosses below %D while %K is above its overbought threshold OR cumulative normalized upper-minus-lower wick imbalance < its negative threshold
Exit: long, short-entry condition OR (Bollinger behavior is exit-at-midline AND close crosses above the middle Bollinger Band) OR (RSI behavior is exit-mode AND RSI crosses above its upper threshold) OR (wick imbalance crosses zero)
Exit: short, long-entry condition OR (Bollinger behavior is exit-at-midline AND close crosses below the middle Bollinger Band) OR (RSI behavior is exit-mode AND RSI crosses below its lower threshold) OR (wick imbalance crosses zero)
Calculation: Combined technical rating = average of the moving-average group rating and oscillator group rating; each group is the average of its constituent bullish/neutral/bearish indicator ratings. The moving-average group rates price relative to short- through long-lookback SMAs and EMAs, HMA, VWMA, and Ichimoku conditions. The oscillator group rates RSI, Stochastic, CCI, ADX/DI, Awesome Oscillator, Momentum, MACD, Stochastic RSI, Williams %R, Bulls/Bears Power, and Ultimate Oscillator.
