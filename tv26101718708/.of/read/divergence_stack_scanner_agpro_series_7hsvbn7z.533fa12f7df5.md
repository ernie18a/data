Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a price high or low with the same configurable number of bars on each side; evaluate it on the bar that confirms it. Compare each pivot with the immediately preceding pivot of the same type, using RSI of close, MACD histogram of close, CCI of close, MFI of typical price, and OBV sampled at the pivot. MACD uses a shorter and a longer EMA.
Calculation: Directional divergence = for a low pivot, the pivot low is lower and an oscillator is higher than at the preceding low pivot; for a high pivot, the pivot high is higher and an oscillator is lower than at the preceding high pivot. Store the latest pivot bar of each oscillator's divergence separately for each direction.
Calculation: Stack = at a confirmed pivot, count the directional oscillator divergences whose latest pivot bars equal the current pivot bar; if that count is below the minimum stack, optionally count those within a configurable bar window instead. The selected count must meet the minimum stack.
Calculation: Confirmation = optionally require the confirming close to exceed the low pivot candle's high for a bullish stack or fall below the high pivot candle's low for a bearish stack; a stricter option additionally requires the close to be above or below the shorter EMA, respectively. The other option accepts the pivot alone.
Calculation: Readiness score = rounded, bounded weighted sum of stack count, normalized absolute change between the current and preceding same-side pivot prices divided by ATR at the pivot, trend context, confirmation, and inverse normalized absolute distance from the confirming close to the pivot divided by current ATR, plus a sensitivity adjustment. Trend context has its highest value when the shorter EMA is below the longer EMA for bullish or above it for bearish, an intermediate value when that test fails and close is below the shorter EMA for bullish or above it for bearish, and a lower value otherwise. Confirmation contributes its high value when confirmed, an intermediate value for pivot-only mode, and a lower value otherwise. Normalize swing and distance by separately configured lower and upper bounds and clamp each to its range.
Calculation: Accepted stack = a confirmed pivot with a stack meeting the minimum and readiness score at least a configurable watch floor below the ready threshold. An accepted stack replaces the active event if the previous event is terminal, its direction differs, its score is at least the active score, or its score meets the ready threshold and confirmation holds. Process bullish before bearish when both occur on one bar. Store the pivot bar, direction, score, invalidation and target; an event becomes terminal on invalidation, target, or expiry measured from its pivot bar.
Calculation: Invalidation = for an accepted bullish event, pivot low minus a configurable multiple of ATR at the pivot; for a bearish event, pivot high plus that multiple.
Calculation: Target = for a bullish event, the latest confirmed high pivot if it exceeds the confirming close, otherwise the confirming close plus ATR at the pivot multiplied by the greater of a configurable minimum and a configurable multiple of the normalized pivot-to-pivot swing; reverse high to low, exceeds to is below, and plus to minus for a bearish event. Fix invalidation and target when the active event is replaced.
Long: An accepted bullish stack occurs.
Short: An accepted bearish stack occurs.
Exit: long, active event is bullish AND has not expired AND has not previously been invalidated AND low <= its invalidation.
Exit: long, active event is bullish AND has not expired AND has neither previously reached its target nor been invalidated AND low > its invalidation AND high >= its target.
Exit: short, active event is bearish AND has not expired AND has not previously been invalidated AND high >= its invalidation.
Exit: short, active event is bearish AND has not expired AND has neither previously reached its target nor been invalidated AND high < its invalidation AND low <= its target.
