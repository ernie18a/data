Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by the simple moving average of volume, including the current bar; use 1 when the average is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Range = high − low; body participation = abs(close − open) / range; close location = (close − low) / range, clamped to [0, 1]; range expansion = range / ATR, using true range when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Participation quality = weighted sum of relative volume divided by its shock threshold, body participation divided by its minimum, range expansion divided by its minimum, and directional close location. Clamp each of the first three ratios to its respective upper limit; directional close location is close location for a bullish committed candle, 1 − close location for a bearish committed candle, and a neutral value otherwise. Clamp the weighted sum to its maximum.
Calculation: A bullish shock is a completed bar with close > open, close location >= the bullish location threshold, relative volume >= its shock threshold, body participation >= its minimum, range expansion >= its minimum, and participation quality >= its threshold. A bearish shock uses close < open and close location <= the complementary bearish location threshold, with the same remaining tests.
Calculation: Active shock = the latest bullish or bearish shock, storing its direction, high, low, and bar index. A new shock replaces the previous shock and resets its continuation, absorption, and thin-move flags. The evaluation window begins on the next bar and ends after a configurable number of bars. Continuation and absorption flags are set on their first respective events; the thin-move flag is set on its first event. Update these flags even when another event occurs on the same bar.
Long: bullish shock.
Short: bearish shock.
Long: active shock is bullish AND current bar is within its evaluation window AND close > shock high + ATR × continuation distance AND relative volume >= continuation minimum AND continuation flag is unset.
Short: active shock is bearish AND current bar is within its evaluation window AND close < shock low − ATR × continuation distance AND relative volume >= continuation minimum AND continuation flag is unset.
Short: active shock is bullish AND current bar is within its evaluation window AND close < shock low + (shock high − shock low) × absorption threshold AND close < open AND absorption flag is unset.
Long: active shock is bearish AND current bar is within its evaluation window AND close > shock high − (shock high − shock low) × absorption threshold AND close > open AND absorption flag is unset.
Long: active shock is bullish AND current bar is within its evaluation window AND high > shock high AND close > shock high AND relative volume <= thin-move threshold AND continuation flag is unset AND thin-move flag is unset.
Short: active shock is bearish AND current bar is within its evaluation window AND low < shock low AND close < shock low AND relative volume <= thin-move threshold AND continuation flag is unset AND thin-move flag is unset.
