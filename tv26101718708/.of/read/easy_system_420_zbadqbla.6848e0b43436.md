Calculation: Source = a selectable chart-bar price series.
Calculation: Volume-adjusted price = let flow = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), cumulative flow = flow + cumulative sum of flow, and shadow = (cumulative flow − SMA of cumulative flow) / standard deviation of that difference × standard deviation of high − low; use high + shadow when shadow > 0, otherwise low + shadow.
Calculation: Trend = calculate an EMA of volume-adjusted price and subtract and add a multiple of ATR to obtain lower and upper candidates. The lower band is the greater of its candidate and its previous value when previous source > previous lower band, otherwise its candidate. The upper band is the lesser of its candidate and its previous value when previous source < previous upper band, otherwise its candidate. Trend is 1 when source > previous upper band, −1 when source < previous lower band, otherwise its previous value, initialized to 1. The trend line is the lower band when trend = 1 and the upper band otherwise.
Calculation: Zigzag = on each bar, a low event occurs when the rolling lowest low differs from the preceding bar's rolling lowest low and current low minus that rolling low is at most a configurable price distance; a high event occurs when the rolling highest high differs from the preceding bar's rolling highest high and that rolling high minus current high is at most that distance. Update both remembered extremes on every bar. Direction becomes 0 on a low event, otherwise 1 on a high event, otherwise retains its preceding value, initially −1. Initialize the low and high offsets to zero on each bar. Set the low offset to its preceding value plus 1 if there is no low event, or if direction is unchanged and current low > low at the preceding low offset plus 1 bars ago. Set the high offset analogously if there is no high event, or if direction is unchanged and current high < high at the preceding high offset plus 1 bars ago. Low index = current bar index − low offset; high index = current bar index − high offset.
Calculation: Parabolic stop = initialize uptrend true, new-trend false, extreme point to high, stop to low, and acceleration to an initial parameter. On later bars, update the extreme point with the current high maximum in the preceding uptrend or current low minimum otherwise; reset acceleration to its initial parameter after a preceding new trend, otherwise increase it by an increment up to a maximum when the extreme point changes. Advance the stop by acceleration × (extreme point − previous stop). In a preceding uptrend, cap the stop at the previous two lows; if stop > current low, switch to downtrend, mark a new trend, set stop to the greater of current high and previous extreme point, and set extreme point to the lesser of current and previous lows. In a preceding downtrend, floor the stop at the previous two highs; if stop < current high, switch to uptrend, mark a new trend, set stop to the lesser of current low and previous extreme point, and set extreme point to the greater of current and previous highs. Otherwise retain the preceding trend and clear new-trend.
Calculation: Bull pressure and bear pressure = let R1 = max(open − previous close, high − low) if previous close < open, otherwise high − low; let R2 = max(previous close − open, high − low) if previous close > open, otherwise high − low. If close = open and high − close = close − low, bull pressure is max(high − open, close − low) if previous close > open, otherwise R1; bear pressure is max(open − low, high − close) if previous close < open, otherwise R2. If close = open and high − close > close − low, bull pressure is max(high − previous close, close − low) if previous close < open, otherwise high − open; bear pressure is R2. If close = open and high − close < close − low, bull pressure is R1; bear pressure is max(previous close − low, high − close) if previous close > open, otherwise open − low. If close < open, bull pressure is max(high − previous close, close − low) if previous close < open, otherwise max(high − open, close − low); bear pressure is R2. If close > open, bull pressure is R1; bear pressure is max(previous close − low, high − close) if previous close > open, otherwise max(open − low, high − close).
Calculation: Wave = source movement is 1, −1, or 0 according as source is above, below, or equal to its previous value. Update movement trend to the current nonzero movement when it differs from the previous movement; otherwise retain the previous movement trend, initially 0. Update wave to movement trend when it differs from the previous wave and source is strictly above every preceding source in a lookback or strictly below every preceding source in that lookback; otherwise retain the previous wave, initially 0. Accumulate volume while wave is unchanged; reset accumulated volume to current volume when wave changes. Up volume is accumulated volume when wave = 1 and zero otherwise; down volume is zero when wave = 1 and accumulated volume otherwise.
Calculation: Score = sum of: 3 if source > parabolic stop, otherwise −3; 3 if trend = 1, otherwise −3; 1 if low index < high index, otherwise −1; 1 if source rate of change > 0, otherwise −1; 2 if positive directional indicator − negative directional indicator >= 0, otherwise −2; 1 if CCI of source > 0, otherwise −1; 2 if up volume > down volume, otherwise −2; 1 if source > its SMA, otherwise −1; 1 if Awesome Oscillator > 0, −1 if < 0, otherwise 0; 1 if source momentum > 0, −1 if < 0, otherwise 0; 3 if the MACD histogram of source > its previous value, otherwise −3; 1 if SMA of bull pressure − bear pressure > 0, otherwise −1; 2 if source > the midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low, otherwise −2; and 1 if source > its Hull moving average, otherwise −1. Awesome Oscillator uses a shorter and a longer SMA; MACD uses shorter and longer EMAs and a signal EMA.
Calculation: Trend-line fractal = a top occurs when the trend line two bars ago is strictly greater than each of the two bars before it and the two bars after it; a bottom occurs when it is strictly less than each of those four bars. Compare each confirmed fractal with the preceding confirmed fractal of the same kind, using the trend-line value and the price high or low at each fractal's center.
Long: trend = 1 AND (score > a positive entry threshold OR low index < high index)
Short: trend = −1 AND (score < negative entry threshold OR low index >= high index)
