Calculation: Trend bands and direction = Set basic upper and lower bands to the selected price source plus and minus an ATR multiple. Initialize each trailing band to its basic band. Thereafter, replace the upper band with its basic band if the basic band is lower than the previous upper band OR the previous close is above the previous upper band; otherwise retain the previous upper band. Replace the lower band with its basic band if the basic band is higher than the previous lower band OR the previous close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous lower band. Initialize direction as up; change down to up when close exceeds the previous upper band, and up to down when close falls below the previous lower band. The trend line is the lower band in an uptrend and the upper band in a downtrend.
Calculation: Compression release = Compression is enabled AND the current trailing-band width, normalized between its rolling minimum and maximum, is at or below a threshold; use zero when the minimum and maximum are equal. A release occurs when the preceding bar was compressed and the current bar is not.
Calculation: Distance expansion = The current transformed distance exceeds its previous value, with the first bar compared with itself. Distance is absolute close-to-trend-line distance divided by ATR, or zero if ATR is zero; clamp it between zero and a fixed upper bound, divide by that bound, raise to a positive exponent, and clamp between zero and one.
Calculation: Directional quality score = Sum one point each for directional strength, directional acceptance, distance expansion, and a compression release in that direction. Strength passes if ADX context is disabled OR ADX is at least its threshold AND the direction's DI exceeds the opposite DI AND, if rising ADX is required, ADX exceeds its previous value. Acceptance passes if VWAP context is disabled OR close is on the direction's side of both session VWAP and the trend line. Expansion passes if distance expansion is disabled OR distance expansion occurs.
Calculation: Exhaustion = The current direction's quality score is at or below the exhaustion threshold AND direction did not change on this bar AND NOT (compression release occurred AND the quality score is at or above the compressed-break threshold). The selected signal profile jointly adjusts the quality and clue thresholds.
Calculation: Bullish clue score = Sum one point each for positive DI above negative DI, close above session VWAP, close above previous close, and distance expansion.
Calculation: Bearish clue score = Sum one point each for negative DI above positive DI, close below session VWAP, close below previous close, and distance expansion.
Calculation: Bullish setup = Exhaustion AND bullish clue score at or above its threshold AND ((countertrend signals enabled AND direction down) OR (continuation signals enabled AND direction up)).
Calculation: Bearish setup = Exhaustion AND bearish clue score at or above its threshold AND ((countertrend signals enabled AND direction up) OR (continuation signals enabled AND direction down)).
Calculation: Signal sequence = Initially neither direction is active. A bullish primary signal occurs when signals are permitted for the chart timeframe AND bullish setup is true AND was false on the previous bar AND the bullish sequence is inactive. A bearish primary signal is symmetric. A bullish primary signal activates the bullish sequence and deactivates the bearish sequence; a bearish primary signal activates the bearish sequence and deactivates the bullish sequence. Apply bullish update before bearish update if both occur on the same bar. Timeframe permission can optionally disable signals above a chart-timeframe threshold.
Long: Trend direction is up.
Short: Trend direction is down.
Long: Bullish primary signal occurred OR signals are permitted for the chart timeframe AND bullish sequence is active after primary-signal updates AND bullish setup is true AND bullish primary signal did not occur.
Short: Bearish primary signal occurred OR signals are permitted for the chart timeframe AND bearish sequence is active after primary-signal updates AND bearish setup is true AND bearish primary signal did not occur.
