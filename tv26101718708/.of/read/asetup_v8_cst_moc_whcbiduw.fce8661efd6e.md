Calculation: EMA stack = three close EMAs with short, medium, and long lookbacks. Higher-timeframe EMA stack uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with corresponding lookbacks.
Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored cumulative sum of typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, using the current chart bar; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Qualified volume = volume >= a multiplier times its chart-timeframe SMA AND, if the expanding-volume filter is enabled, volume > previous volume AND previous volume > volume before it.
Calculation: Bull score = number of true conditions among short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; close > VWAP AND VWAP > VWAP a lookback ago; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; RSI within the bullish inclusive range; MACD line > signal line AND histogram > previous histogram; qualified volume. Bear score reverses the EMA, VWAP, and MACD comparisons and uses the bearish RSI range. The two RSI ranges have separately configurable bounds.
Calculation: Bull trend count = number of true conditions among short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; close > VWAP; higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA. Bear trend count reverses the comparisons.
Calculation: Body = abs(close - open); range = high - low; body fraction = body / range when range > 0, otherwise zero; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low.
Calculation: Support levels = last completed higher-timeframe daily low and close, last completed higher-timeframe weekly low, current forming day's low, medium EMA, VWAP, current day's pre-session low, current day's opening-range low, and most recent confirmed swing low. Resistance levels use the corresponding highs, with the same previous daily close, EMA and VWAP. Session extremes accumulate through the current chart bar and reset on a new day; a swing is confirmed using equal configurable numbers of bars on each side.
Calculation: Bull pattern = any enabled condition: previous close < previous open AND close > open AND close > previous open AND open <= previous close AND body > previous body; lower wick > body times a wick multiplier AND upper wick < body times a smaller wick multiplier AND body fraction < a threshold AND close >= open; close > highest high of a preceding-bar lookback AND close > open AND body fraction > a threshold; low within a configurable percentage of any available support level AND close > open AND body fraction > a threshold AND close > (high + low) / 2; or spring. Bear pattern = any enabled condition: previous close > previous open AND close < open AND close < previous open AND open >= previous close AND body > previous body; upper wick > body times the wick multiplier AND lower wick < body times the smaller wick multiplier AND body fraction < the threshold AND close <= open; close < lowest low of a preceding-bar lookback AND close < open AND body fraction > the breakout threshold; high within the configurable percentage of any available resistance level AND close < open AND body fraction > the bounce threshold AND close < (high + low) / 2; or upthrust.
Calculation: Spring = low < lowest low over a preceding-bar lookback, storing that low as a pending level, followed on the sweep bar or within a configurable number of bars by close > the stored level AND close > open AND qualified volume. Upthrust = high > highest high over a preceding-bar lookback, storing that high as a pending level, followed within the same limit by close < the stored level AND close < open AND qualified volume. Track each direction independently; ignore new sweeps while its level is pending, and clear the level after confirmation or expiration. Both require the Wyckoff pattern to be enabled.
Calculation: Regular signal = enabled tier's directional score threshold met AND corresponding pattern AND strict trend filter disabled OR corresponding trend count >= its threshold, with the trend alternatives grouped together; also require the directional regular-signal cooldown, an allowed time window, and outside the configurable end-of-day session. The top tier requires the maximum score; the lower tier uses a lower inclusive score range. Track regular long and short cooldowns independently, updating each on its corresponding regular signal.
Calculation: End-of-day signal = inside the configurable end-of-day session AND directional score >= its threshold AND corresponding pattern AND strict trend filter disabled OR corresponding trend count >= its threshold, with the trend alternatives grouped together; also require qualified volume, the shared end-of-day cooldown, and the tier enabled. For long, the last completed higher-timeframe daily high must be unavailable OR close must be below that high by a configurable percentage; for short, the corresponding daily low must be unavailable OR close must be above that low by a configurable percentage. Update the shared cooldown on either end-of-day signal. Regular allowed windows exclude configurable blocked clock-time intervals in the specified timezone when the time filter is enabled.
Calculation: Trade levels = at the first same-direction regular or end-of-day signal while that direction is inactive, entry price = close. Set the initial long stop to entry minus an ATR multiple, or, when ATR stops are disabled, a configurable percentage below min(current low, previous low, medium EMA). Set the initial short stop to entry plus an ATR multiple, or a configurable percentage above max(current high, previous high, medium EMA). Risk is the distance from entry to initial stop. The first and second targets are entry plus risk times their respective reward multipliers for long, and entry minus those amounts for short; the second multiplier is greater. On the first target touch, move the stop to entry before evaluating full exits on that same bar. Track long and short positions independently, including the entry bar; count elapsed bars after entry.
Long: Regular long signal = true OR end-of-day long signal = true OR spring = true.
Short: Regular short signal = true OR end-of-day short signal = true OR upthrust = true.
Long: Bull pattern = true OR MACD line > signal line AND histogram > previous histogram OR higher-timeframe short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA OR bull score >= the dashboard trend threshold OR RSI < the dashboard lower extreme.
Short: Bear pattern = true AND bull pattern = false OR MACD line < signal line AND histogram < previous histogram OR higher-timeframe short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA OR bear score >= the dashboard trend threshold AND bull score < the dashboard trend threshold OR RSI > the dashboard upper extreme.
Long: Long and short positions are both inactive AND bars since the last regular short signal < the reversal lookback AND bull score >= the reversal threshold AND bull pattern = true AND bull trend count >= its threshold AND the regular allowed-time condition = true.
Short: Long and short positions are both inactive AND bars since the last regular long signal < the reversal lookback AND bear score >= the reversal threshold AND bear pattern = true AND bear trend count >= its threshold AND the regular allowed-time condition = true.
Exit: long, first target has not previously been touched AND high >= first target.
Exit: short, first target has not previously been touched AND low <= first target.
Exit: long, high >= second target OR low <= current stop OR short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA AND close < VWAP AND bear pattern = true OR time stop enabled AND elapsed bars >= time-stop threshold.
Exit: short, low <= second target OR high >= current stop OR short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA AND close > VWAP AND bull pattern = true OR time stop enabled AND elapsed bars >= time-stop threshold.
Exit: long, position remains active after full-exit evaluation AND bear score >= the reversal threshold AND bear pattern = true AND (close < VWAP OR short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA).
Exit: short, position remains active after full-exit evaluation AND bull score >= the reversal threshold AND bull pattern = true AND (close > VWAP OR short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA).
