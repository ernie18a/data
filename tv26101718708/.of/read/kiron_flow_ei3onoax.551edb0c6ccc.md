Calculation: Bar delta = sum of intrabar volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low); for zero-range intrabars use volume × the sign of the close change from the prior intrabar. If up/down-bar estimation is selected, use positive volume for up intrabars and negative volume for down intrabars, with the same tick rule for unchanged bars. Use lower-timeframe bars, taking their completed values; if none are available, estimate from the chart bar using the same selected method.
Calculation: Session CVD = cumulative bar delta, reset at the VWAP anchor; CVD high/low for a bar = its starting CVD plus the maximum/minimum running sum of intrabar deltas.
Calculation: Time-of-day relative volume = current bar volume divided by the average volume for the same time slot over prior sessions.
Calculation: Session VWAP bands = session VWAP plus/minus the corresponding multiple of volume-weighted standard deviation, reset at the VWAP anchor.
