Calculation: Anchor-period VWAP and bands = Reset at each anchor-period boundary and use the current, still-forming higher-timeframe anchor period. For each chart bar, use either the selected chart-bar price and its square, or the volume-weighted means of that price and its square across the completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; if their total volume is zero, use their unweighted means. Weight these two chart-bar values by chart-bar volume across the anchor period. VWAP is the first weighted mean; standard deviation is the square root of the second weighted mean minus VWAP squared, floored at zero. Bands are VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiple of standard deviation; the second band uses a larger multiple than the first.
Calculation: Bar delta and cumulative volume delta (CVD) = For each completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar, choose either volume times (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or signed volume according to whether close is above or below open. For a zero-range bar in the first method, or an unchanged bar in the second, use volume signed by close versus the preceding lower-timeframe close within the chart bar; for the first lower-timeframe bar use its open as that reference. Equal prices give zero. Sum these contributions for bar delta. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable, apply the selected method to the chart bar, using the preceding chart close for the fallback tick rule. Reset CVD to zero at each anchor-period boundary; CVD high and low are the prior cumulative value plus the maximum and minimum running sums of intrabar delta, including zero.
Calculation: Time-of-day relative volume = Current chart-bar volume divided by the mean volume of prior chart bars with the same time of day, using a configurable number of most recent occurrences.
Long: Time-of-day relative volume >= threshold AND high − low <= ATR × range threshold AND ATR > 0 AND bar delta < 0 AND close >= (high + low) / 2 AND ((low <= anchor-period VWAP AND close >= anchor-period VWAP) OR low <= lower second band) AND anchor-period standard deviation > 0.
Short: Time-of-day relative volume >= threshold AND high − low <= ATR × range threshold AND ATR > 0 AND bar delta > 0 AND close <= (high + low) / 2 AND ((high >= anchor-period VWAP AND close <= anchor-period VWAP) OR high >= upper second band) AND anchor-period standard deviation > 0.
Long: Low < lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND low <= the selected lower first or second band AND CVD low > CVD low at the most recent previous bar in the current anchor period whose low was below the lowest low of its own preceding lookback bars AND the distance from that previous bar >= minimum separation AND anchor-period standard deviation > 0.
Short: High > highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND high >= the selected upper first or second band AND CVD high < CVD high at the most recent previous bar in the current anchor period whose high was above the highest high of its own preceding lookback bars AND the distance from that previous bar >= minimum separation AND anchor-period standard deviation > 0.
Long: A preceding or current long absorption or divergence decision arms a setup until the next anchor-period boundary, a close below that event bar's low, expiration of the trigger window, or a long flow signal; a new long event replaces the previous setup AND the setup is armed AND bar delta > 0 AND close > open AND close > previous close AND close > lower second band.
Short: A preceding or current short absorption or divergence decision arms a setup until the next anchor-period boundary, a close above that event bar's high, expiration of the trigger window, or a short flow signal; a new short event replaces the previous setup AND the setup is armed AND bar delta < 0 AND close < open AND close < previous close AND close < upper second band.
