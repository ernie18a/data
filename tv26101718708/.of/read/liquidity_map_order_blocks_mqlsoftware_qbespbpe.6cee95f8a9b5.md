Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a pivot high or low confirmed after the configured number of right-hand bars, using the configured left- and right-hand lookbacks; its price is the high or low of the pivot bar.
Calculation: Structure state = start neutral with no latest or protected swings. On each confirmed bar process a newly confirmed swing high before a newly confirmed swing low. For a high: if already bullish and the latest high is absent or the new high is greater, set the protected low to the latest low and record an upward structure event; otherwise, if not bullish and the new high exceeds the protected high when bearish or the latest high when neutral, set state bullish, set the protected low to the latest low, and record an upward structure event. Then update the latest high. For a low: if already bearish and the latest low is absent or the new low is lower, set the protected high to the latest high and record a downward structure event; otherwise, if not bearish and the new low is below the protected low when bullish or the latest low when neutral, set state bearish, set the protected high to the latest high, and record a downward structure event. Then update the latest low. Comparisons with an absent reference are false.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure state = apply the same structure-state calculation independently on each higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Liquidity draw target = on each bar with a newly confirmed pivot, and on the last chart bar, rebuild pools separately from the most recent capped buffer of confirmed swing highs and lows within a lookback. Sort each side by price and group consecutive pivots whose price differences do not exceed an ATR multiple; retain groups with at least the minimum touches. A pool's level is its mean pivot price and its birth is its oldest pivot bar. Retain a capped history of sweeps; suppress a sweep if the most recently retained sweep is on the same side, within the configured bar interval, and its level differs by less than the clustering ATR distance. A pool is swept if a retained same-side sweep after its birth is within the clustering ATR distance of its level. For each unswept pool, score = 100 times (1 minus exp(-touch saturation coefficient times touches)) times exp(-ln(2) times age divided by age half-life) times the optional mean pivot volume divided by current volume SMA, clamped to its configured bounds, or 1 when volume scoring is disabled or unavailable, times mean pivot rejection-wick fraction clamped to its configured bounds. The high-pivot wick fraction is (high minus max(open, close)) divided by range; the low-pivot fraction is (min(open, close) minus low) divided by range. Pull = score divided by 100 times exp(-absolute distance from close in ATR units divided by proximity parameter) times a structural factor: 1 when neutral, 1 plus the structural-prior parameter when the pool lies in the structure direction, otherwise 1 minus that parameter; multiply also by the product over all other unswept pools strictly between close and the pool of (1 minus path-clearance parameter times that pool's score divided by 100). Select the greatest pull, breaking ties by the first pool in ascending high-pool order followed by ascending low-pool order. Carry the last calculated target between rebuilds.
Long: A confirmed upward structure event occurs.
Short: A confirmed downward structure event occurs.
Long: On a confirmed bar, low is below the latest confirmed swing low minus an ATR multiple AND close is above that swing low.
Short: On a confirmed bar, high is above the latest confirmed swing high plus an ATR multiple AND close is below that swing high.
Long: The chart structure state is bullish.
Short: The chart structure state is bearish.
Long: A higher-timeframe structure state is bullish.
Short: A higher-timeframe structure state is bearish.
Long: A liquidity draw target exists AND its level is above close.
Short: A liquidity draw target exists AND its level is at or below close.
