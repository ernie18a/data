Calculation: Money flow = Money Flow Index of typical price and volume.
Calculation: Impulse = EMA of the one-bar change in money flow; flow volatility = standard deviation of that change over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Ownership = bullish when money flow exceeds an upper ownership threshold, bearish when it falls below a lower ownership threshold, and otherwise unchanged; initialize as neutral.
Calculation: Bull run = consecutive bars with money flow above its midpoint; bear run = consecutive bars with money flow below its midpoint.
Calculation: Compression = maximum percentile minus the percentile rank, over a longer lookback, of the standard deviation of money flow over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Disagreement = previous disagreement multiplied by a retention factor, plus a larger increment when a confirmed price pivot high exceeds the preceding pivot high while money flow at that pivot is lower and impulse is negative, or when a confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding pivot low while money flow at that pivot is higher and impulse is positive; otherwise add a smaller increment for either divergence. Also add a failure increment when a bullish continuation or lower reversal event is followed within the current cooldown by money flow crossing below its midpoint, or when a bearish continuation or upper fade event is followed within the current cooldown by money flow crossing above its midpoint. For each side, a new event replaces the previously armed event, and a failure or expiry disarms it. Pivots use equal left and right confirmation lookbacks.
Calculation: Regime candidate = the first matching condition in this order: dead zone if compression exceeds its upper threshold and money flow is within a specified distance of its midpoint; exhaustion if money flow is at or above its upper extreme and impulse is nonpositive; capitulation if money flow is at or below its lower extreme and impulse is nonpositive; accumulation if money flow is below the lower ownership threshold and impulse is positive; distribution if money flow is above the upper ownership threshold and either impulse is negative or disagreement is at or above its threshold; bull expansion if ownership is bullish and impulse is positive; bear expansion if ownership is bearish and impulse is negative; otherwise drift. The regime changes only after the same different candidate persists for the required consecutive bars; initialize as drift.
Calculation: Event cooldown = a longer cooldown when the current regime is exhaustion or capitulation, otherwise a shorter cooldown. Each boundary event type has its own last-event bar; an event is accepted only when the elapsed bars since its preceding accepted event exceed the current cooldown.
Calculation: Boundary events = bullish continuation when money flow crosses above its upper continuation threshold, bull run is at least its required length, and impulse is positive; bearish continuation when money flow crosses below its lower continuation threshold, bear run is at least its required length, and impulse is negative; upper fade when money flow crosses below its upper extreme and impulse is negative; lower reversal when money flow crosses above its lower extreme and impulse is positive. The upper extreme is above the upper continuation threshold, which is above the midpoint; the lower extreme is below the lower continuation threshold, which is below the midpoint.
Long: Money flow is above the upper continuation threshold AND money flow is at or below the upper extreme.
Short: Money flow is below the lower continuation threshold AND money flow is at or above the lower extreme.
Long: Impulse is nonnegative.
Short: Impulse is negative.
Long: Ownership changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Ownership changes from bullish to bearish.
Long: Regime equals bull expansion OR accumulation.
Short: Regime equals bear expansion OR distribution OR exhaustion OR capitulation.
Long: An accepted bullish continuation event occurs OR an accepted lower reversal event occurs.
Short: An accepted bearish continuation event occurs OR an accepted upper fade event occurs.
Long: Impulse crosses above zero AND the absolute change in impulse over a two-bar interval exceeds flow volatility multiplied by a threshold factor.
Short: Impulse crosses below zero AND the absolute change in impulse over a two-bar interval exceeds flow volatility multiplied by a threshold factor.
