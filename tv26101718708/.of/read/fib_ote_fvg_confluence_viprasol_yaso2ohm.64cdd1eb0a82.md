Calculation: Higher-timeframe range = high minus low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; equilibrium = its (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: OTE bounds = for long bias, higher-timeframe high minus higher-timeframe range times each of two retracement parameters; for short bias, higher-timeframe low plus higher-timeframe range times each parameter. Use the first result as the top and the second as the bottom for long bias, and the first as the bottom and the second as the top for short bias. Bias is configurable as long, short, or automatic; automatic is long when the last completed higher-timeframe close >= open and short otherwise. Require a positive higher-timeframe range.
Calculation: Gap = on a confirmed chart bar, a bullish gap has top = low and bottom = high two bars ago; a bearish gap has top = low two bars ago and bottom = high. Retain each qualifying gap with its direction, bounds, and confluence status fixed at formation. Process the formation bar as the first possible tap bar. A gap remains active until, on a confirmed bar, it is filled: for a bullish gap, low <= bottom in wick mode, close < bottom in close mode, or low <= midpoint in average mode; for a bearish gap, high >= top in wick mode, close > top in close mode, or high >= midpoint in average mode. Check for a tap before removing a filled gap.
Calculation: Confluent gap = at formation, the OTE bounds are available AND, in OTE mode, gap top >= OTE bottom AND gap bottom <= OTE top; or, in favored-half mode, a bullish gap midpoint < equilibrium or a bearish gap midpoint > equilibrium.
Calculation: First tap = a confirmed bar with low <= an active gap's top AND high >= its bottom, when no earlier confirmed bar has tapped that gap.
Long: On a confirmed bar, low > high two bars ago AND bullish gap bottom > 0 AND (gap top - gap bottom) / gap bottom >= minimum gap fraction AND (displacement filter disabled OR close one bar ago > high two bars ago) AND (confluence-only filter disabled OR gap is confluent).
Short: On a confirmed bar, high < low two bars ago AND bearish gap bottom > 0 AND (gap top - gap bottom) / gap bottom >= minimum gap fraction AND (displacement filter disabled OR close one bar ago < low two bars ago) AND (confluence-only filter disabled OR gap is confluent).
Long: First tap of an active confluent bullish gap.
Short: First tap of an active confluent bearish gap.
