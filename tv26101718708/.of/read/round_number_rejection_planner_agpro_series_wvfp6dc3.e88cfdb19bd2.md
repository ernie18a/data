Calculation: Round level = round(close / step) × step, where step is either a positive manual parameter or a price-dependent step selected from an absolute-close threshold ladder.
Calculation: Band width = ATR × band-width multiplier; distance = abs(close − round level) / ATR; candle range = high − low; lower wick = min(open, close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close).
Calculation: Reaction count = number of bars in the reaction lookback, including the current bar, whose high is at or above their own round level minus their own band width and whose low is at or below their own round level plus their own band width.
Calculation: Bull rejection = distance <= maximum active distance AND high >= round level − band width AND low <= round level + band width AND close > round level + band width × close-offset multiplier AND lower wick / candle range >= minimum wick ratio.
Calculation: Bear rejection = distance <= maximum active distance AND high >= round level − band width AND low <= round level + band width AND close < round level − band width × close-offset multiplier AND upper wick / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND bull rejection = false.
Calculation: Failed reference = distance <= maximum active distance AND abs(round level − previous round level) <= level-equality tolerance AND ((close > round level + ATR × invalidation multiplier AND previous close < round level − ATR × invalidation multiplier) OR (close < round level − ATR × invalidation multiplier AND previous close > round level + ATR × invalidation multiplier)).
Calculation: Rejection score = clamp to the score range the sum of weighted, individually clamped components: directional wick ratio divided by its saturation parameter; directional close distance from round level divided by ATR and its saturation parameter; one minus distance divided by maximum active distance; reaction count divided by its saturation parameter; current volume divided by its moving average and a saturation parameter, or a fixed contribution when volume scoring is disabled; and step divided by ATR and a saturation parameter.
Calculation: Failure risk = clamp to the risk range the sum of a failed-reference contribution when failed reference is true, a contribution proportional to the maximum score minus the weighted close-distance component of the rejection score, and a contribution proportional to reaction count capped at a count parameter.
Calculation: Required score = the greater of the monitor score plus one score unit and the minimum ready score adjusted by the selected confirmation-mode offset and bounded to the score range.
Long: Bar index > max(reaction lookback, volume moving-average length, ATR length) + warmup buffer AND ATR > 0 AND step > 0 AND bull rejection = true AND rejection score >= required score AND failure risk < failure-risk threshold.
Short: Bar index > max(reaction lookback, volume moving-average length, ATR length) + warmup buffer AND ATR > 0 AND step > 0 AND bear rejection = true AND rejection score >= required score AND failure risk < failure-risk threshold.
