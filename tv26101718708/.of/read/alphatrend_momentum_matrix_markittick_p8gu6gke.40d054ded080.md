Calculation: Trailing line = Let volatility be the simple moving average of true range through the previous bar. Calculate money flow index from previous-bar typical price and current-bar volume, using the same lookback as volatility. If money flow index is at least its midpoint, the line is the greater of its previous value and previous low minus volatility times a multiplier; otherwise it is the lesser of its previous value and previous high plus volatility times that multiplier. Replace an unavailable previous line value with zero.
Calculation: Upward raw signal = Trailing line one bar ago is greater than three bars ago AND trailing line two bars ago is less than or equal to four bars ago, OR breakout override is enabled AND the absolute difference between the previous two closes exceeds the previous bar's volatility times a sensitivity multiplier AND previous close is above the trailing line one bar ago AND close two bars ago is at or below the trailing line two bars ago. Replace unavailable line values in the non-strict comparisons with zero.
Calculation: Downward raw signal = Trailing line one bar ago is less than three bars ago AND trailing line two bars ago is greater than or equal to four bars ago, OR breakout override is enabled AND the absolute difference between the previous two closes exceeds the previous bar's volatility times a sensitivity multiplier AND previous close is below the trailing line one bar ago AND close two bars ago is at or above the trailing line two bars ago. Replace unavailable line values in the non-strict comparisons with zero.
Calculation: Upward and downward counters = Initialize each counter to zero; on each bar reset its counter to zero if its corresponding raw signal occurred on the previous bar, otherwise increment it by one.
Calculation: Valid upward signal = Upward raw signal AND upward counter is greater than downward counter. Valid downward signal = Downward raw signal AND downward counter is greater than upward counter.
Calculation: Trend direction = Initialize bullish. On each bar set bullish if the trailing line one bar ago is greater than three bars ago, or bearish if it is less; otherwise retain the previous direction. Then, if breakout override is enabled and the absolute difference between the previous two closes exceeds the previous bar's volatility times the sensitivity multiplier, set bullish on a close cross above the trailing line or bearish on a close cross below it, using the same crosses as the raw signals.
Long: Trend direction is bullish.
Short: Trend direction is bearish.
Long: Valid upward signal.
Short: Valid downward signal.
Long: Valid upward signal on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the current chart bar is one chart bar after the start of a higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Valid downward signal on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the current chart bar is one chart bar after the start of a higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Trend direction on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is bullish.
Short: Trend direction on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is bearish.
Exit: long, no newly rising valid upward or downward signal on the current bar AND upward counter is less than downward counter AND previous close is below the trailing line one bar ago.
Exit: short, no newly rising valid upward or downward signal on the current bar AND downward counter is less than upward counter AND previous close is above the trailing line one bar ago.
