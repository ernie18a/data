Calculation: AlphaTrend = if MFI(typical price, lookback) >= 50, max(previous AlphaTrend, previous low − ATR × coefficient); otherwise min(previous AlphaTrend, previous high + ATR × coefficient), where ATR is the simple moving average of prior-bar true range over the lookback
Long: (AlphaTrend[1] > AlphaTrend[3] AND AlphaTrend[2] <= AlphaTrend[4] OR abs(close[1] − close[2]) > ATR[1] × sensitivity AND close[1] > AlphaTrend[1] AND close[2] <= AlphaTrend[2]) AND bars since the prior raw long signal > bars since the prior raw short signal; OR first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period AND the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar had a valid long signal
Short: (AlphaTrend[1] < AlphaTrend[3] AND AlphaTrend[2] >= AlphaTrend[4] OR abs(close[1] − close[2]) > ATR[1] × sensitivity AND close[1] < AlphaTrend[1] AND close[2] >= AlphaTrend[2]) AND bars since the prior raw short signal > bars since the prior raw long signal; OR first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period AND the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar had a valid short signal
Exit: long, bars since the prior raw long signal < bars since the prior raw short signal AND close[1] < AlphaTrend[1]
Exit: short, bars since the prior raw short signal < bars since the prior raw long signal AND close[1] > AlphaTrend[1]
