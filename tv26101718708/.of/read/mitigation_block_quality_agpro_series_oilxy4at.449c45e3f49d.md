Calculation: Bullish block = on confirmation of a pivot low with equal left and right swing lengths, use the high and low of the nearest bearish candle found by scanning backward from the pivot candle through twice the swing length; create no block if none is found. A bearish block is defined symmetrically from a pivot high and a bullish candle.
Calculation: Tracked blocks = process new blocks first and retain only the newest blocks up to the per-side cap; then retire blocks whose age from the source candle exceeds the lifetime or whose minimum absolute distance from close to either edge exceeds the distance threshold times ATR. Invalidate a bullish block when close is below its lower edge, or low is below it in wick-break mode; invalidate a bearish block when close is above its upper edge, or high is above it in wick-break mode. Invalidation is processed before touches. Retired blocks are removed; invalidated blocks remain tracked but cannot be touched again.
Calculation: First touch = the first bar after block creation on which high is at least the lower edge and low is at most the upper edge while the block is not invalidated; the creation bar can also be the first touch.
Calculation: Grade A reaction = at the reaction-window bar count after first touch, for a bullish block, (the greater of its upper edge and the highest high over the reaction-window bars immediately preceding the current bar, minus its upper edge) divided by current ATR; for a bearish block, (its lower edge minus the lesser of its lower edge and the lowest low over those same bars) divided by current ATR. Grade A requires this ratio to be at least the sensitivity-adjusted Grade A threshold. The measured window includes the touch bar and excludes the grading bar.
Calculation: Nearest block = the non-invalidated tracked block with the smallest distance from close to the block, where distance is zero inside the block and otherwise the minimum absolute distance to either edge; resolve ties in favor of the first bullish block, then the first bearish block, in creation order.
Long: A bullish block is created.
Short: A bearish block is created.
Long: A tracked bullish block reaches its grading bar AND its Grade A reaction meets the threshold AND the block is not invalidated.
Short: A tracked bearish block reaches its grading bar AND its Grade A reaction meets the threshold AND the block is not invalidated.
Long: The nearest block is bullish AND the higher-timeframe close is above its higher-timeframe simple moving average; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: The nearest block is bearish AND the higher-timeframe close is below its higher-timeframe simple moving average; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
