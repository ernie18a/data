Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low occurring a chosen equal number of chart bars before and after it; a pivot high is at least as high as every earlier high and higher than every later high in that window, and a pivot low is at most as low as every earlier low and lower than every later low. Append pivots when confirmed, high before low if both confirm on the same bar.
Calculation: Recent pivots = the latest confirmed pivots in confirmation order; for each pivot, use its type and price. Evaluate pivot-pattern decisions only on a bar that confirms at least one new pivot.
Calculation: Four-pivot changes = for the latest four alternating pivots, H1 and H2 are the earlier and later pivot-high prices, L1 and L2 are the earlier and later pivot-low prices, high change = (H2 - H1) / H1, and low change = (L2 - L1) / L1.
Long: The shortest close SMA crosses above the next-shorter close SMA, OR the shortest crosses above the next-longer, OR the next-shorter crosses above the next-longer, OR the next-longer crosses above the longest.
Short: The shortest close SMA crosses below the next-shorter close SMA, OR the shortest crosses below the next-longer, OR the next-shorter crosses below the next-longer, OR the next-longer crosses below the longest.
Long: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest three pivot types are low, high, low AND abs(third price - first price) / first price <= pattern tolerance AND the second price > both the first and third prices.
Short: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest three pivot types are high, low, high AND abs(third price - first price) / first price <= pattern tolerance AND the second price < both the first and third prices.
Long: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest five pivot types are low, high, low, high, low AND the third price < both the first and fifth prices AND abs(fifth price - first price) / first price <= pattern tolerance AND abs(fourth price - second price) / second price <= pattern tolerance multiplied by 1.5.
Short: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest five pivot types are high, low, high, low, high AND the third price > both the first and fifth prices AND abs(fifth price - first price) / first price <= pattern tolerance AND abs(fourth price - second price) / second price <= pattern tolerance multiplied by 1.5.
Long: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest four pivots alternate between highs and lows AND abs(high change) < flatness tolerance AND low change > flatness tolerance.
Short: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest four pivots alternate between highs and lows AND abs(low change) < flatness tolerance AND high change < -flatness tolerance.
Short: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest four pivots alternate between highs and lows AND high change > flatness tolerance AND low change > flatness tolerance AND low change > high change.
Long: A new pivot is confirmed AND the latest four pivots alternate between highs and lows AND high change < -flatness tolerance AND low change < -flatness tolerance AND abs(high change) > abs(low change).
