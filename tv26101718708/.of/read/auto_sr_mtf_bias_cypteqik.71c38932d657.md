Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is the highest across equal lookbacks on both sides; confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is the lowest across equal lookbacks on both sides. A pivot is available only after the following lookback bars complete.
Calculation: Double top = the latest three confirmed pivots are high-low-high, the two highs differ by no more than the configured pattern tolerance as a fraction of the first high, and the middle low is below both highs.
Calculation: Double bottom = the latest three confirmed pivots are low-high-low, the two lows differ by no more than the configured pattern tolerance as a fraction of the first low, and the middle high is above both lows.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders top = the latest five confirmed pivots are high-low-high-low-high; the middle high exceeds both outer highs; the outer highs differ by no more than the configured pattern tolerance as a fraction of the first high; and the two intervening lows differ by no more than 1.5 times that tolerance as a fraction of the first intervening low.
Calculation: Inverse head-and-shoulders = the latest five confirmed pivots are low-high-low-high-low; the middle low is below both outer lows; the outer lows differ by no more than the configured pattern tolerance as a fraction of the first low; and the two intervening highs differ by no more than 1.5 times that tolerance as a fraction of the first intervening high.
Calculation: Ascending triangle = from the latest four alternating confirmed pivots, let hdiff be the change from the earlier to later pivot high divided by the earlier high, and ldiff the corresponding change between pivot lows divided by the earlier low; absolute hdiff is less than the configured flat tolerance and ldiff exceeds it.
Calculation: Descending triangle = using the latest four alternating confirmed pivots and the high and low changes defined above, absolute ldiff is less than the configured flat tolerance and hdiff is below its negative.
Calculation: Rising wedge = using the latest four alternating confirmed pivots and the high and low changes defined above, both changes exceed the configured flat tolerance and ldiff exceeds hdiff.
Calculation: Falling wedge = using the latest four alternating confirmed pivots and the high and low changes defined above, both changes are below the negative configured flat tolerance and absolute hdiff exceeds absolute ldiff.
Long: shorter-lookback SMA crosses above longer-lookback SMA OR double bottom detected OR inverse head-and-shoulders detected OR ascending triangle detected OR falling wedge detected
Short: shorter-lookback SMA crosses below longer-lookback SMA OR double top detected OR head-and-shoulders top detected OR descending triangle detected OR rising wedge detected
