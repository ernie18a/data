Calculation: Price source = a selected chart-bar price: open, high, low, close, (high + low) / 2, (high + low + close) / 3, (open + high + low + close) / 4, or (high + low + 2 × close) / 4.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = sum of price source × weight divided by sum of weight from the anchor through the current chart bar; it is unavailable when the weight sum is zero. If cumulative volume through the current bar is positive, weight is bar volume with missing volume replaced by zero; otherwise, weight is 1. Choose the anchor as either the start of a selected calendar period, or the most recent bar with the highest high, lowest low, or highest volume within a configurable lookback ending at the current bar. Calendar choices are session, week, month, quarter, year, or a change in year / 10 or year / 100; automatic mode uses session for intraday charts and month otherwise. The first bar is always a calendar anchor.
Long: Price source > anchored VWAP.
Short: Price source <= anchored VWAP OR anchored VWAP is unavailable.
