Calculation: Pivot length = max(minimum length, round((sensitivity scale maximum minus sensitivity) × dynamic multiplier)); dynamic multiplier = EMA(ATR divided by SMA of ATR, using ATR itself when its SMA is unavailable) raised to a fixed exponent when automatic sensitivity is enabled, otherwise 1. Medium and long structure lengths are successively larger fixed multiples of the short structure length.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = high or low at a bar with the specified pivot length of bars on each side; recognize it on the confirming bar. For each structure length and pivot side, retain a separate bounded history of all detected pivots. A pivot's features are ATR divided by SMA of ATR and volume divided by SMA of volume, evaluated at the pivot bar; its stored score is 1. Its confidence is 0.5 when history size is at most the neighbor count, otherwise the mean score of the nearest neighbors by Manhattan distance between feature pairs. Evaluate confidence before adding the pivot to history.
Calculation: Active structure high or low = price of the latest confidence-qualified pivot of that side and structure length when its structure lines are enabled; a newer qualified pivot replaces it. Remove an active high when close exceeds it and an active low when close falls below it, after evaluating the current bar's decisions.
Calculation: Selected structure high and low = latest detected pivot high and low, respectively, of the selected structure length, regardless of confidence. Automatic selection chooses the longest enabled structure length; no selection leaves both unavailable. Bias factor = (close − selected structure low) / max(small positive constant, selected structure high − selected structure low).
Calculation: Raw bullish break = active short-structure high exists AND close > active short-structure high AND bullish close position >= threshold AND (maximum-volume filter disabled OR signal volume percentage <= maximum) AND (minimum-volume filter disabled OR volume >= prior volume) AND (prior-bias filter disabled OR prior bearish bias OR prior neutral bias) AND (same-color filter disabled OR current and preceding consecutive candles are not all bullish). Raw bearish break uses the active short-structure low, close < that low, bearish close position, prior bullish or neutral bias, and bearish candles instead. Bullish close position = (close − low) / (high − low); bearish close position = (high − close) / (high − low); both are zero when high = low. Signal volume percentage = volume / lowest volume over the preceding volume-lookback bars, or a fixed large value if that lowest volume is not positive. Bullish bias = close > selected structure high OR bias factor > midpoint; bearish bias = close < selected structure low OR bias factor < midpoint; neutral bias = neither bullish nor bearish bias.
Long: A confirmed pivot high at any enabled labeling structure length has confidence >= threshold.
Short: A confirmed pivot low at any enabled labeling structure length has confidence >= threshold.
Long: An active structure high at any enabled structure length exists AND close > that high AND its break label is enabled.
Short: An active structure low at any enabled structure length exists AND close < that low AND its break label is enabled.
Long: Raw bullish break AND prior bar's raw bullish break is false.
Short: Raw bearish break AND prior bar's raw bearish break is false.
Long: Candle bias coloring is enabled AND selected structure high and low exist AND no current bullish or bearish break signal AND no bullish break signal on the prior bar followed by close < open AND no bearish break signal on the prior bar followed by close > open AND close >= selected structure low AND (close > selected structure high OR bias factor > midpoint).
Short: Candle bias coloring is enabled AND selected structure high and low exist AND no current bullish or bearish break signal AND no bullish break signal on the prior bar followed by close < open AND no bearish break signal on the prior bar followed by close > open AND (close < selected structure low OR (close <= selected structure high AND bias factor < midpoint)).
