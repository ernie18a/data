Calculation: Levels = On each completed chart bar with positive ATR, generate high and low candidates using either raw pivots confirmed with equal left and right lookbacks, or kernel extrema. For kernel extrema, calculate a Gaussian-weighted mean of closes from the current bar through twice the kernel half-window ago, with weights centered on the half-window and a configurable bandwidth. If the previous mean is strictly higher or lower than both neighboring means, use respectively the high or low from one bar more than the half-window ago. Process the high candidate before the low candidate. Match each candidate to the first retained level within a configurable multiple of current ATR; update an unbroken match to the count-weighted mean of its candidates, leave a broken match unchanged, or append a new unbroken level if unmatched. If the level count exceeds its limit, remove the level farthest from the current close, including broken levels. A level cannot break on its creation bar.
Calculation: Break and retest events = On each completed chart bar with positive ATR, process retained levels in insertion order. An unbroken level breaks upward when close > level + displacement multiple × ATR AND previous close <= level; it breaks downward when close < level - displacement multiple × ATR AND previous close >= level. Freeze its price and record its direction and break bar. A broken level that has neither failed nor retested fails if, within the false-break window, close is strictly back across the level. Otherwise, on a later bar within the retest window, it retests upward when absolute(low - level) <= retest tolerance × current ATR AND, if the optional hold filter is enabled, close > level; it retests downward when absolute(high - level) <= retest tolerance × current ATR AND, if the optional hold filter is enabled, close < level. A failed or retested level cannot retest again. If multiple breaks or retests occur on the same bar, the direction of the last event processed is the direction of all break and retest markings on that bar.
Calculation: Entry risk = ATR on the entry bar × stop multiplier; entry price is the signal bar close. A new break or retest event replaces the prior live setup, including when the previous setup has not exited.
Long: The last break or retest event on the completed chart bar has upward direction.
Short: The last break or retest event on the completed chart bar has downward direction.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= entry price - entry risk OR high >= entry price + target multiple × entry risk OR bars since entry >= holding horizon.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= entry price + entry risk OR low <= entry price - target multiple × entry risk OR bars since entry >= holding horizon.
