Calculation: Rolling high = highest open across the prior 16 completed bars of the daily timeframe, which is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Rolling low = lowest open across the prior 16 completed bars of the daily timeframe, which is higher than the chart timeframe
Long: close > rolling high AND rolling high two chart bars ago > rolling high one chart bar ago
Short: close < rolling low AND rolling low two chart bars ago > rolling low one chart bar ago
Exit: Long, high reaches the profit target above the entry price OR low reaches the stop loss below the entry price
Exit: Short, low reaches the profit target below the entry price OR high reaches the stop loss above the entry price
