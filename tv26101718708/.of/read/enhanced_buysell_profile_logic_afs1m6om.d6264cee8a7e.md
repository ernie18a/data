Calculation: Estimated buy share = for each bar, with range=max(high−low, minimum price increment): weighted average of (close−low)/range, 0.5+0.5×(close−open)/range, and 1 if close is above the bar midpoint (0 if below, 0.5 if equal); normalize by the sum of the three weights. A doji within one minimum price increment has share 0.5 when neutral bars are split. Estimated buy volume = volume × estimated buy share; estimated sell volume = volume × (1−estimated buy share).
Calculation: Historical net pressure (%) = 100 × (sum estimated buy volume−sum estimated sell volume)/(sum estimated buy volume+sum estimated sell volume), over the selected lookback window or current session.
Calculation: Profile rows = divide the price range of the selected lookback window or current session into equal rows; allocate each bar’s estimated buy and sell volume across the rows it touches, weighted by overlap with the candle body and wicks and optionally by proximity to the close. Point of control (POC) is the row with greatest total volume; VWMP is the volume-weighted mean of row midpoints.
Calculation: Active buy/sell cluster = a contiguous run of adjacent profile rows, each with row volume at least the configured fraction of the highest-volume row and estimated buy/sell dominance at least the cluster threshold; retain the qualifying buy and sell runs with the greatest summed dominance. Use the top of the active sell cluster and bottom of the active buy cluster as their respective reference prices.
Long: Bullish absorption armed by a low touching the top of the active sell cluster plus the cluster buffer AND low ≤ that reference + buffer AND close > reference + buffer AND close > open AND close > previous close AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume over the longer volume lookback × confirmation multiplier) AND signal cooldown elapsed
Long: Bull reclaim armed by a prior close below the top of the active sell cluster AND previous close < reference AND close > reference + cluster buffer AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume over the longer volume lookback × confirmation multiplier) AND (reversal candle not required OR close > open) AND signal cooldown elapsed
Long: Bear pressure failure armed while historical net pressure ≤ −strong-pressure threshold and no bearish structural failure, then historical net pressure ≤ −strong-pressure threshold AND at least one bearish structural failure (price near POC and close > POC + buffer, or price near VWMP and close > VWMP + buffer, or close > top of active sell cluster + buffer) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ average volume over the longer volume lookback × confirmation multiplier) AND (previous-close confirmation disabled OR close > previous close) AND (structure confirmation disabled OR price is near POC or VWMP or an active sell cluster exists) AND signal cooldown elapsed
Long: Historical net pressure (%) > 0 AND previous historical net pressure (%) < 0
