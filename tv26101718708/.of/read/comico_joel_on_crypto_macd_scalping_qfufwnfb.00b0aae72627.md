Calculation: Trend averages = independently selected SMA, EMA, DEMA, or TEMA of close, with a shorter-lookback average and a longer-lookback average; DEMA = 2 × EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close)); TEMA = 3 × EMA(close) − 3 × EMA(EMA(close)) + EMA(EMA(EMA(close))), using the same lookback in each nested EMA.
Calculation: Trend separation = abs((shorter average / longer average if that ratio > 0, otherwise longer average / shorter average) − 1).
Calculation: Effective trend = the sign of shorter average − longer average, optionally inverted. On a change in whether the shorter average is above the longer average, reset the reversal state. If direction-change reversal is enabled, the above comparison has not changed, and whether the shorter average is rising changes, invert the original trend for the rest of that trend session, optionally requiring trend separation > a threshold. A latched reversal overrides the initial optional inversion.
Calculation: Histogram = shorter EMA of the selected price source − longer EMA of that source − EMA of their difference, calculated on the lower timeframe; use the last completed lower-timeframe bar. If the selected timeframe equals the chart timeframe, use the chart bar.
Calculation: Tick = the latest histogram, the average of the latest selected number of lower-timeframe histogram bars, or their maximum when the latest histogram > 0 and their minimum otherwise. When the selected timeframe equals the chart timeframe, use the latest histogram for the threshold averages.
Calculation: Upper and lower tick thresholds = fixed parameters, or separate EMAs of held nonnegative and negative tick values respectively. Update each held value only when the tick has its corresponding sign; otherwise retain its previous value. On a lower timeframe, multiply each new held value by the applicable normal or reversal multiplier before smoothing.
Calculation: Eligible tick = tick outside the inclusive interval from lower to upper threshold AND ((tick > 0 AND effective trend is bearish) OR (tick <= 0 AND effective trend is bullish)).
Calculation: Signal ordinal = consecutive eligible-tick count, reset to zero on an ineligible tick or when the completed position-group limit has been reached; evaluate the current eligible tick as the previous count plus one.
Calculation: Trade count = count of qualifying direction signals since the shorter average's above-longer comparison last changed, reset on that change. Increment on a bullish qualifying signal only when the count is within its permitted range, and on every bearish qualifying signal, even when an order is blocked. A qualifying signal meets the eligible-tick, position-group, signal-ordinal and trend-separation conditions.
Calculation: Position-group count = count of transitions from at least one open trade to no open trades since the shorter average's above-longer comparison last changed; reset when that comparison changes.
Calculation: Squeeze state = latch on when trend separation falls below a squeeze threshold after having exceeded the minimum trend-separation threshold in the current trend session; reset when the shorter average's above-longer comparison changes. Select take-profit and stop-loss percentages from squeeze, reversal, or normal parameters in that priority order.
Long: Eligible tick AND effective trend is bullish AND signal ordinal is within its permitted range AND trade count is within its permitted range AND position-group count < its limit AND minimum trend separation < trend separation < maximum trend separation AND chart-bar datetime is inside the configured date, weekday, and session filters AND (no prior long-order reference while in the current position OR close >= the retained long-order reference). The retained long-order reference is the latest qualifying order close × (1 − countertrend allowance), updated only when its corresponding unadjusted close is no greater than the current close; reset when flat or on a short order. If a limit offset is selected, require a subsequent price <= signal close × (1 − limit offset) for the order to fill.
Short: Eligible tick AND effective trend is bearish AND signal ordinal is within its permitted range AND trade count is within its permitted range AND position-group count < its limit AND minimum trend separation < trend separation < maximum trend separation AND chart-bar datetime is inside the configured date, weekday, and session filters AND (no prior short-order reference while in the current position OR close <= the retained short-order reference). The retained short-order reference is the latest qualifying order close × (1 + countertrend allowance), updated only when its corresponding unadjusted close is no greater than the current close; reset when flat or on a long order. If a limit offset is selected, require a subsequent price >= signal close × (1 + limit offset) for the order to fill.
Exit: long, high >= signal close × (1 + selected take-profit percentage) OR low <= signal close × (1 − selected stop-loss percentage). On the first squeezed bar while effective trend is bullish, replace both levels using that bar's close and squeezed percentages.
Exit: short, low <= signal close × (1 − selected take-profit percentage) OR high >= signal close × (1 + selected stop-loss percentage). On the first squeezed bar while effective trend is bearish, replace both levels using that bar's close and squeezed percentages.
Exit: long, trailing stop enabled AND price has reached signal close × (1 + trailing activation percentage) AND price <= highest price since activation − signal close × trailing offset percentage.
Exit: short, trailing stop enabled AND price has reached signal close × (1 − trailing activation percentage) AND price >= lowest price since activation + signal close × trailing offset percentage.
Exit: long, reversal state changes from unlatched to latched OR configured Friday or Saturday close-session ends.
Exit: short, reversal state changes from unlatched to latched OR configured Friday or Saturday close-session ends.
