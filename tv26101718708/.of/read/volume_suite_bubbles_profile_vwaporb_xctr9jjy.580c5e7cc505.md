Calculation: Volume delta = sum of signed volume of lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including the lower-timeframe bar still forming when present. A lower-timeframe bar's volume is positive if its close is above its open and negative if below; if equal, use the sign of its close change from the previous lower-timeframe close, retaining the previous sign if unchanged.
Calculation: Metric tier = for volume or absolute volume delta separately, compare the current value with its linearly interpolated percentile over each of three chart-bar lookbacks, including the current bar; the lookbacks are short, medium, and long. A percentile passes when the current value is greater than or equal to the percentile in a configurable minimum number of lookbacks. Assign the highest passing tier: big, medium, or small, with successively lower percentile parameters; assign no tier if none passes.
Calculation: Cluster tier = use the volume tier, delta tier, lower of the two tiers, or higher of the two tiers according to the selected detection method; use the volume tier when delta is unavailable. No tier ranks below small.
Long: Cluster tier is a selected tier AND ((classification is candle direction AND close >= open) OR (classification is delta direction AND volume delta > 0) OR (classification is both AND close >= open AND volume delta > 0) OR (volume delta is unavailable AND close >= open)).
Short: Cluster tier is a selected tier AND ((classification is candle direction AND close < open) OR (classification is delta direction AND volume delta < 0) OR (classification is both AND close < open AND volume delta < 0) OR (volume delta is unavailable AND close < open)).
