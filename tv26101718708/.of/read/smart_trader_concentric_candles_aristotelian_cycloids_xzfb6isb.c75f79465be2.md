Calculation: Selected candles = starting one bar before the chosen basis bar, scan backward and select the first two bars whose high-low range is greater than the preceding bar's range. Optionally also select the largest-range bar in a lookback ending at the basis bar, choosing the most recent on ties and omitting duplicates. The basis is either the current chart bar or the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Cycloid price = for each selected candle, calculate Yang-Zhang volatility at that candle using a volatility lookback and a positive floor. Set R = ln(high/low)/(2 × volatility); set r to R, ln(high/max(open,close))/(2 × volatility), or ln(min(open,close)/low)/(2 × volatility). For each positive r, calculate points sequentially from the selected candle through the current bar: at each integer bar offset x, solve x = Rθ + r sin(θ) by the source's bounded Newton-bisection method, starting from the preceding point's θ, with a limited iteration count and residual tolerance; price = low × exp((R + r cos(θ)) × volatility).
Calculation: Contact = a cycloid at the current chart bar for which abs(close − cycloid price) is less than current Yang-Zhang volatility × close × a tolerance factor OR low ≤ cycloid price ≤ high.
Calculation: Contact conviction = for each contact, count all cycloid prices at the chosen basis bar within the current contact tolerance of its current cycloid price. Let z = (ln(cycloid price/selected candle low)/selected candle volatility − R)/r. Multiply a curve base by a zone multiplier and a confluence multiplier, and cap the result at one. The outer-curve base is a positive constant; the wick-curve base is a smaller positive constant plus a positive constant times r/R. The zone multiplier is one plus a positive constant plus a positive apex bonus proportional to max(0,z), multiplied by max(0, 2/(1 + exp(−a × (abs(z) − b))) − 1), where a and b are positive parameters. The confluence multiplier is one plus a positive constant times max(0, 2/(1 + exp(−c × (count − 1)/2)) − 1), where c is positive. Select the contact with the greatest conviction, retaining the first selected candle and outer, upper-wick, then lower-wick curve order on ties.
Long: A selected contact exists AND close > its cycloid price.
Short: A selected contact exists AND close ≤ its cycloid price.
