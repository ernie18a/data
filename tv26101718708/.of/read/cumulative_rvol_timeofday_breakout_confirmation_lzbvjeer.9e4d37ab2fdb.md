Calculation: Breakout level = an enabled previous-day high or low, an enabled opening-range high or low, or an enabled positive manual level. The previous-day high and low come from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar. The opening-range high and low are the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured interval after the session open; they become available after that interval.
Calculation: Cumulative relative volume = cumulative chart-bar volume since the session open divided by the average cumulative volume at the same session time slot over completed sessions, calculated with both a longer and a shorter session lookback. Align slots by elapsed time from the session open; forward-fill missing slots in completed sessions, discard sessions whose first observed bar is later than the second slot, and require the configured minimum number of completed sessions. Use the available sessions if fewer than the lookback exist. If contract-roll filtering is enabled, use only completed sessions since the configured roll date once their count reaches the configured minimum; otherwise use continuous history.
Long: The chart bar opens within the session and before the signal cutoff AND close > an enabled previous-day high, opening-range high, or manual level AND previous close <= that same current level AND this is the first upward cross of that level since the day began while within the session and before the cutoff AND (longer-lookback cumulative relative volume >= normal volume OR shorter-lookback cumulative relative volume >= normal volume OR either cumulative relative volume is unavailable).
Short: The chart bar opens within the session and before the signal cutoff AND close < an enabled previous-day low, opening-range low, or manual level AND previous close >= that same current level AND this is the first downward cross of that level since the day began while within the session and before the cutoff AND (longer-lookback cumulative relative volume >= normal volume OR shorter-lookback cumulative relative volume >= normal volume OR either cumulative relative volume is unavailable).
