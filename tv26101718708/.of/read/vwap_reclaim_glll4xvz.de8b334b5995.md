Calculation: Daily-anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of price × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset each day; price is the configured VWAP source.
Long: Close crosses above VWAP, within the reclaim-to-retest bar limit a later bar has low ≤ VWAP + retest proximity and closes above VWAP, and within the retest-to-trigger bar limit a later bar has high > the retest bar’s high; VWAP ≥ its value at the longer slope lookback; the reclaim occurs within the configured session if the session filter is enabled. Abandon the setup if a bar closes below VWAP or either bar limit is exceeded.
Short: Close crosses below VWAP, within the reclaim-to-retest bar limit a later bar has high ≥ VWAP − retest proximity and closes below VWAP, and within the retest-to-trigger bar limit a later bar has low < the retest bar’s low; VWAP ≤ its value at the longer slope lookback; the reclaim occurs within the configured session if the session filter is enabled. Abandon the setup if a bar closes above VWAP or either bar limit is exceeded.
