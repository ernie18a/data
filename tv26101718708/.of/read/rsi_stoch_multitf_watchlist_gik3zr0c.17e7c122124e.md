Calculation: Smoothed stochastic %K = simple moving average of [100 × (close − lowest low over the lookback) / max(highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback, minimum price increment)], calculated on each configured timeframe.
Long: For any active watchlist symbol, RSI < oversold threshold OR smoothed stochastic %K < oversold threshold on any of the three configured timeframes (each timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the latest available value, with higher-timeframe values from the last completed bar).
Short: For any active watchlist symbol, RSI > overbought threshold OR smoothed stochastic %K > overbought threshold on any of the three configured timeframes (each timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the latest available value, with higher-timeframe values from the last completed bar).
