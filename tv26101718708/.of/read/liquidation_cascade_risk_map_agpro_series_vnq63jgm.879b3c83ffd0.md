Calculation: Cascade risk score = weighted combination of normalized ATR expansion, range expansion, candle-body expansion, absolute participation change, absolute participation-change velocity, and absolute distance from the slow EMA; normalize each against its longer-term baseline, using a shorter lookback for fast pressure and a longer lookback for the slow EMA. Use open interest when available, otherwise volume.
Short: Cascade risk score ≥ cascade threshold AND (a bearish candle closes below both fast and slow EMAs OR upper wick is > a parameterized fraction of candle range and close is below fast EMA) AND close is below slow EMA by > a parameterized ATR distance AND the proportion of recent bars meeting these same conditions ≥ a persistence threshold.
Long: Cascade risk score ≥ cascade threshold AND (a bullish candle closes above both fast and slow EMAs OR lower wick is > a parameterized fraction of candle range and close is above fast EMA) AND close is above slow EMA by > a parameterized ATR distance AND the proportion of recent bars meeting these same conditions ≥ a persistence threshold.
