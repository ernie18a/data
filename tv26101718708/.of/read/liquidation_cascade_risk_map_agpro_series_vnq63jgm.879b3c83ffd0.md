Calculation: Safe ATR = maximum of ATR and a positive price-floor parameter; use true range when ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Fast and slow baselines = EMAs of close, with the fast EMA using the shorter lookback.
Calculation: ATR shock, range shock, and body shock = respectively ATR / close, (high - low) / close, and abs(close - open) / close, each divided by its own SMA over the risk-context lookback; use a neutral shock parameter when the corresponding SMA is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Volume change = percentage change in volume over the fast EMA lookback; volume velocity = EMA of the bar-to-bar change in volume change, replacing an unavailable previous volume change with zero.
Calculation: Distance = (close - slow baseline) / safe ATR.
Calculation: Cascade score = clamp to the score range a weighted sum of separately clamped normalized components: ATR shock, range shock, body shock, absolute volume change, absolute volume velocity, and absolute distance. Normalize each shock by subtracting its own offset and dividing by its own scale; normalize each remaining component by its own scale. Use the same risk-context lookback for the three shock baselines.
Calculation: Upside cascade persistence = percentage of bars in the persistence lookback, including the current bar, satisfying volume > 0 AND cascade score >= cascade threshold AND ((close > open AND close > fast baseline AND close > slow baseline) OR ((min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, price-floor parameter) > wick threshold AND close > fast baseline)) AND distance > distance threshold.
Calculation: Downside cascade persistence = percentage of bars in the persistence lookback, including the current bar, satisfying volume > 0 AND cascade score >= cascade threshold AND ((close < open AND close < fast baseline AND close < slow baseline) OR ((high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, price-floor parameter) > wick threshold AND close < fast baseline)) AND distance < negative distance threshold.
Long: Volume > 0 AND cascade score >= cascade threshold AND ((close > open AND close > fast baseline AND close > slow baseline) OR ((min(open, close) - low) / max(high - low, price-floor parameter) > wick threshold AND close > fast baseline)) AND distance > distance threshold AND upside cascade persistence >= persistence threshold.
Short: Volume > 0 AND cascade score >= cascade threshold AND ((close < open AND close < fast baseline AND close < slow baseline) OR ((high - max(open, close)) / max(high - low, price-floor parameter) > wick threshold AND close < fast baseline)) AND distance < negative distance threshold AND downside cascade persistence >= persistence threshold.
