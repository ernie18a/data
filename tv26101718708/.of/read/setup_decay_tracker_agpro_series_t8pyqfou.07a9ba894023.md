Calculation: Close location = clamp((close - low) / (high - low), 0, 1).
Calculation: Automatic side = long when the shorter EMA of close is at or above the longer EMA and the longer EMA's slope is nonnegative; short when the shorter EMA is at or below the longer EMA and its slope is nonpositive; otherwise long when close is at or above the midpoint of the current rolling high-low range, and short otherwise. The slope is the longer EMA minus its value a fixed number of bars earlier. A configured side can override the automatic side.
Calculation: Setup state = start a setup when its selected side's trigger occurs and there is no setup, the previous setup is invalidated or expired, or its age exceeds the sum of the validation and tracking windows. Store the trigger bar, close, ATR, and long-side low minus an ATR buffer or short-side high plus an ATR buffer. Track the highest high for a long setup or lowest low for a short setup from the trigger bar onward. Invalidate when close reaches or crosses the stored buffer on the adverse side. Before validation, expire when setup age exceeds the validation window. After validation, expire when age since validation reaches the tracking window, or when decay score reaches the expiry threshold and age since validation reaches the validation window. Invalidation takes precedence over validation and expiry. Record whether close has ever moved to the adverse side of the trigger close; record the first subsequent return to the trigger side after validation, before invalidation or expiry.
Calculation: Favorable travel = the nonnegative distance from the trigger close to the setup's tracked extreme, divided by ATR at the trigger.
Calculation: Validation quality = rounded, bounded weighted sum of favorable travel normalized between lower and higher multiples of the required validation travel; directional close location when close holds the trigger side, otherwise a fixed low score; a fixed higher or lower score according to whether close is on the favorable side of the trend EMA; and volume divided by its moving average, normalized between lower and higher participation thresholds. Use neutral participation when volume is unavailable or its average is nonpositive. Directional close location is close location for a long setup and one minus close location for a short setup.
Calculation: Decay score = rounded, bounded weighted sum of time risk, momentum loss, volatility loss, failed follow-through, and trigger-side loss penalty. Time risk is age since validation normalized between an early fraction and the full tracking window. Momentum loss is the normalized difference between prior and current directional close changes over consecutive equal lookbacks, divided by current ATR and multiplied by a sensitivity factor; impose a minimum loss when the current directional change is negative. Volatility loss is a bounded weighted sum of normalized current ATR divided by ATR at validation and current ATR divided by a moving average of ATR; for each ratio use clamp((bad threshold - ratio) / (bad threshold - good threshold), 0, 1). Failed follow-through is one minus favorable travel normalized between required validation travel and the larger follow-through target. Trigger-side loss penalty uses a low value if the trigger side was never lost, a higher value after it is lost, and an intermediate value after its first reclaim. All normalization and weighted components use a common score scale.
Long: A new long setup is eligible AND the selected side is long AND (close is above the highest high of the preceding setup-lookback bars AND previous close is at or below that same high OR close crosses above the trend EMA AND close location is at or above a threshold OR close is above open AND above the previous high AND absolute candle body divided by ATR is at or above a threshold AND close location is at or above a threshold).
Short: A new short setup is eligible AND the selected side is short AND (close is below the lowest low of the preceding setup-lookback bars AND previous close is at or above that same low OR close crosses below the trend EMA AND close location is at or below a threshold OR close is below open AND below the previous low AND absolute candle body divided by ATR is at or above a threshold AND close location is at or below a threshold).
Long: The active setup is long AND has not been validated or invalidated AND setup age is greater than zero and at most the validation window AND favorable travel is at least the required validation travel AND validation quality is at least its threshold AND close is at or above the trigger close.
Short: The active setup is short AND has not been validated or invalidated AND setup age is greater than zero and at most the validation window AND favorable travel is at least the required validation travel AND validation quality is at least its threshold AND close is at or below the trigger close.
Long: The active setup is long AND validated AND not invalidated or expired AND close previously fell below the trigger close AND close is now at or above the trigger close AND no previous reclaim has occurred.
Short: The active setup is short AND validated AND not invalidated or expired AND close previously rose above the trigger close AND close is now at or below the trigger close AND no previous reclaim has occurred.
