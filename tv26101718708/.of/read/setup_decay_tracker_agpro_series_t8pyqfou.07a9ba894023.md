Calculation: ATR = average true range; the setup ATR is ATR on the trigger bar.
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the setup lookback, excluding the current bar; current range high/low = highest high/lowest low including the current bar.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Auto side = long when the short-term EMA is at or above the long-term EMA and the long-term EMA is non-falling; short when the short-term EMA is at or below the long-term EMA and the long-term EMA is non-rising; otherwise long when close is in the upper half of the current lookback range, short otherwise. The short-term EMA uses a shorter length than the long-term EMA.
Calculation: Validation quality = rounded 0–100 clamp of 42% travel score + 24% close quality + 20% trend score + 14% volume score. Travel score linearly scales favorable extreme-price travel / setup ATR from 35% to 135% of the validation travel threshold, clamped to 0–100. Close quality is 100 × close location for long or 100 × (1 − close location) for short when close holds the trigger side, otherwise 15. Trend score is 76 when close is on the favorable side of the long-term EMA, otherwise 34. Volume score linearly scales volume / its moving average from 0.70 to 1.35, clamped to 0–100; use 1 when volume is unavailable or its average is zero.
Calculation: Decay score = rounded 0–100 clamp of 22% time risk + 24% momentum loss + 16% volatility loss + 26% failed progress + 12% reclaim penalty after validation; before validation, 100 − validation quality. Time risk linearly scales validated bars elapsed from one-third of the maximum tracking bars to the maximum, or unvalidated bars elapsed from the validation window to four bars beyond it, clamped to 0–100. Momentum loss linearly scales (prior directional close movement / ATR − current directional close movement / ATR) from −0.10 to 1.10, clamped to 0–100; if current directional movement is negative, use at least 78. The two directional movements are consecutive, equal-length momentum lookbacks, with the earlier movement preceding the current one. Volatility loss = 68% × low-good score of current ATR / validation ATR (good 0.88, bad 0.55) + 32% × low-good score of current ATR / its longer-lookback ATR average (good 0.90, bad 0.58), clamped to 0–100; a low-good score linearly maps good to 100 and bad to 0. Failed progress = 100 − follow-through progress after validation, or 100 − travel score before validation; follow-through progress linearly scales favorable extreme-price travel / setup ATR from the validation travel threshold to the follow-through target, clamped to 0–100. Reclaim penalty is 8 if trigger side has never been lost, 88 if lost and not reclaimed, and 34 if lost then reclaimed.
Long: Long side is selected (forced long or Auto side); an eligible new setup starts on a confirmed bar when the selected-side setup is absent, invalidated, expired, or older than the validation window plus maximum tracking bars, and the selected trigger model matches: Range Acceptance = close crosses above prior range high; Trend Reclaim = close crosses above long-term EMA and close location ≥ 0.58; Impulse Continuation = close > open and prior high, body / ATR ≥ 0.45 × sensitivity factor, and close location ≥ 0.60. Also mark long when a tracked long setup validates: confirmed bar, setup age > 0 and ≤ validation window, favorable extreme-price travel / setup ATR ≥ validation travel threshold, validation quality ≥ minimum, and close ≥ trigger price. Also mark long on the first confirmed decay review bar while validated and active when decay score ≥ review threshold.
Short: Short side is selected (forced short or Auto side); an eligible new setup starts on a confirmed bar when the selected-side setup is absent, invalidated, expired, or older than the validation window plus maximum tracking bars, and the selected trigger model matches: Range Acceptance = close crosses below prior range low; Trend Reclaim = close crosses below long-term EMA and close location ≤ 0.42; Impulse Continuation = close < open and prior low, body / ATR ≥ 0.45 × sensitivity factor, and close location ≤ 0.40. Also mark short when a tracked short setup validates: confirmed bar, setup age > 0 and ≤ validation window, favorable extreme-price travel / setup ATR ≥ validation travel threshold, validation quality ≥ minimum, and close ≤ trigger price. Also mark short on the first confirmed decay review bar while validated and active when decay score ≥ review threshold.
Exit: Long, confirmed close ≤ trigger price − setup ATR × invalidation buffer; or the tracked setup expires before validation when setup age > validation window; or, after validation, valid age ≥ maximum tracking bars or decay score ≥ expiry threshold with valid age ≥ validation window.
Exit: Short, confirmed close ≥ trigger price + setup ATR × invalidation buffer; or the tracked setup expires before validation when setup age > validation window; or, after validation, valid age ≥ maximum tracking bars or decay score ≥ expiry threshold with valid age ≥ validation window.
