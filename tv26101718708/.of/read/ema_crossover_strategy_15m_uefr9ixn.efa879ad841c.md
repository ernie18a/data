Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA values = EMA of higher-timeframe close using the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with separate shorter and longer lookbacks; their previous values are from the preceding higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe ADX and ATR also use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; ADX uses equal directional-movement and smoothing lengths.
Calculation: Raw bullish crossover = previous shorter EMA <= previous longer EMA AND shorter EMA > longer EMA. Raw bearish crossover = previous shorter EMA >= previous longer EMA AND shorter EMA < longer EMA.
Calculation: EMA intersection = previous shorter EMA + (previous shorter EMA - previous longer EMA) / ((previous shorter EMA - previous longer EMA) - (shorter EMA - longer EMA)) * (shorter EMA - previous shorter EMA), provided the absolute denominator exceeds a small tolerance.
Calculation: Stop = EMA intersection at entry; target = entry price + risk-reward multiplier * (entry price - stop) for a long, or entry price - risk-reward multiplier * (stop - entry price) for a short. Keep these levels fixed for each entry.
Long: Raw bullish crossover AND previous chart bar's raw bullish crossover is false AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP of typical price) AND (ADX filter disabled OR higher-timeframe ADX >= threshold) AND (EMA separation filter disabled OR absolute difference between higher-timeframe EMAs >= higher-timeframe ATR * separation multiplier) AND EMA intersection is defined AND close > EMA intersection.
Short: Raw bearish crossover AND previous chart bar's raw bearish crossover is false AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP of typical price) AND (ADX filter disabled OR higher-timeframe ADX >= threshold) AND (EMA separation filter disabled OR absolute difference between higher-timeframe EMAs >= higher-timeframe ATR * separation multiplier) AND EMA intersection is defined AND close < EMA intersection.
Exit: long, high >= target OR low <= stop, including on the entry bar.
Exit: short, low <= target OR high >= stop, including on the entry bar.
