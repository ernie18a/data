Calculation: Moving-average rating = average of 15 scores: for each of the 6 simple and 6 exponential moving averages with progressively longer lookbacks, plus the 9-period Hull moving average and 20-period volume-weighted moving average, score +1 when close is above the average, −1 when below, and 0 when equal; the Ichimoku score is +1 when the first leading line is above the second, close is above the first leading line, below the base line, and crossed above the conversion line from the previous bar, −1 for the inverse conditions, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Oscillator rating = average of 11 scores: RSI scores +1 below 30 and rising, −1 above 70 and falling; smoothed stochastic scores +1 when both lines are below 20 and the K line crosses above D, −1 when both exceed 80 and K crosses below D; CCI scores +1 below −100 and rising, −1 above 100 and falling; ADX directional score is +1 when ADX exceeds 20 and +DI crosses above −DI, −1 when ADX exceeds 20 and +DI crosses below −DI; Awesome Oscillator scores +1 when crossing above zero or positive and forming a higher middle value than both adjacent values, −1 when crossing below zero or negative and forming a lower middle value than both adjacent values; momentum scores +1 when rising, −1 when falling; MACD scores +1 when its line is above its signal line, −1 when below; Stochastic RSI uses 14-period RSI, 14-period stochastic, and 3-period smoothing for K and D, scoring +1 when close is below the 50-period EMA, both lines are below 20, and K crosses above D, −1 when close is above the EMA, both lines exceed 80, and K crosses below D; Williams %R scores +1 below −80 and rising, −1 above −20 and falling; Bull/Bear Power scores +1 when close is above the 50-period EMA and low minus 13-period EMA is negative but rising, −1 when close is below the 50-period EMA and high minus 13-period EMA is positive but falling; Ultimate Oscillator scores +1 above 70 and −1 below 30. Each score is 0 when neither condition applies. RSI uses 14 periods; stochastic uses 14 periods with 3-period K and D smoothing; CCI uses 20 periods; ADX and directional indicators use 14 periods; momentum uses 10 periods; MACD uses 12/26/9 periods; Ultimate Oscillator uses 7/14/28 periods.
Calculation: Combined rating = average of the moving-average rating and oscillator rating.
Long: moving-average rating > 0.1 OR oscillator rating > 0.1 OR combined rating > 0.1
Short: moving-average rating < −0.1 OR oscillator rating < −0.1 OR combined rating < −0.1
