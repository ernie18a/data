Calculation: Analysis bars = chart-timeframe bars or bars aggregated to any of three independently selected timeframes. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical chart bars and the still-forming bar in real time; for a lower timeframe, use the last available intrabar within each chart bar.
Calculation: Component score = +1 if its bullish condition holds, otherwise -1 if its bearish condition holds, otherwise 0. Exclude a component until the inputs required by its calculation are available.
Calculation: Moving-average rating = mean of the available component scores for six simple and six exponential moving averages of close, using the same six ordered lookbacks for each type, plus a Hull moving average, a volume-weighted moving average, and the Ichimoku component. Each moving-average component is bullish when close is above the average and bearish when close is below it. For Ichimoku, each line is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its lookback; the conversion lookback is shorter than the base lookback, which is shorter than the second leading-line lookback. The first leading line is the mean of conversion and base. The component is bullish when first leading line > second leading line AND close > first leading line AND close < base AND previous close < current conversion AND close > conversion; bearish when second leading line > first leading line AND close < second leading line AND close > base AND previous close > current conversion AND close < conversion. Leading lines are unshifted.
Calculation: Oscillator rating = mean of the available component scores defined as follows. RSI of close: bullish below its lower threshold and rising; bearish above its upper threshold and falling. Smoothed stochastic of close: bullish when K and D are below their lower threshold AND K > D AND previous K < previous D; bearish when K and D are above their upper threshold AND K < D AND previous K > previous D. CCI of close: bullish below its lower threshold and rising; bearish above its upper threshold and falling. Directional movement: bullish when ADX is above its threshold AND previous +DI < previous -DI AND +DI > -DI; bearish when ADX is above its threshold AND previous +DI > previous -DI AND +DI < -DI. Awesome Oscillator: bullish when it crosses above zero OR is positive on both current and previous bars AND current > previous AND two-bars-ago > previous; bearish when it crosses below zero OR is negative on both current and previous bars AND current < previous AND two-bars-ago < previous. Momentum of close: bullish when rising, bearish when falling. MACD of close: bullish when MACD > signal, bearish when MACD < signal. Smoothed stochastic RSI: bullish when close < trend EMA AND K and D are below their lower threshold AND K > D AND previous K < previous D; bearish when close > trend EMA AND K and D are above their upper threshold AND K < D AND previous K > previous D. Williams %R: bullish below its lower threshold and rising; bearish above its upper threshold and falling. Bear power = low - EMA of close; bull power = high - the same EMA. Bull/bear power is bullish when close > trend EMA AND bear power < 0 AND bear power is rising; bearish when close < trend EMA AND bull power > 0 AND bull power is falling. Ultimate oscillator: bullish above its upper threshold, bearish below its lower threshold; calculate buying pressure as close - min(low, previous close), true range as max(high, previous close) - min(low, previous close), and average the ratios of their rolling sums over short, medium and long lookbacks with weights proportional to long lookback / short lookback, long lookback / medium lookback, and 1, respectively.
Calculation: Combined rating = mean of the available moving-average and oscillator ratings. Selected rating = moving-average, oscillator, or combined rating according to the selected rating base. All ratings use analysis bars; previous values refer to the previous analysis bar.
Long: Selected rating > 0 OR moving-average rating > positive rating threshold OR oscillator rating > positive rating threshold OR combined rating > positive rating threshold.
Short: Selected rating < 0 OR moving-average rating < negative rating threshold OR oscillator rating < negative rating threshold OR combined rating < negative rating threshold.
