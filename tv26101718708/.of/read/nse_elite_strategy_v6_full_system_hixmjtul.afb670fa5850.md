Calculation: Confirmed pivot high or low = a high or low with the same parameterized number of chart bars on each side; recognize it only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Bullish structure = the latest confirmed pivot high exceeds the preceding pivot high OR the latest confirmed pivot low exceeds the preceding pivot low. Bearish structure = the latest confirmed pivot high is below the preceding pivot high OR the latest confirmed pivot low is below the preceding pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish order-block range = the preceding bar's low and high at the most recent bar whose close exceeds the preceding close by a threshold percentage. Bearish order-block range = the preceding bar's low and high at the most recent bar whose close is below the preceding close by a threshold percentage.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the lowest low over a shorter lookback is below the lowest low over a longer lookback AND the lowest RSI over the shorter lookback is above the lowest RSI over the longer lookback. Bearish divergence = the highest high over the shorter lookback is above the highest high over the longer lookback AND the highest RSI over the shorter lookback is below the highest RSI over the longer lookback AND the short EMA is below the medium EMA. Both lookbacks include the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = enabled bullish engulfing OR enabled bullish pin bar OR enabled bullish inside-bar breakout OR enabled bullish doji. Bearish candle pattern = enabled bearish engulfing OR enabled bearish pin bar OR enabled bearish inside-bar breakout OR enabled bearish doji. Bullish engulfing requires close > open, previous close < previous open, close > previous open, and open < previous close; bearish engulfing reverses all inequalities. A bullish pin bar requires positive high minus low, lower wick >= body times a parameter, and upper wick <= body times another parameter; a bearish pin bar swaps upper and lower wicks. An inside-bar breakout requires the preceding bar's high < its preceding high and its low > its preceding low, and the current close above that earlier high for bullish or below that earlier low for bearish. A doji requires body <= range times a parameter and the preceding close below its open for bullish or above its open for bearish. Body = absolute close minus open; upper wick = high minus the greater of open and close; lower wick = the lesser of open and close minus low.
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of one base weight each for short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA; bullish structure OR the structure filter disabled; RSI above its midpoint and below its overbought threshold; MACD line above its signal and above zero; MACD line crossing above its signal; smoothed stochastic K above its signal and below its upper threshold; and bullish candle pattern. Add a larger weight for volume > volume SMA times a multiplier AND close > open; an intermediate weight each for bullish Supertrend and the bar overlapping the bullish order-block range; and the larger weight for bullish divergence. The larger weight is three times the base weight and the intermediate weight is twice the base weight.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of one base weight each for short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA; bearish structure OR the structure filter disabled; RSI below its midpoint and above its oversold threshold; MACD line below its signal and below zero; MACD line crossing below its signal; smoothed stochastic K below its signal and above its lower threshold; and bearish candle pattern. Add a larger weight for volume > volume SMA times a multiplier AND close < open; an intermediate weight each for bearish Supertrend and the bar overlapping the bearish order-block range; and the larger weight for bearish divergence. The larger weight is three times the base weight and the intermediate weight is twice the base weight.
Long: Bullish score >= score threshold AND ATR / close < volatility threshold AND profit-target ATR multiplier / stop-loss ATR multiplier >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND (opening exclusion disabled OR bar time is outside the configured opening exclusion) AND (closing exclusion disabled OR bar time is outside the configured closing exclusion).
Short: Bearish score >= score threshold AND ATR / close < volatility threshold AND profit-target ATR multiplier / stop-loss ATR multiplier >= minimum reward-to-risk ratio AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured session) AND (opening exclusion disabled OR bar time is outside the configured opening exclusion) AND (closing exclusion disabled OR bar time is outside the configured closing exclusion).
Long: Short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA.
Short: Short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA.
Long: Latest confirmed pivot high > preceding confirmed pivot high.
Short: Latest confirmed pivot high < preceding confirmed pivot high.
Long: RSI < oversold threshold.
Short: RSI > overbought threshold.
Long: MACD line crosses above signal OR MACD line > signal AND MACD line > zero.
Short: MACD line crosses below signal OR MACD line < signal AND MACD line < zero.
Long: Smoothed stochastic K > its signal AND smoothed stochastic K < upper threshold.
Short: Smoothed stochastic K < its signal AND smoothed stochastic K > lower threshold.
Long: Supertrend direction is bullish.
Short: Supertrend direction is bearish.
Long: Low <= upper bound of bullish order-block range AND high >= lower bound of bullish order-block range.
Short: Low <= upper bound of bearish order-block range AND high >= lower bound of bearish order-block range AND the bar does not overlap the bullish order-block range.
Long: Low minus high two bars earlier > close times fair-value-gap threshold.
Short: Low two bars earlier minus high > close times fair-value-gap threshold.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, short EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < long EMA.
Exit: Short, short EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > long EMA.
Exit: Long, low <= signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR high >= signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times target multiplier OR trailing enabled AND price has reached the signal-bar close AND low <= highest price since trailing activation minus signal-bar ATR times trailing multiplier.
Exit: Short, high >= signal-bar close plus signal-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR low <= signal-bar close minus signal-bar ATR times target multiplier OR trailing enabled AND price has reached the signal-bar close AND high >= lowest price since trailing activation plus signal-bar ATR times trailing multiplier.
