Calculation: Kernel estimates = For each bar, take the weighted mean of closes over a lookback, using the current bar's bandwidth h and lag i from zero: Gaussian weight exp(-i²/(2h²)), Epanechnikov weight max(0, 1-(i/h)²), or tricube weight max(0, (1-|i/h|³)³). Slow h is a base bandwidth optionally multiplied by a linear interpolation between minimum and maximum scales using the ATR percentile rank over its lookback; fast h is a smaller fraction of slow h. EMA-smooth the slow estimate and EMA-smooth the fast estimate over a shorter period.
Calculation: Residual measures = Residual is close minus slow kernel. Sigma is an EMA of the rolling standard deviation of residual. Upper and lower bands are slow kernel plus and minus a band multiplier times sigma. Z-score is residual divided by sigma, or zero when sigma is zero. Spread is fast kernel minus slow kernel.
Calculation: Regime = Choppiness index is 100 times log10 of the sum of true range over its lookback divided by the lookback high-low range, divided by log10 of the lookback length; use a neutral fallback when the range is zero. Efficiency ratio is the absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, or zero when the denominator is zero. Chop is choppiness index at or above its threshold. Trend is non-chop AND efficiency ratio at or above its threshold AND absolute spread greater than a threshold times sigma. Bull trend has positive spread; bear trend has negative spread. Balance is non-chop AND not trend.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = Calculate close and shorter- and longer-period close EMAs on the higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar on historical chart bars and the bar still forming on the current realtime chart bar. Bull bias requires shorter EMA greater than longer EMA AND close greater than shorter EMA; bear bias requires shorter EMA less than longer EMA AND close less than shorter EMA.
Calculation: Rejection = Bull rejection is close greater than open AND (close minus low) divided by bar range greater than a threshold. Bear rejection is close less than open AND (high minus close) divided by bar range greater than a threshold. For zero-range bars neither rejection is true.
Calculation: Divergence = Confirm a price pivot high or low after the same pivot lookback number of bars on each side; record the latest confirmed pivot price and the slow kernel at its pivot bar, updating them before testing the current bar. Bull divergence requires current low less than the latest pivot low AND current slow kernel greater than its recorded kernel AND close at or below lower band plus sigma. Bear divergence requires current high greater than the latest pivot high AND current slow kernel less than its recorded kernel AND close at or above upper band minus sigma. Each direction has its own minimum bar cooldown, measured from its previous divergence detection.
Calculation: Long setup = Bull rejection AND ((bull trend AND spread greater than zero AND spread greater than its previous value AND (close crosses above upper band OR (low at or below slow kernel plus an ATR tolerance AND close greater than slow kernel))) OR (balance AND Z-score at or below negative fade threshold) OR (bull divergence AND (balance OR bear trend))). Each setup type may be independently enabled or disabled.
Calculation: Short setup = Bear rejection AND ((bear trend AND spread less than zero AND spread less than its previous value AND (close crosses below lower band OR (high at or above slow kernel minus an ATR tolerance AND close less than slow kernel))) OR (balance AND Z-score at or above fade threshold) OR (bear divergence AND (balance OR bull trend))). Each setup type may be independently enabled or disabled.
Calculation: Confluence score = Add one point for each true condition: the corresponding trend regime OR spread is directional and widening; Z-score OR spread has the corresponding sign; the higher-timeframe filter is disabled OR the corresponding higher-timeframe bias holds; the volume filter is disabled OR volume is at least its moving average times a multiplier; the corresponding rejection holds; the regime is non-chop; the corresponding setup holds.
Long: Bull divergence.
Short: Bear divergence.
Long: Spread greater than a threshold times sigma.
Short: Spread less than negative threshold times sigma.
Long: Spread greater than zero AND spread greater than its previous value.
Short: Spread less than zero AND spread less than its previous value.
Long: Higher-timeframe bull bias.
Short: Higher-timeframe bear bias.
Long: Long setup AND long confluence score at or above its minimum AND non-chop AND (volume filter disabled OR volume at least its moving average times a multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured session) AND bars since the last long or short signal at or above the signal cooldown.
Short: Short setup AND short confluence score at or above its minimum AND non-chop AND (volume filter disabled OR volume at least its moving average times a multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in the configured session) AND bars since the last long or short signal at or above the signal cooldown.
Exit: Long, choppiness index at or above its threshold AND previous regime was non-chop.
Exit: Short, choppiness index at or above its threshold AND previous regime was non-chop.
