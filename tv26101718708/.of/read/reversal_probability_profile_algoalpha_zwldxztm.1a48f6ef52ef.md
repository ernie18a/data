Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = the high of a bar that is at least as high as every high in the preceding left-side lookback and strictly higher than every high in the following right-side lookback; it becomes available on the last right-side bar. A confirmed pivot low uses the inverse comparisons on lows.
Calculation: Active pivot levels = confirmed pivot highs as resistance and confirmed pivot lows as support. On each bar, process the high before the low; add a level only if its distance from every unbroken level exceeds ATR multiplied by the overlap parameter, using a minimum price increment parameter if ATR is unavailable. Then remove levels older than the level lookback measured from the pivot bar, mark resistance broken when close exceeds it and support broken when close falls below it, and remove the oldest stored levels until the level-count limit is met. Broken levels remain stored if broken-level retention is enabled; otherwise remove them immediately. Clear all levels when pivot-line display is disabled.
Calculation: Reversal profile = retain confirmed pivots up to the pivot-memory limit and pivot lookback, measured from each pivot bar, with the newest pivot low ahead of a pivot high confirmed on the same bar. Divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high over the pivot lookback plus the current bar into equal price bins. Assign each retained pivot within that range to its bin, clamping the upper endpoint to the last bin, and add one count of its type to that bin and each neighboring bin within the smoothing radius. Each bin's relative density is its total pivot count divided by the largest total count. Calculate the profile only on the last available chart bar, and only when the range is nonzero and at least one pivot is retained.
Long: A pivot low is confirmed.
Short: A pivot high is confirmed.
Long: Close exceeds an active resistance level.
Short: Close falls below an active support level.
Long: Close is within a nonempty reversal-profile bin, inclusive of both boundaries, AND its relative density is at least the density threshold AND its pivot-low count exceeds its pivot-high count.
Short: Close is within a nonempty reversal-profile bin, inclusive of both boundaries, AND its relative density is at least the density threshold AND its pivot-high count exceeds its pivot-low count.
