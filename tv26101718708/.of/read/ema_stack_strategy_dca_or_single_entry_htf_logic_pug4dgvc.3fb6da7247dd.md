Calculation: Moving averages = independently selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of a selected price source; the first and second averages form the primary pair, with optional third and fourth averages extending the stack in order. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. ATR uses the same timeframe and completion rule.
Calculation: Stack count = consecutive logic updates for which every enabled adjacent pair of moving averages is strictly ordered in the specified direction; reset to zero when the ordering fails. A logic update occurs on every chart bar for chart-timeframe calculations, or on the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar for higher-timeframe calculations.
Calculation: Price distance = a selectable fixed price distance, a percentage of the specified anchor price, or an ATR multiple; each use can select its own method and parameters.
Calculation: DCA level = first entry price or current average entry price, minus for a long or plus for a short the sum of geometric spacing steps through the current number of entries. The first step is a price distance anchored to the selected entry price; each subsequent step multiplies the preceding step by a configurable scaling factor. Recalculate the distance using current ATR when applicable.
Calculation: Trailing stop = highest high since position opening minus a selectable price distance for a long, or lowest low since position opening plus that distance for a short. Activate permanently for the position when that extreme reaches the average entry price plus the activation distance for a long, or minus it for a short. Calculate both distances from the current average entry price and current ATR when applicable; DCA and single-entry trailing have independent settings.
Calculation: Bracket stop and target = for an ATR bracket, average entry price minus stop ATR multiple and plus target ATR multiple for a long, with signs reversed for a short; for a percentage bracket, average entry price minus stop percentage and plus target percentage for a long, with signs reversed for a short; for a risk-reward bracket, stop at average entry price minus an ATR multiple or the chart-timeframe rolling lowest low minus a fixed buffer for a long, and average entry price plus an ATR multiple or the rolling highest high plus a fixed buffer for a short, with target at average entry price plus the signed entry-to-stop risk multiplied by the reward-to-risk ratio for a long and minus it for a short. Recalculate levels each chart bar using current ATR or swing values.
Calculation: Break-even stop = average entry price plus a fixed offset for a long or minus it for a short. Arm permanently when high reaches average entry price plus the selected activation distance for a long or low reaches average entry price minus it for a short. When enabled and armed for a bracket, replace its stop with the higher of the bracket and break-even stops for a long or the lower for a short.
Long: The first moving average crosses above the second moving average.
Short: The first moving average crosses below the second moving average.
Long: On a logic update, the bullish stack count first reaches the required count.
Short: On a logic update, the bearish stack count first reaches the required count.
Long: Position is flat AND a logic update occurs AND bullish stack count is at least the required count AND bar opening time is outside all enabled inclusive no-entry windows in the selected time zone AND configured base order size is positive.
Short: Position is flat AND a logic update occurs AND bearish stack count is at least the required count AND bar opening time is outside all enabled inclusive no-entry windows in the selected time zone AND configured base order size is positive.
Long: DCA mode is enabled AND DCA additions are enabled AND position is long AND entry count is below its maximum AND the next configured order size and remaining cash allowance are positive AND bar opening time is outside enabled no-entry windows if blocking additions is enabled AND low is at or below the DCA level.
Short: DCA mode is enabled AND DCA additions are enabled AND position is short AND entry count is below its maximum AND the next configured order size and remaining cash allowance are positive AND bar opening time is outside enabled no-entry windows if blocking additions is enabled AND high is at or above the DCA level.
Exit: long, DCA mode AND average-entry target selected AND high reaches average entry price plus its selected profit distance.
Exit: short, DCA mode AND average-entry target selected AND low reaches average entry price minus its selected profit distance.
Exit: long, DCA mode AND last-entry target selected AND entry count is at least its maximum AND high reaches the most recent DCA level, or the initial fill price if there has been no addition, plus a fixed or percentage offset based on that price.
Exit: short, DCA mode AND last-entry target selected AND entry count is at least its maximum AND low reaches the most recent DCA level, or the initial fill price if there has been no addition, minus a fixed or percentage offset based on that price.
Exit: long, DCA mode AND average-entry target selected AND moving-average kill switch enabled AND first moving average is below the second moving average at chart-bar close.
Exit: short, DCA mode AND average-entry target selected AND moving-average kill switch enabled AND first moving average is above the second moving average at chart-bar close.
Exit: long, trailing is enabled for the current mode AND trailing is active AND low reaches the trailing stop.
Exit: short, trailing is enabled for the current mode AND trailing is active AND high reaches the trailing stop.
Exit: long, single-entry mode AND moving-average exit selected AND first moving average crosses below the second moving average at chart-bar close.
Exit: short, single-entry mode AND moving-average exit selected AND first moving average crosses above the second moving average at chart-bar close.
Exit: long, single-entry mode AND time stop selected AND confirmed chart bars since position opening reach the configured count.
Exit: short, single-entry mode AND time stop selected AND confirmed chart bars since position opening reach the configured count.
Exit: long, single-entry mode AND a bracket is selected AND low reaches its effective stop OR high reaches its target.
Exit: short, single-entry mode AND a bracket is selected AND high reaches its effective stop OR low reaches its target.
