Calculation: Candle range = high - low.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / simple moving average of volume over the absorption lookback, including the current bar; use 0 if that average is 0.
Calculation: Range ratio = max(candle range / simple moving average of candle range over the absorption lookback, including the current bar, 0.05); use 1 if that average is 0.
Calculation: Absorption score = volume ratio / range ratio.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / candle range; use 0.5 if candle range is 0.
Calculation: Intrabar delta = sum of volume on completed lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar whose close > open minus sum of volume on those whose close < open; bars whose close = open contribute 0.
Long: close >= open.
Short: close < open.
