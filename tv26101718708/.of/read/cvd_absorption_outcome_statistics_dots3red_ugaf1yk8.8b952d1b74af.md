Calculation: Bar delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1) when high > low; otherwise 0. Treat missing volume as 0.
Calculation: Pending event = initially absent; on each completed bar, first clear an existing event if its age in bars reaches the outcome window or if its event close − current close is at least a reversal multiplier × its event ATR for a buying-absorbed event, or if current close − its event close is at least a reversal multiplier × its event ATR for a selling-absorbed event. After clearing, if absent and either entry condition is met, store the current bar index, close, ATR, and event type. Buying-absorbed takes precedence if both conditions hold.
Long: Pending event is absent AND volume SMA > 0 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier AND high − low <= ATR × range multiplier AND volume > 0 AND bar delta <= −volume × delta fraction.
Short: Pending event is absent AND volume SMA > 0 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier AND high − low <= ATR × range multiplier AND volume > 0 AND bar delta >= volume × delta fraction.
