Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of typical price (high + low + close) / 3 times volume divided by cumulative volume, resetting at each trading session.
Calculation: Relative strength = weighted sum of the current mapped daily close divided by its values at four progressively longer chart-bar lookbacks; the shortest-lookback ratio has twice the weight of each other ratio, and the weights sum to one. On historical bars, map the last completed daily bar when daily is higher than the chart timeframe, the last available daily bar within each chart bar when daily is lower, and the current bar when timeframes are equal.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the greater of the current high reduced by the trailing percentage and the previous long trailing stop; initialize the previous stop to zero when flat.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the lesser of the current low increased by the trailing percentage and the previous short trailing stop; initialize the previous stop to 9999 when flat.
Long: Time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive date range AND (position is flat OR opposite-signal closing is enabled) AND (normal mode AND RSI was at or below its oversold threshold within the lookback ending on the current bar AND SMA crosses above session VWAP OR flip mode AND RSI was at or above its overbought threshold within the lookback ending on the current bar AND SMA crosses below session VWAP) AND every enabled trend qualifier is satisfied: close > medium-term and long-term close SMAs; medium-term SMA > long-term SMA; long-term SMA > its value at the configured earlier bar; short-term SMA > medium-term and long-term SMAs; close > short-term SMA; close / lowest low over its lookback - 1 >= configured minimum; 1 - close / highest high over its lookback <= configured maximum; relative strength >= its threshold. The all-criteria qualifier enables all these conditions together.
Short: Time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive date range AND (position is flat OR opposite-signal closing is enabled) AND (long-and-short mode AND RSI was at or above its overbought threshold within the lookback ending on the current bar AND SMA crosses below session VWAP OR flip mode AND RSI was at or below its oversold threshold within the lookback ending on the current bar AND SMA crosses above session VWAP).
Exit: long, take profit enabled AND high >= entry price increased by the take-profit percentage OR trailing stop enabled AND low <= active long trailing stop OR trailing stop disabled AND stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price reduced by the stop-loss percentage OR opposite-signal closing enabled AND short-entry conditions are satisfied.
Exit: short, take profit enabled AND low <= entry price reduced by the take-profit percentage OR trailing stop enabled AND high >= active short trailing stop OR trailing stop disabled AND stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price increased by the stop-loss percentage OR opposite-signal closing enabled AND long-entry conditions are satisfied.
