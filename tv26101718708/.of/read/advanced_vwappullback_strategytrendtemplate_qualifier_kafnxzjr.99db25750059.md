Calculation: weighted daily momentum = 0.4 × (daily close / daily close from a longer lookback) + 0.2 × (daily close / daily close from each of two progressively longer lookbacks) + 0.2 × (daily close / daily close from the longest lookback); use the daily bar, which is higher timeframe when applicable and may still be forming
Long: (order mode is not Flip AND RSI was at or below its oversold threshold within the recent lookback AND the chart-timeframe SMA crosses above VWAP) OR (order mode is Flip AND RSI was at or above its overbought threshold within the recent lookback AND the chart-timeframe SMA crosses below VWAP); AND every enabled trend qualifier passes: close > 150-period SMA AND close > 200-period SMA; 150-period SMA > 200-period SMA; 200-period SMA > its value at a longer lookback; 50-period SMA > 150-period SMA AND 50-period SMA > 200-period SMA; close > 50-period SMA; close is at least 25% above the lowest low over a long lookback; close is within 25% of the highest high over that same lookback; weighted daily momentum >= its configured threshold; AND datetime is within the configured date range; AND (fast exit is enabled OR position is flat)
Short: (order mode is Long & Short AND RSI was at or above its overbought threshold within the recent lookback AND the chart-timeframe SMA crosses below VWAP) OR (order mode is Flip AND RSI was at or below its oversold threshold within the recent lookback AND the chart-timeframe SMA crosses above VWAP); AND datetime is within the configured date range; AND (fast exit is enabled OR position is flat)
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss fraction) when fixed stop loss is enabled OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured take-profit fraction) when take profit is enabled OR low <= the highest value since entry of high × (1 − configured trailing-stop fraction) when trailing stop is enabled
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss fraction) when fixed stop loss is enabled OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured take-profit fraction) when take profit is enabled OR high >= the lowest value since entry of low × (1 + configured trailing-stop fraction) when trailing stop is enabled
