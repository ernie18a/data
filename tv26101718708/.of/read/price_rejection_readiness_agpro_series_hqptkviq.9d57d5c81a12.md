Calculation: Candle measures = body = abs(close - open); range = max(high - low, price precision); safe body = max(body, price precision); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower and upper wick ratios = respective wick / safe body; lower and upper wick shares = respective wick / range; close location = (close - low) / range. Price precision is a configurable positive substitute because the data has no tick-size field.
Calculation: Rejection score for each side = round(clamp(wick contribution + close contribution + reference contribution + volume contribution + follow-through contribution, 0, maximum score)). Clamp each contribution to its stated range. For the bullish side, wick contribution = maximum wick points × lower wick ratio / (minimum wick ratio × wick scaling factor); close contribution = maximum close points × (close location - close baseline) / close scaling factor. For the bearish side, use upper wick ratio and (bearish close baseline - close location) / close scaling factor. Volume contribution = maximum volume points × (volume / max(SMA(volume), minimum volume baseline) - volume baseline ratio) / volume scaling factor. The reference contribution is high when the bullish low is at or below the previous longer-lookback lowest low plus an ATR offset, or the bearish high is at or above the previous longer-lookback highest high minus an ATR offset; otherwise it is medium when the bullish low is below the previous shorter-lookback lowest low plus a separate ATR offset, or the bearish high is above the previous shorter-lookback highest high minus a separate ATR offset; otherwise it is low. Follow-through contribution is high when bullish close > open or bearish close < open; otherwise medium when bullish close > previous close or bearish close < previous close; otherwise low. The contribution limits, baselines, offsets, scaling factors, and score thresholds are configurable parameters.
Calculation: Active plan = the most recent readiness decision, replacing any previous plan, subject to a cooldown measured from the previous readiness decision. At its bar, save its side, bar index, bullish body boundary = min(open, close) or bearish body boundary = max(open, close), bullish confirmation rail = high + ATR × confirmation offset or bearish confirmation rail = low - ATR × confirmation offset, bullish invalidation = low - ATR × risk buffer or bearish invalidation = high + ATR × risk buffer, and target = close plus for bullish or minus for bearish the product of abs(close - invalidation) and target multiple. Reset confirmed, failed, and target-hit flags when a new plan is created. Evaluate subsequent decisions only on later closed bars; on each bar create a new plan before evaluating the active plan. Failure takes precedence over confirmation and target; confirmation and target may both occur on the same bar. Set each corresponding flag when its event occurs. A target hit does not prevent later confirmation or failure.
Long: On a closed bar, lower wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND lower wick share >= minimum wick share AND close location >= minimum close location AND lower wick > upper wick AND bullish rejection score >= minimum score AND bars since previous readiness decision >= cooldown, or no previous readiness decision exists.
Short: On a closed bar, upper wick ratio >= minimum wick ratio AND upper wick share >= minimum wick share AND close location <= 1 - minimum close location AND upper wick > lower wick AND bearish rejection score >= minimum score AND bars since previous readiness decision >= cooldown, or no previous readiness decision exists.
Long: Active plan is bullish AND current closed bar is later than its creation bar AND plan has not failed or confirmed AND close >= invalidation AND ((strict mode AND close > confirmation rail) OR (balanced mode AND close > body boundary AND close > open) OR (fast mode AND high >= confirmation rail AND close > body boundary)).
Short: Active plan is bearish AND current closed bar is later than its creation bar AND plan has not failed or confirmed AND close <= invalidation AND ((strict mode AND close < confirmation rail) OR (balanced mode AND close < body boundary AND close < open) OR (fast mode AND low <= confirmation rail AND close < body boundary)).
Exit: long, active plan is bullish AND current closed bar is later than its creation bar AND plan has not failed AND close < invalidation.
Exit: short, active plan is bearish AND current closed bar is later than its creation bar AND plan has not failed AND close > invalidation.
Exit: long, active plan is bullish AND current closed bar is later than its creation bar AND plan has not failed or hit its target AND close >= invalidation AND high >= target.
Exit: short, active plan is bearish AND current closed bar is later than its creation bar AND plan has not failed or hit its target AND close <= invalidation AND low <= target.
