Calculation: Drawdown = (close / highest close over the major-high lookback - 1); use available history when shorter than the lookback.
Calculation: Average liquidity = SMA of close * volume divided by a liquidity scaling parameter, over a liquidity lookback.
Calculation: New-low count = number of bars in a count lookback whose low is less than or equal to the lowest low over the floor lookback ending on the preceding bar; use available history for the floor lookback.
Calculation: Aged support = lowest low over a support lookback ending an age parameter bars ago; clamp the support lookback to available history.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume - SMA of volume) / standard deviation of volume over the volume lookback; use available history and return zero when the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Invalidation level = at the latest long signal, clamp the lowest low over a stop lookback reduced by a stop percentage between the signal close reduced by a maximum stop-distance percentage and the signal close reduced by a smaller minimum stop-distance percentage; if that lowest low is unavailable or nonpositive, use the signal-bar low.
Long: Drawdown <= negative minimum drawdown AND drawdown <= shallower band edge AND drawdown > deeper band edge AND average liquidity >= minimum liquidity AND new-low count <= maximum count AND bars since previous long signal >= cooldown AND close > open AND close >= low + a minimum fraction of (high - low) AND high > low AND ((low < aged support AND close > aged support) OR (previous low < previous aged support AND previous close < previous aged support AND close > previous aged support) OR (lowest low over the spring lookback ending on the previous bar < current aged support AND lowest close over that same window < current aged support AND previous close <= current aged support AND close > current aged support)).
Exit: long, close < invalidation level AND volume z-score >= minimum volume z-score AND bars since latest long signal <= invalidation lifetime.
