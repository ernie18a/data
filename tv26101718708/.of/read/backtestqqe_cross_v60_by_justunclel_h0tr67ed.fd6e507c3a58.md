Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA(RSI(close, lookback), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Fast QQE trailing line = recursive trailing band around smoothed RSI; band distance is EMA(EMA(abs(change in smoothed RSI), 2 × RSI lookback − 1), 2 × RSI lookback − 1) × QQE factor; use the lower band while trend is up and the upper band while trend is down, switching up when smoothed RSI crosses above the prior upper band and down when it crosses below the prior lower band; each band trails by taking the maximum of its prior value and the new lower band during an uptrend, and the minimum of its prior value and the new upper band during a downtrend
Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow moving averages = selected moving average types of close at progressively longer lookbacks; alternate ribbon averages use the same types on the chart timeframe with proportionally longer lookbacks
Calculation: Medium MA direction = rising or falling over the last three chart bars
Long: smoothed RSI crosses above 50 + threshold OR smoothed RSI crosses above the fast QQE trailing line OR smoothed RSI crosses above 50 OR (flat AND the selected entry signal crosses upward on the previous chart bar AND its applicable filters pass; optionally require this to be the first long entry signal in the current trend)
Short: smoothed RSI crosses below 50 − threshold OR smoothed RSI crosses below the fast QQE trailing line OR smoothed RSI crosses below 50 OR (flat AND the selected entry signal crosses downward on the previous chart bar AND its applicable filters pass; optionally require this to be the first short entry signal in the current trend)
Exit: long, selected close signal crosses downward; when the close signal is the QQE trailing-line cross and the entry signal is not that same cross, also exit on a downward zero cross or downward threshold-channel cross
Exit: short, selected close signal crosses upward; when the close signal is the QQE trailing-line cross and the entry signal is not that same cross, also exit on an upward zero cross or upward threshold-channel cross
