Calculation: Twin Range Filter = calculate two smoothed ranges from close-to-close absolute changes, using a shorter and a longer lookback, each multiplied by its own factor; average the ranges. Apply this range to close in a recursive filter that ratchets upward or downward, then derive upper and lower bands by adding or subtracting the range from the filter. A bullish cross is close crossing above the filter; a bearish cross is close crossing below it.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend state = bullish when the previous completed bar's close is above its Twin Range Filter; otherwise bearish. Apply the same Twin Range Filter parameters to each higher timeframe.
Calculation: VIX trend state = bullish when the current, possibly still-forming higher-timeframe bar's close is above its Twin Range Filter; otherwise bearish. Apply the same Twin Range Filter parameters as for the other trend states.
Calculation: Strat candle type = outside if high > previous high and low < previous low; inside if high <= previous high and low >= previous low; otherwise 2U if high > previous high, or 2D if not. Bullish patterns are outside-2D-2U, outside-inside-2U, or 2D-2U; bearish patterns are outside-2U-2D, outside-inside-2D, or 2U-2D.
Calculation: Session VWAP deviation bands = session-anchored volume-weighted average of hlc3, with bands at one and two standard deviations above and below it.
Calculation: Overextended up = close at or above the session +2σ band OR (close − overnight open) / previous completed higher-timeframe ATR >= threshold. Overextended down = close at or below the session −2σ band OR (close − overnight open) / previous completed higher-timeframe ATR <= −threshold.
Calculation: Long stop = signal bar low − ATR × multiplier. Short stop = signal bar high + ATR × multiplier. ATR is calculated on chart-timeframe bars.
Long: confirmed chart-timeframe close crosses above its Twin Range Filter AND higher-timeframe trend state is bullish AND (either of the two longer higher-timeframe trend states is bullish) AND VIX higher-timeframe trend state is bearish AND NOT overextended up AND time is 9:45 a.m.–12:00 p.m. New York time.
Long: bullish Strat pattern AND (outside-2D-2U or outside-inside-2U AND any open or close of its three bars is at or below the corresponding session −1σ band OR 2D-2U AND any open or close of its two bars is below the corresponding session −2σ band AND overextended down) AND VIX higher-timeframe trend state is bearish AND time is 9:45 a.m.–12:00 p.m. New York time.
Short: confirmed chart-timeframe close crosses below its Twin Range Filter AND higher-timeframe trend state is bearish AND VIX higher-timeframe trend state is bullish AND time is 9:45 a.m.–12:00 p.m. New York time.
Short: bearish Strat pattern AND (outside-2U-2D or outside-inside-2D AND any open or close of its three bars is at or above the corresponding session +1σ band OR 2U-2D AND any open or close of its two bars is above the corresponding session +2σ band AND overextended up) AND (VIX higher-timeframe trend state is bullish OR overextended up) AND time is 9:45 a.m.–12:00 p.m. New York time.
Exit: long, low <= long stop.
Exit: short, high >= short stop.
