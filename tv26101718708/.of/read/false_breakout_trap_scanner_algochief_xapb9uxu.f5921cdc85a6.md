Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; available after the bars on the right complete. Keep the most recent qualifying pivot as the anchor; a high qualifies when it exceeds the lowest low over twice the pivot lookback by at least a minimum ATR multiple, and a low qualifies when the highest high over twice the pivot lookback exceeds it by at least a minimum ATR multiple.
Calculation: Long 0.618 retracement = paired swing high − 0.618 × (paired swing high − swept swing low); short 0.618 retracement = paired swing low + 0.618 × (swept swing high − paired swing low).
Long: Current low < most recent qualifying swing low AND previous close ≥ that swing low, establishing a sweep; within 1–5 bars after the sweep and within the fib entry window, close > swept swing low AND low ≤ long 0.618 retracement AND close ≥ swept swing low; if candle confirmation is enabled, close > open AND close − low ≥ 0.25 × (high − low); if the trend filter is enabled, close > 200-period EMA; and enough bars have elapsed since the previous signal to satisfy the cooldown.
Short: Current high > most recent qualifying swing high AND previous close ≤ that swing high, establishing a sweep; within 1–5 bars after the sweep and within the fib entry window, close < swept swing high AND high ≥ short 0.618 retracement AND close ≤ swept swing high; if candle confirmation is enabled, close < open AND high − close ≥ 0.25 × (high − low); if the trend filter is enabled, close < 200-period EMA; and enough bars have elapsed since the previous signal to satisfy the cooldown.
