Calculation: Structural anchors = Confirm pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks. At confirmation, accept a pivot high only if it exceeds the lowest low of the latest twice-the-pivot-lookback bars by at least an ATR multiple; accept a pivot low only if the highest high of those bars exceeds it by at least that ATR multiple. Maintain the latest accepted high and low separately. In limited scan mode, accept only the first configured number of qualifying pivots of each type, counted from the start of the data.
Calculation: Active sweeps = When both anchors exist, a low sweep occurs if low < current low anchor AND previous close >= current low anchor; record the bar, low anchor and current high anchor. A high sweep occurs if high > current high anchor AND previous close <= current high anchor; record the bar, high anchor and current low anchor. A new sweep of the same type replaces the previous one. Clear a low sweep if its age > entry window + 5 bars OR close < recorded low anchor - 3 × current ATR; clear a high sweep if its age > entry window + 5 bars OR close > recorded high anchor + 3 × current ATR. Clear a sweep after its signal. Process low sweeps and long signals before high sweeps and short signals on each bar.
Long: An active low sweep exists AND sweep age >= 1 AND sweep age <= 5 AND sweep age <= entry window AND close > recorded low anchor AND low <= recorded high anchor - 0.618 × (recorded high anchor - recorded low anchor) AND (candle confirmation disabled OR (close > open AND close - low >= 0.25 × (high - low))) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA of close) AND (no prior signal exists OR bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown).
Short: An active high sweep exists AND sweep age >= 1 AND sweep age <= 5 AND sweep age <= entry window AND close < recorded high anchor AND high >= recorded low anchor + 0.618 × (recorded high anchor - recorded low anchor) AND (candle confirmation disabled OR (close < open AND high - close >= 0.25 × (high - low))) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA of close) AND (no prior signal exists OR bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown) AND no long signal occurred on the current bar.
