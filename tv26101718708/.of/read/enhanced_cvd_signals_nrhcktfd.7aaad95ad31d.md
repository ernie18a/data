Calculation: Raw CVD candle = accumulate signed volume from completed lower-timeframe bars in chronological order within each chart bar; its open is the cumulative value before those bars, high and low are the maximum and minimum cumulative values including the open, and close is the final cumulative value. Reset the cumulative value at the start of each selected higher-timeframe anchor period or selected session. In session mode, sign lower-timeframe volume positive when close > open, negative when close < open, otherwise positive when close >= the previous lower-timeframe close and negative otherwise. In timeframe-anchor mode, sign volume positive when close > open, negative when close < open; when close = open, use the comparison with the previous lower-timeframe close, carrying forward the previous volume sign when those closes are equal. Use only available completed lower-timeframe bars; tick mode requires tick data.
Calculation: Bar delta = raw CVD close minus raw CVD open. Average absolute delta = simple moving average of absolute bar delta, including the current bar; absorption and turnaround use the same lookback.
Calculation: Displayed CVD open and close = select raw CVD open and close, their linear regressions with a common lookback, or Heikin-Ashi CVD open and close. Heikin-Ashi close is the mean of raw CVD open, high, low and close; its open is raw CVD open on an anchor reset or when the previous Heikin-Ashi open is unavailable, otherwise the mean of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close. If detrending is enabled, subtract the same current simple moving average of the selected CVD close from both selected values; require that average to be available.
Calculation: Near a high = the highest price high of the current and previous chart bars >= the highest price high over the selected extreme lookback including the current bar. Near a low = the lowest price low of the current and previous chart bars <= the lowest price low over the selected extreme lookback including the current bar. Each signal family has its own extreme lookback.
Calculation: Prior delta run = the maximal consecutive sequence of nonzero bar deltas with the same sign ending on the previous chart bar; its length is the number of bars and its sum is their net delta.
Calculation: Cooldown condition = the number of chart bars since the last emitted signal of the same family and direction >= that family's cooldown, or no such signal has occurred. Each family and direction maintains its own last emitted signal; evaluate signals on completed chart bars.
