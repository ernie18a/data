Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; range = high - low.
Calculation: Bullish pattern cooldown = bars since the last bullish engulfing or hammer decision; bearish pattern cooldown = bars since the last bearish engulfing or shooting-star decision. Each cooldown passes if no corresponding pattern decision has occurred or if its value is greater than the pattern-gap parameter.
Calculation: Bullish pullback cooldown = bars since the last bullish pullback decision; bearish pullback cooldown = bars since the last bearish pullback decision. Each cooldown passes if no corresponding pullback decision has occurred or if its value is greater than the pullback-gap parameter.
Long: Short-term EMA of close crosses above medium-term EMA of close AND close > long-term EMA of close AND RSI of close < upper cross RSI threshold.
Short: Short-term EMA of close crosses below medium-term EMA of close AND close < long-term EMA of close AND RSI of close > lower cross RSI threshold.
Long: Medium-term EMA of close > long-term EMA of close AND low <= medium-term EMA AND close > medium-term EMA AND close > open AND close > long-term EMA AND bullish pullback cooldown passes.
Short: Medium-term EMA of close < long-term EMA of close AND high >= medium-term EMA AND close < medium-term EMA AND close < open AND close < long-term EMA AND bearish pullback cooldown passes.
Long: Bullish pattern cooldown passes AND close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close >= previous open AND open <= previous close AND candle body > previous candle body multiplied by the engulfing body factor AND candle body >= ATR multiplied by the engulfing ATR factor AND previous close < close at an earlier lookback bar.
Short: Bearish pattern cooldown passes AND close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close <= previous open AND open >= previous close AND candle body > previous candle body multiplied by the engulfing body factor AND candle body >= ATR multiplied by the engulfing ATR factor AND previous close > close at the same earlier lookback bar.
Long: Bullish pattern cooldown passes AND candle body > 0 AND range >= ATR multiplied by the wick-pattern ATR factor AND lower wick >= candle body multiplied by the longer-wick factor AND upper wick <= candle body multiplied by the shorter-wick factor AND previous close < close at the earlier lookback bar AND RSI of close < lower wick-pattern RSI threshold.
Short: Bearish pattern cooldown passes AND candle body > 0 AND range >= ATR multiplied by the wick-pattern ATR factor AND upper wick >= candle body multiplied by the longer-wick factor AND lower wick <= candle body multiplied by the shorter-wick factor AND previous close > close at the earlier lookback bar AND RSI of close > upper wick-pattern RSI threshold.
