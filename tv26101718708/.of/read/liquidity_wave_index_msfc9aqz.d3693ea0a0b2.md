Calculation: Adaptive EMA(x, length, lag) = recursively alpha*x + (1-alpha)*previous value, initialized to x; alpha = 2/(length+1) * min(1, delta/highest(delta, length)), delta = abs(x-x[lag]); division by zero returns zero.
Calculation: Cycle and signal lines = adaptive EMA of the selected OHLC-derived price source with the base length and adaptation lookback gives baseline; deviation = source-baseline; normalized deviation = deviation/(0.015 * adaptive EMA(abs(deviation), base length, adaptation lookback)), with zero returned for zero denominator. Apply adaptive EMA to normalized deviation using the cycle smoothing length and fast lag, then adaptive EMA to that result using max(2, round(cycle smoothing length*0.35)) and slow lag. Apply the same final EMA smoothing length separately to both results to obtain the cycle and signal lines.
Calculation: Liquidity pressure = scale * EMA(volume * (close-open)/(high-low), pressure smoothing length) / EMA(volume, pressure smoothing length); the candle body/range ratio is zero when the range is zero.
Calculation: Matched pivot pairs = on each completed bar, confirm price high/low and cycle-line high/low pivots using equal left and right pivot lengths. Store the three most recent confirmed oscillator pivots of each type. When a price pivot is confirmed, match it to the nearest already-confirmed oscillator pivot of the same type within the maximum pivot gap, choosing the most recent oscillator pivot on a tie. Compare the resulting pair with each of the three previously stored matched pairs of the same type, then store it, discarding the oldest. The price pivots in a comparison must be separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum pivot span. Evaluate divergence on the price pivot's confirmation bar.
Long: Cycle line crosses above signal line AND (threshold filter disabled OR cycle line < negative threshold) AND (pressure confirmation off OR same-direction confirmation selected AND liquidity pressure > 0 OR zero-cross confirmation selected AND liquidity pressure crosses above 0).
Short: Cycle line crosses below signal line AND (threshold filter disabled OR cycle line > positive threshold) AND (pressure confirmation off OR same-direction confirmation selected AND liquidity pressure < 0 OR zero-cross confirmation selected AND liquidity pressure crosses below 0).
Long: Liquidity pressure crosses above 0.
Short: Liquidity pressure crosses below 0.
Long: A newly confirmed matched low pair has an eligible previous matched low pair AND (current price low < previous price low AND current oscillator low > previous oscillator low OR hidden divergence enabled AND current price low > previous price low AND current oscillator low < previous oscillator low) AND (zero-line filter disabled OR both oscillator lows <= 0).
Short: A newly confirmed matched high pair has an eligible previous matched high pair AND (current price high > previous price high AND current oscillator high < previous oscillator high OR hidden divergence enabled AND current price high < previous price high AND current oscillator high > previous oscillator high) AND (zero-line filter disabled OR both oscillator highs >= 0).
Exit: long, bar is after entry bar AND (low <= entry price * (1 - stop percentage) OR high >= entry price * (1 + target percentage)).
Exit: short, bar is after entry bar AND (high >= entry price * (1 + stop percentage) OR low <= entry price * (1 - target percentage)).
Calculation: Target and stop percentages = use separate configurable percentages for cycle-cross and divergence entries; if both levels are touched on the same bar, the stop takes precedence.
