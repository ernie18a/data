Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume), provided the SMA is positive.
Calculation: Long setup = close > open AND (close > highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars OR (low <= short EMA AND low >= long EMA - ATR * pullback allowance AND close > short EMA AND close > long EMA AND close > previous high)).
Calculation: Short setup = close < open AND (close < lowest low of the preceding breakdown-lookback bars OR (high >= short EMA AND high <= long EMA + ATR * pullback allowance AND close < short EMA AND close < long EMA AND close < previous low)).
Calculation: Long raw = close > short-term trend SMA AND close > long-term trend SMA AND close > long EMA AND short EMA > long EMA AND long EMA > its previous value AND SuperTrend is bullish AND RSI > bullish lower threshold AND RSI < bullish upper threshold AND relative volume >= normal minimum AND close != 0 AND ATR / close >= minimum ATR fraction AND ATR > 0 AND (close - long EMA) / ATR <= maximum extension AND long setup.
Calculation: Short raw = close < short-term trend SMA AND close < long-term trend SMA AND close < long EMA AND short EMA < long EMA AND long EMA < its previous value AND SuperTrend is bearish AND RSI < bearish upper threshold AND RSI > bearish lower threshold AND relative volume >= normal minimum AND close != 0 AND ATR / close >= minimum ATR fraction AND ATR > 0 AND (long EMA - close) / ATR <= maximum extension AND short setup.
Calculation: Strong long raw = long raw AND RSI >= strong bullish lower threshold AND RSI <= strong bullish upper threshold AND relative volume >= strong minimum AND (high - low <= 0 OR close >= high - (high - low) * strong-close fraction).
Calculation: Strong short raw = short raw AND RSI >= strong bearish lower threshold AND RSI <= strong bearish upper threshold AND relative volume >= strong minimum AND (high - low <= 0 OR close <= low + (high - low) * strong-close fraction).
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the stop lookback ending on the entry bar minus entry-bar ATR * stop buffer; long target = entry price + (entry price - long stop) * reward-to-risk multiple.
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over the stop lookback ending on the entry bar plus entry-bar ATR * stop buffer; short target = entry price - (short stop - entry price) * reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: Position is flat AND no exit occurred on this bar AND long raw AND ((strong long raw AND previous-bar strong long raw is false) OR (strong long raw is false AND previous-bar long raw is false)).
Short: Position is flat AND no exit occurred on this bar AND short raw AND ((strong short raw AND previous-bar strong short raw is false) OR (strong short raw is false AND previous-bar short raw is false)).
Exit: long, bar is after the entry bar AND (low <= long stop OR high >= long target).
Exit: short, bar is after the entry bar AND (high >= short stop OR low <= short target).
