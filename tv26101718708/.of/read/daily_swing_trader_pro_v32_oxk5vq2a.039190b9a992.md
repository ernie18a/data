Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its moving average over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over a lookback ending at entry, minus an ATR buffer; short stop = highest high over that lookback, plus an ATR buffer. Freeze the stop at entry.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward/risk multiple; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward/risk multiple. Freeze the target at entry.
Long: No open position AND no exit on this bar AND bar is confirmed AND (bullish trend (close > 50-period SMA AND close > 200-period SMA AND close > 21-period EMA AND 10-period EMA > 21-period EMA AND 21-period EMA rising) AND bullish SuperTrend AND RSI > 50 AND RSI < 75 AND relative volume ≥ minimum AND ATR / close ≥ minimum AND (close − 21-period EMA) / ATR ≤ maximum extension AND ((close > highest high of the prior breakout lookback AND close > open) OR (low ≤ 10-period EMA AND low ≥ 21-period EMA − ATR × pullback allowance AND close > 10-period EMA AND close > 21-period EMA AND close > prior high AND close > open))) AND (the long condition is true now and was false on the previous bar OR the same long condition with RSI 55–70, relative volume ≥ the stronger minimum, and close in the top quarter of the bar range is true now and was false on the previous bar).
Short: No open position AND no exit on this bar AND bar is confirmed AND (bearish trend (close < 50-period SMA AND close < 200-period SMA AND close < 21-period EMA AND 10-period EMA < 21-period EMA AND 21-period EMA falling) AND bearish SuperTrend AND RSI < 50 AND RSI > 25 AND relative volume ≥ minimum AND ATR / close ≥ minimum AND (21-period EMA − close) / ATR ≤ maximum extension AND ((close < lowest low of the prior breakdown lookback AND close < open) OR (high ≥ 10-period EMA AND high ≤ 21-period EMA + ATR × pullback allowance AND close < 10-period EMA AND close < 21-period EMA AND close < prior low AND close < open))) AND (the short condition is true now and was false on the previous bar OR the same short condition with RSI 30–45, relative volume ≥ the stronger minimum, and close in the bottom quarter of the bar range is true now and was false on the previous bar).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR (high ≥ long target AND low > long stop)
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR (low ≤ short target AND high < short stop)
