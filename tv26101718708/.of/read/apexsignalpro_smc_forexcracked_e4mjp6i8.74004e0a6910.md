Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot using equal numbers of bars on each side, available only after the right-side bars have completed; retain the latest confirmed value of each.
Calculation: Structure direction = initially neutral; set bullish when close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high and the previous close was at or below that same high; set bearish when close falls below the latest confirmed swing low and the previous close was at or above that same low. Update pivots before testing breaks; if both breaks occur, bearish takes precedence.
Calculation: Active order blocks = on an upward structure break with bullish candle body greater than an ATR multiple, record the high, low, and bar of the most recent bearish candle within a lookback; mirror for downward breaks and bullish candles. Remove bullish blocks when their age from the source candle exceeds the maximum age or close falls below their low; remove bearish blocks when their age exceeds the maximum age or close rises above their high. Create and remove blocks before evaluating confluence.
Calculation: Active fair value gaps = record a bullish gap from the high two bars ago to the current low when current low is above that high and the gap exceeds an ATR multiple; record a bearish gap from current high to the low two bars ago when current high is below that low and the gap exceeds an ATR multiple. Remove gaps when their age from creation exceeds the maximum age, bullish gaps when low is at or below their bottom, and bearish gaps when high is at or above their bottom. Create and remove gaps before evaluating confluence.
Calculation: Range midpoint = average of the latest confirmed swing high and low, substituting the highest high or lowest low over a fallback lookback for either pivot that is unavailable.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = sum of one point each for bullish structure direction, current low within an active bullish order block expanded on both sides by an ATR multiple, current low within an active bullish fair value gap inclusive, close below the range midpoint, and higher-timeframe close above its EMA. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including one completing on the current chart bar.
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = sum of one point each for bearish structure direction, current high within an active bearish order block expanded on both sides by an ATR multiple, current high within an active bearish fair value gap inclusive, close above the range midpoint, and higher-timeframe close below its EMA. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, including one completing on the current chart bar.
Long: Bullish confluence score >= minimum score AND close > open AND close - open > ATR times a body-size threshold AND close > previous close AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND previous signal direction is not long.
Short: Bearish confluence score >= minimum score AND close < open AND open - close > ATR times a body-size threshold AND close < previous close AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum ADX) AND previous signal direction is not short.
