Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on chart-timeframe bars using one lookback shared by all ATR-based conditions.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the configured number of chart-timeframe bars on both sides.
Calculation: Structure direction = bullish when chart close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high from at or below it; bearish when it crosses below the latest confirmed swing low from at or above it; otherwise retain the prior direction.
Calculation: Active bullish order block = the most recent bearish candle among the configured recent chart bars before an upward displacement whose body exceeds the configured ATR multiple and whose close crosses above the latest swing high; active bearish order block is the inverse. Remove a bullish block when chart close falls below its low or its age exceeds the configured maximum; remove a bearish block when close rises above its high or its age exceeds the maximum.
Calculation: Active bullish fair value gap = a gap where current low exceeds the high two chart bars earlier by more than the configured minimum ATR multiple; active bearish fair value gap is the inverse. Remove a bullish gap when low reaches its lower boundary or its age exceeds the configured maximum; remove a bearish gap when high reaches its lower boundary or its age exceeds the maximum.
Calculation: Range midpoint = midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high and low, using the chart-timeframe highest high and lowest low over the configured fallback lookback for either missing pivot.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close above/below its EMA, both calculated on the higher timeframe using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of these conditions: structure direction is bullish; chart low is between an active bullish order block's low minus the configured ATR buffer and its high plus that buffer; chart low is within an active bullish fair value gap; chart close is below the range midpoint; higher-timeframe bias is bullish. Bearish score counts the inverse conditions, using chart high for order-block proximity and fair-value-gap intersection, and chart close above the midpoint.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest active bullish order-block low minus the configured ATR buffer, or entry price minus 1.5 ATR if none is active; short stop = highest active bearish order-block high plus the configured ATR buffer, or entry price plus 1.5 ATR if none is active.
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus the distance from entry to long stop multiplied by the configured risk/reward ratio; short target = entry price minus the distance from entry to short stop multiplied by that ratio.
Long: bullish score meets the configured minimum AND close > open AND close - open exceeds the configured ATR multiple AND close > prior chart close AND ADX meets the configured minimum AND the most recent signal is not long.
Short: bearish score meets the configured minimum AND close < open AND open - close exceeds the configured ATR multiple AND close < prior chart close AND ADX meets the configured minimum AND the most recent signal is not short.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
