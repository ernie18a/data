Calculation: Currency strength = cumulative sum within each reset-frequency period of the average per-bar percentage change against the other seven currencies; for gold, average against the eight currencies. A currency’s change is positive when it is the pair’s base and negative when it is the quote; pair percentage change = (close − open) / open × 100, using chart-timeframe bars.
Long: A pair’s absolute strength difference is among the three largest gaps across all pairs of the eight non-gold currencies and XAU/USD, AND the stronger currency precedes the weaker currency in this priority order: XAU, EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD, USD, CAD, CHF, JPY.
