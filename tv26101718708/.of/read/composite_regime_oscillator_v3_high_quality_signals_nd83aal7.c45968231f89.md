Calculation: Scaled value = clamp((input - lower bound) / (upper bound - lower bound), 0, 1); use the midpoint if the bounds are equal. Each component has its own scaling bounds.
Calculation: Market inputs = fast ATR / slow ATR; (highest high over the longer drawdown lookback - close) / that highest high; (close - lowest low over the shorter range lookback) / that lowest low; (close - EMA of close) / fast ATR; (close - range-lookback lowest low) / (range-lookback highest high - range-lookback lowest low), using the midpoint for a zero range; volume / SMA of volume; and (range-lookback highest high - close) / that highest high. Ratios with a zero denominator are unavailable except for range position.
Calculation: Regime stress = SMA of the weighted sum of scaled fast-to-slow ATR ratio, scaled drawdown, and scaled rebound from the range-lookback low; use the midpoint when any of these inputs is unavailable.
Calculation: Location = SMA of the weighted sum of scaled range position, scaled rebound from the range-lookback low, and the complement of scaled distance from the range-lookback high; use the midpoint when either distance is unavailable.
Calculation: Bullish overreaction = SMA of the weighted sum of scaled complement of RSI and scaled negative ATR-normalized distance from the EMA. Bearish overreaction = SMA of the weighted sum of scaled RSI and scaled ATR-normalized distance from the EMA. Use the midpoint when RSI is unavailable.
Calculation: Pressure = SMA of the weighted sum of scaled relative volume and scaled fast-to-slow ATR ratio; use the midpoint when relative volume is unavailable. All component-score SMAs use the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion = EMA of the weighted sum of the complement of location, bullish overreaction, regime stress, and pressure. Bearish exhaustion = EMA of the weighted sum of location, bearish overreaction, the complement of regime stress, and pressure. Both EMAs use the same smoothing length. Each weighted sum uses its own weights, and complements are taken relative to the score scale.
Long: Bullish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold.
Short: Bearish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold.
Long: Bullish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND (reversal-trigger confirmation is disabled OR close > previous high OR (close > open AND previous close < previous open) OR RSI crosses above its lower trigger threshold OR close > every close in the preceding trigger lookback) AND the entire preceding condition was false on the previous bar AND (no prior long signal has occurred OR bars since the last long signal > cooldown).
Short: Bearish exhaustion >= exhaustion threshold AND (reversal-trigger confirmation is disabled OR close < previous low OR (close < open AND previous close > previous open) OR RSI crosses below its higher trigger threshold OR close < every close in the preceding trigger lookback) AND the entire preceding condition was false on the previous bar AND (no prior short signal has occurred OR bars since the last short signal > cooldown).
