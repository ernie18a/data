Calculation: Structural levels = six levels ordered by increasing anchor duration: the current-period volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 and its previous completed-period value; the current-period volume-profile point of control and its previous completed-period value for each of two longer anchor periods. Current-period levels use the period still forming. Build each volume profile from chart bars: at the start of its anchor period, fix an equal-width price grid centered on that bar's close with an ATR-scaled half-range; use a wider range for the longer anchor period. Optionally decay existing bin volumes each chart bar, with a longer decay half-life for the longer anchor period, then distribute each bar's volume equally over grid bins intersecting its low-to-high range. The point of control is the center of the highest-volume bin, choosing the lowest bin on ties. Use close times a configurable fraction as the grid's ATR fallback when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive.
Calculation: Direction score = sum, over the six structural levels, of +1 when the level exceeds its value a configurable number of chart bars ago, -1 when it is below that value, and 0 otherwise or when either value is unavailable.
Calculation: Stack score = sort the six levels ascending, retaining their original increasing-anchor-duration order for ties; for each sorted position, compare its original rank with the corresponding rank in the perfectly bullish and perfectly bearish orderings. Divide the bearish total absolute rank distance minus the bullish total absolute rank distance by the maximum possible total distance.
Calculation: Compression score = 100 minus the percentile rank of (highest structural level minus lowest structural level) / ATR over a configurable chart-bar window; substitute zero for unavailable widths when ranking, but leave the score unavailable when the current width is unavailable.
Calculation: Price-tightness score = 100 minus the percentile rank, over the same ranking window as compression, of (highest high minus lowest low over a configurable chart-bar window) / ATR; substitute zero for unavailable values when ranking, but leave the score unavailable when its current input is unavailable.
Calculation: Tightening = the structural width divided by ATR a configurable number of chart bars ago minus its current value; use the same lookback as the direction score.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe regime = parabolic SAR and close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. An up regime has SAR below close; a down regime has SAR above close; otherwise neither regime is active.
Calculation: Strong uptrend = close above a long-lookback SMA AND (highest high over that lookback minus close) / highest high at or below a configurable threshold AND direction score at or above a positive threshold. Strong downtrend = close below that SMA AND (close minus lowest low over that lookback) / lowest low at or below the same threshold AND direction score at or below the negative of that direction threshold. Either trend lockout may be disabled together.
Calculation: Bull coil state = compression score at or above its threshold AND direction score at or above a positive threshold AND price-tightness score at or above its threshold AND the higher-timeframe regime is not down when that filter is enabled AND (absolute close change over a configurable lookback divided by ATR is at or above its threshold OR current high exceeds the highest high of the preceding lookback window). The early bull coil state uses a lower compression threshold AND tightening above zero, with the other conditions unchanged.
Calculation: Bear coil state = compression score at or above its threshold AND direction score at or below the negative of the bull direction threshold AND price-tightness score at or above its threshold AND the higher-timeframe regime is not up when that filter is enabled AND (absolute close change over the same lookback divided by ATR is at or above its threshold OR current low is below the lowest low of the preceding lookback window). The early bear coil state uses the same lower compression threshold as the early bull coil state AND tightening above zero, with the other conditions unchanged.
Calculation: Rollover state = a new swing high has armed the state AND the highest high in a configurable rolling window is at least a configurable number of bars old AND (that highest high minus close) / ATR is at or above a threshold AND close is below its value a configurable number of bars ago AND high is below the previous high AND low is below the previous low. Arm whenever high exceeds the highest high of the preceding rolling window; disarm only when a bear fire occurs while rollover state is true. The rollover path may be disabled.
Calculation: Bear trigger state = (bear coil state OR rollover state) AND (absolute close change over the chop lookback divided by ATR is at or above its threshold OR current low is below the lowest low of the preceding chop window). If the net change is unavailable, treat the chop restriction as passed.
Calculation: Thrust states = short-period ATR at or above a configurable multiple of longer-period ATR AND direction score positive for bullish thrust or negative for bearish thrust AND close position within the current high-low range at or above a configurable upper threshold for bullish thrust or at or below its complementary lower threshold for bearish thrust AND close change over a configurable lookback at or above a positive ATR multiple for bullish thrust or at or below its negative for bearish thrust. Use a midpoint close position when high equals low. The thrust paths may be disabled.
Calculation: Canonical state = all six levels and a positive ATR are available AND every level is at or below close AND the current longer-anchor profile is below the current shorter-anchor profile AND the current shorter-anchor profile is at or below the current anchored VWAP AND the previous longer-anchor profile is below the current longer-anchor profile AND close minus current anchored VWAP is below a configurable ATR multiple AND close minus previous longer-anchor profile is at or above another configurable ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bull fire = (bull coil state changes from false to true OR early bull coil state changes from false to true OR bullish thrust state changes from false to true OR canonical state changes from false to true AND close exceeds the previous high) AND bars since the previous bull fire are at or above a configurable cooldown AND bars since the previous bear fire are at or above a configurable anti-flip interval AND (the chart-timeframe parabolic SAR proximity filter is disabled OR absolute close-to-SAR distance divided by ATR is at or above its threshold) AND strong downtrend is false. Initialize both fire counters as sufficiently old; increment before evaluating each bar and reset the appropriate counter after firing.
Calculation: Bear fire = (bear trigger state changes from false to true OR early bear coil state changes from false to true OR bearish thrust state changes from false to true) AND bars since the previous bear fire are at or above the same cooldown AND bars since the previous bull fire are at or above the same anti-flip interval AND (the chart-timeframe parabolic SAR proximity filter is disabled OR absolute close-to-SAR distance divided by ATR is at or above its threshold) AND strong uptrend is false. Evaluate both fires using the counters before either is reset.
Long: stack score at or above a positive threshold.
Short: stack score at or below the negative of the same threshold.
Long: canonical state is true.
Long: bull fire is true.
Short: bear fire is true.
