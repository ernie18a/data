Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest close × (1 − trailing percentage) since entering the long position
Calculation: Short trailing stop = lowest close × (1 + trailing percentage) since entering the short position
Long: close > VWAP AND close > EMA AND RSI > bullish threshold
Short: close < VWAP AND close < EMA AND RSI < bearish threshold
Exit: long, low <= long trailing stop
Exit: short, high >= short trailing stop
