Calculation: Flow line = Apply a selected EMA, SMA, WMA, HMA, or RMA to a source computed as 2 × (previous open + previous close) − current high − current low − current open; if the source is below current low, replace it with its average with current low, then if it is above current high, replace it with its average with current high.
Calculation: Wave high and wave low = Smooth each OHLC field separately using a selected SMA, EMA, or HMA and a pre-smoothing lookback; substitute the corresponding raw field wherever its smoothed value is unavailable. Compute Heikin-Ashi close as the average of the four smoothed fields and Heikin-Ashi open as the average of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close, initialized as the average of the smoothed open and close. Compute Heikin-Ashi high as the maximum of smoothed high, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close, and Heikin-Ashi low as their minimum using smoothed low. Smooth these high and low series with a selected SMA, EMA, or HMA and an independently selected post-smoothing lookback. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar when a higher timeframe is selected; otherwise use the current chart bar.
Long: close >= flow line AND wave high >= wave high AND wave low >= wave low
Short: close < flow line AND wave high >= wave high AND wave low >= wave low
Long: low > wave high AND high > wave high AND wave low >= wave low AND flow line >= flow line
Short: high < wave low AND low < wave low AND wave high >= wave high AND flow line >= flow line
