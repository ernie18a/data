Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the range session, reset at each local day boundary; range size = session high - session low.
Calculation: Eligible trade window = the configured trade session on a day whose range size is positive and during whose preceding invalidation session no high exceeded the session high and no low fell below the session low.
Calculation: Sweep state = reset daily; on the first eligible trade-window bar with high > session high or low < session low, record its opening time and direction, choosing short if both occur; until displacement is confirmed, update the short sweep extreme to the highest high or the long sweep extreme to the lowest low since that bar, including the current bar.
Calculation: Body-to-wick ratio = sum of abs(close - open) over the configured consecutive chart-bar sample divided by the sum over the same sample of high - max(open, close) + min(open, close) - low; undefined when the denominator is zero.
Long: Eligible trade window AND low < session low AND no sweep state existed on the preceding bar.
Short: Eligible trade window AND high > session high AND no sweep state existed on the preceding bar.
Long: Eligible trade window AND sweep direction = long AND displacement has not previously been confirmed that day AND close > open AND previous close > previous open AND low > high two bars ago AND elapsed whole minutes from sweep bar opening time to current bar opening time <= maximum delay AND (close - sweep extreme) / range size >= minimum displacement fraction AND body-to-wick ratio >= minimum ratio.
Short: Eligible trade window AND sweep direction = short AND displacement has not previously been confirmed that day AND close < open AND previous close < previous open AND high < low two bars ago AND elapsed whole minutes from sweep bar opening time to current bar opening time <= maximum delay AND (sweep extreme - close) / range size >= minimum displacement fraction AND body-to-wick ratio >= minimum ratio.
