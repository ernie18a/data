Calculation: Long score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of (trend points × ADX-based trend weight) + (support/resistance points × inverse ADX-based trend weight) + momentum points + higher-timeframe points + risk/reward points; trend points reward close above the selected long MA, long-term EMA below shorter-term EMA, rising adaptive MA, MACD line above signal line, and close above the Ichimoku cloud; support/resistance points reward clear higher-timeframe resistance, bullish Fibonacci position, close above cloud, proximity above support, close above an active rising trendline, channel support, a broken falling trendline, bullish squeeze breakout, and penalize channel resistance and close below nearest support; momentum points reward RSI below its overbought level and bullish RSI divergence; higher-timeframe points reward higher-timeframe EMA50 above EMA200, RSI below overbought, price above cloud, and higher-timeframe volume above its long-term average, and penalize volume below its long-term average; risk/reward points increase with prospective reward-to-risk ratio. Use a shorter-term adaptive MA for its slope and a longer-term EMA pair for trend; select the long MA from the 14 SMA, EMA, and adaptive-MA candidates with greatest cumulative long-only backtest profit, recalculated weekly.
Calculation: Short score = the mirrored long score: reward close below the selected short MA, long-term EMA below shorter-term EMA, falling adaptive MA, MACD line below signal line, and close below cloud; reward clear higher-timeframe support, bearish Fibonacci position, proximity below resistance, close below an active falling trendline, channel resistance, a broken rising trendline, and bearish squeeze breakout, while penalizing channel support and close above nearest resistance; momentum rewards RSI above its oversold level and bearish RSI divergence; higher-timeframe points reward EMA50 below EMA200, RSI above oversold, price below cloud, and strong volume, and penalize weak volume; include prospective reward-to-risk points. Select the short MA by greatest cumulative short-only backtest profit, recalculated weekly. Higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = price makes a lower low while RSI makes a higher low, comparing consecutive confirmed pivot lows.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = price makes a higher high while RSI makes a lower high, comparing consecutive confirmed pivot highs.
Calculation: Long entry bonus = points for a bounce at nearest support (low reaches within an ATR-based distance and close finishes above support, without being below support), plus additional points for a bullish rejection candle or bullish engulfing candle at that support, adequate long reward-to-risk at that support, and an ongoing bullish divergence streak relative to its historical average.
Calculation: Short entry bonus = mirrored long entry bonus for a bounce and bearish rejection or engulfing candle at nearest resistance, adequate short reward-to-risk, and an ongoing bearish divergence streak.
Calculation: Risk/reward ratio = distance from close to the selected target resistance/support, divided by distance from close to the nearest opposing stop level; use the second target when the first is within an ATR-based distance.
Calculation: Long range-entry qualification = recent bullish divergence OR support bounce OR proximity to lower channel band OR channel support OR oversold RSI with weak ADX OR ADX below its threshold with RSI in the lower-middle range.
Calculation: Short range-entry qualification = recent bearish divergence OR resistance bounce OR proximity to upper channel band OR channel resistance OR overbought RSI with weak ADX OR ADX below its threshold with RSI in the upper-middle range.
Calculation: Trailing stop = ratcheting stop based on the strongest support below a long entry or strongest resistance above a short entry, tightened to preserve at least the initial risk distance after price moves beyond that distance; combine with a ratcheting ATR trail based on the highest high since long entry or lowest low since short entry.
