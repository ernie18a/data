Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi close = average of open, high, low, and close; smoothed Heikin-Ashi open = prior smoothed open and prior smoothed close averaged, initialized from the average of open and close.
Calculation: Trend quality = absolute change in smoothed Heikin-Ashi close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar changes over that same lookback.
Calculation: Shadow Rail = use the average smoothed Heikin-Ashi true range over a lookback as mean span. Widen the rail spread with the absolute one-bar smoothed-close change divided by mean span. Adjust the lower and upper spreads by the smoothed Heikin-Ashi candle’s close position within its range, smoothed as an EMA of volume-weighted flow. Set the lower reference to smoothed close minus mean span times adjusted lower spread; set the upper reference to smoothed close plus mean span times adjusted upper spread. Initialize in the up state with the lower reference as the lower rail. In the up state, raise the rail by any new high in the lower reference; otherwise move it down or up by the lower reference’s change divided by a damped step. In the down state, initialize the upper rail at the upper reference; lower it by any new low in the upper reference; otherwise move it up or down by the upper reference’s change divided by a damped step. The damped step is based on rail drive and trend quality, with separate slower and faster damping for adverse and favorable reference moves. Switch from up to down when smoothed close is below the lower rail; switch from down to up when smoothed close is above the upper rail.
Long: Shadow Rail state changes from down to up AND (higher-timeframe last completed close > its EMA OR higher-timeframe filter is disabled) AND trend quality >= minimum trend quality.
Short: Shadow Rail state changes from up to down AND (higher-timeframe last completed close <= its EMA OR higher-timeframe filter is disabled) AND trend quality >= minimum trend quality.
