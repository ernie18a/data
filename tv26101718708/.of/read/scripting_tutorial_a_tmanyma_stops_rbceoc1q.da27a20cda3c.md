Calculation: Three moving averages = independently selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, or VWMA, each with its own source, lookback, and timeframe. Their lookbacks are independently configurable and need not be ordered. For a higher timeframe, use the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe, use the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
Long: Any selected pair of moving averages, ordered first, second, third, has its earlier-numbered average cross above its later-numbered average: the earlier average is greater than the later average AND its previous value was less than or equal to the later average's previous value.
Short: Any selected pair of moving averages, ordered first, second, third, has its earlier-numbered average cross below its later-numbered average: the earlier average is less than the later average AND its previous value was greater than or equal to the later average's previous value.
Exit: long, the moving-average pair that triggered the entry crosses below as defined by the short condition.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + entry-signal bar close × take-profit percentage OR low <= entry price - entry-signal bar close × stop-loss percentage.
