Calculation: Chart-timeframe bullish gap = current low > high two bars ago AND the difference >= a minimum gap size × current ATR AND (displacement filter disabled OR previous close − previous open >= a displacement multiplier × previous ATR); its top is current low and bottom is the high two bars ago. A bearish gap is the mirror image, with top equal to the low two bars ago and bottom equal to current high.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe gaps = gaps detected from the last completed higher-timeframe bar and the bar two higher-timeframe bars before it; require the gap size >= a minimum gap size × the last completed higher-timeframe ATR when that ATR is available. Do not apply the displacement filter. Register each gap on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Live gap = a detected gap tracked from the chart bar after registration. Optionally merge a new chart-timeframe gap into the most recently registered live chart-timeframe gap when both have the same original direction, the older gap is uninverted and untouched, its age is within the merge window, and their ranges overlap or are separated by no more than an ATR-based tolerance. Replace its boundaries with the union and reset its confirmation bar. Limit each gap collection to its configured capacity by removing its oldest gap. Remove a gap after its configured maximum age, if enabled.
Calculation: Gap lifecycle = for an uninverted bullish gap, touch occurs when low <= top, midpoint is reached when low <= (top + bottom) / 2, wick fill occurs when low <= bottom, and close-through occurs when close < bottom; reverse the inequalities and swap top with bottom for a bearish gap. Process first touch and midpoint before close-through and wick fill. A close-through turns the gap into an inverse gap of the opposite direction if inversion is enabled; otherwise remove it. If there is no close-through, remove a wick-filled gap when wick-fill removal is enabled. For an inverse gap, use its new direction and original boundaries; remove it on a close-through before evaluating its first retest, which occurs on its first touch. An inverse gap cannot be retested on its inversion bar.
Calculation: Test entry price = for a chart-timeframe uninverted gap, the more favorable of the bar open and the selected entry level, which is the near edge or midpoint; for an inverse gap, use the more favorable of the bar open and the near edge. The near edge is the top for a long and bottom for a short. The stop is the far edge minus an ATR buffer for a long, or plus an ATR buffer for a short, using ATR on the entry bar. Risk is the absolute difference between entry price and stop; target is entry price plus risk × a target multiplier for a long, or minus that amount for a short. Only open a test when risk > 0.
Long: A new bullish chart-timeframe gap is registered OR a new bullish higher-timeframe gap is registered OR a bearish live gap closes through and inversion is enabled.
Short: A new bearish chart-timeframe gap is registered OR a new bearish higher-timeframe gap is registered OR a bullish live gap closes through and inversion is enabled.
Long: A live uninverted bullish chart-timeframe gap first reaches the selected entry level AND risk > 0 OR a live bullish inverse chart-timeframe gap has its first retest without closing through AND risk > 0.
Short: A live uninverted bearish chart-timeframe gap first reaches the selected entry level AND risk > 0 OR a live bearish inverse chart-timeframe gap has its first retest without closing through AND risk > 0.
Exit: long, low <= the entry-time stop OR (bar after entry AND high >= the entry-time target) OR bars since entry >= the maximum holding period.
Exit: short, high >= the entry-time stop OR (bar after entry AND low <= the entry-time target) OR bars since entry >= the maximum holding period.
Calculation: Exit execution = evaluate stop before target before time expiry. On bars after entry, fill a stop at the open when the open gaps through it, otherwise at the stop; fill a target at the more favorable of the open and target. A stop can execute on the entry bar, but a target cannot. Fill a time exit at the close.
