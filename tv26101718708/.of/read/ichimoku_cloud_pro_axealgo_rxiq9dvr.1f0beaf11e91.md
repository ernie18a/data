Calculation: Ichimoku lines = Tenkan is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the shortest lookback; Kijun is the same midpoint over a longer lookback; Span A is the midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun; Span B is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the longest lookback. The chart cloud is bounded by the Span A and Span B values from the cloud displacement minus one chart bars ago.
Calculation: Chikou clearance = bullish when close is above both the high at the Chikou offset and the chart cloud at that historical bar; bearish when close is below both the low at that offset and the chart cloud at that historical bar. The Chikou offset equals the cloud displacement minus one chart bars.
Calculation: Kijun direction = rising when the change in Kijun over its slope lookback exceeds a positive ATR threshold, falling when it is below the negative of that threshold, and flat otherwise.
Calculation: Ichimoku regime = bullish when close is above the chart cloud and Tenkan is above Kijun; bearish when close is below the chart cloud and Tenkan is below Kijun; neutral otherwise. For each of the two higher timeframes, calculate the regime using its last completed bar and its own historical bars.
Calculation: Bias = sum of five components, each equal to +1 for bullish, -1 for bearish, and 0 otherwise: close above or below the chart cloud; Tenkan above or below Kijun; bullish or bearish Chikou clearance; current Span A above or below current Span B; and rising or falling Kijun direction.
Calculation: Chikou open-air state = bullish when close is above the current chart cloud, the chart cloud at the Chikou offset, and every high in the historical window whose newest offset is max(1, Chikou offset minus Tenkan lookback) and whose oldest offset is Chikou offset plus the open-air trailing lookback minus one; bearish when close is below the corresponding cloud bottoms and every low in that window.
Calculation: Choppy state = at least two of ADX below its threshold, Kijun direction flat, and positive ATR with chart-cloud thickness below an ATR multiple.
Calculation: Breakout score = the weighted sum of: chart-cloud thickness divided by an ATR multiple, capped at 1; an indicator for directional Chikou clearance; an indicator for Tenkan being on the breakout side of Kijun; close's distance beyond the relevant cloud boundary divided by an ATR multiple, capped at 1; the fraction of valid higher timeframes whose last completed-bar regime agrees with the breakout; relative volume's excess over its baseline divided by its scaling threshold, clamped to 0–1; and directional RSI distance from its midpoint divided by its scaling threshold, clamped to 0–1. Relative volume is volume divided by its simple moving average. Use zero for the ATR-based components when ATR is not positive. Normalize the sum to a percentage of the applicable weights, excluding the higher-timeframe weight when no higher timeframe is valid and the volume weight when average volume is not positive; use zero if applicable weights sum to zero. When chop handling is set to penalty and the choppy state holds, subtract the configured penalty and floor the result at zero.
Calculation: Confirmed swings = pivot highs and lows confirmed after an equal lookback on each side. Process a confirmed high before a confirmed low on the same bar. Append a swing of the opposite type; replace the latest swing with a more extreme swing of the same type; ignore a less extreme or equal swing of the same type.
Calculation: N-wave state = for the latest four alternating confirmed swings, compare the newest swing and the preceding same-type swing, and compare the two intervening opposite-type swings. Exclude the state when the same-type swings differ by no more than an ATR tolerance. A high-ending N wave has both the newer high and newer low above their predecessors; a low-ending N wave has both below their predecessors.

Short: Close is below the chart cloud AND Tenkan is below Kijun.
Long: Bias is at or above the bullish bias threshold.
Short: Bias is at or below the bearish bias threshold.
Long: Current Span A is at or above current Span B.
Short: Current Span A is below current Span B.
Long: Span A crosses above Span B AND the absolute change in their difference over the twist-strength lookback is at least an ATR multiple, or ATR is not positive AND the bars since the last accepted twist in either direction are at least the twist spacing. The ATR and spacing clauses both apply to the cross.
Short: Span A crosses below Span B AND the absolute change in their difference over the twist-strength lookback is at least an ATR multiple, or ATR is not positive AND the bars since the last accepted twist in either direction are at least the twist spacing. The ATR and spacing clauses both apply to the cross.
Long: Tenkan crosses above Kijun AND bars since the last accepted Tenkan/Kijun cross in either direction are at least the cross spacing.
Short: Tenkan crosses below Kijun AND bars since the last accepted Tenkan/Kijun cross in either direction are at least the cross spacing.
Long: Previous close is above the previous chart-cloud top AND Kijun is above the current chart-cloud top AND Kijun direction is rising or flat AND low is at or below Kijun AND low is at or above Kijun minus an ATR multiple AND close is above Kijun AND close is above open AND bars since the last accepted Kijun bounce in either direction are at least the bounce spacing.
Short: Previous close is below the previous chart-cloud bottom AND Kijun is below the current chart-cloud bottom AND Kijun direction is falling or flat AND high is at or above Kijun AND high is at or below Kijun plus an ATR multiple AND close is below Kijun AND close is below open AND bars since the last accepted Kijun bounce in either direction are at least the bounce spacing.
Long: Close crosses above the chart-cloud top AND, when the full-cloud rule is enabled, the most recent prior close outside the cloud was below its bottom AND bullish breakout score is at least its threshold AND bars since the last accepted breakout in either direction are at least the breakout spacing AND chop suppression is disabled or the choppy state does not hold.
Short: Close crosses below the chart-cloud bottom AND, when the full-cloud rule is enabled, the most recent prior close outside the cloud was above its top AND bearish breakout score is at least its threshold AND bars since the last accepted breakout in either direction are at least the breakout spacing AND chop suppression is disabled or the choppy state does not hold.

Short: Close is below Tenkan AND Tenkan is below Kijun AND Kijun is below the chart-cloud bottom AND bearish Chikou clearance holds AND this conjunction did not hold on the previous bar AND bars since the last accepted perfect alignment in either direction are at least the alignment spacing.
Long: Bullish Chikou open-air state holds AND it did not hold on the previous bar.
Short: Bearish Chikou open-air state holds AND it did not hold on the previous bar.

Short: Positive-ATR normalized distance (close minus Kijun) divided by ATR is at or below the negative stretch threshold AND it was above that threshold on the previous bar; use zero distance when ATR is not positive.

Short: The chart and both higher timeframes have bearish Ichimoku regimes AND they were not all bearish on the previous chart bar.

Short: A newly appended confirmed swing is a high AND the preceding three swing prices A, B, C satisfy A > C AND C > B.

Short: A confirmed swing is appended or replaced AND the resulting N-wave state ends at a low.
