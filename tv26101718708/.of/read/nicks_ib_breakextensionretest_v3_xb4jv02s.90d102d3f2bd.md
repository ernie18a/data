Calculation: Initial balance = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars in the configured initial-balance session; its range is their difference. Reset all break, retest and entry state at the start of each initial-balance session.
Calculation: Break = during the configured trading session after the initial balance, while its range is positive, walk completed lower-timeframe bars in order; optionally use the chart bar instead. The first high above the initial-balance high is an upward break; otherwise the first low below the initial-balance low is a downward break. If both occur in the same walked bar, select upward. Record the containing chart bar and whether it is in the configured break window.
Calculation: Extension = since the break, the maximum of 1 and, for an upward break, 1 + (walked-bar high - initial-balance high) / initial-balance range; for a downward break, 1 + (initial-balance low - walked-bar low) / initial-balance range. Update it before testing the retest on each walked bar.
Calculation: Retest = the first walked bar after or including the break whose extension reaches the configured minimum and whose low is at or below the initial-balance high for an upward break, or whose high is at or above the initial-balance low for a downward break. A retest consumes the setup even if its filters fail.
Calculation: Against-divergence = a newly confirmed chart-timeframe MACD-line pivot high is below the preceding pivot high while its corresponding price high is above the preceding corresponding price high, for an upward break; or a newly confirmed MACD-line pivot low is above the preceding pivot low while its corresponding price low is below the preceding corresponding price low, for a downward break. Compare consecutive oscillator pivots and use price at each oscillator pivot. Record divergence after processing the chart bar's walk, only while a break exists and no retest has occurred.
Calculation: Valid retest = a retest AND, if enabled, the chart-timeframe EMA is above its value at the configured slope lookback for an upward break or below it for a downward break AND, if enabled, the break occurred within the configured break window AND, if enabled, no against-divergence was recorded between break and retest AND, if enabled, the MACD slope/level kill is false. The upward kill is the chart-timeframe MACD line reaching or exceeding the positive extreme within the longer slope lookback AND its current value being below its value at that lookback; the downward kill is its reaching or falling below the negative extreme within that lookback AND its current value being above its value at that lookback. If the optional reprieve is enabled, suppress the upward kill when MACD is above its value at the shorter immediate-slope lookback, and suppress the downward kill when it is below that value.
Long: The first break is upward.
Short: The first break is downward.
Long: A valid retest occurs after an upward break.
Short: A valid retest occurs after a downward break.
Long: After or on a valid upward retest, before an entry has fired for it, the previous walked-bar RSI is above the long threshold AND the current walked-bar RSI is at or below that threshold AND entry has not expired. Use RSI from each completed lower-timeframe bar, or chart-timeframe RSI when the chart bar is used. Expire entry after processing a chart bar when its index minus the retest chart-bar index exceeds the configured entry window; reset entry state at the next initial-balance session.
Short: After or on a valid downward retest, before an entry has fired for it, the previous walked-bar RSI is below the short threshold AND the current walked-bar RSI is at or above that threshold AND entry has not expired. Use the same RSI and expiry rules as for long entry.
