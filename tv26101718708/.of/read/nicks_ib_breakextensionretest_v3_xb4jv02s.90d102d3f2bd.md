Calculation: Initial Balance high = highest high during the Initial Balance window
Calculation: Initial Balance low = lowest low during the Initial Balance window
Calculation: Initial Balance range = Initial Balance high − Initial Balance low
Calculation: Maximum extension = greatest value since the first break of 1 + (price excursion beyond the broken Initial Balance boundary ÷ Initial Balance range)
Calculation: Regime EMA slope = current regime EMA − its value at the longer lookback
Calculation: Bearish MACD divergence = a confirmed MACD-line pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high while its price high is higher; bullish MACD divergence = a confirmed MACD-line pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low while its price low is lower. Use equal left and right pivot lookbacks.
Long: After the Initial Balance window, the first break has high > Initial Balance high
Long: First break is upward AND a later first retest reaches Initial Balance high after Maximum extension ≥ the minimum extension AND the pullback low ≤ Initial Balance high AND the regime EMA slope > 0 when the EMA gate is enabled AND the break occurs in the allowed timing window when the timing gate is enabled AND no bearish MACD divergence occurred between break and retest when the divergence pre-kill is enabled AND NOT (MACD line reached an upper extreme within the longer slope lookback AND current MACD line < its value at that lookback AND it has not risen over the immediate slope lookback) when the MACD slope kill is enabled
Long: After a valid upward retest, lower-timeframe RSI crosses down through its long threshold within the entry window; RSI is calculated on bars lower than the chart timeframe, processed in chronological order using each available sub-bar (including the forming sub-bar), with the signal final at chart-bar close
Short: After the Initial Balance window, the first break has low < Initial Balance low
Short: First break is downward AND a later first retest reaches Initial Balance low after Maximum extension ≥ the minimum extension AND the pullback high ≥ Initial Balance low AND the regime EMA slope < 0 when the EMA gate is enabled AND the break occurs in the allowed timing window when the timing gate is enabled AND no bullish MACD divergence occurred between break and retest when the divergence pre-kill is enabled AND NOT (MACD line reached a lower extreme within the longer slope lookback AND current MACD line > its value at that lookback AND it has not fallen over the immediate slope lookback) when the MACD slope kill is enabled
Short: After a valid downward retest, lower-timeframe RSI crosses up through its short threshold within the entry window; RSI is calculated on bars lower than the chart timeframe, processed in chronological order using each available sub-bar (including the forming sub-bar), with the signal final at chart-bar close
