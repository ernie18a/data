Calculation: Impulse histogram = let the smoothed high and low be Wilder moving averages of high and low over the same lookback; let the zero-lag EMA be twice the EMA of typical price minus the EMA of that EMA, using the same lookback; let displacement equal zero-lag EMA minus smoothed high when above smoothed high, zero-lag EMA minus smoothed low when below smoothed low, and zero otherwise; impulse histogram is displacement minus its shorter-lookback SMA.
Calculation: Uptrend = impulse filtering is disabled OR both the current and previous impulse histogram are nonzero; AND, for the single-EMA approach, the close EMA is greater than its previous value, OR, for the two-EMA approach, the shorter-lookback close EMA is greater than the longer-lookback close EMA.
Calculation: Downtrend = impulse filtering is disabled OR both the current and previous impulse histogram are nonzero; AND, for the single-EMA approach, the close EMA is less than its previous value, OR, for the two-EMA approach, the shorter-lookback close EMA is less than the longer-lookback close EMA.
Long: Uptrend on the chart timeframe OR uptrend on any selected higher timeframe using its last completed bar OR uptrend on any selected lower timeframe using its last completed bar.
Short: Downtrend on the chart timeframe OR downtrend on any selected higher timeframe using its last completed bar OR downtrend on any selected lower timeframe using its last completed bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe trend changes to uptrend AND uptrend on each of three successively higher timeframes using their last completed bars.
Short: Chart-timeframe trend changes to downtrend AND downtrend on each of three successively higher timeframes using their last completed bars.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus a stop multiplier times the higher-timeframe ATR at entry OR high >= entry price plus a target multiplier times the higher-timeframe ATR at entry.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus a stop multiplier times the higher-timeframe ATR at entry OR low <= entry price minus a target multiplier times the higher-timeframe ATR at entry.
