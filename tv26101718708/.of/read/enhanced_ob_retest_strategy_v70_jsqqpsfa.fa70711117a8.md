Calculation: Higher-timeframe OHLC = final OHLC of the higher-timeframe bar containing each chart bar, made available from the start of that higher-timeframe bar; offsets and rolling lookbacks on these values count chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish order-block zones = on each chart bar, compare higher-timeframe close with the highest higher-timeframe high or lowest higher-timeframe low over the preceding swing lookback of chart bars. An upward or downward break also requires abs(close - swing level) / swing level * 100 >= breakout threshold. For an upward break, find the nearest bearish chart candle in the order-block search lookback; for a downward break, find the nearest bullish chart candle. Use the higher-timeframe open and close at that chart-bar offset as the candidate zone bounds. Accept the candidate if its height >= an ATR multiple. Replace the existing same-side zone with the candidate, or with their union if their price ranges overlap. After creating zones, if chart close > bearish-zone top + an ATR multiple, replace the bullish zone with the bearish zone and remove the bearish zone; then, if chart close < bullish-zone bottom - the same ATR multiple, replace the bearish zone with the bullish zone and remove the bullish zone. Zone overlap uses inclusive price bounds. Evaluate bullish-versus-bearish zone overlap after zone creation and before zone flips.
Calculation: High and low wick zones = when chart high equals the highest high over the wick lookback, replace the high wick zone with [max(open, close), high]; when chart low equals the lowest low over that lookback, replace the low wick zone with [low, min(open, close)]. After evaluating the current bar's decisions, remove the high wick zone if close > its top and remove the low wick zone if close < its bottom. Wick-zone overlap requires wick-zone drawing to be enabled; wick boundary values can still be updated when drawing is disabled.
Calculation: Trade eligibility = the pre-flip bullish and bearish order-block zones do not overlap AND no position is open AND no prior entry exists OR the minimum number of chart bars has elapsed since the last entry; apply the OR only to the last-entry test.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = on the first bar with an open long position, close minus an ATR multiple; on each later bar with the position open, the maximum of the previous stop and close minus the same ATR multiple. The stop calculated at a bar's close becomes active on the next bar.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = on the first bar with an open short position, close plus an ATR multiple; on each later bar with the position open, the minimum of the previous stop and close plus the same ATR multiple. The stop calculated at a bar's close becomes active on the next bar.
Long: Long entries enabled AND bullish order blocks enabled AND datetime >= backtest start AND trade eligibility AND close > bullish-zone bottom AND close < bullish-zone top AND (bullish-zone top - close) / (bullish-zone top - bullish-zone bottom) >= minimum retrace depth AND (bullish-zone top - close) / (bullish-zone top - bullish-zone bottom) <= maximum entry depth.
Short: Short entries enabled AND bearish order blocks enabled AND datetime >= backtest start AND trade eligibility AND close > bearish-zone bottom AND close < bearish-zone top AND (close - bearish-zone bottom) / (bearish-zone top - bearish-zone bottom) >= minimum retrace depth AND (close - bearish-zone bottom) / (bearish-zone top - bearish-zone bottom) <= maximum entry depth.
Long: High wick zone overlaps bearish order-block zone AND those zones did not overlap on the previous chart bar.
Short: Low wick zone overlaps bullish order-block zone AND those zones did not overlap on the previous chart bar.
Long: Wick-zone drawing enabled AND wick signals enabled AND low < low-wick-zone top AND close crosses above low-wick-zone top.
Short: Wick-zone drawing enabled AND wick signals enabled AND high > high-wick-zone bottom AND close crosses below high-wick-zone bottom.
Exit: Long, low <= active long trailing stop.
Exit: Short, high >= active short trailing stop.
