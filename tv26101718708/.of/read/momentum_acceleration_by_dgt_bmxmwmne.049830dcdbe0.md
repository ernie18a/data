Calculation: Momentum acceleration = short-term simple moving average of (selected series − selected series from one lookback ago) / lookback, minus the change in that average over one lookback divided by the lookback; selected series is closing price or on-balance volume
Long: Momentum acceleration > 0 AND previous bar's momentum acceleration ≤ 0
Short: Momentum acceleration < 0 AND previous bar's momentum acceleration ≥ 0
Exit: Long, short condition OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction)
Exit: Short, long condition OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction)
