Calculation: Equal-high and equal-low levels = Maintain separate live zones and waiting swing candidates for highs and lows. A swing is a pivot confirmed after the same number of bars on each side. On each closed bar, first test existing zones for sweeps or breaks and remove every taken zone; remove an untaken zone if its age since its last touch exceeds the enabled maximum age. Then remove waiting high candidates if high exceeds their price plus the current tolerance, and waiting low candidates if low falls below their price minus the current tolerance; also remove candidates older than the maximum touch gap plus the pivot confirmation delay. Process newly confirmed swings afterward. Tolerance is a configurable multiple of current ATR or a configurable percentage of current close. For each new swing, find the closest live zone on its side whose level is within tolerance. If found, discard the swing when its distance in bars from the zone's last touch is less than the minimum touch gap; otherwise add the touch and update the level to the highest touch for highs or lowest touch for lows. If no zone matches, combine the swing with all waiting candidates within tolerance and within the minimum and maximum touch gaps; remove those candidates and create a zone at the extreme touch if at least one matched. Otherwise store the swing as a waiting candidate, removing the oldest candidate if the per-side candidate limit is exceeded. If creating a zone exceeds the per-side zone limit, remove the zone whose level is farthest from the current close. Zones created or updated on a bar are first eligible for sweep or break detection on the next bar.
Long: Low < a live equal-low level AND close >= that level.
Long: High > a live equal-high level AND close > that level.
Short: High > a live equal-high level AND close <= that level.
Short: Low < a live equal-low level AND close < that level.
