Calculation: Opening drive = either the first configured number of chart bars of each day, or all chart bars in a configured opening session; use the first open, maximum high, minimum low, last close, and average volume. Lock on the last drive bar in first-bars mode, or on the first bar outside the opening session in session mode. Reset all drive and event state when a new drive starts. Drive range = high − low. Drive side is long if close > open, or if close = open AND close >= (high + low) / 2; otherwise it is short.
Calculation: Drive score = rounded weighted sum of thrust, close location, range, and optional volume scores, each clamped to 0–100. Thrust = 125 × abs(drive close − drive open) / drive range. Close location = 100 × (drive close − drive low) / drive range for a long drive, or 100 × (drive high − drive close) / drive range for a short drive. Range score = 100 × drive range / (1.20 × ATR at lock). Volume score = 100 × average drive volume / (1.35 × volume SMA at lock). When volume scoring is enabled and both volume measures are positive and available, weights are 0.32, 0.28, 0.25, and 0.15 respectively; otherwise omit volume and use weights 0.40, 0.32, and 0.28.
Calculation: Drive levels = fixed at lock. For a long drive, zone bottom = drive high − drive range × pullback-depth parameter, zone top = drive high, risk = min(drive low, zone bottom − ATR at lock × risk-buffer parameter), and target = drive high + drive range × target-multiple parameter. For a short drive, zone bottom = drive low, zone top = drive low + drive range × pullback-depth parameter, risk = max(drive high, zone top + ATR at lock × risk-buffer parameter), and target = drive low − drive range × target-multiple parameter.
Calculation: Tracking = the drive is locked AND the bar is outside the drive window AND bars since the last drive bar are between zero and the drive-memory parameter inclusive AND, in session-window mode, the bar is inside the tracking session. First-bars mode has no tracking-session restriction. During tracking, retain the highest high and lowest low since lock, initialized to the drive high and low.
Calculation: Follow-through = max(tracked high − drive high, 0) / current ATR for a long drive, or max(drive low − tracked low, 0) / current ATR for a short drive; use zero when ATR is unavailable or nonpositive. Follow-through is confirmed when tracking AND follow-through >= its threshold. Zone overlap means high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top. Pullback held means zone overlap AND close >= zone bottom AND close >= risk for a long drive, or zone overlap AND close <= zone top AND close <= risk for a short drive.
Calculation: Readiness score = round(0.45 × drive score + 0.32 × follow score + 0.23 × pullback score), clamped to 0–100. Follow score = round(clamp(100 × follow-through / follow-through threshold, 0, 100)). Pullback depth = clamp((drive high − low) / drive range, 0, 2) for a long drive, or clamp((high − drive low) / drive range, 0, 2) for a short drive. Pullback score is 5 if risk is broken; otherwise, if pullback is held, it is clamp(100 − 35 × abs(pullback depth − pullback-depth parameter / 2) / pullback-depth parameter, 65, 100); otherwise it is 58 if the zone overlaps, 62 if follow-through is confirmed, 35 if tracking AND bars since the drive end exceed the follow-through review parameter AND follow-through is not confirmed, and 50 otherwise. Round the pullback score.
Long: Drive locks AND drive side is long.
Short: Drive locks AND drive side is short.
Long: Tracking AND drive side is long AND follow-through is confirmed AND readiness score >= minimum-quality parameter AND no earlier follow-through event has occurred for this drive.
Short: Tracking AND drive side is short AND follow-through is confirmed AND readiness score >= minimum-quality parameter AND no earlier follow-through event has occurred for this drive.
Long: Tracking AND drive side is long AND follow-through is confirmed AND pullback is held AND readiness score >= minimum-quality parameter AND no earlier pullback event has occurred for this drive.
Short: Tracking AND drive side is short AND follow-through is confirmed AND pullback is held AND readiness score >= minimum-quality parameter AND no earlier pullback event has occurred for this drive.
Exit: long, tracking AND drive side is long AND close < risk.
Exit: short, tracking AND drive side is short AND close > risk.
