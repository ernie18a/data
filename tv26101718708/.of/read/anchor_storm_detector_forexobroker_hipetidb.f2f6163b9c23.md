Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of chart-bar typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of each of three progressively longer calendar anchor periods; use typical price if cumulative volume is zero. Each anchor can be enabled independently.
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by a longer-lookback SMA of ATR if that SMA is positive; otherwise 1.
Calculation: Up-break count = number of enabled anchors for which close > current anchored VWAP + current ATR × break-margin parameter AND previous close <= previous anchored VWAP + current ATR × break-margin parameter. Down-break count = number of enabled anchors for which close < current anchored VWAP - current ATR × break-margin parameter AND previous close >= previous anchored VWAP - current ATR × break-margin parameter.
Calculation: Anchor sum = sum over enabled anchors of +1 if close > anchored VWAP, -1 if close < anchored VWAP, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Storm score = round(min(100, 25 × max(up-break count, down-break count) + min(30, 60 × max(ATR ratio - 1, 0)) + alignment score)), where alignment score = min(25, 25 × abs(anchor sum) / enabled-anchor count) if enabled-anchor count > 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bars since signal = current bar index minus the index of the most recent confirmed long or short signal; initialize the most recent signal index to zero and update it only when a confirmed signal fires.
Long: Up-break count >= 2 AND ATR ratio > expansion threshold AND close > EMA of close AND EMA of close > its value at the slope lookback AND storm score >= background threshold.
Short: Down-break count >= 2 AND ATR ratio > expansion threshold AND close < EMA of close AND EMA of close < its value at the slope lookback AND storm score >= background threshold.
Long: Up-break count >= 2 AND ATR ratio > expansion threshold AND close > EMA of close AND EMA of close > its value at the slope lookback AND storm score >= signal threshold AND bars since signal >= cooldown.
Short: Down-break count >= 2 AND ATR ratio > expansion threshold AND close < EMA of close AND EMA of close < its value at the slope lookback AND storm score >= signal threshold AND bars since signal >= cooldown.
