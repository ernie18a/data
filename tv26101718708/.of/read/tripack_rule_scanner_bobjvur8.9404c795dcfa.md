Calculation: Smoothed StochRSI = SMA of 100 × (RSI of close − lowest RSI) / (highest RSI − lowest RSI) over the stochastic lookback, using a separate smoothing lookback
Calculation: Bollinger %B = 100 × (close − lower Bollinger band) / (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band)
Calculation: SMA distance % = 100 × (close − SMA of close) / SMA of close
Long: At least one enabled buy rule exists AND all enabled buy-rule comparisons hold, or at least one holds, according to the selected AND/OR join. Each comparison uses a selected operator from >, <, >=, <=, ==, !=, cross above, or cross below; its left operand is selected from open, close, RSI, smoothed StochRSI, Bollinger %B, ROC, PPO, SMA distance %, or CMO, and its right operand is either a selected value or another selected operand from the same list. Oscillators use close; PPO uses shorter and longer EMA lookbacks. Evaluate on the selected scan timeframe: if higher than the chart timeframe, use the last completed bar on historical bars and the forming bar in real time; if lower, use the last available completed intrabar; if equal, use the chart bar. Cross above means current left > right AND previous left <= right; cross below means current left < right AND previous left >= right.
Short: At least one enabled sell rule exists AND all enabled sell-rule comparisons hold, or at least one holds, according to the independently selected AND/OR join. Each comparison uses the same operand choices, operators, calculations, and timeframe handling as the long rules.
