Calculation: Source = a selectable price series derived from the bar fields; the default is (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Effective ATR = the greater of minimum price increment and ATR; when ATR is unavailable, use the greater of high - low and minimum price increment. Treat minimum price increment as a parameter.
Calculation: VWAP reference = session-anchored VWAP of Source; if unavailable, use the longer-period EMA of Source.
Calculation: Engine direction = initially neutral; on each completed bar, set to long if the long readiness conditions are true, otherwise set to short if the short readiness conditions are true, otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Frozen risk = at each setup, the greater of a minimum price increment multiple and the distance from entry price to the stop candidate. For a long setup, the stop candidate is the lesser of the lowest low over the stop lookback including the setup bar and entry price minus an Effective ATR multiple. For a short setup, it is the greater of the highest high over the stop lookback including the setup bar and entry price plus an Effective ATR multiple. Freeze risk until the next setup.
Calculation: Stop and targets = entry price minus Frozen risk for a long stop and plus Frozen risk for a short stop; long targets are entry price plus Frozen risk times each of three target multipliers, and short targets are entry price minus those amounts. Freeze levels until the next setup; each target multiplier is independently selectable.
Long: On a completed bar, shorter-period EMA of Source > longer-period EMA of Source AND close > longer-period EMA of Source + Effective ATR times gate multiplier AND close > VWAP reference AND shorter-period EMA of Source > its previous-bar value AND previous Engine direction != long.
Short: On a completed bar, shorter-period EMA of Source < longer-period EMA of Source AND close < longer-period EMA of Source - Effective ATR times gate multiplier AND close < VWAP reference AND shorter-period EMA of Source < its previous-bar value AND previous Engine direction != short.
Exit: Long, on a completed bar after the setup bar, low <= long stop OR high >= any of the three long targets.
Exit: Short, on a completed bar after the setup bar, high >= short stop OR low <= any of the three short targets.
